LN905 · 15–25 min lesson

Annotated bibliography 不是摘要：它是一份“带判断的来源档案”

你已经有十篇中文梗概。现在真正要学的是：怎样把其中三篇压缩成正式、客观、能服务自己论证的 100–300 词英文 annotation。

今天的可见成果：看懂一个 Lessmann worked example，并独立搭出 Khandani 的第一版 annotation。

1. 你到底在写什么？

每一项由两部分构成：先给完整 reference，再给一段短 annotation。annotation 不只是回答“这篇文章说了什么”，还必须回答“我为什么相信/限制它，以及我会拿它做什么”。

Reference + Summary → Evaluation → Intended use
Summary

它做了什么？

问题、方法/数据、核心发现。只选与你 essay question 直接相关的部分。

Evaluation

证据有多强？

一个具体 strength，加一个具体 limitation，并说明这个 limitation 限制了哪种结论。

Intended use

我为什么需要它？

它将 support、challenge 或 qualify 你哪一个精确判断，并和哪篇来源形成对话。

约 45% Summary
约 35% Evaluation
约 20% Use

建议目标：先写 160–220 词。这样足够覆盖课程标准，又不容易把段落写成 mini essay。上面的比例是写作策略，不是课程硬性评分比例。

最常见误区：把论文 abstract 换几个词，再加一句 “This source is useful.” 这仍然只是 description，因为它没有独立评价，也没有说明具体用途。

2. 用六句话完成第一稿

  1. 身份 + 范围：作者从什么专业位置、面向什么受众，研究什么问题？
  2. 方法 + 数据：用什么设计、数据或理论来回答？
  3. 核心发现：只写最能回答你 essay question 的结果。
  4. 具体优势：哪一个设计特征提高了可信度？为什么？
  5. 具体局限：缺陷是什么，因此你不能得出什么结论？
  6. 精确用途：它将 support / challenge / qualify 你的哪一段判断？和谁一起读？
判断一句 evaluation 是否合格：如果把文章标题换掉，这句话还适用于几乎所有论文（如 “the sample is large” 或 “the paper is useful”），它就还不够具体。

3. Lessmann：把仓库梗概压缩成 annotation

Reference
Lessmann, S., Baesens, B., Seow, H.-V., & Thomas, L. C. (2015). Benchmarking state-of-the-art classification algorithms for credit scoring: An update of research. European Journal of Operational Research, 247(1), 124–136. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejor.2015.05.030

Summary · scope and authorityWritten by credit-scoring researchers and published in the European Journal of Operational Research, Lessmann et al. (2015) examine whether advanced classification methods consistently outperform logistic regression for credit scoring.
Summary · methodThey compare 41 classifiers across eight real datasets using repeated cross-validation, six performance measures and simulated misclassification costs.
Summary · findingSeveral advanced methods outperform logistic regression, but rankings vary with the metric and cost structure, so the statistically most accurate model is not always the most valuable.
Evaluation · strengthThe breadth of the benchmark is a major strength because it makes the reported gains less dependent on one dataset, algorithm or evaluation measure.
Evaluation · limitation + consequenceHowever, the cost analysis is a simplified simulation that excludes interpretability, governance, maintenance and regulatory costs; the study therefore cannot show that its best-performing model should automatically be deployed.
Intended useI will use this source to define when predictive gains are material and stable, while reading it alongside Rudin (2019), who places the burden on complex-model advocates to show that no similarly accurate interpretable alternative exists.
注意逻辑：这里不是“先夸再挑刺”。strength 和 limitation 都在回答同一个问题：这篇文献能在多大程度上支持“复杂模型值得牺牲可解释性”。

4. 快速判断：每句话在干什么？

把每句归类为 Summary、Evaluation 或 Intended use。答完再检查。

A. The authors use transaction, balance and credit-bureau data from one commercial bank to predict serious delinquency.
B. Because the data come from a single bank during the financial crisis, the estimated gains may not generalise to other lenders or periods.
C. I will use the paper to provide an economic scale for a material predictive gain, while qualifying the claim that algorithmic complexity alone produced that gain.

6. 提交前的七项检查

最后两项待核验：课程采用的具体 referencing style；reference 是否计入词数。仓库现有材料没有确认这两点，不应凭习惯猜。

一句话模板，只在卡住时用

[Author] examines [problem/context] using [method/data]. The study finds/argues [central claim]. Its main strength is [specific strength] because [reason], although [specific limitation] restricts [scope/inference]. This source will support/challenge/qualify [precise paragraph job] and will be read alongside [source + relationship].

模板是用来检查功能是否齐全的，不是要求每篇都写成相同句型。理论/评论型来源（如 Rudin）应把 “method/data” 换成论证材料与案例基础。
