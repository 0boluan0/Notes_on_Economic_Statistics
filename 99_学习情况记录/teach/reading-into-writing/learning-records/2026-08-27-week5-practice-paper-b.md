> [!note] 转写说明
> 2026-08-27 根据 DigiExam 答题界面照片归档。保留原稿的措辞、拼写、语法、标点和五段结构；只统一屏幕自动换行，不作语言纠正。

Nudge,described as "any aspect of the choice architecture that alters people's behaviour in a predictable way without forbidding any options ot significantly changing their economic incentives" (Thaler and Sunstein, 2008,p.6,cited by Goodwin, 2012), is widely considered and used by many governments and international organizations. While it can change people's actions in a slight but useful way, it also brings some criticism like unable to deal with big problems. By analysing the advantages and disadvantages of this kind of policy, this essay claims that nudge can be used in certain areas to deal with small and not urgent questions by the governments. For those essential and urgent questions, boosting and government intervention is required.

Firstly, nudge does have some benefits for both policy makers and individuals. The most significant benefits is that it have the ability to adjust people's behaviour unconsiously while preserving their freedom of choice. Sleek (2013) uses the example of organ donations in the USA and European countries to contribute to this benefit. Similarly, Hertwig et al. (2019) claim that 'nudges' can change people's action in a nonmonetary and nonregulatory way. Taken together, they all agree that 'nudges' can protect citizens' freedom because they do not force people to act as a certain way. What's more, nudging can make relatively huge progress by little cost. Using the data of reducing the wasted appinotments rates by nudging, Sleek (2013) suggests that even small change that let the patient to write appoinment card by themselves can reduce the wasted time and inefficency in hospital. This exchange does not require more labour and maybe more equipments, but can make a notable difference in improving of both hospitals and patients. All in all, nudge can make positive exchanges by a inaction way that protects freedom and effective.

However, there are still some criticism about nudging, mainly about the paternalistic, the lack of long term effects and lack of ability to make big change.Sleek (2013) and Goodwin (2012) both mention that nudging policy may refer to as 'paternalism'. That means nudging may control or influcent people's lives too much, even if in a unconcious way. Moreover, Goodwin (2012) suggests that nudge is "unable to deliver the kind of substantive changes that are needed to tackle the big problems that society faces". By claiming this, he conveys the ideal that nudge maybe only effective for the small questions and fail to make enomous changes. Sleek (2013) contributes that there is remain no evidence for long-term effects of nudge policy. Both Sleek (2013) and Goodwin (2012) 's claims suggest that nudges have a certain sweet point for questions it can handle. Taken together, nudge policy do have some lacks in dealing with long-term and severe problems.

Considering about the negative and positive aspects of nudging, this essay claims that nudge can and should be used by governments, but only in certain areas and for short term influences, and also need help from boosting and government intervention. For those problems which not urgent and do not need big changes, nudge may be a good choice to deal with the problems in a inaction but effective way and protect the choice freedom of all individuals influenced, But when it comes to the areas like like Hertwig et al. (2019) highlights, nudging itself can not suit to all the problems in the society, especially for urgent or long-term problems. In this case, boosting, mentioned by Hertwig et al. (2019), may have more efforts on it. So, nudge pollicy can be used by governments but need to carefully consider about the areas they applied.

To sum up, nudge is useful for changing individuals' behaviours in a silent but efficent and effective way, but fails to dealing with long-term and urgent problems. Governments should use nudge policies but need to consider carefully to use them. Working with deliberative models can be more effective way to deal social problems.

- 高风险强词最后检查：`all`、`only`、`required`、`no evidence`、`fails`、`more effective`。没有原文支持时改成 `may / remains uncertain / is insufficient on its own`。


## One whole-output revision

只交一次完整修订稿，约 550–700 词：

1. 保留你的条件式答案，但把判断限定为：nudges 适合边界清楚、日常、低强制的行为问题；结构性问题不能只靠 nudge。
2. 好处段只写材料能支持的效果与选择自由，不补成本、设备或长期因果。
3. 局限段分清三件事：操控/透明度问题、应对结构性大问题的规模限制、长期效果仍不确定。不要改写成“紧急 versus 不紧急”。
4. 建议段准确区分 regulation、deliberative engagement 和 boosts；不要声称 boosts 已被证明更有效。
5. 每写 `similarly`、`both`、`taken together` 前，先确认两个来源的**命题关系**真的成立。全文意义稳定后，最后统一修反复出现的语言模式。
