# `Among the many reasons for X, the most compelling are A and B.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Among the many reasons for X, the most compelling are A and B.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：在承认原因很多的同时明确本文的选择和优先级。
>
> **适用时机**：题目原因很多但篇幅有限时。
>
> **边界**：A、B 必须有最强 evidence，而不是最容易想到。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Among the many explanations for persistent gender stereotypes, the most compelling are repeated social communication and institutional backlash.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`Of the various factors..., A and B appear most significant.`。
