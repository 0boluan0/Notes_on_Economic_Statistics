# `Warrant: why do these grounds support this claim?`

> [!question] 正面
> `Warrant: why do these grounds support this claim?`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：说出证据到结论之间的桥。
>
> **适用时机**：证据存在但推论感觉“跳了一步”时。
>
> **边界**：warrant 不能凭空发明；若来源没提供，只能标为 assumption 或 limitation。
>
> **例句/例子**：Lower emissions support policy effectiveness only if the decline can reasonably be linked to the policy rather than other factors.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：问：`What must be true for this evidence to support the claim?`。
>
