# `Paragraphs are organised by the writer’s claims or themes, not Source A/B/C.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Paragraphs are organised by the writer’s claims or themes, not Source A/B/C.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：实现真正 synthesis。
>
> **适用时机**：从 source map 进入 outline 时。
>
> **边界**：同段多源必须有清楚 relation；不能把三份 summary 粘在一起。
>
> **例句/例子**：A paragraph on labour-force adaptation combines evidence from all relevant extracts.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：先写 topic sentence，再填 source roles。
>
