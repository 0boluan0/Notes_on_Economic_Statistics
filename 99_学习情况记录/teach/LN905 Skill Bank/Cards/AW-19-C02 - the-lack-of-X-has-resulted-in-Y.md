# `The lack of X has resulted in Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `The lack of X has resulted in Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把“缺少某物”名词化为明确原因，再连接结果。
>
> **适用时机**：材料真正支持缺失因素与结果之间的因果方向时。
>
> **边界**：has resulted in 是较强因果；若证据只显示可能影响，改用 may have contributed to。
>
> **例句**：`The lack of clear climate communication has resulted in low public understanding of policy trade-offs.`
>
