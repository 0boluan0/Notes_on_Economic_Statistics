# `Both X and Y suggest that Z.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Both X and Y suggest that Z.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把两个来源的共同点放进 writer-led sentence。
>
> **适用时机**：来源支持同一 proposition，但 evidence/method 可不同。
>
> **边界**：Z 必须真是 overlap，不能抹平重要差异。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Both the survey and the experiment suggest that patterns of use matter more than total screen time.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`There is agreement between X and Y that...`。
>
