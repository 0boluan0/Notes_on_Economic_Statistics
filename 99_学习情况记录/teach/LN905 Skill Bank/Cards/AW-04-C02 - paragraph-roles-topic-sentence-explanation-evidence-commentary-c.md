# `Paragraph roles: topic sentence → explanation → evidence → commentary → conclusion/transition.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Paragraph roles: topic sentence → explanation → evidence → commentary → conclusion/transition.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把一个段落写成完整的小论证。
>
> **适用时机**：规划 paragraph job 或发现 evidence 前后没有解释时。
>
> **边界**：角色可合并但不能省掉逻辑；不是每段都机械五句。
>
> **例句/例子**：Claim that framing effects vary → explain audience mechanism → cite study → interpret limitation → link to policy communication.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：课程称其为 paragraph as a mini-essay。
