# `Unity: every sentence serves one paragraph purpose.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Unity: every sentence serves one paragraph purpose.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：保证一个段落只完成一个主要 job。
>
> **适用时机**：段落越写越长、出现新机制或新问题时。
>
> **边界**：相关主题不等于同一职能；新论点通常需要新段。
>
> **例句/例子**：A paragraph on labour supply should not suddenly evaluate fertility incentives without a transition and new topic sentence.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：删、移或另起段，而不是靠 `Moreover` 硬接。
>
