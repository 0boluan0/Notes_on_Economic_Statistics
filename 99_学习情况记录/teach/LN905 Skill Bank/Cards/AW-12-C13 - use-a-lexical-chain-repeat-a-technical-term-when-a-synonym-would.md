# `Use a lexical chain; repeat a technical term when a synonym would distort it.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Use a lexical chain; repeat a technical term when a synonym would distort it.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：让同一概念跨句保持可追踪。
>
> **适用时机**：担心重复而开始用不准确“高级同义词”时。
>
> **边界**：academic cohesion 优先于词汇花哨；technical meaning 不能漂移。
>
> **例句/例子**：Repeat `fertility rate` rather than alternating with `birth tendency` and `child production`.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：可用 hypernym 或 precise reference noun，但先保义。
>
