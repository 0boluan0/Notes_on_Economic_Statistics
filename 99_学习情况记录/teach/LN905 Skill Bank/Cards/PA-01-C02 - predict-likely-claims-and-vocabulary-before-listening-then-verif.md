# `Predict likely claims and vocabulary before listening, then verify rather than force the prediction.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Predict likely claims and vocabulary before listening, then verify rather than force the prediction.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：降低第一次听的 processing load。
>
> **适用时机**：看到 title、speaker 或 guiding question 后。
>
> **边界**：prediction 是 hypothesis，不是答案；听到相反内容必须更新。
>
> **例句/例子**：A talk on population decline may discuss labour, fertility and public finance, but only the talk determines its actual structure.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：写 3–5 个可能概念，不写完整 model answer。
