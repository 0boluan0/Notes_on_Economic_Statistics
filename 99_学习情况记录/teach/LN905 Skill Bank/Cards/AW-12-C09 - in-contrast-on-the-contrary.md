# `in contrast ≠ on the contrary`

> [!question] 正面
> `in contrast ≠ on the contrary`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：区分“两个对象不同”与“直接否定前一句”。
>
> **适用时机**：准备表达 contrast 时。
>
> **边界**：`on the contrary` 只用于纠正或反转一个命题；普通比较用 `in contrast`。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Urban populations grew; in contrast, several rural regions declined.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`This is not inevitable. On the contrary, some regions have adapted successfully.`。
>
