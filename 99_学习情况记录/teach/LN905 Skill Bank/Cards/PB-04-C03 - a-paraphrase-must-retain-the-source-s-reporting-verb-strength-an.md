# `A paraphrase must retain the source’s reporting verb strength and scope.`

> [!question] 正面
> `A paraphrase must retain the source’s reporting verb strength and scope.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：避免 meaning drift。
>
> **适用时机**：改写 source claim 后。
>
> **边界**：`suggests` 不能升级为 `proves`; `some` 不能变 `all`。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The study suggests a small association among adolescents` retains both strength and population.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：technical terms 可重复。
>
