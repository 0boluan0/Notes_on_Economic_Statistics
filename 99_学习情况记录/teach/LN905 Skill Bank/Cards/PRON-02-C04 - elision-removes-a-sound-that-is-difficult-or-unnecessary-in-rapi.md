# `Elision removes a sound that is difficult or unnecessary in rapid speech.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Elision removes a sound that is difficult or unnecessary in rapid speech.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：识别和产出自然连接语流。
>
> **适用时机**：相邻辅音 cluster 中。
>
> **边界**：目标仍是 intelligibility；不能随意吞掉关键词的辨音线索。
>
> **例句/例子**：`next day` may lose /t/ in rapid connected speech.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：先能听出，再适度使用。
