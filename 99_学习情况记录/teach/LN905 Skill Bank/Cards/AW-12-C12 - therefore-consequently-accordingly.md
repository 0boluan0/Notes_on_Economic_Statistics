# `therefore / consequently / accordingly`

> [!question] 正面
> `therefore / consequently / accordingly`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：表达有依据的结果或推论。
>
> **适用时机**：前文确实支持后文 result 时。
>
> **边界**：不能用连接词制造不存在的 causality。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The studies measure different behaviours; therefore, their estimates are not directly comparable.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`as a result`; `thus`; `hence`。
>
