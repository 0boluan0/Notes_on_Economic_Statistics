# `Read only the topic sentences: can they reconstruct the essay argument?`

> [!question] 正面
> `Read only the topic sentences: can they reconstruct the essay argument?`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：检查段落顺序和宏观叙事是否清楚。
>
> **适用时机**：outline 完成后或整篇 revision 时。
>
> **边界**：topic sentences 不能只是同一主题的不同标签。
>
> **例句/例子**：If the sequence reads mechanism → limitation → implication, the reader can follow the writer’s argument.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：把 thesis 和所有 topic sentences 连起来朗读。
>
