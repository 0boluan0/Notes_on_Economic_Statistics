# `Rebuttal: acknowledge a valid exception or counter-view and answer it.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Rebuttal: acknowledge a valid exception or counter-view and answer it.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：显示论证经得住真正的反例。
>
> **适用时机**：存在会改变 claim scope 的 counter-evidence 时。
>
> **边界**：不能只写 `However, others disagree`；必须说明对本结论的影响。
>
> **例句/例子**：Even if immigration eases labour shortages, its fiscal effects depend on migrants’ age and employment profiles.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：rebuttal 可缩小 claim，而不必把自己推翻。
>
