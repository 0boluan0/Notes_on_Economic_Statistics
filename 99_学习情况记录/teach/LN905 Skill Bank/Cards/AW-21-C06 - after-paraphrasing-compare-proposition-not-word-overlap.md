# `After paraphrasing, compare proposition—not word overlap.`

> [!question] 正面
> `After paraphrasing, compare proposition—not word overlap.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：用意义边界检查改写。
>
> **适用时机**：完成整句或整段后。
>
> **边界**：只盯相同单词会误判准确改写；只盯流畅度会漏掉 meaning drift。
>
> **例句/例子**：Check who claims what, with what certainty, about which group/context, and with what causal relation.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：四项检查：ownership / certainty / scope / causality。
