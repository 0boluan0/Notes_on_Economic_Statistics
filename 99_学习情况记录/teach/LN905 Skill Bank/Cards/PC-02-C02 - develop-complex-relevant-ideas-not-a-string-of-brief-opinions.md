# `Develop complex relevant ideas, not a string of brief opinions.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Develop complex relevant ideas, not a string of brief opinions.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：对齐 Paper C 高档 criteria。
>
> **适用时机**：准备或复盘 discussion 时。
>
> **边界**：复杂不等于句法越难；重点是 idea 有 reason/example/qualification。
>
> **例句/例子**：A qualified point with one concrete mechanism is stronger than three unsupported opinions.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：clarity over grammatical complexity。
>
