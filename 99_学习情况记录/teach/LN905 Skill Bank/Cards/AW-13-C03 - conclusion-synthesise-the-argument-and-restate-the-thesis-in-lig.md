# `Conclusion: synthesise the argument and restate the thesis in light of the evidence.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Conclusion: synthesise the argument and restate the thesis in light of the evidence.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：让结尾回答“经过论证，现在能得出什么”。
>
> **适用时机**：全文证据与评价完成后。
>
> **边界**：不能逐段机械复述，也不能加入新 source evidence。
>
> **例句/例子**：Restate that population ageing creates conditional rather than inevitable fiscal pressure, then integrate the key conditions.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：不要照抄 introduction thesis；体现证据后的精确化。
