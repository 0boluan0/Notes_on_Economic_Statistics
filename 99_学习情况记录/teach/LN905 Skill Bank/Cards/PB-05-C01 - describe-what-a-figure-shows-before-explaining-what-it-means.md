# `Describe what a figure shows before explaining what it means.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Describe what a figure shows before explaining what it means.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：分开 observation 与 implication。
>
> **适用时机**：extract 包含 graph/table 时。
>
> **边界**：不能把 interpretation 写成图表直接显示的 fact。
>
> **例句/例子**：Description: the share aged 65+ rises. Implication: this may increase care demand if other conditions remain constant.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：先 axes/unit/time/population，再 trend/comparison。
>
