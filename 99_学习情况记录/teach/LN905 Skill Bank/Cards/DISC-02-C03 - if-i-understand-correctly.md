# `If I understand correctly, you're saying that X.`

> [!question] 正面
> `If I understand correctly, you're saying that X.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：先用自己的话复述对方 main point，再邀请确认。
>
> **适用时机**：观点复杂、你将要挑战它，或需要确保没有误解时。
>
> **边界**：复述必须保留对方的 certainty、scope 和 causality；不能偷偷写成更弱或更荒谬的版本。
>
> **例句**：`If I understand correctly, you're saying that gender equality can benefit men as well as women.`
>
