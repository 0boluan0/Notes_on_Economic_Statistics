# `may / might / could / seem / appear / likely / possible`

> [!question] 正面
> `may / might / could / seem / appear / likely / possible`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：在 evidence 不完整或 scope 有限时 hedge。
>
> **适用时机**：需要降低 certainty 或表达条件性时。
>
> **边界**：不要每句堆 hedge；每个 hedge 应对应真实 uncertainty。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The framing effect may vary across audiences.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`appears to`; `is likely to`; `it is possible that`。
>
