# `X has a direct bearing on Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `X has a direct bearing on Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：说明一项发现与当前 question/claim 直接相关。
>
> **适用时机**：需要评价 relevance，而非 strength 时。
>
> **边界**：bearing on 表示相关影响，不表示证明。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Evidence on age-specific employment has a direct bearing on claims about the fiscal effects of ageing.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`X is directly relevant to Y because...`。
>
