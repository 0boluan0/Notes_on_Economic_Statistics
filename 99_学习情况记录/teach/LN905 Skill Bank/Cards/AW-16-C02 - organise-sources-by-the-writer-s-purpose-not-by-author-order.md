# `Organise sources by the writer’s purpose, not by author order.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Organise sources by the writer’s purpose, not by author order.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把多来源材料变成 writer-led essay。
>
> **适用时机**：三份 extracts 读完后建立 source map 时。
>
> **边界**：一个 paragraph 可用多源；一个 source 也可服务多个 paragraph jobs。
>
> **例句/例子**：Group evidence by labour supply, fertility and immigration rather than Source A/B/C.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：先写 theme/claim 列，再把 source evidence 路由进去。
>
