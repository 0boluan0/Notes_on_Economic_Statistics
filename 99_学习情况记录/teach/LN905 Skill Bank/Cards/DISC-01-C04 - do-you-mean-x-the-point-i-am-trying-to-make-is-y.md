# `Do you mean X? / The point I am trying to make is Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Do you mean X? / The point I am trying to make is Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：进行 clarification 与 repair。
>
> **适用时机**：没听懂同伴或发现自己被误解时。
>
> **边界**：复述要保留对方 meaning，不能偷换成更容易反驳的版本。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Do you mean that the effect is small on average, not that it is absent?`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`To put it another way...`; `What I mean is...`。
