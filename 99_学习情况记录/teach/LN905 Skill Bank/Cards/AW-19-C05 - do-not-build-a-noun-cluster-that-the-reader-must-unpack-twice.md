# `Do not build a noun cluster that the reader must unpack twice.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Do not build a noun cluster that the reader must unpack twice.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：防止 nominalisation 把清晰动词句压成难读名词串。
>
> **适用时机**：连续出现三个以上前置名词时。
>
> **边界**：压缩只有在关系仍一眼清楚时才有价值。
>
> **例句/例子**：Replace `rapid renewable energy investment policy implementation failure` with a clause that states who failed to implement what.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：需要时恢复 active verb。
>
