# `Are the speaker and evidence credible, current and potentially biased?`

> [!question] 正面
> `Are the speaker and evidence credible, current and potentially biased?`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：评价 evidence source，而非只评价话题。
>
> **适用时机**：lecture 引用研究、机构或个人经验时。
>
> **边界**：bias 是可能影响选择/呈现，不是自动证明内容错误。
>
> **例句/例子**：A campaign speaker may select benefits strategically even when the cited data are accurate.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：用 CRAAP 中最相关维度。
