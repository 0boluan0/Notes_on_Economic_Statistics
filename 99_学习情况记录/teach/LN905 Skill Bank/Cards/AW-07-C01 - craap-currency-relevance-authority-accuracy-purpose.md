# `CRAAP: Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose.`

> [!question] 正面
> `CRAAP: Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：用五个维度判断来源是否可靠且适合当前用途。
>
> **适用时机**：annotated bibliography、source selection 或评价 lecture evidence 时。
>
> **边界**：不是五项机械打勾；与当前 claim 最有关的维度优先。
>
> **例句/例子**：A recent expert source may still be weak for a causal claim if its method is anecdotal.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：每次评价至少指出具体 evidence。
>
