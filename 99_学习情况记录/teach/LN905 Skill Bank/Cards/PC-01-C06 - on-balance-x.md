# `On balance, X.`

> [!question] 正面
> `On balance, X.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：在比较多项理由后给出综合立场。
>
> **适用时机**：Synthesis presentation 结尾。
>
> **边界**：不能成为空泛 `both sides`; 必须说明哪一侧/条件更有分量。
>
> **例句/例子**：`On balance, population decline is a policy challenge, but not an inevitable economic crisis.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`Overall`; `Having weighed the merits...`。
>
