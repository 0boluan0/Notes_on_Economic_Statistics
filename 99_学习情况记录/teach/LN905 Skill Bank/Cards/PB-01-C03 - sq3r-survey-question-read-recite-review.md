# `SQ3R: Survey → Question → Read → Recite → Review.`

> [!question] 正面
> `SQ3R: Survey → Question → Read → Recite → Review.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把长文阅读从被动划线变成问题驱动检索。
>
> **适用时机**：独立 topic reading 或 Paper B 准备时。
>
> **边界**：考试时间紧时可压缩，但不能跳过 question 与 recall of meaning。
>
> **例句/例子**：Survey headings, ask what each section contributes, read for answers, restate from memory, then review gaps.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：课件将其用于 climate misinformation text。
>
