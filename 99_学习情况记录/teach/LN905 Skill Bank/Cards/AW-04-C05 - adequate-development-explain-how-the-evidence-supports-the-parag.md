# `Adequate development: explain how the evidence supports the paragraph claim.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Adequate development: explain how the evidence supports the paragraph claim.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：避免“一句观点 + 一个 citation”就结束。
>
> **适用时机**：有 evidence 但阅卷人仍要替你推理时。
>
> **边界**：不能重复 evidence 当作 explanation；必须说出 warrant 或 implication。
>
> **例句/例子**：After reporting a correlation, explain why it supports only an association claim and not a causal one.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：问：`How? Why? So what? To what extent?`。
