# `I understand where you are coming from; however, X.`

> [!question] 正面
> `I understand where you are coming from; however, X.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：礼貌承认对方立场后提出实质异议。
>
> **适用时机**：有合理 disagreement 时。
>
> **边界**：前半句不是空礼貌；X 要回应对方真实 reasoning。
>
> **例句/例子**：`I understand where you are coming from; however, the evidence is correlational rather than causal.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`I am afraid I do not entirely agree.`。
