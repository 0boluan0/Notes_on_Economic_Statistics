# `A nuanced answer states conditions, degree or trade-offs—not merely “both sides”.`

> [!question] 正面
> `A nuanced answer states conditions, degree or trade-offs—not merely “both sides”.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：提高 thesis 和 synthesis 的 analytical depth。
>
> **适用时机**：题目问 extent、effectiveness 或 concern 时。
>
> **边界**：nuance 必须来自 evidence，不是随意写 `it depends`。
>
> **例句/例子**：Social media effects are small on average but can be substantial for particular users and use patterns.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：用 scope、mechanism、population 或 time horizon 限定。
>
