# `How far can this finding be generalised, and what evidence disagrees?`

> [!question] 正面
> `How far can this finding be generalised, and what evidence disagrees?`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：同时检查 scope 与 counter-evidence。
>
> **适用时机**：source 做 broad claim 时。
>
> **边界**：不适用更大 population 不代表对原 sample 无效。
>
> **例句/例子**：A Western sample may be valid locally but insufficient for a universal gender claim.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：把 claim 缩到 evidence 支持范围。
>
