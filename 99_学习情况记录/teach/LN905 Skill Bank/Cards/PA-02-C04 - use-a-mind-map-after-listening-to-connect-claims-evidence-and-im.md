# `Use a mind map after listening to connect claims, evidence and implications.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Use a mind map after listening to connect claims, evidence and implications.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把线性输入转成可写的 meaning/evidence map。
>
> **适用时机**：talk 有多个互相关联的 points 时。
>
> **边界**：mind map 是整理步骤，不是最终答案；不应加入 speaker 没说的机制。
>
> **例句/例子**：Central claim in the centre; branches for causes, evidence, limitations and implications.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：Demographics listening 课上明确练过 notes → mind map。
>
