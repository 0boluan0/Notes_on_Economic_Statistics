# `X is limited due to Y / questionable because Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `X is limited due to Y / questionable because Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把评价词与具体理由绑定。
>
> **适用时机**：需要简洁评价 scope、method 或 logic 时。
>
> **边界**：Y 不能只是同义重复，如 `limited because it has limitations`。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The comparison is limited due to differences in how platform use was measured.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：概括多项有明确依据的局限时用 `X has been criticised for A, B, and C.`；单项评价可用 `X has shortcomings because...` 或 `X is inconsistent with Y.`。
>
