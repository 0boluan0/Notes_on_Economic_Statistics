# `X has been criticised for A, B, and C.`

> [!question] 正面
> `X has been criticised for A, B, and C.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把多个有来源依据的批评写成平行、完整的成分。
>
> **适用时机**：引言预告 disadvantages，或局限段开头列出后文真正要分析的 concerns 时。
>
> **边界**：A/B/C 必须是材料中的真实批评；`unknown` 不能偷换成 `failure`。如果三项包括批评、未知状态和能力限制的混合，用下面的 `sources raise concerns` 变体。
>
> **四主题例句**：`Gender stereotypes have been criticised for prescribing behaviour, reinforcing power inequalities, and penalising counter-stereotypical choices.`
>
> **真实写作变体**：先让步再引入批评：`Although X may Y, it has been criticised for A and B.`；来源贡献不同类型的 concern 时：`The sources raise three concerns about X: A, B, and C.`；只评价一个限制时仍可用 `X is limited because Y.`
>
