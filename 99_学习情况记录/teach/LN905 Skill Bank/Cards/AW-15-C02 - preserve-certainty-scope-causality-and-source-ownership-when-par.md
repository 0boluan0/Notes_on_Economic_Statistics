# `Preserve certainty, scope, causality and source ownership when paraphrasing.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Preserve certainty, scope, causality and source ownership when paraphrasing.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：定义准确改写的四条不可改变边界。
>
> **适用时机**：任何 source transformation 时。
>
> **边界**：词汇和句法可以变；这四类意义不能被加强、扩大、因果化或换主人。
>
> **例句/例子**：`may be associated with some users` cannot become `causes harm to all users`.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：写完后反向问：原作者会同意这句吗？
