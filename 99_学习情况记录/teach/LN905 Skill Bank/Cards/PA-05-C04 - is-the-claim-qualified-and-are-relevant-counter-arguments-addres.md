# `Is the claim qualified, and are relevant counter-arguments addressed?`

> [!question] 正面
> `Is the claim qualified, and are relevant counter-arguments addressed?`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：检查 lecture 是否承认 scope 和 rebuttal。
>
> **适用时机**：central claim 很广或强时。
>
> **边界**：缺少 counter-view 可能削弱 balance，但短 talk 不必覆盖所有反对意见。
>
> **例句/例子**：A claim about all men and women needs qualification across class, race and context.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：评价具体 omitted condition。
>
