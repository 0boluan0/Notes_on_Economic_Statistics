# `The suggestion that X is important / limited because Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `The suggestion that X is important / limited because Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把作者的 reporting verb 变成可由你评价的“观点对象”，然后说明你自己的判断。
>
> **适用时机**：引用作者观点后，需要马上加入 writer voice，而不是停在 summary 时。
>
> **边界**：Y 必须解释“为什么重要/受限”；不能只换一个形容词而不给理由。
>
> **例句**：`The suggestion that media literacy can reduce climate misinformation is important because it shifts attention from correction to prevention.`
>
