# `Use notes as prompts, not a script.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Use notes as prompts, not a script.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：保持 eye contact、自然语速和 responsive delivery。
>
> **适用时机**：Paper C presentation 和 discussion。
>
> **边界**：关键词 notes 应足以提醒结构；逐字读会损害互动。
>
> **例句/例子**：Write three claim/evidence keywords and one conclusion cue.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：rehearse aloud, then reduce notes。
>
