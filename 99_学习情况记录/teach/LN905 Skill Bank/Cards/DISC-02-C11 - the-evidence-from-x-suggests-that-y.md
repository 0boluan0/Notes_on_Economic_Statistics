# `The evidence from X suggests that Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `The evidence from X suggests that Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把 source evidence 引入 discussion。
>
> **适用时机**：需要让观点不只依赖个人意见时。
>
> **边界**：必须准确归属并保留 claim strength。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The evidence from the longitudinal study suggests that average effects are small.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`According to the article...`。
>
