# `Introduction: context → thesis → approach.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Introduction: context → thesis → approach.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：用三步把读者从题目带到本文答案和路线。
>
> **适用时机**：Paper B 或 assignment 开头。
>
> **边界**：context 要服务题目，不能用空泛世界背景消耗字数。
>
> **例句/例子**：Context on demographic ageing → thesis on conditional fiscal effects → roadmap by labour, fertility and immigration.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：课程中的 three moves。
