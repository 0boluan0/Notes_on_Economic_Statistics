# `Acronyms often stress the final element; compounds often stress the first.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Acronyms often stress the final element; compounds often stress the first.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：提高多词单位的自然重音。
>
> **适用时机**：缩写和 compound noun 时。
>
> **边界**：不同固定表达可能不同；以常见用法为准。
>
> **例句/例子**：`the L-S-E` tends toward final prominence; `CLIMATE change` foregrounds the compound topic.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：先确认 expression 是否真是 compound。
