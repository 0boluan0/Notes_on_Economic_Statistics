# `clearly / substantial / definite / evidently / in fact`

> [!question] 正面
> `clearly / substantial / definite / evidently / in fact`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：在证据充分时 boost claim。
>
> **适用时机**：需要强调 robust evidence 或纠正误解时。
>
> **边界**：booster 会提高证明责任；不能用语气替代证据。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The repeated pattern clearly indicates a difference between the groups.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`unmistakable`; `plainly` 较强，谨慎使用。
>
