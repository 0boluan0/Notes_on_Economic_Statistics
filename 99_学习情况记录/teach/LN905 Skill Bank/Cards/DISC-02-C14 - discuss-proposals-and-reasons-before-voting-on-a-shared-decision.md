# `Discuss proposals and reasons before voting on a shared decision.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Discuss proposals and reasons before voting on a shared decision.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：让 group decision 建立在交流而非即时多数。
>
> **适用时机**：pyramid discussion、role play 或共同 recommendation 时。
>
> **边界**：每个人应有发言机会；决定要考虑 group-wide benefit。
>
> **例句/例子**：List the main proposals, test consequences, seek agreement, then decide.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：课程明确要求不立即投票。
