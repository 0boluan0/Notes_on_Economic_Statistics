# `X may contribute to Y, but the evidence does not establish that X causes Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `X may contribute to Y, but the evidence does not establish that X causes Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：同时表达可能作用与因果限制。
>
> **适用时机**：evidence 是 observational、anecdotal 或多因素结果时。
>
> **边界**：不能先用强因果动词再靠 hedge 修补。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Passive use may contribute to lower well-being, but the evidence does not establish that it causes the decline.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`is associated with`; `could be interpreted as`。
>
