# `A thesis must directly answer the question and be specific and focused.`

> [!question] 正面
> `A thesis must directly answer the question and be specific and focused.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：用课程标准检查 thesis 是否真的是答案。
>
> **适用时机**：写完 provisional thesis 后、开始 paragraph plan 前。
>
> **边界**：“This essay will discuss...”、只有 topic、或笼统写“有利有弊”都不是控制性答案。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Population ageing is manageable when immigration and labour-force policies are combined` is a thesis; `This essay discusses population ageing` is not.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：用反问检查：读者只看这句，能否知道你的结论与 scope？
