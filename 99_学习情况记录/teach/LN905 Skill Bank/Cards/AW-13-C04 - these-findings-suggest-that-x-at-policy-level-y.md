# `These findings suggest that X; at policy level, Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `These findings suggest that X; at policy level, Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：从综合结论进入 implication 或 recommendation。
>
> **适用时机**：结论中证据足以支持一个谨慎的实践含义时。
>
> **边界**：Y 的强度不能超过 findings；新政策细节若正文没讨论要避免。
>
> **例句/例子**：`These findings suggest that framing should be audience-sensitive; at policy level, communicators should test messages across groups.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：也可写 future research implication。
