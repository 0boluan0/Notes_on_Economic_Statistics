# `Use all three extracts, but not necessarily equally.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Use all three extracts, but not necessarily equally.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：满足 task 并按 relevance 分配篇幅。
>
> **适用时机**：planning evidence selection 时。
>
> **边界**：每份 source 都要有实质用途；不能 name-drop 一次就算使用。
>
> **例句/例子**：A highly relevant source may support two paragraphs, while a narrower source qualifies one claim.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：篇幅由 writer purpose 决定。
>
