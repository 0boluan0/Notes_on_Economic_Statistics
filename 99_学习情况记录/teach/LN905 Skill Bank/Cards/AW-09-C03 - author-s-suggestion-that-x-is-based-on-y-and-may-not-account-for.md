# `Author's suggestion that X is based on Y and may not account for Z.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Author's suggestion that X is based on Y and may not account for Z.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把 source proposition 变成句子主题，主句用于评价依据与限制。
>
> **适用时机**：需要同时准确归属 source 和表达 writer evaluation 时。
>
> **边界**：X、Y、Z 必须分别对应原 claim、evidence 和真实 omission。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The speaker's suggestion that population decline is necessarily harmful is based on aggregate growth measures and may not account for per-capita welfare.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`The argument that X rests on Y but overlooks Z.`。
>
