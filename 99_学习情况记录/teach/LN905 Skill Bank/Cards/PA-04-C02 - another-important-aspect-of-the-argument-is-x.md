# `Another important aspect of the argument is X.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Another important aspect of the argument is X.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：引入第二个 key point，并保持重要性层级。
>
> **适用时机**：central claim 后组织 supporting blocks 时。
>
> **边界**：不能用它堆 unrelated detail；X 必须推进 central argument。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Another important aspect is the effect of gender equality on work–family balance.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：可用更具体 relation 代替机械 `another`。
>
