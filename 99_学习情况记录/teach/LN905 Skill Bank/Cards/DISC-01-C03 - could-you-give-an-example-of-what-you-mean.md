# `Could you give an example of what you mean?`

> [!question] 正面
> `Could you give an example of what you mean?`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：让抽象 claim 变得可检验。
>
> **适用时机**：同伴观点太 general 时。
>
> **边界**：例子是澄清，不自动证明 general claim。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Could you give an example of how this frame might affect a particular audience?`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`Can you illustrate that point?`。
>
