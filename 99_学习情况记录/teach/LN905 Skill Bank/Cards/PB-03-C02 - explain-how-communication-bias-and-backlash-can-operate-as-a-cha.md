# `Explain how communication, bias and backlash can operate as a chain rather than listing them.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Explain how communication, bias and backlash can operate as a chain rather than listing them.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把来源连接成 mechanism sequence。
>
> **适用时机**：来源分别解释传播、维持和惩罚机制时。
>
> **边界**：顺序必须受材料支持，不能自创 causal chain。
>
> **例句/例子**：Communication transmits stereotypes; confirmation bias reinforces them; backlash discourages deviation.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：教师反馈明确要求 explain how sources work together。
