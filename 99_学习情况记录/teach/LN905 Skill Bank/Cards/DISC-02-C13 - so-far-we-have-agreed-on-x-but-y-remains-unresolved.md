# `So far we have agreed on X, but Y remains unresolved.`

> [!question] 正面
> `So far we have agreed on X, but Y remains unresolved.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：总结共识与剩余分歧，让小组继续推进。
>
> **适用时机**：转 topic、做共同决定或 discussion 临近结束时。
>
> **边界**：不能抹平不同意见；Y 要准确。
>
> **例句/例子**：`So far we have agreed that framing matters, but the most effective frame remains unresolved.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`Would you agree?`; `Right, let us move to...`。
