# `If I understand correctly, you are saying that X.`

> [!question] 正面
> `If I understand correctly, you are saying that X.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：先总结对方观点，再回应。
>
> **适用时机**：准备 disagree、build 或比较时。
>
> **边界**：要给对方修正机会；不能把 summary 当成宣布对方立场。
>
> **例句/例子**：`If I understand correctly, you are saying that immigration can delay, but not eliminate, fiscal pressure.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：后接 `Is that right?`。
>
