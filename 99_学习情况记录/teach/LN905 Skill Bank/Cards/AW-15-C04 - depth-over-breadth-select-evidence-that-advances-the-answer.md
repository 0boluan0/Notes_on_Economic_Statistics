# `Depth over breadth: select evidence that advances the answer.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Depth over breadth: select evidence that advances the answer.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：在有限字数中优先解释最有用的 evidence。
>
> **适用时机**：想把所有材料都塞进 essay 或 summary 时。
>
> **边界**：selection 不能遗漏 task-required key points；Paper B 仍须使用三份 extract。
>
> **例句/例子**：Two well-developed mechanisms are stronger than six named but unexplained factors.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：用 paragraph job 筛 evidence。
>
