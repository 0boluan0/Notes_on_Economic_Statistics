# `Distinguish source evidence from source opinion and from your inference.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Distinguish source evidence from source opinion and from your inference.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：保护 source fidelity 和 writer voice。
>
> **适用时机**：extract 有 cited research、author interpretation 或 quoted source 时。
>
> **边界**：不能把 source 引用的二手观点当作 source author 自己的 finding。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Author A cites Study B as evidence...`; your synthesis then states what follows.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：必要时使用 secondary citation。
>
