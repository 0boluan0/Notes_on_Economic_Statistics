# `However links propositions; punctuation must show its position.`

> [!question] 正面
> `However links propositions; punctuation must show its position.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：正确放置 conjunctive adverb。
>
> **适用时机**：句首或句中表达 concession/contrast 时。
>
> **边界**：不能用逗号把两个完整句硬连成 comma splice。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The evidence is suggestive. However, it is not conclusive.` / `The evidence, however, is not conclusive.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：分号形式：`The evidence is suggestive; however, it is not conclusive.`。
>
