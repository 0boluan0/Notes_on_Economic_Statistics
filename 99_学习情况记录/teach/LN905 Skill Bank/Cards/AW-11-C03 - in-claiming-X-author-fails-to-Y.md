# `In claiming that X, Author fails to Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `In claiming that X, Author fails to Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把被批评的具体 claim 与它缺失的分析动作直接连起来。
>
> **适用时机**：需要精准批评作者没有解释、区分、考虑或证明某一点时。
>
> **边界**：X 必须准确复述作者；Y 必须是能检验的缺口，不能只是笼统写 fails to be convincing。
>
> **例句**：`In claiming that population decline necessarily reduces prosperity, Author C fails to consider productivity growth.`
>
