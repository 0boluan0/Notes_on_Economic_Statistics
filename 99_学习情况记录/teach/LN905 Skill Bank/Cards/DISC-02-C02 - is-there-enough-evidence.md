# `Is there enough evidence to support that claim?`

> [!question] 正面
> `Is there enough evidence to support that claim?`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把讨论从立场冲突转回 evidence sufficiency。
>
> **适用时机**：对方 claim 可能超过 sample、scope、certainty 或 causality 时。
>
> **边界**：提出问题后要愿意听 evidence；不能把它当成自动否定任何观点的套话。
>
> **例句**：`Is there enough evidence to support the claim that social media causes lower well-being?`
> **可替换形式**：`Have you considered the possibility that ...?`
>
