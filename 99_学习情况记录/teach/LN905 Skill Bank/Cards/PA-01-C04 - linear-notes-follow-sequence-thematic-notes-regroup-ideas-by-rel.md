# `Linear notes follow sequence; thematic notes regroup ideas by relationship.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Linear notes follow sequence; thematic notes regroup ideas by relationship.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：根据 talk 结构选择笔记方式。
>
> **适用时机**：lecture 清楚线性推进或多个主题反复出现时。
>
> **边界**：不要边听边大规模重排而漏信息；可先线性记，写作前再 thematic map。
>
> **例句/例子**：A lecture that returns to work and family benefits may be regrouped under two themes after listening.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：Cornell margin 可放 main points/questions。
>
