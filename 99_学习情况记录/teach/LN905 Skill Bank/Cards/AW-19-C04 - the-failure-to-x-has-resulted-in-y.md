# `The failure to X has resulted in Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `The failure to X has resulted in Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把“没有采取行动”变成段落主题，并表达后果。
>
> **适用时机**：讨论 omission 或 policy inaction 时。
>
> **边界**：`resulted in` 是因果强词，必须有支持。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The failure to regulate targeted advertising has resulted in weak protection for younger users.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`A lack of X has contributed to Y.`。
>
