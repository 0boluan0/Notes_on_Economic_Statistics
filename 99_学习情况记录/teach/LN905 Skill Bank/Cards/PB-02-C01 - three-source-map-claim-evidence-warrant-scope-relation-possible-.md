# `Three-source map: claim, evidence, warrant, scope, relation, possible paragraph role.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Three-source map: claim, evidence, warrant, scope, relation, possible paragraph role.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把 extracts 转成 writer-usable evidence。
>
> **适用时机**：每读完一份 source 后。
>
> **边界**：不能只做 author summary；relation 可在三源都读完后补。
>
> **例句/例子**：Source A defines problem; B provides causal mechanism; C limits generalisability.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：所有 material proposition 保留 ownership。
