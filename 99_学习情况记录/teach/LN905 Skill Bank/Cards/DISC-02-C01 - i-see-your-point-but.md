# `I see your point, but X. / I'm not sure I completely agree.`

> [!question] 正面
> `I see your point, but X. / I'm not sure I completely agree.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：先承认对方有合理部分，再礼貌提出不同判断。
>
> **适用时机**：需要 disagreement，但希望保留 collaborative discussion 时。
>
> **边界**：but 后必须回应对方真实观点；礼貌开头不能掩盖误解或无证据反驳。
>
> **例句**：`I see your point, but migration may ease labour shortages without reversing population ageing.`
>
