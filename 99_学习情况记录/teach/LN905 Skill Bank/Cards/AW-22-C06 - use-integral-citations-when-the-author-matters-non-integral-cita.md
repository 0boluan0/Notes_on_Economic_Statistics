# `Use integral citations when the author matters; non-integral citations when the proposition matters.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Use integral citations when the author matters; non-integral citations when the proposition matters.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：让 citation form 配合 writer focus。
>
> **适用时机**：整合 literature 时。
>
> **边界**：不能为避免 author-led prose 而隐藏 source ownership。
>
> **例句/例子**：Integral: `Smith argues that X.` Non-integral: `X may vary by context (Smith, 2024).`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：交替使用，避免每句作者开头。
>
