# `A conclusion introduces no new evidence.`

> [!question] 正面
> `A conclusion introduces no new evidence.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：保持结尾只做 synthesis、answer 与 implication。
>
> **适用时机**：revision conclusion 时。
>
> **边界**：新的 citation、example 或 mechanism 应移到 body。
>
> **例句/例子**：If a new study appears only in the conclusion, the reader has no body analysis with which to evaluate it.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：问：这句话是在综合，还是在第一次证明？
