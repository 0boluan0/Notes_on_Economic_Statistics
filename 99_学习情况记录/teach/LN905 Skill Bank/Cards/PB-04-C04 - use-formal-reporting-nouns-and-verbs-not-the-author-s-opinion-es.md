# `Use formal reporting nouns and verbs, not “the author’s opinion/essay says”.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Use formal reporting nouns and verbs, not “the author’s opinion/essay says”.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：提高 source attribution 的准确性和 academic style。
>
> **适用时机**：introducing source claims 时。
>
> **边界**：先判断是 argues、suggests、finds、reports 还是 highlights。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Bloom and colleagues argue that...` rather than `Bloom’s opinion is...`。
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：教师反馈点名该问题。
>
