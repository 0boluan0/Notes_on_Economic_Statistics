# `Can a reader identify every pronoun, `this`, and source label without guessing?`

> [!question] 正面
> `Can a reader identify every pronoun, `this`, and source label without guessing?`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：检查 reference clarity。
>
> **适用时机**：final language pass 时。
>
> **边界**：模糊 reference 会同时破坏 cohesion 和 source ownership。
>
> **例句/例子**：Replace `This shows it is bad` with `This sampling limitation weakens the causal claim.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：优先用 `This/These/Such + precise noun`。
>
