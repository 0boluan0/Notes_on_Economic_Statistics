# `Rebuttal: counter-claim → fair concession → precise objection → own conclusion.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Rebuttal: counter-claim → fair concession → precise objection → own conclusion.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：针对一个常见主张建立反驳。
>
> **适用时机**：你不同意题目 premise 或 dominant view 时。
>
> **边界**：先准确呈现对方；不能只说 `I totally disagree`。
>
> **例句/例子**：Acknowledge that population decline can reduce labour supply, then challenge the assumption that GDP is the only welfare measure.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`There is some truth in X, but it overlooks Y.`。
