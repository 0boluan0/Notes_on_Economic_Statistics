# `arises from / derives from / stems from / results from`

> [!question] 正面
> `arises from / derives from / stems from / results from`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：表达问题、观点或结果的来源。
>
> **适用时机**：需要写 origin 或 causal source 时。
>
> **边界**：`results from` 因果较强；`derives from` 可指思想来源；不要混用成完全相同。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The disagreement stems from different assumptions about carrying capacity.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`has its origins in`; `is based on`; `is formed by`。
>
