# `Rebuttal: claim → counter-claim → rebuttal → restatement.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Rebuttal: claim → counter-claim → rebuttal → restatement.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：公平呈现反方后说明其为何不足。
>
> **适用时机**：题目包含强反对意见，或你需要反驳常见解释时。
>
> **边界**：counter-claim 必须准确而有力；不能制造 straw man。
>
> **例句/例子**：Present the claim that population decline necessarily harms growth, then test its assumptions and limits.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：可先部分承认：`Although elements of this argument are persuasive, ...`。
