# `It can be seen / argued / assumed that X.`

> [!question] 正面
> `It can be seen / argued / assumed that X.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：用 impersonal passive 谨慎提出观察或论点。
>
> **适用时机**：agent 不重要，且主句需要保持客观 focus 时。
>
> **边界**：会弱化 source ownership；有明确作者时通常直接引用更清楚。
>
> **例句/例子**：`It could be argued that demographic decline is not inherently harmful.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`It is widely believed/known`; `It is strongly recommended`。
>
