# `Summariser: state the group’s current answer and unresolved point.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Summariser: state the group’s current answer and unresolved point.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：防止讨论分散，并为下一步建立共享状态。
>
> **适用时机**：转主题、达成部分共识或结束时。
>
> **边界**：summary 不能加入新立场或抹掉 disagreement。
>
> **例句/例子**：`So far, we agree that stereotypes persist, but we differ on whether communication or institutions matter more.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：必要时问 `Would you agree?`。
>
