# `X takes a more qualified / causal / individual-level view than Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `X takes a more qualified / causal / individual-level view than Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：比较两个来源的 stance、claim strength 或 level of analysis。
>
> **适用时机**：差异不是简单赞成/反对时。
>
> **边界**：比较词必须有可观察依据。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Source B takes a more qualified view of population decline than Source A.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`X places greater emphasis on...`。
>
