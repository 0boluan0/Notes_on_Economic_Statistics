# `Use a precise verb instead of an empty verb + noun phrase.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Use a precise verb instead of an empty verb + noun phrase.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：让句子更短、更直接。
>
> **适用时机**：出现 `came to the conclusion`, `made a recommendation` 等时。
>
> **边界**：名词若是段落主题或需要修饰，仍可保留。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The researchers came to the conclusion that...` → `The researchers concluded that...`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`be in a position to recommend` → `recommend`。
>
