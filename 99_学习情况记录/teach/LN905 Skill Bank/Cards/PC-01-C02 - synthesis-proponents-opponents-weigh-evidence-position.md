# `Synthesis: proponents → opponents → weigh evidence → position.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Synthesis: proponents → opponents → weigh evidence → position.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：公平呈现两边后作判断。
>
> **适用时机**：题目有合理争议、需要 balance 时。
>
> **边界**：不能把最后 position 省掉；双方要准确而非稻草人。
>
> **例句/例子**：Compare arguments for and against fertility incentives, then state which conditions matter most.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`On balance...`; `Having weighed the merits...`。
