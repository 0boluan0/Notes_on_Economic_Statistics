# `What do you think? / Have you any views on X?`

> [!question] 正面
> `What do you think? / Have you any views on X?`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：邀请他人加入并保持讨论互动。
>
> **适用时机**：表达完自己的 point 后或有人尚未参与时。
>
> **边界**：问题要开放且与当前 point 有关，不能用来把对方考倒。
>
> **例句/例子**：`We have discussed regulation; what do you think about platform design?`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`How do you see this?`; `What is your opinion?`。
>
