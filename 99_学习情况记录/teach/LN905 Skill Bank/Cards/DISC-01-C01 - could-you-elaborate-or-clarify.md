# `Could you elaborate on that point? / Could you clarify what you mean by X?`

> [!question] 正面
> `Could you elaborate on that point? / Could you clarify what you mean by X?`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：请对方补充解释或澄清一个模糊概念。
>
> **适用时机**：你听到了观点，但无法准确回应它的 meaning、scope 或 evidence 时。
>
> **边界**：要点名真正不清楚的部分；不要用澄清问题拖延或暗中改变对方观点。
>
> **例句**：`Could you clarify what you mean by effective climate policy?`
>
