# `Content words are usually stressed; function words are usually reduced.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Content words are usually stressed; function words are usually reduced.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：建立句子节奏和信息焦点。
>
> **适用时机**：自然语速表达 academic point 时。
>
> **边界**：function word 在 contrast、回答或特别强调时可以重读。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The STUDY found a SMALL EFFECT in SOME GROUPS.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：articles/prepositions/auxiliaries 通常弱读。
