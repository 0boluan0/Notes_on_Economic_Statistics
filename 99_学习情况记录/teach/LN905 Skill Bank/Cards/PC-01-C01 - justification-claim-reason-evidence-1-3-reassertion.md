# `Justification: claim → reason/evidence 1–3 → reassertion.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Justification: claim → reason/evidence 1–3 → reassertion.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：在 1–2 分钟内清楚支持一个立场。
>
> **适用时机**：Paper C presentation 题适合单一主张时。
>
> **边界**：理由必须不同且有证据；避免三个同义理由。
>
> **例句/例子**：State why climate communication should use multiple frames, support with audience evidence, then restate conditionally.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`I would argue... because... For example... Therefore...`。
>
