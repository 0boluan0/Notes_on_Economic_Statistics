# `X is consistent with Y, but does not prove it.`

> [!question] 正面
> `X is consistent with Y, but does not prove it.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：表达 evidence 与 explanation 相符但非唯一证明。
>
> **适用时机**：存在 alternative explanations 时。
>
> **边界**：不能把 `consistent with` 写成 `confirms`。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The pattern is consistent with a social-comparison mechanism, but does not prove it.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`supports, but does not establish`。
