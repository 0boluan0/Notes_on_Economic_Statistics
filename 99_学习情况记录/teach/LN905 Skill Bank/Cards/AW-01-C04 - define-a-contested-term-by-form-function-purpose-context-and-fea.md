# `Define a contested term by form, function, purpose, context and features.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Define a contested term by form, function, purpose, context and features.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：给题目中的可争议概念一个可用于选材的 operational definition。
>
> **适用时机**：题目中的词没有唯一边界，例如 social media、privacy 或 policy effectiveness。
>
> **边界**：定义必须服务本题；不能只是抄词典，也不能宽到任何例子都能算。
>
> **例句/例子**：Social media can be defined by its digital form, interaction function, communication purpose, user context and networked features.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：不必五项全写进正文，但要用它们检查边界。
