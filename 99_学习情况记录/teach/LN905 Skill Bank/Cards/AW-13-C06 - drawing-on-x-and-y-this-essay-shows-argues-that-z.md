# `Drawing on X and Y, this essay shows / argues that Z.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Drawing on X and Y, this essay shows / argues that Z.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：在 introduction 或 conclusion 中概括证据基础与控制性判断。
>
> **适用时机**：需要说明论文依赖哪些类型材料时。
>
> **边界**：不能把 sources 列成 bibliography；X/Y 应是 evidence domains 或明确 literature。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Drawing on demographic and labour-market research, this essay argues that ageing does not produce uniform fiscal outcomes.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：过去作业总结可用 `it was shown that`，一般 essay 用现在时更自然。
>
