# `This suggests that some X may Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `This suggests that some X may Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：用 suggest + some + may 同时限制证据力度、population scope 与 certainty。
>
> **适用时机**：小样本、局部数据或初步 finding 只能支持谨慎结论时。
>
> **边界**：不能删掉 some/may 后扩大成全体或确定事实；也不要在强证据上机械堆满 hedge。
>
> **例句**：`This suggests that some young adults may experience stronger social pressure through image-based platforms.`
>
