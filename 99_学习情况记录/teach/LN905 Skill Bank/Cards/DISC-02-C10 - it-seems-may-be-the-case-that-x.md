# `It seems / may be the case that X.`

> [!question] 正面
> `It seems / may be the case that X.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：在口头讨论中 hedge 不确定 claim。
>
> **适用时机**：evidence 有限或你在提出 interpretation 时。
>
> **边界**：不能用 hedge 逃避给 reason；仍要说明依据。
>
> **例句/例子**：`It may be the case that message fatigue affects highly exposed audiences more strongly.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`to some extent`; `perhaps`; `I would suggest`。
>
