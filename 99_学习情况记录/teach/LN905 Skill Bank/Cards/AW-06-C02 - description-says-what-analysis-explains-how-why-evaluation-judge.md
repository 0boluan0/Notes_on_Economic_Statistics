# `Description says what; analysis explains how/why; evaluation judges strength or significance.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Description says what; analysis explains how/why; evaluation judges strength or significance.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把三种写作动作分开，并知道下一句该做什么。
>
> **适用时机**：引用来源后不知道如何继续时。
>
> **边界**：analysis 不能只换词复述，evaluation 不能只说“good/bad”。
>
> **例句/例子**：Description: a study found X. Analysis: X may operate through Y. Evaluation: the design cannot establish that Y caused X.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：一段可按 description → analysis → evaluation 推进。
>
