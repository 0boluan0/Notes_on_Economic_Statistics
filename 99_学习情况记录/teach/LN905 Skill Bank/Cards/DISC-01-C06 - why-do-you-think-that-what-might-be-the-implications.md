# `Why do you think that? / What might be the implications?`

> [!question] 正面
> `Why do you think that? / What might be the implications?`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：用 follow-up 推进 depth。
>
> **适用时机**：同伴已有 claim 但缺 reason 或 significance 时。
>
> **边界**：不要连发问题而不回应内容。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Why do you think negative framing would be more effective for this audience?`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`How does that relate to...?`。
>
