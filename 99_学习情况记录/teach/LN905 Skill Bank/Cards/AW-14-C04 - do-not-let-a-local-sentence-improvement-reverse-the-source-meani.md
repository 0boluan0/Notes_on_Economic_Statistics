# `Do not let a local sentence improvement reverse the source meaning.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Do not let a local sentence improvement reverse the source meaning.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：在语言修改中保持 epistemic fidelity。
>
> **适用时机**：替换 reporting verb、hedge 或 causal verb 时。
>
> **边界**：更“高级”的词不一定更准确。
>
> **例句/例子**：Changing `is associated with` to `causes` improves neither accuracy nor style.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：每次改动检查 ownership、certainty、scope、causality。
