# `Quote when the exact wording is definitional, precise or uniquely significant.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Quote when the exact wording is definitional, precise or uniquely significant.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：决定何时 direct quotation 比 paraphrase 更合适。
>
> **适用时机**：原词本身是分析对象或无法无损改写时。
>
> **边界**：不能因懒得 paraphrase 而大量 quotation；Paper A/B 重在 own words。
>
> **例句/例子**：Quote a contested definition, then explain how it affects the essay’s scope.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：quotation 后仍需 writer commentary。
>
