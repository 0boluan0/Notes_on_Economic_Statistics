# `Leader: open the task, keep the group focused and invite contributions.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Leader: open the task, keep the group focused and invite contributions.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：协调 academic interaction，而非做 mini-presentation。
>
> **适用时机**：被分配 Leader 或 group 缺少结构时。
>
> **边界**：Leader 不垄断内容，不替别人总结所有观点。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Right, our task is to compare the explanations. Which one should we start with?`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：使用 open questions。
>
