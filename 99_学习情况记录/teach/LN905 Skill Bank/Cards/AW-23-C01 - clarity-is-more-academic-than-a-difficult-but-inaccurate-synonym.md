# `Clarity is more academic than a difficult but inaccurate synonym.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Clarity is more academic than a difficult but inaccurate synonym.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把 academic style 建立在准确和可读性上。
>
> **适用时机**：想用 thesaurus 抬高语言时。
>
> **边界**：简单词只要精确就可以；口语化、模糊词仍需替换。
>
> **例句/例子**：Use `shows a difference` rather than an obscure verb that reverses the relation.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：准确 → 清楚 → 简洁 → variation。
>
