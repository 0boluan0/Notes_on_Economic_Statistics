# `A credible author is not automatically relevant evidence for this claim.`

> [!question] 正面
> `A credible author is not automatically relevant evidence for this claim.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：分开作者权威与证据适配度。
>
> **适用时机**：想用“作者是专家”直接证明观点时。
>
> **边界**：authority 不能替代 method、sample、scope 或 question fit。
>
> **例句/例子**：A climate economist may be authoritative, but a speech alone may not establish policy effectiveness.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：写成 `Author is credible on X; however, this evidence is limited for Y because...`。
>
