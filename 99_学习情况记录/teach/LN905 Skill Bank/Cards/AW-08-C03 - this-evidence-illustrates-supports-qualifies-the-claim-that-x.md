# `This evidence illustrates / supports / qualifies the claim that X.`

> [!question] 正面
> `This evidence illustrates / supports / qualifies the claim that X.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：在引用后重新接管段落声音。
>
> **适用时机**：source sentence 后需要说明它对本段 claim 的作用时。
>
> **边界**：动词强度要匹配：illustrates 是例证，supports 是支持，qualifies 是限制。
>
> **例句/例子**：`This evidence qualifies the claim that social media use is uniformly harmful.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`This finding is important because...`。
>
