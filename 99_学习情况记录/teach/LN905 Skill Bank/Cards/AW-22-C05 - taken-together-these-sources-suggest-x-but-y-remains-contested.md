# `Taken together, these sources suggest X, but Y remains contested.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Taken together, these sources suggest X, but Y remains contested.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：先综合共同结论，再保留未解决争议。
>
> **适用时机**：多个来源部分一致、部分冲突时。
>
> **边界**：X/Y 必须对应真实 evidence；不能用 `taken together` 掩盖矛盾。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Taken together, the sources suggest that framing matters, but the most effective frame remains contested.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`Collectively, the evidence indicates..., although...`。
>
