# `Taken together, A's evidence about X, B's challenge to Y, and C's distinction between Z and W suggest Q.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Taken together, A's evidence about X, B's challenge to Y, and C's distinction between Z and W suggest Q.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：先给三篇来源分配不同角色，再由 writer voice 写出 Q；不预设三篇已经共同说过同一句话。
>
> **适用时机**：三篇 extracts 分别提供 evidence、challenge、qualification、alternative 或 distinction，需要综合成自己的判断时。
>
> **边界**：source-role 动词必须逐篇成立；Q 是写作者从关系中得到的 inference，不能冒充三位作者的共同原话。
>
> **四主题例句**：`Taken together, evidence that misinformation weakens public understanding, the challenge posed by worldview conflict, and the distinction between debunking and pre-emptive inoculation suggest that climate communication requires more than factual correction alone.`
>
> **只有真实共享命题时才用的变体**：`Taken together, the sources suggest X, but Y remains unresolved.`；写不出每篇都支持的 X 时，不用这个变体。
>
