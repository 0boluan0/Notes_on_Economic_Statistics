# `Read each extract through the essay question, not for complete content coverage.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Read each extract through the essay question, not for complete content coverage.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：只提取能回答 task 的 propositions。
>
> **适用时机**：Paper B 30 分钟 reading 阶段。
>
> **边界**：不能因此漏掉改变 source claim 的 qualification 或 counter-evidence。
>
> **例句/例子**：For a question on policy effectiveness, prioritise mechanism, evidence, limitations and outcomes.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：给题目 subparts 建列。
>
