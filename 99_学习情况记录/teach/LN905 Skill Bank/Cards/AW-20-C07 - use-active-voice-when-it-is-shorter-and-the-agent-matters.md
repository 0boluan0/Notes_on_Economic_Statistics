# `Use active voice when it is shorter and the agent matters.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Use active voice when it is shorter and the agent matters.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：在 concise writing 中避免不必要被动。
>
> **适用时机**：句子有清楚 agent，passive 只让句子更长时。
>
> **边界**：若 focus 是 process/result，passive 仍可能更合适。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The policy was evaluated by the researchers` → `The researchers evaluated the policy.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：信息焦点优先于“一律 active”。
>
