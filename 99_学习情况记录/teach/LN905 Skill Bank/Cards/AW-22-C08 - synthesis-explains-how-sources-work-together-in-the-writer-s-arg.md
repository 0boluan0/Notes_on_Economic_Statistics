# `Synthesis explains how sources work together in the writer’s argument.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Synthesis explains how sources work together in the writer’s argument.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：区别 synthesis 与三个 summary 并排。
>
> **适用时机**：Paper B 或 literature paragraph 规划时。
>
> **边界**：至少要说出 similarity、difference、complementarity、sequence 或 tension。
>
> **例句/例子**：One source identifies a mechanism, another tests it, and a third limits its scope.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：先写 relation，再选句型。
>
