# `How many parts does the question contain, and are they equally important?`

> [!question] 正面
> `How many parts does the question contain, and are they equally important?`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：拆出题目的所有 sub-questions，并决定篇幅权重。
>
> **适用时机**：题目有 and、why、to what extent、implications 等多个要求时。
>
> **边界**：不能完成第一问后把第二问塞进 conclusion；权重应由题目中心而非词数平均决定。
>
> **例句/例子**：A definition may need one short section, while the evaluative part needs most of the essay.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`Part A → Part B → relationship between A and B`。
