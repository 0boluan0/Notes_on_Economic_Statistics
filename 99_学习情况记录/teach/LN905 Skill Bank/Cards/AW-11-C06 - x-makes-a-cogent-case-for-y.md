# `X makes a cogent case for Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `X makes a cogent case for Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：评价一套 reasoning 整体连贯且有说服力。
>
> **适用时机**：来源不仅有数据，还把 evidence 与 conclusion 合理连接时。
>
> **边界**：必须能指出使其 cogent 的 evidence 或 warrant。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The lecture makes a cogent case for audience-sensitive climate communication by linking framing effects to empirical studies.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`X offers a persuasive account of Y.`。
>
