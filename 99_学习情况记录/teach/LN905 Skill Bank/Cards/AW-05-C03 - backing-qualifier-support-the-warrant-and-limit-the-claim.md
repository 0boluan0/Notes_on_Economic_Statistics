# `Backing + qualifier: support the warrant and limit the claim.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Backing + qualifier: support the warrant and limit the claim.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：增强推理依据，同时校准 certainty 与 scope。
>
> **适用时机**：warrant 有争议或 evidence 只支持条件性结论时。
>
> **边界**：qualifier 不是装饰性 `maybe`；要对应真实不确定性。
>
> **例句/例子**：Comparable trends in untreated regions provide backing; `may` limits the causal claim.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`in this sample / under these conditions / to some extent`。
>
