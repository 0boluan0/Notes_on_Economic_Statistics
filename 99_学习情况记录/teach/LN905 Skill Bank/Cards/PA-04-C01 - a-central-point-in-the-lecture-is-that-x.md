# `A central point in the lecture is that X.`

> [!question] 正面
> `A central point in the lecture is that X.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：开头准确呈现 central claim。
>
> **适用时机**：critical summary 第一段或第一句。
>
> **边界**：X 应是 whole-talk message，而不是最有趣的 detail。
>
> **例句/例子**：`A central point in the lecture is that gender equality can benefit men as well as women.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`The speaker’s central argument is that...`。
>
