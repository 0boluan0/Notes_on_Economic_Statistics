# `This finding supports previous research by Author, who found that X.`

> [!question] 正面
> `This finding supports previous research by Author, who found that X.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：说明两项研究在同一命题上相互支持，而不是把它们分别罗列。
>
> **适用时机**：两个来源的 findings 方向一致，需要建立 synthesis 时。
>
> **边界**：只有真正支持同一 claim 才能用 supports；如果只是相关，应写 complements、is consistent with 或分别说明差异。
>
> **例句**：`This finding supports previous research by Author B, who found that gender stereotypes remained stable across age groups.`
>
