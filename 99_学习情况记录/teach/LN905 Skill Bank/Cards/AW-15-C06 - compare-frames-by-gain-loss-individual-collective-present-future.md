# `Compare frames by gain/loss, individual/collective, present/future and local/global focus.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Compare frames by gain/loss, individual/collective, present/future and local/global focus.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：用明确维度比较 message framing。
>
> **适用时机**：Climate Change 或其他四大话题的 source comparison 时。
>
> **边界**：frame effectiveness 依 audience/context；不能假设正面或负面 frame 永远更有效。
>
> **例句/例子**：A future global catastrophe frame may motivate one audience but create distance or fatigue in another.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：可组合多个 frame，并评价 message fatigue。
>
