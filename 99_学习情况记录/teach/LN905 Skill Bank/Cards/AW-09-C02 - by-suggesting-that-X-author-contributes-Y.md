# `By suggesting that X, Author makes / contributes Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `By suggesting that X, Author makes / contributes Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：先压缩作者的观点，再说明该观点对你的讨论或研究有什么贡献。
>
> **适用时机**：需要把 source summary 与 relevance 连在同一句里时。
>
> **边界**：贡献必须来自该观点本身；不能凭空写成 key contribution，也不能把有限发现吹成突破。
>
> **例句**：`By suggesting that care burdens shape women’s employment choices, Author A contributes to explanations of the gender pay gap.`
> **可替换形式**：`By arguing that X, Author highlights Y.`
>
