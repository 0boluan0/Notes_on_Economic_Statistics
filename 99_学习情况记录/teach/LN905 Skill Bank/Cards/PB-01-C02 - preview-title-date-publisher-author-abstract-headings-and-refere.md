# `Preview title, date, publisher, author, abstract, headings and references before close reading.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Preview title, date, publisher, author, abstract, headings and references before close reading.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：快速判断 source purpose、structure、authority 和 relevance。
>
> **适用时机**：第一次打开 academic extract 时。
>
> **边界**：preview 不能替代正文理解；标题/abstract 是预测。
>
> **例句/例子**：A policy report and a peer-reviewed study may offer different evidence roles.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：先 survey，再形成 questions。
>
