# `Chunk speech by meaning, not by every written word.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Chunk speech by meaning, not by every written word.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：让复杂学术句子可听懂。
>
> **适用时机**：presentation 或 discussion 中有从句、list、contrast 时。
>
> **边界**：停顿位置不能拆开紧密 noun phrase 或 verb-object relation。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Although the average effect is small / some users may face substantial harm.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：chunk boundary 通常对应 meaning unit。
>
