# `X appears reasonable; however, Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `X appears reasonable; however, Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：先认可合理部分，再精确指出限制。
>
> **适用时机**：source 不是全对或全错时。
>
> **边界**：Y 应限制 claim 的一部分，不应在后半句无证据地完全推翻 X。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The proposed mechanism appears reasonable; however, the study does not test it directly.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`Although X is persuasive, it remains limited by Y.`。
>
