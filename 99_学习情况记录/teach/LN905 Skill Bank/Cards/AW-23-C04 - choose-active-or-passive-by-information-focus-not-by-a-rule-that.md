# `Choose active or passive by information focus, not by a rule that academic writing must be passive.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Choose active or passive by information focus, not by a rule that academic writing must be passive.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：避免把“正式”误解为被动堆积。
>
> **适用时机**：每次改写 voice 时。
>
> **边界**：source ownership 或 responsibility 重要时保留 agent。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Researchers found...` and `A difference was found...` foreground different information.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：先问该句的 topic 是谁/什么。
>
