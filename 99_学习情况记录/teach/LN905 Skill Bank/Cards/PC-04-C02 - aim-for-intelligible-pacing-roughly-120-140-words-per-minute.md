# `Aim for intelligible pacing, roughly 120–140 words per minute.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Aim for intelligible pacing, roughly 120–140 words per minute.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：避免过快导致吞音、过慢导致失去连贯。
>
> **适用时机**：两分钟 presentation rehearsal。
>
> **边界**：数字是参考，不是硬门槛；清楚和自然优先。
>
> **例句/例子**：A 240–280 word script is already near the upper range for two minutes.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：用 purposeful pauses 替代 fillers。
>
