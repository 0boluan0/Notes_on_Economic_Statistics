# `Plan sections as topic sentences plus supporting detail.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Plan sections as topic sentences plus supporting detail.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把抽象 outline 变成可直接写作的 paragraph plan。
>
> **适用时机**：thesis 和宏观结构确定后。
>
> **边界**：不要用只有名词的 headings，如 `Advantages`; 要写可争辩的句子。
>
> **例句/例子**：Topic sentence: `Immigration can ease labour shortages, but its fiscal effect depends on migrants’ employment profiles.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：每条 supporting detail 标 evidence role。
>
