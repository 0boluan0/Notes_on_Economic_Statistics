# `Narrow the scope until the claim can be supported in the available words.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Narrow the scope until the claim can be supported in the available words.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把过大的题目变成可在规定字数内证明的答案。
>
> **适用时机**：材料很多、每个方向都想写时。
>
> **边界**：缩小 scope 不是回避题目；必须保留题目核心关系。
>
> **例句/例子**：Focus on two mechanisms with the strongest evidence rather than naming six possible causes.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`This essay focuses on X and Y because...`。
