# `On the basis of X, it may / might / could be argued that Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `On the basis of X, it may / might / could be argued that Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：先点明依据，再以谨慎力度提出 writer claim。
>
> **适用时机**：需要从若干 finding 综合推出解释或判断，但证据还不能支持强断言时。
>
> **边界**：X 必须足以支撑 Y；被动结构不能用来隐藏一个需要明确归属的来源主张。
>
> **例句**：`On the basis of these demographic projections, it could be argued that care policy requires earlier reform.`
>
