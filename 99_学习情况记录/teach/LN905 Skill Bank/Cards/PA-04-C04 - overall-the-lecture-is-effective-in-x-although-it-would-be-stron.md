# `Overall, the lecture is effective in X, although it would be stronger if Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Overall, the lecture is effective in X, although it would be stronger if Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：用平衡评价结束 critical summary。
>
> **适用时机**：已经总结主要 claim/evidence 后。
>
> **边界**：Y 必须是能真正增强 argument 的缺口，不是要求 speaker 讨论所有事情。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Overall, the lecture is effective in reframing equality as mutually beneficial, although it would be stronger if the research evidence were explained in more detail.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：避免只写 `I agree`。
