# `Choose `noun + of + noun` or a noun compound by meaning and readability.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Choose `noun + of + noun` or a noun compound by meaning and readability.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：控制 nominalisation 后的 noun phrase 关系。
>
> **适用时机**：需要压缩 action/property 成名词时。
>
> **边界**：多个名词连堆会产生歧义；清晰优先于短。
>
> **例句/例子**：`the failure of governments to act` may be clearer than `government action failure`。
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：固定搭配可用 `climate risk communication`。
>
