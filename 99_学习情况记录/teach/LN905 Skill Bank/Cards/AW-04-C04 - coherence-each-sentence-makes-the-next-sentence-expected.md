# `Coherence: each sentence makes the next sentence expected.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Coherence: each sentence makes the next sentence expected.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：建立句间可追踪的逻辑发展。
>
> **适用时机**：句子都相关但读起来像列表时。
>
> **边界**：连接词不能替代真实关系。
>
> **例句/例子**：A claim introduces a mechanism; the next sentence explains it; evidence then tests it; commentary states the implication.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：用 given information → new information 顺序。
>
