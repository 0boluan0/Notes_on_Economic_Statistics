# `Mark whether sources agree, complement, sequence, qualify or contradict one another.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Mark whether sources agree, complement, sequence, qualify or contradict one another.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：为 synthesis 预先保存 source relations。
>
> **适用时机**：三份 source 初步理解后。
>
> **边界**：不能把不同 level/method 当直接 contradiction。
>
> **例句/例子**：One source may explain a mechanism while another gives outcome data: complementary, not necessarily agreement.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：relation 必须写成 proposition-level。
