# `Avoid idioms, clichés, contractions and slang in assessed academic writing.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Avoid idioms, clichés, contractions and slang in assessed academic writing.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：保持正式、可跨文化理解的 register。
>
> **适用时机**：Paper A/B/assignment revision 时。
>
> **边界**：Paper C discussion 可更口语；语域规则随 output type 变化。
>
> **例句/例子**：Write `many factors` rather than `a whole bunch of things`; write `does not` rather than `doesn’t`.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：避免 `in today’s society` 等空泛 cliché。
