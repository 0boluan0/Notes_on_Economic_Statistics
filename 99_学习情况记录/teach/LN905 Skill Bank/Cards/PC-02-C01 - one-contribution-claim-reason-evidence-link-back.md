# `One contribution = claim → reason/evidence → link back.`

> [!question] 正面
> `One contribution = claim → reason/evidence → link back.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：让 discussion 发言完整而不冗长。
>
> **适用时机**：回答 general question 或加入讨论时。
>
> **边界**：不要变成 mini-presentation；通常 30–60 秒并给别人空间。
>
> **例句/例子**：State a view on climate framing, cite one reason, then link it to the group’s question.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：必要时先 build on previous speaker。
>
