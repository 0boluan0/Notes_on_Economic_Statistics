# `While A [reports/shows] X, B [argues/qualifies] Y.`

> [!question] 正面
> `While A [reports/shows] X, B [argues/qualifies] Y.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：不仅比较两篇来源的内容，还把它们不同的 source role 写清楚。
>
> **适用时机**：两篇来源处理同一主题，但一篇提供例证／转述观点，另一篇提出主张／限定时。
>
> **边界**：先判断每篇到底是 `reports / shows / argues / qualifies / challenges`；不能用 `both mention` 抹平作者身份和论证动作。
>
> **四主题例句**：`While one social-media extract shows that curated self-presentation can trigger harmful comparison, another qualifies broad concern by arguing that outcomes depend on how people use social media.`
>
> **source-owner 变体**：`While A reports that critics regard X as Y, B argues that Z.`；先检查 [[99_学习情况记录/teach/LN905 Skill Bank/Cards/LEX-01-C10 - claim-argue-report|claim ≠ argue ≠ report]]。
>
