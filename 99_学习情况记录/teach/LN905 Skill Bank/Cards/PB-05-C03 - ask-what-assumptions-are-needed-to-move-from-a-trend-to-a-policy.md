# `Ask what assumptions are needed to move from a trend to a policy implication.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Ask what assumptions are needed to move from a trend to a policy implication.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：让图表 evidence 进入 argument 而不越界。
>
> **适用时机**：数据支持 policy paragraph 时。
>
> **边界**：trend 不自动决定原因、future projection 或最佳 policy。
>
> **例句/例子**：An ageing trend implies fiscal pressure only under assumptions about employment, health and policy design.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：用 Toulmin warrant 检查。
>
