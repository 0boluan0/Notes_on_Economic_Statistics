# `Governments should respond to X through A and B, while C should play only a supporting role.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Governments should respond to X through A and B, while C should play only a supporting role.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：直接回答 `How should governments react/respond?`，同时给措施排序，而不是罗列所有可能政策。
>
> **适用时机**：政策建议题要求提出并权衡多项 response 时。
>
> **边界**：A/B/C 都必须有来源支持；`supporting role` 不能把条件性政策绝对写成无效。
>
> **例句**：`Governments should respond to population ageing through longer working lives and investment in younger generations, while immigration should play only a supporting role.`
>
