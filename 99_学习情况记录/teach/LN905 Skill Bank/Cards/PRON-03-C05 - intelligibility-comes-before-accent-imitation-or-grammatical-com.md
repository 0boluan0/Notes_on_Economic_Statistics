# `Intelligibility comes before accent imitation or grammatical complexity.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Intelligibility comes before accent imitation or grammatical complexity.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把练习优先级放在听众能否准确理解。
>
> **适用时机**：Paper C 准备与反馈时。
>
> **边界**：不是忽略 grammar，而是不让复杂句导致含糊、过快或吞音。
>
> **例句/例子**：A clear qualified sentence is better than a long sentence the listener cannot parse.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：关键词、节奏、音量、pause 优先。
>
