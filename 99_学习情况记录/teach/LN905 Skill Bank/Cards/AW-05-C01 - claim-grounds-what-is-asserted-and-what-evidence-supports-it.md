# `Claim + grounds: what is asserted, and what evidence supports it?`

> [!question] 正面
> `Claim + grounds: what is asserted, and what evidence supports it?`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：区分结论与证据，防止把两者混写。
>
> **适用时机**：分析自己或来源的 argument 时。
>
> **边界**：grounds 相关不代表足够；claim 的强度不能超过 grounds。
>
> **例句/例子**：Claim: a policy is effective. Grounds: emissions declined after implementation.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：先标 C/G，再判断是否还缺 warrant。
>
