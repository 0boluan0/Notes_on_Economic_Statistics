# `prove → demonstrate/show → indicate/suggest → may be argued`

> [!question] 正面
> `prove → demonstrate/show → indicate/suggest → may be argued`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：按 evidence strength 选择 claim verb。
>
> **适用时机**：从 finding 形成 claim 时。
>
> **边界**：`prove` 通常不适合社会科学材料；不确定性越高，动词越弱。
>
> **例句/例子**：A controlled repeated experiment may demonstrate an effect; a cross-sectional survey may only suggest an association.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：课件的 claim-strength ladder。
>
