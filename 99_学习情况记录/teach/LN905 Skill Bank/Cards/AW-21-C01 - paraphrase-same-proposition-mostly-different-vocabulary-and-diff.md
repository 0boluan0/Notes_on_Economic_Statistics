# `Paraphrase = same proposition, mostly different vocabulary and different syntax.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Paraphrase = same proposition, mostly different vocabulary and different syntax.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：定义合格 paraphrase 的最低标准。
>
> **适用时机**：用 source idea 写进 Paper B/assignment 时。
>
> **边界**：technical terms 可保留；只换同义词或词形不够。
>
> **例句/例子**：Change both the clause structure and wording while preserving ownership, certainty, scope and causality.
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：先理解后离开原句，用自己的信息顺序重写。
