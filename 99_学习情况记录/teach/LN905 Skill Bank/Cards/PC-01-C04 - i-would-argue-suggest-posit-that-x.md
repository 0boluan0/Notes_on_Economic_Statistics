# `I would argue / suggest / posit that X.`

> [!question] 正面
> `I would argue / suggest / posit that X.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：用适当强度提出 presentation claim。
>
> **适用时机**：开场 10–15 秒内。
>
> **边界**：`posit` 较正式且强；claim 强度必须匹配 evidence。
>
> **例句/例子**：`I would argue that social-media regulation should target design features rather than screen time alone.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：`From my perspective`; `I advocate...`; `I am in favour of...`。
>
