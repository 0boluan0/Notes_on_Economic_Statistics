# `Use passive voice in methods when the process matters more than the researcher.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Use passive voice in methods when the process matters more than the researcher.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：把注意力放在 procedure、sample 或 result。
>
> **适用时机**：报告研究设计时。
>
> **边界**：不是所有 academic writing 都应被动；过度使用会冗长和模糊。
>
> **例句/例子**：`Participants were interviewed twice over six months.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：若 agent 影响可信度，保留 `by...` 或改 active。
>
