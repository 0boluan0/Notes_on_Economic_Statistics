# `Do not record a contentious example as an established fact.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Do not record a contentious example as an established fact.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：在笔记阶段保留 source status 和 certainty。
>
> **适用时机**：speaker 转述个人经历、quotation 或反方观点时。
>
> **边界**：笔记缩写也不能丢 ownership；否则成文时容易误归属。
>
> **例句/例子**：Note `participant claimed...` rather than `women all experience...`。
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：可用 `ex`, `quote`, `research`, `counter` 标签。
>
