# `Two-syllable nouns/adjectives often stress the first syllable; verbs often stress the second.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Two-syllable nouns/adjectives often stress the first syllable; verbs often stress the second.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：用常见模式预测 word stress。
>
> **适用时机**：遇到 record/present 等词类变化时。
>
> **边界**：这是倾向而非无例外规则；词典音标优先。
>
> **例句/例子**：`REcord` (noun) versus `reCORD` (verb).
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：用句子练词类与重音一起变化。
>
