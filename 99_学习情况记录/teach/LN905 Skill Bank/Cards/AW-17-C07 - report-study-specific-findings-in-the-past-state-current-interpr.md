# `Report study-specific findings in the past; state current interpretations or general claims in the present.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Report study-specific findings in the past; state current interpretations or general claims in the present.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：用时态区分研究发生过什么与现在如何解释。
>
> **适用时机**：写 literature evidence 时。
>
> **边界**：不是机械规则；原文的时间范围和已改变事实仍需判断。
>
> **例句/例子**：`The study found a small association. This pattern suggests that context matters.`
>
> **可替换形式或操作提示**：research procedure 常用过去时，writer claim 常用现在时。
>
