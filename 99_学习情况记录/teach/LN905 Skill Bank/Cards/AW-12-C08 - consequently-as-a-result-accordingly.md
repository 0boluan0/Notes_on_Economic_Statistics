# `Consequently / As a result / Accordingly, X.`

> [!question] 正面
> `Consequently / As a result / Accordingly, X.`

> [!answer]- 反面
> **功能**：标出前文 evidence 或 reasoning 导出的结果、行动或下一层判断。
>
> **适用时机**：reader 需要看见“前一句怎样推出后一句”时。
>
> **边界**：连接词不能替代真实因果或推理；如果前文只相关，不要用它制造因果。
>
> **例句**：`Current projections remain uncertain. Accordingly, policy should be tested across several demographic scenarios.`
>
