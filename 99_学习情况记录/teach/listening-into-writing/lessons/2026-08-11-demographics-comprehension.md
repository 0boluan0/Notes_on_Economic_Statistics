# Demographics listening comprehension

<!-- student-os:material-ready: 2026-08-11 -->

> [!summary] 今天只学一件事
> 从一段讲座中恢复「讲者最想说什么」和「他用哪两块内容支撑它」，然后写成 3 句范围准确的 summary。

## 材料

- [TED 官方 YouTube：Hans Rosling, *Global population growth, box by box*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fTznEIZRkLg&t=180s)
- 首听范围：**03:00–09:15**，只播放一次，无字幕，不暂停。
- [TED 官方 transcript](https://www.ted.com/talks/hans_rosling_global_population_growth_box_by_box?view=transcript)：只在你发来首听笔记后使用。

2010most people middle. gap huge, 
2050 only tecnic avoid cilmate change lower middle move to upper , buy car.  poor nothing happen , popularaty more. no people poor, rather that population will keep growth

more child will survive and less children will every women give birth. population will boom in one generation.investment is necessary to reach a sustainable world 

## 完成证据

你能根据 transcript 核对后，用自己的英文写出 3 句：一句 central claim，两句 supporting blocks；不把预测写成已经发生的事实，不把某个地区的情况写成所有国家都一样。
