# Course map · 课程地图

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Open **Student OS Hub → External learning → Course map** in Obsidian. Courses run left to right by prerequisite order and are grouped vertically by field. Use the research/employment, role and field filters to narrow the catalog. Within a course, **Course panorama**, **Lesson/module study**, **Cross-course comparison**, and **Prerequisites and next directions** provide four ways to explore the same course and knowledge mappings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Obsidian 打开 **Student OS Hub → 校外学习 → 公开课与知识地图**。横向按先修关系展开，纵向按领域聚类，可按研究／就业、课程作用和领域筛选。进入课程后，**课程全景、逐节／模块学习、跨课对照、先修与去向**从四个角度展示同一门课及其知识映射。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A unit checkbox records that you have studied that unit. Its reviewed knowledge mappings light up wherever the same scope appears, with links back to the originating course and unit. Other courses remain uncompleted. Undo removes only that source; a topic stays lit when another confirmed source still covers it. Independent mastery is separate from having studied a topic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

章节复选框表示你确认学完这一单元。经核对的同范围知识会在其他课程里同步点亮，并标明来源课程和章节；其他课程本身不会被标成已完成。撤销只移除该学习来源，另有来源的知识仍然亮着。独立掌握与学过分开记录。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The comparison selector can compare your actual learning with a target course, or compare the full mapped scope of another course. Full-course comparison is hypothetical and does not write learning records. A completely covered set of mapped topics does not establish that every detail of a chapter can be skipped. Empty mappings mean unknown coverage, not no overlap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“比较起点”可选择我的实际已学，或对比另一整门课程的已核知识范围。整课比较只作假设分析，不写学习记录。“所列主题已覆盖”不表示整章每个细节都可跳过；没有映射表示范围待核对，不表示一定没有重合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **Knowledge coverage** view groups reviewed scopes by field. Search a concept, filter to scopes with learning records or scopes shared across courses, and expand a card to see its precise boundary, confirmed learning sources and course units. Clicking a unit opens that exact location in the course. This view always uses your actual records, even when a course comparison is active. Comparison choices are ordered by the number of equivalent mapped scopes they share with the target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**知识覆盖**视图按领域展示已核范围。搜索概念，筛选已有学习记录或跨课程共用的知识点，展开卡片即可查看范围边界、已学来源和所在课程单元；点击单元直接进入对应位置。这里始终使用实际学习记录。课程内的对照选项按与目标课程共有的同范围知识数量排序。

## Course position and two progress measures · 课程位置与双维进度

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**School learning** and **External learning** are separate selected areas. The location bar shows the current area, course and unit; returning to a previous course restores its tab and position. Course exploration stays in the same workspace, with prerequisite and successor relationships in their own tab. The full map keeps its filters and scroll position when you return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**学校学习**与**校外学习**为独立选中的区域。位置栏显示当前区域、课程与单元；返回上一课程会恢复其页签与位置。课程探索保持在同一工作区，先修与后续关系有独立页签；回到全图会恢复筛选和滚动位置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each course displays its own studied units and its deduplicated knowledge coverage separately. Counts retain the source unit: MIT 18.06SC has 36 sessions including the final review; ENGR108 is organized into 16 textbook chapters; MIT 6.036 uses 15 teaching chapters across 13 weeks. The other outlines now follow their official lectures, readings or video units; incomplete publication and unresolved mappings remain visible. **Cross-course comparison** projects shared knowledge and additional scope into each target chapter. Selecting a knowledge item opens its sources and exact locations in a supporting pane before any course navigation. Course-unit learning records open inside that unit and retain an unfinished draft while browsing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每门课分别显示本课已学单元和去重后的知识覆盖。计数保留原来源的单位：MIT 18.06SC 为含期末复习的 36 节，ENGR108 按教材的 16 章组织，MIT 6.036 为 13 周中的 15 个教学章节。其他目录已按官方讲次、阅读课或视频展开；尚未发布的部分与待核映射单独标明。**跨课对照**按目标课程的章节列出共有知识与额外范围。点选知识点先打开详情，可看已学来源及其他课程的准确单元，再决定是否跳转。课程单元的学习记录在本节内打开，浏览过程中保留未完成的填写内容。

## Course panorama · 课程全景

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The panorama initially opens as a **Learning route**. Select a course part to read its chapters or modules in official order, with each unit's knowledge shown alongside its title. A continuous completed prefix within that part folds into **Expand to review**; later units remain visible, and the selected completed unit expands automatically. Select a unit title to enter it, or a knowledge item to inspect its scope, learning sources and connections to other courses.

Switch to **Knowledge matrix** for a **chapters × knowledge** view of the same course part. Columns are chapters or modules; rows are reviewed knowledge scopes. A filled green dot means that the same scope has a personal learning record, an empty dot means that this unit teaches it but no personal record exists, and a dash means it is not mapped to that unit. The checkmark above a column records completion of that course unit separately. Select a column number to enter it, or a knowledge name or dot to inspect its sources. Both panorama modes always use your actual learning records.

Each course-part card shows unit completion and knowledge coverage separately; whole-course knowledge counts deduplicate scopes appearing in several parts. Topic-outline courses retain module labels. Units awaiting scope verification remain accessible through their materials; courses awaiting alignment with an existing plan show that their unit progress is pending alignment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程全景初始显示**学习路线**。选择课程分部，按官方目录顺序阅读章节或模块，每个单元的标题旁列出其中的知识。该分部开头连续已学的章节折叠为**展开回顾**，后续单元保持可见；选中的已学章节会自动展开。点章节标题进入学习；点知识查看范围、已学来源及其他课程中的连接。

切换到**知识矩阵**，可查看同一分部的**章节 × 知识**对应表。列为章节或模块，行为已核对的知识范围。绿色实心圆表示同一范围已有本人学习记录；空心圆表示本单元包含该范围、但尚无本人记录；横线表示本单元没有映射到该范围。列上方的勾号单独表示本课这一单元已学。点章节编号进入该单元，点知识名称或圆点查看来源。两种全景模式始终使用实际学习记录。

分部卡片分别显示单元完成和知识覆盖；整课知识数量会对跨分部出现的相同范围去重。大纲课程保留“模块”口径。尚待核对知识范围的单元仍可进入材料；与原学习计划尚未对齐的课程会标明“章节进度待对齐”。

## Reading a course · 阅读一门课

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The app bar separates **School courses** and **External learning**. Within a course, the title, two independent progress measures, and four view tabs remain visible. **Lesson study** is available for complete outlines; **Module study** identifies syllabus-level groupings. The directory and reading pane scroll independently. **My continuation points** opens the first unit without a completion record directly; completed courses open their panorama, while courses awaiting unit alignment return to their original plan. **Continue learning** within a course also enters the next unrecorded unit using your actual learning state. Selection never records completion. The current unit provides its materials, knowledge rows, completion control and, where available, an expandable **Evidence for the knowledge scope** section linking to official materials.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

顶部入口区分**学校课程**与**校外学习**。课程标题、两种独立进度和四个视图页签持续可见。完整目录显示**逐节学习**，大纲主题分组显示**模块学习**。目录与阅读区各自滚动。**我的接续点**直达首个尚无完成记录的单元；已完成课程进入全景，尚待逐节对齐的课程返回原学习计划。课程内的**继续学习**也会使用本人实际学习状态进入下一未记录单元。选择单元不会自动标记完成。当前单元提供材料、知识行和完成复选框；有映射依据时，可展开**知识范围的依据**核对官方材料。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Select a knowledge row to inspect its precise scope and the course units that supplied your personal learning records. Other locations are grouped by course, with a **Compare these courses** action and exact chapter/module destinations. Each location lists additional scopes without your learning records. **Enter this lesson/module** follows the knowledge to that location; the destination retains the selected knowledge and a return link to the origin. An existing knowledge-note link appears when the scope has linked atoms. On wide panes, the inspector sits beside the lesson; on medium and narrow panes it temporarily occupies the reading area while retaining the directory and course context. Close it or press Escape to return to the lesson.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

点选知识行，先查看准确范围，以及本人已学记录来自哪些课程单元。其他位置按课程分组，每组提供**对照两门课**和准确的章节／模块入口；每个位置列出其中还有哪些范围尚无本人记录。点击**进入这一节／这个模块**，沿知识点进入对应位置；目标保留所选知识和返回来源的入口。范围已有原子链接时，还可打开已有知识笔记。宽窗口并排显示章节与知识详情；中小窗口暂用阅读区展示详情，保留目录和课程位置。关闭详情或按 Escape 回到章节。

## Cross-course comparison · 跨课对照

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Cross-course comparison** keeps the baseline and target explicit: **My actual learning** uses your records across courses; **Full course scope** uses the selected source course's reviewed mappings. The chapter table places overlap and additional content alongside each target chapter, with filters for all chapters, chapters with additional content, or chapters whose mapped scopes are fully covered. **View differences from this chapter** enters the first chapter with a mapped difference in official directory order and activates the difference-only filter. The lesson's previous/next controls then visit only chapters containing additional or unrecorded scopes. Unmapped and overview/review units are excluded from this sequence. **Return to full-course comparison** restores the comparison baseline and filter. This is a route for inspecting differences, not a recommendation to skip other chapters.

Full-course mode labels overlap as overlap and disables the unit-completion checkbox; switch back to actual learning to record completion. The knowledge inspector still shows your personal state separately. Opening **Course panorama**, **Lesson/module study**, or **Continue learning** directly returns to your actual learning state.

For example, with the full MIT 18.06SC scope as the baseline, Stanford ENGR108 currently has **33 mapped scopes: 19 overlapping and 14 additional**. Four of its 16 chapters have all listed scopes represented in the baseline; 12 contain additional scope. Additional scope can be a distinct derivation of a familiar result: the chapter on least squares separately maps the derivation from first-order conditions and the shared normal equations. This compares the current mappings, not your completion or every detail taught in those chapters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**跨课对照**持续标明比较起点和目标：**我的实际已学**汇总本人跨课程的学习记录；**完整课程范围**使用所选来源课程的已核映射。章节表在目标章节旁并列展示重合和额外内容，可筛选全部章节、含额外内容、所列范围已覆盖。**从这一章查看差异**按官方目录顺序进入首个存在映射差异的章节，并启用只看差异的筛选；章节内的前后按钮随后只经过含额外或未记录范围的章节。范围待核对及概览／复习单元不加入这条序列。**返回全课对照**恢复比较基准和筛选。这是查看差异的路径，不表示建议跳过其余章节。

整课模式将共有内容标为“重合”，并禁用章节完成复选框；切回实际已学后可记录完成。知识详情仍单独展示本人的学习状态。直接打开**课程全景、逐节／模块学习**或**继续学习**，会返回本人实际学习状态。

例如，以 MIT 18.06SC 完整范围为基准，当前 Stanford ENGR108 的 **33 个映射范围中有 19 个重合、14 个额外范围**。其 16 章中，4 章所列范围均被基准包含，12 章含额外范围。额外范围可以是熟悉结论的另一种推导角度：最小二乘一章把“一阶条件推导”和共有的正规方程分别映射。这比较的是当前映射，不代表本人完成情况，也不代表已覆盖这些章节的每一个细节。

## Prerequisites and next directions · 先修与去向

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This view separates prerequisite courses from direct downstream courses. Each relationship shows its reason, whether it is an official prerequisite or an inferred content bridge, and an evidence link. Preparation requirements show personal coverage only for the topics already mapped, alongside the other stated requirements. One covered relationship does not establish readiness for the entire next course. Official course materials, edition details and mapping notes appear below the relationships.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一页分别列出前置课程和直接后续方向。每条关系显示衔接原因、属于官方先修还是内容推断，以及依据链接。准备要求只对已映射主题显示本人学习覆盖，同时列出其他要求；满足一条衔接关系不代表已经具备整门后续课的全部准备。关系下方集中提供官方材料、课程版本与映射说明。

## Probability and Bayesian route · 概率与贝叶斯路线

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

## Data maintenance · 数据维护

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
`course-data/{math,probability,cs,ai,bayesian}.json` owns public course metadata and reviewed scope mappings. Canonical knowledge notes remain the objects identified by `atom_id`; catalog outcomes may link to several existing atoms. Personal completion is not stored in the public catalog.

MIT 18.06 unit controls update the existing canonical plan through Student OS. Other pre-existing bound tracks keep their original plans until unit alignment is reviewed. New course confirmations are written to the ignored private record `99_学习情况记录/课程地图/学习记录.md`, created on the first confirmation. Record identity includes course, edition and unit; changing editions never transfers completion automatically. No record is created merely by browsing or comparing.

Only `mapping: reviewed-outline` grants shared coverage. New syllabus records stay `syllabus` until their semantic scope has been checked. Preserve stable course/edition/unit identities, split deeper outcomes when needed, and cite the official source for each unit and dependency. Run `node course-check.js`, the existing plugin checks, then `bash deploy.sh` after changes. The deploy script builds a native Obsidian plugin bundle and runs the Student OS regression suite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公开课程元数据和范围映射位于 `course-data/{math,probability,cs,ai,bayesian}.json`。知识笔记继续由 `atom_id` 标识，单项课程知识范围可以链接多篇已有原子。公开目录不保存个人完成状态。

MIT 18.06 通过 Student OS 直接更新原有唯一学习计划。其他已有课程在逐节对齐之前继续使用原计划。新课程的确认写入被 Git 忽略的私有记录 `99_学习情况记录/课程地图/学习记录.md`，首次确认时才创建。记录标识包含课程、版本与章节；更换版本不会自动继承已学。浏览和比较不会创建学习记录。

只有 `mapping: reviewed-outline` 参与跨课点亮。大纲条目保留 `syllabus`，待语义范围核对后再映射。保持课程／版本／章节身份稳定；更深入的内容按需要分开，章节与依赖均保留官方依据。更新后运行 `node course-check.js`、已有插件检查和 `bash deploy.sh`；部署脚本会构建原生 Obsidian 插件并执行 Student OS 回归检查。
