- The 6.041SC index contains 25 numbered lectures and three assessment pages. Individual lecture topic lists were checked. L03 supports conditional independence; L05 and L08 distinguish PMFs from CDFs and continuous expectation; L14 explicitly teaches exponential interarrival times and memorylessness. These checks prevent false gaps when comparing the MIT and Harvard probability paths.
- MIT 18.650 has 24 numbered lectures in the slide directory and only 22 video pages. For the 22 available recordings, official VTT transcripts were fetched and inspected for the content allocated to continuation lectures.
- Harvard Stat110 uses the official Fall 2011 iTunes U syllabus table of contents, with all 34 lectures and the original review lectures. Homework and strategic practice remain companion material. MIT 18.675 uses Nike Sun’s 25-lecture topic PDF, not student-written notes.
- Stanford STATS200 uses the 29 linked Lecture01–Lecture29 PDFs in the Autumn 2016 schedule. The web page still says tentative; the actual published PDF sequence is the directory evidence. The optional R orientation and separate examinations are not counted as numbered lectures.
- The remaining MIT courses use every numbered row of the official version-specific calendar; EE364A uses every published SEE lecture. Long calendar topic lists remain attached to their actual lecture instead of being split into invented sessions.
- Knowledge scopes were reviewed semantically. Composite scopes that span multiple sessions were replaced by narrower mappings, while old canonical definitions were retained. Reviews and exams do not auto-grant the entire course. Unknown scope remains `syllabus` instead of an empty reviewed lecture.
