## Validation boundary

Unit IDs are newly explicit (`lec`, `tut`, `read`, `lab`, etc.) and do not reuse coarse `mNN` identities. Existing actual progress must be reconciled by the parent integration, not copied onto arbitrary individual lectures. Per-unit `label` exposes a human-readable official number/date/chapter label; `kind` distinguishes labs, tutorials, guests, reviews and readings.

Knowledge definitions were reused only for matching scope. Newly narrow scopes were added for logistic basics versus the existing regularized logistic outcome, perceptron updates versus convergence, individual theory tools, specific NLP/graph tasks, LM data filtering versus source documentation, and Bayesian hierarchical parameterization. No shared definition was changed to accommodate a new lecture.
