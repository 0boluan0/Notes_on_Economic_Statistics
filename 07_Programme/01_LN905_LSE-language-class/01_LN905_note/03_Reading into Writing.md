# 阅读综合转写：Reading into Writing

*From multiple texts to a synthesised response*

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> Paper B 不是“三篇文章摘要”，而是 **用三篇材料回答一个 essay question**。稳定流程是：先拆 question → 给每篇材料建立 claim/evidence/scope/limitation/role → 横向找 relation → 形成自己的 thesis 与 paragraph jobs → 按 claim 选材并 synthesis → 最后检查 attribution、paraphrase 和 task fulfilment。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Paper B is not a set of three summaries. It asks you to use three texts to answer an essay question. Analyse the task, map each source's claim, evidence, scope, limitation and role, compare across sources, create a thesis and paragraph jobs, synthesise evidence under your claims, and verify attribution, paraphrase and task fulfilment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先看清任务

*Understand the task first*

| 项目 | 正式要求 |
|---|---|
| 输入 | 一个 essay question + 三篇 adapted extracts |
| 输出 | 90 分钟内约 600 词 response：约 30 分钟阅读/计划 + 60 分钟写作 |
| Reading 权重 | 60/90：text use、synthesis、selection、paraphrasing |
| Writing 权重 | 40/90：task fulfilment、structure、style/range、accuracy |
| 最高档特征 | 围绕写作目的改造材料；识别文内与跨文 links；选材精确；quote/paraphrase/summary 熟练；结构推进清楚 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Paper B provides an essay question and three adapted extracts for a roughly 600-word response in 90 minutes: approximately 30 minutes for reading and planning, then 60 minutes for writing. Reading is judged through text use, synthesis, selection and paraphrasing; writing through task fulfilment, structure, style and range, and accuracy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Band 分水岭

*Band boundaries*

| Band | Reading | Writing |
|---|---|---|
| 80+ | excellent text use；按写作目的 adapt；文内/跨文 links 清楚；source handling 优秀 | 完整回答；progression、paragraphing、cohesion 极强；精确灵活、几乎无错 |
| 70–75 | selection、adaptation 与 synthesis 很好，偶有局部 description | 回答全部或大部分 task；推进清楚；少量 minor errors |
| 60–65 | 能使用材料，但跨文联系和 selection 不稳定、偏 descriptive | 基本完成；结构大致清楚；语言够用但不够灵活精确 |
| ≤50 | 材料未真正服务论证，links 弱或 source handling 有严重问题 | 漏 task；paragraphing/progression 弱；错误影响理解 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bands distinguish purposeful adaptation and cross-text synthesis from competent but partial use, then descriptive or unstable handling, and finally material that fails to serve the task.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!important] 评分含义
> “三篇都提到”不等于 text use 好；真正的高分是把 source content **adapt to the purpose of writing**。不相关的信息即使引用正确，也会降低 selection；按 Text 1、Text 2、Text 3 排列，即使内容准确，也几乎没有 synthesis。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Mentioning all three texts is not enough. High-level text use adapts source content to the writing purpose. Irrelevant but accurate material weakens selection, while a Text 1–Text 2–Text 3 structure suppresses synthesis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 写作前的 reading toolkit

*The input-side reading toolkit*

### 预读审计：先判断这是什么来源

*Preview audit: identify the source first*

在正文前检查 title 对内容的预测、publication date、publisher、author credentials 与 reference list。接着读 abstract 和 headings，先写出预期结构与可能的 central claim。这样做不是提前评价“好/坏”，而是建立阅读问题：来源在回答什么、凭什么、在什么 scope 内回答。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before close reading, predict from the title, inspect date, publisher, author credentials and references, then use the abstract and headings to anticipate structure and the central claim. This establishes questions about purpose, evidence and scope rather than a premature good-or-bad label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Skimming：在限时内找骨架

*Skimming: find the structure under time pressure*

课程 Week 2 用五分钟 skim 来确认 text says what。先读 title、abstract、section headings、首尾句、重复关键词和 figure captions；目标是画结构与定位证据，不是在第一次阅读就理解每个句子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Week 2 exercise uses a five-minute skim to identify what the text is doing. Read titles, abstracts, headings, opening and closing sentences, repeated terms and figure captions to map structure and locate evidence rather than decode every sentence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### SQ3R：把长 section 读成可回忆内容

*SQ3R: turn a long section into retrievable knowledge*

1. **Survey**：扫 headings、subheadings 与 key terms。
2. **Question**：把 heading 改写成自己要回答的问题。
3. **Read**：只为这些问题寻找 claim、reason 与 evidence。
4. **Recite**：合上文本，用口头或一句话复述 subsection。
5. **Review**：回查 key points、自己的疑问、评价与可复用 vocabulary。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
SQ3R surveys structure, converts headings into questions, reads for answers, requires recall without the page, and then reviews key points, evaluation, remaining questions and useful language.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Jigsaw：用“教给没读过的人”检验理解

*Jigsaw reading: test understanding by teaching it*

每人深读一个 section，再向其他组员解释，使所有人获得 full-text map。有效分享必须交代 section claim、最关键 evidence、与全文的关系及一个 limitation/question；照着原文念不算完成，因为听者无法判断层级。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In jigsaw reading, each person masters one section and teaches it to others. A useful report gives the section claim, strongest evidence, role in the whole text and one limitation or question; reading the source aloud does not reveal hierarchy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Figure reading：description 与 implication 分开

*Figure reading: separate description from implication*

先写 figure depicts 的 variables、unit、time/place 和主要 trend/comparison，再写 implication；没有 research design 支持时，不把 correlation 写成 causation。Figure 的作用可能是 support、qualify 或 challenge text claim，不能只把轴标签改写一遍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Describe variables, units, scope and the main trend or comparison before stating an implication. Do not infer causation without design support, and identify whether the figure supports, qualifies or challenges the textual claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Language mining：只收可复用 pattern

*Language mining: collect reusable patterns*

Highlighter 不抄整句，而是记录功能与搭配，例如 `X is conditional upon Y`（qualification）、`This contrast implies…`（interpretation）、`evidence remains limited to…`（scope）。Grammar mining 也应围绕自己确实不稳定的项目，观察多个 authentic examples 后总结 pattern。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mine reusable functions and collocations rather than whole sentences—for example, qualification, interpretation and scope patterns. Grammar mining should target a real weakness and infer a pattern from several authentic examples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两小时的完整流程

*A complete two-hour workflow*

> [!tip] 起始时间分配｜AI 推导
> 可先试 `10 分钟拆 question → 35 分钟 skim + targeted reading → 15 分钟 synthesis matrix、thesis 与 paragraph jobs → 50 分钟写作 → 10 分钟核验`。这是起始方案，不是课件规定；无 plan 会导致逐篇摘要，无 final check 会留下 attribution 与 task omission。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A practical starting allocation is ten minutes for the question, thirty-five for skimming and targeted reading, fifteen for the synthesis matrix and plan, fifty for drafting, and ten for verification. This is not a course rule; retain planning and final checks.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第一步：在读文本前拆 question

*Step 1: analyse the question before reading*

用四格写在题目旁：

| 格 | 要问什么 |
|---|---|
| Task | 要 position、comparison、evaluation、cause、consequence 还是 solution？ |
| Parts | 有几部分；是否等权；`and` 前后是否都要回答？ |
| Restrictions | time、place、population、case、perspective 有何限制？ |
| Definitions | 哪些 contested terms 会改变答案，必须 operationalise？ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the required intellectual action, the number and weight of parts, restrictions of time, place, population, case or perspective, and contested terms whose definition would change the answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

题目没有给 restriction 或 structure 时，不是“没有要求”，而是作者要主动提供。Thesis 必须直接回答 task，并说明最关键的 qualification；否则后续选材没有标准。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the question supplies no restriction or structure, the writer must create them. The thesis must answer the task and state the decisive qualification so that source selection has a criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第二步：把三篇材料压成一张 source map

*Step 2: compress the texts into a source map*

每篇只留以下字段：

| Source | Main claim | Best evidence | Scope | Limitation | Role in my answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 一句话 | 数字/机制/例子 | 谁、何地、何时 | method/data/warrant | support / challenge / qualify / example |
| B | 一句话 | 数字/机制/例子 | 谁、何地、何时 | method/data/warrant | support / challenge / qualify / example |
| C | 一句话 | 数字/机制/例子 | 谁、何地、何时 | method/data/warrant | support / challenge / qualify / example |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reduce each source to one main claim, its strongest usable evidence, scope, limitation and intended role in the answer. This prevents memory of source order from controlling essay structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着横向找五类 relation：共同结论；直接 disagreement；同结论但不同 mechanism；不同 scope 所造成的表面冲突；一篇能 qualify 另一篇的条件。Synthesis 从这一步开始，不是等到写句子时才加 `however`。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare across sources for shared conclusions, direct disagreement, different mechanisms, apparent conflict caused by scope, and evidence that qualifies another source. Synthesis begins in the map, not when a connector is added during drafting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后把 map 转置成真正服务写作的 **theme × source matrix**：每一行是一个 paragraph theme/job，每一列是 Text A/B/C，每格只记 `claim / evidence / scope / role (support-challenge-qualify)`。Per-source map 帮你读懂；theme matrix 才决定怎样写。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Transpose the source summaries into a theme-by-source matrix whose rows are paragraph jobs and whose cells record claim, evidence, scope and role. Per-source notes secure comprehension; the thematic matrix controls synthesis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第三步：先定 thesis，再给每段一个 job

*Step 3: set the thesis, then assign paragraph jobs*

一个可用 thesis 通常是 `direct answer + decisive mechanism/reason + qualification`。段落则按 argument job 排列，例如：定义或判断标准 → 最强支持机制 → counterargument/limitation → 综合判断。不要按作者顺序分段，也不要为了“平均使用三篇”给每篇固定三分之一篇幅。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A usable thesis combines a direct answer, decisive reason or mechanism, and qualification. Organise paragraphs by argumentative jobs—such as criterion, main mechanism, counterargument or limitation, and judgement—not by author order or equal source quotas.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每段在动笔前写一句 writer-led topic sentence，然后才把 sources 填进去。若 topic sentence 只能写成 `Smith discusses…`，说明段落仍由 source 控制；应先问“这一段要证明什么”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write a writer-led topic sentence before inserting sources. If the only available opening is “Smith discusses…”, the source still controls the paragraph; first identify what the paragraph must prove.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第四步：让 synthesis 在段落里可见

*Step 4: make synthesis visible within paragraphs*

高分段落的工作顺序是：

`writer's claim → Source A evidence → relation to B/C → why the relation matters → evaluation/qualification → link to question`

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strong paragraph moves from the writer's claim to evidence, relates that evidence to other sources, explains why the relationship matters, evaluates or qualifies it, and reconnects to the question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Synthesis 的七个可见信号

*Seven visible signs of synthesis*

1. 同一段出现多个来源，但围绕一个 shared issue。
2. 使用 `scholars`、`recent research`、`the literature` 等准确 generalisation。
3. 多来源共同支持一个命题时使用 clustered citations。
4. 混合 narrative/integral 与 parenthetical/non-integral citation。
5. `similarly`、`by contrast`、`however`、`consequently` 表达真实关系。
6. Topic sentence 与 concluding sentence 都是 writer's voice。
7. 不只说明 sources 不同，还解释差异为何影响 question 的答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Visible synthesis groups multiple sources around one issue, uses accurate generalisations and clustered citations, varies citation position, encodes real relationships, frames the paragraph with the writer's voice, and explains why source relationships change the answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 假 synthesis
> 同一段放三个作者名、在句间加 `however`，仍可能只是 source-by-source description。判断方法是删掉作者名后看段落是否还剩一个清楚的 writer claim 和 reasoning chain。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Three author names and a “however” can still form source-by-source description. Remove the names mentally: a synthesised paragraph should retain a clear writer claim and reasoning chain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Quote、paraphrase、summary 的分工

*The division of labour among quotation, paraphrase and summary*

- **Quote**：definition、必须精确的 wording 或具有独特分析价值的短语。
- **Paraphrase**：保留一项具体 reasoning、mechanism 或 evidence。
- **Summary**：压缩一整段或整篇材料的方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quote definitions or uniquely important wording, paraphrase specific reasoning, mechanisms or evidence, and summarise broader direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三种形式都必须有 attribution，并由自己的句子解释它为什么在此处有用。只换同义词、保留原句结构不是 paraphrase；长 quote 即使不构成 plagiarism，也会削弱 selection 和 writer's voice。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All three require attribution and interpretation. Synonym substitution that preserves source structure is not paraphrase; a long accurate quotation may avoid plagiarism yet still weaken selection and writer control.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Citation：把“我读过什么”说准确

*Citation: represent what you actually read*

准备三种基本形式：`Author (Year) argues…`、`… (Author, Year)`、三位以上作者的课程指定 `et al.` 形式。Narrative citation 适合强调谁提出观点；parenthetical citation 适合让 claim/evidence 留在前景。不要每句都从作者名开始。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use narrative citation when authorship matters and parenthetical citation when the claim or evidence should remain foregrounded. Do not begin every sentence with an author name.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 已解 secondary referencing 题｜讲义明示

*Solved secondary-referencing task: lecturer-provided*

如果你只读了 Lee and Hancock (2024)，其中提到 Beyens et al. (2021)，但你没有读 Beyens 原文：

- Narrative：`Beyens et al. (2021, cited in Lee & Hancock, 2024) claim…`
- Parenthetical：`… (Beyens et al., 2021, cited in Lee & Hancock, 2024).`

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When you have read Lee and Hancock but not the original Beyens study, identify the secondary route explicitly in either narrative or parenthetical form. Never cite Beyens as though you had read it first-hand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

能找到 original 时优先读 original，因为 secondary source 的概括可能省略 method、scope 或 qualification；考试材料不允许追原文时，则如实标明 secondary reference。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Prefer the original where available because a secondary account may omit method, scope or qualification. When the assessment materials prevent this, state the secondary route honestly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 已解综合练习｜AI 推导

*Solved synthesis exercise: AI-derived*

假设 question 是：`To what extent does flexible working improve employee performance?` 三篇材料分别得到：A 发现独立任务产出上升，但样本只含 technology firms；B 发现 teamwork coordination 变差；C 认为 autonomy 的效果取决于 manager support。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume one source finds higher output on independent tasks in technology firms, another finds weaker coordination in teamwork, and a third argues that the effect of autonomy depends on managerial support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Source map 的答案

*Source-map answer*

| Source | Role | 与其他文献的关系 |
|---|---|---|
| A | support，但 scope 窄 | 被 B 的 task difference 与 C 的 condition 限定 |
| B | challenge 普遍正效应 | 说明 performance 维度不只 individual output |
| C | qualify / mechanism | 解释 A 与 B 为什么可能同时成立 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Source A supports a bounded benefit, B challenges a universal positive effect by changing the performance dimension, and C provides the condition or mechanism that can reconcile them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Thesis 的答案

*Thesis answer*

Flexible working can improve performance on autonomous individual tasks, but the benefit is conditional rather than general because collaborative performance depends on coordination and managerial support.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This thesis answers directly, identifies task type as the main mechanism, and qualifies the scope through coordination and managerial support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Paragraph 的答案

*Paragraph answer*

Flexible working appears most beneficial when performance depends on autonomous rather than tightly coordinated activity. Source A reports higher individual output, although its technology-firm sample restricts the claim's scope. Source B's evidence of weaker team coordination therefore does not simply contradict A; it shows that a different dimension of performance may deteriorate. Source C helps reconcile the findings by identifying managerial support as a condition under which autonomy can be converted into effective work. Taken together, the extracts support a task- and context-dependent benefit rather than a universal productivity gain.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paragraph is writer-led, uses all three sources for different roles, explains rather than merely labels their relationship, evaluates scope, and ends with a judgement tied to the question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 交卷前按评分标准反查

*Final check derived from the marking criteria*

- **Task fulfilment**：thesis 直接回答 question；每个 part 和 restriction 都出现。
- **Text use**：每次引用都服务某个 claim，不为覆盖而塞材料。
- **Selection**：保留最有证明力的信息，三篇不必平均分配。
- **Synthesis**：至少有明确 agreement、difference、mechanism 或 qualification，并解释其意义。
- **Paraphrasing**：meaning、scope 与 attribution 不变；句法和信息包装已改变。
- **Structure**：每段一个 job，progression 可追踪，writer's voice 控制段首段尾。
- **Language**：range 不牺牲 accuracy 与 clarity；cohesive device 表达真实关系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Verify full task coverage, purposeful text use, selective evidence, explained cross-source relationships, accurate paraphrase and attribution, one job per paragraph, visible writer control, and language whose range does not sacrifice clarity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 练习反馈怎样使用 AI

*How to use AI during practice*

课程 workbook 要求先独立完成 essay。反馈时依次问：我的 thesis 是否回答 task；每段 job 是什么；所有 source use 是否可定位；哪里是 synthesis、哪里仍是 description；最影响成绩的一个 revision target 是什么。要求 AI 引用原句、对照 criteria、不要给分或改写；你修订后再让它只验证目标是否真正解决。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After an independent draft, ask whether the thesis answers the task, what job each paragraph performs, where each source is used, where synthesis becomes description, and which single revision has the highest value. Require exact textual evidence and criteria-based diagnosis, then revise yourself and request verification only.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] Workbook 内部冲突
> Workbook 第 4 页明确规定 AI 不得重写句子，但第 14 页又要求 AI 提供三种 rewording，二者冲突。本笔记采用更严格且更有利于学习的规则：AI 只定位过近措辞、解释风险并提出改写策略；你自己重写，再让 AI 核验 meaning、distance 与 citation。目录还列有 Prompt bank（p.28），但当前 PDF 实际只到标页 26，不能把不存在的页面当作已学内容。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The workbook contradicts itself: page 4 prohibits AI rewriting, while page 14 asks it for three rewordings. This note follows the stricter learning rule: AI identifies excessive closeness, explains the risk and suggests a strategy; the student rewrites and then requests verification of meaning, distance and citation. The contents also lists a Prompt bank on page 28, but the current PDF ends at labelled page 26.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 四周 practice 的 deliberate-practice loop

*Four-week deliberate-practice loop*

| Week | 主焦点 | 核验问题 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | essay frame | thesis 是否直接回答；intro/conclusion 是否对应 |
| 2 | sources 与 critical voice | 每次 source use 的 role；哪里 synthesis、哪里 description |
| 3 | structure 与 argument | 每段 job、progression、counterargument 与 link |
| 4 | language 与 concision | recurring error pattern；删改是否提高 precision 而不损 meaning |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The weekly sequence progresses from the essay frame to source use and critical voice, then argument structure, and finally language and concision. Each week preserves one diagnosed pattern and one independently completed revision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检

*Minimum self-check*

1. 为什么平均使用三篇材料可能降低 selection？
2. 同一段出现三位作者为什么仍不一定是 synthesis？
3. Source C 在已解练习中为什么比“第三个例子”更有价值？
4. 什么时候必须使用 secondary reference？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
&nbsp;

**1.** Why can equal source coverage weaken selection?<br>
**2.** Why do three authors in one paragraph not guarantee synthesis?<br>
**3.** Why is Source C more valuable as a mechanism than as a third example?<br>
**4.** When is secondary referencing required?<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 参考答案
> 1. 各材料对 question 的相关性不同，平均篇幅会迫使作者加入次要信息。2. 若仍按作者逐一报告、没有共同 claim 与关系解释，就只是 description。3. 它能解释 A/B 表面矛盾并形成条件性判断。4. 你使用某原研究的观点，却只从另一篇已读文本中接触它时。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Sources differ in relevance, so equal space can force weak evidence into the answer. Author-by-author reporting lacks synthesis even within one paragraph. Source C reconciles apparent conflict through a condition. Secondary citation is required when a claim from an unread original is known only through the text you actually read.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
