# Week 4：Climate Change

> [!summary] 快速恢复

## Topic Input：问题、语言与 framing

*Topic Input: the problem, language and framing*

开场问题要求同时处理：climate change 的定义、原因与后果；自己国家是否已有可感变化；government、companies 与 individuals 谁应承担主要责任；technological、political、societal barriers；以及它主要是 scientific、political 还是 moral issue。不同 framing 会改变责任、紧迫性与可行动性，不能把语言当成中性包装。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The opening discussion connects causes and consequences, observed local change, responsibility among governments, companies and individuals, technological, political and social barriers, and whether climate change is primarily scientific, political or moral. Framing changes perceived responsibility, urgency and agency rather than merely decorating the same message.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

David Wallace-Wells 的短讲题目要求解释为什么“生活的每一面都会被触及”；为何人类历史上超过一半的 fossil-fuel emissions 在最近 25 年产生尤其重要；若不改变路径，世纪末 agriculture、economics、conflict 与 natural disasters 会怎样；以及结尾 adjectives 与整体 optimism/pessimism。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The David Wallace-Wells questions ask why every aspect of life will be affected, why producing more than half of historical fossil-fuel emissions in the last twenty-five years matters, how agriculture, economics, conflict and disasters may look by century's end, and whether his final language is optimistic or pessimistic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

语言对比：climate change / crisis / catastrophe / emergency；denier / sceptic；carbon emissions / greenhouse-gas emissions；global warming / global heating；biodiversity / wildlife；fish stocks / fish populations。强度、技术精确度、隐含责任与情绪唤起都可能不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paired terms differ in intensity, technical precision, implied responsibility and emotional effect: change versus crisis or emergency, denier versus sceptic, carbon versus greenhouse-gas emissions, warming versus heating, biodiversity versus wildlife, and fish stocks versus populations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 四组 framing 答案

- **Gain vs Loss**：gain frame 对 mitigation 通常更有效；loss frame 对 adaptation 更有冲击力。
- **Individual vs Collective**：个人责任与社会责任各有优缺点，效果取决于 audience/context。
- **Present vs Future**：present frame 制造 urgency/emotional engagement；future frame 支持 long-term thinking 与 transformative change。
- **Local vs Global**：local frame 增强个人相关性；global frame 呈现规模与复杂性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gain framing tends to support mitigation and loss framing adaptation. Individual and collective frames work differently by audience and context. Present framing creates urgency and emotion, while future framing encourages long-term transformation. Local framing increases personal relevance, whereas global framing conveys scale and complexity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Topic Listening：LSE academics 的四组方案

*Topic Listening: four groups of solutions from LSE academics*

四组问题分别是：如何改造 economy；如何改造 cities/transport；如何摆脱 fossil fuels；如何改变 land use、保护 forests 与 ecosystems。先分组听，再在组内整合，随后与其他组交换 summary，最终完成方案表并练习正/负 framing。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four listening groups cover economic transformation, cities and transport, transition from fossil fuels, and land use, forests and ecosystems. Students first consolidate within groups, then exchange summaries across groups and complete a common benefits-and-challenges table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| Possible solution | Benefits | Challenges |
|---|---|---|
| Carbon tax | 激励低碳行为与 green innovation | 可能对部分群体更不利；需要 public support |
| Electric vehicles | 比 combustion engines 更容易制造/有替代潜力 | 价格高；需要适配基础设施 |
| Self-driving vehicles | 可能减少所需车辆与 road space | 改变 land use；也可能增加 road use |
| Solar energy | clean、renewable、effective | diffusion/部署仍需解决 |
| Hydro power | clean、renewable、effective | 大坝 costly、risky，可能 displacement |
| Biofuels | fossil-fuel alternative | 效果不明确；可能造成 green grabbing |
| Forest carbon markets | offsets carbon use | 可能造成 green grabbing |

## Additional listening：Mark Carney, “From Climate Crisis to Real Prosperity”

*Additional listening: Mark Carney, “From Climate Crisis to Real Prosperity”*

Carney 的主线是 finance 必须把 climate risk 转化为 reporting、risk management 与 returns。当前金融配置仍对应 3°C 以上升温；企业与资产需要可比较的 warming-potential ratings，市场要区分 “fifty shades of green”，避免把所有承诺当成相同质量。Offsets 与 nature-based solutions 有作用，但不能代替真实减排，也有 integrity 风险。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Carney argues that finance must translate climate risk into reporting, risk management and returns. Current capital allocation remains consistent with warming above 3°C; comparable warming-potential ratings and distinctions among “fifty shades of green” are needed. Offsets and nature-based solutions can help, but cannot replace real emissions cuts and require integrity safeguards.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

转型还需要 policy：carbon pricing、公共与私人 investment、明确 rules，以及 just transition。个人可以追问 employer 的 climate plan、自己的 savings 如何投资、国家是否有可信路径。Markets 不是价值的全部，却能动员 innovation、investment 与 profit incentive；最终应让 values guide value，而不是把 GDP 当作繁荣的唯一尺度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The transition also needs carbon pricing, public and private investment, clear rules and a just transition. Individuals can question employer plans, savings and national pathways. Markets are not the whole of value, but can mobilise innovation, investment and profit incentives; values should guide value rather than treating GDP as the sole measure of prosperity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

讨论应涵盖 greenwashing 与 short-term politics、citizens' assemblies、China、转型速度、climate-justice funding 与 negative-emissions technology。完整音频和 transcript 都已保留。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Follow-up discussion covers greenwashing, short-term politics, citizens' assemblies, China, transition speed, climate-justice finance and negative-emissions technologies. Both the full audio and transcript are preserved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Topic Reading：Lewandowsky（2021）

*Topic Reading: Lewandowsky (2021)*

Climate science 的 expert consensus 超过 97%，但公众持续低估。阻力不仅来自缺少 information，也来自 worldview 与 elite cues：free-market libertarian identity、partisanship、populism 与 conspiracy thinking 会让 mitigation 被看作身份或制度威胁。Accumulated emissions 的机制本身也违反直觉，增加 cognitive difficulty；有组织的 misinformation/disinformation 进一步放大问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expert consensus on climate science exceeds 97%, yet the public underestimates it. Resistance reflects not only missing information but worldviews and elite cues: free-market libertarian identity, partisanship, populism and conspiracy thinking can make mitigation feel like an identity or institutional threat. The unintuitive accumulation of emissions and organised misinformation add further difficulty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

较有效的回应包括：解释 climate mechanism；明确展示 scientific consensus；使用 culturally aligned message 与 messenger；在接触 false claim 前用 inoculation 预先揭示操纵方式；接触后进行清楚 debunking。Lewandowsky 同时强调 communication alone 不足：若 economic、political、cultural power distribution 不变，仅增加 messages 很难推进 mitigation。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Effective responses include explaining mechanisms, highlighting scientific consensus, using culturally aligned messages and messengers, inoculating audiences before misinformation, and debunking afterwards. Communication alone is insufficient, however, when economic, political and cultural power structures remain unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 阅读与讨论任务

先区分 information、misinformation、disinformation，并核查 author background、publisher 与 date。用 SQ3R 处理四部分：physical science versus social cognition、climate change versus human cognition、climate science versus disinformation、communicating in an adversarial environment。组间分享后必须理解全文。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tasks distinguish information, misinformation and disinformation, verify author, publisher and date, and apply SQ3R to four sections on physical science and social cognition, human cognition, disinformation, and adversarial communication before reconstructing the whole text across groups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

讨论题：结论中的 Swedish school girl 是 Greta Thunberg；她在 2019 年通过 school strike 与全球动员改变公共议程，但要评价她“教会世界什么”。还要讨论“掌权者 delayed, denied and misled”到什么程度成立；disinformation 通过哪些社会渠道传播，以及哪些 solutions 可能有效。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Discussion identifies Greta Thunberg and evaluates what her school strike and 2019 mobilisation taught the world, assesses the claim that those in power delayed, denied and misled, and maps the social spread of climate disinformation and possible responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Discussion & Debate：Toshoya Bank 投资 role-play

*Discussion and Debate: Toshoya Bank investment role-play*

决策标准包括 political stability、economic viability、ROI、duration、partners、exchange-rate risk、guarantees，并可补充 environmental/social impact、concentration risk、reputation 与 strategic diversification。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Decision criteria include political stability, viability, return, duration, partners, exchange-rate risk and guarantees, with room to add environmental and social effects, concentration risk, reputation and strategic diversification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| Project | Amount | Duration | Partners/guarantee | Return |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Malaysia dam | $500m | 5 years | Malay International Bank | 7% |
| Senegal irrigation | $290m | 1 year | World Bank + EU | 9% |
| South Korea shipyard | $360m | 2 years | none | 6% |
| Canada bridge | $475m | 4 years | Canadian government | 5% |

五个角色的真实偏好不能漏：A 是 chair/president，偏向 Malaysia，强调经验与稳定；B 负责 West Africa，偏 Senegal 的最高回报、World Bank/EU 和发展需求，反对 Malaysia 的高成本、currency 与 concentration；C 负责 South Korea，强调订单、skilled workforce 与 currency，反对 Senegal 的 instability/devaluation；D 负责 North America，偏 Canada 的 government backing/tolls，反对 Korea 缺乏 backing、tanker market 低迷，并要求 diversification；E 负责 Malaysia，强调 dam、growth、track record 与 stability，反对 Canada 的低 yield 与缺乏 network。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The five roles have distinct interests: A chairs and favours Malaysia for experience and stability; B favours Senegal for the highest return, World Bank/EU backing and need, while opposing Malaysia's cost, currency and concentration; C favours South Korea for orders, skills and currency but questions Senegal's instability; D favours Canada's government backing and tolls, opposes Korea's unbacked exposure to a weak tanker market, and seeks diversification; E supports Malaysia's dam, growth and track record while criticising Canada's low yield and weak network.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必须使用的互动功能包括：starting、eliciting opinions、stating an opinion、responding、clarifying、restating、adding、interrupting、agreeing、objecting 与 summarising。目标不是赢得角色立场，而是让所有信息进入共同 decision，并能说明最后取舍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Required interaction moves include starting, eliciting opinions, stating and responding, clarifying, restating, adding, interrupting, agreeing, objecting and summarising. The goal is not simply to win for one's role, but to bring all information into a defensible collective decision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Pronunciation Session 4：Word stress 与 sentence stress

*Pronunciation Session 4: word and sentence stress*

Word stress 的基础规则：多音节词有一个主要重读音节；只有 vowel sound 承担 syllable stress。多数双音节 nouns/adjectives 第一音节重读，多数双音节 verbs 第二音节重读，但都有例外，实际使用前要用 Collins、Cambridge 或 Oxford 核对。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multisyllabic words have one primary stressed syllable, and stress falls on a vowel sound. Most two-syllable nouns and adjectives stress the first syllable, while many two-syllable verbs stress the second, but dictionary checking is essential because exceptions are common.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Suffix 线索：stress 常落在 `-able, -ial, -cian, -osis, -ic, -tion, -ible, -ish` 前一音节；`-ade, -ee, -ese, -ette, -que, -oon` 自身重读；`-ery, -cy, -ty, -phy, -al` 前两个音节处常重读。Acronym 的最后一个字母重读；compound noun 与 noun–noun collocation 通常第一部分重读。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stress often falls immediately before `-able, -ial, -cian, -osis, -ic, -tion, -ible, -ish`; on the suffix itself for `-ade, -ee, -ese, -ette, -que, -oon`; and two syllables before `-ery, -cy, -ty, -phy, -al`. Acronyms stress the final letter, while compound nouns and noun–noun collocations normally stress the first element.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Sentence stress 改变重点与含义：`I don't think she should buy that shirt` 中依次强调 I / don't / think / she / should / buy / that / shirt，会分别改变“谁这样想、是否同意、确定程度、谁行动、规范判断、行动类型、对象选择、物品类别”。通常 nouns、main verbs、adjectives、adverbs 承担信息 stress，非重读 grammar words 同时弱化成 schwa；两者结合才形成清晰 rhythm。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sentence stress changes focus and meaning: stressing different words in “I don't think she should buy that shirt” changes the thinker, disagreement, certainty, actor, obligation, action, choice or item. Nouns, main verbs, adjectives and adverbs usually carry information stress, while unstressed grammar words reduce towards schwa to create clear rhythm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
