# 口头论证转写：Speaking into Writing

*Speaking, Listening and Academic Interaction*

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> 这一模块训练两种能力：**把一个观点压缩成有结构的 1–2 分钟口头论证**，以及 **听懂、接住并推进他人的观点**。它通向写作的方式不是把整场讨论抄下来，而是留下可用的 claim、cross-source relation、limitation 和 academic language，再把这些材料放进自己的 thesis 与 paragraph plan。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This module trains two abilities: compressing a position into a structured one- to two-minute argument, and understanding, responding to and advancing other contributions. It feeds writing by preserving usable claims, cross-source relationships, limitations and academic language—not by transcribing the discussion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Paper C 到底评什么

*What Paper C assesses*

Interview 包含 general questions、1–2 分钟 presentation 和 in-depth questions。Speaking 看 fluency & clarity、accuracy、structure、impact；Listening 看 comprehension 与 interaction。高分不是说得最多，而是内容有深度、结构清楚、回应准确，并能通过澄清、追问、agreement/disagreement、turn-taking 和邀请他人让讨论继续发展。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The interview combines general questions, a one- to two-minute presentation and in-depth questions. Speaking is judged on fluency, clarity, accuracy, structure and impact; listening on comprehension and interaction. High performance develops ideas clearly while clarifying, probing, agreeing or disagreeing constructively, managing turns and involving others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Paper C 提供 Speaking 总分的 90/90，并提供 Listening 总分的 30/90；因此 interaction 不是“附加礼貌”，而是正式 listening evidence。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Paper C supplies all speaking marks and thirty of the ninety listening marks, so interaction is assessed listening evidence rather than optional politeness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Band 分水岭

*Band boundaries*

| Band | Speaking | Listening / interaction |
|---|---|---|
| 80+ | 高度流利清楚；几乎无错；观点复杂、相关且有充分 depth | 全部理解；必要时主动澄清；有效回应并邀请他人；自然处理 agreement/disagreement 与 turns |
| 70–75 | 大多清楚流利；minor errors；结构与发展很好 | 理解大多数/全部；回应、澄清与 turn-taking 有效 |
| 60–65 | 偶有 hesitation/不清；organisation、relevance 或 depth 不稳 | 理解多数，但该澄清时可能不澄清；互动一般有效 |
| ≤55 | breakdown；错误影响理解；观点不聚焦或未发展 | 误解却不澄清；难以获得/保持 floor，或支配讨论而不邀请他人 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bands separate fully developed, responsive interaction from generally effective performance, then from unstable depth or clarification, and finally from breakdown, misunderstanding or domination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 三部分的最短 response pattern

*Minimum response patterns for the three parts*

- Part 1：`direct answer → reason → short example → qualification`。
- Part 2：从 Justification / Synthesis / Rebuttal 中选一条完整路线。
- Part 3：`acknowledge previous point → clarify/paraphrase if needed → respond with reason/evidence → connect or invite`。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Part 1 expands a direct answer; Part 2 selects one complete argument structure; Part 3 demonstrates listening by acknowledging, clarifying, responding and reconnecting the group.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一次高质量 contribution 的最小单位

*The smallest complete contribution*

`回应前文 → 给出 point → 解释 reason/mechanism → 给 evidence/example → 必要时 qualify → 交还 floor`

只给 opinion 会浅；只给 evidence 不说明意义会散；完全不回应上一位 speaker 会变成并列 mini-presentations。每次不必把六步都说长，但必须让听众知道你在回应什么、主张什么、为什么。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A complete contribution links back, states a point, explains a reason or mechanism, adds evidence or an example, qualifies when needed, and returns the floor. Opinion alone is shallow, evidence without interpretation is loose, and ignoring the previous speaker produces parallel mini-presentations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两分钟 presentation：先选结构

*Choose a two-minute structure before wording*

| 结构 | 路线 | 最适合 | 主要风险 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Justification | own claim → 2 reasons/evidence → conclusion | 题目要求清楚立场、时间很短 | 忽略 counterargument |
| Synthesis | view A → view B → compare/qualify → judgement | 题目本身有真实争议 | 两边复述、自己的答案太晚 |
| Rebuttal | fair opposing view → precise rebuttal → own case | 需要直接回应一个常见论点 | straw man |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Justification is efficient for a direct position but may ignore opposition. Synthesis fits a genuine debate but can delay the judgement. Rebuttal is persuasive only when the opposing case is represented fairly rather than weakened into a straw man.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两分钟通常只容得下一个明确 claim、两个充分展开的 reasons、各自一个 evidence/example、一个必要 qualification 和一句 conclusion。五个未解释的点不如两个完成的点。第一句就回答问题；最后一句重新回答，而不是只说 `That is all`。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two minutes normally fit one clear claim, two developed reasons with evidence or examples, one necessary qualification and a conclusion. Answer the question in the first sentence and answer it again at the end.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!tip] 准备卡｜AI 推荐
> 只写一行 answer、2–3 个 reasons/evidence、一个 counterpoint/qualification 和结尾；不要写逐字 script。卡片的作用是保留 route，让眼神、语速和现场措辞仍然自然。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Write one-line answer, two or three reasons or pieces of evidence, one counterpoint or qualification, and the ending. This AI-recommended card preserves the route without becoming a script.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 已解结构辨认题｜讲义明示

*Solved structure-identification task: lecturer-provided*

Week One slides 给出三篇 response：先陈述 critics 再反驳的是 **Rebuttal**；先明确自己立场并列两条理由的是 **Justification**；平衡 benefits 与 harms 后作条件性判断的是 **Synthesis**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the Week One exercise, the response that presents critics before answering them is Rebuttal; the response that states its own position and develops reasons is Justification; and the response that weighs benefits against harms before a qualified judgement is Synthesis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 口头功能库：按作用记，不按句子背

*A function bank, not a script bank*

| 功能 | 可调用表达 |
|---|---|
| 接住前文 | `Can I pick up on your point about…?` / `That connects to…` |
| 提出立场 | `I would argue that…` / `From my perspective…` |
| 给理由/证据 | `This is due to…` / `A clear example is…` / `This is supported by…` |
| 澄清 | `Do you mean…?` / `Could you be more specific about…?` |
| 部分同意 | `There is some truth in…, but…` |
| 反驳 | `That is persuasive to an extent; however, it overlooks…` |
| 推进 | `Why would that follow?` / `How does this compare with…?` |
| 总结并交出 | `So far, we seem to agree that… What do you think, X?` |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Learn expressions by communicative function: link, claim, support, clarify, partly agree, rebut, probe, summarise and hand over. A small controlled repertoire is more useful than a long memorised script.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Gain、maintain、yield the floor

*Gain, maintain and yield the floor*

**Gain**：优先接住上一句，而不是从零开场。**Maintain**：先 point 后 reason/example；被打断后用 `The point I am trying to make is…` 恢复。**Yield**：阶段性总结后点名邀请同伴，尤其是 quieter participant。若没听懂，澄清本身就是 Listening competence，不是假装理解的失败。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gain the floor by linking to the previous contribution, maintain it through point-plus-support, and yield it through a brief summary and an invitation to another participant. Clarification demonstrates listening competence; pretending to understand does not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Academic Interaction：共同建构，不是轮流汇报

*Academic Interaction: co-construction, not serial reporting*

七个角色只是让 group 从不同角度处理文本：Leader 保持方向并邀请发言；Connector 跨文本/课堂建立 synthesis；Evaluator 检查 citation、currency、method、sample 与 generalisability；Highlighter 抽取可用于写作的 academic/discipline language；Contextualiser 补背景；Visualiser 把关系或数据变清楚；Summariser 在需要时恢复主线。角色不是权限边界，每个人都仍需 listen、respond、question 和 connect。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The roles diversify the group's work: facilitation, connection and synthesis, source evaluation, useful language, context, visual representation and timely summary. They are prompts rather than boundaries; everyone still listens, responds, questions and connects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 角色 | 应留下的 writing product |
|---|---|
| Leader | question boundary 与可能 paragraph route |
| Summariser | main claim 与 key evidence |
| Connector | cross-text agreement/disagreement |
| Evaluator | credibility、method、sample、scope、limitation |
| Highlighter | reporting/evaluative/discipline phrase bank |
| Contextualiser | 必要背景；未经核验的外部事实不能直接塞进 Paper B |
| Visualiser | trend、comparison、data relation 与 implication |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each role should leave a concrete writing product: boundaries and route, claims and evidence, source relations, evaluation, useful language, verified context or interpretable data relationships.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 每周 deliberate-practice loop

*Weekly deliberate-practice loop*

课程流程是 `准备 10–15 分钟 → 讨论约 25–30 分钟 → group reflection → self-reflection → tutor feedback → 换角色并带一个 target 进入下一周`。反思要找一个可观察 behaviour，例如“追问后是否真的用了答案”，而不是写 `be more confident`。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The weekly loop combines preparation, a sustained discussion, group and individual reflection, tutor feedback, a changed role and one observable target. “Use the answer to a follow-up question” is actionable; “be more confident” is not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Evaluator 的五问

*Five evaluator questions*

1. Claim 的依据是什么，citation 是否可追？
2. Source 是否够新；若旧，旧是否影响结论？
3. Method 和 sample 能回答 claim 吗？
4. 结论可推广到哪些 people/place/time？
5. 其他来源是 support、challenge 还是 qualify？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The evaluator checks traceable evidence, currency, fit between method and claim, legitimate scope, and whether other sources support, challenge or qualify the conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从 speaking 转成 writing

*Convert speaking into writing*

讨论后只保留四栏，避免写流水账：

| 保留项 | 写作中的去向 |
|---|---|
| strongest claim | thesis 或 topic sentence 候选 |
| agreement / disagreement | synthesis relation |
| limitation / qualification | evaluation 或 counterargument |
| useful reporting/evaluative phrase | 可控的 academic language |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After discussion, retain only the strongest claim, cross-source agreement or disagreement, a limitation or qualification, and useful reporting or evaluative language. These become thesis or topic-sentence candidates, synthesis relations, evaluation and controlled academic wording.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

然后进行一次 writer takeover：口头上由 A、B、C 分别说出的观点，在写作中必须改由自己的 paragraph claim 统领；speaker names 和 source names 只能提供 evidence，不能决定段落顺序。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The writer must then take control. Contributions made by different speakers should be reorganised under the writer's paragraph claim; speaker or source order must not become paragraph structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 已解转换练习｜AI 推导

*Solved transformation exercise: AI-derived*

假设讨论留下三句：A 说 online seminars 扩大 access；B 说弱网学生反而被排除；C 指出两人的 evidence 都来自单一 institution。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume a discussion yields three contributions: online seminars expand access; poor connectivity creates exclusion; and both claims rely on evidence from one institution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!failure] 不能直接写成
> A said access improves. B disagreed. C said the evidence was limited.
>
> 这是 discussion chronology，不是 argument。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This reproduces the chronology of the discussion rather than building an argument.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!success] 可变成 paragraph plan
> **Claim**：Online seminars redistribute rather than uniformly increase access. **Evidence relation**：A 的 geographical access 与 B 的 digital exclusion 形成 qualification。**Evaluation**：两者均为 single-institution evidence，结论只能暂时限定在类似 context。**Paragraph judgement**：access 取决于 physical 与 digital barriers 的相对强度。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Claim: online seminars redistribute rather than uniformly increase access. Relation: geographical reach is qualified by digital exclusion. Evaluation: single-institution evidence limits generalisation. Judgement: access depends on the balance between physical and digital barriers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Discussion drills：把技巧练到可调用

*Discussion drills: make the skills callable*

| 课程练习 | 真正训练什么 | 成功答案 |
|---|---|---|
| Dead conversation | follow-up、example、interest、connection | 不停在 `Japan — Cool`，而是追问 place/reason/example 并接自己的 experience |
| Micro-skill cards | why/how、clarify、build、compare、invite、summarise、compromise | 句子必须推进上一观点或 group task，不是机械“说过一次” |
| Balloon debate | initial case → Q&A → rebuttal → vote | vote 看 persuasion；case 有 claim、展开理由、回应与 opposing argument |
| Pyramid discussion | individual → pair → 4 → 8 → class | 每轮先找 common ground，再解释 disagreement criterion；不能立即投票 |
| Hidden-role negotiation | criteria → evidence → comparison → compromise → decision | everyone speaks；同一 criteria 比选项；事实/假设/利益分开；结尾总结风险 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The discussion activities train sustained follow-up, functional micro-skills, persuasive case and rebuttal, consensus building, and evidence-based negotiation. Success is measured by movement in shared reasoning, not by uttering a stock phrase.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 通用 presentation 补充：不是 Paper C 硬要求

*General presentation supplement, not a Paper C requirement*

*Delivery: make structure audible*

控制 speed、room-appropriate volume、tone 与 hesitation；卡住时停顿、呼吸再继续，比填充音更清楚。不要背逐字 script；只保留 key words 和 route。提前查人名、理论和关键术语的 pronunciation。语言以可控为先，复杂句只有在能稳定完成时才使用。Slides 只放 key points，字号与颜色要从远处可读，academic presentation 最后一页放 references。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Control speed, room-appropriate volume, tone and hesitation; pause and restart rather than fill the gap. Use route notes instead of a full script, check key pronunciation, prefer controlled clarity to unstable complexity, keep slides readable and sparse, and end an academic deck with references.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这部分来自通用 Presentation Guide；slides、reference page 与 online setup 不属于 Paper C prompt-card interview 的硬要求。Guide 的文字版是自动 transcription，个别 wording 需回看原视频核验。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Slides, reference pages and online setup come from the general Presentation Guide rather than the Paper C prompt-card requirements. Its transcript was generated automatically, so uncertain wording should be checked against the video.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检

*Minimum self-check*

1. 为什么 speaking 最多不等于 interaction 最好？
2. Synthesis presentation 最大的结构风险是什么？
3. Academic Interaction 结束后，哪四类信息值得进入 writing notes？
4. 澄清问题为什么同时体现 Listening 与 Speaking？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
&nbsp;

**1.** Why does speaking the most not equal the best interaction?<br>
**2.** What is the main structural risk of a synthesis presentation?<br>
**3.** Which four kinds of information should survive into writing notes?<br>
**4.** Why does clarification demonstrate both listening and speaking?<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 参考答案
> 1. Interaction 还要求理解、回应、turn-taking 和让 group 前进。2. 两边复述太久，自己的 judgement 出现太晚。3. Claim、cross-source relation、limitation/qualification、可复用 academic language。4. 你必须先识别理解缺口，再用准确语言修复共同理解。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Interaction also requires comprehension, response, turn management and group progress. Synthesis may delay the speaker's judgement. Preserve claims, source relations, qualifications and useful language. Clarification first detects and then repairs a gap in shared understanding.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
