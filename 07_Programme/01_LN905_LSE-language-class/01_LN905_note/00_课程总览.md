# LN905 课程总览

*LN905 Course Overview*

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> LN905 的学习主线是四种可迁移转换：**听 lecture → critical summary；口头形成、检验并推进观点 → writing material；读多篇 texts → synthesised response；研究 question 与 literature → academic essay。** 议题只是练习载体，知识入口只有下面四份能力笔记。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> LN905 develops four transferable transformations: lecture to critical summary; spoken formation, testing and development of ideas to writing material; multiple texts to a synthesised response; and a research question plus literature to an academic essay. Topics are practice contexts rather than learning objects.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 四项能力

*The four skills*

| 顺序 | 能力笔记 | 解决的问题 | 核心产物 |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[01_Listening into Writing]] | 怎样实时筛选、准确改写并有依据地评价 lecture？ | 200–400 词 critical summary |
| 2 | [[02_Speaking into Writing]] | 怎样结构化表达、回应他人，并把 discussion 变成可写论证？ | Paper C response + argument notes |
| 3 | [[03_Reading into Writing]] | 怎样预读、比较三篇 texts，并由自己的 claim 组织 synthesis？ | 约 600 词 Paper B response |
| 4 | [[04_Academic Writing]] | 怎样从 question、thesis、sources 到完整 essay，并解答讲义练习？ | 2,000 词 assignment |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four notes progress from real-time listening, through structured spoken interaction, to timed cross-text synthesis and finally an independently researched essay. Each note contains assessment criteria, a usable process, solved exercises and self-checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它们怎样连接

*How the skills connect*

`predict / question → select evidence → identify relationships → form a claim → test or qualify it → communicate it → revise against criteria`

Listening 训练实时 selection；Speaking 让 claim 在 interaction 中暴露缺口；Reading 要把多来源 relation 变成 writer-led paragraph；Academic Writing 把同一套 reasoning 扩展到独立选题、检索、计划和 revision。四门课真正共享的是 **task control、source control、evidence-based criticality 和 clear structure**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Listening trains real-time selection; speaking exposes a claim to clarification and challenge; reading turns cross-source relationships into writer-led paragraphs; academic writing extends the same reasoning into independent research and revision. Their shared core is control of task, sources, evidence-based criticality and structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Assessment 权重地图

*Assessment weighting map*

| 能力总分 90 | 组成 |
|---|---|
| Listening | Paper A 60 + Paper C 30 |
| Speaking | Paper C 90 |
| Reading | Paper B 60 + Assignment 30 |
| Writing | Paper A 10 + Paper B 40 + Assignment 40 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The weighting explains strategic priorities: Paper A accuracy dominates its language contribution; Paper C interaction supplies listening evidence as well as all speaking marks; Paper B heavily rewards text use; and the independent assignment carries substantial reading and writing weight.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用方式

*How to use these notes*

第一次按 1→4 阅读，建立共同框架；之后直接进入当前 assessment 对应笔记。每份笔记中的 `讲义明示` 可回查课件答案，`AI 推导` 是根据课程 criteria 完成的参考答案，不冒充 tutor 唯一解。练习时先独立作答，再展开 answer callout 对照。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read the four notes in sequence once, then enter the note for the current assessment. Lecturer-provided answers can be checked against the materials; AI-derived answers solve open tasks from the course criteria without claiming to be the tutor's only answer. Attempt exercises before opening answer callouts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
