# 听讲转写：Listening into Writing

*From live listening to a critical summary*

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> Paper A 的核心不是“记得越多越好”，而是把一次性听到的 lecture 转换成 **准确、经过筛选、真正改写过并带有限评价的 critical summary**。稳定流程是：听 signposts 建结构 → 区分 claim / evidence / example / qualification → 听后重组笔记 → 用 reporting language 写内容 → 只在有依据处评价 → 最后反查准确性与连续原词。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Paper A is not a memory contest. It asks you to transform a one-off live lecture into an accurate, selective, genuinely paraphrased critical summary. Listen for structure, distinguish claims from evidence, examples and qualifications, reorganise the notes, report before evaluating, and finally verify accuracy and copied phrasing.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先看清任务

*Understand the task first*

| 项目 | 正式要求 |
|---|---|
| 输入 | 20–25 分钟 live lecture，只能依靠自己当场笔记 |
| 输出 | 40 分钟内完成 200–400 词 critical summary |
| Listening 权重 | 60/90：accuracy、paraphrasing、length、content、focus |
| Writing 权重 | 10/90：range、accuracy、style、structure、cohesion、critical comment |
| 最高档硬线 | 所有陈述准确；覆盖所有 key points；detail/depth 合适；不得连续复制五个原词 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The assessment uses a 20–25-minute live lecture followed by 40 minutes for a 200–400-word critical summary. Listening contributes 60 of 90 listening marks, while this task contributes 10 of 90 writing marks. The highest band requires complete accuracy, all key points at suitable depth, and no sequence of five words copied from the lecture.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Band 分水岭

*Band boundaries*

| Band | Listening | Writing |
|---|---|---|
| 80+ | statements 全准确；all key points；detail/depth 高度合适；不得连续复制五个原词 | critical comments 有依据且融入；几乎无语言错误；structure/cohesion 极清楚 |
| 70–75 | 大多数内容准确；覆盖 most key points；长串复制罕见；detail 合适 | 评论大多有支持并融合，少量仍 descriptive；只有 minor errors |
| 60–65 | 有若干误报；漏 1–2 个 key points；长度或细节层级略失控 | comment/description 边界不稳；cohesion 较有限；语言错误较多 |
| ≤50 | 多处误报、整句复制、漏超过两个 key points、明显过长/短 | 评论无依据、过度概括或脱离 lecture；结构不清 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bands separate complete accuracy and integrated evaluation from mostly accurate reporting, then from omissions and descriptive writing, and finally from systematic inaccuracy, copying and unsupported comment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!important] 评分含义
> Listening 分远高于本题的 Writing 分，所以优美但听错的句子是负资产。先保证 **谁说了什么、因果方向、数字、比较对象和限定条件** 正确，再追求语言复杂度。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Listening carries much more weight here than Writing. Elegant prose built on a misheard claim is a liability. Secure ownership, causal direction, figures, comparison groups and qualifications before adding linguistic complexity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一条完整转换链

*The complete transformation chain*

`lecture structure → selective notes → note map → summary plan → reported content → integrated evaluation → accuracy check`

任何一步跳过，后面都会补不回来：没有先辨认结构，就只能记录散句；没有先整理笔记，写作时就会边猜边写；没有先 report，评价会变成脱离 lecture 的个人意见。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each stage protects the next. Without a structural listen, notes become fragments; without a note map, drafting becomes simultaneous guessing; without accurate reporting, evaluation becomes detached personal opinion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 课程练习真正训练的输入法

*Input methods trained across the course*

Week 2–4 的 topic listening 虽然换了议题，反复练的其实是同一组方法：

1. **Predict**：先看 title、speaker、questions 和关键词，预测结构与可能答案，但把预测当 hypothesis，不能听到相似词就强行确认。
2. **One-play simulation**：正式 Paper A 是 live lecture；练习时定期只播放一次，暴露自己在实时选择上的问题。
3. **Question-guided notes**：预读问题可以建立 listening targets，但仍要记录问题列表之外的 central argument 与 signposts。
4. **Notes → mind map**：听后把线性碎片改造成层级图，暴露 claim-evidence 关系和缺口。
5. **Jigsaw retelling**：先与听同一材料的人校准，再向没听过的人作短 summary；对方能否复述主线，是 focus 是否成功的测试。
6. **Decision table**：把复杂材料压成 option / benefit / challenge，迫使自己区分 proposal、support 与 limitation。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Across changing topics, the course repeatedly trains prediction from titles, speakers and questions; one-play simulation; question-guided but not question-limited notes; conversion of notes into a mind map; jigsaw retelling to a new listener; and decision tables that separate options, benefits and challenges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] Prediction 不是答案
> Pre-listening prediction 的作用是让你准备 vocabulary、categories 和可能的 contrasts；真正听到的信息必须覆盖预测。若 prediction 与 lecture 冲突，以 lecture 为准，并在笔记中显式改掉旧假设。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Prediction prepares vocabulary, categories and possible contrasts; it does not supply the answer. Evidence from the lecture must replace any conflicting hypothesis in the notes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 听前：把纸设计成筛选器

*Before listening: turn the page into a filter*

在纸的一侧留宽 margin，主体记录 lecture，margin 留给听后写 3–7 词标签、补关系和圈 key points。可用一套很小的固定符号：`C` claim、`R` reason、`E` evidence、`Ex` example、`Q` qualification、`↔` contrast、`?` 未听清。==这套符号是 AI 推荐的实现，不是课件规定==；目的不是速记表演，而是让信息层级可见。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Leave a wide margin for post-listening labels, relationships and key-point selection. The compact symbol set is an AI-recommended implementation rather than a course requirement; its purpose is to expose hierarchy, not to perform elaborate shorthand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

建议把第一页预先分成以下字段：

| 字段 | 要记录什么 |
|---|---|
| Speaker / source | 讲者身份；某观点究竟属于讲者还是被引用者 |
| Central question | lecture 试图回答什么 |
| Main line | 讲者最终主张或总体结论 |
| Sections | signposts 暗示的 2–4 个部分 |
| Evidence alerts | 数字、研究、例子、因果机制、反例 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Prepare fields for source ownership, the central question, the main line, two to four signposted sections, and evidence alerts such as figures, studies, mechanisms and counterexamples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 听中：按层级选择，而不是逐句追赶

*During listening: select by hierarchy*

### 第一优先级：结构信号

*First priority: structural signals*

听到 `three main reasons`、`turning to`、`however`、`for example`、`the key point is`、`in conclusion` 时，立即在笔记中换层级或标关系。Signpost 比单个生词更重要，因为它告诉你后面的内容在整场 lecture 中承担什么功能。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the speaker announces reasons, changes direction, contrasts, exemplifies or concludes, change the level or mark the relationship in the notes. A signpost matters more than an isolated unknown word because it reveals the function of what follows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 第二优先级：四层信息

*Second priority: four information levels*

1. **Central argument**：整场 lecture 最终要你接受什么？
2. **Main points**：哪些理由或机制直接支撑 central argument？
3. **Evidence**：哪些研究、数字或比较真正支撑 main point？
4. **Example**：哪些材料只是帮助理解，并非必须进入 summary？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separate the central argument, its main supporting points, evidence that actually supports them, and illustrations that merely aid understanding. Capture the first two levels before deciding which evidence is indispensable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 所有权错误
> `Some critics claim X`、`One controversial example is X` 与 `The lecturer concludes X` 是三种完全不同的陈述。笔记中必须标明 voice；否则 summary 会把被批评的观点误写成讲者结论。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A view attributed to critics, a contentious example and the lecturer's own conclusion are not interchangeable. Mark voice in the notes or the summary may assign a rejected claim to the speaker.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对数字、否定、比较级、因果词和 scope 特别敏感：`associated with` 不能升级成 `causes`；`in one UK sample` 不能扩成 `people generally`；未听清的数字保留 `?`，绝不靠常识补。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Treat numbers, negation, comparison, causality and scope as high-risk details. Do not turn association into causation, a bounded sample into a universal claim, or an uncertain figure into a guess.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 听后：先把笔记变成地图

*After listening: turn notes into a map before prose*

先做四个动作：给每组笔记写短标签；删除重复；把每条 evidence 挂回对应 claim；圈出缺失就会改变 lecture 逻辑的 key points。此时才选择结构：

- **Linear**：lecture 本身按清楚的因果、时间或论证顺序推进。
- **Thematic**：lecture 在几个主题间往返，原顺序会让 summary 重复或跳跃。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Label each note cluster, remove repetition, attach evidence to its claim, and circle the points whose omission would distort the lecture. Retain a linear order when the lecture has a clear sequence; reorganise thematically when the original moves repeatedly across overlapping themes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!tip] 40 分钟时间分配｜AI 推导
> 可先试 `5 分钟建图与列段落 → 27 分钟写作 → 5 分钟检查 criticality/paraphrase → 3 分钟核对字数、voice 与语言`。这是起始方案，不是课件规定；不能取消最后核验，因为 accuracy 和连续原词必须回查。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A practical starting allocation is five minutes to map, twenty-seven to draft, five to inspect criticality and paraphrase, and three to check length, voice and language. This is not a course rule; retain final verification because accuracy and copied strings require comparison with the notes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 写作：先报告，再评价

*Drafting: report first, then evaluate*

### 最小骨架

*The minimum structure*

1. 开头一句交代 speaker、topic 与 central argument。
2. 中间 2–4 段各处理一个 main point，并配最必要的 evidence。
3. 在相关 point 旁加入有依据的 evaluation，而不是另写“我的看法”一段。
4. 只有 lecture 确实导向 implication 时才在结尾点出，不另起新论点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the speaker, topic and central argument; develop two to four main points with only the necessary evidence; integrate evaluation beside the relevant point; and mention an implication only when the lecture itself supports it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Voice 必须可见

*Make voice visible*

- Reporting：`The speaker argues…`、`The lecture identifies…`、`According to X…`
- Evaluation：`This is persuasive because…`、`The evidence is limited to…`、`The claim requires qualification because…`

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use reporting expressions to attribute lecture content and evaluative expressions to mark your judgement. The reader should never have to guess whose voice a sentence represents.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Criticality 的五层问题

*Five levels of critical questioning*

按由近到远的顺序检查：证据可靠吗？证据真的支持 claim 吗？中间的 warrant 是否说得通？结论能推广到什么 time/place/population？讲者是否处理了重要 counterargument？评价至少回答其中一个“为什么”，不能只写 `convincing`、`weak` 或 `biased`。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Evaluate the reliability of evidence, the fit between evidence and claim, the warrant connecting them, the legitimate scope of the conclusion, and the treatment of counterarguments. A label such as “convincing” or “biased” is not criticality until it explains why.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Paraphrase 的真正标准

*The real standard for paraphrase*

最高档要求不得连续复制五个 lecture 原词，但“换五个同义词”仍不等于改写。合格 paraphrase 同时改变 vocabulary、syntax 和 information packaging，并保留 technical term、原意、因果方向与 qualification。最有效的办法是先看笔记中的意思和关系写句子，再回查原 phrasing，而不是盯着 transcript 改词。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four-word ceiling is only a detection rule, not a paraphrasing method. Genuine paraphrase changes vocabulary, syntax and information packaging while preserving technical terms, meaning, causal direction and qualification. Draft from the meaning map, then check the source phrasing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Cohesion 只表达真实关系

*Use cohesion for real relationships*

`however` 必须真的引出 contrast，`therefore` 必须真的有因果，`for example` 后面必须只是 illustration。连接词不是装饰；段落结构、指代和关键词重复也在承担 cohesion。机械地把每句用 `moreover` 接起来会掩盖逻辑问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cohesive devices must encode real contrast, cause, illustration or sequence. Paragraphing, reference and controlled lexical repetition also create cohesion; repeated decorative connectors cannot repair a missing relationship.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 已解微型练习｜AI 推导

*Solved micro-exercise: AI-derived*

假设笔记是：`speaker: Patel / remote work; C: productivity depends on task; E: survey 600 UK employees, self-report; Ex: coding vs mentoring; Q: 2021 only`。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume the notes identify Patel as the speaker, argue that remote-work productivity depends on task type, cite a self-report survey of 600 UK employees, contrast coding with mentoring, and limit the evidence to 2021.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!failure] 低质量写法
> Remote work increases productivity. Patel proves this with a large study, and this is very convincing.
>
> 问题：把 conditional claim 写成普遍因果；把 self-report 说成 proof；忽略 UK/2021 scope；`convincing` 没有理由。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This version turns a conditional claim into universal causation, treats self-report as proof, drops the UK and 2021 limits, and offers “convincing” without a reason.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!success] 可用写法
> Patel argues that the effect of remote work on productivity varies by task, with individual coding appearing to benefit more than mentoring. This distinction is supported by a survey of 600 UK employees, although its reliance on self-reports and data from 2021 limits the strength and wider applicability of the conclusion.
>
> 这里先准确 report，再紧贴 evidence 评价 method 与 scope；没有把 limitation 夸大成“研究无效”。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This version reports accurately before evaluating method and scope beside the evidence. It qualifies the conclusion without exaggerating the limitation into total invalidity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 评分标准反推检查

*Checklist derived from the marking criteria*

- **Accuracy**：每句都能在笔记中找到，voice、数字、方向和 scope 不变。
- **Content & focus**：所有 key points 出现；supporting detail 没挤掉主线。
- **Paraphrasing**：没有五个连续原词；句法和信息包装也已改变。
- **Length**：200–400 词，既非 outline，也非 lecture reconstruction。
- **Criticality**：评论有 criterion 和 reason，并与被评内容整合。
- **Writing**：结构可追踪；cohesion 表达真实关系；语言复杂度没有牺牲准确性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Verify traceable accuracy, complete key-point coverage, genuine paraphrase, appropriate length, criterion-based integrated evaluation, and a structure whose language and cohesion clarify rather than distort the content.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 练习反馈怎样使用 AI

*How to use AI during practice*

课程 workbook 的底线是：你先独立完成 summary，AI 只指出证据和问题，不代写。最有效的反馈请求是：“分别按 Listening 与 Writing 指出一个 strongest feature 和一个 highest-priority weakness；每项引用我原文证据；不要给分，不要改写；最后只问一个帮助我自己修订的问题。”

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The workbook requires an independent draft before AI feedback. Ask for one strongest feature and one highest-priority weakness under Listening and Writing, exact evidence from your text, no mark or rewrite, and one question that makes you perform the revision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 四次 practice 的 deliberate-practice loop

*The four-practice deliberate-practice loop*

| Practice | 主焦点 | 留下的证据 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | accuracy、key points、focus | 哪个内容被误报/遗漏，下一次两个 targets |
| 2 | structure、cohesion、linking | paragraph map 与 recurring organisation problem |
| 3 | paraphrasing、reporting、style | 过近 phrasing 与 reporting-language pattern |
| 4 | criticality、balance、readiness | comment 的 criterion、持续弱项与无 AI 应试动作 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four practices progress from content selection to organisation, language control and assessment-ready criticality. Each cycle compares evidence with the previous attempt and records a recurring issue rather than producing a one-off impression.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检

*Minimum self-check*

1. 为什么 vivid example 不能自动成为 key point？
2. 什么时候 thematic summary 优于 linear summary？
3. 为什么 `The argument is convincing` 仍不算 critical comment？
4. “最多四个连续原词”为什么不能单独保证 paraphrase 成功？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
&nbsp;

**1.** Why is a vivid example not automatically a key point?<br>
**2.** When is a thematic summary preferable to a linear one?<br>
**3.** Why is “The argument is convincing” not yet a critical comment?<br>
**4.** Why does the four-word ceiling not by itself guarantee successful paraphrase?<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 参考答案
> 1. Example 的功能可能只是解释；只有支撑 central argument 的不可替代信息才是 key point。2. 原 lecture 在重叠主题间跳跃、照原顺序会重复或失去逻辑时。3. 它没有给判断 criterion、evidence 或 reason。4. 机械换词仍可能保留原句法、信息结构甚至曲解原意。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An example may merely illustrate rather than carry the argument. Use a thematic structure when the original sequence overlaps or jumps. “Convincing” lacks a criterion and reason. Mechanical word replacement may retain the source syntax and information structure or distort its meaning.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
