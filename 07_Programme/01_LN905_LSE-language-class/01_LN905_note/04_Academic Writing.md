# 学术写作：Academic Writing

*Techniques and solved lecture exercises*

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> Academic Writing 不是把语言“写得像论文”，而是完成一串可核验的决策：**把 question 变成明确 task → 用 thesis 先给答案 → 给每段一个 argumentative job → 找、评并综合 sources → 用 evidence 解释 claim → 在 conclusion 回答同一个问题。** 本笔记同时是 lecture answer book：`讲义明示` 表示答案可从课件后页或颜色动画核对；`AI 推导` 表示讲义只给问题，我根据其原则完成并解释答案。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Academic writing is a chain of auditable decisions: turn the question into a precise task, answer through a thesis, assign each paragraph an argumentative job, find, evaluate and synthesise sources, explain claims with evidence, and answer the same question in the conclusion. This note also serves as an answer book: “lecturer-provided” answers are recoverable from later slides or colour animations; “AI-derived” answers solve questions for which the materials provide no explicit key.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Assignment 的硬要求

*Non-negotiable assignment requirements*

- 约 2,000 词，topic 与自己的 academic field 相关。
- 至少六篇 academic references。
- Introduction 包含 clear thesis；body paragraphs 结构清楚；有 conclusion。
- References / bibliography 单独一页。
- 11/12 号清楚字体，1.5 或 double spacing，段间留空，justified text，page numbers。
- Cover sheet 包含 essay title、姓名、tutor、date、word count。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The assignment is approximately 2,000 words, uses at least six academic references, contains a thesis-led introduction, structured body, conclusion and separate bibliography, and follows the stated formatting and cover-sheet requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. Question analysis：四格法

*Question analysis: four lenses*

1. **Task**：position、compare、evaluate、explain causes、propose solution，还是组合动作？
2. **Parts**：有几部分；是否等权；题干是否暗示顺序？
3. **Restrictions**：time、place、case、theory、population 或 perspective 有何限制？没有时如何主动限定？
4. **Definitions**：哪些 contested terms 会改变答案，必须界定？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Analyse the intellectual task, the number and weight of parts, explicit or self-imposed restrictions, and contested terms whose definition changes the answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] `Discuss` 不是“介绍一下”
> 课件把 `discuss` 与 `critically discuss` 都解释为形成自己的 opinion，并由 evidence 支持。开放题没有结构，不代表可以随意写；结构责任转移给作者。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Discuss” and “critically discuss” require an evidence-supported judgement, not a topic overview. An open question transfers structural responsibility to the writer.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 已解题 1：nationalism｜讲义明示 + AI 补全

*Solved task 1: nationalism — lecturer-provided and AI-completed*

题干要求讨论“20 世纪最成功的 nationalism 是 imperial collapse 的受益者”，并从三个 region/time windows 中选一个。答案：Task 是评估命题并给立场；restriction 是只能选 Europe after 1918、European decolonisation after 1945 或 Soviet breakup after 1989 之一；题目没给段落结构，作者可按 mechanism、supporting cases、counterexample 与 judgement 组织；需界定 `successful nationalism`、`beneficiary` 与 `imperial collapse`。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The task is to evaluate the proposition and take a position. The response must select one of the stated historical regions and periods. Because no paragraph route is supplied, the writer may organise mechanism, supporting cases, counterexample and judgement, while defining success, benefit and imperial collapse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 已解题 2：citizen participation｜讲义明示 + AI 补全

*Solved task 2: citizen participation — lecturer-provided and AI-completed*

`Is citizen participation democratising? If so, why? If not, why not?` 是开放 evaluation，不必被迫选绝对 yes/no；可以回答“在何种条件下”。题目没有 time/place restriction，也没有结构，必须主动限定。至少界定 `citizen participation` 是 consultation、deliberation、co-production 还是 decision power，以及 `democratising` 用 inclusion、accountability、equal influence 还是其他 criterion 判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an open evaluation and can be answered conditionally rather than through an absolute yes or no. The writer must supply scope and structure and operationalise both participation and democratisation through defensible criteria.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 已解题 3：anti-globalisation｜讲义明示

*Solved task 3: anti-globalisation — lecturer-provided*

题目已给结构：分别评估 anti-globalisation arguments 对 international capital flows 与 personal income equality 的 strengths/weaknesses，再综合判断。两个 parts 都必须回答；`critically` 要求自己的 evidence-based judgement；需界定 `anti-globalisation arguments`、`capital flows` 与 `income equality`。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The question supplies its structure: evaluate strengths and weaknesses in relation to international capital flows and personal income equality, then synthesise a judgement. Both parts require coverage and key terms require definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 选一个能写好的 question

*Choose a question that can be answered well*

好 question 同时满足 feasibility、interest、disciplinary relevance 和 literature availability。排除五类：窄到只能复述一个事件；宽到 2,000 词无法判断；只要求 description；关键词 vague；只测试 recall。Case study 应用于检验更大的 analytical claim，而不是吞掉整篇 essay。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A viable question combines feasibility, interest, disciplinary relevance and available literature. Reject questions that are too narrow, too broad, merely descriptive, vague or recall-based. A case should test a broader analytical claim rather than become the whole question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Solved question-redesign task: lecturer-provided*

| `What are Giddens' concepts?` | knowledge recall | 评估 structuration theory 对某类现象的解释力与限制 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lecturer-provided revisions broaden isolated cases into analytical relationships, replace description with evaluation, define scope and mechanism, clarify vague concepts, and turn recall into assessment of explanatory usefulness.

## 3. Thesis：先给答案，再告诉读者为什么

*Thesis: answer first, then explain why*

Thesis 应直接回答 question、specific/focused，并通常在 introduction 出现、在 conclusion 重述。稳妥公式是：

`direct answer + decisive reason/mechanism + qualification/scope + optional route`

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A thesis directly answers the question, remains specific and focused, and normally appears in the introduction and is restated in the conclusion. A useful pattern combines answer, decisive reason or mechanism, qualification or scope, and an optional route.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 三个陷阱｜讲义明示

*Three thesis traps: lecturer-provided*

- **Non-thesis thesis**：`This essay will consider advantages and disadvantages…` 只宣布活动，没有 position。
- **Overly broad thesis**：`The government has the right to limit free speech` 没有 condition、case 或 justification。
- **No-structure thesis**：`There are many reasons…` 有方向，却不告诉读者哪些 reasons 承担论证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three lecturer-identified failures are an announcement without an answer, an unqualified overbroad claim, and a position that gives no route through its reasons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Thesis 诊断法｜AI 推导

*Thesis diagnostic: AI-derived*

把 thesis 遮住，问读者能否预测：作者答案偏向什么；最关键因果/criterion 是什么；答案在哪些条件下会改变；正文至少会有哪两项工作。若四项都看不出，thesis 仍只是 topic statement。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Test whether a reader can predict the position, decisive mechanism or criterion, conditions that might alter the answer, and at least two body tasks. If none are visible, the sentence remains a topic statement rather than a thesis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. LSE essay 与 IELTS essay 的差别

*LSE essay versus IELTS essay*

> [!answer] 比较题答案｜讲义明示
> IELTS sample 没有真正 thesis，只说会看两边再 conclusion；按 advantages / disadvantages 排列；解释和 evidence 少，追求 breadth。LSE sample 有明确 thesis；引用 academic literature；按 counterargument/rebuttal 与支持自己立场的 arguments 组织；每个 claim 用 explanation/evidence 展开，更重 depth。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The IELTS sample announces two sides without a thesis, follows an advantages/disadvantages route, and favours breadth with little evidence. The LSE sample states a thesis, uses academic literature, organises counterargument and supporting arguments, and develops claims through explanation and evidence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

真正差别不在词汇更长，而在 **谁控制论证**：LSE essay 由 writer's answer 决定 sources 和 paragraph sequence；sources 不是轮流上场的主角。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The essential difference is control: the writer's answer determines source use and paragraph sequence rather than allowing sources to appear one by one as the main actors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. Introduction：三个 moves

*Introduction: three moves*

Introduction 通常约占全文 10%，并完成三项工作：

1. **Context / issue / gap**：题目为何值得回答，已有 literature 到哪里，缺口是什么。
2. **Thesis**：concise、specific、focused 的答案。
3. **Approach / route**：将用什么 theory、sources、cases 或 paragraph route 证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An introduction is often about ten per cent of the essay and establishes context or a gap, states a concise and focused thesis, and indicates the approach, evidence or route used to support it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 坏 introduction 的诊断｜讲义题，AI 推导理由

*Diagnosing the weak introduction: lecturer task, AI-derived rationale*

原练习中的 opening 使用 `basically`、`messes with people's heads`、`right?`、`literally everyone`、`whatever`、个人 scrolling 轶事与 `I hope you enjoy…`。这些内容的问题不是“听起来不高级”，而是语域不合适、overgeneralisation、没有 academic evidence、没有 clear question/thesis/route，并加入与论证无关的作者情绪。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exercise opening is weak because its informal register, unsupported universals, personal anecdote and reader address do not establish an academic problem, evidence base, thesis or route.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Tense 练习答案｜讲义明示

*Tense exercise answer: lecturer-provided*

<span style="color:#008c95"><strong>Present perfect</strong></span>（如 `there have been attempts`）回顾过去开始、延续到现在的 research/events；<span style="color:#c7a600"><strong>simple future</strong></span>（`This essay will focus / will contend`）预告 essay 将完成的动作。课件同时说明 present simple（`This essay focuses / contends`）也可接受。`I` 或 `This essay` 取决于 discipline；Economics、Accounting、Finance 等数字型作业通常较少用 `I`。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Present perfect reviews research or events extending to the present; simple future previews what the essay will do. Present-simple route statements are also acceptable. The choice between first person and “This essay” depends on disciplinary convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. Body paragraph：每段只做一个 job

*Body paragraphs: one argumentative job each*

稳妥顺序是：`writer's topic claim → reason/mechanism → evidence from one or more sources → synthesis/evaluation → link to thesis/question`。Topic sentence 不能只写 `Smith (2020) argues…`；作者名是 evidence 的入口，不是段落目的。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A reliable paragraph moves from the writer's topic claim through reason or mechanism, evidence, synthesis or evaluation, and a link to the thesis. An author name introduces evidence; it does not define the paragraph's purpose.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!tip] Paragraph job test｜AI 推导
> 用一个动词完成 `This paragraph ____ the thesis by…`：defines、establishes、explains、tests、qualifies、rebuts、compares 或 synthesises。若只能填 `talks about`，段落工作仍不清楚。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Complete “This paragraph ___ the thesis by…” with a precise verb such as defines, explains, tests, qualifies or rebuts. If only “talks about” fits, the paragraph job remains unclear.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. Conclusion：回答同一个问题，但答案更具体

*Conclusion: answer the same question with an earned judgement*

Conclusion 不加入正文没有论证的新 claim。它先重述 thesis，再综合 main points 如何共同支持答案，最后可给 implication、recommendation、prediction 或 future research。不是逐段换词复述，也不是突然拔高。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A conclusion introduces no unsupported new claim. It restates the thesis, synthesises how the main points support it, and may draw an implication, recommendation, prediction or research direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> <span style="color:#e32620"><strong>红色｜重述 thesis</strong></span>，但比 introduction 更具体。<br>

>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->

## 8. Academic style：清楚比“像学术”重要

*Academic style: clarity before performance*

`I` 是否使用取决于 discipline；不用时可写 `This paper argues…`，避免没有 agent 的 `It is argued…`。Active/passive 由 focus 决定：强调 object/process 或不知道 agent 时用 passive；需要明确责任和 actor 时用 active。避免 idioms、proverbs、slang、contractions、随意 abbreviations；acronym 第一次写全称。Thesaurus 给出的词若改变 collocation 或 meaning，不用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First-person use is discipline-dependent. Use active or passive voice according to informational focus, not prestige, and avoid agentless claims that hide ownership. Exclude idioms, slang, contractions and unexplained abbreviations, expand acronyms on first use, and reject thesaurus choices that alter meaning or collocation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer] Active / passive 题答案｜讲义明示
> `The credit crunch was partly caused…` 把 outcome 放在前景；`Those banks…caused…` 把 responsible actor 放在前景。`Substance X is produced…` 合理，因为 lab technicians 不是重点；`It has been claimed…` 不合理，因为读者不知道谁 claim，无法追责或核验。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Passive voice foregrounds the outcome or process; active voice foregrounds the responsible actor. It is useful when the agent is irrelevant, but an agentless “It has been claimed” hides ownership and prevents verification.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 9. Sources：search、reference、evaluate、use

*Sources: search, reference, evaluate and use*

优先从 LSE Library catalogue、subject databases、department/programme reading lists 和 Google Scholar 找来源。先确定 department 要求的 referencing style；未指定时选一种并保持一致。不要盲信自动生成的 Google Scholar / catalogue reference，因为 capitalisation、edition、page range、DOI 与 source type 可能错误。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Search through the LSE Library, subject databases, departmental reading lists and Google Scholar. Follow the department's referencing style or use one consistently, and verify machine-generated references rather than copying them blindly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### CRAAP 不是五个标签，而是五组决定

*CRAAP as five decisions, not five labels*

| 维度 | 实际要判断什么 |
|---|---|
| Currency | 日期是否影响 claim；旧来源是经典理论还是已过时 evidence？ |
| Relevance | 是否直接帮助回答 question，而不仅是同一 topic？ |
| Authority | author、publisher、institution 的 credentials 与领域匹配吗？ |
| Accuracy | data、method、references 与其他可靠来源支持 claim 吗？ |
| Purpose | inform、persuade、sell、advocate；利益与 framing 会怎样影响内容？ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CRAAP evaluates whether date affects the claim, whether the source answers the actual question, whether authority fits the field, whether evidence and method support the conclusion, and how purpose or interests shape the account.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!tip] AI 补充的 method check
> CRAAP 之后继续问：research design 能支持 causation 还是只有 association？sample 与 measurement 能否外推？作者承认了哪些 limitation？这三问比笼统写“来源可靠”更接近 academic evaluation。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After CRAAP, test whether the design supports causal or only associational claims, whether sample and measurement permit generalisation, and which limitations the authors acknowledge.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 10. Paraphrase：先理解，再关掉原句重建

*Paraphrase: understand, close the source, then rebuild*

合格 paraphrase 同时改变 vocabulary、sentence structure 与 information packaging，保留 meaning、scope、causal direction、technical terms 和 citation。最稳流程是：读懂 → 写出一行 meaning map → 暂时移开原句 → 按自己的 argument 重建 → 回查遗漏、夸大与措辞重合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Genuine paraphrase changes vocabulary, syntax and information packaging while preserving meaning, scope, causal direction, technical terms and citation. Build from a meaning map and then compare against the source for omission, exaggeration and excessive overlap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 同义词与词形题｜讲义明示

*Synonym and word-family task: lecturer-provided*

| 原词 | 讲义答案 / 可用词形 |
|---|---|
| explanation | reason；explanation / explain |
| study | study / study / studious / studiously |
| mechanism | mechanism / mechanise / mechanical / mechanically |
| economy | economy/economics / economise / economic/economical / economically |
| combination | combination / combine / combined |
| consequently | thus / as a result |
| economise | save |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The slides use synonym and word-family changes as tools. They also contain a typo in “studious”; the corrected forms are shown here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 讲义自己的关键提醒
> `by studying → through the study of`、`mechanisation → mechanising` 等词形变化仍不足以构成 paraphrase，因为其余句法和信息顺序太接近原文。Word-family transformation 是局部操作，不是最终答案。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Changes such as noun-to-verb conversion remain too close when the surrounding syntax and information order are preserved. Word-family transformation is a local tool, not a complete paraphrase.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 哪些词不应硬换｜讲义明示

*Words that should not be forced into synonyms: lecturer-provided*

保留 discipline-specific 或无法自然替代的词，例如 `Industrial Revolution`、`demand factors`、`eighteenth century`、`steam engine`、`mechanical spinning`、`coal`、`labour`。`globalisation` 与 `globalism` 相关但不是同义词；thesaurus suggestion 不能推翻概念差异。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Retain technical and factual terms that cannot be replaced without distortion. Related concepts such as globalisation and globalism are not interchangeable simply because a thesaurus links them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 最佳 paraphrase 选择题｜AI 推导

*Best-paraphrase selection: AI-derived*

**Industrial Revolution 题选 b**：它保留英国独有的 high wages + cheap coal 组合及机械化为何 profitable 的因果链，同时重组句法；a 省略独特性，c 过近且改变信息。更紧凑的答案是：`Allen (2009) argues that Britain's unusual combination of costly labour and inexpensive coal made investment in labour-saving machinery profitable.`

## 11. Quotation：只保留必须原样出现的词

*Quotation: preserve only wording that must remain exact*

### 什么时候 quote｜讲义明示

*When to quote: lecturer-provided*

课件选 **1、6、8**：definition；关键片段太短、改写会损失意义；作者以 unique and powerful wording 表达。`尊敬专家`、`没时间做笔记` 或单纯想让开头有趣，都不是好理由。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The slides select definitions, wording too compact to paraphrase without loss, and uniquely powerful expression. Respect, lack of preparation or decorative interest are not valid reasons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 五个 quotation questions｜讲义明示 + AI 解释

*Five quotation questions: lecturer-provided with AI explanation*

1. 两个 definitions 并列是为了显出概念差异；`25`、`6` 是 source page numbers。
2. `[f]` 表示为嵌入当前句法而改了原文首字母大小写。
3. 四行以上 long quote 通常缩进、可用略小字号，不加 quotation marks；只有整段 wording 本身必须保留时才用。
4. `…` / `[…]` 表示省略原文部分，不能改变 meaning。
5. `states` 可按 stance 换成 `argues`、`contends`、`maintains`、`suggests`、`observes`，但这些动词并非“高级同义词”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exercise tests contrasting definitions and page numbers, square brackets for editorial change, block-quotation format, ellipsis for omission, and reporting verbs whose stance must match the source.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课件还显示：`This is that…` 指代不清，应直接用分号或 `which is that…` 连接；一段长 quote 若只有 `a valueless discipline` 具有独特价值，就 paraphrase 其余内容，只保留这个短语。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The slides also repair unclear reference in “This is that…” and demonstrate that a long quotation should be paraphrased when only one distinctive phrase deserves exact preservation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 12. Reporting verbs：动词同时报告 source 与 writer stance

*Reporting verbs encode source action and writer stance*

> [!answer] Clegg extract 的 reporting expressions｜AI 识别
> `analyses`、`discusses`、`claims`、`argues`、`uses`、`emphasises`、`represents`、`formulates`、`derives`、`considers`、`maintains`，以及被动结构中的 `proposed`。其中有些报告作者的 claim，有些报告其研究或写作动作，不能全部换成 `says`。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The extract uses reporting expressions for both claims and scholarly actions: analyses, discusses, claims, argues, uses, emphasises, represents, formulates, derives, considers, maintains, and the passive “proposed”. They are not interchangeable versions of “says”.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般现在时报告在当前论证中仍有效的 claim；一般过去时报告 completed method、observation 或 result；现在完成时概括持续积累到现在的 research 或接受状态。Tense 是时间与 rhetorical function 的选择，不由 source year 自动决定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Present tense treats a claim as active in the current discussion, past tense reports completed research actions or findings, and present perfect summarises research accumulated to the present. Publication year alone does not choose the tense.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 含义配对题｜讲义明示

*Meaning-matching task: lecturer-provided*

| Sentence verb | Answer | 功能 |
|---|---:|---|
| A `argues` | 2 | strong opinion |
| B `suggests` | 5 | possible explanation / partial solution |
| C `claims` | 1 | writer 保留或暗示 evidence 不足 |
| D `admits` | 4 | acknowledges limitation |
| E `found` | 6 | states a result |
| F `maintains` | 7 | continues to hold a position |
| G `contends` | 3 | disputes another view |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matching exercise shows that reporting verbs are evaluative choices: they can strengthen, hedge, acknowledge, report, persist or contest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 相近动词的边界｜AI 推导

*Boundaries between related reporting verbs: AI-derived*

- `note` 简短指出；`highlight` 强调重要性。
- `describe` 说明特征；`analyse` 拆解关系并解释意义。
- `suggest` 有依据但保留余地；`speculate` 更推测、evidence 更弱。
- Strong：argue、maintain、contend、assert、insist；Neutral：report、state、note、observe、find；Tentative：suggest、indicate、imply、propose、speculate。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Near-neighbours differ in emphasis, analytical depth and evidential commitment. Choose the verb that truthfully represents both what the source did and how confidently the current writer presents it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Grammar 选择题｜AI 推导

*Grammar selection task: AI-derived*

1. `Barker claims that these results are false.`
2. `Both writers believe such theories to be based on an essentially Marxist view of history.`
3. `Higgins rejects this notion as impossible in the physical world.`
4. `Zimmer suggests rethinking the current approach.`

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The answers demonstrate four different complements: a that-clause, noun phrase plus infinitive, noun phrase plus adjective phrase, and gerund phrase.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 五句 reporting exercise｜AI 推导

*Five-sentence reporting exercise: AI-derived*

- Frey and Osborne (2017) **argue** that automation threatens a substantial share of employment.
- Spencer (2018) **examines** how institutional arrangements shape automation's effect on work.
- Ford (2009) **suggests** that automation may eventually outpace job creation.
- Spencer (2018) **acknowledges** technology's labour-saving potential while maintaining that ownership shapes its benefits.
- Spencer (2018) **challenges** predictions that automation will necessarily eliminate work.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The five sentences deliberately mix position, research action, tentativeness, concession and challenge. The verb is selected for function rather than variety alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 13. Synthesis：让 writer's question 组织 sources

*Synthesis: let the writer's question organise the sources*

单一来源即使有很多 linking words，也不是 synthesis。合格 synthesis 由 writer-led topic sentence 控制，围绕同一 issue 组合多个 sources，混合 narrative/parenthetical citations，以 paraphrase/summary 为主，按 agreement、contrast、cause、qualification 等真实关系推进，并由自己的 concluding judgement 收束。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Synthesis requires writer control, several sources organised around one issue, varied citation position, predominantly paraphrase and summary, explicit relationships, and a concluding judgement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!important] 颜色答案不能丢
> Synthesis slides 中，<span style="background-color:#fff176"><strong>黄色</strong></span>先标 source citation / topic framing，<span style="background-color:#80deea"><strong>青色</strong></span>显示另一种 citation 或 writer comment，<span style="color:#00a651"><strong>绿色</strong></span>标 paraphrase/summary 与 writer-led topic sentence，<span style="color:#e32620"><strong>红色</strong></span>标 direct quotation 或 source-led opening。颜色不是装饰，而是在回答“谁控制 voice、信息以何种形式进入”。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The synthesis slides use colour to distinguish citation position, writer comment, paraphrase or summary, topic-sentence control, direct quotation and source-led openings. The colour layer answers who controls the voice and how source material enters the paragraph.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Automation synthesis 题｜讲义明示，压缩重写

*Automation synthesis task: lecturer-provided and condensed*

> [!answer] 参考答案

Frey and Osborne (2017) and Ford (2009) both anticipate substantial displacement, with Ford extending the claim to a structural point at which new industries cannot absorb lost jobs. Spencer (2018), by contrast, argues that capitalist institutions may preserve work while making it more insecure and exploitative. >
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->

> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

讲义最终动画的功能颜色是：<span style="background-color:#fff176"><strong>黄色</strong></span> topic/concluding frame 与第一来源；<span style="background-color:#80deea"><strong>青色</strong></span> 用 `Similarly` 接第二来源；<span style="color:#00a651"><strong>绿色</strong></span> 用 `In contrast` 引入第三来源；<span style="color:#c2185b"><strong>洋红</strong></span> 直接跨来源比较。判断重点是 relation，而不是颜色本身。

The final animation uses colour to reveal framing, similarity, contrast and direct cross-source comparison. The transferable answer is the relationship, not the colour itself.

## 14. Annotated bibliography：summary + evaluation + intended use

*Annotated bibliography: summary, evaluation and intended use*

每项 100–300 词，包含统一格式 reference、author authority / audience、research question 或 claim、theory/method/data、key finding、strength/weakness/limitation，以及它具体怎样 support、challenge 或 qualify 自己 essay 的哪一部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each 100–300-word entry combines a consistent reference, authority and audience, question or claim, theory or method, finding, strengths and limitations, and the precise role the source will play in the essay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Cognitive-bias 改写题｜AI 推导

*Cognitive-bias rewriting task: AI-derived*

> [!answer] Introduction
> Rushed decisions can produce systematic errors because they rely on cognitive shortcuts (Tversky and Kahneman, 1974). Although additional deliberation may improve decision quality, this essay argues that time alone cannot eliminate cognitive bias. It first examines biases affecting rapid judgement, then evaluates debiasing strategies, and finally explains why subconscious heuristics continue to influence decisions even under deliberate reflection.
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The revision replaces vague first-person wording with a problem, qualified thesis and route while keeping the responsible source visible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer] Conclusion
> Rapid decisions often rely on heuristics that conserve time but generate predictable errors. Deliberation improves judgement only when decision-makers actively test assumptions and apply debiasing strategies; time alone is insufficient because many biases remain subconscious and are reinforced by confirmation seeking. Additional time should therefore be treated as an opportunity for structured scrutiny, not as a guarantee of bias-free judgement.

## 16. 评分标准反推 revision

*Revision derived from the marking criteria*

| 层级 | Source use / argument | Writing |
|---|---|---|
| 80+ | 广泛且相关；按写作目的重组；文内/跨文 links 清楚；quote/paraphrase/summary 熟练 | 完整回答；progression、paragraphing 与 cohesion 极清楚；精确灵活、几乎无错 |
| 70–75 | 来源使用与 synthesis 很好，偶有局部 description | 任务大多完整；结构清楚；minor errors 不影响表达 |
| 60–65 | 能使用来源，但 selection/links 不稳定、偏 descriptive | 基本完成；结构大致清楚；语言够用但不够灵活精确 |
| ≤50 | source handling 弱、与写作目的脱节或引用有严重问题 | 漏答 task；progression/paragraphing 弱；错误影响理解 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Band improvement comes from better adaptation and synthesis of sources, fuller task fulfilment, clearer progression and paragraphing, and language that becomes more precise without sacrificing accuracy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Revision 顺序应是：先查 question/thesis alignment；再查 paragraph jobs 与 evidence warrant；再查 source synthesis 与 citation；最后才做 sentence-level language polishing。反过来只会把没有回答问题的 essay 写得更流畅。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revise in descending order of impact: task-thesis alignment, paragraph jobs and warrants, source synthesis and citation, then sentence-level language. Fluency cannot rescue an essay that answers the wrong question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Check full task coverage, one job per paragraph, evidence and warrants for major claims, writer-led synthesis, accurate source boundaries, two-way consistency between citations and bibliography, and complete presentation requirements.
