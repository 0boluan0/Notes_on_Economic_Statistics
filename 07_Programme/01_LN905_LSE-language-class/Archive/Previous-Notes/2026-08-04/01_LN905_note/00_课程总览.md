Begin with [[LN905 核心技法|LN905 Core Skills]]; the topic files remain source records for practising those skills.

## 核心学习路径

*Core learning path*

下面按课程真正训练的能力连续阅读：先从实时听取中筛选并写 critical summary，再把口头观点组织和互动转成论证材料，继而跨三篇文本 synthesis，最后扩展成完整 academic essay。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read the course as a sequence of transferable abilities: select and evaluate a live lecture for a critical summary, convert spoken interaction into argumentative material, synthesise three readings, and extend the same architecture into a researched academic essay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
