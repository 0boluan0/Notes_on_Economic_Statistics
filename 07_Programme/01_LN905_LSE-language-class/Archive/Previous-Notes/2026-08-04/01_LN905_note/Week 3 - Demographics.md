# Week 3：Demographics

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> 本周的争论不是“人口老龄化有没有成本”，而是成本究竟是**金融数字**还是**真实资源、劳动生产率与分配能力**的问题。Topic materials 从人口爆炸与人口萎缩两种叙事切入；Nersisyan et al. 认为货币“偿付能力”会误导对老龄化的理解；Ezeh et al. 比较高增长、低增长与负增长社会需要的不同政策。语言技能训练 connected speech，并用 balloon debate 和 pyramid discussion 练习说服与共识形成。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The central dispute is not whether ageing creates costs, but whether those costs are best understood as financial balances or as real resources, productivity and distribution. Topic materials contrast population-explosion and population-decline narratives; Nersisyan et al. challenge financial-solvency framing; Ezeh et al. compare policy needs across high-, low- and negative-growth societies. Language work develops connected speech, persuasion and consensus-building.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Topic Input：人口爆炸还是人口萎缩？

*Topic Input: population explosion or population decline?*

Demography 是对人类人口的统计研究，常围绕 fertility、mortality、age structure、migration 与 dependency。材料把两种公共叙事并置：Paul Ehrlich 的 *The Population Bomb* 预言社会崩溃、英国消失和印度饥荒，具体时间判断失败，却留下末日式增长框架；另一侧是人口 implosion 的担忧，例如 Elon Musk 与 Shanna Swan 对低生育率和生殖能力下降的警告。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Demography statistically studies population through fertility, mortality, age structure, migration and dependency. The materials contrast Paul Ehrlich's failed apocalyptic population-growth predictions with current fears of population implosion, low fertility and declining reproductive capacity associated with figures such as Elon Musk and Shanna Swan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

印度案例提醒我们，fertility decline 可能同时来自农业与经济变化、sterilisation policies、maternal/infant health 改善及家庭选择；不能用单一原因解释。课堂 population game 与 country-policy discussion 要求根据不同人口结构选择政策，而不是先假定“人口越少/越多越好”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
India's fertility decline reflects agricultural and economic change, sterilisation policies, improvements in maternal and infant health, and household choice. The population game therefore asks students to choose policy for a specific demographic structure rather than assume that either more or fewer people is always desirable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Topic Listening：Demographic Timebomb

*Topic Listening: Demographic Timebomb*

22:51 的 documentary 分三部分：Bloomberg economists Jamie Murray 与 Mike McDonough 讨论大型经济体；Stephen Chan 从外交与学术角度评论；第三部分观察德国汽车业如何适应老年员工，以及意大利南部的人口流动。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The 22-minute-51-second documentary combines Bloomberg economists Jamie Murray and Mike McDonough, commentary from Stephen Chan, and cases on how the German car industry adapts to older workers and how population moves through southern Italy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

观看时必须回答：demographics 为什么重要；正在怎样改变；aging 的经济后果；哪些国家接近 demographic sweet spot、哪些国家令人担忧；政府必须作出什么选择；China、Japan、Germany 面临什么挑战及如何回应；Daimler 与 Satriano 如何适应；影片如何结论。听后用笔记画 population mind map，并讨论自己国家的主要挑战与政府回应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The listening questions cover why demographics matter, current change, economic consequences, demographic “sweet spots” and risks, government choices, responses in China, Japan and Germany, the adaptations of Daimler and Satriano, and the documentary's conclusion. The follow-up is a population mind map and a country-specific policy discussion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Topic Reading：Nersisyan, Liu & Wray（2023）

*Topic Reading: Nersisyan, Liu and Wray (2023)*

文章的核心论点是：老龄化确实会增加照护与消费需求，但真正约束是未来社会能生产多少 real goods and services、有多少 workers、生产率多高，以及如何分配产出；对拥有货币发行能力的政府，把问题说成 Social Security “没钱/破产”会混淆金融账户与资源能力。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paper argues that ageing raises care and consumption needs, but the real constraint is future production, labour, productivity and distribution. For a currency-issuing government, framing Social Security as “running out of money” confuses financial accounts with the economy's capacity to provide real resources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

China 被称为 canary in the coal mine：one-child policy 造成 4:2:1 家庭结构，一个孩子可能面对两位父母和四位祖辈；old-age dependency 上升，但 youth dependency 同时下降，生产率增长可能抵消较少 workers。Japan、South Korea 和 Europe 也面对低生育与老龄化；美国的 total dependency ratio 预计仍低于 1965 峰值。因此不能只看老年人口比例，必须同时看儿童负担、劳动参与与生产率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
China is the “canary in the coal mine”: the one-child policy created a 4:2:1 family structure in which one child may support two parents and four grandparents. Yet rising old-age dependency coincides with falling youth dependency, and productivity may offset fewer workers. Japan, South Korea, Europe and the United States likewise require analysis of total dependency, labour participation and productivity rather than the elderly share alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政策重点：投资 physical/human capital 与 productivity；建设 health、elder-care 与公共服务能力；通过 immigration 和较高 labor-force participation 缓解 worker shortage；公平配置成本。Austerity 或削减公共福利不会消灭真实负担，只会把它转移给家庭，尤其是女性与低收入群体。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recommended responses include investment in physical and human capital, productivity, health and elder care, immigration, labour-force participation and equitable public provision. Austerity does not remove the real burden; it shifts it onto households, especially women and lower-income groups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 阅读任务与 15 个核心问题

阅读前检查 title prediction、publication date、publisher reputation、author credibility 与 references。读 abstract 后找被用作证据的国家、突出的问题、authors' argument、说服力与其乐观/悲观取向；再按 headings 预测全文。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pre-reading checks the title, date, publisher, author credibility and references. Abstract work identifies the countries used as evidence, the highlighted problems, the authors' argument, its persuasiveness and its optimistic or pessimistic stance, followed by prediction from the section headings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正文问题覆盖：各国对 aging 的首要担忧；为何用 resource provision 而非 finance framing；低 fertility/人口停止增长的潜在好处；China 的警示趋势、2035 影响与 one-child policy；China 与 Europe 的相似点；Japan/South Korea 的挑战；美国世纪末结构；Social Security sustainability；政策建议与 physical/human capital；“silver tsunami”；4:2:1 effect；immigration 的作用。另有六幅 figures 描述任务、可复用 vocabulary 收集，以及从一段正文观察 articles、prepositions、tenses 或 complex sentences 的 grammar task。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The fifteen main questions cover ageing concerns, resource rather than finance framing, possible benefits of lower fertility, China's warning trend and one-child legacy, comparisons with Europe, Japan and South Korea, the future US population, Social Security sustainability, policy and capital investment, the “silver tsunami”, the 4:2:1 effect and immigration. Additional tasks describe six figures, collect vocabulary and analyse one grammar feature in context.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Academic Interaction：Ezeh, Bongaarts & Mberu（2012）

*Academic Interaction: Ezeh, Bongaarts and Mberu (2012)*

文章区分四类 population growth：超过 2% 的最贫困国家/撒哈拉以南非洲；1–2% 的 India、Indonesia、North Africa 与 western Latin America；0–1% 的先进经济体及 China、Brazil；以及 Eastern Europe、Japan 和部分 Western Europe 的下降。Rapid growth 会压迫教育、医疗、就业、基础设施、经济与环境；voluntary family planning 是重要回应。Low/negative growth 则带来 aging、pensions、health-care cost、labour shortage 与 slower growth。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paper distinguishes growth above 2% in the poorest and many sub-Saharan countries, 1–2% in countries such as India and Indonesia, 0–1% in advanced economies plus China and Brazil, and decline in eastern Europe, Japan and parts of western Europe. Rapid growth strains services, jobs, infrastructure, economies and environments; low or negative growth raises ageing, pension, health, labour and growth challenges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Population forecasts 对 fertility assumptions 极度敏感；撒哈拉以南非洲可能翻倍，而 below-replacement advanced economies 面临收缩。政策必须随 context 改变，并在 reproductive choice、国家目标和 welfare 之间处理伦理张力。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Population forecasts are highly sensitive to fertility assumptions. Sub-Saharan Africa may double, while below-replacement advanced economies face contraction. Policy must therefore vary by context and balance reproductive choice, national objectives and welfare.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Academic Interaction 继续采用 30 分钟讨论、self/group reflection、teacher feedback 和角色轮换；本周要为下一周 climate text 独立准备。重点是让 evidence、反例、clarification 与 group synthesis 进入讨论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The seminar retains the 30-minute discussion, reflection, teacher feedback and role rotation. Preparation now shifts to the next climate text, with evidence, counterexamples, clarification and group synthesis expected in the discussion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Discussion & Debate

*Discussion and Debate*

### Balloon debate

每组 5–6 个 characters，各用 1–2 分钟说服 audience 自己应留在 balloon，之后 Q&A，再按 persuasiveness 而非 likability 投票。可加入 rebuttal 与 audience defence。成功要素是明确价值、具体证据、预判反对意见和对他人发言的回应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the balloon debate, five or six characters each make a one- or two-minute case, answer questions, and are judged on persuasiveness rather than likability. Rebuttals and audience defence reward clear value, concrete evidence, anticipation of objections and direct response to other speakers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Pyramid discussion

先个人选择，再由 pair、four、eight、whole class 逐层合并，最终从 16 项能力中选出 LSE international student 最重要的三项。每一轮都必须说明保留/放弃标准，让共识的形成过程可解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The pyramid discussion moves from individual choice to pairs, groups of four, groups of eight and the whole class, ending with three priorities from sixteen international-student skills. Each stage must explain the criteria for keeping or dropping an item so that consensus remains reasoned.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Pronunciation Session 3：Connected Speech

*Pronunciation Session 3: connected speech*

Connected speech 的变化不是“错误”，而是自然语速为了更省力、更有 rhythm 的系统性调整。五类核心现象如下：

- **Elision**：省略音或音节，尤其是弱化 schwa、`/t/ /d/ /h/`，如 `Chris(t)mas`、`mus(t) go`、`san(d)wich`、`tell (h)im`、`wan(t) to → wanna`。
- **Geminates**：相邻词以同一 consonant 收尾/开头时不重复爆破，而是稍微延长共享音，如 *black coffee*；`/tʃ/` 与 `/dʒ/` 相邻时仍分开发音。
- **Intrusion**：vowel-to-vowel 连接时加入 `/r/ /j/ /w/`，如 *four apples*、*three oranges*、*two olives*；英式非卷舌口音中 `r` 结尾词在后接 vowel 时出现 linking `/r/`，也可能出现拼写中没有 `r` 的 intrusive `/r/`。
- **Catenation**：consonant-ending word 后接 vowel 时，consonant 仿佛黏到下一个词，如 `an egg → a-neg`、`pick it up`、`not at all`。
- **Assimilation**：相邻 consonants 融合或向较容易的口型靠拢，如 `could you /ˈkʊdʒə/`、`don't you`，以及 `/n/` 在 `/b/ /p/` 前趋近 `/m/`。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Connected speech makes natural-speed English easier and more rhythmic through elision, held geminates, vowel-linking intrusion, consonant-to-vowel catenation and assimilation. These are systematic pronunciation patterns rather than careless errors, but assimilation should be practised at natural speed so that it does not sound like a mispronounced isolated word.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
