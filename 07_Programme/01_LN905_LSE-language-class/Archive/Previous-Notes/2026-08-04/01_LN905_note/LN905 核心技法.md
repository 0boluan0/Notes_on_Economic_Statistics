# LN905 核心技法

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> LN905 真正反复训练的是四次转换：**听懂并筛选 → 写成 critical summary；读三篇材料 → 围绕问题综合成论证；把观点组织成可互动的口头表达；把研究问题、文献和证据写成完整 academic essay。** 四部分共用同一条原则：先判断任务，再选择信息；用自己的结构统领来源；每个 claim 都要有 reason/evidence；评价必须指向具体证据、范围或限制。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> LN905 repeatedly trains four transformations: listening and selecting into a critical summary; synthesising three readings into an argument; organising ideas for interactive spoken delivery; and turning a research question, literature and evidence into an academic essay. All four require task analysis, selective use of information, writer-led structure, claim-reason-evidence development, and evaluation tied to specific evidence, scope or limitations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 四部分怎样连起来

*How the four components connect*

| 课程部分 | 输入 | 最终产出 | 核心难点 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Listening into Writing | 20–25 分钟 live lecture | 40 分钟内写 200–400 词 critical summary | 一次听取时分清主点、证据、例子和立场 |
| Speaking / Academic Interaction | 问题、文本和同伴发言 | 1–2 分钟 presentation 或持续 group discussion | 有结构地说，并真正回应、追问和推进他人观点 |
| Reading into Writing | 三篇 adapted extracts + essay question | 两小时内约 600 词 response | 按自己的论点跨文选材和 synthesis，而非逐篇摘要 |
| Academic Writing | 自定问题 + 至少六篇 academic references | 2,000 词 assignment | 从审题、thesis、段落到引证形成一条完整论证线 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four components differ in input and output but share one architecture. Listening requires real-time selection; speaking requires structured contribution and interaction; Reading into Writing requires cross-text synthesis for a question; Academic Writing extends the same process into an independently researched essay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Listening into Writing

*Listening into Writing*

> [!source] 本节依据
> [[07_Programme/01_LN905_LSE-language-class/PDF/03_Paper-A-Listening-into-Writing/Listening into Writing Introduction.pdf|Listening into Writing Introduction]] · [[07_Programme/01_LN905_LSE-language-class/PDF/01_Assessment/Marking-Criteria/Paper A_Marking Criteria_Listening into Writing.pdf|Paper A marking criteria]] · [[Week 2-4 - Paper A and B AI Feedback#Paper A：Listening into Writing|AI feedback workflow]]

### 任务本质

Paper A 不是听写。高分要求同时完成五件事：内容准确；不遗漏 key points；detail/depth 合适；成功 paraphrase；在 200–400 词内形成清楚、连贯且带有支持的 critical comments 的 summary。Listening 占 Paper A 的主要权重，因此“写得漂亮但听错内容”不会被语言质量挽救。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Paper A is not dictation. A strong response reports the lecture accurately, covers the key points at suitable depth, paraphrases successfully, and produces a coherent 200–400-word summary with supported critical comment. Since listening carries most of the paper's weight, elegant prose cannot compensate for inaccurate content.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 听前：把纸变成筛选器

- 留一条宽 margin；主体区域记录 lecture，margin 留给听后压缩、重命名与补充。
- 预先固定少量符号，例如 `C` claim、`R` reason、`E` evidence、`Ex` example、`Q` qualification、`?` 不确定；不要临场发明复杂体系。
- 先记结构，不追完整句。特别留意 signposts：`three main ways`、`move on to`、`however`、`for example`、`in conclusion`。
- 预留 speaker/source 位置。谁提出观点、是否引用他人、是否把争议观点当例子，都会影响准确性和后续评价。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Create a wide margin for later compression and use a small, stable symbol set for claims, reasons, evidence, examples, qualifications and uncertainty. Listen first for structure and signposts rather than complete sentences, and record who owns each claim so that a contentious example is not mistakenly reported as the lecturer's factual position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 听中：只抓能进入 summary 的层级

把内容分成四层：lecture 的 central argument；支撑它的 main points；真正能证明 main point 的 evidence；只用于解释的 examples。优先记录前两层，再选最有区分度的 evidence；重复、轶事、铺垫和你已经理解的细节可以放掉。任何数字、因果方向、比较对象和限定词都要特别准确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separate the central argument, main supporting points, evidence and illustrative examples. Capture the first two levels before selecting the most diagnostic evidence. Repetition, anecdotes and expendable detail can be released, while numbers, causal direction, comparison groups and qualifiers demand exact attention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 最常见的听力错误
> 讲者说 “some critics claim X” 时，不能写成 “the lecturer claims X”；举例不等于结论；一个 vivid detail 不等于 key point；没有听清的数字不能靠常识补全。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Do not convert “some critics claim X” into “the lecturer claims X”, confuse an example with a conclusion, promote a vivid detail into a key point, or reconstruct an unclear number from background knowledge.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 听后：先建图，再写句子

先用 margin 给每组笔记写一个 3–7 词标签，然后做三件事：删重复；把 evidence 挂回对应 claim；圈出不能丢的 key points。接着判断结构：lecture 本身逻辑很清楚时用 **linear summary**；原讲座跳跃或多个主题交叉时用 **thematic summary**，重新按主题组织。不要一边辨认笔记一边直接写正文。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Label each note cluster in the margin, remove repetition, attach evidence to the claim it supports, and identify the indispensable points. Use a linear summary when the lecture has a clear logical sequence; use a thematic summary when the material is fragmented or overlapping. Build this map before drafting prose.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 写作骨架

一个稳妥的 critical summary 可以这样组织：开头一句交代 speaker、topic 与 central argument；中间按 2–4 个 main points 展开，每一点用 reporting language 标清 lecture content；在最适合的位置加入一两句 evaluation；结尾只在需要时概括 implication，不另起与 lecture 无关的个人观点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A reliable critical summary identifies the speaker, topic and central argument, develops two to four main points with clear reporting language, integrates one or two evaluations where the evidence permits, and closes with an implication only when useful. It should not end with an unrelated personal opinion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Reporting 和 evaluation 必须分得开

- Reporting：`The speaker argues…`、`According to X…`、`The lecture identifies…`
- Evaluation：`This is persuasive because…`、`The evidence is limited to…`、`The claim requires qualification because…`
- 可评价的对象：source credibility/bias、central argument、evidence quality、claim-evidence 之间的 warrant、time/place/population scope、rebuttal 与 implication。
- Critical comment 必须与被评价的 lecture point 放在一起，并说明为什么；`This is convincing` 单独出现仍只是空判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reporting language attributes lecture content; evaluative language judges it. Evaluation may address credibility, bias, evidence, the warrant between evidence and claim, scope, rebuttal or implication. It must be placed beside the relevant lecture point and explain why; “This is convincing” alone is unsupported assertion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Paraphrase 的硬检查

Paper A 的最高档明确要求：不能连续复制 lecture 的五个词，即 **最多只允许四个连续原词**。这不是鼓励机械换同义词；真正的 paraphrase 要同时改变词汇、句法和信息包装，同时保留技术术语、因果方向与原意。完成一段后，用笔记反查是否无意保留了 speaker 的整块 phrasing。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The top Paper A band permits no more than four consecutive words copied from the lecture. This is not a synonym-substitution rule: genuine paraphrase changes wording, syntax and information packaging while preserving technical terms, causal direction and meaning. Check each paragraph against the notes for accidental phrase copying.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 交卷前检查

- 每个 statement 都能在 notes 中找到依据。
- 所有 key points 都出现， supporting detail 没有挤掉主点。
- 200–400 词；不是过长的 lecture reconstruction，也不是过短的 outline。
- Speaker voice 与 writer evaluation 清楚分开。
- Critical comments 有 evidence、qualification 或理由，不 overgeneralise。
- 段落顺序清楚，cohesive devices 表示真实关系，而不是机械堆叠 `moreover`。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before submission, verify every statement against the notes, cover all key points at appropriate depth, remain within 200–400 words, distinguish speaker and writer voices, support and qualify evaluation, and use paragraphing and cohesive devices to express real relationships.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Speaking、Listening 与 Academic Interaction

*Speaking, Listening and Academic Interaction*

> [!note] 名称说明
> Moodle 没有单独名为 “Speaking into Writing” 的 section。这里按你的学习目标，把 Paper C: Speaking & Listening、Academic Interaction 和 Discussion & Debate 合在一起，并保留它们怎样为 Reading into Writing 产生 argument、synthesis、evaluation 与 language material。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Moodle has no separate section titled “Speaking into Writing”. This section combines Paper C, Academic Interaction, and Discussion and Debate, with particular attention to how oral work generates arguments, synthesis, evaluation and useful language for Reading into Writing.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!source] 本节依据
> [[07_Programme/01_LN905_LSE-language-class/PDF/01_Assessment/Marking-Criteria/Paper C_Marking Criteria_Speaking and Listening.pdf|Paper C criteria]] · [[07_Programme/01_LN905_LSE-language-class/PDF/05_Paper-C-Speaking-and-Listening/2-minute-presentations/Week One_LN905.pdf|Two-minute presentation structures]] · [[07_Programme/01_LN905_LSE-language-class/PDF/05_Paper-C-Speaking-and-Listening/Presentation Guide - Timestamped Transcript.pdf|Presentation Guide]] · [[07_Programme/01_LN905_LSE-language-class/PDF/06_Academic-Interaction/Academic Interaction.pdf|Academic Interaction introduction]] · [[07_Programme/01_LN905_LSE-language-class/PDF/06_Academic-Interaction/Academic Interaction Roles.pdf|Roles]]

### Paper C 在评什么

Interview 有三部分：general questions、1–2 分钟 short presentation、in-depth questions。Speaking 看 fluency & clarity、accuracy、structure、impact；Listening 看 comprehension 与 interaction。高分不是“一个人说得最多”，而是表达清楚、有深度、能听懂并有效回应问题、会澄清、会建设性地处理 agreement/disagreement，并让 group discussion 继续前进。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Paper C contains general questions, a one- to two-minute presentation and in-depth questions. Speaking is judged on fluency, clarity, accuracy, structure and impact; listening on comprehension and interaction. High performance means clear, developed ideas together with effective response, clarification, constructive agreement and disagreement, and contribution to the group's progress—not simply speaking the most.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 两分钟 presentation：三种结构

| 结构 | 骨架 | 优点 | 风险 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Justification | claim → 2–3 reasons + evidence → re-assert claim | 立场直接、容易说清 | 不处理 counterargument |
| Synthesis | position A → position B → weigh/qualify → conclusion | 展示完整 debate 和比较能力 | 容易变成两边复述、立场出现太晚 |
| Rebuttal | opposing position → fair account → rebuttal → own argument | 说服力强、适合回应题干 | 容易制造 straw man |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Justification is direct but can ignore counterarguments. Synthesis demonstrates command of the debate but may become descriptive or delay the speaker's position. Rebuttal can be persuasive only when it represents the opposing case fairly and avoids a straw man.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两分钟内，通常只够一个明确 claim、两个充分展开的 reasons、各自的 evidence/example、一个必要 qualification，以及一句 conclusion。宁可完整发展两个点，也不要报出五个没有解释的理由。开头立即回答问题；结尾重新回答，不只是说 `That is all`。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two minutes normally allow one clear claim, two developed reasons with evidence or examples, one necessary qualification, and a conclusion. Two completed points are stronger than five undeveloped reasons. Answer the question immediately and answer it again in the conclusion rather than merely announcing the end.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 能直接调用的口头功能

- Claim：`From my perspective…`、`I would argue that…`
- Reason/evidence：`This is due to…`、`A clear example would be…`、`This is supported by…`
- Contrast：`By contrast…`、`Those in favour… whereas critics…`
- Partial rebuttal：`There is some truth in…, but…`、`Although this is persuasive, it overlooks…`
- Balance：`On balance…`、`Having weighed both sides…`
- Emphasis/summary：`What is especially important is…`、`To sum up…`

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Build a compact repertoire for claims, reasons and evidence, contrast, partial rebuttal, balance, emphasis and summary. The function matters more than reciting many memorised phrases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Seminar 的目标不是轮流 mini-presentation

Academic Interaction 要 co-create shared understanding。Leader 维持方向并用 open questions 邀请发言；Connector 把不同课堂文本与现实连接起来；Evaluator 检查 citation、currency、methodology、sample、generalisability 与 disagreement；Highlighter 抽取可用于写作的 academic/discipline language；Contextualiser 补背景；Visualiser 把数据和关系变成图；Summariser 在需要时恢复主线。角色是 contribution prompt，不是边界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Academic Interaction aims to co-create understanding rather than stage consecutive mini-presentations. The roles prompt different contributions: facilitation, connection and synthesis, evaluation, useful language, context, visual representation and timely summary. They guide contribution without restricting it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 获得、保持并交出 floor

- 获得：接住上一句，而非重启话题：`Can I pick up on what you said about…?`
- 保持：先说 point，再 reason/example；被打断后用 `The point I am trying to make is…` 恢复。
- 澄清：听不懂就问 `Do you mean…?` 或 `Can you be more specific?`，不要假装理解。
- 推进：追问 why/how/example，比较两种观点，连接较早的 comment，引入新 perspective。
- 交出：用问题邀请另一人，尤其是 quieter participant；阶段性 summarise 后再转 topic。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gain the floor by linking to the previous contribution, hold it through a point followed by reason or example, seek clarification rather than pretend understanding, move the discussion through probing and connecting, and hand the floor to another participant—especially a quieter one—after a brief summary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 口头讨论怎样转成写作材料

讨论后不要保留流水账；只提取四类东西：可以直接成为 paragraph claim 的判断；两篇以上来源之间的 agreement/disagreement；Evaluator 发现的 limitation/qualification；Highlighter 找到的可复用 reporting/evaluative language。这样 Academic Interaction 才真正为 Friday Reading into Writing 降低审题和构段负担。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After discussion, retain only paragraph-ready claims, cross-source agreements or disagreements, limitations or qualifications identified by evaluation, and reusable reporting or evaluative language. This converts oral interaction into material that reduces the planning burden for Friday Reading into Writing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Delivery 诀窍

控制 speed、room-appropriate volume、tone 与 hesitation；不要背逐字 script，因为忘词会增加填充音并破坏 eye contact。关键词、人名、地名和 theory 要提前查 pronunciation。语言以清楚为先，复杂结构只有在能稳定控制时才使用。Signposting 告诉听众接下来是什么，linking words 不必每句都放。Slides 只留 key points，字号和颜色必须从远处可读，最后一页给 references；notes 同样只写关键词。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regulate speed, volume, tone and hesitation. Avoid memorising a full script because recall failure increases fillers and destroys eye contact. Check the pronunciation of key terms and names, prefer controlled clarity to unstable complexity, use signposting selectively, and keep slides and notes to readable key points with a final reference slide.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Reading into Writing

*Reading into Writing*

> [!source] 本节依据
> [[07_Programme/01_LN905_LSE-language-class/PDF/04_Paper-B-Reading-into-Writing/Reading into Writing.pdf|Reading into Writing Introduction]] · [[07_Programme/01_LN905_LSE-language-class/PDF/01_Assessment/Marking-Criteria/Paper B_Marking Criteria_Reading into Writing (1).pdf|Current Paper B criteria]] · [[Week 2-4 - Paper A and B AI Feedback#Paper B：Reading into Writing|AI feedback workflow]]

### 任务本质

Paper B 给三篇 adapted extracts 和一个 essay question，两小时内写约 600 词。Reading 分看 text use、synthesis、selection、paraphrasing；Writing 分看 task fulfilment、structure、language style & range、language accuracy。关键不是证明自己读了三篇，而是把三篇中有用的内容改造成自己回答问题所需的证据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Paper B provides three adapted extracts and an essay question for a roughly 600-word response in two hours. Reading is judged through text use, synthesis, selection and paraphrasing; writing through task fulfilment, structure, stylistic range and accuracy. The aim is not to display coverage of three texts but to adapt useful material into evidence for the answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 第一步：先拆 question

在读材料前写下：task verb 要求 position、comparison、evaluation、cause 还是 solution；问题有几个 parts、权重是否相同；time/place/population 等 restrictions；哪些 contested terms 必须定义。若 question 没给 scope 或 structure，就由你主动限定并提供。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before reading, identify the task verb, the number and weight of question parts, restrictions such as time, place or population, and contested terms that need definition. When the question supplies no scope or structure, the writer must create them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 第二步：把三篇文献压成 source map

每篇只记录五项：main claim；最有用的 evidence；scope；limitation；它在你的答案中能做什么（support / challenge / qualify / example）。然后横向找 common themes、agreements、disagreements、不同 mechanisms 和不同适用范围。不要按 Text 1 → Text 2 → Text 3 的顺序开始写。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reduce each text to its main claim, strongest usable evidence, scope, limitation and role in the answer—support, challenge, qualification or example. Then compare across texts for common themes, disagreement, mechanisms and scope. Do not draft in Text 1–Text 2–Text 3 order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 第三步：先形成自己的 thesis 和 paragraph jobs

Thesis 必须回答 question，并说明关键 qualification；paragraph plan 要按 argument jobs 排列，例如定义/机制、主要支持、counterargument/limitation、综合判断。每段先写一句 writer-led topic sentence，再决定哪两篇或三篇材料能共同完成这项工作。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The thesis must answer the question and state the decisive qualification. Plan paragraphs by argumentative function—such as definition or mechanism, main support, counterargument or limitation, and overall judgement. Begin each paragraph with a writer-led topic sentence, then select the sources that jointly perform that job.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 高分 paragraph 的内部结构

`Writer's claim → Source A evidence → relation to Source B/C → explanation of why the relation matters → evaluation/qualification → link back to question.` 来源不是段落主角；作者名不能代替 topic sentence。Quotation 后必须解释 `In other words…` 或说明其作用，不能让引语自行得出结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strong paragraph moves from the writer's claim to evidence from one source, relates it to other sources, explains why that relationship matters, evaluates or qualifies it, and reconnects to the question. Author names should not replace the topic sentence, and quotations require interpretation rather than being left to make the point alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Synthesis 的可见证据

- 同一段出现多个来源，但它们围绕一个 shared issue，而不是排队介绍。
- 使用 generalisations，如 `scholars`、`recent research`、`the literature`。
- 必要时用 clustered citations 表示多来源共同支持一个命题。
- 混合 integral/narrative 与 non-integral/parenthetical references，避免每句都从作者名开头。
- 用 `similarly`、`by contrast`、`however`、`consequently` 表明真实关系。
- 强 topic sentence 与 concluding/link sentence 把 writer's voice 放在段落两端。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Visible synthesis includes several sources organised around one shared issue, appropriate generalisations and clustered citations, a mix of narrative and parenthetical referencing, connectors that express real relationships, and writer-led topic and linking sentences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Quote、paraphrase、summary 怎样分工

Quote 只留给 definition、必须精确的 wording 或特别有分析价值的短语；paraphrase 用于保留具体 reasoning/evidence；summary 用于压缩整段或整篇的方向。高分不是“全部 paraphrase”，而是三种方式都准确、目的清楚，并与自己的论证融合。只换同义词、保留原句结构仍有 plagiarism risk。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quote definitions or uniquely valuable wording, paraphrase specific reasoning or evidence, and summarise broader direction. Strong work uses all three accurately and purposefully within its own argument. Synonym substitution that preserves source structure remains a plagiarism risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Citation 诀窍

首次看到 source details 就确定三种短格式：`Author (Year) argues…`、`… (Author, Year)`、多作者的 `et al.` 形式。若只在所读文本中看到另一研究，而没读 original，优先不用；必须使用时标成 secondary reference，例如 `Beyens et al. (2021, cited in Lee & Hancock, 2024)`，不能假装读过原文。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Prepare narrative, parenthetical and `et al.` citation forms as soon as source details are available. Avoid secondary citation when possible; when the original has not been read, state this explicitly with a form such as “Beyens et al. (2021, cited in Lee & Hancock, 2024)”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 常见失分模式

- 回答了主题，却没有精确回答 question 的 task/restriction。
- 三段分别总结三篇，缺少 common themes 和 cross-text links。
- 为了“用到所有文献”塞入无关信息，selection 变差。
- 有 citation 但 source content 没被改造成自己的 argument。
- 评价只写 `weak/unconvincing`，没有 method、evidence、scope 或 warrant。
- Cohesive words 很多，但 paragraph progression 不清楚。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Marks fall when the response misses the task or restriction, summarises sources separately, includes irrelevant material for coverage, cites without adapting evidence to the argument, evaluates without criteria, or uses many connectors without logical progression.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 交卷前检查

- Thesis 直接回答 question；每段都能说明自己在回答的哪一部分。
- 三篇材料都经过 selection，而不是机械平均分配篇幅。
- 至少有清楚的 cross-text agreement、difference 或 qualification。
- 每个 source use 都有正确 attribution，quotation/paraphrase/summary 边界清楚。
- Writer's voice 统领 topic sentence、evaluation 和 paragraph conclusion。
- 约 600 词，结构完整；语言 range 不以牺牲 accuracy 和 clarity 为代价。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before submission, verify a question-answering thesis, a clear job for every paragraph, selective rather than equal source coverage, explicit cross-text relationships, correct attribution, writer-led evaluation and conclusions, a complete structure near 600 words, and language range that does not sacrifice accuracy or clarity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Academic Writing

### 与 IELTS essay 的根本区别

LSE academic essay 必须有清楚 thesis、academic literature、分析性结构，以及被 explanation/evidence 支持的 argument；重点是 depth，不是列出很多优缺点。LN905 assignment 要求约 2,000 词、至少六篇 academic references、introduction、清楚段落、conclusion 与独立 bibliography page。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An LSE academic essay requires a clear thesis, academic literature, analytical structure, and arguments supported by explanation and evidence. Depth matters more than listing many advantages and disadvantages. The LN905 assignment is approximately 2,000 words with at least six academic references, an introduction, structured paragraphs, a conclusion and a separate bibliography.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 选题：先排除不能写好的问题

好问题同时满足 feasibility、interest、field relevance 与 literature availability。避免五类问题：过窄到只能讲一个事件；过宽到无法在 2,000 词内形成判断；只要求 description；关键术语 ambiguous；只测试 knowledge recall。Case study 应服务更大的 analytical claim，不能让案例本身吞掉问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A viable question combines feasibility, interest, disciplinary relevance and available literature. Reject questions that are too narrow, too broad, merely descriptive, ambiguously worded or based only on recall. A case study should illustrate a broader analytical claim rather than become the entire question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 审题：四格法

1. **Task**：要 position、solution、evaluation、cause、compare 还是其他动作？
2. **Structure**：有几个 parts，是否等权，题干是否暗示顺序？
3. **Restriction**：time、place、case、theory 或 perspective 有何限制；没有时如何主动限定？
4. **Definition**：哪些 contested terms 会改变答案，必须先界定？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Analyse every question through four lenses: the required task, the number and weight of parts, explicit or self-imposed restrictions, and contested terms whose definition changes the answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Introduction：三个 moves

Introduction 通常约占全文 10%，从较广 context/gap/issue 逐步收窄到自己的答案。三个 moves 是：说明 academic/real-world context 与为什么重要；给出 concise、specific、focused thesis；说明 approach/structure 和将使用的 theory/source/case。`This essay will discuss…` 不是 thesis，因为它只描述动作，不回答问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An introduction is often around ten per cent of the essay and narrows from context, gap or issue to the answer. Its three moves establish importance, state a concise and focused thesis, and indicate the approach, structure or concepts used. “This essay will discuss…” describes an activity rather than answering the question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Thesis 的三个陷阱

- **Non-thesis thesis**：只说将讨论 advantages/disadvantages，没有 position。
- **Overly broad thesis**：立场太大，没有 condition、mechanism 或 justification。
- **No-structure thesis**：有立场，却不告诉读者哪些 reasons 将承担论证。

一个可用 thesis 通常包含 `answer + decisive reason/mechanism + qualification/scope`，必要时再简短暗示 paragraph route。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Avoid a non-thesis that only announces discussion, an overly broad position without mechanism or qualification, and a thesis that gives no route through the argument. A usable thesis normally combines an answer, decisive reason or mechanism, and qualification or scope, with a brief structural cue when useful.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Body paragraph：让 writer's voice 先出现

稳妥顺序是：topic sentence 提出本段自己的 claim；解释 reason/mechanism；用一篇或多篇来源的 evidence 支持；比较或评价 evidence；用 concluding/link sentence 说明本段怎样推进 thesis。不要用 `Smith (2020) argues…` 直接取代 topic sentence，也不要把 paragraph 写成 citation chain。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strong body paragraph begins with the writer's claim, explains the reason or mechanism, supports it with evidence from one or more sources, compares or evaluates that evidence, and closes by showing how the paragraph advances the thesis. An author citation should not replace the topic sentence or turn the paragraph into a citation chain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Sources：search、evaluate、adapt

用 LSE Library、subject databases、programme/department reading lists 和 Google Scholar 找文献；先按 department 要求选 style，未指定时保持一种格式全篇一致。CRAAP 用 Currency、Relevance、Authority、Accuracy、Purpose 检查来源。Annotated bibliography 不是 summary list：每篇 100–300 词可记录 author authority、main claim、methodology、relevance、strengths、weaknesses、limitations 与能怎样服务 essay。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Search through the LSE Library, subject databases, programme and departmental reading lists, and Google Scholar. Follow the department's referencing style or apply one style consistently. Use CRAAP to assess currency, relevance, authority, accuracy and purpose. An annotated bibliography should evaluate authority, claim, methodology, relevance, strengths, weaknesses, limitations and intended use rather than merely summarise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Paraphrase 和 quotation

合格 paraphrase 同时改变 vocabulary、sentence structure 与 information packaging，并保留原意、技术术语和 citation；只替换同义词或剪贴后改几处仍是 plagiarism。Quotation 只用于 definition、精确措辞或独特短语；短引语嵌入句子，长引语按格式缩进。每个 quote 后都要解释其意义或用途。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Genuine paraphrase changes vocabulary, structure and information packaging while preserving meaning, technical terminology and citation. Synonym replacement or lightly edited copying remains plagiarism. Quote only definitions, exact wording or distinctive phrases, format quotations appropriately, and explain the function of every quotation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Reporting verbs 和 citation 位置

Reporting verbs 同时表达 source action 与 writer stance，且语法搭配不同；`argue/claim/suggest/demonstrate/find` 不能只按“高级程度”互换。Integral/narrative citation 把 author 放入句法，适合强调谁提出观点；non-integral/parenthetical citation 把 evidence 放在前景，适合 writer-led synthesis。两种形式要按功能混合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reporting verbs encode both source action and writer stance and take different grammatical patterns; they are not interchangeable upgrades in formality. Narrative citation foregrounds the author, while parenthetical citation foregrounds the claim or evidence. Mix them according to purpose.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Conclusion：不只是换词重复 introduction

Conclusion 先重新回答 question/restate thesis；再综合 main argument，说明各点如何共同支持 thesis，而不是逐段复述；最后可给 implication、recommendation、prediction 或 future research。不要在结尾突然加入正文没有论证的新 claim。Introduction 与 conclusion 应有清楚对应，但 conclusion 必须体现论证后得到的更具体判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A conclusion restates the answer, synthesises how the main points jointly support it, and may close with an implication, recommendation, prediction or future research. It should not introduce an unsupported new claim. It must echo the introduction while expressing the more specific judgement earned by the argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Academic style：清楚优先

`I` 是否可用取决于 discipline；不用 `I` 时可写 `This paper argues…`，但避免没有 agent 的 `It is argued…`。Active/passive 由要强调 subject 还是 object 决定，不是 passive 越多越学术。避免 idioms、proverbs、slang、contractions 和随意 abbreviation；acronym 第一次写全称。不要为“高级”使用不确定的 synonym：准确、清楚、可控比长词重要。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use of `I` depends on discipline; `This paper argues…` is a clear alternative, while agentless `It is argued…` may hide ownership. Choose active or passive voice according to focus, not prestige. Avoid idioms, proverbs, slang, contractions and unexplained abbreviations, expand acronyms on first use, and prefer precise controlled language to uncertain sophistication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 提交前的结构检查

- 问题的每一部分都被回答，thesis 与 conclusion 对应。
- 每段只有一个主要 job，topic sentence 和 link sentence 都由 writer's voice 控制。
- 每个 major claim 有 explanation/evidence；每个 citation 都真正服务该 claim。
- 多来源之间有 synthesis，不是 author-by-author summary。
- Quote、paraphrase、summary 与 secondary citation 边界清楚。
- 格式包含清楚字体（11/12）、1.5/double spacing、段间距、justified text、page numbers、cover sheet 与独立 bibliography。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before submission, check full task coverage, alignment between thesis and conclusion, one clear job per paragraph, evidence for every major claim, purposeful citations, cross-source synthesis, accurate source boundaries, and the required presentation format, cover sheet and bibliography.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*One-page master check*

| 检查问题 | Listening into Writing | Speaking / Interaction | Reading into Writing | Academic Writing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 我是否直接完成 task？ | 覆盖 lecture key points | 立即回答 prompt 并保持相关 | 回答 essay question 所有部分 | 回答自定问题而非介绍主题 |
| 谁控制结构？ | 我选择 linear/thematic | 我用 claim/reason/evidence 或 debate structure | 我的 thesis 和 paragraph jobs | 我的 thesis 与 argument route |
| 来源怎样进入？ | speaker attribution + accurate notes | 回应同伴并借用文本证据 | 跨三文 selection/synthesis | 独立检索的 literature synthesis |
| Criticality 在哪里？ | credibility、evidence、warrant、scope | clarification、challenge、qualification | method、scope、cross-text conflict | evidence evaluation、counterargument、implication |
| 最危险的失败 | 听错却写得流畅 | mini-presentations、支配或不回应 | 三篇各写一段、描述性 | 没有 thesis、广而不深、citation chain |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Across all four components, verify task fulfilment, writer- or speaker-controlled structure, purposeful use of sources, evidence-based criticality, and avoidance of the characteristic failure mode for that task.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
