## Academic Writing：引用、改写与学术诚信

*Academic Writing: sources, paraphrasing and academic integrity*

### 找来源与引用体系

- 先从 LSE Library、学科数据库、MSc/departmental reading lists 与 Google Scholar 找文献；Google Scholar 适合发现来源，但最终仍需评价来源质量。
- 优先遵循系里的指定格式；若未指定，就选择一种格式并全篇一致。课程列出的常见体系包括 APA、Chicago 与 OSCOLA，APA 7 在社会科学中常见。
- 可使用 EndNote、Mendeley、Zotero 或 Cite Them Right 管理引用，但软件输出仍要人工检查。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the LSE Library, subject databases, programme and departmental reading lists, and Google Scholar to locate literature. Follow the department's required referencing style; if none is specified, choose one and apply it consistently. Reference managers can help, but their output still requires manual checking.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Plagiarism 与有效 paraphrase

Plagiarism 是在没有适当标明来源的情况下使用他人的已发表或未发表作品，也包括其他学生的作业。合格 paraphrase 要同时满足：意义等价、词汇大部分不同、句子结构不同、来源清楚；必要的学科术语可以保留。只替换几个同义词、保留原句框架，或把原文剪贴后稍作编辑，都不算独立改写。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Plagiarism includes using another person's published or unpublished work, including student work, without proper acknowledgement. A sound paraphrase preserves meaning while changing most wording and sentence structure and crediting the source; necessary technical terms may remain. Synonym substitution or lightly edited copying is not independent paraphrase.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程中的 Wickham 例子区分了几种情况：没有 citation/quotation marks 的照抄是欺骗；有 citation 但复制原词而没有 quotation marks 仍是 plagiarism；quotation 必须准确复现原文，通常还要给页码；真正的 paraphrase 用自己的语言并标明来源。AI 可以帮助核对意义是否等价、结构是否过近、关键术语是否被误改，但不能替你生成 paraphrase。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Wickham examples distinguish unattributed copying, copying with a citation but no quotation marks, exact quotation, and genuine paraphrase. Quotations must reproduce the source accurately and normally include a page number. AI may check equivalence, structural closeness and technical terminology, but it must not write the paraphrase for you.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 什么时候使用 quotation

直接引语适合定义、必须保持精确的措辞、独特或有力量的表达，或短到无法有效改写的 passage；不应因为“尊重名人”、懒得概括或只是陈述普通事实而使用。短引语嵌入句子；长引语通常另起缩进且不加 quotation marks。方括号可标示为语法需要作出的改动，省略号可标示有意义的删节。多数情况下 paraphrase/summary 更能保持自己的 voice 与段落连贯。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quote definitions, wording that must remain exact, distinctive language, or a short passage that cannot be usefully paraphrased. Do not quote merely out of respect, convenience or for routine summary. Integrate short quotations; format long quotations as indented blocks without quotation marks. Brackets show necessary changes and ellipses meaningful omissions. Paraphrase and summary usually preserve the writer's voice more effectively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Reporting verbs

Reporting verbs 同时表达原作者在做什么，以及你对该主张的立场。大多使用 present tense；强、中性、试探性动词不能随意互换，而且语法搭配不同，例如有的接 `that` clause，有的接 noun phrase。选择动词时要同时检查 meaning、strength 与 grammar pattern。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reporting verbs indicate both the source author's action and the writer's stance. They are usually in the present tense, differ in strength, and take different grammatical patterns such as a `that` clause or noun phrase. Choose them for meaning, force and grammar together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Academic Writing：Synthesis

*Academic Writing: synthesis*

Synthesis 是围绕自己的问题和论证，把多个来源的相同点、差异、因果或限制组织成一条 narrative；一段只讲一个来源不是 synthesis。课程总结的七个可见特征是：使用 scholars/researchers 等 generalisation；paraphrase 配 non-integral citation；clustered citations；结果/后果连接；主体用自己的措辞；明确 topic sentence；结尾句概括本段工作。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Synthesis organises agreements, differences, causal links and limitations across sources around the writer's question and argument. A paragraph based on only one source is not synthesis. Visible features include generalisations, paraphrase with non-integral citations, clustered citations, consequence markers, the writer's own wording, a clear topic sentence and a concluding sentence that captures the paragraph's work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

The annotated sequence distinguishes a one-source paragraph from genuine five-source synthesis, contrasts integral and non-integral referencing, shows paraphrase and summary, identifies a strong topic sentence and writer-led opening, and demonstrates why quotations need follow-up comment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Automation debate 的材料提供了可综合的真实分歧：Frey & Osborne 认为 algorithms/robots 正扩展到 non-routine work，约 47% 的美国岗位处于高风险；Spencer 认为在资本主义所有制下，技术可能继续制造工作并恶化工作质量，真正减少和改善工作需要更激进的 ownership change；Ford 则警告自动化可能造成严重失业与需求下行。写作时应呈现它们的机制与适用范围，而不是把三个结论排成清单。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The automation texts form a real debate: Frey and Osborne estimate that algorithms and robots place roughly 47% of US jobs at high risk; Spencer argues that technology under capitalist ownership may sustain work while worsening its quality; Ford warns of severe unemployment and a downward demand spiral. A synthesis should compare their mechanisms and scope rather than list conclusions author by author.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Annotated bibliography

*Annotated bibliography*

### CRAAP 评价

- **Currency**：发表/更新日期是什么，是否仍适用于当前问题？
- **Relevance**：是否真正帮助回答论题；是否有更直接的来源？
- **Authority**：作者、出版者与机构有什么资历？
- **Accuracy**：信息从何而来，是否无明显错误、平衡、可由其他来源印证并提供 references？
- **Purpose**：目的是 inform、sell、persuade、guide 还是 entertain；是否服务某种 agenda；事实与意见如何区分？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CRAAP evaluates currency, relevance, authority, accuracy and purpose: whether a source is current and directly useful, who produced it, how its claims are supported and corroborated, and whether its aim or agenda affects the evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完成顺序：先找到可能相关的来源 → 快速筛选最相关者 → 用规定格式完整引用 → 为每篇写约 100–300 词 annotation。Annotation 可包含作者权威/理论背景、受众、内容与 main claim、与自己问题的关系、strengths、weaknesses、limitations、methodology，以及可用的观点或材料。文体要 formal、objective、factual，完整句或 bullets 均可，但格式必须一致。正式任务是提交 **三篇文本** 的 annotated bibliography。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Locate candidate sources, select the most relevant, cite each accurately, and write an annotation of roughly 100–300 words. It may cover authorial authority, theoretical background, audience, content and main claim, relevance, strengths, weaknesses, limitations, methodology and usable ideas. Use a formal, objective and consistent format. The assignment requires annotations for three texts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] Lesson 4 的颜色
> [[07_Programme/01_LN905_LSE-language-class/PDF/02_Academic-Writing/Week-2-Gender/Week 2 Lesson 4.pdf|Lesson 4]] 的蓝色批注指出：第一句如何充当 topic/overview、哪些句子只是 detail、哪里开始 evaluation，以及哪些 sample annotation 只做 description、哪些在 summary 与 evaluation 之间较平衡。读 sample 时必须跟着蓝色批注判断功能，不能只抽取黑色正文。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In Lesson 4, blue annotations identify the opening overview, supporting detail, the point where evaluation begins, and whether each sample annotation is merely descriptive or balances summary with evaluation. The blue comments are part of the answer logic and cannot be discarded while reading only the black text.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Topic Input：为什么支持 equality，却不使用 feminist 标签？

*Topic Input: support for equality without the feminist label*

Scharff（BBC, 2019）指出，#MeToo、Women's March 和名人参与使 feminism 进入主流，但身份认同仍偏低。英国女性自认 feminist 的比例由 2013 年 27% 升至 2018 年 34%；欧洲五国从德国 8% 到瑞典 40% 不等。与此同时，约八成受访者支持男女平等，英国赞同传统“男主外、女主内”角色者从 1984 年 43% 降到 2017 年 8%。因此争议常在标签、阶级归属、种族/交叉性与刻板印象，而不一定在 equality 原则本身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Scharff argues that feminism has become highly visible through #MeToo, the Women's March and celebrity advocacy, yet identification remains limited. UK identification rose from 27% in 2013 to 34% in 2018, while European figures ranged from 8% in Germany to 40% in Sweden. Support for equal treatment is much higher, and support for traditional gender roles in the UK fell from 43% in 1984 to 8% in 2017. The divide therefore concerns labels, class, race, intersectionality and stereotypes as well as the principle of equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必须能回答/讨论：为什么各国比例差异大；三十年间性别角色态度为何改变；income/class 与 feminist identity 的联系；“仇男、女同性恋、不女性化”等 stereotypes 从何而来；现代 feminism 的目标；celebrity endorsement 是帮助还是 self-promotion；为何美国不同种族女性的认同不同，以及运动是否更多服务 white women；不同文化中的外表、行为、爱好、家庭管理、玩具和约会付款规范；这些规范如何影响态度、心理健康、社会与 equality。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Discussion should explain cross-country variation, generational change, class and income differences, stereotypes of feminists, modern goals, celebrity advocacy, racial and intersectional differences, and culturally specific expectations about appearance, behaviour, hobbies, care, toys and dating. It should then trace their effects on attitudes, mental health, society and equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Topic Listening：Adichie, “We Should All Be Feminists”

*Topic Listening: Adichie's “We Should All Be Feminists”*

听前用三个片段建立论点：04:46 说明特权者可能看不见对女性显而易见的差异；11:29 说明把男孩训练成“hard man”会制造脆弱自我，同时让女孩缩小自己来照顾男性 ego；18:41 指出生物差异存在，但 socialisation 会夸大它们并造成 self-fulfilling process。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The pre-listening extracts establish three claims: privilege can make obvious inequalities invisible; training boys to prove toughness creates fragile egos and teaches girls to shrink themselves; and socialisation magnifies biological difference into a self-fulfilling process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

听力题：记录她遇到的 feminist stereotypes；她被男性区别对待的例子；历史男性统治与现代领导的联系；她提出怎样创造更公平、更快乐的世界；男孩和女孩分别被教成什么样；文化如何被使用；她如何定义 feminism。听后还要比较 Nigeria 与自己的国家/经历。核心词汇：**unsolicited**（未被要求的）、**tongue-in-cheek**（半开玩笑/反讽）、**dawned on**（突然意识到）、**breadwinner**（主要经济供养者）、**emasculated**（感到男性身份/力量被削弱）。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The listening questions cover stereotypes of feminists, examples of unequal treatment, the link between historical male dominance and current leadership, a fairer world, the socialisation of boys and girls, culture and Adichie's definition of feminism. Key vocabulary includes unsolicited, tongue-in-cheek, dawned on, breadwinner and emasculated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Topic Reading：Ellemers（2018）Gender Stereotypes

*Topic Reading: Ellemers (2018), Gender Stereotypes*

Gender stereotypes 同时是 descriptive（认为男女通常怎样）和 prescriptive（规定男女应该怎样）。性别分类快速、显著、常被二元化和极化；所谓差异通常只有很小的 “kernel of truth”，而组内差异往往大于组间差异。社会化与角色分工会把小差异放大。刻板印象虽能快速估计群体，却会在教育、招聘、晋升、care roles 与 self-efficacy 中误判个人，并通过媒体、语言、身体姿势、情绪表达、confirmation、群体规范、just-world belief、行动效率需求和 privilege discomfort 持续存在。更可靠的做法是为个体建立具体、预先确定且可核验的评价标准。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gender stereotypes are both descriptive and prescriptive. Gender categorisation is rapid, salient, binary and polarised, although the “kernel of truth” is small and within-gender variation often exceeds between-gender variation. Socialisation and roles amplify differences. Stereotypes may enable quick group estimates but distort individual judgements in education, employment, promotion, care and self-efficacy. Media, language, posture, emotion, confirmation, group norms, just-world beliefs, efficiency motives and discomfort with privilege help them persist. Individualised, pre-specified and verifiable criteria are safer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阅读必须回答的 14 组问题：一般 stereotype 的问题；对女性教育、就业、晋升和对男性的影响；外表与 romantic attractiveness；为何男女都报告 work–family conflict，而 parenthood 主要惩罚女性职业；stereotype 的功能与难以改变的原因；儿童、媒体和语言的传播；身体姿势与情绪表达；刻板印象如何限制人且人为何仍 conform；把选择看成纯个人选择会发生什么；女性挑战 status quo 的后果；privilege 如何解释对 equality 的反对。小组还需讨论：stereotype 持续时能否实现 equality、文本三段引语的社会后果、对男性的负面影响、以及培训/配额之外的应对办法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The fourteen question groups cover harms in education, work and promotion; effects on men; appearance and attractiveness; unequal career effects of parenthood; the function and persistence of stereotypes; transmission through children, media and language; posture and emotion; conformity; individual-choice explanations; penalties for challenging the status quo; and privilege-based opposition to equality. Follow-up discussion asks whether equality is possible while stereotypes persist and what interventions might work beyond training and quotas.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Academic Interaction：Haines et al.（2016）

*Academic Interaction: Haines et al. (2016)*

研究比较 1983 与 2014 年美国样本对 agency、communion、gender roles、occupations 与 physical characteristics 的刻板印象，发现尽管现实社会角色明显改变，核心 stereotypes 仍高度持久，女性 stereotype 甚至在部分维度增强；没有出现简单 convergence。限制包括两个年代样本不完全匹配、分类过宽和 intersectionality 不足。实践含义是：不能因为“已有一些成功女性”就假定 stereotyping 消失；决策前要明确标准、移除 gendered cues，并对看似中性的判断保持警觉。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Haines et al. compare US gender stereotypes in 1983 and 2014 across agency, communion, roles, occupations and physical traits. Despite substantial social-role change, the central stereotypes remained highly durable and some stereotypes of women strengthened rather than converged. Limitations include unmatched samples, broad categories and limited intersectionality. Practical implications are to set criteria before decisions, remove gendered cues and avoid treating visible female success as proof that stereotyping has disappeared.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每周 Academic Interaction 流程是：约 30 分钟讨论 → group/self reflection → teacher feedback → 学习下一次角色/建议 → 独立准备下一周文本；本周无需提前准备，之后需要。重点不是复述论文，而是用问题、证据、回应和角色配合推进 group meaning-making。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The weekly cycle is a roughly 30-minute discussion, group and self-reflection, teacher feedback, new role guidance, and independent preparation of the next text. The aim is not to recite the article but to develop shared understanding through questions, evidence, responses and coordinated roles.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Pronunciation Session 2：Schwa /ə/

*Pronunciation Session 2: the schwa /ə/*

Schwa 是自然英语中最常见、始终 unstressed、非常短的元音。Content words（nouns、main verbs、adjectives、adverbs）通常承受 stress；articles、pronouns、auxiliaries、prepositions、linkers 等 grammar words 以及多音节词的非重读音节常被弱化成 /ə/。这不仅影响口语自然度，也影响听懂快速语流。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The schwa is a very short, always unstressed vowel and one of the most important sounds in natural English. Content words are normally stressed, while grammar words and unstressed syllables in longer words are often reduced to /ə/. Recognising this pattern improves both natural speech and comprehension of rapid speech.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键形式：`the /ðə/`（元音前通常 `/ðiː/`）、`a /ə/`、`an /ən/`；非强调位置的 `you /jə/`、`your /jə/`、`us /əz/`、`them /ðəm/`、`that /ðət/`；`and /ən(d)/`、`however /haʊevə/`、`another /ənʌðə/`。`you` 位于 clause/sentence 末尾时通常完整发音。否定 auxiliaries 与简短回答中的 auxiliary 通常被强调、完整发音；`on` 是常见不使用 schwa 的例外。多音节词中，`-er/-or, -ation/-on, -ative, -al, -ance/-ence, -able, -ness/-less/-ous, -ment, -ity, -ful, -ward, -some, -en` 等非重读 suffix 常出现弱化，但应以听音和词典核对为准。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Important reductions include the articles, selected pronouns, common auxiliaries, prepositions and linkers such as `and /ən(d)/`. Final or emphasised pronouns and stressed negative or answer-form auxiliaries retain fuller pronunciation; `on` is a notable exception. Many unstressed suffixes also contain schwa, but listening and dictionary checks remain more reliable than treating suffix patterns as absolute rules.
