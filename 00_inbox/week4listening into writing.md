This podcast, given by Mann and Spring, is about 4 tactics of disinformation of climate change and a potential solution of climate change and disinformation in this. 

At the beginning , Mann highlights that climate change has become more and more severe since the world wide using of fossil fuels. He uses  the wild fire he witnessed in Australia to back this opinion. Then, he mentions that a part of individuals still deny this climate change, holding an opinion that this is nothing to do with them.

Then, Mann lists four tactics of disinformation which he calls 4-d tactics, which is delay, division, deflection and doomsim. He uses the example like the speech given by Donald Trump to back his opinion.After that, Mann argues that the key to solve climate change is political willpower.What's more, the podcast also demonstrates that the abuse of social media may also lead to disinformation.In the end, Mann mentions a useful way to deal with the climate change is that involving more children and grandchildren generation kids to this topic. His claims are generatively convincing because not only he uses the figures but also use the celebrities' word and action. However, this podcast fails to account for more detailed reasons why these kinds of disinformation wars happened.

All in all, Mann alarms that the climate change and the disinformation about this topic is more and more severe nowadays, more actions need to be taken to deal with this. This argument is strong but may need more deeper analysis about the reason why this phenomenon happened.
