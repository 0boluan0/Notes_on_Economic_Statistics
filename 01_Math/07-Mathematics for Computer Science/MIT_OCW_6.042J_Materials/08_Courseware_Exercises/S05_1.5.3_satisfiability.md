## Official exercise

Select all of the options that guarantee that predicate \(P(x)\) is satisfiable:

Exercise 1

[ ] 
\(\forall x. P(x)\)

[ ] 
\(\exists x. P(x)\)

[ ] 
\(\forall x. \text{NOT}(P(x))\)

[ ] 
\(\text{NOT}(\forall x. \text{NOT}(P(x)))\)

[ ] 
\(\exists x. \text{NOT}(P(x))\)

[ ] 
\(\text{NOT}(\exists x. \text{NOT}(P(x))\)

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** \(\forall x. P(x)\); \(\exists x. P(x)\); \(\text{NOT}(\forall x. \text{NOT}(P(x)))\); \(\text{NOT}(\exists x. \text{NOT}(P(x))\)
