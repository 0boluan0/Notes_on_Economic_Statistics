## Official exercise

For each positive integer \(i\), let the random variable \(X\_i\) be defined as:

\(\begin{align}\Pr[X\_i= \pm i\cdot 2^i] = f(i)\\\Pr[X\_i=0]= 1-2f(i)\end{align}\)

where \(0\leq f(i) \leq \frac{1}{2}\).

Let \(S\_n:=X\_1+X\_2+\ldots + X\_n\).

What is \(E[S\_n]\)?
