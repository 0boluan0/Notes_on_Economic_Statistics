## Official exercise

Which of the following are equal to \(\Phi(300)\)?

Exercise 1

[ ] 
\(\Phi(3)\cdot\Phi(100)\)

[ ] 
\(\Phi(10)\cdot\Phi(30)\)

[ ] 
\(\Phi(2)^2\cdot\Phi(3)\cdot\Phi(25)\)

[ ] 
\(\Phi(4)\cdot\Phi(3)\cdot\Phi(25)\)

[ ] 
\(\Phi(150)\)

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** \(\Phi(3)\cdot\Phi(100)\); \(\Phi(4)\cdot\Phi(3)\cdot\Phi(25)\)
>
> **Official feedback:**
> \(\Phi(mn) = \Phi(m)\cdot\Phi(n)\) if \(m, n\) are relatively prime.
