## Official exercise

How many total functions are there from set \(A\) to set \(B\) if \(|A|=3\) and \(|B|=7\)? Please enter your answer in the form x^y.

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** 7^3
> **Tolerance:** 0
>
> **Official feedback:**
> Say \(A = \{a\_1, a\_2, a\_3 \}\). There is a bijection between total functions from \(A\) to \(B\) and length-3 vectors of elements of \(B\).
> In this bijection, a given total function \(f\) corresponds to the vector \((f(a\_1), f(a\_2), f(a\_3)) \in B^3\).
> Hence, by the product rule, \(|B^3| = 7^3\).
