## Official exercise

The range of a relation is:

Exercise 1

( ) 
Always smaller than the codomain

( ) 
Equal to or smaller than the codomain

( ) 
The same as the codomain

( ) 
Equal to or larger than the codomain

( ) 
Always larger than the codomain

( ) 
Not related to the codomain

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** Equal to or smaller than the codomain
