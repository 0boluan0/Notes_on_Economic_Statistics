## Official exercise

Consider the following events for rolling 2 dice:

\(\begin{align}A\_i&:=\text{the first die is a \(i\), for \(i\in[1,6]\)}&\\B\_i&:=\text{the second die is a \(i\), for \(i\in[1,6]\)}&\\S\_i&:=\text{sum of the dice is \(i\), for \(i\in[2,12]\)}&\end{align}\)

Which of the following events are independent?

Exercise 1

[ ] 
\(A\_1\) and \(B\_1\)

[ ] 
\(A\_1\) and \(B\_6\)

[ ] 
\(A\_3\) and \(S\_2\)

[ ] 
\(A\_2\) and \(S\_6\)

[ ] 
\(A\_1\) and \(S\_7\)

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** \(A_1\) and \(B_1\); \(A_1\) and \(B_6\); \(A_1\) and \(S_7\)
>
> **Official feedback:**
> Two dice throws are independent.
> 
> \(\Pr[A\_3 \cap S\_2]=\Pr[\emptyset]=0\neq \frac{1}{6}\cdot\frac{1}{36}=\Pr[A\_3]\Pr[S\_2].\)
> \(\Pr[A\_2 \cap S\_6]= \Pr[A\_2 \cap B\_4]=\frac{1}{36}\neq \frac{1}{6}\cdot\frac{5}{36}=\Pr[A\_2]\Pr[S\_6].\)
> \(\Pr[A\_1 \cap S\_7]= \Pr[A\_1 \cap B\_6]=\frac{1}{36} = \frac{1}{6}\cdot\frac{6}{36}=\Pr[A\_1]\Pr[S\_7].\)
