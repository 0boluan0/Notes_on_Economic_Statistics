## Official exercise

Which of the following are true?

Exercise 1

[ ] Each Harmonic number is defined in terms of a simple summation.

[ ] \(H\_n ::= \sum \limits\_{i=0}^n \frac{1}{i} \).

[ ] The overhang of a stable stack of \(n \) books on the edge of a table is equal to the \(n \)-th Harmonic number.

[ ] In calculating the \(\bigtriangleup \)-overhang for the \((n+1) \)-th book that ensures stable stacking, the expression \(\bigtriangleup = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{n+1} \) comes from the equality \(n \cdot \bigtriangleup = 1 \cdot (\frac{1}{2} - \bigtriangleup) \).

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** Each Harmonic number is defined in terms of a simple summation.; In calculating the \(\bigtriangleup \)-overhang for the \((n+1) \)-th book that ensures stable stacking, the expression \(\bigtriangleup = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{n+1} \) comes from the equality \(n \cdot \bigtriangleup = 1 \cdot (\frac{1}{2} - \bigtriangleup) \).
>
> **Official feedback:**
> \(H\_n ::= \sum \limits\_{i=1}^n \frac{1}{i} \). The overhang of a stable stack of \(n \) books on the edge of a table is equal to **half** the \(n \)-th Harmonic number.
