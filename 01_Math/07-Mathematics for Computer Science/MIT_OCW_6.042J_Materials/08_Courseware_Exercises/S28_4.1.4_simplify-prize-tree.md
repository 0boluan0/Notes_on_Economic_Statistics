## Official exercise

Which of the following arguments simplifies the tree model for the Monty Hall SWITCH strategy?

Exercise 1

[ ] 
The probability values of the outcomes are equal.

[ ] 
Branches after the prize location nodes are symmetric.

[ ] 
The tree branches are of the same size.

[ ] 
The door that conceals the prize and the door that the contestant picks can be condensed into whether the contestant picks the door with the prize.

[ ] 
If the door that conceals the prize is picked initially, the SWITCH strategy leads to losing, independent of which door is opened.

[ ] 
The branches that follow the two nodes where a door concealing a goat is picked, lead to the same outcome with the same probability, so these nodes could be combined.

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** Branches after the prize location nodes are symmetric.; The door that conceals the prize and the door that the contestant picks can be condensed into whether the contestant picks the door with the prize.; If the door that conceals the prize is picked initially, the SWITCH strategy leads to losing, independent of which door is opened.; The branches that follow the two nodes where a door concealing a goat is picked, lead to the same outcome with the same probability, so these nodes could be combined.
