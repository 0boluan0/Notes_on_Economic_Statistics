## Official exercise

Which of the following statements is a valid definition of a tree?

Exercise 1

[ ] a connected simple graph with no cycles

[ ] a connected simple graph in which every edge is a cut edge

[ ] a simple graph that is connected and vertex-minimal

[ ] a simple graph that is connected and edge-minimal

[ ] an edge-maximal acyclic graph

[ ] a connected simple graph with \(n \) vertices and \(n-1 \) edges

[ ] a simple graph with a unique path between any 2 vertices

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** a connected simple graph with no cycles; a connected simple graph in which every edge is a cut edge; a simple graph that is connected and edge-minimal; an edge-maximal acyclic graph; a connected simple graph with \(n \) vertices and \(n-1 \) edges; a simple graph with a unique path between any 2 vertices
