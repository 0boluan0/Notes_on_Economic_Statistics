## Official exercise

Let \(R\) and \(S\) be relations on the set \(A\). Which of the following statements about \(R\) and \(S\) are true?

Exercise 1

[ ] 
If \(R\) is reflexive, then the complement of \(R\) is also reflexive.

[ ] 
If \(R\) is symmetric, then the complement of \(R\) is symmetric.

[ ] 
If both \(R\) and \(S\) are reflexive, then \(R\cap S\) is also reflexive.

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** If \(R\) is symmetric, then the complement of \(R\) is symmetric.; If both \(R\) and \(S\) are reflexive, then \(R\cap S\) is also reflexive.
>
> **Official feedback:**
> While \(\leq\) is reflexive, its complement \(>\) is not.
