## Official exercise

For each of the following real-valued functions on **the real numbers** \(\mathbb{R}\), indicate whether it is a *bijection*, a *surjection but not a bijection*, an *injection but not a bijection*, or *neither an injection nor a surjection*.

1. \(x+2\)

2. \(2x \)

   Exercise 2

   [Choose one: a bijection | a surjection but not a bijection | an injection but not a bijection | neither an injection nor a surjection]
3. \(x^2 \)

   Exercise 3

4. \(x^3 \)

   Exercise 4

5. \(\sin x \)

   Exercise 5

6. \(x \sin x \)

   Exercise 6

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** a bijection
>
> **Official feedback:**
> \(x^2 \) is not a surjection, since negative numbers could not be squares of real numbers. \(x^2 \) also is not an injection, since \((-1)^2=1^2\).

> [!answer]- Q2 — official answer
> **Answer:** a bijection
>
> **Official feedback:**
> \(\sin x \) is not a surjection, since \(-1 \leq \sin x \leq 1 \). \(\sin x \) is not an injection, since \(\sin 0 = \sin 2\pi \).

> [!answer]- Q3 — official answer
> **Answer:** neither an injection nor a surjection
>
> **Official feedback:**
> \(x \sin x \) is not an injection, since \(0 \sin 0 = 2\pi \sin 2 \pi \).

> [!answer]- Q4 — official answer
> **Answer:** a bijection
>
> **Official feedback:**
> \(e^x \) is not a surjection, since \(e^x \) is always positive for real values of \(x \).

> [!answer]- Q5 — official answer
> **Answer:** neither an injection nor a surjection

> [!answer]- Q6 — official answer
> **Answer:** a surjection but not a bijection

> [!answer]- Q7 — official answer
> **Answer:** an injection but not a bijection
