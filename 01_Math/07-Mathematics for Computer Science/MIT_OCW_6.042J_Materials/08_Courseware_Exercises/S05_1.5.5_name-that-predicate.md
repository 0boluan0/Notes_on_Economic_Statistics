## Official exercise

We have a procedure to fix any problem.

If the range of \(p\) is the set of procedures and \(i\) is in the set of issues, pick the quantifiers that complete the statement.

\_\_\_\_\_. \(p\) fixes \(i\)

Exercise 1

[Choose one: \(\forall p \exists i\) | \(\exists p \forall i\) | \(\forall p \forall i\) | \(\forall i \exists p\) | \(\exists i \forall p\) | \(\forall i \forall p\)]

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** \(\forall i \exists p\)
