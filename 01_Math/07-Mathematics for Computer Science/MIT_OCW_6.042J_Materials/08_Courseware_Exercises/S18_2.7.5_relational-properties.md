## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** reflexive; transitive
>
> **Official feedback:**
> This relation is actually asymmetric - different people cannot be younger than each other.

> [!answer]- Q2 — official answer
> **Answer:** irreflexive; antisymmetric; transitive
>
> **Official feedback:**
> This relation is irreflexive because a person is not considered to be his/her own descendent.

> [!answer]- Q3 — official answer
> **Answer:** reflexive; transitive
>
> **Official feedback:**
> This relation is not antisymmetric: different brothers have a parent in common.

> [!answer]- Q4 — official answer
> **Answer:** irreflexive; antisymmetric; transitive

> [!answer]- Q5 — official answer
> **Answer:** reflexive
