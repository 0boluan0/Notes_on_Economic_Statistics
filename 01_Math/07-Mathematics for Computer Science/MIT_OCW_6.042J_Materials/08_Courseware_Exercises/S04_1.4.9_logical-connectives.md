# 1.4.9 Logical Connectives

## Official exercise

For each of the following formulas, choose the \(\textrm{AND}\), \(\textrm{OR}\), and \(\textrm{NOT}\) formula equivalent to it.

1. \(P \textrm{ IMPLIES } Q\)   
     

   Exercise 1

   [Choose one: P AND NOT(Q) | NOT(P AND Q) | P OR NOT(Q) | NOT(P) AND Q | NOT(P) OR Q]
2. \(P \textrm{ IFF } Q\)   
     

   Exercise 2

   [Choose one: (P OR Q) AND (NOT(P) OR NOT(Q)) | (P AND Q) OR (NOT(P) AND NOT(Q)) | NOT(P AND Q) AND (NOT(P) OR NOT(Q)) | NOT(P AND Q) OR NOT(P) OR NOT(Q)]
3. \(P \text{ XOR } Q\)   
     

   Exercise 3

   [Choose one: (P IMPLIES NOT(Q)) AND (NOT(P) IMPLIES Q) | (P AND NOT(Q)) OR (NOT(P) AND Q) | (P OR NOT(Q)) AND (NOT(P) OR Q) | (P AND Q) OR (NOT(P) AND NOT(Q)) | NOT(P AND Q)]
4. \(P \text{ NOR } Q\)
     
     

   Exercise 4

   [Choose one: P AND NOT(Q) | NOT(P AND Q) | NOT(P) AND NOT(Q) | (P OR Q) AND (NOT(P) OR Q) | (P OR Q) AND (P OR NOT(Q))]

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** NOT(P) OR Q
>
> **Official feedback:**
> An implication is true exactly when the if-part, in this case \(P\), is FALSE, or the then-part, in this case \(Q\), is TRUE.

> [!answer]- Q2 — official answer
> **Answer:** (P AND Q) OR (NOT(P) AND NOT(Q))
>
> **Official feedback:**
> If \(P\) is TRUE, the answer formula simplifies to \(Q\), so the formula is TRUE only if \(Q\) is also TRUE, that is, only if \(Q\) has the same truth value as \(P\). Likewise, if \(P\) is FALSE, the answer simplifies to \(\text{NOT}(Q)\), so again it is TRUE only if \(Q\) has the same truth value of \(P\), namely FALSE.

> [!answer]- Q3 — official answer
> **Answer:** (P AND NOT(Q)) OR (NOT(P) AND Q)
>
> **Official feedback:**
> \((P \text{ IMPLIES }
> \text{NOT}(Q)) \text{ AND } (\text{NOT}(P) \text{ IMPLIES } Q)\) is equivalent to \(P \text{ XOR } Q\), but it is not an \(\text{AND}\), \(\text{OR}\), and \(\text{NOT}\) formula.

> [!answer]- Q4 — official answer
> **Answer:** NOT(P) AND NOT(Q)
>
> **Official feedback:**
> \( (P \text{ NOR } Q)\) is \(\text{NOT}(P \text{ OR } Q)\). The truth table for \(\text{NOR}\) will produce a TRUE output only when both \(P\) and \(Q\) are FALSE. Thus, \(\text{NOT}(P) \text{ AND } \text{NOT}(Q)\) is a correct answer.
