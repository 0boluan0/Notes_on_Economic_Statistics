## Official exercise

In the bogus induction proof that all horses are of the same color, where does the induction break down and why?

Exercise 1

[Choose one: \(P(1) \), because the base case should be \(P(0) \) | \(P(1) \to P(2) \), because there are no middle horses when \(n=2 \) | \(P(2) \to P(3) \), because there is only one middle horse | \(P(n) \to P(n+1) \text{ for } n \ge 3 \), because the order of the horses is important]

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** \(P(1) \to P(2) \), because there are no middle horses when \(n=2 \)
