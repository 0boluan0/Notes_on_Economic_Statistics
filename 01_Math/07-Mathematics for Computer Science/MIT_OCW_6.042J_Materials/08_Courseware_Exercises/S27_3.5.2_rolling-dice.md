## Official exercise

You roll a pair of dice 25 times, and each time you record the sum of the numbers on the upward faces. At least how many sums are guaranteed to be the same?

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** 3
> **Tolerance:** 0
>
> **Official feedback:**
> Let the integers [2, 12] be the holes, and the sum of the numbers on the dices' upward faces be the pigeons.
> By the Generalized Pigeonhole Principle, some hole has \(\geq \lceil\frac{25}{12-2}\rceil=3\) pigeons.
