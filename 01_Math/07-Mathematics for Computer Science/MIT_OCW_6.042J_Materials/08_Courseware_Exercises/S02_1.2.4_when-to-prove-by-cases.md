## Official exercise

Based on the video and slides, when might we want to prove by cases?

Exercise 1

[ ]  The range of the variables in the problem can be broken up into pieces (i.e. cases).

[ ]  The predicate in each case is easier to prove.

[ ]  All cases together cover all possibilities, such that proving all individual cases completes the proof.

[ ]  When a direct proof is infeasible.

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** The range of the variables in the problem can be broken up into pieces (i.e. cases).; The predicate in each case is easier to prove.; All cases together cover all possibilities, such that proving all individual cases completes the proof.
>
> **Official feedback:**
> Proof by cases might be used when a complicated proof could be broken into cases, where each case is simpler to prove and the cases together cover all possibilities.
