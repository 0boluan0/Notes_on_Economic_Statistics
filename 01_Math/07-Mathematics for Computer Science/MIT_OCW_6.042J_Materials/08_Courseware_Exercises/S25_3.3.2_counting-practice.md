## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** 100000
> **Tolerance:** 0
>
> **Official feedback:**
> There are 10 choices for each of the 5 digits of the PIN. By the product rule, this means there are \(10^5\) or 100000 possible PINs.

> [!answer]- Q2 — official answer
> **Answer:** 40
> **Tolerance:** 0
>
> **Official feedback:**
> Using the sum rule, you have 3+2=5 choices of tops and 4+4=8 choices of bottoms.
> Then using the product rule, we can pair a top and a bottom in 5\(\cdot\)8=40 ways.
