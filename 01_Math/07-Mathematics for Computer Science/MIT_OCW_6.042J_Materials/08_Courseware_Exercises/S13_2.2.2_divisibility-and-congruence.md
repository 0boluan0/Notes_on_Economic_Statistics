## Official exercise

A majority of the following statements are equivalent. List all statements in this majority. Assume that \(n > 0\) and \(a\) and \(b\) are integers.

Exercise 1

[ ] \(a \equiv b\; (\text{mod } n)\)

[ ] \(a = b\)

[ ] \(rem(a, n) = rem(b,n)\)

[ ] \(n\,|\,(a - b)\)

[ ] \(a = b + nk\) for some integer \(k\)

[ ] \((a - b)\) is a multiple of \(n\)

[ ] \(n\,|\,a\) or \(n\,|\,b\)

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** \(a \equiv b\; (\text{mod } n)\); \(rem(a, n) = rem(b,n)\); \(n\,|\,(a - b)\); \(a = b + nk\) for some integer \(k\); \((a - b)\) is a multiple of \(n\)
