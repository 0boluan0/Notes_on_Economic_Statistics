## Official exercise

What is the inverse \((\text{mod } 25)\) of \(32\)?

*Reminder*: Your answer must be an integer in the interval \([0,25)\).

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** 18
>
> **Official feedback:**
> Since \(1 = 9\cdot 25 + (-7)\cdot 32\), it follows that \(-7\) is an inverse of \(32 (\text{mod } 25)\), and therefore so is \(rem(-7,25) = 18\).
