## Official exercise

1. Let \(A \) and \(B \) be any two sets. Which of the following is true about Cantor's Idea?

   Exercise 1

   [ ] When \(A \) surj \(B \), \(A \) is as big as \(B \).

   [ ] When \(A \) inj \(B \), \(A \) is as small as \(B \).

   [ ] When \(A \) bij \(B \), \(A \) is the same size as \(B \).

   [ ] \(\text{pow} (\mathbb{N}) \) bij \(\{0, 1 \}^\omega \).

   [ ] Two infinite sets have the same size when there is a bijection between them.
2. Let \(A \) and \(B \) be any two sets. What does the Schroeder-Bernstein Theorem tell us?

   Exercise 2

   [ ] \(A \) surj \(B \) surj \(A \) IMPLIES \(A \) bij \(B \)

   [ ] \(|A| \ge |B| \ge |A| \) IMPLIES \(|A| = |B| \)

   [ ] \(A \) surj \(B \) inj \(A \) IMPLIES \(A \) bij \(B \)

   [ ] None of the above
3. What property distinguishes infinite sets from finite sets?

   Exercise 3

   [Choose one: Cantor's Idea | Schroeder-Bernstein Theorem | pow(A) surj A | \(A \cup \{d\}\) bij \(A\), where \( d \notin A \) | None of the above]

## Official answers and feedback

> [!answer]- Q1 — official answer
> **Answer:** When \(A \) surj \(B \), \(A \) is as big as \(B \).; When \(A \) bij \(B \), \(A \) is the same size as \(B \).; \(\text{pow} (\mathbb{N}) \) bij \(\{0, 1 \}^\omega \).; Two infinite sets have the same size when there is a bijection between them.

> [!answer]- Q2 — official answer
> **Answer:** \(A \) surj \(B \) surj \(A \) IMPLIES \(A \) bij \(B \); \(|A| \ge |B| \ge |A| \) IMPLIES \(|A| = |B| \)

> [!answer]- Q3 — official answer
> **Answer:** \(A \cup \{d\}\) bij \(A\), where \( d \notin A \)
