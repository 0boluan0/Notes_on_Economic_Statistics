## 1. 先区分“相差不多”究竟指什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Decide what kind of closeness matters*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $f(n)=n^2+n$、$g(n)=n^2$。两者之差 $n$ 越来越大，但比值 $1+1/n$ 趋于 $1$。所以若关心相对误差，$g$ 是越来越准确的近似；若关心绝对误差，它们并没有越来越接近。这正是[[渐近等价]] $f\sim g$ 的含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(n)=n^2+n$ and $g(n)=n^2$, the difference $n$ grows, yet the ratio $1+1/n$ tends to one. The relative approximation improves while the absolute error grows. This is what [[渐近等价|asymptotic equivalence]] $f\sim g$ describes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再把 $f$ 改成 $2n^2$。它与 $n^2$ 比值恒为 $2$，因此不是渐近等价，却仍在同一个平方增长尺度上。[[大Θ记号]]用固定正常数夹住比值来表达这种“同阶”。不要把相对误差趋零和常数倍可比混成一个结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now let $f(n)=2n^2$. Its ratio to $n^2$ is always two, so the functions are not asymptotically equivalent, but share a quadratic scale. [[大Θ记号|Big Theta]] expresses this constant-factor comparability. Vanishing relative error and bounded positive ratios are different claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面先把比较限制在同一趋向无穷的输入域上、最终严格为正的函数。这样比值与反向比值都能使用；带符号误差项或无限零点在第 6 节另作解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For now, compare eventually strictly positive functions on the same input domain tending to infinity. Both ratios are then available. Section 6 separately handles signed errors and persistent zeros.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 六种记号，分别要求多强的控制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Six notations impose different strengths of control*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[大O记号]]说存在一组 $C>0,N$，使每个 $n\ge N$ 都有 $f(n)\le Cg(n)$。它只给上界。例如 $n^2$ 既是 $O(n^2)$，也是 $O(n^3)$；第二个说法正确但更松。[[大Ω记号]]反过来要求最终 $f(n)\ge cg(n)$，其中 $c>0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[大O记号|Big O]] asks for fixed $C>0,N$ such that $f(n)\le Cg(n)$ for every $n\ge N$. It gives only an upper bound: $n^2$ is both $O(n^2)$ and $O(n^3)$, though the latter is looser. [[大Ω记号|Big Omega]] instead asks for an eventual lower bound $f(n)\ge cg(n)$ with $c>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上下界同时成立，就是[[大Θ记号|同阶]] $f=\Theta(g)$。例如对 $n\ge1$，有 $n^2\le n^2+3n\le4n^2$，所以 $n^2+3n=\Theta(n^2)$。上下界若来自不同阈值，取两阈值的最大值即可。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both bounds together give [[大Θ记号|equal order]], $f=\Theta(g)$. For $n\ge1$, $n^2\le n^2+3n\le4n^2$, so $n^2+3n=\Theta(n^2)$. If the bounds initially use different thresholds, take their maximum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[小o记号]]更强：$f/g\to0$，等价于对每个 $\varepsilon>0$，最终 $f\le\varepsilon g$。阈值可以依赖 $\varepsilon$。[[小ω记号]]是反向小 o：$f/g\to\infty$，即最终超过 $g$ 的每个固定正常数倍。[[渐近等价]]则指定比值趋于 $1$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[小o记号|Little o]] is stronger: $f/g\to0$, or for every $\varepsilon>0$, eventually $f\le\varepsilon g$. The threshold may depend on $\varepsilon$. [[小ω记号|Little omega]] reverses little o: $f/g\to\infty$, eventually exceeding every fixed positive multiple of $g$. [[渐近等价|Asymptotic equivalence]] specifically requires the ratio to tend to one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 $n=o(n^2)$、$n^2=\omega(n)$；$2n=\Theta(n)$ 却既不是 $o(n)$，也不是与 $n$ 渐近等价。这些记号比较的是函数，而不是直接给算法贴上“最好”或“最坏”的标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $n=o(n^2)$ and $n^2=\omega(n)$; $2n=\Theta(n)$ but is neither $o(n)$ nor equivalent to $n$. These symbols compare functions rather than labeling an algorithm's best or worst case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 比值极限很好用，但失败时要回到不等式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. A ratio limit is useful, but inequalities remain decisive*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[渐近比较的比值判别]]给出最常用的路线：若 $f/g\to0$，得到小 o；若趋于正的有限常数，得到 Θ；若恰好趋于 $1$，进一步得到 $\sim$；若趋于正无穷，得到小 $\omega$。例如 $(6n^3-n)/(2n^3+7)\to3$，足以说明分子与分母同阶，但不等价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[渐近比较的比值判别|ratio test]] gives the usual route: zero implies little o; a positive finite limit implies Theta; one further implies equivalence; positive infinity implies little omega. For example, $(6n^3-n)/(2n^3+7)\to3$, proving equal order but not equivalence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通极限不存在却不代表所有关系都失败。令 $g(n)=n$、$f(n)=(2+(-1)^n)n$。比值在 $1,3$ 间跳动，没有极限，但始终有 $n\le f(n)\le3n$，所以仍同阶。这是[[同阶不要求比值收敛]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Failure of an ordinary limit does not invalidate every relation. Take $g(n)=n$ and $f(n)=(2+(-1)^n)n$. The ratio alternates between one and three, yet $n\le f(n)\le3n$, so the functions share an order. This is [[同阶不要求比值收敛|equal order without a convergent ratio]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还可以只有单向大 O，却没有小 o：取 $g(n)=n$，偶数处 $f(n)=n$、奇数处 $f(n)=1$。始终 $f\le g$；但奇数处 $g/f=n$ 无界，偶数处 $f/g=1$ 又阻止比值趋零。[[严格大O不等于小o]]把这两种“严格更慢”的说法分开。遇到振荡就检查上下界，而不是凭增长阶表强行排序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One-way big O need not imply little o. Let $g(n)=n$, with $f(n)=n$ on even inputs and one on odd inputs. Always $f\le g$, but $g/f=n$ is unbounded on odd inputs and $f/g=1$ on even inputs prevents convergence to zero. [[严格大O不等于小o|One-way O versus little o]] separates these notions. For oscillation, test bounds instead of forcing functions into a memorized hierarchy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 常见增长层级来自证明，而不是只背顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Prove the familiar hierarchy instead of only memorizing it*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $p>0$，令 $\delta=p/2$。对 $x\ge1$，由对数积分表示，

$$
0\le\ln x=\int_1^x\frac{dt}{t}
\le\int_1^x t^{\delta-1}\,dt
=\frac{x^\delta-1}{\delta}.
$$

除以 $x^p$，右端被 $x^{-p/2}/\delta$ 控制并趋零，故 $\ln x=o(x^p)$，即[[对数慢于正幂]]。固定底数的换底只乘一个常数；如果幂指数自己随 $x$ 改变，就不再是这个定理的条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For fixed $p>0$, choose $\delta=p/2$ and use the displayed integral comparison. Dividing by $x^p$ gives an upper bound $x^{-p/2}/\delta\to0$, hence $\ln x=o(x^p)$: [[对数慢于正幂|a logarithm grows more slowly than every fixed positive power]]. A fixed change of logarithm base only multiplies by a constant. An exponent that varies with $x$ changes the theorem's premise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再固定 $a>1$ 与实数 $r$，写 $x^r/a^x=\exp(r\ln x-x\ln a)$。因为 $\ln x/x\to0$，指数中的负线性项最终占优，趋于负无穷，因此 $x^r=o(a^x)$，即[[幂函数慢于指数函数]]。所以即使 $n^{100}$ 与 $1.01^n$ 的交叉点很远，最终仍是后者增长得更快。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For fixed $a>1$ and real $r$, write $x^r/a^x=\exp(r\ln x-x\ln a)$. Since $\ln x/x\to0$, the negative linear term eventually dominates, proving $x^r=o(a^x)$: [[幂函数慢于指数函数|the exponential outgrows the fixed power]]. Thus $1.01^n$ eventually outgrows $n^{100}$ even if their crossover is far away.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回到已学计数：[[调和数对数增长]]解释 $H_n\sim\ln n$；[[斯特林公式]]解释阶乘的相对近似；[[Robbins阶乘界]]则给有限正整数上可用的明确误差范围。渐近记号压缩结论，并不代替那些对象各自的条件与证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For familiar counts, [[调和数对数增长|harmonic-number growth]] gives $H_n\sim\ln n$; [[斯特林公式|Stirling's formula]] gives a relative factorial approximation; [[Robbins阶乘界|Robbins' bounds]] give explicit finite-input errors. Asymptotic notation compresses these conclusions without replacing their separate conditions and proofs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 隐藏常数也必须真的固定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Hidden constants must actually remain fixed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[渐近界的统一常数]]要求 $\exists C\exists N\forall n\ge N$。若每个 $n$ 都临时换一个 $C_n$，几乎任何有限值都能被包住，却完全没有控制函数继续增长。例如错误归纳从 $2^n\le C$ 推到 $2^{n+1}\le2C$，每一步都换常数，不能证明 $2^n=O(1)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[渐近界的统一常数|Uniform constants]] require $\exists C\exists N\forall n\ge N$. Choosing a new $C_n$ for each input bounds individual values without controlling growth. The false induction from $2^n\le C$ to $2^{n+1}\le2C$ changes the constant at every step and cannot prove $2^n=O(1)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同理，每个固定整数 $i$ 是常数，不代表 $\sum_{i=1}^n i=O(n)$。项数和项的大小一起增长，总和实际是 $n(n+1)/2$。代码里的[[循环成本求和]]也受同一约束：只能在所有相关轮次有统一界时，使用“轮数 × 每轮成本”的证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Likewise, each fixed integer $i$ being constant does not make $\sum_{i=1}^n i=O(n)$: both the term count and their sizes grow, giving $n(n+1)/2$. [[循环成本求和|Loop-cost summation]] obeys the same rule. A proof by "rounds times per-round cost" needs a bound uniform over the relevant rounds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 符号与零点决定哪些快捷写法仍然合法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Signs and zeros determine which shortcuts remain valid*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

误差项可能有正有负。这时可用幅度不等式 $|f|\le C|g|$ 扩展大 O，并对 Θ 使用双侧幅度界；但[[渐近等价]]仍保留带符号比值趋于 $1$，不能悄悄改成绝对值比值。具体语境见[[渐近比较的符号与零点边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Error terms may be signed. One can extend big O using $|f|\le C|g|$ and Theta using two-sided magnitude bounds, while [[渐近等价|asymptotic equivalence]] retains a signed ratio tending to one. Do not silently replace it by an absolute-value ratio. See [[渐近比较的符号与零点边界|sign and zero boundaries]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $g$ 有无限多个零点，直接除以 $g$ 甚至可能没有意义。幅度大 O 的不等式要求在每个足够大的零点也成立，因此那里必须同时 $f=0$。令 $f$ 在偶数处为 $1$、奇数处为 $0$，$g$ 反过来：两个方向都在无限多个输入上违反界，所以互不为大 O。真正的理由是找到了违背统一不等式的输入，而不是仅说“比值没有极限”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $g$ has persistent zeros, dividing by it may be invalid. A magnitude O bound must also hold at every sufficiently large zero, forcing $f$ to vanish there. Let $f$ equal one on even inputs and zero on odd inputs, with $g$ reversed. Each direction violates the bound infinitely often. The reason is failure of a uniform inequality, not merely failure of a ratio limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 经过变换以后，重新检查需要控制的误差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Recheck the required error after a transformation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $f(n)=n+\sqrt n$、$g(n)=n$，虽然 $f\sim g$，但 $3^f/3^g=3^{\sqrt n}\to\infty$。[[渐近等价不保留指数运算]]的原因是：原先控制相对差 $(f-g)/g$，指数比值却取决于绝对差 $f-g$。固定底数 $a>1$ 时，要让 $a^f\sim a^g$，需要 $f-g\to0$；只要同阶，则需要差最终有界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $f(n)=n+\sqrt n$ and $g(n)=n$. Although $f\sim g$, $3^f/3^g=3^{\sqrt n}\to\infty$. [[渐近等价不保留指数运算|Exponentiation need not preserve equivalence]] because the original relative difference is not the absolute difference controlling the transformed ratio. For fixed $a>1$, equivalent exponentials need $f-g\to0$; equal-order exponentials need an eventually bounded difference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对数变换有另一条有条件的结论。若最终正的 $f,g$ 同阶，且 $g\to\infty$，则 $\ln(f/g)$ 有界而 $\ln g\to\infty$，所以

$$
\frac{\ln f}{\ln g}
=1+\frac{\ln(f/g)}{\ln g}\longrightarrow1.
$$

这就是[[同阶发散函数的对数等价]]。增长条件不能省略：常数函数 $f=4,g=2$ 同阶，但 $\ln f/\ln g=2$。课程正文已把这一条件补回相应来源旁边。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If eventually positive $f,g$ have the same order and $g\to\infty$, then $\ln(f/g)$ is bounded while $\ln g\to\infty$, so the displayed ratio tends to one. This is [[同阶发散函数的对数等价|logarithmic equivalence of divergent same-order functions]]. The growth condition matters: constant functions four and two have the same order, but their logarithms have ratio two. The course record states this condition beside the relevant source.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
