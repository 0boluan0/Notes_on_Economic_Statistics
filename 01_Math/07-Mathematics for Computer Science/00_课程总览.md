### 2. 数论、RSA 与图结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Number Theory, RSA, and Graph Structures*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明方法保证离散结论可被检验；数论和图论提供随后要证明的具体结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Proof methods make discrete claims verifiable; number theory and graph theory provide the concrete structures to which those methods are applied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[模运算、欧几里得算法与 RSA]]

数论板块训练用不变量和构造性算法处理整数结构；图论把同样的离散推理转向对象之间的关系，但它是新的使用场景而不是 RSA 的后续步骤。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The number-theory block develops invariant-based and constructive reasoning about integers. Graph theory applies discrete reasoning to relationships among objects, but it is a new use case rather than a subsequent step in RSA.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图论先把对象和关系建成 graph，再用 path、connectivity、BFS 与 DFS 回答“能否到达、怎样遍历、最少经过几条边”。这些是后续结构定理和算法共享的语言。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Graph theory first models objects and relationships as a graph, then uses paths, connectivity, BFS, and DFS to answer whether a destination is reachable, how the graph can be explored, and how few edges a route needs. This is the shared language for the structural theorems and algorithms that follow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[图的基本结构、路径与遍历]]

在共同语言之上，无环结构分成两个方向：undirected tree 保留连通所需的最小骨架，并进一步产生 spanning tree 与 MST；directed DAG 则把先后约束变成 topological order。二者共享“去掉 cycle 后结构变得可递归处理”这一核心，但回答不同问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Above that shared language, acyclic structure branches in two directions. An undirected tree preserves the minimum skeleton needed for connectivity and leads to spanning trees and MSTs, while a directed DAG turns precedence constraints into a topological order. Both become recursively tractable after cycles are removed, but they answer different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[无环图：树、生成树、DAG 与拓扑排序]]

如果 edge 不再表示“可以走过去”，而表示“两件事不能使用同一资源”，问题便从 reachability 转为 conflict partition：vertex coloring 把对象分成若干无冲突组，chromatic number 追问最少需要多少组。这里也会得到识别 bipartite graph 的 odd-cycle criterion。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an edge means not “one can travel between these objects” but “these two objects cannot share a resource,” the problem changes from reachability to conflict partitioning. Vertex coloring divides the objects into conflict-free groups, while the chromatic number asks for the fewest such groups. This route also yields the odd-cycle criterion for recognizing bipartite graphs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[图着色与色数]]

确认一张 graph 具有左右两侧后，可以提出另一种问题：不是给 vertices 分组，而是在允许的 edges 中选择互不争用端点的 assignments。augmenting path 说明已有配对可以怎样连锁重排；Hall condition 则把“能否覆盖左侧全部对象”精确归结为是否存在 bottleneck subset。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once a graph has two sides, a different question becomes available: rather than grouping vertices, choose allowed edges as assignments without competing for endpoints. Augmenting paths explain how an existing assignment can be rerouted through a chain of changes, and Hall's condition reduces full coverage of the left side exactly to the absence of a bottleneck subset.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[二分图匹配与 Hall 定理]]

### 3. 计数、渐近与组合原理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Counting, Asymptotics, and Combinatorial Principles*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结构问题常要求计算对象数量和增长速度，因此从图与数论进入组合计数和渐近分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Structural problems often require the number of objects and their rate of growth, leading from graphs and number theory into combinatorial counting and asymptotic analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[组合计数原理]]

组合原理先给出有限对象的精确数量；当规模变大时，渐近记号保留主导增长率，使不同计数式和算法成本可以比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Combinatorial principles first give exact counts for finite objects. As scale grows, asymptotic notation retains the dominant growth rate, making counting formulae and algorithmic costs comparable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[渐近记号与算法复杂度]]

### 4. 离散概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Discrete Probability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计数把有限可能性量化；离散概率再给这些可能性赋权，并研究随机变量、期望和集中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Counting quantifies a finite set of possibilities. Discrete probability assigns weights to those possibilities and studies random variables, expectations, and concentration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

概率首先需要定义 sample space、event 与 probability，并在信息到来后使用 conditional probability 与 Bayes rule。计数公式只有在基本 outcomes 确实 equiprobable 时才能直接变成概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Probability begins by defining a sample space, events, and a probability measure, then updates information through conditional probability and Bayes' rule. Counting formulas turn directly into probabilities only when the elementary outcomes are genuinely equiprobable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[概率空间、条件概率与 Bayes 法则]]

有了概率模型，还要把复杂 outcome 映射成 random variable，才能用 distribution、expectation、variance 与 covariance 描述数值结果和波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the probability model is specified, complex outcomes are mapped into random variables so that distributions, expectations, variances, and covariances can describe numerical results and their variability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[随机变量、分布与矩]]

当观察到部分信息时，平均值也应随信息更新；conditional expectation 把这一更新写成随机变量，并明确 independence、conditional independence 与 uncorrelated 的差别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After partial information is observed, an average should update with that information. Conditional expectation represents this update as a random variable and separates independence, conditional independence, and lack of correlation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[条件期望与独立性]]

许多离散问题不需要先求完整 distribution，而只问某类对象出现多少次。把每个候选贡献写成 indicator，可以在 events 相关时仍直接求 expected count；若还要 variance 或 occurrence probability，再单独检查 dependence。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Many discrete problems ask only how many objects of a particular kind occur and do not require a full distribution. Writing each possible contribution as an indicator gives the expected count even when the events are dependent; variance and occurrence probabilities then require a separate dependence check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[指示变量与随机计数]]

最后，当目标从 average 转为“偏离 average 多远的概率”时，根据已知的 nonnegativity、variance、independence 与 boundedness 依次选择 Markov、Chebyshev、Chernoff 或 Hoeffding；更强的指数界来自更强的条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, when the target changes from an average to the probability of a large deviation, choose Markov, Chebyshev, Chernoff, or Hoeffding according to the available non-negativity, variance, independence, and boundedness assumptions. Stronger exponential bounds come from stronger conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[概率不等式与集中界]]
