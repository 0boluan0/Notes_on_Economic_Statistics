### 1. 数学证明方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Mathematical Proof Methods*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the default starting point. Use the quick-recovery section to establish the objects, examples, and motivating questions before moving into the details.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这是本课程的默认起点；先用快速恢复建立对象、例子和问题感，再进入详细内容。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin by fixing what counts as a proposition, a sound argument, and a valid counterexample. These five judgments determine whether every later proof has a definite object and admissible evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
先建立“什么是可证明的命题、什么算可靠论证、什么才是合格反例”。这五个判断决定后续证明有没有明确对象和有效证据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the background and every input explicit. A true statement still needs a valid argument; checked examples alone establish a universal claim only when they exhaust the stated domain; any reduction to representative cases needs its own proof. The next five cards distinguish the statement, its allowed starting facts, its proof, the force of a counterexample, and whether a proposed counterexample qualifies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
保持背景与全部输入明确。真陈述仍需要有效论证；仅靠检查例子，须穷尽声明的论域才足以建立全称结论；若化归到代表情形，还须证明化归的覆盖性。接下来的五张卡分别说明陈述、允许的起点、证明、反例的作用，以及候选反例是否合格。

Next read quantifiers and propositional logic. This is not merely a symbol list: quantifier order controls dependence, negation controls the contrary assumption, the truth condition for implication controls refutation, and contraposition opens an equivalent proof route.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
接着读量词和命题逻辑。这里不是符号表：量词次序决定依赖关系，否定规则决定反设，蕴含的真值条件决定怎样反驳，逆否等价则打开另一条证明入口。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read the quantifiers with their scopes before choosing a witness or negating a claim. In propositional logic, compare all assignments to the variables that occur; an implication and its contrapositive agree, but a converse requires a separate argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
先读清量词和作用域，再选择见证或否定目标。命题逻辑中，要比较所涉及变量的全部赋值；蕴含与逆否命题等价，而逆命题需要另行论证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now translate logical form into proof procedures. Direct proof, contraposition, and contradiction are not interchangeable templates: they proceed along the original implication, an equivalent contrapositive, and the negation of the target respectively. Proof by cases separately carries an exhaustive-coverage obligation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
现在把逻辑形状翻译成写作程序。直接证明、逆否与反证不是三个名称相近的模板：它们分别沿原蕴含、等价逆否和目标否定前进；分类证明另行承担覆盖全部可能的义务。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the same domain and hypotheses through each method. To refute a conditional, find a true antecedent and a false consequent; to prove it by contradiction, negate that whole target. Within a proof by cases, retain the common hypotheses in every branch and verify coverage before using the branch conclusions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
每种方法都须保持同一论域与假设。反驳条件句，要找到前件真而后件假的情形；以反证法证明它，要否定完整目标。分类证明的每个分支都须保留共同前提，并先核对覆盖性，再使用各分支的结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When objects are generated by size or recursive construction, proof turns to induction and descent. Learn ordinary induction, strong induction, structural induction, and well ordering separately before comparing why their proving power can agree while the structures they expose differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
当对象按规模或递归构造生成时，证明转向归纳与下降。先分别掌握普通归纳、强归纳、结构归纳和良序法，再看它们为什么证明能力等价，却适合暴露不同的问题结构。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the equivalence card below, the three principles are ordinary induction, strong induction and well ordering on the nonnegative integers. Structural induction follows the bases and constructors of a specified domain consisting exactly of objects obtainable by finite constructions; its card explains the connection using construction height. For any induction argument, check the starting cases and the range of every smaller input. In a least-counterexample argument, a contradiction can come either from proving the selected case true or from finding a smaller failure in the same domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
下方等价卡中的三个原则，具体指非负整数上的普通归纳、强归纳和良序原理。结构归纳则沿基础对象与构造器展开，论域恰由有限次构造所得对象组成；对应卡片用构造高度解释二者的联系。每种归纳都须核对启动情形及较小输入的范围。最小反例论证可以证明所选情形其实为真，也可以在同一论域内找到更小失败；两者都能得到矛盾。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally apply induction language to algorithms and stepwise processes. A state machine first fixes what counts as a legal execution; an invariant constrains every reachable state; a ranking function rules out infinite execution. The two proofs together establish that the program terminates with a correct result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
最后把归纳语言用于算法和逐步过程。状态机先限定什么叫合法执行；不变量证明所有可达状态保持性质；秩函数证明执行不能无限延续。两条证明合在一起，才得到程序一定结束且结果正确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The output guarantee in this bridge requires an extra implication: the initialized, preserved invariant and the exit condition must jointly imply the required result at every return. This establishes partial correctness. Combine it with termination for the same allowed inputs and execution rules to obtain total correctness. A ranking function is one way to prove termination; an arbitrary invariant is not enough to prove the output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
上面从不变量走向输出保证，还需要一步：已初始化且保持的不变量，必须与每个返回处的退出条件共同推出要求的结果。这才得到部分正确性；再与同一允许输入、同一执行规则下的终止性结合，得到全正确性。秩函数是证明终止的一种方法，任意不变量并不足以证明输出。

#### 2.1 整数相除、gcd 与系数构造
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Integer division, gcds, and coefficient construction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Divisibility specifies integer multiples, and division with remainder makes them computational. The gcd is the target; Euclid preserves it while shrinking the inputs. Bézout and the extended algorithm address coefficient existence and computation respectively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

整除规定整数倍关系；带余除法让这个关系变成可以反复执行的计算。gcd 是求解目标，Euclid 保持它不变并缩小输入；Bézout 与扩展算法分别回答系数存在和怎样求系数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Division with remainder is an existence-and-uniqueness theorem; the Euclidean algorithm is the repeated procedure. Its current second coordinate decreases only while positive. At a zero second coordinate, return the first; two zero inputs use the stated gcd convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

带余除法说明商余数的存在与唯一性；Euclid 才是反复执行的过程。当前第二坐标只在为正时递减；归零后返回第一坐标。双零输入使用已声明的 gcd 约定。

#### 2.2 素数与公因公倍关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Primes and gcd–lcm relationships*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use Bézout to prove Euclid's lemma before unique factorization, avoiding circular reasoning. Prime exponents explain gcd and lcm computation; unique factorization does not itself establish efficient factoring.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先用 Bézout 证明素数整除乘积的引理，再证明唯一分解，避免循环论证。素因子指数同时解释 gcd 与 lcm 的计算；分解唯一并不意味着分解算法一定快。

![[素数]]

![[素数整除乘积]]

![[唯一素因数分解]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Factorization has two claims: strong induction supplies factors, and Euclid’s lemma makes their list unique up to order. The existence part alone also supports the next result: no finite list contains every prime. For a finite list of primes, the product-plus-one construction need not itself be prime.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分解有两个结论：强归纳给出素因子，Euclid 引理使计重数的清单仅差顺序。只用存在性还可推出下一条结论：任何有限清单都列不尽素数；有限素数清单乘积加一的构造数本身不必为素数。

![[素数无穷性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lcm definition and product formula below use two positive inputs. Zero requires a separate convention; in particular, the quotient formula cannot compute lcm(0,0) by dividing by gcd(0,0). Compare the direction of divisibility before using either formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面的 lcm 定义和乘积公式以两个正整数为输入。零需要单独约定；尤其不能用除以 gcd(0,0) 的商式计算 lcm(0,0)。使用公式前，先分清 gcd 与 lcm 中整除关系的方向。

#### 2.3 同余、逆元与方程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Congruences, inverses, and equations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Congruence turns divisibility of a difference into residue classes. Modular arithmetic preserves addition, subtraction, and multiplication, but division needs an inverse. Define the linear-congruence problem, use cancellation and solvability to list every solution class, then combine coprime moduli by CRT.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同余把差的整除转成余数类；模运算保留加减乘，但除法必须检查逆元。先认识线性同余方程，再利用消去与可解性条件列全解类，最后用 CRT 合并互素模数的多个条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep three objects separate: an integer, its residue class, and the standard remainder naming that class. For n > 1 there are n classes, while each class contains infinitely many integers. Inverses undo multiplication only when the stated coprimality condition holds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先分清三个对象：一个整数、它所在的同余类、用来代表这个类的标准余数。n > 1 时共有 n 个类，每个类却包含无穷多个整数；逆元仅在相应互素条件成立时撤销乘法。

![[同余]]

![[模运算]]

![[模逆元]]

![[模逆元存在条件]]

![[线性同余方程]]

![[同余消去律]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For ax ≡ b (mod n), write d = gcd(a,n). When d divides b, one class modulo n/d corresponds to d classes modulo the original n. If solvable and d = n, all integers work; no inverse modulo one is needed. CRT then addresses a different task: combine conditions for a finite family of pairwise coprime moduli into one class modulo their product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 ax ≡ b (mod n)，记 d = gcd(a,n)。d 整除 b 时，模 n/d 的一个类对应原模数 n 下的 d 个类；在可解前提下若 d = n，则全体整数成立，不必求模一逆元。随后 CRT 处理另一项任务：将有限个两两互素模数的条件合并为一个模乘积的类。

#### 2.4 欧拉函数与模指数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The totient and modular exponents*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define what the totient counts before computing it with prime-power counting and CRT. Euler's theorem turns a permutation of invertible residues into an exponent identity; Fermat's theorem specializes to prime moduli. Repeated squaring computes modular powers without requiring a coprime base.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

欧拉函数先定义被计数的对象，再用素数幂计数和 CRT 计算。欧拉定理把可逆余数的置换变成指数结论；费马小定理是素数模数的情形。实际算模幂用重复平方，不必要求底数互素。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The count at n = 1 is defined directly by gcd; the inverse and Euler statements below use n > 1. To compute a product count, split into powers of distinct primes and check coprimality. In Euler’s proof, separately justify that multiplication preserves the list, rearranges it, and permits cancellation of its product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

n = 1 的计数直接由 gcd 定义；下面的逆元与欧拉定理采用 n > 1。计算乘积时，先按互异素数分块并检查互素。阅读欧拉证明时，分别核对乘法保留列表、重排列表、约去列表乘积这三个依据。

#### 2.5 RSA 构造、全消息正确性与安全边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*RSA construction, all-message correctness, and the security boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define the public and private objects, then prove recovery for every message prime by prime without importing Euler's coprimality hypothesis as a message restriction. Algebraic recovery and the security of a concrete encryption scheme are separate requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先定义公开与保密的对象，再逐素数证明所有消息都能还原；不要把欧拉定理的互素条件偷偷当成对消息的限制。代数上的正确还原，与具体加密方案抵抗攻击，是不同的验收问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The following construction uses an inverse modulo φ(n); the all-message theorem only needs the corresponding congruence modulo lcm(p−1,q−1). The proof includes zero and noncoprime messages. The security card then asks what an observer can learn from public information; an algebraic round trip alone does not answer that question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面的构造在模 φ(n) 下求逆；全消息定理只需模 lcm(p−1,q−1) 的相应同余。证明包含零与不互素消息。随后安全卡讨论观察者能从公开信息获知什么，代数往返本身不能回答这个问题。

![[RSA公钥加密]]

![[RSA全消息正确性]]

![[RSA安全边界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The number-theory block develops invariant-based and constructive reasoning about integers. Graph theory applies discrete reasoning to relationships among objects, but it is a new use case rather than a subsequent step in RSA.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图论先把对象和关系建成 graph，再用 path、connectivity、BFS 与 DFS 回答“能否到达、怎样遍历、最少经过几条边”。这些是后续结构定理和算法共享的语言。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Graph theory first models objects and relationships as a graph, then uses paths, connectivity, BFS, and DFS to answer whether a destination is reachable, how the graph can be explored, and how few edges a route needs. This is the shared language for the structural theorems and algorithms that follow.

Session 16 接着建立 walk、path、cycle、distance 与 reachability 的语言。删去重复闭合段解释了为什么最短 walk 可以限制为 path。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Session 16 then establishes walks, paths, cycles, distance, and reachability. Deleting closed repeated segments explains why a shortest walk may be restricted to a path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[游走路径与环]]

![[游走去环得到路径]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here, “shortest” minimizes the number of edges, not a sum of arbitrary weights. Removing a nonempty closed detour strictly lowers this count while preserving both endpoints and valid directions. If the endpoints coincide, the shortest walk is the zero-length path; a positive-length cycle is a different requirement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里的“最短”最小化边数，不是任意权重之和。删去非空闭合绕路会严格减少边数，同时保留起终点与合法方向。若起终点相同，最短游走就是零长路径；要求长度为正的环是另一个条件。

![[图距离与可达性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same graph can be stored as outgoing lists or as a table of vertex pairs. First choose the representation and what each entry records: edge existence, multiplicity or weight. That choice determines both the storage cost and the meaning of a matrix calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
同一张图可以存成出邻接表，也可以存成顶点对的数表。先选择表示法，再说明元素记录边的存在性、重数还是权重；这个选择同时决定存储成本与矩阵计算的意义。

![[邻接表与邻接矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With edge-count entries and ordinary multiplication, a matrix power counts walks of exactly the given length. Each intermediate vertex splits the walk uniquely, explaining the product and sum. Repeated edges and vertices are allowed; weighted entries instead produce sums of products, which may cancel.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
采用边数元素与普通乘法时，矩阵幂计数指定长度的游走。每个中间顶点唯一拆分前后两段，解释了为什么先乘再加。允许重复边和顶点；若改存权重，所得是可能相互抵消的乘积之和。

![[邻接矩阵幂计数游走]]

An isomorphism must preserve degrees and connected components. A differing invariant can therefore reject isomorphism, but matching degree sequences alone leave the question open. Read the separate rule and its six-cycle versus two-triangles counterexample before moving on to components.

同构必定保留度数与连通分量。因此某个不变量不同就能排除同构，但仅有相同度数序列还不能确定。接着读独立的判别规则及六边形与两个三角形的反例，再进入连通分量。

Next distinguish undirected connected components from directed strong and weak connectivity. Undirected reachability forms equivalence classes; a digraph must preserve both directions, so one outward search is not an SCC decomposition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Undirected reachability groups vertices into nonoverlapping classes. The general idea is an [[等价关系|equivalence relation]]: a relation that is reflexive, symmetric and transitive. After the definition below, see why each reachability class, together with all original edges between its vertices, forms one connected component.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
无向可达把顶点分成互不重叠的类。这背后的一般概念是[[等价关系]]：同时满足自反、对称、传递的关系。先读下面的定义，再看为什么每个可达类加上其顶点之间的全部原有边，恰好形成一个连通分量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a directed graph, mutual reachability requires a route in each direction; these routes may differ. Forgetting all arrow directions instead gives weak components. In the chain $a\to b\to c$, one outward search reaches all three vertices, but no return routes exist, so the three strong components remain singletons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
有向图的互达要求两个方向各有一条路线，两条路线可以不同；忽略全部箭头得到的则是弱分量。在链 $a\to b\to c$ 中，一次向外搜索能到达全部三点，却没有返回路线，因此三个强分量仍分别是单点。

![[强连通与弱连通]]

把 reachability 变成算法时，BFS 与 DFS 先共享同一个发现不变量：第一次发现就标记并写 parent。在线性时间结论中还必须明确使用 adjacency lists。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To turn reachability into an algorithm, BFS and DFS first share one discovery invariant: mark a vertex and record its parent at first discovery. The linear-time claim must also name adjacency lists as the representation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[搜索标记与父森林]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The time bound counts both vertices and stored adjacency entries. It assumes constant-cost mark/parent access and suitable queue or stack operations. A fresh single-source search can inspect only its reachable region; initializing every vertex or printing every full path adds its own cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
时间界同时计算顶点与存储的邻接项，并以常数成本的标记/父记录访问及合适的队列或栈操作为前提。一次从全未发现状态开始的单源搜索可以只检查可达区域；初始化全部顶点或打印每条完整路径，还须另计成本。

![[邻接表图搜索复杂度]]

BFS 的 FIFO 顺序给真正的 unweighted distance layers；parent pointers 只从可能的多条 shortest paths 中保留一条，所以 tree 可以随 adjacency order 改变而 distances 不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BFS's FIFO order gives the true unweighted distance layers. Parent pointers retain only one of possibly many shortest paths, so adjacency order may change the tree without changing distances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[BFS 分层与无权最短路]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BFS minimizes edge count. Equal positive edge weights give the same ordering by total cost; if all weights are zero, every existing route has equal cost. Each reached non-root vertex has one parent in the previous distance layer. Following parent records backward retrieves a list; reverse that list to obtain the valid forward path in a directed graph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
BFS 最小化边数。共同边权为正数时，按总成本得到相同排序；若全为零，所有存在路线的成本都相同。每个已到达的非根顶点在前一距离层有一个父顶点。有向图中，倒查父记录只是取出一个列表；反转该列表，才得到合法的正向路径。

![[BFS 最短路树的顺序依赖]]

DFS 不承担 shortest-path 语义；它利用活动祖先链揭示 cycles。directed 与 undirected cycle detection 的差别集中在 parent edge，full traversal 又必须与 SCC decomposition 分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DFS carries no shortest-path guarantee; it uses the active ancestor chain to expose cycles. Directed and undirected cycle detection differ at the parent edge, and a full traversal must still be separated from SCC decomposition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[DFS 可达性与非最短路径]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The active DFS chain consists of unfinished recursive calls, not every previously discovered vertex or every pending stack entry. An edge back to this chain closes a directed cycle. In an undirected graph, first exclude the exact edge used to discover the current vertex: reading that same edge backward adds no new edge. A different parallel edge is a different case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
DFS 活动链由尚未完成的递归调用组成，并非所有已发现顶点或所有等待中的栈项。返回这条链的边闭合有向环。无向图则先排除用于发现当前顶点的精确父边：把同一条边反向读取，并没有增加新边；另一条平行边是不同情形。

![[有向 DFS 的环证书]]

![[无向 DFS 的父边例外]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full traversal retains all marks when choosing the next root. On $a\to b$, choosing $b$ first gives a later singleton tree at $a$, although $a$ reaches $b$ in the graph. A later tree therefore records newly discovered vertices, not necessarily every vertex its root can reach. This order dependence does not change the graph's strong or weak components.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
全图遍历在选择新根时保留全部标记。在 $a\to b$ 上先选 $b$，后来从 $a$ 得到的只是单点树，尽管原图中 $a$ 能到达 $b$。因此，后续树记录的是本轮新发现点，不一定包含根可达的全部顶点。这样的根序依赖不改变图本身的强、弱连通分量。

If both endpoints are unmatched, flipping the path gains one edge. Berge's theorem then states that only the absence of such a path certifies a maximum matching.

直接贪心停住后，已有边并非不可移动。alternating path 把“保留一条、撤下一条”的连锁改配写成路径；当两端都未匹配时，翻转会净增一条边。Berge 定理再说明：找不到这样的路径，才真正达到 maximum。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More precisely, an augmenting path starts and ends at distinct unmatched vertices. Flipping adds its unselected edges, removes its selected edges, and keeps every edge outside the path. When using search to establish that no path exists, complete the required exploration; a failed partial attempt is not a certificate. Berge’s equivalence is one way to prove optimality; attaining a matching-size upper bound is another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
准确地说，增广路两端是不同的未匹配顶点。翻转加入路上未选边、撤去路上已选边，路外边保持不变。若用搜索确认“不存在路径”，须完成所需范围的检查；部分尝试失败不构成证书。Berge 的等价判据是一种最优性证明途径；构造达到匹配大小上界也是一种。

![[增广路对称差]]

![[Berge最大匹配定理]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hall's theorem converts “can the left side be covered?” into a neighbourhood count for every left-side subset. One deficient set immediately proves infeasibility. Alternating search turns the frontier where an algorithm stops into exactly such a certificate, explaining why Hall's condition is sufficient as well as necessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
Hall 定理把“能否覆盖左侧”转成全部左侧子集的邻居计数。一个缺额集合立即证明不可行；交替搜索则把算法停止的前沿变成这种证书，也解释了 Hall condition 为什么不仅必要而且充分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here all graphs are finite and each original vertex has unit capacity. The neighborhood of a set is the union of its members’ options. A failed alternating search yields a shortage only after exploring the required reachable vertices; an empty set of unmatched left roots already means coverage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里图均有限，原图每个顶点容量为一。一个集合的邻居是各成员选项的并集。交替搜索必须检查完所需可达范围，失败才产生缺额；若未匹配左侧起点集为空，已经实现覆盖。

![[Hall 缺额证书]]

![[交替搜索的增广与缺额证书]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, return the theorem to a real assignment problem. State the sides, edge meaning, coverage, capacities, and objective. When every left vertex receives at most one unit assignment and right-side capacity counts interchangeable units, integer capacities can become slots or flow. Edge weights introduce an optimisation problem, while two-sided preferences add a no-blocking-pair stability requirement on top of matching feasibility. None is supplied by Hall's condition alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
最后把定理放回真实分配问题。先写清两侧、边、覆盖、容量和目标；当每个左点至多接受一次单位分配、右侧容量只计可互换单位时，整数容量可拆成 slots 或 flow。边权会引入优化问题，而双方偏好会在可行 matching 之上再加入“不存在 blocking pair”的稳定性要求。它们都不能被 Hall condition 一句话代替。

![[匹配建模要素]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before converting capacity into flow, define what can move on each arc and why a discrete optimum exists. The integrality theorem provides existence of an integral maximum, rather than a claim about every optimal flow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
把容量写成流之前，先定义弧上流量，再理解离散最优解为何存在。整数性定理保证存在整数最大流，并不声称每个最优流都为整数。

For preferences, first identify a blocking pair, then define a stable outcome. The following definitions use finite equal-size groups, complete rankings without ties, and one partner per person.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对于偏好，先识别阻挡对，再定义稳定结果。下面两项定义采用有限等大群体、完整无并列排序，以及每人一位伙伴。

### 3. 计数、渐近与组合原理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Counting, Asymptotics, and Combinatorial Principles*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结构问题常要求计算对象数量和增长速度，因此从图与数论进入组合计数和渐近分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Structural problems often require the number of objects and their rate of growth, leading from graphs and number theory into combinatorial counting and asymptotic analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[组合计数原理.canvas]]

计数先固定对象与相等标准，再选择证明结构。互斥分类使用加法，固定延伸数的分阶段构造使用乘法；若对象不好直接数，可以建立双射、修正固定重数表示，或对同一关联集做两次完备计数。鸽巢原理则只从总容量推出必然存在的拥挤。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Counting begins by fixing the objects and their equality criterion. Disjoint cases use addition; staged constructions with a fixed number of extensions use multiplication. Hard objects may be counted by a bijection, by correcting a constant representation multiplicity, or by counting one incidence set in two complete ways. <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Comparing growth scales need not require a ratio tending to one. Define upper and lower bounds separately, then combine them into equal order. These relations compare functions; they do not select an algorithm's best or worst case.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

### 4. 离散概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Discrete Probability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计数把有限可能性量化；离散概率再给这些可能性赋权，并研究随机变量、期望和集中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Counting quantifies a finite set of possibilities. Discrete probability assigns weights to those possibilities and studies random variables, expectations, and concentration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

概率首先需要定义 sample space、event 与 probability，并在信息到来后使用 conditional probability 与 Bayes rule。要把计数比值作为对任意事件通用的概率公式，基本 outcomes 必须 equiprobable。

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[随机计数的指标和]]

最后选择索引单位：对象、位置、类别和无序子结构会回答不同的计数问题。共同成功概率与相互独立是前文 [[Bernoulli和的二项分布|二项分布]] 结论的标准充分条件；缺少这些条件时，必须另查联合机制，不能仅凭边际作出二项判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally choose the indexing unit: objects, positions, categories, and unordered substructures answer different counting questions. A common success probability and mutual independence are standard sufficient conditions for a binomial sum. Without them, inspect the joint mechanism rather than inferring the sum’s distribution from its marginals alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[计数指标的索引策略]]

最后，当目标从 average 转为“偏离 average 多远的概率”时，先明确不等式只给 upper guarantee，而不是 exact probability；这能避免把一个宽松上界误读成预测值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, when the target changes from an average to the probability of a large deviation, first recognise that an inequality supplies an upper guarantee rather than an exact probability. This prevents a loose upper bound from being misread as a prediction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[尾界含义]]

读清结果含义后，再把 target event、threshold 与手头的 nonnegativity、mean、variance、independence 和 boundedness 写出来；选界是条件匹配，不是公式排名。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the result is interpreted correctly, write the target event, threshold, and available non-negativity, mean, variance, independence, and boundedness assumptions. Choosing a bound is an exercise in matching conditions, not ranking formulas.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[概率界选择]]

最少的信息路线从 Markov 开始：对 nonnegative variable，mean 已经能限制右尾，因为尾部上的每一单位概率都至少消耗 threshold 大小的均值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The minimal-information route begins with Markov. For a non-negative variable, the mean already controls the upper tail because each unit of tail probability consumes at least the threshold amount of mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Markov不等式]]

若还知道变量不可能低于某个确定基线，应先移除这段必然质量，再对剩余的非负部分使用 Markov。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the variable is also known never to fall below a deterministic baseline, remove that guaranteed mass before applying Markov to the remaining non-negative part.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[平移Markov界]]

当 finite variance 可用时，把 squared deviation 当作新的非负变量，就从 Markov 得到围绕 mean 的双侧 Chebyshev bound。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a finite variance is available, treating squared deviation as a new non-negative variable turns Markov into the two-sided Chebyshev bound around the mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Chebyshev不等式]]

对随机和使用 Chebyshev 前，必须先把 covariance cross terms 展开。pairwise independence 已足以完成这个二阶步骤，但不能因此假装已获得 mutual independence。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before using Chebyshev on a random sum, expand its covariance cross terms. Pairwise independence is enough for this second-order step, but it must not be promoted to mutual independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[和的方差协方差项]]

本课程前面已经分别嵌入 [[两两独立]]、[[相互独立]] 与 [[两两独立不推出相互独立]]；这里复用这条分界：variance addition 只需要控制每一对，而联合 MGF 的乘积分解需要更强的联合结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course has already embedded [[两两独立|pairwise independence]], [[相互独立|mutual independence]], and their [[两两独立不推出相互独立|boundary]]. Variance addition controls pairs, whereas factorising a joint MGF requires a stronger joint structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指数路线仍以 Markov 为核心，但先 exponentiate tail event，再在所有正参数中优化 MGF bound。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exponential route still rests on Markov, but it first exponentiates the tail event and then optimises the MGF bound over positive parameters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[指数Markov法]]

若各项 mutually independent 且落在 $[0,1]$，用该区间上的 convexity 控制每项 MGF，就得到适合 relative upper tail 的 multiplicative Chernoff bound。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For mutually independent terms in $[0,1]$, convexity on that interval controls each MGF and yields the multiplicative Chernoff bound for a relative upper tail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[乘法Chernoff上界]]

若问题更自然地写成 absolute/additive deviation，则保留各项支持区间长度，使用 general Hoeffding；区间长度决定 exponent 的常数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the problem is more naturally written as an absolute or additive deviation, retain the support-interval length of each term and use general Hoeffding. Those interval lengths determine the constant in the exponent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Hoeffding不等式]]

最后把 sum threshold 写成 $n\varepsilon$，便得到 bounded independent sample mean 的直接保证；$[0,1]$ 区间长度为 1，不能与较松的 symmetric constant 混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, writing the sum threshold as $n\varepsilon$ gives a direct guarantee for a bounded independent sample mean. The interval $[0,1]$ has length one and its constant must not be mixed with a looser symmetric bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
