## 为什么按这条主干组织
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the Course Follows This Backbone*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用证明方法控制论证，再建立离散结构、计数和概率这三组计算机科学数学工具。
课堂只能按时间线展开，这里按概念依赖重排：每一块先解决一个明确问题，再说明为什么下一块会出现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course first uses proof methods to control reasoning, then develops three groups of mathematical tools for computer science: discrete structures, counting, and probability.
A class can only unfold over time, but this view is reorganised by conceptual dependency: each block first solves a definite problem and then explains why the next block is needed.

### 2. 数论、RSA 与图结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Number Theory, RSA, and Graph Structures*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明方法保证离散结论可被检验；数论和图论提供随后要证明的具体结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Proof methods make discrete claims verifiable; number theory and graph theory provide the concrete structures to which those methods are applied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[数论、RSA 与图结构]]

### 3. 计数、渐近与组合原理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Counting, Asymptotics, and Combinatorial Principles*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结构问题常要求计算对象数量和增长速度，因此从图与数论进入组合计数和渐近分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Structural problems often require the number of objects and their rate of growth, leading from graphs and number theory into combinatorial counting and asymptotic analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[计数、渐近与组合原理]]

### 4. 离散概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Discrete Probability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计数把有限可能性量化；离散概率再给这些可能性赋权，并研究随机变量、期望和集中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Counting quantifies a finite set of possibilities. Discrete probability assigns weights to those possibilities and studies random variables, expectations, and concentration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[离散概率]]
