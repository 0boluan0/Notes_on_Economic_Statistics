## Study route / 学习路线

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lectures 1–7 develop probability models and discrete random variables; Lectures 8–12 extend these tools to continuous and joint distributions. Lectures 13–18 introduce Bernoulli and Poisson processes and Markov chains. Lectures 19–25 connect limit theorems to Bayesian and classical statistical inference. The course provides the probability spine and introductory inference; a mathematical statistics course supplies further depth in estimation, testing and regression.

The official prerequisites are single-variable and multivariable calculus, including sequences, limits, series, the chain rule and ordinary or multiple integrals. These tools can be reviewed at their first point of use. The [short-video companion](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-6-012-introduction-to-probability-spring-2018/) is an optional explanation source for a specific difficulty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第 1–7 讲建立概率模型与离散随机变量；第 8–12 讲进入连续及联合分布；第 13–18 讲学习伯努利过程、泊松过程与马尔可夫链；第 19–25 讲将极限定理连接到贝叶斯与经典统计推断。本课提供概率主干与推断入门，参数估计、检验与回归的进一步深度由数理统计课程补足。

官方前置是一元及多元微积分，具体用到数列、极限、级数、链式法则、普通积分与多重积分。这些工具可在首次使用时补习；RES.6-012 短视频作为局部困难的辅助解释来源。
