## 题型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Question Format*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 选择题与填空题共 40 分，包含少量概念题，以计算为主。
- 计算题 6 道，共 60 分。
- 不考复杂证明，但定义、条件和计算步骤必须会用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Multiple-choice and fill-in-the-blank questions are worth 40 marks in total. They include a small number of conceptual questions but focus mainly on calculations.
- Six calculation questions are worth 60 marks in total.
- Complicated proofs are excluded, but definitions, conditions, and calculation procedures must be usable in practice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第一章：概率论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Chapter 1: Probability Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

事件、条件概率与 Bayes 法则见 [[概率空间、条件概率与 Bayes 法则.canvas|概率论基础]]；条件期望及其关系见 [[条件期望.canvas|条件期望关系图]]。本页下列项目界定考试范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use [[概率空间、条件概率与 Bayes 法则.canvas|probability foundations]] for events, conditional probability, and Bayes' rule, and the [[条件期望.canvas|conditional-expectation map]] for conditional expectation and its relations. The list below defines the examinable scope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 随机试验、样本空间、事件、概率公理、条件概率和 Bayes 公式。
- 独立、互斥、不相关的区别；随机变量、分布函数、密度和随机变量函数。
- 期望、方差、协方差、矩、[[条件期望]]与[[全期望公式]]。
- $n$ 维随机变量函数的期望是重点；生成函数不考，特征函数不单独出计算题但需理解性质。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Random experiments, sample spaces, events, probability axioms, conditional probability, and Bayes' formula.
- The distinction among independence, mutual exclusivity, and zero correlation; random variables, distribution functions, densities, and functions of random variables.
- Expectation, variance, covariance, moments, conditional expectation, and the law of total expectation.
- Expectations of functions of $n$-dimensional random variables are important. Generating functions are excluded; characteristic functions will not appear as a standalone calculation but their properties must be understood.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第四章：离散时间 Markov 链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Chapter 4: Discrete-Time Markov Chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[离散时间马尔可夫链.canvas|离散时间马尔可夫链原子地图]]。

- 一步与 $n$ 步转移概率、[[DTMC转移复合|Chapman–Kolmogorov 方程]]，并区分[[n步转移非首达|占据概率与首次到达概率]]。
- [[马氏链沟通类|沟通类]]、[[状态周期|周期]]、[[常返与暂态|常返与暂态]]、[[闭沟通类|闭类]]、[[不可约链|不可约]]与[[有限链类分解|有限状态空间分解]]。
- [[Markov稳态分布|平稳分布]]的求解与[[有限链逐步收敛|长期分布条件]]；不要把“存在平稳分布”和“从任意初始分布收敛”混为一谈。
- 吸收链依次掌握[[吸收链标准形|标准形]]、[[基本矩阵存在条件|暂态块条件]]、[[吸收链基本矩阵|基本矩阵]]、[[吸收概率矩阵为NR|最终吸收概率]]与[[期望吸收时间为N1|平均吸收时间]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- One-step and $n$-step transition probabilities and the Chapman–Kolmogorov equation.
- Communicating classes, periodicity, recurrence, first passage, closed sets, irreducibility, and decomposition of the state space.
- Solving for a stationary distribution and checking conditions for a limiting distribution; do not confuse existence of a stationary distribution with convergence from every initial distribution.
- For absorbing chains: the [[吸收链标准形|canonical form]], [[基本矩阵存在条件|transient-block condition]], [[吸收链基本矩阵|fundamental matrix]], [[吸收概率矩阵为NR|eventual absorption probabilities $B=NR$]], and [[期望吸收时间为N1|mean absorption times $t=N\mathbf1$]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第六、七章：平稳过程、布朗运动与鞅
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Chapters 6 and 7: Stationary Processes, Brownian Motion, and Martingales*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 平稳过程的 [[自协方差与ACF|相关函数]]、[[谱测度与谱密度|功率谱]]、[[均值遍历|均值遍历]]与[[协方差遍历|相关遍历]]分别见对应原子。
- 布朗运动的完整定义见 [[标准布朗运动]]，有限维 Gaussian 律与协方差见 [[布朗有限维分布]]，反向识别条件见 [[协方差核刻画布朗运动]]；[[布朗运动不平稳|水平过程不平稳而增量平稳]]不能混淆。
- 路径层面分开掌握 [[布朗路径处处不可导]]、[[布朗路径无限总变差]] 与 [[布朗二次变差]]；模型变体见 [[带漂移布朗运动]] 和 [[带漂移布朗增量]]，越界问题见 [[布朗反射原理]]。
- 鞅的起点是信息结构：先由[[滤过定义|滤过]]理解信息怎样增长，再区分[[适应过程|适应]]、[[自然滤过]]与[[离散可预测过程|可预测]]。
- 条件均值层要会区分 [[鞅定义|鞅]]、[[次鞅]]、[[超鞅]]与[[鞅差序列]]，并单独掌握[[鞅差部分和]]；[[适应不推鞅]]和[[恒定均值不推鞅]]分别阻断两条常见的错误反推。随后再使用 [[凸变换产生次鞅]]、[[恒均值次鞅即鞅]]和[[Doob分解]]。
- 随机时间层先核验 [[停时定义|停时]]，再区分 [[离散首次到达停时|首次到达]]与[[末次到达通常非停时|末次到达]]，并理解 [[停时信息集]]、[[停时信息单调性]]和[[停止过程保持鞅]]。
- 使用 [[可选停止条件]]前要明确命中哪一条充分控制；[[一致可积与L1收敛|一致可积]]解释了为什么截断期望可以传到随机终值，[[无限翻倍破坏可选停止]]则给出精确失败机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- For stationary-process correlation functions, spectra, mean ergodicity, and correlation ergodicity, see the linked atomic notes above.
- For Brownian motion, separate the definition, finite-dimensional Gaussian law, converse covariance characterization, non-stationary levels, path roughness, quadratic variation, drifted models, and reflection principle using the linked atoms above.
- For martingales, distinguish filtration, natural filtration, adaptedness, predictability, martingale differences, martingales, submartingales, and supermartingales before applying Doob decomposition.
- For random times, verify the stopping-time property, identify the information available at the stopping time, and separate the stopped-process theorem from the extra controls required by optional stopping; uniform integrability and the doubling counterexample mark the crucial boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
