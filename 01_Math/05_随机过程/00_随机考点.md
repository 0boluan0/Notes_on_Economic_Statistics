## 题型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Question Format*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 选择题与填空题共 40 分，包含少量概念题，以计算为主。
- 计算题 6 道，共 60 分。
- 不考复杂证明，但定义、条件和计算步骤必须会用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Multiple-choice and fill-in-the-blank questions are worth 40 marks in total. They include a small number of conceptual questions but focus mainly on calculations.
- Six calculation questions are worth 60 marks in total.
- Complicated proofs are excluded, but definitions, conditions, and calculation procedures must be usable in practice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第一章：概率论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Chapter 1: Probability Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[概率论基础]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Corresponds to [[概率论基础|Foundations of Probability]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 随机试验、样本空间、事件、概率公理、条件概率和 Bayes 公式。
- 独立、互斥、不相关的区别；随机变量、分布函数、密度和随机变量函数。
- 期望、方差、协方差、矩、条件期望与全期望公式。
- $n$ 维随机变量函数的期望是重点；生成函数不考，特征函数不单独出计算题但需理解性质。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Random experiments, sample spaces, events, probability axioms, conditional probability, and Bayes' formula.
- The distinction among independence, mutual exclusivity, and zero correlation; random variables, distribution functions, densities, and functions of random variables.
- Expectation, variance, covariance, moments, conditional expectation, and the law of total expectation.
- Expectations of functions of $n$-dimensional random variables are important. Generating functions are excluded; characteristic functions will not appear as a standalone calculation but their properties must be understood.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第二章：随机过程概念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Chapter 2: Concepts of Stochastic Processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[随机过程、平稳性与遍历性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Corresponds to [[随机过程、平稳性与遍历性|Stochastic Processes, Stationarity, and Ergodicity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $X(t,\omega)$、有限维分布、均值、方差、协方差和相关函数。
- 二阶矩、正交增量与独立增量。
- 严平稳、宽平稳和遍历性的定义、区别与常用判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $X(t,\omega)$, finite-dimensional distributions, the mean, variance, covariance, and correlation functions.
- Second moments, orthogonal increments, and independent increments.
- Definitions, distinctions, and common tests for strict stationarity, wide-sense stationarity, and ergodicity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第三章：泊松与更新过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Chapter 3: Poisson and Renewal Processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[泊松过程与更新过程]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Corresponds to [[泊松过程与更新过程|Poisson and Renewal Processes]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 泊松过程的定义、均值、方差、协方差和无记忆性。
- 到达间隔、等待时间、给定到达次数后的到达时刻分布。
- 非齐次泊松过程的强度与累计强度；复合泊松过程的均值和方差。
- 简单分解是重点；滤过泊松过程不考；更新过程掌握基本概念和性质。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Definition, mean, variance, covariance, and memorylessness of a Poisson process.
- Interarrival times, waiting times, and the distribution of arrival times conditional on the number of arrivals.
- Intensity and cumulative intensity of a nonhomogeneous Poisson process; mean and variance of a compound Poisson process.
- Simple decompositions are important. Filtered Poisson processes are excluded. For renewal processes, know the basic concepts and properties.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第四章：离散时间 Markov 链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Chapter 4: Discrete-Time Markov Chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[离散时间马尔可夫链]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Corresponds to [[离散时间马尔可夫链|Discrete-Time Markov Chains]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 一步与 $n$ 步转移概率、Chapman–Kolmogorov 方程。
- 沟通类、周期、常返、首达、闭集、不可约与状态空间分解。
- 平稳分布的求解与长期分布条件；不要把“存在平稳分布”和“从任意初始分布收敛”混为一谈。
- 吸收链的表示与基本计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- One-step and $n$-step transition probabilities and the Chapman–Kolmogorov equation.
- Communicating classes, periodicity, recurrence, first passage, closed sets, irreducibility, and decomposition of the state space.
- Solving for a stationary distribution and checking conditions for a limiting distribution; do not confuse existence of a stationary distribution with convergence from every initial distribution.
- Representation and basic calculations for absorbing chains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第五章：连续时间 Markov 链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Chapter 5: Continuous-Time Markov Chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[连续时间马尔可夫链]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Corresponds to [[连续时间马尔可夫链|Continuous-Time Markov Chains]].
