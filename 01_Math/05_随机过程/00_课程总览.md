### 1. 概率论基础
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Probability Foundations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是本课程的默认起点；先用快速恢复建立对象、例子和问题感，再进入详细内容。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the default starting point. Use the quick-recovery section to establish the objects, examples, and motivating questions before moving into the details.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[概率空间、条件概率与 Bayes 法则]]

概率空间先规定结果、事件和概率怎样合法组合；随机变量再把结果映射成数值，使分布、期望和方差可以计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A probability space first specifies how outcomes, events, and probabilities fit together. A random variable then maps outcomes to numbers, making distributions, expectations, and variances calculable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[随机变量、分布与矩]]

分布与矩描述未获得额外信息时的总体不确定性；条件期望和独立性进一步说明信息到来后哪些预测会改变、哪些结构保持不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distributions and moments describe uncertainty before additional information arrives. Conditional expectation and independence then determine which predictions change with new information and which structures remain intact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[条件期望与独立性]]

先澄清变量之间的条件关系，才能判断重复观测能否稳定聚合；大数定律与中心极限定理随后给出样本平均的长期位置和波动尺度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conditional relationships among variables must be clear before repeated observations can be aggregated safely. The law of large numbers and central limit theorem then describe the long-run location and fluctuation scale of sample averages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[大数定律与中心极限定理]]

### 2. 随机过程、平稳性与遍历性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Stochastic Processes, Stationarity, and Ergodicity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机变量、联合分布和条件期望是单时点语言；随机过程把它们扩展到一族按时间索引的变量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Random variables, joint distributions, and conditional expectations describe uncertainty at a single time. A stochastic process extends this language to a family of variables indexed by time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[随机过程基础]]

随机过程先把随机变量按时间或空间索引成一个整体；平稳性与遍历性再回答单条长路径何时能代表这个整体的概率规律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stochastic process first organises random variables as an indexed family over time or space. Stationarity and ergodicity then ask when one long path can reveal the probability law of that family.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[平稳性、遍历性与谱]]

### 3. 泊松过程与更新过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Poisson and Renewal Processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般随机过程太宽；泊松与更新过程专门描述事件何时到达，并用独立间隔或更新结构简化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
General stochastic processes are too broad for many arrival problems. Poisson and renewal processes focus on when events occur and simplify the analysis through independent interarrival times or a renewal structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[泊松过程]]

泊松过程以指数等待时间和无记忆性给出最简到达模型；更新过程放松等待时间分布，从而覆盖更一般的重复事件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Poisson process gives the simplest arrival model through exponential waiting times and memorylessness. A renewal process relaxes the waiting-time distribution and therefore covers more general recurring events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[更新过程]]

### 4. 离散时间马尔可夫链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Discrete-Time Markov Chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到达过程记录事件时刻；离散时间 Markov 链改为记录系统所在状态，并用一步转移控制未来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Arrival processes record event times. A discrete-time Markov chain instead records the system's state and uses one-step transitions to govern its future.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[离散时间马尔可夫链]]

### 5. 连续时间马尔可夫链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Continuous-Time Markov Chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

离散链每一步耗时相同；连续时间链加入随机停留时间和生成矩阵来描述真实时间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each step of a discrete chain takes the same abstract amount of time. A continuous-time chain adds random holding times and a generator matrix to model real time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[连续时间马尔可夫链]]

### 6. 布朗运动与鞅
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Brownian Motion and Martingales*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Markov 链的状态通常离散；布朗运动把状态和时间都连续化，鞅则抽取‘给定现在无法预测增益’的核心。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Markov-chain states are often discrete. Brownian motion makes both state and time continuous, while martingales isolate the principle that, given current information, future gains cannot be predicted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[布朗运动]]

布朗运动提供连续时间随机扰动的基准；鞅把“给定当前信息，未来没有可预测漂移”抽象出来，并让停时问题可以严格讨论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Brownian motion provides the benchmark for continuous-time random disturbance. Martingales abstract the idea of no predictable drift given current information and make stopping-time questions precise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[鞅、停时与可选停止]]
