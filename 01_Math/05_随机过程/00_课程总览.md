### 2. 随机过程、平稳性与遍历性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Stochastic Processes, Stationarity, and Ergodicity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单个随机变量描述一个时点或一次试验中的随机量；随机过程把共享同一概率空间的一族随机变量按时间或其他指标组织起来。任取有限多个指标所得的联合分布，再负责描述这些坐标怎样一起变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single random variable describes uncertainty at one index or in one trial. A stochastic process organises a family of random variables on one probability space, indexed by time or another set; finite-dimensional joint laws then describe how finitely many coordinates vary together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3. 泊松过程与更新过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Poisson and Renewal Processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般随机过程太宽；泊松与更新过程专门描述事件何时到达，并用独立间隔或更新结构简化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
General stochastic processes are too broad for many arrival problems. Poisson and renewal processes focus on when events occur and simplify the analysis through independent interarrival times or a renewal structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**原子地图：** [[泊松过程.canvas|打开泊松过程原子地图]]。

![[齐次泊松过程]]

![[泊松过程矩]]

![[泊松指数等价]]

![[泊松到达时刻]]

![[泊松条件时刻]]

![[非齐次泊松过程]]

![[复合泊松过程]]

![[泊松独立分流]]

泊松过程以指数等待时间和无记忆性给出最简到达模型；更新过程放松等待时间分布，从而覆盖更一般的重复事件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Poisson process gives the simplest arrival model through exponential waiting times and memorylessness. A renewal process relaxes the waiting-time distribution and therefore covers more general recurring events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**原子地图：** [[更新过程.canvas|打开更新过程原子地图]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Atom map:** [[更新过程.canvas|Open the renewal-process atom map]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把一次次间隔、更新时刻和累计计数写成同一对象；以后所有有限时点与长期问题都从这个对偶出发。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First express inter-renewal times, renewal epochs, and the cumulative count as one object; every finite-time and long-run question starts from this dual description.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[更新过程定义]]

公式成立以前，先确认“更换”或“维修”真的让未来周期独立且回到同一分布；否则应改模型，而不是硬套更新定理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before using the formulas, verify that replacement or repair truly makes the next cycle independent with the same law; otherwise change the model rather than forcing a renewal theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[普通更新模型边界]]

模型边界固定后，更新函数描述有限时点的期望计数，首周期分解则给出精确递归。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the model boundary is fixed, the renewal function describes the finite-time expected count, and first-cycle decomposition gives its exact recursion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[更新函数更新方程]]

精确有限时点关系不能偷换成线性等式；长期速率是另一个需要极限定理的命题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An exact finite-time recursion must not be replaced by a linear identity; the long-run rate is a separate limiting theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[长期更新率]]

在周期上附加成本、收益或运行时间后，长期率由同一完整周期的分子和分母共同决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After attaching cost, benefit, or uptime to each cycle, the long-run rate must use the numerator and denominator from the same complete cycle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[更新奖励定理]]

若问题是在任意观察时点询问“已经多久、还要多久、这一周期总共多长”，必须先区分三个随机量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an arbitrary observation time, distinguish how long the cycle has run, how long remains, and the total length of the current cycle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[年龄、剩余寿命与总间隔]]

按时间抽样不会均匀抽中周期：长周期占据更多时间，因此产生长度偏置和二阶矩条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sampling in time does not sample cycles uniformly: long cycles occupy more time, creating length bias and a second-moment condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[更新检验悖论]]

最后把更新奖励与真正重置的假设合并，才能比较不同预防更换年龄的长期单位时间成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally combine renewal reward with the genuine-reset assumption to compare long-run cost per unit time across preventive replacement ages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[预防性更换成本率]]

### 4. 离散时间马尔可夫链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Discrete-Time Markov Chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到达过程记录事件时刻；离散时间 Markov 链改为记录系统所在状态，并用一步转移控制未来。

Arrival processes record event times. A discrete-time Markov chain instead records the system's state and uses one-step transitions to govern its future.
