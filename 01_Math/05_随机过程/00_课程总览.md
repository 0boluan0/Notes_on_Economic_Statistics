### 2. 随机过程、平稳性与遍历性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Stochastic Processes, Stationarity, and Ergodicity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机变量、联合分布和条件期望是单时点语言；随机过程把它们扩展到一族按时间索引的变量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Random variables, joint distributions, and conditional expectations describe uncertainty at a single time. A stochastic process extends this language to a family of variables indexed by time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[随机过程、平稳性与遍历性]]

### 3. 泊松过程与更新过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Poisson and Renewal Processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般随机过程太宽；泊松与更新过程专门描述事件何时到达，并用独立间隔或更新结构简化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
General stochastic processes are too broad for many arrival problems. Poisson and renewal processes focus on when events occur and simplify the analysis through independent interarrival times or a renewal structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[泊松过程与更新过程]]

### 4. 离散时间马尔可夫链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Discrete-Time Markov Chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到达过程记录事件时刻；离散时间 Markov 链改为记录系统所在状态，并用一步转移控制未来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Arrival processes record event times. A discrete-time Markov chain instead records the system's state and uses one-step transitions to govern its future.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[离散时间马尔可夫链]]

### 5. 连续时间马尔可夫链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Continuous-Time Markov Chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

离散链每一步耗时相同；连续时间链加入随机停留时间和生成矩阵来描述真实时间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each step of a discrete chain takes the same abstract amount of time. A continuous-time chain adds random holding times and a generator matrix to model real time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[连续时间马尔可夫链]]

### 6. 布朗运动与鞅
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Brownian Motion and Martingales*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Markov 链的状态通常离散；布朗运动把状态和时间都连续化，鞅则抽取‘给定现在无法预测增益’的核心。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Markov-chain states are often discrete. Brownian motion makes both state and time continuous, while martingales isolate the principle that, given current information, future gains cannot be predicted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[布朗运动与鞅]]
