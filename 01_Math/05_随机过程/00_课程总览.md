### 2. 随机过程、平稳性与遍历性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Stochastic Processes, Stationarity, and Ergodicity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单个随机变量描述一个时点或一次试验中的随机量；随机过程把共享同一概率空间的一族随机变量按时间或其他指标组织起来。任取有限多个指标所得的联合分布，再负责描述这些坐标怎样一起变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single random variable describes uncertainty at one index or in one trial. A stochastic process organises a family of random variables on one probability space, indexed by time or another set; finite-dimensional joint laws then describe how finitely many coordinates vary together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!map] 关系图
> [[随机过程基础.canvas|打开随机过程基础 Canvas]]：区分指标集、有限维联合分布、边际信息与样本路径版本。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with one probability experiment shared by all observation times. One outcome may encode an entire sequence of events; the model also specifies how its observations vary jointly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先从所有观测时点共用的一个概率实验开始。一个结果可以编码整串事件；模型还需要指定各个观测怎样共同变化。

![[随机过程]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Selecting several times produces one joint law. Compare a coin copied forever with fresh independent tosses: each time has the same marginal, but the selected pairs behave differently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选取几个时点，就得到一个联合律。把同一枚硬币的结果一直复制，与每次独立重抛作比较：各时点边际相同，选出的配对却不同。

![[有限维分布]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now fix the outcome instead of the observation times. The resulting function is one sample path; a single observed path does not reveal how probable all the alternatives are.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接下来固定结果，而不是固定观测时点。得到的函数是一条样本路径；只观察一条路径，还不知道其他可能路径各有多大概率。

![[样本路径]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now ask whether all the proposed finite observations can coexist. Projection consistency makes their overlapping descriptions agree; the extension theorem then supplies a real-valued process law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再问拟定的有限观测能否同时成立。投影一致性让重叠描述相容，扩张定理随后给出实值过程律。

![[有限维分布决定律]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem needs joint laws of every finite size. Repeating one draw and drawing independently have the same one-time marginals but different prediction and increment behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定理需要每种有限大小的联合律。重复一次抽值与独立重抽虽然边际相同，预测和增量行为却不同。

![[边际分布不定时序依赖]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even matching every finite-dimensional law does not settle the regularity of a particular version. A random spike shows why fixed observation times and an entire path impose different requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使全部有限维分布相同，也没有决定具体版本的路径正则性。随机尖峰说明：固定时点观测与整条路径提出的是不同要求。

![[有限维分布不定路径正则性]]

随机过程先把随机变量按时间或空间索引成一个整体；平稳性与遍历性再回答单条长路径何时能代表这个整体的概率规律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stochastic process first organises random variables as an indexed family over time or space. Stationarity and ergodicity then ask when one long path can reveal the probability law of that family.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[严平稳定义]]

![[宽平稳定义]]

先把两种平稳性的对象分开，再检查它们之间的蕴含为什么需要矩条件，以及两个反例分别打断哪一条错误推理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First separate the objects defined by strict and wide-sense stationarity. Then examine why the implication needs a moment condition and which mistaken converse each counterexample blocks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[严平稳与宽平稳]]

![[Cauchy严平稳反例]]

![[宽平稳非严平稳反例]]

![[平稳性路径边界]]

接着进入二阶结构：先区分总体与时间两个平均方向，再统一 covariance、raw correlation 与 ACF 记号，之后才能检验核、联合过程和频谱是否合法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next move into second-order structure: distinguish ensemble and time averages, fix the covariance/raw-correlation/ACF convention, and only then test kernels, joint processes, and spectra.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[总体平均与时间平均]]

![[自协方差与ACF]]

![[自协方差核条件]]

![[联合宽平稳互协方差]]

![[谱测度与谱密度]]

![[谱峰解释边界]]

有了一条路径以后，必须明确要恢复的是均值还是固定滞后的相关；随机常数反例说明平稳不够，样本均值方差公式才给出可检查的二阶判据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With one observed path, specify whether the target is the mean or a fixed-lag correlation. The random-constant example shows that stationarity is insufficient, while the sample-mean variance formula supplies a checkable second-order criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Finally use the Gaussian exception to see when second-order information determines the full law, then tighten the boundaries around uncorrelatedness, matching ACFs, ergodicity, and mixing.

### 3. 泊松过程与更新过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Poisson and Renewal Processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般随机过程太宽；泊松与更新过程专门描述事件何时到达，并用独立间隔或更新结构简化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
General stochastic processes are too broad for many arrival problems. Poisson and renewal processes focus on when events occur and simplify the analysis through independent interarrival times or a renewal structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**原子地图：** [[泊松过程.canvas|打开泊松过程原子地图]]。

![[齐次泊松过程]]

![[泊松过程矩]]

![[泊松指数等价]]

![[泊松到达时刻]]

![[泊松条件时刻]]

![[非齐次泊松过程]]

![[复合泊松过程]]

![[泊松独立分流]]

泊松过程以指数等待时间和无记忆性给出最简到达模型；更新过程放松等待时间分布，从而覆盖更一般的重复事件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Poisson process gives the simplest arrival model through exponential waiting times and memorylessness. A renewal process relaxes the waiting-time distribution and therefore covers more general recurring events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**原子地图：** [[更新过程.canvas|打开更新过程原子地图]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Atom map:** [[更新过程.canvas|Open the renewal-process atom map]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把一次次间隔、更新时刻和累计计数写成同一对象；以后所有有限时点与长期问题都从这个对偶出发。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First express inter-renewal times, renewal epochs, and the cumulative count as one object; every finite-time and long-run question starts from this dual description.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[更新过程定义]]

公式成立以前，先确认“更换”或“维修”真的让未来周期独立且回到同一分布；否则应改模型，而不是硬套更新定理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before using the formulas, verify that replacement or repair truly makes the next cycle independent with the same law; otherwise change the model rather than forcing a renewal theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[普通更新模型边界]]

模型边界固定后，更新函数描述有限时点的期望计数，首周期分解则给出精确递归。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the model boundary is fixed, the renewal function describes the finite-time expected count, and first-cycle decomposition gives its exact recursion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[更新函数更新方程]]

精确有限时点关系不能偷换成线性等式；长期速率是另一个需要极限定理的命题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An exact finite-time recursion must not be replaced by a linear identity; the long-run rate is a separate limiting theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[长期更新率]]

在周期上附加成本、收益或运行时间后，长期率由同一完整周期的分子和分母共同决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After attaching cost, benefit, or uptime to each cycle, the long-run rate must use the numerator and denominator from the same complete cycle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[更新奖励定理]]

若问题是在任意观察时点询问“已经多久、还要多久、这一周期总共多长”，必须先区分三个随机量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an arbitrary observation time, distinguish how long the cycle has run, how long remains, and the total length of the current cycle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[年龄、剩余寿命与总间隔]]

按时间抽样不会均匀抽中周期：长周期占据更多时间，因此产生长度偏置和二阶矩条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sampling in time does not sample cycles uniformly: long cycles occupy more time, creating length bias and a second-moment condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[更新检验悖论]]

最后把更新奖励与真正重置的假设合并，才能比较不同预防更换年龄的长期单位时间成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally combine renewal reward with the genuine-reset assumption to compare long-run cost per unit time across preventive replacement ages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4. 离散时间马尔可夫链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Discrete-Time Markov Chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到达过程记录事件时刻；离散时间 Markov 链改为记录系统所在状态，并用一步转移控制未来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Arrival processes record event times. A discrete-time Markov chain instead records the system's state and uses one-step transitions to govern its future.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**原子地图：** [[离散时间马尔可夫链.canvas|打开离散时间马尔可夫链原子地图]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Atomic map:** [[离散时间马尔可夫链.canvas|Open the discrete-time Markov-chain atomic map]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先建立对象本身，再把“状态是否已经汇总了必要历史”与“转移规则是否随日历时点改变”拆开。状态不足时，扩充状态是一种建模修复；时间齐次则是另一项独立假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First specify what one step and one state mean. This course uses finite or countable states; the Markov condition compares forecasts with and without the earlier path, while time homogeneity remains a separate assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先规定一步和一个状态各指什么。本课程使用有限或可数状态；Markov条件比较是否加入早期路径的两种预测，时间齐次仍是独立假设。

![[离散时间Markov链]]

![[Markov性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare forecasts for different histories assigned the same state. Sufficiency concerns a specified information set and future law, and a usable state must also carry enough information to update.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较那些被归入同一状态的不同历史所给出的预测。充分性针对指定信息和未来分布；可用状态还必须保留继续更新所需的信息。

![[Markov充分状态]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the next observation depends only on the latest k values, collect those values into a shifting tuple. This proves a first-order representation; a time-invariant rule is a separate requirement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若下一观测只依赖最近k个值，把它们组成逐步移位的元组，就得到一阶表示；规则不随时间改变是另一项要求。

![[状态扩充]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the state is sufficient, ask whether the same transition rule can be used at every date. A known seasonal rule may change while the process remains Markov.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

状态充分之后，再问每个日期能否重复使用同一转移规则。已知季节使规则变化时，过程仍然可以具有Markov性。

![[DTMC时间齐次转移核]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the Markov row or column convention, then compose transitions with Chapman–Kolmogorov. Column probabilities obey $x_{k+1}=Px_k$; the same transitions use $R=P^T$ and $p_{k+1}=p_kR$ for row probabilities. Occupancy at step $n$ differs from first arrival at step $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

然后固定 Markov 矩阵的行列约定，再用 Chapman–Kolmogorov 方程复合多步转移。第 $n$ 步位于某状态和首次在第 $n$ 步到达该状态是两个不同对象。

![[Markov矩阵]]

![[Markov矩阵左右约定]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Split a multistep route at an intermediate time and add its possible intermediate states. With changing kernels, preserve the order of the dates when multiplying.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在中间时点拆开多步路线，对所有可能的中间状态求和。核随时间改变时，相乘必须保留日期先后。

![[DTMC转移复合]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An entry of a matrix power checks the state at the end. First passage also forbids earlier visits; for an absorbing target the matrix entry gives cumulative arrival, not arrival exactly at that step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵幂条目只检查终点时刻在哪里；首达还禁止早先访问。目标为吸收状态时，矩阵条目给出累计到达概率，不是恰在该步到达。

![[n步转移非首达]]

接着从正概率路径读状态结构。沟通类、闭沟通类和不可约链是三个不同概念；常返与暂态先定义，有限链的类分解定理随后才使用这些词。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under a fixed transition rule, first group mutually reachable states into maximal classes. Positive routes express possibility; they do not assert certain return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定转移规则后，先把互相可达的状态归入最大类。正概率路线表达可能性，并没有断言必然返回。

![[马氏链沟通类]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A class is closed when no transition can leave it. Motion can continue inside, and incoming routes from outside are allowed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

类没有向外的转移时才闭；类内仍可持续运动，也允许外部路线进入。

![[闭沟通类]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ask next whether the whole declared state space forms one class. Choosing an initial state inside a closed class does not change the irreducibility of the original full chain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再问整个指定状态空间是否就是一个类。把初态选在某个闭类内部，不会改变原全链的不可约性。

![[不可约链]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep three tests separate: no exit from a set, mutual reachability within a maximal class, and one class covering the whole state space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分清三个检查：集合是否没有出口、最大类内部是否互达，以及一个类是否覆盖整个状态空间。

![[闭类不等于不可约]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replace a short observation of visits with an infinite-horizon question: starting here, is a positive-time return certain? A self-loop already counts as a return at step one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把短期访问记录换成无限时域问题：从这里出发，是否以概率一在正时刻回返？自环已经算一步回返。

![[常返与暂态]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only after establishing recurrence do we classify the mean waiting time. The integer random walk shows that a finite realised return time can still have infinite expectation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确认常返，再按平均等待分类。整数随机游走说明：实际回返时间几乎必然有限，期望仍可以无穷。

![[正常返与零常返]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A period is the gcd of every possible positive return length. A self-loop is sufficient for period one, but return lengths three and four also establish period one without a self-loop.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

周期是全部可能正回返步数的最大公约数。自环足以保证周期一，但三步和四步都可返回，也能在没有自环时证明周期一。

![[状态周期]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Connecting routes prove that distinct communicating states share a period, even when the class has exits. The next theorem explains the proof and the no-return singleton exception.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

往返路线证明不同的互通状态周期相同，即使该类有出口也成立。下面的定理解释证明，以及无法正时刻返回的单点例外。

![[沟通状态周期相同]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The finite-state theorem turns the graph into a long-run statement about paths: some closed class is reached almost surely. A possible route alone is insufficient; a uniform positive chance over finite blocks supplies the probability-one argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限状态定理把转移图变成关于轨迹的长期结论：以概率一进入某个闭类。只找到一条可能路线还不够，还需用有限区段内统一的正概率给出论证。

![[有限链类分解]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First define a stationary distribution, then ask about existence, uniqueness and convergence separately. Each finite irreducible closed class has a unique stationary distribution; the stationary distributions of a finite reducible chain are mixtures of those class distributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长期行为先定义稳态，再分别问存在、唯一与收敛。有限不可约链先给每个闭类唯一稳态，有限可约链的全部稳态随后才由这些类内分布作凸组合。

![[Markov稳态分布]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finiteness already guarantees existence, without irreducibility or aperiodicity. The average-distribution argument below proves that limited claim; the following class structure supplies stronger conclusions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限性本身已经保证存在，无须不可约或非周期。下面的分布平均论证只证明这个结论，后续类结构再提供更强结果。

![[有限Markov链稳态存在]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stationary distribution must be the actual initial law to make a homogeneous Markov chain stationary from the outset. Possessing such a distribution is a different statement from starting in it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要让齐次Markov链从一开始就平稳，实际初始律必须采用一个平稳分布。拥有这种分布，与实际从它开始，是两件事。

![[齐次不等于平稳]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one finite irreducible region, existence strengthens to uniqueness and positive mass at every state. This conclusion already holds for a periodic cycle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在一个有限不可约区域里，存在性进一步成为唯一性及每个状态质量为正。周期循环也已经满足这一结论。

![[有限不可约链稳态]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With several closed classes, solve the stationary proportions within each class and choose how much total mass to assign to it. Nonnegative weights summing to one give the entire family; transient coordinates receive no stationary mass.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若有多个闭类，先确定各类内部的平稳比例，再决定分给各类多少总质量。非负且总和为一的权重给出全部分布，暂态坐标不承载平稳质量。

![[有限链平稳分解]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The probability-mixture description gives a useful algebraic test: uniqueness means that the full linear fixed space has one dimension. The set of normalized probability distributions itself is not a vector space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

概率混合的描述还给出一个代数检验：唯一性意味着整个线性固定空间一维；归一化概率分布的集合本身不是向量空间。

![[Markov稳态唯一判据]]

详细平衡和时间可逆是两个可分别复习的概念；它们彼此等价并推出平稳，而一般平稳链仍可保留净循环流。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weight each directional transition by the probability of its starting state. Equal weighted flows need not mean equal transition probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给每个方向的转移概率乘上起点质量；加权流量相等，并不要求转移概率相等。

![[DTMC详细平衡]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now compare a complete stationary recording with its reverse. The initial law matters, and the symmetry can hold even when states alternate periodically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再比较整段平稳录像与它的倒放。初始律很重要；即使状态周期交替，也可以具有这种对称性。

![[DTMC可逆性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Move the probability mass along a finite path using pairwise balance. This proves the equivalence without division by stationary masses, including zero masses; a circulating ring explains why stationarity is weaker.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利用逐对平衡沿有限路径传递概率质量，不必除以平稳质量，便能证明等价，包括零质量情形；循环流反例说明平稳性为何更弱。

![[详细平衡与可逆]]

稳态唯一仍不等于从任意初态逐步收敛。非周期给出逐步路线；若周期造成振荡，Cesàro 分布平均与单路径时间平均是并列的替代结论，不是谁排在谁之后。

![[稳态唯一不推收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Matrix-power convergence asks whether all starting distributions have limits, which may differ. The spectral condition allows a repeated fixed eigenvalue but excludes every other eigenvalue on the unit circle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵幂收敛询问全部初始分布是否各自有极限，极限可以不同。谱条件允许固定特征值重复，但排除单位圆上的其他特征值。

![[Markov矩阵幂收敛判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Add uniqueness to convergent powers to obtain a common stationary limit. This is stronger than having a limit for each starting state separately, and it does not require every state to have positive stationary probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在幂收敛之外再加唯一性，就得到共同稳态极限。这比每个出发状态分别有极限更强，也没有要求每个状态的稳态概率为正。

![[Markov幂的秩一稳态极限]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite irreducible chain, aperiodicity supplies the missing condition for convergence from every start. Keep this late-time distribution separate from the averages that follow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限不可约链，非周期补上从每个初态逐步收敛的条件。先分清较晚时点的分布与后面的平均。

![[有限链逐步收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On countably many states, irreducibility and aperiodicity do not supply a probability equilibrium by themselves. Require a stationary probability and then use this separate convergence theorem. It will later justify observing a continuous-time chain at equally spaced times.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

状态可数无限时，不可约与非周期本身不能保证有概率平衡。补上平稳概率，再用这条独立收敛定理；之后，它将为连续时间链的等间隔观察提供前置。

![[可数Markov链平衡收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Average the transition probabilities over the first N dates. Under irreducible positive recurrence this removes periodic oscillation and yields stationary masses, including on countable state spaces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把前 N 个时点的转移概率取平均。不可约正常返时，这能消除周期振荡并得到平稳质量，也适用于可数状态空间。

![[周期链Cesaro平均]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now average values actually observed along one random path. The almost-sure theorem for bounded observations is a separate conclusion; the deterministic average of probabilities does not by itself prove it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在平均同一条随机路径上实际观察到的数值。有界观测的几乎必然收敛是另一个结论，不能仅靠确定性的概率平均来证明。

![[Markov时间平均收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A return cycle links stationary mass to mean positive return time. Count its starting visit once, exclude its ending visit, and normalize within the irreducible positive recurrent class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回返周期把平稳质量与平均正回返连接起来。起始访问计一次、终点访问不重复计算，并在不可约正常返类内归一化。

![[Kac回返公式]]

首达问题从第一步方程开始。几乎必达不自动保证有限期望；进入吸收链后，也要依次验证必然吸收、标准分块和暂态块的谱条件，才能使用基本矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify whether the target is ever reached, including time zero. The boundary is one on the target; finite-horizon iteration explains why the minimal nonnegative solution is the correct probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先明确是否最终命中目标，并包括时刻零。目标上的边界为一；有限时限迭代说明为什么最小非负解才是真实概率。

![[命中概率第一步方程]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the mean, replace that boundary by zero and charge one step outside the target. Capped waiting times justify the equation even when the full mean is infinite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求均值时，把目标边界改成零，并在目标外计一步成本。封顶等待让完整均值为无穷时，方程仍有依据。

![[期望命中时间方程]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check the implication using a fair walk started one step from zero. Finite barriers show both certain arrival and an unbounded sequence of lower bounds on its mean; finite homogeneous chains have a different boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用距零一步的公平游走检查这个推论。有限边界同时证明必达，以及平均时间具有无界下界；有限齐次链的结论则不同。

![[必达不推有限期望]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin locally: once this state is entered, can the next step ever leave it under the fixed rule? Routes into the state do not decide whether it is absorbing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先在单个状态上检查：固定规则下，进入后下一步还能否离开？外部进入路线并不决定该状态是否吸收。

![[吸收状态]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next check every state of the finite homogeneous chain. Each nonabsorbing state needs a positive route to some absorbing endpoint; one isolated endpoint is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再检查有限齐次链中的每个状态：每个非吸收态都需要通往某个吸收终点的正概率路线，只有孤立终点还不够。

![[吸收链]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finiteness supplies a common block length and success bound. Repeated conditioning proves both probability-one absorption and finite mean waiting without assuming independent attempts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限性给出共同的分段长度与成功下界。重复条件化便能证明概率一吸收及平均等待有限，不必假定各次尝试独立。

![[吸收链必然吸收]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Put transient states first and absorbing states last, reordering rows and columns together. The Q block retains probability within the active region; R records one-step exits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把暂态放在前、吸收态放在后，同时重排行与列。Q保留仍在活动区的概率，R记录一步出口。

![[吸收链标准形]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before summing visits, check convergence. For finite matrices the power, spectral-radius and series conditions agree; the stronger inverse test depends on the nonnegative substochastic structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

统计全部访问之前，先核对收敛。有限矩阵的幂、谱半径与级数条件等价；额外的可逆性判据依赖非负次随机结构。

![[基本矩阵存在条件]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interpret each row as expected visits from one transient start. Count the initial visit, exclude the absorbing endpoint, and distinguish an expected count from a hitting probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把每行读成从某暂态起点出发的期望访问次数。计入初始访问，排除吸收终点，并区分期望次数与命中概率。

![[吸收链基本矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To find the final destination, weight those expected visits by each exit probability. An indirect route can give a positive eventual probability even when the direct next-step exit is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求最终去向时，用每个出口概率给期望访问次数加权。即使下一步直接出口为零，间接路线仍能给出正的最终概率。

![[吸收概率矩阵为NR]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To find the waiting time instead, add all pre-absorption visits. On each path these visits equal the transitions up to the first absorbing state; an already absorbed start needs zero steps.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若改问等待时间，就加总吸收前的全部访问。逐条路径看，访问数恰等于首次进入吸收态所走的步数；已吸收的起点需要零步。

![[期望吸收时间为N1]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, consider two applications of DTMCs. Graph walks lead to dangling-node choices, PageRank convergence and the interpretation of rankings. Markov-switching models also need an observation mechanism; hidden-state Markov structure does not automatically pass to the observations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后看两个使用 DTMC 但不能与 DTMC 本身画等号的应用。图上随机游走先独立定义，悬空节点是模型边界；PageRank 的收敛保证与排名解释也分别成卡。Markov switching 则还需要状态依赖的观测模型，潜在 Markov 性不会自动传给观测序列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start by deciding what counts as a state and how outgoing edges or weights become probabilities. A graph alone does not specify the starting distribution or every rule at a dead end.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先决定状态是什么，再把出边或权重变成概率。只有一张图，还没有指定起始分布或死端处的全部规则。

![[图上随机游走]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a vertex with no outgoing weight, a zero row loses mass. Compare staying, returning and restarting to see how an explicit replacement row changes the walk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有出权重时，零行会让质量消失。比较停留、返回和重新开始，看清补行规则怎样改变游走。

![[悬空节点转移]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After completing every row, add a restart option at every state. Keep the link-following probability separate from the restart probability, and keep row and column conventions consistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

补全每一行之后，再为每个状态加入重新开始的选择。分清跟随链接与重启的概率，并保持行、列约定一致。

![[PageRank随机传送]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The shared restart term makes probability differences shrink. This gives a unique limiting law for any teleportation probability vector, including vectors that assign zero mass to some pages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共同的重启项使概率差距缩小。任意传送概率向量都能得到唯一极限，包括对部分页面分配零概率的向量。

![[PageRank阻尼收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full support adds a stronger conclusion: every transition and every stationary component is positive. It is sufficient for those properties, while the preceding damping theorem already gives uniqueness and convergence without it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全支持再增加更强的结论：每个转移元素与稳态分量都为正。它足以保证这些性质；前面的阻尼定理不需要它就已保证唯一与收敛。

![[全支持传送收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now keep the numerical question separate from the modeling choice. Two perfectly convergent PageRank models can give opposite rankings when their restart preferences differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把数值问题与模型选择分清。两套都完全收敛的 PageRank 模型，仅因重启偏好不同就可能给出相反排名。

![[PageRank模型依赖]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A different application places the Markov state behind the measurements. Specify how the hidden regime evolves and how observations depend on it; a transition matrix alone is not a complete data model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一类应用把 Markov 状态放在测量背后。既要说明潜在区制怎样变化，也要说明它怎样产生观测；只有转移矩阵还不是完整的数据模型。

![[Markov switching模型]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Follow the two-state noisy-sensor example through its Bayes updates. Two histories ending in the same latest reading give different next predictions, showing exactly where the earlier information remains useful.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿两状态带噪传感器例子逐步做 Bayes 更新。两段最新读数相同的历史给出不同的下一步预测，由此看到早先信息在哪一步仍有作用。

![[观测序列不必Markov]]

### 5. 连续时间马尔可夫链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Continuous-Time Markov Chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续时间链把“跳到哪里”与“要等多久”分开；学习入口见 [[连续时间马尔可夫链.canvas|连续时间马尔可夫链原子地图]]。以下原子按建模语义、半群与计算、可数状态边界、长期行为、模型与数据的顺序展开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuous-time chains separate where the process jumps from how long it waits. Open the [[连续时间马尔可夫链.canvas|CTMC atomic map]] for the semantic routes; the atoms below proceed through model semantics, semigroup calculations, countable-state boundaries, long-run behaviour, and applications.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with a device path: it waits in a discrete state and changes at real-valued times. The first card separates the clock, the state set and the Markov prediction rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看设备的一条路径：它在离散状态中等待，于实数时刻改变。先分清时钟、状态集合和 Markov 预测条件。

![[CTMC定义]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next ask whether equal-duration forecasts change with the starting clock time. Time homogeneity concerns the transition rule; the distribution over states can still evolve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再问：同样长度的预测区间，会不会因起始时点而改变？时间齐次约束转移规则，状态分布仍然可以变化。

![[CTMC时间齐次转移函数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before deriving the holding-time theorem, read the waiting-time distribution and its residual-wait property. These explain both the rate parameter and why elapsed waiting can become irrelevant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在推导停留时间之前，先读等待分布及其剩余等待性质，弄清速率参数，以及为什么已经等待多久可以不影响后续分布。

![[指数分布]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A jump occurs at a random time. Read the [[停时定义|stopping-time]] test and [[停时信息集|stopped information]], then the strong Markov theorem: before explosion, a homogeneous chain restarts conditionally on the state reached. This supplies the justification for using the holding law at each entry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

跳跃发生在随机时刻。先看[[停时定义|停时判定]]与[[停时信息集]]，再读强 Markov 定理：爆炸前，齐次链可以在给定到达状态后重新开始。这说明为何每次进入状态时都能使用停留规律。

![[CTMC强Markov性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now connect that property to homogeneous Markov paths. Distinguish a fresh holding time, a remaining wait at an observation time, and the infinite wait at an absorbing state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把这条性质接到齐次 Markov 路径上，分清一次新停留、观察时刻之后的剩余等待，以及吸收态的无限等待。

![[CTMC指数停留时间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The generator collects all local rates. Read a row as the possible directions of departure and their total; negative diagonal entries account for loss from the starting state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

生成矩阵汇总局部速率。把一行读成各个离开方向与总离开率；负对角元记录起点状态的概率减少。

![[生成矩阵约束]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Convert each rate into a probability approximation only after multiplying by a short duration. The one-way failure example shows why even a model allowing only one jump has a nonzero remainder.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

速率乘上短时长，才得到概率近似。单向故障例子说明：即使最多只跳一次，也有非零余项。

![[CTMC短时概率展开]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sum the outgoing rates to get the exit rate. Its reciprocal is the mean wait at a positive-rate state; it does not determine the destination split.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把各方向速率合起来，得到出口率。正出口率的倒数是平均等待，但仅凭总速率还不能知道去向比例。

![[出口率]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Remove waiting times from a path and keep the order of genuine state changes. This produces the jump chain; absorbing-state repetitions and virtual clock events require separate bookkeeping.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从路径中去掉等待时长，只保留真实状态变化的顺序，就得到跳链；吸收态补全和虚时钟事件要分别处理。

![[嵌入跳链]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recombine speed and destination choice. Conditional on a positive-rate entry state, the waiting time and next destination factor; row scaling then reconstructs the generator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把速度与去向重新组合。给定正出口率的进入状态后，等待与下一站的联合概率可分解，按行缩放就恢复生成矩阵。

![[生成矩阵分解]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Construct one departure by racing independent exponential clocks. With positive finite total rate, the winning time and destination separate; zero or infinite total rate needs different treatment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把一次离开构造成多个独立指数时钟的竞争。总率正且有限时，最先响的时间与胜出方向可以分开；总率为零或无穷时要另行处理。

![[竞争指数时钟]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return from individual jumps to observations at specified times. Summing over the intermediate state explains how two durations compose, including the missing probability mass of a minimal explosive process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再从一次次跳跃回到指定时点的观察。按中间状态求和，说明两段时长怎样拼接，也能解释最小爆炸过程在原空间中缺失的概率质量。

![[CTMC转移半群]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite homogeneous chain, differentiate the composition rule at its first or last short interval. The two Kolmogorov equations have the same matrix-exponential solution; neither requires diagonalizability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限齐次链，从最初或最后一个短区间求导，得到两条 Kolmogorov 方程。它们具有同一个矩阵指数解，不要求生成矩阵可对角化。

![[Kolmogorov前后向方程]]

![[CTMC矩阵指数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With one finite bound on all exit rates, an independent dominating Poisson clock and a discrete chain reproduce the continuous-time law exactly. Some clock events leave the state unchanged; this is not a fixed-grid approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有出口率有共同有限上界时，用一个速率不低于所有出口率的独立 Poisson 时钟配合离散链，就能精确复现连续时间规律。部分候选事件不改变状态；这不是固定网格近似。

![[CTMC均匀化]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a countable state space, infinitely many positive waits may have a finite sum. Distinguish this accumulation of genuine jumps from absorption and from an infinite sequence spread over an infinite lifetime.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可数状态空间中，无限多段正等待的总和仍可能有限。要把真实跳时的聚积、吸收，以及铺展在无限寿命上的无限跳跃区分开。

![[CTMC爆炸]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The minimal construction sends an exploded path to a cemetery state. When that label is omitted from the transition array, the missing row mass is exactly the probability of explosion by the observation time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小构造把爆炸路径送往墓地状态。若转移数组省略这个标签，行和缺额恰好就是截至观察时点已经爆炸的概率。

![[最小CTMC]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A return after explosion is additional model information. The independent-restart example keeps the same local rates while changing later transition probabilities; other return rules need their own consistency checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

爆炸后的返回规则是额外模型信息。独立重启例保留相同局部速率，却改变后续转移概率；其他返回方式须各自核验一致性。

![[爆炸后延拓]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common finite ceiling on exit rates prevents infinitely many jumps in finite time. This is a sufficient condition; finite rates state by state are weaker, and an unbounded family can still be nonexplosive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

出口率存在共同有限上界，能够排除有限时间内无限跳跃。这是充分条件；每个状态分别有限更弱，而无界率族也可能非爆炸。

![[有界率非爆炸]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now restrict the route: every genuine jump increases the level by one. The next destination is fixed, but the speed at each level can vary; a zero rate stops the upward route.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在限制路径：每次真实跳跃都只把层级增加一。下一站确定，各层速度却可以不同；零率会阻断向上的路线。

![[纯生链]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the reachable strictly positive finite-rate tail of a pure-birth chain, summing reciprocal rates gives an exact explosion test. The divergent direction requires an argument beyond merely observing infinite expected lifetime.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对纯生链可达的正且有限速率尾部，倒数率级数给出精确爆炸判据。级数发散这一方向，不能只凭“寿命期望无穷”就下结论。

![[纯生链爆炸判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After checking the lifetime of a path, recover its route structure. For the minimal chain, a finite positive-rate route is exactly what makes another state accessible; changing positive rate magnitudes leaves the communication classes unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检查路径寿命后，再看路线结构。对最小链，有限正率路线恰好决定可达性；只改变正率大小，不改变沟通类。

![[跳链决定CTMC沟通类]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Returning is a route event, but the duration includes every holding period. Compare the two-jump example with its calendar return time, and keep absorbing states separate from genuine departure-and-return cycles.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是否回返是路线事件，花多久还要加上每段停留。用两跳例比较步数与日历回返时间，并把吸收态与真实离开—返回周期分开。

![[常返与停留时间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now ask about a probability allocation that remains unchanged at every elapsed duration. Individual paths may continue to jump; an invariant distribution alone does not yet answer whether other starting laws approach it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在改问：哪种概率分配在任意时长之后都保持不变？单条路径仍可继续跳跃；不变分布的定义还没有回答其他初始律是否会趋近它。

![[CTMC平稳分布]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a finite state space, the whole family of invariance equations reduces to one linear system. Keep nonnegativity and normalization beside the left-zero equation, and distinguish total flow balance from pairwise balance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限状态下，整族不变方程化为一个线性方程组。左零方程旁必须保留非负与归一化限制，还要区分总流平衡和逐对平衡。

![[有限CTMC平稳生成矩阵判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mutual reachability gives a useful sufficient condition: a finite irreducible chain has one strictly positive stationary law. This concerns existence and uniqueness, even when its genuine jump chain is periodic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

互相可达给出一个实用充分条件：有限不可约链有唯一、处处为正的平稳概率。这里判断存在与唯一，即使真实跳链有周期也成立。

![[有限不可约CTMC稳态]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A reducible finite chain can have several closed classes. Find the unique law inside each class, extend it by zero outside, and mix these laws to obtain every stationary probability. The mixture weights are probabilities, not arbitrary linear coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可约有限链可能有多个闭类。先求各类内部唯一稳态，在类外补零，再作混合，便得到全部平稳概率。混合权重是概率，不是任意线性系数。

![[有限CTMC平稳分解]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stationary mixtures describe possible equilibria; entry probabilities determine which mixture a chosen initial law approaches. Every finite homogeneous chain has a limit from each start, while one closed class makes that limit independent of the start.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平稳混合描述可能的平衡，入类概率决定选定初始律趋向哪一种混合。有限齐次链从每个初态都有极限；只有一个闭类时，极限才与初态无关。

![[有限CTMC全局收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finiteness previously supplied an equilibrium automatically within a closed irreducible class. On a countable space, inspect the actual nonexplosive process and the expected calendar return cycle, including the initial wait; merely returning eventually is not enough.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限性此前在闭不可约类内自动提供平衡。换成可数空间，要核对实际非爆炸过程和包含最初等待的平均日历回返周期；最终会回来还不够。

![[可数CTMC稳态存在]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once an irreducible nonexplosive chain has a stationary probability, ask whether distributions approach it from other starts. The proof uses the countable discrete theorem for equal-time observations, then a local rate bound fills the gaps. Genuine jump times give a different chain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不可约非爆炸链有平稳概率之后，再问其他初态的分布是否趋向它。证明先对等间隔观察使用可数离散定理，再用局部速率界填补时刻间隙；真实跳时产生的是另一条链。

![[可数CTMC平衡收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Visit counts and clock time sample different objects. In a finite closed irreducible class with at least two states, multiply jump-visit proportions by mean holding times and normalize. In the countable case, distinguish relative invariant weights, normalized balance candidates and genuine process stationarity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数访问次数和按时钟观察针对不同对象。有限闭不可约类至少有两状态时，用平均停留时间乘访问比例，再归一化。可数情形还须区分相对不变权重、归一化平衡候选和真正的过程平稳性。

![[跳链频率时间加权]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Total inflow can match total outflow while probability circulates around a loop. Detailed balance imposes a stronger pairwise condition: occupancy times rate must match in both directions. Solve for weights first, then check whether they can be normalized.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总流入等于总流出时，概率仍可能沿环流动。详细平衡要求更强的逐对条件：两个方向的占据权重乘速率相同。先求权重，再检查能否归一化。

![[CTMC详细平衡]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Translate local flow into a question about an equilibrium movie. Reversibility compares the joint observations after reflecting the entire time axis; with unequal gaps, reversing only the recorded value list is a different comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把局部流量转成平衡录像的问题。可逆性比较整个时间轴反射后的联合观察；间隔不等时，仅把记录值列表倒序，是另一种比较。

![[CTMC可逆性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The finite-state theorem connects these two descriptions for the same probability law, including zero-weight states. Both imply stationarity, but a three-state directed cycle shows why stationarity alone does not remove a preferred time direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限状态定理把这两种描述连接起来，始终使用同一概率律，也允许零权重状态。二者都推出平稳；三状态单向环则说明，只有平稳性并不能消除时间方向。

![[CTMC详细平衡与可逆]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to the Poisson model from the jump-chain viewpoint: start the count at zero and give every level the same positive birth rate. IID exponential waits yield the whole homogeneous Poisson process, not only its one-time count distributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从跳链视角回到 Poisson 模型：计数从零开始，每层使用同一正出生率。独立同分布的指数等待给出整个齐次 Poisson 过程，不只是单个时点的计数分布。

![[Poisson是常速纯生链]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Allowing both upward and downward changes creates a birth–death model. First specify what one state counts, which neighbouring changes are possible, and what happens at the endpoints. These structural choices precede any equilibrium calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同时允许向上与向下变化，就得到生灭模型。先明确状态计数什么、哪些相邻变化可能发生，以及端点如何处理；这些结构选择先于平衡计算。

![[生灭链]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Across one adjacent edge, balance occupied mass times the outward rate against the reverse flow. The lower boundary forces zero net flux, giving relative weights. Normalize those weights and, on an infinite space, verify the nonexplosive process to which they refer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一条相邻边，把占据质量乘出口率，与反向流量平衡。下边界使净流为零，从而递推相对权重；再归一化，并在无限空间核对这些权重所对应的非爆炸过程。

![[生灭链平稳递推]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specialize to independent Poisson arrivals and one exponential server with unlimited capacity. Count everyone in the system, including the person being served. The ratio of arrival to service rates now decides whether the geometric weights have a finite sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再取到达为 Poisson、一个指数服务台且两种机制独立、容量无限的特例。计数包含正在服务的人；到达率与服务率之比决定几何权重能否求得有限总和。

![[M-M-1稳态人数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now reverse the modelling task: estimate rates from a completely observed path over a fixed window. Jump counts need the time at risk as their denominator, including the last unfinished wait. Hourly snapshots do not reveal those same statistics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在反过来，从固定窗口内完整观察到的路径估计速率。跳数需要以可能发生跳跃的暴露时间作分母，包括最后未完成的等待；每小时快照不能给出相同统计量。

![[CTMC完整路径估计]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If several rates share a known proportional parameterization, impose that relation before maximizing the likelihood. Each available edge contributes its weighted exposure, even when no jump was seen on that edge; pooling is not an unweighted average of fitted rates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若多条速率共享已知比例参数，先把关系代入再最大化似然。每条可能出口都贡献加权暴露，即使该边没有观察到跳跃；汇总不是把拟合速率简单平均。

![[CTMC共同率估计]]

### 6. 布朗运动与鞅
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Brownian Motion and Martingales*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Markov 链的状态通常离散；布朗运动把状态和时间都连续化，鞅则抽取‘给定现在无法预测增益’的核心。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Markov-chain states are often discrete. Brownian motion makes both state and time continuous, while martingales isolate the principle that, given current information, future gains cannot be predicted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**原子地图：** [[布朗运动.canvas|打开布朗运动原子地图]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replace jump paths with a real-valued continuous-path model. Begin with an increment of length $h>0$: its law is $N(0,h)$, with mean zero and variance equal to the elapsed time. Standard Brownian motion adds a zero start, mutual independence of disjoint increments, and continuity of the whole path on one probability-one event.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把跳跃路径换成实值连续路径模型。先看长度 $h>0$ 的一段增量：它服从 $N(0,h)$，均值零、方差等于经过时长。标准布朗运动再要求从零开始、不交增量相互独立，以及同一个概率一事件上的整条路径连续。

![[标准布朗运动]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose finitely many observation times and build their values by accumulating independent Gaussian increments. This gives their full joint distribution, not merely separate normal marginals. Repeating a time or including zero exposes the degenerate cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选定有限多个观察时刻，把独立 Gaussian 增量逐段累加，就得到完整联合分布，而不只是各自的正态边际。重复时刻或加入零时刻，会显露退化情形。

![[布朗有限维分布]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a reverse test, a centered jointly Gaussian process with the Brownian covariance kernel already has the right finite-dimensional laws. Path continuity remains a statement about the chosen version; the existence of a continuous modification does not make every version continuous.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反向检验时，中心联合 Gaussian 过程配上布朗协方差核，已经有正确的有限维分布。路径连续性仍针对所选版本；存在连续修正版本，不代表每个版本都连续。

![[协方差核刻画布朗运动]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First separate a position from its displacement over a fixed window. The position accumulates uncertainty, while equal-length increments share a common law. Overlap can create dependence even when those increment laws remain stationary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先分清位置与固定窗口内的位移。位置的不确定性不断累积，同长度增量却有相同规律；窗口重叠仍可产生依赖，即使增量律保持平稳。

![[布朗运动不平稳]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing the clock changes increment variance. Rescale space by the square root of the clock multiplier to recover the standard process; equality of process laws is different from equality along one observed path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

改变时钟会改变增量方差；按时钟倍数的平方根修正空间，就恢复标准过程。过程同分布不等于同一条已观察路径上的相等。

![[布朗尺度变换]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To describe a mean that changes with time and a different fluctuation scale, add a deterministic line and multiply the Brownian part by a fixed constant. Read the starting level, drift rate and diffusion scale separately before computing a distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要描述移动的平均水平和不同波动尺度，可加入确定性直线并按固定常数缩放布朗部分。先分别理解起点、漂移率和扩散尺度，再计算分布。

![[带漂移布朗运动]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtract two positions to remove the starting level. The next calculation distinguishes a variance growing with elapsed time from a standard deviation growing with its square root, and states exactly which intervals give independent increments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把两个位置相减就会消去起点。接下来区分随时长增长的方差与随时长平方根增长的标准差，并明确哪些区间的增量独立。

![[带漂移布朗增量]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before examining Brownian path roughness, introduce a deterministic way to measure all the up-and-down movement of a function. A finite list of observations gives only one partition sum; total variation asks for a bound valid for every finite partition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

研究布朗路径粗糙性之前，先介绍一个确定性的函数运动量指标。有限个观测只给出一套分割的和；总变差询问能否同时约束所有有限分割。

![[函数总变差]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite total variation has a local implication: a derivative exists outside a zero-length set of times. This analytic theorem permits corners and jumps, and its time-wise “almost everywhere” must be kept distinct from probability-one claims about paths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限总变差具有局部后果：除去零长度的时点集合，导数都存在。这条分析学定理允许尖角和跳点；时间上的“几乎处处”须与针对随机路径的概率一断言区分。

![[有界变差函数几乎处处可导]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Brownian roughness is stronger than having isolated corners. The next theorem rules out a finite derivative at every time on one common probability-one event; a separate probability-one argument for each fixed time would not suffice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

布朗路径的粗糙程度远强于存在孤立尖角。下面的定理在同一个概率一事件上排除每个时刻的有限导数，不能只对各个固定时刻分别作概率一论证。

![[布朗路径处处不可导]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The BV theorem now gives one route from nowhere differentiability to infinite accumulated absolute movement. A second proof uses the [[布朗二次变差|quadratic-variation result]] developed just below. These are distinct measurements, and neither should be replaced by how smooth a finite plotted interpolation looks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有界变差定理给出从处处不可导到累计绝对变化无限的一条路线。另一条证明使用下文的[[布朗二次变差]]。这些指标各有含义，不能用有限采样插值图看起来是否平滑来替代。

![[布朗路径无限总变差]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now square the increments on a chosen deterministic grid instead of taking absolute changes over all partitions. State the grid sequence and the convergence mode: vanishing mesh gives mean-square convergence, with additional schemes giving a pathwise almost-sure limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在改为在指定的确定性网格上把增量平方，不再对所有分割的绝对变化取上确界。须说清网格序列和收敛方式：网格趋零给出均方收敛，适当的附加分割方案给出逐路径的几乎必然极限。

![[布朗二次变差]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Crossing questions select a random time from the path. Introduce the Brownian strong Markov theorem first: at an almost-surely finite stopping time for the natural information, the recentered future is a fresh Brownian motion independent of that information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

越界问题从路径中选出随机时刻。先引入布朗强 Markov 定理：在自然信息下几乎必然有限的停时，减去当前位置后的未来是新的布朗运动，并与当时已知信息独立。

![[布朗强Markov性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the first hit of a positive level, symmetry of the restarted motion pairs paths that end below the level with reflected paths that end above it. Truncating the first-hit time at the fixed horizon keeps the stopping time bounded, so this argument does not presume eventual hitting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

首次触及正水平后，重启运动的对称性把终点低于水平的路径与反射后高于水平的路径配对。用固定期限截断首次触及时刻，停时就有界，论证无需预先假定最终必定命中。

![[布朗反射原理]]

布朗运动提供连续时间随机扰动的基准；鞅把“给定当前信息，未来没有可预测漂移”抽象出来。学习这一组时要分清三层：信息如何增长、条件均值怎样受约束，以及随机停止怎样使用已有信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Brownian motion provides the benchmark for continuous-time random disturbance. The martingale section separates the growth of information, conditional-mean restrictions, and what may be concluded at random stopping times.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**原子地图：** [[鞅与停时.canvas|打开鞅与停时原子地图]]。

#### 信息结构

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Information is represented by collections of decidable events on the same outcomes. First define how those collections grow, then ask whether a process is observable in them. The raw natural filtration retains the full observed history, which can contain more information than the current value alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信息用同一结果空间上可判断的事件集合表示。先定义这些集合怎样增长，再判断过程是否可观测。原始自然滤过保留完整观察历史，可能比单独的当前值含有更多信息。

![[滤过定义]]

![[适应过程]]

![[自然滤过]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In discrete time, knowing a value when it is observed differs from fixing it before the next step. Predictability uses the information at integer time $n-1$ for a quantity indexed by $n\ge1$; it does not mean accurately forecasting an unseen random outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

离散时间里，“观察到时知道”与“下一步之前已经确定”不同。可预测性要求编号 $n\ge1$ 的量使用整数时刻 $n-1$ 的信息，并不意味着能准确预报未知随机结果。

![[离散可预测过程]]

#### 条件均值结构

![[鞅定义]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adaptation supplies observability, while the martingale property also requires integrability and a conditional-mean equality. Keep the filtration fixed when checking that equality: enlarging the information can change which future changes have conditional mean zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

适应性提供可观测性，鞅性质还要求可积与条件均值等式。检验等式时须明确同一个滤过：增加信息可能改变哪些未来增量具有条件零均值。

![[适应不推鞅]]

![[鞅差序列]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Martingale differences describe changes; a martingale describes accumulated levels. Start with an integrable $\mathcal F_0$-measurable value $M_0$, then add the first $n$ differences. The finite-sum argument uses their conditional zero means and does not require independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

鞅差描述变化量，鞅描述累计水平。从可积且对 $\mathcal F_0$ 可测的初值 $M_0$ 出发，加上前 $n$ 个增量。有限求和使用条件零均值，无需独立性。

![[鞅差部分和]]

![[次鞅]]

![[超鞅]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Averaging over all available information leaves every martingale with the same unconditional mean at each time. Reversing that implication is invalid: positive and negative conditional drifts can cancel in the overall average.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把所有信息状态一同平均，鞅各期的无条件均值就保持相同。这个推理不能反过来使用：正负条件漂移可能在总体平均中抵消。

![[恒定均值不推鞅]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before transforming a fair process, compare transforming an average with averaging a transform. Ordinary Jensen establishes the direction; its conditional version keeps the currently available information fixed and supplies the martingale argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在变换公平过程之前，先比较“平均的变换”和“变换的平均”。普通Jensen给出方向；条件版本固定当前可用信息，为鞅的变换提供证明工具。

![[期望的Jensen不等式]]

![[条件Jensen不等式]]

![[凸变换产生次鞅]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The submartingale inequality gives every conditional expected gain the same nonnegative sign. If the overall mean stays fixed, no positive conditional gain can remain. This extra one-sided restriction is what makes the converse valid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

次鞅不等式使各信息状态下的条件期望增益均非负。总体均值若不增加，就不能留下正的条件增益；正是这条单向限制，使反推鞅性质成为可能。

![[恒均值次鞅即鞅]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In discrete time, collect each gain predicted from the previous information into a running total. The remainder has conditional mean-zero changes. Fix the initial predictable total—for the standard normalization, set $A_0=0$—to remove the ambiguity in the decomposition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

离散时间里，把每一步由上期信息预测出的增益累积起来，剩余部分的条件增量均值便为零。需固定可预测累计量的初值；标准规范化取 $A_0=0$，以消除分解的歧义。

![[Doob分解]]

#### 随机时间与停止

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stopping rule must be decidable as observations arrive, rather than selected after inspecting the whole path. The definition allows time zero and never stopping. The finite-union proof for first entrance below is discrete-time; a last visit can fail the event test even within a fixed finite horizon.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

停时规则必须随着观察到来就能判断，不能在查看整条路径之后才选定。定义允许零时刻及永不停止。下文首次进入的有限并集证明适用于离散时间；即使固定有限期限，末次到达仍可能不通过事件检验。

![[停时定义]]

![[离散首次到达停时]]

![[末次到达通常非停时]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Information at stopping is one sigma-algebra, not a different object chosen separately on each path. Use the event test with $\{T\le t\}$ in either time scale; the exact-branch form is a discrete-time specialization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

停止时的信息是同一个 σ 代数，不是每条路径单独选一个对象。离散和连续时间都可用 $\{T\le t\}$ 检验；恰好停止分支的形式是离散时间特例。

![[停时信息集]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two stopping times of the same filtration, a pointwise order $S\le T$ orders their information as well. This event-level fact works in either time scale. It neither makes the stopped values integrable nor proves an equality of their expectations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一滤过的两个停时，若逐路径满足 $S\le T$，停止时的信息也按此包含。这是离散、连续时间都成立的事件结论，本身不保证停止值可积或期望相等。

![[停时信息单调性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a fixed finite integer $n$, freeze a discrete-time martingale at the earlier of $n$ and $T$. The result still uses only finitely many time steps, even when $T=\infty$ is possible. A statement about this whole stopped process is not yet a statement about a random terminal value $M_T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在固定有限整数 $n$，把离散鞅冻结在 $n$ 与 $T$ 中较早的时刻。即使 $T$ 可能无穷，这仍只涉及有限步。关于整个停止过程的结论，尚不是关于随机终值 $M_T$ 的结论。

![[停止过程保持鞅]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The limiting step concerns average absolute error: [[L1收敛|L1 convergence]] allows expectations to pass to the limit. Uniform integrability will provide the missing control over rare large values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极限步骤关心平均绝对误差：[[L1收敛]]允许期望随极限收敛，一致可积将补上罕见大值的尾部控制。

![[L1收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three integral theorems explain when limits can pass through expectation: nonnegative increasing approximations, the lower-limit bound of Fatou, and domination by one integrable envelope. They supply the proof tools used by the uniform-integrability criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个积分定理分别处理非负递增逼近、Fatou下极限界，以及同一个可积包络控制的极限；它们也为一致可积判据提供证明工具。

![[单调收敛定理]]

![[Fatou引理]]

![[控制收敛定理]]

![[一致可积]]

![[L1有界不推一致可积]]

![[Lp有界推出一致可积]]

![[一致可积与L1收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The next result separates bounded stopping from passage to an unbounded terminal time. For an a.s.-finite stopping time, UI of the stopped family, or a common integrable envelope, provides sufficient limit control. These are alternative sufficient routes; finite mean stopping time alone is not enough. The stated theorem is discrete-time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接下来分清有界停止与传至无界终止时间的极限步骤。停时几乎必然有限时，停止族一致可积或共同可积包络都是足够的极限控制。这些是不同的充分路线；仅平均停时有限仍不够。这里陈述的是离散时间定理。

![[可选停止条件]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Follow the doubling strategy through its actual finite-round gains. The first win has finite mean waiting time and a bounded terminal gain, yet rare large losses retain an expected contribution at every finite deadline. This exposes the missing uniform tail control.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿着翻倍策略逐轮计算真实盈亏：首次赢的平均等待有限，终值收益也有界，但到每个有限截止时刻，罕见的大额亏损仍留下期望贡献。这样可以看见缺少的统一尾部控制。

![[无限翻倍破坏可选停止]]
