#### 6.1 平稳过程的概念与例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Concept and Examples of Stationary Processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- “平稳”必须说清口径：[[严平稳定义|严平稳]]约束所有有限维联合分布；[[宽平稳定义|宽平稳]]只约束前两阶矩。它们都不是 Markov 链的“平稳分布”。
- “统计特性不随时间推移而改变”只是直观；严平稳与宽平稳对“统计特性”的数学范围不同。
- 在自然科学、工程技术中经常遇到 **平稳过程**，例如，纺织过程中棉纱横截面积的变化，导航中飞行受到的湍流影响产生的随机波动，以及通信中的干扰噪声等。
- 对 [[Gaussian宽严平稳等价|实 Gaussian 过程]]，宽平稳与严平稳等价；这依赖有限维分布由均值和协方差完全决定，不能推广到一般非 Gaussian 过程。
- 平稳过程可以用于描述具有**不随时间变化**的统计特征的随机现象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The word **stationary** needs a declared scope. [[严平稳定义|Strict stationarity]] constrains every finite-dimensional law, whereas [[宽平稳定义|wide-sense stationarity]] constrains only the first two moments. Neither is the stationary distribution of a Markov chain.
- “Statistical behaviour does not change over time” is only intuition; SSS and WSS give it different mathematical scopes.
- Stationary processes arise frequently in natural science and engineering. Examples include variation in the cross-sectional area of cotton yarn, random fluctuations caused by turbulence in navigation, and interference noise in communications.
- For a [[Gaussian宽严平稳等价|real Gaussian process]], WSS and SSS are equivalent because its finite-dimensional laws are determined by their mean vectors and covariance matrices. This equivalence does not extend to arbitrary non-Gaussian processes.
- Stationary processes describe random phenomena whose statistical features do not vary over time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 6.2 联合平稳过程及相关函数的性质
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Jointly Stationary Processes and Properties of Correlation Functions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **定义 6.1：[[联合宽平稳互协方差|联合宽平稳]]**。$X(t)$ 和 $Y(t)$ 必须各自宽平稳，并且互协方差 $\gamma_{XY}(\tau)=\operatorname{Cov}(X(t+\tau),Y(t))$ 只依赖滞后 $\tau$。两个过程分别宽平稳，不足以保证它们联合宽平稳。
- **[[自协方差与ACF|口径]]**：本页以去均值的协方差 $\gamma$ 为主。教材若用 raw correlation $R_{XY}(\tau)=E[X(t+\tau)Y(t)]$，则在常均值下 $\gamma_{XY}(\tau)=R_{XY}(\tau)-\mu_X\mu_Y$。
- **实值宽平稳过程自协方差的性质**：
    - $\gamma(0)=\operatorname{Var}(X(t))\geq0$；
    - $\gamma(-\tau)=\gamma(\tau)$；复值情形改为 Hermitian 共轭对称；
    - $|\gamma(\tau)|\leq\gamma(0)$；
    - 对任意时点 $t_i$ 和复系数 $a_i$，$\sum_{i,j}a_i\overline{a_j}\gamma(t_i-t_j)\geq0$，即核半正定；
    - 若 $X(t+T)=X(t)$ 几乎处处成立，则 $\gamma(\tau+T)=\gamma(\tau)$。
- **互协方差性质**：$\gamma_{XY}(-\tau)=\gamma_{YX}(\tau)$，且 $|\gamma_{XY}(\tau)|^2\leq\gamma_X(0)\gamma_Y(0)$。互协方差本身一般不是偶函数；反转滞后时要交换过程下标。
- **和过程**：若 $W(t)=X(t)+Y(t)$ 且 $X,Y$ 联合宽平稳，则 $\gamma_W(\tau)=\gamma_X(\tau)+\gamma_Y(\tau)+\gamma_{XY}(\tau)+\gamma_{YX}(\tau)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Definition 6.1: [[联合宽平稳互协方差|joint wide-sense stationarity]]**. Each of $X(t)$ and $Y(t)$ must be WSS, and $\gamma_{XY}(\tau)=\operatorname{Cov}(X(t+\tau),Y(t))$ must depend only on the lag. Separate WSS does not imply joint WSS.
- **[[自协方差与ACF|Convention]]**: this page uses centred covariance $\gamma$. If a text uses raw correlation $R_{XY}(\tau)=E[X(t+\tau)Y(t)]$, then under constant means $\gamma_{XY}(\tau)=R_{XY}(\tau)-\mu_X\mu_Y$.
- **Autocovariance properties for a real WSS process**:
    - $\gamma(0)=\operatorname{Var}(X(t))\geq0$;
    - $\gamma(-\tau)=\gamma(\tau)$; for a complex process the rule is Hermitian conjugate symmetry;
    - $|\gamma(\tau)|\leq\gamma(0)$;
    - $\sum_{i,j}a_i\overline{a_j}\gamma(t_i-t_j)\geq0$ for arbitrary times and complex coefficients, so the kernel is positive semidefinite;
    - if $X(t+T)=X(t)$ almost surely, then $\gamma(\tau+T)=\gamma(\tau)$.
- **Cross-covariance properties**: $\gamma_{XY}(-\tau)=\gamma_{YX}(\tau)$ and $|\gamma_{XY}(\tau)|^2\leq\gamma_X(0)\gamma_Y(0)$. A cross-covariance need not itself be even; reversing lag exchanges the process labels.
- **Sum process**: if $W(t)=X(t)+Y(t)$ and $X,Y$ are jointly WSS, then $\gamma_W(\tau)=\gamma_X(\tau)+\gamma_Y(\tau)+\gamma_{XY}(\tau)+\gamma_{YX}(\tau)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 6.4 平稳过程的各态历经性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Ergodicity of Stationary Processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **遍历性要指定统计量和收敛方式**。[[总体平均与时间平均|总体平均]]横跨多个可能实现，时间平均只沿一条实现；平稳性本身不保证两者相等。
- **[[均值遍历|均值遍历]]**：对均值为 $\mu$ 的实值宽平稳过程，若 $\bar X_T=T^{-1}\int_0^T X(t)dt$ 按指定方式收敛到 $\mu$，则过程对均值遍历。
- **[[样本均值方差判据|均方收敛判据]]**：对上述实值过程，只要均方积分存在，
  $$
  \operatorname{Var}(\bar X_T)=\frac{2}{T}\int_0^T\left(1-\frac{\tau}{T}\right)\gamma(\tau)d\tau.
  $$
  因此均方意义的均值遍历当且仅当这个带三角权重的协方差平均趋于 $0$。
- **[[协方差遍历|相关遍历]]**：对每个固定滞后 $h$，去均值乘积的时间平均要收敛到 $\gamma(h)$。这是乘积过程的均值遍历命题，一般需要四阶矩或等价的四阶依赖信息；不能只凭 $X(t)$ 的二阶协方差公式就下结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Ergodicity must name both the statistic and the mode of convergence**. An [[总体平均与时间平均|ensemble average]] ranges over possible realisations, while a time average follows one realisation; stationarity alone does not make them equal.
- **[[均值遍历|Mean ergodicity]]**: for a real-valued WSS process with mean $\mu$, $\bar X_T=T^{-1}\int_0^T X(t)dt$ must converge to $\mu$ in the stated sense.
- **[[样本均值方差判据|Mean-square criterion]]**: for that real-valued process, whenever the mean-square integral exists,
  $$
  \operatorname{Var}(\bar X_T)=\frac{2}{T}\int_0^T\left(1-\frac{\tau}{T}\right)\gamma(\tau)d\tau.
  $$
  Mean-square mean ergodicity holds exactly when this triangularly weighted covariance average tends to zero.
- **[[协方差遍历|Correlation ergodicity]]**: at each fixed lag, the time average of centred products must converge to $\gamma(h)$. This is a mean-ergodicity claim about a product process and generally depends on fourth-order information, so a second-order covariance formula alone is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 重要概念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Important Concepts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **[[严平稳定义|严平稳]] (strict-sense stationary)**：随机过程的所有有限维联合分布在共同时间平移后不变。
    - 这意味着，对于任意时间 $t_1, t_2, ..., t_n$ 和任意时间差 $\tau$, 随机变量 $(X(t_1), X(t_2), ..., X(t_n))$ 和 $(X(t_1+\tau), X(t_2+\tau), ..., X(t_n+\tau))$ 的联合分布是相同的。
- **[[宽平稳定义|宽平稳]] (wide-sense stationary)**：过程有有限二阶矩，均值为常数，且自协方差只依赖滞后。
    - 当教材改用 raw autocorrelation $R$时，因均值为常数，“$R$ 只依赖滞后”与“$\gamma$ 只依赖滞后”等价，但两者不应在公式中混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **[[严平稳定义|Strict-sense stationarity]]**: every finite-dimensional distribution is invariant under a common shift in time.
    - For arbitrary times $t_1,t_2,\ldots,t_n$ and any lag $\tau$, the random vectors $(X(t_1),X(t_2),\ldots,X(t_n))$ and $(X(t_1+\tau),X(t_2+\tau),\ldots,X(t_n+\tau))$ have the same joint distribution.
- **[[宽平稳定义|Wide-sense stationarity]]**: the process has finite second moments, a constant mean, and autocovariance depending only on lag.
    - Under a constant mean, the equivalent raw-correlation formulation is valid, but raw correlation and covariance should not be mixed within one formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 补充
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Supplement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **均方连续**：若 $lim_{h\to0} E[|X(t+h) - X(t)|^2] = 0$ 则称 $X(t)$ 在 $t$ 点均方连续
- **均方导数**：若存在二阶矩过程$X'(t)$ 满足 $lim_{h\to0} E[|(X(t+h)-X(t))/h - X'(t)|^2] = 0$，则 $X(t)$ 在 $t$ 点均方可微
- **均方积分**：随机过程${X(t), t \in T}$ 在区间$[a,b]$上均方可积，则有 $\int_a^b X(t) dt$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Mean-square continuity**: $X(t)$ is mean-square continuous at $t$ if $lim_{h\to0}E[|X(t+h)-X(t)|^2]=0$.
- **Mean-square derivative**: $X(t)$ is mean-square differentiable at $t$ if there exists a second-order process $X'(t)$ such that $lim_{h\to0}E[|(X(t+h)-X(t))/h-X'(t)|^2]=0$.
- **Mean-square integral**: if the stochastic process ${X(t),t\in T}$ is mean-square integrable on $[a,b]$, then the integral $\int_a^bX(t)dt$ is defined.
