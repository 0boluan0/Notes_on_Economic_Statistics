#### 6.1 平稳过程的概念与例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Concept and Examples of Stationary Processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **平稳过程** ([[Stationary Distribution|Stationary]] Process) 的概念是相对于 **[[Wide-Sense Stationary Process|宽平稳过程]]** ([[Wide-Sense Stationary Process|Wide-Sense Stationary Process]]) 提出的。
- **平稳过程** 的统计特性不随时间推移而改变。这意味着，**无论何时观察过程，其统计行为都是相同的**。
- 在自然科学、工程技术中经常遇到 **平稳过程**，例如，纺织过程中棉纱横截面积的变化，导航中飞行受到的湍流影响产生的随机波动，以及通信中的干扰噪声等。
- **对于正态过程** (Gaussian Process) 而言，**宽平稳性与严平稳性是等价的**。 也就是说，如果一个正态过程是宽平稳的，那么它也一定是严平稳的，反之亦然。
- 平稳过程可以用于描述具有**不随时间变化**的统计特征的随机现象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- A **stationary process** is distinguished from a [[Wide-Sense Stationary Process]].
- The statistical properties of a stationary process do not change over time. Its statistical behaviour is therefore the same whenever the process is observed.
- Stationary processes arise frequently in natural science and engineering. Examples include variation in the cross-sectional area of cotton yarn, random fluctuations caused by turbulence in navigation, and interference noise in communications.
- For a **Gaussian process**, wide-sense stationarity and strict stationarity are equivalent: a wide-sense stationary Gaussian process is strictly stationary, and conversely.
- Stationary processes describe random phenomena whose statistical features do not vary over time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 6.2 联合平稳过程及相关函数的性质
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Jointly Stationary Processes and Properties of Correlation Functions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **定义 6.1：联合平稳过程**：若两个平稳过程 X(t) 和 Y(t) 的 **互相关函数** 和 **[[Autocorrelation Function|自相关函数]]** 仅与时间差 τ 有关，而与时间 t 无关，则称 X(t) 和 Y(t) 是**联合平稳随机过程**。
    - 这意味着两个过程之间的**统计关系不随时间改变**。
- **联合平稳过程的相关函数**包括：
    - **互相关函数 (Cross-correlation function)**：$R_{XY}(τ) = E[X(t)Y(t+τ)]$ 。 它衡量了两个随机过程在不同时间点的**相关程度**。
    - **[[Autocorrelation Function|自相关函数]] ([[Autocorrelation|Autocorrelation]] function)**：$R_{XX}(τ) = E[X(t)X(t+τ)]$。 它衡量了单个随机过程在不同时间点的**相关程度**。
- **宽平稳过程相关函数的性质**:
    - **非负性**：$R(0) ≥ 0$，自相关函数在零点的取值是非负的。
    - **对称性**：$R(τ) = R(-τ)$，自相关函数是偶函数，这意味着时间反转不影响相关性。
    - **最大值**：$|R(τ)| ≤ R(0)$，自相关函数在零点取最大值。
    - **非负定性**：相关函数是非负定的，这意味着对于任意实数 $t_1, t_2, ..., t_n$ 及复数 $a_1, a_2, ..., a_n$，都有 $\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n} R(t_i-t_j)a_i\overline{a_j} \geq 0$ 。
    - **周期性**：对于周期为 T 的平稳过程，$R(τ) = R(τ+T)$，自相关函数也具有相同的周期性。
- **联合平稳过程的互相关函数性质**：
    - **互相关函数幅值界限**：$|R_{XY}(τ)|^2 ≤ R_{XX}(0)R_{YY}(0)$。
    - **互相关函数对称性**：$R_{XY}(-τ) = R_{YX}(τ)$，互相关函数在时间反转后需要交换下标。
- **两个平稳过程之和的相关函数**：可以表示为各个随机过程的自相关函数与它们之间互相关函数之和。
    - 例如，若 $W(t) = X(t) + Y(t)$，则 $R_W(τ) = R_X(τ) + R_Y(τ) + R_{XY}(τ) + R_{YX}(τ)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Definition 6.1: jointly stationary processes**. Two stationary processes $X(t)$ and $Y(t)$ are jointly stationary if their cross-correlation and [[Autocorrelation Function|autocorrelation]] functions depend only on the lag $τ$, not on calendar time $t$.
    - Their statistical relationship does not change over time.
- **Correlation functions for jointly stationary processes** include:
    - **Cross-correlation function**: $R_{XY}(τ) = E[X(t)Y(t+τ)]$, which measures dependence between two processes at different times.
    - **[[Autocorrelation Function]]**: $R_{XX}(τ) = E[X(t)X(t+τ)]$, which measures dependence within one process across time.
- **Properties of the correlation function of a wide-sense stationary process**:
    - **Nonnegativity at zero**: $R(0) ≥ 0$.
    - **Symmetry**: $R(τ)=R(-τ)$, so the autocorrelation function is even.
    - **Maximum at zero**: $|R(τ)|≤R(0)$.
    - **Positive semidefiniteness**: for arbitrary real times $t_1,t_2,\ldots,t_n$ and complex coefficients $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n$, $\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}R(t_i-t_j)a_i\overline{a_j}\geq0$.
    - **Periodicity**: if a stationary process has period $T$, then its autocorrelation also satisfies $R(τ)=R(τ+T)$.
- **Properties of cross-correlation for jointly stationary processes**:
    - **Magnitude bound**: $|R_{XY}(τ)|^2≤R_{XX}(0)R_{YY}(0)$.
    - **Reversal symmetry**: $R_{XY}(-τ)=R_{YX}(τ)$; reversing the lag also exchanges the process labels.
- **Correlation function of the sum of two stationary processes**: it is the sum of their two autocorrelation functions and the two cross-correlation functions.
    - If $W(t)=X(t)+Y(t)$, then $R_W(τ)=R_X(τ)+R_Y(τ)+R_{XY}(τ)+R_{YX}(τ)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 6.4 平稳过程的各态历经性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Ergodicity of Stationary Processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **[[Ergodicity|各态历经性]] ([[Ergodicity|Ergodicity]])** 或者 **遍历性**：**平稳随机过程的统计特性可以用一个样本函数的时间平均来代替**。
    - 这意味着，对于一个具有各态历经性的平稳过程，可以通过**长时间观察一个样本**来推断整个过程的统计特性，从而避免了对大量样本进行统计分析的需要。
- **定理 6.10 (均值各态历经性)**：如果 X(t) 是均方连续的平稳过程，则其均值具有各态历经性的充要条件为：$lim_{T\to\infty} \frac{1}{2T}∫_{-T}^{T} [R(τ) - m_x^2] dτ = 0$。
    - 这个定理给出了一个**判断均值是否具有各态历经性的方法**。
- **定理 6.11 (相关函数各态历经性)**： 如果 X(t) 是均方连续的平稳过程，则其相关函数具有各态历经性的充要条件为：$lim_{T\to\infty} \frac{1}{2T}∫_{-T}^{T} |B(τ)|^2 dτ = 0$。
    - 这个定理给出了一个**判断相关函数是否具有各态历经性的方法**。
- **均值各态历经性**：时间平均等于统计平均，即 $\bar{m_x} = m_x$。
    - 这表明了对于具有均值各态历经性的平稳过程，**时间平均可以用来估计统计均值**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **[[Ergodicity]]** means that the statistical properties of a stationary process can be recovered from time averages along one sample path.
    - A long observation of a single path can therefore reveal the process's ensemble properties without requiring many independent sample paths.
- **Theorem 6.10 (mean ergodicity)**: if $X(t)$ is a mean-square continuous stationary process, its mean is ergodic if and only if $lim_{T\to\infty}\frac{1}{2T}∫_{-T}^{T}[R(τ)-m_x^2]dτ=0$.
    - This gives a criterion for deciding whether the mean is ergodic.
- **Theorem 6.11 (correlation ergodicity)**: if $X(t)$ is a mean-square continuous stationary process, its correlation function is ergodic if and only if $lim_{T\to\infty}\frac{1}{2T}∫_{-T}^{T}|B(τ)|^2dτ=0$.
    - This gives a criterion for deciding whether the correlation function is ergodic.
- **Mean ergodicity**: the time average equals the ensemble mean, $\bar{m_x}=m_x$.
    - Thus, for a mean-ergodic process, the time average can estimate the ensemble mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 重要概念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Important Concepts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **严平稳 ([[Strict-Sense Stationary Process|Strict]]-sense stationary)**：**随机过程的有限维分布不随时间的推移而改变**。
    - 这意味着，对于任意时间 $t_1, t_2, ..., t_n$ 和任意时间差 $\tau$, 随机变量 $(X(t_1), X(t_2), ..., X(t_n))$ 和 $(X(t_1+\tau), X(t_2+\tau), ..., X(t_n+\tau))$ 的联合分布是相同的。
- **宽平稳 ([[Wide-Sense Stationary Process|Wide]]-sense stationary)**：**二阶矩过程均值常数，相关函数仅与时间差有关**。
    - 这意味着，过程的均值不随时间变化，并且过程的自相关函数只取决于时间差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **[[Strict-Sense Stationary Process|Strict-sense stationarity]]**: the finite-dimensional distributions of a stochastic process are invariant under shifts in time.
    - For arbitrary times $t_1,t_2,\ldots,t_n$ and any lag $\tau$, the random vectors $(X(t_1),X(t_2),\ldots,X(t_n))$ and $(X(t_1+\tau),X(t_2+\tau),\ldots,X(t_n+\tau))$ have the same joint distribution.
- **[[Wide-Sense Stationary Process|Wide-sense stationarity]]**: the second-order process has a constant mean and a correlation function that depends only on the time lag.
    - The mean is constant over time, and the autocorrelation function depends only on the difference between observation times.

#### 补充
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Supplement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **均方连续**：若 $lim_{h\to0} E[|X(t+h) - X(t)|^2] = 0$ 则称 $X(t)$ 在 $t$ 点均方连续
- **均方导数**：若存在二阶矩过程$X'(t)$ 满足 $lim_{h\to0} E[|(X(t+h)-X(t))/h - X'(t)|^2] = 0$，则 $X(t)$ 在 $t$ 点均方可微
- **均方积分**：随机过程${X(t), t \in T}$ 在区间$[a,b]$上均方可积，则有 $\int_a^b X(t) dt$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Mean-square continuity**: $X(t)$ is mean-square continuous at $t$ if $lim_{h\to0}E[|X(t+h)-X(t)|^2]=0$.
- **Mean-square derivative**: $X(t)$ is mean-square differentiable at $t$ if there exists a second-order process $X'(t)$ such that $lim_{h\to0}E[|(X(t+h)-X(t))/h-X'(t)|^2]=0$.
- **Mean-square integral**: if the stochastic process ${X(t),t\in T}$ is mean-square integrable on $[a,b]$, then the integral $\int_a^bX(t)dt$ is defined.
