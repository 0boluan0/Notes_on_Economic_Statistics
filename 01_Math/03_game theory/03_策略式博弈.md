# 1. 囚徒困境
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Prisoner's Dilemma*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1. 策略式表达
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strategic-Form Representation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[博弈的表示、策略与占优#Worked example：囚徒困境|囚徒困境]]（Prisoner's Dilemma）可以写成一个 [[博弈的表示、策略与占优#策略式与扩展式|策略式博弈]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[博弈的表示、策略与占优#Worked example：囚徒困境|Prisoner's Dilemma]] can be represented as a [[博弈的表示、策略与占优#策略式与扩展式|strategic-form game]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 玩家：两名囚徒。
- 策略：每个囚徒可选择 $C$（cooperate，合作）或 $D$（defect，背叛）。
- 收益：由收益矩阵给出，左侧为行玩家收益，右侧为列玩家收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Players: two prisoners.
- Strategies: each prisoner chooses $C$ (cooperate) or $D$ (defect).
- Payoffs: the payoff matrix lists the row player's payoff first and the column player's payoff second.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|  | C | D |
|---|---|---|
| **C** | $(-1,-1)$ | $(-9,0)$ |
| **D** | $(0,-9)$ | $(-6,-6)$ |

>[!note] 囚徒困境的核心
> 对每个人来说，背叛都是个人最优反应；但两人都背叛时的结果比两人都合作更差。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Defection is individually optimal for each player, yet mutual defection leaves both players worse off than mutual cooperation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 为什么它重要
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why It Matters*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

囚徒困境展示了一个基本矛盾：个人理性不一定推出集体最优。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Prisoner's Dilemma reveals a fundamental tension: individual rationality need not produce a collectively optimal outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也是后面学习 [[纳什均衡与混合策略#最佳反应与 Nash 均衡|纳什均衡]] 时最重要的直觉来源：均衡只要求“没人愿意单独偏离”，不要求结果对所有人最好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This supplies the central intuition for [[纳什均衡与混合策略#最佳反应与 Nash 均衡|Nash Equilibrium]]: equilibrium requires only that no player wants to deviate unilaterally, not that the outcome be best for everyone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 关键定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Key Definitions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1. 策略式博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strategic-Form Game*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

策略式博弈（strategic-form game）由三部分构成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strategic-form game has three components:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
G=\langle N,\{S_i\}_{i\in N},\{u_i\}_{i\in N}\rangle.
$$

- $N$：玩家集合。
- $S_i$：玩家 $i$ 的策略集合。
- $u_i$：玩家 $i$ 的收益函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $N$: the set of players.
- $S_i$: player $i$'s strategy set.
- $u_i$: player $i$'s payoff function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2. 严格占优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strict Dominance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对玩家 $i$ 而言，若对任意 $s_{-i}\in S_{-i}$ 都有
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For player $i$, strategy $s_i$ strictly dominates strategy $s_i'$ if, for every $s_{-i}\in S_{-i}$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
u_i(s_i,s_{-i})>u_i(s_i',s_{-i}),
$$

则称策略 $s_i$ 严格占优（strictly dominates）策略 $s_i'$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
holds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 严格占优和弱占优
> 把 $>$ 换成 $\ge$ 得到弱占优（weak dominance）。弱占优允许某些情形下收益相等，因此用它删除策略时要更谨慎。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Replacing $>$ with $\ge$ gives weak dominance. Weak dominance permits equal payoffs in some cases, so deleting weakly dominated strategies requires greater care.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3. 严格劣势策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strictly Dominated Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若存在某个策略 $s_i\in S_i$ 严格占优 $s_i'\in S_i$，则 $s_i'$ 是玩家 $i$ 的 [[博弈的表示、策略与占优#严格与弱占优|严格劣势策略]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If some strategy $s_i\in S_i$ strictly dominates $s_i'\in S_i$, then $s_i'$ is a [[博弈的表示、策略与占优#严格与弱占优|Strictly Dominated Strategy]] for player $i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

严格劣势策略不会是理性玩家的最优选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strictly dominated strategy cannot be optimal for a rational player.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.4. 严格优势策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strictly Dominant Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若策略 $s_i$ 严格占优玩家 $i$ 的所有其他可选策略，则 $s_i$ 是严格优势策略（strictly dominant strategy）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If strategy $s_i$ strictly dominates every other strategy available to player $i$, then $s_i$ is a strictly dominant strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在囚徒困境中，对每个囚徒来说，$D$ 都严格占优 $C$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the Prisoner's Dilemma, $D$ strictly dominates $C$ for each prisoner.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.5. 优势策略均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Dominant-Strategy Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若每个玩家都选择自己的优势策略，得到的策略组合称为 [[博弈的表示、策略与占优#严格与弱占优|优势策略均衡]]（dominant-strategy equilibrium）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When every player chooses a dominant strategy, the resulting profile is a [[博弈的表示、策略与占优#严格与弱占优|Dominant Strategy Equilibrium]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 优势策略均衡比纳什均衡更强
> 优势策略要求“不管别人怎么选，我都选它”；纳什均衡只要求“给定别人当前选择，我不想改”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A dominant strategy is optimal regardless of what others choose. Nash equilibrium requires only that a player not want to change given the others' current choices.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.6. 严格劣势策略迭代删除
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Iterated Deletion of Strictly Dominated Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[博弈的表示、策略与占优#严格与弱占优|严格劣势策略迭代删除]]（iterated deletion of strictly dominated strategies, IDSDS）指反复删除当前博弈中的严格劣势策略，直到无法继续删除。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[博弈的表示、策略与占优#严格与弱占优|Iterated Deletion of Strictly Dominated Strategies]] (IDSDS) repeatedly removes strategies that are strictly dominated in the current reduced game until no further deletion is possible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A=\times_{i\in N}A_i\subseteq S$。若可构造序列
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A=\times_{i\in N}A_i\subseteq S$. If one can construct a sequence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
A_i^0=S_i,\ldots,A_i^T=A_i,
$$

且每一步只删除当前剩余博弈中的严格劣势策略，则最终幸存集合 $A$ 是 IDSDS 的结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
in which each step removes only strategies that are strictly dominated in the currently remaining game, then the final surviving set $A$ is the result of IDSDS.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.7. 纳什均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Nash Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若策略组合 $s^*\in S$ 满足对每个玩家 $i\in N$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strategy profile $s^*\in S$ is a [[纳什均衡与混合策略#最佳反应与 Nash 均衡|Nash Equilibrium]] if, for every player $i\in N$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
u_i(s_i^*,s_{-i}^*)\ge u_i(s_i,s_{-i}^*),\quad \forall s_i\in S_i,
$$

则 $s^*$ 是 [[纳什均衡与混合策略#最佳反应与 Nash 均衡|纳什均衡]]（Nash equilibrium）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
holds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 一句话判断
> 固定别人不变，任何一个玩家都不能通过单独改策略提高自己的收益。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Holding everyone else's strategy fixed, no player can raise their payoff by changing strategy alone.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.8. 最优反应刻画
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Best-Response Characterisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

纳什均衡也可以用最优反应来刻画：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nash equilibrium can also be characterised through best responses:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
s^*\text{ 是 NE}\quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad s_i^*\in B_i(s_{-i}^*)\ \text{for all }i.
$$

这说明纳什均衡就是所有玩家的最优反应同时成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus, a Nash equilibrium is a profile in which every player's strategy is simultaneously a best response.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.9. 最优反应函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Best-Response Correspondence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对玩家 $i$，[[纳什均衡与混合策略#最佳反应与 Nash 均衡|最优反应函数]] $B_i$ 是集合值函数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For player $i$, the [[纳什均衡与混合策略#最佳反应与 Nash 均衡|best-response correspondence]] $B_i$ is the set-valued function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
B_i(s_{-i})
=
\{s_i\in S_i\mid u_i(s_i,s_{-i})\ge u_i(s_i',s_{-i}),\ \forall s_i'\in S_i\}.
$$

## 2.10. 可理性化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Rationalisability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[博弈的表示、策略与占优#严格与弱占优|可理性化]]（rationalisability）描述一个策略是否可以被某种关于对手行为的合理信念支持为最优反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[博弈的表示、策略与占优#严格与弱占优|Rationalisability]] asks whether a strategy can be supported as a best response by some reasonable belief about the opponents' behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $R=\times_{i\in N}R_i$ 是可理性化策略集合，$Z$ 是纳什均衡集合，$A$ 是 IDSDS 后的幸存集合，则有：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $R=\times_{i\in N}R_i$ is the set of rationalisable strategies, $Z$ is the set of Nash equilibria, and $A$ is the set surviving IDSDS, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Z\subseteq R\subseteq A\subseteq S.
$$

>[!attention] 可理性化不是纳什均衡
> 纳什均衡一定是可理性化的，但可理性化策略之间不一定相互构成纳什均衡。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every Nash-equilibrium strategy is rationalisable, but rationalisable strategies need not form a Nash equilibrium with one another.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. 严格劣势策略迭代删除
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Iterated Deletion of Strictly Dominated Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. 例子设定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Example Setup*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行玩家选择 $T/B$，列玩家选择 $L/M/R$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The row player chooses $T/B$, and the column player chooses $L/M/R$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|  | L | M | R |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T** | $(4,3)$ | $(2,7)$ | $(0,4)$ |
| **B** | $(5,5)$ | $(5,-1)$ | $(-4,-2)$ |

一开始不一定能看到某个玩家有严格优势策略，但仍可能通过迭代删除缩小策略空间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strictly dominant strategy may not be apparent at the start, but iterated deletion can still reduce the strategy space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. 删除过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Deletion Process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一步，对列玩家而言，$M$ 严格占优 $R$：

- 若行玩家选 $T$，列玩家选 $M$ 得 $7$，选 $R$ 得 $4$。
- 若行玩家选 $B$，列玩家选 $M$ 得 $-1$，选 $R$ 得 $-2$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, $M$ strictly dominates $R$ for the column player:

- if the row player chooses $T$, $M$ gives the column player $7$, while $R$ gives $4$;
- if the row player chooses $B$, $M$ gives the column player $-1$, while $R$ gives $-2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

删除 $R$ 后：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After deleting $R$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|  | L | M |
|---|---|---|
| **T** | $(4,3)$ | $(2,7)$ |
| **B** | $(5,5)$ | $(5,-1)$ |

第二步，对行玩家而言，$B$ 严格占优 $T$：

- 若列玩家选 $L$，行玩家选 $B$ 得 $5$，选 $T$ 得 $4$。
- 若列玩家选 $M$，行玩家选 $B$ 得 $5$，选 $T$ 得 $2$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Second, $B$ strictly dominates $T$ for the row player:

- if the column player chooses $L$, $B$ gives the row player $5$, while $T$ gives $4$;
- if the column player chooses $M$, $B$ gives the row player $5$, while $T$ gives $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

删除 $T$ 后：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After deleting $T$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|  | L | M |
|---|---|---|
| **B** | $(5,5)$ | $(5,-1)$ |

第三步，对列玩家而言，$L$ 严格占优 $M$，最终剩下 $(B,L)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Third, $L$ strictly dominates $M$ for the column player, leaving $(B,L)$ as the final outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 复习时要看的不是“删了什么”
> 关键是每次删除都必须在“当前剩余博弈”里重新比较，不能拿已经被删掉的策略继续当依据。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every deletion must be justified by a fresh comparison within the currently remaining game. A strategy that has already been removed cannot continue to serve as evidence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4. 如何走出囚徒困境
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How Can Players Escape the Prisoner's Dilemma?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. 两个犯人先后审问
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sequential Interrogation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果两个犯人先后审问，后行动者可以根据前行动者的回答选择策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the prisoners are interrogated sequentially, the second mover can condition their strategy on the first mover's answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但在标准囚徒困境的收益结构下，前行动者预期后行动者会背叛，于是自己也会背叛，仍然落入囚徒困境。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the standard Prisoner's Dilemma payoffs, however, the first mover anticipates that the second mover will defect and therefore defects as well. The dilemma remains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2. 重复博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Repeated Games*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[寡头竞争：Cournot、Bertrand 与 Stackelberg#重复互动入口|重复博弈]]把一次性囚徒困境扩展成多期互动。典型例子是可口可乐和百事可乐的价格战。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[寡头竞争：Cournot、Bertrand 与 Stackelberg#重复互动入口|Repeated Games]] extend a one-shot Prisoner's Dilemma into a multi-period interaction. A standard example is a price war between Coca-Cola and Pepsi.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在重复博弈中，今天的降价不仅影响今天利润，也会改变对方未来的定价策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a repeated game, cutting the price today affects not only current profit but also the rival's future pricing strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.2.1. 有尽头的重复博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Finitely Repeated Games with a Known End*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若重复次数有限且最后一期已知，最后一期就是一次性囚徒困境，双方都会低价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the number of repetitions is finite and the final period is known, that final period is a one-shot Prisoner's Dilemma and both firms choose the low price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再向前倒推，每一期都会因为“下一期也会低价”而选择低价，因此合作仍难以维持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Working backwards, each preceding period also produces a low price because both firms anticipate a low price next period. Cooperation remains difficult to sustain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.2.2. 没有确定尽头的重复博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Repeated Games Without a Known Final Period*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果双方不知道哪一期是最后一期，未来惩罚可能约束今天的背叛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If neither firm knows which period will be the last, the prospect of future punishment may deter defection today.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

此时双方可能为了长期收益不率先降价，从而维持合作。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both firms may then refrain from cutting price first in order to preserve long-run gains, allowing cooperation to persist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] 记忆方式
> 有确定最后一期时，逆向归纳会拆掉合作；没有确定最后一期时，未来惩罚才可能支撑合作。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A known final period allows backward induction to unravel cooperation. Without a known final period, future punishment may support it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.3. 黑社会组织案例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*External-Enforcement Example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果囚徒困境外部存在额外惩罚机制，原来的收益矩阵会被改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An external punishment mechanism changes the original Prisoner's Dilemma payoff matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20240305225834.png]]

这说明所谓“走出囚徒困境”通常不是在原矩阵内 magically 选择合作，而是改变收益结构或重复互动环境。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus, “escaping” the Prisoner's Dilemma usually does not mean magically choosing cooperation within the original matrix. It means changing the payoff structure or the environment of repeated interaction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.4. Crazy Eddie 和 Newmarket & Lewis
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Crazy Eddie and Newmarket & Lewis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两家销售音响电子产品的商家 Crazy Eddie 与 Newmarket & Lewis 都使用低价承诺。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two audio-electronics retailers, Crazy Eddie and Newmarket & Lewis, both use low-price guarantees.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Crazy Eddie 承诺：“不可能有人卖得比我们更低。”
- Newmarket & Lewis 承诺：“如果你在别处以更低价格买到同样商品，我们双倍返还差价。”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Crazy Eddie promises that nobody can sell for less.
- Newmarket & Lewis promises to refund twice the difference if a customer finds the same product cheaper elsewhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乍看像价格战，但关键机制不同：当 Crazy Eddie 降价后，Newmarket & Lewis 的顾客不会马上流失，反而会拿着低价信息回来要求返还差价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This looks like a price war, but the mechanism is different. When Crazy Eddie cuts its price, Newmarket & Lewis does not immediately lose its customers; instead, they return with the lower-price information and demand the promised refund.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 这个案例的关键
> 囚徒困境依赖“对方降价会抢走我的顾客”。低价保证改变了这个机制，使直接降价不再有效抢客。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The Prisoner's Dilemma relies on a rival's price cut taking away my customers. A low-price guarantee changes that mechanism, making a direct price cut ineffective as a way to capture customers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.5. 其他相似情形
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Other Related Cases*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

电商平台降低了价格比较成本，但影响不完全相同：

- 对完全竞争的小件商品，价格比较更容易压低价格。
- 对大宗且生产商较少的商品，平台也可能让厂商更容易观察对手价格，形成新的策略互动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
E-commerce platforms reduce the cost of comparing prices, but their effects differ across markets:

- for small goods sold in competitive markets, easier comparison tends to push prices down;
- for large purchases supplied by only a few producers, the platform may also make rivals' prices easier to observe and thereby create new strategic interaction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

钻石、房产中介平台等案例也都可以理解为：信息结构和市场制度改变后，原来的价格竞争逻辑会变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diamond markets, property-listing platforms, and similar cases can be understood in the same way: changing the information structure or market institution changes the original logic of price competition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 5. 纯策略纳什均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pure-Strategy Nash Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.1. 为什么需要纳什均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why Nash Equilibrium Is Needed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑下列博弈：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the following game:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|  | L | M | R |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T** | $(4,3)$ | $(6,7)$ | $(0,4)$ |
| **B** | $(5,5)$ | $(5,-1)$ | $(-4,-2)$ |

迭代删除严格劣势策略后可得到：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Iterated deletion of strictly dominated strategies yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|  | L | M |
|---|---|---|
| **T** | $(4,3)$ | $(6,7)$ |
| **B** | $(5,5)$ | $(5,-1)$ |

此时无法继续删除严格劣势策略，但仍然需要判断哪些策略组合稳定。这就是 [[纳什均衡与混合策略#纯策略求解步骤|纯策略纳什均衡]] 要解决的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No further strictly dominated strategies can now be removed, yet we still need to determine which strategy profiles are stable. This is the problem addressed by [[纳什均衡与混合策略#纯策略求解步骤|Pure Strategy Nash Equilibrium]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 6. 如何找纯策略纳什均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How to Find Pure-Strategy Nash Equilibria*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6.1. 画箭头
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Arrow Method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在每个格子中，箭头表示给定对方策略时自己的最优反应方向。箭头汇聚的格子就是纳什均衡候选。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In each cell, an arrow points towards a player's best response given the opponent's strategy. A cell at which both players' arrows converge is a candidate Nash equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20240305233643.png]]

>[!item] 箭头法适合直观判断
> 一个格子如果对两个玩家而言都没有向外偏离的箭头，就是纯策略纳什均衡。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A cell is a pure-strategy Nash equilibrium if neither player has an arrow pointing away from it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6.2. 画横线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Underlining Method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下划线法是在每一列标出行玩家的最高收益，在每一行标出列玩家的最高收益。两个玩家都被标出的格子就是纯策略纳什均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The underlining method marks the row player's highest payoff in each column and the column player's highest payoff in each row. A cell marked for both players is a pure-strategy Nash equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20240305234344.png]]

这个方法是 [[纳什均衡与混合策略#纯策略求解步骤|求纯策略纳什均衡]] 最稳定的手算方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the most reliable hand-calculation method for [[纳什均衡与混合策略#纯策略求解步骤|Finding Pure Strategy Nash Equilibria]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 7. 最优反应与可理性化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Best Responses and Rationalisability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7.1. 最优反应函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Best-Response Correspondence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定对方策略时，我方能取得最高收益的策略集合就是最优反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given the opponents' strategies, the set of my strategies that attain the highest payoff is my best-response set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
B_i(s_{-i})
=
\{s_i\in S_i\mid u_i(s_i,s_{-i})\ge u_i(s_i',s_{-i}),\ \forall s_i'\in S_i\}.
$$

如果一个策略组合中，每个玩家的策略都是对其他玩家策略的最优反应，那么该策略组合就是纳什均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If every player's strategy in a profile is a best response to the others' strategies, that profile is a Nash equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7.2. 可理性化策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Rationalisable Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个策略是可理性化的，意思是：存在某种关于对手行为的合理信念，使得这个策略成为最优反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strategy is rationalisable if some reasonable belief about the opponents' behaviour makes it a best response.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可理性化允许“我相信你会这样选，所以我这样选；你也可以相信我会那样选，所以你那样选”的逻辑回环。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rationalisability permits a loop of reasoning: I choose this because I believe you will choose that, while you may choose that because you hold a corresponding belief about me.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但只有当这些最优反应在同一个策略组合上同时成立时，才构成纳什均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These best responses form a Nash equilibrium only when they hold simultaneously within the same strategy profile.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 8. 混合策略扩展
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mixed-Strategy Extension*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8.1. 为什么引入混合策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why Introduce Mixed Strategies?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

配对硬币和石头剪刀布没有纯策略纳什均衡，但玩家仍可以通过概率分布来随机化选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Matching Pennies and Rock–Paper–Scissors have no pure-strategy Nash equilibrium, but players can still randomise their choices using probability distributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

混合策略允许玩家不是选择某一个纯策略，而是选择纯策略集合上的概率分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A mixed strategy lets a player choose a probability distribution over pure strategies rather than one particular pure strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8.2. 混合扩展博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Mixed Extension of a Game*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定策略式博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given the strategic-form game
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
G=\langle N,\{S_i\}_{i\in N},\{u_i\}_{i\in N}\rangle,
$$

其混合扩展为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
its mixed extension is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Gamma=\langle N,\{\Delta(S_i)\}_{i\in N},\{U_i\}_{i\in N}\rangle.
$$

其中：

- $\Delta(S_i)$ 是玩家 $i$ 在纯策略集合 $S_i$ 上的概率分布集合。
- $\Delta(S)=\times_{i\in N}\Delta(S_i)$。
- $U_i:\Delta(S)\to\mathbb{R}$ 是 vNM 期望效用函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where:

- $\Delta(S_i)$ is the set of player $i$'s probability distributions over the pure-strategy set $S_i$;
- $\Delta(S)=\times_{i\in N}\Delta(S_i)$;
- $U_i:\Delta(S)\to\mathbb{R}$ is the von Neumann–Morgenstern expected-utility function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8.3. 混合策略纳什均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mixed-Strategy Nash Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\sigma^*\in\Delta(S)$ 满足
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\sigma^*\in\Delta(S)$ satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
U_i(\sigma_i^*,\sigma_{-i}^*)\ge U_i(\sigma_i,\sigma_{-i}^*)\quad
\forall \sigma_i\in\Delta(S_i),\ \forall i\in N,
$$

则 $\sigma^*$ 是 [[纳什均衡与混合策略#混合策略|混合策略纳什均衡]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
then $\sigma^*$ is a [[纳什均衡与混合策略#混合策略|Mixed Strategy Nash Equilibrium]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等价地：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalently,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sigma_i^*\in B_i(\sigma_{-i}^*)\quad \text{for all }i.
$$

# 9. 混合策略纳什均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Calculating Mixed-Strategy Nash Equilibria*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 9.1. 配对硬币
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Matching Pennies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回到配对硬币。设列玩家以概率 $q$ 选 $H$，以概率 $1-q$ 选 $T$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to Matching Pennies. Let the column player choose $H$ with probability $q$ and $T$ with probability $1-q$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行玩家选 $H$ 与 $T$ 的期望收益分别为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The row player's expected payoffs from $H$ and $T$ are, respectively,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
U_1(H,q)=q(-1)+(1-q)(1)=1-2q,
$$

$$
U_1(T,q)=q(1)+(1-q)(-1)=2q-1.
$$

因此：

- 若 $q<\frac12$，行玩家选 $H$。
- 若 $q>\frac12$，行玩家选 $T$。
- 若 $q=\frac12$，行玩家在 $H$ 与 $T$ 之间无差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore:

- if $q<\frac12$, the row player chooses $H$;
- if $q>\frac12$, the row player chooses $T$;
- if $q=\frac12$, the row player is indifferent between $H$ and $T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20240313200645.png]]

两个玩家最优反应函数的交点就是混合策略纳什均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The intersection of the two players' best-response correspondences is the mixed-strategy Nash equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 9.2. 无差异条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Indifference Condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 混合策略的关键
> 如果一个玩家在均衡中真正混合多个纯策略，那么这些被赋予正概率的纯策略必须给他相同的期望收益。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a player genuinely mixes among several pure strategies in equilibrium, every pure strategy assigned positive probability must give that player the same expected payoff.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以配对硬币为例，若行玩家以概率 $x$ 选 $H$，列玩家对 $H/T$ 无差异要求：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Matching Pennies, if the row player chooses $H$ with probability $x$, making the column player indifferent between $H$ and $T$ requires
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
2x-1=1-2x.
$$

由此得到 $x=\frac12$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
which gives $x=\frac12$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 9.3. 性别博弈的混合策略均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mixed Equilibrium in the Battle of the Sexes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设行玩家选 Cafe 的概率为 $x$，列玩家选 Cafe 的概率为 $y$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the row player choose Cafe with probability $x$, and the column player choose Cafe with probability $y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行玩家选 Cafe 与 Pub 的期望收益：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The row player's expected payoffs from Cafe and Pub are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
U_1(Cafe)=4y+1(1-y),\qquad U_1(Pub)=3(1-y).
$$

行玩家无差异：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The row player's indifference condition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
4y+1-y=3(1-y)\Rightarrow y=\frac13.
$$

列玩家无差异可得：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The column player's indifference condition gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
x=\frac23.
$$

因此除两个纯策略均衡外，还存在一个混合策略均衡：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In addition to the two pure-strategy equilibria, there is therefore a mixed-strategy equilibrium:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
(x,y)=\left(\frac23,\frac13\right).
$$

# 10. 混合策略中的占优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Dominance with Mixed Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 10.1. 纯策略占优推出混合策略占优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pure-Strategy Dominance Extends to Mixed Opponent Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果一个纯策略无论别人选什么纯策略都优于另一个策略，那么别人使用任意混合策略时，这个纯策略仍然优于它。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If one pure strategy is better than another against every pure strategy of the opponents, it remains better when the opponents use any mixed strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是因为混合策略的期望收益只是各纯策略情形收益的加权平均。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This follows because the expected payoff under a mixed strategy is simply a weighted average of the payoffs against pure strategies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 10.2. 被混合策略严格占优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strict Dominance by a Mixed Strategy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有些策略不被任何单个纯策略严格占优，但会被某个混合策略严格占优。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some strategies are not strictly dominated by any single pure strategy but are strictly dominated by a mixed strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|  | L | M | R |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T** | $(4,10)$ | $(3,0)$ | $(1,3)$ |
| **B** | $(0,0)$ | $(2,10)$ | $(10,3)$ |

对列玩家而言，$R$ 不被单个纯策略 $L$ 或 $M$ 严格占优。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the column player, neither pure strategy $L$ nor pure strategy $M$ strictly dominates $R$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但若列玩家以 $1/2$ 选 $L$、以 $1/2$ 选 $M$，则对任意行玩家混合概率 $x$，其期望收益都超过 $R$ 的收益 $3$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
However, if the column player chooses $L$ with probability $1/2$ and $M$ with probability $1/2$, the resulting expected payoff exceeds the payoff of $3$ from $R$ for every row-player mixing probability $x$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac12(10x)+\frac12(10(1-x))=5>3.
$$

所以 $R$ 仍是严格劣势策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus, $R$ is still strictly dominated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20240314080537.png]]

## 10.3. 占优于某个混合策略不能说明太多
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Dominating One Mixed Strategy Says Little by Itself*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面的例子说明：某个纯策略占优于某个混合策略，并不等于它占优于组成该混合策略的每个纯策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The following example shows that a pure strategy dominating one mixed strategy does not imply that it dominates every pure strategy in that mixture's support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|  | L | M | R |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T** | $(3,10)$ | $(0,0)$ | $(1,7)$ |
| **B** | $(0,0)$ | $(3,10)$ | $(1,7)$ |

对列玩家而言：

- $R$ 不严格占优 $L$。
- $R$ 不严格占优 $M$。
- 但 $R$ 可以严格占优某些 $L/M$ 的混合策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the column player:

- $R$ does not strictly dominate $L$;
- $R$ does not strictly dominate $M$;
- but $R$ can strictly dominate some mixtures of $L$ and $M$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 删除策略时要看被删对象
> 要删除一个纯策略，需要证明它被某个纯策略或混合策略严格占优；“它占优了某个混合策略”不能推出它可以删除别人。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To delete a pure strategy, prove that it is strictly dominated by a pure or mixed strategy. Showing that it dominates some mixed strategy does not justify deleting another strategy.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 10.4. 三人博弈中的混合占优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mixed Dominance in a Three-Player Game*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三人博弈中，一个人选行，一个人选列，第三个人选格子 $X/Y/Z$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In this three-player game, one player chooses the row, one chooses the column, and the third chooses the table $X/Y/Z$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**$X$：**

|  | L | R |
|---|---|---|
| **U** | $0$ | $2$ |
| **D** | $0$ | $3$ |

**$Y$：**

|  | L | R |
|---|---|---|
| **U** | $3$ | $0$ |
| **D** | $2$ | $0$ |

**$Z$：**

|  | L | R |
|---|---|---|
| **U** | $2$ | $0$ |
| **D** | $0$ | $2$ |

若行玩家以 $p$ 的概率选 $U$，列玩家以 $q$ 的概率选 $L$，要让 $Z$ 被某个混合策略 $\sigma_M$ 严格占优，必须对所有 $p,q$ 都更好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the row player chooses $U$ with probability $p$ and the column player chooses $L$ with probability $q$. For a mixed strategy $\sigma_M$ to strictly dominate $Z$, it must yield a higher payoff for every $p,q$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特别看 $p=q=1$ 与 $p=q=0$ 两个极端，会得到：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Examining the two extreme cases $p=q=1$ and $p=q=0$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
3\sigma_M(X)+2[1-\sigma_M(X)-\sigma_M(Y)]>2,
$$

$$
3\sigma_M(Y)+2[1-\sigma_M(X)-\sigma_M(Y)]>2.
$$

这两个条件无法同时满足，因此不能证明 $Z$ 是严格劣势策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These two conditions cannot hold simultaneously, so $Z$ cannot be shown to be strictly dominated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
