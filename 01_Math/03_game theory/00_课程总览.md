### 1. 策略式博弈：表示、理性筛选与 Nash 均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Strategic-form Games: Representation, Rational Screening, and Nash Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是本课程的默认起点。先从[[01_Math/03_game theory/02_初步认识博弈|初步认识博弈]]辨认战略互动，再进入[[01_Math/03_game theory/03_策略式博弈|策略式博弈课堂笔记]]连续看完定义、推导和课堂例子。下面的嵌入按概念依赖重排；[[策略式博弈.canvas|主题 Canvas]]只负责显示全局关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the course's default starting point. Begin with the [[01_Math/03_game theory/02_初步认识博弈|introduction to games]] to recognise strategic interaction, then read the [[01_Math/03_game theory/03_策略式博弈|strategic-form class notes]] continuously for definitions, derivations, and worked class examples. The embeds below reorganise the material by conceptual dependency; the [[策略式博弈.canvas|Topic Canvas]] shows only the global relationships.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[策略式博弈.canvas]]

先把对象写对。博弈模型列出参与者、选择环境与收益；策略式表示再把每个人的完整策略放进同一个收益函数中。行动与策略在同时行动的一步博弈里可能同名，但在一般博弈中不能混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First specify the objects correctly. A game model records the participants, choice environment, and payoffs; the strategic form then places each player's complete strategies inside one payoff function. An action and a strategy may share a label in a one-shot simultaneous game, but they are not interchangeable in general.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[博弈模型]]

![[行动与策略]]

![[策略式博弈]]

占优先问一项策略能否不依赖对手选择就胜过另一项；若每个人都有这样的优势策略，就得到比 Nash 更强的优势策略均衡。多数博弈没有这种捷径，于是必须转向“给定别人怎么做，我该怎样最好回应”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dominance first asks whether one strategy beats another independently of the opponents' choices. If every player has such a dominant strategy, the result is a dominant-strategy equilibrium, a stronger solution concept than Nash. Most games offer no such shortcut, so the analysis must turn to the question: given what others do, how should I respond optimally?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[严格与弱占优]]

![[优势策略均衡]]

![[最佳反应]]

混合策略是在完整纯策略上随机化。它既扩展了最佳反应与 Nash 的候选范围，也会让某些没有被任何单个纯策略支配的策略暴露为严格劣势；因此，进入迭代删除前要先知道比较集合是否包含混合策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A mixed strategy randomises over complete pure strategies. It both enlarges the candidates for best responses and Nash equilibrium and reveals some strategies as strictly dominated even though no single pure strategy dominates them. Before iterated deletion, therefore, determine whether the comparison set includes mixed strategies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[混合策略]]

![[混合占优]]

![[劣势策略迭代删除]]

![[可理性化策略]]

Nash 均衡要求每个人同时对其他人的实际策略作最佳反应；可理性化只要求某套不断加深的理性信念能够支持该策略，所以条件更弱。找到候选均衡后，还要把“无人愿意单边偏离”与“结果是否有效率”分开判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nash equilibrium requires every player to best respond to the others' actual strategies at the same time. Rationalizability is weaker: some hierarchy of rational beliefs need only support the strategy. After locating an equilibrium candidate, keep “no profitable unilateral deviation” separate from “is the outcome efficient?”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[纳什均衡]]

![[均衡与效率]]

最后进入真正的混合 Nash。正概率支持内的纯策略必须无差异并达到最高收益，这把均衡条件转成可计算方程；解出来以后，再用存在性和奇数性定理检查自己究竟能断言什么、哪些退化情形又超出了定理边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally move to genuinely mixed Nash equilibrium. Pure strategies used with positive probability must tie at the maximal payoff, turning the equilibrium condition into computable equations. After solving, use the existence and oddness results to distinguish what is guaranteed from what fails in degenerate cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[混合策略均衡]]

![[混合均衡求解]]

![[纳什存在性]]

![[均衡奇数性]]

#### 先建立基准，再辨认模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Establish the Benchmark, Then Identify the Game*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单一决策者的垄断问题给出“内部化全部价格影响”的基准。完全价格歧视再把数量效率与剩余分配分开。进入多方互动后，必须先根据控制变量、时序、产品差异、容量和期限选择模型，不能从企业数量直接跳到某个公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The monopoly problem supplies a benchmark in which one decision-maker internalises the entire price effect. Perfect price discrimination then separates quantity efficiency from surplus distribution. With several strategic actors, choose the model from actions, timing, differentiation, capacity, and horizon rather than jumping from firm count to a formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 从反应方向回到收益结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From Response Directions Back to Payoff Structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

战略替代与互补描述单值最佳反应怎样随对手行动移动；超模与次模分析则回到更基础的递增或递减差异、策略空间与正则条件。局部曲线斜率不是完整的全局定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strategic substitutes and complements describe how a single-valued best response moves with a rival's action. Supermodular and submodular analysis returns to the deeper conditions of increasing or decreasing differences, ordered strategy spaces, and regularity. A local curve slope is not a complete global definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 相对投入决定概率：广告份额竞赛
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Relative Effort Determines Success Probability: The Advertising-Share Contest*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

广告份额竞赛先定义比例成功函数和零投入规则，再推导一条先升后降的最佳反应，最后用两条反应条件得到唯一正投入均衡。零点不存在最佳反应，却不妨碍内部均衡存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The advertising-share contest first defines the proportional success function and its zero-effort rule, then derives a best response that changes direction, and finally intersects the response conditions at a unique positive-effort equilibrium. A missing best response at zero does not prevent an interior equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[广告份额竞赛]]

![[广告竞赛反应函数]]

![[广告竞赛均衡]]

#### 最高投入者获胜：全支付竞赛
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Highest Bid Wins: The All-Pay Contest*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全支付竞赛同样让所有人承担投入，却用最高投入确定赢家。只要双方价值均为正、投入连续且规则保持不变，并列可被略微超过，严格领先又可以压低成本，所以价值是否相等都不存在纯均衡；双方在 $[0,v]$ 上均匀随机化则是等价值基准的进一步结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The all-pay contest also makes everyone pay, but the highest bid determines the winner. With positive valuations, continuous bids, and the stated rules, a tie can be slightly overbid and a strict lead can be reduced, so valuation equality is not needed for pure-equilibrium failure. Uniform randomisation on $[0,v]$ is the further equal-value result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[全支付竞赛]]

![[全支付竞赛无纯均衡]]

![[全支付竞赛混合均衡]]

#### 重复互动的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundaries of Repeated Interaction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一次阶段博弈的竞争变量并不会因为重复而消失。无限期互动能否支持合作取决于可监控偏离、折现和可信续局；共同知道末期的有限重复则可能被倒推拆解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repetition does not erase the stage game's strategic variable. Indefinite interaction can support cooperation only through monitoring, discounting, and credible continuation play, while a commonly known final period may unravel a finite repetition by backward induction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 扩展式表示、可信续局与子博弈精炼
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Extensive-Form Representation, Credible Continuations, and Subgame Refinement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从[[01_Math/03_game theory/06_扩展性博弈|课堂笔记]]进入时，先连续读进入威慑、扩展式转策略式、逆向归纳与千足虫例子，然后用下面的原子顺序回顾定义、求解方法与适用边界。[[扩展式博弈.canvas|主题 Canvas]]展示全局关系；这里的嵌入顺序是可连续阅读的学习路径。

扩展式先保留时序与观察结构；策略式表示再把每名玩家的[[行动与策略|完整应对计划]]及其诱导收益放到同一个比较空间。这张共享原子同时服务于策略式与扩展式主题，不在本节重复嵌入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Extensive form first preserves timing and observation. Strategic form then places each player's [[行动与策略|complete contingent plan]] and its induced payoff in one comparison space. That shared atom serves both the strategic-form and extensive-form topics and is linked here rather than embedded twice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行动可观察且树有限时，逆向归纳把已解出的续局向前代回；有并列最优时要保留所有分支，不能把“可倒推”误读成“均衡唯一”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With observed actions and a finite tree, backward induction substitutes solved continuations into earlier decisions. Tied best actions must all be retained, so solvability by backward induction does not imply a unique equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[逆向归纳]]

单次偏离原理是一项带条件的缩减定理，不是 SPE 的定义。在有限期多阶段可观察行动博弈中，它才允许用逐历史的一次局部偏离检验 SPE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The one-shot deviation principle is a conditional reduction theorem, not the definition of SPE. In a finite-horizon multistage game with observed actions, it permits SPE to be tested through one local deviation after each history.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

威胁是否可信，要假定相应续局真的发生，再比较执行者的后续收益。承诺若真能改变这项结论，它改变的是行动集、收益、时序或信息中的至少一项；因此必须先改模型，再重新求解。

To assess credibility, suppose the relevant continuation is actually reached and compare the executor's continuation payoffs. If commitment changes the answer, it must have changed at least one of the action set, payoffs, timing, or information. The model must therefore be modified before it is solved again.

Beyond private information, the order of actions changes what can be inferred. 有限标准信号博弈中，mixed pooling 与 mixed separating PBE 都能纯化。因此，如果两个纯端点均不存在，均衡存在性与三分法会迫使至少一份 PBE 是半分离的。

weak PBE 留下的离轨信念自由会造成多重均衡；直观准则与 D1 从类型偏离激励出发进一步筛选。两个准则各自有不能塞回定义卡的边界：直观准则在所有类型均被均衡支配时保持沉默，D1 的弱包含与严格包含口径则只在集合恰好相等时分歧。D1 最终比直观准则更严格，但这是一条需要单独理解的比较结论。

The freedom of off-path beliefs under weak PBE creates multiplicity. The Intuitive Criterion and D1 further restrict beliefs using type-specific deviation incentives. Each has a boundary that should not be folded into its definition: the Intuitive Criterion is silent when every type is equilibrium dominated, while weak- and proper-inclusion versions of D1 differ only when the relevant sets are equal. D1 is ultimately more restrictive, but that is a separate comparison result to understand.
