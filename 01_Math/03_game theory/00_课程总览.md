### 2. 纳什均衡、混合策略与寡头竞争
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Nash Equilibrium, Mixed Strategies, and Oligopoly Competition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

写清参与者、策略与收益后，才能问每个人面对他人选择时的最优反应，并由此定义 Nash 均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once players, strategies, and payoffs are explicit, we can ask for each player's best response to the choices of others and thereby define a Nash equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[纳什均衡、混合策略与寡头竞争]]

### 3. 贝叶斯博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Bayesian Games*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Nash 均衡默认博弈结构已知；当玩家掌握私人类型时，策略必须对类型作出条件反应，于是进入 Bayesian 博弈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A Nash equilibrium assumes that the structure of the game is known. When players have private types, strategies must condition on those types, leading to Bayesian games.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[贝叶斯博弈]]

### 4. 扩展式博弈、子博弈精炼与序贯信念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Extensive-Form Games, Subgame Refinement, and Sequential Beliefs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

私人信息之外，行动先后也改变可行推理；扩展式表示把时间、信息集、信念和序贯理性放到同一棵树上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Beyond private information, the order of actions changes what can be inferred. The extensive form places time, information sets, beliefs, and sequential rationality on a single game tree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[扩展式博弈、子博弈精炼与序贯信念]]
