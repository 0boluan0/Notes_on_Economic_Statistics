## 1.1. 从普通性别博弈出发
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Starting from the Standard Battle of the Sexes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

贝叶斯版性别博弈（Bayesian Battle of the Sexes）是在 [[03_策略式博弈#5.2. 性别博弈：咖啡厅还是酒吧|性别博弈]] 上加入不完全信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Bayesian Battle of the Sexes adds incomplete information to the [[03_策略式博弈#5.2. 性别博弈：咖啡厅还是酒吧|standard Battle of the Sexes]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原来的问题是：两人都想见面，但偏好地点不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the original problem, both people want to meet but prefer different locations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在的变化是：玩家 1 不知道玩家 2 的类型，而玩家 2 知道自己的类型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The new feature is that player 1 does not know player 2's type, while player 2 observes their own type.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 两种类型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Two Types*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

玩家 2 有两种类型：$2_l$ 与 $2_h$。两种类型各以概率 $\frac12$ 出现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Player 2 has two possible types, $2_l$ and $2_h$, each occurring with probability $\frac12$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**Type $2_l$：**

|  | Cafe | Pub |
|---|---|---|
| **Cafe** | $(4,3)$ | $(1,1)$ |
| **Pub** | $(0,0)$ | $(3,4)$ |

**Type $2_h$：**

|  | Cafe | Pub |
|---|---|---|
| **Cafe** | $(4,0)$ | $(1,4)$ |
| **Pub** | $(0,3)$ | $(3,1)$ |

>[!note] 贝叶斯版本的关键
> 玩家 2 知道自己是哪种类型；玩家 1 不知道玩家 2 的类型，只知道类型分布。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Player 2 knows their own type. Player 1 does not know player 2's type and knows only its probability distribution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3. 转成有限类型代理博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Converting to a Finite Type-Agent Game*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子的类型空间有限，因此可以为了计算，把玩家 2 的每种类型暂时列成一个[[类型代理表示|代理决策者]]。原博弈仍只有玩家 1 和玩家 2；下面是用来逐类型检查最佳反应的代理博弈：

- 代理决策者集合：$N^A=\{1,2_l,2_h\}$。
- 玩家 1 的策略：$C$ 或 $P$。
- 类型 $2_l$ 的策略：$C_l$ 或 $P_l$。
- 类型 $2_h$ 的策略：$C_h$ 或 $P_h$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because this example has a finite type space, calculation can temporarily list each type of player 2 as [[类型代理表示|an agent]]. The original game still has players 1 and 2; the following agent game checks best responses type by type:

- type-agent set: $N^A=\{1,2_l,2_h\}$;
- player 1's strategies: $C$ or $P$;
- type $2_l$'s strategies: $C_l$ or $P_l$;
- type $2_h$'s strategies: $C_h$ or $P_h$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收益表如下，每格为 $(u_1,u_{2_l},u_{2_h})$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The payoff table is shown below, with each cell ordered as $(u_1,u_{2_l},u_{2_h})$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| $s_1$ \ $(s_{2_l},s_{2_h})$ | $(C_l,C_h)$ | $(C_l,P_h)$ | $(P_l,C_h)$ | $(P_l,P_h)$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $C$ | $(4,3,0)$ | $(\frac52,3,4)$ | $(\frac52,1,0)$ | $(1,1,4)$ |
| $P$ | $(0,0,3)$ | $(\frac32,0,1)$ | $(\frac32,4,3)$ | $(3,4,1)$ |

玩家 1 的收益需要对玩家 2 的类型取期望；类型玩家自己的收益按自己那张矩阵计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Player 1's payoff is averaged over player 2's possible types. Each type-agent's payoff is read from the matrix corresponding to that type.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.4. 纯策略均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pure-Strategy Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

纯策略 [[贝叶斯纳什均衡|贝叶斯纳什均衡]] 要求：

- 玩家 1 的选择是对两种类型平均后的最优反应。
- 类型 $2_l$ 的选择是其自身类型下的最优反应。
- 类型 $2_h$ 的选择是其自身类型下的最优反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pure-strategy [[贝叶斯纳什均衡|Bayesian Nash Equilibrium]] requires that:

- player 1's choice is a best response after averaging over the two types;
- type $2_l$'s choice is a best response under that type's payoff matrix;
- type $2_h$'s choice is a best response under that type's payoff matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] 手算方法
> 在这个[[类型代理表示|有限类型代理表示]]中，先把不同类型列成代理决策者，再使用普通纳什均衡的划线法；最后把结果翻译回“原玩家在每个类型下选什么”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In this [[类型代理表示|finite type-agent representation]], list the types as agent decision makers and apply the usual marking method for Nash equilibrium. Then translate the result back into an action for the original player at each type.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.5. 混合策略均衡验证
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Verifying a Mixed-Strategy Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

验证混合策略均衡时，关键是无差异条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The key to verifying a mixed-strategy equilibrium is the indifference condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面把有限类型展开成代理后，直接给每个类型代理一份行动分布；这对应[[贝叶斯混合与行为策略|逐类型的行为策略]]。它在本例中可由完整纯策略上的混合分布实现，但两者不是同一个概率对象，见[[贝叶斯随机化结果等价]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After expanding the finite types into agents, the calculation below assigns an action distribution directly to each type-agent; this is a [[贝叶斯混合与行为策略|type-contingent behavioral strategy]]. It can be implemented by a mixed distribution over complete pure strategies in this example, but the two probability objects are not identical; see [[贝叶斯随机化结果等价|Bayesian randomization outcome equivalence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如候选：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the candidate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\left\{\left(\frac13,\frac23\right),\ P_l,\ \left(\frac23,\frac13\right)\right\}.
$$

对玩家 1：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For player 1:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
U_1(C)=\frac23\cdot\frac52+\frac13\cdot 1=2,
$$

$$
U_1(P)=\frac23\cdot\frac32+\frac13\cdot 3=2.
$$

因此玩家 1 在 $C$ 与 $P$ 之间无差异，可以混合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Player 1 is therefore indifferent between $C$ and $P$ and can mix between them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对类型 $2_l$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For type $2_l$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
U_{2_l}(C_l)=\frac13\cdot 3+\frac23\cdot 0=1,
$$

$$
U_{2_l}(P_l)=\frac13\cdot 1+\frac23\cdot 4=3.
$$

所以 $2_l$ 选择 $P_l$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus, type $2_l$ chooses $P_l$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对类型 $2_h$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For type $2_h$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
U_{2_h}(C_h)=\frac13\cdot 0+\frac23\cdot 3=2,
$$

$$
U_{2_h}(P_h)=\frac13\cdot 4+\frac23\cdot 1=2.
$$

因此 $2_h$ 可以混合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus, type $2_h$ can mix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 贝叶斯博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bayesian Games*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1. 模型对象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Objects in the Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[贝叶斯博弈]]在普通策略式博弈的玩家、行动与收益函数之外，还要说明收益相关状态、私人类型和每个类型的事中信念。教材有时把收益状态并入类型记号，但这只是特殊写法，见[[类型与收益参数]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[贝叶斯博弈|Bayesian game]] augments the players, actions, and payoff functions of an ordinary strategic-form game with payoff-relevant states, private types, and each type's interim belief. Some texts fold the payoff state into the type notation, but this is a special representation; see [[类型与收益参数|types versus payoff parameters]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 元素 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| 玩家 | 谁在决策 |
| 行动 | 玩家实际可选择的行为 |
| 收益参数 | 影响给定行动组合收益的物理状态 |
| 类型 | 同一个玩家可能观察到的私人信息状态 |
| 收益函数 | 行动组合与收益参数怎样决定效用 |
| 事中信念 | 玩家给定自身类型后，对收益参数和他人类型的条件判断 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Component | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Players | The decision makers |
| Actions | What a player can actually choose |
| Payoff parameters | Physical states that affect payoffs for a given action profile |
| Types | Private-information states a player may observe |
| Payoff functions | How action profiles and payoff parameters determine utility |
| Interim beliefs | A player's conditional assessment of payoff parameters and other players' types after observing their own type |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2. 玩家
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Players*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的玩家仍然是自然人或组织，不把不同类型直接当成不同玩家。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The players remain the actual people or organisations; distinct types are not literally separate players.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

类型信息由下一项单独描述。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Type information is described separately in the next component.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3. 类型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Types*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[博弈类型|类型]]（type）表示玩家关于收益参数的私人信息状态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[博弈类型|type]] represents a player's private information about payoff parameters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个玩家在不同类型下可能有不同收益函数、成本、偏好或能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same player may have different payoff functions, costs, preferences, or capabilities under different types.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.4. 行动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Actions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行动（action）是玩家在某个节点或某个情境下实际选择的行为。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An action is the behaviour a player actually chooses at a particular node or in a particular situation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 类型和行动不要混淆
> 类型是“我是谁/我处于什么状态”；行动是“我做什么”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A type describes who I am or what state I am in; an action describes what I do.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.5. 信念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Beliefs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[事中信念|事中信念（interim belief）]]是玩家给定自身类型后，对未知收益参数和其他玩家类型的条件分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[事中信念|interim belief]] is the player's conditional distribution over unknown payoff parameters and the other players' types after observing their own type.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在本章的静态 [[贝叶斯纳什均衡|BNE]] 中，这些事中信念已由博弈模型给定；只有进入[[信号博弈与PBE.canvas|信号博弈与 PBE]]、观察行动之后，后验信念（posterior belief）才需按相应的一致性条件与策略及已观察行为衔接。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the static [[贝叶斯纳什均衡|BNE]] studied here, [[事中信念|interim beliefs]] are primitives of the game. Only after moving to [[信号博弈与PBE.canvas|signalling games and PBE]], where actions are observed, must posterior beliefs satisfy the relevant consistency condition linking beliefs, strategies, and observed behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.6. 收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Payoffs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般表述把收益写成行动组合与收益参数的函数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the general formulation, payoffs are functions of the action profile and payoff-relevant state:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
u_i:\Theta\times A\to\mathbb R.
$$

若类型剖面已经完整索引收益状态，也可以简写成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the type profile already indexes the entire payoff state, this may be abbreviated as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
u_i(a_1,\ldots,a_n;t_1,\ldots,t_n).
$$

# 3. 有限类型的代理表示
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Agent Representation with Finite Types*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. 有限类型的代理玩家表示
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Type-Agent Representation for Finite Type Spaces*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在有限类型空间中，解题时常把“每个玩家的每种类型”视为策略式计算里的一个[[类型代理表示|代理决策者]]。这是为了逐类型检查最佳反应的计算表示，不会改变原博弈的真实玩家集合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite type space, calculation can treat each possible type as [[类型代理表示|an agent in a strategic-form representation]]. This checks best responses type by type; it does not change the original set of players.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如玩家 2 有 $2_l,2_h$ 两种类型，就把它们拆成两个类型玩家。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if player 2 has types $2_l$ and $2_h$, represent them as two type-agents.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. 策略转换
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Converting Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[贝叶斯策略|贝叶斯博弈中的完整策略]]不是单一行动，而是一个类型到行动的函数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[贝叶斯策略|complete strategy in a Bayesian game]] is not a single action but a function from types to actions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
s_i:T_i\to A_i.
$$

也就是说，策略必须说明“如果我是每一种可能类型，我会怎么做”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In other words, a strategy must say what the player would do under every possible type.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.3. 收益转换
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Converting Payoffs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

转换后仍然用期望收益判断最优反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After the conversion, best responses are still determined using expected payoffs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不知情玩家的收益需要对未知类型取期望；知情类型玩家按自己的类型收益计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An uninformed player's payoff is averaged over unknown types, while an informed type-agent evaluates the payoff corresponding to that type.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 这就是 BNE 的手算核心
> 先把类型展开，再用普通纳什均衡逻辑检查每个类型是否愿意偏离。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Expand the types first, then use ordinary Nash-equilibrium reasoning to check whether each type wants to deviate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. 模型设定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Model Setup*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

含噪声的性别博弈在收益中加入私人扰动：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The noisy Battle of the Sexes adds private payoff shocks:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|  | Cafe | Pub |
|---|---|---|
| **Cafe** | $(4+\varepsilon,3)$ | $(1+\varepsilon,1+\delta)$ |
| **Pub** | $(0,0)$ | $(3,4+\delta)$ |

其中：

- $a>0$；玩家 1 观察自己的扰动 $\varepsilon$，玩家 2 观察自己的扰动 $\delta$。
- 下面的闭式计算明确采用 $\varepsilon,\delta\overset{ind}{\sim}U[0,a]$：两个扰动彼此独立，且都在 $[0,a]$ 上均匀分布。
- 双方知道这一分布，但只观察自己的实现值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where:

- $a>0$; player 1 observes their own shock $\varepsilon$, and player 2 observes their own shock $\delta$;
- the closed-form calculation below explicitly assumes $\varepsilon,\delta\overset{ind}{\sim}U[0,a]$: the shocks are independent and each is uniform on $[0,a]$;
- both players know this distribution but observe only their own realization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 这是为补全计算而明示的假设
> 原课堂手写图只画出了支持集 $[0,a]$，没有写密度或独立性。但 $\Pr(\varepsilon<\varepsilon^*)=\varepsilon^*/a$ 只能在均匀分布下直接使用；为复现课堂后续的数值，本页把“独立均匀”作为额外模型假设明示写出。若原课程意图使用其他分布，应把下面的比例换成相应 CDF，数值也要重算。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The handwritten class diagrams show only the support $[0,a]$; they do not state a density or independence. The identity $\Pr(\varepsilon<\varepsilon^*)=\varepsilon^*/a$ follows directly only under a uniform distribution. To reproduce the later classroom number, this page now states independent uniform shocks as an additional modelling assumption. If the course intended another distribution, the ratios below must be replaced by the relevant CDFs and the numerical cutoff recomputed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2. 临界值策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Threshold Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这类博弈常出现[[贝叶斯阈值策略|临界值策略]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Games of this kind often produce [[贝叶斯阈值策略|threshold strategies]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以玩家 1 为例：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For player 1, for example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
S_1(\varepsilon)=
\begin{cases}
\text{Pub}, & 0\le \varepsilon<\varepsilon^*,\\
\text{Cafe}, & \varepsilon^*<\varepsilon\le a.
\end{cases}
$$

在 $\varepsilon=\varepsilon^*$ 处，玩家 1 无差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $\varepsilon=\varepsilon^*$, player 1 is indifferent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 临界值的含义
> 在这张收益表里，更高的 $\varepsilon$ 提高玩家 1 选 Cafe 的收益，所以玩家 1 在低类型选 Pub、高类型选 Cafe；更高的 $\delta$ 提高玩家 2 选 Pub 的收益，所以玩家 2 在低类型选 Cafe、高类型选 Pub。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In this payoff table, a higher $\varepsilon$ raises player 1's payoff from Cafe, so player 1 chooses Pub below the cutoff and Cafe above it. A higher $\delta$ raises player 2's payoff from Pub, so player 2 chooses Cafe below the cutoff and Pub above it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.3. 临界值求解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Solving for the Threshold*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设玩家 1 在 $\varepsilon<\varepsilon^*$ 时选 Pub，玩家 2 在 $\delta<\delta^*$ 时选 Cafe。由独立均匀假设，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose player 1 chooses Pub when $\varepsilon<\varepsilon^*$, while player 2 chooses Cafe when $\delta<\delta^*$. Under the independent-uniform assumption,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
q_1:=\Pr(S_1=P)=\frac{\varepsilon^*}{a},
\qquad
q_2:=\Pr(S_2=C)=\frac{\delta^*}{a}.
$$

对玩家 2，选 Cafe 与 Pub 的事中期望收益分别是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Player 2's interim expected payoffs from Cafe and Pub are, respectively,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
U_2(C\mid\delta)=3(1-q_1),
$$

$$
U_2(P\mid\delta)=(1-q_1)(1+\delta)+q_1(4+\delta)
=1+\delta+3q_1.
$$

因此玩家 2 选 Pub 而非 Cafe 的收益差为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Player 2's payoff difference from choosing Pub rather than Cafe is therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta_2(\delta;\varepsilon^*)
=\delta-2+\frac{6\varepsilon^*}{a}.
$$

边际类型 $\delta^*$ 无差异，所以
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indifference of the marginal type $\delta^*$ therefore gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\delta^*=2-\frac{6\varepsilon^*}{a}.
$$

对玩家 1，同样的计算给出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For player 1, the corresponding calculation gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
U_1(C\mid\varepsilon)=1+\varepsilon+3q_2,
\qquad
U_1(P\mid\varepsilon)=3(1-q_2),
$$

$$
\Delta_1(\varepsilon;\delta^*)
:=U_1(C\mid\varepsilon)-U_1(P\mid\varepsilon)
=\varepsilon-2+\frac{6\delta^*}{a},
$$

因而
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
and hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\varepsilon^*=2-\frac{6\delta^*}{a}.
$$

令两个临界值相等，得到对称候选
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imposing equal cutoffs yields the symmetric candidate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\bar\delta=\bar\varepsilon=:c^*=\frac{2a}{6+a}.
$$

## 4.4. 两侧激励与非唯一性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Incentives on Both Sides and Non-Uniqueness*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意 $a>0$，$0<c^*<a$。把对称条件代回收益差，可得
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every $a>0$, $0<c^*<a$. Substituting the symmetric condition back into the payoff differences gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta_1(\varepsilon;c^*)=\varepsilon-c^*,
\qquad
\Delta_2(\delta;c^*)=\delta-c^*.
$$

所以玩家 1 在 $\varepsilon<c^*$ 时确实选 Pub、在 $\varepsilon>c^*$ 时选 Cafe；玩家 2 在 $\delta<c^*$ 时确实选 Cafe、在 $\delta>c^*$ 时选 Pub。这完成了对称候选的两侧激励验证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Player 1 therefore chooses Pub for $\varepsilon<c^*$ and Cafe for $\varepsilon>c^*$, while player 2 chooses Cafe for $\delta<c^*$ and Pub for $\delta>c^*$. This verifies incentives on both sides of the symmetric candidate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但对称候选不是所有参数下的唯一阈值均衡。把临界最佳反应限制在 $[0,a]$ 内，可写成

$$
BR_1(\delta^*)
=\min\!\left\{a,\max\!\left\{0,2-\frac{6\delta^*}{a}\right\}\right\},
$$

$$
BR_2(\varepsilon^*)
=\min\!\left\{a,\max\!\left\{0,2-\frac{6\varepsilon^*}{a}\right\}\right\}.
$$

解这两条最佳反应的交点：

- $0<a<6$ 时，除 $(c^*,c^*)$ 外，还有 $(\varepsilon^*,\delta^*)=(0,\min\{2,a\})$ 与 $(\min\{2,a\},0)$ 两个非对称解；
- $a=6$ 时，任意 $\varepsilon^*,\delta^*\in[0,2]$ 且 $\varepsilon^*+\delta^*=2$ 都满足两个临界最佳反应；
- $a>6$ 时，这组裁剪后的阈值最佳反应只交于对称解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symmetric candidate is not the unique cutoff equilibrium for every parameter value. Clipping the two cutoff best responses to $[0,a]$ gives the two displayed $BR$ functions. Their intersections imply:

- when $0<a<6$, two asymmetric solutions, $(\varepsilon^*,\delta^*)=(0,\min\{2,a\})$ and $(\min\{2,a\},0)$, coexist with $(c^*,c^*)$;
- when $a=6$, every pair $\varepsilon^*,\delta^*\in[0,2]$ satisfying $\varepsilon^*+\delta^*=2$ solves the two cutoff best responses;
- when $a>6$, the clipped cutoff best responses intersect only at the symmetric solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 这一节究竟证明了什么
> $c^*=2a/(6+a)$ 是已经通过两侧激励检查的**对称阈值 BNE**；它不能在不查其他最佳反应交点时被称为“唯一均衡”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The calculation establishes $c^*=2a/(6+a)$ as a **symmetric cutoff BNE** with both sides of the cutoff checked. It does not justify calling that candidate the unique equilibrium without checking the other intersections of the best-response correspondence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 5. 全局博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Global Games*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.1. 基本直觉
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Core Intuition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[全局博弈]]是[[贝叶斯博弈]]的一个特殊族，而不是与它并列的模型。与前一节“两个私人收益扰动独立”的例子不同，此处玩家对同一个随机收益基本面 $\theta$ 作带噪观察，例如 $x_i=\theta+\sigma\varepsilon_i$。因此观察自身信号会同时更新对 $\theta$ 和对方信号的[[相关类型|条件判断]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[全局博弈|global game]] is a special family of [[贝叶斯博弈|Bayesian games]], not a separate alternative. Unlike the preceding example with independent private payoff shocks, players now observe the same random payoff fundamental $\theta$ with private noise, for example $x_i=\theta+\sigma\varepsilon_i$. Observing one's own signal therefore updates beliefs about both $\theta$ and the [[相关类型|other player's signal]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 例子 | 私人信息关系 |
|---|---|
| 前一节的独立噪声例子 | $\varepsilon$ 与 $\delta$ 相互独立 |
| 全局博弈 | 自己的信号会影响对对方信号的判断 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Example | Relationship between private information |
|---|---|
| Preceding independent-noise example | $\varepsilon$ and $\delta$ are independent |
| Global game | A player's own signal changes their inference about the other player's signal |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 全局博弈的关键
> 知道自己的信号，也就大概知道对方可能看到什么信号。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Observing my own signal also gives me information about the signal the other player is likely to have observed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.2. 政府招标例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Government Procurement Example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两家公司勘查后得到工程成本估计。这个估计就是各自的类型；只有当估计来自同一随机工程成本与各自测量噪声时，这个例子才具有[[全局博弈|全局博弈]]的共同基本面结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After conducting surveys, two firms obtain estimates of the project's cost. Each estimate is the firm's type. The example has the common-fundamental structure of a [[全局博弈|global game]] only when the estimates arise from the same random project cost plus firm-specific measurement noise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果我知道自己的成本较低，也会推断对方的成本可能也不高；因此我的报价策略不仅取决于自己成本，也取决于我对对方信号的推断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If I observe a low cost estimate, I also infer that my rival's estimate may be low. My bidding strategy therefore depends not only on my own estimate but also on what it implies about the rival's signal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.3. 一级和二级密封拍卖
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First- and Second-Price Sealed-Bid Procurement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 一级密封拍卖：所有人密封报价，报价最低者中标，并按自己报价履约。
- 二级密封拍卖：报价最低者中标，但按第二低报价履约。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- First-price sealed-bid procurement: all bidders submit sealed bids; the lowest bidder wins and is paid its own bid.
- Second-price sealed-bid procurement: the lowest bidder wins but is paid the second-lowest bid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在风险中性、独立同分布私人成本、对称单调均衡、最低成本中标且最高成本边界类型获得同一期望租金等条件下，两种机制具有相同的事前期望支付，见[[采购拍卖支付等价]]。上一节若采用共同成本成分或相关信号，就不能自动套用这一结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With risk-neutral bidders, i.i.d. private costs, symmetric monotone equilibrium, efficient lowest-cost allocation, and the same expected rent for the highest-cost boundary type, the two mechanisms have the same ex-ante expected payment; see [[采购拍卖支付等价|payment equivalence in procurement auctions]]. A common-cost component or correlated signals from the preceding example do not automatically satisfy these conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.4. Global Game 在 Bayesian Game 中的位置
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Where Global Games Sit within Bayesian Games*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全局博弈本身就是贝叶斯博弈；这里真正需要区分的是两种私人信息结构：

- 前一节明确假设两个私人噪声独立，所以一方的噪声不更新对另一方噪声的判断。
- 本节的全局博弈里，私人信号共同受潜在基本面影响，所以玩家的条件信念会随自身信号变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A global game is itself a Bayesian game. The relevant distinction here is between two private-information structures:

- the preceding example explicitly assumes independent private noise, so one player's shock does not update their belief about the other's shock;
- in the global game here, both private signals depend on a latent fundamental, so a player's conditional belief changes with their own signal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种相关信号与战略互补常使阈值分析有用，但私人噪声本身不保证唯一均衡；唯一性还依赖优势区域、单调性和噪声尺度等条件，见[[全局博弈唯一性边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Correlated signals and strategic complementarity often make cutoff analysis useful, but private noise alone does not guarantee a unique equilibrium. Uniqueness also depends on dominance regions, monotonicity, the scale of noise, and related conditions; see [[全局博弈唯一性边界|the uniqueness boundary of global games]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 6. 协同进攻
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Coordinated Attack*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6.1. 基本信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basic Setup*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[#6. 协同进攻|协同进攻]]中，两位将军选择进攻（Attack）或撤退（Run）。本节先用完全信息的 $2\times2$ 协调博弈建立基准，到第 7 节才加入相关私人信号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[#6. 协同进攻|Coordinated Attack Game]], two generals choose Attack or Run. This section first gives a complete-information $2\times2$ coordination benchmark; correlated private signals enter only in Section 7.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 玩家：两位将军。
- 策略：$A$ 或 $R$。
- 进攻成本：$c$。
- 协同进攻成功收益：$v$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Players: two generals.
- Strategies: $A$ or $R$.
- Cost of attacking: $c$.
- Payoff from a successful coordinated attack: $v$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|  | A | R |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | $(v-c,v-c)$ | $(-c,0)$ |
| **R** | $(0,-c)$ | $(0,0)$ |

## 6.2. 纯策略均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pure-Strategy Equilibria*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $0<c<v$，则 $(A,A)$ 与 $(R,R)$ 都是纯策略纳什均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $0<c<v$, both $(A,A)$ and $(R,R)$ are pure-strategy Nash equilibria.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 如果对方进攻，我也进攻更好。
- 如果对方撤退，我也撤退更好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- If the other general attacks, I am better off attacking too.
- If the other general retreats, I am better off retreating too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6.3. 混合策略均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mixed-Strategy Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设玩家 1 以概率 $x$ 选择 $A$。玩家 2 选择 $A$ 的期望收益为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose player 1 chooses $A$ with probability $x$. Player 2's expected payoff from choosing $A$ is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
x(v-c)+(1-x)(-c)=xv-c.
$$

玩家 2 选择 $R$ 的收益为 $0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Player 2's payoff from choosing $R$ is $0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无差异条件为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The indifference condition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
xv-c=0\Rightarrow x^*=\frac{c}{v}.
$$

![[Pasted image 20240411195529.png]]

>[!item] 成本越高，维持混合所需的对手进攻概率越高
> $x^*=c/v$ 是让我对进攻和撤退恰好无差异的**对手**进攻概率。固定我对对手行动的信念时，更高的成本会降低我的进攻意愿；比较对称混合均衡时，为了让双方继续无差异，均衡中每人的进攻概率反而要随 $c$ 升高。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $x^*=c/v$ is the **opponent's** attack probability that makes me indifferent between attacking and retreating. Holding my belief fixed, a higher cost weakens my incentive to attack; across symmetric mixed equilibria, however, each player's equilibrium attack probability must rise with $c$ to preserve the other's indifference.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 7. 相关类型变体
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Variant with Correlated Types*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7.1. 信号结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Signal Structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在进攻成功收益固定为 10，但成本不确定。两方都派人打探成本，得到的信号可能是真实值，也可能偏离一步。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now the payoff from a successful attack is fixed at 10, but the cost is uncertain. Both sides investigate the cost and receive a signal that may equal the true value or differ from it by one unit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信号分布可以写成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The signal distribution can be written as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\tilde c=
\begin{cases}
c-1, & \text{w.p. } \frac14,\\
c, & \text{w.p. } \frac12,\\
c+1, & \text{w.p. } \frac14.
\end{cases}
$$

这三个概率只给出了“给定真实成本时，单个信号怎样扰动”的似然核 $q(x\mid c)$，还不是完整的贝叶斯模型。要从自己的信号推断对方，还必须知道：

- 真实成本 $C$ 的支持集与先验 $\pi(c)$；
- 给定 $C$ 后，两人的信号误差是否条件独立；
- 当 $c-1$ 或 $c+1$ 越过成本支持集边界时，概率质量怎样处理。

现有课堂笔记没有保存这三项，因而不足以唯一算出 $E[C\mid X_i=x]$ 或 $\Pr(A_j\mid X_i=x)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three probabilities specify only a likelihood kernel $q(x\mid c)$ for one signal conditional on the true cost; they do not yet define a complete Bayesian model. Inferring the opponent's signal additionally requires the support and prior $\pi(c)$ of the true cost $C$, the joint structure of the two signal errors conditional on $C$, and a rule for probability mass that would cross the boundary of the cost support. The surviving class notes do not specify these objects, so they do not uniquely determine $E[C\mid X_i=x]$ or $\Pr(A_j\mid X_i=x)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7.2. 条件化计算框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditional Calculation Framework*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若未来从课件或老师原始例子中找回先验 $\pi$ 与边界规则，首先应对每个观察 $x$ 用 Bayes 法则计算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the prior $\pi$ and boundary rule are recovered from the original source, Bayes' rule gives, for each observation $x$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Pr(C=c\mid X_i=x)
=\frac{\pi(c)q(x\mid c)}{\sum_k\pi(k)q(x\mid k)},
\qquad
E[C\mid X_i=x]
=\sum_c c\,\Pr(C=c\mid X_i=x).
$$

设对手 $j$ 在信号集 $B_j$ 上进攻。若两人的信号在给定 $C$ 后条件独立，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose opponent $j$ attacks on a set of signals $B_j$. If the two signals are conditionally independent given $C$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Pr(A_j\mid X_i=x)
=\sum_c \Pr(C=c\mid X_i=x)
\sum_{y\in B_j}q(y\mid c).
$$

若不条件独立，内层和式必须换成 $\Pr(X_j\in B_j\mid C=c,X_i=x)$；这就是为什么不能从单个信号的边际分布猜对手的进攻概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without conditional independence, the inner sum must be replaced by $\Pr(X_j\in B_j\mid C=c,X_i=x)$. A one-signal marginal distribution therefore cannot by itself determine the opponent's attack probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从第 6 节的收益表可知，撤退收益为 0；进攻且对方也进攻时得 $10-C$，单独进攻时得 $-C$。因而给定自身信号 $x$，进攻相对撤退的事中收益差恰为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From the Section 6 payoff table, retreat yields zero, coordinated attack yields $10-C$, and attacking alone yields $-C$. Conditional on one's signal $x$, the interim payoff difference between attack and retreat is therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta_i(x;B_j)
=10\Pr(A_j\mid X_i=x)-E[C\mid X_i=x].
$$

只有算出这个差，才能判定信号 $x$ 下应进攻、撤退还是无差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only after evaluating this difference can one decide whether signal $x$ prescribes attack, retreat, or indifference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7.3. 课堂数值结论的核验状态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Verification Status of the Classroom Numerical Conclusion*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 原课堂记录：待核
> 原笔记记录了“信号 $0,1,2,3,4$ 时进攻，信号 $6,7,8,9,10$ 时撤退，信号 $5$ 为临界”。但当前可用材料没有真实成本先验、双方信号的联合结构和边界处理，因而这组数字现在**不能被证明**，也不应用一句“战略互补”代替条件化计算。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The surviving notes record attack at signals $0,1,2,3,4$, retreat at $6,7,8,9,10$, and a cutoff at $5$. The available material omits the prior over true cost, the joint signal structure, and the boundary rule, so these particular numbers **cannot currently be proved**. Strategic complementarity alone cannot replace the conditional calculation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要核实“临界信号为 5”，应以对称候选 $B=\{0,1,2,3,4\}$ 为起点，然后对每个可能信号 $x$ 依次计算

1. $\Pr(C=c\mid X_i=x)$ 与 $E[C\mid X_i=x]$；
2. $\Pr(X_j\in B\mid X_i=x)$；
3. $\Delta_i(x;B)=10\Pr(X_j\in B\mid X_i=x)-E[C\mid X_i=x]$。

只有在 $x\in B$ 时 $\Delta_i(x;B)\ge0$、$x\notin B$ 时 $\Delta_i(x;B)\le0$，且 $x=5$ 的等号或跳跃处理与规定一致，这个阈值候选才是 BNE。若未来补回的先验或边界规则不同，临界值可能也不是 5。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To verify a cutoff at 5, start from the symmetric candidate attack set $B=\{0,1,2,3,4\}$. For every possible signal $x$, compute the posterior and conditional expected cost, the probability $\Pr(X_j\in B\mid X_i=x)$, and then $\Delta_i(x;B)=10\Pr(X_j\in B\mid X_i=x)-E[C\mid X_i=x]$. The candidate is a BNE only if $\Delta_i(x;B)\ge0$ on $B$, $\Delta_i(x;B)\le0$ outside $B$, and the equality or jump convention at $x=5$ is handled consistently. A different recovered prior or boundary rule may produce a cutoff other than 5.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
