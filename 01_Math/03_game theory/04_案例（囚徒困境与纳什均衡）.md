# 1. 垄断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Monopoly*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1. 基本设定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basic Setup*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当市场需求在相关区间可逆，而且价格与产量写法对应同一组可行交易时，垄断厂商可以选择价格，也可以等价地选择产量。完整的最优化条件与边界见[[垄断利润最大化]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When market demand is invertible on the relevant domain and the two formulations describe the same feasible transactions, a monopolist may equivalently choose price or output. See [[垄断利润最大化|monopoly profit maximization]] for the complete optimality and boundary checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若以价格 $p$ 为决策变量：

- $x(p)$：价格 $p$ 下的需求量。
- $c(x(p))$：生产该需求量的成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If price $p$ is the decision variable:

- $x(p)$ is quantity demanded at price $p$;
- $c(x(p))$ is the cost of producing that quantity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利润最大化问题为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The profit-maximisation problem is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\max_p \pi(p)=\max_p\{p\,x(p)-c(x(p))\}.
$$

若以产量 $q$ 为决策变量：

- $p(q)$：产量 $q$ 对应的反需求函数。
- $c(q)$：生产 $q$ 的成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If output $q$ is the decision variable:

- $p(q)$ is the inverse demand function at output $q$;
- $c(q)$ is the cost of producing $q$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利润最大化问题为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The profit-maximisation problem is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\max_q \pi(q)=\max_q\{p(q)q-c(q)\}.
$$

在上述可逆性条件下，二者只是写法不同：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under that invertibility condition, these are two equivalent formulations:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\max_p\{p\,x(p)-c(x(p))\}\iff \max_q\{p(q)q-c(q)\}.
$$

若需求存在平段、跳跃、配给或多值反需求，就必须先说明价格怎样决定销量，不能机械地把两种写法互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If demand has flat segments, jumps, rationing, or a multivalued inverse, the mapping from price to sales must be specified before the two formulations can be treated as interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 一阶条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First-Order Condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $q$ 求导：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiating with respect to $q$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p'(q^*)q^*+p(q^*)=c'(q^*).
$$

其中：

- 边际收益（marginal revenue）：$r'(q)=p'(q)q+p(q)$。
- 边际成本（marginal cost）：$c'(q)$。
- 内点候选条件：$MR(q^*)=MC(q^*)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where:

- marginal revenue is $r'(q)=p'(q)q+p(q)$;
- marginal cost is $c'(q)$;
- an interior candidate satisfies $MR(q^*)=MC(q^*)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一阶条件只给出候选。还要检查可行域、停产或容量边界，以及利润函数是否凹；否则满足 $MR=MC$ 的点未必是全局最优。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first-order condition identifies only a candidate. Feasibility, shutdown or capacity boundaries, and global concavity still need to be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 垄断为什么价格高、产量低
> 因为需求曲线向下倾斜，$p'(q)<0$，所以 $MR(q)<p(q)$。垄断厂商在 $MR=MC$ 处生产，对应的价格通常高于边际成本。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Because the demand curve slopes downward, $p'(q)<0$, so $MR(q)<p(q)$. A monopolist produces where $MR=MC$, and the corresponding price is generally above marginal cost.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20240321224444.png]]

图中完全竞争均衡在右侧，垄断产量更低、价格更高，中间阴影区域对应无谓损失（deadweight loss）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The competitive equilibrium appears on the right of the figure. Monopoly output is lower and its price is higher; the shaded area between them is the deadweight loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3. 完全价格歧视
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Perfect Price Discrimination*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[完全价格歧视]]要求垄断者识别每位消费者或每一边际单位的最高支付意愿，并能阻止转售。标准基准下，它可以收走全部消费者剩余。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[完全价格歧视|Perfect price discrimination]] requires the monopolist to identify each consumer's or marginal unit's maximum willingness to pay and prevent resale. In the benchmark model, it captures all consumer surplus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但“消费者剩余为零”不等于“存在传统无谓损失”。理想的一级价格歧视会让所有支付意愿不低于边际成本的交易发生，因此数量达到有效水平；变化的是剩余分配。现实实施困难、信息成本、隐私与公平问题需要单独分析，不能反过来改写这个理论基准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero consumer surplus is not the same as deadweight loss. Ideal first-degree discrimination implements every trade whose willingness to pay covers marginal cost, so the benchmark quantity is efficient while the surplus distribution changes. Practical information, implementation, privacy, and fairness concerns are separate questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 静态寡头竞争
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Static Oligopoly Competition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1. 先辨认竞争机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Identify the Competitive Mechanism First*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

静态寡头竞争（static oligopoly）中，厂商在同一时期选择策略。常见分类如下：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a static oligopoly, firms choose their strategies in the same period. Common forms include:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 竞争对象 | 典型模型 | 策略互动 |
|---|---|---|
| 价格 | [[Bertrand竞争|Bertrand 竞争]] | 低价者抢占需求 |
| 产量 | [[Cournot竞争|Cournot 竞争]] | 总产量影响市场价格 |
| 相对投入 | [[广告份额竞赛]] | 投入份额决定获胜概率或市场份额 |
| 最高投入 | [[全支付竞赛]] | 最高投入者获胜，但所有人都承担投入 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Competitive variable | Standard model | Strategic interaction |
|---|---|---|
| Price | [[Bertrand竞争|Bertrand Competition]] | The lower-priced firm captures demand |
| Output | [[Cournot竞争|Cournot Competition]] | Total output determines the market price |
| Relative effort | [[广告份额竞赛|Advertising-share contest]] | Effort shares determine success probabilities or market shares |
| Highest effort | [[全支付竞赛|All-pay contest]] | The highest effort wins, but everyone pays |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] 先判断竞争变量
> 题目问价格、产量还是投入，是 [[寡头模型选择|选择模型]] 和写最优反应函数的第一步。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Determining whether the problem concerns price, output, or investment is the first step in [[寡头模型选择|choosing a model]] and deriving a best-response function.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. 线性需求下的古诺竞争
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cournot Competition with Linear Demand*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. 模型信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Model Setup*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Cournot竞争|Cournot 模型中]]，两家厂商选择产量，市场价格由总产量决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In [[Cournot竞争|Cournot competition]], two firms choose outputs, and the market price is determined by their total output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 玩家：两家厂商 $i\in\{1,2\}$。
- 策略：厂商 1 选择 $x\in[0,\infty)$，厂商 2 选择 $y\in[0,\infty)$。
- 价格：$P=\max\{a-b(x+y),0\}$。
- 单位成本：$c$，并假设 $a>c>0$、$b>0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Players: two firms $i\in\{1,2\}$.
- Strategies: firm 1 chooses $x\in[0,\infty)$ and firm 2 chooses $y\in[0,\infty)$.
- Price: $P=\max\{a-b(x+y),0\}$.
- Unit cost: $c$, with $a>c>0$ and $b>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在正价格区域，收益函数为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the positive-price region, the payoff functions are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\pi_1=x[a-b(x+y)-c],\qquad
\pi_2=y[a-b(x+y)-c].
$$

若总产量使未截断价格为负，市场价格按零处理；正边际成本使任何正的零价格产量都劣于停产。这个边界比较是[[Cournot反应函数|完整最佳反应]]的一部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If aggregate output would make the untruncated price negative, price is set to zero. With positive marginal cost, producing a positive amount in that region is dominated by shutting down. This boundary comparison is part of the [[Cournot反应函数|complete best response]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. 最优反应函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Best-Response Functions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对厂商 1 的产量 $x$ 求一阶条件：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first-order condition with respect to firm 1's output $x$ is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\partial \pi_1}{\partial x}
=a-2bx-by-c=0.
$$

整理得到厂商 1 的 [[Cournot反应函数|最优反应函数]]：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rearranging gives firm 1's [[Cournot反应函数|best-response function]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
B_1(y)=\frac{a-c-by}{2b}.
$$

在考虑非负产量约束时，应写作：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imposing the nonnegativity constraint gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
B_1(y)=\max\left\{0,\frac{a-c-by}{2b}\right\}.
$$

对称地：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By symmetry,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
B_2(x)=\max\left\{0,\frac{a-c-bx}{2b}\right\}.
$$

>[!note] 古诺竞争的反应函数向下倾斜
> 对手产量越高，市场价格越低，我的边际收益越低，因此我的最优产量下降。产量是策略替代。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When a rival produces more, the market price falls and my marginal revenue declines, so my optimal output decreases. Outputs are strategic substitutes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20240322141305.png]]

## 3.3. 对称均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Symmetric Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 [[Cournot均衡|对称均衡]] 中 $x=y$。代入一阶条件：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[Cournot均衡|symmetric Cournot equilibrium]], $x=y$. Substituting into the first-order condition gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
2bx=a-bx-c.
$$

因此：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
3bx=a-c,\qquad x^*=y^*=\frac{a-c}{3b}.
$$

![[Pasted image 20240322213743.png]]

两条反应函数的交点给出静态 Nash 均衡。若另行假设企业在下一期采用对上一期产量的最佳反应，这个特定线性模型的调整过程会收敛；但那是额外的动态假设，不是“迭代删除”的定义，也不是均衡成立的证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The intersection of the two reaction functions gives the static Nash equilibrium. A best-response adjustment process converges in this particular linear model only after adding a separate dynamic assumption; it is neither the definition of iterated deletion nor the proof that the equilibrium exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.4. 拓展到 $n$ 家寡头
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Extension to $n$ Firms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若有 $n$ 家 Cournot 厂商（见 [[Cournot厂商数]]），总产量 $Q=\sum_{i=1}^n q_i$，对称一阶条件可写为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $n$ Cournot firms (see [[Cournot厂商数|the number-of-firms comparison]]) and total output $Q=\sum_{i=1}^n q_i$, the symmetric first-order condition can be written as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(Q)+q_iP'(Q)=c.
$$

对称时 $q_i=Q/n$，因此：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under symmetry, $q_i=Q/n$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(Q)+P'(Q)\frac{Q}{n}=c.
$$

>[!note] $n$ 的作用
> 厂商数量越多，单个厂商对价格的影响越弱，结果越接近完全竞争。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> As the number of firms rises, each firm's influence on price weakens and the outcome approaches perfect competition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4. 伯特兰竞争
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bertrand Competition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. 基本信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basic Setup*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Bertrand竞争|伯特兰竞争]]中，厂商选择价格而不是产量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In [[Bertrand竞争|Bertrand Competition]], firms choose prices rather than outputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 玩家：两家厂商 $i\in\{1,2\}$。
- 策略：厂商 $i$ 选择价格 $p_i\in[0,\infty)$。
- 产品：同质产品。
- 成本：相同边际成本 $c$。
- 需求：低价者获得全部市场；同价时平分市场。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Players: two firms $i\in\{1,2\}$.
- Strategy: firm $i$ chooses a price $p_i\in[0,\infty)$.
- Products: homogeneous goods.
- Costs: identical marginal cost $c$.
- Demand: the lower-priced firm serves the entire market; equal-priced firms split it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] Bertrand 竞争的直觉
> 如果两家价格都高于成本，任一家都能略微降价抢走全部需求，所以高于成本的相同价格不是稳定结果。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If both prices exceed cost, either firm can undercut the other slightly and capture all demand. Equal prices above cost are therefore not stable.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2. 纳什均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Nash Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Bertrand边际成本均衡|标准同质产品 Bertrand 模型的均衡]]是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Bertrand边际成本均衡|equilibrium of the standard homogeneous-goods Bertrand model]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p_1^*=p_2^*=c.
$$

理由：

- 若 $p_i>p_j$，厂商 $i$ 没有需求。
- 若 $p_i=p_j>c$，任一厂商都可略微降价并获得全部市场。
- 若 $p_i<c$，厂商亏损，愿意提高价格或退出。
- 只有 $p_i=p_j=c$ 时，单独偏离不能提高利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The reasoning is:

- if $p_i>p_j$, firm $i$ has no demand;
- if $p_i=p_j>c$, either firm can lower its price slightly and capture the entire market;
- if $p_i<c$, the firm makes a loss and would prefer to raise its price or exit;
- only when $p_i=p_j=c$ can no unilateral deviation raise profit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 这里通常不用一阶条件
> 同质产品 Bertrand 模型的需求是不连续的，不能直接套连续可导反应函数。可导反应函数通常来自差异化产品 Bertrand 模型。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Demand in the homogeneous-goods Bertrand model is discontinuous, so a smooth best-response calculation cannot be applied directly. Differentiable best-response functions generally arise in differentiated-product Bertrand models.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

产品差异、容量、成本、搜索或重复互动改变时，必须重新求解；各项机制怎样切断基准降价逻辑，见 [[Bertrand基准边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiation, capacity, costs, search, or repeated interaction require a new solution. See [[Bertrand基准边界|the boundary of the Bertrand benchmark]] for how each mechanism changes the undercutting argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 5. Hotelling 模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Hotelling Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.1. 基本信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basic Setup*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Hotelling价格竞争|Hotelling 模型]]用线性城市解释空间差异化下的价格竞争。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Hotelling价格竞争|Hotelling Model]] uses a linear city to explain price competition under spatial differentiation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 两家厂商位于线性城市两端。
- 消费者均匀分布在 $[0,1]$。
- 厂商选择价格 $p_i,p_j$。
- 单位生产成本为 $c$。
- 单位运输成本为 $t$。
- 每位消费者购买一单位的保留价值为 $v$；以下对称均衡使用 $v\ge c+2t$，使相关偏离下市场保持覆盖。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Two firms are located at opposite ends of a linear city.
- Consumers are uniformly distributed on $[0,1]$.
- The firms choose prices $p_i,p_j$.
- Unit production cost is $c$.
- Unit transport cost is $t$.
- Each consumer has unit reservation value $v$; the symmetric equilibrium below uses $v\ge c+2t$ so the market remains covered under relevant deviations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

消费者购买厂商 $i$ 产品的总成本为价格加交通成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The total cost to a consumer of buying from firm $i$ is the price plus the transport cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.2. 无差异消费者
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Indifferent Consumer*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设厂商 $i$ 位于 0 端，厂商 $j$ 位于 1 端。位置为 $z$ 的消费者对两家无差异时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let firm $i$ be located at 0 and firm $j$ at 1. A consumer at location $z$ is indifferent between them when
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p_i+tz=p_j+t(1-z).
$$

解得无差异消费者位置：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solving gives the location of the indifferent consumer:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat z=\frac{t+p_j-p_i}{2t}.
$$

若市场完全覆盖且 $\hat z\in[0,1]$，厂商 $i$ 的需求为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under market coverage, if $\hat z\in[0,1]$, demand for firm $i$ is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
q_i=\frac12+\frac{p_j-p_i}{2t}.
$$

这是[[Hotelling需求系统|内点需求分支]]。若 $\hat z<0$ 或 $\hat z>1$，一家企业会进入零需求或全市场角点；若保留价值不足，还可能有人不购买。后面的反应函数不能越过这些边界继续外推。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the [[Hotelling需求系统|interior demand branch]]. If $\hat z$ lies outside $[0,1]$, demand reaches a zero- or whole-market corner; insufficient reservation value can also leave the market uncovered. The response formula below cannot be extrapolated across those boundaries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.3. 最优反应函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Best-Response Function*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

厂商 $i$ 的利润为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Firm $i$'s profit is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\pi_i=(p_i-c)\left(\frac12+\frac{p_j-p_i}{2t}\right).
$$

对 $p_i$ 求导：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiating with respect to $p_i$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\partial \pi_i}{\partial p_i}
=
\frac{t+p_j-2p_i+c}{2t}=0.
$$

[[Hotelling反应函数|内点反应候选]]为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Hotelling反应函数|interior response candidate]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
B_i(p_j)=\frac{t+c+p_j}{2}.
$$

[[Hotelling均衡|对称均衡]]满足 $p_i=p_j=p^*$，因此：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[Hotelling均衡|symmetric equilibrium]] satisfies $p_i=p_j=p^*$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p^*=t+c.
$$

![[Pasted image 20240328215330.png]]

联立内点一阶条件还不够：必须把抢下全部市场和失去全部市场的利润与内点候选比较。在 $v\ge c+2t$ 的当前基准中，这项全局检查确认 $(c+t,c+t)$ 是 Nash 均衡，详见[[Hotelling均衡]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solving the interior first-order conditions is not sufficient by itself. Whole-market and zero-demand deviations must also be compared with the interior candidate. Under $v\ge c+2t$, that global check confirms $(c+t,c+t)$ as a Nash equilibrium; see [[Hotelling均衡|the Hotelling equilibrium]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.4. Hotelling 与 Cournot 的辨析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distinguishing Hotelling from Cournot*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Hotelling 和 Cournot 都可以用最优反应函数分析，但二者的 [[战略替代与互补|反应方向]]相反。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both Hotelling and Cournot competition can be analysed with best-response functions, but their [[战略替代与互补|best-response directions]] run in opposite directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 模型 | 竞争变量 | 对手提高策略变量时 | 反应函数斜率 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cournot | 产量 | 对手增产压低市场价格 | 向下倾斜 |
| Hotelling | 价格 | 对手提价会把消费者推向我方 | 向上倾斜 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Model | Competitive variable | When the rival raises its strategic variable | Best-response slope |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cournot | Output | The rival's extra output lowers the market price | Downward |
| Hotelling | Price | The rival's higher price shifts consumers towards me | Upward |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 不要说 Cournot 是 Hotelling 的 $t=0$ 特例
> $t=0$ 时产品无差异，Hotelling 的价格竞争会退化为接近 Bertrand 的同质品价格竞争，而不是 Cournot 产量竞争。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When $t=0$, the products are homogeneous and Hotelling price competition approaches homogeneous-goods Bertrand competition, not Cournot output competition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 6. 次模与超模博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Submodular and Supermodular Games*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6.1. 从收益差异到反应方向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From Payoff Differences to Response Directions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[超模与次模博弈]]的原始条件不是“图上曲线朝哪边”，而是提高自己的策略所带来的增量收益，会怎样随对手策略改变。递增差异表示对手策略更高时，提高自身策略更有吸引力；递减差异表示这种吸引力变弱。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The primitive condition behind [[超模与次模博弈|supermodular and submodular games]] is not the direction of a plotted curve. It asks how the incremental payoff from raising one's own action changes with a rival's action. Increasing differences strengthen that incentive; decreasing differences weaken it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在二阶连续可微的标量模型中，交叉偏导给出快速检验：

$$
\frac{\partial^2u_i}{\partial s_i\partial s_j}\ge0
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\text{递增差异},
\qquad
\frac{\partial^2u_i}{\partial s_i\partial s_j}\le0
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\text{递减差异}.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For twice continuously differentiable scalar payoffs, the sign of the cross-partial is a quick test for increasing or decreasing differences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 收益结构 | 在适当有序策略空间与正则条件下的反应方向 | 本页例子 |
|---|---|---|
| 递减差异／次模方向 | 对手策略变量上升，本方最佳反应下降 | [[Cournot反应函数|Cournot 产量]] |
| 递增差异／超模方向 | 对手策略变量上升，本方最佳反应上升 | [[Hotelling反应函数|Hotelling 内点价格]] |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Payoff structure | Response direction under suitable ordered strategy spaces and regularity | Example in this note |
|---|---|---|
| Decreasing differences / submodular direction | A rival's higher action lowers one's best response | [[Cournot反应函数|Cournot quantity]] |
| Increasing differences / supermodular direction | A rival's higher action raises one's best response | [[Hotelling反应函数|interior Hotelling price]] |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 斜率是简单模型里的投影，不是完整定义
> 若最佳反应是集合、存在跳跃，或在不同区间改变方向，就要回到收益差异、策略集格结构和最优解存在条件。[[广告竞赛反应函数]]先升后降，正是不能用一条局部斜率给整个博弈贴标签的例子。仅讨论单值反应方向时，使用[[战略替代与互补]]即可。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A slope is only a projection in a simple single-valued model, not the full definition. Correspondence-valued, discontinuous, or nonmonotone responses require the payoff-difference and lattice conditions. [[广告竞赛反应函数|The advertising-contest response]] changes direction across regions and cannot be assigned one global label from a local slope.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 7. 广告博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Advertising Contest*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7.1. 模型与零投入规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Model and the Zero-Effort Rule*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本节的[[广告份额竞赛|广告博弈]]是两人线性 Tullock 彩票竞赛。两家厂商同时选择广告投入 $x,y\ge0$，所争夺的市场或奖品价值为 $v>0$。若总投入为正，投入份额决定成功概率；若双方都不投入，必须另设公平平局规则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This [[广告份额竞赛|advertising game]] is a two-player linear Tullock lottery contest. Firms choose nonnegative efforts simultaneously for a prize worth $v$. Positive effort shares determine success probabilities, while zero–zero requires a separate fair-tie rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p_1(x,y)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{x}{x+y}, & x+y>0,\\[2mm]
\dfrac12, & x=y=0,
\end{cases}
\qquad
\pi_1(x,y)=v\,p_1(x,y)-x.
$$

厂商 2 对称。原比例式在 $(0,0)$ 分母为零，所以零点规则不是可省略的记号细节；它会直接影响最佳反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Firm 2 is symmetric. The proportional expression is undefined at zero–zero, so the tie rule is substantive rather than cosmetic: it changes the best-response correspondence at the boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7.2. 完整最佳反应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Complete Best Response*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $y>0$ 时，厂商 1 最大化 $vx/(x+y)-x$。一阶与二阶条件为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y>0$, firm 1 maximizes $vx/(x+y)-x$. Its first- and second-order conditions are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\partial \pi_1}{\partial x}
=\frac{vy}{(x+y)^2}-1=0,
\qquad
\frac{\partial^2\pi_1}{\partial x^2}
=-\frac{2vy}{(x+y)^3}<0.
$$

内点候选为 $x=\sqrt{vy}-y$。加入非负边界，并单独处理 $y=0$，得到[[广告竞赛反应函数|完整反应对应]]

$$
BR_1(y)=
\begin{cases}
\varnothing, & y=0,\\[2mm]
\{\sqrt{vy}-y\}, & 0<y<v,\\[2mm]
\{0\}, & y\ge v.
\end{cases}
$$

公平平局规则下，$\pi_1(0,0)=v/2$；任何足够小的正投入都更好，但每个正投入又可被更小的正投入改进。因此 $BR_1(0)$ 是空集，不是不存在于实数中的“$0^+$”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The positive interior candidate is $\sqrt{vy}-y$. Nonnegativity truncates it at zero when $y\ge v$. Against $y=0$, arbitrarily small positive effort improves on the fair tie, but every positive effort can be reduced further; the supremum is not attained, so the best-response set is empty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20240325200723.png]]

> [!note] 图片只画正对手投入的分支
> 图中的平方根曲线帮助观察 $y>0$ 时的形状；它不能替代上面对 $y=0$ 的空集判断。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The plotted square-root branch helps visualize positive rival effort. It does not replace the separate empty-set analysis at $y=0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7.3. 为什么反应先升后降
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the Response First Rises and Then Falls*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在内点分支，

$$
\frac{d\,BR_1(y)}{dy}
=\frac{\sqrt v}{2\sqrt y}-1.
$$

当 $y<v/4$ 时，跟进对手能显著提高自身成功概率，最佳反应随 $y$ 上升；当 $v/4<y<v$ 时，继续追赶的成本占上风，最佳反应下降。于是这个博弈不能在整个策略域被草率归为一个全局[[超模与次模博弈|超模或次模博弈]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The interior response rises below $v/4$ and falls above it. Following a low rival effort has a large success-probability benefit, whereas chasing a high effort eventually costs more than it adds. The game therefore has no single global complement/substitute label across its entire action space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7.4. 唯一纯策略均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Unique Pure-Strategy Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均衡不能落在坐标轴上，因为任何玩家面对零投入都没有最佳反应。双方正投入时，两条一阶条件为

$$
(x+y)^2=vy,
\qquad
(x+y)^2=vx.
$$

两式先推出 $x=y$，再得到

$$
x^*=y^*=\frac v4.
$$

这不是靠“模型对称”直接猜出的答案，而是[[广告竞赛均衡|两条最佳反应的唯一正交点]]。每人均衡期望收益为 $v/4$，总投入为 $v/2$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An equilibrium cannot lie on an axis. With both efforts positive, the two first-order conditions force $x=y$ and then $x^*=y^*=v/4$. This is the unique positive intersection of the best responses, not a conclusion from symmetry alone. Each player earns $v/4$, and total effort is $v/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 8. 投资博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Investment Contest*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8.1. 把故事识别为全支付竞赛
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Recognizing the Story as an All-Pay Contest*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本节的投资博弈是一个两人、完全信息、等价值的[[全支付竞赛]]。双方同时选择 $b_i\ge0$，奖品价值为 $v>0$；最高投入者获胜，并列时平分获胜概率，但输家也支付自己的投入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This investment game is a two-player complete-information equal-value [[全支付竞赛|all-pay contest]]. Players choose nonnegative bids simultaneously for a prize worth $v$; the higher bid wins, ties are split, and losing bids are still paid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
u_i(b_i,b_j)=
\begin{cases}
v-b_i, & b_i>b_j,\\[1mm]
\dfrac v2-b_i, & b_i=b_j,\\[1mm]
-b_i, & b_i<b_j.
\end{cases}
$$

它与广告博弈都让每个人承担投入，却不是同一个模型：广告博弈按相对投入平滑地分配成功概率；全支付竞赛由最高投入者确定性获胜，收益在追平对手处跳跃。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both contests make every player pay, but they allocate success differently. The advertising contest varies winning probability smoothly with relative effort; the all-pay contest awards the prize deterministically to the highest bidder and creates a payoff jump at the rival's bid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8.2. 为什么没有纯策略均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why There Is No Pure-Strategy Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[全支付竞赛无纯均衡|完整证明]]只需穷尽两类投入组合：

1. **若 $b_1=b_2=b$：** 每人收益为 $v/2-b$。任一方把投入提高任意 $0<\varepsilon<v/2$，即可把收益提高到 $v-b-\varepsilon$，所以并列不稳定。
2. **若 $b_1>b_2$：** 若 $b_2>0$，输家降到零可把收益从 $-b_2$ 提高到零；若 $b_2=0$，赢家可把投入降到任意 $b_1'\in(0,b_1)$，仍然获胜并节省成本。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**1.** At a tie, either player can overbid by less than $v/2$ and replace a half chance of winning with certainty.<br>
**2.** With unequal bids, a positive losing bid is profitably dropped to zero; if the losing bid is already zero, the winner can lower its positive bid while still winning.<br>
These cases exhaust all pure profiles, so none is a Nash equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此不是“通常看起来没有纯均衡”，而是在当前基准中确实不存在纯策略 Nash。虽然本节为了继续求出简单的均匀混合均衡而设双方价值都为 $v$，上面的无纯均衡证明只需要双方价值各自严格为正；价值不对称本身不会让纯均衡回来。离散投入、零价值玩家、预算约束、非线性成本或其他分配规则则需要重新分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The absence of a pure equilibrium is exact, not a claim that one is merely unusual. Although this section keeps equal values to obtain the simple uniform mixed equilibrium next, the deviation proof only requires both valuations to be positive; asymmetry alone does not restore a pure equilibrium. Discrete bids, zero valuations, budgets, nonlinear costs, or other allocation rules require a new analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8.3. 唯一混合均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Unique Mixed Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有纯均衡并不等于没有均衡。[[全支付竞赛混合均衡|唯一混合均衡]]中，双方独立地在 $[0,v]$ 上均匀随机化：

$$
F(b)=\frac b v,\qquad 0\le b\le v.
$$

若对手采用该分布，自己固定选择任意 $b\in[0,v]$ 的期望收益为

$$
vF(b)-b
=v\frac b v-b
=0.
$$

所有支持内投入都同样最优；$b>v$ 即使确定获胜也给出 $v-b<0$，所以支持外没有有利偏离。每人期望投入为 $v/2$，总期望投入为 $v$，每人均衡期望收益为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the unique mixed equilibrium, each player independently draws uniformly from $[0,v]$. Every supported bid earns $vF(b)-b=0$, and a bid above $v$ earns a negative payoff even if it wins for sure. Each player bids $v/2$ on average, total expected expenditure equals the prize value, and each expected payoff is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
