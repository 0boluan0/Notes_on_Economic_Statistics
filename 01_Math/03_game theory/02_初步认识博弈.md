## 1.1. 为什么要区分表达方式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why Distinguish Between Representations?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个博弈可以用不同方式表达。最常见的是策略式表达和扩展式表达。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same game can be represented in different ways. The two most common are the strategic form and the extensive form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 选择表达方式的标准
> 如果问题强调“谁有哪些选择、每种组合给多少收益”，优先用策略式表达；如果问题强调“谁先行动、谁看见了什么”，优先用扩展式表达。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use the strategic form when the problem emphasizes each player's available choices and the payoff from each combination. Use the extensive form when it emphasizes who moves first and what each player observes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 策略式表达
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strategic-Form Representation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[策略式博弈]]（strategic-form game）通常用三要素描述：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[策略式博弈|strategic-form game]] is usually described by three components:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
G=\langle N,\{S_i\}_{i\in N},\{u_i\}_{i\in N}\rangle.
$$

| 要素 | 记号 | 含义 |
|---|---|---|
| 玩家 | $N$ | 参与决策的人或组织 |
| 策略集合 | $S_i$ | 玩家 $i$ 可选的完整方案 |
| 收益函数 | $u_i$ | 每个策略组合下玩家 $i$ 的收益 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Component | Notation | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Players | $N$ | The people or organisations making decisions |
| Strategy set | $S_i$ | The complete plans available to player $i$ |
| Payoff function | $u_i$ | Player $i$'s payoff under each strategy profile |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.2.1. 玩家
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Players*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[博弈模型|玩家]]（players）是参与博弈并作出选择的主体。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[博弈模型|Players]] are the decision-making participants in a game.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 玩家集合记作 $N$。
- 某个具体玩家记作 $i$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The set of players is denoted by $N$.
- A particular player is denoted by $i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.2.2. 策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[行动与策略|策略]]（strategies）是玩家在博弈中可以选择的完整行动方案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[行动与策略|Strategies]] are the complete plans of action available to a player in a game.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 对每个玩家 $i$，可选策略集合记作 $S_i$。
- 策略组合（strategy profile）是所有玩家策略的组合，常记作 $s=(s_i,s_{-i})$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- For each player $i$, the available strategy set is denoted by $S_i$.
- A strategy profile combines the strategies of all players and is often written as $s=(s_i,s_{-i})$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.2.3. 收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Payoffs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[博弈模型|收益]]（payoffs）描述给定策略组合后，各玩家得到的效用或偏好排序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[博弈模型|Payoffs]] describe each player's utility or preference ranking under a given strategy profile.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] 效用排序示例
> 商贩最低可接受价格为 40，顾客最高可接受价格为 50。
>
> - 对商贩：成交价格越高，收益排序越高；不成交通常最低。
> - 对顾客：成交价格越低，收益排序越高；不成交通常最低。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A seller's minimum acceptable price is 40, while a customer's maximum acceptable price is 50.
>
> - For the seller, a higher transaction price receives a higher payoff ranking; no transaction is usually worst.
> - For the customer, a lower transaction price receives a higher payoff ranking; no transaction is usually worst.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 不要跨玩家比较收益数字
> 同一个格子里的两个收益数分别属于不同玩家。通常只能比较同一玩家在不同策略组合下的收益。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The two payoff numbers in a cell belong to different players. In general, compare only one player's payoffs across different strategy profiles.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3. 扩展式表达
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Extensive-Form Representation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[扩展式博弈]]（extensive-form game）强调行动顺序、信息结构和历史路径，常用博弈树呈现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[扩展式博弈|extensive-form game]] emphasizes the order of actions, the information structure, and the history of play, usually through a game tree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

扩展式表达适合处理：

- 谁先行动、谁后行动。
- 后行动者能否观察到前面的行动。
- 某些[[可信威胁|威胁是否可信]]。
- 自然状态或类型信息是否会影响行动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The extensive form is useful for analysing:

- who moves first and who moves later;
- whether a later mover observes earlier actions;
- whether a [[可信威胁|threat is credible]];
- whether states of nature or type information affect actions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.4. 两种表达方式的关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Relationship Between the Two Representations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

策略式表达和扩展式表达不是互相替代，而是各有用途。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The strategic and extensive forms are not substitutes; each is useful for a different kind of question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个动态互动可以先用扩展式写清时序与信息，再通过每名玩家的完整策略转成策略式；这种转换会保留偏离后的收益比较，却会压缩掉树上的时序与观察结构。具体转换见[[扩展式转策略式]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same dynamic interaction can first be written in extensive form and then converted through each player's complete contingent plans. The resulting strategic form preserves payoff comparisons across complete deviations but compresses the timing and observation structure; see [[扩展式转策略式|converting extensive form to strategic form]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 问题特征 | 更适合的表达 |
|---|---|
| 同时行动、收益矩阵、静态选择 | 策略式表达 |
| 顺序行动、博弈树、历史路径 | 扩展式表达 |
| 要找纯策略或混合策略纳什均衡 | 策略式表达 |
| 要判断[[可信威胁]]或[[子博弈判定|子博弈]] | 扩展式表达 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Feature of the problem | More suitable representation |
|---|---|
| Simultaneous moves, payoff matrices, static choices | Strategic form |
| Sequential moves, game trees, histories | Extensive form |
| Finding pure- or mixed-strategy Nash equilibria | Strategic form |
| Assessing [[可信威胁|credible threats]] or [[子博弈判定|subgames]] | Extensive form |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
