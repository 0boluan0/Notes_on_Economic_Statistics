## 1.1. 博弈论研究什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What Does Game Theory Study?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[策略式博弈.canvas|博弈论]]（Game Theory）研究理性参与者在相互影响中的决策问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[策略式博弈.canvas|Game Theory]] studies decision-making among rational participants whose choices affect one another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

核心不是单独问“我怎么选”，而是问：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The central question is not simply “What should I choose?” but rather:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 博弈论的核心视角
> 我的选择会改变别人怎么选；别人怎么选又会反过来改变我的收益。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> My choice changes what others choose, and their choices in turn change my payoff.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，博弈论适合处理“一个人的最优选择依赖于别人选择”的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Game theory is therefore suited to problems in which one person's best choice depends on the choices of others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 一个市场案例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Market Example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在市场经济中，卖方常见的竞争环境可以分为三类：完全竞争、寡头和垄断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a market economy, sellers commonly operate under one of three competitive structures: perfect competition, oligopoly, or monopoly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] 读这一章时先抓住一个问题
> 市场结构越接近寡头，厂商之间的策略互动越明显，也就越适合用博弈论分析。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The closer a market is to an oligopoly, the more pronounced the strategic interaction among firms becomes, and the more useful game theory is for analysing it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3. 完全竞争
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Perfect Competition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完全竞争（perfect competition）中，单个卖方是价格接受者（price taker）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under perfect competition, each individual seller is a price taker.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这意味着单个厂商的产量或定价决策几乎不影响市场价格，所以战略互动较弱。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An individual firm's output or pricing decision has almost no effect on the market price, so strategic interaction is limited.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 市场特征 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| 厂商数量很多 | 单个厂商影响很小 |
| 产品高度同质 | 消费者容易替代 |
| 厂商接受市场价格 | 个体没有明显定价权 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Market feature | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Many firms | Each individual firm has little influence |
| Highly homogeneous products | Consumers can readily switch between sellers |
| Firms accept the market price | No individual firm has meaningful pricing power |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.4. 寡头
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Oligopoly*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

寡头（oligopoly）市场中厂商数量有限，任何一家厂商的行为都可能直接影响其他厂商的收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An oligopoly contains only a small number of firms, so the actions of any one firm may directly affect the payoffs of the others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] 两类典型寡头竞争
> - [[Cournot竞争|古诺竞争]]：厂商围绕产量（quantity）决策。
> - [[Bertrand竞争|伯特兰竞争]]：厂商围绕价格（price）决策。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> - In [[Cournot竞争|Cournot Competition]], firms choose quantities.
> - In [[Bertrand竞争|Bertrand Competition]], firms choose prices.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

寡头是博弈论最典型的市场应用场景，因为每个厂商都必须预测竞争对手的反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Oligopoly is a classic application of game theory because every firm must anticipate how its competitors will respond.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下一步不要先背模型名称，而要进入[[04_案例（囚徒困境与纳什均衡）#2. 静态寡头竞争|课程案例]]，根据企业控制的是产量、价格、相对投入还是最高投入，以及行动时序与产品差异，选择准确模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The next step is not to memorize model names. Use the [[04_案例（囚徒困境与纳什均衡）#2. 静态寡头竞争|course cases]] to identify whether firms control quantities, prices, relative effort, or highest effort, together with timing and product differentiation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.5. 垄断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Monopoly*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

垄断（monopoly）看似只有一家厂商，但并不意味着没有策略互动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A monopoly may contain only one firm, but that does not mean strategic interaction disappears.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

垄断者与消费者之间仍可能存在动态博弈，例如：

- 京东 30 天价保。
- 电子产品的动态降价。
- 消费者预期未来降价后选择等待。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dynamic games may still arise between a monopolist and consumers. Examples include:

- a thirty-day price-protection policy offered by an online retailer;
- dynamic price reductions for electronic products;
- consumers choosing to wait because they expect future price cuts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 垄断不等于没有博弈
> 只要一方的当前选择会改变另一方的未来选择，就仍然可以出现博弈论问题。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A game-theoretic problem can arise whenever one party's current choice changes another party's future choice.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
