# EC400 MathsCamp

课程阅读按 SOFP、DPDE、PSI 三个单元各自的讲课顺序展开。正式课堂笔记保留完整讲解和例题；这里串起主干，并把本段所需的知识原子嵌入。跨课程共用同一个原子，回看时可以沿原子的来源进入另一门课的例子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read SOFP, DPDE, and PSI in the lecture order of each unit. Permanent course notes retain full explanations and examples; this overview connects the backbone and embeds the exact shared atoms needed at each stage. Sources on each atom lead back to applications in other courses.

## SOFP Lecture 1：从二次变化到函数形状

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*SOFP Lecture 1: from quadratic changes to function shape*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完整笔记：[[01_SOFP Lecture 1 - 二次型、Taylor 展开与凹凸性]]。

先把平方项与交叉项装进二次型。每个方向的二次型符号，比矩阵元素的正负更能说明曲率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin by collecting squares and cross terms in a quadratic form. Its sign across directions describes curvature more meaningfully than signs of individual matrix entries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着用行列式和主子式把“所有方向”变成有限个可计算条件。顺序主子式是所有主子式中特定的一串；定与半定不能用同一串弱不等号机械替换。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Determinants and principal minors turn the all-direction sign question into finitely many computable conditions. Leading minors form one particular sequence, and the strict and semidefinite tests require different collections.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Taylor 部分把一般函数接回二次型。先由偏导与链式法则得到方向变化，再由 Hessian 组合二阶信息；驻点处消失的是一阶项，后续判断仍需要曲率与余项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taylor expansion connects a general function back to quadratic forms. Partial derivatives and the chain rule give directional change, while the Hessian combines second-order information. At a stationary point only the linear term vanishes; classification still needs curvature and a remainder bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后把局部判断扩展到全域。凸集保证线段可行，凹性控制弦和切平面；拟凹性改为检查所有达到同一门槛的点是否构成凸集。两种性质的强弱决定哪些最优性结论可以使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last part moves from local tests to the whole domain. Convex sets keep segments feasible, concavity controls chords and tangent planes, and quasiconcavity instead asks whether every common-threshold region is convex. Their different strengths determine which optimality conclusions are available.

### PSI Lecture 2：分布怎样表达不确定性

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*PSI Lecture 2: representing uncertainty with distributions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[04_PSI Lecture 2 - 随机变量、条件分布与独立性|第二讲正式笔记]]从上一讲的概率空间接着往下走：合法事件的概率怎样变成数值变量的分布，分布又怎样产生均值、方差和分位数。完整推导、原例和符号解释保留在正式笔记中。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[04_PSI Lecture 2 - 随机变量、条件分布与独立性|Lecture 2]] moves from measurable events to numerical distributions, then to moments and thresholds. The course note retains the complete derivations and examples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把事件映到数轴。CDF 统一表示分布，PDF 只适用于具有密度的情形；并界用于尚不清楚依赖关系时控制事件概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First map outcomes to numbers. CDFs represent all real-valued laws; densities apply to the appropriate subset. The union bound controls event probabilities without dependence assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着按概率权重计算。LOTUS 允许直接平均变换后的值；中心化与幂次则形成不同的矩。分位数另从累计概率反查门槛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next use probability weights. LOTUS averages transformed values, moments centre and raise them to powers, and quantiles invert cumulative thresholds.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Uniform and normal laws provide basic models; mixed laws retain atoms. Transformations start from events, with scale correction and endpoint care.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One joint table supports marginalisation or normalised conditional slices. Bayes reverses the conditioning direction through two factorizations of the same cell.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conditional laws give within-group means and variances. Total expectation and total variance recover the population and connect to prediction and error decomposition.

### 2. 行列式、特征值与线性动力系统
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Determinants, Eigenvalues, and Linear Dynamical Systems*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线性方程组建立向量空间和秩；特征值进一步描述线性系统在反复作用下的方向与稳定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Linear systems establish vector spaces and rank. Eigenvalues then describe the directions and stability of a linear system under repeated application.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[行列式]]

行列式先判断线性变换是否压扁空间；把同一判断用于 $A-\lambda I$，便得到特征值，并由此进入对角化与动力系统。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The determinant first detects whether a linear transformation collapses space. Applying the same test to $A-\lambda I$ identifies eigenvalues and opens the route to diagonalisation and linear dynamics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[特征值、对角化与线性动力系统.canvas|特征值与动力系统主题地图]]

### 3. 多元微分与约束优化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Multivariate Differentiation and Constrained Optimisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线性结构处理一阶近似；多元微分和 Hessian 把分析扩展到非线性目标与约束优化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Linear structure handles first-order approximation. Multivariate differentiation and the Hessian extend the analysis to nonlinear objectives and constrained optimisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[全微分]]

[[多元微分.canvas|多元微分主题地图]]

梯度、Hessian 与隐函数先描述多变量系统的局部变化；优化理论再用这些工具寻找候选解、处理约束并判断比较静态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gradients, Hessians, and implicit functions first describe local change in multivariable systems. Optimisation then uses those tools to find candidates, handle constraints, and derive comparative statics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[无约束一阶条件]]
