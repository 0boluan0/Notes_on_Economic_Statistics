## SOFP Lecture 1：从二次变化到函数形状

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*SOFP Lecture 1: from quadratic changes to function shape*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完整笔记：[[01_SOFP Lecture 1 - 二次型、Taylor 展开与凹凸性]]。

先把平方项与交叉项装进二次型。每个方向的二次型符号，比矩阵元素的正负更能说明曲率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin by collecting squares and cross terms in a quadratic form. Its sign across directions describes curvature more meaningfully than signs of individual matrix entries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The next two entries separate the scalar quadratic function from the array used to represent it. In fixed coordinates, symmetrization preserves that function and gives a unique symmetric representative; it need not preserve the original linear map.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面两张卡把二次标量函数与记录它的矩阵区分开。固定坐标下，对称化保持该函数并给出唯一对称代表，但不一定保持原来的线性映射。

![[00_Knowledge/二次型]]

![[00_Knowledge/二次型对称化]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For symmetric real or Hermitian complex matrices, sign is tested on all vector directions. Positive definite means strictly positive on nonzero vectors; positive semidefinite allows zero and includes the definite case. Entrywise signs are a different question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵的定号，要检验所有向量方向。正定要求非零向量处严格正；半正定允许零，也包含正定情形。元素逐项的正负是另一回事。

![[00_Knowledge/正定矩阵]]

![[00_Knowledge/半正定矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To reverse a strict sign test, negate the matrix: a real symmetric $A$ is negative definite exactly when $-A$ is positive definite. For the pure quadratic function $x^TAx$, strict negativity away from zero makes the origin its unique global maximum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把矩阵取负号，就把严格正性检验反向：实对称 $A$ 负定，当且仅当 $-A$ 正定。对纯二次函数 $x^TAx$，非零处严格负使原点成为唯一全局最大点。

![[00_Knowledge/负定矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replacing strict negativity by nonpositivity gives negative semidefiniteness. It includes the negative-definite case and the zero matrix. If a nonzero zero-value direction exists, the pure quadratic function has more than one global maximizer; this is not automatically a conclusion about a general nonlinear function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把严格负放宽为非正，就得到半负定，其中包括负定情形与零矩阵。若存在非零零值方向，纯二次函数便有不止一个全局最大点；不能把这个结论直接套到一般非线性函数。

![[00_Knowledge/半负定矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A different outcome requires two actual witnesses: one input gives a positive value and another a negative value. Such a form is indefinite. A zero quadratic value alone proves neither sign change nor singularity, and testing only coordinate directions may miss both signs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一种结论需要两个实际见证：某个输入取正值，另一个输入取负值，这时二次型才是不定的。单个零二次值既不能证明变号，也不能证明奇异；只检验坐标方向还可能漏掉正负两种取值。

![[00_Knowledge/不定二次型]]

接着用行列式和主子式把“所有方向”变成有限个可计算条件。顺序主子式是所有主子式中特定的一串；定与半定不能用同一串弱不等号机械替换。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Determinants and principal minors turn the all-direction sign question into finitely many computable conditions. Leading minors form one particular sequence, and the strict and semidefinite tests require different collections.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[00_Knowledge/行列式]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A determinant can be taken from a smaller square block. Retaining the same nonempty row and column index set gives a principal submatrix; its determinant is a [[主子式|principal minor]]. The block is a matrix and the minor is a number, even when the retained indices are not consecutive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行列式也能取自较小的方形块。保留相同的非空行列下标集，就得到主子矩阵；该块的行列式才是[[主子式]]。方块是矩阵，主子式是数，下标也不必连续。

![[00_Knowledge/主子式]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now restrict that choice to the first $k$ indices in the current order. These nested top-left blocks give one leading principal minor per order. Reordering coordinates can change this particular sequence, even though it only relabels the full collection of principal minors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把选择限制为当前顺序下的最前 $k$ 个下标。逐层扩大的左上角方块，每阶给出一个顺序主子式。重新排列坐标可能改变这条特定序列，却只是重新标记全部主子式的集合。

![[00_Knowledge/顺序主子式]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real symmetric matrices, Sylvester’s theorem turns strict positivity of all leading principal minors into a complete positive-definiteness test; it also holds for complex Hermitian matrices. The determinant of the whole matrix is only the last test. Weak nonnegative signs require the different all-principal-minor criterion below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实对称矩阵，Sylvester 定理把全部顺序主子式严格为正变成完整的正定判据；复 Hermitian 矩阵也适用。整个矩阵的行列式只是最后一项。非严格的非负符号要使用下文另外的全部主子式判据。

![[00_Knowledge/Sylvester 正定判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Negating a real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrix turns negative definiteness into positive definiteness. A block of order $k$ contributes $k$ factors of $-1$ to its determinant. Applying Sylvester to the negated matrix therefore gives strict alternating signs through every leading order, not a check of only the final determinant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵取负，就把负定转成正定。一个 $k$ 阶方块的行列式会获得 $k$ 个 $-1$ 因子。因此，对取负后的矩阵使用 Sylvester，得到的是每一阶顺序主子式严格交替的符号，而非只检查最终行列式。

![[00_Knowledge/负定顺序主子式判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Allowing zero changes the principal-minor test: for a real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrix, every nonempty principal minor must be nonnegative, not only the leading ones. The full collection is sufficient as well as necessary. Zero determinants are allowed, while positive-definite matrices also pass.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

允许零以后，主子式判据的检查范围会改变：对实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵，每个非空主子式都须非负，不能只看顺序一串。完整集合既必要又充分；它允许行列式为零，也包含正定矩阵。

![[00_Knowledge/半正定主子式判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply that full nonnegative test to the negated matrix. For a real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrix, negative semidefiniteness is therefore equivalent to all odd-order principal minors being nonpositive and all even-order ones being nonnegative. Weak signs apply to the complete collection; merely weakening the leading-minor test would miss a possible positive direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对取负后的矩阵使用刚才完整的非负判据。于是，对实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵，半负定等价于全部奇数阶主子式非正、全部偶数阶主子式非负。弱符号用于完整集合；只放宽顺序主子式判据，会漏掉可能存在的正方向。

![[00_Knowledge/半负定主子式判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In two variables, completing the square changes coordinates to separate the mixed term. For $ax^2+2bxy+cy^2$ with $a\ne0$, the new coordinates are $u=x+(b/a)y$ and $v=y$, so the weights are $a$ and $(ac-b^2)/a$. Their signs classify the form because the change is invertible. They are generally not eigenvalues; if $a=0$, inspect the remaining terms without dividing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二维配方通过换坐标分离交叉项。对 $a\ne0$ 的 $ax^2+2bxy+cy^2$，取 $u=x+(b/a)y$、$v=y$，平方项权重就是 $a$ 与 $(ac-b^2)/a$。由于换元可逆，可用两权重的符号判断二次型；这些权重一般不是特征值。若 $a=0$，直接检查剩余项，不做除法。

![[00_Knowledge/二维二次型配方]]

Taylor 部分把一般函数接回二次型。先由偏导与链式法则得到方向变化，再由 Hessian 组合二阶信息；驻点处消失的是一阶项，后续判断仍需要曲率与余项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taylor expansion connects a general function back to quadratic forms. Partial derivatives and the chain rule give directional change, while the Hessian combines second-order information. At a stationary point only the linear term vanishes; classification still needs curvature and a remainder bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the dot-product formula uses full differentiability at the chosen point: one linear approximation controls all small input changes. Coordinate partials alone do not supply this hypothesis, and a direction vector need not have unit length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的点积公式以选定点处全可微为前提，即一个线性近似控制所有小输入变化。仅有坐标偏导不提供这个假设，方向向量也不必是单位向量。

![[00_Knowledge/方向导数梯度公式]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For second-order changes, hold the input velocity vector fixed and assume the scalar function is C² near the base point. The second parameter derivative along that line is the Hessian quadratic form. The vector need not have unit length, and doubling it multiplies this second-order response by four.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考察二阶变化时，先固定输入速度向量，并假设标量函数在基点附近为 C²。沿这条直线的二阶参数导数是 Hessian 二次型。向量不必是单位长度；把它放大两倍，二阶响应会变成四倍。

![[00_Knowledge/Hessian方向二阶导数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The constant-coefficient quadratic case makes the Hessian concrete. For fixed real $A$, differentiating $q(x)=x^TAx$ gives $H_q=A+A^T$; symmetry reduces this to $2A$. The conventional factor $\tfrac12$ cancels that two. Its Taylor formula is exact, whereas the general approximation that follows needs a remainder.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常系数二次型给出一个具体的 Hessian。固定实矩阵 $A$ 时，对 $q(x)=x^TAx$ 求导得到 $H_q=A+A^T$；对称时化为 $2A$。常见的前置系数 $\tfrac12$ 恰好抵消这个二。二次函数的 Taylor 公式精确成立，接下来的一般近似则需要余项。

![[00_Knowledge/二次型的Hessian]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second-order local model includes the constant value, gradient linear term and one half of the Hessian quadratic term. Under C² regularity, its remaining error is negligible relative to the squared input displacement. This approximation provides the basis for later classification, whose stationarity and sign conditions must still be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二阶局部模型包含原函数值、梯度线性项与 Hessian 二次项的一半。在 C² 条件下，剩余误差相对输入位移的平方可以忽略。这个近似为后续分类提供基础，但分类所需的驻点与正负性条件仍须分别检查。

![[00_Knowledge/多元Taylor近似]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second-order tests assume an interior stationary point and C² regularity nearby. A singular semidefinite Hessian leaves the classification incomplete because the remainder can matter in zero quadratic directions. Some exclusions remain: a nonzero positive-semidefinite Hessian cannot occur at a local maximum, with the opposite statement for negative semidefiniteness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二阶检验假设基点是内点驻点，且函数在附近为 C²。奇异半定 Hessian 的零二次方向可能由余项决定，因此分类尚未完成。不过仍有排除信息：非零半正定 Hessian 不可能出现在局部极大点；半负定有相反结论。

![[00_Knowledge/半定Hessian无结论]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last part moves from local tests to the whole domain. Convex sets keep segments feasible, concavity controls chords and tangent planes, and quasiconcavity instead asks whether every common-threshold region is convex. Their different strengths determine which optimality conclusions are available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后把局部判断扩展到全域。凸集保证线段可行，凹性控制弦和切平面；拟凹性改为检查所有达到同一门槛的点是否构成凸集。两种性质的强弱决定哪些最优性结论可以使用。

![[00_Knowledge/凸集]]

![[00_Knowledge/凹凸性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The shape definition comes first. Differentiability then turns concavity into a global tangent upper bound; second derivatives supply another equivalent test when their stated regularity holds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先理解形状定义；可微时，凹性可等价改写为切平面全域上界。满足相应正则条件时，二阶导数再提供另一种等价检验。

![[00_Knowledge/凹性一阶判据]]

![[00_Knowledge/Hessian凹凸性判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strengthening semidefiniteness to definiteness everywhere is sufficient for strict concavity. The reverse implication fails, as the quartic example shows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把处处半负定加强为处处负定，足以得到严格凹；四次函数反例说明反向推不回去。

![[00_Knowledge/严格凹与负定Hessian]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply the tangent bound at a stationary point to obtain a global maximum. Without differentiability, the convex-segment argument still turns any local maximum of a concave objective into a global one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将切平面上界用于驻点，便得到全局最大。即使不可微，凸线段论证仍能把凹目标的局部最大推广为全局最大。

![[00_Knowledge/凹函数驻点全局最优]]

![[00_Knowledge/凸优化全局性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are conditional optimality statements. Strict concavity excludes two different maximizers; a separate argument must establish that a maximizer is attained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些最优性结论都带条件。严格凹排除两个不同最大点，另一个证明才负责说明最大点确实取得。

![[00_Knowledge/严格凹不保证解存在]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, build concave objectives by adding finitely many concave terms with fixed nonnegative weights. An affine cost term preserves the shape of a profit objective.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后用有限个固定非负权重组合凹函数，构造新的凹目标；仿射成本项也能保留利润函数的凹性。

![[00_Knowledge/凹函数非负加权和]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The next definitions sort inputs by their output values. Fix the function and its domain, then distinguish an exact value, a minimum acceptable value, and a maximum allowed value. Equality belongs to all three comparisons at the threshold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接下来按输出值筛选输入。固定函数及定义域，分别区分恰好等于、至少达到、不超过某个值；门槛处的等号都包含在各自集合中。

![[00_Knowledge/水平集]]

![[00_Knowledge/上轮廓集]]

![[00_Knowledge/下轮廓集]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a convex domain, quasiconcavity requires every upper contour set to be convex; quasiconvexity requires every lower contour set to be convex. Both check every real threshold. After these definitions, concavity supplies a stronger sufficient condition for quasiconcavity. The product example shows why the reverse implication fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在凸定义域上，拟凹性要求全部上轮廓集凸，拟凸性要求全部下轮廓集凸；两者都覆盖所有实数门槛。先理解定义，再看较强的凹性如何保证拟凹性；乘积反例说明反向为何不成立。

![[00_Knowledge/拟凹函数]]

![[00_Knowledge/拟凸函数]]

![[00_Knowledge/凹性推出拟凹性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The economic interpretation begins with what the inputs and scores mean. A utility function ranks specified bundles of goods; an indifference curve collects bundles tied in that ranking. Read these definitions before treating mixtures or slopes as preference statements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经济解释先从输入与分数的含义开始：效用函数给确定的商品组合排序，无差异曲线收集排序中一样好的组合。理解这两个定义后，再把混合与斜率解释成偏好。

![[00_Knowledge/效用函数]]

![[00_Knowledge/无差异曲线]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For quasiconcave utility, a mixture is guaranteed to match or exceed the worse endpoint. With equally valued endpoints it is weakly preferred to both. To discuss a trade-off rate, fix one indifference curve and add the differentiability and nonzero-denominator conditions; diminishing MRS also needs the convex upper region and positive marginal utilities stated in its theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拟凹效用保证混合不差于较差端点；两端同效用时，便能保证同时不差于两端。讨论交换比率时，要固定同一无差异曲线，并加入可微性与分母非零条件；递减 MRS 还需要定理中给出的凸上区域和正边际效用。

![[00_Knowledge/拟凹效用与混合偏好]]

![[00_Knowledge/边际替代率]]

![[00_Knowledge/拟凹效用的递减MRS]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In one dimension, monotonicity on an interval already gives quasiconcavity and [[拟凸函数|quasiconvexity]]. A real-valued transformation that is nondecreasing on the original range also preserves quasiconcavity; strict increase preserves the ranking of the original values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一维区间上的单调性已足以得到拟凹性与[[拟凸函数|拟凸性]]。实值变换在原函数值域上非减，就保留拟凹性；严格递增还保留原值的完整排序。

![[00_Knowledge/一维单调函数的拟凹拟凸性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rise-then-fall shape on an interval is another sufficient condition for quasiconcavity. It controls the value at every intermediate point. The next two examples show why neither coordinatewise increase in several dimensions nor a unique maximum supplies that control.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区间上先升后降，也是拟凹性的充分形状条件，因为它控制每个中间点的值。后面两个例子说明，多维逐坐标递增或唯一最大点，都没有提供这样的控制。

![[00_Knowledge/一维单峰函数拟凹性]]

![[00_Knowledge/多元单调不推拟凹]]

![[00_Knowledge/唯一峰值不推拟凹]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After those dimensional limits, return to changing the numerical score while keeping the input domain fixed. A nondecreasing transformation preserves quasiconcavity; strict increase also preserves the original ranking and ties.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看清维度上的限制后，再回到定义域固定、只改变数值分数的问题。非减变换保持拟凹性，严格递增还保留原排序和相等关系。

![[00_Knowledge/单调变换保持拟凹性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The segment criteria turn those all-threshold definitions into inequalities for every pair of inputs and every mixing weight. Quasiconcavity compares with the smaller endpoint value; quasiconvexity compares with the larger one. Neither comparison is an endpoint average.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线段判据把“全部门槛”的定义化为任意两点、任意混合权重的不等式。拟凹性比较较小端点值，拟凸性比较较大端点值；两者的参照都不是端点平均值。

![[00_Knowledge/拟凹性线段判据]]

![[00_Knowledge/拟凸性线段判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, return to optimality. The cubic is monotone and has both quasi-properties, but its stationary point at zero has nearby values on both sides. The earlier concave stationary-point theorem therefore cannot be transferred by replacing concavity with quasiconcavity alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后回到最优性：三次函数单调且具有两种拟凹凸性质，零点虽是驻点，附近却同时有更大、更小的值。因此不能只把先前驻点定理中的凹性换成拟凹性。

![[00_Knowledge/拟凹驻点不保证最优]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
SOFP continues with unconstrained, equality-constrained, and inequality-constrained optimization in Lecture 2, then comparative statics and fixed points in Lecture 3. The first lecture supplies shared objects used in those later conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## PSI：从识别、概率到统计推断

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

### 3. 多元微分与约束优化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Multivariate Differentiation and Constrained Optimisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线性结构处理一阶近似；多元微分和 Hessian 把分析扩展到非线性目标与约束优化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Linear structure handles first-order approximation. Multivariate differentiation and the Hessian extend the analysis to nonlinear objectives and constrained optimisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A multivariable first-order model uses one linear map for every small displacement, with a remainder negligible relative to displacement length. A Jacobian represents that map under differentiability; checking partial derivatives separately is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Gradients, Hessians, and implicit functions first describe local change in multivariable systems. Optimisation then uses those tools to find candidates, handle constraints, and derive comparative statics.

### 4. 拓扑基础：开集、闭集与连续
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Topological Foundations: Open Sets, Closed Sets, and Continuity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

优化中的连续性、极值存在和邻域语言需要更一般的空间基础，因此最后补上开集、闭集和紧致性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity, existence of extrema, and neighbourhood language in optimisation require a more general foundation for spaces, so the course concludes with open sets, closed sets, and compactness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with a concrete meaning of distance: an open ball collects all points closer than a chosen radius. This is the local language used by the next definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先建立具体的距离含义：开球收集距离小于给定半径的全部点。下一张卡用这种局部语言定义开集。

![[度量空间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now ask whether each point of the set has a small ball that stays inside the set. The radius can depend on the point; testing one common radius would be a different requirement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着问：每个点是否各有一个留在集合内的小球？半径可以随点而变，不能改成全体点共用一个半径。

![[开集]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The open sets produced by a metric obey union and intersection rules. A topology takes those rules as the starting point, so the language can extend to spaces without a chosen distance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

度量产生的开集满足并、交规则。拓扑把这些规则当作起点，使这套语言也适用于没有指定距离的空间。

![[拓扑]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once openness is fixed, look at the complement in the same ambient space. Closedness means that this complement is open; it is not the logical opposite of openness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开性确定后，再看同一环境中的补集。补集开才叫闭，并不是把“开”直接取反。

![[闭集]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Combining closed conditions means taking intersections or unions. Complement identities explain why every intersection remains closed while only finite unions have a general guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

组合闭条件对应取交或取并。补集恒等式说明任意交为何仍闭，也说明并集为何只有有限情形才普遍保证闭。

![[闭集运算]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before judging a subset, fix the world in which it lives. For example, $[0,1/2)$ is open inside $[0,1]$ with its usual relative topology, although it is not open in the real line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断集合前先固定它所在的环境。例如 $[0,1/2)$ 在带通常相对拓扑的 $[0,1]$ 中开，在整条实数线上却不开。

![[子空间拓扑]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within that fixed world, collect the points with an open neighbourhood entirely inside the set. These points form the largest open subset, called the interior.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定环境后，收集那些周围有一个开邻域完全留在集合内的点。它们组成集合的最大开部分，即内部。

![[集合内部]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Instead ask which points cannot be separated from the set by an open neighbourhood. Adding these points produces the smallest closed superset, the closure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换一个问题：哪些点无法用开邻域与这个集合分隔？把这些点包含进来，便得到最小闭超集，即闭包。

![[集合闭包]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally compare the two sides: every open neighbourhood of a boundary point meets both the set and its complement. The point itself may belong to either side.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后同时看两侧：边界点的每个开邻域都碰到集合及其补集，而这个点自身可以属于任意一侧。

![[集合边界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before using sequences, distinguish numerical distance from the open sets it produces. Two metrics on the same set can define the same topology even when one is bounded and the other is not. Their convergent sequences and limits still agree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用序列之前，先区分数值距离与它产生的开集。同一集合上的两个度量可以定义同一拓扑，即使一个有界、另一个无界；它们的收敛序列及极限仍相同。

![[拓扑等价度量]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the ambient metric space and a candidate point in it. Convergence means that, for each positive tolerance, all sufficiently late terms are closer than that tolerance to the point. A repeatedly selected subsequence is insufficient, and the cutoff may depend on the tolerance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定环境度量空间及其中的候选点。给定任何正容许误差，充分靠后的每一项距候选点都必须小于该误差。只控制反复选中的子列还不够；门槛可以随容许误差改变。

![[序列收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once convergence is established, the metric axioms exclude two different limits. This separates uniqueness from existence: the theorem does not make every sequence converge.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

确定收敛后，度量公理排除两个不同极限。这把唯一性与存在性分开：定理不会使每条序列都收敛。

![[序列极限唯一]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A point whose every neighbourhood contains arbitrarily late terms is a sequence cluster point. The alternating sequence with values −1 and 1 has two such points and no full-sequence limit. Repeated visits count by their indices, not by the number of distinct values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个邻域都能在任意晚的时候再被访问，只说明序列聚点。取值为 −1 与1的交替序列有两个这样的点，却没有整列极限；重复访问按索引计数，不是按不同取值计数。

![[聚点不等于收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to the closure question using this precise meaning of convergence. In a metric space, a point lies in the closure of a set exactly when some sequence from that set converges to it. Terms may repeat, so a point already in the set has a constant witnessing sequence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用这个精确的收敛含义回到闭包问题。在度量空间中，一点属于集合闭包，当且仅当存在来自集合内并收敛到它的序列。项可以重复，因此集合已有的点可用常数序列作证。

![[闭包的序列判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Closedness then becomes a universal test: every sequence from the set that converges in the specified ambient metric space must retain its limit in the set. This does not require every sequence to converge, and a limit outside the ambient space is not a counterexample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

闭性由此成为全称检验：每条来自集合、并在指定环境度量空间中收敛的序列，都必须把极限保留在集合内。不要求每条序列都收敛；环境空间之外的极限也不能作为反例。

![[闭集的序列判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now move from approaching points to maps between spaces. Continuity pulls each open set of the codomain back to an open set of the domain. A preimage collects inputs producing the chosen outputs; it does not require the map to have an inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着从点的逼近转向空间之间的映射。连续性把陪域的每个开集拉回为定义域的开集。原像收集产生指定输出的输入，并不要求映射存在反函数。

![[连续性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For maps between metric spaces, four tests express the same continuity condition: $\varepsilon$–$\delta$ control, open-set preimages, closed-set preimages, and preservation of every convergent sequence and its limit. In arbitrary topology, preserving sequence limits alone need not imply continuity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于度量空间之间的映射，四种检验表达同一个连续性条件：$\varepsilon$–$\delta$ 控制、开集原像、闭集原像，以及保持每条收敛序列及其极限。在任意拓扑中，单凭保持序列极限未必能推出连续。

![[连续性的等价判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity leads to a global question: can every open cover be reduced to finitely many of its original sets? Compactness answers this in general topology, before any distance or coordinate criterion is used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续性之后，转向一个整体问题：每个开覆盖是否都能保留原族中的有限个集合？紧致性先在一般拓扑中回答它，不预先假定距离或坐标判据。

![[紧致性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a metric space, first isolate a weaker finite-cover idea. At every positive radius, finitely many balls cover a totally bounded set. One large bounding ball is insufficient, and total boundedness alone does not guarantee compactness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在度量空间中，先分出一个较弱的有限覆盖概念：每个正半径下都能用有限多个球覆盖，叫全有界。只有一个大球界住还不够；全有界本身也不足以保证紧致。

![[全有界性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now connect open covers with sequences. In a metric space, compactness means every sequence has a convergent subsequence with its limit still in the set. The full proof uses finite ball covers; in arbitrary topology the equivalence cannot be assumed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再把开覆盖与序列连接起来。度量空间中，紧致等价于每条序列都有极限仍在集内的收敛子列；完整证明使用有限球覆盖。在任意拓扑中不能直接套用这项等价。

![[紧致性的序列判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before the real-coordinate proofs, distinguish the tightest upper bound from a largest member. A supremum compares all upper bounds and can lie outside the set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

进入实坐标证明之前，先区分最紧的上界与最大的集合成员。上确界比较的是所有上界，本身可以不属于集合。

![[上确界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definition does not itself supply such a bound. Real completeness guarantees a real supremum for every nonempty set bounded above; this is the existence step used in the following interval and extrema arguments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义本身不负责提供这个界。实数完备性保证每个非空有上界集合都有实数上确界；后续区间与极值论证正是借助这项存在保证。

![[实数的确界性质]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With finitely many real coordinates and the usual Euclidean metric, compactness is equivalent to being closed and bounded. The coordinate argument explains why this reverse implication must not be transferred to arbitrary metric spaces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在有限个实坐标与通常欧氏度量下，紧致等价于闭且有界。坐标论证说明，为什么不能把这条逆向保证搬到任意度量空间。

![[Heine–Borel 定理的边界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to continuous maps: pulling an open cover of the image back to the domain transfers compactness to the image. It need not make the whole codomain compact, and the map need not be one-to-one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回到连续映射：把像集的开覆盖拉回定义域，就能把紧致性传给像集。结论不保证整个陪域紧致，也不要求映射一一对应。

![[连续像保持紧致]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonempty compact domain and a continuous real-valued function, the compact image contains its largest and smallest values. This proves attainment, not a unique optimizer or a method for finding one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若定义域非空且紧致、函数连续且实值，紧致像就包含自身最大值和最小值。这证明能够取得极值，不保证极值点唯一，也不是寻找它们的算法。

![[极值定理]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A second branch asks whether the space can be separated into two disjoint nonempty open pieces. For a subset, the relevant openness is relative to that subset; an arbitrary partition is not enough.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一条分支追问：空间能否分成两个不交、非空的开部分？对子集要使用相对开性，任意划成两份并不足以证明不连通。

![[连通性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A hypothetical separation of a continuous image would pull back to a separation of the domain. Hence a connected domain has connected image, even if distinct inputs are identified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续像若能分离，原像就会把定义域也分离。因此，连通定义域具有连通像，即使不同输入被映到同一点。

![[连续像保持连通]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the usual real line, nonempty connected subsets are precisely intervals, including singletons. A least-upper-bound argument proves the reverse direction. Together with preservation under continuous images, this explains why intermediate values are attained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在通常实线上，非空连通子集恰是区间，这里包含单点。反向结论由最小上界论证证明；结合连续像保持连通，就解释了中间值为什么能够取到。

![[实线连通集是区间]]
