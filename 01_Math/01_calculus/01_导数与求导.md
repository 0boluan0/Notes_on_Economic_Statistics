## 1. 导数回答的是局部问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. A derivative answers a local question*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $x_0$ 走到 $x_0+h$，函数的平均变化率是
$$
\frac{f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)}{h}.
$$
它是图像上两点之间的割线斜率。若在 $h\to0$ 时，这个比率从两侧趋于同一个有限数，就得到 [[导数]]
$$
f'(x_0)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)}h.
$$
瞬时速度、切线斜率和局部线性响应都指向这个同一极限。差商在 $h=0$ 时没有定义，所以求导不是“把 $h$ 代成零”，而是先在 $h\ne0$ 时化简，再问附近的值是否稳定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The quotient $[f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)]/h$ is an average rate and a secant slope. If it approaches one finite value from both sides as $h\to0$, that limit is the [[导数|derivative]]. Instantaneous velocity, tangent slope, and local-linear response are readings of this same limit. The quotient is undefined at $h=0$, so differentiation simplifies for nonzero increments before taking a limit; it does not substitute zero at the outset.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x)=x^2$，
$$
\frac{(x+h)^2-x^2}{h}=2x+h\longrightarrow2x.
$$
这不只得到一条公式。它还说，在很小的 $h$ 上，
$$
(x+h)^2-x^2=2xh+h^2
$$
的主要部分是 $2xh$，而 $h^2$ 相对 $h$ 更快消失。导数正是把一阶局部变化从更小的余项中分离出来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2$, the difference quotient is $2x+h$ and tends to $2x$. Equivalently, the increment $2xh+h^2$ has first-order part $2xh$, while $h^2$ vanishes faster relative to $h$. A derivative isolates this first-order local response from the smaller remainder.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存在函数值不等于可导。跳跃先破坏连续，尖角则可能连续却使左右斜率不同，竖直切线会产生无界差商。逻辑方向是 [[可导必连续|可导推出连续]]，逆方向由 $|x|$ 在零点的 [[连续不必可导|反例]]否定。先检查这一层，后面的规则才有对象可算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Having a function value does not imply differentiability. A jump breaks continuity, a corner can preserve continuity while giving different one-sided slopes, and a vertical tangent produces an unbounded quotient. The valid implication is [[可导必连续|differentiability implies continuity]]; $|x|$ at zero supplies the [[连续不必可导|counterexample to the converse]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 基础规则是差商证明的压缩
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Basic rules compress difference-quotient arguments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[导数线性法则]]先处理有限加法与常数倍：
$$
(af+bg)'=af'+bg'.
$$
它来自把差商逐项拆开。配合 [[整数幂求导]]，多项式便可逐项处理。对正整数 $n$，二项展开给出
$$
\frac{(x+h)^n-x^n}{h}
=nx^{n-1}+\binom n2x^{n-2}h+\cdots+h^{n-1},
$$
极限只留下 $nx^{n-1}$。负整数幂则借助倒数和商法则，并保留 $x\ne0$ 的定义域。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[导数线性法则|linearity rule]] handles finite sums and scalar multiples. Combined with the [[整数幂求导|integer power rule]], it differentiates polynomials term by term. For positive integer $n$, the binomial expansion of the difference quotient leaves only $nx^{n-1}$ in the limit. Negative integer powers follow through reciprocals, with zero retained as an excluded point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三角函数的公式还需要一个几何桥梁。单位圆的面积比较在**弧度制**下给出 [[正弦基本极限]]
$$
\lim_{h\to0}\frac{\sin h}{h}=1,
\qquad
\lim_{h\to0}\frac{1-\cos h}{h}=0.
$$
把和角公式放进差商，便得到 [[正弦余弦求导]]
$$
(\sin x)'=\cos x,
\qquad
(\cos x)'=-\sin x.
$$
若输入用度数，差商会多出 $\pi/180$；“弧度”是公式条件，不是排版习惯。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trigonometric derivatives need one geometric bridge. Unit-circle area bounds establish the [[正弦基本极限|fundamental sine limit]] in radians. Substituting the angle-addition formulas into the difference quotients then yields the [[正弦余弦求导|sine and cosine derivatives]]. Degree-valued inputs acquire an extra factor of $\pi/180$; radians are part of the theorem's conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 乘积、商与复合对应三种结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Products, quotients, and compositions are different structures*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对乘积，增量中有两个一阶来源。向分子加减同一项后，[[乘积法则]]成为
$$
(uv)'=u'v+uv'.
$$
它不是 $u'v'$：若长方形两边同时小幅变化，一阶面积增量来自两条边带；两个增量相乘的角落只是二阶小量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A product has two first-order sources of change. Adding and subtracting the same intermediate term in its difference quotient yields the [[乘积法则|product rule]] $(uv)'=u'v+uv'$. The product of both small increments is a second-order corner term, not the entire first-order change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 $v(1/v)=1$ 可先求出 $(1/v)'=-v'/v^2$，再得到 [[商法则]]
$$
\left(\frac uv\right)'=\frac{u'v-uv'}{v^2},\qquad v\ne0.
$$
分母非零是原函数的定义条件。任何代数化简都不能把原先排除的点偷偷放回定义域。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiate $v(1/v)=1$ to obtain the reciprocal derivative and then the [[商法则|quotient rule]]. The condition $v\ne0$ belongs to the original function's domain; algebraic simplification cannot silently restore excluded points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对复合 $f(g(x))$，[[链式法则]]给出
$$
(f\circ g)'(x)=f'(g(x))g'(x).
$$
例如
$$
\frac d{dx}(3x^2+1)^5
=5(3x^2+1)^4\cdot6x.
$$
外层导数读取“输出对中间量”的响应，内层导数读取“中间量对原输入”的响应；漏掉 $6x$ 就只完成了半条变化链。$dy/dx=(dy/du)(du/dx)$ 是好用的记忆方式，但严格证明来自局部线性余项，不是把符号当普通分数任意约掉。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a composition, the [[链式法则|chain rule]] multiplies the outer response at the inner value by the inner response. In $(3x^2+1)^5$, omitting $6x$ ignores how the intermediate quantity responds to the original input. The differential-fraction notation is a mnemonic; the proof composes local-linear remainders rather than cancelling ordinary fractions unconditionally.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 再求一次导数，就进入新的变化层次
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Differentiating again creates a new level of change*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[高阶导数]]把导函数继续求导。位置 $s(t)$ 的一阶导数是速度，二阶导数是加速度；一般函数的二阶导数则描述斜率如何变化。记号
$$
f^{(n)}(x)=D^nf(x)=\frac{d^nf}{dx^n}
$$
表示第 $n$ 阶导数，不是 $f(x)$ 的 $n$ 次幂。存在 $f'$ 并不自动保证存在 $f''$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[高阶导数|Higher derivatives]] differentiate the derivative function again. For position, the first two derivatives are velocity and acceleration; for a general function, the second derivative tracks the change in slope. The notation $f^{(n)}$ is an order, not a power, and existence at one order does not guarantee the next.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反复使用乘积法则会产生二项式结构。[[高阶乘积法则]]写成
$$
(uv)^{(n)}=\sum_{k=0}^n\binom nk u^{(k)}v^{(n-k)}.
$$
每次求导都选择落在左因子或右因子，$\binom nk$ 正是在 $n$ 次选择中让 $k$ 次落在左边的路径数。例如 $D^5(xe^x)=xe^x+5e^x$，因为 $x$ 的二阶及以上导数全为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated product differentiation creates a binomial pattern. The [[高阶乘积法则|higher-order product rule]] counts the paths that place $k$ of the $n$ derivatives on the left factor. For $xe^x$, only the zero- and first-derivative terms of $x$ survive, giving $D^5(xe^x)=xe^x+5e^x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 隐式关系和反函数仍由链式法则统领
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Implicit relations and inverse functions still follow the chain rule*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有些曲线天然以关系 $F(x,y)=0$ 给出，而不是先解成 $y=f(x)$。[[隐式求导]]沿一条已有局部解支把 $y$ 看成 $y(x)$。对圆
$$
x^2+y^2=25
$$
求导得 $2x+2yy'=0$，所以在 $y\ne0$ 的点
$$
y'=-\frac xy.
$$
在 $(3,4)$ 处斜率是 $-3/4$；在 $(\pm5,0)$，分母为零正对应竖直切线。一般公式及其局部存在条件由共享原子 [[隐函数导数公式]] 承担；$F_y=0$ 只说明这个公式失效，不单独证明解支不存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some curves are naturally given by $F(x,y)=0$. [[隐式求导|Implicit differentiation]] follows an existing local branch $y(x)$. The circle gives $2x+2yy'=0$ and hence $y'=-x/y$ where $y\ne0$. The shared [[隐函数导数公式|implicit-function derivative formula]] carries the general statement and existence conditions; $F_y=0$ makes that formula unavailable but does not by itself prove that no branch exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 在 $x_0$ 附近连续可微且 $f'(x_0)\ne0$，它有局部反函数。对恒等式 $f(f^{-1}(y))=y$ 使用链式法则，[[反函数导数]]给出
$$
(f^{-1})'(y)=\frac1{f'(f^{-1}(y))}.
$$
函数与反函数图像关于 $y=x$ 对称，所以对应切线斜率互为倒数。这个公式还产生 [[反三角函数求导|反正弦、反余弦和反正切导数]]；主值分支决定平方根符号与定义域。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f$ is continuously differentiable near $x_0$ and $f'(x_0)\ne0$, it has a local inverse. Applying the chain rule to $f(f^{-1}(y))=y$ yields the [[反函数导数|inverse derivative formula]]. Reflection across $y=x$ explains the reciprocal slopes. The same theorem generates the [[反三角函数求导|inverse-trigonometric derivatives]], with principal branches fixing signs and domains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 指数与对数是一对反函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Exponentials and logarithms are inverse functions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[指数函数]]固定正底数 $a$，把 $a^x$ 连续定义到实指数并保持 $a^{x+h}=a^xa^h$。于是
$$
\frac d{dx}a^x
=a^x\lim_{h\to0}\frac{a^h-1}{h}
=M(a)a^x.
$$
底数只通过常数 $M(a)$ 影响瞬时比例变化率。选择唯一使 $M(e)=1$ 的底数，得到 [[自然指数函数]] $e^x$，它满足 $(e^x)'=e^x$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[指数函数|exponential function]] fixes a positive base and preserves the exponent addition law. Its difference quotient factors into $a^x$ times a base-dependent constant $M(a)$. Choosing the unique base for which $M(e)=1$ gives the [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]], whose derivative equals itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[自然对数]]是 $e^x$ 从 $(0,\infty)$ 到 $\mathbb R$ 的反函数：
$$
y=\ln x\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad e^y=x.
$$
由反函数导数，$(\ln x)'=1/x$。又因为 $a^x=e^{x\ln a}$，[[指数函数求导]]和 [[对数函数求导]]统一为
$$
\frac d{dx}a^x=(\ln a)a^x,\qquad
\frac d{dx}\log_a x=\frac1{x\ln a}.
$$
前者要求 $a>0$；后者还要求 $a\ne1$ 且 $x>0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] is the inverse of $e^x$ from positive reals to real numbers, so the inverse derivative gives $(\ln x)'=1/x$. Rewriting $a^x=e^{x\ln a}$ produces the general [[指数函数求导|exponential derivative]], while change of base gives the [[对数函数求导|logarithmic derivative]]. Their base and input restrictions are part of the formulas.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当底数和指数都在动时，先取对数。对 $x>0$，[[对数求导]]处理
$$
y=x^x,\qquad
\ln y=x\ln x,\qquad
y'=x^x(1+\ln x).
$$
同样，固定实数 $r$ 时以 $x^r=e^{r\ln x}$ 定义正半轴上的实数幂，可得 [[实数幂求导]] $d(x^r)/dx=rx^{r-1}$。这条统一公式不应抹掉某个具体幂函数原有的更大或更小定义域。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When both base and exponent move, logarithms separate them. The [[对数求导|logarithmic-differentiation]] example $x^x$ yields $x^x(1+\ln x)$. For fixed real $r$, defining $x^r=e^{r\ln x}$ on $x>0$ gives the [[实数幂求导|real-power rule]], while particular exponents may have different real domains that must still be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对数也把移动指数极限化成差商。令 $h=1/n$，
$$
\ln\left(1+\frac1n\right)^n
=\frac{\ln(1+h)-\ln1}{h}\to1,
$$
所以 [[自然常数极限]]给出 $(1+1/n)^n\to e$。这里知识顺序很重要：本路径先按指数斜率定义 $e$，再求对数导数，最后证明极限，避免把同一结论既当定义又当证明前提。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Logarithms also turn a moving-exponent limit into the difference quotient of $\ln$ at one. This proves the [[自然常数极限|limit $(1+1/n)^n\to e$]]. The present order first defines $e$ by exponential slope, then derives the logarithmic derivative, and only afterwards proves the limit, avoiding circularity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 双曲函数把指数增长和衰减配成一对
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Hyperbolic functions pair exponential growth and decay*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[双曲函数]]定义
$$
\sinh x=\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}2,
\qquad
\cosh x=\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}2,
$$
并满足 $\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x=1$。名称来自双曲线 $u^2-v^2=1$，不是因为它们是普通三角函数的反函数。逐项使用指数与链式法则，[[双曲函数求导]]得到
$$
(\sinh x)'=\cosh x,\qquad
(\cosh x)'=\sinh x.
$$
第二条没有普通余弦导数中的负号，定义本身就解释了差别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[双曲函数|hyperbolic functions]] are the odd and even combinations of $e^x$ and $e^{-x}$ and satisfy a hyperbolic rather than circular identity. Differentiating their definitions gives the [[双曲函数求导|hyperbolic derivative rules]]. The derivative of $\cosh$ has no minus sign because of its exponential construction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8. 看到表达式时怎样选择规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. Choosing a rule from the expression's structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 先问什么 | 识别到的结构 | 使用的对象 | 必查条件 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 是有限加法或常数倍吗？ | $af+bg$ | [[导数线性法则]] | 每项在该点可导 |
| 是固定整数或实数幂吗？ | $x^n$、$x^r$ | [[整数幂求导]]、[[实数幂求导]] | 原函数的实值定义域 |
| 是两个函数相乘或相除吗？ | $uv$、$u/v$ | [[乘积法则]]、[[商法则]] | 商的分母不为零 |
| 是函数套函数吗？ | $f(g(x))$ | [[链式法则]] | 内外层对应点都可导 |
| 变量由关系式确定吗？ | $F(x,y)=0$ | [[隐式求导]] | 解支存在；除法前检查相应偏导 |
| 底数和指数都变化吗？ | $u(x)^{v(x)}$ | [[对数求导]] | 正值或合法局部分支 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The table is a structural decision aid: finite sums use linearity; fixed powers use the matching power rule; products and quotients use their own rules; nested functions require the chain rule; relations require a valid implicit branch; and a moving base with a moving exponent usually calls for logarithmic differentiation. Domain and differentiability conditions travel with every choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后做四项验算：原函数定义域是否保留；链式法则的内层因子是否齐全；单位是否从“输出”变成“输出/输入”；在容易判断的点上，导数符号是否符合图像的增减。公式给候选结果，这些检查决定它是否仍在回答原问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finish with four checks: retain the original domain, account for every inner derivative, verify that units change from output to output per input, and compare the sign with visible increase or decrease at simple points. A formula produces a candidate expression; these checks determine whether it still answers the original problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 章节自检
> 1. 为什么连续不足以推出可导？
> 2. 为什么乘积法则有两项而链式法则是相乘？
> 3. $d[\ln(\cos x)]/dx$ 在哪里可用？
> 4. 对 $e^y+y=x$，经过点 $(1,0)$ 的局部解支斜率是多少？
> 5. 为什么 $(1+1/n)^n\to e$ 的对数证明必须说明采用了哪一种 $e$ 的定义？
>
> **答案：** 1. 连续只约束函数值接上，差商仍可从左右趋向不同值。2. 乘积有两个一阶增量来源；复合是两次连续局部缩放。3. 需 $\cos x>0$；在这样的区间导数为 $-\tan x$。4. $(e^y+1)y'=1$，所以 $y'=1/2$。5. 若同时用该极限定义 $e$ 又用依赖它的对数导数证明极限，就会循环论证。
