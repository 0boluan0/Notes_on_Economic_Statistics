#### 1.1 极限先描述附近趋势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1 Limits first describe nearby behaviour*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

目标点的值与附近趋势必须先分开；分段点还要分别检查左右方向，无穷则要区分它描述的是输入还是输出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separate the value at the target point from the nearby trend first. At a piecewise boundary, inspect both directions separately; when infinity appears, distinguish whether it describes the input or the output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[函数极限]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The limit examines allowed inputs near the target, with the target itself removed. A missing or altered point value can therefore leave the limit intact; changing nearby inputs or outputs is a different operation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极限检查的是目标点之外的附近合法输入。因此，目标点缺值或改值可以不影响极限；改变附近的输入范围或输出则是另一回事。

![[点值不决定极限]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare left and right limits only when the domain supplies inputs arbitrarily close from both sides. At an endpoint, use the available side; at an isolated point, the punctured-limit convention supplies no limit to compare.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有定义域两侧都有任意近的输入时，才比较左右极限。端点使用可用的一侧；孤立点按这里的去心极限约定没有可比较的极限。

![[双侧极限判定]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The next distinction separates where the input goes from what the output does. Input infinity requires an unbounded domain; output infinity is an eventual threshold guarantee, stronger than merely finding large values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面把输入走向哪里与输出怎样变化分开。输入趋于无穷需要无界定义域；输出趋于无穷要求最终通过每个门槛，比偶尔取到大值更强。

![[无穷极限与无穷远极限]]

#### 1.2 计算前先诊断形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2 Diagnose the form before choosing a technique*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直接代入只是第一步。结果若未定，就要判断哪种改写保持原极限，并核对所用定理的条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Direct substitution is only the first step. If it is indeterminate, choose a rewrite that preserves the original limit and verify the hypotheses of any theorem used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the diagnostic above, substitution is useful when the component limits justify it; it is not a compulsory first operation. The symbol $0/0$ records two nearby limits equal to zero, not merely two zero point values. Algebra, bounds and one-sided analysis can establish the limit directly when appropriate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的代入诊断以各部分极限提供依据为前提，并非每题强制的第一步。$0/0$ 记录的是附近的两个极限都为零，不只是两个点值为零。条件合适时，可以直接通过代数、估界或分侧分析得到极限。

![[零比零未定式]]

![[极限定律适用条件]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The next procedure chooses a method from the expression and its domain. Limit laws, algebraic rewrites, squeezing and other theorems are alternatives with their own hypotheses; they are not a list that must be exhausted in order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面的程序根据表达式和定义域选择方法。极限定律、代数改写、夹逼与其他定理各有适用条件，不是必须依次试完的清单。

![[极限计算流程]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bound can settle a limit even when a factor oscillates. Keep the same allowed inputs for all three functions, and make the upper and lower limits agree; the theorem then proves the middle limit exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使某个因子振荡，上下界仍可能确定极限。三个函数使用同一批合法输入，上下界趋于同一个数，定理便同时证明中间极限存在并确定其值。

![[夹逼定理]]

#### 1.3 连续把趋势接到点值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3 Continuity joins the nearby trend to the point value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续性先给出一点处的三项检查，再说明间断能否通过补一个点修复，以及它与可导和区间中间值的关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity supplies three checks at a point, distinguishes discontinuities that can be repaired by filling one point, and connects the concept to differentiability and intermediate values on an interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three-check description above applies at a domain cluster point; at an interior point the relevant limit is two-sided. The direct continuity definition also covers endpoints using domain inputs and isolated points, where continuity is automatic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的三项检查适用于定义域聚点；在内点处，相应极限是双侧极限。直接的连续性定义还覆盖端点的域内趋近，以及自动连续的孤立点。

![[一点连续]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before naming a discontinuity, state whether the point belongs to the domain and which sides allow approach. Compare finite side limits with the point value, and distinguish a signed infinite limit from outputs that are merely unbounded. Different sides can have different behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给间断命名之前，先说明该点是否属于定义域、哪些侧可以趋近。比较有限侧极限与点值，并把确定的正负无穷极限和仅仅无界的输出分开；两侧可以具有不同表现。

![[间断类型判别]]

![[可去间断的唯一补值]]

![[跳跃间断不可补值]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiability controls more than the output value: it controls output change divided by input change. A finite limiting ratio therefore implies continuity at the same point. The following converse card shows why continuity alone is weaker.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可导性不只控制输出值，还控制输出变化除以输入变化的比率。比率有有限极限，便保证同一点连续；紧随其后的逆命题卡说明为什么连续性本身较弱。

![[可导必连续]]

![[连续不必可导]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The focus now changes from one input to an entire interval. A real function continuous throughout $[a,b]$, including domain-relative continuity at its endpoints, attains every output between the endpoint outputs. Strictly intermediate outputs are attained at interior inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把目光从单个输入转向整段区间。实函数在 $[a,b]$ 每一点连续，包括端点的域内连续，就能取到端点输出之间的全部值；严格居中的输出在内部输入处取到。

![[介值定理]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Existence, uniqueness and numerical accuracy answer different questions. An additional strict-monotonicity argument may prove uniqueness; a bisection procedure adds reliable sign evaluations, interval updates and a stopping rule. These do not follow from the existence statement alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存在性、唯一性与数值精度回答不同的问题。额外的严格单调性论证可以保证唯一；二分程序还要加入可靠的符号计算、区间更新和停止标准。这些内容不由存在性陈述单独给出。

![[介值定理只保证存在性]]

极限先给出严格讨论“趋近”的语言；导数随后把差商的极限变成局部变化率与切线斜率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Limits first provide a rigorous language for approaching a value. Derivatives then turn the limit of a difference quotient into a local rate of change and a tangent slope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Pair $e^x$ with $e^{-x}$ for real inputs: their half-difference and half-sum define hyperbolic sine and cosine. Their odd/even symmetry and difference-of-squares identity explain the name without treating them as inverse trigonometric functions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实数输入，把 $e^x$ 与 $e^{-x}$ 配对：半差、半和分别定义双曲正弦、余弦。奇偶性与平方差恒等式解释其名称，它们并非普通三角函数的反函数。

#### 2.1 局部近似必须同时回答模型与误差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1 A local approximation needs both a model and an error account*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

切线给一阶局部模型，曲率把它提升为二次模型；Taylor 多项式统一逐阶匹配，而余项与误差尺度决定这个近似能否用于目标范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tangent supplies the first-order model and curvature adds a quadratic term. Taylor polynomials unify derivative matching, while the remainder and the chosen error scale determine whether the approximation is usable on the target range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a base point before estimating a nearby value. Differentiability gives a tangent remainder $o(h)$; matching the second derivative gives the quadratic model under its additional assumptions. For a chosen nonzero step, check an actual error estimate separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先选基点，再估计附近函数值。可导给出余项为 $o(h)$ 的切线模型；再满足二阶条件，才匹配二阶导数得到二次模型。对指定的非零步长，须另核具体误差。

![[线性近似]]

![[绝对误差与相对误差]]

![[二次近似]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Matching more derivatives extends the local polynomial model. The order is finite and bounds its degree; it fixes coefficients at the centre, without yet certifying an error at a chosen input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

匹配更多导数，就把局部模型推广成有限阶多项式。阶数限制次数并确定中心处的系数，但尚未保证指定输入处的误差。

![[Taylor多项式]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The remainder is the exact difference from the function. Under the next theorem’s whole-segment derivative assumptions, an additional derivative bound supplies a usable error estimate. Infinite-series equality is a separate limiting question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

余项是与原函数的精确差。满足下面定理的整段导数条件，再给出导数上界，才得到可用的误差估计；无穷级数是否等于函数是另一个极限问题。

![[Taylor余项]]

#### 2.2 先切开定义域，再由符号拼出函数形状
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2 Split the domain first, then assemble shape from signs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

临界点和二阶导数为零都只产生候选；渐近线、连通区间、导数符号与凹凸变化共同约束最终图形。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Critical points and zeros of the second derivative merely nominate candidates. Asymptotes, connected domain intervals, derivative signs, and changes in concavity jointly constrain the graph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The candidate definition below uses interior points of the real-line domain: a zero or absent derivative may nominate a point, but does not classify it. Endpoints remain separate candidates when the optimization problem includes them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面按实线定义域的内点定义临界点：零导数或导数不存在可以产生候选，却不决定类型。若优化问题包含端点，端点仍须另查。

![[临界点]]

![[导数单调性判据]]

![[凹凸性]]

弦不等式给出概念，下一张判据解释何时可以用导数代替逐条检查弦。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The chord inequality defines the concept; the next criterion explains when derivatives can replace checking each chord.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a differentiable real function on an open interval, compare the first derivative across the whole interval. Nondecrease is equivalent to convexity; an everywhere-existing second derivative converts this to a nonnegative sign test. Included endpoints need their own extension check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在开区间上，对可导实函数比较整个区间的一阶导数：非减等价于凸性；二阶导数处处存在时，才可改为非负号检验。包含端点时还须检查向端点的延伸。

![[导数判凹凸]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inflection is a genuine change of bending at a continuous interior graph point. A zero second derivative is only a candidate, and a missing second derivative need not rule an inflection out. A domain gap is not a graph point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拐点是连续的内部图像点处真正改变弯曲方向。二阶导数为零只给候选；二阶导数不存在也不一定排除拐点。定义域缺点并非图像点。

![[拐点]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify the approach direction with each asymptote. One signed infinite one-sided limit suffices for a vertical line; at an infinite end, a vanishing difference from an affine line gives a horizontal or slant asymptote. Finite intersections are compatible with that limiting statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每条渐近线都要注明趋近方向。一侧带符号无穷极限即可给竖直线；在无穷方向，与仿射直线的差趋零给水平或斜渐近线。有限处相交与这种极限陈述相容。

![[渐近线]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Combine the domain, boundary limits and whole-interval derivative signs into one consistent picture. Missing inputs determine branch boundaries; an excluded input is neither a critical point nor an inflection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把定义域、边界极限和全区间导数符号合成一致图像。缺失的输入界定图像分支；被排除的输入既不是临界点，也不是拐点。

![[曲线描绘]]

#### 2.3 极值题要区分存在、候选、分类与全局证明
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3 Extremum problems separate existence, candidacy, classification, and global proof*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“导数为零”从来不是完整答案。先说清局部还是绝对极值，再检查极值是否取得、候选是否找全，最后用比较、符号、曲率或凸性完成论证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Derivative equals zero” is never a complete answer. Specify local versus absolute extrema, establish attainment, enumerate all candidates, and finish with comparison, sign, curvature, or convexity evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare only allowed inputs: “local” uses a neighbourhood within the domain, while “absolute” uses all of it. The distinction includes strict versus non-strict comparisons and the difference between an input location and its attained output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较对象始终是合法输入：局部只看定义域内的邻域，绝对则看整个定义域。还要区分严格与非严格比较，以及取得位置和输出值。

![[局部极值与绝对极值]]

![[极值定理]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first-order necessary condition now separates interior differentiable candidates from endpoints and corners. At an interior local extremum, differentiability at the point forces a zero derivative; a zero derivative alone does not determine whether the point is a minimum, a maximum or neither.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一阶必要条件现在把可微内点候选与端点、尖角区分开来。内点局部极值若在该点可微，导数必为零；仅有零导数，仍不能判断它是极小、极大还是两者都不是。

![[无约束一阶条件]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a finite nondegenerate closed interval, continuity gives attainment. The complete candidate set includes endpoints and interior zero-or-absent-derivative points. It may be infinite, so identifying all candidates need not mean enumerating a finite table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在有限非退化闭区间上，连续性保证取得极值。完整候选包括端点和内部导数为零或不存在的点；候选可能无限，找全候选不一定能化成有限表格。

![[闭区间极值法]]

![[一阶导数检验]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an interior stationary point, differentiability nearby and a finite second derivative at that point suffice for this test. Its nonzero sign determines a strict local type; zero leaves the question open, and no global comparison follows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在内部驻点，附近可导且该点二阶导数有限，就能使用本检验。非零符号确定严格局部类型；零时仍未定，也不能由此完成全局比较。

![[二阶导数检验]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Turn the verbal constraints into a feasible one-variable model before differentiating, retaining every allowed branch. A critical point is a candidate; compare feasible values and boundary behaviour to establish global optimality and whether the optimum is actually attained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把文字约束转成可行的一元模型并保留全部合法分支，再求导。临界点只是候选；还要比较可行取值与边界行为，说明全局最优及其是否取得。

![[单变量约束优化]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a convex feasible set, convex objectives turn local minima into global minima; concave objectives turn local maxima into global maxima. This comparison does not by itself prove that an optimum is attained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在凸可行集上，凸目标把局部最小推广为全局最小，凹目标把局部最大推广为全局最大；这项比较本身不证明最优值被取得。

![[凸优化全局性]]

#### 2.4 切线模型也连接速率问题与数值求根
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4 The tangent model also connects rate problems and numerical root finding*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相关变化率沿共同时间参数求导；Newton 法则令当前切线为零。前者最怕过早代入瞬时常数，后者必须把局部收敛与全局安全性分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Related-rates problems differentiate along a common time parameter, whereas Newton's method sets the current tangent model to zero. The former fails when instantaneous values are substituted too early; the latter requires a clear separation between local convergence and global safeguards.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The relation must hold near the time of interest, with the changing quantities differentiable there. Differentiate with respect to their common time before substituting the instant’s values, and distinguish fixed geometric parameters from moving quantities and signed velocity from speed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关系须在所求时刻附近持续成立，各变化量在该时刻可导。先对共同时间求导，再代入瞬时值；固定几何参数与变化量、带方向速度与非负速率分别处理。

#### 2.5 中值定理把局部导数升级为区间结论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.5 Mean-value theorems turn local derivatives into interval conclusions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

罗尔定理是等高端点的基型；减去割线后得到拉格朗日中值定理，再由导数界控制有限函数差与整段不等式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rolle's theorem is the equal-endpoint case. Subtracting a secant line yields the Lagrange mean value theorem, after which derivative bounds control finite function differences and interval-wide inequalities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The next theorems concern a whole interval. Continuity includes the endpoints, while differentiability is required only inside. Equal endpoint values first lead to a horizontal tangent somewhere in the interior.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接下来的定理讨论整段区间：连续性包括端点，可导性只要求内部。先从端点等高的情形出发，证明内部某处存在水平切线。

![[罗尔定理]]

![[拉格朗日中值定理]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two-function version compares two endpoint changes with derivatives at one common interior input. Its multiplication form avoids dividing by a quantity that might be zero; its ratio form will later support [[洛必达法则|L’Hôpital’s rule]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

双函数版本把两个端点变化量与同一个内部输入处的导数联系起来。乘法形式避免除以可能为零的量；满足额外非零条件的比值形式随后支持[[洛必达法则]]。

![[柯西中值定理]]

![[导数界控制函数差]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inequality can be proved by comparing a difference function with a known reference value. The derivative controls movement away from that reference, so the direction and equality cases must be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不等式可以转成差函数与已知基准值的比较。导数控制离开基点后的变化，因此必须核对左右方向与等号情形。

![[导数法证明不等式]]

#### 2.6 从变化率恢复函数，再把变化规律写成方程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.6 Recover functions from rates, then write laws of change as equations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

微分保留一阶线性变化；原函数反向恢复导数，并在连通区间留下一个加法常数。这三张卡也是下一积分单元的桥。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The differential retains first-order linear change. An antiderivative reverses differentiation and leaves one additive constant on each connected interval. These atoms also bridge into the next integration unit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[微分]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First recover one function whose derivative agrees everywhere on the chosen interval. The next theorem explains why every other solution differs by one constant there; separate domain intervals may have separate constants. An indefinite integral names this family of functions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先找出在所选区间处处求导后都相符的一个函数。紧接的定理说明该区间内其他解为何只差一个常数；分开的定义域区间可以有不同常数。不定积分记号表示这一族函数。

![[原函数]]

![[原函数相差常数]]

常微分方程的未知对象是整条函数轨迹。分离变量只适用于特定乘积结构，并且必须补回被除掉的平衡解、报告最大解区间、处理方程奇点；指数、Logistic 与正交轨线是这套判断的三个应用方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ODE's unknown is an entire function trajectory. Separation works only for a particular product structure and requires restoring divided-out equilibria, stating the maximal interval, and treating singular points. Exponential growth, logistic growth, and orthogonal trajectories are three applications of this reasoning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The unknown is now a function, not a number. Begin by identifying the independent variable and the interval on which all required derivatives and the equation exist; then verify a candidate at every input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在的未知对象是函数而非一个数。先认清自变量与所需导数、原方程都有定义的区间，再逐点验证候选函数。

![[常微分方程]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A parameter describes unspecified starting information. Selecting it from an initial value chooses a member of a family; a separate completeness or uniqueness argument is needed to exclude other trajectories.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参数记录尚未指定的起始信息。初值确定参数，只是在解族内选择成员；要排除其他轨迹，还需完整性或唯一性的论证。

![[通解与初值]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For continuous separated factors, check zeros of the state factor before division. On a state interval where that factor is nonzero, integrating the chain-rule identity gives an implicit relation; its inverse and its solution interval still require checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续的分离因子先查状态因子的零点，再做除法。在状态因子非零的区间上，对链式恒等式求原函数得到隐式关系；反解和解区间仍须核对。

![[分离变量]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A state where the divided factor vanishes can give a valid constant solution. Restore such states from the original equation, and avoid treating this restoration as a proof that all nonconstant branches or possible joins have been found.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

被除因子为零的状态，可能给出合法常数解。要代回原方程补回它们，但不能由此断言全部非恒定解支或可能的拼接已经找齐。

![[分离变量漏平衡解]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the same trajectory and the same equation while testing extension. A removable defect in one formula is different from blow-up or leaving the equation’s domain, and maximality by itself does not imply uniqueness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检查延拓时必须保留原轨迹和原方程。某个表达式的可去缺口不同于爆破或离开方程定义域，而最大性本身也不意味着唯一性。

![[微分方程最大区间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fixed proportional rate, rather than a fixed absolute increment, leads to an exponential trajectory. Interpret growth and decay with the initial sign and time direction specified; the zero solution must be retained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定的是比例变化率，而非绝对增量，这才得到指数轨迹。解释增长或衰减时要说明初值符号与时间方向，并保留零解。

![[指数增长与衰减]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The logistic model replaces a constant per-capita rate by one that falls linearly with the current level. With positive parameters and a positive initial level, forward convergence to capacity is compatible with a finite backward endpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Logistic 模型把固定人均比例率改为随当前规模线性下降的比例率。参数与初始规模为正时，向前趋于承载量，并不排除向后只有有限的解区间。

![[Logistic增长]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Perpendicularity concerns tangent directions at each relevant crossing. Nonzero tangent vectors with dot product zero cover vertical as well as finite-slope cases; only two finite nonzero slopes can be related by the negative-reciprocal shortcut.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

垂直性针对各相关交点的切向。非零切向量点积为零也能处理竖直情形；只有两个有限非零斜率才能使用负倒数捷径。

![[正交轨线]]

正交是切线方向的几何条件；将它转成微分方程，还需要消元、检查斜率是否有限非零，再求解和核对分支。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Orthogonality is a geometric condition on tangent directions. Turning it into an ODE requires parameter elimination, a finite-nonzero-slope check, solution, and a final branch check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solve the slope equation on its derivation domain, then check the geometry it omitted. For the family $y=ax^2$, the ellipses have positive level $C$, while the vertical axis is an additional geometric trajectory. An explicit upper or lower branch is only part of the full curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先在斜率方程的导出域内求解，再补查它遗漏的几何。对 $y=ax^2$，椭圆的参数满足 $C>0$，竖直轴则是另一个几何轨线；显式的上下分支各只是整条曲线的一部分。

![[正交轨线求解]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original equation must be defined at every input of a solution interval. Values chosen to patch separate branches may make a smooth function, but cannot manufacture a missing equation or force the branch constants to agree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原方程必须在解区间的每个输入都有定义。补值拼接可能产生光滑函数，却不能制造缺失的方程，也不能自动迫使两侧常数相等。

![[方程未定义处分支独立]]

#### 3.1 从矩形累加到一个确定的数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From sampled rectangles to a well-defined number*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先分清有限近似与极限定义，再检查极限存在的条件；和式识别把同一结构反向用于求极限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separate finite approximation from the limiting definition, establish existence, then reuse the same structure to recognise limits of sums.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each finite sampled total adds a height times the width of its own piece; equal widths are only one choice. To pass from these numbers to an integral, every sufficiently fine tagged partition must approach the same limit, not just one convenient sampling rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个有限采样和都把高度乘以对应小段的宽度再相加；等宽只是其中一种选择。要从这些数走到积分，必须让所有充分细的带取样点分割趋于同一极限，而不只是某一种方便的取样规则。

![[黎曼和]]

![[定积分]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity on the whole finite closed interval is a convenient guarantee that all fine sampling schemes agree. The proof controls the largest oscillation on every short piece at once. A jump need not destroy integrability, so this guarantee is not a necessary test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

整个有限闭区间上的连续性，是各种充分细采样都趋于同一值的方便保证。证明要同时控制每一小段内的最大波动；跳跃也可能仍然可积，因此不能把这个充分条件当作必要检验。

![[连续函数可积]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the reverse direction, identify a fixed interval, a fixed integrable function and the shrinking widths before taking the limit. For $n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n(2+3i/n)^2$, using $[2,5]$ introduces width $3/n$, so the integral needs the factor $1/3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反向识别时，先确定固定区间、固定的可积函数及趋零的段宽，再求极限。例如 $n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n(2+3i/n)^2$ 若按 $[2,5]$ 识别，宽度是 $3/n$，积分前就须保留 $1/3$。

![[和式识别为积分]]

#### 3.2 先学会计算，再审查累积的符号与边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Evaluate first, then examine signs and bounds of accumulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程先给端点评价公式（MIT 的 FTC I），让面积与变化率问题变得可算；它的证明稍后借助累积求导。已有的 [[原函数]] 与 [[原函数相差常数]] 在这里复用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course introduces endpoint evaluation—MIT's FTC I—before proving it through accumulation differentiation. The existing [[原函数|antiderivative]] and [[原函数相差常数|constant-difference theorem]] are reused.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When reusing the antiderivative family for endpoint evaluation, keep one interval containing the whole path. Under the continuous-integrand theorem below, the primitive extends continuously to the endpoints and its additive constant cancels; the resulting definite integral is a number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把原函数族用于端点评价时，要在同一个区间内覆盖整段路径。按下面连续被积函数的定理，原函数连续延伸至端点，加法常数在相减时消去，所得定积分是一个数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The continuous case is the first useful route. The more general Riemann version assumes an antiderivative $F$ already exists: it is continuous on the closed interval and satisfies $F'=f$ inside. Integrability of $f$ alone does not promise such an $F$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续情形是最先使用的版本。更一般的黎曼版本先假设原函数 $F$ 已经存在：它在闭区间连续、内部满足 $F'=f$。仅有 $f$ 可积，还不能保证存在这样的 $F$。

![[Newton-Leibniz公式]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a position with continuous velocity, endpoint subtraction gives displacement, while integrating speed gives distance. With $v(t)=2t-2$ on $[0,2]$, the two signed pieces are $-1$ and $1$: the net change is $0$, but total movement is $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

位置具有连续速度时，端点相减得到位移，积分速率得到路程。对 $[0,2]$ 上的 $v(t)=2t-2$，两段带符号贡献为 $-1$ 与 $1$：净变化是 $0$，累计运动却是 $2$。

![[净变化与总变化]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To combine contributions over one interval, add their integrals with the same fixed coefficients. A coefficient depending on the integration variable must remain inside; multiplying two functions is not the same operation as adding them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一区间合并贡献时，用相同的固定系数把各积分相加。随积分变量变化的系数要留在积分号内；函数相乘也不能套用相加规则。

![[定积分线性性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now keep the function fixed and split the interval. If all three endpoints lie in one interval where the function is Riemann integrable, the oriented identity $\int_a^c f=\int_a^b f+\int_b^c f$ holds in any order of $a,b,c$. Subtracting accumulations from the same starting point leaves the oriented integral between their endpoints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接下来保持函数不变，拆开区间。只要三个端点都位于函数黎曼可积的同一区间内，有向恒等式 $\int_a^c f=\int_a^b f+\int_b^c f$ 就适用于 $a,b,c$ 的任意顺序。从同一起点出发的两段累积相减，恰好留下两个终点之间的有向积分。

![[定积分区间可加性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pointwise comparison of two integrable functions gives an integral comparison on a forward interval. Reversing the bounds reverses the inequality. Equality of totals need not mean that the functions agree at every point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在正向区间上，两个可积函数逐点的大小关系给出积分的大小关系；交换上下限，不等号反向。总量相等也不代表函数在每一点都相同。

![[积分保序性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking the absolute value after accumulation preserves earlier cancellation. Taking it before accumulation removes that cancellation. On a finite forward interval, Riemann integrability of $f$ also guarantees integrability of $|f|$, so the two totals can be compared.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先累积再取绝对值，仍保留此前的正负抵消；先取绝对值再累积，则去掉了抵消。在有限正向区间上，$f$ 黎曼可积也保证 $|f|$ 可积，因而可以比较这两个总量。

![[积分绝对值界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bound valid throughout each piece gives a bound for that piece’s integral; add the results over a finite partition. Refining with equally strong or tighter valid bounds cannot worsen the estimate. A few sampled heights alone do not certify a bound between samples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在每一小段处处有效的函数界，可以控制该段积分，再对有限分割逐段相加。细分后若保留同样强或更紧的有效界，估计就不会变差；只看几个采样高度，还不能保证样本之间也满足这些界。

![[积分上下界估计]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[积分平均值|integral average]] is the integral divided by the positive interval length. Bounding it is one question; whether the function takes that exact value is another. Continuity on the finite closed interval guarantees attainment, without choosing a unique point or forcing the midpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[积分平均值]]是积分除以正的区间长度。把平均值夹在两个数之间，与函数是否真正取到这个数，是两个问题。有限闭区间上的连续性保证取到，但不指定唯一位置，也不要求它是中点。

![[积分中值定理]]

换元通过链式法则改变表示。[[定积分换元]]负责完整复合导数型公式与有向上下限；需要逆映射消去残余变量时，再查 [[非单调换元需分支]]。这两个原子在下一单元统一嵌入，此处直接复用链接。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[定积分换元|Definite substitution]] handles complete composite derivatives and oriented bounds. If residual variables require an inverse, use the separate [[非单调换元需分支|branch check]]. Both atoms are embedded in the following unit and linked here without duplicating their objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once Riemann integrability on the whole finite symmetric interval is established, reflecting the left half gives cancellation for odd functions and doubling for even functions. Continuity is sufficient, not required. A singularity such as that of $1/x$ requires separate improper-integral convergence checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确认整个有限对称区间上的黎曼可积性，再把左半边反射到右半边：奇函数抵消，偶函数加倍。连续是充分条件，不是必要条件；遇到 $1/x$ 这样的奇点，还须分别检查反常积分收敛。

![[奇偶函数积分]]

#### 3.3 让终点移动，累积就成为函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Moving the endpoint turns accumulation into a function*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先定义对象，再证明它的导数是什么；这给出 MIT 的 FTC II，也补全前面端点评价的证明。变限求导沿用 [[链式法则]]，不是另记一套无来由的规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define the object before proving its derivative. This yields MIT's FTC II and completes the earlier endpoint proof. Variable-bound differentiation reuses the [[链式法则|chain rule]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With both endpoints fixed, the definite integral is one number. Fix only the starting point and let the endpoint vary: this defines a new function whenever every required interval is integrable. Its continuity and its derivative are separate questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个端点都固定时，定积分是一个数；只固定起点，让终点变化，只要每个所用区间都可积，就定义出一个新函数。这个函数是否连续、是否可导，需要分别说明。

![[累积函数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a Riemann-integrable integrand, continuity at the particular interior point is enough to recover its value as the accumulation derivative. At a domain endpoint, use continuity and differentiation from inside the interval. Continuity everywhere gives the familiar antiderivative construction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对黎曼可积的被积函数，在所考察的内点连续，就足以让累积函数的导数恢复该点函数值。定义域端点则使用区间内侧的连续性与导数；处处连续时，得到熟悉的原函数构造。

![[积分求导定理]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now let both endpoints move while the integrand stays fixed and continuous on an interval containing the whole integration path. Write the result as two accumulations from one fixed base, then apply the chain rule to each endpoint. Reversed or coincident bounds do not remove either chain factor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在让两个端点都移动，保持被积函数固定，并在包含整段积分路径的区间上连续。把结果写成从同一基点出发的两段累积之差，再分别对端点用链式法则。上下限反向或在某点重合，都不能省掉任何一个链式因子。

![[变限积分求导]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Accumulating $1/t$ from $1$ gives the existing natural logarithm on $x>0$: its derivative is $1/x$, and its value at $1$ is zero. When $0<x<1$, orientation makes the integral negative. Passing through $0$ would leave this finite-integral formula's domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $1$ 开始累积 $1/t$，就在 $x>0$ 上得到已有的自然对数：导数为 $1/x$，在 $1$ 处为零。$0<x<1$ 时，积分方向使结果为负；跨过 $0$ 就超出了这个有限积分公式的定义域。

![[自然对数积分表示]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An antiderivative can exist without an elementary expression. For continuous $f$ on an interval, integration from a fixed base constructs it and gives its derivative at interior points. This still allows bounds, numerical approximations, and sometimes a simple exact definite value; convergence at infinity is another question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原函数可以存在，却没有初等表达式。对区间上的连续函数，从固定基点积分就能构造它，并在内点求出导数。仍可进行估计、数值近似，有些定积分甚至有简单精确值；无穷端点的收敛性则是另一个问题。

![[无初等原函数的积分]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An accumulation's height records everything collected since its base; the current integrand gives its slope. Thus a positive but falling integrand can produce a rising curve whose slope decreases. A zero alone does not classify an extremum; a slope tending to zero alone does not guarantee a horizontal asymptote.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

累积曲线的高度记录从基点至今的总量，被积函数当前的值则给出斜率。因此，被积函数虽然下降，只要仍为正，累积曲线就仍上升，只是斜率变小。一个零点本身不足以判定极值；斜率趋零本身也不保证存在水平渐近线。

![[累积函数图像]]

#### 3.4 微元和实际几何决定积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Elements and physical geometry determine the integral*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面积先看一条细带，体积先看一个截面。旋转轴决定选择垫圈还是壳层；原平面上的两块区域分开，不代表旋转后也不重叠。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Area starts from a strip and volume from a cross section. The axis guides the washer-or-shell choice; disjoint planar pieces can still overlap after rotation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First identify the actual strip, including any gaps. Between two integrable graphs on a finite interval, its vertical length is the absolute difference of their heights. A horizontal strip uses right minus left instead; choose the direction that makes these boundaries easier to describe.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先认清实际细条，不能把空隙算进去。有限区间上的两条可积图像之间，竖条长度为两个高度差的绝对值；横条则用右减左。选取能更清楚描述边界的方向。

![[曲线间面积]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A solid replaces strip length by complete cross-sectional area. For an ordinary bounded solid with integrable section area, add that area times perpendicular thickness. A sphere uses disk areas; another solid may have square or disconnected sections. The slice need not come from rotation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

立体把条长换成完整截面积。对通常的有界立体，截面积可积时，就累加面积乘垂直厚度。球体用圆盘面积，其他立体也可能有正方形或分离的截面；切片并不要求立体由旋转生成。

![[切片体积]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For perpendicular slices of a solid of revolution, find the actual distances from the axis before squaring. An annulus contributes $\pi(R^2-r^2)$ with $R\ge r\ge0$. A segment crossing the axis may produce a full disk, so subtracting the squares of its two endpoint distances can invent a hole.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对旋转体的垂直切片，先找实际到轴距离，再平方。圆环贡献 $\pi(R^2-r^2)$，其中 $R\ge r\ge0$。线段跨过旋转轴时可能生成完整圆盘；直接拿两个端点距离的平方相减，可能凭空扣出一个孔。

![[圆盘垫圈法]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A parallel strip suggests a shell, but radius is a nonnegative distance and thickness is radial. At each radius, use the total axial length present after rotation and count it once. Pieces on opposite sides of the axis can reach the same shell; their overlapping lengths must be combined by union.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平行于轴的细条提示壳法，但半径是非负距离，厚度沿半径方向。每个半径上，用旋转后实际存在的轴向总长度，并只计一次。轴两侧的区域可能到达同一壳层，重叠的长度须取并集。

![[圆柱壳法]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To decide whether two pieces overlap after rotation, compare both their radius and their position along the axis. At the same radius, height intervals $[0,2]$ and $[1,3]$ occupy a union of length $3$, not a total length of $4$. For perpendicular sections, perform the corresponding union of radial intervals before computing area.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断两块区域旋转后是否重叠，要同时比较半径和沿轴位置。在同一半径上，高度区间 $[0,2]$ 与 $[1,3]$ 的并集长度为 $3$，不能按两段长度相加得到 $4$。改用垂直截面时，则先合并径向区间，再算面积。

![[旋转体重复覆盖]]

#### 3.5 平均、质心与功：先确定权重和单位
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Averages, centres of mass, and work: identify weights and units*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区间平均按长度平均；质量平均则按质量份额平均。沿曲线平均需要 [[参数曲线弧长与重复描画|弧长微元]]。质心定义、具体计算和利用形心求旋转体积是三个可以分别使用的对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An interval average uses length weights, a mass average uses mass shares, and a curve average needs the [[参数曲线弧长与重复描画|arc-length element]]. Defining the centre of mass, calculating it, and using a centroid to find a rotational volume are distinct reusable objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return now to the quantity whose attainment was studied earlier: the average itself. It exists for a real Riemann-integrable function on a finite interval of positive length. Unequal pieces contribute in proportion to their widths; changing to arc-length or mass weights changes what is being averaged uniformly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在回到前面讨论过能否被取到的那个数：平均值本身。实函数在长度为正的有限区间黎曼可积，就能定义它。不等宽小段按各自宽度贡献；改为弧长或质量权重，也就改变了“按什么均匀平均”。

![[积分平均值]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replace equal-length influence by a nonnegative weight density, then divide by a strictly positive total weight. On a finite interval, require both the density and its product with the measured value to be integrable. For the temperature example, weighting by volume gives $80$ degrees Celsius, while uniform height weighting gives $85$ degrees Celsius.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把等长度的影响改为非负权重密度，再除以严格为正的总权重。在有限区间上，要保证权重密度及其与所测值的乘积都可积。后面的温度例子按体积平均为 $80$ 摄氏度，按高度均匀平均则为 $85$ 摄氏度。

![[加权平均]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Position is now the quantity being averaged, and mass supplies the weights. A finite positive total mass and finite absolute first moments give finite centre coordinates. Uniform area density gives equal mass to equal areas; that case produces the area centroid. Signed coordinates keep track of which side of an axis contains the material.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在平均的是位置，权重来自质量。总质量有限且正、绝对一阶矩有限，才能得到有限的质心坐标。面积密度均匀时，相同面积获得相同质量权重，此时得到面积形心。带符号的坐标保留材料位于轴哪一侧的信息。

![[质心]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose strips whose mass and mean position are easy to describe. Add the total mass and the signed first moments over the same body, then divide; separate pieces must first contribute their own masses and moments. For a uniform sheet between continuous upper and lower curves, the strip's centre height is the average of those two heights. Variable density must remain in the mass calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择质量和平均位置容易描述的细条。对同一个物体累加总质量与带符号一阶矩，再做除法；分离的部分先各自贡献质量和矩。均匀薄片位于连续上下边界之间时，细条中心高度就是两个高度的平均。密度不均匀时，必须把它保留在质量计算中。

![[质心积分计算]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a bounded plane region with piecewise-smooth boundary, positive area $A$, and the whole region on one side of a coplanar axis, boundary contact is allowed. A full revolution gives volume $A(2\pi d)$, where $d$ is the area centroid's perpendicular distance to the axis. Use the geometric area centroid even when the physical material has nonuniform density; an axis crossing the region requires a fresh coverage check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对边界分段光滑、有界且面积 $A>0$ 的平面区域，若整个区域位于共面轴的一侧，允许边界接触轴。转一整圈的体积为 $A(2\pi d)$，其中 $d$ 是面积形心到轴的垂直距离。即使材料密度不均匀，也使用几何上的面积形心；轴穿过区域时，要重新检查实际覆盖。

![[Pappus体积定理]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work adds a force component times actual signed displacement. Name the force first: the spring's restoring force and a slow balancing external force have opposite signs. For ideal pumping, split the water into layers instead; multiply each layer's weight by its own lifting distance. The layer thickness selects how much water is moved, not how far it travels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

功累加的是力分量乘实际带符号位移。先说明是哪股力：弹簧回复力与缓慢平衡它的外力符号相反。理想抽水则分割水层，以每层重量乘它自己的提升距离；层厚决定移动多少水，不是水移动多远。

![[功的积分]]

概率也是累积，但先要满足 [[概率密度函数|密度的非负性与归一化]]。[[累积分布函数]] 的一般定义允许跳跃；有密度时才写成密度积分。[[期望]] 复用概率加权平均，[[方差]] 则对中心化平方取期望，有限值各有矩存在条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Probability is accumulation only after a [[概率密度函数|density's nonnegativity and normalisation]] are established. A general [[累积分布函数|CDF]] may jump; only the density case has the corresponding integral representation. [[期望|Expectation]] reuses probability-weighted averaging, while [[方差|variance]] averages squared centred values, with the appropriate moment conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3.6 数值公式和误差证书分别检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Check the numerical formula and its error certificate separately*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限网格上的公式给一个近似数；全区间的光滑性和导数界可以给出误差保证。这里统一用 $N$ 表示小区间数，Simpson 要求 $N$ 为偶数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finite-grid formula produces an approximation; interval-wide smoothness and derivative bounds certify its error. Here $N$ counts subintervals, and Simpson requires an even $N$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with the sampling decision: on each small interval, use the height at its left or right end and multiply by the width. Monotonicity alone can place the true integral between these two values. The derivative bounds used later are sufficient ways to certify error, not the only possible route; agreement between endpoint sums without extra conditions is not a certificate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先决定每小段使用左端还是右端高度，再乘段宽。单调性本身就能让两个结果夹住真积分。后面的导数界是保证误差的充分途径，并非唯一途径；没有额外条件时，左右和相等也不能证明准确。

![[左右端点矩形法]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Joining consecutive sampled points changes the approximation from rectangles to straight-sided strips. Each contribution is width times the average of the two endpoint heights, including negative heights. On the same partition, the full sum is exactly the average of the left and right rectangle sums; $N$ strips share $N+1$ endpoint locations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把相邻采样点连起来，就从矩形改为直边条带。每段贡献是宽度乘两端高度的平均，负高度也保留符号。在同一分割上，总和恰好等于左右矩形和的平均；$N$ 段共享 $N+1$ 个端点位置。

![[梯形法]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A different choice keeps one rectangle per piece but samples its centre. This needs $N$ heights on $N$ pieces and permits either odd or even $N$. Symmetry makes every affine function exact. For a general Riemann-integrable function, refinement converges to the integral; guaranteeing a particular finite-grid accuracy is a separate step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一种选择仍是一段一个矩形，但把采样点移到中心。$N$ 段只需 $N$ 个高度，段数可以奇也可以偶。对称性使一次或常函数精确；对一般黎曼可积函数，细分后会收敛，保证某个有限网格的具体精度则是下一件事。

![[中点法]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pair equal-width pieces and integrate the polynomial of degree at most two through each pair's left, middle and right heights. Shared endpoints produce the composite weights $1,4,2,\ldots,4,1$. The number $N$ counts small intervals and must be even. Symmetry also gives cubic exactness; it does not make the rule exact for every curved graph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把等宽小段两两配对，对经过每对左、中、右高度的至多二次多项式积分。共享端点产生复合权重 $1,4,2,\ldots,4,1$。$N$ 数的是小区间，必须为偶数；对称性还使三次多项式精确，但一般曲线仍只是近似。

![[Simpson法]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is also a way to reuse already computed values. Trapezoidal and midpoint values on the same $n$ coarse intervals together contain all samples for $2n$ fine intervals. Their weighted combination is exactly $S_{2n}=(T_n+2M_n)/3$ for any finite sampled values. This equality compares approximations, rather than certifying their distance from the true integral.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还可以复用已经算过的值：同一 $n$ 段粗网格的梯形值和中点值，合起来包含 $2n$ 段细网格需要的全部采样值。对任意有限采样值都有 $S_{2n}=(T_n+2M_n)/3$。它比较的是近似值，并不证明近似值离真积分有多近。

![[Simpson组合恒等式]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now supply information between the samples. On a finite positive-length interval, a real $C^2$ function with a valid whole-interval bound $|f''|\le K_2$ has trapezoidal absolute error at most $K_2(b-a)^3/(12N^2)$. This bound can choose a sufficient integer $N$ for a tolerance. It concerns exact function values and arithmetic; measurement and rounding errors are additional.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在补充采样点之间的信息。在有限正长度区间上，实值 $C^2$ 函数若有覆盖全段的 $|f''|\le K_2$，梯形绝对误差就不超过 $K_2(b-a)^3/(12N^2)$。这可以给出满足容差的充分整数段数；它默认函数值和运算准确，测量与舍入误差另算。

![[梯形积分误差界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The midpoint proof cancels the two opposite slope contributions and leaves nonnegative weights on the second derivative. Under the same interval, count and valid $K_2$ used for the trapezoidal bound, its guaranteed upper bound is half as large. This compares guarantees, not a universal ratio of actual errors; curvature changing sign can cause cancellation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中点证明把两侧相反的斜率贡献抵消，留下二阶导数的非负加权积分。在相同区间、段数与有效 $K_2$ 下，中点保证上界是梯形界的一半。这比较的是保证值，不是实际误差的普遍比例；曲率变号时还可能发生抵消。

![[中点积分误差界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For Simpson's rule, control the fourth derivative throughout the interval instead. With real $f\in C^4[a,b]$, finite $a<b$, even $N\ge2$, and $|f^{(4)}|\le K_4$, the bound is $K_4(b-a)^5/(180N^4)$. Choose an admissible even $N$ by rounding upward. Doubling $N$ divides this same upper bound by $16$, without forcing every actual error to obey that ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Simpson 法改为控制全区间上的四阶导数。实值 $f\in C^4[a,b]$、有限 $a<b$、偶数 $N\ge2$ 且 $|f^{(4)}|\le K_4$ 时，上界为 $K_4(b-a)^5/(180N^4)$。选段数时向上取到合格偶数；把 $N$ 加倍使同一上界缩为十六分之一，不强迫每次实际误差都服从这个比例。

![[Simpson误差界]]

最后，高斯积分把平方后的累积看成体积，由圆盘与方形夹逼求出精确值，同时检查 [[反常积分|两侧无穷尾部的收敛]]。这个值又给出正态密度的归一化常数。Exercise 064 原式没有归一化，原题与两种不同的修正含义已在课堂记录中并列说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, the Gaussian integral turns squared accumulation into volume and squeezes it between disks and squares, establishing an exact value and [[反常积分|convergence of both infinite tails]]. That value determines the normal normalisation constant. The unnormalised source formula in Exercise 064 and the two distinct corrections are explained alongside the original in the class record.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The full-line value for $e^{-x^2}$ is $\sqrt\pi$, while each half-line contributes $\sqrt\pi/2$. The proof first compares volumes over finite regions, then obtains both separate tail limits. This distinction also explains why the error function uses $2/\sqrt\pi$, whereas a density on the full line uses the corresponding full total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $e^{-x^2}$，全线积分为 $\sqrt\pi$，每条半线贡献 $\sqrt\pi/2$。证明先比较有限区域上的体积，再得到两侧各自的尾积分极限。这个区别也解释了误差函数为何使用 $2/\sqrt\pi$，而全线密度须按相应的全线总量归一化。

![[高斯积分]]

### 4. 积分方法、参数曲线与极坐标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Integration Techniques, Parametric Curves, and Polar Coordinates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本定理说明为何可由原函数算积分；积分方法进一步解决找不到直接原函数以及非直角坐标的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Fundamental Theorem explains why an antiderivative can be used to evaluate an integral. Integration techniques then address cases without an immediate antiderivative and problems expressed in non-Cartesian coordinates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[积分方法.canvas|打开“积分方法”语义地图]]

先建立方法选择顺序：它不是保证成功的口诀，而是“结构识别 → 条件检查 → 求导或边界验算”的工作流。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the selection sequence. It is a workflow of structural recognition, condition checks, and verification—not a guaranteed recipe.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The opening choice is what must be found: an antiderivative family, a finite definite value, or a limit over a problematic endpoint. Inspect the original domain before algebraic cancellation. A promising rewrite must transform the full differential and preserve the relevant interval; after obtaining a candidate, check it independently. Failure of one route does not prove that no antiderivative exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先分清要找的是原函数族、有限定积分值，还是问题端附近的极限。代数消去之前先记录原定义域。可用的改写须同步处理完整微分并保留相关区间，找到候选答案后再独立验算；一种路线走不通，不证明不存在原函数。

![[积分方法选择]]

换元是最基本的反向求导结构；定积分还必须处理有向上下限。若代换后仍需用逆映射消去残余变量，非单调映射的分支问题另作一张边界卡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substitution is the basic reversed-differentiation pattern. A definite integral also has oriented bounds; if residual variables require an inverse map, non-monotone branches are handled as a separate boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recognizing the complete chain factor gives a direct reverse-differentiation route. If it is absent, renaming the inner expression does not create it; a valid inverse substitution can still be possible on a suitable branch, without necessarily simplifying the integral.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别完整链式因子，可直接反向求导。缺少因子时，给内层改名不会把它变出来；在合适分支上仍可能合法反解换元，但不一定简化计算。

![[换元积分]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the sufficient definite-integral theorem, check a continuously differentiable inner map and outer continuity over its entire image. Map the two bounds in their original order. The full composite formula permits turning maps; inverse-based calculations have their own branch conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用这条充分定积分换元定理时，检查内函数连续可微及外函数在完整像域连续，并按原顺序映射两个端点。完整复合公式允许内函数转向；依赖反解的算法另有分支条件。

![[定积分换元]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A leftover expression need not require an inverse: after $u=x^2$, a remaining $x^2$ is already $u$. If the selected calculation really needs $x$, choose valid inverse branches, preserve the inverse derivative’s sign and the order of bounds, and check any branch endpoints separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

留下原变量的表达式不一定要反解：令 $u=x^2$ 后，残余 $x^2$ 已经就是 $u$。只有所选计算确实需要恢复 $x$ 时，才选择合法逆分支，保留逆导数符号与上下限顺序，并另查分支端点。

![[非单调换元需分支]]

三角幂并非一张万能奇偶表：正弦余弦依靠奇偶性，正切正割则优先寻找完整的导数配对。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trigonometric powers do not share one universal parity table: sine and cosine use parity, whereas tangent and secant are routed by derivative pairs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nonnegative integer powers, parity is a way to keep track of available factors. Leave one sine factor to differentiate cosine, or one cosine factor to differentiate sine; rewrite the remaining pairs by the unit-circle identity. When both powers are even, power reduction is the useful route. These chain-rule substitutions do not need a global inverse of sine or cosine.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非负整数幂，奇偶性是在清点可用因子。留下一个正弦配余弦的导数，或一个余弦配正弦的导数，再用单位圆恒等式改写成对部分。两指数均偶时用降幂；这里是链式复合换元，不要求正弦或余弦有全局反函数。

![[正弦余弦幂积分]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For tangent and secant, check both the domain and the factors actually present. Positive even secant powers supply the derivative of tangent; an odd tangent power together with at least one secant supplies the derivative of secant. These two polynomial routes leave other cases to identities or integration by parts. If both exponents are zero, the integrand is simply $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正切与正割既要检查定义域，也要检查实际存在的因子。正偶数次正割提供正切的导数；奇次正切加上至少一个正割，则提供正割的导数。两条多项式路线没有覆盖全部情形，其余可能还需恒等式或分部积分；两个指数均零时，被积函数只是 $1$。

![[正切正割幂积分]]

二次根式在配方后可能适合三角代换，但角度范围与符号是方法的一部分；若直接换元更短，应优先直接换元。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A completed-square radical may suggest trigonometric substitution, but its angle range and sign branch are part of the method. Prefer a shorter direct substitution when one is available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The angle range is what makes a radical substitution unambiguous. With $a>0$, the sine, tangent and signed secant choices turn the three standard quadratic radicals into known-sign trigonometric expressions. Keep the differential and both ordered bounds with that branch; a root in a denominator still excludes its zeros. A shorter direct substitution may already handle the same integral.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

角度范围使根式代换具有明确含义。取 $a>0$ 后，正弦、正切和带符号的正割选择，把三种标准二次根式化成符号已知的三角表达式。微分与两个有向上下限都沿用同一分支；分母中的根式零点仍须排除。有时更短的直接换元已经足够。

![[三角代换]]

有理函数和乘积结构分别引出两套不同的代数改写：部分分式要完整列项；普通分部积分通常要让新积分变简单，而有限次重复后形成可解线性等式的情形另作循环模式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rational functions and product structures require different rewrites: partial fractions need complete templates, ordinary integration by parts should simplify the remaining integral, and a finite solvable cycle is treated as a separate pattern.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Partial fractions reverses adding fractions. First separate any polynomial part, then allow every power of each repeated factor; an irreducible quadratic requires a general linear numerator at every power. After multiplying away the denominator, solve a polynomial identity and recombine to check. That identity may be evaluated at a root to find coefficients, but the original fraction remains undefined there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

部分分式把通分倒过来做。先分离多项式部分，再把重复因子的每一重幂列齐；不可约二次因子的各重幂都配一般一次分子。乘掉分母后，解多项式恒等式并重新通分检查。可以向这个恒等式代入原分母的根来求系数，但原分式在该点仍无定义。

![[部分分式积分]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Integration by parts transfers a derivative using the product rule. Choose one primitive that is actually available; for the definite form, keep the complete boundary term and the same bounds on the remaining integral. A legal choice need not make the next integral easier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分部积分用乘积法则转移求导。先选定一个确实可求的原函数；定积分形式保留完整端点项，并让剩余积分使用相同上下限。合法选元不一定让下一步更容易。

![[分部积分]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some useful choices return to the original integral. A fixed lower limit makes it the same function at every step, so $I=G+cI$ determines it when the known constant $c\ne1$. When $c=1$, the identity adds no such determination; progress need not be a decrease in complexity at every step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有用的选元有时会回到原积分。固定下限，才能让各步指向同一个函数；得到 $I=G+cI$ 且已知常数 $c\ne1$ 时即可解出它。若 $c=1$，恒等式不能这样确定原积分；进展不必表现为每一步复杂度都下降。

![[循环分部积分]]

掌握常见原函数和换元技巧后，参数方程与极坐标进一步改变曲线的表示方式，因此长度、面积和微分元素也要随表示重建。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the standard antiderivative and substitution techniques are available, parametric and polar coordinates change how curves are represented, so arc length, area, and differential elements must be rebuilt for that representation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[参数曲线与极坐标.canvas]]

参数曲线先把点集变成带方向和覆盖次数的运动；切线、弧长与旋转曲面分别读取速度向量、速率和到旋转轴的距离。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Parametric curves turn a point set into an ordered traversal with direction and multiplicity. Tangents, arc length, and surfaces of revolution then use the velocity vector, speed, and distance to the axis, respectively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with a continuous map and its parameter interval. The coordinates give a position at each input; taking only the image keeps the visited points and forgets their order. Differentiability, velocity and speed enter only under additional assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从连续映射及其参数区间开始。坐标给出每个输入处的位置；只取像集会保留经过的点而忘掉顺序。可导性、速度和速率都需要另加条件。

![[参数曲线]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the unit circle to separate shape from traversal: one turn, two turns and a reversed turn have the same image. A monotone bijective change of parameter uses each old stage once; preserving nonzero velocity additionally requires a regular change of parameter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用单位圆分清形状与行进：一圈、两圈和反向一圈有同一像集。单调双射换参数把旧阶段各使用一次；要保留非零速度，还须换参数正则。

![[同轨迹不等于同参数化]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a $C^1$ interior parameter value, nonzero velocity supplies the tangent line. The condition $x'\ne0$ guarantees a local graph over $x$ and justifies $y'/x'$. A vertical tangent may exist when $x'=0$. The two zero-speed examples show why $0/0$ cannot settle the tangent question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $C^1$ 曲线的参数内点，非零速度给出切线。$x'\ne0$ 保证局部 $x$ 函数图像存在，并允许使用 $y'/x'$；$x'=0$ 时仍可能有竖直切线。两个零速例说明 $0/0$ 不能决定有无切线。

![[参数曲线切线的速度条件]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a continuous, piecewise $C^1$ path on a finite interval, integrate speed to obtain travelled distance. Zero velocity is allowed. Returning to the starting point does not cancel the trip, and retracing a whole arc counts its length again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限区间上的连续分段 $C^1$ 路径，可积分速率得到路程，允许零速度。回到起点不会抵消行程，重新走过一整段弧则须再计一次长度。

![[参数曲线弧长与重复描画]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A surface strip contributes circumference times arc length: $2\pi\rho\,ds$, where $\rho$ is the nonnegative distance to the rotation axis. Under the stated finite-interval regularity, the integral counts generated patches with multiplicity. Even a plane profile traced once can generate the same positive-area patch twice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

旋转带贡献圆周长乘弧长 $2\pi\rho\,ds$，其中 $\rho$ 是到旋转轴的非负距离。在所述有限区间正则条件下，积分按生成次数计入表面各块；即使平面母线只描一次，也可能把同一正面积表面生成两遍。

![[旋转曲面面积与重复覆盖]]

极坐标给平面点换了一种坐标表示；只有当曲线写成 $r=f(\theta)$ 时，$\theta$ 才充当曲线参数。坐标不唯一、负半径和周期共同决定实际覆盖，再由扇形微元计算面积。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Polar coordinates provide another coordinate representation for plane points. Only when a curve is written as $r=f(\theta)$ does the angle act as its parameter. Non-unique coordinates, negative radii, and periodicity determine what is actually traced before sector elements can be integrated for area.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Treat $(r,\theta)$ as instructions passed through $(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)$, with angles in radians. Distance from the origin is $|r|$; a negative radius selects the opposite ray. Recover an angle from its quadrant and both coordinate components.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $(r,\theta)$ 看作送入 $(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)$ 的指令，角度以弧度计。到原点的距离是 $|r|$，负半径取反向射线；反求角度时要结合象限与两个坐标分量。

![[极坐标]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full turns preserve direction, while a half-turn together with radial sign reversal preserves the point. A positive radius and one half-open angular turn select one representation of each non-origin point. The origin still needs its own angle convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

转整圈保持方向，转半圈并同时反转半径符号则保持点。正半径与一圈半开角区间为每个非原点选出一个表示；原点仍须单独约定角度。

![[极坐标表示不唯一]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Follow the full Cartesian point map over the allowed angle interval. For $a>0$, $r=2a\cos\theta$ traces its circle once on a closed interval of length $\pi$ and twice over $2\pi$. The scalar radius function's period alone does not determine this count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿允许的角区间追踪完整的直角坐标点映射。$a>0$ 时，$r=2a\cos\theta$ 在长度为 $\pi$ 的闭区间描圆一次，在 $2\pi$ 上两次；仅靠标量半径函数的周期不能确定这个次数。

![[极坐标曲线的覆盖与重描]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the swept radial region before integrating $r^2/2$. Continuous nonnegative outer radius over at most one positive turn gives a simple sufficient setup. Signed radii can also work after a justified split; a boundary that never repeats a point still need not sweep each interior region only once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

积分 $r^2/2$ 前先确定扫过的径向区域。连续非负外径配上不超过一圈的正角宽，是一组直接可用的充分条件。带负半径也可在合法分段后使用；边界从不重访一个点，仍不保证内部区域只扫一次。

![[极坐标面积与单次覆盖]]

### 5. 无穷级数与反常积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Infinite Series and Improper Integrals*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限区间上的积分技巧不足以处理无穷区间和无限相加，因此最后要建立收敛与误差判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Techniques for finite-interval integrals cannot by themselves handle infinite intervals or infinite sums, so the final block develops convergence and error criteria.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在处理无穷区间、反常积分与级数之前，先补上满足严格条件时计算比值极限的工具。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before studying infinite intervals, improper integrals, and series, add the ratio-limit tool that applies only under its precise hypotheses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[柯西中值定理|two-function mean value theorem]] supplies the intermediate point used in this proof. Fix one approach direction and verify the derivative assumptions before transferring a derivative-ratio limit; every repeated use needs a fresh check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明中的中间点由[[柯西中值定理]]提供。先固定趋近方向、核对导数假设，再传递导数比的极限；重复使用时每一步都须重新检查。

![[洛必达法则]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem gives sufficient conditions. If its derivative-ratio test fails, the original quotient may still converge; use a different argument to decide. The familiar 0/0 and infinity/infinity forms also have a broader denominator-divergent version, explained in the preceding proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定理给的是充分条件。导数比检验失败时，原比值仍可能收敛，需另作论证。常见的零比零与无穷比无穷形式还有更宽的分母发散版本，上一张卡的证明已解释其条件。

![[洛必达适用边界]]

级数与反常积分都用极限扩展有限运算：前者处理无限项求和，后者处理无界区间或被积函数的奇点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Series and improper integrals both extend finite operations through limits: the former handles infinitely many summands, while the latter handles unbounded intervals or singular integrands.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先从 [[反常积分.canvas|反常积分知识图]] 看清“坏点拆分—基准比较—主值边界”的关系，再按下面的原子顺序学习。课程中的具体题解仍留在 Session 91–93。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Open the [[反常积分.canvas|improper-integral map]] first to see how endpoint splitting, benchmark comparison, and the principal-value boundary connect. Then study the atoms below in order; course-specific worked solutions remain in Sessions 91–93.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 定义与坏点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Definition and problematic points*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An infinite interval or an unbounded endpoint changes the question. First every finite truncated interval must have a proper Riemann integral; then its accumulated values must tend to one finite real number. An endless interval may have finite total, while bounded oscillating truncated values can still fail to converge. The limit belongs to the integral values, not merely to the height of the curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无穷区间或无界端点带来另一步问题：先保证每个有限截断有正常黎曼积分，再看累积值是否趋于同一个有限实数。无限长区间可能总量有限，而截断值即使有界也可能振荡不收敛。取极限的是积分值，不只是曲线高度。

![[反常积分]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An antiderivative evaluates a legal finite truncation before any infinite limit is taken. For example, the reciprocal-square integral gives $1-1/R$ for finite $R>1$, then tends to $1$. An expression such as $F(\infty)$ can only abbreviate a specified limit. Keep a finite difference together when its individual terms would give divergent improper integrals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原函数先计算合法有限截断，再取无穷极限。例如倒数平方在有限 $R>1$ 时给出 $1-1/R$，之后才趋于 $1$。$F(\infty)$ 只能缩写已说明的极限。若分开的项各自形成发散反常积分，就保留原来的有限差，再检查整体极限。

![[无穷不是积分端点]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Count approaches, not just locations: an interior singularity has a left side and a right side. With finitely many problematic points, isolate every required limit, adding an ordinary intermediate point whenever one piece has two bad ends. Add the resulting values only when every independent limit is finite. Convergence at infinity cannot repair a divergent interior side.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要数逼近方向，而不只是问题位置：内部奇点有左侧和右侧。问题点有限多个时，隔离每个所需极限；一段两端都有问题，就再选普通中间点。各侧独立得到有限值之后才能相加；无穷尾端收敛，不能修复内部某侧发散。

![[反常积分逐坏点拆分]]

#### 基准与比较
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Benchmarks and comparison*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The family $x^{-p}$ provides a benchmark with an exact calculation. For any fixed real $p$ and positive finite starting point, the tail converges exactly when $p>1$. Handle $p=1$ with the logarithm instead of the power formula. Changing the positive starting point changes only a finite regular piece; a function merely tending to zero need not pass this test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

幂函数族 $x^{-p}$ 给出能够精确计算的基准。固定实数 $p$ 和正的有限起点，尾部恰在 $p>1$ 收敛；$p=1$ 须另用对数，不能代入幂公式。改变正起点只改一段正常有限积分；函数仅仅趋于零，并不足以保证收敛。

![[无穷远 p 积分判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Near zero, the same powers have the opposite threshold: the integral from $0$ to a finite $b>0$ converges exactly when $p<1$. Larger $p$ now strengthens accumulation near the endpoint. For $p\le0$ the function has a continuous endpoint extension, so no unbounded singularity is present. If both zero and infinity are included, both independent tests are required; no real $p$ passes them together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在零附近，同一幂函数族的门槛相反：从 $0$ 到有限 $b>0$ 恰在 $p<1$ 收敛，因为增大 $p$ 会加重端点附近的累积。$p\le0$ 可连续补上端点，并无无界奇点。若同时含零与无穷两端，两个独立检验都要通过；没有实数 $p$ 能同时满足。

![[零附近 p 积分判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A curve approaching the axis still has an unlimited horizontal interval over which to accumulate. For $1/x$, each doubling interval has the same positive area $\ln2$, so the total diverges although the height tends to zero. Compare this with $1/x^2$, whose tail is finite: the common height limit does not settle accumulated area.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

曲线靠近横轴，仍有无限长的水平区间可供累积。对 $1/x$，每个倍增区间都贡献相同的正面积 $\ln2$，所以高度虽趋零，总量仍发散。与尾部有限的 $1/x^2$ 对照可见：相同的高度极限不能判定累积面积。

![[趋零不推尾积分收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The reverse implication also needs its own check. Place continuous triangles of height one at successive integers, but make their widths shrink geometrically. Their areas add to a finite total while peaks keep reappearing. Even continuity and nonnegativity alone therefore do not make a convergent tail force $f(x)\to0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反方向也须单独检查：在相继整数位置放置高为一的连续三角形，同时让底宽按几何比例缩小。面积和可以有限，峰顶却不断出现。因此即使连续、非负，尾积分收敛也不能单独迫使 $f(x)\to0$。

![[尾积分收敛不推函数趋零]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To control an unknown nonnegative accumulation, choose a justified bound on the same tail or singular side. A larger function with finite integral proves convergence; a smaller one with infinite integral proves divergence. These directions follow from every proper finite truncation. A convergent lower bound or divergent upper bound leaves the unknown integral undecided.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

控制未知的非负累积时，应在同一尾部或奇点侧选择有依据的界。积分有限的较大函数证明收敛，积分无穷的较小函数证明发散；方向来自每个正常有限截断上的保序性。收敛下界或发散上界都不能决定未知积分。

![[反常积分直接比较判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sometimes the ratio is easier to simplify than an inequality is to guess. For locally integrable functions that are positive near the same problematic end, a ratio tending to $0<c<\infty$ traps one between two positive multiples of the other. This gives equivalent convergence, not equal values. A ratio tending to zero or infinity does not give that two-sided conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有时化简比值比猜不等式容易。对同一问题端附近为正、正常有限截断可积的函数，若比值趋于 $0<c<\infty$，一个函数最终被另一个的两个正常数倍夹住。这给出同敛散，不是数值相等；比值趋于零或无穷时，没有这个双向结论。

![[反常积分极限比较判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Comparison can start beyond an inconvenient finite prefix only after that prefix has a well-defined finite integral. Moving the start then adds or subtracts a fixed finite number and leaves tail convergence unchanged. A finite interval containing an unexamined singularity cannot be discarded. Isolate each problematic approach before applying its local test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确认初始片段有定义明确的有限积分，才可越过它开始比较。移动起点只是加减固定有限数，不改变尾部敛散。若有限区间藏着尚未检查的奇点，就不能略去；应先隔离每个问题方向，再作局部检验。

![[反常积分敛散的局部性]]

#### 奇点与定义边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Singularities and definitional boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A principal value specifies symmetry before taking the limit. Around one interior singularity it removes equal distances on the two sides; at infinity the standard truncation is $[-R,R]$. For $1/x$ on $[-1,1]$, equal cutoffs give zero, but a right cutoff twice the left gives $-\ln2$. Only the equal-distance rule here is the standard principal value; neither result creates the missing separate improper limits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主值先规定对称规则，再取极限：单个内部奇点两侧挖去等距离，无穷远则用标准 $[-R,R]$ 截断。$[-1,1]$ 上的 $1/x$ 用相等截断得到零，右截断取左侧两倍却得到 $-\ln2$。这里仅等距离规则是标准主值；两种结果都不能补出缺失的各侧反常极限。

![[Cauchy 主值与反常积分]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Correct derivatives on separate components do not justify subtracting only the two outer endpoint values. Compute each proper truncation and inspect the inner limits. When those limits are finite, constants may be adjusted to align them and join the pieces; infinite or oscillating limits cannot be fixed this way. The false negative result from integrating $1/x^2$ across zero shows why checking the interval hypotheses comes first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两段的导数各自正确，不自动允许只相减两个外端点值。先算正常截断，再检查内侧极限；极限有限时，才可调常数使它们对齐并连接。无穷或振荡极限不能这样修复。跨零对 $1/x^2$ 算出负结果，说明必须先检查整段区间前提。

![[跨奇点积分拆分]]

#### 从部分和进入无穷级数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From partial sums to infinite series*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Session 94 开始把积分中的“先截断、再取极限”用于求和。需要连读解释、证明与例子时，打开 [[01_Math/01_calculus/05_无穷级数与幂级数|无穷级数与幂级数学习路径]]；用 [[无穷级数与幂级数.canvas|主题图]] 回看判别条件与函数展开的关系。以下按学习依赖嵌入同一组共享原子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Session 94 applies truncation and limits to summation. Read the [[01_Math/01_calculus/05_无穷级数与幂级数|continuous series chapter]] for explanation, proofs, and examples, and use the [[无穷级数与幂级数.canvas|topic map]] for relationships. The shared atoms below follow the learning dependencies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with an ordered list and its running totals. Adding $1/2,1/4,1/8,\ldots$ makes the terms approach zero and the partial sums approach one: those are different limits. An infinite series is assigned a finite sum only through convergence of the whole sequence of finite partial sums, with the starting index kept explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先区分有序列表与累计总量。依次加入 $1/2,1/4,1/8,\ldots$ 时，通项趋零而部分和趋一，这是两个不同极限。无穷级数只有通过全部有限部分和的收敛才得到有限和，起始索引也必须明确。

![[无穷级数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One new term is the difference between two adjacent totals. If both totals approach the same finite limit, their difference must vanish. This gives a quick rejection test when the terms fail to tend to zero, but passing that necessary condition leaves convergence undecided. The harmonic series and the geometric example above supply opposite outcomes with the same zero term limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

新增一项是两个相邻累计值之差。若两者趋于同一个有限极限，差就必须趋零。因此通项不趋零时可以快速排除收敛；满足这项必要条件后却仍未判定。调和级数与前述几何例的通项都趋零，敛散结果相反。

![[级数通项判别]]

先求得有限部分和，才有理由讨论无限和。有限 [[几何和]] 与无限几何级数使用不同的条件；望远镜结构也必须保留截断时的末项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with finite partial sums. A [[几何和|finite geometric sum]] and its infinite series have different conditions; telescoping also retains the final truncated term.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[几何级数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A second useful structure appears when neighbouring pieces cancel inside each finite sum. For $1/[n(n+1)]$, the first $N$ terms leave $1-1/(N+1)$: the last term must remain until its limit is taken. Other telescoping patterns leave several terms at each end. Cancellation supplies a shorter expression; checking its limiting boundary values decides convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一种有用结构，是在每个有限部分和里让相邻片段抵消。$1/[n(n+1)]$ 的前 $N$ 项留下 $1-1/(N+1)$；最后一项必须保留到取极限时。其他望远镜结构可能每端留下多项。消项只得到较短表达式，边界值的极限才决定收敛。

![[望远镜级数]]

#### 非负尾部：用已知基准与面积控制累积
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Nonnegative tails: control accumulation by benchmarks and areas*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

调和级数是通项趋零仍发散的基准，具体增长与堆叠应用复用 [[调和数对数增长]]。Session 95 的判别法围绕“尾部由谁夹住”展开，Session 96 再把调和和式放回砖块质心的几何问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The harmonic series is the benchmark for divergence despite vanishing terms; its growth and stacking application reuse [[调和数对数增长|harmonic-number growth]]. Session 95 controls tails through comparison, and Session 96 returns the sum to a centre-of-mass problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nonnegative tails, compare accumulated totals by comparing each term in the same direction. A summable upper bound proves convergence; a divergent lower bound proves divergence. The inequalities need only hold eventually, while every earlier term must remain a defined finite number. A divergent upper bound or convergent lower bound alone settles neither outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非负尾部，逐项同向比较就能比较累计量：可求和的上界证明收敛，发散的下界证明发散。不等式只须最终成立，但之前每项仍须有定义且有限。仅有发散上界或收敛下界，都不能决定结论。

![[级数直接比较判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a ratio is easier to simplify, choose a positive reference tail. A ratio tending to a finite positive constant eventually traps the target between two positive multiples of that reference, so both series have the same convergence behaviour. The constant need not be one. Ratios tending to zero or infinity do not give this two-sided equivalence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若比值更容易化简，可选一个正的参考尾部。比值趋于有限正常数，就把目标最终夹在参考项的两个正常数倍之间，从而同敛散；该常数不必等于一。比值趋零或无穷时，没有这项双向等价。

![[级数极限比较判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sampled decreasing curve gives another comparison: unit-width rectangles sit above or below its area. With a positive continuous nonincreasing interpolating function on a whole tail, this links convergence of the sampled series to convergence of its improper integral. Finite sums and proper truncated integrals establish the inequalities before any infinite limit is taken; their limiting values need not coincide.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采样递减曲线提供另一种比较：宽度为一的矩形位于相应曲线面积之上或之下。若整个尾部有正、连续、单调不增的插值函数，就能联系采样级数与反常积分的敛散。先用有限和及正常截断积分建立不等式，再取无穷极限；最终数值不必相同。

![[级数积分判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply that argument to reciprocal powers with a fixed exponent. For positive $p$, the integral of $x^{-p}$ changes from infinite to finite precisely above one. At $p=1$ the logarithm still grows without bound; for $p\le0$, the terms themselves do not vanish. These separate cases establish the criterion for every real $p$, and provide standard comparison tails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把论证用于固定指数的倒数幂。正 $p$ 时，$x^{-p}$ 的尾积分恰在指数超过一时由无穷转为有限；$p=1$ 的对数仍无界，$p\le0$ 则连通项趋零都不满足。分别处理这些情况，才能得到全部实数 $p$ 的判据，并把它们用作常见比较基准。

![[p级数判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After proving convergence, ask how much a partial sum has omitted. Under the integral-test tail conditions, the first missing term is indexed $N+1$: integrating from there gives a lower error bound, while starting at $N$ gives an upper bound. For $1/n^2$, the remainder lies between $1/(N+1)$ and $1/N$. An upper bound below a requested tolerance guarantees accuracy; an upper bound above it does not prove the true error is too large.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明收敛后，再问部分和遗漏了多少。在积分判别的尾部条件下，首个漏项的索引为 $N+1$；从这里积分给误差下界，从 $N$ 开始给上界。对 $1/n^2$，余项位于 $1/(N+1)$ 与 $1/N$ 之间。上界低于要求可保证精度，上界超过要求却不证明真实误差超标。

![[积分判别余项界]]

#### 带符号的级数：先分清两种收敛
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Signed series: distinguish the two convergence types*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对带符号的项，非负项工具可以先用于绝对值。绝对值级数收敛是很强的保证；若它发散，就要继续分析原顺序的抵消，不能立即否定原级数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For signed terms, nonnegative tools may first be applied to magnitudes. Absolute convergence gives a strong guarantee; if it fails, the original order's cancellation still needs examination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep two running totals separate: one adds the original terms, while the other adds their magnitudes. Absolute convergence means the latter total has a finite limit, and it applies to complex magnitudes as well as real absolute values. It does not mean cancellation disappears or that the magnitude of the original sum equals the sum of magnitudes. The next theorem explains why this stronger control guarantees ordinary convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分清两个累计量：一个相加原项，另一个相加各项大小。绝对收敛要求后者具有有限极限，既适用于实数绝对值，也适用于复数的模。它不表示抵消消失，也不表示原和的模等于各项模的和。接下来的定理说明，这种更强控制为何保证普通收敛。

![[绝对收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The important control concerns whole tails, not individual terms. For any two late partial sums, their difference has magnitude no larger than the magnitude tail between them. A finite total magnitude makes this uniformly small, so completeness supplies a limit, even for complex terms. The same estimate bounds the remaining error after a cutoff.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键是控制整段尾和，而不只是单项。两个较晚部分和之差的大小，不超过它们之间的幅度尾和。总幅度有限，就能统一压小这种差，再由完备性得到极限，复数项也成立。同一估计还能控制截断后的余量。

![[绝对收敛推出收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the magnitude total grows without bound, return to the original order instead of declaring divergence immediately. Conditional convergence requires a finite limit for the original partial sums and divergence of the magnitude series. The alternating harmonic example establishes those conclusions separately. Neither alternating signs alone nor failure of absolute convergence alone establishes conditional convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果幅度累计值无界增长，应回到原顺序继续判断，而不能立即宣布原级数发散。条件收敛必须同时满足原部分和趋于有限值、绝对值级数发散；交错调和例将分别完成两步。只有符号交错，或只有不绝对收敛，都不足以独自作出这个分类。

![[条件收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Alternation becomes useful when the step sizes eventually never increase and tend to zero. Even and odd partial sums then approach from opposite sides, with a vanishing gap, so all partial sums share a finite limit. Equal step sizes and a tail that becomes zero cause no difficulty. This proves convergence; identifying its type still requires examining the magnitude series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当步幅最终不再增大，并趋于零时，符号交替就能发挥作用。奇偶部分和从两侧靠近，间隔趋零，因此全部部分和具有共同的有限极限。相等步幅或最终变为零的尾部都允许。这先证明收敛；判断类型仍要考察绝对值级数。

![[交错级数判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same enclosing interval gives a quantitative guarantee once the cutoff lies in the controlled tail. After the first $N$ terms, the next magnitude $b_{N+1}$ bounds the absolute remainder. For alternating reciprocal squares, $31$ terms give the bound $1/1024<0.001$, with the true sum between $S_{31}$ and $S_{32}$. A bound above the tolerance does not prove the true error is too large; numerical roundoff remains a separate issue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

截断点位于受控尾部时，同一夹逼区间还能给出定量保证。加完前 $N$ 项后，下一项幅度 $b_{N+1}$ 控制绝对余项。交错平方倒数的前31项给出 $1/1024<0.001$ 的界，真值夹在 $S_{31}$ 与 $S_{32}$ 之间。上界超标不证明真实误差超标，数值舍入也仍须另行考虑。

![[交错级数误差界]]

在原顺序下估误差，与把整条无穷序列重新排列，是不同操作。下面两张卡分别说明何时顺序无关、何时顺序会改变结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Estimating a remainder in the original order differs from rearranging the infinite sequence. The next two atoms identify when the sum is order-independent and when it can change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A valid rearrangement is a bijection of the original indices: each appears exactly once, at a finite position. Under absolute convergence, every sufficiently long new partial sum includes any chosen original finite prefix; the remaining selected terms are controlled by the absolute tail. Both the sum and the magnitude total are preserved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有效重排是原下标的双射：每个下标在有限位置恰好出现一次。绝对收敛时，每个足够长的新部分和都已收齐任意选定的原有限前缀，剩余选中项由绝对尾和控制。因此原和与幅度总和都保持不变。

![[绝对收敛级数重排]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a conditionally convergent real series, positive contributions and negative magnitudes each have unbounded totals. To reach a chosen real target, take unused positive terms in their original order until crossing above it, then unused negative terms in their original order until crossing below, and repeat. Insert any zero terms along the way. Shrinking crossing errors control every late partial sum while every original index is eventually used. This is a separate theorem about order, not a change to finite addition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实数级数条件收敛时，正项总量与负项幅度总量都无界。为达到选定的实数目标，分别按原顺序取尚未使用的正项越到上方，再取负项越到下方，如此重复，并在过程中插入所有零项。跨越误差缩小，控制所有后期部分和，同时每个原下标最终都被用到。这是关于无穷顺序的另一定理，并未改变有限加法。

![[条件收敛重排]]

#### 从几何衰减走向可执行的选法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From geometric decay to a practical choice of test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阶乘、指数和整体的 $n$ 次幂常适合比值或根值判别。它们控制的是绝对收敛；临界值为一时需要换方法。选择程序把前面的工具放进同一条判断路线，而不是要求逐个试完。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Factorials, exponentials, and nth powers often suit ratio or root tests. These establish absolute convergence, and the critical value one calls for another method. The selection procedure organizes the tools into a reasoning path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adjacent division can reveal a geometric bound that was hard to see in the terms themselves. For magnitudes $3^n/n!$, the ratio is $3/(n+1)$ and eventually at most $1/2$. The ratio-limit version requires eventually nonzero terms; a limit below one proves absolute convergence, above one proves divergence, and equality leaves the question open.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相邻相除可能显出通项中不易看见的几何控制。例如幅度 $3^n/n!$ 的比值是 $3/(n+1)$，最终至多为 $1/2$。比值极限版本要求各项最终非零；极限小于一得绝对收敛，大于一得发散，等于一仍需另判。

![[级数比值判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before allowing oscillating root magnitudes, define their limit superior. Take the supremum over each entire tail and then move the starting index forward. This can have a well-defined extended-real limit even when the original sequence oscillates; it is not simply the largest value in the whole sequence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了允许根值振荡，先定义上极限：对每个完整尾部取上确界，再让起点后移。即使原数列振荡，这些尾部上界也有明确的扩展实数极限；它不是整列数值中的一个最大值。

![[数列上极限]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply the upper-limit idea to $|a_n|^{1/n}$. If its limsup is below one, all sufficiently late magnitudes lie beneath one convergent geometric sequence. If it is above one, large terms occur arbitrarily late and cannot tend to zero. Zeros are allowed: odd terms zero and even terms $2^{-n}$ have root magnitudes alternating between zero and $1/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把上极限用于 $|a_n|^{1/n}$。小于一时，足够晚的全部幅度被同一收敛几何数列压住；大于一时，任意靠后仍有大项，因此通项不能趋零。这里允许零项：奇数项为零、偶数项为 $2^{-n}$ 时，根值在零与 $1/2$ 间交替。

![[级数根值判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful test matches a feature of the terms to a verified hypothesis. Keep the goal explicit: deciding convergence, proving absolute or conditional convergence, finding a sum, and controlling truncation error are different tasks. An inconclusive value of one, an uncomputed term limit, or failure of a chosen test is a reason to find another justified argument, not a verdict of divergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有效选法是把通项特征与已核对的定理条件相接。始终分清任务：判断收敛、证明绝对或条件收敛、求和、控制截断误差并不相同。临界值一、尚未算出的通项极限，或某个判别不适用，都应促使寻找另一条有根据的论证，而不是宣布发散。

![[级数判别法选择]]

#### 幂级数：先确定输入范围
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Power series: determine the input range first*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Session 97 将输入 $x$ 放进每一项，得到一族数项级数。先求中心附近的收敛半径，再把端点各自代回；完整收敛区间不必左右对称。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Session 97 inserts an input into every term, producing a family of numerical series. Find the radius, then test each endpoint separately; the complete convergence interval need not be symmetric.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The centre and coefficients stay fixed while the input varies. For each chosen input, convergence still means a limit of finite partial sums. At the centre every positive power vanishes, so the constant term gives a value even when no surrounding input works.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中心和系数保持固定，变化的是输入。每个输入处的收敛，仍指有限部分和的极限。在中心处所有正次幂都消失，因此即使周围没有别的收敛输入，常数项仍给出一个值。

![[幂级数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The radius is a distance from the centre, not the coordinate of an endpoint. A convergent point away from the centre gives geometric control at every smaller distance. The resulting threshold may be zero or infinite; a rational expression can still be defined beyond the region represented by its particular series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半径是到中心的距离，不是端点坐标。一个非中心收敛点能通过几何比较控制更近的全部输入；因此得到的阈值也可能为零或无穷。有理式在展开区域之外仍可能有定义，但这一份级数未必在那里表示它。

![[幂级数收敛半径]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only a finite positive radius leaves two finite endpoints to test. Substitution can produce different signed series on the two sides, although their magnitude series agree. Even when an endpoint series converges, identifying its sum with a proposed function value is a further step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有有限正半径才留下两个有限端点。左右代入可能产生不同符号的数项级数，但其绝对值级数相同。即使某个端点级数收敛，把它的和认定为目标函数值仍是下一步。

![[幂级数端点判别]]

#### Taylor 表示：从有限匹配到余项趋零
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Taylor representation: from finite matching to vanishing remainders*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Sessions 98–99 复用本课程前面已经定义的 [[Taylor多项式]] 与 [[Taylor余项]]。无限 Taylor 级数以这些多项式为部分和，但仍需证明余项随次数消失，才能把级数的和认作原函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sessions 98–99 reuse the existing [[Taylor多项式|Taylor polynomial]] and [[Taylor余项|remainder theorem]]. An infinite Taylor series has those polynomials as partial sums, but identifying its sum with the function requires vanishing remainders.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivatives at the centre specify one candidate coefficient sequence: divide the derivative of order $n$ by $n!$. The first $N+1$ terms form the degree-at-most-$N$ Taylor polynomial. Forming all those coefficients answers a definition question; convergence and equality are still separate questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中心处的导数确定一套候选系数：$n$ 阶导数除以 $n!$。前 $N+1$ 项组成次数至多为 $N$ 的 Taylor 多项式。构造所有系数解决的是定义问题，收敛和等于函数仍要分别判断。

![[Taylor级数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the input before sending the order to infinity. The exact difference $f(x)-P_N(x)$ must approach zero for the partial sums to represent $f(x)$. A derivative bound may change with $N$, so the whole remainder estimate must tend to zero; the factorial alone does not settle it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定输入，再让阶数趋于无穷。部分和要表示 $f(x)$，精确差值 $f(x)-P_N(x)$ 就必须趋零。导数上界可能随 $N$ 变化，因此要考察整个余项估计，不能只看阶乘。

![[Taylor级数等于函数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The flat function $f(0)=0$, $f(x)=e^{-1/x^2}$ for $x\ne0$, shows why that last check matters. All derivatives at zero vanish, so its Taylor series converges everywhere to zero. The function is positive at every nonzero input: convergence of the candidate is not enough for representation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零点补零的 $e^{-1/x^2}$ 说明了最后一步为什么必要。零点所有阶导数均为零，因此 Taylor 级数处处收敛到零；但每个非零输入的函数值都为正。候选级数收敛，还不足以表示原函数。

![[光滑不代表解析]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the exponential, the remainder can be controlled on the entire segment joining zero to the chosen input. The bound $e^{|x|}|x|^{N+1}/(N+1)!$ tends to zero at each fixed real $x$, proving the equality as well as giving finite-order error control. At zero the sum is directly one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指数函数的余项可以在零与目标输入之间的整段上控制。上界 $e^{|x|}|x|^{N+1}/(N+1)!$ 对每个固定实数 $x$ 趋零，既证明级数等式，也控制有限阶误差。零点直接得到和为一。

![[指数函数幂级数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For sine, measured in radians, the four-step derivative cycle leaves only alternating odd powers. Every derivative is bounded in magnitude by one, so the finite Taylor remainder proves the identity for every real input. A chosen truncation still needs its own error estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正弦使用弧度制，四步导数循环只留下交错奇次幂。各阶导数绝对值不超过一，因此有限 Taylor 余项证明等式对所有实输入成立；选定的有限截断仍须配套误差估计。

![[正弦函数幂级数]]

#### 用运算得到新的函数展开
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Generate new representations through valid operations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Session 100 在严格的收敛半径内部进行求导、积分、乘法与代入。把正弦求导得到余弦，把几何级数积分得到对数；每次操作都保留范围说明，端点另查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Session 100 performs differentiation, integration, multiplication, and substitution within their valid ranges. Differentiating sine gives cosine; integrating a geometric series gives a logarithm. Track the domain and retest endpoints after each operation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With positive radius, a common tail estimate on a smaller interval justifies differentiating the infinite sum at every interior point. The coefficients change as in a finite polynomial and the radius stays the same. Endpoint behaviour must be checked anew, even if the ordinary function is differentiable there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半径为正时，较小区间上的共同尾界保证在每个内点对无穷和求导合法。系数按有限多项式规则变化，半径保持不变；即使普通函数在端点可导，导数级数的端点行为仍须重查。

![[幂级数逐项求导]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiating the sine series is valid at every finite real input because its radius is infinite. The result starts with one and then alternates through even powers. This establishes the cosine representation; it does not make one fixed-degree polynomial equally accurate on the whole unbounded real line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正弦级数半径为无穷，因此每个有限实输入都允许逐项求导。得到的余弦展开从一开始，随后是交错偶次幂。这证明余弦表示成立，并不表示一个固定次数的多项式能在整条无界实轴上保持相同精度。

![[余弦函数幂级数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Integration uses an error bound valid over the whole finite segment inside the radius. Starting at the centre fixes the accumulated integral there to zero; an arbitrary constant recovers all antiderivatives on the interior interval. An infinite radius still does not by itself authorize an integral to infinity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

积分需要一个对严格内部整段有限区间都有效的误差界。从中心起积使累积函数在中心为零，再加任意常数得到内部区间上的全部原函数。即使半径无穷，也不能仅凭半径把积分上限换成无穷。

![[幂级数逐项积分]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Integrating the geometric expansion of $1/(1+x)$ gives the logarithm identity strictly inside $|x|<1$. Integrating a finite geometric identity, with its remainder retained, separately proves the value $\ln2$ at $x=1$. The resulting interval $(-1,1]$ is smaller than the ordinary function domain $x>-1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $1/(1+x)$ 的几何展开积分，先得到严格内部 $|x|<1$ 的对数等式。保留有限几何恒等式的余项再积分，另外证明 $x=1$ 的和为 $\ln2$。最终展开区间 $(-1,1]$ 小于普通函数定义域 $x>-1$。

![[对数函数幂级数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two series with the same centre, collect every pair of terms whose degrees add to the desired degree. Absolute tails justify this collection in the common interior. Cancellation may enlarge the resulting radius; that extension does not turn a divergent original factor into a convergent numerical series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个同中心级数相乘，要收齐所有次数之和等于目标次数的配对。共同内部的绝对尾和保证整理合法。抵消可能扩大所得级数的半径，但不能因此把原来发散的因子当作收敛数项级数求值。

![[幂级数乘法]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substitution changes both the entire term and the allowed input. In the geometric identity, replacing the input by $4x^2$ changes the condition to $|x|<1/2$. A fixed admissible substitution is already justified by the original sum; expanding a more general inner function and collecting infinitely many new powers would be another operation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

代入要同时替换整个通项和有效输入范围。几何恒等式代入 $4x^2$ 后，条件变为 $|x|<1/2$。固定一个允许的输入时，原恒等式已经保证代入成立；若还要展开一般内函数并收集无穷多新幂次，则是另一个需要依据的操作。

![[幂级数代入]]

高斯形状的积分没有初等原函数，但仍能先定义一个函数，再用级数计算。这里复用 [[高斯积分]] 的归一化结论；全轴收敛半径并不自动允许把逐项积分的上限换成无穷。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Gaussian kernel has no elementary antiderivative, but its integral defines a function that can be computed by a series. Its normalization reuses the [[高斯积分|Gaussian integral]]. An infinite radius alone does not justify termwise integration to infinity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The normalization $2/\sqrt\pi$ makes the positive infinite-end limit one, but every finite input gives a value strictly between minus one and one. It takes negative values at negative inputs, so it is not itself a cumulative probability distribution. For the standard normal distribution function $\Phi$, the precise conversion is $\Phi(z)=[1+\operatorname{erf}(z/\sqrt2)]/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

归一化倍数 $2/\sqrt\pi$ 使正无穷端的极限为一，但有限输入的值严格位于负一与一之间。负输入会给出负值，因此它本身不是累积概率分布函数。对标准正态分布函数 $\Phi$，准确换算为 $\Phi(z)=[1+\operatorname{erf}(z/\sqrt2)]/2$。

![[误差函数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After substituting $u=-t^2$ into the exponential, integrate over the finite segment from zero to $x$. Integration supplies the factor $2n+1$, in addition to the existing $n!$. A separate finite Taylor argument bounds truncation at every real input; the alternating-series shortcut needs a decreasing tail. Neither estimate controls numerical rounding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在指数展开中代入 $u=-t^2$ 后，再沿零到 $x$ 的有限段积分。积分补上因子 $2n+1$，与原有的 $n!$ 相乘。独立的有限 Taylor 论证在全部实输入处给出截断界；交错级数捷径则须满足单调尾部。两类估计都不控制数值舍入。

![[误差函数幂级数]]
