### 2. 导数的应用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Applications of Derivatives*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

导数给出局部变化率；应用部分把这个局部模型用于近似、曲线判断和优化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Derivatives give local rates of change; this block applies that local model to approximation, curve analysis, and optimisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[导数的应用]]

### 3. 定积分与微积分基本定理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Definite Integrals and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

导数描述瞬时变化，定积分描述变化的累积；微积分基本定理把两种运算接起来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Derivatives describe instantaneous change, while definite integrals describe accumulated change; the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus connects the two operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[定积分与微积分基本定理]]

### 4. 积分方法、参数曲线与极坐标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Integration Techniques, Parametric Curves, and Polar Coordinates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本定理说明为何可由原函数算积分；积分方法进一步解决找不到直接原函数以及非直角坐标的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Fundamental Theorem explains why an antiderivative can be used to evaluate an integral. Integration techniques then address cases without an immediate antiderivative and problems expressed in non-Cartesian coordinates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[积分方法、参数曲线与极坐标]]

### 5. 无穷级数与反常积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Infinite Series and Improper Integrals*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限区间上的积分技巧不足以处理无穷区间和无限相加，因此最后要建立收敛与误差判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Techniques for finite-interval integrals cannot by themselves handle infinite intervals or infinite sums, so the final block develops convergence and error criteria.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[无穷级数与反常积分]]
