# 定积分与应用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Definite integrals and their applications*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[03_Definite_Integrals|原始课程记录：Unit 3 · Sessions 43–67]] · [[定积分与应用.canvas|主题图]] · [[02_导数的应用|上一主题：导数的应用]] · [[04_Techniques_of_Integration|下一主题：积分技巧]]

上一主题从导数读出局部变化；这一主题反过来问：知道每一小段的变化率、密度或截面，怎样恢复整个区间的总量？课程先用有限和定义这种累积，再证明它与求导的联系，最后把同一个结构用于几何、平均、概率和数值计算。这里按课堂顺序连成一条阅读路径；逐课练习、原题与讲义仍保存在上方原始课程记录。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The previous topic used derivatives to describe local change. This topic asks the reverse question: given a rate, density, or cross-section on each small interval, how do we recover a total? The course defines accumulation through finite sums, proves its connection with differentiation, and applies the same structure to geometry, averages, probability, and numerical computation. This page follows the teaching sequence; session exercises, original questions, and lecture materials remain in the linked course record.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 从有限累积走到定积分：Sessions 43–46
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From finite accumulation to a definite integral*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先不要找原函数。若速度在一小段时间内近似不变，该段位移约为“速度 × 时间”；若曲线在窄条内近似水平，小条有向面积约为“高度 × 宽度”。将 $[a,b]$ 分成 $a=x_0<\cdots<x_N=b$，在每段取样本点 $\xi_i$，就得到[[黎曼和]]：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Do not look for an antiderivative yet. When velocity is nearly constant over a short time interval, displacement is approximately velocity times elapsed time. Likewise, a narrow strip has signed area approximately height times width. A partition $a=x_0<\cdots<x_N=b$ and one sample point $\xi_i$ in each subinterval produce a [[黎曼和|Riemann sum]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{N}f(\xi_i)\Delta x_i,
\qquad \Delta x_i=x_i-x_{i-1}.
$$

[[定积分]]不是某一种取点方法的有限和，而是所有足够细的带样本分割都趋向的同一个数。这里“足够细”指最大段宽 $\max_i\Delta x_i\to0$，不只是段数增加。对有限闭区间上的有界函数，如果这个共同极限存在，就记作 $\int_a^bf(x)\,dx$。[[连续函数可积]]提供本单元最常用的充分条件：连续性在紧区间上给出一致连续性，使每条小区间内的最高、最低函数值之差一起变小；总的上下近似差因此趋于零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[定积分|definite integral]] is not one finite sampling sum. It is the common limit approached by every sufficiently fine tagged partition. Fineness means $\max_i\Delta x_i\to0$, not merely more subintervals. For a bounded function on a finite closed interval, this common limit, when it exists, is denoted by $\int_a^bf(x)\,dx$. The result that [[连续函数可积|continuous functions are integrable]] supplies the usual sufficient condition: uniform continuity on the compact interval makes the oscillation on every short subinterval small, forcing the gap between upper and lower approximations to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

贯穿例子取 $f(x)=x^2$、$0\le x\le1$，先把坐标视为无量纲。等分并取右端点，平方和公式给出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Our running example is $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$, initially with dimensionless coordinates. Equal subdivisions and right-endpoint sampling give, using the sum of squares,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left(\frac iN\right)^2
=\frac{N(N+1)(2N+1)}{6N^3}
\longrightarrow\frac13.
$$

所以 $\int_0^1x^2dx=1/3$。计算时用了一列右端点和，但其极限确实等于定积分，还依赖 $x^2$ 连续可积。反方向遇到极限和式时，[[和式识别为积分]]的关键是先找“宽度”和“位置”：例如 $\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N(2+3i/N)^2$ 可以直接看成 $\int_0^1(2+3t)^2dt$；若改用区间 $[2,5]$，宽度是 $3/N$，外面就必须补系数 $1/3$，答案为 $13$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $\int_0^1x^2dx=1/3$. We calculated one sequence of right-endpoint sums; continuity and integrability justify identifying its limit with the integral. In the reverse direction, [[和式识别为积分|recognising an integral in a sum]] begins with width and position. For example, $N^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^N(2+3i/N)^2$ represents $\int_0^1(2+3t)^2dt$. If the interval is instead taken as $[2,5]$, its width is $3/N$, so an outside factor $1/3$ is required. The value is $13$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 用端点差计算，但先守住符号与条件：Sessions 47–50
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Endpoint evaluation, signs, and conditions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每次从平方和起算太费力。[[原函数]]把问题改写成“谁求导后等于 $f$？”若 $f$ 在 $[a,b]$ 连续，$F$ 在闭区间连续且在内部满足 $F'=f$，[[Newton-Leibniz公式|FTC I：Newton–Leibniz 公式]]告诉我们
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rebuilding every integral from finite sums is laborious. An [[原函数|antiderivative]] asks which function differentiates to $f$. If $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$ and $F$ is continuous there with $F'=f$ in the interior, [[Newton-Leibniz公式|FTC I, the Newton–Leibniz formula]], gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\int_a^bf(x)\,dx=F(b)-F(a).
$$

于是贯穿例子立即变成 $[x^3/3]_0^1=1/3$。这里端点差是定理的结论，不是前面定积分的定义。加在原函数上的常数会在端点相减时消失；下一节会说明为什么所有原函数的差恰好只能是常数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The running example now reduces to $[x^3/3]_0^1=1/3$. Endpoint evaluation is a theorem about the previously defined integral, not its definition. An additive constant cancels between endpoints; the next section explains why two antiderivatives can differ only by a constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限和保留的代数结构也留在积分中。[[定积分线性性]]允许把可积函数的常数倍与相加拆开，如 $\int_0^1(3x^2-2)dx=-1$；它不允许把两个函数的乘积拆成两个积分的乘积。若函数在包含所有端点与分点的闭区间上可积，[[定积分区间可加性]]就允许沿该分点拆分。配合 $\int_b^af=-\int_a^bf$ 和 $\int_a^af=0$，它也处理反向端点，而不必假设分点总在两个端点之间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The algebra of finite sums survives in integrals. [[定积分线性性|Linearity]] permits constant multiples and sums of integrable functions to be separated, giving, for example, $\int_0^1(3x^2-2)dx=-1$. It does not turn a product into a product of integrals. If the function is integrable on a closed interval containing all endpoints and the splitting point, [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]] permits splitting there. Together with $\int_b^af=-\int_a^bf$ and $\int_a^af=0$, it also handles oriented bounds without requiring the splitting point to lie between the endpoints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

符号有实际意义。对于 $C^1$ 位置函数 $s$、速度 $v=s'$，[[净变化与总变化]]分别是 $\int_a^bv(t)dt$ 与 $\int_a^b|v(t)|dt$。例如 $v(t)=2t-2$、$0\le t\le2$，前半段后退、后半段前进，位移为 $0$，总路程为 $2$。同样，曲线与横轴的几何面积用绝对值；正负区域不能在面积问题中相互抵消。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Signs carry physical meaning. For a $C^1$ position function $s$ and velocity $v=s'$, [[净变化与总变化|net change and total variation]] are $\int_a^bv(t)dt$ and $\int_a^b|v(t)|dt$. With $v(t)=2t-2$ on $[0,2]$, motion is backward and then forward: displacement is $0$, but distance travelled is $2$. Similarly, geometric area between a curve and the horizontal axis uses an absolute value; positive and negative regions cannot cancel in an area calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不求原函数也能比较总量。设 $a<b$，且 $f,g$ 在 $[a,b]$ 可积，[[积分保序性]]把整段上的 $f\le g$ 变成 $\int_a^bf\le\int_a^bg$；取 $-|f|\le f\le|f|$ 就得到[[积分绝对值界]]。进一步，[[积分上下界估计]]把 $m\le f\le M$ 转成 $m(b-a)\le\int_a^bf\le M(b-a)$。例如 $1/(1+x)$ 在 $[0,4]$ 介于 $1/5$ 与 $1$，所以 $4/5\le\ln5\le4$；在 $x=1$ 分段后，界可收紧到 $11/10\le\ln5\le5/2$。更细的局部信息，给出更紧的整体估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Totals can be compared without an antiderivative. For $a<b$ and functions integrable on $[a,b]$, [[积分保序性|order preservation]] converts a pointwise bound $f\le g$ into $\int_a^bf\le\int_a^bg$. Applying this to $-|f|\le f\le|f|$ gives the [[积分绝对值界|absolute-value inequality]]. More generally, [[积分上下界估计|bounding an integral]] converts $m\le f\le M$ into $m(b-a)\le\int_a^bf\le M(b-a)$. Since $1/(1+x)$ lies between $1/5$ and $1$ on $[0,4]$, we get $4/5\le\ln5\le4$. Splitting at $x=1$ improves this to $11/10\le\ln5\le5/2$. Better local information produces a tighter global estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[奇偶函数积分]]利用的是区间和函数同时具有的对称性：在整个 $[-a,a]$ 可积的奇函数积分为零，偶函数积分是右半段的两倍。一定先检查积分存在；$1/x$ 在 $0$ 有奇点，$\int_{-1}^1dx/x$ 不能凭奇性写成 $0$，而须按[[反常积分]]分别检查左右极限。类似地，跨过奇点直接代原函数端点，不满足本节的 FTC 条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[奇偶函数积分|Odd/even symmetry]] requires both a symmetric interval and the appropriate function symmetry. An odd function integrable throughout $[-a,a]$ has integral zero; an even one has twice the right-half integral. Existence comes first: $1/x$ is singular at zero, so $\int_{-1}^1dx/x$ cannot be declared zero by oddness. Its two one-sided limits must be checked as [[反常积分|improper integrals]]. Likewise, evaluating an antiderivative across a singularity does not satisfy the FTC conditions used here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[定积分换元]]来自链式法则。若 $g\in C^1[a,b]$、$f$ 在包含 $g([a,b])$ 的区间连续，那么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[定积分换元|Substitution for definite integrals]] comes from the chain rule. If $g\in C^1[a,b]$ and $f$ is continuous on an interval containing $g([a,b])$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\int_a^bf(g(x))g'(x)\,dx=\int_{g(a)}^{g(b)}f(u)\,du.
$$

例如令 $u=x^3+2$，$du=3x^2dx$，就有 $\int_1^2(x^3+2)^5x^2dx=\frac13\int_3^{10}u^5du=(10^6-3^6)/18$。必须同时变换函数、微分和上下限。这个完整复合导数公式不要求 $g$ 单调：$\int_{-1}^12xe^{x^2}dx=\int_1^1e^udu=0$。但若换元后还需用逆函数消去残余的 $x$，[[非单调换元需分支]]；对 $\int_{-1}^1x^2dx$ 使用 $u=x^2$ 时，就必须分别处理 $x=-\sqrt u$ 与 $x=\sqrt u$，否则会错误地算成零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $u=x^3+2$ and $du=3x^2dx$ give $\int_1^2(x^3+2)^5x^2dx=\frac13\int_3^{10}u^5du=(10^6-3^6)/18$. Transform the function, differential, and bounds together. This exact composite-derivative formula does not require monotonicity: $\int_{-1}^12xe^{x^2}dx=\int_1^1e^udu=0$. However, when residual occurrences of $x$ require an inverse, [[非单调换元需分支|non-monotone substitution needs separate branches]]. Substituting $u=x^2$ in $\int_{-1}^1x^2dx$ requires the branches $x=-\sqrt u$ and $x=\sqrt u$; otherwise a false cancellation gives zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 为什么积分与求导互相恢复：Sessions 51–52
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why accumulation and differentiation undo each other*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把固定右端点换成变量，就从一个数得到[[累积函数]] $A(x)=\int_a^xf(t)dt$。$t$ 是积分内部的哑变量，$x$ 才是函数输入；$A(a)=0$。贯穿例子成为 $A(x)=x^3/3$。直觉上，端点移动很小的量 $h$，新添有向面积近似为 $f(x)h$，所以累积量的变化率应是当前高度 $f(x)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replacing a fixed upper endpoint by a variable turns a number into an [[累积函数|accumulation function]], $A(x)=\int_a^xf(t)dt$. Here $t$ is a dummy integration variable, while $x$ is the input; $A(a)=0$. The running example becomes $A(x)=x^3/3$. Intuitively, moving the endpoint by a small amount $h$ adds signed area approximately $f(x)h$, so the rate of accumulation should equal the current height $f(x)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[积分求导定理|FTC II：积分求导定理]]把这个直觉变成证明。设 $f$ 连续，且 $x$ 是定义区间的内点。区间可加性和绝对值界给出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[积分求导定理|FTC II, differentiation of an integral]], turns the intuition into a proof. Assume $f$ is continuous and $x$ lies inside the interval. Additivity and the absolute-value bound give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{A(x+h)-A(x)}h-f(x)
&=\frac1h\int_x^{x+h}[f(t)-f(x)]\,dt,\\
\left|\frac{A(x+h)-A(x)}h-f(x)\right|
&\le \sup_{|t-x|\le|h|}|f(t)-f(x)|\longrightarrow0.
\end{aligned}
$$

这里取 $h$ 足够小，使附近各点仍在定义区间；有向积分与绝对值处理了 $h<0$，而连续性让最后的上界趋零。因此 $A'(x)=f(x)$。证明从分割极限定义的积分出发，只使用积分性质和连续性，没有预先使用原函数端点评价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $h$ small enough that the nearby points remain in the domain. Oriented integration and absolute values handle $h<0$, while continuity drives the final bound to zero. Hence $A'(x)=f(x)$. This proof starts from the integral defined by partition limits and uses integral properties and continuity, without assuming antiderivative endpoint evaluation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在才回头证明 FTC I。若另一个函数 $F$ 也满足 $F'=f$，则 $(F-A)'=0$。由中值定理支持的[[原函数相差常数]]，$F(x)-A(x)=C$；代入 $x=a$ 得 $C=F(a)$，再代入 $x=b$ 就得到 $F(b)-F(a)=A(b)=\int_a^bf$。这样，“微小累积的极限”“累积函数的导数”和“原函数的端点差”才真正闭合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We can now prove FTC I. If another function satisfies $F'=f$, then $(F-A)'=0$. The mean value theorem implies that [[原函数相差常数|antiderivatives differ only by a constant]], so $F-A=C$. Substituting $x=a$ gives $C=F(a)$, and then $x=b$ yields $F(b)-F(a)=A(b)=\int_a^bf$. This completes the connection between partition limits, derivatives of accumulation functions, and antiderivative endpoint differences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[变限积分求导]]只需在这一结论外再套链式法则。固定在区间 $I$ 上连续的被积函数 $f$，让上下限 $u(x),v(x)$ 可导且始终取值在 $I$ 内，便有
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[变限积分求导|Differentiation with variable limits]] adds the chain rule. For a fixed integrand continuous on an interval $I$ and differentiable endpoints $u(x),v(x)$ that remain in $I$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac d{dx}\int_{u(x)}^{v(x)}f(t)dt
=f(v(x))v'(x)-f(u(x))u'(x).
$$

例如 $\frac d{dx}\int_x^{x^2}e^{-t^2}dt=2xe^{-x^4}-e^{-x^2}$，完全不必先写出积分的初等表达式。此公式中的被积函数本身不随外部参数 $x$ 改变；若写成 $f(t,x)$，还会出现参数变化项，不能只处理上下限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $\frac d{dx}\int_x^{x^2}e^{-t^2}dt=2xe^{-x^4}-e^{-x^2}$, without an elementary expression for the integral. This formula assumes the integrand itself does not depend on the external parameter $x$. An integrand $f(t,x)$ contributes an additional parameter-variation term, so endpoint terms alone are insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检：先判结构，再求导
> 若连续函数 $f$ 满足 $x\sin(\pi x)=\int_0^{x^2}f(t)dt$，求 $f(4)$。答案：求导得 $\sin(\pi x)+\pi x\cos(\pi x)=2xf(x^2)$，再代 $x=2$，故 $f(4)=\pi/2$。不是先把积分上限 $4$ 代进原等式就能直接读出 $f(4)$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a continuous function satisfies $x\sin(\pi x)=\int_0^{x^2}f(t)dt$, find $f(4)$. Answer: differentiate to obtain $\sin(\pi x)+\pi x\cos(\pi x)=2xf(x^2)$, then substitute $x=2$, giving $f(4)=\pi/2$. Substituting an upper endpoint of $4$ into the original integral equation would not directly reveal the integrand's value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 积分可以认识旧函数，也可以创造新函数：Sessions 53–55
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Integrals identify familiar functions and create new ones*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $L(x)=\int_1^xdt/t$，定义域为 $x>0$。FTC II 给出 $L'(x)=1/x$，并且 $L(1)=0$；与已有自然对数比较导数和初值，便得到[[自然对数积分表示]] $L(x)=\ln x$。乘法性质也能直接从积分看出：对 $a,b>0$，先在 $a$ 处分段，再在第二段令 $t=au$，得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $L(x)=\int_1^xdt/t$ for $x>0$. FTC II gives $L'(x)=1/x$, and $L(1)=0$. Matching the derivative and initial value of the existing logarithm establishes its [[自然对数积分表示|integral representation]], $L(x)=\ln x$. The product rule also follows directly: for $a,b>0$, split at $a$ and substitute $t=au$ in the second integral:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
L(ab)=\int_1^a\frac{dt}{t}+\int_a^{ab}\frac{dt}{t}
=L(a)+\int_1^b\frac{du}{u}=L(a)+L(b).
$$

反过来，$E(x)=\int_0^xe^{-t^2}dt$ 没有初等表达式，但这不妨碍它存在、可导或可计算。[[无初等原函数的积分]]区分的是三个问题：定义域内的有限闭区间上是否可积、是否有原函数、原函数能否写成有限组合的初等函数。本例在全实线上连续，连续性和 FTC 已解决前两个；“非初等”是第三个问题的答案，课程没有在此证明一般的非初等性判据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In contrast, $E(x)=\int_0^xe^{-t^2}dt$ has no elementary expression, but it still exists, is differentiable, and can be computed. The distinction in [[无初等原函数的积分|integrals without elementary antiderivatives]] separates three questions: integrability on finite closed intervals within the domain, existence of an antiderivative, and representation by a finite combination of elementary functions. This integrand is continuous on the entire real line, so continuity and the FTC settle the first two. Non-elementarity concerns the third; the course does not prove a general non-elementarity criterion here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[累积函数图像]]让导数代替闭式表达式：$E'=e^{-x^2}>0$，所以一直递增；$E''=-2xe^{-x^2}$，所以负半轴向上凹、正半轴向下凹；偶被积函数配合从 $0$ 开始的有向积分，使 $E$ 是奇函数，且原点切线为 $y=x$。至于水平渐近线的高度，不能仅凭“导数趋零”判定；第 9 节的收敛证明才给出 $\pm\sqrt\pi/2$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[累积函数图像|Graphing an accumulation function]] uses derivatives in place of a closed formula. Since $E'=e^{-x^2}>0$, it is increasing everywhere. Since $E''=-2xe^{-x^2}$, it is concave up on the negative half-line and concave down on the positive half-line. The even integrand and oriented accumulation from zero make $E$ odd, with tangent $y=x$ at the origin. A derivative tending to zero does not establish a horizontal asymptote; the convergence proof in Section 9 supplies the heights $\pm\sqrt\pi/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 几何建模：先画微元，再选积分：Sessions 56–59
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Geometric modelling: draw the element before choosing the integral*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[曲线间面积]]把“高度”换成两条边界的差。若连续函数 $f\ge g$，竖条面积是 $[f(x)-g(x)]dx$；若边界自然写成 $x=R(y),L(y)$，横条面积是 $[R(y)-L(y)]dy$。交点不只提供端点，也提示上下或左右次序是否改变。贯穿区域 $D=\{0\le x\le1,\ 0\le y\le x^2\}$ 的面积还是 $A=1/3$；按水平条算则为 $\int_0^1(1-\sqrt y)dy=1/3$。两种写法累加的是同一个区域。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For [[曲线间面积|area between curves]], strip height becomes the difference between boundaries. With continuous $f\ge g$, a vertical strip has area $[f(x)-g(x)]dx$. If the boundaries are naturally $x=R(y),L(y)$, use $[R(y)-L(y)]dy$. Intersections determine both endpoints and possible changes of ordering. Our running region $D=\{0\le x\le1,\ 0\le y\le x^2\}$ still has area $A=1/3$. Horizontal strips give $\int_0^1(1-\sqrt y)dy=1/3$, accumulating the same region.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[切片体积]]把小矩形升级成薄片：对于本课程的规则立体，若截面积 $A(x)\ge0$ 可积，$dV=A(x)dx$，所以 $V=\int A(x)dx$。例如半径 $R$ 的球在坐标 $x$ 处截面积是 $\pi(R^2-x^2)$，积分范围为 $[-R,R]$，结果 $4\pi R^3/3$。这个原则适用于非旋转体；旋转只让截面积更容易表达。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[切片体积|Volume by slicing]] replaces rectangles with thin slabs. For the regular solids considered here, an integrable nonnegative cross-sectional area gives $dV=A(x)dx$ and $V=\int A(x)dx$. A sphere of radius $R$ has cross-sectional area $\pi(R^2-x^2)$ over $[-R,R]$, yielding $4\pi R^3/3$. The principle also applies to non-rotational solids; rotation simply makes many cross-sections easier to describe.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $D$ 绕 $x$ 轴旋转，垂直于轴的截面是半径 $x^2$ 的圆盘。[[圆盘垫圈法]]给出 $V_x=\pi\int_0^1(x^2)^2dx=\pi/5$。若有空洞，就用外圆面积减内圆面积 $\pi(R^2-r^2)$，其中 $R\ge r\ge0$ 都是到轴的距离；不是 $\pi(R-r)^2$，也不是把有符号坐标直接当半径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rotate $D$ about the $x$-axis. A cross-section perpendicular to the axis is a disk of radius $x^2$, so the [[圆盘垫圈法|disk/washer method]] gives $V_x=\pi\int_0^1(x^2)^2dx=\pi/5$. A hole requires subtracting the inner disk area from the outer one, $\pi(R^2-r^2)$, where $R\ge r\ge0$ are distances from the axis. This is not $\pi(R-r)^2$, and a signed coordinate is not automatically a radius.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把同一区域绕 $y$ 轴旋转，竖条现在平行于轴，生成半径 $x$、高度 $x^2$、厚度 $dx$ 的薄壳。[[圆柱壳法]]给出 $V_y=2\pi\int_0^1x(x^2)dx=\pi/2$。改用水平垫圈也能核对：同一高度 $y$ 的半径从 $\sqrt y$ 到 $1$，所以 $V_y=\pi\int_0^1(1-y)dy=\pi/2$。选择方法的依据是切片方向和边界表达，不是只看“绕哪个轴”的口诀。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rotate the same region about the $y$-axis. Vertical strips are now parallel to the axis and generate shells of radius $x$, height $x^2$, and thickness $dx$. The [[圆柱壳法|shell method]] gives $V_y=2\pi\int_0^1x(x^2)dx=\pi/2$. Horizontal washers verify this: at height $y$, radii run from $\sqrt y$ to $1$, so $V_y=\pi\int_0^1(1-y)dy=\pi/2$. Choose the method from slice direction and boundary expressions, not from an axis-name mnemonic alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这两种方法都必须防止[[旋转体重复覆盖]]。将 $D$ 关于 $y$ 轴镜像后，两侧一起旋转仍只生成同一个实体；把两侧的非负壳体积相加会算两遍，用有符号 $x$ 又会错误抵消。通用做法是按非负半径组织薄壳，在同一半径合并实际覆盖的高度区间，每个实体点只算一次。跨轴本身不等于一定重叠，真正要检查的是旋转后的覆盖。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both methods must avoid [[旋转体重复覆盖|repeated coverage in a solid of revolution]]. Reflecting $D$ across the $y$-axis and rotating both halves still produces the same solid. Adding both nonnegative shell volumes counts it twice, while using signed $x$ causes false cancellation. Organise shells by nonnegative radius, take the union of the occupied height intervals at each radius, and count each part of the solid once. Crossing the axis alone does not prove overlap; the generated coverage must be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 从总量到平均、质心与功：Sessions 60–61 及应用题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From totals to averages, centroids, and work*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[积分平均值]]是把总累积摊回单位区间长度：$f_{\rm avg}=\frac1{b-a}\int_a^bf(x)dx$，其中 $a<b$、$f$ 可积。$x^2$ 在 $[0,1]$ 上的平均值为 $1/3$，而不是两个端点值的平均 $1/2$。若再假设 $f$ 连续，[[积分中值定理]]保证有某个 $c\in[a,b]$ 使 $f(c)=f_{\rm avg}$：平均值在最小值与最大值之间，再由介值定理取得。对 $x^2$，该点是 $c=1/\sqrt3$，并非区间中点；只可积而不连续时，平均值未必被取得。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[积分平均值|average value of a function]] spreads total accumulation over the interval length: $f_{\rm avg}=\frac1{b-a}\int_a^bf(x)dx$, with $a<b$ and integrable $f$. For $x^2$ on $[0,1]$, it is $1/3$, not the endpoint average $1/2$. If $f$ is also continuous, the [[积分中值定理|mean value theorem for integrals]] guarantees a point $c$ with $f(c)=f_{\rm avg}$: the average lies between the minimum and maximum and is attained by the intermediate value theorem. Here $c=1/\sqrt3$, not the midpoint. Integrability without continuity need not give attainment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平均之前必须问“按什么等权？”[[加权平均]]写成 $\bar q=\int qw/\int w$，要求 $w\ge0$、总权重有限且正、$qw$ 绝对可积。按时间平均时 $w=1$；按质量平均温度时，$w(x)dx$ 应代表质量；沿曲线按长度平均则使用 $ds$。例如只描画一次、长度有限且正的 $C^1$ 图像有 $ds=\sqrt{1+(y')^2}dx$，所以均匀弧长平均一般不同于均匀横坐标平均。一般参数形式与重复经过的计数边界见[[参数曲线弧长与重复描画]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before averaging, ask what receives equal weight. A [[加权平均|weighted average]] is $\bar q=\int qw/\int w$, requiring nonnegative weights, positive finite total weight, and absolute integrability of $qw$. A time average uses $w=1$; a mass-weighted temperature average uses mass elements $w(x)dx$; an arc-length average uses $ds$. For a once-traced $C^1$ graph of positive finite length, $ds=\sqrt{1+(y')^2}dx$, so uniform arc-length averaging generally differs from uniform horizontal-coordinate averaging. See [[参数曲线弧长与重复描画|parametric arc length and repeated tracing]] for the general form and its counting boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[质心]]就是位置的质量加权平均，要求非负质量、正的有限总质量和有限绝对一阶矩。均匀薄片的密度在比值中消去，此时质心等于面积形心。对 $g(x)\le y\le f(x)$ 的均匀区域，[[质心积分计算]]从竖条面积 $dA=(f-g)dx$ 及其中心高度 $(f+g)/2$ 得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[质心|center of mass]] is the mass-weighted mean position, requiring nonnegative mass, positive finite total mass, and finite absolute first moments. A uniform lamina has constant density that cancels in the ratio, so its center of mass equals its area centroid. For a uniform region between $g(x)$ and $f(x)$, [[质心积分计算|centroid calculation by integration]] uses strip area $dA=(f-g)dx$ and strip-center height $(f+g)/2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
A=\int_a^b(f-g)dx,\qquad
\bar x=\frac{\int_a^bx(f-g)dx}{A},\qquad
\bar y=\frac{\frac12\int_a^b(f^2-g^2)dx}{A}.
$$

回到均匀区域 $D$，$A=1/3$，所以 $\bar x=(1/4)/(1/3)=3/4$，$\bar y=(1/10)/(1/3)=3/10$。形心不等于上下左右端点的平均，也不必位于非凸区域内部。[[Pappus体积定理]]把这个形心重新接回体积：有界规则平面区域位于同一共面旋转轴的一侧，且轴不穿过内部时，旋转体积为 $V=A\,2\pi d$，$d$ 是面积形心到轴的距离；允许本例中轴接触边界。对 $D$ 绕 $x$ 轴，$(1/3)2\pi(3/10)=\pi/5$；绕 $y$ 轴，$(1/3)2\pi(3/4)=\pi/2$，与切片结果一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the uniform region $D$, $A=1/3$, giving $\bar x=(1/4)/(1/3)=3/4$ and $\bar y=(1/10)/(1/3)=3/10$. A centroid is not an average of boundary endpoints and need not lie inside a non-convex region. [[Pappus体积定理|Pappus's volume theorem]] reconnects it to volume: a bounded regular plane region lying on one side of a coplanar axis that does not cross its interior generates volume $V=A\,2\pi d$, where $d$ is the area centroid's distance from the axis. Boundary contact is allowed here. Rotating $D$ about the $x$-axis gives $(1/3)2\pi(3/10)=\pi/5$; about the $y$-axis gives $(1/3)2\pi(3/4)=\pi/2$, agreeing with slicing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[功的积分]]累加的是“沿位移方向的力 × 位移”，而不是单纯累加力。在一维位置依赖力模型中，$W=\int_a^bF(x)dx$。弹簧回复力为 $-kx$，缓慢向外拉伸所施加的力为 $+kx$，因此从 $0$ 拉到 $L>0$ 的外力功为 $kL^2/2$，弹簧力所做的功为其负值。抽水问题则改画水平水片：质量密度 $\rho$、截面积 $A(y)$、高于全部水片的出口高度 $H$，在忽略损耗和动能变化时给出 $dW=\rho gA(y)(H-y)dy$，再对所有水片累加；每片水的提升距离不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[功的积分|Work by integration]] accumulates the force component along displacement times displacement, not force alone. For a one-dimensional position-dependent force, $W=\int_a^bF(x)dx$. A spring's restoring force is $-kx$, while the force applied during slow outward stretching is $+kx$. Stretching from zero to $L>0$ requires applied work $kL^2/2$; the spring does its negative. Pumping instead uses horizontal water slices: mass density $\rho$, cross-sectional area $A(y)$, and an outlet above all layers give $dW=\rho gA(y)(H-y)dy$ when losses and kinetic-energy changes are neglected. Each slice travels a different lifting distance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检：公式前先检查微元
> 为什么绕 $y$ 轴旋转 $D$ 时，$\int_0^1\pi x^4dx$ 不对？答案：$dx$ 竖条平行于 $y$ 轴，生成的是半径 $x$、高度 $x^2$ 的壳；正确微元为 $2\pi x\cdot x^2dx$。$\pi x^4dx$ 对应的是绕 $x$ 轴的圆盘。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why is $\int_0^1\pi x^4dx$ wrong for rotating $D$ about the $y$-axis? Answer: a vertical $dx$ strip is parallel to that axis and generates a shell of radius $x$ and height $x^2$, with element $2\pi x\cdot x^2dx$. The element $\pi x^4dx$ describes disks about the $x$-axis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 概率是总权重为一的累积：Session 62
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Probability as accumulation with unit total weight*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[概率密度函数]] $p$ 必须非负且全域积分为 $1$。在这一绝对连续模型中，区间概率是 $\int_a^bp(x)dx$，密度高度本身不是概率，甚至可以大于 $1$。[[累积分布函数]]的一般定义是 $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$；有密度时，它可写成 $F_X(x)=\int_{-\infty}^xp(t)dt$。在密度连续的点，FTC II 给出 $F_X'=p$，而区间概率由累计差 $F_X(b)-F_X(a)$ 得到。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[概率密度函数|probability density]] must be nonnegative and integrate to one over its full domain. In this absolutely continuous model, interval probability is $\int_a^bp(x)dx$; density height is not itself probability and may exceed one. A [[累积分布函数|cumulative distribution function]] is generally defined by $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$. When a density exists, $F_X(x)=\int_{-\infty}^xp(t)dt$. At continuity points of the density, FTC II gives $F_X'=p$, while interval probabilities are cumulative differences $F_X(b)-F_X(a)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

贯穿函数 $x^2$ 在 $[0,1]$ 的总积分为 $1/3$，所以把它除以总量，才得到 $p(x)=3x^2$（$0\le x\le1$；其他位置为零）。对应的 CDF 在 $x<0$ 为 $0$，在 $0\le x\le1$ 为 $x^3$，在 $x>1$ 为 $1$；于是 $P(X\le1/2)=1/8$，而 $p(1)=3$ 完全合法。这里与前面均匀薄片的联系是：竖条面积与 $x^2dx$ 成正比，所以随机按面积取一个点时，它的横坐标就服从这个密度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Our running function has total integral $1/3$ on $[0,1]$. Dividing by that total produces $p(x)=3x^2$ on $[0,1]$, zero elsewhere. Its CDF is zero for $x<0$, $x^3$ on $[0,1]$, and one for $x>1$. Thus $P(X\le1/2)=1/8$, while $p(1)=3$ is perfectly valid. This connects with the uniform lamina: vertical strip area is proportional to $x^2dx$, so the horizontal coordinate of a point sampled uniformly by area has this density.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[期望]]是位置的概率加权平均，有限值要求 $\int|x|p(x)dx<\infty$；[[方差]]是相对于均值的平方偏离的期望，有限值要求二阶矩有限。在本例中有界支撑保证这些条件，计算得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[期望|Expectation]] is the probability-weighted mean position, with a finite value requiring $\int|x|p(x)dx<\infty$. [[方差|Variance]] averages squared deviations from that mean and is finite when the second moment is finite. Bounded support ensures these conditions in our example:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E[X]=\int_0^1x\cdot3x^2dx=\frac34,\qquad
E[X^2]=\frac35,\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac35-\left(\frac34\right)^2=\frac3{80}.
$$

$E[X]=3/4$ 与薄片横坐标形心一致，因为二者的权重相同。对于无界分布，不能用对称抵消代替矩存在性检查；概率总量为一，也不保证均值或方差有限。前面由旧函数定义新函数的思路，在这里就成为“用密度定义累计概率”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mean $3/4$ matches the lamina's horizontal centroid because the weights are identical. For unbounded distributions, symmetry does not replace checking moment existence. Unit total probability alone does not guarantee a finite mean or variance. Defining a new function by accumulation has now become defining cumulative probability from a density.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8. 没有便利原函数时如何计算和保证精度：Sessions 63–64
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Numerical computation and accuracy guarantees*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在回到有限和，但目标已从“定义积分”变成“用有限次函数求值近似积分”。以下统一设 $a<b$，$N$ 为小区间数，$h=(b-a)/N$，$x_i=a+ih$，$f_i=f(x_i)$。有 $N+1$ 个端点，不能把节点数和区间数混用。[[左右端点矩形法]]分别取每段左、右高度；[[中点法]]把取点移到段的中心；[[梯形法]]连接相邻端点，以线性插值曲线下的有向面积近似积分：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We return to finite sums, now to approximate an already defined integral using finitely many function evaluations. Throughout, let $a<b$, let $N$ count subintervals, and set $h=(b-a)/N$, $x_i=a+ih$, and $f_i=f(x_i)$. There are $N+1$ endpoints, so nodes and subintervals are not interchangeable. The [[左右端点矩形法|left/right endpoint rules]] use the respective endpoint heights, the [[中点法|midpoint rule]] samples each center, and the [[梯形法|trapezoidal rule]] integrates the linear interpolant through consecutive endpoints:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
L_N&=h\sum_{i=1}^Nf_{i-1},&R_N&=h\sum_{i=1}^Nf_i,\\
M_N&=h\sum_{i=1}^Nf\!\left(a+(i-\tfrac12)h\right),&
T_N&=\frac{L_N+R_N}{2}
=\frac h2\left[f_0+2\sum_{i=1}^{N-1}f_i+f_N\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

若 $f$ 递增，则 $L_N\le I\le R_N$，其中 $I=\int_a^bf$；这一方向判断来自单调性。若 $f$ 凸，梯形弦高于曲线，$T_N$ 高估，而 $M_N$ 低估；这一判断来自曲率，不应与递增混为一谈。贯穿例子取 $N=2$：$L_2=1/8$、$R_2=5/8$、$M_2=5/16$、$T_2=3/8$，而真值 $I=1/3$，可以直接核对这些关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f$ is increasing, $L_N\le I\le R_N$, where $I=\int_a^bf$; this direction comes from monotonicity. If $f$ is convex, trapezoidal chords lie above the graph, so $T_N$ overestimates and $M_N$ underestimates. This uses curvature, not monotonicity. For the running example with $N=2$, $L_2=1/8$, $R_2=5/8$, $M_2=5/16$, and $T_2=3/8$, while $I=1/3$, verifying these relations directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Simpson法]]每两个小区间用经过左端、中点、右端的二次插值近似；因此 $N$ 必须为正偶数。每对区间的权重是 $1,4,1$，相邻对共享端点后，内侧偶数节点的权重变成 $2$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[Simpson法|Simpson's rule]] uses a quadratic interpolant through the left endpoint, midpoint, and right endpoint of each pair of subintervals, so $N$ must be a positive even integer. Each pair has weights $1,4,1$; shared endpoints give weight $2$ at interior even-indexed nodes:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
S_N=\frac h3\left[f_0+4\!\sum_{\substack{1\le i<N\\i\ \mathrm{odd}}}f_i
+2\!\sum_{\substack{2\le i<N\\i\ \mathrm{even}}}f_i+f_N\right].
$$

虽然拟合的是二次多项式，关于每对区间中点的三次奇项也被对称积分消去，所以 Simpson 对三次及以下多项式精确。贯穿例子给出 $S_2=\frac16[0+4(1/4)+1]=1/3$。计算 $\ln2=\int_1^2dx/x$ 时则不是精确值：同取 $N=2$，$M_2=24/35$、$T_2=17/24$、$S_2=25/36\approx0.694444$，与 $\ln2\approx0.693147$ 接近但不相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Although the interpolant is quadratic, a centered cubic odd term also cancels by symmetry over each pair. Simpson's rule is therefore exact through degree three. The running example gives $S_2=\frac16[0+4(1/4)+1]=1/3$. For $\ln2=\int_1^2dx/x$, however, the rule is not exact: with $N=2$, $M_2=24/35$, $T_2=17/24$, and $S_2=25/36\approx0.694444$, close to but different from $\ln2\approx0.693147$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Simpson组合恒等式]]比较的是一致定义的粗细网格。先取 $n$ 个粗区间计算 $T_n$ 和 $M_n$，再把粗区间中点加入网格，得到 $N=2n$ 个细区间。令细步长为 $h=(b-a)/(2n)$，则 $T_n$ 用偶数节点，$M_n$ 用奇数节点，展开得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Simpson组合恒等式|Simpson combination identity]] compares explicitly related grids. Compute $T_n$ and $M_n$ on $n$ coarse subintervals, then add the coarse midpoints to create $N=2n$ fine subintervals. With fine step $h=(b-a)/(2n)$, $T_n$ uses the even nodes and $M_n$ the odd nodes:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
T_n&=h[f_0+2f_2+\cdots+2f_{2n-2}+f_{2n}],\\
M_n&=2h[f_1+f_3+\cdots+f_{2n-1}],\\
S_{2n}&=\frac{T_n+2M_n}{3}.
\end{aligned}
$$

这里不能把右侧下标同时改成 $2n$ 还声称比较的是同一公式；那会换成另一组取点。恒等式来自逐点权重相等，并不要求 $f$ 为多项式；多项式精确性是另一个结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing the right-hand subscripts to $2n$ would change the sampling grid and no longer give this identity. The equality comes from matching node weights and does not require a polynomial integrand. Polynomial exactness is a separate result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个近似值还需要误差保证。若 $f\in C^2[a,b]$，且全区间上 $|f''|\le K_2$，[[梯形积分误差界]]和[[中点积分误差界]]分别给出前两个界；若 $f\in C^4[a,b]$、$|f^{(4)}|\le K_4$ 且 $N$ 为正偶数，[[Simpson误差界]]给出第三个界：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An approximation needs an accuracy guarantee. If $f\in C^2[a,b]$ and $|f''|\le K_2$ throughout the interval, the [[梯形积分误差界|trapezoidal]] and [[中点积分误差界|midpoint error bounds]] give the first two inequalities. If $f\in C^4[a,b]$, $|f^{(4)}|\le K_4$, and $N$ is a positive even integer, the [[Simpson误差界|Simpson error bound]] gives the third:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
|I-T_N|\le\frac{K_2(b-a)^3}{12N^2},\qquad
|I-M_N|\le\frac{K_2(b-a)^3}{24N^2},\qquad
|I-S_N|\le\frac{K_4(b-a)^5}{180N^4}.
$$

为什么是这些阶数？梯形与中点每个宽度 $h$ 的局部误差分别不超过 $K_2h^3/12$、$K_2h^3/24$，累加 $N$ 段便得到 $N^{-2}$；Simpson 每对宽度 $2h$ 的误差不超过 $K_4h^5/90$，累加 $N/2$ 对便得到 $N^{-4}$。精确常数与局部余项证明保存在三张误差卡。$N$ 加倍时，保证界分别缩小到原来的 $1/4$、$1/4$、$1/16$；这不意味着每个函数的实际误差都严格按这些比例缩小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why these orders? A width-$h$ trapezoidal or midpoint panel has local error at most $K_2h^3/12$ or $K_2h^3/24$; summing $N$ panels yields order $N^{-2}$. Each width-$2h$ Simpson pair has error at most $K_4h^5/90$; summing $N/2$ pairs yields order $N^{-4}$. The three error notes retain the exact constants and local-remainder proofs. Doubling $N$ reduces the guaranteed bounds by factors $1/4$, $1/4$, and $1/16$, respectively; actual errors need not follow these exact ratios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $x^2$、$N=2$，$K_2=2$，梯形误差 $3/8-1/3=1/24$ 与中点误差 $1/3-5/16=1/48$ 恰好达到各自的界；四阶导数为零，Simpson 界也正确给出零。若指定容许误差 $\varepsilon>0$，例如梯形法应取 $N\ge\sqrt{K_2(b-a)^3/(12\varepsilon)}$ 并向上取整。导数界必须覆盖整个区间，不能只从采样点猜测；输入数据与舍入误差也不包含在这些离散近似误差界中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x^2$ with $N=2$, $K_2=2$: trapezoidal error $1/24$ and midpoint error $1/48$ attain their respective bounds. The fourth derivative is zero, correctly giving zero Simpson error. For a tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the trapezoidal rule, for example, requires $N\ge\sqrt{K_2(b-a)^3/(12\varepsilon)}$, rounded upward. Derivative bounds must hold throughout the interval, not just at sampled points. Data and rounding errors are separate from these discretisation bounds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 9. 高斯积分：先证明收敛，再得到归一化：Session 65
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Gaussian integral: prove convergence before normalising*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数值法可以计算有限区间的钟形曲线面积，但为什么全线总面积恰好为 $\sqrt\pi$？[[高斯积分]]把一维问题变成一个可用旋转体方法计算的二维面积累积。为避免先假设无穷积分收敛，先只处理有限 $R>0$，定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Numerical rules approximate finite portions of a bell curve, but why is its total area exactly $\sqrt\pi$? The [[高斯积分|Gaussian integral]] turns the one-dimensional problem into two-dimensional accumulation that can be evaluated through rotational volume. To avoid assuming convergence, begin only with finite $R>0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Q_R=\int_{-R}^{R}e^{-x^2}dx,\qquad
Q_R^2=\int_{-R}^{R}\int_{-R}^{R}e^{-(x^2+y^2)}\,dy\,dx.
$$

有限正方形上的被积函数连续，逐次积分合法，且因 $e^{-(x^2+y^2)}=e^{-x^2}e^{-y^2}$，二重积分等于 $Q_R^2$。另一方面，半径 $R$ 的圆盘上，径向高度为 $e^{-r^2}$；半径 $r$、厚度 $dr$ 的圆环给出薄壳体积 $2\pi r e^{-r^2}dr$，因此圆盘上方的体积为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The integrand is continuous on the finite square, so iterated integration is valid; its factorisation gives $Q_R^2$. On a disk of radius $R$, the radial height is $e^{-r^2}$. An annulus of radius $r$ and thickness $dr$ produces shell volume $2\pi r e^{-r^2}dr$, yielding
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
V_R=\int_0^R2\pi r e^{-r^2}dr=\pi(1-e^{-R^2}).
$$

半径 $R$ 的圆盘包含于正方形 $[-R,R]^2$，后者又包含于半径 $\sqrt2R$ 的圆盘。非负高度使包含关系变成体积不等式，所以
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The radius-$R$ disk lies inside the square $[-R,R]^2$, which lies inside the radius-$\sqrt2R$ disk. Nonnegative height converts these inclusions into volume inequalities:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\pi(1-e^{-R^2})\le Q_R^2\le\pi(1-e^{-2R^2})
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Q_R\longrightarrow\sqrt\pi.
$$

偶性说明每个半轴的截断积分都是 $Q_R/2$，故左右两个反常积分分别收敛，得到通常意义下的 $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2}dx=\sqrt\pi$，而不只是一个对称主值。本证明只在有限区域换序；若直接在整个平面交换非负积分，可用 Tonelli 定理，但其有限值仍需另证。尺度代换随后给出 $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-\alpha x^2}dx=\sqrt{\pi/\alpha}$（$\alpha>0$）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Evenness makes each half-line truncation equal to $Q_R/2$, so the two one-sided improper integrals converge separately. Thus $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2}dx=\sqrt\pi$ is an ordinary improper integral, not merely a symmetric principal value. This proof interchanges integration only on finite regions. A whole-plane interchange for a nonnegative integrand can instead use Tonelli's theorem, but finiteness still needs proof. Scaling then gives $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-\alpha x^2}dx=\sqrt{\pi/\alpha}$ for $\alpha>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在才有根据写出标准正态密度 $\phi(x)=e^{-x^2/2}/\sqrt{2\pi}$，因为它的全线积分确为 $1$。再由 $x=\mu+\sigma z$、$\sigma>0$，得到一般正态密度 $e^{-(x-\mu)^2/(2\sigma^2)}/(\sigma\sqrt{2\pi})$。若需要验证标准正态二阶矩，下一主题的[[分部积分]]提供桥梁：有限 $R$ 上 $\int_{-R}^Rx^2e^{-x^2/2}dx=[-xe^{-x^2/2}]_{-R}^R+\int_{-R}^Re^{-x^2/2}dx$；边界项趋零，再除以 $\sqrt{2\pi}$，得到二阶矩 $1$。这是先有高斯积分，再推出概率性质。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only now is the standard normal density $\phi(x)=e^{-x^2/2}/\sqrt{2\pi}$ justified: its full integral equals one. The substitution $x=\mu+\sigma z$, with $\sigma>0$, gives the general normal density $e^{-(x-\mu)^2/(2\sigma^2)}/(\sigma\sqrt{2\pi})$. To check the standard normal second moment, the next topic's [[分部积分|integration by parts]] provides a bridge. For finite $R$, $\int_{-R}^Rx^2e^{-x^2/2}dx=[-xe^{-x^2/2}]_{-R}^R+\int_{-R}^Re^{-x^2/2}dx$. The boundary term tends to zero; division by $\sqrt{2\pi}$ gives second moment one. The Gaussian integral establishes the probability properties, not the other way around.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 10. 综合收束：Sessions 66–67
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Putting the pieces together for review and Exam 3*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个 $x^2$ 已走过一条完整路径：黎曼和定义面积 $1/3$，FTC 用 $x^3/3$ 计算它；改变右端点得到累积函数；改变微元得到绕两轴的体积；除以总权重得到平均、形心或密度；最后有限采样与导数界给出近似及保证。不同应用共用的是“局部贡献的累加”，不是可以不加判断地互换的物理量。实际建模时，应为 $x^2$ 这样的关系补齐有量纲系数，再检查面积、体积、质量和功的单位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same $x^2$ has now completed a full route: Riemann sums define area $1/3$, and the FTC evaluates it using $x^3/3$. A moving endpoint creates an accumulation function; different elements produce volumes about two axes; division by total weight produces an average, centroid, or density; finite sampling and derivative bounds supply approximations with guarantees. What applications share is accumulation of local contributions, not interchangeable physical quantities. Real models need dimensionally appropriate coefficients and checks on area, volume, mass, and work units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

Integration still recovers a total from a rate, but the volume and concentration model must be correct first.
