# 无穷级数与幂级数：先让无限求和有意义，再让它成为计算工具
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Infinite series and power series: justify infinite summation before using it for calculation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一章沿 MIT 18.01SC Sessions 94–101 阅读。你已经会算有限和、极限、导数与积分；现在的问题是：把有限运算延伸到无穷以后，哪些结论还成立？读完应能判断一条级数的敛散、解释为什么可以展开某个函数，并给近似值配上可检验的误差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This chapter follows MIT 18.01SC Sessions 94–101. Starting from finite sums, limits, derivatives, and integrals, it asks which operations remain valid in the infinite setting. The goal is to judge convergence, justify a function expansion, and attach a verifiable error bound to an approximation.

## 1. 无穷和究竟在取什么极限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What limit defines an infinite sum?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$，先写 $S_N=a_1+\cdots+a_N$。[[无穷级数]] 收敛，就是这些已经加好的部分和趋于有限数 $S$。因此 $a_n\to0$ 和 $S_N\to S$ 是两种不同的判断：前者看单次增加量，后者看累计量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First form $S_N=a_1+\cdots+a_N$. An [[无穷级数|infinite series]] converges when these accumulated totals approach a finite number. The limit of an individual increment and the limit of the accumulated sum are different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 $a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}$，收敛一定推出 $a_n\to0$。这给出 [[级数通项判别]]：例如 $n/(n+1)\to1$，所以其级数立即判为发散；但通项趋零后，还要继续分析累积方式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}$, convergence forces $a_n\to0$. The [[级数通项判别|term test]] immediately rejects $\sum n/(n+1)$, whose terms tend to one. Vanishing terms still require further analysis of accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先学两种能直接求出部分和的结构。对 [[几何级数]]，由有限 [[几何和]] 的移位相减，
$$
(1-r)(1+r+\cdots+r^N)=1-r^{N+1}.
$$
当 $|r|<1$ 时，尾部幂趋零，故无穷和为 $1/(1-r)$。当 $r=-1$ 时，部分和在 $1,0$ 间振荡；把 $-1$ 硬代入闭式得到 $1/2$，并不是通常意义的级数和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two structures allow explicit partial sums. Shift-and-subtract for a [[几何和|finite geometric sum]] yields the displayed identity. Its [[几何级数|infinite series]] sums to $1/(1-r)$ only when $|r|<1$. At $r=-1$, partial sums alternate between one and zero; substituting into the quotient does not give an ordinary convergent sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一种是 [[望远镜级数]]：
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac1{n(n+1)}
=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\left(\frac1n-\frac1{n+1}\right)
=1-\frac1{N+1}\longrightarrow1.
$$
两种方法都先保留最后一项，再取极限。无限符号不能让末项自动消失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a [[望远镜级数|telescoping series]], partial fractions expose cancellation in each finite partial sum. Both methods retain the last term before taking the limit; the infinity symbol does not by itself remove a remainder.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 调和级数为什么一直变大
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does the harmonic series keep growing?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

调和级数的项 $1/n$ 趋零，却还能持续累积。把它分组：
$$
1+\frac12+\left(\frac13+\frac14\right)
+\left(\frac15+\cdots+\frac18\right)+\cdots.
$$
从 $2^{k-1}+1$ 到 $2^k$ 的一组有 $2^{k-1}$ 项，每项至少 $1/2^k$，所以每组至少贡献 $1/2$。组数没有上限，部分和也就没有上限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Although $1/n\to0$, grouping terms from $2^{k-1}+1$ through $2^k$ gives $2^{k-1}$ terms, each at least $1/2^k$. Every group contributes at least one half. Arbitrarily many groups force unbounded partial sums.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

积分比较还能告诉我们它长得多快。由递减曲线 $y=1/x$ 上下的单位宽矩形，
$$
\ln(N+1)\le H_N\le1+\ln N.
$$
所以 [[调和数对数增长|$H_N$ 渐近等价于 $\ln N$]]，很慢，但仍无界。Session 96 用相同的均匀单位长砖块，按逐层临界平衡的单列构造得到叠伸量 $H_N/2$，就是这个结论的几何应用。这足以证明任意远伸出量可以实现，不宣称在所有可能布局中最优；任意有限块数仍只有有限伸出量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unit-width rectangles under and over $1/x$ bound $H_N$ between $\ln(N+1)$ and $1+\ln N$. Thus [[调和数对数增长|harmonic growth is logarithmic]] but unbounded. Session 96 constructs overhang $H_N/2$ with identical uniform unit-length blocks in a single stack at successive limiting balances. This proves that arbitrary distances are achievable, not optimality among all possible layouts; every finite stack still has finite overhang.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这段矩形论证推广成 [[级数积分判别]]：若最终 $a_n=f(n)$，且 $f$ 正、连续、递减，则级数与对应尾积分同敛散。通过已有的 [[无穷远 p 积分判别]]，得到 [[p级数判别]]：$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-p}$ 恰在 $p>1$ 时收敛。$p\le0$ 时项已不趋零，不需要积分判别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[级数积分判别|integral test]] generalizes the rectangle argument for an eventually positive, continuous, decreasing interpolating function. Combining it with the [[无穷远 p 积分判别|improper p-integral test]] gives the [[p级数判别|p-series threshold]] $p>1$. For $p\le0$, the term condition already fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 判别法不是一个必须全部试过的清单
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Choose a test from the structure of the terms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[级数判别法选择]] 先识别结构。非负项若容易夹住，用 [[级数直接比较判别]]；若主导项相同但不等式难写，用 [[级数极限比较判别]]。例如
$$
\frac{n}{n^3+1}\Big/\frac1{n^2}=\frac{n^3}{n^3+1}\longrightarrow1,
$$
于是它与 $p=2$ 的级数同收敛。比较对象不是随便找的相似式，而是尾部真正同量级的已知基准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[级数判别法选择|choice of test]] starts with structure. Use [[级数直接比较判别|direct comparison]] for convenient inequalities, or [[级数极限比较判别|limit comparison]] when leading terms match. The displayed ratio shows that $n/(n^3+1)$ has the same convergence behaviour as $1/n^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

遇到阶乘和指数，[[级数比值判别]] 往往会消掉大部分表达式。例如 $a_n=(n!)^2/(2n)!$，
$$
\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}
=\frac{n+1}{2(2n+1)}\longrightarrow\frac14<1.
$$
尾部因此可被一个公比小于一的几何级数控制。若表达式整体有 $n$ 次幂，[[级数根值判别]] 常更直接。两者在临界值 $1$ 时都可能无结论：$\sum1/n$ 与 $\sum1/n^2$ 的比值极限都为 $1$，实际敛散却不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[级数比值判别|ratio test]] often simplifies factorials and exponentials. Here the ratio tends to $1/4$, allowing a convergent geometric bound on the tail. The [[级数根值判别|root test]] is useful for an overall nth power. A critical value of one is inconclusive: harmonic and inverse-square series share that ratio limit but have different outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明“有和”不等于求出了和。如果用前 $N$ 项近似一个正项级数，可再用 [[积分判别余项界]] 夹住遗漏部分。例如对 $\sum1/n^2$，
$$
\frac1{N+1}\le\sum_{n=N+1}^{\infty}\frac1{n^2}\le\frac1N.
$$
这不需要先知道整条级数的精确和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Convergence does not identify the sum. For approximation by the first $N$ terms, the [[积分判别余项界|integral remainder bounds]] place the inverse-square tail between $1/(N+1)$ and $1/N$, without requiring the exact infinite sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 正负抵消带来的能力与限制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What cancellation enables—and what it does not guarantee*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先取每项的绝对值。如果 $\sum|a_n|$ 收敛，就叫 [[绝对收敛]]；由 [[绝对收敛推出收敛]]，原级数也收敛。如果原级数收敛但绝对值级数发散，则叫 [[条件收敛]]。这两个名称描述不同的数学事实，不能只凭符号是否交错来判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Convergence of $\sum|a_n|$ is [[绝对收敛|absolute convergence]], which [[绝对收敛推出收敛|implies convergence]] of the original series. Convergence of the original with divergence after taking absolute values is [[条件收敛|conditional convergence]]. Alternating signs alone do not distinguish the two.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $b_n\ge0$ 且最终单调下降到零的情形，[[交错级数判别]] 保证 $\sum(-1)^{n-1}b_n$ 收敛。偶数部分和从一侧靠近，奇数部分和从另一侧靠近，两侧间距是一项 $b_n\to0$。因此 $\sum(-1)^{n-1}/n$ 收敛；再结合调和级数发散，才得到它条件收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nonnegative magnitudes eventually decreasing to zero, the [[交错级数判别|alternating-series test]] proves convergence. Even and odd partial sums approach from opposite sides, separated by a vanishing term. Applied to $1/n$, this proves convergence; the separate divergence of the harmonic series establishes conditional convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一夹逼还给出 [[交错级数误差界]]：在单调尾部截断后，误差绝对值不超过下一项幅度；非零余项与下一项同号，余项也可能恰为零。原顺序下，这很好用；但若把整条无限序列重新排序，[[条件收敛重排]] 说明和可能改变。相反，[[绝对收敛级数重排]] 保证每项恰用一次的任意重排都不改变和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same bracketing gives the [[交错级数误差界|alternating remainder bound]]: the absolute error is at most the next magnitude, and a nonzero remainder has that term's sign; the remainder can also be zero. This relies on the given order. [[条件收敛重排|Conditional rearrangements]] may change the sum, whereas [[绝对收敛级数重排|absolute convergence]] guarantees invariance under bijective rearrangement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 把输入放进级数：半径和端点分开判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Insert a variable: separate the radius from endpoint tests*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[幂级数]] 是 $\sum c_n(x-a)^n$。每固定一个 $x$，它就是刚才学过的数项级数。[[幂级数收敛半径]] $R$ 把输入分成内部绝对收敛与外部发散；$0<R<\infty$ 时，两个端点还没有决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[幂级数|power series]] becomes a numerical series at each fixed input. Its [[幂级数收敛半径|radius]] separates interior absolute convergence from exterior divergence. A finite positive radius does not settle its two endpoints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x^n/n$：当 $x\ne0$ 时，绝对值比值趋于 $|x|$；$x=0$ 时直接得到恒零级数。因此 $R=1$。按 [[幂级数端点判别]] 分别代入：$x=1$ 给调和级数，发散；$x=-1$ 给交错调和级数，条件收敛。于是完整区间为 $[-1,1)$。以中心对称的是开区间内部，加入端点后的区间不一定对称。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\sum x^n/n$ with $x\ne0$, the ratio limit is $|x|$; at zero the series is identically zero. The radius is therefore one. [[幂级数端点判别|Separate endpoint tests]] yield divergence at $1$ and conditional convergence at $-1$, so the full interval is $[-1,1)$. The open interior is symmetric about the centre; endpoint inclusion need not be.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 从导数配系数，还要再证明等于函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Derivative matching determines coefficients, not equality with the function*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一个函数确实在中心附近由幂级数表示，逐阶求导并在中心代入，给出 $c_n=f^{(n)}(a)/n!$。反过来，我们据此定义 [[Taylor级数]]；它的有限部分和就是 [[Taylor多项式]]。但是构造了这些系数，还没有证明级数收敛到原函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a function has a power-series representation near the centre, successive derivatives give $c_n=f^{(n)}(a)/n!$. These coefficients define its [[Taylor级数|Taylor series]], whose finite partial sums are [[Taylor多项式|Taylor polynomials]]. Constructing coefficients alone does not prove convergence to the function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

写 $f(x)=P_{N,a}(x)+R_{N,a}(x)$。[[Taylor级数等于函数]] 的精确条件是固定 $x$ 后 $R_{N,a}(x)\to0$。例如 $e^x$ 在连接 $0$ 与固定 $x$ 的区间上，所有阶导数都不超过 $e^{|x|}$；[[Taylor余项]] 给
$$
|R_{N,0}(x)|\le e^{|x|}\frac{|x|^{N+1}}{(N+1)!}\longrightarrow0.
$$
因此 [[指数函数幂级数|$e^x=\sum x^n/n!$]] 在整个实轴成立。有限次数时，同一个余项界还能给数值精度：当 $|x|\le0.1$，用 $1+x+x^2/2$ 的误差小于 $1.85\times10^{-4}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exact criterion for [[Taylor级数等于函数|equality with the function]] is a vanishing remainder at each fixed input. The [[Taylor余项|remainder bound]] displayed above proves the [[指数函数幂级数|exponential expansion]] on the whole real line. At finite degree it also controls accuracy: the quadratic approximation on $|x|\le0.1$ has error below $1.85\times10^{-4}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么必须多做这一步？[[光滑不代表解析]] 的函数在 $x\ne0$ 取 $e^{-1/x^2}$、在零点取零，所有阶导数在零点都为零。因此它的 Taylor 级数恒为零，却不能代表附近为正的函数；这里级数自身处处收敛，错的是把其和认作原函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[光滑不代表解析|smooth nonanalytic example]] equals $e^{-1/x^2}$ away from zero and zero at zero. Every derivative at zero vanishes, so its Taylor series is identically zero and converges everywhere, yet misses the positive function nearby. Convergence and representation are separate claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 用已有展开生成新展开，再检查范围与误差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Build new expansions from established ones, keeping domains and errors explicit*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[正弦函数幂级数]] 和 [[余弦函数幂级数]] 使用导数的四步循环；各阶导数的绝对值不超过一，所以余项也在每个实数处趋零。得到
$$
\sin x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^nx^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!},\qquad
\cos x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^nx^{2n}}{(2n)!}.
$$
这里角度使用弧度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivative cycles for [[正弦函数幂级数|sine]] and [[余弦函数幂级数|cosine]] have magnitude at most one, so their remainders vanish at every real input. The displayed expansions use radians.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在收敛半径严格内部，[[幂级数逐项求导]] 与 [[幂级数逐项积分]] 允许把无穷级数当作多项式操作；半径保持不变，但端点可能改变。例如从 $1/(1+x)=\sum(-x)^n$ 在 $|x|<1$ 上逐项积分，得到 [[对数函数幂级数]]。在 $x=1$，不能仅凭内部公式代入；用有限几何式积分的余项可证明遗漏绝对值不超过 $1/(N+1)$，从而得到交错调和级数的和为 $\ln2$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inside the radius, [[幂级数逐项求导|termwise differentiation]] and [[幂级数逐项积分|integration]] are valid and preserve the radius, though endpoints may change. Integrating the geometric expansion of $1/(1+x)$ gives the [[对数函数幂级数|logarithm series]] for $|x|<1$. A finite geometric remainder, bounded by $1/(N+1)$ after integration to one, independently proves its endpoint sum is $\ln2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[幂级数乘法]] 按次数收集卷积系数。共同内部保证乘法合法，但乘积的收敛半径**至少**为两半径的较小值，抵消可使它更大。例如 $(1-x)\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n$ 的乘积系数给出常数 $1$：运算先在 $|x|<1$ 得到等式，而结果的常数级数可以在全轴求值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[幂级数乘法|Multiplication]] collects coefficients by convolution. It is valid in the common interior, and the product radius is at least the smaller input radius. Cancellation may enlarge it: multiplying the geometric series by $1-x$ leaves the constant series one. The original multiplication is justified on $|x|<1$, while the resulting constant series has infinite radius.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后，用 [[幂级数代入]] 把 $u=-x^2$ 代入指数展开，得到 $e^{-x^2}$；再积分，便有 [[误差函数幂级数]]。[[误差函数]] 本身定义为
$$
\operatorname{erf}(x)=\frac2{\sqrt\pi}\int_0^x e^{-t^2}\,dt,
$$
其归一化复用 [[高斯积分]]。这里我们先有函数的积分定义，再得到计算它的级数；无需强求初等原函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[幂级数代入|Substitution]] of $u=-x^2$ into the exponential expansion, followed by integration, gives the [[误差函数幂级数|error-function series]]. The [[误差函数|error function]] is defined by the displayed integral and normalized using the shared [[高斯积分|Gaussian integral]]. Its series supplies computation without an elementary antiderivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8. 三个贯通问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three checks connecting the chapter*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- $\sum(-1)^{n-1}/\sqrt n$ 属于哪一种收敛？能否用下一项控制误差？
> 幅度单调趋零，所以交错判别给收敛；绝对值是 $p=1/2$ 的发散级数，因此条件收敛。按原顺序取前 $N$ 项，误差至多 $1/\sqrt{N+1}$。保证收敛不代表这个误差界下降得快。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Classify the alternating inverse-square-root series and bound its error. **Answer:** The alternating test gives convergence, while its absolute-value p-series diverges. It is conditionally convergent, with original-order error at most $1/\sqrt{N+1}$. Convergence need not mean rapid accuracy improvement.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- $\sum x^n/n$ 求导后，能否继续保留左端点 $-1$？
> 不能。内部求导得到 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x^{n-1}$，在 $x=-1$ 的通项不趋零。求导保留半径一，但原来的条件收敛端点丢失。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Does differentiating $\sum x^n/n$ preserve convergence at $-1$? **Answer:** No. The derivative series is geometric and its terms fail to vanish there. The radius stays one, but that endpoint is lost.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 若只写“$f(x)\approx P_3(x)$”，还缺什么？
> 缺展开中心、目标输入范围，以及能在这段范围上成立的误差依据。知道前三阶导数只能构造多项式，不能自动证明近似达到指定精度。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** What is missing from “$f(x)\approx P_3(x)$”? **Answer:** The expansion centre, target range, and a valid error bound on that range. Derivative matching constructs a polynomial but does not guarantee the requested accuracy.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
