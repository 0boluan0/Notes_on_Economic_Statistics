<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Matrix multiplication can be read one entry, one column, one row, or one outer product at a time. These readings explain why the same row operations can solve several systems together. Applying them to $[A\mid I]$ records both the reduction of $A$ and the matrix that undoes it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵乘法可以逐个元素、逐列、逐行，也可以逐个外积来理解。这些读法解释了为什么同一套行操作能同时解多个方程组：对 $[A\mid I]$ 做行化简，既化简了 $A$，也记录下能撤销 $A$ 的矩阵。

## 1. Four readings of the same product
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同一个乘积的四种读法*

$$
A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n},\qquad B\in\mathbb R^{n\times p},\qquad C=AB\in\mathbb R^{m\times p}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inner dimensions must match. The common dimension $n$ is the number of terms being combined; the outer dimensions $m,p$ give the size of the result. The four readings below regroup the same [[矩阵乘法|definition of matrix multiplication]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内侧尺寸必须相同。共同维数 $n$ 决定每次组合有多少项；外侧的 $m,p$ 决定结果尺寸。下面四种读法重新组织的是同一个[[矩阵乘法|矩阵乘法定义]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### 1.1 Row times column: one entry
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行乘列：得到一个元素*

$$
c_{ij}=\sum_{k=1}^{n}a_{ik}b_{kj}
=\underbrace{A_{i,:}}_{1\times n}\underbrace{B_{:,j}}_{n\times1}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose row $i$ of $A$ and column $j$ of $B$. Their dot product gives the entry at position $(i,j)$. This is the usual calculation rule, rather than entrywise multiplication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $A$ 的第 $i$ 行与 $B$ 的第 $j$ 列，它们的点积就是结果的 $(i,j)$ 元素。这是通常的计算规则，并不是对应位置逐元素相乘。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### 1.2 Matrix times column: one output column
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵乘列：得到结果的一整列*

$$
C_{:,j}=AB_{:,j}=\sum_{k=1}^{n}b_{kj}A_{:,k}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Column $j$ of $B$ supplies scalar weights for the **columns of $A$**. For example, if $A=[a_1\ a_2]$, then $A(2,3)^T=2a_1+3a_2$. Thus every column of $AB$ belongs to the column space of $A$. This [[矩阵乘积逐列作用|columnwise reading]] will turn $AX=I$ into several ordinary systems $Ax_j=e_j$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$B$ 的第 $j$ 列提供标量系数，对 **$A$ 的各列**作线性组合。例如 $A=[a_1\ a_2]$ 时，$A(2,3)^T=2a_1+3a_2$。因此 $AB$ 的每一列都在 $A$ 的列空间中。这个[[矩阵乘积逐列作用|逐列读法]]稍后会把 $AX=I$ 拆成几个普通方程组 $Ax_j=e_j$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### 1.3 Row times matrix: one output row
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行乘矩阵：得到结果的一整行*

$$
C_{i,:}=A_{i,:}B=\sum_{k=1}^{n}a_{ik}B_{k,:}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Row $i$ of $A$ supplies scalar weights for the **rows of $B$**. In particular, $(2,3)B=2B_{1,:}+3B_{2,:}$. This [[矩阵乘积逐行作用|rowwise reading]] explains the previous lecture: left multiplication by $E$ performs row operations on the matrix to its right. In both readings, individual entries supply scalar weights; neither whole matrix becomes a scalar.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$A$ 的第 $i$ 行提供标量系数，对 **$B$ 的各行**作线性组合。例如 $(2,3)B=2B_{1,:}+3B_{2,:}$。这个[[矩阵乘积逐行作用|逐行读法]]解释了上一讲的结论：左乘 $E$，会对右侧矩阵作行操作。两种读法中，充当标量系数的是具体元素，并不是某个完整矩阵变成了标量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### 1.4 Column times row: one matrix contribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*列乘行：得到一整个矩阵贡献项*

$$
AB=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\underbrace{A_{:,k}}_{m\times1}\underbrace{B_{k,:}}_{1\times p}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pair column $k$ of $A$ with row $k$ of $B$. Each [[矩阵乘积外积展开|outer product]] has the full size $m\times p$; adding these $n$ matrices produces $AB$. A single example is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $A$ 的第 $k$ 列与 $B$ 的第 $k$ 行配对，每个[[矩阵乘积外积展开|外积]]都是完整的 $m\times p$ 矩阵；把这 $n$ 个矩阵相加，才得到 $AB$。一个单独的外积是

$$
\begin{pmatrix}2\\3\\4\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&6\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}2&12\\3&18\\4&24\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Its columns lie on the line spanned by $(2,3,4)^T$ in $\mathbb R^3$; its rows lie on the line spanned by $(1,6)$ in $\mathbb R^2$. These are lines in different spaces. Because both factors are nonzero, this outer product has [[秩一矩阵外积刻画|rank one]]. An individual term can instead be zero, and a sum of outer products need not have rank one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它的列落在 $\mathbb R^3$ 中由 $(2,3,4)^T$ 张成的直线上，行落在 $\mathbb R^2$ 中由 $(1,6)$ 张成的直线上。这是不同空间中的两条直线。两个因子都非零，因此这个外积的[[秩一矩阵外积刻画|秩为一]]。一般展开中的某一项也可能为零，多个外积之和则未必仍是秩一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 2. Blocks obey the same rule when their sizes match
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*分块之后，尺寸匹配时仍按同一规则相乘*

$$
\begin{pmatrix}A_{11}&A_{12}\\A_{21}&A_{22}\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}B_{11}&B_{12}\\B_{21}&B_{22}\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}
A_{11}B_{11}+A_{12}B_{21}&A_{11}B_{12}+A_{12}B_{22}\\
A_{21}B_{11}+A_{22}B_{21}&A_{21}B_{12}+A_{22}B_{22}
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[分块矩阵乘法|Block multiplication]] groups the shared-index sum into larger pieces. If $A_{ik}$ is $m_i\times n_k$, then $B_{kj}$ must be $n_k\times p_j$. The blocks can be rectangular and unequal in size. What must match is each inner width with its corresponding inner height; the order of the factors must also stay unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[分块矩阵乘法]]把共同指标上的求和分成较大的几组。若 $A_{ik}$ 是 $m_i\times n_k$，则 $B_{kj}$ 必须是 $n_k\times p_j$。各块可以是矩形，大小也可以不同；必须匹配的是对应的内侧宽度与高度，因子的顺序也必须保留。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the augmented matrix used below, the useful special case is simply
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于下文要用的增广矩阵，最有用的特例就是

$$
E[A\mid I]=[EA\mid EI]=[EA\mid E].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 3. An inverse must undo the whole transformation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*逆矩阵必须撤销整个变换*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a square $n\times n$ matrix, an [[逆矩阵|inverse]] satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $n\times n$ 方阵，[[逆矩阵]]满足

$$
A^{-1}A=AA^{-1}=I.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The terms **invertible** and **nonsingular** describe the same property. A square matrix is invertible exactly when it has full rank $n$. In elimination, this means a pivot in every column; Gauss–Jordan reduction can then turn it into $I$. The notation $A^{-1}$ does not mean taking the reciprocal of each entry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**Invertible（可逆）**与 **nonsingular（非奇异）**表示同一个性质。方阵可逆，当且仅当它满秩，即秩为 $n$；在消元中，这意味着每一列都有主元，随后 Gauss–Jordan 化简能把它变为 $I$。$A^{-1}$ 不是把每个元素分别取倒数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The crucial obstruction is a **nonzero** vector sent to zero. If $Az=0$ with $z\ne0$, an inverse would imply $z=A^{-1}Az=0$, a contradiction. The zero vector itself tells us nothing about invertibility, because every matrix sends it to zero. See [[非零齐次解与不可逆|the nonzero-null-vector criterion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键障碍是：有一个**非零**向量被送到零。如果 $Az=0$ 且 $z\ne0$，假设逆存在，就有 $z=A^{-1}Az=0$，产生矛盾。零向量本身不能说明可逆性，因为每个矩阵都会把它送到零。参见[[非零齐次解与不可逆|非零齐次解判据]]。

$$
S=\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\2&6\end{pmatrix},\qquad
S\begin{pmatrix}3\\-1\end{pmatrix}=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the two columns lie on one line. Both $0$ and $(3,-1)^T$ produce the same output, so no inverse transformation could recover which input was used. This makes singularity a loss of information, rather than merely a failed calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里两列共线，$0$ 与 $(3,-1)^T$ 产生同一个输出；逆变换就无法从输出恢复原来是哪一个输入。非奇异性讨论的因此是能否恢复信息，而不只是计算是否成功。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 4. Gauss–Jordan: solve all inverse columns together
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*Gauss–Jordan：同时求出逆矩阵的所有列*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $X=[x_1\ \cdots\ x_n]$. By the columnwise reading,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $X=[x_1\ \cdots\ x_n]$。按逐列读法，

$$
AX=I\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
Ax_1=e_1,\ \ldots,\ Ax_n=e_n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All systems have the same coefficient matrix $A$, so they can share the same row operations. Put all right-hand sides into $[A\mid I]$, reduce the left block to $I$, and read the solutions from the right block. Gaussian elimination first reaches an upper triangular matrix; [[Gauss-Jordan求逆|Gauss–Jordan]] continues by clearing above each pivot and scaling pivots to one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些方程组的系数矩阵都是 $A$，因此可以共用一套行操作。把全部右端一起放进 $[A\mid I]$，将左半块化为 $I$，再从右半块读取解。高斯消元先得到上三角矩阵；[[Gauss-Jordan求逆|Gauss–Jordan]] 进一步消掉主元上方的元素，并把主元归一。

$$
\left[\begin{array}{cc|cc}1&3&1&0\\2&7&0&1\end{array}\right]
\xrightarrow{R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1}
\left[\begin{array}{cc|cc}1&3&1&0\\0&1&-2&1\end{array}\right]
\xrightarrow{R_1\leftarrow R_1-3R_2}
\left[\begin{array}{cc|cc}1&0&7&-3\\0&1&-2&1\end{array}\right].
$$

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\2&7\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}7&-3\\-2&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Why the right block is the inverse.** Let $E$ be the product of all the elementary matrices used. Applying the same operations to both blocks gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**为什么右半块就是逆矩阵？** 令 $E$ 为这次用到的全部初等矩阵按操作次序相乘的结果。对两块施行同一套操作，有

$$
E[A\mid I]=[EA\mid E]=[I\mid E],\qquad EA=I.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every elementary row operation is reversible, so $E$ is invertible. From $EA=I$, multiply on the left by $E^{-1}$ to obtain $A=E^{-1}$; hence $AE=I$ as well. Thus $E$ is the two-sided inverse $A^{-1}$. This argument proves the conclusion without assuming in advance that $A^{-1}$ exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个初等行操作都可以撤销，所以 $E$ 可逆。由 $EA=I$，左乘 $E^{-1}$ 得 $A=E^{-1}$，于是也有 $AE=I$。因此 $E$ 就是双侧逆 $A^{-1}$。这段推理无需预先假设 $A^{-1}$ 存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the square left block cannot be reduced to $I$, the ordinary inverse does not exist. The right block should only be called $A^{-1}$ after the left block has actually become $I$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果左侧方阵无法化为 $I$，普通逆矩阵就不存在。只有在左半块确实化为 $I$ 后，才能把右半块称作 $A^{-1}$。

### 5.1 Exercise 3.1: the distributive law
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*练习 3.1：分配律*

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
C=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\5&6\end{pmatrix}.
$$

$$
AB=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\3&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
AC=\begin{pmatrix}10&12\\20&24\end{pmatrix},\qquad
AB+AC=\begin{pmatrix}11&12\\23&24\end{pmatrix}.
$$

$$
B+C=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\5&6\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A(B+C)=\begin{pmatrix}11&12\\23&24\end{pmatrix}=AB+AC.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This example checks the [[矩阵乘法分配律|distributive law]]. The general reason is that each entry is a sum: $\sum_k a_{ik}(b_{kj}+c_{kj})=\sum_k a_{ik}b_{kj}+\sum_k a_{ik}c_{kj}$. The order of the factors is unchanged; distributivity does not imply commutativity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子检验了[[矩阵乘法分配律]]。一般理由是，每个元素都是求和：$\sum_k a_{ik}(b_{kj}+c_{kj})=\sum_k a_{ik}b_{kj}+\sum_k a_{ik}c_{kj}$。因子的次序始终保留；分配律不意味着交换律。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### 5.2 Exercise 3.2: invert an upper triangular matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*练习 3.2：上三角矩阵求逆*

$$
U=\begin{pmatrix}1&a&b\\0&1&c\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The pivots are already one. First remove $a$ from row 1, then $c$ from row 2. The first operation also changes the entry $b$ to $b-ac$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主元已经都是一。先消掉第一行的 $a$，再消掉第二行的 $c$；第一步也把 $b$ 改成了 $b-ac$：

$$
\left[\begin{array}{ccc|ccc}1&a&b&1&0&0\\0&1&c&0&1&0\\0&0&1&0&0&1\end{array}\right]
\xrightarrow{R_1\leftarrow R_1-aR_2}
\left[\begin{array}{ccc|ccc}1&0&b-ac&1&-a&0\\0&1&c&0&1&0\\0&0&1&0&0&1\end{array}\right].
$$

$$
\xrightarrow{R_2\leftarrow R_2-cR_3}
\left[\begin{array}{ccc|ccc}1&0&b-ac&1&-a&0\\0&1&0&0&1&-c\\0&0&1&0&0&1\end{array}\right]
\xrightarrow{R_1\leftarrow R_1-(b-ac)R_3}
\left[\begin{array}{ccc|ccc}1&0&0&1&-a&ac-b\\0&1&0&0&1&-c\\0&0&1&0&0&1\end{array}\right].
$$

$$
\boxed{U^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&-a&ac-b\\0&1&-c\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The term $ac-b$ accounts for the indirect effect through row 2 as well as the original entry $b$; simply changing every off-diagonal sign would miss that effect. A direct check of the top-right entry of $UU^{-1}$ gives $(ac-b)+a(-c)+b=0$. The other off-diagonal entries cancel too, and all diagonal entries are one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$ac-b$ 同时补偿了经由第二行产生的间接影响与原来的 $b$；仅把所有非对角元素换成负号，会漏掉这一项。直接检查 $UU^{-1}$ 的右上角，得到 $(ac-b)+a(-c)+b=0$；其余非对角元素也抵消，对角元素均为一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Notation:** row reduction is written $[U\mid I]\longrightarrow[I\mid U^{-1}]$. Both augmented matrices are $3\times6$. Then extract the right $3\times3$ block as $U^{-1}$. A row-operation arrow indicates row equivalence; it does not assert that the original and reduced matrices are equal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**记号：** 行化简应写成 $[U\mid I]\longrightarrow[I\mid U^{-1}]$，两侧增广矩阵都是 $3\times6$，之后再把右边的 $3\times3$ 块单独写成 $U^{-1}$。行操作箭头表示行等价，不表示化简前后的矩阵相等。
