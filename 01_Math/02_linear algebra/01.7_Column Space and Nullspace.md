<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same matrix defines two different sets: its column space contains all attainable **outputs**, while its nullspace contains all **inputs** sent to zero. Both are subspaces because they remain closed under every linear combination. The exercises connect this principle to intersections, sums, and shifted solution sets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个矩阵定义两个不同集合：列空间收集全部可达的**输出**，零空间收集被映成零的全部**输入**。二者都是子空间，因为它们对任意线性组合封闭。本讲习题把这个原则接到交集、子空间之和及平移后的解集。

## 1. Closure: the bridge from the previous lecture
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*封闭性：接回上一讲*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recall [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.6_Transposes, Permutations, Vector Spaces#4. A vector space allows every linear combination|the previous lecture's closure test]]. A nonempty subset $W$ of a real vector space is a [[子空间|subspace]] exactly when, using the inherited operations, every $u,v\in W$ and every $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb R$ satisfy
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接回[[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.6_Transposes, Permutations, Vector Spaces#4. A vector space allows every linear combination|上一讲的封闭性判据]]：实向量空间的非空子集 $W$ 使用原有运算时，是[[子空间]]当且仅当任意 $u,v\in W$ 和任意实数 $\alpha,\beta$ 都满足

$$
\alpha u+\beta v\in W.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Addition and subtraction alone are insufficient. Integer lattice points $\mathbb Z^2$ remain integer-valued after addition or subtraction, but $\tfrac12(1,0)=(\tfrac12,0)$ leaves the set. The required multiplication is by **any real scalar**. Containing zero is necessary, but by itself does not establish closure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只检查加减还不够。例如整数格点 $\mathbb Z^2$ 加减后仍是整数格点，但 $\tfrac12(1,0)=(\tfrac12,0)$ 已离开集合；必须允许**任意实数数乘**。包含零向量是必要条件，但单凭这一点不能证明封闭性。

## 2. Union, intersection, and sum are three operations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*并集、交集与子空间之和是三种运算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $S,T$ be subspaces of the same real vector space. A union chooses a vector already in either set; an intersection requires membership in both; a sum takes one vector from each set and adds them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $S,T$ 是同一实向量空间的子空间。并集选取原本就在任一集合中的向量；交集要求同时属于两边；子空间之和则从两边各取一个向量，再相加。

$$
\begin{aligned}
S\cup T&=\{v:v\in S\ \text{or}\ v\in T\},\\
S\cap T&=\{v:v\in S\ \text{and}\ v\in T\},\\
S+T&=\{s+t:s\in S,\ t\in T\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

![[98_attachment/linear_algebra/mit18_06sc/mit18.06sc-l07-union-intersection-sum.png|900]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $S$ is the horizontal axis and $T$ the vertical axis. Their union contains only the two axes, their intersection is $\{0\}$, and their sum fills $\mathbb R^2$: every $(a,b)$ equals $(a,0)+(0,b)$. The shaded panel shows a finite window of the whole plane.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图中 $S$ 是横轴，$T$ 是纵轴。并集只有两条轴，交集只有 $\{0\}$，子空间之和则填满 $\mathbb R^2$，因为任意 $(a,b)=(a,0)+(0,b)$。阴影只是整个平面的有限显示窗口。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The union fails addition: $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ belong to it, but $(1,1)$ does not. In general, the [[子空间并集判据|union is a subspace exactly when one subspace contains the other]]. Thus, for a plane $P$ and a line $L$ through zero, $P\cup L$ fails only when $L$ is not contained in $P$; if $L\subseteq P$, their union is simply $P$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

并集不对加法封闭：$(1,0)$ 和 $(0,1)$ 属于并集，但 $(1,1)$ 不属于。一般而言，[[子空间并集判据|并集是子空间当且仅当一方包含另一方]]。所以对过原点的平面 $P$ 与直线 $L$，必须在 $L$ 不包含于 $P$ 时，才能断言 $P\cup L$ 不是子空间；若 $L\subseteq P$，并集就是 $P$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Their [[子空间的交集|intersection is always a subspace]]. Zero belongs to both. If $u,v$ belong to both, $\alpha u+\beta v$ remains in $S$ and also in $T$, so it remains in their intersection. For a line outside a plane in $\mathbb R^3$, both through zero, that intersection is $\{0\}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它们的[[子空间的交集|交集总是子空间]]：零向量属于两边；若 $u,v$ 同时在两边，则 $\alpha u+\beta v$ 在 $S$ 中，也在 $T$ 中，因此仍在交集中。在 $\mathbb R^3$ 中，若过原点的直线不包含于过原点的平面，二者交集就是 $\{0\}$。

## 3. Exercise 6.1: why the sum is a subspace
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*习题 6.1：子空间之和为何仍是子空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definition of [[子空间之和|$S+T$]] permits new vectors formed by addition. To prove closure, start with **two arbitrary members of the sum**, $s_1+t_1$ and $s_2+t_2$. Regroup their linear combination into one vector from each original subspace:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[子空间之和|$S+T$ 的定义]]允许通过相加得到新向量。证明封闭时，先取**和空间中的任意两个元素** $s_1+t_1$ 与 $s_2+t_2$，再把它们的线性组合重新分组：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha(s_1+t_1)+\beta(s_2+t_2)
&=(\alpha s_1+\beta s_2)+(\alpha t_1+\beta t_2)\\
&\in S+T.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first bracket lies in $S$, the second in $T$; also $0=0+0$ belongs to the sum. This proves both addition and scalar closure, rather than merely asserting that the rules hold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一个括号属于 $S$，第二个属于 $T$；而 $0=0+0$ 也在和空间中。这一步同时证明加法与数乘封闭，不能只用“自然满足规则”代替。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Part (b) asks why [[子空间和与并集的张成|the sum equals the span of the union]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

(b) 要求解释[[子空间和与并集的张成|子空间之和为何等于并集的张成]]：

$$
S+T=\operatorname{span}(S\cup T).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every $s+t$ is a linear combination of vectors in the union, giving one inclusion. Conversely, take any finite linear combination of vectors from the union. Assign each term to a subspace containing it, group the $S$ terms and the $T$ terms, and use closure inside each group. The result has the form $s+t$. A vector in both sets may be assigned to either group.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一方面，每个 $s+t$ 都是并集中向量的线性组合。反过来，任取并集中向量的有限线性组合，把每项归到一个包含它的子空间，再将 $S$ 中的项合成一个 $s$，将 $T$ 中的项合成一个 $t$，结果就是 $s+t$。同时属于两边的向量归到任意一边即可。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two **distinct** lines through zero, the sum is their plane and the union is only those two lines. In $\mathbb R^m$ with $m>2$, that plane is generally a proper subspace of $\mathbb R^m$. If the lines coincide, both the union and sum equal the original line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两条**不同**的过原点直线，其和是它们张成的平面，而并集只是两条线。若环境为 $m>2$ 的 $\mathbb R^m$，这个平面通常并非整个环境空间。若两条直线重合，并集与和都等于原来的直线。

## 4. Column space: which outputs can be reached?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*列空间：哪些输出能够到达？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$, the [[列空间|column space]] consists of every linear combination of its columns. Equivalently, it is the set of all outputs $Ax$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，[[列空间]]由各列的全部线性组合构成，也就是全部输出 $Ax$ 的集合：

$$
C(A)=\{Ax:x\in\mathbb R^n\}
     =\operatorname{span}\{a_1,\ldots,a_n\}
     \subseteq\mathbb R^m.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero is an output, and $\alpha Ax+\beta Ay=A(\alpha x+\beta y)$ is another output, proving closure. The equation $Ax=b$ has a solution **if and only if** $b\in C(A)$: membership means exactly that some coefficients combine the columns to produce $b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零向量是一个输出，而 $\alpha Ax+\beta Ay=A(\alpha x+\beta y)$ 仍是输出，因此列空间封闭。$Ax=b$ 有解**当且仅当** $b\in C(A)$：属于列空间，正是存在一组系数把各列组合成 $b$。

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&2\\
2&1&3\\
3&1&4\\
4&1&5
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad a_3=a_1+a_2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the lecture example, the third column adds no new direction. The first two are independent: $r a_1+s a_2=0$ gives $r+s=0$ and $2r+s=0$, hence $r=s=0$. Therefore $C(A)$ is a **two-dimensional plane inside $\mathbb R^4$**, not all of $\mathbb R^4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本讲例子中，第三列没有增加新方向。前两列独立，因为 $r a_1+s a_2=0$ 的前两行给出 $r+s=0$、$2r+s=0$，从而 $r=s=0$。因此 $C(A)$ 是 **$\mathbb R^4$ 中的二维平面**，并非整个 $\mathbb R^4$。

$$
r a_1+s a_2=(r+s,\ 2r+s,\ 3r+s,\ 4r+s)^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The target $(1,2,3,4)^T=a_1$ is attainable. The target $(0,0,0,1)^T$ is not: its first two entries force $r=s=0$, contradicting its last entry. Four equations with three unknowns therefore need not be inconsistent; the answer depends on the chosen right-hand side.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

目标 $(1,2,3,4)^T=a_1$ 可达；$(0,0,0,1)^T$ 不可达，因为前两个分量迫使 $r=s=0$，这与最后一个分量为 $1$ 矛盾。所以“四个方程、三个未知数”并不意味着每个右端都无解，仍要检查具体的 $b$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 5. Nullspace: which inputs disappear?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*零空间：哪些输入被映成零？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[零空间|nullspace]] contains **every solution** of the homogeneous equation, including the zero input:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[零空间]]包含齐次方程的**全部解**，包括零输入：

$$
N(A)=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:Ax=0\}\subseteq\mathbb R^n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $Au=Av=0$, then $A(\alpha u+\beta v)=\alpha Au+\beta Av=0$. Together with $A0=0$, this proves that $N(A)$ is a subspace. In the same example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $Au=Av=0$，则 $A(\alpha u+\beta v)=\alpha Au+\beta Av=0$；再加上 $A0=0$，便证明 $N(A)$ 是子空间。对同一个例子，

$$
\begin{aligned}
Ax&=x_1a_1+x_2a_2+x_3(a_1+a_2)\\
  &=(x_1+x_3)a_1+(x_2+x_3)a_2.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $a_1,a_2$ are independent, this is zero exactly when $x_1+x_3=x_2+x_3=0$. Hence all solutions, not just one, are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $a_1,a_2$ 独立，上式为零当且仅当 $x_1+x_3=x_2+x_3=0$。因此全部解为

$$
N(A)=\{t(1,1,-1)^T:t\in\mathbb R\},
\qquad \dim N(A)=1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Object | What its vectors represent | Ambient space | Shape in this example |
|---|---|---|---|
| $C(A)$ | All attainable outputs $b$ | $\mathbb R^m$ | A plane in $\mathbb R^4$ |
| $N(A)$ | Inputs $x$ sent to zero | $\mathbb R^n$ | A line in $\mathbb R^3$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

列空间看输出 $b$，所以看行数 $m$；零空间看输入 $x$，所以看未知量个数即列数 $n$。本例中两者分别是 $\mathbb R^4$ 内的平面和 $\mathbb R^3$ 内的直线。**所在空间有几个坐标，与子空间自身有几个独立方向，是两件事。**

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 6. Nonzero right-hand sides shift the nullspace
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*非零右端把零空间平移*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $Ax=b$ has a particular solution $x_p$, its entire solution set is [[特解加零空间|$x_p+N(A)$]]. Both directions matter:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $Ax=b$ 存在特解 $x_p$，则整个解集为[[特解加零空间|$x_p+N(A)$]]，两个方向都要说明：

$$
\begin{aligned}
Az=0&\ \Longrightarrow\ A(x_p+z)=b,\\
Ax=b&\ \Longrightarrow\ A(x-x_p)=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $b=(1,2,3,4)^T$, take $x_p=(1,0,0)^T$. The solutions form a line parallel to $N(A)$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $b=(1,2,3,4)^T$，一个特解为 $x_p=(1,0,0)^T$。所有解构成一条与 $N(A)$ 平行的直线：

$$
x=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}
 +t\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\-1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad t\in\mathbb R.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It contains $(1,0,0)^T$ and $(0,-1,1)^T$, but not zero. For any fixed $b\ne0$, the solution set is not a subspace: zero cannot solve the equation, and adding two solutions gives output $2b$ instead of $b$. If no particular solution exists, the solution set is empty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条线经过 $(1,0,0)^T$ 与 $(0,-1,1)^T$，但不经过原点。对固定的 $b\ne0$，解集不是子空间：零向量不可能满足方程，而且两个解相加的输出为 $2b$，不再是 $b$。若不存在特解，解集则为空。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 7. Exercise 6.2: a parallel plane and a typo
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*习题 6.2：平行平面与题面笔误*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The problem sheet prints the homogeneous plane as $x-3y-x=0$. The intended equation is **$x-3y-z=0$**, as confirmed by the official solution. Rearrange the nonhomogeneous equation $x-3y-z=12$ to obtain $x=12+3y+z$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

题单把对应齐次平面误写为 $x-3y-x=0$。结合原方程和官方答案，应为 **$x-3y-z=0$**。将非齐次方程 $x-3y-z=12$ 改写为 $x=12+3y+z$：

$$
\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\\z\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}12\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}
+y\begin{pmatrix}3\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}
+z\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two direction vectors independently satisfy the homogeneous equation and span its plane. Adding $(12,0,0)^T$ moves that plane to the required one. The normal vector $(1,-3,-1)^T$ is unchanged; the shifted plane misses zero, so it is an affine plane rather than a vector subspace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个方向向量各自满足齐次方程，并独立地张成齐次平面。加上 $(12,0,0)^T$，便将其平移到目标平面。法向量 $(1,-3,-1)^T$ 不变；平移后的平面不经过原点，因此是仿射平面，不是向量子空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 8. Exercise 6.3: stacking matrices imposes both constraints
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*习题 6.3：上下拼接就是同时施加两组约束*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation places **every row of $B$ below every row of $A$**. For this to be possible, $A$ and $B$ must have the same number of columns; their row counts may differ. If $A$ is $p\times n$ and $B$ is $q\times n$, then $C$ is $(p+q)\times n$. By [[分块矩阵乘法|block multiplication]],
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个记号表示把 **$B$ 的全部行放在 $A$ 的全部行下面**。因此两者必须列数相同，行数可以不同。若 $A$ 为 $p\times n$、$B$ 为 $q\times n$，则 $C$ 为 $(p+q)\times n$。依据[[分块矩阵乘法]]，

$$
C=\begin{bmatrix}A\\B\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
Cx=\begin{bmatrix}Ax\\Bx\end{bmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the whole output to be zero, both blocks must be zero. This gives the [[纵向拼接的零空间|nullspace of a vertical stack]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要让整列输出为零，上下两块必须同时为零。因此[[纵向拼接的零空间]]满足

$$
\begin{aligned}
x\in N(C)
&\iff Ax=0\ \text{and}\ Bx=0\\
&\iff x\in N(A)\cap N(B),\\
N(C)&=N(A)\cap N(B).
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, let $A=(1,0,0)$ and $B=(0,1,0)$. Then $Ax=0$ means $x_1=0$, while $Bx=0$ means $x_2=0$. Stacking them requires both, so $N(C)=\{(0,0,t)^T:t\in\mathbb R\}$, the intersection of the two coordinate planes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如取 $A=(1,0,0)$、$B=(0,1,0)$。$Ax=0$ 要求 $x_1=0$，$Bx=0$ 要求 $x_2=0$；上下拼接后必须同时满足，所以 $N(C)=\{(0,0,t)^T:t\in\mathbb R\}$，正是两个坐标平面的交线。
