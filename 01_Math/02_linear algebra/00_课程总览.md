### 2. 正交投影与最小二乘

*2. Orthogonal Projection and Least Squares*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解 Ax=b 先确定可解空间；当方程无解时，正交投影把目标移到列空间中并产生最小二乘。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solving $Ax=b$ first identifies the space in which an exact solution can exist. When the system is inconsistent, orthogonal projection moves the target into the column space and produces the least-squares solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用上一节已经建立的正交与正交补，有限维中的正交直和分解给出唯一的子空间分量；正交投影把这个分量定义成 $P_Sb$，最近点定理再把“离子空间最近”化成“残差与子空间正交”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the notions of orthogonality and orthogonal complement established above, the finite-dimensional orthogonal direct-sum theorem gives a unique subspace component. Orthogonal projection defines that component as $P_Sb$, and the nearest-point theorem then turns “closest to the subspace” into “residual orthogonal to the subspace.”

Once the geometric projection is written as a linear operator, symmetry and idempotence characterise its matrix. An orthonormal basis gives $QQ^T$ directly; a general full-column-rank basis gives $A(A^TA)^{-1}A^T$ through its Gram matrix.
