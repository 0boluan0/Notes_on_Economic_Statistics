Build the language of spaces first: a vector space specifies addition and scalar multiplication, and a subspace inherits these operations. The union of two subspaces stays a subspace exactly when one contains the other. Spanning asks whether every target can be reached; independence asks whether coefficients are unique. A basis satisfies both, and the common number of vectors in any basis defines dimension.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先建立最基础的空间语言：向量空间提供运算环境，子空间继承这套运算。两个子空间的并集通常会破坏封闭性，只有一方包含另一方时例外。张成回答能否覆盖目标空间，线性无关回答表示中是否有冗余；两者合起来形成基，基的大小定义维数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.6_Transposes, Permutations, Vector Spaces|Session 1.6 classroom note]] makes closure concrete. A nonzero vector brings its entire line of scalar multiples; a second independent direction brings the whole plane. This explains the complete list of subspaces in two and three dimensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.6_Transposes, Permutations, Vector Spaces|第六讲课堂笔记]]把封闭性落实为几何：一个非零向量带来整条倍数直线，第二个独立方向带来整片平面，由此得到二维、三维中的全部子空间类型。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.7_Column Space and Nullspace|Session 1.7 note]] distinguishes three operations: union retains existing members, intersection keeps common members, and the sum allows one member from each side to be added. Closure explains why their outcomes differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.7_Column Space and Nullspace|第七讲正式笔记]]区分三种运算：并集保留原有成员，交集保留共同成员，子空间之和允许从两边各取一个向量相加；封闭性解释三者为何不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ordinary sum tells us which vectors can be formed. A direct sum adds a useful question: is the component chosen from each subspace unique? The coordinate-axis example makes this distinction explicit before the later basis and dimension arguments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通的和说明哪些向量可以组成；直和再问一步：来自各子空间的分量是否唯一？先看坐标轴的例子，可以为后面关于基与维数的论证准备好这种区别。

![[直和]]


![[张成]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.10_Independence, Basis and Dimension|Session 1.10 note]] joins these definitions: spanning guarantees a representation, independence guarantees its uniqueness, and a basis satisfies both. Different bases may describe the same space, but their common size is its dimension.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.10_Independence, Basis and Dimension|第十讲正式笔记]]把这些定义接起来：张成保证存在表示，无关保证表示唯一，基同时满足两者。同一空间可以有不同的基，但基的共同大小就是维数。

![[线性无关]]

![[线性无关与张成]]

![[基]]

![[维数]]

再把 $Ax$ 读成列向量的线性组合，并单独定义列空间与零空间。列空间控制右端是否可达，零空间记录哪些输入会被抹去；矩阵秩与零度分别记录这两个空间的维数。解的存在性与唯一性本身只由列空间和零空间决定，秩与零度则把同一结构压缩成可计数的结论。相容后，完整解集是一个特解加零空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with one input $x$: its coordinates tell us how to scale the columns of $A$ and add them. The resulting $Ax$ is one output, and the same rule applies to rectangular matrices. This interpretation lets us ask whether a prescribed target can be reached without assuming an inverse exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定一个输入 $x$：各坐标告诉我们怎样缩放 $A$ 的各列并相加，得到一个输出 $Ax$。矩形矩阵也遵守同一规则，因此可以先问目标是否可达，而不必先假定逆矩阵存在。

![[Ax的列组合解释]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next let every input coefficient vary. The outputs form a whole subspace, the column space, which includes zero and every column combination. To compute a basis using RREF, first find the pivot-column indices and then select those columns from the original matrix; elimination need not preserve the column space itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再让全部输入系数变化，输出便组成整个列空间，包含零向量和任意列组合。用 RREF 求基时，先确定主元列号，再回原矩阵选对应列；消元不一定保持列空间本身。

![[列空间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The column picture explains how coefficients combine columns to reach a particular target. Letting the coefficients vary collects all attainable targets into the column space. The interpretation and the set have different roles.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

列图像解释系数如何组合各列来达到某个目标；允许系数任意变化，全部可达目标便组成列空间。解释视角与数学集合承担不同作用。

![[列图像与列空间]]


<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now look for changes of input that produce no change of output. Two inputs give the same output exactly when their difference lies in the nullspace. Thus a trivial nullspace guarantees uniqueness for an attainable target, while column-space membership still decides whether that target is attainable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着寻找不改变输出的输入增量。两个输入的输出相同，当且仅当它们的差属于零空间。因此零空间平凡保证可达目标的输入唯一；目标本身是否可达，仍由列空间判断。

![[零空间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stacking two matrices vertically requires both homogeneous equations to hold for the same input. Their nullspaces therefore intersect, connecting the set operation to matrix notation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把两个矩阵上下拼接，要求同一个输入同时满足两组齐次方程，因此零空间取交，将集合运算落实为矩阵记号。

![[纵向拼接的零空间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To count how many output directions remain independent, take the dimension of the column space. This exact rank is neither the number of nonzero entries nor a numerical tolerance decision. Full column rank makes coefficient representations unique when they exist; full row rank makes every output target attainable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要数出还有多少个独立输出方向，就取列空间的维数。这个精确秩不是非零元素个数，也不是数值容差的选择。满列秩保证已有的系数表示唯一，满行秩保证每个输出目标都可达。

![[矩阵秩]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The nullspace is a set; nullity is its dimension. In an $n$-variable homogeneous system with $r$ pivots, the $n-r$ free coordinates provide independent coefficients for every solution. Zero nullity means the nullspace is $\{0\}$, not an empty solution set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零空间是集合，零度是这个集合的维数。含 $n$ 个未知量、$r$ 个主元的齐次系统有 $n-r$ 个自由坐标，用它们作为独立系数即可表示全部解。零度为零表示零空间为 $\{0\}$，不是无解。

![[零度]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Existence and uniqueness now separate cleanly: first test whether $b$ is attainable, then whether a nonzero input change can leave its output unchanged. The classification is none, one, or multiple solutions. For a consistent system over the real or complex numbers, a nonzero nullspace direction produces infinitely many solutions; over a finite field, multiple need not mean infinite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在可以把存在与唯一分开：先问 $b$ 是否可达，再问是否有非零输入增量不改变输出。分类是无解、唯一解或多个解。系统有解时，实数或复数域上的一个非零零空间方向会给出无穷多个解；有限域上的多个解则不必是无穷多个。

![[方程组解分类]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a consistent system, choose any one exact solution $x_p$. Subtracting it from another solution leaves a vector in $N(A)$, and adding any vector of $N(A)$ to it gives a solution. These two directions prove that the entire solution set is $x_p+N(A)$; changing the chosen base point does not change the set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系统相容时，任选一个精确解 $x_p$。从其他解减去它，差属于 $N(A)$；给它加上任意 $N(A)$ 中的向量，仍然得到解。这两个方向证明全部解恰为 $x_p+N(A)$，更换所选特解并不改变整个解集。

![[特解加零空间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.9_Solving Ax = b|Session 1.9 classroom note]] follows the same matrix with a nonzero right-hand side: row dependencies impose consistency conditions, then a particular solution translates the nullspace. Full column rank removes free directions; full row rank makes every right-hand side attainable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.9_Solving Ax = b|第九讲课堂笔记]]将同一矩阵推广到非零右端：行关系给出相容条件，特解再把零空间平移。满列秩消除自由方向，满行秩使每个右端都可达。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.3_Elimination with Matrices|third lecture's worked elimination]] connects row combinations to left multiplication: $E_{32}(E_{21}A)=U$. Apply the same operations to $b$; invertibility of those operations preserves the entire solution set. Distinguish a zero pivot position that can be repaired by a row exchange from a column that supplies no new pivot.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.3_Elimination with Matrices|第三讲的完整消元例子]]把行的线性组合接到左乘矩阵：$E_{32}(E_{21}A)=U$。对 $b$ 同步操作，而操作的可逆性保证全部解不变；遇到零主元位置时，再区分可通过换行修复与该列无法提供新主元两种情况。

消元的逻辑从一次可逆行操作开始：初等矩阵表示这一步，一串操作给出行等价关系；只有对增广矩阵同步操作，方程组的解集才保持不变。高斯消元据此建立主元并识别自由变量，主元数给出秩；行最简形提供规范输出，而且同一个矩阵的最终 RREF 唯一。置换矩阵记录换行，消元乘子则组织成 LU 分解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Record one reversible row operation on an identity matrix. The resulting elementary matrix acts on any matrix with the matching row count; applying it to the whole augmented matrix also carries the right-hand side. If one operation follows another, its matrix goes on the left of the earlier record.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先在单位矩阵上记录一次可逆行操作。所得初等矩阵可用于行数匹配的任何矩阵；作用于整个增广矩阵时，右端也随之变换。后执行的操作矩阵写在先前记录的左边。

![[初等矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finite sequence of these operations defines row equivalence between same-sized matrices over the same field. If its invertible combined record is $E$ and the new coefficient matrix is $R=EA$, the transformed system is $Rx=Eb$. Keeping the original $b$ while changing only $A$ does not in general preserve the solutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一数域上的同型矩阵，能经有限次这样的操作互相得到，就称为行等价。若可逆的合成记录为 $E$、新系数矩阵为 $R=EA$，变换后的系统是 $Rx=Eb$。只改 $A$ 而保留原 $b$，一般不能保持解集。

![[行等价]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equivalence applies to every candidate vector, not just to one solution already found. Multiplying both sides by the invertible record $E$ gives the transformed system; multiplying by $E^{-1}$ recovers the original. Thus reversible synchronized operations preserve empty and multiple solution sets too, even when $A$ is rectangular or singular.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种等价针对每一个候选向量，而不只是已经找到的某个解。两侧左乘可逆记录 $E$ 得到变换后的系统，左乘 $E^{-1}$ 则恢复原系统。因此，即使 $A$ 是矩形或奇异矩阵，同步可逆操作也保持无解或多解的情况。

![[行操作的解集不变性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Row operations preserve solutions by transforming the right-hand side together with the matrix. Requiring equal solution sets for every unchanged right-hand side is stronger: it forces the matrices themselves to be equal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行操作保持解集时，右端随矩阵一起变换。若要求对每个保持不变的右端都有相同解集，条件就更强：这迫使两个矩阵本身相等。

![[全部右端的解集确定矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The solving procedure builds the staircase one pivot at a time. A zero candidate calls for a row swap if a usable nonzero entry remains below it; if none remains in that coefficient column, skip the column. A row $0=d$ with $d\ne0$ proves inconsistency. Otherwise choose the free unknowns and back-substitute upward; this also works for rectangular or singular coefficient matrices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求解过程逐个主元建立阶梯。候选位置为零时，若下方仍有可用非零元，就换行；若该系数列已没有可用非零元，则跳过。出现 $0=d$ 且 $d\ne0$ 就说明无解；否则先任取自由未知量，再向上回代。矩形或奇异系数矩阵同样适用。

![[高斯消元]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The pivot is the first nonzero entry of a nonzero echelon row, not necessarily an original diagonal entry. Its numerical value can change under row operations; with the coefficient-column order fixed, the pivot-column indices do not. Only the reduced form requires every pivot to be $1$. A pivot in the augmented column represents a contradiction rather than an extra unknown.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主元是阶梯形非零行的首个非零元，不一定是原矩阵的对角元。行操作可以改变它的数值；固定系数列顺序时，主元列的编号却保持确定。只有最简形要求每个主元为 $1$。增广列中的主元表示矛盾，而不是额外未知量。

![[主元]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a consistent system, a free variable can appear in the equations. For $x_1+2x_3=4$ with unknowns $x_1,x_2,x_3$, both $x_2$ and $x_3$ are free, although $x_3$ appears; choosing them determines $x_1=4-2x_3$. If a contradiction is present, there are no solution parameters to choose. This distinguishes the column pattern from actual freedom in the solution set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在相容系统中，自由变量也可以出现在方程里。以 $x_1,x_2,x_3$ 为未知量的 $x_1+2x_3=4$ 中，$x_2$ 和 $x_3$ 都是自由变量，尽管 $x_3$ 出现在方程中；选定它们后，$x_1=4-2x_3$ 随之确定。若存在矛盾，就没有可供选择的解参数。因此应区分列的图案与解集中真实的自由选择。

![[自由变量]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before counting solution parameters, check whether the reduced augmented equations are consistent. With $n$ unknowns and $r$ coefficient pivots, a consistent system has $n-r$ freely chosen coordinates; each choice determines the others. A pivot in the augmented column instead certifies that no solution exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数解的参数之前，先检查化简后的增广方程是否相容。$n$ 个未知量与 $r$ 个系数主元给相容系统留下 $n-r$ 个可任取的坐标，其余坐标随之确定；增广列出现主元则说明无解。

![[主元与自由变量]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The reduced form makes the equations easy to read: zero rows come last, each nonzero row has first nonzero entry one, the leading positions move right, and each leading column is otherwise zero. These are shape conditions; an augmented matrix can satisfy them and still display a contradiction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行最简形让方程容易读取：零行在末尾，各非零行的首个非零元为 $1$，首个非零位置逐行右移，所在列的其他元素为零。这些是矩阵形状条件；一个增广矩阵满足它们，仍可能包含矛盾方程。

![[行最简形]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Row reduction can always reach a reduced form in finitely many steps, and exact arithmetic gives the same RREF along every legal path that reaches one. The proof uses the preserved row space to identify the pivot columns and then each normalized row. This uniqueness concerns the final matrix: intermediate echelon forms, operation sequences and choices of subspace bases can still differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总能经过有限次行化简到达最简形；在精确运算下，任何到达最简形的合法路径都给出同一个 RREF。证明利用保持不变的行空间，先确定主元列，再确定每条归一化行。唯一性针对最终矩阵：中间阶梯形、操作顺序和各子空间基的选择仍可不同。

![[RREF存在唯一性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A permutation matrix makes the row-order record explicit: it has one $1$ in each row and column and zeros elsewhere. Its transpose reverses the rearrangement. A single swap is its own inverse, but a three-cycle is not; left row reordering and right column reordering must be read from the entries rather than assigned the same index list.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

置换矩阵把行序记录明确写出：每行每列恰有一个 $1$，其余为零，转置能够撤销重排。单次交换等于自身的逆，三循环则不是。左乘重排行与右乘重排列应按元素读取，不能直接套用同一张编号表。

![[置换矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated row permutations follow cycles. A three-cycle still returns after three applications when embedded in a larger matrix with additional fixed rows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重复换行的恢复时间由置换循环决定。在三循环外增加固定行，不会把它的周期变为矩阵阶数。

![[循环置换的周期]]


<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a square coefficient matrix, elimination can be stored as $PA=LU$, with $P$ recording row order, $L$ unit lower triangular and $U$ upper triangular. Later row exchanges must carry the already stored multiplier prefixes in $L$. When $A$ is invertible, solve $Ly=Pb$ and then $Ux=y$; the same factors can handle a new $b$ without repeating elimination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对方阵系数矩阵，可以把消元保存为 $PA=LU$：$P$ 记录行序，$L$ 是单位下三角，$U$ 是上三角。后续换行时，$L$ 中已保存的乘子前缀也要跟着移动。$A$ 可逆时，先解 $Ly=Pb$，再解 $Ux=y$；更换 $b$ 后仍可复用因子，无须重复消元。

![[消元与LU分解]]

再补齐行空间与左零空间。行秩等于列秩说明行、列两种维数计数一致；秩—零度定理分配定义域的维数。四个基本子空间由此分居输出侧与输入侧，而可逆行操作对它们的影响并不相同：行空间和零空间保持，列空间与左零空间按相应可逆映射改变。要进一步得到两对正交补，先定义正交与子空间正交补；左零空间相容判据随后把正交关系变成可解性判据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The row picture draws the solution set of each equation; the row space instead combines the coefficient rows. For real matrices, writing those rows as columns identifies their span with $C(A^T)$. Over complex numbers the ordinary algebraic span is still $C(A^T)$ in column notation, whereas the standard Hermitian orthogonal geometry uses $C(A^*)$, with conjugation; these need not be the same set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行图像画出每条方程的解集，行空间则组合各条方程的系数行。实矩阵的行按列写后，其张成为 $C(A^T)$。复数时，普通代数行空间按列写仍是 $C(A^T)$，而标准 Hermitian 正交几何使用含共轭的 $C(A^*)$；两个集合不一定相同。

![[行空间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real $A$, a left-null vector records weights whose combination of the rows is zero. The same weights must cancel the entries of every attainable right-hand side: $y^Tb=0$ for every $y\in N(A^T)$. One failed check proves inconsistency, while passing only one check may not suffice. For complex Hermitian orthogonality, replace transpose by conjugate transpose and use $N(A^*)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实矩阵 $A$，左零向量记录一组把各行组合为零的权重。这些权重也必须使每个可达右端的分量抵消，即对每个 $y\in N(A^T)$ 都有 $y^Tb=0$。一个检查不通过就可判无解，只通过其中一个却可能不够。复数的 Hermitian 正交关系应使用共轭转置和 $N(A^*)$。

![[左零空间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The row and column descriptions count the same number of independent directions, even though their vectors can have different lengths and belong to different spaces. The proof factors the matrix through a basis of its columns, then applies the same argument to its transpose; it does not assume that the two spans coincide.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行与列两种描述数出相同的独立方向数，但向量长度和所在空间可能不同。证明先用列空间的一组基把矩阵分解，再对转置使用相同论证；并不预设两个张成空间相同。

![[行秩等于列秩]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Extend a basis of the kernel to a basis of the finite-dimensional input space. The images of the added basis vectors form a basis of the range, so the two counts add to the input dimension. For an $m\times n$ matrix of rank $r$, this gives nullity $n-r$; applying it to the transpose and using equal row and column ranks gives left nullity $m-r$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把零空间的一组基扩充成有限维输入空间的基。新增基向量的像构成值域的基，所以两项计数之和等于输入维数。对秩为 $r$ 的 $m\times n$ 矩阵，零度为 $n-r$；对转置矩阵应用同一定理并利用行列同秩，左零度便是 $m-r$。

![[秩零度定理]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a consistent system, rank–nullity also counts the free directions of its solution set. A new equation changes that count only when its coefficient row contributes an independent restriction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对相容系统，秩—零度也给出解集中的自由方向数。新增方程只有在系数行提供独立限制时，才会改变这个计数。

![[独立约束与自由度]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.11_The Four Fundamental Subspaces|Session 1.11 notes]] distinguish the length of a vector from the number of independent directions. The four spaces share two ambient spaces; equal row and column dimensions do not identify their members.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.11_The Four Fundamental Subspaces|第十一讲正式笔记]]区分向量的长度和空间的独立方向数。四空间分居输入侧与输出侧，行列维数相等不意味着成员相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real $m\times n$ matrix of rank $r$, organize the input side into row space and nullspace, with dimensions $r$ and $n-r$, and the output side into column space and left nullspace, with dimensions $r$ and $m-r$. A zero-dimensional member is still the space $\{0\}$. In complex Hermitian geometry, replace the transpose by the conjugate transpose in the paired spaces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对秩为 $r$ 的实 $m\times n$ 矩阵，输入侧的行空间、零空间维数为 $r,n-r$；输出侧的列空间、左零空间维数为 $r,m-r$。某个空间零维时仍是 $\{0\}$，不是空集。复数 Hermitian 几何中的对应配对要把转置换为共轭转置。

![[四个基本子空间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal dimensions do not mean equal sets of vectors. If $R=EA$ for real $A$ and invertible $E$, the row space and nullspace are unchanged, while outputs transform by $E$ and left-null constraints by $E^{-T}=(E^{-1})^T$. The latter two spaces may move without losing any dimensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

维数相同不表示向量集合相同。实矩阵满足 $R=EA$ 且 $E$ 可逆时，行空间与零空间保持；输出按 $E$ 变换，左零约束按 $E^{-T}=(E^{-1})^T$ 变换。后两空间可能移动，而维数并未减少。

![[行操作与四个子空间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before using perpendicularity and nearest points, establish the coordinate operations: the [[向量点积|dot product]] gives the sum of products of matching coordinates, and the [[欧氏范数|Euclidean norm]] measures length. Cauchy–Schwarz relates the two.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在使用垂直与最近点之前，先明确坐标运算：[[向量点积]]给出配对坐标的乘积和，[[欧氏范数]]给出长度，Cauchy–Schwarz把二者联系起来。

![[向量点积]]

![[欧氏范数]]

![[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The dot product is one inner product. The general definition explains how to choose a consistent rule for length and perpendicularity, including complex coordinates and weighted geometry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

点积是一种内积。一般定义解释如何统一规定长度与垂直关系，也包括复数坐标和带权几何。

![[内积]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Orthogonality depends on the chosen inner product. Perpendicularity alone does not imply unit length; the zero vector is orthogonal to every vector even though it has no angle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正交取决于所选内积。垂直不等于单位长度；零向量与全部向量正交，但没有夹角。

![[正交]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now use that same inner product to measure length. Orthogonality makes squared lengths add; for two real vectors the equality also implies orthogonality. For two complex vectors it checks only the real part, so the converse needs a separate examination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接下来用同一内积衡量长度。正交使长度平方相加；对两个实向量，等式也能反推正交。对两个复向量，它只检查实部，因此还要单独审查逆命题。

![[正交向量勾股关系]]

![[复空间勾股不推正交]]

![[正交补]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To find every vector perpendicular to a plane, it is enough to test a spanning set. Linearity extends those finitely many zero dot products to every vector in the plane; omitting a required direction can give too large a set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求与平面垂直的所有向量时，可以只检验一组生成向量。线性性把有限个点积为零的条件推广到平面中的每个向量；漏掉必需方向会把结果放大。

![[按生成向量检验正交]]

![[双重正交补]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The double complement returns the original linear subspace in finite dimensions. In Hilbert space it also includes the subspace’s limit points: the result is its closure. Taking the complement twice of an arbitrary set first involves its linear span, so the set itself need not be recovered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限维中，双重正交补回到原来的线性子空间。在 Hilbert 空间中，结果还包括子空间的极限点，即它的闭包。若起点只是任意集合，还要先考虑线性张成，因此并不保证回到原集合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now strengthen the dimension picture to a geometric statement. With the standard inner product, each input or output pair contains all directions perpendicular to its partner. This completeness gives a unique decomposition of every vector in its own ambient space; merely knowing that two smaller spaces are perpendicular would not suffice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把维数图景加强为几何结论。标准内积下，输入侧、输出侧每一对都包含与对方垂直的全部方向。这种完整性让所在空间的每个向量都能唯一分解；仅知道两个较小的空间互相垂直，还不足以得到这个结论。

![[基本子空间正交补]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A linear relation that cancels the rows must cancel the right-hand side with the same weights. For real matrices, the complete output-space complement proves the converse too: passing every left-nullspace test guarantees a solution. A basis covers all tests; one failed test is an inconsistency certificate. Complex Hermitian tests use $A^*$ together with $y^*b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

某组权重把各行抵消为零，就必须把右端也抵消为零。实矩阵的完整输出侧正交补关系还证明反方向：通过全部左零空间检验就保证有解。一组基覆盖全部检验，而一次失败即是无解证书。复数 Hermitian 检验要配合使用 $A^*$ 与 $y^*b$。

![[左零空间相容性]]

最后回到计算。同一个 RREF 对四个基本子空间提供的证据并不对称：列空间要回到原矩阵取主元列，行空间取 RREF 的非零行，零空间解齐次方程，左零空间则解转置系统或追踪行操作矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose the requested original space before copying vectors. Keep $A$ for its pivot columns, use the reduced nonzero rows for its row space, and solve the homogeneous reduced equations for its nullspace. To recover original left-null weights from this same elimination, retain the operation matrix $E$; the reduced matrix alone generally loses that information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

抄写向量前，先确定所求的原空间。保留 $A$ 以选原主元列，取化简非零行求行空间基，解化简齐次方程求零空间基。若要从同一次消元恢复原左零权重，必须保留操作矩阵 $E$；仅剩化简矩阵时，一般已丢失这部分信息。

![[RREF读取子空间基]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Row operations can move the column space, while preserving all coefficient relations between columns. Thus elimination identifies the pivot indices, and those indices select the basis from the original matrix. The distinction matters even when the original and reduced spaces have the same dimension.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行操作可能移动列空间，却保持列之间的全部系数关系。因此消元用于确定主元编号，再依据编号从原矩阵中选择基。即使原空间与化简后空间的维数相同，也不能省略“返回原矩阵”这一步。

![[RREF读取列空间基]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For rows the situation is different: reversible row operations preserve their span in both directions. Distinct pivot columns then prove the nonzero reduced rows independent, so those rows themselves form a basis. If every row is zero, the empty list is a basis of the zero space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对行则不同：可逆行操作让新旧行的张成互相包含。各非零行的不同主元列又证明它们线性无关，所以直接取这些化简后的行作为基。若所有行均为零，空列表就是 $\{0\}$ 子空间的一组基。

![[RREF读取行空间基]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each free coordinate now gives one complete special solution: set it to one, set the other free coordinates to zero, and calculate the pivot coordinates. These vectors span every homogeneous solution and are independent by their free-coordinate entries. Restore the original variable order after any column permutation; no free coordinates means an empty basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个自由坐标给出一个完整特殊解：令它取一、其余自由坐标取零，再计算主元坐标。这些向量覆盖全部齐次解，其自由坐标位置也证明线性无关；交换过列时须恢复原变量次序。没有自由坐标时，使用空基。

![[RREF读取零空间基]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

Collecting a nullspace basis into a matrix makes the parameterization explicit; using the identity/free block formula also requires keeping track of the variable order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.8_Solving Ax = 0|第八讲课堂笔记]]沿同一消元例子，从自由变量走到全部齐次解。把零空间基并排组成矩阵，就能明确写出参数式；使用单位块与自由块公式时，还需要追踪变量顺序。

![[零空间矩阵]]

![[列置换后的解向量]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Attach an identity block to retain the coefficients used to combine original rows. A zero row of the reduced matrix then identifies a cancelling combination; the corresponding recorder rows supply the original left-nullspace basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

附加单位矩阵保存原行的组合系数。化简结果的零行指出相互抵消的组合；记录矩阵中对应的行给出原矩阵左零空间的基。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real $m\times n$ $A$, the recorder is $m\times m$: transpose each row of $E$ whose matching row in $R=EA$ is zero. Those columns form the original left-nullspace basis. In complex Hermitian geometry, conjugate-transpose the recorded rows to obtain a basis of $N(A^*)$; ordinary transpose instead gives the algebraic kernel $N(A^T)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实矩阵 $A$ 为 $m\times n$ 时，记录矩阵是 $m\times m$：将 $R=EA$ 零行所对应的 $E$ 行转置为列，即得原左零空间基。复数 Hermitian 几何中须对记录行共轭转置，得到 $N(A^*)$ 的基；普通转置给出的是代数核 $N(A^T)$。

![[消元追踪左零空间基]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lecture then treats whole matrices as vectors. Requiring a matrix to be both upper triangular and symmetric gives a concrete intersection of matrix subspaces; the diagonal example also separates the dimension of a space from the rank of one matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本讲接着把整个矩阵视为向量：同时要求上三角与对称，便得到矩阵子空间交集的具体例子；对角矩阵例子也区分一个空间的维数与其中单个矩阵的秩。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before combining the constraints, identify their zero patterns: triangular matrices allow entries on and to one side of the diagonal; diagonal matrices allow them only on the diagonal itself. These definitions make the intersection argument readable entry by entry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

合并条件前，先辨认零元素的位置：三角矩阵允许主对角线及其某一侧有非零元素，对角矩阵只允许对角线上非零。掌握这两个定义后，就能逐个位置理解交集证明。

The symmetric and upper triangular spaces each contribute six directions, while their three common diagonal directions must be counted only once.

[[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.12_Matrix Spaces, Rank 1, Small World Graphs|第十二讲正式笔记]]从交集继续走到子空间之和。对称空间与上三角空间各贡献六个方向，共同的三个对角方向只应计数一次。

The [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.4_Multiplication and Inverse Matrices|Session 1.4 classroom note]] develops the multiplication rules into a method for finding an inverse. Start with the row-times-column definition; regrouping the same sum gives the column, row, and outer-product readings below.

[[01_Math/02_linear algebra/01.4_Multiplication and Inverse Matrices|第四讲课堂笔记]]把矩阵乘法的各种读法串到求逆方法。先从行乘列的定义出发；重新组织同一个求和，就得到下方的逐列、逐行与外积读法。

![[矩阵乘法]]

矩阵乘法还可以从三个互补方向理解。逐列作用说明结果的列来自左因子的列空间，逐行作用说明结果的行来自右因子的行空间；外积展开则沿共同的中间指标拆开乘积。前两条包含关系给出乘积的秩上界，单个非零外积恰好刻画秩一矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A$ of size $m\times n$ and $B$ of size $n\times p$ over the same real or complex field, place the columns $b_j$ of $B$ side by side as inputs. The outputs are $Ab_j$, so each result column lies in $C(A)$. For abstract maps, multiplying their representation matrices directly follows the coordinate path when the intermediate ordered basis matches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同一实数或复数域上的 $m\times n$ 矩阵 $A$ 与 $n\times p$ 矩阵 $B$，把 $B$ 的各列 $b_j$ 并排视为输入，输出就是各列 $Ab_j$，所以结果列都在 $C(A)$ 中。对抽象映射，中间有序基一致时，直接相乘表示矩阵就遵循正确的坐标路径。

![[矩阵乘积逐列作用]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The zero-product case asks when every intermediate output is sent to zero. The columnwise reading turns this into a precise relation between the right factor's column space and the left factor's nullspace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零乘积情形要判断每个中间输出何时都被映成零。逐列理解乘法，就能把它转化为右因子的列空间与左因子的零空间之间的包含关系。

![[矩阵乘积为零]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exercise 11.2 now lets the whole right-hand matrix vary. Each column can be chosen separately, so the kernel and image dimensions of left multiplication count contributions from every column, not just one vector input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

11.2 让右侧整个矩阵变化。每列可独立选择，因此左乘映射的核与像要累加全部列的自由度，不能只计算一个向量输入。

![[矩阵左乘映射的秩与零度]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rowwise reading assigns the roles differently: one row of $A$ supplies scalar coefficients for combining whole rows of $B$. The output row therefore stays in the algebraic row span of $B$, including over the complex field without conjugation. This row containment and the preceding column containment provide the two sides of the product-rank bound; neither containment promises equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逐行读法中的角色不同：$A$ 的一行给出标量系数，用来组合 $B$ 的整行。因此结果行留在 $B$ 的代数行张成中，复数域上也不取共轭。这条行包含与前面的列包含共同给出乘积秩的两侧上界；两条包含都不保证相等。

![[矩阵乘积逐行作用]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix one shared index $k$. Column $k$ of $A$ and row $k$ of $B$ produce a whole output-sized matrix; adding over $k$ reconstructs $AB$. These contributions can repeat directions or cancel, so the number of written terms does not determine the product rank.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定一个共同指标 $k$，将 $A$ 第 $k$ 列与 $B$ 第 $k$ 行相乘，就得到一张完整结果尺寸的矩阵；对 $k$ 求和重建 $AB$。这些贡献可能重复方向或抵消，因此写出多少项，并不能确定乘积的秩。

![[矩阵乘积外积展开]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare the dimensions of the two containments already established: output columns stay in $C(A)$, and output rows stay in the algebraic row span of $B$. Row rank equals column rank, so the product rank is bounded by each factor. An invertible square factor preserves the other factor’s rank, but nonzero factors alone can still produce zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较前面两条包含关系的维数：结果列留在 $C(A)$ 中，结果行留在 $B$ 的代数行张成中。行秩等于列秩，因此乘积的秩同时受两个因子限制。可逆方阵因子保持另一个因子的秩，但只要求两因子非零，仍可能得到零乘积。

![[矩阵乘积秩上界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The single-term case also has a converse. A nonzero outer product has only one independent column direction; any rank-one matrix can be built by choosing a nonzero column and recording the multiplier for every column. Both factors must be nonzero, and reciprocal rescaling changes the factors without changing the matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单项情形还有反向结论：非零外积只有一个独立列方向；任意秩一矩阵都可选一条非零列，再记录每列的倍数来构造。两个因子都必须非零；互为倒数的缩放会改变因子，却不改变矩阵。

![[秩一矩阵外积刻画]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Selecting independent columns and recording their coefficients turns the outer-product reading into a minimal construction: rank counts the fewest rank-one pieces needed. This construction also explains why a fixed-rank set need not survive addition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选取独立列并记录组合系数，就把外积读法变成最少项数的构造：秩等于所需秩一矩阵的最少数量。这个构造也帮助理解固定秩集合为何可能不对加法封闭。

![[秩分解与秩一展开]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Grouping the shared index into blocks gives block multiplication; distributing each entry sum over addition gives the distributive laws. Both require compatible dimensions and preserve factor order. The block rule will also explain why synchronized row operations act on $[A\mid I]$ as $[EA\mid E]$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把共同指标分组，得到分块乘法；把每个元素中的求和对加法展开，得到分配律。两者都要求尺寸相容，并保留因子顺序。分块规则还会解释为什么同步行操作将 $[A\mid I]$ 变成 $[EA\mid E]$。

### 2. 正交投影与最小二乘
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Orthogonal Projection and Least Squares*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解 Ax=b 先确定可解空间；当方程无解时，正交投影把目标移到列空间中并产生最小二乘。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solving $Ax=b$ first identifies the space in which an exact solution can exist. When the system is inconsistent, orthogonal projection moves the target into the column space and produces the least-squares solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite-dimensional subspace, first build the component from an orthonormal basis. The remainder is perpendicular to every basis direction, hence to the whole subspace. Uniqueness follows because a vector belonging to both the subspace and its orthogonal complement must be zero. Projection names this component; the nearest-point theorem then explains its distance-minimizing property.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限维子空间，先用标准正交基构造分量。剩余部分垂直于每个基方向，因而垂直于整个子空间。唯一性来自：同时属于子空间及其正交补的向量只能为零。正交投影给这个分量命名，最近点定理再说明它为什么使距离最小。

![[正交分解定理]]

![[正交投影]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To compare the projection with another point in the subspace, split the new error into the perpendicular residual and a change within the subspace. Pythagoras turns that change into an additional nonnegative squared error; equality requires the same point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把投影与子空间中的另一点比较时，将新误差拆成垂直残差与子空间内部的位移。勾股关系把位移变成额外的非负平方误差；只有同一点才能取等。

![[最佳逼近与正交残差]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Writing a geometric projection as a linear map gives a symmetric, idempotent matrix over the real numbers. With orthonormal columns, $Q^Tb$ reads the coordinates and $QQ^Tb$ rebuilds the nearest vector. General independent columns need the Gram correction $A(A^TA)^{-1}A^Tb$. The [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] in $Q^TQ=I$ leaves every coordinate unchanged. For complex data, use conjugate transpose throughout.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实数域中，几何投影对应对称且幂等的矩阵。标准正交列先用 $Q^Tb$ 读取坐标，再用 $QQ^Tb$ 合成最近向量；一般独立列要通过 Gram 逆修正重叠。$Q^TQ=I$ 中的[[单位矩阵]]保持各坐标不变。复数域须统一改用共轭转置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before imposing perpendicularity, [[幂等矩阵|idempotence]] says only that applying the map a second time changes nothing. The oblique projection example shows why self-adjointness is an additional requirement for an orthogonal projector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先理解幂等所表达的“再做一次不再改变”，再看为何正交投影还需要自伴条件。斜投影的例子能把两个要求分开。

![[幂等矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the standard inner product before checking the matrix: idempotence keeps the projected component fixed, while self-adjointness makes the discarded part perpendicular. For a prescribed target subspace, also check that it is exactly the range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定标准内积，再检查矩阵：幂等保证已投影分量保持不变，自伴保证被去掉的部分垂直。若题目指定了目标子空间，还要核对它恰好等于值域。

![[正交投影矩阵判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An orthonormal family gives independent unit directions. Before using it as a basis for a plane or a whole space, check that its span is exactly that space; a tall column matrix need not have an inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准正交组给出线性无关的单位方向。把它当作某平面或整个空间的基之前，还要核对张成范围；长方形列矩阵不必存在逆。

![[标准正交组]]

![[标准正交基投影公式]]

![[Gram矩阵正定判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Linear least squares chooses coefficients whose fitted vector is nearest to the target vector. In finite dimensions the fitted vector always exists and is unique. The normal equations characterise all minimisers, including when columns are dependent; full column rank is exactly what makes the coefficient vector unique. These geometric conclusions require no stochastic model. When applied to regression, sample residual orthogonality still does not establish population exogeneity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线性最小二乘选择系数，使拟合向量离目标向量最近。有限维中拟合向量总存在且唯一。正规方程刻画全部最小化解，列依赖时也成立；满列秩恰好决定系数向量唯一。这些几何结论不需要随机模型。用于回归时，样本残差正交仍不能证明总体外生性。

![[最小二乘解唯一性]]

![[样本正交与总体外生性]]

Removing the invisible input component yields the shortest coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[AA+列空间投影]]

![[A+A行空间投影]]

With independent columns, thin QR reduces least squares to an invertible triangular system. In this full-column-rank case, the Gram matrix has the square of the original matrix’s 2-norm condition number. <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gram–Schmidt preserves each prefix span in exact arithmetic; a zero remainder contributes no new direction. In floating point, check reconstruction and orthogonality separately. CPQR uses successive remaining column lengths as rank evidence; these need not equal singular values, and its rank decision still does not make a basic solution shortest. Separate full-row-rank underdetermined systems from rank deficiency. Complete orthogonal factorisation adds input-space rotations, while SVD pairs singular directions; both enforce minimum norm for the stated exact or thresholded rank model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

精确算术中，Gram–Schmidt 保持逐步张成空间，零余量不贡献新方向；浮点中要分别检查重建与正交性。CPQR 的逐步剩余列长度不一定等于奇异值，其判秩也不保证基本解最短。先分清欠定满行秩与秩亏；完整正交分解增加输入空间旋转，SVD 则配对奇异方向，两者都针对声明的精确或阈值秩模型实现最小范数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply the inverse formula to Ax=b and inspect just one output component. Its numerator is the determinant formed by replacing the corresponding column with b. Column linearity gives another explanation of the same replacement, while singular examples show why zero denominators need a different method.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把求逆公式用于Ax=b，再看其中一个解分量：它的分子正是把对应列换成b后的行列式。逐列线性还会给出另一条解释；奇异算例则说明为什么分母为零时需要换用其他方法。

![[Cramer 法则]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return now to geometry: a real square matrix sends a unit cube to a parallelepiped. Separate the ordinary volume factor from the sign of an ordered coordinate frame, and check what a zero determinant actually collapses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在回到几何：实方阵把单位立方体变成平行多面体。先分清普通体积因子与有序坐标架的取向符号，再看零行列式究竟压扁了什么。

![[行列式体积与取向]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A triangle or tetrahedron uses the same edge vectors with a different restriction on their coefficients. First identify the filled shape and its vertices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三角形或四面体也使用这些边向量，但组合系数受到不同限制。先认清这个包含内部的形状，以及它的顶点。

![[单纯形]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the shape defined, choose one vertex as the common origin for every edge. The determinant scales the standard simplex; slicing that standard shape explains the factor $1/n!$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

形状明确后，选同一个顶点作为所有边的起点。行列式负责缩放标准单纯形，而对标准形状逐层切片会解释 $1/n!$ 从哪里来。

![[单纯形行列式体积]]

> [!note] 跨主题入口
> [[Jacobian行列式]]和[[矩形Jacobian体积因子]]都要先理解[[Jacobian矩阵]]。输入、输出同维时，方阵 determinant 同时记录体积和取向；低维参数化进入高维空间时，矩形 Jacobian 没有 determinant，要改用 Gram 行列式平方根计算局部低维体积。换元、局部逆与 Jacobian 奇异边界分别由[[多元换元公式]]、[[逆函数定理]]和[[Jacobian奇异不推局部非单射]]承担。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The linear determinant interpretation carries over to the derivative of a differentiable real map between spaces of the same dimension. Its sign records orientation and its absolute value records ordinary volume scaling. This describes the local linear approximation; it does not by itself certify global injectivity or a finite-region integral formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线性行列式的解释可以用于同维实可微映射的导数：符号记录取向，绝对值记录普通体积缩放。这描述局部线性近似，本身不保证全局单射，也不独自给出有限区域的积分公式。

![[Jacobian行列式]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For k parameters mapping differentiably into m-dimensional Euclidean space with k ≤ m, the factor $\sqrt{\det(J^TJ)}$ measures the k-dimensional volume of the derivative’s tangent columns; $\det(J^TJ)$ is the square of that volume. Full column rank makes the factor positive. It does not prevent a parametrization from covering the same geometric set more than once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 k 个参数可微地映到 m 维欧氏空间，且 k ≤ m，因子 $\sqrt{\det(J^TJ)}$ 测量导数切向列张成的 k 维体积；$\det(J^TJ)$ 本身是该体积的平方。满列秩使因子为正，但不能阻止参数化重复覆盖同一个几何集合。

![[矩形Jacobian体积因子]]

行列式先判断线性变换是否压扁空间；把同一判断用于 $A-\lambda I$，便得到特征值，并由此进入对角化与动力系统。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The determinant first detects whether a linear transformation collapses space. Applying the same test to $A-\lambda I$ identifies eigenvalues and opens the route to diagonalisation and linear dynamics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

需要先看整体关系时，打开[[特征值、对角化与线性动力系统.canvas|特征值、对角化与线性动力系统总图]]。总图依次分成特征对象、相似与对角化、Jordan/Schur 与矩阵函数、稳定性、Markov 长期行为五个区域；正文则按下面的顺序连续展开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a relational overview, open the [[特征值、对角化与线性动力系统.canvas|eigenvalues, diagonalisation, and linear dynamics map]]. It separates the topic into five regions: spectral objects, similarity and diagonalisation, Jordan/Schur and matrix functions, stability, and long-run Markov behaviour. The sequence below provides the continuous reading path.

#### 3.1 特征对、特征空间与特征多项式

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with one nonzero direction that a matrix only rescales. Fixing its scale factor produces an entire eigenspace, including zero; testing when the shifted matrix has a nonzero kernel produces the characteristic polynomial. These are three related objects with different roles.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先找一个只被矩阵按倍数伸缩的非零方向。固定伸缩倍数，会得到包含零向量的整个特征空间；检查移位矩阵何时有非零核，则得到特征多项式。三个对象相连，但各自回答不同问题。

![[特征对]]

![[特征空间]]

![[特征多项式]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The scalar field fixes which roots and eigenvector coordinates are allowed; enlarging it does not alter the polynomial coefficients. Once a root is available, distinguish how often it repeats in the polynomial from how many independent directions its eigenspace supplies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标量域限制可以使用哪些根和特征向量坐标，扩大数域并不改变多项式的系数。找到一个根后，再分清它在多项式中重复几次，以及特征空间提供多少个独立方向。

![[特征多项式底层域]]

![[代数重数与几何重数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The eigenvalues also recover the determinant: factor the characteristic polynomial over the complex numbers and count every root with its algebraic multiplicity. This works even when there are too few eigenvectors to diagonalize the matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特征值还能还原行列式：把特征多项式在复数范围内分解，并按代数重数计入每个根。即使特征向量不足以让矩阵对角化，这个结论仍成立。

![[行列式与特征值乘积]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The determinant multiplies all eigenvalues; the trace adds them. Count both with algebraic multiplicity, allowing complex roots when needed. The diagonal sum therefore gives a quick check before individual roots are known.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行列式把全部特征值相乘，迹则把它们相加。两者都按代数重数计入必要的复数根；因此还没逐个求根，就能先用对角元之和检查特征值总和。

![[迹与特征值总和]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Vectors attached to distinct eigenvalues cannot cancel one another in a nontrivial linear combination. This gives independence across the different eigenspaces; within each eigenspace, choose a basis rather than arbitrary vectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应互异特征值的向量，不能通过非零系数的线性组合互相抵消。这给出了不同特征空间之间的独立性；同一个特征空间内部，则仍要选择基，而不是随意抽取向量。

![[互异特征值向量独立]]

#### 3.2 相似、特征基与对角化

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing a basis changes coordinates while preserving the underlying linear action. Track which direction the basis-change matrix maps coordinates before using its inverse. The characteristic-polynomial proof preserves algebraic data; the explicit correspondence between eigenspaces also preserves their dimensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换基改变坐标表示，保留底层的线性作用。先弄清换基矩阵把坐标送向哪一边，再使用它的逆。行列式证明保留特征多项式的代数资料，特征空间之间的显式对应则进一步保留维数。

![[相似矩阵]]

![[相似保持特征多项式]]

![[相似保持特征空间维数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Similarity preserves these spectral data, but the converse needs more information. Equal polynomials can conceal different eigenspaces. If both matrices are diagonalizable in the same field, however, equal root lists including repetitions allow the same diagonal form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相似会保留这些谱资料，反向推断却需要更多信息。相同多项式可能掩盖不同特征空间。不过，若两矩阵都在同一域可对角化，包含重复次数的根列表相同，就能排列成同一个对角形。

![[同谱不必相似]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diagonalization is the special basis change in which every basis vector has its own scalar action. The definition describes the resulting diagonal matrix; the criterion asks whether the available eigenspaces together provide enough independent directions. Repeated eigenvalues alone do not decide this question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对角化是一种特殊换基：每个基向量都只受自己的标量作用。定义描述变成对角矩阵的结果，判据则检查全部特征空间能否共同提供足够的独立方向。仅凭出现重复特征值，还不能下结论。

![[对角化]]

![[可对角化判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Turn the basis criterion into a practical count: first check that all roots lie in the chosen field, then compare geometric and algebraic multiplicities at each root. Row reduction can help solve each eigenspace equation, but row-reducing the original matrix to a diagonal array is a different operation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把基的判据变成可操作的计数：先确认所有根都在当前域，再逐根比较几何重数与代数重数。行化简可以帮助求解特征空间的方程，但把原矩阵直接行化简成对角数组，是另一种操作。

![[重根对角化条件]]

![[对角化与行化简]]

#### 3.3 矩阵幂、矩阵指数与 Jordan 结构

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated application becomes simpler in eigenvector coordinates: each coordinate receives its eigenvalue raised to the number of steps. Match the eigenvalue order to the basis columns; zero steps give the identity, and stepping backwards requires an invertible matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在特征坐标中，反复作用变成各坐标乘相应特征值的步数次幂。特征值顺序须与基矩阵的列配对；零步对应单位矩阵，向后倒推则要求矩阵可逆。

![[对角化计算矩阵幂]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matrix exponential combines powers into a convergent series for every square matrix. After defining it, differentiate the series to verify the differential equation and initial value; a separate inverse-exponential argument establishes uniqueness. This order keeps the definition, verification and uniqueness proof distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵指数把各次幂组合成对所有方阵都收敛的级数。先定义它，再求导核对微分方程与初值，最后用逆指数论证唯一性。这样能分别看清定义、验证和唯一性证明各自在做什么。

![[矩阵指数]]

![[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When an eigenvector basis is available, computation has three steps: express the input in that basis, multiply each coordinate by its own scalar exponential, then return to the original coordinates. The eigenvalue order must match the basis-column order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有特征向量基时，计算分三步：把输入转成这组基下的坐标，各坐标乘对应标量指数，再回到原坐标。特征值顺序始终要与基矩阵的列顺序对应。

![[对角化计算矩阵指数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ordinary eigenvector is killed by one application of the shifted map $A-\lambda I$. A generalized eigenvector may need several applications. Those successive images, ordered from the first nonzero level outward, build a Jordan chain; being a generalized eigenvector alone does not fix a chain’s normalization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通特征向量经一次 $A-\lambda I$ 作用就归零；广义特征向量可能要作用多次。把逐次作用所得的向量从最先归零的非零层向外排列，就形成 Jordan 链；仅仅属于广义特征向量，还没有固定链的归一关系。

![[广义特征向量]]

![[Jordan链]]

![[Jordan链向量线性无关]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read one chain in its own ordered basis: the repeated diagonal scalar records the eigenvalue, and the superdiagonal ones record movement to the preceding chain vector. A whole Jordan form needs chains that together form a basis of the entire space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先在一条链自身的有序基中读矩阵：重复的对角标量记录特征值，超对角线上的1记录向前一个链向量的移动。整个 Jordan 标准形则需要全部链共同组成全空间的基。

![[Jordan块]]

![[Jordan标准形]]

![[Jordan标准形的域条件]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Existence asks whether the chosen field supports ordinary Jordan blocks. Uniqueness asks which blocks every valid construction must produce: track the nullspaces of successive powers for each eigenvalue, and use their increments to recover block lengths. Changing chain vectors or reordering whole blocks preserves those data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存在性检查当前数域是否容许普通 Jordan 块。唯一性则问每种合法构造都必须得到哪些块：对各特征值跟踪连续幂的零空间，用维数增量恢复块长。更换链向量、重排整块都不改变这些资料。

![[Jordan形唯一性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Powers describe repeated discrete steps; the exponential combines all powers for continuous evolution. For a Jordan block, a scalar part commutes with a nilpotent shift, making both formulas finite in that shift. Zero powers and zero eigenvalues need their stated conventions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

幂描述离散的反复作用，矩阵指数把各次幂组合起来描述连续演化。Jordan 块的标量部分与幂零移位可交换，使两条公式在移位部分都成为有限和；零次幂与零特征值按各自约定处理。

![[Jordan块的幂]]

![[Jordan块的指数]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A transformation may move individual vectors while keeping a whole subspace inside itself. Establish this invariant-subspace idea before interpreting the leading columns of a Schur factor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个变换可以移动向量，同时让整个子空间仍留在自身内部。先理解不变子空间，再解释 Schur 因子的前若干列。

![[不变子空间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Schur uses a length-preserving change of basis: [[Unitary 矩阵|unitary]] over the complexes, or [[正交矩阵|orthogonal]] over the reals. Complex Schur form is upper triangular; real Schur form may contain 2×2 diagonal blocks. Leading invariant subspaces in the real form must include complete blocks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Schur 采用保持长度的换基：复数域用酉矩阵，实数域用正交矩阵。复 Schur 为上三角，实 Schur 可能包含2×2对角块；实数情形取前导不变子空间须包含完整块。

![[Schur分解]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The existence proof explains how to obtain this form: isolate an invariant direction or plane, complete an orthonormal basis, then work on a smaller compressed matrix. The orthogonal complement need not itself be invariant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存在性证明说明这种形式怎样得到：先取不变方向或平面，补全标准正交基，再处理较小的压缩矩阵。所取正交补本身不必是不变子空间。

![[Schur分解存在性]]

#### 3.4 谱、轨迹与线性系统稳定性

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For long-run discrete behavior, collect the complex eigenvalue moduli into the spectral radius. A radius below one makes all matrix powers vanish, including defective cases; applying these powers to arbitrary initial states gives asymptotic stability. The radius alone does not measure the largest short-term amplification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

研究离散系统的长期行为时，把全部复特征值的模汇总成谱半径。谱半径小于一会使矩阵幂趋零，包括不可对角化的情形；将这些幂作用于任意初值，就得到渐近稳定。谱半径本身不衡量最大的短期放大。

![[谱半径]]

![[矩阵幂趋零判据]]

![[离散系统谱稳定性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the boundary, repeated roots are not the issue by themselves. First distinguish a semisimple eigenvalue from a simple root and from diagonalizability of the entire matrix; then apply that local condition to bounded trajectories.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到了边界，重根本身不是决定因素。先分清单个特征值半单、单根与整个矩阵可对角化，再把这个局部条件用于轨迹有界性。

![[半单特征值]]

![[离散系统有界判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuous time changes the test: scalar modes evolve exponentially, so their real parts govern decay. Asymptotic stability combines eventual convergence to zero with keeping small initial perturbations small at every time. Boundary cases on the imaginary axis require a separate boundedness test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续时间需要换一个检验：标量模式按指数演化，因此衰减取决于实部。渐近稳定既要求最终趋零，也要求小初始扰动始终保持小；虚轴上的边界情形须另外检查有界性。

![[连续系统谱稳定性]]

![[连续系统有界判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These tests cover every initial direction. A single response may omit a growing mode or hide it from the observed output. Conversely, the later discussion of [[非正规矩阵瞬态|nonnormal transient growth]] shows why early amplification can precede eventual decay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些判据覆盖全部初始方向。一条响应可能没有激活增长模式，也可能在观测输出中隐藏它；反过来，后面的[[非正规矩阵瞬态]]还会说明为什么早期放大可以伴随最终衰减。

![[单轨迹与系统稳定性]]

#### 3.5 Markov 约定、稳态与收敛

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A probability transition must preserve nonnegative mass totaling one. Choose column distributions with left multiplication, or transpose both matrix and distribution for row notation. Conservation then forces eigenvalue one; it does not yet establish uniqueness or convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

概率转移须保持非负性及总质量一。可选择矩阵左乘概率列，也可把矩阵与分布同时转置成行约定。守恒随后保证特征值一，但还没有解决唯一性或收敛。

![[Markov矩阵]]

![[Markov矩阵左右约定]]

![[Markov矩阵必有特征值一]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nonnegative redistribution bounds all powers in a suitable norm. This first places the spectrum in the closed unit disk and then excludes long Jordan chains on its boundary. Boundary oscillations can still remain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非负重分配使全部矩阵幂在适当范数下有界：先由此得到闭单位圆谱界，再排除边界长 Jordan 链；边界振荡仍然允许。

![[Markov矩阵谱边界]]

![[Markov单位圆特征值半单]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fixed eigenvector becomes a stationary distribution only when it is nonnegative and normalized. Keep that definition separate from the finite-state theorem guaranteeing that at least one such probability vector exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定特征向量还须非负、归一化，才成为稳态分布。先理解这个定义，再看有限状态为什么保证至少存在一个这样的概率向量。

![[Markov稳态分布]]

![[有限Markov链稳态存在]]

> [!note] 从固定点空间到稳态个数
> 矩阵计算可以直接检查固定点空间的维数；要说明这个维数为何恰好对应有限链的闭沟通类与全部稳态分布，还要用到[[有限链平稳分解]]。这条跨主题结果来自[[离散时间马尔可夫链.canvas|离散时间马尔可夫链]]。

![[有限链平稳分解]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The class description and the linear calculation meet at uniqueness. One closed class gives one normalized stationary law; the corresponding real fixed space is one-dimensional, even when some stationary probabilities are zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

类结构与线性计算在唯一性处汇合：一个闭类给出一个归一化稳态，对应实固定空间一维，即使某些稳态概率为零也成立。

![[Markov稳态唯一判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A unique fixed probability does not force other distributions to approach it. Follow a two-state flip to see what a surviving oscillating mode does.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

唯一的固定概率并不会迫使其他分布向它靠近。沿两状态交换算几步，就能看到未消失的振荡模式。

![[稳态唯一不推收敛]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now ask whether the whole transition matrix has a limit. All modes except the fixed ones must decay; the fixed eigenvalue may repeat, and the limit can retain several independent directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在再问整个转移矩阵有没有极限。固定模式之外的模式都必须衰减；固定特征值可以重复，极限也可以保留多个独立方向。

![[Markov矩阵幂收敛判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally combine convergent powers with stationary uniqueness. The limiting projection then repeats one probability column, so every initial probability distribution reaches the same limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后把幂收敛与稳态唯一合起来。极限投影才会重复同一个概率列，使每个初始概率分布都趋向共同极限。

![[Markov幂的秩一稳态极限]]

### 4. 对称矩阵、正定性与奇异值分解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Symmetric Matrices, Positive Definiteness, and Singular Value Decomposition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面为 Schur 分解已经定义了 orthogonal 与 unitary。这里进一步研究谱几何最整齐的矩阵：先从实对称矩阵与实谱定理出发，再扩展到复数域的 Hermitian 与 normal，最后进入二次型、正定与半正定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Orthogonal and unitary matrices were introduced above for Schur decomposition. This section now studies the matrices with the cleanest spectral geometry: real symmetric matrices and the real spectral theorem, followed by Hermitian and normal matrices over the complex field, and then quadratic forms, positive definiteness, and semidefiniteness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[实对称矩阵]]

![[正交矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Symmetry gives more than real eigenvalues: it supplies enough perpendicular unit eigenvectors to form a basis. In that basis the matrix acts by scaling each coordinate, giving $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$. Repeated eigenvalues still permit an orthonormal choice; they do not make every arbitrary choice of eigenvectors perpendicular.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对称性不仅保证特征值为实数，还提供足够多彼此垂直的单位特征向量来组成基。在这组基下，矩阵逐坐标缩放，得到 $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$。重复特征值仍允许选取标准正交基，但并不使任意选出的特征向量自动垂直。

![[实对称矩阵谱定理]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over complex coordinates, reflection across the diagonal must also conjugate each entry. A matrix equal to this conjugate transpose is Hermitian, so its diagonal entries are real. This is a symmetry condition; it does not by itself require positive quadratic values or preservation of length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

进入复坐标后，沿对角线交换位置时还要逐项取共轭。矩阵等于这个共轭转置，就称为 Hermitian，因此对角元为实数。这是对称性条件，本身不要求二次型为正，也不要求保持长度。

![[Hermitian 矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To change complex coordinates without altering standard lengths or inner products, use a square matrix $U$ with $U^*U=I$. Its inverse is the conjugate transpose $U^*$, so the coordinate change can be undone without stretching. Square shape matters: orthonormal columns in a taller matrix give a length-preserving embedding, not a two-sided inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要改变复坐标却不改变标准长度和内积，可用满足 $U^*U=I$ 的方阵。其逆为共轭转置 $U^*$，因而可以不经伸缩地撤销换坐标。方阵条件很重要：高矩阵的标准正交列只给出保持长度的嵌入，不提供双侧逆。

![[Unitary 矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two adjoint identities answer different questions. The scalar matrix $[i]$ preserves length but is not equal to its conjugate transpose; $[2]$ equals its conjugate transpose but doubles lengths. In a spectral factorization, also distinguish the unitary matrix of basis vectors from the matrix being diagonalized.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两种共轭转置恒等式回答不同问题：标量矩阵 $[i]$ 保持长度，却不等于共轭转置；$[2]$ 等于共轭转置，却把长度加倍。在谱分解中，也要区分由基向量组成的酉矩阵与被对角化的原矩阵。

![[Unitary不等于Hermitian]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For complex vectors, replace transpose by conjugate transpose when measuring perpendicularity. A Hermitian matrix still has a real spectrum and a complete orthonormal eigenbasis, now assembled into a unitary matrix $U$. The resulting $A=U\Lambda U^*$ is a stronger conclusion than merely finding some real eigenvalues.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复向量的垂直关系需要共轭转置。Hermitian 矩阵仍有实谱和完整的标准正交特征基，只是此时把基向量组成 unitary 矩阵 $U$，得到 $A=U\Lambda U^*$。这比仅找到一些实特征值更强。

![[Hermitian 谱定理]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hermitian and unitary matrices share a weaker condition: each commutes with its conjugate transpose. This defines normality, which permits more matrices than either class. For instance, the diagonal matrix with entries $2$ and $i$ is normal but is neither Hermitian nor unitary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Hermitian 与酉矩阵具有共同的较弱条件：都与自身的共轭转置可交换。这就定义了正规性，其范围比任一类都广。例如，对角元为 $2$ 与 $i$ 的对角矩阵正规，却既非 Hermitian 也非酉。

![[Normal 矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Normality is exactly what makes a complete orthonormal eigenbasis possible for a finite complex square matrix. The basis change preserves length; the diagonal eigenvalues describe the remaining action. Repeated eigenvalues still allow an orthonormal choice. A real normal matrix may need complex eigenvectors, as a quarter-turn rotation shows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限复方阵，正规性恰好保证能够选取完整的标准正交特征基。换基本身保持长度，对角特征值描述剩余的作用；重复特征值仍允许标准正交选取。实正规矩阵也可能需要复特征向量，四分之一圈旋转就是例子。

![[Normal 矩阵谱定理]]

有了正规矩阵作对照，现在可以准确说明一般非正规矩阵的有限时边界：谱控制渐近速度，却不单独控制过程中可能出现的放大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With normal matrices available as a reference case, the finite-time boundary for a general nonnormal matrix can now be stated precisely: the spectrum controls asymptotic rates but not the transient amplification along the way.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[非正规矩阵瞬态]]

谱坐标把矩阵结构转成方向逐项可读的二次型；严格正、非负以及零方向的差别随后由各自原子维护。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Spectral coordinates turn matrix structure into direction-by-direction quadratic information. Separate atoms then preserve the distinctions among strict positivity, nonnegativity, and flat directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A quadratic form turns a vector into a scalar by multiplying coordinate pairs. For example, the two off-diagonal positions combine into one cross-term coefficient. This scalar function is the object whose sign we will study.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二次型把坐标成对相乘，再合成一个标量。例如，两个非对角位置共同组成一个交叉项系数。接下来判断正负的是这个标量函数。

![[二次型]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Different arrays can record the same quadratic form. In fixed coordinates, averaging a real matrix with its transpose selects the unique symmetric representative. This preserves the scalar values, not necessarily the original matrix’s eigenvalues.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不同矩阵可以记录同一个二次型。固定坐标下，把实矩阵与其转置取平均，就选出唯一的对称代表；它保持标量值，不一定保持原矩阵的特征值。

![[二次型对称化]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A general complex matrix need not give a real scalar $z^*Az$. To isolate the real part, replace the matrix by its Hermitian part $(A+A^*)/2$. The remaining skew-Hermitian part contributes a purely imaginary scalar, which need not vanish. Entrywise real parts do not perform this reduction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般复矩阵给出的标量 $z^*Az$ 未必为实数。若只考察实部，应把矩阵换成 Hermitian 部分 $(A+A^*)/2$。剩余的 skew-Hermitian 部分贡献纯虚标量，不一定为零；逐元素取实部不能代替这一步。

![[复二次型实部]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding identity recovers the real part of a complex quadratic expression. Now ask when the whole value $z^*Az$ is real for every complex vector $z$: that property characterizes Hermitian matrices. Testing only real vectors is insufficient, and real-valuedness still leaves the sign undecided.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面的恒等式恢复复二次表达式的实部。现在进一步问：何时对每个复向量 $z$，整个 $z^*Az$ 都是实数？这恰好刻画 Hermitian 矩阵。只测试实向量不够，而取实值也尚未决定正负。

![[Hermitian二次型实值判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now impose a sign condition on every nonzero vector. For a real symmetric matrix use $x^TAx$; for a complex Hermitian matrix use $x^*Ax$. Positive definiteness refers to these scalar values, not to every matrix entry being positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在对每个非零向量施加符号条件：实对称矩阵用 $x^TAx$，复 Hermitian 矩阵用 $x^*Ax$。正定说的是这些标量值严格为正，不是矩阵每个元素都为正。

![[正定矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Allowing zero values gives positive semidefiniteness. The definition still includes every positive-definite matrix; it does not demand a nonzero zero-value direction. Such a direction appears in the singular case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

允许零值，就得到半正定。这个定义仍包含所有正定矩阵，并不要求一定存在非零零值方向；后者出现在奇异情形。

![[半正定矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrix, orthonormal eigenvector coordinates turn the quadratic form into a sum of eigenvalues times squared coordinate magnitudes. All positive weights give positive definiteness; all nonnegative weights give semidefiniteness. This complete test uses every eigenvalue, and the symmetry assumption matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限阶实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵，标准正交特征坐标把二次型化成“特征值乘坐标模平方”的和。全部权重为正给正定，全部非负给半正定。这个完整判据使用每个特征值，而且不能省略对称性前提。

![[正定与半正定的谱判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive values in every nonzero direction give more than a sign for one fixed finite real symmetric or Hermitian matrix. There is one positive constant that bounds the quadratic value below relative to squared length, uniformly in the input vector. The smallest eigenvalue is the best constant; a family of positive-definite matrices need not share a single positive bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一个固定的有限阶实对称或 Hermitian 矩阵，每个非零方向取正不仅给出符号，还能用同一个正常数统一下界控制“二次型相对于长度平方”的大小。最小特征值就是最优常数；一整族正定矩阵却不一定共享一个正常数。

![[正定谱下界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within the semidefinite class, no positive and negative spectral terms can cancel. A zero quadratic value therefore occurs exactly on the nullspace: the matrix sends that input to zero. An indefinite form can instead have a zero value by cancellation, so its zero-value set need not be a nullspace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在半正定范围内，不会有正负谱项互相抵消。因此，二次型取零恰好发生在零空间，也就是矩阵把输入映为零时。不定二次型却可能通过抵消取零，所以它的零值集合不必是零空间。

![[半正定零方向]]

定义和谱解释已经稳定后，再学习如何判定。正定情形可以看主元、顺序主子式或 Cholesky；半正定情形则必须把所有主子式与只查顺序主子式区分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the definitions and spectral interpretation are secure, the focus shifts to tests. Positive definiteness can be read from pivots, leading principal minors, or a Cholesky factorisation; positive semidefiniteness requires distinguishing all principal minors from leading principal minors alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Completing squares gives a direct route from a form to its signs. In a scalar-diagonal factorization $A=LDL^*$, with unit lower triangular $L$, the coordinates $L^*x$ turn the form into a weighted sum of squared magnitudes. Since this change is invertible, all diagonal weights must be positive exactly in the positive-definite case. A zero scalar pivot can stop this recurrence even when the matrix itself is invertible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

配方提供了从二次型到符号的直接路线。在 $L$ 为单位下三角、$D$ 为对角矩阵的分解 $A=LDL^*$ 中，坐标 $L^*x$ 把二次型变成模平方的加权和。这个变换可逆，因此正定恰好要求全部对角权重为正。零标量主元可能使这种递推停止，即使矩阵本身仍可逆。

![[LDL正定判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[主子式|principal minor]] of an index set is the determinant of the block retaining those same rows and columns. [[顺序主子式|Leading principal minors]] use only the initial sets $\{1,\ldots,k\}$. For a real symmetric or Hermitian matrix, strict positivity of this entire leading sequence is enough for positive definiteness. Nonnegative leading values alone are insufficient for semidefiniteness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[主子式]]是保留同组行列下标所得方块的行列式；[[顺序主子式]]进一步只取最前面的下标集 $\{1,\ldots,k\}$。对实对称或 Hermitian 矩阵，这整串数严格为正就足以保证正定；仅有顺序主子式非负却不足以保证半正定。

![[Sylvester 正定判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the diagonal weights in the LDL factorization are positive, their positive square roots can be absorbed into the triangular factor. The result is $A=LL^*$ with positive real diagonal in $L$. Conversely, the associated squared norm is positive for every nonzero input. Fixing the coordinate order and the positive diagonal makes this Cholesky factor unique; it is generally different from the symmetric or Hermitian principal square root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

LDL 分解的对角权重为正时，可把它们的正平方根吸收到三角因子中，得到 $A=LL^*$，其中 $L$ 的对角元为正实数。反过来，相应范数平方对每个非零输入都为正。固定坐标顺序和正对角规范后，Cholesky 因子唯一；它通常不同于实对称或 Hermitian 的主平方根。

![[Cholesky 正定判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrices, the nonnegative minor test examines every nonempty principal selection. Restricting a semidefinite form to selected coordinates proves necessity. For the converse, adding a positive number to every diagonal entry makes all leading minors strictly positive; the card explains why the full collection is needed for that step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵，非负主子式判据检查每个非空主下标集。把半正定二次型限制到所选坐标，便得到必要性。反向证明则给每个对角元加上正数，使全部顺序主子式严格为正；下文解释这一步为什么依赖完整的主子式集合。

![[半正定主子式判别]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The smaller leading sequence can hide a negative direction when a zero factor appears. A two-dimensional diagonal matrix with entries $0,-1$ has leading minors $0,0$, although the second coordinate gives value $-1$. The failure concerns this shortened test, not the existence of other complete semidefinite tests.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零因子出现时，较小的顺序序列可能遮住负方向。对角元为 $0,-1$ 的二维矩阵，顺序主子式为 $0,0$，第二坐标方向的值却为 $-1$。失败的是这个缩短的检验，不是说其他完整半正定判据不存在。

![[顺序主子式不推半正定]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The scalar LDL recurrence divides by a pivot, so a zero pivot requires a separate decision. The invertible two-by-two matrix with zero diagonal and off-diagonal entries 1 cannot start that scalar recurrence. Other matrices can retain an LDL factorization with a zero diagonal weight, sometimes with a nonunique triangular factor. A block pivot and a scalar pivot therefore have different rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标量 LDL 递推要除以主元，所以零主元需要单独判断。对角为零、非对角元为 1 的二阶矩阵可逆，却不能启动这种标量递推。另一些矩阵仍能保留含零对角权重的 LDL 分解，有时三角因子还不唯一。因此，块主元与标量主元应使用不同规则。

![[LDL零主元边界]]

先用两个反例阻断“只看对角元”和“只看 determinant”的过快判定，再练习按题目已知信息选择完整判据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two counterexamples first block the shortcuts of checking only diagonal entries or only the determinant. The following comparison then shows how to choose a complete test from the information actually given.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Checking diagonal entries tests one coordinate axis at a time. Positive entries pass those necessary checks, while a combination of coordinates can still expose a negative cross-term contribution. The next example separates this failure from the special case of a diagonal matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检查对角元，每次只检验一条坐标轴。对角元全正通过了这些必要检查，但把坐标组合起来，仍可能显露交叉项带来的负贡献。下面的例子也区分了这种失败与矩阵本身确为对角矩阵的特殊情形。

![[正对角元的正定边界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The full determinant compresses the real symmetric spectrum into a product. A positive product permits two negative factors, so it cannot replace checking all required signs. In two dimensions one positive leading entry together with a positive determinant completes the test; in higher dimensions even a positive diagonal plus positive determinant can fail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

整个行列式把实对称矩阵的谱压缩成一个乘积。正乘积允许两个负因子，不能替代完整的符号检查。二维时，一个正的一阶顺序主子式加上正行列式可以完成判断；更高维时，即使对角元全正且行列式为正，仍可能失败。

![[正行列式的正定边界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding criteria offer alternative complete proofs, rather than a checklist that must all be computed. Confirm real symmetry or Hermitian symmetry, then choose the certificate that fits the available information. For example, a completed-square expression needs positive weights and no nonzero common zero direction; a leading-minor test needs every required order, not just the determinant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面的判据提供的是可替代的完整证明路线，无需全部重算。先确认实对称或 Hermitian 结构，再选与已有信息相配的证书。例如，平方和证明需要权重为正且没有非零的共同零方向；顺序主子式判据需要检查每一阶，不能只看整个行列式。

![[正定判据的选择]]

判据已经分清后，再把正定/半正定结构接回可逆性、椭球、优化与协方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the tests separated, positive-definite and semidefinite structure can now be applied to invertibility, ellipsoids, optimisation, and covariance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To invert a semidefinite matrix, first exclude a nonzero nullspace direction. In finite dimensions this is exactly the step from semidefiniteness to positive definiteness. A Gram or covariance construction guarantees semidefiniteness, but an ordinary inverse still needs this extra rank check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给半正定矩阵求逆之前，先排除非零零空间方向。在有限维中，这恰好是从半正定走到正定的一步。构造成 Gram 或协方差矩阵会保证半正定，但使用普通逆仍需额外检查秩。

![[正定与半正定可逆性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After the rank check, inversion preserves positive definiteness for real symmetric and Hermitian matrices. In each orthonormal eigenvector direction it replaces a positive scale by its reciprocal. The smallest eigenvalue of the inverse therefore comes from the largest eigenvalue of the original matrix, not from the smallest one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通过秩的检查以后，求逆保持实对称和 Hermitian 矩阵的正定性。在每个标准正交特征向量方向上，正的尺度换成其倒数。因此，逆矩阵的最小特征值来自原矩阵的最大特征值，而不是最小特征值。

![[正定逆仍正定]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking the matrix square root replaces each nonnegative eigenvalue by its nonnegative square root. Keeping the same orthonormal eigenvectors and taking nonnegative square roots of the eigenvalues produces the unique positive-semidefinite root, even when some eigenvalues vanish. The uniqueness concerns the semidefinite class; other matrix square roots can exist. Taking square roots of individual entries is a different operation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对矩阵取主平方根，就是把每个非负特征值换成其非负平方根。保持同一组标准正交特征向量、对特征值取非负平方根，就得到唯一半正定主平方根，即使某些特征值为零也成立。这里的唯一性限于半正定类别；其他矩阵平方根仍可能存在。逐个矩阵元素开方是另一种运算。

![[半正定主平方根]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If every eigenvalue is strictly positive, invert the principal root. The resulting matrix $W$ satisfies $WAW=I$, so it normalizes the quadratic scaling in all directions. A singular semidefinite matrix cannot have this ordinary inverse root: a zero direction cannot be converted into a unit direction by an invertible change. The linked card distinguishes the range projector obtained with a pseudoinverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全部特征值严格为正时，可对主平方根取逆。所得矩阵 $W$ 满足 $WAW=I$，把各个方向的二次尺度归一化。奇异半正定矩阵没有这种普通逆平方根：可逆变换不能把零方向变成单位方向。下文进一步区分使用伪逆时得到的列空间投影。

![[正定逆平方根]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now read a positive-definite quadratic form geometrically. Its unit-level boundary is an ellipsoid: eigenvectors orient its axes, while each positive eigenvalue determines the reciprocal square-root semiaxis length. Distinguish this boundary from the filled region and from a semidefinite level set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在从几何上理解正定二次型。它的单位等值边界是椭球：特征向量决定主轴方向，每个正特征值的平方根倒数给出半轴长度。注意区分边界、包含内部的实心区域，以及半正定二次型的等值集合。

![[正定二次型的椭球]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To apply positive-definite geometry to a nonlinear real function, assume it is C² near an interior stationary point. The Hessian quadratic form satisfies $h^THh\ge m\|h\|_2^2$ for every $h$ with one constant $m>0$. This bound dominates the Taylor remainder, giving a strict local minimum. The stronger global conclusion for a purely quadratic function comes from its exact expansion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把正定几何用于非线性实值函数时，假设函数在内点驻点附近为 C²。Hessian 二次型对每个 $h$ 都满足 $h^THh\ge m\|h\|_2^2$，其中 $m>0$ 是同一个常数。这个界压过 Taylor 余项，保证严格局部极小。纯二次函数能够得到更强的全局结论，则来自它的精确展开。

![[Hessian 局部极小判据]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real symmetric semidefinite matrix, zero quadratic value means a nullspace direction. In a nonlinear function, this only removes the quadratic contribution along that direction, not the full change. A singular semidefinite Hessian therefore leaves classification incomplete; nonzero positive semidefiniteness still excludes a local maximum at the C² stationary point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实对称半定矩阵，二次型为零意味着零空间方向。放到非线性函数中，这仅消掉该方向的二次贡献，并不消掉全部变化。奇异半定 Hessian 因此仍不足以完成分类；在 C² 驻点处，非零半正定仍排除局部极大。

![[半定Hessian无结论]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finite sum $\sum_i v_iv_i^*$, pairing each vector with its own conjugate transpose, is positive semidefinite because its quadratic form is a sum of absolute squares. Population covariance uses a different construction: it takes the expectation of a centered outer product. First read the matrix definition, then its semidefiniteness theorem and the exact zero-variance condition for singularity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限和 $\sum_i v_iv_i^*$ 把每个向量与自身的共轭转置配对，其半正定性来自绝对平方和。总体协方差则对中心化外积取期望：先读清矩阵定义，再看半正定定理以及奇异性对应的精确零方差条件。

SVD first chooses separate orthonormal input and output coordinates. Its existence proof constructs those directions from a positive semidefinite Gram matrix, even when the original matrix has no complete eigenvector basis. Read the definition before following the construction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

SVD 先分别选择输入与输出的标准正交坐标。存在性证明从半正定 Gram 矩阵构造这些方向，即使原矩阵没有完整特征向量基也成立；先读定义，再跟随构造。

![[奇异值分解]]

![[SVD存在性]]

奇异值给出各方向的伸缩量，奇异向量则分别指出输入和输出方向；两条独立谱刻画再说明怎样从 Gram 特征值与特征向量计算它们。手算流程把这些恒等式和存在性证明中的构造顺序变成可执行步骤。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Singular values give the stretch magnitudes, while singular vectors identify the corresponding input and output directions. Two separate spectral characterisations explain how Gram eigenvalues and eigenvectors recover them. The hand-computation procedure turns these identities and the constructive existence proof into an executable sequence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First distinguish a stretch amount from the directions it connects. For an $m\times n$ matrix, the singular-value list here has $\min(m,n)$ entries, while a full SVD still supplies $n$ input and $m$ output basis vectors. Gram eigenvectors construct paired directions only after their positive eigenvalues have been identified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先区分伸缩量与它连接的方向。对 $m\times n$ 矩阵，这里列出 $\min(m,n)$ 个奇异值，完整 SVD 却仍有 $n$ 个输入基向量与 $m$ 个输出基向量。先辨认 Gram 矩阵的正特征值，才能构造配对方向。

![[奇异值]]

![[奇异值与Gram谱]]

![[奇异向量]]

![[奇异向量与Gram特征向量]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two Gram matrices share the positive squared stretches, but a rectangular map can give them different numbers of zero eigenvalues. In hand computation, divide by a singular value only when it is positive; complete the remaining left and right bases separately. Checking the reconstructed matrix catches unpaired signs and bases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个 Gram 矩阵共享正的伸缩平方，长方形尺寸却可能带来不同数量的零特征值。手算只对正奇异值相除，其余左右基分别补齐；最后重建原矩阵，检查符号与基是否配对。

![[SVD手算流程]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now compare the questions these factorizations answer. Jordan tracks exact similarity structure; Schur organizes eigenvalue computation with an orthonormal change of basis. SVD connects separate input and output bases, so it also applies to rectangular maps. A square matrix can have only zero eigenvalues and still have a positive singular stretch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在比较各分解回答的问题：Jordan 描述精确相似结构，Schur 通过标准正交换基组织特征值计算；SVD 连接不同输入、输出基，也适用于长方形映射。一个方阵即使所有特征值为零，也可能存在正奇异伸缩量。

![[Jordan、Schur与SVD用途边界]]

![[SVD与特征分解]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each positive stretch contributes one independent attainable output, so their count gives the exact rank. The full input and output bases then split into active and null directions, recovering the four fundamental subspaces. A short list with no zero singular value does not guarantee both nullspaces are empty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个正伸缩贡献一个独立可达输出，正值数因此给出精确秩。完整输入与输出基再分为有效方向和零方向，恢复四个基本子空间；较短的奇异值列表里没有零，并不保证两侧零空间都为空。

![[奇异值与秩]]

![[SVD与四个基本子空间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keeping every positive singular direction gives an exact compact representation with rank-sized factors. Economy size instead uses the smaller matrix dimension and may retain zero directions. Removing a positive direction changes the represented matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保留全部正奇异方向，得到按秩确定尺寸的精确紧表示。经济型按较小矩阵维数取尺寸，可能保留零方向；删除正方向才会改变表示的矩阵。

![[紧SVD]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expanding the compact product explains how the matrix acts one direction at a time: read an input coordinate, multiply by its stretch, and reconstruct its output contribution. Summing all positive terms is exact; truncating them will lead to low-rank approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把紧分解乘积展开，就能逐方向理解矩阵作用：读取输入坐标，乘以伸缩量，再构造输出贡献。全部正值项之和是精确表示；删除其中一些项，才进入低秩近似。

![[SVD秩一展开]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An SVD need not report unique vector columns. A simple positive value permits a common phase on the paired vectors. An entire repeated positive block permits the same unitary change of basis on both sides. The two nullspace bases instead vary independently, and may have different dimensions. These changes preserve the matrix for different reasons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

SVD 的向量列不必唯一。简单正值允许配对向量共同变相位；整个重复正值组允许两侧使用同一酉换基。左右零空间的基则可以独立改变，维数还可能不同。这几种自由保留原矩阵的原因各不相同。

![[奇异向量共同相位]]

![[重奇异值下方向不唯一]]

![[SVD零块补基不唯一]]

谱范数先定义最大的 Euclidean 伸缩，再由 SVD 识别为最大奇异值；Frobenius 范数先取全部矩阵元素的模平方和再开平方，再由 SVD 改写为奇异值平方和的平方根。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The spectral norm is first defined as the largest Euclidean stretch and then identified with the largest singular value. The Frobenius norm is the square root of the sum of squared magnitudes of all matrix entries; SVD rewrites it as the square root of the sum of squared singular values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[谱范数]]

![[谱范数与最大奇异值]]

![[Frobenius范数]]

![[Frobenius范数与奇异值]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to the determinant through SVD: its magnitude multiplies the singular values, while the two-norm condition number of an invertible square matrix compares the largest with the smallest. Uniform shrinking and unequal directional stretching have different relative sensitivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回到行列式的跨主题应用：有了 SVD，才能准确比较总体积乘积与最弱方向，避免把“小行列式”误当成病态矩阵的充分必要条件。

![[行列式与条件数边界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First fix a rank budget and an error measure, then construct the truncated matrix. The optimality theorem rules out better candidates under that same budget. Uniqueness needs another check: a strict cutoff gap settles the nontrivial Frobenius case, but spectral-norm minimizers remain nonunique whenever $1\le k<\operatorname{rank}(A)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定秩预算与误差度量，再构造截断矩阵。最优性定理排除同预算下更好的候选；唯一性还需另查：严格截断间隔决定非平凡 Frobenius 情形，而只要 $1\le k<\operatorname{rank}(A)$，谱范数最优解仍不唯一。

![[低秩近似]]

![[截断SVD]]

![[截断SVD最佳低秩近似]]

![[Frobenius低秩近似唯一性]]

![[谱范数低秩近似不唯一]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonzero matrix, retained energy controls relative squared Frobenius reconstruction error. It becomes a sample explained-variance ratio when applied to nonzero centred data. Neither percentage measures prediction loss: the next four-point example keeps $100/101$ of the energy while discarding the target-bearing direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非零矩阵的保留能量控制相对 Frobenius 平方重构误差；作用于非零中心化数据时，才直接成为样本解释方差比。这些百分比不度量预测损失：后面的四点例子保留 $100/101$ 能量，却丢掉承载目标的方向。

### 5. 线性变换、换基与广义逆
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Linear Transformations, Change of Basis, and Generalised Inverses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些分解都描述线性变换；换基和广义逆把它们放回不同坐标、非方阵和不可逆问题中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All these decompositions describe linear transformations. Change of basis and generalised inverses apply them in different coordinate systems and to rectangular or non-invertible problems.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主题图谱：[[线性变换与换基.canvas|线性变换与换基]]。下面先定义抽象线性映射，再分清它与坐标矩阵，然后沿“基像 → 矩阵列 → 坐标方向 → 双边换基”展开；复合定理、两个基像边界与正交基特例各自作为支路。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Topic map: [[线性变换与换基.canvas|Linear transformations and change of basis]]. The main sequence runs from the definition and coordinate distinction through basis images and matrix columns to coordinate direction and two-sided basis changes; composition, the two basis-image boundaries, and the orthonormal-basis special case remain separate branches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with the rule acting on vectors, before choosing coordinates. Linearity means that addition and scaling can be performed either before or after the rule with the same result. This describes a shear or differentiation of polynomials as well as multiplication by a fixed matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看作用于向量的规则，暂不选择坐标。线性性表示相加和数乘可以在规则作用之前或之后完成，结果相同。这既描述剪切和多项式求导，也描述固定矩阵乘法。

![[线性映射]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To calculate with a finite-dimensional linear map, give its inputs and outputs separate ordered bases. A matrix then converts input coefficients into output coefficients. The diagonal-line projection illustrates how two different arrays can describe one map; changing bases can, but need not, change the array.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算有限维映射时，要分别为输入、输出指定有序基，矩阵才把输入系数变成输出系数。对角线投影说明不同数表可以描述同一映射；换基可能改变数表，却并非每次必然改变。

![[线性映射与矩阵表示]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The basis does more than shorten a calculation: prescribing arbitrary outputs for all its vectors produces exactly one linear map on the whole space. Each input has a unique coefficient expansion, which makes the construction well-defined. The chosen outputs may be zero or dependent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基不只是缩短计算：为它的全部向量任意指定输出，就能在整个空间上得到恰好一个线性映射。每个输入的系数展开唯一，因此构造没有歧义；所选输出可以为零或线性相关。

![[线性映射的基向量像]]

“基”的两个条件不能混成一句口号：线性依赖集要先检查指定像是否保持关系；线性无关但不张成的集合则留下未定方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two jobs performed by a basis must be kept distinct: dependent inputs impose consistency constraints on their images, while an independent but non-spanning set leaves directions unspecified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When prescribing images on a list that need not be a basis, check whether different descriptions of the same input give the same output. If $u_3=u_1+u_2$, then its prescribed image must be $w_3=w_1+w_2$. Preserving every such relation defines a unique linear map on their span; this becomes the whole map when that span is the domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把指定像推广到非基输入组时，先检查同一输入的不同表示是否给出同一输出。若 $u_3=u_1+u_2$，指定像就须满足 $w_3=w_1+w_2$。保持每条这样的关系，才能在这些输入的张成空间上得到唯一线性映射；张成整个定义域时，才覆盖全映射。

![[相关向量像需保持关系]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Existence and uniqueness now separate. An independent list that does not span a finite-dimensional domain can be extended to a basis. Its specified images leave the added basis images free, producing distinct maps when the codomain contains a nonzero vector. If the original inputs are dependent, consistency must be checked first; for the zero codomain, the zero map is always the only map.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接下来把存在性与唯一性分开。有限维定义域中不张成整个空间的无关组可以补成基，已知像不会约束新增基方向的像；陪域含非零向量时，便有不同的延拓。原输入若相关，则先检查指定像的一致性；陪域只有零向量时，始终只有零映射。

![[非张成集像不定全映射]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now record the images numerically. Apply the map to input basis vector $v_j$, express its image in the output basis, and place those coefficients in column $j$. With $n$ input and $m$ output basis vectors, this gives an $m\times n$ matrix; differentiation on polynomials makes the column rule concrete.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把像记录成数字：先对输入基向量 $v_j$ 施加映射，再将像写成输出基的组合，把系数放在第 $j$ 列。输入基有 $n$ 个向量、输出基有 $m$ 个时，矩阵为 $m\times n$；多项式求导给出这个列规则的具体例子。

![[表示矩阵的列]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The column rule also translates coordinates of the same vector. Place the old coordinates of each new basis vector in $P$: multiplying a new coordinate column by $P$ reconstructs the old coordinate column. Reversing this translation uses $P^{-1}$, or equivalently solves a linear system with coefficient matrix $P$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

列规则也能翻译同一向量的坐标。把每个新基向量的旧坐标放入 $P$ 的列，新坐标列左乘 $P$ 就重建旧坐标列。反向翻译要用 $P^{-1}$，等价于解以 $P$ 为系数矩阵的线性方程组。

![[换基矩阵的方向]]

一般换基必须使用真正的逆矩阵。只有同一内积下的两组标准正交基，才保证换基矩阵在实数域上正交、在复数域上酉，从而可把逆简化为转置或共轭转置。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A general change of basis requires the actual inverse. Only two orthonormal bases of the same inner-product space guarantee an orthogonal change-of-basis matrix over the reals or a unitary one over the complexes, allowing the inverse to be replaced by the transpose or adjoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding orthonormal-basis condition is sufficient, not necessary, for this simplification. Changing any basis to itself gives $P=I$, even when that basis is not orthonormal. What must actually hold is $P^TP=I$ over the reals or $P^*P=I$ over the complexes. Having both bases orthonormal for the same inner product guarantees that property.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的标准正交基条件是这种简化的充分条件，并非必要条件。任何一组基换到自身都给出 $P=I$，即使这组基并不标准正交。实际须满足的是实数域的 $P^TP=I$ 或复数域的 $P^*P=I$；同一内积下两组基都标准正交，便能保证这一点。

![[正交基换基矩阵]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now translate both ends of a fixed map. With $P$ converting new input coordinates to old ones and $Q$ doing the same for outputs, the path is $Q^{-1}AP$. Any invertible $P,Q$ define some new bases through their columns; for prescribed bases, those columns and directions must match. This two-sided change need not be a similarity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在同时翻译固定映射的两端。$P$ 将新输入坐标转为旧输入坐标，$Q$ 在输出端采用同样约定，完整路径便是 $Q^{-1}AP$。任意可逆 $P,Q$ 都能通过其列定义某些新基；若基已指定，列与转换方向就必须匹配。这种双边换基未必是相似变换。

![[一般线性映射的换基]]

复合映射是这条主线的一条支路：只有前一个映射的输出坐标与后一个映射的输入坐标使用同一组中间基，两个表示矩阵才能直接相乘。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Composition is a branch of the main route: two representing matrices multiply directly only when the first output and the second input use the same intermediate basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding matching-basis rule guarantees that the coordinate path joins correctly. With different middle bases, insert their conversion matrix $R$ between the first matrix $B$ and the second matrix $A$, obtaining $ARB$. An accidental equality $AB=ARB$, for instance when one map is zero, does not imply that the middle bases coincide.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的中间基一致规则保证坐标路径正确衔接。中间基不同时，在先作用的 $B$ 与后作用的 $A$ 之间加入转换矩阵 $R$，得到 $ARB$。若其中一个映射为零等特殊情形使 $AB=ARB$ 偶然成立，不能反推两组中间基相同。

![[复合映射的表示矩阵]]

当定义域与陪域是同一空间、两侧同步换到同一新基时，双边换基退化为 [[相似矩阵|相似变换]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When domain and codomain are the same space and both sides move to the same new basis, the two-sided formula becomes [[相似矩阵|similarity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换基分清了线性映射本身与它的坐标表示；当矩阵不可逆或不是方阵时，Moore–Penrose 伪逆继续利用子空间几何选出最小二乘解中的最小范数者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Change of basis separates a linear map from its coordinate representation. When a matrix is singular or rectangular, the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse uses subspace geometry to select the minimum-norm member among least-squares solutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主题图谱：[[广义逆与最小范数解.canvas|广义逆与最小范数解]]。下面按学习顺序展开其中的精确命题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Topic map: [[广义逆与最小范数解.canvas|Generalised inverses and minimum-norm solutions]]. The precise propositions from that map are embedded below in learning order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check rank as well as shape. Full column rank makes the pseudoinverse recover every input from its attainable output and gives unique least-squares coefficients for any target. Full row rank instead guarantees every target is attainable, and its pseudoinverse chooses the shortest exact input. The two Gram formulas express these different guarantees.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

形状之外还要检查秩。满列秩时，伪逆能从可达输出恢复每个输入，并为任意目标给出唯一最小二乘系数；满行秩则保证每个目标可达，伪逆再从精确解中选最短输入。两种 Gram 公式表达的是不同保证。

![[左逆]]

![[左逆与满列秩]]

![[满列秩伪逆公式]]

![[右逆]]

![[右逆与满行秩]]

![[满行秩伪逆公式]]

![[方阵单侧逆]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the one-condition convention, a generalized inverse satisfies $AGA=A$ and returns an input for each attainable output. It need not provide least squares for unattainable targets or minimum norm among exact solutions. The additional Moore–Penrose conditions make those geometric choices precise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单条件口径的广义逆满足 $AGA=A$，为每个可达输出返回一个输入。它不必为不可达目标给出最小二乘解，也不必在精确解中选最小范数；Moore–Penrose 的附加条件才把这些几何选择确定下来。

![[广义逆]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[幂等矩阵|idempotent]] products in the four Penrose equations retain a component after one application. Their Hermitian condition makes that retention perpendicular. The resulting rule first keeps the nearest attainable output and then selects its shortest input; the SVD formula reverses only positive stretches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

四个 Penrose 方程中的[[幂等矩阵|幂等]]乘积表示第一次作用后便保留一个分量，Hermitian 条件再使其成为垂直投影。最终规则先找最近可达输出，再选最短输入；SVD 公式只撤销正伸缩。

![[Moore–Penrose 伪逆]]

![[伪逆SVD公式]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The SVD formula constructs a candidate satisfying the four conditions; uniqueness requires showing that every candidate has the same blocks in those coordinates. The [[AA+列空间投影|output projector]] fixes the best fit, and the [[A+A行空间投影|input projector]] removes invisible components, explaining the [[最小范数最小二乘解|minimum-norm choice]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

SVD 公式构造出满足四条件的候选；唯一性还须证明每个候选在这些坐标中的各块都相同。[[AA+列空间投影|输出投影]]固定最佳拟合，[[A+A行空间投影|输入投影]]删去不可见分量，进而解释[[最小范数最小二乘解|最小范数选择]]。

![[伪逆存在唯一性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After understanding exact inversion, specify the working scale and numerical-rank rule. An SVD cutoff retains $\sigma_i>\tau$ and solves a restricted recovery problem. Ridge instead changes the objective to trade fitting error against coefficient size. Its inverse gain and its retained fraction are different quantities, and neither rule can identify pure noise from singular values alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理解精确反演后，再说明工作尺度与数值秩规则。SVD 截断保留 $\sigma_i>\tau$，求解受限恢复问题；岭则改变目标，在拟合误差与系数大小间取舍。反演增益和相对保留比例是不同的量，两种规则都不能仅凭奇异值判定某方向只有噪声。

![[小奇异值放大噪声]]

![[数值秩]]

![[截断SVD反演]]

![[岭正则化]]

![[岭的SVD滤波公式]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The comparison uses an all-coordinate ridge penalty. TSVD makes a discrete retention choice; ridge changes each positive-direction inverse gain smoothly. They can reduce noise amplification while losing useful signal. In the special case where TSVD retains every positive value, it equals the original pseudoinverse; an operator change also need not change the answer for every particular target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面按全部坐标受罚的岭定义比较。TSVD 离散选择保留方向，岭则连续改变各正方向的反演增益。两者都可能抑制噪声放大，也可能损失信号；若 TSVD 保留所有正值，就等于原伪逆。算子不同，也不表示每个具体目标的答案都不同。
