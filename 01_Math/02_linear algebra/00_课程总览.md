### 2. 正交投影与最小二乘
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Orthogonal Projection and Least Squares*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解 Ax=b 先确定可解空间；当方程无解时，正交投影把目标移到列空间中并产生最小二乘。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solving $Ax=b$ first identifies the space in which an exact solution can exist. When the system is inconsistent, orthogonal projection moves the target into the column space and produces the least-squares solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用上一节已经建立的正交与正交补，有限维中的正交直和分解给出唯一的子空间分量；正交投影把这个分量定义成 $P_Sb$，最近点定理再把“离子空间最近”化成“残差与子空间正交”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the notions of orthogonality and orthogonal complement established above, the finite-dimensional orthogonal direct-sum theorem gives a unique subspace component. Orthogonal projection defines that component as $P_Sb$, and the nearest-point theorem then turns “closest to the subspace” into “residual orthogonal to the subspace.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[正交分解定理]]

![[正交投影]]

![[最佳逼近与正交残差]]

把几何投影写成线性算子后，对称且幂等刻画正交投影矩阵。标准正交基直接给出 $QQ^T$；一般满列秩基再通过 Gram 矩阵得到 $A(A^TA)^{-1}A^T$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the geometric projection is written as a linear operator, symmetry and idempotence characterise its matrix. An orthonormal basis gives $QQ^T$ directly; a general full-column-rank basis gives $A(A^TA)^{-1}A^T$ through its Gram matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[正交投影矩阵判别]]

![[标准正交组]]

![[标准正交基投影公式]]

![[Gram矩阵正定判据]]

![[满列秩投影公式]]

最小二乘把这个几何结构用于数据拟合：先明确平方残差目标，再用正规方程表达残差正交。满列秩只负责系数唯一；样本内的残差正交也不能替代总体外生性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Least squares applies this geometry to data fitting: first state the squared-residual objective, then express residual orthogonality through the normal equations. Full column rank guarantees coefficient uniqueness only, and in-sample residual orthogonality cannot replace population exogeneity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[线性最小二乘]]

![[最小二乘正规方程]]

![[最小二乘解唯一性]]

![[样本正交与总体外生性]]

若矩阵秩亏，拟合值仍是同一个列空间投影，但系数不再唯一。$AA^+$ 在输出空间保留唯一拟合，$A^+A$ 在输入空间保留行空间分量并删除零空间分量；两者合起来说明伪逆怎样把拟合几何接到最小范数选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the matrix is rank deficient, the fitted value remains the unique projection onto the column space although the coefficient vector is no longer unique. The output-space projector $AA^+$ preserves the unique fit, while the input-space projector $A^+A$ retains the row-space component and removes the null-space component. Together they connect fitting geometry to minimum-norm selection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[AA+列空间投影]]

![[A+A行空间投影]]

![[最小范数最小二乘解]]

最后把完整 QR、薄 QR 和具体算法分开：经典 Gram–Schmidt 在精确算术下构造薄 QR，却可能在浮点计算中失去正交性；满列秩薄 QR 将最小二乘化为三角求解，而 Gram 矩阵的二范数条件数是原矩阵的平方。CPQR 可以提供秩证据，但仅在主元列回代并把其余变量置零不保证最小范数。还要先区分欠定满行秩与真正秩亏；确认秩亏或明确阈值降秩后，完整正交分解与 SVD 才是保证所选秩模型下最小范数系数的两条标准路线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally separate full QR, thin QR, and the numerical algorithm. Classical Gram–Schmidt constructs a thin QR factorisation in exact arithmetic but can lose orthogonality in floating-point arithmetic. A full-column-rank thin QR reduces least squares to a triangular solve, while the 2-norm condition number of the Gram matrix is the square of that of the original matrix. CPQR can provide evidence about rank, but pivot-only back substitution with remaining variables set to zero does not guarantee minimum norm. A full-row-rank underdetermined problem must also be separated from true rank deficiency. Once rank deficiency or explicit thresholding is established, complete orthogonal factorisation and the SVD are the standard minimum-norm routes for the selected rank model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4. 对称矩阵、正定性与奇异值分解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Symmetric Matrices, Positive Definiteness, and Singular Value Decomposition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面为 Schur 分解已经定义了 orthogonal 与 unitary。这里进一步研究谱几何最整齐的矩阵：先从实对称矩阵与实谱定理出发，再扩展到复数域的 Hermitian 与 normal，最后进入二次型、正定与半正定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Orthogonal and unitary matrices were introduced above for Schur decomposition. This section now studies the matrices with the cleanest spectral geometry: real symmetric matrices and the real spectral theorem, followed by Hermitian and normal matrices over the complex field, and then quadratic forms, positive definiteness, and semidefiniteness.
