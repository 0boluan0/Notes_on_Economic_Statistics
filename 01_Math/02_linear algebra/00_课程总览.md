### 2. 正交投影与最小二乘
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Orthogonal Projection and Least Squares*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解 Ax=b 先确定可解空间；当方程无解时，正交投影把目标移到列空间中并产生最小二乘。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solving $Ax=b$ first identifies the space in which an exact solution can exist. When the system is inconsistent, orthogonal projection moves the target into the column space and produces the least-squares solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[正交投影与最小二乘]]

### 3. 行列式、特征值与线性动力系统
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Determinants, Eigenvalues, and Linear Dynamical Systems*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小二乘关注投影和近似；行列式与特征值转而揭示线性变换如何缩放、分解并反复演化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Least squares focuses on projection and approximation; determinants and eigenvalues reveal how a linear transformation scales, decomposes, and evolves under repeated application.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[行列式]]

行列式先判断线性变换是否压扁空间；把同一判断用于 $A-\lambda I$，便得到特征值，并由此进入对角化与动力系统。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The determinant first detects whether a linear transformation collapses space. Applying the same test to $A-\lambda I$ identifies eigenvalues and opens the route to diagonalisation and linear dynamics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[特征值、对角化与线性动力系统]]

### 4. 对称矩阵、正定性与奇异值分解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Symmetric Matrices, Positive Definiteness, and Singular Value Decomposition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般矩阵的谱行为可能复杂；对称与正定结构保证实特征值和稳定几何，SVD 再把这一结构推广到任意矩阵。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The spectral behaviour of a general matrix can be complicated. Symmetry and positive definiteness guarantee real eigenvalues and stable geometry, while the SVD extends this structure to arbitrary matrices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[对称矩阵与正定二次型]]

对称矩阵的谱分解展示了正交方向上的伸缩；SVD 把这种几何推广到任意矩形矩阵，并允许按奇异值截断得到低秩近似。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The spectral decomposition of a symmetric matrix exposes scaling along orthogonal directions. SVD extends that geometry to any rectangular matrix and permits low-rank approximation by truncating singular values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[奇异值分解与低秩近似]]

### 5. 线性变换、换基与广义逆
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Linear Transformations, Change of Basis, and Generalised Inverses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些分解都描述线性变换；换基和广义逆把它们放回不同坐标、非方阵和不可逆问题中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All these decompositions describe linear transformations. Change of basis and generalised inverses apply them in different coordinate systems and to rectangular or non-invertible problems.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[线性变换与换基]]

换基分清了线性映射本身与它的坐标表示；当矩阵不可逆或不是方阵时，广义逆继续利用子空间几何选出最小二乘或最小范数解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Change of basis separates a linear map from its coordinate representation. When the matrix is singular or rectangular, the pseudoinverse continues to use subspace geometry to select least-squares or minimum-norm solutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[广义逆与最小范数解]]
