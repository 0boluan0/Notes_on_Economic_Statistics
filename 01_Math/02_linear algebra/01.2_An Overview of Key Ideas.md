<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This lecture connects **vectors → matrices → subspaces** through two concrete operations. A difference matrix with a fixed starting value can be undone by cumulative sums. A cyclic difference matrix loses the common level of its inputs. The contrast explains what an inverse must recover, why some targets are unreachable, and what the column space and nullspace describe.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本讲用两种具体运算串起**向量 → 矩阵 → 子空间**。固定起点后的求差可以用累加还原；循环求差则会丢掉所有输入共同的水平。比较二者，就能理解逆矩阵要恢复什么、为什么有些目标不可达，以及列空间与零空间分别描述什么。

## 1. Read a matrix as an operation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把矩阵读成一种运算*

$$
u=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
v=\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\\-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
w=\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A=[u\ v\ w]=
\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\-1&1&0\\0&-1&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

$$
Ax=x_1u+x_2v+x_3w
=\begin{pmatrix}x_1\\x_2-x_1\\x_3-x_2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $x_i$ are **scalars**: numbers weighting the column vectors. The [[Ax的列组合解释|column-combination view]] and the row-by-row calculation produce the same vector. Here the matrix computes successive differences, with the first entry measured from the fixed reference $x_0=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$x_i$ 是 **scalars（标量）**，即给列向量加权的数。[[Ax的列组合解释|列组合的读法]]与逐行计算得到同一个向量。这里矩阵计算相邻差值，第一项相当于相对固定参考值 $x_0=0$ 求差。

$$
A\begin{pmatrix}1\\4\\9\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}1\\3\\5\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The useful question is “what does this matrix do?” Once its action is visible, solving $Ax=b$ becomes the problem of reversing that action.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

值得先问的是“这个矩阵在做什么？”看清它的作用后，解 $Ax=b$ 就变成了如何反向恢复输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 2. Undo differences by cumulative sums
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*用累加撤销求差*

$$
Ax=b
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\begin{cases}
x_1=b_1,\\
x_2-x_1=b_2,\\
x_3-x_2=b_3
\end{cases}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\begin{cases}
x_1=b_1,\\
x_2=b_1+b_2,\\
x_3=b_1+b_2+b_3.
\end{cases}
$$

$$
x=Sb,\qquad
S=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\1&1&0\\1&1&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad AS=SA=I,\qquad S=A^{-1}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[逆矩阵|inverse matrix]] undoes the original map in both orders. Applying $S$ to $(1,3,5)^T$ restores $(1,4,9)^T$. Every $b\in\mathbb R^3$ gives exactly one input, so the columns of $A$ cover $\mathbb R^3$ without redundant directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[逆矩阵|逆矩阵]]在两个顺序上都能撤销原来的映射。把 $S$ 作用于 $(1,3,5)^T$，就恢复 $(1,4,9)^T$。每个 $b\in\mathbb R^3$ 都恰好对应一个输入，因此 $A$ 的列既覆盖整个 $\mathbb R^3$，又没有冗余方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The boundary condition matters. If only $x_2-x_1$ and $x_3-x_2$ were known, adding the same constant to every $x_i$ would preserve both differences. The first equation $x_1=b_1$ fixes that freedom. More generally, [[固定初值的差分反演|a known initial value makes cumulative sums recover the original sequence]]; differencing alone does not recover an unknown starting level.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

边界条件很关键。如果只知道 $x_2-x_1$ 和 $x_3-x_2$，给每个 $x_i$ 加同一个常数，差值不会改变。第一条方程 $x_1=b_1$ 固定了这一自由度。更一般地，[[固定初值的差分反演|已知初值时才能通过累加恢复原序列]]；差值本身不能找回未知的起始水平。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 3. Cyclic differences lose a common offset
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*循环求差会丢掉共同常数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replace only the top-right entry of $A$ by $-1$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只把 $A$ 的右上角改为 $-1$：

$$
C=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&-1\\-1&1&0\\0&-1&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Cx=\begin{pmatrix}x_1-x_3\\x_2-x_1\\x_3-x_2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

$$
C\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}=0,
\qquad
C(x+t\mathbf1)=Cx,\qquad
\mathbf1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix},\ t\in\mathbb R.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The output cannot distinguish $x$ from $x+t\mathbf1$. For example, $(1,4,9)^T$ and $(2,5,10)^T$ both produce $(-8,3,5)^T$. A unique inverse cannot recover which input was used: the common offset has disappeared.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输出无法区分 $x$ 与 $x+t\mathbf1$。例如 $(1,4,9)^T$ 和 $(2,5,10)^T$ 都产生 $(-8,3,5)^T$。一个唯一的逆映射无法判断原来是哪一个输入，因为共同常数已经被抹去了。

> [!important] A nonzero input is essential · 必须是非零输入
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The valid test is **there exists $z\ne0$ with $Cz=0$**, not merely $Cz=0$. Every matrix sends the zero vector to zero, including the invertible identity matrix. If an inverse existed, $z=C^{-1}Cz=C^{-1}0=0$ would contradict $z\ne0$. See [[非零齐次解与不可逆|the nonzero homogeneous-solution test]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 正确判据是**存在 $z\ne0$ 使 $Cz=0$**，不能只写 $Cz=0$。任何矩阵都会把零向量送到零，包括可逆的单位矩阵。若逆矩阵存在，就有 $z=C^{-1}Cz=C^{-1}0=0$，与 $z\ne0$ 矛盾。详见[[非零齐次解与不可逆|非零齐次解判据]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For this matrix, $Cz=0$ means $z_1=z_2=z_3$. Thus its [[零空间|nullspace]] is exactly the line of constant inputs:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对这个矩阵，$Cz=0$ 等价于 $z_1=z_2=z_3$。因此它的[[零空间]]恰好是由常数输入组成的直线：

$$
N(C)=\{t\mathbf1:t\in\mathbb R\}=\operatorname{span}\{\mathbf1\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 4. Which targets can cyclic differences produce?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*循环求差能产生哪些目标？*

$$
Cx=b
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\begin{cases}
x_1-x_3=b_1,\\
x_2-x_1=b_2,\\
x_3-x_2=b_3.
\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding the equations cancels every $x_i$. Therefore **$b_1+b_2+b_3=0$ is necessary**. This is the correct formula; the printed summary on page 3 drops the $b$ before the second subscript. Necessity alone does not yet prove that every target satisfying the condition can be reached.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三式相加，所有 $x_i$ 抵消，所以 **$b_1+b_2+b_3=0$ 是必要条件**。这是正确公式；讲义第 3 页的排版漏掉了第二个下标前的 $b$。仅有必要性，还不能证明满足条件的每个目标都能达到。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove sufficiency, suppose the sum is zero and choose $x_1=0$. The last two equations give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明充分性：假设分量和为零，取 $x_1=0$，再由后两式得到

$$
x_p=\begin{pmatrix}0\\b_2\\b_2+b_3\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Cx_p=\begin{pmatrix}-b_2-b_3\\b_2\\b_3\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}b_1\\b_2\\b_3\end{pmatrix}=b.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So the condition is sufficient too. The [[列空间|column space]], the set of all reachable outputs, is the plane
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此这个条件也充分。所有可达输出组成的[[列空间]]就是平面

$$
\operatorname{Col}(C)=\{b\in\mathbb R^3:b_1+b_2+b_3=0\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is also a direct column explanation. The third column is $w_*=-u-v$, so it adds no direction beyond the independent columns $u,v$. Their combinations fill a plane, not all of $\mathbb R^3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

列向量也能直接解释：第三列是 $w_*=-u-v$，没有在相互独立的 $u,v$ 之外增加方向。它们的组合填满一个平面，而不是整个 $\mathbb R^3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whenever the target is reachable, all solutions are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

目标可达时，所有解为

$$
x=x_p+t\mathbf1,\qquad t\in\mathbb R.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every such vector works because $C\mathbf1=0$. Conversely, any solution $x$ satisfies $C(x-x_p)=0$, so $x-x_p$ must be a constant vector. This verifies both directions of [[特解加零空间|particular solution plus nullspace]] in this example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $C\mathbf1=0$，上述每个向量都是解。反过来，任何解 $x$ 都满足 $C(x-x_p)=0$，所以 $x-x_p$ 必须是常数向量。这就在本例中验证了[[特解加零空间|特解加零空间]]的两个方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $b=(-8,3,5)^T$, the complete solution is $(0,3,8)^T+t(1,1,1)^T$. The solution line lies in the **input space**; the plane $\operatorname{Col}(C)$ lies in the **output space**. They describe different objects even though both ambient spaces are $\mathbb R^3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $b=(-8,3,5)^T$，完整解是 $(0,3,8)^T+t(1,1,1)^T$。解的直线位于**输入空间**，平面 $\operatorname{Col}(C)$ 位于**输出空间**。尽管两边的环境空间都是 $\mathbb R^3$，它们描述的对象仍然不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 5. Subspaces and bases make the geometry precise
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*用子空间与基精确描述几何*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[子空间|subspace]] is a nonempty subset closed under vector addition and scalar multiplication. For any $u,v$ in the set and any scalars $\alpha,\beta$, the combination $\alpha u+\beta v$ must remain in it. “Multiplication” here means **a scalar times a vector**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[子空间]]是对向量加法和标量数乘封闭的非空子集。集合中的任意 $u,v$ 与任意标量 $\alpha,\beta$ 形成的 $\alpha u+\beta v$，都必须仍在集合内。这里“相乘”指的是**标量乘向量**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every subspace contains zero: pick a vector in the nonempty set and multiply it by zero. The one-point set $\{0\}$ is itself a subspace, because sums and scalar multiples of zero stay zero. But containing zero is only a necessary condition. The union of the two coordinate axes contains zero, yet $(1,0)+(0,1)=(1,1)$ leaves that union.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个子空间都包含零向量：从非空集合中取一个向量，乘以零即可。单点集合 $\{0\}$ 本身也是子空间，因为零的和及数乘仍为零。但包含原点只是必要条件。例如两条坐标轴的并集包含原点，可是 $(1,0)+(0,1)=(1,1)$ 跑出了这个并集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The subspaces of $\mathbb R^3$ are $\{0\}$, lines through the origin, planes through the origin, and $\mathbb R^3$ itself. A line or plane shifted away from the origin fails the zero-vector condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\mathbb R^3$ 的子空间只有四类：$\{0\}$、过原点的直线、过原点的平面，以及整个 $\mathbb R^3$。平移后不过原点的直线或平面，不满足零向量条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[基|basis]] is both linearly independent and spanning. For $A$, the three columns $u,v,w$ form a basis of $\mathbb R^3$. For $C$, the two columns $u,v$ form a basis of $\operatorname{Col}(C)$; adding the redundant $w_*$ produces a spanning list but not a basis. Three coordinates per vector do not force a three-dimensional span.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[基]]必须同时满足线性无关和张成目标空间。$A$ 的三列 $u,v,w$ 是 $\mathbb R^3$ 的一组基；$C$ 的两列 $u,v$ 是 $\operatorname{Col}(C)$ 的一组基。再加入冗余的 $w_*$，虽然仍能张成同一空间，三列却不再是一组基。每个向量有三个坐标，并不意味着它们一定张成三维空间。

## 6. A compact comparison and the next connection
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*对照回忆与后续接口*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Question | Anchored differences $A$ | Cyclic differences $C$ |
|---|---|---|
| Which targets are reachable? | Every $b\in\mathbb R^3$ | Exactly those with $b_1+b_2+b_3=0$ |
| Which inputs disappear? | Only $0$ | Every constant vector $t\mathbf1$ |
| How many solutions for a reachable target? | One | Infinitely many: $x_p+t\mathbf1$ |
| Does an ordinary inverse exist? | Yes: cumulative sums | No: the common offset is lost |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定起点的 $A$ 对所有目标都有唯一解，只把零向量映到零，所以能由累加逆转。循环差分的 $C$ 只产生分量和为零的目标，把整个常数方向映到零；目标可达时有无穷多解，因此不可逆。判断方程组时，把“是否可达”和“是否唯一”分开问。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $A^T$ denotes the transpose; $A'$ is another common notation for the same operation in this real-matrix setting. It exchanges rows and columns and is different from $A^{-1}$. The lecture ends by previewing rectangular matrices: if $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$, then $A^TA$ is $n\times n$ and symmetric. Being square does not by itself make it invertible; the relevant condition is [[满列秩伪逆公式|linearly independent columns of $A$]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$A^T$ 表示转置；本讲实矩阵语境下，$A'$ 也是同一运算的常见记法。它交换行与列，与 $A^{-1}$ 不同。视频结尾预告了矩形矩阵：若 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，则 $A^TA$ 是 $n\times n$ 对称矩阵。但方阵不自动可逆；相关条件是 [[满列秩伪逆公式|$A$ 的列线性无关]]。
