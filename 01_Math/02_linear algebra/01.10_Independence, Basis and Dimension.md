> [!info] Topics · 主题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Independence; bases and coordinates; dimension and free variables.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 线性无关；基与坐标；维数与自由变量。
>
> > [!question]- Prompt 1 · 具体问题
> > <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> > What must be true of the solutions to $c_1v_1+\cdots+c_nv_n=0$ for the vectors to be independent?
> > <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> >
> > 若 $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ 线性无关，方程 $c_1v_1+\cdots+c_nv_n=0$ 的系数解必须满足什么条件？
> >
> > > [!success]- Answer 1 · 答案
> > > <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> > > The only solution is $c_1=\cdots=c_n=0$. A zero combination always exists; independence rules out a nontrivial one. See [[#1. Independence tests the coefficients|the coefficient test]].
> > > <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> > >
> > > 只能有全零系数解。零组合始终存在；线性无关排除的是非平凡零组合。见[[#1. Independence tests the coefficients|系数检验]]。
>
> > [!question]- Prompt 2 · 具体问题
> > <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> > Once a basis is fixed, why can a vector not have two different coordinate lists in that basis?
> > <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> >
> > 固定一组基后，为什么同一个向量不可能有两套不同的坐标？
> >
> > > [!success]- Answer 2 · 答案
> > > <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> > > Subtract the two representations. Their coefficient differences give a zero combination, so independence forces every difference to be zero. See [[#2. A basis gives existence and uniqueness|the subtraction proof]].
> > > <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> > >
> > > 两种表示相减，系数之差组成零组合；线性无关迫使每个系数之差为零。见[[#2. A basis gives existence and uniqueness|作差证明]]。
>
> > [!question]- Prompt 3 · 具体问题
> > <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> > If a $4\times6$ matrix has rank $3$, how many vectors belong in a basis of its nullspace?
> > <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> >
> > 一个 $4\times6$ 矩阵的秩是 $3$，它的零空间的一组基包含几个向量？
> >
> > > [!success]- Answer 3 · 答案
> > > <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> > > Three: there are $6-3$ free variables. The count uses the number of columns, because the nullspace consists of input vectors in $\mathbb R^6$. See [[#3. Dimension counts independent directions|rank and nullity]].
> > > <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> > >
> > > 三个，对应 $6-3$ 个自由变量。这里用列数，因为零空间中的输入向量属于 $\mathbb R^6$。见[[#3. Dimension counts independent directions|秩与零度]]。

## Quick self-test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*快速自测，约 5–10 分钟*

> [!example] Test 1 · 小测一
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $u=(1,0,1)^T$, $v=(0,1,1)^T$, and $w=(1,1,2)^T$. Find a basis for $S=\operatorname{span}\{u,v,w\}$ and justify your choice.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $u=(1,0,1)^T$、$v=(0,1,1)^T$、$w=(1,1,2)^T$。为 $S=\operatorname{span}\{u,v,w\}$ 找一组基，并说明理由。
>
> > [!question]- Hint · 提示
> > <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> > Compare the coordinates of $w$ with those of $u$ and $v$ before doing elimination.
> > <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> >
> > 先逐个比较 $w$ 与 $u,v$ 的坐标，再决定是否需要消元。
>
> > [!success]- Solution and checks · 解答与核对
> > <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> > $\{u,v\}$ is a basis: $w=u+v$, so it spans $S$; the first two coordinates of $au+bv=0$ force $a=b=0$, so it is independent. Other valid bases are accepted. Check both spanning and independence, not just membership. See [[#2. A basis gives existence and uniqueness|the two requirements]].
> > <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> >
> > $\{u,v\}$ 是一组基：$w=u+v$，所以这两个向量张成 $S$；$au+bv=0$ 的前两个坐标又迫使 $a=b=0$，所以它们线性无关。其他合法的基同样成立。核对张成和无关两个条件，仅证明向量属于空间还不够。见[[#2. A basis gives existence and uniqueness|基的两个要求]]。

> [!example] Test 2 · 小测二
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Find a basis for the plane $P=\{(x,y,z)^T:2x-y+z=0\}$ and explain why your vectors form a basis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 为平面 $P=\{(x,y,z)^T:2x-y+z=0\}$ 找一组基，并解释为什么它们构成基。
>
> > [!question]- Hint · 提示
> > <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> > Isolate $y$ and write a general point of the plane as a column vector.
> > <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> >
> > 把 $y$ 单独移到等号一侧，再把平面的一般点写成列向量。
>
> > [!success]- Solution and checks · 解答与核对
> > $$
> > (x,y,z)^T=x(1,2,0)^T+z(0,1,1)^T.
> > $$
> >
> > <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> > These two vectors span every point satisfying the equation. In a zero combination, the first and third coordinates force both coefficients to be zero. Thus they form a basis. Accept any independent pair spanning the same plane; merely substituting two vectors into the equation does not prove spanning. Return to [[#6. Exercise 9.2: describe three spaces|the plane parameterisation]].
> > <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> >
> > 这两个向量张成所有满足方程的点。若它们的组合为零，第一个和第三个坐标迫使两个系数都为零，因此构成一组基。任何张成同一平面的线性无关向量对均可；仅代入方程验证两个向量在平面上，并没有证明张成。回到[[#6. Exercise 9.2: describe three spaces|平面的参数表示]]。

## 1. Independence tests the coefficients
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*线性无关检查的是系数，而不是能不能得到零向量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Place the vectors into the columns of $A=[v_1\ \cdots\ v_n]$. They are [[线性无关|linearly independent]] exactly when
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把向量作为列放进 $A=[v_1\ \cdots\ v_n]$。它们[[线性无关]]，恰好意味着

$$
c_1v_1+\cdots+c_nv_n=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad c_1=\cdots=c_n=0,
\qquad\text{equivalently }N(A)=\{0\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The all-zero coefficients always work. A **nontrivial** combination has at least one nonzero coefficient; finding one that gives zero proves dependence. In particular, a list containing the zero vector is dependent: give that vector coefficient $1$ and every other vector coefficient $0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全零系数始终成立。**非平凡**组合是至少一个系数非零；找到这样的零组合，就证明线性相关。尤其是一组向量只要包含零向量，就线性相关：让零向量的系数为 $1$，其他系数为 $0$ 即可。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Elimination gives the same test: every column must be a pivot column, so $\operatorname{rank}(A)=n$. If $n>m$, an $m\times n$ matrix cannot have $n$ pivots; its columns must be dependent. Having $n\le m$ only makes independence possible—it does not guarantee it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

消元给出同一个判据：每一列都必须是主元列，即 $\operatorname{rank}(A)=n$。若 $n>m$，一个 $m\times n$ 矩阵不可能有 $n$ 个主元，所以各列必定相关。$n\le m$ 只是允许线性无关，并不保证无关。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 2. A basis gives existence and uniqueness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*张成保证能表示，线性无关保证表示唯一*

$$
\operatorname{span}\{v_1,\ldots,v_k\}
=\{c_1v_1+\cdots+c_kv_k:c_1,\ldots,c_k\in\mathbb R\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[张成|Span]] includes **all** linear combinations. A [[基|basis]] for a specified space $S$ is a list of vectors in $S$ that is both independent and spans $S$. Two independent vectors in $\mathbb R^3$ form a basis for their plane, but cannot form a basis for all of $\mathbb R^3$. Always say which space the basis belongs to.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[张成]]包括**所有**线性组合。指定空间 $S$ 的一组[[基]]，必须由 $S$ 中的向量组成，同时满足线性无关和张成 $S$。$\mathbb R^3$ 中两个无关向量可以是它们所在平面的一组基，却不是整个 $\mathbb R^3$ 的基。讨论基时必须说明目标空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**A basis need not be unique; coordinates in a fixed basis are unique.** Spanning gives at least one representation. For uniqueness, subtract two representations:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**基可以不唯一；固定基后的坐标是唯一的。** 张成保证至少有一种表示。证明唯一性时，把两种表示相减：

$$
\sum_i c_iv_i=\sum_i d_iv_i
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum_i(c_i-d_i)v_i=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad c_i=d_i\ \text{for every }i.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, both $\{(1,0)^T,(0,1)^T\}$ and $\{(1,0)^T,(1,1)^T\}$ are bases of $\mathbb R^2$. The vector $(2,1)^T$ has coordinates $(2,1)$ in the first basis and $(1,1)$ in the second, in the displayed order. Changing the basis changes the coordinates, not the vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\{(1,0)^T,(0,1)^T\}$ 与 $\{(1,0)^T,(1,1)^T\}$ 都是 $\mathbb R^2$ 的基。按列出的顺序，向量 $(2,1)^T$ 在前一组基下的坐标为 $(2,1)$，在后一组基下为 $(1,1)$。换基改变坐标，不改变向量本身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 3. Dimension counts independent directions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*维数数独立方向，不数向量有几个坐标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every basis of a finite-dimensional space has the same number of vectors; this number is its [[维数|dimension]]. A plane through the origin in $\mathbb R^3$ has dimension $2$, although its vectors have three coordinates. The zero space has the empty basis and dimension $0$; the list containing the zero vector is not a basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限维空间的每一组基都含相同数量的向量，这个数量就是[[维数]]。$\mathbb R^3$ 中经过原点的平面维数为 $2$，尽管每个向量有三个坐标。零子空间 $\{0\}$ 的基是空集，维数为 $0$；只含零向量的向量组不是基。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ with rank $r$, the column and row spaces have dimension $r$, while $n-r$ free variables give the nullspace dimension. Thus [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity]] reads
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对秩为 $r$ 的 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，列空间与行空间的维数均为 $r$；$n-r$ 个自由变量给出零空间的维数。因此[[秩零度定理]]为

$$
\dim C(A)=\dim C(A^T)=r,\qquad
\dim N(A)=n-r,\qquad
\boxed{\dim C(A)+\dim N(A)=n}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $n$ is the **number of columns**, the dimension of the input space. The formula adds dimensions, not the two subspaces themselves: $C(A)\subseteq\mathbb R^m$ whereas $N(A)\subseteq\mathbb R^n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $n$ 是**列数**，即输入空间的维数。公式相加的是维数，而不是两个子空间本身：$C(A)\subseteq\mathbb R^m$，$N(A)\subseteq\mathbb R^n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 4. One matrix, two bases
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同一个矩阵，分别求列空间与零空间的基*

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3&1\\1&1&2&1\\1&2&3&1\end{pmatrix}
\longrightarrow
R=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&1&1\\0&1&1&0\\0&0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are two pivots, in columns $1$ and $2$. For the [[RREF读取列空间基|column-space basis]], take those columns from the **original** matrix. They are independent, and $a_3=a_1+a_2$, $a_4=a_1$ show that they span every column:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个主元在第 $1,2$ 列。求[[RREF读取列空间基|列空间基]]时，回到**原矩阵**取这两列。它们无关，而 $a_3=a_1+a_2$、$a_4=a_1$ 说明它们张成所有列：

$$
\mathcal B_{C(A)}=
\left\{\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix},\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\\2\end{pmatrix}\right\},
\qquad \dim C(A)=2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the [[RREF读取零空间基|nullspace basis]], solve $Rx=0$. The free variables are $x_3=s$ and $x_4=t$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求[[RREF读取零空间基|零空间基]]时，解 $Rx=0$。令自由变量 $x_3=s$、$x_4=t$：

$$
x_1=-s-t,\quad x_2=-s,\qquad
x=s\begin{pmatrix}-1\\-1\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}
+t\begin{pmatrix}-1\\0\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These two special solutions span all homogeneous solutions. Their third and fourth coordinates also prove independence immediately. Thus they form a basis of $N(A)\subseteq\mathbb R^4$, with dimension $2=4-2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这两个特殊解张成全部齐次解；看第三、第四个坐标，又能立即证明它们无关。因此它们构成 $N(A)\subseteq\mathbb R^4$ 的一组基，维数为 $2=4-2$。

> [!warning] Source correction · 讲义笔误
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The session summary, page 3, prints the second vector with both its first and last entries equal to $-1$. The correct vector is $(-1,0,0,1)^T$: $A(-1,0,0,1)^T=-a_1+a_4=0$. The printed vector gives $-a_1-a_4\ne0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 本讲 summary 第 3 页把第二个向量的首项和末项都印成了 $-1$。应为 $(-1,0,0,1)^T$，因为 $A(-1,0,0,1)^T=-a_1+a_4=0$；原印刷向量给出 $-a_1-a_4\ne0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The recitation offers another useful route: when a list of vectors is placed in the **rows**, the nonzero rows after elimination form a basis for the original row span. Those are new combinations of the original rows. When the vectors are placed in **columns**, pivot indices select original columns. See [[RREF读取子空间基|the distinction between these procedures]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

习题讲解还给出另一条路线：把给定向量放在**行**里，消元后的非零行就是原行张成空间的一组基；它们是原行的新组合。若把向量放在**列**里，则用主元位置挑选原列。见[[RREF读取子空间基|两种方法的区别]]。

## 5. Exercise 9.1: why the maximum is three
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*不只挑出三个，还要说明为什么不能更多*

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_1&=(1,-1,0,0)^T,&v_2&=(1,0,-1,0)^T,&v_3&=(1,0,0,-1)^T,\\
v_4&=(0,1,-1,0)^T,&v_5&=(0,1,0,-1)^T,&v_6&=(0,0,1,-1)^T.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, $c_1v_1+c_2v_2+c_3v_3=(c_1+c_2+c_3,-c_1,-c_2,-c_3)^T$. Setting it to zero forces all three coefficients to be zero, so the first three vectors are independent. Next,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看 $c_1v_1+c_2v_2+c_3v_3=(c_1+c_2+c_3,-c_1,-c_2,-c_3)^T$；若它为零，三个系数只能全为零，所以前三个向量无关。另一方面，

$$
v_4=v_2-v_1,\qquad v_5=v_3-v_1,\qquad v_6=v_3-v_2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All six vectors lie in the span of those three independent vectors. Their common span therefore has dimension $3$, so no four of them can be independent. The maximum is **three**. Geometrically, this span is the hyperplane $x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4=0$ in $\mathbb R^4$: every such vector is a combination of $v_1,v_2,v_3$, with coefficients $-x_2,-x_3,-x_4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

六个向量全在这三个无关向量张成的空间里，所以共同张成空间维数为 $3$，其中任选四个都不可能无关。最多选**三个**。几何上，它们张成 $\mathbb R^4$ 中的超平面 $x_1+x_2+x_3+x_4=0$：任意满足此式的向量，都可由 $v_1,v_2,v_3$ 以系数 $-x_2,-x_3,-x_4$ 表示。

## 6. Exercise 9.2: describe three spaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同一平面，分别找平面、交线和垂直方向的基*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**The plane.** Its equation is a homogeneous system with one row:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**平面本身。** 把平面方程写成只有一行的齐次系统：

$$
P=\{(x,y,z)^T:x-2y+3z=0\}=N\!\begin{pmatrix}1&-2&3\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $x=2y-3z$, every vector in $P$ has the form below. Conversely, every such combination satisfies the equation, so this parameterisation covers exactly the plane:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 $x=2y-3z$，平面中的每个向量都可以写成下面的形式。反过来，每个这样的组合也满足方程，因此参数式恰好覆盖整个平面：

$$
\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\\z\end{pmatrix}
=y\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}}_{p}
+z\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}-3\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}}_{q}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second and third coordinates of $\alpha p+\beta q=0$ force $\alpha=\beta=0$. Hence $\{p,q\}$ is a basis and $\dim P=2$. This also agrees with rank $1$ and two free variables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\alpha p+\beta q=0$ 的第二、第三个坐标迫使 $\alpha=\beta=0$，故 $\{p,q\}$ 是一组基，$\dim P=2$。这也与秩为 $1$、有两个自由变量一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**The intersection with the $xy$-plane.** Add $z=0$ to the original equation; both conditions must hold. Then $x=2y$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**与 $xy$ 平面的交集。** 在原方程之外增加 $z=0$，两个条件必须同时满足。于是 $x=2y$，得到

$$
P\cap\{z=0\}=\{t(2,1,0)^T:t\in\mathbb R\},
\qquad\mathcal B=\{(2,1,0)^T\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**All vectors perpendicular to the plane.** Let $n=(a,b,c)^T$. It is enough to require perpendicularity to **both** basis vectors: the dot product with $yp+zq$ is then zero for every $y,z$. This is the [[按生成向量检验正交|spanning-set test for orthogonality]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**与平面垂直的所有向量。** 设 $n=(a,b,c)^T$。只需让它与**两个**基向量都垂直，因为这样它与任意 $yp+zq$ 的点积也为零。这就是[[按生成向量检验正交]]。

$$
n\cdot p=2a+b=0,\qquad n\cdot q=-3a+c=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
n=a(1,-2,3)^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The perpendicular vectors form a line. A basis is $\{(1,-2,3)^T\}$; the **whole line** is $\{t(1,-2,3)^T:t\in\mathbb R\}$. The equation's coefficient vector gives a normal directly because $x-2y+3z=(1,-2,3)\cdot(x,y,z)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有垂直向量组成一条直线。一组基是 $\{(1,-2,3)^T\}$；**整条直线**是 $\{t(1,-2,3)^T:t\in\mathbb R\}$。方程的系数向量直接给出一个法向量，是因为 $x-2y+3z=(1,-2,3)\cdot(x,y,z)$。
