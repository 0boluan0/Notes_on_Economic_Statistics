## 1. Consistency comes from the augmented matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*相容条件从增广矩阵读出*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continue the matrix from [[01.8_Solving Ax = 0|Session 1.8]], now with a possibly nonzero right-hand side. Apply each row operation to the entire augmented matrix:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用[[01.8_Solving Ax = 0|第八讲]]的矩阵，现在允许右端非零。每一步行操作都施加于整个增广矩阵：

$$
\left[\begin{array}{rrrr|r}
1&2&2&2&b_1\\
2&4&6&8&b_2\\
3&6&8&10&b_3
\end{array}\right]
\ \longrightarrow\
\left[\begin{array}{rrrr|r}
1&2&2&2&b_1\\
0&0&2&4&b_2-2b_1\\
0&0&0&0&b_3-b_2-b_1
\end{array}\right].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The operations are $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$, $R_3\leftarrow R_3-3R_1$, then $R_3\leftarrow R_3-R_2$. The last equation requires $b_3=b_1+b_2$. When it holds, the two pivot equations can be solved for any chosen free variables, so this condition is also sufficient here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

依次进行 $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$、$R_3\leftarrow R_3-3R_1$，再做 $R_3\leftarrow R_3-R_2$。最后一个方程要求 $b_3=b_1+b_2$。条件成立时，任意选择自由变量后都能求出两个主元变量，所以在本例中它也充分。

$$
Ax=b\text{ is solvable}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad b_3=b_1+b_2
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad b\in C(A).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The row interpretation is that **a combination producing zero on the left must produce zero on the right**. Here row 3 equals row 1 plus row 2. The column interpretation is that $b$ must be an attainable [[Ax的列组合解释|combination of the columns]]. The general row-dependence criterion is [[左零空间相容性|left-nullspace compatibility]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从行看，**左侧某个组合为零，右侧同一组合也必须为零**；本例第三行等于前两行之和。从列看，$b$ 必须是可达到的[[Ax的列组合解释|列向量线性组合]]。一般的行关系条件见[[左零空间相容性|左零空间相容性]]。

## 2. One base point and every nullspace direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一个基点，加上零空间的全部方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $b=(1,5,6)^T$, which satisfies the consistency condition. Choose the free variables $x_2=x_4=0$. The pivot equations then give $x_3=3/2$ and $x_1=-2$. This produces one particular solution. Combining it with the nullspace basis from Session 1.8 gives every solution:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取满足相容条件的 $b=(1,5,6)^T$。令自由变量 $x_2=x_4=0$，主元方程给出 $x_3=3/2$、$x_1=-2$，得到一个特解。结合第八讲的零空间基，全部解为：

$$
x=x_p+c_1v_1+c_2v_2
=
\begin{pmatrix}-2\\0\\3/2\\0\end{pmatrix}
c_1\begin{pmatrix}-2\\1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}
c_2\begin{pmatrix}2\\0\\-2\\1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad c_1,c_2\in\mathbb R.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Setting free variables to zero is a convenient choice, not a definition of a particular solution. Any one vector satisfying $Ax_p=b$ can serve as the base point. To justify [[特解加零空间|the entire solution set $x_p+N(A)$]], prove both directions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把自由变量设为零是一种方便的选法，不是特解的定义。任意满足 $Ax_p=b$ 的向量都可以作基点。要证明[[特解加零空间|完整解集恰好为 $x_p+N(A)$]]，需要两个方向：

$$
z\in N(A)\ \Longrightarrow\ A(x_p+z)=b+0=b;
$$

$$
Ax=b\ \Longrightarrow\ A(x-x_p)=0
\ \Longrightarrow\ x=x_p+z\text{ for some }z\in N(A).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Geometrically, $v_1,v_2$ are two independent directions, and all their linear combinations form a two-dimensional plane through the origin in $\mathbb R^4$. Adding $x_p$ translates that plane through $x_p$. Since $b\ne0$, the translated plane does not contain zero and is not a subspace. Its dimension is $n-r=4-2=2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

几何上，$v_1,v_2$ 是两个独立方向，它们的全部线性组合构成 $\mathbb R^4$ 中经过原点的二维平面。加上 $x_p$，就把平面平移到经过 $x_p$ 的位置。由于 $b\ne0$，平移后的平面不含零向量，因此不是子空间；其维数是 $n-r=4-2=2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The printed summary on p.2 omits $c_1$ before the first nullspace vector, although it names both $c_1$ and $c_2$. The formula above includes both arbitrary coefficients, as required for all solutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原讲义第 2 页的公式在第一个零空间向量前漏印了 $c_1$，但文字仍提到 $c_1,c_2$。上式保留两个任意系数，才能表示全部解。

## 3. Rank separates existence from uniqueness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*用秩分别判断存在性与唯一性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ with rank $r$, column rank equals row rank and $r\le\min(m,n)$. There are $n-r$ free variables. The two questions in [[方程组解分类|classifying solutions]] are whether this $b$ belongs to $C(A)$ and whether $N(A)$ contains any nonzero direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对秩为 $r$ 的实矩阵 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，列秩等于行秩，且 $r\le\min(m,n)$；自由变量有 $n-r$ 个。[[方程组解分类|解的分类]]分成两个问题：这个 $b$ 是否属于 $C(A)$，以及 $N(A)$ 是否含有非零方向。

| Rank / 秩 | Attainable right-hand sides / 可达右端 | Free variables / 自由变量 | Solutions / 解的数量 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $r=n<m$ — full column rank / 满列秩 | $b\in C(A)\subsetneq\mathbb R^m$ | $0$ | One if consistent, otherwise none / 相容时一个，否则无解 |
| $r=m<n$ — full row rank / 满行秩 | Every $b\in\mathbb R^m$ / 所有右端 | $n-m>0$ | Infinitely many for every $b$ / 每个右端均有无穷多解 |
| $r=m=n$ — invertible / 可逆 | Every $b\in\mathbb R^m$ / 所有右端 | $0$ | Exactly one for every $b$ / 每个右端恰有一个解 |
| $r<m,\ r<n$ | $b\in C(A)\subsetneq\mathbb R^m$ | $n-r>0$ | Infinitely many if consistent, otherwise none / 相容时无穷多，否则无解 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Full column rank** means $N(A)=\{0\}$, so a consistent solution cannot move in any nonzero nullspace direction. It gives at most one solution, not existence for every $b$. For example, $A=(1,0)^T$ reaches $(1,0)^T$ but cannot reach $(0,1)^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**满列秩**意味着 $N(A)=\{0\}$，相容时没有非零的零空间方向可供移动，所以至多一个解；它不保证每个 $b$ 都有解。例如 $A=(1,0)^T$ 可以达到 $(1,0)^T$，却不能达到 $(0,1)^T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Full row rank** means $C(A)=\mathbb R^m$, so every right-hand side is attainable. For the wide matrix $A=(1\ 0)$, the equation is $x_1=b$ while $x_2$ remains arbitrary. Thus “full rank” alone does not mean “zero or one solution”: for a rectangular matrix it means $r=\min(m,n)$, and the shape still matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**满行秩**意味着 $C(A)=\mathbb R^m$，因此每个右端都可达。对宽矩阵 $A=(1\ 0)$，方程只要求 $x_1=b$，而 $x_2$ 任意。因此单说“满秩”不能推出“零个或一个解”：矩形矩阵满秩指 $r=\min(m,n)$，还需要区分形状。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If this distinction remains unclear, revisit the original lecture from **full column rank**, through **full row rank**, to the four-case summary. The viewing goal is to explain separately why $r=n$ removes free variables and why $r=m$ makes every $b$ attainable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若这一区别仍不清楚，回看原课从 **full column rank** 开始，经 **full row rank** 到四种情况总结的部分；观看目标是分别说明：为什么 $r=n$ 消除自由变量，为什么 $r=m$ 保证所有 $b$ 都可达。

## 4. Exercise 8.1: three statements to repair
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*习题 8.1：修正三个判断*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**(a) A particular solution cannot be given an arbitrary coefficient.** If $Ax_p=b$ and $Az=0$, then $A(\alpha x_p+\beta z)=\alpha b$. For $b\ne0$, solving the original equation requires $\alpha=1$. The homogeneous part may be any vector in $N(A)$; when its dimension exceeds one, a single fixed $z$ does not cover all directions. If $b=0$, all solutions are homogeneous and arbitrary linear combinations remain solutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**(a) 特解前的系数不能任意改变。** 若 $Ax_p=b,\ Az=0$，则 $A(\alpha x_p+\beta z)=\alpha b$。当 $b\ne0$，仍要满足原方程就必须取 $\alpha=1$。齐次部分可以是 $N(A)$ 中任意向量；若零空间维数大于一，一个固定 $z$ 的倍数也不足以覆盖所有方向。若 $b=0$，则所有解本来就是齐次解，任意线性组合仍是解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**(b) A particular solution means one selected solution, not a uniquely determined vector.** If the system is consistent and $N(A)$ contains a nonzero vector $z$, then $x_p+tz$ gives infinitely many distinct choices. If $r=n$, a consistent system has exactly one choice. Without consistency, there is no particular solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**(b) 特解指选定的某一个解，不表示它天然唯一。** 若系统相容且零空间含非零向量 $z$，则 $x_p+tz$ 给出无穷多个不同选择。若 $r=n$，相容系统只有一个解可选；若不相容，则没有特解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**(c) An invertible matrix has the trivial nullspace $\{0\}$, not an empty nullspace.** Every matrix satisfies $A0=0$. When $A$ is invertible, multiplying $Ax=0$ by $A^{-1}$ gives $x=0$, proving this is the only solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**(c) 可逆矩阵的零空间为 $\{0\}$，不是空集。** 任何矩阵都有 $A0=0$。若 $A$ 可逆，对 $Ax=0$ 左乘 $A^{-1}$ 得 $x=0$，说明零向量是唯一解。

## 5. Exercise 8.2: reduce both the matrix and the right-hand side
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*习题 8.2：矩阵与右端同步化简*

$$
U=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3\\0&0&4\end{pmatrix},
\qquad c=\begin{pmatrix}5\\8\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a worked model of the method, use the [MIT problem-solving video, Solving Ax=b](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06sc-linear-algebra-fall-2011/resources/solving-ax-b/): follow the augmented-matrix elimination, the choice of a free variable, and the assembly of the complete solution. Then attempt this problem before opening the calculation below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

需要完整示范时，看 [MIT 配套解题视频 Solving Ax=b](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06sc-linear-algebra-fall-2011/resources/solving-ax-b/)，跟完增广矩阵消元、选自由变量及组合完整解的过程；随后先尝试本题，再展开下方计算。

> [!success]- Worked solution · 参考过程
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> First divide row 2 by $4$, then subtract three times the new row 2 from row 1. These operations give
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 先将第 2 行除以 $4$，再从第 1 行减去新第 2 行的三倍，得到
>
> $$
> [U\mid0]\longrightarrow[R\mid0],
> \qquad
> [U\mid c]\longrightarrow[R\mid d],
> \qquad
> R=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},
> \quad d=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\2\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $Rx=0$, set the free variable $x_2=t$: $x_1=-2t,\ x_3=0$. For $Rx=d$, the same choice gives $x_1=-1-2t,\ x_3=2$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $Rx=0$，令自由变量 $x_2=t$，得到 $x_1=-2t,\ x_3=0$；对 $Rx=d$，同样取 $x_2=t$，得到 $x_1=-1-2t,\ x_3=2$：
>
> $$
> x_{\mathrm{hom}}=t\begin{pmatrix}-2\\1\\0\end{pmatrix};
> \qquad
> x=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\0\\2\end{pmatrix}
> t\begin{pmatrix}-2\\1\\0\end{pmatrix},
> \quad t\in\mathbb R.
> $$
>
> $$
> U\begin{pmatrix}-2\\1\\0\end{pmatrix}=0,\qquad
> U\begin{pmatrix}-1\\0\\2\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}5\\8\end{pmatrix}=c.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The official solution chooses $(2,-1,0)^T$ as a homogeneous solution and $(-3,1,2)^T$ as a particular solution. These are equally valid: the first reverses the basis direction, and the second corresponds to $t=1$. Different choices can describe exactly the same solution set.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 官方答案选取齐次解 $(2,-1,0)^T$ 与特解 $(-3,1,2)^T$，同样有效：前者反转了基向量方向，后者对应上式的 $t=1$。不同的选择可以描述完全相同的解集。

## 6. Exercise 8.3: keep the same right-hand side
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*习题 8.3：必须保持右端相同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The answer is **yes**: [[全部右端的解集确定矩阵|if $Ax=b$ and $Cx=b$ have the same complete solution set for every identical $b$, then $A=C$]]. To prove it, choose an arbitrary input $v$ and set $b=Av$. Then $v$ solves the first system, hence also the second. Therefore $Cv=Av$ for all $v$; in particular, each pair of corresponding columns is equal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

答案是 **Yes**：[[全部右端的解集确定矩阵|若对每个相同的 $b$，$Ax=b$ 与 $Cx=b$ 的完整解集都相同，则 $A=C$]]。证明时任取输入 $v$，令 $b=Av$；于是 $v$ 是第一组方程的解，也必须是第二组的解。因此对所有 $v$ 都有 $Cv=Av$；特别地，两矩阵的对应列相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying a matrix by a nonzero scalar generally changes the solution for a fixed nonzero right-hand side. For example, $A=2I,\ C=I$ give $x=b/2$ and $x=b$. The valid scaling equivalence changes both sides:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保持非零右端不变时，把矩阵乘非零常数通常会改变解。例如 $A=2I,\ C=I$ 对应 $x=b/2$ 和 $x=b$。有效的缩放等价需要两侧同时改变：

$$
Ax=b\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad(kA)x=kb,\qquad k\ne0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal solution counts do not suffice either: two different invertible matrices each give one solution for every $b$, but those solutions need not be the same vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解的数量相同也不够：两个不同的可逆矩阵都对每个 $b$ 给出唯一解，但解向量未必相同。
