## 1. Elimination preserves the nullspace
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*消元为何不改变零空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each elementary row operation is reversible. If their product is the invertible matrix $E$, elimination gives $U=EA$, and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个初等行操作都可逆。设这些操作的乘积为可逆矩阵 $E$，消元得到 $U=EA$，于是

$$
Ax=0\iff EAx=0\iff Ux=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The forward implication follows by multiplication; the reverse follows by multiplying by $E^{-1}$. Thus $N(A)=N(U)$, even for a rectangular or singular matrix. An augmented zero column may be omitted because every row operation keeps it zero. For $Ax=b$ with a nonzero right-hand side, the same operations must also be applied to $b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正向是左乘 $E$，反向是再左乘 $E^{-1}$，所以 $N(A)=N(U)$，矩阵非方阵或不可逆也不影响这个结论。齐次系统的增广零列可以省略，因为行操作后它仍是零；若右端为非零 $b$，则必须对 $b$ 同步操作。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[行操作与四个子空间|effect on column space is different]]: $C(U)=E\,C(A)$, which generally changes the set of output vectors. Row operations preserve the solutions' input coordinates; they transform the outputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[行操作与四个子空间|列空间的变化不同]]：$C(U)=E\,C(A)$，输出向量的集合一般会改变。行操作保留解的输入坐标，却变换了输出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 2. Follow the pivots, including the skipped column
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*沿主元消元，也要识别跳过的列*

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}
1&2&2&2\\
2&4&6&8\\
3&6&8&10
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$ and $R_3\leftarrow R_3-3R_1$, then subtract the new second row from the third:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先做 $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$、$R_3\leftarrow R_3-3R_1$，再把新的第二行从第三行减去：

$$
A\longrightarrow
\begin{pmatrix}
1&2&2&2\\
0&0&2&4\\
0&0&2&4
\end{pmatrix}
\longrightarrow
U=\begin{pmatrix}
1&2&2&2\\
0&0&2&4\\
0&0&0&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Column 2 has no available nonzero pivot below the first pivot row, so move to column 3 without consuming another pivot row. The pivots are in columns 1 and 3. There are two pivots, hence [[主元与自由变量|rank $r=2$]], with pivot variables $x_1,x_3$ and [[自由变量|free variables]] $x_2,x_4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一行主元确定后，第二列剩余位置全为零，无法提供新主元，因此保持当前行，继续检查第三列。主元位于第 1、3 列，共两个，所以[[主元与自由变量|秩 $r=2$]]；主元变量是 $x_1,x_3$，[[自由变量]]是 $x_2,x_4$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A free column is a fixed column of the matrix. What is freely chosen is its coefficient in the solution. The equations do not determine $x_2,x_4$ separately; once those values are chosen, they determine the pivot variables. The zero row records a redundant equation: the original third row equals the sum of the first two.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“自由列”本身仍是矩阵中确定的列；可以自由选取的是它在解中的系数。方程不能单独确定 $x_2,x_4$，但给定二者后，主元变量便被确定。零行则表示一个冗余方程：原第三行等于前两行之和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 3. From free parameters to all solutions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从自由参数写出全部解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set $x_2=s$ and $x_4=t$. Back-substitution gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $x_2=s,\ x_4=t$，回代得到

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x_3+4x_4=0&\implies x_3=-2t,\\
x_1+2x_2+2x_3+2x_4=0&\implies x_1=-2s+2t.
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
x=
\begin{pmatrix}-2s+2t\\s\\-2t\\t\end{pmatrix}
=s\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}-2\\1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}}_{v_1}
+t\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}2\\0\\-2\\1\end{pmatrix}}_{v_2},
\qquad s,t\in\mathbb R.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[RREF读取零空间基|special solutions]] arise by setting one free variable to $1$ and the other to $0$. They span the nullspace because every choice $(s,t)$ appears above. They are independent because the second and fourth entries of $sv_1+tv_2=0$ force $s=t=0$. Therefore $N(A)$ is a two-dimensional plane inside $\mathbb R^4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[RREF读取零空间基|特殊解]]来自“一个自由变量取 $1$，其余取 $0$”。它们张成零空间，因为上式已包含任意 $(s,t)$；它们线性无关，因为 $sv_1+tv_2=0$ 的第 2、4 个分量直接迫使 $s=t=0$。因此 $N(A)$ 是 $\mathbb R^4$ 中的二维平面。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an $m\times n$ matrix of rank $r$, the same argument gives $n-r$ free variables and independent special solutions. This is the elimination form of the [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity theorem]]. With no free variables, only the zero solution remains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对秩为 $r$ 的 $m\times n$ 矩阵，同一论证给出 $n-r$ 个自由变量及独立特殊解。这就是[[秩零度定理]]在消元中的表现。若没有自由变量，则只有零解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 4. RREF and the block formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行最简形与分块公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continue from $U$: subtract row 2 from row 1, then divide row 2 by $2$. The [[行最简形|reduced row echelon form]] has pivot ones with zero entries above and below:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $U$ 继续：先用第一行减第二行，再把第二行除以 $2$，便得到[[行最简形]]，主元都是 $1$，且主元列其余位置为零：

$$
R=\begin{pmatrix}
1&2&0&-2\\
0&0&1&2\\
0&0&0&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To display the pivot columns first, swap **columns 2 and 3**, and reorder the coordinates at the same time. Write
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要把主元列集中到前面，应交换**第 2、3 列**，并同时重排变量坐标。记

$$
z=(x_1,x_3,x_2,x_4)^T,
\qquad
\widetilde R=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&2&-2\\
0&1&0&2\\
0&0&0&0
\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}I_2&F\\0&0\end{bmatrix},
\quad
F=\begin{pmatrix}2&-2\\0&2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now $\widetilde Rz=0$ says $z_{\mathrm{pivot}}=-Fz_{\mathrm{free}}$. Choosing the two standard basis vectors for the free part, one at a time, and placing the results side by side gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

此时 $\widetilde Rz=0$ 给出 $z_{\mathrm{pivot}}=-Fz_{\mathrm{free}}$。让自由部分依次取两个标准基向量，再把结果并排放置，得到

$$
\widetilde N=\begin{bmatrix}-F\\I_2\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}
-2&2\\
0&-2\\
1&0\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lower identity block represents several choices made **in separate columns**; a single free-variable vector has not become an identity matrix. The rows of $\widetilde N$ follow the reordered coordinates $(x_1,x_3,x_2,x_4)$. To recover the original coordinates, swap its rows 2 and 3:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下方的单位矩阵表示**不同列分别采用不同取值**，并不是把一个自由变量向量直接变成矩阵。$\widetilde N$ 的行按 $(x_1,x_3,x_2,x_4)$ 排列；要恢复原变量顺序，必须交换它的第 2、3 行：

$$
N=\begin{pmatrix}
-2&2\\
1&0\\
0&-2\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
x=N\begin{pmatrix}s\\t\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a [[零空间矩阵|nullspace matrix]]: its columns are a basis, while the whole nullspace is $\{Nc:c\in\mathbb R^2\}$. In general, if a column permutation gives $\widetilde R=RQ$, then [[列置换后的解向量|original coordinates satisfy $x=Qz$]] and $N=Q\widetilde N$. The block formula must be interpreted in its stated variable order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就是[[零空间矩阵]]：它的列构成一组基，整个零空间则是 $\{Nc:c\in\mathbb R^2\}$。一般地，若列置换给出 $\widetilde R=RQ$，则[[列置换后的解向量|原坐标满足 $x=Qz$]]，从而 $N=Q\widetilde N$。使用分块公式时，必须明确当前变量顺序。

## 5. The transpose example has a different nullspace
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*转置例子的零空间不同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the transpose of the same lecture matrix, elimination gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同一课堂矩阵的转置，消元得到

$$
A^T=\begin{pmatrix}
1&2&3\\
2&4&6\\
2&6&8\\
2&8&10
\end{pmatrix}
\longrightarrow
U_T=\begin{pmatrix}
1&2&3\\
0&2&2\\
0&0&0\\
0&0&0
\end{pmatrix}
\longrightarrow
R_T=\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&1\\
0&1&1\\
0&0&0\\
0&0&0
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second pivot requires moving a nonzero row above a zero row. There are still two pivots, consistent with [[行秩等于列秩|equality of row and column rank]], but now only three variables. With $y_3=t$, the solution is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

寻找第二个主元时，需要把非零行换到零行上方。主元仍有两个，与[[行秩等于列秩]]一致；但未知量现在只有三个。令 $y_3=t$，得到

$$
N(A^T)=\left\{t\begin{pmatrix}-1\\-1\\1\end{pmatrix}:t\in\mathbb R\right\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result also follows from the original row relation: minus row 1 minus row 2 plus row 3 is zero. The original nullspace has dimension $4-2=2$ in $\mathbb R^4$; the transpose nullspace has dimension $3-2=1$ in $\mathbb R^3$. The free parameter is essential when describing the whole space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也可由原矩阵的行关系直接读出：负第一行、负第二行加第三行为零。原零空间是 $\mathbb R^4$ 中的二维空间，转置后的零空间则是 $\mathbb R^3$ 中的一维空间；描述整个空间时，自由参数不能省略。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 6. Exercise 7.1: RREF, rank, and scaled solutions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*习题 7.1：行最简形、秩与缩放后的解*

$$
A_H=\begin{pmatrix}
1&5&7&9\\
0&4&1&7\\
2&-2&11&-3
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After $R_3\leftarrow R_3-2R_1$, the third row is $(0,-12,-3,-21)=-3R_2$, so eliminate it with $R_3\leftarrow R_3+3R_2$. Divide row 2 by $4$ and subtract five times the normalized row from row 1:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

做 $R_3\leftarrow R_3-2R_1$ 后，第三行是 $(0,-12,-3,-21)=-3R_2$，再用 $R_3\leftarrow R_3+3R_2$ 消掉它。将第二行除以 $4$，再用第一行减去归一后第二行的五倍：

$$
R_H=\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&23/4&1/4\\
0&1&1/4&7/4\\
0&0&0&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \operatorname{rank}(A_H)=2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The free variables are $x_3,x_4$. Setting each to $1$ in turn gives the standard special solutions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自由变量为 $x_3,x_4$，依次令其中一个为 $1$、另一个为 $0$，得到标准形式的特殊解

$$
v_1=\begin{pmatrix}-23/4\\-1/4\\1\\0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
v_2=\begin{pmatrix}-1/4\\-7/4\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying either vector by a nonzero scalar preserves its solution direction. The integer vectors below are $-4v_1$ and $-4v_2$, so they are also independent nullspace vectors and form a valid basis:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把任一向量乘以非零常数，不改变它代表的解方向。下列整数向量分别为 $-4v_1$、$-4v_2$，因此也都是正确且独立的零空间向量，构成有效的基：

$$
w_1=\begin{pmatrix}23\\1\\-4\\0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
w_2=\begin{pmatrix}1\\7\\0\\-4\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A_Hw_1=A_Hw_2=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When using the lecture's convention for “special solutions,” normalize the activated free entry to $1$. When asked for the full nullspace, include arbitrary coefficients: $N(A_H)=\{cv_1+dv_2:c,d\in\mathbb R\}$, equivalently using $w_1,w_2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若按课上“特殊解”的约定书写，将被激活的自由分量归一为 $1$ 即可。若题目要求整个零空间，则写出任意系数：$N(A_H)=\{cv_1+dv_2:c,d\in\mathbb R\}$；改用 $w_1,w_2$ 表示完全等价。

## 7. Exercise 7.2: a nonzero matrix can annihilate another
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*习题 7.2：非零矩阵也能把另一矩阵映成零*

$$
B=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}
=[b\ b],\qquad b=(1,1)^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By [[矩阵乘积逐列作用|columnwise multiplication]], $MB=[Mb\ Mb]$. The product has rank $1$ when $Mb\ne0$ and rank $0$ when $Mb=0$. Two valid choices are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按照[[矩阵乘积逐列作用|逐列乘法]]，$MB=[Mb\ Mb]$。若 $Mb\ne0$，乘积的秩为 $1$；若 $Mb=0$，乘积的秩为 $0$。两种有效构造为

$$
A_1=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-1&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A_1B=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \operatorname{rank}(A_1B)=1;
$$

$$
A_2=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A_2B=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity is another valid $A_1$, but the first choice above is equally correct. For $A_2$, every row sums to zero, so it annihilates $(1,1)^T$. More generally, [[矩阵乘积为零|$AB=0$ exactly when every column of $B$ belongs to $N(A)$]]. Both matrices can be nonzero: the intermediate outputs all lie in directions the next matrix erases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$A_1$ 也可以取单位矩阵，但上面的构造同样正确。$A_2$ 的每行元素和为零，因而把 $(1,1)^T$ 映成零。一般地，[[矩阵乘积为零|$AB=0$ 当且仅当 $B$ 的每一列都在 $N(A)$ 中]]。两个矩阵都可以非零，因为前一个阶段产生的输出恰好都落在后一个阶段抹去的方向上。
