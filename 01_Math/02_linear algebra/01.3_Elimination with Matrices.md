<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Elimination replaces a system by an easier one with the **same solutions**. The key connection is that a row vector times a matrix combines its rows, so every elimination step can be written as left multiplication by an elementary matrix. Combining those matrices records the whole procedure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

消元把方程组变成一个**解集相同、却更容易求解**的系统。关键联系是：行向量乘矩阵，会对矩阵的各行作线性组合，因此每一步消元都能写成左乘一个初等矩阵；把这些矩阵相乘，就记录了整个过程。

## 1. From equations to an upper triangular system
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从方程组到上三角系统*

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&1\\3&8&1\\0&4&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\mathbf x=\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\\z\end{pmatrix},\qquad
b=\begin{pmatrix}2\\12\\2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rows of $A\mathbf x=b$ are $x+2y+z=2$, $3x+8y+z=12$, and $4y+z=2$. [[高斯消元|Gaussian elimination]] first removes $x$ from the lower equations, then removes $y$ from the last equation. Carry $b$ along in the augmented matrix:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$A\mathbf x=b$ 的三行分别是 $x+2y+z=2$、$3x+8y+z=12$、$4y+z=2$。[[高斯消元]]先从下面的方程中消掉 $x$，再从最后一式中消掉 $y$。用增广矩阵把 $b$ 一起带上：

$$
\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}
1&2&1&2\\3&8&1&12\\0&4&1&2
\end{array}\right]
\xrightarrow{R_2\leftarrow R_2-3R_1}
\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}
1&2&1&2\\0&2&-2&6\\0&4&1&2
\end{array}\right]
\xrightarrow{R_3\leftarrow R_3-2R_2}
\left[\begin{array}{ccc|c}
1&2&1&2\\0&2&-2&6\\0&0&5&-10
\end{array}\right].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[主元|pivots]] are $1,2,5$. Each multiplier is the entry to eliminate divided by the current pivot: $3/1=3$, then $4/2=2$. The second step uses the **updated** second row. The entry below the first pivot in row 3 is already zero, so that step does nothing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[主元]]是 $1,2,5$。每次的乘子等于“待消元素除以当前主元”：先是 $3/1=3$，再是 $4/2=2$。第二步使用的是**已经更新过的**第二行。第三行第一列本来就是零，因此那里无需再做实质操作。

$$
U=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&1\\0&2&-2\\0&0&5\end{pmatrix},\qquad
c=\begin{pmatrix}2\\6\\-10\end{pmatrix},\qquad U\mathbf x=c.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Back substitution** works from the bottom upward: $5z=-10$ gives $z=-2$; $2y-2z=6$ gives $y=1$; $x+2y+z=2$ gives $x=2$. Substitution into the original equations checks all three right-hand sides: $2,12,2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**Back substitution（回代）**从下往上进行：由 $5z=-10$ 得 $z=-2$，由 $2y-2z=6$ 得 $y=1$，再由 $x+2y+z=2$ 得 $x=2$。代回原方程，三个左端分别为 $2,12,2$，与原右端一致。

## 2. Why the elimination matrix goes on the left
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么消元矩阵放在左边*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are two complementary ways to read multiplication. If $A$ has columns $a_1,a_2,a_3$ and rows $r_1,r_2,r_3$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵乘法可以从两个互补方向读。若 $A$ 的列为 $a_1,a_2,a_3$，行为 $r_1,r_2,r_3$，则

$$
A\begin{pmatrix}\alpha\\\beta\\\gamma\end{pmatrix}
=\alpha a_1+\beta a_2+\gamma a_3,
\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&\beta&\gamma\end{pmatrix}A
=\alpha r_1+\beta r_2+\gamma r_3.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first expression combines **columns**; the second combines **rows**. In a product $EA$, each row of $E$ supplies the coefficients for one output row. This [[矩阵乘积逐行作用|rowwise interpretation]] explains the placement of $E$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前式组合**列**，后式组合**行**。在 $EA$ 中，$E$ 的每一行提供一组系数，用来生成结果中的对应行。这个[[矩阵乘积逐行作用|逐行读法]]解释了 $E$ 为什么要放在左边。

$$
E_{21}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\-3&1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
E_{21}A=\begin{pmatrix}r_1\\-3r_1+r_2\\r_3\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rows $(1,0,0)$ and $(0,0,1)$ copy rows 1 and 3 unchanged; $(-3,1,0)$ replaces row 2 by $r_2-3r_1$. An [[初等矩阵|elementary matrix]] is obtained by performing that same single row operation on the identity matrix. Here the subscript $21$ locates the entry being eliminated: row 2, column 1.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$(1,0,0)$ 和 $(0,0,1)$ 分别原样取出第一、第三行；$(-3,1,0)$ 把第二行替换为 $r_2-3r_1$。构造[[初等矩阵]]时，只需对单位矩阵施行同一个行操作。这里下标 $21$ 标记被消去元素的位置：第二行、第一列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 3. Combine the steps without changing their order
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把多步合起来，但保留操作顺序*

$$
E_{32}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&1&0\\0&-2&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
E_{32}(E_{21}A)=(E_{32}E_{21})A=U.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$E_{21}$ acts first because it is closest to $A$; $E_{32}$ then uses the new second row. Associativity permits regrouping the product, while exchanging the factors would change the operations. In this example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$E_{21}$ 最靠近 $A$，所以先作用；$E_{32}$ 随后使用新的第二行。结合律允许改变括号，交换两个因子则改变了操作过程。本例中

$$
E=E_{32}E_{21}
=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\-3&1&0\\6&-2&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
EA=U,\qquad Eb=c.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $6$ records an indirect effect on the final third row: $r_3-2(r_2-3r_1)=r_3-2r_2+6r_1$. Reversing the factors would instead give a zero in position $(3,1)$, so it would not perform the same elimination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中的 $6$ 记录了第一步对最终第三行的间接影响：$r_3-2(r_2-3r_1)=r_3-2r_2+6r_1$。若交换两个因子，乘积的 $(3,1)$ 位置会变成零，执行的就不是同一套消元。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 4. Why elimination preserves every solution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么消元保持全部解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtracting $3r_1$ from row 2 can be undone by adding it back. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二行减去 $3r_1$ 后，再加回 $3r_1$ 就能恢复，因此

$$
E_{21}^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\3&1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
E_{21}^{-1}E_{21}=I.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each step is reversible, so their product $E$ is invertible. This proves [[行操作的解集不变性|equality of the solution sets]] in both directions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每一步都可逆，组合后的 $E$ 也可逆。这给出了[[行操作的解集不变性|解集相同]]的双向证明：

$$
A\mathbf x=b
\ \Longrightarrow\ E(A\mathbf x)=Eb
\ \Longrightarrow\ (EA)\mathbf x=Eb,
$$
$$
(EA)\mathbf x=Eb
\ \Longrightarrow\ E^{-1}(EA)\mathbf x=E^{-1}Eb
\ \Longrightarrow\ A\mathbf x=b.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both $A$ and $b$ must receive the **same** operations. Keeping the old $b$ while replacing $A$ by $U$ generally defines a different system. Multiplying an entire row by zero is also invalid as an equivalence step, because it can erase a constraint irreversibly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$A$ 与 $b$ 必须接受**同一套**操作。只把 $A$ 换成 $U$ 却保留原来的 $b$，一般就变成了另一个方程组。把整行乘零也不能作为等价变形，因为它可能不可逆地抹掉一条约束。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Geometrically, the equations describe planes in the space of inputs $(x,y,z)$. Reversible combinations can replace individual planes, but preserve their **common intersection**. In this example, the point $(2,1,-2)$ stays fixed while the equations become easier to solve. Preserving the solutions does not require preserving each plane separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从几何上看，方程描述输入空间 $(x,y,z)$ 中的平面。可逆地组合方程可以换掉某个平面，却保持它们的**共同交集**。本例的交点 $(2,1,-2)$ 始终相同，只是方程变得更容易求解。解集保持不变，并不要求每个平面都保持原样。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 5. A zero pivot position needs a diagnosis
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*遇到零主元位置，要先分情况*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pivot itself must be nonzero; otherwise the multiplier would involve division by zero. A zero in the intended pivot position does **not** by itself show that the matrix is singular.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主元本身必须非零，否则计算乘子时会除以零。预定的主元位置出现零，**本身不能**证明矩阵不可逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **A nonzero entry exists below it in the same column:** exchange rows and continue. For example, $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$ becomes $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ after swapping its rows, and is invertible.
- **No nonzero entry remains in that column below or at the current position:** that column supplies no new pivot. Continue to later columns when describing the general solution. For a square matrix, failure to obtain a pivot in every column means it is singular; the system may have no solution or infinitely many, depending on the right-hand side.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **同列下方还有非零元素：** 交换行后继续。例如 $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$ 换行后成为 $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$，仍然可逆。
- **当前位置及同列下方全为零：** 这一列不能提供新主元。求一般解时仍可继续处理后面的列。对方阵而言，最终无法在每列取得主元就说明不可逆；方程组可能无解，也可能有无穷多解，取决于右端。

$$
\left[\begin{array}{cc|c}1&1&2\\0&0&0\end{array}\right]
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x+y=2,
\qquad
\left[\begin{array}{cc|c}1&1&2\\0&0&1\end{array}\right]
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 0=1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first system has a line of solutions; the second is inconsistent. Thus “failure” here means failure to obtain a **unique solution**, while elimination still reveals the structure. The number of pivots counts independent coefficient rows; consistency must also be checked using the augmented column.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左边的系统有一条解直线，右边则矛盾无解。因此这里的“失败”指无法得到**唯一解**，消元仍然能揭示结构。主元数计算独立系数行的数量；方程是否相容，还必须一起检查增广列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 6. Row exchanges and undoing the process
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*换行，以及如何撤销整个过程*

$$
P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This [[置换矩阵|permutation matrix]] exchanges the first two rows in $PA$, and the first two columns in $AP$. To swap equations, use $[PA\mid Pb]$. Column swaps instead change the ordering of variables, which must be tracked. Here $P^{-1}=P$ because repeating the same swap restores the original order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个[[置换矩阵]]在 $PA$ 中交换前两行，在 $AP$ 中交换前两列。交换方程时应使用 $[PA\mid Pb]$；交换列则会改变变量次序，必须记录这种变化。这里 $P^{-1}=P$，因为同样的交换做两次就回到原来的顺序。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the two elimination steps above, undo the last operation first:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对前面的两步消元，撤销时先恢复最后一步：

$$
E_{32}E_{21}A=U
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
A=E_{21}^{-1}E_{32}^{-1}U,
\qquad
E_{21}^{-1}E_{32}^{-1}
=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\3&1&0\\0&2&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matrix on the right records how to reconstruct $A$ from $U$. This is the starting point for [[消元与LU分解|the later factorization $A=LU$]]; the current lesson only needs the reversible operations and their order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

右边这个矩阵记录如何从 $U$ 恢复 $A$，也是后续[[消元与LU分解|分解 $A=LU$]]的起点。本讲先把可逆操作及其先后顺序理解清楚。

## 7. A brief recall check
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*简短回忆检查*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without looking back, write the matrix for $R_3\leftarrow R_3-2R_2$, explain why it multiplies on the left, and give the reverse operation. Then explain why a zero in the first pivot position does not always prevent a unique solution. These checks connect the calculation to its reason.
