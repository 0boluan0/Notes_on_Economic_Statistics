The central question is **whether the columns of a matrix can combine to produce a target vector**. The familiar equations, their geometric intersections, and the compact notation $A\mathbf{x}=b$ describe the same problem. The column picture makes it possible to ask a further question: which targets can this matrix produce at all?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本讲的核心问题是：**矩阵的列向量能否组合出指定的目标向量？** 熟悉的方程组、几何图形的交点，以及紧凑写法 $A\mathbf{x}=b$，描述的是同一个问题。列图像还让我们继续追问：这个矩阵究竟能产生哪些目标？

## 1. Equations and matrix notation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*方程组与矩阵写法*

$$
\begin{cases}
2x-y=0,\\
-x+2y=3
\end{cases}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}}_{A}
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\end{pmatrix}}_{\mathbf{x}}
=
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}0\\3\end{pmatrix}}_{b}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$A$ records the coefficients, $\mathbf{x}$ contains the unknowns, and $b$ contains the right-hand sides. Expanding the matrix product recovers the original equations exactly. Solving by substitution remains valid: $y=2x$, so $-x+4x=3$, giving $(x,y)=(1,2)$. Matrix notation organises the same calculation and extends it to larger systems.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$A$ 收集系数，$\mathbf{x}$ 收集未知数，$b$ 收集等号右侧的数。展开矩阵乘法，就准确回到原方程组。代入法仍然有效：由 $y=2x$，得到 $-x+4x=3$，所以 $(x,y)=(1,2)$。矩阵记号把同一套计算组织起来，并推广到更大的方程组。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$, there are $n$ input coefficients and $m$ output coordinates:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，输入有 $n$ 个系数，输出有 $m$ 个坐标：

$$
\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb R^n,\qquad A\mathbf{x}\in\mathbb R^m,\qquad b\in\mathbb R^m.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 2. Row picture and column picture
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行图像与列图像*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[方程组的行图像|row picture]], each equation is a constraint on the unknowns. The lines $y=2x$ and $y=(x+3)/2$ intersect at $(1,2)$; that point satisfies both equations. In three unknowns, an equation with a nonzero coefficient vector describes a plane. A system asks for the common intersection of all such planes, which need not be a single point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[方程组的行图像|行图像]]中，每个方程都是对未知数的约束。直线 $y=2x$ 与 $y=(x+3)/2$ 相交于 $(1,2)$，这个点同时满足两个方程。三个未知数时，系数向量非零的一个方程对应一个平面；方程组要求所有这些平面的公共交集，交集并不一定只有一个点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the **column picture**, separate the matrix into its columns:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在**列图像**中，把矩阵拆成两列：

$$
a_1=\begin{pmatrix}2\\-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
a_2=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A\mathbf{x}=xa_1+ya_2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The unknowns are now the weights of two vectors. Multiplication by a scalar stretches a vector, reverses it if the scalar is negative, or gives zero if the scalar is zero. Vector addition combines the resulting arrows. Here, one copy of $a_1$ plus two copies of $a_2$ reaches $b$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

未知数现在是两个向量的权重。数乘会伸缩向量；系数为负时反向，为零时得到零向量。向量相加就是组合这些箭头。这里一份 $a_1$ 加上两份 $a_2$，恰好到达 $b$：

$$
\begin{pmatrix}2\\-1\end{pmatrix}
+2\begin{pmatrix}-1\\2\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}0\\3\end{pmatrix}.
$$

![[98_attachment/linear_algebra/mit18_06sc/mit18.06sc-l01-input-output.png|900]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The left panel locates the **input** $\mathbf{x}=(1,2)^T$; the right panel constructs the **output** $b=(0,3)^T$. Their roles remain different even though both happen to have two coordinates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左图寻找的是**输入** $\mathbf{x}=(1,2)^T$，右图组合出来的是**输出** $b=(0,3)^T$。即使它们恰好都有两个坐标，所扮演的角色也不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, [[Ax的列组合解释|a matrix–vector product is a column combination]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，[[Ax的列组合解释|矩阵乘向量就是列的线性组合]]：

$$
A=[a_1\ \cdots\ a_n]
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
A\mathbf{x}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}x_ja_j.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why does this agree with multiplying rows by the vector? The $i$th coordinate of the column combination is $\sum_j x_j a_{ij}$, exactly the $i$th coordinate of the matrix product. These are two readings of the same multiplication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么它和“每一行乘向量”的计算一致？列组合的第 $i$ 个坐标是 $\sum_j x_j a_{ij}$，恰好就是矩阵乘积的第 $i$ 个坐标。二者是同一次乘法的两种读法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lecture's three-dimensional example uses
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

视频中的三维例子使用

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1&0\\-1&2&-1\\0&-3&4\end{pmatrix},
\qquad b=\begin{pmatrix}0\\-1\\4\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $b$ is already the third column, so $\mathbf{x}=(0,0,1)^T$ is a solution immediately. The same input locates the common point of the three row planes. The column picture can reveal a solution before any elimination is performed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $b$ 本身就是第三列，因此立刻得到一个解 $\mathbf{x}=(0,0,1)^T$。同一个输入也给出了三个行平面的公共点。列图像有时可以在消元之前直接揭示一个解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 3. One target versus every target
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一个目标可达，与每个目标都可达*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[列空间|column space]] is the set of all possible outputs:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[列空间|列空间]]收集所有可能的输出：

$$
C(A)=\left\{\sum_{j=1}^{n}x_ja_j:x_1,\ldots,x_n\in\mathbb R\right\}.
$$

$$
A\mathbf{x}=b\text{ has a solution}\iff b\in C(A),
\qquad
A\mathbf{x}=b\text{ is solvable for every }b\in\mathbb R^m
\iff C(A)=\mathbb R^m.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first statement concerns one chosen $b$. The second asks whether the columns cover the whole output space. A matrix may solve some right-hand sides perfectly while being unable to reach others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一句话讨论某个选定的 $b$；第二句话讨论列向量能否覆盖整个输出空间。同一个矩阵可能对某些右端完全可解，对另一些右端却根本不可达。

$$
D=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
D\begin{pmatrix}u\\v\end{pmatrix}
=(u+2v)\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both columns point along the same line. Every output has its second coordinate equal to twice its first. Thus $(3,6)^T$ is reachable, whereas $(3,7)^T$ is not. For the reachable target, the solutions are $(u,v)=(3-2t,t)$, $t\in\mathbb R$: many different weights give the same output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两列沿着同一条直线，因此任何输出的第二个坐标都等于第一个坐标的两倍。$(3,6)^T$ 可以到达，$(3,7)^T$ 则不能。对可达目标，全部解是 $(u,v)=(3-2t,t)$、$t\in\mathbb R$：多组不同权重可以产生同一输出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### Independence and coverage have different roles
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*线性无关与覆盖性各自解决什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[线性无关|Linear independence]] means that only all-zero coefficients produce the zero vector. Dependence means that some **nonzero coefficient vector** produces zero, revealing redundancy. If a new column is a combination of the existing columns, adding it does not enlarge their span.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[线性无关|线性无关]]指只有全零系数才能组合出零向量；线性相关则指存在一组**不全为零的系数**使组合为零，说明方向中存在冗余。如果新加入的列能够由已有列组合出来，它就不会扩大原来的张成空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an $n\times n$ matrix, the following conditions are equivalent: the $n$ columns are independent; they span $\mathbb R^n$; the matrix is invertible; every $b$ has a unique solution. This square-matrix equivalence must not be applied unchanged to rectangular matrices. [[线性无关与张成|Independence controls uniqueness; spanning controls coverage]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $n\times n$ 方阵，以下条件等价：这 $n$ 列线性无关；它们张成 $\mathbb R^n$；矩阵可逆；每个 $b$ 都有唯一解。这组方阵等价关系不能原样套用到矩形矩阵。[[线性无关与张成|无关性控制表示是否唯一，张成性控制目标是否全部可达]]：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Two independent vectors in $\mathbb R^3$ can span a plane, but cannot fill all of $\mathbb R^3$.
- Three vectors $(1,0)^T,(0,1)^T,(1,1)^T$ span $\mathbb R^2$, although the third is the sum of the first two.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $\mathbb R^3$ 中两个线性无关向量可以张成一个平面，却不能覆盖整个 $\mathbb R^3$。
- 三个向量 $(1,0)^T,(0,1)^T,(1,1)^T$ 可以张成 $\mathbb R^2$，尽管第三个等于前两个之和。

## 4. Independent constraints and degrees of freedom
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*独立约束与自由度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before imposing equations, $n$ real unknowns can vary freely in $\mathbb R^n$. For a **consistent** system, adding one genuinely independent linear constraint reduces the solution-set dimension by one. “Independent” is stronger than “written differently.” Starting with $x+y=1$, compare three possible additions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有方程时，$n$ 个实未知数可以在 $\mathbb R^n$ 中自由变化。对**相容，即有解**的方程组，增加一条真正独立的线性约束，会把解集维数减少一。“独立”比“写得不一样”更严格。从 $x+y=1$ 出发，比较三种新增方程：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Additional equation | Effect | Solution set |
|---|---|---|
| $2x+2y=2$ | Redundant: twice the original | Same line |
| $x-y=0$ | Independent constraint | One point: $(1/2,1/2)$ |
| $x+y=2$ | Inconsistent with the original | Empty |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 新增方程 | 作用 | 解集 |
|---|---|---|
| $2x+2y=2$ | 冗余：原式的两倍 | 同一直线 |
| $x-y=0$ | 独立约束 | 一个点：$(1/2,1/2)$ |
| $x+y=2$ | 与原式矛盾 | 空集 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first addition contributes no new restriction; the second leaves no free direction; the third removes every candidate. The precise counting rule, developed later in the course, is $n-\operatorname{rank}(A)$ degrees of freedom **when the system is consistent**. This explains the intuition without assuming that every extra equation reduces dimension. See [[独立约束与自由度|the condition and a short justification]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一种没有增加限制；第二种消除了最后一个自由方向；第三种排除了所有候选解。后续课程会给出准确计数：**系统相容时**，自由度为 $n-\operatorname{rank}(A)$。这保留了直觉，同时避免把“每多一个方程”都误当作减少一维。条件与简短论证见[[独立约束与自由度]]。

## 5. Exercises 1.1–1.3
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*第一次练习的推理与纠错*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### 5.1 Three coordinates do not imply a three-dimensional span
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*三个坐标，不等于张成三维空间*

$$
w_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
w_2=\begin{pmatrix}4\\5\\6\end{pmatrix},\qquad
w_3=\begin{pmatrix}7\\8\\9\end{pmatrix}.
$$

$$
w_1-2w_2+w_3
=\begin{pmatrix}1-8+7\\2-10+8\\3-12+9\end{pmatrix}
=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficient vector $(1,-2,1)^T$ is nonzero, so the vectors are dependent. Equivalently, $w_3=2w_2-w_1$: the third column adds no new direction. Since $w_1$ and $w_2$ are not scalar multiples, they supply exactly two independent directions. The three vectors therefore span a plane through the origin.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数向量 $(1,-2,1)^T$ 非零，因此这组向量线性相关。等价地，$w_3=2w_2-w_1$，第三列没有增加新方向。前两列并非彼此的倍数，所以恰好提供两个独立方向。这三个向量因此张成一个过原点的平面。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The plane is $q_1-2q_2+q_3=0$. Every listed vector satisfies this equation. **The ambient space is $\mathbb R^3$; the span has dimension two.** The number of written coordinates describes the ambient space, while [[维数|dimension of the span]] counts independent directions within it. The matrix $W=[w_1\ w_2\ w_3]$ is not invertible because $W(1,-2,1)^T=0$ has a nonzero solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个平面可以写成 $q_1-2q_2+q_3=0$，三个向量都满足该式。**所在的环境空间是 $\mathbb R^3$，张成空间的维数却是二。** 写出的坐标数量描述环境空间，而[[维数|张成空间的维数]]计算其中的独立方向数。矩阵 $W=[w_1\ w_2\ w_3]$ 不可逆，因为 $W(1,-2,1)^T=0$ 存在非零解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### 5.2 Multiply by combining columns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*用列组合计算矩阵乘向量*

$$
\begin{pmatrix}1&2&0\\2&0&3\\4&1&1\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}3\\-2\\1\end{pmatrix}
=3\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\4\end{pmatrix}
-2\begin{pmatrix}2\\0\\1\end{pmatrix}
+\begin{pmatrix}0\\3\\1\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\9\\11\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The weights $3,-2,1$ multiply entire columns. Coordinatewise arithmetic gives $3-4+0=-1$, $6+0+3=9$, and $12-2+1=11$. This is also a direct check of the row-by-vector calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

权重 $3,-2,1$ 分别乘整根列向量。逐坐标相加得到 $3-4+0=-1$、$6+0+3=9$、$12-2+1=11$，也与逐行乘向量的结果一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### 5.3 Check dimensions before multiplying matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵相乘前先检查尺寸*

$$
(3\times2)(2\times3)\longrightarrow3\times3,
\qquad
(m\times n)(n\times p)\longrightarrow m\times p.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The statement in Problem 1.3 is **true**. The inner dimensions must agree so that the row–column sums are defined; the outer dimensions determine the result. Equivalently, each of the $p$ columns of $B$ has length $n$, and $A$ maps it to an $m$-component column, producing $p$ such columns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第 1.3 题为 **true**。中间的尺寸必须相等，逐行与逐列的乘积和才有定义；外侧尺寸决定结果大小。等价地，$B$ 的每一列长度为 $n$，经 $A$ 变为 $m$ 个分量；共有 $p$ 列，所以结果是 $m\times p$。
