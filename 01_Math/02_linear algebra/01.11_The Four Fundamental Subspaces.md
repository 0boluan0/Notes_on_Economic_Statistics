## 1. Locate the four spaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先看向量有几个坐标，再看空间有几个独立方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ have rank $r$. Its [[四个基本子空间|four fundamental subspaces]] are the column space, nullspace, row space, and left nullspace. A column has $m$ entries; a row has $n$. The ambient space follows from this length, while dimension counts independent directions within it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，秩为 $r$。[[四个基本子空间]]分别为列空间、零空间、行空间、左零空间。列有 $m$ 个分量，行有 $n$ 个分量；所在空间由向量长度决定，维数则数其中的独立方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Space | Definition | Ambient space | Dimension |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Column space $C(A)$ | All $Ax$ | $\mathbb R^m$ | $r$ |
| Left nullspace $N(A^T)$ | All $y$ satisfying $A^Ty=0$ | $\mathbb R^m$ | $m-r$ |
| Row space $C(A^T)$ | All $A^Ty$ | $\mathbb R^n$ | $r$ |
| Nullspace $N(A)$ | All $x$ satisfying $Ax=0$ | $\mathbb R^n$ | $n-r$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The column space and left nullspace lie on the output side, with dimensions adding to $m$; the row space and nullspace lie on the input side, with dimensions adding to $n$. Equal dimensions of row and column spaces do not make them the same set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

列空间与左零空间在输出侧 $\mathbb R^m$，维数之和为 $m$；行空间与零空间在输入侧 $\mathbb R^n$，维数之和为 $n$。行空间和列空间的维数相等，并不表示它们是同一个集合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the [[左零空间|left nullspace]], transposing the equation gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[左零空间]]，把方程转置得到

$$
A^Ty=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad y^TA=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $x\in N(A)$ provides coefficients that combine **columns** into zero; $y\in N(A^T)$ provides coefficients that combine **rows** into zero. The name “left” refers to $y^T$ multiplying $A$ on the left. The vector $y$ itself is still a column vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，$x\in N(A)$ 给出把**各列**组合成零的系数；$y\in N(A^T)$ 给出把**各行**组合成零的系数。“左”来自 $y^T$ 在 $A$ 左侧相乘，而 $y$ 本身仍按列向量书写。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 2. What elimination preserves
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行空间与零空间保持不变，列空间一般不保持*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose reversible row operations produce $R=EA$. The matrix $E$ is invertible. Every row of $R$ is a combination of rows of $A$; conversely, $A=E^{-1}R$ expresses every row of $A$ using rows of $R$. Hence their row spaces are equal. Also,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若可逆行操作得到 $R=EA$，则 $E$ 可逆。$R$ 的每条行都是 $A$ 的行组合；反过来，$A=E^{-1}R$ 又把 $A$ 的每条行写成 $R$ 的行组合，所以行空间相同。同时，

$$
Rx=0\iff EAx=0\iff Ax=0,
\qquad N(R)=N(A).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Column vectors instead undergo the transformation $a_j\mapsto Ea_j$, giving $C(R)=E\,C(A)$. The resulting column space can differ, but need not: if $A$ has full row rank, both column spaces are $\mathbb R^m$. So “row operations do not preserve column space in general” is the correct statement. See [[行操作与四个子空间|the transformation rules]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

列向量则经历 $a_j\mapsto Ea_j$ 的变换，所以 $C(R)=E\,C(A)$。结果可能改变，但不是必然改变：若 $A$ 满行秩，前后列空间都等于 $\mathbb R^m$。准确表述是“行操作一般不保持列空间”。见[[行操作与四个子空间]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $R$ is in reduced row echelon form, its nonzero rows are independent: looking at their pivot columns forces every coefficient in a zero combination to vanish. They therefore form a [[RREF读取行空间基|basis for the original row space]]. For the column-space basis, use the pivot indices to select **original columns of $A$**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $R$ 是行最简形，其非零行无关：逐个看主元列，零组合中的每个系数都被迫为零。因此这些非零行构成[[RREF读取行空间基|原行空间的一组基]]。列空间求基则要用主元位置挑选 **$A$ 的原列**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 3. The identity matrix records the row combinations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*右边放单位矩阵，是为了记住每条新行来自哪里*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To find $N(A^T)$, we need the coefficients of combinations of original rows that give zero. A zero row in $R$ shows that a cancellation happened, but does not by itself retain those coefficients. Attach an identity matrix as a recorder and apply each row operation to both blocks:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求 $N(A^T)$ 时，需要知道“原来的各行按什么系数组合成零”。$R$ 中出现零行只说明发生了抵消，单独留下这个零行并没有保存组合系数。把单位矩阵接在右边作为记录器，两侧同步做每一步行操作：

$$
[\,A_{m\times n}\mid I_{m\times m}\,]
\longrightarrow
[\,R_{m\times n}\mid E_{m\times m}\,].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the elementary matrices are $E_1,\ldots,E_k$, their product $E=E_k\cdots E_1$ satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若依次施加的初等矩阵为 $E_1,\ldots,E_k$，令 $E=E_k\cdots E_1$，则

$$
E[A\mid I_m]=[EA\mid EI_m]=[R\mid E].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the same recording mechanism used in Gauss–Jordan inversion. Here $A$ need not be square or invertible. The recorder $E$ is invertible because **each elementary row operation is invertible**. Only when $A$ is square and invertible, and $R=I$, does $E=A^{-1}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与 Gauss–Jordan 求逆使用同一种记录方法，但此处的 $A$ 不必方阵，也不必可逆。$E$ 可逆，是因为**每一步初等行操作都可逆**。只有 $A$ 为可逆方阵且 $R=I$ 时，才有 $E=A^{-1}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let row $i$ of $E$ be $y_i^T$. Matrix multiplication gives $(EA)_{i:}=y_i^TA=R_{i:}$. Therefore a zero row of $R$ identifies a vector $y_i\in N(A^T)$. These vectors form a basis, because they are independent rows of the invertible matrix $E$, and their number is $m-r=\dim N(A^T)$. See [[消元追踪左零空间基|the elimination method]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $E$ 的第 $i$ 行为 $y_i^T$。矩阵乘法给出 $(EA)_{i:}=y_i^TA=R_{i:}$，所以 $R$ 的零行对应一个 $y_i\in N(A^T)$。这些向量确实构成基：它们来自可逆矩阵 $E$ 的无关行，数量又恰好等于 $m-r=\dim N(A^T)$。见[[消元追踪左零空间基]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $R$ is in echelon form, all zero rows lie at the bottom: take the bottom $m-r$ rows of $E$, then transpose them to write the basis vectors as columns. Their order is irrelevant. If $m=r$, there are no such rows and the left nullspace is $\{0\}$, whose basis is empty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$R$ 为阶梯形时，零行都在底部：取 $E$ 最下面的 $m-r$ 行，再转置成列向量即可。顺序不影响基。若 $m=r$，没有这样的行，左零空间为 $\{0\}$，其基为空集。

## 4. Four bases from the lecture example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同一个例子，核对四种求基方法*

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3&1\\1&1&2&1\\1&2&3&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
E=\begin{pmatrix}-1&2&0\\1&-1&0\\-1&0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
EA=R=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&1&1\\0&1&1&0\\0&0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One route is: subtract row 1 from rows 2 and 3, negate row 2, then subtract twice the new row 2 from row 1. Apply those same steps to $I_3$ to obtain the displayed $E$. Here $m=3$, $n=4$, and $r=2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条消元路线是：第 2、3 行各减第 1 行，第 2 行变号，然后第 1 行减去新第 2 行的两倍。对 $I_3$ 同步做这些步骤，就得到上面的 $E$。此例 $m=3,n=4,r=2$。

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal B_{C(A)}&=\{(1,1,1)^T,(2,1,2)^T\},\\
\mathcal B_{C(A^T)}&=\{(1,0,1,1)^T,(0,1,1,0)^T\},\\
\mathcal B_{N(A)}&=\{(-1,-1,1,0)^T,(-1,0,0,1)^T\},\\
\mathcal B_{N(A^T)}&=\{(-1,0,1)^T\}.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first pair uses original pivot columns; the second uses nonzero rows of $R$, written as column vectors. The third comes from $x_1=-x_3-x_4$, $x_2=-x_3$. The last comes from row 3 of $E$ and states the original row relation $-A_{1:}+A_{3:}=0$. Their dimensions are respectively $2,2,2,1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一组取原矩阵主元列；第二组取 $R$ 的非零行，并转成列向量书写；第三组来自 $x_1=-x_3-x_4$、$x_2=-x_3$；最后一组取 $E$ 第 3 行，记录原行之间的关系 $-A_{1:}+A_{3:}=0$。四个维数依次为 $2,2,2,1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A left-null vector also gives a compatibility check. If $Ax=b$, then $y^Tb=y^TAx=0$. Here $y=(-1,0,1)^T$ imposes $b_3=b_1$; the independent first two rows make this condition sufficient as well. This connects the left nullspace to the consistency conditions in [[01.9_Solving Ax = b|Session 1.9]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左零向量也给出相容性检查：若 $Ax=b$，必有 $y^Tb=y^TAx=0$。此例 $y=(-1,0,1)^T$ 要求 $b_3=b_1$；前两行无关，所以该条件也足够。这把左零空间接回[[01.9_Solving Ax = b|第九讲]]的相容条件。

## 5. Exercises 10.1 and 10.2
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*分别判断右端能否达到、已有解是否唯一*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**10.1.** Some $b\in\mathbb R^m$ make $Ax=b$ unsolvable. Therefore the column space does not fill $\mathbb R^m$, so $r<m$. Rank is also bounded by the number of columns:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**10.1。** 存在某些 $b\in\mathbb R^m$ 使 $Ax=b$ 无解，说明列空间没有覆盖整个 $\mathbb R^m$，故 $r<m$。秩同时不能超过列数：

$$
\boxed{r<m,\qquad r\le n.}
\qquad \dim N(A^T)=m-r>0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The positive dimension guarantees nonzero left-null vectors, answering part (b). No ordering between $m$ and $n$ follows. Rank-one examples with shapes $2\times3$, $2\times2$, and $2\times1$ all satisfy the hypothesis:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左零空间维数为正，保证存在非零向量，回答了 (b)。题设不能确定 $m,n$ 的大小关系；以下秩为 1 的矩阵分别为 $2\times3$、$2\times2$、$2\times1$，都满足题设：

$$
\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**10.2.** Treat $A^T$ as the coefficient matrix. Its possible outputs form $C(A^T)$, the row space of $A$, so $A^Ty=d$ is solvable exactly when $d\in C(A^T)$. Conditional on existence, the solution is unique exactly when $N(A^T)=\{0\}$, the **left nullspace of $A$**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**10.2。** 把 $A^T$ 当作系数矩阵。它所有可能的输出构成 $C(A^T)$，即 $A$ 的行空间，所以 $A^Ty=d$ 有解当且仅当 $d\in C(A^T)$。在有解的前提下，解唯一当且仅当 $N(A^T)=\{0\}$，这里是 **$A$ 的左零空间**。

$$
A^Ty_1=A^Ty_2=d
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
A^T(y_1-y_2)=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the left nullspace contains only zero, the difference vanishes. Conversely, any nonzero $z\in N(A^T)$ produces a second solution $y+z$ from one solution $y$. The identity $A^T0=0$ alone cannot prove uniqueness; it holds for every matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若左零空间只有零向量，两解之差只能为零；反过来，只要存在非零 $z\in N(A^T)$，从一个解 $y$ 就能得到另一个解 $y+z$。单独写 $A^T0=0$ 不能证明唯一性，因为任何矩阵都满足它。

## 6. Matrices can themselves be vectors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把整个矩阵当作向量空间里的一个元素*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All real $3\times3$ matrices form a [[向量空间|vector space]] under matrix addition and scalar multiplication. The elements are now matrices; the vector-space rules concern $A+B$ and $cA$, and do not require matrix multiplication. Upper triangular matrices and symmetric matrices are subspaces of this space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有 $3\times3$ 实矩阵在矩阵加法和数乘下构成一个[[向量空间]]。这里每个“向量”本身是矩阵；向量空间规则讨论的是 $A+B$ 和 $cA$，不要求矩阵乘法。上三角矩阵与对称矩阵分别构成这个空间的子空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Their [[上三角与对称矩阵的交集|intersection is the space of diagonal matrices]]: an upper triangular matrix has zeros below the diagonal, and symmetry then forces zeros above it. This is an entire subspace, not a single intersection point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它们的[[上三角与对称矩阵的交集|交集是对角矩阵空间]]：上三角要求对角线下方为零，对称性又迫使上方为零。这里的 intersection 是一整个子空间的**交集**，不是一个交点。

$$
D_1=\operatorname{diag}(1,0,0),\quad
D_2=\operatorname{diag}(1,3,0),\quad
D_3=\operatorname{diag}(0,0,7).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These lecture matrices form a basis of the diagonal subspace $D$. Every diagonal matrix satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课堂列出的这三个矩阵构成对角子空间 $D$ 的一组基，因为任意对角矩阵都满足

$$
\operatorname{diag}(a,b,c)=\left(a-\frac b3\right)D_1+\frac b3D_2+\frac c7D_3.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero combination also forces all three coefficients to zero, by inspecting the diagonal entries. Hence $\dim D=3$. This is the dimension of a **space of matrices**, not the rank of one chosen matrix in it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，若组合为零，逐个看对角元素就迫使三个系数全为零，故 $\dim D=3$。这是一个**矩阵集合构成的空间**的维数，不是其中某个矩阵的秩。
