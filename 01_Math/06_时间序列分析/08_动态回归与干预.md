# 动态回归与干预：从实时信息到长期乘数与因果边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Dynamic regression and intervention: from real-time information to long-run multipliers and causal limits*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条路径解决一个比“给 ARIMA 加几个 $x$”更完整的问题：当结果序列既受外部变量驱动，又有自己的时间记忆时，怎样把**预测时真正知道的信息、动态放在哪里、输入影响怎样传播、区间条件于什么、系数能否谈因果**连成同一个模型判断。[[回归预测与动态回归.canvas|主题 Canvas]] 给出全局关系；本章负责把关系按推理顺序展开。课堂板书、原题和作业仍保留在 [[01_Math/06_时间序列分析/05_多方程模型Multi-equation Time Series Models|多方程模型课堂记录]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This path addresses more than “add some predictors to ARIMA.” When an outcome is driven by external variables and also carries temporal memory, we must connect what was genuinely known at forecast time, where dynamics enter, how input effects propagate, what predictive intervals condition on, and whether coefficients support causal language. The Canvas supplies the global map; this chapter supplies the continuous argument. Original board work and exercises remain in the source course note.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 先问预测发生在什么时候，而不是先问模型叫什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Start with when the forecast is made, not with the model's name*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设要在时点 $T$ 预测 $y_{T+h}$。回归预测除了需要历史 $y$，还需要模型右侧在未来用到的 $x_{T+1:T+h}$。因此第一步沿用 [[预测时点与信息集]]：区分 forecast origin、target time 与 horizon，并列出在 $T$ 真正可见的数据版本。一个拟合得很漂亮的模型，如果未来 predictor 在部署时根本拿不到，就还不是可执行预测系统。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the task is to forecast $y_{T+h}$ at time $T$. A regression forecast may also need future predictor values. Begin by separating forecast origin, target time, and horizon, and list the data vintages actually available at $T$. A well-fitted model is not operational if its predictors are unavailable at deployment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[事前与事后回归预测]]随后把两个经常混用的结果分开。事前预测只用 $\mathcal F_T$，未知的未来 $x$ 必须另行预测或写成情景；事后预测可以在后来代入已经实现的 $x$，但不能把预测期的 $y$ 送回估计。事后版本可回答“如果 predictor 路径预测得完美，这个 $y\mid x$ 模型会怎样”，却不能冒充当时已经能够发布的预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ex-ante forecasts use only $\mathcal F_T$ and must forecast or posit unknown future predictors. Ex-post forecasts may later insert realised predictor paths, while still withholding future outcomes from estimation. They reveal how the conditional model would have behaved with perfect predictor information, but they are not forecasts that could have been issued at $T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这直接决定区间含义。[[预测变量路径与条件区间]]要求把输出写成“给定这条未来 $x$ 路径的 $y$ 分布”。日历变量可能真已知；天气、价格、收入通常只是预测或情景。常见软件区间传播 $y$ 的创新，却不自动传播 $x$ 走错路径的风险。要做无条件预测，就要给 $x$ 建概率模型，并把每条模拟路径传进 $y$ 模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This determines the meaning of the interval. Calendar variables may be genuinely known, whereas weather, prices, and income are usually forecasts or scenarios. Standard regression intervals propagate outcome innovations but usually not uncertainty in the predictor path. An unconditional forecast needs a probability model for $X$ and joint simulation through the $Y$ model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 动态放在哪里，决定参数在说什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Where dynamics enter determines what the parameters mean*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[动态回归模型]]是一个模型族入口：它始终围绕一个结果 $y_t$，但动态可能来自三处。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dynamic regression is a model-family entry centred on one outcome. Its dynamics may enter in three places.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. $x_t,x_{t-1},\ldots$：解释变量的作用延迟或分期到达。
2. $y_{t-1},y_{t-2},\ldots$：过去结果直接进入条件均值并继续传播影响。
3. $\eta_t$ 的 ARIMA 结构：回归没有解释的偏离具有时间依赖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Predictor lags represent delayed inputs; lagged outcomes propagate the conditional mean; and an ARIMA law for the regression error represents persistence in unexplained deviations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预测教材常把第三种特称为 dynamic regression，计量教材也常把前两种纳入。为了不让术语替代模型，本库总是继续写出精确子类。尤其是 [[ARIMA误差回归]]：
$$
y_t=\beta_0+\beta'x_t+\eta_t,
\qquad
\phi(B)(1-B)^d\eta_t=\theta(B)\varepsilon_t.
$$
这里 $\eta_t$ 是 regression error，$\varepsilon_t$ 是新到达的 innovation；应接近白噪声的是后者。它不是 OLS 完成后只替换一个 robust standard error，因为误差动态还改变 likelihood、预测递推和区间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Forecasting texts often reserve “dynamic regression” for the third case, while econometrics uses it more broadly. We therefore name the exact subtype. In regression with ARIMA errors, $\eta_t$ is the regression deviation and $\varepsilon_t$ is the new innovation; only the innovation should resemble white noise. This is not merely OLS followed by a robust standard error because the error law changes the likelihood and forecast distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 温度与电力需求
> 令 $y_t$ 为日用电量，以最高温度 $T_t$、$T_t^2$、工作日和日历项解释条件均值，再让误差服从季节 ARIMA。二次项允许冷、热两端用电较高。预测未来十四天时，工作日可由日历知道，温度却需要天气预报或情景。把温度固定为 26°C 得到的是**26°C 路径下的条件预测**。FPP3 的这个例子还留下异方差、残差相关和厚尾，提醒我们“模型能生成点预测”不等于区间已经可靠。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Model daily electricity demand with temperature, its square, working-day status, and calendar terms, while giving the residual a seasonal ARIMA law. The quadratic term captures high demand in both cold and hot weather. Working days are known from the calendar, but future temperature needs a forecast or scenario. Holding it at 26°C yields a forecast conditional on that path. The example's remaining heteroscedasticity, correlation, and heavy tails show that usable point forecasts do not guarantee reliable interval coverage.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 从分布滞后到 ADL：直接到达与继续传播
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. From distributed lags to ADL: direct arrival and further propagation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[分布滞后模型]]先只描述解释变量一侧：
$$
y_t=\alpha+\gamma_0x_t+\gamma_1x_{t-1}+\cdots+\gamma_qx_{t-q}+\eta_t.
$$
$(\gamma_0,\ldots,\gamma_q)$ 是 transfer weights。它们回答同一次输入变化在不同滞后如何直接到达 $y$；不含滞后 $y$，所以不能把这个名称和 ADL 无差别互换。选择 $q$ 时，各候选还必须使用相同有效样本，避免“模型变了”与“训练数据变了”同时发生。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A distributed-lag model places current and lagged predictors on the right-hand side. Its transfer weights describe when an input arrives directly at the outcome. It does not contain lagged outcomes and should not be used interchangeably with ADL. Candidate lag lengths must also be compared on the same effective sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[分布滞后乘数]]把“输入路径”和“结果路径”严格配对。一次性单位 pulse 在期限 $h$ 的响应是 $\gamma_h$；从时点 $t$ 起永久增加一个单位的 step，在期限 $h$ 的响应是 $\sum_{j=0}^{\min(h,q)}\gamma_j$。所以系数和只回答永久阶跃，不回答一次脉冲。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a one-period unit pulse, the horizon-$h$ response is $\gamma_h$. For a permanent unit step, it is the partial sum of transfer weights through horizon $h$. The coefficient sum therefore answers a permanent-step experiment, not a one-off pulse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[自回归分布滞后]]再加入过去的 $y$：
$$
y_t=\alpha+\sum_{i=1}^{p}\phi_i y_{t-i}
+\sum_{j=0}^{q}\gamma_jx_{t-j}+\varepsilon_t.
$$
解释变量的作用先经 $\gamma_j$ 到达，又经自回归部分继续传播。此时某个 $\gamma_j$ 更不能单独代表完整动态效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ADL adds lagged outcomes. Predictor effects first arrive through the distributed-lag weights and are then propagated by the autoregressive part, so no single contemporaneous coefficient generally represents the full dynamic effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.1. ADL(1,1) 的脉冲与阶跃推导
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1. Pulse and step derivation for ADL(1,1)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对
$$
y_t=\alpha+\rho y_{t-1}+\beta_0x_t+\beta_1x_{t-1}+u_t,
$$
先给 $x_t$ 一个只持续一期的单位脉冲。响应依次是
$$
\delta_0=\beta_0,
\qquad
\delta_1=\rho\beta_0+\beta_1,
\qquad
\delta_h=\rho^{h-1}(\rho\beta_0+\beta_1),\ h\ge2.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a one-period unit pulse in an ADL(1,1), the impact response is $\beta_0$, the next response is $\rho\beta_0+\beta_1$, and later responses decay through the autoregressive coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

永久 step 是每期再叠加一个新 pulse，因此期限响应是这些 $\delta$ 的部分和。若 $|\rho|<1$，[[ADL长期乘数]]为
$$
\sum_{h=0}^{\infty}\delta_h
=\frac{\beta_0+\beta_1}{1-\rho}.
$$
注意：$\rho\beta_0+\beta_1$ 是**原始 pulse 在下一期的响应**；阶跃在下一期还会送来一个新的 $\beta_0$，所以当时的总水平响应是 $\beta_0+\beta_1+\rho\beta_0$。把这两者都叫“下一期效应”会把两种实验混在一起。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A permanent step adds a new pulse every period, so its response is the partial sum of the pulse responses. Under $|\rho|<1$, the long-run multiplier is $(\beta_0+\beta_1)/(1-\rho)$. The term $\rho\beta_0+\beta_1$ is the original pulse's next-period response; a step also contributes a fresh $\beta_0$ at that date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 一个能手算的路径
> 取 $(\rho,\beta_0,\beta_1)=(0.5,2,1)$。pulse 响应为 $2,2,1,0.5,\ldots$，总和为 6；step 的水平响应为 $2,4,5,5.5,\ldots$，逐渐趋近 6。手算这两列，是避免把 impact、下一期 pulse response 和 long-run step multiplier 混写的最快办法。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With $(\rho,\beta_0,\beta_1)=(0.5,2,1)$, pulse responses are $2,2,1,0.5,\ldots$ and sum to 6; step responses are $2,4,5,5.5,\ldots$ and converge to 6. Computing both columns is the quickest way to separate impact, next-period pulse response, and long-run step multiplier.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 滞后 $y$ 与 ARIMA 误差不是两种写法的同义词
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Lagged outcomes and ARIMA errors are not synonymous notations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[因变量滞后与ARIMA误差]]分别承载“条件均值本身有状态”和“未解释偏离会持续”。二者有时产生接近的预测，甚至可以代数改写。若
$$
y_t=\beta x_t+\eta_t,qquad \eta_t=\rho\eta_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t,
$$
则
$$
y_t=\rho y_{t-1}+\beta x_t-\rho\beta x_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t.
$$
它是一个**受限** ADL：滞后 $x$ 的系数被锁定为 $-\rho\beta$。自由 ADL 不受这条限制。预测接近不代表每个系数拥有同一结构含义，也不代表可以把大量 $y/x$ 滞后和高阶 ARIMA error 同时堆入模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lagged outcomes encode state in the conditional mean; ARIMA errors encode persistence in unexplained deviations. An AR(1) regression error can be rewritten as a restricted ADL whose lagged-$x$ coefficient is tied to $-\rho\beta$. An unrestricted ADL need not satisfy this restriction. Similar forecasts do not imply identical parameter meanings or justify stacking every dynamic component together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若水平序列含单位根，所有这些规格还要经过 [[协整与差分边界]]。滞后项不会自动消除伪回归：没有稳定长期组合时应重新定义尺度；有协整时可转为误差修正形式，同时保留长期约束和短期调整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nonstationary levels require a separate cointegration decision. Lags do not automatically eliminate spurious regression. Without a stable long-run combination, redefine the scale; with cointegration, use an error-correction representation that preserves both long-run restriction and short-run adjustment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 干预先编码输入，再讨论响应与因果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Encode the intervention input before interpreting response or causality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[干预函数类型]]把事件后的输入路径分清：pulse 只在事件期为 1，step 从事件期起一直为 1，ramp 随事件后时间增长。一次 pulse 仍可经 AR/transfer dynamics 影响很多期；一个 step 的长期位移则是不断叠加 pulse 的结果。先说输入，再说传播，可以避免从结果图形反推一个看似最漂亮的 intervention function。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pulse equals one only at the event date, a step remains one, and a ramp grows with time since the event. A pulse may still affect many outcome periods through dynamic propagation, while a step accumulates a sequence of pulses. State the input path before the propagation mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但是，正确编码不等于正确识别。[[干预系数因果边界]]要求追问：政策是否因为预期 $y$ 即将变化才启动？同一日期是否有别的改革或测量变化？主体是否在正式日期前已经反应？若答案可能为是，干预时点与反事实误差相关。ARIMA errors 可以吸收序列相关，不能把一个内生时点变成外生时点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Correct coding is not identification. Ask whether policy timing responds to anticipated outcomes, whether other reforms or measurement changes coincide, and whether behaviour changes before the official date. ARIMA errors can absorb serial dependence but cannot turn endogenous timing into exogenous timing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此一个合格结论分两层写：预测层可以说“给定该 step 路径，模型预测结果水平怎样变化”；因果层只有在反事实和时点外生性有额外依据时，才说“干预造成怎样的变化”。显著的 step 系数和近似白噪声创新都不能替代这层依据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sound conclusion has two layers. Predictively, report how the model maps the supplied step path into the outcome. Causally, claim an intervention effect only when the counterfactual and timing assumptions have independent support. Significance and white innovations do not provide that support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 把模型做成可部署、可比较的预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Turn the model into a deployable and comparable forecast*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后沿 [[动态回归规格流程]] 收束：先固定任务与信息时间线，再决定水平、差分或 ECM 尺度；按机制提出少量 ARIMA-error、distributed-lag 或 ADL 候选；保持共同样本和 likelihood 口径；检查根、innovation residual、方差、异常点与断点；最后在 [[滚动起点评估]] 的每个 origin 内重新生成 predictor forecast 和全部预处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finish with a disciplined workflow: fix the task and information timeline; choose levels, differences, or an ECM; propose a small mechanism-based candidate set; preserve common samples and likelihood conventions; diagnose roots, innovations, variance, outliers, and breaks; then regenerate predictor forecasts and preprocessing within every rolling origin.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!check] 最小交付清单
> - 模型右侧每个未来变量在 origin 时是已知、预测还是情景？
> - 动态来自 predictor lags、lagged outcome 还是 ARIMA errors，各自为何存在？
> - pulse、step 与长期乘数对应的是哪条输入路径？
> - 区间是否传播 predictor uncertainty？
> - 系数只作预测解释，还是另有因果识别依据？
> - 所有候选是否在共同样本与真实 rolling origins 上比较？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A complete delivery names predictor availability, locates each source of dynamics, matches pulse or step inputs to the reported multiplier, states whether predictor uncertainty is propagated, separates predictive from causal interpretation, and compares candidates on common samples under genuine rolling origins.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 对课堂材料的四个主动校正
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Four active corrections to the course material*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 讲义 pp. 197–199 对从某期开始永久为 1 的 $z_t$ 计算的是 step response；单独 pulse 的期限响应是 $c_0a_1^h$。二者不能都简称“impulse response”。
2. 旧综合页把 $\rho\beta_0+\beta_1$ 写成“下一期额外效应”，容易让人误读为永久 step 的全部下一期响应；它准确对应原始 pulse 的下一期响应。
3. 课程中“干预系数显著”只说明给定规格中的统计差异；讲义 p. 213 自己也承认 no-feedback 是结构解释的难题，因此显著不能单独推出政策因果。
4. 含滞后因变量且误差自相关时，OLS 通常会因 $y_{t-1}$ 与当前误差相关而失去一致性；但“误差自相关”本身并不使所有静态、严格外生回归的 OLS 系数自动有偏。必须连同回归量与误差的正交条件一起判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Four corrections matter. The lecture's persistent intervention is a step, whereas an isolated pulse has response $c_0a_1^h$. The old composite's “next-period additional effect” is specifically the original pulse's next response, not the entire next-period step response. Statistical significance does not establish a causal intervention effect, especially when no-feedback is uncertain. Finally, serial correlation generally makes OLS inconsistent in a lagged-outcome model because the lagged outcome and current error become related; serial correlation alone does not automatically bias coefficients in every static regression with strictly exogenous regressors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
