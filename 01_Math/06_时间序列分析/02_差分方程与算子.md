## 1. 先问四个问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Begin with four questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑
$$
y_t=c+ay_{t-1}+u_t.
$$
面对这条式子，先不要急着算。依次问：

1. **方程规定了什么？** 它把当前值、上一期状态和本期输入联系起来。
2. **还缺什么？** 若从有限时点开始，要给初值；若要从无限过去构造平稳过程，要给收敛、因果和创新条件。
3. **哪一部分在传播？** 初值、常数输入和每一期 $u_t$ 都有各自的动态权重。
4. **结论是哪一种？** 固定点存在、确定性偏离衰减、随机过程平稳与样本路径收敛是不同声明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a first-order recurrence, separate four issues: the relation imposed by the equation, the auxiliary information selecting a solution, the propagation of initial state and forcing, and the exact type of stability claim being made. A fixed point, decay of a deterministic deviation, stochastic stationarity, and pathwise convergence are not interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这四问会贯穿整章。很多“根在单位圆内还是外”“有稳态为什么不收敛”“无穷级数为什么能写”的混乱，都来自把其中两问压成一句口诀。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Most confusion about roots, steady states, and infinite expansions comes from collapsing these separate questions into one slogan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 方程、阶数与强迫项
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Equation, order, and forcing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[差分方程]]是在离散索引上约束一条序列的方程。课程集中研究[[线性常系数差分方程]]：
$$
y_t=a_0+a_1y_{t-1}+\cdots+a_ny_{t-n}+x_t,\qquad a_n\ne0.
$$
$n$ 由最远的非零输出滞后决定；$x_t$ 是强迫过程，可以是时间趋势、季节项、其他变量或随机冲击。给定 $x$ 之后，方程对 $y$ 的结构仍是线性常系数的。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A difference equation constrains a sequence across discrete indices. In the course's linear constant-coefficient form, the furthest nonzero output lag determines the order, while the forcing process may be deterministic, observed, or stochastic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阶数不是文件标签，而是解的自由度。二阶递推通常需要两个相邻初值；若只给 $y_0$，许多不同的 $y_1$ 都能生成不同轨迹。反过来，输入里出现 $x_{t-12}$ 并不使一阶输出递推变成十二阶。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Order records the number of independent initial values normally needed for a forward solution. A long lag of an external input does not raise the order of the output recurrence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 差分与滞后是两种操作语言
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Differences and lags are two operator languages*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[滞后算子约定|滞后算子]] $L$ 只做时间位移：$L^ky_t=y_{t-k}$。[[差分算子]]则比较相邻时点：
$$
\Delta y_t=y_t-y_{t-1}=(1-L)y_t,\qquad
\Delta^2y_t=(1-L)^2y_t.
$$
因此 $\Delta^2y_t=y_t-2y_{t-1}+y_{t-2}$，并不等于 $y_t-y_{t-2}$。后者是 $(1-L^2)y_t$，属于[[普通与季节差分|周期为 2 的季节差分]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lag operator shifts a sequence, while the difference operator compares adjacent values. Repeated differencing and seasonal differencing are different polynomials in the same lag operator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

算子记号的价值不是把式子写短，而是让因式分解和组合可见。例如
$$
(1-L^{12})=(1-L)(1+L+\cdots+L^{11}),
$$
说明月度季节差分本身已经含有一个普通单位根因子。它不意味着每条季节序列都只做一次季节差分，而是提醒我们：继续加普通差分前，要先检查已经作用了什么滤波器。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operator notation exposes factorisation. Since $1-L^{12}$ already contains $1-L$, applying another ordinary difference is a modelling decision that needs evidence rather than an automatic next step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 一阶方程：先找中心，再看偏离
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. First order: find the centre, then study deviations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[一阶仿射递推]]
$$
y_t=ay_{t-1}+b,
$$
若 $a\ne1$，先求固定点 $y^*=b/(1-a)$，再相减：
$$
y_t-y^*=a(y_{t-1}-y^*).
$$
于是
$$
y_t=y^*+a^t(y_0-y^*).
$$
这比死背等比数列更有解释力：$y^*$ 决定中心，$a$ 决定偏离的符号与衰减速度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtracting the fixed-point equation turns an affine recurrence into a pure deviation recurrence. The fixed point supplies the centre and powers of $a$ describe the entire adjustment path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以 $y_t=0.6y_{t-1}+4$ 为例，$y^*=10$。从 $y_0=0$ 出发，路径为
$$
0,4,6.4,7.84,\ldots,\qquad y_t=10-10(0.6)^t.
$$
若把 $0.6$ 改成 $-0.6$，偏离会在稳态两侧交替，但包络仍衰减；改成 $-1.2$，固定点仍存在，偏离却交替放大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sign of the coefficient determines whether adjustment alternates; its modulus determines whether the envelope shrinks or grows. A fixed point can exist even when generic trajectories move away from it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这正是[[稳态与稳定性]]的区别。稳态回答“不动点在哪里”，稳定性回答“扰动后会怎样”。$a=1$ 时应直接求解为 $y_t=y_0+bt$；若再有创新，就进入[[带漂移随机游走]]，而不是把 $b/(1-a)$ 解释成一个无限大的稳态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $a=1$, the process accumulates the constant and, with innovations, becomes a random walk with drift. A zero denominator marks a unit-root structure rather than an infinite equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 迭代把每一种来源拆开
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Iteration separates every source of the path*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对
$$
y_t=c+ay_{t-1}+u_t,\qquad t\ge1,
$$
[[递推迭代]]给出
$$
y_t=a^ty_0+c\sum_{j=0}^{t-1}a^j+\sum_{j=0}^{t-1}a^ju_{t-j}.
$$
检查索引只要看三个点：当前 $u_t$ 权重是 1，上一期 $u_{t-1}$ 权重是 $a$，初值 $y_0$ 权重是 $a^t$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated substitution decomposes the solution into the propagated initial state, accumulated constant forcing, and the weighted history of time-varying inputs. Current forcing has weight one, previous forcing weight $a$, and the initial state weight $a^t$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限初值公式对任何 $a$ 都能逐期计算。只有把起点推到无限过去并删除初值项时，才要求 $|a|<1$ 及相应的收敛条件。若 $u_t=\varepsilon_t$，便得到
$$
y_t=\frac{c}{1-a}+\sum_{j=0}^{\infty}a^j\varepsilon_{t-j},
$$
这是[[AR(p)模型|AR(1)]] 的因果稳定解，也是[[ARMA无限MA表示]]的最小例子。形式上写出一个无穷和不等于证明它收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The finite initial-value solution remains computable even when it is unstable. Removing the initial state and extending the solution into the infinite past requires a convergent causal filter, which is a separate claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 通解为何是“一个特解 + 全部齐次解”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Why the full solution is particular plus homogeneous*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从[[线性常系数差分方程]]中去掉常数与所有输入，得到[[齐次差分方程]]。它回答：没有新强迫时，系统自身和初始状态还能产生哪些模式？若 $y^p$ 是原非齐次方程的一条特解，线性性给出[[差分方程通解]]：
$$
y_t=y_t^p+y_t^h.
$$
任意两条特解之差一定齐次；反过来，把任何齐次解加到一条特解上仍满足同一强迫。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The homogeneous equation isolates internal modes. Linearity then makes the complete nonhomogeneous solution an affine family: one particular solution plus the entire homogeneous solution space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求解流程因此不是“先找一个看起来对的式子”就停：

1. 构造齐次方程并找齐独立模式；
2. 找任意一个特解；
3. 相加得到含自由常数的通解；
4. 用足够的初值确定这些常数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The practical workflow is to find the full homogeneous basis, obtain one particular solution, add them, and then use enough initial conditions to determine the free constants.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“特解”不自动等于“稳定解”。稳定性可以在无穷多条解里选出一条从过去输入生成的解，但那是额外选择条件；特解的定义只是“确实满足给定强迫”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A particular solution is not automatically a stable solution. Stability and causality may select one member of the solution family, but they are additional requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 特征方程把递推变成模式问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. The characteristic equation turns recursion into a mode problem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $n$ 阶齐次方程尝试 $y_t=r^t$，[[差分特征方程]]为
$$
r^n-a_1r^{n-1}-\cdots-a_n=0.
$$
根不是抽象装饰：每个 $r$ 都表示一种每期乘以 $r$ 的内部模式。所有模式严格落在单位圆内，才保证任意初值偏离最终消失；这与[[离散系统谱稳定性]]是同一个结论的标量与矩阵版本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trying exponential modes produces the characteristic polynomial. Its roots are the scalar counterparts of companion-matrix eigenvalues, and asymptotic stability requires every mode to decay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程例子
$$
y_t=0.9y_{t-1}-0.2y_{t-2}+3,\qquad y_0=13, y_1=11.3
$$
的齐次特征方程是
$$
r^2-0.9r+0.2=(r-0.5)(r-0.4)=0.
$$
常数特解为 10，所以通解是
$$
y_t=A_1(0.5)^t+A_2(0.4)^t+10.
$$
代入两个初值得 $A_1=1,A_2=2$，最终
$$
y_t=(0.5)^t+2(0.4)^t+10.
$$
两个模式都衰减，才使路径趋向 10。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the course example, roots 0.5 and 0.4 generate the two homogeneous modes, a constant particular solution gives the centre 10, and the two initial values select their coefficients. Convergence follows from the roots, not merely from finding the constant solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8. 二阶根的三种形态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. The three second-order root forms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[二阶差分根形态]]把所有实系数二阶方程分成三类：

- 不同实根 $r_1,r_2$：$C_1r_1^t+C_2r_2^t$；
- 二重根 $r$：$(C_1+C_2t)r^t$；
- 共轭根 $\rho e^{\pm i\theta}$：$\rho^t[C_1\cos(t\theta)+C_2\sin(t\theta)]$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinct roots give independent exponential modes; a repeated root needs the generalized mode $tr^t$; a complex pair combines into a real sinusoid whose modulus controls the envelope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重根时不能把同一个 $r^t$ 写两遍，因为那没有增加独立方向。复根时则看模 $\rho$，不是只看实部：$\rho<1$ 的振荡会衰减，$\rho=1$ 持续，$\rho>1$ 放大。即使重根带来 $t$，严格的指数衰减仍会最终压过固定次数多项式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a repeated root, duplication does not create another independent solution. For complex roots the modulus, rather than the real part alone, determines asymptotic decay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 9. 为什么有时说根在内，有时说根在外
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9. Why roots are sometimes said to lie inside and sometimes outside*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一递推还有滞后多项式
$$
A(L)=1-a_1L-\cdots-a_nL^n.
$$
[[递推根与滞后零点]]满足
$$
P(r)=r^nA(1/r).
$$
所以动态根 $r$ 与滞后多项式零点 $z$ 互为倒数：动态根稳定时在单位圆内，lag-polynomial 零点则在单位圆外。$y_t=0.8y_{t-1}$ 的两个数字分别是 $0.8$ 与 $1.25$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The dynamic polynomial and lag polynomial use reciprocal variables. Dynamic roots must be inside the unit circle, while zeros of the stated lag polynomial must be outside. Software may plot inverse roots, so the object must always be named.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[AR因果根条件]]沿用“滞后多项式零点在外”的说法；软件若画 inverse roots，点又会显示在圆内。单位圆上的根不会因换变量而消失，且只有在[[单位根最小表示|约掉不可识别的公共因子]]后，才能把它解释成可观测过程的单位根。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unit-circle roots remain on the boundary under reciprocation. Common AR and MA factors must be removed before treating a displayed root as a genuine unit root of the observable process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 10. 特解：先认输入，再检查重叠
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*10. Particular solutions: identify the input, then check overlap*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[未定系数法]]利用时间平移不会把简单函数族打散这一点。常数输入试常数，多项式输入试同次数的完整多项式，$q_d(t)\lambda^t$ 试多项式乘同一指数，正弦输入要同时保留同频率的正弦和余弦。代回方程后比较同类项，得到一个特解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Undetermined coefficients chooses a trial family closed under shifts, substitutes it, and matches coefficients. Constants, polynomials, exponential-polynomial terms, and sine-cosine pairs each have their natural trial spaces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

唯一不能机械照抄的是重叠。对
$$
y_t-y_{t-1}=1,
$$
常数强迫对应 $\lambda=1$，它也正是齐次根；试 $C$ 只会得到 $0=1$。[[共振特解升阶]]要求乘上足够次数的 $t$：试 $Ct$ 得 $C=1$，所以通解是 $y_t=t+A$。若该根重数为 2，就要乘 $t^2$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the forcing mode already belongs to the homogeneous solution, the ordinary trial is annihilated. Multiplication by the appropriate power of time produces an independent generalized mode; the power equals the root multiplicity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条规则解释单位根为何把常数输入累积成线性趋势。它不是“分母为零后的修补”，而是识别到输入与系统固有模式发生重合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The time factor is a structural response to resonance, not an algebraic patch for a zero denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 11. 滞后多项式求逆不是普通除法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*11. Lag-polynomial inversion is not ordinary division*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若
$$
A(L)y_t=x_t,
$$
写 $y_t=A(L)^{-1}x_t$ 还只是提出目标。[[滞后多项式求逆]]要求先固定过去方向并验证收敛。例如
$$
(1-aL)^{-1}=1+aL+a^2L^2+\cdots,\qquad |a|<1,
$$
所以
$$
y_t=x_t+ax_{t-1}+a^2x_{t-2}+\cdots.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inverse lag polynomial is meaningful only on a stated class of sequences and in a stated time direction. Zeros outside the unit circle permit the usual causal one-sided expansion in current and past inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若过去方向的系数不衰减，形式分式仍可能写在纸上，却不是稳定因果滤波器。某些根配置可以用未来输入构造非因果解，但这不适合实时预测。另一方面，给定有限初值后，即使系统不稳定，递推仍能在有限期逐步算出；不能把“没有稳定无限表示”说成“方程无解”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A divergent causal series does not invalidate the finite initial-value recurrence. It means only that the desired stable infinite-past representation is unavailable; a future-dependent noncausal representation is a different object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 12. 蛛网模型：一个符号同时承担经济机制与稳定性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*12. The cobweb model: economic timing becomes a stability coefficient*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[蛛网模型]]设
$$
d_t=a-\gamma p_t,\qquad
s_t=b+\beta p_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t,\qquad
d_t=s_t.
$$
生产者按上一期价格决定本期供给，本期价格再使市场出清。联立得到
$$
p_t=\frac{a-b}{\gamma}-\frac{\beta}{\gamma}p_{t-1}-\frac1\gamma\varepsilon_t,
\qquad
p^*=\frac{a-b}{\gamma+\beta}.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cobweb model turns delayed supply response and current market clearing into a first-order price recurrence. The negative coefficient captures overshooting around the no-shock equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无持续冲击时，
$$
p_t-p^*=-\frac\beta\gamma(p_{t-1}-p^*).
$$
因此[[蛛网收敛条件]]是 $\beta<\gamma$：负号使价格在均衡两侧交替，$\beta/\gamma$ 决定振幅是否缩小。取 $a=100,b=20,\gamma=4,\beta=2$，则 $p^*\approx13.33$；从 $p_0=8$ 出发，价格依次为 $16,12,14,13,13.5,\ldots$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without continuing shocks, deviations alternate with multiplier $-\beta/\gamma$. The path converges exactly when the supply response is smaller than the absolute demand slope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在加回持续的 $\varepsilon_t$。当 $\beta<\gamma$ 时，初值与**每一次既定冲击**的作用会衰减，冲击权重为
$$
-\frac1\gamma\left(-\frac\beta\gamma\right)^h.
$$
但新冲击每期都来，所以样本路径通常不会停在 $p^*$；正确结论是价格可以围绕 $p^*$ 形成稳定因果过程。这一处必须把“确定性路径收敛”与“随机过程平稳”分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With ongoing shocks, the initial condition and each individual shock decay under the same root condition, but new shocks keep arriving. The process may be stationary around the equilibrium without any sample path converging to that constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 13. 把本章接回时间序列主线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*13. Rejoin the time-series backbone*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

差分方程提供动态代数，后续主题分别加上统计含义：

1. [[AR(p)模型]]把随机强迫解释为创新，[[AR因果根条件]]决定能否由当前和过去创新生成；
2. [[ARMA无限MA表示]]把逆多项式系数解释成冲击响应，并补上均方收敛与有限方差条件；
3. [[单位根最小表示]]说明根在单位圆上时为何要差分、又为何必须先约掉公共因子；
4. [[ADL长期乘数]]把永久输入看作一串脉冲，并在稳定条件下汇总全部动态效应；
5. [[普通与季节差分]]则把不同单位圆频率对应到不同滤波器。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Later time-series topics add statistical meaning to the same algebra: AR models identify innovations, causal roots justify one-sided generation, infinite-MA coefficients become impulse responses, unit roots motivate carefully chosen differences, and stable dynamic regression turns a permanent input into the sum of pulse effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际解题时，按下面顺序足够稳：先写清未知序列、输入和初值；再决定是有限迭代还是通解；通解中先解齐次根，再按强迫找特解并检查共振；最后才判断稳定性、平稳性或长期乘数。每一步都注明自己求的是动态根、lag-polynomial 零点，还是软件显示的 inverse roots。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A reliable workflow specifies the sequence, forcing, and auxiliary conditions; chooses finite iteration or a general solution; solves homogeneous modes and a nonoverlapping particular solution; and only then makes a precisely named claim about stability, stationarity, or long-run response.

> [!question]- 章节自检
> 对 $y_t=0.9y_{t-1}-0.2y_{t-2}+3$，请解释：为什么常数 10 不是完整答案？为什么根 $0.5,0.4$ 在圆内，而 lag-polynomial 的零点却在圆外？
>
> **答案：** 10 只是一个特解；还要加两个齐次模式，并用两个初值定系数。动态根与 lag-polynomial 零点互为倒数，所以 $0.5,0.4$ 分别对应 2 与 2.5；两种单位圆说法描述同一稳定性。
