### 2. 平稳时间序列、相关结构与 ARMA
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Stationary Time Series, Dependence Structure, and ARMA*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

差分方程和滞后算子给出动态模型的代数；ARMA 用随机冲击把它变成可估计的平稳序列模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Difference equations and lag operators supply the algebra of dynamic models. ARMA adds random shocks to turn that algebra into an estimable model for a stationary series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[平稳时间序列、相关结构与 ARMA]]

### 3. 条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Conditional Heteroskedasticity: ARCH and GARCH*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ARMA 主要解释条件均值和线性相关；当残差方向难预测但幅度成群时，要单独建模条件方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ARMA primarily explains the conditional mean and linear dependence. When the direction of residuals is hard to predict but their magnitudes cluster, the conditional variance needs its own model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH]]

### 4. 动态回归、VAR、脉冲响应与 Granger 因果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Dynamic Regression, VAR, Impulse Responses, and Granger Causality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单变量模型忽略变量间反馈；动态回归和 VAR 把多条序列放入同一系统并分析冲击传播。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Univariate models ignore feedback among variables. Dynamic regression and VAR place several series in one system and trace how shocks propagate through it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[动态回归、VAR、脉冲响应与 Granger 因果]]

### 5. 趋势、单位根与差分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Trends, Unit Roots, and Differencing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些模型先假定稳定依赖；趋势和单位根解释为何水平序列可能不平稳及何时需要差分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding models assume stable dependence. Trends and unit roots explain why a series in levels may be non-stationary and when differencing is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[趋势、单位根与差分]]

### 6. 协整与误差修正模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Cointegration and Error-Correction Models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多个非平稳序列各自漂移却可能受长期关系约束；协整和 ECM 同时表示长期均衡与短期修正。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Several non-stationary series may drift individually while remaining constrained by a long-run relationship. Cointegration and an ECM represent long-run equilibrium together with short-run adjustment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[协整与误差修正模型]]
