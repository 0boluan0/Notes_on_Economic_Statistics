### 0. 概率与渐近工具
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*0. Probability and Asymptotic Tools*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这门课的导论先复习依概率收敛、依分布收敛、弱大数定律、经典中心极限定理、Slutsky 定理与连续映射定理。它们分别回答估计量是否靠近目标、标准化误差趋向什么分布，以及能否把一致估计量或连续变换代入已有极限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The introductory review covers convergence in probability, convergence in distribution, the weak law of large numbers, the classical central limit theorem, Slutsky's theorem, and the continuous mapping theorem. Together they distinguish consistency from distributional approximation and justify continuous transformations and plug-in estimators.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!map] 关系图
> [[收敛与极限定理.canvas|打开收敛与极限定理 Canvas]]：先比较四种收敛概念及其单向蕴含，再进入大数定律和极限运算。依概率、依分布、弱大数定律、中心极限定理、Slutsky 定理与连续映射定理是导论讲义的核心复习项；几乎必然收敛、均方收敛和强大数定律作为共享延伸，帮助看清这条关系链的边界。

四种概念先分别回答“样本路径”、“二次平均误差”、“偏离目标的概率”和“分布形状”如何趋稳。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four concepts first answer how sample paths, mean squared error, the probability of departing from a target, and distributional shape can each stabilise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The implications are one-way: pathwise or mean-square control first yields convergence in probability, and convergence in probability then yields convergence in distribution; reverse directions require additional conditions.

The laws of large numbers apply these convergence modes to sample means: the weak law gives convergence in probability, the bounded-variance version supplies one verifiable set of sufficient conditions, and the strong law gives almost-sure convergence.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the basic convergence results are in place, the central limit theorem describes the limiting law of a standardised error, while the continuous mapping and Slutsky theorems show which transformations and plug-in operations preserve the required limit.

先把“方程”与“算子”分开。方程规定哪些序列是解，阶数由最远的未知输出滞后决定；差分算子只是作用在序列上的一种变换，不能因为都含“差分”二字就混为一个对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
