## 2.1. [[协整|协整的定义]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Definition of cointegration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 协整的定义
>Engle和Granger（1987）的协整概念可以用一个**均衡误差模型**来说明。例如，假设有$n$维向量时间序列$x_t = (x_{1t}, x_{2t}, \dots, x_{nt})’$，其中每个分量都是$I(1)$的非平稳过程。那么如果存在一个$n\times 1$的常数向量$β = (β_1, β_2, \dots, β_n)’$，使得线性组合
> $$
> e_t = β’ x_t
> $$
> 得到的$e_t$是平稳序列，那么我们就称$x_t$的各分量是协整的。这里$e_t$可以理解为各变量之间长期均衡关系的**误差项**或**残差**，即变量组合偏离长期关系的程度。
>
> 更正式地，按照Engle和Granger（1987）的定义，如果$x_t$的各个分量存在协整关系，我们称$x_t \sim \text{CI}(d, b)$（即$x_t$是$d$阶单整、$b$阶协整），需满足以下两个条件：
>
> 1. $x_t$向量中的所有变量都是$d$阶单整的（记作$I(d)$）。
> 2. 存在一个非零向量$β$，使得$β’x_t$集成阶数为$(d-b)$（通常$d-b=0$表示平稳），其中$b>0$。此时向量$β$被称为**协整向量**。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Engle and Granger's (1987) concept of cointegration can be explained with an **equilibrium-error model**. Suppose, for example, that $x_t = (x_{1t}, x_{2t}, \dots, x_{nt})’$ is an $n$-dimensional vector time series and that every component is a nonstationary $I(1)$ process. If there is an $n\times 1$ constant vector $β = (β_1, β_2, \dots, β_n)’$ such that the linear combination
> $$
> e_t = β’ x_t
> $$
> is stationary, the components of $x_t$ are said to be cointegrated. Here $e_t$ is the **equilibrium error**, or residual, in the long-run relationship: it measures how far the variables have moved away from that relationship.
>
> More formally, following Engle and Granger (1987), if the components of $x_t$ are cointegrated, we write $x_t \sim \text{CI}(d, b)$—that is, $x_t$ is integrated of order $d$ and cointegrated of order $b$—when both conditions below hold:
>
> **1.** Every variable in the vector $x_t$ is integrated of order $d$, written $I(d)$.<br>
> **2.** There is a nonzero vector $β$ such that $β’x_t$ is integrated of order $(d-b)$, where $b>0$. Usually $d-b=0$, so the combination is stationary. The vector $β$ is then called a **cointegrating vector**.<br>
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在经济数据中最常见的是CI(1, 1)的情形，也就是各变量为$I(1)$，但存在一个线性组合是$I(0)$平稳的（即$b=1$）。我们通常关心的协整关系大多属于这种CI(1,1)类型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The most common case in economic data is CI(1,1): each variable is $I(1)$, but some linear combination is stationary, or $I(0)$, so $b=1$. Most cointegrating relationships of practical interest have this form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个定义只给出统计长期约束；稳定组合的经济身份、调整变量和因果方向都要另行论证，见[[协整解释边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This definition supplies only a statistical long-run restriction. The economic identity of the stationary combination, the adjusting variable, and causal direction all require separate arguments; see [[协整解释边界|the interpretation boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般而言，对于$n$个$I(1)$变量，最多可能存在$r = n-1$个独立的协整关系（即协整向量），对应$n-r$个独立的共同趋势（下面将介绍）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, a system of $n$ $I(1)$ variables can have at most $r=n-1$ independent cointegrating relationships, represented by independent cointegrating vectors. It then has $n-r$ independent common trends, discussed below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2. 协整与共同随机趋势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2. Cointegration and common stochastic trends*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


协整变量之间存在**共同的随机趋势**（common stochastic trend）。按照Stock和Watson（1988）的观点，如果多个变量存在协整关系，那么它们必然共享一个或多个共同的随机趋势成分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cointegrated variables share one or more **common stochastic trends**. In the Stock and Watson (1988) formulation, cointegration among several variables necessarily implies that at least one stochastic-trend component is common to them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，两个 $I(1)$ 变量若有一条协整关系，在标准共同趋势表示下只剩一个共同随机趋势 $s_t$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if two $I(1)$ variables have one cointegrating relation, their standard common-trend representation contains one shared stochastic trend $s_t$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_t = c_y s_t + e^y_t
$$
$$
z_t = c_z s_t + e^z_t,
$$

其中 $c_y,c_z$ 是两个变量对共同趋势的载荷，$e^y_t,e^z_t$ 是平稳部分。若 $(β_1,β_2)'$ 是协整向量，非平稳部分必须满足
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $c_y,c_z$ are the loadings on the common trend, while $e^y_t,e^z_t$ are stationary components. If $(β_1,β_2)'$ is a cointegrating vector, the nonstationary component must satisfy
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
β_1 c_y+β_2 c_z=0,
$$

因而 $β_1y_t+β_2z_t$ 中的 $s_t$ 正好抵消，只留下平稳组合。这表明两个变量的非平稳部分由同一趋势方向驱动，不是说任意拆出的两个“随机趋势”必须逐期相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
so the $s_t$ term cancels from $β_1y_t+β_2z_t$, leaving a stationary combination. The claim is that the nonstationary parts are driven by one common direction, not that two arbitrarily chosen trend components must be numerically equal period by period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==协整向量使两个变量对共同趋势的载荷在线性组合中相互抵消。==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==The cointegrating vector makes the two variables' loadings on the common trend cancel in the linear combination.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3. 协整与误差修正模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3. Cointegration and error-correction models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!quote] 长期关系与短期关系
>
> | **关系类型** | **含义**                         |
> | -------- | ------------------------------ |
> | **短期关系** | 当前变化依赖近期因素，比如滞后项、冲击            |
> | **长期关系** | 某个水平线性组合保持平稳；要称为经济比例或均衡，还需理论与识别 |
> 类比：橡皮筋模型（最直观）
> - 两个变量是“随机游走者” ⇒ 各自乱晃
> - 某个水平组合像“橡皮筋长度”一样保持平稳 ⇒ **统计长期关系**
> - 在相容而稳定的 ECM 中，至少一个非零调整载荷会响应偏离；这一步来自动态表示，不是协整定义单独给出的结论
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> | **Type of relationship** | **Meaning** |
> | --- | --- |
> | **Short run** | Current changes depend on recent factors, such as lagged values and shocks. |
> | **Long run** | A linear combination of levels is stationary; calling it an economic ratio or equilibrium requires theory and identification. |
> An intuitive analogy: the elastic-band model
> - The two variables are “random walkers,” each moving unpredictably.
> - The length of an “elastic band” represents a stationary **long-run statistical combination**.
> - In a compatible stable ECM, at least one nonzero adjustment loading responds to disequilibrium; that dynamic claim is not supplied by the definition alone.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Granger 表示定理的正则条件下，协整系统具有相容的误差修正表示：上一期长期偏离通过至少一个非零调整载荷进入本期变化。哪些变量调整、方向与速度如何，要从具体 **[[ECM长短期结构|误差修正模型]]**（Error Correction Model, ECM）读取，不能只凭协整定义断言。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the regularity conditions of the Granger representation theorem, a cointegrated system has a compatible error-correction representation: lagged disequilibrium enters current changes through at least one nonzero adjustment loading. Which variables adjust, and in what direction and at what speed, must be read from the specified [[ECM长短期结构|error-correction model (ECM)]], not inferred from the definition alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] 长期利率与短期利率的例子
>- $r_{Lt}$：长期利率
> - $r_{St}$：短期利率
> 经济理论认为：长期利率和短期利率应有**长期稳定关系**
>
> 模型表达：
> ECM 形式如下：
>
> $\Delta r_{St} = \alpha_S (r_{Lt-1} - \beta r_{St-1}) + \varepsilon_{St},\quad \alpha_S > 0$
> $\Delta r_{Lt} = -\alpha_L (r_{Lt-1} - \beta r_{St-1}) + \varepsilon_{Lt},\quad \alpha_L > 0$
> 这里：
> - $r_{Lt-1} - \beta r_{St-1}$：上一期**长期偏离**
> - $\alpha_S, \alpha_L$：**误差修正速度**
>
> 令 $e_t=r_{Lt}-\beta r_{St}$。由上面两条方程直接相减可得
>
> $$
> e_t=(1-\alpha_L-\beta\alpha_S)e_{t-1}+\varepsilon_{Lt}-\beta\varepsilon_{St}.
> $$
>
> 因而，在这里声明的有限方差创新与因果动态条件下，长期偏离平稳还要求
>
> $$
> \left|1-\alpha_L-\beta\alpha_S\right|<1.
> $$
>
> 若长期关系为正向，$\beta>0$，这等价于 $0<\alpha_L+\beta\alpha_S<2$。例如 $\beta=1$、$\alpha_S=0.2$、$\alpha_L=0.3$ 时，偏离根是 $0.5$，误差会逐期衰减；仍取 $\beta=1$ 而让两个载荷都为 $1.2$ 时，偏离根是 $-1.4$，系统会交替过冲并发散。可见载荷符号告诉我们“朝哪边调”，[[误差修正方向判读|偏离根与稳定条件]]才告诉我们“能不能拉回来”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> - $r_{Lt}$: the long-term interest rate
> - $r_{St}$: the short-term interest rate
> Economic theory suggests that the two rates should have a **stable long-run relationship**.
>
> Model specification:
> An ECM can be written as
>
> $\Delta r_{St} = \alpha_S (r_{Lt-1} - \beta r_{St-1}) + \varepsilon_{St},\quad \alpha_S > 0$
> $\Delta r_{Lt} = -\alpha_L (r_{Lt-1} - \beta r_{St-1}) + \varepsilon_{Lt},\quad \alpha_L > 0$
> where
> - $r_{Lt-1}-\beta r_{St-1}$ is the previous period's **long-run disequilibrium**;
> - $\alpha_S$ and $\alpha_L$ are the **speeds of error correction**.
>
> Let $e_t=r_{Lt}-\beta r_{St}$. Subtracting the two equations gives
>
> $$
> e_t=(1-\alpha_L-\beta\alpha_S)e_{t-1}+\varepsilon_{Lt}-\beta\varepsilon_{St}.
> $$
>
> Under the stated finite-variance innovation and causal-dynamics conditions, the disequilibrium is stationary only if
>
> $$
> \left|1-\alpha_L-\beta\alpha_S\right|<1.
> $$
>
> For a positive long-run relation, $\beta>0$, this becomes $0<\alpha_L+\beta\alpha_S<2$. If $\beta=1$, $\alpha_S=0.2$, and $\alpha_L=0.3$, the disequilibrium root is $0.5$ and the error decays. Keeping $\beta=1$ but setting both loadings to $1.2$ gives a root of $-1.4$, so the system alternates while diverging. The loading signs tell us the direction of adjustment; the [[误差修正方向判读|disequilibrium root and stability condition]] tell us whether the system actually returns.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Engle-Granger 的**表示定理**（[[Granger表示定理|Granger Representation Theorem]]）指出：如果一组$I(1)$的非平稳变量存在$r>0$个协整关系（即$x_t$~CI(1,1)情形的推广），那么一定存在相应的误差修正模型来描述它们的动态变化，反之亦然——协整关系与误差修正形式是等价的两种刻画同一现象的方式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **Granger Representation Theorem** states that if a set of nonstationary $I(1)$ variables has $r>0$ cointegrating relationships—a multivariate extension of $x_t\sim\text{CI}(1,1)$—then a corresponding error-correction model exists to describe their dynamics, and conversely. Cointegration and the error-correction form are thus equivalent ways to characterize the same phenomenon.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设$x_t$是一个包含$n$个$I(1)$变量的向量，考虑其 [[VAR(p)模型|VAR($p$)]]（$p$阶向量自回归）模型：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $x_t$ is a vector containing $n$ $I(1)$ variables, and consider its [[VAR(p)模型|VAR($p$)]] model, a vector autoregression of order $p$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
x_t = A_1 x_{t-1} + A_2 x_{t-2} + \cdots + A_p x_{t-p} + \varepsilon_t,
$$

其中$A_i$为$n\times n$系数矩阵，$\varepsilon_t$为$n\times1$扰动项向量（平稳）。我们可以将上述VAR模型改写为**误差修正模型**（VECM）形式，通过对模型两边取一阶差分并整理得到：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each $A_i$ is an $n\times n$ coefficient matrix, and $\varepsilon_t$ is an $n\times1$ vector of stationary disturbances. Taking first differences and rearranging converts the VAR into a **vector error-correction model (VECM)**:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta x_t = π_0 + πx_{t-1} + \sum_{i=1}^{p-1} π_i \Delta x_{t-i} + \varepsilon_t
$$

其中 $\pi_0$ 是 $n\times1$ 常数向量，$\pi$ 是由 $A_i$ 组合得到的 $n\times n$ 矩阵，$\pi_i$ 也是 $n\times n$ 系数矩阵。若 $x_t$ 是 $I(1)$，则各 $\Delta x_{t-i}$ 为 $I(0)$；要让 VECM 两侧同为平稳过程，$\pi x_{t-1}$ 也必须平稳。因此 $\pi$ 的每个非零行向量都给出一个平稳线性组合，但这些行未必彼此独立。标准分解 $\pi=\alpha\beta^T$ 表明：$\pi$ 的行空间就是由 $\beta^T$ 张成的协整空间，而 $\operatorname{rank}(\pi)=r$ 才是独立协整关系的个数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\pi_0$ is an $n\times1$ vector of constants, $\pi$ is an $n\times n$ matrix assembled from the $A_i$, and each $\pi_i$ is an $n\times n$ coefficient matrix. If $x_t$ is $I(1)$, the lagged differences are $I(0)$, so $\pi x_{t-1}$ must also be stationary for both sides of the VECM to be stationary. Every nonzero row of $\pi$ therefore gives a stationary linear combination, but the rows need not be independent. The standard factorization $\pi=\alpha\beta^T$ shows that the row space of $\pi$ is the cointegration space spanned by $\beta^T$, while $\operatorname{rank}(\pi)=r$ counts the independent cointegrating relationships.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**Granger 表示定理**告诉我们，协整关系保证了误差修正模型的存在。在上面的误差修正形式中，如果变量是协整的（例如CI(1,1)情形），则矩阵$π$必然是秩$r$的（$0 < r \le n-1$），从而存在非零的协整向量使$π x_{t-1}$项起作用；这等价于说这些$I(1)$变量间存在能够纠正偏离的长期均衡关系。如果变量不存在协整关系（比如全部独立随机游走），那么$π$矩阵将是秩0的零矩阵，模型退化为纯差分形式，无法刻画任何长期约束关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Granger Representation Theorem tells us that cointegration guarantees an error-correction representation. If the variables in the model above are cointegrated, as in a CI(1,1) system, $π$ has rank $r$, with $0<r\le n-1$, so nonzero cointegrating vectors make the $πx_{t-1}$ term operative. Equivalently, the $I(1)$ variables obey long-run equilibrium relationships that correct departures from equilibrium. Without cointegration—for example, if all variables are independent random walks—$π$ has rank zero, the model reduces to a pure differenced form, and no long-run restriction is represented.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在$n$元系统中，如果$π$矩阵的秩为$r$（其中$0<r<n$），则存在$r$个独立的协整关系（协整向量），同时意味着该系统中存在$n-r$个独立的共同随机趋势推动变量的长期变化。这一点正是我们在协整检验中所关注的核心。下面将介绍具体的协整检验方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an $n$-variable system, if $π$ has rank $r$, where $0<r<n$, the system has $r$ independent cointegrating relationships and $n-r$ independent common stochastic trends driving its long-run movements. Determining this rank is the central task of cointegration testing, to which we now turn.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==压缩成一句：在标准 $I(1)$ VECM 条件下看 $\pi$ 的约化秩；$0<\operatorname{rank}(\pi)<n$ 才是非平凡协整情形，不要改成数 $\pi$ 的普通特征值。==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==In one sentence: under the standard $I(1)$ VECM conditions, inspect the reduced rank of $\pi$. The nontrivial cointegration case has $0<\operatorname{rank}(\pi)<n$; do not replace this with a count of the ordinary eigenvalues of $\pi$.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在有 $r$ 条协整关系的 VECM 中，某变量对应的 $\alpha$ 整行若为零，表示它的方程不响应任何协整偏离；在标准高斯 VECM 及针对整个 $\beta$ 空间的推断中，这是该变量对这些长期参数[[弱外生性与调整载荷|弱外生]]的限制。只看某一个载荷的不显著性不够，也不能把这个结论扩张成“对所有参数都外生”。判断 **[[弱外生与Granger非因果|Granger 非因果]]**则要联合检查相关滞后差分系数与长期水平系数块 $\alpha_y\beta_x'$ 是否为零；弱外生与 Granger 非因果不是同一限制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a VECM with $r$ cointegrating relations, a zero row of $\alpha$ means that the corresponding variable's equation responds to none of the cointegrating deviations. In the standard Gaussian VECM, for inference on the full $\beta$ space, this is the restriction for [[弱外生性与调整载荷|weak exogeneity]] with respect to those long-run parameters. Insignificance of one loading is not enough, and the conclusion must not be extended to every model parameter. Testing whether $x$ fails to [[弱外生与Granger非因果|Granger-cause]] $y$ instead requires joint restrictions on the relevant lagged differences and on the long-run level-effect block $\alpha_y\beta_x'$. Weak exogeneity and Granger noncausality are different restrictions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. [[Engle-Granger两步法|EG两步法]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1. [[Engle-Granger两步法|Engle–Granger two-step method]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] **EG两步检验**（Engle-Granger Two-Step）。
>
> 1. **单整阶数预检**：首先对每个变量进行单位根检验（如ADF检验或PP检验），以确定各变量的积分阶数。经典 Engle–Granger 同阶协整定义要求相关变量同阶单整；若混有 $I(0)$、$I(1)$ 或 $I(2)$，不能继续机械套用标准两步法，应先重新说明变量角色或改用相应框架。
> 2. **估计长期关系并检验残差平稳**：如果变量都是$I(1)$，在第二步中，对假定存在的长期均衡关系进行回归估计。通常选定其中一个变量作为被解释变量$y_t$，其余$I(1)$变量作为解释变量$x_{it}$，估计回归方程：
>     $$
>     y_t = β_0 + β_1 x_{1t} + β_2 x_{2t} + \cdots + β_n x_{nt} + e_t,
>     $$
>
>     得到残差序列${\hat e_t}$。然后对残差$\hat e_t$进行残差型单位根检验（例如使用相应 Engle–Granger 临界值的 ADF 检验）。操作上的零假设是残差存在单位根，即**无协整关系**；拒绝该零假设则支持残差为$I(0)$，因而支持变量间存在协整关系。
>
> 3. **建立误差修正模型**：如果第二步确认存在协整关系（残差$I(0)$），则可以在第三步建立包含误差修正项的动态模型。例如，对第2步中的回归方程构建对应的误差修正模型，一般形式如：
>
>     $$
>
>     \Delta y_t = \gamma_0 + \gamma_1 \Delta x_{1t} + \cdots + \gamma_n \Delta x_{nt} + \phi \hat e_{t-1} + u_t
>
>     $$
>
>     其中$\hat e_{t-1}$为滞后一期的长期关系残差（即误差修正项），$\phi$为调整系数（期望$\phi<0$表示$y_t$受到偏离均衡向下调整的拉回力度），$u_t$为噪音项。通过这个模型可以同时刻画短期波动（差分项部分）和长期均衡偏离的校正（误差修正项部分）。
> 4. **模型检验**：最后，检查残差自相关、条件异方差、异常值、参数稳定性和替代滞后规格，并联合检验长期关系与调整载荷。正态性是否必要取决于所用推断，不能把“残差必须独立同分布”当成一条无条件验收线。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> **1.** **Pretest the order of integration.** First run unit-root tests, such as the ADF or PP test, on every variable. The classical Engle–Granger definition requires the relevant variables to be integrated of the same order. If the set mixes $I(0)$, $I(1)$, or $I(2)$ variables, do not mechanically continue with the standard two-step procedure; first redefine their roles or use the appropriate framework.<br>
> **2.** **Estimate the long-run relationship and test whether its residual is stationary.** If the variables are all $I(1)$, choose one as the dependent variable $y_t$ and regress it on the other $I(1)$ variables $x_{it}$:<br>
> $$
> y_t = β_0 + β_1 x_{1t} + β_2 x_{2t} + \cdots + β_n x_{nt} + e_t.
> $$
>
> Obtain the residual series $\hat e_t$ and apply a residual-based unit-root test, such as an ADF test with the appropriate Engle–Granger critical values. The operational null is that the residual has a unit root, which corresponds to **no cointegration**. Rejecting that null indicates that $\hat e_t$ is $I(0)$ and therefore supports a cointegrating relationship.
>
> **3.** **Build an error-correction model.** If the second step finds an $I(0)$ residual, construct a dynamic model containing the lagged equilibrium error. For the regression above, a typical specification is<br>
>
> $$
>
> \Delta y_t = \gamma_0 + \gamma_1 \Delta x_{1t} + \cdots + \gamma_n \Delta x_{nt} + \phi \hat e_{t-1} + u_t
>
> $$
>
> where $\hat e_{t-1}$ is the previous period's long-run residual, or error-correction term; $\phi$ is the adjustment coefficient, conventionally expected to be negative when positive disequilibrium is corrected; and $u_t$ is noise. The differenced terms describe short-run fluctuations, while the error-correction term describes adjustment toward long-run equilibrium.
> **4.** **Diagnose the model.** Check residual autocorrelation, conditional heteroskedasticity, outliers, parameter stability, and alternative lag specifications, and jointly test the long-run relation and adjustment loadings. Whether normality is required depends on the inferential method; i.i.d. residuals are not a universal acceptance rule.<br>
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条 ECM 只是最小结构示意。同期 $\Delta x_t$ 若作为单方程解释变量，需要相应的条件外生性或结构识别；更一般的动态规格会让若干滞后差分进入方程。具体包含哪些短期项应由理论、频率和残差诊断决定，不能从协整关系本身机械推出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This ECM is only a minimal structural illustration. Treating a contemporaneous $\Delta x_t$ as a single-equation regressor requires an appropriate conditional-exogeneity or structural-identification argument; a more general dynamic specification includes lagged differences. Theory, data frequency, and residual diagnostics determine the short-run terms rather than the cointegrating relation alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“两步检验”指长期回归与残差单位根检验；后续 ECM 属于建模与诊断。Engle–Granger 原文所称“两步估计量”是先估计协整向量、再估计 ECM；两种数法不应混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the “two-step test” means the long-run regression followed by the residual unit-root test; the ECM is subsequent modelling and diagnosis. Engle and Granger's original “two-step estimator” instead counts estimation of the cointegrating vector and then estimation of the ECM. The two labels should not be conflated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==若 $x_1$ 与 $x_2$ 共享一个随机趋势，而 $y$ 另有独立随机趋势，完整向量仍可因 $(0,1,-\beta)'$ 而协整；但把 $y$ 的系数归一化为 1 的单方程 EG 回归无法表示这条关系。这正是[[EG多变量局限|单方程 EG 在多关系系统中的局限]]。==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==If $x_1$ and $x_2$ share one stochastic trend while $y$ has an independent trend, the full vector can still be cointegrated through $(0,1,-\beta)'$. A single-equation Engle–Granger regression normalized on $y$, however, cannot represent that relation. This is the [[EG多变量局限|multivariate limitation of single-equation EG]].==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.1.1. 残差型检验的两种处理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1.1. Two treatments of residual dependence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一阶段长期斜率在经典条件下可以[[协整回归超一致性|超一致]]，但这不使普通平稳回归的 $t/F$ 推断自动有效。第二阶段还必须使用[[EG残差检验|估计残差专用的临界值]]：EG-ADF 在残差回归中加入滞后差分来参数化短期相关；[[Phillips-Ouliaris检验|Phillips–Ouliaris]] 则用非参数长期协方差修正残差型统计量。两者都以无协整为原假设，也都受确定性项、变量数、归一化、带宽或滞后选择及结构突变影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first-stage long-run slope can be [[协整回归超一致性|superconsistent]] under classical conditions, but ordinary stationary-regression $t/F$ inference does not thereby become valid. The second stage still needs [[EG残差检验|critical values designed for estimated residuals]]. EG-ADF parameterizes short-run dependence with lagged residual differences, whereas the [[Phillips-Ouliaris检验|Phillips–Ouliaris tests]] apply nonparametric long-run-covariance corrections. Both have no cointegration as the null, and both depend on deterministic terms, system dimension, normalization, lag or bandwidth choices, and structural stability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. [[Johansen检验|Johansen 系统协整检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2. Johansen system cointegration test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Johansen检验|Johansen]] (1988) 针对至多为 $I(1)$ 的 [[VAR到VECM重参数化|VAR/VECM]] 系统，在单位圆上除所建模的 $1$ 根外不再有季节或其他根、也无爆炸根且创新非奇异等标准条件下，把 $\operatorname{rank}(\pi)$ 解释为协整秩；其高斯最大似然推导还假定创新项独立同分布且服从正态分布。秩检验统计量来自两个残差块的样本乘积矩阵所构造的[[Johansen广义特征值|广义特征值／典型相关问题]]，而不是来自 $\pi$ 的普通特征值。该方法避免了 Engle–Granger 的两步估计，可在同一系统中联合估计并检验多个协整向量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Johansen检验|Johansen]] (1988) method treats $\operatorname{rank}(\pi)$ as the cointegration rank in a [[VAR到VECM重参数化|VAR/VECM]] whose variables are at most $I(1)$, provided that the unit circle contains no seasonal or other roots beyond the modelled roots at $1$, there are no explosive roots, and innovations are nonsingular, among the usual regularity conditions. The Gaussian maximum-likelihood derivation additionally assumes i.i.d. normal innovations. Its rank statistics come from a [[Johansen广义特征值|generalized-eigenvalue/canonical-correlation problem]] built from sample product moments of two residual blocks, not from the ordinary eigenvalues of $\pi$. Unlike the Engle–Granger two-step procedure, it estimates and tests multiple cointegrating vectors jointly within one system.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!source] 本节核验依据
> - [[lecture.pdf#page=291|课程 lecture pp. 291–297]] 与 [[lecture-dual.pdf#page=581|lecture-dual 对应页]]：核对 VECM 秩分类、迹统计量和最大特征根统计量。
> - [Johansen (1988), *Statistical Analysis of Cointegration Vectors*](https://doi.org/10.1016/0165-1889%2888%2990041-3)：核对 $I(1)$ Gaussian VAR 的最大似然语境、残差块典型相关／广义特征问题与秩检验。

考虑一个包含 n 个变量的一阶 VAR 系统：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a first-order VAR system containing $n$ variables:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$\mathbf{x}_t = A\,\mathbf{x}_{t-1} + \epsilon_t.$$
则可改写为差分形式：
$$\Delta \mathbf{x}_t = (A - I)\,\mathbf{x}_{t-1} + \epsilon_t \equiv \pi\ \mathbf{x}_{t-1} + \epsilon_t.$$

此处矩阵 $\pi$ 就等于 $A-I$。在上述至多 $I(1)$、只允许已建模的 $1$ 根且无爆炸根的设定中，若 $0<r=\operatorname{rank}(\pi)<n$，就可写成 $\pi=\alpha\beta^T$：$\beta$ 的 $r$ 个独立列张成协整空间，公共随机趋势的个数为 $n-r$。完整的[[VECM的Π秩|三种秩情形]]以这些前提为边界；它们不是任意矩阵都自带的解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\pi=A-I$. Under the maintained at-most-$I(1)$ conditions, with only the modelled roots at $1$ on the unit circle and no explosive roots, if $0<r=\operatorname{rank}(\pi)<n$, then $\pi=\alpha\beta^T$: the $r$ independent columns of $\beta$ span the cointegration space, and there are $n-r$ common stochastic trends. The complete [[VECM的Π秩|three-case rank classification]] is bounded by these assumptions; it is not a context-free interpretation of an arbitrary matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 在这些维持条件下，当 $\operatorname{rank}(\pi)=0$ 时不存在协整关系，并有 $n$ 个共同随机趋势。
- 当 $\operatorname{rank}(\pi)=n$ 时，$\pi$ 非奇异；在无其他单位圆根且无爆炸根的维持条件下，这对应水平变量为 $I(0)$，不再是 $I(1)$ 协整情形。仅凭“$\pi$ 满秩”本身，不能排除一个季节单位根或爆炸系统，也不能推出 $A$ 满秩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Under these maintained conditions, $\operatorname{rank}(\pi)=0$ means no cointegrating relations and $n$ common stochastic trends.
- If $\operatorname{rank}(\pi)=n$, then $\pi$ is nonsingular; with no other unit-circle roots and no explosive roots, this corresponds to level stationarity and is no longer an $I(1)$ cointegration case. Full rank of $\pi$ alone rules out neither a seasonal unit root nor explosiveness, and it does not imply that $A$ itself is full rank.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Johansen 方法使用误差修正形式 $\Delta x_t=\pi x_{t-1}+\sum_{i=1}^{p-1}\pi_i\Delta x_{t-i}+\varepsilon_t$。在上述 $I(1)$ 与根条件下，$r=\operatorname{rank}(\pi)$ 是协整秩，$n-r$ 是共同随机趋势数；但最大似然秩检验不是先无约束估计 $\hat\pi$ 再数它的普通特征值，而是比较不同约束秩下的集中似然。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Johansen's method uses the error-correction form $\Delta x_t=\pi x_{t-1}+\sum_{i=1}^{p-1}\pi_i\Delta x_{t-i}+\varepsilon_t$. Under the maintained $I(1)$ and root conditions, $r=\operatorname{rank}(\pi)$ is the cointegration rank and $n-r$ is the number of common stochastic trends. The maximum-likelihood rank test does not estimate an unrestricted $\hat\pi$ and count its ordinary eigenvalues; it compares concentrated likelihoods under alternative rank restrictions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体而言，对于一个$n$维变量组成的VAR系统：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More specifically, for an $n$-dimensional VAR system,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

x_t = A_1 x_{t-1} + \cdots + A_p x_{t-p} + \varepsilon_t,

$$

可以整理为误差修正形式：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
the model can be rearranged into the error-correction form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->



$$

\Delta x_t = π x_{t-1} + \sum_{i=1}^{p-1} π_i \Delta x_{t-i} + \varepsilon_t,

$$



其中 $\pi=-(I-A_1-\cdots-A_p)$。这里要分清两层：对任意矩阵，$\operatorname{rank}(\pi)$ 由[[奇异值与秩|非零奇异值的个数]]刻画，不等于一般意义上“$\pi$ 的非零[[特征对|普通特征值]]个数”。Johansen 程序先把 $\Delta x_t$ 与 $x_{t-1}$ 分别对滞后差分和确定性项回归，记两组残差为 $R_{0t}$ 与 $R_{1t}$，再构造样本乘积矩阵

$$
S_{ij}=T^{-1}\sum_{t=1}^{T}R_{it}R_{jt}^T,\qquad i,j\in\{0,1\}.
$$

广义特征值由

$$
\det\!\left(\lambda S_{11}-S_{10}S_{00}^{-1}S_{01}\right)=0
$$

给出（假定相关乘积矩阵可逆）。因此程序使用的是残差乘积矩阵构成的广义特征问题，而不是 $\hat\pi$ 的普通特征值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $\pi=-(I-A_1-\cdots-A_p)$. Two layers must be kept separate. For a general matrix, $\operatorname{rank}(\pi)$ is the [[奇异值与秩|number of nonzero singular values]], not in general the number of nonzero [[特征对|ordinary eigenvalues]] of $\pi$. Johansen's procedure first residualizes $\Delta x_t$ and $x_{t-1}$ against lagged differences and deterministic terms, producing residual blocks $R_{0t}$ and $R_{1t}$, and then forms the sample product-moment matrices

$$
S_{ij}=T^{-1}\sum_{t=1}^{T}R_{it}R_{jt}^T,\qquad i,j\in\{0,1\}.
$$

The generalized eigenvalues solve

$$
\det\!\left(\lambda S_{11}-S_{10}S_{00}^{-1}S_{01}\right)=0,
$$

assuming the required product-moment matrices are nonsingular. Thus the procedure uses a residual product-moment generalized eigenproblem, not the ordinary eigenvalues of $\hat\pi$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些广义特征值通常记作 $\hat{\lambda}_1\ge\cdots\ge\hat{\lambda}_n$；在相关乘积矩阵正定的标准样本问题中，它们位于 $[0,1]$，是两组残差化变量之间的平方样本典型相关。它们不是 $\pi$ 的普通特征值。Johansen 检验也不是简单地问“$\hat\lambda_i$ 是否小于 1”；迹检验和最大特征根检验在逐步的秩假设下，用这些根构造似然比统计量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These generalized eigenvalues are usually written $\hat{\lambda}_1\ge\cdots\ge\hat{\lambda}_n$. In the standard sample problem with the relevant product-moment matrices positive definite, they lie in $[0,1]$ and equal squared sample canonical correlations between the two residualized variable blocks. They are not ordinary eigenvalues of $\pi$. Nor does Johansen testing simply ask whether $\hat\lambda_i<1$: under sequential rank hypotheses, the trace and maximum-eigenvalue procedures use these roots to form likelihood-ratio statistics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->



[[Johansen秩检验|Johansen 检验给出两个相关统计量]]，用于检验协整秩 $r$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Again, the Johansen procedure uses two related statistics to test the cointegration rank $r$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **迹统计量（Trace test）**：检验零假设$H_0: \text{rank}(π) \le r$（最多有$r$个协整关系）对立假设$H_1: \text{rank}(π) > r$的检验统计量定义为：

    $$
    \lambda_{\text{trace}}(r) = -T \sum_{i=r+1}^{n} \ln(1 - \hat{\lambda}_i)
    $$

    其中 $\hat{\lambda}_i$ 是按降序排列的样本广义特征值，$T$ 为有效样本数。迹统计量使用从 $r+1$ 到 $n$ 的根；这些根越大，统计量越大，再用 Johansen 的相应临界值决定是否拒绝 $H_0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Trace statistic.** This tests $H_0:\text{rank}(π)\le r$ against $H_1:\text{rank}(π)>r$:

    $$
    \lambda_{\text{trace}}(r) = -T \sum_{i=r+1}^{n} \ln(1 - \hat{\lambda}_i).
    $$

    The $\hat{\lambda}_i$ are ordered sample generalized eigenvalues and $T$ is the effective sample size. The trace statistic uses the roots from $r+1$ through $n$; larger roots increase the statistic, which is compared with the appropriate Johansen critical value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **最大特征值统计量（Max-eigenvalue test）**：检验零假设$H_0: \text{rank}(π) = r$对立假设$H_1: \text{rank}(π) = r+1$的统计量定义为：
    $$
  \lambda_{\text{max}}(r, r+1) = -T \ln(1 - \hat{\lambda}_{r+1})
    $$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Maximum-eigenvalue statistic.** This tests $H_0:\text{rank}(π)=r$ against $H_1:\text{rank}(π)=r+1$:
    $$
  \lambda_{\text{max}}(r, r+1) = -T \ln(1 - \hat{\lambda}_{r+1}).
    $$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一版本只用到排序第 $r+1$ 大的广义特征值 $\hat{\lambda}_{r+1}$；它通过 $-T\log(1-\hat\lambda_{r+1})$ 形成统计量，再与 Johansen 的非标准临界值比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This version uses only the $(r+1)$th-largest generalized eigenvalue. It forms $-T\log(1-\hat\lambda_{r+1})$ and compares that statistic with Johansen's nonstandard critical values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->




在实际应用中，通常从 $H_0:r=0$（无协整）开始，逐步增加 $r$，直到首次无法拒绝相应零假设。Johansen 检验必须使用与确定性项设定相匹配的专用临界值；非标准的是单位根边界下似然比统计量的渐近分布，不是说创新残差本身必须服从某种“非标准分布”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In applications, testing usually begins with $H_0:r=0$, meaning no cointegration. Increase $r$ sequentially until the first null that cannot be rejected; this determines the estimated number of cointegrating relationships. Johansen-specific critical values must match the deterministic specification because the likelihood-ratio statistics have nonstandard asymptotic distributions at the unit-root boundary. “Nonstandard” describes that asymptotic distribution, not the distribution of the innovation residuals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->



**注释：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Notes:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Trace 检验的原假设是协整秩 $\le r$，Max-eigen 检验比较秩 $r$ 与 $r+1$。前者同时使用从 $r+1$ 起的多个广义特征值，后者只使用下一个；哪一个在具体样本中更有检验力取决于备择方向与数据生成过程，不能只因备择更窄就一概断言 Max-eigen 更强。

- 在判定协整关系个数时，Trace 统计量和 Max-eigen 统计量可能给出不同结果。此时应检查滞后阶数、确定性项、残差诊断与稳健性，并报告两种结果；不能仅凭“备择更尖锐”机械地优先采用 Max-eigen。

- Johansen 方法能在系统中同时检验多个协整关系，并对各变量对称处理，不需先指定单一因变量；但结果对样本量、VAR 滞后阶数及常数/趋势等确定性项敏感。应用前应确认变量至多为 $I(1)$、排除未建模的 $I(2)$ 情形，并明确任何 $I(0)$ 变量的角色；随后用诊断与信息准则选择滞后阶数和确定性项设定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The trace test has a null of at most $r$ cointegrating vectors, whereas the maximum-eigenvalue test compares rank $r$ with rank $r+1$. The trace statistic uses several generalized eigenvalues and the maximum-eigenvalue statistic uses the next one. Relative power depends on the alternative and the data-generating process, so a narrower alternative does not justify a universal preference.

- The two statistics can select different ranks. When they disagree, inspect the lag order, deterministic terms, residual diagnostics, and robustness, and report both results rather than mechanically preferring the maximum-eigenvalue statistic merely because its alternative is narrower.

- Johansen's method can test several cointegrating relationships simultaneously and treats all variables symmetrically, so no dependent variable must be chosen in advance. Its reliability, however, can be sensitive to sample size, VAR lag order, and the treatment of constants and trends. Before applying it, verify that the variables are at most $I(1)$, rule out an unmodelled $I(2)$ case, state the role of any $I(0)$ variables, and select lags and deterministic terms using diagnostics and information criteria.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.3. 从秩结论到可用模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3. From a rank result to a usable model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选出一个秩还不是分析终点。先用[[Johansen规格选择|滞后与确定性项的相邻合理规格]]检查秩是否稳定，再声明[[协整向量归一化|归一化]]；多关系系统还要记住[[αβ分解非唯一性|$\alpha\beta'$ 的基变换不唯一]]。随后分别读取[[VECM的α与β|$\beta$ 的长期空间和 $\alpha$ 的调整载荷]]，按[[误差修正方向判读|协整项的符号约定]]解释方向，而不是把某个负系数直接称作“回归均衡”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A selected rank is not the endpoint. Check whether it survives adjacent reasonable [[Johansen规格选择|lag and deterministic specifications]], then state the [[协整向量归一化|normalization]]. In a multiple-relation system, remember that the [[αβ分解非唯一性|basis of the $\alpha\beta'$ factorization is not unique]]. Next read the [[VECM的α与β|long-run space in $\beta$ and adjustment loadings in $\alpha$]] separately, and interpret direction using the [[误差修正方向判读|sign convention of the cointegrating term]] rather than labeling any negative coefficient as “return to equilibrium.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后检查[[弱外生性与调整载荷|弱外生限制]]与[[弱外生与Granger非因果|Granger 非因果限制]]是否被混淆，并用[[标准协整流程边界|$I(2)$、断点与小样本边界]]以及残差、系统根和样本外表现验收。[[协整分析流程]]把这些判断收束为可重复执行的顺序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, keep [[弱外生性与调整载荷|weak-exogeneity restrictions]] separate from [[弱外生与Granger非因果|Granger-noncausality restrictions]], and validate the model against [[标准协整流程边界|$I(2)$, breaks, and small-sample limits]], residual diagnostics, system roots, and out-of-sample performance.
