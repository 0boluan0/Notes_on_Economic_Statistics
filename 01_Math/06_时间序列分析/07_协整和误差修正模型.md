## 2.1. 协整的定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Definition of cointegration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 协整的定义
>Engle和Granger（1987）的协整概念可以用一个**均衡误差模型**来说明。例如，假设有$n$维向量时间序列$x_t = (x_{1t}, x_{2t}, \dots, x_{nt})’$，其中每个分量都是$I(1)$的非平稳过程。那么如果存在一个$n\times 1$的常数向量$β = (β_1, β_2, \dots, β_n)’$，使得线性组合
> $$
> e_t = β’ x_t
> $$
> 得到的$e_t$是平稳序列，那么我们就称$x_t$的各分量是协整的。这里$e_t$可以理解为各变量之间长期均衡关系的**误差项**或**残差**，即变量组合偏离长期关系的程度。
>
> 更正式地，按照Engle和Granger（1987）的定义，如果$x_t$的各个分量存在协整关系，我们称$x_t \sim \text{CI}(d, b)$（即$x_t$是$d$阶单整、$b$阶协整），需满足以下两个条件：
>
> 1. $x_t$向量中的所有变量都是$d$阶单整的（记作$I(d)$）。
> 2. 存在一个非零向量$β$，使得$β’x_t$集成阶数为$(d-b)$（通常$d-b=0$表示平稳），其中$b>0$。此时向量$β$被称为**协整向量**。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Engle and Granger's (1987) concept of cointegration can be explained with an **equilibrium-error model**. Suppose, for example, that $x_t = (x_{1t}, x_{2t}, \dots, x_{nt})’$ is an $n$-dimensional vector time series and that every component is a nonstationary $I(1)$ process. If there is an $n\times 1$ constant vector $β = (β_1, β_2, \dots, β_n)’$ such that the linear combination
> $$
> e_t = β’ x_t
> $$
> is stationary, the components of $x_t$ are said to be cointegrated. Here $e_t$ is the **equilibrium error**, or residual, in the long-run relationship: it measures how far the variables have moved away from that relationship.
>
> More formally, following Engle and Granger (1987), if the components of $x_t$ are cointegrated, we write $x_t \sim \text{CI}(d, b)$—that is, $x_t$ is integrated of order $d$ and cointegrated of order $b$—when both conditions below hold:
>
> 1. Every variable in the vector $x_t$ is integrated of order $d$, written $I(d)$.
> 2. There is a nonzero vector $β$ such that $β’x_t$ is integrated of order $(d-b)$, where $b>0$. Usually $d-b=0$, so the combination is stationary. The vector $β$ is then called a **cointegrating vector**.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在经济数据中最常见的是CI(1, 1)的情形，也就是各变量为$I(1)$，但存在一个线性组合是$I(0)$平稳的（即$b=1$）。我们通常关心的协整关系大多属于这种CI(1,1)类型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The most common case in economic data is CI(1,1): each variable is $I(1)$, but some linear combination is stationary, or $I(0)$, so $b=1$. Most cointegrating relationships of practical interest have this form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般而言，对于$n$个$I(1)$变量，最多可能存在$r = n-1$个独立的协整关系（即协整向量），对应$n-r$个独立的共同趋势（下面将介绍）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, a system of $n$ $I(1)$ variables can have at most $r=n-1$ independent cointegrating relationships, represented by independent cointegrating vectors. It then has $n-r$ independent common trends, discussed below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2. 协整与共同随机趋势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2. Cointegration and common stochastic trends*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


协整变量之间存在**共同的随机趋势**（common stochastic trend）。按照Stock和Watson（1988）的观点，如果多个变量存在协整关系，那么它们必然共享一个或多个共同的随机趋势成分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cointegrated variables share one or more **common stochastic trends**. In the Stock and Watson (1988) formulation, cointegration among several variables necessarily implies that at least one stochastic-trend component is common to them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，对于两个$I(1)$的协整变量$y_t$和$z_t$，我们可以将它们分别表示为随机趋势和平稳部分之和：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, two cointegrated $I(1)$ variables, $y_t$ and $z_t$, can each be decomposed into a stochastic trend and a stationary component:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_t = \mu^y_t + e^y_t
$$
$$
z_t = \mu^z_t + e^z_t
$$

其中$\mu^y_t$和$\mu^z_t$代表变量$y$和$z$各自的随机趋势成分（可以想象为各自含有的随机游走部分），$e^y_t$和$e^z_t$是各自的平稳部分。如果$y_t$和$z_t$是协整的（例如CI(1,1)），则存在系数$(β_1, β_2)$使得$β_1 y_t + β_2 z_t$是平稳的。这意味着长期趋势被抵消，即
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mu^y_t$ and $\mu^z_t$ are the stochastic-trend components of $y$ and $z$—they can be thought of as the random-walk parts—while $e^y_t$ and $e^z_t$ are stationary components. If $y_t$ and $z_t$ are cointegrated, for example CI(1,1), coefficients $(β_1,β_2)$ exist such that $β_1y_t+β_2z_t$ is stationary. The long-run trends must therefore cancel:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

β_1 \mu^y_t + β_2 \mu^z_t = 0,

$$

由此可以推出两个随机趋势成分之间满足$\mu^y_t = -\frac{β_2}{β_1} \mu^z_t$。换言之，$y_t$和$z_t$这两个$I(1)$过程实际上共享同一个随机趋势（在尺度上的一个常数倍）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It follows that $\mu^y_t=-\frac{β_2}{β_1}\mu^z_t$. In other words, the two $I(1)$ processes $y_t$ and $z_t$ are driven by the same stochastic trend, up to a constant scaling factor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==这意味着两个随机趋势之间的非平稳部分可以相互抵消==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==The nonstationary components of the two stochastic trends can therefore cancel each other.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3. 协整与误差修正模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3. Cointegration and error-correction models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!quote] 长期关系与短期关系
>
> | **关系类型** | **含义**                         |
> | -------- | ------------------------------ |
> | **短期关系** | 当前变化依赖近期因素，比如滞后项、冲击            |
> | **长期关系** | 序列之间存在某种**稳定的经济比例或等式**，即使短期有波动 |
> 类比：橡皮筋模型（最直观）
> - 两个变量是“随机游走者” ⇒ 各自乱晃
> - 但它们之间有一根“橡皮筋”拴着 ⇒ **长期关系**
> - 拉开可以，但最终会被拉回来 ⇒ 这就是协整
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> | **Type of relationship** | **Meaning** |
> | --- | --- |
> | **Short run** | Current changes depend on recent factors, such as lagged values and shocks. |
> | **Long run** | The series obey a **stable economic ratio or equation**, even though they fluctuate in the short run. |
> An intuitive analogy: the elastic-band model
> - The two variables are “random walkers,” each moving unpredictably.
> - An “elastic band” ties them together, representing the **long-run relationship**.
> - They can move apart temporarily, but the band eventually pulls them back together: this is cointegration.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

协整关系暗示着一个重要的动态调整原理：当变量偏离它们的长期均衡关系时，这种**误差**（偏离程度）将会影响变量的短期变动，使其朝着恢复均衡的方向调整。这种机制体现在所谓的**[[Error Correction Model|误差修正模型]]**（Error Correction Model, ECM）中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cointegration implies an important principle of dynamic adjustment. When variables depart from their long-run equilibrium relationship, the resulting **error**, or disequilibrium, affects their short-run movements and pushes them back toward equilibrium. This mechanism is represented by an [[Error Correction Model|error-correction model (ECM)]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] 长期利率与短期利率的例子
>- $r_{Lt}$：长期利率
> - $r_{St}$：短期利率
> 经济理论认为：长期利率和短期利率应有**长期稳定关系**
>
> 模型表达：
> ECM 形式如下：
>
> $\Delta r_{St} = \alpha_S (r_{Lt-1} - \beta r_{St-1}) + \varepsilon_{St},\quad \alpha_S > 0$
> $\Delta r_{Lt} = -\alpha_L (r_{Lt-1} - \beta r_{St-1}) + \varepsilon_{Lt},\quad \alpha_L > 0$
> 这里：
> - $r_{Lt-1} - \beta r_{St-1}$：上一期**长期偏离**
> - $\alpha_S, \alpha_L$：**误差修正速度**
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> - $r_{Lt}$: the long-term interest rate
> - $r_{St}$: the short-term interest rate
> Economic theory suggests that the two rates should have a **stable long-run relationship**.
>
> Model specification:
> An ECM can be written as
>
> $\Delta r_{St} = \alpha_S (r_{Lt-1} - \beta r_{St-1}) + \varepsilon_{St},\quad \alpha_S > 0$
> $\Delta r_{Lt} = -\alpha_L (r_{Lt-1} - \beta r_{St-1}) + \varepsilon_{Lt},\quad \alpha_L > 0$
> where
> - $r_{Lt-1}-\beta r_{St-1}$ is the previous period's **long-run disequilibrium**;
> - $\alpha_S$ and $\alpha_L$ are the **speeds of error correction**.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Engle-Granger 的**表示定理**（[[协整与误差修正模型#误差修正表示|Granger Representation Theorem]]）指出：如果一组$I(1)$的非平稳变量存在$r>0$个协整关系（即$x_t$~CI(1,1)情形的推广），那么一定存在相应的误差修正模型来描述它们的动态变化，反之亦然——协整关系与误差修正形式是等价的两种刻画同一现象的方式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **Granger Representation Theorem** states that if a set of nonstationary $I(1)$ variables has $r>0$ cointegrating relationships—a multivariate extension of $x_t\sim\text{CI}(1,1)$—then a corresponding error-correction model exists to describe their dynamics, and conversely. Cointegration and the error-correction form are thus equivalent ways to characterize the same phenomenon.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设$x_t$是一个包含$n$个$I(1)$变量的向量，考虑其$[[VAR Model|VAR]](p)$（$p$阶自回归向量）模型：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $x_t$ is a vector containing $n$ $I(1)$ variables, and consider its [[VAR Model|VAR($p$)]] model, a vector autoregression of order $p$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
x_t = A_1 x_{t-1} + A_2 x_{t-2} + \cdots + A_p x_{t-p} + \varepsilon_t,
$$

其中$A_i$为$n\times n$系数矩阵，$\varepsilon_t$为$n\times1$扰动项向量（平稳）。我们可以将上述VAR模型改写为**误差修正模型**（VECM）形式，通过对模型两边取一阶差分并整理得到：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each $A_i$ is an $n\times n$ coefficient matrix, and $\varepsilon_t$ is an $n\times1$ vector of stationary disturbances. Taking first differences and rearranging converts the VAR into a **vector error-correction model (VECM)**:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta x_t = π_0 + πx_{t-1} + \sum_{i=1}^{p-1} π_i \Delta x_{t-i} + \varepsilon_t
$$

其中$π_0$是$n\times1$的常数项向量，$π$是$n\times n$矩阵（由$A_i$矩阵组合得到，其具体形式为$π = -(I - A_1 - \cdots - A_p)$），$π_i$为$n\times n$的系数矩阵。该形式中，所有$\Delta x_{t-i}$都是$I(0)$平稳的，因为$x_t$是$I(1)$。因此右侧除$πx_{t-1}$一项外其余都为平稳序列，要使等式成立（左侧$\Delta x_t$是平稳的），必然要求$πx_{t-1}$也是平稳的。这就意味着矩阵$π$的每一行都必须提供一个使$π x_{t-1}$平稳的线性组合——==换言之，每一行对应的向量就是$x_t$的一个协整向量。矩阵$π$的秩（rank）即表示独立协整关系的个数$r$。==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $π_0$ is an $n\times1$ vector of constants, $π$ is an $n\times n$ matrix assembled from the $A_i$ matrices—specifically, $π=-(I-A_1-\cdots-A_p)$—and each $π_i$ is an $n\times n$ coefficient matrix. Because $x_t$ is $I(1)$, every $\Delta x_{t-i}$ is stationary, or $I(0)$. All terms on the right-hand side except $πx_{t-1}$ are therefore stationary. Since the left-hand side $\Delta x_t$ is also stationary, $πx_{t-1}$ must be stationary as well. Each relevant row of $π$ consequently defines a stationary linear combination of $x_{t-1}$: ==the associated row vectors span the cointegrating space, and the rank of $π$ equals the number $r$ of independent cointegrating relationships.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Granger表示定理**告诉我们，协整关系保证了误差修正模型的存在。在上面的误差修正形式中，如果变量是协整的（例如CI(1,1)情形），则矩阵$π$必然是秩$r$的（$0 < r \le n-1$），从而存在非零的协整向量使$π x_{t-1}$项起作用；这等价于说这些$I(1)$变量间存在能够纠正偏离的长期均衡关系。如果变量不存在协整关系（比如全部独立随机游走），那么$π$矩阵将是秩0的零矩阵，模型退化为纯差分形式，无法刻画任何长期约束关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Granger Representation Theorem tells us that cointegration guarantees an error-correction representation. If the variables in the model above are cointegrated, as in a CI(1,1) system, $π$ has rank $r$, with $0<r\le n-1$, so nonzero cointegrating vectors make the $πx_{t-1}$ term operative. Equivalently, the $I(1)$ variables obey long-run equilibrium relationships that correct departures from equilibrium. Without cointegration—for example, if all variables are independent random walks—$π$ has rank zero, the model reduces to a pure differenced form, and no long-run restriction is represented.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在$n$元系统中，如果$π$矩阵的秩为$r$（其中$0<r<n$），则存在$r$个独立的协整关系（协整向量），同时意味着该系统中存在$n-r$个独立的共同随机趋势推动变量的长期变化。这一点正是我们在协整检验中所关注的核心。下面将介绍具体的协整检验方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an $n$-variable system, if $π$ has rank $r$, where $0<r<n$, the system has $r$ independent cointegrating relationships and $n-r$ independent common stochastic trends driving its long-run movements. Determining this rank is the central task of cointegration testing, to which we now turn.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==叽里咕噜说了一堆,总之就是看$\pi$.看特征根和秩,秩不能满也不能为0==.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==After all that: inspect $\pi$, especially its eigenvalues and rank. For a cointegrated system of $I(1)$ variables, the rank must be neither zero nor full.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

协整关系要求 $a_{12}$ 和 $a_{21}$ 这两个系数不能同时为 0，如果$a_{12} = a_{21} = 0$，系统退化为两个完全独立的自回归过程.不过其中一个系数为 0 是可能的。如果 $a_{12} = 0$，则 $y_t$ 只对自身的冲击作出反应，我们称 $y_t$ 在模型中是**弱外生**的。在协整系统中，需要重新理解 **[[Granger Causality Test|Granger]] 因果关系**：如果在 $\Delta z_t$ 的方程中没有滞后的 $\Delta y_t$ 项，并且 $z_t$ 也不对长期偏离作出反应，那么我们认为序列 $\{y_t\}$ 对 $\{z_t\}$ **没有格兰杰因果作用**。当 $a_{12} = 0$ 时，$\{y_t\}$ 是弱外生的，并且不受 $\{z_t\}$ 的格兰杰因果影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cointegration requires that $a_{12}$ and $a_{21}$ not both equal zero; if $a_{12}=a_{21}=0$, the system collapses into two completely independent autoregressive processes. One coefficient may nevertheless be zero. If $a_{12}=0$, $y_t$ responds only to its own shocks, so $y_t$ is **weakly exogenous** in the model. **[[Granger Causality Test|Granger causality]]** must also be interpreted carefully in a cointegrated system. If the equation for $\Delta z_t$ contains no lagged $\Delta y_t$ terms and $z_t$ does not respond to long-run disequilibrium, then $\{y_t\}$ does **not Granger-cause** $\{z_t\}$. When $a_{12}=0$, $\{y_t\}$ is weakly exogenous and is not Granger-caused by $\{z_t\}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. 协整检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Cointegration tests*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. [[协整与误差修正模型#Engle–Granger 两步法|EG两步法]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1. [[协整与误差修正模型#Engle–Granger 两步法|Engle–Granger two-step method]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] **EG两步检验**（Engle-Granger Two-Step）。
>
> 1. **单整阶数预检**：首先对每个变量进行单位根检验（如ADF检验或PP检验），以确定各变量的积分阶数。只有当所有相关变量均为$I(1)$（或同阶单整）时，才有可能存在协整关系。如果变量阶数不一致（例如一个是$I(1)$另一个是$I(0)$），则一般不存在协整关系，可直接停止分析。
> 2. **估计长期关系并检验残差平稳**：如果变量都是$I(1)$，在第二步中，对假定存在的长期均衡关系进行回归估计。通常选定其中一个变量作为被解释变量$y_t$，其余$I(1)$变量作为解释变量$x_{it}$，估计回归方程：
>     $$
>     y_t = β_0 + β_1 x_{1t} + β_2 x_{2t} + \cdots + β_n x_{nt} + e_t,
>     $$
>
>     得到残差序列${\hat e_t}$。然后对残差$\hat e_t$进行单位根检验（例如ADF检验），以检验残差是否为$I(0)$平稳序列。残差平稳的零假设对应“有协整关系”的假设（因为残差平稳意味着存在协整），而残差存在单位根则表示“无协整关系”（协整检验的原假设通常是不存在协整）。如果检验拒绝了残差存在单位根的原假设，意味着残差是平稳的，我们可以认定变量间存在协整关系。
>
> 3. **建立误差修正模型**：如果第二步确认存在协整关系（残差$I(0)$），则可以在第三步建立包含误差修正项的动态模型。例如，对第2步中的回归方程构建对应的误差修正模型，一般形式如：
>
>     $$
>
>     \Delta y_t = \gamma_0 + \gamma_1 \Delta x_{1t} + \cdots + \gamma_n \Delta x_{nt} + \phi \hat e_{t-1} + u_t
>
>     $$
>
>     其中$\hat e_{t-1}$为滞后一期的长期关系残差（即误差修正项），$\phi$为调整系数（期望$\phi<0$表示$y_t$受到偏离均衡向下调整的拉回力度），$u_t$为噪音项。通过这个模型可以同时刻画短期波动（差分项部分）和长期均衡偏离的校正（误差修正项部分）。
> 4. **模型检验**：最后，对所建立的误差修正模型进行模型适定性检验，包括检验残差的独立同分布性、稳健性等，以及检验协整关系和误差修正项在模型中的显著性，从而验证模型的有效性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> 1. **Pretest the order of integration.** First run unit-root tests, such as the ADF or PP test, on every variable to determine its order of integration. Cointegration in the usual Engle–Granger setting is possible only when the relevant variables are all $I(1)$, or at least integrated of the same order. If their orders differ—for example, one variable is $I(1)$ and another is $I(0)$—the standard procedure generally stops here.
> 2. **Estimate the long-run relationship and test whether its residual is stationary.** If the variables are all $I(1)$, choose one as the dependent variable $y_t$ and regress it on the other $I(1)$ variables $x_{it}$:
> $$
> y_t = β_0 + β_1 x_{1t} + β_2 x_{2t} + \cdots + β_n x_{nt} + e_t.
> $$
>
> Obtain the residual series $\hat e_t$ and apply a residual-based unit-root test, such as an ADF test with the appropriate Engle–Granger critical values. The operational null is that the residual has a unit root, which corresponds to **no cointegration**. Rejecting that null indicates that $\hat e_t$ is $I(0)$ and therefore supports a cointegrating relationship.
>
> 3. **Build an error-correction model.** If the second step finds an $I(0)$ residual, construct a dynamic model containing the lagged equilibrium error. For the regression above, a typical specification is
>
> $$
>
> \Delta y_t = \gamma_0 + \gamma_1 \Delta x_{1t} + \cdots + \gamma_n \Delta x_{nt} + \phi \hat e_{t-1} + u_t
>
> $$
>
> where $\hat e_{t-1}$ is the previous period's long-run residual, or error-correction term; $\phi$ is the adjustment coefficient, conventionally expected to be negative when positive disequilibrium is corrected; and $u_t$ is noise. The differenced terms describe short-run fluctuations, while the error-correction term describes adjustment toward long-run equilibrium.
> 4. **Diagnose the model.** Finally, assess the adequacy of the error-correction model, including residual independence and distributional behavior, robustness, and the significance of the cointegrating relationship and adjustment term.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==但是如果y有一个单位根,x1和x2共用一个单位根,按照Engle-Granger的讲法这个就不叫协整.==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==However, if $y$ has one unit root while $x_1$ and $x_2$ share another unit root, this does not count as cointegration in the Engle–Granger sense used here.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. Johansen系统协整检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2. Johansen system cointegration test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[协整与误差修正模型#Johansen 方法|Johansen]] (1988) 提出的协整检验方法严重依赖于 [[VAR Model|VAR]] 模型中矩阵 $\pi$ 的秩与其特征根之间的关系。该方法可以避免两步估计，直接在一个步骤中同时估计并检验多个协整向量的存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[协整与误差修正模型#Johansen 方法|Johansen]] (1988) method exploits the relationship between the rank of $\pi$ in a [[VAR Model|VAR]] model and the system's characteristic roots. Unlike the two-step procedure, it estimates and tests for multiple cointegrating vectors jointly within a single system.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑一个包含 n 个变量的一阶 VAR 系统：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a first-order VAR system containing $n$ variables:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$\mathbf{x}_t = A\,\mathbf{x}_{t-1} + \epsilon_t.$$
则可改写为差分形式：
$$\Delta \mathbf{x}_t = (A - I)\,\mathbf{x}_{t-1} + \epsilon_t \equiv \pi\ \mathbf{x}_{t-1} + \epsilon_t.$$

此处矩阵 $\pi$ 就等于 $A - I$。矩阵 $\pi$ 的秩（rank）等于协整向量的个数，而公共随机趋势的个数则等于 $n - \text{rank}(\pi)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\pi=A-I$. The rank of $\pi$ equals the number of cointegrating vectors, while the number of common stochastic trends equals $n-\text{rank}(\pi)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 当 $\text{rank}(\pi) = 0$ 时，说明不存在任何线性组合能够使过程平稳——即变量之间没有协整关系（此时所有变量都有单位根，需要差分才能使其稳定）。
- 当 $\text{rank}(\pi) = n$ 时（满秩），表示 $\pi$ 是非奇异矩阵，因而 A 满秩，系统中所有变量实际上都是平稳的（没有单位根）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- If $\text{rank}(\pi)=0$, no linear combination can remove the unit-root behavior: the variables are not cointegrated, and each must be differenced to become stationary.
- If $\text{rank}(\pi)=n$, $\pi$ is nonsingular and the system is stationary in levels rather than an $I(1)$ cointegrated system.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Johansen检验给出了两个相关的统计量，用于检验协整秩$r$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Johansen procedure provides two related statistics for testing the cointegration rank $r$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **迹统计量（Trace test）**：检验零假设$H_0: \text{rank}(π) \le r$（最多有$r$个协整关系）对立假设$H_1: \text{rank}(π) > r$的检验统计量定义为：
	$$
    \lambda_{\text{trace}}(r) = -T \sum_{i=r+1}^{n} \ln(1 - \hat{\lambda}_i)
    $$

    其中$\hat{\lambda}_i$是按降序排列的样本特征值，$T$为样本观测期数。直观上，$\lambda_{\text{trace}}(r)$把序号$r+1$到$n$的较小特征值都考虑进来：如果这些特征值中有不少显著大于0，则统计量会偏大，有利于拒绝$H_0$（意味着协整关系数大于$r$）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Trace statistic.** This tests $H_0:\text{rank}(π)\le r$, meaning at most $r$ cointegrating relationships, against $H_1:\text{rank}(π)>r$:
	$$
    \lambda_{\text{trace}}(r) = -T \sum_{i=r+1}^{n} \ln(1 - \hat{\lambda}_i).
    $$

    The $\hat{\lambda}_i$ are sample eigenvalues ordered from largest to smallest, and $T$ is the number of observations. The statistic aggregates the smaller eigenvalues from positions $r+1$ through $n$. If several are appreciably above zero, the statistic becomes large and favors rejecting $H_0$, implying more than $r$ cointegrating relationships.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **最大特征值统计量（Max-eigenvalue test）**：检验零假设$H_0: \text{rank}(π) = r$对立假设$H_1: \text{rank}(π) = r+1$的统计量定义为：
    $$
  \lambda_{\text{max}}(r, r+1) = -T \ln(1 - \hat{\lambda}_{r+1})
    $$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Maximum-eigenvalue statistic.** This tests $H_0:\text{rank}(π)=r$ against $H_1:\text{rank}(π)=r+1$:
    $$
  \lambda_{\text{max}}(r, r+1) = -T \ln(1 - \hat{\lambda}_{r+1}).
    $$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这实际上只关注排序第$r+1$大的特征值$\hat{\lambda}_{r+1}$本身：如果$\hat{\lambda}_{r+1}$显著大于0，那么可以拒绝$H_0$认为秩至少为$r+1$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This statistic considers only the $(r+1)$th-largest eigenvalue $\hat{\lambda}_{r+1}$. If it is significantly above zero, $H_0$ is rejected in favor of a rank of at least $r+1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Johansen方法依赖于前述误差修正形式$\Delta x_t = π x_{t-1} + \sum_{i=1}^{p-1} π_i \Delta x_{t-i} + \varepsilon_t$中的$π$矩阵特性。重复一下关键点：矩阵$π$的秩$r = \text{rank}(π)$等于协整向量的个数，而$n-r$则是系统中共同随机趋势的个数。Johansen检验的基本思路是通过对估计得到的$π$矩阵进行秩检验来推断协整关系的存在和数量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Johansen method relies on the properties of $π$ in the error-correction form $\Delta x_t=πx_{t-1}+\sum_{i=1}^{p-1}π_i\Delta x_{t-i}+\varepsilon_t$. The key point is worth repeating: $r=\text{rank}(π)$ is the number of cointegrating vectors, and $n-r$ is the number of common stochastic trends. The test infers the existence and number of cointegrating relationships by testing the rank of the estimated $π$ matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体而言，对于一个$n$维变量组成的VAR系统：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More specifically, for an $n$-dimensional VAR system,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

x_t = A_1 x_{t-1} + \cdots + A_p x_{t-p} + \varepsilon_t,

$$

可以整理为误差修正形式：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
the model can be rearranged into the error-correction form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->



$$

\Delta x_t = π x_{t-1} + \sum_{i=1}^{p-1} π_i \Delta x_{t-i} + \varepsilon_t,

$$



其中$π = -(I - A_1 - \cdots - A_p)$。Johansen方法通过对上述系统进行最大似然估计，得到$\hat π$矩阵，并考察$\hat π$的特征值来判断其秩$r$。这里涉及到线性代数的一个性质：矩阵$π$的秩$r$等于其特征根（[[Eigenvalues|特征值]]）中显著不为零的个数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $π=-(I-A_1-\cdots-A_p)$. Johansen's method estimates the system by maximum likelihood and uses the resulting generalized eigenvalue problem to determine the rank $r$. In the simplified linear-algebra intuition, the rank corresponds to the number of directions whose associated [[Eigenvalues|eigenvalues]] are nonzero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

需要强调的是，Johansen检验中实际使用的特征值并不是直接对$π$矩阵本身求得的特征值，而是通过对相关矩阵进行广义特征分解计算出的_相关矩阵的特征值_。这些特征值通常记作$\hat{\lambda}_1, \hat{\lambda}_2, \dots, \hat{\lambda}_n$，按照从大到小排序，一般介于0和1之间（这是由于计算过程中构造的矩阵性质导致的）。**注意：** 经典教材中的某些表述可能令人困惑，例如有的教材将Johansen检验描述为检验特征值是否“小于1”，但更准确地说，我们关注的是有多少个特征值显著大于0。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Importantly, the eigenvalues used by the Johansen test are not obtained by directly taking the ordinary eigenvalues of $π$. They arise from a generalized eigenvalue decomposition of covariance-related matrices constructed from the system. They are usually written $\hat{\lambda}_1,\hat{\lambda}_2,\dots,\hat{\lambda}_n$, ordered from largest to smallest, and generally lie between zero and one. **Note:** some textbook wording can be misleading—for example, describing the procedure as testing whether an eigenvalue is “less than one.” The operative question is how many canonical-correlation eigenvalues are significantly greater than zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->



Johansen检验给出了两个相关的统计量，用于检验协整秩$r$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Again, the Johansen procedure uses two related statistics to test the cointegration rank $r$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **迹统计量（Trace test）**：检验零假设$H_0: \text{rank}(π) \le r$（最多有$r$个协整关系）对立假设$H_1: \text{rank}(π) > r$的检验统计量定义为：

    $$
    \lambda_{\text{trace}}(r) = -T \sum_{i=r+1}^{n} \ln(1 - \hat{\lambda}_i)
    $$

    其中$\hat{\lambda}_i$是按降序排列的样本特征值，$T$为样本观测期数。直观上，$\lambda_{\text{trace}}(r)$把序号$r+1$到$n$的较小特征值都考虑进来：如果这些特征值中有不少显著大于0，则统计量会偏大，有利于拒绝$H_0$（意味着协整关系数大于$r$）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Trace statistic.** This tests $H_0:\text{rank}(π)\le r$ against $H_1:\text{rank}(π)>r$:

    $$
    \lambda_{\text{trace}}(r) = -T \sum_{i=r+1}^{n} \ln(1 - \hat{\lambda}_i).
    $$

    The $\hat{\lambda}_i$ are ordered sample eigenvalues and $T$ is the number of observations. The trace statistic combines all eigenvalues from $r+1$ through $n$; several values appreciably above zero make rejection of $H_0$ more likely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **最大特征值统计量（Max-eigenvalue test）**：检验零假设$H_0: \text{rank}(π) = r$对立假设$H_1: \text{rank}(π) = r+1$的统计量定义为：
    $$
  \lambda_{\text{max}}(r, r+1) = -T \ln(1 - \hat{\lambda}_{r+1})
    $$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Maximum-eigenvalue statistic.** This tests $H_0:\text{rank}(π)=r$ against $H_1:\text{rank}(π)=r+1$:
    $$
  \lambda_{\text{max}}(r, r+1) = -T \ln(1 - \hat{\lambda}_{r+1}).
    $$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这实际上只关注排序第$r+1$大的特征值$\hat{\lambda}_{r+1}$本身：如果$\hat{\lambda}_{r+1}$显著大于0，那么可以拒绝$H_0$认为秩至少为$r+1$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This version focuses only on $\hat{\lambda}_{r+1}$. If that eigenvalue is significantly above zero, the null is rejected and the rank is inferred to be at least $r+1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->




在实际应用中，我们通常从$H_0: r=0$（无协整）开始进行检验，逐步增加$r$直到无法拒绝假设为止。据此确定协整关系的个数。需要使用Johansen协整检验临界值（由于残差序列的非标准分布，其临界值由模拟计算得到，在统计附表中提供）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In applications, testing usually begins with $H_0:r=0$, meaning no cointegration. Increase $r$ sequentially until the first null that cannot be rejected; this determines the estimated number of cointegrating relationships. Johansen-specific critical values must be used because the statistics have nonstandard asymptotic distributions; the relevant values are obtained by simulation and reported in statistical tables or software.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->



**注释：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Notes:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Trace检验的原假设是协整向量个数$\le r$，Max-eigen检验的原假设是协整向量个数正好等于$r$。二者侧重点略有不同：Trace检验同时考虑多个特征值之和，偏向检测是否存在_超过_r个以上的协整关系；Max-eigen检验只看下一个特征值，针对增加一个协整向量的假设。通常Max-eigen因为针对性更强（假设更明确）而被认为具有更高的**检测力度**。

- 在判定协整关系个数时，Trace统计量和Max-eigen统计量的结果可能出现不一致的情况。这时需要综合考虑两种统计量，并参考经济含义和经验事实来做出判断。实务中不少研究者更信任Max-eigen检验的结果，因为它的备择假设更尖锐明确。

- Johansen方法的优点是能够同时检验多个协整关系，并且所有变量对称对待，不需要预先指定哪个是因变量。但该方法对数据要求较高（需要较大的样本容量）并且对模型假定较为敏感（如VAR阶数、是否包含趋势项等）。在应用时应先做好单位根检验确保所有变量同阶单整，再谨慎地设定VAR模型，并结合统计检验和信息准则选择适当的滞后阶数和确定模型中的常数/趋势项处理，以保证Johansen检验的有效性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The trace test has a null of at most $r$ cointegrating vectors, whereas the maximum-eigenvalue test has a null of exactly $r$. The trace statistic aggregates several eigenvalues and asks whether there are more than $r$ relationships; the maximum-eigenvalue statistic focuses on whether adding one more vector is warranted. The latter is sometimes described as more powerful against its narrower alternative, but this is not a universal reason to prefer it.

- The two statistics can select different ranks. When they disagree, consider both results together with the model specification, economic meaning, and robustness checks rather than choosing mechanically. Some applied researchers favor the maximum-eigenvalue result because its alternative is more sharply defined.

- Johansen's method can test several cointegrating relationships simultaneously and treats all variables symmetrically, so no dependent variable must be chosen in advance. Its reliability, however, can be sensitive to sample size, VAR lag order, and the treatment of constants and trends. Before applying it, establish compatible integration orders, specify the VAR carefully, choose lags with diagnostics and information criteria, and select the deterministic terms deliberately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
