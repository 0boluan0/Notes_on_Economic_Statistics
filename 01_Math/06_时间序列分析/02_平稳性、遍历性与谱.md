# 平稳性、遍历性与谱：从总体不变到一条路径可估
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stationarity, ergodicity, and the spectrum: from an invariant ensemble to inference from one path*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!tip] 这条路径解决什么
> 时间序列只有一条已实现的历史，但概率模型描述的是许多可能历史组成的总体。本章依次回答三个不能混在一起的问题：总体规律是否随日历时间改变；依赖怎样分布在滞后与频率上；以及一条足够长的路径何时能代表总体。
>
> 全局关系见[[平稳性、遍历性与谱.canvas|平稳性、遍历性与谱主题图]]；原始课堂展开与例题保留在[[01_Math/06_时间序列分析/03_平稳时间序列模型|平稳时间序列模型课堂笔记]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A time series gives us one realised history, whereas a stochastic model describes an ensemble of possible histories. This chapter keeps three questions separate: whether the ensemble law changes with calendar time, how dependence is distributed across lags and frequencies, and when one sufficiently long path can represent the ensemble. Use the topic Canvas for the global structure and the classroom note for the original exposition and exercises.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 先分清三个问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Begin with three distinct questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设想同一个随机机制被平行运行许多次。固定在日期 $t$，横着看所有可能实现，得到的是总体分布和总体平均；沿着其中一条已实现路径向前看，得到的是时间平均。现实数据通常只给后者，模型却首先定义前者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine running the same random mechanism across many parallel histories. Looking across histories at a fixed date gives an ensemble distribution and ensemble average; following one realised history through time gives a time average. Data usually provide the latter, while the model is initially defined through the former.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此需要三层不同的保证：

1. **平稳性：** 共同平移时间指标后，总体规律是否不变？
2. **相关结构与谱：** 若只看二阶信息，不同滞后怎样共同变动，方差又集中在哪些频率？
3. **遍历性：** 一条路径的时间平均能否恢复相应的总体量？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three layers are therefore distinct: stationarity asks whether the ensemble law is invariant to a common time shift; covariance and spectral analysis describe second-order dependence across lags and frequencies; ergodicity asks whether time averages from one path recover the corresponding ensemble quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这三层不是同义词。一个过程可以平稳却不遍历；有相同 ACF 的过程也可以有完全不同的尾部与非线性依赖。后面所有定义和例子都服务于这两个边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These layers are not synonyms. A process may be stationary but non-ergodic, and two processes with the same ACF may have very different tails or nonlinear dependence. The rest of the chapter makes these boundaries operational.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 平稳性固定的是总体规律，不是路径形状
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Stationarity fixes the ensemble law, not the shape of a path*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1 严平稳：完整联合分布不随共同平移改变

[[严平稳定义|严平稳]]要求：任取有限多个时点 $t_1,\ldots,t_k$，再把它们共同移动 $h$ 期，所得随机向量的联合分布不变：

$$
(X_{t_1},\ldots,X_{t_k})\overset d=(X_{t_1+h},\ldots,X_{t_k+h}).
$$

“共同移动”很重要。只知道每一期的边际分布相同，仍不知道 $X_t$ 与 $X_{t+h}$ 之间的依赖会不会随日历时间改变。严平稳保存的是所有有限维联合分布，而不是只有一阶、二阶矩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strict stationarity preserves every finite-dimensional joint distribution under a common shift. Identical one-date marginals are insufficient because temporal dependence may still vary with calendar time. The requirement concerns the entire finite-dimensional law, not only first and second moments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.2 宽平稳：只固定做二阶分析所需的部分

[[宽平稳定义|宽平稳]]要求有限二阶矩、常均值和只依赖滞后的协方差：

$$
E[X_t]=\mu,\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(X_{t+h},X_t)=\gamma(h).
$$

它没有宣称完整分布不变。这个较弱对象之所以有用，是因为线性预测、ACF 和通常的功率谱分析只使用均值与协方差。若研究尾部、非线性条件分布或联合极端，宽平稳本身提供的信息就不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Wide-sense stationarity retains finite second moments, a constant mean, and covariance that depends only on lag. It is useful because linear prediction, the ACF, and ordinary spectral analysis use precisely these quantities. It does not determine tails, nonlinear conditional laws, or joint extremes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.3 两种平稳性之间只有带条件的单向桥

[[严平稳与宽平稳|严平稳加有限二阶矩]]会推出宽平稳：一维分布平移不变固定均值与方差，二维联合分布平移不变使协方差只依赖时差。有限二阶矩不能从句子里删掉，[[Cauchy严平稳反例|IID Cauchy 序列]]严平稳，却连有限均值和方差都没有。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strict stationarity implies WSS only when second moments are finite. One-dimensional invariance fixes the moments and two-dimensional invariance makes covariance lag-only. An IID Cauchy sequence shows why the moment condition cannot be omitted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反方向一般不成立。[[宽平稳非严平稳反例|交替使用两个同均值、同方差的边际分布]]，同时令各期独立，可以得到常均值、常方差和零非零滞后协方差，却在平移一期后改变边际分布。宽平稳看不见这个变化，因为它只保存二阶信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The converse generally fails. Alternating between two independent marginal laws with the same mean and variance preserves every second-order quantity but changes the one-date distribution after a one-step shift. WSS cannot see this higher-order change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后，[[平稳性路径边界|平稳绝不等于路径平坦]]。白噪声可以每期剧烈波动而仍严格平稳；一段曲线看起来很平，也不能排除尚未显现的趋势、断点或方差变化。平稳性是关于生成总体的命题，不是肉眼对一条有限路径的形容词。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stationarity does not mean a flat path. White noise may fluctuate sharply at every date and still be strictly stationary; a calm-looking finite plot cannot rule out trend, breaks, or changing variance. Stationarity is a claim about the data-generating ensemble, not a visual adjective for one path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 自协方差把二阶依赖写在时域里
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Autocovariance represents second-order dependence in the time domain*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.1 先固定记号，否则同一个“相关”会指三件事

对均值为 $\mu$ 的实值宽平稳过程，[[自协方差与ACF|自协方差、raw correlation 与标准化 ACF]]分别是

$$
\gamma(h)=E[(X_{t+h}-\mu)(X_t-\mu)],
$$

$$
R(h)=E[X_{t+h}X_t]=\gamma(h)+\mu^2,
\qquad
\rho(h)=\frac{\gamma(h)}{\gamma(0)}.
$$

时间序列课程通常把 $\rho$ 称作 ACF，部分工程课程则把 $R$ 称作 autocorrelation。非零均值时它们不同；若 $\gamma(0)=0$，标准化 ACF 的除法没有定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Autocovariance, the raw second moment, and the normalised ACF are different objects. Time-series texts usually call $\rho$ the ACF, while some engineering texts use “autocorrelation” for $R$. They differ when the mean is nonzero, and the normalised ACF is undefined for a zero-variance process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个可计算的锚点是因果平稳 AR(1)：

$$
X_t=\phi X_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t,qquad |\phi|<1,qquad
\operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon_t)=\sigma_\varepsilon^2.
$$

此时

$$
\gamma(h)=\frac{\sigma_\varepsilon^2}{1-\phi^2}\phi^{|h|},
\qquad
\rho(h)=\phi^{|h|}.
$$

当 $\phi=0.8$ 时，滞后一期的相关是 $0.8$，滞后五期仍约为 $0.33$。这正是后续 [[AR(p)模型|AR 模型]]用少数参数生成整条相关结构的方式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a causal stationary AR(1), autocovariance and the ACF decay geometrically. With $\phi=0.8$, the lag-one correlation is $0.8$ and the lag-five correlation is still about $0.33$. This is how a low-dimensional AR model generates an entire dependence pattern.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.2 不是每个看起来像相关函数的曲线都合法

[[自协方差核条件|合法自协方差核]]必须 Hermitian（实值情形为偶函数）、满足 $|\gamma(h)|\le\gamma(0)$，并且对任意有限系数都产生非负二次型：

$$
\sum_{i,j}a_i\overline{a_j}\gamma(t_i-t_j)\ge0.
$$

这不是额外装饰；左边就是线性组合 $\sum_i a_iX_{t_i}$ 的方差。一个候选函数即使对称、零滞后最大，只要某个有限 Toeplitz 协方差矩阵不是半正定，就不可能来自任何二阶过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A valid autocovariance must be Hermitian, bounded by its zero-lag value, and positive semidefinite on every finite set of dates. The quadratic form is the variance of a linear combination, so symmetry and a maximum at zero are not enough.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.3 多个序列各自平稳，不代表它们共同平稳

若同时分析 $X_t$ 和 $Y_t$，[[联合宽平稳互协方差|联合宽平稳]]还要求互协方差

$$
\gamma_{XY}(h)=\operatorname{Cov}(X_{t+h},Y_t)
$$

只依赖 $h$。两条序列分别宽平稳，并不能阻止它们之间的关系随日历时间改变。实值情形有 $\gamma_{XY}(-h)=\gamma_{YX}(h)$；一个互协方差函数自身一般不是偶函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Joint WSS requires cross-covariance to depend only on lag in addition to separate WSS of the two series. Each series can be stable while their relationship changes over calendar time. For real processes, reversing the lag also swaps the variable order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.4 ACF 是压缩后的二阶摘要

[[ACF信息边界|相同 ACF 不等于相同过程]]。Gaussian 白噪声与取值为 $\pm1$ 的白噪声都可有零非零滞后 ACF，却有完全不同的尾部。更进一步，线性相关为零也不排除平方、绝对值或其他非线性函数之间存在可预测关系。因此 ACF 适合描述线性二阶结构，不是完整依赖的身份证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Matching ACFs do not identify a process. Gaussian and Rademacher white noise can share the same ACF while having different tails, and zero linear correlation does not remove dependence among squares, absolute values, or other nonlinear transforms. The ACF is a compressed second-order summary, not a complete dependence signature.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 谱把同一二阶结构换到频率域
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. The spectrum expresses the same second-order structure in frequency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[谱测度与谱密度|Herglotz 对应]]说明：离散时间宽平稳过程的合法自协方差与频率圆周上的唯一有限非负谱测度 $F$ 对应，

$$
\gamma(h)=\int_{[-\pi,\pi)}e^{ih\omega}\,dF(\omega),
\qquad F([-\pi,\pi))=\gamma(0).
$$

谱测度才是一般对象。只有 $F$ 对 Lebesgue 测度绝对连续时，才存在普通函数形式的谱密度 $f=dF/d\omega$。若 $\sum_h|\gamma(h)|<\infty$，密度存在且

$$
f(\omega)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\sum_{h=-\infty}^{\infty}\gamma(h)e^{-ih\omega},
\qquad
\gamma(h)=\int_{-\pi}^{\pi}e^{ih\omega}f(\omega)\,d\omega.
$$

绝对可和是方便的充分条件，不是密度存在的必要条件。离散谱线也不能被强行塞进普通密度函数里。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Herglotz's theorem pairs every valid discrete-time autocovariance with a unique finite nonnegative spectral measure on the frequency circle. A density exists only for an absolutely continuous measure. Absolute summability of autocovariances is a convenient sufficient condition, not a necessary one, and discrete spectral lines must remain measure-valued.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

AR(1) 的同一结构在频率域写成

$$
f(\omega)=\frac{\sigma_\varepsilon^2}{2\pi\lvert1-\phi e^{-i\omega}\rvert^2}.
$$

若 $\phi$ 接近 1，低频附近的质量更大，对应时域中的缓慢衰减；若 $\phi$ 接近 $-1$，质量会向高频移动，对应正负交替。时域的 ACF 与频域的谱不是两套现象，而是同一个二阶对象的 Fourier 两面。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The AR(1) spectrum is the Fourier counterpart of its geometric autocovariance. Positive persistence concentrates mass near low frequencies, while a coefficient near $-1$ shifts mass toward high-frequency alternation. The ACF and spectrum are two representations of the same second-order object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但[[谱峰解释边界|谱峰只说明方差在某个频带集中]]。随机振荡、共振滤波以及有限样本 periodogram 的波动都能产生峰；一个峰本身不能证明每条路径都以固定相位和振幅周期重复。解释前要区分离散谱线、连续宽峰和抽样噪声。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A spectral peak indicates concentration of second-order variation, not a deterministic cycle by itself. Random oscillation, resonant filtering, and periodogram noise can all produce peaks. Interpretation must distinguish spectral lines, broad continuous bands, and sampling variability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 遍历性回答：一条路径够不够
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Ergodicity asks whether one path is enough*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 5.1 总体平均与时间平均来自两个方向

[[总体平均与时间平均|总体平均]]固定 $t$ 并跨可能实现取 $E[X_t]$；时间平均固定一条实现并沿时间计算

$$
\bar X_n=\frac1n\sum_{t=1}^nX_t.
$$

平稳性只让总体目标不随日期漂移。[[均值遍历|均值遍历]]才进一步要求 $\bar X_n$ 以明确的方式收敛到 $\mu$；二阶课程常用均方收敛。不能把“均值不变”直接读成“样本均值一致”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ensemble average crosses possible histories at one date, while a time average follows one history. Stationarity keeps the ensemble target from drifting; mean ergodicity is the additional claim that the sample mean converges to that target in a stated mode, often mean square in a second-order course.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 5.2 一个反例足以拆掉“平稳 + 很长 = 可代表总体”

先抽一个非退化随机变量 $Z$，再令每一期都等于它：$X_t=Z$。[[随机常数非遍历反例|这个随机常数过程]]严格平稳，但任意样本长度下

$$
\bar X_n=Z,
\qquad
E[(\bar X_n-EZ)^2]=\operatorname{Var}(Z)>0.
$$

更长的路径只让我们更确定这一次抽到了哪个 $Z$，不会补出没有实现的其他路径。数据长度不能替代遍历条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A random-constant process is strictly stationary, yet every sample mean equals the path-specific draw $Z$. A longer record identifies that draw more precisely but cannot recover unrealised histories. Sample length is not a substitute for ergodicity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 5.3 样本均值的方差给出可检查的二阶判据

对宽平稳序列，把样本均值方差中的双重求和按滞后分组，得到[[样本均值方差判据|三角权重公式]]：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_n)
&=\frac1{n^2}\sum_{s=1}^n\sum_{t=1}^n\gamma(t-s)\\
&=\frac1n\left[\gamma(0)+2\sum_{h=1}^{n-1}\left(1-\frac hn\right)\gamma(h)\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

因为 $E[\bar X_n]=\mu$，均方意义的均值遍历等价于这个方差趋于零。$\sum_h|\gamma(h)|<\infty$ 足以保证它，但并非必要。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Grouping the double covariance sum by lag yields the triangular-window formula. Since the sample mean is unbiased, mean-square mean ergodicity is exactly the requirement that this variance vanish. Absolute summability of the autocovariance is sufficient but not necessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回到平稳 AR(1)，当 $n$ 很大时

$$
\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_n)
\approx \frac{\gamma(0)}n\frac{1+\phi}{1-\phi}.
$$

若 $\phi=0.8$，系数是 $9$：对“估计均值”这个特定任务，$n$ 个高度持续的观测只提供大约相当于 $n/9$ 个同方差独立观测的信息。这里的 $n/9$ 是渐近、任务特定的直觉，不是整个过程通用的“有效样本量”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a stationary AR(1), the large-sample variance inflation factor for estimating the mean is $(1+\phi)/(1-\phi)$. At $\phi=0.8$ it equals nine, giving an approximate mean-estimation effective sample size of $n/9$ relative to independent observations with the same marginal variance. This is an asymptotic, statistic-specific interpretation, not a universal sample-size label for the process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 5.4 估计相关函数需要另一项遍历保证

[[协方差遍历|固定滞后的样本协方差]]是乘积过程

$$
Z_t^{(h)}=(X_{t+h}-\mu)(X_t-\mu)
$$

的时间平均。$X_t$ 对均值遍历，不会自动让每个 $Z_t^{(h)}$ 也对均值遍历；后者的方差涉及四阶矩和四阶依赖。于是“样本均值可靠”和“样本 ACF 可靠”是两个需要分别论证的结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fixed-lag covariance estimation is a time-average problem for a product process. Mean ergodicity of $X_t$ does not automatically make each product process mean-ergodic, because the latter involves fourth moments and fourth-order dependence. Reliability of the sample mean and reliability of the sample ACF therefore require separate arguments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. Gaussian 特例说明二阶信息何时真的够用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. The Gaussian exception shows when second-order information is enough*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般过程不能由均值和协方差识别，但 [[Gaussian过程均值协方差决定性|Gaussian 过程]]的任意有限向量都联合 Gaussian，其分布由均值向量和协方差矩阵完全决定。于是对实 Gaussian 过程，[[Gaussian宽严平稳等价|宽平稳会推出严平稳]]：共同平移不改变均值向量与协方差矩阵，也就不改变任何有限维联合分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, mean and covariance do not identify a process. For a Gaussian process, however, every finite vector is jointly Gaussian and is fully determined by its mean vector and covariance matrix. Consequently a real Gaussian WSS process is also strictly stationary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，[[联合高斯独立判据|零协方差推出独立]]要求**联合** Gaussian。只知道 $X$ 和 $Y$ 各自边际正态仍不够：若 $X=Z$、$Y=SZ$，其中 $Z\sim N(0,1)$，独立随机符号 $S$ 等概率取 $\pm1$，则两者边际都标准正态且不相关，却满足 $|X|=|Y|$，所以不独立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Likewise, zero covariance implies independence only under joint Gaussianity. Gaussian marginals alone are insufficient: with $X=Z$ and $Y=SZ$, where $S$ is an independent random sign, both marginals are standard normal and uncorrelated, yet $|X|=|Y|$ almost surely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Gaussian 结构让二阶工具异常强大，但也正因如此容易被误用。每当从 ACF、协方差或谱跳到完整分布、独立性或条件预测时，都应先问：这里是否真的有联合 Gaussian 或其他足够结构？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gaussian structure makes second-order tools unusually powerful and therefore unusually easy to overextend. Before moving from covariance, the ACF, or the spectrum to a claim about a full law, independence, or conditional prediction, check whether joint Gaussianity or another sufficient structure is actually present.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 平稳、遍历与 mixing 的层级不能互换
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Stationarity, ergodicity, and mixing are not interchangeable levels*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[平稳遍历与mixing|三者的职责]]可以这样记：

- **平稳性**让 shift 不改变概率律；
- **均值或协方差遍历**只保证某个指定统计量的时间平均恢复总体量；
- **完整遍历性**排除概率介于 0 与 1 之间的 shift-invariant 事件，并通过遍历定理支持更广泛的时间平均；
- **mixing**进一步要求相隔越来越远的事件渐近解耦。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stationarity makes the law shift-invariant. Mean or covariance ergodicity concerns a specified statistic. Full ergodicity rules out nontrivial shift-invariant events and supports a broader class of time averages. Mixing adds asymptotic decoupling of increasingly separated events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在标准的 stationary-shift 语境中，

$$
\text{strong mixing}\Longrightarrow\text{weak mixing}\Longrightarrow\text{ergodicity},
$$

反向一般不成立。相关衰减、均值遍历、完整遍历和 strong mixing 也不能彼此替换。写出一个极限定理或标准误公式时，必须保留它实际需要的那一层假设，而不是统称“序列平稳所以可以”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the standard stationary-shift setting, strong mixing implies weak mixing, which implies ergodicity; the converses fail in general. Correlation decay, mean ergodicity, full ergodicity, and strong mixing are distinct. A limit theorem or standard-error formula must retain the precise dependence assumptions it needs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8. 面对一个新序列时，按这个顺序判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. A working order for a new series*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **先说清对象。** 分析的是水平、去趋势残差、差分、收益率还是平方收益？一个变换后的序列平稳，不会让原水平自动平稳。
2. **再说清所需平稳性。** 只做线性二阶分析时，宽平稳可能够用；若结论涉及完整联合分布或非线性事件，就不能偷换成宽平稳。
3. **固定二阶记号。** 明确使用 $\gamma$、raw $R$ 还是标准化 $\rho$，再检查候选协方差核是否合法。
4. **选择时域或频域表示。** ACF 适合看滞后传播，谱适合看方差怎样分布在频率上；二者是同一二阶结构，不是相互独立的证据。
5. **声明从一条路径估计什么。** 均值、固定滞后协方差和完整分布需要不同遍历保证；给出收敛方式或实际使用的 mixing 条件。
6. **最后才解释。** 零 ACF 不等于独立，谱峰不等于确定性周期，平稳也不等于模型正确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a new series, first identify the transformed object being modelled. Then choose the level of stationarity the argument needs, fix the covariance convention, select a time- or frequency-domain representation, state exactly what one path is being used to estimate, and only then interpret. Zero ACF is not independence, a spectral peak is not a deterministic cycle, and stationarity is not model correctness.
