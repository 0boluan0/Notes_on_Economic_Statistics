# 2. 时间序列分析的目标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Objectives of Time-Series Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 数据描述.将数据分为趋势部分,季节性部分,周期性部分和不规则部分.这门课主要是不规则部分.描述后可以进行预测
2. 检验经济理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Describe the data by separating trend, seasonal, cyclical, and irregular components. This course focuses mainly on the irregular component; description then supports forecasting.<br>
**2.** Test economic theories.<br>
