# 含趋势序列：先判断是谁在推动长期路径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Trending Series: First Identify What Moves the Long-Run Path*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这章不是教你“看到上升就做一阶差分”，而是回答一个更早的问题：水平序列为什么不平稳？如果长期路径是固定的确定性函数，就应估计并去掉它；如果路径由冲击不断改写，就要识别未抵消的单位根，再决定差分。两种误判会保留或删掉完全不同的信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This chapter is not a rule to difference every visibly rising series. It asks why the level is nonstationary. A fixed deterministic path calls for detrending; a path continually revised by shocks calls for unit-root analysis and, when justified, differencing. Confusing the two preserves or destroys the wrong information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主题关系先看 [[趋势、单位根与差分.canvas|趋势、单位根与差分主题图]]；下文按“趋势身份 → 随机游走 → 差分 → 检验 → 失效机制 → 建模决策”连续展开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the [[趋势、单位根与差分.canvas|topic Canvas]] for the global structure. The reading path below moves from trend identity to random walks, differencing, testing, failure modes, and the final modelling decision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 1. 同一张趋势图可能来自两种机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. The Same Trending Plot Can Come from Two Different Mechanisms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先比较两个最小模型：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with two minimal models:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 机制 | 水平表示 | 一次普通冲击的长期影响 | 合适的第一步 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[趋势平稳定义|确定性趋势]] | $y_t=m(t)+u_t$，$u_t\sim I(0)$ | 由 $u_t$ 的稳定短期动态决定，普通冲击不改写 $m(t)$ | 估计并去掉 $m(t)$ |
| [[随机趋势]] | $(1-B)y_t=\varepsilon_t$ | 冲击进入此后每一期水平 | 识别单位根并检验所需差分 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Mechanism | Level representation | Long-run effect of an ordinary shock | First transformation to consider |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[趋势平稳定义|Deterministic trend]] | $y_t=m(t)+u_t$, with $u_t\sim I(0)$ | Governed by the stable short-run dynamics of $u_t$; the shock does not rewrite $m(t)$ | Estimate and remove $m(t)$ |
| [[随机趋势|Stochastic trend]] | $(1-B)y_t=\varepsilon_t$ | The shock remains in every later level | Identify the unit root and test the required differencing |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Box–Jenkins 方法需要一个适合平稳短期建模的对象，但“不平稳”并不意味着原数据被判为无用。任务是先把确定性路径或随机趋势处理清楚，再对留下的对象建 ARMA 动态，并在最终关心的水平尺度上还原和评价预测。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Box–Jenkins modelling needs a target with stable short-run dynamics, but nonstationarity does not make the original data unusable. First handle the deterministic path or stochastic trend, then model the remaining ARMA dynamics, and finally reconstruct and evaluate forecasts on the level scale that matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 确定性趋势：路径固定，偏离可平稳
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Deterministic Trend: A Fixed Path with Stationary Deviations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程中最常用的 [[趋势平稳定义|趋势平稳]] 模型是
$$
y_t=\alpha+\delta t+u_t,\qquad u_t\sim I(0).
$$
更高阶多项式也可以描述弯曲但仍由时间确定的路径。这里“确定”不表示 $\alpha$、$\delta$ 已知，而表示给定参数后 $m(t)$ 不随未来随机冲击改写。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The standard trend-stationary model is $y_t=\alpha+\delta t+u_t$ with $u_t\sim I(0)$. Higher-order polynomials can represent a curved but still deterministic path. “Deterministic” does not mean that the coefficients are known; it means that, conditional on them, future shocks do not rewrite the path $m(t)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估计趋势后取
$$
\widehat u_t=y_t-\widehat m(t),
$$
再对 $\widehat u_t$ 建模。关键检查不是“残差图看着更平”，而是趋势规格有依据、残差的平稳短期模型合格，而且最终水平预测把 $\widehat m(t)$ 加回。若存在水平或斜率突变却只拟合一条直线，后面的单位根检验也会被误导。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After estimating the trend, model $\widehat u_t=y_t-\widehat m(t)$. The relevant checks are not merely visual: the trend specification must be defensible, the residual short-run model must be adequate, and level forecasts must add $\widehat m(t)$ back. An ignored level or slope break can also mislead the unit-root tests that follow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. 随机趋势：未来路径由新冲击改写
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Stochastic Trend: New Shocks Rewrite the Future Path*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. 无漂移随机游走
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1. Driftless Random Walk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小例子是 [[无漂移随机游走]]：
$$
y_t=y_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
y_t=y_0+\sum_{i=1}^{t}\varepsilon_i.
$$
即使增量均值为零，水平也不会围绕固定均值波动。每个 $\varepsilon_s$ 都留在所有 $t\ge s$ 的水平中，这就是 [[随机趋势]] 中“冲击永久”的精确含义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The minimal example is a [[无漂移随机游走|driftless random walk]]. Although its increments have zero conditional mean, the level does not fluctuate around a fixed mean. Every innovation remains in every later level; this is the precise meaning of a permanent shock in a stochastic trend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. 带漂移随机游走
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2. Random Walk with Drift*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[带漂移随机游走]] 满足
$$
y_t=a_0+y_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
y_t=y_0+a_0t+\sum_{i=1}^{t}\varepsilon_i.
$$
$a_0t$ 让条件均值沿直线移动，但减去这条直线后仍剩累积创新。因此“有一条线性平均路径”和“围绕固定趋势平稳”不是一回事。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[带漂移随机游走|random walk with drift]] adds the linear conditional-mean path $a_0t$, but subtracting that line still leaves cumulated innovations. A linear mean path is therefore not the same as stationarity around a fixed trend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.3. 水平预测为什么越来越不确定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3. Why Level Forecasts Become Increasingly Uncertain*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

向前递推给出 [[随机游走预测]]：
$$
E_t(y_{t+h})=y_t+ha_0,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}_t\!\left(y_{t+h}-E_t y_{t+h}\right)=h\sigma^2,
$$
其中第二式还要求未来创新条件同方差且互不相关。点预测只延长当前水平与漂移；新的信息却逐期累积，所以标准差按 $\sqrt h$ 扩大。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Forward iteration gives the [[随机游走预测|random-walk forecast]]. The point forecast extends the current level by the drift, while new uncertainty accumulates each period. Under conditional homoskedasticity and orthogonality, the variance grows with $h$ and the standard deviation with $\sqrt h$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.4. 差分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Differencing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若过程的非平稳性来自未抵消的有限重单位根，且差分后剩余动态满足平稳条件，可以通过相应次数的差分得到平稳对象。以随机游走加漂移模型为例：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When nonstationarity comes from uncancelled finite-multiplicity unit roots and the remaining dynamics are stationary, the corresponding number of differences yields a stationary target. For a random walk with drift:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$ \Delta y_t = a_0 + \varepsilon_t. $$
很容易看出，$\Delta y_t$ 是平稳的过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is immediate that $\Delta y_t$ is stationary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于一般的 [[ARIMA模型|ARIMA]]$(p,1,q)$，判断必须基于 [[单位根最小表示|最小表示]]。若
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a general [[ARIMA模型|ARIMA]]$(p,1,q)$ process, work with the [[单位根最小表示|minimal representation]]. If
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
A(L)=(1-L)A^*(L),
$$
且 $A^*(z)$ 的根都在单位圆外，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
and every root of $A^*(z)$ lies outside the unit circle, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
(1-L)A^*(L)y_t=B(L)\varepsilon_t
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
A^*(L)\Delta y_t=B(L)\varepsilon_t.
$$
$\Delta y_t$ 因而具有平稳 ARMA 动态。$B(z)$ 的根在单位圆外是可逆性条件，不是这一平稳结论所必需的条件；但 $A$ 与 $B$ 若有公共因子，必须先约去，否则会数到并不存在于可观测过程中的“单位根”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The differenced series therefore has stationary ARMA dynamics. Roots of $B(z)$ outside the unit circle concern invertibility, not the stationarity conclusion itself. Any common factor of $A$ and $B$ must be cancelled first, or an observationally irrelevant factor may be mistaken for a unit root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这可推广到 [[I(d)定义|$I(d)$]]：若最小表示含 $(1-L)^d$ 这一未抵消因子，且剩余 AR 动态稳定，那么 $d$ 是使普通差分结果成为 $I(0)$ 的最小次数。周期为 $s$ 的季节根则由 [[普通与季节差分|$(1-L^s)$]] 处理，不能和普通差分阶数混为一谈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This extends to [[I(d)定义|$I(d)$]]: in a minimal representation, $d$ is the smallest number of ordinary differences that produces an $I(0)$ target. Seasonal roots instead call for the [[普通与季节差分|seasonal operator $(1-L^s)$]] and must not be confused with the ordinary integration order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

差分次数服从 [[最小差分原则]]。少一次会把随机趋势留在残差里，多一次则可能制造负短期相关，甚至把 MA 根推到不可逆边界。课程中对白噪声再差一次的精确反例也在该原子中；它解释了为什么“差到图看起来最乱”为止不是方法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The order follows the [[最小差分原则|minimum-differencing principle]]. Too few differences leave a stochastic trend; one too many can manufacture negative short-run dependence and even place an MA root on the noninvertible boundary. The same atom derives the exact white-noise counterexample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4. [[DF检验|单位根检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*[[DF检验|Unit-Root Tests]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参见：[[趋势、单位根与差分.canvas|单位根与差分主题图]] · [[单位根证据组合|平稳性检验对比（DF–ADF–PP–KPSS）]]

在单位根原假设下，滞后水平是累积创新，普通平稳回归的中心极限定理和 $t$ 临界值不能直接用于这个系数统计量，因此要用单位根问题的非标准临界值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under a unit-root null, the usual stationary large-sample approximation and standard central limit theorem do not apply, so nonstandard critical values are required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验前先固定 [[单位根确定项规格|确定性项规格]]。无常数、仅常数、常数加趋势不是同一个检验加了不同装饰：它们允许不同的漂移和平稳备择，也使用不同临界值。不能把三种规格都跑一遍后只报告最容易拒绝的一种。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the [[单位根确定项规格|deterministic specification]] before testing. No intercept, an intercept only, and an intercept plus trend are different hypotheses with different drift allowances, stationary alternatives, and critical values. Running all three and reporting only the easiest rejection is specification search, not a valid decision rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. DF检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Dickey–Fuller Test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**目的**：在所写的最小 AR 与确定性项规格中，检验 $y_t$ 是否含单位根；拒绝与否都只针对该规格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Purpose:** in the stated minimal AR and deterministic specification, test whether $y_t$ contains a unit root; either conclusion is conditional on that specification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**基本模型**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Basic model**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
y_t = \phi y_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t,
$$
其中 $\varepsilon_t$ 为白噪声。令 $\delta = \phi - 1$，则等价于
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $\varepsilon_t$ is white noise. Defining $\delta=\phi-1$ gives the equivalent regression
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
\Delta y_t = y_t - y_{t-1} = \delta y_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t.
$$

原假设 $H_0: \phi = 1$（即 $\delta = 0$，存在单位根）。左尾拒绝沿 $\phi<1$ 的方向反对这个根；只有把备择预先维持在 $-1<\phi<1$ 时，才能把它解释为平稳 AR(1)，因为 $\delta<0$ 本身并不排除 $\phi\le -1$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The null is $H_0:\phi=1$, equivalently $\delta=0$. A left-tail rejection is evidence against that root in the direction $\phi<1$. It supports a stationary AR(1) only under the maintained alternative $-1<\phi<1$, because $\delta<0$ alone does not exclude $\phi\le -1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**检验统计量**
基于OLS 估计 $\delta$，计算 $t$ 统计量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Test statistic**
Estimate $\delta$ by OLS and calculate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\tau = \frac{\hat\delta}{\mathrm{SE}(\hat\delta)}.
$$

与临界值比较（Dickey–Fuller 表）来决定是否拒绝 $H_0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare this statistic with Dickey–Fuller critical values to decide whether to reject $H_0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


## 4.2. [[ADF检验|ADF 检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*[[ADF检验|Augmented Dickey–Fuller Test]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**目的**：在 DF 检验基础上，考虑残差序列可能存在自相关，通过增加滞后差分项来修正。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Purpose:** extend the DF test by adding lagged differences to remove possible serial correlation from the residuals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**基本模型**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Basic model**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

带截距和趋势的形式为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With an intercept and trend, the regression is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta y_t = \alpha + \beta t + \delta y_{t-1}
- \sum_{i=1}^p \theta_i\Delta y_{t-i} + \varepsilon_t  $$

- $\alpha$ 为常数项，
- $\beta,t$ 为趋势项（可选），
- $p$ 为滞后阶数，用于消除自相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $\alpha$ is the intercept;
- $\beta t$ is an optional deterministic trend;
- $p$ is the lag order chosen to remove residual autocorrelation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**检验假设**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Hypotheses**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_0: \delta = 0\quad(\text{存在单位根}),\qquad
H_1: \delta < 0\quad(\text{在维持的非爆炸备择下反对单位根}).
$$

被检验的仍是滞后水平系数的 $t$ 形统计量，但加入滞后差分不会把它恢复成普通 Student-$t$；仍须使用与确定性项规格匹配的 DF/ADF 非标准临界值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The statistic has the same form as in the DF test, but it must be compared with the appropriate nonstandard critical values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

滞后阶数是推断的一部分：太少会留下序列相关并扭曲检验大小，太多会损失有效样本与功效。信息准则和残差诊断帮助选择 $p$，却不能把选择的不确定性变成一个永远正确的固定数。完整边界见 [[ADF检验]]，未拒绝后的措辞见 [[ADF未拒绝的含义]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lag order is part of the inference. Too few lags leave serial correlation and distort size; too many consume observations and reduce power. Information criteria and residual diagnostics help choose $p$, but no fixed order is universally correct. See [[ADF检验]] and [[ADF未拒绝的含义]] for the exact boundaries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.3. [[PP检验|PP 检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Phillips–Perron Test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

PP 保留低阶 DF 型回归，不加入 ADF 的滞后差分，而用残差长期方差对系数统计量与 $t$ 统计量作非参数修正。它允许更一般的弱依赖和异方差，但仍需选择核与带宽，也仍使用与确定性项规格匹配的非标准临界值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
PP keeps a low-order DF regression and, instead of adding ADF lagged differences, uses a residual long-run variance to make nonparametric corrections to the coefficient and $t$ statistics. It permits more general weak dependence and heteroskedasticity, but still requires kernel and bandwidth choices and specification-matched nonstandard critical values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，“ADF 是参数化修短期动态、PP 是换成稳健标准误”只说对了一半。PP 修正的是专门的单位根统计量，不是给原回归随手套一个普通 HAC 标准误；强负 MA 误差下，它的有限样本大小也可能很差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus PP is not merely DF with an ordinary robust standard error. It makes unit-root-specific corrections, and its finite-sample size can still be poor, especially with strong negative MA errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.4. [[KPSS检验|KPSS 检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*KPSS Test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**目的**：与 DF/[[ADF检验|ADF]] 的原假设方向相反，KPSS 以指定的水平平稳或趋势平稳为原假设，以随机趋势成分为备择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Purpose:** unlike DF and [[ADF检验|ADF]], KPSS uses a specified level- or trend-stationary null and a stochastic-trend component as the alternative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**基本模型**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Basic model**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将 $y_t$ 分解为确定性趋势、随机游走成分和误差：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Decompose $y_t$ into a deterministic trend, a random-walk component, and an error:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$

y_t = \delta t + r_t + \varepsilon_t,

\qquad

r_t = r_{t-1} + \eta_t,

$$

其中 $\varepsilon_t$ 为平稳误差，$\eta_t$ 为随机趋势创新。若 $\operatorname{Var}(\eta_t)=0$，$r_t$ 退化为常数：不含 $\delta t$ 的规格给出水平平稳原假设，含 $\delta t$ 的规格给出趋势平稳原假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\varepsilon_t$ is the stationary error and $\eta_t$ is the random-trend innovation. If $\operatorname{Var}(\eta_t)=0$, $r_t$ becomes constant: omitting $\delta t$ gives a level-stationary null, while including it gives a trend-stationary null.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**检验统计量**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Test statistic**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先计算残差序列 $\hat\varepsilon_t$ 并构造累计和
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First obtain residuals $\hat\varepsilon_t$ and form their cumulative sums
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
S_t = \sum_{i=1}^t \hat\varepsilon_i.
$$
然后计算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then calculate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
\mathrm{LM} = \frac{1}{T^2}\sum_{t=1}^T S_t^2 \bigg/ \hat\sigma^2,
$$
其中 $\hat\sigma^2$ 为 $\varepsilon_t$ 的长期方差估计，核、带宽与残差部分和的完整关系见 [[KPSS统计量]]。与 KPSS 临界值比较：
- 若 $\mathrm{LM}$ 大于临界值，拒绝所选的水平或趋势平稳 $H_0$，证据指向随机趋势备择；
- 否则不拒绝所选的水平或趋势平稳原假设，但不能据此证明平稳。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\hat\sigma^2$ estimates the long-run variance of $\varepsilon_t$; [[KPSS统计量]] explains how the residual partial sums, kernel, and bandwidth enter. Compare the LM statistic with KPSS critical values:
- if $\mathrm{LM}$ exceeds the critical value, reject the chosen level- or trend-stationary null in favour of the stochastic-trend alternative;
- otherwise, do not reject the chosen level- or trend-stationary null; this is not proof of stationarity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.5. 四种检验怎样共同提供证据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How the Four Tests Contribute Evidence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 检验 | 原假设 | 如何处理短期相关 | 最容易误读的地方 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DF | 给定规格下有单位根 | 假设低阶回归误差为白噪声 | 统计量不是普通 $t$ |
| [[ADF检验|ADF]] | 给定规格下有单位根 | 加入滞后差分 | 未拒绝不等于证明单位根 |
| [[PP检验|PP]] | 给定规格下有单位根 | 长期方差修正统计量 | 不是普通稳健标准误 |
| [[KPSS检验|KPSS]] | 指定的水平或趋势平稳 | 长期方差进入 LM 统计量 | 水平与趋势规格不能混用 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Test | Null hypothesis | Treatment of short-run dependence | Common misreading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DF | Unit root under the chosen specification | Assumes a white-noise error in the low-order regression | The statistic is not an ordinary $t$ |
| [[ADF检验|ADF]] | Unit root under the chosen specification | Adds lagged differences | Failure to reject does not prove a unit root |
| [[PP检验|PP]] | Unit root under the chosen specification | Corrects the statistic with a long-run variance | It is not an ordinary robust standard error |
| [[KPSS检验|KPSS]] | Specified level or trend stationarity | Uses a long-run variance in the LM statistic | Level and trend specifications are not interchangeable |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 5. 检验结果不是标签：先解释冲突，再选择模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. A Test Result Is Not a Label: Explain Conflict Before Choosing a Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.1. 把 ADF、PP 与 KPSS 当作有条件的证据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.1. Treat ADF, PP, and KPSS as Conditional Evidence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[单位根证据组合]] 给出一个诊断起点，而不是多数表决：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[单位根证据组合]] provides a diagnostic starting point, not a majority vote:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 单位根检验拒绝、KPSS 不拒绝：与所选平稳规格相容，但不是证明。
- 单位根检验不拒绝、KPSS 拒绝：与随机趋势相容，但仍要检查规格与断点。
- 两边都拒绝：当前两个简单模型可能都不合适，常见线索包括断点、非线性或方差变化。
- 两边都不拒绝：证据常常太弱，不能硬贴“平稳”或“单位根”标签。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Unit-root rejection with KPSS non-rejection is compatible with the chosen stationary specification, not proof of it.
- Unit-root non-rejection with KPSS rejection is compatible with a stochastic trend, but specification and breaks still need checking.
- Rejection on both sides can mean that neither simple model fits, perhaps because of a break, nonlinearity, or changing variance.
- Non-rejection on both sides often means weak information, not a licence to force either label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

尤其要守住 [[ADF未拒绝的含义]]：未拒绝只表示样本没有提供足够反证。报告时应同时写出样本区间、确定性项、滞后或带宽、统计量、临界值以及残差诊断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The key wording is [[ADF未拒绝的含义|failure to reject]]. It means insufficient evidence against the null in this sample and specification. Report the sample, deterministic terms, lag or bandwidth choice, statistic, critical value, and residual diagnostics together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.2. 两种会伪装成单位根的情况
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.2. Two Cases That Can Resemble a Unit Root*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一种是 [[近单位根]]。在 $y_t=\rho y_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t$ 中，$\rho=0.98$ 的冲击最终会消失，但在短样本中衰减极慢，路径和 ACF 都可能像 $\rho=1$。把一次未拒绝直接翻译成永久差分，可能制造过度差分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first is a [[近单位根|near unit root]]. With $\rho=0.98$, shocks eventually decay, but so slowly that a short sample can resemble $\rho=1$. Permanently differencing after one non-rejection can therefore over-difference the series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二种是 [[结构突变伪单位根|未建模结构突变]]。一个前后分别趋势平稳、但在某时点改变水平或斜率的序列，会让无断点检验难以拒绝单位根。若断点由制度事件事先给定，和在样本中搜索最显著断点所需的分布不同；这个选择边界见 [[单位根断点选择]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second is an [[结构突变伪单位根|unmodelled structural break]]. A series may be trend-stationary on each side of a level or slope change yet defeat a no-break unit-root test. A date fixed by external institutional evidence and a date selected by searching the same sample require different distributions; see [[单位根断点选择]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.3. 为什么水平回归会伪显著，以及协整为何是例外
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.3. Why Levels Regressions Can Be Spurious, and Why Cointegration Is the Exception*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把两个独立随机游走的水平互相回归，也可能得到高 $R^2$、显著的普通 $t$ 统计量和强自相关残差；[[时间序列伪回归]] 说明共同“看起来在漂移”足以破坏常规平稳回归推断。显著性不能替代对积分阶数与残差的检查。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regressing one independent random-walk level on another can still produce a high $R^2$, conventional significance, and strongly autocorrelated residuals. [[时间序列伪回归|Spurious regression]] shows that visually similar drifting paths are enough to invalidate ordinary stationary-regression inference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但两个 $I(1)$ 序列若有某个平稳线性组合，就可能协整。此时机械分别差分会删掉长期约束；[[协整与差分边界]] 要求保留水平组合并进入下一章的误差修正模型。协整仍只是统计关系，经济均衡、调整方向与因果需要额外理论和识别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two $I(1)$ series may instead be cointegrated if a linear combination is stationary. Mechanical separate differencing then discards the long-run restriction; [[协整与差分边界]] leads into the next chapter's error-correction model. Cointegration is still a statistical relation, not automatic evidence of economic equilibrium, adjustment direction, or causality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.4. 最后的变换服从问题，而不是服从一张图
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.4. Let the Question Choose the Transformation, Not a Single Plot*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[去趋势与差分]] 保存的信息不同：去趋势保留相对固定路径的水平偏离；差分把研究对象改为跨期变化。选定模型后，必须回到同一个最终目标比较样本外表现——差分模型要把变化累加回水平，去趋势模型要把趋势加回，协整模型则同时保留变化与长期偏离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[去趋势与差分|Detrending and differencing]] preserve different information. Detrending retains level deviations from a fixed path; differencing changes the target to period-to-period movements. Candidate models should ultimately be compared out of sample on the same final target: cumulate differenced forecasts back to levels, restore deterministic trends, and preserve long-run deviations in a cointegrated model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 6. 蒙特卡洛为什么出现在这一章
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Why Monte Carlo Simulation Appears in This Chapter*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位根统计量在零假设下不是普通 $t$ 分布，因此有限样本临界值、检验大小和功效不能只凭常规回归直觉判断。蒙特卡洛实验可以在明确的数据生成过程、样本量、初值和重复次数下模拟统计量分布，检查名义 5% 检验实际拒真率是否接近 5%，以及近单位根、负 MA 误差或断点如何改变功效与大小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unit-root statistics do not have an ordinary $t$ distribution under the null, so finite-sample critical values, size, and power cannot be read from standard regression intuition. A Monte Carlo experiment simulates the statistic under a stated data-generating process, sample size, initial condition, and replication count, then examines whether a nominal 5% test has the intended size and how near roots, negative MA errors, or breaks change power and size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
