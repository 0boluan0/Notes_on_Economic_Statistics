## 1.1. 自回归移动平均模型ARMA(p,q) model
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1. Autoregressive moving-average models: ARMA$(p,q)$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|ARMA]](p.q)模型
>形如:$$y_{t}=a_{0}+\sum_{i=1}^p a_{i}Y_{t-i}+\sum_{i=0}^q\beta_{i}\epsilon_{t-i}$$
>
> *  $y_t$：时间序列在$t$时刻的观测值。
> * $a_0$：常数项（截距）。
> *  $a_i$：自回归部分的系数，描述当前值对过去值的依赖程度。
> * $p$：自回归部分的阶数（AR阶）。
> *  $\beta_i$：移动平均部分的系数，描述当前值对过去随机扰动的依赖程度。
> *  $q$：移动平均部分的阶数（MA阶）。
> *  $\epsilon_t$：序列${\varepsilon_t}$称为 ARMA(p,q) 模型的**创新项**（innovation），是该过程的基本驱动。在本章中，我们假定${\varepsilon_t}$是**[[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#弱平稳与白噪声|白噪声过程]]**（white noise process）。白噪声（随机扰动），通常假设$E(\epsilon_t) = 0$，方差为常数$\sigma^2$，并且不同时刻之间不相关。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An **[[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|ARMA]]$(p,q)$ model** has the form
> $$y_t=a_0+\sum_{i=1}^p a_i y_{t-i}+\sum_{i=0}^q\beta_i\varepsilon_{t-i}.$$
> * $y_t$ is the observation at time $t$.
> * $a_0$ is the intercept.
> * $a_i$ are the autoregressive coefficients, and $p$ is the AR order.
> * $\beta_i$ are the moving-average coefficients, and $q$ is the MA order.
> * $\varepsilon_t$ is the **innovation** that drives the process. In this chapter, $\{\varepsilon_t\}$ is assumed to be [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#弱平稳与白噪声|white noise]]: it has mean zero, constant variance $\sigma^2$, and zero autocovariance at nonzero lags.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->


参见：ARMA · [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ACF 与 PACF|自相关函数]] · [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ACF 与 PACF|偏自相关函数]] · 白噪声过程

### 1.1.1. AR过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1.1. AR process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自回归([[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|AR]])部分由公式中的$$\sum_{i=1}^p a_i  y_{t-i}$$
表示，说明当前值$y_t$与其前$p$期的观测值之间存在线性依赖关系。系数$a_i$反映了过去第$i$期对当前时刻$t$的影响大小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The autoregressive ([[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|AR]]) component is
$$\sum_{i=1}^p a_i y_{t-i}.$$
It makes $y_t$ depend linearly on its previous $p$ values; $a_i$ measures the contribution of the value observed $i$ periods earlier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.1.2. MA过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1.2. MA process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

 **移动平均（MA）部分**由公式中的$$\sum_{i=0}^q \beta_i  \epsilon_{t-i}$$
表示，说明当前值$y_t$还会受到前$q$期随机扰动（噪声）的影响。这里的$\beta_0$对应当期扰动$\epsilon_t$对$y_t$的直接影响，$\beta_1$对应上一期扰动$\epsilon_{t-1}$对$y_t$的影响，以此类推。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **moving-average (MA) component** is
$$\sum_{i=0}^q\beta_i\varepsilon_{t-i}.$$
It lets the current observation depend on the current innovation and the previous $q$ innovations. Thus $\beta_0$ is the immediate effect of $\varepsilon_t$, $\beta_1$ is the effect of $\varepsilon_{t-1}$, and so on.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.1.3. ARIMA过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1.3. ARIMA process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] ARIMA过程
>如果特征根（characteristic root）中有一个等于1，那么${y_t}$是一个**单位根过程**（integrated process），模型称为 [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ARIMA 作为差分后的 ARMA|ARIMA]] 模型（即自回归积分移动平均模型，Autoregressive Integrated Moving Average model）。这种情况下需要差分才能平稳。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If an AR characteristic root equals one, $\{y_t\}$ contains a **unit root** and is integrated rather than stationary in levels. The corresponding model is an [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ARIMA 作为差分后的 ARMA|ARIMA]] model—an autoregressive integrated moving-average model—and the series must be differenced to obtain stationarity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 三种‘没有关系’的辨析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2. Three notions of “no relationship”*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先明确三种‘没有关系’的定义:
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First distinguish three notions that impose progressively weaker restrictions on temporal dependence:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

 >[!note] 1. 独立同分布I.I.D independent and identically distributed
 >若序列 $\varepsilon_1, \varepsilon_2, \dots$ 独立同分布，则称 ${\varepsilon_t}$ 为 i.i.d. 过程。这通常用于研究模型的概率结构（特别在非线性时间序列分析中）。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A sequence $\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2,\ldots$ is **independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.)** when its elements are mutually independent and share the same distribution. This strong assumption is often used to specify a model's full probabilistic structure, especially in nonlinear time-series analysis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 2. 鞅差序列 M.D.S martingale difference sequence
> 若在概率空间 $(S, \mathcal{B}, P)$ 上，满足 $E(\varepsilon_t \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1}) = 0$ 几乎处处（a.s.），其中 $\mathcal{F}_{t-1}$ 表示 $t-1$ 时刻的信息（$\sigma$-代数），则 ${\varepsilon_t}$ 为鞅差序列。m.d.s. 更贴近经济理论。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A sequence $\{\varepsilon_t\}$ is a **martingale difference sequence (MDS)** with respect to the information filtration $\{\mathcal F_t\}$ if
> $$E(\varepsilon_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})=0\quad\text{a.s.}$$
> Here $\mathcal F_{t-1}$ represents the information available by time $t-1$. This condition is often closer to restrictions implied by economic theory.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 3. [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#弱平稳与白噪声|白噪声过程]]（White Noise process）：
>若过程${\varepsilon_t}$满足对于任意时间$t$都有$E(\varepsilon_t) = 0$、$E(\varepsilon_t^2) = \sigma^2$、并且对于任意不等于0的滞后$s$和任意$j$都有$E(\varepsilon_t \varepsilon_{t-s}) = E(\varepsilon_{t-j}\varepsilon_{t-j-s}) = 0$，则称其为白噪声过程 。也就是说，白噪声是均值为0、方差为常数$\sigma^2$且不同时期误差不相关的过程（若进一步假定正态分布，则是经典的高斯白噪声）。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A process $\{\varepsilon_t\}$ is [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#弱平稳与白噪声|white noise]] if $E(\varepsilon_t)=0$, $E(\varepsilon_t^2)=\sigma^2$ for every $t$, and $E(\varepsilon_t\varepsilon_{t-s})=0$ for every nonzero lag $s$. It therefore has zero mean, constant variance, and no serial correlation. If it is also Gaussian, it is Gaussian white noise.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参见：独立同分布（i.i.d.） · 鞅差序列 · 白噪声过程

>[!note] 鞅

==当$E(\varepsilon_t^2)=\sigma^2$（方差有限且期望相同）时，有：独立同分布（均值为0）$\Rightarrow$ 鞅差序列 $\Rightarrow$ [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#弱平稳与白噪声|白噪声]] 。==<span style="color: yellow;">一般而言，i.i.d. 序列必定是 m.d.s.，m.d.s. 序列一定是白噪声，但反之不成立。</span>
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==When $E(\varepsilon_t^2)=\sigma^2<\infty$, a zero-mean i.i.d. sequence is a martingale difference sequence, and a martingale difference sequence is [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#弱平稳与白噪声|white noise]]: i.i.d. $\Rightarrow$ MDS $\Rightarrow$ white noise.== <span style="color: yellow;">The reverse implications do not generally hold.</span>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!quote] 证明I.I.D , M.D.S , 白噪声之间的包含关系
> 独立同分布且均值为0显然满足鞅差条件（因为独立性和零均值蕴含了条件期望为0）；而鞅差序列进一步蕴含零均值和不相关性（通过迭代律可证明）。因此i.i.d.是最强假设，鞅差次之，白噪声更弱。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A zero-mean i.i.d. sequence satisfies the martingale-difference condition because independence and a zero mean imply a zero conditional mean. A martingale difference sequence, in turn, has mean zero and is serially uncorrelated, as follows from the law of iterated expectations. Under the constant finite-variance condition stated above, it is therefore white noise. Thus i.i.d. is the strongest of these assumptions, MDS is weaker, and white noise is weaker still.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] [[有效市场假说与事件研究|有效市场假说]]（Efficient Market Hypothesis, EMH）
>令$r_t$表示某资产的回报率。在有效市场条件下，有：$$E(r_t - \mu \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1}) = 0$$
>其中$\mu = E(r_t)$是回报率的均值 。
>
>    直观理解:有效市场假说认为资产收益不可被历史信息预测。这实际上说明收益序列${r_t}$在给定过去信息$\mathcal{F}_{t-1}$的条件下无偏（条件期望为零），因此${r_t - \mu}$可被视为鞅差序列。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $r_t$ denote an asset's return. Under an efficient market,
> $$E(r_t-\mu\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})=0,$$
> where $\mu=E(r_t)$ is the mean return.
>
> **Intuition:** past information cannot be used to predict excess returns. Conditional on the information available at $t-1$, $r_t-\mu$ has mean zero and can therefore be treated as a martingale difference sequence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] 鞅差序列但非独立同分布的例子 (M.D.S. but not i.i.d.)
>考虑ARCH(1)过程：$$X_t = \sigma_t e_t \qquad \sigma_t^2 = \alpha_0 + \alpha_1 X_{t-1}^2$$
>其中$e_t \sim i.i.d.,N(0,1)$（零均值、方差1的独立同分布序列） 。
>
>可以验证：由于$E(X_t \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1}) = 0$(这个是因为e是独立的且为0,0乘任何数都为0)，${X_t}$是一个鞅差序列；但由于条件方差$E(X_t^2 \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1}) = \sigma_t^2 = \alpha_0 + \alpha_1 X_{t-1}^2$依赖于过去信息，不是常数.
>因此${X_t}$并非独立同分布过程。这说明鞅差序列不一定是 i.i.d.，[[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH#ARCH 与 GARCH|ARCH]] 模型即为鞅差而非独立同分布的典型例子。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider the ARCH(1) process
> $$X_t = \sigma_t e_t \qquad \sigma_t^2 = \alpha_0 + \alpha_1 X_{t-1}^2,$$
> where $e_t \overset{\mathrm{i.i.d.}}{\sim} N(0,1)$.
>
> Because $e_t$ is independent of past information and has mean zero,
> $$E(X_t \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1})=\sigma_tE(e_t\mid\mathcal{F}_{t-1})=0.$$
> Hence, $\{X_t\}$ is a martingale difference sequence. Its conditional variance,
> $$E(X_t^2 \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1})=\sigma_t^2=\alpha_0+\alpha_1X_{t-1}^2,$$
> depends on past information and is not constant, so $\{X_t\}$ is not i.i.d. The [[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH#ARCH 与 GARCH|ARCH]] model is therefore a standard example of a martingale difference process whose observations are not independently and identically distributed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] 白噪声但非鞅差序列的例子 (White Noise but not M.D.S.)
>考虑非线性移动平均过程：
$$X_t = \alpha e_{t-1} e_{t-2} + e_t \qquad e_t \sim i.i.d.N(0,1)$$
可以验证：
(1) $E(X_t) = 0$；
(2) $E(X_t^2) = 1 + \alpha^2$；
(3) 对于任何非零滞后$s$，$E(X_t X_{t-s}) = 0$ 。
因此${X_t}$满足白噪声的所有条件。
然而，计算其条件期望：$E(X_t \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1}) = \alpha, e_{t-1} e_{t-2} \neq 0$（除非$\alpha=0$），这表明${X_t}$并非鞅差序列 。
该例说明白噪声过程不一定是鞅差序列——尽管过程各期不相关，但存在非零的条件期望结构。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider the nonlinear moving-average process
> $$X_t=\alpha e_{t-1}e_{t-2}+e_t,\qquad e_t\overset{\mathrm{i.i.d.}}{\sim}N(0,1).$$
> One can verify that (1) $E(X_t)=0$; (2) $E(X_t^2)=1+\alpha^2$; and (3) $E(X_tX_{t-s})=0$ for every nonzero lag $s$. Thus $\{X_t\}$ is white noise.
>
> Its conditional mean is nevertheless
> $$E(X_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})=\alpha e_{t-1}e_{t-2},$$
> which is generally nonzero unless $\alpha=0$. Hence $\{X_t\}$ is not a martingale difference sequence. The example shows that serial uncorrelatedness does not rule out predictable nonlinear dependence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前三项可直接代入定义验证，过程从略。~~这个例子中的前三项好计算,所以略.~~
条件期望 $E(X_t \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1})$的计算:
$$X_t = \alpha e_{t-1} e_{t-2} + e_t$$
其中：
• $e_t$ 是独立的、**不可预测的噪声项**，所以在 $\mathcal{F}_{t-1}$ 下它的期望是0；
• $e_{t-1}, e_{t-2}$ 是过去的值，**在** $\mathcal{F}_{t-1}$ **中是已知的**。
所以：
$$E(X_t \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1}) = \alpha e_{t-1} e_{t-2} + E(e_t \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1}) = \alpha e_{t-1} e_{t-2}$$
这个不为0（除非 $\alpha$ = 0），因此不是鞅差序列。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first three claims follow directly from the definition, so their calculations are omitted. To compute the conditional expectation $E(X_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})$, write
$$X_t = \alpha e_{t-1} e_{t-2} + e_t$$
- $e_t$ is an independent, **unpredictable innovation**, so $E(e_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})=0$;
- $e_{t-1}$ and $e_{t-2}$ are past values and are therefore **known given** $\mathcal F_{t-1}$.

Therefore,
$$E(X_t \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1}) = \alpha e_{t-1} e_{t-2} + E(e_t \mid \mathcal{F}_{t-1}) = \alpha e_{t-1} e_{t-2}$$
This is generally nonzero unless $\alpha=0$, so the process is not a martingale difference sequence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1. 平稳性定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Definition of stationarity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在介绍平稳性之前,先引入两个概念:
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before introducing stationarity, we introduce two concepts:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **总体平均（ensemble average）**：同时观察多条时间序列，固定时间计算平均。
	例：在时间 $t$，4 台机器的平均：$$\bar{y}_t = \frac{1}{4}\sum_{i=1}^{4} y_{it}$$这里需要知道多个时间序列（多台机器）的信息。
2. **时间平均（time average）**：通常我们只有一条时间序列的观测（如一台机器的连续产出），我们计算同一台机器在多个时间点的平均：$$\bar{y} = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T} y_{1t}$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** An **ensemble average** holds time fixed and averages across many realizations. For four machines observed at time $t$,<br>
   $$\bar y_t=\frac14\sum_{i=1}^4y_{it}.$$
**2.** A **time average** follows one realization across many dates. For one machine observed for $T$ periods,<br>
   $$\bar y=\frac1T\sum_{t=1}^Ty_{1t}.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] [[平稳性、遍历性与谱#严平稳与宽平稳|平稳性]]
>如果${y_t}$是一个平稳时间序列，那么充分长的单条序列的时间均值、方差、自相关等将近似于总体的对应值 。即在平稳性条件下，一条实现（realization）的长时间观测可以替代大量同时独立样本的集合平均。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a [[平稳性、遍历性与谱#严平稳与宽平稳|stationary]] process, the relevant distributional properties do not change with calendar time. A sufficiently long realization can be used to recover ensemble moments only with an additional **ergodicity** condition; stationarity by itself is not enough.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平稳分为两种:
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are two types of stationarity:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 1. 弱平稳（弱平稳性，协方差平稳性，二阶平稳性，宽平稳性）
>一个随机过程如果满足：
>
> 1. **有限常数均值**：对任意 $t$，$E(y_t) = \mu$，且 $\mu$ 为常数（与 $t$ 无关）；
>
> 2. **有限常数方差**：对任意 $t$，$\mathrm{Var}(y_t) = E[(y_t - \mu)^2] = \sigma^2$（与 $t$ 无关）；
>
> 3. **自协方差仅与滞后有关**：对任意 $t$ 和滞后 $s$，$\mathrm{Cov}(y_t, y_{t-s}) = E[(y_t - \mu)(y_{t-s} - \mu)] = \gamma_s$（仅与 $s$ 有关，而与具体 $t$ 无关）。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A process is **weakly stationary**—also called covariance-stationary, second-order stationary, or wide-sense stationary—if:
> **1.** it has a finite, time-invariant mean, $E(y_t)=\mu$;<br>
> **2.** it has a finite, time-invariant variance, $\operatorname{Var}(y_t)=\sigma^2$; and<br>
> **3.** its autocovariance depends only on the lag: $\operatorname{Cov}(y_t,y_{t-s})=\gamma_s$, independent of the calendar date $t$.<br>
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满足以上条件则称 ${y_t}$ 协方差平稳（covariance stationary），也称**弱平稳（weakly stationary）或二阶平稳（second-order stationary）**。第三条也意味着对于任何相隔 $j$ 期的数据，协方差同样只取决于相隔期数 $j$（即时间平移不改变协方差）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A process satisfying these conditions is covariance-stationary, or equivalently weakly or second-order stationary. The third condition says that shifting a pair of observations together in calendar time leaves their covariance unchanged; only their separation matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] [[平稳性、遍历性与谱#自协方差、相关与谱|Autocorrelation]] 标准化后的协方差：
> $$\rho_s = \frac{\gamma_s}{\gamma_0}$$
> 其中 $\gamma_0 = \text{Var}(y_t) = \sigma^2$，所以 $\rho_s \in [-1, 1]$，用来表示“记忆程度”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[平稳性、遍历性与谱#自协方差、相关与谱|Autocorrelation]] is standardized autocovariance:
> $$\rho_s = \frac{\gamma_s}{\gamma_0}$$
> where $\gamma_0=\operatorname{Var}(y_t)=\sigma^2$. Thus $\rho_s\in[-1,1]$ and measures the strength and direction of linear dependence at lag $s$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.2.1. [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|AR]](1)的平稳性判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2.1. Stationarity of an [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|AR]](1) process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参见：AR(1) 过程的平稳性

考虑AR(1)过程：
$$y_t = a_0 + a_1 y_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the AR(1) process:
$$y_t = a_0 + a_1 y_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中${\varepsilon_t}$为白噪声过程。假设初始值$y_0$已知且过程从$t=0$开始 。通过递归展开，可以得到$y_t$的通解：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $\{\varepsilon_t\}$ is white noise. Suppose the initial value $y_0$ is known and the process begins at $t=0$. Recursive substitution gives the general solution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_t = a_0 \sum_{i=0}^{t-1} a_1^i + a_1^ty_0 + \sum_{i=0}^{t-1} a_1^i \varepsilon_{t-i}.
$$

上式第一项是从0到$t-1$期累积的常数项贡献，第二项是初始值$y_0$经过$t$期自回归的影响，第三项是$t$期之前各个创新$\varepsilon$经过衰减后的贡献 。根据此解：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term accumulates the intercept, the second carries the effect of the initial condition through $t$ autoregressive steps, and the third accumulates innovations with geometrically decaying weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 均值：取期望$E(y_t)$，由于$E(\varepsilon_{t-i})=0$，得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Mean:** because $E(\varepsilon_{t-i})=0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$E(y_t) = a_0 \sum_{i=0}^{t-1} a_1^i + a_1^t E(y_0)$$
显然$E(y_t)$依赖于$t$本身（除非$a_1=1$或$a_0=0$等特例），一般情况下均值随时间变化。类似地，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$$E(y_t)=a_0\sum_{i=0}^{t-1}a_1^i+a_1^tE(y_0).$$
For an arbitrary initial condition this expression generally depends on $t$. Likewise,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$E(y_{t+s}) = a_0 \sum_{i=0}^{t+s-1} a_1^i + a_1^{t+s} E(y_0)$$

可以看到$E(y_{t+s}) \neq E(y_t)$，因此${y_t}$在有初始值影响时**不平稳** 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unless the initial distribution is chosen to be the stationary one, $E(y_{t+s})\neq E(y_t)$ in finite samples, so the initialized process is not yet covariance-stationary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 然而，如果$|a_1| < 1$（AR(1)系数的绝对值小于1），则随着$t \to \infty$，$a_1^t \to 0$，上述表达式趋于稳定值。具体地，当$t \to \infty$时，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- If $|a_1|<1$, then $a_1^t\to0$, so the effect of the initial condition disappears. The process approaches the stationary representation below in distribution and moments; the random sequence need not converge pathwise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\lim_{t \to \infty} y_t = \frac{a_0}{1 - a_1} + \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} a_1^i \varepsilon_{t-i}$$

右侧包含两部分：$\frac{a_0}{1-a_1}$是均衡水平或**无条件均值**，第二项是无限长的加权误差序列（相当于MA($\infty$)表示） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term, $a_0/(1-a_1)$, is the equilibrium level or **unconditional mean**. The second is an infinite weighted innovation sequence—an MA$(\infty)$ representation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **均值：** 对上式取期望可得，当$t$充分大时，$E(y_t) \approx \frac{a_0}{1 - a_1} \equiv \mu$ 。也就是当$|a_1|<1$时，AR(1)过程存在有限均值$\mu = \frac{a_0}{1-a_1}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Mean:** once the initial-condition effect is negligible, $E(y_t)\approx a_0/(1-a_1)\equiv\mu$. Thus a stationary AR(1) process with $|a_1|<1$ has finite mean $\mu=a_0/(1-a_1)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **方差：** 将$y_t$表示为均值$\mu$加上扰动，即$y_t = \mu + ( \varepsilon_t + a_1 \varepsilon_{t-1} + a_1^2 \varepsilon_{t-2} + \cdots )$。因为$\varepsilon$是白噪声，互不相关且方差$\sigma^2$，可以求得该无限序列的方差（假设$|a_1|<1$保证和收敛）：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Variance:** write $y_t=\mu+\varepsilon_t+a_1\varepsilon_{t-1}+a_1^2\varepsilon_{t-2}+\cdots$. Since the innovations are uncorrelated with common variance $\sigma^2$, and $|a_1|<1$ makes the geometric sum converge,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\mathrm{Var}(y_t) = E[(y_t - \mu)^2] = \sigma^2 \left(1 + a_1^2 + a_1^4 + \cdots \right) = \frac{\sigma^2}{1 - a_1^2}. $$

（推导：$\mathrm{Var}(\varepsilon_t + a_1\varepsilon_{t-1} + \ldots) = \sigma^2(1 + a_1^2 + a_1^4 + \ldots)$，该等比级数和为$(1-a_1^2)^{-1}$ 。）因此当$|a_1|<1$时方差有限且不随时间变，是常数$\sigma^2/(1-a_1^2)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cross terms vanish because white-noise innovations at different dates are uncorrelated. The remaining geometric series sums to $(1-a_1^2)^{-1}$, so the variance is the time-invariant constant $\sigma^2/(1-a_1^2)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **协方差：** 对于任意滞后$s$，考虑$y_t - \mu = \varepsilon_t + a_1 \varepsilon_{t-1} + a_1^2 \varepsilon_{t-2} + \cdots$和$y_{t-s} - \mu = \varepsilon_{t-s} + a_1 \varepsilon_{t-s-1} + \cdots$，则协方差$E[(y_t-\mu)(y_{t-s}-\mu)]$可以通过展开二者的乘积并利用白噪声不相关性计算得到：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Covariance:** For any lag $s$, write $y_t-\mu=\varepsilon_t+a_1\varepsilon_{t-1}+a_1^2\varepsilon_{t-2}+\cdots$ and $y_{t-s}-\mu=\varepsilon_{t-s}+a_1\varepsilon_{t-s-1}+\cdots$. Expanding their product and using the fact that white-noise innovations are uncorrelated gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\gamma_s = E[(y_t-\mu)(y_{t-s}-\mu)] = \sigma^2 \left(a_1^s + a_1^{s+2} + a_1^{s+4} + \cdots \right) = \frac{\sigma^2a_1^s}{1 - a_1^2}. $$

推导中只有$\varepsilon_{t}\cdot \varepsilon_{t-s}$通过$s$次滞后得到共同项，其系数$a_1^s$；再加上更长滞后的乘积项$a_1^{s+2},a_1^{s+4},\ldots$，求和即得上述结果 。因此**自协方差**$\gamma_s = \sigma^2 a_1^s/(1-a_1^2)$，取决于滞后$s$但不依赖$t$。对应的**自相关系数**$\rho_s = a_1^s$（因为$\gamma_0 = \sigma^2/(1-a_1^2)$）。这说明当$|a_1|<1$时，AR(1)模型的自相关函数随滞后$s$指数衰减。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The overlapping innovation terms have coefficients $a_1^s,a_1^{s+2},a_1^{s+4},\ldots$; summing this geometric series yields the expression above. Thus the **autocovariance** $\gamma_s=\sigma^2a_1^s/(1-a_1^2)$ depends on the lag $s$ but not on time $t$. Since $\gamma_0=\sigma^2/(1-a_1^2)$, the corresponding **autocorrelation coefficient** is $\rho_s=a_1^s$. When $|a_1|<1$, the AR(1) autocorrelation function therefore decays geometrically with the lag.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==综上，当$|a_1|<1$且忽略初始条件的影响（或假定过程从无限远的过去开始），AR(1)过程达到平稳状态：$y_t$具有常数均值$\mu = a_0/(1-a_1)$、常数方差$\sigma^2/(1-a_1^2)$，[[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ACF 与 PACF|自相关函数]]$\rho_s = a_1^s$只由$s$决定 。反之，如果$|a_1|\ge 1$，则均值或方差发散，过程不平稳。
这意味着：**平稳 AR(1)** 需要模型的齐次解部分衰减为 0（或等价于“过程从无限过去开始”），且 $|a_1| < 1$。==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==In summary, if $|a_1|<1$ and the process is initialized in its stationary distribution—or the effect of a finite initial condition is allowed to vanish—then AR(1) has mean $\mu=a_0/(1-a_1)$, variance $\sigma^2/(1-a_1^2)$, and [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ACF 与 PACF|autocorrelation function]] $\rho_s=a_1^s$. If $|a_1|\ge1$, the usual finite-variance stationary solution does not exist. Thus stationarity requires the homogeneous component to decay, which is equivalent to $|a_1|<1$.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.2.2. 一般的ARMA(p,q)的平稳性判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2.2. Stationarity of a general ARMA$(p,q)$ process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
#必考
对于一般的 [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|ARMA]](p,q) 模型，平稳性同样要求齐次解为0，即模型**无单位根**。通常，只需关注 [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|AR]] 部分的根是否在单位圆内，因为 MA 部分（包括有限或无限阶）不会影响协方差平稳性条件。具体来说：==ARMA(p,q) 模型的平稳性仅取决于 AR 部分的特征根==。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Required*
For a general [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|ARMA]]($p,q$) model, stationarity requires the homogeneous AR component to decay, so the AR polynomial has no unit root. Only the [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|AR]] part determines stationarity; a finite MA component does not change the covariance-stationarity condition. In the lag-polynomial convention, every root of the AR polynomial must lie **outside** the unit circle; equivalently, every companion-matrix eigenvalue must lie **inside** it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必要充分条件：一个无限长的MA过程（即$y_t = \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} \beta_i \varepsilon_{t-i}$）是平稳的，当且仅当以下两个条件成立 ：
(i) $\sum_{i=0}^{\infty} \beta_i^2 < \infty$；
(ii) 对于所有$s>0$，$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \beta_k \beta_{s+k}$存在有限值 。
第一条件确保方差有限，第二条件确保协方差收敛。这两个条件实际上要求**脉冲响应序列**${\beta_i}$绝对平方可和。==实际上，利用柯西-施瓦茨不等式可以证明条件(ii)自动成立于条件(i) 。因此只要${\beta_i}$平方和收敛，则协方差也有限==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the infinite moving-average process $y_t=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\beta_i\varepsilon_{t-i}$, finite second moments require
(i) $\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}\beta_i^2<\infty$,
and the lag-$s$ autocovariance involves
(ii) $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\beta_k\beta_{s+k}$ for each $s>0$.
Condition (i) makes the variance finite. By Cauchy–Schwarz, it also guarantees that the sum in (ii) is finite. Hence square summability of the impulse-response coefficients is the key condition for covariance stationarity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!quote] 一个例子的具体推导
>考虑一个具体模型 [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|ARMA]](2,1)：
> $$y_t = a_1 y_{t-1} + a_2 y_{t-2} + \varepsilon_t + \beta_1 \varepsilon_{t-1}$$
>
> 假设其AR部分的特征根满足$|r|<1$（即$1 - a_1 z - a_2 z^2 = 0$的根在单位圆外），我们尝试将$y_t$表示为无限MA形式。假设一个特解为：
>
> $$y_t = \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} c_i \varepsilon_{t-i}$$
>
> 即$y_t$表示为当前及各滞后误差的线性组合 。将此表达代入原方程(2)，并令两边对应滞后项的系数相等，可得到递推关系 ：
> • $c_0 = 1$，因为$y_t$中当期$\varepsilon_t$的系数应为1（对应方程右端$\varepsilon_t$）
> • $c_1 = a_1 c_0 + \beta_1$，因为$y_t$中$\varepsilon_{t-1}$的系数$c_1$来源于两个部分：一是原方程左侧$a_1 y_{t-1}$中的$\varepsilon_{t-1}$贡献（$a_1 c_0$），二是右侧$\beta_1 \varepsilon_{t-1}$ ；
> • 对于$i \ge 2$，有递推：$c_i = a_1 c_{i-1} + a_2 c_{i-2}$ 。这是因为$y_t$中$\varepsilon_{t-i}$可以通过两条途径产生：从$y_{t-1}$迁移（系数$a_1 c_{i-1}$）或从$y_{t-2}$迁移（系数$a_2 c_{i-2}$），右侧没有直接$\varepsilon_{t-i}$项（当$i\ge2$时）。该递推实际上与AR部分的特征方程对应。
>
> **Remark:** 若特征方程的根在单位圆内，则${c_i}$序列**几何收敛**于0 。也就是说，当$|r|<1$时，$c_i$会随着$i$增长快速减小。这确保了$y_t$的MA($\infty$)表示中各滞后误差的影响逐渐衰减，从而总方差收敛。特别地，如果根在单位圆内，序列${c_i}$是平方可和的（即$\sum_{i=0}^\infty c_i^2 < \infty$） 。
>
> 在此条件下，我们验证ARMA(2,1)模型达到平稳：对任意$t$和$s$，根据$y_t = \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} c_i \varepsilon_{t-i}$：
>
> • 均值：$E(y_t) = \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} c_i E(\varepsilon_{t-i}) = 0$（因为白噪声均值0）。同理$E(y_{t-s})=0$，均值不随时间 。
>
> • 方差：$E(y_t^2) = E!\Big[\big(\sum_{i\ge0} c_i \varepsilon_{t-i}\big)^2\Big] = \sigma^2 \sum_{i\ge0} c_i^2$（利用不相关性，仅保留$\mathrm{Var}(\varepsilon)$项） 。由于${c_i}$平方可和，上式收敛于某常数，因此方差有限且与$t$无关 。
>
> • 协方差：对于滞后$s>0$，
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \mathrm{Cov}(y_ty_{t-s}) &= E(y_ty_{t-s}) = E\Big[\Big(\sum_{i\ge0} c_i \varepsilon_{t-i}\Big)\Big(\sum_{j\ge0} c_j \varepsilon_{t-s-j}\Big)\Big] \
> &= \sigma^2(c_s + c_{s+1}c_1 + c_{s+2}c_2 + \cdots)
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> 其中展开乘积后，只有$\varepsilon_{t-k}$与$\varepsilon_{t-k}$（当两和式索引满足$i = s+j$）项有非零期望，其贡献为$\sigma^2 c_i c_j$。整理得到上式 。由于${c_i}$序列收敛，在$s$固定时，上述和是有限常数，并且显然不依赖$t$，仅依赖于$s$ 。因此协方差$\gamma_s$不随时间，是滞后$s$的函数。特别地，当$s=0$时恢复方差$\sigma^2\sum c_i^2$。
>
> 综上，对于ARMA(2,1)模型，只要方程(2)的特征根$|r|<1$，则$y_t$的均值、方差、协方差都与时间无关，过程是协方差平稳的 。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider the ARMA(2,1) model
> $$y_t = a_1 y_{t-1} + a_2 y_{t-2} + \varepsilon_t + \beta_1 \varepsilon_{t-1}$$
> Assume that the roots $z$ of the AR lag polynomial $1-a_1z-a_2z^2=0$ lie outside the unit circle. Equivalently, the roots $r$ of the coefficient recursion $r^2-a_1r-a_2=0$ satisfy $|r|<1$. We then seek the following infinite-MA representation:
> $$y_t = \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} c_i \varepsilon_{t-i}$$
> Equating coefficients of each innovation gives
> - $c_0=1$;
> - $c_1=a_1c_0+\beta_1$; and
> - $c_i=a_1c_{i-1}+a_2c_{i-2}$ for $i\ge2$.
> The last relation is governed by the AR characteristic equation.
> **Remark:** When the recursion roots satisfy $|r|<1$, the coefficients ${c_i}$ converge geometrically to zero. Thus the influence of older innovations in the MA($\infty$) representation decays, and ${c_i}$ is square-summable: $\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}c_i^2<\infty$. This is the same condition expressed above by lag-polynomial roots outside the unit circle.
> Under this condition, the ARMA(2,1) model is covariance-stationary. From $y_t=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}c_i\varepsilon_{t-i}$:
> - Mean: $E(y_t)=\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}c_iE(\varepsilon_{t-i})=0$ because the innovations have zero mean. Likewise $E(y_{t-s})=0$, so the mean is time-invariant.
> - Variance: $E(y_t^2)=\sigma^2\sum_{i\ge0}c_i^2$, which is finite and independent of $t$ because the coefficients are square-summable.
> - Covariance: for a fixed lag $s>0$, expanding the product leaves only pairs that refer to the same innovation, with $i=s+j$. Their contributions sum to $\sigma^2\sum_{j\ge0}c_{s+j}c_j$, which is finite by square summability and depends only on $s$, not on $t$. At $s=0$ this reduces to the variance $\sigma^2\sum c_i^2$.
> Therefore the mean, variance, and autocovariance are time-invariant, and the ARMA(2,1) process is covariance-stationary whenever the recursion roots satisfy $|r|<1$—equivalently, whenever the AR lag-polynomial roots lie outside the unit circle.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. ACF与PACF
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. ACF and PACF*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 3.1 定义
>
> >[!note] 自相关系数 ([[平稳性、遍历性与谱#自协方差、相关与谱|autocorrelation]]  function [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ACF 与 PACF|ACF]])
> >定义**自相关系数** $\rho_s = \frac{\gamma_s}{\gamma_0}$。显然 $\rho_0 = 1$。通常通过 **自相关图（correlogram）** 描绘 $\rho_s$ 随 $s$ 变化的情况，用于识别模型。(以防我忘了:$\gamma$是方差、自协方差值)
>
> >[!note] 偏自相关系数 ([[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ACF 与 PACF|Partial]] ACF   PACF)
> > $y_t$与$y_{t-s}$之间扣除了所有介于两者之间的滞后变量影响后的相关程度。
>
> PACF使用ACF计算
>
> ==回归法构建==
>
> 1. 对序列去均值，得中心化序列${y^*_t}$（$y^*_t = y_t - \bar{y}$） 。
> 2. 做一个一阶自回归回归：$y^*_t = \phi_{11}y^*_{t-1} + e_t$。此时$\phi_{11}$恰好等于样本自相关$\rho_1$，也是偏自相关$\alpha_1$，因为在只有1阶滞后的模型中，自相关就是直接相关 。
> 3. 为计算二阶偏自相关，控制$y_{t-1}$的影响，即进行二阶回归：$y^*_t = \phi_{21} y^*_{t-1} + \phi_{22} y^*_{t-2} + e_t$。此时$\phi_{22}$即为$y_t$与$y_{t-2}$在给定$y_{t-1}$情况下的偏自相关，即$\alpha_2$ 。
> 4. 类似地，可以构建高阶$p$的回归：$y^*_t = \phi_{p1} y^*_{t-1} + \phi_{p2} y^*_{t-2} + \cdots + \phi_{pp} y^*_{t-p} + e_t$，则$\phi_{pp}$就是滞后$p$的偏自相关$\alpha_p$ 。
>
> • $\phi_{11} = \rho_1$ 。
> • $\displaystyle \phi_{22} = \frac{\rho_2 - \rho_1^2}{1 - \rho_1^2}$ 。这是二阶偏自相关系数的公式：将$y_1$对$y_2$的影响“净掉”后计算$y_2$与$y_0$的相关。
>
> ==行列式法构建==
>
> 还可以通过行列式公式直接表达低阶 PACF。令自相关矩阵
>
> $$
> \mathbf{R}k =
> \begin{pmatrix}
> 1      & \rho(1) & \rho(2) & \cdots & \rho(k-1)\\
> \rho(1)& 1       & \rho(1) & \cdots & \rho(k-2)\\
> \vdots & \vdots  & \vdots  & \ddots & \vdots     \\
> \rho(k-1)&\rho(k-2)&\rho(k-3)&\cdots &1
> \end{pmatrix}
> $$
>
> 并令向量
> $$
> \boldsymbol{\rho}k =
> \bigl(\rho(1),\rho(2),\dots,\rho(k)\bigr)^\top
> $$
> 则
> $$
> \alpha(k)
> =\frac{\det
> \begin{pmatrix}
> \mathbf{R}{k-1} & \boldsymbol{\rho}_{k-1}\\
> \boldsymbol{\rho}_{k-1}^\top & \rho(k)
> \end{pmatrix}}
> {\det(\mathbf{R}_k)}.
> $$
>
> - 对于 $k=2$，公式简化为
>     $$
>     \alpha(2)
>     =\frac{\rho(2)-\rho(1)^2}{1-\rho(1)^2}.
>     $$
>
> ==Levinson–Durbin 递推算法==
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> Define $\rho_s=\gamma_s/\gamma_0$, with $\rho_0=1$. A **correlogram** plots $\rho_s$ against the lag $s$ and is often used for model identification. Here $\gamma_0$ is the variance and $\gamma_s$ is the lag-$s$ autocovariance.
>
> The lag-$s$ partial autocorrelation is the correlation between $y_t$ and $y_{t-s}$ after removing the linear effects of the intervening lags.
>
> **Computing the PACF from the ACF**
>
> ==Regression construction==
>
> **1.** Center the series: $y_t^*=y_t-\bar y$.<br>
> **2.** Fit $y_t^*=\phi_{11}y_{t-1}^*+e_t$. Then $\phi_{11}=\rho_1=\alpha_1$.<br>
> **3.** To obtain the second-order partial autocorrelation, fit $y_t^*=\phi_{21}y_{t-1}^*+\phi_{22}y_{t-2}^*+e_t$. The coefficient $\phi_{22}$ is $\alpha_2$, the correlation between $y_t$ and $y_{t-2}$ conditional on $y_{t-1}$.<br>
> **4.** More generally, fit $y_t^*=\phi_{p1}y_{t-1}^*+\cdots+\phi_{pp}y_{t-p}^*+e_t$. The final coefficient $\phi_{pp}$ is the lag-$p$ partial autocorrelation $\alpha_p$.<br>
>
> In particular,
> - $\phi_{11}=\rho_1$;
> - $\displaystyle \phi_{22}=\frac{\rho_2-\rho_1^2}{1-\rho_1^2}$.
>
> ==Determinant construction==
>
> Low-order PACFs can also be written directly as determinant ratios using the autocorrelation matrix $\mathbf R_k$ and the vector $\boldsymbol\rho_k=(\rho(1),\ldots,\rho(k))^\top$. For $k=2$, the formula reduces to
> $$\alpha(2)=\frac{\rho(2)-\rho(1)^2}{1-\rho(1)^2}.$$
>

### (1) [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|AR]](1)的ACF
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) ACF of an [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|AR]](1) process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑平稳的 AR(1) 模型（$|a_1|<1$）：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the stationary AR(1) model ($|a_1|<1$):
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$y_t = a_1 y_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t,$$

其中$E(\varepsilon_t)=0,\ \mathrm{Var}(\varepsilon_t)=\sigma^2$且互不相关。根据先前推导，AR(1)的自协方差为$\gamma_s = \frac{\sigma^2 a_1^s}{1 - a_1^2}$。
• $\gamma_0 = \frac{\sigma^2}{1 - a_1^2}$ ；
• $\gamma_s = \frac{\sigma^2 a_1^s}{1 - a_1^2}$，对于$s \ge 1$ 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where the innovations have mean zero, variance $\sigma^2$, and zero autocovariance. As derived above, the AR(1) autocovariances are
- $\gamma_0 = \frac{\sigma^2}{1 - a_1^2}$ ;
- $\gamma_s = \frac{\sigma^2 a_1^s}{1 - a_1^2}$, for $s \ge 1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此**自相关系数**：
• $\rho_0 = 1$；
• $\rho_s = \frac{\gamma_s}{\gamma_0} = a_1^s$（当$s\ge1$） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore, the **autocorrelation coefficients** are
- $\rho_0 = 1$;
- $\rho_s = \frac{\gamma_s}{\gamma_0} = a_1^s$ when $s\ge1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可见AR(1)模型的ACF以滞后阶数的指数形式衰减，$\rho_s = a_1^s$。当$|a_1|<1$时，$\rho_s$随$s$增大几何级数趋向0 。在图形上，自相关函数对滞后作图（亦称**自相关图**或**correlogram**）将表现为指数衰减的形状。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The AR(1) ACF is $\rho_s=a_1^s$, so it decays geometrically to zero when $|a_1|<1$. A **correlogram** therefore shows a gradually decaying tail; if $a_1<0$, the signs alternate as the magnitude decays.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (2) [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|AR]](2)的ACF
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) ACF of an [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|AR]](2) process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于稳定的AR($p$)过程，Yule-Walker方程描述了自相关函数（[[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ACF 与 PACF|ACF]]）和模型参数之间的关系：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a stationary AR($p$) process, the Yule–Walker equations relate the [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ACF 与 PACF|ACF]] to the model coefficients:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般形式为：
$$\gamma_k = \phi_1 \gamma_{k-1} + \phi_2 \gamma_{k-2} + \dots + \phi_p \gamma_{k-p}, \quad k \geq 1$$
这里：
- $\gamma_k = Cov(y_t, y_{t-k})$ 为滞后为 $k$ 时的协方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The general recursion is
$$\gamma_k = \phi_1 \gamma_{k-1} + \phi_2 \gamma_{k-2} + \dots + \phi_p \gamma_{k-p}, \quad k \geq 1$$
where $\gamma_k=\operatorname{Cov}(y_t,y_{t-k})$ is the lag-$k$ autocovariance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体来说，计算ACF的步骤为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For AR(2), calculate the ACF as follows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

 1. **第一步：求解初始协方差 $\gamma_0$、$\gamma_1$**
	对于AR(2)，将下面方程组联立求解：$$\begin{cases}
\gamma_0 = \phi_1 \gamma_1 + \phi_2 \gamma_2 + \sigma^2 \\ \gamma_1 = \phi_1 \gamma_0 + \phi_2 \gamma_1 \\ \gamma_2 = \phi_1 \gamma_1 + \phi_2 \gamma_0 \end{cases}$$
	注意这里有三个方程，三个未知数，可以解出 $\gamma_0,\gamma_1,\gamma_2$。
2. **第二步：计算高阶协方差（$k>2$）**
	通过递推关系：$$\gamma_k = \phi_1 \gamma_{k-1} + \phi_2 \gamma_{k-2}, \quad k>2$$	即可不断求出高阶协方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Solve the initial autocovariances.** Use the three equations<br>
   $$\begin{cases}
\gamma_0 = \phi_1 \gamma_1 + \phi_2 \gamma_2 + \sigma^2 \\ \gamma_1 = \phi_1 \gamma_0 + \phi_2 \gamma_1 \\ \gamma_2 = \phi_1 \gamma_1 + \phi_2 \gamma_0 \end{cases}$$
   to solve for $\gamma_0,\gamma_1,\gamma_2$.
**2.** **Generate higher-order autocovariances.** For $k>2$, apply<br>
   $$\gamma_k=\phi_1\gamma_{k-1}+\phi_2\gamma_{k-2}.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

3. **第三步：转换为ACF**
	自相关函数定义为：$$\rho_k = \frac{\gamma_k}{\gamma_0}$$通过以上计算出的协方差 $\gamma_k$ 即可获得任意滞后的自相关系数 $\rho_k$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** **Step 3: Convert the autocovariances to the ACF.**<br>
   The autocorrelation function is defined by $$\rho_k=\frac{\gamma_k}{\gamma_0}.$$ The autocovariances calculated above therefore determine the autocorrelation coefficient at every lag.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

 >[!example] AR(2)过程的ACF计算 (使用Yule-walker)
 >- AR(2)模型为：$y_t = 0.5 y_{t-1} + 0.2 y_{t-2} + \varepsilon_t$；
 >- 白噪声方差：$\sigma^2 = 1$。
 > 首先列出协方差方程组：
> $$
> \begin{cases}
> \gamma_0 = 0.5 \gamma_1 + 0.2 \gamma_2 + 1 \\
> \gamma_1 = 0.5 \gamma_0 + 0.2 \gamma_1 \\
> \gamma_2 = 0.5 \gamma_1 + 0.2 \gamma_0
> \end{cases}
> $$
> 解第二个方程，得：
> $$
> \gamma_1 - 0.2\gamma_1 = 0.5\gamma_0 \Rightarrow 0.8\gamma_1 = 0.5\gamma_0 \Rightarrow \gamma_1 = 0.625\gamma_0
> $$
> 再解第三个方程：
> $$
> \gamma_2 = 0.5 \gamma_1 + 0.2 \gamma_0 = 0.5(0.625\gamma_0)+0.2\gamma_0=0.3125\gamma_0+0.2\gamma_0=0.5125\gamma_0
> $$
> 将$\gamma_1,\gamma_2$带入第一个方程：
> $$
> \gamma_0 = 0.5(0.625\gamma_0) + 0.2(0.5125\gamma_0) + 1
> $$
> 解方程：
> $$
> \gamma_0 = 0.3125\gamma_0 + 0.1025\gamma_0 + 1 \\
> \Rightarrow \gamma_0 - 0.415\gamma_0 = 1 \\
> \Rightarrow 0.585\gamma_0 = 1 \\
> \Rightarrow \gamma_0 \approx 1.709
> $$
> 则：
> - $\gamma_1=0.625\times 1.709\approx 1.068$
> - $\gamma_2=0.5125\times 1.709\approx 0.876$
> ACF为：
> - $\rho_0=1$ （自相关0阶为1）
> - $\rho_1=\gamma_1/\gamma_0=1.068/1.709\approx 0.625$
> - $\rho_2=\gamma_2/\gamma_0=0.876/1.709\approx 0.5125$
> 更高阶的ACF用递推式依次算出即可。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $y_t=0.5y_{t-1}+0.2y_{t-2}+\varepsilon_t$ with $\sigma^2=1$, the Yule–Walker system above gives $\gamma_1=0.625\gamma_0$ and $\gamma_2=0.5125\gamma_0$. Substituting both into the first equation yields $\gamma_0\approx1.709$, and hence
> - $\gamma_1=0.625\times 1.709\approx 1.068$
> - $\gamma_2=0.5125\times 1.709\approx 0.876$
> Standardizing gives
> - $\rho_0=1$
> - $\rho_1=\gamma_1/\gamma_0=1.068/1.709\approx 0.625$
> - $\rho_2=\gamma_2/\gamma_0=0.876/1.709\approx 0.5125$
> Higher-order autocorrelations follow from the same recursion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!quote] Yule-Walker公式的推导,以AR(2)为例
> 考虑一个平稳的 AR(2) 模型：
> $$y_t = a_1 y_{t-1} + a_2 y_{t-2} + \varepsilon_t,$$
>
> 假定算子方程 $1 - a_1 L - a_2 L^2 = 0$ 的根都在单位圆外（确保过程平稳） 。我们可以利用**Yule-Walker 方程**（尤尔-沃克方程）推导其自相关关系 :
> 将模型两边同乘以$y_{t-s}$并取期望，得到对任意$s \ge 0$：
> 1. 对$s=0$：$E(y_t y_t) = a_1 E(y_{t-1}y_t) + a_2 E(y_{t-2}y_t) + E(\varepsilon_t y_t)$。注意到$E(\varepsilon_t y_t) = E(\varepsilon_t (a_1 y_{t-1} + a_2 y_{t-2} + \varepsilon_t)) = \sigma^2$（因为$E(\varepsilon_t^2)=\sigma^2$且$\varepsilon_t$与$y_{t-1},y_{t-2}$不相关），上式可写为：$$\gamma_0 = a_1 \gamma_1 + a_2 \gamma_2 + \sigma^2.$$
> 2. 对$s=1$：$E(y_t y_{t-1}) = a_1 E(y_{t-1}y_{t-1}) + a_2 E(y_{t-2}y_{t-1}) + E(\varepsilon_t y_{t-1})$。由于$\varepsilon_t$与$y_{t-1}$不相关，最后一项为0，且$E(y_{t-2}y_{t-1}) = \gamma_1$，$E(y_{t-1}y_{t-1}) = \gamma_0$，得到：$$\gamma_1 = a_1 \gamma_0 + a_2 \gamma_1.$$
> 3. 对$s \ge 2$：$E(y_t y_{t-s}) = a_1 E(y_{t-1}y_{t-s}) + a_2 E(y_{t-2}y_{t-s}) + E(\varepsilon_t y_{t-s})$。当$s \ge 2$时，$\varepsilon_t$与$y_{t-s}$也不相关，最后一项为0，且$E(y_{t-1}y_{t-s}) = \gamma_{s-1}$，$E(y_{t-2}y_{t-s}) = \gamma_{s-2}$。于是得到递推：$$\gamma_s = a_1 \gamma_{s-1} + a_2 \gamma_{s-2}, \qquad s \ge 2.$$
> 现在整理上述Yule-Walker方程组：
> $$
> \begin{cases}
> \gamma_0 = a_1 \gamma_1 + a_2 \gamma_2 + \sigma^2,\\[6pt]
> \gamma_1 = a_1 \gamma_0 + a_2 \gamma_1,\\[3pt]
> \gamma_s = a_1 \gamma_{s-1} + a_2 \gamma_{s-2},\quad s\ge2~.
> \end{cases}
> $$
> 从第二式解得：$\gamma_1 (1 - a_2) = a_1 \gamma_0$，因此
> $$\rho_1 = \frac{\gamma_1}{\gamma_0} = \frac{a_1}{1 - a_2}. $$
> 这是AR(2)的一阶自相关与参数的关系 。第三式表明，对于$s\ge2$：
> $$\rho_s = \frac{\gamma_s}{\gamma_0} = a_1 \frac{\gamma_{s-1}}{\gamma_0} + a_2 \frac{\gamma_{s-2}}{\gamma_0} = a_1 \rho_{s-1} + a_2 \rho_{s-2}. $$
> 因此AR(2)模型的自相关函数满足线性**递推关系**：$\rho_s = a_1 \rho_{s-1} + a_2 \rho_{s-2}$，初始$\rho_0=1$，$\rho_1 = a_1/(1-a_2)$ 。这实际上是一个二阶线性差分方程，其解的行为取决于特征根$a_1, a_2$的位置。如果两根都小于1，则$\rho_s$也会随$s$衰减（可能呈振荡形式）。总之，AR(2)的ACF一般表现为**拖尾**（tailing off）：对大滞后逐渐衰减至0，而非在某一有限阶截断。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Deriving the Yule–Walker equations for AR(2)**
> Consider a stationary AR(2) model:
> $$y_t = a_1 y_{t-1} + a_2 y_{t-2} + \varepsilon_t,$$
> Assume the roots of $1-a_1L-a_2L^2$ lie outside the unit circle. Multiply the model by $y_{t-s}$ and take expectations. At lag zero, $E(\varepsilon_ty_t)=\sigma^2$, whereas at positive lags the innovation is uncorrelated with past values. Therefore
> $$\gamma_0=a_1\gamma_1+a_2\gamma_2+\sigma^2,$$
> $$\gamma_1=a_1\gamma_0+a_2\gamma_1,$$
> and, for $s\ge2$,
> $$\gamma_s=a_1\gamma_{s-1}+a_2\gamma_{s-2}.$$
> The second equation gives $\gamma_1(1-a_2)=a_1\gamma_0$, so
> $$\rho_1 = \frac{\gamma_1}{\gamma_0} = \frac{a_1}{1 - a_2}. $$
> Dividing the recursion by $\gamma_0$ gives, for $s\ge2$,
> $$\rho_s = \frac{\gamma_s}{\gamma_0} = a_1 \frac{\gamma_{s-1}}{\gamma_0} + a_2 \frac{\gamma_{s-2}}{\gamma_0} = a_1 \rho_{s-1} + a_2 \rho_{s-2}. $$
> Thus the AR(2) ACF obeys a second-order difference equation with $\rho_0=1$ and $\rho_1=a_1/(1-a_2)$. Under stationarity it **tails off** toward zero, possibly with damped oscillation, rather than cutting off at a finite lag.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (3) MA(1)的ACF
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) ACF for MA(1)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 MA(1) 模型：
$$y_t = \varepsilon_t + \beta\varepsilon_{t-1}$$
其中${\varepsilon_t}$是方差$\sigma^2$的白噪声。可以直接计算自协方差：
* $\gamma_0 = E[(\varepsilon_t + \beta \varepsilon_{t-1})^2] = (1+\beta^2)\sigma^2$ 。
* $\gamma_1 = E[(\varepsilon_t + \beta \varepsilon_{t-1})(\varepsilon_{t-1} + \beta \varepsilon_{t-2})] = \beta\sigma^2$ （因为$\varepsilon_t \varepsilon_{t-2}$和$\varepsilon_{t-1}\varepsilon_{t-2}$期望为0，仅$\varepsilon_t \varepsilon_{t-1}$和$\varepsilon_{t-1}\varepsilon_{t-1}$有贡献，前者0后者$\beta \sigma^2$）。
* 对于$s > 1$，$y_t$与$y_{t-s}$之间永远隔着超过1期的间隔，导致乘积中总有一些$\varepsilon$指数不匹配，可以验证$\gamma_s = 0$ 当$s>1$ 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the MA(1) model
$$y_t = \varepsilon_t + \beta\varepsilon_{t-1}$$
where $\{\varepsilon_t\}$ is white noise with variance $\sigma^2$. Direct calculation gives
* $\gamma_0 = E[(\varepsilon_t + \beta \varepsilon_{t-1})^2] = (1+\beta^2)\sigma^2$ .
* $\gamma_1 = E[(\varepsilon_t + \beta \varepsilon_{t-1})(\varepsilon_{t-1} + \beta \varepsilon_{t-2})] = \beta\sigma^2$, because only the common innovation $\varepsilon_{t-1}$ contributes;
* $\gamma_s=0$ for $s>1$, because $y_t$ and $y_{t-s}$ then share no common innovation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此MA(1)的自相关函数为：
* $\rho_0 = 1$；
* $\rho_1 = \dfrac{\gamma_1}{\gamma_0} = \dfrac{\beta \sigma^2}{(1+\beta^2)\sigma^2} = \frac{\beta}{1+\beta^2}$ ；
* 当$s > 1$时，$\rho_s = 0$ 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So the autocorrelation function of MA(1) is:
* $\rho_0 = 1$;
* $\rho_1 = \dfrac{\gamma_1}{\gamma_0} = \dfrac{\beta \sigma^2}{(1+\beta^2)\sigma^2} = \frac{\beta}{1+\beta^2}$ ;
* When $s > 1$, $\rho_s = 0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也就是说，MA(1)模型的ACF在滞后1处可能非零，但在滞后超过1时**截尾**（cut off）为0。这是纯MA有限序列的一般性质：MA(q)模型的自相关在滞后$q$之后全部为零。对于MA(1)，自相关函数除了$\rho_0,\rho_1$外，其余$\rho_s$ (s≥2)均为0，图形上表现为在滞后1处有值，其后立即跌至0。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The MA(1) ACF may be nonzero at lag 1 but **cuts off** thereafter. More generally, the ACF of an MA$(q)$ process is zero beyond lag $q$. For MA(1), the correlogram therefore has possible spikes only at lags 0 and 1, followed by zeros.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (4) [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|ARMA]](1,1)的ACF
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(4) ACF for [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|ARMA]](1,1)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 ARMA(1,1) 模型：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the ARMA(1,1) model:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$y_t = a_1 y_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t + \beta_1 \varepsilon_{t-1},$$

同样假定平稳($|a_1|<1$)。利用Yule-Walker方程或协方差法，我们可以推导其自相关结构 。列出关键方程：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume stationarity, $|a_1|<1$. The Yule–Walker or direct covariance calculations give the following key equations:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{cases}
\gamma_0 = a_1 \gamma_1 + \sigma^2 + \beta_1 (a_1 + \beta_1)\sigma^2,\\[6pt]
\gamma_1 = a_1 \gamma_0 + \beta_1 \sigma^2,\\[3pt]
\gamma_2 = a_1 \gamma_1,\\
\gamma_s = a_1 \gamma_{s-1}, \quad s \ge 2~.
\end{cases}
$$

其中第一式中$\beta_1(a_1+\beta_1)\sigma^2$来自$E(\beta_1\varepsilon_{t-1} \cdot \varepsilon_t)$和$E(\beta_1\varepsilon_{t-1}\cdot \beta_1\varepsilon_{t-1})$等项之和 。解此组方程：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The extra term $\beta_1(a_1+\beta_1)\sigma^2$ in the first equation reflects the contemporaneous contribution of the MA innovation terms. Solving the system gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 从$\gamma_1$式：$\gamma_1 = a_1 \gamma_0 + \beta_1\sigma^2$。将$\gamma_1$代入$\gamma_0$式，可解得：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- From $\gamma_1=a_1\gamma_0+\beta_1\sigma^2$, substitute into the equation for $\gamma_0$ to obtain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\gamma_0 = \frac{1 + 2a_1 \beta_1 + \beta_1^2}{1 - a_1^2}\sigma^2.$$
• 再由$\gamma_1$式，代入$\gamma_0$结果：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$$\gamma_0 = \frac{1 + 2a_1 \beta_1 + \beta_1^2}{1 - a_1^2}\sigma^2.$$
- Substituting this result back into the equation for $\gamma_1$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\gamma_1 = a_1 \gamma_0 + \beta_1\sigma^2 = \frac{(1 + a_1 \beta_1)(a_1 + \beta_1)}{1 - a_1^2}\sigma^2.$$

• 对于$s \ge 2$，由$\gamma_s$递推：$\gamma_s = a_1 \gamma_{s-1}$。因此从$s=2$开始，$\gamma_s$满足与纯AR(1)相同的关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- For $s\ge2$, $\gamma_s=a_1\gamma_{s-1}$. From lag 2 onward, the autocovariances therefore follow the same recursion as those of a pure AR(1) process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将以上协方差标准化，得到ARMA(1,1)的自相关函数：
• $\rho_0 = 1$；
• $\displaystyle \rho_1 = \frac{\gamma_1}{\gamma_0} = \frac{(1+a_1 \beta_1)(a_1+\beta_1)}{1 + 2a_1 \beta_1 + \beta_1^2}$  ；
• 对$s \ge 2$，$\displaystyle \rho_s = \frac{\gamma_s}{\gamma_0} = a_1 \frac{\gamma_{s-1}}{\gamma_0} = a_1 \rho_{s-1}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Normalizing these autocovariances gives the ARMA(1,1) autocorrelation function.
- $\rho_0 = 1$;
- $\displaystyle \rho_1 = \frac{\gamma_1}{\gamma_0} = \frac{(1+a_1 \beta_1)(a_1+\beta_1)}{1 + 2a_1 \beta_1 + \beta_1^2}$   ;
- For $s \ge 2$, $\displaystyle \rho_s = \frac{\gamma_s}{\gamma_0} = a_1 \frac{\gamma_{s-1}}{\gamma_0} = a_1 \rho_{s-1}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由此可见，对于滞后$s \ge 2$，ARMA(1,1)过程的自相关系数满足$\rho_s = a_1 \rho_{s-1}$，这与AR(1)的尾部行为类似。因此在滞后超过1之后，ARMA(1,1)的ACF呈指数衰减的尾部特征 。直观上，MA(1)部分使$\rho_1$的值发生了改变，但更高阶的相关性仍由AR(1)部分主导并逐步递减。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $s\ge2$, the ARMA(1,1) autocorrelation satisfies $\rho_s=a_1\rho_{s-1}$. The MA(1) component changes the initial value $\rho_1$, while the AR(1) component determines the geometrically decaying tail at higher lags.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. 使用样本数据测算ACF
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1. Estimating the ACF from sample data*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==用均值代整体==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==Use sample moments to estimate population moments.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定平稳时间序列${y_t}_{t=1}^T$的观测，我们可以计算：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given observations $\{y_t\}_{t=1}^T$ from a stationary time series, calculate:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **样本均值**：$\displaystyle \bar{y} = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T} y_t$ 。
• **样本方差**：$\displaystyle \hat{\sigma}^2 = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T} (y_t - \bar{y})^2$ 。
• **样本自相关**$\hat{r_s}$：
$$\displaystyle \hat{r_s} = \frac{\sum_{t=s+1}^{T} (y_t - \bar{y})(y_{t-s} - \bar{y})}{\sum_{t=1}^{T} (y_t - \bar{y})^2}.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- the **sample mean**, $\displaystyle \bar y=\frac1T\sum_{t=1}^Ty_t$;
- the **sample variance**, $\displaystyle \hat\sigma^2=\frac1T\sum_{t=1}^T(y_t-\bar y)^2$; and
- the **sample autocorrelation** $\hat r_s$,
$$\displaystyle \hat{r_s} = \frac{\sum_{t=s+1}^{T} (y_t - \bar{y})(y_{t-s} - \bar{y})}{\sum_{t=1}^{T} (y_t - \bar{y})^2}.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中$s=0,1,2,\ldots$，通常我们关注$1 \le s \le m$的若干滞后自相关。$r_0$按定义为1。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $s=0,1,2,\ldots$. In practice, inspect a finite range $1\le s\le m$; by definition, $r_0=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些足够计算ACF了.最后用中心极限定理可以建立置信区间,然后判断自相关是否显著
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These sample moments determine the estimated ACF. Large-sample approximations then provide confidence bands for judging whether individual autocorrelations differ significantly from zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 MA(s–1) 模型来说：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For MA(s-1) model:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 当 s = 1，样本自相关的方差是：
    $\boxed{\text{Var}(r_1) = \frac{1}{T}}$
- 当 s > 1，样本自相关的方差是：
    $\boxed{ \text{Var}(r_s) = \frac{1}{T} \left( 1 + 2 \sum_{j=1}^{s-1} \rho_j^2 \right) }$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- When $s=1$, the variance of the sample autocorrelation is
    $\boxed{\text{Var}(r_1) = \frac{1}{T}}$
- When $s>1$, its variance is
    $\boxed{ \text{Var}(r_s) = \frac{1}{T} \left( 1 + 2 \sum_{j=1}^{s-1} \rho_j^2 \right) }$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于 **[[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|AR]](p)** 模型来说：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the **[[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|AR]](p)** model:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 如果你估计了比实际阶数 **更大的偏自相关系数**（也就是 $\hat{b}_{p+1}, \hat{b}_{p+2}, \dots$）； 那这些估计量的真实值应该是 **0**，只是有误差；
- 这些估计值的**分布近似服从正态分布**：$\hat{b}_{p+i} \sim \mathcal{N}(0,\ 1/T)$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- If partial-autocorrelation coefficients beyond the true order are estimated—for example, $\hat b_{p+1},\hat b_{p+2},\ldots$—their population values should be **zero**, although their sample estimates fluctuate around zero.
- Their sampling distributions are approximately normal: $\hat b_{p+i}\sim\mathcal N(0,1/T)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，我们可以通过 **构造置信区间** 来判断：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We can therefore construct confidence intervals to assess:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 一个样本 [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ACF 与 PACF|ACF]]（判断 MA）或者样本 [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ACF 与 PACF|PACF]]（判断 AR）是否显著≠0。
- 标准线就是：
    - ACF 用 $\pm 1.96 \sqrt{\text{Var}(r_s)}$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- whether a sample [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ACF 与 PACF|ACF]] coefficient, used especially for identifying MA order, or a sample [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#ACF 与 PACF|PACF]] coefficient, used especially for identifying AR order, differs significantly from zero; and
- the approximate 95% ACF bounds, $\pm1.96\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(r_s)}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

    - PACF 用 $\pm 1.96 \cdot \sqrt{1/T} \approx \pm \frac{1.96}{\sqrt{T}}$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $\pm 1.96 \cdot \sqrt{1/T} \approx \pm \frac{1.96}{\sqrt{T}}$ for PACF
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只要估计值**超过这个范围**，就认为它是**显著的** ⇒ 可能是模型的真实阶数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An estimate outside the corresponding band is treated as **statistically significant** and may indicate a lag that belongs in the model, although model order should not be selected from one spike alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2. 使用样本数据测算PACF
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2. Estimating the PACF from sample data*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==[[OLS 线性回归|OLS]]==

样本偏自相关可以通过与理论定义类似的方法计算。例如，通过逐步回归来估计。实际操作中常用回归法：对于给定的滞后$p$，执行回归
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sample PACF can be estimated by the regression construction used in its theoretical definition. For a chosen lag $p$, fit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$y_t = b_0 + b_1 y_{t-1} + \cdots + b_p y_{t-p} + e_t,$$

使用普通最小二乘（OLS）得到估计系数$\hat{b}_0, \hat{b}_1, \ldots, \hat{b}_p$ 。其中$\hat{b}_p$就是样本偏自相关函数在滞后$p$处的估计值，即$\hat{\alpha}_p$，因为$\hat{b}_p$反映了给定所有介于$y_t$和$y_{t-p}$之间的滞后时，这两者的纯相关程度 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Estimate $\hat b_0,\hat b_1,\ldots,\hat b_p$ by [[OLS 线性回归|ordinary least squares]]. The final coefficient $\hat b_p$ is the sample partial autocorrelation $\hat\alpha_p$: it measures the remaining linear association between $y_t$ and $y_{t-p}$ after controlling for the intervening lags.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.3. 使用MLE拟合MA过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3. Fitting an MA process by MLE*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

纯AR(p)过程可以使用OLS拟合.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pure AR(p) processes can be fitted using OLS.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于MA或ARMA中的MA部分的$\epsilon$不能直接观测出来,所以使用MLE来测算MA部分的参数.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The innovations in the MA component of an MA or ARMA model are latent rather than directly observed, so its parameters are commonly estimated by maximum likelihood.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般来说，使用MLE估计参数包含以下步骤 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, estimating parameters using MLE involves the following steps:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **写出似然函数并取对数**。根据模型假定的分布，写出观测数据的联合密度函数$L(\theta)$，然后取对数得到对数似然$\ell(\theta) = \log L(\theta)$ 。
2. **求一阶条件**。对参数求偏导，将对数似然的一阶导数设为0（一阶条件），解方程得到MLE估计$\hat{\theta}$ 。
3. **检验二阶条件**。计算对数似然的 Hessian 矩阵（即二阶偏导矩阵）在$\hat{\theta}$处的值，检查其是否为负定矩阵 。负定意味着达到局部最大值而非极小值或鞍点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Write the likelihood and log-likelihood.** Use the assumed distribution to obtain the joint density $L(\theta)$ and set $\ell(\theta)=\log L(\theta)$.<br>
**2.** **Solve the first-order conditions.** Differentiate $\ell(\theta)$ with respect to the parameters and solve the score equations for $\hat\theta$, usually numerically for an ARMA model.<br>
**3.** **Check the second-order condition.** A negative-definite Hessian at $\hat\theta$ confirms a local maximum rather than a minimum or saddle point.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满足上述条件后，$\hat{\theta}$即为MLE估计量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A parameter value satisfying these conditions is a local maximum-likelihood estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> This is the expansion of $\varepsilon_t=y_t/(1+\beta L)$ implied by $y_t=(1+\beta L)\varepsilon_t$. Each residual $\varepsilon_t(\beta)$ therefore depends on the trial parameter $\beta$. Substituting these residuals into the Gaussian log-likelihood shows that maximizing the likelihood is equivalent, up to constants, to minimizing
> $$Q(\beta,\sigma^2) = \frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\sum_{t=1}^{T} \Big(\sum_{i=0}^{t-1}(-\beta)^i y_{t-i}\Big)^2 + \frac{T}{2}\log\sigma^2.$$
> Differentiating with respect to $\sigma^2$ gives the familiar estimate $\hat\sigma^2=T^{-1}\sum_t\varepsilon_t(\beta)^2$. Because $\beta$ enters the residuals nonlinearly and recursively, its score equation usually has no closed-form solution and must be solved numerically.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#必考
>[!note] MA过程的**可逆性（invertibility）**
> 可逆性（invertibility）是 MA 或 [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#AR、MA 与 ARMA|ARMA]] 模型能够**唯一表示、可识别、可估计**的必要条件。
> - 对 ARMA(p,q) 来说，判断它是否可逆，就要检查 MA 多项式：$$\theta(L) = 1 + \beta_1 L + \dots + \beta_q L^q$$
> 如果这个多项式的**根都在单位圆外**（即模长大于 1），那么模型就是**可逆的**。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Required*
> **Invertibility of an MA process**
> Invertibility lets an MA or ARMA model be represented uniquely and makes its innovations identifiable and estimable.
> - For ARMA($p,q$), inspect the MA polynomial: $$\theta(L) = 1 + \beta_1 L + \dots + \beta_q L^q.$$
> The model is **invertible** when every root of this polynomial lies outside the unit circle, so every root has modulus greater than one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==AR部分不影响ARMA的可逆性.判断ARMA过程是否可逆,仅观察MA部分==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==The AR component does not affect ARMA invertibility; invertibility is determined solely by the MA polynomial.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.4. 模型选择准则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.4. Model-selection criteria*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在拟合模型时，如果包含更多的滞后项（无论AR还是MA），一定能降低样本残差的平方和（提高样本内拟合度），因为增加参数提供了更大的灵活性来逼近数据 。但是模型过于复杂会导致参数冗余、自由度减少、预测效果未必更好（过拟合）。因此需要在**残差拟合优度**和**模型简洁性**之间进行权衡 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding AR or MA lags weakly improves in-sample fit because extra parameters provide more flexibility. An unnecessarily complex model, however, uses degrees of freedom, introduces redundant parameters, and may forecast worse through overfitting. Model selection therefore balances **fit** against **parsimony**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (1) [[回归模型比较与选择#AIC 与 BIC：likelihood fit 加 complexity penalty|AIC]]

$$\displaystyle \text{AIC} = T \ln\Big(\sum_{t=1}^{T} \hat{\varepsilon}_t^2\Big) + 2n,$$
其中$\hat{\varepsilon}_t$是模型残差，$n$是模型中估计的参数个数（对于ARMA(p,q)模型，$n = p + q + 1$，包括$p$个AR系数、$q$个MA系数和常数项），$T$是样本容量 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$$\displaystyle \text{[[回归模型比较与选择#AIC 与 BIC：likelihood fit 加 complexity penalty|AIC]]} = T \ln\Big(\sum_{t=1}^{T} \hat{\varepsilon}_t^2\Big) + 2n,$$
Here $\hat\varepsilon_t$ is a residual, $n$ is the number of estimated parameters, and $T$ is the sample size. For an ARMA$(p,q)$ model with an intercept, $n=p+q+1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (2) [[回归模型比较与选择#AIC 与 BIC：likelihood fit 加 complexity penalty|BIC]](SBC)

$$\displaystyle \text{SBC} = T \ln\Big(\sum_{t=1}^{T} \hat{\varepsilon}_t^2\Big) + n \ln T.$$

模型的残差平方和$\sum \hat{\varepsilon}_t^2$衡量拟合误差，$n$和$\ln T$项是对模型复杂度的惩罚。一般地，给定若干候选模型，我们选择AIC或SBC值**最小**的模型（因为$\ln(\text{残差和})$越小越好，参数数越多惩罚越大） 。需要注意的是，不同软件有时对AIC/SBC可能使用略不同的定义（比如除以$T$之类的)，但选择标准一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The residual sum of squares measures lack of fit, while the term involving $n$ penalizes model complexity. Among models fitted to the same data, choose the one with the **smallest** AIC or SBC. Software packages may report algebraically rescaled versions, but the ranking principle is the same.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==通常SBC对模型阶数的惩罚比AIC更严（因为$\ln T > 2$当$T>7.4$），因此SBC往往选择更简单的模型==。两者各有使用习惯，SBC在大样本下具有一致性，会倾向于真模型阶数，但小样本下AIC可能表现更好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==SBC/BIC generally penalizes extra lags more heavily than AIC because $\ln T>2$ once $T>e^2\approx7.4$, so it tends to select simpler models.== BIC is consistent for order selection under suitable regularity conditions, whereas AIC may have better predictive performance in some finite samples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.5. [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#识别、估计与诊断|白噪声检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.5. [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#识别、估计与诊断|white noise test]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拟合模型后，我们常需要检验残差是否为白噪声，以验证模型是否已经捕捉了数据的相关结构。或者，我们有时直接想检验某序列${y_t}$是否为白噪声。
白噪声的假设为：$H_0:\ \rho_1 = \rho_2 = \cdots = \rho_s = 0$，==这个部分的教材是错的,不是r,因为检验的总体的而非样本的.实际上使用样本的来近似总统.==即到某个滞后$s$为止所有真自相关都为0。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After fitting a model, test whether its residuals are white noise to assess whether the model has captured the serial dependence in the data. The same tests can also be applied directly to a series $\{y_t\}$.
The white-noise null is $H_0:\ \rho_1=\rho_2=\cdots=\rho_s=0$. ==The population autocorrelations are denoted by $\rho_k$, not the sample autocorrelations $r_k$; the sample values estimate the population quantities.== Thus all population autocorrelations through lag $s$ are zero under the null.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验的常用统计量是
>[!note] **Box-Pierce Q统计量**：
>
> $$Q = T \sum_{k=1}^{s} r_k^2$$
>
> 其中$r_k$是样本自相关 。在$H_0$成立（真的是白噪声）且$T$充分大时，$Q$渐近地依分布收敛到自由度$s$的$\chi^2$分布 。因此可以选择适当的$s$计算$Q$，再查$\chi^2(s)$的临界值进行假设检验。如果$Q$过大（意味着一些$r_k$显著偏离0），则拒绝白噪声假设。反之$Q$较小则无法拒绝，表示数据无显著自相关，可被视为白噪声。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The statistic commonly used is:
> $$Q = T \sum_{k=1}^{s} r_k^2$$
> where $r_k$ is the sample autocorrelation at lag $k$. Under $H_0$ and for large $T$, $Q$ is approximately $\chi^2(s)$. A large value of $Q$ indicates that some sample autocorrelations are too far from zero and leads us to reject white noise; a smaller value means the null cannot be rejected.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] **[[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测#识别、估计与诊断|Ljung-Box检验]]**
> 是对Box-Pierce统计量的小样本修正，定义为：
>
> $$Q^{*} = T(T+2) \sum_{k=1}^{s} \frac{r_k^2}{T - k}. $$
>
> 在$H_0$下，$Q^* \xrightarrow{d} \chi^2(s)$（近似服从$\chi^2$） 。相比$Q$，$Q^*$对于样本容量较小或$s$较大的情况更加稳健。实践中常用Ljung-Box检验，对残差序列作白噪声诊断，比如看$Q^*$对应的$p$值是否显著小于0.05。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is a small-sample correction to the Box–Pierce statistic, defined by
> $$Q^{*} = T(T+2) \sum_{k=1}^{s} \frac{r_k^2}{T - k}. $$
> Under $H_0$, $Q^*\xrightarrow{d}\chi^2(s)$ approximately. Compared with $Q$, $Q^*$ behaves better in smaller samples or when $s$ is relatively large. In practice, the Ljung–Box test is commonly applied to model residuals as a white-noise diagnostic; a sufficiently small $p$-value, such as one below 0.05, leads to rejection of the white-noise null.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**注意：** 在用Ljung-Box检验模型残差时，如果模型估计了$p+q$个参数，那么$Q$统计量在自由度上要扣除这些参数（近似地，自由度= $s - p - q$，若包含截距则减去$p+q+1$） 。这一点将在后面的三阶段建模步骤中提及。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Note:** When applying the Ljung–Box test to fitted-model residuals, adjust the reference degrees of freedom for estimated parameters. A common approximation is $s-p-q$ for an ARMA$(p,q)$ model; conventions about whether to subtract an intercept vary by implementation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] **Box-Jenkins 三阶段建模流程 (Box-Jenkins Three-Stage Modeling Procedure)**
>Box 和 Jenkins 提出了时序模型建模的经典三步法 ：
> 1. **识别阶段（Identification）**：首先对时间序列做初步分析，包括作时序图观察数据趋势/季节性等，计算并绘制样本ACF和PACF等，以便猜测可能的模型形式和阶数 。例如，通过ACF和PACF判断模型是AR、MA还是ARMA，大致的阶数范围等。在经济序列中，还包括先检验平稳性（如单位根ADF检验）并做必要的差分或去趋势，使序列平稳。
>
> 2. **估计阶段（Estimation）**：对步骤1猜测的候选模型，用数据估计其参数（如用MLE)，并检验参数的显著性、模型的稳定性等 。通常会拟合多个候选模型，比较它们的参数估计和适配优度。目标是选出一个既能充分拟合数据又相对简单的**平稳且简约**的模型。
>
> 3. **诊断检验阶段（Diagnostic Checking）**：对选定模型的残差进行检验，确保其行为类似白噪声 。如果残差中仍存在显著的相关结构，说明模型不充分，需要回到步骤1或2修正模型（比如提高阶数或加入遗漏的成分）。常用的诊断包括：残差ACF图、Ljung-Box检验（检验残差是否白噪声）、残差正态QQ图（检验误差正态性）等。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Box–Jenkins three-stage modeling procedure**
> **1.** **Identification:** Inspect the time plot for trend and seasonality, then use the sample ACF and PACF to propose plausible AR, MA, or ARMA orders. For economic series, first test stationarity and difference or detrend the data when needed.<br>
> **2.** **Estimation:** Estimate each candidate model, often by MLE, and examine parameter significance and stationarity. Compare several candidates and seek a model that fits adequately while remaining stationary and parsimonious.<br>
> **3.** **Diagnostic checking:** Test whether the selected model's residuals behave like white noise. If residual dependence remains, return to identification or estimation and revise the model. Common checks include the residual ACF, the Ljung–Box test, and a normal Q–Q plot.<br>
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 5. 预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Forecasting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.1. 使用t时刻的信息预测t+s时刻的y
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.1. Forecasting $y_{t+s}$ with information available at time $t$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以AR(1)模型为例
$$y_t = a_0 + a_1 y_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t,$$
假定模型参数已知。我们可以对$y_{t+1}$做一步预测：在$t$时刻已知$y_t$的情况下，$y_{t+1}$的条件期望为：
$$\hat{y}_{t+1|t} = E_t(y_{t+1}) = a_0 + a_1 y_t.$$
这是最优的线性预测，因为$\varepsilon_{t+1}$的条件期望为0。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the AR(1) model
$$y_t = a_0 + a_1 y_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t,$$
Assume the parameters are known. Given $y_t$ and the information available at time $t$, the one-step-ahead forecast is the conditional expectation
$$\hat{y}_{t+1|t} = E_t(y_{t+1}) = a_0 + a_1 y_t.$$
This is the minimum-mean-square-error forecast because $E_t(\varepsilon_{t+1})=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

迭代得到:$j$步预测的一般公式：对于$j \ge 1$，
$$\hat{y}_{t+j|t} = E_t(y_{t+j}) = a_0(1 + a_1 + \cdots + a_1^{j-1}) + a_1^j y_t.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Iterating the recursion gives the general $j$-step-ahead forecast, for $j\ge1$:
$$\hat{y}_{t+j|t} = E_t(y_{t+j}) = a_0(1 + a_1 + \cdots + a_1^{j-1}) + a_1^j y_t.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上式等价于$\displaystyle a_0\frac{1 - a_1^j}{1 - a_1} + a_1^j y_t$（当$|a_1|\neq1$）。可以看到，预测$y_{t+j}$用到了当前值$y_t$和常数项的累积贡献。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a_1\neq1$, this equals $\displaystyle a_0\frac{1-a_1^j}{1-a_1}+a_1^jy_t$. The forecast combines the accumulated contribution of the intercept with the decaying influence of the current observation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述$\hat{y}_{t+j|t}$被称为**预测函数**（forecast function），它表示将$t$期的信息映射为$j$期以后的预测值的函数 。对AR(1)而言，这是一个简单的指数加权形式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mapping from information at time $t$ to $\hat y_{t+j|t}$ is the **forecast function**. For AR(1), the influence of $y_t$ is weighted geometrically by $a_1^j$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当$|a_1|<1$时，随着预测步数$j \to \infty$，$\hat{y}_{t+j|t}$会趋向某个极限 。
$$\lim_{j\to\infty} \hat{y}_{t+j|t} = \frac{a_0}{,1 - a_1}$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $|a_1|<1$, the forecast converges as the horizon $j\to\infty$:
$$\lim_{j\to\infty} \hat{y}_{t+j|t} = \frac{a_0}{,1 - a_1}$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也就是$y$的无条件均值$\mu$。这说明对于任何平稳的ARMA模型，**长期预测**最终都会收敛到序列的均值 。这是合理的：随着预测步长增大，我们对未来越来越不确定，最优预测就是常数均值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The limit is the unconditional mean $\mu$. More generally, forecasts from a stationary ARMA model converge to the series mean at long horizons: as current information loses predictive power, the conditional mean approaches the unconditional mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.2. 预测误差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.2. Forecast error*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] $j$步预测误差：
>$$e_t(j) \equiv y_{t+j} - \hat{y}_{t+j|t}$$
>即真实值与预测值之差 。对AR(1)模型：
>
> • 一步误差：$e_t(1) = y_{t+1} - \hat{y}_{t+1|t} = y_{t+1} - (a_0 + a_1 y_t) = \varepsilon_{t+1}$ 。
>
> • 两步误差：$e_t(2) = y_{t+2} - \hat{y}_{t+2|t}$。用模型表达$y_{t+2} = a_0 + a_1 y_{t+1} + \varepsilon_{t+2}$，而$\hat{y}_{t+2|t} = a_0 + a_1 \hat{y}_{t+1|t} = a_0 + a_1(a_0 + a_1 y_t)$。两者之差为：
>
> $$
>
> \begin{aligned}
>
> e_t(2) &= a_0 + a_1 y_{t+1} + \varepsilon_{t+2} - [a_0 + a_1(a_0 + a_1 y_t)] \
>
> &= a_1 (y_{t+1} - a_0 - a_1 y_t) + \varepsilon_{t+2} \
>
> &= a_1 \varepsilon_{t+1} + \varepsilon_{t+2}.
>
> \end{aligned}
>
> $$
>
> • 类似地，可以推导一般$j$步误差为：
>
> $$e_t(j) = \varepsilon_{t+j} + a_1 \varepsilon_{t+j-1} + a_1^2 \varepsilon_{t+j-2} + \cdots + a_1^{j-1} \varepsilon_{t+1}. $$
>
这是几何权重的过去$j$个创新的累积。直观地，$j$步预测因为无法知道未来$j$期的$\varepsilon$，这些创新对预测造成误差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **$j$-step forecast error** is
$$e_t(j) \equiv y_{t+j} - \hat{y}_{t+j|t}$$
the realized value minus its forecast. For AR(1):
- the one-step error is $e_t(1)=\varepsilon_{t+1}$;
- the two-step error is $e_t(2)=a_1\varepsilon_{t+1}+\varepsilon_{t+2}$; and
- in general,
$$e_t(j) = \varepsilon_{t+j} + a_1 \varepsilon_{t+j-1} + a_1^2 \varepsilon_{t+j-2} + \cdots + a_1^{j-1} \varepsilon_{t+1}. $$
The forecast error is therefore a geometrically weighted sum of the future innovations that were unknown at time $t$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于预测是无偏的,所以可以得到$E[e_t(j)] = 0$（因为预测是条件期望，误差即为未来的零均值噪声线性组合） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because the forecast is a conditional expectation and future innovations have conditional mean zero, $E_t[e_t(j)]=0$ and hence $E[e_t(j)]=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.3. 预测误差的方差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.3. Variance of forecast error*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 预测误差的方差$\mathrm{Var}[e_t(j)]$
> 随着$j$增加而**增大**。
> 以上$e_t(j)$是$j$个不相关噪声的线性组合，其方差为：
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \mathrm{Var}[e_t(j)] &= \sigma^2 [1 + a_1^2 + a_1^4 + \cdots + a_1^{2(j-1)}] \
> &= \sigma^2 \frac{1 - a_1^{2j}}{1 - a_1^2}
> \end{aligned}
> $$
> 在极限 $j \to \infty$ 时，方差趋于 $\frac{\sigma^2}{1-a_1^2}$，即与无条件方差相同。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The error $e_t(j)$ is a linear combination of $j$ uncorrelated innovations, so
> $$\mathrm{Var}[e_t(j)]=\sigma^2\bigl(1+a_1^2+a_1^4+\cdots+a_1^{2(j-1)}\bigr)=\sigma^2\frac{1-a_1^{2j}}{1-a_1^2}.$$
> As $j\to\infty$, this variance approaches $\sigma^2/(1-a_1^2)$, the unconditional variance.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

进一步缩小范围,如果 ${\varepsilon_t}$ 正态（iid），则可以构造**预测区间**。例如 95% 一步预测区间：$E_t[y_{t+1}] \pm 1.96 \sigma,$
更一般 $j$步也可，但需用 $\sigma \sqrt{1 + a_1^2 + \cdots + a_1^{2(j-1)}}$ 乘以1.96。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the innovations are i.i.d. Gaussian, this variance yields a **prediction interval**. The 95% one-step interval is $E_t[y_{t+1}]\pm1.96\sigma$. At horizon $j$, replace $\sigma$ by $\sigma\sqrt{1+a_1^2+\cdots+a_1^{2(j-1)}}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.4. 更高阶的模型的预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.4. Forecasting of higher-order models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还是递推.中间使用Yule-walker来处理.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use recursive conditional expectations; the same AR dynamics that underlie the Yule–Walker equations govern the multi-step forecasts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!quote] 以一个具体例子ARMA(2,1)说明：
> 模型：
> $$y_t = a_0 + a_1 y_{t-1} + a_2 y_{t-2} + \varepsilon_t + \beta_1 \varepsilon_{t-1}.$$
>
> **一步预测：** $\hat{y}_{t+1|t} = E_t(y_{t+1}) = a_0 + a_1 y_t + a_2 y_{t-1} + \beta_1 \varepsilon_t$ 。注意这里假设我们**知道**时间$t$的误差$\varepsilon_t$（实际上可用拟合残差替代）。在真实预测时，$\varepsilon_t$未知但可以取零或模型残差。此公式表明一步预测除了AR部分的贡献，还需要考虑MA部分最近一期误差的影响。
>
>   **两步预测：** $\hat{y}_{t+2|t} = E_t(y_{t+2}) = a_0 + a_1 \hat{y}_{t+1|t} + a_2 y_t$，因为$y_{t+2}$依赖$y_{t+1}, y_t$。将$\hat{y}_{t+1|t}$代入：
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \hat{y}_{t+2|t} &= a_0 + a_1 [a_0 + a_1 y_t + a_2 y_{t-1} + \beta_1 \varepsilon_t] + a_2 y_t \
> &= a_0(1 + a_1) + (a_1^2 + a_2) y_t + a_1 a_2 y_{t-1} + a_1 \beta_1 \varepsilon_t.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
> 整理可得：
>
> $$\hat{y}_{t+2|t} = a_0(1+a_1) + (a_1 + a_2) y_t + a_1 a_2 y_{t-1} + a_1 \beta_1 \varepsilon_t.$$
>
>   这包含了$t$期和$t-1$期的$y$，以及$t$期的误差。若$t$期误差$\varepsilon_t$也不知，可用0近似。更一般地，对于$j\ge2$，
>
> $$\hat{y}_{t+j|t} = a_0 + a_1 \hat{y}_{t+j-1|t} + a_2 \hat{y}_{t+j-2|t},$$
>
> 即用递推算出每步预测 。这个递推需要初始的$\hat{y}_{t+1|t}$和$\hat{y}_{t+2|t}$，然后逐步往前推。MA部分误差对超过一步的预测没有直接贡献（因为超过一步的未来误差期望为0），但当前残差对多步预测有间接影响（如上面出现的$\varepsilon_t$）。
>
> **多步误差递推：** 对于ARMA(2,1)，有
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> e_t(j) &= y_{t+j} - \hat{y}_{t+j|t}
> = a_1(y_{t+j-1} - \hat{y}_{t+j-1|t}) + a_2(y_{t+j-2} - \hat{y}_{t+j-2|t}) + \varepsilon_{t+j} + \beta_1 \varepsilon_{t+j-1} = a_1 e_t(j-1) + a_2 e_t(j-2) + \varepsilon_{t+j} + \beta_1 \varepsilon_{t+j-1}.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
> 可以看到$e_t(j)$由AR部分前两阶预测误差递推，加上未来$j$期和$j-1$期的噪声 。随着$j$增加，误差表达会包含$\varepsilon_{t+j}, \varepsilon_{t+j-1},\ldots$等。
>
> 与AR(1)类似，对于任何稳定的ARMA模型，$\hat{y}_{t+j|t}$会随着$j\to\infty$收敛到该模型的均值$a_0/(1-\sum a_i)$，预测误差方差趋于序列方差 。因此长期预测只能给出均值水平，置信区间相当宽，预测价值有限。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider the ARMA(2,1) model
> $$y_t = a_0 + a_1 y_{t-1} + a_2 y_{t-2} + \varepsilon_t + \beta_1 \varepsilon_{t-1}.$$
> **One-step forecast:**
> $$\hat y_{t+1|t}=a_0+a_1y_t+a_2y_{t-1}+\beta_1\varepsilon_t.$$
> In practice, use the fitted innovation at time $t$ in place of the unobserved $\varepsilon_t$.
>
> **Two-step forecast:** because all innovations dated after $t$ have conditional mean zero,
> $$\hat y_{t+2|t}=a_0+a_1\hat y_{t+1|t}+a_2y_t.$$
> Expanding gives the coefficient $(a_1^2+a_2)y_t$; the later Chinese display containing $(a_1+a_2)y_t$ is a typographical error. For longer horizons,
> $$\hat{y}_{t+j|t} = a_0 + a_1 \hat{y}_{t+j-1|t} + a_2 \hat{y}_{t+j-2|t},$$
> so forecasts are generated recursively. The fitted current innovation affects later forecasts only indirectly through the predicted values; future innovations have zero conditional expectation.
>
> **Multi-step error recursion:**
> $$e_t(j)=a_1e_t(j-1)+a_2e_t(j-2)+\varepsilon_{t+j}+\beta_1\varepsilon_{t+j-1}.$$
> As $j$ grows, the error accumulates more future innovations. For any stationary ARMA model, the point forecast converges to $a_0/(1-\sum_i a_i)$ and the forecast-error variance converges to the unconditional variance, so long-horizon intervals become wide.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.5. 预测效果评估
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.5. Evaluating forecast performance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果知道真正的形成模型,那此时$e_T(1) = y_{T+1} - \mathbb{E}_T[y_{T+1}] = \epsilon_{T+1}$.这意味着预测误差就是纯粹的“未来扰动”，你无论什么模型都不可能预测它 ,这东西是未来的信息.是真正的不可预测部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the true data-generating process is known, then $e_T(1)=y_{T+1}-E_T[y_{T+1}]=\varepsilon_{T+1}$. The remaining error is the genuinely unpredictable future innovation; no model using information available at time $T$ can forecast it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但是现在是不知道真正模型是什么,只是找了几个可行的模型，此时需要比较哪一个模型的预测表现更好。**样本外预测评估**是关键步骤，因为最优拟合（样本内）不一定意味着最优预测能力。以下介绍几种预测比较的方法：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In practice, the true model is unknown and several plausible specifications must be compared. **Out-of-sample forecast evaluation** is essential because the best in-sample fit need not deliver the best forecasts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (1) 保留样本检验法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Holdout-sample evaluation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将数据分为训练集和测试集。先用前$N$期数据估计模型，再对后$H$期进行预测，将预测与实际比较 。例如：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Split the observations into a training sample and a test sample. Estimate each model on the first $N$ periods, forecast observations in the next $H$ periods, and compare those forecasts with the realizations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 用前100期数据分别估计模型A（如AR(1)）和模型B（如MA(1)），预测第101期值；然后用前101期重新估计两模型，预测第102期值；重复直到利用前$100+K-1$期预测第$100+K$期（K为留出的测试样本长度，如50） 。收集两模型在测试期每一期的预测$f_{1i}$和$f_{2i}$（$i=1,\ldots,H$），以及相应预测误差$e_{1i}, e_{2i}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- For example, estimate models A and B on periods 1–100 and forecast period 101. Re-estimate them on periods 1–101 and forecast period 102, then continue until the end of the $H$-period holdout sample. Collect each model's forecasts $f_{1i},f_{2i}$ and errors $e_{1i},e_{2i}$ for $i=1,\ldots,H$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 计算各模型的**均方预测误差**（Mean Squared Prediction Error (MSPE)）作为指标：$\displaystyle \text{MSPE}_1 = \frac{1}{H}\sum_{i=1}^{H} e_{1i}^2,\quad \text{MSPE}_2 = \frac{1}{H}\sum_{i=1}^{H} e_{2i}^2$ 。MSPE越小，表示预测精度越高。常选择MSPE较小的模型作为优胜者 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Use each model's **mean squared prediction error (MSPE)** as the comparison metric: $\displaystyle \text{MSPE}_1=\frac1H\sum_{i=1}^He_{1i}^2,\quad \text{MSPE}_2=\frac1H\sum_{i=1}^He_{2i}^2$. A smaller MSPE indicates greater forecast accuracy, so the model with the lower MSPE is usually preferred.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 进一步，可以构造两模型MSPE之比的F统计检验：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Under restrictive assumptions, compare the two error sums of squares with the ratio
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$F = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{H} e_{1i}^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{H} e_{2i}^2}.$$

在一些理想假设（见下）下，$F$统计量服从自由度$(H,H)$的F分布，可用于检验两模型预测精度有无显著差异 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the idealized assumptions below, this statistic is compared with an $F(H,H)$ distribution to test equal forecast-error variances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述保留法其实就是模拟预测真实未知值的过程，通过多次滚动预测可以稳定估计预测误差分布。关键在于将历史数据末尾的一部分藏起来验证模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The holdout method mimics genuine forecasting: the final observations remain hidden when each forecast is produced. Repeated rolling-origin forecasts then reveal how each model performs on values that were not used for estimation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (2) 预测误差假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Assumptions about forecast errors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在进行$F$检验之前，需要对预测误差做一些假定（通常比较理想化）：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before the $F$ test, some assumptions (usually idealized) need to be made about the forecast error:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• A1: 预测误差均值为0且正态。
• A2: 预测误差不相关（不同预测时段之间独立）。
• A3: 两个模型的预测误差序列在同一时期没有相关性（即模型1和模型2的误差同期不相关）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- A1: Forecast errors have mean zero and are normally distributed.
- A2: Forecast errors are independent across forecast origins.
- A3: The two models' forecast errors are contemporaneously uncorrelated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这些假设下，可以证明上面的$F$统计量服从$F(H,H)$分布 。不过在实际中，这些假设往往过于理想，如不同模型对同一序列的误差常有相关性（比如都漏掉某种模式导致相似误差），违反A3。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under these assumptions, the ratio has the stated $F(H,H)$ reference distribution. In practice the assumptions are often implausible: two models forecasting the same series usually make correlated errors because both respond to the same shocks or omit the same pattern, violating A3.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (3) Granger-Newbold 检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Granger–Newbold test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] Granger-Newbold 检验
> 为放松假设A3（允许两模型误差同期相关），Granger和Newbold (1976)提出如下检验 ：
>
> • 构造两个新序列：
> $x_i = e_{1i} + e_{2i}$
> $z_i = e_{1i} - e_{2i}$，$i=1,\ldots,H$ 。可以验证，在A1和A2下，$E(x_i) = 0, E(z_i) = 0$且$x_i, z_i$不相关之外，$E(x_i z_i) = E(e_{1i}^2 - e_{2i}^2)$ 。$x_i$和$z_i$的相关系数$\rho_{xz}$实际上反映了两模型误差方差差异。
>
> • 计算样本相关系数$r_{xz}$，并构造统计量：
>
> $$T = \frac{r_{xz}}{\sqrt{(1-r_{xz}^2)/(H-1)}}.$$
>
> 其在A1和A2下服从自由度$H-1$的t分布 。
>
> 若$T$显著，则表明$\rho_{xz}\neq 0$，即$E(e_{1i}^2) \neq E(e_{2i}^2)$，两个模型的预测方差有差异，可判定哪个较小更优。这个检验就是检验两个模型MSPE是否有显著差别，克服了直接比$F$的不足。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Granger-Newbold test**
> To relax A3 and allow the two models' errors to be contemporaneously correlated, Granger and Newbold (1976) proposed the following test:
> - Construct two new sequences:
> $x_i = e_{1i} + e_{2i}$
> $z_i = e_{1i} - e_{2i}$, for $i=1,\ldots,H$. Their product is $x_iz_i=e_{1i}^2-e_{2i}^2$, so the correlation between $x_i$ and $z_i$ reflects the difference in the two forecast-error variances.
> - Calculate the sample correlation coefficient$r_{xz}$ and construct a statistic:
> $$T = \frac{r_{xz}}{\sqrt{(1-r_{xz}^2)/(H-1)}}.$$
> Under A1 and A2, this statistic has a $t$ distribution with $H-1$ degrees of freedom. A significant result rejects equal expected squared error; the sign indicates which model has the smaller MSPE. The transformation avoids the direct $F$ test's assumption that the two models' errors are contemporaneously uncorrelated.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==如果t显著,说明两模型的MSPE是真的有差距的,选更小的,如果t不显著,说明二者没大区别,MSPE更小的一方只是运气好而已.==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==A significant statistic provides evidence that the models' MSPEs differ, so prefer the model with the smaller MSPE. Without significance, the observed difference may simply be sampling variation.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (4) Diebold-Mariano 检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(4) Diebold–Mariano test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] Diebold-Mariano 检验
> 为了同时放松A1和A2，Diebold和Mariano (1995)提出了DM检验，可用于一般损失函数下的预测比较 。
>
> • 不局限于均方误差，可以选取任意损失函数$g(\cdot)$（如绝对误差的$g(e)=|e|$等）。定义差异序列：$d_i = g(e_{1i}) - g(e_{2i})$，及其样本平均：
>
> $$\bar{d} = \frac{1}{H}\sum_{i=1}^{H} [g(e_{1i}) - g(e_{2i})].$$
>
> $\bar{d}$表示模型1相对模型2的平均损失差。若$\bar{d}=0$，则两模型表现相当；$\bar{d}<0$则模型1损失小于模型2。
>
> • 在较宽松的正态性条件下（允许$d_i$有自相关），可以证明：
>
> $$\frac{\bar{d}}{\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(\bar{d})}} \xrightarrow{d} N(0,1). $$
>
> 也就是说，$\bar{d}$的标准化近似服从标准正态，可据此进行假设检验。关键在于估计$\mathrm{Var}(\bar{d})$时考虑$d_i$的自相关（因预测误差可能随$i$有相关性）。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Diebold-Mariano test**
> Diebold and Mariano (1995) proposed the DM test to compare forecasts under a general loss function without requiring the restrictive assumptions A1 and A2.
> - Choose a loss function $g(\cdot)$, such as absolute loss $g(e)=|e|$, and define $d_i=g(e_{1i})-g(e_{2i})$ with sample mean
> $$\bar{d} = \frac{1}{H}\sum_{i=1}^{H} [g(e_{1i}) - g(e_{2i})].$$
> The value $\bar d$ is model 1's average loss relative to model 2. Equal predictive accuracy implies $E(d_i)=0$; a negative value favours model 1.
> - Under weak regularity conditions that allow $d_i$ to be serially correlated,
> $$\frac{\bar{d}}{\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(\bar{d})}} \xrightarrow{d} N(0,1). $$
> so the standardized loss differential is asymptotically standard normal. Estimating $\operatorname{Var}(\bar d)$ must account for serial correlation in $d_i$, especially for overlapping multi-step forecast errors.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• DM检验考虑了三种情况来估计方差 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The DM test considers three scenarios to estimate the variance:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**Case 1:** 若${d_i}$无自相关，则$\mathrm{Var}(\bar{d}) = \gamma_0/(H-1)$，其中$\gamma_0$是$d_i$的方差 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Case 1:** If $\{d_i\}$ is serially uncorrelated, use $\operatorname{Var}(\bar d)=\gamma_0/(H-1)$, where $\gamma_0$ is the variance of $d_i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**Case 2:** 若已知$d_i$在前$q$阶存在自相关（超出$q$阶可忽略），则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Case 2:** If autocorrelation is material through lag $q$ but negligible beyond it, use
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\mathrm{Var}(\bar{d}) \approx \frac{\gamma_0 + 2\gamma_1 + \cdots + 2\gamma_q}{H-1}$$

其中$\gamma_j$是$d_i$的滞后$j$协方差 。（这是利用Newey-West方法截尾窗口$q$的方差估计。）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $\gamma_j$ is the lag-$j$ autocovariance of $d_i$. This is a truncated long-run-variance estimate related to the Newey–West approach.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**Case 3:** 若$q$未知，则使用一般的Newey-West, 1987方法估计$\bar{d}$的稳健长期方差 。计算出标准误差后，将$Z = \bar{d}/\sqrt{\widehat{\mathrm{Var}}(\bar{d})}$与$N(0,1)$比较即可。若$Z$绝对值大于临界值（如1.96），则认为两模型预测有显著差异 。正的$Z$表示模型1损失高于模型2，负的$Z$则模型1更优。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Case 3:** If the dependence horizon is unknown, estimate the long-run variance robustly with a general Newey–West procedure. Compare $Z=\bar d/\sqrt{\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(\bar d)}$ with a standard normal distribution. For a two-sided 5% test, $|Z|>1.96$ rejects equal predictive accuracy. A positive $Z$ means model 1 has higher loss; a negative $Z$ favours model 1.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

DM检验也可推广到多步预测比较，通过调整损失函数定义，或对$d_i$引入必要的自相关校正（因为多步预测误差本身存在序列相关结构）。总的来说，DM检验成为预测比较的一个标准方法 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The DM test also applies to multi-step forecasts provided the variance estimator accounts for the serial dependence created by overlapping forecast horizons. It is now a standard test of equal predictive accuracy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.6. 合并预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.6. Combining forecasts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有时，与其试图选择单一“最佳”模型，不如采用**组合预测**的方法，将多个模型的预测结果加权平均，以期提高预测精度 。理论和实践表明，适当的组合往往能取得比单一模型更稳定和优秀的效果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rather than select one “best” model, a **combined forecast** averages predictions from several models. A suitable combination can be more stable and accurate than any single specification because different models make different errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设我们有$n$个可行模型，对$y_t$给出各自的一步预测值$f_{1t}, f_{2t}, \ldots, f_{nt}$ 。我们采用权重$w_1,\ldots,w_n$（满足$w_i \ge 0$且$\sum_{i=1}^n w_i = 1$）构造组合预测：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $n$ models produce one-step forecasts $f_{1t},f_{2t},\ldots,f_{nt}$ for $y_t$. Choose weights $w_i\ge0$ with $\sum_{i=1}^nw_i=1$ and define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$f_{ct} = w_1 f_{1t} + w_2 f_{2t} + \cdots + w_n f_{nt}.$$

$w_i$可以理解为对模型$i$的信任程度或分配比例。组合预测的误差$e_{ct} = y_t - f_{ct}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The weight $w_i$ represents the share assigned to model $i$. The combined forecast error is $e_{ct}=y_t-f_{ct}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果各单项预测都是无偏的（即$E_{t-1}(y_t - f_{it}) = 0$，预测值的期望等于真实值），那么任意线性组合仍然是无偏的 。特别地，组合预测$f_{ct}$的偏差$E_{t-1}(y_t - f_{ct}) = \sum w_i E_{t-1}(y_t - f_{it}) = 0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If each individual forecast is conditionally unbiased, $E_{t-1}(y_t-f_{it})=0$, then any weighted combination whose weights sum to one is also unbiased:
$$E_{t-1}(y_t-f_{ct})=\sum_iw_iE_{t-1}(y_t-f_{it})=0.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑$n=2$的情形，两模型误差分别为$e_{1t}, e_{2t}$，假设$e_{1t}$与$e_{2t}$不相关且方差相等（即两个模型预测不相关、精度相当），取$w_1 = w_2 = 0.5$。此时组合误差方差为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two models with errors $e_{1t}$ and $e_{2t}$, suppose the errors are uncorrelated and have equal variance. With equal weights $w_1=w_2=0.5$, the combined-error variance is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\mathrm{Var}(e_{ct}) = w_1^2 \mathrm{Var}(e_{1t}) + w_2^2 \mathrm{Var}(e_{2t}) + 2w_1 w_2 \mathrm{Cov}(e_{1t}, e_{2t}).$$
由于假设协方差为0且$w_1=w_2=0.5$，得到$\mathrm{Var}(e_{ct}) = 0.25\mathrm{Var}(e_{1t}) + 0.25\mathrm{Var}(e_{2t}) = 0.5\mathrm{Var}(e_{1t})$（因为$\mathrm{Var}(e_{1t})=\mathrm{Var}(e_{2t})$）。也就是说组合预测的方差是单个模型的一半 。组合降低了预测不确定性。这一结论要求误差不相关且方差相等，否则需要考虑协方差项
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$$\mathrm{Var}(e_{ct}) = w_1^2 \mathrm{Var}(e_{1t}) + w_2^2 \mathrm{Var}(e_{2t}) + 2w_1 w_2 \mathrm{Cov}(e_{1t}, e_{2t}).$$
With zero covariance and equal component variances, this becomes $0.5\operatorname{Var}(e_{1t})$: equal-weight averaging halves the variance. This conclusion depends on equal variances and zero covariance; otherwise the covariance term must be retained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以进一步选择最优权重以使组合预测误差方差最小，相当于一个投资组合优化问题 。对于$n=2$，最优权重（可变$w_1$, $w_2=1-w_1$）是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weights can instead be chosen to minimize the combined forecast-error variance, exactly as in a two-asset portfolio problem. For $n=2$ with $w_2=1-w_1$, the optimum is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$ w_1^* = \frac{\mathrm{Var}(e_2) - \mathrm{Cov}(e_1,e_2)}{\mathrm{Var}(e_1) + \mathrm{Var}(e_2) - 2\mathrm{Cov}(e_1,e_2)}$$

$w_2^* = 1 - w_1^*$ 。当$e_1, e_2$方差相等且不相关，上式给$w_1^* = 0.5$。更一般地，如果一个模型明显更准（方差小），最优权重会向其倾斜。如果误差存在相关性，组合预测可以利用两者差异减少方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set $w_2^*=1-w_1^*$. Equal, uncorrelated error variances yield $w_1^*=0.5$. A more accurate model receives more weight, while imperfect correlation creates diversification gains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在实际操作中，各模型的权重可以根据历史预测性能确定（如逆MSPE加权等），或者通过回归优化确定（有文献如Bates-Granger提出简单平均往往很有效）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In practice, the weights of each model can be determined based on historical prediction performance (e.g. inverse MSPE weighting) or by regression optimization (e.g. simple averaging proposed by Bates-Granger is often effective).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*7. Seasonality*

许多经济时间序列存在**季节性**波动，即周期为年、季度、月等固定长度的重复模式 。例如：农业产出受季节气候影响、每年感恩节到圣诞节期间零售额激增等等 。常用的经济指标往往经过**季节调整**（seasonally adjusted），以去除季节性成分。然而理解和建模季节性仍很重要。

Agricultural output responds to seasonal weather, for example, while retail sales rise between Thanksgiving and Christmas. Published indicators are often **seasonally adjusted**, but understanding and modelling the underlying seasonality remains important.

# 8. 参数稳健性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. Parameter robustness*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不会讲的很细
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This topic will not be covered in great detail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Box-Jenkins方法隐含假设模型的结构在整个样本期间保持不变，即参数稳定 。然而现实中，经济机制可能发生**结构断裂**（structural break）或**参数漂移**。例如，政策制度改变、技术革新、金融危机等都可能导致模型参数在某个时间点前后不同。这一节讨论如何检验模型的结构稳定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Box–Jenkins method implicitly assumes that the model's structure remains unchanged throughout the sample period—that is, its parameters are stable. In practice, however, an economic process may undergo a **structural break** or **parameter drift**. Changes in policy regimes, technological innovations, and financial crises, for example, can cause model parameters to differ before and after a particular date. This section explains how to test a model's structural stability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


>[!note] **结构变化的检验：Chow检验**。
>
> Chow检验用于已知潜在结构变化时点的情形。其步骤：
>
> • 假设我们怀疑在$t_m$时序列发生结构突变，将$T$个观测分为两段：前$T_1 = t_m$期和后$T_2 = T - t_m$期 。分别用两段数据估计ARMA(p,q)模型，得到每段的残差平方和：$\text{SSR}_1$和$\text{SSR}_2$ 。同时，用整个样本估计模型得到$\text{SSR}$。
>
> • 原假设$H_0$为“模型在两段的参数完全相同”（即无结构变动），备择为参数不同。检验统计量构造为：
>
> $$F = \frac{(\text{SSR} - \text{SSR}_1 - \text{SSR}_2)/n}{(\text{SSR}_1 + \text{SSR}_2)/(T - 2n)}.$$
>
> 其中$n$是模型参数数（两段各估计$n$个参数，总参数翻倍），$T$是全样本长度 。这个统计量在$H_0$下服从自由度$(n, T-2n)$的F分布。
>
> • $F$值过大意味着将样本分段可以显著降低残差平方和，暗示参数在两段不一致，拒绝$H_0$。反之则不拒绝视为参数稳定。
>
> Chow检验要求**断点已知**（如某已知政策实施日）。然而有时断点未知甚至不止一个，这就需要更一般的方法。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The Chow test applies when the possible break date is known. The procedure is:
> - If a break is suspected at $t_m$, divide the $T$ observations into two subsamples of lengths $T_1=t_m$ and $T_2=T-t_m$. Estimate the ARMA$(p,q)$ model separately on the two subsamples to obtain $\text{SSR}_1$ and $\text{SSR}_2$, and estimate it on the full sample to obtain $\text{SSR}$.
> - The null $H_0$ states that all model parameters are the same in both subsamples; the alternative allows them to differ. The test statistic is
> $$F = \frac{(\text{SSR} - \text{SSR}_1 - \text{SSR}_2)/n}{(\text{SSR}_1 + \text{SSR}_2)/(T - 2n)}.$$
> Here $n$ is the number of parameters in each subsample model and $T$ is the full sample size. Under $H_0$, the statistic has an $F(n,T-2n)$ distribution.
> - A large $F$ means that splitting the sample substantially reduces the residual sum of squares, providing evidence that the parameters differ across the two periods; reject $H_0$. Otherwise, do not reject parameter stability.
>
> The Chow test requires a **known break point**, such as a known policy implementation date. Unknown or multiple breaks require more general methods.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**内生结构变化**：当变点未知时，称为**内生性**结构突变问题。例如无法预先指定哪一年发生了金融自由化影响参数改变 。这个有别的办法处理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Endogenous structural change:** when the break date is unknown, it must be estimated from the data rather than specified in advance. For example, one may not know which year financial liberalization changed the model parameters. Such cases require tests designed for unknown break points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
