### 2. 多元正态分布与 Wishart 分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Multivariate Normal and Wishart Distributions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值向量和协方差只给前两阶描述；多元正态进一步给出可推导边际、条件和抽样分布的联合模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mean vector and covariance matrix provide only first- and second-order descriptions. The multivariate normal supplies a joint model from which marginal, conditional, and sampling distributions can be derived.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多元正态分布.canvas]]

先固定“联合 Gaussian”究竟要求什么：它不是各坐标分别正态，而是每一个线性方向都正态；半正定协方差也允许退化的 Gaussian 向量存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First fix what “jointly Gaussian” means. Coordinate-wise normality is insufficient: every linear direction must be normal, and a positive-semidefinite covariance may define a degenerate Gaussian vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[联合Gaussian]]

有了定义，仿射变换便给出最常用的计算规则；取边际只是选择部分坐标的特殊矩阵变换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definition yields the main computational rule: affine transformations preserve Gaussianity, and marginalisation is a coordinate-selection special case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Gaussian仿射闭包]]

接着按协方差秩分流。正定协方差产生全维椭球密度；奇异协方差仍定义 Gaussian 律，但概率只落在低维仿射支撑上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next branch by covariance rank. Positive definiteness gives a full-dimensional ellipsoidal density, whereas a singular covariance confines a valid Gaussian law to a lower-dimensional affine support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Gaussian密度与奇异支撑]]

分块以后，条件分布把观测到的一块转化为另一块的线性均值调整；普通 Schur-complement 公式要求被条件化的协方差块可逆。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After partitioning the vector, conditioning turns one observed block into a linear adjustment of the other. The ordinary Schur-complement formula requires an invertible conditioning covariance block.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Gaussian条件分布]]

条件分布的残差推导会用到 Gaussian 的一个特殊边界：联合 Gaussian 下零交叉协方差推出独立，只有 Gaussian 边际则仍然不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conditional-residual argument uses a special Gaussian boundary: zero cross-covariance implies independence for a jointly Gaussian vector, but Gaussian marginals alone remain insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The characteristic function supplies a representation of a probability law that also works without a density. It is the prerequisite for the transform proof of Gaussian independence below; the card introduces complex exponentials from the unit circle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特征函数提供没有密度时也适用的概率律表示，是下方Gaussian独立性变换证明的前置。卡片从单位圆出发解释复指数。

![[特征函数]]

![[联合高斯独立判据]]

用真实总体均值和正定协方差时，白化把平方 Mahalanobis 距离变成独立标准正态的平方和；用同一数据估计均值和协方差后不能照搬这条精确卡方律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the true population mean and positive-definite covariance, whitening turns squared Mahalanobis distance into a sum of independent standard-normal squares. Fitting the centre and covariance on the same sample invalidates that exact reference law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The next theorem uses the chi-square distribution, which describes a sum of independent squared standard normals. Read its definition first to see why whitening turns a vector distance into a scalar reference distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下一个定理使用卡方分布：它描述独立标准正态的平方和。先读定义，就能理解白化为什么把向量距离转成一个标量参考分布。

![[卡方分布]]

![[Gaussian马氏平方律]]

先固定诊断的认识论边界：边际正态只检查坐标方向，同样本 Mahalanobis 图又不是精确独立的卡方抽样。两者都能暴露不相容，却不能单独证成联合 Gaussian。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First fix the epistemic boundary: normal marginals inspect only coordinate directions, and fitted-sample Mahalanobis plots do not have an exact independent chi-square law. Neither can establish joint Gaussianity by itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[边际与马氏图不证联合Gaussian]]

最后执行完整诊断流程：把数据来源、边际图、联合投影、Mahalanobis 结构与下游敏感性合并解释，而不是让一张图代替模型判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally apply the full workflow by combining data provenance, marginal plots, joint projections, Mahalanobis structure, and downstream sensitivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[联合Gaussian诊断]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The multivariate normal model specifies the joint distribution within each observation. Independent observations from one such population give a Wishart law for the appropriately scaled sample covariance; the sampling assumptions are part of this conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多元正态模型规定每次观测内部的联合分布；从同一总体独立抽样后，适当缩放的样本协方差服从Wishart律。抽样假设是该结论的一部分。

![[Wishart 分布与样本协方差推断.canvas]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the positive-integer Gaussian outer-product construction to identify the random matrix and covariance scale. The positive-definite density then extends the family to real degrees of freedom greater than dimension minus one. The scalar construction is a scaled chi-square variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先用正整数自由度的Gaussian外积和说明随机矩阵与协方差型尺度。正定密度将分布族延拓到大于维数减一的实自由度；一维构造是缩放后的卡方变量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before using that distribution, the finite outer-product theorem explains why its integer construction is positive semidefinite for every realized list of vectors. Independence and Gaussian assumptions specify the Wishart law; the algebraic nonnegativity itself needs neither.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在使用分布结论前，有限外积和定理解释整数生成式为什么对每组实现值都半正定。独立与Gaussian假设指定Wishart抽样律，代数上的非负性本身不依赖这些假设。

![[外积和的半正定性]]

![[Wishart分布]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n\ge2$ iid Gaussian vectors, centering permits $n-1$ independent Gaussian contrasts. Their outer-product sum is the scatter matrix, which yields the sample-covariance law. The same decomposition separates the sample mean from the residual block and hence from the covariance statistic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对$n\ge2$个iid Gaussian向量，中心化后可选出$n-1$个独立Gaussian对比。它们的外积和是散布矩阵，由此得到样本协方差律；同一分解还把样本均值与残差块分开，进而得到均值与协方差统计量独立。

![[样本协方差Wishart律]]

![[正态均值协方差独立]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the positive-integer construction, degrees of freedom count generating vectors and bound the available span. For an iid Gaussian sample with positive-definite population covariance, the sample covariance is invertible almost surely iff $n\ge p+1$. For arbitrary fixed data, that sample-size condition alone is insufficient; actual rank still matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在正整数构造中，自由度计算生成向量并限制可张成的维数。总体协方差正定的iid Gaussian样本中，样本协方差几乎必然可逆当且仅当$n\ge p+1$。对任意固定数据，仅有这一样本量条件仍不够，还须检查实际秩。

![[Wishart秩与可逆性]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally check the matrix convention and the sampling model. Precision is the inverse of covariance scale, while common covariance or unbiasedness alone does not imply the exact Wishart law. When the standard assumptions are absent, the desired inference needs a new justification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后核对矩阵约定与抽样模型。精度是协方差型尺度的逆矩阵；共同协方差或无偏性本身不推出精确Wishart律。缺少标准假设时，所需推断必须另外论证。

![[Wishart参数化]]

![[Wishart抽样假设]]

### 3. 多元均值推断与 MANOVA
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Inference for Multivariate Means and MANOVA*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gaussian and Wishart sampling theory gives exact classical calibration under its model assumptions. We now use it to compare mean vectors and construct confidence regions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Gaussian与Wishart抽样理论在相应模型假设下给出经典精确校准。现在用它比较均值向量并构造置信区域。

![[Hotelling T² 与多元均值推断.canvas]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before the multivariate test, review how Student t handles a normal numerator divided by an independent random scale. Its scalar quantiles will also set the fixed-direction confidence intervals below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

进入多元检验前，先理解Student t如何处理正态分子与独立随机尺度的比值。后面的固定方向置信区间也会用到它的分位数。

![[Student t分布]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then understand the F reference distribution: it compares two independent chi-square quantities after division by their own degrees of freedom. Its ordered parameters will become p and n−p in the one-sample theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再理解F参考分布：两个独立卡方量各除以自身自由度后取比值。在单样本定理中，有序参数将是p与n−p。

![[F分布]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then define the mean-departure statistic and check joint Gaussian iid sampling, positive-definite population covariance and n>p before using its exact F conversion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再定义均值偏离统计量。使用精确F转换之前，核对联合Gaussian iid抽样、总体协方差正定及n>p。

![[单样本Hotelling T²]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A covariance-adjusted departure can be strongest in a linear combination. The maximum-projection identity explains that joint evidence without implying that every coordinate is significant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑协方差后的偏离可能在某个线性组合上最突出。最大投影恒等式解释这种联合证据，但不推出每个坐标都显著。

![[Hotelling投影极值]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a scientific question $D\mu=\psi_0$ fixed before the data, first check that redundant constraints are consistent and retain a full-row-rank representation. Under the stated Gaussian model, the transformed test requires $n$ to exceed that row rank; the original sample covariance need not be invertible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若科学问题是事先固定的 $D\mu=\psi_0$，先检查冗余约束是否相容，再保留满行秩表示。在正文的Gaussian模型下，变换后的检验只要求样本量 $n$ 超过这个行秩，原始样本协方差可以奇异。

![[线性约束Hotelling T²]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Invert the test to collect all non-rejected mean vectors. Projections of that one ellipsoid cover every linear combination simultaneously; its coordinate shadows alone do not reconstruct the full region.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反演检验，收集所有未拒绝的均值向量。同一椭球的投影同时覆盖每个线性组合；只看坐标投影不能还原整个区域。

![[Hotelling置信椭球]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a prespecified finite family, Bonferroni intervals distribute the total error across valid scalar intervals. They use projected variances without inverting the sample covariance. Showing a subset of an already protected family preserves its guarantee; adding directions selected after seeing the data needs further justification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对事先指定的有限组，Bonferroni区间把总错误率分配到有效的标量区间。它使用投影方差，无需求样本协方差的逆。已有同时覆盖保证的一组可以只展示其子集；看数据后新增方向则须另外论证。

![[Bonferroni对比区间]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally choose the sampling unit. Paired analysis applies the one-sample test to iid Gaussian difference vectors; $n$ counts pairs. The pooled test instead compares two independent Gaussian groups under a common positive-definite covariance model, combining variation around each group mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后明确抽样单位：配对分析把单样本检验用于独立同分布的正态差向量，$n$ 数的是对数。pooled检验则比较两个独立Gaussian组，并在共同正定协方差模型下合并各组均值周围的变动。

![[配对Hotelling T²]]

![[pooled Hotelling T²]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hotelling’s tests compare one or two mean vectors. MANOVA extends joint mean inference to several groups or model effects, accounting for covariance among responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Hotelling $T^2$ 先比较一个多元均值向量；MANOVA 再把同一思想推广到多个组或多个效应，同时检验一组相关结果变量。

![[MANOVA 多元方差分析.canvas]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First fix the MANOVA question: a shared-design model uses several responses to test a prespecified joint effect. Equality of one-way population mean vectors is a special case; for two independent Gaussian groups with common positive-definite covariance and sufficient residual rank, the corresponding test agrees with pooled Hotelling’s test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定 MANOVA 的对象：同一批观测共享设计矩阵，同时拥有多个相关响应；检验用 $H$ 与 $E$ 联合评价预设的系数—响应假设。单因素均值向量相等只是最小特例，两组共同协方差情形与 pooled Hotelling $T^2$ 是同一检验。

![[MANOVA]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For classical calibration, check independent Gaussian errors with common positive-definite covariance, estimability and sufficient residual rank. Observation means may differ even when errors are identically distributed. Diagnostics can reveal conflicts but cannot prove population assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

统计量能否按经典参考分布解释，先由独立观测、联合多元正态、共同组内协方差、可估性和残差秩决定；诊断只会发现冲突，不能证明这些总体假设。

![[MANOVA经典假设]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a one-way design, total SSCP splits into between-group $H$ and within-group $E$. Off-diagonal entries retain cross-products between responses. This arithmetic identity holds without a sampling model; inference from it needs additional assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在单因素设计中，总 SSCP 分成组间 $H$ 与组内 $E$。非对角元保留响应间交叉乘积，因此这不是一列列平方和的简单并排。

![[MANOVA SSCP分解]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $E$ positive definite, the nonnegative roots comparing $H$ with $E$ can be aggregated in four ways. Smaller Wilks and larger Pillai, Hotelling–Lawley or Roy values oppose the null. The six-observation example carries the same matrices from the decomposition into all four criteria.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$H$ 相对 $E$ 的广义特征根可以按四种方式汇总。Wilks 越小越反对零假设，另外三种越大越反对；它们不必产生相同的近似 $F$ 或 $p$ 值。

![[MANOVA四种统计量]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some fixed-effects simulation comparisons favour Pillai–Bartlett, but the scope matters. A lower false-rejection rate than competing tests may still exceed the nominal level. This evidence does not replace design, covariance, rank or calibration checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择统计量时，Pillai–Bartlett trace 在部分固定效应仿真中表现出较好的比较稳健性，但这是一条有范围的经验结论，不能替代独立性、协方差、秩与校准检查。

![[Pillai稳健性边界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally distinguish the criterion from its calibration. The model, hypothesis dimension, response dimension and residual degrees of freedom determine the reference law; software commonly uses an approximate F conversion. [[多响应联合显著边界|Response-wise follow-up]] requires its own interpretation and multiplicity control, while [[多响应因果边界|causal interpretation]] additionally needs identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后区分统计量与校准：软件常报告近似 $F$，其换算依赖假设维数、响应维数和误差自由度。总体拒绝后的逐响应定位见 [[多响应联合显著边界]]；能否作因果解释另见 [[多响应因果边界]]。

![[MANOVA检验校准]]

### 4. 多元线性回归
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Multivariate Linear Regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mean comparisons treat group membership as a discrete explanatory variable. Multivariate regression generalises this setting to continuous or discrete predictors jointly modelling the conditional mean of a response vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值比较把组别当离散解释变量；多响应回归把它推广为多个连续或离散预测变量共同建模一个响应向量的条件均值。

![[多元线性回归.canvas]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with $Y=XB+U$: $n$ rows are observations, $q$ columns are responses, and the $k$ columns of $X$ contain the predictors and any intercept. The condition $\mathbb E[U\mid X]=0$ makes $XB$ the conditional mean. It does not require the realised model errors to sum to zero; fitted residuals are a later, different object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先打开真正的概念入口：多响应线性回归用同一设计矩阵同时建模一个响应向量的条件均值，并写成 $Y=XB+U$。这里 $n$ 是观测数，$q$ 是响应数，$k$ 是设计列数（包括所用截距）；$\mathbb E[U\mid X]=0$ 不是要求一次样本中的模型误差之和为零，也不是对拟合残差的定义。

![[多响应线性回归]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First fix the terminology: multivariate regression here is defined by several responses. The course chapter with the same Chinese title mainly treats one-response multiple regression, so the title alone cannot merge the two models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定术语：本节的 multivariate regression 按多个响应定义；本课程同名第 7 章实际主要讲单响应 multiple regression，不能靠中文标题合并。

![[多响应与多解释变量回归]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[零假设与备择假设|null hypothesis]] states the population claim being tested. Before estimation, write its coefficient and response directions as $CBA=D$: $D$ contains the proposed values, and the restrictions must be estimable from the mean model. The hypothesis and error SSPs must refer to the same response space, with $E_A=A^TE_{SSP}A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[零假设与备择假设|零假设]]说明要检验的总体说法。在估计之前先用 $CBA=D$ 写清系数与响应方向，$D$ 给出假设值，并检查限制能否由均值模型确定。假设与误差 SSP 必须处于同一响应空间，误差项为 $E_A=A^TE_{SSP}A$。

![[多响应一般线性假设]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matrix-normal model adds a joint Gaussian distribution to the mean model and specifies separable error covariance. For fixed $X$, write $Y\sim MN(XB,\Omega,\Sigma)$: $\Omega$ is the $n\times n$ row factor and $\Sigma$ the $q\times q$ response factor. Stacking columns of $U$ gives covariance $\Sigma\otimes\Omega$; swapping the factors changes that convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵正态模型在均值模型之外加入联合 Gaussian 分布和可分误差协方差。固定 $X$ 后写成 $Y\sim MN(XB,\Omega,\Sigma)$；$\Omega$ 是 $n\times n$ 的行因子，$\Sigma$ 是 $q\times q$ 的响应因子。按列堆叠 $U$ 时协方差是 $\Sigma\otimes\Omega$，不能把两个方向互换。

![[矩阵正态误差结构]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For error row vectors written as columns $u_i$, separability means $\operatorname{Cov}(u_i,u_j)=\Omega_{ij}\Sigma$. The whole block is not just $\Omega_{ij}$, and the two factors need a scale convention. $\Omega=I_n$, an unequal positive diagonal, and nonzero off-diagonal entries describe common-scale uncorrelated rows, heteroskedastic rows, and correlated rows respectively. Under joint Gaussianity, uncorrelatedness implies independence; zero covariance alone does not establish independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把第 $i$ 行模型误差写成列向量 $u_i$，可分结构的准确含义是 $\operatorname{Cov}(u_i,u_j)=\Omega_{ij}\Sigma$，不是直接把 $\Omega_{ij}$ 当作整个协方差块；两个因子还需固定尺度约定。$I_n$、异方差对角矩阵和带非零非对角元的相关行模型，是三种不同假设；在联合 Gaussian 条件下，行间不相关可推出独立；单凭零协方差不能保证独立。

![[观测行协方差]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common rows, a shared design, ordinary squared loss, and freely varying coefficient columns let the objective split by response; this algebra does not require Gaussian errors. Response correlation still enters joint uncertainty. A fixed positive-definite response weighting can keep the same OLS minimisers even when the weighted objective no longer separates by columns; it is different from weighting observations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共享行、共享设计与普通平方损失使点估计按响应列分离；响应相关性仍会进入联合不确定性。这里还要求系数列可自由变化，不能另加跨响应约束；按列分离本身不需要 Gaussian 假设。固定的正定响应权重虽可使目标不再按列分开，仍可保留同一 OLS 最小点；这与给观测行加权不同。

![[共享设计的逐响应OLS]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After fitting, $R=Y-X\hat B$ is an $n\times q$ residual matrix and $E_{SSP}=R^TR$ is a $q\times q$ matrix of sums of squares and cross-products. Their dimensions and diagnostic uses differ, but both arise from the same residual space, of dimension $\nu=n-\operatorname{rank}(X)$. The fitted $R$ is not the unobserved model error $U$, and $E_{SSP}$ has not yet been divided by any denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估计后必须区分逐观测残差矩阵 $R=Y-X\hat B$ 与压缩后的误差 SSP $E_{SSP}=R^TR$；二者维度和诊断用途不同，却来自同一个维数为 $\nu=n-\operatorname{rank}(X)$ 的残差空间，不能说它们各有不同的残差自由度。拟合残差 $R$ 也不等于未观测的模型误差 $U$；误差 SSP 本身尚未除以任何分母。

![[多响应残差与误差SSP]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For fixed $X$ and a correct mean $XB$, iid zero-mean Gaussian model-error rows with common positive-definite covariance $\Sigma$ yield $E_{SSP}\sim W_q(\Sigma,\nu)$ when $\nu=n-\operatorname{rank}(X)>0$. The original fitted residual rows generally remain dependent; when $\nu=0$, $E_{SSP}=0$. With positive $\nu$, $E_{SSP}/\nu$ is unbiased even without Gaussianity if error rows are uncorrelated, zero-mean, and share a finite covariance. Under the Gaussian model, the internal covariance MLE is $E_{SSP}/n$ only when $E_{SSP}$ is positive definite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $X$、正确均值 $XB$，以及具有共同正定协方差 $\Sigma$ 的独立同分布零均值 Gaussian 模型误差行，给出 $\nu=n-\operatorname{rank}(X)>0$ 时的 $E_{SSP}\sim W_q(\Sigma,\nu)$；原拟合残差行一般仍相关。$\nu=0$ 时 $E_{SSP}=0$。正自由度下，$E_{SSP}/\nu$ 的无偏性只需零均值、不相关误差行及共同有限协方差；Gaussian 模型中的内部协方差 MLE 则要求 $E_{SSP}$ 正定，才可写为 $E_{SSP}/n$。

![[误差SSP抽样律]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A correctly calibrated omnibus rejection gives evidence against the prespecified joint null. It neither proves a particular response differs nor establishes significance for every response. The next card shows why a combination can be unusual even when neither coordinate rejects, then separates that joint evidence from follow-up comparisons with suitable multiplicity control.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

校准正确的总体拒绝提供反对预设联合零假设的证据，不证明某个响应必然不同，更不能扩写成“每个响应都显著”。下卡说明为什么逐坐标都不拒绝时，组合仍可能异常；再把联合证据与需要多重性控制的后续比较分开。

![[多响应联合显著边界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Statistical localisation and causal identification are different questions. Define the intervention and target population before interpreting a response coefficient as an effect. The next card constructs nonzero associations with zero individual causal effects, then states a sufficient identification route. A causal joint test needs that identification argument as well as valid statistical calibration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

统计定位和因果识别是两件事。把响应系数解释为效应之前，先定义干预与目标总体。下卡先构造个体因果效应全为零、观察关联却不为零的例子，再说明一条充分识别路径。因果联合检验既需要识别论证，也需要有效统计校准。

![[多响应因果边界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now check residual rank. Let $r=\operatorname{rank}(X)$ and $\nu=n-r$; ordinary least squares gives $\operatorname{rank}(E_{SSP})=\operatorname{rank}(R)\le\min(q,\nu)$. Thus $q>\nu$ forces singularity. Having $q\le\nu$ merely permits full rank for fixed data, and invertibility does not guarantee stable estimation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

然后检查残差秩。令 $r=\operatorname{rank}(X)$、$\nu=n-r$，普通最小二乘满足 $\operatorname{rank}(E_{SSP})=\operatorname{rank}(R)\le\min(q,\nu)$。响应数超过残差自由度时必奇异；不超过也只容许满秩，并不保证固定数据一定满秩，能够求逆更不保证估计稳定。

![[误差SSP秩边界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated measurements, clusters, and time ordering are reasons to check dependence, not automatic proofs of it. If model-error rows are correlated, independent-row standard errors and the usual exact Wishart calibration generally cannot be carried over; point estimation and inference must be checked separately. A zero conditional error mean gives conditional OLS unbiasedness under a fixed full-column-rank design, but additional conditions are needed for consistency. The single-response [[协方差失效下的OLS|nonspherical-error OLS boundary]] explains a further distinction: a classical covariance formula or BLUE conclusion can survive in particular nonspherical designs, while the usual residual variance estimate can still fail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重复测量、群组和时序资料提示应检查行间依赖，本身并不证明误差一定相关。若模型误差行确有相关，就一般不能照搬独立行标准误与通常的精确 Wishart 校准；点估计和推断必须分开检查。固定满列秩设计下，零条件误差均值给出 OLS 条件无偏性，但一致性还需额外条件。单响应的[[协方差失效下的OLS|非球形误差边界]]还区分另一件事：特定非球形设计可以保留经典协方差公式或 BLUE 结论，而通常的残差方差估计仍可能失效。

![[相关行不容独立推断]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the separable error covariance $\Sigma\otimes\Omega$, known positive-definite $\Omega$, positive-definite $\Sigma$, and a full-column-rank design, GLS uses the same $\Omega^{-1}$-weighted projection for every response. $\Omega=cI_n$ with $c>0$ is sufficient for GLS and OLS to agree, but is not necessary for every design; the GLS card gives a correlated-row example with the same ordinary mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在可分误差协方差 $\Sigma\otimes\Omega$、已知正定 $\Omega$、正定 $\Sigma$ 及满列秩设计下，GLS 对每个响应使用同一个 $\Omega^{-1}$ 加权投影。$\Omega=cI_n$（$c>0$）足以使 GLS 与 OLS 相同，却不是对每个设计都必要；下面的 GLS 卡给出相关行仍产生同一个普通平均数的例子。

![[相关行下的多响应GLS]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a clustering level from the target population, sampling and assignment design, and plausible dependence. Standard one-way cluster inference allows within-cluster dependence but relies on many conditionally independent clusters and regularity conditions, including control of dominant clusters. Few clusters or substantial cross-cluster dependence need further justification; merely using a broader label does not guarantee valid inference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

聚类层级应由目标总体、抽样与分配设计，以及可信的依赖机制决定。常规单向聚类推断允许簇内依赖，但依赖大量在给定设计后独立的簇及控制主导簇等正则条件。少簇或明显跨簇依赖需要进一步论证；简单改用更大的分组标签不保证推断有效。

![[聚类标准误边界]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally treat units and the statistical question together. With invertible $T$ and $Y^*=YT$, the same hypothesis $CBA=D$ becomes $CB^*(T^{-1}A)=D$. For a full-column-rank design, or an estimable contrast under rank deficiency, transporting the contrast weights preserves its hypothesis and error SSPs. The full-response criteria likewise agree when defined and consistently transformed. Keeping old contrast weights after changing units can ask a different question; dimension reduction and regularisation need separate checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后把单位与统计问题一起处理。可逆变换 $Y^*=YT$ 后，同一个 $CBA=D$ 要写成 $CB^*(T^{-1}A)=D$。满列秩设计下，或秩亏但对比可估时，同步搬运对比权重会保留其假设与误差 SSP；完整响应统计量在有定义且同步变换时也一致。改单位却沿用原权重可能是在问另一个问题；降维与正则化另需检查。

![[响应尺度与线性变换]]

### 6. 判别分析与聚类
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Discriminant Analysis and Clustering*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

降维先提取结构，判别和聚类再利用距离、密度或中心把观察分到已知或未知的群组。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dimensionality reduction first extracts structure; discriminant analysis and clustering then use distances, densities, or centres to assign observations to known or unknown groups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[判别分析.canvas]]

先分清问题本身：标签已经定义时，判别与分类学习怎样分配新对象；没有标签时，聚类只是在给定表示、距离与算法下探索分组。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with the task itself. With defined labels, discrimination and classification learn how to assign new observations; without labels, clustering explores groups under a chosen representation, distance, and algorithm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[判别分类与聚类]]

有了类别，Bayes 原则先把“选哪个类别”写成行动与损失问题。最大 posterior 只是 0–1 loss 的特例；先验与误判成本改变时，最优规则也随之改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once classes are defined, the Bayes principle formulates classification as a decision-and-loss problem. Choosing the largest posterior is the 0–1 loss special case; changing priors or error costs changes the optimal rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Bayes分类规则]]

![[先验与分类风险]]

若再为每一类指定 Gaussian 分布，posterior 比较可写成判别得分。共享 covariance 得到 LDA 的线性边界；类别各有 covariance 得到通常为二次的 QDA 边界；Fisher 则从投影分离准则进入同一问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If each class is given a Gaussian model, posterior comparison becomes a discriminant score. Shared covariance gives LDA's linear boundary; class-specific covariances give QDA's usually quadratic boundary; Fisher approaches the same problem through a projection-separation criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Gaussian判别得分]]

![[LDA共享协方差]]

![[QDA类别协方差]]

![[Fisher判别]]

![[Fisher与LDA]]

经典公式都需要估计 covariance。先分清 LDA 的合并组内估计与 QDA 的逐类估计，再单独检查样本量和维数给出的秩上界；实际能否求逆、估计是否稳定，还取决于精确共线与特征值的条件性。正则化是在证据不足时加入结构约束，而不是让 QDA 自动变得可靠。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every classical formula requires covariance estimation. Discriminant analysis learns a classification boundary from known labels. Clustering searches for structure without labels; although both produce groups, their information and evaluation criteria are fundamentally different.

聚类不是一个从数据中读取“真实类别”的按钮，而是一类无监督探索任务。它先定义对象怎样进入分析：观察单位和特征决定什么差异可见，缩放给变量设权，距离把“什么算接近”写成可计算规则；随后还要检查这套相异度是否真的匹配所选算法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Clustering is not a button that reads true classes from data, but a family of unsupervised exploratory tasks. It first defines how objects enter the analysis: observational units and features determine which differences are visible, scaling assigns feature weights, and distance turns the meaning of proximity into a computation. The chosen dissimilarity must then be checked against the algorithm that will use it.
