### 2. 多元正态分布与 Wishart 分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Multivariate Normal and Wishart Distributions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值向量和协方差只给前两阶描述；多元正态进一步给出可推导边际、条件和抽样分布的联合模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mean vector and covariance matrix provide only first- and second-order descriptions. The multivariate normal supplies a joint model from which marginal, conditional, and sampling distributions can be derived.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多元正态分布.canvas]]

先固定“联合 Gaussian”究竟要求什么：它不是各坐标分别正态，而是每一个线性方向都正态；半正定协方差也允许退化的 Gaussian 向量存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First fix what “jointly Gaussian” means. Coordinate-wise normality is insufficient: every linear direction must be normal, and a positive-semidefinite covariance may define a degenerate Gaussian vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[联合Gaussian]]

有了定义，仿射变换便给出最常用的计算规则；取边际只是选择部分坐标的特殊矩阵变换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definition yields the main computational rule: affine transformations preserve Gaussianity, and marginalisation is a coordinate-selection special case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Gaussian仿射闭包]]

接着按协方差秩分流。正定协方差产生全维椭球密度；奇异协方差仍定义 Gaussian 律，但概率只落在低维仿射支撑上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next branch by covariance rank. Positive definiteness gives a full-dimensional ellipsoidal density, whereas a singular covariance confines a valid Gaussian law to a lower-dimensional affine support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Gaussian密度与奇异支撑]]

分块以后，条件分布把观测到的一块转化为另一块的线性均值调整；普通 Schur-complement 公式要求被条件化的协方差块可逆。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After partitioning the vector, conditioning turns one observed block into a linear adjustment of the other. The ordinary Schur-complement formula requires an invertible conditioning covariance block.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Gaussian条件分布]]

条件分布的残差推导会用到 Gaussian 的一个特殊边界：联合 Gaussian 下零交叉协方差推出独立，只有 Gaussian 边际则仍然不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conditional-residual argument uses a special Gaussian boundary: zero cross-covariance implies independence for a jointly Gaussian vector, but Gaussian marginals alone remain insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[联合高斯独立判据]]

用真实总体均值和正定协方差时，白化把平方 Mahalanobis 距离变成独立标准正态的平方和；用同一数据估计均值和协方差后不能照搬这条精确卡方律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the true population mean and positive-definite covariance, whitening turns squared Mahalanobis distance into a sum of independent standard-normal squares. Fitting the centre and covariance on the same sample invalidates that exact reference law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Gaussian马氏平方律]]

先固定诊断的认识论边界：边际正态只检查坐标方向，同样本 Mahalanobis 图又不是精确独立的卡方抽样。两者都能暴露不相容，却不能单独证成联合 Gaussian。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First fix the epistemic boundary: normal marginals inspect only coordinate directions, and fitted-sample Mahalanobis plots do not have an exact independent chi-square law. Neither can establish joint Gaussianity by itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[边际与马氏图不证联合Gaussian]]

最后执行完整诊断流程：把数据来源、边际图、联合投影、Mahalanobis 结构与下游敏感性合并解释，而不是让一张图代替模型判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally apply the full workflow by combining data provenance, marginal plots, joint projections, Mahalanobis structure, and downstream sensitivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[联合Gaussian诊断]]

多元正态分布规定总体随机向量的联合形状；从该总体抽样后，Wishart 分布刻画样本协方差矩阵的随机性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The multivariate normal distribution specifies the joint shape of the population vector. Under sampling from that population, the Wishart distribution describes the randomness of the sample covariance matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Wishart 分布与样本协方差推断.canvas]]

先用整数自由度的 Gaussian 外积和固定 Wishart 的对象与尺度，再把正定密度下的实数自由度看作这一分布族的延拓；一维卡方是整数生成式的特例。

![[Wishart分布]]

把 Gaussian 样本中心化后，剩下的 $n-1$ 个残差方向产生样本协方差的精确 Wishart 律；同一个正交分解还把样本均值与样本协方差分开。

![[样本协方差Wishart律]]

![[正态均值协方差独立]]

自由度不仅是分布参数，也限制外积和能张成的维数；因此求逆前必须先检查 $n$ 与 $p$ 的关系。

![[Wishart秩与可逆性]]

最后核对两个边界：作者把矩阵参数放在尺度还是 precision 位置，以及数据是否真的满足 iid 多元正态。前者防止代错公式，后者决定精确有限样本推断能否成立。

![[Wishart参数化]]

![[Wishart抽样假设]]

### 3. 多元均值推断与 MANOVA
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Inference for Multivariate Means and MANOVA*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有了多元正态和 Wishart 抽样理论，才能构造均值向量的置信区域、Hotelling 检验和 MANOVA。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The multivariate normal and Wishart sampling theory make it possible to construct confidence regions for mean vectors, Hotelling tests, and MANOVA.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Hotelling T² 与多元均值推断.canvas]]

先固定单样本统计量、精确 F 校准和不可逆边界；后续设计都复用这条核心定理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First fix the one-sample statistic, its exact F calibration, and its invertibility boundary; later designs reuse this core theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[单样本Hotelling T²]]

再从所有线性投影理解“全局显著”的含义：它只定位到某个方向，不会自动让每个响应都显著。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next use all linear projections to interpret omnibus significance: it identifies some direction, not every response automatically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Hotelling投影极值]]

若科学问题只约束 $D\mu$，就在约束的实际秩上降维：精确校准只要求样本量超过该秩，不要求原始 $p$ 维样本协方差可逆。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the scientific question constrains only $D\mu$, reduce to the actual rank of the restrictions. Exact calibration requires the sample size to exceed that rank, not invertibility of the original $p$-dimensional sample covariance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[线性约束Hotelling T²]]

反演全局检验得到均值参数的置信椭球；同一几何事件同时控制每一个线性方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inverting the omnibus test gives a confidence ellipsoid for the mean parameter, whose single geometric event controls every linear direction simultaneously.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Hotelling置信椭球]]

若只关心少数预先指定的方向，可改用覆盖这组有限对比的 Bonferroni 区间；它只用投影方差而不求 $S^{-1}$，但不能看见显著结果后再选方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a small prespecified set of directions, finite-family Bonferroni intervals use projected variances rather than $S^{-1}$; the directions cannot be selected after significance is observed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Bonferroni对比区间]]

最后按抽样单位分流：真实配对资料先在每一对内部取差，两个独立总体只有在共同协方差模型下才使用 pooled 版本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally branch by sampling unit: genuine pairs are differenced within pair, whereas independent populations use the pooled version only under a common covariance model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[配对Hotelling T²]]

![[pooled Hotelling T²]]

Hotelling $T^2$ 先比较一个多元均值向量；MANOVA 再把同一思想推广到多个组或多个效应，同时检验一组相关结果变量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hotelling’s $T^2$ first compares a multivariate mean vector. MANOVA extends the same idea to several groups or effects while testing a set of correlated outcomes jointly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[MANOVA 多元方差分析.canvas]]

先固定 MANOVA 的对象：同一批观测共享设计矩阵，同时拥有多个相关响应；检验用 $H$ 与 $E$ 联合评价预设的系数—响应假设。单因素均值向量相等只是最小特例，两组共同协方差情形与 pooled Hotelling $T^2$ 是同一检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First fix the MANOVA object: observations share one design and carry several responses, while hypothesis and error SSCP matrices evaluate a prespecified joint hypothesis. Equality of one-way group mean vectors is the simplest special case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[MANOVA]]

统计量能否按经典参考分布解释，先由独立观测、联合多元正态、共同组内协方差、可估性和残差秩决定；诊断只会发现冲突，不能证明这些总体假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Classical calibration first requires independent observations, a joint Gaussian model, common within-group covariance, estimability, and sufficient residual rank. Diagnostics can reveal conflicts but cannot prove population assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[MANOVA经典假设]]

在单因素设计中，总 SSCP 分成组间 $H$ 与组内 $E$。非对角元保留响应间交叉乘积，因此这不是一列列平方和的简单并排。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a one-way design, total SSCP splits into between-group $H$ and within-group $E$. Off-diagonal cross-products preserve dependence among responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[MANOVA SSCP分解]]

$H$ 相对 $E$ 的广义特征根可以按四种方式汇总。Wilks 越小越反对零假设，另外三种越大越反对；它们不必产生相同的近似 $F$ 或 $p$ 值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The generalized roots comparing $H$ with $E$ can be aggregated in four ways. Smaller Wilks and larger Pillai, Hotelling–Lawley, or Roy values oppose the null, but their approximate tests need not agree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[MANOVA四种统计量]]

选择统计量时，Pillai–Bartlett trace 在部分固定效应仿真中表现出较好的比较稳健性，但这是一条有范围的经验结论，不能替代独立性、协方差、秩与校准检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Comparative studies often favour Pillai–Bartlett trace in some fixed-effects settings, but this qualified robustness evidence does not replace design, covariance, rank, or calibration checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pillai稳健性边界]]

最后区分统计量与校准：软件常报告近似 $F$，其换算依赖假设维数、响应维数和误差自由度。总体拒绝后的逐响应定位见 [[多响应联合显著边界]]；能否作因果解释另见 [[多响应因果边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally distinguish the criterion from its calibration. Software commonly reports an approximate $F$ whose conversion depends on hypothesis dimension, response dimension, and error degrees of freedom. The later [[多响应联合显著边界|joint-significance boundary]] owns response-wise follow-up, while [[多响应因果边界|the causal boundary]] owns identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[MANOVA检验校准]]

### 4. 多元线性回归
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Multivariate Linear Regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值比较把组别当离散解释变量；多响应回归把它推广为多个连续或离散预测变量共同建模一个响应向量的条件均值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mean comparisons treat group membership as a discrete explanatory variable. Multivariate regression generalises this setting to continuous or discrete predictors jointly modelling the conditional mean of a response vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多元线性回归.canvas]]

先打开真正的概念入口：多响应线性回归用同一设计矩阵同时建模一个响应向量的条件均值，并写成 $Y=XB+U$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with the actual concept entry: multivariate linear regression uses one shared design to model the conditional mean of a response vector and writes the model as $Y=XB+U$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多响应线性回归]]

先固定术语：本节的 multivariate regression 按多个响应定义；本课程同名第 7 章实际主要讲单响应 multiple regression，不能靠中文标题合并。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First fix the terminology: multivariate regression here is defined by several responses. The course chapter with the same Chinese title mainly treats one-response multiple regression, so the title alone cannot merge the two models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多响应与多解释变量回归]]

在估计之前先写清要检验的系数方向和响应方向。一般问题是 $CBA=D$；若 $A\ne I_q$，误差 SSP 也必须同步投影为 $A^TE_{SSP}A$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before estimation, state the coefficient and response directions to be tested. The general question is $CBA=D$; when $A\ne I_q$, the error SSP must also be projected to $A^TE_{SSP}A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多响应一般线性假设]]

经典精确推断把观测行协方差与同一观测内的响应协方差分开；$I_n$ 与 $\Sigma$ 不能互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Classical exact inference separates covariance across observational rows from covariance among responses within a row; $I_n$ and $\Sigma$ are not interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[矩阵正态误差结构]]

在这个模型中，$\Omega$ 专门描述观测行之间的协方差。$I_n$、异方差对角矩阵和带非零非对角元的相关行模型，是三种不同假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within this model, $\Omega$ specifically describes covariance across observational rows. $I_n$, a heteroskedastic diagonal matrix, and a correlated-row matrix with nonzero off-diagonal entries are different assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[观测行协方差]]

共享行、共享设计与普通平方损失使点估计按响应列分离；响应相关性仍会进入联合不确定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common rows, a shared design, and ordinary squared loss make the point estimate separable by response, while response dependence still enters joint uncertainty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[共享设计的逐响应OLS]]

估计后必须区分逐观测残差矩阵与压缩后的误差 SSP；二者维度、诊断用途和自由度都不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After fitting, distinguish the case-level residual matrix from the compressed error SSP. They have different dimensions, diagnostic roles, and degrees of freedom.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多响应残差与误差SSP]]

加入独立 Gaussian 行后，误差 SSP 才获得自由度为 $n-\operatorname{rank}(X)$ 的 Wishart 抽样律；无偏协方差估计和 Gaussian MLE 因准则不同而使用不同分母。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only after adding independent Gaussian rows does the error SSP acquire a Wishart law with $n-\operatorname{rank}(X)$ degrees of freedom. The unbiased covariance estimator and the Gaussian MLE use different denominators because they optimise different criteria.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[误差SSP抽样律]]

总体拒绝只定位到至少一个被检验组合，不能扩写成“每个响应都显著”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An omnibus rejection locates at least one tested combination; it cannot be expanded into significance for every response.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多响应联合显著边界]]

统计定位和因果识别是两件事。多响应形式不会自动提供随机分配、无混杂或其他识别依据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Statistical localisation and causal identification are different questions. A multivariate response model does not automatically provide randomisation, no-confounding conditions, or another identification design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多响应因果边界]]

然后检查两个不同的失效方向。响应数接近或超过残差自由度时，误差 SSP 会不稳定或必然奇异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then check two distinct failure directions. When the number of responses approaches or exceeds residual degrees of freedom, the error SSP becomes unstable or necessarily singular.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[误差SSP秩边界]]

重复测量、群组和时序资料会使独立行标准误与精确 Wishart 校准失效；点估计和推断必须分开检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated-measures, clustered, and time-ordered data invalidate independent-row standard errors and exact Wishart calibration; point estimation and inference must be checked separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[相关行不容独立推断]]

若 $\Omega$ 已知且正定，GLS 对每个响应使用同一个 $\Omega^{-1}$ 加权投影；$\Omega\propto I_n$ 时才退化为普通 OLS。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\Omega$ is known and positive definite, GLS applies the same $\Omega^{-1}$-weighted projection to every response; it reduces to ordinary OLS only when $\Omega\propto I_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[相关行下的多响应GLS]]

若改用 cluster-robust 标准误，聚类层级必须覆盖共同冲击或共同处理分配；少簇时默认大样本 $p$ 值不可靠。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With cluster-robust standard errors, the clustering level must cover shared shocks or treatment assignment; default large-sample $p$-values are unreliable with few clusters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[聚类标准误边界]]

最后把单位与统计问题一起处理：可逆响应变换能保留完整空间检验，却会改变坐标解释；具体对比、降维和正则化并不自动尺度不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally treat units and the statistical question together. An invertible response transformation can preserve a full-space test while changing coordinate interpretations; specific contrasts, dimension reduction, and regularisation are not automatically scale invariant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[响应尺度与线性变换]]

### 5. 主成分分析与因子分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Principal Component Analysis and Factor Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归保留预先指定的响应—解释方向；PCA 和因子分析转而处理协方差结构，但前者做低维压缩，后者提出潜变量生成模型。先用主题图看完整路径，再按原子逐项恢复定义、计算和边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regression preserves a pre-specified response–explanatory direction. PCA and factor analysis both use covariance structure, but PCA compresses observed variation whereas factor analysis posits a latent-variable model. Use the map first, then recover each definition and boundary from the atoms.

PCA transforms centred multivariate data into linear combinations that are uncorrelated and ordered by decreasing variance. This definition then leads to the optimisation target, eigensystem, and observation-level scores.

Scores are observation coordinates along component directions. Loadings relate original variables to components, but must be defined as eigenvector coefficients or variable–component correlations. Explained variance is first a defined share of total variance; the fact that it is not a universal measure of task information is a separate boundary. Dimension choice must also reflect purpose, stability, and independent validation rather than a universal cumulative cutoff.

Covariance and correlation PCA weight variables differently. Overall sign reversal and repeated-eigenvalue basis non-uniqueness are distinct identification boundaries affecting software comparisons and subspace stability.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
PCA compresses observed variables by retaining total variance. Factor analysis instead assumes that a smaller set of latent factors generates the common covariance, so it addresses explanatory structure rather than compression alone.

### 6. 判别分析与聚类
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Discriminant Analysis and Clustering*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

降维先提取结构，判别和聚类再利用距离、密度或中心把观察分到已知或未知的群组。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dimensionality reduction first extracts structure; discriminant analysis and clustering then use distances, densities, or centres to assign observations to known or unknown groups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[判别分析.canvas]]

先分清问题本身：标签已经定义时，判别与分类学习怎样分配新对象；没有标签时，聚类只是在给定表示、距离与算法下探索分组。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with the task itself. With defined labels, discrimination and classification learn how to assign new observations; without labels, clustering explores groups under a chosen representation, distance, and algorithm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[判别分类与聚类]]

有了类别，Bayes 原则先把“选哪个类别”写成行动与损失问题。最大 posterior 只是 0–1 loss 的特例；先验与误判成本改变时，最优规则也随之改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once classes are defined, the Bayes principle formulates classification as a decision-and-loss problem. Choosing the largest posterior is the 0–1 loss special case; changing priors or error costs changes the optimal rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Bayes分类规则]]

![[先验与分类风险]]

若再为每一类指定 Gaussian 分布，posterior 比较可写成判别得分。共享 covariance 得到 LDA 的线性边界；类别各有 covariance 得到通常为二次的 QDA 边界；Fisher 则从投影分离准则进入同一问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If each class is given a Gaussian model, posterior comparison becomes a discriminant score. Shared covariance gives LDA's linear boundary; class-specific covariances give QDA's usually quadratic boundary; Fisher approaches the same problem through a projection-separation criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Fisher判别]]

![[Fisher与LDA]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every classical formula requires covariance estimation. First distinguish LDA's pooled within-class estimator from QDA's classwise estimators, then check the separate rank limits imposed by sample size and dimension. Actual invertibility and stability also depend on exact collinearity and eigenvalue conditioning. Regularisation adds structural constraints when evidence is limited rather than making QDA automatically reliable.

最后把“算出了类别”与“模型可用”分开。不同指标有不同条件分母，全部数据驱动步骤必须留在训练折，概率还要单独校准；部署前则要回到标签、封闭类别、分布与人群漂移做诊断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally separate producing a class label from establishing that the model is useful. Metrics use different conditioning denominators, all data-dependent steps must remain inside training folds, and probabilities require separate calibration. Deployment also requires checks of labels, closed classes, distributional assumptions, and population shift.

判别分析利用已知标签学习分类边界；聚类在没有标签时寻找内部结构，两者看似都“分组”，但监督信息和评价标准完全不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Discriminant analysis learns a classification boundary from known labels. Clustering searches for structure without labels; although both produce groups, their information and evaluation criteria are fundamentally different.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[聚类.canvas]]

聚类不是一个从数据中读取“真实类别”的按钮，而是一类无监督探索任务。它先定义对象怎样进入分析：观察单位和特征决定什么差异可见，缩放给变量设权，距离把“什么算接近”写成可计算规则；随后还要检查这套相异度是否真的匹配所选算法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Clustering is not a button that reads true classes from data, but a family of unsupervised exploratory tasks. It first defines how objects enter the analysis: observational units and features determine which differences are visible, scaling assigns feature weights, and distance turns the meaning of proximity into a computation. The chosen dissimilarity must then be checked against the algorithm that will use it.
