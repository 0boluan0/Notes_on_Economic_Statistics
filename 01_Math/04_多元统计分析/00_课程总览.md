### 2. 多元正态分布与 Wishart 分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Multivariate Normal and Wishart Distributions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值向量和协方差只给前两阶描述；多元正态进一步给出可推导边际、条件和抽样分布的联合模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mean vector and covariance matrix provide only first- and second-order descriptions. The multivariate normal supplies a joint model from which marginal, conditional, and sampling distributions can be derived.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多元正态分布与 Wishart 分布]]

### 3. 多元均值推断与 MANOVA
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Inference for Multivariate Means and MANOVA*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有了多元正态和 Wishart 抽样理论，才能构造均值向量的置信区域、Hotelling 检验和 MANOVA。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The multivariate normal and Wishart sampling theory make it possible to construct confidence regions for mean vectors, Hotelling tests, and MANOVA.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多元均值推断与 MANOVA]]

### 4. 多元线性回归
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Multivariate Linear Regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值比较把组别当离散解释变量；多元回归把它推广为多个连续或离散预测变量共同解释多响应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mean comparisons treat group membership as a discrete explanatory variable. Multivariate regression generalises this setting to several continuous or discrete predictors jointly explaining multiple responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多元线性回归]]

### 5. 主成分分析与因子分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Principal Component Analysis and Factor Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归保留预先指定的响应—解释方向；PCA 和因子分析转而从协方差结构中寻找低维表示和潜在结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regression preserves a pre-specified response–explanatory direction. PCA and factor analysis instead search the covariance structure for lower-dimensional representations and latent structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[主成分分析与因子分析]]

### 6. 判别分析与聚类
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Discriminant Analysis and Clustering*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

降维先提取结构，判别和聚类再利用距离、密度或中心把观察分到已知或未知的群组。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dimensionality reduction first extracts structure; discriminant analysis and clustering then use distances, densities, or centres to assign observations to known or unknown groups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[判别分析与聚类]]
