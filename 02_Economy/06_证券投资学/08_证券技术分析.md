# 证券技术分析：从行情记录到可检验的交易规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Technical analysis: from market records to testable trading rules*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面的 [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/07_股票与企业估值|估值课]] 从经营、现金流和风险出发解释一股股票值多少。本章换一个观察对象：已经发生的价格和成交，能够被怎样描述、怎样变成规则，又怎样检验？沿正文向下读，先理解行情语言，再完成一条有真实持仓与现金核算的教学规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/07_股票与企业估值|valuation chapter]] connects operations, cash flows, and risk to value per share. This chapter asks how observed prices and trading can be described, converted into rules, and tested. Read downward from market-data language to a worked rule with explicit holdings and cash accounting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[证券技术分析.canvas|全局关系]] · [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/08_证券投资技术分析概述|概述课堂记录]] · [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/09_技术分析理论和方法|方法课堂记录]] · [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/00_课程总览|课程总览]]

本章把三件事分别核对：形态或指标的定义、依据它形成的操作规则、关于后续收益的证据。定义清楚是后两步的起点，不是后两步已经成立。数值路径和盘口例均为条件化教学计算，不是投资建议或经验收益记录。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep three questions separate: the definition of a pattern or indicator, the rule built from it, and evidence about subsequent returns. A clear definition enables the latter questions without answering them. Numerical paths and order books are conditional teaching examples, not investment recommendations or empirical performance records.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一、研究什么数据，先作出什么承诺
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Define the data and the claim before drawing a conclusion.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原概述从信息收集讲起：宏观资料、公司披露和交易记录各自回答不同问题；收集、分类、核实以后，才形成有方法、理由和适用期限的分析报告。到技术分析这里，最低要求是明确证券、频率、价格和成交量字段、调整口径，以及每条记录在当时是否已可取得。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original overview starts with collecting and verifying macroeconomic information, company disclosures, and trading records before producing a reasoned report with a stated scope. Here, specify the security, frequency, price and volume fields, adjustment basis, and when each observation actually became available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[证券技术分析]]
![[预测时点与信息集]]

例如今天下载到一份后来更正的财务报告，不能说过去的交易者当时已经读到同一版本；今天生成的复权历史也要核对它使用了哪些后来事件。基本面可以决定研究哪些公司，技术规则可以规定何时尝试交易，但组合使用不免除任何一侧的信息时点检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A subsequently corrected report downloaded today was not necessarily available in that version to a historical trader. Restated price history also requires its event cutoff. Fundamental analysis can guide security selection and technical rules can govern attempted timing, but combining them does not remove information-timing requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原课的三项假设分别提供三个研究动机：行情包含线索、方向可能延续、相似模式可能再现。下面各自给出含义；不要把“学派为什么这样研究”直接答成“市场已经被证明永远这样运行”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course's three assumptions motivate examining information in market records, persistence of direction, and recurring patterns. Each has its own meaning; a motivation for the school's method is not proof that every market always follows it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 二、先辨方向和尺度：道氏理论的观察顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Separate direction and horizon in the Dow framework.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

日线回调可以处在周线涨势中。若不先固定观察尺度，同一段价格会被一句“涨”和另一句“跌”同时描述，看似矛盾，实际说的不是同一层次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A daily pullback can sit inside a weekly advance. Without a horizon, two descriptions of the same record can appear contradictory while referring to different levels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[价格趋势]]
![[道氏理论]]
![[道氏趋势层级]]

道氏框架用价格识别主要方向，成交量提供辅助观察，再要求不同指数相互确认。这里最容易漏掉的是时间：工业指数先过前峰，运输指数几天后才过自己的前峰，双指数确认只能从后者发生时开始，不能回填到第一个突破日。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Dow framework identifies direction primarily from price, adds volume observations, and seeks confirmation between averages. If the industrial average crosses its previous peak before the transport average crosses its own, joint confirmation begins at the later event, not the first breakout date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[道氏指数确认]]

原课把顺主要方向扩量作为辅助线索，还把窄幅盘整称为“道氏线”；后面用 [[矩形整理]] 理解相似的区间几何。课件 PDF699 的历史交易表和 PDF700 未说明口径的 3%／6% 收益率阈值，不足以单独证明现代规则的净收益。考察这种主张需要回到本章最后的完整检验步骤。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course treats volume expansion with the main move as supporting evidence and calls narrow consolidation a Dow line; [[矩形整理|rectangular consolidation]] provides the related geometry. The historical table on PDF699 and unspecified 3%/6% yield thresholds on PDF700 do not by themselves establish net returns for a current rule. Such claims require the testing steps at the end of this chapter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

吞没和孕育的关键是包含方向：后一实体包住前一实体，还是后小实体落在前长实体里面。影线可以另有高低，不应把实体条件擅自升级为全区间包含。

Star patterns insert a small-body pause before opposite-direction penetration and remain incomplete before the third close. Soldiers and crows emphasise successive advances or declines; the three-methods patterns emphasise an initial move, an internal pause, and a renewed breakout.

Across many periods, support and resistance describe candidate price regions, trend lines connect selected turning points, and the trend-line channels used here add a parallel boundary. These objects use the same price record for different descriptive purposes.

## 五、多期形态：先辨轮廓，再确定何时完成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Multi-period patterns: identify the outline and its completion time.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反转指随后方向与原趋势相反，延续指整理后继续原方向；在未来尚未发生时，图形只能是某种解释的候选。原课的“价、量、时、空”在这里可以一起用：价格给轮廓，成交量作辅助观察，时间规定观察周期与持续过程，空间指价格幅度。形态维持更久或幅度更大，并不自动意味着预测更可靠。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reversal means a subsequent move against the prior trend; continuation means resuming it after consolidation. Before that future occurs, a formation is a candidate interpretation. The course's price, volume, time and space refer here to the outline, supporting volume observations, observation horizon and duration, and price amplitude. Greater duration or amplitude does not automatically improve predictive reliability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 从两个峰谷到头肩结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From repeated peaks and troughs to head and shoulders.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个相近的高点不等于双重顶已经完成：还要知道中间低点在哪里、颈线如何设定，以及价格何时按约定跌破。底部形态按相反方向阅读；三次试探、中央峰更高、缓慢转弯则是不同轮廓，不必因为都被归到“反转类”而混成一个定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two similar highs do not establish a completed double top. Locate the intervening low, define the neckline and record when the chosen downside-break criterion is met. Read bottoms in the opposite direction. Three tests, a higher central peak and gradual rounding are distinct outlines even when grouped as reversal candidates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[三重顶]]

## 六、波浪框架与选讲缺口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The wave framework and optional gap material.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

波浪理论从嵌套结构解释涨跌过程。先读基本八段模式，再区分一个驱动阶段里的推进段和回调段；“五浪阶段”不能被读成其中每个子浪都与更大一级方向相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Wave theory interprets price movement through nested structures. Start with its basic eight-wave pattern, then distinguish advances and pullbacks within a motive phase. A five-wave phase does not mean that every constituent wave moves in the larger trend's direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Test a proposed impulse count against its stated constraints. The following endpoint example shows how to reject an inconsistent label. Price checks alone do not verify all internal subdivisions or establish a uniquely correct market count.

## 七、把价格变成指标：计算口径先于买卖解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From prices to indicators: specify the calculation before interpreting signals.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图形把结构画出来，指标则把选定数据代入明确计算。窗口、初值、平滑方法、缺失值和零分母政策，都是定义的一部分；只写“用十四日 RSI”未必足以让两个人算出同一条曲线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Patterns display structure; indicators apply specified calculations to selected data. Windows, seeds, smoothing, missing values and zero-denominator conventions all affect the result. “Use a fourteen-period RSI” may not be enough for two implementations to agree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[技术指标]]
![[简单移动平均]]
![[指数移动平均]]

简单平均每个窗口内等权，指数平均递归保留较早数据的影响。缩短窗口或加大递推权重，通常使输出对近期变化更敏感，也改变平滑与响应速度；是否更适合某个决策，要在固定任务和样本划分后检验，不能从“更快”直接推出“更准”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simple average weights observations equally within its window; an exponential average recursively retains earlier influence. A shorter window or larger update weight generally increases responsiveness to recent changes, altering smoothing and lag. Suitability for a decision requires a fixed task and evaluation split; faster does not establish more accurate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 葛兰维尔八条：读完整条件，再写算法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Granville's eight rules: read the complete conditions before specifying an algorithm.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

PDF826–827 的四买四卖可按下表记忆。它描述传统候选动作：第 1–3、5–7 条围绕均线附近的突破或回靠，第 4、8 条却是价格远离均线后的反向回归想法。两组动机不同，不能缩成一句“上穿就买、下穿就卖”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
PDF826–827 presents the following four candidate buys and four candidate sells. Rules 1–3 and 5–7 concern breaks or approaches near the average; rules 4 and 8 instead anticipate reversion after a large separation. They cannot be reduced to one crossing rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 条目 | 原课候选条件与动作 |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 均线由持续下降转为走平或上升，价格从下向上穿过均线并继续向上延伸：候选买入。 |
| 2 | 均线仍在上升，价格跌到均线下方：候选买入。 |
| 3 | 价格在均线上方回落，未跌破均线便再上涨：候选买入。 |
| 4 | 均线下降，价格跌到远低于均线的位置：候选买入，押注回归。 |
| 5 | 均线由上升转为走平或下降，价格从上向下穿过均线：候选卖出。 |
| 6 | 均线仍在下降，价格上穿后又跌回均线下方：候选卖出。 |
| 7 | 价格从均线下方向上接近，未触及均线便再下跌：候选卖出。 |
| 8 | 均线上升，价格突然暴涨至远离均线的位置：候选卖出，押注回归。 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Rule | Traditional candidate condition and action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The average turns from persistent decline to flat or rising; price crosses above it and extends upward: candidate buy. |
| 2 | The average still rises while price falls below it: candidate buy. |
| 3 | Price retreats above the average, then rises without breaking it: candidate buy. |
| 4 | The average falls and price drops far below it: candidate reversion buy. |
| 5 | The average turns from rising to flat or falling; price crosses below it: candidate sell. |
| 6 | The average still falls; price crosses above, then back below it: candidate sell. |
| 7 | Price approaches from below without reaching the average, then falls again: candidate sell. |
| 8 | The average rises and price suddenly surges far above it: candidate reversion sell. |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些句子里的“转平”“大幅”“突然”“远”还不是计算参数，“继续延伸”也需规定观察与确认窗口，卖出还未区分平多与开空。第九节的 [[均线交叉规则]] 是另一个明确指定的教学策略：它使用两条均线，并完整规定等号、初值、成交和退出；不能把那套策略的结果反过来当作上述八条已经得到验证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Flat,” “large,” “sudden” and “far” still need numerical definitions; upward extension needs an observation and confirmation window, and selling does not distinguish closing a long from opening a short. Section nine's [[均线交叉规则|moving-average crossover rule]] is a separate, fully specified two-average teaching strategy. Its results would not validate all eight traditional rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[MACD]]

MACD 的 DIF、DEA 和柱线来自同一组价格及递推。这里采用卡中明确的 EMA 初值与 $H=2(DIF-DEA)$；原笔记未乘 2 的差值柱只是另一尺度，课件“算术均值”的 DEA 文字也不能替代具体平滑定义。看到“DIF 高于 DEA”又看到正柱，不是新取得了两份彼此独立的信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
MACD's DIF, DEA and histogram derive from the same prices and recursions. This path uses the stated EMA seeds and $H=2(DIF-DEA)$. The unscaled difference in the old notes is a different histogram scale, while the slides' arithmetic-mean wording does not specify this smoothing rule. DIF above DEA and a positive histogram repeat the same sign condition rather than providing two independent observations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 八、动量、量与市场宽度：同一数值不一定同一含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Momentum, volume and breadth: interpret each output through its inputs.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

RSI 比较一段时间里上涨与下跌变动的平滑均值。“超买／超卖”描述按某套指标门槛判定的极端区域，不等于价格已经高于内在价值，也不等于下一期必然反转。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
RSI compares smoothed average gains and losses. Overbought or oversold denotes an extreme region under an indicator convention, not a valuation conclusion or a necessary next-period reversal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[RSI]]
![[超买超卖]]
![[超买超卖不保证反转]]

威廉指标与 KDJ 从“收盘位于近期高低区间的哪里”出发，和 RSI 的涨跌均值不是同一分母。下面先读负值 %R，再换成正向 RSV；本路径 KDJ 采用 $J=3K-2D$。原课 WR 从顶部计正距离，WMS 从底部计正位置，两者方向互补；这些尺度和另一条 J 式都须先换口径，才可比较阈值与方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Williams %R and KDJ locate the close within a recent high–low range, unlike RSI's average gains and losses. Read negative %R before converting to positive RSV. This path uses $J=3K-2D$. The course's WR measures positive distance from the top, while WMS measures positive position from the bottom. These complementary directions and the alternate J formula require explicit conversion before comparing thresholds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 六项应用语言
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Six ways the course describes indicator use.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

PDF819 的六项法则是一套读指标的语言，不是六条已经完整写好的交易程序。背离尤其要保留价格与指标峰谷的配对方式；事后看见两个峰，不代表第二个峰刚出现时就已经可以确认它。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The six approaches on PDF819 describe how indicators are read, rather than six executable trading programs. Divergence especially requires matched price and indicator turning points; seeing two peaks afterward does not mean the second was identifiable when it first occurred.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 用法 | 读什么，接到哪里 |
| --- | --- |
| 背离 | 同尺度价格与指标对应峰谷是否方向相反 → [[指标背离]]。 |
| 交叉 | 两条指定序列的相对大小如何改变；例如 [[均线交叉规则]]。 |
| 极端值 | 数值是否进入事先规定区域 → [[超买超卖]]。 |
| 形态 | 指标曲线本身的高低点轮廓；先规定峰谷与确认方法，不能直接继承价格形态的经验结果。 |
| 转折 | 指标自身从升到降或从降到升；先定义变化与确认窗口。 |
| 趋势 | 指标在指定尺度上的持续方向；不要把指标趋势与 [[价格趋势]] 混为同一序列。 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The six approaches are divergence between matched turns; crossings of two specified series; entry into preset extreme regions; shapes in the indicator's own turning points; changes in its direction; and persistence of its direction over a stated horizon. Each requires its own identification convention. Price-pattern results do not automatically transfer to indicator shapes, and indicator trends are not the price series itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 从个股成交量转到市场上涨家数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From one security's volume to advancing and declining issues.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原课把 OBV 留作自学。它把整个期间的成交量按收盘涨跌赋号累计，不是在识别“真实净买入资金”。接下来的 OBOS、ADL 和 ADR 则先统计同一市场股票池的上涨与下跌家数，回答参与上涨是否广泛；它们不是个股动量指标的别名。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course marks OBV for self-study. It accumulates entire-period volume signed by the closing-price change; it does not identify net buying cash. OBOS, ADL and ADR instead use advancing and declining issue counts for a consistent universe to describe market participation, rather than renaming single-security momentum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[OBV]]
![[OBOS]]
![[腾落线]]
![[涨跌比率]]

三天上涨家数为 6、2、7，下跌家数为 3、6、2 时，三日 OBOS 是 4；零起点 ADL 依次是 3、−1、4；三日 ADR 是 $15/11$，不是每日三个比率的平均。这组共用输入让“滚动差额、累计路径、窗口比率”之间的区别直接可算。教材另有 BIAS，原笔记的市场人气指标一节注明“没讲”；BIAS 与 PSY 仍可回课件按需查阅，不据此推定已讲或已掌握。

For advancing counts of 6, 2 and 7 and declining counts of 3, 6 and 2, three-day OBOS is 4, zero-seeded ADL follows 3, −1 and 4, and three-day ADR is $15/11$, not the average of three daily ratios. Shared inputs make the distinction between a rolling difference, cumulative path and window ratio calculable. ## 九、从交易信号走到账户结果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Complete a rule's trading and evaluation cycle.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到这里可以把整章收束到一个问题：当时只知道那些数据，按事前写定的规则究竟会持有什么、剩多少现金？先建立账户和时序，再读两个条件完整的教学策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bring the chapter back to one question: using only the data available then and a rule specified beforehand, what would the account hold and how much cash would remain? Establish accounting and timing before reading two fully specified teaching strategies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[交易策略回测]]
![[交易信号时序]]

股票池也必须回到当时。只拿今天仍存续的股票回看历史，会把后来退市者的损失和处置过程丢掉；拆股与分红则要求同时维护每股单位、持股和现金，不能直接拿今天的一条复权曲线充当过去可成交价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reconstruct the historical universe as well. Looking back only at today's survivors removes delisted holdings and their disposal outcomes. Splits and dividends require consistent per-share units, holdings and cash; today's adjusted series is not a historical execution-price record.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[历史股票池]]
![[复权与成交价格]]
![[均线交叉规则]]

上例的交叉日先产生决定，下一时段才尝试成交；限价以外的剩余量取消，所以请求 8 股不等于买到 8 股。沿卡中日表核算，最后现金为 965.60，而非只比较两处价格得出收益。再读突破版，看同样的账户原则怎样复用于另一种入场条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The crossover example decides after the signal close and attempts execution in a later session. Cancellation beyond the limit means requesting eight shares does not imply buying eight. Its daily account ends with cash of 965.60, which cannot be obtained by merely comparing two signal prices. The breakout version reuses the accounting principles with a different entry condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[突破确认规则]]
![[最大回撤]]

最大回撤保留峰先谷后的顺序，回答“中途经历了多深的下跌”；区间收益率回答“最后怎样”。实际执行还需要成本和容量约束：手续费、价差、执行偏差和冲击要按一致口径计入，同一项摩擦不要既藏在成交价里又重复扣一遍。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maximum drawdown preserves peak-before-trough order. It answers how deep an interim loss became, whereas return describes the endpoint. Realistic execution also needs consistent charges, spreads, execution deviations and impact without counting the same friction both in prices and as a separate deduction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[证券交易成本]]

订单规模增加还会改变执行结果，见 [[市场冲击]]。实际成交相对基准的全部价格变化，不都由自己的订单造成；成本分解与因果冲击估计应分清。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Order size also changes execution; see [[市场冲击|market impact]]. Not every execution-price change relative to a benchmark was caused by one's own order. Cost decomposition and causal impact estimation are distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 从一次可回放计算走向可相信的证据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From a reproducible replay to credible evidence.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把参数改到这段历史最好看，只能证明你找到了这段数据里一个较好看的版本。先用训练段构造，在验证段选择；测试段承担最后一次独立评价。时序任务还要模拟多次真实预测起点，不能随机混洗后让未来参与过去的选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tuning a rule until one historical segment looks attractive identifies a favorable version for that segment. Construct on training data, select on validation data and reserve test data for independent final evaluation. Time-series evaluation should simulate genuine forecast origins rather than shuffle future observations into earlier decisions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[训练验证测试划分]]
![[滚动起点评估]]

冻结的是程序与选择规则，不是禁止程序使用后来正常到达的数据。按照事先约定滚动更新，与研究者看测试成绩后改窗口或阈值不同；后一种属于 [[测试集自适应复用]]。固定 SMA／EMA 的历史递推也不必在测试边界清空，只能继续使用当时已到达的价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Freeze the procedure and selection rules, not its access to subsequently arriving data. Pre-specified rolling updates differ from changing windows or thresholds after inspecting test performance—the latter is [[测试集自适应复用|adaptive test reuse]]. A fixed SMA or EMA recursion need not erase its history at a test boundary, but must use only prices available then.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准化、股票筛选、阈值和指标选择也属于被评价流程，不能只把最后一行预测留到样本外。具体分割还应匹配你想推广到的时间、证券或市场；这两个问题分别复用 [[验证管线隔离]] 与 [[验证结构匹配]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Scaling, security screening, thresholds and indicator selection belong to the evaluated pipeline; moving only the final prediction out of sample is insufficient. The split must also match the intended future periods, securities or markets. These requirements use [[验证管线隔离|pipeline isolation]] and [[验证结构匹配|validation structure matching]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

绩效指标也要各司其职：Sharpe 比较逐期超额收益的均值与其标准差，不能拿一次总收益代替逐期均值；分母为零时不能得到有限比率。当同频无风险收益恒定时，该标准差等于组合收益标准差；若无风险收益随期变化，应先逐期相减再计算。负值排序另见 [[负Sharpe排序边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Performance measures have distinct jobs. Sharpe compares the mean and standard deviation of periodic excess returns, not one total return with a periodic standard deviation; a zero denominator gives no finite ratio. With a constant matching risk-free return, that standard deviation equals portfolio-return volatility; otherwise subtract the risk-free return period by period before calculating it. See the [[负Sharpe排序边界|qualification for negative-ratio ranking]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Sharpe比率]]

Alpha 检查相对指定风险模型还剩什么。先用 [[Beta与总波动]] 分清市场暴露与整体波动：一个策略跌得少，可能只是持有更多现金或承担更少市场风险，并不自动说明有正 Alpha。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Alpha asks what remains relative to a specified risk model. Use [[Beta与总波动|beta versus total volatility]] to separate market exposure from overall variation. A smaller loss may reflect more cash or less market exposure without establishing positive alpha.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Alpha基准依赖性]]
![[市场异象复核]]

报告时把完整收益分布、换手与持仓规模、极端亏损和不同预先界定时期的表现一并保留。一个平均数会掩盖路径差异：小盘股策略要配自己的流动性与成本口径，行情阶段的划分也不能为了保住结果而事后移动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Retain the return distribution, turnover and position scale, extreme losses, and performance across pre-specified periods. An average can conceal different paths. A small-cap rule needs a matching liquidity and cost model, and period boundaries must not be moved afterward to preserve a favorable result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后再进入 [[市场效率检验口径]] 的讨论。本章短数列证明的是账目可以复算，不是已经完成了训练、验证和测试，也不足以认证 Alpha 或宣布市场无效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only then consider the [[市场效率检验口径|scope of a market-efficiency test]]. The chapter's short synthetic sequences demonstrate reproducible accounting; they do not constitute completed training, validation and testing or establish alpha or market inefficiency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
