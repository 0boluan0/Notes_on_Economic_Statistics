#### 1. 先读权利，再读名称
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Identify the rights before classifying the name*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样写“5%”，债券息票、优先股股利和基金历史回报并不是同一种承诺。先确定付款人、时点、条件和选择权；缺少条款时，正确的结论可以是“尚不能确定”。这一步决定随后能用哪种模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A 5% bond coupon, preferred dividend, and historical fund return are different statements. Identify the payer, dates, conditions, and options first. Missing terms can legitimately leave the answer undetermined. This decides which model can follow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[证券权利识别]]

#### 2. 股票与债券：谁有请求权，谁分剩余
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equity and debt: contractual claims and residual distributions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先分清权利本身与它的证券载体。[[股权]]说明公司所有权份额带来的权利；股票是这一份额的证券表达。向另一位股东买股票，钱付给卖方；公司另外宣告的股利，才是公司向股东的现金分配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish the right from its security representation. [[股权|Equity]] describes ownership rights; stock represents an ownership unit as a security. Buying from another shareholder pays the seller, whereas a separately declared dividend is a company distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[股票]]

![[普通股]]

![[优先股]]

债权则从另一边进入：它约定发行人的偿付义务。承诺可以清楚写在合同里，但到期仍可能无法兑现；因此应先认识债券，再问资源不足时怎样分配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Debt begins with the issuer's payment obligation. Clear contractual promises can still go unpaid. Define the bond first, then ask how insufficient resources are allocated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[债券]]

![[受偿顺序]]

![[资产担保与第三方保证]]

现在把文字落成一个最小模型：只有一层到期债务和普通股，没有其他优先权与执行成本。下面复用金融契约课程的同一个分配原子。有限责任限定股东的额外责任，不使债权人的损失消失；先写这些条件，才可以使用 min/max。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now use a minimal model with one debt layer, common equity, and no other priority claims or enforcement costs. The following is the same allocation atom used by the financial-contracts course. Limited liability restricts shareholders' additional liability; it does not remove creditors' losses. Establish these conditions before applying min/max.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[有限责任]]

![[股债到期分配]]

这里算出的是到期分配，不是买入后的利润。假设股东最后分得 20，买入该权利却花了 30，仍然亏 10；这条区别会在期权部分再次出现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are terminal distributions, not investment profits. Receiving 20 after paying 30 for the equity claim loses 10. The same distinction will reappear for options.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3. 债券条款如何变成一条现金流时间线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Turn bond terms into a dated cash-flow schedule*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券分类不能排成一串互斥名称。先读金额规则：没有期间息票、固定息票、按利率重置、按物价指数调整分别改变什么？然后另查谁拥有改变时间或支付形式的选择权。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bond labels are not one mutually exclusive list. First identify the payment rule: no interim coupons, fixed coupons, rate resets, or price-index adjustments. Then separately identify who can change the payment date or form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[零息债券]]

![[固定息票债券]]

![[浮动利率债券]]

![[通胀挂钩债券]]

固定票息率不必产生固定金额：刚才的通胀挂钩例中，变化的是本金。选择权也是单独的一轴：发行人可按条款提前赎回；持有人可按条款回售，或转换成股份。每种权利都会改变原现金流表的某个分支。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fixed coupon rate need not produce a fixed amount: the inflation-linked example changes principal. Options form another axis. Issuers may call, holders may put, or holders may convert under the respective terms, changing a branch of the original payment schedule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[可赎回债券]]

![[可回售债券]]

![[可转换债券]]

支付日期为什么不能忽略？一年后的 5 和两年后的 105 需要分别贴现，不能先加成 110 再一起贴现两年。先复用共同评价时点与复利，再读逐笔债券定价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why retain the dates? A payment of 5 in one year and 105 in two years need separate discounting; their sum cannot all be discounted for two years. Reuse the common-date and compounding principles before valuing the bond.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[现金流评价时点]]

![[复利与贴现]]

![[固定现金流债券定价]]

本课到这里能够从条款写出并贴现固定现金流。原课完整的存款、年金和贷款推导仍在[[01_证券投资工具#2.4 债券收益率的计算(Calculation of Bond Yield)|课堂时间价值部分]]；[[货币时间价值与贴现.canvas|时间价值关系图]]把普通年金、期初年金和永续条件连接起来。收益率曲线、净价/全价和信用利差的完整后续课直接复用[[02_Economy/05_财务管理/06_债券定价与收益率|债券定价与收益率]]，不另维护一套公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
You can now turn fixed terms into discounted cash flows. The original deposit, annuity, and loan derivations remain in the [[01_证券投资工具#2.4 债券收益率的计算(Calculation of Bond Yield)|classroom time-value section]]. The [[货币时间价值与贴现.canvas|time-value map]] connects ordinary, advance, and perpetual annuities. Continue yield curves, clean/dirty prices, and credit spreads in the shared [[02_Economy/05_财务管理/06_债券定价与收益率|bond valuation lesson]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 4. 基金：先看资产池，再分四个维度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Funds: start with the pool, then separate four dimensions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基金并非股和债之间一个固定风险等级。它把投资装进资产池，再给投资者份额权利。第一步看份额代表什么，第二步才把总净资产除以份额数，得到每份净值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fund is not a fixed risk rank between equities and bonds. It packages investments in a pool and gives investors unit rights. First establish those rights, then divide net assets by outstanding units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[证券投资基金]]

![[基金份额净值]]

接下来分别问四个问题。一个基金可以同时取得多个标签，例如“公司型、开放式、交易所交易、跟踪债券指数”，这些词各自回答不同的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ask four separate questions. One fund can be company-based, open-end, exchange-traded, and tracking a bond index: each label answers a different question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 问题 | 对应概念 | 判断重点 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 谁持有什么法律权利？ | 契约型／公司型 | 基金合同、公司治理与份额权利 |
| 能否向基金交回份额？ | 开放式／封闭式 | 申赎或转让，份额是否注销 |
| 份额在哪里交易、怎样联系资产池？ | ETF／LOF 等具体结构 | 二级交易与直接申赎的资格和对价 |
| 资产池以什么为投资目标？ | 指数基金／FOF 等 | 追踪基准或主要持有其他基金 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Question | Concepts | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What legal rights are held? | Contractual/company form | Fund contract, governance, unit rights |
| Can units be returned to the fund? | Open-end/closed-end | Redemption versus transfer and cancellation |
| How are shares traded and linked to the pool? | ETF/LOF structures | Secondary trading, direct-dealing eligibility and consideration |
| What does the pool aim to hold or track? | Index funds/FOFs | A benchmark or primarily other fund units |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[契约型基金]]

![[公司型基金]]

![[开放式基金]]

![[封闭式基金]]

能够在市场卖出，与能够向基金赎回是两种出口。下面把 ETF 的两层交易和 LOF 的渠道讲清，再问为什么份额价格不必等于净值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reselling on a market and redeeming with a fund are two exits. Clarify ETF primary/secondary dealings and LOF channels before examining why price can differ from NAV.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[ETF]]

![[上市开放式基金]]

![[基金折溢价]]

![[ETF申赎价格联结]]

“按指数投资”和“在交易所交易”也不能互换。前者是策略，后者是渠道；而 FOF 说明主要买的是什么。判断分散效果仍须穿透底层持仓，不能只数基金名称。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tracking an index is a strategy; exchange trading is a channel. FOF identifies the main holdings. Assess diversification through underlying exposures, not by counting fund names.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 5. 衍生工具：先判义务和选择，再写支付
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Derivatives: commitments, choices, and payments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把目光从资产本身转到合同：未来某个价格或利率变化时，双方如何支付？识别时始终保留两个问题：我是买方还是卖方，途中是否需要现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Turn from assets to contracts: how do the parties pay when a price or rate changes? Keep track of which side you hold and whether interim cash is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[衍生工具]]

![[远期合约]]

![[期货合约]]

远期的到期差额与期货未计息的逐日盈亏可以有相似形式，现金发生的时间却不同。期货最终赚钱，并不说明你有足够资金熬过中途追加保证金。期权再改变一个更根本的部分：有一方拿到选择权。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A forward's terminal difference and undiscounted futures settlements can look similar while occurring on different dates. Eventual futures profit does not establish the ability to fund interim margin. An option changes something more fundamental by giving one party an exercise choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[期权]]

![[看涨期权]]

![[看跌期权]]

读完两个 max 函数后，不妨问自己：为什么标的涨了，看涨期权买方仍可能亏钱？答案要把权利金放回去，并统一支付的评价时点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After the two max functions, ask why a call buyer can lose even when the underlying rises. Restore the premium and use a consistent cash-flow date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[到期支付与投资损益]]

互换则把支付安排延伸到多个日期。先逐条列出收付两腿，再区分计算本金、当期支付和合约市值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A swap extends the payment arrangement across dates. List both legs, then separate the calculation base, a period's payment, and contract market value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[金融互换]]

![[衍生品名义本金]]

#### 6. 把合约接回真实业务：套保与国债交割
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Reconnect contracts to economic activity: hedging and Treasury delivery*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

农场卖期货不是为了让那笔期货单独总赚钱，而是为了让未来售粮收入更可控。先找原风险方向，再看基差、数量和资金要求，才能解释课堂上涨与下跌两种情形。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A farmer's short futures position need not be profitable on its own. Its purpose is to make future crop receipts more predictable. Identify the original exposure, then basis, quantity, and funding requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[套期保值]]

国债期货还有一个特殊问题：多种可交割债券票息和期限不同，不能把同一个期货报价直接当作每只券的净价。转换因子提供折算系数；先分清“97.2914 的价格”和“0.9729 的比例”，再计算实际交割货款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deliverable Treasuries differ in coupon and maturity, so one futures quote cannot simply be each bond's clean price. The conversion factor supplies a scaling coefficient. Distinguish the price 97.2914 from the ratio 0.9729 before calculating the invoice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[国债期货转换因子]]

![[债券应计利息]]

![[国债期货交割货款]]

这里的数例用于理解单位与现金流，不是某张在市债券的官方转换因子表。买券交割是否划算，还需要券的实际全价、融资、付息和已有期货结算记录；仅比较券的现金价格，不能决定最便宜可交割券。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These examples explain units and cash flows, not official factors for a listed bond. Delivery economics also require the actual dirty bond price, financing, coupons, and earlier futures settlements. The lowest cash bond price alone does not identify the cheapest-to-deliver security.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 7. 另类资产与结构：继续问底层和权利
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Alternative assets and structures: retain the underlying/right distinction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[另类投资]]

原课在这一节把资产类别、持有载体与信用合约放在一起。辨认时分开：房地产是底层资产，REIT 是房地产投资载体；ABS 用资产池现金流支持证券，CDO 对债务资产或相应信用风险池分层；CDS 则是信用事件触发的保护支付与保费合约。资产池分层的机制沿[[证券化]]理解，信用合约的事件与结算可继续读下方链接。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original section combines asset classes, holding vehicles, and credit contracts. Separate them: property is an underlying asset; a REIT is a property-investment vehicle; ABS use pool cash flows to support securities; a CDO tranches debt assets or the corresponding pool of credit exposures; a CDS exchanges protection payments conditional on credit events for premiums. Follow [[证券化|securitization]] for pooling and the link below for credit events and settlement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一对象区分依据 [SEC 的 REIT 定义](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/real-estate-investment-trust-reit)、[美联储 ABS/CDO 词条](https://www.federalreserve.gov/regreform/reform-glossary.htm)及 [Gibson，合成 CDO 的参考组合与分层，PDF 第 3 页](https://www.federalreserve.gov/pubs/feds/2004/200436/200436pap.pdf#page=3)；不将某一国家的 REIT 税务制度外推为通用定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The distinction follows the SEC REIT definition, the Federal Reserve ABS/CDO glossary, and Gibson's reference-portfolio and tranching explanation on PDF p. 3. No jurisdiction's REIT tax regime is generalized.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要继续计算信用事件后的合同赔付，进入[[CDS信用事件与结算|CDS 的事件、可交割义务与结算边界]]；本课先掌握“信用合约不等于资产池分层”的区别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For contractual payouts after a credit event, continue to [[CDS信用事件与结算|CDS events, deliverable obligations, and settlement boundaries]]. This introductory step distinguishes the credit contract from asset-pool tranching.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 8. 最后再算：这段持有期间究竟赚了多少
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Only now calculate the gain over the holding period*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完成权利与现金流识别后，才有资格填回报公式。100 买入、98 卖出不一定亏 2%；若另收 5 的息票，现金总增益就是 3。多期还需区分“平均一期怎样”和“这笔钱最终增长多少”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rights and cash-flow identification supply the return formula's inputs. Buying at 100 and selling at 98 need not lose 2%: a separate coupon of 5 gives a total gain of 3. Across periods, distinguish the average period from compounded wealth growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[持有期收益率]]

![[算术与几何平均收益]]

单期组合用期初权重汇总各资产总回报，再把各期的组合回报复合；债券的到期收益率则是使承诺现金流现值等于价格的求解结果，两者不要混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Aggregate asset total returns with beginning-of-period weights, then compound the portfolio's successive returns. A bond's yield to maturity instead solves the promised-cash-flow pricing equation; it is not automatically the realized result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[单期组合收益]]

![[到期收益率与实现收益]]

风险也不能压成一个历史波动率：偿付可能失败，份额可能难卖，期货可能需要途中补现金。回到对应的权利和资金时间线，才能知道还缺哪项分析。接下来进入[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/02_证券市场与交易|证券市场与交易]]，研究这些权利如何真正报价、成交和结算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Historical volatility cannot capture every risk: promised payments can fail, units can be hard to sell, and futures can require interim cash. Return to rights and funding dates to identify the missing analysis. Next, [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/02_证券市场与交易|markets, orders, and settlement]] examines how these rights are actually quoted, traded, and settled.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 自检：把三个常见混淆拆开
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Check the distinctions with three applications*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 债权 80、公司可分资源 60；股东此前投入 20。债权人和股东各收多少，股东是否自动补足缺口？
> 在只有这一层债务、股东足额出资且无个人保证或其他责任的模型下，债权人收 60，股东收 0；缺口不自动成为股东个人债务。依据是受偿顺序和有限责任，而非“债券安全”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With debt of 80, resources of 60, fully paid equity, and no personal guarantee or other liability, creditors receive 60 and shareholders zero. The shortfall is not automatically personal shareholder debt. The reasoning uses priority and limited liability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 基金净值 10、市场价 9；以后两者都变为 8。折价消失为何仍亏钱？
> 忽略分配与费用，买入份额的 9 只变回 8，损失 1。折溢价描述价格相对净值的偏离，持有期收益才描述投资者的总增益；两个对象不同。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Buying a fund share at 9 when NAV is 10, then selling at 8 when NAV is also 8, loses 1 before distributions and costs. The discount closes, but NAV falls. The relative deviation and the investor's total return answer different questions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 看涨期权执行价 100、权利金 8、到期股价 105；买方和裸卖方分别赚亏多少？
> 每单位到期支付为 5；忽略利息和费用，买方亏 3，卖方赚 3。这个状态的结果不限制其他状态的损失：股价无上界的模型中，裸卖方损失无上界。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A call with strike 100, premium 8, and terminal stock price 105 pays 5 per unit. Before interest and costs, the buyer loses 3 and the writer gains 3. This state does not bound other outcomes: an uncovered writer has unbounded loss when stock prices have no upper bound.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先弄清每种证券承诺什么现金流和控制权，才能理解这些权利如何在市场中报价、成交、清算和结算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cash-flow and control rights promised by each security must be clear before one can understand how those rights are quoted, traded, cleared, and settled.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 二、同一笔交易中，各机构分别做什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Different institutions perform different parts of a trade*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

发行人提供证券、募集资金；投资者持有或转让权利。中介可能帮助发行销售、执行客户委托、自己承担头寸，或负责会计、法律和信用意见。区分角色比背机构名单更有用：看到某证券公司时，先问它在**这笔业务中**是代理人，还是以自己的账户作为交易一方。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Issuers supply securities and raise funds; investors hold or transfer claims. Intermediaries may assist distribution, execute client orders, take positions or provide accounting, legal and credit services. Identify the role in this transaction rather than relying on the institution's name: is the firm an agent or a principal?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[证券经纪与自营]]

接着区分组织交易与完成交收。交易所规定怎样进入和成交；场外也能有电子平台、规则和监管。下文第五节再展开清算与结算，不要把“属于同一集团”误当作“同一职责”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next distinguish organizing execution from completing settlement. Exchanges establish access and trading rules; OTC markets can also have electronic platforms, rules and oversight. Section 5 develops clearing and settlement. Common corporate ownership does not make these functions identical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[场外市场]]

交易所或行业组织可在授权范围内制定和执行会员规则，监管机关则承担法定监管职责。自律不等于不受监管，也不保证每个参与者的投资结果。课堂对会员制、公司制的优劣评价，可作为检查治理和利益冲突的线索，不能从形式直接推出“无破产风险”或“必然公平”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exchanges and industry bodies may make and enforce member rules within their authority; public regulators exercise statutory oversight. Self-regulation is neither freedom from supervision nor a guarantee of investment outcomes. The course's comparisons of mutual and corporate exchanges raise governance questions, not automatic conclusions about solvency or fairness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[发行注册与投资价值]]

至此可把三个问题分开：公司是否符合发行条件，市场是否按规则运行，以及证券按当前价格是否值得持有。它们互相影响，却没有哪一个能替另外两个作答。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three questions are now separate: does the issuer meet issuance requirements, does the market operate under its rules, and is the security attractive at its current price? They interact, but none answers the others.

##### 先处理单位变化：拆股不应凭空制造跌幅
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Handle changes in share units before interpreting returns*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

四只股票均价为 20 元，其中一股一拆三后，直接平均得到 15 元。这里变的是每股单位，不能说市场因此亏了 25%。把事件前后的指数设为相等，就能求出新的除数，而不用死记一个调整系数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four-stock mean is 20. After one stock splits three-for-one, a naive mean becomes 15. This is a change of share units, not a 25% market loss. Equating the index immediately before and after the event determines the new divisor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[指数除数调整]]

原课还给出“修正股价”的另一种算法：把拆后 D 股的价格换回原来的股数单位，得 $(10+15+25+3\times10)/4=20$。两种方法在拆股这个机械事件前后都保持 20，但若随后一直沿用各自的权重，它们对 D 股后续涨跌的敏感度可以不同；事件处同值不等于两条指数以后完全相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course also adjusts the split stock's price back to the original share unit: $(10+15+25+3\times10)/4=20$. Both methods preserve 20 across this mechanical event. If their respective weights are then retained, however, they can respond differently to D's later price changes. Agreement at the event does not make the subsequent index paths identical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 再处理权重：按股价、按市值和按等金额加权
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Different weights: equal shares, capitalization and equal cash amounts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价格加权让高单价股票影响更大，市值加权看纳入指数的总市场价值，等权则在再平衡时给各成分相同金额。下面三张卡使用相同的[[单期组合收益|期初价值权重恒等式]]；变化的是持仓规则，不是另造三套收益数学。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price weighting emphasizes high share prices; capitalization weighting uses included market value; equal weighting allocates equal amounts at rebalancing. All three reuse the same [[单期组合收益|beginning-value portfolio-return identity]]. What changes is the holding rule, not the return arithmetic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 回到课堂公式：先认清分子、分母和数量时点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Return to the course formulas: identify numerators, denominators and quantity dates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“简单算术法”先算各股价格比；“综合法”先把价格加总再作比。前者给每个价格比同样的发言权，后者隐含地偏重高基期价格。几何平均又是在平均对数变化。先看各公式的对象，再计算结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The simple arithmetic method averages individual price ratios; the aggregate method takes a ratio of price sums. The former equally weights price relatives, while the latter implicitly favors high base prices. Geometric averaging instead averages log changes. Identify the operands before calculating.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[价格相对数几何平均]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Introducing quantities asks which period's basket is compared. Laspeyres fixes old quantities; Paasche fixes current quantities. The numerator and denominator must use the same basket. Changing quantities between them mixes price and quantity changes.

#### 四、从看到报价到真正成交
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. From a displayed quote to an executed trade*

指数描述市场，交易却发生在具体证券、数量和时间上。开户和权限检查之后，投资者提交含方向、数量、价格约束和有效期的指令。能否成交、如何成交，还取决于对手方流动性与市场机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indices describe markets, but execution concerns a particular security, quantity and time. After account and permission checks, the investor submits an instruction specifying side, quantity, price constraints and duration. Execution also depends on counterparties and the trading mechanism.

##### 一笔 120 股订单：为什么最佳卖价不够用

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

卖盘在 100 元有 50 股、100.20 元有 100 股。假设期间无人撤单、没有新单、规则允许该市价单逐档成交，买 120 股就必须跨过第一档。先看可供数量，再算成交均价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The book offers 50 shares at 100 and 100 at 100.20. With no cancellations or new orders and rules permitting the market order to walk the book, buying 120 shares must cross the first level. Determine available quantities before calculating the average price.

在同一算例中，限价 100 元的买单最多立即取得第一档可用的 50 股，剩余数量按有效期和市场规则排队、取消或另作处理。只保留限价单成功成交的样本，会漏掉等待和错失交易的代价。策略回测因此要明确何时知道信号、何时下单、最多能成交多少及用什么成本基准。

#### 五、成交之后：义务、交收与持有人记录
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. After execution: obligations, settlement and ownership records*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

成交回报确认了价格和数量，但证券与资金未必已最终转移。先把“算清应付什么”与“真正交付完成”分开；清算可能净额，也可能保留逐笔义务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An execution report establishes price and quantity but need not establish final transfer of securities and funds. Separate determining obligations from completing transfers; clearing may net obligations or retain them individually.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Without reliable linkage, one party might pay irrevocably and receive no securities. Delivery versus payment addresses this principal risk. <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Changing the funding arrangement: financed purchases and short sales*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用自有现金买股票，只建立证券多头；借钱再买，还多了一笔必须偿还的债务。因此，资产回报和投资者权益回报的分母不同，持有期末的收益算式也不能替账户承受中途追保。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fully cash-funded purchase creates a long position; borrowing to buy adds a repayment obligation. Asset and equity returns use different denominators, and a terminal-return identity does not ensure the account can meet interim margin needs.

Reverse the direction: selling borrowed shares produces cash alongside an obligation to return shares. Prices determine the spread, while borrow availability, costs and contractual terms determine whether the position can be maintained.

#### 七、把整条交易路径走一遍
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Walk through the whole trade*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一笔计划中的交易，从“信号什么时候可知”开始，依次核对账户与产品权限、指令约束、可见深度及成交规则、实际费用、清算义务、券款交收、持有人记录和后续分配。融资或卖空时，另跟踪贷款、保证金和借券义务。每一步都可能改变最终可实现的结果，而不是给理论成交价加一个固定手续费就够了。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with when the signal becomes known. Check account and product eligibility, instruction constraints, visible depth and matching, actual costs, clearing obligations, securities and cash settlement, ownership records and later distributions. Financing and shorting add loans, margin and borrowing obligations. Implementation requires more than adding a fixed commission to a theoretical execution price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 同一收盘价产生信号，又假设全部订单以这个收盘价成交，哪里需要检查？
> 先确认信号在下单截止前是否已经完整可知，再看是否参加了相应撮合、该时点可用数量和队列约束。否则可能既用了未来信息，又假定了无法获得的满额成交；即使无信息泄露，费用与结算仍须单独处理。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Check whether the complete signal was available before the relevant order cutoff, whether the order entered that matching process, and what quantity and queue position were available. Otherwise the assumption may combine look-ahead bias with impossible full execution. Costs and settlement still matter even without information leakage.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 股价从 100 除息到 95、股息 5；指数和投资者都亏了 5% 吗？
> 价格收益为 -5%，但忽略其他现金流与费用的持有期总收益为 0%。还要确认指数是价格版还是全收益版；指数点数变化不能直接代替投资者财富变化。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Price return is -5%, while holding-period total return is 0% before other cash flows and costs. Identify the index's return variant rather than equating index-point changes with investor wealth.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 市值 9,000、借款 6,000，权益要求为证券市值的 40%；投入多少现金偿债才刚好达标？
> 现权益 3,000，要求 3,600，投入 600 偿债后，债务为 5,400、权益为 3,600。若改成另一个包含留存现金的资产分母，必须重新列式，不能混用同一个答案。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Equity is 3,000 against a requirement of 3,600. Repaying 600 reduces debt to 5,400 and restores equity of 3,600. A different denominator including retained cash requires a new calculation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 有 DvP，卖方仍未交券；买方不会先付掉本金，为什么交易还可能有成本？
> 重买证券可能更贵，还可能需要临时融资或借券以履行其他义务。DvP 的本金保护不等于保证按时履约、保证成交价格或消除全部系统风险。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Replacement securities may cost more, and other obligations may require temporary funding or borrowing. DvP's principal protection does not ensure timely performance or eliminate all risks.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1. 发现偏差以后，为什么价格不一定立即纠正
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why an identified deviation need not be corrected immediately*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先保留上一课的基准：[[有效市场假说]]并不要求每个人都不犯错；发现异常收益还受[[市场效率联合假设]]约束。行为金融的任务，是把可观察的判断机制与市场结果连接起来。为了避免后面把“有望赚钱”和“无风险套利”混为一谈，先分清纠偏交易是什么。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Retain the preceding benchmark: [[有效市场假说|market efficiency]] does not require every person to be error-free, and abnormal-return evidence faces the [[市场效率联合假设|joint hypothesis]]. Behavioral finance connects observed decision mechanisms to outcomes. Distinguish potentially profitable correction trades from strict arbitrage first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[行为金融]]

![[套利]]

![[套利与价差交易]]

![[套利限制]]

一个研究者估计股票值 100，而市场价为 90，不代表他已经找到了套利。100 可能估错，现金流可能变化，价格也可能先跌到 80。接下来先研究需求为什么会偏，再研究纠偏头寸为什么承担风险；这两个环节缺一不可。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A valuation of 100 against a price of 90 is not automatically an arbitrage: the estimate can be wrong, cash flows can change, and the price can first fall to 80. We now study demand distortions, then risks in correcting them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2. 前景理论先问：你把什么当作收益和损失
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Prospect theory starts by locating gains and losses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值方差选择给定收益分布后比较均值和风险，且有自己的[[均值方差偏好边界|偏好适用边界]]。前景理论改换了描述选择的对象：同一最终财富，相对于不同参考点，可能被体验为收益或损失。先定位参考点，再看两侧的评价，不能从一幅 S 形图直接背出“人永远怕亏”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mean–variance choice has its own [[均值方差偏好边界|preference limits]]. Prospect theory describes outcomes as gains or losses relative to a reference point. Locate that point before comparing the two sides; an S-shaped value function does not mean people always avoid risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[前景理论]]

![[前景参考点]]

![[损失厌恶]]

![[损失厌恶与风险厌恶]]

例如最终拿到 100：以 90 为参照是收益 10，以 110 为参照是损失 10。这里变化的是得失编码，不是这张 100 元钞票变了。解释实际选择时，参考点必须有独立依据，不能看见选择后再挑一个恰好讲得通的参考点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Receiving 100 is a gain of 10 relative to 90 but a loss of 10 relative to 110. The coding changes, not the money. An empirical reference point needs independent justification rather than being selected afterward to fit a choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3. 决策权重不是换了名字的概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Decision weights are not probabilities with a new name*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价值函数回答“一个结果对我有多大价值”；权重回答“这个结果在整体选择中占多大影响”。不要把两者都叫作价值函数，也不要把每个客观概率分别变换后，便以为得到了适用于任意多结果前景的通式。下面先定义权重，再实际算一次累积前景权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The value function evaluates an outcome; decision weights determine its contribution to overall choice. Transforming each individual probability independently is not a universal formula for arbitrary many-outcome prospects. Define weights, then compute a cumulative example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[前景决策权重]]

![[累积前景权重计算]]

读课堂 PDF 第 324 页的两幅图时，左图横轴应理解为相对参考点的得失；右图要把端点与区间内部区分开。小概率的超权重不等于投资者真的认为概率变大，曲率、损失侧不对称和概率加权也不能互相替代。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the two diagrams on course PDF page 324, the left horizontal axis measures reference-relative outcomes. On the right, distinguish endpoints from interior probabilities. Overweighting is not necessarily a probability belief; curvature, loss asymmetry, and weighting are separate components.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 4. 从得失评价到卖赢持亏，还缺一个决策问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From gain–loss evaluation to selling winners and holding losers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“赚钱就卖、亏钱不卖”是可观察的持仓处置模式；损失厌恶是偏好的一个性质。两者之间还隔着买入、继续持有和卖出的动态选择，以及税费、预期回报与参考点变化。因此先识别行为，再评估解释，不把定义直接当作因果证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Selling winners and holding losers is an observable disposal pattern; loss aversion is a preference property. Dynamic buying, holding, selling, taxes, expected returns, and reference-point changes lie between them. Identify the pattern before testing its explanation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 5. 同一条消息，为什么会产生不同判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the same news can produce different judgments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一条路线不是改变对得失的喜好，而是改变相信什么、相信得有多准，以及实际看到了哪些信息。以下五个概念不能用“非理性”一词抹平：过度自信关乎校准，自我归因关乎如何解释成败，代表性关乎模式推断，锚定关乎先前数值的牵引，有限注意关乎处理能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second route concerns beliefs, confidence, and processed information rather than gain–loss preferences. Overconfidence concerns calibration; attribution concerns explanations of outcomes; representativeness concerns pattern inference; anchoring concerns initial numbers; limited attention concerns processing capacity.

#### 6. BSV、DHS、HS 怎样把判断接到价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How BSV, DHS, and HS connect judgments to prices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不要把三个缩写背成“三种解释动量的理论”。先问各自改了哪个假设，再沿着同一模型的消息、预期和交易过程往下走。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Do not memorize three acronyms as interchangeable momentum explanations. Identify each altered assumption and follow its information, belief, and trading mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[BSV信念模型]]

BSV 的关键是“在错模型里认真更新”。DHS 换了问题：对自己的私人信号相信得过头；再加入不对称自我归因，信心本身也会随结果变化。下面把固定信心和反馈机制分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BSV updates carefully inside the wrong model. DHS instead overweights private-signal precision, then adds outcome-dependent confidence through asymmetric attribution. Separate fixed confidence from its feedback extension.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[DHS私人信息模型]]

![[DHS信心反馈]]

HS 又不需要让每个人都犯同一种判断错误，而是限制两组交易者各自使用的信息。纠正别人的反应不足，也可能制造新的超调。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
HS restricts the information used by two groups rather than imposing one shared judgment error. Trading against others' underreaction can itself create overshooting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[HS信息扩散模型]]

|---|---|---|
| BSV | 盈利生成模型设错 | 模型内可以正确使用贝叶斯规则 |
| DHS | 自己的私人信号精度被高估 | 固定信心与自我归因反馈是不同层次 |
| HS | 信息扩散缓慢、两组策略受限 | 早期纠偏与后期超调可来自同一互动 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BSV misspecifies earnings while permitting Bayesian updating. DHS distorts private precision, with a separate confidence-feedback extension. HS combines gradual diffusion and restricted strategies, allowing early correction and later overshooting within one interaction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 7. 纠偏交易承担的风险来自哪里
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Where the risks of corrective trading come from*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解释需求为何偏移还不够。DSSW 的噪声交易者风险模型让我们问：即使基本面没有出现坏消息，下一期情绪更极端会怎样？这与公司的现金流真的变坏，是两种风险来源。先把交易者与风险分别定义，再作对照。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Demand distortions are not the whole story. The DSSW noise-trader-risk model asks what happens if sentiment worsens even without adverse fundamentals. That differs from genuinely deteriorating cash flows. Define traders and risks separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[噪声交易者]]

![[噪声交易者风险]]

课堂还列出孪生股票价差、封闭式基金折价和过度波动。这些是待解释的现象，不是某个心理模型已被证明正确。孪生股票先核对经济权利及能否转换；基金折价还可能涉及费用、税负和清算预期；过度波动要明确现金流与贴现基准。不要从“看起来反常”跳到“存在无风险利润”。

Twin-share gaps, closed-end-fund discounts, and excess volatility are phenomena to explain, not proof of a particular psychology. Check claim equivalence and conversion, fees and liquidation expectations, or cash-flow and discount benchmarks respectively. Apparent anomalies do not establish riskless profits.

#### 8. 判断最终正确，为什么仍可能被迫退出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why an ultimately correct judgment can still end in forced exit*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在给专业交易者加上实际的资金来源。出资人无法直接看见策略质量时，可能依据近期业绩决定继续投入还是赎回。若误价加深先造成亏损，恰恰在纠偏机会看似更大时，管理人能动用的资本反而可能减少。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now add funding. Investors unable to observe strategy quality may infer it from recent performance. Widening mispricing can cause losses and reduce usable capital just when the corrective opportunity appears larger.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 9. 把解释写成可以失败的预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Turn an explanation into a prediction that can fail*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在可以回到开头那家公司：若你认为连续增长造成过度外推，就应找到预期怎样变化、谁据此交易，以及哪些纠偏约束更强。只说它后来跌了，仍没有完成论证；公司也可能收到新的坏消息，或风险补偿发生改变。

Return to the initial company. An extrapolation claim needs evidence on changing expectations, resulting trades, and correction constraints. A later fall alone is insufficient: new adverse news or changing risk compensation could explain it.

### 3. 宏观、行业与公司基本面分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Macroeconomic, Industry, and Company Fundamental Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有效市场提供价格形成基准；基本面分析从宏观、行业和公司机制形成独立现金流判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Efficient markets provide a benchmark for price formation. Fundamental analysis develops an independent view of cash flows from macroeconomic, industry, and company mechanisms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面的市场与定价讨论告诉我们，价格会反映信息。这一章换到研究者的位置：面对一条增长、通胀或政策消息，怎样解释它对具体公司的影响？先认清经济状态，再走进行业和公司，最后把经营判断写成能重算、能被新证据修正的现金流预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding discussion established that prices respond to information. This chapter takes the analyst's perspective: how does news about growth, inflation, or policy affect a particular company? Establish the economic conditions, work through the industry and the business, and translate the argument into cash-flow forecasts that can be recalculated and revised with evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

The numerator is neither a dividend already paid nor all accounting profit, and growth must be sustainable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

Irrecoverable past expenditure cannot replace an [[项目增量现金流|incremental comparison of continuation and exit]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

PDF 第 481 页将进入和退出障碍合写为“越小越有吸引力”，需要拆开理解：对既有企业而言，高进入壁垒可能限制新增供给，低退出壁垒可能帮助过剩产能离开；这是两个不同渠道。需求大、增长快、五力较弱或风险低，也不能单独完成股票估值。原表的行业吸引力因素应作为问题清单，不能当可无条件相加的评分定律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Slide 481 combines entry and exit barriers into a claim that lower barriers improve attractiveness. Separate the channels: high entry barriers may restrict new supply, while low exit barriers may ease excess capacity. Demand, growth, weaker forces, or lower risk alone do not complete a stock valuation. The table is a set of analytical questions, not an unconditional additive scoring rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 成本优势从哪里来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Where cost advantages come from*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

企业规模大与经验多往往一起出现，但必须问单位成本究竟为什么下降。一个解释是同一技术下扩大当期生产规模，另一个是反复实践改变了能力。它们对进入者、扩张决策和竞争优势持续性的含义不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Large firms often also have more experience, but the cause of lower unit cost matters. One explanation is greater current production scale at the same technology; another is capability improved through practice. They have different implications for entrants, expansion, and the durability of advantages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[规模经济]]
![[学习效应]]

金融中介重复使用系统与合同的具体机制，可继续读[[金融交易成本的规模经济]]。不要从任何一张经验曲线直接推出“只要扩大就能降低成本”：协调、质量、投入价格、产能利用率和产品组合仍需核对。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For reusable systems and contracts in finance, see [[金融交易成本的规模经济|financial transaction-cost scale economies]]. No experience curve alone establishes that expansion must reduce cost; coordination, quality, input prices, utilization, and product mix remain relevant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 政策、技术和社会变化怎样改写行业
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How policy, technology, and social change reshape industries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原课政策例依次是结构政策中的低空经济、组织政策中的稀土重整、技术路线中的油电之争、区域布局中的东数西算；补贴、税收、关税与规则是作用工具。它们应落实到需求、成本、进入条件或产能布局，不从政策名称直接跳到股价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course illustrates structural policy with the low-altitude economy, organization policy with rare-earth restructuring, technological routes with vehicle power systems, and regional allocation with computing infrastructure. Subsidies, taxes, tariffs, and rules work through demand, cost, entry, or capacity location, not directly through a policy label into stock returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

技术例包括智能手机带来新需求、合成生物改造传统活动、AI 芯片提高效率并形成新活动、电子商务改变规模与渠道、机器人扩展应用需求。第 465 页还给出邹家华致朱镕基的手写信图，讨论汽车能源与电池技术跨越。该图作为课堂技术政策材料保留；原图末端已截断，当前材料不能补出可靠日期或证明技术主张最终正确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Technology examples include smartphones creating demand, synthetic biology changing established activities, AI chips enabling efficiency and new activities, e-commerce changing scale and channels, and robotics extending applications. Slide 465 reproduces a handwritten letter discussing a leap in vehicle-energy and battery technology. It remains a classroom policy illustration; the cropped original slide does not establish a reliable date or prove the proposal's eventual success.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/证券投资学.pdf#page=465]]

生活习惯会改变消费、储蓄、投资和交易方式；人口结构、全球化或逆全球化会改变需求与产业布局。原课把国际资源整合、产业转移、先天禀赋与后天积累联系起来。技术替代可能加速或跳过生命周期阶段，也可能重塑旧行业；不能从某一产品被替代推断整个行业必然消失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Habits affect consumption, saving, investment, and transactions. Demographics, globalization, and deglobalization change demand and location. The course connects resource integration and industry relocation with endowments and acquired capabilities. Technological substitution can interrupt a life cycle or renew an old industry; replacing one product does not prove that the entire industry disappears.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 从景气读数到可核验的判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From activity readings to testable judgments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结构相对慢变，订单、库存和调查信号则帮助判断短期经营状态。两类证据要配合：五力不能代替当期数据，一个向上的景气数字也不能证明竞争优势已经形成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Structure changes relatively slowly, while orders, inventories, and surveys help assess current conditions. Use both: five forces cannot replace current data, and a rising activity statistic cannot establish a competitive advantage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[行业景气指标]]
![[景气指标口径核对]]

原课的月度或季度观察、领先性和 50/100 临界值需要按指标说明解释。报告方式可以是总体研究、专题、预测或动态跟踪；研究方法可以结合历史、调查、归纳演绎、比较与数理统计。定性研究也需要证据，定量研究也受样本、口径和模型限制；二者不是主观与客观的简单分界，更不能由相关性直接认定因果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interpret monthly or quarterly observations, lead times, and thresholds according to each indicator's documentation. Reports may be broad surveys, focused studies, forecasts, or monitoring updates. Historical research, surveys, reasoning, comparisons, and statistical methods can be combined. Qualitative work requires evidence, while quantitative work depends on samples and models; neither the qualitative-quantitative label nor a correlation establishes causality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 带着作业进入公司分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Use the assignment to move into company analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第五次作业要求解释成长、周期、防御与生命周期，说明行业环境和阶段特征，并对任选行业做五力分析。已有白酒答案应作为完整的课堂应用保留：品牌与渠道、集中度与竞争、进入者资金和工艺、其他酒类替代、原料供应及终端消费者和经销商，都是值得继续核验的判断。先补产品价格带、地域和时期；再问每项证据支持谁有何种选择，避免用“消费者弱”覆盖所有渠道客户。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assignment 5 asks for the four definitions, industry environment and stage characteristics, and a five-forces application. Preserve the existing liquor analysis as a complete classroom exercise: brands and channels, concentration and rivalry, entrant resources, substitutes, inputs, and end consumers versus distributors all require evidence. Specify segment, geography, and period, then identify whose alternatives each observation supports.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行业分析的结果是公司分析的输入，而不是终点。行业的平均利润水平不能替代公司的具体商业模式、成本、资本需求和执行能力；下一步把这些条件落到公司收入、利润、再投资和现金流，并说明什么新证据会推翻判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industry analysis supplies inputs to company analysis rather than ending it. Average industry profitability cannot replace a company's business model, costs, capital needs, or execution. Next translate these conditions into company revenue, profit, reinvestment, and cash flow, and specify evidence that would overturn the thesis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 三、公司分析：客户、活动与优势证据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Company analysis: customers, activities, and evidence of advantage.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 从行业条件落到客户和经营活动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Company analysis: connect industry conditions to customers and operating activities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行业分析解释同类企业面对什么约束，公司分析接着问：这家企业具体做什么，客户为什么选择它，它怎样交付，又怎样从中取得收入。公司历史、产品用途、客户类型、生产技术和销售渠道是这个问题的证据，不能用一个行业标签代替。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industry analysis identifies constraints shared by firms. Company analysis then asks what this business does, why customers choose it, how it delivers, and how it earns revenue. Its history, product uses, customer groups, technology, and sales channels supply evidence; an industry label cannot replace that explanation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[商业模式]]

先区分使用者、购买决策者和付款者，他们未必是同一个人。再问客户在什么替代方案之间选择：省钱、省时间、降低故障风险或获得更方便的服务，都应落实到具体需求和相对价格，而不是停在“产品好”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish the user, purchase decision-maker, and payer; they need not be the same person. Identify the alternatives the customer considers. Saving money or time, reducing failures, or improving convenience must connect to a specific need and relative price, rather than the vague claim that the product is good.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[价值主张]]

客户愿意选择还不够，企业必须能持续交付承诺。把采购、研发、生产、物流、销售、服务和支持活动连起来，检查一个环节的节省是否把成本或风险转移给了另一个环节。例如包装更紧凑可减少运输空间，却可能增加组装工作；净效果取决于整个交付安排和客户接受程度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Customer appeal is insufficient unless the firm can deliver consistently. Connect procurement, R&D, production, logistics, sales, service, and support, checking whether savings in one activity shift costs or risks elsewhere. Compact packaging can reduce shipping space but increase assembly work; the net effect depends on the delivery system and customer acceptance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[价值链]]

原课的经济区位分析也放进这些活动：接近客户、供应商、港口或人才，可能改变运输成本、交付时间和招聘难度；供电、基础设施及当地政策也可能构成约束。要比较具体地点与可行替代地点，并检查政策期限和迁移成本。“位于某产业集群”是一条待解释的信息，不是成本优势的自动证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Location analysis belongs within these activities. Proximity to customers, suppliers, ports, or skilled labour may affect shipping costs, lead times, and hiring; utilities, infrastructure, and local policy may also constrain operations. Compare the actual site with feasible alternatives, including policy expiry and relocation costs. Membership in an industrial cluster is information to investigate, not automatic proof of a cost advantage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 先整理竞争位置，再分清战略选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Company analysis: organize the competitive position, then distinguish strategic choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对照竞争者的客户、产品、成本和资源，才能判断本公司的相对强弱。SWOT适合整理这批材料，但每一项都要说明对象和证据：订单增加可能来自需求扩张，也可能来自暂时降价；大份额可能伴随利润，也可能由高补贴换来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Relative strengths and weaknesses require comparison with competitors' customers, products, costs, and resources. SWOT organizes that evidence, but each item needs a defined object and support. More orders may reflect demand growth or temporary price cuts; a large share may generate profit or be purchased through subsidies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[SWOT分析]]

有了位置判断，再问公司主要依靠什么参与竞争。成本领先追求同口径交付下的相对成本优势，不是简单把价格压低。原课给出的历史公司案例是讨论线索；若用于今天的判断，必须重查可比成本、产品组合和竞争条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the position established, ask how the firm competes. Cost leadership pursues a relative cost advantage on a comparable delivery basis; it does not simply mean charging less. Historical company examples in the course suggest questions to investigate, but current claims require fresh comparable-cost, product-mix, and competitive evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[成本领先]]

另一条路径是客户重视的区别，例如可靠性、设计或交付服务。比较收益时，不能只看到售价溢价，还要计入实现区别的额外成本，以及销量、留存和竞争者模仿的变化。成本与差异化是分析维度，不意味着现实企业只能机械地选择其中一个。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Another route is a distinction customers value, such as reliability, design, or delivery service. Evaluate not just the price premium but also the added cost, volume, retention, and competitors' imitation. Cost and differentiation are analytical dimensions, not a rule forcing every real firm to choose only one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[差异化战略]]

还要单独确定竞争范围。服务某个狭窄客户群，与面向较广市场，是范围上的选择；在狭窄范围内仍可以追求成本优势或差异化。企业规模小并不足以证明采用了集聚战略，关键在于选定的细分需求和相应活动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Competitive scope is a separate choice. Serving a narrow customer group rather than a broad market defines the scope; the narrow segment can still support either cost or differentiation advantages. Small company size alone does not establish a focus strategy. The relevant evidence is the selected segment's needs and the activities serving them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[集聚战略]]

##### 优势为何可能持续，又会怎样消失
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Company analysis: explain why an advantage might persist and how it could erode.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

竞争定位描述企业打算怎样做，持久性则要求进一步解释竞争者为什么不能轻易复制、替代或绕过。[[规模经济]]与[[学习效应]]可能影响成本；网络效应则先描述参与者如何影响使用价值。不要把“用户很多”直接改写成企业一定能收取高价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positioning describes how the firm intends to compete. Durability additionally requires explaining why rivals cannot readily copy, substitute for, or bypass it. [[规模经济|Economies of scale]] and [[学习效应|learning effects]] may affect costs; network effects first describe how participation changes users' benefits. Many users do not automatically imply the ability to charge high prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[网络效应]]

用户可以同时使用多个平台，网络效应仍可能存在；互操作、拥挤、质量变化和替代选择也会改变它的强弱。把这种具体机制与价格、成本、留存及竞争反应核对，才有理由讨论优势的持续时间。单期高毛利和高份额不能完成这一步。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Users may use several platforms while network effects still exist. Interoperability, congestion, quality, and alternatives can change their strength. Assess the mechanism against pricing, costs, retention, and competitive responses before judging durability. One period of high margins or market share cannot complete that test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[护城河证据]]

##### 管理与成长判断要留下可核对的证据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Company analysis: make management and growth assessments evidence-based.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原课随后检查管理层、员工、治理、激励、内部控制和生产组织。把“管理优秀”改成可观察的问题：谁批准重大投资，谁复核资金使用，关键控制是否实际执行，生产计划偏差怎样处理，核心人才为何留任。制度名称或经理人履历不能独自证明执行有效；持股激励也须结合[[股权激励的代理边界|控制与利益冲突的边界]]理解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course next examines management, employees, governance, incentives, internal controls, and production organization. Turn “good management” into observable questions: who authorizes major investment, who reviews spending, whether key controls operate, how production deviations are handled, and why essential staff stay. Formal structures or executive credentials alone do not establish effectiveness; ownership incentives also require the [[股权激励的代理边界|control and conflict-of-interest qualifications]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经营效率比较同样需要配对：人均销售可能受外包和产品价格影响，设备利用率很高也可能伴随维护不足或交付拥堵。先检查业务、资本密集度、产品组合和会计口径，再解释同业差异；不要把所有指标都当作越高越好的排名表。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Efficiency comparisons need matching definitions. Sales per employee may reflect outsourcing and product prices, while high capacity utilization can coexist with inadequate maintenance or congested delivery. Check the business, capital intensity, product mix, and accounting basis before explaining peer differences; not every metric is a higher-is-better score.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

成长性分析把这些经营证据放进时间：历年销售、利润和资产怎样变化，公司与行业增长有何差异，新增产能是否有客户需求支持，研发、营销、投资与融资计划是否相互适配。只扩建工厂不能证明市场会吸收供给；只看到历史EPS上升，也不能略过成本、股数和融资条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Growth analysis puts the operating evidence on a timeline: changes in sales, profit, and assets; differences from industry growth; demand supporting new capacity; and consistency among R&D, marketing, investment, and financing plans. Building a plant does not establish demand for its output. Historical EPS growth does not remove the need to check costs, share count, and financing conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

调研到这里，应能交出一份可追溯的经营解释：客户为什么买、企业怎样交付、相对竞争者的区别、支持管理判断的事实，以及增长需要哪些资源。下一步把这些判断转成明确的数量、价格、成本和再投资假设，再检验利润、现金与价值是否一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The research should now yield a traceable operating explanation: why customers buy, how the firm delivers, what differs from competitors, the facts supporting management assessments, and the resources required for growth. Next translate those judgments into explicit volume, price, cost, and reinvestment assumptions, then check consistency among earnings, cash, and value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 四、利润、现金与价值：把判断变成可重算的模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Earnings, cash, and value: make the argument reproducible.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 把业务判断放进一张能重算的经营表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Translate the business argument into an operating forecast that can be recalculated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

走到这里，“产品好”“需求回暖”“品牌强”都还只是待检验的机制。下一步不是立刻找估值倍数，而是问每个机制改变哪一个输入。先从计费方式选择收入单位，再把需求、份额和价格放到同一张表。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“A good product,” “recovering demand,” and “a strong brand” remain hypotheses about mechanisms. Before choosing a valuation multiple, identify which input each mechanism changes. Select the revenue unit from the billing model, then align demand, share, and price in one forecast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[收入驱动预测]]

沿用卡片中的同一家公司：市场销量增长 10%，公司份额从 10% 到 11%，带来公司销量从 100 到 121；但为了争取客户，售价由 10 降为 9.5。收入增长 14.95% 的代价是单位价格下降。现在假设单位变动成本仍为 6，固定经营成本仍为 300，先算经营利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continue with the same firm: market volume grows 10% and share rises from 10% to 11%, taking company volume from 100 to 121. Winning those customers requires cutting price from 10 to 9.5. The 14.95% revenue increase therefore comes with a lower unit price. With variable cost per unit still 6 and fixed operating costs still 300, compute EBIT next.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[经营杠杆]]

基期利润为 $(10-6)\times100-300=100$；新情景为 $(9.5-6)\times121-300=123.5$。利润增长 23.5%，不能拿基期 DOL 为 4 乘销量增长 21%，因为 DOL 的那一步比较把售价固定了，而这个情景同时降价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Baseline EBIT is $(10-6)\times100-300=100$; the new scenario yields $(9.5-6)\times121-300=123.5$, up 23.5%. Multiplying the baseline DOL of 4 by the 21% volume increase would be wrong: that comparison holds price fixed, while this scenario cuts price too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也解释了为什么[[规模经济]]、[[学习效应]]与经营杠杆必须区分：它们分别关注规模与单位成本、累计经验与效率、给定成本结构下利润对销售的敏感性。看到利润率提高，不能单凭结果挑一个喜欢的解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This separates [[规模经济|economies of scale]], [[学习效应|learning effects]], and operating leverage: scale and unit cost, accumulated experience and efficiency, and profit sensitivity under a given cost structure are different mechanisms. A higher margin alone does not identify which mechanism caused it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 利润之后还要支付什么：从经营到现金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
What remains to be paid after earning profit? From operations to cash.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

确认收入不等于收到款项，形成利润也不等于完成了扩张所需的付款。公司估值需要明确现金最终属于哪一组资本提供者；本段先按非金融企业的全部资本口径理解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recognising revenue does not mean collecting cash, and reporting profit does not settle expansion spending. Valuation also needs to identify which providers of capital receive the cash. This section first uses the all-capital-provider perspective for a nonfinancial company.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[企业自由现金流]]

继续上面的降价扩张情景，暂设税率为零以隔离现金机制：与不扩张相比，当年应收多占 30、存货多占 25、应付多提供 10，净资本开支多 15。若不扩张时没有净再投资，则两方案当年 FCFF 分别为 100 与 $123.5-(30+25-10)-15=63.5$。营业利润多了 23.5，当年可分配现金却少了 36.5。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continue the price-cut expansion scenario, setting tax to zero to isolate cash mechanics. Relative to no expansion, receivables consume 30, inventory consumes 25, payables provide 10, and net capex consumes 15. With no net reinvestment in the baseline, annual FCFF is 100 without expansion and 63.5 with expansion. EBIT rises by 23.5, while available current cash falls by 36.5.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面另用“收入增长 20%、利润率维持 10%”这一不同假设作对照。两个例子的利润率假设不同，不能混算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The next contrast instead assumes 20% revenue growth with a constant 10% operating margin. The two examples make different margin assumptions and must not be combined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 愿意为增长付多少钱：资本回报与增值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
How much is growth worth? Capital returns and value creation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当期现金减少不一定是坏投资：扩张可能今天付款、以后回收。反过来，收入和利润增加也不足以证明好投资。现在需要一个把经营利润接到投入资本的量，再把全部现金流接到机会成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lower current cash need not indicate a bad investment: expansion can require payment now and yield benefits later. Conversely, rising revenue and profit do not establish a good investment. Relate operating profit to invested capital, then compare the full cash-flow path with its opportunity cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[投入资本回报率]]

这条关系让预测相互约束：若声称长期利润增长很快，却既不增加资本，也不改善现有资产效率，就欠缺增长来源；若用历史平均 ROIC 替代未来新增项目回报，则可能高估扩张质量。接下来使用财管课的同一个净现值规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The relation disciplines the forecast. Rapid sustained earnings growth needs new capital or improved efficiency of existing assets. Replacing prospective marginal returns with historical average ROIC can overstate expansion quality. Apply the same NPV rule used in Financial Management next.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 回到价格，并约定以后怎样检查自己
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Turning that judgment into an actual trade additionally requires [[基本面风险|fundamental-risk]], [[套利限制|limits-to-arbitrage]], and [[证券交易成本|transaction-cost]] checks. > [!question]- 自检：新增收入就足以改变结论吗？
> 公司销量增长 21%，同时降价 5%，固定成本未变。研究者用原 DOL 推算利润，再把全部利润增长当自由现金流增长，并说行业成长证明股票值得买。哪里需要重新计算？
>
> **答案：** 先按新量价重算收入与 EBIT；再扣同一情景下新增经营营运资本和资本开支，使用一致税收口径；评价未来完整现金流相对投入的 NPV；最后检查支付价格及原已定价预期。销量增长、利润增长、现金增长和投资收益不是同一个量。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A firm increases volume 21% while cutting price 5%, with unchanged fixed costs. An analyst uses the old DOL for profit, treats all extra profit as extra free cash flow, and calls industry growth proof that the stock is attractive. What must be recalculated?
>
> **Answer:** Recompute revenue and EBIT with the new price and volume; deduct the scenario's working-capital and capital spending using consistent taxes; assess the full incremental cash-flow NPV; then check the price paid and prior expectations. Volume, earnings, cash flow, and investor returns are distinct quantities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 先找到最重要的确认与计量判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Locate the recognition and measurement judgments that matter most.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原课 PDF488–489 以收入与金融资产为例。收入不能仅看是否开票或收款；在 IFRS 15 的五步框架下，依次识别合同、识别履约义务、确定交易价格、按相对单独售价分摊，再于履约义务满足时确认。一个“设备加一年服务”的合同可能有两个不同的确认进度，不能只因总价一次收齐便把服务收入全部提前入账。这是理解原课控制权思路的补充，不替代具体合同和适用准则的完整判断。[IFRS 15 官方说明](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

##### 总变动确定，并不意味着每项贡献唯一
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fixed total change does not imply unique component contributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[00_Knowledge/连环替代法]]
![[00_Knowledge/连环替代顺序边界]]

把原课 PDF525–526 的一般式完整写出来。设基期 $P_0=A_0B_0C_0$、比较期 $P_1=A_1B_1C_1$，按 A→B→C 替代：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write out the course's general procedure. For baseline $P_0=A_0B_0C_0$ and comparison $P_1=A_1B_1C_1$, update A, then B, then C:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_0\to Q_A=A_1B_0C_0\to Q_B=A_1B_1C_0\to P_1.$$
$$\Delta_A=(A_1-A_0)B_0C_0,\quad
\Delta_B=A_1(B_1-B_0)C_0,\quad
\Delta_C=A_1B_1(C_1-C_0).$$

相邻相减后相加，中间的 $Q_A,Q_B$ 抵消，所以三项恰好加回总差；换顺序会改变交互项分给谁。例如净利率 8%→6%、周转率 1→1.2、乘数 2→3，ROE 沿本顺序为 16%→12%→14.4%→21.6%，分别分摊 −4、+2.4、+7.2 个百分点。可以说“按这个顺序，杠杆分摊最大”，不能把它称为唯一因果贡献，更不能由此建议公司直接加债。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The intermediate values cancel when differences are summed. Changing the order reallocates interactions. A margin change of 8%→6%, turnover of 1→1.2, and multiplier of 2→3 trace ROE of 16%→12%→14.4%→21.6%, allocating −4, +2.4, and +7.2 percentage points. Leverage has the largest allocation under this order, not a uniquely identified causal contribution or an automatic case for borrowing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

显性预测期应覆盖经营向稳定状态过渡的过程，不是随意选满五年便完成。后续期仍需经营、再投资和资本成本假设，只是用一个终值表达。先认清它在哪个时点，再检查增长需要的投入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The explicit forecast should cover the transition to stability, not simply fill five rows. Continuing operations still require operating, reinvestment, and return assumptions, summarised in a terminal value at a specific date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 六、从经营现金现值走到一股普通股
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Move from operating cash value to one common share.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与股利、FCFE 不同，FCFF 归属于全部资本提供者。贴现前应把风险与融资政策配好，而不是从账上找一个历史借款利率来代用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
FCFF belongs to all capital providers, unlike dividends and FCFE. Match risk and financing policy before discounting, rather than substituting an old borrowing rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先检查[[名义与实际贴现]]：按各期货币金额、含预期通胀预测的现金，配名义贴现率；按不变购买力预测的现金，配实际贴现率。终值增长率也必须沿用同一口径；只确认币种相同还不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check [[名义与实际贴现|nominal–real consistency]] first: cash flows in future money amounts, including expected inflation, require nominal rates; constant-purchasing-power flows require real rates. Terminal growth must use the same basis. Matching currency alone is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里使用的是风险现金的期望预测。把坏结果纳入预测，与把现金转成确定性等价，是两种不同操作。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The model uses expected risky cash flows. Including adverse outcomes in the forecast differs from converting cash into certainty equivalents.

#### 七、用市场可比价格交叉检查，而不是省略经济条件

*Use comparable prices without losing economic conditions.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相对估值把同行市场价格带进分析，省去了一些显性预测，却没有消灭那些预测。增长、风险、利润率和再投资差异仍会藏在倍数里。

Relative valuation uses peer prices, reducing explicit forecasting without eliminating its assumptions. Growth, risk, margins, and reinvestment remain embedded in the multiple.

Establish each numerator and denominator before examining conditional fair multiples. PE times EPS yields value per share, not total equity; the book denominator in P/B is not a liquidation guarantee.

### 6. 投资组合理论与 CAPM
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Portfolio Theory and CAPM*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单项证券估值不足以决定组合；组合理论研究相关性如何改变整体风险，CAPM 再问哪些不可分散风险应获得回报。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Valuing individual securities is not enough to determine a portfolio. Portfolio theory studies how dependence changes total risk, while CAPM asks which non-diversifiable risks should earn a return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/10_均值方差与资产配置|本段独立阅读与贯穿练习]] · [[均值—方差投资组合理论.canvas|均值—方差关系图]]

#### 1. 把持仓变成一个风险收益点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From positions to a risk–return point*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先分清权重与收益：权重是期初投入比例，收益是随后才实现的随机结果。二者的结合给出一个组合；对这个组合求均值和方差，才得到图上的坐标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weights are initial allocations; returns are subsequently realized random outcomes. Combining them defines the portfolio return, whose mean and variance locate it on the diagram.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[投资组合]]

![[单期组合收益]]

![[组合均值与方差]]

贯穿本课的自拟例子使用同一期间、同一货币的两项资产：$\mu_A=8\%,\mu_B=12\%$，$\sigma_A=20\%,\sigma_B=30\%$，$\rho=0.25$，所以 $\sigma_{AB}=0.015$。这些是用来理解模型的假定总体输入，不是行情估计或投资建议。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The running example assumes two assets over one common horizon and currency: means $8\%,12\%$, volatilities $20\%,30\%$, and correlation $0.25$, giving covariance $0.015$. These are illustrative population inputs, not market estimates or investment advice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2. 分散化究竟省掉了什么风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What diversification actually reduces*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

组合的期望收益容易平均，波动却不能照做：某项资产下跌时，另一项是否也下跌，决定了两者的风险能否抵消。先把权重固定，单独观察相关性的作用，再看完全相关的端点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expected returns average linearly but volatilities do not. Joint movements determine cancellation. Hold weights fixed to isolate correlation, then examine its endpoints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[两资产分散化]]

![[完全相关的风险抵消]]

两资产的抵消逻辑可以推广，但“多买几个”不等于“共同风险也不见了”。下面把可分散残差与共同暴露明确分开；之后接 CAPM 时，还要另加均衡定价假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The logic extends beyond two assets, but adding names need not remove common exposure. Separate diversifiable residuals from common factors before adding CAPM's equilibrium assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[分散化与共同风险]]

#### 3. 从可行组合中筛出值得考虑的部分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From feasible portfolios to worthwhile choices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

卖不卖空、能否借钱会改变可行范围；偏好则决定怎样评价范围内的组合。先讲清评价规则及其边界，再讨论被支配、最小方差与有效前沿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Shorting and borrowing rules determine feasibility; preferences evaluate the feasible choices. Establish the criterion and its limits before distinguishing dominance, minimum variance, and efficiency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Minimum variance at a target mean compares portfolios with that same mean. Efficiency also checks alternatives at other means. The global minimum-variance point separates the two branches in the standard unconstrained model.

#### 6. 加入无风险资产，先画固定组合的配置线

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在令同期间无风险收益 $r_f=2\%$。先暂时固定风险组合为上面的等权组合 P：你只能改变投给 P 的总比例，不能改变 P 内部 A、B 的比例。这一步得到的是资本配置线，还没有理由把它叫资本市场线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the same-horizon risk-free return be $2\%$. First fix the risky fund P at equal asset weights. Change only the amount allocated to P, not its internal composition. This produces a capital allocation line, not yet a capital market line.

#### 8. 从组合选择走到市场均衡

##### 葛兰维尔八条：读完整条件，再写算法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 从一次可回放计算走向可相信的证据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

Construct on training data, select on validation data and reserve test data for independent final evaluation. Time-series evaluation should simulate genuine forecast origins rather than shuffle future observations into earlier decisions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[滚动起点评估]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准化、股票筛选、阈值和指标选择也属于被评价流程，不能只把最后一行预测留到样本外。具体分割还应匹配你想推广到的时间、证券或市场；这两个问题分别复用 [[验证管线隔离]] 与 [[验证结构匹配]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Performance measures have distinct jobs. Sharpe compares the mean and standard deviation of periodic excess returns, not one total return with a periodic standard deviation; a zero denominator gives no finite ratio. With a constant matching risk-free return, that standard deviation equals portfolio-return volatility; otherwise subtract the risk-free return period by period before calculating it. See the [[负Sharpe排序边界|qualification for negative-ratio ranking]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Retain the return distribution, turnover and position scale, extreme losses, and performance across pre-specified periods. An average can conceal different paths. A small-cap rule needs a matching liquidity and cost model, and period boundaries must not be moved afterward to preserve a favorable result.
