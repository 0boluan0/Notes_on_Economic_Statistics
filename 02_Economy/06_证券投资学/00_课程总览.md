### 1. 证券工具、市场与交易
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Securities, Markets, and Trading*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是本课程的默认起点；先用快速恢复建立对象、例子和问题感，再进入详细内容。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the default starting point. Use the quick-recovery section to establish the objects, examples, and motivating questions before moving into the details.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[证券工具与现金流权利]]

先弄清每种证券承诺什么现金流和控制权，才能理解这些权利如何在市场中报价、成交、清算和结算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cash-flow and control rights promised by each security must be clear before one can understand how those rights are quoted, traded, cleared, and settled.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[证券市场、订单与结算]]

### 2. 资产定价、有效市场与行为金融
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Asset Pricing, Efficient Markets, and Behavioural Finance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

认识证券与交易机制后，才能讨论价格是否及时吸收信息以及风险为何要求回报。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only after securities and trading mechanisms are understood can we ask whether prices incorporate information promptly and why risk commands a return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本]]

CAPM 先给出风险与预期收益的均衡基准；有效市场假说和事件研究再检验信息是否已被价格吸收，以及异常收益能否持续。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CAPM first supplies an equilibrium benchmark linking risk to expected return. The efficient-market hypothesis and event studies then test whether information is already incorporated into prices and whether abnormal returns persist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[有效市场假说与事件研究]]

发现价格偏离基准并不足以推翻有效市场；行为金融还要说明偏差从何而来，以及套利限制为何阻止理性交易者立即消除它。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A price departure from a benchmark is not enough to reject market efficiency. Behavioural finance must also explain the source of the bias and why limits to arbitrage prevent rational traders from eliminating it immediately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[行为金融与套利限制]]

### 3. 宏观、行业与公司基本面分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Macroeconomic, Industry, and Company Fundamental Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有效市场提供价格形成基准；基本面分析从宏观、行业和公司机制形成独立现金流判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Efficient markets provide a benchmark for price formation. Fundamental analysis develops an independent view of cash flows from macroeconomic, industry, and company mechanisms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[宏观、行业与公司基本面分析]]

### 4. 财务报表、财务比率与财务预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Financial Statements, Financial Ratios, and Forecasting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定性基本面最终要落到报表质量、盈利能力和可持续增长这些可核验数据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Qualitative fundamentals must ultimately be grounded in verifiable data on reporting quality, profitability, and sustainable growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财务报表、财务比率与财务预测]]

### 5. 证券估值：债券与股票
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Security Valuation: Bonds and Equities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

报表预测给出未来现金流和增长；证券估值把它们折现或映射到可比倍数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial forecasts supply future cash flows and growth assumptions. Security valuation discounts them or maps them to comparable multiples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[债券定价、收益率与信用利差]]

债券先用较明确的合同现金流建立贴现和风险溢价基准；股票估值再面对剩余现金流、增长和终值都更不确定的情形。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bonds first establish discounting and risk-premium logic with relatively explicit contractual cash flows. Equity valuation then confronts residual cash flows, growth, and terminal value that are all more uncertain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[股票与企业价值评估]]

### 6. 投资组合理论与 CAPM
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Portfolio Theory and CAPM*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单项证券估值不足以决定组合；组合理论研究相关性如何改变整体风险，CAPM 再问哪些不可分散风险应获得回报。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Valuing individual securities is not enough to determine a portfolio. Portfolio theory studies how dependence changes total risk, while CAPM asks which non-diversifiable risks should earn a return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[均值—方差投资组合理论]]

均值—方差框架先描述单个投资者的可行集和最优风险组合；CAPM 再加入市场出清，把组合选择转成系统风险的均衡价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mean–variance framework first describes an investor’s feasible set and optimal risky portfolio. CAPM then adds market clearing and turns portfolio choice into an equilibrium price for systematic risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本]]

### 7. 证券技术分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Technical Analysis of Securities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本面与组合定价从现金流和风险解释价格；技术分析则研究价格成交数据是否含可重复信号，并必须接受样本外检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fundamental and portfolio pricing explain prices through cash flows and risk. Technical analysis asks whether price and volume data contain repeatable signals, which must be tested out of sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[证券技术分析]]
