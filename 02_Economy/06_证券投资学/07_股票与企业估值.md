# 股票与企业估值：从经营预测到每股价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equity valuation: from operating forecasts to value per share*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面的[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/04_宏观到公司基本面|基本面分析]]说明公司怎样赚钱，[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/06_财务报表与公司诊断|报表诊断]]检查利润、资产和现金是否可信。本章把这些判断推进一步：在明确的经营、融资和风险假设下，这些现金今天值多少，最后有多少属于一股普通股？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/04_宏观到公司基本面|Fundamental analysis]] explains how a business earns money; [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/06_财务报表与公司诊断|financial diagnosis]] tests earnings, assets, and cash. This chapter translates those findings into value under explicit operating, financing, and risk assumptions, then allocates that value to one ordinary share.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一、先问估的是什么，再讨论值多少钱
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Specify the claim before estimating its value.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

买股票不是按比例买走账上的全部资产，而是取得特定的剩余请求权。先认清普通股、优先权和债务之间的顺序，才不会把企业经营价值直接当作股价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A share is a particular residual claim, not a pro-rata withdrawal of all recorded assets. Identify ordinary equity and prior claims before treating operating value as a share price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[股权]]
![[股票内在价值]]

估值还要固定日期、币种、合并范围，以及是否维持现有经营策略。账面权益是会计记录，市值是交易价格，模型值是条件估计；这三者不同不构成逻辑矛盾。真正要解释的是，哪项未来现金或风险假设造成了差额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the date, currency, consolidation scope, and operating strategy. Book equity is an accounting measure, market value a trading price, and model value a conditional estimate. Explain the cash-flow or risk assumptions behind their differences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 二、从一期股利和卖价，走到整个持有期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Extend dividends and resale value through time.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原课从一年后股利 5、收息后卖价 110、必要回报 15% 得到今天价值 100。这个例子不是说卖价可以凭空给定，而是把尚未展开的后续现金集中在 110 里。继续追问买方为什么肯付 110，就得到多期股利贴现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course values a dividend of 5 plus an ex-dividend resale value of 110 one year ahead at 100 using a 15% required return. The 110 summarises later cash flows; asking why the next buyer would pay it extends the dividend-discount argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[股利贴现模型]]

逐年预测到无限远不现实。若有依据认为每股股利已进入稳定增长，就能使用下面的特例；若只是短期高增长，则要分阶段。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Forecasting every future year is impractical. A supported stable-growth assumption permits the following special case; temporary high growth requires staging.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[戈登增长模型]]

课件 PDF635 的完整例子：刚付股利 $D_0=1$，预期增长 5%，无风险利率 4%，市场预期回报 10%，Beta 为 1.43。按所给 CAPM 假设，$k_e=4\%+1.43(10\%-4\%)=12.58\%$；先把股利推进一年，$D_1=1.05$，再得 $V_0=1.05/(0.1258-0.05)\approx13.85$。题设市价 15 高于这个条件估值，但不是一项无条件交易信号。必要回报的模型含义见[[证券市场线]]；无自身历史 Beta 时，仍可研究[[项目Beta匹配|可比业务风险]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In slide PDF635, $D_0=1$, growth is 5%, the risk-free rate 4%, market return 10%, and beta 1.43. CAPM gives $k_e=12.58\%$; next year's dividend is 1.05 and conditional value is approximately 13.85. The assumed price of 15 exceeds that model value, without creating an unconditional trading rule. See the [[证券市场线|SML]] and [[项目Beta匹配|business-risk comparables]] for the required-return assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多阶段股利估值]]

到这里，我们是给定必要回报求价值。如果反过来给定买价，问这些预期现金隐含什么回报，便得到另一个问题。反解出的数并不会自动成为现实中已实现的收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So far, required return determines value. Holding the purchase price fixed and solving for the return implied by forecast cash flows reverses the question; that result is not a realised return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[戈登隐含收益率]]

## 三、公司赚的利润，怎样变成可分配现金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Translate earnings into distributable cash.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经营分析常从收入和利润开始，估值却需要现金。先扣维持预测经营所需的税和再投资，才能知道业务为全部资本提供者留下什么；然后考虑利息和净举债，才走到股东一侧。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operating analysis begins with revenue and profit, but valuation requires cash. Deduct operating taxes and reinvestment before identifying cash for all capital providers; then account for interest and net borrowing to reach equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[企业自由现金流]]
![[股权自由现金流]]

这里的折旧加回也应包含模型已从经营利润扣除的相关摊销；营运资本按非现金经营口径计算。如果从管理报表直接使用净经营资产变动，先看下面的现金分类桥。不能从一种口径拿利润，再从另一种口径拿投资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The non-cash add-back includes relevant amortisation already deducted from operating income. Working capital uses the non-cash operating convention. If using changes in net operating assets, reconcile cash scope first rather than mixing profit and investment conventions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[自由现金流的净经营资产表示]]

公司未将全部 FCFE 发成股利，不意味着剩余现金没有价值。需要把留存用途和未来分配写完整，才能比较两条估值路线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Undistributed FCFE is not automatically valueless. Trace its use and eventual distribution before comparing the two valuation routes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[股利与自由现金流估值差异]]

## 四、增长需要投入，竞争优势决定投入能赚多少
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Growth needs investment; competitive advantage affects its return.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“收入增长 10%”还不是完整预测。先问增加多少资本，再问新增资本什么时候产生多少利润。历史平均 ROIC 有参考意义，但不能不加解释地当作每笔新项目未来的回报。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A revenue growth forecast is incomplete without the capital required and its earnings timing. Historical average ROIC can inform, but cannot automatically determine, returns on new investments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[投入资本回报率]]
![[再投资增长关系]]
![[增长不等于增值]]

把一般判断放进一个具体现金流模型，便能看见原课“现有资产价值加增长价值”的来源。它不是给公司贴上“有护城河”后随意加一个溢价，而是逐笔新投资的净现值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an explicit cash-flow model, the course's assets-in-place/growth decomposition follows from the NPV of new investments, not an arbitrary premium for a perceived moat.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[新增投资价值分解]]

原记录的 100、ROIC 15%、资本成本 10%、增长 4% 算例，可以在一致条件下重建：第一年 NOPAT 为 100，每年按 $b=g/R=0.04/0.15$ 再投资，第一笔 FCFF 为 $100(1-0.04/0.15)=73.3333$。稳定增长给出 $V_0=73.3333/(0.10-0.04)=1222.22$；基准业务值 1000，增长价值为 222.22。这里同时固定了新旧资本回报、投资时点、风险和维护性支出，不能只观察到历史 ROIC 15% 就直接得出这个估值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The note's example can be reconstructed consistently: first-year NOPAT 100, return 15%, required return 10%, and growth 4% imply reinvestment $b=0.04/0.15$. First FCFF is 73.3333, giving value 1222.22, comprising baseline value 1000 and growth value 222.22. This assumes matched investment timing, risk, maintenance, and continuing returns on old and new capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 五、显性期结束之后，企业并没有突然消失
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The firm continues after the explicit forecast ends.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

显性预测期应覆盖经营向稳定状态过渡的过程，不是随意选满五年便完成。后续期仍需经营、再投资和资本成本假设，只是用一个终值表达。先认清它在哪个时点，再检查增长需要的投入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The explicit forecast should cover the transition to stability, not simply fill five rows. Continuing operations still require operating, reinvestment, and return assumptions, summarised in a terminal value at a specific date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[估值终值]]
![[终值增长的再投资约束]]

竞争优势衰减可以表现为未来新增投资逐渐只赚必要回报。即使如此，盈利不一定停止增长；下面的特殊终值正好把这一点算清。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fading advantage may mean new investments eventually earn only the required return. Earnings can still grow; the following special terminal relation makes the distinction explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[正常回报终值]]

现在重算原记录的两阶段例子。固定股数，第一年每股盈利 $E_1=120$，每年留存 40%；前三年末的新投资从次年起赚 20%，此后新投资只赚股权成本 10%，既有盈利持续。前三笔股利和下一期盈利为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reconstruct the two-stage example with constant shares and first-year EPS of 120. Retain 40% annually; investments made at the first three year-ends earn 20% from the following year, after which new investment earns the 10% equity cost. Existing earnings persist:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 年末 / Year-end | 每股盈利 / EPS | 当年股利 / Dividend | 股利现值 / Dividend PV |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 120 | 72 | 65.4545 |
| 2 | 129.6 | 77.76 | 64.2645 |
| 3 | 139.968 | 83.9808 | 63.0960 |

第三年末投入的资金还会使 $E_4=139.968(1+0.4\times0.2)=151.16544$。因此 $P_3=E_4/0.10=1511.6544$，不是 $E_3/0.10$；把它折回今天，再加三笔股利，得到 $P_0\approx1328.54335$。若前三年的新投资也只赚 10%，同一模型给出 1200，优势贡献为 128.54335。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Investment at year-end 3 raises $E_4$ to 151.16544. Hence $P_3=E_4/0.10=1511.6544$, not $E_3/0.10$. Discounting this and adding the first three dividends gives approximately 1328.54335. If early new investment also earns only 10%, value is 1200, a difference of 128.54335.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这三个结果与原记录中的 1070、800、270 不一致；上述逐期现金和终值给出了可复核的修正。课件 PDF621 没有那组数例，且把可变 $R_n$ 写在单一增长幂内。本例明确只用恒定高回报阶段；若逐年 $R_j$ 不同，则应递推 $E_{j+1}=E_j(1+bR_j)$，不能用某一年回报率代替全部历史增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These results differ from the note's 1070, 800, and 270; the explicit cash flows make the correction verifiable. Slide PDF621 contains no such numerical example and uses ambiguous variable-return notation. This example fixes the high-phase return. With varying $R_j$, use $E_{j+1}=E_j(1+bR_j)$ rather than one year's return for all past growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 六、从经营现金现值走到一股普通股
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Move from operating cash value to one common share.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与股利、FCFE 不同，FCFF 归属于全部资本提供者。贴现前应把风险与融资政策配好，而不是从账上找一个历史借款利率来代用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
FCFF belongs to all capital providers, unlike dividends and FCFE. Match risk and financing policy before discounting, rather than substituting an old borrowing rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先检查[[名义与实际贴现]]：按各期货币金额、含预期通胀预测的现金，配名义贴现率；按不变购买力预测的现金，配实际贴现率。终值增长率也必须沿用同一口径；只确认币种相同还不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check [[名义与实际贴现|nominal–real consistency]] first: cash flows in future money amounts, including expected inflation, require nominal rates; constant-purchasing-power flows require real rates. Terminal growth must use the same basis. Matching currency alone is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里使用的是风险现金的期望预测。把坏结果纳入预测，与把现金转成确定性等价，是两种不同操作。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The model uses expected risky cash flows. Including adverse outcomes in the forecast differs from converting cash into certainty equivalents.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[风险调整不重复计价]]

![[加权平均资本成本]]
![[企业现金流估值]]

原记录的一步 FCF 例子中，第一年 CapEx 是 20，后续却改为折旧的 1.1 倍。因此，不能同时维持“首年 FCFF=60，此后自动增长 3%”。若把 EBIT、折旧和年度非现金营运资本增加都按 3% 增长，并保留第二年起 $CapEx=1.1Dep$，则第二年 FCFF 为 $103(1-25\%)+10.3-5.15-11.33=71.07$，不是 61.8。按题设 9.5% 折现，并假定从第二笔开始稳定增长 3%，经营价值为 $[60+71.07/(0.095-0.03)]/1.095\approx1053.32$。这只是将其现金路径明确后的条件复算，尚未证明题设债务和市值权重属于同一个融资预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The note sets first-year capex at 20 but subsequently at 1.1 times depreciation, so FCFF cannot also grow automatically from 60 at 3%. If EBIT, depreciation, and annual non-cash working-capital investment grow at 3%, second-year FCFF is 71.07 rather than 61.8. At the given 9.5% rate, with stable 3% growth from the second cash flow, operating value is approximately 1053.32. This conditional recalculation does not establish consistency of the note's debt and market-value weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经营价值之后还有两步：先把未包含的资产和不属于母公司普通股的权利逐一对齐，再处理现有普通股与潜在权利的分配。下面两张卡使用同一个算例，第一步得到 800，第二步得到每股 7.60。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two steps remain: reconcile excluded assets and non-parent/common claims, then allocate value between outstanding ordinary shares and other equity rights. The next two atoms use one example, moving first to 800 and then to 7.60 per share.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[经营价值转股权价值]]

800只是这个算例的权利清点结果；真实公司另有优先股或养老金缺口时，先按上面的价值桥补齐，再走到每股价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The 800 reflects this example's claim inventory. For a real company with preferred stock or a pension shortfall, complete the value bridge above before proceeding to per-share value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[估值日每股价值]]

## 七、用市场可比价格交叉检查，而不是省略经济条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Use comparable prices without losing economic conditions.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相对估值把同行市场价格带进分析，省去了一些显性预测，却没有消灭那些预测。增长、风险、利润率和再投资差异仍会藏在倍数里。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Relative valuation uses peer prices, reducing explicit forecasting without eliminating its assumptions. Growth, risk, margins, and reinvestment remain embedded in the multiple.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[相对估值]]

先复习比率的准确分子和分母，再看条件模型为何允许不同公司有不同的合理倍数。PE 乘 EPS 得到元／股，不是公司股权总额；P/B 的账面分母也不是清算保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Establish each numerator and denominator before examining conditional fair multiples. PE times EPS yields value per share, not total equity; the book denominator in P/B is not a liquidation guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[市盈率]]
![[市盈率基本面约束]]
![[市净率]]
![[市净率基本面约束]]

收入比利润更靠上游，但收入相同的公司可以有完全不同的成本和资本需求。PS 的可计算性，不等于收入确认必然可靠，也不等于任何时期都适用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revenue precedes profit, but equal revenue can coexist with very different costs and capital needs. A computable P/S ratio does not establish revenue reliability or universal applicability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[市销率]]

增长修正也不是把所有差异都除掉。先把增长率的百分数单位写对，再区分 PEG 的定义与它能支持的比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Growth scaling does not remove all differences. Specify percentage units, then distinguish the PEG definition from the comparisons it supports.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[PEG]]
![[PEG比较边界]]

如果改用企业口径倍数，分子也必须跨到企业一侧。EBITDA 尚未扣掉维持经营所需的资本开支，不能把它当成另一种名称的 FCFF。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An enterprise multiple requires an enterprise numerator. EBITDA excludes capital expenditure needed to sustain operations and is not simply another name for FCFF.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[EV-EBITDA倍数]]

## 八、把价格判断写成可以被新证据修正的论点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Make the valuation argument revisable with evidence.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后不要只保留一个每股数。记录最影响结论的假设、支持它的证据和改变判断的条件，才能在下次财报出来时知道该更新哪里。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep more than a price target: identify the influential assumptions, supporting evidence, and conditions for revision so that new results lead to specific updates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面的两阶段模型都依赖预测期之后的现金。先辨认这种依赖有多大，再把检验接到具体的经营与回报假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The earlier two-stage models depend on cash beyond the forecast. Measure that reliance before testing the specific operating and return assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[终值现值占比]]

![[估值敏感性]]
![[反向估值]]
![[投资论点核验]]

合上公式后，试着解释一条完整路线：为何选这类现金流、为什么用这个回报要求、终值覆盖哪些年份、哪些资产或权利还要调整，以及市价需要怎样的经营结果。如果其中一步只能回答“模板就是这么写”，先回到那一步核对。接下来的[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/10_均值方差与资产配置|组合理论]]再讨论：即使单项估值有依据，把它放进组合后，整体风险如何改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without looking at the formulas, explain the cash-flow choice, required return, terminal years, remaining claim adjustments, and operating conditions needed to support price. Revisit any step justified only by a template. [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/10_均值方差与资产配置|Portfolio theory]] then asks how adding the security changes total portfolio risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
