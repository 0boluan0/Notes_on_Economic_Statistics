# 一. 名词解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Definitions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 自由现金流贴现模型：自由现金流贴现模型是一种企业价值评估方法，其核心思想是将企业未来的自由现金流按照一定的贴现率折现到当前，从而得出企业的内在价值。自由现金流是指企业在支付了运营成本和资本支出后，可以自由分配给股东和债权人的现金流量。DCF 模型的关键在于预测未来的自由现金流以及确定合适的贴现率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Discounted free-cash-flow model: this is a method of valuing a firm by discounting its future free cash flows to the present at an appropriate rate, thereby estimating intrinsic value. Free cash flow is the cash remaining after operating costs and capital expenditure that can be distributed to shareholders and creditors. The key tasks in a DCF model are forecasting future free cash flow and choosing an appropriate discount rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
2. WACC 是加权平均资本成本，它表示企业为筹集资本（包括股权资本和债务资本）所需支付的平均成本。WACC 的计算公式为：$WACC=(E/D+E)×Ke+(D/E+D)×Kd×(1−T)$
	E是股权的市场价值。D是债务的市场价值。Ke是股权资本成本。Kd是债务资本成本。T是企业所得税税率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
2. WACC is the weighted average cost of capital: the average cost a firm pays to obtain financing from equity and debt. The formula is written here as $WACC=(E/D+E)×Ke+(D/E+D)×Kd×(1−T)$. $E$ is the market value of equity, $D$ is the market value of debt, $Ke$ is the cost of equity, $Kd$ is the cost of debt, and $T$ is the corporate income-tax rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
3. FCFF 是企业自由现金流，是指企业在支付了运营成本和资本支出后，可以自由分配给所有资本提供者（包括股东和债权人）的现金流量。FCFF 的计算公式为：$FCFF=EBIT×(1−T)+折旧−资本支出−营运资本增加额$EBIT是息税前利润。T是企业所得税税率。折旧是固定资产折旧费用。资本支出是企业用于购置或维护固定资产的支出。营运资本增加额是企业为维持日常运营所需的流动资产增加额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
3. FCFF is free cash flow to the firm: cash available to all capital providers, including shareholders and creditors, after operating costs and capital expenditure. The formula is $FCFF=EBIT×(1−T)+depreciation−capital expenditure−increase in working capital$. $EBIT$ is earnings before interest and tax, $T$ is the corporate income-tax rate, depreciation is the depreciation charge on fixed assets, capital expenditure is spending to acquire or maintain fixed assets, and the increase in working capital is the additional current assets needed for day-to-day operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
4. FCFE 是股东自由现金流，是指企业在支付了运营成本、资本支出和债务利息后，可以自由分配给股东的现金流量。FCFE 的计算公式为：$FCFE=FCFF−利息费用×(1−T)+新增债务−偿还债务$利息费用是企业支付的债务利息。新增债务是企业新增的债务融资。偿还债务是企业偿还的债务本金。FCFE 是股东价值评估的基础，通常使用股权资本成本作为贴现率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
4. FCFE is free cash flow to equity: cash available for distribution to shareholders after operating costs, capital expenditure, and debt interest. The formula is $FCFE=FCFF−interest expense×(1−T)+new borrowing−debt repayment$. Interest expense is the interest paid on debt, new borrowing is additional debt financing, and debt repayment is principal repaid. FCFE is a basis for equity valuation and is normally discounted at the cost of equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 二.简答题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*II. Short-answer questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


1. 成本领先战略
指公司通过实现规模经济、提高生产效率、控制成本等手段，在行业中以最低成本提供产品或服务，从而获得较高的市场份额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Cost-leadership strategy
A company uses economies of scale, higher production efficiency, cost control, and similar measures to offer a product or service at the industry's lowest cost and thereby gain a larger market share.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
2. 差异化战略
公司通过提供具有独特属性或附加价值的产品（如设计、技术、服务等），使其在消费者心中形成区别于竞争对手的形象，从而获得溢价能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
2. Differentiation strategy
A company offers products with distinctive attributes or added value—such as design, technology, or service—so that consumers perceive them as different from competitors' offerings, giving the company pricing power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
3. 集中化战略
也称为聚焦战略，是指公司集中资源服务于某一特定市场细分领域或客户群体。可分为成本集中和差异化集中两种模式。此战略适用于市场规模较小但利润率较高的细分市场，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
3. Focus strategy
Also called a concentration strategy, it directs company resources toward a particular market segment or customer group. It can take the form of cost focus or differentiation focus and is suited to relatively small but high-margin segments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


相对估值模型和绝对估值模型是两种常用的股票估值方法。相对估值模型基于市场比较，通过比较类似企业的市场指标（如市盈率、市净率等）来确定企业价值，具有简单直观、便于比较的特点，但受市场情绪影响较大，且基准选择不当可能导致估值不准确。绝对估值模型则注重企业的内在价值，基于公司未来的现金流、盈利能力等内在因素进行评估，理论上更为完备，但需要对未来的现金流进行预测并选择合适的折现率，预测难度较大，且折现率的确定具有一定的主观性。相对估值模型适合市场比较，但受市场情绪影响较大；绝对估值模型适合独立评估，但对预测能力和折现率选择要求较高。两者各有优缺点，具体选择需根据企业的行业特点、市场环境及投资者的分析能力来决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Relative and absolute valuation are two common approaches to equity valuation. Relative valuation uses market comparisons and values a firm against metrics for similar companies, such as price-to-earnings and price-to-book ratios. It is simple, intuitive, and convenient for comparison, but it is sensitive to market sentiment and to the choice of comparable firms. Absolute valuation focuses on intrinsic value derived from factors such as future cash flow and earning power. It is theoretically more complete, but it requires difficult forecasts and a discount rate whose selection contains judgment. Relative valuation is useful for market comparison, whereas absolute valuation supports a stand-alone assessment; the appropriate choice depends on the firm's industry, the market environment, and the analyst's capabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*I. Short-answer questions*

## 1. 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **市场价格反映一切信息假设**
市场价格中已经充分反映了所有已知信息，投资者只需依据价格及成交量走势来进行分析，不必额外关注新闻、基本面等因素。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. **The assumption that market prices reflect all information**
Market prices already fully reflect all known information, so investors need only analyze price and volume movements rather than separately consider news or fundamentals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
2. **价格沿趋势运动假设**
市场价格在运行过程中会形成趋势，即$\text{上升趋势}$、$\text{下降趋势}$或$\text{横盘趋势}$，技术分析正是利用这一趋势特征来研判未来走势。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
2. **The assumption that prices move in trends**
Market prices form upward, downward, or sideways trends. Technical analysis uses this tendency to assess future price movements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
3. **历史会重演假设**
由于市场参与者的行为和心理相似，价格走势的形态和模式会重复出现，因而过去形成的图形和技术指标在未来也具有参考意义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
3. **The assumption that history repeats itself**
Because market participants display similar behavior and psychology over time, price patterns recur, giving past chart formations and technical indicators potential relevance for the future.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 图表分析
• 特点：通过价格和成交量图表（如K线图、趋势线、支撑位与阻力位）观察和判断市场走势；直观反映市场心理和情绪，依赖视觉模式识别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Chart analysis
Characteristic: it assesses market movements using price and volume charts, such as candlestick charts, trend lines, support, and resistance. It provides an intuitive representation of market psychology and sentiment and relies on visual pattern recognition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
2. **指标分析**
• 特点：利用数学公式构造的技术指标（例如$MA$均线、$MACD$、$RSI$等）对价格数据进行量化分析；较为客观，便于建立交易模型和自动化交易。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
2. **Indicator analysis**
Characteristic: it uses mathematically constructed indicators, such as moving averages, MACD, and RSI, to analyze price data quantitatively. It is relatively objective and facilitates trading models and automated execution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
3. **周期分析**
• 特点：分析市场的周期性波动规律，通过不同时间周期的变化来判断价格的周期性转折和调整；强调历史数据中周期性因素的重复性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
3. **Cycle analysis**
Characteristic: it studies recurring market fluctuations and uses changes across time horizons to identify cyclical turning points and corrections, emphasizing the repetition of periodic factors in historical data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **基本分析确定投资标的**
基本分析侧重于研究公司的$盈利能力$、$财务报表$、$宏观经济环境$等基本面因素，用于判断企业的内在价值，从而选择基本面良好的股票。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. **Use fundamental analysis to select an investment**
Fundamental analysis examines earning power, financial statements, the macroeconomic environment, and other fundamental factors to assess intrinsic value and select companies with sound fundamentals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
2. **技术分析把握交易时机**
在确定了投资标的后，利用技术分析判断市场的$买入$和$卖出$时机，观察价格走势和技术指标，寻求短期市场波动的机会。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
2. **Use technical analysis to time trades**
After selecting an investment, use price trends and technical indicators to judge entry and exit timing and seek opportunities arising from short-term market fluctuations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
3. **两者互补提高决策效率**
通过基本分析提供长期价值依据，确保投资具有扎实的基本面支撑；利用技术分析细化入市与退市的时机选择，降低交易风险，提高整体收益率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
3. **Combine the two approaches to improve decisions**
Fundamental analysis supplies the long-term value case and verifies that an investment has solid underlying support, while technical analysis refines entry and exit timing to reduce trading risk and improve overall returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
