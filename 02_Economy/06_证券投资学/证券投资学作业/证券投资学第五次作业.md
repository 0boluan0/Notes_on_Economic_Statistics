# 一. 名词解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Definitions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 成长型产业。成长型产业的运动状态与经济活动总水平的周期及其振幅无关。这些产业销售收入和利润的增长速度不受宏观经济周期性变化的影响，特别是经济衰退的消极影响。它们依靠技术进步、推出新产品、提供更优质的服务及改善经营管理，可实现持续长。例如，在过去的20年内，信息产业和生物制药产业就是典型的成长型产业。
2. 周期型产业。周期型产业的运动状态直接与经济周期相关。当经济处于上升时期时，这些产业会紧随其扩张;当经济衰退时，这些产业也相应跌落。产生这种现象的原因是，当经济衰退时，对这些产业产品的购买被延迟到经济改善之后，如珠宝业、耐用品制造业及其他依赖于需求的具有收入弹性的产业就属于典型的周期型产业。
3. 防御型产业。防御型产业与周期型产业刚好相反，这类产业的运动状态并不受经济周期的影响。也就是说，无论宏观经济处在经济周期的哪个阶段，产业的销售收入和利润均呈缓慢增长态势或变化不大。正是由于这个原因，对这类产业的投资便属于收入投资，而非资本利得投资。例如，食品业和公用事业就属于防御型产业，因为社会需求对其产品的收入弹性较小，所以这些产业的收入相对稳定。
4. 产业生命周期。与世界上的万事万物一样，产业也会经历一个由产生到成长再到衰落的发展演变过程，这个过程称为产业的生命周期。产业的生命周期可分为四个阶段，即初创阶段(又称初创期)、成长阶段(又称成长期)、成熟阶段(又称成熟期)和衰退阶段(又称衰退期)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Growth industry. A growth industry's performance is largely independent of the timing and amplitude of the overall business cycle. Its revenue and profit growth are comparatively resistant to cyclical fluctuations, especially recessions. Sustained growth instead comes from technological progress, new products, better services, and improved management. The information and biopharmaceutical industries were typical examples over the preceding twenty years.<br>
**2.** Cyclical industry. A cyclical industry's performance moves directly with the business cycle: it expands during an upswing and contracts in a recession. One reason is that consumers postpone purchases from these industries until economic conditions improve. Jewellery, durable-goods manufacturing, and other demand-dependent industries with high income elasticity are typical examples.<br>
**3.** Defensive industry. A defensive industry's sales and profits are relatively insensitive to the business cycle, growing slowly or changing little across different macroeconomic conditions. Investment in such an industry therefore tends to emphasise income rather than capital gains. Food and public utilities are typical examples because demand for their products has low income elasticity, making industry revenue relatively stable.<br>
**4.** Industry life cycle. Like other economic activities, an industry typically evolves from emergence through growth and eventually into decline. This process is called the industry life cycle and is commonly divided into four stages: introduction, growth, maturity, and decline.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic45-industry:start HW01 -->
> [!note] 四个定义的适用边界
> [[成长型行业]]的持续驱动不等于周期影响为零；[[周期型行业]]的反应是相对特征，不保证逐期机械同向；[[防御型行业]]也可有资本利得或股价损失。原课第 449 页还列“成长周期型”，见[[成长与周期敏感性]]。[[行业生命周期]]是分析框架，不是每个行业必经固定轨道；上面英文部分已有相对语气，但中文绝对表述应按本注理解。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[成长型行业|Structural growth]] does not remove cyclicality. [[周期型行业|Cyclical sensitivity]] is relative rather than mechanical in every period, and [[防御型行业|defensive industries]] can produce capital gains or stock losses. Slide 449 also includes a growth-cyclical category; see [[成长与周期敏感性|the distinction]]. The [[行业生命周期|life cycle]] is a framework, not an inevitable fixed path. Some original English already qualifies the stronger Chinese wording; this note states the intended boundary.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 二, 简答
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*II. Short answers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 产业环境分析包括的内容：
主要包括宏观环境分析（政治、经济、社会、技术、环境、法律，即PESTEL分析）、产业竞争环境分析（行业竞争状况、产业结构、产业规模和成长性、进入与退出壁垒等）和微观企业环境分析（企业内部资源、能力、战略等）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** An industry-environment analysis includes:<br>
The main components are the macro environment—political, economic, social, technological, environmental, and legal factors, or PESTEL analysis—the competitive environment, including competitive intensity, industry structure, scale and growth, and barriers to entry and exit, and the firm-level environment, including internal resources, capabilities, and strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic45-industry:start HW02 -->
> [!note] 区分三层分析对象
> 宏观、行业与公司三层可以连接，但企业内部资源、能力、战略属于公司分析，不全部是行业外部环境。[[行业分析]]先固定行业，再用[[波特五力]]分析关系；需要比较公司时用[[行业可比样本选择]]。对应原课第 461–466 页及 CFA 行业分析公开摘要，见原子来源。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Macro, industry, and company analysis connect, but internal resources, capabilities, and strategy are not all external industry conditions. [[行业分析|Industry analysis]] fixes the boundary, [[波特五力|five forces]] examines relationships, and [[行业可比样本选择|peer selection]] supports company comparisons. The sources are course slides 461–466 and the CFA Institute summary cited in those atoms.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

2. **产业生命周期各阶段的特征：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **Characteristics of each stage of the industry life cycle:**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **导入期**：市场规模小，需求不确定，技术尚未成熟，竞争少，风险高。
• **成长期**：市场快速扩张，需求快速增长，技术趋于成熟，竞争逐渐增加，利润率较高。
• **成熟期**：市场规模大，增长缓慢甚至停滞，技术成熟，竞争激烈，利润率下降。
• **衰退期**：市场需求减少，规模萎缩，利润进一步下降，企业逐步退出或转型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Introduction stage**: The market is small, demand is uncertain, technology is immature, competition is limited, and risk is high.
• **Growth stage**: The market and demand expand rapidly, technology approaches maturity, competition increases, and profit margins remain relatively high.
• **Maturity stage**: The market is large, growth slows or stops, technology is mature, competition is intense, and profit margins decline.
• **Decline stage**: Demand falls, the market contracts, profits decline further, and firms gradually exit or transform their businesses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic45-industry:start HW03 -->
> [!note] 阶段特征是可检验的概括
> [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/证券投资学.pdf#page=469|第 469 页图表]]的纵轴为产量，表中成熟期“利润最高”不能改读为利润率最高；本答案所写利润率变化与总利润不是同一对象。[[行业生命周期判断]]需并看需求、产能、进入退出与利润证据；竞争或利润率不会只因阶段名称而必然变化，阶段也不单独决定股票回报。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/证券投资学.pdf#page=469|Slide 469]] plots output and describes total profit, not a guaranteed maximum margin. The answer's margin changes concern a different measure. [[行业生命周期判断|Stage assessment]] combines demand, capacity, entry and exit, and profit evidence; no stage name alone determines rivalry, margins, or stock returns.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

3. 白酒行业波特五种竞争力模型分析：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** Applying Porter's five-forces framework to the baijiu industry:<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **行业内竞争者的竞争程度**：白酒行业竞争激烈，品牌众多，市场集中度较高，龙头企业占据明显优势。
• **新进入者的威胁**：进入壁垒较高，品牌影响力、渠道建设、资金和技术要求较高，新进入者威胁较小。
• **替代品的威胁**：葡萄酒、啤酒及其他酒类产品是潜在替代品，但白酒文化消费习惯稳定，替代威胁有限。
• **供应商议价能力**：原材料供应充足且标准化，供应商议价能力相对较弱。
• **购买者议价能力**：消费者品牌忠诚度高，需求稳定，个体购买者议价能力较弱，但渠道经销商议价能力相对较强。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Rivalry among existing competitors**: Competition is intense, with many brands and a relatively concentrated market in which leading firms enjoy clear advantages.
• **Threat of new entrants**: Entry barriers are high because brand building, distribution channels, capital, and production expertise are demanding; the threat from new entrants is therefore limited.
• **Threat of substitutes**: Wine, beer, and other alcoholic drinks are potential substitutes, but entrenched cultural preferences for baijiu limit this threat.
• **Bargaining power of suppliers**: Raw materials are plentiful and standardised, so suppliers have relatively weak bargaining power.
• **Bargaining power of buyers**: Brand loyalty and stable demand weaken the bargaining power of individual consumers, although distributors have relatively stronger bargaining power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic45-industry:start HW04 -->
> [!note] 白酒五力答案保留为待证据检验的应用
> 这五项是原作业判断，不是已完成的当前白酒实证研究。先说明价格带、地域、时期与渠道；[[市场集中度]]高和[[行业内竞争]]激烈可以同时成立。品牌、工艺和渠道是否构成有效[[进入壁垒]]要有证据；葡萄酒、啤酒是白酒边界外的[[替代品威胁|替代方式]]，若改研究全部酒类则关系会改变。[[供应商议价能力]]须分普通原料和特殊投入，[[买方议价能力]]须分经销商与终端客户；文化偏好或忠诚度不能单独证明威胁有限或谈判能力弱。框架支持见 HBS 五力原文及原课第 473–478 页，不新增对白酒市场的事实断言。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These are original assignment judgments, not a completed current empirical study. Specify price segment, geography, period, and channels. High [[市场集中度|concentration]] can coexist with intense [[行业内竞争|rivalry]]. Brands, expertise, and channels need evidence as [[进入壁垒|entry barriers]]. Wine and beer are [[替代品威胁|substitutes]] outside a baijiu boundary, but relationships change for an all-alcohol boundary. Separate inputs for [[供应商议价能力|supplier power]] and distributors from end users for [[买方议价能力|buyer power]]. Preference or loyalty alone is insufficient. The framework is supported by HBS and slides 473–478; no new empirical claims about baijiu are asserted.
