一.名词解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
I. Definitions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产组合理论:科维茨的资产组合理论否定了古典定价理论中关于投资者的单一预期假设, 即期望收益最大化假设,因为该假设要求投资者只投资所有证券中期望收益最大的证券或者证券组合,而与现实中投资者的分散化投资组合相违背。资产组合理论在现实的基础上,提出了资产组合均值方差理论。用资产组合的均值代表期望收益, 用方差代表组合的风险,投资者理性的投资方式是实现预期收益最大化(风险不变) 或者风险最小化(预期收益不变)的资产组合方式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Portfolio theory: Markowitz's portfolio theory rejects the idea that investors choose assets solely by maximising expected return. That rule would require an investor to hold only the security or portfolio with the highest expected return, contrary to the diversification observed in practice. Mean–variance portfolio theory instead represents expected return by the portfolio mean and risk by its variance. A rational investor therefore seeks either the greatest expected return for a given level of risk or the lowest risk for a given expected return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[CAPM|CAPM]]:资本资产定价模型从投资者效用最大化出发,认为在市场均衡条件下,单一资产或资产组合的收益由两方面组成,即无风险收着和风险溢价,并且这种组合方式可以线性的形式表示.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[CAPM|CAPM]]: The capital asset pricing model begins with investor utility maximisation. In market equilibrium, the expected return on an individual asset or portfolio consists of the risk-free return plus a risk premium, and this relationship can be written in linear form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CCAPM:该模型继续假设投资者追求效用最大化,将投资视为将来消费的资金来源或者资金保障,从而把产品市场、要素市场和金融市场上的各种变量通过消费和投资的关系联系起来,进而获得了真正意义上的资产组合决策的一般均衡分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CCAPM: The consumption-based capital asset pricing model also assumes utility-maximising investors, but treats investment as a way of financing and safeguarding future consumption. The consumption–investment relationship links variables across product, factor, and financial markets, producing a general-equilibrium account of portfolio choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前景理论:也称展望理论或视野理论，由 D.Kahneman 与 A.Tversky 于1979年提出，用于解释个人的决策行为。 他们认为，通常情况下概率和价值评估等主观判断、决策行为依赖于有限的可供利用的数据，这些依据直观推断与经验规则得到的信息会产生系统性偏误。 **前景理论**以“有限理性”为前提，反映决策者的主观风险偏好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Prospect theory: Proposed by D. Kahneman and A. Tversky in 1979, this theory explains individual decision-making under risk. Judgements about probability and value are often based on limited information, while intuitive inference and rules of thumb can create systematic bias. **Prospect theory** therefore departs from full rationality and describes decision-makers' subjective attitudes toward risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

期货溢价理论:赫希雷弗从市场不完善的角度出发,即部分权利不能在市场上进行交易和交易者存在进入期货市场的固定成本,认为当期货市场达到均衡时, 期货风险溢价不仅取决于系统性风险,还取决于对冲风险,因此对冲压力在期货风险溢价中古有重要地位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Futures risk-premium theory: Hirshleifer approaches futures pricing through market imperfections: some claims cannot be traded, and traders face fixed costs of entering futures markets. In equilibrium, the futures risk premium therefore depends not only on systematic risk but also on hedging risk, giving hedging pressure an important role in the premium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

II. Short-answer questions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 资产组合理论主要研究投资者如何通过资产分散配置来实现风险和收益的最佳平衡，其核心思想是利用资产收益之间的不完全相关性来构建有效前沿组合；而CAPM则在此基础上进一步引入了无风险资产和市场组合概念，并且假定投资者具有同质预期，通过均衡分析推导出了资产的定价公式，明确提出单个资产的预期收益取决于该资产对市场组合的系统风险
2. 资产收益受到多个宏观经济因素或系统性因素共同影响，资产价格的形成过程中，如果存在套利机会（即无风险获得收益），理性的投资者会迅速买入低估资产、卖出高估资产，使套利机会消失。因此，APT强调的是 多因素模型，认为资产预期收益取决于多个系统性风险因素，而非仅取决于市场整体风险一个因素。与CAPM的区别主要在于：CAPM强调单一市场风险因子，通过资产与市场组合的相关性确定资产价格，且假设严格（如投资者同质预期）；而APT则强调多个风险因素共同作用，假设相对宽松，不需要投资者具有同质预期。二者的联系在于：APT可看作CAPM的扩展与推广，CAPM是APT在仅考虑单一市场风险因子情况下的特例，两者都强调系统性风险决定资产收益，都以风险补偿为定价基础。
3. 
	弱式有效市场下，股价已经反映了历史价格信息，技术分析将失去作用，但通过基本面分析或内幕信息可能还能获得超额收益，因此适合采取基本面分析策略。
	半强式有效市场下，股票价格反映了所有公开的信息，基本面分析和技术分析都难以获得持续的超额收益，投资者更多地应采取指数化投资（如投资指数基金）以降低成本，实现市场平均收益。
	强式有效市场下，股票价格已经反映了所有信息，包括非公开的信息，投资者不论采用何种分析手段都无法持续获取超额收益，因此，投资策略最好是被动式投资，如长期持有市场组合。

1. Portfolio theory asks how diversification can give investors the best attainable trade-off between risk and return. Its central insight is that imperfect correlation among asset returns makes it possible to construct an efficient frontier. CAPM builds on that framework by introducing a risk-free asset and the market portfolio and by assuming homogeneous expectations. Its equilibrium pricing relation says that an asset's expected return depends on its systematic exposure to the market portfolio.
2. Asset returns may respond to several macroeconomic or systematic factors. If mispricing creates a risk-free profit, rational investors buy the undervalued asset and sell the overvalued one until the opportunity disappears. APT therefore uses a multifactor model: expected returns compensate exposure to several systematic risk factors, rather than only aggregate market risk. CAPM instead uses a single market factor, relates prices to an asset's covariance with the market portfolio, and relies on stronger assumptions such as homogeneous expectations. APT can thus be viewed as a broader framework, with the single-factor CAPM as a special case. Both models price systematic risk through a risk premium.
3.
	Under weak-form efficiency, prices already reflect historical price information, so technical analysis cannot consistently earn excess returns. Fundamental analysis or non-public information may still have value, making fundamental analysis a possible strategy.
	Under semi-strong-form efficiency, prices reflect all public information, so neither fundamental nor technical analysis can consistently earn excess returns. Investors are therefore better served by low-cost index investing that captures the market return.
	Under strong-form efficiency, prices reflect all information, including non-public information. No analytical method can consistently produce excess returns, so a passive strategy such as holding the market portfolio over the long term is most appropriate.
