## 1.反转形态定义和两种举例

## 2.八浪结构：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. The eight-wave structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

8浪结构是艾略特波浪理论的基本形态，由5个推动浪（推动趋势）与3个调整浪（反趋势）组成。结构如下：
推动浪：第1浪、3浪、5浪（主趋势方向）
调整浪：第2浪、4浪（主趋势中的回调）
回调浪（A-B-C）：整体趋势的修正部分
总体为：1-2-3-4-5-A-B-C，共8浪。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The eight-wave structure is the basic Elliott Wave pattern. It consists of five motive waves that advance the trend and three corrective waves that move against it.
Motive waves: waves 1, 3, and 5, which move in the direction of the main trend.
Corrective waves within the motive sequence: waves 2 and 4, which retrace the main trend.
The A–B–C correction: the corrective phase of the complete movement.
Together the sequence is 1–2–3–4–5–A–B–C, for a total of eight waves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.支撑线和压力线的作用及转化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. The roles and transformation of support and resistance lines*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支撑线：当股价下跌到某个价位附近时，会停止下跌甚至回升，这个价位被称为支撑线。
压力线：当股价上涨到某个价位附近时，会停止上涨甚至回落，这个价位被称为压力线。
作用：(1)支撑线起阻止价格继续下跌的作用。(2)压力线起阻止格继续上升的作用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Support: a price level near which a falling share price stops declining and may rebound.
Resistance: a price level near which a rising share price stops advancing and may fall back.
Their roles are: (1) support impedes further price declines; and (2) resistance impedes further price increases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支撑线和压力线的相互转化：
支撑线转化为压力线：当支撑线被跌破后，原来的支撑区域可能会转化为压力线，阻止股价进一步下跌。
压力线转化为支撑线：当压力线被突破后，原来的阻力区域可能会转化为支撑线，阻止股价进一步上涨。转化的条件转化的关键在于价格的突破。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Support and resistance can exchange roles.
Support becomes resistance when a former support area is broken to the downside and subsequently acts as resistance; the source states that it prevents a further decline.
Resistance becomes support when a former resistance area is broken to the upside and subsequently acts as support; the source states that it prevents a further rise. The key condition for either transformation is a price breakout.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.对称三角形整理过程和空间计算方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Formation of a symmetrical triangle and calculation of its price objective*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

整理过程：对称三角形出现在上涨或下跌趋势中，代表多空双方力量趋于平衡，价格高点逐渐降低，低点逐渐升高，形成收敛形态。通常是趋势中继形态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Formation: a symmetrical triangle can appear within an upward or downward trend. It represents increasing balance between buyers and sellers as successive highs fall and successive lows rise, producing a converging shape. It is usually treated as a continuation pattern.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

空间计算方法：向上或向下突破时，目标价格 = 突破点 +（或 -）三角形底边的高度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price objective: after an upward or downward breakout, target price equals the breakout point plus or minus the height of the triangle's base.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6.简述移动平均线的葛南杰法则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Briefly describe Granville's rules for moving averages*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

葛南杰法则（Granville’s Rules）共有**八大法则**，四买四卖：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Granville's Rules comprise eight rules: four buy signals and four sell signals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

四买点:
1. 价格由下向上突破均线
2. 均线走平后价格回调不破均线
3. 均线由下转上
4. 均线缓慢上升，价格回踩均线后反弹
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Four buy signals:
**1.** Price crosses the moving average from below.<br>
**2.** After the moving average flattens, price pulls back without breaking below it.<br>
**3.** The moving average turns upward.<br>
**4.** The moving average rises gradually and price rebounds after retesting it.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

四卖点：
1. 价格由上向下跌破均线
2. 均线走平后价格反弹不破均线
3. 均线由上转下
4. 均线缓慢下降，价格反弹遇阻回落
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Four sell signals:
**1.** Price crosses the moving average from above.<br>
**2.** After the moving average flattens, a price rebound fails to break above it.<br>
**3.** The moving average turns downward.<br>
**4.** The moving average declines gradually and a price rebound meets resistance and reverses.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7.技术指标中参数的选择是否影响技术指标的结论？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Does parameter choice affect the conclusions of a technical indicator?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

技术指标的参数选择**显著影响结论**。参数设置不同会改变指标的敏感度与滞后性，从而影响买卖信号的频率与准确性。例如，较小周期的RSI会更敏感，适合短线操作，而较大周期更适合趋势判断。

Parameter selection **materially affects the conclusion**. Different settings change an indicator's sensitivity and lag, thereby changing the frequency and accuracy of trading signals. A shorter-period RSI, for example, is more sensitive and better suited to short-term trading, whereas a longer period is more suitable for identifying trends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8.RSI、KD指标的计算和使用法则？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. How are RSI and KD indicators calculated and used?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

RSI（相对强弱指标）
1. **计算**：RSI = 100 - [100 / (1 + RS)]，其中 RS = 平均上涨幅度 / 平均下跌幅度（通常取14日）
2. **使用法则**：
RSI > 70 表示超买，可能回调
RSI < 30 表示超卖，可能反弹
与价格背离可作为重要信号
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
RSI, the Relative Strength Index:
**1.** **Calculation:** RSI = 100 − [100 / (1 + RS)], where RS is the average gain divided by the average loss, usually over 14 days.<br>
**2.** **Rules of use:**<br>
RSI above 70 indicates an overbought condition and a possible pullback.
RSI below 30 indicates an oversold condition and a possible rebound.
Divergence from price can serve as an important signal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

KDJ（随机指标）
1. **计算**：基于随机值RSV，K、D、J分别为不同周期的平滑移动平均值
2.  **使用法则**：
K线与D线的交叉为买卖信号
J值过高（>100）或过低（<0）提示市场极端情况
与价格背离可辅助判断转折点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
KDJ, the stochastic indicator:
**1.** **Calculation:** it starts from the raw stochastic value, with K, D, and J constructed from smoothed moving averages over different periods.<br>
**2.** **Rules of use:**<br>
Crossovers of the K and D lines provide buy or sell signals.
An unusually high J value above 100 or a low value below zero indicates an extreme market condition.
Divergence from price can help identify a turning point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6.什么叫卖空？如果你有本金1000元，卖空股票A并将收入的500元连同本金全部投资于股票B，在你的资产组合中，股票A、股票B的权数各是多少？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. What is short selling? If you have capital of 1,000 yuan, short 500 yuan of stock A, and invest the proceeds together with your original capital in stock B, what are the portfolio weights of A and B?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

投资者预测某种资产价格将会下跌，此时投资者可能到证券商那里去借这种股票，并按现行的价格售出，等以后再以低价购回，从中赚取价差，这种投资策略叫卖空。卖空时，投资的权数为负值。股票A的权数为-0.5,股票B的权数为1.5。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When an investor expects an asset's price to fall, the investor may borrow the shares from a broker, sell them at the current price, and later repurchase them at a lower price to earn the difference. This strategy is short selling, and the short position has a negative portfolio weight. Stock A has weight −0.5 and stock B has weight 1.5.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 如果两项资产完全正相关，你能把它们组成一个方差为零的资产组合吗？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. If two assets are perfectly positively correlated, can they form a zero-variance portfolio?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设资产A和B，σA为资产A的标准差，σB为资产B的标准差，ρAB=1。资产组合的方差为σ2P=xA2σ2A+xB2σ2B+2xAxBρABσAσB=[xAσA+(1-xA)σB]2,即σP=[xAσA+(1-xA)σB],令σP=0,可得xA=-σB/σA-σB,xB=σA/σA-σB。可见，当允许卖空的情况下，可以使得两项完全正相关的资产组合的方差为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the two assets be A and B, with standard deviations σA and σB and correlation ρAB = 1. The portfolio variance is written as σ2P=xA2σ2A+xB2σ2B+2xAxBρABσAσB=[xAσA+(1-xA)σB]2, so σP=[xAσA+(1-xA)σB]. Setting σP=0 gives xA=-σB/σA-σB and xB=σA/σA-σB. Thus, when short selling is permitted, the source concludes that two perfectly positively correlated assets can form a zero-variance portfolio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 10.    请分析资产的数量与资产组合风险的关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*10. Analyze the relationship between the number of assets and portfolio risk.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随着资产组合中资产数量的增加，当n趋于无穷大时，资产组合的风险就趋于零。也就是说，在资产完全不相关的情况下，资产组合的风险会随着资产数量的增加而消失。在现实生活中，资产完全不相关或完全负相关的情况不多，大部分处于不完全正相关状态。此时，资产组合的风险主要来自资产价格的共同运动。当n趋于无穷大时，资产之间的协方差就成了资产组合方差的决定因素，协方差是不能靠资产组合多元化来降低的。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As the number of assets in a portfolio increases and $n$ approaches infinity, portfolio risk approaches zero when the assets are entirely uncorrelated. In reality, perfectly uncorrelated or perfectly negatively correlated assets are uncommon; most assets are imperfectly positively correlated. Portfolio risk then comes mainly from their common price movements. As $n$ grows without bound, covariance between assets becomes the determining component of portfolio variance, and this common component cannot be eliminated through diversification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 11.什么是系统性风险和非系统性风险？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*11. What are systematic and unsystematic risk?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

答:非系统性风险是资产组合个别的风险，可以通过多样化消除。系统性风险又称市场风险，是不能通过多样化予以消除的部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Answer: unsystematic risk is asset-specific risk within a portfolio and can be eliminated through diversification. Systematic risk, also called market risk, is the component that diversification cannot eliminate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
