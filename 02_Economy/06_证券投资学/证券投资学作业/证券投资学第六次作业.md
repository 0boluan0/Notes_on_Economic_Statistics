# 一.名词解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Definition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 流动比率。流动比率是反映企业偿债能力的重要指标。企业能否偿还短期债务，要看它有多少债务以及有多少可用于变现偿债的资产。流动资产越多、短期债务越少，则企业的偿债能力具强。流动比率这一指标认为，对企业债务进行偿付的保障是企业的流动资产，如现金、存货、应收账款等。其计算公式为:流动比率=流动资产/流动负债。
2. 速动比率。虽然流动比率可以用来评价流动资产总体的变现能力，但利益相关人还希望获得比流动比率更进一步的有关变现能力的比率指标，常用速动比率来体现，又称酸性测试比率，速动比率是从流动资产中扣除存货部分，再除以流动负债的比值。速动比率的计算公式为:速动比率=（流动资产-存货）/流动负债。
3. 财务杠杆。财务杠杆是指企业利用债务融资来放大股东收益的能力。当企业使用债务融资时，股东只需投入部分资金（所有者权益），而通过负债获得额外资金用于经营，从而可能放大股东的回报。但如果企业经营不善，债务利息等固定成本也会放大股东的损失。财务杠杆=总资产/所有者权益。
4. 利息保障倍数。利息保障倍数是指企业息税前利润与利息费用的比率，用以衡量企业偿付借款利息的能力。利息保障倍数=息税前利润/利息费用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Current ratio.** The current ratio is an important measure of a firm's ability to meet its obligations. Its capacity to repay short-term debt depends on both the amount of debt and the assets that can be converted into cash for repayment. More current assets and fewer short-term liabilities imply stronger solvency. The ratio treats current assets—such as cash, inventory, and accounts receivable—as the resources available to cover liabilities. Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities.<br>
**2.** **Quick ratio.** Although the current ratio evaluates the overall liquidity of current assets, stakeholders often want a stricter measure. The quick ratio, also called the acid-test ratio, subtracts inventory from current assets before dividing by current liabilities. Quick ratio = (current assets − inventory) / current liabilities.<br>
**3.** **Financial leverage.** Financial leverage is a firm's use of debt financing to magnify returns to shareholders. Debt lets shareholders contribute only part of the funding through equity while the firm borrows additional funds for operations, which can amplify returns. If the firm performs poorly, however, fixed costs such as interest also magnify shareholder losses. Financial leverage = total assets / shareholders' equity.<br>
**4.** **Interest coverage ratio.** The interest coverage ratio is earnings before interest and taxes divided by interest expense. It measures the firm's ability to service the interest on its borrowings. Interest coverage ratio = EBIT / interest expense.<br>
