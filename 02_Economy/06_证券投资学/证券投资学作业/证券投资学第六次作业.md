# 一.名词解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Definition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 流动比率。流动比率是反映企业偿债能力的重要指标。企业能否偿还短期债务，要看它有多少债务以及有多少可用于变现偿债的资产。流动资产越多、短期债务越少，则企业的偿债能力具强。流动比率这一指标认为，对企业债务进行偿付的保障是企业的流动资产，如现金、存货、应收账款等。其计算公式为:流动比率=流动资产/流动负债。
2. 速动比率。虽然流动比率可以用来评价流动资产总体的变现能力，但利益相关人还希望获得比流动比率更进一步的有关变现能力的比率指标，常用速动比率来体现，又称酸性测试比率，速动比率是从流动资产中扣除存货部分，再除以流动负债的比值。速动比率的计算公式为:速动比率=（流动资产-存货）/流动负债。
3. 财务杠杆。财务杠杆是指企业利用债务融资来放大股东收益的能力。当企业使用债务融资时，股东只需投入部分资金（所有者权益），而通过负债获得额外资金用于经营，从而可能放大股东的回报。但如果企业经营不善，债务利息等固定成本也会放大股东的损失。财务杠杆=总资产/所有者权益。
4. 利息保障倍数。利息保障倍数是指企业息税前利润与利息费用的比率，用以衡量企业偿付借款利息的能力。利息保障倍数=息税前利润/利息费用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Current ratio.** The current ratio is an important measure of a firm's ability to meet its obligations. Its capacity to repay short-term debt depends on both the amount of debt and the assets that can be converted into cash for repayment. More current assets and fewer short-term liabilities imply stronger solvency. The ratio treats current assets—such as cash, inventory, and accounts receivable—as the resources available to cover liabilities. Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities.<br>
**2.** **Quick ratio.** Although the current ratio evaluates the overall liquidity of current assets, stakeholders often want a stricter measure. The quick ratio, also called the acid-test ratio, subtracts inventory from current assets before dividing by current liabilities. Quick ratio = (current assets − inventory) / current liabilities.<br>
**3.** **Financial leverage.** Financial leverage is a firm's use of debt financing to magnify returns to shareholders. Debt lets shareholders contribute only part of the funding through equity while the firm borrows additional funds for operations, which can amplify returns. If the firm performs poorly, however, fixed costs such as interest also magnify shareholder losses. Financial leverage = total assets / shareholders' equity.<br>
**4.** **Interest coverage ratio.** The interest coverage ratio is earnings before interest and taxes divided by interest expense. It measures the firm's ability to service the interest on its borrowings. Interest coverage ratio = EBIT / interest expense.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-hw:02:start -->
> [!note] 四个名词的条件
> [[流动比率]]是账面比较，不保证资产按时变现；[[速动比率]]须说明扣除哪些项目。A/E对应[[权益乘数]]这一杠杆度量，不等于EPS对EBIT的敏感系数DFL；[[利息保障倍数]]也不等于现金到账保障。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[流动比率|current ratio]] is not a timely-payment guarantee; define [[速动比率|quick-asset exclusions]]. A/E is the [[权益乘数|equity multiplier]], not DFL. [[利息保障倍数|Interest coverage]] does not establish timely cash receipts.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **杜邦分解的含义及对企业经营的意义：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **The Meaning of DuPont Analysis and Its Significance to Corporate Operations:**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

杜邦分解是将企业的净资产收益率（ROE）拆分成销售净利率、总资产周转率和权益乘数三个指标进行分析的方法，即$ROE = 销售净利率 \times 总资产周转率 \times 权益乘数$对企业经营的意义在于帮助企业明确盈利能力、运营效率和财务杠杆三方面对净资产收益率的贡献情况，便于管理者针对性地制定和调整经营战略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DuPont analysis decomposes a company's return on equity into net profit margin, total asset turnover, and the equity multiplier:
$ROE = \text{net profit margin} \times \text{total asset turnover} \times \text{equity multiplier}$.
For management, the decomposition reveals how profitability, operating efficiency, and financial leverage each contribute to ROE, making it easier to formulate and adjust business strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

2. **上市公司通过关联方交易操纵利润的方式包括：**
	通过关联方高价销售或低价购买商品或服务，人为提高或降低收入和成本。
	通过关联方之间的不公平交易，虚构交易或调整交易价格，增加收入或减少成本费用。
	关联方之间的资产转移或债务转移，以调节企业利润。
	在关联方之间实施利润输送，规避税收或虚增资产价值，操控财务报表利润表现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **Listed companies may manipulate profit through related-party transactions in several ways:**<br>
   Selling goods or services to related parties at inflated prices, or buying from them at artificially low prices, can distort revenue and costs.
   Fictitious transactions, unfair terms, or manipulated transfer prices can inflate revenue or suppress expenses.
   Assets or liabilities may be transferred among related parties to shift profit between entities or periods.
   Related parties may transfer profit, evade tax, or inflate asset values in order to alter the profit reported in the financial statements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**📘 杜邦分析体系（DuPont Analysis）**

杜邦分析将 **净资产收益率（ROE）** 拆解为三项因素：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DuPont analysis decomposes return on equity (ROE) into three factors:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

$$

\text{ROE} = \text{净利率} \times \text{总资产周转率} \times \text{权益乘数}

$$

  

即：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
That is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

$$

\text{ROE} = \left( \frac{\text{净利润}}{\text{营业总收入}} \right)

\times \left( \frac{\text{营业总收入}}{\text{总资产}} \right)

\times \left( \frac{\text{总资产}}{\text{净资产}} \right)

$$
