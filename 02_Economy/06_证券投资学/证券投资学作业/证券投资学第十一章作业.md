# 一.名词解释题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Definition questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1.资本市场线：资本市场线是在以预期收益和标准差为轴的坐标系中，表示风险资产 的有效组合与一种无风险资产(通常为国债或货币市场账户)再组合的有效率的组合线，或者 说是资本配置线自货币市场账户(或国债账户)通过市场资产组合的延伸线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Capital market line: in a coordinate system with expected return and standard deviation on its axes, the capital market line represents efficient combinations of an efficient risky portfolio with a risk-free asset, usually a government security or money-market account. Equivalently, it is the extension of the capital allocation line from the money-market or government-security account through the market portfolio.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

2.证券市场线：当资本市场处于均衡状态时，任何一种资产(包括风险资产或无风险资 产)的预期收益与其承担的与市场风险相关的值之间呈线性关系，我们把这一线性关系表示 在以预期收益和值为坐标轴的坐标平面上，就是一条以无风险收益率为起点的射线，这条射 线被称为证券市场线.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** Security market line: when the capital market is in equilibrium, the expected return on any asset, whether risky or risk-free, has a linear relationship with its exposure to market risk. Plotting expected return against that risk measure produces a ray beginning at the risk-free rate, called the security market line.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

***(1) Calculate the $\beta$ values***

$$
\beta_i =\dfrac{\operatorname{Cov}(R_i,R_m)}{\operatorname{Var}(R_m)}
=\dfrac{\rho_{im}\sigma_i\sigma_m}{\sigma_m^{2}}
=\rho_{im}\dfrac{\sigma_i}{\sigma_m}
$$
