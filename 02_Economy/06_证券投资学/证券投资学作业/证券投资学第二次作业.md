一.名词解释题.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
I. Definitions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一级市场:一级市场是通过发行股票进行筹资活动的市场, 方面为资本的需求者提供筹集资金的渠道,另一方面为资本的供应者提供投资场所发行市场是实现资本职能转化的场所,它通过发行股票,把社会闲散资金转化为生产资本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Primary market: This is the market in which companies raise funds by issuing shares. It gives firms and other users of capital a channel through which to obtain financing, while giving suppliers of capital a place to invest. By issuing shares, the primary market transforms otherwise idle funds in society into productive capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- sec-note:H01:start -->
> [!note] 一级市场的发行对象
> 上述原答以股票融资解释一级市场，但发行市场不只包括股票，也包括新发行债券等证券；应将“新发行取得资金”与“已发行证券的转手交易”区分。具体投资人取得的权利仍须分别做[[证券权利识别]]，不能将所有证券发行都当作股权融资。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The original answer explains the primary market through share issuance, but primary issuance also includes newly issued bonds and other securities. Distinguish financing through a new issue from trading an already-issued security. [[证券权利识别|Identify the investor's rights]] separately: not every securities issue is equity financing.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> [SEC：Corporate Bonds（债券发行融资）](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/bonds-or-fixed-income-products)。
<!-- sec-note:H01:end -->

二级市场:二级市场是有价证券的交易场所。二级市场是有价证券的流通市场,是已发行的有价证券进行买卖交易的场所。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Secondary market: This is the market in which securities that have already been issued are bought and sold. It therefore provides a venue for securities to circulate among investors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

除权价:在股票发生分红、送股、配股等权益分配行为后，经过调整计算得出的股票价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ex-rights price: The adjusted share price calculated after a dividend, bonus issue, rights issue, or another distribution of shareholder rights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

填权:股票除权后的除权价不一定等同于除权日的理论开盘价，当股票实际开盘价交易高于这一理论价格时的股票。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price recovery after ex-rights: The market price after a stock goes ex-rights need not equal its theoretical ex-rights opening price. When the stock trades above that theoretical benchmark, the price is said to be recovering after ex-rights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

贴权:在除权除息后的一段时间里，如果多数人不看好该股，交易市价低于除权（除息）基准价，即股价比除权除息前有所下降，则为**贴**权。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price decline after ex-rights: If investors remain pessimistic after a stock goes ex-rights or ex-dividend and its market price falls below the corresponding benchmark price, so that the adjusted share price is lower than before the distribution, the stock is said to have suffered a **post-ex-rights decline**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- sec-note:H02:start -->
> [!note] 除权基准与投资人财富
> 填权／贴权比较的是除权除息后的相应基准，不是简单比较除权前的名义股价，也不只限于开盘瞬间。例如忽略税费与其他变化，股价100派现金10、除息后价90，投资人仍持有90的股票与10的现金，不能仅凭股价下降就判断亏损。涉及送股或配股还须计入新股与额外出资，结合[[持有期收益率]]评估；原答所述市场情绪不是这种基准定义的必要条件。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Post-distribution recovery or decline is assessed against the relevant ex-rights or ex-dividend benchmark, not simply the pre-distribution nominal price, and not only at the opening instant. Ignoring taxes, fees and other changes, a share priced at 100 that pays a cash dividend of 10 and then trades at 90 leaves its holder with a 90 share and 10 in cash. The price drop alone is not a loss. Bonus or rights issues also require accounting for new shares and additional contributions when evaluating the [[持有期收益率|holding-period return]]. The sentiment described in the original answer is not necessary to define the benchmark comparison.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Specialist broker: A central participant on the exchange floor who performs several roles. A specialist may act as an agent for other brokers, trade directly to narrow the gap between bids and offers, serve as auctioneer for large trades, assess other brokers' bids and offers to establish a fair price, facilitate trading in the assigned area, and provide information to other brokers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

会员制证券交易所:会员制证券交易所是不以营利为目的,由会员自治自律、互相约束,参与经营的会员可以参加股票交易中的股票买卖与交割的交易所。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Member-owned stock exchange: A not-for-profit exchange governed through member self-management, self-discipline, and mutual oversight. Its participating members may trade and settle shares on the exchange.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公司制证券交易所:公司制证券交易所是以营利为目的,提供交易场所和服务人员,以便利证券商的交易与交割的证券交易所。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For-profit corporate stock exchange: A profit-seeking exchange that provides a trading venue and support staff so that securities dealers can trade and settle transactions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二.简答题:
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
II. Short-answer questions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 证券市场分为一级市场和二级市场.一级市场：融资活动，连接资 金需求和资金供给，闲散资金 转化为生产资本。 二级市场（交易市场）  交易，价格发现，资产配置，晴 雨表
2. 我国股票交易市场主要采用集合竞价与连续竞价相结合的交易机制。集合竞价是在交易日开盘前（9:15-9:25）和收盘前（14:57-15:00）通过集中买卖申报确定股票开盘价和收盘价的方式；连续竞价则在开盘后交易时段（9:30-11:30，13:00-14:57）实时撮合买卖双方的委托订单，按照价格优先、时间优先的原则，自动匹配成交，从而实现股票的连续交易与价格发现功能。
3. 沪深300指数属于价格指数，采用调整市值加权的计算方法。当成分股出现除权除息时，指数编制机构（中证指数有限公司）会对该股票的除权除息事件作出相应调整。具体做法是对成分股的价格和股本进行调整，使得指数保持连续性。因此，在正常情况下，成分股的除权除息不会直接导致沪深300指数发生大幅波动，但由于除权除息后股票交易价格降低，若市场投资者预期良好，买盘增强，则可能刺激股价上涨，间接推升指数。股指期货合约以沪深300指数作为标的，当指数因成分股除权除息而进行调整时，期货价格通常也会随之作出相应调整，以反映指数的变化和投资者的预期。然而，在除权除息期间，市场短期情绪可能出现波动，部分投资者可能对除权后股票价格变化预期不明朗，引发短期波动性增加，股指期货价格可能因此产生短期波动甚至偏离现货指数，进而出现基差（期货价格与现货指数差）的短暂扩大。但随着投资者逐渐消化除权除息信息，这种影响一般较快消退，基差回归正常水平。
4. 游资打板策略本身并不一定就是股价操纵，但在实际操作中，存在部分游资可能通过打板手法进行人为操纵股价的情形。可通过观察股票是否存在连续异常涨跌、虚假买卖盘和监管层的风险提示等特征进行辨别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** The securities market consists of a primary market and a secondary market. The primary market connects users and suppliers of capital, financing productive activity by transforming idle funds into productive capital. The secondary, or trading, market supports trading, price discovery, asset allocation, and the market's role as an economic barometer.<br>
**2.** China's stock market mainly combines call auctions with continuous auctions. Before the market opens, orders submitted from 9:15 to 9:25 are pooled to determine the opening price; orders submitted from 14:57 to 15:00 are pooled to determine the closing price. During continuous trading—9:30 to 11:30 and 13:00 to 14:57—buy and sell orders are matched in real time under price-priority and then time-priority rules. This mechanism enables continuous trading and price discovery.<br>
**3.** The CSI 300 is a price index calculated using adjusted free-float market-capitalisation weights. When a constituent goes ex-rights or ex-dividend, China Securities Index Company Limited adjusts the relevant price and share-count inputs so that the index series remains continuous. Such corporate actions therefore do not normally cause a large mechanical jump in the CSI 300. They may nevertheless affect investor expectations and trading, which can move the constituent's market price and hence the index indirectly. CSI 300 index-futures prices usually adjust with the underlying index and expectations. Short-lived uncertainty around an ex-rights or ex-dividend event can temporarily increase volatility and widen the basis—the difference between the futures price and the spot index—but the effect generally fades as the market absorbs the information.<br>
**4.** A short-term speculative strategy of buying stocks at their daily price limit is not necessarily market manipulation. In practice, however, some speculative funds may use limit-up trading tactics to influence prices artificially. Warning signs include repeated abnormal price movements, spoofed or false orders, and risk alerts issued by regulators.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 第3题：现金分红与送配股不能合并处理
> 中证2024《股票指数计算规则》PDF9／印刷6的§3.2.1明确区分：样本现金分红时，价格指数不修正除数、任其自然回落；全收益与净收益指数作相应调整。送股、配股、拆股或缩股则按规则修正相关指数除数。因此，原答“除权除息均调整而保持价格指数连续”的概括过强。股指[[期货合约]]价格还反映预期分红等持有成本与市场条件，不能从除息事件推出基差必然短暂扩大、随后快速恢复的确定路径；该题原有答案仍完整保留。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Section 3.2.1 of CSI's 2024 Stock Index Calculation Rules, PDF page 9 / printed page 6, distinguishes these events. Cash dividends do not trigger a divisor adjustment for a price index, allowing its natural decline; total-return and net-return indexes are adjusted. Bonus issues, rights issues, splits and reverse splits instead trigger the applicable divisor adjustments. The original answer therefore overgeneralizes when it treats all ex-rights and ex-dividend events as preserving price-index continuity. Stock-index [[期货合约|futures]] also reflect expected dividends and other carry and market conditions. An ex-dividend event alone does not imply a guaranteed temporary basis widening followed by a quick recovery. The original answer is preserved in full.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> [中证官方2024《股票指数计算规则》PDF9／印刷6](https://oss-ch.csindex.com.cn/contract/cms_add/20240726155028-%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%AD%E8%AF%81%E6%8C%87%E6%95%B0%E6%9C%89%E9%99%90%E5%85%AC%E5%8F%B8%E8%82%A1%E7%A5%A8%E6%8C%87%E6%95%B0%E8%AE%A1%E7%AE%97%E8%A7%84%E5%88%99%E3%80%8B.pdf#page=9)。
