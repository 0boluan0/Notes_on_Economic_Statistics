# 均值方差与资产配置
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mean–variance portfolio choice and capital allocation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选出两只“好股票”，还没有回答它们该各买多少。这一课从持仓核算开始，先问哪些组合值得考虑，再问怎样在收益和风险间取舍，最后加入无风险资产。给定收益分布后的组合选择，与市场如何形成均衡预期收益，是前后相接的两个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choosing two promising assets does not determine their weights. This lesson starts with position accounting, identifies worthwhile combinations, introduces preference-based choice, and then adds a risk-free asset. Choosing from a given return distribution comes before explaining equilibrium expected returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 把持仓变成一个风险收益点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From positions to a risk–return point*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先分清权重与收益：权重是期初投入比例，收益是随后才实现的随机结果。二者的结合给出一个组合；对这个组合求均值和方差，才得到图上的坐标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weights are initial allocations; returns are subsequently realized random outcomes. Combining them defines the portfolio return, whose mean and variance locate it on the diagram.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[投资组合]]

![[单期组合收益]]

![[组合均值与方差]]

贯穿本课的自拟例子使用同一期间、同一货币的两项资产：$\mu_A=8\%,\mu_B=12\%$，$\sigma_A=20\%,\sigma_B=30\%$，$\rho=0.25$，所以 $\sigma_{AB}=0.015$。这些是用来理解模型的假定总体输入，不是行情估计或投资建议。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The running example assumes two assets over one common horizon and currency: means $8\%,12\%$, volatilities $20\%,30\%$, and correlation $0.25$, giving covariance $0.015$. These are illustrative population inputs, not market estimates or investment advice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 分散化究竟省掉了什么风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What diversification actually reduces*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

组合的期望收益容易平均，波动却不能照做：某项资产下跌时，另一项是否也下跌，决定了两者的风险能否抵消。先把权重固定，单独观察相关性的作用，再看完全相关的端点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expected returns average linearly but volatilities do not. Joint movements determine cancellation. Hold weights fixed to isolate correlation, then examine its endpoints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[两资产分散化]]

![[完全相关的风险抵消]]

两资产的抵消逻辑可以推广，但“多买几个”不等于“共同风险也不见了”。下面把可分散残差与共同暴露明确分开；之后接 CAPM 时，还要另加均衡定价假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The logic extends beyond two assets, but adding names need not remove common exposure. Separate diversifiable residuals from common factors before adding CAPM's equilibrium assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[分散化与共同风险]]

## 3. 从可行组合中筛出值得考虑的部分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From feasible portfolios to worthwhile choices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

卖不卖空、能否借钱会改变可行范围；偏好则决定怎样评价范围内的组合。先讲清评价规则及其边界，再讨论被支配、最小方差与有效前沿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Shorting and borrowing rules determine feasibility; preferences evaluate the feasible choices. Establish the criterion and its limits before distinguishing dominance, minimum variance, and efficiency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[均值方差准则]]

![[均值方差偏好边界]]

![[投资组合可行集]]

![[均值方差支配]]

![[最小方差边界]]

![[均值方差有效前沿]]

“给定收益下最小方差”只比较收益相同的组合；“有效”还得检查其他收益水平有没有更好的选择。为准确找出这条分界，下一节先求全局最小方差点，再看它的上下两侧。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Minimum variance at a target mean compares portfolios with that same mean. Efficiency also checks alternatives at other means. The global minimum-variance point separates the two branches in the standard unconstrained model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 求出边界并识别有效上支
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Solving the boundary and identifying its efficient branch*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面的闭式解限定为：协方差正定，只要求权重和为 1，允许卖空；目标收益解还要求收益约束不与预算约束重复。先理解要优化的对象，再看逆矩阵公式如何从约束中出现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The closed forms assume positive-definite covariance, a budget constraint only, and unrestricted shorting. The target-mean formula additionally needs an independent mean constraint. Define the optimization object before applying its inverse-matrix solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[全局最小方差组合]]

![[全局最小方差解]]

![[目标收益最小方差解]]

![[最小方差边界的有效上支]]

在贯穿例子中，全局最小方差权重为 $(0.75,0.25)$，收益 $9\%$、波动约 $18.37\%$。等权组合收益 $10\%$、波动 $20\%$，位于有效上支；只持 A 的收益 $8\%$、波动也是 $20\%$，被等权组合压过。两者同在两资产的最小方差边界上，却只有一个有效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The running example's GMV weights are $(0.75,0.25)$ with mean $9\%$ and volatility about $18.37\%$. Equal weights give $(10\%,20\%)$ on the efficient branch; holding only A gives $(8\%,20\%)$ and is dominated. Both lie on the two-asset minimum-variance boundary, but only the former is efficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[双基金分离]]

## 5. 约束和估计误差会改变答案
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Constraints and input uncertainty change the answer*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

闭式答案出现负权重，并不意味着把负数删掉再归一化就是禁止卖空的最优解。改变约束要重新求解；即使数学上求解正确，用样本估计代替未知总体输入也会带来另一层不确定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deleting negative weights and renormalizing does not solve the long-only problem. Changed constraints require a new optimization; estimated rather than known inputs introduce a separate uncertainty even when the optimization is solved correctly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[禁止卖空的最小方差优化]]

![[组合优化的输入误差]]

## 6. 加入无风险资产，先画固定组合的配置线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Adding a risk-free asset to a fixed risky portfolio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在令同期间无风险收益 $r_f=2\%$。先暂时固定风险组合为上面的等权组合 P：你只能改变投给 P 的总比例，不能改变 P 内部 A、B 的比例。这一步得到的是资本配置线，还没有理由把它叫资本市场线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the same-horizon risk-free return be $2\%$. First fix the risky fund P at equal asset weights. Change only the amount allocated to P, not its internal composition. This produces a capital allocation line, not yet a capital market line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[无风险资产]]

![[资本配置线]]

![[Sharpe比率]]

P 的 Sharpe 为 $(0.10-0.02)/0.20=0.4$。将 $y=0.5$ 投给 P，其余持无风险资产，完整组合收益为 $6\%$、波动 $10\%$；若允许同利率借款，$y=1.5$ 给出收益 $14\%$、波动 $30\%$。杠杆沿这条线移动，不会凭空改变 P 的正 Sharpe。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
P has Sharpe $0.4$. Investing half in P gives mean $6\%$ and volatility $10\%$. Borrowing at the same rate to invest $1.5$ times net wealth gives $14\%$ and $30\%$. Leverage moves along the line without improving its positive Sharpe slope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[负Sharpe排序边界]]

## 7. 再优化风险组合本身
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Optimizing the risky fund itself*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

放开 P 内部的资产比例，才开始比较不同配置线。标准条件下，我们希望找到斜率最高的正 Sharpe 配置线及其切点；归一化公式的分母也必须检查，不能算出一个权重和为 1 的向量就宣布成功。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing the fund's internal weights lets us compare allocation lines. Under the standard assumptions, seek the largest positive Sharpe slope and its tangency fund. The normalization denominator must be checked; weights summing to one do not by themselves establish a valid positive tangency solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[切点组合]]

![[切点组合闭式解]]

![[切点组合存在边界]]

本例切点权重为 $(39/70,31/70)$，期望收益约 $9.7714\%$、波动约 $19.3570\%$，Sharpe 约 $0.401479$，略高于等权 P 的 $0.4$。接下来是另一个选择：对某位投资者，投多少给这个共同风险基金？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tangency weights are $(39/70,31/70)$, giving mean about $9.7714\%$, volatility $19.3570\%$, and Sharpe $0.401479$, slightly above P's $0.4$. The next question is how much a particular investor allocates to this risky fund.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[风险资产与无风险分离]]

![[借贷利率不等的配置边界]]

## 8. 从组合选择走到市场均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From portfolio choice to market equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到这里，$\mu,\Sigma$ 仍是给定输入。只有再加入[[CAPM假设|共同预期、共同交易机会与市场出清等假设]]，共同切点基金才被识别为理论市场组合 M；这时相应的有效配置射线称为资本市场线。不能把本课自拟的 A、B 切点直接称为真实市场。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So far, return moments are given. Only with [[CAPM假设|CAPM's shared beliefs, trading opportunities, and market clearing]] is the common tangency fund identified with the theoretical market portfolio M. The corresponding efficient ray is then the capital market line. The hypothetical A–B fund is not thereby an actual market portfolio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[资本市场线]]

![[资本市场线与证券市场线]]

## 9. 用同一组数字检查自己是否贯通
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Check the whole chain with the same inputs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 不看公式卡，解释这四件事
> 1. 为什么等权组合的波动是 $20\%$，而不是 $25\%$？
> 2. 为什么只持 A 也在最小方差边界，却不是有效组合？
> 3. 将 P 放大到 150% 持仓，有没有提高它的 Sharpe？
> 4. 为什么求出切点组合，还没有证明某只股票应该按 Beta 获得多少回报？
>
> **核对：** 协方差交叉项参与方差；预算与目标收益在两资产中确定唯一权重，但 A 被同波动更高收益的等权组合支配；同利率融资只是同比例放大正超额收益和波动；Beta 的均衡价格还需要 CAPM 的市场假设与出清。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> &nbsp;
> **1.** Why is the equal-weight portfolio's volatility $20\%$, rather than $25\%$?<br>
> **2.** Why does holding A alone lie on the minimum-variance boundary without being efficient?<br>
> **3.** Does increasing the position in P to $150\%$ of net wealth improve its Sharpe ratio?<br>
> **4.** Why does finding a tangency portfolio not yet establish the return a stock should earn for its beta?
>
> **Check:** Covariance contributes a cross term to portfolio variance. With two distinct asset means, the budget and target-mean constraints determine unique weights, but A is dominated by equal weights with the same volatility and a higher mean. Common-rate financing scales positive excess return and volatility together. Equilibrium beta pricing additionally requires CAPM's market assumptions and market clearing.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
