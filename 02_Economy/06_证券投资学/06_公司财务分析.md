### 1.1**影响会计数据质量的因素**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1 **Factors Affecting the Quality of Accounting Data*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **会计准则的缺陷** – 现行会计准则可能存在不足之处，例如对研发阶段费用的处理、金融资产公允价值计量等方面的规定不完善。
2. **预测的偏差** – 会计基于权责发生制，对未来情况的预测可能出现偏差（如研发费用资本化比例不当、应收账款坏账准备计提不足等）。
3. **管理层人为操纵** – 经理人员可能通过影响会计数据来达到自身目的：
	• 维护管理层个人利益
	• 满足在资本市场上筹资的条件
	• 满足借款合同中财务指标的要求
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Deficiencies in Accounting Standards** – Current accounting standards may have shortcomings, such as inadequate treatment of research and development (R&D) expenses and imprecise rules for measuring the fair value of financial assets.<br>
**2.** **Bias in Forecasts** – Accounting is based on accrual basis, which can lead to forecast biases (such as inappropriate capitalization rates for R&D expenses and insufficient provision for bad debts on accounts receivable).<br>
**3.** **Manipulation by Management** – Managers may manipulate accounting data to achieve their own objectives:<br>
   * To protect personal interests of management
   * To meet conditions required for raising capital in the capital market
   * To satisfy financial metrics stipulated in loan agreements
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


**2017年会计准则的主要变动**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Major Changes in the 2017 Accounting Standards**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **收入确认**：新收入准则以控制权转移替代风险报酬转移作为收入确认时点的标准，将原收入准则和建造合同准则统一为单一的收入确认模型【包括“在一段时间内”或“在某一时点”确认收入的指引】。对于包含多重交易安排的合同，在合同开始日需识别各项履约义务，并按照各履约义务的相对独立售价分摊交易价格，在履行每项履约义务时确认相应的收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Revenue Recognition**: The new revenue standard shifts from the criterion of risk and reward transfer to control transfer as the point at which revenue is recognized, unifying the original revenue standard and construction contract standard into a single model for recognizing revenue (including guidance on recognizing revenue either over time or at a point in time). For contracts involving multiple performance obligations, identification of each obligation should occur at contract inception, and the transaction price should be allocated to each obligation based on their relative standalone selling prices. Revenue should then be recognized as each obligation is fulfilled.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **金融资产处理**：新的金融工具准则与IFRS 9趋同。修订后的准则减少了金融资产分类，简化了嵌入衍生工具的会计处理，强化了金融工具减值的要求。新准则将金融资产分为三类：以摊余成本计量（如银行存款、贷款、应收账款、债权投资等）、以公允价值计量且其变动计入其他综合收益（如长期股权投资、债券投资等）、以公允价值计量且其变动计入当期损益（如交易性金融资产）【取消了原“贷款和应收款项”“持有至到期投资”“可供出售金融资产”等类别】。对于混合合同（主合同为金融资产），不再将嵌入衍生工具分拆，整体按照金融资产核算。在减值方面，由“已发生损失法”改为“预期信用损失法”，引入前瞻性信息评估减值损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Financial Asset Treatment**: The new financial instrument standards have converged with IFRS 9. The revised standards reduce the classification of financial assets, simplify the accounting treatment for embedded derivatives, and strengthen requirements for impairment of financial instruments. Under the new standards, financial assets are classified into three categories: measured at amortized cost (such as bank deposits, loans, accounts receivable, and held-to-maturity investments), measured at fair value through other comprehensive income (such as long-term equity investments and debt investments), and measured at fair value through profit or loss (such as trading financial assets). [The original categories "loans and receivables," "held-to-maturity investments," and "available-for-sale financial assets" have been eliminated.] For hybrid contracts (where the main contract is a financial asset), embedded derivatives will no longer be separated; they will be accounted for as a single financial asset. In terms of impairment, the "loss recognition approach" has been replaced by the "expected credit loss approach," and forward-looking information is used to assess impairment losses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:02:start -->
> [!note] 金融资产分类
> “长期股权投资＝FVOCI”不能当分类规则；需先辨工具、业务模式、合同现金流及适用选择。英文债权投资也不应译为已取消的分类名 held-to-maturity investments。详见[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/06_财务报表与公司诊断#先找到最重要的确认与计量判断|确认与计量判断]]及其官方来源。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Long-term equity investment does not automatically mean FVOCI. Identify the instrument, business model, cash flows, and applicable elections. The debt-investment category is not the former held-to-maturity category. See [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/06_财务报表与公司诊断#先找到最重要的确认与计量判断|recognition and measurement judgments]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2会计数据分析的步骤
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Steps in Accounting Data Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **第一步**：弄清哪些会计政策对企业财务影响最大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Step 1: Clarify which accounting policies have the greatest impact on a company's financials.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **第二步**：重点检查容易出现数据不真实的会计科目：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Step Two:** Focus on reviewing accounting subjects that are prone to inaccuracies in data:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **与销售额增加相关的应收账款的增加**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Increase in accounts receivable associated with higher sales**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

_目的_：增加本年度利润。
_手段_：年末大量确认销售收入，但与买方私下约定下一年退货。【上期销售退回会冲减本期销售收入】
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Objective:** To increase annual profits.
**Means:** Large revenue recognition at year-end, but privately agreeing to return goods next year. [Sales returns from previous sales will reduce current period's revenue.]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:03:start -->
> [!note] 退货与确认时点
> 次年退货须结合原合同退货权、控制转移与期后证据处理，不能统一认作冲减次年收入；年末交易也非天然虚假。查验程序见[[盈利质量核查]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Subsequent returns require contract, control-transfer, and later evidence, rather than universally reducing next-year revenue. Year-end sales are not inherently false; apply [[盈利质量核查|the evidence procedure]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:03:end -->

2. **报表利润与经营现金流量比例的异常变化**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **Abnormal Changes in Profit from Reports and Operating Cash Flow Ratios**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正常情况下，在会计政策无变动时，利润与经营现金流有相对稳定的比例关系。管理层可以通过改变费用摊销和计提方式影响报表利润，但无法同时影响经营活动产生的现金流。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under normal circumstances, there is a relatively stable ratio between profits and operating cash flow when accounting policies remain unchanged. Management can influence reported profits by altering the methods of amortization and accruals, but cannot simultaneously affect cash flows from operating activities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:04:start -->
> [!note] 利润与现金的差额
> CFO 受应收、存货、应付与交易时点影响；会计政策不变并不保证固定比例，现金流也可受交易安排影响。用[[现金营运指数]]追调节，不能直接把差额判为虚假利润。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Working capital and transaction timing affect CFO. Unchanged policies do not guarantee a fixed profit-to-cash ratio, and transactions can affect cash flows. Use [[现金营运指数|the cash operating index]] to investigate rather than declare fraud.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:04:end -->

3. **因处置长期资产而产生的巨额利润**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** **Profits Arising from the Disposal of Long-term Assets in Massive Amounts**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 债务重组，将应收账款转为长期股权投资以减少坏账损失
• 向关联方出售长期股权投资取得一次性收益
• 改变长期股权投资的核算方法（例如由权益法改为成本法确认投资收益）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Debt restructuring involves reclassifying accounts receivable as long-term equity investments to reduce bad debt losses.
• Selling long-term equity investments to related parties for a one-time gain.
• Changing the accounting method for long-term equity investments (e.g., switching from the equity method to cost method to recognize investment gains).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:05:start -->
> [!note] 处置与重组的条件
> 重组、出售和核算方法变动须满足各自条件；换科目或方法不自动消除损失，也不是可任选的增利办法。[[盈利质量]]区分真实报告与收益能否延续。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Restructuring, disposals, and method changes have specific requirements. Relabeling does not automatically remove losses or permit elective profit creation. [[盈利质量|Earnings quality]] distinguishes faithful reporting from persistence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:05:end -->

4. **中期报表与年度报表利润差异悬殊**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**4.** **Mid-year Reports vs Annual Reports: Significant Variations in Profits**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

_关注_：公司业绩在年度内大幅波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Noted: The company's performance has fluctuated significantly within the year.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

_分析思路_：如果中期不分红、不配股，中期报表通常无需审计。这可能给公司机会通过操纵中期利润来影响股价（与庄家合谋抬高或压低中期业绩）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Analysis Approach:** If no dividends are declared and no rights issue is undertaken, the mid-year report typically does not require an audit. This could give the company an opportunity to manipulate mid-year profits to influence stock prices (colluding with insiders to inflate or deflate mid-year performance).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:06:start -->
> [!note] 中报波动的证据边界
> 审计要求需按实际制度核对；未审计、季节波动或利润差异本身不能证明串谋，应查重大交易与年末调整。见[[盈利质量核查]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Verify the actual audit requirements. Unaudited status or seasonal profit differences do not establish collusion; examine transactions and adjustments through [[盈利质量核查|evidence-based checks]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:06:end -->

5. **关联交易带来的利润增加**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**5.** **Increased Profits from Related Party Transactions**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

_手段_：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Means:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 向关联方出售劳务，计入其他业务收入以增加利润
• 直接或间接由关联方承担本应由公司承担的费用：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Sell services to related parties, recording them under other operating income to boost profits
• Directly or indirectly bear expenses that should be borne by the company:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

A. 转让研发成果给关联方，由其支付费用
B. 以费用分担方式将本期费用（如保险费、广告费等）转移给关联方承担
C. 将资产或土地使用权出租给关联方获取收入
D. 向关联方借款融资以降低本期财务费用支出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A. Transfer R&D results to an associated party, with them bearing the cost

B. Transfer expenses (such as insurance fees and advertising costs) via cost-sharing arrangements to an associated party

C. Lease assets or land use rights to an associated party to generate income

D. Borrow funds from an associated party for financing to reduce the current interest expense
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:07:start -->
> [!note] 关联关系不等于操纵
> 服务、租赁、费用分担与融资可以真实发生；需查定价、实质、确认及合并抵销。见[[关联方交易]]与[[关联交易不等于利润操纵]]。上方英文的费用方向应为关联方承担本公司费用，而不是反过来。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Services, leases, allocations, and financing can be genuine. Check pricing, substance, recognition, and consolidation; see [[关联方交易|related transactions]] and [[关联交易不等于利润操纵|their inference boundary]]. The intended expense direction is the related party bearing this company's expenses.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:07:end -->

6. **利用会计政策或会计估计的变更调整利润**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**6.** **Adjusting profits through changes in accounting policies or estimates**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 选择是否计提资产减值准备来调节利润（如对存货跌价、投资减值准备的计提或转回）
• 改变折旧相关的会计估计（如固定资产使用年限、净残值、折旧方法）
• 改变存货计价方法（如先进先出法、加权平均法、移动平均法、后进先出法、个别计价法）以影响当期成本和利润
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Deciding whether to accrue for asset impairment to adjust profits (such as provisions or reversals for inventory write-downs, investment impairment provisions)
• Changing accounting estimates related to depreciation (such as useful lives of fixed assets, salvage values, and depreciation methods)
• Changing inventory valuation methods (such as first-in, first-out, weighted average, moving average method, last-in, first-out, and individual costing) to impact cost and profit in the current period
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:08:start -->
> [!note] 政策与估计受规则约束
> 减值不是可随意决定计不计的项目；方法选择、估计变更与转回须核对准则。该清单也不表示中国或 IFRS 企业可以自由选择 LIFO；见[[盈利质量核查]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Impairment is not freely elective. Methods, estimates, and reversals require the applicable rules; this list does not authorize LIFO for Chinese or IFRS reporters. See [[盈利质量核查|the review procedure]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:08:end -->

7. **利用其他应收款科目回避费用计提**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**7.** **Using Other Receivables Account to Avoid Expense Accrual**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

_手段_：将应收账款转列为其他应收款（变相形成一项短期融资）。
_目的_：减少应收账款余额和坏账准备计提，从而降低当期损益表中的费用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Means: Convert accounts receivable to other receivables (effectively creating a short-term financing arrangement).
Purpose: Reduce the balance of accounts receivable and bad debt provisions, thereby lowering expenses on the income statement for the current period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:09:start -->
> [!note] 重分类不消除回收风险
> 应收转成其他应收或投资后仍需按权利实质和可回收性评估，不能靠名称变化免去损失；见[[盈利质量核查]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Reclassification does not remove recoverability requirements. Assess the rights and losses rather than the account label; see [[盈利质量核查|earnings-quality checks]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:09:end -->

8. **推迟费用确认入账**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**8.** **Postpone Expense Recognition for Accounting Entry**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 将应计入本期的费用挂账在“待处理财产损益”科目，延后确认
• 将本期费用挂在“待摊费用”科目，在以后期间分摊
• 已发生的费用记入“预提费用”的借方，推迟结转入损益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Post the expenses that should be included in this period to the “Accrued Properties and Losses” account as a suspense account, deferring their recognition.
• Post the expenses of this period to the “Prepaid Expenses” account, to be amortized over future periods.
• Record the incurred expenses in the debit side of the “Accrued Expenses” account, deferring their entry into profit and loss for later periods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

9. **利用偶发性的其他收入增加利润总额**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**9.** **Utilize other incidental income to increase total profit**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 争取地方政府补贴计入营业外收入
• 利用营业外收入科目确认非常规收益，抬高利润总额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
·Strive for local government subsidies to be included in non-operating income
·Using non-operating income to recognize unconventional income and inflate total profits
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:10:start -->
> [!note] 偶发收益与真实性
> 正常补贴可以准确入账，但不能自动作为持续主业利润外推；有补贴也不等于造假。见[[盈利质量]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Subsidies can be faithfully reported without establishing recurring operating earnings; their presence does not prove fraud. See [[盈利质量|earnings quality]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:10:end -->

### **第三步**：对不真实的会计数据进行“修复”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Step 3:** “Repair” accounting data that do not faithfully represent the underlying economics.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

_“修复”重点_：关注财务报表附注和现金流量表揭示的信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
"Recovery Focus": Emphasize the information revealed in the notes to the financial statements and cash-flow statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

_判断数据被操纵的线索_：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Clues Indicating Data Manipulation:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 应收账款与其他应收款的增减变动，以及坏账准备计提是否合理。
• 应收账款与长期投资的增减变动，以及坏账准备计提是否异常。
• 将“待摊费用”和“待处理财产损失”科目的余额从利润中扣除，评估对利润的影响。
• 比较短期借款、其他应付款与财务费用之间的关系，分析利息支出与财务费用的匹配情况。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Changes in accounts receivable and other receivables, as well as the reasonableness of bad debt provisions.
* Changes in accounts receivable and long-term investments, and whether bad debt provisions are abnormal.
* Deducting balances from the "Accrued Expenses" and "Loss on Property Held for Sale" accounts from profits to assess their impact on profit.
* Comparing short-term borrowings, other payables, and financial expenses to analyze the matching of interest expenses and financial expenses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:11:start -->
> [!note] 调整须逐项核实
> 不能将全部预付、待摊或待处理余额机械从利润扣除；先查资产确认、归属期间和是否已经确认损失。[[财务报表附注]]提供具体依据。课件 PDF500–502 三案例仍是课堂材料，本轮未独立核验监管原决定；说明见[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/06_财务报表与公司诊断#一、先问会计数字能支持什么判断|会计信息核查]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Do not automatically deduct all prepaid or suspense balances. Establish recognition, period, and prior loss treatment using [[财务报表附注|the notes]]. Cases on PDF500–502 remain course accounts, not independently verified regulatory decisions; see [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/06_财务报表与公司诊断#一、先问会计数字能支持什么判断|the evidence discussion]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2.财务报表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Financial Statements*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
  
**对财务报表的总体认识**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Overview of Financial Statements**:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 财务报表是依据一定的会计准则或会计原则编制的，不同国家的具体标准可能不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

## 2.2**资产负债表**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2 Balance sheet*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产负债表是反映企业在某一特定日期财务状况的会计报表，它表明企业在该日期所拥有或控制的经济资源、所承担的现有义务，以及所有者对净资产的要求权。资产负债表遵循会计恒等式：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A balance sheet is an accounting statement of a company's financial position at a specific date. It reports the economic resources the company owns or controls, its existing obligations, and the owners' claims on net assets. The balance sheet follows the accounting equation:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$资产 = 负债 + 所有者权益$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**资产的构成**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Composition of Assets**:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **流动资产**：短期内可以变现或运用的资产，例如现金和银行存款、短期有价证券、应收账款、原材料和库存商品等。
• **长期投资**：对其他公司股票、债券等有价证券的投资，以及其他长期投资。
• **固定资产**：指单位价值高、使用期限长、能够在生产经营中反复使用并具有实物形态的资产（如机器设备、厂房等），在资产负债表中通常按原始成本列示，并配以累计折旧备抵。
• **无形资产**：没有实物形态但具有价值的长期资产，如专利权、版权、商标权等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Flow Assets**: Short-term assets that can be converted or utilized, such as cash and bank deposits, short-term securities, accounts receivable, raw materials, and finished goods.
• **Long-term Investments**: Investments in stocks and bonds of other companies, as well as other long-term investments.
• **Fixed Assets**: Assets with high unit value, long usage periods, and capable of being repeatedly used in production and operations (such as machinery and equipment, buildings), which are typically listed at original cost in balance sheet and offset by accumulated depreciation.
• **Intangible Assets**: Long-term assets with value but no physical form, such as patents, copyrights, and trademarks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**负债及所有者权益的构成**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Composition of Liabilities and Owner's Equity**:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **流动负债**：短期内（通常一年内）需要偿付的债务，如应付账款、应付票据、短期借款、应交税金等。
• **长期负债**：偿还期在一年以上的债务，如长期借款、应付长期票据、公司发行的长期债券等。
• **所有者权益**：所有者投入的资本和留存于企业的收益，包括股本、资本公积、盈余公积和未分配利润（留存收益）等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Flow Liabilities**: Short-term debts that need to be settled within a year, such as accounts payable, short-term notes payable, short-term loans, and taxes payable.
• **Long-term Liabilities**: Debts with repayment periods exceeding one year, including long-term loans, notes payable due in more than one year, and long-term bonds issued by the company.
• **Owners' Equity**: Capital invested by owners and retained earnings of the company, comprising share capital, capital reserve, surplus reserve, and undistributed profits (retained earnings).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2**损益表**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2 **Income Statement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] **损益表**（利润表）
>反映企业在一定会计期间的经营成果，展示利润或亏损的形成过程。
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Profit and Loss Statement** (income statement)
>Reflects the operating results of a company over a specified accounting period, displaying the process of profit or loss formation.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **揭示的信息**：企业某一时期的经营业绩（净利润或净亏损）及其形成过程。简而言之，损益表提供该期间收入、成本费用和利润的情况。
• **编制原理**：损益表遵循收支配比原则，以“收入-费用=利润”为基本公式，即：
$$销售收入 - 成本 = 利润$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Revealed Information**: The operating performance of a company over a certain period (net profit or net loss) and its formation process. In essence, the income statement provides information about the revenue, cost expenses, and profit during that period.
• **Principles of Compilation**: The income statement adheres to the principle of matching revenues and expenses, using the basic formula "revenue - expenses = profit," which is:
$$Sales Revenue - Cost = Profit$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **理解报表需注意的问题**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* **Issues to Consider When Understanding Reports**:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• ==报表上的利润不等于同期的现金流量==（因为采用权责发生制核算，存在应收应付等）。
• 不同的会计政策选择会导致利润结果不同（例如存货计价方法、折旧方法的不同）。
• 会计利润与经济利润并不相同（会计利润未考虑机会成本等经济因素）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• ==Accounting profit is not the same as cash flow for the same period== because accrual accounting recognizes receivables, payables, and other non-cash timing differences.
• Different accounting-policy choices produce different reported profit—for example, inventory-valuation and depreciation methods.
• Accounting profit is not economic profit because it omits economic factors such as opportunity cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3**现金流量表**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3**cash-flow statement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**现金流量表**反映企业在一定期间的现金流入和流出情况，被视为企业偿付能力和财务弹性的“显示器”，能够揭示企业现金来源和用途。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**cash-flow statement** Reflects the cash inflow and outflow of an enterprise over a certain period of time and is regarded as a "display" of the solvency and financial elasticity of the enterprise, which can reveal the source and use of cash of the enterprise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **揭示的信息**：通过对企业一定期间现金及现金等价物的增减变动进行分析，说明企业资金的来龙去脉，反映企业获取现金和支出现金的能力。简单来说，现金流量表追踪“钱从哪里来，又用了到哪里去”。
• **编制原理**：现金流量表编制有直接法和间接法两种：
• **直接法**：根据现金收支的主要类别列示企业经营活动的现金流量，通常以损益表中的营业收入为起点，直接计算各项现金收入和支出，公式为：$$\text{现金流入} - \text{现金流出} = \text{现金净增加额}$$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Revealed Information**: By analyzing the changes in cash and cash equivalents over a certain period of time, this analysis elucidates the origins and destinations of an enterprise's funds, reflecting its ability to generate and disburse cash. In essence, cash-flow statement tracks "where the money comes from and where it goes."

• **Principles of Compilation**: cash-flow statement is compiled using two methods: direct method and indirect method.

• **Direct Method**: The cash flow from operating activities is listed according to major categories of cash receipts and payments. Typically, starting from revenue on the income statement, the direct calculation of cash inflows and outflows is performed, as shown by the formula: $$\text{Cash inflow} - \text{Cash outflow} = \text{Net increase in cash}.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **间接法**：以当期净利润为起点，调整不涉及现金的收入和费用项目，以及营运资本的变动，推导出经营活动的现金流量。例如，将折旧、摊销等非现金支出加回净利润，并调整应收应付等营运项目的增减，以得到经营活动产生的现金净额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Indirect Method: Starting from net income in the current period, adjustments are made to non-cash revenue and expense items, as well as changes in working capital, to derive cash flow from operating activities. For example, adding back depreciation, amortization, and other non-cash expenses to net income, and adjusting for increases and decreases in accounts receivable and payable, to arrive at the net cash generated from operating activities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**现金流量的三大活动分析**：现金流量表将现金流分为经营活动、投资活动和筹资活动三部分，各部分现金流量反映企业不同方面的财务状况：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Analysis of the Three Activities of Cash Flows: cash-flow statement categorizes cash flows into operating activities, investing activities, and financing activities. Each part of the cash flow reflects different aspects of a company's financial condition:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **经营活动**：重点分析企业在不依赖外部融资的情况下，凭借自身经营产生的现金流量是否足以偿还到期债务、支付股利以及进行必要的对外投资。经营活动现金流能够说明企业日常经营的造血能力。
• **投资活动**：分析企业为获取未来收益而进行的资源投入（现金流出）以及由过去的投资所带来的现金收回（现金流入）。投资活动现金流反映企业扩张、更新资产及对外投资的力度，以及这些投资的回报情况。
• **筹资活动**：分析企业通过向所有者和债权人筹集资金所产生的现金流入，以及因偿还债务、分配股利等产生的现金流出。筹资活动现金流可以帮助判断企业未来需要偿还的现金流出义务，以及为获得这些资金所付出的代价大小（如利息成本、股利）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Operating Activities**: Focuses on analyzing whether a company's cash flows from its own operations are sufficient to repay maturing debts, pay dividends, and make necessary investments externally. Cash flow from operating activities indicates a company's ability to generate "blood" for its daily operations.
• **Investing Activities**: Analyzes the cash outflows resulting from the resources invested by a company to obtain future earnings, as well as the cash inflows generated by past investments. Cash flow from investing activities reflects the intensity of a company's expansion, updating assets, and external investments, as well as the returns from these investments.
• **Financing Activities**: Analyzes the cash inflows generated by a company through raising funds from owners and creditors, as well as the cash outflows resulting from debt repayment and dividend distributions.Cash flows from financing activities can help assess the future cash outflows that a company will need to meet, as well as the cost associated with obtaining these funds (such as interest costs and dividends).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:17:start -->
> [!note] 现金流的调节范围
> 直接法列主要现金收付，不必以营业收入为唯一起点；间接法还须排除非经营项目。现金余额变化可能另含汇率影响，范围须与附注对齐。见[[现金流量表]]与[[财务报表勾稽]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Direct presentation reports major receipts and payments without requiring revenue as the sole starting point. Indirect reconciliation also removes non-operating items. Exchange effects and cash scope require reconciliation; see [[现金流量表|cash flows]] and [[财务报表勾稽|statement reconciliation]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3.**杜邦分析**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. **DuPont Analysis**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

杜邦分析法通过对财务比率的分解，将综合性的盈利能力指标拆解为若干要素指标，以帮助深入分析企业业绩和财务状况。具体包括以下几个方面：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DuPont analysis through the decomposition of financial ratios breaks down comprehensive profitability indicators into several component indicators to facilitate a deeper analysis of corporate performance and financial conditions. Specifically, this includes the following aspects:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **3.1分析的思路**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1 Approach to Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

杜邦分析从股东（所有者）的角度出发，将综合性最强的盈利能力指标——[[权益净利率|权益净利率]]（即净资产收益率，通常记作$ROE$）进行分解。通过分解$ROE$，可以深入了解公司经营业绩的来源，并将各组成部分与历史数据或其他公司进行比较。这一方法提供了分析指标变化原因和趋势的途径，为今后制定改进措施指明方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DuPont analysis From the perspective of shareholders (owners), we break down the most comprehensive profitability metric—[[权益净利率|Net Operating Return on Equity]] (return on equity, commonly denoted as $ROE$)—to gain deeper insights into the sources of a company's performance. By decomposing $ROE$, we can understand the components that contribute to a company's operational results and compare these components with historical data or those of other companies. This approach provides a pathway to analyze the reasons and trends behind changes in the indicator, guiding future improvement strategies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **3.2指标分解原理**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2 Principle of Indicator Decomposition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

杜邦分析通常将权益净利率分解为三个部分：[[营业净利率|销售净利率]]、资产周转率和权益乘数，各部分侧重反映企业经营的不同方面：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DuPont analysis Typically, return on equity (ROE) is decomposed into three parts: asset turnover ratio, equity multiplier, and operating performance aspects of the enterprise, each highlighting different facets of corporate operations:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **销售净利率**（净利润 / 销售收入）反映企业的盈利能力（销售获得利润的高低）。==企业经营管理状况==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **[[营业净利率|Net Profit Margin]]** (Net Profit / Revenue) reflects a company's profitability (the extent to which sales generate profits). ==Business Management Condition==<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

2. **资产周转率**（销售收入 / 资产总额）反映资产的使用效率，可进一步细分为存货周转率、应收账款周转率等具体指标，以分析资产各组成部分的运转效率和可能存在的库存积压或应收账款回收问题。也可以检查资产构成是否合理。==企业资产管理状况==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **Asset Turnover Ratio** (sales revenue / total assets) reflects the efficiency of asset utilization. It can be further broken down into inventory turnover ratio, accounts receivable turnover ratio, among others, to analyze the operational efficiency of each component of assets and potential issues such as inventory buildup or problems with accounts receivable collection. It can also be used to check if the composition of assets is reasonable. ==Asset Management Status of Enterprises==<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

3. **[[权益乘数|权益乘数]]**（资产总额 / 所有者权益）反映财务杠杆程度（负债融资程度），权益乘数越高表明负债比率越高、财务风险越大。（此处计算资产负债率或权益乘数时，通常采用资产和负债的年初年末平均值。）==企业债务管理状况==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** **[[权益乘数|Equity Multiplier]]** (Total Assets / Shareholders' Equity) reflects the degree of financial leverage (degree of debt financing), with a higher equity multiplier indicating a higher debt ratio and greater financial risk. (When calculating the debt-to-equity ratio or equity multiplier, the average values for assets and liabilities at the beginning and end of the year are typically used.) ==Financial Leverage Management Status of Enterprises==<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将上述三者相乘，即可重新表示权益净利率：
$$ \text{权益净利率} = \text{销售净利率} \times \text{资产周转率} \times \text{权益乘数} $$
即：
$$ \frac{\text{净利润}}{\text{销售收入}} \times \frac{\text{销售收入}}{\text{资产总额}} \times \frac{\text{资产总额}}{\text{所有者权益}} = \frac{\text{净利润}}{\text{所有者权益}} $$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying the three components gives another expression for [[权益净利率|return on equity]]:
$$ \text{Return on equity} = \text{Net profit margin} \times \text{Total asset turnover} \times \text{Equity multiplier} $$
That is:
$$ \frac{\text{Net profit}}{\text{Sales revenue}} \times \frac{\text{Sales revenue}}{\text{Total assets}} \times \frac{\text{Total assets}}{\text{Shareholders' equity}} = \frac{\text{Net profit}}{\text{Shareholders' equity}} $$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述公式表明：**权益净利率（[[权益净利率|ROE]]）**取决于销售净利率（利润率）、资产周转率（效率）和权益乘数（杠杆）这三方面的共同作用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As the formula indicates: **Net Operating Return on Equity (ROE)** depends on the combined effect of three aspects: net profit margin (profitability), asset turnover ratio (efficiency), and equity multiplier (leverage).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:18:start -->
> [!note] 杜邦抵消条件
> 此处英文为 Return on Equity，不是 Net Operating Return on Equity。三因素为净利率、周转率、权益乘数；两处资产必须采用同一存量，利润与权益归属也须匹配，才能精确抵消。见[[杜邦分解]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The term is Return on Equity. Net margin, turnover, and the multiplier cancel exactly only with matched asset measures and profit/equity attribution. See [[杜邦分解|DuPont decomposition]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:18:end -->

## **3.3杜邦财务分析体系的应用**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3 Application of the DuPont Financial Analysis System*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **指标体系作用**：杜邦分析为公司管理层提供了一张透视公司经营管理效率和股东投资回报的路线图。其中，销售净利率主要反映企业经营获利能力（盈利管理状况），资产周转率反映资产管理效率，权益乘数反映债务融资状况。这三项指标分别对应企业的收益管理、资产管理和负债管理状况，它们共同决定了企业的权益报酬率水平。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Indicator System Role**: DuPont analysis provides a roadmap for company management to view the efficiency of their operations and shareholders' investment returns. Specifically, the net profit margin primarily reflects the profitability of the enterprise (profit management), while the asset turnover ratio measures asset management efficiency. The equity multiplier indicates the debt-finance situation. These three indicators correspond to the management status of revenue, assets, and liabilities within the company, collectively determining the level of return on equity.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

2. **提高业绩的具体路径**：根据杜邦分解结果，管理层可以有针对性地改善公司的财务表现：
	• **提高销售净利率**：通过提高销售收入增长率高于成本费用增长率，或降低各种成本和费用，以获取更高的销售利润率。
	• **提高总资产周转率**：加强对资产的管理和运用效率，例如加快存货周转、加强应收账款回收等，以较少的资产支撑更多的销售。
	• **在可控风险范围内提高财务杠杆**：在不危及公司财务安全的前提下，适度增加负债融资规模，以提高权益乘数，从而放大股东收益（需权衡增加的风险）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **Specific Paths to Improve Performance**: According to the DuPont analysis results, management can targetably improve the company’s financial performance:<br>
   - **Boost Net Profit Margin on Sales**: By increasing the growth rate of sales revenue higher than that of cost expenses, or by reducing various costs and expenses, to achieve a higher profit margin.
   - **Increase Asset Turnover Ratio**: By managing and utilizing assets more efficiently, such as speeding up inventory turnover and strengthening accounts receivable collection, to support more sales with fewer assets (subject to risk management).
   - **Increase Financial Leverage within Controllable Risk Limits**: Within the limits that do not jeopardize the company’s financial safety, moderately increase the scale of debt financing to enhance the equity multiplier, thereby amplifying shareholder returns (balancing increased risks).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

3. **指标变动原因和趋势分析**：杜邦分析可以用于拆解公司业绩变动的原因并分析趋势，为管理决策提供依据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** **Analysis of Changes in Indicators and Trends**: DuPont analysis can be used to decompose the reasons for changes in company performance and analyze trends, providing basis for management decisions.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:19:start -->
> [!note] 分解不是独立旋钮
> 加债可能改变利息、净利润和风险，成本削减也可能改变销量；提高乘数不单独证明经营改善或创造价值。见[[ROE与杠杆的解释边界]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Borrowing may change interest, profit, and risk, while costs may affect sales. Raising the multiplier alone proves neither operating improvement nor value creation. See [[ROE与杠杆的解释边界|the leverage boundary]].

# 4.财务报表的分析方法 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Methods for Analyzing Financial Statements*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财务报表分析常用的方法包括对比分析法、因素分析法和趋势分析法等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common methods include comparative analysis, factor analysis, and trend analysis, among others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **4.1对比分析法**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1 Comparative Analysis Method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对比分析法是利用比较基准对财务数据进行横向或纵向比较，从中发现差异的一种方法。对比分析既可以使用**绝对数**比较，也可以使用**相对数**（百分比）比较：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The comparative analysis method involves comparing financial data horizontally or vertically against a benchmark to identify differences. Comparative analysis can use absolute numbers for comparison or relative numbers (percentages) for comparison:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **绝对数比较**：直接比较相关项目的绝对金额差异，以发现增减变化。例如将本期与上期、实际数与预算数进行对比，找出差额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Absolute Number Comparison**: Directly compare the differences in absolute amounts of related projects to identify changes in increases and decreases. For example, compare figures from this period with those from the previous period or actual figures with budgeted figures to find the discrepancies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **相对数比较**：通过计算增长率、完成率等百分比指标来比较差异。例如计算销售收入同比增长百分比，或实际业绩对预算的完成百分比，以分析业务增长和目标达成情况。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Relative Number Comparison**: By calculating percentage indicators such as growth rates and completion rates, differences can be compared. For example, computing the year-over-year growth percentage of sales revenue, or the percentage of actual performance against budget to analyze business growth and achievement of targets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2**因素分析法**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2 Factor Analysis Method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因素分析法通过将一个综合指标分解为若干个因素指标，定量评估各因素对该指标的影响程度。这种方法一方面可以全面分析各因素对某一财务指标的综合影响，另一方面也可以分别衡量某一单一因素变化对该指标的影响大小。杜邦分析法实际上就是因素分析法在财务比率分析中的典型应用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Factor analysis involves breaking down a comprehensive indicator into several factor indicators to quantitatively assess the impact of each factor on the overall indicator. This method allows for a comprehensive analysis of how each factor influences a particular financial metric, as well as the ability to measure the magnitude of influence that a single factor change has on the metric. DuPont analysis refers to a typical application of factor analysis in financial ratio analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，假设某财务指标$P$由三大因素$A、B、C$相乘构成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, suppose a financial indicator $P$ is the product of three factors, $A$, $B$, and $C$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际指标：$P_0 = A_0 \times B_0 \times C_0$
基准指标：$P_s = A_s \times B_s \times C_s$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Actual Indicators: $P_0 = A_0 \times B_0 \times C_0$
Benchmark Indicators: $P_s = A_s \times B_s \times C_s$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

则实际值与基准值的差异$\Delta P = P_0 - P_s$可以分解为各因素变化的影响之和：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The difference between the actual and benchmark values, $\Delta P = P_0 - P_s$, can then be decomposed into the sum of the effects of the individual factors:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 因素$A$变动的影响：$(A_0 - A_s) \times B_s \times C_s$
• 因素$B$变动的影响：$A_0 \times (B_0 - B_s) \times C_s$
• 因素$C$变动的影响：$A_0 \times B_0 \times (C_0 - C_s)$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• The impact of factor $A$ changing: $(A_0 - A_s) \times B_s \times C_s$
• The impact of factor $B$ changing: $A_0 \times (B_0 - B_s) \times C_s$
• The impact of factor $C$ changing: $A_0 \times B_0 \times (C_0 - C_s)$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将以上三项相加，正好等于总差异$\Delta P$。通过这种分解，可以明确每一因素对综合指标差异的贡献度，便于针对性地分析和改进。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding the three effects gives the total difference, $\Delta P$. The decomposition identifies each factor's contribution to the difference in the composite indicator, supporting targeted diagnosis and improvement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:20:start -->
> [!note] 贡献依赖替代顺序
> 上式按 A→B→C 分摊；换顺序通常改变单项贡献，总和仍等于总差。这是[[连环替代法]]而非因果识别；见[[连环替代顺序边界]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These formulas use A→B→C order. Reordering generally changes individual effects, not their sum. This is [[连环替代法|chain substitution]], not causal identification; see [[连环替代顺序边界|order dependence]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:20:end -->

## 4.3**趋势分析法**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3 **Trend Analysis Method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

趋势分析法是通过比较企业连续多个时期的财务数据，观察各项财务指标随时间的变化趋势，从而判断公司财务状况和经营成果的发展方向。通常可以编制比较资产负债表、比较利润表和比较现金流量表，将若干期的同项指标数据并列，对其变动幅度进行分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trend analysis involves comparing a company's financial data over multiple consecutive periods to observe the changes in various financial metrics over time, thereby assessing the direction of its financial health and operational performance. Typically, comparative balance sheet, comparative income statement, and comparative cash-flow statement can be prepared to juxtapose data for several periods of the same metric and analyze their magnitude of change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

趋势分析既可以采用**绝对数趋势分析**，也可以采用**相对数趋势分析**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trend analysis can employ either **absolute number trend analysis** or **relative number trend analysis**:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **绝对数趋势分析**：比较各期同一指标的绝对值变化，如逐年销售收入增加或减少的金额。
• **相对数趋势分析**：比较各期同一指标的变动率，如销售收入同比增长率，或以某一年为基准计算指数，来观察长期趋势。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Absolute Number Trend Analysis**: Comparing changes in the absolute values of the same indicator across periods, such as the amount by which annual sales revenue increases or decreases.
• **Relative Number Trend Analysis**: Comparing changes in the rate of the same indicator across periods, such as year-over-year growth rates in sales revenue, or calculating indices relative to a base year to observe long-term trends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==前一期是环比,固定期是定基==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
== The previous period is ring comparison, and the fixed period is fixed base ==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通过趋势分析，可以识别企业业绩是持续上升、下降还是波动，以及增长是否稳定，为预测未来趋势提供依据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Through trend analysis, we can identify whether a company's performance is consistently increasing, decreasing, or fluctuating, and whether this growth is stable, providing basis for forecasting future trends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:21:start -->
> [!note] 指数与增长率
> 本期／前期为环比指数，本期／固定基期为定基指数；增长率还要减1，120%指数对应20%增长。英文用 period-on-period、fixed-base，而不是 ring comparison。例子见[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/06_财务报表与公司诊断#三、比较之前固定口径，再学习怎样分解差额|比较与分解]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Period-on-period and fixed-base indexes divide by their selected base. Subtract one for growth: an index of 120% means growth of 20%. See [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/06_财务报表与公司诊断#三、比较之前固定口径，再学习怎样分解差额|comparison examples]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 5.[[财务比率|财务比率分析]] 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.[[财务比率|Financial Ratio Analysis]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财务比率分析是通过计算财务报表中各有关项目的比值，来评价企业财务状况和经营成果的方法。本节将介绍财务比率分析的一般概念和主要类别，包括流动性分析、运营效率分析、负债比率分析、盈利能力分析、投资收益分析和现金流量分析等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial ratio analysis is a method of evaluating a company's financial condition and operational results by calculating the ratios between relevant items in the financial statements. This section will introduce the general concepts and main categories of financial ratio analysis, including liquidity analysis, operational efficiency analysis, leverage ratio analysis, profitability analysis, investment return analysis, and cash flow analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **5.1财务比率分析方法综述**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.1 Overview of Financial Ratio Analysis Methods*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**财务比率的意义**：财务比率通常是由两个相互关联的会计数据相除得到的相对数，用于评价企业某方面的绩效。一项或几项比率的机械计算本身并不能自动提供对一家复杂公司的深刻洞察，财务比率更像是财务状况的线索：单一比率可能具有误导性，但将多个比率结合起来，并结合对公司业务和所处行业经济环境的了解，能够讲述企业财务状况的完整故事。需要注意的是，没有任何一个比率存在放之四海而皆准的“正确”值，比率的好坏需要结合公司的具体情况和行业特征来判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Meaning of Financial Ratios:** Financial ratios are typically derived by dividing two interrelated accounting figures to yield relative numbers used to evaluate a company's performance in a particular area. The mechanical calculation of one or several ratios alone does not automatically provide deep insights into a complex company. Financial ratios serve more as clues to the financial health of an organization: a single ratio can be misleading, but when combined with multiple ratios and informed understanding of the company’s operations and the economic environment of its industry, they can tell the complete story of the company’s financial condition. It is important to note that there is no universally correct value for any single ratio; the quality of a ratio needs to be judged based on the specific circumstances of the company and the characteristics of its industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**财务比率比较的类型**：为了更好地解读比率，需要将其进行有意义的比较。主要有以下两类比较方式：
• **内部比较**：将公司自身不同时期的比率进行比较（如本期与上期、与历史平均水平的比较），或者将实际比率与公司预算目标值进行对比，从中评价业绩趋势或目标达成度。
• **外部比较**：将公司的比率与其他具有可比性的公司或行业平均水平进行比较。通过横向比较，可判断公司相对竞争对手的财务优劣。但需确保不同公司的财务数据口径一致、可比性强，做到“苹果对苹果”的比较（如对财务数据进行适当标准化处理），否则结论可能失真。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Types of Financial Ratio Comparisons: To better interpret these ratios, meaningful comparisons need to be made. There are primarily two types of comparison methods:

• **Internal Comparison**: Compare financial ratios within a company across different periods (such as comparing this period with the previous one, or with historical average levels), or compare actual ratios against budget targets set by the company. This allows for evaluating performance trends or the achievement of goals.

• **External Comparison**: Compare a company's ratios with those of comparable companies or industry averages. Through horizontal comparisons, it is possible to assess the financial strengths and weaknesses relative to competitors. However, it is crucial that the financial data from different companies have consistent and comparable metrics; otherwise, conclusions may be misleading. Ensuring that the data are appropriately standardized can help maintain accuracy in such comparisons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.2负债比率分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.2 Analysis of Debt Ratio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

负债比率分析评估企业长期偿债能力和资本结构稳健性。主要指标包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Analysis of debt ratio evaluates a company's long-term debt-paying ability and the stability of its capital structure. Key indicators include:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **[[资产负债率|资产负债率]]**：总负债 / 总资产。
	1. 表示企业资产中有多大比例是通过负债融资的。该比率越高，说明企业运用了越多的债务融资，财务杠杆越大，债权人承担的风险也越高。资产负债率也反映在企业清算时，债权人对资产的优先要求权大小。一般来说，资产负债率过高可能财务结构偏向冒险，过低则可能未充分利用财务杠杆。
2. **[[产权比率|负债权益比率]]**：负债总额/所有者权益。
	1. 它反映债权人提供的资金与股东提供的资金的相对关系，以及股东资本对债权人资本的保障程度。权益负债比率越高，说明相对于债务，股东投入的资本越多，财务结构越稳健，清算时债权人的利益保障程度越高。反之，如果权益与负债的比例过低（即债务相对于权益过高），则表明企业杠杆率高，财务风险较大。
3. **有形资产净值债务率**：总负债 / 有形净资产(即股东权益-无形资产净值)。
	1. 这里有形净资产通常指股东权益扣除无形资产后的净值。该比率是对负债与股东权益比率的延伸，更谨慎地反映在清算时债权人投入资金受到股东权益保障的程度。因为无形资产在清算时可能难以变现，剔除无形资产后的净资产能够更加保守地衡量债权人的保障。**有形资产净值债务率**越低，说明扣除无形资产后股东实际净资产对债务的覆盖越充足，长期偿债安全性越高。企业还常结合
4. “长期负债与营运资金比率”（长期负债 / 营运资金）
5. **已获利息倍数**（[[利息保障倍数|利息保障倍数]]）：息税前利润（EBIT）/ 利息费用。
	1. 表示企业当期获得的息税前利润是当期应付利息的多少倍。该倍数越高，说明企业赚取的利润对利息的覆盖程度越高，偿付债务利息的保障越强。已获利息倍数低于1则表示企业经营利润不足以支付利息，存在重大偿债风险。
6. **其他长期偿债能力因素**：除了上述比率，还应考虑影响企业长期偿债能力的其他因素，例如债务的期限结构（短期债务与长期债务的搭配）、或有负债情况、经营现金流的稳定性和充足性、再融资能力、利率变动风险等。这些因素通常无法通过单一比率体现，但对企业偿债安全有重要影响。在进行负债比率分析时，应结合这些定性因素进行综合判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **[[资产负债率|Debt-to-assets ratio]]:** total liabilities / total assets.<br>
↳ **1.** This is the share of the firm's assets financed by liabilities. A higher ratio means greater use of debt, more financial leverage, and more risk for creditors. It also indicates the extent of creditors' prior claim on assets in liquidation. A ratio that is too high may signal an aggressive capital structure; one that is too low may indicate underuse of financial leverage.<br>
**2.** **[[产权比率|Debt-to-equity ratio]]:** total liabilities / shareholders' equity.<br>
↳ **1.** This compares funds supplied by creditors with funds supplied by shareholders and indicates how much protection shareholder capital provides to creditors. More equity relative to debt implies a sounder financial structure and greater protection in liquidation. Too little equity relative to debt indicates high leverage and financial risk.<br>
**3.** **Debt-to-tangible-net-worth ratio:** total liabilities / tangible net worth, where tangible net worth equals shareholders' equity minus net intangible assets.<br>
↳ **1.** Tangible net worth deducts intangible assets from shareholders' equity. This conservative extension of the debt-to-equity ratio measures creditor protection in liquidation because intangible assets may be difficult to realize. A lower ratio means that tangible shareholder capital provides more coverage of debt and therefore greater long-term solvency. Firms also commonly examine:<br>
**4.** **Long-term-debt-to-working-capital ratio:** long-term liabilities / working capital.<br>
**5.** **[[利息保障倍数|Times interest earned]], or interest coverage:** EBIT / interest expense.<br>
↳ **1.** This is the number of times current EBIT covers current interest due. A higher multiple indicates stronger interest-paying capacity. A value below 1 means operating profit is insufficient to cover interest and signals serious solvency risk.<br>
**6.** **Other determinants of long-term solvency:** debt maturity structure; contingent liabilities; the stability and sufficiency of operating cash flow; refinancing capacity; interest-rate risk; and similar qualitative factors. No single ratio captures them, but they materially affect debt-service safety and should be considered alongside the leverage ratios.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 负债比率的方向
> 上式是负债／权益即[[产权比率]]；“比率越高，股东相对债务投入越多”误用了倒数解释。负债比率不决定法律清偿顺序或回收额；零负有形净值或营运资本不能常规排序，利息保障低于1也不单独证明已违约。见[[偿债能力的报表外核查]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The formula is [[产权比率|D/E]]; claiming more equity for a higher ratio reverses it. Leverage does not determine legal priority or recoveries. Zero/negative denominators invalidate ordinary ranking, and low interest coverage alone does not prove default. See [[偿债能力的报表外核查|additional solvency checks]].

## 5.3投资收益分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.3 Analysis of Investment Returns*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

投资收益分析从股东和投资者角度出发，评估公司股票的投资价值和股东回报情况。常用指标包括每股收益、[[市盈率|市盈率]]、股利分配指标等：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Investment return analysis examines the investment value of a company's stock from the perspectives of shareholders and investors, assessing shareholder returns. Common metrics include earnings per share (EPS), [[市盈率|P/E Ratio]], and dividend payout ratios, among others:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **每股净收益（EPS）**：即每股收益，计算公式为：本年净利润 / 普通股期末数量。表示每股普通股在本年所获得的净利润。该指标反映股东每持有一股所分享的当期盈利，是衡量公司盈利能力和为股东创造收益能力的重要指标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Earnings Per Share (EPS): also known as earnings per share, is calculated by: net income for the year / Number of ordinary shares at year-end. It represents the net income earned per share of common stock during the year. This metric reflects the portion of net profit that each shareholder receives and is a crucial indicator of a company's profitability and ability to generate profits for shareholders.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:23:start -->
> [!note] EPS 的股数与归属
> [[基本每股收益]]采用归属于普通股股东的利润与期间加权股数；期末股数只是特定情况下的代用。它的单位是金额／股，不是百分数，也不等于实际收到的股利；潜在普通股另见[[稀释每股收益]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[基本每股收益|Basic EPS]] uses ordinary-attributable earnings and weighted period shares. Year-end shares are only a conditional proxy. EPS is currency per share, not a percentage or received dividend; see [[稀释每股收益|diluted EPS]] for potential shares.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:23:end -->

• **市盈率（P/E）**：每股市价 / 每股收益，又称“本益比”。它表示投资者愿意为每1元的公司当前净利润支付多少价格（股价是当年每股收益的多少倍）。市盈率越高，通常意味着市场给予公司盈利较高的预期或者股票价格相对当前收益偏高；市盈率越低，可能意味着公司盈利能力较强或股票价格相对偏低。市盈率是衡量股票估值水平和市场预期的重要参考指标（价值投资者往往偏好市盈率较低的股票，但也要结合成长性分析）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **P/E Ratio (P/E)**: Market Price per Share / Earnings Per Share, also known as "Price-Earnings Ratio." It indicates how much investors are willing to pay for each yuan of the company's current net profit (how many times the stock price is of the company's annual earnings per share). A higher price-to-earnings ratio typically suggests that the market has high expectations for the company's profits or that the stock price is relatively high compared to its current earnings; a lower price-to-earnings ratio may indicate stronger profitability of the company or that the stock price is relatively low. price-to-earnings ratio is an important reference indicator for evaluating stock valuation levels and market expectations (value investors often prefer stocks with lower P/E ratios, but should also consider growth analysis).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:24:start -->
> [!note] 价格倍数的比较条件
> [[市盈率]]须注明历史或预测盈利、基本或稀释口径；低倍数可能来自利润周期高点，不单独证明便宜或盈利更强。零或负盈利也不能按通常正倍数排序。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[市盈率|P/E]] requires historical/forecast and basic/diluted earnings definitions. A low multiple may reflect peak earnings, not proven cheapness or stronger profits. Zero and negative earnings do not support ordinary positive-multiple ranking.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:24:end -->

• **股利发放率**：每股股利 / 每股净收益。表示公司当年净利润中用于向股东分配股利的比例。该比率反映利润用于回报股东的程度。股利发放率高，说明公司将较大比例的利润回报给股东，常见于盈利稳定、成长性较低的成熟行业公司；股利发放率低，说明公司保留更多利润再投资自身成长，常见于高速成长的新兴行业公司。公司制定股利政策时，会权衡再投资机会和股东现金回报之间的关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Dividend payout ratio: dividends per share / net income per share. It represents the proportion of a company's net profit distributed to shareholders in that year. This ratio indicates how much of the profits are returned to shareholders. A high dividend payout ratio suggests that the company is returning a larger portion of its profits to shareholders, which is common in mature industries with stable earnings and lower growth; a low dividend payout ratio indicates that the company is retaining more profits for reinvestment in its growth, which is typical in fast-growing emerging industries. When setting its dividend policy, companies balance the opportunity for reinvestment against shareholder cash returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:25:start -->
> [!note] 支付率的匹配
> [[股利支付率]]的每股式只有在股数与利润分配期间一致时才等于总股利／相应利润。亏损、零利润、特别股利及跨期支付须另作说明；留存利润也不等于现金。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The per-share [[股利支付率|payout ratio]] equals aggregate dividends over earnings only with matched shares and periods. Losses, zero profit, special dividends, and payment timing require qualification; retention is not cash.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:25:end -->

• **股票获利率**：每股股利 / 每股市价，又称股息率。表示按当前股价计算的股东年度现金股利收益率，即投资者每投入1元股票市价，能够获得多少现金股利回报。该指标衡量股东通过股息获得的直接收益，常被偏好稳健收益的投资者关注。股票获利率高的公司，其股票对追求长期稳定收益的投资者具有吸引力（例如公用事业公司的股息率通常较高）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Stock Yield: Earnings per share (EPS) / Market price per share, also known as dividend yield. It represents the annual cash dividend yield based on the current stock price, meaning how much cash dividend an investor can receive for every 1 yuan invested in a stock's market price. This metric measures the direct earnings shareholders receive from dividends and is often of interest to investors seeking steady returns. Companies with high stock yields are attractive to investors seeking long-term stable returns (such as utilities companies, which typically have higher dividend yields).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:26:start -->
> [!note] 股利收益率的分子
> 上段英文写成 EPS／股价，与中文不符；[[股利收益率]]的分子是每股现金股利。历史股利不保证未来继续支付，高收益率也可能来自股价下跌，且不等于含资本损益的总回报。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The preceding English numerator should be cash dividends per share, not EPS. [[股利收益率|Dividend yield]] does not guarantee future dividends or equal total return including capital gains; high yield may reflect a falling price.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:26:end -->

•==每股净资产==：期末股东权益 / 普通股股数，即每股所代表的净资产价值，反映股东每股所拥有的公司净资产。每股净资产是支撑公司股票价值的基础，通常被视为公司股票在极端情况下（清算或股价大跌时）的保底价值。每股净资产越高，表明公司资产实力越雄厚。对于价值型投资者来说，每股净资产也是一个重要参考指标，他们希望购买股价低于其每股净资产的股票，以提高投资安全边际。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Earnings Per Share (EPS): Net assets at period-end / Number of common shares, representing the net asset value each shareholder owns in the company. EPS serves as the foundation for a company's stock value and is often considered the bottom-line value of its stock in extreme situations (such as liquidation or significant price drops). Higher EPS indicates greater financial strength of the company. For value investors, EPS is an important reference indicator; they prefer to purchase stocks trading below their EPS, thereby increasing the safety margin of their investments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:27:start -->
> [!note] BVPS 与价格下限
> 上段应为 Book Value per Share（BVPS），不是 EPS；需使用同一普通股范围的账面权益与时点股数。[[账面净资产不是股价下限]]，股数、计量与未来盈利不同也使“每股数较高＝实力更强”不能直接成立。定义见[[每股净资产]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The term is BVPS, not EPS, using matched ordinary equity and dated shares. [[账面净资产不是股价下限|Book value is not a price floor]], and higher per-share amounts alone do not prove greater strength. See [[每股净资产|BVPS]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:27:end -->

• **净资产倍率（P/B）**：每股市价 / 每股净资产。它评价股票的市价相对于其账面净资产的倍数。净资产倍率越小，意味着股票市价相对于账面价值越低，股票的投资价值相对越高，股价下行风险越小；净资产倍率越高，意味着市场给予公司的账面资产较高的溢价。通常成长性高、轻资产运营的公司可能净资产倍率较高，而资产密集型或业绩低迷的公司净资产倍率较低。该指标常与ROE结合分析：高ROE的公司往往享有较高的市净率溢价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Net Asset Multiple (P/B): Market Price per Share / Book Value per Share. It evaluates the multiple of a stock's market price relative to its book value. A lower net asset multiple indicates that the stock's market price is relatively low compared to its book value, suggesting higher investment value and lower downside risk; a higher net asset multiple implies that the market is giving the company a premium for its book assets. Typically, companies with high growth potential and light asset operations may have higher net asset multiples, while those with heavy asset-intensive or poor performance may have lower multiples. This metric is often analyzed in conjunction with ROE: Companies with high ROE often enjoy a higher P/B ratio premium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:28:start -->
> [!note] 市净率不是安全评级
> [[市净率]]低可能反映未来低回报或资产损失，不能单凭倍数较小便断定价值更高、下行更少。归属与计量可比且权益为正，是通常高低比较的前提。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Low [[市净率|P/B]] may reflect weak future returns or asset losses rather than higher value or less downside. Match attribution and measurement, with positive equity for ordinary ranking.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.3流动性分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.3 Analysis of Liquidity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**含义**：流动性分析关注企业偿还短期债务的能力，即短期偿债能力。它主要通过衡量流动资产和流动负债的关系来进行评价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Meaning**: Liquidity analysis focuses on a company's ability to repay short-term debts, which is known as short-term solvency. It primarily evaluates this through measuring the relationship between current assets and current liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**主要比率指标**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Key Ratio Indicators**:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$ \text{流动比率} = \frac{\text{流动资产}}{\text{流动负债}} $$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$$ \text{Current Ratio} = \frac{\text{Current Assets}}{\text{Current Liabilities}} $$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$ \text{速动比率} = \frac{\text{速动资产}}{\text{流动负债}} $$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$$ \text{Acid-Test Ratio} = \frac{\text{Quick Assets}}{\text{Current Liabilities}} $$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


其中，速动资产是指流动资产中可以在短期内迅速变现的部分（通常为流动资产减去存货和预付款项等流动性较差的项目）。此外，还有一个**绝对数指标**用来衡量流动性：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Where, quick assets refer to a portion of current assets that can be quickly converted into cash (typically defined as current assets minus inventory and prepaid items, among others with lower liquidity). Additionally, an **absolute measure indicator** is used to gauge liquidity:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$ \text{净营运资本} = \text{流动资产} - \text{流动负债} $$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$$ \text{Net Working Capital} = \text{Current Assets} - \text{Current Liabilities} $$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净营运资本为正表示流动资产足以偿还流动负债，为负则可能存在短期偿债压力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive net working capital indicates that current assets are sufficient to cover current liabilities, while a negative value suggests potential short-term debt repayment pressure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**指标解读**：流动比率通常用于衡量企业以流动资产偿还流动负债的能力。一般而言，该比率越高，短期偿债能力越强，但过高也可能意味着资产利用效率不高。速动比率剔除了存货等流动性较弱的资产，更为严格地衡量短期偿债能力。速动比率低于流动比率且明显偏低，往往提示存货占用了大量流动资金，存在存货变现风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Interpretation of Indicators**: The current ratio is commonly used to gauge a company's ability to repay its short-term liabilities with its liquid assets. Generally, a higher ratio indicates stronger short-term debt-paying capacity; however, an excessively high ratio may suggest inefficient asset utilization. The quick ratio excludes less liquid assets like inventory, providing a more stringent measure of short-term debt-paying capacity. A quick ratio that is significantly lower than the current ratio often signals that a substantial portion of working capital is tied up in inventory, posing a risk of inventory liquidation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**分析流动性比率需注意的问题**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Considerations in Analyzing Liquidity Ratios:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **计价基础差异**：流动比率将流动资产与流动负债直接相除，但两者在计价基础上可能不一致。流动负债多以到期偿还金额计价，而流动资产中既包含按现值计价的现金与短期存款、也包含按历史成本计价的存货、按未来可收回金额计价的应收账款等。这种计价混杂可能影响比率的解释。
• **时点局限**：流动比率反映的是资产负债表日的静态状况，企业可能在报表日采取措施暂时提高该比率（例如暂缓采购、增加现金持有），因此需结合报表附注和期后情况判断流动比率的代表性。
• **风险隐含**：单一的流动比率无法反映流动性风险的全貌。例如，它未体现负债的结构和期限分布，也未体现企业获得后续融资的能力。因此在评价短期偿债能力时，还应结合速动比率、经营活动现金流等其他信息综合考虑。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Differences in measurement basis:** The current ratio divides current assets by current liabilities, but the two sides may use different measurement bases. Current liabilities are commonly recorded at the amount due at maturity; current assets combine cash and short-term deposits measured at present value, inventory at historical cost, and receivables at expected recoverable amounts. This mixture affects interpretation.
• **Point-in-time limitation:** The current ratio is a snapshot at the balance-sheet date. A firm can temporarily improve it—for example, by delaying purchases or accumulating cash—so the notes and post-balance-sheet events are needed to judge whether it is representative.
• **Risks left implicit:** The current ratio alone cannot capture the full liquidity-risk picture. It omits the structure and maturity distribution of liabilities and the firm's ability to obtain further financing. Short-term solvency should therefore also be assessed with the quick ratio, operating cash flow, and other information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:29:start -->
> [!note] 账面缓冲不保证支付
> 正[[营运资本]]只说明流动资产账面额较高，不能保证及时变现偿债。速动资产排除范围需明确；下方BW原练习仅扣存货，其他严格口径还扣预付。应结合[[偿债能力的报表外核查]]，而非将每类资产固定理解为同一种计量基础。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Positive [[营运资本|working capital]] is a book buffer, not guaranteed timely payment. Define the quick-asset exclusions and inspect actual measurement and availability through [[偿债能力的报表外核查|additional solvency checks]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.4运营效率比率分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.4 Analysis of Operational Efficiency Ratios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

运营效率分析关注企业资产的周转效率和经营管理效率。常用指标包括：[[应收账款周转率|应收账款周转率]]、[[存货周转率|存货周转率]]、固定资产周转率和总资产周转率等。一般公式形式为某项周转率 = 当期销售或消耗量 / 平均资产余额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operational efficiency analysis focuses on the turnover efficiency of a company's assets and management efficiency. Common metrics include: [[应收账款周转率|Accounts Receivable Turnover Ratio]], [[存货周转率|Inventory Turnover Ratio]], fixed asset turnover rate, and total asset turnover rate, among others. The general formula is that a certain turnover rate equals current sales or consumption volume divided by average asset balance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **应收账款周转率**：衡量应收账款的回收速度，反映收款管理效率。
• **存货周转率**：衡量存货的周转速度，反映存货管理和销售情况。
• **固定资产周转率**：衡量固定资产支持销售的效率，反映固定资产的利用水平。
• **总资产周转率**：衡量全部资产创造收入的效率，是对资产利用效率的总体评估。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**[[应收账款周转率|accounts receivable turnover rate]]**: measures the recovery speed of accounts receivable and reflects the efficiency of collection management.
**[[存货周转率|inventory turnover rate]]**: measures inventory turnover speed, reflecting inventory management and sales.
· **Fixed asset turnover**: measures the efficiency of fixed assets to support sales and reflects the utilization level of fixed assets.
***Total asset turnover**: A measure of the efficiency with which all assets generate revenue and is an overall assessment of the efficiency with which assets are utilized.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

运营效率比率越高，通常表示相关资产周转越快、利用效率越高。但需结合行业特点判断，例如零售业正常存货周转率远高于重工业。通过这些比率，可以找出资产运用中的瓶颈（如应收账款回收慢或库存积压）并加以改善。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Higher operational efficiency ratios typically indicate faster asset turnover and higher utilization rates. However, industry characteristics must be taken into account; for instance, normal inventory turnover rates in retail are significantly higher than those in heavy industry. By analyzing these ratios, we can identify bottlenecks in asset utilization (such as slow accounts receivable collection or excess inventory) and address them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:30:start -->
> [!note] 周转口径与经营判断
> 赊销额、收入或成本分子不可互换；平均与期末存量、360与365天也须说明。高周转可能伴随缺货、保理或减值，不能单独证明效率改善；见[[财务比率口径匹配]]与[[存货周转的收入与成本口径]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Credit sales, revenue, and cost numerators differ, as do stock measures and day counts. High turnover may reflect shortages, factoring, or impairment rather than improvement. See [[财务比率口径匹配|basis matching]] and [[存货周转的收入与成本口径|inventory numerator conventions]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic46-sec:30:end -->

## 5.5盈利能力分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.5 Analysis of Profitability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

盈利能力分析考察企业获取利润的能力及其收益质量。常用的盈利比率包括：销售利润率、总资产报酬率、权益净利率等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Profitability analysis assesses a company's ability to generate profits and the quality of those earnings. Common profitability ratios include: sales profit margin, total asset return, and net operating return on equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **销售利润率**（净利润 / 销售收入）：反映每单位销售收入带来的净利润是多少，表示企业销售获利的能力。分为**毛利率**（毛利/销售收入）、**营业利润率**（营业利润/销售收入）、**净利润率**（净利润/销售收入）等层次，用于分析成本控制和费用管理效率。
• **总资产报酬率**（息税前利润 / 平均总资产，[[EBIT资产盈利率|ROA]]）：反映企业全部资产获取息税前利润的能力，即资产的综合回报水平。通常排除利息和税收影响以便不同资本结构企业之间可比。
• **[[权益净利率|净资产收益率]]**（净利润 / 平均股东权益，即权益净利率，$ROE$）：反映股东权益获得净利润的收益水平，是综合性最强的盈利能力指标。ROE受净利率、资产周转率和财务杠杆共同影响（可用杜邦分析分解）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Profit margin on sales** (net profit / sales revenue): net profit earned per unit of sales, measuring profitability from selling activity. Related layers include the **gross profit margin** (gross profit / sales), **operating profit margin** (operating profit / sales), and **net profit margin** (net profit / sales); together they help diagnose cost control and expense management.
• **Return on assets** (EBIT / average total assets, ROA): the firm's ability to generate earnings before interest and tax from all assets. Excluding interest and tax improves comparability across capital structures.
• **[[权益净利率|Return on equity]]** (net profit / average shareholders' equity, $ROE$): the return earned on shareholders' capital and the most comprehensive profitability measure. ROE reflects net margin, asset turnover, and financial leverage and can be decomposed with DuPont analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在盈利能力分析中，需要关注利润的来源和可持续性，如主营业务盈利 VS 非经常性损益，以及同行业对比企业利润率的高低等。持续、稳定且来源健康的盈利能力更受投资者青睐。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In profitability analysis, it is crucial to focus on the sources and sustainability of profits, such as comparing core business earnings versus non-recurring gains, and the relative profit margins of comparable companies in the same industry. Investors tend to favor sustained and stable profitability that has healthy and sustainable sources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:31:start -->
> [!note] 两种资产回报分别定义
> 本段 EBIT／资产对应[[EBIT资产盈利率]]，净利润／资产对应[[总资产净利率]]，不能互为别名；三因素杜邦使用后者。销售利润率也应明确毛利、营业利润还是净利润层；ROE 英文是 Return on Equity，归属与存量见[[权益净利率]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[EBIT资产盈利率|EBIT ROA]] and [[总资产净利率|net-income ROA]] are different definitions; three-factor DuPont uses the latter. Specify the margin layer, and use Return on Equity with matched attribution and stocks; see [[权益净利率|ROE]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.7现金流量分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.7 Cash Flow Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金流量分析关注企业现金流的充足性和质量，评估其现金获取能力和偿债能力等。主要涉及对现金流量表中各项指标和比率的考察：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash flow analysis focuses on the adequacy and quality of a company's cash flows, evaluating its ability to generate cash and its debt-paying capacity. It primarily involves examining various indicators and ratios in cash-flow statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **经营活动现金流量**与**净利润**的对比：分析企业利润的含金量，看净利润是否由真实的现金流支撑。如果净利润远高于经营活动现金净流量，需警惕利润的可持续性和真实性。常用指标如“经营活动现金净流量/净利润”比率等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Comparison of operating activities cash flow with net profit: Analyze the quality of the company's profits to determine if the net profit is supported by real cash flows. If the net profit is significantly higher than the operating cash flow net, there may be cause for concern regarding the sustainability and authenticity of the profits. Common metrics include ratios such as "operating cash flow net / net profit."
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **现金流动负债比**：经营活动产生的现金净流量 / 流动负债，衡量企业依靠经营现金偿还短期债务的能力。类似地还有经营现金流与总负债比等指标用于评价偿债安全性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Cash Flow to Debt Ratio: Operating cash flow from activities / Current liabilities, measuring a company's ability to repay short-term debts using operating cash flows. Similar metrics like operating cash flow to total liabilities are used to evaluate its debt-servicing safety.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **自由现金流**：在满足必要的资本支出和营运资金需求后剩余的经营现金流，可用于企业扩张、偿债和分红。自由现金流为正且稳定增长，说明企业有良好的内部造血功能和对外分配能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **free cash flow**: After meeting necessary capital expenditures and working capital requirements, any remaining operating cash flow can be used for enterprise expansion, debt repayment, and dividends. A positive and stable growth in free cash flow indicates a good internal self-sufficiency and external distribution capability of the enterprise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic46-sec:32:start -->
> [!note] 现金指标的完整边界
> CFO 已含营运资本变动，不能无条件再扣一遍；FCFF面向全部资本提供者，定义见[[企业自由现金流]]。利润与CFO无固定比例，历史现金也不保证未来偿债。原课PDF588–595的每股现金“最大股利”、五年投资满足比和营运指数还需分别限定，连续讲解见[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/06_财务报表与公司诊断#五、把利润诊断接到真实现金需要|从利润到现金]]与[[每股经营现金流不限定股利]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> CFO already reflects working-capital changes; do not deduct them again without a different stated basis. [[企业自由现金流|FCFF]] concerns all capital providers. Profit and CFO have no fixed ratio, and historical cash does not guarantee future payments. See [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/06_财务报表与公司诊断#五、把利润诊断接到真实现金需要|the complete cash discussion]] and [[每股经营现金流不限定股利|the dividend boundary]].
