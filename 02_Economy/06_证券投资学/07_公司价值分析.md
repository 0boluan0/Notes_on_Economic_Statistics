# 1.公司基本素质分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Analysing a Company's Fundamental Quality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1公司基本情况分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1 Company Background and Operations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分析公司所处的产业、公司的主要经营业务 。包括公司产品和服务的情况、产品所处的生命周期；消费者如何使用公司的产品和服务；客户及其类型；生产要素的供给状况；生产的组织形式和技术水平；公司的组织结构；营销和销售策略等 。
• **竞争对手分析**：分析公司主要竞争对手的各方面情况 ：
• 竞争对手的目标和战略 ；
• 经营状况和财务状况 ；
• 技术经济实力 ；
• 领导者和管理者素质 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with the firm's industry and core business. Examine its products and services and where they sit in their life cycles; how customers use them and who those customers are; access to labour, materials, and other production inputs; production organisation and technology; organisational structure; and marketing and sales strategy.

• **Competitor analysis:** assess each major competitor's objectives and strategy, operating and financial condition, technological and economic strength, and the quality of its leadership and management.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **公司在产业中的竞争地位（SWOT分析）**：分析公司的优势、劣势、机会、威胁 。
• 优势（内部因素）：资金充足、公司形象良好、技术力量强、规模经济、产品质量高、市场份额大、成本优势等 。
• 劣势（内部因素）：设备老化、技术薄弱、管理混乱、经营不善、竞争力差等 。
• 机会（外部因素）：新产品、新市场、新技术、新需求出现，市场壁垒解除，竞争对手失误等 。
• 威胁（外部因素）：竞争对手增多、替代产品出现、市场紧缩、产业政策变化、经济衰退、消费者偏好改变、突发事件等 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* **Competitive position in the industry (SWOT):** assess the company's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
* **Strengths (Internal Factors)**: Adequate funding, good corporate image, strong technical capabilities, economies of scale, high-quality products, large market share, cost advantages, etc.
* **Weaknesses (Internal Factors)**: Outdated equipment, weak technology, chaotic management, poor business performance, low competitiveness, etc.
* **Opportunities (External Factors)**: The emergence of new products, new markets, new technologies, new demands, removal of market barriers, competitors' mistakes, etc.
* **Threats (External Factors)**: Increased competition, the appearance of substitute products, market contraction, changes in industry policies, economic downturns, shifts in consumer preferences, emergencies, etc.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic45-company:FQ-02:start -->

> [!note] 分类不等于验证
> [[SWOT分析]]按内外因素组织判断；“份额大”“品牌强”等课堂示例仍需结合竞争对象和证据评价，不自动证明持久优势。若要检验持续性，见[[护城河证据]]。核验来源：[[证券投资学.pdf#page=601|原 PDF 第 601 页]]及该两卡所列来源。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[SWOT分析|SWOT]] organizes internal and external factors. Classroom examples such as large share or a strong brand still require comparative evidence and do not automatically establish durability; see [[护城河证据|testing durable-advantage claims]]. Checked against [[证券投资学.pdf#page=601|source PDF p. 601]] and the sources in those atoms.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic45-company:FQ-02:end -->

• **公司经济区位分析**：关注公司所处经济区位的条件和影响因素 。经济区位是指地理范畴的经济增长点和辐射范围，包括：区位内的自然条件和基础设施条件；区位内政府相关政策；区位内的经济特色（例如深圳的高新技术产业、福建的对外贸易特色） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Economic-location analysis:** examine how the firm's location affects its prospects, including local natural resources and infrastructure, government policy, and the area's economic specialisation—for example, high technology in Shenzhen or export-oriented trade in Fujian.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2公司竞争地位分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2 Competitive-Position Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (1)成本领先战略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Cost Leadership Strategy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

企业的目标是成为行业中的低成本生产厂商 。实现成本领先通常来源于规模经济、专有技术、优惠的原材料、廉价劳动力等因素 。例如，台积电就是成功实施成本领先战略的案例 。逻辑要求是企业成为唯一的成本领先者，以显著的成本优势获取竞争优势 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A cost-leadership strategy aims to make the firm its industry's lowest-cost producer. The advantage may come from economies of scale, proprietary technology, favourable access to raw materials, or low labour costs. TSMC is given here as an example. The strategy works only if the firm establishes a meaningful and defensible cost advantage rather than merely matching several other low-cost competitors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic45-company:FQ-04:start -->

> [!note] 战略取向、成功与唯一地位分开
> [[证券投资学.pdf#page=605|原 PDF 第 605 页]]确有“唯一”及台积电案例。[[成本领先]]定义的是追求相对低成本的战略，不能把唯一最低成本地位当作所有采用者的必要条件；低售价也不等于低成本。案例保留为课堂语境，不能仅凭其名称断言该企业当前仍具有某项优势，须重新核查同口径成本与竞争条件。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[证券投资学.pdf#page=605|Source PDF p. 605]] contains the uniqueness claim and TSMC example. [[成本领先|Cost leadership]] identifies a strategy pursuing relatively low costs; a uniquely lowest-cost position is not necessary to identify every firm pursuing it. Low price is also distinct from low cost. The company example remains classroom context, not current evidence without renewed comparable-cost and competitive analysis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic45-company:FQ-04:end -->

### (2)差异化战略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Differentiation Strategy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

企业力求在一种或多种为许多客户所看重的特质上独树一帜，从而获得高于行业平均水平的收益 。差异化可以基于产品本身的独特属性、卓越的销售和交付体系、特殊的营销渠道以及其他使企业经营独具特色的因素 。其逻辑要求是企业选择有利于竞争且能使自身经营独具特色的特质进行培养 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A differentiation strategy makes the firm distinctive on one or more attributes that customers value, allowing it to earn above-average returns. Differentiation may come from product features, superior sales and delivery, distinctive distribution channels, or another capability that competitors cannot easily match. The firm must choose an attribute that matters to customers and build its operations around delivering it consistently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic45-company:FQ-05:start -->

> [!note] 差异化不是利润保证
> [[差异化战略]]须比较客户愿意支付的溢价与额外成本，并考虑销量及模仿反应；[[证券投资学.pdf#page=606|原 PDF 第 606 页]]的高收益表述应作为战略目标而非无条件结论。只向狭窄客户群提供这种区别时，属于[[集聚战略|差异化集聚]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[差异化战略|Differentiation]] requires comparing willingness to pay with added costs and considering volume and imitation. The superior-return wording in [[证券投资学.pdf#page=606|source PDF p. 606]] describes an objective, not a guarantee. Targeting the distinction at a narrow customer group is [[集聚战略|differentiation focus]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic45-company:FQ-05:end -->

### (3)目标集聚战略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Focus Strategy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集聚战略（目标集聚战略）指企业聚焦于行业内的某一细分市场，通过**成本集聚战略**或**差异集聚战略**获取该细分市场的竞争优势 。实施集聚战略的企业选择一个或一组细分市场提供产品或服务，而不服务于整个市场 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **focus strategy** targets one segment, or a small group of segments, rather than serving the entire industry. Within that niche, the firm pursues either a cost advantage or a differentiation advantage:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **成本集聚战略**：在目标市场上寻求成本优势 。
• **差异集聚战略**：在目标市场上追求差异优势 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Cost focus:** seek a cost advantage within the target segment.
• **Differentiation focus:** offer distinctive value within the target segment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3公司盈利能力分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3 Analysis of Company Profitability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **主要衡量指标**：评价公司盈利能力的常用指标包括资产报酬率、销售毛利率、[[营业净利率|销售净利率]]、[[权益净利率|净资产收益率]]、股东权益收益率等 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Main metrics:** common profitability measures include return on assets, gross margin, [[营业净利率|net profit margin]], and [[权益净利率|return on equity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **盈利能力预测**：可以根据公司过去的每股盈利（EPS）水平来预测未来每股盈利 ，并分析过去销售收入与每股盈利之间的相关关系 。公司盈利是销售收入（或营业收入）减去成本和费用后的余额，即：$$\text{公司盈利} = \text{销售收入} - \text{成本和费用}$$ 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* **Profitability forecast:** use the company's EPS history and the historical relationship between revenue and EPS to form a forecast of future earnings per share. Profit is the residual after costs and expenses are deducted from sales revenue: $$\text{Company Profit} = \text{Sales Revenue} - \text{Costs and Expenses}$$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic45-company:FQ-06:start -->

> [!note] 历史关系是预测输入，不是固定规律
> 从历史收入／EPS相关性走向预测，应明确销量、价格、成本、融资及股数假设；经营结构变化时，相关关系未必保持。可先用[[收入驱动预测]]建立经营基线，再区分[[利润增长不等于现金增长|利润与现金增量]]及[[增长不等于增值|增长与新增价值]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Forecasting from historical revenue/EPS relationships requires assumptions about volume, price, costs, financing, and share count; relationships may change with the operating structure. Begin with a [[收入驱动预测|revenue-driven baseline]], then distinguish [[利润增长不等于现金增长|profit from incremental cash]] and [[增长不等于增值|growth from value creation]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- topic45-company:FQ-06:end -->

## 1.4公司经营管理能力分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.4 Analysis of Company Management Capabilities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公司经营管理能力分析包括以下几个方面 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assess management capability across the following areas:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **公司法人治理结构**：公司的治理结构是否规范健全，包括股权结构是否规范、董事会决策机制是否有效、独立董事制度是否完善、监事会是否独立履行监督责任、经理层是否优秀，以及相关利益者是否参与治理等 。
• **经理层的素质**：公司的高级管理人员应具备从事管理工作的意愿、专业技术能力、良好的道德品质、人际关系协调能力，以及观念更新和持续学习的能力等 。
• **从业人员的素质和创新能力**：公司的员工应具备专业技术能力、对企业的忠诚度和责任感，具有团队合作精神和创新能力等 。
• **经营效率**：关注企业运营的高效性。例如：生产设备开工率是否达到额定标准，人均产量和人均销售额是否高于主要竞争对手，资产周转率和毛利率是否高于主要竞争对手，等等 。
• **人力资源管理**：是否建立了合理有效的激励制度，是否做到内部人才合理流动、任人唯贤，能否稳定留住企业内部的核心人才和员工队伍 。
• **内部控制机制**：内部控制制度是否覆盖企业生产经营管理的各个方面，并有效影响企业各内部组织机构的运作 。
• **生产运作管理**：企业是否根据目标制定各项生产计划，合理安排生产任务，适时调整生产规模以及产品品种、质量、规格、数量和产出时间等，以满足经营目标 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Corporate governance:** is the ownership structure sound, is board decision-making effective, are independent directors and the supervisory board able to perform their roles, is management capable, and do relevant stakeholders participate appropriately?
• **Management quality:** do senior managers have the motivation, professional competence, integrity, interpersonal skills, and capacity to learn and update their thinking?
• **Employee quality and innovation:** do employees have the necessary technical skills, loyalty, responsibility, teamwork, and capacity to innovate?
• **Operating efficiency:** examine capacity utilisation, output and sales per employee, asset turnover, and gross margin relative to major competitors.

• **Human-resource management:** are incentives effective, are promotion and internal mobility based on merit, and can the firm retain key people and maintain a stable workforce?

• **Internal controls:** do controls cover the main areas of production and operations, and do they work effectively across organisational units?

• **Production and operations management:** does the firm translate its objectives into production plans, allocate tasks sensibly, and adjust scale, product mix, quality, specifications, quantities, and timing as conditions change?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.5公司成长性分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.5 Analysis of Company Growth Potential*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公司成长性分析主要评估企业的战略规划和未来发展潜力。例如：了解企业内部的优劣势，剖析企业外部环境，以帮助企业迎接未来挑战；为企业明确未来的目标和方向，并使企业每个成员了解企业的长期目标 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Growth analysis evaluates the firm's strategy and future development potential. It combines an internal assessment of strengths and weaknesses with analysis of the external environment, then asks whether the company has clear long-term objectives and whether its members understand the direction of travel.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**规模变动特征及扩张潜力分析**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Analysis of Scale Dynamics and Expansion Potential**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 纵向比较公司历年的销售、利润、资产规模等数据，分析公司发展的趋势。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare sales, profit, assets, and other measures across years to identify the company's development trend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 判断公司规模的扩张是由供给推动还是由市场需求拉动所致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Determine whether a company's expansion is driven by supply-push or demand-pull.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 将公司的增长率与行业平均水平及主要竞争对手的数据进行比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare the company's growth rate with its industry's average and with major competitors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 分析并预测公司主要产品的市场前景。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Analyze and predict the market prospects for the company's main products.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 分析公司的财务状况以及公司的投资和筹资潜力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Analyze the company’s financial condition and its investment and financing potential.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.7公司竞争优势分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.7 Analysis of Company Competitive Advantage*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- equity-valuation-note:decomp:start -->

> [!note] 价值分解的现金流与时点
> [[证券投资学.pdf#page=620|原课件 PDF620]]第一项是 $NOPAT_1/w$：从第一年末起的既有业务现金流，不是后文原式中的任意 $NOPAT_t/w$。将 NOPAT 直接当现金，须已处理必要维护投资；第 $t$ 年末扩张投资从第 $t+1$ 年起产生收益，再把项目净现值折回今天。后文逗号及感叹号是转写标点，不能读作新的乘法规则或阶乘。精确定义、条件和推导见[[新增投资价值分解]]；增长与投入按[[再投资增长关系]]配对，历史平均 ROIC 高并不单独证明新增投资的永久超额回报。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[证券投资学.pdf#page=620|Source slide PDF620]] starts with $NOPAT_1/w$, the existing business's cash stream from year 1, rather than an arbitrary $NOPAT_t/w$. Treating NOPAT as cash requires maintenance investment already to be accounted for. Expansion at year-end $t$ earns from year $t+1$; its project NPV is then discounted to today. Commas and exclamation marks in the later transcription are not extra mathematical operations or factorials. See [[新增投资价值分解|the value decomposition]] and [[再投资增长关系|reinvestment timing]]; high historical average ROIC alone does not establish permanent excess returns on new capital.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- equity-valuation-note:decomp:end -->
- 公司的总价值$$V$$可以分成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The total value $V$ of the company can be divided into:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    1. **现有资产价值**（不再追加投资时的现金奶牛）；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Value of assets in place** (the cash flows generated if the firm makes no further investment)<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    2. **增长价值**（继续追加资本、并在保持$$ROIC>w$$的前提下赚取超额收益）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **Growth value** (the value created by reinvesting additional capital while $$ROIC>w$$).<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    
- 当$$NOPAT$$（税后经营利润）与投入资本$$C$$都按固定增速$$g$$扩张时，米勒-莫迪利安尼（M&M）公式被化简成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- When net operating profit after tax ($$NOPAT$$) and invested capital $$C$$ both grow at a constant rate $$g$$, the Modigliani–Miller (M&M) value decomposition simplifies to:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    $$
    
    V=NOPAT_1\left(\frac1w+\underbrace{\frac{ROIC-w}{wROIC}}_{\text{独特优势}}\times\underbrace{\frac{g}{w-g}}_{\text{增长因子}}\right)
    
    $$
    
    其中$$w$$是加权平均资本成本$$WACC$$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $w$ is the weighted average cost of capital, $WACC$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
<!-- equity-valuation-note:two-stage:start -->

> [!note] 两阶段的每股口径与逐期复算
> 本节两处模型中，$E_n$ 应按每股盈利读取。若新增回报逐年变化，应递推 $E_{j+1}=E_j(1+\rho R_j)$，不能把某一年的 $R_n$ 放进覆盖全部历史的单一幂。显性期折现的是全部股利，不只是“超额红利”；正常回报阶段满足[[正常回报终值|稳定留存等条件]]时，第 $t$ 年末终值是 $E_{t+1}/k$，不是 $E_t/k$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Read $E_n$ as earnings per share in both presentations below. With changing new-investment returns, use $E_{j+1}=E_j(1+\rho R_j)$ rather than one year's return in a power covering the entire history. Discount all explicit-period dividends, not only excess dividends. Under the [[正常回报终值|normal-return terminal assumptions]], the year-$t$ terminal value uses $E_{t+1}/k$, not $E_t/k$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对后文 $E_1=120$、留存40%、前三个年末投入从次年起赚20%、此后新增投入赚10%的例子，三笔股利为72、77.76、83.9808，$E_4=151.16544$；在其余盈利持续、股数固定等条件下，价值约1328.54335，全程新增投入只赚10%时为1200，优势差额为128.54335。原记录1070、800、270与此时序不符，且[[证券投资学.pdf#page=621|原课件 PDF621]]未列这组数例。完整过程见[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/07_股票与企业估值#五、显性期结束之后，企业并没有突然消失|新课程第五节]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the later example, EPS starts at 120 and retention is 40%. Investments at the first three year-ends earn 20% from the following year; later investment earns 10%. Dividends are 72, 77.76 and 83.9808, with $E_4=151.16544$. Under persistent existing earnings, fixed shares and the stated assumptions, value is approximately 1328.54335, versus 1200 if all new investment earns 10%; the difference is 128.54335. The recorded 1070, 800 and 270 do not follow this timing, and [[证券投资学.pdf#page=621|slide PDF621]] contains no such numerical example. See the [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/07_股票与企业估值#五、显性期结束之后，企业并没有突然消失|complete reconstruction in section five]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- equity-valuation-note:two-stage:end -->
- 科登-戈登提出的“两阶段成长模型”则进一步强调：**竞争优势终将衰减**。因此把生命周期分成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The following two-stage competitive-advantage model emphasises that **excess returns eventually fade**. It divides the firm's life into:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    1. $$n\le t$$：再投资回报率$$R_n>k$$（超额收益阶段）；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** For $n\le t$, return on reinvestment satisfies $R_n>k$: the excess-return phase.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    2. $$n>t$$：$$R_n=k$$（优势消失，回报率仅够覆盖股权成本）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** For $$n>t$$, $$R_n=k$$: the advantage has disappeared and reinvestment earns only the cost of equity.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    
    对应股票理论价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The corresponding theoretical share value is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    $$
    
    p_0=\sum_{n=1}^{t}\frac{E_1(1-\rho)(1+\rho R_n)^{n-1}}{(1+k)^n}+\frac{E_1(1+\rho R_t)^t}{k(1+k)^t}
    
    $$
    
    其中$$E_1$$为下一年股净收益，$$\rho$$为留存收益率，$$k$$为股权成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $E_1$ is next year's net income attributable to equity, $\rho$ is the retention ratio, and $k$ is the cost of equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

米勒-莫迪利安尼价值分解公式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Modigliani–Miller Value-Decomposition Formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **1. 原式**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Original Expression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

V=\frac{NOPAT_t}{w}+\sum_{t=1}^{\infty}\frac{(ROIC_t-w),\Delta C_t}{w(1+w)^t}

$$

|**记号**|**含义**|
|---|---|
|$NOPAT_t$|第$$t$$期税后经营利润|
|$w$|企业的$$WACC$$|
|$ROIC_t$|投入资本回报率|
|$\Delta C_t$|第$$t$$期新增投资额|
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| **Symbol** | **Meaning** |
| --- | --- |
| $NOPAT_t$ | Net operating profit after tax in period $t$ |
| $w$ | The firm's weighted average cost of capital, $WACC$ |
| $ROIC_t$ | Return on invested capital in period $t$ |
| $\Delta C_t$ | New investment in period $t$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **第一项**：假设企业立刻停止一切扩张，把当前资本当“摇钱树”运转，就能折现出这一块“现有资产价值”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **First term:** assume the firm stops expanding and simply earns cash flow on its existing capital. Discounting that stream gives the **value of assets in place**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **第二项**：若企业继续追加$$\Delta C_t$$，其中每$$1$$元能赚到$$ROIC_t$$，超出资本成本$$w$$的部分即超额收益。对其折现累加就得到“增长价值”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Second term:** if the firm continues to add $$C_t$$, each unit of new investment earns $$ROIC_t$$. The portion above the capital cost $$w$$ is an excess return; discounting and summing those excess returns gives **growth value**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

### **2. 恒速增长化简**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Constant Growth Simplification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

若$$NOPAT$$与$$C$$都以同一固定增速$$g$$增长，且$$ROIC_t$$恒为$$ROIC$$，可推得
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $$NOPAT$$ and $$C$$ both grow at the same constant rate $$g$$, while $$ROIC_t$$ remains equal to $$ROIC$$, the expression simplifies to
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

$$

V=NOPAT_1!\Bigl(\frac1w+\frac{ROIC-w}{w,ROIC}\times\frac{g}{w-g}\Bigr)

$$

- **独特优势系数**：$$(ROIC-w)/(w,ROIC)$$ —— $ROIC$相对$w$越高，企业护城河越宽。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Competitive-advantage coefficient:** $$(ROIC-w)/(w,ROIC)$$ —— the farther $ROIC$ lies above $w$, the wider the firm's economic moat.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **增长因子**：$$g/(w-g)$$ —— 再投资节奏越快（$g$越大），成长杠杆越显著。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Growth factor:** $$\frac{g}{(w-g)}$$ —— a higher $g$ gives reinvestment greater leverage over value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

> **如果$$ROIC=w$$（没有超额收益），第二项为零，企业只有现有资产价值；这就解释了为什么“竞争优势”值钱——它使$$ROIC>w$$从而拉高第二项。**
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **If $ROIC=w$, so there is no excess return, the second term is zero and the firm has only the value of its existing assets. This explains why a competitive advantage is valuable: it allows $ROIC>w$ and raises the second term.**
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **3. 数值示例**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Numerical Examples*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

假定：$NOPAT_1=100$，$ROIC=15\%$，$w=10\%$，$g=4\%$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assumptions: $NOPAT_1=100$, $ROIC=15\%$, $w=10\%$, $g=4\%$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

$$

\begin{aligned}

V&=100\left(\frac1{0.1}+\frac{0.15-0.10}{0.1\times0.15}\times\frac{0.04}{0.10-0.04}\right)\

&=100\left(10+\frac{0.05}{0.015}\times0.6667\right)\

&=100\left(10+3.3333\times0.6667\right)=100(10+2.2222)=1,222.2

\end{aligned}

$$

- **现有资产价值**：$$100/0.1=1,000$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Value of Existing Assets**: $$100/0.1=1,000$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **增长价值**：$$222.2$$ 占总值约$$18\%$$ —— 这就是“竞争优势”的货币化结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Growth value:** $$222.2$$, about $$18\%$$ of total value. This is the value created by the firm's competitive advantage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

科登-戈登两阶段竞争优势模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two-Stage Competitive-Advantage Model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **1. 模型思路**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Model Approach*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **阶段一（$$1\le n\le t$$）**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Stage One ($$1 \leq n \leq t$$)**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    - 企业仍具备护城河，追加投资回报率$$R_n>k$$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The firm still has an economic moat, so the return on incremental investment satisfies $R_n>k$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - 留存收益率$$\rho$$决定再投资规模，每年股净收益 $$E_n=E_1(1+\rho R_n)^{n-1}$$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The retention ratio $\rho$ determines the amount reinvested, with annual net income attributable to equity given by $E_n = E_1 (1 + \rho R_n)^{n-1}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    
2. **阶段二（$$n>t$$）**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **Stage Two ($$n > t$$)**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    - 护城河被竞争磨平，$$R_n=k$$，再投资只赚到正常收益，不再创造超额价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The moat has been eroded by competition, $$R_n = k$$, and further investments yield only normal returns without creating excess value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    

### **2. 估值公式**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Valuation Formula*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p_0=\underbrace{\sum_{n=1}^{t}\frac{E_1(1-\rho)(1+\rho R_n)^{,n-1}}{(1+k)^n}}_{\text{阶段一：分红折现}}+_
_\underbrace{\frac{E_1(1+\rho R_t)^{t}}{k(1+k)^t}}_{\text{阶段二：永久年金}}
$$

- 前半部分：把$$t$$年内的“超额红利”一笔笔折现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- First part: discount each “excess dividend” over the $$t$$ years.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- 后半部分：第$$t$$年末起，公司等价于一只普通企业（$$R_n=k$$），因此股值是永续$$E_t/k$$的折现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Second part: from the end of year $$t$$ onward, the firm earns only a normal return ($$R_n=k$$), so its remaining equity value is the discounted perpetuity $$E_t/k$$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

### **3. 小示例**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $$E_1=120$$，$$\rho=40\%$$，$$R_n=20\%$$（$$n\le3$$），$$k=10\%$$，$$t=3$$。
    
- 计算可得$$p_0\approx1,070$$（略去逐期计算过程）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The calculation gives $p_0\approx1,070$; the period-by-period arithmetic is omitted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- 若把$$R_n$$直接设为$$k$$（无护城河），价值仅$$p_0\approx800$$；差额$$270$$就是**三年竞争优势的现值**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- If $$R_n$$ is directly set to $$k$$ (no moat), the value is approximately $$p_0 \approx 800$$. The difference of $$270$$ represents the **present value of three years of competitive advantage**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


**公司资源**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Company Resources**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 公司的有形资产、无形资产和组织资本共同构成了公司的资源 。
• 只有能够超越竞争对手的资源才是有价值的资源，资源的价值可从**稀缺性**、**持久性**和**不可替代性**三个方面进行判断 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• The tangible assets, intangible assets, and organizational capital of a company collectively constitute its resources.
• A resource is strategically valuable only if it helps the firm outperform competitors. Assess it in terms of **scarcity**, **durability**, and **non-substitutability**:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **稀缺性**：来源于资源的物质唯一性、路径依赖性、因果模糊性和经济制约性 。
• **持久性**：取决于资源的流动性。有形资源容易被模仿，无形资源则不易被模仿 。
• **不可替代性**：资源难以被替代或复制 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Scarcity:** may arise from physical uniqueness, path dependence, causal ambiguity, or economic constraints.
• **Durability:** depends on how readily the resource can move or be copied. Tangible resources are often easier to imitate than intangible ones.
• **Non-substitutability:** competitors cannot readily replace or replicate the resource.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**核心能力**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Core Competencies**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 核心能力是使公司能够进入不同市场并获得机会的一种能力，它通过公司的最终产品为顾客所感知，并且不易被竞争者模仿 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Core competencies enable a firm to enter different markets and capture new opportunities. Customers perceive their effects in the firm's final products, yet competitors find the underlying capabilities difficult to imitate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 核心能力是公司在特定经营环境中的竞争能力和竞争优势等方面的知识、技能以及技术体系、管理体系、价值观念与行为规范的有机组合，是用于识别和提供竞争优势的知识体系 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A core competence is an integrated system of knowledge, skills, technology, management practices, values, and behavioural norms through which a firm identifies and delivers competitive advantage in its operating environment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 核心能力的特征包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Core competencies have four important characteristics:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **有价值**：有助于为顾客创造价值，带来长期关键利益，能够为公司创造超额利润 。
• **异质性**：同时具有技术特征和组织特性，很难被竞争对手完全了解或轻易复制，而且无法通过市场交易获得 。
• **扩展性**：为公司提供进入各类市场的潜力，是发展新业务的引擎，决定公司多样化经营策略，也是差异化竞争优势的源泉 。
• **动态性**：公司的发展战略需要根据形势适时提升公司的核心能力 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Value:** they create benefits for customers and support long-run excess profit.
• **Heterogeneity:** their technological and organisational components are hard for competitors to understand, copy, or buy in the market.
• **Extensibility:** they open access to multiple markets, support new businesses, shape diversification, and underpin differentiation.
• **Dynamism:** the firm must adapt its strategy and renew its competencies as conditions change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2资产重组
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2 Asset Restructuring*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**资产重组的方式主要包括** ：扩张型资产重组、调整型资产重组、控股权变更型公司重组。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**The main forms of asset restructuring are:** expansionary restructuring, adjustment-oriented restructuring, and restructuring associated with a change of control.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **扩张型资产重组**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Expansionary asset restructuring**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 以扩大公司经营规模和资产规模为目的的重组行为 。
• 方式：通过购买房地产、债权、业务部门、生产线、商标等有形或无形资产来扩张 。
• 实效：重组后公司的主营业务收入和净利润会有一定程度的增长，但盈利能力并未同步提高 。
• 战略作用：通过扩大市场规模和生产规模来降低成本，从而巩固或增强公司的市场竞争力 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Purpose:** expand the scale of the firm's operations and asset base.
• **Method:** acquire tangible or intangible assets such as property, debt claims, business units, production lines, or trademarks.
• **Observed effect:** revenue and net profit may rise after restructuring even when profitability does not improve proportionately.
• **Strategic role:** greater market reach and production scale may lower costs and strengthen the firm's competitive position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **调整型资产重组**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Adjustment-oriented asset restructuring**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 不改变控制权的股权置换
• 股权与资产置换
• 不改变公司资产规模的资产置换
• 缩小公司规模的资产出售
• 公司分立
• 资产剥离（将不良或非核心资产从公司剥离）
• 注意鉴别“报表性重组”和“实质性重组”（区分仅通过会计处理实现的重组与真正改善公司运营的重组） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Equity Swap Without Changing Control
• Equity and Asset Swap
• Asset swaps that leave the total asset base unchanged
• Asset Disposal to Reduce Company Size
• Corporate demergers
• Asset divestitures that remove non-core or impaired assets
• Distinguish **accounting-only restructuring** from **substantive restructuring** that genuinely improves operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **控股权变更型公司重组**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* **Restructuring following a change of control**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 国有股的无偿划拨
• 股权协议转让
• 公司股权托管和公司托管
• 股份回购
• 表决权信托与委托书收购
• 交叉控股
• **控制权变更后的整合**：控股权变更后需进行恰当的经营重组以发挥重组效益 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Transfer of state-owned shares without consideration
* Transfer of shares by agreement
* Entrusted custody of shares or the company
* Share repurchase
* Voting trusts and proxy contests
* Cross-shareholding
* **Post-control integration:** after control changes, the firm needs genuine operational restructuring to realise the expected benefits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**关联交易**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Related party transactions**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **定义**：关联交易是指公司与其关联方之间发生的交换资产、提供商品或劳务的交易行为 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Definition:** a related-party transaction is an exchange of assets, goods, or services between a company and a related party.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **经营活动中的关联交易**：例如关联方之间的购销，费用负担的转嫁，资产租赁，资金占用，信用担保，研发经费转移，许可协议，关键管理人员报酬等 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* **Operating transactions with related parties:** examples include purchases and sales, shifting expenses between entities, asset leases, use of company funds, credit guarantees, transfers of R&D funding, licence agreements, and compensation of key managers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **资产重组中的关联交易**：例如资产转让与置换，托管经营、承包经营，合作投资，相互持股等 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* **Related-party transactions in restructuring:** examples include asset transfers and swaps, entrusted or contracted operations, joint investment, and cross-holdings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3.绝对估值法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Absolute Valuation Method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通股的估值有两种基本方法：现值法和市盈率估值（P/E）法 。绝对估值法指通过贴现预期现金流来评估股票的内在价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two broad approaches are introduced here: present-value valuation and price-to-earnings valuation. **Absolute valuation** estimates a stock's intrinsic value by discounting its expected future cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- equity-valuation-note:ddm:start -->

> [!note] DDM 的范围与稳定增长条件
> [[股利贴现模型|DDM]]是贴现现金流估值的一类，专门追踪股东分配，不是所有 DCF 的统称。暂时不分红仍可预测未来分配；若永不通过股利、回购、清算等把现金归于股东，便不能仅靠无限转售涨价支持同一现金流模型的正值。[[戈登增长模型]]还要求第一笔未来股利 $D_1$、可持续的恒定增长与 $k_e>g$；短期不稳定并不排除使用[[多阶段股利估值]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[股利贴现模型|DDM]] is a discounted-cash-flow model of shareholder distributions, not a name for all DCF valuation. No current dividend does not rule out future distributions. If shareholders never receive cash through dividends, repurchases or liquidation, endless resale appreciation alone cannot support positive value in the same cash-flow framework. The [[戈登增长模型|Gordon model]] needs next-period dividend $D_1$, sustainable constant growth and $k_e>g$; temporary instability can instead be treated with [[多阶段股利估值|multi-stage valuation]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- equity-valuation-note:ddm:end -->
## 3.1贴现现金流模型（DDM）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1 Dividend Discount Model (DDM)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **定义**：股票的理论价格等于股票未来预期现金流（股利）的现值 。股票的收益包括两个部分：股利和价差 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* **Definition:** a stock's theoretical price equals the present value of its expected future cash flows to shareholders. Over a holding period, return consists of dividends and capital gain or loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **一般模型**：假设只持有一period，股利 $D_1$ 在期末发放且期末股价为 $P_1$，则股票现价为：$$P_0 = \frac{D_1 + P_1}{1 + k}$$  根据例如：如果下一期股票价格 $P_1 = 110$ 元、股利 $D_1 = 5$ 元，市场必要收益率 $k = 15\%$，那么股票当前的理论价格 $P_0 = \frac{110 + 5}{1 + 0.15} = 100$ 元 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**General model:** suppose the stock is held for one period, pays dividend $D_1$ at the end of that period, and can then be sold for $P_1$. Its current value is $P_0 = \frac{D_1 + P_1}{1 + k}$. For example, if $P_1=110$ yuan, $D_1=5$ yuan, and the required return is $k=15\%$, then $P_0=\frac{110+5}{1+0.15}=100$ yuan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2稳定增长股利贴现模型（Gordon 模型）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2 Stable Growth Dividend Discount Model (Gordon Model)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设股利以固定增长率 $g$ 增长，则根据模型公式有：$$P_0 = \frac{D_1}{k - g}$$  。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assuming dividends grow at a fixed rate $g$, the model formula yields: $$P_0 = \frac{D_1}{k - g}$$ .
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **模型局限性**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Limitations of the Model:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 不适用于从不发放股利的公司；
2. 不适用于股利发放与公司收益没有直接联系的公司；
3. 对于没有交易历史的公司，不能直接估计自身历史 Beta；但仍可按 [[项目Beta匹配|纯业务可比公司法]] 估计与业务风险匹配的必要收益率；
4. 不适用于股利发放不稳定的公司。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Not applicable to companies that do not distribute dividends;<br>
**2.** Not applicable to companies where dividend payments have no direct correlation with company earnings;<br>
**3.** A company without trading history cannot supply its own historical beta, but a required return can still be estimated from [[项目Beta匹配|pure-play comparables]] matched to its operating risk;<br>
**4.** Not applicable to companies with unstable dividend payments.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **模型应用**：评估公司权益资本的必要收益率 $K_e$ 常采用 CAPM 模型进行计算（根据公司的无风险利率、贝塔值和市场风险溢价估计） 。
[[03_资产定价理论及其发展#2.1.3 基础资产定价理论]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Application:** the required return on equity, $K_e$, is often estimated with CAPM from the risk-free rate, the company's beta, and an estimate of the market risk premium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.3自由现金流贴现模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3 Free-Cash-Flow Discount Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **3.3.1 两种贴现视角**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3.1 Two Discounting Perspectives*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| **估值口径** | **贴现现金流** | **折现率** | **[[贴现率口径匹配|现值]]**              |
| -------- | --------- | ------- | ------------------- |
| 公司总价值    | $FCFF_t$  | $WACC$  | $V_{\text{Firm}}$   |
| 股权价值     | $FCFE_t$  | $K_e$   | $V_{\text{Equity}}$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| **Valuation Scope** | **Discounted Cash Flow** | **Discount Rate** | **[[贴现率口径匹配|present value]]**              |
| -------- | --------- | ------- | ------------------- |
| Total Firm Value    | $FCFF_t$  | $WACC$  | $V_{\text{Firm}}$   |
| Equity Value     | $FCFE_t$  | $K_e$   | $V_{\text{Equity}}$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $FCFF$：对“股权＋债权”全部资本提供者的自由现金流 
- $FCFE$：流向股东的自由现金流
- $WACC$：加权平均资本成本
- $K_e$：股权资本成本（常用CAPM计算）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $FCFF$: free cash flow available to all providers of debt and equity capital.
- $FCFE$: free cash flow available to equity holders.
- $WACC$: weighted average cost of capital.
- $K_e$: cost of equity, commonly estimated with CAPM.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.3.2 现金流的拆解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3.2 Decomposition of Cash Flows*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$FCFF_t = EBIT_t(1-T) + \text{Dep}_t - \Delta WC_t - CAPEX_t$
$FCFE_t = FCFF_t - \text{Interest}_t(1-T) + \text{Net Borrowing}_t$

通常情况下会给,不用自己算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In many exercises, these cash-flow inputs are supplied rather than derived from raw accounts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.3.3 折现率计算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3.3 Calculation of Discount Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$WACC = \dfrac{E}{D+E}K_e + \dfrac{D}{D+E}K_d(1-T)$

### 3.3.4 核心贴现公式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3.4 Core Discount Formula*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

V_{\text{Firm}} = \sum_{t=1}^{\infty} \frac{FCFF_t}{(1+WACC)^t},

\qquad

V_{\text{Equity}} = \sum_{t=1}^{\infty} \frac{FCFE_t}{(1+K_e)^t}

$$

# 4 相对估值法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4 Relative Valuation Method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相对估值法利用市场上可比公司的估值来推断目标公司的价值。在一个运行良好的市场中，对于未来盈利预期相同的资产，投资者会给予相同的估值 。相对估值法正是利用同行业公司之间的相似性，通过研究行业内可比公司的比率指标，并将其作为乘数乘以目标公司的价值驱动因素来计算公司价值 。其基本公式为：**公司价值 = 价值驱动因素 × 乘数** 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Relative valuation infers a target company's value from market valuations of comparable firms. In a well-functioning market, assets with similar expected earnings and risk should receive similar valuations. The analyst therefore selects an appropriate multiple from comparable companies and applies it to the target's corresponding value driver: **Company Value = Value Driver × Multiple**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **优点** ：计算简单；使用市场当前价格，能反映实时信息，提高估值准确性；对于少数股权投资是很实用的方法。
• **缺点** ：难以寻找充分可比的公司；估值结果的准确性受整体市场行情影响大；不同公司的会计政策选择可能影响比较的结果；如果只采用单一年度的盈余进行估值，无法体现公司未来发展潜力对价值的影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Advantages:** simple to calculate, incorporates current market information, and is practical for valuing minority equity stakes.
• **Disadvantages:** truly comparable firms may be hard to find; market-wide overvaluation or undervaluation affects the result; accounting-policy differences reduce comparability; and a single year's earnings may not capture long-run growth potential.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- equity-valuation-note:pe-peg:start -->

> [!note] PE 的价值单位与 PEG 的增长单位
> [[市盈率]]乘每股收益得到每股价格，不是公司股权总额。静态、TTM和forward必须注明各自盈利期间；预测PE使用哪一年EPS，应与可比公司保持一致。其增长、支付和风险条件见[[市盈率基本面约束]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[市盈率|P/E]] times EPS gives value per share, not total equity value. Specify the earnings period for fiscal-year, TTM and forward measures, and align forecast years across comparables. See the [[市盈率基本面约束|fundamental P/E conditions]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[PEG]]的分母使用增长的百分数值：20%增长对应小数 $g=0.20$，但分母是 $100g=20$，不是20%。例如PE30对应PEG1.5。原式多余标点及 $100\%\times g$ 不完成这个单位转换；PEG1也不是通用的价值／成长分界或低估保证，风险、支付和增长期限仍须按[[PEG比较边界]]检查。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[PEG|PEG]] uses the percentage number: 20% growth is decimal $g=0.20$, but the denominator is $100g=20$, not 20%. P/E of 30 therefore gives PEG of 1.5. The recorded punctuation and $100\%\times g$ do not perform this conversion. PEG of 1 is not a universal value/growth boundary or an undervaluation guarantee; examine risk, payout and forecast duration through the [[PEG比较边界|comparison boundary]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- equity-valuation-note:pe-peg:end -->
# 4.1   [[市盈率|市盈率]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1   [[市盈率|P/E Ratio]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **定义**：市盈率（P/E）是常用的投资评价指标，定义为每股股票价格与每股盈利（EPS）的比率，即：$$\text{市盈率} = \frac{P}{EPS}$$ 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Definition**: P/E Ratio (P/E) is a commonly used investment evaluation metric defined as the ratio of the stock price per share to the earnings per share (EPS), given by: $$\text{P/E Ratio} = \frac{P}{EPS}$$ .
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **类型**：市盈率分为滚动市盈率、动态市盈率和静态市盈率等类别 。
	1. 静态市盈率过去一个财年
	2. 滚动市盈率,过去12个月
	3. 动态市盈率则对静态市盈率进行调整以反映未来增长（增长系数取决于每股收益增长率 $i$ 和持续增长年数 $n$ 等因素) 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Types:** the note distinguishes static, trailing, and forward or growth-adjusted P/E ratios.

**1.** **Static P/E:** based on the last completed fiscal year.<br>
**2.** **Trailing P/E:** based on earnings over the most recent 12 months.<br>
**3.** **Forward or dynamic P/E:** adjusts the historical measure to reflect expected growth, using assumptions such as EPS growth rate $i$ and the number of years $n$ for which growth persists.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **估值计算**：公司股权价值可以用可比公司的市盈率乘以本公司每股收益来估计，即：$$\text{股权价值} = (\text{可比公司}~P/E) \times \text{公司每股收益}$$ 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Valuation:** estimate the target's share value by applying a comparable-company P/E multiple to the target's EPS: $$\text{Equity Value} = (\text{Comparable Company's } P/E) \times \text{Company's Earnings Per Share}$$ .
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **增长与市盈率**：市盈率与盈利增长的关系通常用市盈率相对盈利增长比（PEG）来衡量，$$PEG = \frac{P/E}{\text{年复合增长率} \times 100]\%}$$ 。一般认为，价值型公司 PEG < 1，成长型公司 PEG > 1，但需结合同行业公司作比较参考 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Growth and P/E:** the PEG ratio relates the P/E multiple to earnings growth: $$PEG = \frac{P/E}{\text{Annual Compound Growth Rate} \times 100\%}$$ . The note uses PEG below 1 as a rough value signal and PEG above 1 as a growth signal, but the ratio is meaningful only when compared with similar firms in the same industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- equity-valuation-note:multiple-scope:start -->

> [!note] 账面、收入与经营倍数的适用条件
> 后文PB差额式是[[市净率基本面约束|条件模型]]，不是所有市场价格的恒等式：固定股数、清洁盈余、正的期初每股账面权益 $B_0$，下一期盈利 $E_1=ROE\,B_0$；稳定留存 $0\le b<1$、恒定的正ROE给出 $g=bROE$，另须对恒定股权成本满足 $g<k_e$ 的收敛条件。不能直接用任意历史平均ROE替代这里的预期ROE。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The later P/B spread formula is a [[市净率基本面约束|conditional model]], not an identity for all market prices. It requires fixed shares, clean surplus, positive opening book equity per share $B_0$, and next-period earnings $E_1=ROE\,B_0$. Stable retention $0\le b<1$ and constant positive ROE give $g=bROE$; convergence separately requires $g<k_e$ for the constant equity cost. An arbitrary historical average ROE is not the expected ROE used here.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[市销率]]可以在亏损但有正收入时计算，不表示收入确认天然可靠或任何时期都适用。[[EV-EBITDA倍数]]则要求经营范围、预测期间及资本权利与分母一致：另列现金和持股、债务与其他权利均须核对；不能将总净利润直接加回几项就假定经营范围已匹配。资本密集型企业仍需要维护资本开支，EBITDA没有扣掉这项支出，不能据行业名称认定该倍数最合适。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[市销率|P/S]] can be computed with losses and positive revenue, without proving revenue reliability or universal applicability. [[EV-EBITDA倍数|EV/EBITDA]] requires matched operations, forecast periods and capital claims: reconcile separately valued cash and holdings, debt and other rights rather than assuming that a few add-backs to total net profit ensure operating consistency. Capital-intensive businesses still require maintenance capex, which EBITDA has not deducted; an industry label does not establish the best multiple.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4.2市净率估值模型 (P/B)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2 Price-to-Book Valuation Model (P/B)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **定义**：[[市净率|市净率]]（P/B）是每股股票价格与每股净资产（Book Value per Share, BVPS）的比值 。即：$$P/B = \frac{P}{BVPS}$$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Definition**: [[市净率|P/B Ratio]] (P/B) is the ratio of the stock price to the book value per share (BVPS). That is: $$P/B = \frac{P}{BVPS}$$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **推导关系** ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Derivation:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• $$P/B = 1 + \frac{ROE - k}{k - g}$$

• $$P/B \times BVPS = BVPS + \frac{ROE - k}{k - g} \times BVPS$$

• $$P = BVPS + \frac{ROE - k}{k - g} \times BVPS$$

以上推导表明，$\frac{ROE - k}{k - g} \times BVPS$ 部分代表资本市场愿意为该股票支付的溢价 （当 $ROE > k$ 时有溢价，当 $ROE = k$ 时 $P = BVPS$）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivation shows that $\frac{ROE - k}{k - g} \times BVPS$ is the premium the capital market is willing to pay above book value. When $ROE > k$, the stock commands a premium; when $ROE = k$, $P = BVPS$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.3市销率 (P/S)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3 Price-to-Sales Ratio (P/S)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **定义**：[[市销率|市销率]]（P/S）= 公司市值 / 销售收入 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Definition**: [[市销率|Price-to-Sales Ratio]] (P/S) = Company Market Value / Revenue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **优点** ：市销率在任何时期都可使用；销售收入相对于利润而言不易被操纵，真实性更强；且市销率通常较为稳定，不会像盈利指标那样波动剧烈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Advantages:** P/S remains usable when earnings are negative; revenue is generally harder to manipulate than profit; and the ratio is often more stable than earnings-based measures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **局限性** ：无法反映成本结构不同但销售收入相同的公司之间的差异，忽略了盈利能力的区别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Limitation:** firms with the same revenue can have very different cost structures and profitability, which P/S does not capture.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **适用性**：市销率估值模型适用于已经发展到一定规模且拥有稳定收入的公司。市销率常被用于互联网、通信设备、公共事业、制药等行业公司的估值参考 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Applicability:** The price-to-sales valuation model suits companies that have reached a meaningful scale and generate stable revenue. It is often used as a valuation reference for internet, telecommunications-equipment, utility, and pharmaceutical companies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.4公司价值乘数模型 (EV/EBITDA)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.4 Firm Value Multiplier Model (EV/EBITDA)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **定义**：公司价值乘数是公司价值与息税折旧摊销前利润（EBITDA）的比率，即 EV/EBITDA。 其中公司价值 (Enterprise Value, EV) = 股权价值 + 净债务（债务 - 现金） ，EBITDA = 净利润 + 所得税 + 利息 + 折旧 + 摊销 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Definition:** EV/EBITDA divides enterprise value by earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortisation. Here, enterprise value equals equity value plus net debt, where net debt is debt minus cash; EBITDA can be reconciled from net profit by adding back tax, interest, depreciation, and amortisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **估值计算**：目标公司的价值可以通过可比公司的 EV/EBITDA 乘以目标公司的 EBITDA 来估算 ：$$\text{目标公司价值} = (\text{可比公司 EV/EBITDA}) \times \text{目标公司 EBITDA}$$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Valuation Calculation**: The value of a target company can be estimated by multiplying the EV/EBITDA of comparable companies with the EBITDA of the target company: $$\text{Target Company Value} = (\text{Comparable Company EV/EBITDA}) \times \text{Target Company EBITDA}$$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **适用范围**：公司价值乘数模型适用于资本密集型行业的公司估值，如电信、钢铁、石化、航空等行业 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Scope:** EV/EBITDA is often used for capital-intensive sectors such as telecommunications, steel, petrochemicals, and aviation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
