## 1.1公司基本情况分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1 Company Background Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分析公司所处的产业、公司的主要经营业务 。包括公司产品和服务的情况、产品所处的生命周期；消费者如何使用公司的产品和服务；客户及其类型；生产要素的供给状况；生产的组织形式和技术水平；公司的组织结构；营销和销售策略等 。
• **竞争对手分析**：分析公司主要竞争对手的各方面情况 ：
• 竞争对手的目标和战略 ；
• 经营状况和财务状况 ；
• 技术经济实力 ；
• 领导者和管理者素质 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Analyze the industry in which the company operates and its main business activities. This includes information on the company's products and services, their stage of life cycle; how consumers use the company's products and services; customer types and demographics; supply conditions for production factors; organizational forms and technological levels of production; the company's organizational structure; marketing and sales strategies, etc.

• **Competitor Analysis**: Analyze the various aspects of the company's main competitors:
• Their goals and strategies;
• Operating and financial conditions;
• Techno-economic strength;
• Qualifications of leaders and managers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **公司在产业中的竞争地位（SWOT分析）**：分析公司的优势、劣势、机会、威胁 。
• 优势（内部因素）：资金充足、公司形象良好、技术力量强、规模经济、产品质量高、市场份额大、成本优势等 。
• 劣势（内部因素）：设备老化、技术薄弱、管理混乱、经营不善、竞争力差等 。
• 机会（外部因素）：新产品、新市场、新技术、新需求出现，市场壁垒解除，竞争对手失误等 。
• 威胁（外部因素）：竞争对手增多、替代产品出现、市场紧缩、产业政策变化、经济衰退、消费者偏好改变、突发事件等 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* **Positioning of the Company in the Industry (SWOT Analysis)**: Analyze the company's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.
* **Strengths (Internal Factors)**: Adequate funding, good corporate image, strong technical capabilities, economies of scale, high-quality products, large market share, cost advantages, etc.
* **Weaknesses (Internal Factors)**: Outdated equipment, weak technology, chaotic management, poor business performance, low competitiveness, etc.
* **Opportunities (External Factors)**: The emergence of new products, new markets, new technologies, new demands, removal of market barriers, competitors' mistakes, etc.
* **Threats (External Factors)**: Increased competition, the appearance of substitute products, market contraction, changes in industry policies, economic downturns, shifts in consumer preferences, emergencies, etc.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **公司经济区位分析**：关注公司所处经济区位的条件和影响因素 。经济区位是指地理范畴的经济增长点和辐射范围，包括：区位内的自然条件和基础设施条件；区位内政府相关政策；区位内的经济特色（例如深圳的高新技术产业、福建的对外贸易特色） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Company Economic Location Analysis**: Focuses on the conditions and influencing factors of the economic location where a company is situated. An economic location refers to the economic growth point and its radiation range within a geographical area, encompassing: natural and infrastructural conditions within the location; government policies within the location; and economic characteristics (such as high-tech industries in Shenzhen and export-oriented trade in Fujian).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2公司竞争地位分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2 Analysis of Company Competitive Position*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (1)成本领先战略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Cost Leadership Strategy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

企业的目标是成为行业中的低成本生产厂商 。实现成本领先通常来源于规模经济、专有技术、优惠的原材料、廉价劳动力等因素 。例如，台积电就是成功实施成本领先战略的案例 。逻辑要求是企业成为唯一的成本领先者，以显著的成本优势获取竞争优势 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The goal of a company is to become a low-cost producer in its industry. Achieving cost leadership typically stems from factors such as scale economies, proprietary technology, favorable raw materials, and cheap labor. For instance, TSMC is a case study of a successful cost-leadership strategy. The logic requires that a company becomes the sole cost leader to gain competitive advantage through significant cost advantages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (2)差异化战略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Differentiation Strategy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

企业力求在一种或多种为许多客户所看重的特质上独树一帜，从而获得高于行业平均水平的收益 。差异化可以基于产品本身的独特属性、卓越的销售和交付体系、特殊的营销渠道以及其他使企业经营独具特色的因素 。其逻辑要求是企业选择有利于竞争且能使自身经营独具特色的特质进行培养 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A firm seeks to stand out in one or more traits that many customers value highly, thereby achieving above-average returns. Differentiation can stem from unique attributes of the product, an outstanding sales and delivery system, distinctive marketing channels, and other factors that make the business operation uniquely its own. The logic behind this is that a company should choose traits that are advantageous for competition and that allow its operations to be uniquely its own.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (3)目标集聚战略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Concentrated Objective Strategy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集聚战略（目标集聚战略）指企业聚焦于行业内的某一细分市场，通过**成本集聚战略**或**差异集聚战略**获取该细分市场的竞争优势 。实施集聚战略的企业选择一个或一组细分市场提供产品或服务，而不服务于整个市场 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strategic Concentration (Target Concentration Strategy) refers to a business focusing on a particular segment within an industry, leveraging either a cost concentration strategy or a differentiation concentration strategy to gain competitive advantage in that segment. Enterprises implementing strategic concentration choose to provide products or services to one or a group of segments rather than serving the entire market:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **成本集聚战略**：在目标市场上寻求成本优势 。
• **差异集聚战略**：在目标市场上追求差异优势 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Cost Concentration Strategy**: Seeking cost advantages in the target market.
• **Differentiation Concentration Strategy**: Pursuing differentiation advantages in the target market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3公司盈利能力分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3 Analysis of Company Profitability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **主要衡量指标**：评价公司盈利能力的常用指标包括资产报酬率、销售毛利率、[[Net Profit Margin|销售净利率]]、[[Return on Equity|净资产收益率]]、股东权益收益率等 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Main metrics**: Commonly used metrics to evaluate a company's profitability include asset return on investment, sales gross margin, [[Net Profit Margin|Net Profit Margin]], [[Return on Equity|Return on Equity]], and return on equity for shareholders.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **盈利能力预测**：可以根据公司过去的每股盈利（EPS）水平来预测未来每股盈利 ，并分析过去销售收入与每股盈利之间的相关关系 。公司盈利是销售收入（或营业收入）减去成本和费用后的余额，即：$$\text{公司盈利} = \text{销售收入} - \text{成本和费用}$$ 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* **Profitability Forecast**: One can predict future earnings per share (EPS) based on a company's past EPS levels and analyze the relationship between past sales revenue and EPS. Company profits are the balance after subtracting costs and expenses from sales revenue, i.e., $$\text{Company Profit} = \text{Sales Revenue} - \text{Costs and Expenses}$$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.4公司经营管理能力分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.4 Analysis of Company Management Capabilities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公司经营管理能力分析包括以下几个方面 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An analysis of corporate management capability includes the following aspects:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **公司法人治理结构**：公司的治理结构是否规范健全，包括股权结构是否规范、董事会决策机制是否有效、独立董事制度是否完善、监事会是否独立履行监督责任、经理层是否优秀，以及相关利益者是否参与治理等 。
• **经理层的素质**：公司的高级管理人员应具备从事管理工作的意愿、专业技术能力、良好的道德品质、人际关系协调能力，以及观念更新和持续学习的能力等 。
• **从业人员的素质和创新能力**：公司的员工应具备专业技术能力、对企业的忠诚度和责任感，具有团队合作精神和创新能力等 。
• **经营效率**：关注企业运营的高效性。例如：生产设备开工率是否达到额定标准，人均产量和人均销售额是否高于主要竞争对手，资产周转率和毛利率是否高于主要竞争对手，等等 。
• **人力资源管理**：是否建立了合理有效的激励制度，是否做到内部人才合理流动、任人唯贤，能否稳定留住企业内部的核心人才和员工队伍 。
• **内部控制机制**：内部控制制度是否覆盖企业生产经营管理的各个方面，并有效影响企业各内部组织机构的运作 。
• **生产运作管理**：企业是否根据目标制定各项生产计划，合理安排生产任务，适时调整生产规模以及产品品种、质量、规格、数量和产出时间等，以满足经营目标 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Corporate Governance Structure**: The structure of corporate governance is whether it is standardized and sound, including whether the equity structure is standardized, whether the decision-making mechanism of the board of directors is effective, whether the independent director system is complete, whether the supervisory committee fulfills its supervisory responsibilities independently, whether the management team is excellent, and whether relevant stakeholders participate in governance.
• **Managerial Qualifications**: Senior managers should possess a willingness to engage in management work, professional management capabilities, good moral character, interpersonal coordination skills, as well as the ability for concept updating and continuous learning.
• **Qualifications and Innovation Ability of Employees**: Employees should possess professional management capabilities, loyalty and responsibility towards the company, and teamwork spirit and innovation abilities.
• **Operational Efficiency**: Focus on the efficiency of enterprise operations.For example: Is the production equipment utilization rate up to the standard? Are the average output per person and average sales revenue higher than those of major competitors? Are asset turnover rates and gross margins higher than those of major competitors, and so on.

• **Human Resource Management**: Has a reasonable and effective incentive system been established? Is internal talent flow and the selection of capable individuals done reasonably? Can core employees and the workforce be stably retained within the company?

• **Internal Control Mechanism**: Is the internal control system comprehensive enough to cover all aspects of the enterprise's production and operation management, and does it effectively influence the operations of various internal organizational units?

• **Production Operations Management**: Does the company formulate production plans according to its goals, reasonably allocate production tasks, adjust production scales and product varieties, quality, specifications, quantities, and output times in a timely manner to meet business objectives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.7公司竞争优势分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.7 Analysis of Company Competitive Advantage*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 公司的总价值$$V$$可以分成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The total value $V$ of the company can be divided into:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    1. **现有资产价值**（不再追加投资时的现金奶牛）；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. **Current Asset Value** (Cash Cow When No Additional Investment is Made)
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    2. **增长价值**（继续追加资本、并在保持$$ROIC>w$$的前提下赚取超额收益）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
2. **Growth Value** (adding capital continuously while earning excess returns as long as $$ROIC > w$$ holds).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    
- 当$$NOPAT$$（税后经营利润）与投入资本$$C$$都按固定增速$$g$$扩张时，米勒-莫迪利安尼（M&M）公式被化简成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- When both the tax-adjusted operating profit ($$NOPAT$$) and the invested capital $$C$$ grow at a fixed rate $$g$$, Miller-Morckian (M&M) formula is simplified to:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    $$
    
    V=NOPAT_1\left(\frac1w+\underbrace{\frac{ROIC-w}{wROIC}}_{\text{独特优势}}\times\underbrace{\frac{g}{w-g}}_{\text{增长因子}}\right)
    
    $$
    
    其中$$w$$是加权平均资本成本$$WACC$$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $w$ is the weighted average cost of capital, $WACC$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- 科登-戈登提出的“两阶段成长模型”则进一步强调：**竞争优势终将衰减**。因此把生命周期分成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The "two-stage growth model" proposed by Kotter and John further emphasizes that **competitive advantage will eventually wane**. Thus, the lifecycle is divided into:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    1. $$n\le t$$：再投资回报率$$R_n>k$$（超额收益阶段）；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. For $n\le t$, the return on reinvestment satisfies $R_n>k$: the excess-return phase.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    2. $$n>t$$：$$R_n=k$$（优势消失，回报率仅够覆盖股权成本）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
2. $$n > t$$: $$R_n = k$$ (Advantage disappears; return rate is just enough to cover equity costs).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    
    对应股票理论价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Corresponding Stock Theoretical Value
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    $$
    
    p_0=\sum_{n=1}^{t}\frac{E_1(1-\rho)(1+\rho R_n)^{n-1}}{(1+k)^n}+\frac{E_1(1+\rho R_t)^t}{k(1+k)^t}
    
    $$
    
    其中$$E_1$$为下一年股净收益，$$\rho$$为留存收益率，$$k$$为股权成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $E_1$ is next year's net income attributable to equity, $\rho$ is the retention ratio, and $k$ is the cost of equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

米勒-莫迪利安尼价值分解公式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Miller-Madow Value Decomposition Formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **1. 原式**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Original Expression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

V=\frac{NOPAT_t}{w}+\sum_{t=1}^{\infty}\frac{(ROIC_t-w),\[[Delta|Delta]] C_t}{w(1+w)^t}

$$

|**记号**|**含义**|
|---|---|
|$NOPAT_t$|第$$t$$期税后经营利润|
|$w$|企业的$$WACC$$|
|$ROIC_t$|投入资本回报率|
|$\[[Delta|Delta]] C_t$|第$$t$$期新增投资额|
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| **Symbol** | **Meaning** |
| --- | --- |
| $NOPAT_t$ | Net operating profit after tax in period $t$ |
| $w$ | The firm's weighted average cost of capital, $WACC$ |
| $ROIC_t$ | Return on invested capital in period $t$ |
| $\Delta C_t$ | New investment in period $t$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **第一项**：假设企业立刻停止一切扩张，把当前资本当“摇钱树”运转，就能折现出这一块“现有资产价值”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **First**, assume that the enterprise immediately stops all expansion and uses its current capital as a "money tree" to generate income. This would then reveal the "value of existing assets".
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **第二项**：若企业继续追加$$\[[Delta|Delta]] C_t$$，其中每$$1$$元能赚到$$ROIC_t$$，超出资本成本$$w$$的部分即超额收益。对其折现累加就得到“增长价值”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Second item**: If the company continues to add $$C_t$$, where each $1 earns a return on invested capital ($$ROIC_t$$), the excess returns beyond the cost of capital $$w$$ constitute the additional income. Summing up these discounted excess returns yields the "growth value".
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

### **2. 恒速增长化简**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Constant Growth Simplification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

若$$NOPAT$$与$$C$$都以同一固定增速$$g$$增长，且$$ROIC_t$$恒为$$ROIC$$，可推得
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If both $$NOPAT$$ and $$C$$ grow at a constant fixed rate $$g$$, and if $$ROIC_t$$ is constant at $$ROIC$$, then we can deduce that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

$$

V=NOPAT_1!\Bigl(\frac1w+\frac{ROIC-w}{w,ROIC}\times\frac{g}{w-g}\Bigr)

$$

- **独特优势系数**：$$(ROIC-w)/(w,ROIC)$$ —— $ROIC$相对$w$越高，企业护城河越宽。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Unique Competitive Advantage Coefficient**: $$(ROIC-w)/(w,ROIC)$$ —— $ROIC$ Higher relative to $w$ indicates a wider moat for the enterprise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **增长因子**：$$g/(w-g)$$ —— 再投资节奏越快（$g$越大），成长杠杆越显著。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Growth Factor**: $$\frac{g}{(w-g)}$$ —— The reinvestment rate is faster ($g$ larger), the growth leverage is more significant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

> **如果$$ROIC=w$$（没有超额收益），第二项为零，企业只有现有资产价值；这就解释了为什么“竞争优势”值钱——它使$$ROIC>w$$从而拉高第二项。**
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **If $ROIC=w$, so there is no excess return, the second term is zero and the firm has only the value of its existing assets. This explains why a competitive advantage is valuable: it allows $ROIC>w$ and raises the second term.**
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Kotler-Gordon Two-Stage Competitive Advantage Model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **1. 模型思路**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Model Approach*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **阶段一（$$1\le n\le t$$）**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. **Stage One ($$1 \leq n \leq t$$)**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    - 企业仍具备护城河，追加投资回报率$$R_n>k$$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The enterprise still possesses a moat, with the incremental investment return rate $R_n > k$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - 留存收益率$$\rho$$决定再投资规模，每年股净收益 $$E_n=E_1(1+\rho R_n)^{n-1}$$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The retention yield $\rho$ determines the reinvestment size, with annual net equity income given by $E_n = E_1 (1 + \rho R_n)^{n-1}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    
2. **阶段二（$$n>t$$）**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
2. **Stage Two ($$n > t$$)**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    - 护城河被竞争磨平，$$R_n=k$$，再投资只赚到正常收益，不再创造超额价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The moat has been eroded by competition, $$R_n = k$$, and further investments yield only normal returns without creating excess value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    

### **2. 估值公式**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Valuation Formula*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p_0=\underbrace{\sum_{n=1}^{t}\frac{E_1(1-\rho)(1+\rho R_n)^{,n-1}}{(1+k)^n}}_{\text{阶段一：分红折现}}+_
_\underbrace{\frac{E_1(1+\rho R_t)^{t}}{k(1+k)^t}}_{\text{阶段二：永久年金}}
$$

- 前半部分：把$$t$$年内的“超额红利”一笔笔折现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- First part: discount each “excess dividend” over the $$t$$ years.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- 后半部分：第$$t$$年末起，公司等价于一只普通企业（$$R_n=k$$），因此股值是永续$$E_t/k$$的折现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The latter part: starting from the end of year $$t$$, the company is equivalent to a regular enterprise ($$R_n = k$$), so the stock value is the discounted perpetuity of $$E_t / k$$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**公司资源**

**Company Resources**

• 公司的有形资产、无形资产和组织资本共同构成了公司的资源 。
• 只有能够超越竞争对手的资源才是有价值的资源，资源的价值可从**稀缺性**、**持久性**和**不可替代性**三个方面进行判断 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• The tangible assets, intangible assets, and organizational capital of a company collectively constitute its resources.
• Only resources that can outperform competitors are valuable resources, and the value of these resources can be assessed from three aspects: **scarcity**, **permanence**, and **uniqueness**:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **稀缺性**：来源于资源的物质唯一性、路径依赖性、因果模糊性和经济制约性 。
• **持久性**：取决于资源的流动性。有形资源容易被模仿，无形资源则不易被模仿 。
• **不可替代性**：资源难以被替代或复制 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Scarcity**: Derived from the unique nature of resources, path dependence, causal ambiguity, and economic constraints.
• **Durability**: Dependent on the liquidity of resources. Tangible resources can be easily imitated, whereas intangible resources are harder to imitate.
• **Uniqueness**: Resources are difficult to substitute or replicate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Core competencies are abilities that enable a company to enter different markets and seize opportunities, perceived by customers from the company's final product, and are difficult for competitors to imitate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 核心能力是公司在特定经营环境中的竞争能力和竞争优势等方面的知识、技能以及技术体系、管理体系、价值观念与行为规范的有机组合，是用于识别和提供竞争优势的知识体系 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Core competencies are an organic combination of knowledge, skills, and technological systems, management systems, value orientations, and behavioral norms that a company possesses in specific operating environments to identify and provide competitive advantages. These core competencies constitute a knowledge system aimed at recognizing and delivering such advantages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 核心能力的特征包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Characteristics of core capabilities include:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **有价值**：有助于为顾客创造价值，带来长期关键利益，能够为公司创造超额利润 。
• **异质性**：同时具有技术特征和组织特性，很难被竞争对手完全了解或轻易复制，而且无法通过市场交易获得 。
• **扩展性**：为公司提供进入各类市场的潜力，是发展新业务的引擎，决定公司多样化经营策略，也是差异化竞争优势的源泉 。
• **动态性**：公司的发展战略需要根据形势适时提升公司的核心能力 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Valuable**: Helps create value for customers, bringing long-term key benefits, and enabling the company to generate excess profits.
• **Heterogeneity**: It possesses both technological and organizational characteristics that are difficult for competitors to fully understand or easily replicate, and cannot be acquired through market transactions.
• **Scalability**: Provides potential for entering various markets, serving as a driving force for developing new businesses, determining the company's diversification strategy, and also being a source of differentiated competitive advantage.
• **Dynamicity**: The company's development strategies need to be adjusted in response to changing circumstances to enhance its core capabilities.

# 3.绝对估值法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Absolute Valuation Method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通股的估值有两种基本方法：现值法和市盈率估值（P/E）法 。绝对估值法指通过贴现预期现金流来评估股票的内在价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The valuation of common stocks can be approached in two basic ways: the present value method and the P/E (Price-Earnings) valuation (price-to-earnings ratio). Absolute valuation methods assess a stock's intrinsic value by discounting expected cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1贴现现金流模型（DDM）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1 Discounted Cash Flow Model (DCF)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **定义**：股票的理论价格等于股票未来预期现金流（股利）的现值 。股票的收益包括两个部分：股利和价差 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Definition: The theoretical price of a stock equals the present value of its future expected cash flows (dividends). Stock returns consist of two parts: dividends and capital gains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **一般模型**：假设只持有一period，股利 $D_1$ 在期末发放且期末股价为 $P_1$，则股票现价为：$$P_0 = \frac{D_1 + P_1}{1 + k}$$  根据例如：如果下一期股票价格 $P_1 = 110$ 元、股利 $D_1 = 5$ 元，市场必要收益率 $k = 15\%$，那么股票当前的理论价格 $P_0 = \frac{110 + 5}{1 + 0.15} = 100$ 元 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**General model**: Assuming that only one period is held, dividends $D_1$ are paid at the end of the period and the closing price is $P_1$, then the current price of the stock is: $P_0 = \frac{D_1 + P_1}{1 + k}$$According to, for example, if the next period stock price $P_1 = 110$ yuan, dividends $D_1 = 5$ yuan, market necessary return $k = 15\%$ yuan, then the current theoretical price of the stock $P_0 = \frac{110 + 5}{1 + 0.15} = 100$ yuan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2稳定增长股利贴现模型（Gordon 模型）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2 Stable Growth Dividend Discount Model (Gordon Model)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设股利以固定增长率 $g$ 增长，则根据模型公式有：$$P_0 = \frac{D_1}{k - g}$$  。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assuming dividends grow at a fixed rate $g$, the model formula yields: $$P_0 = \frac{D_1}{k - g}$$ .
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **模型局限性**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Limitations of the Model:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 不适用于从不发放股利的公司；
2. 不适用于股利发放与公司收益没有直接联系的公司；
3. 对于没有交易历史的公司，无法使用 [[CAPM|CAPM]] 来估计必要收益率；
4. 不适用于股利发放不稳定的公司。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Not applicable to companies that do not distribute dividends;
2. Not applicable to companies where dividend payments have no direct correlation with company earnings;
3. For companies without trading history, [[CAPM|CAPM]] cannot be used to estimate the required yield;
4. Not applicable to companies with unstable dividend payments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **模型应用**：评估公司权益资本的必要收益率 $K_e$ 常采用 [[CAPM|CAPM]] 模型进行计算（根据公司的无风险利率、贝塔值和市场风险溢价估计） 。
[[03_资产定价理论及其发展#2.1.3 基础资产定价理论]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Model Application**: The required rate of return for a company's equity capital is typically assessed using the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM). This calculation involves the company's [[CAPM|CAPM]], Beta value, and an estimated market risk premium. $K_e$ employs the CAPM to determine the necessary yield (based on the company's risk-free rate, Beta, and market Echo estimate).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4 相对估值法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4 Relative Valuation Method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相对估值法利用市场上可比公司的估值来推断目标公司的价值。在一个运行良好的市场中，对于未来盈利预期相同的资产，投资者会给予相同的估值 。相对估值法正是利用同行业公司之间的相似性，通过研究行业内可比公司的比率指标，并将其作为乘数乘以目标公司的价值驱动因素来计算公司价值 。其基本公式为：**公司价值 = 价值驱动因素 × 乘数** 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Relative valuation relies on valuations of comparable companies in the market to infer the value of a target company. In a well-functioning market, assets with similar future earnings expectations receive similar valuations. Relative valuation leverages the similarities within an industry by studying ratio metrics of comparable companies within the industry and using these as multipliers to calculate the company's value based on its value drivers. The basic formula is: **Company Value = Value Drivers × Multiplier**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **优点** ：计算简单；使用市场当前价格，能反映实时信息，提高估值准确性；对于少数股权投资是很实用的方法。
• **缺点** ：难以寻找充分可比的公司；估值结果的准确性受整体市场行情影响大；不同公司的会计政策选择可能影响比较的结果；如果只采用单一年度的盈余进行估值，无法体现公司未来发展潜力对价值的影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Advantages** : Simple computation; uses current market prices to reflect real-time information, enhancing valuation accuracy; a practical method for valuing minority equity investments.
• **Disadvantages** : Difficult to find sufficiently comparable companies; valuation results can be significantly affected by overall market conditions; differences in accounting policies among companies may influence comparisons; using only annual earnings for valuation does not reflect the impact of future development potential on value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4.1   [[Price-to-Earnings Ratio|市盈率]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1   [[Price-to-Earnings Ratio|P/E Ratio]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **定义**：[[Price-to-Earnings Ratio|市盈率]]（P/E）是常用的投资评价指标，定义为每股股票价格与每股盈利（EPS）的比率，即：$$\text{[[Price-to-Earnings Ratio|市盈率]]} = \frac{P}{EPS}$$ 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Definition**: [[Price-to-Earnings Ratio|P/E Ratio]] (P/E) is a commonly used investment evaluation metric defined as the ratio of the stock price per share to the earnings per share (EPS), given by: $$\text{[[Price-to-Earnings Ratio|P/E Ratio]]} = \frac{P}{EPS}$$ .
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **类型**：市盈率分为滚动市盈率、动态市盈率和静态市盈率等类别 。
	1. 静态市盈率过去一个财年
	2. 滚动市盈率,过去12个月
	3. 动态市盈率则对静态市盈率进行调整以反映未来增长（增长系数取决于每股收益增长率 $i$ 和持续增长年数 $n$ 等因素) 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Type**: price-to-earnings ratio is categorized as rolling price-to-earnings ratio, dynamic price-to-earnings ratio, and static price-to-earnings ratio.

1. Static price-to-earnings ratio for the past fiscal year.
2. Rolling price-to-earnings ratio, over the past 12 months.
3. Dynamic price-to-earnings ratio adjusts the static price-to-earnings ratio to reflect future growth (the growth factor depends on factors such as earnings per share growth rate $i$ and the number of years of sustained growth $n$).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **估值计算**：公司股权价值可以用可比公司的市盈率乘以本公司每股收益来估计，即：$$\text{股权价值} = (\text{可比公司}~P/E) \times \text{公司每股收益}$$ 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Valuation Calculation**: The equity value of a company can be estimated by multiplying the price-to-earnings (P/E) ratio of comparable companies with the company's earnings per share, i.e., $$\text{Equity Value} = (\text{Comparable Company's } P/E) \times \text{Company's Earnings Per Share}$$ .
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **增长与市盈率**：市盈率与盈利增长的关系通常用市盈率相对盈利增长比（PEG）来衡量，$$PEG = \frac{P/E}{\text{年复合增长率} \times 100]\%}$$ 。一般认为，价值型公司 PEG < 1，成长型公司 PEG > 1，但需结合同行业公司作比较参考 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Growth and price-to-earnings ratio**: The relationship between price-to-earnings ratio and profit growth is often measured using the price-to-earnings to growth (PEG) ratio, where $$PEG = \frac{P/E}{\text{Annual Compound Growth Rate} \times 100\%}$$ . Generally, value-oriented companies have a PEG < 1, while growth-oriented companies have a PEG > 1; however, this should be compared with peers within the same industry for a more accurate assessment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4.2市净率估值模型 (P/B)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2price-to-book ratio Valuation Model (P/B)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **定义**：[[Price-to-Book Ratio|市净率]]（P/B）是每股股票价格与每股净资产（Book Value per Share, BVPS）的比值 。即：$$P/B = \frac{P}{BVPS}$$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Definition**: [[Price-to-Book Ratio|P/B Ratio]] (P/B) is the ratio of the stock price to the book value per share (BVPS). That is: $$P/B = \frac{P}{BVPS}$$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **推导关系** ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Derivation Relationship** :
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• $$P/B = 1 + \frac{[[Return on Equity|ROE]] - k}{k - g}$$

• $$P/B \times BVPS = BVPS + \frac{[[Return on Equity|ROE]] - k}{k - g} \times BVPS$$

• $$P = BVPS + \frac{[[Return on Equity|ROE]] - k}{k - g} \times BVPS$$

以上推导表明，$\frac{[[Return on Equity|ROE]] - k}{k - g} \times BVPS$ 部分代表资本市场愿意为该股票支付的溢价 （当 $[[Return on Equity|ROE]] > k$ 时有溢价，当 $[[Return on Equity|ROE]] = k$ 时 $P = BVPS$）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivation shows that $\frac{[[Return on Equity|ROE]] - k}{k - g} \times BVPS$ is the premium the capital market is willing to pay above book value. When $[[Return on Equity|ROE]] > k$, the stock commands a premium; when $[[Return on Equity|ROE]] = k$, $P = BVPS$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.3市销率 (P/S)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3price-to-sales ratio (P/S)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **定义**：[[Price-to-Sales Ratio|市销率]]（P/S）= 公司市值 / 销售收入 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Definition**: [[Price-to-Sales Ratio|Price-to-Sales Ratio]] (P/S) = Company Market Value / Revenue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **优点** ：市销率在任何时期都可使用；销售收入相对于利润而言不易被操纵，真实性更强；且市销率通常较为稳定，不会像盈利指标那样波动剧烈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Advantages** : price-to-sales ratio can be used at any time; sales revenue is less susceptible to manipulation compared to profits, making it more authentic; and price-to-sales ratio is typically more stable, unlike profitability metrics which can fluctuate more dramatically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **局限性** ：无法反映成本结构不同但销售收入相同的公司之间的差异，忽略了盈利能力的区别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Limitations**: It fails to account for differences between companies with different cost structures but the same revenue, thereby ignoring distinctions in profitability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **适用性**：市销率估值模型适用于已经发展到一定规模且拥有稳定收入的公司。市销率常被用于互联网、通信设备、公共事业、制药等行业公司的估值参考 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Applicability:** The price-to-sales valuation model suits companies that have reached a meaningful scale and generate stable revenue. It is often used as a valuation reference for internet, telecommunications-equipment, utility, and pharmaceutical companies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.4公司价值乘数模型 (EV/EBITDA)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.4 Firm Value Multiplier Model (EV/EBITDA)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **定义**：公司价值乘数是公司价值与息税折旧摊销前利润（EBITDA）的比率，即 EV/EBITDA。 其中公司价值 (Enterprise Value, EV) = 股权价值 + 净债务（债务 - 现金） ，EBITDA = 净利润 + 所得税 + 利息 + 折旧 + 摊销 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Definition**: The enterprise value multiple is a ratio of a company's enterprise value to its earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA), represented as EV/EBITDA. Here, the company's enterprise value (EV) = equity value + net debt (debt - cash), and EBITDA = net profit + tax + interest + depreciation + amortization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **估值计算**：目标公司的价值可以通过可比公司的 EV/EBITDA 乘以目标公司的 EBITDA 来估算 ：$$\text{目标公司价值} = (\text{可比公司 EV/EBITDA}) \times \text{目标公司 EBITDA}$$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Valuation Calculation**: The value of a target company can be estimated by multiplying the EV/EBITDA of comparable companies with the EBITDA of the target company: $$\text{Target Company Value} = (\text{Comparable Company EV/EBITDA}) \times \text{Target Company EBITDA}$$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **适用范围**：公司价值乘数模型适用于资本密集型行业的公司估值，如电信、钢铁、石化、航空等行业 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Scope: The company value multiplier model is applicable for valuing capital-intensive companies, such as telecommunications, steel, petrochemical, and aviation industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
