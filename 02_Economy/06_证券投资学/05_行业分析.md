## 1.1行业与产业的含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1 Definitions of Industry and Sector*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**行业**通常是指一群企业由于其提供的产品在很大程度上具有可替代性，而使这些企业彼此之间形成紧密联系，并与其他不同行业的企业群体相区别。例如，外贸行业中的各企业提供的服务具有相似的替代性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industry typically refers to a group of enterprises whose products are highly substitutable, leading these enterprises to form a close-knit relationship and distinguish themselves from other industry groups. For example, enterprises in the export industry offer services that are similar in substitutability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**产业**则是指在社会生产力布局中发挥某种相同作用的一类行业的统称。产业是一种宏观层面的行业集合概念，由多个相关的行业组成。例如，“第三产业”包含了服务业等多个行业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industry refers to a collective of industries that play a similar role in the social layout of productivity. Industry is a macro-level concept of an industry aggregation, composed of multiple related industries. For example, "the tertiary industry" encompasses various sectors such as services.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换言之，**行业**强调的是生产过程的技术特点和产品的同质性；而**产业**强调的是在社会生产力宏观布局上的分工地位，一个产业可以由多个行业构成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Put differently, the industry focuses on the technical characteristics of the production process and the homogeneity of products; whereas the industry emphasizes its position in the macro-arrangement of social productivity; an industry can consist of multiple industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.1.1行业的分类
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1.1 Classification of Industries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对行业进行分类的方法很多，常见的分类方式包括：按照经济周期相关性、要素密集程度、技术程度、发展前景等进行划分。此外，在国民经济和证券市场中也有一套标准的行业分类方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are many methods for classifying industries, and common classification approaches include: by their economic cycle relevance, intensity of factor inputs, technological sophistication, and prospects. Additionally, there is a standard system for industry classification in both national economies and securities market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (1)常用分类
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Common Categories*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **按产业与经济周期的相关性分类**：根据行业发展与宏观经济周期波动的关系，可将行业分为成长型产业、周期型产业和防御型产业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Categorized by Industry and Economic Cycle Correlation**: According to the relationship between an industry's development and fluctuations in the macroeconomic cycle, industries can be classified as growth-oriented, cyclical, and defensive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    - **成长型产业**：行业发展速度快于整个国民经济的平均增长率，其增长与经济周期的波动无关或相关性很小，呈现持续增长的态势。例如生物制药、移动通信、计算机、网络工程、航空业等属于成长型行业【4】。这类行业在经济衰退时期仍可能保持增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Growth Industries**: These industries grow faster than the average growth rate of the national economy, with their growth being unrelated or having a minimal correlation to economic cycles, exhibiting sustained growth. Examples include biopharmaceuticals, mobile communications, computers, network engineering, and aviation. Such industries may still experience growth during economic downturns [4].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - **周期型产业**：行业的发展与经济周期**正相关**，行业业绩随宏观经济的繁荣或衰退而周期性波动。【4】当经济上升时，周期型行业业绩上升；经济下降时，该行业业绩和股票价格下滑。比如金融业、房地产、汽车、有色金属、钢铁、化工、水泥、电力、煤炭等通常被视为周期性行业。当经济处于上升阶段时，这些行业往往表现较强；当经济转入衰退阶段时，这些行业的股价往往跌幅较大【53】。此外，不同行业受到经济波动冲击的时滞不同：经济状况恶化时，建筑业首先受到影响，其次是旅游业、房地产、高端消费品，公用事业受影响最小；而在经济复苏初期，往往房地产、建筑业最先回暖，随后是汽车业、化工业等【66】。把握周期型行业投资的关键在于识别宏观经济萧条或复苏的早期信号【66】。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Cyclical Industries**: The performance of these industries is **positively correlated** with economic cycles, experiencing cyclical fluctuations in their performance as the economy booms or declines. [4] During an economic upturn, the performance of cyclical industries improves; during a downturn, their stock prices decline. For example, finance, real estate, automobiles, nonferrous metals, steel, chemicals, cement, electricity, and coal are often considered cyclical industries. When the economy is in an upward phase, these sectors typically perform well; when it enters a recessionary phase, their stock prices often fall significantly [53]. Additionally, the lags at which different industries are affected by economic fluctuations vary: when economic conditions deteriorate, construction is the first to be impacted, followed by tourism, real estate, and high-end consumer goods, with utilities being least affected; conversely, during the early stages of economic recovery, it is often the real estate and construction sectors that recover first, followed by the automotive and chemical industries [66].The key to investing in cyclical industries lies in identifying early signals of economic downturns or recoveries. [66]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - **防御型产业**：行业产品的市场需求相对稳定，受经济周期变化影响较小，能较稳定地发展【4】。即使在经济衰退时期，这类行业仍能保持一定增长或业绩下滑有限，例如食品业、公用事业（供水、供气、供电等）属于防御型行业【4】。投资这类行业通常获得的是稳定的收益（偏重于股息等收入），而非高速成长带来的资本利得。一般而言，当经济处于衰退阶段时，防御型行业的股票相对表现较好；而经济上升阶段周期型行业表现更佳【63】。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Defensive Industries**: The demand for products in these industries is relatively stable, experiencing less fluctuation due to economic cycles. They can develop more consistently. Even during economic downturns, such industries can maintain steady growth or experience limited declines, such as the food industry and utilities (water, gas, electricity) [4]. Investments in these sectors typically yield stable returns (focusing on dividends, etc.), rather than capital gains from rapid growth. Generally, during recessions, stocks of defensive industries tend to perform better; conversely, during economic expansions, cyclical industries generally perform better [63].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
- **按产业要素的集中程度分类**：根据产业所依赖的主要生产要素，可将行业分为以下几类：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Categorized by the Concentration of Industrial Inputs**: Industries can be classified based on the primary production inputs they rely on as follows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    - **劳动力密集型产业**：生产过程中主要依靠人力投入，劳动成本在总成本中占比高。这类行业往往技术含量较低，进入壁垒较低，如服装业、纺织业等属于劳动力密集型产业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Labor-intensive industries**: These involve a high reliance on manual labor, with labor costs accounting for a significant portion of total costs. Such industries typically have lower technological content and entry barriers; examples include the garment industry and textile industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - **资本密集型产业**：生产主要依赖于大量资金和资本投入，固定资产投资额大。如钢铁、石化、汽车制造等重工业通常是资本密集型产业，进入这些行业需要大规模投资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Capital-intensive industries**: Industries that rely primarily on large amounts of capital and investment, with significant investments in fixed assets. For example, steel, petrochemicals, and automobile manufacturing are typically capital-intensive industries, requiring substantial initial investments to enter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - **资源密集型产业**：生产对自然资源依赖程度高，消耗大量原材料或能源。典型如采掘业（矿业）、原油开采、林业等，这些行业的发展受资源获取和成本影响较大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Resource-intensive industries**: Industries that have a high dependency on natural resources, consuming large amounts of raw materials or energy. Typical examples include mining (extractive industries), oil extraction, and forestry. The development of these sectors is significantly influenced by the availability and cost of resources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - **技术密集型产业**：生产依赖高技术和研发投入，具有较高的知识含量和技术门槛。例如生物医药、航空航天、新一代信息技术产业等属于技术密集型产业，技术创新对这些行业的发展至关重要。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Technologically Intensive Industries**: These industries rely on high technology and substantial research and development investments, possessing a high level of knowledge content and technological barriers. For example, industries such as biopharmaceuticals, aerospace, and new-generation information technology fall under technologically intensive industries, where innovation in technology is crucial for their development.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
- **按行业采用技术的先进程度分类**：可划分为新兴产业和传统产业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Classified by the advanced degree of technology adopted**: it can be divided into new industries and traditional industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    - **新兴产业**：指采用新技术、新工艺，代表未来发展方向的新兴行业。通常这些产业尚处于成长初期，但具有巨大潜力，例如新能源、新材料、人工智能等产业被视为新兴产业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **New Emerging Industries**: Refers to industries utilizing new technologies and processes that represent future development directions. These sectors are typically in their early stages of growth but have significant potential; for example, industries such as new energy, new materials, and artificial intelligence are considered new emerging industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - **传统产业**：指技术发展成熟、历史较长的行业。这些行业的产品和工艺相对稳定，创新变化较慢，例如纺织、采矿、传统制造业等可归为传统产业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Traditional industries**: Refers to sectors where technology has matured and has a long history. Products and processes in these industries tend to be stable, with slower innovation and change; for example, textiles, mining, traditional manufacturing, etc., can be categorized as traditional industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
- **按产业发展前景分类**：根据行业未来发展的预期，可分为朝阳产业、夕阳产业，以及有时提及的幼稚产业和成熟产业等：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **By industry development prospects**: According to the expected future development of an industry, they can be categorized as sunrise industries, sunset industries, and sometimes mentioned as infant industries and mature industries:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    - **朝阳产业**：未来发展前景看好的产业，处于上升阶段的新兴行业，预期将保持较高速增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Sunrise industry**: industries with promising future development prospects, emerging industries in the rising stage, are expected to maintain relatively high growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - **夕阳产业**：未来发展前景不乐观的产业，所处市场趋于饱和或衰退，增长缓慢甚至萎缩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Sunset industry**: industries with unfavorable future development prospects, where the market tends to be saturated or declining, slow growth or even shrinking.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - **幼稚产业**：发展处于初期、规模和实力较弱的产业，相当于尚未成长壮大的新兴行业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Infant industry**: an industry that is in its infancy, weak in scale and strength, equivalent to an emerging industry that has not yet grown.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - **成熟产业**：已经发展成熟，拥有较强的行业实力和竞争力，增速趋于平稳的产业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **mature industry**: an already developed sector with strong industry strength and competitiveness, experiencing a slowdown in growth rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        

#### (2)国民经济行业的分类
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Classification of National Economy Industries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在国民经济宏观层面，我国采用国家标准的行业分类。**《国民经济行业分类与代码》（GB/T 4754-2002）**将整个国民经济分为若干门类，并用字母编码标识。例如，A类代表农业、林业、牧业、渔业，B类采掘业，C类制造业，…一直到 T类国际组织等。这种分类覆盖第一产业、第二产业、第三产业的各个行业门类，为宏观统计和分析提供了统一标准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the macroeconomic level of the national economy, China adopts industry classifications based on national standards. **The Classification of Industries in the National Economy and Coding (GB/T 4754-2002)** divides the entire national economy into several categories and codes them using alphabetical identifiers. For instance, Category A represents agriculture, forestry, animal husbandry, and fishing, while Category B covers mining, Category C manufacturing, and so forth, up to Category T for international organizations. This classification encompasses all industries within the primary, secondary, and tertiary sectors, providing a unified standard for macro-statistics and analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.1.2行业分析的分类
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Classification of Industry Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据分析内容和用途的不同，行业分析报告大致可以分为以下几种类型：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Depending on the content and purpose, industry analysis reports can generally be categorized into several types:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **总体报告**：全面反映某行业整体状况的分析报告。此类报告覆盖范围广、信息量大，内容深入全面，分析难度也较高，需要对行业的各个方面进行综合研究。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Overall Report**: A comprehensive analysis report that reflects the overall condition of a particular industry. Such reports cover a wide range of information and have high data volume; they are also deeply analytical, requiring a thorough study of various aspects of the industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **专项报告**：针对行业中某一特定领域或专题进行深入分析的报告。例如有关行业中的技术创新、生产规模、公司内部管理、产品市场、客户结构等特定方面的专题分析报告。专项报告聚焦特定问题，内容相对具体。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Special Report**: A report that conducts in-depth analysis on a specific sector or topic within an industry. For example, reports focusing on specific aspects such as technological innovation, production scale, internal management of companies, product markets, customer structures, etc. Special Reports focus on specific issues and have relatively specific content.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **预测报告**：侧重于对行业未来发展趋势和前景进行预测的分析报告。此类报告基于对宏观环境和行业历史数据的分析，预测行业未来的变化方向，为投资决策提供前瞻性意见。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Predictive Report**: Focused on analysis reports predicting future trends and prospects of an industry. Such reports analyze macro-environmental factors and historical data of the industry to forecast changes in the industry's direction, providing forward-looking advice for investment decisions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **动态报告**：也称行业跟踪报告，主要追踪报道行业最新动态的分析报告。特点是实时性强，具有信息的超前性和预测功能【6】。动态报告通常涵盖行业内企业的最新动态、技术进展状况以及主要厂商或行业龙头的经营变化【6】。通过对最新数据的分析，动态报告能够对行业的发展状态做出描述，并预测短期走势。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Dynamic Reports**: Also known as industry tracking reports, these analyze the latest developments in an industry. They are characterized by their timeliness, providing information that is ahead of the curve and possessing predictive capabilities [6]. Dynamic reports typically cover the latest activities of companies within the industry, technological advancements, and changes in operations of major firms or industry leaders [6]. By analyzing the most recent data, dynamic reports can describe the current state of the industry and forecast short-term trends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

（以上分类并非相互排斥，不同类型的分析可以结合使用。例如，在总体报告中也可能包含对未来的预测部分。）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
(The above categories are not mutually exclusive; different types of analysis can be combined. For instance, a future prediction section may also be included in the overall report.)
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 行业分析的基本内容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Basic Content of Industry Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行业分析的基本内容是指对一个行业进行全面评价时需要考察的主要方面。一般来说，行业分析应包括：行业的基本经济特性、行业所处的环境、行业结构以及行业的周期性特征等【39】。本章按照这几方面展开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The basic contents of industry analysis involve the main aspects to be evaluated when conducting a comprehensive assessment of an industry. Generally, industry analysis should include: the fundamental economic characteristics of the industry, the environment in which it operates, the structure of the industry, and the cyclical nature of the industry [39]. This chapter will proceed according to these aspects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1行业的基本经济特性分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1 Analysis of the Basic Economic Characteristics of the Industry*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“行业的基本经济特性”是指从经济角度刻画行业的重要属性。分析一个行业的基本经济特性，通常包括以下几个方面【44】：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
"Basic economic characteristics of an industry" refer to important attributes of the industry as depicted from an economic perspective. Analyzing an industry's basic economic characteristics typically involves the following aspects [44]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **产业特征**：行业在国民经济中的地位、行业边界的确定等。即该行业属于第几产业，在整个经济体系中扮演什么角色，与上下游行业的关系如何，等等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Industry Characteristics**: The position of the industry in the national economy, the determination of industry boundaries, etc. That is, which sector the industry belongs to, what role it plays in the overall economic system, its relationships with upstream and downstream industries, and so on.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **资本特征**：行业所需的资金投入规模及来源，例如是否属于资金密集型行业、固定资产占比高低等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Capital Characteristics**: The scale and source of capital required by the industry, such as whether it is a capital-intensive sector and the proportion of fixed assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **与经济周期的关系**：该行业的发展是否与宏观经济周期高度相关，属于周期性或非周期性行业（成长型或防御型）的哪一种。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Relationship with Economic Cycles**: Whether the industry's development is highly correlated with macroeconomic cycles determines whether it is a cyclical or non-cyclical industry (growth or defensive).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **市场特征**：行业的市场结构如何，市场竞争程度如何（例如完全竞争、寡头垄断、完全垄断等类型），行业内企业数目及规模分布情况。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Market Characteristics**: The structure of the industry, such as the level of competition (e.g., perfect competition, oligopoly, monopoly), the number and size distribution of firms within the industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **主要厂商和产品**：行业内主要公司的名单及其核心产品是什么，市场份额如何。是否存在行业龙头企业或寡头垄断者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Main Manufacturers and Products**: What is the list of major companies in the industry and what are their core products? How do they hold market share? Are there any dominant players or oligopolistic firms in the industry?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **主要供应商和原材料**：行业上游供应商的情况，主要的原材料种类，以及原材料供应是否充裕、价格走势如何等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Major Suppliers and Raw Materials**: Information on upstream suppliers in the industry, types of main raw materials, and whether supply is adequate and how price trends are developing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **总体技术水平与特征**：行业采用的技术工艺水平如何，是否技术密集，当前的技术瓶颈是什么。该行业产品和生产的技术特点是什么（例如技术更新速度快慢）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Overall Technological Level and Characteristics**: How advanced is the technology and process used in the industry, whether it is technology-intensive, and what current technological bottlenecks exist. What are the technological characteristics of the products and production processes in this industry (for example, how fast does the technology update).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **产业技术发展前景**：该行业在技术上的潜在进步空间，有无重大的技术革新趋势，将如何影响行业未来的发展。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Prospects for Industrial Technology Development**: The potential advancements in technology within this industry, including significant trends of technological innovation, and how these will impact its future development.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

上述方面定性地描述了行业的经济属性。为了进一步定量分析行业特性，通常会考察一些**主要经济变量及其表现形式**，如下表所示：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The above aspects qualitatively describe the economic attributes of the industry. To conduct a more quantitative analysis of its characteristics, typically some **key economic variables and their manifestations** are examined, as shown in the table below:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|**产业利润水平**|行业平均毛利率、净利率水平，盈利能力及预测；历史业绩及其波动原因；行业财务指标均值；长期利润率趋势与预测等|

Above table lists some key variables commonly used in analyzing the basic economic characteristics of an industry. For instance, by examining market size, one can gauge the overall magnitude of demand and supply for the industry; by assessing profit levels, one can determine the industry's profitability and its trends; by considering the stage of the industry's lifecycle, one can evaluate its level of maturity; and the scale of competitors and consumers, as well as product differentiation, reflect the competitive landscape of the industry. Analyzing these variables helps investors gain a comprehensive understanding of the fundamental characteristics of the industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2行业环境分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2 Industry Environment Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行业环境分析是对影响行业发展的外部宏观和微观环境因素进行评估。主要包括政策、技术、社会和国际环境等方面【9】。这些因素往往决定了行业发展的外部条件和约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industry environment analysis involves evaluating external macro and micro-environmental factors that impact industry development. This typically includes policy, technology, social, and international environments. These factors often determine the external conditions and constraints of industry development.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **政策因素**：产业政策是国家为指导产业发展所制定的一系列政策目标和措施的总和【9】。产业政策包括产业结构政策（如鼓励或限制某些产业的发展方向，例：“低空经济”政策）、产业组织政策（如行业整合措施，例：稀土行业重组整合）、产业技术政策（如“油电之争”反映新能源汽车技术路线之争）以及产业布局政策（例：“东数西算”工程）等【57】。政府可以通过**财政补贴、税收优惠、关税壁垒、法规标准**等具体措施来影响产业发展【57】。积极的产业政策扶持能促进行业成长，而限制性的政策可能抑制某些行业的发展。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Policy Factors**: Industrial policies encompass a set of policy objectives and measures aimed at guiding industrial development [9]. Industrial policies include structural policies (such as encouraging or restricting certain directions for industry development, e.g., "low-altitude economy" policies), organizational policies (such as consolidation measures, e.g., restructuring in the rare earth industry), technological policies (such as the "oil-electricity debate" reflecting the competition between different routes for new energy vehicle technology) and spatial policies (e.g., the "East Data West Computing" initiative). Governments can influence industrial development through specific measures such as fiscal subsidies, tax incentives, tariff barriers, and regulatory standards [57]. Active industrial policies can foster industry growth, while restrictive policies may hinder the development of certain industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **技术进步**：技术因素对行业兴衰有决定性影响。技术进步一方面能**创造新产品，形成新产业，淘汰旧产品**（典型例子如智能手机的出现淘汰了传统功能手机）；另一方面，技术进步可以**推动现有产业升级**（例如合成生物技术提升传统生物制药产业的工艺水平）；还可以**提高新兴产业的生产效率，加速新产业的扩张**（如AI芯片的发展提高了人工智能产业的效率）；**实现规模经济，壮大新产业**（例如电子商务利用互联网技术实现规模效应）；以及**创造新的市场需求，拓展产业发展空间**（如服务机器人技术创造出全新的应用需求）【8】。总之，技术进步往往促进行业结构演变，新技术的出现常常催生某些新行业的兴起，同时使技术落后的产业面临被淘汰的风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Technological Progress**: Technological factors have a decisive impact on industry prosperity and decline. Technological progress, on one hand, can **create new products, form new industries, and displace old ones** (e.g., the emergence of smartphones displacing traditional feature phones); on the other hand, it can **drive existing industries to upgrade** (e.g., synthetic biology enhancing the production processes of traditional biopharmaceuticals); it can also **increase the efficiency of emerging industries and accelerate their expansion** (e.g., the development of AI chips boosting the efficiency of the AI industry); **achieve economies of scale, bolstering new industries** (e.g., e-commerce leveraging internet technology to achieve economies of scale); and **generate new market demands, expanding the scope of industrial development** (e.g., service robotics creating new application needs) [8]. In summary, technological advancement often leads to structural changes in industries. The emergence of new technologies frequently gives rise to new industries, while lagging behind in technology puts backward industries at risk of **being displaced**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **社会因素**：社会消费习惯、人口结构和文化潮流的变化会影响产业格局。**社会习惯的改变**可能导致某些产业式微、新的产业兴起。例如人们生活方式的改变正淘汰过时的产业形态，促进新兴产业的发展【8】。社会因素还包括消费者偏好、城乡人口变化等，它们会影响消费、储蓄、投资和贸易的结构，从而影响相关产业的发展速度和生命周期更替【8】。另外，国际交流与文化传播会改变本地的社会习惯，从而间接影响本国产业的发展方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Social Factors**: Changes in social consumption habits, population structures, and cultural trends can influence the industrial landscape. Shifts in social habits can lead to the decline of certain industries and the rise of new ones. For instance, changes in lifestyle are eroding outdated industry forms while fostering the development of new industries [8]. Social factors also include consumer preferences and shifts in urban-rural populations, which affect the structure of consumption, savings, investment, and trade, thereby influencing the speed and lifecycle of related industries [8]. Additionally, international exchanges and cultural dissemination can alter local social habits, indirectly impacting the direction of domestic industries [8].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **国际环境（全球化）因素**：全球化程度和国际贸易环境对行业也有重大影响。近年来出现的**逆全球化**趋势，需要分析其对产业的影响：逆全球化可能阻碍本国产业**整合全球资源**、开拓海外市场的进程【8】。同时，全球化逆转将影响产业的**全球性转移**（产业链有从高成本国家向低成本国家转移的趋势会被打断或逆转），改变传统的国际分工格局。一个国家或地区的资源禀赋以及后天因素（如劳动力素质、基础设施等）都会在全球化背景下影响产业布局【8】。因此，关税壁垒、贸易协定、国际政治关系等都会成为行业环境中必须考虑的因素。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **International Environment (Globalization)** factors: Globalization and trade environments have significant impacts on industries. Recent trends of **de-globalization** require analysis of their effects on industries: De-globalization may hinder the domestic industry's ability to **integrate global resources** and expand overseas markets [8]. Additionally, a reversal in globalization will affect the **global transfer** of industries (the trend of moving production from high-cost countries to low-cost countries is likely to be interrupted or reversed), altering traditional international division of labor. In a globalized context, resource endowments and postnatal factors such as workforce quality and infrastructure will influence industrial layouts [8]. Therefore, tariffs, trade agreements, and international political relations will all become factors that need to be considered in the industry environment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

## 2.3行业结构分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3 Industry Structure Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行业结构分析关注行业内部竞争格局，包括市场结构和竞争强度等方面。其中一个核心问题是市场上**竞争者的构成及其力量对比**。常用的分析工具包括产业集中度指标和迈克尔·波特的“五力模型”等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industry structure analysis focuses on the competitive landscape within an industry, encompassing aspects such as market structure and competitive intensity. One of the core issues is the composition and relative strength of competitors in the market. Common analytical tools include indicators of industry concentration and Michael Porter's "Five Forces" model, among others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **市场结构与集中度**：市场结构反映了行业内市场份额在企业间的分配情况。为了衡量市场集中程度，常使用**产业集中率**（Concentration Ratio, $CR_n$）和**赫希曼指数**（Herfindahl-Hirschman Index, HHI）等指标。$CR_n$通常指行业中最大$n$家企业市场份额之和，比如$CR_4$表示最大4家公司的市场份额总和，数值越大表示市场越集中于少数巨头。**赫希曼指数**则定义为行业内所有企业市场份额的平方和：$HHI = \sum_{i=1}^{N} s_i^2$, 其中$s_i$为第$i$家企业的市场份额（0～1之间）。HHI综合考虑了所有企业份额分布，==指数越大说明市场被少数公司高度垄断，越小则表示市场竞争越分散==。产业集中度高的行业，通常竞争者少且实力悬殊，而集中度低的行业竞争者众多且相对均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Market Structure and Concentration**: Market structure reflects the distribution of market share among companies within an industry. To measure market concentration, metrics such as the **Industry Concentration Ratio** ($CR_n$) and **Herfindahl-Hirschman Index** (HHI) are commonly used. $CR_n$ typically refers to the sum of the market shares of the largest $n$ companies in the industry, for example $CR_4$ representing the total market share of the top four companies. A higher value indicates greater concentration towards a few major players.The Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) is defined as the sum of the squares of all firms' market shares: $HHI = \sum_{i=1}^{N} s_i^2$, where $s_i$ represents the market share of the $i$ firm (between 0 and 1). HHI considers the distribution of all firms' shares and ==a higher index indicates a highly concentrated market dominated by a few companies, while a lower index suggests more dispersed competition==. Industries with high concentration typically have fewer competitors with significant disparities in strength, whereas those with low concentration have many competitors that are relatively balanced.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.3.1波特的五力模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3.1 Porter's Five Forces Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
#考点 
- **行业竞争结构分析（波特五力模型）**：美国学者迈克尔·波特提出了分析行业竞争结构的**五种基本竞争力量模型**。这五种竞争力量包括：==新进入者的威胁、供应商的议价能力、购买者的议价能力、替代品的威胁，以及产业内现有竞争者之间的竞争程度。==对这五种力量的强弱进行分析，有助于评估行业的吸引力和盈利水平。具体而言：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Exam focus*
- **Industry competitive-structure analysis—Porter's Five Forces:** Michael Porter proposed a model of five fundamental competitive forces: ==the threat of new entrants, suppliers' bargaining power, buyers' bargaining power, the threat of substitutes, and rivalry among existing competitors.== Assessing the strength of each force helps evaluate an industry's attractiveness and profit potential. Specifically:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
#### (1). **潜在进入者的威胁**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1). **Threats from Potential Entrants**:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

新进入者如果大举进入，将增加行业竞争、瓜分市场份额。当一个产业的**利润率或发展前景优于其他行业**时，就可能吸引新的进入者。潜在进入者能否进入，取决于行业**进入壁垒**的高低。进入壁垒是阻碍潜在竞争者进入产业的各种因素。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
New entrants, if they enter in large numbers, will increase industry competition and erode market share. When an industry offers higher profit margins or better prospects than others, it may attract new entrants. Whether potential entrants can enter depends on the **barriers to entry** of the industry. Barriers to entry are various factors that hinder potential competitors from entering the industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见的进入壁垒包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common barriers to entry include:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**规模经济**（现有企业已经形成大规模生产，后来者若无法达到相当规模，成本劣势明显）、
**产品差异化**（现有企业的品牌忠诚度和产品差异使新进入者难以吸引顾客）、
**专有技术和资金要求**（进入某些行业需要特殊技术专长或巨额资本）、
**政府政策和法律法规**（如牌照许可、资质要求等）、
**销售渠道控制**（现有企业对渠道有控制力，新进入者难以进入流通渠道）、
**原材料和资源控制**（现有企业掌握最佳原材料来源）、
**地理位置优势**等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Economies of scale (where an existing enterprise has established large-scale production, making it difficult for newcomers to achieve a comparable size and incur significant cost disadvantages),
Product differentiation (where existing enterprises leverage brand loyalty and product differences to make it hard for new entrants to attract customers),
Specialized technology and capital requirements (entering certain industries necessitates specialized technical expertise or substantial capital),
Government policies and legal regulations (such as licensing requirements, qualification standards, etc.),
Control over distribution channels (existing enterprises have control over distribution channels, making it challenging for new entrants to enter the distribution network),
Control over raw materials and resources (existing enterprises have access to the best sources of raw materials),
and so on.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

进入壁垒越高，潜在进入者的威胁越小；反之，进入壁垒低的行业更容易出现新的竞争者，压低行业盈利水平。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The higher the entry barriers, the smaller the threat from potential entrants; conversely, industries with low entry barriers are more prone to new competitors, which can drive down industry profits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
        
#### (2)**购买方（客户）的议价能力**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2)**Buyer (Customer) Bargaining Power**:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指行业下游客户在价格和其他交易条件上对厂商的压迫能力。购买者通过压低采购价格或要求更高的产品/服务质量，来影响行业中企业的盈利能力。购买者的议价能力主要取决于以下因素：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The power of downstream industry customers to exert pressure on manufacturers in terms of price and other transaction conditions. Purchasers influence the profitability of companies in an industry by lowering procurement prices or demanding higher product/service quality. The power of buyers mainly depends on the following factors:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **购买量和集中度**：如果下游购买者采购量大且采购行为集中（少数买家购买大量产品），其议价能力就强。例如，大型连锁超市对食品供应商就有较强的话语权。            
- **购买方对产品的依赖程度**：如果某类产品在买方总成本中占比很高，买方会更加在意价格，从而更有动力压低价格；反之，若占比不高则敏感度低。
 - **产品差异化和转换成本**：如果供应商提供的产品是标准化的、差异很小，购买方可以轻易寻找替代供应，一旦供应商涨价买方就转向他人，因而买方议价能力强。相反，如果产品有独特性且买方转换供应商的成本高（例如更换零部件供应需要调整生产线），买方议价能力就弱【11】。
- **购买方自身的盈利状况**：当买方利润率很低时，会千方百计压低上游价格以改善自身收益，此时其议价动力强。
- **后向整合的威胁**：如果购买方有可能向后整合自己生产所需产品（比如大型汽车厂商可能收购零部件厂自行生产），那么供应商在谈判中会有所忌惮，买方议价能力因此增强。
- **信息掌握程度**：买方如果掌握了充分的市场和价格信息，也更能在议价时占据主动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Purchase volume and concentration:** Buyers have greater bargaining power when they purchase large quantities and buying is concentrated among a few customers. Large supermarket chains, for example, have substantial leverage over food suppliers.
- **Buyer's dependence on the product:** If a product accounts for a large share of the buyer's total cost, the buyer is more price-sensitive and has a stronger incentive to press for a lower price. A small cost share reduces that sensitivity.
- **Product differentiation and switching costs:** When suppliers offer standardized, minimally differentiated products, buyers can switch easily after a price increase and therefore possess strong bargaining power. Unique products and high switching costs—for example, having to reconfigure a production line when changing component suppliers—weaken buyer power [11].
- **Buyer's profitability:** A buyer with a low profit margin has a strong incentive to push upstream prices down to improve its own return.
- **Threat of backward integration:** A buyer that could produce the input itself—for example, a large automaker acquiring a component supplier—has greater leverage because suppliers fear losing the business.
- **Information:** Buyers with good market and price information negotiate from a stronger position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
当买方具备上述多项条件时，其议价能力强，行业利润可能被压缩；反之，如果行业内买家众多且分散、单个买家采购量小、产品差异化高或替换不易，则买方对行业企业的议价能力弱。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When buyers possess multiple such conditions, their bargaining power is strong, potentially compressing industry profits; conversely, if there are many and dispersed buyers within an industry, individual buyers have small procurement volumes, high product differentiation, or difficulty in substitution, then buyers have weak bargaining power over industry enterprises.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
#### (3). **供应商的议价能力**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3). **Supplier Bargaining Power**:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
   
   这是从上游来看，供应行业对本行业的影响力。供应商是向本行业企业提供生产所需原材料、设备或服务的企业群体。如果供应商拥有较强的议价能力，本行业企业的成本将受上游挤压。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This refers to the upstream industry's influence on the downstream sector. Suppliers are a group of enterprises that provide raw materials, equipment, or services needed by companies in the downstream sector. If suppliers have strong bargaining power, the costs of downstream sector enterprises will be squeezed from above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
   
   供应商议价能力强的情形包括：
- **供应行业集中度高**：上游若被少数几家供应商垄断，甚至出现唯一供应商，本行业企业缺乏议价空间。尤其当某关键原料**来源具有稀缺性**时，供应商说了算【11】。
- **供应商无替代品竞争**：如果供应商提供的投入品没有可替代的产品或材料，那么下游行业只能依赖这些供应，谈判中处于弱势【11】。
- **供应商不太依赖下游行业**：即使失去对本行业的销售，供应商也无碍（比如供应商还有其他行业客户或者总体规模大），这种情况下，供应商不怕失去本行业客户，议价时不会让步【11】。
- **供应品对下游重要且差异化**：如果供应商提供的是本行业运作所必需且独特的产品（不可或缺且有独特性能），下游离不开，上游就更有议价权【11】。同时如果供应品是差异化的、品牌专用的，也增强了议价能力。
- **转换成本高**：对于本行业企业来说，更换供应商可能要付出较高代价（例如重新认证原料供应、调整生产流程等），那么本行业企业不敢轻易与供应商闹翻，供应商就可据此要求更高价格【11】。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppliers have strong bargaining power when:
- **The supplier industry is concentrated:** If a few suppliers, or even a single supplier, dominate upstream supply, firms in the focal industry have little room to negotiate. The supplier is especially powerful when a critical raw material is scarce [11].
- **The input has no substitute:** If no alternative product or material can replace the input, the downstream industry must depend on these suppliers and negotiates from a weak position [11].
- **Suppliers do not depend heavily on the downstream industry:** A supplier with customers in other industries or a large overall market can afford to lose this industry's business and therefore has little reason to concede [11].
- **The input is important and differentiated:** An indispensable input with unique performance gives its supplier greater power. Brand-specific or otherwise differentiated inputs reinforce that power [11].
- **Switching costs are high:** If changing supplier requires costly recertification or production-process changes, downstream firms cannot switch easily, allowing suppliers to demand higher prices [11].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
反之，若供应商很多且分散、供应充裕或容易找到替代材料，并且供应商高度依赖下游销路（下游行业对其很重要），则供应商在谈判中就处于弱势。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, if there are many suppliers that are dispersed, ample supply is readily available, or there are easy alternatives to materials, and if these suppliers are highly dependent on downstream demand (downstream industries are very important to them), then the suppliers will be in a weaker position during negotiations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
#### (4) **替代品的威胁**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(4) **Threats from Alternatives**:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

替代产品是指能够实现本产业产品相同或相似功能的其它产品【11】。替代品不一定与本行业产品属于同类，但会因满足相同需求而构成竞争。例如，对传统石油燃料汽车来说，公共交通、电动车就是替代品；对糖类制造业来说，甜味剂就是替代品。替代品的存在会限制本行业产品的价格上限和市场空间：如果替代品价格更低廉或性能更优，本行业就难以维持高利润。替代品的威胁主要取决于**技术发展**以及**性价比**：当新的技术出现可以生产出功能类似但更便宜或更好的产品时，本行业将面临替代冲击。企业需要密切关注能够满足客户相同需求的其他产品动向，评估替代品出现的可能性以及对行业的影响。如果替代品很少或替代成本高，则本行业相对安全；如果替代品众多且不断涌现，则行业前景可能受到压制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substitutes are products that perform the same or a similar function as the industry's product [11]. They need not belong to the same product category; satisfying the same customer need is enough to create competition. Public transport and electric vehicles, for example, substitute for conventional petroleum-fueled cars, while sweeteners substitute for sugar.
Substitutes limit the industry's price ceiling and market space. If they are cheaper or perform better, incumbent firms cannot sustain high profits. The threat depends mainly on **technological development** and **price–performance**: a new technology that supplies a similar function more cheaply or effectively can disrupt the industry. Firms should monitor other ways customers can meet the same need and assess both the likelihood and impact of substitution. Few substitutes or high substitution costs make the industry safer; numerous and continually emerging substitutes constrain its prospects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
#### (5). **产业内现有竞争者的竞争程度**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(5). **Intensity of Competition from Existing Competitors in the Industry**:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这反映行业内部现有企业之间竞争的激烈程度，又称行业**竞争格局**。现有竞争者的威胁大小与行业竞争结构密切相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This reflects the intensity of competition among existing firms within the industry, also known as the industry **competitive structure**. The size of the threat from existing competitors is closely related to the industry's competitive structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当行业中**竞争者数量众多且规模相近**时，竞争往往更为激烈；反之，如果只有少数几家巨头垄断市场，竞争反而相对缓和（但垄断会导致其他问题）。
**产品差异化**程度也是关键：当各公司产品同质化严重时，竞争往往集中在价格方面，削价竞争会降低整个行业利润；若产品有差异，每家公司有自己的细分市场，则竞争不至于白热化【52】。
**退出障碍**（如巨额沉没成本、资产专用性、高额解散费用等）如果很高，企业难以退出亏损行业，就可能硬撑着导致恶性竞争持续。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When there are numerous and similarly sized competitors in an industry, competition tends to be more intense; conversely, if only a few dominant firms monopolize the market, competition is generally less heated (but this can lead to other issues).
The degree of **product differentiation** is also crucial: when companies' products are highly homogeneous, competition often focuses on price, leading to cutthroat pricing that reduces the industry's overall profit; if products have differences, each company has its own niche market, thereby preventing the competition from becoming overly intense.
High **exit barriers** (such as large sunk costs, asset specificity, high dissolution fees, etc.) make it difficult for firms to exit loss-making industries, potentially leading to prolonged vicious competition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

概括来说，影响产业内竞争强度的主要因素包括：
- **竞争者的数量和规模分布**：竞争者越多且实力越接近，竞争越激烈；一两家独大的情况反而竞争有限。
- **行业增长率（成长性）**：如果行业整体处于高成长，市场蛋糕在变大，企业可以各得其利，竞争可能较温和。但若行业增长停滞甚至萎缩，各企业只能抢夺存量市场，竞争会异常激烈【52】。
- **生产能力过剩及集中度**：当行业产能过剩且高度集中时，企业为出货往往降价倾销，导致激烈竞争。另一方面，如果产能集中在少数企业且有协同，竞争可能有所克制。
- **产品差异性和顾客转换成本**：前面提到，产品越差异化，客户忠诚度越高，企业间避免卷入纯粹价格战；相反，如果产品差不多且客户转换供应商毫无障碍，那么竞争将主要靠价格吸引客户，竞争会非常激进【52】。
- **固定成本比例**：如果行业固定成本占比高，而需求又不足，则企业为摊薄固定成本可能降价销售，诱发价格战。固定成本相对于可变成本的比例越高，企业越倾向于争夺市场份额以分摊成本，从而使竞争加剧【52】。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In summary, the intensity of rivalry within an industry depends mainly on:
- **Number and size distribution of competitors:** More competitors of similar strength create more intense rivalry; one or two dominant firms may face less direct competition.
- **Industry growth:** Rapid growth expands the market, allowing firms to grow without taking share from one another. When growth stagnates or reverses, firms must fight over the existing market and rivalry becomes much more intense [52].
- **Excess capacity and concentration:** Excess capacity encourages firms to cut prices to utilize capacity, intensifying competition. If capacity is concentrated among a few coordinated firms, rivalry may be more restrained.
- **Product differentiation and customer switching costs:** Differentiation builds loyalty and reduces pure price competition. Homogeneous products and frictionless switching make price the principal way to win customers, producing aggressive rivalry [52].
- **Share of fixed costs:** When fixed costs are high and demand is weak, firms may cut prices to spread those costs over more sales. A larger fixed-cost share strengthens the incentive to pursue market share and intensifies rivalry [52].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
除上述因素外，行业文化（例如是否形成默契不打价格战）和法律环境（反垄断法规等）也会影响竞争激烈程度。总体而言，现有竞争者之间竞争越激烈，行业盈利水平越低。反之，如果行业结构趋向寡头垄断且竞争方式更多元（如注重差异化而非价格），行业平均利润率会较高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Beyond these factors, industry culture (such as whether a tacit understanding exists to avoid price wars) and legal environment (such as antitrust regulations) also influence the level of competition. In general, when existing competitors are more competitive against each other, the overall profitability of the industry is lower. Conversely, if the industry structure tends towards oligopoly and competition is more diversified (such as focusing on differentiation rather than price), the average profit margin of the industry is higher.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
## 2.4周期分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4 Periodic Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

周期分析关注行业随宏观经济和自身发展阶段的波动规律，主要包括宏观经济周期对行业的影响，以及行业自身的生命周期特征两个方面。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Periodic analysis focuses on the fluctuation patterns of industries in response to macroeconomic conditions and their own developmental stages. It primarily encompasses the impact of macroeconomic cycles on industries, as well as the lifecycle characteristics inherent to each industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.4.1行业的经济周期与投资分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4.1 Industry Economic Cycles and Investment Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

宏观经济周期（繁荣、衰退等）对不同行业有不同影响。在经济周期的不同阶段，行业的相对表现也不同。一般地：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Macroeconomic cycles (booms, recessions, etc.) have varying impacts on different industries. During different stages of the economic cycle, the relative performance of various industries also varies. Generally speaking:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 当**经济处于上升阶段**时，许多**周期型行业**（如可选消费品、原材料、资本品行业等）的需求随之上升，业绩大幅改善，股票价格表现强于大盘【63】。因此在经济繁荣期，投资者可重点配置周期性行业以获取超额收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- When the **economy is in an upward phase**, the demand for many **cyclical industries** (such as discretionary consumer goods, raw materials, capital goods, etc.) rises, leading to significant improvements in performance, and stock prices outperform the market [63]. Therefore, during periods of economic prosperity, investors should focus on allocating resources to cyclical industries to achieve excess returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- 当**经济进入衰退阶段**时，**防御型行业**由于基本需求稳定，业绩受影响较小，股价相对抗跌，往往表现好于整体市场【63】。因此在经济不景气时，防御性行业（如食品饮料、公用事业、医疗保健等）成为投资避风港，投资者可增加对这类行业的配置以降低组合波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- When the **economy enters a recession phase**, defensive industries benefit from stable basic needs, experiencing relatively less performance impact, leading to stock prices that hold up better than the overall market. [63] Consequently, during economic downturns, defensive sectors (such as food and beverage, utilities, healthcare, etc.) serve as investment havens, allowing investors to increase their allocation to these sectors to reduce portfolio volatility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

换言之，投资者应根据宏观经济所处的周期阶段来调整行业配置：“经济上行选周期，经济下行选防御”是一个经验法则。当然，必须能够**前瞻性判断经济周期拐点**。对此，**行业景气指标**是有用的分析工具（后文将介绍行业景气分析）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Put simply, investors should adjust their industry allocations based on the phase of the economic cycle: "select cyclical sectors during an upturn and defensive sectors during a downturn" is a rule of thumb. Of course, it is necessary to be able to **make forward-looking assessments of economic cycle turning points**. For this, **industry health indicators** are useful analytical tools (which will be introduced in a later section on industry health analysis).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.4.2行业的生命周期分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4.2 Industry Life Cycle Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个行业从诞生到衰亡，一般都要经历**幼稚期、成长期、成熟期和衰退期**四个阶段【63】。行业所处的生命周期阶段不同，其市场特征和投资价值也不同：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each industry generally goes through four stages: the **infancy, growth, maturity, and decline** phases of its lifecycle [63]. The market characteristics and investment value of an industry vary depending on which stage it is in:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **幼稚期**（初创期）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Infancy** (Startup Phase)
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    - 行业特点：行业创立不久，进入者的数量很少，市场处于启蒙状态。由于规模小、经验不足，多数企业尚未盈利甚至处于亏损状态【63】。行业技术尚不成熟，商业模式有待验证。整体而言，幼稚期行业的不确定性高，失败风险大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Industry Characteristics: The industry is relatively new, with a limited number of entrants, and the market is in an embryonic stage. Due to small scale and lack of experience, many companies have yet to achieve profitability, and some are even operating at a loss [63]. The technology within the industry is still immature, and its business models require validation. In general, industries in their infancy exhibit high levels of uncertainty and significant failure risks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - 证券表现：不少幼稚期的企业会选择在创业板或科创板上市以融资，但由于投资者对其未来高增长有预期，这些公司的估值往往较高（股价相对于当前业绩偏贵）【12】。对于投资者而言，投资此类行业风险很大，属于高风险高潜在回报的投机型投资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Stock performance: Many immature enterprises opt to list on the Growth Enterprise Market (GEM) or the Sci-Tech Innovation Board (STAR Market) for financing purposes. However, due to investors' expectations of their high future growth, the valuations of these companies tend to be higher (stocks are relatively expensive compared to current performance). For investors, investing in such industries carries a great deal of risk, falling under the category of speculative investments with high potential returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
- **成长期**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Growth Stage**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    - 行业特点：行业进入稳定高速的发展阶段，市场需求迅速扩大，销售量急剧上升，行业前景看好【63】。新的进入者增多，但领先企业已经开始确立市场地位。整个行业的盈利能力逐渐提高，很多企业开始盈利并得到快速成长。不过在成长过程中，行业仍可能面临技术迭代、竞争加剧等风险，但总体风险相对幼稚期降低。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Industry characteristics: The industry is entering a stable and rapid development stage, with market demand expanding rapidly and sales soaring. The outlook for the industry is promising [63]. More new entrants are appearing, but leading companies have already established their market positions. The profitability of the entire industry is gradually improving, with many companies starting to make profits and experiencing rapid growth. However, during this period of growth, the industry may still face risks such as technological upgrades and increased competition; nonetheless, these risks are generally lower compared to the early stages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - 证券表现：成长期行业的股票往往涨势迅猛，并呈现出较为明显的趋势性上涨特征（基本面持续改善支撑股价）【12】。虽然短期可能有波动，但总体上股价随业绩增长而上升。对于投资者来说，成长期行业能提供相当丰厚的回报，但仍需警惕成长道路上的波动和不确定性。通常建议选择行业中的头部企业投资，以分享行业高速增长的成果【12】。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Stock Performance: Stocks in growth-stage industries often exhibit a rapid rise with pronounced trend characteristics (fundamentals continuously improving support stock prices). Although there may be short-term fluctuations, overall stock prices tend to rise with improved earnings. For investors, growth-stage industries offer substantial returns but should be approached with caution regarding the volatility and uncertainties along the growth path. Typically, it is recommended to invest in the leading companies within an industry to benefit from its high-growth trajectory [12].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
- **成熟期**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Maturity Stage**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    - 行业特点：行业发展规模已相当大，市场趋于饱和，增长速度逐渐放缓至与经济增速相当或更低【63】。市场竞争格局基本稳定，行业进入者变少甚至开始出现整合淘汰。此阶段行业的收益增长虽不及成长期迅猛，但波动性降低，行业进入相对稳定的发展阶段，利润率可能趋于平均化。成熟期行业适合风险偏好较低的市场参与者，因为行业地位和盈利相对稳定【63】。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Industry characteristics: The industry has grown to a considerable size, with the market approaching saturation, and growth rates have slowed down to match or even fall below economic growth [63]. The competitive landscape is largely stable, with fewer new entrants or even consolidation and elimination of weaker players. While revenue growth in this stage may not be as rapid as during the growth phase, volatility decreases, and the industry enters a relatively stable development phase where profit margins may tend towards average levels. Mature industries are suitable for market participants with lower risk preferences, as their positions and profits tend to be more stable [63].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - 证券表现：成熟期行业公司业绩增长放缓，但由于风险较小，股票价格一般缓步攀升，波动较小【12】。成熟行业股票通常具备长期投资价值，投资者持有这类股票可以获得较稳定的回报（包括稳健的股息等）。对厌恶风险的投资者来说，成熟期行业是**长期持有**的良好选择【12】。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Stock Performance: In mature industries, company performance growth slows down, but due to lower risks, stock prices generally rise steadily with relatively small fluctuations [12]. Stocks from mature sectors typically offer long-term investment value, allowing investors to obtain stable returns (including steady dividends) [12]. For risk-averse investors, mature industries are a **good choice for long-term holding** [12].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
- **衰退期**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Decline Phase**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    - 行业特点：行业总需求开始减少，市场规模萎缩【63】。技术被替代、需求转移或其他因素导致销售持续下滑。不少企业在衰退期会出现亏损，甚至被迫退出市场或破产【63】。整个行业面临重大的结构转型或消亡风险，只有少数适应变化的公司可能存活下来。投资者在此阶段应非常谨慎，持续关注行业动态以避免踩雷。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Industry characteristics: industry total demand begins to decrease, market size shrinks [63]. Sales continue to decline due to replacement of technology, shift in demand, or other factors. Many companies may incur losses during the recession period, forced to exit the market or go bankrupt [63]. The entire industry faces significant structural transformation or extinction risks, with only a few companies that adapt to changes likely to survive. Investors should be very cautious in this stage and closely monitor industry developments to avoid getting caught off guard.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
    - 证券表现：衰退期行业由于基本面恶化，投资价值大打折扣。多数相关公司股票价格低迷，很多沦为低价股或“垃圾股”【12】。投资者若持有这类股票，需要密切关注其市场价格变化，及时调整投资组合以规避进一步损失【63】。一般而言，在行业衰退阶段，应尽量回避该行业投资，或只选择其中少数有重组转型希望的企业谨慎介入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Stock Performance: During a recessionary period, industries suffer from deteriorating fundamentals, significantly reducing their investment value. Many related companies see depressed stock prices, with many becoming low-priced stocks or "junk stocks" [12]. Investors holding such stocks should closely monitor price movements and promptly adjust their portfolios to avoid further losses [63]. Generally, during the industry's downturn phase, it is advisable to avoid investing in that sector or cautiously enter only a few companies with potential for restructuring and transformation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        

上述生命周期各阶段的划分并非绝对，每个行业受技术创新、政策等影响，其生命周期长短和特征可能有所不同。但总体规律是：**高成长伴随高风险，高成熟对应稳收益**。在实际投资中，判断行业所处的生命周期并相应调整策略非常重要。此外，行业生命周期阶段还会影响证券市场的表现：通常幼稚期和成长期行业的股票贝塔值较高（波动较大），而成熟期行业股票防御性较好。投资者应结合自身风险偏好，选择处于适当生命周期阶段且基本面良好的行业进行投资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The division of each stage in the lifecycle is not absolute; the duration and characteristics of an industry's lifecycle can vary depending on technological innovation, policies, among other factors. However, generally speaking, **high growth is accompanied by high risk, while high maturity corresponds to stable returns**. In actual investment practice, identifying where a particular industry stands in its lifecycle and adjusting strategies accordingly is crucial. Additionally, the lifecycle stages of an industry can influence the performance of securities market: typically, stocks from industries in the infant and growth stages have higher betas (higher volatility), whereas those from mature industries exhibit better defensive qualities. Investors should select industries that are at appropriate lifecycle stages and have solid fundamentals, based on their own risk tolerance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**行业的生命周期与证券表现**可以结合上述特征总结如下：成长初期，行业前景诱人但风险巨大，股票估值高企；成长期，行业高速成长，龙头股价随业绩腾飞，是获取超额收益的黄金期；成熟期，行业稳定发展，股票成为“现金牛”，适合长期配置；衰退期，行业日薄西山，股票沦为垃圾股，需要回避。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lifecycle of an industry can be summarized as follows in relation to its impact on securities: during the growth startup phase, the industry offers attractive prospects but carries significant risks; stock valuations are high. During the growth phase, the industry experiences rapid growth, with leading stocks soaring as their performance improves; this is a golden period for generating excess returns. In the mature phase, the industry stabilizes, and stocks become "cash cows," suitable for long-term allocation. Finally, in the decline phase, the industry declines, and stocks become "junk," necessitating avoidance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3 行业选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3 Industry Selection*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行业分析的最终目的是进行行业选择，即根据宏观环境和行业自身特征，选择出具有投资价值或成长潜力的行业。本节从几个实用角度讨论行业选择需要考虑的因素：行业景气程度、行业吸引力、行业所处生命周期阶段，以及影响行业兴衰的关键因素等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ultimate purpose of industry analysis is to make industry selection, that is, to choose industries with investment value or growth potential based on macro-environmental conditions and the characteristics of the industry itself. This section discusses several practical factors to consider in industry selection from a few angles: industry momentum, industry attractiveness, the stage of the industry's lifecycle, and key factors influencing the rise and fall of the industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1行业景气分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1 Industry Ecosystem Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**行业景气分析**是利用经济统计数据衡量行业当前所处的景气阶段和运行趋势的方法。通常通过编制**景气指数**（又称景气度）来实现。景气指数是对所反映的经济现象的一种量化描述指标，能够反映和预测经济或行业的发展状态.行业景气指标具有一定的超前性和预测功能，可靠性较强
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Industry business-cycle analysis** uses economic statistics to identify an industry's current cyclical condition and operating trend. It is commonly implemented through a **business-climate index**, a quantitative measure that describes current conditions and helps anticipate how the economy or industry may develop. These indicators often have leading and forecasting value and can be relatively reliable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

景气指数一般设定**100或50**为景气的临界值（视具体编制而定）：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The leading index is typically set at **100 or 50** as a critical threshold (this can vary depending on specific compilation methods).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当指数高于100（或50）时，表示经济状况或行业景气趋于上升，行业处于景气扩张阶段；当指数低于100（或50）时，表示经济状况下滑，行业处于不景气状态 。通过观察景气指数的变化，可以分析判断经济或行业发展处于周期波动的哪个阶段，并据此进行行业选择。例如，如果某行业景气指数连续上升并越过景气线，表明该行业进入繁荣阶段，投资者可考虑增持该行业；反之则应谨慎。景气分析为行业选择提供了重要的定量依据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the index exceeds 100 (or 50), it indicates that economic conditions or industry conditions are trending upwards, placing the industry in a phase of expansion; when the index falls below 100 (or 50), it suggests economic conditions are deteriorating, and the industry is in a downturn state. By observing changes in the prosperity index, one can analyze and judge which stage of the economic or industry cycle the market is in, and make industry selections accordingly. For instance, if an industry's prosperity index shows continuous upward movement and crosses the prosperity line, it indicates that the industry has entered a period of prosperity, at which point investors may consider increasing their holdings in that industry; conversely, caution should be exercised. Prosperity analysis provides important quantitative basis for industry selection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2行业吸引力分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2 Industry Attraction Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**行业吸引力**指的是一个行业对投资者和企业的吸引程度，通常反映为行业盈利能力和成长空间。行业吸引力受多种因素影响，包括前述“五力”竞争结构因素以及行业自身的成长性等。主要影响因素及其作用可总结如下 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industry Attraction refers to the degree of appeal that an industry holds for investors and businesses, typically reflected in the industry's profitability and growth potential. Industry Attraction is influenced by a variety of factors, including the aforementioned "five forces" competitive structure factors as well as the industry's own growth prospects. The main influencing factors and their effects can be summarized as follows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **行业增长潜力**：增长潜力越大，行业对投资越具吸引力（未来空间大）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Industry Growth Potential**: The greater the growth potential, the more attractive the industry is to investment (with vast future prospects).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **行业发展的主要驱动力**：行业的关键成功要素（如核心技术、资源等）掌握在企业手中越多，行业吸引力越强。 _(注：主要驱动力指推动行业增长的因素，比如政策支持、技术创新、消费升级等。企业能够利用这些驱动力获益，行业就有吸引力。）_
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Main drivers of industry development**: The more key success factors of the industry (such as core technologies, resources, etc.) are in the hands of enterprises, the more attractive the industry is. (Note: The main drivers refer to factors that promote industry growth, such as policy support, technological innovation, consumption upgrading, etc.)If companies can benefit from these drivers, the industry will be attractive.)_
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **进入/退出障碍**：进入障碍越高，新的竞争者越难进入，现有企业盈利越能持续；退出障碍越低，行业能及时出清过剩产能。这两者障碍综合越小（即进出比较自由但新进入者不易大量进入的平衡状态），行业吸引力越强。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Entry and exit barriers:** High entry barriers make it harder for new competitors to enter and help incumbents sustain profits; low exit barriers let the industry eliminate excess capacity promptly. The most attractive configuration combines meaningful barriers to mass entry with relatively easy exit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **市场需求状况**：行业的市场需求越大且增长越快，说明有广阔的市场空间，行业吸引力越强。反之需求疲软则减弱吸引力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Market Demand Situation**: The larger and faster the demand in an industry, the greater the market space and the stronger its attractiveness. Conversely, weak demand diminishes its appeal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **行业竞争力情况**：这里主要指行业内现有的竞争强度（可对应波特五力中的内部竞争、替代品威胁等）。一般来说，**五种竞争压力越弱，行业吸引力就越强**。例如竞争者少且弱、替代品少、供应商和买方议价能力不强，则行业利润空间大，吸引力强、
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Industry Competitiveness Situation**: This primarily refers to the current level of competition within the industry (corresponding to Porter's Five Forces such as internal competition, threat of substitutes, etc.). Generally, the stronger the five competitive pressures, the weaker the attractiveness of the industry. For instance, when there are fewer and weaker competitors, fewer substitute products, and lower bargaining power from suppliers and buyers, the profit margins are larger, and the attractiveness is stronger.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **行业风险及不确定性**：行业所面临的政策、技术、市场等方面的不确定性和波动风险越低，越适合投资者参与，吸引力越大；相反，如果行业高度波动或存在重大不确定性（比如政策反复或技术路线未定），则吸引力打折扣。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Industry Risk and Uncertainty**: The lower the level of uncertainty and volatility in an industry regarding policy, technology, and market factors, the more attractive it is for investors; conversely, if an industry is highly volatile or faces significant uncertainty (such as policy reversals or uncertain technological pathways), its attractiveness is diminished.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **行业总体利润水平**：如果一个行业整体的平均利润率高于社会平均水平且可持续，那么对资本和企业就很有吸引力。高利润会吸引更多资源进入该行业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Overall Industry Profitability Level**: If the average profitability of an industry is higher than the societal average and sustainable, it is very attractive to capital and enterprises. High profits will attract more resources into that industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
通过以上因素综合评价，可以判断一个行业是否值得进入或投资。如果大部分因素都呈现有利状况（高增长、大需求、竞争温和、风险可控、利润率高），那么这个行业对投资者就是有吸引力的，属于应重点考虑的行业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By evaluating these factors comprehensively, one can determine whether an industry is worth entering or investing in. If most of these factors indicate favorable conditions (high growth, large demand, moderate competition, manageable risk, high profit margins), then this industry is attractive to investors and falls under the category of industries that should be given priority consideration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.3生命周期及行业特征
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3 Lifecycle and Industry Characteristics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行业的生命周期阶段也影响行业选择决策。一般而言，从投资角度==应优先选择成长型的行业，以及处于成长阶段或成熟阶段的行业== .因为这两类行业要么高速发展，要么已经建立稳定的盈利模式，具有较好的投资回报前景。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The stages of a sector's lifecycle also influence industry selection decisions. Generally, from an investment perspective, one should prioritize sectors that are either growth-oriented or those in the growth or maturity stages. These sectors either experience rapid development or have already established stable profit models, offering good prospects for investment returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 所谓**成长型行业**，是指增长速度持续快于整个国民经济平均增速的行业 ([证券投资学.pdf](file://xn--file-vajrb8ezcrzdbtvqsdqdmh%23:~:text=-rr64dy30jvp6c0d0fca8154omw8amszklwof/))。这类行业通常伴随新技术或新需求兴起，市场空间不断拓展，蕴含较大潜力。因此成长型行业往往是投资者重点关注的对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By definition, a **growth industry** refers to an industry that grows at a faster rate than the average growth of the entire national economy. Such industries typically arise alongside the emergence of new technologies or new demands, expanding market spaces and holding significant potential. Therefore, growth industries are often a focal point for investors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **成熟期的行业**虽然增速放缓，但基础稳固、盈利逐年增加，行业龙头具备稳定的市场地位 ([证券投资学.pdf](file://xn--file-vajrb8ezcrzdbtvqsdqdmh%23:~:text=:-dz26d1sh5ks83jndfw2nda8763ezzmpszhy2b9q0e8pnkz1fiz7adl3gdb5eeh4dcf1cfam/))。对于风险规避型投资者，成熟期且仍有一定成长性的行业也很有吸引力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Mature industry sectors**, although experiencing slower growth, have stable foundations and increasing profitability year over year, with industry leaders maintaining a solid market position. ([Investment Securities Studies.pdf](file://xn--file-vajrb8ezcrzdbtvqsdqdmh%23:~:text=:-dz26d1sh5ks83jndfw2nda8763ezzmpszhy2b9q0e8pnkz1fiz7adl3gdb5eeh4dcf1cfam/)) For risk-averse investors, mature industries that still exhibit some growth potential are also very attractive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

相比之下，处于幼稚期的行业不确定性太高、失败率高，投资风险大；而衰退期行业前景黯淡，投资价值不佳。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compared to industries in their nascent stages, those in decline are too uncertain, have high failure rates, and carry significant investment risks; in contrast, industries in the maturing phase exhibit higher levels of uncertainty but also offer greater potential for investment value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 影响行业兴衰的主要因素
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Key factors influencing the rise and fall of industries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断一个行业是否值得长期投资，还需要认识驱动**产业兴衰更替**的关键要素。从长期看，行业的兴起或衰落往往由以下因素主导：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To determine whether an industry is worth long-term investment, one must also understand the key factors that drive the rise and fall of industries. In the long run, the emergence or decline of an industry is often influenced by the following factors:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **技术变革**：核心技术的突破往往催生新行业兴起，同时让旧有行业走向衰落（如数码相机的出现终结了胶卷行业，智能手机兴起打击了传统PC行业）。技术进步是产业更替最主要的驱动力之一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Technological Transformation**: Breakthroughs in core technologies often give rise to new industries while old ones decline (such as the emergence of digital cameras ending the film industry, and the rise of smartphones diminishing the traditional PC sector). Technological progress is one of the primary drivers of industrial succession.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **政策导向**：政府产业政策的扶持或限制会大大影响行业的兴衰。例如可再生能源行业因政策补贴迅速壮大，而高污染行业可能因环保政策收紧而加速衰退。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Policy Orientation**: Government industrial policies can significantly influence an industry's rise and fall. For instance, the renewable energy sector has grown rapidly due to policy subsidies, while high-polluting industries may accelerate their decline as stringent environmental policies tighten.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **需求变化**：消费者偏好和需求结构的改变直接决定行业的命运。如果社会需求发生转移（例如健康饮食趋势导致碳酸饮料需求下滑而健康食品行业崛起），相应行业就会此消彼长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Demand Changes**: Shifts in consumer preferences and demand structures directly determine an industry's fate. If societal demands shift (such as a trend towards healthier eating leading to a decline in soft drink demand while the health food sector grows), corresponding industries will rise and fall.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **社会与人口因素**：人口结构变迁（老龄化、出生率下降）会令相关产业兴衰（养老护理产业兴起，婴幼儿产业萎缩）；生活方式变化、城镇化进程等也会造就新的产业机会或淘汰旧模式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Social and Demographic Factors**: Changes in population structure (aging populations, declining birth rates) will lead to the rise and fall of related industries (the growth of the elderly care industry, the shrinking of the baby boomer industry); lifestyle changes and urbanization processes will also create new opportunities for industries or eliminate outdated models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **全球化因素**：全球经济一体化或逆全球化都会重塑产业格局。全球化使一些产业向优势地区转移（制造业向新兴市场转移促进当地行业兴盛）；逆全球化和贸易摩擦则可能打击出口依赖型产业并扶植本土替代产业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Globalization Factors**: Global economic integration or de-integration will reshape industrial landscapes. Globalization shifts some industries to advantageous regions (manufacturing moving to emerging markets boosts local industries); de-globalization and trade frictions may dampen export-dependent industries and foster domestic alternatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

以上因素往往共同作用决定了产业的生命周期长短和兴衰路径。对于投资者而言，需要密切关注这些宏观和结构性因素，对行业所处的大环境有清晰认识，从而预测行业的长期趋势。这也是“自上而下”进行行业选择时的重要考虑：选择受到正面因素驱动（技术进步、政策利好、需求上升）的朝阳行业，回避受到负面因素冲击（技术替代、政策压制、需求萎缩）的夕阳行业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These factors often work together to determine the length and trajectory of an industry's lifecycle. For investors, it is crucial to keep a close eye on these macro- and structural factors and have a clear understanding of the industry’s broader environment in order to predict long-term trends. This consideration is also important when making “top-down” industry selections: choosing industries that are driven by positive factors (such as technological progress, policy benefits, and rising demand), and avoiding those that are impacted by negative factors (such as technological obsolescence, policy constraints, and declining demand).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.4 行业分析方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.4 Industry Analysis Methods*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在完成对行业基本面的研究之后，分析者还需要掌握科学的方法论来开展行业分析。常用的行业分析方法有以下几种，它们可定性、定量地从不同角度研究行业：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After completing a fundamental analysis of the industry, analysts need to master scientific methodological approaches to conduct industry analysis. Common methods for industry analysis include several qualitative and quantitative techniques that can examine the industry from various perspectives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **定性分析法**：运用专家判断、经验和直觉，对行业的结构、趋势和关键因素进行质量上的分析的方法。定性分析侧重对难以量化的内容（如政策影响、管理效率、商业模式等）进行评估，依赖分析师对行业的深入了解和逻辑推理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Qualitative Analysis Method**: Utilizing expert judgment, experience, and intuition to analyze the structure, trends, and key factors of an industry qualitatively. Qualitative analysis focuses on assessing unquantifiable aspects (such as policy impacts, management efficiency, business models, etc.) through analysts' deep understanding of the industry and logical reasoning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **定量分析法**：运用数学、统计和计量模型，对行业相关的数据进行数量化分析的方法。通过构建模型、处理数据，定量分析可以较客观地揭示行业变量之间的关系（比如销量与收入增长率、利润与成本结构等），常用的工具有回归分析、趋势预测模型等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Quantitative Analysis Method**: Utilizing mathematical, statistical, and econometric models to quantitatively analyze industry-related data. By constructing models and processing data, quantitative analysis can reveal relationships between variables in an industry in a relatively objective manner (such as sales versus revenue growth rate, profit versus cost structure, etc.). Common tools include regression analysis and trend forecasting models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **历史资料研究法**：收集整理行业发展的**历史数据和资料**，对行业演进规律进行研究的方法。通过研究行业过去的周期、重大事件和演变过程，分析师可以从历史中寻找启示，了解行业波动的原因和模式，用于推断未来可能的发展轨迹。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Historical Research Method**: This involves collecting and organizing historical data and materials related to industry development, and conducting research on the evolution patterns of the industry. By studying the cycles, significant events, and developmental processes of the industry in the past, analysts can draw insights from history to understand the reasons and patterns behind industry fluctuations, which can then be used to infer possible future developments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **调查研究法**：通过问卷调查、深度访谈、实地走访等手段获取**一手资料**，对行业现状进行研究的方法。这种方法可以获得最新、最直接的行业信息，例如企业经营者对未来的预期、消费者偏好变化等，有助于弥补公开数据的滞后性和局限性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Survey Research Method**: Through methods such as questionnaire surveys, in-depth interviews, and field visits, one obtains primary data to conduct research on industry conditions. This method can provide the most recent and direct information about the industry, such as business operators' expectations for the future and changes in consumer preferences, helping to fill gaps in publicly available data that may be outdated or limited.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **比较研究法**：通过对不同行业之间或同一行业在不同地区、不同时间的状况进行对比分析，归纳行业共性与个性的方法。例如比较国内外同一行业的发展水平，或比较本行业与其他行业的投资回报率等，从比较中发现影响行业差异的因素，找到行业的竞争优势或不足。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Comparative Research Method**: An analytical approach that involves comparing the conditions of different industries or the same industry across different regions and time periods to identify commonalities and unique characteristics. For example, comparing the development levels of the same industry domestically and internationally, or comparing the investment returns of a particular industry with other industries. From these comparisons, one can discern factors influencing industry differences, and identify strengths or weaknesses within the industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **归纳与演绎法**：这是两种逻辑推理的方法。**归纳法**是从具体的个别现象出发，总结提炼出一般性的行业规律；**演绎法**是从一般理论或原理出发，推导出特定行业的结论。在行业分析中，两者结合使用：先演绎运用经济学原理形成对行业的假设，再通过归纳实证检验这些假设，不断修正认识。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Induction and Deduction**: These are two methods of logical reasoning. **Induction** involves starting from specific individual phenomena to derive general industry rules; **deduction** begins with general theories or principles to derive specific conclusions about an industry. In industry analysis, both methods are often used together: first applying economic principles deductively to form hypotheses about the industry, then empirically testing these hypotheses through induction, and continuously refining understanding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **数理统计法**：运用统计学和数学的方法处理行业数据，分析其内在规律的方法。比如利用时间序列分析预测行业景气指数走势、采用聚类分析对行业内企业分类、利用投入产出模型量化行业关联度等。数理统计法能为行业分析提供严谨的定量支撑，提高结论的可靠性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Statistical and Mathematical Methods**: Utilizing statistical and mathematical methods to process industry data and analyze its inherent patterns. For instance, employing time series analysis to forecast the trend of industry prosperity indices, using cluster analysis to classify companies within an industry, and utilizing input-output models to quantify the industry's interconnectivity. Statistical and mathematical methods provide rigorous quantitative support for industry analysis, enhancing the reliability of conclusions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

不同的方法各有侧重，实际分析中常常需要综合运用多种方法：先定性后定量，宏观比较与微观调查相结合，历史分析与模型预测相结合，以便对行业有全方位、多层次的把握。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Different methods have their own emphases; in actual analysis, it is often necessary to combine multiple methods: qualitative analysis followed by quantitative analysis, combining macro comparisons with micro surveys, integrating historical analysis with model predictions, in order to gain a comprehensive and multi-layered understanding of the industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    - **行业分析概述**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Overview of Industry Analysis**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
        - 行业与产业含义：行业=产品可替代性强的一群企业；产业=由相关行业构成的大领域（例：第三产业）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Meaning of *industry* and *sector*: an industry is a group of firms whose products are close substitutes; a sector is a broader field composed of related industries—for example, the tertiary sector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
        - 行业的分类：按经济周期相关性（成长型、周期型、防御型）、要素密集度（劳动力、资本、资源、技术密集型）、技术先进程度（新兴产业 vs 传统产业）、发展前景（朝阳产业 vs 夕阳产业，幼稚产业 vs 成熟产业）等多种分类方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Industry classification: By economic cycle relevance (growth-oriented, cyclical, defensive), intensity of inputs (labor-intensive, capital-intensive, resource-intensive, technology-intensive), level of technological advancement (new industries vs traditional industries), growth prospects (morning star industries vs sunset industries, infant industries vs mature industries), and other classification methods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
        - 行业分析的分类：总体报告（全面、信息量大）、专项报告（聚焦特定方面）、预测报告（预判未来趋势）、动态报告（跟踪最新动态）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Classification of industry analysis: Comprehensive reports (comprehensive, information-rich), specialized reports (focusing on specific aspects), forecast reports (predicting future trends), and dynamic reports (tracking latest developments).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
    - **行业分析的基本内容**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Basic Content of Industry Analysis**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
        - 行业基本经济特性：考察行业的产业地位与边界、资本特征、周期属性、市场结构、主要厂商/产品、主要供应商/原料、技术水平及前景等；列出主要经济变量（市场规模、利润率、生命周期阶段、竞争范围、竞争者/消费者规模、技术更新速度、产品差异化、规模经济、学习曲线、进入退出障碍等）及其表现形式，以刻画行业基本面。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Industry Basic Economic Characteristics: Investigate the industry's position and boundaries, capital characteristics, cyclical attributes, market structure, major manufacturers/products, main suppliers/raw materials, technological level, and prospects; list the main economic variables (market size, profit margin, life cycle stage, competitive scope, competitor/consumer scale, rate of technological innovation, product differentiation, economies of scale, learning curve, barriers to entry and exit) and their manifestations to portray the fundamental aspects of the industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
        - 行业环境分析：评估外部环境对行业的影响，包括政策因素（产业政策导向、扶持或限制措施）、技术因素（技术进步催生新产业/淘汰旧产业）、社会因素（消费习惯改变影响产业兴衰）、国际因素（全球化或逆全球化重塑行业格局）等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Industry Environment Analysis: Evaluating the impact of external environments on an industry, including policy factors (industrial policies, supportive or restrictive measures), technological factors (technological advancements creating new industries or obsoleting old ones), social factors (changes in consumption habits affecting the rise and fall of industries), and international factors (globalization or de-globalization reshaping industry landscapes).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
        - 行业结构分析：分析行业内部竞争格局。首先看市场结构（产业集中度指标：CR_n、HHI衡量行业垄断程度）；然后运用**波特五力模型**评估五种竞争压力（新进入者威胁、供应商议价力、购买者议价力、替代品威胁、现有竞争者竞争程度）。五力越弱，行业吸引力越强。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Industry Structure Analysis: Analyze the competitive landscape within the industry. Start by examining the market structure (indicators of industry concentration: CR_n, HHI measuring industry monopoly); then apply the **Porter's Five Forces Model** to assess five types of competitive pressures (threat of new entrants, bargaining power of suppliers, bargaining power of buyers, threat of substitute products, intensity of competition among existing competitors). The weaker the Five Forces, the stronger the industry attractiveness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
        - 周期分析：观察行业与宏观经济周期和自身生命周期。宏观方面，当经济上行时周期性行业表现好，经济下行时防御性行业抗跌。行业自身方面，判断行业处于幼稚、成长期、成熟或衰退阶段，不同阶段特征：幼稚期高风险、高投入；成长期高速增长、盈利上升；成熟期增长放缓、竞争稳定；衰退期需求萎缩、企业退出。结合阶段特征分析投资价值（成长/成熟期行业投资价值更高）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Cyclical Analysis: Observing industry performance in relation to economic cycles and the lifecycle of the company. Macro-wise, during an economic upturn, cyclical industries tend to perform well, while defensive industries fare better during downturns. At the industry level, assessing whether it is in its infancy, growth, maturity, or decline stage reveals different characteristics: infancy involves high risk and high investment; growth is marked by rapid expansion and rising profits; maturity sees a slowdown in growth and stable competition; decline is characterized by shrinking demand and business exits. Analyzing these characteristics helps determine investment value (higher for mature/growth industries).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
    - **行业选择**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Industry Selection**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
        
        - 行业景气分析：通过景气指数等**领先指标**判断行业景气度。景气指数 > 临界值（50或100）表示行业繁荣上升，< 临界值表示行业低迷下降。据此决定增减对该行业的投资权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Industry business-cycle analysis: use a business-climate index and other **leading indicators** to assess conditions. An index above its threshold—often 50 or 100—signals expansion, while a value below the threshold signals contraction. Investors can use the result to raise or lower the industry's portfolio weight.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
        - 行业吸引力分析：综合评估行业的投资吸引力因素（增长潜力、主要驱动力、进入壁垒、市场需求、竞争格局、风险不确定性、利润水平等）。这些因素越有利，行业整体吸引力越强。可利用五力模型结果和行业指标来比较不同行业的吸引力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Industry Attraction Analysis: A comprehensive evaluation of investment attractiveness factors in an industry (growth potential, main drivers, entry barriers, market demand, competitive landscape, risk uncertainty, profit levels, etc.). The stronger these factors, the greater the overall attractiveness of the industry. Utilize results from the Five Forces model and industry indicators to compare the attractiveness of different industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
        - 生命周期特征与行业选择：优选成长型行业和生命周期处于成长期/成熟期的行业进行投资，规避幼稚期（不确定高）和衰退期（前景不佳）的行业。成长型行业增长快于GDP，成熟期行业基础稳健盈利稳定，这两类行业预期收益/风险比更优。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Characteristics of the life cycle and industry selection: prioritize investing in growth-oriented industries and those in the growth or maturity stages of their life cycles, while avoiding industries in the infancy stage (high uncertainty) and decline phase (poor prospects). Growth industries grow faster than GDP, mature industries have a solid foundation with stable profits, making these two types of industries more favorable in terms of expected returns/risk ratios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
        - 行业兴衰因素：关注驱动行业兴起或衰落的关键**长期因素**——技术变革（新技术带来新行业，淘汰旧行业）、政策导向（政策扶持或打压）、需求结构变化（消费趋势改变行业命运）、社会人口因素（如老龄化、新生代偏好）、国际环境变化（全球化与贸易格局）。这些因素决定行业的长周期走势，是进行战略性行业选择时必须评估的背景。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Factors of Industry Rise and Decline: Focus on the key **long-term factors** driving industry growth or decline—technological change (new technologies creating new industries while obsoleting old ones), policy orientation (supporting or suppressing policies), shifts in demand structures (consumption trends altering industry fates), social demographic factors (such as aging populations, preferences of younger generations), and changes in the international environment (globalization and trade patterns). These factors determine the long-term trajectory of an industry and are essential considerations when making strategic industry choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Qualitative Analysis: Utilizing expert judgment and experience to make qualitative assessments of industry structures and trends (suitable for unquantifiable factors).

- Quantitative Analysis: Collecting industry data to establish models for quantitative analysis (utilizing statistical and econometric methods, resulting in objective conclusions).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
        - 历史资料研究：研究行业历史演变规律，总结周期和事件影响，为预测未来提供依据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Historical Data Research: Investigating the patterns of industry evolution, summarizing the impacts of cycles and events, to provide a basis for forecasting the future.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
        - 归纳与演绎：应用逻辑推理，从个别现象归纳普遍规律，再从普遍原理演绎具体结论，验证并深化对行业的认识。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Induction and Deduction: Applying logical reasoning to derive general principles from individual phenomena, then deducing specific conclusions from these universal principles to deepen understanding of the industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
            
        - 数理统计：运用统计学工具和计量模型，对行业数据进行严谨分析（如景气指数预测、相关性分析等），提高结论的可靠性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Mathematical Statistics: Utilizing statistical tools and modeling techniques, rigorous analysis of industry data is conducted (such as predicting business indices and correlation analyses), thereby enhancing the reliability of conclusions.
