### 1.1.1. 价格指数可以解释和反应大部分行为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1.1. Price indices discount most market activity.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场的价格指数预先反映了市场参与者整体的市 场行为，无论是来自于投资者、中介者还是监管者； 收盘价在道氏理论中是最重要的价格，因为它包 含了交易日内的所有行为和信息，因此相比盘中 价格而言更加不容易被操纵
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market price indices already incorporate the aggregate actions and information of investors, intermediaries, and regulators. In Dow Theory, the closing price is the most important price because it reflects the full trading day's activity and information and is therefore harder to manipulate than an intraday price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.1.2. 市场中包括3种趋势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1.2. Market trends include three types.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (1) 主要趋势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Primary Trend*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主要多头市场:每一个后续折返都比前一个反弹达到了更高的价格，同时每一 个次级折返都在比前一折返更高的价位停止
主要空头市场:每一个后续折返都将价格压到更低的水平，同时每一个次级折 返未能将价格拉升到前一折返的顶部之上。
主要趋势是真正的长期投资者唯一关注的趋势。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Primary bull market: Each successive rally reaches a higher high, and each secondary reaction ends at a higher low than the preceding one.
Primary bear market: Each successive decline reaches a lower low, and each secondary rally fails to rise above the preceding rally's high.
For a genuinely long-term investor, the primary trend is the trend that matters most.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (2)次级趋势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Secondary Trend*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

又称为次级折返（secondary reaction）或中期折 返（intermediate reaction） 
• 价格在沿着主要趋势防线演进中产生的重要回调。 既可以是多头市场中的下跌走势，也可以是空头市 场中的上涨走势 
• 次级趋势通常持续3周至几个月的时间。在此期间内， 折返走势的价格回调幅度通常为前一次级折返走势 后价格主要变动幅度的1/3到2/3 
• 回调少于1/3的波动不称为次级折返。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Also called a *secondary reaction* or *intermediate reaction*.

- It is a material counter-trend move within the primary trend: a decline during a primary bull market or a rally during a primary bear market.
- A secondary trend normally lasts from 3 weeks to several months. Its retracement is commonly between one-third and two-thirds of the preceding primary move.
- A move that retraces less than one-third is not treated as a secondary reaction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (3)微小趋势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Minor Trend*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也可称为短期趋势，它是指非常短暂的价格波动 
• 微小趋势的持续时间通常在3周以内——通常会少于6天， 而个别情况下可能会延续到6周左右 
• 受到了短期投资者以及投机者的青睐，因为他们只关心短 期的价格变动，并试图从中获得超常收益 
• 是主要趋势和次级趋势的组成部分，因此其走向对于判断 次要趋势进而判断主要趋势具有一定的启示作用 
• 易被人为因素所操纵，因此从这种日间波动中所推出的结 论通常会误导投资者
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Also called a short-term trend, a minor trend is a brief price fluctuation.

- It normally lasts less than 3 weeks—often less than 6 days, although it can occasionally continue for about 6 weeks.
- It attracts short-term traders and speculators who seek abnormal returns from short-run price movements.
- Because minor trends make up secondary and primary trends, their direction can offer clues about the larger trend.
- They are comparatively easy to manipulate, so conclusions drawn from day-to-day fluctuations can mislead investors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2 道氏理论的缺陷
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2 Deficiencies of Dow Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

道氏理论发出的信号具有滞后性 
	买入和卖出信号通常发生在指数到达峰位或谷底 之后20%~25%的位置；
	 从市场历史中所总结出的经验来看，当工业指数 的收益率降低至3%以下时，通常表明市场已经到 达顶部，即将转入下跌行情，而当指数收益率达 到6%以上时则意味着市场已经到达底部，趋势即 将出现反弹
		  • 如果指数收益率达到了上述水平，而两种指数 并没有相互确认，道氏理论并不会将这种形态 视作买入或卖出的信号。
指数确认的时间间隔并不明确 
	两种指数的波动不可能完全同步，在大多数情况 下即使它们能够相互确认从而对趋势加以肯定， 在它们之间也会存在一定的时间间隔 
	道氏理论并没有明确地指出两种指数相互确认的 间隔时间的有效性。这使得投资者在运用道氏理 论对两种指数同时进行分析时，常常会显得比较 迷茫
道氏理论只对长期投资者有较大的指导意义 
	道氏理论对于中期趋势和短期趋势的反转只给出了 很少或者根本不给出任何信号 4
道氏理论并不能指导个股投资 
	尽管所有的股票都倾向于与市场（以指数为标志） 同向运动，但实际各只具体股票的价格在市场中所 留下的轨迹有较大的差异 指数毕竟仅仅是一种把多组数据通过简单的数学方 法（通常是加权）编织成一组数据的工具
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dow Theory signals are delayed.

Buy and sell signals commonly arrive only after an index has already moved 20%–25% away from its peak or trough. Historically, a yield on the industrial average below 3% was taken as evidence that the market was near a top and might turn down, whereas a yield above 6% suggested that the market was near a bottom and might rebound. Even at those levels, Dow Theory does not recognize a buy or sell signal unless the two averages confirm one another.

The permitted interval between confirmations is unclear.

The two averages cannot move in perfect synchrony, so a time gap usually exists even when they eventually confirm the same trend. Dow Theory does not specify how long that gap may remain valid, which makes simultaneous interpretation difficult.

Dow Theory is most useful to long-term investors.

It gives few, if any, signals for reversals in intermediate or short-term trends.

Dow Theory does not select individual stocks.

Although individual stocks tend to move with the broader market represented by an index, their actual price paths differ substantially. An index is only a mathematically aggregated—usually weighted—summary of many securities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20250509134422.png]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diagram: the engulfing pattern, also known here as the embracing or outside-bar pattern.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别依据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Criteria for Identification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1、在吞没形态之前，市场必须处在清晰可辨的上升趋势或下降趋势中， 哪怕这个趋势只是最短期的。 
2、吞没形态必须由2条K线组成。其中第二根K线的实体必须覆盖第一根K 线的实体（但不一定要吞没前者的上下影线）。 
3、吞没形态的第2个实体必须与第一个实体的颜色相反。这条标准的例外 情况是：第一条线的实体必须非常小，几乎构成了一根十字线，即在长期 的下降趋势后，一个小小的白色实体被一个巨大的白色实体所吞没，也可 能构成底部翻转形态。反之，在上升趋势中，如果一个小小的黑色实体为 一个巨大的黑色实体所吞没，也可能构成顶部反转形态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Before an engulfing pattern appears, the market must be in a clearly identifiable uptrend or downtrend, even if it is only a very short-term trend.
2. The pattern consists of 2 candlesticks. The second real body must engulf the first real body, although it need not engulf the first candle's shadows.
3. The second real body normally has the opposite color from the first. An exception can arise when the first body is so small that it is almost a doji: after a prolonged downtrend, a very small white body engulfed by a large white body may still mark a bottom reversal; conversely, in an uptrend, a very small black body engulfed by a large black body may mark a top reversal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

注：以下情况出现时吞没形态的信号更强烈
1、在吞没形态中，第一天的实体非常小，而第二天的实体非常大。
2、在超长期的或非常急剧的市场运动之后。 
3、吞没形态中的第二个实体伴有超额的交易量。 
4、在吞没形态中，第二天的实体向前吞没的实体不止一个。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The engulfing signal is stronger in the following circumstances:

1. The first day's real body is very small and the second day's real body is very large.
2. It appears after an exceptionally long or sharp market move.
3. The second real body is accompanied by unusually high trading volume.
4. The second day's real body engulfs more than 1 preceding real body.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乌云盖顶形态一般出现在上升趋势之后，有时也可能出 现在水平调整区间的顶部。在这一形态中，第一天是一 根坚挺的白色实体，第二天的开盘价超过了第一天的最 高价（即第一天的上影线的顶端，但是收盘价却收在接 近当日的最低价的水平，并且收盘价明显地向下扎入到 第一天白色实体的内部。第二天的黑色实体向下穿进第 一天的白色实体的程度越深（超过前一天白色实体的 50%），则该形态构成顶部反转的可能性就越大。如果黑色实体的收盘价没 有向下穿过前一天白色K线的中点，则还须有进一步的看跌信号验证。

The dark cloud cover pattern normally appears after an uptrend, although it can also form at the top of a sideways consolidation range. The first day has a strong white real body. On the second day, the market opens above the first day's high—the top of its upper shadow—but closes near the day's low and well inside the first day's white body. The deeper the second day's black body penetrates the first day's white body—especially beyond 50%—the more likely the pattern is to mark a top reversal. If the black candle does not close below the midpoint of the preceding white candle, further bearish confirmation is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.2数浪划分原则

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数浪铁律： 
推动浪以5浪运行，修正浪以3浪运行

在同一级别的推动浪中3浪不允许是最短的 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hard rules for wave counting:

- A motive wave unfolds in 5 waves; a corrective wave unfolds in 3 waves.
- Wave 2 may not move beyond the starting point of Wave 1.
- Among the motive waves of the same degree, Wave 3 may not be the shortest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交替原则

Guidelines for wave counting:

- Principle of alternation.
- Wave 4 is highly likely to take a complex form, and it commonly ends within the price range of Wave 4 at the next lower degree.

### (1)MA

先 求 移动 平均 值 ， 再 在 坐标 图 上 绘制 成 线 YP 移动 平均 数 MA = 扎 N N: 计算 的 日 期 数
![[Pasted image 20250509153639.png]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First calculate the moving average, then plot the sequence as a line on a coordinate chart. An $N$-period simple moving average can be written as

$$MA_N=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}P_i,$$

where $N$ is the number of periods and $P_i$ is the observed price in period $i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

葛兰维尔均线系统八大买卖法则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Granville's Eight Rules for Moving Averages
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1.当MA由持续下降转为平衡或上升，股价从MA下方突 破并向上延升时，买进
2.MA呈上升状态，股价跌至MA以下时，买进 
3.股价在MA之上，且向MA靠近，在尚未击穿MA又再度 上升时，买进 
4.当MA下降，股价在MA以下大幅下降时，买进
5.MA上升后转为平移或下降状态，股价则跌至MA之下， 卖出  
6.MA持续下降，股价在突破MA上升后又回落到MA以下 时，卖出 
7.股价线在MA的下方，并朝着MA的方向上升，在未到达 MA时再次回落，卖出
8.MA呈上升态势，股价线在其上方突然暴涨至远离MA时， 卖出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Buy when the MA stops falling and flattens or turns upward while the stock price breaks above it and continues to rise.
2. Buy when the MA is rising and the stock price pulls back below it.
3. Buy when the price remains above a rising MA, approaches it without breaking through, and then rises again.
4. Buy when a falling price moves unusually far below a declining MA.
5. Sell when a rising MA flattens or turns down and the price falls below it.
6. Sell when the MA continues to decline and the price briefly breaks above it before falling back below it.
7. Sell when the price is below a declining MA, rises toward it without reaching it, and then turns down again.
8. Sell when the MA is rising but the price suddenly surges far above it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==看相对位置,股价相对升高就卖,低了就买==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==Focus on relative position: consider selling when the price becomes high relative to the MA and buying when it becomes low relative to the MA.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (2)平滑异同移动平均线MACD
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用DIF绘制.DIF使用EMA绘制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DIF is plotted from the difference between EMAs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$DIF=EMA(12)-EMA(26)$

$EMA(12)=\frac{2}{(12+1)}\times 今日收盘价+\frac{11}{(12+1)}\times 昨日EMA(12)$
$EMA(26)=\frac{2}{(26+1)}\times 今日收盘价+\frac{25}{(26+1)}\times 昨日EMA(26)$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$EMA(12)=\frac{2}{(12+1)}\times \text{today's closing price}+\frac{11}{(12+1)}\times \text{yesterday's }EMA(12)$
$EMA(26)=\frac{2}{(26+1)}\times \text{today's closing price}+\frac{25}{(26+1)}\times \text{yesterday's }EMA(26)$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

DEA是DIF的移动平均
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DEA is a moving average of DIF and serves as MACD's signal line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$MACD=DIF-DEA$

DIF和DEA都大于0时是多头市场,DIF向上穿过DEA买入,叫金叉
反之叫死叉
DIF二次死叉的时候下跌幅度很深二次金叉上升会高
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When both DIF and DEA are above 0, the market is bullish. A cross of DIF above DEA is a *golden cross* and a buy signal; the reverse is a *death cross*. A second death cross is often followed by a steep decline, whereas a second golden cross can precede a strong rise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==行情的高点比前一次的高点高，MACD指标的高点 却比指标前一次的高点低（顶背离）时，暗示行情很快 将会反转下跌==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==When the market makes a higher high but MACD makes a lower high—a bearish divergence—the market may soon reverse downward.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==行情的低点比前一次的低点低，MACD指标的低 点却比前一次的低点高（底背离）时，暗示行情很快就 会反转上涨==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==When the market makes a lower low but MACD makes a higher low—a bullish divergence—the market may soon reverse upward.==

### (1)强弱指标RSJ
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Relative Strength Index (RSI)*

容易判定行情的强弱势头，对制定投资策略有很 大的帮助
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Advantages:

- RSI is a leading indicator and may reveal a top, bottom, or trend breakout relatively early.
- It makes the strength of the market easier to assess and can therefore help formulate an investment strategy.

缺点
该项指标比较复杂和难以掌握，特别是在图形形态 分析方面比较容易导致主观性的判断

Disadvantages:

- The indicator is relatively complex and difficult to master; chart-pattern interpretation in particular can invite subjective judgments.

### (2)威廉指标WMS
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Williams %R (WMS)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$WR=\frac{H_{n}-C}{H_{n}-L_{n}} \times 100\%$

C：当日收市价 
Ln ：n日内最低价 
Hn ：n日内最高价 
n：分析周期（10日或20日） 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$C$: today's closing price

$L_n$: the lowest price during the preceding $n$ days

$H_n$: the highest price during the preceding $n$ days

$n$: the analysis window, such as 10 or 20 days
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$WMS=\frac{当日收盘价- N日内最低价}{N日内最高价-N日内最低价}$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$WMS=\frac{\text{current closing price}-\text{lowest price over }N\text{ days}}{\text{highest price over }N\text{ days}-\text{lowest price over }N\text{ days}}$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

WMS应用法则:
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rules for Applying WMS:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当下跌至80以下后，再向上突破80超买线时，买进 
当上升至20以上后，向下跌破20超卖线时，卖出 
由超卖区向上爬升时，只是表示行情趋势转向， 若是突破50中轴线，便是涨势转强，考虑追买
由超买区向下滑落，跌破50中轴线，可以确认跌 势转强，应追卖
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- After WMS falls below 80, buy when it crosses back above the 80 overbought line.
- After WMS rises above 20, sell when it crosses back below the 20 oversold line.
- A rise out of the oversold region shows only that the direction is changing. A break above the 50 centerline indicates that the advance is strengthening and may justify buying.
- A fall out of the overbought region and below the 50 centerline confirms that the decline is strengthening and may justify selling.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集超买超卖信号和买卖信号于一身，出现超卖(买) 信号不等于可以买进（卖出），只是警告不要盲目在 此价值追卖（买）而已.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
WMS combines overbought or oversold warnings with trading signals. An oversold or overbought reading does not by itself mean that one should immediately buy or sell; it is chiefly a warning not to keep selling into an oversold market or buying into an overbought market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (1)OBOS超买超卖指标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Overbought/Oversold Indicator (OBOS)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$OBOS(n)=P_{1}-P_{2}$
P_1:n日内每天上涨家数之和
P_2:n日内每天下跌家数之和
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$$OBOS(n)=P_1-P_2$$

$P_1$: the sum of the daily number of advancing issues over $n$ days

$P_2$: the sum of the daily number of declining issues over $n$ days
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OBOS离0越远，则说明市场多空力量的对比越悬殊， 大盘处于明显的上涨或下跌之中
OBOS过分大或者过分小时，都说明市场的涨势或跌 势走过了头
当股票指数的表现与OBOS的走势步调不一致时，将 出现背离现象
形态与趋势分析的工具应用在OBOS上，在高位出现 的双顶、三顶等的意义尤其重要
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The farther OBOS moves from 0, the larger the imbalance between advancing and declining issues and the more clearly the broad market is rising or falling. An extremely high or low OBOS suggests that the move has gone too far. A divergence occurs when the stock index and OBOS no longer move together. Chart-pattern and trend-analysis tools can also be applied to OBOS; double and triple tops at high levels are especially significant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (2)腾落指标ADL
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Advance-Decline Line (ADL)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

今日ADL=前一日ADL+NA-ND 
NA：上涨家数 ND：下跌家数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Today's $ADL=$ previous day's $ADL+NA-ND$

$NA$: number of advancing issues

$ND$: number of declining issues
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指数与ADL同向，趋势不变
ADL与指数背离，大盘将转向 
大盘在较高较低，ADL走平，转势一触即发 
ADL在趋势中发生转折然后又恢复，趋势依然成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- When the index and ADL move in the same direction, the existing trend remains intact.
- When ADL diverges from the index, the broad market may be about to reverse.
- If the market is at an extreme high or low while ADL moves sideways, a reversal may be imminent.
- If ADL briefly turns against the trend and then resumes its original direction, the trend remains intact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (3)涨跌比率ADR
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Advance-Decline Ratio (ADR)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 一 定时 期 内 股票 的 上 涨 家 数 与 下 跌 家 数 的 比值 ， 测 股票 市 场 多 空 力 量 的 对 比 ， 进 而 推断 大 势 的 去 向 计算 公式 : ADR, =f ow) P: nn 日 内 每 天 上 涨 家 数 之 和 已 。n 日 内 每 天 下 跌 家 数 之 和
![[Pasted image 20250509163705.png]]
ADR 取 值 区 间 : 0 以 上 0.5-1.5: 多 空 双方 平衡 ，- 观望 ADR 与 指数 涨 跌 默契 ， 短 期 震荡 横 盘 ADR 与 指数 背离 ， 短 期 内 反弹 或 回调 ADR 从 低 向 高 超过 0. 5， 并 在 0、5 上 下 波动 多 次 时 ， 跌 势 进入 尾声 ADR 从 高 向 低下 降 到 0. 75 时 ， 是 短期 反弹 的 信号
![[Pasted image 20250509163712.png]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ADR compares the number of advancing and declining issues over a fixed period to gauge the balance between buying and selling pressure and infer the broad market's direction.

$$ADR(n)=\frac{P_1}{P_2}$$

$P_1$: the sum of the daily number of advancing issues over $n$ days

$P_2$: the sum of the daily number of declining issues over $n$ days

ADR is greater than 0. A value between 0.5 and 1.5 indicates that bullish and bearish forces are roughly balanced, suggesting a wait-and-see stance. When ADR moves in step with the index, the market may consolidate sideways in the short term; when ADR diverges from the index, a short-term rebound or pullback may follow. If ADR rises through 0.5 and fluctuates around that level repeatedly, the decline may be nearing its end. If ADR falls from a high level to 0.75, the note treats this as a signal of a short-term rebound.
