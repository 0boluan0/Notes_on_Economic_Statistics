## 2.1 单个资产的收益,风险和资产间的相互关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1 Individual-asset returns and risk, and relationships between assets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.1单个资产的收益、风险和资产间的相互关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.1 Individual-asset returns and risk, and relationships between assets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 预期收益 ($E(R)$)：预期收益率是各种可能的收益率按其发生的概率进行加权平均而得到的收益率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Expected return, $E(R)$: the probability-weighted average of all possible rates of return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.2资产组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.2 Portfolios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设初始财富为 $W$，投资于 $N$ 种风险资产。资产组合可以表示为 $(W_1, W_2, \dots, W_N)$，其中投资于资产 $j$ 的资金为 $W_j$，并满足 $\sum_{j=1}^N W_j = W$（全部财富都用来投资）。时间 0 时投资于资产 $j$，时间 1 的总回报为 $W_j(1+R_j)$（连本带息，其中 $R_j$ 为资产 $j$ 的随机收益率）。该资产组合的净收益率为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let initial wealth be $W$ and suppose it is invested in $N$ risky assets. The portfolio is $(W_1, W_2, \dots, W_N)$, where $W_j$ is the amount invested in asset $j$ and $\sum_{j=1}^N W_j = W$, so all wealth is invested. An investment in asset $j$ at time 0 has gross payoff $W_j(1+R_j)$ at time 1, where $R_j$ is its random rate of return. The portfolio's net rate of return is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

R_p = \frac{\sum_{j=1}^N W_j R_j}{W} = \sum_{j=1}^N w_j R_j,

$$

其中 $w_j = W_j/W$ 为资产 $j$ 在投资组合中的权重，是 $N$ 维列向量 $\mathbf{w}$ 的分量。该资产组合的净收益率的期望为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $w_j=W_j/W$ is the portfolio weight of asset $j$ and a component of the $N$-dimensional column vector $\mathbf{w}$. The expected net portfolio return is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

E(R_p) = \sum_{j=1}^N w_j E(R_j),

$$

该资产组合的净收益率的方差为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The variance of the portfolio's net return is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mathrm{Var}(R_p) = \sum_{i=1}^N\sum_{j=1}^N w_i w_j \mathrm{Cov}(R_i, R_j),
$$

其中 $\mathrm{Cov}(R_i, R_j)$ 表示资产 $i$ 与资产 $j$ 收益率的协方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $\mathrm{Cov}(R_i,R_j)$ is the covariance between the returns on assets $i$ and $j$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.3Markowitz投资组合理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.3 Markowitz portfolio theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $N$ 种资产。
- $V$ 是协方差矩阵。
- $\mu$ 是资产收益率的期望值。
- $\mathbf{w}$ 是资产组合的权重向量（用矩阵表示），是 $N$ 维列向量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- There are $N$ assets.
- $V$ is the covariance matrix.
- $\mu$ is the vector of expected asset returns.
- $\mathbf{w}$ is the portfolio-weight vector, represented as an $N$-dimensional column vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- MV-02:start -->
> [!note] MV-02｜单期权重与矩的前提
> 上面的 [[单期组合收益]] 以非零初始净财富为分母，本课取 $W>0$，并在期初确定权重；期内再平衡不能直接沿用同一固定权重公式。$\mu=(E[R_1],\ldots,E[R_N])^T$ 是向量，$V$ 是同一持有期、同一收益率口径下的协方差矩阵。写出 [[组合均值与方差]] 时假设各收益率具有有限二阶矩；$V$ 因而必须满足 [[协方差矩阵半正定性]]。若允许卖空，权重可为负，但仍须满足预算和实际交易约束。对应 [[证券投资学.pdf#page=905|第905页]]、[[证券投资学.pdf#page=927|第927–930页]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[单期组合收益|single-period portfolio-return formula]] divides by nonzero initial net wealth; this course takes $W>0$ and fixes weights initially. Rebalancing during the period requires a different accounting of returns. The symbol $\mu$ is the vector of expected returns, and $V$ uses one holding period and consistent return units. The [[组合均值与方差|mean and variance formulas]] assume finite second moments, so the covariance matrix must be [[协方差矩阵半正定性|positive semidefinite]]. Short positions permit negative weights subject to budget and trading constraints. See [[证券投资学.pdf#page=905|page 905]] and [[证券投资学.pdf#page=927|pages 927–930]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- MV-02:end -->

模型和模型的求解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The model and its solution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Markowitz模型的目标是在给定预期收益率的条件下使投资组合的风险（用方差度量）最小化。模型可以描述为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Markowitz model minimizes portfolio risk, measured by variance, for a specified expected return. It can be written as follows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

\begin{aligned}

& \min_{\mathbf{w}} ; \mathbf{w}^T V \mathbf{w} \

& \text{s.t. } \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{1} = 1,\quad \mathbf{w}^T \mu = E(R),

\end{aligned}

$$

其中 $E(R)$ 表示投资组合的目标期望收益率，由外部给定。不同的 $E(R)$ 对应着不同的最优投资组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $E(R)$ is the externally specified target expected return. Each target value corresponds to a different optimal portfolio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- MV-03:start -->
> [!note] MV-03｜可行目标、卖空与闭式解
> 为避开原式排版歧义，令目标均值为 $m$，本段无卖空限制的模型为 $\min_{w\in\mathbb R^N}w^TVw$，满足 $\mathbf1^Tw=1,\ \mu^Tw=m$。目标须属于 [[投资组合可行集]]；若禁止卖空，还须加 $w_i\ge0$，此时 $m$ 只能落在 $[\min_i\mu_i,\max_i\mu_i]$ 内，应解 [[禁止卖空的最小方差优化]]。后面的逆矩阵 [[目标收益最小方差解]] 采用 $V\succ0$ 且 $\mu$ 不与 $\mathbf1$ 共线的非退化情形。若所有资产均值相同，只有该共同均值可达；若 $V$ 奇异，不能直接使用 $V^{-1}$。原模型见 [[证券投资学.pdf#page=931|第931–934页]]；[[证券投资学.pdf#page=911|第911页]] 的卖空例出现负权重，与该页同时列出的 $x_i\ge0$ 不属于同一约束情形。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Write the target mean as $m$: the unrestricted problem minimizes $w^TVw$ over real weights with $\mathbf1^Tw=1$ and $\mu^Tw=m$. The target must be [[投资组合可行集|feasible]]. A [[禁止卖空的最小方差优化|no-short-sale problem]] additionally imposes nonnegative weights and restricts the target to the range of individual expected returns. The following [[目标收益最小方差解|inverse-matrix solution]] assumes $V\succ0$ and a mean vector not proportional to $\mathbf1$. Equal individual means permit only that common target; a singular covariance matrix cannot be inverted directly. See [[证券投资学.pdf#page=931|pages 931–934]]. The negative weight in the [[证券投资学.pdf#page=911|page 911 short-sale example]] is incompatible with the nonnegativity constraint also printed there.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- MV-03:end -->

一阶条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First-order conditions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为求解上述优化问题，构造拉格朗日函数，引入乘子 $\lambda$ 和 $\gamma$。一阶条件包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To solve the optimization problem, construct a Lagrangian and introduce multipliers $\lambda$ and $\gamma$. The first-order conditions include:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
V \mathbf{w} - \lambda \mathbf{1} - \gamma \mu = 0,
$$
$$

\mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{1} = 1,\quad \mathbf{w}^T \mu = E(R).

$$

<!-- MV-04:start -->
> [!note] MV-04｜一阶条件中的系数
> 原目标 $w^TVw$ 的梯度是 $2Vw$。上述 $Vw-\lambda\mathbf1-\gamma\mu=0$ 可由等价目标 $\tfrac12w^TVw$ 与拉格朗日函数 $L=\tfrac12w^TVw+\lambda(1-\mathbf1^Tw)+\gamma(m-\mu^Tw)$ 得到；也可理解为重新缩放乘子，最优权重不变。[[证券投资学.pdf#page=932|教材第932页]] 实际先写了这个 $1/2$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The gradient of $w^TVw$ is $2Vw$. The displayed first-order condition follows from the equivalent objective $\tfrac12w^TVw$ and the stated Lagrangian, or from rescaling the multipliers; the optimal weights are unchanged. The factor $1/2$ is explicitly included on [[证券投资学.pdf#page=932|source page 932]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- MV-04:end -->

最优资产组合的收益和风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return and risk of the optimal portfolio
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最优投资组合的期望收益率为 $E(R^)$，其风险（方差）为 $\mathrm{Var}(R^)$。经过计算，可以得到收益率与风险之间满足以下关系：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the optimal portfolio's expected return be $E(R^)$ and its risk, measured by variance, be $\mathrm{Var}(R^)$. Calculation gives the following relationship between return and risk:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mathrm{Var}(R^) = a [E(R^)]^2 - 2b E(R^) + c
$$

_这表明风险 $\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(R^)}$ 与收益 $E(R^*)$ 之间呈双曲线（hyperbola）或抛物线（parabola）关系。最低风险组合对应于上式中的最小值点，其收益和风险可以通过上述公式计算得到。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
_This indicates a hyperbolic or parabolic relationship between risk, $\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(R^)}$, and return, $E(R^*)$. The minimum-risk portfolio is the minimum point of the expression above, and its return and risk can be calculated from that formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- MV-05:start -->
> [!note] MV-05｜常数、坐标与有效上支
> 原文的 $R^$ 记号应读作最优组合收益 $R_p^*$。沿用 [[证券投资学.pdf#page=934|第934–936页]] 的命名，令 $A=\mathbf1^TV^{-1}\mu$、$B=\mu^TV^{-1}\mu$、$C=\mathbf1^TV^{-1}\mathbf1$、$D=BC-A^2>0$，则 $a=C/D,b=A/D,c=B/D$，并有
> $$\sigma_{\min}^2(m)=\frac{Cm^2-2Am+B}{D}=\frac CD\left(m-\frac AC\right)^2+\frac1C.$$
> 因而均值—**方差**坐标下是抛物线，均值—**标准差**坐标下是双曲线的一支，不是同一坐标中两种曲线任选。[[全局最小方差组合]] 的均值为 $A/C$、方差为 $1/C$；完整 [[最小方差边界]] 还包含低收益的无效部分。按均值越高、方差越低的偏好，[[最小方差边界的有效上支]] 为 $m\ge A/C$，包括最低方差点。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Read the incomplete symbol $R^$ as the optimal portfolio return $R_p^*$. With the constants defined as on [[证券投资学.pdf#page=934|pages 934–936]], the coefficients are $a=C/D$, $b=A/D$, and $c=B/D$, giving the completed-square formula above. The mean–**variance** plot is parabolic; the mean–**standard-deviation** plot is a branch of a hyperbola. The [[全局最小方差组合|global minimum-variance portfolio]] has mean $A/C$ and variance $1/C$. The full [[最小方差边界|minimum-variance boundary]] includes an inefficient low-return portion; its [[最小方差边界的有效上支|efficient upper branch]] has $m\ge A/C$, including the minimum-variance point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.4马可维茨模型存在的缺陷
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.4 Limitations of the Markowitz model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 马可维茨模型是局部均衡分析，而不是一般均衡分析。因此，虽然可以分析投资者行为对其最优选择的影响，但无法研究投资者行为对资产市场价格的影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The Markowitz model is a partial-equilibrium analysis rather than a general-equilibrium analysis. It can study how investor behavior affects an investor's optimal choice, but not how that behavior affects market asset prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- 虽然马可维茨模型所刻画的投资者行为是一般化的，不依赖于具体偏好结构，并且在理论上可以描述投资者的无差异曲线，但实际上投资者本人也难以准确知道自己的无差异曲线，从而难以计算自己的期望收益率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Although the investor behavior represented by the Markowitz model is general and does not rely on one specific preference structure, and although indifference curves can be described in theory, investors may not know their own indifference curves accurately enough to determine a target expected return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- MV-06:start -->
> [!note] MV-06｜配置选择不等于均衡定价
> 这里把给定的 $\mu,V$ 当作输入来选择配置，确实没有单独给出市场出清后的价格。但已知权重时，$E[R_p]=w^T\mu$ 不需要先知道投资者的无差异曲线；偏好决定的是选择哪个可行组合。[[均值方差准则]] 是特定的选择准则，并不涵盖一切偏好与收益分布；与期望效用之间的成立条件见 [[均值方差偏好边界]]。原讨论见 [[证券投资学.pdf#page=937|第937–938页]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Taking $\mu$ and $V$ as inputs defines a portfolio-choice problem, not a market-clearing pricing model. Once weights are given, $E[R_p]=w^T\mu$ does not require the investor's indifference curves; preferences select a feasible portfolio. The [[均值方差准则|mean–variance criterion]] is not a representation of every preference and return distribution; its connection to expected utility has [[均值方差偏好边界|specific boundaries]]. See the original discussion on [[证券投资学.pdf#page=937|pages 937–938]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- MV-06:end -->
    
- 马可维茨模型使用收益率的方差来度量风险。然而，实际资产市场中资产收益率的分布通常呈现尖峰厚尾特征，这意味着方差有时可能发散。通过计算收益率的样本方差无法得到方差的无偏估计，因此作为描述风险的统计量，方差存在缺陷。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The Markowitz model measures risk with return variance. Actual asset returns often have peaked, heavy-tailed distributions, so variance may sometimes diverge. The source further states that sample return variance may fail to provide an unbiased estimate, which limits variance as a risk statistic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- MV-07:start -->
> [!note] MV-07｜厚尾、矩存在与无偏性是三个问题
> [[证券投资学.pdf#page=938|第938页]] 的原说法不能由“非正态、尖峰厚尾”直接推出。厚尾分布可能仍有有限方差；若 $R_1,\ldots,R_n$ 为 iid、$n\ge2$ 且方差有限，$s^2=\sum_i(R_i-\bar R)^2/(n-1)$ 仍是总体方差的无偏估计，不要求正态性，证明见 [[样本协方差无偏分母]]。若总体方差无限，则有限协方差矩阵模型失去该输入；若数据有依赖，标准 iid 无偏性论证也不能直接套用。估计误差和均值—方差偏好的适用性应分别判断。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Nonnormality or heavy tails alone do not establish the claim on [[证券投资学.pdf#page=938|page 938]]. A heavy-tailed distribution can have finite variance. Under iid sampling, $n\ge2$, and finite variance, the sample variance with divisor $n-1$ remains unbiased without normality; see the [[样本协方差无偏分母|centering-identity proof]]. Infinite population variance invalidates a finite covariance-matrix input, while dependent observations require reconsidering the iid argument. Estimation error and the suitability of mean–variance preferences are separate issues.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- MV-07:end -->
