### 2.1.1马科维茨 资产组合理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.1 Markowitz portfolio theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 提出了如何定量计算股票投资的收益和风险以及投资组合的 收益和风险；
2. 用模型揭示出，股票投资收益和风险成正比；
3. 说明股票投资风险由系统风险和非系统风险两部分构成，通 过适当的投资组合，可以避免非系统性风险；
4. 提出有效投资组合的概念。有效投资组合具有如下要求：在 相同的风险水平下，投资组合有最高的收益；在相同的期望收 益下，投资组合有最低的风险；
5. 如果有以下三个变量的数据，即每个股票的收益、收益的标 准差(风险)、每个股票之间的协方差，就可以决定投资组合的 期望收益和期望风险，从而建立有效投资组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** It introduced a quantitative way to calculate the returns and risks of individual stocks and portfolios.<br>
**2.** The model links higher expected investment returns to higher risk.<br>
**3.** It separates stock-investment risk into systematic and unsystematic components; suitable diversification can eliminate unsystematic risk.<br>
**4.** It introduced the efficient portfolio: the portfolio with the highest expected return for a given risk, or the lowest risk for a given expected return.<br>
**5.** Expected portfolio return and risk can be determined from each stock's expected return and standard deviation together with the covariances between stocks, allowing the efficient set to be constructed.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- MV-08:start -->
> [!note] MV-08｜均值—方差模型不主张收益与风险成正比
> [[证券投资学.pdf#page=305|原课第305页]] 的第2点表述过强：单个资产或任意组合不必风险越高、期望收益越高，更不必成正比。模型根据权重、均值、协方差及交易约束构造 [[投资组合可行集]]，再按 [[均值方差支配]] 剔除被支配的组合。仅“给定均值的最小方差”还包含边界下支，不应全部叫 [[均值方差有效前沿]]。第5点中的统计输入可用于计算，但还须给定权重，或指定目标与约束后求解权重。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Point 2 on [[证券投资学.pdf#page=305|source page 305]] is too strong: higher risk does not necessarily imply a higher expected return for an individual asset or arbitrary portfolio, much less proportionality. Weights, moments, and trading constraints define the [[投资组合可行集|feasible set]], and [[均值方差支配|mean–variance dominance]] identifies dominated portfolios. Minimum variance at each target mean also includes the lower boundary, so the entire boundary is not the [[均值方差有效前沿|efficient frontier]]. The inputs in point 5 require specified weights, or an objective and constraints for choosing them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- MV-08:end -->

区分单个证券收益变动对证券组合收益的影响，降低风险需要分散 投资及降低证券之间的相关性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The effect of a single security on portfolio return must be distinguished from portfolio risk. Risk reduction requires diversification and lower correlation among securities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- MV-09:start -->
> [!note] MV-09｜降低相关性的条件与风险分解
> 固定两资产的权重及标准差时，$\partial\sigma_p^2/\partial\rho_{12}=2w_1w_2\sigma_1\sigma_2$。因此降低相关性会在两权重同为正时降低方差，但一多一空时方向相反；[[两资产分散化]] 不能脱离权重谈相关性。增加资产种类也不保证每个新配置的风险下降。另需注意 [[证券投资学.pdf#page=907|第907页]] 把方差矩阵对角项称为“非系统风险”、非对角项称为“系统风险”，并非一般成立：单个资产方差本就可能含共同因子风险，而协方差项也可为负。[[分散化与共同风险]] 需要明确因子模型与分散化条件，不能仅按矩阵位置命名。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At fixed weights and standard deviations, the derivative of portfolio variance with respect to correlation is $2w_1w_2\sigma_1\sigma_2$. Lower correlation therefore reduces variance for two positive weights but has the opposite effect for a long–short pair. [[两资产分散化|Two-asset diversification]] depends on weights, and adding securities does not make every new allocation safer. The diagonal/off-diagonal labels on [[证券投资学.pdf#page=907|page 907]] are not a general systematic/specific-risk decomposition: individual variances can contain common-factor risk, and covariances can be negative. [[分散化与共同风险|A common-risk decomposition]] requires an explicit model and diversification conditions, not just matrix positions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- MV-09:end -->

### 2.1.2 托宾两基金分离定理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.2 Tobin's Two-Fund Separation Theorem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将资产分为了两部分:风险资产和无风险资产.风险资产的最优组合是一定的.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assets are separated into risky and risk-free assets. Under the theorem's assumptions, all investors hold the same optimal risky portfolio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

托宾提出资产组合的两分法： 
1. 选择风险资产的最优组合；
2. 依据投资者偏好将资金在最优组合和无风险资产间进行配置。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tobin's two-stage portfolio choice is:
**1.** select the optimal portfolio of risky assets; and<br>
**2.** allocate wealth between that risky portfolio and the risk-free asset according to the investor's preferences.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- MV-10:start -->
> [!note] MV-10｜“同一个风险组合”的条件
> [[证券投资学.pdf#page=306|第306页]] 的 [[风险资产与无风险分离|两阶段结论]] 以共同的风险资产机会集、相同均值与协方差判断、同一无风险借贷利率及相应的均值—方差偏好为前提，还须满足 [[切点组合存在边界|有效切点的存在条件]]。不同约束、不同预期或 [[借贷利率不等的配置边界|借贷异率]] 可能改变风险基金。“投资者选择同一风险基金”本身也不证明该基金就是市场组合；后者还需要市场出清等均衡条件。本段的无风险资产加风险基金，与仅含风险资产的 [[双基金分离]] 应区分。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[风险资产与无风险分离|two-stage separation]] on [[证券投资学.pdf#page=306|page 306]] assumes a common risky opportunity set, identical assessments of means and covariances, a common risk-free borrowing and lending rate, suitable mean–variance preferences, and [[切点组合存在边界|existence of the relevant efficient tangency portfolio]]. Different constraints, beliefs, or [[借贷利率不等的配置边界|borrowing and lending rates]] can change the risky fund. A common risky fund is not identified with the market portfolio without additional equilibrium conditions such as market clearing. This risk-free-plus-risky separation differs from [[双基金分离|two-fund separation using risky portfolios alone]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- MV-10:end -->

### 2.1.3 基础资产定价理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.3 Equilibrium Asset-Pricing Models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 夏普CAPM:
>在市场均衡条件下，单一资产或资产组合的收益由两方面组成，即 **无风险收益** 和 **风险溢价**：
>$$ E(R_i) = R_0 + \beta_i \times [E(R_m) - R_0] $$  
>$$ E(R_i) - R_0 = \alpha + \beta_i \times [E(R_m) - R_0] $$  
> **无风险收益** 代表资金的时间价值，**风险溢价** 代表资产价格波动带来的风险补偿。
> **风险资产的期望收益** 是投资者推迟消费的补偿与承担资产价格波动的风险报酬之和。
> $$ \beta = \frac{\text{Cov}(R_i, R_m)}{\text{Var}(R_m)} $$  
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Sharpe's CAPM:**
>In market equilibrium, the expected return on an individual asset or portfolio has two components: the **risk-free return** and a **risk premium**:
>$$ E(R_i) = R_0 + \beta_i \times [E(R_m) - R_0] $$
>$$ E(R_i) - R_0 = \alpha + \beta_i \times [E(R_m) - R_0] $$
>The **risk-free return** represents the time value of money, while the **risk premium** compensates investors for exposure to asset-price risk.
>The **expected return on a risky asset** combines compensation for postponing consumption with compensation for bearing price risk.
>$$ \beta = \frac{\text{Cov}(R_i, R_m)}{\text{Var}(R_m)} $$
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

夏普CAPM是单一因素模型,只有市场风险这一个因素
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sharpe's CAPM is a single-factor model: market risk is the sole priced factor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2.1.3.1 罗斯套利定价理论APT
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.3.1 Ross's Arbitrage Pricing Theory (APT)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产价格受多因素影响，为多因素模型，不存在套利机会；
建立了证券收益与宏观经济及其他因素相关关系 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
APT is a multifactor model in which asset returns respond to several common factors and prices rule out arbitrage opportunities. It links security returns to macroeconomic and other systematic factors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2.1.3.2.默顿：跨期CAPM模型（ICAPM） 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.3.2. Merton: Intertemporal CAPM Model (ICAPM)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

风险补偿转换为证券收益与投资者边际效用的协方差表示 
风险溢价由多个β决定，系统风险及其他表述投资组合特征的变 量 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Risk compensation is expressed through the covariance between security returns and investors' marginal utility. Risk premia can depend on several betas capturing systematic risks and other state variables relevant to the investment opportunity set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2.1.3.3卢卡斯基于消费的资本资产定价模型（CCAPM）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.3.3 Lucas's Consumption-Based Capital Asset Pricing Model (CCAPM)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设：效应最大化，投资为未来消费提供资金； 
资产收益率与总消费增长率协方差作为风险补偿 
将产品市场、要素市场及金融市场通过消费与投资联系起来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assumption: investors maximize utility, and investment finances future consumption.
Risk compensation depends on how an asset's return covaries with aggregate consumption growth.
Consumption and investment connect product, factor, and financial markets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.2.1 期货定价理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2.1 Futures Pricing Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 持有成本理论
>由于投资者往往持有存货,而存货占用库房和保险费用,并且占用一部分资金,从而产生机会成本;存货还面临着价格波动的风险,持有人需要进行风险补偿 。期货价格=现货价格+风险补偿
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Cost-of-carry theory**
>Holding inventory incurs storage and insurance costs and ties up capital, creating an opportunity cost. Inventory is also exposed to price risk for which holders may require compensation. The note summarizes this as: futures price = spot price + risk compensation.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 凯恩斯——延期交割费用理论：
>期货市场的套期保值功能和价格形成功能不可能同时满足。（套保者降低风险，低价出售期货，无法解释现货溢价及远期折价）
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Keynes's theory of normal backwardation:**
>The note argues that the futures market's hedging and price-discovery functions cannot both be satisfied mechanically: hedgers reduce risk by selling futures at a discount, an account that does not by itself explain every spot premium or forward discount.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 赫希雷弗-期货溢价理论：
>部分权利无法交易且进入期货市场存在固定成本。当期货市场达到均衡时,期货风险溢价不仅取决于系统性风险,还取决于对冲风险,因此对冲压力在期货风险溢价中占有重要地位
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Hirshleifer's futures-risk-premium theory:**
>Some claims cannot be traded, and participation in futures markets involves fixed costs. In equilibrium, a futures risk premium therefore depends not only on systematic risk but also on hedging demand, giving hedging pressure an important role.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1 传统资产定价理论无法解释的市场异象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1 Market Anomalies Unexplained by Traditional Asset-Pricing Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] Grossman-Stiglitz 悖论 
>在完全竞争的市场中,如果不确定性仅仅来源于未来收益的随机性,则完全揭示了竞争性理性预期均衡不具有稳定性,甚至不存在 。 
>均衡价格反映私人信息，无需搜集信息，则搜集信息可以获得超额收益。
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Grossman-Stiglitz paradox**
>A perfectly revealing rational-expectations equilibrium is unstable, and may not exist, even in a competitive market where uncertainty comes only from random future returns.
>If equilibrium prices already reveal all private information, investors have no reason to pay to acquire it. Yet information can enter prices only if someone has an incentive—such as an excess return—to collect it. This is the paradox.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bf-course-correction:BF-01:start -->
> [!note] BF-01｜完全揭示与有成本信息搜集
> 核心条件是搜集信息有成本：若价格无偿完整揭示所购信息，搜集激励便会消失。噪声使价格只能部分揭示信息，可以容许理性预期下的信息获取均衡；这不是所有竞争性理性预期均衡都不存在，也不是心理偏差的证据。应区分[[有效市场假说|信息效率基准]]与获取信息所需的补偿。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Costly information acquisition needs compensation. Fully revealing prices would remove that incentive; noisy, partially revealing prices can support an information-acquisition equilibrium with rational expectations. This does not rule out every competitive RE equilibrium or establish psychological bias. Distinguish the [[有效市场假说|information-efficiency benchmark]] from compensation for acquiring information.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 来源：[Grossman–Stiglitz (1980), printed 393–395 / PDF 2–4](https://www.pims.math.ca/files/Grossman_Stiglitz1980.pdf#page=2)，支持有成本信息和部分揭示的理性模型。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Source: Grossman–Stiglitz supports costly information and partial revelation with rational expectations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bf-course-correction:BF-01:end -->
>[!note] 收益长期反转与中期惯性现象 
>长期反转：股票的历史累计收益与未来的长期收益负相关,投资者可以进行相应的投资组合获得超额收益
>中期惯性：过去3-12个月赚钱的股票组合在随后的3-12个月仍然表现较好
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Long-term reversal and medium-term momentum**
>Long-term reversal: a stock's cumulative past return is negatively associated with its subsequent long-horizon return, so portfolios formed on past winners and losers may earn abnormal returns through reversal.
>Medium-term momentum: portfolios of stocks that performed well over the previous 3–12 months tend to continue performing well over the following 3–12 months.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 期间效应 
>股票在星期一的收益率为负值的概率明显 高于为正值的概率,而股票在星期五的收益率 明显高于其他交易日
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Day-of-the-week effect**
>Stock returns are markedly more likely to be negative than positive on Mondays, while Friday returns are markedly higher than returns on other trading days.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bf-course-correction:BF-02:start -->
> [!note] BF-02｜经验模式需要样本与收益基准
> 上面两组模式都需指定市场、样本、期限与正常收益基准，不能视为保证净超额收益的交易规则。尤其“负收益出现频率”与“平均收益”是不同统计量；原讲义没有给出周一概率比较的样本证据。实际应用须做[[市场异象复核]]；收益延续也不能自动识别反应不足，见[[动量识别边界]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both patterns require a market, sample, horizon, and normal-return benchmark; neither guarantees net alpha. The frequency of negative returns differs from the mean return, and the slides provide no sample evidence for the Monday probability comparison. ## 3.2 基于行为金融学的资产定价理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2 Behavioral Asset-Pricing Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.2.1 前景理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2.1 Prospect Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**卡尼曼和特维斯基**：使用**价值函数**替代**预期效用函数**:
- **价值函数**使用**损失**或**收益函数**定义；传统函数使用**最终财富**；
- **损失和收益的非对称性**：损失为凸函数，收益为凹函数；
- **投资者对损失与收益的价值权重**不依赖于结果的**客观概率**，而是依赖于**非线性转换**的**主观概率** $\pi(p)$。  
  - 当**客观概率 $p$ 较小**，$\pi(p) > p$，投资者**过于重视小概率事件**；
  - 当**客观概率 $p$ 较大**，$\pi(p) < p$，投资者**忽略大概率事件**；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Kahneman and Tversky** replace the **expected-utility function** with a **value function**:
- The **value function** is defined over **gains and losses**, whereas the traditional utility function is defined over **final wealth**.
- **Losses and gains are asymmetric**: the value function is convex over losses and concave over gains.
- The decision weight attached to a gain or loss is not its **objective probability** itself, but a nonlinearly transformed **subjective decision weight** $\pi(p)$.
  - When the **objective probability $p$ is small**, $\pi(p) > p$: investors **overweight low-probability events**.
  - When the **objective probability $p$ is large**, $\pi(p) < p$: investors **underweight high-probability events**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bf-course-correction:BF-06:start -->
> [!note] BF-06｜参考点、曲率与损失厌恶分开
> 结果 $x$ 是相对指定[[前景参考点]]的变化。收益凹、损失凸描述敏感度递减；等量损失影响更强，还需要损失侧更陡的[[损失厌恶]]。风险态度由价值与决策权重共同决定，不能仅由这幅图推出所有收益赌局都风险厌恶、所有损失赌局都风险寻求，见[[损失厌恶与风险厌恶]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Outcomes are changes relative to a stated [[前景参考点|reference point]]. Concavity for gains and convexity for losses describe diminishing sensitivity; a steeper loss side represents [[损失厌恶|loss aversion]]. Risk attitudes depend jointly on values and decision weights, so the diagram alone does not imply risk aversion for every gain gamble or risk seeking for every loss gamble; see [[损失厌恶与风险厌恶|loss aversion versus risk aversion]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 来源：[Kahneman–Tversky (1979), printed 277–280 / PDF 15–18](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_Tversky_1979_Prospect_theory.pdf#page=15)，支持三种形状性质及权重对风险态度的作用；[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/证券投资学.pdf#page=324|课堂图 3-1]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Source: Kahneman–Tversky supports the separate shape properties and the role of weights in risk attitudes; the course diagram is Figure 3-1.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bf-course-correction:BF-06:end -->
<!-- bf-course-correction:BF-07:start -->
> [!note] BF-07｜决策权重不是信念概率
> [[前景决策权重|$\pi(p)$]]衡量给定概率对选择价值的影响，不是投资者认为事件发生的主观概率。它仍以 $p$ 为输入，但不必满足概率可加性；端点为 $\pi(0)=0$、$\pi(1)=1$。小概率超权重是描述性模式，极小概率也可能在编辑时被忽略；“低权重”更不等于完全忽略事件。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[前景决策权重|The decision weight $\pi(p)$]] measures the stated probability's impact on choice value, not a subjective belief about occurrence. It still depends on $p$ but need not be additive, with endpoints $\pi(0)=0$ and $\pi(1)=1$. Overweighting rare events is a descriptive pattern; extremely unlikely events may also be discarded during editing. Underweighting does not mean ignoring an event entirely.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 来源：[Kahneman–Tversky (1979), printed 280–283 / PDF 18–21](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_Tversky_1979_Prospect_theory.pdf#page=18)，支持权重与概率信念、端点及编辑边界；[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/证券投资学.pdf#page=324|课堂图 3-2]]为示意图而非固定参数估计。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Source: Kahneman–Tversky supports the distinction from probability beliefs, endpoints, and editing limits. Course Figure 3-2 is schematic, not a fixed parameter estimate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bf-course-correction:BF-07:end -->
**前景理论的价值函数**
$$
V = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \pi(p_i) v(x_i)
$$
其中：
- $V$ 为**不确定性下的价值函数**，
- 投资者通过**最大化 $V$ 进行决策**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**The prospect-theory value function**
where:
- $V$ is the **value assigned to the uncertain prospect**;
- investors choose by **maximizing $V$**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bf-course-correction:BF-08:start -->
> [!note] BF-08｜原式的版本与适用范围
> $v$ 评价结果，$V$ 评价整个前景。1979 [[前景理论]]的式(1)处理至多两个非零结果的 regular prospect；全正或全负前景另按式(2)分出确定部分。例如原论文的 $(400,0.25;100,0.75)$ 使用
> $$V=v(100)+\pi(0.25)[v(400)-v(100)],$$
> 而不能一般地改成 $\pi(0.25)v(400)+\pi(0.75)v(100)$。1992 的[[累积前景权重计算]]则按结果排序，用累计概率的变换之差构造权重；不能把各个 $p_i$ 独立变换的原课堂和式当作任意多结果 CPT 的通式。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $v$ values outcomes, whereas $V$ values the whole prospect. The 1979 [[前景理论|prospect-theory]] regular-prospect rule covers at most two nonzero outcomes; strictly positive or negative prospects instead segregate a sure component. The displayed example therefore cannot generally be evaluated as $\pi(0.25)v(400)+\pi(0.75)v(100)$. The 1992 [[累积前景权重计算|cumulative weighting calculation]] ranks outcomes and takes differences of transformed cumulative probabilities; it is not independent transformation of every $p_i$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 来源：[KT (1979), printed 275–276 / PDF 13–14, equations (1)–(2)](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_Tversky_1979_Prospect_theory.pdf#page=13)；[TK (1992), printed 299–302 / PDF 3–6](https://psych.fullerton.edu/mbirnbaum/teaching/psych466/articles/Tversky_Kahneman_JRU_92.pdf#page=3)。分别支持原版的分支规则与累积扩展。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Sources: KT (1979) supports the original branching rules; TK (1992) supports the cumulative extension.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

The original list gives applications, not unconditional consequences of any noise trading.

>[!note] 3.BSV模型：
>投资者心理偏差引入未来收益预 测，错误预测了状态转换概率，导致过度反 应 .
>例如在牛市的时候都觉得以后都是牛市
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**3. BSV model:**
>Investors' psychological biases enter their forecasts of future returns. Misjudging the probability of switching between states can produce overreaction.
>For example, during a bull market, investors may expect the bull market to continue indefinitely.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bf-course-correction:BF-10:start -->
> [!note] BF-10｜BSV 是错误模型内的贝叶斯更新
> [[BSV信念模型]]中真实盈利是随机游走，投资者却相信盈利变化在均值回归与趋势延续两状态间切换，并在固定的错误模型内用贝叶斯法更新当前状态信念。偏信回归会弱化单次新闻的影响，连续同号新闻又可能强化趋势外推；所以模型兼含反应不足与过度反应。原牛市例只是直觉，不能代替其公司盈利过程与参数条件。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the [[BSV信念模型|BSV model]], true earnings follow a random walk, but the investor believes changes switch between reversion and continuation. Bayesian updating occurs within this fixed misspecified model. Reversion beliefs can mute isolated news, while repeated same-sign news can encourage extrapolation, generating underreaction and overreaction under model conditions. The bull-market example is only an analogy.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 来源：[Barberis–Shleifer–Vishny (1998), printed 318–322 / PDF 12–16](https://shleifer.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum10626/files/shleifer/files/model_invest_sent.pdf#page=12)，支持错误盈利模型与模型内 Bayes，而非一般概率计算错误。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Source: BSV supports earnings misspecification with Bayesian updating, not a generic arithmetic error in probabilities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 4.DHS模型：
>投资者存在过度自信和有偏自我归因， 投资者对私人信息的过度自信导致 过度反应，对公共信息认识存在偏差，导致价格回归缓慢
>觉得自己有私人信息,对常识视而不见 
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**4. DHS model:**
>Investors are overconfident and exhibit biased self-attribution. Excessive confidence in private information causes overreaction, while biased responses to public information make prices revert only slowly.
>In plain terms, they believe too strongly in their own private signals and disregard common knowledge.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bf-course-correction:BF-11:start -->
> [!note] BF-11｜DHS 的固定信心与动态反馈
> [[DHS私人信息模型]]把过度自信具体化为高估私人信号精度，而非高估所有公共信号精度；公共消息平均逐步纠正先前的过度反应。进一步加入[[DHS信心反馈]]时，确认性新闻提高信心多于否认性新闻降低信心，价格可继续过度反应并产生动量。因此不能把动量直接等同于反应不足。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[DHS私人信息模型|fixed-confidence DHS model]] overstates private-signal precision, not the precision of every public signal; public news gradually corrects the initial overreaction on average. With [[DHS信心反馈|asymmetric confidence feedback]], confirming news raises confidence more than disconfirming news reduces it, so continuing overreaction can generate momentum. Momentum therefore does not identify underreaction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 来源：[Daniel–Hirshleifer–Subrahmanyam (1998), printed 1841–1846、1858–1859, §§II–III](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/0022-1082.00077)，支持私人/公共精度区别及有条件的动量机制。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->

>[!note] 5.HS模型：

><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**5. HS model:**
>The model distinguishes news watchers from momentum traders. Because information diffuses gradually and the two groups respond differently, prices can show underreaction and short-run momentum, followed by overreaction and long-run reversal.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [[HS信息扩散模型]]同时要求：新闻观察者不从价格提取别人的信息，动量交易者只能使用受限的简单历史价格策略，私人信息在人群中逐步扩散。正是这些条件的互动形成先不足后过度的价格调整；不是任意两类投资者的存在都足够。相似收益图形仍需[[动量识别边界|额外证据区分机制]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[HS信息扩散模型|HS model]] combines newswatchers who do not infer others' information from prices, momentum traders restricted to simple price-history strategies, and gradual private-information diffusion. Those conditions jointly generate delayed adjustment and overshooting; two investor types alone are insufficient. Similar returns still require [[动量识别边界|additional identifying evidence]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 来源：[Hong–Stein (1999), printed 2143–2146 / PDF 2–5](https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~cengel/IMF/HongSteinJF1999.pdf#page=2)，支持三项设定与互动机制。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Source: Hong–Stein supports the three assumptions and their interaction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4. 市场有效假设理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Efficient Market Hypothesis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1市场有效的前提
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1 Conditions for Market Efficiency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 理性市场参与者。理性市场参与者可以正确 评估到达资本市场的新信息，对资本市场的金融产品进行准确地定价。 
2. 理性偏差独立。如果市场存在非理性的参与者，且所有参与者的理性 偏差是独立的，偏乐观与偏悲观的市场参与者的数量相当，则资本市场 依然能够达到有效的状态。
3. 存在进行套利交易的专业市场参与者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Rational market participants. They can correctly evaluate new information reaching the capital market and accurately price financial assets.<br>
**2.** Independent deviations from rationality. Even if some participants are irrational, their biases can cancel out when they are independent and roughly equal numbers of investors are overly optimistic and overly pessimistic; the market may therefore remain efficient.<br>
**3.** Professional market participants are available to conduct arbitrage.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信息的完全性:
	市场上的所有信息都是真实信息 信息的流动是顺畅的 其流动和核实不存在任何的成本
信息发生 的随机性:
	信息是以随机方式进入市场的 每一次信息的发生是完全相互独立的
市场参与者 的完全理性:
	市场参与者都是能够理性地进行投资 决策的经济人 风险偏好 消化吸收信息 预期收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Completeness of information:
	All market information is genuine, flows freely, and can be transmitted and verified at no cost.
Randomness of information occurrence:
	Information arrives randomly, and each arrival is independent of the others.
Perfect rationality of market participants:
	Market participants are rational economic agents who make investment decisions by considering risk preferences, processing information, and forming expected returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
	
## 4.2 对于有效市场的分类
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2 Classification of Efficient Markets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1.  弱式有效市场假设
	1. 当前的股票价格已经充分反映了全部历史价格信息和 交易信息。试图通过分析历史价格数据预测未来股价的走 势，期望从过去价格数据中获益将是徒劳的
	2. 全部历史信息
	3. 不能使用技术分析
	4. 基本分析是有效的
2. 半强式有效市场假设
	1. 当前的股票价格已经充分反映了与公司前景有关的全 部公开信息。试图通过分析公开信息，是不可能取得超额收益的.
	2. 基本分析和技术分析都无效
	3. 只有内幕消息能够获得超额收益
3. 强式有效市场
	1. 当前的股票价格反映了全部信息的影响，全部信息 不但包括历史价格信息、全部公开信息，而且还包括私人 信息以及未公开的内幕信息等
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Weak-form Efficient Market Hypothesis<br>
↳ **1.** Current stock prices already incorporate all historical prices and trading information. Trying to forecast future prices from past price data, and thereby profit from those data, is futile.<br>
↳ **2.** The relevant information set is all historical market information.<br>
↳ **3.** Technical analysis cannot generate abnormal returns.<br>
↳ **4.** Fundamental analysis may still be useful.<br>
**2.** Semi-strong-form Efficient Market Hypothesis<br>
↳ **1.** Current stock prices already incorporate all public information about a company's prospects. Analysing public information cannot generate abnormal returns.<br>
↳ **2.** Neither fundamental nor technical analysis can generate abnormal returns.<br>
↳ **3.** Only non-public inside information could generate abnormal returns.<br>
**3.** Strong-form Efficient Market Hypothesis<br>
↳ **1.** Current stock prices incorporate all information: historical prices, all public information, private information, and undisclosed inside information.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.3 市场异常显现
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3 Market Anomalies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

日历异常： 一类与时间因素有关的异常现象
事件异常： 与特定事件相关的异常现象 
公司异常：一类由公司本身或投资者对公司的认同 程度引起的异常现象 
会计异常：一类与会计信息相连后发生的股价 变动的异常现象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Calendar anomalies: anomalies associated with time or calendar patterns.
Event anomalies: anomalies associated with particular events.
Firm anomalies: anomalies caused by characteristics of the firm itself or by how investors perceive the firm.
Accounting anomalies: unusual stock-price movements associated with accounting information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.4 有效市场理论的现实意义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.4 Practical Implications of Market Efficiency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.4.1对投资策略的影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.4.1 Implications for Investment Strategy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据市场情况进行投资.在强式有效市场只进行被动投资
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose the investment approach to match the degree of market efficiency. In a strong-form efficient market, only passive investment is warranted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主动投资：
		积极投资者认为市场并非总是有效，信息并不完全，且解读存在分歧与非理性行为，因此价格可能偏离价值。
被动投资：
		相信有效市场的人认为，难以长期战胜市场，因而复制市场是更优策略。~~无论怎样 努力…最 好的策略~~
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Active investment:
		Active investors believe that markets are not always efficient: information is incomplete, interpretations differ, and irrational behaviour can cause prices to diverge from value.
Passive investment:
		Investors who accept market efficiency believe that consistently beating the market is difficult, so replicating the market is the better strategy. ~~No matter how hard one tries … the best strategy~~
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
	
### 4.4.2 技术分析:
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.4.2 Technical Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只能在非有效市场获得超额收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Technical analysis can generate abnormal returns only in an inefficient market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当有新信息出现在市场上时，并不是每个人都能获得该信 息，传递信息的典型途径应是从专业分析师到一部分投资 者再到大部分的投资者
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When new information reaches the market, not everyone receives it at once. A typical diffusion path runs from professional analysts, to a subset of investors, and then to most investors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.4.3 基本面分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.4.3 Fundamental Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

弱势及以下的市场可以获得超额收益,半强式及以上都没有
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fundamental analysis may generate abnormal returns in a weak-form or less efficient market, but not in a semi-strong-form or stronger market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

投资者不会很快地分析信息并做出行动，需要一段时间 股票以渐进的方式调整到新的均衡价格，这将导致股票 价格变动趋势保持连续性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If investors do not analyse and act on information immediately, stock prices adjust gradually toward a new equilibrium. This gradual adjustment can make price trends persist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
