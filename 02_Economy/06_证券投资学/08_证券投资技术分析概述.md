• **案头研究**

• 首先应根据自己的研究主题和分析方向，确定所需的信息资料。

• 其次是利用证券投资分析的专门方法和手段，对占有的资料进行仔细的分析。

• 最后是作出分析结论，也就是得出有关指标与证券价格之间相关关系的正式结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Desk research**

• First, determine what information is needed from the research topic and analytical direction.

• Second, apply specialist methods of securities analysis to examine the available material carefully.

• Finally, formulate the analytical conclusion, including a formal conclusion about the relationship between the relevant indicators and security prices.

# 3 证券投资技术分析概述
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Overview of technical analysis of securities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **定义**：从证券的市场行为分析证券价格未来变化趋势的方法。市场行为最基本的表现形式包括市场价格、成交量、价和量的变化，以及所经历的时间。**目的**：预测市场价格变动趋势，把握市场趋势。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Definition:** a method that analyzes the market behavior of a security to assess the future direction of its price. The most basic forms of market behavior are price, trading volume, changes in price and volume, and the time over which those changes occur. **Purpose:** to forecast price trends and identify the prevailing market direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
#考点 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Examinable topic*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
• **理论基础（技术分析的三大假设）**：

1. 市场行为涵盖一切信息。

2. 价格沿趋势波动，并保持趋势。

3. 历史会重复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Theoretical foundation: the three assumptions of technical analysis**

1. Market behavior discounts all information.

2. Prices move in trends and tend to maintain those trends.

3. History repeats itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **技术分析的四大要素**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **The four major elements of technical analysis:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **价格和成交量**：量价是市场最基本的关系，量为价先，对价格的认同程度通过成交量大小得到确认。量价不配合往往意味着价格趋势发生变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Price and trading volume:** the price–volume relationship is the most basic market relationship. Volume is treated as leading price, and the degree of agreement with a price move is confirmed by the amount traded. A divergence between price and volume often signals a change in the price trend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **时间和空间**：时空是市场潜在量能的表现。**时间**：价格有可能在何时出现上升或下降，反映价格变化的周期特征；**空间**：价格有可能上升或下降到什么价位，表示价格变动的幅度大小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Time and price range:** these express the market's latent capacity to move. **Time** asks when a rise or fall may occur and reflects periodic features of price changes. **Price range** asks how far price may rise or fall and describes the magnitude of the movement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **成交量与价格趋势的一般关系**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **General relationships between trading volume and price trends:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 价涨量增：价格随成交量上涨而上升，这种价量关系表示价格将继续上升。

• 价涨量不增：如果价格出现新高，而成交量没有创出新高，则上升趋势令人怀疑。

• 价涨量缩
• 先缓后暴
• 两跌关系
• 价跌量增
• 暴跌见底
• 价格形态确认
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Rising price with rising volume: price advances together with volume, which is interpreted as support for further gains.

• Rising price without rising volume: if price reaches a new high but volume does not, the uptrend becomes questionable.

• Rising price with contracting volume
• A gradual move followed by an abrupt move
• The relationship between two forms of decline
• Falling price with rising volume
• A sharp fall marking a bottom
• Confirmation of price patterns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 成交量是价格的先行指标。

• 成交量可以检验价格变动的趋势性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Trading volume is treated as a leading indicator of price.

• Trading volume can be used to assess whether a price movement has trend support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **时间、空间和价格趋势的关系**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Relationship among time, price range, and price trends:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 时间越长、波动空间越大，对今后价格趋势的影响和预测作用越显著。

• 时间越短、波动空间越小，对今后价格趋势的影响和预测作用越小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• The longer the elapsed time and the larger the price range, the more significant the inferred effect on, and predictive value for, the subsequent trend.

• The shorter the elapsed time and the smaller the price range, the weaker the inferred effect and predictive value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **特点**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Characteristics:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 与市场接近，考虑问题比较直接。

• 证券买卖见效快，获利周期短。

• 对市场的反应比较直接，分析的结果也更接近实际市场的局部现象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• It remains close to the market and addresses questions directly.

• Securities trades produce results quickly, with a short profit horizon.

• It responds directly to the market, and its conclusions are closer to local phenomena observed in actual markets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **适用范围**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Scope of application:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 适用于较短时间的行情预测。

• 得到的结论都应该以概率的形式出现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• It is suited to shorter-horizon market forecasts.

• Its conclusions should be expressed probabilistically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **Limitations:**

• 不能绝对依赖技术分析。

• Use several technical methods together when making a judgment.

• Revise conclusions drawn from past data continually.

• Do not rely on technical analysis absolutely.

• **两种分析方法的区别**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Differences between the two analytical approaches:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **分析依据**：基本分析根据价格变动的原因，各种宏观微观因素来预测证券未来行情；技术分析根据市场价格变化规律，采用过去及现在的数据资料，得出未来行情。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Basis of analysis:** fundamental analysis forecasts future securities-market conditions from the causes of price changes and from macroeconomic and microeconomic factors. Technical analysis infers future conditions from regularities in market-price movements using past and current data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **思维方式**：基本分析为理性思维，列举所有影响行情的因素，进行质的分析，具有前瞻性；技术分析为经验性的思维，所有因素的变动最终反映在价格和交易量的动态指标上，进行量的分析，具有滞后性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Mode of reasoning:** fundamental analysis is presented as rational and forward-looking, enumerating the factors that affect markets and assessing them qualitatively. Technical analysis is empirical and quantitative, treating changes in all factors as ultimately reflected in dynamic price and volume indicators, and is therefore described as lagging.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **投资策略**：基本分析侧重于证券的内在投资价值，研究价格的长期走势；技术分析侧重于对市场趋势的预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Investment strategy:** fundamental analysis emphasizes intrinsic investment value and long-run price movements, while technical analysis emphasizes forecasting market trends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **操作方法**：基本分析着重研究各种因素与价格的内在联系和逻辑，需要准确判断和推理能力；技术分析着重于图表分析，使用证券的价量资料，用统计方法得出结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Method of operation:** fundamental analysis studies the internal links and logic connecting different factors to price and requires accurate judgment and reasoning. Technical analysis emphasizes charts and uses security price and volume data to draw statistical conclusions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **优势互补**：基本分析有助于证券市场的长期稳定，引导投资者关注国家大事，有利于投资者整体素质的提高；技术分析不需要掌握专门的金融证券知识和齐全的情报资料，对非专业投资者也具有积极意义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Complementary advantages:** fundamental analysis is said to support long-run market stability, direct investors' attention to major national developments, and improve investor sophistication. Technical analysis requires neither specialist financial knowledge nor complete information resources and is therefore also useful to non-professional investors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
