## 一、CAPM 的基本假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Fundamental Assumptions of CAPM*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[CAPM假设|CAPM]] 模型是建立在一系列假设基础之上的。这些假设的设定，是为了简化现实复杂的经济环境，使我们能够构建可操作的模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[CAPM假设|CAPM]] model is built on a set of assumptions. These assumptions are made to simplify the complex economic environment, allowing us to construct operational models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 假设设定标准：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Assuming a standard is set:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 假设应尽可能简单，使得问题可以抽象化、模型可以建立。
* 关键是模型是否能帮助我们理解和预测经济行为，而非假设是否与现实完全一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Assumptions should be as simple as possible, allowing for abstraction and model building.
* The key is whether the model aids in understanding and predicting economic behavior rather than whether the assumptions perfectly align with reality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### CAPM 模型的假设包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The assumptions of the model include:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 投资者以期望收益率和标准差来评价证券组合的好坏（资产组合理论）。
2. 所有投资者的投资周期相同。
3. 所有投资者对信息处理方式相同，具有相同预期（对收益率、方差、协方差的估计一致），都是风险厌恶者。
4. 市场完全：无税收、无交易成本。
5. 每种证券都是无限可分的。
6. 投资者是风险厌恶者。
7. 所有投资者追求更高收益。
8. 无风险利率对所有投资者相同，可无限借贷。
9. 信息是免费的、充分的，可得的。
10. 所有信息自由流通。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Investors evaluate the quality of security portfolios based on expected returns and standard deviation (portfolio theory).<br>
**2.** All investors have the same investment period.<br>
**3.** All investors process information in the same way, with the same expectations regarding yields, variances, and covariances (consistent estimates), and they are all risk-averse.<br>
**4.** Market completeness: No taxes, no transaction costs.<br>
**5.** Each security is infinitely divisible.<br>
**6.** All investors are risk-averse.<br>
**7.** All investors seek higher returns.<br>
**8.** risk-free rate is the same for all investors and can be borrowed and lent infinitely.<br>
**9.** Information is free, complete, and accessible.<br>
**10.** All information is freely available.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 二、[[资本市场线|资本市场线]]（[[资本市场线|Capital Market Line]], CML）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*II. Capital Market Line (Capital Market Line, CML)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- MV-RF-01:start -->
> [!note] MV-RF-01｜从切点选择到市场均衡的条件
> 下文第二、四、五节共同使用标准单期模型：投资者具有相同的均值—协方差机会集，采用相容的均值—方差偏好，并可按同一利率借贷。在协方差矩阵正定、风险资产允许无限卖空的闭式模型中，通常的有限正 Sharpe [[切点组合]] 还需 $d=\mathbf1^\top\Sigma^{-1}(\mu-r_f\mathbf1)>0$，等价于 $r_f<\mu_{GMV}$；零、负分母另见 [[切点组合存在边界]]。不能仅由“大家都风险厌恶”推出这些条件。
>
> [[风险资产与无风险分离]] 首先给出共同风险基金 $T$；再用市场出清把共同需求与风险资产的市值供给相匹配，才得到 $T=M$。因此“一致预期”不是单独把任意切点或股票指数认作市场组合的充分条件。[[资本配置线]] 是固定风险基金加无风险资产的配置关系，[[资本市场线]] 则保留这里的均衡含义。
>
> 来源：[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/证券投资学.pdf#page=944|原课件 PDF 第 944–955、958–959 页]]（同率借贷、分离与出清）；[Zivot 矩阵组合课件 PDF 第 34–38 页](https://faculty.washington.edu/ezivot/econ424/portfoliotheorymatrixslides.pdf#page=34)（切点归一化与符号边界）。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Sections II, IV, and V use the same standard one-period framework: a common mean–covariance opportunity set, compatible mean–variance preferences, and borrowing and lending at one common rate. In the positive-definite covariance model with unrestricted risky-asset short sales, the usual finite positive-Sharpe [[切点组合|tangency portfolio]] also requires $d=\mathbf1^\top\Sigma^{-1}(\mu-r_f\mathbf1)>0$, equivalently $r_f<\mu_{GMV}$. Zero and negative denominators have a separate [[切点组合存在边界|existence boundary]]. Risk aversion alone does not establish these premises.
>
> [[风险资产与无风险分离|Risky/risk-free fund separation]] first identifies a common risky fund $T$. Market clearing must then match common demand to the value-weighted supply of risky assets before $T=M$ follows. Homogeneous expectations alone therefore do not identify any tangency portfolio or stock index as the market. A [[资本配置线|CAL]] describes allocation with a fixed risky fund; the [[资本市场线|CML]] retains the equilibrium meaning used here.
>
> Sources: [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/证券投资学.pdf#page=944|Original slides, PDF pp. 944–955 and 958–959]] support common-rate financing, separation, and market clearing; [Zivot's matrix portfolio slides, PDF pp. 34–38](https://faculty.washington.edu/ezivot/econ424/portfoliotheorymatrixslides.pdf#page=34) support normalization and its sign boundaries.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- MV-RF-01:end -->

CAPM 模型借助 CML 推导而来，在市场均衡状态下，市场组合与切点组合重合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The model is derived from CML (Capital Market Line). In the state of market equilibrium, the market portfolio coincides with the tangency portfolio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据一致性预期假设，所有投资者都以市场组合作为风险资产投资，因此市场组合 $M$ 与无风险资产构成的所有资产组合效率前沿即为所有投资者可选择的最佳集合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
According to the assumption of market consistency, all investors invest in the market portfolio as their risky assets. Therefore, the market portfolio $M$ and any combination of risk-free assets form the efficient frontier for all investors, which constitutes the set of all optimal choices available to them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 定义：
>
> CML 是连接无风险资产与市场证券组合 $M$ 的连线，代表市场均衡下的有效边界。
>
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Definition:**
>
>The CML is the line joining the risk-free asset and the market portfolio $M$. It represents the efficient frontier in market equilibrium.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 方程：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equation:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本市场线的表达式为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The expression for the Capital Market Line (CML) is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(R_p) = r_f + \frac{E(R_M) - r_f}{\sigma_M} \cdot \sigma_p
$$

其中：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Where:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* $E(R_p)$：组合 $P$ 的期望收益率
* $\sigma_p$：组合 $P$ 的标准差
* $r\_f$：[[无风险利率口径|无风险利率]]
* $E(R_M)$：市场组合的期望收益率
* $\sigma_M$：市场组合的标准差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* $E(R_p)$: The expected return of the combination of $P$
* $\sigma_p$: The standard deviation of the combination of $P$
* $r\_f$: [[无风险利率口径|risk-free rate]]
* $E(R_M)$: The expected return of the market portfolio
* $\sigma_M$: The standard deviation of the market portfolio
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 说明：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Note:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 投资者增加一单位风险，就需获得额外的期望收益补偿
* CML 截距为 $r_f$，表示时间价值（推迟消费的收益）
* 斜率为风险价格（每单位风险所要求的收益）
* 理性投资者会选择 CML 上的组合，具体点取决于其风险偏好
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Investors require an additional expected return for each unit of increased risk.
* The intercept of the Capital Market Line (CML) is $r_f$, representing time value (the benefit of delaying consumption).
* The slope represents the risk premium (the additional return required for each unit of risk).
* Rational investors will choose a portfolio on the CML, depending on their level of risk preference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三、资产组合的投资选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*III. Investment Selection for Asset Portfolios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### （一）仅有风险资产时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(I) When there is only risk assets:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 投资者选择效率前沿上的点（如与其无差异曲线相切的点）
* 不会选择效率前沿外的点（如 \$A\$ 点）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Investors choose points on the efficient frontier (such as tangency points with their indifference curves)
* They would not choose points outside the frontier (such as point \$A\$)
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### （二）风险资产与无风险资产同时存在时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(II) When both risky assets and risk-free assets coexist:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据投资者风险偏好不同：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
According to different investor risk preferences:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 风险承受能力弱者：选择靠近无风险资产一端（如 $A$ 点），其组合中无风险资产比重较大。
* 风险承受能力强者：选择靠近高风险端（如 $B$ 点），可能借入资金投资风险资产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Risk-averse individuals: Choose a position closer to the risk-free asset (e.g., $A$), where the proportion of risk-free assets in their portfolio is larger.
* Risk-tolerant individuals: Choose a position closer to the high-risk end (e.g., $B$), potentially borrowing funds to invest in risky assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 四、[[风险资产与无风险分离|分离定理]]（[[风险资产与无风险分离|Separation]] Property）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*IV. [[风险资产与无风险分离|Separation Theorem]] Separation Property*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产选择过程可分为两阶段：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The process of asset selection can be divided into two stages:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 阶段一：风险资产选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stage One: Selection of Risk Assets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 根据风险资产的期望收益、风险以及相互关系构建效率前沿
* 从无风险资产 \$r\_f\$ 向效率前沿引切线，切点 \$M\$ 为最优风险资产组合（适用于所有投资者）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Construct the efficient frontier based on the expected return, risk, and interrelationships of risky assets.
* Draw a tangent from the risk-free asset \$r\_f\$ to the efficient frontier; the point \$M\$ where this tangent intersects is the optimal risky asset portfolio (applicable to all investors).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 阶段二：最终资产组合选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stage Two: Selection of Final Portfolio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 将 \$M\$ 与 \$r\_f\$ 结合，构造整体资产组合
* 投资者依据风险偏好决定 \$r\_f\$ 与 \$M\$ 的配比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Combine \$M\$ with \$r\_f\$ to construct an overall asset portfolio.
* Investors decide on the ratio of \$r\_f\$ to \$M\$ based on their risk preference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该分离原理说明：机构可根据最佳组合决策进行投资，而不需考虑投资者个体的风险偏好；风险偏好只影响其组合中无风险资产与风险资产的比例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This separation principle states that an institution can make investment decisions based on optimal portfolio combinations without considering individual investors' risk preferences; instead, risk preferences influence the proportion of risk-free assets to risky assets within the portfolio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 五、市场资产组合与一致性预期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*V. Market Asset Portfolio and Consistent Expectations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 市场资产组合的定义：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Definition of Market Asset Portfolio:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有投资者共同持有的风险资产组合称为市场资产组合。假设所有投资者面对相同的信息，并对各资产的收益和风险判断一致（即一致性预期假设），则他们将构造出相同的最优风险资产组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The risk asset portfolio held by all investors together is known as the market portfolio. Assuming that all investors face the same information and have consistent expectations regarding the returns and risks of each asset (the assumption of identical expectations), they will construct the same optimal risk asset portfolio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 推论：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Inference:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 每个投资者的切点组合均为市场组合。
- 市场组合包含所有证券，且投资比例等于该证券的市值在市场总市值中的占比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The tangency portfolio for each investor is the market portfolio.
- The market portfolio includes all securities and has investment proportions equal to the market value of each security as a percentage of the total market capitalization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 六、证券市场线（Security Market Line, SML）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*securities market Line (Security Market Line)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **1. CAPM 核心公式**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. CAPM Core Formula*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CAPM 说明：在均衡市场中，一项风险资产的**期望收益率**只取决于两部分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CAPM  Explanation: In an equilibrium market, the expected return of a risky asset depends on two parts
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

（1）无风险利率 $r_f$ —— 纯粹的时间价值
（2）该资产的**[[Beta与总波动|系统风险]]**（用 $\beta$ 衡量）所获得的风险补偿
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
(1) The risk-free rate $r_f$: the pure time value of money.
(2) Compensation for the asset's **[[Beta与总波动|systematic risk]]**, measured by $\beta$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(R_i)=r_f+\beta_i[E(R_M)-r_f]
$$

| **符号**    | **含义**                 |
| --------- | ---------------------- |
| $E(R_i)$  | 资产 $i$ 的期望收益率          |
| $E(R_M)$  | 市场组合 $M$ 的期望收益率        |
| $r_f$     | 无风险利率                  |
| $\beta_i$ | 资产 $i$ 对市场的敏感度（系统风险系数） |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| **Symbol**    | **Meaning**                 |
| --------- | ---------------------- |
| $E(R_i)$  | Expected return of asset $i$      |
| $E(R_M)$  | Expected return of market portfolio $M$        |
| $r_f$     | [[无风险利率口径|Risk-free rate]] |
| $\beta_i$ | Sensitivity of asset $i$ to the market, or its systematic-risk coefficient |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **2. 证券市场线 (SML) = CAPM 的几何图像**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. The Security Market Line (SML): a graphical representation of CAPM*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **横轴**：$\beta$（系统风险）
- **纵轴**：$E(R)$（期望收益）
- **斜率**：$E(R_M)-r_f$（市场风险溢价）
- **截距**：$r_f$  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Horizontal Axis**: $\beta$ ([[Beta与总波动|Systematic Risk]])
- **Vertical Axis**: $E(R)$ (Expected Return)
- **Slope**: $E(R_M)-r_f$ (Market Slope)
- **Intercept**: $r_f$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直线方程正是上面的 CAPM 公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation of this line is exactly the CAPM equation above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **投资要点**：SML 比较的是对未来平均收益的估计与 CAPM 要求收益。在现金流预期与模型假设给定时，预测期望收益高于 SML 可作为“相对 CAPM 可能低估”的线索；某一期已实现收益高于 SML 只是一项模型异常收益，不足以证明低估或投资能力。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Investment implication:** The SML compares a forecast of average future return with the CAPM required return. Given the cash-flow forecast and model assumptions, a forecast above the SML can indicate possible undervaluation relative to CAPM; one realised return above the line is only a model abnormal return and does not by itself establish undervaluation or skill.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **3. 如何计算 $\beta$**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. How to Calculate $\beta$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对历史收益序列做回归即可得到斜率：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Perform a regression on the historical return sequence to obtain the slope:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\beta_i=\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(R_i,R_M)}{\operatorname{Var}(R_M)}
$$

含义：在这条历史回归的线性拟合中，市场超额收益每变化 1 个百分点，资产超额收益的拟合值平均变化 $\beta_i$ 个百分点；这不是逐期机械比例，也不是总波动倍数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Meaning: In this fitted historical linear regression, a one-percentage-point change in market excess return changes the fitted asset excess return by $\beta_i$ percentage points on average. It is neither a mechanical period-by-period ratio nor a multiple of total volatility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


>[!example] **4. 数值例子（一步到位理解）**
>
> > 假设
>
> - > 无风险利率 $r_f=3%$
>
> - > 市场组合期望收益率 $E(R_M)=9%$    
> - > 某只股票估计的 $\beta=1.3$
>
> **① 代入 CAPM 公式求必要收益率**
>
> $$
> E(R_i)=0.03+1.3\times(0.09-0.03)=0.03+1.3\times0.06=0.03+0.078=0.108
> $$
>
> 所以 $$E(R_i)=10.8\%$$。
>
> **② 判断估值高低**
>
> - 如果分析师预测这只股票未来平均只能赚 $9\%$，低于 $10.8\%$，则相对这组 CAPM 与现金流假设更像是价格偏高；还需检查预测和模型，而不是机械得出“不值得买”。
>
> - 如果预测能赚 $13\%$，高于 $10.8\%$，则相对这组假设更像是价格偏低；这仍是待检验的估值判断，不是自动买入信号。
>
>
> **③ 在 SML 图上定位**
>
> - 坐标 $x$ 轴取 $\beta=1.3$，$y$ 轴对应理论收益 $10.8\%$。
>
> - 预测收益若在此点上方或下方，先形成“相对 CAPM 可能低估或高估”的假设，再核验现金流预测、Beta 和市场风险溢价。
>
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>****4. Numerical Example: Putting the Idea Together****
>
>Assume:
>
>- > The risk-free rate is $r_f=3%$.
>
>- > The expected return on the market portfolio is $E(R_M)=9%$.
>- > A stock has an estimated $\beta=1.3$.
>
>**① Substitute the values into the CAPM equation to obtain the required return.**
>
>
>Thus, $$E(R_i)=10.8\%$$.
>
>**② Judge whether the stock is overvalued or undervalued.**
>
>- If an analyst expects the stock to earn only $9\%$ on average, below the required $10.8\%$, the price looks high relative to this CAPM and cash-flow forecast; the forecast and model still need checking rather than yielding an automatic “do not buy” decision.
>
>- If the expected return is $13\%$, above the required $10.8\%$, the price looks low relative to those assumptions; this remains a valuation hypothesis, not an automatic buy signal.
>
>
>**③ Locate the stock on the SML.**
>
>- Its horizontal coordinate is $\beta=1.3$, and its SML-implied vertical coordinate is a return of $10.8\%$.
>
>- A forecast above or below that point motivates a possible CAPM-relative under- or overvaluation hypothesis, followed by checks of cash-flow forecasts, beta, and the market risk premium.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### **5. SML 与 CML 区别易混？一句话区分**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. What's the Quick Distinction Between SML and CML?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|          | SML                             | CML                                              |
| -------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **横轴**   | $\beta$（系统风险）                   | $\sigma$（总风险，标准差）                                |
| **适用对象** | 单只资产 **或** 组成后仍含非系统风险的任意组合      | 只适用于**有效组合**（已完全多样化、只剩系统风险）                      |
| **公式**   | $$E(R)=r_f+\beta[E(R_M)-r_f]$$ | $$E(R)=r_f+\dfrac{E(R_M)-r_f}{\sigma_M}\sigma$$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
|  | SML | CML |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Horizontal axis** | $\beta$—systematic risk | $\sigma$—total risk measured by standard deviation |
| **Applicable object** | A single asset **or** any portfolio that still contains unsystematic risk | Only an **efficient portfolio** that is fully diversified and therefore contains only systematic risk |
| **Equation** | $$E(R)=r_f+\beta[E(R_M)-r_f]$$ | $$E(R)=r_f+\dfrac{E(R_M)-r_f}{\sigma_M}\sigma$$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- MV-RF-02:start -->
> [!note] MV-RF-02｜横轴、对象与有效方向

## **6. CAPM / SML 的主要局限**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Main Limitations of CAPM and the SML*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 依赖“投资者只关心 $$E(R),\sigma$$”等一系列假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** It depends on assumptions such as investors caring only about $$E(R),\sigma$$.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
2. 现实数据常出现“高 β 资产回报并未更高”等异常。
3. 不能解释规模效应、账面市值比效应等多因子现象 → 促生 **APT**、**Fama-French 三因子** 等模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** Real data often exhibit anomalies such as "high beta assets do not return more."<br>
**3.** Unable to explain phenomena like size effects and book-to-market ratio effects, which are multi-factorial – this has led to the development of models such as APT and the Fama-French three-factor model.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 套利定价理论 APT
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*arbitrage pricing theory APT*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 假设：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Assumption:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 资产收益率与多个因素之间线性相关
- 存在套利者，他们会利用错误定价获得无风险利润
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Asset yield is linearly related to multiple factors.
- Arbitrageurs exist who will exploit mispricings to gain risk-free profits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### APT 模型表达式：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*APT Model Expression:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(R_i) = r_f + \sum_{j=1}^{k} \lambda_j b_{ij}
$$

- $\lambda_j$：第 $j$ 个因素的风险溢价
- $b_{ij}$：资产 $i$ 对因素 $j$ 的敏感性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $\lambda_j$: the risk premium for factor $j$.
- $b_{ij}$: the sensitivity of asset $i$ to factor $j$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

APT 关键：若证券的收益与其敏感性不成线性关系，则存在套利机会，投资者将买入低估证券，卖出高估证券，市场价格调整至无套利均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
APT Key: If the returns of a security do not exhibit a linear relationship with its sensitivity, there exists an arbitrage opportunity. Investors will buy undervalued securities and sell overvalued ones until the market price reaches the no-arbitrage equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Sharp Ratio (Sharpe Ratio)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- MV-RF-03:start -->
> [!note] MV-RF-03｜Sharpe 的可计算口径
> 原记录下方公式存在转义显示缺损；准确口径复用 [[Sharpe比率]]：令 $D=R_P-R_f$，事前 $S=E(D)/\operatorname{sd}(D)$；若单期无风险收益确定，则可写成 $(E(R_P)-r_f)/\sigma_P$。事后使用逐期 $D_t$ 的样本均值除以样本标准差，而非某一期的 $R_P$ 实现值。
>
> 分母须有限且严格为正；全持无风险资产给出 $0/0$，不是 Sharpe 为零。比较还须匹配频率、币种、期间和费用。[[负Sharpe排序边界]] 说明数值较大未必代表原组合的均值—方差优势；历史高比率也不是未来表现或投资能力的保证。
>
> 来源：[Sharpe（1994），The Sharpe Ratio，The Ratio、Time Dependence、Correlations 与脚注 1](https://web.stanford.edu/~wfsharpe/art/sr/SR.htm)：事前／事后差额收益、标准差和比较范围。原公式及本节其他指标保持原记录。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The formula below in the original record has escape-rendering damage. The precise convention is given by [[Sharpe比率|the Sharpe ratio]]: with $D=R_P-R_f$, ex ante $S=E(D)/\operatorname{sd}(D)$. It reduces to $(E(R_P)-r_f)/\sigma_P$ when the one-period risk-free return is certain. The historical version divides the sample mean of period-by-period $D_t$ by its sample standard deviation, rather than using a single realized $R_P$.
>
> The denominator must be finite and strictly positive. An entirely risk-free holding gives $0/0$, not a zero Sharpe ratio. Comparisons require matching frequencies, currencies, periods, and fees. [[负Sharpe排序边界|The negative-Sharpe ranking limitation]] explains why a higher number need not indicate mean–variance superiority of the original portfolio; a high historical ratio guarantees neither future performance nor skill.
>
> Source: [Sharpe (1994), The Sharpe Ratio, The Ratio, Time Dependence, Correlations, and endnote 1](https://web.stanford.edu/~wfsharpe/art/sr/SR.htm), for ex ante/ex post differential returns, standard deviation, and comparison scope. The original formula and the other indicators in this section remain preserved.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
