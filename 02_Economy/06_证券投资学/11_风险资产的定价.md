## 一、[[CAPM|CAPM]] 的基本假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Fundamental Assumptions of [[CAPM|CAPM]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[CAPM|CAPM]] 模型是建立在一系列假设基础之上的。这些假设的设定，是为了简化现实复杂的经济环境，使我们能够构建可操作的模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[CAPM|CAPM]] model is built on a set of assumptions. These assumptions are made to simplify the complex economic environment, allowing us to construct operational models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 假设设定标准：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Assuming a standard is set:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 假设应尽可能简单，使得问题可以抽象化、模型可以建立。
* 关键是模型是否能帮助我们理解和预测经济行为，而非假设是否与现实完全一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Assumptions should be as simple as possible, allowing for abstraction and model building.
* The key is whether the model aids in understanding and predicting economic behavior rather than whether the assumptions perfectly align with reality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### [[CAPM|CAPM]] 模型的假设包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The assumptions of the model include:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 投资者以期望收益率和标准差来评价证券组合的好坏（资产组合理论）。
2. 所有投资者的投资周期相同。
3. 所有投资者对信息处理方式相同，具有相同预期（对收益率、方差、协方差的估计一致），都是风险厌恶者。
4. 市场完全：无税收、无交易成本。
5. 每种证券都是无限可分的。
6. 投资者是风险厌恶者。
7. 所有投资者追求更高收益。
8. 无风险利率对所有投资者相同，可无限借贷。
9. 信息是免费的、充分的，可得的。
10. 所有信息自由流通。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Investors evaluate the quality of security portfolios based on expected returns and standard deviation (portfolio theory).
2. All investors have the same investment period.
3. All investors process information in the same way, with the same expectations regarding yields, variances, and covariances (consistent estimates), and they are all risk-averse.
4. Market completeness: No taxes, no transaction costs.
5. Each security is infinitely divisible.
6. All investors are risk-averse.
7. All investors seek higher returns.
8. risk-free rate is the same for all investors and can be borrowed and lent infinitely.
9. Information is free, complete, and accessible.
10. All information is freely available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 二、[[Capital Market Line|资本市场线]]（[[Capital Market Line|Capital Market Line]], CML）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*II. [[Capital Market Line|Capital Market Line]] ([[Capital Market Line|Capital Market Line]], CML)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[CAPM|CAPM]] 模型借助 CML 推导而来，在市场均衡状态下，市场组合与切点组合重合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The model is derived from CML (Capital Market Line). In the state of market equilibrium, the market portfolio coincides with the tangency portfolio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据一致性预期假设，所有投资者都以市场组合作为风险资产投资，因此市场组合 $M$ 与无风险资产构成的所有资产组合效率前沿即为所有投资者可选择的最佳集合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
According to the assumption of market consistency, all investors invest in the market portfolio as their risky assets. Therefore, the market portfolio $M$ and any combination of risk-free assets form the efficient frontier for all investors, which constitutes the set of all optimal choices available to them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 定义：
>
> CML 是连接无风险资产与市场证券组合 $M$ 的连线，代表市场均衡下的有效边界。
>
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Definition:**
>
>The CML is the line joining the risk-free asset and the market portfolio $M$. It represents the efficient frontier in market equilibrium.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 方程：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equation:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本市场线的表达式为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The expression for the Capital Market Line (CML) is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(R_p) = r_f + \frac{E(R_M) - r_f}{\sigma_M} \cdot \sigma_p
$$

其中：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Where:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* $E(R_p)$：组合 $P$ 的期望收益率
* $\sigma_p$：组合 $P$ 的标准差
* $r\_f$：[[Risk-free Rate|无风险利率]]
* $E(R_M)$：市场组合的期望收益率
* $\sigma_M$：市场组合的标准差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* $E(R_p)$: The expected return of the combination of $P$
* $\sigma_p$: The standard deviation of the combination of $P$
* $r\_f$: [[Risk-free Rate|risk-free rate]]
* $E(R_M)$: The expected return of the market portfolio
* $\sigma_M$: The standard deviation of the market portfolio
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 说明：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Note:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 投资者增加一单位风险，就需获得额外的期望收益补偿
* CML 截距为 $r_f$，表示时间价值（推迟消费的收益）
* 斜率为风险价格（每单位风险所要求的收益）
* 理性投资者会选择 CML 上的组合，具体点取决于其风险偏好
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Investors require an additional expected return for each unit of increased risk.
* The intercept of the Capital Market Line (CML) is $r_f$, representing time value (the benefit of delaying consumption).
* The slope represents the risk premium (the additional return required for each unit of risk).
* Rational investors will choose a portfolio on the CML, depending on their level of risk preference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三、资产组合的投资选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*III. Investment Selection for Asset Portfolios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### （一）仅有风险资产时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(I) When there is only risk assets:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 投资者选择效率前沿上的点（如与其无差异曲线相切的点）
* 不会选择效率前沿外的点（如 \$A\$ 点）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Investors choose points on the efficient frontier (such as tangency points with their indifference curves)
* They would not choose points outside the frontier (such as point \$A\$)
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### （二）风险资产与无风险资产同时存在时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(II) When both risky assets and risk-free assets coexist:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据投资者风险偏好不同：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
According to different investor risk preferences:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 风险承受能力弱者：选择靠近无风险资产一端（如 $A$ 点），其组合中无风险资产比重较大。
* 风险承受能力强者：选择靠近高风险端（如 $B$ 点），可能借入资金投资风险资产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Risk-averse individuals: Choose a position closer to the risk-free asset (e.g., $A$), where the proportion of risk-free assets in their portfolio is larger.
* Risk-tolerant individuals: Choose a position closer to the high-risk end (e.g., $B$), potentially borrowing funds to invest in risky assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 四、[[Separation Theorem (Finance)|分离定理]]（[[Separation Theorem (Finance)|Separation]] [[Property Rights|Property]]）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*IV. [[Separation Theorem (Finance)|Separation Theorem]] [[Separation Theorem (Finance)|Separation]] [[Property Rights|Property]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产选择过程可分为两阶段：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The process of asset selection can be divided into two stages:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 阶段一：风险资产选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stage One: Selection of Risk Assets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 根据风险资产的期望收益、风险以及相互关系构建效率前沿
* 从无风险资产 \$r\_f\$ 向效率前沿引切线，切点 \$M\$ 为最优风险资产组合（适用于所有投资者）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Construct the efficient frontier based on the expected return, risk, and interrelationships of risky assets.
* Draw a tangent from the risk-free asset \$r\_f\$ to the efficient frontier; the point \$M\$ where this tangent intersects is the optimal risky asset portfolio (applicable to all investors).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 阶段二：最终资产组合选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stage Two: Selection of Final Portfolio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 将 \$M\$ 与 \$r\_f\$ 结合，构造整体资产组合
* 投资者依据风险偏好决定 \$r\_f\$ 与 \$M\$ 的配比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Combine \$M\$ with \$r\_f\$ to construct an overall asset portfolio.
* Investors decide on the ratio of \$r\_f\$ to \$M\$ based on their risk preference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该分离原理说明：机构可根据最佳组合决策进行投资，而不需考虑投资者个体的风险偏好；风险偏好只影响其组合中无风险资产与风险资产的比例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This separation principle states that an institution can make investment decisions based on optimal portfolio combinations without considering individual investors' risk preferences; instead, risk preferences influence the proportion of risk-free assets to risky assets within the portfolio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 五、市场资产组合与一致性预期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*V. Market Asset Portfolio and Consistent Expectations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 市场资产组合的定义：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Definition of Market Asset Portfolio:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有投资者共同持有的风险资产组合称为市场资产组合。假设所有投资者面对相同的信息，并对各资产的收益和风险判断一致（即一致性预期假设），则他们将构造出相同的最优风险资产组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The risk asset portfolio held by all investors together is known as the market portfolio. Assuming that all investors face the same information and have consistent expectations regarding the returns and risks of each asset (the assumption of identical expectations), they will construct the same optimal risk asset portfolio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 推论：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Inference:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 每个投资者的切点组合均为市场组合。
- 市场组合包含所有证券，且投资比例等于该证券的市值在市场总市值中的占比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The tangency portfolio for each investor is the market portfolio.
- The market portfolio includes all securities and has investment proportions equal to the market value of each security as a percentage of the total market capitalization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 六、证券市场线（Security [[Market Risk|Market]] Line, SML）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*securities market Line (Security [[Market Risk|Market]] Line)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **1. [[CAPM|CAPM]] 核心公式**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. [[CAPM|CAPM]] Core Formula*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[CAPM|CAPM]] 说明：在均衡市场中，一项风险资产的**期望收益率**只取决于两部分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[CAPM|CAPM]]  Explanation: In an equilibrium market, the expected return of a risky asset depends on two parts
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

（1）[[Risk-free Rate|无风险利率]] $r_f$ —— 纯粹的时间价值
（2）该资产的**[[Systematic Risk|系统风险]]**（用 $\beta$ 衡量）所获得的风险补偿
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
(1) The [[Risk-free Rate|risk-free rate]] $r_f$: the pure time value of money.
(2) Compensation for the asset's **[[Systematic Risk|systematic risk]]**, measured by $\beta$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(R_i)=r_f+\beta_i[E(R_M)-r_f]
$$

| **符号**    | **含义**                 |
| --------- | ---------------------- |
| $E(R_i)$  | 资产 $i$ 的期望收益率          |
| $E(R_M)$  | 市场组合 $M$ 的期望收益率        |
| $r_f$     | [[Risk-free Rate|无风险利率]]                  |
| $\beta_i$ | 资产 $i$ 对市场的敏感度（系统风险系数） |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| **Symbol**    | **Meaning**                 |
| --------- | ---------------------- |
| $E(R_i)$  | Expected return of asset $i$      |
| $E(R_M)$  | Expected return of market portfolio $M$        |
| $r_f$     | [[Risk-free Rate|Risk-free rate]] |
| $\beta_i$ | Sensitivity of asset $i$ to the market, or its systematic-risk coefficient |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **2. 证券市场线 (SML) = [[CAPM|CAPM]] 的几何图像**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. The Security Market Line (SML): a graphical representation of [[CAPM|CAPM]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **横轴**：$\beta$（[[Systematic Risk|系统风险]]）
- **纵轴**：$E(R)$（期望收益）
- **斜率**：$E(R_M)-r_f$（市场风险溢价）
- **截距**：$r_f$  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Horizontal Axis**: $\beta$ ([[Systematic Risk|Systematic Risk]])
- **Vertical Axis**: $E(R)$ (Expected Return)
- **Slope**: $E(R_M)-r_f$ (Market Slope)
- **Intercept**: $r_f$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直线方程正是上面的 [[CAPM|CAPM]] 公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation of this line is exactly the [[CAPM|CAPM]] equation above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **投资要点**：若某资产实际收益率 $>$ SML 高度 → **被低估**；反之若实际收益率 $<$ SML → **被高估**。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Investment implication:** If an asset's actual or expected return lies above the SML, the asset is **undervalued** relative to CAPM; if it lies below the SML, it is **overvalued**.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **3. 如何计算 $\beta$**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. How to Calculate $\beta$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对历史收益序列做回归即可得到斜率：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Perform a regression on the historical return sequence to obtain the slope:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\beta_i=\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(R_i,R_M)}{\operatorname{Var}(R_M)}
$$

含义：当市场收益 $R_M$ 变动 1%，资产 $i$ 平均跟着变动 $\beta_i$ %。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Meaning: When the market return $R_M$ changes by 1%, the asset $i$ is expected to change on average by $\beta_i$ %.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


>[!example] **4. 数值例子（一步到位理解）**
>
> > 假设
>
> - > [[Risk-free Rate|无风险利率]] $r_f=3%$
>
> - > 市场组合期望收益率 $E(R_M)=9%$    
> - > 某只股票估计的 $\beta=1.3$
>
> **① 代入 [[CAPM|CAPM]] 公式求必要收益率**
>
> $$
> E(R_i)=0.03+1.3,(0.09-0.03)=0.03+1.3\times0.06=0.03+0.078=0.108
> $$
>
> 所以 $$E(R_i)=10.8\%$$。
>
> **② 判断估值高低**
>
> - 如果分析师预测这只股票未来平均只能赚 $9\%$ → 低于 $10.8\%$ → **高估**（不值得买）。
>
> - 如果预测能赚 $13\%$ → 高于 $10.8\%$ → **低估**（值得买）。
>
>
> **③ 在 SML 图上定位**
>
> - 坐标 $x$ 轴取 $\beta=1.3$，$y$ 轴对应理论收益 $10.8\%$。
>
> - 实际预测收益若在此点 **上方** → 买入；在 **下方** → 卖出或回避。
>
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>****4. Numerical Example: Putting the Idea Together****
>
>Assume:
>
>- > The [[Risk-free Rate|risk-free rate]] is $r_f=3%$.
>
>- > The expected return on the market portfolio is $E(R_M)=9%$.
>- > A stock has an estimated $\beta=1.3$.
>
>**① Substitute the values into the [[CAPM|CAPM]] equation to obtain the required return.**
>
>
>Thus, $$E(R_i)=10.8\%$$.
>
>**② Judge whether the stock is overvalued or undervalued.**
>
>- If an analyst expects the stock to earn only $9\%$ on average, below the required $10.8\%$, the stock is **overvalued** and is not worth buying.
>
>- If the expected return is $13\%$, above the required $10.8\%$, the stock is **undervalued** and is worth buying.
>
>
>**③ Locate the stock on the SML.**
>
>- Its horizontal coordinate is $\beta=1.3$, and its SML-implied vertical coordinate is a return of $10.8\%$.
>
>- If the forecast return lies **above** that point, buy; if it lies **below**, sell or avoid the stock.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### **5. SML 与 CML 区别易混？一句话区分**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. What's the Quick Distinction Between SML and CML?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|          | SML                             | CML                                              |
| -------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **横轴**   | $\beta$（[[Systematic Risk|系统风险]]）                   | $\sigma$（总风险，标准差）                                |
| **适用对象** | 单只资产 **或** 组成后仍含非系统风险的任意组合      | 只适用于**有效组合**（已完全多样化、只剩系统风险）                      |
| **公式**   | $$E(R)=r_f+\beta,[E(R_M)-r_f]$$ | $$E(R)=r_f+\dfrac{E(R_M)-r_f}{\sigma_M},\sigma$$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
|  | SML | CML |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Horizontal axis** | $\beta$—[[Systematic Risk|systematic risk]] | $\sigma$—total risk measured by standard deviation |
| **Applicable object** | A single asset **or** any portfolio that still contains unsystematic risk | Only an **efficient portfolio** that is fully diversified and therefore contains only systematic risk |
| **Equation** | $$E(R)=r_f+\beta,[E(R_M)-r_f]$$ | $$E(R)=r_f+\dfrac{E(R_M)-r_f}{\sigma_M},\sigma$$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **6. [[CAPM|CAPM]] / SML 的主要局限**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Main Limitations of [[CAPM|CAPM]] and the SML*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 依赖“投资者只关心 $$E(R),\sigma$$”等一系列假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. It depends on assumptions such as investors caring only about $$E(R),\sigma$$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
2. 现实数据常出现“高 β 资产回报并未更高”等异常。
3. 不能解释规模效应、账面市值比效应等多因子现象 → 促生 **APT**、**Fama-French 三因子** 等模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
2. Real data often exhibit anomalies such as "high beta assets do not return more."
3. Unable to explain phenomena like size effects and book-to-market ratio effects, which are multi-factorial – this has led to the development of models such as APT and the Fama-French three-factor model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 套利定价理论 APT
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*arbitrage pricing theory APT*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 假设：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Assumption:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 资产收益率与多个因素之间线性相关
- 存在套利者，他们会利用错误定价获得无风险利润
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Asset yield is linearly related to multiple factors.
- Arbitrageurs exist who will exploit mispricings to gain risk-free profits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### APT 模型表达式：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*APT Model Expression:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(R_i) = r_f + \sum_{j=1}^{k} \lambda_j b_{ij}
$$

- $\lambda_j$：第 $j$ 个因素的风险溢价
- $b_{ij}$：资产 $i$ 对因素 $j$ 的敏感性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $\lambda_j$: the risk premium for factor $j$.
- $b_{ij}$: the sensitivity of asset $i$ to factor $j$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

APT 关键：若证券的收益与其敏感性不成线性关系，则存在套利机会，投资者将买入低估证券，卖出高估证券，市场价格调整至无套利均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
APT Key: If the returns of a security do not exhibit a linear relationship with its sensitivity, there exists an arbitrage opportunity. Investors will buy undervalued securities and sell overvalued ones until the market price reaches the no-arbitrage equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 九、期权定价理论（OPT）

*Nine. Option Pricing Theory (OPT)*

### 期权价格包含两部分：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

2. **时间价值**：超出内在价值部分

2. **Time Value**: Beyond the intrinsic value

### 二项式期权定价模型：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Binomial Option Pricing Model:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果股票价格经历 $n$ 次变化：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the stock price changes $n$ times:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

$$

C = \sum_{i=0}^{n} inom{n}{i} p^i (1-p)^{n-i} \cdot ext{Payoff}_i \cdot e^{-rT}

$$

  

### Black-Scholes 模型：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Black-Scholes Model:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设股票价格服从对数正态分布：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assuming that stock prices follow a log-normal distribution:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

$$

C = S_0 N(d_1) - E e^{-rT} N(d_2)

$$

  

$$

d_1 = 
rac{\ln(S_0 / E) + (r + 
rac{\sigma^2}{2})T}{\sigma\sqrt{T}}, \quad d_2 = d_1 - \sigma\sqrt{T}

$$
