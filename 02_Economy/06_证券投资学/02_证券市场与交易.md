# 证券市场与交易：从发行融资到成交与交收
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Securities markets and trading: from financing to execution and settlement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上一章弄清了股票、债券和基金份额赋予什么权利。这一章追问：谁把钱交给谁，这些权利怎样定价和转手，屏幕上的价格又怎样变成账户中最终收到的证券与资金？理解这条路径后，才有条件判断一个投资想法能否按设想执行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The previous chapter established the rights conveyed by shares, bonds and fund units. This chapter asks who pays whom, how those rights are priced and transferred, and how a displayed price becomes securities and funds finally received in an account. This path is essential for judging whether an investment idea can be implemented as intended.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Read in course order: issuance and resale → participants → indices → orders and execution → clearing and settlement → financing and short selling. Explanations and examples unfold inline. Atoms can also be opened individually for review without requiring repeated navigation on a first reading.

## 一、先跟着资金走：发行与流通
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Follow the money: issuance and resale*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“证券市场”不是“证券交易所”的同义词。先建立整个交易网络，再用资金和证券的流向区分一级与二级；不要把交易场所、发行阶段和证券种类混成同一条分类树。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A securities market is not synonymous with a stock exchange. Begin with the network, then distinguish primary and secondary activity by tracing securities and cash. Venue, issuance stage and instrument type are different classification dimensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[证券市场]]

![[一级市场]]

![[二级市场]]

公司发行新股时取得资金，投资者之间转卖时通常没有新钱流入公司。但两层市场仍有联系：如果投资者担心将来卖不掉，今天认购时就会要求更好的条件。下面只比较退出成本的变化，其他现金流和风险暂时固定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Issuing new shares finances the company; investor-to-investor resale ordinarily does not. The two layers remain connected: anticipated difficulty selling later can worsen the terms investors demand today. The following comparison changes exit costs while holding other cash flows and risks fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 二、同一笔交易中，各机构分别做什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Different institutions perform different parts of a trade*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

发行人提供证券、募集资金；投资者持有或转让权利。中介可能帮助发行销售、执行客户委托、自己承担头寸，或负责会计、法律和信用意见。区分角色比背机构名单更有用：看到某证券公司时，先问它在**这笔业务中**是代理人，还是以自己的账户作为交易一方。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Issuers supply securities and raise funds; investors hold or transfer claims. Intermediaries may assist distribution, execute client orders, take positions or provide accounting, legal and credit services. Identify the role in this transaction rather than relying on the institution's name: is the firm an agent or a principal?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[证券经纪与自营]]

接着区分组织交易与完成交收。交易所规定怎样进入和成交；场外也能有电子平台、规则和监管。下文第五节再展开清算与结算，不要把“属于同一集团”误当作“同一职责”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next distinguish organizing execution from completing settlement. Exchanges establish access and trading rules; OTC markets can also have electronic platforms, rules and oversight. Section 5 develops clearing and settlement. Common corporate ownership does not make these functions identical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[证券交易所]]

![[场外市场]]

交易所或行业组织可在授权范围内制定和执行会员规则，监管机关则承担法定监管职责。自律不等于不受监管，也不保证每个参与者的投资结果。课堂对会员制、公司制的优劣评价，可作为检查治理和利益冲突的线索，不能从形式直接推出“无破产风险”或“必然公平”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exchanges and industry bodies may make and enforce member rules within their authority; public regulators exercise statutory oversight. Self-regulation is neither freedom from supervision nor a guarantee of investment outcomes. The course's comparisons of mutual and corporate exchanges raise governance questions, not automatic conclusions about solvency or fairness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[证券监管目标]]

![[发行注册与投资价值]]

至此可把三个问题分开：公司是否符合发行条件，市场是否按规则运行，以及证券按当前价格是否值得持有。它们互相影响，却没有哪一个能替另外两个作答。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three questions are now separate: does the issuer meet issuance requirements, does the market operate under its rules, and is the security attractive at its current price? They interact, but none answers the others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三、“市场上涨”究竟在平均什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. What is being averaged when “the market rises”?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程在交易程序前先介绍指数，是因为行情需要一个汇总尺度。先区分同一时点的平均价格与跨时点的价格变化，再决定怎样加权。不能看到“平均”就把所有式子当成一回事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course introduces indices before trading procedures because market quotations need summary measures. Distinguish an average price at one time from a change across times, then choose the weighting rule. The word “average” does not make the formulas interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[股票价格指数]]

![[股价平均数]]

### 先处理单位变化：拆股不应凭空制造跌幅
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Handle changes in share units before interpreting returns*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

四只股票均价为 20 元，其中一股一拆三后，直接平均得到 15 元。这里变的是每股单位，不能说市场因此亏了 25%。把事件前后的指数设为相等，就能求出新的除数，而不用死记一个调整系数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four-stock mean is 20. After one stock splits three-for-one, a naive mean becomes 15. This is a change of share units, not a 25% market loss. Equating the index immediately before and after the event determines the new divisor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[指数除数调整]]

原课还给出“修正股价”的另一种算法：把拆后 D 股的价格换回原来的股数单位，得 $(10+15+25+3\times10)/4=20$。两种方法在拆股这个机械事件前后都保持 20，但若随后一直沿用各自的权重，它们对 D 股后续涨跌的敏感度可以不同；事件处同值不等于两条指数以后完全相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course also adjusts the split stock's price back to the original share unit: $(10+15+25+3\times10)/4=20$. Both methods preserve 20 across this mechanical event. If their respective weights are then retained, however, they can respond differently to D's later price changes. Agreement at the event does not make the subsequent index paths identical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 再处理权重：按股价、按市值和按等金额加权
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Different weights: equal shares, capitalization and equal cash amounts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价格加权让高单价股票影响更大，市值加权看纳入指数的总市场价值，等权则在再平衡时给各成分相同金额。下面三张卡使用相同的[[单期组合收益|期初价值权重恒等式]]；变化的是持仓规则，不是另造三套收益数学。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price weighting emphasizes high share prices; capitalization weighting uses included market value; equal weighting allocates equal amounts at rebalancing. All three reuse the same [[单期组合收益|beginning-value portfolio-return identity]]. What changes is the holding rule, not the return arithmetic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[价格加权指数]]

![[市值加权指数]]

![[等权指数]]

### 回到课堂公式：先认清分子、分母和数量时点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Return to the course formulas: identify numerators, denominators and quantity dates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“简单算术法”先算各股价格比；“综合法”先把价格加总再作比。前者给每个价格比同样的发言权，后者隐含地偏重高基期价格。几何平均又是在平均对数变化。先看各公式的对象，再计算结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The simple arithmetic method averages individual price ratios; the aggregate method takes a ratio of price sums. The former equally weights price relatives, while the latter implicitly favors high base prices. Geometric averaging instead averages log changes. Identify the operands before calculating.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[价格相对数算术平均]]

![[价格相对数几何平均]]

引入数量后，核心问题变成“拿哪一期的篮子作比较”。拉氏固定旧数量，派氏固定新数量。两式的上下必须使用同一篮子；如果分子用新数量、分母用旧数量，比较就同时包含价格和数量变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Introducing quantities asks which period's basket is compared. Laspeyres fixes old quantities; Paasche fixes current quantities. The numerator and denominator must use the same basket. Changing quantities between them mixes price and quantity changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[拉氏价格指数]]

![[派氏价格指数]]

![[Fisher价格指数]]

把课件 PDF p.232 的同一组数完整代入，便能看到“算法不同”具体意味着什么。下面只作给定两期价格与数量的课堂计算，不涉及拆股或样本变更。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the data from course PDF p.232 makes the differences concrete. This is a two-period exercise with given prices and quantities, without splits or membership changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 股票 / Stock | 基期价 $p_0$ | 报告期价 $p_1$ | 基期量 $q_0$ | 报告期量 $q_1$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | 5 | 8 | 100 | 150 |
| B | 8 | 12 | 50 | 90 |
| C | 10 | 14 | 120 | 70 |

基值都取 100：算术平均为 $100(1.6+1.5+1.4+1.2)/4=142.5$；总价比为 $100\times52/38\approx136.8421$；个股价格比几何平均为 $100(1.6\times1.5\times1.4\times1.2)^{1/4}\approx141.7034$。数量加权的中间值是 $\sum p_1q_0=4160$、$\sum p_0q_0=3000$、$\sum p_1q_1=4700$、$\sum p_0q_1=3370$，所以拉氏为 138.6667，派氏为 139.4659，Fisher 为约 139.0657。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With base 100, the arithmetic average is 142.5, the ratio of price sums is approximately 136.8421, and the geometric mean of individual relatives is approximately 141.7034. The weighted totals are $\sum p_1q_0=4160$, $\sum p_0q_0=3000$, $\sum p_1q_1=4700$ and $\sum p_0q_1=3370$. Laspeyres is 138.6667, Paasche 139.4659 and Fisher approximately 139.0657.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 最后核对收入和样本：指数不等于可投资产品
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Finally check income and membership: an index is not an investment product*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指数算法选对了，仍可能漏掉分红。债券还要处理应计利息、到期和估值来源。比较一条指数与另一条指数、或与基金收益时，应先对齐这些口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Correct weighting can still omit distributions. Bonds additionally require accrued interest, maturity and valuation inputs. Align these conventions before comparing indices or fund returns.

## 四、从看到报价到真正成交
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. From a displayed quote to an executed trade*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指数描述市场，交易却发生在具体证券、数量和时间上。开户和权限检查之后，投资者提交含方向、数量、价格约束和有效期的指令。能否成交、如何成交，还取决于对手方流动性与市场机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indices describe markets, but execution concerns a particular security, quantity and time. After account and permission checks, the investor submits an instruction specifying side, quantity, price constraints and duration. Execution also depends on counterparties and the trading mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[报价驱动市场]]

![[做市商]]

![[订单驱动市场]]

![[限价订单簿]]

交易商报价与订单簿不是互斥世界：做市商也可向订单簿提交买卖限价单。现在把注意力放到一个订单簿上，分别看指令给出了什么约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dealers and order books are not mutually exclusive: market makers can post limit orders in a book. Now focus on one book and ask what each instruction constrains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[市价单]]

![[限价单]]

止损类指令再加一个“何时激活”的条件。触发条件与触发后的成交约束是两层，不能把触发价看作保险赔付价；并非每个市场都提供同一种原生止损指令。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stop instructions add an activation condition. Activation and the execution constraints after activation are separate; the trigger is not an insured execution price. Native order support varies by market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[止损单]]

![[止损限价单]]

同样的限价还要经过排队规则。价格时间优先是常见机制，不是所有市场的自然法则；集合竞价与连续竞价决定了何时比较这些订单、如何形成成交价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Orders also pass through allocation rules. Price–time priority is common, not universal. Call and continuous auctions differ in when orders are compared and how the execution price is formed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一笔 120 股订单：为什么最佳卖价不够用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A 120-share order: why the best ask is not enough*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

卖盘在 100 元有 50 股、100.20 元有 100 股。假设期间无人撤单、没有新单、规则允许该市价单逐档成交，买 120 股就必须跨过第一档。先看可供数量，再算成交均价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The book offers 50 shares at 100 and 100 at 100.20. With no cancellations or new orders and rules permitting the market order to walk the book, buying 120 shares must cross the first level. Determine available quantities before calculating the average price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[市场深度]]

![[逐档成交均价]]

![[买卖价差]]

![[市场冲击]]

![[市场流动性]]

![[证券交易成本]]

在同一算例中，限价 100 元的买单最多立即取得第一档可用的 50 股，剩余数量按有效期和市场规则排队、取消或另作处理。只保留限价单成功成交的样本，会漏掉等待和错失交易的代价。策略回测因此要明确何时知道信号、何时下单、最多能成交多少及用什么成本基准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the same book, a buy limit of 100 can immediately obtain at most the available 50 shares; the remainder is queued, cancelled or otherwise handled under duration and venue rules. Studying only filled limit orders omits waiting and missed-trade costs. Backtests must specify signal availability, submission time, executable quantity and cost benchmarks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 五、成交之后：义务、交收与持有人记录
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. After execution: obligations, settlement and ownership records*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

成交回报确认了价格和数量，但证券与资金未必已最终转移。先把“算清应付什么”与“真正交付完成”分开；清算可能净额，也可能保留逐笔义务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An execution report establishes price and quantity but need not establish final transfer of securities and funds. Separate determining obligations from completing transfers; clearing may net obligations or retain them individually.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[证券清算]]

![[证券结算]]

若两条交收腿没有可靠联结，一方可能已经不可撤销地付款，却收不到证券。货银对付解决这个本金风险；它与 CCP 是不同层面的安排。[[中央清算与多边净额|CCP 的接纳和法律净额边界]]说明何时合同对手发生替换，下面则只问券和钱怎样最终交换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without reliable linkage, one party might pay irrevocably and receive no securities. Delivery versus payment addresses this principal risk. It differs from [[中央清算与多边净额|CCP acceptance and legal netting]], which determine contractual counterparties and netting scope. The next atoms concern the final exchange of securities and money.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[货银对付]]

![[货银对付的风险边界]]

对 CCP 的风险缓冲与损失分配，继续复用[[CCP违约瀑布]]，不用在本章重建一套对象。持有人记录同样须识别账户层级：最终投资者可能直接登记，也可能通过经纪商或名义持有人间接持有，不能全球统一理解成每笔成交后立即把个人名字写进发行人名册。[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/02_证券市场#4.5 登记过户|原登记过户记录与校注]]给出对应来源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CCP resources and loss allocation reuse the [[CCP违约瀑布|default waterfall]]. Ownership records also depend on account structure: investors may be directly registered or hold through intermediaries and nominees. A trade does not universally place the individual immediately on the issuer's register. The [[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/02_证券市场#4.5 登记过户|annotated transfer record]] provides the source context.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 六、改变资金安排：融资买入与借券卖空
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Changing the funding arrangement: financed purchases and short sales*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用自有现金买股票，只建立证券多头；借钱再买，还多了一笔必须偿还的债务。因此，资产回报和投资者权益回报的分母不同，持有期末的收益算式也不能替账户承受中途追保。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fully cash-funded purchase creates a long position; borrowing to buy adds a repayment obligation. Asset and equity returns use different denominators, and a terminal-return identity does not ensure the account can meet interim margin needs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[融资买入]]

![[融资买入收益率]]

![[证券账户初始保证金]]

![[证券账户维持保证金]]

![[融资账户追保计算]]

再把方向反过来：卖出借来的股票，得到的现金对应着将来返还股票的义务。股票涨跌决定价差，借券、费用与合同期限决定头寸能否按计划维持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reverse the direction: selling borrowed shares produces cash alongside an obligation to return shares. Prices determine the spread, while borrow availability, costs and contractual terms determine whether the position can be maintained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[卖空]]

![[卖空损益]]

![[卖空借券与定位]]

这也解释了[[套利限制]]中的实施障碍：估值最终正确，不意味着资金和券源允许一直等下去。证券融资的权益要求与[[净额与抵押品#VM 与 IM 覆盖不同时间段|衍生品 IM、VM]]有不同口径；期货每日结算与期权支付继续使用上一章的[[期货合约]]、[[期权]]和[[到期支付与投资损益]]，不因跨章节而复制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These mechanics explain implementation constraints in [[套利限制|limits of arbitrage]]: eventual valuation accuracy does not guarantee funding or securities until then. Securities-account equity requirements differ from [[净额与抵押品#VM 与 IM 覆盖不同时间段|derivatives IM and VM]]. Futures settlement and option payoffs reuse [[期货合约|futures]], [[期权|options]] and [[到期支付与投资损益|payoff versus profit]], rather than duplicating them for another chapter.

## 七、把整条交易路径走一遍
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Walk through the whole trade*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一笔计划中的交易，从“信号什么时候可知”开始，依次核对账户与产品权限、指令约束、可见深度及成交规则、实际费用、清算义务、券款交收、持有人记录和后续分配。融资或卖空时，另跟踪贷款、保证金和借券义务。每一步都可能改变最终可实现的结果，而不是给理论成交价加一个固定手续费就够了。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with when the signal becomes known. Check account and product eligibility, instruction constraints, visible depth and matching, actual costs, clearing obligations, securities and cash settlement, ownership records and later distributions. Financing and shorting add loans, margin and borrowing obligations. Implementation requires more than adding a fixed commission to a theoretical execution price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 同一收盘价产生信号，又假设全部订单以这个收盘价成交，哪里需要检查？
> 先确认信号在下单截止前是否已经完整可知，再看是否参加了相应撮合、该时点可用数量和队列约束。否则可能既用了未来信息，又假定了无法获得的满额成交；即使无信息泄露，费用与结算仍须单独处理。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Check whether the complete signal was available before the relevant order cutoff, whether the order entered that matching process, and what quantity and queue position were available. Otherwise the assumption may combine look-ahead bias with impossible full execution. Costs and settlement still matter even without information leakage.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 股价从 100 除息到 95、股息 5；指数和投资者都亏了 5% 吗？
> 价格收益为 -5%，但忽略其他现金流与费用的持有期总收益为 0%。还要确认指数是价格版还是全收益版；指数点数变化不能直接代替投资者财富变化。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Price return is -5%, while holding-period total return is 0% before other cash flows and costs. Identify the index's return variant rather than equating index-point changes with investor wealth.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 市值 9,000、借款 6,000，权益要求为证券市值的 40%；投入多少现金偿债才刚好达标？
> 现权益 3,000，要求 3,600，投入 600 偿债后，债务为 5,400、权益为 3,600。若改成另一个包含留存现金的资产分母，必须重新列式，不能混用同一个答案。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Equity is 3,000 against a requirement of 3,600. Repaying 600 reduces debt to 5,400 and restores equity of 3,600. A different denominator including retained cash requires a new calculation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 有 DvP，卖方仍未交券；买方不会先付掉本金，为什么交易还可能有成本？
> 重买证券可能更贵，还可能需要临时融资或借券以履行其他义务。DvP 的本金保护不等于保证按时履约、保证成交价格或消除全部系统风险。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Replacement securities may cost more, and other obligations may require temporary funding or borrowing. DvP's principal protection does not ensure timely performance or eliminate all risks.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
