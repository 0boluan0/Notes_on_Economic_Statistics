### 1.1.3 宏观经济分析的主要方法

*1.1.3 Main Methods of Macroeconomic Analysis*

>[!note] 总量分析法
>* 总量：是反映整个社会经济活动的经济变量 
>* 量的总和：国民收入、总供给、总需求、总储蓄 
>* 平均量或比例量:价格水平、利润率、利息率
>研究总量的决定、变动及其相互之间的关系，从整体上说明国民经济的情况及其变动趋势 ，是一种动态分析。
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Analysis of Total Quantities**
>
>* **Total:** An economic variable that reflects the entire social and economic activities.
>* **Sum of quantities:** National income, total supply, total demand, total savings.
>* **Average quantity or ratio quantity:** Price level, profit rate, interest rate.
>
>The study of the determinants, changes, and mutual relationships of total quantities aims to explain the situation and trend of the national economy from a holistic perspective. This is a dynamic analysis.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 经济指标法
>通过对宏观经济指标进行计算、分析、 对比以了解宏观经济运行概况， 并对经济运行做简单预测。
>先行指标 同步指标 滞后指标 综合经济指标
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Economic Indicator Method**
>By calculating, analyzing, and comparing macroeconomic indicators to understand the overall operation of the macroeconomy, and making simple forecasts of economic operations.
>Leading Indicators, Coincident Indicators, Lagging Indicators, Comprehensive Economic Indicators
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 结构分析法 
>对经济系统中各个组成部分及它们的对比关系 变动规律进行分析 ，是一种静态分析
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>Structural Analysis
>It involves analyzing the components and their comparative relationships and patterns of change within an economic system, which is a form of static analysis.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 计量经济模型 
>是一个或一组反映经济指标、宏观经济之间数量关系的方程式 ，经济现象及其代表变量之间的关系大多 属于相关关系或函数关系. 
>三个要素:经济变量,参数,随机误差
>==缺陷:容易忽略某些作用较大的变量==
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Econometric Models**
>are a single or a set of equations that reflect the quantitative relationships between economic indicators and macroeconomic variables. The relationships between economic phenomena and their representative variables are often of a correlation or functional nature.
>
>Three elements: economic variables, parameters, and random errors.
>**Defects:** Often overlooks significant variables.

### 1.1.4 宏观经济形势及基本指标复习
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1.4 Review of Macroeconomic Conditions and Basic Indicators*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (1)GDP

按市场价格计算的一个国家（或地区）所有常住单位在一定时期内生 产活动的最终成果。 
GDP的增长速度一般用来衡量经济增长率，这是反映一定时期经济发 展水平变化程度的动态指标，也是反映一个国家经济是否具有活力的 基本指标。
GDP=C+I+G+(X-M)衰退性顺差 
经济增长率与股市关联性 • 经济增长率变动会引起资本规模的相应变动，从而引起股价的 变动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
GDP is the market value of the final output produced over a given period by all resident units of a country or region.
GDP growth is commonly used to measure economic growth. It is a dynamic indicator of changes in the level of economic development and a basic gauge of an economy's vitality.
$GDP=C+I+G+(X-M)$. A trade surplus may also be recessionary.
**Economic growth and the stock market:** changes in the growth rate alter the scale of capital activity and can therefore affect share prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (2)失业率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Unemployment Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是劳动力人口中失业人数所占的比重，但并不包括有劳动能力却不寻找工作的自愿失业情 况。 
失业率是以GDP与潜在GDP水平相对应的。 
充分就业并不是完全就业，充分就业情况下存在结构性失业。 
失业率过高会引发一系列社会问题。 我国目前统计的失业率是城镇登记失业率，并不包括农村户口，因而低估了我国的失业状况。
失业率上升：影响宏观经济的增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The unemployment rate is the proportion of the labor force that is unemployed. It excludes people who are able to work but voluntarily choose not to seek employment.
Unemployment is related to the gap between actual GDP and potential GDP.
Full employment does not mean that every person is employed; structural unemployment still exists at full employment.
Very high unemployment can cause a range of social problems. China's statistic here is the registered urban unemployment rate. Because it excludes people with rural household registration, it understates unemployment nationwide.
A rising unemployment rate weakens macroeconomic growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (3)通货膨胀
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Inflation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指的是社会经济生活中，一般物价水平在较长的时期内持续以较高的幅度上涨。
通货膨胀对经济的影响体现在两方面 
	• 会产生财富和收入再分配效应
	• 扭曲相对价格，从而降低资源的配置效率 
通货膨胀与股市的关系 
	• 通货膨胀本身对股价的影响是复杂的, 
	• 调节通货膨胀的政策对股市会产生直接的影响 
	• 需求拉动通膨 
	• 成本推动通膨 
	• 结构性通膨
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inflation is a sustained and substantial rise in the general price level over an extended period.
Its economic effects include:
	• redistributing wealth and income;
	• distorting relative prices and thereby reducing allocative efficiency.
Its relationship with the stock market is complex:
	• inflation itself can affect share prices in different ways;
	• policies used to control inflation directly affect the stock market;
	• important forms include demand-pull, cost-push, and structural inflation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通货膨胀对经济的影响： 收入和财产的再分配以及改变产品产量与类型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inflation affects the economy by redistributing income and wealth and by changing the quantity and composition of output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (4)通货紧缩
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(4) Deflation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通货紧缩造成经济衰退或萧条
使厂商的经营规模被迫压缩，社会总产出萎缩， 投资冲动降低，股市呈现“熊市” 特征
通货紧缩会导致股市持续低迷 
通货紧缩会使证券市场的重心向债券业务倾斜 
证券市场与通货紧缩互为因果关系 
证券市场尤其是股市的崩溃会直接引发通货紧缩
证券市场的萧条会加重通货紧缩的治理难度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deflation leads to economic recessions or depressions, forcing firms to reduce their scale of operations, shrinking total social output, dampening investment enthusiasm, and causing stock markets to exhibit "bear market" characteristics. Deflation can also lead to sustained downturns in stock markets. Deflation shifts the focus of securities market towards bond business. securities market and deflation reinforce each other causally. The collapse of securities market, especially its depression, increases the difficulty of combating deflation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (2)利率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Interest Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

又称利息率，是指在借贷期内资金贷入方向贷出方承担利息额占所贷资金的比率。
利率是资金的价格，从宏观角度看，利率反映了整个资金市场的供需状况： 
	• 经济繁荣增长，资金需求增加，利率提高。
基准利率是政府货币政策的目标。 利率水平也是债券价格的主要决定因素。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The interest rate is the interest paid by the borrower to the lender during the loan period as a proportion of the amount borrowed.
Interest is the price of funds. At the macroeconomic level, the interest rate reflects supply and demand across the market for funds:
	• during an economic expansion, demand for funds rises and interest rates tend to increase.
The benchmark interest rate is an important target or instrument of monetary policy. Interest rates are also a major determinant of bond prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (3)汇率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) exchange rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

外汇市场上一国货币与其他国货币相互交换的比率，也可以将其看作 是以一国货币表示的另一国货币的价格。 
汇率对股市的影响 
	• 汇率上升，本国出口会受到成本上升的消极影响，但由于本币升值往 往反映了本国经济增长的强劲势头和经济竞争力的上升，会鼓舞投资 者的信心，股市上涨的概率较高，尤其是银行股和地产股；同时，本 币升值将导致资本流入本国，使本国证券市场需求增加。 
	• 本币贬值，出口型企业将受益；但本币贬值将导致资本流出本国，使 本国证券市场需求减少；同时，有可能因为进口商品价格提高，带动 国内物价水平上涨，引发通货膨胀。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the foreign-exchange market, an exchange rate is the rate at which one country's currency exchanges for another; equivalently, it is the price of one currency expressed in another.
**Effects on the stock market**
	• In the convention used here, a rise in the exchange rate is associated with domestic-currency appreciation. Higher export costs may hurt exporters, but appreciation can also signal strong growth and competitiveness, bolster investor confidence, and support share prices—especially bank and property shares. Appreciation may also attract capital inflows and increase demand for domestic securities.
	• Domestic-currency depreciation benefits exporters, but it can cause capital outflows and reduce demand for domestic securities. By raising import prices, it can also increase the domestic price level and generate inflation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

LAS SAS， P P, 四 SAS, Py | 一 一 -天 水 A AD O 一 Y Y, Y
![[Pasted image 20250317141737.png]]
这个图展示的是短期总供给（$SAS$）曲线向左上方移动对经济均衡的影响，其中长期总供给（$LAS$）保持不变，总需求（$AD$）也没有发生变化。
具体解释如下：
• 初始时，经济均衡点在$A$点，此时价格水平为$P_0$，实际产出为潜在产出$\overline{Y}$。
• 当短期总供给曲线从$SAS_0$向左上方移动到$SAS_1$（例如由于生产成本增加），短期内产出会从$\overline{Y}$减少到$Y_1$，经济均衡从点$A$移动到点$B$。
• 此时价格水平会上升，从$P_0$上升到$P_1$，出现了通货膨胀。
• 长期来看，由于潜在产出$\overline{Y}$没有改变，如果没有需求面的政策干预，经济最终会回到潜在产出水平，但价格水平会更高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The figure shows how an upward and leftward shift in short-run aggregate supply, $SAS$, affects equilibrium while long-run aggregate supply, $LAS$, and aggregate demand, $AD$, remain unchanged.
The sequence is:
• Initially, the economy is at equilibrium point $A$, with price level $P_0$ and actual output equal to potential output, $\overline{Y}$.
• If the short-run aggregate-supply curve shifts upward and leftward from $SAS_0$ to $SAS_1$, perhaps because production costs rise, short-run output falls from $\overline{Y}$ to $Y_1$, and equilibrium moves from $A$ to $B$.
• The price level rises from $P_0$ to $P_1$, producing inflation.
• In the long run, potential output $\overline{Y}$ has not changed. Without demand-side intervention, the economy eventually returns to potential output, but at a higher price level.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图 3-2（总需求冲击）可概括为：$AD$ 右移使经济短期从 $A\to B$（产出上升、价格上升），随后 $SAS$ 左移使经济回到潜在产出（$B\to C$），价格进一步上升。
这张图描述的是经济中总需求（$AD$）发生正向冲击（向右移动）后的调整过程。
具体讲解如下：
• 最初，经济处于长期均衡状态（点$A$），实际产出等于潜在产出$\overline{Y}$，价格水平为$P_0$。
• 发生总需求冲击后，总需求曲线从$AD_0$向右移动到$AD_1$（可能由于扩张性的财政或货币政策）。
• 在短期内，经济均衡点由$A$移动到$B$，导致实际产出从$\overline{Y}$增加到$Y_1$，价格水平也由$P_0$上升至$P_1$。
• 由于短期内经济产出超过潜在产出，生产要素和资源变得紧张（例如劳动力供不应求），导致工资和生产成本逐步上涨。因此，短期总供给曲线$SAS$向左上方移动，从$SAS_0$移动到$SAS_1$。
• 经济逐渐从点$B$向新的长期均衡点$C$调整，在此过程中，价格水平进一步上升至$P_2$，实际产出则回归到潜在产出$\overline{Y}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Figure 3-2, an aggregate-demand shock, can be summarized as follows: a rightward shift in $AD$ moves the economy from $A\to B$ in the short run, raising both output and the price level. A subsequent leftward shift in $SAS$ returns output to potential as the economy moves from $B\to C$, while the price level rises further.
The figure shows the adjustment process after a positive, rightward shock to aggregate demand, $AD$.
The sequence is:
• Initially, the economy is in long-run equilibrium at point $A$. Actual output equals potential output, $\overline{Y}$, and the price level is $P_0$.
• The aggregate-demand shock shifts the aggregate-demand curve rightward from $AD_0$ to $AD_1$, perhaps because of expansionary fiscal or monetary policy.
• In the short run, equilibrium moves from $A$ to $B$. Actual output rises from $\overline{Y}$ to $Y_1$, and the price level rises from $P_0$ to $P_1$.
• Because short-run output exceeds potential, factors of production become scarce—for example, labor demand exceeds supply—so wages and production costs gradually rise. The short-run aggregate-supply curve, $SAS$, therefore shifts upward and to the left from $SAS_0$ to $SAS_1$.
• The economy gradually adjusts from $B$ to the new long-run equilibrium at $C$. The price level rises further to $P_2$, while actual output returns to potential output, $\overline{Y}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存款准备金率上升，商业银行的资金来源减少，贷款能力下降 ，货币供应就会紧缩，社会资金就会紧张，股票价格有下跌的趋 势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Required reserve ratio
==The core mechanism is the amount of money available in the market: a higher reserve requirement leaves less money available, so securities prices tend to fall.==
When the required reserve ratio rises, commercial banks have fewer lendable funds and less capacity to extend credit. Money-supply growth tightens, funds become scarcer, and share prices tend to decline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再贴现率对证券市场的影响 
再贴现率提高，市场货币量减少，证券价格 下降。 
公开市场业务对证券市场的影响 政府通过购回国债来增大货币供应量，一 方面减少了国债的供给，从而减少证券市场的 总供给，导致证券价格上升。另一方面，货币 供应量的增加会使市场资金相对充裕，对证券 的需求增加，证券价格上升。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Effect of the rediscount rate on the securities market**
A higher rediscount rate reduces the amount of money in the market and lowers securities prices.
**Effect of open-market operations on the securities market**
When the government or central bank purchases government bonds, it expands the money supply. The purchase also reduces the supply of government bonds available to the market, lowering the total supply of securities and raising their prices. At the same time, the larger money supply makes funds more abundant, increases demand for securities, and further raises securities prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利率
在股利折现模型中，股票的内在价值$V$由下一期股利$D_1$、必要收益率$k$与永续增长率$g$决定：
$$
V=\frac{D_1}{k-g}
$$
- $D_1$：下一期预期股利，反映公司盈利能力
- $k$：投资者要求的回报率，通常受无风险利率和风险溢价共同影响
- $g$：长期可持续增长率，往往与利润留存再投资能力相关
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interest rate

In the dividend discount model, the intrinsic value of a stock $V$ is determined by the dividend expected in the next period $D_1$, the required rate of return $k$, and the perpetual growth rate $g$:

- $D_1$: The expected dividend for the next period, reflecting the company's profitability
- $k$: The investor's required rate of return, typically influenced by risk-free rate and risk premium
- $g$: The long-term sustainable growth rate, often related to the company's ability to reinvest retained earnings
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**利率变化的两条传导链**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Two Channels of Transmission for Interest Rate Changes**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| **传导链**     | **变量受影响**                   | **方向**                             | **结果**        |
| ----------- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------- |
| **公司财务成本链** | 借款成本↑或↓ → 利润↓或↑ → $D_1$、$g$ | 利率↑ ⇒ $D_1↓$、$g↓$利率↓ ⇒ $D_1↑$、$g↑$ | **分子、分母同时变动** |
| **资金配置链**   | 市场无风险利率↑或↓ → 投资者要求收益率$k$↑或↓ | 利率↑ ⇒ $k↑$利率↓ ⇒ $k↓$               | **仅分母变动**     |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| **Transmission channel** | **Variables affected** | **Direction** | **Result** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Corporate financing-cost channel** | Borrowing costs ↑ or ↓ → profit ↓ or ↑ → $D_1$ and $g$ | Interest rate ↑ ⇒ $D_1↓$, $g↓$; interest rate ↓ ⇒ $D_1↑$, $g↑$ | **Both numerator and denominator change** |
| **Portfolio-allocation channel** | Market risk-free rate ↑ or ↓ → investors' required return $k$ ↑ or ↓ | Interest rate ↑ ⇒ $k↑$; interest rate ↓ ⇒ $k↓$ | **Only the denominator changes** |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **利率↑ → 公司财务费用增加**    
    - 借款利率上升 → 利息支出增加 → 净利润下降 → $D_1$与$g$下降
    - 在 $$V=\frac{D_1}{k-g}$$ 中，分子$D_1$下降且分母$(k-g)$扩大，双重挤压导致$V$下跌 → **股价下跌**
2. **利率↑ → 资金从证券市场流向银行**
    - 储蓄或理财收益更具吸引力 → 投资者要求更高回报率 → $k$上升
    - 分母增大 $(k-g)↑$ → $V$下降 → **债券与股票价格齐跌**
3. **利率↓ → 公司财务费用减少**
    - 融资成本降低 → 净利润提升 → $D_1$与$g$上升
    - 分子↑、分母↓ → $V$上升 → **股价上升**
4. **利率↓ → 资金由银行流入证券市场**
    - 无风险利率下降 → 投资者降低回报要求 → $k$下降
    - 分母缩小 $(k-g)↓$ → $V$上升 → **债券与股票价格齐涨**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Interest rate ↑ → corporate financing costs rise**<br>
   - Higher borrowing rates → greater interest expense → lower net profit → lower $D_1$ and $g$.
   - In $$V=\frac{D_1}{k-g}$$, the numerator $D_1$ falls while the denominator $(k-g)$ widens. Both effects reduce $V$ → **share price falls**.
**2.** **Interest rate ↑ → funds move from securities to bank deposits**<br>
   - Saving and wealth-management products become more attractive → investors require a higher return → $k$ rises.
   - The denominator $(k-g)$ widens → $V$ falls → **bond and share prices both fall**.
**3.** **Interest rate ↓ → corporate financing costs fall**<br>
   - Lower funding costs → higher net profit → higher $D_1$ and $g$.
   - The numerator rises and the denominator narrows → $V$ rises → **share price rises**.
**4.** **Interest rate ↓ → funds move from banks into securities**<br>
   - A lower risk-free rate reduces investors' required return → $k$ falls.
   - The denominator $(k-g)$ narrows → $V$ rises → **bond and share prices both rise**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1、利率对负债高,借款大的公司影响大
2、利率变化对证券市场的影响程度要受投资者是 否预期到此次变化以及实际的变化和预期的变化的差 距影响。 
3、利率变化对证券市场的影响程度同时受当时市 场所处的平均价格水平影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Interest rates have a greater impact on companies with high debt and large borrowings.<br>
**2.** The impact of interest rate changes on securities market is influenced by whether investors anticipated this change, as well as the difference between actual changes and expected changes.<br>
**3.** The impact of interest rate changes on securities market is also affected by the average price level at which the market is situated at the time.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通货膨胀对证券市场的影响： 由于名义利率=实际利率+通货膨胀率，所以通货膨胀率的变化 也会导致k 的变化。通货膨胀率上升，k增加，证券价格下降。同 时，通货膨胀率的变化也会导致D 的变化，但变化的方向有时与 通货膨胀率的变化一致，有时又不一致。这是因为当通货膨胀率 较小时，有利于经济的发展，D值会增加，证券价格上升；而当 通货膨胀率较大时，不利于经济的发展，D值会下降，证券价格 下降。综合来看，在通货膨胀初期，通货膨胀率较小，会导致证 券价格上升。而在通货膨胀中、后期，通货膨胀率较大，会导致 证券价格下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The impact of inflation on securities market: Given that the nominal interest rate equals the real interest rate plus the inflation rate, changes in the inflation rate also lead to changes in k. An increase in the inflation rate results in an increase in k, leading to a decrease in securities prices. Additionally, changes in the inflation rate affect D as well, though the direction of change is sometimes consistent with the inflation rate's change and sometimes not. This is because when inflation rates are low, it benefits economic development, increasing D values and raising securities prices; conversely, when inflation rates are high, they hinder economic development, decreasing D values and lowering securities prices. In summary, during the early stages of inflation, when the inflation rate is relatively small, it leads to an increase in securities prices. However, in the middle and later stages of inflation, when the inflation rate is large, it results in a decline in securities prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Its effects on securities markets are gradual but persistent.

==Expansionary fiscal policy increases the amount of money and demand in the economy, so securities prices rise.==
Higher government spending and lower fiscal revenue increase aggregate demand, improve corporate performance, reduce business risk, and raise household income.

==紧缩型财政政策,社会上钱少了,证券市场价格下降==减少财政支出，增加财政收入，减少社会总需求， 抑制过热经济，公司业绩下降，居民收入减少，证券价格下跌。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==Contractionary fiscal policy reduces money and demand in the economy, so securities prices fall.==
Lower government spending and higher fiscal revenue reduce aggregate demand and restrain an overheating economy. Corporate performance and household income decline, and securities prices fall.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税收:
进口关税（根据奖出限入的原则，出口货 物基本上免征关税）
对证券市场的影响（以关 税下降为例） 
①关税下降会减少公司进口物品的成本， 从而提高公司的效益。比如，以进口原料为主 和 进 口 大 宗 设 备 的 企 业 将 从 中 受 惠 。 
②关税下降导致进口量增加，从而在一定 程度会抑制国内生产和就业，特别是生产水平 相差较大的一些行业受此冲击较大。
③关税下降将导致国内生产要素从相对劣势生产部门转 移至相对优势部门，从而提高优势行业的竞争力。 关税下降→劣势部门的生产能力下降→ →劣势部门的生产要素过剩→生产要素的价格下降→ 优势部门的生产要素价格下降→成本下降→竞争力提 高 关税下降→进口增加→贸易逆差扩大→货币贬值→国 内企业竞争力争强

Taxation:
Import tariffs follow the principle of encouraging exports and restricting imports; exported goods are generally exempt from tariffs.
**Effects on securities markets, using a tariff reduction as an example**
① Lower tariffs reduce firms' costs for imported inputs. Firms that rely heavily on imported raw materials or large imported equipment therefore benefit.
② Lower tariffs increase imports and may suppress domestic production and employment, especially in industries with a large productivity disadvantage.
③ Lower tariffs shift domestic factors of production from relatively disadvantaged sectors toward relatively advantaged sectors, strengthening the latter's competitiveness.
Tariffs ↓ → productive capacity in disadvantaged sectors ↓ → surplus factors in those sectors → factor prices ↓ → input costs in advantaged sectors ↓ → competitiveness ↑.
Tariffs ↓ → imports ↑ → trade deficit widens → currency depreciates → domestic firms become more competitive.

Exchange rate:
**1.** In the convention used here, a rise in the exchange rate means domestic-currency depreciation. Domestic products become more competitive and exporters benefit, so their share and bond prices rise. Import-dependent firms face higher costs and lower profits, so their securities prices fall.<br>
**2.** Depreciation can cause capital outflows. The loss of capital reduces demand in the domestic securities market and lowers market prices.<br>
**3.** Depreciation raises the domestic-currency price of imports and can generate inflation. The effect on securities must be evaluated in light of the economic environment, the firm concerned, and the policy response.<br>
**4.** To stabilize the exchange rate, the government may draw on foreign-exchange reserves and sell foreign currency. This reduces the domestic money supply and may lower securities prices until the exchange rate returns toward equilibrium; the reversal may subsequently support securities prices.<br>
**5.** The government may coordinate bond- and foreign-exchange-market operations to restrain the exchange-rate increase without shrinking the money supply. Selling foreign exchange while repurchasing government bonds raises government-bond prices.<br>

During the recovery phase, loose policies proved effective, with GDP growth accelerating and remaining above potential, while inflation continued to decline. Surviving enterprises had higher technology levels and stronger management capabilities, with adequate financial reserves.
