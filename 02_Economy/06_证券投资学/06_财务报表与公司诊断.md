### 先找到最重要的确认与计量判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Locate the recognition and measurement judgments that matter most.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原课 PDF488–489 以收入与金融资产为例。收入不能仅看是否开票或收款；在 IFRS 15 的五步框架下，依次识别合同、识别履约义务、确定交易价格、按相对单独售价分摊，再于履约义务满足时确认。一个“设备加一年服务”的合同可能有两个不同的确认进度，不能只因总价一次收齐便把服务收入全部提前入账。这是理解原课控制权思路的补充，不替代具体合同和适用准则的完整判断。[IFRS 15 官方说明](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revenue requires more than an invoice or receipt. IFRS 15 identifies the contract and performance obligations, determines and allocates the price, then recognizes revenue as obligations are satisfied. Equipment bundled with a year of service may have different recognition patterns; receiving all cash upfront does not automatically recognize all service revenue. This explains the course's control-based approach rather than replacing contract-specific accounting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金融资产的“摊余成本／FVOCI／FVTPL”也不是“长期／中期／短期”的另一种说法。先辨金融工具种类，再看管理它的业务模式、合同现金流特征以及适用选择；例如债务工具的本金和利息条件与权益工具的指定规则不是一回事。原课把长期股票笼统放入 FVOCI，只能作为需要进一步检查的简写。减值也不能靠把“应收账款”改名“其他应收款”来规避。[IFRS 9 官方说明](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-9-financial-instruments/)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Amortized cost, FVOCI, and FVTPL are not alternative labels for long-, medium-, and short-term holdings. Identify the instrument, business model, cash-flow characteristics, and applicable elections. Debt-instrument conditions differ from equity designations. The course's blanket FVOCI label for long-term shares needs further examination, and relabeling a receivable does not remove loss-assessment requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

|---|---|---|

| 补贴和其他偶发收入增大 | 获得条件、确认依据、持续性及主业利润 | 正常补贴等于舞弊，或补贴等于主业改善 |

None of these observations alone establishes fraud or permits an automatic write-off.

## 三、比较之前固定口径，再学习怎样分解差额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix measurement bases before comparing and decomposing changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个比率把两项数字放在一起，分母决定它到底在问什么。净利润／资产与 EBIT／资产、净利润／期末权益与净利润／平均权益，可能都对，却不是同一个问题。先做这个检查，再看数值高低。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A denominator defines the question. Net-income-to-assets differs from EBIT-to-assets, and closing-equity returns differ from average-equity returns. Each can be valid on a stated basis. Establish that basis before ranking the result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[00_Knowledge/财务比率口径匹配]]
![[00_Knowledge/财务报表比较分析]]

原课 PDF522–533 的比较可以这样落地：绝对数告诉我们差多少金额；结构百分比告诉我们收入或资产中的份额如何变化；连续年份的指数告诉我们相对基期走到了哪里。例如收入 100→120→132，第二年相对第一年的水平指数是 120%，增长率才是 20%；第三年环比指数 110%、定基指数 132%，对应增长率 10% 和 32%。基数为零或负数时，不照普通正基数解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Absolute comparisons measure amount differences; common-size percentages measure composition; time indexes locate values relative to a base. Revenue of 100→120→132 gives a second-year index of 120% but growth of 20%. The third year's chain index is 110% and fixed-base index is 132%, corresponding to growth of 10% and 32%. Zero or negative bases require different interpretation.

### 用三个中间桥梁拆开 ROE
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use three matching bridges to unpack ROE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[00_Knowledge/杜邦分解]]

在原课 A/B 对比中，A 的第二年净利率 3%、周转率 2，B 分别为 5%、1.2，二者资产净利率都为 6%。A 更依赖销售规模相对资产的周转，B 每元销售留下更多净利润；这不证明风险或价值相同。A 权益乘数若为 2.02，ROE 才进一步得到 $6\%\times2.02=12.12\%$。PDF521 下半页的 6% 实际处于资产净利率层，不能按该处误写的 ROE 标题理解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

Both produce net-income ROA of 6%, through different operating patterns, without establishing equal risk or value. With A's equity multiplier of 2.02, ROE is 12.12%. The lower part of PDF521 labels the 6% row incorrectly: its calculation is ROA, not ROE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前一年 $4.53\%\times1.6304=7.385712\%$，再乘 2.02 得 $14.91913824\%$。若先把 ROA 舍入成 7.39% 再乘，得到约 14.93%，这是中间舍入差异；后文复算都先保留未舍入值，最后显示结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 总变动确定，并不意味着每项贡献唯一
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fixed total change does not imply unique component contributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[00_Knowledge/连环替代法]]
![[00_Knowledge/连环替代顺序边界]]

把原课 PDF525–526 的一般式完整写出来。设基期 $P_0=A_0B_0C_0$、比较期 $P_1=A_1B_1C_1$，按 A→B→C 替代：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write out the course's general procedure. For baseline $P_0=A_0B_0C_0$ and comparison $P_1=A_1B_1C_1$, update A, then B, then C:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_0\to Q_A=A_1B_0C_0\to Q_B=A_1B_1C_0\to P_1.$$
$$\Delta_A=(A_1-A_0)B_0C_0,\quad
\Delta_B=A_1(B_1-B_0)C_0,\quad
\Delta_C=A_1B_1(C_1-C_0).$$

相邻相减后相加，中间的 $Q_A,Q_B$ 抵消，所以三项恰好加回总差；换顺序会改变交互项分给谁。例如净利率 8%→6%、周转率 1→1.2、乘数 2→3，ROE 沿本顺序为 16%→12%→14.4%→21.6%，分别分摊 −4、+2.4、+7.2 个百分点。可以说“按这个顺序，杠杆分摊最大”，不能把它称为唯一因果贡献，更不能由此建议公司直接加债。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The intermediate values cancel when differences are summed. Changing the order reallocates interactions. A margin change of 8%→6%, turnover of 1→1.2, and multiplier of 2→3 trace ROE of 16%→12%→14.4%→21.6%, allocating −4, +2.4, and +7.2 percentage points. Leverage has the largest allocation under this order, not a uniquely identified causal contribution or an automatic case for borrowing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 五、把利润诊断接到真实现金需要
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Connect the profit diagnosis to actual cash needs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

BW 的现有数据能告诉我们盈利与资产结构，却不能直接给出现金答案。原课 PDF585–595 因此转向四个问题：是否按时偿债、销售和资产怎样产生现金、能否应付投资与分红、现金差额怎样解释利润质量。先从净利润到 CFO 的桥开始，而不是把 CFO 直接设成“收入减成本减税”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BW's available data describe earnings and asset structure, not a full cash answer. PDF585–595 therefore ask about timely debt service, cash from sales and assets, investment and dividends, and cash evidence about earnings quality. Start with the profit-to-CFO bridge rather than simply setting CFO equal to revenue less costs and taxes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如净利润 100 含处置收益 10，另含非付现折旧 20；设没有其他非现金、税项或分类调整，按 CFO 调节范围计算的非现金经营营运资本增加 30。这里的变动只包括已调节非现金交易和重分类的存货、经营应收减经营应付等净额，排除现金及现金等价物本身与融资性债务，不能直接套入 CPA 管理用报表可能包含经营现金的[[经营营运资本]]口径。先去掉不属于本期经营现金的处置收益，再加回折旧，最后扣新增占用，CFO 为 $100-10+20-30=80$。购置设备的付款还在投资活动中，不要在 CFO 里再扣一遍。这个简例与 BW 是不同公司，不把缺失资料补造成 BW 事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose net income of 100 includes a disposal gain of 10 and non-cash depreciation of 20, with no other non-cash, tax, or classification adjustments, while non-cash operating working capital within the CFO reconciliation rises by 30. This change comprises inventories and operating receivables less operating payables after non-cash and reclassification adjustments; it excludes cash, cash equivalents, and financing debt. It differs from the CPA management-reporting definition of [[经营营运资本|operating working capital]], which may include operating cash. Reversing the gain, adding depreciation, and deducting additional working capital gives CFO of 80. Equipment payments belong to investing and are not deducted again from CFO. This separate hypothetical company does not fill missing BW data with invented facts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每股收益度量本期赚取的会计利润，现金股利则是分配决策，二者之间通过支付率联系。对公司而言，留存多少利润影响融资需要；对投资者而言，按什么价格买入决定股利的价格收益率。这是两个分母完全不同的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
EPS measures earned accounting profit, while cash dividends reflect distribution decisions; payout connects them. Retention affects company financing needs, whereas the investor's price determines dividend yield. These questions have different denominators.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[00_Knowledge/股利支付率]]
![[00_Knowledge/股利收益率]]

股价放到盈利旁边得到市盈率，放到账面权益旁边得到市净率。它们是价格相对于某个参照量的倍数，不是会计报表替我们做出的买卖判断。比较倍数之前，先明确时期、归属范围、盈利是否正常以及权益是否为正。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price relative to earnings yields P/E; price relative to book equity yields P/B. These are benchmark multiples, not buy-or-sell judgments delivered by accounting. Establish periods, attribution, earnings persistence, and the sign of equity before comparisons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[00_Knowledge/市盈率]]
![[00_Knowledge/市净率]]

若公司亏损，收入仍可能为正，于是估值实践还会参考市销率。它保留了规模参照，但没有消除盈利、资本需求与杠杆问题。读完这张补充卡，再回看 BW：即使知道收入2211，没有价格和普通股市值数据，也不能硬算它的市销率或市盈率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A loss-making company can still have positive revenue, motivating a P/S comparison. That preserves a scale reference without solving profitability, capital requirements, or leverage. BW revenue of 2211 alone is insufficient to compute P/S or P/E without share prices or ordinary market capitalization.
