#### ETS 学习链：从描述边界到跨模型比较

先把历史结构的分解与未来外推分开；否则一条平滑趋势线很容易被误当成预测模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First separate historical decomposition from future extrapolation; otherwise a smooth trend line is easily mistaken for a forecasting model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[时间序列分解与预测]]

没有需外推的趋势或季节时，SES 只更新水平。加入趋势后，必须明确远期是否无限延伸；再加入季节后，还要选择差值还是比率表示。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With no trend or seasonality to extrapolate, SES updates only a level. Adding trend raises the long-horizon extrapolation question, while adding seasonality requires a difference-versus-ratio choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[SES权重机制]]

![[Holt阻尼趋势]]

![[Holt-Winters季节形式]]

平滑递推只给点预测方法；ETS 再加入创新状态空间，固定 E/T/S 位置，并把“成分怎样相加”与“误差怎样进入”分成两个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smoothing recursions define point-forecast methods. ETS adds an innovations state-space model, fixes the E/T/S positions, and separates component combination from the way random errors enter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[ETS三轴模型]]

![[加法平滑与加法误差]]

误差形式还固定了应检查哪一种一步 innovation。随后才可一致地谈 likelihood、初始状态与诊断；但样本内合格仍不能排除未来断点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The error form determines which one-step innovation should be inspected. Likelihood estimation, initial-state treatment, and diagnostics can then be stated consistently, but in-sample adequacy still cannot rule out future breaks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[ETS创新残差]]

![[ETS似然与初始状态]]

![[ETS残差诊断边界]]

最后把 ETS 与 ARIMA 的重叠范围说精确：先看两族为什么互不包含，再用 SES 特例核对参数符号与初始化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally delimit the ETS–ARIMA overlap: establish why neither family contains the other, then use the SES special case to check parameter signs and initialisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[ETS与ARIMA边界]]

![[SES-ARIMA受限等价]]

ETS 用状态更新组织水平、趋势与季节；ARMA 转而建立随机滞后与误差机制。下面进入 ARMA 学习链，并复用共同的估计、诊断和预测评估边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ETS organises level, trend, and seasonality through state updating. ARMA instead specifies stochastic lag and error mechanisms. The next chain develops ARMA while reusing the shared estimation, diagnostic, and forecast-evaluation boundaries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 预测组合、层级协调与情景
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Forecast combination, reconciliation, and scenarios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续解释、推导和产品—地区算例见[[02_Economy/08_经济预测与决策/02_组合协调与情景|预测组合、协调与情景：从模型分歧到一致决策]]；全局关系见[[预测组合、层级协调与情景.canvas|主题 Canvas]]。以下 16 个原子按“组合 → 聚合结构 → 协调 → 情景与判断”的依赖顺序嵌入；预测任务、实时信息、输入路径与 rolling-origin 直接复用前文共享对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[02_Economy/08_经济预测与决策/02_组合协调与情景|continuous chapter]] supplies the derivations and product-by-region example, while the [[预测组合、层级协调与情景.canvas|topic Canvas]] shows the global relationships. The sixteen atoms below follow the dependency order from combination through aggregation and reconciliation to scenarios and judgment. Forecast-task, real-time information, predictor-path, and rolling-origin objects are reused from the earlier course path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确认成分预测属于同一任务，再看误差是否真正互补。等权是无需估计的组合基准；任何复杂权重都必须按 horizon 在 validation origins 上学习并在 final holdout 前冻结。合法但单独较弱的模型仍可能因误差协方差互补而增加组合价值，所以成分应按共同外样本上的组合增量贡献选择，不能只看单模型排名。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First align the component forecasts and ask whether their errors genuinely diversify one another. Equal weighting is the estimation-free benchmark; more complex weights must be learned by horizon on validation origins and frozen before the final holdout. A valid but individually weaker forecast may still add value through complementary errors, so select components by incremental combination value rather than standalone rank.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

层级和分组结构先由加总矩阵固定；相干性说明结果必须落在哪个空间，预测协调再决定怎样使用各节点 base forecasts。单层方法只保留一个聚合层级；oracle MinT 在无偏且真实正定 $W_h$ 的条件下满足 $V_h\preceq W_h$，逐节点总体方差不升。这个结论不保证每次实现都改善，也不自动延伸到估计或近似 $W_h$ 的现实 pipeline；两者都不能用“数字能相加”替代外样本准确性评价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The summing matrix fixes hierarchical or grouped identities. Coherence defines the admissible space, and reconciliation determines how node-level base forecasts enter it. Single-level methods retain one aggregation level. With unbiased base forecasts and the true positive-definite $W_h$, oracle MinT gives $V_h\preceq W_h$, so every node's population variance is no larger. This does not guarantee improvement on every realisation or automatically extend to an estimated covariance pipeline; arithmetic consistency still cannot replace out-of-sample accuracy evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后把“尚未确定的未来”与“已经确认但模型未见的新信息”分开：前者保留为带假设的条件情景，后者才可能进入显式判断调整。情景没有概率结构就不是预测分布；人工调整没有未调整基线就不能审计增量价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally distinguish unresolved futures from newly confirmed information absent from the model. The former remain explicit conditional scenarios; only the latter may justify a judgmental adjustment. Scenarios without a probability structure are not a predictive distribution, and overrides without an untouched baseline cannot be audited for incremental value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
