### 2. 指数平滑、回归与时间序列预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Exponential Smoothing, Regression, and Time-Series Forecasting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确定目标、时间切分和基准，才能判断更复杂的指数平滑、回归或 ARIMA 是否真的增加样本外价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only after the target, temporal split, and benchmark are fixed can we determine whether a more complex exponential-smoothing, regression, or ARIMA model genuinely adds out-of-sample value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[指数平滑与 ETS]]

ETS 用递归更新直接追踪水平、趋势和季节性；ARMA 转而建立随机滞后与误差机制，并要求用相关结构和残差诊断验证模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ETS tracks level, trend, and seasonality through recursive updating. ARMA instead specifies stochastic lag and error mechanisms and requires validation through dependence patterns and residual diagnostics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测]]

ARMA 主要从序列自身的历史提取动态；动态回归加入外部预测变量、干预或传递滞后，同时仍需处理误差的时间依赖。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ARMA extracts dynamics mainly from a series’ own history. Dynamic regression adds external predictors, interventions, or distributed effects while still accounting for time dependence in the errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[回归预测与动态回归]]

单个模型给出一条预测路径；组合、层级协调和情景分析进一步处理模型不确定性、跨层级一致性与无法用单一概率路径表达的未来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single model produces one forecast path. Forecast combinations, hierarchical reconciliation, and scenarios then address model uncertainty, cross-level coherence, and futures that cannot be represented by one probabilistic path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
