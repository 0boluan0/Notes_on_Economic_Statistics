# 预测组合、协调与情景：从模型分歧到一致决策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Forecast combination, reconciliation, and scenarios: from model disagreement to coherent decisions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!tip] 这条路径解决什么
> 单个模型只能给出一个条件视角；真实系统却常同时面对三种不同问题：几个合理模型意见不一、产品与地区预测加不起来、关键未来驱动又无法用一条路径概括。本章依次用预测组合处理模型不确定性，用预测协调处理聚合约束，用情景和判断调整处理明确但尚未进入模型的信息。
>
> 全局关系见[[预测组合、层级协调与情景.canvas|预测组合、层级协调与情景主题图]]；全课程顺序见[[02_Economy/08_经济预测与决策/00_课程总览|经济预测与决策课程总览]]。本页负责连续解释、推导与算例；链接到的原子负责独立复习和跨课程复用。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real forecasting system often faces three distinct problems: defensible models disagree, forecasts across products and regions fail to add up, and important future drivers cannot be represented by one path. This chapter uses forecast combination for model uncertainty, reconciliation for aggregation constraints, and scenarios or judgmental adjustment for explicit information not yet represented by the statistical model. The topic Canvas shows the global relationships; this page supplies the continuous argument, derivations, and examples.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 先分清三个问题，避免一种方法包打天下
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Separate the three problems before choosing a method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设一家公司每月底预测下一季度销量。ETS、ARIMA 和动态回归各给一套结果；市场部同时要求公司总量、地区和产品预测精确加总；而下季度是否按期推出新产品仍有两种可能。这里不是一个“选最复杂模型”的问题：模型分歧、加总冲突和未来条件不确定，分别需要不同操作。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a company forecasts next-quarter sales each month. ETS, ARIMA, and dynamic regression disagree; company, regional, and product forecasts must add exactly; and a product launch may be either on time or delayed. Model disagreement, aggregation conflict, and uncertainty about future conditions are different objects and need different operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共同起点仍是 [[预测任务定义]] 与 [[预测时点与信息集]]：固定 target、origin、horizon、当时可得的数据、交付尺度和损失。若三套模型预测的根本不是同一对象，后面的平均和评价都没有意义；若地区与总量口径不同，强制相加只会把口径错误伪装成相干。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The common starting point is a fixed target, origin, horizon, real-time information set, delivery scale, and loss. Forecasts for different objects cannot be meaningfully averaged, and inconsistent accounting definitions cannot be repaired by forcing numbers to add.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 预测组合不是选冠军，而是保留互补信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Forecast combination preserves complementary information rather than selecting a champion*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同一 forecast case，令第 $m$ 个模型给出 $\hat y^{(m)}_{T+h\mid T}$。[[预测组合]]的线性形式为
$$
\hat y^{(c)}_{T+h\mid T}=\sum_{m=1}^{M}w_{m,h}\hat y^{(m)}_{T+h\mid T}.
$$
常见约束是 $\sum_m w_{m,h}=1$；再加 $w_{m,h}\ge0$ 就得到凸组合。和为 1 使固定权重在成分预测无偏时保留无偏性，非负则让结果留在成分预测的范围内，但二者都是需要说明的建模限制，不是“平均”二字自动附带的事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A linear combination forms a weighted sum of component forecasts for the same case. Unit-sum weights preserve unbiasedness when fixed components are unbiased; adding non-negativity keeps the result within their convex hull. These are modelling restrictions that must be declared, not automatic consequences of the word “average”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

组合前要逐项对齐：

1. 同一个目标和单位，例如都预测原尺度销量，而不是把销量和对数销量直接相加；
2. 同一个 origin 与数据 vintage，不允许某个模型偷看后来修订值；
3. 同一个 horizon，一步优势不自动延伸到十二步；
4. 同一组 forecast cases，缺失预测不能悄悄改变比较样本；
5. 同一种点预测含义，例如条件均值不能与条件中位数不加说明地平均。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before combination, align the target and unit, origin and data vintage, horizon, forecast cases, and the functional represented by the point forecast. A mean, median, log-scale forecast, or forecast using revised information cannot be silently treated as the same object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 真正的分散来自误差协方差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Genuine diversification comes from forecast-error covariance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $e_{m,h}=y_{T+h}-\hat y_{m,h}$，把各成分误差收进 $e_h$，并记 $\Sigma_h=\operatorname{Var}(e_h)$；组合预测为 $\hat y_{c,h}=w_h'\hat y_h$。一般的组合误差是
$$
e_{c,h}=(1-\mathbf 1'w_h)y_{T+h}+w_h'e_h.
$$
只有在固定权重满足 $\mathbf 1'w_h=1$ 时，它才简化为 $e_{c,h}=w_h'e_h$，所以 [[组合误差协方差|组合方差]]为
$$
\operatorname{Var}(e_{c,h})=w_h'\Sigma_h w_h.
$$
这条条件不是要求所有合法组合都必须权重和为 1；它说明何时成分误差协方差足以描述组合风险。若权重和不为 1，目标项仍在误差中，必须一并计算其方差和协方差。模型数量本身没有神奇作用：对角项记录每个模型各自有多不准，非对角项记录它们是否在同一时期、同一方向犯错。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $e_{m,h}=y_{T+h}-\hat y_{m,h}$, collect the component errors in $e_h$, and define $\Sigma_h=\operatorname{Var}(e_h)$. For $\hat y_{c,h}=w_h'\hat y_h$, the general error is $e_{c,h}=(1-\mathbf 1'w_h)y_{T+h}+w_h'e_h$. Only fixed unit-sum weights reduce this to $w_h'e_h$ and give variance $w_h'\Sigma_h w_h$. This is a condition for using component-error covariance alone, not a rule that every admissible combination must be unit-sum. The off-diagonal terms determine whether models fail together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个模型的组合最容易看清：
$$
\operatorname{Var}(e_c)
=w^2\sigma_1^2+(1-w)^2\sigma_2^2+2w(1-w)\sigma_{12}.
$$
若两模型误差标准差都为 10、相关为 0.2，等权组合方差为
$$
0.25(100)+0.25(100)+2(0.5)(0.5)(20)=60,
$$
所以误差标准差约为 $7.75$。若两个误差完全相同，协方差为 100，等权后方差仍是 100：复制同一种模型十次也不会凭空产生十份信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two equally weighted errors with standard deviation 10 and correlation 0.2, the variance falls to 60 and the standard deviation to about 7.75. If the errors are identical, the variance remains 100. Replicating one model under several names does not create independent evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里需要的是历史**预测误差**，不是预测值相关、训练 residual 相关或参数相关。误差必须由与部署相同的 [[滚动起点评估|rolling origins]] 产生，并按 horizon 分开；否则估计的 $\Sigma_h$ 与实际组合任务不是同一个对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The relevant dependence is among genuine forecast errors, not forecast levels, parameter estimates, or convenient in-sample residuals. It must be generated at deployment-like rolling origins and estimated separately for decision-relevant horizons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 为什么等权是严肃基准，而不是偷懒
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Why equal weighting is a serious benchmark rather than a shortcut*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\Sigma_h$ 已知，可以求最小方差权重；现实中它必须从有限误差历史估计。模型多、origins 少时，逆协方差权重会把噪声放大成极端正负权重。[[等权组合基准]]放弃样本内的名义最优，却不需要估权，因此常有更低的估计风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Oracle minimum-variance weights require a known covariance matrix. In practice it is estimated from a finite error history, and an inverse-covariance solution can turn noise into extreme positive and negative weights. Equal weighting sacrifices oracle optimality while avoiding weight-estimation risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等权不是终点，而是一条必须被击败的比较线。[[组合权重验证]]要求在 validation origins 上估计权重、选择是否非负和是否随 horizon 变化，再冻结整套规则；final holdout 只用于一次评价。若估计权重只在用来求权重的时期内胜出，或窗口移动一个月就完全翻转，还没有证明新增复杂度有价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal weighting is a benchmark, not a universal optimum. Estimate weights and select constraints on validation origins, freeze the full rule, and use the final holdout once. In-sample superiority or violent weight reversals after a small window change do not establish value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个稳妥的交付表应并列：最强单模型、等权组合、候选估计组合；每种都按同一 horizon 和损失报告，并说明成分集合如何确定。先看测试结果再删掉拖后腿模型，也是在测试集上调权。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A defensible report compares the strongest component, equal weighting, and candidate estimated combinations on the same horizons and losses, with the component-selection rule declared in advance. Dropping weak components after inspecting the test results is another form of test-set tuning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单模型排名也不能代替成分选择。一个合法但单独稍弱的模型，若误差与强模型互补，仍可能降低组合风险：两项无偏误差的方差为 $1$ 与 $1.21$、协方差为 $0$ 时，等权组合方差是 $0.5525$。因此应在共同 validation origins 上检验“加入它后组合是否稳定改善”，而不是看到它单独落后就删除；泄漏、错目标或部署时不可生成的预测则应在评价前排除。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Standalone rank is not a component-selection rule. A valid but individually weaker model may still reduce combination risk through complementary errors: variances 1 and 1.21 with zero covariance give an equal-weight variance of 0.5525. Test whether adding a component improves the combination on common validation origins; exclude leakage, target mismatch, or forecasts unavailable at deployment before evaluation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 层级与分组：先把加总结构写成对象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Hierarchies and groups: represent the aggregation structure explicitly*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模型组合解决“同一序列有多个预测”，但公司还可能要求许多相关序列同时可用。[[层级与分组时间序列]]区分两类结构：门店—地区—全国是一条嵌套层级；地区与产品交叉时，同一底层单元既进入地区总量，也进入产品总量，形成没有唯一上卷顺序的分组结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Combination addresses several forecasts for one series. Forecasting systems also need collections of related series. Outlet-region-country data form a nested hierarchy; crossing region with product creates a grouped structure in which each bottom cell contributes to several marginal totals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[加总矩阵]] $S$ 把这些业务恒等式集中写出。最小层级 $T=A+B$ 可表示为
$$
b_t=\begin{bmatrix}A_t\\B_t\end{bmatrix},\qquad
y_t=\begin{bmatrix}T_t\\A_t\\B_t\end{bmatrix},\qquad
S=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix},
\qquad y_t=Sb_t.
$$
$S$ 不是模型从数据中学习出的近似关系；它来自固定的分类、单位和会计口径。若地区边界改变、产品被重新分类，结构应先被更新，不能让协调方法默默吸收定义变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The summing matrix collects deterministic aggregation identities. In the simple total-equals-A-plus-B hierarchy, its first row forms the total and its identity rows retain the bottom series. The matrix comes from classification and accounting rules rather than being a fitted approximation; structural reclassification must be handled explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 相干性与预测协调
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Coherence and forecast reconciliation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若预测向量可写成 $\tilde y_h=S\tilde b_h$，它就满足 [[层级预测相干性|相干性]]。假设总量模型给 110，地区 A、B 模型给 60 和 42；三者不能同时为真，因为 $60+42\ne110$。不相干没有告诉我们哪个模型错，只说明这三项不能作为同一计划共同使用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A forecast is coherent if it can be written as $S\tilde b_h$. Forecasts of 110 for the total and 60 and 42 for two regions cannot jointly hold. Incoherence does not identify the erroneous node; it says that the collection cannot be used as one consistent plan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[预测协调]]从对各节点独立建模得到的 base forecasts $\hat y_h$ 出发，构造
$$
\tilde y_h=SG\hat y_h.
$$
由于结果左乘 $S$，它自动落在相干空间。这里 base forecast 只表示“协调前预测”，不要与简单 [[预测基准|benchmark]] 混为一谈。不同的 $G$ 代表不同的取舍：保留哪些节点、怎样依据误差精度重新分配差额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reconciliation maps independently generated node forecasts to $\tilde y_h=SG\hat y_h$, guaranteeing coherence. Here “base” means unreconciled, not a benchmark forecast. The mapping matrix records which node forecasts are retained and how discrepancies are redistributed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若简单取 $W=I$ 做 OLS projection，上述 110、60、42 在
$$
S=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}
$$
下被协调为约 $107.33,62.67,44.67$。总量向下移动 $2.67$，两个地区各向上移动 $2.67$，最后 $62.67+44.67=107.34$（差异来自四舍五入）。它既没有强迫地区去凑原总量，也没有简单接受地区和，而是在假设各节点误差尺度相同的度量下寻找最近的相干向量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under identity-weighted OLS reconciliation, the forecasts 110, 60, and 42 become approximately 107.33, 62.67, and 44.67. The method neither forces regional forecasts to match the original total nor simply accepts their sum; it finds the nearest coherent vector under an equal-error-scale geometry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 单层方法的直觉与代价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. The intuition and cost of single-level methods*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[单层协调方法]]包括：bottom-up 只预测底层再向上加总；top-down 只预测总量再按比例向下分；middle-out 从选定中层向两边展开。它们都能保证相干，优点是规则清楚、需要估计的对象少；共同代价是其他层级的 base forecasts 全被舍弃。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bottom-up, top-down, and middle-out all guarantee coherence through a clearly chosen level. Their simplicity reduces estimation burden, but base forecasts from every other level are discarded.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

bottom-up 在底层数据丰富且局部模式重要时自然，却会受稀疏和噪声影响；top-down 能利用较平滑的总量，却依赖分摊比例，容易错过新产品或地区结构变化；middle-out 只是把这一取舍移到中间。方法选择不能只看组织图，而要在真实 origins 上逐层、逐 horizon 比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bottom-up preserves local signals but can suffer from sparse and noisy bottom series. Top-down exploits a smoother aggregate but relies on allocation proportions that can miss local structural change. Middle-out relocates the trade-off. Validation must therefore be by level and horizon, not by organisational intuition alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8. MinT：在相干空间中利用全部节点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. MinT: use forecasts from all nodes within the coherent space*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[MinT协调]]令 $W_h=\operatorname{Var}(y_{T+h}-\hat y_h)$，在线性协调和无偏保持条件下选择
$$
\tilde y_h
=S(S'W_h^{-1}S)^{-1}S'W_h^{-1}\hat y_h.
$$
其目标是最小化协调误差协方差的 trace，也就是全部节点误差方差之和。误差较小的节点通常更少被移动；高度相关的节点不会被当成多份独立证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
MinT uses the base-forecast error covariance and, under linear coherence and unbiasedness preservation, minimises the trace of the reconciled-error covariance. More precise nodes tend to move less, while highly correlated errors are not counted as independent evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $M=S(S'W_h^{-1}S)^{-1}S'W_h^{-1}$、$V_h=MW_hM'$。在 base forecasts 无偏且 $W_h$ 为真实正定协方差的 oracle 条件下，
$$
W_h-V_h=(I-M)W_h(I-M)'\succeq0.
$$
所以每个节点的总体误差方差都不高于其 base forecast；MinT 也在同一线性无偏类中最小化任意固定正定加权的期望二次损失。trace 是这个方差支配结果的汇总，不是靠牺牲某个节点的总体方差换取全局改善。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $M=S(S'W_h^{-1}S)^{-1}S'W_h^{-1}$ and $V_h=MW_hM'$. With unbiased base forecasts and the true positive-definite covariance, $W_h-V_h=(I-M)W_h(I-M)'\succeq0$. Thus every node's population error variance is no larger than its base variance, and the same linear-unbiased oracle solution minimises expected quadratic loss under any fixed positive-definite weighting matrix. Trace summarises this covariance dominance; it is not obtained by sacrificing one node's population variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总体保证不等于每次误差都改善。对 $T=A+B$、$W_h=I$，oracle 映射为 $M=I-qq'/3$，其中 $q=(1,-1,-1)'$；三个节点的总体方差都从 $1$ 降到 $2/3$。但某次 base error 若为 $(0,1,1)'$，协调后变成 $(2/3,1/3,1/3)'$：总量在这一次从完全正确变成有误。现实中真实 $W_h$ 又未知，[[MinT协方差边界]]因此是方法的一部分；节点数相对 origins 很大时，完整样本协方差可能奇异或主要由噪声决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Population dominance does not mean every realised error improves. For $T=A+B$ and $W_h=I$, the oracle map is $M=I-qq'/3$ with $q=(1,-1,-1)'$, so every population variance falls from 1 to $2/3$. Yet a realised base error $(0,1,1)'$ becomes $(2/3,1/3,1/3)'$: the total moves from exactly right to wrong on that case. In practice the true $W_h$ is unknown, so covariance estimation is part of the method and may dominate performance when nodes outnumber reliable origins.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实践中应并列检验 $W_h\propto I$ 的 OLS、只保留各节点方差的 scaling、只依赖 $S$ 的 structural scaling，以及保留部分误差相关的 shrinkage MinT。样本短时，更简单的近似可能因为估计误差小而在外样本胜出。所有方案必须连同 base models 和估计窗口一起在 rolling origins 中重放。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Practical candidates include identity-weighted OLS, variance scaling, structure-only scaling, and shrinkage MinT. A simpler approximation may win out of sample because it estimates fewer unstable quantities. The complete pipeline, including base models and covariance window, must be replayed at each origin.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 9. 相干只是约束，不是成绩单
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9. Coherence is a constraint, not a performance report*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[相干不等于准确]]：把一组全部高估 20% 的底层预测向上相加，仍会得到完美相干的总量；协调不会从错误模型中创造信号。反过来，总量 base forecast 在某一次实现上可能恰好完全准确，却因满足共同约束而被移动。这不否定 oracle MinT 的逐节点总体方差保证，只说明单次实现和总体风险是两个问题。只展示“数字终于相加”没有提供任何外样本证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Coherence is not accuracy. Aggregating bottom forecasts that are all twenty percent too high still produces a perfectly coherent total. Conversely, an aggregate base forecast that happens to be exact on one realisation may move to satisfy the common constraint. This does not contradict oracle MinT's nodewise population-variance guarantee; realised error and population risk are different claims. Arithmetic consistency is not out-of-sample evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

验收至少要按层级与 horizon 并列 base 和 reconciled losses，并检查偏差、区间覆盖或分布评分。若估计后的现实 pipeline 中公司总量改善但关键地区恶化，应检查协方差估计、偏差、损失错位或额外约束，并把结果如实呈现；不能用 oracle 标签或平均 RMSE 掩盖它。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Acceptance compares base and reconciled losses by level and horizon, including bias and distributional calibration where relevant. If an estimated production pipeline improves the total while a critical region deteriorates, diagnose covariance estimation, bias, loss mismatch, or added constraints and report the result rather than hiding it behind an oracle label or pooled RMSE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 10. 情景回答“如果”，不是替概率分布起名字
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*10. Scenarios answer “what if”; they are not renamed probability distributions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当新产品可能按期或延期上市，直接代入一个“最可能日期”会掩盖决策分叉。[[情景预测]]分别写出假设：上市日期、初始覆盖门店、替代旧产品比例、促销强度和供应限制，再将每条内部一致的路径输入模型。输出回答“若这条路径成立，销量怎样变化”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a launch may be on time or delayed, one “most likely date” can conceal the decision fork. Scenario forecasting declares the launch date, store coverage, substitution, promotion, and supply constraints for each coherent path, then asks what follows under that path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若动态回归在给定 predictor 路径后产生区间，该区间通常只包含结果 innovation 等条件不确定性，并不自动包含“最终走哪条情景”的不确定性。必须把假设路径与区间一起交付，不能只留下阴影带。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An interval generated after fixing a predictor path usually represents conditional outcome uncertainty, not uncertainty about which scenario will occur. The path assumptions must travel with the interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[情景与概率预测]]的分界很严格。低、中、高三个情景若没有互斥、穷尽的状态定义和可信概率，就不能直接当分布，更不能把三者平均称为期望值。只有另有 $p_s=P(S=s\mid\mathcal F_T)$ 和每个情景的条件分布 $F_s$ 时，才可写
$$
F(y\mid\mathcal F_T)=\sum_s p_sF_s(y).
$$
极端情景也不自动对应 95% 或 99% 分位数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Low, central, and high scenarios do not form a predictive distribution without mutually exclusive and exhaustive states and defensible probabilities. Only then can their conditional distributions be mixed by the law of total probability. An extreme scenario is not automatically a named quantile.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 11. 判断调整只接受真正的新信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*11. Judgmental adjustment admits genuinely new information only*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[判断调整]]从统计基线 $\hat y^{stat}$ 出发，把模型尚未见到的及时信息写成显式修正 $a$：
$$
\hat y^{adj}=\hat y^{stat}+a.
$$
刚确认的促销取消、政策落地或大型活动可以构成理由；“我觉得曲线不好看”或对普通噪声的重新解读不构成理由。反复出现的系统性错误应进入数据、模型或 calibration，而不是长期靠人补丁。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Judgmental adjustment adds an explicit modification to a statistical baseline using timely information absent from the model. A confirmed cancellation or policy change may qualify; discomfort with ordinary noise does not. Recurring error belongs in data, model, or calibration changes rather than permanent manual patching.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要分开 forecast 与 target。因缺货成本高而提高备货量，是在预测之后应用不对称决策损失；为了激励团队而提高销量目标，是管理选择。两者都不应被伪装成关于未来中心位置的新预测信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Forecasts must remain separate from targets and actions. Raising stock because shortage costs are asymmetric is a decision based on the forecast; raising a sales target is a management choice. Neither is new evidence about the centre of the future outcome distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[判断调整审计]]要求同时保留未调整基线、调整后数值、信息内容及可得时间、提出者、horizon 和成熟 outcome。用同一损失比较
$$
\Delta L=L(y,\hat y^{stat})-L(y,\hat y^{adj})
$$
才能知道人是否增值。若某类新信息持续有效，应把它升级成结构化 predictor、干预或情景；若持续无效，就应收紧 override。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Auditing retains the original baseline, adjusted value, information and availability time, responsible forecaster, horizon, and mature outcome. Comparing both versions under the same loss reveals whether judgment added value. Repeatedly useful information can then become a structured predictor, intervention, or scenario.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 12. 把三部分接成可部署流程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*12. Join the three parts into a deployable process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条可执行的端到端顺序是：

1. **定义任务。** 固定 target、origin、horizons、信息集、输出分布和决策损失。
2. **生成成分预测。** 每个模型在相同历史起点重做拟合、选择和 predictor 生成。
3. **组合模型。** 先保留等权基准，再只用 validation errors 估计候选权重。
4. **表达聚合结构。** 用有效的 $S$ 固定所有层级或分组恒等式。
5. **协调预测。** 比较单层方法、OLS/scaling 与 shrinkage MinT，并按层级和 horizon 评价。
6. **展开情景。** 对尚未确定但决策重要的驱动保留具名、内部一致的条件路径。
7. **受控判断。** 只有模型外新信息才能调整基线，并同时保存未调整版本。
8. **最终验收。** 在 untouched holdout 上比较单模型、组合、协调及必要的条件输出；部署后等 outcome 成熟再监控。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The end-to-end process defines the task, regenerates component forecasts at common origins, validates combinations against equal weighting, records the aggregation matrix, compares reconciliation methods by level and horizon, retains explicit scenarios for unresolved drivers, governs judgmental overrides against an untouched baseline, and performs final evaluation only after the entire pipeline is frozen.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

组合与协调作用在不同维度：前者在同一序列的多个模型之间聚合，后者在多个相关序列的加总结构内投影。现实 pipeline 可以先在每个节点组合后统一协调，也可以先协调每套模型再组合；不要凭代数直觉假定二者在所有权重和协方差设定下等价。应固定一种可重放顺序，并把整条 pipeline 作为评价对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Combination operates across models for one series, while reconciliation operates across related series in an aggregation structure. A production pipeline may combine within nodes before reconciliation or reconcile each model before combination. These operations need not be equivalent under arbitrary weights and covariance estimates, so one reproducible order must be fixed and validated end to end.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 章节自检
> 公司有三套模型、四个地区与一个总量，并考虑“新品按期/延期”两个未来。请分别指出哪一部分由组合、协调和情景承担。
>
> **答案：** 组合处理每个目标上三套模型的分歧；协调使地区与总量满足加总结构；情景保留新品时点不同的两套条件未来。若人再依据刚确认的模型外信息改数，还要留下统计基线并进入判断调整审计。
