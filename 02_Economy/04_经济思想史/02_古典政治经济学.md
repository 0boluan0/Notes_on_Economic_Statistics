# 古典政治经济学
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Classical political economy: Smith and Ricardo*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一座制针工场为什么能用同样人数生产更多？商品售价怎样支持下一轮生产？如果食物越来越难取得，增加的产出又会怎样分给工人、资本所有者和地主？Smith 与 Ricardo 都研究商业社会的财富和分配，但他们提出问题的顺序、使用的概念和依赖的条件并不完全相同。本章沿着这些问题阅读两人的具体论证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why can a pin factory produce more with the same workforce? How do selling prices sustain further production? If food becomes harder to obtain, how is output divided among workers, capital owners and landlords? Smith and Ricardo both study wealth and distribution in commercial society, but their questions, concepts and assumptions differ. This chapter follows their arguments through those questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从这里向下连续读，必需的解释和例子已经嵌入。[[古典政治经济学.canvas|打开全局关系图]]；[[02_Economy/04_经济思想史/00_课程总览|返回经济思想史总览]]。前一主题 [[重商主义与重农主义.canvas|重商主义与重农主义]] 讨论金属、贸易和农业净产品；这里把注意力转向一般生产、价值与收入分配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read straight through the embedded explanations and examples. The preceding topic examined specie, trade and agricultural net product; this chapter turns to production more generally, value and the distribution of income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 分工为何增加产出，又靠什么维持
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. How division of labour raises output and what sustains it*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Smith 的起点很具体：观察同一项生产在不同工作安排下的结果。先弄清分工改变了什么，再判断它是否提高生产率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith begins with a concrete comparison of production under different work arrangements. First identify what division of labour changes, then ask whether and how it raises productivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[分工]]
![[分工增产机制]]

但“做得更快”还没有解释一个专业职业怎样养活自己。制钉匠需要足够买者，也需要在货款到手以前吃饭、取得材料和工具。下面两张卡分别处理销售范围和生产期间的资源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Producing faster does not explain how a specialist makes a living. A nail maker needs enough buyers and provisions, materials and tools before receiving sales proceeds. The next two explanations separate market access from resources during production.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[分工的市场限制]]
![[分工的预付条件]]

接下来要问，什么样的劳动和收入用途能够补回这些预付？Smith 的“生产性”是一个历史分析口径。重农主义也使用生产与不生产的分类，但两者的边界不同，不能因为字面相似就沿用同一判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
What kinds of labour and uses of income replenish those advances? Smith's category of productivity belongs to a particular historical analysis. Physiocracy also classified activity as productive or unproductive, but its boundary differs; similar wording does not make the classifications interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Smith生产性劳动]]
![[Smith积累机制]]

这条链解释了产出可能怎样增加，却没有穷尽对劳动者的影响。同一个人更擅长一种动作，并不等于更善于处理陌生问题。读完下面的能力后果，再看本章第3节的公共教育与政府职责，就能理解 Smith 为什么同时讨论市场收益和公共责任。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The chain explains possible output gains but not every consequence for workers. Greater skill at one operation need not improve judgment in unfamiliar situations. The capability consequences below connect to public education and government duties in Section 3, showing why Smith discusses both market benefits and public responsibilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[分工的能力代价]]

## 2. 从“有什么用”到价格怎样支持生产
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. From usefulness to prices that sustain production*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果水对生活如此重要，为什么它的交换价格可能低于钻石？这个问题首先要求分清我们在用“价值”说什么，随后才能比较劳动、成本和后来边际主义的解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If water is so important to life, why might it exchange for less than diamonds? The question first requires distinguishing meanings of value before comparing explanations based on labour, costs and later marginalism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Smith的价值区分]]
![[劳动支配与劳动投入]]

劳动可以用来描述商品的交换能力，也可以是生产投入。进入有资本和土地所有权的商业社会后，Smith 还要解释工资、利润和地租怎样得到支付。自然价格回答的是维持生产与运送所需的通常报酬，市场价格则是实际成交价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Labour can describe a commodity's purchasing power or an input into its production. In commercial society with capital and land ownership, Smith also asks how wages, profits and rent are paid. Natural price concerns the usual remuneration supporting production and delivery; market price is the actual selling price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Smith自然价格]]

“需求大于供给”中的需求又是什么意思？有人强烈想要一件物品，和有人能按支持生产的价格买下它，不是同一件事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
What does demand mean when supply is insufficient? Strongly wanting an item differs from being able to buy it at a price that supports its production.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Smith有效需求]]
![[自然价格的趋向机制]]

趋向过程依靠有人能够进入、扩产、退出或转移资源。因此不能只记住“价格会回去”：还要检查这些调整是否实际可行，以及自然价格本身是否已经改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adjustment depends on the ability to enter, expand, exit or move resources. The claim is not simply that prices return: check whether those responses are feasible and whether natural price itself has changed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[垄断与自然价格]]

## 3. 私人动机、公共结果与制度责任
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Private motives, public outcomes and institutional responsibilities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面已见到两种不同后果：追逐报酬可以引导资源流动，阻挡竞争也可能维持高价。带着这一区别再读“看不见的手”，才能把一句著名表述放回它实际回答的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding analysis shows different outcomes: seeking returns can redirect resources, while barriers to competition can sustain high prices. With that distinction in mind, read the invisible-hand passage in relation to the question it addresses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[看不见的手的原语境]]
![[Smith公共职责]]

道路让较远的买者进入交换范围，教育回应狭窄工作的能力代价。这些关系使政府职责和分工分析接在一起；具体怎样筹资、维护和提供服务，还需要另外判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Roads can connect distant buyers to markets; education can address the capability costs of narrow work. These links connect public duties to the division-of-labour analysis, while leaving financing, maintenance and provision as further questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. Ricardo：从土地差异进入分配问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Ricardo: from land differences to distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Smith 的自然价格按通常率计入工资、利润和地租。Ricardo 转而追问，土地差异怎样使某些所有者得到收益，决定新增粮食生产的条件又是什么。两种论述不能仅凭收入账目相似就拼成一条相同的因果理论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith's natural price accounts for wages, profit and rent at their usual rates. Ricardo investigates how land differences generate income for particular owners and which conditions govern additional food production. Similar income accounts do not make their causal explanations identical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[级差地租]]
![[土地租金与改良回报]]

新增食物不一定来自另一块较差的土地；同一块地追加相等资本，也可能得到不同产出。这个区别决定我们应当在哪里寻找“最后一笔必要投入”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Additional food need not come from a different, poorer plot. Equal additional investments on the same land can also yield different outputs. This distinction determines where to look for the last necessary increment of investment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[土地的集约边际]]

现在用一个明确给定成本和容量的粮食市场检验“边际”究竟意味着什么。这个现代构造保持无租成本不变，帮助分清一类土地部分启用和全部满产的不同情形；它不把 Ricardo 的全部分配理论塞进一条阶梯供给线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a food market with specified costs and capacities to test what a margin means. This modern construction holds non-rent costs fixed and distinguishes a partly used land class from fully utilised capacity. It is an aid to understanding margins, not a representation of Ricardo's entire distribution theory as a stepped supply curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[有限容量供给画法]]
![[边际土地零租条件]]

知道无租边际所需的条件后，才能理解 Ricardo 的因果判断：如果生产技术、必要的边际投入和需求没有改变，只让地主放弃租金，会不会降低生产粮食所需的劳动？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the conditions for a no-rent margin are clear, consider Ricardo's causal question. If technology, the necessary marginal investment and demand are unchanged, does a landlord's renunciation of rent reduce the labour required to produce food?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[地租与粮价因果]]

## 5. 生活资料、工资和利润率怎样相连
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Connecting necessities, wages and profit rates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

土地分析让“生活资料更难生产”有了具体含义。接下来要先定义工资的维持标准，再讨论市场工资怎样变化，最后才问它对利润和积累有什么影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The land analysis gives concrete meaning to necessities becoming harder to produce. Next define the maintenance standard for wages, examine market-wage adjustment, and then consider profits and accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[李嘉图自然工资]]
![[工资与人口调整]]

人口课程用工资—劳动图把这套反馈画出来。如果正在复习那门课，可读 [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/02_人口增长与经济发展#3.2 模型内容|工资—劳动图的连续推导]]：固定与上升的维持工资使用同一生产函数作比较，原课堂图和数例放在一起。这里继续看分配论本身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The population course illustrates the feedback with wage–labour diagrams. Its [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/02_人口增长与经济发展#3.2 模型内容|continuous diagram derivation]] compares fixed and rising maintenance wages using one production function, alongside the original classroom figures. Here, continue with distribution theory itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[工资利润的分配约束]]

“工资上涨”可能指劳动者生活改善，也可能只是货币单位改变。要让工资成本挤压利润的判断成立，必须说明产出、销售收入、其他成本和资本价值中哪些保持不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A wage increase may reflect improved living standards or merely a change in the monetary unit. A claim that wages compress profits must specify what happens to output, sales revenue, other costs and capital valuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[粮价与货币贬值]]
![[利润率与积累压力]]

从这里得到的是带有农业技术、食品来源、工资和投资条件的增长压力。利润率下降与利润总额下降也不是一回事。下面转向贸易，问它能否改变取得生活资料的成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The resulting accumulation pressure depends on agricultural technology, food supplies, wages and investment. Falling profit rates also differ from falling total profits. Trade raises the next question: can it change the cost of obtaining necessities?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 贸易如何改变选择和分配
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. How trade changes opportunities and distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一国即使生产两种商品都更快，也可能通过交换取得更多商品；判断的关键是相对机会成本。先读已经在发展经济学中使用的同一组定义与数例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A country can gain through exchange even if it produces both goods more efficiently; relative opportunity costs determine the relevant advantage. Begin with the same definitions and numerical example used in development economics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[比较优势]]
![[贸易收益与分配]]

比较优势说明为什么交换可能有总体收益，却没有直接说明 Ricardo 的一般利润率为什么会提高。若进口的是劳动者必需品，还需要经过生活资料价格和工资成本这条具体渠道；只让奢侈品更便宜，不会自动产生同一种机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Comparative advantage explains possible aggregate gains from exchange, not by itself why Ricardo's general profit rate rises. Imports of workers' necessities must operate through the specific channel of living costs and wages. Cheaper luxuries do not automatically activate the same mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[必需品降价与利润]]

## 7. 把整章重新讲出来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Reconstruct the chapter's argument*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一座扩张中的工场作线索：先解释任务细分怎样提高产出，再说明销售范围和预付资源的限制；接着解释价格如何支付通常报酬、竞争调整在哪一步可能受阻。最后加入食物生产越来越困难的条件，分别追问地主、劳动者和资本的收入如何变化，以及技术和贸易可能改变哪一环。每一个箭头都应说得出条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use an expanding workshop to reconstruct the argument. Explain how specialised tasks raise output, the market and advance-provision constraints, and how prices cover usual remuneration. Identify where competitive adjustment can be obstructed. Then introduce increasingly difficult food production and trace consequences for landlords, workers and capital, specifying where technology or trade can change the chain. State the condition behind each causal step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完成后，用 [[古典政治经济学.canvas|关系图]] 检查有没有把价格定义当成收敛保证、把全部农场租金当成级差租、把最高成本在产地直接当成零租边际。能解释这些区别，再回到课程总览继续读 Marx 对劳动、剩余和资本积累的不同处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the relationship map to check the distinctions between a price definition and guaranteed convergence, total farm payments and differential rent, and the highest-cost active plot and a no-rent margin. Then return to the course overview for Marx's different treatment of labour, surplus and accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
