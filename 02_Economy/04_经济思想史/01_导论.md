# 研究基本方法论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basic Research Methodology*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

学习经济思想史，不是要故意掉书袋，而是要从前述三个原则来从整体上把握经济思想史发展中的流变及其相应的意蕴：
1. 为什么某个思想会出现在某个历史时代？
2. 这个思想的主轴是什么？
3. 它对谁有利？对谁有害？
4. 这个思想为什么又会被历史所淘汰？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Studying the history of economic thought is not an exercise in displaying erudition. It is a way to understand, as a whole, how economic ideas changed over time and what those changes meant, by asking:
1. Why did a particular idea emerge in a particular historical period?
2. What is the central organising principle of that idea?
3. Who benefits from it, and who is harmed by it?
4. Why was the idea eventually displaced by history?
