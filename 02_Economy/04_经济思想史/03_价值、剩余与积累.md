# 价值、剩余与积累

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Value, surplus and accumulation in Marx*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果资本家按价值买进生产资料，也按价值支付劳动力，最后为什么还能得到剩余？Marx 把问题从买卖转入生产：先区分劳动能力与它的实际使用，再追问新价值怎样形成、怎样分配，以及剩余怎样进入下一轮生产。本章沿着这条论证阅读，最后接到资本构成、就业和利润率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If capitalists purchase means of production at their values and also pay the value of labour-power, how can a surplus remain? Marx moves from exchange into production. He distinguishes the capacity to work from its actual use, then asks how new value is created and divided, and how surplus enters the next round of production. This chapter follows that argument through to capital composition, employment and the profit rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全局关系见 [[价值、剩余与积累.canvas|主题图]]；发展经济学的公式与原始课堂记录见 [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/06_经济增长理论#1. 马克思的资本积累模型|资本积累模型这一节]]。下面嵌入的都是共享原子，两门课使用同一对象。

## 1. 商品中究竟在比较什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. What is being compared in commodities?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确定对象。一件东西有用，不表示它已经具有商品形式；有用的方式，也不是它交换时所表现的数量关系。Marx 从商品出发，把这些问题逐一分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with the object. Being useful does not by itself make something a commodity, and the way it is useful is different from the quantitative relation in which it exchanges. Marx separates these questions through his analysis of the commodity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Marx商品]]

![[Marx使用价值]]

有用性回答“拿来做什么”。价值分析接着问：不同用途的劳动产品，在交换中为什么被放进同一种数量比较？这里需要的是 Marx 自己的价值概念。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Usefulness answers what an object can do. Value analysis then asks why products serving different purposes enter a common quantitative comparison in exchange. The next step requires Marx's particular concept of value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Marx商品价值]]

![[Marx交换价值]]

价值与交换价值分别指被表达的内容与表达它的形式。读到某个交换比例时，先说明两端是什么、表达谁的价值；后面分析生产中的价值形成，不能把任何观察到的成交价直接当作定义本身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Value and exchange-value refer to the content expressed and its form of expression. For an exchange relation, identify its two sides and whose value is being expressed. The later analysis of value formation in production cannot simply substitute any observed transaction price for the definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 同一劳动的两个方面

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Two aspects of the same labour*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

裁衣与织布产生不同产品，也使用不同工具和操作。若把它们作为形成价值的劳动来比较，又需要另一层规定。这不是让工人在工作日中先做一种劳动、再做另一种劳动，而是对同一活动问两个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tailoring and weaving produce different things through different tools and operations. Comparing them as value-forming labour requires a different determination. The worker does not perform one kind first and the other later in the day: these are two questions about the same activity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[具体劳动]]

![[抽象劳动]]

即使已经抽去具体形式，也不能把每个人实际耗费的时间原样加到商品价值上。慢工多花的时间，还要经过社会正常生产条件这一尺度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even after abstracting from concrete forms, one cannot count every person's actual hours directly as commodity value. Extra hours spent by a slower producer must still be judged against normal social production conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[社会必要劳动时间]]

这使我们能够分清“同样时间生产更多物品”与“同样时间创造更多价值”。生产技术改变时，应先查单位产品需要的社会劳动如何变化，再判断价值量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This distinguishes producing more objects in the same time from creating more value in that time. When technique changes, first examine the social labour required per unit, then assess the change in value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[生产率与单位价值]]

## 3. 资本购买的是劳动能力

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Capital purchases a capacity to work*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

卖掉自己做的鞋去买食物，与预付货币并以更多货币回流为目的，属于不同的运动。先看资本的定义，再找它能够购买的特殊商品。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Selling shoes one has made in order to buy food differs from advancing money with the aim of receiving more money back. Begin with capital's definition, then identify the distinctive commodity it can purchase.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Marx资本]]

![[Marx劳动力]]

劳动能力并非在任何社会条件下都会成为可以购买的商品。劳动力市场的成立还涉及劳动者能否支配自身能力，以及能否凭自己的生产条件独立生活。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The capacity to work does not become a purchasable commodity under every social arrangement. Its sale also depends on workers' control over their own capacities and their access to the conditions for independent production and subsistence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[劳动力商品条件]]

现在分别看两个量：再生产一天劳动力需要多少价值，以及使用它一天可以形成多少新价值。前一个量先由生活资料、训练及相应的社会条件说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We can now distinguish two magnitudes: the value required to reproduce a day's labour-power and the new value its use can form during a day. The first is explained through subsistence, training and the relevant social conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[劳动力价值]]

## 4. 旧价值的转移与新价值的形成

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Transferring old value and creating new value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

进入生产后，机器、原料和劳动力的价值作用不同。分清这两类资本，才能读懂产品价值中的各个部分；当天的产品也不会自动承载一台仍能继续使用的机器的全部价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In production, machinery, materials and labour-power play different roles in value formation. Distinguishing these parts of capital makes the components of product value intelligible. A day's product does not automatically contain the entire value of a machine that remains available for further use.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[不变资本]]

![[可变资本]]

![[产品价值分解]]

下面回到工作日：活劳动创造的新价值中，一部分相当于劳动力自身的价值，超过这部分的劳动形成剩余。原料与机器转来的旧价值，不在这两个时间部分之间重新分配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return now to the working day. Part of the new value formed by living labour is equivalent to the value of labour-power; labour beyond that part forms a surplus. Old value transferred from materials and machinery is not redistributed between these two portions of working time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[必要劳动]]

![[剩余劳动]]

“必要”在这里出现了两次：一种时间给商品价值定量，另一种时间划分工作日。把对象说清，才能将它们通过劳动力价值连接起来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The word “necessary” now appears in two contexts: one measure determines a commodity's value, while the other divides the working day. Identifying the object of each lets us connect them through the value of labour-power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[两种必要劳动时间]]

![[剩余价值]]

剩余价值是一个量；要问它相对于购买劳动力的资本有多大，就需要确定分母。工作日总长和全部资本投入分别回答别的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Surplus-value is a magnitude. To measure it relative to the capital purchasing labour-power, the denominator must be specified. The total working day and the entire capital advanced answer different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[剩余价值率]]

这些定义已经够用来回答开头的问题。按顺序追踪购买、生产和出售：价值增殖发生在哪一步，剩余又在哪一步实现？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These definitions are enough to return to the opening question. Follow purchase, production and sale in order: at which step does valorisation occur, and at which is the surplus realised?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[等价交换与剩余]]

## 5. 怎样改变工作日中的剩余部分

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Changing the surplus portion of the day*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以八小时中必要劳动四小时为共同起点。第一种比较保持必要部分，延长工作日；第二种保持工作日，研究劳动力再生产需要的价值如何下降。每次只沿已说明的条件改变变量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use an eight-hour day with four necessary hours as the common starting point. The first comparison holds the necessary portion fixed and extends the day. The second holds the day fixed and examines a reduction in the value required to reproduce labour-power. Each comparison changes variables under explicitly stated conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[绝对剩余价值生产]]

![[相对剩余价值生产]]

## 6. 剩余怎样变成下一轮资本

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Turning surplus into the next round of capital*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

生产出剩余与把剩余用于积累是两步。原预付要先补回；剩余中用于消费的部分与作为追加资本使用的部分，还需要区分。货币安排又必须能够对应真实的生产资料和劳动条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Producing surplus and using it for accumulation are separate steps. The original advance must first be replaced. The portions of surplus devoted to consumption and to additional capital must then be distinguished. Monetary arrangements also have to correspond to actual means of production and conditions of labour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Marx资本积累]]

![[积累的物质条件]]

继续扩张时，不只要看资本总额变多了多少，还要看生产资料与劳动的关系怎样改变。这种关系可以从实物或价值两面来量；“有机构成”则指其中有特定联系的一层。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expansion raises a further question: not only how much total capital grows, but how the relation between means of production and labour changes. This relation can be considered in physical or value terms. Organic composition refers to a particular connection between those dimensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[资本技术构成]]

![[资本价值构成]]

![[资本有机构成]]

## 7. 资本增长与劳动需求并非同一个量

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Capital growth and labour demand are different magnitudes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本中用于劳动力的比重下降，与用于劳动力的绝对量下降，不是一回事。把工资、工时和周转条件固定后，才可以继续把资本量换算为用工量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A smaller share of capital devoted to labour-power is not the same as a smaller absolute amount. Relating that amount to employment also requires specifying wages, hours and turnover conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[积累与劳动需求]]

劳动需求随积累、技术和生产周期发生变化，就有了“相对谁而言过剩”的问题。产业后备军的定义需要说明这种关系，不能只贴一个失业人数标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As accumulation, technique and the production cycle change labour demand, the question becomes: surplus relative to what? Defining the industrial reserve army requires specifying that relation rather than merely attaching an unemployment count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[产业后备军]]

## 8. 读利润率以前，先对齐资本的口径

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. Align the capital measures before reading the profit rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

机器与原料都属于不变资本，但它们的价值回到货币形态的方式可以不同。周转分类与价值增殖分类各有用途；把二者摆在一起，才能正确处理预付额与当期耗用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Machinery and materials both belong to constant capital, yet their values can return to money in different ways. Classification by turnover and classification by role in valorisation serve different purposes. Considering both lets us distinguish the advance from the amount consumed during a period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[固定与流动资本]]

![[两种资本分类]]

![[预付与耗用资本]]

现在才把利润放到资本分母上。先说明计算哪一期间、哪些预付额，以及是否暂取利润等于该资本自身生产的剩余价值；随后才使用简化等式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We can now relate profit to a capital denominator. Specify the period, the advances included, and whether profit is temporarily assumed equal to the surplus-value produced by that particular capital. Only then apply the simplified equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Marx利润率]]

## 9. 从条件比较到动态问题

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9. From conditional comparisons to dynamics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

发展经济学课堂把利润率写成 $P=x/(1+j)$。确定 $x$、$j$ 与计算期间以后，这个比率可用于比较；要让它描述实际变化，还须解释两个量为什么那样变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The development-economics lecture writes the profit rate as $P=x/(1+j)$. Once $x$, $j$ and the period are defined, the ratio supports a comparison. Describing actual change also requires an explanation of why those magnitudes move as assumed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[资本构成与利润率]]

![[利润率反作用机制]]

最后再把百分比换回绝对量。相同的比率下降，对不同资本规模会给出不同的利润额；能否继续积累，还取决于剩余如何使用及生产条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, translate percentages back into amounts. The same fall in a rate produces different profit magnitudes at different capital scales. Continued accumulation also depends on how surplus is used and on production conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[利润率与利润量]]

## 10. 自己重建一遍

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*10. Reconstruct the argument*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先合上卡片，用一个八小时工作日解释：购买的是什么，为什么可以按价值购买而仍有剩余，哪些价值是旧价值转移，哪些由当期活劳动形成。再用同一组符号算出剩余价值率与利润率，并注明资本分母和期间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Close the cards and use an eight-hour day to explain what is purchased, how purchase at value can coexist with surplus, which value is transferred, and which is newly formed by living labour. Then compute the rate of surplus-value and the profit rate in consistent notation, stating the capital denominator and period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着只改变一个条件：工作日延长、生活资料生产率提高、机器单位价值下降，或资本总额扩张。逐次说清哪个量直接变化、还需要哪些条件，最后判断原来的结论能否保持。能够完成这些转换，才说明你会使用这一套论证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next change one condition at a time: extend the day, raise productivity in subsistence production, cheapen machinery, or expand total capital. Identify what changes directly and what additional conditions are needed, then assess whether the earlier conclusion still holds. These transformations test whether you can use the argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
