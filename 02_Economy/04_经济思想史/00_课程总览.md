### 1. 经济思想史：重商主义到边际革命
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. History of Economic Thought: From Mercantilism to the Marginal Revolution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是本课程的默认起点；先用快速恢复建立对象、例子和问题感，再进入详细内容。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the default starting point. Use the quick-recovery section to establish the objects, examples, and motivating questions before moving into the details.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[重商主义与重农主义]]

重商主义与重农主义分别把繁荣重心放在贸易积累和农业剩余；古典政治经济学把问题扩展为一般的生产、价值、分配与增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mercantilism and physiocracy locate prosperity in trade accumulation and agricultural surplus, respectively. Classical political economy broadens the question to production, value, distribution, and growth in general.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Smith、Ricardo 与古典政治经济学]]

Marx 继承了古典学派对劳动、剩余和阶级分配的关注，但把它重构为剩余价值、剥削关系与资本积累的动态批判。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Marx inherits the classical concern with labour, surplus, and class distribution, but reconstructs it as a dynamic critique of surplus value, exploitative relations, and capital accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Marx 的价值、剩余与资本积累]]

边际革命把分析单位从阶级与生产关系转向稀缺条件下的个体边际选择；Marshall 再用时间、需求与成本把边际分析组织成局部均衡框架。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The marginal revolution shifts the unit of analysis from class and production relations to individual marginal choice under scarcity. Marshall then organises marginal analysis into a partial-equilibrium framework through time, demand, and cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
