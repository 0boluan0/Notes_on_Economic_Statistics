### 1. 重商主义与重农主义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Mercantilism and Physiocracy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是本课程的默认起点。先在 [[重商主义与重农主义.canvas|知识地图]] 中看两条并行问题链，再按下列顺序阅读原子；重商主义和重农主义不是同一理论的前后版本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the default starting point. First use the [[重商主义与重农主义.canvas|knowledge map]] to see two parallel problem chains, then read the atoms in the order below. Mercantilism and physiocracy are not successive versions of one theory.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先限定“重商主义”这个后设标签，避免把多个世纪、国家和作者伪造成同一个模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin by bounding the retrospective label “mercantilism,” so that writers from different centuries and polities are not turned into one model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[重商主义]]

在此边界内，再重建贸易、可跨境支付的金属、航运、税收与国家能力为何会在部分政策论述中相联。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within that boundary, reconstruct why trade, internationally acceptable specie, shipping, taxation, and state capacity could be linked in some policy arguments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[重商主义与国家能力]]

Hume 随后用一个会反过来改变顺差条件的价格调整，批评金属可以只向一国单向积累的设想。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hume then uses a price adjustment that changes the conditions sustaining a surplus to challenge the idea of one-way specie accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[价格—贵金属流动机制]]

重农主义走另一条路线：先精确定义农业年度再生产补足年度预付和原始预付的年度更新后留下的净产品。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Physiocracy takes a different route, beginning with the net product left after agricultural annual reproduction restores annual advances and renews original advances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[重农主义净产品]]

《经济表》把这项剩余放进三阶级的购买与支付，检查下一年的预付能否恢复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The *Tableau* places that surplus within purchases and payments among three classes and asks whether the next year's advances are restored.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Quesnay 经济表]]

先把《经济表》中的“不生产阶级”定义清楚：它参与生产和交换，但按重农主义的核算不留下净产品。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First define the Tableau's “sterile class”: it participates in production and exchange but leaves no net product under physiocratic accounting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[重农主义不生产阶级]]

再单独处理它的口径边界：“不生产”不等于没有产出，更不能直接翻译成“对现代 GDP 没有增加值贡献”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then treat its accounting boundary separately: “sterile” means neither no output nor zero contribution to modern value added or GDP.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[不生产阶级不等于无产出]]

税制路线还需要另一个独立前提：重农主义的“自然秩序”需要稳定法权保护人身、财产、耕作预付和交易连续性，不是政府完全消失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tax route needs another independent premise: physiocratic “natural order” uses stable law and rights to protect persons, property, cultivation advances, and continuity of exchange; it is not the disappearance of government.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[重农主义自然秩序]]

重农主义把这一秩序前提、只有农业产生净产品的剩余来源判断，以及间接税最终损害土地收入或耕作预付的归宿判断，合在一起推出直接地税。任一前提失效，原有推导都不再充分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Physiocracy combines that order, the claim that agriculture alone generates net product, and an incidence claim that indirect taxes ultimately reduce land revenue or cultivation advances to derive direct land taxation. If any premise fails, the original derivation is no longer sufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[单一地税的重农推导]]

这条推导的政策结论是单一地税：以土地净产品为税基，用一项直接税取代多重间接税。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The policy conclusion is a single land tax: one direct levy on net land product intended to replace multiple indirect taxes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[重农主义单一地税]]

两条路线留下共同的后续问题：财富不能只靠金属存量描述，生产性也不能不加论证地只归给农业。古典政治经济学把对象扩展到一般的生产、价值、分配与增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two routes leave shared downstream questions: wealth cannot be described only by a stock of specie, and productivity cannot be confined to agriculture without argument. Classical political economy broadens the object to production, value, distribution, and growth in general.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[02_Economy/04_经济思想史/02_古典政治经济学]]

Marx 继承了古典学派对劳动、剩余和阶级分配的关注，但把它重构为剩余价值、剥削关系与资本积累的动态批判。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Marx inherits the classical concern with labour, surplus, and class distribution, but reconstructs it as a dynamic critique of surplus value, exploitative relations, and capital accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[02_Economy/04_经济思想史/03_价值、剩余与积累]]

边际革命把稀缺约束下的个体边际选择推到价值分析中心，但三位奠基者并没有用同一个模型整齐取代生产与协调问题；Marshall 再用时间、需求与成本把边际分析组织成局部均衡框架。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The marginal revolution moved individual choice at the margin under scarcity to the centre of value analysis, but its three founders did not replace production and coordination questions with one identical model. Marshall then organised marginal analysis into a partial-equilibrium framework through time, demand, and cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把边际量与总量分开；这是理解价值悖论、消费配置和后续均衡框架的共同起点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin by separating marginal from total magnitudes. This is the common starting point for the paradox of value, consumer allocation, and the later equilibrium frameworks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[边际效用与总效用]]

![[边际效用与市场价格]]

随后区分三位边际主义奠基者，不把共享概念误写成同一套理论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next distinguish the three marginalist founders rather than treating a shared concept as one identical theory.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[边际革命的三条理论路径]]

![[等边际原则]]

从这里分出两种互补的问题尺度：Walras 联立多个市场，Marshall 有意识地隔离一个市场。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The route then splits into complementary scales of analysis: Walras links markets jointly, while Marshall deliberately isolates one market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Walras 一般均衡]]

![[Marshall 供求与调整期限]]

![[Marshall 局部均衡]]

最后用弹性表达局部响应，但始终保留价格点、期限和市场边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, elasticity expresses a local response while retaining its price point, horizon, and market boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[价格弹性的测量边界]]
