# 1. [[异方差与自相关#两类问题及后果|异方差]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Heteroskedasticity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1. 异方差的本质
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1. The nature of heteroskedasticity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

异方差指误差项方差不是常数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Heteroskedasticity means that the variance of the error term is not constant:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}(u_i\mid X_i)=\sigma_i^2
$$

而经典 OLS 假设要求：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
whereas the classical OLS assumption requires:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}(u_i\mid X_i)=\sigma^2
$$

>[!note] 核心判断
> 异方差问的是“误差波动大小是否随样本特征变化”，不是问误差均值是不是 0。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Heteroskedasticity asks whether the dispersion of the error varies with sample characteristics, not whether the error has mean zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 异方差的来源
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2. Sources of heteroskedasticity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.2.1. 经济理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2.1. Economic mechanisms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收入越高的家庭消费差异可能越大；企业规模越大，利润波动可能越大。这类现象会导致误差方差随解释变量变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consumption may be more dispersed among higher-income households, and profits may fluctuate more for larger firms. Such mechanisms make the error variance change with explanatory variables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 异方差对最小二乘估计量的影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Effects of heteroskedasticity on least squares*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在零条件均值仍成立时，异方差不会破坏 OLS 的无偏性和一致性，但会带来两个问题：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the zero conditional-mean assumption still holds, heteroskedasticity does not destroy OLS unbiasedness or consistency, but it creates two problems:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- OLS 不再是最有效的线性无偏估计量。
- 常规标准误错误，导致 t 检验、F 检验和置信区间不可靠。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- OLS is no longer the most efficient linear unbiased estimator.
- Conventional standard errors are wrong, making $t$ tests, $F$ tests, and confidence intervals unreliable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 这章最容易错的点
> 异方差通常先伤害“标准误和检验”，不一定直接伤害“系数是否一致”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Heteroskedasticity usually harms standard errors and tests first; it does not necessarily make the coefficient estimator inconsistent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. 异方差的检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Tests for heteroskedasticity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. 画图
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1. Graphical diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 检验方式 | 用什么判断 | 适合做什么 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 画残差图 | 残差散布是否随 $X$ 或拟合值变化 | 初步观察 |
| LM 检验 | $nR^2$ 是否显著 | 检验指定方差形式 |
| White 检验 | 残差平方是否被变量、平方项、交叉项解释 | 通用诊断 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Diagnostic | Criterion | Best use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Residual plot | Whether residual dispersion changes with $X$ or the fitted value | Initial inspection |
| LM test | Whether $nR^2$ is significant | Testing a specified variance form |
| White test | Whether squared residuals are explained by variables, squares, and interactions | General diagnostic |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把残差或残差平方与拟合值、解释变量画在一起。如果残差散布呈现“喇叭口”或系统变化，可能存在异方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Plot residuals or squared residuals against fitted values and explanatory variables. A funnel shape or another systematic change in dispersion may indicate heteroskedasticity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. 拉格朗日乘数检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2. Lagrange multiplier test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般思路：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The general procedure is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 先估计原模型，得到残差 $\hat u_i$。
2. 用 $\hat u_i^2$ 对可能解释方差变化的变量做辅助回归。
3. 计算 $LM=nR^2$。
4. 与 $\chi^2$ 分布比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Estimate the original model and obtain residuals $\hat u_i$.
2. Run an auxiliary regression of $\hat u_i^2$ on variables that may explain changes in the variance.
3. Calculate $LM=nR^2$.
4. Compare it with the $\chi^2$ distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.3. 怀特 White 检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3. White test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[异方差与自相关#异方差诊断|White 检验]] 不需要预先指定异方差形式。辅助回归通常包括解释变量、平方项和交叉项：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The White test does not require a variance form to be specified in advance. Its auxiliary regression usually includes regressors, their squares, and interaction terms:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat u_i^2=\delta_0+\delta_1X_{1i}+\delta_2X_{2i}
+\delta_3X_{1i}^2+\delta_4X_{2i}^2+\delta_5X_{1i}X_{2i}+v_i
$$

原假设是所有非截距项系数为 0。统计量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The null hypothesis is that every non-intercept coefficient is zero. The statistic is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
LM=nR^2\sim \chi^2(q)
$$

其中 $q$ 是辅助回归中非截距解释变量的个数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $q$ is the number of non-intercept regressors in the auxiliary regression.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] White 检验怎么读
> 看辅助回归的 $nR^2$ 是否显著。显著说明残差平方能被解释变量系统解释，也就是误差方差不是常数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Ask whether $nR^2$ from the auxiliary regression is significant. Significance means that the regressors systematically explain squared residuals, indicating a nonconstant error variance.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4. 异方差的补救方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Remedies for heteroskedasticity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. [[异方差与自相关#WLS 与 GLS|加权最小二乘估计 WLS]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1. Weighted least squares (WLS)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 方法 | 改什么 | 适用目标 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WLS | 系数估计方式 | 已知方差结构 |
| FGLS | 先估方差再加权 | 想提高效率 |
| White 稳健标准误 | 只改标准误 | 保留 OLS 系数并修正检验 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Method | What changes | Appropriate objective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WLS | Coefficient-estimation method | Variance structure is known |
| FGLS | Estimate the variance, then weight | Improve efficiency |
| White robust standard errors | Standard errors only | Retain OLS coefficients while repairing inference |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果已知误差方差为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the error variance is known to be:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}(u_i\mid X_i)=\sigma^2h_i
$$

则两边除以 $\sqrt{h_i}$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
divide both sides by $\sqrt{h_i}$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{Y_i}{\sqrt{h_i}}
=\beta_0\frac{1}{\sqrt{h_i}}
+\beta_1\frac{X_i}{\sqrt{h_i}}
+\frac{u_i}{\sqrt{h_i}}
$$

变换后的误差项同方差，可以用 OLS 估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The transformed error is homoskedastic, so the transformed model can be estimated by OLS.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2. 可行的加权最小二乘 FGLS
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2. Feasible generalized least squares (FGLS)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现实中 $h_i$ 通常未知，所以使用 [[异方差与自相关#WLS 与 GLS|FGLS]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In practice $h_i$ is usually unknown, which motivates FGLS.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.2.1. 第一阶段
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.1. First stage*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 对原模型做 OLS，得到残差 $\hat u_i$。
2. 设定方差模型，例如：

   $$
   \ln(\hat u_i^2)=\gamma_0+\gamma_1Z_{1i}+\cdots+\gamma_mZ_{mi}+v_i
   $$

3. 得到 $\widehat{\ln h_i}$，进而构造权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Estimate the original model by OLS and obtain residuals $\hat u_i$.
2. Specify a model for the variance, such as the one shown above.
3. Obtain $\widehat{\ln h_i}$ and use it to construct weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.2.2. 第二阶段
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.2. Second stage*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用估计出的权重对原模型加权，再进行 OLS。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weight the original model using the estimated weights, then run OLS on the transformed model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.3. 不补救，只修正检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3. Keep the estimator and repair inference only*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果研究重点是系数一致性，而不是提高效率，可以保留 OLS 系数，并使用 [[异方差与自相关#稳健标准误|White 稳健标准误]]。稳健标准误不改变系数，只修正常规标准误，使 t 检验和置信区间在异方差下更可靠。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the objective is consistent coefficient estimation rather than greater efficiency, retain the OLS coefficients and use White heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors. Robust standard errors do not change the coefficients; they replace the conventional standard errors so that $t$ tests and confidence intervals remain more reliable under heteroskedasticity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] 两条补救路线
> WLS/FGLS 是改变估计方式；White 稳健标准误是不改系数、只修正检验。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> WLS and FGLS change the estimation method. White robust standard errors leave the coefficients unchanged and repair inference only.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
