# 1. 数据平稳性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Stationarity of the data*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一章的顺序不能倒过来：先区分确定性趋势与随机趋势，再判断最小差分阶数，然后选择与确定性项和短期动态匹配的检验，最后才决定去趋势、差分或保留协整关系。主题的全局关系见 [[趋势、单位根与差分.canvas|趋势、单位根与差分主题图]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The order matters: first distinguish deterministic from stochastic trend, then determine the minimum integration order, choose tests that match the deterministic terms and short-run dynamics, and only then decide whether to detrend, difference, or preserve a cointegrating relationship. See the [[趋势、单位根与差分.canvas|topic Canvas]] for the global structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参见：[[宽平稳定义|平稳随机过程]]、[[DF检验|单位根检验]]、[[ADF检验|ADF 检验步骤]]。

## 1.1. 协方差平稳
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1. Covariance stationarity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

协方差平稳要求：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Covariance stationarity requires:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. $E(y_t)$ 是有限常数。
2. $\operatorname{Var}(y_t)$ 是有限常数。
3. $\operatorname{Cov}(y_t,y_{t-k})$ 只取决于间隔 $k$，不取决于具体时间 $t$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** $E(y_t)$ is a finite constant.<br>
**2.** $\operatorname{Var}(y_t)$ is a finite constant.<br>
**3.** $\operatorname{Cov}(y_t,y_{t-k})$ depends only on the lag $k$, not on the calendar time $t$.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 稳定 ARMA 的冲击直觉
> 在稳定、因果的 ARMA 表示中，一次创新的脉冲响应会衰减；单位根则会让冲击永久进入水平路径。这是特定动态模型的结论，不是“协方差平稳”的定义；一般平稳过程的相关未必衰减。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a stable causal ARMA representation, the impulse response to one innovation decays, whereas a unit root leaves the shock permanently in the level path. This is a conclusion about a particular dynamic model, not the definition of covariance stationarity; a general stationary process need not have decaying correlation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 确定性趋势与随机趋势不是同一种“不平稳”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2. Deterministic and Stochastic Trends Are Different Forms of Nonstationarity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若
$$
y_t=m(t)+u_t,\qquad u_t\sim I(0),
$$
则 $y_t$ 是 [[趋势平稳定义|趋势平稳]] 的：长期路径 $m(t)$ 是非随机时间函数，普通冲击只进入 $u_t$，不会不断改写这条路径。估计 $m(t)$ 后去趋势，保留的是相对该路径的水平偏离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $y_t=m(t)+u_t$ with $u_t\sim I(0)$, the series is [[趋势平稳定义|trend-stationary]]. The long-run path is a nonrandom function of time; ordinary shocks enter $u_t$ rather than continually rewriting that path. Detrending retains level deviations relative to it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $(1-B)y_t=\epsilon_t$，路径则由创新逐期累积。这个 [[随机趋势]] 没有一条事先固定的曲线可减掉；一次冲击会永久进入以后水平。两类序列的图都可能向上，但 [[去趋势与差分]] 保存的信息完全不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $(1-B)y_t=\epsilon_t$, innovations accumulate into a [[随机趋势|stochastic trend]]. There is no prespecified curve that removes it, because each shock remains in later levels. Both mechanisms can produce an upward-looking plot, but [[去趋势与差分|detrending and differencing]] preserve different information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3. [[随机趋势|随机趋势：单位根过程]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3. Stochastic trend: a unit-root process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位根过程最简单形式是 [[无漂移随机游走]]：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The simplest unit-root process is a [[无漂移随机游走|driftless random walk]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_t=y_{t-1}+\epsilon_t,\qquad \epsilon_t\sim iid(0,\sigma^2)
$$

递归展开：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recursively expanding gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_t=y_0+\sum_{i=1}^t\epsilon_i
$$

冲击会永久进入序列，影响不会衰减，所以随机游走是非平稳的，记作 $y_t\sim I(1)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each shock enters the series permanently and never decays, so a random walk is nonstationary and is denoted $y_t\sim I(1)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

加上常数漂移得到 [[带漂移随机游走]]：
$$
y_t=\mu+y_{t-1}+\epsilon_t
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
y_t=y_0+\mu t+\sum_{i=1}^{t}\epsilon_i.
$$
$\mu t$ 是条件均值路径，累积创新仍是随机趋势，所以它并不因为“看起来沿直线上升”就成为趋势平稳。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding a constant drift gives a [[带漂移随机游走|random walk with drift]]. The term $\mu t$ is the conditional-mean path, but the cumulated innovations remain a stochastic trend, so a visually linear rise does not make the process trend-stationary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在鞅差、条件同方差与正交条件下，[[随机游走预测]] 为
$$
E_t(y_{t+h})=y_t+h\mu,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}_t(y_{t+h}-E_t y_{t+h})=h\sigma^2.
$$
点预测延续当前水平与漂移，预测标准差却按 $\sqrt h$ 增长；长期预测不会像稳定 AR 模型那样收敛到固定无条件均值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under martingale-difference, conditional-homoskedasticity, and orthogonality conditions, the [[随机游走预测|random-walk forecast]] extends the current level by its drift, while forecast-error variance grows with $h$ and its standard deviation with $\sqrt h$. Unlike a stable AR forecast, it does not converge to a fixed unconditional mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.4. 单整阶数与差分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.4. Integration Order and Differencing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[I(d)定义|$I(d)$]] 表示 $d$ 是使普通差分结果成为 $I(0)$ 的最小次数。若一次差分仍非 $I(0)$、二次差分才成为 $I(0)$，则原序列是 $I(2)$；不能因为研究者恰好差了两次就这样命名。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[I(d)定义|$I(d)$]] means that $d$ is the minimum number of ordinary differences required to obtain an $I(0)$ process. A series is $I(2)$ only when one difference is still non-$I(0)$ and two differences produce $I(0)$; the label does not follow merely from having applied two differences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个判断建立在 [[单位根最小表示|最小滞后多项式表示]] 上：AR 与 MA 的公共因子要先约去，剩余 AR 动态也必须稳定。普通差分 $(1-B)$ 针对零频根，[[普通与季节差分|季节差分 $(1-B^s)$]] 同时含多个季节单位圆根，不能把二者的次数混成一个 $d$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The diagnosis uses the [[单位根最小表示|minimal lag-polynomial representation]]: cancel common AR and MA factors first and verify that the remaining AR dynamics are stable. Ordinary differencing $(1-B)$ targets the zero-frequency root, whereas [[普通与季节差分|seasonal differencing $(1-B^s)$]] contains several seasonal unit-circle roots; the two orders are not one interchangeable number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

遵循 [[最小差分原则]]：不足差分会保留随机趋势，过度差分会制造负短期相关，甚至把 MA 根推到不可逆边界。最终选择还取决于解释对象——增长率模型和水平偏离模型回答的不是同一个问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Follow the [[最小差分原则|minimum-differencing principle]]. Underdifferencing leaves stochastic trend; overdifferencing can manufacture negative short-run dependence and place an MA root on the noninvertible boundary. The final choice also depends on the estimand: a growth-rate model and a level-deviation model answer different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.5. 平稳过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.5. Stationary processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.5.1. 均值回归
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.5.1. Mean reversion*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_t=\rho y_{t-1}+\epsilon_t,\qquad |\rho|<1
$$

序列受到冲击后会逐渐回到 0。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After a shock, the series gradually returns to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.5.2. 截距
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.5.2. Intercept*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_t=\alpha+\rho y_{t-1}+\epsilon_t,\qquad |\rho|<1
$$

长期均值为 $\alpha/(1-\rho)$，不是简单地向 $\alpha$ 回归。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The long-run mean is $\alpha/(1-\rho)$; the process does not simply revert to $\alpha$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.5.3. 截距和时间趋势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.5.3. Intercept and time trend*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_t=\alpha+\beta t+\rho y_{t-1}+\epsilon_t
$$

若 $|\rho|<1$，序列围绕确定性趋势路径波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $|\rho|<1$, the series fluctuates around a deterministic trend path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.6. 协方差平稳与稳定 AR 动态的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.6. Boundary between covariance stationarity and stable AR dynamics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 协方差平稳只要求有限常均值、有限常方差和只依赖滞后的协方差；它本身不要求协方差随滞后衰减。
- 在稳定因果 ARMA 模型中，AR 根条件才推出创新冲击逐步衰减和围绕常均值波动。
- 围绕确定性趋势波动的水平序列是“趋势平稳”；在去趋势前，它不是协方差平稳。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Covariance stationarity requires only a finite constant mean, finite constant variance, and lag-only covariance; it does not itself require covariance to decay with lag.
- In a stable causal ARMA model, the AR-root condition is what makes innovation effects decay and the process fluctuate around a constant mean.
- A level series that fluctuates around a deterministic trend is trend-stationary; before detrending, it is not covariance-stationary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 单位根检验和平稳性检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Unit-root and stationarity tests*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 检验 | 原假设 | 处理自相关方式 | 备注 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DF | 有单位根 | 假设误差为白噪声 | 最基础 |
| ADF | 有单位根 | 加入滞后差分项 | 最常用 |
| PP | 有单位根 | 以长期方差修正 DF 系数与 $t$ 统计量 | 允许一般弱依赖与异方差 |
| KPSS | 指定的水平或趋势平稳 | 长期方差进入 LM 统计量 | 原假设方向与前三者相反 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Test | Null hypothesis | Treatment of autocorrelation | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DF | Unit root | Assumes white-noise errors | Most basic |
| ADF | Unit root | Adds lagged differences | Most commonly used |
| PP | Unit root | Uses long-run variance to correct DF coefficient and $t$ statistics | Allows general weak dependence and heteroskedasticity |
| KPSS | Specified level or trend stationarity | Uses a long-run variance in an LM statistic | Reverses the null-hypothesis direction |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无常数、仅常数、常数加趋势对应不同的 [[单位根确定项规格|检验问题与非标准临界值]]。确定性项应由生成机制、图形和研究问题在看最终显著性之前决定，而不是跑完三种规格后挑一个结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No intercept, an intercept only, and an intercept plus trend define different [[单位根确定项规格|testing problems and nonstandard critical values]]. Choose the deterministic terms from the data-generating mechanism, the plot, and the research question before inspecting the final significance, rather than selecting whichever specification gives the desired result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1. [[DF检验|单位根检验]]：DF 检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Unit-root test: the Dickey–Fuller test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Dickey–Fuller 检验在所写的最小 AR 与确定性项规格中检验时间序列是否含单位根；结论只针对该规格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Dickey–Fuller test examines whether a series contains a unit root in the stated minimal AR and deterministic specification; its conclusion is conditional on that specification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.1. DF 检验的基本形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.1. Basic form of the DF test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 AR(1) 出发：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start from an AR(1) process:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_t=\rho y_{t-1}+\epsilon_t
$$

两边减去 $y_{t-1}$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtract $y_{t-1}$ from both sides:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta y_t=\gamma y_{t-1}+\epsilon_t
$$

其中 $\gamma=\rho-1$。检验 $\rho=1$ 等价于检验 $\gamma=0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\gamma=\rho-1$. Testing $\rho=1$ is equivalent to testing $\gamma=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.2. 原假设和备择假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.2. Null and alternative hypotheses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_0:\gamma=0
$$

表示存在单位根，序列非平稳。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This means a unit root is present and the series is nonstationary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_1:\gamma<0
$$

表示沿左尾方向反对根 $\rho=1$；只有把备择预先维持在 $-1<\rho<1$ 时，才能把拒绝解释为平稳 AR(1)，因为 $\gamma<0$ 本身并不排除 $\rho\le -1$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is evidence against the root $\rho=1$ in the left-tailed direction. It supports a stationary AR(1) only under the maintained alternative $-1<\rho<1$, because $\gamma<0$ alone does not exclude $\rho\le -1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.3. 统计量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.3. Test statistic*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\tau=\frac{\hat\gamma}{se(\hat\gamma)}
$$

它形式上像 t 统计量，但不能使用普通 t 分布临界值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It resembles a $t$ statistic, but ordinary $t$ critical values cannot be used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] DF 检验最重要的坑
> DF 统计量长得像 t 统计量，但单位根假设下分布不同，不能拿普通 t 临界值判断。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The DF statistic looks like a $t$ statistic, but its distribution under the unit-root null is different. Ordinary $t$ critical values are invalid.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.4. 分布和临界值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.4. Distribution and critical values*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

DF 统计量服从非标准分布，临界值取决于模型是否包含常数项和趋势项：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The DF statistic has a nonstandard distribution, and its critical values depend on whether the regression includes an intercept and a trend:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 无常数、无趋势。
- 有常数、无趋势。
- 有常数、有趋势。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- no intercept and no trend;
- intercept but no trend;
- intercept and trend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际检验时必须使用对应情形的 DF 临界值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The test must use the DF critical values appropriate to the chosen deterministic terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.5. 数学证明的直观解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.5. Intuition behind the nonstandard distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在单位根假设下：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the unit-root null:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_t=y_0+\sum_{i=1}^t\epsilon_i
$$

$y_{t-1}$ 是过去冲击的累积和，不是普通独立同分布变量。因此 $\hat\gamma/se(\hat\gamma)$ 的极限分布不是普通 t 分布，而是单位根下的非标准分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$y_{t-1}$ is the cumulative sum of past shocks rather than an ordinary i.i.d. variable. Consequently, the limiting distribution of $\hat\gamma/se(\hat\gamma)$ is not a standard $t$ distribution but the nonstandard unit-root distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2. [[ADF检验|ADF 检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2. Augmented Dickey–Fuller test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.2.1. ADF 检验内容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2.1. ADF regression specifications*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ADF 在 DF 回归中加入 $\Delta y$ 的滞后项，以处理误差项自相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ADF test adds lags of $\Delta y$ to the DF regression to absorb autocorrelation in the errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无常数无趋势：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No intercept and no trend:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta y_t=\gamma y_{t-1}
+\sum_{i=1}^k\alpha_i\Delta y_{t-i}+\epsilon_t
$$

有常数无趋势：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Intercept but no trend:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta y_t=\alpha+\gamma y_{t-1}
+\sum_{i=1}^k\alpha_i\Delta y_{t-i}+\epsilon_t
$$

有常数有趋势：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Intercept and trend:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta y_t=\alpha+\beta t+\gamma y_{t-1}
+\sum_{i=1}^k\alpha_i\Delta y_{t-i}+\epsilon_t
$$

### 2.2.2. 为什么需要 ADF 检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2.2. Why the ADF test is needed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

DF 检验假设误差项是白噪声。若误差项存在自相关，DF 检验会失真。ADF 通过加入滞后差分项吸收自相关，使残差更接近白噪声。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The DF test assumes white-noise errors. Autocorrelated errors distort the test. By adding lagged differences, the ADF regression absorbs the autocorrelation and makes the remaining disturbance closer to white noise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.2.3. ADF 检验的具体步骤
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2.3. Steps in an ADF test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 根据图形和理论决定是否加入常数项、趋势项。
2. 用信息准则或残差诊断选择滞后阶数 $k$。
3. 估计 ADF 回归。
4. 检验 $H_0:\gamma=0$。
5. 若拒绝原假设，只能说当前规格下有证据反对该单位根原假设；还要检查其他根、残差和模型设定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Use the plot and theory to decide whether to include an intercept and a trend.<br>
**2.** Select the lag order $k$ using information criteria or residual diagnostics.<br>
**3.** Estimate the ADF regression.<br>
**4.** Test $H_0:\gamma=0$.<br>
**5.** Rejecting the null is evidence against that unit-root null under the current specification; other roots, residuals, and model specification still need checking.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.2.4. 如果非要用 DF，后果是什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2.4. What happens if DF is used despite autocorrelation?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若误差项存在自相关却仍使用 DF，标准误和临界值比较都会失真，容易误判单位根。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using DF when the errors are autocorrelated distorts the standard error and the comparison with critical values, making unit-root classification unreliable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3. [[PP检验|PP 检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3. Phillips–Perron test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

PP 检验与 ADF 一样检验单位根，但处理自相关和异方差的方式不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Like ADF, the PP test tests for a unit root, but it handles autocorrelation and heteroskedasticity differently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.3.1. PP 检验的基本思想
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3.1. Basic idea of the PP test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ADF 在回归方程里加入滞后差分项；PP 不加入这些滞后项，而是用残差长期方差对 DF 的系数统计量与 $t$ 统计量作非参数修正，使其允许一般弱依赖和异方差。这不只是替换一个普通回归标准误。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ADF adds lagged differences to the regression. PP leaves those lags out and uses a residual long-run-variance estimate to make specific nonparametric corrections to the DF coefficient and $t$ statistics, allowing general weak dependence and heteroskedasticity. This is more than swapping in an ordinary robust standard error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.3.2. PP 检验模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3.2. PP regression model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本回归与 DF 相同：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The underlying regression is the same as in the DF test:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta y_t=\gamma y_{t-1}+\epsilon_t
$$

也可以加入常数项和趋势项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An intercept and a trend may also be included.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.3.3. PP 检验的步骤
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3.3. Steps in a PP test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 估计 DF 形式的回归。
2. 计算原始统计量。
3. 用 Newey-West 型长期方差修正统计量。
4. 与 PP 检验临界值比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Estimate a DF-form regression.<br>
**2.** Compute the original statistic.<br>
**3.** Apply a Newey–West-type long-run-variance correction.<br>
**4.** Compare the corrected statistic with the PP critical values.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.3.4. PP 检验与 ADF 检验的差别
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3.4. Difference between PP and ADF*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- ADF：通过加入滞后差分项处理自相关。
- PP：通过修正统计量处理自相关和异方差。
- ADF 需要选择滞后阶数；PP 需要选择长期方差带宽。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- ADF handles autocorrelation by adding lagged differences.
- PP handles autocorrelation and heteroskedasticity by correcting the statistic.
- ADF requires a lag-order choice; PP requires a bandwidth choice for the long-run variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] ADF 和 PP 怎么分
> ADF 把自相关放进模型里处理；PP 不改模型，而是修正统计量。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> ADF handles autocorrelation inside the regression; PP keeps the regression unchanged and corrects the statistic.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.4. [[KPSS检验|KPSS 检验]]：把平稳放在原假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4. KPSS Test: Put Stationarity in the Null*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

KPSS 把序列分解为确定性部分、随机游走成分和平稳误差：
$$
y_t=\delta t+r_t+e_t,
\qquad
r_t=r_{t-1}+u_t.
$$
原假设是随机趋势创新方差 $\operatorname{Var}(u_t)=0$。若只去常数，检验水平平稳；若先去常数和线性趋势，检验趋势平稳。两者不能混用同一组解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
KPSS decomposes the series into a deterministic component, a random-walk component, and a stationary error. Its null sets the random-trend innovation variance to zero. Removing only a constant tests level stationarity; removing a constant and linear trend tests trend stationarity. These are distinct hypotheses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按所选规格取得残差 $\widehat e_t$，构造部分和 $S_t=\sum_{j=1}^{t}\widehat e_j$，再计算
$$
\operatorname{KPSS}
=\frac{T^{-2}\sum_{t=1}^{T}S_t^2}{\widehat\omega^2}.
$$
[[KPSS统计量]] 中的 $\widehat\omega^2$ 是残差长期方差，不是普通样本方差；核与带宽会改变有限样本结果。统计量过大时拒绝所选平稳原假设，未拒绝则只是没有发现足够反证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After obtaining residuals under the chosen specification, KPSS cumulates them and normalizes the squared partial sums by a long-run variance $\widehat\omega^2$. As [[KPSS统计量]] explains, this is not the ordinary residual variance, and kernel and bandwidth choices affect finite-sample results. A large statistic rejects the specified stationary null; non-rejection is only an absence of sufficient contrary evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.5. 怎样组合相反原假设的证据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.5. Combining Evidence from Opposite Null Hypotheses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[单位根证据组合]] 不是“三个检验投票”：ADF 与 PP 共享单位根原假设，KPSS 反过来以平稳为原假设，但它们仍使用同一份有限样本，也共同受确定性项、断点和调节参数影响。一个实用读法是：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[单位根证据组合]] is not a vote. ADF and PP use a unit-root null while KPSS reverses the direction, but all tests use the same finite sample and remain sensitive to deterministic terms, breaks, and tuning choices. A practical reading is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- ADF/PP 拒绝且 KPSS 不拒绝：与所选平稳规格相容，不是平稳证明。
- ADF/PP 不拒绝且 KPSS 拒绝：与随机趋势相容，但仍要检查断点与规格。
- 两边都拒绝：当前简单模型可能都不合适。
- 两边都不拒绝：样本可能没有足够区分力，不能强行归类。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- ADF/PP rejection with KPSS non-rejection is compatible with the selected stationary specification, not proof of it.
- ADF/PP non-rejection with KPSS rejection is compatible with a stochastic trend, but breaks and specification still need checking.
- Rejection on both sides suggests that neither simple model may be adequate.
- Non-rejection on both sides often means that the sample cannot discriminate sharply, not that one label must be forced.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此必须遵守 [[ADF未拒绝的含义]]：$p=0.18$ 不能写成“序列已证明为 $I(1)$”。同样，拒绝单位根也不能自动证明残差白噪声、排除季节根或保证预测模型正确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hence the wording in [[ADF未拒绝的含义]] matters: $p=0.18$ does not prove that the series is $I(1)$. Conversely, rejecting a unit root does not establish white-noise residuals, rule out seasonal roots, or validate the forecasting model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.6. 近单位根和结构突变会怎样改变结论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.6. How Near Roots and Structural Breaks Change the Conclusion*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $y_t=\rho y_{t-1}+\epsilon_t$，[[近单位根|$\rho$ 很接近 1]] 时冲击最终会衰减，却可能在有限样本中像永久冲击，导致单位根检验功效很低。一次未拒绝因此不能决定永久差分；还应比较近单位根、差分和去趋势模型在同一最终预测目标上的表现。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $\rho$ is [[近单位根|close to one]], shocks eventually decay but can look permanent in a finite sample, leaving unit-root tests with low power. One non-rejection therefore cannot decide permanent differencing; compare near-root, differenced, and detrended models on the same final forecasting target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[结构突变伪单位根|未建模的水平或斜率突变]] 也可能让一个分段趋势平稳序列伪装成单位根。由样本外制度事件事先固定断点，与在同一数据中搜索“最显著”日期，是不同的 [[单位根断点选择|选择机制]]，必须使用各自对应的检验分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[结构突变伪单位根|unmodelled level or slope break]] can also make a piecewise trend-stationary series resemble a unit root. A breakpoint fixed in advance by external institutional evidence and a date chosen by searching the same sample involve different [[单位根断点选择|selection mechanisms]] and require their corresponding test distributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. [[协整]]和误差纠正机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Cointegration and the error-correction mechanism*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

协整指多个非平稳序列本身是 $I(1)$，但某个非零线性组合是 $I(0)$。这首先是一条稳定的长期统计约束；把它解释成经济均衡或因果机制还需要理论与识别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cointegration occurs when several nonstationary series are individually $I(1)$ but some nonzero linear combination is $I(0)$. This is first a stable long-run statistical restriction; interpreting it as economic equilibrium or causality requires theory and identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一条关系可以乘任意非零常数，所以[[协整向量归一化|归一化]]只是选择表达尺度，不是经济识别。对 $n$ 个标准 $I(1)$ 变量，若有 $r$ 条线性独立的稳定组合，就只剩[[协整秩与共同趋势|$n-r$ 个共同随机趋势]]；高相关、长期共同上升或一个显著水平回归都不能替代这个定义。完整解释边界见[[协整解释边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same relation can be multiplied by any nonzero constant, so [[协整向量归一化|normalization]] chooses a scale of representation rather than providing economic identification. With $n$ standard $I(1)$ variables and $r$ linearly independent stationary combinations, [[协整秩与共同趋势|$n-r$ common stochastic trends]] remain. High correlation, common upward movement, or a significant levels regression cannot replace this definition; see [[协整解释边界|the interpretation boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 协整的含义
> 两个变量可以各自漂移，但某个特定线性组合不能无限漂移。该组合是长期统计约束；它不自动告诉我们谁调整、为何调整或谁导致谁。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Two variables may each wander, but one specified linear combination cannot wander without bound. This is a long-run statistical restriction; it does not by itself identify who adjusts, why adjustment occurs, or causal direction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 概念 | 判断对象 | 结论 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 单位根 | 单个水平序列是否含未抵消单位根 | 提醒常规平稳回归推断是否需要差分、去趋势或协整等专门处理 |
| 伪回归 | 非平稳序列直接回归是否误导 | 显著不一定真实 |
| 协整 | 多个非平稳序列是否有平稳组合 | 可以保留统计上的长期约束；经济均衡和因果仍需理论与识别 |
| ECM | 指定模型中哪些变量响应上一期长期偏离 | 调整系数在该规格下给出方向与速度 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Concept | Object of diagnosis | Conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unit root | Does a level series contain an uncancelled unit root? | Signals whether ordinary stationary-regression inference needs differencing, detrending, cointegration, or another specialised treatment |
| Spurious regression | Is a direct regression of nonstationary series misleading? | Significance need not reflect a real relationship |
| Cointegration | Do several nonstationary series have a stationary combination? | A statistical long-run restriction can be retained; economic equilibrium and causality still require theory and identification |
| ECM | Which variables respond to the lagged long-run deviation in the specified model? | Adjustment coefficients give direction and speed conditional on that specification |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. 例子说明
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1. Example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个随机游走：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider two random walks:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_t=y_{t-1}+u_t
$$

$$
x_t=x_{t-1}+v_t
$$

它们各自非平稳。但如果存在 $\beta$，使得：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each is nonstationary. But suppose there is a $\beta$ such that:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\epsilon_t=y_t-\beta x_t
$$

并且 $\epsilon_t\sim I(0)$，则 $y_t$ 和 $x_t$ 协整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\epsilon_t\sim I(0)$, then $y_t$ and $x_t$ are cointegrated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.1.1. 检验协整关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1.1. Testing for cointegration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 估计长期关系：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Estimate the long-run relationship:<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

   $$
   y_t=\alpha+\beta x_t+\epsilon_t
   $$

2. 保存残差 $\hat\epsilon_t$。
3. 检验残差是否平稳。
4. 若已确认变量为 $I(1)$，且估计残差用专用协整临界值拒绝单位根，才支持当前规格下存在协整关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** Save the residuals $\hat\epsilon_t$.<br>
**3.** Test whether the residuals are stationary.<br>
**4.** If the variables have been established as $I(1)$ and the estimated residual rejects a unit root against the dedicated cointegration critical values, the result supports cointegration under the current specification.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. [[时间序列伪回归|伪回归]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2. Spurious regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

伪回归指两个无关的非平稳序列做回归时，可能出现很高的 $R^2$ 和显著 t 统计量，但这种显著性并不代表真实关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A spurious regression occurs when two unrelated nonstationary series produce a high $R^2$ and significant $t$ statistics even though no genuine relationship exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原因是随机趋势会共同制造“看起来一起变化”的假象。对 $I(1)$ 序列，不能直接套用普通回归结论，必须先检查平稳性和协整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The common appearance of trending creates the illusion that the series move together. Ordinary regression conclusions cannot be applied directly to $I(1)$ series; stationarity and cointegration must be checked first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.3. [[协整]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3. Cointegration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_t\sim I(1),\qquad x_t\sim I(1)
$$

且
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_t-\beta x_t\sim I(0)
$$

则 $y_t$ 与 $x_t$ 协整：这个线性组合是稳定的长期统计约束。是否以及由哪个变量修正偏离，要看误差修正表示中的调整系数；不能仅凭协整定义断言经济系统会按某一方向“回到均衡”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
then $y_t$ and $x_t$ are cointegrated: the linear combination is a stable long-run statistical restriction. Whether, and through which variable, deviations are corrected depends on the adjustment coefficients in an error-correction representation; cointegration alone does not establish an economic return mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.3.1. [[ECM长短期结构|误差纠正机制 ECM]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3.1. Error-correction model (ECM)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若长期均衡误差为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the lagged long-run equilibrium error is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\epsilon_{t-1}=y_{t-1}-\beta x_{t-1}
$$

在[[Granger表示定理|表示定理]]的正则条件下，协整关系与相容的误差修正表示彼此对应。二变量误差纠正模型可写成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
under the regularity conditions of the [[Granger表示定理|Granger representation theorem]], cointegration and a compatible error-correction representation imply one another. A bivariate error-correction model can be written as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta y_t=\alpha+\delta\Delta x_t+\gamma\epsilon_{t-1}+u_t
$$

若 $\gamma<0$，表示上一期 $y$ 高于长期均衡时，本期会向下修正；上一期低于长期均衡时，本期会向上修正。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\gamma<0$, an above-equilibrium value of $y$ last period induces a downward correction this period, while a below-equilibrium value induces an upward correction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个符号结论依赖上面把误差定义为 $y-\beta x$；若把协整项整体乘以 $-1$，调整系数也必须同时反号，见[[误差修正方向判读]]。式中的同期 $\Delta x_t$ 还要求相应的条件外生性或联合系统解释；一般经验规格会按理论和诊断加入滞后差分，不能把这条最小示意式当作固定模板。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This sign conclusion depends on defining the disequilibrium as $y-\beta x$. Multiplying the cointegrating term by $-1$ requires reversing the adjustment coefficient as well; see [[误差修正方向判读|the sign rule]]. A contemporaneous $\Delta x_t$ also requires a suitable conditional-exogeneity or joint-system interpretation. Empirical specifications usually add lagged differences according to theory and diagnostics rather than treating this minimal illustration as a fixed template.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4. 基于残差的协整检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Residual-based cointegration tests*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本思想是先估计长期关系，再检验残差是否平稳。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The basic idea is to estimate the long-run relationship first and then test whether its residual is stationary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. [[Engle-Granger两步法|EG 两步检验法]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1. Engle–Granger two-step test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 第一步估计长期方程：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** First, estimate the long-run equation:<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

   $$
   y_t=\alpha+\beta x_t+\epsilon_t
   $$

2. 第二步对残差 $\hat\epsilon_t$ 做单位根检验。
3. 若估计残差在与第一阶段规格匹配的专用临界值下拒绝单位根，则支持当前规格下存在协整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** Second, perform a unit-root test on the residual $\hat\epsilon_t$.<br>
**3.** If the estimated residual rejects a unit root using critical values matched to the first-stage specification, the result supports cointegration under that specification.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

注意：残差是估计出来的，不是直接观测变量，所以临界值不能直接用普通 ADF 表，要使用 Engle-Granger/MacKinnon 临界值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The residual is estimated rather than directly observed, so ordinary ADF critical values are invalid. Use Engle–Granger or MacKinnon critical values instead.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

EG 检验适合单一协整关系，但难以处理多个协整向量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The EG test is suitable for a single cointegrating relationship but is not well suited to multiple cointegrating vectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] EG 两步法怎么记
> 先估长期组合，再用适用于估计残差的临界值检验它是否为 $I(0)$。通过这一步支持的是协整这一统计约束，不是经济均衡或因果的自动证明。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Estimate the long-run combination, then test the residual for $I(0)$ using critical values appropriate to estimated residuals. This supports the statistical cointegration restriction; it does not by itself prove economic equilibrium or causality.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一阶段斜率可以[[协整回归超一致性|超一致]]，但普通 $t/F$ 推断仍可能失效。[[EG残差检验]]用估计残差专用的分布；EG-ADF 通过滞后差分参数化短期相关，[[Phillips-Ouliaris检验|Phillips–Ouliaris]] 则用非参数长期协方差修正统计量。多变量系统还受[[EG多变量局限|单一归一化方向]]限制，不能用一次 EG 回归决定完整协整秩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first-stage slope can be [[协整回归超一致性|superconsistent]], while ordinary $t/F$ inference still fails. The [[EG残差检验|EG residual test]] uses a distribution specific to estimated residuals: EG-ADF parameterizes short-run dependence with lagged differences, whereas the [[Phillips-Ouliaris检验|Phillips–Ouliaris test]] applies a nonparametric long-run-covariance correction. In a multivariate system, the [[EG多变量局限|single normalized direction]] also prevents one EG regression from determining the full cointegration rank.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 5. 基于误差纠正机制的协整检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Cointegration tests based on the error-correction representation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.1. [[Johansen检验|Johansen 检验]]的基本思想
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.1. Basic idea of the Johansen test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 方法 | 适用场景 | 能识别几条协整关系 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EG 两步法 | 两变量或单一长期关系 | 通常一条 |
| Johansen 检验 | 多变量系统 | 可以多条 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Method | Appropriate setting | Number of relationships identified |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EG two-step method | Two variables or one long-run relationship | Usually one |
| Johansen test | Multivariate system | Potentially several |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Johansen 检验基于[[VAR到VECM重参数化|VAR 的误差纠正重参数化]]：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Johansen test is based on the [[VAR到VECM重参数化|error-correction reparameterization of a VAR]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta y_t=\Pi y_{t-1}
+\sum_{i=1}^{k-1}\Gamma_i\Delta y_{t-i}
+\epsilon_t
$$

在变量至多为 $I(1)$、单位圆上除模型已表示的 $1$ 根外没有季节根或其他根、系统也无爆炸根，且其他正则条件成立时，关键总体对象是矩阵 $\Pi$ 的秩：$0<\operatorname{rank}(\Pi)<n$ 时，该秩等于协整关系数。这一解释不是任意矩阵的无条件等式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the variables are at most $I(1)$, the unit circle contains no seasonal or other roots beyond the modelled roots at $1$, the system has no explosive roots, and the other regularity conditions hold, the key population object is the rank of $\Pi$. If $0<\operatorname{rank}(\Pi)<n$, that rank equals the number of cointegrating relationships. This interpretation is not a context-free identity for an arbitrary matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三种[[VECM的Π秩|$\Pi$ 秩情形]]必须在这些前提下解释：$r=0$ 没有水平约束，$0<r<n$ 是非平凡协整，$r=n$ 则表示水平平稳。中间情形可写成 $\Pi=\alpha\beta'$，其中[[VECM的α与β|$\beta$ 张成协整空间、$\alpha$ 记录调整载荷]]；但[[αβ分解非唯一性|这组矩阵对并不唯一]]，仍需归一化或可检验的经济限制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three [[VECM的Π秩|rank cases for $\Pi$]] must be interpreted under these maintained conditions: $r=0$ means no level restriction, $0<r<n$ is nontrivial cointegration, and $r=n$ means stationary levels. In the middle case, $\Pi=\alpha\beta'$, where [[VECM的α与β|$\beta$ spans the cointegration space and $\alpha$ contains the adjustment loadings]]. Yet [[αβ分解非唯一性|the matrix pair is not unique]], so normalization or testable economic restrictions are still required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.2. Johansen 检验的步骤
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.2. Steps in the Johansen test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 根据整合阶数、经济问题和数据结构设定变量系统，明确常数、趋势等确定性项。
2. 结合信息准则与残差诊断选择水平 VAR 的滞后阶数，再写成 VECM。
3. 把 $\Delta y_t$ 和 $y_{t-1}$ 分别对短期滞后与确定性项残差化，由两组残差乘积矩阵解[[Johansen广义特征值|广义特征值／典型相关问题]]。
4. 用与确定性项规格匹配的[[Johansen秩检验|trace 或最大特征值检验]]判断协整秩，而不是数 $\hat\Pi$ 的普通特征值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Define the system from the variables' integration orders, the economic question, and the data structure, including the placement of constants and trends.<br>
**2.** Select the level-VAR lag order using information criteria and residual diagnostics, then write the corresponding VECM.<br>
**3.** Residualize $\Delta y_t$ and $y_{t-1}$ against the short-run lags and deterministic terms, and solve the [[Johansen广义特征值|generalized-eigenvalue/canonical-correlation problem]] formed from the two residual product-moment blocks.<br>
**4.** Determine the rank with a [[Johansen秩检验|trace or maximum-eigenvalue test]] whose reference values match the deterministic specification; do not count ordinary eigenvalues of $\hat\Pi$.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.3. Johansen 检验统计量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.3. Johansen test statistics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Trace 统计量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trace statistic:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它检验 $H_0:\operatorname{rank}(\Pi)\le r$ 对 $H_1:\operatorname{rank}(\Pi)>r$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It tests $H_0:\operatorname{rank}(\Pi)\le r$ against $H_1:\operatorname{rank}(\Pi)>r$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Trace}
=-T\sum_{i=r+1}^{n}\ln(1-\hat\lambda_i)
$$

最大特征值统计量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maximum-eigenvalue statistic:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它比较 $H_0:\operatorname{rank}(\Pi)=r$ 与相邻备择 $H_1:\operatorname{rank}(\Pi)=r+1$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It compares $H_0:\operatorname{rank}(\Pi)=r$ with the adjacent alternative $H_1:\operatorname{rank}(\Pi)=r+1$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{MaxEigen}
=-T\ln(1-\hat\lambda_{r+1})
$$

其中 $T$ 是进入残差化样本的有效观测数；$\hat\lambda_i$ 是两组残差样本矩的广义特征值，也就是平方样本典型相关，不是 $\hat\Pi$ 的普通特征值。检验通常从 $r=0$ 开始，逐步增加 $r$，直到第一次不能拒绝原假设；临界值必须与确定性项规格匹配。Johansen 检验可以在一个系统中识别多条协整关系，但结论仍需滞后、残差、稳定性和断点检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $T$ is the effective number of observations entering the residualized sample. The $\hat\lambda_i$ are generalized eigenvalues of the two residual sample-moment blocks—equivalently, squared sample canonical correlations—not ordinary eigenvalues of $\hat\Pi$. Testing usually starts at $r=0$ and increases $r$ until the first null that cannot be rejected, using reference values matched to the deterministic specification. Johansen can identify several cointegrating relations within one system, but the result still needs lag, residual, stability, and break diagnostics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] 何时用 Johansen
> 若理论事先指定了一条归一化的长期关系，EG 可作为透明的单方程起点；若多变量系统可能有多条协整关系，或需要联合判断秩与空间限制，Johansen 更自然。方法选择不能替代整合阶数、规格与有限样本诊断。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If theory prespecifies one normalized long-run relation, EG can be a transparent single-equation starting point. Johansen is more natural when a multivariate system may have several relations or when rank and space restrictions must be assessed jointly. Method choice does not replace integration-order, specification, or finite-sample diagnostics.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选秩以后，先检查[[Johansen规格选择|滞后阶数与确定性项规格]]的稳健性，再解释 $\alpha$、$\beta$ 和短期动态；零调整载荷涉及[[弱外生性与调整载荷|针对指定长期参数的弱外生性]]，不等于[[弱外生与Granger非因果|Granger 非因果]]。最后用[[标准协整流程边界|$I(2)$、断点与小样本边界]]、残差和系统稳定性验收，完整顺序见[[协整分析流程]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After selecting a rank, test robustness to [[Johansen规格选择|lag-order and deterministic-term specifications]], then interpret $\alpha$, $\beta$, and the short-run dynamics. A zero adjustment loading concerns [[弱外生性与调整载荷|weak exogeneity for specified long-run parameters]] and is not the same as [[弱外生与Granger非因果|Granger noncausality]]. Finally validate the model against [[标准协整流程边界|$I(2)$, breaks, and small-sample limits]], residual diagnostics, and system stability; see [[协整分析流程|the complete workflow]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
