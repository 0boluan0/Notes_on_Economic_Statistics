# 1. [[面板数据：Pooled OLS、固定效应与随机效应.canvas|面板数据模型]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Panel data models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1. 模型内容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1. Model structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面板数据同时包含截面维度和时间维度。基本模型为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Panel data contain both a cross-sectional dimension and a time dimension. A basic model is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_{it}=\alpha+\beta X_{it}+\epsilon_{it}
$$

[[面板误差分解|误差项常拆成]]：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[面板误差分解|disturbance is often decomposed]] as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\epsilon_{it}=\mu_i+\lambda_t+u_{it}
$$

其中：

- $\mu_i$：个体效应，表示不同个体的不可观测差异。
- $\lambda_t$：时间效应，表示共同时间冲击。
- $u_{it}$：剩余扰动项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $\mu_i$: the individual effect, representing unobserved differences across units.
- $\lambda_t$: the time effect, representing shocks common to all units in a given period.
- $u_{it}$: the remaining idiosyncratic disturbance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平衡面板中每个单位观察相同的时期数；[[非平衡面板]] 允许不同单位有不同的观测数 $T_i$。若各单位观测数相同但时间点组合不同，更精确的名称是 weakly balanced。在这里的标准未加权 FE、RE 与 CRE 变换中，要先用结果与整组回归量锁定共同完整样本，再按单位各自进入该样本的 $T_i$ 期计算所有均值，不能让不同变量使用 pairwise 非缺失期，也不能把缺失时期当作 0，见 [[非平衡面板均值]]。数据结构本身与退出、缺失造成的 [[非平衡面板选择|样本选择]] 仍是两件事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a balanced panel every unit has the same number of observations; an [[非平衡面板|unbalanced panel]] allows unit-specific observation counts $T_i$. When the counts are equal but the observed time points differ, the more precise term is weakly balanced. For the standard unweighted FE, RE, and CRE transformations considered here, estimation first locks one common complete-case sample for the outcome and the full regressor set, then computes every unit mean over that unit's $T_i$ periods in the common sample; variable-specific pairwise means and zero-filled missing periods are not equivalent. The data structure remains distinct from [[非平衡面板选择|selection through attrition or missingness]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 面板数据的优势
> 面板数据能同时利用“个体之间的差异”和“同一个体随时间的变化”，所以比单纯截面或时间序列更灵活。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Panel data exploit both differences across units and changes within the same unit over time, making them more flexible than purely cross-sectional or time-series data.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 面板数据的混合估计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2. Pooled estimation of panel data*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[面板Pooled OLS|混合 OLS]] 直接把所有单位时期观测堆叠为一组普通回归，并把复合误差写成 $v_{it}=\mu_i+u_{it}$。模型显式含截距时，要一致估计目标斜率，核心要求是 $\operatorname{Cov}(x_{it},v_{it})=0$；再配合可逆的中心化总体二阶矩和适合单位内依赖的样本矩收敛，见 [[Pooled OLS正交条件]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[面板Pooled OLS|Pooled OLS]] stacks all unit-period observations as one ordinary regression and treats $v_{it}=\mu_i+u_{it}$ as the composite error. With an explicit intercept, consistency of the target slope requires the centred moment $\operatorname{Cov}(x_{it},v_{it})=0$, an invertible centred second moment, and sample-moment convergence compatible with within-unit dependence; see the separate [[Pooled OLS正交条件|pooled-OLS orthogonality condition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数一致性和推断必须分开。即使该总体矩成立，同一单位的复合误差仍可跨期相关；标准误应按误差依赖或处理分配层级聚类，而簇数很少时还需小样本修正或设计相容的替代推断，详见 [[聚类标准误边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Coefficient consistency and inference are separate. Even when the population moment holds, composite errors may remain dependent within a unit. Standard errors should cluster at the dependence or assignment level, and few clusters require small-sample corrections or design-compatible alternatives; see [[聚类标准误边界|the clustered-standard-error boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 估计方式 | 是否控制个体效应 | 关键假设 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[面板Pooled OLS|混合 OLS]] | 否 | [[Pooled OLS正交条件|复合误差与回归量总体正交]] |
| [[个体固定效应|固定效应]] | 是 | 允许个体效应与回归量相关；仍需 idiosyncratic shock 对整条路径严格外生 |
| [[随机效应]] | 是 | 进一步要求 [[随机效应正交假设|个体效应与整条回归量路径正交]] |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Estimator | Controls for individual effects? | Key assumption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[面板Pooled OLS|Pooled OLS]] | No | [[Pooled OLS正交条件|Regressors are population-orthogonal to the composite error]] |
| [[个体固定效应|Fixed effects]] | Yes | Allows unit-effect correlation; still needs strict exogeneity of idiosyncratic shocks over the full path |
| [[随机效应|Random effects]] | Yes | Additionally requires [[随机效应正交假设|orthogonality between the unit effect and the entire regressor path]] |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 固定效应与随机效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Fixed effects and random effects*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1. 模型形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Model specification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见静态面板模型为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common static panel model is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_{it}=\alpha+\beta X_{it}+\mu_i+\lambda_t+u_{it}
$$

其中 $\mu_i$ 是个体效应，$\lambda_t$ 是时间效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mu_i$ is the individual effect and $\lambda_t$ is the time effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下方固定效应和随机效应的比较主要针对静态面板；动态面板因为含滞后被解释变量，通常还会出现额外内生性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The comparison between fixed and random effects below concerns static panels. Dynamic panels contain a lagged dependent variable and therefore usually introduce an additional endogeneity problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2. [[个体固定效应|固定效应]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2. Fixed effects*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

个体固定效应允许单位效应与解释变量路径相关，例如
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unit fixed effects allow the unit effect to be correlated with the regressor path, for example:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(\mu_i,X_{it})\ne0
$$

若这种相关使 $E[x_{it}(\mu_i+u_{it})]\ne0$，忽略 $\mu_i$ 的混合 OLS 就不一致。FE 只消去加性且时间不变的 $\mu_i$，仍要求 [[固定效应严格外生|每期冲击对整条回归量路径严格外生]]；时变遗漏仍可能与回归量的单位内变化相关，因此“加入 FE”本身不是因果识别证明，见 [[固定效应因果边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If this correlation makes $E[x_{it}(\mu_i+u_{it})]\ne0$, pooled OLS that omits $\mu_i$ is inconsistent. FE removes only the additive time-invariant component and still requires [[固定效应严格外生|strict exogeneity over the full regressor path]]. Time-varying omissions may remain correlated with within-unit regressor changes, so adding FE is not itself a causal identification argument; see the [[固定效应因果边界|fixed-effects causal boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3. [[随机效应]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3. Random effects*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机效应把 $\mu_i$ 作为复合误差中的单位层随机分量，并在额外正交限制下保留组内与组间信息。标准 RE 的 [[随机效应正交假设|单位效应正交条件]] 常写成 $E(\mu_i\mid X_i)=0$；逐期协方差写法是其常用简记：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Random effects treat $\mu_i$ as a unit-level random component of the composite error and retain within and between information under an additional restriction. The standard RE [[随机效应正交假设|unit-effect orthogonality condition]] is commonly written $E(\mu_i\mid X_i)=0$; the period-specific covariance is a shorthand:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(\mu_i,X_{it})=0
$$

随机抽取单位不会自动保证这项正交。只有单位效应正交、idiosyncratic shocks 外生且 RE 协方差结构适当时，[[随机效应GLS]] 才能通过组内和组间信息获得通常的效率收益；若单位效应正交失败，GLS 权重不会修复不一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Random sampling of units does not imply this orthogonality. Only when unit-effect orthogonality, idiosyncratic-shock exogeneity, and the RE covariance structure are credible can [[随机效应GLS|random-effects GLS]] obtain its usual efficiency gain from within and between information. GLS weighting does not repair inconsistency when orthogonality fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] FE 和 RE 的核心分歧
> 关键不是名字或单位是否随机抽样，而是 $\mu_i$ 与整条 $X_i$ 路径的正交限制能否由机制支持。FE 允许这项相关，却仍需自己的严格外生条件；RE 同时使用组内与组间信息，因此多承担一层正交限制。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The names and random sampling are not the point. What matters is whether orthogonality between $\mu_i$ and the full $X_i$ path is credible. FE permits this correlation but retains its own strict-exogeneity condition; RE uses both within and between information and therefore carries an additional orthogonality restriction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. 静态面板数据模型的估计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Estimating static panel-data models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. 固定效应估计：[[组内变换|within]] 与 [[Within与LSDV等价|LSDV]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1. Fixed-effects estimation by LSDV*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

LSDV 通过加入个体虚拟变量来控制个体固定效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
LSDV controls for individual fixed effects by adding individual dummy variables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 方法 | 操作直觉 | 保留的信息 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Within与LSDV等价|LSDV]] | 加入个体虚拟变量 | 与同样本、同规格的 within 估计只在共同斜率上等价 |
| [[组内变换]] | 对变量减去实际估计样本中的个体均值 | 使用个体内部时间变化 |
| [[时间固定效应]] | 加入时间虚拟变量 | 只控制每期对所有单位共同的加性冲击 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Method | Operational intuition | Information retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Within与LSDV等价|LSDV]] | Add individual dummies | Matches within only for common slopes in the same sample and specification |
| [[组内变换|Within transformation]] | Subtract estimation-sample unit means | Uses changes within a unit over time |
| [[时间固定效应|Time fixed effects]] | Add time dummies | Controls only additive period shocks common to all units |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模型：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Model:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_{it}=\alpha_i+\beta x_{it}+u_{it}
$$

引入个体虚拟变量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Introduce individual dummy variables:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_{it}=\alpha_0+\beta x_{it}
+\sum_{i=1}^{N-1}\mu_iD_i+u_{it}
$$

若包含截距项，只能放入 $N-1$ 个个体虚拟变量，否则会出现完全多重共线性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With an intercept in the model, only $N-1$ individual dummies can be included; otherwise perfect multicollinearity results.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OLS 估计时，$\beta$ 的识别来自同一个体内部随时间变化的部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under OLS estimation, $\beta$ is identified by variation over time within the same unit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在线性模型中，LSDV 与组内变换在**同一估计样本和同一回归量规格**下给出相同的 $\beta$ 斜率；这不表示两者的截距归一化、单位效应、$R^2$ 展示或自由度处理完全相同。样本、权重或时间效应不同，也不能援引 [[Within与LSDV等价|这条等价]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a linear model, LSDV and the within transformation produce the same slope $\beta$ only with the same estimation sample and regressor specification. Their intercept normalisation, unit effects, reported $R^2$, and degrees-of-freedom treatment need not be identical, and [[Within与LSDV等价|the equivalence]] cannot be invoked across different samples, weights, or time-effect specifications.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] 固定效应的直觉
> 固定效应不是神秘方法，就是把每个个体不随时间变的差异先控制掉。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fixed effects are not mysterious: they first control for every difference between units that does not change over time.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. LSDV 的直观含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2. Intuitive meaning of LSDV*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.2.1. 模型形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2.1. Model specification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_{it}=\beta_0+\beta_1x_{it}+\mu_i+\lambda_t+u_{it}
$$

实际研究中通常更关心 $\beta_1$，而不是每个虚拟变量的具体系数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Empirical research usually focuses on $\beta_1$, not on the particular coefficient of every dummy variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.2.2. LSDV 估计法的步骤
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2.2. Steps in LSDV estimation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以把固定效应理解为“先在共同完整估计样本中按单位去掉均值，再做回归”。均值怎样取见 [[非平衡面板均值]]，变换本身见 [[组内变换]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fixed effects can be understood as subtracting unit means within one common complete-case estimation sample and then running the regression. The [[非平衡面板均值|unbalanced-panel mean rule]] and the [[组内变换|within transformation]] are recorded separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $y$ 和 $x$ 分别去掉个体均值：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtract the unit-specific means from $y$ and $x$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_{it}-\bar y_i=\beta_1(x_{it}-\bar x_i)+(u_{it}-\bar u_i)
$$

然后用去均值后的变量做 OLS。这个估计量也叫组内估计量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then run OLS on the demeaned variables. The resulting estimator is also called the within estimator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.2.3. 含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2.3. Interpretation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定效应估计利用同一单位内部的时间变化，因此能控制加性且不随时间变化、但可能与解释变量相关的个体特征。时间不变回归量自身也会被吸收；变化极少的回归量只由很弱的组内信号支撑，见 [[组内变异边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fixed-effects estimation uses variation over time within each unit and therefore controls additive time-invariant characteristics that may correlate with regressors. A time-invariant regressor itself is absorbed, and a nearly invariant regressor is supported by little within signal; see the [[组内变异边界|within-variation boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.3. LSDV 的 $R^2$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3. $R^2$ in LSDV models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

软件输出的总体 $R^2$ 可能受到大量虚拟变量影响。解释固定效应模型时，更常看组内 $R^2$，因为它反映的是去除个体效应后，解释变量对个体内部变化的解释力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The overall $R^2$ reported by software can be inflated by the many dummy variables. In a fixed-effects model, the within $R^2$ is often more informative because it measures how much of the within-unit variation the regressors explain after individual effects have been removed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.3.1. [[时间固定效应|时间效应处理方式同理]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3.1. Time effects are handled analogously*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

时间固定效应通过时期虚拟变量吸收对所有单位共同的加性冲击，例如全国性经济周期或金融危机。它不能吸收单位特有的时变混杂，也不能自动修复错位处理下的异质效应比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Time fixed effects use period indicators to absorb additive shocks common to all units, such as a national business cycle or financial crisis. They do not absorb unit-specific time-varying confounding or automatically repair heterogeneous staggered-treatment comparisons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.4. [[随机效应GLS|随机效应估计]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.4. Random-effects estimation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机效应利用 GLS/FGLS 处理误差项结构。由于同一个体不同期都包含 $\mu_i$，误差项在个体内部相关，随机效应通过估计这种协方差结构提高效率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Random-effects estimation uses GLS or FGLS to handle the disturbance covariance structure. Because observations for the same unit all contain $\mu_i$, their composite errors are correlated over time. Random effects gain efficiency by estimating this covariance structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体变换是按 $T_i$ 和方差分量只减去单位均值的一部分，因此 RE 同时使用 within 与 between 信息。若担心 $\mu_i$ 与 $X_i$ 相关，可用 [[相关随机效应]] 把时变回归量的单位均值加入方程；在同一样本与规格下，它和 FE 对时变回归量的共同斜率关系见 [[CRE与FE斜率等价]]。CRE 能报告时间不变变量的系数，但其解释仍受 [[CRE时间不变系数边界]] 约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The transformation subtracts only a fraction of the unit mean, with the fraction determined by $T_i$ and the variance components, so RE combines within and between information. If $\mu_i$ may correlate with $X_i$, [[相关随机效应|correlated random effects]] adds unit means of time-varying regressors to parameterise that dependence. Its relation to FE slopes under the same sample and specification is stated in [[CRE与FE斜率等价|the CRE-FE slope equivalence]]. CRE can report coefficients on time-invariant regressors, but their interpretation remains subject to the [[CRE时间不变系数边界|time-invariant-coefficient boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.5. [[Hausman检验|豪斯曼检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.5. Hausman test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经典 FE–RE Hausman 应用比较共同斜率：FE 在原假设和允许 $\mu_i$ 与 $X_i$ 相关的备择下都一致；RE 只在原假设下既一致又更有效。原假设成立时，两组共同斜率不应出现系统差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the classical FE–RE Hausman comparison, FE is consistent under both the null and the alternative that permits correlation between $\mu_i$ and $X_i$, while RE is consistent and more efficient only under the null. Their common slopes should therefore show no systematic difference under the null.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.5.1. 豪斯曼检验的基本原理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.5.1. Logic of the Hausman test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原假设：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Null hypothesis:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_0:\operatorname{Cov}(\mu_i,X_{it})=0
$$

在经典共同斜率规格中，这表示用于 RE 的单位效应正交限制成立，且比较所需的其他条件也保持一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the classical common-slope specification, this represents the unit-effect orthogonality restriction used by RE, together with the other conditions needed for a compatible comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

备择假设：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Alternative hypothesis:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_1:\operatorname{Cov}(\mu_i,X_{it})\ne0
$$

这使 RE 的关键正交限制可疑；它不等于“FE 已被证明为因果模型”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This casts doubt on the key RE orthogonality restriction; it does not prove that FE has a causal interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

统计量可写成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The statistic can be written as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H=(\hat\beta_{FE}-\hat\beta_{RE})'
\left[\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_{FE}-\hat\beta_{RE})\right]^{-1}
(\hat\beta_{FE}-\hat\beta_{RE})
\sim\chi^2(k)
$$

### 3.5.2. 豪斯曼检验的步骤
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.5.2. Steps in the Hausman test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 用同一估计样本、共同斜率和可比规格估计 FE 与 RE。
2. 确认经典效率关系和协方差实现适用于当前 VCE；cluster-robust 情形不能机械套普通 Hausman。
3. 比较共同系数的系统差异及其方差。
4. 小 $p$ 值表示数据反对当前 RE 限制，随后要检查机制、规格和相容的替代估计。
5. 大 $p$ 值只表示没有检测到足够大的系统差异；它不证明 $E(\mu_i\mid X_i)=0$，也不能单独决定使用 RE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Estimate FE and RE on the same sample with common slopes and compatible specifications.<br>
**2.** Verify that the classical efficiency relation and covariance implementation fit the VCE; do not mechanically use the ordinary Hausman formula with a cluster-robust VCE.<br>
**3.** Compare the systematic difference in common coefficients and its variance.<br>
**4.** A small $p$-value is evidence against the current RE restrictions and calls for substantive and specification checks.<br>
**5.** A large $p$-value only means that a sufficiently large systematic difference was not detected; it neither proves $E(\mu_i\mid X_i)=0$ nor selects RE by itself.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] Hausman 是诊断，不是选型口诀
> 先从机制判断 $\mu_i$ 与 $X_i$ 是否可能相关，再按 [[Hausman检验边界]] 检查共同系数、样本、规格与 VCE 是否相容。需要稳健 VCE 时，可在 CRE 增广回归中实施 [[Mundlak检验]]；任何不拒绝都不能替代正交论证。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Begin with a substantive argument about whether $\mu_i$ may correlate with $X_i$, then use the [[Hausman检验边界|Hausman boundary]] to verify common coefficients, sample, specification, and VCE compatibility. With a robust VCE, implement the [[Mundlak检验|Mundlak test]] in an augmented CRE regression. Nonrejection never replaces the orthogonality argument.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4. [[动态面板模型|动态面板数据模型]]简介
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Introduction to dynamic panel-data models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. 动态面板数据的内生性问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1. Endogeneity in dynamic panel data*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[动态面板模型]] 包含滞后被解释变量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[动态面板模型|dynamic panel model]] contains a lagged dependent variable:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_{it}=\alpha+\rho y_{i,t-1}+\beta x_{it}+\epsilon_{it}
$$

其中：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\epsilon_{it}=\mu_i+\lambda_t+u_{it}
$$

只要存在个体效应 $\mu_i$，滞后被解释变量 $y_{i,t-1}$ 往往就与误差结构相关，产生内生性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whenever an individual effect $\mu_i$ is present, the lagged dependent variable $y_{i,t-1}$ is generally correlated with the composite error structure, creating endogeneity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 动态面板的额外难点
> 静态面板控制个体效应已经不简单；一旦加入滞后因变量，内生性会重新出现，不能直接套静态 FE/RE。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Controlling for individual effects is already nontrivial in a static panel. Adding a lagged dependent variable reintroduces endogeneity, so static FE or RE methods cannot simply be carried over.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.1.1. GLS 估计的有偏和非一致性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1.1. Bias and inconsistency of GLS*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机效应 GLS 不能解决 $y_{i,t-1}$ 与个体效应 $\mu_i$ 的相关性，因此动态面板中直接用 GLS 可能有偏且不一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Random-effects GLS does not resolve the correlation between $y_{i,t-1}$ and the individual effect $\mu_i$. Direct GLS estimation of a dynamic panel can therefore be biased and inconsistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

差分后：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After first differencing:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta y_{it}=\rho\Delta y_{i,t-1}
+\beta\Delta x_{it}+\Delta u_{it}
$$

但 $\Delta y_{i,t-1}$ 包含 $u_{i,t-1}$，而 $\Delta u_{it}=u_{it}-u_{i,t-1}$ 也包含 $u_{i,t-1}$，二者相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
But $\Delta y_{i,t-1}$ contains $u_{i,t-1}$, while $\Delta u_{it}=u_{it}-u_{i,t-1}$ also contains $u_{i,t-1}$, so the two are correlated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.1.2. LSDV 估计的有偏和非一致性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1.2. Bias and inconsistency of LSDV*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定效应去均值后，滞后被解释变量仍与去均值误差共享过去冲击，产生 [[动态固定效应偏误|Nickell bias]]。在经典稳定 AR(1) 条件下偏误量级为 $O(1/T)$；当 $T$ 固定时，让 $N\to\infty$ 不会消除它，只会更精确地估计错误的概率极限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After fixed-effects demeaning, the lagged dependent variable and demeaned error share past shocks, creating the [[动态固定效应偏误|Nickell bias]]. Under the canonical stable AR(1) conditions its order is $O(1/T)$; with fixed $T$, sending $N\to\infty$ does not remove the bias and only estimates the wrong probability limit more precisely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2. 广义矩估计方法 [[GMM矩条件|GMM]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2. Generalized method of moments (GMM)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[GMM矩条件|GMM]] 可在明确的序列相关、外生性与初始条件下，用满足相应矩条件的滞后变量作工具；并非所有更早滞后都自动有效或足够强。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
GMM can use lags satisfying explicit moment conditions as instruments under stated serial-correlation, exogeneity, and initial-condition assumptions; earlier lags are not automatically valid or sufficiently strong.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 步骤 | 作用 |
| --- | --- |
| 差分 | 消除个体效应 |
| 使用滞后工具变量 | 处理滞后因变量内生性 |
| GMM 加权有效矩条件 | 在矩条件与秩条件成立时得到一致估计 |
| J 检验 | 检查过度识别约束 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Step | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| First differencing | Remove individual effects |
| Use lagged instruments | Address endogeneity of the lagged dependent variable |
| GMM weighting of valid moments | Obtain consistency when the moment and rank conditions hold |
| J test | Check overidentifying restrictions |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.2.1. 动态面板数据模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.1. Dynamic panel-data model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_{it}=\alpha+\rho y_{i,t-1}+\beta x_{it}+\mu_i+u_{it}
$$

### 4.2.2. 内生性问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.2. Endogeneity problem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$y_{i,t-1}$ 与 $\mu_i$ 相关；差分后，$\Delta y_{i,t-1}$ 与 $\Delta u_{it}$ 相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$y_{i,t-1}$ is correlated with $\mu_i$; after differencing, $\Delta y_{i,t-1}$ is correlated with $\Delta u_{it}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.2.3. 差分 GMM 方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.3. Difference GMM*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先差分消除个体效应：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First difference the equation to eliminate the individual effect:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta y_{it}=\rho\Delta y_{i,t-1}
+\beta\Delta x_{it}+\Delta u_{it}
$$

再用更早期的滞后水平变量作为工具变量，例如 $y_{i,t-2}$、$y_{i,t-3}$ 等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then use earlier lagged levels, such as $y_{i,t-2}$ and $y_{i,t-3}$, as instruments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩条件为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The moment condition is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(z_{it}\Delta u_{it})=0
$$

GMM 通过最小化样本矩条件的加权平方和来估计参数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
GMM estimates the parameters by minimizing the weighted sum of squared sample moments:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat\theta=\arg\min_\theta g(\theta)'Wg(\theta)
$$

### 4.2.4. [[过度识别检验边界|J 检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.4. Hansen J test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

J 检验检查过度识别矩条件在当前模型下是否联合相容；它不是逐个证明工具变量有效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The J test assesses whether the overidentifying moment restrictions are jointly compatible with the maintained model; it does not validate each instrument separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原假设：维持的全部矩条件在同一个参数值下成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Null hypothesis: all maintained moment conditions hold at the same parameter value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

统计量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Statistic:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
J=NT\cdot g(\hat\theta)'Wg(\hat\theta)\sim\chi^2(m-k)
$$

其中 $m$ 是矩条件个数，$k$ 是待估参数个数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $m$ is the number of moment conditions and $k$ is the number of estimated parameters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 若 $p$ 值小，拒绝原假设，说明工具、排除限制或其他结构设定中至少有一部分与数据不相容。
- 若 $p$ 值大，只能说没有发现联合冲突；低功效、弱识别或共同无效通道仍可能存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- A small $p$-value rejects the null and indicates a conflict in the instruments, exclusion restrictions, or another maintained part of the structural model.
- A large $p$-value only means that no joint conflict was detected; low power, weak identification, or a shared invalid channel may remain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] 动态面板的复习线索
> 差分消除个体效应；满足具体矩条件的滞后变量才可提供工具；J 检验寻找这些矩条件的联合冲突。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Differencing removes individual effects; only lags justified by the stated moment conditions can serve as instruments; the J test looks for a joint conflict among those restrictions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 5. 从面板模型进入 DID 研究设计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. From panel models to the DID research design*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位固定效应和时间固定效应描述怎样吸收加性单位差异与共同时间冲击，却不会自动说明某个政策系数具有因果意义。政策比较还要先定义处理、目标效应与逐期对照组，再论证对照组的未处理变化为何能代表处理组缺失的反事实。连续阅读进入[[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/14_双重差分法#双重差分法（DID）|双重差分法（DID）]]；全局关系见[[双重差分法（DID）.canvas|DID 主题 Canvas]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unit and time fixed effects describe how additive unit differences and common time shocks are absorbed, but they do not by themselves make a policy coefficient causal. A policy comparison must first define treatment, the target effect, and period-specific controls, then explain why the controls' untreated change represents the treated group's missing counterfactual. Continue with the [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/14_双重差分法#双重差分法（DID）|Difference-in-Differences chapter]]; see the [[双重差分法（DID）.canvas|DID topic Canvas]] for the global relationships.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
