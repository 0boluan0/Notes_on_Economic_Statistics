# 1. 回归：从身高问题的分析讲起
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Regression: beginning with the height example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1. 起源
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1. Origins*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“回归”最早来自生物统计中的平均回归现象：父母身高很高时，子女身高通常仍高于平均水平，但不会无限高；父母身高很矮时，子女身高通常仍低于平均水平，但也会向平均水平靠近。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The term "regression" originated in biostatistics from regression toward the mean. Children of very tall parents tend to remain taller than average, but not without bound; children of very short parents tend to remain shorter than average, yet also move closer to the population mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计量经济学中的回归不只是在描述“回到均值”。在线性条件均值设定正确时，它估计条件平均关系；不作这个设定时，OLS 仍可估计最佳线性投影。两者必须先区分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regression in econometrics is not merely about a return to the mean. When a linear conditional-mean specification is correct, it estimates that conditional-average relationship; without that specification, OLS can still estimate a best linear projection. The two objects must be distinguished.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] 回归的第一层含义
> 回归先回答“平均关系怎么变”，不是天然回答“因果影响是多少”。能不能解释为因果，要看后面的外生性假设。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Regression first answers how an average relationship changes; it does not automatically identify a causal effect. A causal interpretation depends on the exogeneity assumptions introduced later.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 意义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2. Purpose*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归分析回答的是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regression analysis asks:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $X$ 与 $Y$ 是否存在系统关系？
- $X$ 变化 1 单位时，$Y$ 的平均变化是多少？
- 这种关系是否显著、稳定，并且能否解释为经济含义？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Is there a systematic relationship between $X$ and $Y$?
- When $X$ changes by one unit, how much does $Y$ change on average?
- Is the relationship statistically significant and stable, and can it be given an economically meaningful interpretation?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 问题       | 回归里对应什么              |
| -------- | -------------------- |
| 是否存在系统关系 | 斜率 $\beta_1$ 的符号和显著性 |
| 关系有多大    | $\beta_1$ 的数值        |
| 模型解释多少波动 | $R^2$                |
| 结果是否可靠   | 外生性、同方差、无自相关等假设      |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Question | Regression counterpart |
| --- | --- |
| Is there a systematic relationship? | Sign and significance of the slope $\beta_1$ |
| How large is it? | Numerical value of $\beta_1$ |
| How much variation does the model explain? | $R^2$ |
| Are the results reliable? | Assumptions such as exogeneity, homoskedasticity, and no autocorrelation |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. [[条件均值与线性投影|一元线性回归]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Simple linear regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1. 总体回归方程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Population regression equation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一元线性回归可有两种不同读法：把下式设为线性条件均值模型，或只把它定义为 $Y$ 对截距和 $X$ 的最佳线性投影：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Simple linear regression admits two distinct readings: the equation below may specify a linear conditional mean, or it may define only the best linear projection of $Y$ on an intercept and $X$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i+u_i
$$

其中：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $\beta_0$ 是截距项。
- $\beta_1$ 是斜率；在线性条件均值读法下，它表示 $X$ 增加 1 单位时 $Y$ 的条件均值变化多少；在线性投影读法下，它表示总体最佳直线的斜率。
- $u_i$ 是随机误差项，包含模型没有解释的部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $\beta_0$ is the intercept.
- $\beta_1$ is the slope. Under the linear conditional-mean reading, it measures the change in the conditional mean of $Y$ when $X$ increases by one unit; under the projection reading, it is the slope of the population best-fitting line.
- $u_i$ is the random error, containing the part not explained by the model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若采用线性条件均值设定，总体条件期望写作：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the linear conditional-mean specification, the population conditional expectation is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(Y_i\mid X_i)=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i
$$

## 2.2. 如何从总体回归方程推到总体期望方程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2. From the population regression equation to the conditional-mean equation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Starting from
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i+u_i
$$

在给定 $X_i$ 的条件下取期望：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
take expectations conditional on $X_i$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(Y_i\mid X_i)=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i+E(u_i\mid X_i)
$$

如果满足零条件均值假设 $E(u_i\mid X_i)=0$，就得到：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the zero conditional-mean assumption $E(u_i\mid X_i)=0$ holds, this becomes:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(Y_i\mid X_i)=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i
$$

## 2.3. 零条件均值何时是建模假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3. When zero conditional mean is a modelling assumption*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若先把 $u_i$ 定义为 $Y_i-E(Y_i\mid X_i)$，零条件均值按定义成立，但条件均值未必是一条直线。若先规定线性式 $Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i+u_i$，再要求 $E(u_i\mid X_i)=0$，这就是“条件均值确实线性”的实质假设；任意非线性遗漏不能只靠改截距或斜率吸收。若只定义最佳线性投影，则要求的是较弱的总体正交 $E[X_i u_i]=0$，不是完整零条件均值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $u_i$ is first defined as $Y_i-E(Y_i\mid X_i)$, zero conditional mean holds by construction, but the conditional mean need not be linear. If one first posits $Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i+u_i$ and then requires $E(u_i\mid X_i)=0$, the substantive assumption is that the conditional mean is truly linear; an arbitrary nonlinear omission cannot be absorbed merely by changing the intercept or slope. If the equation defines only a best linear projection, the weaker population orthogonality condition $E[X_i u_i]=0$ applies instead of full zero conditional mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.4. 样本回归方程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4. Sample regression equation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总体参数 $\beta_0,\beta_1$ 不可直接观察，只能用样本估计：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The population parameters $\beta_0$ and $\beta_1$ cannot be observed directly and must be estimated from a sample:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat Y_i=\hat\beta_0+\hat\beta_1X_i
$$

残差为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The residual is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat u_i=Y_i-\hat Y_i
$$

样本回归方程写成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sample regression equation is written as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_i=\hat\beta_0+\hat\beta_1X_i+\hat u_i
$$

| 名称 | 写法 | 含义 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 总体回归方程 | $Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i+u_i$ | 线性条件均值设定或最佳线性投影分解；含义取决于怎样定义 $\beta$ 与 $u$ |
| 总体条件期望 | $E(Y_i\mid X_i)=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i$ | 只有线性条件均值设定成立时，才是给定 $X$ 后 $Y$ 的平均值 |
| 样本回归方程 | $Y_i=\hat\beta_0+\hat\beta_1X_i+\hat u_i$ | 用样本估计出来的关系 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Name | Expression | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Population regression equation | $Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i+u_i$ | A linear conditional-mean specification or best-linear-projection decomposition; its meaning depends on how $\beta$ and $u$ are defined |
| Population conditional expectation | $E(Y_i\mid X_i)=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i$ | The mean of $Y$ given $X$ only when the linear conditional-mean specification holds |
| Sample regression equation | $Y_i=\hat\beta_0+\hat\beta_1X_i+\hat u_i$ | The relationship estimated from the sample |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. [[普通最小二乘|最小二乘法]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Ordinary least squares (OLS)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. 推导
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1. Derivation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[普通最小二乘|OLS]] 的思想是选择一条直线，使所有样本点到拟合直线的竖直距离平方和最小：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
OLS chooses the line that minimizes the sum of squared vertical distances between the observations and the fitted line:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\min_{\hat\beta_0,\hat\beta_1} RSS
=\min_{\hat\beta_0,\hat\beta_1}\sum_{i=1}^n\hat u_i^2
=\min_{\hat\beta_0,\hat\beta_1}\sum_{i=1}^n(Y_i-\hat\beta_0-\hat\beta_1X_i)^2
$$

这里最小化的是竖直方向的误差平方，而不是点到直线的欧几里得距离。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The minimized quantity is the squared vertical error, not the Euclidean distance from a point to the line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] OLS 一句话
> OLS 选的是让残差平方和最小的直线；残差是竖直距离，不是点到直线的最短距离。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> OLS selects the line with the smallest residual sum of squares; residuals are vertical distances, not shortest point-to-line distances.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. 正规方程组
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2. Normal equations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $\hat\beta_0$ 和 $\hat\beta_1$ 分别求一阶条件：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the first-order conditions with respect to $\hat\beta_0$ and $\hat\beta_1$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\partial RSS}{\partial \hat\beta_0}
=-2\sum_{i=1}^n(Y_i-\hat\beta_0-\hat\beta_1X_i)=0
$$

$$
\frac{\partial RSS}{\partial \hat\beta_1}
=-2\sum_{i=1}^nX_i(Y_i-\hat\beta_0-\hat\beta_1X_i)=0
$$

因此：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sum_{i=1}^n\hat u_i=0,\qquad \sum_{i=1}^nX_i\hat u_i=0
$$

这说明残差与常数项、解释变量都正交。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus the residuals are orthogonal to both the constant and the explanatory variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.3. $\hat\beta_0$ 和 $\hat\beta_1$ 的解法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3. Solving for $\hat\beta_0$ and $\hat\beta_1$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由正规方程组可得：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The normal equations imply:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat\beta_1=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\bar X)(Y_i-\bar Y)}
{\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\bar X)^2}
=\frac{\widehat{\operatorname{Cov}}(X,Y)}{\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(X)}
$$

$$
\hat\beta_0=\bar Y-\hat\beta_1\bar X
$$

直觉是：斜率等于 $X$ 和 $Y$ 一起变化的程度，除以 $X$ 自己变化的程度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Intuitively, the slope is the extent to which $X$ and $Y$ move together divided by the extent to which $X$ itself varies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4. 高斯-马尔可夫定理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Gauss–Markov theorem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. 基本假定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1. Basic assumptions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.1.1. 线性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1.1. Linearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“线性”指参数线性，不一定要求变量本身只能以一次项出现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
"Linear" means linear in the parameters; the variables themselves need not appear only to the first power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $Y_t=\beta_0+\beta_1X_t+u_t$：参数线性。
- $Y_t=\beta_0+\beta_1X_t^2+u_t$：参数线性。
- $Y_t=\beta_0+\sqrt{\beta_1}X_t+u_t$：若直接以 $\beta_1$ 为参数，则不是参数线性；若令 $\gamma=\sqrt{\beta_1}$，才可改写为线性参数模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $Y_t=\beta_0+\beta_1X_t+u_t$: linear in parameters.
- $Y_t=\beta_0+\beta_1X_t^2+u_t$: linear in parameters.
- $Y_t=\beta_0+\sqrt{\beta_1}X_t+u_t$: not linear in the parameter $\beta_1$ as written; defining $\gamma=\sqrt{\beta_1}$ makes it a model linear in the new parameter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.1.2. 给定完整设计矩阵的零条件均值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1.2. Zero conditional mean given the full design matrix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限样本条件无偏使用：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite-sample conditional unbiasedness uses:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(u_i\mid X_1,\ldots,X_n)=0
$$

这里的 $X$ 是整个样本设计矩阵。独立随机横截面下，逐个观测的 $E(u_i\mid X_i)=0$ 可推出这条向量条件；时间序列中的严格外生则要求每期误差在给定全部时期解释变量后均值为零，确实强于只要求“当期不相关”。若解释变量与结构误差相关，就会出现[[内生性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $X$ is the full sample design matrix. In an independently sampled cross-section, observation-level conditions $E(u_i\mid X_i)=0$ imply this vector condition. Strict exogeneity in a time series instead requires every period's error to have zero mean conditional on regressors from all periods and is indeed stronger than contemporaneous uncorrelatedness. Correlation between a regressor and the structural error creates endogeneity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.1.3. 球形条件方差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1.3. Spherical conditional variance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在上面的零条件均值之外，经典 Gauss–Markov 效率比较还使用：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In addition to zero conditional mean, the classical Gauss–Markov efficiency comparison uses:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2I.
$$

它同时要求给定 $X$ 后各误差同方差，并且不同观测误差之间条件不相关。若方差不恒定，出现[[异方差]]；若误差项跨期相关，出现[[误差序列相关]]。这些失败不自动造成 OLS 有偏，但会破坏经典方差公式和经典 BLUE 效率结论，完整条件见[[协方差失效下的OLS]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This condition requires equal conditional error variances and zero conditional covariance across distinct observations. Nonconstant variance is [[异方差|heteroskedasticity]], while correlation across periods is [[误差序列相关|serial error dependence]]. These failures do not automatically bias OLS, but they invalidate the classical variance formula and the classical BLUE efficiency conclusion; see [[协方差失效下的OLS|OLS under nonspherical errors]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 高斯-马尔可夫假设怎么记
> 参数线性规定模型形式，满列秩保证系数唯一，给定 $X$ 的零条件均值保证条件无偏，球形条件方差使经典方差公式成立并让 OLS 在经典线性无偏类中有效率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Linearity in parameters specifies the model form, full column rank gives unique coefficients, zero conditional mean given $X$ gives conditional unbiasedness, and spherical conditional variance validates the classical variance formula and makes OLS efficient within the classical linear-unbiased class.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2. [[经典 Gauss–Markov 定理|高斯-马尔可夫定理内容]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2. Statement of the Gauss–Markov theorem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在经典线性回归假设下，OLS 是 BLUE，即最佳线性无偏估计量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the classical linear-regression assumptions, OLS is BLUE: the best linear unbiased estimator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.2.1. 线性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.1. Linearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OLS 估计量可以写成 $Y_i$ 的线性组合，所以它属于线性估计量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An OLS estimator can be written as a linear combination of the $Y_i$, so it is a linear estimator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.2.2. 无偏性与一致性回答不同问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.2. Unbiasedness and consistency answer different questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经典定理使用的有限样本无偏性是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The finite-sample unbiasedness used by the classical theorem is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(\hat\beta_1)=\beta_1
$$

另行加入总体正交、识别和样本矩收敛条件后，才有大样本一致性：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With separate population-orthogonality, identification, and sample-moment convergence conditions, large-sample consistency is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{plim}\hat\beta_1=\beta_1
$$

无偏性强调重复抽样平均正确；一致性强调样本量趋于无穷时估计量收敛到目标。后者不是经典 Gauss–Markov 方差比较本身额外证明的结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unbiasedness means the estimator is correct on average across repeated samples. Consistency means it converges to its target as the sample size grows. The latter is not an additional conclusion of the classical Gauss–Markov variance comparison itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.2.3. 最优性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.3. Optimality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在课程采用的经典版本中，OLS 在所有线性无偏估计量中具有最小条件方差；现代版本的比较范围见链接原子中的边界说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the classical version used by this course, OLS has the smallest conditional variance among linear unbiased estimators. See the linked atom for the boundary introduced by modern formulations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.3. 拟合优度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3. Goodness of fit*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.3.1. 思想
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3.1. Idea*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拟合优度衡量模型解释了 $Y$ 总波动中的多少。核心分解是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Goodness of fit measures how much of the total variation in $Y$ the model explains. The central decomposition is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
TSS=ESS+RSS
$$

其中：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $TSS=\sum(Y_i-\bar Y)^2$：总平方和。
- $ESS=\sum(\hat Y_i-\bar Y)^2$：解释平方和。
- $RSS=\sum(Y_i-\hat Y_i)^2$：残差平方和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $TSS=\sum(Y_i-\bar Y)^2$: total sum of squares.
- $ESS=\sum(\hat Y_i-\bar Y)^2$: explained sum of squares.
- $RSS=\sum(Y_i-\hat Y_i)^2$: residual sum of squares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.3.2. 推导
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3.2. Derivation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_i-\bar Y=(\hat Y_i-\bar Y)+\hat u_i
$$

平方并加总：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
squaring and summing gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sum(Y_i-\bar Y)^2
=\sum(\hat Y_i-\bar Y)^2+\sum\hat u_i^2
+2\sum(\hat Y_i-\bar Y)\hat u_i
$$

由正规方程组，交叉项为 0，所以得到 $TSS=ESS+RSS$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The normal equations make the cross-product term zero, yielding $TSS=ESS+RSS$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判定系数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficient of determination is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
R^2=\frac{ESS}{TSS}=1-\frac{RSS}{TSS}
$$

### 4.4.1. 参数推导
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.4.1. Test statistics and standard errors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 检验对象 | 常见原假设 | 使用统计量 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 单个斜率 | $H_0:\beta_j=0$ | t 统计量 |
| 多个参数 | $H_0:\beta_1=\beta_2=\cdots=0$ | F 统计量 |
| 全部斜率 | $H_0:\beta_1=\cdots=\beta_k=0$ | F 统计量 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Object of the test | Common null hypothesis | Statistic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One slope | $H_0:\beta_j=0$ | $t$ statistic |
| Several parameters | $H_0:\beta_1=\beta_2=\cdots=0$ | $F$ statistic |
| All slopes | $H_0:\beta_1=\cdots=\beta_k=0$ | $F$ statistic |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要检验斜率是否等于某个理论值 $\beta_1^0$，构造：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To test whether the slope equals a theoretical value $\beta_1^0$, construct:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
t=\frac{\hat\beta_1-\beta_1^0}{se(\hat\beta_1)}
$$

在经典假设下：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the classical assumptions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
se(\hat\beta_1)=\sqrt{\frac{\hat\sigma^2}{\sum(X_i-\bar X)^2}}
$$

$$
\hat\sigma^2=\frac{RSS}{n-2}
$$

截距的标准误为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The standard error of the intercept is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
se(\hat\beta_0)=
\sqrt{\hat\sigma^2\left(\frac{1}{n}+\frac{\bar X^2}{\sum(X_i-\bar X)^2}\right)}
$$

### 4.4.2. 参数检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.4.2. Parameter tests*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (1) [[回归t检验|t 检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) $t$ test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见原假设是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common null hypothesis is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_0:\beta_1=0,\qquad H_1:\beta_1\ne0
$$

若 $|t|$ 大于相匹配的临界值，或 $p$ 值小于预先给定的显著性水平，就拒绝原假设。准确的结论是：在当前模型、标准误口径与参考分布下，数据与 $\beta_1=0$ 不相容；这支持一个统计上可区分于零的线性关系，但不自动证明因果影响或实际重要性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $|t|$ exceeds its matching critical value, or the $p$-value falls below the prespecified significance level, reject the null. Precisely, the data are incompatible with $\beta_1=0$ under the stated model, covariance estimator, and reference distribution. This supports a nonzero linear association, not automatically a causal or practically important effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (2) [[p值条件解释|p 值]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) $p$-value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$p$ 值表示：在原假设和用于校准检验的模型条件成立时，得到当前统计量或更极端统计量的尾概率。$p$ 值越小，数据与这套“原假设 + 模型”越不相容；它不是原假设为真的概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $p$-value is the tail probability, under the null and the model used to calibrate the test, of obtaining the observed statistic or one more extreme. A smaller value means less compatibility with that null-plus-model combination; it is not the probability that the null is true.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] 读检验结果的顺序
> 先看原假设是什么，再看统计量或 $p$ 值，最后翻译成经济语言。不要只写“显著”两个字。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Identify the null hypothesis first, then inspect the statistic or $p$-value, and finally translate the result into economic language. Do not stop at the word "significant."
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.5. 例：中国的消费函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.5. Example: China's consumption function*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

消费函数可写成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The consumption function can be written as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_t=\beta_0+\beta_1Y_t+u_t
$$

若估计得到 $\hat\beta_1=0.8$，则解释为：收入每增加 1 单位，消费平均增加 0.8 单位。此时 $\hat\beta_1$ 就是[[一元 OLS 斜率|边际消费倾向]]的估计值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\hat\beta_1=0.8$, the interpretation is that a one-unit increase in income is associated with an average increase of 0.8 units in consumption. Here $\hat\beta_1$ estimates the marginal propensity to consume.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解释结果时要同时看：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When interpreting the result, also examine:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 符号是否符合理论。
- 系数是否显著。
- $R^2$ 在样本内描述了多少拟合变异，以及残差是否仍显示系统结构。
- 是否存在异方差、自相关或内生性问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- whether the sign agrees with theory;
- whether the coefficient is significant;
- how much in-sample fitted variation $R^2$ describes and whether residuals still show systematic structure; and
- whether heteroskedasticity, autocorrelation, or endogeneity is present.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.6. [[置信区间覆盖率|区间估计]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.6. Interval estimation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

斜率参数的置信区间为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The confidence interval for the slope is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat\beta_1\pm t_{1-\alpha/2,n-2}\cdot se(\hat\beta_1)
$$

它表达的是：在重复抽样意义下，用这种方法构造的区间有 $1-\alpha$ 的比例会覆盖真实参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In repeated sampling, a proportion $1-\alpha$ of intervals constructed by this procedure will cover the true parameter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一对应要求 standard error、临界值和参考分布来自同一套假设。把经典 $t$ 临界值与不相匹配的 robust standard error 拼在一起，并不会自动保留覆盖率。单个参数的推断到这里完成；多个限制继续读 [[03_多元线性回归#3.2. 多参数检验：F 检验|多参数 F 检验]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This interpretation requires the standard error, critical value, and reference distribution to come from one coherent set of assumptions. Combining a classical t critical value with a mismatched robust standard error does not automatically preserve coverage. Inference for one parameter ends here; continue to the [[03_多元线性回归#3.2. 多参数检验：F 检验|joint F test]] for several restrictions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.7. MC 仿真
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.7. Monte Carlo simulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Monte Carlo 仿真的基本思想是重复生成样本、重复估计模型，然后观察估计量的分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The basic idea of Monte Carlo simulation is to generate samples repeatedly, re-estimate the model, and examine the resulting sampling distribution of the estimator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用于一元回归时，可以这样做：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a simple regression, the procedure can be:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 设定真实模型 $Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i+u_i$。
2. 随机生成 $X_i$ 和 $u_i$。
3. 用 OLS 估计 $\hat\beta_0,\hat\beta_1$。
4. 重复很多次。
5. 观察估计量是否围绕真值、方差大小如何、检验是否有效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Specify the true model $Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i+u_i$.<br>
**2.** Generate random values of $X_i$ and $u_i$.<br>
**3.** Estimate $\hat\beta_0$ and $\hat\beta_1$ by OLS.<br>
**4.** Repeat many times.<br>
**5.** Examine whether the estimates are centered on the true values, how large their variances are, and whether the tests have the intended behavior.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
