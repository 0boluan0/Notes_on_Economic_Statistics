# 双重差分法（DID）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Difference-in-Differences (DID)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[双重差分法]]不是“带两组固定效应的回归”的别名。它是一种反事实研究设计：先说明谁受到什么处理、谁在每个时期仍能代表未处理状态，再用对照组的同期变化补出处理组看不见的未处理变化。[[双重差分法（DID）.canvas|主题 Canvas]]负责展示全局关系；本章负责把定义、识别、估计和诊断连成一条可连续阅读的路径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[双重差分法|Difference-in-differences]] is not another name for a regression with two sets of fixed effects. It is a counterfactual research design: define who receives which treatment, identify who can still represent the untreated state in each period, and use the comparison group's contemporaneous change to recover the treated group's missing untreated change. The [[双重差分法（DID）.canvas|topic Canvas]] displays the global relationships; this chapter connects definition, identification, estimation, and diagnosis into one continuous path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. DID 补的是哪一个看不见的量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Which missing quantity DID is meant to recover*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $G=1$ 表示处理组，$G=0$ 表示对照组；$t=0$ 是处理前，$t=1$ 是处理后。处理组在处理后的目标通常是

$$
ATT=E\bigl[Y_1(1)-Y_1(0)\mid G=1\bigr].
$$

我们观察到 $E[Y_1(1)\mid G=1]$，却观察不到同一批处理单位在没有处理时的 $E[Y_1(0)\mid G=1]$。这正是[[反事实不可同时观察|因果问题中缺失的反事实]]。DID 的办法不是凭模型猜一个处理后水平，而是借用对照组从 $t=0$ 到 $t=1$ 的实际变化，推断处理组若未处理本会怎样变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $G=1$ denote the treated group and $G=0$ the comparison group; $t=0$ is before treatment and $t=1$ is after treatment. The usual target is the ATT shown above. We observe $E[Y_1(1)\mid G=1]$ but not $E[Y_1(0)\mid G=1]$ for those same treated units. This is the [[反事实不可同时观察|missing counterfactual in a causal question]]. DID does not ask a model to guess a post-treatment level from scratch. It borrows the comparison group's observed change from $t=0$ to $t=1$ to infer how the treated group would have changed without treatment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

四格 DID 写成

$$
\widehat{DID}
=\bigl(\bar Y_{1,1}-\bar Y_{1,0}\bigr)
-\bigl(\bar Y_{0,1}-\bar Y_{0,0}\bigr).
$$

第一个括号是处理组的前后差，但它同时包含政策作用和所有共同时间变化；第二个括号估计共同时间变化。再差一次，意图把后者扣掉。这就是[[双重差分法|双重差分法的定义性机制]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first bracket is the treated group's before-after change, but it contains both the treatment effect and common time movements. The second bracket estimates the common movement. Differencing once more is intended to remove it. This is the [[双重差分法|defining mechanism of DID]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，处理组均值从 20 升到 26，对照组从 30 升到 34。处理组多了 6，但对照组同期也多了 4，因此 DID 为 $6-4=2$。这里不要求两组起点一样；它要求对照组的 4 能代表处理组未处理时本会发生的变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the treated mean rises from 20 to 26 while the comparison mean rises from 30 to 34. The treated group gains 6, but the comparison group gains 4 over the same period, so DID is $6-4=2$. Equal starting levels are unnecessary. What matters is whether the comparison group's change of 4 represents the untreated change the treated group would otherwise have experienced.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 四格差何时等于 ATT
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. When the four-cell contrast equals the ATT*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

四个均值永远可以相减；把差值称为因果效应，则需要一组关于反事实的条件。最核心的是[[平行趋势]]：若没有处理，两组的平均未处理潜在结果会有相同变化，

$$
E[Y_1(0)-Y_0(0)\mid G=1]
=E[Y_1(0)-Y_0(0)\mid G=0].
$$

它约束的是 $Y(0)$ 的变化，不是观测结果的水平。处理组可以一直比对照组高 40；只要无处理时两组本会同增 5，这个固定水平差不会破坏平行趋势。相反，某一处理前时点均值相同，也不能保证未来未处理变化可比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four means can always be subtracted; calling the contrast causal requires assumptions about the counterfactual. The central condition is [[平行趋势|parallel trends]]: absent treatment, the groups' average untreated potential outcomes would have changed by the same amount. It constrains changes in $Y(0)$, not observed outcome levels. The treated group may remain 40 units above the comparison group; a fixed level gap is compatible with parallel untreated changes. Equal means at one pretreatment date do not guarantee comparable future untreated changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二项条件是[[DID无预期条件|无预期]]。如果政策九月生效，但企业三月得知消息后四月已经招聘，四月至八月对就业而言就不再是纯粹的处理前期。有效处理时点应由制度事实和可能反应机制决定，不能只抄法规生效日期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second condition is [[DID无预期条件|no anticipation]]. If a policy takes effect in September but firms learn about it in March and begin hiring in April, April through August are no longer untreated periods for employment. Effective treatment timing must follow institutional facts and plausible behavioural responses, not merely the statutory implementation date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要保证结果、处理与总体口径稳定：

- 处理必须定义清楚，处理组在处理后确实处于所声称的状态；
- 对照组不能被政策间接改变，见[[DID 的外溢污染|外溢污染]]；
- 前后四格应代表同一个目标总体，见[[DID 的样本构成变化|样本构成变化]]；
- 测量规则和同时发生的组别特定冲击不能恰好在处理时点改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Treatment must be well defined, and the treated group must actually enter the stated exposure.
- The comparison group must not be indirectly changed by the policy; see [[DID 的外溢污染|spillover contamination]].
- The four cells must represent a stable target population; see [[DID 的样本构成变化|changes in sample composition]].
- Measurement rules and group-specific concurrent shocks must not change exactly at treatment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这些条件下，对照组的实际变化可以代替处理组缺失的未处理变化：

$$
E[Y_1(0)\mid G=1]
=E[Y_0(0)\mid G=1]
+\Bigl(E[Y_1(0)\mid G=0]-E[Y_0(0)\mid G=0]\Bigr).
$$

将它代回 ATT，便得到四格 DID。[[2×2 DID 识别 ATT]]记录的正是这一步识别；它通常得到的是处理组平均效应，而不是对所有人的 ATE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under these conditions, the comparison group's observed change can replace the treated group's missing untreated change. Substituting that expression into the ATT yields the four-cell DID. [[2×2 DID 识别 ATT|The $2\times2$ ATT identification result]] records precisely this step. It usually identifies the average effect for the treated group, not the ATE for everyone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 回归只负责计算，识别必须另行成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Regression computes the contrast; identification remains separate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最简单的饱和回归是

$$
Y_{it}=\alpha+\gamma G_i+\lambda Post_t
+\tau(G_i\times Post_t)+u_{it}.
$$

四格条件均值分别是 $\alpha$、$\alpha+\gamma$、$\alpha+\lambda$ 与 $\alpha+\gamma+\lambda+\tau$，所以交互项 $\tau$ 恰好等于第二个差分。[[2×2 DID回归等价]]是代数命题；[[2×2 DID 识别 ATT]]是因果命题。前者即使在错误对照组上也成立，后者不会。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the saturated regression above, the four conditional means are $\alpha$, $\alpha+\gamma$, $\alpha+\lambda$, and $\alpha+\gamma+\lambda+\tau$, so the interaction coefficient $\tau$ equals the second difference exactly. [[2×2 DID回归等价|Regression equivalence]] is an algebraic claim; [[2×2 DID 识别 ATT|identification of the ATT]] is a causal claim. The former still holds with a bad comparison group. The latter does not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面板数据跟踪同一单位，重复截面每期抽取新单位；二者都能计算 DID，却承担不同的样本论证。面板会遇到退出、缺失和存活选择；重复截面需要每个组—时期样本持续代表同一总体。加入单位固定效应和时间固定效应不会自动修复这些问题，也不会吸收只影响某一组的同期冲击。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Panel data follow the same units, whereas repeated cross-sections draw new units each period. Both can compute DID, but their sampling arguments differ. Panels face attrition, missingness, and survival selection. Repeated cross-sections require each group-period sample to continue representing the same population. Unit and time fixed effects do not automatically repair these problems or absorb shocks confined to one group.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

推断也与识别分开。政策常在州、学校或企业层面分配，结果又会在同一单位内跨期相关。标准误应覆盖共同处理分配或共同冲击的层级，而不是机械地按数据最细的行聚类；独立 cluster 很少时，默认大样本 $p$ 值仍可能严重失真。具体边界复用[[聚类标准误边界]]，而不是在 DID 卡片里维护另一套标准误规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inference is also separate from identification. Policies are often assigned at the state, school, or firm level, while outcomes remain serially correlated within units. Standard errors should cover the level of shared assignment or shocks rather than mechanically clustering by the finest row. With few independent clusters, default large-sample $p$-values may still be badly distorted. The exact boundary is shared through [[聚类标准误边界|the clustered-standard-error atom]] rather than duplicated inside the DID topic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 协变量、处理前趋势与事件研究分别回答什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. What covariates, pretreatment trends, and event studies each answer*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若年龄、行业或处理前结果历史决定自然趋势，而这些特征在两组中的分布不同，可以把无条件平行趋势改成处理前 $X$ 下的条件平行趋势。但[[DID 协变量调整|协变量调整]]会改变比较对象、权重和共同支持区域；它不是“多放变量总会更稳”。处理后收入、就业或企业规模可能已受政策影响，把它们作为控制会截断作用路径或引入选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If age, industry, or pretreatment outcome history determines natural trends and is distributed differently across groups, unconditional parallel trends may be replaced with parallel trends conditional on pretreatment $X$. But [[DID 协变量调整|covariate adjustment]] changes the comparisons, weights, and overlap population; it is not a rule that more controls are always safer. Post-treatment income, employment, or firm size may already respond to the policy, so controlling for them can block causal pathways or induce selection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结果尺度也是识别的一部分。水平值上相同的绝对变化，在对数尺度上可能对应不同增长率；对数上平行也不推出水平值平行。[[DID结果尺度]]要求先决定要解释的是结果单位、增长率还是其他变换上的效应，再为该尺度上的未处理趋势提供机制理由。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The outcome scale is also part of identification. Equal absolute changes in levels may imply different growth rates in logs, while parallel log changes do not imply parallel level changes. [[DID结果尺度|The DID outcome-scale boundary]] requires the researcher to decide whether the target concerns outcome units, growth rates, or another transformation, then justify untreated trends on that scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

处理前趋势图可以暴露明显不相容，但无法观察政策之后处理组的 $Y(0)$。因此，[[前趋势检验边界|pretrend 系数未显著]]只表示样本没有检测到所检验的处理前差异；它也可能反映噪声大、处理组少或处理前窗口太短。若只在预检“通过”后保留规格，还会对看起来平行的样本条件化，从而扭曲估计与覆盖率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pretreatment plots can expose clear incompatibility, but they cannot observe the treated group's post-policy $Y(0)$. Thus [[前趋势检验边界|insignificant pretrend coefficients]] only mean that the sample did not detect the tested prior differences; noise, few treated groups, or a short pretreatment window may also explain the result. Retaining a specification only after it passes a pretest conditions on looking parallel and can distort both estimates and coverage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在多期数据中，令 $G_i$ 表示单位 $i$ 的首次处理期。事件研究常写成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In multi-period data, let $G_i$ denote unit $i$'s first treatment period. An event-study regression is commonly written as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_{it}=\alpha_i+\lambda_t+
\sum_{k\ne -1}\beta_k\mathbf 1\{t-G_i=k\}+u_{it},
$$

其中 $k$ 是相对处理时间。每个 $\beta_k$ 都相对于省略期存在，并取决于窗口、端点是否合并、哪些 cohort 在该 $k$ 有数据，以及谁在那一期仍能作对照。[[事件研究系数语境]]要求读图时把这些比较写出来；动态曲线不是脱离规格而存在的绝对效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the event-study equation above, $k$ is time relative to treatment. Each $\beta_k$ exists relative to an omitted period and depends on the event window, endpoint binning, which cohorts contribute at that $k$, and who can still serve as a control then. [[事件研究系数语境|The event-study interpretation atom]] therefore requires those comparisons to be stated explicitly. A dynamic curve is not an absolute effect independent of its specification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无预期和平行趋势也不能被混成一句“没有 pretrend”。提前反应意味着处理实际开始得更早；对照组不能代表处理组则威胁平行趋势。前者要重画处理时间和基准窗口，后者要重审对照机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No anticipation and parallel trends should not be collapsed into the phrase “no pretrend.” Anticipatory behaviour means treatment effectively began earlier. A comparison group that cannot represent the treated group threatens parallel trends. The former changes treatment timing and the reference window; the latter requires a new comparison argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 处理时间错开后，先恢复可解释的小效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Under staggered timing, recover interpretable building blocks first*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多期数据中，不同单位常在不同日历时间进入处理。标准 cohort 记法用 $G_i=g$ 表示单位首次在 $g$ 期处理；这要求处理为二元、进入后持续，而且首次进入时点足以编码后续暴露。若剂量变化或单位反复进出，必须定义完整处理路径；[[错位 DID 的吸收处理条件]]划清了这个作用域。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In multi-period data, units often adopt treatment in different calendar periods. The canonical cohort notation $G_i=g$ means that unit $i$ is first treated at $g$. It requires binary, absorbing treatment whose later exposure path is encoded by first adoption. Changing doses or repeated entry and exit require the full treatment history; [[错位 DID 的吸收处理条件|the absorbing-treatment boundary]] marks this scope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

传统静态 TWFE

$$
Y_{it}=\alpha_i+\lambda_t+\beta D_{it}+u_{it}
$$

在错位处理下会组合许多两组两期 DID，其中一些把早已处理的 cohort 当作后来处理 cohort 的对照。Goodman–Bacon 分解中的组成 $2\times2$ 权重本身非负，但某些组成比较减去了早处理组仍在变化的处理效应；若进一步把最终系数展开到底层异质效应，便可能出现负权重与符号反转。[[错位 DID 的已处理对照]]负责这个精确边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under staggered adoption, the conventional static TWFE regression combines many two-group/two-period DIDs, some using an already-treated cohort as the control for a later-treated cohort. The component $2\times2$ weights in the Goodman–Bacon decomposition are non-negative, but some components subtract the evolving effect of an early-treated group. Expanding the final coefficient over underlying heterogeneous effects can then produce negative weights and sign reversals. [[错位 DID 的已处理对照|The already-treated-control atom]] records this precise boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现代 DID 的共同原则是把识别、聚合与估计分开。先定义

$$
ATT(g,t)=E\bigl[Y_t(g)-Y_t(0)\mid G=g\bigr],
$$

再为每个 $(g,t)$ 指定可信的 never-treated 或当时尚未处理的 not-yet-treated 对照。最后才按研究问题聚合：按 cohort 回答哪些处理组经历了什么，按暴露时长回答处理后第几期的动态，按日历时间回答某一年已处理单位的平均效应。[[Group-time ATT 聚合]]把这些不同问题保留下来，而不是先压成一个不透明的总系数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The common principle of modern DID is to separate identification, aggregation, and estimation. Define $ATT(g,t)$ first, then assign credible never-treated or currently not-yet-treated controls to each $(g,t)$. Aggregate only afterward for the substantive question: by cohort for what different treated groups experienced, by exposure length for treatment dynamics, or by calendar time for the average effect among units treated in a given year. [[Group-time ATT 聚合|Group-time ATT aggregation]] preserves these distinct questions instead of immediately compressing them into an opaque coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长暴露期通常只由更早处理且仍留在样本中的 cohort 提供。若事件时间 $e=1$ 与 $e=5$ 的 cohort 构成不同，两点之差同时反映动态效应和组别构成。要比较同一批 cohort，就限制在共同事件窗口并重新说明目标总体。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Long exposure horizons are usually supported only by cohorts treated early enough and still observed. If different cohorts contribute at event times $e=1$ and $e=5$, their difference mixes treatment dynamics with cohort composition. Comparing the same cohorts requires a common event window and an explicit statement of the retained target population.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 设计边界不是稳健性清单
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Design boundaries are not a robustness checklist*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面几类问题需要先改变 estimand 或设计，而不是换一条回归命令：

- 政策改变谁会被观察：区分原始单位效应与当前总体效应，见[[DID 的样本构成变化]]；
- 政策越过行政、市场或网络边界：区分直接、外溢和总效应，见[[DID 的外溢污染]]；
- 处理是连续剂量或可逆状态：不能只按首次处理 cohort 描述；
- 第三个比较维度要消除残余偏差：[[DDD 的偏差消除条件|DDD]]要求两个组成 DID 在无处理时具有相同偏差，而不是“多差一次自然更可信”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- If treatment changes who remains observed, distinguish effects on original units from effects on the current population; see [[DID 的样本构成变化|sample composition]].
- If treatment crosses geographic, market, or network boundaries, distinguish direct, spillover, and total effects; see [[DID 的外溢污染|interference and spillovers]].
- Continuous doses and reversible treatment cannot be represented solely by first-adoption cohorts.
- If a third comparison dimension is intended to remove residual bias, [[DDD 的偏差消除条件|DDD]] requires the two component DIDs to have the same untreated bias; an extra difference is not inherently more credible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

替代对照、placebo 和窗口变化只有在针对具体失效机制时才有信息。一个理论上也会受政策影响的结果不是有效 placebo；任意换窗口后系数相似也不能证明无预期。每项检查都应先说：若哪一种威胁存在，它为什么应该改变这个结果？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Alternative controls, placebos, and window changes are informative only when they target a specific failure mechanism. An outcome that theory says should also respond to treatment is not a valid placebo. Similar estimates after arbitrary window changes do not prove no anticipation. Each check should first state which threat would make its result change and why.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 一条可执行的 DID 顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. An executable DID sequence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **定义处理。** 写清公告、生效、可能预期、剂量、退出和外溢。
2. **定义 estimand。** 说明目标是 2×2 ATT、$ATT(g,t)$、暴露时长动态、直接效应还是总效应。
3. **逐期指定对照。** 让读者看见每个效应是谁与谁的比较，以及对照为何尚未受影响。
4. **论证反事实。** 用制度、共同冲击、样本构成和处理前结果支持平行趋势；图和检验只是其中的证据。
5. **再选估计量。** 简单设计可用四格或饱和回归；错位异质效应采用 cohort-time 相容的方法。
6. **按依赖结构推断。** 聚类层级对应处理分配与共同冲击；少簇时使用有适用条件的小样本或设计相容方法。
7. **透明报告。** 展示原始趋势、处理 cohort、事件基准期、样本、权重、聚合规则、区间与不可识别范围。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. **Define treatment.** State announcement, implementation, anticipation, dose, exit, and spillover possibilities.
2. **Define the estimand.** Specify a $2\times2$ ATT, $ATT(g,t)$, exposure-length dynamics, a direct effect, or a total effect.
3. **Name controls period by period.** Show who is compared with whom for every effect and why the controls remain unaffected then.
4. **Defend the counterfactual.** Use institutions, common shocks, composition, and pretreatment outcomes to support parallel trends; plots and tests are only part of that evidence.
5. **Then choose the estimator.** Four-cell or saturated regression can implement a simple design; staggered heterogeneous effects need a cohort-time-compatible method.
6. **Match inference to dependence.** Clustering follows treatment assignment and shared shocks; few clusters require a justified small-sample or design-compatible method.
7. **Report transparently.** Show raw trends, treatment cohorts, event reference periods, samples, weights, aggregation rules, intervals, and what remains unidentified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条顺序由[[DID 估计目标与对照组]]收束。真正的验收问题不是“有没有跑出显著系数”，而是：目标效应是否明确、缺失反事实由谁补、这个替代为何可信、估计与推断是否忠实执行了同一个设计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[DID 估计目标与对照组|The DID workflow atom]] closes the sequence. The acceptance question is not whether a significant coefficient appeared. It is whether the target effect is explicit, who supplies the missing counterfactual is visible, why that substitution is credible, and whether estimation and inference faithfully implement the same design.

## 8. 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Card & Krueger (1994), [*Minimum Wages and Employment*](https://eml.berkeley.edu/~card/papers/njmin-aer.pdf)：支持新泽西—宾夕法尼亚前后比较的经典应用语境；不把其具体经验结论当作 DID 一般定理。
- Callaway & Sant’Anna (2021), [*Difference-in-Differences with Multiple Time Periods*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2020.12.001)：支持潜在结果定义、有限预期、条件平行趋势、never-treated/not-yet-treated 对照、$ATT(g,t)$ 与透明聚合。
- Goodman-Bacon (2021), [*Difference-in-Differences with Variation in Treatment Timing*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2021.03.014)：支持 2×2 回归等价、错位处理下静态 TWFE 的组成比较与时间异质效应边界。
- Sun & Abraham (2021), [*Estimating Dynamic Treatment Effects in Event Studies with Heterogeneous Treatment Effects*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2020.09.006)：支持传统动态 TWFE 的跨事件期污染、表面预趋势与 cohort-event-time 估计。
- Roth (2022), [*Pretest with Caution*](https://doi.org/10.1257/aeri.20210236)：支持处理前趋势检验的低功效和预检条件化造成的估计、覆盖率失真。
- Roth & Sant’Anna (2023), [*When Is Parallel Trends Sensitive to Functional Form?*](https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA19402)：支持水平、对数与其他结果变换会改变平行趋势假设和目标效应，以及变换不变性需要更强分布条件。
- Bertrand, Duflo & Mullainathan (2004), [*How Much Should We Trust Differences-in-Differences Estimates?*](https://doi.org/10.1162/003355304772839588)：支持多期 DID 中序列相关使常规标准误过小的推断边界。
- Olden & Møen (2022), [*The Triple Difference Estimator*](https://doi.org/10.1093/ectj/utac010)：支持 DDD 作为两个 DID 之差及“两个偏差相同”而非“两套 DID 分别无偏”的识别条件。
- Baker et al. (2026), [*Difference-in-Differences Designs: A Practitioner’s Guide*](https://doi.org/10.1257/jel.20251650)：交叉核验从设计类型与 estimand 出发选择比较、协变量、权重和多期估计量的总体路径。
