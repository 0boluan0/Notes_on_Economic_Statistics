## 1. [[联立方程模型]]：共同决定如何产生内生性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. How joint determination creates endogeneity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

联立方程模型中，多个经济变量同时决定彼此。此时某些解释变量不再是外生给定的，而是由系统内其他方程共同决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simultaneous-equations model, several economic variables determine one another at the same time. Some regressors are therefore not given exogenously but are jointly determined by other equations in the system.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 本章核心
> 内生性的问题不是“变量重要”，而是“解释变量里混进了误差项的信息”，所以 OLS 不再能分离真实影响。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Endogeneity is not about whether a variable is important. It means that a regressor contains information from the error term, so OLS can no longer isolate the underlying effect.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如凯恩斯收入决定模型：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, consider a Keynesian income-determination model:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_t&=\alpha_0+\alpha_1Y_t+u_{1t}\\
I_t&=\beta_0+\beta_1r_t+u_{2t}\\
Y_t&=C_t+I_t+G_t
\end{aligned}
$$

消费 $C_t$ 取决于收入 $Y_t$，但收入 $Y_t$ 又由消费、投资和政府支出共同决定。这种同时决定会使 OLS 面临内生性问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consumption $C_t$ depends on income $Y_t$, while income is itself jointly determined by consumption, investment, and government spending. This simultaneous determination creates endogeneity for OLS.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.1. 内生性问题的产生
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1. How endogeneity arises*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在联立方程中，解释变量可能与误差项相关。例如 $Y_t$ 同时受到消费方程误差 $u_{1t}$ 的影响，因此在消费方程中用 $Y_t$ 解释 $C_t$ 时，$Y_t$ 与 $u_{1t}$ 相关，OLS 不再一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simultaneous system, a regressor may be correlated with the error. For example, $Y_t$ is partly affected by the consumption-equation shock $u_{1t}$. When $Y_t$ is used to explain $C_t$ in that equation, $Y_t$ is correlated with $u_{1t}$ and OLS is no longer consistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同时性只是内生性的一种来源。遗漏的共同原因会产生[[遗漏变量偏差]]；测量误差是否破坏 OLS 则取决于误差落在结果还是解释变量上，见[[测量误差位置]]。这些机制都可能使目标结构矩失败，但不能因为结论同为“OLS 不一致”就共用同一套修复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Simultaneity is only one source of endogeneity. An omitted common cause can generate [[遗漏变量偏差|omitted-variable bias]], while the consequences of measurement error depend on whether it enters the outcome or a regressor; see [[测量误差位置|measurement-error location]]. These mechanisms can all invalidate a structural moment, but a shared symptom—OLS inconsistency—does not imply a shared remedy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. [[内生性|内生性怎样污染多元 OLS]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. How endogeneity contaminates multivariate OLS*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1. 偏误和残差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Bias and the error term*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑模型：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the model:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y=X_1\beta_1+X_2\beta_2+u
$$

如果 $X_2$ 内生，即 $\operatorname{Cov}(X_2,u)\ne0$，则 $\hat\beta_2$ 有偏且不一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $X_2$ is endogenous, so that $\operatorname{Cov}(X_2,u)\ne0$, then $\hat\beta_2$ is biased and inconsistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $X_1$ 与 $X_2$ 相关，内生性通常还可能传导到 $\hat\beta_1$，使原本外生的 $X_1$ 的 OLS 系数也偏离目标；具体大小和方向取决于回归量的联合协方差结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $X_1$ and $X_2$ are correlated, the contamination can also carry over to $\hat\beta_1$ even when $X_1$ itself is exogenous. Its magnitude and direction depend on the regressors' joint covariance structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.2. 外生变量的系数估计量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2. Coefficients on exogenous variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使某个变量本身外生，只要它与内生变量相关，OLS 分解出来的“独立变化”也会混入误差项相关部分。因此内生性不是只污染一个系数，而可能影响整个模型解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even if one variable is itself exogenous, correlation with an endogenous regressor can make its OLS “independent variation” inherit variation related to the error. Endogeneity may therefore contaminate interpretation of the whole model, not just one coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. [[识别与估计|识别先于估计]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Identification precedes estimation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.1. 识别含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1. What identification means*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 阶数检查 | 排除工具数量 | 仅凭数量能得到的结论 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 少于所需数量 | 少于内生变量数量 | 阶条件失败，结构参数不能由这些工具识别 |
| 数量恰好 | 等于内生变量数量 | 只通过必要的计数条件；仍须秩条件与工具有效性 |
| 数量有余 | 多于内生变量数量 | 只有秩条件与工具有效性成立时，才构成可估计的过度识别模型 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Order check | Number of excluded instruments | What counting alone establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Too few | Fewer than the number of endogenous regressors | The order condition fails, so these instruments cannot identify the structural parameters |
| Equal count | Equal to the number of endogenous regressors | Only the necessary count passes; rank and instrument validity are still required |
| Extra count | More than the number of endogenous regressors | An estimable overidentified model exists only if rank and instrument validity also hold |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别问题问的是：仅凭可观测数据，能不能唯一恢复结构方程中的参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identification asks whether the parameters of a structural equation can be recovered uniquely from observable data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3.1.1. 解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1.1. Interpretation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果一个方程不能识别，即使样本无限大，也无法从数据中估计出它的结构参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an equation is not identified, even an infinite sample cannot recover its structural parameters from the data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.2. 引入[[工具变量]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2. Introducing instrumental variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

工具变量用于从解释变量中提取“与误差项无关”的那部分变动。一个合格工具变量 $Z$ 需要满足：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An instrument extracts the part of a regressor's variation that is unrelated to the error. A valid instrument $Z$ must satisfy:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **相关性**：$\operatorname{Cov}(Z,X)\ne0$。
2. **外生性**：$\operatorname{Cov}(Z,u)=0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Relevance:** $\operatorname{Cov}(Z,X)\ne0$.<br>
**2.** **Exogeneity:** $\operatorname{Cov}(Z,u)=0$.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 工具变量的两条命
> 相关性弱，估计会不稳定；外生性错，估计会系统性错误。只满足一条不够。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Weak relevance makes estimation unstable; failed exogeneity makes it systematically wrong. Satisfying only one condition is not enough.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.3. 恰好识别和过度识别
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3. Exact and overidentification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3.3.1. [[识别阶数与秩|恰好识别]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3.1. Exact identification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

工具变量数量等于内生解释变量数量只满足恰好识别的阶数要求；还必须满足相应秩条件，且工具变量有效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equality between the number of instruments and endogenous regressors satisfies only the order requirement for exact identification; the relevant rank condition and instrument validity are also required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3.3.2. [[识别阶数与秩|过度识别]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3.2. Overidentification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

排除工具的数量多于内生解释变量、并且秩条件成立时，模型才可能过度识别。工具与矩条件有效时，可以用 [[过度识别检验边界|过度识别检验]] 检查其联合相容性；未拒绝并不证明每个工具都有效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When excluded instruments outnumber endogenous regressors and the rank condition holds, the model may be overidentified. If the instrument and moment assumptions are valid, an [[过度识别检验边界|overidentification test]] can assess their joint compatibility; failure to reject does not prove that every instrument is valid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.4. 识别条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.4. Identification conditions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3.4.1. 阶条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.4.1. Order condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阶条件是必要条件：被排除在该结构方程之外的外生变量数量，至少要不小于该方程中内生解释变量数量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The order condition is necessary: the number of exogenous variables excluded from the structural equation must be at least as large as the number of endogenous regressors in it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3.4.2. 秩条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.4.2. Rank condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在线性 IV 且有效矩条件已经成立时，第一阶段总体矩阵满列秩是点识别的必要充分条件：被排除外生变量必须真正提供彼此独立的内生变量变化方向，不能只是数量上够。非线性或其他结构模型需要各自的全局识别条件，不能照搬这一句。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a linear IV model with valid moments, full column rank of the population first-stage moment matrix is necessary and sufficient for point identification. The excluded exogenous variables must supply genuinely independent directions of variation in the endogenous regressors, not merely be numerous enough. Nonlinear and other structural models require their own global identification conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. [[工具变量]]、[[工具变量有效条件|有效条件]]与[[2SLS投影|2SLS 投影]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Instrumental variables, validity conditions, and two-stage least squares*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.1. 理解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1. Interpretation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 条件 | 数学含义 | 失败后果 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 相关性 | $\operatorname{Cov}(Z,X)\ne0$ | 弱工具变量，估计不稳 |
| 外生性 | $\operatorname{Cov}(Z,u)=0$ | 工具变量无效，估计有偏 |
| 排除限制 | $Z$ 只通过 $X$ 影响 $Y$ | 结构解释不成立 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Condition | Mathematical meaning | Consequence of failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relevance | $\operatorname{Cov}(Z,X)\ne0$ | A weak instrument and unstable estimation |
| Exogeneity | $\operatorname{Cov}(Z,u)=0$ | An invalid instrument and biased estimates |
| Exclusion restriction | $Z$ affects $Y$ only through $X$ | The structural interpretation fails |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

IV 的目的不是让 $X$ 消失，而是用工具变量提取 $X$ 中外生的那部分变化，再用这部分变化解释 $Y$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
IV does not make $X$ disappear. It uses instruments to extract the exogenous part of variation in $X$ and uses that variation to explain $Y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.2. IV 估计量的计算过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2. Computing an IV estimator*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 4.2.1. 选择工具变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.1. Choosing an instrument*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择 $Z$，使其与内生变量 $X$ 相关，但与结构误差项 $u$ 不相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose $Z$ so that it is related to endogenous $X$ but unrelated to the structural error $u$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 4.2.2. 两阶段最小二乘法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.2. Two-stage least squares*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一阶段：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First stage:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
X=\pi_0+\pi_1Z+\pi_2W+v
$$

得到拟合值 $\hat X$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Obtain fitted values $\hat X$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二阶段：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Second stage:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y=\hat\beta_0+\hat\beta_1\hat X+W\hat\beta_2+\hat r
$$

用 $\hat X$ 替代原来的内生变量来计算系数。上式的
$$\hat r=Y-\hat\beta_0-\hat\beta_1\hat X-W\hat\beta_2$$
只是投影回归的计算残差。原结构方程的真实扰动是
$$u=Y-\beta_0-\beta_1X-W\beta_2,$$
而实际 2SLS 推断使用原始解释变量计算结构残差
$$\hat u=Y-\hat\beta_0-\hat\beta_1X-W\hat\beta_2.$$
$\hat r$、$u$ 与 $\hat u$ 是三个不同对象；不能把手工第二阶段输出的 $\hat r$ 填进结构 2SLS 协方差公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replace the endogenous regressor with $\hat X$ to compute the coefficients. The projected-regression residual is $\hat r=Y-\hat\beta_0-\hat\beta_1\hat X-W\hat\beta_2$. The true structural disturbance is $u=Y-\beta_0-\beta_1X-W\beta_2$, while feasible 2SLS inference uses the structural residual $\hat u=Y-\hat\beta_0-\hat\beta_1X-W\hat\beta_2$, evaluated with the original regressors. These are three different objects; the hand-computed second-stage residual $\hat r$ does not belong in the structural 2SLS covariance formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] 2SLS 怎么记
> 第一阶段把内生变量投影到工具与外生控制变量张成的空间，第二阶段只用这部分变异估计结构系数。它是否可作因果解释仍取决于工具变量条件，而不是拟合值天然“干净”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first stage projects the endogenous regressor onto the space spanned by instruments and exogenous controls; the second uses only that variation to estimate the structural coefficient. Its causal interpretation still depends on instrument validity—the fitted value is not intrinsically “clean.”
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 4.2.3. 工具变量的判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.3. Evaluating instruments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 第一阶段必须提供足够识别信息；[[弱工具推断|弱工具变量]] 不只会让估计不稳定，也会使常规 Wald 推断失真。
- 工具变量外生性不能完全由数据证明，需要理论支持。
- 过度识别时，可以用 [[过度识别检验边界|J 检验或 Sargan 检验]] 作为辅助诊断，但未拒绝不是有效性证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The first stage must supply adequate identifying information; [[弱工具推断|weak instruments]] can distort conventional Wald inference as well as destabilise estimates.
- Instrument exogeneity cannot be established completely from data and requires theoretical support.
- Under overidentification, a [[过度识别检验边界|$J$ or Sargan test]] can provide an auxiliary diagnostic, but failure to reject is not proof of validity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 4.2.4. IV 估计量公式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.4. IV formulas*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单一内生变量和单一工具变量时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With one endogenous variable and one instrument:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat\beta_{IV}
=\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(Z,Y)}{\operatorname{Cov}(Z,X)}
$$

在恰好识别且 $Z'X$ 可逆的情形，矩阵形式为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the exactly identified case with invertible $Z'X$, the matrix form is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat\beta_{IV}=(Z'X)^{-1}Z'Y
$$

2SLS 的矩阵形式为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matrix form of 2SLS is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat\beta_{2SLS}=(X'P_ZX)^{-1}X'P_ZY,\qquad
P_Z=Z(Z'Z)^{-1}Z'
$$

该式给出点估计。实际推断应直接使用 2SLS 的协方差估计；把手工第二阶段当成普通 OLS 会使用错误的残差结构和标准误，见 [[2SLS标准误]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This expression gives the point estimate. Inference must use a 2SLS covariance estimator; treating a manually run second stage as ordinary OLS uses the wrong residual structure and standard errors. See [[2SLS标准误]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. [[内生性检验边界|Durbin–Wu–Hausman 内生性检验的解释边界]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Interpretation boundary of the Durbin–Wu–Hausman endogeneity test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 5.1. 思想原理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.1. Basic idea*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Hausman 型检验比较两个估计量：一个在原假设下有效、在备择下不一致；另一个在原假设和备择下都一致，但在原假设下通常效率较低。若维持条件成立，二者差异过大就构成反对原假设的证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A Hausman-type test compares an estimator that is efficient under the null but inconsistent under the alternative with one that remains consistent under both, though usually less efficient under the null. Conditional on the maintained assumptions, an unusually large difference is evidence against the null.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!item] Hausman 在问什么
> 它不是问哪个系数更大，而是问“在原假设下本该接近的两个估计量，为什么相差到无法由抽样波动解释”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It does not ask which coefficient is larger. It asks why two estimators that should be close under the null differ by more than sampling variation can explain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 5.2. 用于内生性诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.2. Using the comparison to diagnose endogeneity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用于内生性时，可以比较 OLS 与 IV/2SLS。原假设把待检验解释变量视为外生；备择允许其内生。但把估计量差异归因于内生性，还要求二者估计同一个结构参数，并且 IV/2SLS 在备择下仍一致。若工具无效、识别太弱、结构方程错误或协方差口径不匹配，检验差异就没有唯一的“变量内生”解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an endogeneity diagnosis, OLS may be compared with IV or 2SLS. The null treats the regressors under test as exogenous, while the alternative allows endogeneity. Attributing the difference to endogeneity nevertheless requires a common structural target and IV consistency under the alternative. Invalid or weak instruments, misspecification, or a mismatched covariance procedure destroy that unique interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 5.3. 阅读结果的顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.3. How to read the result*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 先核对 OLS 与 IV/2SLS 的目标参数、样本和控制集合是否可比。
2. 再核对工具的外生性、排除限制、秩与识别强度。
3. 使用与同方差、异方差、HAC 或聚类结构匹配的 DWH/Wooldridge/C 检验版本。
4. 显著差异只在上述条件下支持拒绝外生性；未拒绝只表示未发现足够差异，不能证明外生。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Check that OLS and IV/2SLS target comparable parameters in the same sample and control specification.<br>
**2.** Check instrument exogeneity, exclusion, rank, and identification strength.<br>
**3.** Use a DWH, Wooldridge, or C-test implementation whose covariance assumptions match homoskedastic, robust, HAC, or clustered estimation.<br>
**4.** A significant difference rejects exogeneity only under those conditions; nonrejection means insufficient evidence of a difference, not proof of exogeneity.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 从识别主张到可解释结果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. From an identification claim to an interpretable result*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一项 IV 分析不能从“找到了一个相关变量”直接跳到第二阶段系数。先写清目标结构方程、目标人群和 estimand，再说明 OLS 为什么失败：是[[同时性内生|反馈]]、[[遗漏变量偏差|遗漏共同原因]]、[[测量误差位置|测量误差]]，还是另一个选择机制。只有在问题被准确命名后，工具的排除变化才有可判断的对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An IV analysis cannot jump from “we found a correlated variable” to a second-stage coefficient. First state the structural equation, target population, and estimand, then explain why OLS fails—feedback, an omitted common cause, measurement error, or another selection mechanism. Only after the failure is named does an exclusion argument have a definite target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着把工具论证拆成三件事：它是否在给定控制后推动内生变量；为什么它与目标结构误差分离；为什么它不能绕过处理直接改变结果。前两项统计关系与最后两项设计主张不能互相替代，见[[工具变量有效条件]]和[[工具外生与排除]]。工具数量只通过必要计数，真正的点识别还要看[[识别阶数与秩|第一阶段矩阵的秩]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next separate three claims: whether the instrument moves the endogenous regressor after controls, why its assignment is separated from the target structural error, and why it cannot affect the outcome through a route that bypasses treatment. A first-stage relationship cannot substitute for the design claims; see [[工具变量有效条件|instrument validity]] and [[工具外生与排除|exogeneity versus exclusion]]. Instrument counts pass only a necessary screen; point identification still requires rank.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估计时，单工具单内生变量先用[[IV比率估计|约化式除以第一阶段]]建立直觉；多个工具与控制变量进入[[2SLS投影]]。点估计之后仍须单独处理推断：手工第二阶段的普通 OLS 标准误不成立，应使用[[2SLS标准误|匹配误差结构的 2SLS 协方差]]；若识别弱，常规 Wald 近似本身会失真，不能只换成稳健标准误，见[[弱工具推断]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For estimation, begin with the reduced-form-to-first-stage [[IV比率估计|ratio]] in the one-instrument case, then move to [[2SLS投影|2SLS projection]] with controls and multiple instruments. Inference remains a separate task: ordinary OLS standard errors from a generated second stage are invalid, and weak identification can break conventional Wald approximations even after a robust covariance correction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后才解释统计检验和系数对象。[[内生性检验边界|DWH]] 与[[过度识别检验边界|过度识别检验]]都以若干无法由检验自身证明的维持条件为前提；未拒绝不能写成“外生性已证实”。若处理效应异质，二元工具的 Wald 比率还要在独立性、排除、第一阶段、单调性与无干扰等条件下解释为[[IV异质性边界|服从者 LATE]]，不能自动外推为 ATE。更多矩条件如何被组合，则进入[[GMM矩条件]]与[[GMM权重矩阵]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only then interpret diagnostics and the estimand. DWH and overidentification tests depend on maintained assumptions that the tests cannot establish by themselves; nonrejection is not proof of exogeneity. With heterogeneous effects, a binary-instrument Wald ratio identifies a complier LATE only under additional independence, exclusion, first-stage, monotonicity, and no-interference conditions. Combining a larger set of moments leads to GMM and its weighting matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
