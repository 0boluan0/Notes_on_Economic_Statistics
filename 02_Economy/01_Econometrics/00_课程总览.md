### 2. OLS 线性回归
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. OLS Linear Regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先定义反事实和识别问题，才能知道 OLS 系数是在描述条件均值，还是有条件地回答因果问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Counterfactuals and the identification problem must be defined before we can tell whether an OLS coefficient merely describes a conditional mean or answers a causal question under specific conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[OLS 线性回归]]

### 3. 回归推断与模型比较
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Regression Inference and Model Comparison*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OLS 给出点估计；回归推断研究样本变动怎样转化为标准误、区间和限制检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
OLS provides point estimates; regression inference studies how sampling variation becomes standard errors, intervals, and tests of restrictions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[回归推断与模型比较]]

### 4. 模型设定、多重共线性与异常点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Model Specification, Multicollinearity, and Influential Observations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

推断依赖模型足够贴近数据；设定、共线性和影响点诊断检查变量、函数形式和样本是否让系数可解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inference relies on a model that is sufficiently aligned with the data. Diagnostics for specification, collinearity, and influential observations assess whether the variables, functional form, and sample make the coefficients interpretable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[模型设定、多重共线性与异常点]]

### 5. 异方差与自相关
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Heteroskedasticity and Autocorrelation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使均值设定合理，误差方差和相关结构仍可能破坏常规标准误，因此要选择稳健推断或更有效估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even with a sensible conditional-mean specification, the variance and dependence structure of the errors can invalidate conventional standard errors, requiring robust inference or a more efficient estimator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[异方差与自相关]]

### 6. 内生性、识别、IV、2SLS 与 GMM
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Endogeneity, Identification, IV, 2SLS, and GMM*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

异方差和自相关多半影响效率与推断；内生性则让解释变量混入误差，直接破坏因果识别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation mainly affect efficiency and inference. Endogeneity makes explanatory variables carry information from the error term, directly undermining causal identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[内生性、识别、IV、2SLS 与 GMM]]

### 7. 虚拟变量、离散因变量与面板数据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Dummy Variables, Discrete Outcomes, and Panel Data*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

IV 处理某些内生连续变量；虚拟变量、离散结果和面板结构扩展数据与模型，并用固定效应控制不变异质性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
IV addresses some endogenous continuous regressors. Dummy variables, discrete outcomes, and panel structures extend both the data and the model, with fixed effects controlling time-invariant heterogeneity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[虚拟变量、离散因变量与面板数据]]

### 8. 趋势、单位根与差分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. Trends, Unit Roots, and Differencing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面板与时间数据引出趋势问题；单位根说明看似显著的水平关系可能只是共同漂移。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Panel and time-series data introduce trends. Unit roots show how an apparently significant relationship in levels may reflect nothing more than common drift.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[趋势、单位根与差分]]

### 9. 协整与误差修正模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9. Cointegration and Error-Correction Models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若非平稳变量存在稳定长期组合，不能只差分丢掉关系；协整和 ECM 分开长期与短期调整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When non-stationary variables form a stable long-run combination, differencing alone would discard that relationship. Cointegration and an ECM separate the long-run relation from short-run adjustment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[协整与误差修正模型]]

### 10. 双重差分法（DID）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*10. Difference-in-Differences (DID)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面建立识别、面板和动态诊断后，DID 把它们组合为依靠对照组趋势构造反事实的研究设计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After identification, panel structure, and dynamic diagnostics have been established, DID combines them into a research design that constructs the counterfactual from the control group's trend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[双重差分法（DID）]]
