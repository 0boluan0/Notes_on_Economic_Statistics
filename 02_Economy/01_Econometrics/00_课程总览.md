#### 先确定“多不确定”以及标准误凭什么成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First define uncertainty and justify the covariance estimator*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

残差标准差描述观测噪声，coefficient standard error 描述估计量的抽样波动。经典 $\sigma^2(X'X)^{-1}$ 公式来自球形误差条件；一旦允许异方差、时间或组内依赖，就要改用与依赖结构匹配的完整协方差矩阵。标准误修正只处理 uncertainty layer，不能修复内生性、错误 estimand 或错误函数形式；遇到这些问题应回到 [[设定问题分层]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Residual standard deviation describes observation noise, whereas a coefficient standard error describes sampling variation in an estimator. The classical $\sigma^2(X'X)^{-1}$ formula follows from spherical errors. Heteroskedastic, temporal, or within-group dependence requires a matching full covariance estimator. A covariance correction addresses the uncertainty layer; it cannot repair endogeneity, a wrong estimand, or a wrong functional form, which belong in [[设定问题分层|the layered specification diagnosis]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[标准误口径匹配]]

#### 单个限制：统计量、尾概率、区间与实际含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*One restriction: statistic, tail probability, interval, and substantive meaning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上节的 [[BLUE正态性边界]] 已经区分 Gauss–Markov 效率与正态小样本推断。现在把一个线性限制除以相匹配的 standard error，再用与该口径一致的精确或渐近参考分布校准。p value 和 confidence interval 是条件化的推断结果；是否重要仍要回到 effect size、单位、区间和事先给定的实际阈值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding [[BLUE正态性边界|normality boundary]] separated Gauss–Markov efficiency from exact normal-theory inference. A one-dimensional restriction is now divided by its matching standard error and calibrated with the corresponding exact or asymptotic reference distribution. P-values and confidence intervals are conditional inferential outputs; substantive importance still depends on effect magnitude, units, the interval, and a prespecified meaningful threshold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 多个限制：先写清组合，再选择 F 或 Wald
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Several restrictions: define the combination before choosing F or Wald*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

真正的问题常是 $a'\beta$ 或 $R\beta=r$，所以必须通过完整 covariance matrix 传播不确定性。经典 F 用同一样本和响应下的受限/无约束 RSS；Chow 是已知分组或断点下检验整组系数稳定的专门应用；Wald 则从无约束估计出发，直接使用选定的 covariance estimator。联合拒绝只否定整组限制，不等于每个系数都单独显著。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The substantive target is often $a'\beta$ or $R\beta=r$, so uncertainty must be propagated through the full covariance matrix. The classical F test compares restricted and unrestricted RSS on the same response and observations. Chow is a specialised coefficient-stability application for a prespecified group or break. Wald starts from the unrestricted estimate and directly uses the selected covariance estimator. Joint rejection contradicts the restriction set as a whole; it does not make every coefficient individually significant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Finally ask whether the question itself was selected after seeing the data*

以上校准默认待检验模型或限制按其声明方式给定。若同一数据先用于挑变量、模型、断点或最小 p 值，再把胜出结果当成预先指定，普通区间与检验不再自动保留名义保证。下一节讨论怎样比较模型；这里先保留选择过程进入推断的边界。

The preceding calibration treats the model or restriction as given in the stated way. If the same data first select variables, models, break points, or the smallest p-value and the winner is then treated as prespecified, ordinary intervals and tests no longer automatically retain their nominal guarantees. The next section studies model comparison; this boundary first keeps the selection path inside the inferential problem.

#### 先决定“好”指什么，再确认分数真的可比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First define what “good” means, then verify that scores are comparable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有脱离用途的最佳模型。先固定 description、prediction、causal estimation 或 mechanism communication，再把响应、评价样本、权重、似然或损失和候选集写成同一份比较契约。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 样本内拟合、限制检验和信息准则回答不同问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In-sample fit, restriction tests, and information criteria answer different questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

较大嵌套 OLS 总能复制较小模型，所以训练 RSS 不会上升；调整 $R^2$ 只是用自由度给这项机械改善加一层样本内惩罚。若问题是预先给定的一组嵌套限制，回到上一节的 [[嵌套模型F检验]] 或 [[Wald联合检验]]，不要把它们当成自动搜索模型的机器。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A larger nested OLS model can always reproduce the smaller model, so training RSS cannot rise. Adjusted $R^2$ merely adds an in-sample degrees-of-freedom penalty to that mechanical improvement. For prespecified nested restrictions, return to the previous section's [[嵌套模型F检验|nested F test]] or [[Wald联合检验|Wald test]] rather than treating either as an automatic search engine.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[调整R²]]

AIC 与 BIC 都从最大化 likelihood 出发，却有不同惩罚和推导目标。它们只在同一可比候选集内给相对排序；较小值不证明模型绝对正确，也不替代残差与设定诊断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
AIC and BIC both start from a maximised likelihood but use different penalties and target different comparison problems. They provide relative rankings only within a comparable candidate set; a lower value proves neither absolute adequacy nor successful residual and specification diagnostics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 预测选择必须让未见数据保持未见
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Predictive selection must keep unseen data unseen*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

训练、选择和最终评价是三个信息角色。普通 K 折中，每个候选的 preprocessing 与系数拟合只看该折训练数据，validation loss 可以据此选择候选；但它一旦参与选择，就不再是独立最终成绩。若要用折外误差评价包含调参的整条流程，才在 outer-training 内运行 inner selection。切分还要模拟实际部署中的时间、群组或空间边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Training, selection, and final evaluation are three informational roles. In ordinary K-fold CV, each candidate's preprocessing and coefficient fit see only the training portion, while validation loss may select the candidate; once used for selection, that loss is not an independent final score. Only when fold-held-out loss is meant to evaluate the whole tuning procedure must inner selection run inside each outer-training split. Splits must also reproduce temporal, grouped, or spatial deployment boundaries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[训练验证测试划分]]

![[K折交叉验证]]

![[验证管线隔离]]

![[验证结构匹配]]

时间序列把一般结构原则落实为 [[滚动起点评估]]；PCA 与分类分别复用为 [[PCA验证与泄漏]] 和 [[分类验证与泄漏]]。看过最终 test 后继续调整，则发生下面的共享边界：test 已经参与选择，必须换新证据才能再次独立验收。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Time series implement the general structural rule through [[滚动起点评估|rolling-origin evaluation]], while PCA and classification specialise it through [[PCA验证与泄漏|PCA validation]] and [[分类验证与泄漏|classification validation]]. Continued tuning after final-test inspection triggers the shared boundary below: the test has participated in selection, so fresh evidence is required for another independent assessment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[测试集自适应复用]]

最后，把选择过程带回推断：若同一数据先挑模型、变量或最小 p 值，普通区间与检验不会自动保留预先指定模型的名义保证，见 [[选择后推断]]。下一节再处理更早的一步——候选模型本身是否有正确含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally carry the selection path back into inference. When the same data first choose a model, variables, or the smallest p-value, ordinary intervals and tests do not automatically retain prespecified-model guarantees; see [[选择后推断|post-selection inference]]. The next section moves one step earlier and asks whether the candidate specifications themselves have the right meaning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

First separate exact from approximate dependence. Exact multicollinearity makes some individual coefficients non-unique. Near multicollinearity preserves a unique solution but, under exogeneity, mainly appears as loss of precision. Because collinearity occurs among columns of $X$, it cannot substitute for an endogeneity diagnosis.

#### 8.1 先固定两个问题与 OLS 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8.1 Fix the two problems and the OLS boundary first*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

异方差改变 $\Omega$ 的对角线：不同观测的误差尺度不同；误差序列相关改变非对角线：不同时点的未解释冲击共同变化。它们都不等于条件均值有误。在全样本零条件均值仍成立时，OLS 的中心可以保持无偏/一致，但经典协方差公式和通常的 BLUE 结论失效；标准误错误的方向不能只由“正自相关”机械推出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Heteroskedasticity changes the diagonal of $\Omega$, while serial error dependence changes its off-diagonal entries. Neither is the same as an incorrect conditional mean. Under full-sample zero conditional mean, the OLS centre can remain unbiased or consistent, while the classical covariance formula and usual BLUE result fail; even the direction of the standard-error error is not mechanically determined by positive autocorrelation alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 8.2 诊断先定位问题层，再让检验回答窄问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8.2 Diagnose the layer first, then let each test answer a narrow question*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

残差不是可直接观察的真实误差。先按时间、拟合值和关键解释变量检查残差，再看有序数据的 ACF/PACF，并排除均值函数、趋势、季节、结构变化和影响点。BP 针对预先指定的方差变量；White 检验更广但会快速消耗自由度；DW 只适合受限的一阶静态回归；BG 可检验预设的多个滞后并用于许多动态回归。Ljung–Box 是共享的残差序列联合诊断，拟合模型后需调整自由度。拒绝任何一个检验都不自动选择补救，也不识别经济来源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Residuals are generated proxies rather than observed true errors. Inspect them by time, fitted values, and key regressors, then use ACF/PACF while checking the mean function, trend, seasonality, breaks, and influence. BP targets specified variance covariates; White is broader but consumes degrees of freedom rapidly; DW is restricted to a narrow lag-one static setting; BG tests pre-chosen residual lags and applies to many dynamic regressions. Ljung–Box is the shared joint residual-series diagnostic and requires fitted-model degrees-of-freedom adjustment. No rejection selects a remedy or identifies an economic cause by itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 8.3 分开“只改误差条”和“重估系数”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8.3 Separate changing uncertainty from re-estimating coefficients*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the mean and target are credible, HC repairs inference for independent heteroskedastic rows, HAC handles weak dependence in one equally spaced series, and clustering follows repeated groups; these retain OLS coefficients. WLS uses known relative precision, GLS whitens a known general $\Omega$, and estimating $\Omega$ produces FGLS. <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8.4 Make the final choice by objective*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Repair the conditional mean first, derive independent information units from sampling, assignment, and indexing, and only then choose HC, HAC, clustering, WLS/GLS, or respecification according to inference, efficiency, prediction, or dynamics. When squared shocks rather than level errors have dynamics, move to the [[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH.canvas|ARCH/GARCH topic]] instead of treating it as a generic serial-correlation correction.

### 9. 内生性与工具变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9. Endogeneity and Instrumental Variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

异方差和自相关多半影响效率与推断；内生性则让解释变量混入误差，直接破坏因果识别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation mainly affect efficiency and inference. Endogeneity makes explanatory variables carry information from the error term, directly undermining causal identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[内生性与工具变量.canvas]]

#### 9.1 先定义问题，再区分内生性的来源
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9.1 Define the problem before separating sources of endogeneity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从[[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/09_联立方程模型(内生性).md|课程正文]]连续进入。内生性不是某个变量的永久标签，而是它相对目标方程、结构误差和 estimand 的关系。联立方程说明反馈怎样让均衡变量进入结构误差；遗漏共同原因直接复用前文的[[遗漏变量偏差]]，测量误差则要先看误差落在结果还是解释变量上。三种机制都可能破坏 OLS，却不能用同一条口诀修复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Enter through the [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/09_联立方程模型(内生性).md|continuous course reading]]. Endogeneity is not a permanent label on a variable; it is a relation to a target equation, structural error, and estimand. A simultaneous system shows how feedback places equilibrium regressors in the structural error. Omitted common causes reuse [[遗漏变量偏差|the earlier omitted-variable atom]], while measurement error must first be located in the outcome or a regressor. These mechanisms can all break OLS without sharing one automatic remedy.

#### 9.2 工具变量是一种识别设计，不是相关变量的别名
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9.2 An instrument is an identification design, not a synonym for a correlated variable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别先问总体分布与假设能否唯一恢复参数；估计才问有限样本如何逼近它。工具变量给内生回归量提供外生变异，但“推动处理”“与结构误差分离”“没有绕过处理的结果通道”承担不同职责。数量上的阶数条件只做必要筛查，真正识别仍需第一阶段矩阵有足够秩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identification first asks whether the population distribution and assumptions uniquely recover the parameter; estimation asks how a finite sample approximates it. An instrument supplies exogenous variation in an endogenous regressor, but moving treatment, being separated from the structural error, and having no route to the outcome that bypasses treatment are distinct claims. Instrument counts are only a necessary screen; identification still requires sufficient first-stage rank.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 9.3 点估计、标准误、弱识别和 estimand 分开检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9.3 Check point estimation, covariance, weak identification, and the estimand separately*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Wald 比率把工具对结果的约化式变化除以第一阶段变化；2SLS 把这一直觉推广到控制变量和多个工具。投影代数可以复现点估计，却不允许照搬手工第二阶段 OLS 标准误；协方差仍复用[[标准误口径匹配]]。若第一阶段方向接近退化，常规 Wald 近似可能整体失真。处理效应异质时，还必须说明 IV 汇总的是哪一群人的哪一种效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Wald ratio divides the instrument's reduced-form effect on the outcome by its first-stage effect on treatment; 2SLS extends this logic to controls and multiple instruments. Projection algebra can reproduce the point estimate but not ordinary OLS standard errors from a manual second stage; covariance still follows [[标准误口径匹配|the shared matching principle]]. Near-degenerate first-stage directions can invalidate conventional Wald approximations. With heterogeneous effects, the IV estimand must also name whose effect is being averaged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 9.4 多个矩条件进入 GMM，检验仍不能替代设计论证
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9.4 Multiple moments lead to GMM, while tests still cannot replace design arguments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

GMM 用权重矩阵组合样本矩：恰好识别时权重不改变唯一精确根，过度识别时才改变矩条件的折中与效率。过度识别检验只检查额外矩条件能否共同相容；DWH 只在比较估计量和工具条件都成立时解释估计量差异。两者的未拒绝都不是外生性证明。也不要用样本里的 $X'\hat u=0$ 反推总体外生性；那一边界直接复用[[样本正交与总体外生性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
GMM combines sample moments with a weight matrix. Under exact identification the weight does not move a unique exact root; under overidentification it governs the compromise and efficiency. An overidentification test checks joint compatibility of extra moments, while DWH interprets estimator differences only when the comparison estimators and instrument conditions are valid. Nonrejection in either case is not proof of exogeneity. Nor can the sample identity $X'\hat u=0$ establish population exogeneity; that boundary reuses [[样本正交与总体外生性|the shared orthogonality atom]].

#### 10.2 二元因变量模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*10.2 Binary-Outcome Models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 0/1 出现在等号左侧，目标就从“组别如何进入回归”变成“事件发生概率如何随 $X$ 改变”。LPM 直接在线性概率尺度上估计；logit/probit 保证概率边界，但必须把系数转成预测概率或边际效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When 0/1 appears on the left-hand side, the question changes from how a group enters the regression to how an event probability varies with $X$. ##### 把链接尺度翻译成预测与实质效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Logit 胜算比只属于特定的简单系数解释。更一般的共同语言是给定协变量配置的预测概率；logit/probit 预测还必须重建完整指数。连续变量用局部导数，离散变量用两次预测之差，再按目标分布汇总为 AME。概率交互效应是独立的概率尺度对象，不能拿线性指数里的交互系数替代。模型选择最后回到估计目标、支持、规格、推断结构与样本外概率表现。

#### 10.3 面板数据模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*10.3 Panel-Data Models*

#### 10.4 二元结果的面板扩展
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*10.4 Panel Extensions for Binary Outcomes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二元结果跨期重复出现时，必须把三个职责分开。静态 Logit 在联合条件似然成立时可以按单位成功次数条件化，从而消去单位截距；条件似然的共同斜率只由结果变化组识别；一般非线性固定效应 MLE 在短 $T$ 下则会遭遇附带参数偏误。后者与上面的 Nickell 偏误都可能随 $T$ 增大而减弱，但产生机制不同，不能互相代替诊断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a binary outcome is observed repeatedly over time, three responsibilities must remain distinct. Under a valid joint static-logit conditional likelihood, conditioning on each unit's success count can remove the unit intercept. The common slopes in that conditional likelihood are identified only by outcome-switching groups. A general nonlinear fixed-effects MLE with short $T$ instead faces incidental-parameter bias. That bias and the Nickell bias above may both shrink as $T$ grows, but they arise from different mechanisms and are not interchangeable diagnoses.
