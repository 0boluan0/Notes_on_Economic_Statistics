### 2. OLS 线性回归
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. OLS Linear Regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先定义反事实和识别问题，才能知道 OLS 系数是在描述条件均值，还是有条件地回答因果问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Counterfactuals and the identification problem must be defined before we can tell whether an OLS coefficient merely describes a conditional mean or answers a causal question under specific conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[OLS 线性回归]]

### 3. 回归系数推断与线性假设检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Regression Coefficient Inference and Linear Hypothesis Testing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OLS 给出点估计；回归推断研究样本变动怎样转化为标准误、区间和限制检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
OLS provides point estimates; regression inference studies how sampling variation becomes standard errors, intervals, and tests of restrictions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[回归系数推断与线性假设检验]]

### 4. 回归模型比较与选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Regression Model Comparison and Selection*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

得到一个 coefficient table 后，下一步不是选 $R^2$ 最大的 model，而是先明确模型用于 prediction、description 还是 causal estimation，再选择与目标匹配的比较证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After obtaining a coefficient table, the next step is not to select the model with the largest $R^2$. First determine whether the model is for prediction, description, or causal estimation, and then use comparison evidence matched to that purpose.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[回归模型比较与选择]]

### 5. 回归模型设定与函数形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Regression Model Specification and Functional Form*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可比较的 model 必须先是研究问题允许的 model；这里把 outcome、variables、log/polynomial forms、interactions 与 dynamics 组织成有明确解释的 conditional mean。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before models can be compared, they must be admissible for the research question. This section organises the outcome, variables, logarithmic or polynomial forms, interactions, and dynamics into an interpretable conditional mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[回归模型设定与函数形式]]

### 6. 多重共线性与设计矩阵诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Multicollinearity and Design-Matrix Diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

函数形式确定了 $X$ 的 columns；接着要问这些 columns 是否真的带来独立 variation。若它们彼此近似线性组合，model 可以整体拟合良好，却无法精确分开 individual effects。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The functional form determines the columns of $X$. The next question is whether those columns contain independent variation. When they are nearly linear combinations of one another, the model may fit well overall while failing to separate individual effects precisely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多重共线性与设计矩阵诊断]]

### 7. 异常值、杠杆与影响诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Outliers, Leverage, and Influence Diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前一块检查 columns 的 information overlap；这一块改看 rows，区分 outcome-direction outlier、predictor-space leverage 与删除 observation 后真正改变结论的 influence。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding section examined information overlap among columns. This section turns to rows and distinguishes outcome-direction outliers, leverage in predictor space, and influence that truly changes the conclusion after an observation is removed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[异常值、杠杆与影响诊断]]

推断依赖模型足够贴近数据；设定、共线性和影响点诊断检查变量、函数形式和样本是否让系数可解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inference relies on a model sufficiently aligned with the data. Specification, collinearity, and influence diagnostics examine whether the variables, functional form, and observations make the coefficients interpretable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 8. 异方差与自相关
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. Heteroskedasticity and Autocorrelation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使均值设定合理，误差方差和相关结构仍可能破坏常规标准误，因此要选择稳健推断或更有效估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even with a sensible conditional-mean specification, the variance and dependence structure of the errors can invalidate conventional standard errors, requiring robust inference or a more efficient estimator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[异方差与自相关]]

### 9. 内生性、识别、IV、2SLS 与 GMM
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9. Endogeneity, Identification, IV, 2SLS, and GMM*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

异方差和自相关多半影响效率与推断；内生性则让解释变量混入误差，直接破坏因果识别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation mainly affect efficiency and inference. Endogeneity makes explanatory variables carry information from the error term, directly undermining causal identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[内生性、识别、IV、2SLS 与 GMM]]

### 10. 类别、二元结果与重复观察
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*10. Categories, Binary Outcomes, and Repeated Observations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前一部分用 IV 处理解释变量与误差相关的识别问题；这一部分转而区分三种不同的建模变化：解释变量是类别、因变量只有 0/1，以及同一单位被跨期重复观察。它们可能同时出现在一项研究中，但各自改变模型的方式不同，不能压成一个方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding section used IV to address identification when regressors correlate with the error. This section distinguishes three different modeling changes: categorical regressors, an outcome restricted to 0/1, and repeated observations of the same unit over time. They may occur together in one study, but each changes the model in a different way and should not be collapsed into a single method.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 10.1 虚拟变量与交互项
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*10.1 Indicator Variables and Interaction Terms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当解释变量表示地区、制度或组别时，indicator 把类别转成相对基准组的对比；交互项进一步回答“一个变量的作用是否随组别或另一个变量而变”。阅读时要写出各组方程，而不是孤立地看一个主效应或交互系数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a regressor represents a region, institution, or group, an indicator converts the category into a contrast with a reference group. An interaction then asks whether the effect of one variable changes across groups or with another variable. Read the group-specific equations rather than interpreting a main effect or interaction coefficient in isolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[虚拟变量与交互项]]

#### 10.2 二元因变量模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*10.2 Binary-Outcome Models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 0/1 出现在等号左侧，目标就从“组别如何进入回归”变成“事件发生概率如何随 $X$ 改变”。LPM 直接在线性概率尺度上估计；logit/probit 保证概率边界，但必须把系数转成预测概率或边际效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When 0/1 appears on the left-hand side, the question changes from how a group enters the regression to how an event probability varies with $X$. The LPM estimates directly on a linear probability scale; logit and probit respect probability bounds but require coefficients to be translated into predicted probabilities or marginal effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[二元因变量模型：LPM、Logit 与 Probit]]

#### 10.3 面板数据模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*10.3 Panel-Data Models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若同一个人、企业或地区跨期重复出现，观测行不再独立，稳定的单位差异也可能与解释变量相关。pooled OLS、FE 与 RE 分别混合、只用或加权组合组间和组内变异；选择它们就是选择识别比较和相应假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the same person, firm, or region appears across periods, rows are no longer independent and stable unit differences may correlate with regressors. Pooled OLS, FE, and RE respectively mix, isolate, or weight between- and within-unit variation. Choosing among them means choosing an identifying comparison and its assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[面板数据：Pooled OLS、固定效应与随机效应]]

### 11. 趋势、单位根与差分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*11. Trends, Unit Roots, and Differencing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面板与时间数据引出趋势问题；单位根说明看似显著的水平关系可能只是共同漂移。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Panel and time-series data introduce trends. Unit roots show how an apparently significant relationship in levels may reflect nothing more than common drift.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[趋势、单位根与差分]]

### 12. 协整与误差修正模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*12. Cointegration and Error-Correction Models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若非平稳变量存在稳定长期组合，不能只差分丢掉关系；协整和 ECM 分开长期与短期调整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When non-stationary variables form a stable long-run combination, differencing alone would discard that relationship. Cointegration and an ECM separate the long-run relation from short-run adjustment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[协整与误差修正模型]]

### 13. 双重差分法（DID）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*13. Difference-in-Differences (DID)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面建立识别、面板和动态诊断后，DID 把它们组合为依靠对照组趋势构造反事实的研究设计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After identification, panel structure, and dynamic diagnostics have been established, DID combines them into a research design that constructs the counterfactual from the control group's trend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[双重差分法（DID）]]
