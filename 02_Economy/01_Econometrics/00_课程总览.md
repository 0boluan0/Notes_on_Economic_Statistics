#### 最后逐层加入总体假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Finally add population assumptions one layer at a time*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding identities describe the fitted sample. They do not verify zero conditional mean for the target model’s unobserved error. Unbiasedness, consistency, BLUE and exact finite-sample distributions each need their own conditions, developed next.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面的恒等式描述已拟合样本，不能验证目标模型未观察误差的零条件均值。无偏、一致、BLUE 与精确有限样本分布各需自己的条件，接下来逐项建立。

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上一结果固定样本量，在可能样本间取平均。一致性则考察信息量增长时的一串估计；应检查设计与误差平均的极限，不能只靠无偏性或一句方差结论判断依概率收敛。

#### 先确定“多不确定”以及标准误凭什么成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First define uncertainty and justify the covariance estimator*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

残差标准差描述观测噪声，coefficient standard error 描述估计量的抽样波动。经典 $\sigma^2(X'X)^{-1}$ 公式来自球形误差条件；一旦允许异方差、时间或组内依赖，就要改用与依赖结构匹配的完整协方差矩阵。标准误修正只处理 uncertainty layer，不能修复内生性、错误 estimand 或错误函数形式；遇到这些问题应回到 [[设定问题分层]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Residual standard deviation describes observation noise, whereas a coefficient standard error describes sampling variation in an estimator. The classical $\sigma^2(X'X)^{-1}$ formula follows from spherical errors. Heteroskedastic, temporal, or within-group dependence requires a matching full covariance estimator. A covariance correction addresses the uncertainty layer; it cannot repair endogeneity, a wrong estimand, or a wrong functional form, which belong in [[设定问题分层|the layered specification diagnosis]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[标准误含义]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The next calculation estimates two different objects in sequence. With n observations, k fitted coefficients (including any intercept), full column rank and n > k, RSS/(n−k) estimates the noise variance under zero conditional error mean and covariance σ²I. Multiplying by (XᵀX)⁻¹ gives the estimated coefficient covariance matrix. Taking square roots of its diagonal entries gives coefficient standard errors; it does not turn the residual scale itself into a coefficient standard error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下一步依次估计两个对象。n 个观测、k 个拟合系数（包括截距）、满列秩且 n > k 时，在条件误差均值零、协方差 σ²I 的模型下，RSS/(n−k) 估计噪声方差；再乘 (XᵀX)⁻¹，才得到系数协方差估计。对角元开平方给出各系数标准误，不能把残差尺度直接当作系数标准误。

![[OLS经典协方差估计]]

![[标准误口径匹配]]

#### 单个限制：统计量、尾概率、区间与实际含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*One restriction: statistic, tail probability, interval, and substantive meaning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上节的 [[BLUE正态性边界]] 已经区分 Gauss–Markov 效率与正态小样本推断。现在把一个线性限制除以相匹配的 standard error，再用与该口径一致的精确或渐近参考分布校准。p value 和 confidence interval 是条件化的推断结果；是否重要仍要回到 effect size、单位、区间和事先给定的实际阈值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding [[BLUE正态性边界|normality boundary]] separated Gauss–Markov efficiency from exact normal-theory inference. A one-dimensional restriction is now divided by its matching standard error and calibrated with the corresponding exact or asymptotic reference distribution. P-values and confidence intervals are conditional inferential outputs; substantive importance still depends on effect magnitude, units, the interval, and a prespecified meaningful threshold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[回归t检验]]

![[p值条件解释]]

![[置信区间覆盖率]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before interpreting a nonsignificant result, distinguish two repeated-sampling questions. Test size is the worst-case rejection probability over the null; a level-$\alpha$ requirement bounds it by $\alpha$. Power is the rejection probability at a specified alternative, for a fixed rule and design. A low false-positive rate does not guarantee good detection. Read the power card first, then use estimates, uncertainty and meaningful units to judge practical importance. Equivalence asks a further question about prespecified bounds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解释未显著结果前，先分清两个重复抽样问题：检验大小是零假设范围内最坏的拒绝概率，水平 $\alpha$ 要求它不超过 $\alpha$；功效是在固定规则与设计下，某个指定备择成立时的拒绝概率。误报少并不保证发现能力强。先读功效卡，再用估计值、不确定性与有意义的单位判断实际重要性；等效检验另问效应是否落在预设界内。

![[检验功效]]

![[显著性解释边界]]

#### 多个限制：先写清组合，再选择 F 或 Wald
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Several restrictions: define the combination before choosing F or Wald*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

真正的问题常是 $a'\beta$ 或 $R\beta=r$，所以必须通过完整 covariance matrix 传播不确定性。经典 F 用同一样本和响应下的受限/无约束 RSS；Chow 是已知分组或断点下检验整组系数稳定的专门应用；Wald 则从无约束估计出发，直接使用选定的 covariance estimator。联合拒绝只否定整组限制，不等于每个系数都单独显著。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The substantive target is often $a'\beta$ or $R\beta=r$, so uncertainty must be propagated through the full covariance matrix. The classical F test compares restricted and unrestricted RSS on the same response and observations. Chow is a specialised coefficient-stability application for a prespecified group or break. Wald starts from the unrestricted estimate and directly uses the selected covariance estimator. Joint rejection contradicts the restriction set as a whole; it does not make every coefficient individually significant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a combination fixed before inspecting the results, first propagate covariance to the target, then check the reference distribution. A Wald statistic requires a positive definite covariance for the tested restrictions; merely having linearly independent restriction rows does not guarantee this. With the same classical covariance, dividing Wald by the number of restrictions recovers the classical F statistic. A robust covariance instead requires its own valid calibration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对查看结果前已固定的组合，先把协方差传播到目标，再检查参考分布。Wald 要求被检验限制的协方差正定，仅有线性无关的限制行还不够。同一经典协方差下，Wald 除以限制数等于经典 F 统计量；改用稳健协方差后，须使用相应有效的校准。

![[嵌套模型F检验]]

![[Chow结构稳定性检验]]

![[Wald联合检验]]

![[回归联合显著边界]]

#### 最后检查这个问题是否在看数据后才被选中

*Finally ask whether the question itself was selected after seeing the data*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以上校准默认待检验模型或限制按其声明方式给定。若同一数据先用于挑变量、模型、断点或最小 p 值，再把胜出结果当成预先指定，普通区间与检验不再自动保留名义保证。下一节讨论怎样比较模型；这里先保留选择过程进入推断的边界。

If the same data first select variables, models, break points, or the smallest p-value and the winner is then treated as prespecified, ordinary intervals and tests no longer automatically retain their nominal guarantees. The next section studies model comparison; this boundary first keeps the selection path inside the inferential problem.

#### 样本内拟合、限制检验和信息准则回答不同问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In-sample fit, restriction tests, and information criteria answer different questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

较大嵌套 OLS 总能复制较小模型，所以训练 RSS 不会上升；调整 $R^2$ 只是用自由度给这项机械改善加一层样本内惩罚。若问题是预先给定的一组嵌套限制，回到上一节的 [[嵌套模型F检验]] 或 [[Wald联合检验]]，不要把它们当成自动搜索模型的机器。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A larger nested OLS model can always reproduce the smaller model, so training RSS cannot rise. Adjusted $R^2$ merely adds an in-sample degrees-of-freedom penalty to that mechanical improvement. For prespecified nested restrictions, return to the previous section's [[嵌套模型F检验|nested F test]] or [[Wald联合检验|Wald test]] rather than treating either as an automatic search engine.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
AIC and BIC both start from a maximised likelihood but use different penalties and target different comparison problems. They provide relative rankings only within a comparable candidate set; a lower value proves neither absolute adequacy nor successful residual and specification diagnostics.

*Predictive selection must keep unseen data unseen*

训练、选择和最终评价是三个信息角色。普通 K 折中，每个候选的 preprocessing 与系数拟合只看该折训练数据，validation loss 可以据此选择候选；但它一旦参与选择，就不再是独立最终成绩。若要用折外误差评价包含调参的整条流程，才在 outer-training 内运行 inner selection。切分还要模拟实际部署中的时间、群组或空间边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Training, selection, and final evaluation are three informational roles. In ordinary K-fold CV, each candidate's preprocessing and coefficient fit see only the training portion, while validation loss may select the candidate; once used for selection, that loss is not an independent final score. Only when fold-held-out loss is meant to evaluate the whole tuning procedure must inner selection run inside each outer-training split. Splits must also reproduce temporal, grouped, or spatial deployment boundaries.

### 6. 多重共线性与设计矩阵诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Multicollinearity and Design-Matrix Diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read the [[06_多重共线性|course note]] alongside the clarifications and linked cards below. Begin with the [[设计矩阵|design matrix]]: each row is an observation and each column is an included feature, possibly an intercept, transformation or indicator. The following sequence separates coefficient uniqueness, statistical precision, numerical sensitivity and the choice of remedy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

配合下方澄清与知识卡阅读[[06_多重共线性|课程笔记]]。先认识[[设计矩阵]]：每行是一条观测，每列是纳入模型的特征，可能是截距、变换项或指示变量。接下来依次区分系数唯一性、统计精度、数值敏感度与补救选择。

![[多重共线性.canvas]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exact column dependence prevents unique unrestricted OLS coefficients, while the observed fitted vector remains unique. Near dependence can leave very little variation for a particular coefficient. Its precision depends jointly on that remaining variation and the error covariance; conditional zero mean alone does not give a variance formula. Dependence among design columns and endogeneity of the target-equation error are separate questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

精确列依赖使无约束 OLS 系数不唯一，但原观察行上的拟合向量仍唯一。近似依赖可能使某个系数所依靠的剩余变动很少；精度由剩余变动与误差协方差共同决定，只有零条件均值还推不出方差公式。设计列之间的依赖与目标方程误差的内生性是两个问题。

![[多重共线性]]

![[共线性与内生性]]

![[近似共线性精度]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pairwise correlations can miss dependence involving several columns. With an intercept, full rank and a nonconstant focal column, VIF uses the auxiliary regression on all other columns; under spherical errors its variance comparison holds noise and total focal-column variation fixed. Cutoffs do not decide whether a scientifically necessary variable should be removed. A condition number compares the strongest and weakest unit directions, while the right singular vectors identify the combinations. State the scaling convention and distinguish relative conditioning from absolute noise amplification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两两相关可能遗漏多列共同形成的依赖。含截距、满列秩且焦点列非恒定时，VIF 用该列对全部其余列的辅助回归计算；球形误差下，其方差比较保持噪声和焦点列总变动相同。阈值不能决定是否删除研究所需变量。条件数比较最强和最弱单位方向，右奇异向量才指出具体组合；须说明缩放口径，并区分相对条件性与噪声绝对放大。

Centring a complete polynomial or interaction model can re-express the same fitted function without adding information for an unchanged, correctly translated target. Independent repetitions can improve precision without lowering VIF; changing the frequencies of already observed regressor combinations can also lower VIF. New combinations are one possible route, not a requirement. Choose data collection, coding repair, model changes or regularisation in light of the specific target and their separate assumptions.

接着追查依赖的来源：精确编码恒等式与观察数据中的强关系需要不同处理。完整多项式或交互模型的中心化可重新表达同一拟合函数，对保持不变且正确换算的目标并不增加信息。独立重复观测可改善精度而不降低 VIF；改变已有变量组合的出现频率，也能降低 VIF。新组合只是可能的途径，并非必要条件。根据具体目标及各自条件，选择收集数据、修正编码、改模型或正则化。

#### 8.1 先固定两个问题与 OLS 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8.1 Fix the two problems and the OLS boundary first*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Heteroskedasticity concerns conditional variances, while serial correlation concerns nonzero covariance across dates, relative to the stated information. These can occur separately or together; iid sampling of complete observations can still be heteroskedastic. With finite second moments, full column rank and zero error mean conditional on the full design, OLS is conditionally unbiased. Consistency needs additional assumptions. Nonspherical covariance generally removes the classical variance and BLUE guarantees, although particular designs may retain them. The direction of a standard-error error depends on the full covariance and the coefficient weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

异方差针对条件方差，序列相关针对指定信息下跨期协方差非零；它们可分别或同时出现，完整观测 iid 抽样也可以有异方差。有限二阶矩、满列秩且给定全设计的误差均值为零时，OLS 条件无偏；一致性需要额外假设。非球形协方差通常使经典方差公式与 BLUE 失去保证，但特定设计仍可能保留。标准误误差的方向取决于完整协方差与系数权重。

#### 8.2 诊断先定位问题层，再让检验回答窄问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8.2 Diagnose the layer first, then let each test answer a narrow question*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fitting changes residual scales and correlations even when the underlying errors are spherical. Inspect actual order, predictors, leverage and the mean relative to the stated goal. Patterns have competing explanations. BP/Koenker uses prespecified variance directions and version-specific scaling; White expands the auxiliary columns, with degrees of freedom based on rank. Both need valid moment, design and sampling conditions for their asymptotic calibration. DW compares neighbouring residuals; its classical calibration depends on the design and Gaussian spherical-null conditions, and near two does not certify zero correlation. BG retains original regressors and tests pre-chosen residual lags, using the actual auxiliary sample and valid asymptotic conditions; ordinary nR² is not generally heteroskedasticity-robust. Ljung–Box is the shared joint residual-series diagnostic and requires fitted-model degrees-of-freedom adjustment. No rejection selects a remedy or identifies an economic cause by itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使真实误差球形，拟合也会改变残差的尺度与相关。结合实际顺序、解释变量、杠杆与所声明目标检查均值；图案可能有多种解释。BP/Koenker 使用预定方差方向及对应版本的尺度，White 扩展辅助列并按秩确定自由度。两者的渐近校准都需要有效的矩、设计与抽样条件。DW 比较相邻残差，经典校准依赖设计与正态球形零假设条件，接近二不认证零相关。BG 保留原回归量，检验预定残差滞后，按实际辅助样本并在有效渐近条件下校准；普通 nR² 一般不抵抗异方差。Ljung–Box 是共享的残差序列联合诊断，拟合模型后需调整自由度。拒绝任何一个检验都不自动选择补救，也不识别经济来源。

#### 8.3 分开“只改误差条”和“重估系数”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8.3 Separate changing uncertainty from re-estimating coefficients*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
HC, HAC and clustering retain OLS coefficients while estimating uncertainty under their respective sampling conditions. WLS weights squared residuals; under full column rank and finite second moments, inverse relative variances have a BLUE justification for a correct linear mean and uncorrelated errors. GLS uses a known positive definite full covariance shape. Whitening alone does not create independence. FGLS estimates a structured covariance model, so its consistency, efficiency and reference distributions require additional justification. Reweighting can change a descriptive projection target. AR(1) quasi-differencing transforms every column, including the intercept. For known |ρ|<1 and the stated stationary initial covariance, Prais–Winsten rescales and retains the first equation; Cochrane–Orcutt drops that transformed equation, while still using the first observation in the next difference. Estimating ρ adds FGLS conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

HC、HAC 与聚类在各自抽样条件下估计不确定性，保留 OLS 系数。WLS 给残差平方加权；满列秩与有限二阶矩下，线性均值正确且误差不相关时，逆相对方差权重有 BLUE 依据。GLS 使用已知正定的完整协方差形状，白化本身不产生独立。FGLS 先估有结构的协方差模型，其一致性、效率与参考分布需额外论证；重新加权可能改变描述性投影目标。AR(1) 准差分变换每一列，包括截距。已知 |ρ|<1 且满足指定平稳初始协方差时，Prais–Winsten 缩放并保留首条方程；Cochrane–Orcutt 丢掉该变换方程，但下一个差分仍使用原首个观测。估计 ρ 还需 FGLS 条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These covariance choices still need regularity conditions: the relevant independent units, time periods or clusters must supply enough information for the chosen approximation. Report the HC version, or the HAC/cluster specification, together with the reference-law calibration. The point-estimation target may be a conditional-mean coefficient or a well-defined linear projection; covariance correction alone cannot establish a causal interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些协方差选择仍须正则条件：相应的独立单位、时期或聚类要为所用近似提供足够信息。报告 HC 版本，或 HAC/聚类设定，并说明参考分布校准。点估计目标可以是条件均值系数，也可以是定义清楚的线性投影；仅修正协方差不能建立因果解释。

![[异方差稳健协方差]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

A fixed Bartlett bandwidth can remain inconsistent even for stationary AR(1) errors; large-sample theory also constrains how bandwidth grows. The next card separates this long-run covariance target from the reported coefficient variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 8.4 最后按目标作决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8.4 Make the final choice by objective*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with the estimand: a descriptive linear projection, a conditional-mean coefficient and a causal effect need different justification. If the claim requires a correctly specified mean, investigate omitted dynamics; projection inference instead needs assumptions for that projection target. Use sampling, assignment and indexing to identify information units, then choose and calibrate covariance estimation or re-estimation. Diagnostics do not select a method merely by rejecting, and convenient p-values are not a selection criterion. When squared shocks rather than error levels have dynamics, the [[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH.canvas|ARCH/GARCH topic]] studies that variance process.

先说明估计对象：描述性线性投影、条件均值系数与因果效应各需不同论证。若主张要求均值设定正确，就检查遗漏动态；若目标是投影，则论证该投影的推断条件。从抽样、处理分配与索引确定信息单位，再选择并校准协方差估计或系数重估。诊断拒绝并不自动选择方法，也不能按有利的 p 值挑方法。若平方冲击而非误差水平有动态结构，[[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH.canvas|ARCH/GARCH 专题]]研究的是该方差过程。

### 9. 内生性与工具变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9. Endogeneity and Instrumental Variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

异方差和自相关多半影响效率与推断；内生性则让解释变量混入误差，直接破坏因果识别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation mainly affect efficiency and inference. Endogeneity makes explanatory variables carry information from the error term, directly undermining causal identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 9.2 工具变量是一种识别设计，不是相关变量的别名
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9.2 An instrument is an identification design, not a synonym for a correlated variable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别先问总体分布与假设能否唯一恢复参数；估计才问有限样本如何逼近它。工具变量给内生回归量提供外生变异，但“推动处理”“与结构误差分离”“没有绕过处理的结果通道”承担不同职责。数量上的阶数条件只做必要筛查，真正识别仍需第一阶段矩阵有足够秩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identification first asks whether the population distribution and assumptions uniquely recover the parameter; estimation asks how a finite sample approximates it. An instrument supplies exogenous variation in an endogenous regressor, but moving treatment, being separated from the structural error, and having no route to the outcome that bypasses treatment are distinct claims. Instrument counts are only a necessary screen; identification still requires sufficient first-stage rank.

#### 9.3 点估计、标准误、弱识别和 estimand 分开检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9.3 Check point estimation, covariance, weak identification, and the estimand separately*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Wald 比率把工具对结果的约化式变化除以第一阶段变化；2SLS 把这一直觉推广到控制变量和多个工具。投影代数可以复现点估计，却不允许照搬手工第二阶段 OLS 标准误；协方差仍复用[[标准误口径匹配]]。若第一阶段方向接近退化，常规 Wald 近似可能整体失真。处理效应异质时，还必须说明 IV 汇总的是哪一群人的哪一种效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Wald ratio divides the instrument's reduced-form effect on the outcome by its first-stage effect on treatment; 2SLS extends this logic to controls and multiple instruments. Projection algebra can reproduce the point estimate but not ordinary OLS standard errors from a manual second stage; covariance still follows [[标准误口径匹配|the shared matching principle]]. Near-degenerate first-stage directions can invalidate conventional Wald approximations. With heterogeneous effects, the IV estimand must also name whose effect is being averaged.

#### 9.4 多个矩条件进入 GMM，检验仍不能替代设计论证
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*9.4 Multiple moments lead to GMM, while tests still cannot replace design arguments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

GMM 用权重矩阵组合样本矩：恰好识别时权重不改变唯一精确根，过度识别时才改变矩条件的折中与效率。过度识别检验只检查额外矩条件能否共同相容；DWH 只在比较估计量和工具条件都成立时解释估计量差异。两者的未拒绝都不是外生性证明。也不要用样本里的 $X'\hat u=0$ 反推总体外生性；那一边界直接复用[[样本正交与总体外生性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
GMM combines sample moments with a weight matrix. Under exact identification the weight does not move a unique exact root; under overidentification it governs the compromise and efficiency. An overidentification test checks joint compatibility of extra moments, while DWH interprets estimator differences only when the comparison estimators and instrument conditions are valid. Nonrejection in either case is not proof of exogeneity. Nor can the sample identity $X'\hat u=0$ establish population exogeneity; that boundary reuses [[样本正交与总体外生性|the shared orthogonality atom]].

### 10. 类别、二元结果与重复观察
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*10. Categories, Binary Outcomes, and Repeated Observations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前一部分用 IV 处理解释变量与误差相关的识别问题；这一部分转而区分三种不同的建模变化：解释变量是类别、因变量只有 0/1，以及同一单位被跨期重复观察。它们可能同时出现在一项研究中，但各自改变模型的方式不同，不能压成一个方法。

This section distinguishes three different modeling changes: categorical regressors, an outcome restricted to 0/1, and repeated observations of the same unit over time. ##### 从一项指示变量到多类别编码
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

无交互的一项指示变量先建立固定的组间条件对比；多类别变量则要同时处理标签没有数值距离、基准组和满列秩。换基准以后，系数坐标改变，但同一完整模型的拟合和目标对比不变。

### 13. 双重差分法（DID）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*13. Difference-in-Differences (DID)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面建立识别、面板和动态诊断后，DID 把它们组合为依靠对照组趋势构造反事实的研究设计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After identification, panel structure, and dynamic diagnostics have been established, DID combines them into a research design that constructs the counterfactual from the control group's trend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续阅读：[[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/14_双重差分法#双重差分法（DID）|双重差分法：从缺失反事实到错位处理与透明聚合]]。课程正文保留推导、四格例子与实证顺序；下方原子用于单独回忆，主题图用于查看关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuous reading: [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/14_双重差分法#双重差分法（DID）|Difference-in-differences: from the missing counterfactual to staggered adoption and transparent aggregation]]. The course chapter preserves the derivation, four-cell example, and empirical sequence; the atoms support focused recall and the topic map shows their relationships.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看第二个差分到底替代了哪个看不见的反事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with the missing counterfactual that the second difference is intended to replace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[双重差分法]]

这个替代只有在未处理趋势可比、且政策尚未提前改变基准期时，才具有因果含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
That substitution becomes causal only when untreated trends are comparable and the policy has not already changed the baseline through anticipation.

在最简单的四格设计里，手算的第二个差分与回归交互项相同；但这个代数结论不能代替上面的识别论证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the simplest four-cell design, the hand-calculated second difference equals the regression interaction. That algebraic result does not replace the identification argument above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[2×2 DID回归等价]]

真实研究还必须检查四格均值是否仍代表同一总体、对照组是否被政策外溢污染，以及协变量调整究竟改变了哪一项假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An applied design must also ask whether the four cell means still represent the same population, whether spillovers contaminate the comparison group, and which assumption is changed by covariate adjustment.

Parallel trends and the meaning of the effect also depend on the outcome scale. Levels, logs, and proportions are not interchangeable versions of the same question that can be selected after seeing the estimates.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multi-period DID also requires checking whether first adoption fully represents the treatment path. Continuous doses or repeated treatment entry and exit do not fit directly into a binary absorbing-treatment framework.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With staggered timing, conventional TWFE compresses multiple cohorts and periods into an opaque comparison. Modern DID first recovers interpretable group-time effects and only then aggregates them for the research question.

Finally, assemble these judgements into a complete path from the research question to estimation, inference, and reporting.
