# 1. [[多重共线性与设计矩阵诊断|多重共线性]]的概念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. The concept of multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多重共线性指解释变量之间存在较强线性关系。它本身不是“解释变量和误差项相关”，而是解释变量之间互相解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multicollinearity means that the explanatory variables have a strong linear relationship with one another. It is not correlation between a regressor and the error term; it is overlap in the information carried by the regressors themselves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 一句话
> 多重共线性不是偏误问题，而是“解释变量的信息重叠太多”，导致单个系数难以精确估计。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Multicollinearity is not a bias problem. It means that the regressors contain so much overlapping information that individual coefficients are difficult to estimate precisely.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1. 完全的多重共线性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1. Perfect multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若存在不全为 0 的常数 $c_0,c_1,\ldots,c_k$，使得：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Perfect multicollinearity exists if there are constants $c_0,c_1,\ldots,c_k$, not all zero, such that:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
c_0+c_1X_1+\cdots+c_kX_k=0
$$

则存在完全多重共线性。此时 $X'X$ 不可逆，OLS 系数无法唯一估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In that case $X'X$ is singular and the OLS coefficients cannot be estimated uniquely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 不完全的多重共线性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2. Imperfect multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若解释变量之间高度相关但不是完全线性关系，OLS 仍能计算，但标准误会变大，单个系数的 t 检验容易不显著。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the regressors are highly correlated without an exact linear relationship, OLS can still be computed, but standard errors become larger and $t$ tests for individual coefficients are more likely to be insignificant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 产生多重共线性的原因
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Sources of multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1. 经济变量的共同趋势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Common trends in economic variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

宏观变量常常一起随时间上升，例如 GDP、消费、投资和货币供给，这会导致解释变量高度相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Macroeconomic variables such as GDP, consumption, investment, and the money supply often rise together over time, creating strong correlations among regressors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2. 滞后变量的引入
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2. Including lagged variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$X_t$ 与 $X_{t-1}$ 往往高度相关，分布滞后模型和动态模型容易产生共线性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$X_t$ and $X_{t-1}$ are often highly correlated, so distributed-lag and dynamic models are especially prone to multicollinearity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3. 多项式的引入
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3. Including polynomial terms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同时加入 $X$、$X^2$、$X^3$ 时，这些变量通常高度相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $X$, $X^2$, and $X^3$ are included together, these regressors are usually highly correlated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.4. 样本资料的限制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4. Limitations of the sample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

样本量太小、变量波动太少或样本范围太窄，都会增加共线性问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A small sample, little variation in the regressors, or a narrow sampling range can all intensify multicollinearity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. 多重共线性对 OLS 估计量的影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Effects of multicollinearity on OLS*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. 完全多重共线性对 OLS 估计量的影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1. Effect of perfect multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完全共线性使 $X'X$ 不可逆，OLS 无法估计唯一系数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Perfect multicollinearity makes $X'X$ singular, so OLS cannot produce unique coefficient estimates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. 不完全多重共线性对 OLS 估计量的影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2. Effect of imperfect multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不完全共线性不破坏无偏性和一致性，但会：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imperfect multicollinearity does not destroy unbiasedness or consistency, but it does:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 增大系数方差。
- 使 t 统计量变小。
- 使单个变量看起来“不显著”。
- 造成系数符号和大小对样本非常敏感。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Increase coefficient variances.
- Reduce the absolute size of $t$ statistics.
- Make individual variables appear “insignificant.”
- Make coefficient signs and magnitudes highly sensitive to the sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 典型症状
> 整体 $R^2$ 很高、F 检验显著，但很多单个 t 检验不显著时，优先怀疑多重共线性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When overall $R^2$ is high and the $F$ test is significant but many individual $t$ tests are not, multicollinearity should be one of the first possibilities to investigate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4. 对多重共线性的侦查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Detecting multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. 相关系数法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1. Correlation-based diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 方法 | 看什么 | 局限 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 两两相关系数 | 两个解释变量是否高度相关 | 看不出多个变量共同解释 |
| 辅助回归 | 一个解释变量能否被其他变量解释 | 需要逐个变量检查 |
| VIF | 方差被放大多少 | 只是诊断，不自动给处理方案 |
| 肉眼观察 | 高 $R^2$、低 t 统计量 | 只能作为线索 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Method | What it examines | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pairwise correlations | Whether two regressors are highly correlated | Cannot detect joint explanation by several variables |
| Auxiliary regressions | Whether one regressor can be explained by all the others | Must be repeated for each regressor |
| VIF | How much the variance is inflated | A diagnostic, not an automatic remedy |
| Visual inspection | High $R^2$ with small $t$ statistics | Only a clue |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.1.1. 两个解释变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1.1. Two explanatory variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果两个解释变量的相关系数非常高，可能存在共线性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A very high correlation between two regressors may indicate multicollinearity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.1.2. 多个解释变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1.2. Several explanatory variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多个解释变量时，单看两两相关系数不够，因为一个变量可能被多个变量共同解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With several regressors, pairwise correlations are insufficient because one variable may be explained jointly by several others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2. 辅助回归法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2. Auxiliary regressions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把某个解释变量作为被解释变量，用其他解释变量回归：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regress one explanatory variable on all the other explanatory variables:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
X_j=\alpha_0+\alpha_1X_1+\cdots+\alpha_{j-1}X_{j-1}+\alpha_{j+1}X_{j+1}+\cdots+\alpha_kX_k+v_j
$$

若辅助回归的 $R_j^2$ 很高，说明 $X_j$ 能被其他解释变量很好解释，存在共线性风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the auxiliary regression has a high $R_j^2$, the other regressors explain $X_j$ well and there is a risk of multicollinearity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.3. 肉眼观察
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3. Visual clues*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果整体 $R^2$ 很高、F 检验显著，但多数单个 t 检验不显著，往往要怀疑多重共线性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If overall $R^2$ is high and the $F$ test is significant but most individual $t$ tests are not, multicollinearity is often worth investigating.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.4. 方差膨胀因子检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.4. Variance inflation factors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[多重共线性与设计矩阵诊断|VIF]] 定义为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The VIF is defined as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
VIF_j=\frac{1}{1-R_j^2}
$$

$R_j^2$ 越接近 1，$VIF_j$ 越大，说明 $X_j$ 的标准误被共线性放大得越严重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The closer $R_j^2$ is to 1, the larger $VIF_j$ becomes and the more severely multicollinearity inflates the standard error for $X_j$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 5. 多重共线性补救
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Remedies for multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.1. 剔除解释变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.1. Removing an explanatory variable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 补救方法 | 适用情况 | 风险 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 剔除变量 | 变量含义重复 | 可能遗漏重要变量 |
| 增加样本 | 数据可扩充 | 不一定能解决结构性相关 |
| 变换模型 | 共同趋势明显 | 解释口径会变化 |
| 保持模型 | 理论上必须保留 | 标准误仍然较大 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Remedy | Appropriate when | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remove a variable | Variables carry duplicate meanings | May omit an important variable |
| Increase the sample | More data can be collected | May not resolve structural correlation |
| Transform the model | Common trends are prominent | Changes the interpretation |
| Keep the model | Theory requires the variables | Standard errors remain large |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果两个变量表达同一经济含义，可以保留理论上更重要或数据质量更高的变量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If two variables express the same economic concept, retain the one that is more important theoretically or measured more reliably.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.2. 增大样本容量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.2. Increasing the sample size*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更多样本可能增加解释变量的独立变化，从而降低共线性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More observations may provide additional independent variation in the regressors and thereby reduce multicollinearity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.3. 变换模型形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.3. Transforming the model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如改用增长率、差分、对数或合成指标，减少变量共同趋势带来的相关性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Growth rates, differences, logarithms, or composite indices may reduce correlations caused by common trends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.4. 逐步回归法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.4. Stepwise regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逐步加入或剔除变量可以作为诊断工具，但不能机械替代理论判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding or removing variables step by step can be a diagnostic aid, but it cannot mechanically replace theoretical judgment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.5. 保持模型不变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.5. Leaving the model unchanged*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果变量理论上必须保留，且研究重点不是单个系数显著性，可以接受较大的标准误，并在解释时说明共线性风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If theory requires the variables and the research does not depend on the significance of one coefficient, larger standard errors may be acceptable provided the multicollinearity risk is stated clearly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] 不一定要删变量
> 共线性严重时，删除变量只是选项之一。若变量有明确理论地位，保留并说明估计不精确，反而更诚实。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Even under severe multicollinearity, deletion is only one option. If a variable has a clear theoretical role, retaining it and acknowledging imprecision may be the more honest choice.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
