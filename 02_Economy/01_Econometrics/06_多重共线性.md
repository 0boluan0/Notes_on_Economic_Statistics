# 1. [[多重共线性|多重共线性的概念]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. The concept of multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[多重共线性.canvas|打开本主题的全局关系图]]

多重共线性指设计矩阵 $X$ 的列之间存在精确或近似线性依赖。它问的是不同解释变量在当前样本里能否提供足够独立的变异，而不是某个解释变量是否与误差项相关；后一个问题属于[[共线性与内生性|内生性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multicollinearity is exact or approximate linear dependence among columns of the design matrix $X$. It asks whether the regressors provide enough independent variation in the observed sample. It is distinct from correlation between a regressor and the error term, which is an endogeneity question; see [[共线性与内生性|multicollinearity versus endogeneity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 一句话
> 完全共线性使某些单个系数没有唯一答案；近似共线性则是在满列秩和外生性仍成立时，让这些系数虽可估计却不够精确。二者不能压成一句无条件的“只是标准误问题”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Exact multicollinearity makes some individual coefficients non-unique. Near multicollinearity leaves them estimable but imprecise when full rank and exogeneity still hold. The two cases should not be collapsed into the unconditional claim that multicollinearity is merely a standard-error problem.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1. 完全的多重共线性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1. Perfect multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若存在不全为 0 的常数 $c_0,c_1,\ldots,c_k$，使得：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Perfect multicollinearity exists if there are constants $c_0,c_1,\ldots,c_k$, not all zero, such that:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
c_0+c_1X_1+\cdots+c_kX_k=0
$$

则存在完全多重共线性。此时 $X$ 不满列秩，$X'X$ 不可逆，相关的 OLS 系数无法唯一估计；完整的识别边界见[[满列秩与OLS唯一性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then $X$ lacks full column rank, $X'X$ is singular, and the affected OLS coefficients are not uniquely identified; see [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full column rank and OLS uniqueness]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 不完全的多重共线性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2. Imperfect multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若某个解释变量在剔除其他解释变量后只剩很少、但不是零的样本变异，OLS 仍有唯一解；然而数据很难把相邻解释分开，相关系数会出现区间变宽、符号和大小对样本或控制集合敏感等现象。这个机制由[[近似共线性精度]]展开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a regressor retains little but nonzero sample variation after the remaining regressors have been partialled out, OLS still has a unique solution. The data nevertheless struggle to separate nearby explanations, producing wider intervals and coefficients whose signs or magnitudes are sensitive to the sample or control set; see [[近似共线性精度|precision under near multicollinearity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3. 一个贯穿全章的例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3. A running example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设工资方程同时含截距、年龄、受教育年数和工作经验。如果数据把经验机械地定义为
$$
experience=age-education-6,
$$
四列之间存在恒等关系，无法分别识别三个斜率。若经验来自实际记录，上式只是在样本中近似成立，则系数仍能计算，但“保持教育不变而改变年龄”这类比较缺少独立支持，估计会不精确。这个例子提醒我们：问题不只是相关系数大，而是目标比较在设计矩阵里有没有独立方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a wage equation contains an intercept, age, years of education, and work experience. If experience is mechanically defined by $experience=age-education-6$, the four columns satisfy an identity and the three slopes cannot be identified separately. If experience is recorded independently and the relation is only approximate in the sample, the coefficients remain computable, but comparisons such as changing age while holding education fixed have little independent support and are estimated imprecisely. The issue is therefore not merely a large correlation: it is whether the target comparison has its own direction in the design matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 产生多重共线性的原因
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Sources of multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先区分[[结构性与数据性共线|结构性共线与数据性共线]]。前者由编码或构造设计列产生，常能通过正确参数化处理；后者来自样本支持不足，不能靠改写同一批数据凭空创造独立变异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First distinguish [[结构性与数据性共线|structural from data-based multicollinearity]]. The former is induced by coding or constructed design columns and can often be handled by correct parameterisation. The latter comes from limited sample support; rewriting the same data cannot manufacture independent variation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1. 经济变量的共同趋势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Common trends in economic variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

宏观变量常常一起随时间上升，例如 GDP、消费、投资和货币供给，这会让设计列高度相关。若这些序列还是非平稳的，问题就不止是系数精度，还可能出现[[时间序列伪回归|伪回归]]；共线性诊断不能替代单位根与协整分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Macroeconomic variables such as GDP, consumption, investment, and the money supply often rise together, creating strongly related design columns. If the series are nonstationary, the problem extends beyond coefficient precision to possible [[时间序列伪回归|spurious regression]]; collinearity diagnostics do not replace unit-root and cointegration analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2. 滞后变量的引入
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2. Including lagged variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$X_t$ 与 $X_{t-1}$ 往往高度相关，分布滞后模型和动态模型容易产生共线性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$X_t$ and $X_{t-1}$ are often highly correlated, so distributed-lag and dynamic models are especially prone to multicollinearity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3. 多项式的引入
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3. Including polynomial terms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同时加入 $X$、$X^2$、$X^3$ 时，这些构造列通常高度相关。若完整低阶项都保留，在有意义的参考点中心化可以改善参数解释，却不会改变拟合列空间，见[[中心化共线性边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $X$, $X^2$, and $X^3$ are included together, the constructed columns are often strongly related. If the complete lower-order hierarchy is retained, centring at a meaningful reference point can improve parameter interpretation without changing the fitted column space; see [[中心化共线性边界|the centring boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.4. 样本资料的限制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4. Limitations of the sample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

变量波动太少或样本范围太窄，会让目标比较缺少独立支持。样本量小本身不等于高共线性；它会让精度更差，也更难覆盖稀缺的变量组合。增加同一模式的观测与扩展解释变量支持是两件事，见[[新增样本与共线性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Little regressor variation or a narrow sampling range deprives the target comparison of independent support. A small sample is not itself synonymous with high collinearity; it worsens precision and makes rare regressor combinations harder to cover. Adding observations with the same pattern and broadening regressor support are distinct interventions; see [[新增样本与共线性|sample size versus support]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. 多重共线性对 OLS 估计量的影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Effects of multicollinearity on OLS*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. 完全多重共线性对 OLS 估计量的影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1. Effect of perfect multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完全共线性使 $X'X$ 不可逆，OLS 无法估计唯一系数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Perfect multicollinearity makes $X'X$ singular, so OLS cannot produce unique coefficient estimates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. 不完全多重共线性对 OLS 估计量的影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2. Effect of imperfect multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只要模型仍满列秩，而且目标方程所需的外生性等条件成立，近似共线性本身不会使 OLS 系统有偏；它主要削弱精度。若外生性不成立，就不能用“这是共线性”替代对偏误的诊断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Provided that the model retains full column rank and the exogeneity conditions required by the target equation hold, near multicollinearity does not itself systematically bias OLS; its main effect is loss of precision. If exogeneity fails, attributing the problem to multicollinearity does not remove the bias diagnosis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[FWL残差化定理]]，令 $\tilde X_j$ 为把 $X_j$ 对其余设计列回归后的残差。在经典同方差模型下，
$$
\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_j\mid X)
=\frac{\sigma^2}{\sum_i\tilde X_{ij}^2}.
$$
其他变量越能解释 $X_j$，残差变异越小，$\hat\beta_j$ 的条件方差就越大。因此在目标参数、噪声水平和其他设计条件可比时，近似共线性通常会：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By [[FWL残差化定理|the Frisch–Waugh–Lovell theorem]], let $\tilde X_j$ be the residual from regressing $X_j$ on the other design columns. Under the classical homoskedastic model, $\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_j\mid X)=\sigma^2/\sum_i\tilde X_{ij}^2$. The more completely the other variables explain $X_j$, the less residual variation remains and the larger the conditional variance of $\hat\beta_j$. Holding the target parameter, noise, and other design features comparable, near multicollinearity therefore tends to:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 增大系数方差。
- 降低单个系数检验的精度与检验力。
- 造成系数符号和大小对样本非常敏感。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Increase coefficient variances.
- Reduce the precision and power of tests on individual coefficients.
- Make coefficient signs and magnitudes highly sensitive to the sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不是“实际样本中的 $|t|$ 必然下降”或“变量必然变得不显著”的单调定理。比较两个样本或规格时，$t=\hat\beta_j/SE(\hat\beta_j)$ 的分子也会变化，因此实际 $t$、$p$ 值和显著性没有固定方向；可确定的是，在给定可比目标与噪声的条件下，独立变异减少会抬高估计方差、削弱辨认该系数的能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is not a monotonic theorem that the realised $|t|$ must fall or that a variable must become insignificant. Across samples or specifications, the numerator in $t=\hat\beta_j/SE(\hat\beta_j)$ changes as well, so realised $t$ statistics, p-values, and significance have no fixed direction. The defensible conclusion is that, for a comparable target and noise level, less independent variation raises estimation variance and weakens the ability to distinguish that coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 典型症状
> 整体 $R^2$ 较高、联合 F 检验拒绝、但很多单个 t 检验较弱，可能来自共线性，因为联合检验和单个检验回答的是不同问题；也可能来自样本量、噪声或设定。它只是调查线索，不是诊断结论，边界见[[回归联合显著边界]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A relatively high $R^2$ and a rejected joint $F$ test alongside weak individual $t$ tests may reflect multicollinearity, because joint and individual tests answer different questions. It can also reflect sample size, noise, or specification. Treat the pattern as a prompt to investigate, not as a diagnosis; see [[回归联合显著边界|the boundary between joint and individual significance]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4. 对多重共线性的侦查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Detecting multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. 相关系数法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1. Correlation-based diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 方法 | 看什么 | 局限 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[两两相关诊断边界|两两相关系数]] | 两个解释变量是否高度相关 | 看不出三个或更多变量形成的联合依赖 |
| 辅助回归 | 一个解释变量能否被其余设计列共同解释 | 需要逐个变量检查 |
| [[方差膨胀因子|VIF]] | 单个系数的方差相对正交基准放大多少 | 只定位单列，不给出自动处理方案 |
| [[设计矩阵条件数|条件数与奇异值]] | 整个设计矩阵最弱的列组合方向 | 数值依赖缩放口径，须回看弱方向 |
| 肉眼观察 | 高 $R^2$、低 t 统计量 | 只能作为线索 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Method | What it examines | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[两两相关诊断边界|Pairwise correlations]] | Whether two regressors are highly correlated | Cannot detect a joint dependence involving three or more variables |
| Auxiliary regressions | Whether one regressor can be explained by all the others | Must be repeated for each regressor |
| [[方差膨胀因子|VIF]] | How much an individual coefficient variance is inflated relative to an orthogonal benchmark | Locates one column but supplies no automatic remedy |
| [[设计矩阵条件数|Condition number and singular values]] | The weakest joint direction of the whole design | Depends on scaling and must be traced back to the weak direction |
| Visual inspection | High $R^2$ with small $t$ statistics | Only a clue |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.1.1. 两个解释变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1.1. Two explanatory variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果两个解释变量的相关系数非常高，可能存在共线性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A very high correlation between two regressors may indicate multicollinearity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.1.2. 多个解释变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1.2. Several explanatory variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多个解释变量时，单看两两相关系数不够，因为一个变量可能被多个变量共同解释。比如已中心化、方差相同且互不相关的 $X_1,X_2$，若 $X_3=X_1+X_2$，三列已经完全共线，但 $X_3$ 与前两列的相关系数都只有 $1/\sqrt2\approx0.707$。因此相关矩阵只能筛查，不能替代[[两两相关诊断边界|完整设计诊断]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With several regressors, pairwise correlations are insufficient because one variable may be explained jointly by several others. For example, let centred $X_1$ and $X_2$ have equal variances and zero correlation, and set $X_3=X_1+X_2$. The three columns are exactly collinear, while the correlation of $X_3$ with either original column is only $1/\sqrt2\approx0.707$. A correlation matrix is therefore a screen, not a substitute for [[两两相关诊断边界|full-design diagnostics]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2. 辅助回归法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2. Auxiliary regressions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把某个解释变量作为被解释变量，用其他解释变量回归：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regress one explanatory variable on all the other explanatory variables:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
X_j=\alpha_0+\alpha_1X_1+\cdots+\alpha_{j-1}X_{j-1}+\alpha_{j+1}X_{j+1}+\cdots+\alpha_kX_k+v_j
$$

若辅助回归的 $R_j^2$ 很高，说明 $X_j$ 能被其他解释变量很好解释，存在共线性风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the auxiliary regression has a high $R_j^2$, the other regressors explain $X_j$ well and there is a risk of multicollinearity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.3. 肉眼观察
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3. Visual clues*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果整体 $R^2$ 很高、F 检验显著，但多数单个 t 检验不显著，往往要怀疑多重共线性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If overall $R^2$ is high and the $F$ test is significant but most individual $t$ tests are not, multicollinearity is often worth investigating.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.4. 方差膨胀因子检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.4. Variance inflation factors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[方差膨胀因子|VIF]] 定义为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The VIF is defined as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
VIF_j=\frac{1}{1-R_j^2}
$$

$R_j^2$ 越接近 1，$VIF_j$ 越大。在保持 $X_j$ 总变异不变的正交基准下，$VIF_j$ 是**方差**的放大倍数，标准误的放大倍数是 $\sqrt{VIF_j}$，不能把两者混用。固定阈值只能用于筛查，不能自动决定删变量，见[[VIF阈值边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The closer $R_j^2$ is to one, the larger $VIF_j$ becomes. Relative to an orthogonal benchmark that preserves the total variation of $X_j$, VIF is the multiplier for the coefficient **variance**; the multiplier for its standard error is $\sqrt{VIF_j}$. Fixed cutoffs are screens rather than automatic deletion rules; see [[VIF阈值边界|the boundary of VIF thresholds]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.5. 设计矩阵的整体弱方向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.5. Weak directions of the whole design matrix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

VIF 逐列问“哪一个 $X_j$ 可被其余列解释”；[[设计矩阵条件数]]则通过最大与最小奇异值之比，寻找整个设计矩阵最弱的系数组合方向。若最小奇异值为零，设计精确秩亏；若它只是相对很小，沿对应右奇异向量的系数组合会对数据扰动很敏感。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
VIF asks, one column at a time, whether a particular $X_j$ can be explained by the remaining columns. [[设计矩阵条件数|The design-matrix condition number]] instead uses the ratio of largest to smallest singular values to locate the weakest coefficient-combination direction of the whole design. A zero smallest singular value means exact rank deficiency; a merely small one makes the coefficient combination along the corresponding right singular vector sensitive to perturbations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原始条件数会随英镑改成千英镑等单位变化，所以报告时必须说明列是否中心化、是否按标准差或列范数缩放，并查看究竟哪些变量构成弱方向。一个脱离预处理口径的条件数阈值没有独立解释，见[[条件数缩放边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A raw condition number changes when pounds are expressed as thousands of pounds. Report whether columns were centred and scaled by standard deviation or column norm, and inspect which variables form the weak direction. A cutoff detached from its preprocessing convention has no standalone interpretation; see [[条件数缩放边界|the scaling boundary for condition numbers]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 5. [[共线性补救原则|多重共线性补救]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Remedies for multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

补救的目标不是让某个诊断数值尽量小，而是在不偷换研究问题的前提下，恢复目标比较所需的信息。先写清目标是单个系数、联合效应、预测还是某个可识别线性组合，再判断弱方向来自编码、构造项还是样本支持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The aim of a remedy is not to minimise a diagnostic statistic. It is to recover the information needed for the target comparison without silently changing the research question. First state whether the target is an individual coefficient, a joint effect, prediction, or an identifiable linear combination; then determine whether the weak direction comes from coding, constructed terms, or sample support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.1. 剔除解释变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.1. Removing an explanatory variable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 补救方法 | 适用情况 | 风险 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 修正编码或剔除重复变量 | 存在重复列、全套虚拟变量或同一构念的重复测量 | 误删必要控制会造成遗漏变量问题 |
| 扩大样本支持 | 能收集过去缺少的解释变量组合 | 只增加同类观测未必降低 VIF |
| 重参数化或变换模型 | 构造项、共同趋势或目标本来就应按新尺度表达 | 系数含义和估计目标可能变化 |
| 保持模型并改报联合效应或区间 | 理论变量必须保留，数据只精确识别联合方向 | 单个系数仍不精确 |
| 岭等正则化 | 目标是样本外预测并有验证数据 | 收缩系数不能直接解释为结构效应 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Remedy | Appropriate when | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repair coding or remove a duplicate variable | Duplicate columns, a full indicator set, or repeated measures of the same construct | Dropping a necessary control can create omitted-variable problems |
| Broaden sample support | Previously missing regressor combinations can be collected | More observations of the same pattern need not reduce VIF |
| Reparameterise or transform the model | Constructed terms, common trends, or a target naturally expressed on another scale | Coefficient meanings and the estimand may change |
| Keep the model and report a joint effect or interval | Theory requires the variables and the data precisely identify only a joint direction | Individual coefficients remain imprecise |
| Ridge or another regulariser | The target is out-of-sample prediction and validation data are available | Shrunk coefficients are not automatically structural effects |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果两个变量确实表达同一经济含义，可以在理论支持下合成指标，或保留测量更可靠的一个；但不能只因某个变量 VIF 较高、t 值较小就删除它。若它是因果问题所需的控制变量，降低 VIF 可能以[[遗漏变量偏差]]为代价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If two variables genuinely measure the same economic construct, theory may support combining them or retaining the more reliable measure. A large VIF or small $t$ statistic alone does not justify deletion. If the variable is a control required by the causal question, lowering VIF may come at the cost of [[遗漏变量偏差|omitted-variable bias]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.2. 增大样本容量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.2. Increasing the sample size*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增加样本有两种不同作用。来自同一狭窄联合分布的更多观测可以降低抽样噪声，却未必改变辅助回归的 $R_j^2$ 或 VIF；只有新增观测包含过去缺少的变量组合，才真正扩大独立变异、改善设计几何。具体边界见[[新增样本与共线性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More data can help in two distinct ways. Additional observations from the same narrow joint distribution may reduce sampling noise without changing the auxiliary $R_j^2$ or VIF. Only observations containing previously missing regressor combinations broaden independent variation and improve the design geometry; see [[新增样本与共线性|more observations versus broader regressor support]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.3. 变换模型形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.3. Transforming the model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增长率、差分、对数或合成指标有时能更贴合经济机制并减少共同趋势，但它们同时改变系数解释，甚至改变估计目标，不能只为压低相关性而采用。对多项式或交互项，选择有意义的中心并保留完整层级可改善低阶系数的解释；它只是同一列空间的重参数化，不会补回样本缺失的信息，见[[中心化共线性边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Growth rates, differences, logarithms, or composite indices may better match the economic mechanism and reduce common trends, but they also change coefficient interpretation and sometimes the estimand. They should not be chosen merely to lower a correlation. For polynomials or interactions, centring at a meaningful value while preserving the full hierarchy can improve interpretation of lower-order coefficients. It reparameterises the same column space and cannot restore information absent from the sample; see [[中心化共线性边界|the boundary of centring as a collinearity remedy]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.4. 逐步回归法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.4. Stepwise regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逐步加入或剔除变量可以暴露系数对控制集合的敏感性，但数据驱动的选择会改变后续推断的参照过程。它适合定位问题，不是自动定稿模型或恢复显著性的机器。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding or removing variables sequentially can reveal coefficient sensitivity to the control set, but data-driven selection changes the reference process for subsequent inference. It can locate a problem; it is not a machine for finalising the model or recovering significance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.5. 保持模型不变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.5. Leaving the model unchanged*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果变量理论上必须保留，而数据主要识别它们的联合方向，可以报告联合检验、相关线性组合或宽区间，并明确哪些单个效应无法精确分开。保留模型不是假装问题不存在，而是诚实匹配数据实际支持的结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If theory requires the variables while the data mainly identify their joint direction, report a joint test, a relevant linear combination, or wide intervals, and state which individual effects cannot be separated precisely. Keeping the model does not mean ignoring the problem; it means matching the claim to what the data actually support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.6. 预测目标下的正则化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.6. Regularisation for prediction targets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若目标是样本外预测，[[岭正则化]]可以沿信息最弱的方向收缩系数，以偏差换取方差下降；是否有用应由验证数据判断。它改变了估计规则，因此收缩后的单个系数不能未经额外论证就当作结构效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For out-of-sample prediction, [[岭正则化|ridge regularisation]] can shrink coefficients most strongly along weakly informed directions, trading bias for lower variance. Its value should be judged on validation data. Because it changes the estimation rule, individual shrunk coefficients are not structural effects without further justification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] 不一定要删变量
> 共线性严重时，删除变量只是选项之一。若变量有明确理论地位，保留并说明估计不精确，反而更诚实。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Even under severe multicollinearity, deletion is only one option. If a variable has a clear theoretical role, retaining it and acknowledging imprecision may be the more honest choice.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
