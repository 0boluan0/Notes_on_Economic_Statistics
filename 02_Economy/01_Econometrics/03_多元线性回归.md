# 1. 多元线性回归的两个例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Two examples of multiple linear regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1. CD 生产函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1. Cobb–Douglas production function*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Cobb-Douglas 生产函数常写为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Cobb–Douglas production function is commonly written as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y=AK^\alpha L^\beta
$$

取对数后可以转为线性回归：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking logarithms converts it into a linear regression:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\ln Y=\ln A+\alpha\ln K+\beta\ln L+u
$$

其中 $\alpha$ 和 $\beta$ 分别表示资本、劳动的产出弹性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are the output elasticities of capital and labor, respectively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 新凯恩斯混合菲利普斯曲线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2. Hybrid New Keynesian Phillips curve*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

混合菲利普斯曲线通常同时包含预期通胀、滞后通胀和产出缺口：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The hybrid Phillips curve usually includes expected inflation, lagged inflation, and the output gap:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\pi_t=\beta_0+\beta_1E_t(\pi_{t+1})+\beta_2\pi_{t-1}+\beta_3x_t+u_t
$$

它说明多元回归可以同时放入多个解释变量，从而描述某个变量在“其他已含变量不变”条件下的偏线性关联；要读成结构性边际影响，还需要模型与识别条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It illustrates how multiple regression includes several regressors at once and describes the partial linear association of one variable while the other included regressors are held fixed. A structural marginal-effect interpretation additionally requires model and identification conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] 两个例子的共同点
> CD 生产函数和菲利普斯曲线都不是为了展示公式复杂，而是为了说明“控制其他已含变量后看某个变量的偏线性关联”；只有额外条件成立时才进一步称为结构性偏效应。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The Cobb–Douglas function and the Phillips curve are not included merely to display complicated formulas. Both illustrate how to isolate a variable's partial linear association after controlling for other included regressors; further conditions are needed for a structural partial-effect reading.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3. [[多元回归系数|多元线性回归]]的一般形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3. General form of multiple linear regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1X_{1i}+\beta_2X_{2i}+\cdots+\beta_kX_{ki}+u_i
$$

也可以写成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It can also be written as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_i=X_i'\beta+u_i
$$

## 1.4. 偏效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.4. Partial effects*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若方程确实是线性条件均值模型，多元回归中的 $\beta_j$ 是偏效应：在其他已含解释变量不变时，$X_j$ 增加 1 单位，$Y$ 的条件均值变化 $\beta_j$。若只采用最佳线性投影视角，它是控制这些已含列后的偏线性斜率，不声称完整条件均值线性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the equation is genuinely a linear conditional-mean model, $\beta_j$ is a partial effect: holding the other included regressors fixed, a one-unit increase in $X_j$ changes the conditional mean of $Y$ by $\beta_j$. Under a best-linear-projection-only reading, it is a partial linear slope after controlling for those included columns and does not assert that the full conditional mean is linear.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 模型 | 系数读法 | 重点 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 一元回归 | $X$ 变化时 $Y$ 的平均变化 | 没有控制变量 |
| 多元回归 | 控制其他变量后的边际变化 | 关注偏效应 |
| 对数模型 | 弹性或半弹性 | 读法随对数位置变化 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Model | How to read the coefficient | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simple regression | Average change in $Y$ as $X$ changes | No controls |
| Multiple regression | Marginal change after controlling for other variables | Partial effect |
| Logarithmic model | Elasticity or semi-elasticity | Interpretation depends on which variables are logged |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 多元线性回归模型的 OLS 估计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. OLS estimation of multiple linear regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1. 回归系数的估计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Estimating regression coefficients*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OLS 最小化残差平方和：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
OLS minimizes the residual sum of squares:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\min_{\beta_0,\ldots,\beta_k}\sum_{i=1}^n
(Y_i-\beta_0-\beta_1X_{1i}-\cdots-\beta_kX_{ki})^2
$$

一阶条件给出正规方程：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first-order conditions yield the normal equations:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sum_{i=1}^n\hat u_i=0,\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^nX_{ji}\hat u_i=0,\quad j=1,\ldots,k
$$

矩阵形式为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In matrix form:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
X'(Y-X\hat\beta)=0
$$

若 $X'X$ 可逆，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $X'X$ is invertible, then:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat\beta=(X'X)^{-1}X'Y
$$

>[!item] 这条公式为什么重要
> 矩阵形式把多元回归压缩成一个正规方程，后面推导方差、无偏性和大样本性质都会用它。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The matrix expression compresses multiple regression into one normal equation. It will be used again to derive variances, unbiasedness, and large-sample properties.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.1. [[多重共线性]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.1. Multicollinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若解释变量之间存在完全线性关系，$X'X$ 不可逆，OLS 系数无法唯一估计。若只是高度相关，估计仍可计算，但标准误会变大，单个参数检验会变弱。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the regressors satisfy an exact linear relationship, $X'X$ is singular and the OLS coefficients cannot be estimated uniquely. With merely high correlation, estimation remains possible, but standard errors increase and individual coefficient tests lose power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.2. [[经典 Gauss–Markov 定理|多元线性回归的高斯-马尔可夫定理]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.2. Gauss–Markov theorem for multiple regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在参数线性、随机抽样、无完全共线性、零条件均值和球形条件方差等条件下，经典版本说明 OLS 是 BLUE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under linearity in parameters, random sampling, no perfect collinearity, zero conditional mean, and spherical conditional error variance, the classical version establishes that OLS is BLUE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.3. 系数计算：以二元回归模型为例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.3. Computing a coefficient: a two-regressor example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1X_{1i}+\beta_2X_{2i}+u_i
$$

$\hat\beta_1$ 可以理解为：先从 $Y$ 中剔除它对 $X_2$ 的线性拟合部分，再从 $X_1$ 中剔除它对 $X_2$ 的线性拟合部分，然后用两个剩余部分做一元回归。这就是 Frisch-Waugh-Lovell 定理的直观含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\hat\beta_1$ can be understood as follows: first remove from $Y$ its linear fitted component on $X_2$; then remove from $X_1$ its linear fitted component on $X_2$; finally regress the two residual components on each other. This is the intuition behind the Frisch–Waugh–Lovell theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2. [[OLS经典协方差估计|随机误差项方差的估计]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2. Estimating the disturbance variance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多元回归中，误差项方差估计为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In multiple regression, the disturbance variance is estimated by:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat\sigma^2=\frac{RSS}{n-k-1}
$$

其中 $k$ 是解释变量个数，不含截距项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $k$ is the number of explanatory variables, excluding the intercept.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3. [[调整R²|判定系数的调整]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3. Adjusting the coefficient of determination*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!map] 从课程公式进入模型比较
> 本节先用 [[训练拟合单调性]] 解释为什么普通 $R^2$ 不能惩罚新增变量，再由 [[调整R²]] 精确说明自由度修正。它仍是样本内准则；预测选择继续进入 [[训练验证测试划分]]，整体关系见 [[回归模型比较与选择.canvas|回归模型比较与选择总图]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This section first uses [[训练拟合单调性|training-fit monotonicity]] to explain why ordinary $R^2$ cannot penalise added variables, then gives the exact degrees-of-freedom correction through [[调整R²|adjusted $R^2$]]. It remains an in-sample criterion; predictive selection continues to the [[训练验证测试划分|training-validation-test distinction]]. See the [[回归模型比较与选择.canvas|model-comparison map]] for the whole structure.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一响应和样本上的嵌套 OLS 中，普通 $R^2$ 会随着解释变量增加而不下降，所以多元回归常使用调整后 $R^2$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nested OLS models fitted to the same outcome and observations, ordinary $R^2$ never falls when regressors are added, so multiple regression often uses adjusted $R^2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\bar R^2
=1-\frac{RSS/(n-k-1)}{TSS/(n-1)}
$$

调整后 $R^2$ 用残差自由度惩罚新增参数：只有新增变量改善样本内拟合足够多时它才会上升。它可辅助比较同一响应、同一样本上的候选线性模型，但不保证样本外预测、因果识别或函数形式正确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adjusted $R^2$ penalises additional parameters through residual degrees of freedom and rises only when the in-sample fit improves enough. It can assist comparison of candidate linear models fitted to the same response and observations, but it does not guarantee out-of-sample prediction, causal identification, or a correct functional form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*3. Hypothesis testing in multiple linear regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. [[回归t检验|单参数检验：t 检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1. Testing one parameter: the $t$ test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验单个参数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To test a single parameter:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_0:\beta_j=\beta_j^0
$$

统计量为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The statistic is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
t=\frac{\hat\beta_j-\beta_j^0}{se(\hat\beta_j)}
$$

若 $|t|$ 足够大或 $p$ 值足够小，则拒绝原假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reject the null if $|t|$ is sufficiently large or the $p$-value is sufficiently small.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. [[嵌套模型F检验|多参数检验：F 检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2. Testing several parameters: the $F$ test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.2.1. F 检验的基本原理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2.1. Logic of the $F$ test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

F 检验用于同时检验多个线性约束。例如：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $F$ test assesses several linear restrictions jointly. For example:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_0:\beta_2=\beta_3=0
$$

意思是：在当前线性模型中、控制其他已含变量后，这两个变量对应的系数是否同时为零。它是对一组参数限制的检验，不是对模型所有可能解释力的判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This asks whether the two coefficients are jointly zero after conditioning on the other included regressors. It tests a stated set of parameter restrictions, not every possible form of explanatory content in the model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] t 检验和 F 检验怎么分
> 单个参数用 t 检验；多个参数或多个线性约束一起检验，用 F 检验。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use a $t$ test for one parameter. Use an $F$ test to assess several parameters or linear restrictions jointly.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 检验 | 问的问题 | 典型场景 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| t 检验 | 一个系数是否等于某个值 | 某个变量是否显著 |
| F 检验 | 一组约束是否同时成立 | 一组变量是否联合显著 |
| [[Chow结构稳定性检验|Chow 检验]] | 两组样本系数是否稳定 | 事先给定的政策前后、危机前后 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Test | Question | Typical setting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $t$ test | Does one coefficient equal a specified value? | Is one variable significant? |
| $F$ test | Do several restrictions hold jointly? | Is a group of variables jointly significant? |
| [[Chow结构稳定性检验|Chow test]] | Are coefficients stable across two samples? | A prespecified split before versus after a policy or crisis |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.2.2. F 检验统计量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2.2. The $F$ statistic*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $RSS_r$ 是受约束模型的残差平方和，$RSS_u$ 是无约束模型的残差平方和，$q$ 是约束个数，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $RSS_r$ be the residual sum of squares from the restricted model, $RSS_u$ that from the unrestricted model, and $q$ the number of restrictions. Then:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
F=\frac{(RSS_r-RSS_u)/q}{RSS_u/(n-k-1)}
$$

其中 $k$ 是无约束模型中的解释变量个数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $k$ is the number of explanatory variables in the unrestricted model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一样本、同一响应、$q$ 个独立线性限制以及经典正态球形误差条件下，原假设成立时
$$
F\sim F_{q,n-k-1}.
$$
若使用异方差、HAC 或 cluster-robust 协方差，应改用相匹配的 Wald/F 校准，而不是把这个 RSS 公式直接标成“稳健”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the null, with the same observations and response, $q$ independent linear restrictions, and classical normal spherical errors, $F\sim F_{q,n-k-1}$. Heteroskedastic, HAC, or cluster-robust covariance estimators require a matching Wald or adjusted F calibration rather than relabelling this RSS formula as robust.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.2.3. F 检验的步骤
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2.3. Steps in an $F$ test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 写出原假设和备择假设。
2. 估计无约束模型，得到 $RSS_u$。
3. 估计受约束模型，得到 $RSS_r$。
4. 代入 F 统计量。
5. 与临界值或 $p$ 值比较，判断是否拒绝原假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** State the null and alternative hypotheses.<br>
**2.** Estimate the unrestricted model and obtain $RSS_u$.<br>
**3.** Estimate the restricted model and obtain $RSS_r$.<br>
**4.** Substitute the values into the $F$ statistic.<br>
**5.** Compare with a critical value or $p$-value and decide whether to reject the null.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.2.4. 具体步骤示例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2.4. Worked setup*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 Cobb-Douglas 生产函数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the Cobb–Douglas production function:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\ln Y=\beta_0+\beta_1\ln K+\beta_2\ln L+u
$$

若要检验规模报酬不变：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To test constant returns to scale:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_0:\beta_1+\beta_2=1
$$

这里真正的检验对象是系数组合 $\beta_1+\beta_2$，其不确定性依赖两项估计之间的协方差，见 [[线性组合推断]]。受约束模型可以通过代入 $\beta_2=1-\beta_1$ 得到，再与无约束模型比较残差平方和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The target is the coefficient combination $\beta_1+\beta_2$, whose uncertainty depends on the covariance between the two estimates; see [[线性组合推断|inference for linear combinations]]. Obtain the restricted model by substituting $\beta_2=1-\beta_1$, and then compare its residual sum of squares with that of the unrestricted model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.2.5. F 检验的一个例子：[[Chow结构稳定性检验|Chow 结构稳定性检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2.5. An application of the $F$ test: the Chow test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Chow 检验用于判断两个事先给定的时期或 groups 是否共享同一组回归系数。它不是用来先搜索最可疑的断点、再套一次普通 F 临界值的程序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Chow test asks whether two prespecified periods or groups share the same regression coefficients. It is not a procedure for searching for the most suspicious break and then applying an ordinary one-test F critical value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 分别估计两个子样本，得到 $RSS_1$ 和 $RSS_2$。
2. 合并样本估计，得到 $RSS_r$。
3. 无约束残差平方和为 $RSS_u=RSS_1+RSS_2$。
4. 统计量为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Estimate each subsample separately and obtain $RSS_1$ and $RSS_2$.<br>
**2.** Estimate the pooled sample and obtain $RSS_r$.<br>
**3.** The unrestricted residual sum of squares is $RSS_u=RSS_1+RSS_2$.<br>
**4.** The statistic is:<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
F=\frac{(RSS_r-(RSS_1+RSS_2))/k}{(RSS_1+RSS_2)/(n_1+n_2-2k)}
$$

其中 $k$ 是每个方程中待估参数个数。若 $F$ 足够大，说明两组样本的结构不稳定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $k$ is the number of parameters estimated in each equation. A sufficiently large $F$ indicates structural instability between the two samples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个经典 RSS 版本还依赖两组共同误差方差、独立性与其余经典线性模型条件。若误差存在异方差、时间相关或 cluster dependence，应改在合并样本的全交互模型中使用匹配协方差口径的 Wald/structural-break test。拒绝后只能说至少一个被检验系数改变，不能直接把变化归因于断点事件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The classical RSS version also relies on a common error variance across groups, independence, and the remaining classical linear-model conditions. With heteroskedastic, serially correlated, or clustered errors, use a covariance-matched Wald or structural-break test in the fully interacted pooled model. Rejection says only that at least one tested coefficient changed; it does not attribute the change to the event at the break.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

F 检验到这里给出了经典线性模型的 RSS 实现。要把同一组限制写成完整协方差矩阵下的距离，继续读 [[05_多元回归模型的矩阵表达#4.2. Wald 检验|Wald 联合检验]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The F test provides the classical RSS implementation. To express the same restrictions as a distance under a full covariance matrix, continue to the [[05_多元回归模型的矩阵表达#4.2. Wald 检验|joint Wald test]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4. 极大似然估计与似然比检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Maximum likelihood estimation and the likelihood-ratio test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. 极大似然估计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1. Maximum likelihood estimation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极大似然估计先写出样本出现的联合概率，再选择让该概率最大的参数。在线性回归中，若假定误差项正态分布，MLE 与 OLS 的系数估计相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maximum likelihood first writes down the joint probability of observing the sample and then chooses the parameter values that maximize it. In linear regression with normally distributed errors, MLE and OLS produce the same coefficient estimates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2. 似然比检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2. Likelihood-ratio test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

似然比检验比较受约束模型与无约束模型的最大似然值：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The likelihood-ratio test compares the maximized likelihoods of the restricted and unrestricted models:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
LR=-2(\ln L_r-\ln L_u)
$$

若 $LR$ 足够大，说明约束使模型拟合显著变差，应拒绝原假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sufficiently large $LR$ means the restrictions significantly worsen the fit, so the null should be rejected.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 5. 线性回归模型的扩展
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Extensions of the linear regression model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.1. 含有对数变化的模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.1. Models with logarithmic transformations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见解释方式：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common interpretations are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $\ln Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+u$：$X$ 增加 1 单位，对数方程的指数化拟合值约变化 $100\beta_1\%$；它不自动等于算术条件均值 $E(Y\mid X)$ 的变化。
- $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1\ln X+u$：$X$ 增加 1%，$Y$ 约变化 $0.01\beta_1$。
- $\ln Y=\beta_0+\beta_1\ln X+u$：$\beta_1$ 是对数方程指数化拟合值相对于 $X$ 的弹性；原尺度算术均值仍有重变换边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $\ln Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+u$: a one-unit increase in $X$ is associated with an approximate $100\beta_1\%$ change in the exponentiated fitted value from the log equation; this is not automatically the change in the arithmetic conditional mean $E(Y\mid X)$.
- $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1\ln X+u$: a 1% increase in $X$ is associated with an approximate $0.01\beta_1$ change in $Y$.
- $\ln Y=\beta_0+\beta_1\ln X+u$: $\beta_1$ is the elasticity of the exponentiated fitted value from the log equation with respect to $X$; the arithmetic mean on the original scale still has a retransformation boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 模型形式 | $\beta_1$ 的解释 |
| --- | --- |
| 水平-水平 | $X$ 增加 1，$Y$ 平均变 $\beta_1$ |
| 对数-水平 | $X$ 增加 1，对数方程的指数化拟合值约变 $100\beta_1\%$ |
| 水平-对数 | $X$ 增加 1%，$Y$ 约变 $0.01\beta_1$ |
| 对数-对数 | $X$ 增加 1%，对数方程的指数化拟合值约变 $\beta_1\%$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Model form | Interpretation of $\beta_1$ |
| --- | --- |
| Level–level | A one-unit increase in $X$ changes mean $Y$ by $\beta_1$ |
| Log–level | A one-unit increase in $X$ changes the exponentiated fitted value from the log equation by approximately $100\beta_1\%$ |
| Level–log | A 1% increase in $X$ changes $Y$ by approximately $0.01\beta_1$ |
| Log–log | A 1% increase in $X$ changes the exponentiated fitted value from the log equation by approximately $\beta_1\%$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.2. 环境库兹涅茨曲线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.2. Environmental Kuznets curve*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

环境库兹涅茨曲线常用二次项表示：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The environmental Kuznets curve is often specified with a quadratic term:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
WG_i=\beta_0+\beta_1PGDP_i+\beta_2PGDP_i^2+u_i
$$

边际影响为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The marginal effect is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\partial WG_i}{\partial PGDP_i}=\beta_1+2\beta_2PGDP_i
$$

若 $\beta_1>0,\beta_2<0$，拟合函数呈倒 U 型，转折点如下。只有该点落在数据支持范围且设定可信时，才有实质解释：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\beta_1>0$ and $\beta_2<0$, the fitted function is inverted-U-shaped, with the following turning point. It has a substantive interpretation only when it lies within the data-supported range and the specification is credible there:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
PGDP^*=-\frac{\beta_1}{2\beta_2}
$$

*6. Multiple-regression application: money-demand analysis*

## 6.1. JB 正态性检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6.1. Jarque–Bera normality test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Jarque-Bera 检验用于检查残差是否近似正态：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Jarque–Bera test checks whether residuals are approximately normal:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
JB=\frac{n}{6}\left(S^2+\frac{(K-3)^2}{4}\right)
$$

其中 $S$ 是偏度，$K$ 是峰度。若拒绝原假设，说明残差不服从正态分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $S$ is skewness and $K$ is kurtosis. Rejecting the null indicates that the residuals are not normally distributed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 7. 分布滞后模型与解释变量的选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Distributed-lag models and regressor selection*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7.1. [[分布滞后模型]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7.1. Distributed-lag model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分布滞后模型把解释变量的当前值和滞后值同时放入模型：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A distributed-lag model includes both current and lagged values of an explanatory variable:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_t=\alpha+\beta_0X_t+\beta_1X_{t-1}+\cdots+\beta_qX_{t-q}+u_t
$$

它用于描述解释变量影响被解释变量的动态过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It describes how the effect of an explanatory variable on the dependent variable unfolds over time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7.2. 滞后项阶数的选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7.2. Selecting the lag order*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!map] 信息准则在完整路径中的位置
> 先用 [[模型比较可比性]] 固定响应、样本与 likelihood 口径，再分别读 [[AIC]] 和 [[BIC]]；两者是目标不同的相对准则，不是自动验证。若滞后阶数用于预测，还要用 [[滚动起点评估]] 检查目标 horizon 上的样本外表现。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> First use [[模型比较可比性|model comparability]] to fix the outcome, sample, and likelihood convention, then read [[AIC]] and [[BIC]] separately. They are relative criteria with different targets, not automatic validation. If lag order serves forecasting, check out-of-sample performance at the target horizon through [[滚动起点评估|rolling-origin evaluation]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 7.2.1. [[AIC|赤池信息准则 AIC]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7.2.1. Akaike information criterion (AIC)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
AIC=\ln(\hat\sigma^2)+\frac{2k}{n}
$$

这是同方差 Gaussian regression 中删去候选间共同常数、再按 $n$ 缩放后的同序形式；$k$ 的参数计数必须在所有候选中保持一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an order-equivalent form for homoskedastic Gaussian regression after constants common to the candidates are removed and the criterion is scaled by $n$. Parameter counting in $k$ must remain consistent across candidates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 7.2.2. [[BIC|施瓦茨准则 SC]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7.2.2. Schwarz criterion (SC/BIC)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
SC=\ln(\hat\sigma^2)+\frac{k\ln n}{n}
$$

在同一组可比候选中，信息准则越小越优先；绝对数值本身没有独立含义。AIC 与 SC/BIC 的参数惩罚和推导目标不同，不能把二者的胜负理解成其中一个普遍更可靠。如果选出的最后一期滞后项在经济含义上明显不合理，也要回到理论、样本和模型设定重新判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within one comparable candidate set, smaller information-criterion values are preferred; the absolute value has no independent meaning. AIC and SC/BIC use different penalties and target different comparison problems, so neither is universally more reliable. If the final selected lag is economically implausible, return to the theory, sample, and specification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 信息准则不是自动答案
> AIC、SC 只是统计选择工具。若选出的滞后项符号违背清楚的经济机制，要重新检查样本、模型和理论设定。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> AIC and SC are statistical selection tools. If the selected lag has a sign that contradicts a clear economic mechanism, re-examine the sample, model, and theoretical specification.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
