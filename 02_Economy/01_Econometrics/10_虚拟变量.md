# 1. [[事件指示变量|虚拟解释变量]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Indicator explanatory variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

虚拟变量不是一种新的估计方法，而是把“属于哪一类”写进回归的方法。令 $D_i=1$ 表示个体 $i$ 属于目标类别、$D_i=0$ 表示不属于；数学上它就是[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]。当 0/1 放在等号右侧时，我们比较类别；当 0/1 放在等号左侧时，我们解释事件概率。后一种问题从[[#2. 二元结果模型|第 2 节]]开始。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An indicator variable is not a new estimator; it is a way to represent category membership in a regression. Let $D_i=1$ when unit $i$ belongs to the target category and $D_i=0$ otherwise. Mathematically this is an [[事件指示变量|indicator]]. A zero-one variable on the right-hand side compares categories; a zero-one variable on the left-hand side makes event probability the outcome, which begins in [[#2. 二元结果模型|Section 2]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[虚拟变量与交互项.canvas]]

## 1.1 一项指示变量先建立“比较组—基准组”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1 One indicator creates a comparison group and a reference group*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在没有包含 $D$ 的交互项时，可写
$$
E[Y_i\mid D_i,Z_i]
=\beta_0+\beta_DD_i+\gamma'Z_i.
$$
给定同一个 $Z=z$，$D=0$ 基准组的模型均值是 $\beta_0+\gamma'z$，$D=1$ 比较组的是 $\beta_0+\beta_D+\gamma'z$，所以
$$
E[Y\mid D=1,z]-E[Y\mid D=0,z]=\beta_D.
$$
这就是[[指示变量系数]]的读法：$\beta_D$ 是比较组相对基准组的条件均值差，不是比较组本身的水平。没有控制变量时，OLS 的 $\hat\beta_D$ 恰好是两个样本组均值之差；加入控制后，它依赖模型对“相同 $Z$”的表达。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without an interaction involving $D$, write $E[Y_i\mid D_i,Z_i]=\beta_0+\beta_DD_i+\gamma'Z_i$. At the same $Z=z$, the model-implied means for the reference and comparison groups are $\beta_0+\gamma'z$ and $\beta_0+\beta_D+\gamma'z$, so their difference is $\beta_D$. As explained in [[指示变量系数|the indicator-coefficient atom]], this is a conditional contrast with the reference group, not the comparison group's absolute level. Without controls, OLS makes $\hat\beta_D$ the difference between the two sample means; with controls, it relies on the model's representation of equal $Z$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $D$ 表示培训或政策，把它编码成 0/1 并不会让处理随机化。上述差异首先是描述性条件对比；只有一致性、可比性与共同支持等条件成立时，才可进一步解释为处理效应，见[[条件均值因果边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $D$ denotes training or a policy, zero-one coding does not randomise treatment. The contrast is descriptive first; a treatment-effect interpretation additionally requires conditions such as consistency, exchangeability, and common support, as set out in [[条件均值因果边界|the causal boundary for conditional means]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2 多类别变量需要明确基准，而不是把标签当距离
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2 Multicategory variables need a reference, not numerical distances between labels*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若地区有 A、B、C 三类，把它们写成 1、2、3 并直接放入一列，会强加“A 到 B 与 B 到 C 的变化相同”这一额外限制。通常应以 A 为基准，在含截距模型中放入 $D_B$ 和 $D_C$：
$$
E[Y_i\mid G_i]
=\beta_0+\beta_BD_{Bi}+\beta_CD_{Ci}.
$$
三个组的拟合均值分别是 $\beta_0$、$\beta_0+\beta_B$ 和 $\beta_0+\beta_C$。一般地，$m$ 类在含截距模型中使用 $m-1$ 个指示列；若把全部 $m$ 列与截距同时放入，它们逐行相加恒等于 1，设计矩阵便不满列秩。完整规则及无截距参数化见[[多类别指示编码]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For regions A, B, and C, entering labels 1, 2, and 3 as a single regressor imposes the extra restriction that the A-to-B and B-to-C changes are equal. With A as the reference, an intercept model instead uses $D_B$ and $D_C$: $E[Y_i\mid G_i]=\beta_0+\beta_BD_{Bi}+\beta_CD_{Ci}$. The fitted group means are $\beta_0$, $\beta_0+\beta_B$, and $\beta_0+\beta_C$. In general, an intercept model uses $m-1$ indicators for $m$ categories; all $m$ indicators would sum to the intercept column and destroy full column rank. See [[多类别指示编码|multicategory indicator coding]] for the complete rule and the no-intercept alternative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换成 B 作基准后，截距和各行系数会改变，但只要仍表示同一个完整类别模型，三个组的拟合值、残差和任何事先定义的组间对比都不变。[[基准组重编码]]说明：基准组改变的是系数坐标以及单行检验回答的问题，不是底层拟合对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing the reference to B changes the intercept and coefficient rows, but—provided the same complete categorical model is represented—it leaves all three fitted group means, residuals, and prespecified contrasts unchanged. [[基准组重编码|Reference-group reparameterisation]] changes coefficient coordinates and the question asked by an individual test, not the underlying fit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3 类别与连续变量交互：把一条线放宽为两条线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3 Indicator-by-continuous interaction: allowing two regression lines*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要问经验回报是否因组别而变，平行线模型不够。加入乘积项：
$$
E[Y\mid X,D]
=\beta_0+\beta_XX+\beta_DD+\beta_{XD}XD.
$$
分别代入 $D=0$ 和 $D=1$，才会看清四个系数的职责：

| 对象 | 条件均值 | $X$ 的斜率 |
|---|---|---|
| $D=0$ 基准组 | $\beta_0+\beta_XX$ | $\beta_X$ |
| $D=1$ 比较组 | $(\beta_0+\beta_D)+(\beta_X+\beta_{XD})X$ | $\beta_X+\beta_{XD}$ |
| $X=x$ 处的组差 | $\beta_D+\beta_{XD}x$ | — |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To ask whether the return to experience differs by group, replace the parallel-lines restriction with $E[Y\mid X,D]=\beta_0+\beta_XX+\beta_DD+\beta_{XD}XD$. Substituting $D=0$ and $D=1$ reveals each coefficient's role:

| Object | Conditional mean | Slope in $X$ |
|---|---|---|
| Reference group, $D=0$ | $\beta_0+\beta_XX$ | $\beta_X$ |
| Comparison group, $D=1$ | $(\beta_0+\beta_D)+(\beta_X+\beta_{XD})X$ | $\beta_X+\beta_{XD}$ |
| Group difference at $X=x$ | $\beta_D+\beta_{XD}x$ | — |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，$\beta_{XD}$ 是两组斜率之差，$\beta_D$ 只是在 $X=0$ 时的组差。若要报告 10 年经验处的组差，应计算 $\beta_D+10\beta_{XD}$，并用完整协方差矩阵得到它的标准误，见[[类别连续交互]]和[[线性组合推断]]。若 $X=0$ 没有实际意义，可以把 $X$ 中心化到有意义且有数据支持的 $c$；这会把 $\beta_D$ 的参照点移到 $X=c$，却不会创造不存在的比较对象，边界见[[交互主效应基准]]与[[中心化共线性边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $\beta_{XD}$ is the difference between slopes, while $\beta_D$ is the group difference only at $X=0$. A report about ten years of experience needs $\beta_D+10\beta_{XD}$ and its standard error from the full covariance matrix; see [[类别连续交互|indicator-by-continuous interaction]] and [[线性组合推断|linear-combination inference]]. If zero is not meaningful, centring $X$ at a meaningful, well-supported value $c$ moves the interpretation of $\beta_D$ to $X=c$ without creating observations that do not exist; see [[交互主效应基准|the reference-value boundary]] and [[中心化共线性边界|the centring boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保留交互项时，通常也要保留组成它的低阶项。删掉 $X$ 会强制基准组斜率为零；删掉 $D$ 会强制两组在 $X=0$ 处截距相同。除非理论明确要求这些约束，否则不应仅因某个主效应 $p$ 值较大而删除，见[[交互层级原则]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An interaction model should normally retain its component main effects. Omitting $X$ forces the reference-group slope to zero; omitting $D$ forces equal intercepts at $X=0$. Unless theory explicitly requires such restrictions, a large p-value on a main effect is not a reason to impose them; see [[交互层级原则|the hierarchy principle]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.4 两个类别变量交互：先还原四格均值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.4 Interaction between two categorical variables: reconstruct the four cells first*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对两个二元变量 $D_1,D_2$，饱和线性模型是
$$
E[Y\mid D_1,D_2]
=\beta_0+\beta_1D_1+\beta_2D_2+\beta_{12}D_1D_2.
$$
若 $\mu_{ab}=E[Y\mid D_1=a,D_2=b]$，则
$$
\beta_{12}
=(\mu_{11}-\mu_{01})-(\mu_{10}-\mu_{00}).
$$
也就是说，交互系数回答“$D_2$ 从 0 变 1 后，$D_1$ 的组差改变了多少”，而 $\beta_1$ 只是在 $D_2=0$ 层内的组差。完整四格读法由[[类别类别交互]]承担。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For binary $D_1,D_2$, the saturated linear model is $E[Y\mid D_1,D_2]=\beta_0+\beta_1D_1+\beta_2D_2+\beta_{12}D_1D_2$. If $\mu_{ab}=E[Y\mid D_1=a,D_2=b]$, then $\beta_{12}=(\mu_{11}-\mu_{01})-(\mu_{10}-\mu_{00})$. The interaction asks how the $D_1$ group contrast changes when $D_2$ changes from zero to one; $\beta_1$ is only the $D_1$ contrast within $D_2=0$. See [[类别类别交互|the categorical-by-categorical interaction]] for the complete four-cell reading.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $D_1$ 是处理组、$D_2$ 是处理后时期，这个代数对象就是[[2×2 DID回归等价|2×2 DID 回归交互项]]。但交互项等于四格差异之差，并不自动证明它是因果 ATT；平行趋势、无预期和研究设计仍需另行成立。若因变量本身是 0/1，Logit/Probit 的指数交互系数也不等于概率尺度的四格对比，这一边界留到[[概率交互效应]]与[[交互系数不等于概率交互]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $D_1$ marks the treated group and $D_2$ the post period, the algebra becomes the [[2×2 DID回归等价|2×2 DID regression interaction]]. Equality with a four-cell difference in differences does not by itself identify a causal ATT; parallel trends, no anticipation, and the research design remain separate requirements. With a binary outcome, a logit or probit interaction coefficient in the index also differs from the four-cell contrast on the probability scale; that boundary is handled by [[概率交互效应|probability interactions]] and [[交互系数不等于概率交互|the nonlinear interaction-coefficient boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.5 先检查支持，再解释、推断和命名
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.5 Check support before interpretation, inference, and naming*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交互分析真正依赖的是目标组合附近有没有信息。两个类别变量先列每个格子的观测数；类别与连续变量交互则先画各组 $X$ 的范围。空格会让相应饱和对比不可识别；格子太稀或两组几乎没有共同 $X$ 范围时，软件即使返回数字，结论也可能主要来自少数观测或函数形式外推，见[[交互稀疏支持]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interaction analysis depends on information near the target configurations. For two categorical variables, tabulate every cell; for an indicator-by-continuous interaction, plot the support of $X$ within each group. An empty cell makes the corresponding saturated contrast unidentified. Sparse cells or little overlap in $X$ can make a numerical result depend mainly on a few observations or functional-form extrapolation; see [[交互稀疏支持|the sparse-support boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后不要从回归表里挑一行开始解释。先写清编码与基准组，再重建目标配置的完整预测，构造组差、斜率差或四格对比，随后用匹配数据依赖结构的协方差估计量给出区间；只有识别条件满足时才把“模型内条件对比”命名为因果效应。这条可重复的顺序见[[交互解释流程]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Do not begin by selecting one row from a coefficient table. State the coding and reference group, reconstruct complete predictions at the target configurations, form the group difference, slope difference, or four-cell contrast, and then attach an interval using a covariance estimator matched to the data's dependence structure. Call the result causal only after the identifying conditions are met. This reusable sequence is set out in [[交互解释流程|the interaction-interpretation workflow]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

至此，0/1 仍是解释变量的编码。下一节把 0/1 放到等号左侧，研究对象便改为事件发生概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Up to this point, zero-one coding has been used for explanatory variables. The next section places zero-one on the left-hand side, making event probability the object of study.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 二元结果模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Binary-outcome models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二元结果模型不是先从 Logit 或 Probit 开始，而是先认清研究对象：给定 $X$ 时，事件 $Y=1$ 发生的概率。[[二元结果模型.canvas|主题总图]]把 LPM、Logit、Probit、预测解释和面板边界放在同一张关系图上；本节则按可以连续学习的顺序展开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A binary-outcome model does not begin with a choice between logit and probit. It begins by identifying the object of interest: the probability that the event $Y=1$ occurs, conditional on $X$. The [[二元结果模型.canvas|topic map]] places the LPM, logit, probit, interpretation, and panel boundaries in one relationship view; this section develops them in a continuous reading order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1. 先锁定共同的概率目标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Begin with the common probability target*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当被解释变量只取 0 或 1 时，模型解释的是某事件发生的概率：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the outcome takes only 0 or 1, the model describes the probability that an event occurs:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(Y_i=1\mid X_i)
$$

例如是否就业、是否违约、是否购买、是否通过考试。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Examples include whether someone is employed, defaults, makes a purchase, or passes an examination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $p(X_i)=P(Y_i=1\mid X_i)$。因为 $Y_i$ 只能取 0 或 1，
$$
E(Y_i\mid X_i)=p(X_i),
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(Y_i\mid X_i)=p(X_i)[1-p(X_i)].
$$
第一条等式说明，三种常见模型其实都在表达同一个[[二元结果条件均值|条件概率目标]]；第二条等式给出[[二元结果条件方差|二元结果的条件方差]]。它们没有自动赋予因果含义：若问题是“改变 $X$ 会不会改变事件概率”，仍需另外说明干预和识别条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $p(X_i)=P(Y_i=1\mid X_i)$. Because $Y_i$ can take only zero or one, $E(Y_i\mid X_i)=p(X_i)$ and $\operatorname{Var}(Y_i\mid X_i)=p(X_i)[1-p(X_i)]$. The first identity shows that the three common models express the same [[二元结果条件均值|conditional-probability target]]; the second gives [[二元结果条件方差|the conditional variance of a binary outcome]]. Neither identity is automatically causal. A question about what would happen if $X$ were changed still requires a defined intervention and separate identifying conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.1. 潜变量阈值是一种解释入口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.1. A latent threshold is one route to interpretation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解释 Logit 或 Probit 时，可以假设存在一个不可观测的连续潜变量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To interpret logit or probit, suppose there is an unobserved continuous latent variable:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_i^*=X_i'\beta+u_i
$$

### 2.1.2. 阈值把潜变量映射成实际结果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.2. A threshold maps the latent variable into the observed outcome*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际观测到的是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
What is actually observed is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_i=
\begin{cases}
1,&Y_i^*>0\\
0,&Y_i^*\le0
\end{cases}
$$

这里只观察潜变量落在阈值哪一侧，因此潜变量的绝对尺度不能从二元结果中单独识别。Logit 与 Probit 通过不同的误差分布规范化尺度；这条[[潜变量二元选择|潜变量路线]]有助于理解二者，但不是使用 LPM 的前提。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only the side of the threshold is observed, so the absolute scale of the latent variable cannot be identified from the binary outcome alone. Logit and probit normalise that scale through different error distributions. This [[潜变量二元选择|latent-variable route]] helps explain those models, but it is not a prerequisite for using the LPM.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2. [[线性概率模型|线性概率模型]]：直接在线性尺度上近似概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2. The linear probability model: approximating probability on a linear scale*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.2.1. 模型与 OLS 目标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2.1. The model and its OLS target*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线性概率模型写成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The linear probability model is written as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_i=X_i'\beta+u_i
$$

参数通常用 [[LPM的OLS估计|OLS 估计]]；只有在明确模型口径与概率支持后，拟合值才可按模型内概率解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Its parameters are usually obtained by [[LPM的OLS估计|OLS estimation]]. Fitted values can be interpreted as model-implied probabilities only after stating the model target and checking the probability support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若连续变量 $X_j$ 只以单一、未变换、无交互的主效应进入，[[LPM系数|$\beta_j$ 直接是概率斜率]]；若 $D$ 是 0/1 解释变量，相同条件下 $\beta_D$ 是从 $D=0$ 到 $D=1$ 的概率差。平方项、变换或交互会让目标效应变成多个系数的组合。基本加性 LPM 同时施加[[LPM常数斜率|概率斜率不随 $X$ 改变]]的限制；这可以是有用的局部近似，也可能遗漏弯曲或饱和关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a continuous $X_j$ enters only as one untransformed main effect without interactions, [[LPM系数|$\beta_j$ is directly a probability slope]]. Under the same condition, if $D$ is a zero-one regressor, $\beta_D$ is the probability difference between $D=0$ and $D=1$. Squares, transformations, or interactions turn the target effect into a combination of coefficients. A basic additive LPM also imposes the [[LPM常数斜率|restriction that the probability slope does not vary with $X$]]. This can be a useful local approximation or a misspecification that misses curvature or saturation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] “拟合值是概率”的精确边界
> LPM 用 $X_i'\beta$ 近似条件概率；只有落在 $[0,1]$ 内的数值才是合法概率。越界不是显示问题，事后截断也不会修复条件均值规格或原有推断，见 [[LPM预测越界]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The LPM uses $X_i'\beta$ to approximate a conditional probability, but only values in $[0,1]$ are admissible probabilities. An out-of-range fit is not merely a display problem, and clipping it afterwards does not repair the conditional-mean specification or the original inference; see [[LPM预测越界|out-of-range LPM predictions]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.2.2. 问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2.2. Problems*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

LPM 的主要问题：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The main problems with the LPM are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 预测值可能小于 0 或大于 1。
- 条件方差通常随 $p(X)$ 改变，因此实践中通常异方差。
- 基本加性规格把连续解释变量的概率斜率强制设为常数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Predicted values can be below 0 or above 1.
- The conditional variance usually varies with $p(X)$, so heteroskedasticity is typical in practice.
- A basic additive specification forces a continuous regressor's probability slope to be constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] “天然异方差”的精确边界
> 由 $\operatorname{Var}(Y_i\mid X_i)=p_i(1-p_i)$ 可知，只要 $p_i(1-p_i)$ 随 $X_i$ 改变，LPM 误差就异方差，所以实践中通常如此；但若该条件方差在支持集上恰好不变，就不是逻辑上的必然。稳健标准误只修正推断，不会修复越界或错误函数形式，见 [[LPM异方差]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Since $\operatorname{Var}(Y_i\mid X_i)=p_i(1-p_i)$, the LPM error is heteroskedastic whenever this quantity varies with $X_i$, as it usually does in practice. It is not a logical necessity if the conditional variance happens to be constant on the support. Robust standard errors repair inference only; they do not repair out-of-range predictions or a wrong functional form. See [[LPM异方差|heteroskedasticity in the LPM]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] LPM 的边界
> LPM 可以作为直观起点，但预测概率越靠近 0 或 1，线性形式越容易失真。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The LPM is an intuitive starting point, but its linear form becomes more prone to distortion as predicted probabilities approach 0 or 1.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.2.3. 拟合数字不能替代模型诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2.3. Fit statistics do not replace model diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二元模型输出中的普通 $R^2$、各种伪 $R^2$ 与分类准确率回答的不是同一个问题。伪 $R^2$ 不是“被解释的概率方差比例”，不同定义之间也不能随意横比；准确率则依赖分类阈值和事件基准率。它们可以描述某一方面，却不能证明函数形式正确、预测可靠或效应具有因果含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary $R^2$, the various pseudo-$R^2$ measures, and classification accuracy answer different questions in a binary model. A pseudo-$R^2$ is not a proportion of probability variance explained, and distinct definitions are not freely comparable. Accuracy depends on the classification threshold and the event base rate. Each can describe one aspect of fit, but none establishes a correct functional form, reliable probability forecasts, or a causal effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果目标是解释某个概率差或边际效应，就先定义目标量、检查支持和推断口径；如果目标是预测概率，就在未参与拟合的数据上同时检查适当概率损失、区分能力和[[分类概率校准|校准]]。完整的选择顺序见[[二元模型选择]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the goal is to interpret a probability contrast or marginal effect, first define the estimand and check support and inference. If the goal is probability prediction, evaluate a proper probability loss, discrimination, and [[分类概率校准|calibration]] on data not used to fit the model. The full decision sequence is developed in [[二元模型选择|the binary-model selection atom]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3. Logit 与 Probit：用链接函数约束概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3. Logit and probit: constraining probabilities with a link function*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.3.1. 三种表达的差别
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3.1. How the three representations differ*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 模型 | 概率函数 | 优点 | 局限 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LPM | $P_i=X_i'\beta$ | 直观、可用 OLS | 概率可能越界 |
| Logit | Logistic 分布 | 简单主效应可作胜算比解释 | 系数不能直接当概率变化 |
| Probit | 正态分布 | 潜变量解释自然 | 没有胜算比捷径，需回到概率尺度 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Model | Probability function | Advantage | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LPM | $P_i=X_i'\beta$ | Intuitive and estimable by OLS | Probabilities may fall outside the valid range |
| Logit | Logistic distribution | A simple main effect has an odds-ratio interpretation | Coefficients are not direct probability changes |
| Probit | Normal distribution | Natural latent-variable interpretation | No odds-ratio shortcut; return to the probability scale |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了让预测概率落在 $[0,1]$ 内，可以使用 S 形分布函数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To keep predicted probabilities within $[0,1]$, use an S-shaped distribution function:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(Y_i=1\mid X_i)=F(X_i'\beta)
$$

Logit 使用 Logistic 分布，Probit 使用标准正态分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Logit uses the logistic distribution, while probit uses the standard normal distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.3.2. [[Logit模型|Logit 模型]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3.2. Logit model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (1) 概率函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Probability function*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Logit 模型为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The logit model is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P_i=P(Y_i=1\mid X_i)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-X_i'\beta}}
$$

因此：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
1-P_i=\frac{e^{-X_i'\beta}}{1+e^{-X_i'\beta}}
$$

#### (2) [[Logit胜算比|胜算与胜算比]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Odds ratio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

事件的胜算为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The odds are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{P_i}{1-P_i}=e^{X_i'\beta}
$$

取对数得到 logit：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking logarithms gives the logit:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\ln\left(\frac{P_i}{1-P_i}\right)=X_i'\beta
$$

所以当 $X_j$ 只以一个未变换、无交互的主效应进入时，$X_j$ 增加 1 单位会使对数胜算变化 $\beta_j$，并使胜算乘以 $e^{\beta_j}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus, when $X_j$ enters only as one untransformed main effect, a one-unit increase changes the log odds by $\beta_j$ and multiplies the odds by $e^{\beta_j}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若模型含有 $X_j$ 的变换或交互项，一单位变化会同时改变多个指数项，必须联合计算完整线性指数；见 [[Logit胜算比]]。胜算比也不是概率比或概率差：例如概率从 0.2 增至 0.3 时，胜算比约为 1.71，而概率比是 1.5，概率差是 0.1。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the model contains transformations or interactions involving $X_j$, a one-unit change moves several index terms and the complete linear index must be evaluated jointly; see [[Logit胜算比|logit odds ratios]]. An odds ratio is also neither a probability ratio nor a probability difference. For example, a probability increase from 0.2 to 0.3 has an odds ratio of about 1.71, a probability ratio of 1.5, and a probability difference of 0.1.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] Logit 系数怎么解释
> 直接读 $\beta_j$ 容易抽象；在上述简单主效应条件下，读 $e^{\beta_j}$ 更直观，它表示胜算乘以多少。若真正关心的是百分点变化，下一步仍应计算预测概率或边际效应。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $\beta_j$ can feel abstract. Under the simple-main-effect conditions above, $e^{\beta_j}$ is often more intuitive because it states the multiplicative change in the odds. If the substantive question concerns percentage-point changes, predicted probabilities or marginal effects are still required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (3) 分组比例的历史线性化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Historical linearisation of grouped proportions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果样本按相同 $X$ 分组，可以先计算每组样本比例 $\hat P_i$，再对
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If observations are grouped by common values of $X$, first compute the sample proportion $\hat P_i$ for each group, then regress
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\ln\left(\frac{\hat P_i}{1-\hat P_i}\right)
$$

做近似线性回归。这条路线只适用于真正按相同协变量取值汇总的分组二项数据，还需要正确处理组样本量不同带来的精度差异。若某组比例恰为 0 或 1，样本 logit 会发散；若在个体数据上人为分组，还会丢失信息。它是理解 log-odds 线性化的历史入口，不是现代个体二元数据估计的默认方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
on the regressors as a linear approximation. This route applies only to genuinely grouped binomial data with common covariate values, and unequal group sizes must be reflected in precision. A group proportion of exactly zero or one makes the sample logit diverge, while artificial grouping of individual data discards information. It is a historical route to understanding log-odds linearisation, not the default estimator for modern individual binary data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### (4) [[二元响应MLE|Bernoulli 极大似然]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(4) Bernoulli maximum likelihood*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

个体层面的二元选择模型通常用极大似然估计。似然函数为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Individual-level binary-choice models are usually estimated by maximum likelihood. The likelihood is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
L(\beta)=\prod_{i=1}^n P_i^{Y_i}(1-P_i)^{1-Y_i}
$$

对数似然为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The log likelihood is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\ell(\beta)=\sum_{i=1}^n\left[
Y_i\ln P_i+(1-Y_i)\ln(1-P_i)
\right]
$$

通过最大化 $\ell(\beta)$ 得到参数估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Parameter estimates are obtained by maximizing $\ell(\beta)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] 二元选择模型的复习线索
> LPM 是线性近似；Logit 和 Probit 是把概率压进 $[0,1]$ 的非线性模型；估计通常靠极大似然。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The LPM is a linear approximation. Logit and probit are nonlinear models that keep probabilities within $[0,1]$, and they are usually estimated by maximum likelihood.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.3.3. [[Probit模型|Probit 模型]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3.3. Probit model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Probit 模型使用标准正态分布函数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The probit model uses the standard normal distribution function:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(Y_i=1\mid X_i)=\Phi(X_i'\beta)
$$

其中 $\Phi(\cdot)$ 是标准正态累积分布函数。它和 Logit 的用途相似，但尾部形状略有不同，系数大小不能直接与 Logit 系数比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\Phi(\cdot)$ is the standard normal cumulative distribution function. Probit serves a similar purpose to logit, but its tail shape differs slightly and its coefficient magnitudes cannot be compared directly with logit coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.3.4. 有限 MLE 并不自动存在
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3.4. A finite MLE does not automatically exist*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极大似然的数值程序停下来，不等于模型一定有有限、可解释的估计。若某个非零线性组合能够把所有 $Y=1$ 与 $Y=0$ 观测严格分开，就发生[[完全分离]]，通常的 Logit 与 Probit 有限 MLE 不存在；若两类只在分隔边界上相接，则要进一步检查[[准完全分离]]。软件可能删掉变量或观测、给出极大的系数和标准误，或看似“收敛”，但这些表现都不能代替对完整设计矩阵的检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The numerical optimiser stopping does not imply that the model has a finite, interpretable estimate. If a nonzero linear score strictly separates every $Y=1$ observation from every $Y=0$ observation, [[完全分离|complete separation]] occurs and the usual finite MLE for logit and probit does not exist. If the classes meet only on the separating boundary, [[准完全分离|quasi-complete separation]] must be checked separately. Software may drop variables or observations, return very large coefficients and standard errors, or appear to converge, but none of these behaviours replaces inspection of the complete design matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分离是数据支持与有限估计的问题，不是“显著性太强”。扩大样本、重新定义过细类别、使用有明确目标的惩罚或贝叶斯方法都可能改变处理方式，但不能把失败的普通 MLE 当成已经被证明的巨大效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separation is a problem of data support and finite estimation, not “extreme significance”. More data, reconsidering excessively fine categories, or using a penalty or prior chosen for an explicit target may change the appropriate response, but a failed ordinary MLE must not be reported as evidence of an enormous effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.4. 从链接尺度翻译到预测概率和实质效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4. Translate the link scale into predicted probabilities and substantive effects*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.4.1. 预测必须重建完整指数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4.1. Prediction requires the complete index*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对给定协变量配置 $x$，三种模型的[[二元预测概率|模型内预测概率]]分别是
$$
\hat p(x)=
\begin{cases}
x'\hat\beta, & \text{LPM},\\
\Lambda(x'\hat\beta), & \text{Logit},\\
\Phi(x'\hat\beta), & \text{Probit}.
\end{cases}
$$
这里的 $x'\hat\beta$ 必须包含截距、变量变换、类别项和交互项。把某一个系数单独送进 Logistic 或正态 CDF，不会得到某个人或某种政策配置的概率；这条计算规则见[[预测须用完整指数]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a stated covariate configuration $x$, the three [[二元预测概率|model-implied probabilities]] are $x'\hat\beta$ for the LPM, $\Lambda(x'\hat\beta)$ for logit, and $\Phi(x'\hat\beta)$ for probit. The index $x'\hat\beta$ must include the intercept, transformations, categorical terms, and interactions. Feeding one coefficient into the logistic or normal CDF does not produce the probability for a person or policy configuration; see [[预测须用完整指数|the complete-index rule]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预测概率首先是拟合模型在指定 $x$ 上的输出。它是否能代表真实风险，要靠样本外验证；它是否能解释为干预效应，还要靠额外的因果识别条件。模型把数值压进 $(0,1)$ 只解决了概率支持，不会同时解决这两个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A predicted probability is first an output of the fitted model at a stated $x$. Whether it represents real-world risk requires out-of-sample validation; whether a difference can be interpreted as an intervention effect requires additional causal identification. Mapping a number into $(0,1)$ solves the probability-support problem, not either of those further questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.4.2. 连续变量用局部导数，离散变量用状态切换
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4.2. Use a local derivative for a continuous variable and a state change for a discrete variable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $p(x)=G[\eta(x)]$，连续变量 $X_j$ 在给定 $x$ 处的[[非线性边际效应|概率边际效应]]是
$$
\frac{\partial p(x)}{\partial x_j}
=g[\eta(x)]\frac{\partial\eta(x)}{\partial x_j},
\qquad g=G'.
$$
只有当 $X_j$ 仅以 $\beta_jX_j$ 进入指数时，才可简化成 $g(x'\beta)\beta_j$。曲线位置和涉及 $X_j$ 的所有平方项、变换与交互项都要进入导数，所以同一个系数在不同人身上可以对应不同的百分点变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $p(x)=G[\eta(x)]$, the [[非线性边际效应|probability marginal effect]] of a continuous $X_j$ at $x$ is $g[\eta(x)]\,\partial\eta(x)/\partial x_j$, where $g=G'$. It reduces to $g(x'\beta)\beta_j$ only when $X_j$ enters the index solely through $\beta_jX_j$. The location on the probability curve and every square, transformation, and interaction involving $X_j$ enter the derivative, so the same coefficient can imply different percentage-point changes for different observations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 0/1 解释变量 $D$，不存在有实际含义的“无穷小增加”。应计算[[二元离散效应|两种状态的完整预测之差]]：
$$
\Delta_D(z)=G[\eta(D=1,z)]-G[\eta(D=0,z)].
$$
两边都要重算完整指数。多类别变量同理：先说清基准类别和目标类别，再比较两套完整预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a zero-one regressor $D$, an infinitesimal increase has no substantive meaning. Compute the [[二元离散效应|difference between two complete predictions]] instead: $\Delta_D(z)=G[\eta(D=1,z)]-G[\eta(D=0,z)]$. Reconstruct the full index in both states. The same rule applies to a multicategory variable: state the reference and target categories, then compare the two complete predictions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.4.3. 先逐个计算，再决定怎样汇总
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4.3. Compute observation-specific effects before choosing how to aggregate them*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

边际效应依赖协变量取值，因此报告一个效应前必须说明在哪些点上计算。[[平均边际效应|平均边际效应（AME）]]先在每个观测的协变量值上求导数或离散差，再按声明的样本或权重平均。它不同于“在均值人处的边际效应”；改变目标样本、权重或反事实配置，也会改变汇总结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Marginal effects depend on covariate values, so an effect must state where it is evaluated. An [[平均边际效应|average marginal effect (AME)]] first computes a derivative or discrete change at each observation's covariate values and then averages using a declared sample or weighting distribution. This differs from a marginal effect evaluated for a synthetic person at the mean covariates. Changing the target sample, weights, or counterfactual configuration can change the reported aggregate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.4.4. 概率交互不能只读交互项系数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4.4. A probability interaction cannot be read from the interaction coefficient alone*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 Logit 指数为
$$
\eta=-1+0.8D+0.5X-0.6DX.
$$
在 $X=0$ 时，把 $D$ 从 0 切换到 1，预测概率约从 0.269 变为 0.450，离散效应约为 0.181；在 $X=1$ 时，同一切换使概率约从 0.378 变为 0.426，效应约为 0.048。因此[[概率交互效应|概率尺度的交互]]约为 $0.048-0.181=-0.133$，而不是指数里的交互系数 $-0.6$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a logit index $\eta=-1+0.8D+0.5X-0.6DX$. At $X=0$, switching $D$ from zero to one moves the predicted probability from about 0.269 to 0.450, a discrete effect of about 0.181. At $X=1$, the same switch moves the probability from about 0.378 to 0.426, an effect of about 0.048. The [[概率交互效应|interaction on the probability scale]] is therefore about $0.048-0.181=-0.133$, not the index interaction coefficient $-0.6$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非线性链接的曲率本身也会进入概率交互，因此一般存在[[交互系数不等于概率交互|“交互系数不等于概率交互效应”]]这一边界。实际报告应在有数据支持的配置上重建四格预测或条件斜率，并用完整协方差矩阵传播不确定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The curvature of a nonlinear link also enters a probability interaction, which creates the general [[交互系数不等于概率交互|boundary that an interaction coefficient is not a probability interaction]]. A report should reconstruct the four-cell predictions or conditional slopes at supported configurations and propagate uncertainty using the full covariance matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.5. 怎样在 LPM、Logit 与 Probit 之间选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.5. How to choose among the LPM, logit, and probit*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[二元模型选择|模型选择]]应从问题出发，而不是从哪一列系数更显著出发：

- 若目标是一个在样本支持区间内易于解释的平均概率斜率或处理效应，LPM 常给出透明基准，但要检查函数形式、越界和真实依赖结构下的标准误。
- 若目标是保证模型内概率合法、刻画非线性响应或进行似然建模，Logit/Probit 更自然；随后必须把链接尺度翻译成预测或效应。
- Logit 与 Probit 的误差尺度规范化不同，原始系数大小不能直接比较。两者概率预测往往接近时，应根据估计目标、尾部支持、规格和验证表现，而不是系数大小决定。
- 若目标是概率预测，应在未见数据上比较严格适当的概率损失、区分能力与校准；固定阈值下的准确率和训练内伪 $R^2$ 都不能单独作决定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[二元模型选择|Model choice]] begins with the question, not with whichever coefficient table looks more significant:

- If the target is an easily interpreted average probability slope or treatment contrast within the observed support, the LPM often provides a transparent benchmark, but its functional form, out-of-range predictions, and dependence-appropriate standard errors must be checked.
- If the target requires model-implied probabilities inside the valid range, a nonlinear response, or likelihood-based modelling, logit or probit is more natural; the link scale must then be translated into predictions or effects.
- Logit and probit normalise the latent error scale differently, so their raw coefficient magnitudes cannot be compared directly. When their probability predictions are similar, choose by the estimand, tail support, specification, and validation performance rather than coefficient size.
- For probability prediction, compare a strictly proper probability loss, discrimination, and calibration on unseen data. Accuracy at one threshold and an in-sample pseudo-$R^2$ cannot decide alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无论选哪一种链接，加入更多控制变量都不会自动解决内生性，稳健标准误也不会修复错误的条件均值函数。函数形式、推断口径与因果识别是三项不同检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whichever link is chosen, adding more controls does not automatically solve endogeneity, and robust standard errors do not repair a wrong conditional-mean function. Functional form, inference, and causal identification are three separate checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.6. 同一个人被重复观察时：面板边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.6. Repeated observations on the same unit: the panel boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在线性固定效应模型中，组内变换可以精确消去加性单位截距；一般非线性模型没有这个便利。短 $T$ 下若给每个单位都直接估计一个截距，逐单位参数始终只靠少量观测估计，误差会传入共同斜率，形成[[非线性固定效应偏误|附带参数偏误]]；仅增加单位数 $N$ 通常不能消除它。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a linear fixed-effects model, the within transformation can eliminate an additive unit intercept exactly. A general nonlinear model does not share that convenience. With short $T$, directly estimating an intercept for every unit leaves each unit parameter supported by only a few observations, and its estimation error spills into the common slope. This is the [[非线性固定效应偏误|incidental-parameter bias]], which is generally not removed merely by increasing the number of units $N$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

静态 [[条件固定效应Logit|条件固定效应 Logit]] 是一个特殊出口：在给定完整协变量路径的联合条件似然及相应时序条件成立时，对每个单位的成功次数 $S_i=\sum_tY_{it}$ 条件化，可以消去单位截距。但共同斜率只由 $0<S_i<T_i$ 的单位识别，所以全 0 和全 1 的组退出；单位内不变的解释变量仍然无法估计，见[[条件Logit只用变化组]]。一般 Probit 没有同样的低维充分统计量，而且条件 Logit 没有估出单位截距，不能仅凭它恢复每个单位的绝对概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Static [[条件固定效应Logit|conditional fixed-effects logit]] provides a special exit. When the joint conditional likelihood given the complete covariate path and its timing assumptions are valid, conditioning on each unit's success count $S_i=\sum_tY_{it}$ removes the unit intercept. The common slope is then identified only by units with $0<S_i<T_i$, so all-zero and all-one groups leave the likelihood; a regressor that is constant within unit remains unestimable. See [[条件Logit只用变化组|the switching-group boundary]]. A general probit has no analogous low-dimensional sufficient statistic, and conditional logit does not estimate the unit intercepts, so its output alone cannot recover each unit's absolute probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
