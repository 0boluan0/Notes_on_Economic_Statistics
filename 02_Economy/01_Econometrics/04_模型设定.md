# 1. [[回归模型设定与函数形式.canvas|计量经济学模型的设定偏误]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Misspecification in econometric models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1. 模型设定偏误
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1. Model misspecification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模型设定偏误指模型的函数形式、变量选择或随机误差结构与真实数据生成过程不一致，导致估计结果或检验结论失真。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A model is misspecified when its functional form, variable selection, or stochastic-error structure does not match the true data-generating process, distorting estimates or test conclusions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 模型设定偏误的类型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2. Types of misspecification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见类型包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common forms include:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 遗漏重要变量。
- 加入无关变量。
- 函数形式设错。
- 参数不稳定。
- 变量存在测量误差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Omitting an important variable.
- Including an irrelevant variable.
- Using the wrong functional form.
- Allowing unstable parameters to go unmodelled.
- Measuring a variable with error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 类型 | 典型表现 | 主要后果 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 遗漏变量 | 少放了重要解释变量 | 若遗漏项既影响结果又与保留变量相关，焦点系数有偏、不一致 |
| 无关变量 | 多放了无用变量 | 消耗自由度；与既有列共享变异时相关系数方差增大 |
| 函数形式错误 | 线性/非线性设错 | 解释和预测失真 |
| 测量误差 | 变量观测不准 | 可能导致内生性 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Type | Typical manifestation | Main consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Omitted variable | An important regressor is missing | A biased and inconsistent focal coefficient when the omitted term affects the outcome and is related to the retained regressor |
| Irrelevant variable | A useless regressor is included | Lost degrees of freedom; higher variance for affected coefficients when it shares variation with existing columns |
| Wrong functional form | Linearity or nonlinearity is specified incorrectly | Distorted interpretation and prediction |
| Measurement error | A variable is observed inaccurately | May create endogeneity |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 为什么这章重要
> 模型设定错了，后面再精致的估计和检验都可能是在认真计算错误问题。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the model is misspecified, even sophisticated estimation and testing may amount to calculating the wrong answer to the wrong question with great care.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 模型设定偏误的后果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Consequences of model misspecification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1. 模型拟合不足
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Underfitting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.1. 原因
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.1. Causes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拟合不足主要来自遗漏变量或函数形式过于简单。例如真实模型为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Underfitting mainly results from omitted variables or an overly simple functional form. For example, suppose the true model is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1X_{1i}+\beta_2X_{2i}+u_i
$$

但估计时遗漏 $X_2$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
but $X_2$ is omitted in estimation:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_i=\alpha_0+\alpha_1X_{1i}+v_i
$$

此时
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
v_i=\beta_2X_{2i}+u_i
$$

若二阶矩存在、$\operatorname{Var}(X_1)>0$，则只用截距和 $X_1$ 的一元总体投影斜率 $\alpha_1^*$ 与 $\beta_1$ 相差：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With finite second moments and $\operatorname{Var}(X_1)>0$, the population slope $\alpha_1^*$ from projecting $Y$ only on an intercept and $X_1$ differs from $\beta_1$ by:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\alpha_1^*-\beta_1
=\beta_2\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2)}{\operatorname{Var}(X_1)}
$$

在随机抽样与大数定律所需条件下，样本斜率 $\hat\alpha_1$ 概率收敛到 $\alpha_1^*$。因此只有 $\beta_2\neq0$ 与 $\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2)\neq0$ 同时成立时，才有这项[[遗漏变量偏差|遗漏变量偏误]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under random sampling and the conditions needed for a law of large numbers, the sample slope $\hat\alpha_1$ converges in probability to $\alpha_1^*$. This omitted-variable-bias term is nonzero only when both $\beta_2\neq0$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2)\neq0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

偏误方向由 $\beta_2$ 和 $\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2)$ 的符号共同决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The direction of bias is determined jointly by the signs of $\beta_2$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] 遗漏变量偏误的方向
> 看两个符号：遗漏变量对 $Y$ 的影响方向，以及遗漏变量和保留解释变量的相关方向。两个同号，偏误向上；两个异号，偏误向下。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Check two signs: the omitted variable's effect on $Y$, and its correlation with the retained regressor. Matching signs produce upward bias; opposite signs produce downward bias.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2.1.2. 后果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1.2. Consequences*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 当上述偏差项非零时，焦点系数估计有偏且不一致。
- 参数检验和置信区间不可靠。
- 经济解释可能完全错误。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- When the displayed bias term is nonzero, the focal coefficient estimate is biased and inconsistent.
- Parameter tests and confidence intervals are unreliable.
- The economic interpretation may be entirely wrong.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2. 模型过度拟合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2. Overfitting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!map] 过度设定与预测过拟合不是同一个判断
> 本节的“过度拟合”首先指真实系数为零的无关变量被纳入回归，精确代价见 [[无关变量代价]]。预测意义的 overfitting 则由“训练表现改善、未见数据表现恶化”定义：先读 [[训练拟合单调性]]，再按 [[训练验证测试划分]] 与 [[验证管线隔离]] 评价完整流程。全局关系见 [[回归模型比较与选择.canvas|回归模型比较与选择总图]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here “overfitting” first means overspecification by a truly irrelevant regressor; [[无关变量代价|the cost of an irrelevant variable]] states its precise consequences. Predictive overfitting instead means improved training fit but worse performance on unseen data. Begin with [[训练拟合单调性|training-fit monotonicity]], then evaluate the complete procedure under the [[训练验证测试划分|training-validation-test distinction]] and [[验证管线隔离|pipeline isolation]]. See the [[回归模型比较与选择.canvas|model-comparison map]] for the global structure.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本节把加入无关变量作为过度设定的例子；预测意义的 overfitting 还必须用样本外表现判断。若新增变量真实系数为 0，且加入后外生性等原条件仍成立，OLS 仍然无偏并会损失自由度。在经典同方差、给定设计的比较下，新增列与既有列共享变异时会增大相关系数方差；若对焦点列条件正交，该焦点系数方差可以不变。与此同时，[[训练拟合单调性|训练 RSS 仍会机械下降或不变]]，所以样本内拟合改善不能区分有用信号与噪声适配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This section uses an irrelevant regressor as an example of overspecification; predictive overfitting additionally requires out-of-sample evidence. If the added regressor has a true coefficient of zero and the original exogeneity conditions remain valid after its inclusion, OLS remains unbiased and degrees of freedom are lost. Under the classical homoskedastic fixed-design comparison, sharing variation with existing columns raises the variance of affected coefficients; conditional orthogonality can leave a focal coefficient's variance unchanged. At the same time, [[训练拟合单调性|training RSS still falls mechanically or remains unchanged]], so better in-sample fit cannot separate useful signal from adaptation to noise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. 模型误设的检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Tests for misspecification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1. 过度拟合的检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1. Testing for unnecessary variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 怀疑的问题 | 常用检验 | 直觉 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 无关变量 | t 检验 / F 检验 | 新变量是否真的有解释力 |
| 遗漏变量 | LM 检验 | 残差是否还能被遗漏项解释 |
| 函数形式错误 | RESET 检验 | 拟合值高次项是否显著 |
| 非嵌套模型选择 | J 检验 | 另一个模型是否提供额外信息 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Suspected problem | Common test | Intuition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Irrelevant variables | $t$ or $F$ test | Do the new variables add explanatory power? |
| Omitted variables | LM test | Can omitted directions still explain the residuals? |
| Wrong functional form | RESET | Are powers of the fitted value significant? |
| Nonnested model choice | $J$ test | Does another model contain additional information? |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

过度拟合一般用 t 检验或 F 检验。若一组新增变量共同不显著，可以考虑剔除，但不能只看统计显著性，还要看理论含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Overfitting is commonly assessed with $t$ or $F$ tests. If a group of added variables is jointly insignificant, removal may be considered, but theoretical meaning must be considered alongside statistical significance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2. 拟合不足的检验：[[LM设定检验|LM 拉格朗日乘数检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2. Testing underfitting with an LM test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设原模型为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the original model is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_t=\beta_0+\beta_1Y_t+e_t
$$

怀疑遗漏了 $Y_t^2$ 和 $C_{t-1}$。先估计原模型，得到残差 $\hat e_t$，再做辅助回归：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $Y_t^2$ and $C_{t-1}$ may be omitted. Estimate the original model, obtain residuals $\hat e_t$, and run the auxiliary regression:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat e_t=\alpha_0+\alpha_1Y_t+\alpha_2Y_t^2+\alpha_3C_{t-1}+v_t
$$

原假设为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The null hypothesis is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_0:\alpha_2=\alpha_3=0
$$

LM 统计量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The LM statistic is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
LM=nR^2\sim \chi^2(q)
$$

其中 $q$ 是新增变量个数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $q$ is the number of added variables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] LM 检验的读法
> LM 检验不是直接估计完整新模型，而是看原模型残差还能不能被“疑似遗漏项”系统解释。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The LM test does not begin by estimating the full alternative model. It asks whether suspected omitted terms can still explain the original model's residuals systematically.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.2.1. 过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2.1. Procedure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 估计原模型，保存残差。
2. 用残差对原解释变量和可能遗漏的变量做辅助回归。
3. 计算 $nR^2$。
4. 若 $nR^2$ 足够大，拒绝“没有遗漏变量”的原假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Estimate the original model and save the residuals.<br>
**2.** Regress the residuals on the original regressors and the suspected omitted variables.<br>
**3.** Calculate $nR^2$.<br>
**4.** If $nR^2$ is sufficiently large, reject the null of no omitted variables.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.2.2. 评价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2.2. Assessment*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

LM 检验只需要估计原模型和辅助回归，适合检查是否存在遗漏的函数形式或变量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The LM test requires only the original model and an auxiliary regression, making it useful for detecting omitted variables or functional-form terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.3. [[RESET检验边界|拉姆齐 RESET 检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3. Ramsey RESET*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

RESET 检验用拟合值的高次项检查函数形式是否设错。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
RESET uses powers of the fitted value to test for functional-form misspecification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.3.1. 过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3.1. Procedure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 估计原模型，得到 $\hat Y$。
2. 构造辅助回归：

   $$
   Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1X_i+\gamma_2\hat Y_i^2+\gamma_3\hat Y_i^3+v_i
   $$

3. 检验：

   $$
   H_0:\gamma_2=\gamma_3=0
   $$

4. 若拒绝原假设，说明模型可能存在遗漏变量或函数形式错误。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Estimate the original model and obtain $\hat Y$.<br>
**2.** Construct the auxiliary regression shown above.<br>
**3.** Test the stated joint null on $\gamma_2$ and $\gamma_3$.<br>
**4.** Rejection suggests omitted variables or a wrong functional form.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.4. 非嵌套模型的检验：戴维森和麦金农的 J 检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.4. Davidson–MacKinnon $J$ test for nonnested models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个模型互不嵌套时，不能直接用普通 F 检验比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When neither model is nested in the other, an ordinary $F$ test cannot compare them directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.4.1. 过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.4.1. Procedure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设模型 A 和模型 B 分别为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let models A and B be:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y=X\beta+u,\qquad Y=Z\gamma+v
$$

1. 估计模型 B，得到 $\hat Y_B$。
2. 把 $\hat Y_B$ 加入模型 A：

   $$
   Y=X\beta+\lambda\hat Y_B+e
   $$

3. 检验 $H_0:\lambda=0$。
4. 若 $\lambda$ 显著，说明模型 B 含有模型 A 没有解释的信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Estimate model B and obtain $\hat Y_B$.<br>
**2.** Add $\hat Y_B$ to model A as shown above.<br>
**3.** Test $H_0:\lambda=0$.<br>
**4.** A significant $\lambda$ indicates that model B contains information not captured by model A.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 4. 样本数据导致的模型设定问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Specification problems caused by measurement in the sample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. [[测量误差位置|解释变量的误差]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1. Measurement error in an explanatory variable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果真实解释变量为 $Y_t$，但观测到的是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the true explanatory variable is $Y_t$, but the observed value is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_t^*=Y_t+v_t
$$

真实模型为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The true model is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_t=\beta_0+\beta_1Y_t+u_t
$$

用 $Y_t^*$ 回归时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regressing on $Y_t^*$ gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_t=\beta_0+\beta_1Y_t^*+(u_t-\beta_1v_t)
$$

新的误差项包含 $v_t$，而 $Y_t^*$ 本身也包含 $v_t$，所以解释变量与误差项相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The new error contains $v_t$, and $Y_t^*$ also contains $v_t$, so the observed regressor is correlated with the error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] 测量误差的关键区别
> 解释变量有误差，通常会污染系数；被解释变量有误差，若误差独立，主要是让标准误变大。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Error in an explanatory variable usually contaminates the coefficient. If independent, error in the outcome mainly increases standard errors.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.1.1. 后果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1.1. Consequences*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解释变量测量误差通常会造成系数有偏且不一致，经典情形下斜率会向 0 收缩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Measurement error in a regressor generally makes the coefficient biased and inconsistent; in the classical case, the slope is attenuated toward zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2. [[测量误差位置|被解释变量的误差]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2. Measurement error in the outcome*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果观测到的是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the observed outcome is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_t^*=C_t+v_t
$$

真实模型为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The true model is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_t=\beta_0+\beta_1Y_t+u_t
$$

则观测模型为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then the observed model is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_t^*=\beta_0+\beta_1Y_t+(u_t+v_t)
$$

### 4.2.1. 后果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.1. Consequences*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $v_t$ 与解释变量无关，系数估计仍然无偏、一致，但误差方差变大，标准误变大，检验能力下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $v_t$ is unrelated to the regressors, coefficient estimates remain unbiased and consistent, but the error variance and standard errors increase and statistical power falls.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 测量误差位置 | 是否通常污染系数 | 主要影响 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 解释变量 | 是 | 系数有偏、不一致 |
| 被解释变量 | 不一定 | 标准误变大、检验变弱 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Location of measurement error | Does it usually contaminate coefficients? | Main effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explanatory variable | Yes | Biased and inconsistent coefficients |
| Outcome variable | Not necessarily | Larger standard errors and weaker tests |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 5. 蒙特卡洛仿真
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Monte Carlo simulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

蒙特卡洛仿真可以用来观察模型设定错误的后果。例如：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Monte Carlo simulation can reveal the consequences of misspecification. For example:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 先设定真实模型。
2. 随机生成数据。
3. 分别估计正确模型、遗漏变量模型、过度拟合模型。
4. 重复很多次。
5. 比较估计量偏误、方差和检验拒绝率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Specify the true model.<br>
**2.** Generate data at random.<br>
**3.** Estimate the correct model, an omitted-variable model, and an overfitted model.<br>
**4.** Repeat the experiment many times.<br>
**5.** Compare estimator bias, variance, and test rejection rates.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
