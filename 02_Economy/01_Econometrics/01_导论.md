理论是基础,数据是依据，方法是工具 (Theory is the foundation, data is the basis, methods are the tools)

# 计量经济学研究示例：以边际消费倾向的测度为例 (Example of Econometric Research: Measuring [[Marginal Revolution|Marginal]] Propensity to Consume)

1. 理论或假说的陈述——研究的理论基础，目前为止没有出现定量的部分。(Statement of theory or hypothesis - the theoretical foundation of the research, no quantitative part has appeared so far.)
2. 理论的数学表述——数理模型，这个模型通常是非常理想化的的。所以跟实际上的事实有很大的出入，所以他是非常理想化的 (Mathematical formulation of theory - mathematical model, this model is usually very idealized. So there's a big gap with actual facts, therefore it's very idealized.)
3. 计量经济学模型的设定，相较于理论的梳理模型，这里加入了一个随机变量来表示实际情况中会发生的随机扰动（error term）。(Specification of econometric model - compared to the theoretical model, a random variable is added here to represent the random disturbance (error term) that occurs in reality.)
4. 获取样本（sample）样本的每一行叫做一个样本观测点，样本中的个体数量叫做样本容量（size）。拟合。(Obtain sample - each row of the sample is called a sample observation point, the number of individuals in the sample is called sample size. Fitting.)
5. 假设检验，注意此处我们通常倾向于拒绝原假设，原因在与第二类错误的出现不可控。(Hypothesis testing - note that here we usually tend to reject the null hypothesis, because the occurrence of Type II error is uncontrollable.)
6. 预测 (Forecasting)
7. 评价 (Evaluation)
