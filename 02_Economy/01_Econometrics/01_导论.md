# 1. 课程总论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Course Overview*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计量经济学的任务不是单纯“算一个回归”，而是把经济理论、数据和统计方法放在一起，用可检验的模型回答经验问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Econometrics is not simply the exercise of “running a regression.” It combines economic theory, data, and statistical methods to answer empirical questions with testable models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 本章抓手
> 计量经济学的主线是：先把经济问题翻译成模型，再用数据估计和检验，最后回到经济含义。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The central path in econometrics is to translate an economic question into a model, use data to estimate and test that model, and then return to the economic meaning of the result.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

核心链条是：

1. 从经济理论提出问题。
2. 把理论关系写成可估计的模型。
3. 用样本数据估计参数。
4. 检验参数、模型设定和数据假设。
5. 回到经济含义，解释结果是否支持原来的理论判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The core sequence is:

1. Formulate a question from economic theory.
2. Express the theoretical relationship as an estimable model.
3. Estimate its parameters with sample data.
4. Test the parameters, model specification, and data assumptions.
5. Return to the economic interpretation and assess whether the result supports the original theoretical claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 计量经济学实证研究三要素
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Three Elements of Empirical Econometric Research*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理论是基础，数据是依据，方法是工具。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Theory supplies the foundation, data supply the evidence, and methods supply the tools.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **理论**：告诉我们变量之间可能有什么关系，例如收入上升会提高消费。
- **数据**：提供可观测证据，例如居民收入、居民消费、宏观时间序列或面板数据。
- **方法**：把理论关系转化为可估计、可检验的模型，例如 [[OLS 线性回归|OLS]]、工具变量、时间序列模型或面板模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Theory** suggests why variables may be related; for example, higher income may raise consumption.
- **Data** provide observable evidence, such as household income and consumption, macroeconomic time series, or panel data.
- **Methods** turn theoretical relationships into estimable and testable models, such as [[OLS 线性回归|OLS]], instrumental variables, time-series models, or panel models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 要素 | 解决的问题 | 如果缺失会怎样 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 理论 | 为什么这些变量应当有关 | 回归变成机械找相关 |
| 数据 | 关系是否能被观察到 | 模型无法落地检验 |
| 方法 | 怎样估计和判断可靠性 | 结论没有统计依据 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Element | Question it addresses | Consequence if missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Theory | Why should these variables be related? | Regression becomes a mechanical search for correlation. |
| Data | Can the relationship be observed? | The model cannot be tested empirically. |
| Method | How can the relationship be estimated and its reliability assessed? | The conclusion has no statistical foundation. |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. 计量经济学研究示例：以[[OLS 线性回归#一元与多元系数解释|边际消费倾向]]的测度为例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. An Econometric Research Example: Measuring the Marginal Propensity to Consume*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[OLS 线性回归#一元与多元系数解释|边际消费倾向]]是“收入增加 1 单位时，消费平均增加多少”。计量经济学研究可以按下面顺序展开：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[OLS 线性回归#一元与多元系数解释|marginal propensity to consume]] is the average increase in consumption associated with a one-unit increase in income. An econometric study can proceed as follows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] 这一节怎么读
> 不要把这个例子看成单独知识点，它是在示范一篇实证研究从“问题”走到“结论”的完整流程。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Treat the example as a demonstration of the complete path from an empirical question to a conclusion, not as an isolated fact to memorise.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **提出问题**：居民收入变化是否会显著影响消费？边际消费倾向大约是多少？
2. **设定理论关系**：消费函数可以写成

   $$
   C_i=\beta_0+\beta_1Y_i+u_i
   $$

   其中，$C_i$ 是消费，$Y_i$ 是收入，$\beta_1$ 表示边际消费倾向。

3. **收集数据**：获得一组收入和消费数据，可以是截面数据、时间序列数据或面板数据。
4. **估计参数**：用 [[OLS 线性回归|OLS]] 或其他合适方法估计 $\beta_0$ 和 $\beta_1$。
5. **检验结果**：检查 $\beta_1$ 是否显著、符号是否符合理论、模型是否存在异方差、自相关或内生性等问题。
6. **解释经济含义**：如果 $\hat\beta_1=0.8$，可解释为收入每增加 1 单位，消费平均增加 0.8 单位。
7. **形成结论**：判断模型是否足以支持政策或理论判断；如果不够，需要重新检查变量、样本和模型设定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. **Formulate the question:** Does household income significantly affect consumption, and approximately how large is the marginal propensity to consume?
2. **Specify the theoretical relationship:** Write the consumption function as shown above, where $C_i$ is consumption, $Y_i$ is income, and $\beta_1$ is the marginal propensity to consume.
3. **Collect data:** Obtain income and consumption observations in cross-sectional, time-series, or panel form.
4. **Estimate the parameters:** Use [[OLS 线性回归|OLS]] or another appropriate method to estimate $\beta_0$ and $\beta_1$.
5. **Test the result:** Check whether $\beta_1$ is statistically significant, whether its sign accords with theory, and whether heteroskedasticity, autocorrelation, endogeneity, or another problem undermines the model.
6. **Interpret the economics:** If $\hat\beta_1=0.8$, a one-unit increase in income is associated with an average increase of 0.8 units in consumption.
7. **Reach a conclusion:** Decide whether the model adequately supports the policy or theoretical claim. If it does not, revisit the variables, sample, and specification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
