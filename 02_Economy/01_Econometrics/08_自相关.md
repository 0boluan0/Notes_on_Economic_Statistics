# 1. [[误差序列相关|误差自相关]]的含义和表现形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Meaning and forms of autocorrelation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1. 含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1. Meaning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自相关指同一个模型的误差项在不同观测期之间相关：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Autocorrelation means that the error terms from the same model are correlated across observation periods:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(u_t,u_{t-s})\ne0
$$

时间序列模型中最常见的是一阶自相关：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The most common case in time-series models is first-order autocorrelation:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
u_t=\rho u_{t-1}+\epsilon_t
$$

其中 $|\rho|<1$ 时误差项有均值回复，$\epsilon_t$ 是白噪声。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $|\rho|<1$, the error process is mean-reverting, and $\epsilon_t$ is white noise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 自相关抓手
> 异方差看“不同样本点误差方差是否一样”；自相关看“同一个误差序列前后是否互相牵连”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Heteroskedasticity asks whether error variance differs across observations; autocorrelation asks whether successive values in the same error sequence move together.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里讨论的是回归解释之后留下的误差相关，不是结果变量本身的持久性，也不是“原始序列 ACF 很高”的同义说法；完整对象边界见[[误差序列相关]]。全章沿着“均值是否漏动态 → 残差诊断 → 正式检验 → 只修推断还是建模协方差”的顺序阅读。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This chapter concerns dependence left in regression errors, not persistence in the outcome or a large raw-series ACF. See [[误差序列相关|serial correlation in regression errors]] for the exact object. Read the chapter as a path from mean-dynamics checks through residual diagnosis and formal testing to a choice between robust inference and covariance modelling.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2. 表现形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2. Forms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **正自相关**：$\rho>0$，上一期误差为正时，本期误差也倾向为正。
- **负自相关**：$\rho<0$，误差项倾向于正负交替。
- **高阶自相关**：误差项与更早期误差相关，例如 $u_t$ 与 $u_{t-2}$、$u_{t-3}$ 相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Positive autocorrelation:** when $\rho>0$, a positive error in the previous period tends to be followed by another positive error.
- **Negative autocorrelation:** when $\rho<0$, errors tend to alternate between positive and negative values.
- **Higher-order autocorrelation:** the error is correlated with errors from more distant periods, such as a relationship between $u_t$ and $u_{t-2}$ or $u_{t-3}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. 自相关的来源
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Sources of autocorrelation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1. 惯性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Persistence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经济变量常具有惯性，例如消费、投资、价格和利率不会在一期内完全调整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Economic variables are often persistent: consumption, investment, prices, and interest rates do not adjust fully within a single period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2. 模型函数形式设定不正确
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2. Functional-form misspecification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果真实关系是动态的，但模型只放入当期变量，遗漏的动态结构会进入误差项，引发自相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the true relationship is dynamic but the model includes only contemporaneous variables, the omitted dynamics enter the error term and generate autocorrelation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3. 数据处理引起的自相关
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3. Autocorrelation induced by data processing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

移动平均、插值、季节调整等数据处理方式可能使误差项跨期相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Data transformations such as moving averages, interpolation, and seasonal adjustment can induce correlation in errors across periods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.4. 某些模型中的随机误差项特性带来的自相关
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4. Persistence inherent in the disturbance process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金融和宏观时间序列中，冲击常常持续影响若干期，所以误差项自然可能相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In financial and macroeconomic time series, shocks often persist for several periods, so serial correlation in the disturbance process can arise naturally.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. 忽略自相关的后果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Consequences of ignoring autocorrelation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果解释变量严格外生，OLS 系数仍可无偏、一致，但常规标准误错误，导致 t 检验、F 检验和置信区间失真；常规标准误偏大还是偏小没有固定方向。若模型中含有滞后因变量且误差序列相关，滞后结果通常与当前误差相关，OLS 一般会有偏且不一致。完整逻辑见[[协方差失效下的OLS]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With strictly exogenous regressors, OLS coefficients can remain unbiased and consistent, but conventional standard errors are incorrect; the direction of their error is not universal. With a lagged dependent variable and serially correlated errors, the lagged outcome is generally correlated with the current error, making OLS biased and inconsistent. See [[协方差失效下的OLS|OLS under nonspherical errors]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*4. Tests for autocorrelation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1. [[残差协方差诊断|图示与相关图诊断]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1. Graphical diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 检验 | 可处理的问题 | 关键限制 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 图示法 | 初步观察残差模式 | 主观、不正式 |
| DW 检验 | 一阶自相关 | 含滞后因变量时，经典临界值校准失效 |
| BG 检验 | 高阶自相关、动态模型 | 需要设定滞后阶数 |
| Q 检验 | 多个滞后自相关整体检验 | 更偏残差序列诊断 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Test | Problem it can address | Main limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graphical diagnosis | Preliminary inspection of residual patterns | Subjective and informal |
| DW test | First-order autocorrelation | Its classical critical-value calibration fails with a lagged dependent variable |
| BG test | Higher-order autocorrelation and dynamic models | Requires a chosen lag order |
| Q test | Joint testing of autocorrelations across several lags | Primarily a residual-series diagnostic |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

画残差随时间变化的图。如果残差连续为正或连续为负，或者呈现周期性波动，可能存在相关；但同样的图案也可能来自遗漏趋势、季节或结构变化。再看残差 ACF/PACF 来确定值得正式检查的滞后，而不是把某个尖峰直接当作真实误差模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Plot residuals over time. Runs of the same sign or cycles may indicate dependence, but omitted trend, seasonality, or structural change can produce the same pattern. Use residual ACF/PACF to identify lags worth formal investigation rather than treating one spike as the true error model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2. [[Durbin-Watson检验|德宾–沃森 DW 检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2. Durbin–Watson (DW) test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.2.1. DW 检验前提条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.1. Conditions for using the DW test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

DW 统计量主要针对一阶自相关。用经典的 $d_L/d_U$ 临界值作推断时，解释变量不能包含滞后因变量；若模型含有滞后因变量，统计量仍然可以计算，但这套经典校准不再有效，不能据此直接下结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The DW statistic is aimed mainly at first-order autocorrelation. Inference from the classical $d_L/d_U$ bounds assumes that the regressors do not include a lagged dependent variable. If one is present, the statistic can still be calculated, but that classical calibration is invalid and cannot support a direct conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!attention] DW 的边界
> 模型里有滞后因变量时，不要套用 DW 的经典临界值；通常改用 BG 检验，只有在 Durbin $h$ 检验自身条件满足时才考虑它。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With a lagged dependent variable, do not apply the classical DW critical values. Usually use the BG test; consider Durbin's $h$ test only when its own assumptions are satisfied.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 4.2.2. DW 基本原理与步骤
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2.2. Logic and procedure of the DW test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

DW 统计量为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The DW statistic is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
d=\frac{\sum_{t=2}^T(\hat u_t-\hat u_{t-1})^2}
{\sum_{t=1}^T\hat u_t^2}
$$

近似关系：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It has the approximate relationship:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
d\approx2(1-\hat\rho)
$$

因此：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $d\approx2$：无一阶自相关。
- $d<2$：可能存在正自相关。
- $d>2$：可能存在负自相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $d\approx2$: no first-order autocorrelation.
- $d<2$: possible positive autocorrelation.
- $d>2$: possible negative autocorrelation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这只是数值直觉。经典检验要根据解释变量、样本量和显著性水平查 $d_L,d_U$，并保留“无法判定”区间；$d\approx2$ 也不是无自相关的证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is numerical intuition only. The classical test uses regressor- and sample-dependent lower and upper bounds and includes an inconclusive region; $d\approx2$ is not proof of no serial correlation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验假设为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The hypotheses are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_0:\rho=0,\qquad H_1:\rho\ne0
$$

## 4.3. [[Breusch-Godfrey检验|布罗施–戈弗雷 BG 检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3. Breusch–Godfrey (BG) test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

BG 检验可以用于高阶自相关，也可以用于含滞后因变量的模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The BG test can detect higher-order autocorrelation and can also be used in models containing a lagged dependent variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!item] BG 检验的用途
> BG 检验比 DW 更通用：可以检验多阶自相关，也能处理动态回归模型。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The BG test is more general than the DW test: it handles autocorrelation at multiple lags as well as dynamic regression models.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

步骤：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Procedure:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 估计原模型，得到残差 $\hat u_t$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Estimate the original model and obtain residuals $\hat u_t$.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
2. 做辅助回归：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** Run the following auxiliary regression:<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

   $$
   \hat u_t=\alpha_0+\alpha_1X_{1t}+\cdots+\alpha_kX_{kt}
   +\rho_1\hat u_{t-1}+\cdots+\rho_p\hat u_{t-p}+v_t
   $$

3. 检验：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** Test the joint null hypothesis:<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

   $$
   H_0:\rho_1=\cdots=\rho_p=0
   $$

4. 使用 $LM=nR^2\sim\chi^2(p)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**4.** Use $LM=nR^2\sim\chi^2(p)$.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 $n$ 是**实际进入辅助回归的观测数**，并不预先固定为 $T-p$。前 $p$ 个残差滞后没有自然观测值时，可以从 $t=p+1$ 开始、删去前 $p$ 行；若没有其他缺失，此时 $n=T-p$。也可以先在残差前补 $p$ 个零再构造滞后，从而保留原回归的全部 $T$ 个观测；statsmodels 的 `acorr_breusch_godfrey` 采用这一口径。两种实现的有限样本结果不必相同，因此 $nR^2$ 中的 $n$ 和 F 版本的分母自由度都必须跟随实际辅助样本及设计矩阵的实际秩，不能要求所有软件一律减去 $p$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $n$ is the number of observations actually used in the auxiliary regression, not a quantity fixed in advance at $T-p$. One implementation drops the first $p$ rows and starts at $t=p+1$, giving $n=T-p$ if there are no other missing observations. Another prepends $p$ zeros to the residuals before forming the lag matrix and retains all $T$ observations from the original regression; statsmodels' `acorr_breusch_godfrey` uses this convention. Because the finite-sample results need not coincide, both the $n$ in $nR^2$ and the denominator degrees of freedom in the F version must follow the auxiliary sample and actual design rank used by the implementation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.4. [[Ljung-Box检验|Ljung–Box Q 检验]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.4. Q test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Q 检验使用残差自相关系数整体判断选定滞后窗口内是否仍有序列相关。常见 Ljung–Box 统计量为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Q test uses residual autocorrelations to test jointly for serial correlation. The commonly used Ljung–Box statistic is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Q=T(T+2)\sum_{k=1}^m\frac{\hat\rho_k^2}{T-k}.
$$

直接检验未拟合序列时常用 $\chi^2(m)$ 近似；检验拟合模型残差时必须按模型与软件口径扣除相应拟合自由度。若拒绝原假设，说明至少某些受检滞后仍有线性相关；未拒绝不等于模型正确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an unfitted series, a $\chi^2(m)$ reference is common. Fitted-model residuals require a model-specific degrees-of-freedom adjustment. Rejection indicates remaining linear correlation at one or more tested lags; non-rejection does not validate the whole model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.1. [[广义最小二乘|广义最小二乘估计 GLS]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.1. Generalized least squares (GLS)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 方法 | 改系数吗 | 主要用途 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GLS | 改 | 已知自相关结构 |
| FGLS / CO | 改 | 估计自相关结构后重新估计 |
| Newey-West | 不改 | 修正标准误和检验 |
| ARCH/GARCH | 另一个方差动态模型 | 建模平方冲击与波动率聚集，不是修复普通误差水平自相关 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Method | Changes coefficient estimates? | Main purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GLS | Yes | Known autocorrelation structure |
| FGLS / CO | Yes | Re-estimate after estimating the autocorrelation structure |
| Newey–West | No | Correct standard errors and inference |
| ARCH/GARCH | A separate variance-dynamics model | Model squared shocks and volatility clustering, not ordinary level-error serial correlation |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 5.1.1. GLS 原理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.1.1. Logic of GLS*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若误差项满足：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the error term satisfies:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
u_t=\rho u_{t-1}+\epsilon_t
$$

>[!item] 补救时先问一个问题
> 如果目标是提高估计效率，可以做 GLS/FGLS；如果只想让检验可靠，常用 Newey-West 修正标准误。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the goal is greater estimation efficiency, use GLS or FGLS. If the goal is only reliable inference, Newey–West standard errors are often appropriate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原模型为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original model is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_t=\alpha+\beta X_t+u_t
$$

滞后一期：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lagging it by one period gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_{t-1}=\alpha+\beta X_{t-1}+u_{t-1}
$$

用第一式减去 $\rho$ 倍第二式：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtract $\rho$ times the second equation from the first:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_t-\rho Y_{t-1}
=\alpha(1-\rho)+\beta(X_t-\rho X_{t-1})+\epsilon_t
$$

若 AR(1) 误差设定正确且 $\rho$ 已知，变换后误差项为白噪声，可以用 OLS 估计变换后的模型。对 $t\ge2$ 的式子直接回归会丢掉第一期；完整 GLS/Prais–Winsten 还要按平稳 AR(1) 协方差变换并保留首期，详见[[AR1准差分]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the AR(1) error model is correct and $\rho$ is known, the transformed disturbance is white noise. Regressing only the equations for $t\ge2$ drops the first period; exact GLS/Prais–Winsten additionally transforms and retains the first observation, as explained in [[AR1准差分|AR(1) quasi-differencing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 5.1.2. [[可行GLS|两步可行 GLS]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.1.2. Durbin two-step method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 先用 OLS 估计原模型，得到残差 $\hat u_t$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Estimate the original model by OLS and obtain residuals $\hat u_t$.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
2. 用残差估计自相关系数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** Use the residuals to estimate the autocorrelation coefficient:<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

   $$
   \hat\rho=
   \frac{\sum_{t=2}^T\hat u_t\hat u_{t-1}}
   {\sum_{t=2}^T\hat u_{t-1}^2}
   $$

3. 对数据做准差分：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** Quasi-difference the data:<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

   $$
   Y_t^*=Y_t-\hat\rho Y_{t-1},\qquad
   X_t^*=X_t-\hat\rho X_{t-1}
   $$

4. 估计：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**4.** Estimate the transformed equation:<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

   $$
   Y_t^*=\alpha^*+\beta X_t^*+\epsilon_t
   $$

其中 $\alpha^*=\alpha(1-\hat\rho)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\alpha^*=\alpha(1-\hat\rho)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.2. [[AR1准差分|科克伦–奥克特 CO 迭代估计]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.2. Cochrane–Orcutt (CO) iterative estimation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CO 方法是 Durbin 两步法的迭代版本：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The CO method is an iterative version of Durbin's two-step procedure:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. OLS 估计原模型，得到残差。
2. 估计 $\hat\rho$。
3. 用 $\hat\rho$ 做准差分并重新估计。
4. 用更新后的系数回到原尺度方程构造 $\hat u_t$，再估计 $\hat\rho$；不要把变换回归的创新残差直接当成原误差。
5. 重复直到参数变化很小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Estimate the original model by OLS and obtain residuals.<br>
**2.** Estimate $\hat\rho$.<br>
**3.** Quasi-difference the data using $\hat\rho$ and re-estimate the model.<br>
**4.** Use the updated coefficients to reconstruct residuals in the original equation, then re-estimate $\hat\rho$; do not mistake transformed innovation residuals for the original errors.<br>
**5.** Repeat until the parameter changes are negligible.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CO 丢掉首期；Prais–Winsten 在平稳 AR(1) 设定下保留首期。迭代收敛只说明算法稳定，不证明 AR(1) 协方差模型正确，估计后仍要检查变换残差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cochrane–Orcutt drops the first period, whereas Prais–Winsten retains it under stationary AR(1) errors. Convergence establishes only algorithmic stability, not the truth of the AR(1) covariance model; diagnose the transformed residuals afterwards.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5.3. [[HAC协方差|Newey–West/HAC]] 修正推断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.3. Newey–West standard-error correction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Newey-West 方法不改变 OLS 系数，只修正标准误，使其同时对异方差和自相关稳健。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Newey–West method leaves the OLS coefficients unchanged and adjusts only the standard errors, making inference robust to both heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $g_t=x_t\hat u_t$，采用未除以样本量的交叉乘积和：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $g_t=x_t\hat u_t$ and use unnormalised cross-product sums:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\widehat G_0=\sum_{t=1}^{T}g_tg_t',
\qquad
\widehat G_\ell=\sum_{t=\ell+1}^{T}g_tg_{t-\ell}',
$$
$$
\widehat S_L=\widehat G_0+
\sum_{\ell=1}^{L}w_\ell
(\widehat G_\ell+\widehat G_\ell'),
\qquad
\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}_{HAC}(\hat\beta)
=(X'X)^{-1}\widehat S_L(X'X)^{-1}.
$$

对 Bartlett 核，$w_\ell=1-\ell/(L+1)$。这个口径可以当场验算：$L=0$ 且不加小样本修正时，$\widehat S_0=X'\operatorname{diag}(\hat u_t^2)X$，所以公式恰好退化为 HC0。若另一本教材把 $\widehat\Gamma_\ell=T^{-1}\widehat G_\ell$ 称为样本自协方差，三明治必须相应乘回 $T$，不能混用两个归一化口径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the Bartlett kernel, $w_\ell=1-\ell/(L+1)$. The normalisation is directly checkable: with $L=0$ and no small-sample correction, $\widehat S_0=X'\operatorname{diag}(\hat u_t^2)X$, so the estimator reduces exactly to HC0. If a source instead defines averaged sample autocovariances $\widehat\Gamma_\ell=T^{-1}\widehat G_\ell$, its sandwich must restore the matching factor of $T$; do not mix the two conventions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

带宽 $L$ 应结合数据频率、依赖时间尺度和预先说明的自动规则选择；$L$ 过小会遗漏相关性，过大则会引入较远滞后的噪声。用于选择 ARMA 均值阶数的信息准则不是无条件通用的 HAC 带宽规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose bandwidth $L$ from data frequency, the dependence horizon, and a stated automatic rule. Too small a bandwidth omits dependence; too large a bandwidth adds noisy distant-lag estimates. Information criteria for ARMA mean order are not a universal HAC-bandwidth rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*5.4. ARCH model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 5.4.1. ARCH 模型的原理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.4.1. Logic of the ARCH model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ARCH 模型用于刻画条件异方差：误差项本身可能不相关，但误差平方会成团出现，表现为波动率聚集。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ARCH models describe conditional heteroskedasticity: the errors themselves may be uncorrelated, while large and small squared errors cluster over time, producing volatility clustering.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] ARCH 和自相关的关系
> ARCH 不是普通误差项自相关，而是“误差平方”有动态结构，所以更像波动率的自相关。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> ARCH is not ordinary autocorrelation in the errors. It is dynamic dependence in squared errors, which is better understood as persistence in volatility.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此本节是与误差协方差主题相邻的出口，不是对普通误差水平自相关的第四种通用补救。完整学习路径已在[[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH.canvas]]维护；下面只保留本课程原有的最小模型和检验桥梁。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This section is therefore an adjacent exit rather than a fourth generic remedy for level-error serial correlation. The complete learning path lives in [[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH.canvas|the ARCH/GARCH Canvas]]; the material below retains only the course's minimal model and testing bridge.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 5.4.3. 实现步骤
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5.4.3. Implementation steps*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 用 OLS 估计均值方程，得到残差 $\hat\epsilon_t$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Estimate the mean equation by OLS and obtain residuals $\hat\epsilon_t$.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
2. 做辅助回归：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** Run the following auxiliary regression:<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

   $$
   \hat\epsilon_t^2=\alpha_0+\alpha_1\hat\epsilon_{t-1}^2+\cdots+\alpha_q\hat\epsilon_{t-q}^2+v_t
   $$

3. 用 F 检验或 LM 检验判断滞后平方残差是否显著。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** Use an $F$ test or LM test to determine whether the lagged squared residuals are jointly significant.<br>

**4.** If they are significant, an ARCH effect is present and an ARCH- or GARCH-family model may be appropriate.<br>
