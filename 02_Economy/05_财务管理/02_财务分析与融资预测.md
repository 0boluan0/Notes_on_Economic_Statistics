# 财务分析与融资预测：从报表读到增长需要的资金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From financial statements to the funding required for growth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一章要把三个问题接起来：企业现在的利润和现金从哪里来；哪些比率能解释经营与融资；如果明年继续增长，还要补多少资金。读完不是只得到一张指标表，而是能沿一套报表解释数字，再把经营假设推到预计利润、资产和融资缺口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This chapter connects three questions: where current profit and cash come from, which ratios explain operations and financing, and how much funding future growth requires. The aim is to interpret a connected set of statements and translate operating assumptions into forecast profit, assets, and funding needs.

## 一、先确定分析要回答什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Begin with the decision the analysis must support.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给贷款人看，到期偿付和可动用资源很重要；给股东看，经营回报、资本需要及承担的风险很重要；给内部管理者看，还要找出可改变的经营环节。相同的报表可以服务不同问题，所以先说明分析目的，再选择指标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lenders focus on payments due and available resources; shareholders examine returns, capital requirements, and risk; managers also need actionable operating explanations. The same statements serve different questions, so establish the purpose before selecting ratios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财务报表分析]]

原课的“战略—会计—财务—前景”是一条检查顺序：从[[商业模式|企业怎样赚钱]]理解行业和业务，检查会计信息是否忠实表达这些活动，再分析报表，最后形成有条件的预测。不能跳过会计信息的可靠性，就把一个精确到两位小数的比率当成可靠结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course's strategy–accounting–financial–prospective framework is a sequence of checks. Understand [[商业模式|how the business earns money]], assess how accounting represents it, analyse the statements, and form conditional forecasts. Decimal precision does not compensate for unreliable inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财务报表局限]]

先把分析对象说清，再看数据。估计、重述、合并范围和一次性交易会影响可比性；它们可能合理，也可能需要进一步核查。具体盈利真实性和持续性问题由[[盈利质量核查]]承接，异常本身不是舞弊结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define the object before interpreting the data. Estimates, restatements, consolidation changes, and one-off transactions affect comparability. They may be legitimate or require investigation; use [[盈利质量核查|earnings-quality checks]] rather than treating anomalies as proof of fraud.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 二、把报表接成同一家公司
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Connect the statements into one account of the business.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

日常说“三表”，是在突出资产负债表、利润表和现金流量表的联系；完整财务报表还包括权益变动和附注等。先分清哪些数记录某一天的余额，哪些数覆盖一段期间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Three statements” highlights the balance sheet, income statement, and cash-flow statement. A complete set also includes equity changes and notes. Distinguish balances at a date from activity over a period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财务报表]]
![[资产负债表]]
![[会计恒等式]]

资产不是一袋随时可分配的现金，而是按相应会计规则确认和计量的资源。利润也不等于收款；赊销、折旧、应计费用会让利润确认和现金时点不同。接着沿利润、权益和现金把同一期间走完。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assets are recognised and measured resources, not a bag of distributable cash. Profit also differs from receipts: credit sales, depreciation, and accruals separate recognition from payment. Trace the same period through profit, equity, and cash.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[现金流量表]]

|---|---:|---|

Assets of 2,000 equal liabilities of 1,040 plus equity of 960. Current assets of 700 less current liabilities of 300 give working capital of 400. Revenue is 3,000 and net income 136. Operating, investing, and financing cash flows sum to 19, moving cash from 25 to 44 in this example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本例的三类现金流已能说明现金差额；一般报表仍要核对汇率影响等调节项。随后计算周转和收益率时，教材为简便通常采用**期末存量**；做真实期间分析通常优先考虑合适的平均存量，但不能悄悄改掉例题分母。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three cash-flow categories explain this example's cash change; general statements may require exchange effects and other reconciling items. Later textbook examples generally use ending balances for simplicity. Appropriate average balances may better represent a real period, but changing the denominator silently changes the exercise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三、先选比较基准，再分解差异
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Choose a comparison basis before attributing differences.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个比率只有放到问题和基准里才有解释力。同业比较要求业务和会计口径可比；历史比较要留意重述和业务变化；预算比较还要检查预算本身是否合理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A ratio becomes informative in relation to a question and benchmark. Peer comparisons require comparable activities and accounting; historical comparisons require checks for restatements and business changes; budget comparisons must also assess whether the budget was reasonable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财务报表比较分析]]
![[结构百分比报表]]
![[财务比率]]
![[财务比率口径匹配]]

找到差异后，可以沿一个明确的函数分解。连环替代例把材料成本写成“产量 × 单耗 × 单价”：基准 $120\times9\times5=5400$，实际 $140\times8\times6=6720$。按产量、单耗、单价依次替代，中间值为 5,400 → 6,300 → 5,600 → 6,720；三项贡献分别为 +900、−700、+1,120，合计 +1,320。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After identifying a difference, decompose it through an explicit function. The materials-cost example is output × input per unit × price: 5,400 initially and 6,720 actually. Substituting output, input intensity, and price in that order gives 5,400 → 6,300 → 5,600 → 6,720, with contributions of +900, −700, and +1,120, totalling +1,320.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[连环替代法]]
![[连环替代顺序边界]]

这些贡献是给定替代路径的算术分摊。改变顺序会重新分配交互项，因此不能仅据此说某个部门“造成了”确切金额的变化。后面的杜邦分解也遵守这条边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are arithmetic allocations along a specified substitution path. Changing the order reallocates interactions, so the amounts do not by themselves identify a department's causal contribution. The same boundary applies to DuPont analysis below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 四、偿债：先看资金缓冲，再看支付承诺
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Solvency: connect financial buffers to payment commitments.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

短期偿债从流动资产和流动负债开始，但金额差、比率和实际现金分别回答不同问题。先定义营运资本，再逐步收紧可动用资产范围，最后检查历史现金流，以及附注和合同中的偿付条件与承诺。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Short-term analysis starts with current assets and liabilities, but their difference, their ratio, and actual cash answer different questions. Define working capital, narrow the liquid-asset set, and then examine cash flow and payment conditions and commitments disclosed in notes and contracts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[营运资本]]
![[营运资本融资恒等式]]
![[流动比率]]
![[速动比率]]
![[现金比率]]
![[现金流量比率]]

ABC 本年流动比率 $700/300\approx2.33$；教材速动资产为货币资金 44、应收票据 20、应收账款 398、其他应收款 12，合计 474，速动比率为 1.58；现金比率按货币资金算为 $44/300\approx0.147$。不能只从速动资产减去“应收账款 398”就说剩下全是现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ABC's current ratio is about 2.33. The textbook's quick assets comprise cash 44, notes receivable 20, accounts receivable 398, and other receivables 12, totalling 474; the quick ratio is 1.58. The cash ratio is about 0.147. Subtracting only accounts receivable from quick assets does not leave cash alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经营现金流对流动负债为 $246/300=0.82$。这与现金比率不矛盾：246 是期间净流量，44 是期末余额，资金还用于投资、还债或其他支付。比例不能把过去产生的现金重复当成未来可用现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operating cash flow divided by current liabilities is $246/300=0.82$. This does not contradict the cash ratio: 246 is a period flow and 44 an ending balance, after investment and other payments. A ratio cannot turn past cash generation into cash still available in the future.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长期偿债还要分别看整体融资比例、长期资本构成，以及利润和现金对利息的覆盖。总负债包括经营性应付，并不等于全部有息借款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Longer-term analysis separates overall leverage, long-term capital composition, and interest coverage from profit or cash. Total liabilities include operating payables and are not identical to interest-bearing borrowing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[长期资本负债率]]

同一 ABC 数据给出资产负债率 52%、产权比率约 1.083、权益乘数约 2.083。息税前利润 310、利息支出 110，盈利覆盖约 2.82 倍；经营现金流覆盖则约 2.24 倍。两个覆盖数接近也不保证每笔本金到期都能付款，还需看期限、受限资产及提款条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ABC has liabilities/assets of 52%, liabilities/equity of about 1.083, and an equity multiplier of about 2.083. EBIT of 310 covers interest of 110 about 2.82 times; operating cash flow covers it about 2.24 times. Neither guarantees principal payment on every due date; maturity, restrictions, and drawdown conditions still matter.

## 五、经营与盈利：销售怎样使用资产并留下利润
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Operations and profitability: how sales use assets and leave profit.*

### 从应收与存货走到全部资产
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From receivables and inventory to the full asset base.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

周转指标把期间业务流量与占用的存量联系起来。先看应收和存货各自对应什么流量，再扩到整类资产。存货用销售收入还是销售成本，具有不同用途；名称相同不代表分子可以互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Turnover relates period activity to resources tied up. Match receivables and inventory to their respective flows before moving to asset classes. Inventory turnover based on sales versus cost serves different purposes; identical labels do not make the numerators interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[应收账款周转率]]
![[存货周转率]]
![[存货周转的收入与成本口径]]
![[流动资产周转率]]
![[营运资本周转率]]
![[非流动资产周转率]]
![[总资产周转率]]
![[资产周转分解]]

ABC 的应收口径包括应收票据，因此本例分母为 $398+20=418$，按总销售代理得到周转 $3000/418\approx7.18$ 次。采用 365 天，折算为约 50.86 天。它不是单凭应收账款 398 得到的数，也不是已经逐张发票测出的真实平均付款天数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ABC's receivables include notes, giving a denominator of 418. Total sales as a proxy produce turnover of about 7.18 and, using 365 days, about 50.86 equivalent days. This differs from using accounts receivable alone and is not a direct invoice-level estimate of actual collection time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全部资产收入比可以按组成拆开：$2000/3000=700/3000+1300/3000$，因此相同 365 天口径下，243.33 个资产折算天数等于 85.17 加 158.17。但 $3000/2000$ 的周转次数不能由两个分项次数相加，243.33 天也不意味着全公司资产每隔这些天就被卖掉换成现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Asset-to-sales ratios add across components, so 243.33 equivalent asset days equal 85.17 current-asset days plus 158.17 noncurrent-asset days. Turnover frequencies do not add, and these equivalent days do not imply that the company sells and converts all assets into cash at that interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 利润率、资产回报与股东回报
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Margin, asset returns, and shareholder returns.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净利率问每元销售留下多少净利润；资产净利率问每元资产对应多少净利润；权益净利率问相应股东的账面权益对应多少净利润。只有主体和时点一致，才能把它们接成恒等式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net margin measures profit per unit of sales, asset profitability profit per unit of assets, and ROE profit relative to matching shareholders' book equity. Only consistent scopes and timing allow an exact identity linking them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[营业净利率]]
![[总资产净利率]]
![[权益净利率]]
![[杜邦分解]]

ABC 使用期末分母：$136/3000\approx4.53\%$，$3000/2000=1.5$，$2000/960\approx2.083$。三项保持未舍入值相乘，恰为 $136/960\approx14.17\%$。息税前利润版本的资产盈利率是另一口径，不能在这里把 136 换成 310 后仍要求得到同一个 ROE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using ABC's ending balances, net margin is about 4.53%, turnover 1.5, and the equity multiplier about 2.083. Multiplying unrounded values yields ROE of about 14.17%. EBIT-based asset profitability uses a different numerator; replacing 136 with 310 would not preserve this ROE identity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[ROE与杠杆的解释边界]]

教材从上年 ROE 18.18% 到本年 14.17%，按净利率、资产周转、权益乘数的顺序替代，得到中间值 18.18% → 14.68% → 12.98% → 14.17%。约 −3.50、−1.70、+1.18 **个百分点**相加为约 −4.02 个百分点：杠杆上升在这个分摊顺序下抵消了一部分经营指标下降，并未证明风险下降或创造价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replacing margin, turnover, and the equity multiplier in order moves textbook ROE from about 18.18% through 14.68% and 12.98% to 14.17%. Contributions of approximately −3.50, −1.70, and +1.18 percentage points sum to −4.02 points. The leverage contribution offsets part of the decline under this allocation; it does not establish lower risk or value creation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

各步显示值已经四舍五入，但贡献按原始比率计算：最后一项为 $14.166667\%-12.981818\%=1.184848$ 个百分点，总差为 $14.166667\%-18.181818\%=-4.015152$ 个百分点。因此不能再把表面显示的 14.17 与 12.98 相减来核对 1.18；教材提前舍入得到的 1.19 与 −4.01 属于另一舍入顺序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Displayed steps are rounded, but contributions use the original ratios: the final contribution is 1.184848 percentage points and the total change is −4.015152 points. Subtracting the displayed 14.17 and 12.98 therefore does not reproduce the rounded contribution of 1.18. The textbook's 1.19 and −4.01 follow earlier rounding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 从企业利润走到每股与市场价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From company profit to per-share measures and market price.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把利润或账面权益写成每股数，首先要固定普通股股东范围和股数时点。每股收益是期间流量分摊，每股净资产是时点余额分摊；有优先股、少数股东或股数变化时尤其不能省略这个区别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Per-share profit and equity require matching ordinary-shareholder scope and share-count timing. EPS allocates a period flow; book value per share allocates a point-in-time balance. Preference shares, noncontrolling interests, and share-count changes make this distinction especially important.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[稀释每股收益]]
![[每股净资产]]
![[市盈率]]
![[市净率]]
![[市销率]]

这些比率连接会计与价格，但不能靠低倍数直接得出便宜的结论。价格可能反映未来利润下降、资本需要或风险；完整价值模型在后续证券估值部分继续，本章先保证分子分母说的是同一个对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These multiples connect accounting to price, but a low multiple alone does not establish cheapness. Price may reflect future earnings decline, capital needs, or risk. Valuation models follow later; here the priority is consistent numerators and denominators.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 六、把经营回报与融资效果分开
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Separate operating performance from financing effects.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

传统杜邦使用净利润，已经混合经营成果、利息与税。要进一步解释“业务本身赚得怎样”和“融资如何改变股东回报”，可以按经营与金融功能重组报表。管理用分类服务分析目的，不等同于照搬会计科目里的“金融资产”名称。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Traditional DuPont starts from net income, which already combines operations, interest, and tax. Reorganising statements by operating and financing function helps separate business returns from financing effects. Management-use classification serves analysis rather than mechanically copying accounting category names.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[管理用报表分类]]
![[净经营资产]]
![[经营营运资本]]
![[净金融负债]]
![[管理用资产负债表恒等式]]

ABC 本年经营流动资产 700、经营流动负债 228，经营营运资本为 472；经营长期资产净额为 $1300-50=1250$，因此净经营资产为 1,722。另一边净金融负债 762 加权益 960，也为 1,722。普通营运资本 400 和经营营运资本 472 使用不同负债集合，并不互相矛盾。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ABC's operating working capital is $700-228=472$, and net long-term operating assets are $1300-50=1250$, giving net operating assets of 1,722. Net financial debt of 762 plus equity of 960 gives the same total. Ordinary working capital of 400 differs because it uses a different liability set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着拆利润。金融资产收益会抵减净融资成本，因此教材的净融资费用并非直接使用财务费用 110；本例税前净融资费用是 $110-6=104$。按教材统一有效税率 32% 分配经营与融资税负，才能得到下列对应关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next split profit. Financial-asset income offsets net financing cost, so the textbook uses $110-6=104$, not finance expense of 110 alone. Its simplified 32% effective tax allocation connects operating and financing profit as follows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$NOPAT=304(1-0.32)=206.72,\qquad NFC=104(1-0.32)=70.72,$$
$$NI=206.72-70.72=136.$$

实际税额不同、利息抵扣受限或存在递延税时，需要重做分配，不能把 32% 当成适用于其他企业的法定税率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Different actual tax effects, deduction limits, or deferred taxes require revised allocations. The example's 32% is not a statutory rate to apply to other companies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

管理用表中，上年净经营资产为 1,446，本年为 1,722，增加 276。按本例的匹配再投资假设，企业自由现金流为 $206.72-276=-69.28$。另假定折旧 45、经营营运资本增加 21、资本支出 300，同样得到 $206.72+45-21-300=-69.28$。这不是将传统现金流量表的 CFO 246 改写成 −69.28；经营后再投资现金流与传统报表分类是不同对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net operating assets increase from 1,446 to 1,722, or 276. Under the example's matched reinvestment assumptions, FCFF is $206.72-276=-69.28$. With assumed depreciation of 45, operating working-capital investment of 21, and capex of 300, the expanded calculation gives the same result. This does not redefine reported CFO of 246 as −69.28: post-reinvestment operating cash flow and conventional statement classifications differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还有一步需要和估值课接齐：上述 CPA 经营营运资本**含必要经营现金**；共享 FCFF 卡的简化公式采用**非现金**营运资本。保持税后经营利润、折旧和资本支出等假设不变，扣现金后的期末与期初经营营运资本分别为 $472-44=428$、$451-25=426$，仅增加 2，因此非现金版本算得 $206.72+45-2-300=-50.28$。再留出新增必要经营现金 $44-25=19$，才得到 CPA 的 −69.28。这是两种现金保留约定之间的数值桥，不是把两个结果直接当作同口径答案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One further bridge connects this example to valuation: CPA's operating working capital includes required operating cash, whereas the shared FCFF atom's simple formula uses non-cash working capital. Holding NOPAT, depreciation, capex, and the other assumptions fixed, non-cash working capital rises from 426 to 428, an increase of 2. The non-cash calculation gives −50.28. Reserving the additional required cash of 19 then produces CPA's −69.28. This reconciles two cash-retention conventions rather than treating their results as identical measures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 例 2-2：把销售扩张算到融资缺口

### 增长上限是由哪些条件限定的
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Identify the conditions behind a financing-based growth limit.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接下来换成例 2-3 的参数：经营负债为 **185**，不再是上例的 278；净经营资产收入比因此为 $(2000-185)/3000=0.605$。净利率 4.5%、留存率 70%，允许自然经营负债随销售增加，不新增融资性债务或股权时，内含增长率约为 5.49%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Example 2-3 changes operating liabilities to 185, not the previous 278. Net operating assets are therefore 0.605 of sales. With a 4.5% margin, 70% retention, and spontaneous operating-liability growth but no new financing debt/equity, internal growth is about 5.49%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[内含增长率]]

若允许融资性负债随权益同比增加，但不增发或回购股权，问题变为固定财务政策下的可持续增长。这里最常见的错误不是乘法，而是把期初、期末、平均权益的 ROE 混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Allowing financing liabilities to expand proportionately with equity, while excluding equity issues and repurchases, gives sustainable growth under fixed financial policies. A common error is not multiplication but mixing beginning-, ending-, and average-equity ROE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[增长率与权益时点]]
![[实际增长与可持续增长]]

在例 2-4 中，销售 1,100 → 1,650 的 50% 增长伴随杠杆一次提高；当期权益仅增长 13.64%。下一年把杠杆恢复旧水平，销售在其余题设下变为 1,375，下降 16.67%。这组数说明资金政策改变能产生过渡效应，不是说任何高速增长企业下一年都会萎缩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Example 2-4, sales growth from 1,100 to 1,650 accompanies a one-time leverage increase, while equity grows 13.64%. Returning leverage to its earlier level gives sales of 1,375 next year under the other assumptions, a 16.67% decline. This illustrates a policy transition, not an inevitable contraction after all rapid growth.

## 八、把分析结果交还给决策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Return the analysis to the decision.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本章结束时，应能拿一家公司走完四句话：利润来自什么、资产被哪里占用、到期资金靠什么支付、增长计划还缺多少资金。每个结论都要有对应报表、公式口径和经营解释，而不只是“比率变好”或“增长可持续”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the end, explain where profit comes from, where assets are tied up, how payments due will be funded, and what additional funding the growth plan needs. Each conclusion requires a statement, consistent calculation, and business explanation—not merely a favourable ratio or a sustainable-growth label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

试着连答三个问题：ABC 有净利润 136，为什么不代表当期多了现金 136？ROE 的杠杆贡献为正，为什么仍不能断言经营更好？年度融资缺口为零，为什么还需要月度现金预算？前两个问题分别回到报表勾稽和杜邦口径，第三个回到现金收付时点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Try three connected questions: why does ABC's net income of 136 not imply 136 more cash; why does a positive leverage contribution to ROE not prove better operations; and why does zero annual EFN not eliminate a monthly cash budget? The answers follow from statement reconciliation, DuPont interpretation, and payment timing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
