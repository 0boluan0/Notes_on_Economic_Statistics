## 一.短期偿债能力比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Short-term solvency ratios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
现金为王,反映企业的经营能力.很多时候步子跨太大就容易资金链断裂.债主可以要求提前还款,所以要让短期偿债能力看起来不错.不然债主就会找上门.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash is king because it reflects a firm's ability to keep operating. Expanding too aggressively can break the funding chain, and creditors may demand early repayment. A company therefore needs credible short-term solvency to avoid immediate creditor pressure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)可偿债资产与短期债务的存量比较
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Stock comparisons between assets available for repayment and short-term debt*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1. 存量差额比较:
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Comparing stock differences*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

营运资本=流动资产-流动负债=长期资本-长期资产
	1. 这个推导为:流动资产=总资产-非流动资产.且流动负债=总负债-非流动负债,并且总资产=总负债+股东权益.所以一套流程下来得出营运资本=长期资本-长期资产.
	2. 长期资本优先供长期资产用,用剩下的给营运,也可以这么理解,
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Working capital = current assets − current liabilities = long-term capital − long-term assets.
	1. The identity follows from current assets = total assets − non-current assets, current liabilities = total liabilities − non-current liabilities, and total assets = total liabilities + shareholders' equity. Combining them gives working capital = long-term capital − long-term assets.
	2. Intuitively, long-term capital first finances long-term assets, and any remainder supports day-to-day operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2. 存量比率比较
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Comparing stock ratios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

某些流动资产/流动负债
1. 流动比率:流动资产/流动负债
2. 速动比率:速动资产/流动负债(不考虑预付帐款,存货,一年内到期的非流动资产)
3. 现金比率:现金/流动负债(速动资产-应收账款)
这个就忽略了比率的影响.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These ratios compare selected current assets with current liabilities:
1. Current ratio: current assets divided by current liabilities.
2. Quick ratio: quick assets divided by current liabilities, excluding prepayments, inventory, and non-current assets due within one year.
3. Cash ratio: cash divided by current liabilities; the note treats cash as quick assets less receivables.
This still overlooks the influence of the chosen ratio specification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

流动资产要考虑行业标准问题.重工业流动资产是很高的.但是高新技术产业没什么原材料投入. 
流动资产中有两个坑:预付账款和存货.eg:冰墩墩.存货是最难变现的.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Current-asset levels must be interpreted against industry norms. Heavy industry may hold substantial current assets, whereas high-technology industries may require little raw-material input.
Two common traps within current assets are prepayments and inventory. The Bing Dwen Dwen example illustrates how inventory can become especially difficult to realize.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

速动资产是流动资产中变现很快的部分,货币资金,交易性金融资产和各种应收账款等.报表就是按照变现能力排列的.
存货,预付账款,一年内到期的非流动资产等算是非速动资产,预付账款也是特殊的,预付账款在流动资产中,但是基本不可能要回来了.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quick assets are the current assets that can be converted into cash rapidly, including cash, financial assets held for trading, and receivables. Balance-sheet assets are ordered broadly by liquidity.
Inventory, prepayments, and non-current assets due within one year are treated here as non-quick assets. Prepayments are unusual: they appear within current assets but generally cannot simply be recovered as cash.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金比率避免了前面两个的问题,但是还是有一个坑,没有区分现金的来源
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cash ratio avoids the two preceding problems, but it still does not distinguish where the cash came from.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)现金流量比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Cash-flow ratio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看你真正的造血能力.这个是时期,而之前的是时点.但是债务是特殊的,以前的已经被还掉的就无所谓了.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This ratio measures the firm's genuine capacity to generate cash internally. Cash flow is measured over a period, whereas the preceding balance-sheet amounts are measured at a point in time. Debt is unusual because obligations already repaid no longer matter at the reporting date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金流量比率=经营活动现金流量净额/流动负债
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash-flow ratio = net cash flow from operating activities divided by current liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*(3) Other factors affecting short-term solvency*

#### 1. 增强短期偿债能力的表外因素

3. 偿债的声誉

2. Non-current assets that can be realized quickly, such as land, unexploited mineral rights, or rented property.

1. 与担保有关的或有负债事项

##  二.长期偿债能力比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*II. Long-term solvency ratios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

流动负债相当于企业的零息借款.真正要付利息的是企业的长期负债.所以在这一部分要考虑的不只是企业的还本能力,还要考虑它的付息能力.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Current liabilities resemble interest-free borrowing for the firm, whereas long-term debt usually carries an explicit interest cost. Long-term solvency therefore concerns both the ability to repay principal and the ability to service interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)存量比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Stock ratios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1.资产负债率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Debt-to-assets ratio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
 
总负债/总资产x100%
总资产中有多少是靠借债来的.反映企业清算的时候对债权人的保护程度.越高越低.保护程度和比率成反比.
但是我们嫌他不够精准,因为总负债中有流动负债,流动负债基本来自供应商.不是债权人要看到的
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Total liabilities divided by total assets, multiplied by 100%.
This shows how much of total assets is financed by debt and, inversely, how much protection creditors have in liquidation: a higher ratio implies less protection.
The measure is imprecise for some purposes because total liabilities include current liabilities, many of which arise from suppliers rather than interest-bearing lenders.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2. 产权比率和权益乘数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Debt-to-equity ratio and equity multiplier*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 产权比率=总负债/股东权益
2. 权益乘数=总资产/股东权益.反映企业的杠杆效率. 股东投入一份钱能撬动多少钱来经营.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Debt-to-equity ratio = total liabilities divided by shareholders' equity.
2. Equity multiplier = total assets divided by shareholders' equity. It describes the firm's leverage: how many units of operating assets are supported by each unit invested by shareholders.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3.长期资本负债率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Long-term debt-to-capital ratio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长期资本负债率=(非流动负债/(非流动负债+股东权益))x100%
相较于[[02_财务报表分析和财务预测#1.资产负债率|资产负债率]].这部分刨除了流动负债的影响.能够反映企业的长期的资本结构 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Long-term debt-to-capital ratio = non-current liabilities divided by the sum of non-current liabilities and shareholders' equity, multiplied by 100%.
Compared with the [[02_财务报表分析和财务预测#1.资产负债率|debt-to-assets ratio]], this measure removes current liabilities and better represents the firm's long-term capital structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)流量比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Flow ratios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1. 利息保障倍数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Times interest earned*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
#难点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Difficult point*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
利息保障倍数=息税前利润/利息支出=(净利润+利息费用+所得税费用)/利息支出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Times interest earned = earnings before interest and tax divided by interest expense = net profit plus interest expense plus income-tax expense, divided by interest expense.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财务报表中没有息税前利润这一概念,所以进行反推.使用净利润+利息费用+所得税费用.分母的利息算的是实际的总利息.是资本化利息+费用化利息.分子的利息是费用化的利息. 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because the financial statements do not report an explicit EBIT line, it is reconstructed as net profit plus interest expense plus income-tax expense. The denominator uses total actual interest, including both capitalized and expensed interest, whereas the interest added back in the numerator is expensed interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

太高太低可能会导致股东恐慌.因为利润是可以调整的.横向和别的同行比,然后在自己的历史里纵向找,最后确定一个较好的区间.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An unusually high or low figure may alarm shareholders, especially because reported profit can be influenced by accounting choices. Interpret the ratio through both peer comparison and the firm's own history before deciding what range is reasonable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2.现金流量利息保障倍数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Cash interest coverage ratio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金流量利息保障倍数=经营活动现金流量净额/利息支出.这里的利息支出也是总的.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash interest coverage ratio = net cash flow from operating activities divided by interest expense. Interest expense again means total interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3.现金流量与负债比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Cash-flow-to-debt ratio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金流量与负债比率=(经营活动现金流量净额/负债总额)x100%
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash-flow-to-debt ratio = net cash flow from operating activities divided by total liabilities, multiplied by 100%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (三)影响长期偿债能力的其他因素
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Other factors affecting long-term solvency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1.债务担保
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Debt guarantees*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

别的企业可以通过控股来攫取利益,通过让A公司给B公司担保,B不还钱,A代B还钱,最后的效果就是把A的钱给到B.
担保项目的时间长短不一,影响长期偿债能力或短期偿债能力.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A controlling company can transfer value by having Company A guarantee Company B's debt. If B defaults and A must repay, the economic effect is a transfer of A's resources to B.
Guarantees have different maturities and may therefore weaken either long-term or short-term solvency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2. 长期租赁
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Long-term leases*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

融资租赁,会发生所有权转移.最后一笔租赁的租金到了的时候就算我的了.企业在签合同之后就算作自己的固定资产,没交的租赁费算到长期负债里.
经营租赁不发生所有权转移.
影响判断的是经营租赁.这个时候如果经常用,但是我们会把它算到流动负债里.实际上应该影响的是长期偿债能力.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finance lease transfers ownership: after the final rental payment, the asset belongs to the lessee. Once the contract is signed, the firm records the asset as a fixed asset and unpaid rentals as a long-term liability.
An operating lease does not transfer ownership.
Operating leases are the more difficult case for analysis. Recurrent lease commitments may be recorded as current obligations even though economically they also affect long-term solvency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3.未决诉讼
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Pending litigation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

企业败诉会影响偿债能力,但是诉讼的周期非常长.eg:万宝路
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Losing a lawsuit can weaken solvency, but litigation may take a very long time, as illustrated by the Marlboro example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三.营运能力比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*III. Operating-efficiency ratios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)应收账款周转率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Accounts-receivable turnover*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 衡量方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Measurement methods*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

周转率低说明回款慢,周转率高说明回款快.这个大部分由行业特色决定.
这个是资产负债表中数据计算的,所以很容易被操纵.因为只要一瞬间高就行了,
还有减值准备的问题,
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A low turnover ratio implies slow collection, while a high ratio implies fast collection, although industry characteristics largely determine the benchmark.
Because the calculation uses a balance-sheet figure, it is easy to manipulate around the reporting date: the balance only needs to look favourable at one moment.
Impairment allowances create an additional measurement problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有三种计算方式:
1. 应收账周转次数=营业收入/应收账款
2. 应收账款周转天数=365/(营业收入/应收账款)
3. 应收账款与收入比=应收账款/营业收入
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are three common calculations:
1. Accounts-receivable turnover = revenue divided by accounts receivable.
2. Days sales outstanding = 365 divided by accounts-receivable turnover.
3. Receivables-to-revenue ratio = accounts receivable divided by revenue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 注意内容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Points to check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 营业收入的赊销比例问题.
2. 应收账款年末余额的可靠性问题、
3. 应收账款的减值准备问题eg.宁德时代操纵减值准备
4. 应收票据是否计入应收帐款周转率
5. 应收账款的周转天数也不是越少越好,太少了说明没实力.
6. 应收账款分析应该与销售额分析和现金分析联系起来 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. The share of revenue generated through credit sales.
2. The reliability of the year-end receivables balance.
3. The effect of impairment allowances, including the CATL example of discretionary provisioning.
4. Whether notes receivable are included in accounts-receivable turnover.
5. Fewer collection days are not always better; an extremely low figure may signal that the firm cannot offer customers competitive credit terms.
6. Receivables analysis should be connected with revenue and cash-flow analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)存货周转率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Inventory turnover*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

既不能越快越好,也不能太慢.太快会发生缺货损失,太慢存货会出问题
企业的链条是买入原材料获得应付账款,然后加工生成存货,然后卖出获得应收账款,而后用卖东西的钱继续采购原材料.所以三者的比率应该是同步的,任意一环的突增突减都是有问题的.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inventory turnover should be neither maximized without limit nor allowed to become too slow. Excessive speed may cause stockouts, while slow turnover may indicate obsolete or unsaleable inventory.
The operating cycle runs from purchasing raw materials and creating payables, through production and inventory, to sales and receivables, after which collected cash finances further purchases. The related turnover ratios should therefore move coherently; an abrupt change in any one link warrants investigation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三种计算方式:
1. 存货周转次数=营业收入或营业 成本/存货
2. 存货周转天数=365/(营业收入或营业成本
3. 存货与收入比=存货/营业收入
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are three common calculations:
1. Inventory turnover = revenue or cost of sales divided by inventory.
2. Inventory days = 365 divided by the turnover measure based on revenue or cost of sales.
3. Inventory-to-revenue ratio = inventory divided by revenue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

靠营业挣来的才是真本事,雅戈尔的老总那样的投资收入过多的是风险很大的.公司靠投资来进行盈利是很有风险的.新东方那种主营业务发生变更的也不能比较,

Profit generated by the core business is the more durable signal. Heavy reliance on investment income, as in the Youngor example, can be risky. Comparisons also become invalid when the principal business changes, as with New Oriental.

### (一)营业净利率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Net profit margin*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每一块钱的销售收入可以为我带来多少钱的净利润这个高了好.但是不可能太高.但是如果比银行存款还低就没必要投资了.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net profit margin asks how much net profit each unit of sales produces. A higher figure is generally preferable, though implausibly high values deserve scrutiny; if the return is below a bank-deposit rate, the investment case is weak.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

营业净利率=(净利率/营业收入)x100%

Net profit margin equals net profit divided by operating revenue, multiplied by 100%.

*(2) Return on assets*

节约资金占用或者增大利润都可以增加总资产净利率.节约资金占用就是提高存货周转率.
总资产净利率=(净利润/总资产)x100%
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return on assets can rise either because the firm ties up less capital or because it earns more profit. Reducing capital tied up in operations includes improving inventory turnover.
Return on assets equals net profit divided by total assets, multiplied by 100%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反映每一元股东权益赚取的净利润,

Return on equity measures the net profit earned per unit of shareholders' equity.

权益净利率=(净利率/股东权益)x100%

## 五,市价比率

*V. Market-value ratios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)市盈率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Price-to-earnings ratio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市盈率=每股市价/每股收益
其中,每股收益=普通股股东净利润/流通在外的普通股加权平均股数.(无优先股的情况)
每股收益=(净利润-优先股股息)/流通在外的普通股加权平均股数.(有优先股的情况)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price-to-earnings ratio = market price per share divided by earnings per share.
Without preferred shares, earnings per share equal net profit attributable to ordinary shareholders divided by the weighted-average number of ordinary shares outstanding.
With preferred shares, earnings per share equal net profit less preferred dividends, divided by the weighted-average number of ordinary shares outstanding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应注意,每股市价反映了投资者对于未来收益的预期,但是市盈率是基于过去年度的收益.所以如果投资者预期收益将从当前水平大幅增长,市盈率将会相当高.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The market price reflects investors' expectations of future earnings, whereas the denominator of the P/E ratio is based on past earnings. If investors expect earnings to grow sharply from the current level, the P/E ratio can therefore be high.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)市净率(市账率)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Price-to-book ratio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市净率=每股市价/每股净资产
其中,每股净资产=普通股股东权益/流通在外的普通股股数.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price-to-book ratio = market price per share divided by book value per share.
Book value per share equals ordinary shareholders' equity divided by the number of ordinary shares outstanding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (三)市销率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Price-to-sales ratio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市销率=每股市价/每股营业收入
每股营业收入=营业收入/流通在外普通股加权平均股数.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price-to-sales ratio = market price per share divided by revenue per share.
Revenue per share equals operating revenue divided by the weighted-average number of ordinary shares outstanding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 六.[[财务报表、财务比率与财务预测#杜邦分解|杜邦分析体系]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*VI. [[财务报表、财务比率与财务预测#杜邦分解|DuPont analysis]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)杜邦分析体系的核心比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) The core ratio in DuPont analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==[[02_财务报表分析和财务预测#(三)权益净利率|权益净利率]]==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==[[02_财务报表分析和财务预测#(三)权益净利率|Return on equity]]==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为企业是股东的企业,股东最关心的最直接的比率就是权益净利率.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because shareholders own the firm, return on equity is the most direct ratio from their perspective.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
权益净利率=\frac{净利率}{营业收入}\times\frac{营业收入}{总资产}\times\frac{总资产}{股东收益}
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first expression decomposes return on equity into profit relative to revenue, revenue relative to assets, and assets relative to shareholder funding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Basicly,it means:

$$权益净利率=营业净利率\times总资产周转次数\times权益乘数$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In words, return on equity equals net profit margin multiplied by total-asset turnover and the equity multiplier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)杜邦分析体系的基本框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) The basic DuPont framework*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

杜邦框架可写为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The DuPont framework may be written as follows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
ROE=\text{营业净利率}\times\text{总资产周转率}\times\text{权益乘数}.
$$
其中权益乘数刻画杠杆水平，总资产周转率刻画资产使用效率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equity multiplier captures leverage, while total-asset turnover captures how efficiently assets are used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中权益乘数可以简要理解为杠杆.
现在国家资金在空转,大部分企业没加杠杆.问题在于除了房地产在使劲加杠杆之外,别的行业的利率不入银行,
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equity multiplier can therefore be read as a compact measure of leverage.
The note observes that funds may circulate without reaching productive firms: most companies are not increasing leverage, while real estate has borrowed aggressively and financing does not flow readily to other industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (三)权益净利率的驱动因素分解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Decomposing the drivers of return on equity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

营业净利率反应销售效率的程度,总资产周转率体现生产的效率.权益乘数体现加的杠杆.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net profit margin reflects sales profitability, total-asset turnover reflects operating efficiency, and the equity multiplier reflects leverage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (四)杜邦分析体系的局限性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(4) Limitations of DuPont analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 总资产净利率的对象不匹配,总资产属于全部出资者,净利率属于股东.
2. 没有区分金融活动损益和经营活动损益.eg:做服装的雅戈尔.搞投资去了在牛市挣了不少钱
3. 没有区分金融资产与经营资产.
4. 没有区分金融负债与经营负债.
经营资产-经营负债得到净经营资产,金融负债-金融资产得到净金融负债,
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Return on assets mismatches its components: total assets belong to all capital providers, while net profit belongs to shareholders.
2. It does not separate gains and losses from financing activities from those generated by operations, as illustrated by Youngor's move from clothing into investment.
3. It does not distinguish financial assets from operating assets.
4. It does not distinguish financial liabilities from operating liabilities.
Operating assets less operating liabilities give net operating assets; financial liabilities less financial assets give net financial liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净经营资产是干这个活实际要花的钱.净金融负债是真的负债.比如一个月饼厂找银行借100万,只想借2个月来做月饼,但是银行1年起借,所以在借到钱后两个月挣到了150万,但是这时候银行不收欠款,因为要挣利息,所以月饼厂必须拿这笔钱去做点投资.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net operating assets represent the capital genuinely committed to the business, while net financial liabilities represent financing debt. For example, a mooncake producer may need a ¥1 million loan for only two months, but the bank lends for at least one year. After the seasonal business generates ¥1.5 million, the bank does not accept early repayment because it wants the interest, so the producer must temporarily invest the surplus cash.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 七.管理用财务报表体系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*VII. Management financial statements*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)管理用资产负债表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Management balance sheet*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 区分经营资产和金融资产
2. 区分经营负债和金融负债
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Separate operating assets from financial assets.
2. Separate operating liabilities from financial liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

管理用资产负债表的核心恒等式可写为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The core identities of the management balance sheet are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
\text{净经营资产}=\text{经营资产}-\text{经营负债},
$$
$$
\text{净金融负债}=\text{金融负债}-\text{金融资产},
$$
$$
\text{净经营资产}=\text{股东权益}+\text{净金融负债}.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net operating assets equal operating assets less operating liabilities. Net financial liabilities equal financial liabilities less financial assets. Net operating assets are financed by shareholders' equity plus net financial liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)管理用利润表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Management income statement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 区分经营损益和金融损益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Separate operating profit and loss from financing profit and loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

管理用利润表核心拆分为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The central decomposition in the management income statement is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
\text{净利润}=\text{税后经营净利润}-\text{税后利息费用}.
$$
其中税后项可由“税前金额 $\times (1-\tau)$”得到。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net profit equals net operating profit after tax less after-tax interest expense. An after-tax amount can be obtained from the corresponding pre-tax amount multiplied by $(1-\tau)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (三)管理用现金流量表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Management cash-flow statement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税前利息费用-利息费用抵免的所得税=税后费用
实体现金流量=水后经营净利润-净经营资产增加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After-tax interest expense equals pre-tax interest expense less the income-tax saving generated by that expense.
Entity free cash flow equals net operating profit after tax less the increase in net operating assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (四)管理用财务分析体系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(4) Management financial-analysis framework*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

管理用财务分析可进一步分解为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Management financial analysis can be decomposed further as follows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
ROE=\frac{\text{税后经营净利润}}{\text{净经营资产}}\times\frac{\text{净经营资产}}{\text{股东权益}}
\;-\;
\frac{\text{税后利息费用}}{\text{股东权益}}.
$$
对应地，经营回报、经营杠杆与融资成本可以分开评估。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This separates operating return, operating leverage, and financing cost so that each can be evaluated independently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一.财务预测的意义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Why financial forecasting matters*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

狭义的财务预测仅指估计公司未来的融资需求.广义的还得编制财务报表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the narrow sense, financial forecasting estimates the firm's future financing needs. In the broad sense, it also prepares forecast financial statements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 财务预测是融资计划的前提
2. 财务预测有助于改善投资决策
3. 预测有助于应变

基本就是计算需要多少现金.有多少经营资产决定了多大规模的营业收入.

This step effectively estimates how much funding is required. The amount of operating assets constrains the scale of revenue the business can support.

## 三.财务预测的方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*III. Financial-forecasting methods*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)销售百分比法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Percentage-of-sales method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假定相关资产,负债和营业收入存在稳定的百分比关系.然后根据预计营业收入和相应的百分比预计相关资产负债最后确定融资需求.   
并且计划销售净利率可以涵盖借款利息的增加.不然没必要扩规模
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The method assumes stable percentage relationships between relevant assets, liabilities, and revenue. Forecast revenue and those percentages are used to project the corresponding assets and liabilities, after which the financing requirement is determined.
The planned net profit margin must also cover the additional interest on new borrowing; otherwise, expanding the scale of operations would not be worthwhile.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1.确定经营资产和经营负债项目的销售百分比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Determine the sales percentages for operating assets and operating liabilities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

销售百分比法可按“总需求-内源资金”框架估算外部融资需求：  
总需求增长 = 预计净经营资产 - 基期净经营资产；  
内源资金来源包括可动用金融资产与新增留存收益；  
剩余缺口再由新增金融负债和增发股本补足。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the percentage-of-sales method, external financing can be estimated through a “total requirement minus internal funding” framework:
growth in the total requirement = forecast net operating assets − base-period net operating assets;
internal funding includes available financial assets and additional retained earnings;
any remaining gap is financed with new financial liabilities or new equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资金是可以循环使用的,所以资金的总需求是预期净经营资产-基期净经营资产.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because funds can be reused through the operating cycle, the total additional funding requirement is forecast net operating assets less base-period net operating assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2.预计各项经营资产和经营负债
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Forecast each operating asset and operating liability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

四种补充资金需求的难度从上到下依次升高.动用现存的金融资产是最简单的,就是把账上的理财卖出去.
增加留存收益,就是资产负债表中的未分配利润.是属于股东的钱,是有弹性的所有者权益.股东开个会就能决定.不需要外面的人审批或者担保什么的.
此时还不够的话就要向外部寻求帮助.增加金融负债,负债需要抵押物,抵押物100万只能借到30万.远水解不了近渴.
最后的办法是增加股本,也就是增资扩股.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four ways to fill a financing need become progressively harder. The easiest is to use existing financial assets, such as selling investments already on the balance sheet.
The next source is retained earnings, reported as undistributed profit. This is flexible equity belonging to shareholders and can be retained through an internal decision without an external guarantor or approval.
If that is insufficient, the firm must seek external funding. Additional debt may require collateral, and assets worth ¥1 million might support only ¥300,000 of borrowing, so it may not solve an urgent gap.
The final option is to issue additional equity and enlarge the share capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3. 预计可动用的金融资产
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Estimate financial assets available for use*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 4.预计增加的留存收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Estimate the increase in retained earnings*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

留存收益增加=预计营业收入x预计营业净利率x(1-预计股利支付率)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Increase in retained earnings = forecast revenue × forecast net profit margin × (1 − forecast dividend-payout ratio).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 二.可持续增长率的测算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*II. Measuring the sustainable growth rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)可持续增长率的概念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Concept of sustainable growth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不增发新股或回购股票,不改变筋骨硬效率和财务政策时.其下期销售所能达到的最大的增长率.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sustainable growth rate is the maximum growth in next period's sales when the firm neither issues nor repurchases shares and does not change its operating efficiency or financial policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设条件:
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Assumptions:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设条件可概括为：  
1. 不增发新股或回购股票；  
2. 经营效率与财务政策保持稳定（利润率、周转率、杠杆和分红政策不变）；  
3. 资产、负债与销售额维持既有比例关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The assumptions can be summarized as follows:
1. The firm neither issues new shares nor repurchases existing shares.
2. Operating efficiency and financial policy remain stable: profit margin, turnover, leverage, and dividend policy do not change.
3. Assets and liabilities retain their existing proportional relationships with sales.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)可持续增长率计算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Calculating the sustainable growth rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1.根据期初股东权益计算可持续增长率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Calculation using beginning shareholders' equity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要和杜邦分析体系一起理解.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This calculation should be understood together with the DuPont framework.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$权益净利率=营业净利率\times总资产周转次数\times权益乘数
$$
$$权益净利率=\frac{净利率}{营业收入}\times\frac{营业收入}{总资产}\times\frac{总资产}{股东收益}$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equations express return on equity as net profit margin × total-asset turnover × the equity multiplier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于资产周转率不变,所以营业收入增长率等于总资产增长率.
同时资本结构不变,所以资产增长率等于所有者权益和负债增长率.且不增发新股.所有者权益增长全部来自于留存收益的增长率.所以留存收益的增长率等于上面的一串.
且
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because asset turnover is constant, the growth rate of revenue equals the growth rate of total assets.
Because the capital structure is also constant, asset growth equals the growth of both equity and liabilities. With no new share issue, all growth in shareholders' equity comes from retained earnings, so the retained-earnings growth rate is determined by the preceding decomposition.
Therefore:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$可持续增长率=股东权益增长率=\frac{本期净利润\times本期利润留存率}{期初股东权益}$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sustainable growth rate equals the growth rate of shareholders' equity: current net profit multiplied by the retention ratio, divided by beginning shareholders' equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

撇去本期利润留存率,剩下的部分和权益净利率很像,只是将权益乘数换成了期末总资产期初权益乘数.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After setting aside the current retention ratio, the remaining expression resembles return on equity, except that the usual equity multiplier is replaced by ending total assets divided by beginning equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

all in all

$$可持续增长率=营业净利率\times总资产周转率\times期末总资产起初权益净利率\times利润留存率$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Overall, sustainable growth is expressed as net profit margin × total-asset turnover × the end-assets-to-beginning-equity multiplier × the retention ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2.根据期末股东权益计算可持续增长率.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Calculation using ending shareholders' equity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$可持续增长率=\frac{x}{1-x}$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using ending equity, the sustainable growth rate is $x/(1-x)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中$$x=期末杜邦权益净利率\times利润留存率$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here, $x$ equals ending-period DuPont return on equity multiplied by the retention ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
