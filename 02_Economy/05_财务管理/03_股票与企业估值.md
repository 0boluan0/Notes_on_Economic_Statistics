# 股票与企业估值：从股东现金到经营价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stock and enterprise valuation: from shareholder cash to operating value
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估值先回答三个问题：这笔现金属于谁、什么时候到、为了得到它还要投入什么。沿着这三个问题，本课从普通股的分配权利出发，先推导股利估值，再把报表预测转成企业与股东现金流，最后处理终值、每股权利和市场倍数。公式相似不代表估的对象相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with three questions: who owns the cash, when does it arrive, and what must be invested to obtain it? This lesson begins with shareholder distribution rights, derives dividend valuation, translates financial forecasts into firm and equity cash flows, and then addresses terminal value, per-share claims, and market multiples. Similar formulas need not value the same claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 买到的是什么，哪些分配真的带来现金？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
What is purchased, and which distributions actually transfer cash?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

买股票不是买一张自带固定收益的票据，而是买特定股份类别对应的权利。先读这个已经与其他课程共享的定义，再考虑如何给这份权利定价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Buying a share means acquiring the rights of a particular share class, not a certificate with a fixed return. Begin with the definition shared across courses before pricing those rights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[股权]]

现金股利使公司付出现金；“未分配利润”却是权益中的累计会计项目，不是一只装着同额现金的账户。公司有利润，不代表这些利润已全部收现，更不代表它必须立即派完。股票股利增加股份单位并在权益内部重分类，本身没有把现金交给股东；拆股同样改变单位数，但会计机制与股票股利并不完全相同。二者都不能直接当成下面公式中的每股现金股利 $D_t$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A cash dividend transfers company cash. Retained earnings, however, is an accumulated accounting equity item, not an account containing an equal amount of cash. Profit need not already be collected or immediately distributed. A stock dividend increases share units through an internal equity reclassification without itself paying cash to shareholders. A split also changes units, although its accounting mechanism differs. Neither is directly the per-share cash dividend $D_t$ used below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回购则向卖出股份的股东支付现金，同时改变剩余股份数量。分析留在公司的股东时，要跟踪公司付出的现金和缩小的分母，不能只看到每股收益上升就断言财富增加。这些分配机制用于识别估值现金流；具体分配程序、税收与除权除息日期须按适用市场及当时规则另查，不能把2023版教材的历史条款当成现行通则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A repurchase pays cash to selling shareholders and changes the number of remaining shares. For continuing holders, track both the cash paid out and the smaller denominator; higher EPS alone does not establish a wealth gain. These mechanisms identify valuation cash flows. Procedures, taxes, and ex-dates require the relevant market's applicable rules rather than treating historical provisions in the 2023 textbook as universal current rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，面值、账面每股净资产、市场成交价和模型估计值回答不同问题。以下估值都附带预测与风险假设；它们帮助理解价格，而不保证市场何时按这个答案成交。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Face value, book value per share, market price, and model value answer different questions. Every valuation below is conditional on forecasts and risk assumptions. It helps interpret price without promising when the market will trade at the model value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 先从持有一年推到股利贴现模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Derive the DDM from a one-year holding period
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

站在今天，持有一年可能得到两笔东西：期末股利，以及领完这笔股利后仍然持有的股票。卖出只是把剩余权利变成售价；所以有限持有期不能漏掉卖价，也不能在已经含同一股利权的价格上再加股利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From today's perspective, a one-year holding provides the period-end dividend and the share still owned after receiving it. Selling converts the remaining rights into a price. A finite holding period therefore needs a sale value, but a price already including the same dividend right must not have that dividend added again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[股利贴现模型]]

无限股利式不是假设你本人必须持有到永远。它把未来买家愿意支付的剩余权利继续向后展开，并明确折现终端残项消失的条件。于是，估值的困难从“有没有售价”变成“未来股利路径和风险要求有什么依据”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The infinite-dividend expression does not require you personally to hold forever. It recursively expands the rights a future buyer purchases and states the condition under which the discounted residual terminal term vanishes. The challenge becomes supporting the dividend path and required return, rather than merely including a sale price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 零增长与戈登增长：先认准下一笔现金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Level and Gordon growth: identify the next cash payment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果预计每年末永远派同样的现金股利，这就是已有的[[固定永续年金]]；不是只有若干期的普通等额年金。若未来股利按固定增长率延续，则复用[[增长永续年金]]。数学没有换，变化的是现金来自哪份股权以及应使用什么风险回报要求。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A constant cash dividend paid at every year-end forever is the existing [[固定永续年金|level perpetuity]], not a finite level annuity. Constantly growing dividends use the [[增长永续年金|growing perpetuity]]. The mathematics is shared; the underlying equity claim and its required return determine the economic inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[戈登增长模型]]

阅读教材的2元、12%、16%例子时，先在时间线上标出：2元已经付过，未来第一笔才是2.24元。$D_0$ 与 $D_1$ 的差别不是符号习惯，而是这笔现金是否仍属于今天买入的人。12%也只是这个练习给定的永久增长假设，不是现实估值可以直接照抄的参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the textbook's 2, 12%, and 16% example, mark the timeline first: the dividend of 2 has already been paid, while 2.24 is the first future payment. The distinction between $D_0$ and $D_1$ identifies cash rights still purchased today. The 12% perpetual growth rate is an exercise assumption, not a parameter to copy into an actual valuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 高增长会结束：把切分日与终值放对
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When high growth ends, date the cutoff and continuation value correctly
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当未来几年增长率不同，先展开这些年，再压缩稳定尾段。最有用的检查不是记住“第几年开始稳定”，而是说清终值覆盖的第一笔现金。下面保留教材例3-25的完整计算，并用两种切分核对同一条现金流。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When growth varies across the next few years, forecast those years explicitly and compress the stable tail. The useful check is identifying the first cash payment covered by terminal value, not memorising a year label. The following complete version of textbook example 3-25 verifies the same cash stream using two cutoffs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多阶段股利估值]]

读完后应能自己解释：129.024在第3期末、覆盖第4期及以后；115.2在第2期末、覆盖第3期及以后。两条路线不是两种股价预测，而是同一价值的两种分组。以后把股利换成 FCFF 或 FCFE，这个时点原则仍然保留。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
You should now be able to explain why 129.024 belongs at year 3 and covers year 4 onward, while 115.2 belongs at year 2 and covers year 3 onward. These are two groupings of one valuation, not competing price forecasts. The same timing principle survives when dividends are replaced by FCFF or FCFE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 已知市价时，收益率是反解出来的
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With market price given, the return is an inverse calculation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面给风险所要求的 $k_e$，计算价值 $V_0$；现在给市场价格 $P_0$，反问“按照我假设的股利路径，什么利率能让现值等于这个价格？”这是模型隐含收益率。它是否达到你的必要回报，以及未来是否真的实现，是后续两个不同判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Previously, the risk-based required return $k_e$ produced value $V_0$. Now market price $P_0$ is supplied: which rate makes the assumed dividends worth that price? This gives the model-implied return. Whether it meets the required return and whether it is subsequently realised are separate questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[戈登隐含收益率]]

这里的 $D_1/P_0$ 精确复用[[股利收益率]]中的前瞻口径。若证券分析表只给上一年实际派息，应先识别它是历史指标，再决定能否用来预测下一笔；不要把它悄悄替换成未来股利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The term $D_1/P_0$ uses the forward definition in [[股利收益率|dividend yield]]. If an analysis table reports the preceding year's actual dividend, identify it as historical before deciding whether it supports the next-payment forecast. Do not silently substitute it for a future dividend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 从“实际发多少”转向“经营能留下多少”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Move from actual payouts to cash available from operations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股利路径不稳定，或企业暂时大量留存现金时，可以先预测业务需要的经营投入，再问剩余现金属于哪些资本提供者。下方的 FCFF 定义与证券分析、报表分析共享同一个原子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When dividends are unstable or cash is retained, forecast the investment needed by the business and then identify the capital providers entitled to the remainder. The FCFF definition below is the same shared object used in securities and financial-statement analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[企业自由现金流]]

FCFF尚未扣债权人的融资现金收支。要走到股东，扣税后利息，再把新借款与本金偿还的净额计入；这是现金归属的改变，不是凭借融资让经营多赚了利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
FCFF precedes debt financing cash flows. To reach equity, deduct after-tax interest and include borrowing net of principal repayment. This changes the cash claim; financing has not thereby created additional operating profit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[股权自由现金流]]

即使已经算出 FCFE，公司也未必全额派发。留存款若被合理投资、之后完整回到股东手里，与已经派掉并没有简单的“谁一定更值钱”关系；若留存被浪费或后续分配漏算，结论又会改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even calculated FCFE need not all be distributed. When retained cash is invested appropriately and eventually reaches shareholders, retention versus immediate payout has no simple automatic ranking. Wasteful investment or omitted future distributions changes that conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[股利与自由现金流估值差异]]

换分子也必须换对分母：股利与 FCFE属于股权，用匹配的 $k_e$；FCFF属于债权与股权等全部资本，用匹配的 WACC。已经用税后经营现金构造 FCFF 时，不能再扣一次利息却仍宣称在估经营业务的全部价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Match the denominator to the numerator: dividends and FCFE belong to equity and use an appropriate $k_e$; FCFF belongs to all capital providers and uses an appropriate WACC. Deducting interest again from after-tax operating FCFF changes the claim and no longer represents the same all-capital operating value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先检查[[名义与实际贴现]]：按各期货币金额、含预期通胀预测的现金，配名义贴现率；按不变购买力预测的现金，配实际贴现率。终值增长率也必须沿用同一口径；只确认币种相同还不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check [[名义与实际贴现|nominal–real consistency]] first: cash flows in future money amounts, including expected inflation, require nominal rates; constant-purchasing-power flows require real rates. Terminal growth must use the same basis. Matching currency alone is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选定现金流后，还要确认预测只是情景的期望，还是已作确定性等价调整；两者对贴现率的要求不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After choosing the cash-flow claim, establish whether the forecast is an expectation across scenarios or has already been converted to certainty equivalents; the matching discount rates differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[风险调整不重复计价]]

![[加权平均资本成本]]

## 7. 一次走通报表预测：销售到股东现金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work through one financial forecast from sales to shareholder cash
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CPA例7-1、表7-2与表7-3提供了一个可连续手算的桥。以下金额均为万元，以20×0年为基期。销售为1000，下一年增长12%；营业成本和销售管理费用分别占销售70%与5%；净经营资产占销售80%，净负债占销售40%，债务利息率6%，税率25%。这些是练习给定的比例预测，不是任何公司的固定规律；现实使用[[销售百分比法]]时应先验证各项目是否真的随销售变动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CPA example 7-1 and tables 7-2 and 7-3 provide a complete numerical bridge. Amounts are in ten-thousand yuan, with year 20×0 as the base. Sales are 1000 and grow 12% next year; cost of sales and selling/administrative costs are 70% and 5% of sales. NOA is 80% of sales, net debt 40%, debt interest 6%, and tax 25%. These are exercise assumptions, not universal ratios. Applying the [[销售百分比法|percentage-of-sales method]] requires checking which items actually vary with sales.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先算业务经营产生的税后利润，不把债务利息混入这一步：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First calculate after-tax operating profit, without inserting debt interest at this stage:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
Sales_1&=1000(1.12)=1120,\\
EBIT_1&=1120-1120(70\%)-1120(5\%)=280,\\
NOPAT_1&=280(1-25\%)=210.
\end{aligned}
$$

210还不是全部可分现金。为支撑销售增加，净经营资产从 $1000(80\%)=800$ 增至 $1120(80\%)=896$，增加96。教材以净经营资产增加代表匹配的净现金再投资，因而本例“实体现金流量”为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The 210 is not all available cash. Supporting higher sales raises NOA from 800 to 896, an increase of 96. The textbook treats the NOA increase as matched net cash reinvestment, giving its firm cash flow:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$CF_{firm,1}=210-(896-800)=114.$$

这个替代需要[[自由现金流的净经营资产表示]]中的条件：折旧与资本支出要匹配，非现金资产变动要调节，经营现金范围也要一致。表7-3只有80%的总净经营资产假设，没有单列必要现金，因此114先按教材自身口径理解，不能断言它已经等于剔除现金营运资本版本的 FCFF。若需要另留经营现金，也不能在净经营资产已扣过后再扣一次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This substitution requires the conditions in [[自由现金流的净经营资产表示|the NOA representation of free cash flow]]: matched depreciation and capex, adjustments for non-cash asset changes, and consistent operating-cash scope. Table 7-3 gives only the aggregate 80% NOA assumption, without separately identifying required cash. Interpret 114 within that textbook convention rather than asserting it is already the non-cash-working-capital FCFF measure. Do not reserve operating cash again if it was already deducted within NOA.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再走融资桥：净负债从400增至448，练习将增加48当作净举债现金。教材**按本年销售对应的期末净负债448计息**，于是税后利息为 $448(6\%)(1-25\%)=20.16$。这不是通用的“所有利息都按期末余额算”；若真实借款在年内分批发生，要按实际现金融资与计息时序重建。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next apply the financing bridge. Net debt rises from 400 to 448, and the exercise treats the increase of 48 as net borrowing cash. The textbook calculates interest using **448, the ending net debt associated with current-year sales**, giving after-tax interest of 20.16. This is not a universal end-balance interest convention. Actual borrowing at different dates requires its own cash-financing and interest schedule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$FCFE_1=114-20.16+48=141.84.$$

这里 FCFE 大于实体现金114，是因为净借款48超过了税后利息20.16，不是股东在没有融资支持时也能拿走141.84。它仍是“可分配”的预测，不能直接当作公司已承诺的股利。继续预测下一年时，同样把销售、利润、投资和融资连起来；只给利润增长率而不说明资金需求，不能完成现金估值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
FCFE exceeds firm cash flow because net borrowing of 48 exceeds after-tax interest of 20.16, not because shareholders could withdraw 141.84 without financing support. It is still projected distributable cash, not a promised dividend. Subsequent years must connect sales, profit, investment, and financing in the same way; profit growth alone does not complete a cash valuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8. 把整条经营现金流折回今天
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Discount the operating cash-flow path to today
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

报表预测提供分子，资本成本提供匹配的时间价格。有限年份逐期折现；预测期之后的业务仍存在，就在预测期末计算一笔覆盖后续现金的价值。这个框架与刚才的多阶段股利计算相同，但 FCFF 的现值首先属于经营业务的全部资本提供者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial forecasts supply the numerator and capital costs supply the matched time price. Discount explicit years separately. If the business continues, value the subsequent stream at the forecast horizon. This repeats the structure of multistage dividend valuation, but FCFF present value belongs initially to all capital providers of the operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[企业现金流估值]]

一定区分两个日期：终值在哪一天，以及终值覆盖的第一笔现金在哪一天。稳定阶段风险可能与前面不同，稳定期资本成本用来压缩稳定现金，前面几年的资本成本则负责把终值再折到今天。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish the terminal value's date from the first cash payment it covers. Stable-phase risk may differ from earlier risk: the stable cost of capital compresses the tail, while earlier-period costs discount that terminal value back to today.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[估值终值]]

稳定增长还要“长得出来”。若增长依靠新增投入，调高 $g$ 时通常也要增加再投资，改变现金流分子。下面把这个约束写清，避免只缩小分母就得到一个看起来更高的价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stable growth also needs an economic source. If growth requires investment, raising $g$ usually increases reinvestment and changes the cash-flow numerator. The following condition prevents a higher value being manufactured merely by shrinking the denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[终值增长的再投资约束]]

## 9. 从经营价值走到今天的每一股
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bridge operating value to each share outstanding today
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

算出经营价值后，先清点哪些资产尚未估入、哪些权利不属于母公司普通股。现金、非经营持股、债务和子公司的外部股东都要与合并范围对齐。不能把“净债务”当作一个不用展开的黑箱，也不能在扣净债务后再加同一笔已净掉的现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After valuing operations, identify excluded assets and claims not belonging to parent common shareholders. Align cash, non-operating holdings, debt, and outside subsidiary owners with the consolidation scope. Net debt must be defined explicitly; cash already netted against debt cannot be added a second time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[经营价值转股权价值]]

若实际企业另有优先股或养老金缺口，先按上面的价值桥补齐，再进入普通股与期权的分配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the actual firm has preferred stock or a pension shortfall, complete the adjustments in the value bridge above before allocating value between common shares and options.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再把股权价值分配给具体权利。这个问题看起来像每股收益的分母，实际却不同：EPS分摊一个会计期间的利润，估值在一个日期分配未来经济利益。现存期权与未来薪酬也不是同一批权利，不能用增加股数和扣权利价值两种办法重复处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then allocate equity value among the actual claims. This resembles an EPS denominator but answers a different question: EPS allocates accounting-period earnings, whereas valuation allocates future benefits at a particular date. Existing options and future compensation are different claims; do not handle the same rights twice through both extra shares and a claim-value deduction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[估值日每股价值]]

## 10. 用相对估值交叉检查，不能省掉可比条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cross-check with relative valuation without dropping comparability conditions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金流估值从业务自己的预测出发；相对估值则借助别人的市场定价。它可以给出另一个参照，却继承可比公司的价格水平和选择偏差。先把“相对于谁便宜”与“自身现金能支持多少价值”分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash-flow valuation starts from the business's own forecasts, whereas relative valuation uses other firms' market pricing. It offers another benchmark but inherits comparable prices and selection choices. Separate being cheap relative to peers from the value supported by the business's own cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[相对估值]]

市盈率不是一个与增长、分红和风险无关的价格标签。把戈登式除以每股收益，就能看见它为什么随这些基本面变化。因此，同行业只是选可比对象的起点，不能保证同一个市盈率合理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
P/E is not a price label independent of growth, payout, and risk. Dividing the Gordon value by earnings per share reveals those fundamental dependencies. Industry membership is only a starting point for comparables, not a guarantee that one multiple is appropriate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[市盈率基本面约束]]

市净率把分母换成账面股权，问题就变成“每单位账面资本能产生多少利润、还要留存多少、承担多少风险”。账面计量相似、ROE口径一致，才有讨论倍数差异的基础。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
P/B replaces the denominator with book equity, shifting attention to earnings produced per unit of book capital, required retention, and risk. Comparable accounting and consistent ROE measurement are prerequisites for interpreting differences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[市净率基本面约束]]

如果进一步用增长调整市盈率，先弄清 PEG 的数值单位，再读它没有消除哪些差异。一个比率除以增长率，不会自动把风险、现金分配和预测可靠性全部标准化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When adjusting P/E for growth, first establish PEG's numerical units, then examine which differences it leaves intact. Dividing by growth does not automatically standardise risk, payout, or forecast reliability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[PEG]]

![[PEG比较边界]]

如果分子改为整个经营业务的价值，就不能仍配只属于普通股的每股收益。EV/EBITDA采用全资本口径作比较，但 EBITDA 不是扣完税和再投资的自由现金流；本课前面从利润走到114的投资扣除，不会因为用了倍数就消失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A numerator representing operating enterprise value cannot be paired indiscriminately with earnings belonging only to common equity. EV/EBITDA uses an all-capital comparison, but EBITDA is not free cash flow after tax and reinvestment. The investment deductions that produced 114 earlier do not cease to matter when a multiple is used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[EV-EBITDA倍数]]

## 11. 最后检查：结论依赖什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Final check: what drives the conclusion?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

得出每股值之后，不急着把它当成答案。回到最影响结果的假设：利润率是否可持续、增长需要多少投资、稳定期什么时候开始、风险回报要求和权利口径有没有一致。一次只改变一个输入的敏感性，与一组联动假设的情景分析，也应分开解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Do not treat a per-share number as the end of the reasoning. Revisit sustainable margins, investment required for growth, the start of stable operations, required returns, and claim consistency. Distinguish changing one input from a scenario in which connected assumptions move together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先量出结果有多大程度依赖显性期之后，再决定优先检验哪些假设。下面沿用第8节的经营现金算例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First measure reliance on cash beyond the explicit period, then prioritise the assumptions to test. The following atom reuses the operating-cash example from section 8.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[终值现值占比]]

![[估值敏感性]]

还可以固定市场价，把问题倒过来：什么增长、利润率或持续期限才能支持这个价格？先固定模型与其余输入，再反求选定变量；这与第5节反求收益率是相通的思路，但不能用一个价格同时识别所有经营和风险假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Alternatively, hold market price fixed and ask which growth, margin, or duration would support it. Fix the model and other inputs before solving for one selected variable. This parallels the implied-return calculation in section 5, but one price cannot simultaneously identify every operating and risk assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[反向估值]]

完成这条路径的最小检验是：能独立复算多阶段股利的两种切分；从1120销售推到114实体现金与141.84股权现金；解释二者用什么折现率；再说明为什么经营价值不能直接除以普通股数。若某一步说不清，回到对应原子补这一段理解，而不是重新背整章公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A minimal completion test is to reproduce both multistage-dividend cutoffs, derive firm cash of 114 and equity cash of 141.84 from sales of 1120, match their discount rates, and explain why operating value cannot simply be divided by common shares. If one step remains unclear, revisit its exact atom instead of memorising the entire chapter again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
