## 一.基准利率及特征([[利率、即期远期与期限结构|Benchmark Interest Rate]] and Characteristics)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Benchmark Interest Rate and Its Characteristics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 基准利率(Benchmark Interest Rate)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Benchmark Interest Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利率时一定时期内利息和本金的比率.靳准利率是中央银行公布的商业银行存款.贷款,贴现等业务等指导性利率.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An interest rate is the ratio of interest to principal over a specified period. The note defines a benchmark rate as a reference rate announced by the central bank for commercial-bank deposits, loans, discounting, and related business.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 基准利率特征
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Characteristics of a Benchmark Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 市场化.基准利率必须由市场供求关系决定
2. 基础性:基准利率在利率体系.金融产品价格体系中处于基础地位.与其他金融市场利率或金融资产价格有较强的关联性.
3. 传递性:反映市场信号
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Market determination: the benchmark should reflect market supply and demand.<br>
**2.** Foundational role: it anchors the broader interest-rate system and is closely related to other market rates and asset prices.<br>
**3.** Transmission: it carries market signals through the financial system.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三.市场利率的影响因素(Influencing Factors of Market Interest Rates)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Factors Affecting Market Interest Rates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$r=r^*+RP=r^*+IP+DRP+LRP+MRP$$

$r^*$是纯粹利率.使用一年期国债的利率计量.RP是风险溢价.风险溢价又能分为IP通货膨胀溢价,DRP违约风险溢价,LRP流动性风险溢价MRP期限风险溢价.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$r^*$ is the pure, or risk-free, rate, approximated here by a one-year government-bond rate. $RP$ is the total risk premium, decomposed into an inflation premium $IP$, default-risk premium $DRP$, liquidity-risk premium $LRP$, and maturity-risk premium $MRP$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Future Value under Compound Interest*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特定的现在的钱按照复利计算的将来一定时间的价值,本金和利息之和
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Future value is the amount to which a present sum grows under compound interest, including principal and accumulated interest.

### (二)复利现值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Present Value under Compound Interest*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

和复利终值对应的概念.反过来算就行
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Present value reverses the future-value calculation by discounting a future amount back to today.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Quoted, or Nominal, Rate*

说的数.

#### 2. 计息期利率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Periodic Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计息期利率=报价利率/复利次数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Effective Annual Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

年计息期利率
