## 一.[[资本预算与投资决策#NPV|净现值法]]([[资本预算与投资决策#NPV|Net Present Value]] Method)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Net Present Value Method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)净现值(Net Present Value)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Net Present Value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1.含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Meaning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净现值是指特定项目未来现金流入的现值与未来现金流出的现值之间的差额.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net present value is the present value of a project's future cash inflows minus the present value of its future cash outflows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2. 计算公式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Formula*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
\text{净现值} = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{I_k}{(1 + i)^k} - \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{O_k}{(1 + i)^k}
$$

折旧看作是一种现金流入.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The note treats depreciation as a cash inflow; more precisely, depreciation is non-cash but can create a cash tax shield when it reduces taxable income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3.决策原则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Decision Rule*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当净现值大于0,投资项目可行.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Accept an independent project when its NPV is greater than zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 4. 特点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Characteristics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净现值法具有广泛的适用性， 在理论上也比其他方法更完善。 但是净现值反映一个项目按现金流量计量的净收益现值， 它是个金额的绝对值， 在比较投资额不同的项目时有一定的局限性.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NPV is widely applicable and theoretically stronger than many alternative rules. It reports an absolute amount of value created, however, so under a binding capital constraint it does not by itself rank projects by value created per unit of scarce investment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)现值系数([[资本预算与投资决策#NPV|Present Value Index]])
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Profitability Index*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1.含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Meaning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现值指数是未来现金流入现值与现金流出现值的比率。 因为净现值是绝对值指标， 对于初始投资者不相等的项目无法作比较， 因此用现值指数相对值指标， 能够比较初始投资额不相等的项目。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The profitability index is the present value of future cash inflows divided by the present value of cash outflows. As a relative measure, it can help compare projects of different sizes when investment capital is constrained, although it does not replace maximising the NPV of the feasible project portfolio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2. 计算公式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Formula*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现值指数=未来现金流入的现值/未来现金流出的现值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Profitability index = present value of future cash inflows / present value of future cash outflows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3. 决策原则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Decision Rule*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

pi>1可行,小于一不行
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an independent project, accept when $PI>1$ and reject when $PI<1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 4. 特点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Characteristics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现值指数消除了投资额的差异,但还没有小胡项目期限的差额.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The index normalises for investment size but does not eliminate differences in project duration or resolve every ranking conflict among mutually exclusive projects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 一.投资项目现金流量的构成

### (一)现金流量的含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Meaning of Cash Flow*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所谓现金流量， 在投资决策中是指一个项目引起的公司现金支出和现金收入增加的数量。 这里的“现金”是广义的现金， 它不仅包括各种货币资金， 而且包括项目需要投入公司拥有的非货币资源的变现价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In capital budgeting, project cash flow means the incremental cash payments and receipts caused by accepting the project. “Cash” is interpreted broadly: it includes monetary funds and the realisable value of non-cash resources already owned by the company but committed to the project.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不用管沉没成本.决策所引起的才算,别的不算.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ignore sunk costs. Count only cash flows that change because of the decision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 相关成本与非相关成本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Relevant and Irrelevant Costs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相关成本是指与特定决策有关的、 在分析评价时必须加以考虑的成本。 例如， 差额成本 未来成本、 重置成本、 机会成本等都是属于相关成本。 与此相反， 与特定决策无关的，在分析评价时不必加以考虑的成本是非相关成本。 例如， [[资本预算与投资决策#增量现金流|沉没成本]]、 过去成本等
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Relevant costs change with the decision and must be included, such as differential future costs, replacement costs, and opportunity costs. Irrelevant costs do not differ between accepting and rejecting the project; sunk and other past costs are examples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果厂房本来能出租但是现在为了项目不出租,这就算是相关成本

只看由于决策所引起的才算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a building could otherwise be rented out but is used by the project, the forgone rent is a relevant opportunity cost. The governing rule is to count only changes caused by the decision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
