## 一.[[净现值决策|净现值法]]([[净现值决策|Net Present Value]] Method)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Net Present Value Method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)净现值(Net Present Value)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Net Present Value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1.含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Meaning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净现值是指特定项目未来现金流入的现值与未来现金流出的现值之间的差额.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net present value is the present value of a project's future cash inflows minus the present value of its future cash outflows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2. 计算公式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Formula*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
\text{净现值} = \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{I_k}{(1 + i)^k} - \sum_{k=0}^{n} \frac{O_k}{(1 + i)^k}
$$

折旧本身不是现金流入；只有在税法允许税前扣除且该扣除能被利用时，它才通过少缴税形成现金税盾。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Depreciation itself is not a cash inflow. It creates a cash tax shield only when tax law permits the deduction and the firm can use it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3.决策原则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Decision Rule*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当净现值大于0,投资项目可行.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Accept an independent project when its NPV is greater than zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 4. 特点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Characteristics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净现值法具有广泛的适用性，反映项目创造的绝对价值。在没有硬资本约束的互斥选择中，投资规模不同不是放弃 NPV 排序的理由；若存在硬资本限额，单个项目的 NPV 不能单独解出最优组合，需要在约束下最大化组合总 NPV。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NPV is widely applicable and reports the absolute value a project creates. For mutually exclusive alternatives without a binding capital constraint, differences in project scale are not a reason to abandon NPV ranking. Under a hard capital constraint, however, individual-project NPV does not by itself identify the value-maximising feasible portfolio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)现值指数([[资本限额下的项目组合|Profitability Index]])
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Profitability Index*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1.含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Meaning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现值指数是未来现金流入现值与现金流出现值的比率。它把项目价值换成单位投入的比率，在单期资本限额、项目可分割、只有一种稀缺投入且没有依赖或互斥时可帮助分配资本；它不能替代互斥项目或不可分项目组合的总 NPV 最大化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The profitability index is the present value of future cash inflows divided by the present value of cash outflows. It can guide allocation only under narrow conditions: a one-period capital constraint, divisible projects, one homogeneous scarce input, and no dependencies or mutual exclusion. It does not replace maximising total NPV for mutually exclusive or indivisible projects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2. 计算公式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Formula*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现值指数=未来现金流入的现值/未来现金流出的现值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Profitability index = present value of future cash inflows / present value of future cash outflows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3. 决策原则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Decision Rule*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对独立项目，$PI>1$ 时接受，$PI<1$ 时拒绝；$PI=1$ 时与不投资在财务价值上无差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an independent project, accept when $PI>1$ and reject when $PI<1$; at $PI=1$, the project is financially indifferent to not investing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 4. 特点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Characteristics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现值指数对投资规模做了比率化，但没有消除项目期限差异，也不保证互斥或不可分项目的正确排序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The index normalises for investment size but does not eliminate differences in project duration or guarantee the correct ranking of mutually exclusive or indivisible projects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 一.投资项目现金流量的构成

### (一)现金流量的含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Meaning of Cash Flow*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所谓现金流量， 在投资决策中是指一个项目引起的公司现金支出和现金收入增加的数量。 这里的“现金”是广义的现金， 它不仅包括各种货币资金， 而且包括项目需要投入公司拥有的非货币资源的变现价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In capital budgeting, project cash flow means the incremental cash payments and receipts caused by accepting the project. “Cash” is interpreted broadly: it includes monetary funds and the realisable value of non-cash resources already owned by the company but committed to the project.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不用管沉没成本.决策所引起的才算,别的不算.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ignore sunk costs. Count only cash flows that change because of the decision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 相关成本与非相关成本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Relevant and Irrelevant Costs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相关成本是指与特定决策有关的、 在分析评价时必须加以考虑的成本。 例如， 差额成本 未来成本、 重置成本、 机会成本等都是属于相关成本。 与此相反， 与特定决策无关的，在分析评价时不必加以考虑的成本是非相关成本。 例如， [[项目增量现金流|沉没成本]]、 过去成本等
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Relevant costs change with the decision and must be included, such as differential future costs, replacement costs, and opportunity costs. Irrelevant costs do not differ between accepting and rejecting the project; sunk and other past costs are examples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果厂房本来能出租但是现在为了项目不出租,这就算是相关成本

只看由于决策所引起的才算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a building could otherwise be rented out but is used by the project, the forgone rent is a relevant opportunity cost. The governing rule is to count only changes caused by the decision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
