# 债券定价与收益率：从合同现金流读到市场价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bond pricing and yields: from contractual cash flows to market prices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先不要找“债券公式”。先问买入后究竟在什么日期、按什么条件收到多少钱，再问今天应付多少。这条阅读路径沿用一只两年期债券，把票面利率、贴现率、报价、实现收益和信用风险逐层分开。下面的教学算例不计税费。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with what the buyer receives, on which dates and under which conditions—not with a bond formula. Follow one two-year bond to separate its coupon rate, discount rate, quoted price, realized return, and credit risk. The teaching examples exclude taxes and transaction costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 先画支付时间线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Start with the payment timeline*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设债券面值 100，年票面利率 5%，每年付息一次，两年后按面值还本；今天正好是付息后。暂时假定不会违约，也没有提前赎回或转股权。[[债券面值|面值]]决定计息和还本的合同基数，[[债券票面利率|票面利率]]决定每年息票为 5；它们都不直接告诉你市场价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take a bond with face value 100, a 5% annual coupon, annual payments, and redemption at par in two years. Today is just after a coupon payment. Assume no default or embedded options. [[债券面值|Face value]] sets the contractual base for interest and principal; the [[债券票面利率|coupon rate]] gives an annual coupon of 5. Neither directly determines the market price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 距今天 / Years from today | 息票 / Coupon | 还本 / Principal | 合计 / Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 5 | 0 | 5 |
| 2 | 5 | 100 | 105 |

这是一只[[固定息票债券]]。最后一期的 105 不是“105 的本金”，而是息票与本金同日支付。换成[[零息债券]]，前面的息票消失，只有到期约定支付；“零息”并不意味着投资者没有收益，更不保证任何市场利率下都折价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a [[固定息票债券|fixed-coupon bond]]. The final 105 combines coupon and principal; it is not principal of 105. A [[零息债券|zero-coupon bond]] removes interim coupons and leaves one promised maturity payment. Zero coupon does not mean zero investment return or a discount price under every interest-rate environment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 票面利率管支付，市场贴现率管现值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. The coupon sets payments; market discount rates set present values*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用[[现金流同点折现加总]]的规则，把 5 和 105 分别带回今天。如果市场给出一年期、两年期[[即期利率]] $s_1,s_2$（均按有效年率），[[固定现金流债券定价|这只无违约债券的价格]]是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply [[现金流同点折现加总|cash-flow additivity at one valuation date]] to the payments 5 and 105. With one- and two-year [[即期利率|spot rates]] $s_1,s_2$, both effective annual rates, [[固定现金流债券定价|the default-free bond price]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_0=\frac{5}{1+s_1}+\frac{105}{(1+s_2)^2}.$$

为了把第一遍计算做清楚，暂取两期限即期率都为 6%。于是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the first calculation, set both spot rates to 6%. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_0=\frac5{1.06}+\frac{105}{1.06^2}=98.1666.$$

合同仍每年付 5；市场要求的回报高于这只债券的票面支付水平，购买价就低于 100。这正是[[票面利率与贴现率]]的分工。一般曲线不平时，应保留[[期限匹配贴现因子|各期限的贴现因子]]，不能因为课本例题只给一个率，就把整条曲线当成水平线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The contract still pays 5 per year. A higher required return than the coupon level is accommodated by a price below 100. That is the distinction between [[票面利率与贴现率|coupon and discount rates]]. With a non-flat curve, retain [[期限匹配贴现因子|maturity-matched discount factors]]; a one-rate exercise does not make the market curve flat.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在倒过来：如果已知市场全价 98.1666，求使两笔承诺支付的现值等于这个价格的单一率，就得到[[到期收益率]] 6%。它是把一只债券的价格压缩成一个内部收益率，不是重新观察到了每一期即期率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reverse the calculation: given a full market price of 98.1666, solve for the single rate discounting the promised payments to that price. The [[到期收益率|yield to maturity]] is 6%. This compresses one bond's price into an internal rate of return; it does not reveal every spot rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 半年付息时，先确认给的是哪一种年率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. For semiannual coupons, identify the annual-rate convention first*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每年付息 $m$ 次时，每期息票为 $Fc/m$，剩余规则期间数为 $N=mT$。[[债券付息周期换算]]有两个不同入口：按 $m$ 次复利报价的名义年收益率 $y^{(m)}$，每期率是 $y^{(m)}/m$；若给的是[[有效年利率]] $r$，每期率则是 $(1+r)^{1/m}-1$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $m$ payments per year, each coupon is $Fc/m$ and there are $N=mT$ regular periods. [[债券付息周期换算|Period conversion]] has two distinct inputs: a nominal annual yield compounded $m$ times gives a period rate $y^{(m)}/m$; an [[有效年利率|effective annual rate]] $r$ gives $(1+r)^{1/m}-1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，面值 1000、年票息 8%、半年付息，若名义年收益率为 8%，每期息票 40、每期率 4%，在付息后按面值估值。若题目给的是有效年收益率，必须给到 $1.04^2-1=8.16\%$ 才是同一个 4% 半年率。CPA 教材这一节正采用有效年率，不能不看口径直接除以二。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For face value 1000 and an 8% annual coupon paid semiannually, a nominal annual yield of 8% gives coupons of 40 and a 4% half-year rate, hence a par price just after payment. The equivalent effective annual rate is $1.04^2-1=8.16\%$. The CPA section uses effective annual rates; dividing its rate by two changes the convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，“改成更频繁付息会涨价还是跌价”要先说明什么保持不变。[[付息频率估值比较]]分别固定名义年收益率和有效年收益率，给出可复算的对照；这是两种不同实验，不是一句折溢价口诀的例外补丁。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether more frequent coupons raise or lower value depends on what is held fixed. [[付息频率估值比较|The coupon-frequency comparison]] contrasts a fixed nominal annual yield with a fixed effective annual yield. These are different experiments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 价格变化时，别把报价规则当成投资损益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Separate quotation conventions from investment gains and losses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[债券价格收益率反向|固定支付下，单一收益率上升会压低现值]]；[[债券折溢价判别|票息与同周期收益率的比较]]进一步判断付息后价格在面值哪一侧。后一个结论需要规则息票、按面值偿还和一致的期间口径，不能拿一个含应计利息的屏幕价格直接套用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[债券价格收益率反向|For fixed payments, increasing a single yield lowers present value]]. [[债券折溢价判别|Comparing the coupon with the same-period yield]] then identifies which side of par a post-coupon price lies on. This requires regular coupons, redemption at par, and consistent periods—not an arbitrary price containing accrued interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[债券净价]]不含[[债券应计利息]]；在普通名义债券的相同报价单位下，[[债券全价]]=净价+应计利息。全价对应剩余现金流的估值，净价把期间利息调整拆开报价。应计怎么数天、交易是否已进入除息期，须查实际合同和市场规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[债券净价|clean price]] excludes [[债券应计利息|accrued interest]]. For an ordinary nominal bond in matching quotation units, the [[债券全价|dirty price]] equals clean price plus accrued interest. The dirty price values remaining payments; the clean quote separates the interest adjustment. Day counts and ex-dividend entitlement depend on the contract and market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

继续第一只债券。若 6% 收益率不变，第一年付息后只剩下一年后的 105，价格为 $105/1.06=99.0566$。你已经另外收到 5，不能把收到的息票又留在债券价格里。[[债券拉回面值]]描述的是明确时点下的价格序列；最后支付前全价趋向 105，息票和本金全部付清后的债券价值为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the original bond at an unchanged 6% yield, the price just after the first annual coupon is $105/1.06=99.0566$. The coupon of 5 has been received separately and must not remain inside the bond price. [[债券拉回面值|Pull to par]] concerns a specified sequence of valuation dates: just before the final payment the dirty price tends to 105, whereas the bond is worth zero once all payments are settled.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 报价收益率不替你实现收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. A quoted yield does not realize the return for you*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若你一年后收完息票即卖出，实际一年回报是 $(5+P_1-P_0)/P_0$。在上面的恒定收益率情景中，它恰好为 6%；如果届时市场收益率改变，卖出价随之改变，这个结果便不再有保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Selling immediately after the first coupon gives a one-year return $(5+P_1-P_0)/P_0$. It equals 6% in the unchanged-yield example. A different market yield changes the sale price, so that return is not guaranteed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若持有两年到期，第一年收到的 5 再投资一年，利率记为 $j$，到期总财富为 $105+5(1+j)$，实现复合年收益率为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the bond is held to maturity and the first coupon is reinvested for one year at rate $j$, terminal wealth is $105+5(1+j)$. The realized compound annual return is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$r_{\mathrm{realized}}=\left(\frac{105+5(1+j)}{98.1666073}\right)^{1/2}-1.$$

当 $j=6\%$ 时得到 6%；若息票只以零利率保存，到期总额为 110，得到约 5.856%。两种情况下，买入时的 YTM 都是 6%。[[到期收益率与实现收益]]因此单独处理再投资、提前出售和履约条件；求 IRR 本身并不需要你先实施再投资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $j=6\%$, the result is 6%; with zero reinvestment interest, terminal wealth is 110 and the compound return is about 5.856%. The initial YTM is 6% in both cases. [[到期收益率与实现收益|YTM versus realized return]] separates reinvestment, early sale, and payment performance; calculating an IRR does not require reinvestment to have occurred.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证券投资学 PDF 的“债券收益率”例题另有把总收益直接除以年数的单利年化口径。它可以作为指定口径的答案，但不是一般多期现金流的 YTM，也不是上面的复合年增长率。先写清“算哪一种收益”，再使用对应公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Securities Investment PDF also uses a simple annualization that divides total return by years held. That can answer a question under its stated convention, but it is neither the general YTM of multi-period payments nor the compound annual growth rate above. Identify the return measure before calculating it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 加入违约后，承诺支付和实际支付分开
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. With default, distinguish promised payments from actual payments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券收益率比选定基准高多少，是[[信用利差]]的报价问题。它不等于“同样多的违约概率”：要先匹配期限、币种和计息约定，还要区分回收、坏状态风险补偿、流动性及合同条款。[[利率风险结构]]负责这些横向差异，[[信用利差与违约概率]]专门解释为什么只观察一个利差不足以反推出历史 PD。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The yield difference from a specified benchmark is a [[信用利差|credit-spread quotation]]. It is not a default probability of the same numerical size. Match maturity, currency, and compounding, then distinguish recovery, priced bad-state risk, liquidity, and contractual terms. [[利率风险结构|The risk structure of interest rates]] organizes these differences; [[信用利差与违约概率|Credit spreads versus default probabilities]] explains why a spread alone does not identify historical PD.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把复杂性暂时压到最小：一年后承诺付 100，违约时同日在面值上回收 40%，无风险有效年率为 3%，风险中性违约概率为 2%。在[[单期信用债定价|这一特定到期回收模型]]里，价格为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a minimal model, promise 100 in one year, recover 40% of face at that same date upon default, set the risk-free effective annual rate to 3%, and use a risk-neutral default probability of 2%. In [[单期信用债定价|this recovery-at-maturity model]], price is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P=\frac{100[1-0.02(1-0.4)]}{1.03}=95.9223.$$

这不是把历史 2% 违约率随手塞进无风险贴现。风险中性概率已经包含定价所需的风险调整；历史风险预测与市场估值的测度分工，和共享的 [[Merton违约模型]]、[[CreditMetrics估值]] 中的区分一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is not a license to insert a historical 2% default rate into risk-free discounting. Risk-neutral probabilities already incorporate the risk adjustment needed for valuation. The separation between historical risk forecasting and market valuation is consistent with the shared [[Merton违约模型|Merton model]] and [[CreditMetrics估值|CreditMetrics valuation]] objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按承诺 100 反解的有效年收益率约为 4.251%，与 3% 相差约 125.10 bp。若改用连续复利年利差，则是 $-\ln(1-0.012)=1.2073\%$，即约 120.73 bp。[[信用利差小损失近似]]说明何时可写成约 $q^{\mathbb Q}\mathrm{LGD}/T$；期间、年化和复利口径都不能省掉。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solving against the promised 100 gives an effective annual yield of about 4.251%, a spread of 125.10 bp over 3%. The continuously compounded annual spread is instead $-\ln(1-0.012)=1.2073\%$, or 120.73 bp. [[信用利差小损失近似|The small-loss spread approximation]] states when $q^{\mathbb Q}\mathrm{LGD}/T$ is appropriate. Horizon, annualization, and compounding cannot be omitted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 有选择权的债券要画支付分支
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Embedded options require branching cash flows*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[可赎回债券]]把提前偿还的选择权给发行人；[[可回售债券]]把按合同要求提前买回的权利给持有人；[[可转换债券]]则允许持有人在约定条件下用债权换股权。先问谁可以选择、何时选择、行权后哪项权利终止，才知道应比较什么现金流。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[可赎回债券|callable bond]] gives early-redemption discretion to the issuer; a [[可回售债券|putable bond]] gives the holder a contractual early-sale right; a [[可转换债券|convertible bond]] permits an exchange of debt for equity under specified conditions. Identify who chooses, when, and which right ends on exercise before comparing cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在其余合同与风险相同的比较下，[[赎回权的价值效应|发行人赎回权不会提高持有人价值]]，[[回售权的价值效应|持有人回售权不会降低价值]]；[[转股权的价值效应|自愿转股选择也不会降低原债权选择的价值]]。选择权有正价值时，前两项才分别严格减值、增值。但实际可转债往往还含赎回、提前转换、稀释和信用联动，不能不看条款就套一个独立股票期权价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding other terms and risks fixed, an [[赎回权的价值效应|issuer call cannot increase holder value]], a [[回售权的价值效应|holder put cannot reduce value]], and a [[转股权的价值效应|voluntary conversion choice cannot reduce the value of the retained debt alternative]]. The first two effects are strict only when the respective option has positive value. Actual convertibles can combine calls, early conversion, dilution, and credit interactions; an independent stock-option price cannot replace contract analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，两只债券的普通 YTM 相差多少，未必是在比较信用好坏；它们可能根本不会沿同一条支付路径走到到期。[[嵌入期权的收益率比较边界]]把这一点与信用利差接起来；进入 [[债券CDS基差]] 时还须进一步锁定 ASW、Z-spread 等具体利差口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A difference in ordinary YTM need not compare credit quality: the two bonds may follow different payment paths before maturity. [[嵌入期权的收益率比较边界|The yield-comparison boundary for embedded options]] connects this issue to credit spreads. [[债券CDS基差|Bond–CDS basis analysis]] additionally requires a fixed spread convention, such as ASW or Z-spread.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
