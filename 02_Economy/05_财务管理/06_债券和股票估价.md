债券发行人为筹集资金依照法定程序发行的、 在约定时间支付利息， 到期偿还本金的一种有价证券。

### (三)债券票面利率(Bond Coupon Rate)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Bond Coupon Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是指债券发行者预计一年内向投资者支付的利息占票面金额的比率。 是指在债券上标识的利率， 在数额上等于债券每年应付给债券持有人的利息总额与债券总面值相除的百分比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coupon rate is the stated annual interest payment divided by face value.

5. 保护性条款： 就是为保障债券持有人能得到还本付息的保障
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. An income bond pays its coupon only when the issuer's earnings are sufficient.
2. A convertible bond may be exchanged for the issuer's ordinary shares under specified terms.
3. A sinking fund is an account administered to accumulate resources for repayment.
4. A call provision lets the issuer repurchase some or all of the bonds at a predetermined price during a specified period; from the issuer's perspective it is an embedded option, while investor protection depends on the exact terms.
5. Protective covenants restrict issuer behaviour to improve the likelihood of timely interest and principal payments.

#### 1. 债券估价的基本模型(Basic Model of Bond Valuation)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basic Bond Valuation Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$ PV = \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{I_t}{(1 + i)^t} + \frac{M}{(1 + i)^n} $$

#### 2. 平息债券的估价(Valuation of Coupon Bonds)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Valuation of Coupon Bonds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

就是发放股利不是按年发的,是按照别的时间尺度发的,这个时候需要使用给的票面利率除以每年发几次得到每一期的利率.然后计算.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When coupons are paid more than once a year, divide the annual coupon amount and quoted annual yield consistently by the number of payment periods and discount each periodic cash flow. The source formula should be read with the time index measured in coupon periods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$ PV = \sum_{t=1}^{mn} \frac{I / m}{(1 + i / m)^{tm}} + \frac{M}{(1 + i / m)^{mn}} $$

#### 3.永久债券(Permanent Bonds)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Perpetual Bonds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有到期日,永不停止定期支付利息的债券.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A perpetual bond has no maturity date and continues to make periodic interest payments indefinitely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$PV=\frac{利息额}{折现率}$$

#### 4. 流通债券(Traded Bonds)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Traded Bonds between Coupon Dates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

已发行并在二级市场上流通的债券.到期时间小于债券发行在外的时间,估价的时间点可以是任何时间,存在“非整数计息期”的问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A traded bond has already been issued and now changes hands in the secondary market. Valuation can occur between coupon dates, so accrued interest and fractional coupon periods must be handled explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)债券价值的影响因素(Factors Affecting Bond Value)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Factors Affecting Bond Value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1. 面值： 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Face Value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面值越大， 债券价值越大（ 同向） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding all proportional terms fixed, a larger face value produces a proportionally larger bond value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2. 票面利率：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Coupon Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

票面利率越大， 债券价值越大（ 同向） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding the required yield fixed, a higher coupon rate raises the bond's value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3. 折现率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Discount Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

折现率越大， 债券价值越小（ 反向） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A higher required discount rate lowers the present value of the promised cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
#### 4. 到期时间 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Time to Maturity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当折现率一直保持至到期日不变时， 随着到期时间的缩短， 债券价值逐渐接近其票面价值。 如果付息期无限小则债券价值表现为一条直线。

1. 平息债券：
	①付息期无限小（ 不考虑付息期间变化）
	溢价： 随着到期时间的缩短， 债券价值逐渐下降
	平价： 随着到期时间的缩短， 债券价值不变
	折价： 随着到期时间的缩短， 债券价值逐渐上升
		债券价值随到期时间缩短向面值收敛：溢价债券价值下降、平价债券价值不变、折价债券价值上升（在折现率恒定时）。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the required yield remains constant, value converges toward face value as maturity approaches. A premium coupon bond declines toward par, a par bond remains at par, and a discount bond rises toward par. Between coupon dates, the quoted dirty price also reflects accrued interest and may follow a saw-tooth path around coupon payments. <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
#### 5. 利息支付频率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Coupon-Payment Frequency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于折价发行的债券， 加快付息频率， 价值下降；
对于溢价发行的债券， 加快付息频率， 价值上升；
对于平价发行的债券， 加快付息频率， 价值不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the note's convention, increasing payment frequency lowers the value of a discount bond, raises the value of a premium bond, and leaves a par bond unchanged. This comparison requires the quoted coupon and required-return conventions to be held consistently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三.债券的收益率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Bond Yield*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到期收益率是指以特定价格购买债券并持有至到期日所能获得的收益率。 它是使未来现金流量现值等于债券购入价格的折现率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Yield to maturity is the single discount rate that equates the present value of promised future cash flows to the bond's purchase price. It is a conditional return measure, not a guarantee of the realised holding-period return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)股票的有关概念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Shares*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股票是一种有价证券， 是股份公司在筹集资本时向出资人发行的、 用以证明出资人的股东身份和权利， 并根据持有人所持有的股份数享有权益和承担义务的凭证。 股票代表着其持有人（ 股东） 对股份公司的所有权
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A share is a security issued by a company to raise equity capital. It records the holder's status and rights as a shareholder, with rights and obligations determined by the number and class of shares held. It represents an ownership interest in the company.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按股票持有者可分为国家股、 法人股、 个人股三种；按股东权益和风险大小， 可以分为普通股、 优先股及两者的混合等多种； 按照认购股票投资者身份和上市地点不同，可以分为A股、 B股、 H股、 N股和S股等的区分； 按享受投票权益可分为单权、 多权及无权三种
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Shares may be classified by holder as state, legal-person, or individual shares; by claim and risk as ordinary, preference, or hybrid shares; by investor eligibility and listing venue as A, B, H, N, or S shares; and by voting rights as single-vote, multiple-vote, or non-voting shares.
