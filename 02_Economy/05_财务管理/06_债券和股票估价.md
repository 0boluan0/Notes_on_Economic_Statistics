## 一. 债券的有关概念(Relevant Concepts of Bonds)

*1. Relevant Bond Concepts*

### (一)债券(Bond)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Bond*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券发行人为筹集资金依照法定程序发行的、 在约定时间支付利息， 到期偿还本金的一种有价证券。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bond is a security issued through the legally prescribed process to raise funds, promising interest at specified times and repayment of principal at maturity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必须证监会审核批准发行,依照法定程序发行.不然就是非法集资.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The source note emphasises that issuance must follow the required regulatory approval and legal process; otherwise the fundraising may be unlawful.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start A01 -->
> [!note] 校注：定义与发行程序的范围
> 上述定义描述常见有到期日的附息债券；本节后面的零息、永续及特殊条款需要另读现金流条件。“必须证监会审核批准”不能作为所有债券的统一判据：[2023 年《公司债券发行与交易管理办法》](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c101953/c7438204/content.shtml)第 2–3、8、14 条区分适用范围、公开与非公开发行，并规定公开发行的审核与注册；审核、注册也不保证投资安全。这里保留课堂措辞，不据它判定任一具体融资是否合法。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The definition describes a common coupon bond with a maturity date; zero-coupon, perpetual, and special-feature instruments require their own cash-flow conditions. The [2023 Measures for Corporate Bond Issuance and Trading](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c101953/c7438204/content.shtml), Articles 2–3, 8, and 14, distinguish scope, public and non-public issuance, and public-offering review and registration. Approval or registration is not an investment guarantee. The classroom wording is retained, but it is not a universal test of whether a financing transaction is lawful.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

### (二)债券面值(Bond Face Value)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Bond Face Value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券发行人为筹集资金依照法定程序发行的、 在约定时间支付利息， 到期偿还本金的一种有价证券。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The source repeats the general bond definition here; face value is the stated principal used to determine promised repayment and, for a coupon bond, coupon payments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start A02 -->
> [!note] 校注：本段缺少的面值定义
> [[债券面值]]是合同记载的本金基数，不是市场成交价；普通到期按面值偿还的债券据此确定本金支付，息票也通常按面值与票面利率计算。原中文重复了债券定义，补义依据为 [[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=111|CPA 印刷 103 页 / PDF 111 页]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[债券面值|Face value]] is the contractual principal base, not the market transaction price. For a conventional bond redeemed at par, it determines principal repayment and usually the coupon calculation. The Chinese source repeats the bond definition; this clarification follows the CPA textbook, printed page 103 / PDF page 111.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

债券的计息方式是极为重要的.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The method used to calculate interest is therefore important.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (三)债券票面利率(Bond Coupon Rate)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Bond Coupon Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是指债券发行者预计一年内向投资者支付的利息占票面金额的比率。 是指在债券上标识的利率， 在数额上等于债券每年应付给债券持有人的利息总额与债券总面值相除的百分比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coupon rate is the stated annual interest payment divided by face value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start A03 -->
> [!note] 校注：票面承诺不等于预期实现
> 此处“预计”应按合同**承诺**理解。[[债券票面利率]]决定应付息票，不等于市场要求的贴现率，也不等于考虑违约后的预期实际收益；区别见 [[票面利率与贴现率]]。CPA 印刷 103 页明确用“承诺一年内支付”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Read “expected” here as **contractually promised**. The [[债券票面利率|coupon rate]] determines the coupon due, not the market discount rate or the expected realized return after allowing for default. See [[票面利率与贴现率|coupon versus discount rates]]. The CPA definition explicitly refers to promised annual interest.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

### (四)债券计息和付息方式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Interest Calculation and Payment Conventions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start A04 -->
> [!note] 补注：计息和付息是两个约定
> 计息说明利息按什么本金、利率和期间累积；付息说明现金何时实际支付。固定年票面利率为 $c$、面值为 $F$、每年规则付息 $m$ 次时，每次息票通常为 $Fc/m$；用于贴现的每期率仍须按给定年率的报价方式另行换算，见 [[债券付息周期换算]]。来源：[[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=113|CPA 印刷 105 页 / PDF 113 页]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Interest calculation specifies the principal, rate, and accrual period; payment specifies when cash changes hands. A fixed annual coupon rate $c$ on face value $F$, paid regularly $m$ times a year, normally gives $Fc/m$ per payment. Convert the discount rate separately under its annual-rate convention; see [[债券付息周期换算|coupon-period conversion]] and CPA printed page 105 / PDF page 113.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

### (五)债券的到期日,到期期限(Bond Maturity Date and Term)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Maturity Date and Time to Maturity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start A05 -->
> [!note] 补注：日期与期限
> 到期日是合同规定的最终偿还日期；剩余到期期限是**估值日到到期日**的时间间隔，不是发行以来已经过去的时间。二级市场估值还要逐项列出剩余付息日。来源：[[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=114|CPA 印刷 106–108 页 / PDF 114–116 页]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The maturity date is the contractual final repayment date. Remaining maturity runs **from the valuation date to that date**, not from issuance to today. Secondary-market valuation must also identify each remaining coupon date. See CPA printed pages 106–108 / PDF pages 114–116.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 4.优先债券与次级债券
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Senior and Subordinated Bonds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
1. 优先债券： 地位优先于其他债权人
2. 次级债券： 只有在其他债权人的债务得到清偿后才能获得清偿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Senior bonds rank ahead of other specified creditors.<br>
**2.** Subordinated bonds are repaid only after higher-ranking claims have been satisfied.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start A07 -->
> [!note] 校注：优先级是相对顺位
> “优先”是相对于指定的次级债权，不代表高于所有其他债权。偿付顺位还须看担保范围、合同从属安排及适用法律；这一点与 [[债务合约]] 的条款边界一致，不能仅凭名称推出实际回收额。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Seniority is relative to specified subordinated claims; it does not mean priority over every creditor. Recovery also depends on collateral coverage, contractual subordination, and applicable law, as explained under [[债务合约|debt contracts]]. The name alone does not determine the recovery amount.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

#### 5.  零息债券
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Zero-Coupon Bonds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
不支付任何利息的债券——零息债券.零息债券的发行价格远低于面值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero-coupon bond makes no periodic interest payments and is issued at a substantial discount to face value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start A08 -->
> [!note] 校注：零息不等于零收益
> [[零息债券]]没有中间息票，投资收益可以来自买价与到期支付额之差。对仅到期支付 $F>0$、剩余 $T>0$ 年的债券，$P=F/(1+y)^T$：正收益率才对应折价，$y=0$ 对应平价，$-1<y<0$ 对应溢价；“远低于面值”不是定义。来源：[[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=114|CPA 印刷 106 页的单笔现值式]]；不同符号的价格关系由该式直接推出。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A [[零息债券|zero-coupon bond]] has no interim coupons, but the difference between price and redemption can generate a return. For one payment $F>0$ in $T>0$ years, $P=F/(1+y)^T$: positive yields imply a discount, zero yield implies par, and $-1<y<0$ implies a premium. A deep discount is not part of the definition. These sign cases follow directly from the single-payment formula on CPA printed page 106.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

#### 6. 具有特殊条款的债券
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bonds with Special Provisions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 收益债券:是否支付票面利息， 要视公司的收益而定， 只有在公司收益足够高时， 债券持有人才会收到票面利息。
2. 可转换债券:债券持有人可以将债券转换为发行公司的普通股票。
3. 偿债基金： 为了偿付债券而由债券信托人管理的帐户。
4. 赎回条款： 允许公司在某一规定期间内以事先确定的价格购回或赎回部分或全部债券.是一种保护性条款.
5. 保护性条款： 就是为保障债券持有人能得到还本付息的保障
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** An income bond pays its coupon only when the issuer's earnings are sufficient.<br>
**2.** A convertible bond may be exchanged for the issuer's ordinary shares under specified terms.<br>
**3.** A sinking fund is an account administered to accumulate resources for repayment.<br>
**4.** A call provision lets the issuer repurchase some or all of the bonds at a predetermined price during a specified period; from the issuer's perspective it is an embedded option, while investor protection depends on the exact terms.<br>
**5.** Protective covenants restrict issuer behaviour to improve the likelihood of timely interest and principal payments.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start A09 -->
> [!note] 校注：谁持有选择权
> [[可转换债券]]的转股条件由合同规定；[[可赎回债券]]的提前赎回选择属于发行人，不能直接称为债券持有人的保护。其余条件相同时，[[赎回权的价值效应|发行人赎回权不会提高持有人价值]]；选择权价值为正时才严格降低。[[限制性条款|保护性契约条款]]可约束行为，但不保证本息必然兑现。来源：[[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=112|CPA 印刷 104 页的转换与赎回分类]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Conversion conditions for a [[可转换债券|convertible bond]] are contractual. The early-redemption option in a [[可赎回债券|callable bond]] belongs to the issuer, so it is not automatically holder protection. Other terms equal, [[赎回权的价值效应|an issuer call cannot increase holder value]]; the reduction is strict only when the option has positive value. [[限制性条款|Protective covenants]] constrain behaviour but cannot guarantee payment. See the conversion and call classifications on CPA printed page 104.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)债券的估价模型([[固定现金流债券定价|Bond Valuation Model]])
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Bond Valuation Models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1. 债券估价的基本模型(Basic Model of Bond Valuation)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basic Bond Valuation Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$ PV = \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{I_t}{(1 + i)^t} + \frac{M}{(1 + i)^n} $$

<!-- bond-source-note:start A10 -->
> [!note] 校注：基本式的时点和现金流口径
> 此式中 $I_t$ 是第 $t$ 期息票，$M$ 是到期本金，$i$ 是与一期长度一致的贴现率；估值日到首笔支付相隔完整一期。它直接计算剩余现金流的 [[债券全价|全价]]，不是任意结算日的屏幕净价。若期限曲线不平，应逐笔用 [[期限匹配贴现因子]]；若会违约，不能把全部承诺支付直接按无风险曲线定价。参见 [[固定现金流债券定价]]及 [[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=113|CPA 印刷 105 页基本模型]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $I_t$ is the coupon in period $t$, $M$ is principal at maturity, and $i$ matches the period length; the first payment is one full period after valuation. The expression values remaining cash flows at their [[债券全价|dirty price]], not an arbitrary settlement-date clean quote. Use [[期限匹配贴现因子|maturity-matched discount factors]] for a non-flat curve; risky promised payments cannot simply be discounted on a default-free curve. See [[固定现金流债券定价|fixed-cash-flow bond pricing]] and CPA printed page 105.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

#### 2. 平息债券的估价(Valuation of Coupon Bonds)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Valuation of Coupon Bonds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

就是发放股利不是按年发的,是按照别的时间尺度发的,这个时候需要使用给的票面利率除以每年发几次得到每一期的利率.然后计算.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When coupons are paid more than once a year, divide the annual coupon amount and quoted annual yield consistently by the number of payment periods and discount each periodic cash flow. The source formula should be read with the time index measured in coupon periods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$ PV = \sum_{t=1}^{mn} \frac{I / m}{(1 + i / m)^{tm}} + \frac{M}{(1 + i / m)^{mn}} $$

<!-- bond-source-note:start A11 -->
> [!warning] 校注：原式的指数与年率需要分别修正
> 平息债券支付的是息票而非股利，也包括一年付息一次。若 $n$ 为剩余年数、$m$ 为每年规则付息次数、$mn$ 为剩余完整期间的正整数、$I$ 为全年息票，令 $q>-1$ 为每期贴现率，则正确计期式为 $P=\sum_{t=1}^{mn}(I/m)/(1+q)^t+M/(1+q)^{mn}$；原求和指标已经按期计数，指数不能再写成 $tm$。按 $m$ 次复利报价的名义年率 $i$ 才有 $q=i/m$；[[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=113|CPA 印刷 105 页]]实际给的是**有效年率** $r_d$，故 $q=(1+r_d)^{1/m}-1$，也可直接用 $(1+r_d)^{t/m}$ 折现。例 3-17 的有效年率 10.25% 对应半年 5%，不是 5.125%；使用教材舍入后的现值系数得到 922.77 元。见 [[债券付息周期换算]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These are coupon payments, not dividends, and level-coupon bonds also include annual payers. With $n$ remaining years, $m$ regular payments per year, a positive integer $mn$ of complete periods remaining, annual coupon $I$, and period yield $q>-1$, use $P=\sum_{t=1}^{mn}(I/m)/(1+q)^t+M/(1+q)^{mn}$. The index already counts payment periods, so its exponent is $t$, not $tm$. Only a nominal annual yield quoted with $m$ conversions gives $q=i/m$. CPA printed page 105 instead specifies an **effective annual rate** $r_d$, requiring $q=(1+r_d)^{1/m}-1$, or discount factors $(1+r_d)^{-t/m}$. Example 3-17 converts 10.25% effective annually into 5% semiannually, not 5.125%; its rounded factors give 922.77. See [[债券付息周期换算|coupon-period conversion]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

#### 3.永久债券(Permanent Bonds)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Perpetual Bonds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有到期日,永不停止定期支付利息的债券.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A perpetual bond has no maturity date and continues to make periodic interest payments indefinitely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$PV=\frac{利息额}{折现率}$$

<!-- bond-source-note:start A12 -->
> [!note] 校注：永续公式的条件
> $P=C/i$ 是 [[固定永续年金]]：每期末永久支付同额 $C$，首笔在一期后，$i>0$ 且与付息周期一致。它不是所有现实永续债的通用价格；递延或取消票息、利率重设、赎回及违约等条款需要另外估值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $P=C/i$ is the [[固定永续年金|level perpetuity]] formula: a constant payment $C$ at each period-end forever, the first one a period away, with a matching rate $i>0$. It is not a universal price for perpetual securities with deferrable coupons, resets, calls, or default risk.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

#### 4. 流通债券(Traded Bonds)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Traded Bonds between Coupon Dates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

已发行并在二级市场上流通的债券.到期时间小于债券发行在外的时间,估价的时间点可以是任何时间,存在“非整数计息期”的问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A traded bond has already been issued and now changes hands in the secondary market. Valuation can occur between coupon dates, so accrued interest and fractional coupon periods must be handled explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start A13 -->
> [!note] 校注：剩余期限与结算价格
> [[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=114|CPA 印刷 106 页]]也使用“发行在外时间”的表述；应区分**原始总期限**与已经流通的时间。剩余期限短于原始期限，但不一定短于已流通时间。非付息日要按剩余支付日折现得到 [[债券全价]]，再依规则扣除 [[债券应计利息]] 得到 [[债券净价]]；教材例 3-20 的三笔剩余支付在 $1/12,13/12,25/12$ 年后，不能漏掉最近的息票。[英格兰银行 WP24，§2.1 脚注 5](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/-/media/boe/files/working-paper/1994/estimating-the-term-structure-of-interest-rates.pdf#page=9)明确区分这一全价和净价。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> CPA printed page 106 uses the same “time outstanding” wording. Distinguish **original term** from elapsed time: remaining maturity is shorter than the original term, but need not be shorter than elapsed time. Between coupon dates, discount the remaining entitled payments to obtain the [[债券全价|dirty price]], then subtract the contractual [[债券应计利息|accrued-interest adjustment]] to obtain the [[债券净价|clean price]]. Example 3-20 has payments at $1/12,13/12,25/12$ years, including the imminent coupon. Bank of England WP24, §2.1 note 5, distinguishes the two prices.

### (二)债券价值的影响因素(Factors Affecting Bond Value)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Factors Affecting Bond Value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1. 面值： 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Face Value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面值越大， 债券价值越大（ 同向） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding all proportional terms fixed, a larger face value produces a proportionally larger bond value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start A14 -->
> [!note] 校注：只改变名义本金规模
> 这里同时固定票面利率、付款日期、信用和贴现条件，让所有现金流随面值同比例放大；[[固定现金流债券定价|现值的线性加总]]才给出价格同比例增加。不能把不同发行人或不同条款债券的面值大小直接作为价值排序。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Hold the coupon rate, payment dates, credit, and discounting conditions fixed so that all payments scale with face value. [[固定现金流债券定价|Linear present-value aggregation]] then scales price proportionally. Face value alone cannot rank bonds with different issuers or terms.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

#### 2. 票面利率：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Coupon Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

票面利率越大， 债券价值越大（ 同向） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding the required yield fixed, a higher coupon rate raises the bond's value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start A15 -->
> [!note] 校注：票息比较保持什么不变
> 固定面值、付款日、到期本金与贴现因子，仅增加各期非负息票，价格才随之上升。这不是“票息高所以投资更划算”；市场价格、信用及期权条件还需分别比较。计算职责见 [[固定现金流债券定价]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fix face value, payment dates, redemption, and discount factors, then increase the coupons: price increases. A higher coupon does not by itself make an investment more attractive; price, credit, and optionality still matter. See [[固定现金流债券定价|fixed-cash-flow bond pricing]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

#### 3. 折现率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Discount Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

折现率越大， 债券价值越小（ 反向） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A higher required discount rate lowers the present value of the promised cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:start A16 -->
> [!note] 校注：价格反向的固定条件
> [[债券价格收益率反向]]固定未来非负现金流（至少一笔未来支付为正），仅改变同一报价口径的收益率，并要求折现基数为正。若付息会浮动或赎回决策改变，现金流也会变化，不能直接套固定现金流结论。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[债券价格收益率反向|The inverse price–yield relation]] holds future nonnegative payments fixed, with at least one positive future payment, and changes only the yield under one convention with a positive discounting base. Floating coupons or changing call decisions alter the cash flows and require further analysis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

#### 4. 到期时间 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Time to Maturity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当折现率一直保持至到期日不变时， 随着到期时间的缩短， 债券价值逐渐接近其票面价值。 如果付息期无限小则债券价值表现为一条直线。

	平价： 随着到期时间的缩短， 债券价值不变
	折价： 随着到期时间的缩短， 债券价值逐渐上升
		债券价值随到期时间缩短向面值收敛：溢价债券价值下降、平价债券价值不变、折价债券价值上升（在折现率恒定时）。

If the required yield remains constant, value converges toward face value as maturity approaches. A premium coupon bond declines toward par, a par bond remains at par, and a discount bond rises toward par. Between coupon dates, the quoted dirty price also reflects accrued interest and may follow a saw-tooth path around coupon payments. A zero-coupon bond rises toward its maturity value as time passes, all else equal.

#### 5. 利息支付频率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Coupon-Payment Frequency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于折价发行的债券， 加快付息频率， 价值下降；
对于溢价发行的债券， 加快付息频率， 价值上升；
对于平价发行的债券， 加快付息频率， 价值不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the note's convention, increasing payment frequency lowers the value of a discount bond, raises the value of a premium bond, and leaves a par bond unchanged. This comparison requires the quoted coupon and required-return conventions to be held consistently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start A18 -->
> [!note] 校注：两种频率比较不是同一实验
> 原口诀以付息后估值、剩余完整规则期间为背景，固定面值、总期限、名义年票面利率和**正的名义年要求收益率**，并将后两者同步除以每年付息次数。若固定的反而是正的有效年要求收益率，增加付息频率将同额正的全年息票的一部分提前支付，现值会上升；如面值 1000、年票息 8%、5 年、有效年率 10%，由年付改半年付，现值从约 924.18 升至 931.59。[[付息频率估值比较]]区分这两种设定；[[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=116|CPA 印刷 108 页]]例 3-21 明确采用有效年率，票息 8% 半年付息与有效年贴现率 8.16% 才对应平价。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The original rule values bonds after a coupon with complete regular periods remaining. It fixes face value, maturity, the nominal annual coupon rate, and a **positive nominal annual required yield**, dividing both annual quotations by payment frequency. Holding a positive effective annual yield fixed is a different experiment: earlier portions of the same positive annual coupon raise present value. For face value 1000, annual coupon 8%, five years, and a 10% effective annual yield, changing annual to semiannual payments raises value from about 924.18 to 931.59. See [[付息频率估值比较|coupon-frequency valuation comparisons]]. CPA printed page 108, Example 3-21, uses effective annual rates: an 8% semiannual-pay coupon is at par with an 8.16% effective annual yield.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

## 三.债券的收益率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Bond Yield*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到期收益率是指以特定价格购买债券并持有至到期日所能获得的收益率。 它是使未来现金流量现值等于债券购入价格的折现率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Yield to maturity is the single discount rate that equates the present value of promised future cash flows to the bond's purchase price. It is a conditional return measure, not a guarantee of the realised holding-period return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start A19 -->
> [!note] 校注：承诺 IRR 与实际实现
> [[到期收益率]]按买入全价和剩余承诺支付求 IRR，计算本身不要求实际发生再投资。若把它解释为持有到期的**实现复合收益率**，则还要本息按约支付，并让中间息票以适当收益再投资；提前出售时还要计入卖价，税费也须另列。详见 [[到期收益率与实现收益]]。[[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=118|CPA 印刷 110–111 页]]支持承诺现金流方程；[英格兰银行的 YTM 说明](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/statistics/yield-curves/terminology-and-concepts)同样把它定义为折现息票与本金的单一率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[到期收益率|YTM]] solves an IRR equation using the purchase dirty price and remaining promised payments; the calculation itself does not require actual reinvestment. Interpreting it as a **realized compound return to maturity** additionally requires contractual payment and appropriate coupon reinvestment. An early sale introduces its sale price, and taxes and costs must be treated separately. See [[到期收益率与实现收益|YTM versus realized return]]. CPA printed pages 110–111 support the promised-payment equation, as does the Bank of England's definition of a single rate discounting coupons and principal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

### (一)股票的有关概念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Shares*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股票是一种有价证券， 是股份公司在筹集资本时向出资人发行的、 用以证明出资人的股东身份和权利， 并根据持有人所持有的股份数享有权益和承担义务的凭证。 股票代表着其持有人（ 股东） 对股份公司的所有权
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A share is a security issued by a company to raise equity capital. It records the holder's status and rights as a shareholder, with rights and obligations determined by the number and class of shares held. It represents an ownership interest in the company.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按股票持有者可分为国家股、 法人股、 个人股三种；按股东权益和风险大小， 可以分为普通股、 优先股及两者的混合等多种； 按照认购股票投资者身份和上市地点不同，可以分为A股、 B股、 H股、 N股和S股等的区分； 按享受投票权益可分为单权、 多权及无权三种
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Shares may be classified by holder as state, legal-person, or individual shares; by claim and risk as ordinary, preference, or hybrid shares; by investor eligibility and listing venue as A, B, H, N, or S shares; and by voting rights as single-vote, multiple-vote, or non-voting shares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)股票的价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Share Price*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股票本身没有价值， 但它可以当做商品出卖， 并且有一定的价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The source distinguishes the physical certificate from the financial claim: the certificate itself has no consumption value, but the ownership claim is tradable and therefore has a market price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic47-finance-note:start A01 -->
> [!note] 价值口径
> 股票代表的[[股权]]可以有经济价值。“凭证没有消费用途”和“股权没有价值”是两件事；上面的英文增加了纸质凭证这一解释性限定，不能把它当成原中文已经写明的前提。[[股票内在价值]]区分未来经济利益的现值、市场报价与账面金额。

The English passage adds a physical-certificate qualification absent from the Chinese sentence. ### (三)股利
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Dividends*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股利指股份公司按发行的股份分配给股东的利润。
股份公司通常在年终结算后， 将盈利的一部分作为股息按股额分配给股东。
股利的主要发放形式有现金股利、 股票股利
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A dividend is a distribution of company profit to shareholders. Companies commonly declare dividends after closing their annual accounts, and the main forms are cash and stock dividends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金股利是未分配利润变成现金发出去,股票股利是钱变股票,进股本和资本公积里面.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A cash dividend distributes cash from retained earnings. A stock dividend issues additional shares and reallocates amounts within equity accounts rather than paying cash out of the company.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic47-finance-note:start A02 -->
> [!note] 利润、现金和股份单位
> [[留存收益不是现金]]：留存收益属于权益，派现金还需要实际可用资金。股票股利是权益内部重分类，并非先把现金变成股票；资本公积转增股本又是不同的权益转项。估值中的 $D_t$ 指股东收到的现金股利，不能把新增股数直接当成这笔现金。机制与完整学习顺序见[[02_Economy/05_财务管理/03_股票与企业估值#1. 买到的是什么，哪些分配真的带来现金？|分配与估值入口]]；来源为CPA2023 PDF309、314的现金股利和股份机制，不沿用历史税收或交易日期规定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the most recent dividend be $D_0$, the next expected dividend be $D_1$, and the constant growth rate be $g$. Then:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$股票价值=\frac{D_1}{R_s-g}$$

<!-- topic47-finance-note:start A05 -->
> [!note] 增长公式的范围
> 正股利从下一期开始按恒定 $g$ 延续、匹配的股权必要报酬率恒定且 $R_s>g$，才可用这个稳定增长式。$D_1$ 是尚未收到的第一笔股利；仅当 $D_0$ 到 $D_1$ 也按同一率增长，才写 $D_1=D_0(1+g)$。条件和2元→2.24元→56元算例见[[戈登增长模型]]；增长分阶段时用[[多阶段股利估值]]，CPA2023 PDF120–121支持这些时点和切分。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The stable-growth formula requires positive dividends growing at constant $g$ from the next payment, a constant matched equity return, and $R_s>g$. The numerator is the first unpaid dividend. Write $D_1=D_0(1+g)$ only when that same growth rate links the last paid and next dividend. See the [[戈登增长模型|Gordon model]] for the 2→2.24→56 example, or [[多阶段股利估值|multistage valuation]] when growth changes. CPA2023 PDF120–121 supports the timing and cutoffs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三.股票的期望收益率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Expected Return on a Share*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)计算方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Calculation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

找到使未来的现金流入现值等于现金流出现值的那一点折现率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solve for the discount rate that makes the present value of expected future cash inflows equal to the current cash outflow. Under the constant-growth model, this corresponds to dividend yield plus expected growth when its assumptions hold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic47-finance-note:start A06 -->
> [!note] 隐含收益率与实现收益
> 这里反解的是给定现价和现金预测的模型利率。稳定增长下为[[戈登隐含收益率|下一期股利收益率加增长率]]；它与因风险而要求的报酬率职责不同，也不保证未来实现。把增长率解释为预期价差收益率，还要保留同一股利路径与未来定价条件；CPA2023 PDF122例3-26给出这套条件。现金或成交价发生变化后，应按[[持有期收益率]]重新计算实际回报。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This inversion gives a model return conditional on price and cash forecasts. Under stable growth it is [[戈登隐含收益率|forward dividend yield plus growth]], distinct from the risk-based required return and not a realised-return guarantee. Interpreting growth as the expected capital-gain yield also requires the same dividend path and future pricing assumptions, as in CPA2023 PDF122, example 3-26. Changed cash or transaction prices require recalculating actual [[持有期收益率|holding-period return]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
