### 2. 现值、利率与利率期限结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Present Value, Interest Rates, and the Term Structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

报表和比率说明企业现在怎样产生现金与融资；利率和现值把不同时点的现金流放到同一尺度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Statements and ratios show how a firm currently generates cash and obtains financing. Interest rates and present value place cash flows from different dates on a common scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[货币时间价值与贴现]]

货币时间价值先用单一贴现率比较不同日期的现金流；期限结构再允许每个期限有不同即期率，并由此推出远期率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The time value of money first compares dated cash flows with a single discount rate. The term structure then allows each maturity to have its own spot rate and derives forward rates from that curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[利率、即期远期与期限结构]]

有了无风险期限曲线，才能逐笔贴现债券现金流，并把到期收益率与信用利差分解为可解释的价格成分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the risk-free term structure is available, bond cash flows can be discounted one by one and yields and credit spreads can be separated into interpretable pricing components.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[债券定价、收益率与信用利差]]

### 3. 证券估值：债券与股票
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Security Valuation: Bonds and Equities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

掌握折现基准后，可把债券和股票未来现金流转成当前价值，并识别增长假设的影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the discounting benchmark is understood, future cash flows from bonds and equities can be converted into present values and the impact of growth assumptions can be identified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[债券定价、收益率与信用利差]]

债券先用较明确的合同现金流建立贴现和风险溢价基准；股票估值再面对剩余现金流、增长和终值都更不确定的情形。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bonds first establish discounting and risk-premium logic with relatively explicit contractual cash flows. Equity valuation then confronts residual cash flows, growth, and terminal value that are all more uncertain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[股票与企业价值评估]]

### 4. 资本预算与投资决策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Capital Budgeting and Investment Decisions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证券估值评价给定现金流；资本预算进一步决定企业是否应创造一组新的项目现金流。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Security valuation evaluates a given stream of cash flows. Capital budgeting goes further by deciding whether the firm should create a new stream of project cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
