## 一.企业组织形式(Forms of Business Organization)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Forms of Business Organization*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)个人独资企业(Sole Proprietorship)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Sole Proprietorship*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

融资不易,无限责任
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financing is difficult, and the owner bears unlimited liability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

业主对企业债务承担无责任
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The owner bears no liability for the enterprise's debts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)合伙企业(Partnership)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Partnership*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

融资不易,有限/无限责任
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financing is difficult; liability may be limited or unlimited.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

包括普通合伙企业和有限合伙企业
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Partnerships include both general and limited partnerships.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如律师事务所这种有较大的信息差的职业,必须要普通合伙,相当于起一个连坐的效果,倒逼律师事务所的人互相监督,以防止利用信息差挣钱的事情发生.
股权不能随意买卖.必须要其余的合伙人同意.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In professions with substantial information asymmetry, such as legal practice, a general partnership is required. Joint liability pushes the partners to monitor one another and discourages them from exploiting their informational advantage for private gain.
An ownership interest cannot be transferred freely; the other partners must consent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (三)公司制企业(Corporation)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Corporation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

融资容易,有限责任.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financing is easier, and shareholders enjoy limited liability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公司不会随创始人的去世而倒闭,能成百年老店.
双重课税企业所得税和个人所得税都要交.
但是有代理问题.总经理可能不干人事.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A corporation does not disappear when its founder dies, so it can endure for generations.
It is subject to double taxation: both corporate income tax and personal income tax must be paid.
However, the separation of ownership and control creates an agency problem: the general manager may not act in the owners' interests.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


## 二.财务管理的主要内容(Main Content of Financial Management)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*II. Main Areas of Financial Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

包括投资和筹资两个领域
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It covers two broad areas: investment and financing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

作为投资和筹资的主体.我们肯定想要投资收益最大化,筹资成本最小化.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because the firm both invests and raises funds, it seeks to maximize investment returns while minimizing financing costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)长期投资(Long-term Investment)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) [[资本预算与投资决策|Long-term Investment]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

投资主体是公司.投资的客体是经营性长期资产.长期投资的直接目的是获取经营活动所需的实物资源.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The company is the investor, and it invests in long-term operating assets. The immediate purpose is to obtain the physical resources required for operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分为直接投资和间接投资.  
看投资目的,不是看投资的东西.直接投资的目的是为了自己的经营性活动.个人或专业投资机构吧钱投到企业等待升值或分红是间接投资.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Long-term investment can be direct or indirect.
The distinction depends on the purpose of the investment, not merely on what is purchased. Direct investment supplies resources for the company's own operations. When an individual or professional investment institution puts money into a company in expectation of capital appreciation or dividends, that is indirect investment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)长期筹资(Long-term Financing)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Long-term Financing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公司筹资,筹到的是长期资本.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Corporate financing raises long-term capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本在这里指的是资金的长期的来源 .和会计学的概念有所不同.是==长期负债+所有者权利==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here, capital means long-term sources of funds, which differs from the accounting usage: ==long-term liabilities + owners' equity==.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在长期筹资中,要着重关注资本结构问题
资本结构指的是钱来自于股东和债务的比例.  股东的钱越多承担风险的能力越强.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Long-term financing requires particular attention to capital structure.
Capital structure is the mix of funds supplied by shareholders and by creditors. The more equity the company has, the greater its capacity to absorb risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要考虑股利分配问题.分红或利润资本化.分红对于中小股东非常重要.eg:茅台
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The firm must also decide whether to distribute profits as dividends or retain and capitalize them. Dividends are especially important to small and medium-sized shareholders; Moutai is one example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (三)营运资本管理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Working Capital Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==流动资产-流动负债==.流动资产大于流动负债才能活下去.不然就活不下去.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==Current assets - current liabilities==. A company needs current assets to exceed current liabilities in order to stay alive; otherwise, it cannot survive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一.财务管理的目标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Financial Management Objectives*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在质与量之间要平衡.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The objective must strike a balance between qualitative and quantitative considerations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)利润最大化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Profit Maximization*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 优点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Advantages*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最容易获得的指标.也是使用最多的指标.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Profit is the easiest metric to obtain and the one used most often.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 局限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Limitations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 没有考虑利润的获得时间
2. 没有考虑获取利润和所投资人资本额的关系.(只看绝对值,不看相对值.)
3. 没有考虑获取利润和承担风险的关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** It ignores when the profit is earned.<br>
**2.** It ignores the relationship between profit and the amount of capital invested, looking only at the absolute amount rather than a relative return.<br>
**3.** It ignores the relationship between profit and the risk borne to earn it.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)每股收益最大化(权益资本净利率最大化)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Maximizing Earnings per Share (or Return on Equity)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 优势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Advantages*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解决了利润的局限性中利润和股东投入的资本联系起来了.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It remedies one limitation of profit maximization by relating profit to the capital supplied by shareholders.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 局限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Limitations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利润的缺陷他也基本继承了.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It nevertheless inherits most of the shortcomings of profit maximization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 仍然没有考虑每股收益取得的时间
2. 仍然没有考虑每股收益的风险
3. 现实中每股股票投入资本差别很大
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** It still ignores when earnings per share are received.<br>
**2.** It still ignores the risk attached to those earnings.<br>
**3.** In practice, the capital invested per share can differ substantially across shares.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (三)股东财富最大化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Maximizing Shareholder Wealth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是最终的目标,因为公司是股东的公司.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the ultimate goal because the company belongs to its shareholders.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 优势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Advantages*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接入了市场,可以反应上面两种评价方式不能反映的东西.所有的东西都能从股价中直接反馈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It brings market pricing into the evaluation and can therefore capture dimensions missed by the previous two criteria. The share price provides a direct market signal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 局限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Limitations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必须上市.起一个定价的作用.不然没有锚定.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This measure requires the company to be listed and have a market price; otherwise, there is no valuation anchor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场也有劣势.财务报表的数字也是可以调的.不碰红线就行.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Markets have weaknesses too. Figures in financial statements can be managed as long as the firm stays within the legal red lines.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

注意这里有些地方也管它叫公司价值最大化,这是不能直接划等号的,因为==公司价值=股东权益+债务价值==如果债务价值不变,公司价值增加与股东权益价值增加具有相同意义。如果 股东投资资本和债务价值不变, 公司价值最大化与股东财富最大化具有相同的意义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some sources call this corporate value maximization, but the two cannot simply be equated because ==corporate value = equity value + debt value==. If debt value is unchanged, an increase in corporate value has the same meaning as an increase in equity value. If both shareholder-contributed capital and debt value are unchanged, maximizing corporate value and maximizing shareholder wealth are equivalent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### [[金融市场中的信息不对称与契约#道德风险与委托代理|委托-代理问题]]

#### 第二类代理成本

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)经营者的利益要求与协商
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Managers' Claims and Coordination*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 公司股东和公司经营者的矛盾.  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conflict Between Shareholders and Managers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是根本性的矛盾,不可能消解,只能缓和.
公司股东要求财富最大化.公司经营者要求报酬,闲暇时间,避免风险.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This conflict is fundamental: it cannot be eliminated, only mitigated. Shareholders seek to maximize wealth, while managers seek compensation and leisure and prefer to avoid risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 经营者利益和股东利益的协调
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Aligning Managers' and Shareholders' Interests*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 道德风险.不尽力,混.伤害不小
2. 逆向选择:经营者花股东的钱花爽了.伤害不大.
3. 杠杆赎买:把公司股票搞低,然后自己买.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Moral hazard: managers shirk or merely go through the motions, which can cause substantial harm.<br>
**2.** Adverse selection: managers enjoy spending shareholders' money; the note describes the resulting harm as relatively limited.<br>
**3.** Leveraged buyout: managers depress the company's share price and then buy it themselves.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 制度性措施
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Institutional Measures*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 监督.
2. 激励.最开始是发奖金.但是短期目标会影响长期目标.后面是股票.但是两方都不是很愿意.最后是发股权.股权是一种期权,只有权利没有义务.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Monitoring.<br>
**2.** Incentives. Bonuses came first, but short-term targets can undermine long-term objectives. Stock-based rewards came later, although neither side was especially willing; the final step was to award equity. The note treats equity as an option that confers rights without obligations.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)债权人的利益要求和协商
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Creditors' Claims and Coordination*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 债权人的要求
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Creditor Requirements*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券人吧钱借给公司,目的是到期收回本金,并获得约定的利息收入.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bondholders lend money to companies with the expectation of recovering the principal at maturity and earning agreed-upon interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 债权人和公司的矛盾
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Tensions Between Creditors and Companies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 股东不经债权人的同意,投资于比债权人预期风险更高的新项目。
2. 股东为了提高公司的利润,不征得债权人的同意而发行新债,致使旧债券的 价值下降, 使旧债权人蒙受损失。债券多了,欠的多了,违约风险增大,更容易违约.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Without creditors' consent, shareholders invest in new projects that are riskier than creditors expected.<br>
**2.** To increase the company's profit, shareholders issue new debt without creditors' consent. This reduces the value of existing bonds and harms existing creditors. More bonds mean more debt, a higher probability of default, and a greater chance that the firm will actually default.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 约束条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Protective Constraints*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

针对性操作.第一, 在借款合同中加入限制性条款,如规定借款的用途、规定不得发行新债或限 制发行新债的额度等.第二, 发现公司有损害其债权利益意图时,拒绝进一步合作,不再提供新的贷款或 提前收回贷款。同时按阶段发放贷款,不要一次性都发完
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use targeted protections. First, include restrictive covenants in the loan agreement, such as specifying how the funds may be used, prohibiting new debt, or limiting the amount of new debt. Second, if the company appears intent on harming creditors' interests, refuse further cooperation, stop extending new loans, or demand early repayment. Loans can also be disbursed in stages rather than all at once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一.金融工具的类型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Types of Financial Instruments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金融工具是资金融通过程中载明双方当事人权利义务的具有法律效力的凭证.如债券,股票,外汇,保单等.公司可以借助金融工具进行筹资和投资.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial instruments are legally enforceable claims that specify the rights and obligations of the parties to a financing transaction. Examples include bonds, shares, foreign exchange, and insurance policies. Companies use them both to raise funds and to invest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (一)固定收益证券
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Fixed-income Securities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可按现金流贴现等公式计算收益的证券（如国债等）。~~啥的~~
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Securities whose returns can be calculated with formulas such as discounted cash flow, including government bonds. ~~Something like that.~~
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

之前国家发过一种到保值贴补债券.是基准理论加上到期时CPI的利率,这个的真实利率虽然在变动,但是可以通过公式计算得出,所以也算是一种固定收益债券
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The government once issued value-maintenance subsidy bonds. Their return combined a benchmark rate with a CPI-linked adjustment at maturity. Although the real interest rate varied, it could still be calculated with a formula, so these were also treated as fixed-income bonds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)权益证券
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Equity Securities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指代表特定公司所有权的份额的证券,股票是其中的一种
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These securities represent an ownership interest in a particular company; shares of stock are one example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (三)衍生证券
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Derivative Securities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

传统的固定收益债券和权益证券等原生资产基础上衍生出的价值随原生资产价格波动而波动的合约.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are contracts derived from underlying assets such as conventional fixed-income and equity securities. Their value moves with the price of the underlying asset.

The distinction is based on maturity. Markets for short-term instruments with maturities of one year or less are money markets; markets for instruments with maturities longer than one year are capital markets.

## 六.资本市场效率

#重点 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Key Point*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### (一)资本市场效率的意义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) Why Capital Market Efficiency Matters*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 资本市场以最低交易成本为资金需求者提供金融资源的能力.
2. 资本市场的资金需求者使用金融资源向社会提供有效产出的能力.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** The ability of capital markets to supply financial resources to those who need funds at the lowest transaction cost.<br>
**2.** The ability of those fund seekers to use the financial resources to provide useful output to society.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相当于是本课的前提.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is effectively a premise of the course.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (二)[[有效市场假说与事件研究|有效资本市场]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) [[有效市场假说与事件研究|Efficient Capital Markets]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Meaning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指资本市场上的价格能够同步地完全地反映全部的可用信息.在有效资本市场中,价格会对新的信息作出迅速,充分的反映.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an efficient capital market, prices fully and contemporaneously reflect all available information. Prices respond rapidly and fully when new information arrives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 资本试产有效的外部标志
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*External Indicators of Market Efficiency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 证券有关信息能够充分披露和均匀分布,使每个投资者都能同体时间内得到等质等量的信息.
2. 价格能迅速根据有关信息变动,而不是没有反映或反映迟钝.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Information about securities is fully disclosed and distributed evenly, so every investor receives information of the same quality and quantity at the same time.<br>
**2.** Prices adjust rapidly to relevant information rather than failing to react or reacting only slowly.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 资本市场有效的基础条件(三者有一个就行)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Foundational Conditions for Market Efficiency (Any One Is Sufficient)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 理性的投资者
2. 独立的理性偏差
3. 套利
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Rational investors<br>
**2.** Independent deviations from rationality<br>
**3.** Arbitrage<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 对财务管理的意义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Significance for Financial Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 管理者不能通过改变会计方法提升股票价值(宁德时代)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Managers cannot increase share value merely by changing accounting methods, as illustrated here with CATL.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

宁德时代想要定向增发股份,定向增发必须发给机构,他要保证后面还有较大的增长空间,就先把业绩藏起来一部分.就大规模计提减值损失,将利润藏起来.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CATL wanted to conduct a private placement, which had to be placed with institutional investors. To preserve apparent room for later growth, it first hid part of its performance by recognizing large impairment losses and shifting profit out of the current period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

2. 管理者不能通过金融投机获利
3. 关注自己公司的股价是有益的.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** Managers cannot earn profits through financial speculation.<br>
**3.** It is useful for managers to monitor their own company's share price.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 市场有效性的理论研究
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Theoretical Research on Market Efficiency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本市场有有效性与公司财务管理的密切关系.
1. 公司通过资本市场建立代理关系
2. 股票市场可以检验公司财务目标的实现程度.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Capital market efficiency is closely related to corporate financial management.
**1.** Companies establish agency relationships through capital markets.<br>
**2.** The stock market evaluates the extent to which a company achieves its financial goals.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (三)资本市场效率的程度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Degrees of Capital Market Efficiency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信息分类为三类,从弱到强分别为:
1. 历史信息
2. 公开信息
3. 内部信息.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Information is divided into three categories, ordered from weak to strong:
**1.** Historical information<br>
**2.** Public information<br>
**3.** Inside information<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按照三类信息的披露程度.把资本市场分为三种有效程度.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Capital markets are classified into three degrees of efficiency according to how much of these three categories of information prices reflect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 弱势有效资本市场.只反映历史信息
2. 半强制有效资本市场.历史信息和公开信息
3. 强式有效资本市场,所有信息都公开 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** A weak-form efficient market reflects only historical information.<br>
**2.** A semi-strong-form efficient market reflects historical and public information.<br>
**3.** In a strong-form efficient market, all information is disclosed.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
