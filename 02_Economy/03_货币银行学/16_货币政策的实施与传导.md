# 货币政策的实施与传导
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Monetary-policy implementation and transmission*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

央行宣布一个利率决定，到家庭改变消费、企业改变投资，中间经过哪些环节？本章从央行实际能操作的账户和价格出发，沿着融资条件、支出与通胀往下读；随后解释预期为何使未来承诺影响今天，以及怎样辨认政策的因果效果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
What connects a central-bank rate announcement to household consumption and business investment? Start with the accounts and prices the central bank can operate, then follow financing conditions into spending and inflation. Expectations explain why future commitments matter today; identification explains how to distinguish causal policy effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 从最终目标到可以操作的变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From ultimate objectives to an operating variable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确定政策在做什么。价格稳定、就业等[[央行法定目标|最终目标]]依制度而定；央行不能像修改账户利率一样，直接把明天的通胀或就业改成目标值。它要先改变一些金融条件，让私人部门的选择发生变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First identify the policy's purpose. [[央行法定目标|Ultimate objectives]] depend on the institution. Inflation and employment cannot be set like an account's interest rate; the central bank changes financial conditions through which private choices respond.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[货币政策]]

“工具”“操作目标”“传导中的价格”应各就其位。以下先用美国联邦基金市场作具体制度例子，再比较两种准备金框架；其他央行的具体工具名和参与资格需要另行对应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separate instruments, operating targets and prices along the transmission chain. The U.S. federal funds market supplies a concrete example before comparing two reserve frameworks. Other central banks require their own mapping of instruments and eligibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[货币政策操作目标]]

## 2. 准备金市场和联邦基金利率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Reserve balances and the federal funds rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以央行准备金完成的银行间最终结算，要转移央行账户余额；公众在商业银行的存款与这个账户不是同一个对象。先认清被交易和结算的资产，再看其融资价格，才能读懂课堂的准备金供需图。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interbank final settlement in central-bank reserves transfers central-bank account balances, distinct from public deposits at commercial banks. Identify the settlement asset before its financing price to make sense of the reserve-demand diagram.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[央行准备金余额]]

![[联邦基金利率]]

看每日数据时，市场的一笔成交、一天的汇总统计和央行目标是三个层次。统计量落在目标区间内，也不代表每笔交易都落在里面。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A transaction price, a daily statistic and a policy target are different objects. A statistic inside the target range does not put every transaction there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[有效联邦基金利率]]

央行可以直接改变合格机构使用某项账户或便利的回报。这个选择会影响市场交易，但能否构成严格套利边界，要等下一节把准入和中介成本补齐。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The central bank can directly change the return on eligible accounts or facilities. That outside option influences trading; access and intermediation costs determine how tight its arbitrage bounds are.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[央行管理利率]]

## 3. 从公开市场交易到利率实施
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From market operations to rate implementation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先追踪交易怎样改变准备金，再判断数量变化对利率的意义。央行购券与央行贷款有不同资产、到期和风险安排，见[[央行购券与贷款]]；这里集中讨论市场交易的实施方式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trace how an operation changes reserves before interpreting the rate effect. [[央行购券与贷款|Purchases and lending]] differ in assets, maturity and risk arrangements; this section focuses on market operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公开市场操作]]

![[回购协议]]

回购的现金流可以从交易两端观察，操作名称却还受机构惯例影响。把“谁先付现金、谁先交证券”画清楚，才不会从 repo 或 reverse repo 一个词猜错准备金方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repo cash flows can be viewed from either side, while operation names also follow institutional conventions. Identify who initially pays cash and delivers securities before inferring the reserve direction from a name.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[央行回购方向]]

同样是增加准备金，起点位于需求曲线的哪一段会改变利率结果。先读稀缺准备金下的走廊，再看供给与平坦需求段相交的框架；两者也都可以调整管理利率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rate effect of more reserves depends on where supply intersects demand. Compare a scarce-reserve corridor with the near-flat demand region of an ample framework. Both can also adjust administered rates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[利率走廊]]

![[充足准备金框架]]

![[管理利率的市场边界]]

现在可以回到一条常见新闻：“央行投放资金”。若这笔操作只是抵销现金流出，就可能在维持原利率目标；若管理利率与未来路径改变，即使准备金数量不动，融资条件仍可能变化。数量、目标设置和金融条件要合读。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to the headline “the central bank injected funds.” An offset to currency outflows can maintain the same rate target. Changes in administered rates and the expected path can alter financing conditions without changing reserve quantity. Read quantities, targets and conditions together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[准备金数量与政策立场]]

课堂记号还须区分：在 $MB=C+R$ 的简化口径下，若借入准备金为 $BR$，则非借入准备金为 $R-BR$，非借入基础货币为 $MB-BR=C+R-BR$；两者差一个 $C$。实际美国统计对通货和库存现金的覆盖另见[[美国货币基础、M1 与 M2]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the teaching notation $MB=C+R$, nonborrowed reserves are $R-BR$, whereas the nonborrowed monetary base is $MB-BR=C+R-BR$. They differ by $C$. For the actual U.S. treatment of currency and vault cash, see [[美国货币基础、M1 与 M2|the monetary-base and broad-money definitions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 利率下限、资产购买与组合调整
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The lower bound, asset purchases and portfolio adjustment*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

短期利率的可用空间有限时，央行还可能改变较长期资产供给与未来政策预期。下限是有条件的约束，不是数字零的一条自然定律；资产购买的效果也要从具体交易和资产替代性推导。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When short-rate room is limited, policy may affect longer-term asset supply and expected future policy. The lower bound is conditional rather than a natural law at zero; purchase effects depend on transactions and asset substitutability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[有效利率下限]]

![[量化宽松]]

购券先产生一组资产负债表变化，再可能影响价格和支出。以下结算链区分向银行与向非银行卖方购买的情况；随后才讨论为什么有人愿意重新配置剩余资产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Purchases first change balance sheets and may then affect prices and spending. The settlement chain distinguishes bank from nonbank sellers; portfolio adjustment explains the subsequent willingness to hold the remaining assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[央行购债结算链]]

![[资产购买的组合再平衡]]

## 5. 从名义利率到支出选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From nominal rates to spending choices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

家庭关心未来可购买多少商品，企业关心项目收入是否覆盖机会成本。因此，先把同一期限的名义利率与预期通胀放到一起，再讨论支出；不能只看名义利率的升降。估值中的确定情景换算与随机通胀下的期望是不同运算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Households care about future purchasing power, and firms about returns relative to opportunity cost. Match nominal rates with inflation expectations over the same horizon before analysing spending. A deterministic valuation conversion is not the same operation as an expectation under uncertain inflation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[事前实际利率]]

![[利率渠道]]

隔夜利率也不是十年贷款的全部定价依据。长期价格取决于预期未来短率，并包含期限与其他风险补偿；因此今天加息，而市场同时下调今后几年利率预期时，长端未必同幅上升。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An overnight rate does not fully price a ten-year loan. Long rates depend on expected future short rates and term and other risk compensation. A hike today can coincide with lower expectations farther ahead, leaving the long end with a different response.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[未来短率预期]]

![[期限溢价]]

现有合同何时重定价，又决定现金流何时变化。先看合同，再看收入从谁转向谁及双方的消费反应，便能解释同一次政策为何对不同家庭产生不同影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Contract repricing determines when cash flows change. Trace the contract, then the income transfer and each party's spending response to explain heterogeneous effects from the same policy change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[合同重定价与政策传导]]

![[现金流再分配渠道]]

## 6. 银行、资产价值与融资约束
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Banks, asset values and financing constraints*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利率影响借款需求，银行自己的融资、利润和资本也会影响贷款供给。把两侧分开后，才能问“贷款下降”究竟由什么引起；银行自身资本与借款人净值又是不同的资产负债表。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rates affect credit demand, while banks' funding, profits and capital affect supply. Separate the sides before explaining falling lending, and distinguish bank capital from borrower net worth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[银行贷款渠道]]

![[金融加速器]]

资产价格连接融资条件与支出，但要先分清价格为什么变，再分清它改变了消费意愿还是融资能力。下面依次读估值、财富和抵押；同一冲击可能同时经过几条链，算例中的金额不能不加区分地相加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Asset prices connect financial conditions to spending. First explain valuation, then distinguish desired consumption from financing capacity. One shock can travel through several channels, so the illustrative amounts cannot simply be added.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[政策传导的估值渠道]]

## 7. 开放经济中的价格与需求
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Prices and demand in an open economy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从国内利率走到汇率，先要给定海外政策、预期和风险补偿，不能把加息固定翻译成升值。这里先条件化地给定汇率变化，分别追踪进口价格与购买者的支出切换；完整资产回报条件和制度选择见[[外汇市场、利率平价与不可能三角.canvas|外汇学习总图]]。

## 8. 反应规则、承诺与预期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Reaction rules, commitment and expectations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政策会对经济反应，公众也会预判这种反应。先看一个把经济状态映射到利率的参照规则，再问规则的反应强度；最后把私人先行决策放进去，考察今天宣布的未来计划为什么未必可信。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Policy responds to the economy, and the public anticipates that response. Begin with a benchmark rule mapping conditions into a rate, then examine its strength. Private decisions made in advance explain why an announced future plan may lack credibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[泰勒规则]]

![[泰勒原则]]

![[政策时间不一致]]

![[相机抉择的通胀偏差]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inflation-bias derivation turns on changed continuation incentives after private actions are fixed, not forgetfulness. Reuse the game-theory commitment object for mechanisms that alter those incentives; [[央行目标与工具独立性|policy independence]] and [[央行透明与问责|accountability]] address the institutional dimensions.

![[指引门槛与触发条件]]

未来政策路径与未来通胀预期互有关联，却不是同一种预期。读完指引，再看公众是否相信中长期物价会回到目标附近；供给冲击则将预期、时滞、真实收入和福利权衡放到一起。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Beliefs about future policy and future inflation are connected but different. After guidance, examine confidence in medium- and long-run price stability. Supply shocks bring expectations, lags, real income and welfare trade-offs together.

## 9. 从事后变化到因果判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From observed changes to causal inference*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知道传导方向，仍不等于看到事后数据就识别了政策效果。经济走弱可能同时导致降息和失业上升；公告中也可能既有政策意外，又有央行透露的经济判断。最后用两个对象分清反事实和消息混合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing a transmission mechanism does not identify its effect from subsequent data. Weak activity can induce both rate cuts and rising unemployment. Announcements can mix policy surprises with economic assessments. Separate the counterfactual problem from the mixture of news.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 10. 贯通应用：读懂一次利率决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An integrated application: interpreting a rate decision*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设同一天看到四条信息：央行把隔夜目标区间提高1个百分点；准备金总量未变；一个家庭的100单位浮息贷款从3%重定价到4%；长期收益率反而下降。仅凭这些信息，你能确定什么，还缺什么？所有数字都属于教学情景。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose an announcement raises the overnight target range by one percentage point, reserve quantity is unchanged, a household's floating loan of 100 reprices from 3 to 4 percent, and long yields fall. What follows, and what information is still missing? All figures are hypothetical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先解释实施：在充足准备金框架内，上调管理利率可以实施更高的市场利率目标，未必需要回收准备金。再解释合同：若本金全年不变、立即重定价且不含费用，利息支出增加1；固定合同没有同样的即时账单变化，却可能提前调整消费。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For implementation, higher administered rates can support a higher market target with ample reserves and no drainage. For the contract, a constant principal repriced immediately for a full year without fees raises interest expense by 1. A fixed contract lacks that immediate bill change but need not prevent anticipatory spending adjustments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

然后补上预期：事前实际利率需要同期限的预期通胀；长期收益率下降可能包含未来短率路径下调或期限溢价下降。最后才判断总支出和因果：需要知道其他家庭与银行的反应、政策相对市场原先预期的意外，以及经济为什么促使央行作出这个决定。一个家庭多付的1，不是整个经济消费下降1的结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next add expectations: the ex-ante real rate needs same-horizon inflation beliefs, while lower long yields can reflect a lower expected short-rate path or term premium. Aggregate spending and causality require responses elsewhere, the surprise relative to prior beliefs, and the reason for the decision. One household's extra interest payment is not an aggregate consumption loss of 1.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
