## 货币与金融体系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Currency and Financial System*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[货币、流动性与货币口径|货币、金融体系与中央银行]]。

- 第 3 章：货币与其他资产的区别、货币功能、支付体系演进、M1/M2 口径及剪刀差。
- 能解释金融市场和中介为何存在，以及中央银行与支付体系在其中的位置。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Chapter 3: How money differs from other assets, the functions of money, the evolution of payment systems, the definitions of M1 and M2, and the gap between their growth rates.
- Be able to explain why financial markets and intermediaries exist, and the place of central banks and payment systems within them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 利率与期限结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Interest rates and term structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[货币时间价值与贴现|现值、利率与利率期限结构]]。

- 第 4 章：现值、债券价格、到期收益率和普通股估值，计算是基础。
- 第 5 章：债券供求图、货币供求图、债券价格与利率反向变化。
- 第 6 章：违约风险、流动性、税收如何改变风险结构；预期理论、市场分割和流动性溢价如何解释收益率曲线。
- 图形题要同时写清横纵轴、移动的是供给还是需求，以及均衡价格和利率怎样变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Chapter 4: Present value, bond prices, yield to maturity, and ordinary-share valuation; the calculations are foundational.
- Chapter 5: Bond-supply and bond-demand diagrams, money-supply and money-demand diagrams, and the inverse relationship between bond prices and interest rates.
- Chapter 6: How default risk, liquidity, and taxation alter the risk structure of interest rates; how expectations theory, market segmentation, and the liquidity-premium theory explain the yield curve.
- For a diagram question, identify both axes, specify whether supply or demand shifts, and explain how the equilibrium price and interest rate change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 资产价格与信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Asset Prices and Information*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|资产定价、有效市场与行为金融]] 与 [[金融市场中的信息不对称与契约|信息不对称、金融结构与金融危机]]。

- 第 7 章：股利折现和 Gordon 增长模型，注意分子使用下一期股利；两阶段模型先求稳定期终值。
- 第 8 章：逆向选择、道德风险、委托—代理及解决机制，能根据例子区分。
- 第 12 章：金融危机的发展过程；比较大萧条与 2007–2009 年危机的原因、表现和救助。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Chapter 7: Dividend discount and Gordon growth model, note that the numerator uses the dividend of the next period; the two-stage model first finds the terminal value of the stable period.
- Chapter 8: Adverse selection, moral hazard, principal–agent problems, and the mechanisms used to mitigate them; be able to distinguish these concepts in concrete examples.
- Chapter 12: How financial crises unfold; compare the causes, symptoms, and policy responses of the Great Depression and the 2007–2009 crisis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 银行经营、监管与货币供给
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Banking operations, supervision and money supply*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[商业银行资产负债管理|商业银行经营、监管与货币供给]]。

- 第 9 章：T 账户、准备金外流的四种应对、流动性管理、资本金、利率缺口与久期。
- 第 10 章：too big to fail、Basel 资本率和盯市记账的基本逻辑。
- 第 14 章：公开市场操作、贴现贷款、准备金、简单存款乘数与货币乘数的边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Chapter 9: T-accounts, four responses to reserve outflows, liquidity management, bank capital, repricing gaps, and duration.
- Chapter 10: too big to fail, Basel capital requirements, and the basic logic of mark-to-market accounting.
- Chapter 14: open-market operations, discount lending, reserves, and the limits of the simple deposit multiplier and the money multiplier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 中央银行、政策与外汇
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Central Banking, Policy and Foreign Exchange*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[货币政策工具、传导与可信度|货币政策与外汇市场]]。

- 第 13、15 章：中央银行独立性、政策工具、操作目标与传导。
