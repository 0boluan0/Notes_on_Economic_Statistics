## 概念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Concepts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* [[Present Value|现值]]([[Present Value|Present Value]])：将来某一时刻将收到的现金流的今天价值
* 折现(Discounting)；计算未来现金流量的PV值的过程
* [[Discount Rate|贴现率]]([[Discount Rate|Discount Rate]])：用于计算未来现金流量PV的利率或回报率
	* 贴现因子([[Discount Rate|Discount]] [[Factor Analysis|Factor]])：1/(1+r) 未来的一美元对应现在的价格
		通俗来讲就是往前推就乘贴现因子。
		如果是多年期就乘方：
		$$
		DF_n=\frac{1}{(1+r)^n}.
		$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* [[Present Value]]: the value today of a cash flow that will be received at a future date.
* Discounting: the process of calculating the present value of a future cash flow.
* [[Discount Rate]]: the interest rate or required rate of return used to calculate a future cash flow's present value.
	* Discount factor: $1/(1+r)$, the present price of one dollar received in the future.
		In plain language, moving a cash flow backwards in time means multiplying it by the discount factor.
		For a cash flow received after several periods, raise the factor to the corresponding power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


## 时间轴(Time Line)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Timeline*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

潜在的现金流时间的线性表示([[Linear Combination|Linear]] Representation of Potential Cash Flow Timing)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A timeline is a linear representation of when potential cash flows occur.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如每学年学费 10000 且按学期支付一半。若今天是第 1 学期起点，则时间轴可写为：
`Date: 0,1,2,3,4`；`Cash flow: -5000,-5000,-5000,-5000,0`。
若各期贴现率可不同，则现值一般写为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose annual tuition is 10,000 and half is paid each term. If today is the start of the first term, the timeline can be written as:
`Date: 0,1,2,3,4`; `Cash flow: -5000,-5000,-5000,-5000,0`.
If the discount rate may differ across periods, present value is generally written as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
PV=\frac{C_1}{(1+r_1)^1}+\frac{C_2}{(1+r_2)^2}+\cdots+\frac{C_N}{(1+r_N)^N}.
$$

如果每一次的利率都一样，那么公式将变为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the interest rate is the same in every period, the formula becomes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
PV=\frac{C_1}{(1+r)^1}+\frac{C_2}{(1+r)^2}+\cdots+\frac{C_N}{(1+r)^N}
=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac{C_n}{(1+r)^n}.
$$

如果涉及多笔的资金时：
0 2 N Ci Ci C CN 我 们 分 两 步 计算 现金 流 的 现 值 。 1. 首先 ， 计 算 每 笔 现金 流 的 现 值 。 2. 然后 合并 现 值 。
也就是“逐笔折现后再加总”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When there are several cash flows, first discount each one separately and then add their present values together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

PV of an extended stream of future cash flows:
$$
PV=\sum_{t=0}^{N}\frac{C_t}{(1+r)^t}
=C_0+\frac{C_1}{1+r}+\frac{C_2}{(1+r)^2}+\cdots+\frac{C_N}{(1+r)^N}.
$$

## [[Net Present Value|NPV]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Net Present Value (NPV)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即PV（收益）- PV（成本）(i.e., PV (Benefits) - PV (Costs))
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NPV equals the present value of benefits minus the present value of costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
