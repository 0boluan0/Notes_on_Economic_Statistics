
# 货币的含义(Meaning of Money)

## 货币（货币供给）money supply(Money Supply)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Money and the money supply (Money Supply)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义为在产品和服 务支付以及债务偿还中被普遍接受的东西.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Money is anything generally accepted as payment for goods and services and in repayment of debts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 通货currency(Currency)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Currency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

纸币和硬币，是货币供给中最常用的一种
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Currency consists of notes and coins and is the most commonly used component of the money supply.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 财富wealth(Wealth)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Wealth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是指用户价值储藏的各项财产总和。它不仅包括货币, 还包括债券. 普通股、艺术品、 土地.家具 汽车和房屋.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Wealth is the total value of the assets a person holds as stores of value. It includes not only money, but also bonds, ordinary shares, art, land, furniture, cars, and houses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 收入income
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Income*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位时间段内收入的流量，而货币是存量的概念，二者概念不同
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Income is a flow measured over a period of time, whereas money is a stock measured at a point in time; the two concepts are therefore distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 交易媒介medium of exchange
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*medium of exchange*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

货币被用来购买产品和服务，节省了产品和服务的交易时间，提升了经济效率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Money is used to buy goods and services. By reducing the time required to exchange them, it raises economic efficiency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要满足交易媒介这个特质，需要：
1. 它必须易于标准化. 从而可以方便地确定其价值, 
2. 它必须被普遍接受, 
3. 它必须易于分割•以便于找零
4. 它必须易于携带
5. 它不会很快腐化变质.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To function well as a medium of exchange, an object must:
**1.** be easy to standardise so that its value can be determined;<br>
**2.** be widely accepted;<br>
**3.** be divisible so that change can be made;<br>
**4.** be easy to carry; and<br>
**5.** resist rapid deterioration.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 记账单位unit of account
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Unit of account*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它可以作为经济社会中价值衡量的手段。又叫做价格尺度.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Money provides a common measure of value in an economy; this function is also called the standard of value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 价值储藏store of value
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Store of value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

跨越时间段的购买力的储藏。将购买力从获得收入之日起储蓄到支出之日。
货币没有升值的能力，但是具有极强的[[05_利率行为#流动性(Liquidity)|流动性]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A store of value transfers purchasing power across time, preserving it from the date income is received until the date it is spent.
Money does not itself promise capital appreciation, but it has very high [[05_利率行为#流动性(Liquidity)|liquidity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

货币作为价值储藏手段的优劣取决于物价水平，如果遇到恶性通货膨胀hyperinflation那么货币就像纸一样。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
How well money serves as a store of value depends on the price level. Under hyperinflation, its purchasing power can collapse until it is worth little more than paper.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 支付体系的演进
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Evolution of the Payment System*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 支付体系payments system
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Payment system*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经济社会中进行交易的方式，我们通过考察支付体系的演进历史可以更好的理解货币的功能和货币形式的发展。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The payment system is the set of methods through which transactions are conducted in an economy. Its historical evolution helps explain both the functions of money and the development of different monetary forms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 商品货币commodity money
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Commodity money*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由贵金属等其他有价值的商品构成的货币称为商品货币。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Money made from precious metals or other valuable goods is called commodity money.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不兑现纸币fiat money
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fiat Money*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最初由纸币paper money （发挥交易媒介功能的纸片）代替了商品货币，此时的纸币还附有可以转化为硬币或一定数量的贵金属的承诺，而后这个承诺取消了，就成了不兑现纸币。即政府将纸币宣布为法定偿还货币 (即在支付债务时. 人们必须接受它)，而不能转化成硬币或贵金属。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Paper money—pieces of paper used as a medium of exchange—initially replaced commodity money while remaining convertible into coins or a stated quantity of precious metal. Once that promise of convertibility was removed, it became fiat money: the government declared the notes legal tender, which people must accept in payment of debts, even though they could no longer be converted into coins or precious metals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 支票
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*cheque*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是向你的开户银行发出的支付指令，要求银行将货币从你的账户上转移到存入该支票人的账户上。使人们无需携带大量通货就能完成交易。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A cheque instructs your bank to transfer money from your account to the account of the person who deposits it. It allows transactions to be completed without carrying large quantities of currency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 货币总量monetary aggregate

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sum of M1 and M2

### M1
最狭义的货币指标，包括流动性最强的资产，即通货，支票账户存款于旅行者支票
这里的通货只算了非银行公众所持有的纸币和硬币，银行手里的不算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The narrowest monetary aggregate, M1 includes the most liquid assets: currency, deposits in chequeable accounts, and travellers' cheques.
Here, currency includes only notes and coins held by the non-bank public; cash held by banks is excluded.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### M2

在M1的基础上，M2增加了一些流动性不及M1的资产/
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
M2 consists of M1 plus several assets that are somewhat less liquid.
