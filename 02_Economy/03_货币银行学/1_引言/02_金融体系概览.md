# 金融市场的结构(Structure of Financial Markets)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Structure of Financial Markets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 债务与股权市场(Debt and Equity Markets)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Debt and Equity Markets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 债务工具
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Debt Instruments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债务工具的**期限(maturity)** 就是距离该项工具最终偿还日的时间。
* 短期（short-term）债务工具：期限在一年以下
* 中期 ( intermediate-term) 债务工具：期限介于1至10年之内
* 长期 ( long-term) 债务工具：期限在十年或十年以上
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **maturity** of debt instruments is the time remaining until the final repayment date.

- **Short-term debt:** maturity of less than one year.
- **Intermediate-term debt:** maturity between one and ten years.
- **Long-term debt:** maturity of ten years or more.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 股权
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 股权 ( equity) 工具承诺持有者按份额享有公司的净收益 (扣除费用和税款后的收入) 和资产。
	由于这种工具没有到期日. 因此被视为长期证券。拥有股票意味着你拥有该公司的一定份额. 因此你有权就公司的重大事项和董事长的选举进行投票。
* 股利（dividend）股权工具的持有者可以得到定期支付。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Equity instruments give their holders a proportional claim on a company's net income—income after expenses and taxes—and assets. Because equity has no maturity date, it is a long-term security. A shareholder owns part of the company and may vote on major corporate matters and the election of directors.
* **Dividends** are distributions paid periodically to equity holders.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一级市场和二级市场(Primary and Secondary Markets)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Primary and Secondary Markets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一级市场
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Primary Market*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不公开进行
一级市场 (primary market) 是筹集资金的公司或政府机构将其新发行的股票或债券等证券销售给最初购买者的金融市场。
* 投资银行 (investmentbank) 是一级市场上协助证券首次出售的重要的金融机构。
* 证券承销 ( underwriting)投资银行确保公司证券能够按照某一价格销售出去， 之后再向公
众推销这些证券。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Primary-market transactions do not take place through ordinary secondary-market trading.
The **primary market** is where a company or government raises funds by selling newly issued stocks or bonds to their first purchasers.
* An **investment bank** helps issuers sell securities for the first time.
* In **underwriting**, the investment bank agrees to place the securities at an arranged price and then markets them to investors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 二级市场
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Secondary Market*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二级市场 ( secondarymarket) 是交易已经发行的证券的金融市场。
* 经纪人 (broker) 是投资者的代理人， 它们负责匹配证券的买方和卖方.
* 交易商 (dealer) 按照报价买卖证券， 从而将买卖双方联结起来.
* 流动性(liquidity):投资者可以更加容易和快捷地出售金融工具， 实现筹措资金的目的.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **secondary market** is where previously issued securities are traded.

* A broker acts as an agent for investors, matching buyers and sellers of securities.
* A dealer stands ready to buy and sell securities at quoted prices.
* **Liquidity** means that investors can sell a financial instrument quickly and readily to raise cash.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 交易所与场外市场(Exchanges and Over-the-Counter Markets)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Exchanges and Over-the-Counter Markets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 交易所（exchange）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Exchange*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即证券的买卖双方 （或者他们的代理人或经纪人） 在一个集中的场所进行交易的二级市场。
	eg：纽约证券交易所和芝加哥商品交易所
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **exchange** is a secondary market in which buyers and sellers, or their brokers, trade in a centralised venue. Examples include the New York Stock Exchange and the Chicago Mercantile Exchange.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 场外市场（over-the-countermarket, OTC）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Over-the-Counter Market (OTC)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即分处各地的拥有证券存货的交易商随时向与其联系并愿意接受其报价的人在 “柜台” 上买卖证券的二级市场。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an **over-the-counter market**, geographically dispersed dealers quote prices from their own inventories and trade directly with counterparties willing to accept those quotes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 货币市场与资本市场(Money Markets and Capital Markets)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Money Markets and Capital Markets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 货币市场（money market）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Money Market*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是交易短期债务工具 （原始期限通常为 1 年以下） 的金融市场； 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **money market** trades short-term debt instruments, usually with an original maturity of less than one year.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 资本市场（capital market）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Capital Market*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是交易长期债务工具 （原始期限通常在 1年或 1 年以上） 与股权工具的金融市场。
货币市场证券的交易更为广泛，更具有流动性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **capital market** trades long-term debt instruments, usually with an original maturity of at least one year, and equity instruments.
Money-market securities generally trade more actively and are more liquid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The federal funds rate reflects the tightness of the market for bank loans. Higher rates indicate that banks have tighter cash positions.

### 交易成本 ( transaction cost)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Transaction Cost*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是在金融交易过程中所耗用的时间和金钱. 这是拥有多余资金需要贷放的人们所面临的主要问题。

### 规模经济 (economies of scale) 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Economies of Scale*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即随着交易规模的扩大. 平摊在每一美元交易上的成本在降低。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Economies of scale** mean that the cost per dollar transacted falls as the volume of transactions increases.

	eg：银行知道如何找到一名好律师来起草一份严密的贷款合同， 这份合同可以在其贷款活动中重复使用. 于是就降低了每笔交易的法律成本。银行不会花 500 美元购买一份贷款合同 (这可能还不够好)， 而是会花 5 000 美元去雇用一个一流的律师. 这个律师所设计的滴水不漏的合同可以供2 000 笔贷款使用 因此每笔贷款的成本就降低到了 2. 50 美元。这样由金融中介机构向卡尔发放 1 000 美元的贷款就变得有利可图。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, a bank knows how to hire a good lawyer to draft a rigorous loan contract that can be reused throughout its lending business, thereby reducing the legal cost of each transaction. Instead of paying $500 for a possibly inadequate contract, the bank can pay $5,000 for a first-rate lawyer. If that watertight contract is used for 2,000 loans, its cost falls to $2.50 per loan, making it profitable for a financial intermediary to lend Karl $1,000.

### 流动性服务 (liquidity services)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Liquidity Services*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是指由流动性服务商提供的一系列服务，旨在提高金融市场中资产的流动性。这些服务商可能是证券公司、专业机构或其他符合条件的组织，它们通过提供持续的双边报价（即同时报出证券的买入价和卖出价）来保证交易的及时成交，从而促进市场的流动性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Liquidity services** make financial assets easier to buy, sell, and convert into cash. Market makers and other intermediaries support liquidity by continuously quoting both bid and ask prices and standing ready to trade.

### 风险 ( risk)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Risk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

投资者资产收益的不确定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Risk** is uncertainty about the return on an investor's assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 风险分担 ( risk sharing) 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Risk Sharing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金融中介机构所设计和提供的资产品种的风险在投资者所能承受的范围之内. 金融中介机构用销售这些资产所获取的资金去购买风险大得多的资产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial intermediaries issue claims with risk characteristics that savers are willing to hold, then use the proceeds to acquire riskier assets such as loans. In this way they redistribute and pool risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 资产转换 (asset transformation)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Asset Transformation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

低交易成本允许金融中介机构以较低的成本进行风险分担. 使得它们能够获取风险资产的收益与已出售资产的成本之间的差额. 这也是金融中介机构的利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Low transaction costs allow intermediaries to perform this risk sharing efficiently. Their profit comes from the spread between the return on the risky assets they acquire and the cost of the safer claims they issue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 多样化 ( diversification)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diversification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

 包括投资于收益变动方向不一致的一系列资产， 即资产组合 (portfolio), 从而将总体风险降低到单个资产的风险之下。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diversification means investing in a collection of assets whose returns do not all move in the same direction—that is, a portfolio—so that total risk falls below the risk of holding a single asset.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 信息不对称：逆向选择和道德风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 信息不对称 ( asymmetric  information)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Information Asymmetry (Asymmetric Information)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在金融市场上, 交易一方对另一方缺乏充分的了解. 以至于无法做出正确的决策。这种不对等的状态被称为信息不对称 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Information asymmetry** exists when one party to a financial transaction knows less than the other about relevant risks or behaviour. Because decisions must be made with incomplete information, funds may be priced or allocated poorly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 逆向选择 (adverse selection)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Adverse Selection*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金融市场上的逆向选择是指. 那些最可能造成不利 (逆向) 后果即制造信贷风险的潜在借款人往往是那些最积极寻求贷款， 并且最可能获取贷款的人。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Adverse selection** arises before a transaction: the prospective borrowers most likely to produce a bad outcome are often the most eager to seek credit and may be the most likely to obtain it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 道德风险 ( moral hazard)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Moral Hazard*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金融市场中的道德风险是借款人可能从事不利于贷款人的 (不道德) 的活动， 因为这些活动加大了贷款无法清偿的概率. 因而造成了风险 (危险）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Moral hazard** arises after funds have been provided: the borrower may take actions that benefit the borrower but harm the lender by increasing the probability that the loan will not be repaid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Financial institutions can benefit from economies of scope, but combining different activities may also create conflicts of interest and other costs.
