### 1. 货币、金融体系与中央银行
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Money, the Financial System, and Central Banks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是本课程的默认起点；先用快速恢复建立对象、例子和问题感，再进入详细内容。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the default starting point. Use the quick-recovery section to establish the objects, examples, and motivating questions before moving into the details.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[货币、流动性与货币口径]]

先界定哪些负债具有货币性和流动性，再看银行与其他中介怎样创造、转换并配置这些流动性权利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One must first identify which liabilities function as money and liquidity. Banks and other intermediaries can then be analysed as creators, transformers, and allocators of those liquid claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[金融体系与金融中介]]

私人中介把期限、风险和流动性进行转换，也会产生清算与挤兑脆弱性；中央银行因此提供最终结算资产和最后贷款人支持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Private intermediaries transform maturity, risk, and liquidity, but also create settlement and run vulnerabilities. The central bank therefore supplies the ultimate settlement asset and lender-of-last-resort support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[中央银行资产负债表、支付与最后贷款人]]

### 2. 现值、利率与利率期限结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Present Value, Interest Rates, and the Term Structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理解货币和金融中介为何存在后，现值与利率把跨期交换变成可比较的价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the roles of money and financial intermediaries are clear, present value and interest rates turn intertemporal exchanges into comparable prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[货币时间价值与贴现]]

货币时间价值先用单一贴现率比较不同日期的现金流；期限结构再允许每个期限有不同即期率，并由此推出远期率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The time value of money first compares dated cash flows with a single discount rate. The term structure then allows each maturity to have its own spot rate and derives forward rates from that curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[利率、即期远期与期限结构]]

有了无风险期限曲线，才能逐笔贴现债券现金流，并把到期收益率与信用利差分解为可解释的价格成分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the risk-free term structure is available, bond cash flows can be discounted one by one and yields and credit spreads can be separated into interpretable pricing components.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[债券定价、收益率与信用利差]]

### 3. 资产定价、有效市场与行为金融
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Asset Pricing, Efficient Markets, and Behavioural Finance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利率给出无风险跨期基准；资产定价再加入风险、预期和市场信息如何进入价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interest rates provide a risk-free intertemporal benchmark. Asset pricing then adds risk, expectations, and the process through which market information enters prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本]]

CAPM 先给出风险与预期收益的均衡基准；有效市场假说和事件研究再检验信息是否已被价格吸收，以及异常收益能否持续。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CAPM first supplies an equilibrium benchmark linking risk to expected return. The efficient-market hypothesis and event studies then test whether information is already incorporated into prices and whether abnormal returns persist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[有效市场假说与事件研究]]

发现价格偏离基准并不足以推翻有效市场；行为金融还要说明偏差从何而来，以及套利限制为何阻止理性交易者立即消除它。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A price departure from a benchmark is not enough to reject market efficiency. Behavioural finance must also explain the source of the bias and why limits to arbitrage prevent rational traders from eliminating it immediately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[行为金融与套利限制]]

### 4. 信息不对称、金融结构与金融危机
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Asymmetric Information, Financial Structure, and Financial Crises*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有效市场是假设性的基准；信息不对称解释为何融资结构、中介和危机会系统性偏离无摩擦市场。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Efficient markets provide a hypothetical benchmark. Asymmetric information explains why financing structures, intermediaries, and crises systematically depart from a frictionless market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[金融市场中的信息不对称与契约]]

逆向选择、道德风险和抵押约束先在单个契约中扭曲融资；当资产负债表同时恶化时，这些摩擦会放大成挤兑、去杠杆和系统性危机。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adverse selection, moral hazard, and collateral constraints first distort individual contracts. When balance sheets deteriorate together, those frictions amplify into runs, deleveraging, and systemic crises.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[金融危机、挤兑与金融加速器]]

### 5. 商业银行经营、监管与货币供给
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Commercial Bank Management, Regulation, and the Money Supply*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信息摩擦落实到银行资产负债表后，就能理解流动性、资本监管以及存款和货币供给怎样形成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once information frictions are placed on a bank balance sheet, liquidity, capital regulation, deposit creation, and the formation of the money supply can be understood together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[商业银行资产负债管理]]

资产负债管理说明单家银行怎样在收益、流动性与风险间配置资产和负债；把银行放在支付网络中，贷款与结算行为便进一步解释存款如何被创造。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Asset–liability management explains how one bank balances return, liquidity, and risk across its balance sheet. Placing banks in the payments network then shows how lending and settlement create deposits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[银行存款创造与货币供给]]

银行体系扩张资产负债表的能力并非无限；资本、流动性和杠杆规则为潜在损失与现金流压力设置制度约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The banking system cannot expand its balance sheet without limit. Capital, liquidity, and leverage rules impose institutional constraints against potential losses and cash-flow pressure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[巴塞尔银行资本与流动性监管]]

### 6. 货币政策与外汇市场
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Monetary Policy and the Foreign-Exchange Market*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中央银行通过银行和市场传导政策，开放经济下又与汇率、资本流动和利率平价相互约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Central-bank policy is transmitted through banks and markets. In an open economy, it is also constrained by exchange rates, capital flows, and interest-rate parity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[货币政策工具、传导与可信度]]

国内货币政策通过利率、信贷和预期传导；在开放经济中，资本流动、汇率制度和利率平价又限制政策可以独立选择的组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Domestic monetary policy transmits through interest rates, credit, and expectations. In an open economy, capital mobility, the exchange-rate regime, and interest parity constrain which policy combination can be chosen independently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[外汇市场、利率平价与不可能三角]]
