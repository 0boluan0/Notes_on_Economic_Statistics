### 1. 货币、金融体系与中央银行
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Money, the Financial System, and Central Banks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是本课程的默认起点。先沿原子路径分清货币的功能、统计口径、负债主体与支付结算，再进入金融中介和中央银行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the default starting point. Follow the atomic path to distinguish the functions of money, statistical aggregates, liability issuers, and payment settlement before moving to financial intermediaries and central banks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

货币先由可接受的支付能力界定，再与财富和收入分开；随后把交换媒介、计价单位和价值储藏三项功能逐一拆开，最后用多维标准判断资产的货币性与流动性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin by defining money through accepted payment capacity and separating it from wealth and income. Then distinguish its medium-of-exchange, unit-of-account, and store-of-value functions before assessing moneyness and liquidity along several dimensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Statistical aggregates compress a continuum of moneyness into published boundaries. Treat those boundaries as institutional conventions before reading the current US M1 and M2 definitions and the 2020 break; any comparison across countries or time must first fix the definition and observation conditions.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next read the balance sheets by issuer and holder. Banknotes, bank deposits, reserve balances, and vault cash are not the same instrument; under current U.S. definitions, the monetary base is not simply a homogeneous lower tier of M1 and M2.

最后沿一笔支付和一笔央行交易追踪余额。区分同一银行内重记账、跨行准备金结算、公众取现、体系准备金总量变化与央行购债，才能避免把 QE 写成机械的贷款或货币乘数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally trace balances through a payment and a central-bank transaction. Distinguishing on-us bookkeeping, interbank reserve settlement, public cash withdrawals, changes in system-wide reserves, and central-bank asset purchases prevents QE from being reduced to a mechanical lending or money multiplier.

先界定哪些负债具有货币性和流动性，再看银行与其他中介怎样创造、转换并配置这些流动性权利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One must first identify which liabilities function as money and liquidity. Banks and other intermediaries can then be analysed as creators, transformers, and allocators of those liquid claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先沿索取权追踪资金：金融体系连接谁与谁，以及直接融资和间接融资究竟在哪一张资产负债表上分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First trace funds through the claims that link the parties: identify whom the financial system connects and where direct and indirect finance diverge across balance sheets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多出一层中介必须有理由。交易处理的规模经济、集中生产信息和关系型贷款的软信息，分别解释标准化业务、借款人筛选和非标准化融资为什么不必由每位储蓄者各做一遍。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An additional intermediary layer needs an economic reason. Scale economies in transaction processing, delegated information production, and soft information in relationship lending explain why standardised operations, borrower assessment, and non-standard finance need not be reproduced by every saver.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中介随后不只是“撮合”两边，而是在自己的索取权结构中重新组合金额、期限、变现能力和损失承担。四类转换必须分开理解，因为每一类服务对应的约束和失败方式不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An intermediary then does more than match the two sides: its own claim structure recombines transaction size, maturity, saleability, and loss bearing. The four transformations must be separated because each service has different constraints and failure modes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后把视线从传统银行移向整个体系。先用资产负债表识别中介，再区分直接融资所依赖的服务链、市场型融资的制度安排和证券化的现金流重组。证券化不会自动消灭中介；非银行、市场型与影子银行也不是同义词。只有追踪每个节点的资产、负债和后备支持，才能看见挤兑与火售怎样跨机构传播。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally widen the view from traditional banks to the whole system. First identify intermediation from balance sheets, then distinguish the service chain used by direct finance, the institutional arrangements of market-based finance, and the cash-flow restructuring performed by securitisation. Securitisation does not automatically eliminate intermediation, and non-bank, market-based, and shadow banking are not synonyms. Only by tracing each node's assets, liabilities, and backstops can one see how runs and fire sales spread across institutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

私人中介把期限、风险和流动性进行转换，也会产生清算与挤兑脆弱性；中央银行提供最终结算资产，并在法定对象、抵押品和项目条件下提供最后贷款人支持，而不是自动为所有中介或损失托底。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Private intermediaries transform maturity, risk, and liquidity, but also create settlement and run vulnerabilities. Central banks supply the ultimate settlement asset and provide lender-of-last-resort support only within the applicable legal perimeter, collateral rules, and programme conditions, not as an automatic backstop for every intermediary or loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2. 现值、利率与利率期限结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Present Value, Interest Rates, and the Term Structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理解货币和金融中介为何存在后，现值与利率把跨期交换变成可比较的价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the roles of money and financial intermediaries are clear, present value and interest rates turn intertemporal exchanges into comparable prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

货币银行学先把“何时支付”翻译成可比较价格：选共同评价时点、画出准确日期，再用复利/贴现把每笔现金流逐笔换算并加总。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Monetary economics first translates “when payment occurs” into a comparable price: choose a common valuation date, mark exact dates, then compound or discount every cash flow before adding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[现金流评价时点]]

![[现金流时间线]]

换算公式本身不会产生利率。债券、外汇与银行现金流的贴现率还必须匹配其期限、币种、风险与通胀口径；报价频率不同时先转成同期间的有效率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conversion formula does not generate its own rate. Bond, foreign-exchange, and banking cash flows require discount rates matched by maturity, currency, risk, and inflation basis; quoted rates must first be converted to an effective rate for the cash-flow interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal periodic cash flows compress per-payment discounting into annuity formulas. Moving payments one period earlier, continuing forever at a level amount, or growing forever changes the timeline and convergence conditions. Only after all incremental cash flows are included does NPV measure value creation.

A price departure from a benchmark is not enough to reject market efficiency. Behavioural finance must also explain the source of the bias and why limits to arbitrage prevent rational traders from eliminating it immediately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 1. 发现偏差以后，为什么价格不一定立即纠正
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why an identified deviation need not be corrected immediately*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先保留上一课的基准：[[有效市场假说]]并不要求每个人都不犯错；发现异常收益还受[[市场效率联合假设]]约束。行为金融的任务，是把可观察的判断机制与市场结果连接起来。为了避免后面把“有望赚钱”和“无风险套利”混为一谈，先分清纠偏交易是什么。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Retain the preceding benchmark: [[有效市场假说|market efficiency]] does not require every person to be error-free, and abnormal-return evidence faces the [[市场效率联合假设|joint hypothesis]]. Behavioral finance connects observed decision mechanisms to outcomes. Distinguish potentially profitable correction trades from strict arbitrage first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[行为金融]]

![[套利]]

![[套利与价差交易]]

![[套利限制]]

一个研究者估计股票值 100，而市场价为 90，不代表他已经找到了套利。100 可能估错，现金流可能变化，价格也可能先跌到 80。接下来先研究需求为什么会偏，再研究纠偏头寸为什么承担风险；这两个环节缺一不可。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A valuation of 100 against a price of 90 is not automatically an arbitrage: the estimate can be wrong, cash flows can change, and the price can first fall to 80. We now study demand distortions, then risks in correcting them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 2. 前景理论先问：你把什么当作收益和损失
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Prospect theory starts by locating gains and losses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值方差选择给定收益分布后比较均值和风险，且有自己的[[均值方差偏好边界|偏好适用边界]]。前景理论改换了描述选择的对象：同一最终财富，相对于不同参考点，可能被体验为收益或损失。先定位参考点，再看两侧的评价，不能从一幅 S 形图直接背出“人永远怕亏”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mean–variance choice has its own [[均值方差偏好边界|preference limits]]. Prospect theory describes outcomes as gains or losses relative to a reference point. Locate that point before comparing the two sides; an S-shaped value function does not mean people always avoid risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[前景参考点]]

![[损失厌恶]]

![[损失厌恶与风险厌恶]]

例如最终拿到 100：以 90 为参照是收益 10，以 110 为参照是损失 10。这里变化的是得失编码，不是这张 100 元钞票变了。解释实际选择时，参考点必须有独立依据，不能看见选择后再挑一个恰好讲得通的参考点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Receiving 100 is a gain of 10 relative to 90 but a loss of 10 relative to 110. The coding changes, not the money. An empirical reference point needs independent justification rather than being selected afterward to fit a choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 3. 决策权重不是换了名字的概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Decision weights are not probabilities with a new name*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价值函数回答“一个结果对我有多大价值”；权重回答“这个结果在整体选择中占多大影响”。不要把两者都叫作价值函数，也不要把每个客观概率分别变换后，便以为得到了适用于任意多结果前景的通式。下面先定义权重，再实际算一次累积前景权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The value function evaluates an outcome; decision weights determine its contribution to overall choice. Transforming each individual probability independently is not a universal formula for arbitrary many-outcome prospects. Define weights, then compute a cumulative example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[前景决策权重]]

![[累积前景权重计算]]

读课堂 PDF 第 324 页的两幅图时，左图横轴应理解为相对参考点的得失；右图要把端点与区间内部区分开。小概率的超权重不等于投资者真的认为概率变大，曲率、损失侧不对称和概率加权也不能互相替代。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the two diagrams on course PDF page 324, the left horizontal axis measures reference-relative outcomes. On the right, distinguish endpoints from interior probabilities. Overweighting is not necessarily a probability belief; curvature, loss asymmetry, and weighting are separate components.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 4. 从得失评价到卖赢持亏，还缺一个决策问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From gain–loss evaluation to selling winners and holding losers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“赚钱就卖、亏钱不卖”是可观察的持仓处置模式；损失厌恶是偏好的一个性质。两者之间还隔着买入、继续持有和卖出的动态选择，以及税费、预期回报与参考点变化。因此先识别行为，再评估解释，不把定义直接当作因果证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Selling winners and holding losers is an observable disposal pattern; loss aversion is a preference property. Dynamic buying, holding, selling, taxes, expected returns, and reference-point changes lie between them. Identify the pattern before testing its explanation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 5. 同一条消息，为什么会产生不同判断

*Why the same news can produce different judgments*

另一条路线不是改变对得失的喜好，而是改变相信什么、相信得有多准，以及实际看到了哪些信息。以下五个概念不能用“非理性”一词抹平：过度自信关乎校准，自我归因关乎如何解释成败，代表性关乎模式推断，锚定关乎先前数值的牵引，有限注意关乎处理能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second route concerns beliefs, confidence, and processed information rather than gain–loss preferences. Overconfidence concerns calibration; attribution concerns explanations of outcomes; representativeness concerns pattern inference; anchoring concerns initial numbers; limited attention concerns processing capacity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 6. BSV、DHS、HS 怎样把判断接到价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How BSV, DHS, and HS connect judgments to prices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不要把三个缩写背成“三种解释动量的理论”。先问各自改了哪个假设，再沿着同一模型的消息、预期和交易过程往下走。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Do not memorize three acronyms as interchangeable momentum explanations. Identify each altered assumption and follow its information, belief, and trading mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[BSV信念模型]]

BSV 的关键是“在错模型里认真更新”。DHS 换了问题：对自己的私人信号相信得过头；再加入不对称自我归因，信心本身也会随结果变化。下面把固定信心和反馈机制分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BSV updates carefully inside the wrong model. DHS instead overweights private-signal precision, then adds outcome-dependent confidence through asymmetric attribution. Separate fixed confidence from its feedback extension.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[DHS私人信息模型]]

![[DHS信心反馈]]

HS 又不需要让每个人都犯同一种判断错误，而是限制两组交易者各自使用的信息。纠正别人的反应不足，也可能制造新的超调。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
HS restricts the information used by two groups rather than imposing one shared judgment error. Trading against others' underreaction can itself create overshooting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[HS信息扩散模型]]

| 模型 | 改变的核心假设 | 必须留住的区别 |
|---|---|---|
| BSV | 盈利生成模型设错 | 模型内可以正确使用贝叶斯规则 |
| DHS | 自己的私人信号精度被高估 | 固定信心与自我归因反馈是不同层次 |
| HS | 信息扩散缓慢、两组策略受限 | 早期纠偏与后期超调可来自同一互动 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BSV misspecifies earnings while permitting Bayesian updating. DHS distorts private precision, with a separate confidence-feedback extension. HS combines gradual diffusion and restricted strategies, allowing early correction and later overshooting within one interaction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 7. 纠偏交易承担的风险来自哪里
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Where the risks of corrective trading come from*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解释需求为何偏移还不够。DSSW 的噪声交易者风险模型让我们问：即使基本面没有出现坏消息，下一期情绪更极端会怎样？这与公司的现金流真的变坏，是两种风险来源。先把交易者与风险分别定义，再作对照。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Demand distortions are not the whole story. The DSSW noise-trader-risk model asks what happens if sentiment worsens even without adverse fundamentals. That differs from genuinely deteriorating cash flows. Define traders and risks separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[噪声交易者]]

![[噪声交易者风险]]

![[基本面风险]]

课堂还列出孪生股票价差、封闭式基金折价和过度波动。这些是待解释的现象，不是某个心理模型已被证明正确。孪生股票先核对经济权利及能否转换；基金折价还可能涉及费用、税负和清算预期；过度波动要明确现金流与贴现基准。不要从“看起来反常”跳到“存在无风险利润”。

Check claim equivalence and conversion, fees and liquidation expectations, or cash-flow and discount benchmarks respectively. Apparent anomalies do not establish riskless profits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
