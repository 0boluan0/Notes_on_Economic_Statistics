### 2. 现值、利率与利率期限结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Present Value, Interest Rates, and the Term Structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理解货币和金融中介为何存在后，现值与利率把跨期交换变成可比较的价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the roles of money and financial intermediaries are clear, present value and interest rates turn intertemporal exchanges into comparable prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[现值、利率与利率期限结构]]

### 3. 资产定价、有效市场与行为金融
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Asset Pricing, Efficient Markets, and Behavioural Finance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利率给出无风险跨期基准；资产定价再加入风险、预期和市场信息如何进入价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interest rates provide a risk-free intertemporal benchmark. Asset pricing then adds risk, expectations, and the process through which market information enters prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[资产定价、有效市场与行为金融]]

### 4. 信息不对称、金融结构与金融危机
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Asymmetric Information, Financial Structure, and Financial Crises*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有效市场是假设性的基准；信息不对称解释为何融资结构、中介和危机会系统性偏离无摩擦市场。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Efficient markets provide a hypothetical benchmark. Asymmetric information explains why financing structures, intermediaries, and crises systematically depart from a frictionless market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[信息不对称、金融结构与金融危机]]

### 5. 商业银行经营、监管与货币供给
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Commercial Bank Management, Regulation, and the Money Supply*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信息摩擦落实到银行资产负债表后，就能理解流动性、资本监管以及存款和货币供给怎样形成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once information frictions are placed on a bank balance sheet, liquidity, capital regulation, deposit creation, and the formation of the money supply can be understood together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[商业银行经营、监管与货币供给]]

### 6. 货币政策与外汇市场
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Monetary Policy and the Foreign-Exchange Market*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中央银行通过银行和市场传导政策，开放经济下又与汇率、资本流动和利率平价相互约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Central-bank policy is transmitted through banks and markets. In an open economy, it is also constrained by exchange rates, capital flows, and interest-rate parity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[货币政策与外汇市场]]
