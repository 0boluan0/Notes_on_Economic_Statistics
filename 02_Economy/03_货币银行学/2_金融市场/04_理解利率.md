## [[复利与贴现|现值]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*[[复利与贴现|present value]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 现值present value
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*present value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

又叫做现期贴现值present  discounted value。指的是未来某一时点或一系列未来时点的现金流量按照一定的折现率折算到当前的价值。简单来说，现值就是未来现金流量在今天的价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Present value**, also called present discounted value, is the value today of one or more future cash flows after discounting them at an appropriate rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

未来的一块钱到现在肯定不值一块钱,所以说想要知道它值现在的多少钱就要给它打个折.就叫贴现.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A dollar received in the future is worth less than a dollar today. **Discounting** converts that future amount into its equivalent value today.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start B01 -->
> [!note] 校注：正利率是这句直觉的条件
> 在 $P=1/(1+r)^T$、$T>0$、$r>-1$ 的同币种单笔现金流模型中，只有 $r>0$ 才有 $P<1$；零利率给出 $P=1$，负利率给出 $P>1$。[[复利与贴现]]是跨时点换算，不是“任何情况下都打折”的定义。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the same-currency single-payment model $P=1/(1+r)^T$, with $T>0$ and $r>-1$, only $r>0$ implies $P<1$. A zero rate gives $P=1$, and a negative rate gives $P>1$. [[复利与贴现|Compounding and discounting]] convert values across dates; discounting does not mean a price reduction in every rate environment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

## 四种类型的信用市场工具(Four Types of Credit Market Instruments)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Four Types of Credit Market Instruments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 普通贷款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Ordinary Loan*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

字面意思,就是普通贷款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ordinary loan is the simplest arrangement: a lender advances principal and the borrower repays the principal plus interest at maturity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

###  固定支付贷款fixed- payment loan 又叫做年金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fixed-Payment Loan, or Annuity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

又名分期偿还贷款fully amortized loan.贷款人向借款人提供一定数出的资金， 在约定的若干年度内.借款人每个期间 （例如每月） 偿还借定的金额. 其中中既包括本金. 也包括利息.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Also called a fully amortised loan, this arrangement gives the borrower a sum of money that is repaid through equal periodic payments, such as monthly payments, over a specified number of years. Each payment contains both principal and interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 息票债券coupon bond
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*coupon bond*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种债券在到期日之前每年向债券持有人支付固定金额的利息 （息票利息coupon rate）, 到期时再偿还事先规定的最终金额(债券面值face value , par value)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before maturity, a coupon bond pays its holder a fixed annual coupon. At maturity, it also repays the stated face value, or par value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start B02 -->
> [!note] 校注：息票金额与票面利率
> 息票是支付的金额，[[债券票面利率|coupon rate]]是全年合同息票与面值之比，两者不是同一量。此处以 [[固定息票债券]] 为例，付息也可以半年或每季一次；本息何时支付仍由合同规定。参见 [[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=111|CPA 印刷 103、105 页 / PDF 111、113 页]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A coupon is a cash amount; the [[债券票面利率|coupon rate]] is annual promised coupon income divided by face value. This example is a [[固定息票债券|fixed-coupon bond]], whose payment frequency may also be semiannual or quarterly. Contractual dates determine payment timing. See CPA printed pages 103 and 105 / PDF pages 111 and 113.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

### 贴现发行债券discount bond
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Discount Bond*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

又叫做零息债券zero-coupon bond.这种债券的购买价格低于其面值,即贴现发行,到期时按照其面值偿付.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Also called a zero-coupon bond, it is issued below face value and repays face value at maturity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start B03 -->
> [!note] 校注：工具分类与市场折价分开
> 本处 discount bond 是课程对 [[零息债券]] 的分类用语；“以折价交易”本身不等于零息，附息债也可能折价。零息债没有中间付息；仅到期按面值支付的 $P=F/(1+y)^T$ 模型，在固定正收益率下才低于面值，零或负收益率另论。来源：[[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=114|CPA 印刷 106 页单笔现值式]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Discount bond” here is the course's label for a [[零息债券|zero-coupon instrument]]. Trading at a discount is a different property: coupon bonds can also trade below par. With no interim payments and redemption at face, $P=F/(1+y)^T$ is below par for a positive yield; zero and negative yields require different conclusions. See the single-payment formula on CPA printed page 106.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

## [[到期收益率|到期收益率]]([[到期收益率|Yield to Maturity]])
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Yield to Maturity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全称叫做持有至到期收益率.把每一年的现金流都转化为现值的过程中使用的r值就是到期收益率.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Yield to maturity (YTM)** is the single discount rate $r$ that makes the present value of all promised cash flows equal to the instrument's current price. It is therefore the return implied by the price if the instrument is held to maturity and its promised payments are made.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start B04 -->
> [!note] 校注：不是任意贴现率，也不是保证回报
> [[到期收益率]]是使承诺支付现值**等于给定买入全价**的那个单一 IRR，期数与复利口径必须匹配。其计算不需要先发生再投资；解释为到期实现的复合收益则还需按约付款并处理息票再投资。见 [[到期收益率与实现收益]]及 [英格兰银行 YTM 定义，脚注 3](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/statistics/yield-curves/terminology-and-concepts)。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[到期收益率|YTM]] is the single IRR that makes promised-payment present value **equal the given purchase dirty price**, with matching periods and compounding. Computing it requires no actual reinvestment; interpreting it as a realized compound return additionally requires payment performance and coupon-reinvestment treatment. See [[到期收益率与实现收益|YTM versus realized return]] and the Bank of England's YTM definition, footnote 3.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

* 普通贷款就是那个利率.
* 固定支付贷款把每年的固定支付的金额都贴现回到现在,并使其跟现在的价格划等号,此时满足这个等式的那个r就是该固定支付贷款的到期收益率
* 息票债券的逻辑和固定支付贷款的理论是一样的.当息票债券的价格等于其面值时,到期收益率就是息票利率.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* For an ordinary loan, YTM is the contractual interest rate.
* For a fixed-payment loan, YTM is the rate $r$ that makes the present value of all scheduled payments equal to the amount borrowed today.
* For a coupon bond, the same principle applies: YTM discounts every coupon and the final principal repayment back to the bond's current price. When price equals face value, YTM equals the coupon rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start B05 -->
> [!note] 校注：贷款年率与平价判断的口径
> 单笔贷款的利率也须换到相同期间与复利口径，不能把多年单利年率直接当作复合 YTM。[[债券折溢价判别|附息债平价时票息率等于收益率]]则限于规则付息、按面值还本、恰当的付息后估值及相同周期报价。例如半年票息率为 4%，平价时每期 YTM 为 4%，名义年 YTM 为 8%，有效年率却为 8.16%。来源：[[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=116|CPA 印刷 108 页例 3-21]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A single-payment loan rate must use matching periods and compounding; a multi-year simple annual rate is not automatically its compound YTM. [[债券折溢价判别|Coupon–yield equality at par]] requires regular coupons, redemption at par, an appropriate post-coupon valuation date, and matching quotation conventions. A 4% semiannual coupon rate gives a 4% period YTM at par: 8% nominal annually, but 8.16% effective annually. See CPA printed page 108, Example 3-21.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->


  	息票债券中有一种特例,即统一公债consol或永续债券perpetuity.这是一种没有到期日,不会偿还本金的息票债券,永远只支付固定息票利.用无穷级数来计算.如果息票债券的到期时间很长,那么就可以使用永续债券来估算该息票债券的到期收益率,因为时间足够的长,所以本金的价格就很少了.相当于只付了利息.ic即档期收益率可以来估算这种息票债券的到期收益率.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A consol, or perpetuity, is a special coupon bond with no maturity date and no repayment of principal; it pays a fixed coupon forever. Its value is obtained from an infinite series. A very long-maturity coupon bond can be approximated as a perpetuity because the present value of its distant principal repayment is small. The current yield, coupon divided by price, can then approximate its yield to maturity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start B06 -->
> [!note] 校注：当期收益率近似有适用条件
> 当期收益率 $i_c=C/P$ 只取全年息票与当前价格之比，不计资本利得。对每年永久付正额 $C$、首笔一年后、恒定 $y>0$ 的 [[固定永续年金]]，$P=C/y$，所以 $C/P=y$。有限期长债近似的一个充分条件是本金现值和被截去的永续息票尾项相对 $C/y$ 都足够小；这不是必要条件，例如[[债券折溢价判别|按相同周期平价估值]]时两项可精确抵消，当期收益率也可等于 YTM。低息、零息或收益率接近零时，单说“期限长”不够。[FINRA 对收益率与回报的说明](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/bond-yield-return)区分当期收益率、YTM 与总回报。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Current yield $i_c=C/P$ uses annual coupon income divided by current price and excludes capital gains. For a [[固定永续年金|level perpetuity]] paying $C>0$ annually, first in one year, with constant $y>0$, $P=C/y$, so $C/P=y$. One sufficient condition for the long-bond approximation is that both principal present value and the omitted perpetual-coupon tail are small relative to $C/y$. This is not necessary: at [[债券折溢价判别|par under matching period conventions]], the two terms can cancel exactly, so current yield can also equal YTM. Long maturity alone is insufficient for low-coupon or zero-coupon bonds, or yields near zero. FINRA distinguishes current yield, YTM, and total return.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

债券现价与利率是 负向相关的： 当利率上升时， 债券价格下跌； 反之亦然
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bond prices are negatively related to interest rates: when interest rates rise, bond prices fall; vice versa.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start B07 -->
> [!note] 校注：固定现金流的比较
> [[债券价格收益率反向]]固定付款日期和非负现金流，仅提高同口径的要求收益率；这不是任意债券价格与任意市场利率的无条件经验相关。浮息、信用及嵌入期权也变化时，要把各项影响分开。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[债券价格收益率反向|The inverse price–yield relation]] fixes payment dates and nonnegative cash flows, increasing only the required yield under one convention. It is not an unconditional empirical correlation between every bond and every market rate. Separate floating-coupon, credit, and embedded-option changes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

# 利率与回报率的区别(Distinction between Interest Rate and Return Rate)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Interest Rates versus Rates of Return*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 回报率(Return Rate)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Rate of Return*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

衡量一个人在一个特定的时间段持有某种债券或其他有价债券所获取的收益的指标.又叫做持有期回报率.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **rate of return**, or holding-period return, measures the gain or loss from holding a bond or another security over a specified period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

 包含两部分,分别是债券的利息(当期收益率)和现在的价差(资本利得率rate of capital gain)
$$
R=i_c+g

$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The return has two components: the bond's interest income, or current yield, and the change in its price, or rate of capital gain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start B08 -->
> [!note] 校注：这条分解以一年持有为例
> 设期初全价 $P_0$，一年后收息 $C$ 并按付息后价格 $P_1$ 卖出，忽略税费，则 $R=(C+P_1-P_0)/P_0=C/P_0+(P_1-P_0)/P_0$。这里两项共用**该持有期的期初价格**，不是永远共用历史买价。其他持有长度或期内多次付息要重新列现金流；单利年化总收益和实现复合年收益也不同。参见 [[到期收益率与实现收益]]；[[02_Economy/06_证券投资学/证券投资学.pdf#page=94|证券投资学 PDF 94–95 页]]保留的是总收益除以年数的单利年化口径，不是一般 YTM。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $P_0$ be the initial dirty price. Receive coupon $C$ after one year and sell at the post-coupon price $P_1$, ignoring taxes and costs. Then $R=(C+P_1-P_0)/P_0=C/P_0+(P_1-P_0)/P_0$. Both components use **this holding period's initial price**, not an unchanging historical cost. Other horizons and interim coupon dates require a new cash-flow schedule. Simple annualized total return also differs from realized compound annual return. See [[到期收益率与实现收益|YTM versus realized return]]; Securities Investment PDF pages 94–95 use simple annualization, not general YTM.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

Because of [[04_理解利率#案例:|interest-rate changes]], the prices and returns of long-term bonds are more volatile than those of short-term bonds, and the sensitivity rises with maturity.

# 实际利率与名义利率的区别
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Difference Between Real Interest Rates and Nominal Interest Rates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 名义利率 nominal interest rate 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*nominal interest rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

获得的利率,拿到的钱的数字
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **nominal interest rate** is the quoted rate before adjusting for inflation; it measures the increase in money rather than the increase in purchasing power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 实际利率real interest rate
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

The nominal interest rate (i) is approximately the real interest rate (r) plus expected inflation (π^{e}), as shown in the formula above. This approximation is suitable when inflation is not very high and should not be used mechanically under hyperinflation.

The lower the real interest rate, the stronger borrowers' incentive to obtain funds and the weaker lenders' incentive to supply them.
