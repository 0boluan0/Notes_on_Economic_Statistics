# # 资产需求的决定因素(Determinants of Asset Demand)

## 财富(Wealth)

 假定其他因素不变， 财富的增长会增加对资产的需求数量。(Assuming other factors remain unchanged, an increase in wealth will increase the quantity of assets demanded.)

## 预期回报率(Expected Return)

预期下个阶段的回报率，一般考虑一种资产相对于其他替代资产的预期回报率(Expected return for the next period, generally considering the expected return of one asset relative to other alternative assets)
相当于投资这个资产的机会成本。(Equivalent to the opportunity cost of investing in this asset.)
 在其他因素不变的情况下， 某一资产相对于其他替代性资产的预期回报率上升会增加对该资产的需求数量。(Other factors remaining unchanged, an increase in the expected return of one asset relative to other alternative assets will increase the quantity demanded of that asset.)
## 风险(Risk)

回报的不确定程度(The degree of uncertainty in returns)
假定其他因素不变， 如果一种资产相对于其他替代性资产的风险增大. 则对该资产的需求数 相应减少(Assuming other factors remain unchanged, if the risk of an asset relative to other alternative assets increases, the quantity demanded of that asset will decrease accordingly)

## 流动性(Liquidity)

资产转化为现金的容易程度和速度(The ease and speed with which an asset can be converted into cash)
假定其他因素不变， 一种资产相对于其他替代性资产的流动性越强， 就越受到人们的青睐， 对它的需求量就越多。(Assuming other factors remain unchanged, the more liquid an asset is relative to other alternative assets, the more popular it becomes and the greater the quantity demanded.)

# 流动性偏好理论:货币市场的供给和需求(Liquidity Preference Theory: Supply and Demand in the Money Market)

## 流动性偏好理论(Liquidity Preference Framework)

是确定均衡利率水平的另外一个模型.这一模型用货而的供给与需求而作债券的供给与需求来说明均衡利率的决定.(This is another model for determining the equilibrium interest rate level. It uses the supply and demand of money instead of bonds to explain the determination of the equilibrium interest rate.)  

假定人们用来储存财富的资产有两类,货币和债券,经济体的财富总量等于债券总量和货币总量之和,且供给和需求相等.(Assume that there are two types of assets people use to store wealth: money and bonds. The total wealth of the economy equals the sum of total bonds and money, and supply equals demand.)
$$
B^s+M^s=B^d+M^d
$$
变换后得到
$$
B^d-B^s=M^d-M^s
$$
这个式子表明,如果货币市场均衡,债券市场也将均衡,所以不管从哪个方面分析最终的结果相同.(This equation shows that if the money market is in equilibrium, the bond market will also be in equilibrium, so the final results will be the same regardless of which aspect is analyzed.)

## 货币市场均衡

### 机会成本(Opportunity Cost)

没有持有替代性资产而舍弃的利息金额(预期回报)(The amount of interest (expected return) foregone by not holding alternative assets)

### 货币市场均衡(Money Market Equilibrium)

![[Pasted image 20240514155913.png]]

假设:中央银行控制货币在一个固定的值,所以是一条垂直线,和需求曲线相交的位置就是均衡利率.(Assumption: The central bank controls the money supply at a fixed value, so it is a vertical line, and the intersection with the demand curve is the equilibrium interest rate.)

==注意,货币市场上不存在利率这个东西,这里的完整解释应该是人们的货币持有量高于预期货币持有量,多出来的那部分钱被用来买债券,买的人多了利率就降了==.(==Note: There is no concept of "interest rate" in the money market. A complete explanation here is that if people's money holdings are higher than their expected money holdings, the excess money will be used to buy bonds. The increased buying will lower interest rates.==)

## 货币需求曲线的位移(Shift in Money Demand Curve)

### 收入效应(Income Effect)

第一. 随着经济的扩张与收入的增加. 财富增长. 人们愿意持有更多的货币来储藏价值.(First: As the economy expands and income increases, wealth grows, and people are willing to hold more money to store value.) 
第二，随着经济的扩张与收入的增加. 人们愿意利用货币这 一交易媒介进行更多的交易. 于是他们就希望持有更多的货币. 结论是收入水平上升导致任一利率水平上的货币需求增加， 推动需求曲线向右位移(Second: As the economy expands and income increases, people are willing to use money as a medium of exchange for more transactions, so they want to hold more money. The conclusion is that an increase in income level leads to an increase in money demand at any interest rate level, shifting the demand curve to the right.)

# 货币和利率(Money and Interest Rates)

## 流动性效应(Liquidity Effect)

弗里德曼认为流动性偏好理论是正确的,但是不完整.他将所有其他条件相同的情况下货币供给增加使得利率降低的结果称为流动性效应,但是他认为流动性效应只是问题的一部分,增加货币供给可能无法保证所有的其他条件均相同,那么其他的经济效应或许会使利率上升,如果这些效应足够大,完全可能出现货币供给增加利率也上升的情况.(Friedman believed that the liquidity preference theory is correct but incomplete. He called the result of money supply increasing and interest rates decreasing under all other equal conditions the "liquidity effect," but he thought this was only part of the story. Increasing the money supply may not ensure all other conditions remain the same, and other economic effects might cause interest rates to rise. If these effects are strong enough, it is entirely possible for interest rates to rise as the money supply increases.)

## 预期通货膨胀效应(Expected Inflation Effect)

货币供给增加使得预期货币通胀率提高引起利率上升.只有物价水平持续上涨预期通货膨胀理论才存在.(An increase in the money supply raises the expected inflation rate, which causes interest rates to rise. The expected inflation theory only exists if the price level continues to rise.)

## 综合(Synthesis)

流动性效应最快,别的比较慢(The liquidity effect is the fastest, while other effects are slower.)
