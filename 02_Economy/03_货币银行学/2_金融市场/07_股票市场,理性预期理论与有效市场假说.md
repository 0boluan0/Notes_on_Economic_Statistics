## 单阶段估值模型(One-Period Valuation Model)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*One-Period Valuation Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设投资时间为1年,那么把一年后收到的股利和卖出的价格都贴现到今天,可以估出股票的现值.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assuming an investment horizon of 1 year, discounting both the dividend received in 1 year and the sale price back to today gives an estimate of the stock's present value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P_0=\frac{D_1}{1+k_e}+\frac{P_1}{1+k_e}
$$

## 推广的股利估值模型(Generalized Dividend Valuation Model)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Generalized Dividend Valuation Model (GDMV)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利用单阶段估值模型,可以推广到任意多多阶段的模型,将任意期限的股票的股利和最终偿还的金额全部贴现到现在得到现值P_0.
$$
P_0=\frac{D_1}{(1+k_e)^1}+\frac{D_2}{(1+k_e)^2}+…+\frac{D_n
}{(1+k_e)^n}+\frac{P_1}{(1+k_e)^n}
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The one-period valuation model extends to any investment horizon: discount every dividend and the final sale price back to the present to obtain $P_0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果时间足够长,那么最终偿还的金额将无意义,被省略掉,现值为股利的无限期的贴现值
$$
P_0=\sum_{t=1}^{∞}\frac{D_t}{(1+k_e)^t}
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If time is sufficient, the amount ultimately repaid will be meaningless and can be omitted, leaving the present value as the infinite-period discounted value of dividends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 理性预期理论adaptive expectation
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Rational Expectations Theory (adaptive expectation)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预期应与利用所有可得信息作出的最优预测(optimal forecast)相一致.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An expectation should coincide with the optimal forecast formed from all available information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理性预期等于基于所有可得信息的最优预测,但是这一预测结果并不是完全精准的.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rational expectations are optimal predictions based on all available information, but this prediction is not perfectly accurate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 理性预期理论的规范表达
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Normative Expression of the Rational Expectations Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对X的预期等于基于所有可得信息的最优预测
$$
X^e=X^{of}
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The expectation of $X$ equals the optimal forecast based on all available information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Xe代表对X的预期,Xof利用所有可得信息对X的最优预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$X^e$ denotes the expectation of $X$, while $X^{of}$ denotes the optimal forecast of $X$ based on all available information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 理性预期理论的依据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Basis of the Rational Expectations Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果不这么做就会付出大代价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Failure to do so would come at a great cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 理性预期理论的含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Meaning of Rational Expectations Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 如果某一变量的运动方式发生变化,那么对该变量预期形成的方式也要随之改变
2. 预期的预测误差平均为0,且事先无法预知.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** If the manner of a certain variable's movement changes, then the expected formation for that variable should also change accordingly.<br>
**2.** The average prediction error is 0, and it cannot be foreseen beforehand.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 有效市场假说efficient market hypothesis:金融市场中的理性预期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Efficient Market Hypothesis: Rational Expectations in Financial Markets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Assumption*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金融市场中证券价格反映了所有可得信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the financial market, the price of securities reflects all available information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 内容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Content*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

持有某一债券的回报率等于证券资本利得(证券价格波动)与现金收入(股利)的总和除以证券最初的购买价格
$$
R=\frac{P_{t+1}-P_t+C}{P_t}
$$
但是Pt+1未知,所以用期望算
$$
R^e=\frac{P_{t+1}^e-P_t+C}{P_t}
$$
同时有效市场假说认为
$$
P_{t+1}^e=P_{t+1}^{of}
$$
那么就有
$$
R^e=R^{of}
$$
且第五章中说证券的预期回报率有向其均衡回报率运动的趋势,即
$$
R^e=R^*
$$
那么有
$$
R^{of}=R^e=R^*
$$
语言表达为:金融市场中的现价水平应当使得根据所有可得信息对证券回报率所做的最优预测等于证券的回报率.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The return from holding a security equals its capital gain (the change in price) plus its cash income (the dividend), divided by the security's initial purchase price.

Because $P_{t+1}$ is unknown, the calculation uses its expected value. The efficient market hypothesis states that the expected future price is the optimal forecast based on all available information, so the expected return equals the optimally forecast return. Chapter 5 also states that a security's expected return tends toward its equilibrium return. Combining these relationships gives $R^{of}=R^e=R^*$.

In words, the current price in a financial market should be such that the optimal forecast of the security's return, based on all available information, equals its equilibrium return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Arbitrage can be divided into pure arbitrage, which is risk-free, and risk arbitrage, whose risk is very small relative to its return.

### 另一个表述
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Another expression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在有效市场中,所有未被利用的盈利机会都会被消除.这并不要求市场中的每一个人都是精明的,只要有精明的投资者追逐利润,就能消除所有的无风险套利机会.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an efficient market, all unexploited profit opportunities are eliminated. This does not require everyone in the market to be sophisticated; it is enough for some sophisticated investors to pursue profits and eliminate every risk-free arbitrage opportunity.
