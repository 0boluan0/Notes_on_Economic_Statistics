通常情况下认为国债没有违约风险,所以讲国债这种没有违约风险的债券叫做无违约风险债券(default- free bond)

## 流动性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*liquidity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

流动性资产是指在其必要时能够低成本迅速转换为现金,资产的流动性越强就越受欢迎.企业债券较差的流动性导致了它与国债的利差.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Liquid assets are those that can be quickly and cheaply converted to cash when needed; the more liquid an asset is, the more desirable it becomes. The poorer liquidity of corporate bonds has contributed to a yield spread between them and government bonds.

# 利率的期限结构([[利率、即期远期与期限结构|Term Structure of Interest Rates]])
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The term structure of interest rates (Term Structure of Interest Rates)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 收益率曲线yield curve([[利率、即期远期与期限结构|Yield Curve]])
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Yield curve (Yield Curve)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具有相同风险,流动性和税收特征的债券,由于距离到期日的时间点不同,利率也会有差异,不同期限债券利率之间的这种关系被称作利率的期限结构([[利率、即期远期与期限结构|term structure of interest rates]]),将到期期限不同其他一切相同的债券的收益率链接成一条曲线,即得到了收益率曲线.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bonds with the same risk, liquidity, and tax characteristics can have different interest rates because their times to maturity differ. This relationship among the interest rates on bonds of different maturities is called the term structure of interest rates. Plotting the yields of otherwise identical bonds against their maturities produces the yield curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收益率曲线可以是向上倾斜的，平坦的和向下倾斜的，向下倾斜的一半被叫做翻转的收益率曲线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A yield curve can slope upward, be flat, or slope downward. A downward-sloping curve is generally called an inverted yield curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个典型“向上倾斜”的收益率曲线示例：短端（1m~6m）接近 0%，1y 约 0.1%，2y 约 0.5%，5y 约 1.7%，10y 约 2.3%，20y 约 3.0%，30y 约 3.4%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A typical "upward-sloping" yield curve example: short end (1m~6m) near 0%, 1y around 0.1%, 2y around 0.5%, 5y around 1.7%, 10y around 2.3%, 20y around 3.0%, and 30y around 3.4%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 经验事实
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Empirical facts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

后面我们想要用三种理论去拟合这三个经验事实
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The following section uses three theories to account for these three empirical facts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
 
1. 不同期限的债券的利率随时间一起变动
2. 短期利率比较低时收益率曲线更有可能向上倾斜,反之则向下.
3. 收益曲线几乎总是向上倾斜的.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** The interest rates of bonds with different maturities vary together over time.<br>
**2.** When short-term interest rates are lower, it is more likely for the yield curve to slope upward; conversely, it is more likely to slope downward.<br>
**3.** The yield curve is almost always upward-sloping.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三种解释利率期限结构的理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three theories of the term structure of interest rates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 预期理论expectation theory
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*expectation theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长期债券利率将等于债券有效期内预期短期利率的平均值.预期理论认为不同期限债券具有不同的利率,原因在于未来日期的短期利率预期值不同.  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Long-term bond rates will equal the average of expected short-term interest rates over the life of the bond. The expectations theory posits that different-maturity bonds carry different yields because expected future short-term interest rates differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键假定:债券购买者对于债券期限没有特别的偏好.  这代表期限不同的债券是完全替代品
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Key Assumption: Bond purchasers have no particular preference for bond maturities. This implies that bonds of different maturities are perfect substitutes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
 
算短期债券和长期债券的利率关系时,应该用加权平均,但事实上使用算数平均,二阶小量被省略了.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strictly speaking, compounding the relevant short-term rates requires a weighted or geometric calculation. The familiar arithmetic-average formula is a first-order approximation that drops second-order terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以解释经验事实1,2,但是不能解释3.3和2冲突,
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theory can explain facts 1 and 2, but not fact 3; facts 2 and 3 pull in opposite directions under this account.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 分割市场理论segmented markets theory
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*segmented markets theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完全与预期理论对立的理论.将到期期限不同的债券市场看作完全独立和相互分割的.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A theory completely contrary to the expectations theory, viewing the bond markets with different maturities as entirely independent and mutually segmented.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只能解释事实3,因为人们便好期限短的,因为利率风险小,所以长期的就得利率大.但是完全无法解释事实1,2.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It can explain only fact 3: people prefer shorter maturities because they carry less interest-rate risk, so long-term bonds must offer higher yields. It cannot explain facts 1 and 2.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 流动性溢价理论liquidity premium theory
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*liquidity premium theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

综合了上述的两个理论,认为长期债券的价格取决于两项之和,第一项是长期债券到期之前预期短期利率的平均值,第二项是随债券供求情况变动而变动的流动性溢价(又叫期限溢价).
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Combining these two theories, it is argued that the price of a long-term bond depends on the sum of two components: the average expected short-term interest rate before the bond’s maturity, and a liquidity premium (also known as a term premium) that fluctuates according to changes in supply and demand for the bond.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以解释三个事实
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It can explain all three facts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
