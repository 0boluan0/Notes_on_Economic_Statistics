Default risk for bonds refers to the issuer's inability or unwillingness to fulfill its previous commitments of paying interest or repaying the face value at maturity. This is a significant factor affecting bond rates. When a company suffers substantial losses, it may delay interest payments.

### 无违约风险债券(default-free bond)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Default-Free Bonds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通常情况下认为国债没有违约风险,所以讲国债这种没有违约风险的债券叫做无违约风险债券(default- free bond)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Government bonds are generally treated as having no default risk. A bond with no default risk is therefore called a default-free bond.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start C01 -->
> [!note] 校注：无违约基准是一项设定
> 课堂以特定国债近似无违约基准，不代表所有国家、币种或债务安排的政府债都无违约风险；也不代表没有利率、通胀或流动性风险。比较时应指明所选发行人、币种、期限与基准，见 [[无风险利率口径]]。[[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=112|CPA 印刷 104 页]]的发行人分类也明确提到部分外国政府债存在违约风险。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The classroom treats a specified government bond as approximately default-free. That is not a claim about every sovereign, currency, or debt arrangement, nor does it eliminate rate, inflation, or liquidity risk. Identify issuer, currency, maturity, and benchmark; see [[无风险利率口径|risk-free-rate conventions]]. CPA printed page 104 explicitly notes that some foreign-government bonds carry default risk.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 风险溢价([[信用利差|risk premium]])
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Risk premium (risk premium)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到期时间相同的情况下,无违约风险债券和有违约风险债券的利差叫做风险溢价risk premium.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For bonds with the same maturity, the yield spread between a default-free bond and a bond carrying default risk is called the risk premium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start C02 -->
> [!note] 校注：保留课程命名，分清观测利差与纯风险补偿
> 本段把有违约风险债券相对基准的 [[信用利差]] 称为 risk premium；这里是课程命名，不应直接等同于仅补偿不可分散风险的“纯风险溢价”。即使期限相同，观测利差仍可能混合预期信用损失、风险补偿、流动性、税收与合同差异；比较还需匹配币种、结算与收益率报价。[[信用利差与违约概率]]说明它也不是违约概率，不能把基点数直接当作百分比 PD。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This paragraph calls the risky-bond [[信用利差|credit spread]] over a benchmark a “risk premium.” Preserve that course terminology, but distinguish the observed spread from compensation solely for non-diversifiable risk. Equal maturity does not remove expected credit loss, risk pricing, liquidity, tax, or contractual effects; currency, settlement, and yield quotations must also match. [[信用利差与违约概率|Credit spreads versus default probabilities]] explains why a basis-point spread is not directly a percentage PD.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

Baa(BBB)投资级债券,低于这个水平的叫做垃圾债券(junk bond)或者叫做投机级债券.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BBB-rated (Baa) bonds are considered investment-grade; anything below this is called junk bonds (junk bond) or speculative-grade bonds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start C03 -->
> [!note] 校注：大类与具体评级
> BBB 与 Baa 是不同评级体系下的最低投资级**大类**；读取实际评级还须保留加减号或数字子等级，不能把两机构记号机械拼成一个分值。评级是信用意见，不是履约保证，也不能单独确定 [[信用利差]]；评级迁移的口径见 [[评级迁移矩阵]]。[SEC 信用评级说明](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/updated-8)核对机构自己的评级符号、BBB− 门槛与评级局限。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> BBB and Baa are the lowest investment-grade **categories** in different rating systems. Preserve agency-specific modifiers or numerical subgrades when reading an actual rating rather than merging the scales. A rating is a credit opinion, not a payment guarantee or a complete explanation of a [[信用利差|credit spread]]. See [[评级迁移矩阵|rating-transition matrices]] for migration conventions. The SEC credit-ratings bulletin explains agency-specific symbols, the BBB− threshold, and rating limitations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 流动性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*liquidity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

流动性资产是指在其必要时能够低成本迅速转换为现金,资产的流动性越强就越受欢迎.企业债券较差的流动性导致了它与国债的利差.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Liquid assets are those that can be quickly and cheaply converted to cash when needed; the more liquid an asset is, the more desirable it becomes. The poorer liquidity of corporate bonds has contributed to a yield spread between them and government bonds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start C04 -->
> [!note] 校注：流动性是利差的一种成分
> 企业债与国债的流动性差异可以影响利差，但不是任意两只债券利差的唯一原因，也不能由发行人类别直接确定其大小。应与前后的信用、税收、期限和条款条件一起比较；这正是 [[利率风险结构]] 与 [[信用利差与违约概率]] 的区分。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Corporate–government liquidity differences can affect spreads, but they are not the sole cause of every bond pair's spread, and issuer category alone does not determine the amount. Compare credit, tax, maturity, and contractual terms as well; see [[利率风险结构|the risk structure of interest rates]] and [[信用利差与违约概率|credit-spread identification limits]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市政债券利率低于国债,因为市政债券不用交联邦所得税,所以低.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The municipal bond interest rate is lower than that of a federal government bond because municipal bonds are exempt from federal taxes, hence the lower rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start C05 -->
> [!note] 校注：税收比较有市场与投资人条件
> 这里是美国免税市政债的教学情景，不是所有市政债或所有投资人的统一税制，也不推出市政债利率必低于国债。[SEC Investor.gov 的市政债说明](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/bonds-or-fixed-income-products-0)将税收优惠与期限、信用等可比条件一起说明：免税可以使投资人接受较低税前收益率；具体债券仍要查税收待遇与其他风险。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is a teaching example of U.S. tax-exempt municipal debt, not a universal tax rule for every municipal bond or investor, nor proof that municipal yields must lie below Treasury yields. [SEC Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/bonds-or-fixed-income-products-0) discusses tax benefits alongside comparable maturity and credit conditions: exemption can make a lower pre-tax yield acceptable, but actual tax treatment and other risks must still be checked.

## 收益率曲线yield curve([[利率期限结构|Yield Curve]])
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Yield curve (Yield Curve)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具有相同风险、流动性、税收与合同口径的债券，仍会因到期日不同而有不同利率；这种跨期限关系称作 [[利率期限结构|利率的期限结构]]。把其他条件可比的利率按期限排列就得到收益率曲线，但还必须说明画的是零息即期率、远期率、平价率还是具体债券的到期收益率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bonds with comparable risk, liquidity, tax, and contractual terms can still have different rates because their maturities differ. This relationship is the [[利率期限结构|term structure of interest rates]]. A yield curve orders comparable rates by maturity, but it must still be identified as a zero-coupon spot, forward, par, or individual-bond YTM curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收益率曲线可以是向上倾斜的，平坦的和向下倾斜的，向下倾斜的一半被叫做翻转的收益率曲线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A yield curve can slope upward, be flat, or slope downward. A downward-sloping curve is generally called an inverted yield curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个典型“向上倾斜”的收益率曲线示例：短端（1m~6m）接近 0%，1y 约 0.1%，2y 约 0.5%，5y 约 1.7%，10y 约 2.3%，20y 约 3.0%，30y 约 3.4%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A typical "upward-sloping" yield curve example: short end (1m~6m) near 0%, 1y around 0.1%, 2y around 0.5%, 5y around 1.7%, 10y around 2.3%, 20y around 3.0%, and 30y around 3.4%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 经验事实
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Empirical facts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

后面我们想要用三种理论去拟合这三个经验事实
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The following section uses three theories to account for these three empirical facts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
 
1. 不同期限的债券的利率随时间一起变动
2. 短期利率比较低时收益率曲线更有可能向上倾斜,反之则向下.
3. 在许多历史样本中，向上倾斜的曲线比倒挂更常见，但并非恒常状态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** The interest rates of bonds with different maturities vary together over time.<br>
**2.** When short-term interest rates are lower, it is more likely for the yield curve to slope upward; conversely, it is more likely to slope downward.<br>
**3.** In many historical samples, upward-sloping curves are more common than inversions, but they are not a permanent state.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三种解释利率期限结构的理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three theories of the term structure of interest rates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### [[预期假说|预期理论]] expectation theory
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*expectation theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长期债券利率将等于债券有效期内预期短期利率的平均值.预期理论认为不同期限债券具有不同的利率,原因在于未来日期的短期利率预期值不同.  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Long-term bond rates will equal the average of expected short-term interest rates over the life of the bond. The expectations theory posits that different-maturity bonds carry different yields because expected future short-term interest rates differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键假定:债券购买者对于债券期限没有特别的偏好.  这代表期限不同的债券是完全替代品
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Key Assumption: Bond purchasers have no particular preference for bond maturities. This implies that bonds of different maturities are perfect substitutes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
 
算短期债券和长期债券的利率关系时,应该用加权平均,但事实上使用算数平均,二阶小量被省略了.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strictly speaking, compounding the relevant short-term rates requires a weighted or geometric calculation. The familiar arithmetic-average formula is a first-order approximation that drops second-order terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以解释经验事实1,2,但是不能解释3.3和2冲突,
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theory can explain facts 1 and 2, but not fact 3; facts 2 and 3 pull in opposite directions under this account.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### [[市场分割|分割市场理论]] segmented markets theory
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*segmented markets theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完全与预期理论对立的理论.将到期期限不同的债券市场看作完全独立和相互分割的.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A theory completely contrary to the expectations theory, viewing the bond markets with different maturities as entirely independent and mutually segmented.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只能解释事实3,因为人们便好期限短的,因为利率风险小,所以长期的就得利率大.但是完全无法解释事实1,2.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It can explain only fact 3: people prefer shorter maturities because they carry less interest-rate risk, so long-term bonds must offer higher yields. It cannot explain facts 1 and 2.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### [[流动性溢价理论]] liquidity premium theory
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*liquidity premium theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

综合前面的预期路径与期限供求，认为长期债券的收益率可近似写成两项之和：到期前预期短期利率的平均路径，加上通常为正且随期限增加的流动性溢价。这个经典理论限制不等于现代模型中的 [[期限溢价]] 必然为正。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Combining the expected short-rate path with maturity-specific demand, the theory approximates a long yield as the average expected short rate over the bond's life plus a liquidity premium that is usually assumed positive and increasing with maturity. This classical restriction does not imply that every modern estimate of the [[期限溢价|term premium]] must be positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以解释三个事实
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It can explain all three facts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

an 流动 性 滋 价 理论 ( 期 限 优先 理论 ) - RN 流动 性 滋 价 1 需 期 理论 的 收益 率 曲 线 0 5 10 15 20 25 30 到 期 期 限 n
