*Content Composition*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*check deposits*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

允许账户持有人向第三方签发支票的银行账户
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bank accounts that allow account holders to issue checks to third parties

#### 非交易存款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Non-trading Deposits*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

储蓄账户和定期存款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Savings Accounts and Term Deposits
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 借款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Borrowing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行可向央行、同业或资本市场融资。央行贷款的名称、资格和条件依赖法域；“federal funds”特指美国合格机构之间的相关余额交易，不是全球同业借款的通称。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Banks can fund through central banks, other institutions, or capital markets. Central-bank facility names and conditions depend on jurisdiction. “Federal funds” is a U.S.-specific market term, not a universal name for interbank borrowing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 银行资本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bank Capital*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行资本在这里指会计权益，即总资产减总负债。它常列在资产负债表的“负债与权益”一侧，但不是欠股东的一笔普通负债；能否计入 CET1、AT1 或 Tier 2 还要看监管资本规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bank capital here means accounting equity, the difference between total assets and total liabilities. It is presented on the liabilities-and-equity side, but is not an ordinary liability owed to shareholders. Eligibility for CET1, AT1, or Tier 2 is a separate regulatory question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 资产
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Assets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*meaning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产是银行控制、预期带来经济利益的资源。银行可以通过吸收存款、借款、发行资本以及贷款创造存款等方式扩表；利润也不只来自资产利息，还要同时看利息费用、非息收支、信用成本、估值和税费。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assets are resources controlled by the bank and expected to provide economic benefits. A bank can expand through deposits, borrowing, capital issuance, and loan-created deposits. Profit is not merely asset interest: interest expense, non-interest items, credit cost, valuation, and tax also matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 构成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Composition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### [[央行准备金余额|准备金]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Reserves*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### [[法定准备金口径|法定准备金要求]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Reserve requirements*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

法定准备金要求是特定法域在特定时期针对特定存款机构和负债设置的规则，不能写成所有银行永远按同一比率把支票存款存回央行。截至 2026-08-29，美联储官方口径仍显示美国准备金要求比率自 2020-03-26 起为 0；历史教材中的 10% 比率只适合标明制度和假设的教学模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reserve requirements are jurisdiction- and date-specific rules applied to specified depository institutions and liabilities; they are not a timeless rule requiring every bank to redeposit the same fraction of checking accounts at the central bank. As checked on 29 August 2026, the Federal Reserve still reports a zero reserve-requirement ratio effective since 26 March 2020. The historical 10% ratio belongs only to a teaching model with its regime and assumptions stated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

##### 超额准备金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Excess Reserves*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在正法定比率制度中，超过最低要求的余额才叫超额准备金。它仍是银行持有的央行结算资产，不是为某位储户逐笔封存的现金。美国现行法定比率为 0 后，旧式“法定/超额”二分不再承担原先的操作含义；银行仍会为支付、流动性管理和利率环境持有准备金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a regime with a positive requirement, balances above the minimum are called excess reserves. They remain central-bank settlement assets held by the bank, not cash earmarked one-for-one for a particular depositor. With the current U.S. requirement at zero, the old required/excess split no longer has its former operational role, although banks still hold reserves for payments, liquidity management, and the interest-rate framework.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 托收中款项
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cash Items in the Process of Collection*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

托收中款项是已入账但结算资金尚未最终收到的应收项目，例如他行支票从客户入账到跨行结算之间的过渡资产；最终应沿实际清算与结算链确认。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash items in collection are receivables already recorded while final settlement funds are still pending, such as the interval between crediting a deposited cheque and interbank settlement. Trace the actual clearing and settlement chain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 银行同业存款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Deposits at Other Banks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存放同业是本行在其他银行持有的存款或往来余额，因此是本行对其他银行的资产。向同业借入的资金则是负债，不能把二者混为一项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deposits at other banks are this bank's claims on other banks and therefore assets. Funds borrowed from other banks are liabilities; the two must not be conflated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 证券
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Securities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证券资产可包括主权、地方政府、机构、公司和其他合格证券，具体构成因银行和法域而异。历史银行管理教材把某些短期、高流动性证券称为“二级准备”，这是管理术语，不是央行账户准备金，也不保证它们在压力中能按账面价值立即出售。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Securities may include sovereign, municipal, agency, corporate, and other eligible instruments, depending on the bank and jurisdiction. Some historical textbooks call short-term liquid securities “secondary reserves.” That is a management term, not central-bank reserve balances, and it does not guarantee immediate sale at book value under stress.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 贷款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Loans*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

贷款常是银行重要的生息资产，但是否贡献利润取决于利率、融资与运营成本、预期信用损失、资本占用和实际回收。发放贷款本身不等于已经赚得利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Loans are often major earning assets, but their profit contribution depends on pricing, funding and operating costs, expected credit losses, capital use, and recoveries. Origination itself is not realized profit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 其他
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*other*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

营业用建筑、设备与其他经营性资产
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Premises, equipment, and other operating assets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 银行的基本业务
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basic Banking Operations*

## 资产转换asset transformation
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*asset transformation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产转换是银行向资金提供者发行一类索取权，同时持有风险、期限、规模和流动性特征不同的资产。它可提供期限、流动性与信用转换服务，但收入必须覆盖融资、运营、信用损失、资本和流动性成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Asset transformation means issuing one set of claims to funders while holding assets with different risk, maturity, size, and liquidity characteristics. It provides maturity, liquidity, and credit-transformation services, but revenue must cover funding, operations, credit loss, capital, and liquidity costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## T账户书写过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Process of T-Account Writing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**1. 现金存入。** Jane 把 100 现金存入第一国民银行：银行的库存现金资产 +100，客户存款负债 +100。这是实物通货转成存款；在历史上把库存现金计入准备的教材口径下，银行的准备与存款同增。它不能概括贷款创造的存款。
![[Pasted image 20240524203957.png]]
**2. 他行支票存入。** 若 Jane 存入一张由第二国民银行付款的 100 支票，第一国民银行先记应收现金项目 +100、存款 +100。清算后，第一国民银行准备金 +100，第二国民银行准备金与原付款人存款各 -100。这是存款和准备金的跨行转移：个别银行同增同减，但合并体系的存款与准备金总量都不因这笔纯转账而增加。
![[Pasted image 20240524204104.png]]
**3. 历史 10% 教学模型中的贷款。** 若第一国民银行因上述存款流入持有 100 准备，法定比率为 10%，则 10 是必需准备，90 是模型中可支持扩张的超额准备。银行放贷 90 时的首笔分录是贷款资产 +90、借款人存款 +90，准备金当场不变。若借款人随后向他行付款，始发行的准备金和借款人存款才各 -90，收款行准备金与存款各 +90。因此图中“准备 10、贷款 90、存款 100”是始发行在跨行付款后的终点，不是贷款创造时的分录；准备金从未被“贷给”公众。
![[Pasted image 20240524204430.png]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **A cash deposit.** Suppose Jane Brown deposits $100 in cash at First National Bank. The bank records vault cash +100 and customer deposits +100. This converts physical currency into a deposit. Under the historical textbook convention that counted vault cash as reserves, the bank's reserves and deposits rise together. It is not a general description of a loan-created deposit.<br>

**2.** **A cheque drawn on another bank.** If Jane deposits a $100 cheque drawn on Second National Bank, First National initially records a cash item in collection +100 and a deposit +100. At settlement, First National's reserve balance rises by 100 while Second National's reserve balance and the payer's deposit each fall by 100. This is an interbank transfer of deposits and reserves: the individual banks move in opposite directions, but consolidated banking-system deposits and reserve balances do not rise because of the pure transfer.<br>

**3.** **A loan in the historical 10% model.** If First National has 100 of reserves after the deposit inflow and the required ratio is 10%, the model labels 10 as required and 90 as excess. Loan origination first records loans +90 and borrower deposits +90, with reserves unchanged. If the borrower then pays another bank, First National's reserves and borrower deposit each fall by 90, while the recipient bank's reserves and deposit each rise by 90. The pictured endpoint of reserves 10, loans 90, and deposits 100 at First National is therefore after the outgoing payment, not the origination entry. Reserves were never lent to the public.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行常以可随时支取或较短期的负债支持较长期资产，并从期限与信用利差、服务费等获得收入；这项[[期限转换|期限转换]]同时产生利率、流动性和续融资风险，收益并非无条件成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Banks often support longer-term assets with demandable or shorter-term liabilities and may earn term and credit spreads plus fees. This [[期限转换|maturity transformation]] also creates interest-rate, liquidity, and rollover risk; profit is not automatic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 银行管理的基本原则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basic Principles of Bank Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 四个问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Four questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 流动性管理liquidity management
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*liquidity management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

确保银行在存款流出、结算、承诺提款和保证金等现金需求到来时，能在不承担不可接受损失的情况下按时付款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ensure that deposit outflows, settlement, commitment drawdowns, margin, and other cash needs can be met on time without unacceptable loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 资产管理asset management
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*asset management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在收益、信用风险、利率风险、流动性、集中度和资本占用之间配置贷款与证券，并持续监测风险调整后回报。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Allocate loans and securities across return, credit risk, interest-rate risk, liquidity, concentration, and capital use, and monitor risk-adjusted performance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 负债管理liability management
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*liability management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在成本之外同时管理资金期限、稳定性、币种、提供者集中、抵押品占用和压力期可得性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Manage funding maturity, stability, currency, provider concentration, collateral use, and stressed availability as well as quoted cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 资本充足性管理capital adequacy management
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*capital adequacy management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

确定能覆盖风险、压力损失与内部缓冲的资本规模，并区分会计权益、监管资本工具和各类资本率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Determine capital sufficient for risk, stress loss, and internal buffers while separating accounting equity, eligible regulatory instruments, and the different capital ratios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 流动性管理与准备金的作用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Role of Liquidity Management and Reserves*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是**历史 10% 准备金模型**的比较，不是现行美国要求。初始准备金 20、存款 100、贷款 80、证券 10、权益 10 时，客户跨行转出存款 10 后，付款行准备金和存款各降 10；贷款、证券和权益不因纯支付改变。若初始准备金只有 10，同样流出会使准备金降至 0。历史模型会称其低于存款 90 所需的 9；在现行零准备金要求下则应直接诊断结算、日内与压力流动性，而不是继续套 9 的法定缺口。

![[Pasted image 20240524205608.png]]

![[Pasted image 20240524205723.png]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a comparison inside the **historical 10% required-reserve model**, not a statement of the current U.S. requirement. If the bank starts with reserves 20, deposits 100, loans 80, securities 10, and equity 10, an interbank deposit outflow of 10 reduces the paying bank's reserves and deposits by 10; loans, securities, and equity do not change merely because of the payment. If initial reserves were only 10, the same outflow would leave reserves at zero and deposits at 90. The historical model calls this 9 below the required amount. Under today's zero U.S. reserve requirement, the relevant diagnosis is instead settlement, intraday, and stressed liquidity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

四类应对必须分别写分录、时点和代价：

1. **借入同业或其他市场资金。** 收到结算资金时，准备金等流动资产增加、借款负债增加；代价是利息、期限与续作风险。
   ![[Pasted image 20240524205836.png]]
2. **出售证券。** 证券减少、结算资产按实际成交净额增加；交易费和售价低于账面价值的部分会侵蚀利润与权益。出售不保证按面值或账面价值变现。
   ![[Pasted image 20240524205940.png]]
3. **向美联储借款。** 准备金资产和对美联储借款负债同时增加，并须满足适用便利的资格、抵押、期限和定价。截至 2026-08-29，官方 H.15 的 primary credit rate 为 3.75%；它是否高于某笔同业融资，必须匹配具体基准，见 [[惩罚性利率的比较基准|贴现窗口定价边界]]。
   ![[Pasted image 20240524210015.png]]
4. **让贷款自然回收、出售贷款或压缩新投放。** 到期回收可带来现金，但强迫客户提前还款未必有合同权利；贷款出售取决于买家、价格、结算和是否实现损失。三者不能笼统写成“收回贷款后存入美联储”。
   ![[Pasted image 20240524210112.png]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are four broad ways to cover the liquidity shortfall:

**1.** **Borrow in interbank or other funding markets.** Settlement assets and borrowing liabilities rise together. Interest cost, maturity, and rollover risk remain.<br>
**2.** **Sell securities.** Securities fall and settlement assets rise by actual net proceeds. Fees and any sale below carrying value reduce profit and equity; sale at par is not guaranteed.<br>
**3.** **Borrow from the Federal Reserve.** Reserve assets and Federal Reserve borrowing liabilities rise together, subject to facility eligibility, collateral, maturity, and pricing. As checked against official H.15 on 2026-08-29, primary credit was 3.75%. Comparison with interbank funding requires a matched benchmark; see [[惩罚性利率的比较基准|discount-window pricing boundary]].<br>
**4.** **Receive scheduled loan cash flow, sell loans, or reduce new origination.** Scheduled repayment can provide cash, but a bank may have no contractual right to force early repayment. Loan sale depends on buyers, price, settlement, and realised loss. These are not one accounting action.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

持有较多准备金可以减少临时借款、被迫卖券或收缩贷款的成本，但机会成本不能再写成“一点钱都不赚”。在美国现行框架中，合格准备金余额按 IORB 获息；银行仍要把这项收益和贷款、证券的预期回报、风险及流动性价值一起比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding more reserve balances can reduce the costs of emergency borrowing, forced securities sales, or loan contraction, but their opportunity cost is no longer accurately described as “earning nothing.” Under the current U.S. framework, eligible reserve balances earn IORB; a bank compares that return with expected loan and securities returns, risk, and the liquidity value of reserves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 资产管理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Asset Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 目标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Objective*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

目标是在风险偏好、资本、流动性、监管和客户需求约束下提高风险调整后收益，而不是同时追求“最高回报”和“最低风险”这组通常不可兼得的目标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The objective is to improve risk-adjusted return subject to risk appetite, capital, liquidity, regulation, and customer demand—not to claim that maximum return and minimum risk can generally be obtained together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 四个实现途径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Four Implementation Pathways*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 按借款人现金流、担保、契约与价格筛选能提供合格风险调整后收益的贷款，而非假定高利率借款人不会违约。
2. 按收益、信用、久期、市场流动性和资本占用共同选择证券；高回报通常伴随需要识别的风险。
3. 通过跨借款人、行业、地区和风险因子的分散降低集中损失，但共同冲击仍可能使相关性上升。
4. 管理无负担资产、抵押品、市场渠道和变现时点，避免把“可出售”直接等同于“可无损即时变现”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Select loans with acceptable risk-adjusted return after assessing borrower cash flow, collateral, covenants, and pricing; do not assume high-rate borrowers cannot default.<br>
**2.** Select securities by return, credit, duration, market liquidity, and capital use; higher return normally comes with risks to identify.<br>
**3.** Diversify across borrowers, sectors, regions, and risk factors while recognising that correlation can rise under common stress.<br>
**4.** Manage unencumbered assets, collateral, market channels, and monetisation time without equating “saleable” with “immediately realisable at no loss.”<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 负债管理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Liability Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

负债管理要比较零售存款、批发借款、担保融资、长期债务和权益的成本、期限、可撤回性、币种、提供者集中及抵押品占用。最低票面利率不一定是最低总成本：短期便宜资金可能带来更大的续作和压力流失风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Liability management compares retail deposits, wholesale borrowing, secured funding, long-term debt, and equity by cost, maturity, withdrawal rights, currency, provider concentration, and collateral use. The lowest quoted rate need not be the lowest total cost: cheap short-term funding can create greater rollover and stressed-runoff risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 资本充足性管理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Capital Adequacy Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 三个原因
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three reasons*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **吸收损失。** 两家银行若各确认房地产贷款损失 5，资产和会计权益各减 5；初始权益更厚的银行更可能仍保持正净值。这里影响发生在损失确认时，足额拨备后的核销不应再二次扣权益，见 [[本金、利息与贷款核销]]。
   ![[Pasted image 20240524210939.png]]
2. **影响股东收益与风险。** 权益越薄，其他条件相同时 ROE 的杠杆放大越强，但损失也更快耗尽权益，见 [[ROA、ROE 与杠杆]]。
3. **满足监管与内部缓冲。** 会计权益还要按审慎规则转换成监管资本，并与 RWA、杠杆暴露和压力损失共同检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Absorb losses.** If two banks each recognise a real-estate loan loss of 5, assets and accounting equity each fall by 5; the bank with more initial equity is more likely to retain positive net worth. The effect occurs when the loss is recognised. A later charge-off against an already sufficient allowance must not reduce equity a second time; see [[本金、利息与贷款核销|repayment, interest, and charge-off entries]].<br>
**2.** **Shape shareholder return and risk.** Other things equal, thinner equity amplifies ROE through leverage, but losses also exhaust that equity faster; see [[ROA、ROE 与杠杆|ROA, ROE, and leverage]].<br>
**3.** **Meet regulatory and internal buffers.** Accounting equity must be translated into eligible regulatory capital and checked against RWA, leverage exposure, and stressed loss.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


# 信用风险管理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*credit risk Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信用风险管理包括承销、定价、限额、担保与契约、持续监测、风险分类、预期信用损失准备和回收。贷款利率必须覆盖预期损失与资本成本；分散能降低个体集中，却不能消除共同宏观冲击。量化入口见 [[信用风险参数与模型.canvas|信用风险参数与模型]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Credit-risk management covers underwriting, pricing, limits, collateral and covenants, monitoring, classification, expected-credit-loss allowance, and recovery. Loan pricing must cover expected loss and capital cost. Diversification reduces idiosyncratic concentration but not common macro shocks; see [[信用风险参数与模型.canvas|credit-risk parameters and models]].

# 利率风险管理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*interest-rate risk Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 利率风险问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*interest-rate risk Questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利率变化会通过重定价、收益率曲线、基差和客户选择权影响净利息收入（NII）与权益经济价值（EVE）。两种视角必须分开，入口见 [[IRRBB 的 NII 与 EVE]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rate changes affect net interest income (NII) and economic value of equity (EVE) through repricing, yield-curve, basis, and customer-option channels. Keep the two perspectives separate; see [[IRRBB 的 NII 与 EVE|the IRRBB entry]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 内容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Content*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在指定时间桶、近似平行且同幅的重定价冲击、资产负债规模不变等简化条件下，若利率敏感负债大于利率敏感资产，利率上升通常压低该规划期的 NII；真实结果还取决于存款 beta、基差、曲线形状、上下限和新业务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within a specified bucket and under simplified assumptions such as a common parallel repricing shock and a static balance sheet, rate-sensitive liabilities above rate-sensitive assets normally make rising rates reduce NII over that horizon. Deposit beta, basis, curve shape, floors, caps, and new business can change the result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 利率敏感性缺口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Interest Sensitivity Gap*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利率敏感性资产-利率敏感型负债
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interest Rate Sensitivity Assets - Interest Rate Sensitive Liabilities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正缺口表示该时间桶内利率敏感资产较多；在上述简化冲击下，加息倾向于提高该规划期 NII。它不是对总利润或 EVE 的无条件方向判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A positive gap means more rate-sensitive assets in that bucket. Under the simplified shock above, rising rates tend to raise NII over that horizon. It is not an unconditional direction for total profit or EVE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 缺口分析gap analysis
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*gap analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在静态资产负债表、同幅重定价和忽略基差/选择权等强假设下，$\Delta NII\approx GAP\times\Delta r$ 只是一阶近似。它不能“立即得到银行利润”，也不能替代 EVE、Duration Gap 或完整行为情景。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under strong assumptions—a static balance sheet, common repricing shock, and no basis or option effects—$\Delta NII\approx GAP\times\Delta r$ is only a first-order approximation. It does not directly give total bank profit or replace EVE, duration gap, and behavioural scenarios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 久期分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Duration Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $D_{mod}$ 是修正久期且收益率发生小幅平行变化，一阶近似为
$$\frac{\Delta P}{P}\approx-D_{mod}\Delta y.$$
若使用麦考利久期，则须先除以 $1+y$ 转为修正久期。久期近似不适合把很大的非平行冲击当作精确值。
课堂例：若把资产久期 3、负债久期 2 都解释为修正久期，资产 100、负债 90，在收益率曲线平行上移 5 个百分点的一阶近似下，资产价值约降 15、负债价值约降 9，EVE 约降 6。由于 500bp 是大冲击，凸性、曲线形状和客户行为都会使这个结果只适合作为方向性演示；完整计算见 [[久期缺口]]。
![[Pasted image 20240525194718.png]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For modified duration $D_{mod}$ and a small parallel yield change, the first-order approximation is
$$\frac{\Delta P}{P}\approx-D_{mod}\Delta y.$$
Macaulay duration must first be divided by $1+y$ to obtain modified duration. A large non-parallel shock should not be treated as an exact duration result.

Duration analysis applies the weighted-average durations of a financial institution's assets and liabilities. Return to First National Bank and assume that asset duration is 3 years, liability duration is 2 years, assets are $100 million, and liabilities are $90 million, so bank capital equals 10% of assets.

If the stated durations are interpreted as modified durations, the classroom first-order calculation for a parallel five-percentage-point rise gives an asset decline of about 15% and a liability decline of about 10%, so EVE falls by about $6 million. Because a 500-basis-point shock is large, convexity, curve shape, and behavioural changes make this an illustration rather than an exact valuation. Detailed treatment belongs in [[久期缺口|Duration Gap]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 表外业务off-balance-sheet activities
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Off-balance-sheet activities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

表外业务是当前资产负债表主表未按全额资产或负债确认、但可产生未来现金流或风险敞口的安排，例如未提款承诺、担保和部分衍生合约。它们会产生费用、信用转换额、抵押品或公允价值项目，并可能在触发或结算后进入表内；“表外”不等于没有资产负债表影响或无需资本。未提款承诺还会在客户实际提款时同时占用现金和资金，因而必须进入压力流动性与应急融资分析，见 [[风险加权资本率|表外项目的资本口径]]和 [[流动性压力管理闭环|承诺提款压力]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Off-balance-sheet business includes arrangements not currently recognised at full notional as assets or liabilities, such as undrawn commitments, guarantees, and some derivatives. They can generate fees, credit-conversion amounts, collateral or fair-value entries and can move on balance sheet when triggered or settled. “Off balance sheet” does not mean no balance-sheet effect or no capital requirement. An undrawn commitment can also consume cash and funding when the customer draws it, so it belongs in stressed-liquidity and contingency-funding analysis; see [[风险加权资本率|the capital treatment of off-balance-sheet items]] and [[流动性压力管理闭环|commitment-draw stress]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 贷款出售loan sale
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*loan sale*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

贷款出售或贷款参与可以把一笔贷款的全部或部分现金流与风险转给买方，但转移程度取决于合同。能否从卖方资产负债表终止确认取决于适用会计规则下是否真正转移控制、风险与报酬；追索、回购义务、担保或保留权益还可能让卖方继续承担风险。它不是“二次抵押贷款（second-lien loan）”的同义词。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A loan sale or participation can transfer all or part of a loan's cash flows and risk to a buyer, but the extent of transfer depends on the contract. Derecognition depends on whether control, risks, and rewards are transferred under the applicable accounting rules; recourse, repurchase duties, guarantees, or retained interests can leave the seller exposed. It is not a synonym for a second-lien loan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 证券化securitisation
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*securitisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证券化把贷款或其他资产池的现金流通过载体和证券交给投资者，可用于融资并重新分配风险。但“收到融资”“会计终止确认”“重大信用风险转移”“监管资本减免”是四项不同判断；服务权、第一损失档、担保、流动性便利或隐性支持都可能让风险留在发起行。机制定义见 [[证券化]]，总边界见 [[资产出售、抵押与无损即时变现|资产变现与风险转移]]；若要判断中介是否真的消失，见 [[证券化不等于去中介化]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Securitisation channels cash flows from a pool of loans or other assets through a vehicle and securities to investors. It can provide funding and reallocate risk, but receiving cash, obtaining accounting derecognition, achieving significant credit-risk transfer, and receiving regulatory capital relief are four separate judgments. Servicing rights, first-loss tranches, guarantees, liquidity facilities, or implicit support can leave risk with the originator. See [[证券化|securitisation]] for the mechanism, [[资产出售、抵押与无损即时变现|asset monetisation and risk transfer]] for the balance-sheet boundary, and [[证券化不等于去中介化|securitisation does not imply disintermediation]] for the system-level conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行向客户提供专业化服务收取的费用

## 交易活动与风险管理工具
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Transactions and Risk Management Tools*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行可用债券、期货、期权和利率互换等调整风险，但对冲本身会产生基差、流动性、抵押品、会计和对手方风险。应先定义要稳定的是 NII 还是 EVE，再衡量对冲在不同曲线和客户行为情景下是否有效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Banks can use bonds, futures, options, and interest-rate swaps to alter exposures, but hedging adds basis, liquidity, collateral, accounting, and counterparty risk. Define whether NII or EVE is being stabilised, then test hedge effectiveness across curve and customer-behaviour scenarios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
