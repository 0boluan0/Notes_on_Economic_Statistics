# 世界各国金融的基本事实(Basic Facts of the World's Financial Systems)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basic Facts of the World's Financial Systems (World's Financial Systems)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 股票不是企业最主要的外部融资来源。
2. 发行可流通的债务和股权证券不是企业为其经营活动筹资的主要方式。
3. 与直接融资（即企业通过金融试产直接从贷款人手中获取资金）相比，间接融资（即有金融机构中介机构参与的融资）的重要性要大出数倍。
4. 金融中介机构，特别是银行，是企业外部资金最重要的来源。
5. 金融体系是经济体中受到最严格监管的部门。
6. 只有信誉卓著的大公司才能够进入金融市场为其经营活动筹资。
7. 抵押品（collateral）是居民个人和企业债务合约的普遍特征。
8. 典型的债务合约是对借款人行为设置了无数限制条件的、极为复杂的法律文本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Stocks are not the primary external source of funding for a company.<br>
**2.** Issuing tradable debt and equity securities is not the main way companies finance their operations.<br>
**3.** Compared to direct financing (where companies obtain funds directly from lenders), indirect financing (where financial intermediaries play a role) is significantly more important.<br>
**4.** Financial intermediaries, particularly banks, are the most significant source of external funds for companies.<br>
**5.** The financial sector is one of the most heavily regulated sectors in an economy.<br>
**6.** Only reputable large companies can access the financial markets to finance their operations.<br>
**7.** Collateral is a common feature of personal and corporate debt contracts.<br>
**8.** Typical debt contracts are extremely complex legal texts that impose numerous restrictions on borrowers.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：基本事实的适用范围
> 这八条是本课用来解释金融结构的经验概括，不能直接当作任何国家、时期和融资口径都成立的统计定律。第 6 条应理解为规模大、声誉好的企业通常更容易进入公开证券市场，而不是所有小企业都被金融市场排除；第 3 条的“金融试产”按语境指金融市场。[[直接与间接融资]]还须按最终融资契约辨别，不能只凭交易中出现中介机构就判断为间接融资。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These are stylised facts used to motivate the course, not statistical laws for every country, period, or financing measure. Point 6 concerns the easier access that large, reputable firms typically have to public securities markets, not universal exclusion of small firms. The wording in point 3 refers to financial markets. [[直接与间接融资|Direct versus indirect finance]] depends on the financing claims, not merely on an intermediary's involvement.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 逆向选择(adverse selection)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*adverse selection(adverse selection)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交易前出现的信息不对称问题.潜在的不良贷款风险来自那些积极寻求贷款的人.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Problems denoted by information asymmetry arise prior to transactions. Potential risks of non-performing loans stem from those actively seeking loans.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：申请积极不等于类型差
> [[逆向选择]]需要说明银行看不清的私人类型怎样影响参与或合约选择，使成交对象偏向银行较不利的类型；不能仅凭积极申请贷款认定一个人风险高。贷款利率怎样改变构成，见[[贷款利率筛选效应]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[逆向选择|Adverse selection]] requires private types to affect participation or contract choice in a way that worsens the bank's transaction pool. Eagerness to borrow alone does not establish high risk. See [[贷款利率筛选效应|loan-rate selection effects]] for the composition mechanism.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 道德风险(moral hazard)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Moral Hazard (moral hazard)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交易完成后发生的,由于借款人从事了与贷款人意愿相背离的活动,增大了贷款违约的可能性,导致贷款人承担较大的风险.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The risk to the lender increases post-completion of the transaction due to activities undertaken by the borrower that deviate from the lender’s wishes, thereby increasing the likelihood of loan default and exposing the lender to significant risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：行动激励与结果时点
> [[道德风险]]的关键是行动不能被充分观察、核验或据以执行契约，而行动后果由双方分担；这不是品德判断。违约发生在放款后，仍可能来自原本就存在的隐藏类型，不能按结果出现的日期分类，见[[隐藏类型与隐藏行动]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[道德风险|Moral hazard]] concerns actions that cannot be fully observed, verified, or contracted on while their consequences are shared; it is not a moral judgement. A default after lending may still reflect a pre-existing hidden type. See [[隐藏类型与隐藏行动|hidden types versus hidden actions]] rather than classifying by outcome dates.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 次品车问题:逆向选择如何影响金融机构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Lemons Problem: How Adverse Selection Affects Financial Institutions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 描述
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Description*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果市场上的次品车比例过高，那么买方就会不愿意支付高价购买汽车，因为他们担心买到的是次品车。这会导致好车车主也不愿意出售自己的汽车，因为他们知道无法以合理的价格卖出。最终，市场上只剩下次品车，买方和卖方都受到损害。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the proportion of defective cars in the market is too high, buyers will be unwilling to pay high prices for cars, as they fear purchasing a defective car. This would also deter good car owners from selling their cars, as they know they cannot sell them at reasonable prices. Ultimately, the market would consist solely of defective cars, resulting in harm to both buyers and sellers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：市场萎缩的条件
> 买方不能充分识别质量时，共同出价可能低于好车卖方的保留价值，因而使好车退出。但最终是部分交易萎缩、只交易低质量产品还是市场完全消失，取决于质量分布、双方估值和可用信息；不能一概断言只剩次品或每个买卖双方都受损。这里保留的是[[逆向选择|质量影响进入市场]]的课堂例子。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When buyers cannot distinguish quality, a pooled offer can fall below good sellers' reservation values. Whether trade partly contracts, only low-quality goods trade, or the market disappears depends on values, quality distributions, and information. Neither a lemons-only market nor a loss for every participant follows universally. The example illustrates [[逆向选择|quality-dependent participation]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 信息的私人生产与销售
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Private Production and Sale of Information*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

向资金供给方提供有关为自身的投资活动融资的个人或公司的详细情况,从而消除信息不对称.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Provide detailed information about individuals or companies seeking to finance their investment activities to the funding sources in order to eliminate information asymmetry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：生产信息只能有条件地缓解信息差
> 信息有生产成本，也可能有误差、过时或被隐瞒。提供资料不自动“消除”[[信息不对称]]；还要判断其真实性、可核验性与能否用于契约。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Information is costly to produce and may be inaccurate, outdated, or concealed. Supplying data does not automatically eliminate [[信息不对称|asymmetric information]]; its reliability, verifiability, and contractual use matter.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 免费搭车者free rider problem
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*free rider problem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当一些人免费利用了其他人付费所获取的信息时,免费搭车者问题就出现了.,市场中生产的信息会越来越少.逆向选择依旧存在.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When some individuals freely utilize information that others have paid to obtain, the free-rider problem arises. Information produced in the market will then decrease. adverse selection remains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：供给不足不等于逐期减少
> 当信息生产者无法收回其创造的全部收益时，私人生产激励可能不足；这不表示信息总量必然随时间越来越少。[[委托筛选与监督]]讨论中介在什么条件下能够保留信息收益。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Producers may underinvest when they cannot capture enough of the benefits they create. This does not imply that information must decrease over time. [[委托筛选与监督|Delegated screening and monitoring]] explains conditions for intermediaries to retain informational returns.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 增加信息的政府监管
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Increased government oversight of information*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只能在一定程度上避免,因为不可能让企业公开其所有的数据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Can only be mitigated to a certain extent, as it is impossible for companies to openly disclose all their data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：披露与核验不同
> 披露要求还需要可靠报告、审计及执行来发挥作用；即使公开了资料，也不等于其真实、充分或能揭示所有隐藏行动。这里说明监管的条件与成本，不是认定监管必然无效。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Disclosure depends on reliable reporting, auditing, and enforcement. Published data need not be true, complete, or revealing of every hidden action. These are conditions and costs of regulation, not a claim that it is necessarily ineffective.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 金融中介
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Financial Intermediaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中介收集信息的话别人不能搭便车
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gathering information through intermediaries means others cannot free-ride.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：中介何时能够内化信息收益
> 私人贷款、不公开的借款人信息及中介保留风险敞口，可以使信息收益更多留在中介内部；但“有中介”不自动排除搭便车或中介自身的代理问题。条件与反例见[[委托筛选与监督]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Private lending, non-public borrower information, and retained exposure can help an intermediary capture information benefits. Intermediation does not automatically prevent free-riding or its own agency problems. See [[委托筛选与监督|delegated screening and monitoring]] for conditions and counterexamples.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 抵押品与净值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Collateral and Net Worth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有当借款人无法归还贷款,导致违约,使得贷款人遭受损失时,逆向选择才会干扰市场的运行,那么有抵押品就能弥补一部分损失,降低危害
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adverse selection disrupts the market when a borrower cannot repay a loan, defaults, and causes the lender a loss. Collateral can then cover part of that loss and reduce the damage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：事前选择与事后回收
> [[逆向选择]]在放贷前就可能改变利率、申请人构成和获贷数量，不必等到实际违约才存在。[[抵押品]]能在担保权有效、资产可处置等条件下改善回收；它不能保证无损回收，也可能通过[[抵押要求与资产贫乏|资产门槛排除优质借款人]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[逆向选择|Adverse selection]] can affect rates, composition, and loan quantities before any default occurs. [[抵押品|Collateral]] can improve recovery when security rights and asset realisation are effective, but does not guarantee full recovery and can [[抵押要求与资产贫乏|exclude good asset-poor borrowers]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净值就是资产-负债,跟抵押品一个性质,还不上钱就拍卖
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net worth is essentially the assets minus liabilities, similar to collateral. If one cannot pay off the money owed, their assets can be auctioned off.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：净值不是一件可拍卖的抵押物
> [[净值]]是同一口径下资产减负债的余额；[[抵押品]]是为特定债务提供担保的资产。净值较高可提供损失缓冲与自有资金激励，但不能直接当作已设定担保权、可由某位债权人拍卖的资产；回收还取决于优先顺序、可执行性和处置价值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[净值|Net worth]] is assets minus liabilities on a consistent basis; [[抵押品|collateral]] is an asset securing a specified debt. Higher net worth can provide a loss buffer and incentive stake, but is not itself a pledged asset that a particular creditor may sell. Priority, enforceability, and realisation value still matter.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 委托-代理问题principal-agent problem:股权合约中的道德风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Principal–Agent Problem: Moral Hazard in Equity Contracts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 描述
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Description*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公司经理(所有者的代理人)仅仅拥有其效力的公司的一小部分,拥有公司大部分股权的股东和公司经理就出现了不一致,所有权和控制权分离的情况下,公司经理对利润最大化的追求远不如股东那么强烈.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A company manager—the owners' agent—usually owns only a small share of the firm for which they work. When ownership and control are separated, the interests of managers and the shareholders who own most of the equity can diverge, and managers may pursue profit maximization less strongly than shareholders do.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：委托关系与激励条件
> [[委托代理关系]]涉及一方授权另一方代其行动；是否形成利益冲突及其程度，还取决于持股、报酬、信息与监督条件。少量持股可能使经理只承担部分在职消费或努力不足的代价，但不能据此断言每个经理都不努力。激励也有自身边界，见[[股权激励的代理边界]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A [[委托代理关系|principal–agent relationship]] delegates action or decision authority. Conflicts depend on ownership, compensation, information, and monitoring. A small ownership stake can leave managers bearing only part of the cost of perks or low effort, but does not imply that every manager shirks. See [[股权激励的代理边界|the agency limits of equity incentives]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 金融中介
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Financial Intermediaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

风险投资公司venture capital firm集聚合伙人的资源,并利用这些资金投资一些初创企业,这些企业不努力就死了,同时风投公司会往这些小公司管理层塞自己人,用来监督.并且如果风投公司投钱了,这个企业的股份就不能卖给风投公司以外的其他人,避免了搭便车问题.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Venture capital firms aggregate the resources of partners and use these funds to invest in nascent companies that will likely fail without significant effort. These firms also insert themselves into the management of these small companies to oversee them. Furthermore, if a venture capital firm invests money in a company, the company’s shares cannot be sold to anyone outside the firm, thereby mitigating the free-rider problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：风投治理不等于禁止所有外部转让
> 私募股权、不公开交易、董事席位和合同约定的转让限制，可以帮助投资者参与治理并保留监督收益；“股份不能卖给风投以外任何人”不是普遍规则，应查具体合同。企业成败也不只由努力决定。这里保留风投主动治理的例子，其信息组织机制复用[[委托筛选与监督]]，分批投入的激励另见[[分期融资]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Private equity, non-public trading, board seats, and contractual transfer restrictions can support governance and help investors retain monitoring benefits. A ban on every transfer to outsiders is not a universal rule; the contract matters. Success also depends on more than effort. The example illustrates active governance, connected to [[委托筛选与监督|delegated screening and monitoring]] and, separately, [[分期融资|staged financing]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 债务合约
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*debt contract*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

纯逃避,不买股权就不会有股权问题. 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One way to avoid the problem altogether is not to buy equity: without an equity contract, this particular equity-contract problem does not arise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：固定偿付减少什么核验
> 在[[有成本状态核验]]的确定性模型中，[[债务合约]]按约足额偿付时，可以免去对真实项目收益的昂贵核验；报告无法履约时才进入约定核验区，见[[债务核验的触发机制]]。这不表示现实正常贷款不需要持续监督。债务仍可能产生[[股东债权人风险转移]]，不能说换成债务就消除了全部代理问题。[Townsend（1979），PDF 第 2、6 页／印刷第 266、270 页](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/2022-09/Optimal%20Contracts%20and%20Competitive%20Markets%20with%20Costly%20State%20Verification.pdf#page=2)支持非核验区固定偿付及模型条件。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a deterministic [[有成本状态核验|costly-state-verification model]], full scheduled payment under a [[债务合约|debt contract]] avoids costly verification of realised project revenue; a reported inability to perform triggers the agreed verification region. See [[债务核验的触发机制|verification triggers under debt]]. This does not eliminate ongoing monitoring of performing loans in practice or [[股东债权人风险转移|shareholder–creditor risk shifting]]. [Townsend (1979), PDF pp. 2 and 6 / printed pp. 266 and 270](https://economics.mit.edu/sites/default/files/2022-09/Optimal%20Contracts%20and%20Competitive%20Markets%20with%20Costly%20State%20Verification.pdf#page=2), supports fixed transfers in the non-verification region under the model's assumptions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 描述
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Description*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

字面意思
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Literal Meaning
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：债务中的风险转移
> 已借入固定偿付的债务后，[[有限责任]]与[[股债到期分配]]可能让股东保留项目上行、把部分下行转给债权人；若项目选择不能充分订约，股东可能偏好使债权价值下降的项目。必须比较同一债务条件下双方的预期收益，不能仅凭“波动更高”或后来违约认定机制成立，见[[股东债权人风险转移]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After fixed-payment debt is issued, [[有限责任|limited liability]] and [[股债到期分配|debt–equity payoffs]] can preserve shareholders' upside while shifting part of the downside to creditors. If project choice cannot be fully contracted on, shareholders may favour a project that reduces debt value. Compare both parties' expected payoffs under the same debt terms; higher volatility or a later default alone is insufficient. See [[股东债权人风险转移|shareholder–creditor risk shifting]].

### 限制性条款

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比如银行不让借款人公司的高管座商务舱和一等座

For instance, banks do not allow senior executives of borrower companies to fly in business class or first class.

> [!note] 校注：条款示例与可执行性
> 商务舱例子用来说明限制资金用途或在职消费，不是银行贷款的统一禁令。[[限制性条款]]须明确行为、信息报告、核验与违约后果，并受具体合同和执行条件约束；列出了限制不等于所有隐藏行动都会被发现。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The travel example illustrates restrictions on fund use or perks, not a universal lending prohibition. [[限制性条款|Covenants]] specify conduct, reporting, verification, and breach consequences subject to contractual and enforcement conditions. A written restriction does not ensure detection of every hidden action.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

金融中介可以雇律师起草一份很详细的文件,私人就很亏.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial intermediaries can hire lawyers to draft detailed contracts; doing the same as a private individual would be prohibitively costly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 校注：合同分摊与持续监督分工
> 可重复使用的合同与专业人员体现[[金融交易成本的规模经济]]；放贷前筛选、放贷后持续监督和执行体现[[委托筛选与监督]]。一份更长的合同不自动更有效，两种机制也都需要收益足以覆盖成本。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Reusable contracts and specialists illustrate [[金融交易成本的规模经济|transaction-cost economies of scale]], whereas pre-loan screening, ongoing monitoring, and enforcement illustrate [[委托筛选与监督|delegated information production]]. A longer contract is not automatically more effective; benefits must justify costs.

# 总结
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Summary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可按“问题-机制-工具”来总览金融结构：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The financial structure can be surveyed through a “problem–mechanism–tool” framework:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 逆向选择（交易前信息不对称）  
对应工具：信息私人生产与销售、政府监管、金融中介、抵押品与净值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Adverse selection (pre-transaction information asymmetry)<br>
Tools: Private Information Production and Sales, Government Regulation, Financial Intermediaries, Collateral and Net Worth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

2. 股权合约中的道德风险（[[委托代理关系|委托-代理问题]]）  
对应工具：信息生产与监督、政府监管、金融中介（如风投治理）、以及通过债务合约回避部分股权激励扭曲。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** Moral Hazard in Equity Contracts ([[委托代理关系|Agency Problem]])<br>
Tools: Information production and oversight, government regulation, financial intermediaries (such as venture capital governance), and mitigating some equity incentive distortions through debt contracts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

3. 债务合约中的道德风险  
对应工具：抵押品/净值、限制性条款（covenants）、金融中介的监督与执行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** Debt contracts' moral hazard<br>
Tools: Collateral/Net Worth, Restrictive Covenants, Supervision and Enforcement by Financial Intermediaries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
