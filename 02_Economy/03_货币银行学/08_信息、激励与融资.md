# 信息、激励与融资
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Information, incentives, and financing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行放出 100 元，合同写着到期收回 110 元。真正要解释的不是这十元利息怎么算，而是：谁会接受合同，拿到钱后会怎样做，到期收入又能否核实。这三个问题分别改变借款人构成、项目选择和还款执行，不能用一个“风险高”概括。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bank lends 100 under a contract promising 110 at maturity. The central questions are who accepts, what they do after receiving the funds, and whether their realised income can be verified. These affect borrower composition, project choice, and repayment enforcement through distinct channels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一、先问：缺的是谁的什么信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Whose information is missing?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[信息不对称]]是交易一方掌握另一方没有的、与交易相关的信息。如果双方都不知道明天是否下雨，只有共同的不确定性；如果申请人知道自己的设备故障率，保险人却分不清，才有这里要研究的信息差。信息差也不等于必然交易失败，还要看它怎样影响选择和收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[信息不对称|Asymmetric information]] means that one party holds transaction-relevant information unavailable to another. Shared uncertainty about tomorrow's weather is different from an applicant privately knowing their equipment's failure rate. An information gap does not itself imply market failure; its effects on choices and payoffs matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[逆向选择]]讨论隐藏的既有类型怎样改变交易参与或契约选择；[[道德风险]]讨论难以观察或写入可执行合同的行动怎样随激励改变。[[隐藏类型与隐藏行动]]给出了辨别方法：先固定“是谁”，看合同改变后到底是不同的人进出，还是同一个人改了行为。“交易前／后”只是记忆提示。保险和贷款复用这两个概念，不各建一份定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[逆向选择|Adverse selection]] concerns how hidden pre-existing types affect participation or contract choice. [[道德风险|Moral hazard]] concerns incentive-driven actions that cannot be fully observed or contractually enforced. The [[隐藏类型与隐藏行动|type–action distinction]] asks whether different people enter and leave or the same person changes behaviour. Before/after contracting is only a mnemonic. Insurance and lending use these same definitions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 二、把合同承诺与实际分配分开
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Separate contractual promises from realised allocations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[债务合约]]规定债权人的偿付请求；[[股权]]是带有相应治理权利的剩余所有权权益。先用一个刻意简化的单期模型：只有一类债务，没有抵押、担保、破产成本或其他优先请求，期末可分配资产收入为 $Y\ge0$，承诺偿付 $D$。在[[有限责任]]下，[[股债到期分配]]是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[债务合约|debt contract]] specifies repayment claims; [[股权|equity]] is residual ownership with associated governance rights. Begin with a deliberately simplified one-period model: one debt class, no collateral, guarantees, bankruptcy costs, or other priority claims. Distributable income is $Y\ge0$ and promised repayment is $D$. Under [[有限责任|limited liability]], the [[股债到期分配|maturity allocation]] is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\text{lender receipt}=\min(Y,D),\qquad
\text{borrower residual}=(Y-D)_+=\max(Y-D,0).
$$

它们加起来恰好为 $Y$。若 $Y=80,D=110$，银行收 80，借款人剩余为零；不是“没付满 110，所以银行收入为零”。这只是期末分配，不是今天的债券或股票价格。价格还需要贴现和风险定价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two claims sum to $Y$. If $Y=80$ and $D=110$, the lender receives 80 and the borrower retains zero. Failure to pay the full promise does not mean zero lender recovery. These are maturity payoffs, not current security prices, which also require discounting and risk pricing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三、同样一笔贷款，加息怎样改变来借钱的人
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. How a higher rate changes who borrows*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面是独立构造的教学例子，不是经验数据。每人借 100；风险中性，无抵押且承担有限责任。银行看到的特征相同，申请人的项目类型既不能被银行识别，也不能由申请人改变。潜在人群中安全型占 80%，项目确定收入 120；风险型占 20%，项目以 0.8 概率收入 150，否则为零。两类项目平均收入都为 120。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a constructed teaching example, not empirical data. Each risk-neutral, uncollateralised, limited-liability applicant borrows 100. Observed characteristics coincide; project type is fixed and privately known. In the potential population, 80% are safe types earning 120 for certain. The remaining 20% earn 150 with probability 0.8 and zero otherwise. Both project means equal 120.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每人不借款都可获得收益 8。因此只有预期剩余严格大于 8 才严格愿意借款；下面三档都不会遇到无差异。计算顺序是先用 $(Y-D)_+$ 判断谁申请，再对实际申请池的 $\min(Y,D)$ 求平均：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each applicant can obtain 8 without borrowing. Borrowing is strictly preferred only when expected residual income exceeds 8; none of the three offers below creates indifference. First use $(Y-D)_+$ to determine participation, then average $\min(Y,D)$ over the actual applicant pool:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 到期承诺 / Promise $D$ | 安全型剩余 / Safe residual | 风险型预期剩余 / Risky expected residual | 实际申请者 / Applicants | 每笔贷款预期回收 / Expected receipt |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 110 | 10 | 32 | 两型 / Both | $0.8(110)+0.2(0.8\times110)=105.6$ |
| 115 | 5 | 28 | 仅风险型 / Risky only | $0.8\times115=92$ |
| 135 | 0 | 12 | 仅风险型 / Risky only | $0.8\times135=108$ |

从 110 到 115，安全型因 $5<8$ 离开，风险型因 $28>8$ 留下。银行对留下的人要价更高，却面对更差的申请池，平均回收反而下降。这就是[[贷款利率筛选效应]]。这里没有谁突然变得不诚实，也没有同一个人换项目。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Moving from 110 to 115 drives safe types out because $5<8$, while risky types remain because $28>8$. The bank charges remaining applicants more but faces a worse pool, lowering average recovery. This is the [[贷款利率筛选效应|rate-induced selection effect]], not a change in honesty or a project switch by a given person.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但从 115 到 135，回收又升到 108，超过 105.6。所以这个例子只证明[[贷款加息的回报边界|加息未必提高预期回收]]，并未证明 110 是银行最优报价。表中是回收金额；若问净收益，还要减资金成本、调查与执行费用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recovery then rises to 108 at 135, exceeding 105.6. The example establishes the [[贷款加息的回报边界|possibility that higher rates reduce expected recovery]], not that 110 is optimal. The table reports receipts; net returns must also deduct funding, investigation, and enforcement costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[信贷配给]]也不是所有拒贷的统称。在 Stiglitz–Weiss 的一类定义中，可观察特征相同的申请人有的获贷、有的被拒，被拒者愿意付更高利率也得不到贷款。要进一步称为均衡，还必须满足[[均衡信贷配给条件]]：有未满足需求，而且在给定申请者选择与资金供给条件下，银行没有更有利的可行偏离。单峰、单一利率图只是一个情形；原论文还允许双利率均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[信贷配给|Credit rationing]] is not a synonym for every loan rejection. One Stiglitz–Weiss definition has observably identical applicants treated differently, with rejected applicants unable to obtain credit even by offering a higher rate. [[均衡信贷配给条件|Equilibrium conditions]] additionally require unmet demand and no profitable feasible bank deviation given applicant choices and funding. The single-peak, single-rate diagram is one case; the paper also allows two-rate equilibria.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 四、再固定借款人：拿到钱后为什么可能改做冒险项目
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Hold the borrower fixed: why might they switch projects after funding?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在换一个问题：贷款已经按原计划定价，借款人随后可选择银行难以约束的项目。由于债权人所得有上限，股东保留超过偿付额的上行收益、下行损失却可能由债权人分担，双方对项目的排序会不同。这是[[股东债权人风险转移]]；分析时不能把“借款人类型改变”偷偷换成“同一借款人行动改变”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now consider a different problem. Debt has already been priced against the original plan, but the borrower can subsequently choose projects that the lender cannot adequately constrain. Debt receipts are capped; shareholders retain upside beyond repayment while creditors can share downside losses. This can reverse project rankings through [[股东债权人风险转移|shareholder–creditor risk shifting]]. A change in a fixed borrower's action must not be substituted silently for a change in borrower types.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单独算一例：债额已固定为 80，两个项目投入成本相同，双方风险中性。安全项目必得 100，股东拿 20、债权人拿 80。风险项目等概率收入 180 或零，股东期望所得为 $0.5(180-80)=50$，债权人为 $0.5\times80=40$。股东更喜欢 50 而不是 20，但总期望收入从 100 降到 90。这个转移依赖融资后能换项目、债权人不能有效约束或重新定价；并不意味着理性贷款人事前必定忽略这些激励。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a separate example, debt is fixed at 80, project investment costs coincide, and both parties are risk-neutral. A safe project yields 100 for certain, leaving shareholders 20 and creditors 80. A risky project yields 180 or zero with equal probabilities, giving expected equity payoff $0.5(180-80)=50$ and debt payoff $0.5\times80=40$. Shareholders prefer 50 to 20 despite total expected income falling from 100 to 90. This requires a post-financing switch that creditors cannot effectively constrain or reprice; it does not imply that rational lenders necessarily ignore those incentives ex ante.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从组织关系看，这是[[委托代理关系]]中的一个激励问题，经理与股东之间也可能发生类似冲突。处理冲突本身有成本，而且监督后仍可能留下偏离。[[代理成本]]区分委托人的监督支出、代理人的保证支出和残余损失；[[股权激励的代理边界]]说明提高经理持股只能改变部分收益权衡，不能保证利益完全一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Organisationally, this is an incentive problem within a [[委托代理关系|principal–agent relationship]]; managers and shareholders can also have conflicting interests. Mitigation costs resources and may leave residual divergence. [[代理成本|Agency costs]] distinguish principal monitoring, agent bonding, and residual loss. The [[股权激励的代理边界|limits of equity incentives]] explain why greater managerial ownership need not fully align interests.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 五、工具究竟改变了哪个约束
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Which constraint does each instrument change?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[净值]]是同一时点、同一计量口径下资产减负债；[[抵押品]]则是支持特定债务、在约定条件下可依法实现担保权的资产。账面净值高，不等于有同等价值的现金或可处置抵押物。[[借款人自有资金激励]]改变借款人自己承担多少后果；[[抵押约束的激励边界]]区分借款人的损失激励与银行的实际回收，两者还受执行成本、估值与其他负债影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[净值|Net worth]] is assets minus liabilities at a common date and valuation basis. [[抵押品|Collateral]] consists of assets supporting particular debt through security rights enforceable under specified conditions. High accounting net worth is not equivalent to cash or realisable collateral. [[借款人自有资金激励|Borrower own-funds incentives]] concern the consequences retained by the borrower; [[抵押约束的激励边界|collateral's incentive limits]] distinguish those losses from the bank's net recovery, which also depends on enforcement costs, valuation, and other claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要求更多抵押也可能把缺少可抵押资产的好项目排除在外，见[[抵押要求与资产贫乏]]。[[限制性条款]]把用途、报告、财务指标等要求写进合同，但必须可核验、可执行；[[分期融资]]把尚未拨付的资金与里程碑或新信息挂钩，不等于贷款人随时有权收回已经发放的贷款，也不等于借款人分期还款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More demanding collateral requirements can exclude good projects held by [[抵押要求与资产贫乏|asset-poor borrowers]]. [[限制性条款|Covenants]] constrain uses of funds, reporting, or financial indicators, subject to verification and enforcement. [[分期融资|Staged financing]] conditions future disbursements on milestones or new information. It is neither unrestricted recall of already disbursed loans nor repayment by instalments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后还要区分“行动看不清”和“收入报不准”。[[有成本状态核验]]考虑借款人知道实现收入、外部人付出成本才能查清的环境。在[[债务核验的触发机制|确定性核验模型]]中，激励相容要求不核验区间支付固定金额；只有再加入原文相应的偏好、成本和可行性条件，才能把核验区确定为低收入区。这种安排可节省逐状态核实收入的成本。允许随机核验等机制后，比较范围又会改变，不能推出债务在所有环境下优于股权。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hidden actions also differ from unverifiable income reports. [[有成本状态核验|Costly state verification]] studies privately observed realised income that outsiders can verify only at a cost. In a [[债务核验的触发机制|deterministic verification model]], incentive compatibility requires a constant payment in the no-verification region. Identifying the verification region with low income additionally requires the paper's relevant preference, cost, and feasibility assumptions. Such arrangements can economise on checking every state. Stochastic verification changes the feasible comparison again; universal debt superiority does not follow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 六、从单个合同走向金融中介与危机
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. From individual contracts to intermediation and crises*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果每个小额投资者都重复调查，信息生产会很昂贵。[[委托筛选与监督]]解释为什么集中调查、持有贷款并内化信息收益可能降低成本；[[关系型贷款与软信息]]解释持续接触为何有助于了解不能轻易编码的经营信息。中介本身仍需要受到激励和约束，不能把“交给银行”当作问题已经消失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated investigation by every small investor is costly. [[委托筛选与监督|Delegated screening and monitoring]] explains how centralised investigation, loan retention, and internalised information returns can reduce costs. [[关系型贷款与软信息|Relationship lending and soft information]] explains the value of repeated contact for hard-to-code business information. Intermediaries themselves still need incentives and constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些成本和激励约束可进入[[外部融资溢价]]；在[[金融加速器]]的特定模型中，借款人财务状况恶化又会推高融资成本，放大实体冲击。[[协调性挤兑]]则是另一条机制，需要看存款契约与他人提款预期。资产价格是否充分反映某类信息，与借贷双方是否知道相同信息、融资是否无摩擦，是不同问题，不能用“有效市场”一次性回答。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These costs and incentive constraints can contribute to the [[外部融资溢价|external finance premium]]. In [[金融加速器|financial accelerator]] models, weaker borrower balance sheets can raise financing costs and amplify real shocks. [[协调性挤兑|Coordination runs]] are a distinct mechanism involving deposit contracts and expectations of others' withdrawals. Informational efficiency of asset prices does not settle whether contracting parties have symmetric information or finance is frictionless.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 七、读完后用一题检查机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Check the mechanism with one question*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

观察到高利率贷款违约更多，能否判定是逆向选择？先别给标签：高风险者可能更愿意申请，同一借款人也可能因还款压力改变行动，银行还可能本来就给可观察的高风险者报更高利率。只看相关性分不开这些解释。需要能分别改变进入时的报价与进入后的激励、并控制可比性的设计；MIT 课程的报价与事后折扣实验示意正是沿这条思路区分机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Does higher default on high-rate loans establish adverse selection? Risky types may select in, a fixed borrower may change behaviour under repayment pressure, or banks may charge more for observable risk. Correlation alone does not distinguish these explanations. Identification needs a design that separately changes entry offers and subsequent incentives while ensuring comparability; MIT's offer-rate and later-discount illustration follows this logic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 财管课程的术语口径
> CPA 教材本身把部分经理在职消费、机会主义行为称作“逆向选择”。复习该教材时保留它的用词；跨课程分析则回到隐藏类型／隐藏行动的机制定义。财管原记录已在对应位置并列说明，两门课链接到同一组概念卡。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The CPA textbook labels some managerial perks and opportunistic behaviour “adverse selection.” Preserve its wording when studying that examination context, but use the hidden-type/hidden-action mechanism distinction across courses. The finance record explains the difference beside the original passage and links to the same concept cards.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
