## 美联储的独立性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the Federal Reserve Is Independent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 它有可观的,独立的收入来源
2. 委员会成员任期长且不可连任,不用讨好国会.
3. 国会通过法律要求美联储有更高的独立性
4. 但是因为工资低所以辞职的多,美联储仍然不能完全摆脱政治压力.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. It has substantial independent sources of revenue.
2. Board members serve long, non-renewable terms, reducing the need to court Congress.
3. Congress has enacted legislation intended to give the Federal Reserve greater independence.
4. Nevertheless, low pay contributes to resignations, and the Federal Reserve cannot be completely insulated from political pressure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 美联储应当保持独立性吗
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Should the Federal Reserve Remain Independent?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 支持独立性的理由
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Case for Independence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果美联储受制于更多的政治压力,就会导致货币政策出现通货膨胀倾向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Greater political pressure on the Federal Reserve may create an inflationary bias in monetary policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反对独立性的理由
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Case against Independence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由一批不对任何人负责的精英分子控制货币政策是不民主的.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Allowing a group of unelected elites who are not directly accountable to the public to control monetary policy is undemocratic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
