### 流通中的现金currency in circulation
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*currency in circulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本章的历史教材符号把 $C$ 定义为公众手中的纸币硬币，并把库存现金纳入 $R$。现行美国官方 monetary base 序列则定义为 currency in circulation 加 reserve balances；前者包含存款机构库存现金，而 H.6 的 M1 currency 会把这部分排除。两种符号系统可以得到同一总量，但不能混用分项或重复计算库存现金。见 [[美国货币基础、M1 与 M2|当前口径边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The chapter's historical textbook notation defines $C$ as notes and coins held by the public and includes vault cash in $R$. The current U.S. official monetary-base series instead defines the base as currency in circulation plus reserve balances; its currency component includes depository institutions' vault cash, whereas H.6 M1 currency excludes vault cash. The two decompositions can describe the same total, but their components must not be mixed or vault cash counted twice. See [[美国货币基础、M1 与 M2|the current-definition boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

注意：
1. 银行库存现金是银行持有的实物通货，不是银行在央行账户中的准备金余额；只在历史教材的准备要求口径下才会把两者合写为 $R$。
2. 美国 Federal Reserve notes 是联邦储备银行负债；硬币由美国财政部铸币局发行，不是联邦储备银行负债。“生产或铸造”不等于已进入流通量，但也不能把硬币误写成进入流通后才变成美联储负债。见 [[纸币与硬币的负债主体|纸币与硬币发行主体]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Note:

**1.** Vault cash is physical currency held by a bank, not the bank's reserve balance at the central bank. The two were combined in $R$ only under the historical textbook convention used for reserve requirements.<br>
**2.** Federal Reserve notes are liabilities of the Federal Reserve Banks. U.S. coins are issued by the Treasury's Mint and are not Federal Reserve Bank liabilities. Production or minting is not the same as entry into currency in circulation, but a coin does not become a Fed liability when it circulates. See [[纸币与硬币的负债主体|the currency-issuer boundary]].<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 准备金reserves 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*reserves*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在本章历史教材符号中，$R$ 合并了存款机构在美联储的准备金余额和库存现金。现行账户概念必须把两者分开：[[央行准备金余额|准备金余额]]是央行账户资产，[[库存现金与准备金|库存现金]]是实物纸币硬币。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In this chapter's historical textbook notation, $R$ combines reserve balances at the Federal Reserve with vault cash. Current account concepts must separate them: reserve balances are central-bank account assets, while vault cash is physical notes and coins held by the bank.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在存在正法定准备比率的制度中，最低要求是法定准备 $RR$，超出要求的部分是超额准备 $ER$。这些分类依法域、日期、机构与负债类型而定；截至 2026-08-29，美国法定准备要求比率仍为 0。见 [[法定准备金口径|准备要求的法域与日期边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a regime with a positive statutory ratio, the minimum amount is required reserves, $RR$, and the amount above it is excess reserves, $ER$. The categories depend on jurisdiction, date, institution, and liability type. As checked on 29 August 2026, U.S. reserve-requirement ratios remain zero. See [[法定准备金口径|the jurisdiction-and-date boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 向金融机构发放的贷款loans to financial institutions
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*loans to financial institutions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

美联储向符合条件的机构发放贷款时，若把款项贷记到借款机构在联储的账户，则银行准备金余额增加，同时对美联储的借款负债增加。但“央行流向银行的任何东西都是准备金”过于宽泛：交付纸币是准备金余额向库存现金的转换，证券或其他资产也不因交易对手是央行就变成准备金。准备金必须按合格持有人的央行账户余额识别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the Federal Reserve lends to an eligible institution and credits that institution's Federal Reserve account, reserve balances and the institution's borrowing liability rise together. But not everything moving from a central bank to a commercial bank is a reserve balance. Delivery of banknotes converts reserve balances into vault cash; securities and other assets do not become reserves merely because the central bank is the counterparty. Reserve balances must be identified as balances in eligible holders' central-bank accounts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 公开市场操作open market operation
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Open-Market Operations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 公开市场购买open market purchase
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*open market purchase*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假定美联储从一级交易商购买 1 亿美元债券。一级交易商可以是银行，也可以是非银行，两种分录不同：

- 若卖方是有联储账户的银行，该行证券 -100、准备金余额 +100；没有公众存款必然同时增加。
- 若卖方是口径内非银行，卖方证券 -100、银行存款 +100，而其往来银行的准备金 +100、存款负债 +100。

因此央行购债对准备金与存款的影响要看卖方与结算链。见 [[央行购债结算链|公开市场购买的对手方边界]]。
![[Pasted image 20240526104313.png]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume that the Federal Reserve purchases $100 million of bonds from a primary dealer. A primary dealer can be a bank or a non-bank. If the seller is an account-holding bank, its securities fall by 100 and reserve balances rise by 100; no public deposit must rise in the same entry. If the seller is an in-scope non-bank, its securities fall and bank deposit rises by 100, while its bank records reserve balances +100 and deposit liabilities +100. The reserve and deposit effect therefore depends on the seller and settlement chain. See [[央行购债结算链|the counterparty boundary for open-market purchases]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 公开市场出售open market sale
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*open market sale*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若美联储向银行出售 1 亿美元债券，该行准备金 -100、证券 +100。若向非银行出售，非银行的存款 -100、证券 +100，其银行的存款负债和准备金各 -100。这是上述两种购买分录各自的反向，不是一个可忽略卖方身份的通用分录。
![[Pasted image 20240526111643.png]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the Federal Reserve sells 100 of bonds to a bank, that bank records reserve balances -100 and securities +100. If it sells to a non-bank, the buyer records deposits -100 and securities +100 while its bank records deposit liabilities and reserve balances -100. These are the reversals of the corresponding purchase cases; the seller or buyer's sector still matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从存款到现金的转换
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conversion from Deposits to Cash*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假定公众提取 1 亿美元现金。若银行已有足额库存现金，它记存款 -100、库存现金 -100，公众持有现金 +100，准备金余额不动。若银行先向美联储领取现金，则还有一笔在先的转换：准备金余额 -100、库存现金 +100。两步不能压成“提款时准备金一定当场下降”。
![[Pasted image 20240526105215.png]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume that the public withdraws $100 million in cash. If the bank already holds enough vault cash, deposits and vault cash fall by 100 while public currency rises by 100; reserve balances do not move. If the bank first orders currency from the Federal Reserve, a preceding conversion reduces reserve balances by 100 and raises vault cash by 100. The two steps must not be compressed into a claim that withdrawal always lowers reserve balances immediately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这个纯粹的存款—现金转换中，现金与准备或库存现金此增彼减，货币基础总量不变；公众现金偏好会改变其构成和银行流动性管理。若央行另外对现金需求进行抵消或收缩操作，那是另一笔交易。见 [[存款转现金|存款转现金的两步边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In this pure deposit-to-currency conversion, currency and reserves or vault cash move in opposite directions and the monetary-base total is unchanged; cash preference changes its composition and banks' liquidity management. Any central-bank accommodation or offset is a separate transaction. See [[存款转现金|the two-step boundary for deposit-to-currency conversion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 向金融机构发放贷款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Loans to Financial Institutions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若美联储向第一国民银行发放 1 亿美元贷款，并把款项贷记该行的联储账户，第一国民银行记准备金余额 +100、对美联储借款 +100；美联储记对金融机构贷款 +100、准备金负债 +100。这是央行与银行之间另起的一笔融资交易，不是商业银行向公众贷款时的分录。
![[Pasted image 20240526111829.png]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the Federal Reserve lends $100 million to First National Bank and credits its Federal Reserve account, the bank's reserve balance and borrowing from the Federal Reserve each rise by $100 million. On the Federal Reserve's balance sheet, loans to financial institutions and reserve liabilities each rise by $100 million. This is a separate central-bank financing transaction, not the entry for a commercial-bank loan to the public.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 对美联储控制基础货币能力的概述
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An Overview of the Fed's Ability to Control the Monetary Base*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

美联储控制公开市场操作,但是不能单方面决定和准确预测银行向美联储借款的金额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Federal Reserve controls open-market operations, but cannot unilaterally determine or accurately predict the amount of money banks borrow from it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基础货币分为两部分,一部分美联储能够完全控制([[公开市场操作|公开市场操作]]),对另一部分管控能力较弱(银行的借款),只能通过利率间接控制.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The monetary base has two components. The Federal Reserve directly controls the part created through [[公开市场操作|open-market operations]], but has less control over the part created by bank borrowing, which it can influence only indirectly through interest rates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

能控制的部分叫非借入准备金 nonborrowed monetary base,MBn
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The controllable portion is called the nonborrowed monetary base, $MB_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
MB_n=MB-BR
$$

<!-- monetary-topic53:base-clarification:start -->
> [!note] 基础货币与准备金的口径
> 上式 $MB_n$ 是**非借入基础货币**，不是非借入准备金：按课堂 $MB=C+R$，后者是 $R-BR$，两者相差 $C$。美国统计的通货与库存现金覆盖见[[美国货币基础、M1 与 M2]]。“控制操作规模”也不等于能逐时精确预测全部余额；其他央行负债和借款需求仍可变化。数量操作怎样维持或改变利率目标，接读[[02_Economy/03_货币银行学/16_货币政策的实施与传导#3. 从公开市场交易到利率实施|公开市场交易与利率实施]]。

## 存款创造：从贷款拨款到客户存款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*[[贷款创造存款|Deposit creation: from loan drawdown to a customer deposit]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行完成信用评估并实际拨款 100 时，银行资产端贷款 +100、负债端客户存款 +100；借款人资产端存款 +100、负债端贷款 +100。银行不是把某位储户的一笔旧存款原样转给借款人。仅仅批准一项尚未提款的额度仍是 [[未提款授信|表外承诺]]，不能提前计作货币。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a bank completes its credit assessment and actually advances 100, its loan assets and customer-deposit liabilities each rise by 100; the borrower records a deposit asset and a loan liability of 100. The bank has not transferred one particular saver's old deposit unchanged. Approval of an [[未提款授信|undrawn facility remains an off-balance-sheet commitment]] and is not yet money creation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 主体 | 资产变化 | 负债或权益变化 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 借款人 | 存款 +100 | 银行贷款 +100 |
| 放贷银行 | 贷款 +100 | 客户存款 +100 |
| 中央银行 | 0 | 0 |

这一步 [[贷款存款配对分录|不同时创造准备金或银行资本]]。借款人的货币资产和债务同时增加，所以 [[贷款与借款人净财富|创造货币不等于等额创造净财富]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same entry [[贷款存款配对分录|does not create reserves or bank capital]]. The borrower's money asset and debt rise together, so [[贷款与借款人净财富|money creation is not equal net-wealth creation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 个别银行、支付结算与合并银行体系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The individual bank, payment settlement, and the consolidated banking system*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若借款人把 100 支付给同一银行的另一客户，银行只重记两个客户的存款，准备金不动。若收款人在另一家银行，放贷行存款和准备金各 -100，收款行准备金和存款各 +100。[[单家银行与体系存款创造|个别银行失去存款]]，不等于合并银行体系的公众存款消失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the borrower pays another customer of the same bank, the bank merely reassigns deposits and no reserves move. If the recipient uses another bank, the lending bank's deposits and reserves each fall by 100 while the receiving bank's reserves and deposits each rise by 100. [[单家银行与体系存款创造|Deposit loss at one bank]] is not the disappearance of public deposits from the consolidated banking system.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 跨行付款后的银行 | 资产变化 | 负债变化 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 放贷行 | 贷款 +100；准备金 -100 | 原借款人存款回到原位 |
| 收款行 | 准备金 +100 | 收款人存款 +100 |
| 合并银行体系 | 对非银行贷款 +100；准备金总量不变 | 公众存款 +100 |

[[贷款后的跨行结算|跨行支付使始发银行产生结算和融资需求]]。它可以使用已有准备金、吸收存款、借同业资金、出售资产，或在制度条件下向央行融资。由此可见，[[贷款创存与稳定融资|贷款创造存款与银行仍需稳定融资并不矛盾]]：前者描述拨款时点，后者描述存款外流后如何持续持有贷款资产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[贷款后的跨行结算|Interbank payment creates a settlement and funding need for the originating bank]]. It can use existing reserves, attract deposits, borrow in interbank markets, sell assets, or obtain central-bank funding subject to the relevant rules. Thus [[贷款创存与稳定融资|deposit creation and the need for stable funding are compatible]]: the first describes loan drawdown, while the second describes how the bank funds the loan after deposits leave.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 银行不能无限创造存款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*[[银行信贷创造约束|Why banks cannot create deposits without limit]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行会计上能够用贷款创造存款，不表示每笔贷款在经济上都可行。扩贷至少同时受以下因素约束：

- 有信用需求和借款人的偿付能力；
- 贷款定价能否覆盖融资、运营、资本和预期损失；
- 信用、利率、集中度和操作风险限额；
- 资本、流动性、杠杆与审慎监管；
- 支付结算资金及其价格、期限和抵押条件；
- 货币政策利率、总体金融条件与公众资产组合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The accounting ability to create deposits through lending does not make every loan economically viable. Expansion remains constrained by creditworthy demand, risk-adjusted profitability, capital, liquidity, leverage and prudential rules, settlement funding, monetary-policy rates, financial conditions, and the portfolio decisions of the public.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 简单存款乘数：条件化的教学模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*[[简单存款乘数|The simple deposit multiplier: a conditional teaching model]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令固定且正的法定准备金率为 $rr$。要把几何级数的第一项写成 $Δ R$ 存款，必须先固定起点：初始交易同时使银行体系准备金与货币持有部门存款各增 $Δ R$，例如央行从口径内非银行购买资产。在此之上还要同时假定：

1. 银行除法定准备外不保留额外准备金；
2. 新贷款被完整支出并回存国内银行体系；
3. 公众不把新增余额持为现金；
4. 每轮都有足够的合格贷款需求；
5. 资本、流动性、风险、融资和监管均不先约束扩张；

才能从几何级数得到

$$
\Delta D
=\Delta R+(1-rr)\Delta R+(1-rr)^2\Delta R+\cdots
=\frac{1}{rr}\Delta R.
$$

例如 $rr=10\%$，央行从口径内非银行购买资产，使准备金和卖方存款各增 100，则封闭模型给出 $100+90+81+\cdots=1{,}000$ 的存款总增量。其中初始交易已带来 100 存款，后续银行贷款与存款创造合计 900。若只是央行向银行贷款等纯准备金注入，首笔交易不增加公众存款；要推导后续扩张，必须另加银行按可用准备充分放贷的行为假设，不能把准备金本身叫作首轮存款。所以这是“若所有条件成立，终点是多少”的命题，不是准备金机械触发贷款的因果律。若 $rr=0$，公式分母为零，表示模型失去封闭条件，不表示银行能无限创造货币。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the required-reserve ratio $rr$ be fixed and positive. To write the first deposit term as $\Delta R$, first specify an initial transaction that raises banking-system reserves and money-holder deposits by the same $\Delta R$, such as a central-bank asset purchase from an in-scope non-bank. Only if banks then hold no reserves above the requirement, all loan proceeds are spent and redeposited domestically, the public holds no extra currency, loan demand is sufficient, and no capital, liquidity, risk, funding, or regulatory constraint binds does the geometric series yield $\Delta D=\Delta R/rr$. With $rr=10\%$ and an initial 100 rise in both reserves and the seller's deposit, the model produces deposits of $100+90+81+\cdots=1{,}000$: 100 came from the initial transaction and 900 from subsequent bank lending and deposit creation. A reserves-only injection, such as a central-bank loan to a bank, creates no public deposit in its first entry; deriving later expansion requires an additional full-lending behavioural assumption, and the reserves themselves are not the first-round deposit. This is a conditional endpoint, not a mechanical causal law. If $rr=0$, the model has lost its closing condition; it has not proved infinite money creation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 法定准备金要求的法域与日期边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*[[法定准备金口径|Jurisdiction and date boundaries of reserve requirements]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

法定准备金要求针对哪些机构、哪些负债、什么维持期，由具体制度决定。它不是逐笔贷款前必须预先划出的“可贷准备金配额”。截至 2026-08-29，美联储官方页面仍显示：美国准备金要求比率自 2020-03-26 起为 0。因此，历史 10% 案例必须标为教学反事实；当前美国银行仍受跨行结算、现金需求、资本和流动性规则约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Which institutions, liabilities, and maintenance periods are covered by reserve requirements depends on the actual regime. A requirement is not a loan-by-loan quota of pre-existing lendable reserves. As checked on 29 August 2026, the Federal Reserve still reports a zero reserve-requirement ratio effective since 26 March 2020. The historical 10% example must therefore be labelled as a teaching counterfactual; U.S. banks remain subject to settlement, cash, capital, and liquidity constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 现金漏损与一般货币乘数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Currency drain and the general money multiplier*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义 $M=C+D$、$B=C+R$，其中 $C$ 是公众现金、$D$ 是口径内存款、$R=RR+ER$。再定义

$$
c=\frac{C}{D},\qquad r=\frac{RR}{D},\qquad e=\frac{ER}{D},
$$

则在这些口径一致时，代数上有

$$
m=\frac{M}{B}=\frac{1+c}{c+r+e},\qquad M=mB.
$$

这里 $c$ 是公众现金—存款比，不能链接到公司财务的“现金比率”。[[现金漏损与货币构成|现金漏损]]会减少回到存款体系的余额，却不必使 $C+D$ 当场下降：公众取现通常只是存款转成口径内现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $c$ is the public's currency-to-deposit ratio, not a corporate-finance cash ratio. A [[现金漏损与货币构成|currency drain]] reduces the balance redeposited into the banking system, yet a withdrawal need not reduce $C+D$ immediately because a deposit has usually been converted into currency included in the same aggregate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[货币乘数的因果边界|货币乘数首先是事后比率和约化关系]]。要把它用于因果预测，还要假定 $c、r、e$ 稳定、基础货币可外生控制，并写明银行、公众和央行的行为。现代充足准备金框架中，准备金数量可能是贷款、结算需求和央行利率操作的结果，而不是固定起点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[货币乘数的因果边界|money multiplier is first an ex post ratio and reduced-form relationship]]. A causal forecast additionally requires stable $c$, $r$, and $e$, an exogenously controlled base, and explicit behaviour by banks, the public, and the central bank. In an ample-reserves framework, reserve quantities may be an outcome of lending, settlement demand, and interest-rate implementation rather than a fixed starting point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 判断货币是否被创造：资产负债表程序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*[[货币创造的记账诊断|Diagnosing money creation: a balance-sheet procedure]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每次判断都要：固定日期、法域、货币口径、发行部门和货币持有部门；为每个主体逐笔写分录；追踪同行、跨行、现金和央行结算；按该口径合并完整货币发行部门并抵销内部项目。若口径纳入 MMF、电子货币机构或其他发行人，只合并商业银行不够。最后还要把交易流量与价格、汇率、核销/减记和重分类引起的存量变化分开。货币持有部门的口径内货币负债因交易增加才叫创造，减少才叫销毁；只换持有人叫转移，在同一口径内存款换现金叫构成变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every diagnosis, fix the date, jurisdiction, aggregate, money-issuing sector, and money-holding sector; write each party's entries transaction by transaction; trace on-us, interbank, cash, and central-bank settlement; and consolidate the full issuing sector defined by that aggregate. Consolidating only commercial banks is insufficient if the aggregate includes MMFs, e-money institutions, or other issuers. Finally separate transaction flows from stock changes caused by price, exchange-rate, write-off/write-down, or reclassification adjustments. An increase in in-scope monetary liabilities held by the money-holding sector through transactions is creation; a fall is destruction; a change of holder is transfer; and an exchange between deposits and currency within the same aggregate is a change in composition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
