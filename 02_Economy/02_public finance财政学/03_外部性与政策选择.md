# 外部性与政策选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Externalities and policy choice*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Bart在上游开工厂，Lisa在下游捕鱼。多生产一单位带来的收入归Bart，少捕到的鱼却由Lisa承担。如果双方各算各的账，这条河会被怎样使用？本课从原课堂的这个问题出发，依次把损害算进去、寻找可交易的改善，再比较税费、补贴、标准和排放交易。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bart operates an upstream factory; Lisa fishes downstream. Bart receives the revenue from extra production, while Lisa bears the loss of fish. How will they use the river if each follows a separate account? Starting from the original classroom example, this lesson incorporates damage, identifies gains from agreement, and compares taxes, subsidies, standards and emissions trading.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 先找到没有进入决策的那一项
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Find the effect missing from the decision*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[外部性]]

关键不是Bart是否知道河里有鱼，而是少排一单位是否会改变他的回报。还要区分另一种情况：如果新工厂只是提高了当地投入品的价格，其他企业的利润也可能下降，但这与污水直接破坏捕鱼条件不是同一机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The issue is not whether Bart knows about the fish, but whether emitting one unit less changes his return. A new factory could also lower other firms' profits by raising input prices; that is a different mechanism from directly damaging fishing conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[金钱外部性]]

确认影响后，下一问是“再多一单位会怎样”，而不是先把全部损失平均分掉。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the effect is identified, ask what one additional unit changes rather than dividing total loss by the number of units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[边际外部损害]]

## 2. 从私人最优算到社会效率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. From the private choice to the efficient quantity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给课堂情境补上一组可以完整计算的函数。产品价格固定为100，生产的真实资源成本为 $q^2/2$，每单位产品排一单位污染，暂时只能靠减产少排污。令 $q\ge0$，忽略其他市场扭曲、收入效应和实施成本，所有人的货币得失按同一权重相加：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use an explicit numerical model for the classroom setting. Output sells at a fixed price of 100, resource cost is $q^2/2$, and each unit of output emits one unit of pollution. Initially, cutting output is the only way to reduce emissions. Let $q\ge0$, exclude other distortions, income effects and implementation costs, and aggregate monetary gains and losses with equal weights:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$B(q)=100q-\frac{q^2}{2},\qquad D(q)=\frac{q^2}{2},\qquad W(q)=B(q)-D(q)=100q-q^2.$$

Bart只最大化 $B$，所以 $100-q_m=0$，得到 $q_m=100$。把Lisa也计入，条件变成 $100-q^*=q^*$，得到 $q^*=50$。这里的社会效率是明确账户下净价值最大，不是对分配公平或环境权利的最终裁决。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bart maximises $B$, so $100-q_m=0$ gives $q_m=100$. Including Lisa changes the condition to $100-q^*=q^*$, giving $q^*=50$. Efficiency here maximises net value within the stated account; it does not settle distribution or environmental rights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[外部性数量偏离]]

回到原课的图。它允许需求曲线向下倾斜，不是上面固定产品价格的同一组函数，但计算的逻辑相同：由市场数量降至 $Q^*$，避免的损害为 $dchg$，牺牲的私人净收益为 $dcg$，两者之差 $dhg$ 才是净改善。这里私人净收益一般包括产品市场消费者和生产者的剩余，不能无条件全叫“供应商利润”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original graph uses a downward-sloping demand curve rather than the fixed output price in our numerical model. Its accounting logic is the same: moving to $Q^*$ avoids damage $dchg$ and sacrifices private net benefit $dcg$; only the difference, $dhg$, is the net gain. In general that private net benefit includes product-market consumer and producer surplus, not solely the supplier's profit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20240315133213.png]]

在数例中，损害减少 $5000-1250=3750$，私人净收益减少 $5000-3750=1250$，所以净改善为2500。产量从100降到50；增加的是扣除损害的净价值，不是物理产量。为什么不继续减到零？要看最后几单位的收益和损害，不能从“污染有害”直接推出答案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the numerical model, damage falls by $5000-1250=3750$, while net private benefit falls by $5000-3750=1250$: the gain is 2500. Physical output falls from 100 to 50; net value rises. Whether to continue to zero depends on the benefits and damage of the remaining units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[最优污染边界]]

## 3. 双方能否自己达成改善
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Can the parties agree on an improvement?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[外部性内部化]]

先假设Bart有排放权。让他从100减至50，要补足1250的私人损失；Lisa最多愿为避免的3750损害付款。因此支付2000时，两人相对原状态都受益。这个区间说明协议有空间，却还没有说明他们必定能谈成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose Bart initially holds the emissions right. Reducing from 100 to 50 costs him 1250; Lisa would pay up to 3750 for the avoided harm. A transfer of 2000 benefits both relative to the initial situation. This establishes room for agreement, not certainty that agreement will occur.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[外部性谈判区间]]

![[科斯定理]]

把权利改给Lisa，谈判起点变成不准排污，付款方向也随之改变。要说最终仍然选择50，还需保证货币转移不改变相关边际估值、双方有能力实现同一配置，以及有效配置在这里唯一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If Lisa instead holds the right to clean water, the outside option is no emissions and the payment direction changes. Obtaining the same final quantity of 50 additionally requires unchanged marginal valuations under transfers, feasibility of the same allocation and uniqueness of the efficient choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[科斯产权不变性]]

现实难点随即出现：谁排的、伤害有多少、谁有权签约、协议怎么执行，都要付出资源。即使已经找到全部当事人，不可缺少的权利持有人也可能利用否决地位重新索价；另一些人则可能想让别人出钱，自己分享净化收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identifying emissions, damage, rights and enforceable terms requires resources. Even after all parties are identified, an indispensable right holder may demand more by withholding consent; others may prefer to let their neighbours finance cleaner water.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[交易成本]]

![[钳制问题]]

![[公共品搭便车]]

原课用“合并”和社会习俗提出另外两条思路。合并改变统一决策的范围；习俗可能改变声誉回报或人对他人得失的重视。两者都应问实际激励和成本怎样变化，不能只换一个组织名称或写一句道德要求。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original class also considers merger and social norms. Merger changes the scope of joint decision-making; norms may alter reputational incentives or concern for others. Their effect depends on actual incentives and costs, not simply an organisational label or a moral instruction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[合并内部化]]

## 4. 税费究竟对什么征收
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. What exactly is being taxed?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把“少生产”推广到“少排污”。先分清产量 $q$、实际排放 $e$、相对于固定基准的减排 $a$；采用清洁投入或末端处理时，三者不再一一对应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Generalise from producing less to emitting less. Distinguish output $q$, actual emissions $e$ and abatement $a$ relative to a fixed baseline. Cleaner inputs or end-of-pipe treatment break a one-to-one mapping.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[产量、排放与减排]]

沿用河流数例，暂仍只能减产。固定无治理排放 $e_0=100$，令 $a=100-e$，$0\le a\le100$。减排成本是放弃的私人净收益：$C(a)=B(100)-B(100-a)=a^2/2$，而不是又加一次原生产成本。于是 $MAC=a$，减排边际收益为 $D'(100-a)=100-a$，仍在 $a=50$ 相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep output reduction as the only abatement method in the river model. Fix baseline emissions at $e_0=100$ and define $a=100-e$, with $0\le a\le100$. Abatement cost is forgone private net benefit, $C(a)=B(100)-B(100-a)=a^2/2$, not another addition of production cost. Thus $MAC=a$ and marginal abatement benefit is $D'(100-a)=100-a$; they meet at $a=50$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[庇古税]]

![[排污费]]

每单位剩余排放收50，Bart会减排至 $MAC=50$。这对齐的是最优点的边际条件：$MPC+t$ 不会在所有数量上都等于 $MPC+MD(q)$，因为本例 $MD(q)=q$ 随数量变化。政府收到 $50\times50=2500$，Lisa剩下的损害却是1250；二者问的不是同一个量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A charge of 50 on each remaining unit induces abatement until $MAC=50$. It aligns the marginal condition at the optimum, not the entire $MPC+t$ and $MPC+MD(q)$ curves, since $MD(q)=q$ varies with quantity. Revenue is $50\times50=2500$, whereas Lisa's remaining damage is 1250.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[庇古税与损害赔偿]]

若允许安装净化设备，只对产品数量征税会漏掉什么激励？看税基是否随着真实减排而下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If purification equipment becomes available, which incentive is missed by taxing output alone? Ask whether genuine abatement reduces the tax base.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[产量税与排放税]]

## 5. 补贴和标准改变了哪些选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Which choices do subsidies and standards change?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[按量减排补贴]]

把按排放收费改成按减排奖励，在固定参与者和固定基准的同一个问题里，也可用每单位50诱导减排50。不过企业得到的钱、政府预算以及长期进入激励都变了。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With fixed participants and a fixed baseline, paying 50 per unit abated can also induce abatement of 50. Payments, public finances and long-run entry incentives nevertheless change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[税费与补贴的减排激励]]

正外部性的研发补贴是另一种用途：补足研究者没有取得的知识外溢收益。它不等于先制造污染再领取减排补贴，也不能把同一外溢同时加在收益端、减在成本端。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A research subsidy addresses a different missing benefit: knowledge gains not captured by the researcher. It is not a payment for reducing pollution from a baseline, and the same spillover must not be counted on both sides of the account.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[正外部性补贴]]

政府也可以直接规定结果或技术。判断这种规则是否合适，需要看目标能否监测、可否替代技术以及各企业成本，而不只是看它是否“强制”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Government can also specify an outcome or a technology. Suitability depends on observability, technological flexibility and firms' costs, not merely on whether compliance is mandatory.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[排放标准与技术标准]]

## 6. 两家工厂如何分担减排
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. How should two factories share abatement?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在换成两个企业的技术模型，不把原课没有提供的成本函数猜出来。两家基准排放各100，$C_1(a_1)=a_1^2/2$、$C_2(a_2)=3a_2^2/2$，各家 $0\le a_i\le100$。先把总减排目标固定为100，暂不讨论它是不是社会最优目标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Switch to an explicit two-firm technology model rather than guessing the functions omitted from the original notes. Both firms have baseline emissions of 100, with $C_1(a_1)=a_1^2/2$, $C_2(a_2)=3a_2^2/2$ and $0\le a_i\le100$. Fix total abatement at 100 before asking whether that target is socially optimal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[成本有效减排]]

解出75和25并不需要政府强迫这两项具体数量。给两家相同的边际价格75，各自选择也能得到同一分担；这里价格是75，不是原课缺少函数支持的50。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The allocation of 75 and 25 need not be directly assigned by government. A common marginal price of 75 decentralises it. This model's price is 75, not the original notes' 50 without specified cost functions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[共同排放价格与减排分担]]

![[总量排放交易]]

交易把“谁拿到初始配额”和“最终谁减排”分开。先问固定配额禀赋是否只改变总额财富，再问配额短缺或富余：低减排成本企业也可能因为初始配额少而成为净买家。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trading separates initial allowance ownership from final abatement. First check whether the endowment changes only lump-sum wealth, then calculate the surplus or deficit of allowances. A low-cost abater can still be a net buyer if its initial allocation is small.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[初始配额中性]]

![[配额净交易与边际减排]]

到这里证明的是“把既定目标做得最省”，还没证明这个目标值得实现。把环境收益加入外层问题，才重新接到本课开始的社会效率判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So far we have minimised the cost of a given target, not established that the target is worthwhile. Adding environmental benefits to the outer problem reconnects this analysis to the efficient quantity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[成本有效与社会最优]]

## 7. 把配置、钱款和制度放回同一张账
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Reconcile quantities, payments and institutions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先回到**单工厂河流数例**。下表只比较明示假设下的最终账户：Bart有初始排放权；补贴基准固定100；实施无成本、无其他税楔、无进入退出；各人货币权重相同。$B$ 已扣除生产资源成本，Lisa的列为损害与转移，未列不随政策变化的其他收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to the **single-factory river model**. The table assumes Bart initially holds the emissions right, a fixed subsidy baseline of 100, costless implementation, no other tax wedges or entry and exit, and equal monetary weights. $B$ already deducts production resources; Lisa's column includes damage and transfers but omits unaffected income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 制度 / Instrument | 排放 $e$ | Bart净额 | Lisa净额 | 政府净额 | 合计 $W$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 无干预 / No intervention | 100 | 5000 | −5000 | 0 | 0 |
| 谈判支付2000 / Bargain | 50 | 3750+2000=5750 | −1250−2000=−3250 | 0 | 2500 |
| 排放税50 / Emissions tax | 50 | 3750−2500=1250 | −1250 | 2500 | 2500 |
| 减排补贴50 / Abatement subsidy | 50 | 3750+2500=6250 | −1250 | −2500 | 2500 |

Bart、Lisa和政府三方的净额相加时，钱款抵消，资源和损害不会抵消。谈判一行让双方相对原状态都改善；税与补贴两行的分配明显不同。相同的合计只属于表头这些假设：若筹资另有超额负担、收入权重不同、补贴诱发进入或执行耗费资源，就要把这些后果再算进来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Transfers cancel across recipients and payers; resources and damage do not. The bargain improves both parties' positions relative to the initial situation. Tax and subsidy have different distributions. Equal totals depend on the assumptions: distortionary financing, distributional weights, subsidy-induced entry and implementation resources require additional accounting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[转移支付核算]]

最后放松监管者事先知道成本的假设。税锁定边际价格，严格总量工具锁定覆盖范围内的数量；该保护价格还是数量，要比较偏离造成的福利损失，不是数一数企业成本有多不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally relax the assumption that the regulator knows costs beforehand. A tax fixes the marginal price; a strict quantity instrument fixes the covered quantity. Compare the welfare cost of deviations rather than inferring the answer from cost differences across firms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[价格与数量选择]]

![[排放价格走廊]]

纸面规则还必须落在真实污染上：监测把排放变成可核对的义务，信用核证检验相对于无项目到底多减了多少，空间限制检验同一吨在不同地点是否真有相同效果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rules must track actual pollution. Monitoring converts emissions into verifiable obligations; crediting checks additional reductions relative to the no-project case; spatial restrictions test whether a tonne has the same environmental effect at different locations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[排放监测与履约]]

![[排放配额与减排信用]]

![[排放总量与局地损害]]

这些都是比较制度时的具体输入。存在外部性说明私人选择可能遗漏什么；是否采用某项政府干预，还要把可执行性和替代方案一起评价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are inputs into institutional comparison. An externality identifies what private choices may omit; selecting an intervention also requires feasibility and alternatives to be evaluated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[政府失灵]]

## 8. 应用自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. Apply the reasoning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不看答案，试着完成三步：河流模型若每单位税只收20，算实际排放和净价值；两厂商配额从50/50改为10/90，算谁净购买；若两污染源对同一居民区的影响系数分别为1和2，判断互换一吨是否仍保住同一环境目标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without the answer, solve three problems: calculate emissions and net value under a river-model tax of 20; identify net buyers when the two-firm allocation changes from 50/50 to 10/90; then test whether a one-tonne swap preserves the same environmental target when exposure coefficients are 1 and 2.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 核对推理
> 河流模型满足 $100-e=20$，所以 $e=80$、$W=100(80)-80^2=1600$，比无干预改善1600，但比最优少900。两企业在前述竞争条件下仍减排75/25，排放25/75，净购买为15/−15；便宜减排者也能净买。排放从5/5变成4/6时，总量仍10，居民区负荷却从15升到16；应重新规定有效环境单位或加入受体约束。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the river model, $100-e=20$ gives $e=80$ and $W=1600$: an improvement of 1600, but 900 below the optimum. Under the stated competitive conditions, the firms still abate 75/25 and emit 25/75; net purchases are 15/−15. Moving emissions from 5/5 to 4/6 leaves total emissions at 10 but raises receptor load from 15 to 16. Environmental units or receptor constraints must therefore be reconsidered.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
