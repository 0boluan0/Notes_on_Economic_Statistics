
# [[税收归宿与超额负担#超额负担|Excess Burden]] 超额负担
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Excess Burden*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 定义(Definition)
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Definition*
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 由于税收扭曲了经济决策，故产生了超额负担：福利损失超过所征收上来的税收收入
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Because a tax distorts economic decisions, it can cause a welfare loss beyond the revenue collected. This additional loss is the excess burden.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 又叫做福利成本(welfare cost)，或者无谓损失(deadweight loss)
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It is also called the welfare cost or deadweight loss of taxation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>

这里是一种非常特殊的情况，在征税前后被征税的物品的价格未发生改变，考虑此时税收是否出现了超额负担。

由E1到E3相当于被征收了一次性总赋税，没有超额负担，从E3到E2是补偿性反应。（又叫做替代效应）

This is a special case in which consumption of the taxed good is unchanged before and after the tax. Movement from E1 to E3 is equivalent to a lump-sum tax and creates no excess burden. Movement from E3 to E2 is the compensated, or substitution, response. The conclusion is that the change in relative prices—and the resulting compensated response—creates excess burden even when the taxed quantity happens not to change overall.

## 一次总赋税
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Lump-Sum Tax*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 概念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Concept*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Lump sum taxes (一次总付税)：纳税人必须支付的一定数额 ，而与纳税人的行为无关
	• 一次总付税的税收收入等于它的等价变化，所以没有超额负担
	• 使消费者处在和课征大麦税时相同的无差异曲线上的一次总付税，能够给政府带来更高的税收收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A lump-sum tax requires a fixed payment independent of the taxpayer's behaviour. Its revenue equals the equivalent variation, so it creates no excess burden. A lump-sum tax that leaves the consumer on the same indifference curve as the barley tax can therefore raise more revenue than the distortionary barley tax.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 优点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Advantage*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无超额负担
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It creates no excess burden because it does not change marginal incentives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Problem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税收公平性问题
e.g., 对富人征收1000元 v.s. 对低收入人群征收1000元。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The central problem is fairness: a fixed payment of 1,000 yuan imposes a very different burden on a rich person and a low-income person.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 图解税收的超额负担
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Graphing the Excess Burden of a Tax*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设税前价格与数量为 $(P_b,q_1)$，从价税税率为 $t_b$ 后消费者价格变为 $(1+t_b)P_b$，数量降至 $q_2$。税收收入为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let pre-tax price and quantity be $(P_b,q_1)$. With an ad valorem tax rate $t_b$, the consumer price becomes $(1+t_b)P_b$, quantity falls to $q_2$, and tax revenue is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
T=t_bP_bq_2
$$
（矩形面积）；超额负担为需求曲线与税后价格线之间、从 $q_2$ 到 $q_1$ 的三角形，近似为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the rectangular revenue area. Excess burden is the triangle between the demand curve and the post-tax price from $q_2$ to $q_1$, approximately:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
\text{Excess burden}\approx \frac{1}{2}\eta P_b q_1 t_b^2.
$$

政府收税收走了浅色的部分，深色的部分并未被政府收走但是还是损失掉了，所以叫做超额负担。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Government collects the light-coloured rectangle. The dark triangle is lost surplus that no one receives, which is why it is called excess burden.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
