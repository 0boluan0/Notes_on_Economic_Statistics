### 差别税收归宿
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Differential Tax Incidence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在政府预算保持不变的情况下,以一种税代替另一种税时,税收归宿有何不同.

由于这是一种考察变化的方法,所以必须找一个参照点,在假设上作为比较基础的“其他税”,通常被假定为一次总付税.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differential incidence asks what changes when one tax replaces another while the government budget remains fixed. Because it analyses a change, it needs a reference tax; a lump-sum tax is commonly used as the hypothetical benchmark.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 税收累进性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Tax Progressivity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 平均税率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Average Tax Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
平均税率=纳税额/收入
$$

*  比例制:平均税率不变
* 累进制:平均税率随收入上升上升.
* 累退制:平均税率随收入上升下降.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The average tax rate equals tax paid divided by income.
* Under a proportional system, the average rate is constant.
* Under a progressive system, it rises with income.
* Under a regressive system, it falls with income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 边际税率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Marginal Tax Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收入每增加一单位税收增加的值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The marginal tax rate is the additional tax due when income increases by one unit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 案例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在某一地区,每个人在计算他的应纳税额时,先从收入中扣除3000美元,剩下的收入按20%计算纳税,如果差值为负数那么获得差值20%的补贴,这是一种边际税率不变的累进制税
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose each person deducts $3,000 from income and pays 20 per cent of the remainder; if the remainder is negative, the person receives 20 per cent of that shortfall as a subsidy. The marginal tax rate is constant, but the average rate rises with income, so the schedule is progressive.

# 局部均衡模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Partial-Equilibrium Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只分析征税的市场,不考虑税收对其他市场的影响,当课税商品的市场与整个经济相比很小时这种分析方式最为恰当.分析工具是完全竞争下的供求模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Partial-equilibrium analysis studies only the taxed market and ignores effects on other markets. It is most appropriate when the taxed market is small relative to the economy, and it uses the competitive supply-and-demand model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 单位税
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Specific Tax*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Definition*
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对销售的每单位商品征收一个固定的税额.法定归宿可以是消费者或生产者
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A specific tax imposes a fixed amount on each unit sold. The statutory obligation may be assigned to either consumers or producers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
### 情形一:法定归宿为消费者
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Case One: Consumers Have the Statutory Obligation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相当于是将需求曲线向下平移.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tax is represented by shifting the demand curve downward by the amount of the tax.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 情形二:法定归宿为生产者
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Case Two: Producers Have the Statutory Obligation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相当于将供给曲线向上平移,最终结果和法定归宿为消费者的情况相同
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tax shifts the supply curve upward. The resulting economic incidence is the same as when consumers have the statutory obligation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==单位税的归宿与对消费者课征还是对生产者课证无关.单位税的归宿取决于供求弹性==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==The incidence of a specific tax does not depend on whether consumers or producers remit it. It depends on supply and demand elasticities.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


* 供给弹性越大，生产者负担的税收越少
* 需求弹性越大，消费者负担的税收越少
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* The more elastic supply is, the smaller the burden borne by producers.
* The more elastic demand is, the smaller the burden borne by consumers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从价税
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Ad Valorem Tax*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Definition*
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 税率为价格一定比例的税
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An ad valorem tax is charged as a percentage of price.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 分析方法和单位税差不多,就是曲线移动变成了百分比移动,斜率也变了
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The analysis resembles that of a specific tax, but the wedge is proportional to price, so the relevant curve also changes slope.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从价税可理解为在每个数量点把价格按比例抬高：消费者支付价与生产者获得价之间形成按比例税楔子。其结果是均衡数量下降，税收收入等于“税楔子 × 税后成交量”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At every quantity, the tax creates a proportional wedge between the price paid by consumers and the price received by producers. Equilibrium quantity falls, and revenue equals the tax wedge multiplied by the post-tax quantity traded.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>

## 哈伯格模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Harberger Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 假设条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Assumptions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 技术:
	1. 各部门的企业都适用资本和劳动来生产其产品.
	2. 规模收益不变,两种投入品都翻一倍,产量也翻一倍
	3. 生产技术的不同表现在资本替代劳动的难易程度(替代弹性)和生产中使用的资本与劳动比率的不同,那个高就是什么密集型行业.
2. 要素供给者行为:
	1. 资本和劳动完全流动,每个部门的劳动的边际收益率必然相等,资本也是.
3. 市场结构
	1. 企业相互竞争且追求利润最大化,所有价格(包括劳动)都有完全弹性.
4. 要素总供给
	1. 资本和劳动的总量是固定的
5. 消费者偏好
	1. 所有消费者都有相同的偏好,税收不可能通过影响人们的收入用途产生任何的分配效应
6. 税收归宿分析框架
	1. 差别税负分析框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Technology: both sectors use capital and labour; production has constant returns to scale; sectors differ in factor-substitution elasticities and capital-labour intensity.
2. Factor mobility: capital and labour move freely, so each factor earns the same marginal return across sectors.
3. Market structure: firms compete and maximise profit, and prices, including wages, are fully flexible.
4. Aggregate factor supplies: total capital and labour are fixed.
5. Consumer preferences: all consumers share the same preferences, ruling out distributional effects through differences in how income is spent.
6. The analysis uses a differential-incidence framework.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
