# 公共支出与项目评价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Public expenditure and project appraisal*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政府多花了钱，是否意味着公共服务改善了？一座桥能够收回建设款，是否意味着值得建？本课把这两个问题连起来：先读懂支出的统计口径和用途，再从资源、行为和福利变化评价一个具体方案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Does more government spending improve public services? Is a bridge worthwhile because it can recover its construction payments? This lesson connects these questions, moving from expenditure measures and purposes to resources, behaviour and welfare in project appraisal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 先问支出数字量的是什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. What does the expenditure figure measure?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财政支出]]

原课将财政支出看作资金配置过程及其数量结果。前者问钱怎样安排，后者问按什么范围报告多少；二者都还没有回答钱用得是否值得。先把同一笔钱放进正确的分类，再比较规模。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original class treats expenditure as both an allocation process and its monetary result. Neither establishes value for money. Classify a transaction correctly before comparing aggregate amounts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财政支出分类]]

学校支付教师工资、购买校舍和发放助学金都服务于教育，却不是同一种经济交易。把功能相同误认为资源含义相同，是本章首先要避免的混淆。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Teacher salaries, school buildings and student grants all serve education, but represent different transactions. A common function does not imply identical resource use.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[购买支出与转移支出]]

![[经常支出与资本支出]]

接着区分“准备花”和“已经花”。一项经批准的预算是授权与计划；执行结果要看相应决算。低于预算可能来自节约，也可能来自项目没有推进，只有支出差额还无法分辨。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next distinguish planned from actual expenditure. An approved budget authorises and plans spending; the outturn records execution. Underspending can reflect efficiency or delayed delivery, and the difference alone cannot distinguish them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[预算支出与决算支出]]

读取国际数据时，还会遇到 expense 和 expenditure。它们是特定统计体系中的量，不应按英语日常同义词处理；尤其不能把购买资产、消耗资产和偿还本金混成同一项“当期成本”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
International data distinguish expense from expenditure as statistical quantities, not everyday synonyms. Asset acquisition, asset consumption and repayment of principal must not be merged into one current-cost category.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[GFS费用与支出]]

### 用同一组数字区分四种规模指标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distinguish four scale measures using one dataset*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设一个自造经济中，财政支出 $G$ 从 100 增至 110，GDP $Y$ 从 1000 增至 1200。两期采用相同部门范围、同一计价口径。我们想知道的是：支出在经济中占多大，增得多快，以及它与 GDP 变化的数量关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed economy, spending rises from 100 to 110 and GDP from 1,000 to 1,200, using a consistent sector and price basis. We distinguish the spending share, its growth and its numerical relationship to GDP growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财政支出占比]]

![[财政支出增长率]]

本例支出增长了 10%，但 GDP 增长了 20%；所以支出占比由 10% 降至约 9.17%。**总量增加和相对份额下降可以同时发生。**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Spending grows by 10% while GDP grows by 20%, so the expenditure share falls from 10% to approximately 9.17%. An increasing total can coexist with a declining share.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财政支出边际倾向]]

![[财政支出弹性]]

增量之比为 $10/200=0.05$，增长率之比为 $10\%/20\%=0.5$；两者分母不同。前者表示本期 GDP 每增加一单位伴随多少支出增加，后者是相对增长关系。这个两期计算本身不能证明“GDP 增加导致政府支出增加”，更不能直接解释财政政策的乘数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The increment ratio is $10/200=0.05$ and the growth-rate ratio is $10\%/20\%=0.5$. Their denominators differ. These two-period descriptions neither establish causation from GDP to expenditure nor estimate a fiscal multiplier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 再问规模为什么变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Why might the scale of expenditure change?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原课随后引入三种历史解释。它们分别从经济社会发展、重大冲击和发展阶段讨论支出变化，不是三条保证所有国家都沿同一路径走的定律。阅读时抓住每个假说的机制，以及它需要什么历史证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original class introduces three historical explanations, focusing on development, major disturbances and development stages. They are mechanisms to investigate, not guarantees of one trajectory for every country.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[瓦格纳假说]]

![[财政支出的替代效应]]

![[发展阶段与财政支出]]

现在可以区分两种回答：“这一时期财政支出确实上升了”是测量结果；“城市化扩大了公共服务需求”或“冲击改变了公众可接受的税负与政府职责”是需要进一步核验的解释。课程里的历史图表可以帮助提出问题，但年代、统计边界不明的数据不宜当成今天的事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Expenditure rose” is a measurement; urbanisation-driven demand or shock-induced changes in tax tolerance and government responsibilities are explanations requiring evidence. Historical course charts can motivate questions, but undated or undefined series should not be treated as current facts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 用部门支出练习判断干预理由
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Diagnose intervention rationales through sectoral expenditure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样叫公共支出，行政、国防、教育、科研和医疗面对的问题并不相同。先把上一课的判断标准接过来：哪些损失来自激励或信息缺口，哪些安排来自明确的分配目标？提出理由之后，还要比较具体工具。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Administration, defence, education, research and health face different problems. Reuse the previous lesson's distinction between incentive or information failures and distributive objectives, then compare concrete instruments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[政府干预的效率与分配理由]]

### 行政与国防：公共目标不等于固定生产方式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Administration and defence: public objectives do not fix the production arrangement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行政支出支持机构运转、公共秩序和一般管理。原课的“非生产性”属于其直接生产与消费分类，不能由此推出行政服务没有社会价值。比如审批系统改进是否值得，应比较资源投入与办事时间、错误率等结果，而不是仅看经费增加还是减少。部门重叠、程序繁杂是需要证据核实的诊断，也不是每笔行政经费天然无效的证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Administrative spending supports institutions, public order and general management. The original class's “non-productive” label does not establish zero social value. An improved approval system should be assessed through resources, processing time and errors, not spending alone. Claims about duplication or cumbersome procedures require evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

国防服务在给定覆盖范围内具有典型的非竞争、难排他特征；军用车辆、军服和采购合同却是投入，不因用途是国防就都变成公共品。由政府筹资，也不必要求每一个零部件由政府生产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Defence services have the familiar non-rival, difficult-to-exclude character within a defined area. Vehicles, uniforms and contracts are inputs, not automatically public goods. Public financing need not mean public production of every component.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公共品的筹资、生产与治理]]

![[Pasted image 20240325172528.png]]

原课“大炮—黄油”图表达资源约束：增加国防产出会占用本可生产民用品的资源。这张图没有画出社会评价，因此**只凭生产可能性曲线找不到最优国防规模**。只有补上目标、可行范围以及光滑内点等条件，才可能用切点求候选解；角点还须单独比较。这里横纵轴是两类产出，不应误读成两个人的效用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original guns–butter figure expresses a resource trade-off, not a social optimum: it has no social valuation curve. Tangency can identify a candidate only after specifying objectives, feasibility and smooth interior conditions; corners require separate comparison. Its axes are outputs, not two people's utilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 教育：私人回报、外溢和分配需要分别说明
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Education: distinguish private returns, spillovers and distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课堂中的教师时间和座位有容量约束，不能把“教育有社会收益”直接翻译成“每一项教学服务都是纯公共品”。教育带来的本人能力和收入改善首先是私人收益；若还影响旁人的生产率或公共参与，才要另查外溢的机制与证据。学生未来工资较高本身，不足以测出外部收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Teacher time and classroom places have capacity limits. Social benefits do not make every educational service a pure public good. The student's own skills and earnings are initially private benefits; effects on others' productivity or civic participation require separate evidence. Higher future wages alone do not measure spillovers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再设一位学生预期能从教育获益，却无法把未来劳动收入充分用于取得今天的贷款：融资约束可能使私人收益高的学习也无法实现。要继续追问契约、信息和可执行性，不能把每次拒贷都叫作严格意义的 [[信贷配给]]。助学贷款针对融资时点，助学金还可能服务于机会与分配目标；普遍免费教育则同时改变价格与供给，三种工具的效果不能互相代替。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A student may expect substantial returns yet be unable to finance today's study against future earnings. Examine contracts, information and enforceability; not every rejection is strict [[信贷配给|credit rationing]]. Loans address financing across time, grants can address opportunity and distribution, and universal free provision changes prices and supply. Their effects are not interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，原课“优值品”和教育公平的论述，应把采用的社会目标说清；不能仅由教育投入增加就断言收入分配必然改善。实际判断仍要看谁新增受益、原有家庭投入是否改变，以及学习结果是否真的改善。[MIT 教育课程](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14-41-public-finance-and-public-policy-fall-2024/ocw_1441_lec09_2024oct02_transcript.pdf)第 1–6 页把干预理由与具体供给、代金券安排的行为后果分开讨论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Merit-good and equity arguments must state the social objective. Increased spending does not establish improved income distribution: examine additional beneficiaries, household responses and learning outcomes. MIT's education lecture, PDF pp. 1–6, distinguishes rationales from the behavioural effects of provision and vouchers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 科研与文化：识别未被计价的影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Research and culture: identify effects missing from private decisions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

科技支出先分清研究活动的目的，再问成果怎样传播与使用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For research expenditure, distinguish the activity's aim before examining dissemination and use.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[基础研究]]

设一项发现公开后，可被未出资的团队使用，原团队却收不到这些收益；私人回报就可能漏掉一部分社会价值。反之，保密、专利许可或收费服务可以改变可排他程度。资助与产权安排分别改变成本负担和收益获取，须比较其激励与使用限制；不能用“研究风险高”单独证明补贴有净收益。共同面对的不确定性也不等于 [[信息不对称]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an openly available discovery benefits non-funding teams without payment to its creators, private returns can omit social value. Secrecy, licensing and services can alter exclusion. Funding and property rights change incentives and access differently; high research risk alone does not establish a subsidy's net benefit. Shared uncertainty is not [[信息不对称|asymmetric information]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

文化领域也要逐项判断。观众享受演出是消费收益；上下游企业获得订单首先是交易收入，不能自动再记一笔同额的外部收益。图书馆、公园和博物馆能否收费、何时拥挤，则是具体的使用条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In culture, audiences' enjoyment is a consumption benefit, while suppliers' orders are initially transaction revenues, not automatically an additional equal spillover benefit. Charging and congestion at libraries, parks and museums depend on actual access conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公共品类别可变性]]

这也预示后面的评价难点：文化享受未必有直接价格，却可以需要非市场估值；与此同时，门票、商户收入和资产升值必须检查是否已包含同一项最终收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This anticipates two appraisal tasks: valuing cultural experiences without direct prices, and checking whether tickets, business receipts and asset values reflect the same underlying benefit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 医疗：先找机制，再看谁付费
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Health: identify the mechanism before tracing payment*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

患者难以判断必要治疗和服务质量，是信息问题；可选择的服务者很少，是竞争结构问题；某种有效防传染措施减少他人感染风险，是外部影响；希望每个人得到基本治疗，则还包含公平目标。这些理由可以并存，但不能把所有诊疗都套成同一种正外部性，也不能由信息差直接推出市场竞争完全消失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Difficulty judging necessary treatment and quality concerns information; few available providers concerns competition; effective infection prevention can affect others' risks; universal access also expresses an equity goal. These mechanisms can coexist without making every treatment the same externality or eliminating all competition whenever information differs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[卫生筹资与服务提供]]

阅读原课的“政府、社会、个人现金卫生支出”时，把它们理解为课程使用的资金口径，而不是医院所有制的三分法。比较制度还要问保障哪些服务、患者自付多少、医院怎样获得支付；最后用健康结果与经济负担检查是否实现目标，而不是以费用总额代替成效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read the original government's, social and out-of-pocket categories as funding categories, not a three-way classification of hospital ownership. Compare coverage, patient payments and provider remuneration, then assess health outcomes and financial burden rather than total spending alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 政府投资：先确定项目，再安排融资与责任
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Government investment: specify the project before its financing and responsibilities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经常运行与长期能力相连：学校需要教师，也需要校舍；道路建成后仍需维护。前面的经济分类回答交易怎样记账，政府投资则把注意力转到要形成什么长期能力、谁组织实施。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Current services depend on lasting capacity: schools need teachers and buildings, and completed roads need maintenance. Economic classification concerns recording transactions; government investment focuses on the capacity created and its delivery.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[政府投资]]

原课用公益性、基础性、竞争性项目区分政策关注点。它们是分析用途的概括，不是可直接替代 GFS 的资产分类。大规模、长周期也不单独证明政府应投资；应具体说明缺少的服务、市场与融资机制，以及对私人活动的替代或补充。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course's public-service, foundational and competitive project categories describe policy roles, not a substitute for GFS asset classifications. Large scale and long duration alone do not justify investment: specify the service gap, market and financing mechanisms, and displacement or complementarity with private activity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一项私人先出建设资金、政府以后按服务表现付款的项目，可能改变全寿命维护激励，也形成未来支付承诺。是否值得，应同时比较服务质量、资源成本、融资条件、合同执行与政府承担的残余风险，不能只看当年财政有没有直接列出全部建设款。

Private upfront financing with later performance-linked government payments may change lifecycle incentives while creating future commitments. Compare quality, resources, finance, enforcement and residual public risks, not merely whether the entire construction payment appears in this year's budget.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 农业应用：产量、销售收入与农民处境不是同一个量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Agriculture: output, sales revenue and farmers' welfare are different quantities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原课把农业、农村、农民放在同一政策框架下，讨论灌溉、农村公共服务、收入与城乡关系。这些目标需要分别衡量。以农业生产为例，丰收增加产量，也可能压低价格；不能只观察收成就断言农民收入必然改善。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course groups agriculture, rural communities and farmers to discuss irrigation, services, incomes and urban–rural relations. These objectives require distinct measures. A larger harvest can depress prices, so output alone does not determine farmers' incomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[价格弹性与总收益]]

这给出了一个有条件的价格—收入机制，不是减产或补贴的直接政策处方。农户利润还取决于成本，家庭处境还取决于非农收入和保障；灌溉工程、收入转移与价格支持所改变的行为也不同。原课所列“三农”、价格剪刀差及政策性银行的历史论述保留在 [[02_Economy/02_public finance财政学/2_财政支出/05_投资性支出.md|投资性支出原始记录]]，阅读时保留其年代与制度背景。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The price–revenue mechanism is conditional, not a direct recommendation to cut output or subsidise it. Farm profit also depends on costs; household welfare includes non-farm income and protection. Irrigation, transfers and price support change different margins. Historical discussion remains in the [[02_Economy/02_public finance财政学/2_财政支出/05_投资性支出.md|original investment notes]] with its period and institutional context.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 把支出变成可评价的项目
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Turn spending into an appraisable proposal*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支出安排明确后，还要把“花钱”转成“准备改变什么”。下面用一座跨河桥梁贯穿评价：建设桥梁是手段，改善两岸通行与安全才是希望得到的结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once expenditure is specified, state what it is meant to change. A river crossing will run through the appraisal: building the bridge is a means; improving access and safety is the intended result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[项目投入产出与结果]]

![[成本—收益分析]]

不要从施工合同直接开始加总。先说明如果不建桥，原有渡运如何运行、路网是否还会改善、需求是否会增长；再确定把两岸居民、使用者、纳税人和受影响社区中的哪些人纳入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before adding the construction contract, explain how ferries, roads and demand would evolve without the bridge. Then define which residents, users, taxpayers and affected communities enter the account.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公共项目反事实]]

![[公共项目评价范围]]

### 从付款表走到资源表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From payments to resources*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

合同需要真实资金支付，但支付的金额与社会资源损失不是同一个概念。这不是把合同成本忽略，而是要说明收款者得到了什么，以及他们为此放弃了什么。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Contracts require financing, but payments and social resource losses are different quantities. Reconciling them means identifying recipients' gains and what they give up, not ignoring the contract.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财务与社会评价]]

![[社会机会成本]]

![[公共项目影子估值]]

## 6. 完整数例：一项两年期桥梁改善
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Worked example: a two-year bridge improvement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面的数字全部是教学假设，不是现实工程预算。为让计算可检查，设评价包含两岸居民、运营者和政府；金额采用同一年不变价；方案只提供两年服务，期末没有剩余价值和退役成本；列出的影响已经覆盖本例所有社会得失。比较基准是维持现有渡运，而不是完全没有交通。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All numbers are instructional assumptions, not an actual engineering budget. Include residents on both banks, the operator and government; use constant-price money. The scheme lasts two years, with no terminal value or decommissioning cost, and the listed impacts exhaust the constructed social account. The baseline retains the existing ferry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**第一步：确认增量结果。** 工程预测需分别给出有方案和无方案的需求、出行时间及风险，本例假设这些预测已形成每年价值 50 的出行收益和价值 20 的其他安全收益，均在各年末计量；两项人群和结果边界已核对，没有重复。若需求随广义成本变化，50 必须包括正确计算的新增出行剩余，而不是用建成后的全部交通量乘原有节约。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Step 1: Identify incremental outcomes.** With- and without-scheme forecasts are assumed to yield annual travel benefits of 50 and distinct safety benefits of 20, both valued at each year-end without overlap. The travel estimate must include correctly valued induced-trip surplus where demand responds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**第二步：计入资源成本。** 相对于维持渡运，今天的建设、占地和施工干扰新增机会成本合计为 100；每个年末新增维护资源成本为 10，已经扣除被替代渡运可避免的运行成本。故两年各自的净社会收益为 $50+20-10=60$。若运营方另收通行费，应在使用者支付和运营者收入两边一致处理；本例为简化设没有收费。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Step 2: Value resources.** Relative to retaining the ferry, incremental construction, land and disruption cost 100 today. Incremental maintenance costs ten at each year-end, net of avoidable ferry operating costs, leaving annual net benefit $50+20-10=60$. Tolls would require consistent user and operator accounting; this example assumes none.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**第三步：折到同一时点。** 设实际社会贴现率恒为 5%；这只是本例假设，不是推荐的公共项目统一参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Step 3: Discount consistently.** Assume a constant real social discount rate of 5% for this example, not as a universal project-appraisal parameter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta SNPV=-100+\frac{60}{1.05}+\frac{60}{1.05^2}
\approx 11.56.
$$

在这些假设下，方案相对于维持渡运增加约 11.56 的当期货币价值。不是把两年的 120 减去今天的 100 就说净收益 20，因为发生日期不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under these assumptions, the scheme adds approximately 11.56 in present monetary value relative to the ferry baseline. The undiscounted difference of twenty ignores timing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**第四步：找出结论依赖什么。** 保持成本与贴现假设不变，令每年的净社会收益为 $b$。刚好收支相抵时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Step 4: Find what drives the conclusion.** Holding costs and discounting fixed, let annual net social benefit be $b$. The switching value is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
b^*=\frac{100}{1/1.05+1/1.05^2}\approx53.78.
$$

若 $b=50$，净现值约为 $-7.03$；若 $b=65$，约为 $20.86$。这告诉我们收益预测是一个需要重点核验的环节，并不告诉我们两种情况的概率各是多少。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $b=50$, NPV is approximately $-7.03$; with $b=65$, it is $20.86$. This identifies a decision-sensitive forecast, not the probabilities of those cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**第五步：报告谁得谁失及未解决事项。** 本例假定影响齐全，现实评估则须另列仍未货币化的后果、预算来源、受损者及执行风险。即使净现值保持正值，也要与渡运升级等其他可行方案比较。补偿是否实际发生，应与“理论上可能补偿”分开记录。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Step 5: Report distribution and unresolved matters.** Unlike this exhaustive constructed account, a real appraisal must disclose remaining unmonetised consequences, funding, losers and delivery risks. Positive NPV also needs comparison with alternatives such as ferry upgrades. Actual compensation remains distinct from the possibility of compensation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 从事前评价回到执行和复盘
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Connect appraisal to delivery and evaluation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20240319151140.png]]

原课把全过程绩效、责任和激励放在支出改革的末尾。现在可以把它落实到桥梁：批准前写清预测与比较基准；执行中记录资源投入、交付和实际使用；建成后再检查出行与风险是否按预期变化、谁实际受益。预算执行率可以暴露进度问题，却不能代替这些结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original reform framework emphasises lifecycle performance, responsibility and incentives. For the bridge, state forecasts and baselines before approval, track resources and delivery during implementation, and evaluate realised travel, risk and distribution afterwards. Budget execution rates cannot substitute for these outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

持续监测回答项目是否按计划推进；评价进一步问哪些变化由项目带来、为什么、对谁有效，以及资源是否值得。若实际收益低于预测，应查需求、估值或执行中哪一环出现偏差，并把证据用于下一次方案比较。这个闭环依据 [Green Book 第 10 章](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book-appraisal-and-evaluation-in-central-government/the-green-book-2026#monitoring-and-evaluation) 的监测与评价区分；不是仅在项目结束时给一个完成分数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Monitoring tracks delivery against plans. Evaluation asks what difference the intervention made, why, for whom and at what resource cost. Investigate whether forecast errors arose in demand, valuation or implementation, then use the evidence in subsequent appraisals. This follows the Green Book's Chapter 10 distinction between monitoring and evaluation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读完后，用下面三问检查能否把整条路径用起来，而不只是记住名词。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use these questions to test the full reasoning path rather than recognition of terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 财政支出增加 10%，能否说明政府占经济的比重扩大、公共服务改善？
> 不能。比重要与同口径 GDP 增长比较；服务改善还需要结果证据。本课的 100/110、1000/1200 例中，支出增加但占比下降。投入也不自动成为改善。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Does 10% spending growth establish a larger government share or better services? No. Compare consistent GDP growth for the share and outcome evidence for services. In the course example, spending rises while its share falls.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 私人借款建桥、政府以后付款，是否同时消除了财政成本与项目风险？
> 没有。借款解决前期融资；合同付款仍需来源，风险须逐项看实际承担能力。评价要比较生命周期资源与社会效果，并另查公共预算承诺。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Does private borrowing with later government payment remove fiscal costs and risk? No. Financing, payment sources and actual risk-bearing capacity are distinct. Compare lifecycle social effects and resources alongside public commitments.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 桥梁净现值为正，又使沿线房价上涨，可以把升值再加进去并直接批准吗？
> 先查升值是否资本化了已计入的时间等收益。即使没有重复计价，仍须比较可行替代、分配、遗漏影响和风险；实际补偿与潜在补偿也要分开。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Can house appreciation be added to positive bridge NPV and approval follow automatically? First check capitalisation of already-counted benefits. Even without duplication, alternatives, distribution, omitted impacts, risks and actual compensation remain relevant.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
