## 为什么按这条主干组织
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the Course Follows This Backbone*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先用福利标准说明“更好”意味着什么，再建立交换与竞争均衡的效率基准；只有这样，公共品、外部性、支出和税收才不是一串互不相干的政策名词。
课堂只能按时间线展开，这里按概念依赖重排：每一块先解决一个明确问题，再说明为什么下一块会出现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course first defines what “better” means in welfare terms and then establishes an efficiency benchmark through exchange and competitive equilibrium. This keeps public goods, externalities, expenditure, and taxation from becoming a disconnected list of policy terms.
A class can only unfold over time, but this view is reorganised by conceptual dependency: each block first solves a definite problem and then explains why the next block is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 2. 交换、竞争均衡与福利定理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Exchange, Competitive Equilibrium, and the Welfare Theorems*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

福利经济学提供评价语言，但“市场失灵”必须相对于一个市场有效运作的基准来定义。Edgeworth box 把禀赋、互利交换、契约曲线和竞争价格连起来，并把效率与公平明确分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Welfare economics supplies the evaluative language, but a “market failure” must be defined relative to a benchmark in which markets work efficiently. The Edgeworth box connects endowments, mutually beneficial exchange, the contract curve, and competitive prices while keeping efficiency distinct from equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[交换经济、Edgeworth box 与福利定理]]

### 3. 公共品与外部性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Public Goods and Externalities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

福利分析先说明政府为何可能介入；公共品和外部性是市场价格未覆盖全部社会收益或成本的典型机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Welfare analysis first explains why government intervention may be warranted. Public goods and externalities are standard mechanisms through which market prices fail to reflect all social benefits or costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公共品、搭便车与 Samuelson 条件]]

公共品展示了非排他和非竞争导致的极端价格缺失；外部性把视野扩展到任何未进入决策者价格的外溢成本或收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Public goods show an extreme form of missing prices created by non-excludability and non-rivalry. Externalities broaden the view to any spillover cost or benefit omitted from a decision maker’s price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[外部性与内部化政策]]

### 4. 公共支出、社会保险与公共选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Public Expenditure, Social Insurance, and Public Choice*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

确认市场失灵后，要看政府实际提供什么以及保险、再分配和政治过程怎样改变资源配置。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once a market failure has been identified, the next questions are what government actually provides and how insurance, redistribution, and political processes alter resource allocation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公共支出与成本—收益分析]]

成本—收益分析先评价一般公共项目是否创造净社会价值；社会保险再加入状态依赖转移、风险共担和行为反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cost–benefit analysis first asks whether a public project creates net social value. Social insurance then adds state-contingent transfers, risk sharing, and behavioural responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[社会保险：风险共担与行为激励]]

即使社会保险在福利上有充分理由，其规模和设计仍由政治激励与政府层级决定，因此还要进入公共选择和财政联邦主义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even when social insurance has a strong welfare rationale, its scale and design are determined by political incentives and levels of government, which brings public choice and fiscal federalism into the analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公共选择与财政联邦主义]]

### 5. 税收归宿与超额负担
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Tax Incidence and Excess Burden*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支出必须由收入支持；税收分析首先区分谁依法缴税与谁因价格调整真正承担负担。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expenditure must be financed by revenue. Tax analysis therefore begins by distinguishing the party legally responsible for payment from the party that ultimately bears the burden after prices adjust.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[税收归宿与超额负担]]

### 6. 最优税收、财政收入与政府预算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Optimal Taxation, Public Revenue, and Government Budgets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知道归宿和效率损失后，最优税收与预算才可在公平、效率、收入需求和政府融资之间作选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once incidence and efficiency losses are understood, optimal-tax and budget analysis can balance equity, efficiency, revenue needs, and government financing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[最优税收：效率、公平与信息约束]]

最优税收研究边际税制怎样在效率、公平和信息之间取舍；预算、公债与财政规则则把这些选择放进总量约束、跨期融资和制度执行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Optimal taxation studies marginal trade-offs among efficiency, equity, and information. Budgets, public debt, and fiscal rules place those choices within aggregate constraints, intertemporal financing, and institutional implementation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[政府公债、预算制度与财政规则]]
