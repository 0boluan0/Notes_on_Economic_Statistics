# 基础信息(Normative Analysis)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basic Information (Normative Analysis)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
* 以一定的价值判断作为分析的起点，首先提出一种标准，再论证研究对象是否符合这个标准，及变化趋势是否接近或远离所设定标准，以及如何才能达到所期望的结果。
* 目标：改善世界
* 回答的问题：什么是最好的？应该做什么？
* 一般来说，规范分析不存在检验的问题，也不可以计量，结论存在多样性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Normative analysis begins from a value judgement. It first proposes a standard, then asks whether the object of study meets that standard, whether observed changes move closer to or farther from it, and what could produce the desired outcome.
* Objective: to improve the world.
* Questions answered: What is best? What ought to be done?
* Normative conclusions generally cannot be settled by a direct empirical test or reduced to a single quantitative answer, so different value judgements can produce different conclusions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
# 福利经济学
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Welfare Economics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 研究各种经济状态下的社会满意程度
* 政策干预涉及到资源再配置，福利经济学的目的在于评价各种配置的社会满意程度
* 福利经济学提供了评价公共政策的系统框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* It studies social welfare under alternative economic states.
* Policy intervention reallocates resources, so welfare economics evaluates the social desirability of alternative allocations.
* It provides a systematic framework for evaluating public policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 福利经济学第二定律
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Second Fundamental Theorem of Welfare Economics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

社会通过对初始资源禀赋的适当安排，然后允许人们自由交易，就能实现帕累托效率配置：既能实现公平又不抑制效率

By appropriately redistributing initial endowments and then allowing people to trade freely, society can reach a Pareto-efficient allocation, separating the choice of equity from the efficiency of exchange.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

The utility possibilities curve (UPC) gives the maximum utility Adam can attain for each fixed level of Eve's utility. Points on the curve, such as p3 and p5, are Pareto efficient; an interior point such as q is inefficient.
A social indifference curve represents society's willingness to trade one person's utility against another's through a social welfare function, $W=F(U_{Adam},U_{Eve})$.
Maximising social welfare selects the attainable point on the UPC that reaches the highest social indifference curve. Graphically, the two curves are tangent at the optimum.

# 政府干预
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Government Intervention*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
存在于市场失灵时，以及市场不失灵的初始时。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Government intervention may be justified by market failure or by an inequitable initial allocation even when markets otherwise function efficiently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 垄断（买卖双方有一方拥有另一方没有的市场力量）
2. 信息不对称（交易一方有另一方没有的信息）
3. [[公共品、搭便车与 Samuelson 条件|公共物品]]
4. [[外部性与内部化政策|外部性]]
对于后三种情况而言，市场已经不存在了。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Monopoly: one side of a transaction possesses market power that the other lacks.
2. Information asymmetry: one side possesses information that the other lacks.
3. Public goods.
4. Externalities.
For the last three cases, the assumptions needed for a complete competitive market are absent.
