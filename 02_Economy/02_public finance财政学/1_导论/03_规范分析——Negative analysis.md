# 基础信息(Normative Analysis)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basic Information (Normative Analysis)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[02_Economy/02_public finance财政学/01_福利评价与政府干预|本单元连续讲解]] · [[福利经济学与政府干预.canvas|主题关系图]]

* 以一定的价值判断作为分析的起点，首先提出一种标准，再论证研究对象是否符合这个标准，及变化趋势是否接近或远离所设定标准，以及如何才能达到所期望的结果。
* 目标：改善世界
* 回答的问题：什么是最好的？应该做什么？
* 一般来说，规范分析不存在检验的问题，也不可以计量，结论存在多样性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Normative analysis begins from a value judgement. It first proposes a standard, then asks whether the object of study meets that standard, whether observed changes move closer to or farther from it, and what could produce the desired outcome.
* Objective: to improve the world.
* Questions answered: What is best? What ought to be done?
* Normative conclusions generally cannot be settled by a direct empirical test or reduced to a single quantitative answer, so different value judgements can produce different conclusions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- topic49-calibration:normative-evidence:start -->
> [!note] 价值标准与事实前提分开核对
> [[规范分析]]的英文是 normative analysis。价值标准不能仅由数据决定，但采用标准后可以作定量比较，其中的事实或因果前提仍需适当证据。因而“不能由实证单独决定标准”不能扩大为“整项分析不能计算或检验”，详见[[规范分析的经验前提]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The English term is [[规范分析|normative analysis]]. Data alone do not determine the value criterion, but a specified criterion permits quantitative comparisons, and factual or causal premises still need appropriate evidence. See [[规范分析的经验前提|empirical premises of normative analysis]] for the distinction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 福利经济学
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Welfare Economics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

* 研究各种经济状态下的社会满意程度
* 政策干预涉及到资源再配置，福利经济学的目的在于评价各种配置的社会满意程度
* 福利经济学提供了评价公共政策的系统框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* It studies social welfare under alternative economic states.
* Policy intervention reallocates resources, so welfare economics evaluates the social desirability of alternative allocations.
* It provides a systematic framework for evaluating public policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

消费的帕累托效率条件：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The condition for Pareto efficiency in consumption is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
MRS_{af}^{Adam}=MRS_{af}^{Eve}
$$

这个等式只刻画可微内点的边际候选；角点、折点和非凸偏好仍要直接检查是否存在可行 Pareto 改进。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This equality characterises a differentiable interior marginal candidate only; corners, kinks, and non-convex preferences still require a direct check for feasible Pareto improvements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 供给侧
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Production Side*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

生产可能性曲线，MRT边际转换率，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The production possibility frontier and the marginal rate of transformation (MRT).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[生产配置的MRS与MRT]]

在可微、内点且没有其他扭曲的标准模型中，生产可变时的帕累托效率条件为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the standard differentiable interior model without other distortions, Pareto efficiency with production requires:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
\begin{aligned}
MRT_{af} &= MRS_{af}^{Adam}=MRS_{af}^{Eve},\\
\frac{MC_a}{MC_f} &= MRS_{af}^{Adam}=MRS_{af}^{Eve}.
\end{aligned}
$$

在上述内点条件下，只要共同边际替代率不等于边际转换率，就有通过改变产出组合实现 Pareto 改进的机会。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under those interior conditions, a difference between the common marginal rate of substitution and the marginal rate of transformation indicates scope for a Pareto improvement through a change in the output mix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 福利经济学第一定律
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The First Fundamental Theorem of Welfare Economics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
在标准 Walrasian 模型中，若偏好理性且局部非饱和，**给定存在的**竞争均衡配置是 Pareto 有效的；这不证明均衡一定存在、唯一或由现实调价过程收敛得到。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the standard Walrasian model, rational and locally nonsatiated preferences make an existing competitive-equilibrium allocation Pareto efficient. This does not prove existence, uniqueness, or convergence of an actual price-adjustment process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[第一福利定理]]

## 福利经济学第二定律
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Second Fundamental Theorem of Welfare Economics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在凸性、连续性、非饱和与非退化等标准条件下，社会可先用预算平衡的一次总额转移调整财富，再由支持价格把一个**已经选定的 Pareto 有效配置**分权为竞争均衡。定理本身不决定哪个配置公平。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under standard convexity, continuity, nonsatiation, and nondegeneracy conditions, budget-balanced lump-sum transfers and supporting prices can decentralise an already chosen Pareto-efficient allocation as a competitive equilibrium. The theorem itself does not decide which allocation is equitable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[第二福利定理]]

### 效率与公平
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Efficiency and Equity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**第二定理不替社会选择公平标准；它说明在满足条件且能做不改变边际激励的一次总额转移时，分配目标与后续价格协调可在模型中分两步处理。**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**The Second Welfare Theorem does not choose an equity criterion. Under its conditions and with transfers that do not alter marginal incentives, it lets the model separate the distributive target from subsequent price coordination.**

The utility possibilities curve (UPC) gives the maximum utility Adam can attain for each fixed level of Eve's utility. Points on the curve, such as p3 and p5, are Pareto efficient; an interior point such as q is inefficient.
A social indifference curve represents society's willingness to trade one person's utility against another's through a social welfare function, $W=F(U_{Adam},U_{Eve})$.
Maximising social welfare selects the attainable point on the UPC that reaches the highest social indifference curve. Graphically, the two curves are tangent at the optimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] 相切是有条件的内点判准
> 社会无差异曲线是[[社会福利函数]]的等值线，不是个人在商品空间中的无差异曲线。可微、允许双侧移动且相关边际福利非零的内点最优才有斜率相等；角点可能最优而不相切，相切也可能不是最大值。完整推导和最小反例见[[社会福利最优的切点条件]]；固定某一人的最大效用也不独自证明[[强弱Pareto边界|强 Pareto 有效]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A social indifference curve is a level set of the [[社会福利函数|social welfare function]], not a person's commodity-space indifference curve. Slope equality requires differentiability, two-sided feasible movements, and the relevant nonzero marginal welfare at an interior optimum. Corners may be optimal without tangency, and tangencies need not maximise welfare. See [[社会福利最优的切点条件|the tangency condition]] and the [[强弱Pareto边界|strong-versus-weak Pareto distinction]].

# 政府干预
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Government Intervention*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
存在于市场失灵时，以及市场不失灵的初始时。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Government intervention may be justified by market failure or by an inequitable initial allocation even when markets otherwise function efficiently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 垄断（买卖双方有一方拥有另一方没有的市场力量）
2. 信息不对称（交易一方有另一方没有的信息）
3. [[公共品、搭便车与 Samuelson 条件.canvas|公共物品]]
4. [[外部性]]
后三种情况下，交易或供给仍可能存在，但价格信号不能保证完整反映质量、社会收益或社会成本，竞争结果因而未必有效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Monopoly: one side of a transaction possesses market power that the other lacks.<br>
**2.** Information asymmetry: one side possesses information that the other lacks.<br>
**3.** Public goods.<br>
**4.** Externalities.<br>
In the last three cases, exchange or provision may still occur, but prices need not fully reflect quality, social benefits, or social costs; the competitive outcome therefore need not be efficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
