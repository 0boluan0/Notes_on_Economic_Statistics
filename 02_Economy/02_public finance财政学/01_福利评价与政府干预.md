# 福利评价与政府干预
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Welfare evaluation and government intervention*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

房租降了，是否意味着住房政策更好？税率提高了，是否意味着政府一定按原税基多收到钱？这两个问题都不能只看一个数字。本课从家庭、企业与政府的行为出发，逐步建立效率、分配和制度比较的语言，最后完整分析一个租金上限案例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Does a lower rent make a housing policy better? Does a higher tax rate necessarily raise revenue by the amount calculated on the old tax base? Neither question can be answered from one number. This lesson starts with households, firms, and government, builds the language of efficiency, distribution, and institutional comparison, and then works through a rent-ceiling case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 从政府在经济中做什么开始
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Start with government's economic role*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财政学]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original class groups government and public enterprises into the public sector, and households and private firms into the private sector. This sector diagram identifies who receives payments, spends, and uses resources; sector labels do not determine efficiency in advance.

接着看循环流。实线画商品、服务与要素流动，虚线画资金流动。家庭向政府缴税，政府可以购买资源、提供服务或向家庭转移收入；同样一笔财政支出，其资源含义取决于它究竟买了什么或转给了谁。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the circular-flow diagram, solid lines represent goods, services, and factors; dashed lines represent payments. Households pay taxes, while government can purchase resources, provide services, or transfer income. The resource meaning of an expenditure depends on what it purchases or to whom it transfers purchasing power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20240302224751.png]]

先不判断政府应当做多少，而问一项规则改变后人会怎样反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before judging how much government should do, ask how people respond to a change in rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[实证分析]]

![[政策效应的机械与行为分解]]

回到课堂的工资税问题：税前时薪为 10，税率从 0 变成 20%。若税前工资暂时不变，净时薪从 10 变成 8；这是规则带来的机械变化。若原来工作 $L_0$ 小时，按原工时算税收是 $2L_0$。实际税收却是 $2L_1$，因为新工时 $L_1$ 还没有求出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to the class wage-tax question. With a pre-tax hourly wage of 10 and a tax rate rising from 0 to 20%, a fixed pre-tax wage implies a net wage falling from 10 to 8. That is the mechanical change. If initial hours are $L_0$, fixed-hours revenue is $2L_0$. Actual revenue is $2L_1$, because the new hours $L_1$ have yet to be determined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[劳动供给效应]]

现在可以准确说出缺什么：不仅要知道净工资下降，还要知道偏好、所在预算区间以及工时反应的大小。若用数据识别，处理组与对照组也必须支持相应反事实；具体方法可延伸到[[因果研究设计检查]]，不以“把其他所有变量放进回归”代替识别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We can now identify what is missing: preferences, the relevant budget segment, and the size of the hours response, not merely the fall in net wages. Empirical identification also requires treatment and comparison groups that support the counterfactual. See [[因果研究设计检查|the causal-design check]] for methods; adding every other variable to a regression is not a substitute for identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使已经知道工时与税收怎样改变，仍没有回答税制是否值得。接下来把评价标准说清楚。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing how hours and revenue change still does not tell us whether the tax is desirable. We next make the evaluative criterion explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 交换与竞争市场提供怎样的效率基准
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. The efficiency benchmark from exchange and competitive markets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

福利经济学提供评价语言，但“市场失灵”必须相对于一个市场有效运作的基准来定义。Edgeworth box 把禀赋、互利交换、契约曲线和竞争价格连起来，并把效率与公平明确分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Welfare economics supplies the evaluative language, but a “market failure” must be defined relative to a benchmark in which markets work efficiently. The Edgeworth box connects endowments, mutually beneficial exchange, the contract curve, and competitive prices while keeping efficiency distinct from equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[交换经济、Edgeworth box 与福利定理.canvas|打开交换经济与福利定理总图]]

先把箱内一点读成资源约束下两个人的完整配置，再用 Pareto 标准区分“可行”与“仍可改进”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First read each point in the box as a complete two-person allocation under resource constraints, then use the Pareto criterion to separate feasibility from remaining room for improvement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[交换经济可行配置]]

![[Pareto改进与有效]]

给定初始禀赋后，互利透镜筛出双方都愿意接受的方向；契约曲线再标出交换收益已经用尽的位置。两者不是同一个集合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given an initial endowment, the mutually beneficial lens identifies directions acceptable to both parties, while the contract curve marks where gains from exchange are exhausted. The two sets are not identical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[互利透镜]]

![[契约曲线]]

效率集合仍不是市场均衡：竞争均衡还必须同时满足由禀赋价值决定的预算最优与每个商品市场出清。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The efficiency set is not yet a market equilibrium: competitive equilibrium additionally requires endowment-valued budget optimisation and market clearing for every commodity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[竞争均衡预算与出清]]

第一福利定理从给定均衡推出效率；第二福利定理的反向分权另需凸性、支持价格与一次总额转移。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The First Welfare Theorem maps a given equilibrium to efficiency. The reverse decentralisation result requires convexity, supporting prices, and lump-sum transfers under the Second Welfare Theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[第一福利定理]]

![[第二福利定理]]

两条定理都不判定公平，也不把交易成本、信息、市场力量或现实税制问题自动消除。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Neither theorem determines equity or makes transaction costs, information, market power, or real tax-system problems disappear.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[福利定理边界]]

若从纯交换扩展到生产，还要把消费者共同 MRS 与生产机会成本 MRT 对齐。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Extending pure exchange to production additionally requires alignment between consumers' common MRS and the production MRT.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[生产配置的MRS与MRT]]

下面这张原课图总结的是上述光滑内点的边际条件；它不是一条不带模型假设的现实市场保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original slide below summarises those smooth interior marginal conditions; it is not an unconditional guarantee about real markets.

## 3. 从商品配置转到效用与社会评价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Move from commodity allocations to utility and social evaluation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读下面的原课图时，先看坐标：横轴是苹果，纵轴是无花果叶，Adam 与 Eve 的原点相反。因此它仍是商品空间的 Edgeworth box，粗线表示契约曲线。要画效用可能性图，还需要把每个配置分别代入两人的效用函数，改用 $(u_{Adam},u_{Eve})$ 作坐标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read the axes first: apples horizontally, fig leaves vertically, with opposite origins for Adam and Eve. This is still an Edgeworth box in commodity space, with a contract curve drawn in bold. A utility-possibility diagram requires evaluating both people's utility at each allocation and changing coordinates to $(u_{Adam},u_{Eve})$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20240312091947.png]]

图中的 $p_3$、$p_5$ 和 $q$ 首先都是商品配置。以下定义才给出从配置到效用空间的准确转换，并说明教材对“可能集”有不同约定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The points $p_3$, $p_5$, and $q$ are initially commodity allocations. The next definition specifies the move to utility space and explains the conventions used for the possibility set.

![[效用可能集]]

有了可行效用范围，再问是否存在“无人受损而至少一人更好”的替代。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given the feasible utility range, ask whether an alternative can benefit someone without harming anyone.

## 4. 货币核算怎样连接福利评价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Connecting monetary accounting to welfare evaluation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在单一市场图里，货币面积比直接估计每个人的效用更容易使用。但需要先知道面积是什么、需要什么条件，以及哪些款项只是换了持有人。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a single-market diagram, monetary areas can be easier to use than direct utility estimates. First establish what each area measures, the conditions it uses, and which payments merely change hands.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[消费者剩余]]

![[生产者剩余]]

固定成本有没有算进来，会改变“生产者剩余”与“利润”之间的关系；不要在同一张表里交换这两个口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether fixed costs are included determines the relationship between producer surplus and profit. Keep the accounting conventions distinct within a calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[生产者剩余与利润]]

![[总剩余]]

知道如何加总后，再问什么数量使这项净收益最大。边际收益等于边际成本的结论需要明确的单一市场与排序条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After defining the sum, ask which quantity maximises it. Equality of marginal benefit and marginal cost requires explicit partial-equilibrium and ordering conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[总剩余最大化]]

![[转移支付核算]]

回到第一节的循环流：同一笔钱由家庭付给政府，不会仅因移动一次就被社会毁掉；但筹集和使用它可能消耗资源、改变行为，也可能改变分配评价。核算与评价因此要分两步。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to the circular flow: a payment moving from a household to government is not destroyed merely by changing hands. Raising and using it can nevertheless consume resources, alter behaviour, and change distributive evaluations. Separate accounting from evaluation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通需求曲线记录固定预算下的实际选择；精确的货币福利比较往往需要保持效用的支出问题。先把两种需求口径放在一起读。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary demand records choices at a fixed money budget; exact money-metric welfare comparisons often require an expenditure problem that holds utility fixed. Read the two demand concepts together first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[普通与补偿需求]]

![[补偿变化]]

![[等价变化]]

![[剩余与补偿度量]]

CV 与 EV 分别选择新价格和旧价格作为货币比较的基准。写题时还要检查符号约定：本路径的两张定义卡用“受益为正”，因此涨价造成的损失给出负数；把消费者恢复到原效用所需支付给他的补偿金额取相反号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CV and EV use new and old prices, respectively, as the money-metric reference. Also check the sign convention: the two definition cards use gains as positive, so a harmful price increase gives negative values. The payment required to restore the consumer's original utility has the opposite sign to CV.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即便得到一组精确的货币损益，“有能力补偿”也仍然不等于已经补偿。现在将它与第二节的 Pareto 标准比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even exact monetary gains and losses do not make the ability to compensate equivalent to compensation actually paid. Compare this distinction with the Pareto criterion from Section 2.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[潜在补偿准则]]

![[卡尔多与希克斯检验]]

这一组对象会在后面的税收课再次出现：[[税收超额负担]]核算政府收入之外的净损失，[[税收公平]]则明确如何评价不同人的负担。税收评价因而同时调用剩余核算与分配标准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These objects recur in taxation: [[税收超额负担|excess burden]] measures the net loss after counting government revenue, while [[税收公平|tax equity]] specifies how different people's burdens are evaluated. Tax evaluation therefore draws on both surplus accounting and distributive criteria.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 从介入理由走到可实施方案
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. From an intervention rationale to an implementable policy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[市场失灵]]

![[政府干预的效率与分配理由]]

识别理由后，先把工具具体化。例如，同样为了让某群体获得服务，可以改变购买价格、规定供给规则、由政府直接提供，或由政府融资而交给其他组织提供。每种安排对行为、信息和预算的要求不同；“政府出钱”与“政府生产”也不是同一个决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After identifying a rationale, specify the instrument. Access to a service might be addressed through purchase prices, supply rules, direct government provision, or public financing of another provider. Each arrangement has different behavioural, informational, and budget requirements. Government financing and government production are separate decisions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[政府失灵]]

制度比较要从同一个现实问题出发，但不必假定各制度获得信息和执行规则的能力完全相同——这些能力及其成本恰好是比较对象的一部分。Coase 在讨论社会成本时要求同时计入制度运行和转换成本，并比较改变制度的全部后果。这里沿用[[福利定理边界|可行制度比较的边界]]，再用[[公共选择分析]]追问谁参与、遵循什么规则、受到什么激励。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Institutional comparison starts from the same real problem, but need not give all institutions identical information or enforcement capabilities: those capabilities and their costs are part of the comparison. Coase's treatment of social cost includes institutional operating and transition costs and the full consequences of change. Use the shared [[福利定理边界|feasible-institution boundary]], then [[公共选择分析|public choice]] to ask who participates, under which rules, and with which incentives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，写政策分析时，应依次说明：当前反事实、政策改动的规则、行为与资源后果、各群体得失、评价标准，以及执行和过渡成本。若替代方案也需要筹资、审批或监管，同样要算它的约束，不能只给其中一个方案算成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A policy analysis should identify the counterfactual, the rule changed, behavioural and resource consequences, group-specific gains and losses, the evaluative criterion, and implementation or transition costs. Alternative policies also need their financing, approval, and oversight constraints counted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 完整应用：租金上限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Worked application: a rent ceiling*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先研究一个刻意简化的市场：住房服务同质，偏好、收入、质量和技术固定；交易竞争且自愿，无外部性及其他市场扭曲，固定成本不随方案变化；上限执行有效，没有绕开上限的额外支付。这个模型描述统一价格上限，不自动覆盖所有只限制租约涨幅或豁免新建房的现实制度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with a deliberately simple market: homogeneous housing services, fixed preferences, income, quality, and technology, competitive voluntary transactions, no externalities or other market distortions, fixed costs unchanged across policies, and an enforced ceiling with no side payments. This uniform-price-ceiling model does not automatically represent every real system that limits rent increases or exempts new construction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[约束性价格上限]]

自拟供需关系为 $Q_d=10-P$、$Q_s=P$，只讨论相关非负区间。令需求量等于供给量，得到 $P^*=Q^*=5$。把合法最高价格设为 $\bar P=3$ 后，需求量是 7，供给量是 3，超额需求为 4。这里是沿着两条既定曲线变动，不是曲线自身移动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the constructed curves $Q_d=10-P$ and $Q_s=P$ over the relevant nonnegative range. Equating them gives $P^*=Q^*=5$. With a ceiling $\bar P=3$, quantity demanded is 7 and quantity supplied is 3, producing excess demand of 4. These are movements along the existing curves, not shifts of the curves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[短边成交条件]]

在充分匹配的附加假设下，新成交量是 3，而不是 7。短缺 4 也不等于“4 个原租户失去住房”：相对均衡，它同时包含少供应的 2 和低价格下新增愿租的 2。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the additional assumption of complete matching, the new traded quantity is 3, not 7. A shortage of 4 does not mean that four original tenants lost their homes: relative to equilibrium, it combines two fewer units offered with two additional units demanded at the lower price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 变量 | 竞争均衡 | 上限为 3、短边充分成交 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 价格 $P$ | 5 | 3 |
| 需求量 $Q_d$ | 5 | 7 |
| 供给量 $Q_s$ | 5 | 3 |
| 成交量 $Q$ | 5 | 3 |
| 超额需求 $Q_d-Q_s$ | 0 | 4 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Variable | Competitive equilibrium | Ceiling of 3, fully matched short side |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Price $P$ | 5 | 3 |
| Quantity demanded $Q_d$ | 5 | 7 |
| Quantity supplied $Q_s$ | 5 | 3 |
| Quantity traded $Q$ | 5 | 3 |
| Excess demand $Q_d-Q_s$ | 0 | 4 |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在才能问福利。但只知道租金和成交量，还不知道哪三单位需求获得了住房。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We can now ask about welfare, but rent and traded quantity alone do not tell us which three units of demand receive housing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[限价下的有效配给]]

为了得到一个能精确复算的基准，再假设准线性偏好且计价商品内点，使本例没有相关收入效应；前三单位最高支付意愿得到满足，成交供应来自最低成本单位，也没有排队、搜寻或额外执行资源成本。逆需求是 $10-q$，边际成本是 $q$。于是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an exactly reproducible benchmark, additionally assume quasilinear preferences with an interior numeraire choice, so relevant income effects are absent. The three highest willingness-to-pay units receive housing, the lowest-cost units supply it, and there are no queuing, search, or additional enforcement resource costs. Inverse demand is $10-q$ and marginal cost is $q$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
CS_0&=\int_0^5(10-q-5)\,dq=12.5,& PS_0&=\int_0^5(5-q)\,dq=12.5,\\
CS_1&=\int_0^3(10-q-3)\,dq=16.5,& PS_1&=\int_0^3(3-q)\,dq=4.5.
\end{aligned}
$$

总剩余从 25 降到 21，无谓损失是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Total surplus falls from 25 to 21. The deadweight loss is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
DWL=\int_3^5\big[(10-q)-q\big]\,dq=4.
$$

这里消费者整体剩余增加 4，生产者剩余减少 8，合计减少 4。但“消费者整体增加”不意味着每个租户都获益。获配顺序一变，消费者剩余就要重算；如果用排队或搜寻争取住房，还会发生本例没有计入的真实资源成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Aggregate consumer surplus rises by 4 and producer surplus falls by 8, for a net loss of 4. The aggregate consumer gain does not mean every tenant benefits. Different recipients require recalculating consumer surplus, and queuing or search can introduce real resource costs omitted from this benchmark.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**短期与长期要分别建模。** 若短期可出租存量完全固定，供给可画成垂直线：上限仍可产生超额需求，却未必立即降低出租数量。长期退出、用途转换、建设和维护反应，则取决于具体规则、成本及时间；不能把这些反应都塞进“降价，所以供给曲线左移”一句话。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Model the short and long run separately.** With a completely fixed short-run rental stock, supply can be vertical: a ceiling can create excess demand without immediately reducing quantity offered. Longer-run withdrawal, conversion, construction, and maintenance depend on the rules, costs, and horizon; they cannot all be represented by saying that a lower price shifts supply left.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用一项研究检查模型能走多远
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Use an empirical study to check the model's reach*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Diamond、McQuade 与 Qian 的 2019 年作者稿研究旧金山 1994 年对部分既有小型多户住宅扩展租金管制，利用建成年份造成的覆盖差异比较租户与地块。作者发现既有受保护租户更可能留下，同时受影响房东会通过产权转换和重建等方式退出受管制租赁用途。这是一个有地点、制度和比较组的经验结果，不是所有限租政策的统一参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diamond, McQuade, and Qian's 2019 author manuscript studies San Francisco's 1994 extension of rent control to some existing small multifamily buildings, using coverage differences by construction year to compare tenants and parcels. Protected incumbent tenants were more likely to remain, while affected landlords responded through conversions and redevelopment that withdrew properties from regulated rental use. This is evidence for a particular location, policy, and comparison group, not a universal parameter for rent regulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

论文概括受影响房东的租赁供给减少约 15%。核对测量口径很重要：Figure 7b 的纵轴是地块租客人数除以该地块 1990–1994 年平均总居住人数，2006 年后的平均系数为 $-0.145$（标准误 $0.075$），图示为 90% 置信区间。它不能改写成“全市住房套数减少 15%”。Figure 8d 还显示相关改造许可增加，因此这篇研究也不能用来证明所有维护都下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paper summarises a roughly 15% rental-supply reduction by affected landlords. Check the measurement: Figure 7b uses parcel renter counts divided by the parcel's average total population in 1990–1994. The post-2006 average coefficient is $-0.145$ (standard error $0.075$), with 90% confidence intervals plotted. This is not a 15% fall in the city's total number of housing units. Figure 8d also shows increased alteration permits, so the study does not establish that all maintenance falls.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子让政策评价回到完整问题：现有租户获得多少住房稳定性，谁未获覆盖，未来租赁供给怎样变化，实施和转换消耗多少资源？若比较住房补贴或供应端改革，也要分别识别其筹资、价格与供给反应。最后再说明所采用的福利与分配标准，而不让一张静态三角形或一个回归系数替代整项判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example returns policy evaluation to its full set of questions: how much stability do incumbents gain, who is uncovered, how does future rental supply change, and what resources do implementation and transition use? Comparisons with housing subsidies or supply-side reforms likewise require financing, price, and supply responses. State the welfare and distributive criterion rather than letting one static triangle or regression coefficient stand in for the entire judgment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 合上笔记后试一次
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Try once with the notes closed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有人说：“限租把房租从 5 降到 3，消费者剩余增加，所以这是 Pareto 改进，应该实行。”请指出这句话在哪几步增加了尚未证明的条件，并重新写成一段合格的政策论证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Someone argues: “Rent control lowers rent from 5 to 3 and raises consumer surplus, so it is a Pareto improvement and should be adopted.” Identify the unproved conditions added at each step, then rewrite it as a defensible policy argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 核对推理
> 先说明市场、时间、上限执行和匹配条件；再说明获配顺序、福利口径及遗漏成本。本例生产者剩余减少 8，固定成本不变且没有额外补偿，因而至少有生产者受损，不构成 Pareto 改进；整体消费者剩余增加也不说明每个租户都获益。是否应当实施，还需比较可行替代方案并给出分配和其他规范标准；潜在补偿不能当成已发生的补偿。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> State the market, horizon, enforcement, and matching conditions; then specify allocation, the welfare measure, and omitted costs. Producer surplus falls by 8, with unchanged fixed costs and no additional compensation, so at least one producer loses: this is not a Pareto improvement. The aggregate consumer gain also does not establish that every tenant benefits. Adoption additionally requires comparison with feasible alternatives and an explicit distributive or other normative criterion. Potential compensation is not compensation paid.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
