# 政府预算与公债融资
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Government budgets and public debt financing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政府决定提供一项服务，需要依次回答：谁有权安排这笔钱，收支怎样记账，缺口怎样融资，以及今天的安排怎样影响未来。本篇沿着这条线读下去。先弄清口径，再从一套账表推到债务路径，最后考察家庭反应与财政规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Providing a public service raises connected questions: who authorises the funds, how transactions are recorded, how a shortfall is financed, and what today's choices imply for the future. This chapter establishes the accounting basis, derives a debt path from one ledger, and then examines household responses and fiscal rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一、先有政策计划，再有支出授权
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From policy plans to spending authority*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“准备建一所医院”与“已经获准支出”不是同一阶段。预算把政策变成资金安排，批准、执行与事后报告则各有职责。先读概念与流程，再看原先已经学过的预算数和决算数如何比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A plan to build a hospital is not yet authority to spend. Budgets translate policy into financial plans; approval, execution and reporting have distinct roles. Read the concept and cycle, then apply the existing distinction between budgeted amounts and outturns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[预算周期]]

![[预算支出与决算支出]]

读到这里，应能区分预测、授权与结果。下一步再核对报表在统计谁、何时记录：即使数字都叫“政府支出”，机构范围或时间基础不同，也不能直接作差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
You can now distinguish forecasts, authority and results. Next establish who is reported and when events are recorded. Figures labelled government expenditure are not directly comparable when coverage or timing differs.

## 二、确定机构边界与记账时间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Establishing the reporting perimeter and recognition date*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般政府与公共部门决定纳入哪些机构；合并决定如何抵销其中的内部往来；现金与权责发生决定把事情记在哪一期。这是三个依次处理的问题。拖欠则把“义务已形成”和“款项已到期”进一步分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sector boundaries determine which units to include; consolidation eliminates reciprocal internal claims; the recording basis assigns events to periods. Arrears then distinguish an incurred obligation from one already past its payment date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[一般政府与公共部门]]

![[财政合并口径]]

![[现金与权责发生]]

![[财政拖欠]]

例如，把给地方的拨款与地方的最终购买重复相加，会高估整体支出；把12月已经验收但1月才支付的服务只看成“1月的事”，又可能漏掉年末义务。先解决这些差异，收入、赤字和债务才有共同的底座。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding an intergovernmental grant to the recipient's final purchase can double-count spending. Treating a December service paid in January as only a January event can omit a year-end obligation. Resolve these differences before connecting revenue, deficits and debt.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三、钱进来了，收入就增加了吗？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does receiving money necessarily raise revenue?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课堂上的“筹集财政资金”可以包含借款；这里计算财政余额时采用GFS统计定义，把收入与融资分开。收到税款、发行债券和出售资产都可能增加现金，却不以同样方式改变政府账户。先定义收入和公债，再逐项比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Classroom discussions of raising public funds may include borrowing. For the fiscal balance here, GFS distinguishes revenue from financing. Taxes, bond issuance and asset sales can all increase cash, but affect the accounts differently. Define revenue and borrowing before comparing the entries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财政收入]]

![[政府公债]]

![[财政收入与融资]]

这里有一个后续会用到的区别：出售金融资产属于融资交易；出售原有非金融资产则减少非金融资产净投资，可以改善净借出／净借入余额。两者都不能仅凭到账金额认作收入。公债作为债务工具的通用合同基础仍复用[[债券]]，无需在财政学另建一份债券定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One distinction will matter below: financial-asset sales are financing transactions, while disposal of existing nonfinancial assets reduces net investment and can improve net lending/borrowing. Neither cash receipt is automatically revenue. The general contractual definition remains the shared [[债券|bond]] atom.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 四、一套账表：赤字10为何需要净借款15？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*One ledger: why can a deficit of 10 require net borrowing of 15?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以下是本篇自造的教学账表，金额使用同一单位、同一年度、同一一般政府合并范围和权责发生基础。没有非债务负债交易。费用中的折耗与净投资中的折耗扣除对应，利息支出已经包含在费用中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The constructed ledger uses one monetary unit, year, consolidated general-government perimeter and accrual basis. There are no non-debt liability transactions. Depreciation in expense matches its deduction in net investment, and interest is already included in expense.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 本期项目 | 金额 | 解释 |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 收入 | 230 | 采用GFS收入定义 |
| 费用 | 220 | 含利息24、固定资本消耗6 |
| 非金融资产购置减处置 | 26 | 此处未扣固定资本消耗 |
| 非金融资产净投资 | 20 | 26−6 |
| 金融资产交易净增加 | 5 | 假设全部为现金与存款积累 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revenue is 230. Expense is 220, including interest of 24 and fixed-capital consumption of 6. Nonfinancial-asset acquisitions less disposals are 26; subtracting fixed-capital consumption gives net investment of 20. Financial assets increase by 5 through cash and deposit accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把余额的公式与初级余额读清，再回到这张表。这里的费用220加净投资20，等于不含折耗的费用214加净购置26，总额都是240，折耗没有被算两次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read the overall and primary balances, then return to the ledger. Expense of 220 plus net investment of 20 equals expense excluding depreciation of 214 plus net acquisitions of 26: both total 240 without counting depreciation twice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财政赤字]]

![[初级预算余额]]

于是，总体余额为$230-220-20=-10$，初级余额为$-10+24=14$。融资还要满足$-10=5-\Delta L_{\mathrm{交易}}$，所以负债交易净增加15。其中10补足赤字，5形成金融资产；“有初级盈余”“总体存在赤字”“债务交易增加15”可以同时成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The overall balance is $230-220-20=-10$, and the primary balance is $-10+24=14$. Financing satisfies $-10=5-\Delta L$, so liabilities rise by 15 through transactions: 10 funds the deficit and 5 builds financial assets. A primary surplus, an overall deficit and debt transactions of 15 can coexist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 五、从收支流量走到债务存量与债务率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From fiscal flows to debt stocks and debt-to-GDP*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在补上资产负债表：上年末债务600，GDP为1000；本期名义GDP增长4%，成为1040。除刚才的15净债务交易外，已有外币债务因重估增加12。先选总债务或净债务指标，再把交易、重估与最终存量接起来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Add the balance sheet: opening debt is 600 and GDP is 1000. Current nominal GDP grows by 4% to 1040. In addition to debt transactions of 15, currency revaluation raises existing debt by 12. Choose the gross or net measure, then reconcile transactions and revaluation with the closing stock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[政府债务总额与净额]]

![[债务存量流量调节]]

期末债务是$600+15+12=627$，而不是610。把赤字以外的变化合计为$A=5+12=17$，利用初级余额14和利息24，可把同一账写成$627=600+24-14+17$。接下来除以本期GDP，推导比例变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Closing debt is $600+15+12=627$, not 610. Let adjustments outside the deficit be $A=5+12=17$. Using interest of 24 and the primary surplus of 14 gives the same account as $627=600+24-14+17$. Divide by current GDP to derive the ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[债务率动态]]

本例有效名义利率$24/600=4\%$，恰好等于名义GDP增长。利差贡献为零，债务率变化完全来自$-14/1040+17/1040=3/1040$，约上升0.2885个百分点，达到60.2885%。若没有金融资产积累与重估，债务610对应58.6538%；这个反事实只改变调节项，其他条件保持原样。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Effective nominal interest is $24/600=4\%$, equal to nominal GDP growth. The differential contributes zero, leaving $-14/1040+17/1040=3/1040$, an increase of about 0.2885 percentage points to 60.2885%. Without asset accumulation and revaluation, debt of 610 would imply 58.6538%. This counterfactual changes only the adjustment term.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[债务稳定初级余额]]

稳定条件只是把变化设为零后解出需要的初级余额。它为政策讨论提供数量要求；这个要求能否实现，还要看下一节的财政能力与融资约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The stabilising condition solves for the primary balance that sets the ratio change to zero. It supplies a quantitative requirement; fiscal capacity and financing determine whether that requirement is feasible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 六、账算得平，偿债路径是否可行？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An accounting-consistent path still needs to be feasible*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逐期恒等式可以写出任意多年的账；跨期约束把这些年份折到同一时点。先保留终端债务，再说明何种条件允许把它消掉。最后才能问：所需盈余和融资安排在现实中是否可信。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Periodic identities can describe many years. The intertemporal constraint values them at one date, retaining terminal debt before specifying conditions that remove it. Then assess whether the implied surpluses and funding are credible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[政府跨期预算约束]]

![[债务可持续性]]

![[利差与债务可持续性]]

利率低于增长减轻了一项压力，但没有替其他项目作保证。另有一些风险尚未成为确定债务，却可能在冲击时转入财政账户；它们需要披露与情景分析，而不能把全部担保额直接塞入当期赤字。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interest below growth eases one pressure without settling the other components. Some exposures are not yet certain debt but can enter fiscal accounts after a shock. They require disclosure and scenarios rather than automatic inclusion of every guarantee's face value in the current deficit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 七、举债改变了谁的消费与资产？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Whose consumption and assets change when government borrows?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到这里，问题从记账转向行为。先固定政府购买路径，只改变征税与举债时点，才能看清融资本身的影响。若同时增加政府项目，就还需要另评项目的资源使用、服务收益与外部性，不能把两种比较混在一个结论里。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We now move from accounting to behaviour. Hold government purchases fixed while changing tax and borrowing dates to isolate financing effects. Adding a public project also requires assessment of its resources, services and externalities; that is a different comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公债融资与资源成本]]

![[公债挤出条件]]

![[公债代际负担]]

资源账户揭示哪些变化必须相互对应，却没有决定家庭究竟储蓄多少。李嘉图等价提供一组足以使消费不变的条件。把这组条件看清，才知道借贷限制、代际税负或其他机制具体改变了哪里。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Resource accounts constrain compatible changes without determining household saving. Ricardian equivalence supplies sufficient conditions for unchanged consumption. Understanding them locates precisely how borrowing limits, intergenerational taxes or other mechanisms alter the result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[李嘉图等价]]

![[借贷约束与税收时点]]

![[代际联系与债务中性]]

这些例子分别固定自己的利率、家庭和约束条件，不是同一个经济体的连续预测。这里的等价基准固定偏好与完整可行集合，因而保留同一最优消费选择；当约束改变时，税款现值相同仍可对应不同消费。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each example specifies its own rates, households and constraints; they are not consecutive forecasts for one economy. The equivalence benchmark holds preferences and the complete feasible set fixed, preserving the same optimal consumption choices. Equal tax present values can coexist with different consumption when constraints change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 八、长期能够偿债，也要接得上每个到期日
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Long-run capacity still requires funding at each maturity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面用有效利率描述整个债务存量；实际融资还要逐笔看期限与合同。相同本金，集中到期与分散到期的现金需求不同；较长期债务若采用浮息，利息也可能很快重置。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The effective rate summarises the debt stock, but financing requires contract-level maturities and terms. Equal principal can produce different cash needs when maturities cluster, while long-term floating-rate debt can reprice quickly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 九、赤字变化中，哪些来自周期？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Which balance changes come from the cycle?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回到年度预算：收入下降时，税收可能依既有规则自动减少。因此，赤字扩大未必意味着政府刚刚主动扩张。财政政策的共同定义仍见[[财政政策]]；这里依次分离自动反应、周期调整与其他暂时因素。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to the annual budget. Taxes may fall automatically with income under existing rules, so a wider deficit need not indicate a new discretionary expansion. Reuse [[财政政策|fiscal policy]] for the general concept, then separate automatic responses, cyclical adjustment and other temporary influences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财政自动稳定器]]

![[周期调整预算余额]]

这一节换用独立的小模型：实际产出95、潜在产出100、收入38、支出42，收入弹性1、支出弹性0。观测余额−4中，估计周期成分为−2，CAB为−2。它不是前面230/220那套账的更新，也不是政策对产出的因果估计。潜在产出的含义与测量困难复用[[潜在产出]]、[[产出缺口]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This section uses a separate small model: actual output 95, potential output 100, revenue 38, spending 42, unit revenue elasticity and zero spending elasticity. An observed balance of −4 decomposes into a cyclical component of −2 and CAB of −2. This neither updates the earlier ledger nor estimates policy's causal effect on output. Reuse [[潜在产出|potential output]] and [[产出缺口|the output gap]] for the underlying concepts.

## 十、把长期约束落实到年度预算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Connecting long-term constraints to annual decisions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

既然财政数字会受周期与估计修订影响，制度就需要明确：究竟约束哪个指标、覆盖哪些机构、怎样处理冲击。先把预算全貌和信息质量弄清，再把多年成本与可安排额度相比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because fiscal figures respond to the cycle and estimate revisions, institutions must specify the constrained measure, coverage and treatment of shocks. Establish comprehensive, usable information before comparing multi-year costs with available allocations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 十一、用一份预算报告检验这条路径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Applying the path to a budget report*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面对一份报告，可以按下面的顺序写一段完整解释：先报机构范围、期间和记账基础；区分收入、非金融资产交易与融资；由总体和初级余额解释债务变化；列明存量流量调节及增长；再评融资期限、冲击与财政调整的可行性；最后核对预算授权、规则指标和执行证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Build a coherent explanation in this order: state coverage, period and basis; distinguish revenue, nonfinancial-asset transactions and financing; connect overall and primary balances to debt; identify adjustments and growth; assess maturities, shocks and feasible fiscal responses; then check budget authority, rule measures and execution evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以先用本篇账表自测：不重新查数，解释为什么初级盈余14仍能伴随债务率上升；再说明要判断这条债务路径是否可持续，还缺哪些行为、融资和制度证据。计算回答“发生了什么”，条件和证据回答“为什么能持续”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Test yourself on the ledger: explain why a primary surplus of 14 can coexist with a rising debt ratio, then identify the behavioural, funding and institutional evidence still needed to assess sustainability. Arithmetic describes the path; conditions and evidence establish its feasibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
