## 连续阅读
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Continuous Reading Path*

### 1. 发展差距、制度与结构转型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Development Gaps, Institutions, and Structural Transformation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是本课程的默认起点；先用快速恢复建立对象、例子和问题感，再进入详细内容。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the default starting point. Use the quick-recovery section to establish the objects, examples, and motivating questions before moving into the details.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[发展、贫困与不平等的测量]]

先把收入、贫困、健康和不平等的差距量清楚，才能区分“发展结果”与解释这些结果的制度或地理机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Income, poverty, health, and inequality gaps must be measured before development outcomes can be separated from the institutional or geographic mechanisms proposed to explain them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[制度、地理与长期发展差距]]

制度与地理解释机会和约束为何长期不同；结构转型进一步追踪这些条件怎样改变部门间的劳动力配置与实际生产率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Institutions and geography explain why opportunities and constraints differ persistently. Structural transformation then traces how those conditions alter labour allocation across sectors and realised productivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[结构转型、生产率重配与非正规部门]]

### 2. 人口增长、马尔萨斯与人口转型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Population Growth, Malthus, and the Demographic Transition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收入、健康、教育与结构指标描述发展差距；人口模型解释家庭选择和生存条件如何改变长期人口路径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Income, health, education, and structural indicators describe development gaps. Population models explain how household choices and survival conditions alter long-run population paths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[马尔萨斯陷阱与现代增长转型]]

马尔萨斯机制先说明前现代收入增长为何会被人口反馈吸收；人口转型再解释死亡率、生育选择和人力资本如何改变这条反馈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Malthusian mechanism first shows why population feedback absorbed income gains in premodern economies. Demographic transition then explains how mortality, fertility choice, and human capital changed that feedback.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[人口转型、生育选择与老龄化]]

### 3. 二元经济与劳动力迁移
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Dual Economies and Labour Migration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

人口变化改变劳动供给与城乡结构；二元经济和迁移模型研究劳动力为何、何时从农业转向城市部门。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Population change alters labour supply and the rural–urban structure. Dual-economy and migration models study why and when workers move from agriculture into the urban sector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Lewis—Fei–Ranis 二元经济模型]]

Lewis—Fei–Ranis 从部门层面解释剩余劳动力怎样被现代部门吸收；Todaro 转到个人迁移决策，说明预期城市收入为何能与城市失业并存。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lewis–Fei–Ranis explains at the sectoral level how the modern sector absorbs surplus labour. Todaro shifts to individual migration decisions and shows how expected urban income can coexist with urban unemployment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Todaro 迁移、城市失业与非正规部门]]

### 4. 经济增长理论：哈罗德—多马、索洛与内生增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Growth Theory: Harrod–Domar, Solow, and Endogenous Growth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

劳动力转移只是增长的一部分；增长模型把资本、人口、技术和生产率放到长期积累框架中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Labour reallocation is only one component of growth. Growth models place capital, population, technology, and productivity in a framework of long-run accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Harrod—Domar 增长模型]]

Harrod—Domar 突出储蓄、资本产出比和刀刃式协调问题；Solow 加入要素替代与资本边际报酬递减，使稳态和收敛可以系统分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Harrod–Domar highlights saving, the capital–output ratio, and knife-edge coordination. Solow adds factor substitution and diminishing returns to capital, making steady states and convergence tractable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛]]

Solow 把长期人均增长的技术进步留在模型外；内生增长理论进一步追问知识、人力资本、研发和政策怎样在模型内持续产生增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solow leaves the technological progress behind long-run per-capita growth outside the model. Endogenous growth theory then asks how knowledge, human capital, research, and policy can generate sustained growth within the model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[内生增长理论]]

### 5. 发展政策、贸易与全球化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Development Policy, Trade, and Globalisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增长机制不能自动给出政策；贸易、产业、教育和国家能力决定技术与结构转型在具体制度中能否实现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Growth mechanisms do not automatically determine policy. Trade, industry, education, and state capacity determine whether technology adoption and structural transformation can be realised within a particular institutional setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[发展政策的国家能力、公共投入与评价]]

先判断国家是否有能力选择、执行和评估公共投入，再讨论开放、产业政策和全球价值链能否真正转化为学习与生产率提升。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The state’s capacity to select, implement, and evaluate public investment comes first. Only then can openness, industrial policy, and global value chains be assessed as routes to learning and productivity growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[贸易、产业政策与全球价值链]]
