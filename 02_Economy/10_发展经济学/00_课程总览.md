This is the default starting point. Use the quick-recovery section to establish the objects, examples, and motivating questions before moving into the details.

![[发展差距、制度与结构转型]]

### 2. 人口增长、马尔萨斯与人口转型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Population Growth, Malthus, and the Demographic Transition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收入、健康、教育与结构指标描述发展差距；人口模型解释家庭选择和生存条件如何改变长期人口路径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Income, health, education, and structural indicators describe development gaps. Population models explain how household choices and survival conditions alter long-run population paths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[人口增长、马尔萨斯与人口转型]]

### 3. 二元经济与劳动力迁移
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Dual Economies and Labour Migration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

人口变化改变劳动供给与城乡结构；二元经济和迁移模型研究劳动力为何、何时从农业转向城市部门。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Population change alters labour supply and the rural–urban structure. Dual-economy and migration models study why and when workers move from agriculture into the urban sector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[二元经济与劳动力迁移]]

### 4. 经济增长理论：哈罗德—多马、索洛与内生增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Growth Theory: Harrod–Domar, Solow, and Endogenous Growth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

劳动力转移只是增长的一部分；增长模型把资本、人口、技术和生产率放到长期积累框架中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Labour reallocation is only one component of growth. Growth models place capital, population, technology, and productivity in a framework of long-run accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[经济增长理论：哈罗德—多马、索洛与内生增长]]

### 5. 发展政策、贸易与全球化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Development Policy, Trade, and Globalisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增长机制不能自动给出政策；贸易、产业、教育和国家能力决定技术与结构转型在具体制度中能否实现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Growth mechanisms do not automatically determine policy. Trade, industry, education, and state capacity determine whether technology adoption and structural transformation can be realised within a particular institutional setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[发展政策、贸易与全球化]]
