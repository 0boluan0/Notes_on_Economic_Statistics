*1. Development Gaps, Institutions, and Structural Transformation*

这是本课程的默认起点；先从下面的连续课程建立对象、例子和问题感，再进入制度与结构转型。

This is the default starting point. Follow the continuous chapter below to establish the objects, examples, and motivating questions before moving to institutions and structural transformation.

#### 制度与地理如何解释差距
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How institutions and geography explain the gaps*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把收入、贫困、健康和不平等的差距量清楚，才能区分“发展结果”与解释这些结果的制度或地理机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Income, poverty, health, and inequality gaps must be measured before development outcomes can be separated from the institutional or geographic mechanisms proposed to explain them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主题图谱：[[制度、地理与长期发展差距.canvas|制度、地理与长期发展差距]]。下面按“解释层次 → 制度/地理通道 → 历史持续 → 经验识别”展开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Topic map: [[制度、地理与长期发展差距.canvas|Institutions, Geography, and Long-Run Development Gaps]]. The sequence moves from explanatory levels through institutional and geographic channels to historical persistence and empirical identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[发展差距的近因与根因]]

![[制度塑造发展激励]]

![[地理的发展双通道]]

![[制度路径依赖]]

![[制度收入的识别边界]]

![[定居者死亡率工具变量]]

制度与地理提供可并存的机制，历史可能通过可验证的再生产环使差异持续；结构转型进一步追踪这些条件怎样改变部门间的劳动力配置与实际生产率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Institutions and geography provide potentially coexisting mechanisms, while history may sustain differences through testable reproduction loops. Structural transformation then traces how those conditions alter labour allocation across sectors and realised productivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/08_结构转型与劳动生产率]]

### 2. 人口增长、马尔萨斯与人口转型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Population Growth, Malthus, and the Demographic Transition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收入、健康、教育与结构指标描述发展差距；人口模型解释家庭选择和生存条件如何改变长期人口路径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Income, health, education, and structural indicators describe development gaps. Population models explain how household choices and survival conditions alter long-run population paths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

马尔萨斯机制先说明前现代收入增长为何会被人口反馈吸收；人口转型再解释死亡率、生育选择和人力资本如何改变这条反馈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Malthusian mechanism first shows why population feedback absorbed income gains in premodern economies. Demographic transition then explains how mortality, fertility choice, and human capital changed that feedback.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/09_人口、家庭选择与增长转型]]

### 3. 二元经济与劳动力迁移
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Dual Economies and Labour Migration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

人口变化改变劳动供给；二元经济模型解释部门间的劳动配置，迁移模型进一步研究改变工作地点与就业机会的选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Population change alters labour supply. Dual-economy models explain labour allocation between sectors, while migration models also examine choices about location and employment opportunities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先从 Lewis 的劳动吸收进入 Fei–Ranis 的农业食品约束，再用 Harris–Todaro 联立迁移、就业概率与城市失业。以下连续课程把三种模型的定义、图形、推导和条件放在同一条阅读路径中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with Lewis's labour absorption, add Fei–Ranis's agricultural food constraint, then jointly determine migration, job probabilities and urban unemployment in Harris–Todaro. The continuous chapter below connects definitions, diagrams, derivations and conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/10_二元经济与劳动力迁移]]

### 4. 经济增长理论：哈罗德—多马、索洛与内生增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Growth Theory: Harrod–Domar, Solow, and Endogenous Growth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

劳动力转移只是增长的一部分；增长模型把资本、人口、技术和生产率放到长期积累框架中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Labour reallocation is only one component of growth. Growth models place capital, population, technology, and productivity in a framework of long-run accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#### 资本积累与分配

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Accumulation and distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先读 Marx 的积累问题：剩余从哪里来，怎样转为追加资本，资本的规模与构成怎样影响用工和利润率。下面的课程节把课堂符号接到这些定义及其适用条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with Marx's accumulation problem: where surplus comes from, how it becomes additional capital, and how capital's scale and composition affect employment and the profit rate. The following section connects the classroom notation to those definitions and conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/06_经济增长理论#1. 马克思的资本积累模型]]

#### 计划一致、要素替代与创新

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Plan consistency, factor substitution and innovation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

积累的来源和分配关系说明了部分问题。Harrod 与 Domar 接着追问投资形成的需求与产能能否彼此相容；后面的模型再逐步改变要素替代和技术进步的处理方式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The source of accumulation and its distributive relations address part of the problem. Harrod and Domar next ask whether the demand and capacity created by investment are compatible. Subsequent models change the treatment of factor substitution and technical progress.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Harrod—Domar 增长模型.canvas]]

先把这个后来的合称拆开：Harrod 与 Domar 得到相似的增长条件，却从不同问题和投资机制出发。

![[Harrod 与 Domar 的机制差异]]

Domar 先看投资的两个时间维度：它既是今天的支出，也形成明天必须被利用的产能。

![[投资双重效应]]

在计划储蓄等于企业所需投资、净投资形成资本且增量资本系数固定的教科书简化下，一致条件可写成 $g_w=s/v$。这是一条有前提的计划一致关系，不是增长预测器。

![[Harrod-Domar 一致增长率]]

Harrod 再把实际发生、企业计划被验证和充分就业供给上限分成三种增长率；只有先分清三者，才看得懂所谓刀锋问题。

![[Harrod 三种增长率]]

偏离保证路径后，投资反馈可能放大产能过紧或闲置；但原始等式没有单独给出完整离轨动态，稳定性判断还需要行为方程。

![[Harrod 离轨不稳定性]]

Harrod 与 Domar 突出计划相容、产能利用以及保证增长与自然增长不一致的问题；Solow 加入要素替代与资本边际报酬递减，使稳态和收敛可以系统分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Harrod and Domar highlight plan consistency, capacity utilisation, and a possible mismatch between warranted and natural growth. Solow adds factor substitution and diminishing returns to capital, making steady states and convergence tractable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛.canvas|打开 Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛总图]]

![[Solow 人均化边界]]

![[Solow 资本积累方程]]

![[Solow 稳态条件]]

![[Solow 转型动态]]

![[Solow 黄金律]]

![[Solow 收敛边界]]

![[储蓄率的水平效应]]

![[Solow 的外生技术边界]]

![[增长核算的 TFP 残差]]

![[跨国增长回归的因果边界]]

Solow 把长期人均增长的技术进步留在模型外；内生增长理论进一步追问知识、人力资本、研发和政策怎样在模型内持续产生增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solow leaves the technological progress behind long-run per-capita growth outside the model. Endogenous growth theory then asks how knowledge, human capital, research, and policy can generate sustained growth within the model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/13_内生增长与创新]]

### 5. 发展政策、贸易与全球化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Development Policy, Trade, and Globalisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增长机制不能自动给出政策；贸易、产业、教育和国家能力决定技术与结构转型在具体制度中能否实现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Growth mechanisms do not automatically determine policy. Trade, industry, education, and state capacity determine whether technology adoption and structural transformation can be realised within a particular institutional setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/11_国家能力、公共投入与政策评价]]

先判断国家是否有能力选择、执行和评估公共投入，再讨论开放、产业政策和全球价值链能否真正转化为学习与生产率提升。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The state’s capacity to select, implement, and evaluate public investment comes first. Only then can openness, industrial policy, and global value chains be assessed as routes to learning and productivity growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/12_贸易、产业政策与全球价值链]]
