 
# 1. 马克思的资本积累模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Marx's Model of Capital Accumulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一节先解释资本积累的来源，再看它怎样改变劳动需求，最后推导课堂的利润率表达式。这里使用 Marx 的价值分析：先把劳动力的价值与使用它所形成的新价值分开，再讨论剩余的使用。完整的商品与劳动基础见 [[02_Economy/04_经济思想史/03_价值、剩余与积累|价值、剩余与积累连续课程]]；全局关系见 [[价值、剩余与积累.canvas|主题图]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This section first explains where accumulation comes from, then how it changes labour demand, and finally derives the lecture's profit-rate expression. Within Marx's value analysis, distinguish the value of labour-power from the new value formed by its use before examining how surplus is used. The full foundations are in [[02_Economy/04_经济思想史/03_价值、剩余与积累|the continuous chapter on value, surplus and accumulation]]; the [[价值、剩余与积累.canvas|topic map]] shows the overall relations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1 从劳动力到剩余价值

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1 From labour-power to surplus-value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

劳动能力与实际发生的劳动是两个对象。用工契约购买一定期间的能力使用，生产过程才把这种能力实际发挥出来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The capacity to work and work actually performed are different objects. An employment contract purchases the use of the capacity for a period; the production process puts that capacity into action.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Marx劳动力]]

![[劳动力价值]]

采用一个共同教学例：劳动强度和价值计量保持一致，每小时平均社会劳动表现为100个价值单位；一天劳动力价值为400，按价值支付。这样，工人需要四小时创造劳动力价值的等价物，但实际工作日可以长于四小时。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a common teaching example with unchanged intensity and valuation: each hour of average social labour is expressed as 100 value units; a day's labour-power has a value of 400 and is paid at that value. Four hours reproduce its equivalent, while the actual day can last longer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[必要劳动]]

![[剩余劳动]]

![[剩余价值]]

八小时工作日中，前述价值分割给出四小时必要劳动、四小时剩余劳动。要比较剩余的相对量，分母取必要部分所对应的可变资本，而不是整个工作日，也不是全部生产投入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an eight-hour day, that value division gives four necessary and four surplus hours. The relative measure uses the variable capital corresponding to the necessary portion as its denominator, rather than the entire working day or all production inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[剩余价值率]]

工作日延长或生活资料生产率变化，会经不同通道改变这个划分，分别见 [[绝对剩余价值生产]] 与 [[相对剩余价值生产]]。因此，后面把剩余价值率设为不变时，是另加了比较条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A longer day and productivity changes in subsistence production alter this division through different channels, explained in [[绝对剩余价值生产|absolute]] and [[相对剩余价值生产|relative surplus-value production]]. Holding the rate of surplus-value fixed later is therefore an additional comparison condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2 生产资料怎样进入产品价值

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2 How means of production enter product value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

生产中不仅有活劳动，还有原料、机器等生产资料。下面两类资本按它们在价值增殖中的作用区分；课堂公式中的字母，需要先对应到这些对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Production uses materials, machinery and other means of production alongside living labour. The following categories distinguish their roles in valorisation. The letters in the lecture formula must first be matched to these objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[不变资本]]

![[可变资本]]

![[产品价值分解]]

这里将当期耗用的生产资料价值记为 $c_u$。课堂写 $W=c+q+s$ 时，若把它理解为一期产品价值分解，就须把其中 $c$ 读作这期转移的价值，把 $q$ 对应这期劳动力价值的等价物。下一步计算利润率，还要单独识别预付资本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the value of means of production consumed during the period as $c_u$. If the classroom equation $W=c+q+s$ is read as a period's product-value decomposition, its $c$ must mean value transferred in that period and its $q$ the equivalent of labour-power's value for that period. Calculating a profit rate also requires a separate identification of the capital advanced.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3 积累与就业：先分总额和构成

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3 Accumulation and employment: amount and composition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本积累使用的是剩余中作为追加资本投入的部分。仅补回原来耗用的资料与劳动力，维持的是原有规模；实际扩大还需有相应的物质和劳动条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Accumulation uses the portion of surplus invested as additional capital. Replacing the means of production and labour-power already consumed maintains the existing scale; expansion requires corresponding material and labour conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Marx资本积累]]

![[积累的物质条件]]

资本扩张可能同时改变生产资料与劳动的关系。先看实物量，再看价值量，最后明确“有机”限定了二者之间怎样的联系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Capital expansion may also change the relation between means of production and labour. Consider physical quantities first, then values, and finally the particular connection denoted by “organic”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[资本技术构成]]

![[资本价值构成]]

![[资本有机构成]]

课堂的 $j=c/q$ 是一个价值比，能否把它解释为有机构成的变化，要看变化来自哪里。工资、设备单位价值或生产组织改变，都需要分别说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The classroom's $j=c/q$ is a value ratio. Interpreting its change as a change in organic composition requires identifying its source. Changes in wages, unit values of equipment and the organisation of production must be distinguished.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[积累与劳动需求]]

![[产业后备军]]

原投影片把劳动供给作无限弹性的概括。若在一幅劳动市场图上用水平线来表达它，就必须另行给定工资、可吸收劳动的范围和持续条件，具体可对照 [[无限劳动供给]]。产业后备军的定义本身不提供一条对任何规模扩张都成立的水平供给曲线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The slide characterises labour supply as infinitely elastic. Representing this by a horizontal labour-market curve requires additional assumptions about the wage, the pool available for recruitment and the duration of those conditions; compare [[无限劳动供给|unlimited labour supply]]. The definition of the industrial reserve army alone does not supply a horizontal curve valid at every scale of expansion.

## 1.4 从资本周转读利润率

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.4 Reading profit rates through capital turnover*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一台机器可以完整参与今天的生产，却只把部分价值转进今天的产品。它与一次耗尽的原料在周转上不同，即使二者都属于不变资本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A machine can participate fully in today's production while transferring only part of its value to today's product. Its turnover differs from materials used up at once, although both belong to constant capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[固定与流动资本]]

![[两种资本分类]]

![[预付与耗用资本]]

用这些区分来读课堂的 $P=s/(c+q)$：分母现在要取为这项生产预付的总资本，而不是只把本期耗用的成本相加。只有在明确的耗用和周转简化下，两处字母才会恰好代表相同金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply these distinctions to the lecture's $P=s/(c+q)$. The denominator now represents total capital advanced for the production, not merely the costs consumed during the current period. Only explicit consumption and turnover simplifications can make the amounts represented in the two equations coincide.

## 2.1 假设条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1 Assumptions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 不存在货币部门,且价格水平不变
2. 劳动力按照由外部因素决定的不变速度增长.$\frac{\frac{dN}{dt}}{N}=n=常数$
3. 社会的储蓄率,即储蓄和收入的比率不变.$\frac{S}{Y}=s=常数$ . S为储蓄,Y为收入,s为储蓄率
4. 社会生产要素只使用劳动N和资本K两种生产要素,且两种要素不能互相替代.
5. 不存在技术进步
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** There is no monetary sector, and the price level is constant.<br>
**2.** The labour force grows at an exogenously determined constant rate:<br>
   $$\frac{\frac{dN}{dt}}{N}=n=\text{constant}.$$
**3.** The saving rate—saving as a share of income—is constant:<br>
   $$\frac{S}{Y}=s=\text{constant},$$
   where $S$ is saving, $Y$ income, and $s$ the saving rate.
**4.** Production uses only labour $N$ and capital $K$, and the two inputs cannot substitute for each other.<br>
**5.** There is no technological progress.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==由假设条件可以推知,生产函数写作:Y=Y(N,K)=min(VK,ZN)==
其中 $V$ 是资本充分利用时的产出—资本系数，$Z$ 是劳动充分就业时的产出—劳动系数，二者在本模型中固定。只有对应约束绑定时，才分别有 $V=Y/K$ 或 $Z=Y/N$；另一要素先绑定时，实际比率可以更低。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The assumptions imply a fixed-proportions production function:
$$Y=Y(N,K)=\min(VK,ZN),$$
where $V$ is the output–capital coefficient at full capital utilisation and $Z$ the output–labour coefficient at full employment. Both are fixed. The realised ratios equal $Y/K=V$ or $Y/N=Z$ only when the corresponding constraint binds; if the other input binds first, the realised ratio may be lower.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2 模型内容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2 Model Content*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于生产函数是两者取最小值,所以两边都要算,算完之后哪边小选哪边.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because output is the minimum of the two capacity constraints, calculate both $VK$ and $ZN$ and use the smaller value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (1).产出与资本的关系：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) The Relationship between Output and Capital*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在资本约束绑定、资本得到充分利用的支路：
$$V=\frac{Y}{K} \Rightarrow Y=VK$$
• 对$Y=VK$公式两边对时间$t$微分得到：
$$\frac{dY}{dt}=V\frac{dK}{dt}$$
闭合经济中实现值的 $S\equiv I$ 是事后核算恒等式；推导[[Harrod-Domar 一致增长率|保证增长率]]还需要一条计划一致条件：计划储蓄供给恰好等于企业所需投资。本课程的简化写作：
$$I = S = sY$$
• 把 $I$ 定义为净投资（或等价地忽略折旧），资本存量的变动率为：
$$I=\frac{dK}{dt} \Rightarrow \frac{dK}{dt}=sY$$
• 将以上式子代入到之前的产出变动式子中，得：
$$\frac{dY}{dt}=V\cdot sY$$
• 整理上述方程，得经济增长率：
$$\frac{\frac{dY}{dt}}{Y}=Vs$$
由此可见，经济增长率$Vs$取决于：
• $V$（产出-资本比），即每单位资本所产生的产出；
• $s$（储蓄率），即储蓄占收入的比例。
模型由此得到经济的“保证增长率”：它使储蓄供给、企业所需投资与资本充分利用彼此一致，不能直接当成实际增长预测。
$$G_W = Vs$$
这一增长率意味着，在 $V$ 固定且资本持续充分利用的支路上，资本存量 $K$ 和产出 $Y$ 必须以同样的增长率同步增长，即：
$$\frac{\frac{dK}{dt}}{K}=Vs$$资本存量的变动等于产出-资本比和储蓄率的乘积.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the branch where capital is binding and fully utilised,

$$V=\frac{Y}{K} \Rightarrow Y=VK$$

• Differentiating $Y=VK$ with respect to time gives

$$\frac{dY}{dt}=V\frac{dK}{dt}$$

In a closed economy, equality of realised saving and investment is an ex-post accounting identity. Deriving the [[Harrod-Domar 一致增长率|warranted growth rate]] additionally requires planned saving supply to equal firms' required investment. In the course's simplified notation:

$$I = S = sY$$

• If $I$ denotes net investment (or depreciation is set to zero), investment is the change in the capital stock:

$$I=\frac{dK}{dt} \Rightarrow \frac{dK}{dt}=sY$$

• Substitution gives
$$\frac{dY}{dt}=VsY.$$
• Dividing by $Y$ gives the growth rate
$$\frac{\frac{dY}{dt}}{Y}=Vs.$$
Thus the conditional consistency rate depends on $V$, output per unit of capital, and $s$, saving as a share of income. The model calls
$$G_W=Vs$$
the **warranted growth rate**: the rate at which saving supply, required investment, and full capital utilisation are mutually consistent. It is not an unconditional forecast. Since $V$ is fixed, capital and output grow at the same proportional rate:
$$\frac{\frac{dK}{dt}}{K}=Vs.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (2).产出与劳动的关系：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) The Relationship between Output and Labour*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑劳动人口增长，根据生产函数的劳动约束条件：
$$Y = ZN$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the labour constraint of the production function,
$$Y=ZN.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的含义是，在劳动充分就业条件下，产出必须等于劳动人口$N$乘以劳动生产率$Z$（固定常数）。
• 对该公式两边按[[导数线性法则|时间 $t$ 求导]]，得到：
$$\frac{dY}{dt}=Z\frac{dN}{dt}$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under full employment, output equals the labour force $N$ times the constant labour-productivity parameter $Z$.
• Differentiating both sides with respect to time gives
$$\frac{dY}{dt}=Z\frac{dN}{dt}$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由此推出：
$$\frac{\frac{dY}{dt}}{Y}=\frac{\frac{dN}{dt}}{N}=n$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This yields:
$$\frac{\frac{dY}{dt}}{Y}=\frac{\frac{dN}{dt}}{N}=n$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在本课程“劳动生产率固定且无技术进步”的特例中，$n$ 就是[[Harrod 三种增长率|自然增长率]]。因此，要保持劳动充分就业，产出必须与劳动人口同速增长；这不是 Harrod 对自然增长率的一般定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In this course's special case of constant labour productivity and no technological progress, $n$ is the [[Harrod 三种增长率|natural growth rate]]. Maintaining full employment then requires output to grow at the same rate as the labour force. This is not Harrod's general definition of natural growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即经济的自然增长率为：
$$G_N = n$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The natural growth rate of the economy is given by:
$$G_N = n$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

哈罗德-多马模型因此存在两个重要的增长率：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore, there exist two important growth rates:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **保证增长率$G_W = Vs$**（资本角度出发的充分利用增长率）
• **自然增长率$G_N = n$**（劳动角度出发的充分就业增长率）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Warranted growth rate $G_W=Vs$**: the rate consistent with full utilization of capital.
• **Natural growth rate $G_N=n$**: the rate consistent with full employment of labour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**重要的推论与现实局限：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Important Inferences and Real-World Limitations:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

哈罗德-多马模型中，经济同时实现资本充分利用与劳动充分就业时，必须满足：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the Harrod–Domar economy to achieve both full capital utilization and full employment, the two rates must coincide:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

Vs = n

$$

但实际上，$Vs$和$n$通常并不相同，这就导致：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
However, $Vs$ and $n$ are typically not the same, which leads to:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 若 $Vs>n$，资本供给能力比劳动供给能力增长得更快；在固定比例生产下，劳动约束先绑定，部分资本会闲置，而不能据此推出劳动失业。
• 若 $Vs<n$，资本供给能力增长得更慢；资本约束先绑定，部分增长中的劳动供给无法被吸收，可能出现劳动失业或就业不足。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• If $Vs>n$, capital capacity grows faster than labour capacity. With fixed proportions, labour is the binding input and some capital is idle; labour unemployment does not follow from this inequality.
• If $Vs<n$, capital capacity grows more slowly. Capital is binding, so part of the growing labour supply may remain unemployed or underemployed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

韩国材料只能用来提示模型遗漏变量，不能单凭两条增长率识别资本流入：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The South Korea comparison can flag omitted mechanisms, but two growth rates alone cannot identify capital inflows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 韩国人口增速较低而 GDP 增长较快，只能说明“固定劳动生产率、无技术进步”的极简模型遗漏了重要机制；仅凭这两个增长率，不能识别出“大量资本流入”。劳动生产率、技术、资本积累、劳动参与率和净资本流动都可能参与解释，必须另有数据才能区分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
South Korea's relatively low population growth and faster GDP growth show that the stripped-down assumptions of constant labour productivity and no technical progress omit important mechanisms. Those two rates alone do not identify a large capital inflow: productivity, technology, capital accumulation, labour-force participation, and net capital flows are competing explanations that require additional evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

哈罗德-多马模型通过假设固定的储蓄率、资本产出比和劳动增长率，建立了经济增长的基本框架，提出了“保证增长率”和“自然增长率”两个重要概念，并指出实际经济中两者可能的不一致问题。然而，这一模型忽略了资本与劳动之间的可替代性、技术进步以及国际资本流动等因素，因此在现实中适用性存在一定限制，但仍为分析经济增长的基础模型提供了重要理论基础。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Harrod–Domar model builds a basic growth framework from a fixed saving rate, output–capital ratio, and labour-force growth rate. It distinguishes the warranted and natural growth rates and shows why they may fail to coincide. Because the model excludes substitution between capital and labour, technological progress, and international capital flows, its real-world scope is limited; nevertheless, it remains an important foundation for thinking about economic growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1 假设条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1 Assumptions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

索罗（[[Solow 人均化边界|Solow]]）模型建立在新古典生产函数的基础上，主要关注经济增长过程中的资本积累、劳动力增长及技术进步三个因素，研究它们对经济长期增长的影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Solow 人均化边界|Solow model]] starts from a neoclassical production function and studies how capital accumulation, labour-force growth, and technological progress shape long-run economic growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**模型的基本假设：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Basic assumptions:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **经济由单一部门构成**，生产出的商品既可用于投资，也可用于消费。
2. **经济是封闭的**，无国际贸易，也无政府干预。
3. **生产具有规模报酬不变**（Constant Returns to Scale, CRS）。
4. **人口增长率$n$、技术进步率（此处先假设为零）和折旧率$\delta$均为外生变量**。
5. **社会储蓄函数**为$$S = sY$$其中，$s$为储蓄率，是外生给定的。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **The economy consists of a single sector**, producing goods that can be used for investment as well as consumption.<br>
**2.** **The economy is closed**, with no international trade and no government intervention.<br>
**3.** **Production exhibits constant returns to scale** (Constant Returns to Scale, CRS).<br>
**4.** **The growth rate of the population ($n$), the rate of technological progress (assumed to be zero here), and the depreciation rate ($\delta$) are exogenous variables**.<br>
**5.** **The social saving function** is given by $$S = sY$$ where $s$ is the savings rate, an exogenously given constant.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2 模型的基本方程与推导过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2 Core Equations and Derivation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**步骤1：新古典生产函数的设定**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Step 1: Specify the neoclassical production function**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设总产出为$Y$，总劳动量为$N$，资本存量为$K$，则经济生产函数为：
$$Y = F(N, K)$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assuming total output $Y$, total labor $N$, and capital stock $K$, the economic production function is:
$$Y = F(N, K)$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该函数满足规模报酬不变（CRS），意味着对任意正数$\lambda$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Constant returns to scale (CRS) means that, for every positive $\lambda$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\lambda Y = F(\lambda N, \lambda K)$$
设$\lambda = \frac{1}{N}$，则：
$$\frac{Y}{N} = F\left(1, \frac{K}{N}\right)$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
\[
\lambda Y = F(\lambda N, \lambda K)
\]
Let $\lambda = \frac{1}{N}$, then:
\[
\frac{Y}{N} = F\left(1, \frac{K}{N}\right)
\]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义人均变量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define variables per capita:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 人均产量$y = \frac{Y}{N}$，
• 人均资本存量$k = \frac{K}{N}$，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Output per person: $y=\frac{Y}{N}$
• Capital per person: $k=\frac{K}{N}$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

则可以进一步简化为人均形式：
$$y = f(k)$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then it can be further simplified to the per capita form:
$$y = f(k)$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**步骤2：资本积累方程的推导**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Step 2: Derivation of the Capital Accumulation Equation**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本存量的变动$\dot{K}$由投资$I$减去折旧$\delta K$决定：
$$\dot{K} = I - \delta K$$
投资来源于储蓄，储蓄率为$s$，因此：
$$I = S = sY$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The change in capital stock, $\dot{K}$, is determined by investment, $I$, minus depreciation, $\delta K$:
$$\dot{K} = I - \delta K$$
Investment comes from savings, and the saving rate is $s$, so:
$$I = S = sY$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

故资本积累的方程为：
$$\dot{K} = sY - \delta K$$
将两侧同时除以$N$，变为人均资本的变化方程：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation for capital accumulation is:
$$\dot{K} = sY - \delta K$$
Dividing both sides by $N$ gives the corresponding expression per person:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac{\dot{K}}{N} = s\frac{Y}{N} - \delta\frac{K}{N}$$

即：
$$\frac{\dot{K}}{N} = sy - \delta k$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
That is,
$$\frac{\dot{K}}{N} = sy - \delta k$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**步骤3：考虑人口增长**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Step 3: Account for population growth**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为$k=\frac{K}{N}$，其对时间的导数为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $k=\frac{K}{N}$, differentiating with respect to time gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\dot{k} = \frac{\dot{K}}{N} - nk$$

此处的$n$为人口增长率（外生给定）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $n$ is the exogenously given population growth rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

代入前式，得到完整的人均资本积累方程：
$$\dot{k} = sf(k) - (n+\delta)k$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substitution gives the full law of motion for capital per person:
$$\dot{k} = sf(k) - (n+\delta)k$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是索罗模型的核心方程，它描述了人均资本$k$随时间的变动情况。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the core equation of Solow's model, describing how capital per capita $k$ changes over time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.3 模型的稳态分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3 Steady-State Analysis of the Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所谓稳定状态（steady-state），就是人均资本$k$达到不再变化的状态，即：
$$\dot{k}=0$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A steady state is a value of capital per person $k$ that no longer changes:
$$\dot{k}=0$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据方程：
$$sf(k) - (n+\delta)k=0$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
According to the equation:
$$sf(k) - (n+\delta)k=0$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解出稳态值$k^*$：
$$sf(k^*) = (n+\delta)k^*$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The steady-state value $k^*$ therefore satisfies
$$sf(k^*)=(n+\delta)k^*.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该状态下，人均资本不再增加或减少，对应的产出水平、储蓄和投资也保持稳定不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under this state, the per capita capital neither increases nor decreases, and the corresponding output levels, savings, and investments remain stable and unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**VI. Economic Interpretation**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **长期稳定增长**：新古典索罗模型认为，没有技术进步的经济最终都会达到稳态，人均产出和资本不再增长。

• **储蓄率提升**：虽然可以提高稳态的产出水平，但无法带来长期持续的增长，因为资本的边际收益递减。

• **Increase in Saving Rate**: While it can raise steady-state output levels, it does not lead to sustained long-term growth, as the marginal returns on capital diminish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **技术进步的重要性**：模型后续引入技术进步后，才能实现人均产出长期增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Importance of Technological Progress**: Only after introducing technological progress can a model achieve sustained long-term growth in per capita output.

• 新的稳态由$A$点变为$A’$点，相较于$A$点：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• The steady state moves from $A$ to $A'$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 人均资本由$k_A$下降到$k’$；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Capital per person falls from $k_A$ to $k'$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 人均产出水平随之降低（因为$f(k)$是随资本水平增加而增加的函数）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Output per person falls with it because $f(k)$ is increasing in $k$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

说明**人口增长率的增加降低了稳态水平的人均资本和人均产出**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus, **faster population growth lowers steady-state capital and output per person**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**（2）人口增长的经济含义：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**(2) Economic Implications of Population Growth:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 人口增长率提高，意味着经济必须投入更多资本维持新增劳动力的资本配备；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Faster population growth requires more investment simply to equip additional workers with capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 这相当于增加了资本稀释的效应，使得人均资本积累变慢；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• This strengthens capital dilution and slows the accumulation of capital per person.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 稳态水平的资本积累被拉低，最终人均收入水平和生活水平下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• The steady-state capital stock is lower, reducing income per person and living standards.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

---

**三、关键结论总结：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**III. Summary of Key Conclusions:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

通过上述的比较静态分析，可以明确两个重要的经济含义：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The comparative statics yield two central conclusions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **储蓄率$s$提高：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Increase in Savings Rate $s$:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 稳态人均资本$k^_$、稳态人均产出$y^_$均提高；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Steady-state capital $k^*$ and output $y^*$ per person both rise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 但长期的增长率不会提高（仅为短期效应）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• The long-run growth rate does not rise; the growth effect is temporary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **人口增长率$n$提高：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Increase in Population Growth Rate $n$:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 稳态人均资本$k^_$、稳态人均产出$y^_$均降低；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Steady-state capital $k^*$ and output $y^*$ per person both fall.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 人均资本被更快稀释，经济长期处于较低的收入水平。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Per capita capital is being diluted at an accelerated rate, leading to sustained lower income levels over the long term.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

新古典索罗增长模型由此得出一个非常重要的启示：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The neoclassical Solow model therefore yields an important insight:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 仅依靠资本积累（增加储蓄率）无法实现经济的持续长期增长；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Capital accumulation (increasing savings rates) alone cannot achieve sustained long-term economic growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 要实现经济持续长期增长，必须依靠**技术进步**这一核心因素。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Sustained growth ultimately requires **technological progress**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

---

**举例理解：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Numerical illustration:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

假设经济中初始稳态资本水平为$k=100$，储蓄率$s=0.2$，人口增长率$n=0.01$，折旧率$\delta=0.04$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume that the initial steady state capital level in the economy is $k=100$, the savings rate $s=0.2$, the population growth rate $n=0.01$, and the depreciation rate $\delta=0.04$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 若储蓄率提高为$s’=0.3$，则新稳态资本水平提高，例如可能变为$k=150$，产出水平也会随之提高，但长期经济增长仍是0；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• If the saving rate rises to $s'=0.3$, the new steady-state capital stock may rise to, say, $k=150$, and output also rises, while long-run growth remains zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 若人口增长率提高为$n’=0.02$，则新稳态资本水平可能降低，例如变为$k=80$，导致产出下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• If population growth rises to $n'=0.02$, the new steady-state capital stock may fall to, say, $k=80$, reducing output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.3 格申克龙的后发优势理论及其发展
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3 Gerschenkron's Theory of Latecomer Advantage and Its Development*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==核心:经济上的相对落后有助于一个国家或地区实现爆发性的经济增长==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Core:** Relative economic backwardness can help a country or region achieve explosive growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原因:
1. 后发国对现代化的认知更丰富
2. 后发国可以大量采用和借鉴先发国成熟的计划,技术,设备,组织结构等
3. 后发国可以跳跃先发国对一些必经发展阶段,特别是技术方面
4. 先发国的发展过程可使后发国对于自己现代化有一定的预测
5. 先发国可以在资本和技术上对后发国提供帮助
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reasons:

**1.** Late-developing countries can draw on a richer accumulated understanding of modernization.<br>
**2.** They can adopt established plans, technologies, equipment, and organizational forms from early developers.<br>
**3.** They may leap over stages that early developers had to pass through, especially in technology.<br>
**4.** The experience of early developers helps latecomers anticipate parts of their own modernization.<br>
**5.** Early developers can supply capital and technology to latecomers.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在此基础上,有了阿布拉莫维茨的追赶假说:一国经济越落后经济增长速度越快.
埃利斯·布勒齐基于后发优势发展了蛙跳模型.后发国家可以直接抢占新科技领域,抢占经济发展的制高点.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Abramovitz's catch-up hypothesis builds on this idea: the farther an economy is behind, the faster it may be able to grow.

Elise Brezis developed a leapfrogging model from the idea of latecomer advantage. A late-developing country may enter a new technological field directly and capture a commanding position in economic development.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.4中等收入陷阱
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.4 Middle-Income Trap*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
