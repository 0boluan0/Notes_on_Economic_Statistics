# 2. [[Harrod—Domar 增长模型|哈罗德-多玛经济增长模型]]

*2. [[Harrod—Domar 增长模型|The Harrod–Domar Growth Model]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#必考 哈罗德-多玛和新古典必考一题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Required exam topic:** expect one question on Harrod–Domar or the neoclassical model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1 假设条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1 Assumptions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 不存在货币部门,且价格水平不变
2. 劳动力按照由外部因素决定的不变速度增长.$\frac{\frac{dN}{dt}}{N}=n=常数$
3. 社会的储蓄率,即储蓄和收入的比率不变.$\frac{S}{Y}=s=常数$ . S为储蓄,Y为收入,s为储蓄率
4. 社会生产要素只使用劳动N和资本K两种生产要素,且两种要素不能互相替代.
5. 不存在技术进步
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** There is no monetary sector, and the price level is constant.<br>
**2.** The labour force grows at an exogenously determined constant rate:<br>
   $$\frac{\frac{dN}{dt}}{N}=n=\text{constant}.$$
**3.** The saving rate—saving as a share of income—is constant:<br>
   $$\frac{S}{Y}=s=\text{constant},$$
   where $S$ is saving, $Y$ income, and $s$ the saving rate.
**4.** Production uses only labour $N$ and capital $K$, and the two inputs cannot substitute for each other.<br>
**5.** There is no technological progress.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==由假设条件可以推知,生产函数写作:Y=Y(N,K)=min(VK,ZN)==
其中$V=\frac{Y}{K}$是产出-资本比,$Z=\frac{Y}{N}$是产出-劳动比.V和Z都是固定的常数.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The assumptions imply a fixed-proportions production function:
$$Y=Y(N,K)=\min(VK,ZN),$$
where $V=\frac{Y}{K}$ is the output–capital ratio and $Z=\frac{Y}{N}$ the output–labour ratio. Both $V$ and $Z$ are constants.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2 模型内容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2 Model Content*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于生产函数是两者取最小值,所以两边都要算,算完之后哪边小选哪边.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because output is the minimum of the two capacity constraints, calculate both $VK$ and $ZN$ and use the smaller value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (1).产出与资本的关系：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1) The Relationship between Output and Capital*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据上述生产函数的设定：
$$V=\frac{Y}{K} \Rightarrow Y=VK$$
• 对$Y=VK$公式两边对时间$t$微分得到：
$$\frac{dY}{dt}=V\frac{dK}{dt}$$
由投资恒等式$I=S$，在均衡状态下（储蓄全部转化为投资）有：
$$I = S = sY$$
• 而投资$I$表示资本存量的变动率，因此：
$$I=\frac{dK}{dt} \Rightarrow \frac{dK}{dt}=sY$$
• 将以上式子代入到之前的产出变动式子中，得：
$$\frac{dY}{dt}=V\cdot sY$$
• 整理上述方程，得经济增长率：
$$\frac{\frac{dY}{dt}}{Y}=Vs$$
由此可见，经济增长率$Vs$取决于：
• $V$（产出-资本比），即每单位资本所产生的产出；
• $s$（储蓄率），即储蓄占收入的比例。
模型推导出经济的“保证增长率”（也称为资本充分利用的增长率）：
$$G_W = Vs$$
这一增长率意味着，为使资本得到充分利用，资本存量$K$和产出$Y$必须以同样的增长率同步增长，即：
$$\frac{\frac{dK}{dt}}{K}=Vs$$资本存量的变动等于产出-资本比和储蓄率的乘积.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From the production function,

$$V=\frac{Y}{K} \Rightarrow Y=VK$$

• Differentiating $Y=VK$ with respect to time gives

$$\frac{dY}{dt}=V\frac{dK}{dt}$$

At equilibrium all saving becomes investment, so $I=S=sY$:

$$I = S = sY$$

• Investment is the change in the capital stock:

$$I=\frac{dK}{dt} \Rightarrow \frac{dK}{dt}=sY$$

• Substitution gives
$$\frac{dY}{dt}=VsY.$$
• Dividing by $Y$ gives the growth rate
$$\frac{\frac{dY}{dt}}{Y}=Vs.$$
Thus growth depends on $V$, output produced per unit of capital, and $s$, saving as a share of income. The model calls
$$G_W=Vs$$
the **warranted growth rate**, or the rate consistent with full utilization of capital. Capital and output must grow at the same proportional rate:
$$\frac{\frac{dK}{dt}}{K}=Vs.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (2).产出与劳动的关系：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) The Relationship between Output and Labour*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑劳动人口增长，根据生产函数的劳动约束条件：
$$Y = ZN$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the labour constraint of the production function,
$$Y=ZN.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的含义是，在劳动充分就业条件下，产出必须等于劳动人口$N$乘以劳动生产率$Z$（固定常数）。
• 对该公式两边对时间$t$[[导数与求导规则#从差商到导数|微分]]，得到：
$$\frac{dY}{dt}=Z\frac{dN}{dt}$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under full employment, output equals the labour force $N$ times the constant labour-productivity parameter $Z$.
• Differentiating both sides with respect to time gives
$$\frac{dY}{dt}=Z\frac{dN}{dt}$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由此推出：
$$\frac{\frac{dY}{dt}}{Y}=\frac{\frac{dN}{dt}}{N}=n$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This yields:
$$\frac{\frac{dY}{dt}}{Y}=\frac{\frac{dN}{dt}}{N}=n$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的$n$称为经济的“自然增长率”，即劳动人口增长率。
换句话说，若想要保持充分就业，经济增长率必须等于劳动人口增长率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $n$, the growth rate of the labour force, is called the economy's **natural growth rate**. Maintaining full employment requires output to grow at the same rate as the labour force.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即经济的自然增长率为：
$$G_N = n$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The natural growth rate of the economy is given by:
$$G_N = n$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

哈罗德-多马模型因此存在两个重要的增长率：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore, there exist two important growth rates:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **保证增长率$G_W = Vs$**（资本角度出发的充分利用增长率）
• **自然增长率$G_N = n$**（劳动角度出发的充分就业增长率）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Warranted growth rate $G_W=Vs$**: the rate consistent with full utilization of capital.
• **Natural growth rate $G_N=n$**: the rate consistent with full employment of labour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**重要的推论与现实局限：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Important Inferences and Real-World Limitations:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

哈罗德-多马模型中，经济同时实现资本充分利用与劳动充分就业时，必须满足：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the Harrod–Domar economy to achieve both full capital utilization and full employment, the two rates must coincide:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

Vs = n

$$

但实际上，$Vs$和$n$通常并不相同，这就导致：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
However, $Vs$ and $n$ are typically not the same, which leads to:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 若$Vs>n$，经济资本过剩，出现失业。
• 若$Vs<n$，经济资本不足，存在资本短缺。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• If $Vs>n$, the economy has excess capital and unemployment appears.
• If $Vs<n$, capital is insufficient and a shortage emerges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

韩国案例即为实际案例：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
South Korea provides a practical example:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 韩国人口增速很低（自然增长率$n$小），但经济增长却较快（如3%），说明资本大量流入韩国，从而突破模型的封闭假设，说明该模型在考虑国际资本流动和技术进步等因素时，存在较大的局限性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
South Korea's population growth rate is very low (natural growth rate $n$ small), but its economic growth rate is relatively fast (such as 3%), indicating a large inflow of capital into South Korea, which breaks the model's assumption of closure and suggests that the model has significant limitations when considering factors such as international capital flows and technological progress.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

哈罗德-多马模型通过假设固定的储蓄率、资本产出比和劳动增长率，建立了经济增长的基本框架，提出了“保证增长率”和“自然增长率”两个重要概念，并指出实际经济中两者可能的不一致问题。然而，这一模型忽略了资本与劳动之间的可替代性、技术进步以及国际资本流动等因素，因此在现实中适用性存在一定限制，但仍为分析经济增长的基础模型提供了重要理论基础。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Harrod–Domar model builds a basic growth framework from a fixed saving rate, output–capital ratio, and labour-force growth rate. It distinguishes the warranted and natural growth rates and shows why they may fail to coincide. Because the model excludes substitution between capital and labour, technological progress, and international capital flows, its real-world scope is limited; nevertheless, it remains an important foundation for thinking about economic growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1 假设条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1 Assumptions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

索罗（[[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛#基本动态方程|Solow]]）模型建立在新古典生产函数的基础上，主要关注经济增长过程中的资本积累、劳动力增长及技术进步三个因素，研究它们对经济长期增长的影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛#基本动态方程|Solow model]] starts from a neoclassical production function and studies how capital accumulation, labour-force growth, and technological progress shape long-run economic growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**模型的基本假设：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Basic assumptions:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **经济由单一部门构成**，生产出的商品既可用于投资，也可用于消费。
2. **经济是封闭的**，无国际贸易，也无政府干预。
3. **生产具有规模报酬不变**（Constant Returns to Scale, CRS）。
4. **人口增长率$n$、技术进步率（此处先假设为零）和折旧率$\delta$均为外生变量**。
5. **社会储蓄函数**为$$S = sY$$其中，$s$为储蓄率，是外生给定的。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **The economy consists of a single sector**, producing goods that can be used for investment as well as consumption.<br>
**2.** **The economy is closed**, with no international trade and no government intervention.<br>
**3.** **Production exhibits constant returns to scale** (Constant Returns to Scale, CRS).<br>
**4.** **The growth rate of the population ($n$), the rate of technological progress (assumed to be zero here), and the depreciation rate ($\delta$) are exogenous variables**.<br>
**5.** **The social saving function** is given by $$S = sY$$ where $s$ is the savings rate, an exogenously given constant.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2 模型的基本方程与推导过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2 Core Equations and Derivation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**步骤1：新古典生产函数的设定**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Step 1: Specify the neoclassical production function**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设总产出为$Y$，总劳动量为$N$，资本存量为$K$，则经济生产函数为：
$$Y = F(N, K)$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assuming total output $Y$, total labor $N$, and capital stock $K$, the economic production function is:
$$Y = F(N, K)$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该函数满足规模报酬不变（CRS），意味着对任意正数$\lambda$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Constant returns to scale (CRS) means that, for every positive $\lambda$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\lambda Y = F(\lambda N, \lambda K)$$
设$\lambda = \frac{1}{N}$，则：
$$\frac{Y}{N} = F\left(1, \frac{K}{N}\right)$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
\[
\lambda Y = F(\lambda N, \lambda K)
\]
Let $\lambda = \frac{1}{N}$, then:
\[
\frac{Y}{N} = F\left(1, \frac{K}{N}\right)
\]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义人均变量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define variables per capita:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 人均产量$y = \frac{Y}{N}$，
• 人均资本存量$k = \frac{K}{N}$，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Output per person: $y=\frac{Y}{N}$
• Capital per person: $k=\frac{K}{N}$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

则可以进一步简化为人均形式：
$$y = f(k)$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then it can be further simplified to the per capita form:
$$y = f(k)$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**步骤2：资本积累方程的推导**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Step 2: Derivation of the Capital Accumulation Equation**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本存量的变动$\dot{K}$由投资$I$减去折旧$\delta K$决定：
$$\dot{K} = I - \delta K$$
投资来源于储蓄，储蓄率为$s$，因此：
$$I = S = sY$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The change in capital stock, $\dot{K}$, is determined by investment, $I$, minus depreciation, $\delta K$:
$$\dot{K} = I - \delta K$$
Investment comes from savings, and the saving rate is $s$, so:
$$I = S = sY$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

故资本积累的方程为：
$$\dot{K} = sY - \delta K$$
将两侧同时除以$N$，变为人均资本的变化方程：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation for capital accumulation is:
$$\dot{K} = sY - \delta K$$
Dividing both sides by $N$ gives the corresponding expression per person:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac{\dot{K}}{N} = s\frac{Y}{N} - \delta\frac{K}{N}$$

即：
$$\frac{\dot{K}}{N} = sy - \delta k$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
That is,
$$\frac{\dot{K}}{N} = sy - \delta k$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**步骤3：考虑人口增长**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Step 3: Account for population growth**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为$k=\frac{K}{N}$，其对时间的导数为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $k=\frac{K}{N}$, differentiating with respect to time gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\dot{k} = \frac{\dot{K}}{N} - nk$$

此处的$n$为人口增长率（外生给定）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $n$ is the exogenously given population growth rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

代入前式，得到完整的人均资本积累方程：
$$\dot{k} = sf(k) - (n+\delta)k$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substitution gives the full law of motion for capital per person:
$$\dot{k} = sf(k) - (n+\delta)k$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是索罗模型的核心方程，它描述了人均资本$k$随时间的变动情况。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the core equation of Solow's model, describing how capital per capita $k$ changes over time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.3 模型的稳态分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.3 Steady-State Analysis of the Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所谓稳定状态（steady-state），就是人均资本$k$达到不再变化的状态，即：
$$\dot{k}=0$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A steady state is a value of capital per person $k$ that no longer changes:
$$\dot{k}=0$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据方程：
$$sf(k) - (n+\delta)k=0$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
According to the equation:
$$sf(k) - (n+\delta)k=0$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解出稳态值$k^*$：
$$sf(k^*) = (n+\delta)k^*$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The steady-state value $k^*$ therefore satisfies
$$sf(k^*)=(n+\delta)k^*.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该状态下，人均资本不再增加或减少，对应的产出水平、储蓄和投资也保持稳定不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under this state, the per capita capital neither increases nor decreases, and the corresponding output levels, savings, and investments remain stable and unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 两条曲线交点$A$对应人均资本稳态值$k_A$，此时$sf(k_A) = (n+\delta)k_A$。

• Their intersection at $A$ gives the steady-state capital stock per person $k_A$, where $sf(k_A)=(n+\delta)k_A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 在$k<k_A$时，投资高于盈亏平衡，资本不断积累；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• When $k<k_A$, investment exceeds break-even point, and capital accumulates continuously.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 在$k>k_A$时，投资低于盈亏平衡，资本存量逐渐减少。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• When $k>k_A$, investment is below the break-even point, and capital stock gradually decreases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最终，经济系统都会向稳态水平$k_A$[[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛#稳态、比较静态与黄金律|收敛]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ultimately, the economy [[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛#稳态、比较静态与黄金律|converges]] to the steady-state level $k_A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**五、举例说明（数字化示例）**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**V. Numerical Example**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

比如，假设生产函数为柯布-道格拉斯函数：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For instance, suppose the production function is the Cobb-Douglas function:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

Y = K^{0.5} N^{0.5}

$$

  

转化成人均形式：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Convert to per capita form:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

$$

y = f(k) = k^{0.5}

$$

  

设储蓄率$s=0.2$，人口增长率$n=0.02$，折旧率$\delta=0.03$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the saving rate be $s=0.2$, the population growth rate $n=0.02$, and the depreciation rate $\delta=0.03$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

稳态条件为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The steady-state condition is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

$$

0.2 k^{0.5} = (0.02+0.03)k

$$

  

解方程得稳态资本：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solving for the steady-state capital yields:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

$$

0.2k^{0.5}=0.05k \

\Rightarrow k^{0.5}=\frac{0.05k}{0.2}=0.25k \

\Rightarrow k^{-0.5}=0.25 \

\Rightarrow k=16

$$

  

此时人均资本稳态为$k^*=16$，对应的稳态人均产出为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Steady-state capital per person is therefore $k^*=16$, and steady-state output per person is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

$$

y^* = k^{0.5} = 16^{0.5}=4

$$

**六、经济含义总结**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**VI. Economic Interpretation**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **长期稳定增长**：新古典索罗模型认为，没有技术进步的经济最终都会达到稳态，人均产出和资本不再增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Long-run steady state:** in the neoclassical Solow model, an economy without technological progress eventually reaches a state in which capital and output per person stop growing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **储蓄率提升**：虽然可以提高稳态的产出水平，但无法带来长期持续的增长，因为资本的边际收益递减。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Increase in Saving Rate**: While it can raise steady-state output levels, it does not lead to sustained long-term growth, as the marginal returns on capital diminish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **技术进步的重要性**：模型后续引入技术进步后，才能实现人均产出长期增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Importance of Technological Progress**: Only after introducing technological progress can a model achieve sustained long-term growth in per capita output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**I. How a Higher Saving Rate Changes the Steady State (Figures 20-6 and 20-7)**

**（1）储蓄率提高对稳态人均资本和人均产量的影响：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**The Impact of an Increase in the Saving Rate on Steady-State Per-Worker Capital and Output per Worker:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这意味着**储蓄率的提高提升了稳态的人均资本水平和人均产出水平**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This means that **an increase in the savings rate raises the steady state level of capital per capita and output per capita**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**（2）短期和长期效应（图20-7）：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**(2) Short- and Long-Run Effects (Figure 20-7)**

• 图（a）：在人均产出水平上，最初（$t_0$时刻）经济处于稳态产出水平$y_0$。储蓄率提高后，人均资本逐步增加，人均产出随之逐步上升，并在$t_1$达到更高的新稳态水平；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Figure (a): Initially ($t_0$), the economy is at a steady-state output level $y_0$ in terms of per capita output. With an increase in the savings rate, per capita capital gradually increases, resulting in a corresponding gradual rise in per capita output, which reaches a higher new steady-state level at $t_1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 图（b）：在人均产出增长率$G$方面：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Figure (b) shows the growth rate of output per person, $G$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 储蓄率提高后的短期内（$t_0$之后），人均产出增长率迅速上升；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• Shortly after the saving rate rises, after $t_0$, the growth rate of output per person jumps.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 随着人均资本积累，资本的边际收益递减，增长率逐步下降；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• As capital accumulates, diminishing marginal returns gradually reduce the growth rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• 最终在$t_1$重新回到稳态增长率水平（无技术进步时增长率为0）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• At $t_1$, the economy returns to its steady-state growth rate—zero when there is no technological progress.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

也就是说，**储蓄率增加仅导致稳态产出水平一次性提高，但长期增长率并未改变**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In short, a higher saving rate causes a one-off rise in the steady-state level of output but does not change the long-run growth rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• The steady state moves from $A$ to $A'$.

• Output per person falls with it because $f(k)$ is increasing in $k$.

说明**人口增长率的增加降低了稳态水平的人均资本和人均产出**。

Thus, **faster population growth lowers steady-state capital and output per person**.

**(2) Economic Implications of Population Growth:**

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• The steady-state capital stock is lower, reducing income per person and living standards.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

The neoclassical Solow model therefore yields an important insight:

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| **方面** | **[[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛|索罗模型]]**           | **库兹涅茨理论**       |

## 4.2 罗斯托的经济增长阶段论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2 Rostow's Stages of Growth Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

罗斯托将经济增长分为六个阶段
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rostow divides economic growth into six stages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

传统社会阶段-起飞准备阶段-起飞阶段-成熟阶段-大众高消费阶段-追求生活质量阶段
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Traditional society → preconditions for take-off → take-off → drive to maturity → age of high mass consumption → search for quality of life
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中前三个阶段和发展中国家关系最为密切.==经济增长阶段的更替可以表现为产业结构的变化==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first three stages are most closely related to developing countries. **A transition between growth stages can appear as a change in industrial structure.**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

罗斯托的中心概念是起飞.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rostow's central concept is takeoff.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

起飞的三个条件：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three conditions for takeoff:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 生产性投资占国民收入的比例上升至10%以上
2. 有一个或多个重要的主导部门,一般是制造业部门高速增长
3. 存在或迅速出现一个政治,社会和制度结构,其利用现代部门扩张的冲力和起飞的潜在外部效应,使增长具有持续性.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Productive investment rises above 10% of national income.<br>
**2.** One or more leading sectors, usually in manufacturing, grow rapidly.<br>
**3.** A political, social, and institutional structure exists—or rapidly emerges—that can harness the momentum of modern-sector expansion and the potential external effects of take-off, making growth self-sustaining.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.3 格申克龙的后发优势理论及其发展
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.3 Gerschenkron's Theory of Latecomer Advantage and Its Development*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==核心:经济上的相对落后有助于一个国家或地区实现爆发性的经济增长==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Core:** Relative economic backwardness can help a country or region achieve explosive growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原因:
1. 后发国对现代化的认知更丰富
2. 后发国可以大量采用和借鉴先发国成熟的计划,技术,设备,组织结构等
3. 后发国可以跳跃先发国对一些必经发展阶段,特别是技术方面
4. 先发国的发展过程可使后发国对于自己现代化有一定的预测
5. 先发国可以在资本和技术上对后发国提供帮助
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reasons:

**1.** Late-developing countries can draw on a richer accumulated understanding of modernization.<br>
**2.** They can adopt established plans, technologies, equipment, and organizational forms from early developers.<br>
**3.** They may leap over stages that early developers had to pass through, especially in technology.<br>
**4.** The experience of early developers helps latecomers anticipate parts of their own modernization.<br>
**5.** Early developers can supply capital and technology to latecomers.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在此基础上,有了阿布拉莫维茨的追赶假说:一国经济越落后经济增长速度越快.
埃利斯·布勒齐基于后发优势发展了蛙跳模型.后发国家可以直接抢占新科技领域,抢占经济发展的制高点.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Abramovitz's catch-up hypothesis builds on this idea: the farther an economy is behind, the faster it may be able to grow.

Elise Brezis developed a leapfrogging model from the idea of latecomer advantage. A late-developing country may enter a new technological field directly and capture a commanding position in economic development.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
