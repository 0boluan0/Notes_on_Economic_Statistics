# 贸易、产业政策与全球价值链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Trade, industrial policy and global value chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一家装配厂的出口翻倍，政府因此准备续发补贴。判断之前，先问三件事：更多出口在本地创造了多少收入？企业或供应商真正学到了什么？这些改善中，有多少来自支持政策，而不是国际需求增长？本章从贸易的资源配置收益出发，逐步把这三个问题拆开再合起来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An assembly plant doubles its exports and the government considers renewing its subsidy. First ask how much income was generated locally, what the firm or its suppliers learned, and how much improvement came from support rather than rising world demand. This chapter separates these questions and then brings them together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 开放贸易首先改变了什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. What does opening to trade change first?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定技术和资源，不假设企业因为出口就自动学会新本领。即使在这个静态起点上，国家也不必把所有东西都自己生产：它可以生产机会成本较低的产品，再用交换获得其他产品。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with technology and resources fixed, without assuming that exporting automatically teaches firms new skills. Even here, a country need not make everything itself: it can produce goods at lower opportunity cost and exchange them for others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[比较优势]]

把布酒例再推进一步，构造一个两边都能改善消费的可行配置。两国各有 1 小时劳动；原来各用半小时织布、半小时酿酒，A 消费 $(5,2.5)$，B 消费 $(3,2)$，括号顺序为布、酒。现在 A 用 0.9 小时织布、0.1 小时酿酒，产出 $(9,0.5)$；B 全部酿酒，产出 $(0,4)$。再按 1 酒换 1.75 布交易 3.5 布与 2 酒，A 最后得到 $(5.5,2.5)$，B 得到 $(3.5,2)$：两边的酒都不减少，各多了 0.5 布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Construct a feasible improvement using one labour hour per country. Initially each splits labour equally: A consumes (5, 2.5) and B (3, 2), ordered as cloth and wine. A then spends 0.9 hours on cloth and 0.1 on wine, producing (9, 0.5); B produces (0, 4). Exchanging 3.5 cloth for two wine leaves A with (5.5, 2.5) and B with (3.5, 2). Each has half a unit more cloth and unchanged wine.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若两种商品都是偏好中的好东西，这个配置使两国代表性消费者都更好。它演示给定资源下的互利可能，**不是已求出的竞争均衡**：要解均衡还需给定偏好、需求和价格形成。更重要的是，代表性消费者的改善仍不能代替国内不同群体的分配分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If both commodities are goods, both representative consumers benefit. This demonstrates a feasible mutual improvement, not a solved competitive equilibrium, which also requires preferences, demand and price determination. Representative-consumer gains cannot replace analysis of distribution across domestic groups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[贸易收益与分配]]

接着问限制进口会怎样。关税让本国部分企业更容易生存，但消费者和下游购买者面对更高价格。下面只做一个条件明确的基准账，后面再加入学习和协调问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A tariff may help some domestic firms survive, while raising prices for buyers. The next atom establishes a conditional benchmark before learning and coordination enter the analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[小国关税的福利成本]]

## 2. 企业学习与政策理由之间还差哪一步
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. What connects learning to a policy rationale?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

静态模型没有解释技术如何改变。进入新产品以后，企业可能逐渐减少返工、改进流程；这首先是一个学习问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The static model leaves technological change unexplained. After entering a new product line, firms may reduce rework and improve processes. First establish what learning means.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[学习效应]]

如果未来收益都能由企业自己取得，而且融资与信息没有其他障碍，眼下亏损、以后盈利的项目本来就可能值得私人投资。“开始成本高”还不足以说明为什么纳税人应该出钱。需要进一步识别私人激励与社会收益之间的缺口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If firms capture future returns and face no additional financing or information problem, an initially loss-making project may already justify private investment. High initial cost alone does not explain public funding. Identify the gap between private incentives and social returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[外部性]]

![[学习外部性]]

学习会改进做事的方法；另一种信息来自“第一次试了才知道这种产品在本地能否做得划算”。这两者都可能惠及后来者，但被发现的东西不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Learning improves how a task is performed. A different kind of information concerns whether producing a particular good locally is profitable in the first place. Both may benefit followers, but the information differs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[成本发现外部性]]

还有一种障碍不是“不知道怎么做”，而是每一项投资都在等另一项先出现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Another obstacle is not ignorance about production, but complementary investments each waiting for the others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[产业投资协调失灵]]

现在可以更准确地谈政策：先说清哪种活动不足、为什么不足，以及工具怎样改变这个缺口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Policy analysis can now name the underprovided activity, explain the shortfall, and connect an instrument to it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[产业政策]]

## 3. 支持何时停止，何时有理由延期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. When should support end or be extended?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政策理由不是实施结果。对“先支持、再学会”的项目，需要提前指定可查证的学习结果、复核时间、失败处置和责任人；也要检验管理部门能否取得成本、质量与受益对象的信息。实施问题可沿用 [[实施能力诊断]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rationale is not an outcome. A learn-with-support project needs observable learning results, review dates, failure procedures and accountable decision-makers. Check whether the implementing organisation can obtain cost, quality and beneficiary information, using the shared [[实施能力诊断|implementation-capability diagnosis]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[产业支持的停止与延期]]

例如补贴装配线前，先写出可检验的 [[变革理论]]：补贴缓解哪一个约束，哪些实践会产生知识，知识怎样到达其他企业。企业订单增多可以作为过程记录，却不等于知识外溢；每件投入下降也须与世界竞争者及产品质量变化比较。最终仍要与可行替代方案比较净收益，沿用 [[成本—收益分析]]，不能仅凭“企业还活着”续期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before subsidising assembly, state a testable [[变革理论|theory of change]]: which constraint is relaxed, which activities generate knowledge and how that knowledge reaches other firms. More orders are process evidence, not proof of spillovers. Falling unit inputs require comparison with world competitors and product quality. Renewal still needs [[成本—收益分析|cost–benefit analysis]] against feasible alternatives, not merely survival.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 沿价值链追踪本地收入和能力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Trace domestic income and capabilities along the chain*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个出口产品可能装着其他国家生产的芯片、软件和零件。先识别生产任务如何跨境，再核算价值在哪里产生。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An exported product may contain foreign chips, software and components. First identify the cross-border production tasks, then locate value creation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[全球价值链]]

![[全球价值链参与方向]]

进入价值链以后，“出口多少钱”和“本地产生多少钱的增加值”仍是两个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After joining a chain, gross exports and domestically generated value added remain distinct quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[出口额与国内增加值]]

![[国内增加值率与总量]]

核算说明价值产生在哪里；升级分析则问企业是否开始承担新的职责。销售更多同一种装配服务，与学会设计、研发或品牌管理，不是同一件事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Accounting locates value creation; upgrading asks whether firms assume new functions. Selling more of the same assembly service differs from acquiring design, research or brand-management capabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[价值链功能升级]]

## 5. 外资进入之后，收益如何传给本地企业
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. How can inward investment benefit domestic firms?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

GVC 是生产安排，FDI 是跨境投资关系。独立企业之间的订单也可以组织价值链，不必把所有国际分工都想成跨国公司的内部生产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
GVCs describe production arrangements; FDI describes investment relationships. Contracts between independent firms can organise a chain without common multinational ownership.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[外国直接投资]]

![[外资正向生产率溢出]]

![[外资溢出的传递方向]]

方向清楚之后，还要检查证据能说明什么。本地采购意味着建立了交易联系；要声称生产率溢出，必须进一步证明知识或能力收益，并处理谁会被选为供应商等识别问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once directions are clear, assess what the evidence identifies. Local purchases establish a commercial link. Productivity spillovers require additional evidence about capability gains and selection into supplier relationships.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[供应商联系与溢出]]

## 6. 抗冲击能力怎样进入这张账
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. How does resilience enter the assessment?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平时效率高的生产网络，也可能因某个关键环节中断而停摆。首先说清所担心的冲击，以及冲击后需要恢复的功能。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A network efficient in normal times may stop when a critical input is disrupted. Specify the shock and the function that must recover.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[供应链韧性]]

![[国产化与供应链韧性]]

比较方案时，正常年份的成本和受冲击后的损失都要列明；不能只算库存成本，也不能只数供应商国籍。课堂的 [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展pre 话题6|贸易冲突展示稿]]保留历史讨论语境，其中 2018 年后的贸易重组与 2025 年政策记录属于不同时段。本章不把它们的“最近”“今天”解释成现行政策，也不把预测的出口转移直接当作已经实现的福利增量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare both normal-time costs and post-shock losses, rather than inventory cost alone or supplier nationality. The preserved [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展pre 话题6|trade-conflict presentation]] combines different historical periods. Its relative dates are not statements of current policy, and projected export diversion is not realised welfare.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 用韩国重化工业政策检验推理链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Test the reasoning with Korea's heavy-industry drive*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课堂的 [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展pre 话题1|东亚工业化展示稿]]提供问题背景：选择性支持、国际竞争和产业升级如何相连。历史上“先有政策、后来增长”还不是因果识别。下面用一项针对韩国 1973–1979 年政策的研究，明确它比较了谁、估计了什么、还有哪些问题未回答。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preserved [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展pre 话题1|East Asian industrialisation presentation]] supplies the motivating question. Growth following a policy is not itself causal identification. A study of Korea's 1973–1979 programme makes the comparison, estimated outcomes and remaining limits explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[韩国重化工业政策的识别]]

展示稿把部分扶持概括为高关税或进口禁令；Lane 对这次 HCI 的证据却不支持“扶持行业普遍提高名义产出保护”这一概括，并显示投入品进口优惠的重要性。因此，引用这个案例时应使用论文实际识别的政策组合，而不是将所有支持措施合称为关税保护。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The presentation describes some support in terms of high tariffs or import bans. Lane's evidence on this HCI episode does not support a general rise in nominal output protection for targeted industries and highlights advantages for imported inputs. Use the policy package actually studied rather than treating every form of support as tariff protection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也是阅读展示稿时的尺度：把政策工具与可观察结果分开，把目标行业表现与整个经济的净收益分开。韩国的局部证据不能直接替台湾、拉美或其他年代作出因果结论；跨地推广须再检查 [[外部效度]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read historical presentations by separating instruments from observed outcomes and targeted-sector performance from economy-wide net benefits. Evidence from Korea does not establish effects in Taiwan, Latin America or other periods; transfer requires an [[外部效度|external-validity]] assessment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8. 把整章用于一次政策复核
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. Apply the chapter in a policy review*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设一项装配补贴即将到期。出口由 100 增至 180，出口中的国内增加值率由 40% 降到 30%；企业报称单位工时下降、本地供应商增多，同时要求再支持五年。以下数字都是练习设定，不是某国统计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose an assembly subsidy is expiring. Exports rose from 100 to 180 while their domestic-value-added share fell from 40% to 30%. The firm reports lower unit labour hours and more local suppliers and requests five more years. All figures are constructed for this exercise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先独立回答：本地增加值总量怎样变化？已有材料能证明学习外部性、功能升级和政策净收益吗？如果不能，复核时还缺哪几项证据？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First work out domestic value added. Then ask whether the evidence establishes learning externalities, functional upgrading or positive policy net benefits, and identify what further evidence is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 展开核对
> 国内增加值由 40 增至 54，比例下降不等于总量下降；这也不是补贴造成增量 14 的证明。工时下降要排除产品与投入变化；供应商增多要查知识传递及未内部化收益；继续装配而未承担新职能不能直接称为功能升级。延期还需要与世界竞争者比较学习进度，检查实施记录、反事实、消费者及其他企业的损益、筹资与机会成本，再与停止或改换工具比较。若提出国产化方案，还要明确冲击和可切换的替代来源。现有四个表面指标不足以作出延期决定。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Domestic value added rose from 40 to 54; a falling share need not mean a falling amount. Nor is the increment of 14 necessarily caused by the subsidy. Lower labour hours require checks for changing products and inputs. More suppliers require evidence about knowledge transfer and uncaptured benefits. Unchanged assembly functions do not establish functional upgrading. Extension requires learning progress relative to world competitors, implementation evidence, a counterfactual, effects on consumers and other firms, financing and opportunity costs, and comparison with ending or changing support. A localisation proposal also needs a specified shock and genuinely switchable alternatives. The four headline indicators cannot settle renewal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完成标准是能说明每一步**依据什么、还缺什么**，并能从关系图直接打开对应定义、机制或边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Completion means explaining the evidence and remaining gap at each step and opening the exact definition, mechanism or boundary from the map.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
