# 内生增长与创新
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Endogenous growth and innovation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设一个经济每年都把产出的五分之一拿去投资。它能不能一直提高人均收入？如果不能，增加受教育时间、雇用研究人员或开发新产品，究竟改变了哪条约束？本章从 Solow 留下的这个问题出发，把“积累什么、如何积累、谁愿意付钱”接成一条推理链。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose an economy invests one fifth of output every year. Can income per person keep rising indefinitely? If not, which constraint changes when people study longer, employ researchers or develop products? Starting from the question left by Solow, this chapter connects what accumulates, how it accumulates and who pays for it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

The chapter follows [[Solow 的外生技术边界|Solow's exogenous-technology boundary]] and leads to [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/11_国家能力、公共投入与政策评价|state capacity, public investment and policy evaluation]].

## 1. 先确定要解释的增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Identify the growth outcome to explain*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在标准 Solow 模型中，增加资本会提高产出，却也逐渐降低资本的边际回报。经济可以向较高的人均水平调整；要让人均产出在长期仍按正率增长，基准模型把劳动增进技术的增长率作为给定条件。现在要问的是：能否把持续增长所依靠的过程写进模型？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In standard Solow, additional capital raises output but gradually lowers its marginal return. The economy can adjust towards a higher per-capita level; sustained positive per-capita growth relies on a given rate of labour-augmenting technological change. Can the process supporting sustained growth instead be specified inside the model?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[内生增长]]

课堂课件第92页用技术引进、干中学来讨论技术来源。这是一个有用的实际问题，但与“模型把什么当成给定”不完全相同。先分开这两个问题，后面才不会把从国外买设备误读成 Solow 的定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Slide 92 discusses technological origins through acquisition and learning by doing. That practical question differs from what a model takes as given. Separating them prevents foreign equipment purchases from being mistaken for the definition of exogenous technology in Solow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[技术来源与内生性]]

## 2. AK：积累本身能否持续推动增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. AK: can accumulation itself sustain growth?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把发明新技术的问题放在一边，保持技术系数不变。改变的假设只有一项：资本增加后，每单位资本的产出不下降。用这个极简模型，可以看清递减回报在 Solow 中做了什么。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hold the technology coefficient fixed and set invention aside. Change one assumption: output per unit of capital does not fall as capital increases. This minimal model reveals the role played by diminishing returns in Solow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[AK模型]]

生产式还没有告诉我们存下多少。先采用给定储蓄率，把总投资、折旧和人口增长逐一计入；不要在写出总量增长率后就称其为人均增长率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Production does not determine saving. Begin with an exogenous saving share and account separately for investment, depreciation and population. An aggregate growth rate is not automatically a per-capita rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[AK人均增长条件]]

现在试着改变储蓄率。刚才例子中，人均产出为5，其中4用于消费、1用于总投资。多存一点确实能让未来积累更快，但今天的消费从哪里来，资源约束已经说得很清楚。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now change saving. In the example, output per person is five: four for consumption and one for gross investment. Saving more can accelerate future accumulation, but the resource constraint identifies today's consumption cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[AK储蓄率效应]]

另一个检验是把初始资本减半，而不改其他参数。Solow 的追赶机制依赖较低资本带来较高边际回报；在纯 AK 中，这个渠道还在吗？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Another test halves initial capital while keeping parameters fixed. Solow catch-up relies on higher marginal returns when capital is scarce. Does that channel remain in pure AK?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[AK收敛边界]]

到这里，我们只是规定家庭储蓄多少。若进一步问家庭愿意选择哪条消费路径，就需要偏好、回报和终端条件。“闭合”指补齐决定行为的关系，不是给同一个公式换名字。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So far, saving has been prescribed. Explaining the consumption path households choose requires preferences, returns and terminal conditions. Closure supplies the missing behavioural relationships rather than renaming a formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[AK闭合方式]]

## 3. 从积累设备到积累个人能力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. From accumulating equipment to accumulating capabilities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个程序员多练习一小时，未来可能更快解决问题。这里增加的不是人数，也不一定是全世界以前没有的新设计，而是这个人拥有的能力。先明确对象，再写它的积累式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An extra hour of practice may help a programmer solve problems faster later. The accumulating object is neither population nor necessarily a globally new design, but an individual's capability. Define the object before writing its accumulation law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[人力资本]]

Lucas 型机制让学习占用本可生产商品的时间。它也把持续增长所需的强假设摆出来：同样的学习时间，能否在技能存量越来越高时仍带来同样的百分比改善？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Lucas-style mechanism uses time for learning that could otherwise produce goods. Its key assumption is explicit: can the same learning time continue delivering the same percentage improvement as skills rise?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[人力资本线性积累]]

课堂所说的干中学是另一条渠道：在生产实践中获得改善，定义沿用 [[学习效应]]。学习者本人能力增加，与未获充分补偿的知识帮助了别的生产者，还需要再分开，后者沿用 [[学习外部性]]。这些是共享概念；以下研发模型会明确写出自己的知识传播机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The classroom's learning-by-doing channel concerns improvements acquired in production, using the shared [[学习效应|learning-effect definition]]. Gains to the learner differ from uncompensated benefits to other producers, covered by [[学习外部性|learning externalities]]. The R&D models below specify their own knowledge-transmission mechanisms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. Romer：一张设计如何变成更多产出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Romer: how does a design increase production?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在换一个对象：可被不同生产者反复使用的设计。工程师的工作时间会被占用，机器也不能同时安放在两座工厂里，但同一设计可以指导多台机器。先用财政学中同一组概念区分技术上的可重复使用与制度上的访问控制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now consider a reusable design. Engineering time is occupied and one machine cannot serve two factories simultaneously, but one design can guide many machines. Use the same rivalry and exclusion concepts as in public finance to distinguish reuse from access control.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[物品的竞争性与排他性]]

![[技术知识的非竞争性]]

“别人使用它不妨碍我使用”只回答消费性质，没有回答研发成本怎么付。下面的成本回收问题，是 Romer 选择特定市场结构的理由；它的结论带有明确条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Simultaneous usability describes consumption, not how research is financed. The next cost-recovery problem motivates Romer's market structure under explicit conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[研发成本回收条件]]

本节采用 Romer（1990）的原始人力资本研发版本：劳动人数 $L$ 和总人力资本 $H$ 固定，后者在商品生产与研发之间分配；新设计增加可用中间品的种类。这里 $A$ 是设计种类的连续测度，$\delta$ 是研发效率，$\gamma=1-\alpha-\beta$ 是中间品指数。它们不再是上一节 AK 的生产率系数与折旧率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This section uses Romer's original human-capital R&D model: labour $L$ and total human capital $H$ are fixed, with the latter allocated between production and research. Designs expand intermediate varieties. Here $A$ measures varieties, $\delta$ is research productivity and $\gamma=1-\alpha-\beta$ is the intermediate-input exponent, not the AK productivity and depreciation parameters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Romer品种扩张模型]]

先固定研发和定价，只看生产技术。同样多的物质资本分散到更多种类，为什么会改变总产出？推导时应把“固定 $K$ 增加品种”与“$K$ 和品种一起增长”分清。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First isolate production from research and pricing. Why does distributing a fixed capital stock across more varieties change output? Distinguish that comparison from a growth path on which capital and varieties both expand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[品种扩张的生产效率]]

更多品种有生产价值，不代表品种会自动出现。研发要使用人力资本；既有设计还会帮助后来的研发。模型中的知识外溢就在这条关系里，而不是只在正文中说“存在外部性”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Productive varieties do not appear automatically. Research employs human capital, and existing designs assist later research. The model specifies the spillover through this relationship rather than merely naming an externality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Romer研发知识外溢]]

## 5. 从租金、研发配置到 Romer 增长率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. From rents and research allocation to Romer growth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还有一个缺口：研究人员为什么不去生产最终商品？先求一项设计能带来多少收入，再比较两种工作的人力资本报酬，最后让家庭消费选择与资本积累相容。顺序很重要：不能一开始就把研发人员比例当成已解出的均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why do researchers not work in final production? First derive a design's income, then compare human-capital remuneration across activities, and finally reconcile household consumption with accumulation. The research share is an equilibrium outcome to derive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Romer中间品加价]]

上式的利润是单位时间的流量；研发者卖出设计拿到的价格是未来收入的现值。利润为正并不表示购买专利后还存在自由进入无法消除的额外净收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Profit above is a flow; a design's sale price is the present value of future income. Positive operating profit does not imply an additional net return after paying for the patent that free entry cannot remove.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Romer专利价值]]

![[Romer研发人力配置]]

两部门报酬相等给出研发配置与利率的关系。利率还影响家庭愿意怎样推迟消费。将两边接起来，才得到增长率以及正研发、正消费和效用有限的适用条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal remuneration relates research allocation to the interest rate, which also affects households' willingness to postpone consumption. Combining both sides determines growth and its positive-research, positive-consumption and finite-utility conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Romer平衡增长率]]

上面的完整例子中，$H_A=0.8$，$H_Y=1$，设计、产出和资本沿平衡路径都按每年 $4\%$ 连续增长。它不是任意初始资本配置都立即实现的路径。下一步还要检验：如果研究资源总量持续上升，原式预言什么，数据能否支持这种规模效应？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the complete example, $H_A=0.8$ and $H_Y=1$, while designs, output and capital grow at a continuously compounded annual rate of $4\%$ on the balanced path. Arbitrary initial capital configurations need not start there. Next examine what the model predicts when research resources expand and what the evidence permits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[研发规模效应边界]]

效率评价也要保持模型口径。Romer 原模型的新设计帮助后续研发，却没有让发明者收齐这部分收益；中间品市场结构还改变设计能获得的报酬。先在这个模型内部比较私人研发与规划者，而不是从“创新很重要”直接提出补贴金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Welfare comparison must retain the model's assumptions. Romer designs assist future research without fully compensating inventors, while intermediate-market structure also affects their remuneration. Compare private research with the planner within this model before discussing policy amounts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Romer研发不足]]

## 6. 创造性破坏：新一代会取代旧一代
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Creative destruction: a new generation replaces the old*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在刚才的品种扩张中，新品种没有把旧品种逐一淘汰。现在考虑质量升级：下一位创新者成功以后，原来那位创新者的收入来源消失。进步仍会留下更高的生产率基础，但私人收益不再一直归最初的发明者所有。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Variety expansion did not sequentially eliminate old products. Now consider quality upgrading: the next successful innovator displaces the previous innovator's income. Progress leaves a higher productivity base even after private returns pass to someone else.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[创造性破坏]]

本节采用 Aghion–Howitt（1992）的线性研发、Cobb–Douglas、剧烈创新版本：技术一次次跳升，没有物质资本积累；固定的熟练劳动 $N$ 在研发 $n$ 与中间品生产 $x=N-n$ 之间分配。这里 $n$ 是研发劳动，$\gamma>1$ 是每次创新的质量倍数，$\lambda$ 是研发到达效率，$r$ 是风险中性家庭的固定贴现率。它们与 AK、Romer 的同名字母不是同一参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the linear-research, Cobb–Douglas, drastic-innovation version of Aghion–Howitt (1992). Technology jumps sequentially, without physical-capital accumulation. Fixed skilled labour $N$ is split between research $n$ and intermediate production $x=N-n$. Here $n$ is research labour, $\gamma>1$ the quality multiplier, $\lambda$ research-arrival productivity and $r$ the risk-neutral household's fixed discount rate. Identical letters in AK or Romer denote different objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先处理“投入研究之后多久会成功”。概率论中的 [[齐次泊松过程]] 描述成功次数，[[泊松指数等价]] 把它与下一次成功的等待时间联系起来；经济模型再说明研发劳动怎样决定到达强度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First model the time until success. The [[齐次泊松过程|homogeneous Poisson process]] describes discovery counts, while the [[泊松指数等价|Poisson–exponential equivalence]] connects them to waiting times. The economic model specifies how research labour determines arrival intensity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[研发的泊松到达]]

![[创新租金的替代风险]]

于是研发报酬不仅依赖成功后的利润，还依赖利润能持续多久。企业雇用的研发劳动，必须与生产部门愿意支付的工资相容；未来创新到达加快又会缩短今天所争取的租金期限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Research rewards depend both on profit after success and on its expected duration. Research employment must be consistent with manufacturing wages; faster future innovation arrivals shorten the rent horizon sought by today's researchers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Aghion–Howitt稳态研发]]

稳定的是研发配置和到达强度，不是每一年恰好发生同样次数的创新。若每次升级使产出乘以 $\gamma$，平均对数路径和平均产出水平又该怎样区分？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stationarity fixes research allocation and the arrival intensity, not the number of innovations in each calendar year. When each upgrade multiplies output by $\gamma$, how do average log growth and growth of expected output differ?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[随机创新的增长口径]]

## 7. 私人创新够不够：把正负作用放进同一比较
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Is private innovation sufficient? Compare the opposing effects*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一次质量升级至少改变了三件事：未来创新站上更高起点，新企业取得利润，原企业失去利润。还要加上研发者本来可以生产多少商品。下面分别辨清机制，再放回同一个福利问题中，不能把所有收入变化都加成社会资源损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A quality upgrade raises the starting point for future innovation, gives profits to an entrant and removes profits from an incumbent. Research also withdraws labour from producing goods. Separate these mechanisms, then compare them within one welfare problem instead of treating every income change as a resource loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[创新的跨期知识外溢]]

![[商业抢夺效应]]

![[垄断下的研发机会成本]]

这里已有推高私人研发和压低私人研发的不同作用。下一张卡给出同一模型中的完整私人解、社会目标和两组数值：不是挑一句口号决定方向，而是把机会成本与未来收益真正算在一起。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some forces raise private research relative to the optimum and others lower it. The next atom supplies the private equilibrium, social objective and two numerical comparisons in the same model, calculating opportunity costs and future benefits together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[私人与社会研发]]

Romer 原模型里的研发不足，与这里私人研发可能过多，并不矛盾。前者增加品种且保留旧品种收益；后者用新质量替代旧租金，资源约束和福利目标也不同。跨模型比较时，先问哪个机制进入了方程，不把一个模型的政策符号搬到另一个模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Romer underinvestment and possible overinvestment here are consistent. The former adds varieties while preserving old-variety returns; the latter replaces old rents through quality improvements, with different resources and welfare assumptions. Compare the mechanisms specified in each model before transferring a policy conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8. 把理论用于一次研发支持判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. Use the theory to assess research support*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设有人提出：“研发会外溢，所以应补贴所有研发支出的20%；补贴后 TFP 上升，就说明政策有效。”仅凭本章模型，还不能接受这个推论。现在可以有次序地问：补贴改变的是研究人员、学习时间还是采用现成技术？原本未进入私人回报的是哪一种收益？资源会从哪里移出？观察到的变化应与什么无政策情形比较？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the claim: subsidise all R&D spending by twenty percent because research spills over, and judge success by subsequent TFP growth. The chapter's models do not establish that conclusion. Ask which activity changes, which benefits private incentives omit, where resources come from and what no-policy outcome provides the comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

工具分析沿用 [[正外部性补贴]] 的适用条件；私人付款与真实资源消耗沿用 [[财务与社会评价]]、[[社会机会成本]]。是否真正增加研发，用 [[公共项目反事实]] 界定比较基准；是否值得做，用 [[成本—收益分析]] 比较可行替代。Aghion–Howitt 的两组例子解释激励偏离，尚未计入具体补贴制度的财政筹资、实施与分配问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the conditions in [[正外部性补贴|positive-externality subsidies]], distinguish financial payments from resources through [[财务与社会评价|financial versus social appraisal]] and [[社会机会成本|social opportunity cost]], and define additionality using [[公共项目反事实|the project counterfactual]]. Compare feasible alternatives with [[成本—收益分析|cost–benefit analysis]]. The Aghion–Howitt examples establish incentive wedges without analysing a particular subsidy's financing, implementation or distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增长核算中的残差也不是“新发明的直接读数”。先沿用 [[增长核算的 TFP 残差]] 理解它扣除了什么、还混着什么，再寻找识别设计。[[跨国增长回归的因果边界]] 给出了收敛回归这一具体场景的警示；它不是对任何研发政策都通用的识别方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The growth-accounting residual is not a direct measure of new inventions. Use [[增长核算的 TFP 残差|the TFP residual atom]] to identify what is removed and what remains, then seek an identification design. [[跨国增长回归的因果边界|The cross-country growth-regression boundary]] addresses the specific convergence-regression setting rather than supplying a universal R&D-policy estimator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 合上推导后，试着连起来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Reconstruct the connections without the derivations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一问：AK 中总产出与人口的年连续增长率分别为4%和5%，是否已经解释持续人均增长？第二问：同一设计能同时用于十座工厂，是否说明设计应该免费，或者十座工厂可以共用同一台机器？第三问：质量升级的到达强度为 $z$，为何私人专利现值出现 $r+z$，而社会评价还须考虑升级之后的后续收益？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, do continuously compounded annual growth rates of four percent for AK aggregate output and five percent for population establish positive per-capita growth? Second, does using one design in ten factories imply free access or one shared physical machine? Third, with innovation intensity $z$, why does private patent valuation involve $r+z$ while social appraisal includes benefits beyond replacement?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 核对思路
> 第一问的人均增长率为负1%，总量增长不能代替人均增长。第二问分别涉及非竞争性、排他和实物资源，三者不能合并。第三问中，下一次创新终止当前租金，却没有抹去本次创新为后续技术留下的更高起点；同时还要计入商业抢夺与研发劳动的机会成本，才能判断私人研发偏多还是偏少。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Per-capita growth in the first question is minus one percent. The second separates nonrivalry, exclusion and physical resources. In the third, the next innovation ends current rents but preserves the higher technological starting point. Business stealing and research labour's opportunity cost must also enter the comparison before judging under- or overinvestment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

学完后的判断标准不是记住四个作者名，而是能为选定机制说明：**增长的对象、资源约束、决策关系、持续增长的条件，以及私人回报与社会收益是否一致。** 接下来将这些判断带入 [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/11_国家能力、公共投入与政策评价|政策实施与评价]]，再看 [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/12_贸易、产业政策与全球价值链|国际分工如何影响学习与能力]]。
