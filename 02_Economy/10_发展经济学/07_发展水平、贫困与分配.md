# 发展水平、贫困与分配
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Development, poverty and distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课堂的国别表把人均产出、基尼系数和 HDI 放在一起。它们都与发展有关，却可能给出不同排序：一个国家平均产出更高，不意味着穷人更少，也不意味着健康与教育结果更好。本章从这张表的问题出发，先弄清各列测量什么，再用一组收入计算贫困与分配，最后回到国别比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course's country table places output per person, the Gini coefficient and HDI together. All concern development, yet their rankings may differ: higher average output does not imply fewer poor people or better health and education. This chapter identifies what each column measures, works through poverty and distribution using a small income dataset, then returns to the country comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关系总图：[[发展、贫困与不平等的测量.canvas|发展、贫困与不平等的测量]]。原课堂记录：[[01_不同国家的经济发展模式]]；原表：[[发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=12|讲义第12页]]。下面已嵌入必要解释与例子，可从头连续读；普通链接用于查证或延伸。

## 1. 先分清发展结果与资源规模
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Development outcomes and the scale of resources*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“发展得更好”需要先说明评价对象。先看发展概念，再把其中的生产维度单独拿出来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Better development” requires an explicit object of evaluation. Start with development, then isolate its production dimension.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[经济发展]]

GDP 回答一个经济体在一段时间内生产了多少。它采用增加值口径，所以面包中已包含的小麦不会再作为一份独立最终产出加一次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
GDP measures an economy's production during a period. Value-added accounting prevents the wheat already embodied in bread from being counted again as separate final output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[国内生产总值]]

从“规模”走到“增长”时，要先排除单纯的涨价；否则相同数量的商品涨价，也可能被误读成生产能力增加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Moving from size to growth requires removing price increases; otherwise unchanged quantities sold at higher prices may look like increased production.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[实际GDP增长]]

从“国家规模”走到“平均到人”时，再处理人口分母。真实产出增长和人口增长共同决定实际人均产出的变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Moving from the size of the economy to a per-person figure adds the population denominator. Real output and population growth jointly determine real output growth per person.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[人均GDP]]

这仍然是在衡量本地生产。若要问哪些初次收入归本地常住单位，还要计入跨境收入归属；后面 HDI 的收入维度正需要这个对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The measure still concerns domestic production. To identify primary income accruing to residents, account for cross-border income. This is the income concept needed later for the HDI.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[国民总收入]]

产出与收入概念说清楚后，还剩一个评价边界：它们提供资源信息，但不能单独替代生活结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once production and income are distinguished, an evaluative boundary remains: they inform us about resources without fully representing people's lives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[GDP与福利]]

## 2. 国别比较先过价格与分类这一关
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Align prices and classifications before comparing countries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两国都报告“美元人均值”，也可能采用不同价格体系。先理解转换率，再按所问的问题选口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two countries can both report a per-person “dollar” value while using different price systems. Understand the conversion rate first, then select the basis for the question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[购买力平价]]

![[汇率与购买力比较]]

收入数值变为“高收入”标签时，还多了一层分类规则。课堂中并列出现的世界银行、UNDP 和 IMF 标签应分开读。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Turning an income number into a “high-income” label adds a classification rule. Read the World Bank, UNDP and IMF labels in the lecture separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[收入分组与发展分类]]

到这里，应当能给一列数据写出完整说明，例如“某年度、某 PPP 比较体系下的人均 GDP”，而不是只写“人均收入”。调查年份与发布版本的进一步检查见[[经济数据的实时口径]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
You should now be able to label a data column fully—for example, GDP per capita for a specified year and PPP comparison system—rather than merely “income per person.” See [[经济数据的实时口径|real-time data conventions]] for further checks on observation years and release vintages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 同样的均值，谁还在贫困线下
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Who remains below the line?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从国家总量转向人的生活，要回到家庭调查。先确定收入或消费的内容、怎样换成人均量和人口权重，再选贫困线；这些处理在任何贫困公式之前。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Moving from national totals to people's living conditions requires household evidence. Define income or consumption, convert household aggregates and weights to the relevant population basis, then choose a poverty line. These decisions precede every poverty formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[贫困测量的福利口径]]

![[贫困线]]

![[绝对贫困与相对贫困]]

下面固定四名等权居民，同一期间的实际收入为 40、90、120、150，绝对贫困线为 100。这里的 100 是教学阈值，不是任何现行国际贫困标准。我们连续问三个问题：多少人不够、总体差多少、最深的短缺有没有缓和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix four equally weighted residents with real incomes of 40, 90, 120 and 150 for the same period, and an absolute line of 100. This is a teaching threshold, not a current international poverty standard. Ask three successive questions: how many fall short, how large is the overall shortfall, and has the deepest deprivation eased?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[贫困人口比例]]

人数指标不记录仍在线下的改善。要看改善有多大，把缺口也算进去；注意下面仍然对所有人平均。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The headcount does not record improvements that remain below the line. Add the shortfalls to measure their size, retaining the whole population as the denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[贫困缺口指数]]

如果问题改成“贫困者自己平均差多少”，就要换成条件分母。乘积关系把它与刚才两个总体指标接起来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Asking how far poor people themselves fall short requires a conditional denominator. The product identity connects that average to the two population measures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[贫困者平均缺口]]

即使总缺口相同，也可能是“一人非常穷、一人接近线”，或“两人离线一样远”。下面用同一笔贫困者内部转移说明怎样区分它们。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An identical total gap can consist of one very poor person and another close to the line, or two equally sized shortfalls. The following within-poor transfer distinguishes these cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[平方贫困缺口]]

## 4. 从贫困短缺转向完整分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. From poverty shortfalls to the full distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

贫困指标围绕一条线；不平等指标比较整个分布的相对差距，线以上的人也参与。先把人口与收入各自累积，再把曲线压缩成面积指标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Poverty measures organize shortfalls around a threshold. Inequality measures compare relative differences across the full distribution, including people above that line. Accumulate population and income shares, then summarize the resulting curve with an area measure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[洛伦兹曲线]]

![[基尼系数]]

回到收入 40、90、120、150：总收入为 400，最低一半人的份额为 $130/400=32.5\%$；六个无序收入差之和为 $50+80+110+30+60+30=360$，所以 $G=720/(2\times16\times100)=0.225$。它与贫困率 0.5 并不矛盾，二者的分子、分母和问题都不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to incomes 40, 90, 120 and 150. Total income is 400; the lower half receives $130/400=32.5\%$. The six unordered differences sum to $50+80+110+30+60+30=360$, giving $G=720/(2\times16\times100)=0.225$. This does not conflict with a poverty rate of 0.5: their numerators, denominators and questions differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基尼不是看不见转移位置，而是按一种特定方式响应转移。这个性质与“同一个基尼仍可隐藏不同分布”要分开理解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Gini is not blind to transfer location; it responds in a particular way. Distinguish that response from the fact that equal Ginis can conceal different distributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[基尼与转移位置]]

![[相同基尼与分布形状]]

## 5. 健康、教育与重叠剥夺
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Health, education and overlapping deprivation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收入与分配说清了资源的一部分。若要把健康、教育一起纳入国别摘要，HDI 提供一套明确的标准化与聚合规则。阅读时留意其收入项是 GNI、教育项有两个，以及最后为什么取几何平均。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Income and distribution describe part of the resource picture. HDI supplies explicit normalization and aggregation rules for adding health and education to a country summary. Notice its use of GNI, its two education inputs, and its final geometric mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[人类发展指数]]

但三个全国平均值并不告诉我们：教育不足、健康受损和缺基本设施的，是不是同一群人。要识别这样的重叠，必须有联合信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three national averages do not reveal whether the same people lack education, health and basic services. Identifying overlap requires joint information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[多维贫困]]

全球 MPI 是一种具体实现。它与一般的“多维贫困”概念不同，有自己的十项指标、权重和门槛；其人数比例与强度也不能混为一个百分比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The global MPI is one specific implementation, with ten indicators, weights and a cutoff. It is not the general concept of multidimensional poverty, and its population incidence and deprivation intensity are distinct quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[全球多维贫困指数]]

## 6. 用两次比较把这一章连起来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Bring the chapter together with two comparisons*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先比较同一人口在两个状态中的收入。增长提供更多总资源，但减贫还取决于分配；下面的反例使用前面的四人基准，所有价格和绝对线保持不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First compare two income states for the same population. Growth adds aggregate resources, but poverty reduction also depends on distribution. The following counterexample uses the four-person baseline with unchanged prices and an unchanged absolute line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[增长与减贫]]

再回到[[发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=12|课堂第12页的国别表]]。以下两行按课堂展示值抄录，用于练习读表；它们不是本章重新核定的历史统计值，也不代表当前状况。课件给三列标注的参考年本来就不同：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now return to the [[发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=12|country table on lecture page 12]]. These two rows reproduce the displayed classroom values for a reading exercise; they are not independently re-established historical statistics or current observations. The slide itself assigns different reference years to the columns:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 课堂展示 / Slide entry | PPP 人均 GDP，标注2020年 / PPP GDP per capita, labelled 2020 | 基尼，标注2017年 / Gini, labelled 2017 | HDI，标注2020年 / HDI, labelled 2020 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 马来西亚 / Malaysia | 34,567 | 41.0 | 0.810 |
| 土耳其 / Türkiye | 29,327 | 41.4 | 0.820 |

在这些展示值中，马来西亚的人均产出列更高，土耳其的 HDI 列却更高。这只能说明两列排序不同；仅凭 HDI 总分，还不能判定是哪一个健康、教育或 GNI 分项造成差别。两国基尼的 0.4 点差距，也需要调查口径与不确定性信息才适合细读。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In these displayed values, Malaysia ranks higher in output per person, while Türkiye ranks higher in HDI. This establishes different column rankings, not which health, education or GNI component causes the difference. Interpreting a 0.4-point Gini gap also requires survey comparability and uncertainty information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这张表没有给出同一贫困线下的家庭分布，因此不能据此判定谁的货币贫困率更低；也没有十项同源家庭剥夺信息，因此不能计算全球 MPI。若要作正式国别结论，应回到每列原始统计来源，对齐观察年、福利定义、PPP 版本和修订记录，不能将课件表头当成已经完成的来源核验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The table supplies neither household distributions under a common monetary poverty line nor the ten joint household indicators needed for the global MPI. It cannot determine which country has the lower monetary poverty rate or yield an MPI calculation. Formal country conclusions require reopening each original statistical source and aligning observation years, welfare definitions, PPP vintages and revisions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 自己完成一次解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain one comparison yourself*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仍以四人收入 40、90、120、150 为起点，只把收入 90 提高到 100，其余不变。先遮住下面的答案，计算均值、$H$、$PG$、$IGR$、$P_2$ 与 $G$；再用一句话解释为什么 IGR 的上升不等于有人收入下降。最后对课堂国别表说出一个可读出的结论和一个不能读出的结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start again from 40, 90, 120 and 150. Raise only the income of 90 to 100. Hide the answer below, then calculate the mean, $H$, $PG$, $IGR$, $P_2$ and $G$. Explain in one sentence why an increase in IGR does not mean somebody lost income. Finally, state one supported and one unsupported conclusion from the classroom country table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 核对
> 均值 $102.5$；$H=0.25$；$PG=0.15$；$IGR=0.6$；$P_2=0.09$；$G=700/(2\times16\times102.5)\approx0.2134$。收入较接近线的人脱贫后退出 IGR 的分母，剩下者平均短缺更深，但没有人变穷。国别表能读出两列排序不同，不能读出共同标准下的贫困率排序。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Mean $102.5$; $H=0.25$; $PG=0.15$; $IGR=0.6$; $P_2=0.09$; $G=700/(2\times16\times102.5)\approx0.2134$. The person nearer the line exits poverty and the IGR denominator. Those remaining have a deeper average shortfall, although nobody becomes poorer. The country table supports differing column rankings, not poverty-rate rankings under a common standard.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

能够算清各个分母、解释这次构成变化，并指出国别表缺少的证据，就完成了本章的核心练习。下一步从“差距怎样量”转到“差距为何长期存在”：回到[[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/00_课程总览#制度与地理如何解释差距|课程总览的制度与地理部分]]，或用[[制度、地理与长期发展差距.canvas|制度主题总图]]看解释通道。
