# 国家能力、公共投入与政策评价

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*State capacity, public investment and policy evaluation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条路可以按时建成，却没有让果农多卖出水果。也可能果农收入确实提高了，但同期果价上涨才是主要原因。再往前一步：即使道路真的改善了收入，它是否比修补旧路更值得花钱？本章沿着这三个问题，把政策的机制、交付、效果和价值分开，再连成一个判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A road may be completed on time without helping growers sell more fruit. Income may rise mainly because fruit prices rose. Even if the road genuinely improves incomes, might repairing the existing route be a better use of resources? This chapter separates mechanism, delivery, impact and value, then connects them into one judgement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> 从下面第一节顺读即可；必要原子已经嵌入。[[国家能力、公共投入与政策评价.canvas|主题图]]用于看清各问题的关系，普通链接供进一步查阅。
> **读完后应能做到：** 面对一个公共投入方案，说明它要改变哪条机制、由谁交付、用什么反事实判断效果、成本是否值得，以及推广时哪些条件要重查。

By the end, explain the mechanism, delivery responsibilities, counterfactual, value for resources and conditions that need reassessment before expansion.

## 1. 先把“修了路”与“解决了问题”分开

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Separate completing a road from solving the problem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课堂 [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=174|PDF 第 174 页]]列出基建形成资产、带动就业收入和降低交易成本；[[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=175|第 175 页]]进一步用果品与道路说明市场可达性。这里先抓住最后一条机制：果子能够低成本种出来，并不保证能在变质前运到愿意购买的消费者那里。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Classroom PDF pages 174–175 connect infrastructure with asset formation, employment and transaction costs, using fruit and roads to motivate market access. Focus first on that last mechanism: producing fruit cheaply does not guarantee getting it to willing buyers before it spoils.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

建造活动的产出核算、需求与就业的变化、长期运输效率、社会净收益是不同问题。不能把“施工增加了支出”直接当作项目值得实施的证据；也不能把施工工资既当作无成本的社会收益，又忽略劳动力用于其他事情的[[社会机会成本|机会成本]]。先给项目中的观测量分类：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Production accounting, demand and employment responses, transport efficiency and net social value are different questions. Construction spending alone does not establish value, and workers' wages are not a free social gain when their [[社会机会成本|opportunity costs]] are ignored. Begin by classifying what the project measures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[项目投入产出与结果]]

对于果农，问题可以写成“降低雨季运输中断与腐损”，而不是预先写成“必须建一条新公路”。后者已经把解法塞进目标；前者才允许比较修补旧路、改善排水、引入货运服务或其他组合。接着把预期改变写成可以被证据推翻的解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define the problem as reducing rainy-season disruption and spoilage, rather than requiring a new highway. The former permits comparison with repairs, drainage and freight services; the latter has already selected the instrument. Next make the expected causal chain testable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[变革理论]]

## 2. 机制说得通，还要看谁能把它做出来

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A plausible mechanism still needs capable delivery*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设我们已经说明道路怎样改善运输。现在问另一件事：政府能否筹集资金、取得真实路况、选出合格施工者，并使维护持续发生？[[制度塑造发展激励|制度]]讨论规则和激励；这里进一步看这些安排所需的实际能力。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the transport mechanism is plausible. Can government mobilise funds, obtain reliable information, select competent contractors and sustain maintenance? [[制度塑造发展激励|Institutions]] concern rules and incentives; the next question is the capacity to make arrangements work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[国家能力]]

“这个国家执行力差”还不能告诉我们怎样改路政项目。同一政府内，征税机构、地方道路部门和采购办公室可能有完全不同的人员、信息和约束。把观察单位缩小到承担具体任务的组织，诊断才开始可操作。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Calling a country weak at implementation does not identify a repair for its road programme. Tax agencies, local road departments and procurement offices may differ markedly. A diagnosis becomes actionable when it identifies the organisation carrying out the task.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公共组织实施能力]]

随后逐步追问：路况资料是谁提供的？拨款何时到账？谁能验收工程质量？发现问题后谁有权修改合同或追责？先找任务在哪一环卡住，再判断培训、信息系统、责任安排或工具调整是否针对这个问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Who supplies road-condition data, when do funds arrive, who verifies quality, and who can act on failures? Identify the obstructed task before selecting training, information systems, accountability changes or a different instrument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[实施能力诊断]]

这些问题并没有预先规定政府必须亲自施工。道路可能收费或拥挤，也不能因为有公共资金就把它当作纯公共品。这里借用公共品课程中可通用的一项制度比较规则：将筹资、生产和治理分开问。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These questions do not require government to build the road itself. Roads may be priced or congested; public funding does not make them pure public goods. Reuse a general institutional comparison: separate financing, production and governance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公共品的筹资、生产与治理]]

## 3. 从立项一直管到道路可用

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Manage the asset beyond project approval*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一次拨款能支付施工，却未必保障多年养护。采购合同写明质量，也未必有人会检验。公共投资管理要连接的是这些跨阶段的责任与资源，而不只是工程审批。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Construction funding does not guarantee years of maintenance, and a quality clause does not guarantee competent inspection. Public investment management connects responsibilities and resources across stages, beyond project approval alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公共投资管理]]

把这项管理要求放回道路案例：若雨季排水是通行的关键，就必须有人维护排水设施、能取得养护经费，并有信息判断是否真的畅通。“配套”由所需服务和实际障碍决定，不是所有地方都必须同时建设同样的道路、仓库和市场。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If drainage is critical to rainy-season access, someone must maintain it, obtain funding and verify passability. Complementary arrangements follow the required service and actual obstruction; they are not an identical package of roads, warehouses and markets for every location.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 分别检查实际交付与项目贡献

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Check delivery and causal contribution separately*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评价应在项目开始前准备：记录谁会受到影响、原来的运输状况、同期其他变化和可行比较对象。等道路修好才发现没有基线或可靠对照，会让“到底改善了多少”更难回答。先明确我们想比较的两种情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Prepare evaluation before implementation: record affected groups, baseline transport conditions, concurrent changes and viable comparisons. Discovering missing baseline or comparison data after construction makes attribution harder. First specify the two situations to compare.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公共项目反事实]]

有无项目的差额是目标量，不是我们手中已经有的答案。随机分配、准实验或模型分析都要说明比较为何可信；选择方法时可进一步使用 [[因果研究设计检查]]。与此同时，必须知道真正实施的政策版本：是全天候通路，还是仅在旱季开放、货运尚未进入的道路？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The with–without contrast defines what to estimate, not an answer already observed. Randomised, quasi-experimental and model-based approaches must justify their comparison; see [[因果研究设计检查|the causal-design audit]]. Also identify the intervention actually delivered: all-weather access is not a dry-season road without freight services.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[过程评价]]

若收入没有明显改善，不能仅凭最终一栏填“失败”。可能道路没有如期通行，可能通行改善了但市场机制不同于预期，也可能观察时间太短或估计不够精确。下面把这些解释分开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inconclusive income result is not a complete failure diagnosis. Access may not have been delivered, market responses may differ from the theory, or the observation window or statistical precision may be inadequate. Separate those explanations next.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[实施与理论失败]]

## 5. 效果与资源价值分开判断

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distinguish impact from value for resources*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使可靠研究表明道路减少了损耗，选择方案仍需考虑建造、养护、土地和环境等得失。这是判断层次，不是等项目做完才算价值：立项前用有依据的预期影响比较方案，实施后再用实际证据更新判断。先说明谁和哪些时期进入评价，才不会把项目寿命内的维护当成账外支出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even a credible reduction in spoilage leaves construction, maintenance, land and environmental trade-offs to assess. This is a distinction between questions, not a requirement to wait until completion: appraisal compares evidence-based expected impacts before approval, and evaluation updates the judgement with actual evidence. Set the population and time horizon before calculating, so lifetime maintenance does not disappear from the account.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公共项目评价范围]]

![[成本—收益分析]]

如果可靠货币估值做不到，仍可以在同一种结果、质量和时期下比较资源用量，但要承认这种比较回答的问题更窄。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When reliable monetisation is unavailable, compare resources for a common outcome, quality and period, while recognising the narrower question being answered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[成本效果分析]]

**综合计算例（构造）：** 假设新路 A 和旧路修复 B 是互斥且可实施的方案，已用同一反事实、人群、时期和估值口径折现。金额为统一单位，并已避免重复计入同一项运输改善。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Constructed comparison:** A new road and repair of the existing route are mutually exclusive, feasible alternatives. Values use a common baseline, population, horizon and valuation, are already discounted, and do not double-count the same transport improvement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 方案 / Option | 已计效益现值 / Benefits | 含维护的成本现值 / Costs incl. maintenance | 净额 / Net |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 新路 A / New road | 18 | 12 | 6 |
| 修复 B / Repair | 9 | 4 | 5 |

就这些已计项目，A 比 B 多 1；不是“18 比 9 大”就结束了。若 A 还有尚未估值的湿地损害，或它假设的养护安排根本无法实现，排序所需的信息仍不完整。这个例子不把这些未知量自动填成零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A exceeds B by one in the included account. Comparing gross benefits of 18 and nine is insufficient. Unvalued wetland damage or infeasible maintenance would leave the ranking incomplete; these unknowns are not zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[公共项目净现值边界]]

## 6. 试点有效，推广仍是一个新问题

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A successful pilot still leaves a new expansion question*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设试点道路由经验丰富的团队维护，服务的是本来就有果品产出的村庄。把结果用于另一地区，需要重新说明那里的人群、地形、市场和执行安排是否支持同一机制。先定义这个“用到别处”的要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose experienced teams maintain the pilot roads in villages already producing fruit. Applying its result elsewhere requires reassessing people, terrain, markets and delivery arrangements. First define what it means to use evidence in a different setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[外部效度]]

全国扩大还可能改变“别处”本身：施工与养护人员需求增加、多条路共同改变市场可达性、交易流向和价格随之调整。它不是把同一试验不变地复制很多次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
National expansion can also change the setting itself: demand for construction and maintenance workers grows, and a road network can alter access, trade flows and prices. Expansion is not necessarily many unchanged copies of one experiment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[试点扩张的效应外推]]

## 7. 用一次判断收束全章

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bring the chapter together in one judgement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给你一份项目汇报：“道路按计划完工；一年后果农平均收入上升 8%；申请把预算扩大十倍。”先不做支持或反对的结论，写五句话：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A report says: “The road was completed as planned; average grower income rose by 8% after one year; we request ten times the budget.” Before deciding, write five sentences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 收入改善的预期机制是什么，哪一步有证据？
2. 实际交付了什么服务，运行与维护由谁保障？
3. 8% 是前后变化还是相对可信反事实的效果？
4. 增量收益、成本、受损者和替代方案是否比较完整？
5. 扩大后哪些人群、实施、市场或政治条件可能改变？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
&nbsp;
**1.** What mechanism is expected, and which links have evidence?<br>
**2.** What service was delivered, and who secures operation and maintenance?<br>
**3.** Is 8% a before–after change or an effect against a credible counterfactual?<br>
**4.** Are incremental benefits, costs, losers and alternatives adequately compared?<br>
**5.** Which population, implementation, market or political conditions could change at scale?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 一个合格的判断
> 完工证据支持工程交付，不单独支持农户收益归因。应检查全年通行、运输使用与成本变化，建立可信反事实；在相同范围和时期下比较含维护的净收益与替代方案，并为扩大后的人员和市场变化补证据。现有两项记录不足以确定预算应扩大十倍，也不足以证明应该停止项目。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Completion supports delivery, not attribution of income gains. Check year-round access, use and transport costs; establish a credible counterfactual; compare lifetime net benefits and alternatives; and investigate staffing and market responses to expansion. The two observations alone justify neither a tenfold expansion nor termination.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
