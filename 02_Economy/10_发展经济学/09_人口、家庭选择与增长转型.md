# 人口、家庭选择与增长转型

同样是“人口变化”，出生人数增加、生育率下降、劳动年龄人口占比提高和老年消费支出增加，说的并不是一件事。这一节沿着一条线读下去：先量清楚人口怎样变，再解释家庭为什么改变选择，最后判断这种变化怎样进入生活水平和代际收支。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More births, lower fertility, a larger working-age share and higher old-age consumption describe different changes. This chapter first measures population change, then explains household choices, and finally traces their implications for living standards and intergenerational resources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

The [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/02_人口增长与经济发展|classroom notes]] and [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=24|original diagram on lecture page 24]] remain available as source records.

## 1. 人口增加了，是哪一个量在变化

先从最简单的账开始。一个地区的人口既受到出生和死亡影响，也受到迁移影响。要解释总人口变化，不能只盯着生育。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with the population balance. Births, deaths and migration all matter, so fertility alone cannot explain total population change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[人口变动恒等式]]

不同规模的人口不能直接比较出生或死亡的绝对人数，因此需要率。下面两项都用整个地区的人口暴露量作分母；它们适合描述总体事件频度，但也受年龄构成影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rates allow comparisons across populations of different sizes. The next two measures use total population exposure as their denominator. Both describe aggregate event frequency and are affected by age composition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[粗出生率]]

![[粗死亡率]]

假如某地出生人数变多，可能是进入生育年龄的人更多，也可能是同龄女性的生育频度提高。总和生育率把年龄别生育率组合起来，回答后一个方向的问题；它仍然是按当期条件构造的指标，不是对某一代女性最终生育数量的直接观察。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More births may reflect more women entering childbearing ages or higher fertility at each age. The total fertility rate combines age-specific fertility rates to summarize the latter pattern. It is a period construction, not a direct observation of a cohort's completed fertility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[总和生育率]]

## 2. 马尔萨斯反馈怎样吸收一次收入改善

有了“人口怎样增减”的口径，再问收入为什么有时难以持续提高。马尔萨斯机制需要把人口响应和资源约束连起来，单独的土地有限或人口众多都不足以解释一个陷阱。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With population change defined, ask why income gains might fail to persist. The Malthusian mechanism links population responses to resource constraints; neither finite land nor a large population alone establishes a trap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[马尔萨斯陷阱]]

![[马尔萨斯生存收入]]

现在把反馈写成一个能逐步推导的模型。先固定技术水平和人口行为，找到稳态；再让技术一次提高，分开冲击瞬间与人口调整之后。这里按总人口计算人均产出，与[[人均GDP|人均 GDP 的分母]]一致；把它解释成工资还需要另外的分配条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The next model makes the adjustment explicit: find a steady state at fixed technology and demographic behaviour, then separate the immediate effect of a technology improvement from later population adjustment. Its population denominator matches [[人均GDP|GDP per capita]]; interpreting output per person as a wage would require additional distributional assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[马尔萨斯技术冲击]]

## 3. 回到课堂图：比较两条增长率，而不是寻找峰值

[[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=24|课堂第 24 页]]画的是另一种表达：横轴为人均收入，纵轴同时画总收入增长率与人口增长率。两条曲线相等时，人均收入不变；两条曲线本身却可以都为正。这与上一节“人口净增长为零”的固定土地模型稳态不是同一个定义，不能直接把两个图上的均衡点互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=24|lecture diagram]] uses a different representation: income per person is on the horizontal axis, while aggregate-income growth and population growth share the vertical axis. Equal rates leave income per person unchanged even when both are positive. This is not the zero-population-growth steady state of the preceding fixed-land model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[人口陷阱相图]]

读图时先找两条曲线谁在上面，再决定人均收入向左还是向右变化。在图的连续调整解释下，判断稳定性看交点两侧的方向；若改成离散时期模型，还要核对是否过度跳跃。若技术或家庭行为改变了曲线，则重新比较新曲线；“提高一次收入”和“改变之后的增长关系”是不同干预。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare the two curves before deciding whether income per person moves left or right. In the diagram's continuous-adjustment interpretation, stability depends on the directions on both sides of an intersection; a discrete-period model also requires checking for excessive jumps. If technology or household behaviour shifts a curve, repeat the comparison: a one-time increase in income differs from changing the subsequent growth relationships.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 家庭为什么会改变生育与教育选择

前面的简化人口方程把家庭响应写成一个函数。现在打开这个函数：资源如何在子女数量、每名子女的投入和其他消费之间分配？先读预算中的交互成本，再分辨究竟是哪一种收入或价格变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The simplified population equation summarized household responses in a function. Now examine the underlying choices between the number of children, investment per child and other consumption. Start with interacting costs, then identify the income or price that changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[生育数量质量权衡]]

![[生育的工资与收入效应]]

这些比较静态没有自动解释历史转型。下面补上一条具体机制：技术变迁提高教育回报，家庭增加每名子女的教育投入，再通过时间预算影响数量。读时一直保留两个区分：技术水平与技术变迁速度不同；育儿资源总量与资源在数量、质量间的分配也不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These comparative statics do not by themselves explain a historical transition. The next mechanism links technological change to educational returns, investment per child and fertility through the time budget. Distinguish the technology level from its rate of change, and total child-related resources from their allocation between quantity and quality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[教育回报与生育转型]]

## 5. 从家庭选择到人口转型，还有年龄结构

家庭机制解释为什么生育可能下降；人口转型描述出生、死亡长期变化的组合。二者有关，但不能把一个条件模型直接变成每个国家必经、同时发生的时间表。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Household mechanisms explain why fertility may fall, while demographic transition describes the long-run combination of birth and death patterns. The two are related, but a conditional model does not provide a universal timetable for every country.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[人口转型]]

当期生育率改变，并不会让过去出生的各代人口消失。年轻队列陆续进入生育年龄，意味着“每名女性少生”与“当年出生人数仍多”可以同时成立；要理解人口总量何时转向，还必须继续看年龄分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A change in current fertility does not remove cohorts born earlier. Large young cohorts entering childbearing ages can sustain many births even when fertility per woman falls. Population trajectories therefore also depend on the age distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[人口惯性]]

## 6. 人口红利怎样进入人均生活水平

先用年龄抚养比描述结构，再问它能不能转化为经济机会。年龄分组是统计口径，不能直接把组内每个人当成就业者或被供养者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the age-dependency ratio to describe composition before asking about economic opportunities. Age groups are statistical categories, not direct counts of workers and dependants.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[年龄抚养比]]

![[人口红利]]

机会怎样兑现，需要把总人口与实际就业连接起来。下一张卡使用同一个[[劳动生产率|劳动生产率对象]]：人均产出取决于每名就业者生产多少，也取决于人口中有多少人实际就业。这把人口主题接回前一节的[[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/08_结构转型与劳动生产率|部门生产率与就业重配]]，不需要再造一套生产率定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Realizing the opportunity requires connecting population to actual employment. The next atom reuses [[劳动生产率|labour productivity]]: output per person depends both on output per employed person and on the employed share of the population. This links back to [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/08_结构转型与劳动生产率|sectoral productivity and employment reallocation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[年龄结构与人均产出]]

## 7. 老龄化怎样改变消费供养与公共收支

当老年人口占比提高，先确认我们描述的是年龄构成，而不是已经算出了经济损失或财政缺口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An increasing older-population share describes age composition; it does not yet quantify an economic loss or a fiscal gap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[人口老龄化]]

不同年龄的人平均劳动收入和消费不同，所以经济供养比要给人数加上相应权重。这一步已经从“按年龄数人”转向“按收入与消费型衡量”，其情景假设也必须跟着保留。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Average labour income and consumption vary by age. The economic support ratio therefore weights population counts using the corresponding age profiles. Moving from headcounts to income and consumption profiles also introduces assumptions that must remain explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[经济供养比]]

总量供养压力之外，还要问一个具体的人或年龄组怎样支付消费。劳动收入低于消费的差额，并不自动等于政府需要补的金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Beyond aggregate support, ask how a person or age group finances consumption. A gap between consumption and labour income does not automatically equal a required government payment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[生命周期赤字]]

![[老年消费的筹资]]

最后单独看政府这本账。财政供养比使用税收与公共受益的年龄型，因此既不等于年龄抚养比，也不等于全经济的劳动收入—消费供养比。退休、税制或福利安排改变时，要更新政策假设后再计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally isolate the public budget. The fiscal support ratio uses age profiles of taxes and public benefits, so it differs from both age dependency and economy-wide labour-income/consumption support. Changes in retirement, taxation or benefits require updated policy assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[财政供养比]]

## 8. 合上材料，沿着整条链做一次判断

一个构造地区期初有 100 万人，当年出生 1.5 万、死亡 1 万、净迁入 0.2 万。另一个比较中，劳动年龄人口占比从 60% 升到 65%，每名就业者实际产出保持 10 万元，但就业人数占总人口的比例从 50% 降到 48%。某老年组人均消费为 8 万元、劳动收入为 2 万元。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed region with one million initial residents, annual births are 15,000, deaths 10,000 and net immigration 2,000. In a separate comparison, the working-age share rises from 60% to 65%, real output per employed person stays at 100,000 yuan, but employment as a share of population falls from 50% to 48%. An older group consumes 80,000 yuan per person and earns labour income of 20,000 yuan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

算出期末人口、人均产出的变化和老年组的生命周期赤字。接着解释：为什么这些数字不能单独给出总和生育率、人口红利已经兑现或政府养老金缺口？最后回到课堂图：若总收入和人口都增长 3%，人均收入是否在增长，是否能据此说人口停止增长？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Calculate final population, the change in output per person and the older group's lifecycle deficit. Why do these figures not determine the total fertility rate, establish a realized demographic dividend or measure a public pension gap? In the classroom diagram, what happens to income per person if aggregate income and population both grow by 3%, and does population stop growing?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 核对思路
> 期末人口为 $100+1.5-1+0.2=100.7$ 万。总和生育率还需要年龄别生育率；年初人口也不是计算粗率时自动可替代的全年人口暴露量。
>
> 人均产出从 $10\times0.50=5$ 万元降至 $10\times0.48=4.8$ 万元，下降 4%。劳动年龄占比提高，没有在这个例子中转化成更高的实际就业比例。
>
> 生命周期赤字为 $8-2=6$ 万元；这部分可能由净公共转移、净私人转移与资产型再分配共同筹资，仅凭这一差额不能确定养老金支出或财政缺口。
>
> 若两条增长率均为 3%，人均收入增长率为 $1.03/1.03-1=0$；人口仍增长 3%。相图中的交点表示人均收入不变，不表示人口数量不变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Final population is 1,007,000. TFR requires age-specific fertility rates, while crude rates require appropriate population exposure rather than an automatic substitution of the opening population.
>
> Output per person falls from 50,000 to 48,000 yuan, a 4% decline. The higher working-age share does not translate into a higher employed share in this example.
>
> The lifecycle deficit is 60,000 yuan. Net public and private transfers and asset-based reallocations may jointly finance it. The deficit alone does not determine pension spending or a fiscal gap.
>
> Equal 3% aggregate-income and population growth gives zero per-capita-income growth, while population still grows by 3%. The diagram's intersection fixes income per person, not population size.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

能够独立说明“用什么人口口径、家庭响应依赖什么条件、年龄结构怎样经过就业或收支进入结果”，就完成这一节。接下来进入[[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/00_课程总览#3. 二元经济与劳动力迁移|二元经济与劳动力迁移]]：人口在哪里工作，会进一步影响收入、迁移激励和城市就业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
You have completed this chapter when you can explain the population measure, the conditions governing household responses, and how age composition affects outcomes through employment or resource flows. Continue to [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/00_课程总览#3. 二元经济与劳动力迁移|dual economies and labour migration]] to study where people work and how that shapes income and migration incentives.
