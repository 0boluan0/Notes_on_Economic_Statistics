第一阶段:生活质量较差,人口出生率和死亡率几乎相等.人口稳定或平稳增长

Stage one: Living standards are poor, and birth and death rates are nearly equal, so the population is stable or grows slowly.
Stage two: Modernization improves public health, nutrition, and medical care. Death rates fall sharply, while birth rates do not yet follow.
Stage three: The cost of raising several children exceeds the perceived benefit, largely as a consequence of higher income.

## 2.3 模型解读
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3 Model Interpretation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在马尔萨斯模型（[[马尔萨斯陷阱与现代增长转型#马尔萨斯反馈环|Malthusian]] Model）中，假设**技术水平**和**自然资源**相对稳定或增长缓慢，当人均收入（或人均产量）提高时，会刺激人口增长率上升；但随着人口不断增加，可用资源的人均占有量下降，又会导致人均收入回落到某个较低的水平，最终形成“陷阱”——也就是**人口增长抵消了收入增长**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[马尔萨斯陷阱与现代增长转型#马尔萨斯反馈环|Malthusian model]], technology and natural resources are assumed to be stable or slow-growing. A rise in income or output per person increases population growth. As the population expands, however, resources per person fall and income per person returns to a low level. The result is a trap in which population growth absorbs income growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **关键假设：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Key assumptions:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 经济中的生产主要依赖有限的土地或资源；
2. 随着人口增加，人均可获得的资源减少，边际产量下降；
3. 当人均收入较高时，人口出生率上升、死亡率下降，人口增长率变高；当人均收入下降时，人口出生率下降、死亡率上升，人口增长率降低甚至为负。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Production in the economy depends primarily on limited land or resources;
2. Marginal product declines as the population increases and resources available per capita decrease;
3. When the per capita income is high, the birth rate rises, the mortality rate falls, and the population growth rate becomes high; when the per capita income falls, the birth rate falls, the mortality rate rises, and the population growth rate decreases or even becomes negative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **结果：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Result:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果技术进步不足以抵消人口增长对资源的“稀释”效应，最终会使人均收入徘徊在维持生存的“**subsistence**（生存线）”附近，这就是“马尔萨斯陷阱”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If technological progress cannot offset the way population growth dilutes resources, income per person eventually hovers near the **subsistence** level. That is the Malthusian trap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

级差地租（Differential Rent）最早由古典经济学家[[Smith、Ricardo 与古典政治经济学#Ricardo 的分配、地租与增长|李嘉图]]（David Ricardo）提出，用于解释为什么在同一市场中，不同地块（或不同质量的资源）会产生不同的地租。其核心观点是：**地租来自于土地肥力（或资源禀赋）的差异**，市场最终由“边际地块”——即肥力最差、刚好能覆盖成本而无剩余的土地——来决定粮食（或其他农产品）的市场价格。更肥沃的土地由于生产成本更低，在相同的市场价格下就能获得超额收益，这部分超额收益便是级差地租

With resources constrained, the subsistence wage depends in practice on food prices. Differential rent means that population growth forces progressively less fertile land into cultivation, raising marginal production costs.

[[Smith、Ricardo 与古典政治经济学#Ricardo 的分配、地租与增长|David Ricardo]] introduced differential rent to explain why plots of different quality earn different rents in the same market. Its central claim is that **rent comes from differences in soil fertility or resource endowments**. The marginal plot—the least fertile land that just covers its costs and earns no surplus—sets the market price of grain. More fertile land has lower costs and therefore earns a surplus at that price; this surplus is differential rent.

供给曲线为何呈“阶梯”形
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why is the supply curve shaped like a staircase?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在古典级差地租理论中，假设有若干块不同肥力的土地，分别记为$L_1, L_2, L_3, \dots$，其单位产出成本从低到高排列：
$$
c_1 < c_2 < c_3 < \dots
$$
• **最好土地**（如$L_1$）成本最低，只要市场价格$P \geq c_1$，就会投入生产。
• **次好土地**（如$L_2$）需要更高的市场价格$P \geq c_2$才能盈利，否则无法投入生产。
• 依此类推，越往后的土地肥力越差，需要的价格阈值越高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the classical theory of differential rent, suppose several plots of differing fertility, $L_1, L_2, L_3, \dots$, are ordered by unit production cost:
$$
c_1 < c_2 < c_3 < \dots
$$
- The **best land** (such as $L_1$) has the lowest cost, and will be put into production as long as the market price $P \geq c_1$ is met.
- The **second-best land** (such as $L_2$) requires a higher market price $P \geq c_2$ to be profitable, otherwise it cannot be put into production.
- This pattern continues, with each subsequent plot of land having lower fertility and requiring increasingly higher price thresholds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，当市场价格提升到某个区间时，就会把新的、较差的土地吸引进来参与生产，导致供给量出现“跳增”。从宏观角度看，粮食供给曲线就呈现出一种“阶梯”或分段的特征：
• **第一段水平**：在价格$[c_1, c_2)$之间，仅$L_1$供应粮食；
• **第二段水平**：在价格$[c_2, c_3)$之间，$L_1$和$L_2$都供应粮食；
• **……**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the market price enters a higher interval, a new and less fertile class of land becomes profitable, so supply jumps. At the aggregate level, the grain-supply curve is therefore stepped or segmented:

• **First segment flat**: Between prices $[c_1, c_2)$, only $L_1$ supplies grain;
• **Second segment flat**: Between prices $[c_2, c_3)$, both $L_1$ and $L_2$ supply grain;
• **...**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**从需求变动看地租如何产生**
1. **初始均衡：需求曲线$d_0$与供给曲线交于$(Q_0, P_0)$**
	• 此时，可能只有肥沃的土地（成本低于$P_0$）在生产；
	• 边际土地的成本$=P_0$，该地块获得的地租为0；
	• 肥沃土地（成本$<P_0$）则获得**级差地租**：
$$
\text{Rent}_i = P_0 - c_i,\quad c_i < P_0.
$$
2. **需求上升：需求曲线从$d_0$移至$d_1$**
	• 为满足更多需求，市场需要产出更多粮食$Q_1$，这就需要开垦新的、较差的土地；
	• 这些新土地的单位成本可能是$c_j > c_i$，最终推高均衡价格至$P_1$；
	• **结果**：
	1. **所有肥沃土地**的地租增加为：$$\text{Rent}_i’ = P_1 - c_i \quad (\text{相比原来增加 }(P_1 - P_0)).$$
	2. **刚投入生产的边际土地**成本$=c_j$满足$P_1=c_j$，其租金为0。
	3. **供给量**从$Q_0$增至$Q_1$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**How a Change in Demand Creates Rent**

1. **Initial Equilibrium: Intersection of the Demand Curve ($d_0$) and the Supply Curve at $(Q_0, P_0)$**
	• At this point, only fertile land with cost below $P_0$ may be in production.
	• The marginal plot has cost $P_0$ and earns zero rent.
	• Fertile plot $i$, with $c_i<P_0$, earns **differential rent**:
$$
\text{Rent}_i=P_0-c_i,\qquad c_i<P_0.
$$
2. **Increase in Demand: Shift of the Demand Curve from $d_0$ to $d_1$**
	• Meeting the higher demand requires output $Q_1$ and therefore the cultivation of new, less fertile land.
	• The new land may have unit cost $c_j>c_i$, which raises the equilibrium price to $P_1$.

• **Results:**
1. Rent on **every fertile plot** rises to $$\text{Rent}_i'=P_1-c_i,$$ an increase of $P_1-P_0$.
2. The newly cultivated marginal plot satisfies $P_1=c_j$ and earns zero rent.
3. Quantity supplied rises from $Q_0$ to $Q_1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，**级差地租**本质上是由更肥沃土地和边际土地之间的成本差异造成的，**市场价格由边际土地决定**，而肥沃土地享受“差额收益”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus, **differential rent** is the cost advantage of fertile land over marginal land. The marginal plot determines the market price, while more fertile plots receive the resulting surplus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
