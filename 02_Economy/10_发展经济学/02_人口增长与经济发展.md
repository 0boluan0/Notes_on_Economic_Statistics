Population first grows steadily, then surges, and finally declines.

第一阶段:生活质量较差,人口出生率和死亡率几乎相等.人口稳定或平稳增长
第二阶段:随着现代化,公共卫生,饮食健康,医疗条件的普遍改善.使死亡率大幅下降.出生率没有随之下降.

Stage one: Living standards are poor, and birth and death rates are nearly equal, so the population is stable or grows slowly.
Stage two: Modernization improves public health, nutrition, and medical care. Death rates fall sharply, while birth rates do not yet follow.
Stage three: The cost of raising several children exceeds the perceived benefit, largely as a consequence of higher income.

## 2.1 两个假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1 Two Assumptions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 假设社会中,存在一个刚好可以维持个人生存的生存工资.如果一个人的工资低于生存工资.则无法生活,假设收入高于生产工资,多余的部分就会养小孩,人口就回增加
2. 收入(粮食)的多寡受到自然资源的约束.尤其是土地资源的不变,会导致投资的边际收益下降.因此收入的增长是有限的.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Society has a subsistence wage that is just sufficient for an individual to survive. Below that wage, survival is impossible; above it, the surplus supports additional children, so the population grows.<br>

**2.** The amount of income (grain) is constrained by natural resources, especially fixed land resources, which can lead to diminishing marginal returns on investment. Consequently, income growth is limited.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在马尔萨斯模型（[[马尔萨斯陷阱|Malthusian]] Model）中，假设**技术水平**和**自然资源**相对稳定或增长缓慢，当人均收入（或人均产量）提高时，会刺激人口增长率上升；但随着人口不断增加，可用资源的人均占有量下降，又会导致人均收入回落到某个较低的水平，最终形成“陷阱”——也就是**人口增长抵消了收入增长**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[马尔萨斯陷阱|Malthusian model]], technology and natural resources are assumed to be stable or slow-growing. A rise in income or output per person increases population growth. As the population expands, however, resources per person fall and income per person returns to a low level. The result is a trap in which population growth absorbs income growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **关键假设：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Key assumptions:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 经济中的生产主要依赖有限的土地或资源；
2. 随着人口增加，人均可获得的资源减少，边际产量下降；
3. 当人均收入较高时，人口出生率上升、死亡率下降，人口增长率变高；当人均收入下降时，人口出生率下降、死亡率上升，人口增长率降低甚至为负。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Production in the economy depends primarily on limited land or resources;<br>
**2.** Marginal product declines as the population increases and resources available per capita decrease;<br>
**3.** When the per capita income is high, the birth rate rises, the mortality rate falls, and the population growth rate becomes high; when the per capita income falls, the birth rate falls, the mortality rate rises, and the population growth rate decreases or even becomes negative.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **结果：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Result:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果技术进步不足以抵消人口增长对资源的“稀释”效应，最终会使人均收入徘徊在维持生存的“**subsistence**（生存线）”附近，这就是“马尔萨斯陷阱”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If technological progress cannot offset the way population growth dilutes resources, income per person eventually hovers near the **subsistence** level. That is the Malthusian trap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果出现足以抵消人口增长的技术进步，才能逐步提高人均收入，并把人口增长率控制在适度水平。否则经济可能长期停留在“低收入—高人口增长—低收入”之间的循环。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only technological progress strong enough to offset population growth can raise income per person over time while keeping population growth moderate. Otherwise, the economy may remain trapped in a cycle of low income → rapid population growth → low income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

3. **现代意义**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** **Modern Significance**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

马尔萨斯陷阱在现代发达经济体中似乎不再明显，但在一些低收入国家或历史发展阶段，确实能观察到类似特征：缺乏技术或制度变革时，收入一旦提高就被快速增长的人口所消耗。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Malthusian trap appears less evident in modern advanced economies but can still be observed in some low-income countries or at certain historical stages: when there is a lack of technological or institutional change, income increases are consumed by rapidly growing populations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. 李嘉图模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. The Ricardian Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

#考点  要会推导均衡的过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Exam point:** be able to derive the adjustment to equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1 基本假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.1 Assumptions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

整个经济只有一家代表性企业,利用资本和劳动力生产一种工业品
生产技术为规模报酬不变
市场为完全竞争
资本家只得到资本回报,工人只获得工资
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The entire economy is represented by a single representative firm that produces a single industrial good using capital and labor. Production technology exhibits constant returns to scale, and the market is perfectly competitive. The capitalist receives only a return on capital, while workers receive wages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.2 模型内容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2 Reading the wage–labour diagrams*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两张课堂图的纵轴都是工资 $w$，横轴都是劳动量 $L$。若把横轴同时读成人口，就需要假设劳动参与率固定，并选好相应单位。以下以工业品为计价物，价格归一为1；工资表示每单位劳动可以买到多少工业品。生存所需的食品篮子即使不变，食品相对工业品变贵，也可能使这一工资提高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both classroom diagrams put the wage $w$ on the vertical axis and labour $L$ on the horizontal axis. Interpreting $L$ as population also requires a fixed labour-force participation rate and appropriate units. Below, the industrial good is the numeraire, with price normalised to one, so wages measure industrial goods per unit of labour. Even an unchanged subsistence food basket may require a higher wage when food becomes more expensive relative to industrial goods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上节保留了课堂的“一种工业品”假设。下面的函数只刻画工业生产，食品部门没有单独建模：食品成本通过另行给定的维持工资关系进入。因此，这里做的是劳动市场的条件比较，不是在同一个单部门封闭经济中同时求出食品相对价格和一般均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding section retains the classroom assumption of one industrial good. The function below describes industrial production only; food production is not modelled separately. Food costs enter through a separately specified maintenance-wage relationship. This is a conditional labour-market comparison, not a general equilibrium that simultaneously determines food's relative price in a closed, one-sector economy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“生存工资”是模型中维持劳动人口的生活标准。Ricardo 本人的 [[李嘉图自然工资]] 还包括社会习惯形成的需要；不能把课堂水平线理解成所有时代都一样的生理最低值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The diagram's subsistence wage represents the living standard that maintains the labouring population within the model. [[李嘉图自然工资|Ricardo's natural wage]] also includes needs shaped by social custom; the horizontal line is not a biological minimum shared by every society and period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 无资源约束：先升工资，再看人口怎样调整
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Without a resource constraint: the wage response and population adjustment*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20250320081141.png]]

图6.2中，$DD_0$ 与水平 $LS$ 的交点 $A$ 是初始状态。资本增加后，劳动需求转到 $DD_1$：若劳动量暂时停在 $L_0$，工资先升到 $B$。只有再加上“高于维持标准的工资会逐步增加劳动人口”这一调整假设，才会沿新的需求线向 $A'$ 移动，最终劳动量增至 $L_1$、工资回到原水平。资本增加和人口调整是两个步骤，不能把它们当成同时发生。图上蓝色手绘的上升线叠加了另一种资源约束情形；本段先读印刷的水平 $LS$，上升情形见下一张图。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Figure 6.2, point $A$, where $DD_0$ intersects the horizontal $LS$, is the initial state. More capital shifts labour demand to $DD_1$. If labour initially remains at $L_0$, the wage rises to $B$. A further assumption—that wages above the maintenance standard gradually expand the labouring population—is needed for movement along the new demand curve towards $A'$, with more labour at $L_1$ and the original wage restored. Capital expansion and population adjustment are separate steps. The blue handwritten rising line superimposes a resource-constrained comparison. This passage first follows the printed horizontal $LS$; the next figure examines the rising relationship.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从同一纵轴截距发出的需求射线，是这张图采用的技术形式，并非所有企业劳动需求都必须满足。下面给它一个可以计算的实现。设竞争性代表企业在给定 $K>0$ 时使用生产函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Demand rays sharing a vertical intercept reflect this diagram's technological specification, not a universal property of labour demand. For a calculable illustration, let a competitive representative firm with given $K>0$ use the production function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
F(K,L)=10L-\frac{L^2}{K},\qquad 0\leq L\leq5K.
$$

这是另行构造的教学函数，取劳动边际产出非负的使用区间。它满足 $F(tK,tL)=tF(K,L)$，所以具有规模报酬不变。企业把市场工资视为给定，增加劳动直到边际产出等于工资：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a constructed teaching function, restricted to the range with non-negative marginal product of labour. It has constant returns to scale because $F(tK,tL)=tF(K,L)$. Taking the market wage as given, the firm hires labour until its marginal product equals the wage:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
w_D(K,L)=F_L(K,L)=10-\frac{2L}{K}=w.
$$

若人口维持工资固定为 $w_s=2$，初始 $K=1$ 时，$10-2L=2$ 给出 $L=4$。资本增至 $K=2$ 后，需求变为 $w_D=10-L$；在劳动尚为4时，市场工资为6。若资本随后保持为2，人口按上述反馈调整，最终 $10-L=2$，即 $L=8$、$w=2$。这里没有从生产函数推导人口变化：人口反馈是额外的模型假设，详见 [[工资与人口调整]]。Ricardo 也明确允许持续的资本扩张使市场工资长期高于自然工资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a fixed maintenance wage $w_s=2$, initial capital $K=1$ gives $10-2L=2$, hence $L=4$. Raising capital to $K=2$ changes demand to $w_D=10-L$. While labour remains at four, the wage is six. If capital then remains at two and population follows the stated feedback, adjustment ends at $10-L=2$: $L=8$, $w=2$. Population growth is an additional assumption, not a deduction from the production function; see [[工资与人口调整|wage–population adjustment]]. Ricardo also explicitly allows sustained capital expansion to keep market wages above natural wages for an extended period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 有资源约束：维持同一生活标准可能需要更高工资
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With a resource constraint: maintaining living standards may require a higher wage*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Pasted image 20250320082523.png]]

图6.4保留工资—劳动坐标，但加入随人口压力上升的维持工资。新的劳动需求与原水平工资线相交于 $A'$，对应 $L_1$；与上升的维持工资线相交于 $A''$，对应较小的 $L_1'$ 和较高的工资。若额外粮食只能在更不利条件下生产，且进口、技术改善没有抵消这种压力，食品相对价格可能提高。维持劳动者生活所需的工业品工资随之上升。它与 [[级差地租]] 的联系在于食物生产条件变化，而不是“地主多收租，所以工资自动变高”；因果方向见 [[地租与粮价因果]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Figure 6.4 retains wage–labour axes but adds a maintenance wage that rises with population pressure. New labour demand intersects the original horizontal wage at $A'$, with labour $L_1$, and the rising maintenance relationship at $A''$, with lower labour $L_1'$ and a higher wage. If additional food requires less favourable production conditions, without offsetting imports or technical improvements, its relative price may rise. Maintaining workers' living standards then requires a higher wage in industrial goods. The connection to [[级差地租|differential rent]] is the change in food-production conditions, not an automatic wage increase caused by landlords charging more rent; see [[地租与粮价因果|the direction of causation between rent and grain prices]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

继续用同一生产函数，只把市场层面的维持工资关系改为下式。它表示劳动量超过4以后，资源压力开始提高维持成本；这条具体折线同样是教学假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the same production function and change only the market-level maintenance-wage relationship as follows. Resource pressure raises maintenance costs once labour exceeds four. This particular kinked relationship is also a teaching assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
w_s(L)=2+\max\{0,L-4\}.
$$

在 $K=1$ 时，初始状态仍为 $L=4,w=2$。资本增至2后，上升段的均衡满足 $10-L=L-2$，所以 $L=6,w=4$。与同样 $K=2$、维持工资固定为2时相比，资源反馈使最终劳动量从8降为6，工资从2升为4。企业仍把工资当作市场价格；不能把整条人口维持关系代入一家企业的工资账单再求导，那会变成工资随自身招聘量改变的另一种模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $K=1$, the initial state remains $L=4,w=2$. After capital rises to two, equilibrium on the rising segment satisfies $10-L=L-2$, giving $L=6,w=4$. Compared with the same $K=2$ and a fixed maintenance wage of two, the resource feedback reduces final labour from eight to six and raises the wage from two to four. The firm still takes the wage as a market price. Substituting the population-wide relationship into a single firm's wage bill before differentiation would instead create a different model in which its own hiring changes its wage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 阴影的面积：先明确扣掉了哪些成本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The shaded area: identify which costs have been deducted*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

劳动需求曲线下面积等于 $F(K,L)-F(K,0)$。本例 $F(K,0)=0$，减去工资矩形 $wL$ 后，剩下 $F-wL$。在上述竞争性雇佣点，规模报酬不变又给出 $F-wL=KF_K$：它是资本收入；资本若按边际产出获得报酬，纯经济利润为0。折旧、固定费用和资本机会成本不能因为图上只画了工资就被省略，具体成本辨析可参照 [[生产者剩余与利润]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The area under labour demand equals $F(K,L)-F(K,0)$. Here $F(K,0)=0$, so subtracting the wage rectangle $wL$ leaves $F-wL$. At the competitive hiring point, constant returns gives $F-wL=KF_K$: this is capital income. If capital is paid its marginal product, pure economic profit is zero. Depreciation, fixed charges and the opportunity cost of capital do not disappear because the graph shows only wages; see [[生产者剩余与利润|the distinction between surplus and profit]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 状态 | $K$ | $L$ | $w$ | 产出 $F$ | 工资总额 $wL$ | 扣工资剩余 $F-wL$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 初始状态 | 1 | 4 | 2 | 24 | 8 | 16 |
| 资本增加，维持工资固定 | 2 | 8 | 2 | 48 | 16 | 32 |
| 资本增加，维持工资上升 | 2 | 6 | 4 | 42 | 24 | 18 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Initially, output is 24, wages total 8 and capital income is 16. At capital two, a fixed maintenance wage gives output 48, wages 16 and capital income 32; a rising maintenance wage gives output 42, wages 24 and capital income 18. Thus the latter income rises relative to the initial state but is lower than in the unconstrained comparison at the same capital stock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资源约束下的剩余从16增至18，并没有比初始状态下降；它只是低于同样资本存量、没有这项资源反馈时的32。要讨论 [[利润率与积累压力]]，还需明确资本计价、投资行为和技术变化，不能仅凭一张静态图宣布整个经济停止增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the resource constraint, the residual rises from 16 to 18 rather than falling from its initial level. It is lower than 32 in the comparison without that feedback at the same capital stock. Analysing [[利润率与积累压力|profit rates and accumulation pressure]] further requires capital valuation, investment behaviour and technical change; a static diagram alone does not establish the end of growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

父母的收入水平:父母收入水平越高,养育孩子的机会成本越大
数量-质量的权衡:随着人力资本的提高.社会更加倾向于密集地使用人力资本的技术,从而提高人力资本的回报.所以家庭对于少生孩子,增加对孩子的教育投资

Parents' Income Level: The higher the parents' income level, the greater the opportunity cost of raising children.

The Trade-off between Quantity and Quality: As human capital improves, society tends to favor technologies that intensively use human capital, thereby increasing its returns. Consequently, families may opt for fewer children and increase their investment in education for those children.

Parents' Education Level: Higher education levels signify a shift in worldview; education allows parents to see beyond the act of having children and find enjoyment in other aspects of life.

Social Security Adequacy: If having children is seen as a means to secure future social benefits such as pensions, and if social security systems are well-developed, it can reduce the incentive to have more children.

Cultural Norms and Geographical Constraints: These vary by region.
