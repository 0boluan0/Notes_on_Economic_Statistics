# 二元经济与劳动力迁移

一名农村劳动者进城，至少改变了三个问题：原来的生产少了多少，自己放弃与获得多少收入，目的地是否有岗位。三个模型抓住的是不同环节。Lewis先解释资本积累如何吸收劳动，Fei–Ranis补上农业食品与工资约束，Harris–Todaro再让获得城市工作的概率进入迁移选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rural worker moving to a city raises three distinct questions: how much production is lost at origin, what income the worker gives up and gains, and whether a destination job is available. Lewis explains labour absorption through accumulation; Fei–Ranis adds agricultural food and wage constraints; Harris–Todaro introduces the probability of urban employment into migration decisions.

## 1. 先问原部门损失什么，再问劳动者放弃什么

前一节已经把人口结构和实际就业分开；现在再把产业、地点与生产组织分开。Lewis的资本主义部门可以包括农业种植园，所以不能先把模型中的两个部门翻译成“所有农村”和“所有城市”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding chapter separated age structure from actual employment. Now distinguish industry, location and production organization. Lewis's capitalist sector can include plantations, so its two sectors cannot simply be translated as all rural and all urban activity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Lewis 二元经济模型]]

课堂从农业的总产出平台出发：在一段劳动投入范围内，移出一些劳动仍能维持产出。这里要判断实际移出的整个数量，而不只是生产函数在某一点的斜率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lecture begins with a plateau in agricultural output: some labour can be withdrawn without reducing production. Assess the entire amount withdrawn, not merely the slope at one point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[剩余劳动力]]

接着换到劳动者的视角。农业没有因此减产，不表示这个人原来没有消费，也不表示工厂可以免费雇到他。收入分享规则把生产结果转成私人选择条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now take the worker's perspective. No agricultural output loss does not mean that the person previously consumed nothing or can be hired for free. Income-sharing arrangements connect production to private choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[边际产出与退出收入]]

## 2. 工资稳定时，资本怎样吸收劳动

知道了工人原来可得的收入，才能解释招工工资。水平供给既需要有人可招，也需要收入基准、工资溢价和计价条件在这段扩张中保持适当稳定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Outside earnings help explain the recruitment wage. Horizontal supply requires available workers and suitable stability in outside income, the wage premium and valuation conditions over the expansion being considered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[无限劳动供给]]

现在沿企业这一侧往前走：先按给定资本和工资决定雇佣，再说明哪些剩余真正变成了下一期生产资本，最后重新求劳动需求。课堂右上方的原文Figure 3展示这条链；右下方另画的工资上升段加入了劳动供给条件变化，不能与原图直接拼成同一条线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Follow the firm: choose employment at the given capital stock and wage, identify surplus actually reinvested in next-period productive capital, and solve labour demand again. The lecture's upper-right original Figure 3 illustrates this chain. The separate lower-right drawing adds changing labour-supply conditions rather than extending the very same original curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[利润再投与劳动吸收]]

## 3. 劳动能移出，食品怎样跟过去

劳动力被工厂吸收后仍然需要吃饭。Fei–Ranis把这一点写进二部门分析：既看农业能释放多少劳动，也看剩下的生产和消费能否支持已经进入工业的人。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Factory workers still need food. Fei–Ranis makes this part of the two-sector analysis, examining both labour release and whether remaining agricultural production and consumption can support those already in industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[费景汉–拉尼斯模型]]

先把课堂图中的两个点找准。下面沿用固定制度食品收入，而不是上一节家庭人数改变后重新均分产出的规则；两种收入安排各自有明确用途，不能在推导中悄悄切换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First locate the two thresholds in the lecture diagram. The following model fixes institutional food income, unlike the earlier household example that redivides output among fewer members. These are different income arrangements and must not be switched during a derivation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[农业短缺点与商业化点]]

两个点之间为什么已经出现约束，要从食品账户看。农业总产出与留农者消费会一起改变，先算差额，再问工业人口每人分到多少。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the food account to understand constraints between the thresholds. Agricultural output and rural consumption change together. First calculate their difference, then divide by the number of industrial workers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[农业剩余]]

![[平均农业剩余]]

最后让相对价格重新进入。固定食品相对价格的课堂图，可以把工资水平段画到商业化点；原文考虑食品供养趋紧带来的价格反馈，工资压力可能更早出现。下面说明的是这一步传导所需的条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally reintroduce relative prices. The lecture's fixed-food-price version can keep the wage line flat until commercialization. The original analysis allows food scarcity to affect relative prices and create earlier wage pressure. The next atom states the conditions for that transmission.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[食品价格与工业工资]]

## 4. 城市有失业，为什么还会有人迁入

前面的劳动吸收路径把招工和迁移接在一起。现在允许有人进入城市却暂时没有获得高工资岗位。Harris–Todaro的核心是比较预期收入，并让迁入人数反过来影响获得岗位的概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preceding absorption mechanism connected hiring to transfer. Now allow people to enter the city without obtaining a high-wage job. Harris–Todaro compares expected income and lets the number entering the urban labour market affect employment probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Harris–Todaro模型]]

概率必须有来源。现有岗位占城市劳动力的比例，在明确的同质分配条件下可以用来算预期收入；它不自动等于某名新迁入者下个月获聘的概率。若在位者保留岗位、只有新增空缺可竞争，问题就变了。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The probability needs a generating rule. Existing jobs divided by the urban labour force can measure expected earnings under explicit homogeneous allocation assumptions. It is not automatically a new arrival's chance of being hired next month. Retained incumbent jobs and competition only for vacancies define a different problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[城市就业概率换算]]

课堂第36页还画了成本、期限与贴现。把这个决策框图缩成一期不等式时，必须说明收入是哪一期的、哪些费用只付一次，以及是否按期望货币收益评价风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lecture page 36 also includes costs, horizons and discounting. Reducing that decision framework to a one-period inequality requires consistent income periods, separation of one-time costs and an explicit treatment of risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[迁移收益现值]]

## 5. 回到双原点图，找出就业和失业各在哪里

双原点图把两部门的劳动配置放在同一条有限长度的轴上。读的时候先确认左右方向，再分别找灵活工资的充分就业配置、制度工资下的城市岗位量，最后由预期收入相等确定迁入后的城市劳动力量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The dual-origin diagram places both sectors on one finite labour-allocation axis. Establish the directions first, then locate flexible-wage full employment, urban jobs at the institutional wage, and finally the urban labour force implied by equal expected income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[迁移双原点图]]

## 6. 分别改变工资与岗位，不把两种政策混算

工资是成功获聘后的收入，岗位量影响成功概率。二者都可以改变迁移，但冲击企业工资成本和外生增加岗位不是同一个实验；农业工资和产品价格是否随配置改变，也会影响结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The wage is income conditional on employment; job numbers affect employment probability. Both can alter migration, but changing firms' wage costs and exogenously creating jobs are different experiments. Adjustment of agricultural wages and goods prices also matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[城市工资与迁移反应]]

![[岗位创造与诱致迁移]]

## 7. 从模型人数回到真实的就业统计

基础模型把未获得城市岗位的人放在零劳动收入的失业状态。用于现实数据时，这个状态需要重新识别：有人正在做非正规工作，有人没有工作但在求职，也有人没有参加劳动力市场。这里直接复用已经核验的共享定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The baseline model puts those without an urban job into unemployment with zero labour earnings. Real data require classification: some people work informally, some are jobless and searching, and others are outside the labour force. The following verified shared definitions distinguish these cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[非正规就业]]

![[失业率]]

所以，“未获正规岗位的比例”不是一个可以直接发布的失业率。也不能把正规与非正规岗位的划分直接改写成农村与城市或农业与工业。若要判断迁移怎样改变整体人均产出，还要沿[[年龄结构与人均产出|实际就业人口比与劳动生产率]]回到前两节的同一套核算，而非只看城市已就业者的工资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus the share without a formal job is not automatically a statistical unemployment rate. Job formality is also distinct from location and industry. To assess output per person, reuse the earlier accounting of [[年龄结构与人均产出|actual employment shares and labour productivity]], rather than observing only wages among employed urban workers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8. 扩展：家庭共同选择与反复迁移

一个人迁移不一定意味着全家迁移，也不一定是他单独决策。把家庭成员之间的收入共享放进来，可以解释为什么一部分人外出、另一部分人留下；这需要在个人预期收入之外增加共同安排。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An individual's move need not be a move by the whole family or a decision made alone. Income-sharing arrangements can help explain why some household members leave while others remain, adding a collective arrangement to individual expected-income comparisons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[迁移的家庭风险分担]]

收入安排是一种机制，反复往返是一种可观察的迁移形态。两者分开定义，才能检验它们在某一地区是否有关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An income-sharing arrangement is a mechanism; repeated movement is a migration pattern. Define them separately before testing their relationship in a particular setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[循环迁移]]

课堂后半段关于中国二元结构、农民工和市民化的问题，继续作为具体应用来读。分析时分别问：户籍、土地权利、住房和公共服务改变的是迁移费用、外出后的可得资源，还是家庭的返回安排？这些通道需要指定地区、时期和证据；不能单凭三个模型宣布某年全国到达转折点，也不能把市民化直接等同于岗位正规化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The later lecture questions on China's dual structure, migrant workers and urban integration remain applications. Ask whether registration, land rights, housing or services change moving costs, resources available after moving or household return arrangements. Each channel requires a location, period and evidence. The three models alone date no nationwide turning point, and urban integration is not identical to job formalization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 9. 用一组判断把整条路径接起来

先不看答案，处理下面三个互相独立的构造场景：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without opening the answer, work through three separate constructed scenarios:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 农场5人均分20单位净产出；一人离开后产出仍为20，且不再参与原产出的分配。他离开造成多少产出损失，放弃多少私人收入？
2. 在本节Fei–Ranis数例中，$N=100$、制度食品收入固定为8。农业劳动从80降到60，农业产出从800降到750。农业剩余与平均农业剩余分别怎样变？农业边际产出已正，是否就能把短缺点和商业化点合并？
3. 某城市起初有80名劳动力，其中40人在高工资岗位。随后岗位增至50，劳动力增至92。暂设其余人都符合统计失业条件。失业人数和失业率各怎样变化？只凭这两个配置，能否证明岗位政策造成了全部迁移？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
&nbsp;
**1.** Five farm members share net output of 20. One leaves without reducing output and no longer receives a share. What production is lost and what private income is forgone?<br>
**2.** In the chapter's Fei–Ranis example, total labour is 100 and institutional food income stays at 8. Agricultural labour falls from 80 to 60 and output from 800 to 750. Calculate total and average agricultural surplus. Does positive marginal product merge the shortage and commercialization points?<br>
**3.** An urban labour force of 80 contains 40 high-wage jobholders. Later, there are 50 jobs and 92 labour-force participants. Assume all others satisfy unemployment criteria. Calculate the unemployment count and rate. Do the two allocations establish that the job policy caused all migration?<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 核对思路
> 第一题产出损失为0，退出者放弃的是原有消费份额4；二者属于不同的判断。
>
> 第二题农业剩余由 $800-8\times80=160$ 增至 $750-8\times60=270$；工业劳动由20增至40，平均农业剩余由8降到6.75。总量增加不保证每人供养量增加；在本例中此时农业边际产出为5，仍低于制度收入8，所以尚未到商业化点。
>
> 第三题失业人数由40增至42，失业率却由50%降至 $42/92\approx45.65\%$。人数和率没有矛盾；但给定的配置只提供核算比较，政策因果关系仍需模型条件或识别证据。若未获高工资岗位者中有人实际上就业，还要先重分就业状态。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first case loses no output but forgoes a previous consumption share of 4. These are different margins.
>
> In the second case, TAS rises from 160 to 270 while industrial labour rises from 20 to 40, lowering AAS from 8 to 6.75. Marginal agricultural product is 5, below institutional income of 8, so commercialization has not yet occurred in this example.
>
> In the third case, unemployment rises from 40 to 42, but its rate falls from 50% to approximately 45.65%. The allocations establish an accounting comparison, not the policy's causal effect. If some people without a high-wage job are employed elsewhere, reclassify them first.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

能够按同一组问题比较三个模型——劳动为何移动、工资与概率如何决定、什么条件改变了转折或均衡——就完成本节。接下来进入[[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/00_课程总览#4. 经济增长理论：哈罗德—多马、索洛与内生增长|长期经济增长]]，把劳动重配放回资本、技术与人口共同变化的框架。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Complete the chapter by comparing all three models along the same dimensions: why labour moves, how wages and probabilities are determined, and what changes a threshold or equilibrium. Continue to [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/00_课程总览#4. 经济增长理论：哈罗德—多马、索洛与内生增长|long-run growth]] to combine reallocation with capital, technology and population change.
