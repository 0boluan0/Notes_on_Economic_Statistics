**引言 (Introduction)**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Introduction**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

2018年中美贸易战爆发以来，全球贸易格局经历了重大冲击和调整。美国前总统特朗普于2018年起对中国进口商品加征高额关税，引发双方一系列针锋相对的关税措施 。这场贸易争端不仅直接影响中美两国的经济，也通过贸易转移效应波及众多发展中国家。在全球贸易环境恶化的背景下（例如2019年全球贸易额增速转为负值 ），许多发展中国家不得不调整关税政策并采取应对策略，以提高本国经济的弹性、维持出口竞争力并积极参与全球产业链重组。本文将重点分析： (1) 中美作为贸易战双方，其关税政策演变过程及经济影响； (2) 东南亚国家（如越南、泰国、马来西亚等）在贸易战中的关税调整与承接供应链转移的情况； (3) 南美国家（如巴西、阿根廷等）在全球贸易紧张形势下的关税政策调整与经济适应措施； (4) 各这些国家为提高经济韧性、维护出口竞争力、参与全球产业链重组所采取的策略分析。报告引用权威英文文献和数据来源（WTO、IMF、World Bank、UNCTAD 等）以支撑论点，并通过典型案例和数据趋势来说明问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since the US–China trade war began in 2018, global trade has undergone major disruption and adjustment. The Trump administration imposed high tariffs on imports from China, prompting a series of retaliatory measures. The dispute affected not only the two economies directly involved but also many developing countries through trade diversion. As the global trading environment deteriorated—global trade growth, for example, turned negative in 2019—developing economies adjusted tariffs and other policies to strengthen resilience, preserve export competitiveness, and participate in the reorganization of global supply chains. This report examines: (1) the evolution and economic effects of US and Chinese tariff policy; (2) tariff adjustment and supply-chain relocation in Southeast Asian economies such as Vietnam, Thailand, and Malaysia; (3) tariff policy and economic adaptation in South American economies such as Brazil and Argentina; and (4) strategies used to strengthen resilience, maintain export competitiveness, and enter reorganized global supply chains. It draws on sources including the WTO, IMF, World Bank, and UNCTAD, together with case studies and data trends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**一、中美贸易战的关税政策演变与经济影响**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**I. Evolution of Tariff Policies in the Sino-US Trade War and Its Economic Impact**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**关税政策演变：** 2018年，美中贸易摩擦骤然升级。美国根据“301调查”结果分阶段对中国商品加征关税：2018年7月对首批价值340亿美元中国进口商品征收25%关税，8月再对160亿美元商品加征25%关税 。中国同步予以报复，对等规模的美国商品加征关税 。此后美国于2018年9月对另外2000亿美元中国商品加征10%关税（2019年提高到25%），2019年9月又对约1100亿美元商品加征15%关税，直至2020年初双方签署第一阶段贸易协议时将部分税率降至7.5% 。至此，美国对华约**5500亿美元**商品中约2/3的进口施加了额外关税，平均税率从贸易战前的3%上升到约19% 。中国则对约**1100亿美元**自美进口商品加征报复性关税，覆盖其自美进口的58%左右，平均税率从战前的8%提高到约21% 。值得注意的是，尽管2020年初双方达成“ phase one ”协议暂停进一步加税，但大部分加征关税措施一直延续至今 。拜登政府上任后基本保留了特朗普时期对华关税，迄今未大幅取消 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Evolution of Tariff Policy:** US–China trade tensions escalated sharply in 2018. Following its Section 301 investigation, the United States imposed a 25% tariff on an initial $34 billion of Chinese imports in July and on another $16 billion in August. China retaliated against an equivalent value of US goods. In September 2018, the United States imposed a 10% tariff on a further $200 billion of Chinese goods, later raising the rate to 25%; in September 2019, it imposed a 15% tariff on roughly another $110 billion. When the Phase One agreement was signed in early 2020, some of the latter tariffs were reduced to 7.5%. By then, additional US tariffs covered roughly two-thirds of the approximately **$550 billion** in annual imports from China, and the average tariff had risen from about 3% before the dispute to about 19%. China imposed retaliatory tariffs on approximately **$110 billion** of US imports, covering around 58% of the total, and raised its average tariff on US goods from about 8% to about 21%. Although the Phase One agreement halted further escalation, most of the additional tariffs remained in place, and the Biden administration retained them with few substantial reductions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**主要经济影响：** 美中关税博弈对两国经济均造成显著冲击。研究表明，这些关税成本主要由进口国消费者和企业承担，导致中美两国实际收入水平均有所下降 。美国方面，加征关税推高了进口商品价格。据测算，美国对华关税使美国家庭每年平均增加约几百美元开支，并造成美国GDP增速略有下降 。美国农产品出口首当其冲：中国针对大豆、猪肉等美国农产品加征报复关税，使美国对华农产品出口锐减。为弥补农民损失，美国政府在2018-2019年动用资金向农户提供了累计约**280亿美元**补贴救助 。中国方面，对美出口受关税影响增速放缓，2019年中国对美出口额出现明显下滑，与此相伴人民币兑美元汇率一度贬值（约3%-5%，部分抵消关税影响）。中国政府也采取了一系列稳外贸措施，包括提高部分出口产品的退税率以减轻企业负担 。例如2018年11月起中国将大部分出口产品增值税退税率提高1-3个百分点（钢铁、电子等397项产品退税率提高至16%等），以支持出口企业应对美国关税 。此外，中国还主动下调了面向其他贸易伙伴的关税总体水平——中国自世界其他国家进口的平均关税税率已从2018年初的8.0%降至2022年的6.5% ——以扩大替代市场的进口来源并稳定国内物价。总体而言，中美互征关税在降低双边贸易的同时，也削弱了两国的经济增长动力，逼使双方调整内部政策予以缓冲。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Main Economic Effects:** The tariffs imposed substantial costs on both economies. Evidence cited in the note indicates that consumers and firms in the importing country bore much of the burden, reducing real income in both the United States and China. In the United States, higher import prices added several hundred dollars a year to the typical household's expenses and slightly reduced GDP growth. US agricultural exports were hit especially hard: China's retaliatory tariffs on products such as soybeans and pork sharply reduced sales, prompting the US government to provide farmers with about **$28 billion** in support during 2018–2019. China's exports to the United States also slowed markedly in 2019. A 3–5% depreciation of the renminbi partly offset the tariff shock, while the Chinese government raised VAT export rebates—by one to three percentage points for many products from November 2018—to reduce exporters' costs. China also cut its average tariff on imports from other trading partners from 8.0% in early 2018 to 6.5% in 2022, broadening alternative sources of supply and helping to stabilize domestic prices. Overall, reciprocal tariffs reduced bilateral trade, weakened growth, and forced both governments to cushion the losses through domestic policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**双边贸易与全球影响：** 关税壁垒直接导致中美双边贸易大幅缩减，美国自中国进口自2018年后持续低于此前趋势水平 。到2022年，中国占美国进口总额的比重已从贸易战前的22%降至约18% 。相应地，美国从包括东南亚在内的其他国家进口显著增加，目前自中国以外地区的进口额比贸易战前增长了逾30% 。中国亦调整了贸易方向，加大对欧盟、东盟等市场出口力度；例如到2020年代前期，东盟取代美国成为中国第一大出口市场 。国际组织分析指出，美中关税冲突的直接效果是扭曲贸易，双边贸易萎缩而第三国贸易填补空缺 。联合国贸发会议（UNCTAD）的研究估计，美国对华加征关税影响的中国出口中，有约**82%**将由其他国家的企业取代，仅有12%由中国国内消化，美国本土厂商仅能增加约6%的供应 。类似地，中国报复关税所减少的美国对华出口中，约85%会被其他国家抢占市场份额 。因此，这场贸易战除了令双方两败俱伤，也为第三方国家创造了出口机会。然而，从全球视角看，贸易紧张局势给全球经济带来更多不确定性和负面影响，包括贸易增长放缓、投资观望情绪上升以及产业链调整的短期阵痛 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Bilateral Trade and Global Effects:** Tariff barriers sharply reduced trade between the United States and China. US imports from China have remained below their pre-war trend since 2018, and China's share of total US imports fell from about 22% before the dispute to roughly 18% in 2022. US imports from other economies, including Southeast Asian countries, increased correspondingly, while China redirected exports toward the EU and ASEAN. By the early 2020s, ASEAN had overtaken the United States as China's largest export market. UNCTAD estimates cited here suggest that third-country firms would replace about **82%** of the Chinese exports displaced by US tariffs; roughly 12% would be absorbed within China and only about 6% replaced by US production. Likewise, other countries would capture about 85% of the US exports displaced by China's retaliatory tariffs. The dispute therefore created opportunities for third countries, but globally it also slowed trade, increased uncertainty, discouraged investment, and imposed adjustment costs as supply chains moved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**二、东南亚国家的关税调整与供应链转移承接**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**II. Tariff Adjustment and Supply-Chain Relocation in Southeast Asia**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**贸易转移与出口激增：** 中美互征关税后，东南亚国家，尤其是越南、泰国、马来西亚等，与美国和中国的贸易迅速升温。这些国家由于成本相对低廉、产业基础完善，成为跨国公司转移产能以规避关税的首选地，明显受益于贸易转移效应 。研究显示，越南、泰国等国在那些美中相互加征关税的产品领域出口增长突出，堪称贸易战中的主要“赢家”之一 。越南是最典型的案例：凭借与中国相似的制造业结构和积极的开放政策，越南成功承接了大量面向美国市场的生产订单。**越南对美出口**自2018年以来呈现跳跃式增长——美国从越南的商品进口额在2019年前9个月同比激增35% 。大量制造业产能（例如电子组装）由中国转移至越南：三星于2019年关闭了在中国的最后一家手机工厂，将生产线迁至越南和印度，带动美国进口的手机中“越南制造”份额从贸易战前的不到10%飙升至22% 。LG电子也在2019年将智能手机组装从韩国转移至越南 。甚至一些美国品牌的供应链亦调整布局，例如任天堂将部分Switch游戏机的生产从中国移至越南，微软的Xbox游戏机也增加了越南产量 。这些转移使越南对美出口实现高速攀升：截至2024年，美国自越南进口额高达**1366亿美元**，比上一年增长19.3% 。美国对越货物贸易逆差亦扩大至**1235亿美元** ，远高于2018年的水平，凸显越南在供应美国产业链中的地位迅速上升。除了越南，**泰国、马来西亚**等国也在特定行业受益：例如泰国和马来西亚在电子、电器等对美出口中取得增长，被视为中国部分出口的替代来源 。有分析指明，越南、泰国、马来西亚、韩国、墨西哥等少数国家抓住了美中贸易紧张所带来的出口机遇，其受关税影响的产品对全球市场的出口增速反而快于未受关税影响的产品 。由此可见，东南亚国家在贸易战中整体上获得了新的市场空间和订单。

**Trade Diversion and Export Growth:**

After the United States and China imposed tariffs on one another, trade between both powers and Southeast Asian economies—especially Vietnam, Thailand, and Malaysia—grew rapidly. Relatively low costs and established industrial bases made these countries attractive locations for multinationals seeking to relocate production and avoid tariffs. Exports grew particularly quickly in products covered by the bilateral tariffs.

Vietnam is the clearest example. Its manufacturing structure resembles China's, and its open policies enabled it to absorb orders aimed at the US market. US goods imports from Vietnam rose by 35% year on year in the first nine months of 2019. Electronics assembly also moved: Samsung closed its last Chinese mobile-phone factory in 2019 and shifted production to Vietnam and India; LG moved smartphone assembly from South Korea to Vietnam; Nintendo relocated part of its Switch production; and Microsoft increased Vietnamese production of Xbox consoles. By 2024, US imports from Vietnam reached **$136.6 billion**, 19.3% above the previous year, while the US goods deficit with Vietnam reached **$123.5 billion**. Thailand and Malaysia also gained export share in electronics and electrical goods. Analyses cited in the note identify Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, South Korea, and Mexico among the economies whose tariff-affected exports grew faster than their unaffected exports.

**关税与政策调整：** 面对突如其来的需求转移，东南亚各国纷纷调整贸易政策和产业策略以充分承接机会。首先，在关税政策上，大多数东盟国家**未**跟随中美提高关税，反而继续推动贸易自由化，保持对外资和贸易的开放态势。这种“不选边站”的做法使其避免卷入大国纠纷，同时成为中美双方都乐于合作的替代生产基地 。例如，**越南**积极利用多边和区域贸易协定来巩固出口市场：2019年加入《全面与进步跨太平洋伙伴关系协定（CPTPP）》，2020年与欧盟签署自由贸易协定（EVFTA），并与东盟其他成员及中国、日本等于2020年共同签署了RCEP区域全面经济伙伴关系协定。通过这些协定，越南等国大幅降低了与主要贸易伙伴之间的关税壁垒（RCEP生效后区域内92%的商品关税将逐步削减 ），为吸引产业链转移创造了有利条件。**马来西亚、泰国**等国也采取类似路径：马来西亚于2022年正式批准CPTPP，泰国加快与欧盟的自贸谈判并推动“东部经济走廊”等投资便利化计划，以提高对国际企业的吸引力。其次，这些国家注重优化国内营商环境和产业配套。越南政府专门成立贸易战应对指导委员会，在税收、土地、劳工等方面出台优惠政策，鼓励海外投资者设厂 。同时，越南意识到过快出口增长也带来挑战，例如部分中国货物借道越南转口以规避美国关税。对此，越南海关部门自2019年起**严查产地证造假和转运**行为，对涉嫌将“中国制造”伪装为“越南制造”再出口的企业加大处罚力度 。这既是为了履行国际贸易规则、维护越南商品声誉，也是避免因**“借道套利”**而引发美国新的贸易制裁（美国曾警告不允许中国商品通过第三国绕过关税）。越南政府的行动表明其在享受贸易红利的同时，亦谨慎地调整监管措施以维护长期出口竞争力。总体而言，东南亚国家通过坚持开放、加入自贸协定、改善投资环境和加强贸易监管，成功在中美贸易战中承接了部分全球供应链转移，实现出口和产业发展的“双赢”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Tariffs and Policy Adjustment:** Most ASEAN economies did not follow the United States and China in raising tariffs. They continued liberalizing trade and remained open to foreign investment, avoiding formal alignment while becoming alternative production bases for both sides. Vietnam used regional agreements to secure export markets: it joined the CPTPP in 2019, signed the EVFTA with the European Union in 2020, and joined the RCEP agreement signed in 2020. The note states that tariffs on 92% of goods traded within RCEP will be reduced gradually. Malaysia ratified the CPTPP in 2022, while Thailand pursued an EU trade agreement and investment programmes such as the Eastern Economic Corridor.

These economies also improved domestic business conditions and supplier networks. Vietnam created a committee to coordinate its response to the trade war and offered incentives involving tax, land, and labour. Rapid export growth nevertheless created a risk that Chinese goods would be routed through Vietnam to evade US tariffs. From 2019 onward, Vietnamese customs therefore tightened checks on fraudulent certificates of origin and transshipment and increased penalties for firms relabelling “Made in China” goods as “Made in Vietnam.” The aim was to comply with trade rules, protect the reputation of Vietnamese exports, and avoid new US sanctions for tariff circumvention. Openness, trade agreements, a better investment climate, and stronger enforcement together allowed Southeast Asian economies to absorb part of the supply-chain shift while preserving longer-run competitiveness.

**三、南美国家的关税政策调整与经济适应措施**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**III. Tariff Policy Adjustments and Economic Adaptation Measures in South American Countries**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**贸易战带来的机遇与挑战：** 中美贸易战对南美洲经济体的影响主要体现在大宗商品贸易和区域贸易政策上。**巴西**和**阿根廷**作为农业和矿产出口大国，在美中互征关税后意外获得了扩大对华出口的良机。典型案例是**大豆贸易**：中国作为全球最大的大豆买家，过去大量进口美国大豆用于榨油和饲料。2018年中方对美国大豆加征25%以上报复性关税（对美国大豆总关税达30-35% ），美国对华大豆出口骤减。为了满足国内需求，中国迅速转向巴西、阿根廷采购大豆。结果，巴西在2018-2019年间一跃成为中国**最大的**大豆供应国：2018年下半年巴西对华大豆出口激增，以弥补美国缺口 。2019年仅7月份，中国自阿根廷进口的大豆同比飙升269%，达到107万吨 ；当月阿根廷跃居中国第二大大豆供应国，而美国仅位列第三 。中国政府还破例与阿根廷达成协议，**首次允许进口阿根廷豆粕（豆粉）**作为饲料，这在此前多年是未有的举措 。这一系列变化表明，贸易战使南美农产品在中国市场的占有率显著上升，部分取代了美国份额 。对此，南美国家的农业部门总体受益：价格上涨推动农产品出口额提升，出口市场更多元化。然而，这种机遇也伴随挑战。例如UNCTAD指出，由于不确定贸易战能持续多久，巴西农户对扩大种植和投资依然持谨慎态度——担心一旦美中和解，价格优势将消失而导致产能过剩 。同时，中国旺盛需求推高了南美本地大豆价格，反而令巴西国内以大豆为原料的下游行业（如畜牧饲料业）成本上升，削弱其竞争力 。因此，南美国家在享受出口增长的同时，也感受到内部产业结构调整的压力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Opportunities and Challenges Arising from the Trade War:**

The impact of the Sino-US trade war on South American economies is primarily felt in commodity trade and regional trade policies. **Brazil** and **Argentina**, as major exporters of agricultural and mineral products, unexpectedly gained an opportunity to expand their exports to China after both countries imposed tariffs on each other. A notable example is the **soybean trade**: China, the world's largest buyer of soybeans, had been importing large quantities of US soybeans for oil production and animal feed. In 2018, China imposed a retaliatory tariff of over 25% on US soybeans (bringing the total tariff rate on US soybeans to 30–35%), leading to a significant reduction in US soybean exports to China. To meet domestic demand, China quickly turned to Brazil and Argentina for soybeans. As a result, Brazil became China's **largest** supplier of soybeans during 2018-2019: Soybean exports from Brazil surged in the second half of 2018 to compensate for the shortfall caused by the reduced US shipments.In July alone of 2019, China's imports of soybeans from Argentina surged by 269% year-over-year to 1.07 million tonnes; this made Argentina China’s second-largest soybean supplier that month and pushed the United States into third place. The Chinese government also broke precedent by reaching an agreement with Argentina for the first time allowing the import of soybean meal (soy flour) as feed. This was a measure previously unprecedented. These developments indicate that the South American share of the Chinese market in agricultural products has significantly increased due to the trade war, at the expense of U.S. share. For the agricultural sector in South American countries, this presents both opportunities and challenges. For instance, according to UNCTAD, Brazilian farmers remain cautious about expanding their cultivation and investing, given the uncertainty over how long the trade war will last – they fear that once a resolution is reached between the U.S. and China, the price advantage could disappear, leading to overproduction.Meanwhile, China’s robust demand has pushed up local soybean prices in South America, thereby increasing costs for downstream industries that rely on soybeans (such as livestock feed) in Brazil, and weakening their competitiveness. As a result, while South American countries benefit from export growth, they also feel pressure to adjust their internal industrial structures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**关税政策调整：** 为应对复杂的外部环境，南美主要经济体在贸易政策上做出了一定调整，既包括开放也包括保护的举措。**巴西**近年在Mercosur（南方共同市场）框架内推动降低对外关税，以融入全球供应链、提高本国产品竞争力。2021年，巴西与阿根廷达成原则共识，同意将南共市对非成员国的**共同对外关税**整体下调10% 。这意味着多数进口商品关税税率降低，旨在“提高竞争力”并促进与世界其他地区的贸易 。随后巴西还数次单方面暂时下调进口关税（覆盖近九成商品）以对抗国内通胀并推动经济开放 。这些举措反映出巴西试图通过**关税降级**来缓解贸易战及疫情等外部冲击导致的物价压力，同时吸引更多进口投入品和技术，支持本国产业升级。相比之下，**阿根廷**受限于国内经济困境（高通胀、外汇短缺），在贸易政策上相对保守，但也参与了南共市关税下调的讨论。同样在2021年，阿根廷与巴西联合声明支持尽快批准南共市下调关税的提案，以体现区域内部一致对外开放的姿态 。然而阿根廷国内也采取了一些保护措施，例如对进口商品实行许可证审批、外汇管制等，来减缓贸易收支失衡和汇率贬值的压力。这表明南美国家在关税政策上力图寻求平衡：一方面适度降低关税壁垒融入全球市场、拓展出口（如努力推进与欧盟的自贸协定谈判，在2019年达成框架协议 ），另一方面针对国内脆弱产业和经济稳定性实施一定保护以渡过难关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Adjustments to Tariff Policies:** In response to a complex external environment, major economies in South America have made certain adjustments to their trade policies, including both liberalizing and protecting measures. **Brazil** has recently pushed for reductions in tariffs within the Mercosur (Southern Common Market) framework to integrate into global supply chains and enhance the competitiveness of its own products. In 2021, Brazil and Argentina reached a principle agreement to collectively reduce the common external tariff for non-Mercosur countries by 10%. This means that the tariff rates on most imported goods will decrease, aimed at "enhancing competitiveness" and promoting trade with other regions around the world. Subsequently, Brazil has repeatedly unilaterally temporarily lowered import tariffs (covering nearly nine-tenths of goods) to combat domestic inflation and promote economic openness.These measures reflect Brazil’s efforts to mitigate price pressures stemming from trade wars and the pandemic through tariff reductions, while also attracting more imported inputs and technologies to support domestic industrial upgrading. In contrast, Argentina, constrained by its domestic economic challenges (high inflation, foreign exchange shortages), has been relatively more cautious in its trade policies. However, it has also participated in discussions regarding South American Common Market (MERCOSUR) tariff reductions. In 2021, Argentina, along with Brazil, issued a joint statement supporting the swift approval of MERCOSUR’s proposal for tariff reductions, aiming to showcase a unified stance of openness within the region. Nevertheless, Argentina has also implemented some protectionist measures domestically, such as requiring import licenses and implementing foreign exchange controls, to alleviate imbalances in trade and pressure on the currency.This indicates that South American countries are striving for a balance in their tariff policies: on one hand, moderately lowering trade barriers to integrate into the global market and expand exports (such as efforts to advance free trade agreement negotiations with the EU, which reached a framework agreement in 2019); on the other hand, implementing some protection for vulnerable domestic industries and economic stability to weather the storm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**经济适应与区域合作：** 面对国际贸易环境的不确定性，南美国家亦在采取更广泛的经济应对策略来增强韧性。首先是**深化对华经贸合作**：中国在贸易战期间加大了从巴西、阿根廷等进口，不仅农产品，还有矿产资源（如铁矿石等）。巴西顺势扩大对华出口的商品种类，与中国探讨推进本币结算、吸引中国投资基础设施等合作，从而巩固对华贸易关系。这在一定程度上弥补了南美因美欧需求放缓而受到的影响。其次，**区域一体化**被寄予厚望以提高共同抵御外部冲击的能力。Mercosur内部除了关税协调，还探讨加强区域供应链分工，推动成员国间产业互补，以减少对单一出口市场或商品的过度依赖。再次，各国政府采取财政和产业政策支持受贸易战波及的行业。例如阿根廷为鼓励本国农业增产出口，在汇率上给予农户优惠汇率结算（所谓“农业美元”政策）以激励其增加对华出口创汇；巴西则在钢铝等金属行业与美国谈判达成配额协议，以确保部分产能仍可对美出口（美国2018年对全球钢铝加税时，巴西通过谈判获得配额豁免，从而维持了对美出口份额 ）。这些措施体现出南美国家为适应外部贸易变化所作的灵活调整：既积极拓展新市场、参与多边机制，也在必要时运用双边谈判和国内政策来保护自身利益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Economic Adaptation and Regional Cooperation:** In the face of the uncertainty of international trade environments, South American countries have also been adopting broader economic response strategies to enhance their resilience. First, **deepening economic and trade cooperation with China** has been prioritized: During the trade war, China increased imports from Brazil, Argentina, among others, not just for agricultural products but also for mineral resources (such as iron ore). Brazil expanded the range of goods it exports to China, engaging in discussions on renminbi settlement and attracting Chinese investment in infrastructure, thereby strengthening its trade relations with China. This helps mitigate some of the impact that South America experienced due to a slowdown in demand from the US and Europe. Secondly, **regional integration** is seen as a key strategy to improve their collective ability to withstand external shocks. Beyond tariff coordination within Mercosur, there is an exploration of enhancing regional supply chain cooperation, promoting complementary industries among member states, and reducing reliance on a single export market or commodity.Again, governments of various countries have implemented fiscal and industrial policies to support industries affected by trade wars. For instance, Argentina has encouraged its agricultural sector to increase production and exports by offering farmers preferential exchange rates for settlement (referred to as the "agricultural dollar" policy) to incentivize them in exporting to China and generating foreign exchange; Brazil negotiated quotas with the US in the steel and aluminum industry to ensure that some capacity could still export to the US (in 2018 when the US imposed tariffs on global steel and aluminum, Brazil was able to secure an exemption from quotas, thereby maintaining its share of exports to the US). These measures demonstrate how South American countries have made flexible adjustments to adapt to external trade changes: they actively seek new markets and participate in multilateral mechanisms, while also using bilateral negotiations and domestic policies to protect their interests when necessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**四、提高经济弹性和维护出口竞争力的策略分析**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**IV. Strategies for Strengthening Economic Resilience and Preserving Export Competitiveness**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

综上所述，面对中美贸易战引发的外部冲击和全球产业链重组浪潮，中国、东南亚、南美等发展中经济体各自采取了多层面的策略来增强经济韧性、保持贸易竞争力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In summary, facing external shocks from the Sino-US trade war and the global reconfiguration of supply chains, developing economies such as China, Southeast Asia, and South America have adopted multifaceted strategies to enhance economic resilience and maintain trade competitiveness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**（1）市场多元化与贸易重组：** 许多国家选择通过市场多元化来降低对单一市场的依赖，从而增强抗风险能力。中国在美国加征关税后加快“东向”“南向”贸易布局，加强与欧盟、东盟、“一带一路”沿线国家的经贸往来。例如2020年签署RCEP涵盖15国的超大型自贸区，促进区域内关税削减和贸易便利化，以开拓区域内市场需求 。东盟国家则利用自身独特地位，同时扩大对美出口和对华进口，在全球供应链中扮演“中间站”和替代供应者的角色 。南美国家也努力开辟亚洲市场、深化南南合作，以减少对美欧市场的过度依赖。这种贸易伙伴的多元化有效缓冲了某一市场需求骤降时对本国经济的冲击。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**(1) Market Diversification and Trade Reorientation:** Many countries reduced their dependence on a single destination by diversifying export markets. After the United States raised tariffs, China expanded trade with the EU, ASEAN, and Belt and Road partners. The fifteen-country RCEP agreement, signed in 2020, supports this strategy by reducing regional tariffs and facilitating trade. ASEAN economies occupy an intermediary position: they can export more to the United States while importing more inputs from China, serving both as alternative suppliers and as links within reorganized supply chains. South American countries have likewise sought Asian markets and deeper South–South cooperation. A wider set of trading partners cushions the domestic economy when demand in any one market falls abruptly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**（2）参与自由贸易协定与降低关税壁垒：** 加入或推动自由贸易协定(FTA)是发展中国家提高出口竞争力的重要战略。CPTPP、RCEP等协定的签署使东亚、拉美的许多发展中经济体享受到了关税减让和明确的贸易规则框架，从而提高了出口产品的价格竞争力和进入主要市场的准入保障。例如越南在加入CPTPP和EVFTA后，对成员国的出口关税大幅下降，纺织服装、水产品等行业出口激增，即使在中美摩擦背景下仍实现对欧盟、加拿大等市场的拓展。又如Mercosur与欧盟的自贸协议一旦生效，阿根廷、巴西的工业和农产品将以更低关税进入欧盟市场，这有助于长远提升其出口多元性和稳定性。与此同时，一些国家主动单边**降低进口关税**，以融入全球价值链、获得更廉价的中间品投入。正如巴西所做的，共同外税的下调不仅利于本国消费者，也帮助本国产品获得更具竞争力的零部件与原材料，从而在出口市场上更具优势 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**(2) Free-Trade Agreements and Lower Tariff Barriers:** Joining or promoting free-trade agreements can improve both price competitiveness and security of market access. Agreements such as CPTPP and RCEP give many developing economies in East Asia and Latin America lower tariffs and clearer trade rules. After joining CPTPP and the EU–Vietnam FTA, for example, Vietnam expanded textile, garment, and seafood exports to the EU, Canada, and other markets despite US–China tensions. If the EU–Mercosur agreement enters into force, Argentine and Brazilian industrial and agricultural products will likewise enter the EU at lower tariffs. Some countries have also cut **import tariffs** unilaterally to obtain cheaper intermediate inputs and integrate into global value chains. Brazil's reduction of the Mercosur common external tariff benefits consumers while lowering the cost of parts and raw materials used by exporters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**（3）吸引投资与本地产业升级：** 为承接全球产业链的重组浪潮，发展中国家纷纷改善投资环境、推动产业升级。越南、泰国等通过完善基础设施、提供税收优惠和产业园区，成功吸引电子、机械等制造业投资迁入，带动本地配套产业发展，提升了在全球价值链中的地位。马来西亚、墨西哥等则注重发展本地供应链能力，与跨国公司合作提升技术水平，以巩固长久竞争力。产业升级提高了这些经济体的**经济弹性**：当外部环境变化时，它们能够迅速调整生产结构，抓住新的需求（如全球对电子产品或医疗物资的需求）来保持出口。相较之下，缺乏多元产业基础的国家在贸易战中受益有限，甚至因过度依赖单一商品（如部分非洲国家依赖初级产品）而难以应对价格和需求的剧烈波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**(3) Attracting Investment and Upgrading Domestic Industry:** Developing economies have improved infrastructure, offered tax incentives, and built industrial parks to capture production displaced by supply-chain restructuring. Vietnam and Thailand attracted investment in electronics and machinery, which in turn supported local suppliers and moved them further up global value chains. Malaysia and Mexico have emphasized domestic supply-chain capability and technological cooperation with multinational firms. This upgrading increases **economic resilience** because firms can adapt production to new demand, such as demand for electronics or medical supplies. Economies with a narrow industrial base benefit less and remain more exposed to abrupt changes in commodity prices and demand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**（4）财政金融支持与社会保障：** 提高经济韧性还需要内部政策的配合。当贸易冲击导致部分行业和就业受到影响时，政府的财政金融手段可以提供缓冲。例如中美贸易战期间美国和中国都对受损行业给予了一定财政补贴（如美国对农民的直接补贴、中国对出口企业增加退税等 ），东南亚一些国家也为中小企业提供贷款优惠或出口信贷，南美国家则通过汇率管理、降低利率等方式帮助出口企业维持竞争力。这些措施在短期内稳住了相关产业，避免大规模失业和产能流失，为经济结构调整争取了时间。不过需要注意，长期的补贴并非长久之计，提高自身竞争力才是根本。因此多数国家将补贴与改革并行推进，在给予临时性扶持的同时，加快培育新兴产业、提升生产率，以实现可持续的发展。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**(4) Fiscal and Financial Support and Social Protection:** Domestic policy can cushion industries and workers affected by trade shocks. During the US–China dispute, the United States subsidized farmers and China increased tax rebates for exporters; some Southeast Asian governments offered concessional loans or export credit to smaller firms, while South American governments used exchange-rate management and lower interest rates to support exporters. These measures can prevent a short-run shock from destroying productive capacity or causing mass unemployment, buying time for adjustment. Because permanent subsidies are unsustainable, however, temporary support must be paired with reform, productivity growth, and the development of new industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**结论**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Conclusion**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

自2018年以来的中美贸易战给全球贸易体系带来了深远影响，发展中国家在这一过程中既面临挑战也获得机遇。中国和美国作为直接参与者，各自的关税策略演变表明贸易战很难有真正的赢家：双方经济均受到负面冲击，不得不动用内部政策手段消化损失。与此同时，东南亚和南美等地区的一些发展中国家通过明智的策略提高了本国经济的弹性。东南亚国家依靠开放与合作，成功吸纳了部分从中国转移出的制造业订单，出口实现快速增长，但也注意监管以防范贸易套利等问题。南美国家利用自身资源禀赋，趁机扩大对华出口，并尝试通过区域合作和关税调整来增强应对外部冲击的能力。总体来看，这些国家采取的提高经济韧性和维护出口竞争力的措施包括：主动融入新的贸易网络、签署自由贸易协定、优化本国营商环境、支持受影响产业转型等。在全球产业链重组加速的背景下，能否抓住机遇、妥善应对挑战，将决定发展中国家在未来全球贸易版图中的地位。权威分析认为，美中贸易冲突从短期看为部分第三国带来出口红利，但从长期看，贸易保护主义和全球经济不确定性对所有国家都是隐患 。因此，维护多边贸易体系的稳定和开放、加强南南合作与政策协调，对于发展中国家共享全球化机遇、抵御外部冲击至关重要。在后贸易战时代，唯有坚持开放包容的发展战略并提高自身竞争力，发展中国家才能在风云变幻的国际经贸环境中立于不败之地。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The US–China trade war has reshaped the global trading system since 2018, creating both challenges and opportunities for developing economies. The experience of the two principal participants shows why a tariff war has no clear winner: both suffered economic losses and used domestic policy to absorb part of the shock. Some Southeast Asian economies nevertheless captured manufacturing orders displaced from China and achieved rapid export growth, while strengthening enforcement against tariff circumvention. South American countries used their resource endowments to expand exports to China and pursued regional cooperation and tariff adjustment to reduce vulnerability. Across these cases, resilience depended on joining new trade networks, concluding free-trade agreements, improving the domestic business environment, and helping affected industries adapt. Third countries may enjoy short-term export gains, but protectionism and persistent uncertainty remain long-term risks for everyone. Stable multilateral rules, openness, South–South cooperation, and stronger domestic productivity are therefore central to developing economies' ability to withstand future shocks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Trump administration publicly described its method for calculating reciprocal tariffs using the following basic formula:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$r=\frac{D}{I}\div2$$

其中$D$代表与某国的贸易逆差，$I$代表该国商品的进口额。举例来说，假设美国对中国的贸易逆差为$295$亿美元，而美国从中国的进口额为$439$亿美元，则原先计算得到的关税目标约为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here, $D$ is the US trade deficit with a country and $I$ is the value of US goods imports from that country. If the deficit with China were $29.5$ billion and imports from China were $43.9$ billion, for example, the formula would give a tariff target of approximately
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$r=\frac{295}{439}\div2\approx33.5\%$

# 2016中美贸易战的过程梳理和每一步政策分析.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Chronology of the US–China Trade Conflict Since 2016*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**2016–2025年中美关税战时间线及分析**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Timeline and Analysis of the U.S.-China Tariff Wars from 2016 to 2025**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**2017–2018年初：贸易战酝酿与初步交锋**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**2017-2018: Trade War Brewing and Initial Rounds of Conflict**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2017年8月**：美国以“中国不公平的知识产权和技术转让做法”为由，启动《1974年贸易法》第301条调查 。特朗普政府声称每年因中方侵权使美国损失高达6000亿美元。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **August 2017:** The United States opened an investigation under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, citing what it described as unfair Chinese intellectual-property and technology-transfer practices. The Trump administration claimed that infringement cost the United States as much as $600 billion a year.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2018年3月**：美国以国家安全为由，对进口钢铁和铝分别加征25%和10%的关税（“232措施”），中国未获豁免 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **March 2018**: The United States imposed a 25% tariff on imported steel and a 10% tariff on aluminum (the "232 measures"), with China not being exempted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2018年4月初**：中国针对232关税实施**等额反制**：对约30亿美元美国商品加征关税，包括水果、坚果、葡萄酒、无缝钢管等加征15%，猪肉和废铝等加征25% 。此举是中方表明不惜反击的姿态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Early April 2018:** China responded to the Section 232 tariffs with measures on about $3 billion of US goods: 15% tariffs on products including fruit, nuts, wine, and seamless steel pipe, and 25% tariffs on pork and aluminium scrap. The response signalled China's willingness to retaliate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2018年4月4日**：美贸易代表公布拟对中国500亿美元商品加征25%关税清单，涵盖航空航天、机械、医疗等高科技行业。**次日**中国立即公布等额500亿美元报复清单，瞄准美国飞机、汽车、大豆、化工品等 。这标志着双方贸易摩擦升级为针锋相对的关税对峙。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **April 4, 2018:** The US Trade Representative proposed a 25% tariff on $50 billion of Chinese goods, including aerospace, machinery, and medical products. **The following day**, China announced a counter-list covering an equivalent value of US aircraft, automobiles, soybeans, chemicals, and other goods. The exchange turned the dispute into a direct tariff confrontation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

*分析：*特朗普上任后迅速兑现竞选承诺，剑指中美巨额贸易逆差和“中国制造2025”等产业政策。2017年的301调查为后续关税战奠定法律基础，美方意在迫使中国停止“侵犯知识产权”和过剩产能倾销等行为。中国则一开始采取小规模报复（30亿美元商品）表示抗议，同时寻求通过对美农产品施压来引起美国国内游说 。这一阶段双方试探性交锋，显示出美方“以攻为守”和中方“奉陪到底”的基本立场。经济层面，美国股市在关税威胁下波动，而中国人民币在此阶段仍被官方维持稳定，没有立即通过贬值应对 。不过中美紧张气氛已令企业开始担忧供应链安全和成本上升。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Analysis:* Upon taking office, Trump swiftly fulfilled his campaign promises, targeting massive U.S.-China trade deficits and the "Made in China 2025" industrial policy. The 301 investigation in 2017 laid the legal groundwork for subsequent tariff wars. The U.S. aimed to compel China to stop what it perceived as "infringing on intellectual property rights" and "overcapacity dumping." Initially, China responded with a small-scale countermeasure ($3 billion worth of goods), protesting while seeking leverage through pressure on American agricultural products to influence domestic lobbying efforts. During this phase, both sides engaged in probing exchanges, reflecting the U.S.'s "offense as defense" stance and China's "stand firm" position. Economically, U.S. stock markets fluctuated under the threat of tariffs, whereas the Chinese yuan remained stable during this period, without immediate devaluation to address the situation. However, the heightened tensions between the two countries have already begun to concern businesses about supply chain security and rising costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**2018年中期：关税战正式爆发（第一轮加税）**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Mid-2018: The Tariff War Begins (First Round of Tariff Increases)**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2018年7月6日**：美国对第一批340亿美元中国商品开征25%关税（301调查“清单1”），重点针对高端制造领域产品。**同日**中国对等报复，对同等规模的美国商品（主要是农产品、汽车、水产品等545项）加征25%关税 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **July 6, 2018**: The United States imposed a 25% tariff on the first batch of $34 billion worth of Chinese goods (items from the "List 1" of the 301 investigation), targeting high-tech manufacturing products. **On the same day**, China retaliated in kind by imposing a 25% tariff on an equivalent amount of US goods (mainly agricultural products, automobiles, and seafood, totaling 545 items).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2018年8月23日**：美国对第二批160亿美元中国商品加征25%关税（“清单2”）生效，中国同步对等加征关税，涉及化工品、能源产品等同等金额的美国产品 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **August 23, 2018:** The United States imposed a 25% tariff on a second $16 billion batch of Chinese goods (List 2). China simultaneously imposed a 25% tariff on an equivalent value of US products, including chemicals and energy goods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2018年9月24日**：美国对第**三**轮2000亿美元中国商品加征10%关税（“清单3”），商品范围大幅扩展至消费电子、家具等约5700项商品，并原定2019年1月起税率提高到25% 。中国则对600亿美元美国商品加征5%或10%不等关税进行报复，涉及五大类约5200项商品 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **September 24, 2018**: The United States imposed a 10% tariff on the third round of approximately $200 billion in Chinese goods (List 3), expanding the scope to include consumer electronics, furniture, and around 5,700 other items. The original plan was to increase the rate to 25% starting January 2019. In retaliation, China imposed tariffs ranging from 5% to 10% on approximately $60 billion worth of US goods, targeting five major categories of about 5,200 items.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

*分析：*2018年中，美方连续三轮措施使加征关税规模迅速扩大：美国当年对华加征关税的商品总额累积达2500亿美元，中国反制规模约1100亿美元 。特朗普政府此时的策略动因在于迫使中国让步，通过**大范围商品清单**提高谈判筹码，并缓解国内对贸易逆差的政治压力。中国的反制原则是**对等同步**，即每次匹配美方加税规模比例，重点打击美农产品和能源出口，以在美国国内施加反向压力 。这一轮关税交锋对双边贸易产生立竿见影的影响——2018年美国对华出口骤减，**大豆对华出口当年同比暴跌约75%** ；中国自美进口总额下降，两国贸易总额增速放缓甚至出现下滑。此外，美国消费者和进口商开始承受更高成本，2018年底美国平均对华关税飙升至12%（战前仅约3%） 。中国对美关税平均税率同时从战前约8%升至18%以上 。关税战初期的经济阵痛已显现：美国农业部门受到重创，相关州出口锐减；中国制造业出口增速下滑，国内股市也在压力下走低。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Analysis:* Three successive US measures rapidly expanded tariff coverage in 2018: the United States ultimately targeted $250 billion of Chinese goods, while China's countermeasures covered about $110 billion. The Trump administration used broad product lists to increase bargaining leverage and answer domestic political pressure over the trade deficit. China retaliated at the same time and on a broadly comparable scale, concentrating on US agricultural and energy exports to generate political pressure within the United States. The effects appeared quickly. US exports to China fell, soybean exports dropped by about 75% year on year, China's imports from the United States declined, and bilateral trade growth slowed. US consumers and importers also began to bear higher costs: the average US tariff on Chinese goods reached about 12% by the end of 2018, up from roughly 3%, while China's average tariff on US goods rose from about 8% to more than 18%. The early costs were therefore visible in both US agriculture and Chinese manufacturing and equity markets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**2018年末：暂缓与再起（扩大范围的关税威胁）**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Late 2018: A Pause Followed by Renewed Tariff Threats**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2018年12月**：中美元首在G20峰会会晤，双方同意暂缓新的关税措施，美国延期原定1月将2000亿清单税率从10%提至25%的计划，开启90天谈判窗口 。中国方面则展示诚意，临时下调汽车及零部件关税至15%并恢复采购美国大豆等，以缓和紧张局势。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **December 2018:** The US and Chinese presidents met at the G20 summit and agreed to postpone new tariffs. The United States delayed its planned January increase from 10% to 25% on the $200 billion list, opening a ninety-day negotiating window. China temporarily lowered tariffs on cars and parts to 15% and resumed purchases of US soybeans to ease tensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2019年5月**：谈判因结构性分歧未果。特朗普指责中方“反悔”，**5月10日起**将对华2000亿美元商品关税从10%提高到25% 。中国随即宣布自6月起把此前报复清单中部分商品税率相应提高至最高25%，涵盖约600亿美元美国产品 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **May 2019**: Negotiations failed due to structural differences. Trump accused China of "reneging," and tariffs on $200 billion worth of Chinese goods increased from 10% to 25% starting May 10. In response, China announced that it would raise the tariffs on certain items on its previously retaliatory list to a maximum of 25%, covering approximately $60 billion worth of US products, effective from June.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2019年8月**：美方宣布9月和12月分两批对余下约3000亿美元中国商品加征10%关税（“清单4”），涵盖几乎所有剩余未加税的消费品。消息引发金融市场动荡。中国国务院关税税则委员会回应拟对750亿美元美国商品分两批加征5%-10%不等关税，并表示将恢复对美汽车加征25%关税。**同时**，中国允许人民币汇率突破7：1关口至11年来最低水平，以部分抵消关税影响，美国财政部据此将中国列为“汇率操纵国” （虽在数月后撤销）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **August 2019:** The United States announced a 10% tariff on nearly all of the remaining $300 billion of Chinese goods, to be introduced in September and December under “List 4.” China responded with planned tariffs of 5–10% on $75 billion of US goods and said it would restore the additional 25% tariff on US automobiles. At the same time, the renminbi moved beyond seven to the dollar for the first time in eleven years, partly offsetting the tariff shock. The US Treasury then designated China a “currency manipulator,” a designation it withdrew several months later.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

*分析：*2018年底至2019年中，双方在谈判与加税之间反复拉锯。特朗普政府在**结构性改革**（如强制技术转让、产业补贴）问题上要求强硬，让步空间有限，因此选择重新施压提高关税率，希望迫使北京达成“全面协议”。中国则认为美国漫天要价，选择**以牙还牙**并采取非常规手段，例如**允许人民币贬值**和酝酿出口管制，以展示自身回旋余地 。2019年关税再升级使双方关税壁垒前所未有：截至当年末，美国对华平均关税率升至约21% ，覆盖自华进口的**约三分之二**（66.6%）商品 ；中方对美平均关税率升至约21% ，覆盖自美进口的**近六成**商品 。贸易战的经济冲击在2019年进一步加深：美国对华商品出口额比2018年减少了11%（降至约1066亿美元） ；中国对美出口也出现近年来少见的下降，致使当年双边贸易总额缩水。制造业供应链开始调整，大批在华制造的低端产品转由他国供货，美国从中国进口的比重自2018年起持续下降，到2020年一度从22%的峰值降至17%左右 。双方此阶段均意识到贸易战无法速胜，转而寻求阶段性停火以止损。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Analysis:* From late 2018 through mid-2019, negotiations repeatedly alternated with tariff increases. The Trump administration demanded structural changes concerning forced technology transfer and industrial subsidies and used renewed tariff pressure to seek a comprehensive agreement. China regarded the demands as excessive, retaliated, allowed the renminbi to depreciate, and considered export controls. By the end of 2019, average tariffs on both sides had reached about 21%; US measures covered roughly two-thirds of imports from China, while Chinese measures covered nearly 60% of imports from the United States. US goods exports to China fell by 11% from 2018 to about $106.6 billion, Chinese exports to the United States also declined, and total bilateral trade contracted. Manufacturers began shifting lower-end production to third countries, and China's share of US imports fell from its 22% peak toward 17% by 2020. Neither side could secure a quick victory, creating an incentive for a limited truce.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**2020年：第一阶段协议与短暂缓和**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**2020: The Phase One Agreement and a Temporary Easing**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2020年1月15日**：双方签署_第一阶段贸易协议_，中国承诺在2020-2021两年额外购买2000亿美元美国商品和服务，涵盖农产品、能源、制造业和服务等领域 ；美方则暂停原定对剩余3000亿中国产品加税计划，并将2019年9月加征的约1200亿美元商品关税税率从15%减半至7.5% 。此前已对2500亿美元中国商品实施的25%关税则维持不变。中国也取消了对部分美国产品拟加征的报复措施，降低汽车加征关税，并启动排除清单机制，允许企业申请豁免额外关税 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **January 15, 2020:** The two sides signed the Phase One trade agreement. China committed to purchase an additional $200 billion of US goods and services over 2020–2021, including agriculture, energy, manufactured goods, and services. In return, the United States suspended planned tariffs on the remaining $300 billion of Chinese products and cut the rate on about $120 billion of goods from 15% to 7.5%. The existing 25% tariff on $250 billion of Chinese goods remained. China withdrew some planned retaliation, reduced its additional tariff on US automobiles, and introduced an exclusion process through which firms could seek exemptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2020年2月**：中国公布对美加征关税商品的排除清单，部分进口商可申请**临时免征**报复性关税 。随后新冠疫情暴发，冲击双边贸易执行节奏。中国虽加大美国农产品采购（如创纪录进口玉米、大豆），但**两年额外采购目标仅完成约60%** 。“第一阶段”协议购买承诺未达预期 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **February 2020:** China announced an exclusion process allowing some importers to obtain temporary relief from retaliatory tariffs on US goods. The COVID-19 pandemic then disrupted implementation. Although China increased purchases of US agricultural products, including corn and soybeans, it fulfilled only about 60% of the two-year purchase commitment, leaving the Phase One target unmet.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2020年下半年**：美国未进一步提高对华关税，但继续通过出口管制将华为等中企列入实体清单，限制高科技出口。中方则保持协议执行，同时依赖宽松货币和财政政策支撑疫情下的经济，未就贸易问题挑起新争端。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Second Half of 2020:** The United States did not raise tariffs further, but continued restricting high-technology exports by adding Huawei and other Chinese firms to the Entity List. China continued implementing the agreement and used expansionary monetary and fiscal policy to support the pandemic-hit economy without opening a new trade dispute.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

*分析：*第一阶段协议是双方在贸易战持续两年后寻求**停战止损**的产物。特朗普希望在大选年前宣称胜利——协议要求中国大规模购买美国产品、加强知识产权保护和金融市场准入等，被视为美方策略成果。而中国借此换取美方缓征新关税、部分降低已有关税，争取经济喘息之机。该协议缓和了当时的紧张局势：2020年中美加征关税规模不再扩大，市场情绪有所修复。然而，协议执行并不理想。受疫情和商业现实影响，中国未能完成采购目标，【**实际新增购买几乎为零**】 。尽管如此，这段停战期使双边贸易在2020年有所回升（中国自美农产品进口反弹，2020年美国对华出口总额重回2017年水平 ）。总体而言，第一阶段协议实现了暂时停火，但并未解决根本矛盾，双方核心争端（产业政策、技术管制）被搁置，关税也多数维持，埋下日后摩擦再起的伏笔。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Analysis:* After two years of conflict, the Phase One agreement was an effort to **halt escalation and limit further losses**. Trump could present China's purchase commitments, stronger intellectual-property protection, and wider financial-market access as an electoral achievement; China obtained a pause in new tariffs and a partial reduction in existing ones. Additional tariff coverage stopped expanding in 2020 and market sentiment improved. Implementation, however, fell well short of the purchase targets—this note characterizes the actual additional purchases as almost zero—because of the pandemic and commercial constraints. Bilateral trade nevertheless recovered somewhat, with Chinese agricultural purchases rebounding and US exports to China returning to their 2017 level. The agreement therefore produced a temporary truce without resolving disputes over industrial policy or technology controls, and most tariffs remained in force.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**2021–2024年：关税战效应延续与政策调整**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**2021–2024: Persistent Tariff Effects and Policy Adjustment**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2021年1月**：拜登政府上台后**保留**了特朗普时期对华关税，大部分加征关税措施继续生效 。拜登表示短期内不取消对华关税，要首先与盟友协调对华策略 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **January 2021:** After taking office, the Biden administration retained most of the Trump-era tariffs on China. Biden said they would not be removed in the short term and that the United States would first coordinate its China strategy with allies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2021–2022年**：美国允许部分企业申请关税豁免，但总体关税框架未变。2022年10月，拜登政府实施_芯片出口管制_等新措施，禁止向中国出口高端半导体和设备，加大科技脱钩力度 。中国对此强烈不满，指责美方科技**“脱钩”**行径，同时继续通过降低整体关税水平来开放市场：到2022年，中国将平均最惠国关税从2018年的8.0%降至约6.5% ，以减轻贸易战和疫情对国内的压力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **2021–2022:** The United States allowed some firms to seek tariff exclusions but left the overall framework intact. In October 2022, the Biden administration introduced chip export controls that restricted sales of advanced semiconductors and production equipment to China, intensifying technological separation. China criticized this policy as technological “decoupling” while continuing to lower tariffs more broadly: by 2022, its average most-favoured-nation tariff had fallen from 8.0% in 2018 to about 6.5%, partly easing pressure from the trade conflict and the pandemic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2022–2023年贸易**：在高关税常态化背景下，中美贸易量出现结构性变化。**美国从中国进口占比**自2018年以来持续下滑——中国在美国进口中的份额已从2017年的22%降至2023年的14% 。美国企业部分转向东南亚等替代供应国，但这些国家又增加自中国进口中间产品，**全球供应链并未与中国真正脱钩** 。另一方面，中国对美出口额在疫情后反弹，2022年中美货物贸易额一度创历史新高，但这主要由美国对华进口增长推动，**美国对华出口恢复迟滞** 。事实上，除农产品外，美制成品对华市场份额被其他国家挤占，如美国汽车、波音飞机对华出口大幅萎缩，芯片销售因出口管制在2022年以后急剧下滑 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Trade in 2022–2023:** Persistently high tariffs changed the structure of bilateral trade. China's share of US imports fell from 22% in 2017 to 14% in 2023. US firms shifted some sourcing to Southeast Asia, but those supplier countries simultaneously increased their imports of Chinese intermediate goods, so global supply chains did not fully decouple from China. Bilateral goods trade reached a record in 2022 because US imports from China rebounded, while US exports to China recovered more slowly. Outside agriculture, US manufactured products lost Chinese market share: automobile and Boeing aircraft exports contracted, and semiconductor sales fell sharply after the 2022 export controls.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **投资与产业影响**：高关税和不确定性使双向投资显著下降。2018年中资对美直接投资骤减逾80%至仅50亿美元，出现净撤资 ；美资对华投资则基本持平在约130亿美元水平 。此后几年这一低潮持续，许多在华制造企业开始寻求多元化布局。而中国政府通过提高出口退税等手段帮助企业渡难关：例如2018年11月起对部分出口商品提高增值税退税率至16% ，减轻关税负担。同时，人民币汇率在2022–2023年保持相对贬值趋势，以保持中国出口品的价格竞争力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Investment and Industrial Effects:** High tariffs and policy uncertainty reduced two-way investment. Chinese direct investment in the United States fell by more than 80% to about $5 billion in 2018, producing net divestment, while US investment in China remained near $13 billion. The weakness persisted and encouraged manufacturers in China to diversify production locations. China supported exporters by raising VAT rebate rates on some products to 16% from November 2018, and a relatively weak renminbi in 2022–2023 helped preserve the price competitiveness of Chinese exports.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

*分析：*拜登任期的前四年中，美中贸易政策进入**僵持期**：美国将前朝关税视作对华施压筹码而不愿轻易放弃，中国则采用“内循环”为主的策略降低对美依赖。高关税成为“双刃剑”，虽然部分实现了供应链多元化，但也推高了美国国内通胀并损及企业利润。据美国研究估计，到2021年特朗普关税战已使美国损失最高达24.5万个就业岗位 。若贸易战持续升级，未来几年美国GDP可能累计损失1.6万亿美元，就业减少逾70万 。在中国方面，关税战叠加疫情使经济增速从2018年的6.6%放缓至2019年的6.1%，乃至2022年的3%左右；但得益于出口退税、人民币走弱和替代市场开拓，中国外贸总体韧性尚存，2021-2022年对美出口额逆势增长。不过，这种表面稳定掩盖了双方日益扩大的战略分歧。拜登政府将关注点转向**高科技**和供应链安全，对华采取“小院高墙”策略；中国则推行产业升级和自主可控，试图摆脱对美国关键技术和市场的依赖。贸易战的**长期效应**已开始显现：双边贸易和投资关系进入“新常态”，即使关税未进一步加码，实质上的经济脱钩亦在缓慢进行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Analysis:* During Biden's first four years, US–China trade policy settled into a **stalemate**. The United States retained the tariffs as leverage, while China emphasized “internal circulation” to reduce dependence on the US market. Tariffs encouraged some diversification but also raised US prices and reduced corporate profits. Research cited here estimates that the trade war had cost the United States as many as 245,000 jobs by 2021 and that further escalation could eventually reduce GDP by $1.6 trillion and employment by more than 700,000. In China, the dispute and the pandemic slowed growth from 6.6% in 2018 to 6.1% in 2019 and about 3% in 2022. Export rebates, a weaker renminbi, and alternative markets nevertheless helped exports remain resilient. Beneath that resilience, the strategic divide widened: the Biden administration adopted a “small yard, high fence” approach to technology and supply-chain security, while China pursued industrial upgrading and self-reliance. Bilateral trade and investment therefore entered a new normal of gradual economic separation even without another broad tariff increase.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**2025年：特朗普第二任期的关税战全面升级**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**2025: A Full Escalation in Trump's Second Term**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2025年1月**：特朗普再度就任美国总统，重提对华强硬贸易立场。上任伊始即表示现有关税不足以纠正对华逆差，扬言对中国进口商品征收**“对等关税”**，总体税率至少60%以上 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **January 2025:** Trump returned to office and renewed his hard-line trade position toward China. He argued that existing tariffs were insufficient to correct the bilateral deficit and threatened “reciprocal tariffs” that would bring the overall rate to at least 60%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2025年2月4日**：美国对所有自华进口商品加征_额外_10%关税（作为“基准关税”第一步）正式生效 。由于此前平均关税已约20%，此举使美对华平均关税跃升至近30%。中国当日宣布“反制措施组合拳”，包括：对美国出口的煤炭、液化天然气、农机等商品加征报复性关税 ；暂停进口部分美国食品；并警告将限制对美关键原材料出口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **February 4, 2025:** The United States imposed an additional 10% tariff on all imports from China, raising the average rate from about 20% to nearly 30%. China responded with tariffs on US coal, liquefied natural gas, agricultural machinery, and other goods, suspended some US food imports, and warned that it could restrict exports of critical raw materials.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2025年3月4日**：美方对华追加第二轮10%关税措施生效，使对华累计加征关税基准提高到20% 。中国同月回应**扩大报复**：对美国关键农产品（大豆、猪肉、牛肉、禽肉等）再加征最高15%的关税 （3月10日起实施），并出台对部分美国公司的业务限制措施 。同时，中国加强**出口管制**，对稀土等战略矿产实施更严格的出口审查，以打击美国高科技产业供应链 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **March 4, 2025:** A second additional 10% US tariff took effect, bringing the new baseline increase to 20%. China expanded its retaliation with tariffs of up to 15% on US soybeans, pork, beef, poultry, and other agricultural products from March 10, restrictions on selected US companies, and tighter export review of rare earths and other strategic minerals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2025年4月3日**：特朗普将此日宣称为“关税解放日（Liberation Day）”，宣布对包括中国在内的多个贸易伙伴征收史无前例的高额关税 。其中对华部分为在现有关税基础上再加征34%，使美国对中国商品的累计关税税率达到**104%**，于4月9日起生效 。美国同时对其他与美存在巨额逆差的经济体征收关税（11%至50%不等） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **April 3, 2025:** Trump declared “Liberation Day” and announced unusually high tariffs on China and several other trading partners. The additional measure would raise the cumulative rate on Chinese goods to **104%** from April 9, while tariffs of 11–50% would apply to other economies with large US trade deficits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **2025年4月4日**：中国政府断然拒绝美方最后通牒，宣布采取**对等报复升级**：财政部通知自4月10日起对所有美国商品额外加征34%的关税，以抵消美方最新措施影响 。中方并向WTO提起诉讼，指控美国关税违反多边贸易规则 。另外，中国暂停进口美国高粱、家禽、副产品等，并将27家美国相关企业列入“不可靠实体清单”，对杜邦等美国公司在华业务启动反垄断调查 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **April 4, 2025:** China rejected the US ultimatum and announced an additional 34% tariff on all US goods from April 10. It also filed a WTO complaint, suspended imports of US sorghum, poultry, and related products, added twenty-seven US-linked companies to the Unreliable Entity List, and opened antitrust investigations into DuPont and other US firms operating in China.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

*分析：*2025年的关税战升级宣告特朗普政府全面转向**贸易极限施压**策略：以历史罕见的关税幅度试图迫使中国在经济上就范。这一系列对华举措使美国贸易加权平均关税一举升至24%，为近一个世纪最高水平 。特朗普声称“关税将让美国更强大”，试图以**超100%关税**实质上切断美对华进口，倒逼制造业回流，同时满足其选民对强硬对华的预期。然而，这种近乎**“贸易核弹”**级别的行动也在迅速反噬美国自身经济：关税消息一出，美股连续暴跌，标普500指数四日内蒸发逾5.8万亿美元市值，跌幅达18.9%，逼近技术性熊市** **；投资者恐慌引发全球股市跟跌，日经指数暴跌4%，台湾加权指数重挫近6%** **。国际货币基金组织(IMF)罕见警告特朗普此举对全球经济构成“重大风险”，呼吁各方克制** **。中国的反制也表明其决心“奉陪到底”：通过**大幅提高报复关税和管制战略物资出口**，中方意在打击美国能源和制造业要害，同时展示即使贸易脱钩也能承受。到4月中旬，双方关税壁垒已近乎全面，“脱钩”局面成形：美国对华进口关税有效税率翻倍至54% ，中国对美进口平均关税也飙升至约56%（在原有约22%的基础上再加34%） 。这意味着曾经每年数千亿美元的双边贸易将大幅缩减，两国经济联系陷入冰点。特朗普政府此阶段的政策动因除贸易逆差外，还夹杂地缘政治考量，试图以经济手段全面压制中国崛起。不过，如此激进的关税战**预期目标难以实现**：大量经济研究和历史经验表明，近乎禁止性的关税只会导致贸易转移和国内通胀上升，而难以逆转复杂的全球供应链 。中国庞大的制造能力和市场不会因关税而消失，反而促使其加速拓展其他贸易伙伴和自给自足。可以预见，这一轮关税战将给世界经济带来深远影响：全球供应链重构将加速，第三国可能从中承接部分贸易转移；同时，美中长期博弈将更加凸显在高科技、金融等领域。正如有评论所言，在这场没有赢家的关税大战中，双方都在付出代价，短期内“脱钩”阵痛难以避免，而新的贸易格局和秩序正在重塑 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Analysis:* The 2025 escalation marked a shift toward **maximum trade pressure**, using historically high tariffs to force economic concessions from China. The measures raised the US trade-weighted average tariff to 24%, its highest level in almost a century. Trump argued that “tariffs will make America stronger” and used rates above **100%** to approach an effective cutoff of some Chinese imports, encourage manufacturing to return, and satisfy voters demanding a tougher China policy. The strategy also imposed immediate domestic costs. After the announcements, the S&P 500 lost more than $5.8 trillion in market value over four days and approached a technical bear market; the Nikkei fell 4% and Taiwan's weighted index nearly 6%. The IMF warned that the policy posed a major risk to the global economy. China's response—higher retaliatory tariffs and tighter controls on strategic-material exports—targeted US energy and manufacturing and signalled a willingness to endure further separation. By mid-April, the note estimates effective US tariffs on Chinese imports at 54% and China's average tariff on US imports at about 56%, implying a severe contraction in bilateral trade. Geopolitical competition, not just the trade deficit, increasingly shaped the policy. Yet nearly prohibitive tariffs are **unlikely to achieve their intended goals**: they tend to redirect trade and raise domestic prices rather than unwind complex global supply chains. China's manufacturing capacity and market will persist, while stronger incentives for alternative partners and self-sufficiency will accelerate supply-chain reorganization. Third countries may capture some diverted trade, but both principal economies bear substantial adjustment costs and their rivalry will increasingly extend into technology and finance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
