## **1. 1. 刘易斯模型的基本思路**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1. The Basic Idea of the Lewis Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

刘易斯模型是一个关于经济发展中“传统(农业)部门”和“现代(工业)部门)”之间劳动力转移的经典理论。核心要点包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Lewis model is a classic account of labour transfer between a traditional agricultural sector and a modern industrial sector during economic development. Its main points are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **农业部门存在大量剩余劳动力**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **The agricultural sector has a large pool of surplus labour**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在农业(或传统)部门，存在“隐藏性失业”或“剩余劳动力”，这意味着即使转移出一部分劳动力到工业部门，农业产量并不会显著下降。用边际产量(Marginal Product)的概念来说就是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In agricultural (or traditional) sectors, there exists "hidden unemployment" or "surplus labor," meaning that even when a portion of the workforce is transferred to industrial sectors, agricultural output does not significantly decline. In terms of the concept of marginal product (Marginal Product), this can be expressed as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$MP_{L,A} = \frac{\partial Q_A}{\partial L_A} \approx 0$$

其中,
• $Q_A$ 表示农业产量,
• $L_A$ 表示农业部门使用的劳动力数量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where:

• $Q_A$ denotes agricultural output,

• $L_A$ denotes labour employed in agriculture.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $$MP_{L,A} \approx 0$$ 时，说明再多雇一个人或少雇一个人，产量几乎不变，存在大量可被转移的剩余劳动力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $$MP_{L,A} \approx 0$$, adding or removing one worker changes output hardly at all. This indicates a large pool of surplus labour that can be transferred elsewhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

2. **工业部门以略高于农业“生存工资(或传统部门工资)”的水平吸收劳动力**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **The industrial sector absorbs labor at a level slightly higher than the “subsistence wage (or traditional-sector wages)” in agriculture**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

工业部门只要提供的工资 $w_I$ 高于农业部门的“生存工资”或“保留工资” $w_A$，就能从农业部门吸引到足够多的劳动力。此时，工业部门对劳动力的供给呈现出**近似无限弹性**(水平线)，直到“剩余劳动力”耗尽为止。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The industrial sector will attract enough labor from agriculture as long as the wages it provides ($w_I$) exceed the "subsistence wage" or "reservation wage" ($w_A$) of the agricultural sector. At this point, the supply of labor to the industrial sector exhibits **infinite elasticity (horizontal line)** until "surplus labor" is exhausted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

3. **当剩余劳动力被逐渐吸收，工资开始上升**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** **As the surplus labor force is gradually absorbed, wages begin to rise.**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一旦农业部门的剩余劳动力消失(即转移到工业部门的人数足够多，农业部门已经没有可随意抽走的劳动力)，再继续增加工业用工就必须提升工资才能吸引更多劳动力。这时，工业部门的劳动力供给曲线开始向上倾斜。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the surplus labor force in the agricultural sector disappears (that is, when the number of workers transferred to the industrial sector is sufficient such that there is no readily available labor in the agricultural sector), increasing industrial employment will require higher wages to attract more workers. At this point, the supply curve for labor in the industrial sector begins to slope upward.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **1.2. 图形的常见画法及含义**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2. Common Ways to Draw the Diagram and What They Mean*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在你给出的图里(“Figure 3”处)，可以看到一条**水平的红线**，在某个转折点之后变成**向上倾斜的红线**。而蓝色的几条弧线(看上去像一组相似的弧)通常可能代表工业部门在不同资本投入(或不同生产规模)下的劳动力需求曲线，也可以是与工资线相交时的均衡雇佣量示意。大致可以这样理解：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the figure you provided ("Figure 3"), you can see a **horizontal red line** that turns into an **upward-sloping red line** after a certain turning point. The several curved blue lines (appearing as a set of similar curves) typically represent labor demand curves for different capital investments (or production scales) in industrial sectors, or they could indicate the equilibrium employment quantity where these curves intersect with the wage line. Roughly put:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **横轴**：工业部门雇用的劳动力数量 $L_I$(或者从农业转移到工业部门的劳动力数量)。
2. **纵轴**：工资水平 $w$(或者说工业部门支付给劳动力的工资)。
3. **蓝色弧线**：有时用来表示工业部门的劳动力需求曲线(如 $D_1, D_2, D_3$ 不同阶段需求)，或者表示工业部门在不断扩张过程中的不同产量水平(等产量线、等成本线等)。它们往往会和工资线相交，给出不同阶段的均衡雇佣量。
4. **红色水平线**：表示在初期，工业部门能够以一个常数工资 $w^*$(略高于农业部门的生存工资)无限制地吸收劳动力。供给曲线在这一阶段是水平的。
5. **红色上升部分**：当“农业剩余劳动力”用完后，再想从农业部门挖人，就得不断提高工资，于是劳动力供给曲线变成向上倾斜。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Horizontal axis:** industrial employment $L_I$, or the amount of labour transferred from agriculture to industry.<br>
**2.** **Vertical axis:** the wage $w$ paid by the industrial sector.<br>
**3.** **Blue curves:** depending on the diagram, these may be industrial labour-demand curves at successive stages, such as $D_1, D_2, D_3$, or curves associated with different output and capital levels. Their intersections with the wage line determine equilibrium employment at each stage.<br>
**4.** **Horizontal red segment:** initially, industry can absorb labour at a constant wage $w^*$, set slightly above the agricultural subsistence wage. Labour supply is therefore horizontal over this range.<br>
**5.** **Rising red segment:** once agricultural surplus labour is exhausted, industry must offer progressively higher wages to attract additional workers, so the labour-supply curve slopes upward.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

简单来说，红线就是“工业部门所面对的劳动力供给曲线”：
• 水平部分：$w = \bar{w},\quad 0 \le L_I \le L_{\max}$(某个区间)
• 斜向上部分：超过 $L_{\max}$ 以后，$w$ 开始随 $L_I$ 的增加而上升。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Simply put, the red line represents the labor supply curve for industrial sectors:
• Horizontal portion: $w = \bar{w},\quad 0 \le L_I \le L_{\max}$ (a certain interval)
• Upward-sloping portion: Beyond $L_{\max}$, $w$ begins to rise as $L_I$ increases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **1.3. 数学表述(示意)**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3. A Schematic Mathematical Formulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在最简单的刘易斯模型中，可以用下面的思路表示：
• **农业部门的工资或生存工资**：
$$w_A = \text{常数}$$
这是农业部门或传统部门给劳动力的报酬，假设它较低。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the simplest Lewis model, the argument can be written as follows.

• **Agricultural or subsistence wage:**
$$w_A=\text{constant}$$
This is the relatively low remuneration paid to labour in the agricultural or traditional sector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **工业部门愿意提供的工资**：
$$w_I = w_A + \epsilon,\quad \text{其中 } \epsilon > 0$$
工业部门略高于 $w_A$ 的工资就能吸引足够多的剩余劳动力(因为在农业部门的边际报酬很低甚至接近0)。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Wage offered by the industrial sector:**
$$w_I = w_A + \epsilon,\quad \text{where } \epsilon > 0$$
A wage only slightly above $w_A$ can attract sufficient surplus labour because its marginal product in agriculture is very low or close to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **工业部门对劳动力的需求**(由边际产量决定)：
$$w_I = p \times MP_L$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The demand for labor in the industrial sector (determined by marginal productivity) is given by:
$$w_I = p \times MP_L$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中
• $p$ 是工业产品的价格(假设为常数)，
• $MP_L = \frac{\partial Q_I}{\partial L_I}$ 表示工业部门的劳动力边际产量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where:

• $p$ is the price of industrial output, assumed constant;
• $MP_L = \frac{\partial Q_I}{\partial L_I}$ is the marginal product of labour in industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $$w_I$$ 给定时，企业会雇用直到 $$p \times MP_L = w_I$$。随着工业资本投入的增长，需求曲线也会右移或发生变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a given $$w_I$$, firms hire until $$p \times MP_L = w_I$$. As industrial capital increases, the labour-demand curve shifts to the right or otherwise changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **劳动力供给曲线**：在低水平的工业雇佣量时，供给曲线近似水平：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Labor Supply Curve**: At low levels of industrial employment, the supply curve is nearly horizontal:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$w = \bar{w} \quad (\text{常数})$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$$w = \bar{w} \quad (\text{constant})$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但超过某个雇佣量阈值 $$L_{\max}$$ 以后，农业中已无“剩余劳动力”，要继续增加工业用工，需要逐步抬高工资：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Beyond the employment threshold $$L_{\max}$$, however, agriculture has no surplus labour left. Further industrial hiring then requires progressively higher wages:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$w = w(L_I), \quad w’(L_I) > 0 \quad \text{(向上倾斜)}$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$$w = w(L_I), \quad w'(L_I) > 0 \quad \text{(upward-sloping)}$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

因此，从图上看就会出现那条水平延伸然后拐弯向上的供给曲线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus, from the graph, one would observe the horizontal supply curve that extends and then bends upward.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **1. 4. 用一个简单的例子来理解**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.4. A Simple Example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设某经济体有大量人口在农业部门，他们每人能得到的生存工资(或在农村的机会成本)是 $100$。如果工业部门开出 $120$ 的月薪，就能从农村吸引足够多的劳动力，因为相对于农业部门的收益，这个报酬已经有足够吸引力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a large part of an economy's population works in agriculture and each worker receives a subsistence wage—or has a rural opportunity cost—of $100$. If industry offers a monthly wage of $120$, it can attract sufficient rural labour because that wage is high enough relative to agricultural earnings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在初期，假设农村真的有非常多的剩余劳动力(比如失业或隐形失业者)。工业部门只要提供 $120$，想雇多少就能雇到多少(供给是水平的)。企业也不需要提高工资。直到工业部门雇佣了足够多的农村劳动力(可能是几百万、几千万)。一旦把农村剩余劳动力“挖”完了，此时农村已经不再有那么多闲置劳动力。要想再从农业部门吸引更多工人，工业部门就不得不逐渐提高工资，比如从 $120$ 涨到 $130$、$150$……，这就对应了图里供给曲线开始往上走的部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Initially, suppose the countryside contains a genuinely large pool of surplus labour, including unemployed and disguised-unemployed workers. At a wage of $120$, industry can hire as many workers as it needs, so the supply curve is horizontal and firms need not bid wages upward. Once millions or tens of millions of rural workers have been absorbed and the surplus is exhausted, industry must progressively raise the wage—for example, from $120$ to $130$ and then $150$—to attract further workers. This is the upward-sloping part of the supply curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **2.1 图形的主要构成与含义**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Main Components of the Diagram and Their Meaning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从示意图来看，一般会分为上下或左右两部分(有时也会合并在同一坐标系中)，但核心内容是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From the diagram, it generally splits into two parts, either vertically or horizontally (sometimes combined in the same coordinate system), but the core content is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **农业部门的劳动投入与产出(或边际产量)关系**
• 横轴通常表示农业部门的劳动力数量(或时间、或转移出的劳动力数量)。
• 纵轴表示农业部门的产出(或边际产量$MP_L$，或平均产量$AP_L$)。
• 农业部门在劳动力极多时，会存在“剩余劳动力”，即一部分劳动力的边际产量$MP_L$接近0。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Relationship between Labor Input and Output in the Agricultural Sector (or Marginal Product)**<br>
• The horizontal axis typically represents the quantity of labor in the agricultural sector (or time, or the amount of labor transferred out).
• The vertical axis represents output (or marginal product $MP_L$, or average product $AP_L$) in the agricultural sector.
• In the case of an extremely large quantity of labor in the agricultural sector, there will be "surplus labor," meaning that the marginal product $MP_L$ of some of these laborers approaches zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

2. **工业部门的劳动力需求与工资水平**
• 工业部门对劳动力的需求曲线往往是向右下方倾斜(或向右上方)的，具体取决于是用工资-劳动量还是产出-劳动量来画。
• 工资$W$(尤其是工业部门的工资)常常被视为高于农业部门的平均收入水平，以吸引农业劳动力向工业转移。
• 随着工业部门资本积累、生产扩张，对劳动力的需求会上升，工业部门的劳动力需求曲线可能向右移动(需求量增大)。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **Labor Demand in Industrial Sectors and Wage Levels**<br>

• The labor demand curve for industrial sectors is typically downward-sloping (or upward-sloping), depending on whether labor quantity versus output quantity is used to draw it.
• Wages, particularly those in industrial sectors ($W$), are often considered higher than the average income levels in agricultural sectors, attracting agricultural labor to shift towards industrial jobs.
• As industrial sectors accumulate capital and expand production, the demand for labor increases, potentially shifting the labor demand curve to the right (increasing the quantity demanded).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

3. **转移过程的三个阶段**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** **Three Stages of Transfer Processes**<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

费景汉-拉尼斯模型常把从农业向工业转移的过程分为三个阶段(有些教材分为两段或更多，但核心思路类似)：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Fei–Ranis model commonly divides the transfer from agriculture to industry into three stages. Some textbooks use two or more segments, but the underlying logic is the same:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **阶段1：完全剩余劳动力阶段**
在这一阶段，农业部门有大量“剩余”劳动力，部分劳动者的边际产量$MP_L$几乎为0。只要工业部门提供的工资$W_M$高于农业部门的平均收入$AP_A$(或边际产量$MP_A$)，就能从农业部门“无损”地转移出劳动力。此时农业总产量几乎不变或变化很小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Stage 1: complete surplus labour**
Agriculture contains a large pool of surplus labour, and some workers have marginal product $MP_L$ close to zero. If the industrial wage $W_M$ exceeds average agricultural income $AP_A$ (or marginal product $MP_A$), workers can be transferred with virtually no loss of agricultural output. Total agricultural output therefore remains almost unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **阶段2：边际产量由0开始上升，但仍有剩余**
随着越来越多劳动力转移到工业部门，农业部门的边际产量$MP_L$不再为0，而是开始变得正且逐渐上升。此时再继续转移劳动力，农业产量会下降，但由于工业部门工资相对较高，转移仍在继续。农业部门逐渐走向“无剩余”状态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Stage 2: marginal product rises above zero, although some surplus remains**
As more workers move into industry, agricultural marginal product $MP_L$ becomes positive and gradually rises. Further transfers now reduce agricultural output, but movement continues because industrial wages remain relatively attractive. Agriculture approaches the point at which no surplus labour remains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **阶段3：农业部门“剩余”完全吸收，转移不再免费**
当剩余劳动力被逐渐转移完后，农业部门已经没有“免费”可转移的劳动力。此时，如果要继续从农业部门吸引劳动力到工业部门，就需要更高的工资$W_M$(或更优的条件)，否则农业部门的产量损失会使得社会总产出或福利下降。在图上，这往往表现为工业部门的劳动力供给曲线开始向上倾斜。==此时,工业劳动力供给曲线的值应该等于下面农业生产函数的斜率==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Stage 3: the agricultural surplus is exhausted, so transfer is no longer costless**
Once surplus labour has been absorbed, agriculture has no more workers who can be transferred “for free.” Industry must offer a higher wage $W_M$ or better conditions to attract additional workers; otherwise, the loss of agricultural output would reduce aggregate output or welfare. In the diagram, the industrial labour-supply curve therefore begins to slope upward. ==At this stage, the industrial labour-supply schedule should reflect the slope of the agricultural production function.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**用一个小例子来理解**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**A small example**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设有一个主要依赖农业的经济体，最初有$100$个人都在农业部门(家庭农场式)。因为耕地和资本有限，实际需要的劳动力也就$70$人，剩余$30$人虽然也在田里干活，但他们的$MP_L \approx 0$。现在外来一座工厂落户，需要招工人，它提供的工资$W_M$略高于农业部门的人均收入。于是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose an agriculture-dependent economy initially has $100$ people working on family farms. Because land and capital are limited, only $70$ workers are actually needed; the remaining $30$ still work in the fields but have $MP_L \approx 0$. A new factory opens and offers a wage $W_M$ slightly above average agricultural income. Then:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **阶段1：转移开始**
• 工厂只要愿意给略高于农业部门平均收入的工资，就能雇到足够的工人。因为对那些“多余”的$30$个人来说，只要离开农业去工厂，他们的机会成本几乎为0(因为在农业中的$MP_L=0$，拿不到更多收入)。
• 农业部门总产量几乎不受影响，大家的平均收入甚至略有提升(人均产量增加了)。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Stage 1: Shift Begins**<br>
• By offering a wage slightly above average agricultural income, the factory can hire enough workers. For the $30$ surplus workers, the opportunity cost of leaving agriculture is almost zero because their agricultural marginal product is $MP_L=0$ and their presence generates no additional income.
• The total output in the agricultural sector remains almost unchanged, and on average, people's incomes increase (with an increase in per capita output).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

2. **阶段2：剩余劳动力逐渐减少**
• 当工厂继续扩张，需要更多的劳动力，已经把最初“闲置”或“过剩”的劳动力都招走了。此时农业中每个劳动力的$MP_L$已经变得正值而且在上升。
• 继续从农业部门挖人，会导致农业产量减少(因为现在每个劳动力都“有用”)。不过只要工厂工资足够有吸引力，转移还会继续发生。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **Stage 2: Gradual Reduction in Surplus Labor**<br>
• As the factory continues to expand and requires more workers, it has already recruited most of the initially “idle” or “excess” labor. At this point, the $MP_L$ for each worker in agriculture is positive and increasing.
• Continuing to draw workers from the agricultural sector will lead to a decrease in agricultural output (since now each worker is “useful”). However, as long as the factory’s wages remain attractive, such transfers will continue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

3. **阶段3：剩余被吸收殆尽**
• 一旦农业里再无“冗余”劳动力，继续招工就必须让出更多的收益(或提供更高工资)来弥补农业部门损失。
• 工业部门如果还想扩张，就需要提高工资$W_M$(或提供其他好处)，因为农业部门的劳动力已不再“廉价”或“无损”可得。
• 这时整个经济的工资水平会进入上涨阶段，标志着二元结构向更现代化的工业经济转型的“后期”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** **Stage 3: The surplus is exhausted**<br>

• Once there is no “surplus” labor in agriculture, hiring more workers will require sacrificing even more profits (or offering higher wages) to compensate for losses in the agricultural sector.
• For industry to expand further, it must raise the wage $W_M$ or offer other benefits because agricultural labour is no longer cheaply or costlessly available.
• At this point, overall wage levels in the economy will enter a phase of increase, marking the later stages of the transition from a dual structure to a more modern industrial economy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1.二元经济结构形成的历史背景
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1. Historical Background of the Formation of a Dual-Economy Structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

建国之前,随着列强的入侵,发展成为以农业为主,城市手工业为辅的传统经济并存的二元经济格局.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before the founding of the People's Republic, foreign incursions helped produce a dual structure in which an agriculture-based traditional economy coexisted with urban handicraft production.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中国刚建国的时候考虑国家安全的问题,需要武器,所以发展重工业.重工业不需要大量的人口,只需要大量的资金.同时有严格的户籍制度,使用剪刀差,让农村向城市输血,从农业中积累工业启动资金.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the early years of the People's Republic, national-security concerns created a demand for armaments and encouraged heavy-industry development. Heavy industry required large amounts of capital rather than a large workforce. A strict household-registration system and the agricultural–industrial price scissors channelled resources from the countryside to the cities, allowing agriculture to finance the initial accumulation needed for industrialisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个农村人来到城市里,没有暂住证,没有粮票,没有饭票,甚至不能生存.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rural resident arriving in a city without temporary-registration papers, grain coupons, or meal coupons could not even secure the necessities of life.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2中国二元经济结构的演变过程与动因.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2. Evolution and Drivers of China's Dual Economic Structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

改革开放之后,为了民生,开始发展轻工业,轻工业需要大量的人口,所以为农村人口来城市务工.二元经济结构固化状态开始松动.1985年取消了农产品统购派购制度.有了市场,需求得到表达
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After reform and opening, light industry expanded to improve living standards. Because light industry required many workers, rural residents began moving to cities for employment and the rigid dual structure started to loosen. In 1985, the unified state procurement and allocation system for agricultural products was abolished. The emergence of markets allowed demand to be expressed.
