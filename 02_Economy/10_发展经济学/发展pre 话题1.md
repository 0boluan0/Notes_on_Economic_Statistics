**东亚选择性保护工业化战略分析：以韩国和台湾为中心 (20世纪中后期)**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Selective Protection and Industrialization in East Asia: South Korea and Taiwan in the Mid-to-Late Twentieth Century**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**引言**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Introduction**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

20世纪中后期，许多发展中国家采用**进口替代工业化（ISI）战略，通过高关税、进口管制等措施保护本国产业。然而在实践中，不同地区表现迥异：拉美国家广泛实施的全面保护往往导致增长停滞，而东亚的韩国和台湾则通过选择性保护政策**实现了经济腾飞，被誉为“东亚奇迹”。所谓**选择性保护**，是指政府有针对性地对特定产业提供保护和支持（如关税壁垒、补贴、配额等），并在适当时机调整政策方向，以促进产业升级和国际竞争力 。本文将深入分析韩国和台湾在战后不同时期的产业结构、选择性保护政策措施及演变，评估其对产业升级和经济增长的效果，探讨存在的问题，并比较这一策略与拉美国家传统ISI路径的差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the mid-to-late 20th century, many developing countries adopted an import substitution industrialization (ISI) strategy, employing high tariffs and import controls to protect their industries. However, in practice, outcomes varied significantly across regions: comprehensive protection in Latin American countries often led to stagnant growth, whereas in East Asia, South Korea and Taiwan achieved economic takeoff through selective protection policies, earning them the title of "East Asian Miracle." Selective protection refers to government-targeted support for specific industries (such as tariffs, subsidies, quotas, etc.), along with timely policy adjustments to promote industrial upgrading and international competitiveness. This paper will delve into the post-war industrial structures, selective protection measures, and evolution of South Korea and Taiwan, assessing their impact on industrial upgrading and economic growth. It will also examine the challenges associated with this approach and compare it with the traditional ISI path employed by Latin American countries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**一、战后经济背景与初期产业结构**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**I. Post-War Economic Background and Initial Industrial Structure**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

韩国和台湾在二战及内战后经济基础薄弱，产业结构以农业为主，工业化程度低。本土市场狭小且资本技术匮乏，但两地都进行了**土地改革**和重建，为工业发展奠定基础。其中，韩国在1953年朝鲜战争结束后“一穷二白”，超过40%人口失业，工业设施大多毁坏 ；台湾则在1949年后接受大量大陆移民和美国援助，进行了土地改革，促进农业增产并释放劳动力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After experiencing economic foundations weakened by World War II and civil wars, both South Korea and Taiwan had a weak industrial structure dominated by agriculture and low levels of industrialization. Their domestic markets were small, and they lacked capital and technology. However, both regions underwent land reform and reconstruction to lay the groundwork for industrial development. In particular, post-1953 South Korea, which was "a wasteland" after the Korean War, saw over 40% of its population unemployed and most industrial facilities destroyed; Taiwan, on the other hand, received a large influx of mainland Chinese immigrants and American aid following 1949, implemented land reform, and boosted agricultural production while freeing up labor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**韩国初期产业结构：战后韩国依赖美援维持经济稳定，50年代末开始建立基础工业和进口替代的轻工业。1950年代实施过进口替代政策**，如限制消费品进口以扶植本土工业，取得了一定成效，为后续发展打下基础 。到1960年，韩国仍以农业为主，工业基础薄弱，人均GDP仅约85美元，属于世界最贫困国家之一 。韩国社会将经济发展提升为攸关生存的国家战略目标，与国防安全紧密相联 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Early Korean Industrial Structure: After the war, South Korea relied on U.S. aid to maintain economic stability, establishing a foundation for industrial development in the late 1950s by developing basic industries and light manufacturing that substituted for imports. In the 1950s, import substitution policies were implemented, such as restricting the import of consumer goods to foster domestic industry, which yielded some success and laid the groundwork for future development. By 1960, agriculture still dominated the economy, with weak industrial foundations; per capita GDP was around $85, placing the country among the world's poorest nations. The Korean society elevated improving living standards to a national strategy of existential importance, closely linked to national defense security.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**台湾初期产业结构：**台湾在50年代亦推动进口替代工业化，建立了一些替代日用品的轻工业。但由于岛内市场有限，到50年代末本地消费品市场趋于饱和，出现生产过剩危机 。例如，纺织、食品等内销导向产业很快遇到需求天花板。1952年台湾工业产值仅占GDP的16%，农业占比高达32% （当时农业仍是主要出口部门）。这种内需局限促使台湾决策者寻求新的出路，即发展外向型产业来吸收产能过剩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Early Industrial Structure in Taiwan:** In the 1950s, Taiwan also promoted import substitution industrialization, establishing some light industries that produced substitutes for imported daily necessities. However, due to the limited domestic market, by the late 1950s, the local consumer market had become saturated, leading to a production surplus crisis. For instance, textile and food industries, which were oriented towards the domestic market, quickly hit demand ceilings. In 1952, industrial output in Taiwan accounted for only 16% of GDP, while agriculture still dominated at 32% (then agriculture was still the main export sector). This domestic demand limitation prompted Taiwanese policymakers to seek new avenues, namely developing outward-oriented industries to absorb excess capacity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

值得注意的是，韩国和台湾都拥有**高素质人力资本**和**有效政府**等有利条件：全民教育的推进和政府强有力的执行能力，为实施产业政策提供了保障。这些背景因素解释了为何两地能够在后续采取选择性保护策略并取得成功。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is noteworthy that both South Korea and Taiwan possess advantageous conditions such as high-quality human capital and effective government. This includes the advancement of universal education and the strong execution capabilities of the government, which provide a foundation for implementing industrial policies. These background factors help explain why both regions were able to adopt selective protection strategies and achieve success in the subsequent stages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**二、选择性保护政策类型及特点**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**II. Types of Selective Protection Policies and Their Characteristics**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

韩国和台湾在工业化过程中，都没有一味奉行自由放任或全面保护，而是采用了**有选择的产业政策组合**，根据发展阶段和产业条件调整策略。主要政策类型包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
South Korea and Taiwan did not adopt either a laissez-faire or comprehensive protectionist approach during their industrialization processes but rather implemented a **selective industrial policy mix**, adjusting strategies based on developmental stages and industry conditions. Main policy types include:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **高关税与进口管制：**在幼稚产业成长初期，对特定工业品施加高额关税或进口配额，保护国内市场。例如台湾在进口替代时期实施贸易和外汇管制，以及高关税措施，使许多原先依赖进口的消费品可在国内生产 。韩国也曾通过“正面清单”严格限制进口，仅允许指定商品进入国内市场 。这种选择性保护确保了本土企业在起步阶段免受国际竞争冲击。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **High Tariffs and Import Controls:** During the early stages of a nascent industry, imposing high tariffs or import quotas on specific industrial products can protect domestic markets. For instance, Taiwan implemented trade and foreign exchange controls during its import substitution period, along with high tariff measures, allowing many consumer goods that were previously imported to be produced domestically. Korea also enforced strict import restrictions through a "positive list," permitting only designated items into the domestic market. This selective protection ensured that local enterprises could avoid international competition at the outset.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **补贴与财政激励：两国政府向重点产业提供直接或间接补贴，以降低其成本、提高竞争力。其中出口补贴**是显著措施：韩国在60年代中期建立了一套激励体系，**对出口企业给予进口投入品免税、退税、出口所得减免税50%等优惠** ；政府还提供低息贷款支持企业扩大生产。此外，**“出口连带进口”奖励**在韩国亦发挥作用：出口实绩突出的厂商可获得额外进口配额，用于引进国内短缺的消费品 ，相当于变相补贴出口。这些政策为企业开拓海外市场提供了强大激励。台湾则通过《奖励投资条例》等法律在60年代初实施**税收减免**，吸引外资和新投资 ；同时对出口企业实行免税、奖勋等奖励，提高厂商出口积极性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Subsidies and fiscal incentives:** Both governments provided direct and indirect subsidies to reduce the costs of priority industries and improve their competitiveness. Export incentives were especially important. In the mid-1960s, South Korea exempted imported inputs from tax, offered rebates, cut tax on export income by 50%, and supplied low-interest loans for expansion. Under its “export-linked import” scheme, high-performing exporters also received additional quotas to import consumer goods that were scarce at home—an indirect export subsidy. Taiwan used legislation such as the Statute for the Encouragement of Investment to offer tax relief, attract foreign capital and technology, and reward exporters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **政府指导投资与信贷：国家通过五年计划等手段选择战略产业**进行重点培育。韩国政府自1962年起实施经济开发五年计划，直接介入产业规划 。在70年代“重化工业化”时期，政府扶植钢铁、造船、化工、汽车等部门，动用政策性银行提供**定向信贷**和资本注入，兴建**国有企业**（如韩国浦项钢铁POSCO）带动产业突破 。台湾政府则主导了**“十大建设”**等大型项目（1970年代中期），兴建高速公路、港口、钢铁、石化等重大基础与工业工程，直接投资建立**中国钢铁公司**、**中油石化园区**等国营龙头企业，打造重工业基础。同时在高科技产业，台湾成立工研院、创建新竹科学园区，政府提供研发经费和风险资本，培育半导体等新兴产业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Government-guided investment and credit: The state selects strategic industries for targeted development through plans such as the Five-Year Plans**. Korea began implementing economic development Five-Year Plans in 1962, directly intervening in industrial planning. During the "heavy and chemical industrialization" period in the 1970s, the government supported sectors like steel, shipbuilding, petrochemicals, and automobiles, providing **targeted loans** and capital injections through policy banks, and establishing state-owned enterprises (such as POSCO, the Pohang Iron and Steel Company) to drive industrial breakthroughs. Taiwan, meanwhile, led large-scale projects like the "Ten Major Construction Projects" (mid-1970s), constructing major infrastructure and industrial engineering such as highways, ports, steel plants, and petrochemical complexes. It also invested directly in establishing state-owned giants like China Steel Corporation and Petrochemical Park, laying the groundwork for heavy industry. Concurrently, in the high-tech sector, Taiwan established the Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI) and created the Hsinchu Science Park, where the government provided research funding and venture capital to nurture new semiconductor and other emerging industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **汇率与贸易政策配合：**两国都采取了有利于出口的汇率政策。韩国在1964年实施大幅贬值，统一汇率并保持人民币（韩元）低估，以提升出口品价格竞争力** **。台湾亦在50年代末调整汇率制度，废除多重汇率并贬值新台币，增强出口利润** **。此外，设立**出口加工区（EPZ）**也是重要举措：台湾于1966年在高雄建立全球首批出口加工区之一，为出口制造企业提供保税、基础设施和外资便利 ；韩国也在1970年设立了马山出口区。这些区域成为吸引外资和技术、推动加工出口的基地。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Coordination of exchange-rate and trade policy:** Both economies adopted exchange-rate policies favourable to exports. South Korea sharply devalued the won in 1964, unified the exchange rate, and kept the won undervalued to improve export price competitiveness. In the late 1950s, Taiwan abolished multiple exchange rates and devalued the New Taiwan dollar, increasing the profitability of exports. Export-processing zones (EPZs) were another important instrument. Taiwan opened one of the world's first EPZs in Kaohsiung in 1966, offering bonded treatment, infrastructure, and facilities for foreign investors; South Korea opened the Masan Free Export Zone in 1970. These zones attracted foreign capital and technology and became bases for processing exports.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

总的来看，韩国和台湾通过**“外向导向+选择保护”**的组合，既避免了全面自由化对弱小工业的冲击，又克服了过度保护所导致的封闭低效，实现了政策平衡。例如，韩国在给予出口企业种种优惠的同时，逐步放松对内销市场的进口限制，从1967年起由“正面清单”改为“负面清单”管理进口，大幅扩大允许进口的商品范围** **。台湾亦在1960年后**降低部分关税、放宽进口管制** 。这种灵活调整正体现了选择性保护政策的特点：**有进有退、动态优化**，以产业国际竞争力提升为最终目标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In summary, South Korea and Taiwan have achieved a balanced policy through the combination of "export-oriented + selective protection," avoiding the adverse effects on nascent industries from full liberalization while overcoming the inefficiency caused by excessive protection. For instance, South Korea provided various incentives to export enterprises while gradually easing restrictions on imports for domestic sales. Starting from 1967, it switched from a "positive list" to a "negative list" management system for imports, significantly expanding the range of permitted goods. Taiwan also lowered tariffs and relaxed import controls after 1960. This flexible adjustment exemplifies the characteristics of selective protection policies: "gradual entry and exit, dynamic optimization," with the ultimate goal being to enhance international competitiveness in their industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**三、政策演变的阶段历程**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**III. Stages of Policy Evolution**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

韩国和台湾的工业化战略并非一成不变，而是随着经济发展不断演进。可大致分为以下阶段：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The industrialization strategies of South Korea and Taiwan changed as their economies developed. They can be divided roughly into the following stages:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**1. 起步的进口替代阶段（1950年代）**：战后恢复期，两地都以替代简单消费品进口为主。政府采取高度保护措施发展轻工业，如纺织、食品加工、日用品制造等，以满足内需。台湾50年代通过进口许可证和高关税鼓励本地生产肥皂、纺织品等 ；韩国同期也建立了基础的纺织、制衣等产业。此阶段效果有限但必要——韩国1953-1961年GDP年均增长约3.9%，通胀得到控制，并在教育、土地改革和初步工业化上打下基础 。然而，到50年代末，**内需市场饱和**的问题浮现：台湾内销工业出现产能过剩 ；韩国进口替代的空间也接近极限。于是两地几乎同步地在60年代初谋求转型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**1. The Import Substitution Phase (1950s):** Post-war recovery saw both regions focusing on substituting imports of simple consumer goods. The government implemented highly protective measures to develop light industries such as textiles, food processing, and household goods to meet domestic demand. Taiwan in the 1950s encouraged local production of soap and textiles through import licenses and high tariffs; Korea established basic textile and clothing industries during the same period. While the effects were limited but necessary—Korea's GDP grew at an average annual rate of about 3.9% from 1953 to 1961, inflation was controlled, and a foundation was laid in education, land reform, and initial industrialization—by the late 1950s, **market saturation became apparent**. In Taiwan, the domestic manufacturing sector experienced overcapacity; in Korea, the space for import substitution had also reached its limits. Consequently, both countries began transitioning in the early 1960s almost simultaneously.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**2. 出口导向转型阶段（1960年代）**：面对内需瓶颈，韩国和台湾开始推行**出口导向型战略**，但方式略有不同。台湾在1958-1960年密集出台改革措施：**1958年**统一汇率、放宽进口管制；**1959-60年**修订贸易法规、实施奖励出口措施，并于1959年底推出“十九点财经改革”，大幅**降低关税、取消出口税**等，以开放经济 。**1960年**又颁布《奖励投资条例》，给予税收减免以吸引外资和技术，引入国际竞争刺激本土企业 。这些政策使台湾迅速由内销转向外销。其效果在**1963年**即显现：当年台湾出现战后首次贸易顺差，开始积累外汇储备；工业产值在国民经济中的比重也首次超越农业，电器、纺织、塑胶等轻工出口迅猛增长 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**2. Export-Oriented Transition (1960s):** Faced with a domestic-demand constraint, South Korea and Taiwan both adopted outward-oriented strategies, though by different routes. Taiwan introduced reforms from 1958 to 1960: it unified the exchange rate and relaxed import controls in **1958**, revised trade rules and export incentives in **1959–1960**, and introduced the “Nineteen-Point Economic and Financial Reform Programme” in late 1959, lowering tariffs and removing export taxes. The **1960** Statute for the Encouragement of Investment used tax relief to attract foreign capital and technology and exposed domestic firms to international competition. By **1963**, Taiwan recorded its first post-war trade surplus, began accumulating foreign-exchange reserves, and saw industrial output overtake agriculture. Exports of electrical goods, textiles, plastics, and other light manufactures grew rapidly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

韩国的转型始于朴正熙政府上台后。**1964年**实行币值贬值稳定经济，**1965年**正式从财政紧缩转向工业化政策，确定“出口导向的外向型经济”取代过去的进口替代战略 。**1962-1966年**韩国实施第一个五年计划，重点发展劳动密集型加工出口产业，并完善基础设施 。政府建立一套强有力的激励机制推动出口：每月由总统主持出口促进会议，督促各部门协助企业完成出口目标；**1960年代中期**颁布《出口振兴法》等法规，将出口业者享有的优惠和奖励制度化 。到**1967年**，韩国完成从“进口管制清单”到“允许清单”的转变，除禁止项外商品均可自由进口，标志着贸易政策的大幅转向 。这一阶段末期，韩国已成为主要的轻工制成品出口国。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
South Korea's transition began after Park Chung-hee came to power. **In 1964**, the government devalued the currency as part of its stabilization programme; **in 1965**, it moved from fiscal austerity toward industrialization and replaced import substitution with an outward-looking, export-oriented strategy. **Between 1962 and 1966**, the first Five-Year Plan emphasized labour-intensive processing exports and infrastructure. Monthly Export Promotion Conferences chaired by the president coordinated government support, while legislation in the **mid-1960s** institutionalized benefits for exporters. By **1967**, South Korea had replaced a restrictive positive list with a negative list under which all goods not explicitly prohibited could be imported. By the end of this stage, it was a major exporter of light manufactures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**3. 重化工业推进阶段（1970年代）**：在积累一定外汇和技术基础后，两国政府将目光投向**资本密集型和技术密集型产业**，以升级工业结构。韩国在1973年启动**重化工业化战略（HCI）**，把经济发展重心转向钢铁、造船、有色金属、石油化工、汽车、电子等领域 。政府提供**更高层次的选择性保护**：一方面，这些重点行业在国内市场得到高关税和进口禁令的保护（如当时韩国几乎不进口外国汽车，以扶植本土汽车厂商）；另一方面，政府斥资兴建关键项目（如浦项钢铁公司于1970年破土动工 ），并要求大型民营企业集团（**财阀**）承担相应项目，通过政策贷款和补贴支持它们扩张 。在强力推动下，韩国的重工业能力迅速提升，1970年代末造船产能跻身世界前列，汽车产业也开始形成。1973-1981年间，重工业占韩国出口的比重大增，带动经济持续高速增长 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**3. Heavy and Chemical Industry Drive (1970s):** After accumulating foreign exchange and a technological base, both governments turned toward **capital- and technology-intensive industries**. In 1973, South Korea launched its **Heavy and Chemical Industry strategy (HCI)**, shifting the centre of development toward steel, shipbuilding, non-ferrous metals, petrochemicals, automobiles, and electronics. These sectors received stronger selective protection: high tariffs and import bans sheltered the domestic market, while the government funded projects such as POSCO and required large private conglomerates—the **chaebols**—to undertake strategic investments backed by policy loans and subsidies. Heavy-industrial capacity rose quickly; by the late 1970s, South Korea ranked among the world's leading shipbuilders and had begun to establish an automobile industry. From 1973 to 1981, heavy industry's share of exports rose sharply and helped sustain rapid growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

台湾在同一时期也进行产业升级，但方式相对温和。蒋经国主政时期（1973-1979年）推动**“十大建设”**等重大投资计划，包括兴建中钢公司、大炼钢厂、石油化学园区、核电厂、南北高速公路等六项交通与三项重工业、一项能源工程** **。这些项目加强了台湾的基础工业实力。例如，1970年代中期建成的中国钢铁公司为下游机械、造船提供原材料保障。台湾政府还鼓励民营企业进入机电、化工等领域，通过合资合作获取技术。与韩国不同，台湾更欢迎外资参与重工业发展，如与日本合作建立石化联合企业等，从而减少技术空白。此外，台湾在70年代末开始筹划高科技产业布局：1974年成立工业技术研究院（ITRI）吸收国外技术，1980年设立新竹科学园区，为电子资讯企业提供优惠，开启半导体产业的发展。总体而言，台湾重化阶段的政策特色是**国家投资大型项目+引进外资技术+扶植民营升级**，同样体现了选择性介入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taiwan also upgraded industry during this period, but in a more restrained fashion. Under Chiang Ching-kuo, the Ten Major Construction Projects combined six transport projects, three heavy-industrial projects, and one energy project, including China Steel, petrochemical facilities, nuclear power plants, ports, and north–south transport links. These investments strengthened Taiwan's basic industrial capacity. China Steel, completed in the mid-1970s, supplied downstream machinery and shipbuilding, while joint ventures and technology partnerships helped private firms enter electrical machinery and chemicals. Taiwan was also more receptive than South Korea to foreign participation in heavy industry, including petrochemical ventures with Japanese firms. From the late 1970s, policy increasingly anticipated high technology: the Industrial Technology Research Institute absorbed foreign technology, and Hsinchu Science Park offered incentives to electronics and information firms. Taiwan's approach combined **state investment in major projects, imported technical expertise, and the upgrading of private firms**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**4. 调整与自由化阶段（1980-1990年代）**：经历前期高速但不平衡的增长后，两国在80年代面对内外新挑战，开始调整政策，逐步走向市场开放。韩国由于70年代末过度倾斜重工业，出现经济结构失衡，加之1979年第二次石油危机爆发，1980年经济一度负增长，国际收支赤字达53亿美元 。危机促使韩国在80年代初**强化市场机制**，压缩政府对经济的直接干预。1980年出台稳定经济和产业调整措施，削减对亏损国企的支持，清理不良贷款，同时鼓励出口企业自主竞争 。随着全球环境好转，韩国抓住“低利率、低汇率、低油价”机会，1986-1988年连续三年实现两位数的高增长且通胀低于3% 。在此繁荣背景下，韩国进一步开放市场：降低工业制成品平均关税（由1984年的22.6%降至1994年的6.2%），大幅取消进口配额，到1994年进口自由化率达98.6%，接近发达国家水平 。1996年韩国加入OECD，标志着其贸易投资体制已基本与全球接轨 。然而值得强调的是，即便在开放过程中，韩国政府仍继续选择性地支持高科技产业发展，例如**半导体**和**IT**领域在80-90年代获得研发补贴和产业政策扶持，从而保持产业升级动力 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**4. Adjustment and Liberalization (1980s–1990s):** Rapid but uneven growth left both economies facing new domestic and external pressures in the 1980s. South Korea's late-1970s emphasis on heavy industry had created structural imbalances; the second oil shock was followed by negative growth in 1980 and a balance-of-payments deficit of $5.3 billion. The government responded by strengthening market mechanisms, reducing direct intervention, withdrawing support from loss-making state-owned enterprises, resolving bad loans, and making exporters compete more independently. When global conditions improved, the combination of low interest rates, a favourable exchange rate, and low oil prices helped South Korea achieve double-digit growth with inflation below 3% from 1986 to 1988.

Prosperity supported further liberalization. The average tariff on industrial goods fell from 22.6% in 1984 to 6.2% in 1994, import quotas were removed on a large scale, and the import-liberalization ratio reached 98.6% by 1994. Joining the OECD in 1996 marked South Korea's broad integration into global trade and investment rules. Liberalization did not end selective industrial policy: semiconductors and information technology continued to receive R&D support during the 1980s and 1990s.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

台湾在80年代同样面临转型压力，包括出口导向使经济过度依赖外需的问题，以及要求金融贸易自由化的呼声。台湾政府采取渐进开放措施：放宽外汇管制，降低大部分进口商品关税，将平均关税从1962年的40%+逐步降至80年代末的不到10% 。1986年台湾宣布结束长期的外汇管制，实现货币可兑换，并推动金融市场改革。在产业政策上，台湾**聚焦高科技和中小企业**：1980年代投入巨资发展电子资讯产业，成功创办环球晶圆、台积电等半导体企业，使电子及电脑产品取代传统轻工品成为出口主力。政府通过科研机构与私人部门合作，引导产业升级，同时减少对夕阳产业的保护。比如，纺织等行业在80年代逐步取消补贴，鼓励其自行转型或向海外投资。总体上，台湾在保有一定产业引导的同时，实现了更高程度的市场开放和产业多元化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taiwan faced similar pressure in the 1980s: its export-oriented economy was highly dependent on external demand, while domestic and foreign actors called for financial and trade liberalization. The government eased foreign-exchange controls and reduced tariffs on most imports, bringing the average tariff down from more than 40% in 1962 to below 10% by the late 1980s. In 1986, it ended long-standing foreign-exchange controls, made the currency convertible, and advanced financial-market reform. Industrial policy shifted toward **high technology and small and medium-sized enterprises**. Investment in electronics and information technology helped establish firms such as UMC and TSMC and made electronics and computers central exports. Research institutes worked with private firms to upgrade technology, while declining industries such as textiles gradually lost subsidies and had to restructure or invest abroad. Taiwan thus retained selective guidance while becoming more open and diversified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

经过上述阶段的发展，到20世纪90年代中期，韩国和台湾均已实现从低收入农业经济向中高收入工业化经济的转变。选择性保护政策在不同阶段的灵活运用，使两地避免了拉美式的“中等收入陷阱”，成功迈向出口导向的成熟经济。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After the development stages outlined above, by the mid-1990s, both South Korea and Taiwan had transitioned from low-income agricultural economies to middle- to high-income industrial economies. The judicious application of selective protection policies during different phases allowed both countries to avoid the "middle-income trap" characteristic of Latin America, and successfully advance to mature export-oriented economies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**四、政策效果分析：产业升级与经济腾飞**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**IV. Analysis of Policy Effects: Industrial Upgrading and Economic Takeoff**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

选择性保护战略促成了韩国和台湾在后发工业化中的显著成就，具体体现在以下方面：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A selective protection strategy has contributed to significant achievements in late industrialization for both South Korea and Taiwan, as evidenced by the following aspects:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**1. 产业结构迅速升级：**两国从轻工业起步，逐步发展出完整的重工业和高科技产业体系。韩国1960年代以纺织、服装等劳动密集产业为出口先锋，至70年代末已建立起钢铁、造船、汽车、电子等资本技术密集产业基地 。例如，韩国汽车工业于1970年代在高度保护下诞生，80年代初开始出口，90年代跻身全球汽车生产大国。台湾则从加工装配业起家，70年代发展石化、机械等行业，80-90年代成功进军半导体和资讯产业，成为全球重要的芯片和电脑制造中心。**工业增加值占GDP比重**大幅提升：韩国制造业占GDP比重从1962年的不足15%升至1987年的近30%；台湾工业产值在1963年超过农业，此后一直是经济增长主力 。这种产业升级带来了产品出口结构的优化——东亚新兴工业经济体实现了由初级产品出口向制成品、高附加值产品出口的转变 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**1. Rapid Upgrading of Industrial Structure:** Both economies moved from light manufacturing into integrated heavy-industrial and high-technology systems. South Korea used textiles, clothing, and other labour-intensive goods as export leaders in the 1960s, then built steel, shipbuilding, automobiles, and electronics by the late 1970s. Its protected automobile industry began exporting in the early 1980s and became globally important in the 1990s. Taiwan progressed from processing and assembly into petrochemicals and machinery in the 1970s and then semiconductors and information technology in the 1980s and 1990s. Manufacturing's share of South Korean GDP rose from below 15% in 1962 to nearly 30% in 1987; in Taiwan, industrial output overtook agriculture in 1963. The composition of exports consequently shifted from primary products toward manufactured and high-value-added goods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**2. 出口大幅增长与国际竞争力提高：**选择性保护政策下，韩国和台湾积极融入国际市场，以出口拉动经济增长。韩国在1962-1979年间，出口额年均增长高达40%，出口成为经济“发动机” 。1962年韩国出口总额仅为不到1亿美元，到1979年已飙升至150亿美元以上 。台湾的表现同样亮眼：1963-1996年，台湾GDP年均增长超过9%，其中出口贡献率极高 。早在1963年台湾就实现了贸易顺差并保持经常项盈余，此后长期保持两位数的出口增速，造就了巨额外汇储备和贸易顺差 。通过在全球市场竞争，企业不断学习进步，涌现出一批具有国际竞争力的品牌和企业（如三星、现代、台积电、宏碁等）。到90年代，韩国和台湾均已跻身世界出口贸易大国行列，韩国更成为当时仅次于日本的亚洲第二大出口经济体。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**2. Export growth and stronger international competitiveness:** Under selective protection, South Korea and Taiwan integrated actively into world markets and used exports to drive growth. From 1962 to 1979, South Korean exports grew by more than 40% a year and became an engine of the economy. Exports totalled less than $100 million in 1962 but exceeded $15 billion by 1979. Taiwan's GDP grew by more than 9% a year from 1963 to 1996, with exports making a major contribution. It recorded its first trade surplus in 1963, maintained a current-account surplus, and sustained double-digit export growth for many years. Competition abroad encouraged firms to learn and innovate, producing internationally competitive companies such as Samsung, Hyundai, TSMC, and Acer. By the 1990s, both economies were major exporters, and South Korea had become Asia's second-largest exporter after Japan at that time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**3. 经济高速增长与居民收入提高：**依托产业升级和出口扩张，韩国和台湾实现了长时期的高速经济增长，被称为“亚洲四小龙”的代表。韩国1962-1979年GDP年均增长9.8%，国民生产总值从25亿美元跃升至385亿美元，人均收入由85美元增至1640美元 。1980年代末，韩国人均GNP超过4000美元，跨入中上等收入国家行列 。台湾的人均GDP增长全球第一：1960-2000年间年均增速位居世界第一 ，从1960年不足200美元提高到2000年的近1万美元。根据世界银行恒定美元测算，韩国人均GDP从1960年的约1028美元（2010年价）攀升到1990年的9367美元，实现了约9倍增长 。快速增长极大改善了民生：韩国的绝对贫困率从1965年的约40%降至1980年的10% ；台湾1960年代的失业和贫困问题也大幅缓解，中产阶级形成。由此，选择性保护战略不仅推动了工业实力提升，更带来了整体经济的腾飞和人民生活水平的显著提高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**3. Rapid growth and higher household income:** Industrial upgrading and export expansion gave South Korea and Taiwan long periods of fast growth, making them leading examples among the Four Asian Tigers. South Korean GDP grew by an average of 9.8% a year from 1962 to 1979; gross national product rose from $2.5 billion to $38.5 billion, and income per person from $85 to $1,640. By the late 1980s, GNP per person exceeded $4,000. Between 1960 and 2000, Taiwan recorded the world's highest average growth rate of GDP per person, rising from less than $200 to nearly $10,000. At constant 2010 prices, South Korean GDP per person rose from about $1,028 in 1960 to $9,367 in 1990, almost a ninefold increase. Living standards improved rapidly: South Korea's absolute-poverty rate fell from roughly 40% in 1965 to 10% in 1980, while unemployment and poverty in Taiwan also declined sharply and an emerging middle class formed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

需要强调的是，这一切并非依赖长期的高保护来实现，反而是在保护中孕育竞争力、然后**适时开放**所取得的动态效益。韩国和台湾利用保护期打造了“幼稚产业”，但并未像某些拉美国家那样永远停留在幼稚阶段，而是将其锻炼为“成年”产业，能够在国际市场上站稳脚跟。这正是选择性保护的成功之处：在不同时机采取进退有据的政策，使产业既享受足够的孵化，又经受充分的竞争洗礼。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is worth emphasizing that this is achieved not through prolonged high protection but by nurturing competitiveness within protection and then **timely opening**. Korea and Taiwan leveraged protection periods to develop "infant industries," yet did not remain in a perpetual infant stage like some Latin American countries, but rather honed them into mature industries capable of standing on the global market. This is the success of selective protection: adopting policies that are both enterprising and competitive at the right times, allowing industries to benefit from adequate incubation while also undergoing sufficient competitive **exposure**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**五、选择性保护下的问题与挑战**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**V. Issues and Challenges Under Selective Protection**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

尽管韩国和台湾的经验堪称成功，但选择性保护政策并非完美无缺。在实施过程中也出现了一些问题和挑战：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
While both South Korea and Taiwan have experienced success with selective protection policies, they are not without their flaws. Issues and challenges have arisen during their implementation as well:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **资源错配与低效风险：**政府选择“重点产业”进行保护，难免可能选错或扶持效果不佳，导致资源浪费。例如，韩国70年代重化工业推进中，一些项目因为决策仓促出现投资失误或重复建设，增加了金融负担。过度偏向特定部门也造成经济结构暂时失衡（韩国在70年代末过于依赖重工业，轻工业和服务业发展相对滞后** **）。另外，受到保护的企业可能因缺乏竞争而效率低下、成本高企，需要持续补贴才能存续。这种**“进口替代后遗症”**在拉美非常突出，在东亚虽被出口压力所缓解但仍存在隐忧。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
* Resource Misallocation and Inefficiency Risks: By prioritizing “key industries,” governments may inadvertently choose incorrectly or fail to effectively support them, leading to resource waste. For instance, in Korea during the 1970s, some projects suffered from investment missteps or overbuilding due to hasty decision-making, adding to financial burdens. Overemphasis on specific sectors also contributes to temporary imbalances in the economic structure (Korea was overly reliant on heavy industry in the late 1970s, with lagging development in light industry and services). Additionally, protected firms might become inefficient and cost-prohibitive due to a lack of competition, requiring continuous subsidies to survive. This "import substitution hangover" is particularly pronounced in Latin America, though it remains a concern in East Asia under export pressures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **企业对政府保护的依赖与腐败风险:** 长期保护可能滋生政商勾结和寻租行为。韩国财阀在受惠于政策贷款和贸易保护的同时，与政府形成了密切关系，部分企业以贿赂等方式确保政策倾斜，侵蚀了政策初衷。此外，保护环境下企业容易形成路径依赖，缺乏创新动力。如果政府没有严格的绩效考核，这些企业可能**习惯于“拿政策红利”**，而非提高生产率。幸运的是，韩国政府通过设定出口目标等手段对受保护企业实施**绩效约束**，一定程度上避免了企业“养懒” 。但在台湾，由于产业以中小企业为主，政府难以及时精准指导，每个企业获得的直接保护较少，反倒减轻了依赖心理 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Dependence on government protection and the risk of corruption:** Long-term protection can foster rent-seeking and collusion between business and government. South Korean chaebols benefited from policy loans and trade protection and formed close political ties; some used bribery to secure preferential treatment. Protection can also create path dependence and weaken incentives to innovate. Export targets imposed performance discipline on protected South Korean firms and partly limited this danger. In Taiwan's SME-dominated economy, direct protection for each firm was more limited, which reduced dependence on government support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **财政与金融负担：**保护政策常以财政补贴、税收减免、低息贷款等形式提供支持，可能对政府财政和金融体系造成压力。韩国在重化工业时期大量举债投资，导致80年代前期债务负担上升、银行不良贷款增加，不得不在危机时进行紧急调整。台湾的补贴相对节制，但为了维持汇率和出口，也投入了大量资金进行外汇市场干预和基础设施建设，占用了公共资源。一旦外部环境变化（如石油危机、利率上升），这些负担容易显现，需要政策应对。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Fiscal and financial burden:** Subsidies, tax exemptions, and low-interest loans place pressure on public finances and the financial system. Heavy borrowing during South Korea's heavy-industry drive increased debt and non-performing loans in the early 1980s, forcing emergency adjustment. Taiwan's subsidies were more restrained, but exchange-rate intervention and infrastructure still consumed substantial public resources. Oil shocks and rising interest rates made these burdens visible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

• **贸易摩擦与市场压力：**选择性保护虽然在国内有理，但在国际贸易体系中容易引起贸易伙伴不满。韩国和台湾在80年代都受到美、欧指责其对部分产业补贴过多、市场开放不足，遭遇贸易摩擦压力，不得不逐步让步开放。例如，美国曾要求台湾开放汽车市场、金融市场等，削弱了部分本土企业的保护伞。如何在保护与履行国际规则间取得平衡，是一大挑战。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
• **Trade frictions and market pressure:** Selective protection may make sense domestically but provoke trading partners. In the 1980s, the United States and Europe criticized South Korean and Taiwanese subsidies and limited market access, forcing both economies to open gradually. The United States, for example, pressed Taiwan to liberalize its automobile and financial markets. Balancing domestic protection with international obligations is therefore difficult.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

总的来说，东亚经验显示这些问题**并非不可克服**：通过及时的政策纠偏和改革，两国将潜在危机化解，反而推动了经济更健康的发展。韩国在1997年亚洲金融危机后痛下决心改革企业和金融部门，提升透明度和效率 ；台湾则早在80年代就开始约束国营部门、鼓励民营竞争，避免了长期的资源错配。因此，选择性保护的负面效应在东亚被控制在相对较小范围，并未演变为全面失败，但它提醒我们政府介入需要智慧和节制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Overall, East Asian experiences show that these issues are not insurmountable: through timely policy adjustments and reforms, the two countries were able to mitigate potential crises and instead foster healthier economic development. South Korea, for instance, took decisive action after the 1997 Asian Financial Crisis to reform its corporate and financial sectors, enhancing transparency and efficiency; Taiwan began curbing state-owned enterprises and encouraging private competition in the 1980s, averting long-term resource misallocation. Consequently, the negative effects of selective protection were contained within a relatively small scope in East Asia and did not lead to a comprehensive failure. However, this underscores the need for government intervention to be wise and restrained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**六、与拉美ISI策略的比较：选择性保护 vs 全面保护**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**VI. Comparison with Latin American ISI: Selective versus Comprehensive Protection**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

东亚的选择性保护路径与拉美国家传统的进口替代战略存在显著差异，这也是两地区发展绩效迥异的关键原因。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are significant differences between the selective protection path adopted by East Asia and the traditional import substitution strategy of Latin American countries, which is also a key reason for the divergent development performance in the two regions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**1. 政策覆盖范围：拉美国家（如巴西、阿根廷、墨西哥）在中期采用的ISI往往对几乎所有制造业**都给予高水平保护，实行**全面保护主义**。高关税、配额乃至进口禁令覆盖从消费品到资本品的广泛部门，形成高度内向型经济。相比之下，东亚的韩国和台湾采取的是**选择性和阶段性的保护**：并非保护所有部门，而是根据比较优势逐步推进。60年代首先支持劳动密集型产业成长，但对不具潜力的部门则较早开放进口；70年代转而重点扶植少数重工业，而一些轻工业逐步面临国际竞争洗礼。这种**差异化处理**避免了东亚经济背上全面低效工业的包袱。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**1. Scope of Policy: Latin American countries (such as Brazil, Argentina, and Mexico) often adopt ISI in the medium term, providing high levels of protection to almost all manufacturing sectors, implementing **comprehensive protectionism**. High tariffs, quotas, and import bans cover a wide range of sectors from consumer goods to capital goods, fostering an inward-looking economy. In contrast, East Asia's South Korea and Taiwan implement **selective and phased protection**: not protecting all sectors but gradually advancing based on comparative advantage. In the 1960s, they first supported the growth of labor-intensive industries, but opened up imports to sectors without potential early on; in the 1970s, they shifted focus to nurturing a few heavy industries while some light industries faced international competition. This **differential treatment** avoids East Asian economies bearing the burden of inefficient industrial sectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**2. 出口导向程度：拉美ISI往往忽视出口，专注于内需替代，导致出口部门发展滞后甚至倒退。一些拉美国家在保护期内出口比重下降，制造业主要满足国内市场，无法获得规模经济和外汇收益。反观东亚，虽然实行保护，但始终把出口绩效**作为衡量产业成败的试金石。韩国政府明确要求受保护企业努力出口，否则将撤销支持 ；台湾则是因内需有限，被迫积极拓展外销市场。这使得东亚国家建立了强大的出口部门。**统计显示**，1965-1990年东亚23个经济体整体增速远超拉美，其中制造品出口是主要动力 。拉美在同期出口竞争力不足，经济缺乏外需拉动。结果，1960-2000年拉美人均GDP年均增长只有1.3%，而东亚高达4.6% 。东亚通过出口获取资金和技术，不断反哺国内工业升级，形成良性循环；拉美则陷入内需饱和和外债累累的恶性循环。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**2. Export Orientation:** Latin American ISI often prioritized replacing imports for the domestic market and neglected exports. Protected manufacturers therefore struggled to achieve scale or earn foreign exchange, and in some cases export shares declined. East Asian governments instead treated export performance as a test of whether protected industries were becoming competitive. South Korea required supported firms to export, while Taiwan's small domestic market made overseas expansion unavoidable. The resulting export sectors were much stronger. From 1965 to 1990, manufacturing exports were a major reason the twenty-three East Asian economies grew faster than Latin America; from 1960 to 2000, average annual growth in GDP per capita was about 4.6% in East Asia and 1.3% in Latin America. Export earnings financed further technology acquisition and industrial upgrading in East Asia, whereas domestic-market saturation and external debt reinforced stagnation in much of Latin America.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**3. 政府执行能力与策略灵活性：拉美国家的政策有时受制于利益集团压力，保护政策一旦出台难以退出，演变为长期扭曲。东亚政府在当时多为高效威权政府，对产业政策有更大自主性和灵活度。韩国和台湾政府能在产业成熟时果断撤销保护、推进自由化**，如韩国90年代几乎完全开放市场 ，台湾取消大部分补贴和管制。这种**政策毅力**在拉美较为缺乏：许多拉美工业由于政治原因被长期保护，即使效率低下仍难以触动，拖累了整体经济。也有学者指出，东亚国家政府**既执著又务实**，在引导经济方面“不亚于拉美国家政府的介入深度，但更具有效性” 。因此，选择性保护不是不干预，而是**更聪明地干预**。拉美全面保护往往演变为对落后部门的永久庇护，东亚选择性保护则强调适时退出和绩效导向，防止了政策被既得利益绑架。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**3. Government Execution Capacity and Strategy Flexibility: Policies in Latin American countries often face pressure from interest groups, making it difficult to withdraw once implemented, leading to long-term distortions. In contrast, governments in East Asia at that time were typically efficient authoritarian regimes with greater autonomy and flexibility in industrial policies. Governments in South Korea and Taiwan were able to decisively revoke protection and promote liberalization when industries matured, such as South Korea’s near-complete market opening in the 1990s and Taiwan’s cancellation of most subsidies and regulations. This **policy tenacity** is notably lacking in Latin America: many Latin American industries have been long-protected due to political reasons, remaining inefficient and resistant to change, thereby hindering overall economic progress. Some scholars also note that East Asian governments are both tenacious and pragmatic, showing an intervention depth in guiding economies that “matches or exceeds that of Latin American governments, but with greater effectiveness.” Therefore, selective protection is not about non-intervention; it is about **more intelligent intervention**.Broad-based protection in Latin America often evolves into permanent shelter for lagging sectors, while selective protection in East Asia emphasizes timely exits and performance-oriented policies to prevent policy capture by vested interests.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**4. 成果对比：**到20世纪末，东亚“四小龙”全部跨入高收入行列，打造了多元且具有全球竞争力的产业体系；反观多数拉美国家仍未摆脱中等收入水平，工业竞争力相对薄弱。一个鲜明例子是汽车产业：阿根廷、巴西等自50年代起保护汽车业，但因缺少出口压力，产品长期成本高、质量一般，本土市场也有限。90年代贸易开放后，这些国家的汽车工业不得不依赖外资整合，国产品牌难成气候。而韩国在高度保护下培育汽车业，同时逼迫企业出口，以量产降低成本，1980年代现代汽车等不仅站稳本国市场，还成功打入北美等地 。可以说，**选择性保护+出口纪律**让韩国汽车业后来居上，全面保护却令拉美汽车业裹足不前。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**4. Outcome Comparison:** By the end of the 20th century, all four Asian Tigers had entered the high-income bracket, establishing diverse and globally competitive industrial systems; in contrast, most Latin American countries remained at the middle-income level, with relatively weak industrial competitiveness. A clear example is the automotive industry: Argentina, Brazil, and other countries began protecting their automotive sectors in the 1950s but lacked export pressure, resulting in high costs and mediocre quality for products that had limited domestic markets. After trade liberalization in the 1990s, these countries' automotive industries were forced to rely on foreign capital for integration, making it difficult for domestic brands to gain traction. In contrast, South Korea cultivated its automotive sector under high protection while compelling companies to export, thereby reducing production costs through mass production. By the 1980s, brands like Hyundai not only secured a foothold in the domestic market but also successfully penetrated North America and elsewhere. It can be said that **selective protection combined with export discipline** propelled the Korean automotive industry to the forefront, whereas comprehensive protection hindered the development of Latin American automotive industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

当然，需要承认拉美与东亚在历史起点、人口、资源禀赋等方面也有差异，比如拉美早期人均收入高于东亚，天然资源丰富，这反而导致其重贸易保护、轻出口竞争的政策倾向。而东亚小国资源贫乏，不得不更加依赖贸易生存。这些背景因素与政策选择相互作用，加剧了两地区发展的分野。总体而言，**东亚选择性保护的成功**在于找到了一条进口替代向出口导向平滑过渡的道路，通过**局部保护争取时间，全面竞争提升效率**，最终实现赶超；而**拉美传统ISI的教训**在于过度保护导致缺乏竞争压力，产业停留在低水平，难以持续增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Of course, it is necessary to acknowledge that Latin America and East Asia have differences in their historical starting points, population sizes, and resource endowments. For instance, early per capita income in Latin America was higher than in East Asia, and it possessed abundant natural resources, which led to a policy inclination towards trade protectionism rather than export competition. Conversely, small East Asian countries were resource-poor and had to rely more heavily on trade for survival. These background factors interacted with policy choices to exacerbate the divergence in development between the two regions. In general, the success of **East Asia’s selective protection** lies in finding a path from import substitution to export orientation, by **seeking time through localized protection and enhancing efficiency through comprehensive competition**, ultimately achieving catch-up; while the **lessons from Latin America’s traditional ISI (Import Substitution Industrialization)** lie in over-protection leading to a lack of competitive pressure, keeping industries at low levels and making sustained growth difficult.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**结论**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Conclusion**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

韩国和台湾在20世纪中后期的发展经验表明：进口替代工业化并非只能走向停滞，通过策略性的调整和演化，完全可能转化为更开放、更有效的增长路径。东亚模式的**选择性保护政策**以国家为“推手”，在关键产业幼年期提供庇护和养分，同时又以国际市场为“试炼场”，锤炼企业的竞争力。一系列关税、补贴、配额措施被巧妙运用，在不同阶段实现了**培育—竞争—升级**的循环。其结果是，本土产业从弱变强、经济实现腾飞，社会福利普遍改善。这一过程并非没有挑战，但韩国和台湾通过持续的政策学习和纠偏，将弊端控制在最低程度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The experience of South Korea and Taiwan in the second half of the twentieth century shows that import-substitution industrialization need not end in stagnation: strategic adjustment can turn it into a more open and effective growth path. In the East Asian model, the state used **selective protection** to shelter priority industries while they were young, but the international market remained the testing ground on which firms had to become competitive. Tariffs, subsidies, and quotas were varied across stages to create a cycle of **nurturing → competition → upgrading**. Domestic industries became stronger, the economy expanded rapidly, and social welfare generally improved. The process was not free of problems, but continual policy learning and correction kept them within bounds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

相比之下，拉美国家的全面保护战略由于缺少选择性和灵活性，常使工业化陷入低效停滞，证明了单纯的高墙并不能筑就强大工业。二者的比较为后发国家提供了宝贵启示： 适度的保护应服务于竞争力培养而非成为永久拐杖，政府干预需要有进有退，以市场反馈为指引。简言之，**“保护”不是目的，“发展”才是目的**。当保护性政策达到促进产业成熟的目的时，就应勇于退出，让市场接力驱动进一步创新和升级。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By contrast, the less selective and less flexible protection adopted in much of Latin America often produced inefficient, stagnant industries: tariff walls alone cannot build industrial strength. The comparison offers a lesson for late developers. Temporary protection should build competitiveness, not become a permanent crutch, and intervention should respond to market feedback. In short, **protection is not the objective; development is**. Once an industry has matured, protection should be withdrawn so that market competition can drive further innovation and upgrading.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在21世纪的新形势下，全球经贸环境更为复杂，新兴国家依然面临产业升级的任务。东亚选择性保护的历史经验仍具现实意义：如何在开放经济中扶持本土企业成长，如何平衡短期保护与长期效率，将是每一个谋求工业化的国家需要思考的问题。韩国和台湾的故事证明，一条精心拿捏的中间道路是存在的——它结合了国家战略眼光和市场竞争活力，最终开创出一条从进口替代走向出口制胜的成功之路。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the more complex global economy of the twenty-first century, emerging economies still face the problem of industrial upgrading. East Asia's experience with selective protection remains relevant: every industrializing country must decide how to foster domestic firms within an open economy and how to balance temporary protection against long-run efficiency. South Korea and Taiwan show that a carefully managed middle path can combine strategic state direction with market competition and support a transition from import substitution to export competitiveness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
