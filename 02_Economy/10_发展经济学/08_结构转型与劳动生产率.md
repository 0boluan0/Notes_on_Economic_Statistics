# 结构转型与劳动生产率

同样是农业就业减少，劳动者进入高生产率部门，还是进入增长缓慢、收入不稳定的工作，结果可能完全不同。这一节从“人在哪里工作、各部门生产多少”出发，学会把结构变化算清楚，再判断这些数字能支持什么结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A decline in agricultural employment can have very different consequences depending on where workers go. This chapter begins with employment and production across sectors, develops an accounting of structural change, and then asks what conclusions the numbers can support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

The [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/01_不同国家的经济发展模式#5. 增长与发展|first classroom record]] includes industrial composition among the dimensions of development. Begin with an observable meaning of structure, then connect structural change to productivity.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First understand how sectors add up to the total. That will show why producing a large share of output differs from employing a large share of workers.

The primary, secondary and tertiary sectors account respectively for 7.24%, 39.29% and 53.47% of value added, and 22.87%, 29.08% and 48.05% of employment. Value added per employed person is 48,700, 208,000 and 171,300 yuan after rounding, versus 153,900 yuan overall.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一产业吸纳约 22.87% 的就业，却形成约 7.24% 的增加值，所以其每就业者增加值约为总体平均的 $7.24/22.87\approx31.7\%$。这是份额恒等式的直接应用。它没有说明把一个农业劳动者迁往第二产业，就能使其增产到第二产业的平均值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The primary sector employs about 22.87% of workers but produces about 7.24% of value added, so its value added per worker is approximately $7.24/22.87\approx31.7\%$ of the aggregate average. This applies the share identity. It does not establish that moving an agricultural worker into the secondary sector would produce that sector's average output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原表把增加值标为“产值”，但给出的三次产业项目是增加值，不能拿企业营业收入替换分子。原页未完整列出价格版本、就业期点与修订口径；这里核对的是表内计算和读法，不据它估计跨年实际生产率增长、工时效率或个人迁移收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Although the slide labels the row “output value,” its sectoral entries are value added; business revenue is not an interchangeable numerator. The slide does not fully specify price vintage, employment timing or revisions. This exercise checks internal arithmetic and interpretation, not real productivity growth over time, hourly efficiency or individual migration returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 生产率上升，究竟上升在哪里

一张某年的表只能显示水平。要解释两个时期之间的总劳动生产率变化，还要同时观察每个部门的生产率和就业份额怎样变。先把每个乘积的变化展开，就能避免把全部增长都算成劳动力转移的功劳。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A one-year table shows levels. Explaining a change in aggregate labour productivity requires changes in both sectoral productivity and employment shares. Expanding each product's change prevents all growth from being attributed to labour reallocation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[劳动生产率分解]]

这张卡的两部门例子把总变化 $2.1$ 分成部门内 $1.4$ 和按末期生产率计算的重配 $0.7$。若把重配按初期生产率计算，则还必须单列交互项 $0.1$，不能把 $0.6$ 与 $0.7$ 当成谁算错了。每次报告“重配贡献”，都要同时报告权重约定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two-sector example splits the total change of $2.1$ into $1.4$ within sectors and $0.7$ of reallocation valued at end-period productivity. Valuing reallocation at initial productivity requires a separate interaction term of $0.1$. The figures $0.6$ and $0.7$ need not reflect an error; a reported reallocation contribution must identify its weighting convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

部门内劳动生产率上升可能伴随资本深化、技术变化或企业构成变化。这与[[增长核算的 TFP 残差|Solow 的投入增长核算]]是不同的划分：本节按产业和就业份额拆总劳动生产率，不把部门内项直接命名为“纯技术进步”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within-sector labour productivity can rise with capital deepening, technological change or changes in the mix of firms. This differs from [[增长核算的 TFP 残差|Solow input growth accounting]]: the present decomposition uses industries and employment shares, so its within-sector term is not pure technological progress.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 从核算结果到迁移判断，还差哪些条件

先问方向：转出农业是不是必然有利？再问机制：即便目的部门平均生产率高，迁入一名劳动者是否真的会得到同样的增产？这两个问题分别需要下面的边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First ask about direction: does leaving agriculture necessarily help? Then ask about mechanism: does a higher destination average imply the same productive gain for an additional worker? The following boundaries answer these distinct questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[离农与生产率重配]]

![[平均生产率与迁移收益]]

这也解释了怎样读[[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/03_人口迁移理论 2|迁移课堂记录]]开头对工业和服务业的比较：它需要特定行业、时期和投入条件。服务业内部包含很不同的活动，不能预先规定“工业转服务一定降低生产率”；反过来，也不能把城市化或服务业就业增加本身当作效率提高的证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These boundaries qualify the industry–services comparison at the start of the [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/03_人口迁移理论 2|migration classroom record]]. Its interpretation needs specific industries, periods and input conditions. Services contain very different activities, so moving from industry to services does not have a predetermined negative effect; urbanization or service-employment growth alone does not prove efficiency gains either.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 非正规活动：先分清单位和工作，再谈约束

“进城以后在什么产业工作”和“工作是否正规”是不同问题。小企业、低生产率、非正规部门和非正规就业经常同时出现，但不能当作同义词。先读两个统计定义，再看一个标签为什么不足以给出政策处方。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industry and formality answer different questions. Small firms, low productivity, the informal sector and informal employment often overlap, but they are not synonyms. Read the two statistical definitions before considering why one label cannot determine a policy prescription.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[非正规部门]]

![[非正规就业]]

单位被归入正规部门，并不等于它的每一个岗位都有正规安排。这里使用 2023 年 ILO 标准；将来与旧调查比较，先查调查采用的定义版本，再比较比例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A unit classified in the formal sector need not provide formal arrangements for every job. This chapter uses the 2023 ILO standard; comparisons with older surveys require checking the definition each survey implements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[非正规标签与经营约束]]

## 6. 从“增加登记”走到“改善结果”

诊断得出了具体约束，才有可能选择合适的干预。即便登记程序确实是一个障碍，取消费用或提供补贴也只是政策设计的一部分；需要观察企业和劳动者后来发生了什么。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identifying a specific constraint makes it possible to choose an intervention. Even when registration is a barrier, removing fees or offering subsidies is only part of a policy design. What subsequently happens to firms and workers still needs to be observed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[正规化政策评价]]

因此，生产率、就业数量、岗位保护和生活结果可以是相互联系但不同的评价对象。一项政策可能改善其中一项而没有改善另一项；回到本节开头的问题时，应当明确自己要解释的到底是哪一个结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Productivity, job numbers, worker protection and living outcomes are related but distinct objects of evaluation. A policy may improve one without improving another. Return to the opening question by specifying exactly which outcome you are trying to explain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 合上材料，完成一次判断

两个部门期初就业份额为 $(0.6,0.4)$，劳动生产率为 $(2,8)$；期末就业份额为 $(0.5,0.5)$，生产率为 $(3,7)$。使用前文相同的可加增加值口径与就业权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two sectors begin with employment shares $(0.6,0.4)$ and labour productivities $(2,8)$. Their final shares are $(0.5,0.5)$ and productivities $(3,7)$. Use the same additive value-added measure and employment weights as above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先算总劳动生产率变化，以及初期份额的部门内项、末期生产率的重配项。再解释：第二部门生产率下降，为什么总量仍可上升？这个结果能否证明一项迁移政策有效？如果同一地区新增了 200 家登记企业，还缺什么证据才能评价正规化政策？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Calculate the total productivity change, the within-sector term using initial shares, and the reallocation term using final productivities. Why can the aggregate rise despite lower productivity in the second sector? Does the result establish the effectiveness of a migration policy? If 200 firms also register in the region, what further evidence is needed to evaluate formalization?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 核对思路
> $P_0=4.4$，$P_1=5$，总变化 $0.6$。部门内项 $0.6(3-2)+0.4(7-8)=0.2$；重配项 $(-0.1)\times3+0.1\times7=0.4$，合计 $0.6$。按初期生产率展开则是 $0.2+0.6-0.2=0.6$，最后一项为交互项。
>
> 第二部门虽然自身生产率下降，期末仍高于第一部门；就业向它增加，对这套分解的重配项贡献为正。核算恒等式没有识别政策的因果作用。新增登记是交付数量；还要核对登记带来的实际安排、企业和劳动者结果、没有政策时的反事实及实施成本。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $P_0=4.4$ and $P_1=5$, giving a total change of $0.6$. The within-sector term is $0.2$ and reallocation at final productivities is $0.4$. The three-term version is $0.2+0.6-0.2=0.6$, including the interaction.
>
> The second sector remains more productive than the first at the end, so its higher employment share contributes positively to this reallocation term despite its own productivity decline. The identity does not identify a policy effect. Registrations measure an output of the intervention; assess effective arrangements, firm and worker outcomes, a no-policy counterfactual and implementation costs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
