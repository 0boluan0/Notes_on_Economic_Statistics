## 第一题组：人口、资源与增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Question Group I: Population, Resources, and Growth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/09_人口、家庭选择与增长转型|人口增长、马尔萨斯与人口转型]]、[[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/06_经济增长理论#1. 马克思的资本积累模型|Marx 的资本积累]]、[[Harrod—Domar 增长模型.canvas|哈罗德—多马]]与 [[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛.canvas|索罗增长总图]]。

> [!tip] 人口题的两种均衡
> 用 [[马尔萨斯技术冲击]] 推导固定土地与给定人口行为下的一次技术改善；用 [[人口陷阱相图]] 解释课堂三交点图。前者的稳态人口不变，后者的交点只要求总收入与人口增长率相等。答题时先说清正在使用哪组方程。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use [[马尔萨斯技术冲击|the one-time technology-shock model]] for fixed land and demographic behaviour, and [[人口陷阱相图|the population-trap phase diagram]] for the classroom's three crossings. The former has constant steady-state population; the latter requires equal income and population growth. State which equations you are using before interpreting equilibrium.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 必须能比较的机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mechanisms you must be able to compare*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **Malthus：** 技术和土地约束下，收入高于生存线会提高人口增长，长期人均收入被拉回生存水平。
- **Ricardo：** 土地质量差异和边际报酬递减使粮价、工资、地租与利润随扩张重新分配。
- **Marx：** [[Marx资本积累|资本积累]]、[[剩余价值]]、[[产业后备军]]和利润率动态构成另一套分配与危机解释。
- **Harrod–Domar：** 固定系数下的储蓄与投资一致条件给出保证增长率；[[Harrod 离轨不稳定性|Harrod 的离轨反馈]]与保证增长率不等于自然增长率是两个不同问题，$s/v$ 一式本身不是完整的稳定性定理。
- **Solow：** 在 [[Solow 稳态条件|标准生产函数与端点条件]]下，资本边际报酬递减支持正的唯一稳态与 [[Solow 转型动态|向稳态调整]]；长期人均增长来自 [[Solow 的外生技术边界|外生技术进步]]。会从 [[Solow 资本积累方程|资本积累方程]]画资本深化与稳态图，并说明 [[储蓄率的水平效应|储蓄率只永久改变水平]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Malthus:** Under technological and land constraints, income above subsistence raises population growth, pulling long-run income per person back toward the subsistence level.
- **Ricardo:** Differences in land quality and diminishing marginal returns redistribute food prices, wages, rents, and profits as the economy expands.
- **Marx:** [[Marx资本积累|Capital accumulation]], [[剩余价值|surplus value]], the [[产业后备军|industrial reserve army]], and profit-rate dynamics provide a different account of distribution and crises.
- **Harrod–Domar:** Under fixed coefficients, consistency between saving and required investment gives the warranted rate. [[Harrod 离轨不稳定性|Harrod's off-path feedback]] and a mismatch between warranted and natural growth are distinct problems; $s/v$ alone is not a complete stability theorem.
- **Solow:** Under the [[Solow 稳态条件|standard production and endpoint conditions]], diminishing returns support a unique positive steady state and [[Solow 转型动态|adjustment toward it]], while long-run growth per person comes from [[Solow 的外生技术边界|exogenous technology]]. Derive the diagram from the [[Solow 资本积累方程|capital law of motion]] and explain the [[储蓄率的水平效应|saving-rate level effect]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

答题时先列假设，再画变量关系，最后说明模型能解释什么、不能解释什么。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an answer, first state the assumptions, then draw the relationships among the variables, and finally explain what the model can and cannot explain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!tip] Marx 题先对齐资本口径
> 用 [[两种资本分类]] 区分增殖作用与周转方式，用 [[预付与耗用资本]] 确定公式分母。推导利润率后，分别检查剩余价值率、价值构成和周转条件；再用 [[利润率与利润量]] 判断绝对剩余是否随比率一起下降。课堂原页与完整讲解在上方课程入口。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use [[两种资本分类|the two capital classifications]] to distinguish valorisation from turnover, and [[预付与耗用资本|advanced versus consumed capital]] to establish the denominator. After deriving the profit rate, check the surplus-value rate, value composition and turnover conditions separately. Then use [[利润率与利润量|rates and amounts]] to assess whether absolute surplus falls with the ratio. The course entry above retains the original lecture pages and full explanation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第二题组：二元经济与迁移
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Question Group II: Dual Economy and Migration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/10_二元经济与劳动力迁移|二元经济与劳动力迁移]]。

- **Lewis：** 传统部门剩余劳动力以相对固定工资进入现代部门，资本积累推动扩张，直到 Lewis turning point。
- **Fei–Ranis：** 细分农业剩余、粮食约束和转折阶段，说明工业工资为何在不同阶段变化。
- **Todaro：** 迁移取决于城乡预期收入而非当前工资差，因此城市失业可与持续迁移并存。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Lewis:** Surplus labour moves from the traditional sector into the modern sector at a relatively fixed wage. Capital accumulation drives expansion until the Lewis turning point.
- **Fei–Ranis:** The model distinguishes stages of agricultural surplus and food constraints to explain why industrial wages change across the transition.
- **Todaro:** Migration depends on expected urban and rural income rather than the current wage gap, so continued migration can coexist with urban unemployment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较题必须写清：劳动力为什么移动、工资怎样决定、何时出现转折、模型怎样解释城市失业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A comparison answer must state clearly why labour moves, how wages are determined, when a turning point occurs, and how each model explains urban unemployment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第三题组：发展差距与结构转型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Question Group III: Development Gaps and Structural Transformation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/00_课程总览#1. 发展差距、制度与结构转型|发展差距、制度与结构转型]]。

- 不只用 GDP 衡量发展；同时看健康、教育、贫困、不平等、部门生产率和就业结构。
- 解释农业—工业—服务业转型时，区分劳动重新配置与部门内部生产率提升。
- 制度、历史和国家能力不是一句“制度重要”，要给出具体机制和识别边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Do not measure development by GDP alone; also consider health, education, poverty, inequality, sectoral productivity, and the structure of employment.
- When explaining the transition from agriculture to industry and services, distinguish labour reallocation from productivity growth within each sector.
- It is not enough to say that institutions, history, and state capacity matter. Specify the mechanism and the boundaries of what the evidence can identify.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第四题组：贸易、政策与全球化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Question Group IV: Trade, Policy, and Globalisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应 [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/00_课程总览#5. 发展政策、贸易与全球化|发展政策、贸易与全球化]]。

- 贸易、外资、技术扩散、产业政策、教育和基础设施分别通过什么机制影响生产率与结构转型。
- 政策效果依赖执行能力、融资、市场规模、外部环境和政治约束；不能脱离情境给统一处方。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Explain the distinct mechanisms through which trade, foreign direct investment, technology diffusion, industrial policy, education, and infrastructure affect productivity and structural transformation.
- Policy effectiveness depends on implementation capacity, financing, market size, the external environment, and political constraints; there is no single prescription independent of context.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 作答检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Answer Checklist*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 先写目标变量和模型假设。
2. 图中轴、曲线移动和均衡点必须解释。
3. 区分模型内部结论与现实政策推论。
4. 比较模型时使用同一维度：资源约束、人口、工资、资本积累、技术和政策含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Write target variables and model assumptions first.<br>
**2.** Axes, curve shifts, and equilibrium points in the graph must be explained.<br>
**3.** Distinguish conclusions internal to the model from real-world policy implications.<br>
**4.** Use the same dimensions when comparing models: resource constraints, population, wages, capital accumulation, technology, and policy implications.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
