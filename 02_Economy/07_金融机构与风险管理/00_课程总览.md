## 连续阅读
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Continuous Reading Path*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1. 市场风险、希腊字母与情景分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Market Risk, Greeks, and Scenario Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是本课程的默认起点；先用快速恢复建立对象、例子和问题感，再进入详细内容。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the default starting point. Use the quick-recovery section to establish the objects, examples, and motivating questions before moving into the details.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[市场风险、Greeks 与动态对冲]]

Greeks 和动态对冲主要刻画局部、小幅且连续的市场变化；压力测试改用大幅、相关或断裂情景，检查局部近似之外的脆弱点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Greeks and dynamic hedging mainly describe local, small, continuous market moves. Stress testing uses large, correlated, or discontinuous scenarios to expose vulnerabilities beyond local approximations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[压力测试与逆向压力测试]]

### 2. 利率风险与久期管理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Interest-Rate Risk and Duration Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场风险先把价格变动映射为损益；久期专门处理利率曲线变化对固定收益和资产负债的影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market-risk analysis first maps price changes into profit and loss. Duration specifically addresses how changes in the yield curve affect fixed-income instruments and the balance sheet.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[债券久期、凸性与收益率曲线风险]]

久期与凸性先度量单个债券或组合的经济价值敏感度；银行账簿视角再加入负债、重定价时点、净利息收入和存款行为。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Duration and convexity first measure the economic-value sensitivity of a bond or portfolio. The banking-book view then adds liabilities, repricing dates, net interest income, and deposit behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[银行账簿利率风险：重定价缺口与 Duration Gap]]

### 3. 波动率、相关性与 Copula
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Volatility, Correlation, and Copulas*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单个利率敏感度不足以描述风险随时间变化和多资产共同尾部，因此进入波动率、相关和 Copula。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single interest-rate sensitivity cannot describe time-varying risk or joint tail behaviour across assets, so the analysis moves to volatility, correlation, and copulas.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[波动率度量：历史、实现与隐含波动率]]

波动率先描述每个风险因子的边际变化尺度；相关性与 Copula 再把这些边际分布连接成联合分布，尤其关注危机时的共同尾部。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volatility first describes the marginal scale of movement for each risk factor. Correlation and copulas then connect those marginal distributions into a joint distribution, with particular attention to common tails in crises.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[相关性、Copula 与尾部依赖]]

### 4. VaR、ES、回测与压力测试
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. VaR, ES, Backtesting, and Stress Testing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有了边际与依赖模型，VaR 和 ES 才能把组合损失分布压缩为可回测的风险度量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once marginal distributions and dependence have been modelled, VaR and ES can compress the portfolio loss distribution into risk measures that can be backtested.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[VaR、ES 与回测]]

VaR 与 ES 把模型中的损失分布压缩成可回测指标；压力测试随后故意离开常态条件，寻找极端但可信情景下的失效位置。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
VaR and ES compress the modelled loss distribution into backtestable measures. Stress testing then deliberately moves beyond ordinary conditions to locate failures under extreme but plausible scenarios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[压力测试与逆向压力测试]]

### 5. 历史模拟、蒙特卡罗与极值理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Historical Simulation, Monte Carlo, and Extreme-Value Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

风险度量给出目标；历史模拟、Monte Carlo 和 EVT 是构造完整或尾部损失分布的不同计算路线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The risk measure defines the target. Historical simulation, Monte Carlo, and EVT are different computational routes to the full loss distribution or its tail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[历史模拟与 Monte Carlo 风险模拟]]

历史与 Monte Carlo 方法试图构造较完整的损失分布；EVT 放弃拟合中心部分，把有限数据集中用于外推最关心的极端尾部。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Historical and Monte Carlo methods seek to construct a relatively complete loss distribution. EVT gives up modelling the centre and concentrates limited data on extrapolating the extreme tail of interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[极值理论 EVT 与尾部风险]]

### 6. 巴塞尔资本监管与 OTC 清算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Basel Capital Regulation and OTC Clearing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内部度量要进入机构治理与资本要求，因此接着理解 Basel、交易账簿和 OTC 清算规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Internal measures must be embedded in institutional governance and capital requirements, which leads to Basel rules, the trading book, and OTC clearing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[巴塞尔银行资本与流动性监管]]

Basel 先约束单家银行的资本、杠杆和流动性；OTC 改革再通过保证金、净额与中央清算改变机构之间的风险传递网络。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Basel first constrains capital, leverage, and liquidity at individual banks. OTC reform then changes the network of risk transmission among institutions through margin, netting, and central clearing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[OTC 衍生品清算、保证金与 CCP 风险]]

### 7. 信用风险、CDS 与信用 VaR
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Credit Risk, CDS, and Credit VaR*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

监管资本覆盖多类风险；信用风险模型专门刻画违约概率、回收率、相关性和信用损失分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regulatory capital covers several risk classes. Credit-risk models specifically describe default probabilities, recovery rates, dependence, and the distribution of credit losses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移]]

先用 PD、LGD、EAD 和评级迁移分解实体信贷损失，才能理解 CDS 与信用利差如何对违约风险定价和转移风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Underlying credit losses must first be decomposed into PD, LGD, EAD, and rating migration before CDS and credit spreads can be understood as pricing and transferring default risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[CDS、信用利差与基差]]

单名 CDS 和利差仍只描述一个债务人的市场风险价格；组合层面还必须加入暴露集中、违约相关和共同尾部，才能得到 Credit VaR。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single-name CDS or spread still describes the market price of one obligor’s risk. Portfolio analysis must add exposure concentration, default dependence, and common tails before Credit VaR can be obtained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[信用组合风险与 Credit VaR]]

### 8. 对手方信用风险、CVA 与 DVA
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. Counterparty Credit Risk, CVA, and DVA*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般信用风险进入衍生品后，暴露随市场变化且双方都可能违约，于是需要 CVA、DVA 和对手方框架。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When ordinary credit risk enters derivatives, exposure changes with the market and either party may default, requiring CVA, DVA, and a counterparty-risk framework.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[对手方信用风险、CVA 与 DVA]]
