### 2. 利率风险与久期管理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Interest-Rate Risk and Duration Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场风险先把价格变动映射为损益。这里从一只固定现金流债券出发，先量出支付时间与价格导数，再区分单券收益率、平行曲线和局部节点的冲击。完整算例与推导可连续读 [[02_Economy/07_金融机构与风险管理/09_久期、凸性与曲线风险|久期、凸性与曲线风险]]，全局关系见 [[债券久期、凸性与收益率曲线风险.canvas|主题图]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market-risk analysis maps price changes into P&L. Begin here with a fixed-cash-flow bond, connect payment timing to price derivatives, then distinguish its own yield from parallel and local curve shocks. Read the worked derivation in [[02_Economy/07_金融机构与风险管理/09_久期、凸性与曲线风险|Duration, Convexity, and Curve Risk]] or explore the [[债券久期、凸性与收益率曲线风险.canvas|topic map]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把现金流的时间重心算出来，再问它如何转成当前价格的斜率。同一到期日不是同一种利率暴露。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First locate the present-value centre of the payments, then translate it into the current price slope. Equal maturity does not imply equal rate exposure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[麦考利久期]]

![[到期日与久期]]

![[修正久期]]

![[久期价格近似]]

切线只在附近有效；价格曲线的弯曲由凸性描述。久期相同的两个组合，仍可能在较大冲击下表现不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tangent is local; convexity describes the curve's bending. Two portfolios with equal duration can still respond differently to larger moves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[债券凸性]]

![[久期凸性价格近似]]

![[同久期不同凸性]]

进入交易规模后，把比例转成金额，再在同一风险因子下聚合。先定符号和单位，才能判断头寸是在相互抵消还是叠加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For actual positions, convert relative measures into monetary exposures and aggregate under the same risk factor. Set signs and units before deciding whether positions offset or reinforce one another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[金额久期]]

![[DV01]]

![[组合久期聚合]]

![[组合DV01聚合]]

![[组合凸性聚合]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now change the question: move the benchmark curve and revalue, rather than changing only the bond's own YTM. Option-dependent payments must be re-estimated by scenario, but curve duration is not restricted to bonds with options.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[收益率久期与曲线久期]]

![[有效久期]]

![[有效凸性]]

最后把曲线风险分到节点。单一 DV01 对冲只抵消选定方向，多个节点必须一起检查；节点久期加总也有明确条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, separate curve exposure by node. A single DV01 hedge offsets one chosen direction; several nodes must be checked together. Adding key-rate durations also requires explicit conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[关键利率久期]]

![[关键久期加总条件]]

久期与凸性先度量单个债券或组合的经济价值敏感度；银行账簿视角再加入负债、重定价时点、净利息收入和存款行为。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Duration and convexity first measure the economic-value sensitivity of a bond or portfolio. The banking-book view then adds liabilities, repricing dates, net interest income, and deposit behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3. 波动率、相关性与 Copula
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Volatility, Correlation, and Copulas*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单个利率敏感度不足以描述风险随时间变化和多资产共同尾部，因此进入波动率、相关和 Copula。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single interest-rate sensitivity cannot describe time-varying risk or joint tail behaviour across assets, so the analysis moves to volatility, correlation, and copulas.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[波动率度量.canvas|波动率度量]]

波动率先描述每个风险因子的边际变化尺度；相关性与 Copula 再把这些边际分布连接成联合分布，尤其关注危机时的共同尾部。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volatility first describes the marginal scale of movement for each risk factor. Correlation and copulas then connect those marginal distributions into a joint distribution, with particular attention to common tails in crises.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[依赖建模.canvas|依赖建模]]

### 4. VaR、ES、回测与压力测试
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. VaR, ES, Backtesting, and Stress Testing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有了边际与依赖模型，VaR 和 ES 才能把组合损失分布压缩为可回测的风险度量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once marginal distributions and dependence have been modelled, VaR and ES can compress the portfolio loss distribution into risk measures that can be backtested.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[VaR、ES与回测.canvas]]

VaR 与 ES 把模型中的损失分布压缩成可回测指标；压力测试用敏感性、严重但合理的情景或逆向分析寻找概率模型之外的脆弱位置。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
VaR and ES compress the modelled loss distribution into backtestable measures. Stress testing uses sensitivities, severe but plausible scenarios, or reverse analysis to locate vulnerabilities beyond the probability model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[压力测试与逆向压力测试.canvas]]

### 5. 历史模拟、蒙特卡罗与极值理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Historical Simulation, Monte Carlo, and Extreme-Value Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

风险度量给出目标；历史模拟、Monte Carlo 和 EVT 是构造完整或尾部损失分布的不同计算路线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The risk measure defines the target. Historical simulation, Monte Carlo, and EVT are different computational routes to the full loss distribution or its tail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[风险模拟.canvas|风险模拟]]

历史与 Monte Carlo 方法试图构造较完整的损失分布；EVT 放弃拟合中心部分，把有限数据集中用于外推最关心的极端尾部。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Historical and Monte Carlo methods seek to construct a relatively complete loss distribution. EVT gives up modelling the centre and concentrates limited data on extrapolating the extreme tail of interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续阅读入口：[[13_极值理论与尾部风险|极值理论与尾部风险]]；关系总览：[[极值理论 EVT 与尾部风险.canvas|主题图]]。先学每块最大值，再学高阈值超额；两种对象有不同的极限定理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the [[13_极值理论与尾部风险|continuous reading path]] and [[极值理论 EVT 与尾部风险.canvas|topic map]]. Block maxima and high-threshold excesses are different objects with distinct limit theorems.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[分块极大值法]]

![[广义极值分布]]

![[极大值吸引域]]

![[极大值极限定理]]

POT 保留所有超过阈值的观测。先定义其条件分布，再问何时能用 GPD 逼近；精确 GPD 的矩条件会决定后面的 ES 是否有限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
POT retains all threshold exceedances. Define their conditional distribution before asking when a GPD approximation is justified. Exact-GPD moment conditions determine whether ES is finite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[超阈值法]]

![[广义Pareto分布]]

![[超阈值极限定理]]

![[广义Pareto矩存在条件]]

GPD 给的是已经进入尾部之后的分布。先乘进入尾部的概率，再反解 VaR；计算 ES 时还要把超额起点移到 VaR，并检查均值是否有限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A GPD is conditional on entering the tail. Multiply by the entry probability before inverting for VaR. ES requires moving the excess origin to VaR and checking finite-mean conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Calculations with specified parameters are only a starting point. Estimation involves threshold, sample-size and approximation trade-offs. Distribution-fit diagnostics differ from out-of-sample forecast validation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[GPD最大似然估计]]

![[均值超额图]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial data may have time-varying volatility and clustered extremes. Specify a conditional or unconditional target before filtering and dependence treatment, and report extrapolation uncertainty alongside the risk number.

### 7. 信用风险、CDS 与信用 VaR
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Credit Risk, CDS, and Credit VaR*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

监管资本覆盖多类风险；信用风险模型专门刻画违约概率、回收率、相关性和信用损失分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regulatory capital covers several risk classes. Credit-risk models specifically describe default probabilities, recovery rates, dependence, and the distribution of credit losses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[信用风险参数与模型.canvas]]

先用 PD、LGD、EAD 和评级迁移分解实体信贷损失，才能理解 CDS 与信用利差如何对违约风险定价和转移风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Underlying credit losses must first be decomposed into PD, LGD, EAD, and rating migration before CDS and credit spreads can be understood as pricing and transferring default risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[CDS定价与基差.canvas]]

单名 CDS 和利差仍只描述一个债务人的市场风险价格；组合层面还必须加入暴露集中、违约相关和共同尾部，才能得到 Credit VaR。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single-name CDS or spread still describes the market price of one obligor’s risk. Portfolio analysis must add exposure concentration, default dependence, and common tails before Credit VaR can be obtained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[信用组合风险与 Credit VaR.canvas]]

### 8. 对手方信用风险与估值调整
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*8. Counterparty Credit Risk and Valuation Adjustments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般信用风险进入衍生品后，暴露随市场变化且双方都可能违约，于是需要 CVA、DVA 和对手方框架。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When ordinary credit risk enters derivatives, exposure changes with the market and either party may default, requiring CVA, DVA, and a counterparty-risk framework.
