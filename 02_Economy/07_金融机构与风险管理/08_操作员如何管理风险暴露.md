### 1.1.1 Delta计算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1.1 Delta calculation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Delta]]的定义:交易产品价格对某一市场变量的偏导数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Definition of [[Delta|Delta]]: Partial derivative of the price of a traded instrument with respect to a market variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

题目中提到:‘假定当黄金价格变化后，交易组合的价格变为116900美元。黄金价格增加0.1 美元会触发交易组合损失100 美元。’
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The question states: "Suppose a change in the gold price reduces the trading portfolio's value to USD 116,900. A USD 0.10 increase in gold causes a USD 100 loss on the portfolio."
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

组合对黄金价格的**敏感性**，也就是**[[Delta|Delta]]**，定义为：
$$
\text{敏感性} = \frac{\text{组合价值的变动}}{\text{黄金价格的变动}}
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **sensitivity** of the portfolio to the gold price, i.e. **[[Delta|Delta]]**, is defined as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\boxed{delta = \frac{-100}{0.1} = -1000}
$$

- 这个 $-1000$ 表示：**当黄金价格上涨 $1$ 美元，整个组合会损失 $1000$ 美元**。 
- 换句话说，组合整体是“做空黄金”的头寸（因为敏感性为负数，黄金涨组合亏钱）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The value $-1000$ means that **a USD 1 increase in the gold price causes a USD 1,000 loss on the portfolio**.
- In other words, the portfolio has an overall short exposure to gold: its sensitivity is negative, so it loses value when gold rises.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.1.2  Delta中性交易组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1.2 Delta-neutral trading portfolio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **Delta中性**：指组合的$\[[Delta|Delta]]=0$，即标的价格涨跌时，组合总价值**几乎不变**，可以对冲一阶风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Delta neutrality** means that the portfolio has $\Delta=0$. Its total value is therefore approximately unchanged by a small rise or fall in the underlying price, which hedges first-order price risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.1.3 如何构造Delta中性策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1.3 How to construct a delta-neutral strategy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==Delta是线性加减的,缺多少补多少就行==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==Delta is additive, so offset the amount that is missing.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 买入Delta为负的产品（比如买入看跌期权，或者做空标的资产）
- 卖出Delta为正的产品（比如卖出看涨期权，或者买入标的资产）
- 让正负$\[[Delta|Delta]]$加起来为零，这样市场微小变动时你的组合风险最小
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Buy an instrument with negative Delta, such as a put option, or short the underlying asset.
- Sell an instrument with positive Delta, such as a call option, or buy the underlying asset.
- Choose the amounts so that the positive and negative Deltas sum to zero; this minimizes first-order portfolio exposure to small market moves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**四、概念总结**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**IV. Conceptual summary**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **Black-Scholes模型假设**：股票价格满足几何布朗运动，市场无摩擦、可做空、无分红时公式最简单。
- **分红率$q$**：若股票发放连续分红，要用$S\exp{-qT}$替换$S$。
- **参数可变时**：利率和波动率非恒定时，需用积分表达。
- **$N(d_1),N(d_2)$**：体现期权到期时标的价格高于行权价的概率权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Black–Scholes assumptions:** the stock price follows geometric Brownian motion; the market is frictionless, and short selling is allowed. The formula is simplest when the stock pays no dividend.
- **Dividend yield $q$:** if the stock pays dividends continuously, replace $S$ with $S\exp{-qT}$.
- **Time-varying parameters:** when interest rates and volatility are not constant, express their effects through integrals over time.
- **$N(d_1)$ and $N(d_2)$:** these terms provide the probability-related weights associated with the underlying price finishing above the strike.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

European and American Option Pricing and Hedging Examples

**欧式期权定价：** 欧式期权可在到期日行权，其理论定价通常使用Black-Scholes模型（BS模型）或相关解析公式。在BS模型假设下（标的资产价格服从几何布朗运动，无套利市场），**欧式看涨期权**（Call）的定价公式为：

**European-option pricing:** A European option may be exercised only at maturity. Its theoretical value is usually obtained from the Black–Scholes model or a related analytical formula. Under the Black–Scholes assumptions—the underlying asset follows geometric Brownian motion and the market is arbitrage-free—the value of a **European call option** is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C = S_0 , N(d_1) - K e^{-r T} N(d_2)

$$

$$
P = K e^{-r T}  N(-d_2) - S_0  N(-d_1)
$$

**European put option** pricing can be obtained by **put-call parity**:

其中各符号含义：$S_0$为当前标的资产价格，$K$为期权执行价，$T$为剩余到期时间（以年计），$r$为连续复利无风险利率（假设无红利情况下），$N(\cdot)$为标准正态累积分布函数。中间变量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here, $S_0$ is the current underlying asset price, $K$ is the strike price, $T$ is the remaining time to maturity in years, $r$ is the continuously compounded risk-free rate under the no-dividend assumption, and $N(\cdot)$ is the standard normal cumulative distribution function. The intermediate quantities are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
d_1 = \frac{\ln(S_0/K) + (r + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2)T}{\sigma \sqrt{T}} \qquad
d_2 = d_1 - \sigma \sqrt{T}
$$

$\sigma$为标的资产价格的年化波动率。上述公式给出了不支付红利的股票期权价格。若标的资产在期限内有连续年化股利收益率$q$，公式中可将$S_0$替换为$S_0 e^{-qT}$（相当于标的按$q$作贴现处理）。对于外汇期权，$q$可对应外币无风险利率；对股指期权，$q$可对应股息收益率。公式的适用也可以扩展至看涨看跌双方，只需注意$N(-x) = 1 - N(x)$的关系来写出看跌价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\sigma$ is the annualized volatility of the underlying asset price. The formula above prices an option on a non-dividend-paying stock. If the underlying pays a continuous annual dividend yield $q$, replace $S_0$ with $S_0e^{-qT}$, effectively discounting the underlying at rate $q$. For an FX option, $q$ corresponds to the foreign risk-free rate; for an equity-index option, it corresponds to the dividend yield. The framework applies to both calls and puts, using $N(-x)=1-N(x)$ when writing the put formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**美式期权定价：** 美式期权可在到期前任何时间行权，定价没有闭式解，一般通过数值方法（如二叉树模型、有限差分、蒙特卡洛模拟等）计算。需要特别指出几点：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**American-option pricing:** An American option may be exercised at any time up to maturity. There is generally no closed-form price, so numerical methods such as a binomial tree, finite differences, or suitable Monte Carlo methods are used. Several points are especially important:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **美式看涨期权（无红利）：** 当标的无分红时，美式看涨期权在到期前提前行权没有经济意义（因为行权会放弃剩余时间价值且没有分红损失），因此其价格应与欧式看涨期权价格相同。只有当标的支付足够大的红利时，美式看涨才可能需要考虑提前行权的价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **American call on a non-dividend-paying asset:** early exercise has no economic benefit, because exercising sacrifices the remaining time value without capturing any dividend. Its price should therefore equal the corresponding European call price. Early exercise may matter only when the underlying pays a sufficiently large dividend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **美式看跌期权：** 提前行权可能是有利的，因为行权可及早获得$K$（行权价）的现金并不再承受标的可能下跌的风险。深度实值的美式看跌往往存在最优提前行权界限。例如著名的Barone-Adesi & Whaley (BAW)近似法给出了美式期权价格的解析近似，他们定义了一个**临界标的价格** $S^*$：当标的价格跌到某个临界点以下时，立即行使看跌期权是最优策略，而高于该价格则应继续持有不行权。美式看跌价格 = 欧式看跌价 + 提前行权带来的权利金（early exercise premium），该权利金反映了在$S < S^*$区域内可以提早行权节省的损失。$S^*$可以通过解相关方程得到，但一般需要迭代数值方法。
- **二叉树模型 (CRR)：** Cox-Ross-Rubinstein二叉树是一种常用方法求解美式期权价。在树状模型中，每个节点向上或向下移动模拟价格走势，计算期权在每个节点的持有价值和行权价值，并取其较大者（因为持有人会选择对自己更有利的操作）。自终端节点倒退至初始，可得到美式期权的合理定价和最优行权策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **American put:** early exercise can be valuable because it realizes the strike-price cash flow $K$ sooner and ends exposure to a further fall in the underlying. A deep-in-the-money American put may therefore have an optimal early-exercise boundary. The Barone-Adesi–Whaley approximation, for example, defines a **critical underlying price** $S^*$: immediate exercise is optimal below that boundary, while continuation is preferred above it. The American put value equals the European put value plus an early-exercise premium. Solving for $S^*$ generally requires numerical iteration.
- **CRR binomial tree:** the Cox–Ross–Rubinstein tree is a standard method for valuing American options. At every node, compare the option's continuation value with its immediate-exercise value and retain the larger one. Backward induction from the terminal nodes then gives both the American option value and the optimal exercise policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**期权对冲实例（动态对冲）：** 期权的卖方（做市商或交易员）通常需要通过动态对冲管理风险。典型案例：某交易员卖出10万份欧式看涨期权获取权利金，同时通过买入标的股票进行Delta对冲。随着时间推移和标的价格变化，交易员不断调整持仓以保持组合Delta中性，最终希望锁定一个接近零的净利润（理想情况下等于初始收到的期权费减去对冲成本）。例如，假设卖出期权时标的股票价格49美元，行权价50，期限20周，无风险利率5%，波动率20%。交易员初始收取的期权费总额为23.96万美元（每份期权$2.396$美元），并用这笔资金中的大部分买入适量股票对冲（初始Delta约0.5左右，所以买入约5万股股票作为对冲）。在对冲期内，若9周后股票价格上涨至53美元，未对冲的期权负债价值增至41.42万美元，产生账面亏损；但交易员持有的股票头寸盈利，同时此前为调整Delta所借入的资金也产生利息负担。经过一系列对冲操作，在第9周末重新评估组合，总体损益可能非常接近零。例如该案例中，期权头寸损失17.46万美元，但股票头寸盈利16.13万美元，同时资金利息成本约1.44万美元，综合净损益约为-0.4216万美元（亏损4216美元）。这个结果表明，通过**持续的Delta对冲**，交易员有效地规避了标的价格波动带来的大部分风险，实现接近风险中性的现金流。实际上，Delta对冲的频率越高，结果越接近理论预期（在无交易成本假设下，可逼近零风险套利），但频繁交易也意味着高成本，因此交易员会根据市场波动权衡对冲频率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Option-hedging example (dynamic hedging):** An option writer, such as a market maker or trader, normally manages exposure through dynamic hedging. Consider a trader who sells 100,000 European call options, receives the premiums, and buys the underlying stock to hedge Delta. As time passes and the stock price changes, the trader repeatedly rebalances the stock position to keep the combined portfolio Delta-neutral. The goal is for the resulting net profit or loss to remain close to zero, apart from the initial premium and the financing and trading costs of replication.

Suppose the stock price is USD 49 when the options are sold, the strike is USD 50, maturity is 20 weeks, the risk-free rate is 5%, and volatility is 20%. The trader initially receives USD 239,600 in option premiums—USD 2.396 per option. With an initial Delta of roughly 0.5, the hedge requires about 50,000 shares. If the stock rises to USD 53 after nine weeks, the unhedged option liability rises to USD 414,200. The stock position gains value, while funds borrowed to adjust the hedge incur interest. In the example, the option position loses USD 174,600, the stock position gains USD 161,300, and financing costs are about USD 14,400, producing a combined loss of roughly USD 4,216.

This illustrates how **continuous Delta hedging** removes most of the exposure to movements in the underlying and produces cash flows close to risk-neutral replication. More frequent rebalancing makes the result closer to its theoretical continuous-time value when transaction costs are ignored, but it also raises actual trading costs. Traders therefore balance hedge frequency against market volatility and cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*2. Application of Taylor Expansion in Risk Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

泰勒展开（[[Taylor Expansion|Taylor]] [[Series|series]] expansion）是风险管理中用于近似评估组合价值变化的强大工具。通过对组合价值函数$V(S,\sigma,t,\dots)$进行多变量泰勒展开，可以将小范围内组合价值的变动分解为各风险因素变动的贡献之和。这对于理解希腊值在组合损益中的作用，以及在**情景模拟**和**对冲策略**设计中都有重要意义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taylor expansion ([[Taylor Expansion|Taylor]] [[Series|series]] expansion) is a powerful tool for approximating changes in portfolio value. A multivariable expansion of $V(S,\sigma,t,\dots)$ decomposes a small portfolio-value change into contributions from the individual risk factors. This makes it useful for understanding how the Greeks drive profit and loss and for designing **scenario simulations** and **hedging strategies**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**基本原理：** 对于标的资产价格$S$的小变动$\[[Delta|Delta]] S$以及其他参数（如波动率$\sigma$、时间$t$）的小变化，组合价值变化可以用一阶、二阶导数近似表示为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Basic principle:** For a small move $\Delta S$ in the underlying price $S$, together with small changes in other parameters such as volatility $\sigma$ and time $t$, the change in portfolio value can be approximated with first- and second-order derivatives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\[[Delta|Delta]] V \approx \[[Delta|Delta]] S \cdot [[Delta|Delta]] + \frac{1}{2} (\[[Delta|Delta]] S)^2 \cdot [[Gamma|Gamma]] + \[[Delta|Delta]]\sigma \cdot Vega + \[[Delta|Delta]] t \cdot [[Theta|Theta]] + \cdots$$

其中$[[Delta|Delta]], [[Gamma|Gamma]], Vega, [[Theta|Theta]]$分别为组合当前的希腊值，$\[[Delta|Delta]] S, \[[Delta|Delta]]\sigma, \[[Delta|Delta]] t$是对应因子的微小变化量。这一公式揭示了希腊值正是泰勒展开中的系数：**$[[Delta|Delta]]$项**代表标的价格线性变化对价值的一阶影响，**$[[Gamma|Gamma]]$项**代表价格二次变化（即大幅波动）的影响，**$Vega$项**捕捉波动率变化的影响，**$[[Theta|Theta]]$项**表示纯时间流逝的影响，高阶还有$[[Gamma|Gamma]]$相对于$sigma$变化的导数项等，但一般可忽略高阶小量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here, $\Delta$, $\Gamma$, Vega, and $\Theta$ are the portfolio's current Greeks, while $\Delta S$, $\Delta\sigma$, and $\Delta t$ are small changes in the corresponding factors. The formula shows that the Greeks are coefficients in a Taylor expansion: the **Delta term** is the first-order effect of a change in the underlying price; the **Gamma term** captures the second-order effect of price movements; the **Vega term** captures changes in volatility; and the **Theta term** captures the passage of time. Still higher-order terms may describe interactions such as changes in Gamma as volatility changes, but they are usually neglected in this approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 8.9

>[!question]
>- 银行持有USD/EUR汇率期权组合
> - 组合的$[[Delta|Delta]]=30000$，$[[Gamma|Gamma]]=-80000$
> - 当前汇率$s=0.90$（1欧元=0.90美元）
> - 汇率变为$s=0.93$后，问新的$[[Delta|Delta]]$是多少？需做什么交易保持$[[Delta|Delta]]$中性？若初始$[[Delta|Delta]]$中性，汇率变动后银行是否有损益？
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>- A bank holds a portfolio of USD/EUR exchange-rate options.
>- The portfolio has $\Delta=30000$ and $\Gamma=-80000$.
>- The current exchange rate is $s=0.90$, meaning USD 0.90 per euro.
>- After the exchange rate changes to $s=0.93$, what is the new Delta? What trade is required to remain delta-neutral? If the initial position was delta-neutral, does the bank make a gain or a loss after the exchange-rate move?
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **1. 汇率从$0.90$涨到$0.93$，$[[Delta|Delta]]$的变化：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Change in $\Delta$ when the exchange rate rises from $0.90$ to $0.93$:*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\[[Delta|Delta]]$的变动：
$$
Delta_{\text{新}} = Delta_{\text{原}} + [[Gamma|Gamma]] \times \[[Delta|Delta]] s
$$
$\[[Delta|Delta]] s = 0.93 - 0.90 = 0.03$
$$
Delta_{\text{新}} = 30000 + (-80000) \times 0.03 = 30000 - 2400 = 27600
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The change in the exchange rate is:
$\Delta s=0.93-0.90=0.03$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **2. 还需要进行什么交易以保持$\[[Delta|Delta]]$中性？**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. What additional trade is required to remain delta-neutral?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 此时应**再卖出$27600$欧元现货**，以使$\[[Delta|Delta]]=0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- At this point, **sell another EUR 27,600 in the spot market** so that $\Delta=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 3.若银行一开始做$[[Delta|Delta]]$中性决策，汇率变动后有无损益？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. If the bank initially established a delta-neutral position, does the exchange-rate move generate a gain or a loss?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 假设银行最初已经$\[[Delta|Delta]]$中性，即**卖空$30000$欧元现货**来对冲（即实际头寸$\[[Delta|Delta]]=0$）。
- 汇率上涨后，组合的$\[[Delta|Delta]]$为$27600$，但现货头寸不变，整体$\[[Delta|Delta]]=27600-30000=-2400$。
- 这时，**因为$\[[Gamma|Gamma]]$为负**，组合价值会因为汇率变化呈现亏损（负$\[[Gamma|Gamma]]$组合遇上涨，损失）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Suppose the bank was initially delta-neutral because it **shorted EUR 30,000 in the spot market**, giving the combined position $\Delta=0$.
- After the exchange rate rises, the option portfolio's Delta is $27600$, while the spot position is unchanged. The combined Delta is therefore $27600-30000=-2400$.
- Because the portfolio has **negative Gamma**, the exchange-rate move reduces its value: a negative-Gamma position loses from a sufficiently large move in either direction once the initial Delta exposure has been hedged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**损益近似计算（可用二阶泰勒展开）：**
$$
\[[Delta|Delta]] V \approx \frac{1}{2} \[[Gamma|Gamma]] (\[[Delta|Delta]] s)^2
$$
带入数据：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Approximate profit and loss using a second-order Taylor expansion:**
Substitute the data:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\[[Delta|Delta]] V \approx \frac{1}{2} \times (-80000) \times (0.03)^2 = \frac{1}{2} \times (-80000) \times 0.0009 = -36
$$

- 即大约**损失 36 美元**。~~损失$36$美元~~（单位为组合价值；更大波动或更复杂产品时，损益幅度会更明显）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The approximate result is therefore a **loss of USD 36**. ~~Loss of USD $36$~~ The amount is expressed in units of portfolio value; larger moves or more complex products can generate a more pronounced profit or loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!question]

>A tradable option has Delta $0.6$, Gamma $1.5$, and Vega $0.8$.
>(a) How many units of the tradable option and how much sterling are required to make the portfolio both gamma-neutral and delta-neutral? State whether each position is long or short.
>(b) How many units of the tradable option and how much sterling are required to make the portfolio both vega-neutral and delta-neutral? State whether each position is long or short.

| -------- | ------- | ------------ | ------------ | -------- |
| 看涨       | $-1000$ | $0.5$        | $2.2$        | $1.8$    |

| 看涨       | $-500$  | $0.7$        | $1.8$        | $1.4$    |

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Call | $-1000$ | $0.5$ | $2.2$ | $1.8$ |
| Call | $-500$ | $0.8$ | $0.6$ | $0.2$ |

**Let $x$ be the number of tradable options and $y$ the sterling spot position.**
