## VaR、ES 与损失模拟
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

## 教师提问形成的高频判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Recurring judgements drawn from the lecturer's questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 利率曲线变化时，哪类资产或衍生品可对冲 duration 暴露？
- 股票组合担心下跌时，期货、put 与动态 Delta 对冲分别改变什么风险？
- 信用风险、市场风险和对手方风险应使用什么工具或资本框架？
- 一个风险数值增大，是头寸、波动、相关、期限还是置信度变化造成的？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- When the yield curve changes, which assets or derivatives can hedge duration exposure?
- If an equity portfolio is exposed to a decline, how do futures, puts, and dynamic Delta hedging change its risks?
- Which instruments or capital frameworks are appropriate for credit risk, market risk, and counterparty risk?
- If a risk measure rises, is the cause the position, volatility, dependence, horizon, or confidence level?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
