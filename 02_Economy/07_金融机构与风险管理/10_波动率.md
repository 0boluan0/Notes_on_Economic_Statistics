
# 1.波动率定义与方差概念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Definitions of volatility and variance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1 波动率,方差定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1 Definitions of volatility and variance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

波动率的定义：金融变量的波动率通常定义为该变量在**单位时间内**连续复利收益率的标准差。例如，假设$S_i$是某资产第$i$天的价格，则**日波动率**可以表示为日收益率$u_i=\ln(S_i/S_{i-1})$的标准差。当用于期权定价时，时间单位通常取一年；当用于风险控制时，时间单位常取一天。更一般地，时间周期为$T$的波动率是$\ln(S_T/S_0)$的标准差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Volatility:** The volatility of a financial variable is usually defined as the standard deviation of its continuously compounded return over a specified unit of time. If $S_i$ is an asset's price on day $i$, its **daily volatility** is the standard deviation of $u_i=\ln(S_i/S_{i-1})$. Option pricing normally quotes volatility per year, whereas daily risk control often uses a one-day unit. More generally, volatility over horizon $T$ is the standard deviation of $\ln(S_T/S_0)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**方差与波动率：**波动率的平方即为方差（有时称“方差率”）。风险管理中经常更关注方差，因为**方差可以线性累加**：若假定每日收益相互独立且方差相同，则$T$天的累计收益方差为单日方差的$T$倍，对应的$T$天波动率则为单日波动率的$\sqrt{T}$倍。例如，通常假设一年有252个交易日，则年波动率与日波动率近似满足关系：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Variance and volatility:** Variance is volatility squared and is sometimes called the variance rate. Variance is useful because independent return variances add linearly: if daily returns are independent and have equal variance, the variance of a $T$-day cumulative return is $T$ times the one-day variance, while its volatility is $\sqrt{T}$ times the one-day volatility. With 252 trading days per year:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$ \sigma_{\text{年}} = \sqrt{252}\,\sigma_{\text{日}}. $$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$$\sigma_{\text{annual}}=\sqrt{252}\,\sigma_{\text{daily}}.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

（在同样的方差可加、同方差假设下，$T$日波动率满足 $\sigma_T = \sqrt{T}\,\sigma_1$，其中$\sigma_1$为日波动率。）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the same variance-additivity and equal-variance assumptions, $\sigma_T=\sqrt{T}\,\sigma_1$, where $\sigma_1$ is one-day volatility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**交易日与日历日：**一年252个交易日是常用的报价与年化约定，不表示非交易日没有风险。相邻收盘价收益会把隔夜、周末和假日累计的信息纳入下一次观测；若要按日历时间缩放，还必须另行说明时钟与方差累积假设。严格来说，只有在相应协方差项为零且单期方差相同等条件下，方差才按期数线性累加，波动率再取其平方根。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Trading days and calendar days:** Using 252 trading days per year is a quotation and annualization convention; it does not imply that non-trading days carry no risk. Close-to-close returns incorporate information accumulated overnight, over weekends, and on holidays into the next observation. Calendar-time scaling requires a separately stated clock and variance-accumulation assumption. Variance adds linearly across periods only under conditions such as zero cross-period covariance and equal one-period variance; volatility is then the square root of the accumulated variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] 例题
> **假设某股票现价$60$美元，日波动率为$2\%$。如果假设每日收益率服从正态分布且相互独立：**
> 1. 在给定正态、独立和小收益近似下，计算明日价格的近似 95% 预测区间。
> 2. 计算10日累计收益的波动率（假设10个交易日）。
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>A stock trades at USD 60 and has daily volatility of 2%. Assume daily returns are independent and normally distributed.
> **1.** Find an approximate 95% interval for tomorrow's stock price.<br>
> **2.** Find the volatility of the cumulative ten-trading-day return.<br>
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**解答：**  
1. 日收益标准差为 $0.02$，用小收益线性近似，价格一日变化的标准差约为 $60\times0.02=1.20$ 美元。正态近似下的 95% 预测区间为 $60\pm1.96\times1.20$，即约 **57.65 美元至 62.35 美元**；这不是参数置信区间，也不是精确 lognormal 价格区间。
2. 若日波动率为$2\%$且假设每日回报独立同分布，则10日累计回报的方差为$10$倍单日方差，10日波动率为$\sqrt{10}\times2\%\approx 2\%\times3.1623=6.32\%$（约为6.3%）。 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Solution**
**1.** The daily return standard deviation is $0.02$, so the approximate one-day standard deviation of the price change is $60\times0.02=1.20$ dollars. A 95% normal interval uses $1.96$ standard deviations: $60\pm1.96\times1.20$, or approximately **USD 57.65 to USD 62.35**.<br>
**2.** With independent, identically distributed daily returns, ten-day variance is ten times daily variance. Ten-day volatility is therefore $\sqrt{10}\times2\%\approx6.32\%$.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2波动率研究发展（阶段与层次）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2 Development of volatility research: stages and levels*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

波动率研究大致经历了不同的发展阶段，同时在研究内容上可以分为不同层次：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volatility research can be described both as a sequence of historical stages and as several distinct layers of inquiry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.2.1波动率研究的三个阶段
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2.1 Three historical stages of volatility research*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **第一阶段（经典金融模型阶段）**：早期金融分析常假定资产收益率服从正态分布且波动率为常数。Black–Scholes 与经典均值—方差模型把波动率视为给定的固定输入，但模型本身并不规定这个输入必须等于历史平均；它可以来自历史估计、市场校准或另一个明确的方法。
2. **第二阶段（波动率可变建模阶段）**：20世纪80年代初，Engle（1982年）提出[[ARCH(q)模型|ARCH]]，随后 Bollerslev（1986年）推广为 [[GARCH(p,q)模型|GARCH]]，使条件方差可以随过去冲击而变化。常见经验模式是[[波动率聚集|波动率聚集]]：原始收益的线性自相关往往很弱，但绝对收益或平方收益呈持续正相关，也就是大的**幅度**倾向于成簇出现；它不能简写成“原始收益自相关”，也不能凭一张样本图当作普遍定律。
3. **第三阶段（高频数据阶段）**：20世纪90年代末以来，随着高频数据的广泛应用，研究者开始利用日内高频数据来更精确地估计和预测波动率。【Andersen、Bollerslev、Diebold、Labys 等人在1998-2001年的系列研究】提出了**[[实现波动率|已实现波动率]]**等新概念和方法。此外，一些专门针对高频性质的模型被提出，例如**自回归条件持续时间模型**(ACD)和**异质自回归模型**(HAR)，用于捕捉交易时间不均匀和多重时间尺度下的波动行为。这一阶段体现出对波动率更深入精细的刻画。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Classical constant-volatility models:** Early finance commonly assumed normally distributed returns and constant volatility. Black–Scholes and classical mean–variance models treat volatility as a fixed input, but the models themselves do not prescribe a historical average; the input may come from historical estimation, market calibration, or another explicitly chosen method.<br>
**2.** **Time-varying volatility models:** Engle introduced [[ARCH(q)模型|ARCH]] in 1982, and Bollerslev generalized it to [[GARCH(p,q)模型|GARCH]] in 1986. A common empirical pattern is [[波动率聚集|volatility clustering]]: raw-return autocorrelations are often weak, while absolute or squared returns remain positively autocorrelated, so large magnitudes tend to occur near other large magnitudes. This is not a universal identity established by one sample plot.<br>
**3.** **High-frequency-data methods:** From the late 1990s, intraday data enabled more precise measurement and forecasting. Work by Andersen, Bollerslev, Diebold, Labys, and others developed **[[实现波动率|realized volatility]]**. Models such as autoregressive conditional duration (ACD) and heterogeneous autoregression (HAR) address irregular trading times and volatility at multiple horizons.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.2.2波动率研究的三个层次
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2.2 Three layers of volatility research*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **波动率的估计方法**：研究如何从数据中计算或提取波动率，包括历史波动率的统计估计方法、基于期权价格的隐含波动率反推方法、高频数据下的已实现波动率计算等。这一层次关注方法论的改进，以得到更准确或更适用的波动率衡量。  
- **波动率的统计特征**：研究波动率（或收益率分布）的统计性质和行为特征。例如波动率的自相关和“簇集”现象（即波动率有持续性，高波动时期和低波动时期交替出现）、波动率的**长记忆**特性（衰减缓慢的自相关，可用分数阶模型如 ARFIMA 描述），以及[[波动不对称与杠杆|统计不对称与杠杆机制的区分]]：负向冲击后的预测波动更大是统计模式，资本结构杠杆只是需要额外证据的解释。EGARCH 等模型可以描述不对称，但不能仅凭系数证明该经济机制。
- **波动率的预测与建模**：研究如何建立模型对未来波动率进行预测，以及对模型参数进行估计和对模型预测能力进行评价。包括采用参数估计方法（如下文讨论的极大似然估计）估计波动率模型的参数，根据历史数据拟合模型，并通过统计检验和误差分析评估模型的有效性，比较不同模型对未来波动率的预测能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Estimation:** extracting a volatility measure from data, including historical volatility, option-implied volatility, and realized volatility from high-frequency returns.
- **Statistical properties:** studying persistence and clustering, long memory, [[波动不对称与杠杆|statistical asymmetry versus a leverage mechanism]], and the distribution of returns. ARFIMA can represent long memory, while models such as EGARCH represent asymmetry; their coefficients alone do not establish the balance-sheet mechanism.
- **Forecasting and modeling:** specifying a model for future volatility, estimating its parameters—often by maximum likelihood—and evaluating fit and out-of-sample forecasting performance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] 例题
> **波动率研究的发展经历了哪几个历史阶段？各阶段的主要特点是什么？此外，波动率研究可以分为哪几个层次？请简要说明各层次所关注的内容。**
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>What historical stages has volatility research passed through, and what distinguishes each stage? Into what layers can current volatility research be divided, and what does each layer study?
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**解答：**  
- 历史上波动率研究可以分为三个阶段：  
  1. **经典常数波动率阶段：**假定波动率为常数，收益服从正态分布（例如Black–Scholes模型）；  
  2. **[[ARCH(q)模型|ARCH]]/[[GARCH(p,q)模型|GARCH]] 阶段：**引入条件异方差模型，允许波动率随时间变化，能够捕捉波动率聚集性（Engle提出ARCH，Bollerslev提出GARCH）；
  3. **高频数据阶段：**利用高频交易数据来度量波动率，引入已实现波动率等新方法，提出ARFIMA、HAR等模型以捕捉长记忆和多尺度特征。  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Solution**
- Three historical stages:
**1.** **Constant volatility:** volatility is fixed and returns are normally distributed, as in the basic Black–Scholes model.<br>
**2.** **[[ARCH(q)模型|ARCH]] and [[GARCH(p,q)模型|GARCH]]:** conditional heteroskedasticity allows volatility to vary through time and captures volatility clustering.<br>
**3.** **High-frequency data:** intraday observations support realized-volatility measures and models such as ARFIMA and HAR for long memory and multiple time scales.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 波动率研究包含以下三个层次：  
  - **估计方法层次：**研究如何从历史数据或市场价格提取波动率，例如历史波动率的计算、隐含波动率的求解、高频数据计算已实现波动率等方法；  
  - **统计特征层次：**研究波动率本身的统计性质，如波动率的自相关（波动簇集）、长记忆（自相关衰减缓慢，可用分数阶模型描述）、冲击符号相关的不对称，以及收益分布的厚尾特征；不对称本身不识别资本结构的杠杆机制。
  - **预测与模型层次：**研究如何构建波动率模型并预测未来波动率，包括参数估计（例如极大似然估计模型参数）、模型检验与评价（检验模型对波动率的拟合和预测能力）。  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Three layers of research:
  - **Estimation:** calculate historical volatility, infer implied volatility, or construct realized volatility from high-frequency data.
  - **Statistical properties:** study clustering, slow-decaying dependence, sign asymmetry, and heavy tails; asymmetry alone does not identify a leverage mechanism.
  - **Forecasting and modeling:** estimate, test, compare, and use models that forecast future volatility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3波动率的估计方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3 Methods for estimating volatility*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

波动率可以通过多种途径进行估计，主要包括**[[历史波动率]]**、**[[隐含波动率]]**和**[[实现波动率|已实现波动率]]**等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The principal approaches include **[[历史波动率|historical volatility]]**, **[[隐含波动率|implied volatility]]**, and **[[实现波动率|realized volatility]]**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.3.1历史波动率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3.1 [[历史波动率|Historical volatility]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**概念：**[[历史波动率]]（Historical Volatility, HV）是对选定过去窗口内收益变动的事后统计量，通常用该窗口收益率的标准差来度量。这个数本身只描述已经发生的路径；只有把它直接用作未来预测时，才额外假设所选历史窗口能代表未来的方差环境。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Concept:** [[历史波动率|Historical volatility]] (HV) is an ex-post statistic for return variation over a selected past window, commonly measured by the standard deviation of returns in that window. The statistic itself describes an observed path. Using it directly as a forecast adds the assumption that this window represents the future variance environment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**计算方法：**历史波动率通常采用移动时间窗口对过去价格的对数收益率计算标准差，再年化得到。基本步骤如下：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Method:** Calculate the standard deviation of log returns over a rolling historical window and annualize it. The main steps are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **收集价格数据：**获取标的资产在固定时间间隔（如每日、每周、每月）的历史价格序列$S_0, S_1, \dots, S_n$。  
2. **计算对数收益率：**对每相邻时段计算对数回报率$u_i = \ln(S_i/S_{i-1})$（当收益率较小时也可近似用$(S_i-S_{i-1})/S_{i-1}$）。这得到一系列历史日收益率$u_1, u_2, \dots, u_n$。  
3. **计算标准差并年化：**若均值未知，先用样本均值$\bar u$中心化，再计算样本标准差$s = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^n (u_i-\bar{u})^2}$。这与“预先把均值固定为0、直接计算围绕0的二阶矩”是两种口径，不能混写。若日方差可近似相加——即该日方差水平可代表年内各日，且收益的跨日协方差可以忽略——则按252个交易日年化为$\sigma_{\text{年}}=s\sqrt{252}$；若这些条件不成立，平方根时间缩放只是一种约定近似。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Collect prices:** obtain $S_0,S_1,\dots,S_n$ at a fixed frequency such as daily, weekly, or monthly.<br>
**2.** **Calculate log returns:** $u_i=\ln(S_i/S_{i-1})$. For small returns, $(S_i-S_{i-1})/S_{i-1}$ is a close approximation.<br>
**3.** **Estimate and annualize the standard deviation:** if the mean is unknown, estimate it with $\bar u$ and compute $s=\sqrt{\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^n(u_i-\bar u)^2}$. This differs from fixing the mean at zero and calculating a second moment around zero. If daily variances are approximately additive—daily variance is representative and cross-day return covariances are negligible—then $\sigma_{\text{annual}}=s\sqrt{252}$ under the 252-trading-day convention. Otherwise, square-root-of-time scaling is only an approximation.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**作为预测时的假设：**历史波动率作为事后测度不需要假设未来等于过去。若把它作为朴素预测，则假设过去窗口对未来仍有代表性；当波动率快速变化或出现制度切换时，这一预测用途可能失效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Assumption when used as a forecast:** Historical volatility as an ex-post measure does not assume that the future equals the past. A naive forecast based on it assumes that the selected past window remains representative, which may fail during rapid volatility changes or regime shifts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.3.2隐含波动率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3.2 [[隐含波动率|Implied volatility]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**概念：**[[隐含波动率]]（Implied Volatility, IV）是从期权市场价格反推出的、依赖所选定价模型的波动率参数。固定标的资产价格$S$、执行价$K$、[[无风险利率口径|无风险利率]]$r$、到期时间$T$、股息或持有成本等其他输入后，IV就是使模型理论价格等于观察到的市场价格的$\sigma$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Concept:** [[隐含波动率|Implied volatility]] (IV) is a model-dependent volatility parameter backed out from an option's market price. After fixing the underlying price $S$, strike $K$, [[无风险利率口径|risk-free rate]] $r$, time to maturity $T$, dividends or carry, and the model's other inputs, IV is the value of $\sigma$ that makes the model price equal the observed market price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**求解方法：**根据期权的实际市场价格，利用定价公式反推波动率：
- 将标的资产现价$S$、行权价$K$、剩余到期时间$T$、无风险利率$r$以及股息或持有成本等输入代入选定的期权定价模型。用数值求根调整$\sigma$，直到模型价格与市场价格一致。
- 对到期前、其他输入固定且vega为正的标准普通期权，如果市场价格位于模型可达到的无套利界内，模型价格随$\sigma$严格上升，因而通常有唯一的隐含波动率。超出可达价格区间时可能无解；其他复杂支付结构也可能不满足唯一性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Solution method:**
- Insert $S$, $K$, $T$, the [[无风险利率口径|risk-free rate]] $r$, dividends or carry, and the other inputs into the chosen pricing model. Use numerical root-finding to vary $\sigma$ until the model value matches the market option price.
- For a standard vanilla option before expiry, with the other inputs fixed and positive vega, the model price is strictly increasing in $\sigma$. An admissible price inside the model's no-arbitrage bounds therefore normally gives a unique IV; an out-of-range price may give no solution, and a more complex payoff need not give a unique one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**隐含波动率的意义：**隐含波动率总结了所选风险中性定价模型下，期权价格对未来不确定性的定价。它受执行价、到期日、模型和波动风险溢价影响，因此不是未来真实世界实现波动率的无偏预测。不同执行价和到期日的期权往往有不同IV，从而形成**波动率微笑或偏斜**与**波动率期限结构**。VIX不是某一只标普500期权的IV，也不是若干IV的平均：Cboe直接聚合多个执行价的SPX看涨、看跌期权中间报价，先计算近期限与次近期限的方差，再插值得到固定30日的年化预期波动率；它常被称为“恐慌指数”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Meaning:** Implied volatility summarizes how option prices value future uncertainty within the chosen risk-neutral pricing model. Strike, maturity, model choice, and the volatility risk premium all affect IV, so it is not an unbiased forecast of future realized volatility under the real-world measure. IV differs across strikes and maturities, producing a **volatility smile or skew** and a **volatility term structure**. VIX is neither one SPX option's IV nor an average of IVs: Cboe directly aggregates mid-quotes from SPX calls and puts across strikes, calculates near- and next-term variances, and interpolates them to a constant 30-day annualized expected-volatility measure. It is often called the market's fear gauge.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 1.3.3已实现波动率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3.3 [[实现波动率|Realized volatility]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**概念：**[[实现波动率|已实现波动率]]（Realized Volatility）是利用日内高频收益构造的事后路径测度。日内收益平方和给出**已实现方差**，它的平方根才是已实现波动率；两者都必须连同采样区间和目标测度一起解释，不能笼统称为不可观测的“真实波动率”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Concept:** [[实现波动率|Realized volatility]] is an ex-post path measure constructed from high-frequency intraday returns. The sum of squared intraday returns is **realized variance**; its square root is realized volatility. Both must be interpreted with their sampling grid and target measure rather than labelled simply as unobservable “true volatility.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**计算方法：**将一天内按一定高频频率（例如每5分钟）记录的价格数据计算连续小区间收益率，并将这些**高频收益率的平方**在一天内累加，得到该日的已实现方差$\sum_{i=1}^m u_{i}^{2}$（$m$为日内细分区间数）。已实现波动率即为该方差的平方根。以6.5小时的美国股票常规交易时段为例，5分钟采样约有$m=78$个区间，因此已实现波动率$=\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{78} u_i^2}$；其他市场或交易时段的$m$不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Method:** Divide the trading day into short intervals, such as five minutes, calculate each intraday return $u_i$, and sum the **squared high-frequency returns**. Realized variance is $\sum_{i=1}^m u_i^2$, and realized volatility is its square root. A 6.5-hour regular U.S. equity session has about 78 five-minute intervals, for example; other markets and sessions have different values of $m$:
$$\text{realized volatility}=\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{78}u_i^2}.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**统计性质与边界：**在没有市场微观结构噪声、采样网格最大间隔趋近于0的理想条件下，已实现方差会收敛到价格过程的**二次变差**。若价格路径连续，二次变差等于区间内的积分方差$\int \sigma_t^2\,dt$；若存在价格跳跃，还会包含跳跃幅度平方之和。因此不能无条件把有限样本的已实现方差，尤其是它的平方根，称为“无偏、一致且有效”的真实波动率估计量。

实际数据存在频率权衡。采样过疏会增加离散化误差并丢失路径信息，但误差方向不保证总是向下；采样过密则会把买卖价跳动、价格离散和异步成交等微观结构噪声计入平方和，使标准已实现方差被噪声主导，甚至失去一致性。5分钟或15分钟只是依赖资产流动性、市场结构和噪声强度的经验折中，并不存在跨资产通用的“最佳频率”；两尺度、多尺度、子采样或realized kernel等稳健估计量可以利用更高频数据并修正噪声。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Statistical properties and boundaries:** With no market-microstructure noise and a sampling mesh whose maximum interval tends to zero, realized variance converges to the price process's **quadratic variation**. For a continuous price path, quadratic variation equals integrated variance $\int\sigma_t^2\,dt$; with price jumps, it also includes the sum of squared jump sizes. It is therefore not valid to label finite-sample realized variance—and especially its square root—unconditionally as an unbiased, consistent, and efficient estimate of “true volatility.”

Actual data create a frequency trade-off. Sparse sampling increases discretization error and loses path information, but the error need not always be downward. Sampling too densely incorporates bid–ask bounce, price discreteness, asynchronous trading, and other microstructure noise, so standard realized variance can become noise-dominated and inconsistent. Five- or fifteen-minute sampling is an asset-, market-, and noise-dependent rule of thumb rather than a universal optimum. Two-scale, multiple-scale, subsampling, and realized-kernel estimators are alternative ways to use denser data while correcting for noise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**应用：**已实现波动率为波动率建模提供了一个非参数的度量，在近期学术文献和实践中大量应用于检验波动率模型、构建波动率预测，以及设计基于波动率的交易策略等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Applications:** Realized volatility supplies a nonparametric target for testing volatility models, constructing forecasts, and designing volatility-based trading strategies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 2. [[EWMA波动率|指数加权移动平均模型]]（[[EWMA波动率|EWMA]]）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. [[EWMA波动率|Exponentially weighted moving average]] (EWMA)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在历史波动率的计算中，通常对过去数据给予相等的权重（等权平均），这在波动率显著变化时可能反应迟缓。**[[EWMA波动率|指数加权移动平均]]**模型（Exponentially Weighted Moving Average, EWMA）通过对越久远的数据赋予越小的权重，实现对波动率的**动态更新**。其思想是最近的观察对当前波动率的影响更大，而远期的历史数据影响逐渐按指数衰减。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simple historical estimate weights all past observations equally and can respond slowly when volatility changes. The **[[EWMA波动率|exponentially weighted moving average]]**, or EWMA, assigns progressively less weight to older returns. Recent shocks therefore affect the current estimate more strongly, while the influence of old observations decays exponentially.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**EWMA模型原理：**令$h_t=\sigma_t^2$表示在$t-1$期信息下对$t$期日方差的一步预测，$u_{t-1}$表示上一期收益冲击。RiskMetrics的日频EWMA通常把条件均值设为0，所以用收益率近似冲击；若均值方程不为0，应使用去除条件均值后的残差。递推公式为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**EWMA principle:** Let $h_t=\sigma_t^2$ be the one-step forecast of day-$t$ variance based on information through day $t-1$, and let $u_{t-1}$ be the previous return innovation. Daily RiskMetrics EWMA usually sets the conditional mean to zero and therefore uses the return as the innovation; a nonzero mean model should use the demeaned residual. The recursion is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$ 
h_{t} = \lambda\, h_{t-1} + (1-\lambda)\,u_{t-1}^2\,,
$$

其中$0<\lambda<1$为衰减因子（“记忆系数”）。$\lambda$越大，表示对历史信息衰减越慢，模型对过去波动率变化“记忆”更长；$\lambda$越小，表示更依赖最新数据、对较久之前的数据衰减更快。反复迭代上述公式，可以展开得到：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $0<\lambda<1$ is the decay factor. A larger $\lambda$ preserves the influence of older observations for longer; a smaller $\lambda$ puts more weight on the latest return. Repeated substitution yields:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$ 
h_t = \lambda^n h_{t-n} + (1-\lambda)\sum_{i=1}^{n} \lambda^{i-1} u_{t-i}^2\,,
$$

即最近的$u_{t-1}^2$权重为$1-\lambda$，再前一日的$u_{t-2}^2$权重为$(1-\lambda)\lambda$，依此类推，权重按指数规律递减。相较于等权重的历史法，EWMA对新的波动情况更加敏感。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The most recent squared return $u_{t-1}^2$ receives weight $1-\lambda$, the preceding return $u_{t-2}^2$ receives $(1-\lambda)\lambda$, and the weights continue to decay geometrically. EWMA therefore reacts faster to new volatility than an equally weighted estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**参数选择：**衰减因子$\lambda$一般根据经验或历史拟合确定。RiskMetrics的日频数据采用$\lambda=0.94$。此时单次冲击权重的半衰期为$\ln(0.5)/\ln(0.94)\approx11.20$个交易日；若把“有效历史”定义为累计覆盖无限权重的99%，则需使$\lambda^n\leq0.01$，得到$n\approx74.43$，即约75个日收益。$1/(1-0.94)\approx16.67$只是最新观察权重的倒数或一种粗略时间尺度，不能当作唯一的“有效记忆长度”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Choosing $\lambda$:** The decay factor is selected empirically or fitted to data. RiskMetrics uses $\lambda=0.94$ for daily data. The half-life of one shock's weight is $\ln(0.5)/\ln(0.94)\approx11.20$ trading days. If “effective history” means retaining 99% of the infinite weight, $\lambda^n\leq0.01$ gives $n\approx74.43$, or about 75 daily returns. The value $1/(1-0.94)\approx16.67$ is only the reciprocal of the newest observation's weight or a rough time scale, not a unique effective-memory length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**EWMA的优点：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Advantages of EWMA:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **数据需求少：**只需保存上一期波动率估计和最新收益率，无需长期历史窗口的数据存储。
- **计算简便：**更新公式简单，易于实时计算新波动率。
- **响应快速：**能较快捕捉波动率的变化趋势，比简单历史均值方法对波动的突变反应更灵敏。
- **广泛应用：**已成为风险管理中常用模型，例如RiskMetrics体系中每日波动率预测即采用$\lambda=0.94$的EWMA模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Low data requirement:** retain only the previous variance estimate and the latest return.
- **Simple computation:** the recursion is easy to update in real time.
- **Fast response:** the estimate responds more quickly to volatility shocks than a simple historical average.
- **Wide use:** RiskMetrics, for example, uses daily EWMA forecasts with $\lambda=0.94$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

需要注意，EWMA方差递推可写成$\omega=0$、$\alpha=1-\lambda$、$\beta=\lambda$且$\alpha+\beta=1$的积分型GARCH(1,1)。它没有独立的长期方差目标，多步预测不会向某个固定方差均值回归。原因是持久性参数之和等于1，而不是因为$\lambda$“接近1”或历史权重和“接近1”；有限展开中初值与冲击权重之和恰好为1。尽管如此，EWMA因其简单和响应快而被广泛使用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
EWMA variance can be written as an integrated GARCH(1,1) recursion with $\omega=0$, $\alpha=1-\lambda$, $\beta=\lambda$, and $\alpha+\beta=1$. It has no separate long-run variance target, so multi-step forecasts do not mean-revert to a fixed variance. This follows from unit persistence, not merely from $\lambda$ being close to one; in the finite expansion, the initial-condition weight and shock weights sum exactly to one. The model remains widely used because it is simple and responsive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**解答：**根据EWMA模型公式：$h_t = 0.94\times0.0001 + (1-0.94)\times(0.02)^2$。计算得到： 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Solution:** Apply $h_t=0.94\times0.0001+(1-0.94)\times(0.02)^2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 昨日方差贡献：$0.94\times0.0001 = 0.000094$； 
- 昨日冲击贡献：$0.06\times0.0004 = 0.000024$（因为$1-\lambda=0.06$, 且$(0.02)^2=0.0004$）。 
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Previous-variance contribution: $0.94\times0.0001=0.000094$.
- Latest-shock contribution: $0.06\times0.0004=0.000024$, because $1-\lambda=0.06$ and $(0.02)^2=0.0004$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两部分相加得新的方差预测$h_t=0.000118$。取平方根得到预测的日波动率$\sigma_t=\sqrt{0.000118}\approx0.01086$，即约**1.09%**。这里$2\%$是一次已观察到的收益冲击，而不是“上一交易日的实际波动率”；由于其绝对值高于上一期预测标准差$1\%$，模型提高了下一交易日的波动率预测。若上一期收益冲击的绝对值足够小，新预测会降低。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The new variance forecast is $h_t=0.000118$. Taking its square root gives $\sigma_t=\sqrt{0.000118}\approx0.01086$, or **1.09%**. The observed 2% return is a return innovation, not the previous trading day's realized volatility. Because its magnitude exceeds the previous 1% forecast standard deviation, it raises the next trading day's volatility forecast; a sufficiently small innovation would lower it.

# 3. [[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH.canvas|条件异方差模型（ARCH、GARCH）]]

*3. Conditional heteroskedasticity models: ARCH and GARCH*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金融时间序列常常表现出**[[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH.canvas|条件异方差与波动率聚集]]**：随着时间推移，条件波动幅度并非恒定，而是高波动和低波动时期成团出现。普通[[异方差]]只说明条件方差不恒定；Engle 的条件异方差模型进一步用过去冲击的信息刻画方差动态，主要包括 ARCH 和 GARCH 系列。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial returns often exhibit **[[条件异方差：ARCH 与 GARCH.canvas|conditional heteroskedasticity and volatility clustering]]**: their conditional variance changes over time, with high- and low-volatility episodes clustering together. Ordinary [[异方差|heteroskedasticity]] only says that conditional variance is nonconstant; Engle's framework additionally models variance dynamics from past shocks through ARCH and GARCH families.

## 3.4 GARCH 的 $T$ 日平均方差与年化波动率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.4 GARCH average variance over $T$ days and its annualized volatility*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $\rho=\alpha+\beta<1$，$h_\infty=\omega/(1-\rho)$，并令 $h_1$ 表示当前信息集下第一个未来交易日的条件方差预测。未来 $T$ 个交易日的**精确离散平均方差预测**为：

$$
\bar h_T
=\frac{1}{T}\sum_{k=1}^{T}E[h_k\mid\mathcal F_0]
=h_\infty+(h_1-h_\infty)\frac{1-\rho^T}{T(1-\rho)}.
$$

按252个交易日的报价约定，其年化平方根为：

$$
\sigma_P(T)=\sqrt{252\,\bar h_T}.
$$

这里的下标 $P$ 提醒我们：如果 GARCH 是用历史收益估计的，这通常是**真实世界概率测度下的预测**。它可以用于风险预测，或在额外假设下作为常波动率公式的粗略代入值；但它不会自动等于期权定价所需的风险中性波动率。无套利期权定价一般还需要风险中性动态、波动风险溢价或直接用期权价格校准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\rho=\alpha+\beta<1$, let $h_\infty=\omega/(1-\rho)$, and let $h_1$ be the conditional-variance forecast for the first future trading day under the current information set. The formula above is the exact discrete forecast of average variance over the next $T$ trading days. Under the 252-trading-day quotation convention, its annualized square root is $\sigma_P(T)=\sqrt{252\bar h_T}$.

The subscript $P$ is a reminder that a GARCH model estimated from historical returns normally produces a **real-world-measure forecast**. It is useful for risk forecasting and may serve as a rough plug-in for a constant-volatility formula only under additional assumptions; it is not automatically the risk-neutral volatility required for arbitrage-free option pricing. That generally requires risk-neutral dynamics, a volatility-risk premium, or direct calibration to option prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $h_\infty$：长期无条件日方差。
- $h_1-h_\infty$：第一个未来日方差预测相对长期方差的偏离。
- $\frac{1-\rho^T}{T(1-\rho)}$：这段偏离在 $T$ 个预测日上的平均权重；$T$ 增大时趋近于0。
- 因而 $T\to\infty$ 时，$\bar h_T\to h_\infty$，$\sigma_P(T)\to\sqrt{252h_\infty}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $h_\infty$ is the long-run unconditional daily variance.
- $h_1-h_\infty$ is the first future variance forecast's deviation from that level.
- $\frac{1-\rho^T}{T(1-\rho)}$ is the average weight on that deviation across the $T$ forecast days and tends to zero as $T$ grows.
- Hence $\bar h_T\to h_\infty$ and $\sigma_P(T)\to\sqrt{252h_\infty}$ as $T\to\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 例如 $\omega=0.000002$、$\alpha=0.03$、$\beta=0.95$，则 $\rho=0.98$、$h_\infty=0.0001$。
- 若 $h_1=0.000225$（第一个未来日的预测波动率为 $1.5\%$）且 $T=20$，直接代入离散公式即可得到20日预测平均方差及其年化平方根。

# 4.波动率的统计特征（厚尾分布与幂律）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Statistical properties of volatility: heavy tails and power laws*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.1 厚尾分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.1 heavy-tailed distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金融资产的收益率分布常偏离正态分布，呈现出**“高峰厚尾”**特征：相对于正态分布，实际分布在均值处更尖峭、在两侧尾部有更大的概率质量。简言之，小波动和极端大波动发生的频率都比正态假设下预期的多。这一厚尾特性意味着金融市场中出现极端事件（如股价暴跌或飙升）的可能性被低估，如果按照正态分布计算风险可能导致严重误判。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial returns often depart from normality and exhibit a **leptokurtic, heavy-tailed** distribution. Relative to a normal distribution, the empirical distribution is more concentrated near the mean and assigns more probability to extreme tails. Both very small moves and very large moves occur more often than normality predicts. A normal risk model can therefore severely understate the frequency of crashes and surges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**厚尾分布实例：**以某汇率日收益率为例，统计其落在多个标准差范围以外的频率，与正态分布预测对比，得到例如下表所示结果：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Heavy-tail example:** Compare the observed fraction of daily exchange-rate returns lying more than several standard deviations from the mean with the corresponding normal probabilities:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> 超过$n$倍标准差的概率（%）—— **现实数据** vs **正态理论**  
> 超过1倍标准差： 23.32% vs 31.73%  
> 超过2倍标准差： 4.67% vs 4.55%  
> 超过3倍标准差： 1.30% vs 0.27%  
> 超过4倍标准差： 0.49% vs 0.01%  
> 超过5倍标准差： 0.24% vs 0.00%  
> ...  
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Probability of exceeding $n$ standard deviations: **observed data** versus **normal model**
> More than 1 standard deviation: 23.32% versus 31.73%
> More than 2 standard deviations: 4.67% versus 4.55%
> More than 3 standard deviations: 1.30% versus 0.27%
> More than 4 standard deviations: 0.49% versus 0.01%
> More than 5 standard deviations: 0.24% versus approximately 0.00%
> ...
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以看到，实际收益超过3倍标准差的频率(1.30%)远高于正态分布预测的0.27%，差距随着阈值增大而愈发明显（例如>4$\sigma$实际为0.49%，正态仅0.01%）。**厚尾特征**说明了市场中**极端事件发生的概率被显著低估**：金融资产价格剧烈波动（比如单日暴跌超过4-5个标准差）的情形虽罕见，但比正态模型预期的要常见得多。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The observed frequency beyond three standard deviations is 1.30%, far above the normal prediction of 0.27%; the gap grows at more extreme thresholds. Beyond $4\sigma$, for example, the observed fraction is 0.49% versus 0.01% under normality. **Heavy tails** mean that severe market moves are rare but much more common than a normal model predicts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**高峰特征**则对应分布曲线在均值附近比正态分布更陡峭，这与厚尾一起反映出分布的尖峰厚尾性质（较高的峭度，即**超额峰度**为正）。许多市场变量（股票收益率、汇率变动等）都被实证发现具有高峰厚尾特征。这种特性促使风险管理者在评估极端风险（如VaR、[[ES定义|ES]]）时要采用考虑厚尾的模型，否则可能低估风险敞口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **sharp central peak** accompanies the heavy tails and produces a leptokurtic distribution with positive **excess kurtosis**. Stock returns and exchange-rate changes commonly exhibit this pattern. Tail-sensitive models are therefore important when measuring risks such as VaR and ES; otherwise exposure to extreme events may be understated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**厚尾的影响及应对：**厚尾分布意味着在投资和风险管理中，应给予极端事件更高的重视。例如，定价深度虚值的期权时，厚尾会提高这些远端事件的发生机率，从而增加深度虚值看涨看跌期权的理论价值（因为极端行情下才有机会行权）。一些投资者会通过买入深度虚值期权来博取黑天鹅事件（极端事件）发生时的巨额回报；相反，如果卖出此类期权则需充分考虑尾部风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Implications and response:** Heavy tails raise the model value of deep out-of-the-money calls and puts because the extreme states in which they pay off are more likely. Buying such options can provide exposure to a black-swan event; selling them creates substantial tail risk that must be managed explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4.2 幂律分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4.2 power-law distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

针对金融变量的厚尾特征，正态分布可能并不是最佳假设。**幂律分布**提供了另一种描述重尾行为的方法。一般地，幂律尾分布可以表示为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The normal distribution may be a poor model for heavy-tailed financial variables. A **power-law distribution** provides one alternative description. Its upper tail can be written:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$ 
\Pr(X > x) = K\,x^{-\alpha}\,,
$$

其中$\alpha>0$称为**幂指数**或尾指数，$K$为正常化常数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\alpha>0$ is the **power exponent**, or tail index, and $K$ is a normalizing constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

就是一种用来描述厚尾的分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is one way to represent a heavy-tailed distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**log-log检验：**幂律分布的一个性质是，在对数坐标下绘制尾部分布（$\ln P(X>x)$ vs $\ln x$），应近似呈线性关系，斜率即为$-\alpha$。对此可以对金融数据的尾部进行log-log回归检验，以判断是否符合幂律。对数检验常发现金融收益尾部接近线性下降，支持幂律假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Log–log test:** If $\Pr(X>x)=Kx^{-\alpha}$, then a plot of $\ln\Pr(X>x)$ against $\ln x$ should be approximately linear with slope $-\alpha$. A log–log regression on the empirical tail can therefore test whether power-law behavior is plausible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!question] 
>**例题：**假定根据经验观察，某金融变量收益绝对值的尾部分布满足幂律衰减，其幂指数$\alpha=3$。已知该变量波动幅度超过10的概率约为$5\%$（即$\Pr(X>10)=0.05$）。请估计该变量波动幅度超过20和超过30的概率各为多少。
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Example:** Suppose the tail of the absolute return on a financial variable follows a power law with $\alpha=3$. The probability that its magnitude exceeds 10 is 5%, so $\Pr(X>10)=0.05$. Estimate the probabilities of magnitudes above 20 and 30.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example]
> **解答：**根据幂律尾分布假设：$\Pr(X>x) = K x^{-3}$。由已知条件，当$x=10$时有 
> $$0.05 = K \times 10^{-3}\,,$$ 
> 据此解得$K = 0.05 \times 10^3 = 50$。  
> - 对于$x=20$：$\Pr(X>20) = 50 \times 20^{-3} = 50 \times \frac{1}{8000} = 0.00625$，即概率约为**0.625%**。  
> - 对于$x=30$：$\Pr(X>30) = 50 \times 30^{-3} = 50 \times \frac{1}{27000} \approx 0.00185$，即概率约为**0.185%**。  
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>**Solution:** Assume $\Pr(X>x)=Kx^{-3}$. Because $0.05=K10^{-3}$, $K=0.05\times10^3=50$.
>- $\Pr(X>20)=50\times20^{-3}=50/8000=0.00625$, or **0.625%**.
>- $\Pr(X>30)=50\times30^{-3}=50/27000\approx0.00185$, or **0.185%**.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 本题的教材式近似，是先求**未来持有期内的预测平均方差**，再开平方，而不是直接使用当前单日波动率。题目给出的 $h_t$ 被视为从现在起第一个持有期日的方差；按在 $\mathcal F_{t-1}$ 已知 $h_t$ 的记号，令 $\rho=\alpha+\beta$：
    $$
    \overline{h}_{t,T} = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{k=0}^{T-1} E_{t-1}[h_{t+k}]
    =h_\infty+(h_t-h_\infty)\frac{1-\rho^T}{T(1-\rho)}.
    $$
    相应的日尺度等效波动率为
    $$
    \overline{\sigma}_{t,T}=\sqrt{\overline h_{t,T}},
    \qquad
    \overline{\sigma}^{\mathrm{ann}}_{t,T}=\sqrt{252\,\overline h_{t,T}}.
    $$
- 代入 $h_t=0.000225$、$h_\infty=0.0001$、$\rho=0.98$：

| $T$ | 预测平均日方差 $\overline h_{t,T}$ | 日尺度等效波动率 | 按252日年化 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 20 | 0.000203873 | 1.42784% | 22.6662% |
| 40 | 0.000186609 | 1.36605% | 21.6854% |
| 60 | 0.000173172 | 1.31595% | 20.8900% |

- 这仍是历史 GARCH 在真实世界概率测度下的预测。只有额外假设风险中性与真实世界动态一致、忽略波动风险溢价，并接受常波动率代入近似时，才能把它直接放进 Black–Scholes；一般的期权定价应使用风险中性模型或市场期权价格校准出的波动率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The textbook plug-in first forecasts **average variance over the option's life** and then takes its square root, rather than inserting current one-day volatility. The supplied $h_t$ is treated as the variance for the first holding-period day. Under the convention that it is known at $\mathcal F_{t-1}$, the closed form above uses $\rho=\alpha+\beta$ and averages $h_t,\ldots,h_{t+T-1}$, with no extra leading factor of $\rho$. The table reports the resulting daily-equivalent volatility and its 252-day annualization.
- This is still a real-world-measure forecast from a historical GARCH model. It can be inserted directly into Black–Scholes only with additional assumptions equating real-world and risk-neutral variance dynamics, ignoring the volatility-risk premium, and accepting a constant-volatility plug-in approximation. Option pricing generally requires risk-neutral dynamics or calibration to option prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 10.23

>[!question] 
>投资组合在1个月后损失超出1000万美元的概率大约为5%:
>(a)假定投资组合价值变化服从正态分布，均值为0，1个月展望期置信度99%的VaR为多少?
>(b)在 a=3的幂律假设前提下，1个月展望期置信度99%的VaR为多少?
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>The probability that the portfolio loses more than USD 10 million over one month is approximately 5%.
>(a) Assuming a zero-mean normal change in portfolio value, what is one-month 99% VaR?
>(b) Assuming a power-law tail with exponent $a=3$, what is one-month 99% VaR?
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**（a）正态分布下，1个月99%VaR为多少？**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**(a) One-month 99% VaR under normality**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**已知：**
- 1个月后损失超过$1,000$万美元的概率约$5\%$
- 损失$L\sim N(0,\sigma^2)$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Given:**
- $\Pr(L>\text{USD 10 million})=5\%$
- $L\sim N(0,\sigma^2)$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

已知 $P(L>1,000)=5\%$，说明 $1,000$ 万美元是 **95% 分位点**（右尾5%），所以 $\sigma$ 可以反解出来：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $P(L>1000)=5\%$, where monetary amounts are measured in units of USD 10,000, the threshold $1000$—USD 10 million—is the **95th percentile**. Therefore $\sigma$ can be recovered from:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(L > 1,000) = 5\%
\Rightarrow
P(L \le 1,000) = 95\%
\Rightarrow
\frac{1,000 - 0}{\sigma} = z_{0.95} = 1.645
$$
所以
$$
\sigma = \frac{1,000}{1.645} \approx 607.9
$$
**99%置信度的VaR:**
$$
VaR_{99\%} = z_{0.99} \cdot \sigma = 2.326 \times 607.9 \approx 1,414\ \text{万美元}
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The implied monthly standard deviation is about USD 6.079 million. Therefore, **99% VaR is approximately USD 14.14 million**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

 **（b）在$a=3$的幂律假设下，1个月99%VaR为多少？**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**(b) One-month 99% VaR under a power-law tail with $a=3$**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
 
题设没给$L_{min}$，我们需要用给定点推断参数。
已知
- $P(L > 1,000) = 5\%$
- $a = 3$
即
$$
P(L>1,000) = \left(\frac{1,000}{L_{min}}\right)^{-3} = 0.05
\implies
\frac{1,000}{L_{min}} = 0.05^{-1/3}
$$
$\quad$解得
$$
0.05^{-1/3} = (20)^{1/3} \approx 2.714
$$
所以
$$
L_{min} = \frac{1,000}{2.714} \approx 368.4
$$
**99%分位点（$p=0.99$）的VaR：**
$$
VaR_{99\%} = L_{min}\cdot(1-0.99)^{-1/3}
= 368.4 \cdot (0.01)^{-1/3}
= 368.4 \cdot 4.6416
\approx 1,709\ \text{万美元}
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The question does not give $L_{\min}$, so infer it from $P(L>1000)=5\%$ and $a=3$. In units of USD 10,000, this gives $L_{\min}\approx368.4$. The 99th-percentile tail loss is about $1709$ units, so **99% VaR is approximately USD 17.09 million**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
