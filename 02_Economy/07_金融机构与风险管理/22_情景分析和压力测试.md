# **情境分析和压力测试**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Scenario Analysis and Stress Testing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **核心概念：** [[压力测试与逆向压力测试.canvas|情境分析与压力测试]]用于评估严重但合理、且可能未被既有 VaR/[[ES定义|ES]] 模型充分覆盖的情形。情景本身没有固定概率，除非另行建立概率模型。VaR（[[VaR定义|风险价值]]）是损失分布的$\alpha$分位数，常写作
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Core idea:** [[压力测试与逆向压力测试.canvas|Scenario analysis and stress testing]] evaluate how a financial institution or portfolio behaves under severe but plausible conditions that may be poorly represented in conventional VaR and ES models. A scenario has no fixed probability unless a separate probability model supplies one. VaR is the $\alpha$-quantile of the loss distribution, commonly written as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    $$\text{VaR}_\alpha(L)=\inf{x: P(L\le x)\ge\alpha},$$_
    
    _而预期亏损（ES）用完整上尾分位积分定义：_
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expected shortfall (ES) is defined by the integrated upper-tail quantile:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    _$$\text{ES}_\alpha(L)=\frac{1}{1-\alpha}\int_\alpha^1 q_u(L)\,du.$$ _
    
    在分位点没有概率质量等条件下，ES 才可写成严格超过相应 VaR 后的条件均值；一般离散分布不能直接套用该条件式。ES 比 VaR 更关注尾部严重度，并满足次可加性等一致风险度量性质。[[压力测试方法|压力测试]]可在不先赋予情景概率的情况下检查分布模型未覆盖的脆弱性；只有另建概率模型时，情景才进入概率分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ES focuses on the severity of tail loss and, unlike VaR in general, is a coherent risk measure satisfying subadditivity. Stress testing therefore complements VaR and ES by specifying extreme scenarios directly and examining risks not captured by the estimated distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **压力测试类型：** [[压力测试方法|常见方法]]包括单变量敏感性分析、多变量情景分析、历史情景重现、全行压力测试和 [[逆向压力测试]]。它们回答的问题、覆盖层级和输出不同，应按测试目标与重大风险选择，不能互相替代。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Types of stress test:** Common forms include single-factor sensitivity tests, multivariable scenario analysis, replay of historical crises, enterprise-wide tests, and reverse stress tests. A reverse stress test starts from a predefined material adverse outcome—such as a regulatory-ratio breach, illiquidity, a large loss, or a threat to viability—and searches for plausible scenarios capable of producing it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

## **VaR与预期亏损（ES）的关系**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Relationship Between VaR and Expected Shortfall (ES)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **定义对比：** VaR 在给定置信水平 $\alpha$ 下给出潜在损失的分位点；ES 则把从 $\alpha$ 到 1 的损失分位数取平均，即 $\text{ES}_\alpha=(1-\alpha)^{-1}\int_\alpha^1 q_u(L)\,du$。只有在相应 VaR 分位点没有概率质量等条件下，ES 才等于严格超过 VaR 后的条件平均损失。一般有 $\text{ES}_\alpha\ge \text{VaR}_\alpha$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Definitions:** At confidence level $\alpha$, VaR is a loss quantile. ES averages the loss quantile function over the upper tail: $\text{ES}_\alpha=(1-\alpha)^{-1}\int_\alpha^1 q_u(L)\,du$. Only under conditions such as no probability mass at the VaR quantile can it be written as the conditional mean of losses strictly exceeding VaR. Ordinarily $\text{ES}_\alpha\ge\text{VaR}_\alpha$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **一致性：** ES是一个**相干风险度量**，满足次可加性等条件，比VaR更好地反映极端损失 。实务中，巴塞尔协议Ⅲ要求市场风险资本计量采用ES而非VaR。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Coherence:** ES is a **coherent risk measure**, including subadditivity, and gives more information about extreme losses. The Basel market-risk framework moved from VaR to ES under the Fundamental Review of the Trading Book.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

> **模拟题 1（简答题）：** 比较VaR与ES两种风险度量的不同。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 1:** Compare VaR and ES.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** VaR 是给定置信水平下的损失分位点阈值，不是最大可能损失，也不说明阈值以外会亏多少；ES 汇总同一置信水平上的完整上尾损失。ES 满足次可加性等一致风险度量性质，但仍依赖数据和模型。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** VaR gives a loss quantile at a chosen confidence level but says nothing about the magnitude of losses beyond that threshold. ES averages the loss quantile function over the upper tail; only when the VaR quantile has no probability mass, among other regularity conditions, does this equal the conditional mean of losses strictly exceeding VaR. ES is coherent, including subadditivity, and therefore captures tail severity more effectively.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

## **[[压力测试方法|单变量压力测试]]**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Single-Factor Stress Tests*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 定义
> ** 假设单一市场变量（如利率、股票指数、汇率等）发生极端变化，其它变量暂不变或按常规假设不变。常用例子：收益率曲线平行上移/下移100个基点、某资产隐含波动率±50%、股票指数±10%、主要货币汇率±6%等 。
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>Assume one market variable—an interest rate, equity index, or exchange rate—moves dramatically while others remain fixed or follow simple assumptions. Examples include a 100 bp parallel yield-curve shift, a ±50% move in implied volatility, a ±10% equity-index move, or a ±6% move in a major exchange rate.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **效应评估：** 小幅变化可用Delta近似，大幅变化要结合Delta和Gamma来估计收益影响：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Estimating the effect:** A small move may be approximated with Delta; a larger move may require both Delta and Gamma:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    $$\Delta V\approx \Delta\times \Delta x + \frac{1}{2}\Gamma (\Delta x)^2,$$
    
    其中$\Delta$、$\Gamma$分别为对资产价格或利率的敏感度 。但在情景变化非常大时，线性/二次近似可能不可靠 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\Delta$ and $\Gamma$ are sensitivities to the asset price or rate. For very large scenario moves, even a quadratic approximation may be unreliable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **实践要点：** 单变量测试是识别核心风险的一步，但应谨防孤立变量变动低估风险；此外，还要注意在极端时波动率往往飙升，对冲成本增加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Practical point:** A single-factor test helps identify dominant exposures, but isolating one factor can understate risk. Extreme markets also tend to bring rising volatility and more expensive hedging.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

> **模拟题 2（计算题）：** 某投资组合对一市场变量的$\Delta=1000$（损益每单位变动的值），$\Gamma=50$，市场变量意外下跌10%。估算组合损失。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 2:** A portfolio has $\Delta=1000$ and $\Gamma=50$ with respect to one market variable. The variable unexpectedly falls by 10%. Estimate portfolio loss.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** 近似损失为 $\Delta\times(-0.10)+\frac{1}{2}\Gamma(-0.10)^2=1000\times(-0.10)+0.5\times50\times0.01=-100+0.25=-99.75$ （单位与$\Delta$相同）。因变动较大，实际损失可能更高（$\Delta$–$\Gamma$近似可能低估风险） 。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Approximate P&L is $\Delta\times(-0.10)+\frac{1}{2}\Gamma(-0.10)^2=1000\times(-0.10)+0.5\times50\times0.01=-100+0.25=-99.75$ in the units of Delta. Because the move is large, actual loss can differ materially; the Delta–Gamma approximation may understate risk.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

> **模拟题 3（简答题）：** 为什么在很大幅度的市场变动下，只使用$\Delta$或$\Delta+\Gamma$近似可能不准确？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 3:** Why can a Delta or Delta-plus-Gamma approximation become inaccurate under a very large market move?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** 因为大幅变动可能使得投资组合的非线性特征凸显，$\Delta$–$\Gamma$近似忽略更高阶的效应。当情景变动非常大时，线性/二次近似都可能产生显著偏差 。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Large moves make nonlinear portfolio features important. Delta–Gamma retains only first- and second-order terms and omits higher-order effects. Both linear and quadratic approximations can therefore have substantial error far from the expansion point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

## **[[压力情景一致性|多变量情景分析]]**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Multivariable Scenario Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **联合冲击：** 当一个市场变量剧烈变化时，其他变量也往往随之波动。因此需要生成多个风险因子同时变化的情景。例如，有机构将所有市场变量按照1987年10月19日美股崩盘（标普500指数单日下跌22.3σ）时的百分比变化作为模拟情景 。类似地，可考虑2001年9月11日、2008年9月15日雷曼破产等历史极值事件的市场变动 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Joint shocks:** A large move in one variable is normally accompanied by moves in others, so a scenario should change several risk factors together. One approach applies the percentage changes observed on 19 October 1987 across current positions. The slide describes the S&P 500 move as 22.3 standard deviations. Other historical anchors include 11 September 2001 and Lehman Brothers' failure on 15 September 2008.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **历史极端情景：** 历史上也有其他市场崩盘或异常波动的日子，如1992年4月10日英国十年期国债收益率变动8.7σ 、2014年下半年原油价格暴跌等。机构可直接使用这些历史一天、数日或数周的变化数据构造场景，并视情况对变动幅度进行放大（如放大3–5倍） 。放大时要注意：单纯放大并不改变相关性，但现实中压力下资产相关性通常走高 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Historical extremes:** Other examples in the slides include an 8.7-standard-deviation move in the UK ten-year government-bond yield on 10 April 1992 and the oil-price collapse in the second half of 2014. A firm can replay one-day, multi-day, or multi-week historical changes and can scale them, for example by three to five times. Simple scaling preserves the historical correlation pattern, however, whereas actual stressed correlations often rise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **相关性变化：** 应考虑危机时相关性的改变。很多情景仅对单日价格波动建模，但信用和流动性等风险可能持续更长时间且联动性更强。举例：1998年俄罗斯债务违约引发安全资产转移，所有持有高风险证券的机构同时抛售，加剧了市场崩盘 。放大历史变动时务必注意，关联交易的连锁效应可能导致损失超过简单叠加预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Changing dependence:** Crisis dependence should be modelled explicitly. A one-day price scenario may be too short for credit and liquidity stress, which can persist and reinforce one another. After Russia's 1998 default, a flight to safety led many holders of risky securities to sell simultaneously, deepening the market decline. Feedback among crowded positions can make loss exceed a simple sum of isolated shocks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

> **模拟题 4（简答题）：** 设计一个基于历史市场极端事件的情景，并说明其适用性和局限性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 4:** Design a scenario based on a historical market extreme and explain its usefulness and limitations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** 例如，可选用1987年10月19日崩盘情景，将当日各市场（股票、债券、汇率等）的变化幅度应用到当前投资组合上 。优点是基于真实极端历史；局限在于未必符合当前市场结构（相关性、市场深度等）且无法反映历史未出现的新型风险  。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** One could replay 19 October 1987 by applying that day's equity, bond, and exchange-rate moves to today's portfolio. It is grounded in a real extreme event, but today's market structure, correlations, and depth differ, and it cannot represent risks that have never appeared in the historical record.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

## **管理层生成压力情景**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Management-Generated Stress Scenarios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **情景头脑风暴：** 一种常用方法是由公司高管组成压力测试委员会，定期召开会议，通过头脑风暴等方式讨论：“市场可能出现哪些意料之外的情景？” 。研究发现，**3–5人**的多元化背景团队效果较好，应鼓励成员跳出各自专业领域，全面审视宏观经济与市场风险 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Scenario brainstorming:** A committee of senior managers can meet regularly to ask what unexpected market conditions could occur. The note cites research favouring a small group of **three to five people** with diverse backgrounds and encourages members to look beyond their own specialisms when considering macroeconomic and market risks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **高管参与的重要性：** 高级管理层参与制定情景有助于提高情景的合理性和权重。例如，2005–2006年时许多人已觉察美国住房泡沫，但直到危机前夕，如果压力测试委员会提前考虑20–30%的全国房价下跌情景，就能更合理地评估风险 。公司决策层应将压力测试视为战略决策的依据，管理层参与情景设计有助于强化对压力测试结果的重视 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Why management participation matters:** Senior participation gives scenarios credibility and decision-making weight. In 2005–06, many observers recognised signs of a U.S. housing bubble. A committee that had considered a nationwide 20–30% house-price decline could have assessed its exposures more realistically. Stress testing should inform strategic decisions, and management involvement makes the results harder to ignore.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

> **模拟题 5（简答题）：** 为什么建议让高层管理人员参与压力测试情景的制定？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 5:** Why should senior managers participate in designing stress scenarios?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** 高层参与可确保压力测试得到重视和采纳，帮助他们理解极端风险并将其纳入战略决策 。高管提出的情景往往基于历史事实并结合当前环境，增强了情景的合理性和执行力度 。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Participation helps ensure that results are understood, taken seriously, and incorporated into strategy. Senior managers can combine historical experience with the current business environment, making scenarios more credible and actionable.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

> **模拟题 6（简答题）：** 压力测试委员会的有效组成应具备哪些特点？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 6:** What makes an effective stress-testing committee?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** 研究表明，委员**3–5人**为宜，成员背景应多样化（宏观、市场、信用等不同领域），并保持畅通的沟通渠道，鼓励跨专业思考以完整覆盖宏观风险 。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The note recommends **three to five members**, varied expertise across macro, market, and credit risk, open communication, and encouragement of cross-disciplinary thinking so that important macro-financial risks are not omitted.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

## **[[压力传导映射|条件压力测试：核心变量与周边变量]]**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditional Stress Testing: Core and Peripheral Variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **核心变量 vs 周边变量：** 管理层制定的情景往往只涉及部分核心市场变量（如利率、股指等）的剧烈变动，可能忽略其他相关的“周边”变量（如信用利差、波动率等） 。一种做法是将周边变量变化暂定为0，这不够现实。更完善的方法是建立周边变量对核心变量的回归关系。即假定周边变量$Y$对核心变量$X$满足$Y=a+bX$，在情景中根据$X$的变化预测$Y$的变化，这称为**条件压力测试** 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Core versus peripheral variables:** A management scenario often specifies only large moves in a few core factors such as rates and equity indices while ignoring related variables such as credit spreads and volatility. Setting every peripheral move to zero is unrealistic. A conditional stress test can instead estimate a relationship such as $Y=a+bX$ and use the scenario move in core factor $X$ to predict peripheral factor $Y$. This captures historical statistical dependence, though the regression alone does not establish causality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **破箭情景（Broken Arrow）：** Kim和Finger提出的“破箭压力测试”假设在压力状态下，变量之间的相关性不是以历史平均值为基准，而以受到冲击时的状态为基准 。这意味着在极端情景下，相关系数可能更高，风险传染更严重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Broken Arrow scenario:** The Broken Arrow stress test associated with Kim and Finger assumes that dependence in a stressed state should reflect relationships observed under stress rather than historical averages. Extreme scenarios can therefore use higher correlations and stronger contagion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

> **模拟题 7（简答题）：** 何谓条件压力测试？与普通场景测试有何不同？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 7:** What is conditional stress testing, and how does it differ from an ordinary scenario test?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** 条件压力测试将核心变量置于剧烈变化情景后，通过回归等方法估计相关周边变量的变化。它比把周边变量固定为零更能表达历史统计依赖，但回归关系本身不证明因果，也可能在压力状态下失效；因此仍需检验叙事、符号和变量路径是否内部一致。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** After shocking core variables, conditional stress testing uses regressions or related models to infer changes in peripheral variables. Unlike a test that shocks core factors in isolation, it incorporates their estimated dependence and can produce a more internally consistent scenario.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

> **模拟题 8（简答题）：** 说明“破箭压力测试”假设的含义。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 8:** Explain the assumption behind a Broken Arrow stress test.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** “破箭”情景假设压力状态下各变量的相关性基于受压时的关系，而非历史平均水平 。也就是说，当市场遭遇极端冲击时，资产间相关性通常上升，破箭测试用更高的相关性来估计损失。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Broken Arrow uses relationships calibrated to stressed conditions rather than long-run average correlations. Because asset correlations often rise during severe shocks, the test uses stronger stressed dependence when estimating loss.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

## **动态影响和连锁效应**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Dynamic Effects and Feedback*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **连锁效应（Knock-on Effect）：** 当二轮反馈对测试目标和范围重大时，[[压力影响估计|压力影响]]不能停在单家机构的首轮损失。例如，共同持仓与同时平仓可能压低价格、扩大信用利差并收紧流动性，形成 [[挤兑火售反馈|火售和去杠杆反馈]]。并非每个组合级敏感性测试都必须建模全系统反馈；是否纳入取决于对象层级、期限和用途。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Knock-on effects:** When financial-system feedback is material to the test's objective and scope, impact estimation should go beyond one institution's first-round loss. During the 2007 U.S. housing collapse, simultaneous losses led to a flight to safety, funding shortages, and wider credit spreads. During the 1998 LTCM episode, crowded hedge-fund strategies led many investors to unwind at once, amplifying price moves. This feedback need not be modelled in every portfolio-level sensitivity test; inclusion depends on the test's level, horizon, and intended use.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **动态对冲成本：** 投资组合的对冲活动在剧烈市场波动下也有重要影响。例如，一个金融机构对某标的做卖出期权并保持Δ中性平衡，当标的价格大幅波动且波动率飙升时，机构需频繁调整头寸以重新Δ中性，对冲成本急剧上升，进一步加大损失 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Dynamic hedging cost:** Hedging itself can matter in a stressed market. An institution short options and maintaining Delta neutrality must trade repeatedly after large underlying-price moves. When volatility and bid–ask spreads jump, rebalancing becomes expensive and can amplify loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

> **模拟题 9（简答题）：** 请举例说明金融市场中的连锁效应，并解释其为何在压力测试中需要考虑。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 9:** Give an example of a market knock-on effect and explain why a stress test should include it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** 如1998年LTCM崩盘，市场风险扩大导致多个机构同时平仓，从而引发流动性恶化和价格急剧下跌 。类似地，2007年房市崩盘后，大量机构同时抛售高风险资产，加剧了市场危机 。这些现象说明风险会通过市场紧密关联传染；当这种二轮反馈对既定目标和范围重大时，压力测试应将其纳入，而不是把它列为每一种压力测试的固定要求。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** During the LTCM crisis, expanding losses drove several institutions to unwind similar positions at the same time, worsening liquidity and price declines. Similar forced selling followed the 2007 housing collapse. These examples show how tightly connected positions transmit and amplify shocks beyond first-round loss.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

> **模拟题 10（简答题）：** 解释在极端市场波动下，为什么对冲成本会增加？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 10:** Why does hedging become more expensive during extreme market volatility?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** 因为市场波动率上升（尤其波动率含义的隐含波动率），Δ对冲需要更频繁地交易标的资产，交易量和价差扩大导致对冲成本提高 。也就是标的剧烈波动时，要保持Δ中性需要投入更高的成本。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Higher realised and implied volatility requires more frequent Delta rebalancing, while trading volume, market impact, and bid–ask spreads rise. Maintaining Delta neutrality therefore consumes more liquidity and incurs greater transaction cost.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **[[压力管理行动|管理行动]]与[[压力结果治理|结果使用]]**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Management actions and use of results*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是否计入管理行动由测试设计决定。若允许计入，行动必须在压力下仍可执行，并受到时点、授权、市场容量、抵押品、成本和其他机构同时行动的约束；不能一边假设市场失灵，一边假设机构能无成本完美对冲。测试结果还应连同关键假设和局限接受可信质疑，并按测试目标反馈到风险偏好、限额、资本、流动性、应急和战略决策。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether management actions are recognised depends on the exercise design. If recognised, they must remain executable under stress and respect timing, authority, market capacity, collateral, cost, and simultaneous action by other firms. A test cannot assume market dysfunction while also granting costless perfect hedging. Results, key assumptions, and limitations should receive credible challenge and feed into the decisions specified by the test objective.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


## **[[逆向压力测试]]**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Reverse Stress Testing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!note] 定义
> **逆向压力测试（Reverse Stress Testing）从预先定义的重大不利结果出发，反向寻找可能导致该结果的情景。结果可以是监管比率越界、重大流动性不足或业务韧性受威胁；破产只是更窄的一种情形。**
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>Reverse stress testing starts from a predefined material adverse outcome and works backward to identify scenarios capable of causing it. Breach of a regulatory ratio, severe illiquidity, or a threat to business resilience can qualify; insolvency is only a narrower case.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **方法：** 优化、搜索、专家设计和迭代情景都是可用路线。若使用求解器，应搜索“达到预定不利结果且仍可解释的情景”，而不只是无约束最大化损失。关键因子数量由重大风险、可识别性和测试用途决定，不存在普遍的 5–10 个固定要求；PCA 等降维只能作为辅助，不能替代低方差但高损失方向与情景合理性的检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Method:** Optimisation, search, expert design, and iterative scenarios are all possible. A numerical routine should search for interpretable scenarios that reach the predefined adverse outcome rather than merely maximise loss without meaningful constraints. The number of key factors depends on material risks and the exercise's purpose; PCA can assist dimension reduction but cannot replace checks of low-variance high-loss directions or scenario plausibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **应用举例：** 例如一个持有期权的机构可设定某一损失门槛，使用模型和求解器确定资产价格和波动率的极值组合，找出可能产生该损失的情景 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Example:** An options portfolio can set a loss threshold and use a model and numerical solver to find combinations of underlying price and volatility capable of reaching that loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

> **模拟题 11（简答题）：** 什么是逆向压力测试？与传统压力测试有何区别？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 11:** What is reverse stress testing, and how does it differ from a traditional stress test?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** 逆向压力测试先定义重大不利结果，再反向寻找可能导致它的情景；传统前向测试则先给情景，再估计影响。重大不利结果不必等于资不抵债，也可以是监管比率、流动性或业务韧性边界。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Reverse stress testing defines a material adverse outcome first and searches for conditions that can produce it. A forward test specifies the scenario first and estimates its impact. The adverse outcome can be a regulatory-ratio breach, severe illiquidity, or a threat to business resilience; it need not be insolvency.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

> **模拟题 12（简答题）：** 简述实施逆向压力测试时常用的技术方法。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 12:** Summarise common techniques for implementing reverse stress testing.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** 先定义重大不利结果和搜索边界，再用专家设计、优化或迭代搜索寻找能达到该结果的情景，并检查这些情景的可解释性与内部一致性。[[主成分分析.canvas|PCA]]等降维工具可以辅助搜索，但只优先保留历史高方差方向，不能证明低方差方向在压力下无害，也不能替代[[解释方差不等于任务信息|解释方差边界]]与情景合理性检查。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Common steps are: identify a small set of key market variables and model other variables conditionally; use dimension-reduction tools such as [[主成分分析.canvas|PCA]] to constrain the search; and define a loss threshold or survival boundary, then solve backward for the factor ranges that reach it. PCA prioritises historically high-variance directions; it does not show that discarded low-variance directions are harmless under stress or replace scenario-plausibility and tail-coverage checks. See [[解释方差不等于任务信息|the explained-variance boundary]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

## **监管要求**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Regulatory Requirements*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **巴塞尔协议建议：** 巴塞尔委员会要求，银行在使用内部VaR模型计量市场风险时，应同时进行“严格和全面”的压力测试；同样，使用内部评级法（IRB）计量信用风险也须附带压力测试以验证模型假设 。2009年5月发布的最终建议强调了压力测试的重要性，特别是在市场持续宽松时更要进行前瞻性测试 。指导意见要求最高管理层和董事会积极参与压力测试的目标制定、情景定义和结果讨论，以支持决策和战略规划 。历史上表现较好的银行通常都将压力测试结果纳入战略决策 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Basel guidance:** Banks using internal VaR models for market risk were required to conduct rigorous and comprehensive stress tests, and IRB credit-risk models likewise require stress testing of assumptions and capital adequacy. Final guidance issued in May 2009 emphasised forward-looking testing even during benign markets. Boards and senior management should participate in setting objectives, defining scenarios, and discussing results so that testing supports decisions and strategic planning. Banks that performed better historically tended to embed stress results in strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **管理与流程：** [[压力测试目标|目标、对象层级和用途]]应先于方法选择；框架需要书面政策、明确责任和可信质疑。[[压力情景一致性|情景]]应覆盖与该测试重大且相关的风险，严重度与多样性要足以检验韧性。系统反馈、资金与资产市场联动、流动性恶化等只在对目标和范围重大时纳入，不是每次组合敏感性测试都要机械加入的固定清单。模型与基础设施还应接受与用途相称的独立审查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Governance and process:** Objectives, organisational level, and intended use should precede method choice. The framework needs written policies, clear responsibilities, and credible challenge. Scenarios should cover risks material to the exercise, and their severity and variety should meaningfully test resilience. System-wide feedback, joint funding- and asset-market stress, and deteriorating liquidity are included when material to the objective and scope, not as a mandatory checklist for every portfolio sensitivity test. Models and infrastructure should receive review proportionate to their use.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **覆盖范围：** 压力测试应考虑包括复杂和定制产品、管道（pipeline）风险、库存风险以及表外业务等在内的全部风险 。还要改进对声誉风险和高杠杆交易对手风险的考量 。风险缓释手段（对冲、信用衍生等）的有效性也要在压力情景中检验 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Coverage:** Testing should include complex and bespoke products, pipeline and inventory risk, and off-balance-sheet activities. It should improve treatment of reputational risk and highly leveraged counterparties. Risk mitigants such as hedges and credit derivatives must themselves be tested under stress.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **美国监管的历史口径：** 本节原始材料描述的是金融危机后早期 CCAR/DFAST 的 500 亿与 100 亿美元门槛、资本计划和宏观情景安排。这些数字和项目要求后来已有变化，只能作为历史制度材料，不能无日期地当作当前美国规则；当前范围应另查当期美联储文件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **U.S. supervision in the historical framework described by the source:** From 2009, the Federal Reserve ran annual stress tests for banks above USD 50 billion in assets. From 2011, CCAR required capital plans including intended distributions and used severe macro scenarios with variables such as GDP growth, unemployment, equity prices, and house prices. The source also describes a USD 10 billion threshold for DFAST, with similar scenarios but no CCAR-style capital-plan submission. These thresholds and programme details are historical and have subsequently changed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **监管逻辑：** 监管机构通过选定衰退或危机情景，强调银行关注经济下行的风险。若发现多个银行从事类似高风险交易，监管可要求它们采用相同的一组情景来评估风险聚集度。也要防止银行仅针对监管情景对冲：监管指出每个情景都代表一类可能的事件，银行应确保持有足够资本应对多种类似情景 ，并已建立相关程序防止通过狭窄对冲规避监管。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Supervisory logic:** Recession and crisis scenarios force attention onto downside risk. If several banks hold similar risky trades, supervisors can require a common scenario to assess concentration. Banks should not hedge only the published scenario: each scenario represents a family of possible events, so institutions need capital and processes robust to related variants rather than a narrow regulatory optimisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

> **模拟题 13（简答题）：** 简要说明巴塞尔委员会关于压力测试的两项主要建议。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 13:** State two main Basel recommendations for stress testing.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** 一是压力测试应嵌入治理与决策，明确董事会、高级管理层和独立质疑的责任；二是按测试目标覆盖重大相关风险，并采用足够严重且多样的情景。流动性、二轮反馈和逆向测试在与目标和范围相关时纳入，而不是每个测试都复制同一清单。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** First, stress testing should be embedded in governance and decision-making, with clear responsibilities for the board, senior management, and independent challenge. Second, it should cover risks that are material and relevant to the test's objective and use sufficiently severe and varied scenarios. Liquidity, second-round feedback, and reverse stress testing are included when relevant to that objective and scope, rather than copied into every test as a fixed checklist.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

> **模拟题 14（简答题）：** CCAR与DFAST两者的区别是什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 14:** How did CCAR and DFAST differ in the historical framework described here?

> **答案：** 在本节原始材料所述的历史制度中，CCAR 对 500 亿美元以上银行要求包含分配计划的资本方案；DFAST 曾以 100 亿美元为门槛并使用相近情景，但不要求同样的资本计划。该答案只用于辨认当时的制度差异，不能当作当前美国门槛。

## **压力情景进入概率模型的特殊做法**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A special case: embedding stress scenarios in a probability model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **与 VaR 结合的模型选择：** Berkowitz（2000）的做法不是一般压力测试定义，而是另建一个混合概率模型：给压力损失主观概率 $p_i$，把剩余概率 $1-p$ 分配给历史情景，再从混合分布计算 $\text{VaR}_\alpha$。结果同时取决于压力损失和主观概率；把数字写进模型并不证明这些概率已经校准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **A separate probability model:** Berkowitz (2000) proposed assigning stress losses subjective probabilities $p_i$, allocating the remaining probability $1-p$ across historical scenarios, and then calculating $\text{VaR}_\alpha$ from the resulting mixture. This is not the definition of stress testing. The output depends on both the stress losses and the chosen probabilities, and inclusion in the mixture does not by itself validate those probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **概率分层：** 研究示例可把压力事件分层并主观指定 0.05%、0.2%、0.5% 等权重，再与历史情景组成混合分布。这些百分比是模型输入，不是由“严重”“极端”等标签自动推出的客观频率；必须做敏感性分析并说明概率依据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Probability tiers:** Stress events can be grouped by severity—for example, an extreme event with 0.05% probability, a severe event with 0.2%, and a secondary event with 0.5%. Probability within each class can be allocated equally or with differentiated weights. This combines rare extreme loss with ordinary historical outcomes in one distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **计算示例：** 比如 给出了一个例子：假设有5个压力情景损失（单位千美元）分别为235、300、450、750、850，对应概率0.5%、0.2%、0.2%、0.05%、0.05%（总计1%），剩余99%的概率由500个历史模拟场景平分。按照这种组合分布，累积概率达到99%时，VaR（99%）约为282,204美元 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Numerical illustration:** The note gives five stress losses, in thousands of dollars, of 235, 300, 450, 750, and 850 with probabilities 0.5%, 0.2%, 0.2%, 0.05%, and 0.05%, totalling 1%. The remaining 99% is split across 500 historical scenarios. In the resulting mixture, the stated 99% VaR is approximately USD 282,204.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

> **模拟题 15（简答题）：** 说明如何在VaR计算中考虑压力测试情景，并给出计算VaR的一般步骤。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Practice 15:** Explain how stress scenarios can enter a VaR calculation and give the general procedure.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **答案：** 只有在明确选择混合概率模型后，才先给压力损失主观概率、把剩余概率分配给历史情景，再从合成分布读取 $\text{VaR}_\alpha$。必须同时报告概率依据和敏感性；普通压力情景本身没有固定发生概率，也不要求进入 VaR。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Only after explicitly choosing a mixture-probability model should one assign a subjective probability to each stress loss so the stress probabilities sum to $p$. Allocate the remaining $1-p$ across the historical scenarios, combine and sort all losses, cumulate their probabilities, and read $\text{VaR}_\alpha$ from the resulting distribution. The probability basis and sensitivity must be reported; an ordinary stress scenario has no fixed probability and need not enter VaR.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
