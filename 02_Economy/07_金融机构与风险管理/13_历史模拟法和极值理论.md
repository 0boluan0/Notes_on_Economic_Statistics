# 1. **[[历史模拟法]]（[[历史模拟法|Historical Simulation Method]]）**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. [[历史模拟法|Historical Simulation]] (Historical Simulation Method)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**定义与假设：** 历史模拟法是假设未来的收益率分布与过去历史分布相同 。也就是说，不对资产收益作特定分布假设，而是直接利用历史数据来模拟未来可能的情景 。具体做法是将历史上的市场因子或资产价格变动视为未来可能发生的情景集合，通过这些情景计算投资组合的损益分布，并以经验分布的分位数估计风险价值（VaR） 。==这个方法的核心假设是**历史重现**：过去发生过的市场变化模式在未来仍可能出现。==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Definition and assumptions:** Historical simulation assumes that the future distribution of returns will resemble the historical distribution. It does not impose a particular parametric distribution on asset returns. Instead, it treats past changes in market factors or asset prices as possible future scenarios, reprices the portfolio under each scenario, and estimates value at risk (VaR) from a quantile of the resulting empirical profit-and-loss distribution. ==Its central assumption is that **history can recur**: patterns of market change observed in the past may reappear in the future.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**计算步骤：** 利用历史模拟法计算VaR的一般步骤如下：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Calculation procedure:** A standard historical-simulation VaR calculation follows these steps:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **数据准备：** 收集足够长度、按日期对齐的历史市场因子数据。第$i$日的整行联合变动记为$\Delta\boldsymbol{x}_i$；同一行中的利率、汇率、股票价格等冲击必须一起使用，不能把不同日期的各列任意拼接。
2. **生成情景并重估：** 保持今天的头寸和合约不变，把第$i$日的整行联合冲击施加到今天的市场状态$\boldsymbol{x}_0$，再用完整定价模型得到情景价值$V_i$。只有在“单一线性资产、历史数据直接是该资产收益率”的特殊情形，才可简写为$V_i=V_0(1+\Delta_i)$。
3. **计算损失分布：** 对每个情景计算正损失$L_i=V_0-V_i$，并按$L_{(1)}\le\cdots\le L_{(N)}$从小到大排序。
4. **取左经验分位数作为VaR：** 在本章统一采用nearest-rank约定，$\widehat{\operatorname{VaR}}_q=L_{(\lceil Nq\rceil)}$。若改按损失从大到小排列，它是第$N-\lceil Nq\rceil+1$大损失。若损失互异且$(1-q)N$为整数，ES取严格超过VaR的最坏$(1-q)N$个损失的平均值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Prepare the data:** Collect a sufficiently long, date-aligned history of market factors. Let $\Delta\boldsymbol{x}_i$ denote the whole vector of joint changes on day $i$. Shocks to rates, exchange rates, equity prices, and other factors from the same row must remain together; columns from different dates must not be mixed.<br>
**2.** **Generate and reprice scenarios:** Hold today's positions and contracts fixed, apply the entire day-$i$ shock vector to today's market state $\boldsymbol{x}_0$, and use the full pricing model to obtain scenario value $V_i$. The shortcut $V_i=V_0(1+\Delta_i)$ is valid only for the special case of one linear asset whose historical observation is that asset's return.<br>
**3.** **Construct the loss distribution:** For each scenario calculate the positive loss $L_i=V_0-V_i$ and order the observations as $L_{(1)}\le\cdots\le L_{(N)}$.<br>
**4.** **Take the left empirical quantile as VaR:** This chapter uses the nearest-rank convention, $\widehat{\operatorname{VaR}}_q=L_{(\lceil Nq\rceil)}$. In descending loss order this is the $(N-\lceil Nq\rceil+1)$-th largest loss. If losses are distinct and $(1-q)N$ is an integer, ES is the mean of the worst $(1-q)N$ losses that strictly exceed VaR.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

>[!example] 示例
> **假设有500个互异的交易日情景损失，以计算1天99%置信水平的VaR。** 将500个情景损失从大到小排序。nearest-rank给出$500-\lceil500\times0.99\rceil+1=6$，所以VaR是第6大损失；恰有5个损失严格超过它。若第6大损失为250万美元，则一天99% VaR为250万美元；99% [[ES定义|ES]]取最坏5个损失的平均值。
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>Suppose 500 distinct daily scenario losses are used to estimate one-day VaR at the 99% confidence level. Sort them from largest to smallest. The nearest-rank convention gives $500-\lceil500\times0.99\rceil+1=6$, so VaR is the sixth-largest loss and exactly five losses strictly exceed it. If the sixth-largest loss is USD 2.5 million, one-day 99% VaR is USD 2.5 million; 99% [[ES定义|ES]] is the mean of the worst five losses.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**VaR估计的精确度（置信区间）：** 由于历史模拟法基于有限历史样本，求得的分位数只是总体真实分位数的近似。Kendall和Stuart的研究给出了样本分位数估计值的标准误公式 。令$q$为置信水平，$n$为样本数量，真实分布在VaR处的概率密度为$f(x_q)$，则$q$分位数估计$\hat{x}_q$的标准误近似为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Precision of the VaR estimate (confidence interval):** Because historical simulation uses a finite historical sample, its estimated quantile only approximates the population quantile. Kendall and Stuart give an approximate standard error for a sample quantile. Let $q$ denote the confidence level, $n$ the sample size, and $f(x_q)$ the true density evaluated at the VaR quantile. The standard error of the estimated $q$-quantile $\hat{x}_q$ is approximately:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$ \operatorname{StdErr}(\hat{x}_q) \approx \frac{\sqrt{q(1-q)}}{\sqrt{n}\,f(x_q)}. $$

利用上述标准误可以构造VaR估计的置信区间。密度$f(x_q)$必须以“损失金额单位的倒数”计量，标准误才会得到相应的损失金额单位。例如，若$n=500$、$q=0.99$、$f(x_{0.99})=0.0027\;({\rm 百万美元})^{-1}$，则标准误为$1.648$百万美元。若VaR点估计为2500万美元，95%置信区间为$25\pm1.96\times1.648=[21.77,28.23]$百万美元。一般而言，置信水平越高，VaR估计的不确定性通常越大；增大样本数量可以降低标准误，但降低幅度与$\sqrt{n}$成反比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This standard error can be used to construct a confidence interval for VaR. The density $f(x_q)$ must be measured in the inverse of the loss unit, so the standard error has that loss unit. For $n=500$, $q=0.99$, and $f(x_{0.99})=0.0027\;({\rm USD\ million})^{-1}$, the standard error is USD 1.648 million. If the VaR point estimate is USD 25 million, the 95% confidence interval is $25\pm1.96\times1.648=[21.77,28.23]$ million. In general, higher-confidence VaR estimates usually have greater sampling uncertainty. Increasing the sample size reduces the standard error, but only in proportion to $1/\sqrt{n}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**方法扩展：** 针对历史模拟法的局限，实务中提出了多种改进方法，以提高VaR估计对当前风险的响应，并刻画有限样本下的抽样不确定性：
- **对历史数据赋予权重（[[加权历史模拟]]）：** 赋予最近的数据更大权重，以反映当前市场状况。若有限窗口含$n$个情景、距离当前$i$天的情景权重按指数衰减，则应正规化为$w_i=(1-\lambda)\lambda^{i-1}/(1-\lambda^n)$，从而$\sum_{i=1}^n w_i=1$。按损失从大到小累计权重，累计权重首次严格超过尾部概率$1-q$时对应的损失即为VaR，这与本章的左经验分位数约定一致。可通过回溯测试选择$\lambda$。
- **[[过滤历史模拟|波动率更新的历史模拟]]：** 单因子情形可用当前波动率与历史波动率之比缩放该日冲击。多因子组合则必须保留同一历史日的联合标准化残差，并用历史与当前的多变量协方差矩阵一致地变换整行冲击；不能逐列独立缩放或重抽样，否则会破坏相关结构。之后仍需把联合冲击施加到当前固定组合并完整重估。该方法依赖所选条件波动率、协方差和残差模型；模型误设、结构变化或历史未见尾部不会因过滤而消失。
- **自助法（[[历史模拟Bootstrap|Bootstrap]]）：** Bootstrap对原历史样本有放回地重复抽样，并在每个重抽样样本上计算VaR，以估计VaR统计量的抽样分布、偏差和置信区间。例如，1万次重抽样所得VaR的2.5%和97.5%分位数若为530万美元和890万美元，95% Bootstrap区间就是530万美元至890万美元。它不会产生原样本之外的新尾部情景，也不会增加有效历史信息；若数据存在时间依赖，应使用block bootstrap，存在波动率聚集时可使用filtered bootstrap。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Extensions:** Practitioners use several variants of historical simulation to make VaR respond more quickly to current risk and to describe sampling uncertainty in a finite sample:
- **Weight the historical observations ([[加权历史模拟|weighted historical simulation]]):** Give recent data more weight. For a finite window of $n$ scenarios, normalized exponential weights are $w_i=(1-\lambda)\lambda^{i-1}/(1-\lambda^n)$, so $\sum_{i=1}^n w_i=1$. Sort losses from largest to smallest and accumulate the weights; VaR is the loss at which cumulative weight first strictly exceeds the tail probability $1-q$, consistent with this chapter's left empirical quantile. Backtesting can help select $\lambda$.
- **[[过滤历史模拟|Volatility-updated historical simulation]]:** In a single-factor setting, scale each historical shock by the ratio of current to historical volatility. For a multi-factor portfolio, preserve each date's joint standardized residual and transform the whole row consistently using the historical and current multivariate covariance matrices. Scaling or resampling columns independently would destroy dependence. Apply the resulting joint shock to today's fixed portfolio and fully reprice it. The method still depends on the chosen conditional-volatility, covariance, and residual model; filtering does not remove misspecification, regime change, or tails absent from the historical window.
- **Bootstrap ([[历史模拟Bootstrap|Bootstrap]]):** Repeatedly sample with replacement from the original history and compute VaR in each resample to estimate the statistic's sampling distribution, bias, and confidence interval. For example, bootstrap percentiles of USD 5.3 million and USD 8.9 million give a 95% interval of USD 5.3–8.9 million. Bootstrap creates neither new tail scenarios nor additional effective historical information. Use a block bootstrap for serial dependence and a filtered bootstrap for volatility clustering.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
## **历史模拟法练习题**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Historical-Simulation Exercises*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**理解题 1：** 历史模拟法计算VaR的主要原理是什么？其假设条件有哪些？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Conceptual question 1:** What is the main idea behind historical-simulation VaR, and what assumptions does the method make?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** 历史模拟法利用过去实际发生的收益/损失分布来估计未来风险。主要原理是假设未来金融市场的收益率分布与历史相同，因此可以将历史价格或收益数据当作未来可能出现的情景。该方法不需要对收益分布作参数化假定，而是直接基于经验分布计算VaR 。其核心假设包括：“历史会重演”，即过去的市场波动特征在未来仍适用；同时要求有足够的历史数据代表潜在风险。只要这些假设大致成立，按照历史数据生成未来情景并取其中相应分位数损失，就可得到组合的VaR。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** Historical simulation estimates future risk from the distribution of profits and losses actually observed in the past. It assumes that future market returns will have approximately the same distribution as historical returns, so past prices or returns can serve as possible future scenarios. No parametric return distribution is imposed; VaR is taken directly from the empirical distribution. The central assumptions are that relevant patterns of market volatility can recur and that the historical sample is sufficiently rich to represent the risks the portfolio may face. If these assumptions are reasonable, repricing the portfolio under historical scenarios and taking the appropriate loss quantile produces a VaR estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**理解题 2：** 简述两种改进历史模拟法的方法及其原理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Conceptual question 2:** Briefly explain two ways to improve historical simulation and the principle behind each.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** 改进历史模拟法的常用方法有：（1）**[[加权历史模拟|加权历史模拟法]]**：对有限窗口使用正规化指数权重$w_i=(1-\lambda)\lambda^{i-1}/(1-\lambda^n)$，使近期数据对VaR计算影响更大。（2）**[[历史模拟Bootstrap|Bootstrap]]自助法**：从现有历史样本有放回地反复抽样，以近似VaR统计量的抽样分布，并估计偏差和置信区间；它不创造新的尾部情景或有效历史信息。若序列存在时间依赖，应采用block bootstrap；若存在波动率聚集，可采用filtered bootstrap。此外，**[[过滤历史模拟|波动率调整法]]**按当前风险尺度更新历史情景；多因子情形必须保留同一日联合残差和协方差结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** Two common extensions are: (1) **[[加权历史模拟|weighted historical simulation]]**, which uses normalized finite-window weights $w_i=(1-\lambda)\lambda^{i-1}/(1-\lambda^n)$ so recent observations matter more; and (2) the **[[历史模拟Bootstrap|bootstrap]]**, which resamples the observed history with replacement to approximate VaR's sampling distribution and estimate bias and confidence intervals. It creates neither new tail scenarios nor additional effective history. Use a block bootstrap for serial dependence and a filtered bootstrap for volatility clustering. A third extension is **[[过滤历史模拟|volatility adjustment]]**; in multiple dimensions it must preserve each date's joint residual and covariance structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**计算题 1：** 某投资组合当前价值为1000万元。过去10天的日收益率（%）分别为：[-2.0, +1.5, -0.5, +0.8, -1.0, +0.4, +1.2, -3.1, +0.6, -0.7]。请使用历史模拟法估计该投资组合1天90%置信水平的VaR值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Calculation question 1:** A portfolio is currently worth CNY 10 million. Its daily returns over the past 10 days, in percent, are [-2.0, +1.5, -0.5, +0.8, -1.0, +0.4, +1.2, -3.1, +0.6, -0.7]. Use historical simulation to estimate its one-day VaR at the 90% confidence level.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** 先把收益换成正损失（万元）：$[20,-15,5,-8,10,-4,-12,31,-6,7]$。从大到小排序为$[31,20,10,7,5,-4,-6,-8,-12,-15]$。本章采用nearest-rank：按降序的名次为$10-\lceil10\times0.90\rceil+1=2$，所以一天90% **VaR为20万元（20万人民币）**。严格超过VaR的最坏10%只有31万元这一项，因此一天90% **ES为31万元（31万人民币）**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** Convert returns to positive losses, in units of CNY 10,000: $[20,-15,5,-8,10,-4,-12,31,-6,7]$. In descending order these are $[31,20,10,7,5,-4,-6,-8,-12,-15]$. Under this chapter's nearest-rank convention, the descending rank is $10-\lceil10\times0.90\rceil+1=2$, so one-day 90% **VaR is CNY 200,000**. Only the worst observation, CNY 310,000, strictly exceeds VaR; therefore one-day 90% **ES is CNY 310,000**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**计算题 2：** 某投资组合用500天历史数据估计了99%置信的一天VaR为2500万美元。假设在VaR处对应的损失分布密度$f(x)=0.0027\;({\rm 百万美元})^{-1}$，试利用分位数标准误公式计算该VaR估计值的95%置信区间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Calculation question 2:** A portfolio uses 500 historical days to estimate one-day 99% VaR at USD 25 million. If the density at the VaR point is $f(x)=0.0027\;({\rm USD\ million})^{-1}$, use the quantile standard-error formula to calculate a 95% confidence interval for the VaR estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** 使用$\displaystyle \operatorname{StdErr}(\hat{x}_{0.99})\approx\frac{\sqrt{0.99\times0.01}}{\sqrt{500}\times0.0027}$，得到$\operatorname{StdErr}\approx1.648$百万美元。以2500万美元为点估计，95%置信区间为$25\pm1.96\times1.648=[21.77,28.23]$百万美元。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** The approximation $\displaystyle \operatorname{StdErr}(\hat{x}_{0.99})\approx\frac{\sqrt{0.99\times0.01}}{\sqrt{500}\times0.0027}$ gives a standard error of USD 1.648 million. Centering the normal 95% interval at USD 25 million gives $25\pm1.96\times1.648=[21.77,28.23]$ million.

# 2.**极值理论（Extreme Value Theory, EVT）**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Extreme-Value Theory (EVT)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**基本原理：** 极值理论主要研究分布尾部的行为特征，关注极端损失发生的概率及幅度。针对金融风险管理，极值理论通常用于分析损失分布的**尾部**（高损失一端）的形状。方法上有两类：块极值法（研究一定区间内的最大值）和超阈值法（Peaks-Over-Threshold, POT）。这里侧重后者，即选定一个高阈值$u$，专注于**超出阈值的极端损失**部分。Gnedenko于1943年的定理奠定了该方法的理论基础：对于大量常见分布，当阈值$u$逐渐增大时，超出$u$的尾部数据的分布将趋近于**广义Pareto分布（[[广义Pareto分布|GPD]]）** 。换言之，足够高的损失超越一定阈值后，其超额分布$F_u(y)=P(X-u \le y \mid X>u)$可以用GPD来近似建模 。这一结论不要求整体分布属于某个特定族，只关注尾部渐进性质。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Core idea:** Extreme-value theory studies the behaviour of a distribution's tail, especially the probability and magnitude of extreme losses. In financial risk management, it models the high-loss tail. Two broad approaches are block maxima, which study the largest observation within each interval, and peaks-over-threshold (POT). The focus here is POT: choose a high threshold $u$ and model only the **extreme losses above that threshold**. Gnedenko's 1943 theorem provides the foundation. For a broad class of underlying distributions, as $u$ becomes sufficiently high, the conditional excess distribution $F_u(y)=P(X-u \le y \mid X>u)$ approaches a **generalized Pareto distribution (GPD)**. The method therefore relies on asymptotic tail behaviour rather than assuming a particular distribution for the entire loss series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] EVT-01 理论归属与适用条件
> 把 Gnedenko 称为理论源头是教材中的概括；现代论述须区分两件事：[[极大值极限定理|Fisher–Tippett–Gnedenko 极限定理]]对适当标准化的样本最大值给出 GEV 极限族；直接支持 POT 超额近似的是 [[超阈值极限定理|Pickands–Balkema–de Haan 定理]]。此处采用连续母分布版本：母分布属于某个[[极大值吸引域]]，当且仅当存在随阈值改变的正的可测尺度 $\beta(u)$，使 $F_u$ 与对应 GPD 的差趋于零。这里是 $u$ 趋近母分布的右端点 $x_F$，不一定是 $u\to\infty$；也不是任何分布、任一有限高阈值都已有精确 GPD 尾部。独立同分布是基础抽样版本；依赖序列须另核相应条件。[Haugh，PDF 第 6–8 页](https://www.columbia.edu/~mh2078/QRM/EVT_MasterSlides.pdf#page=8)支持最大值极限分类；[Embrechts，PDF 第 25–26 页](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~embrecht/ftp/Ottawa2008_english.pdf#page=25)与[McNeil–Frey，PDF 第 7 页及脚注 4](https://statmath.wu.ac.at/~frey/publications/evt-garch.pdf#page=7)支持超额结果、吸引域条件和归属。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Calling Gnedenko the theoretical origin is a textbook summary. Modern treatments distinguish the [[极大值极限定理|Fisher–Tippett–Gnedenko classification]] of nondegenerate limits of normalized sample maxima from the [[超阈值极限定理|Pickands–Balkema–de Haan theorem]] supporting POT excess approximations. For a continuous parent CDF, the latter equates membership in a [[极大值吸引域|maximum domain of attraction]] with the existence of a positive measurable threshold-dependent scale $\beta(u)$. The threshold approaches the distribution's right endpoint $x_F$, not necessarily infinity. It does not give every distribution an exact GPD tail at any finite high threshold. The basic sampling version uses iid observations; dependent sequences require additional conditions. Haugh, pp. 6–8, supports the maxima classification; Embrechts, pp. 25–26, and McNeil–Frey, p. 7 and footnote 4, support the excess result, domain condition and attribution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> 上面的非零 $\xi$ 公式应同时要求 $y\ge0$ 与 $1+\xi y/\beta>0$，且 $\beta>0$。当 $\xi<0$，超额的上端点是 $y_F=-\beta/\xi$：在端点以连续延拓取 CDF 为 $1$，其上也为 $1$；不能把幂式代入端点之外。对应原损失的模型上端点为 $u-\beta/\xi$，而非 $-\beta/\xi$。$\xi=0$ 时指数 CDF 是完整定义分支，不应在含 $1/\xi$ 的表达式里直接除以零。来源：[McNeil–Frey，PDF 第 7 页](https://statmath.wu.ac.at/~frey/publications/evt-garch.pdf#page=7)。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For nonzero $\xi$, require both $y\ge0$ and $1+\xi y/\beta>0$, with $\beta>0$. When $\xi<0$, the excess endpoint is $y_F=-\beta/\xi$; extend the CDF continuously to 1 there and set it to 1 above that endpoint. Do not evaluate the power expression beyond its support. The original loss has model endpoint $u-\beta/\xi$, not $-\beta/\xi$. At $\xi=0$, the exponential CDF is a separate valid branch, not a direct substitution into terms containing $1/\xi$. McNeil–Frey, p. 7, states the support and both branches.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->


其中$\beta>0$为尺度参数，$\xi$为形状参数。**形状参数$\xi$**决定了尾部的形状和厚度：$\xi>0$对应重尾分布（尾部概率以幂律衰减），例如金融数据常呈现的胖尾现象；$\xi=0$对应尾部呈指数衰减（类似指数分布，尾部适中）；$\xi<0$则尾部有界（存在损失的上限）。**尺度参数$\beta$**控制了分布的伸展（类似于标准差的作用，但针对尾部）。需要注意GPD通常用于$x>u$的部分，因此其定义域取决于所选阈值$u$及$\xi$（若$\xi<0$则$y$有最大值$-\beta/\xi$）。根据极值理论，很多常见分布（如正态、对数正态、$t$分布等）的高端尾部，都可以在一定程度上用某个$\xi,\beta$的GPD来逼近 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\beta>0$ is the scale parameter and $\xi$ is the shape parameter. **The shape parameter $\xi$** determines the form and heaviness of the tail: $\xi>0$ gives a heavy, power-law tail; $\xi=0$ gives exponential tail decay; and $\xi<0$ gives a bounded tail with a finite maximum loss. **The scale parameter $\beta$** controls the spread of the tail, playing a role analogous to a standard deviation for exceedances. Because the GPD is applied only when $x>u$, its support depends on the threshold $u$ and on $\xi$; when $\xi<0$, the excess $y$ has the upper endpoint $-\beta/\xi$. Extreme-value theory implies that the upper tails of many common distributions, including the normal, lognormal, and $t$ distributions, can be approximated by a GPD with suitable $\xi$ and $\beta$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!note] EVT-03 尺度、矩与母分布不能混为一谈

>
> $\xi=0$ 时是 **GPD 超额模型本身**呈指数分布；正态与对数正态虽都属于 $\xi=0$ 的极值吸引域，其原始尾部并不都是指数分布。对精确 GPD，均值有限要求 $\xi<1$，方差有限要求 $\xi<1/2$；方差存在时为 $\beta^2/[(1-\xi)^2(1-2\xi)]$，所以 $\beta$ 不是标准差，有限尺度也不保证有限方差。有限样本拟合的负形状参数只给出该模型的有限端点，不证明实际损失绝不可能超过它。来源：[McNeil–Frey，PDF 第 7 页](https://statmath.wu.ac.at/~frey/publications/evt-garch.pdf#page=7) 的分布族与吸引域区分；矩条件由 GPD 密度积分核验。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $\xi=0$, the GPD excess model itself is exponential. Normal and lognormal distributions both lie in the $\xi=0$ maximum domain of attraction, but their original tails are not themselves exponential distributions. An exact GPD has finite mean only for $\xi<1$ and finite variance only for $\xi<1/2$. When finite, variance is $\beta^2/[(1-\xi)^2(1-2\xi)]$; scale is not standard deviation, and finite scale does not ensure finite variance. A fitted negative shape gives a model endpoint, not proof of an absolute real-world loss ceiling. McNeil–Frey, p. 7, distinguishes distribution families and their domains; the moment conditions follow by integrating the GPD density.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Parameter estimation by maximum likelihood:** Given a threshold $u$, write the observed losses above it as ${v_i: i=1,\dots,n_u}$, where $n_u$ is the number of exceedances. POT fits a GPD to the excesses ${y_i = v_i - u}$. A common estimator is **maximum likelihood (MLE)**. With GPD density $f(y) = \frac{1}{\beta}\Big(1+\frac{\xi y}{\beta}\Big)^{-1/\xi - 1}$, the sample log-likelihood is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

$$ \ell(\xi,\beta) = \sum_{i=1}^{n_u}\ln f(y_i) = -n_u\ln \beta -\Big(1+\frac{1}{\xi}\Big)\sum_{i=1}^{n_u}\ln\Big(1+\frac{\xi y_i}{\beta}\Big). $$

> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The displayed likelihood assumes $\xi\ne0$, a positive exceedance count and scale, and $1+\xi y_i/\beta>0$ for every observation. At $\xi=0$, use $\ell(0,\beta)=-n_u\log\beta-\sum_i y_i/\beta$. Zero scores identify interior stationary points, not necessarily a unique solution or a maximum; check support, numerical convergence and candidate likelihoods. The product likelihood treats excesses as approximately iid. Standard asymptotic normal inference requires regularity, including $\xi>-1/2$ for the usual exact-GPD result, and does not apply automatically to every shape. McNeil–Frey, p. 7, states that boundary; the zero-shape branch follows from the exponential density.

通过对$\ell(\xi,\beta)$分别对$\xi$和$\beta$求偏导并设为0，可以数值求解得到$\hat{\xi}$和$\hat{\beta}$ 。由于对数似然方程通常没有解析解，需借助迭代算法获得参数估计值。此外，也可以采用**PWM（概率加权矩法）**等其他方法估计参数，但MLE在大样本下较有效。实践中，阈值$u$的选择会影响参数估计和模型拟合效果。经验法则是选取足够高的$u$（通常可取经验分布95%或更高的分位数作为$u$ ），以确保超额样本集中在极端尾部；同时$u$又不能过高以至于有效数据过少。可以通过绘制尾部参数稳定图等方式来辅助选择合适的$u$值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking partial derivatives of $\ell(\xi,\beta)$ with respect to $\xi$ and $\beta$, setting them equal to zero, and solving numerically yields $\hat{\xi}$ and $\hat{\beta}$. The likelihood equations generally have no closed-form solution, so an iterative optimizer is required. Other estimators, including **probability-weighted moments (PWM)**, are also available, while MLE is efficient under standard large-sample conditions. The threshold $u$ creates a bias–variance trade-off: it must be high enough for the tail approximation to be credible—often the empirical 95th percentile or higher—but not so high that too few exceedances remain. Mean-excess and parameter-stability plots can help select a defensible threshold.

**理解题 1：** Gnedenko定理在极值理论中描述了什么现象？它对于金融风险管理有何意义？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Conceptual question 1:** What limiting phenomenon does the Gnedenko theorem describe, and why does it matter for financial risk management?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** Gnedenko定理是极值理论的核心结果之一，描述了分布尾部的极限行为。简单来说，该定理指出当选取足够高的阈值$u$时，原始分布右尾部分超过$u$的**超额分布**将趋向于一个**广义Pareto分布（GPD）** 。这一现象与具体的原始分布类型无关，具有普适性。因此，对于金融风险管理而言，Gnedenko定理的意义在于提供了**建模极端风险的通用方法**：不论基础损失分布是什么，只要我们聚焦极大损失的尾部并选取高阈值，就可以使用GPD近似建模。这为计算罕见但冲击巨大的风险指标（如高置信水平VaR和ES）奠定了理论基础，使我们能够用统一的方法外推估计超出历史经验范围的极端损失概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** The Gnedenko theorem is a central result in extreme-value theory. It says that, above a sufficiently high threshold $u$, the conditional **excess distribution** of a broad class of underlying distributions converges to a **generalized Pareto distribution (GPD)**. This result is valuable in financial risk management because it supplies a common model for extreme losses without requiring the entire loss distribution to belong to one parametric family. By fitting a GPD to sufficiently high exceedances, analysts can estimate rare, severe risks such as high-confidence VaR and ES and extrapolate beyond the range directly observed in the historical sample.

**理解题 2：** 广义Pareto分布的形状参数$\xi$和尺度参数$\beta$分别反映了什么含义？在风险度量中形状参数有什么作用？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Conceptual question 2:** What do the generalized Pareto distribution's shape parameter $\xi$ and scale parameter $\beta$ represent, and why is the shape parameter important in risk measurement?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** GPD的形状参数$\xi$和尺度参数$\beta$共同决定了尾部分布的特征。其中**形状参数$\xi$直接影响尾部厚度：$\xi>0$表示重尾，尾部概率衰减缓慢，极端损失更频繁（例如$\xi=0.2$可能意味着分布尾比正态更肥尾)；$\xi=0$表示尾部呈指数衰减，极端损失概率以中等速度递减；$\xi<0$则尾部截断，存在损失的上限。形状参数在风险度量中至关重要，它决定了极端置信区间下VaR随置信水平的增长速度**——$\xi$越大，高分位数VaR随置信水平提升而增长越快，因为尾巴更肥厚。**尺度参数$\beta$**则类似于尾部的放大系数，控制尾部的基本波动幅度，$\beta$越大，在相同$\xi$下尾部越分散，VaR数值也越高。总结而言，$\xi$刻画尾部形状类型，$\beta$设定尾部尺度大小。在极值模型中准确估计这两个参数，可以更可靠地评估金融资产组合面临的极端损失风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** The GPD parameters $\xi$ and $\beta$ jointly determine the tail. **The shape parameter $\xi$ controls tail heaviness:** $\xi>0$ gives a heavy tail whose probability decays slowly, so very large losses are relatively more frequent; $\xi=0$ gives exponential decay; and $\xi<0$ gives a bounded tail with a finite upper endpoint. Because $\xi$ determines how quickly high quantiles grow as confidence rises, it is crucial for extreme-risk measurement. **The scale parameter $\beta$** controls the dispersion of exceedances: for a given $\xi$, a larger $\beta$ produces a more spread-out tail and a larger VaR. Accurate estimation of both parameters is therefore essential for assessing a portfolio's exposure to extreme losses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
