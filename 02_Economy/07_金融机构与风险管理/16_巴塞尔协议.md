# 1. **巴塞尔协议 2.5 主要内容**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. **Basel 2.5: Main Provisions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

巴塞尔协议2.5是在2008年金融危机后针对市场风险资本充足要求做出的修正，2011年开始实施 。其主要增加了以下三项内容：**压力风险价值度（[[压力VaR|Stressed VaR]]）**、**增量风险计提（IRC）和综合风险计量（CRM）** 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Basel 2.5 amended the market-risk capital framework after the 2008 financial crisis and began to be implemented in 2011. Its three main additions were **[[压力VaR|stressed value at risk (stressed VaR)]]**, the **incremental risk charge (IRC)**, and the **comprehensive risk measure (CRM)**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1 [[压力VaR|压力VaR]] sVaR
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1 Stressed VaR (sVaR)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **压力VaR**：[[压力VaR|Basel 2.5 sVaR]] 把**当前组合**放在与其风险因子相关的连续 12 个月重大压力期校准下，计算 10 日、99% 单尾 VaR。压力期需要监管认可并定期复核；它不是直接报告危机时期旧组合的损失。历史 [[Basel 2.5 VaR资本块|Basel 2.5 VaR 资本块]]同时计入常态 VaR 与 sVaR，而不是在二者中择一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Stressed VaR:** [[压力VaR|Basel 2.5 stressed VaR]] applies a relevant continuous twelve-month period of significant financial stress to the **current portfolio** and calculates a ten-day, one-tailed 99% VaR. The stress period requires supervisory approval and regular review; the measure does not simply report the loss of an old crisis-era portfolio. The historical [[Basel 2.5 VaR资本块|Basel 2.5 VaR capital block]] included both ordinary VaR and stressed VaR rather than choosing between them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2 IRC增量风险计提
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2 Incremental Risk Charge (IRC)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **增量风险计提（IRC）**：原本的违约风险资本（IDRC）只考虑违约风险，而2007–08年危机显示，信用评级迁移、利差上升、流动性丧失等因素也会带来重大损失。因此IRC要求银行对交易账簿中所有信用相关头寸，以1年展望期、99.9%置信度计算VaR资本 。例如，某债券头寸10天VaR在普通情况下需资本1个单位，但在IRC标准下约需6–7个单位 。银行可在1年内对组合进行调整，违约损失由此部分得到缓解；但在IRC范围内，还必须考虑到评级迁移等非违约风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Incremental Risk Charge (IRC):** The earlier incremental default-risk charge (IDRC) considered only default. The 2007–08 crisis showed that credit-rating migration, spread widening, and loss of liquidity could also produce major losses. IRC therefore required banks to calculate capital for credit-sensitive trading-book positions over a one-year capital horizon at a 99.9% confidence level. For example, a bond position that required one unit of capital under a ten-day VaR measure might require roughly six to seven units under the IRC standard. A bank could rebalance its portfolio during the one-year horizon, which could mitigate default losses, but IRC also had to capture non-default risks such as rating migration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

10day99%VaR变为1y 99.9%VaR
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The horizon and confidence level move from ten-day 99% VaR to one-year 99.9% VaR.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3 CRM 综合风险计量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3 Comprehensive Risk Measure (CRM)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **综合风险计量（CRM）**：针对相关性交易账户（如CDO类证券化产品组合）推出的一种资本计量方式 。在这些产品中，个别资产表现良好并不能降低整体损失风险，因为在相关性急剧上升时可能出现多个资产同时大幅贬值 。CRM采用与IRC相同的1年、99.9%VaR计算框架，并额外考虑如隐含相关性、流动性基差、资本结构等风险因子 。监管通常要求对再证券化产品（如CDO）按标准法计提资本（或直接100%扣除），即大幅高于普通证券化产品 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Comprehensive Risk Measure (CRM):** This capital measure was introduced for the correlation trading portfolio, including portfolios of CDO-type securitisation products. Strong performance by one underlying asset does not eliminate portfolio-wide loss risk, because a sharp rise in correlation can cause several assets to fall together. CRM used the same one-year, 99.9% VaR framework as IRC and additionally captured risk factors such as implied correlation, liquidity basis, and capital structure. Supervisors generally imposed standardised capital treatment—or, in some cases described in the course material, a 100% deduction—on resecuritisations such as CDOs, producing a much higher charge than for ordinary securitisations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **2 | 两层资本缓冲**

*2 | Two Capital Buffers*

Adding the 2.5% CCB to the three minima gives conservation-buffer thresholds of **7% CET1, 8.5% Tier 1, and 10.5% total capital**. These are not redefinitions of the 4.5/6/8 minima: entering the buffer normally constrains distributions, whereas breaching a minimum has different consequences.

# **全球系统重要性银行（G-SIBs）与国内系统重要性银行（D-SIBs）**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Global Systemically Important Banks (G-SIBs) and Domestic Systemically Important Banks (D-SIBs)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**全球系统重要性银行（G-SIBs）**按规模、跨境活动、互联、可替代性和复杂性等全球外部性指标评估。方法由巴塞尔委员会制定，年度名单由金融稳定理事会（FSB）会同巴塞尔委员会和国家当局发布；桶位决定 Basel G-SIB 的 CET1 附加。名单、桶位和生效年份会变，不能把 2014 年银行示例写成当前事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Global systemically important banks (G-SIBs)** are assessed using global-footprint indicators such as size, cross-border activity, interconnectedness, substitutability, and complexity. The Basel Committee designs the methodology; the FSB publishes the annual list with the Committee and national authorities. Buckets determine Basel CET1 surcharges, but the list, bucket and effective year can change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**国内系统重要性银行（D-SIBs）**由国家当局根据对本国金融体系的影响识别，并由本地规则确定附加要求。没有跨法域统一的“资产超过 500 亿美元即为 D-SIB”定义，也没有所有法域共同采用的 0–2.5% 附加区间；规模只是可用指标之一。详见 [[G-SIB 与 D-SIB]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Domestic systemically important banks (D-SIBs)** are designated by national authorities according to their impact on the domestic financial system. There is no universal USD 50 billion threshold or cross-jurisdictional 0–2.5% surcharge range; local rules determine the indicators and additional requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **理解题**：什么是全球系统重要性银行（G-SIB）？G-SIB附加资本的主要决定因素有哪些？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Conceptual question:** What is a global systemically important bank (G-SIB), and what principally determines its capital surcharge?<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    **答案**：G-SIB 是失败会对全球金融体系产生重大外部性的银行，按规模、跨境活动、互联、可替代性和复杂性等指标分桶。FSB 会同 BCBS 与国家当局发布年度名单，适用桶位决定附加 CET1；回答具体银行时必须附名单年份和生效日。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** A G-SIB is a bank whose distress could materially affect the global financial system. Indicators include size, cross-border activity, interconnectedness, substitutability, and complexity. The FSB publishes the annual list with the BCBS and national authorities; a bank-specific answer needs a list year and effective date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
2. **理解题**：D-SIB与G-SIB有何不同？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **Conceptual question:** How does a D-SIB differ from a G-SIB?<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    **答案**：D-SIB 关注对某个国家金融体系的影响，由该国当局按本地体系结构识别和设定附加要求；G-SIB 关注全球外部性并采用全球方法。不存在通用的 500 亿美元门槛或统一 D-SIB 附加区间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** A D-SIB is systemically important within a particular country and is designated under domestic criteria; a G-SIB is assessed for global externalities. There is no universal USD 50 billion D-SIB threshold or common surcharge range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
3. **计算题**：假设某全球系统重要性银行的 CET1 最低要求为 4.5%，资本留存缓冲为 2.5%，其被评定需要额外 1.5% 的 G-SIB 缓冲。问三者合计的 CET1 门槛是多少，并说明它与最低要求的区别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** **Calculation:** Suppose a G-SIB faces a 4.5% CET1 minimum, a 2.5% capital conservation buffer, and a 1.5% G-SIB buffer. What is the combined CET1 threshold, and how does it differ from the minimum?<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    **答案**：合计 CET1 门槛 = 4.5%（最低要求）+ 2.5%（资本留存缓冲）+ 1.5%（G-SIB 缓冲）= **8.5%**。其中只有 4.5% 是这里的 CET1 最低线；跌入缓冲区通常先触发分派限制，不能把 8.5% 全部改叫“最低要求”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** The combined CET1 threshold is $4.5\%+2.5\%+1.5\%=\mathbf{8.5\%}$. Only 4.5% is the CET1 minimum in this setup; entering the buffers normally triggers distribution constraints and is not the same as breaching the minimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
4. **计算题**：假设某国内系统重要性银行按题设的本地规则须持有额外 2% RWA 的 CET1。如果其 RWA 为 1000 亿元人民币，需要多少附加 CET1？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**4.** **Calculation:** A D-SIB must hold an additional buffer equal to 2% of risk-weighted assets. If its total RWA is RMB 100 billion, how much additional capital must it hold?<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    **答案**：额外资本 = 1000亿元 × 2% = **20亿元**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** Additional capital = RMB 100 billion × 2% = **RMB 2 billion**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

# **未定可转换债券、美国Dodd-Frank法案及其他监管制度**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Contingent Convertible Bonds, the U.S. Dodd–Frank Act, and Other Regulatory Regimes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**或有可转换/减记债（CoCo）**是在合同或监管触发发生时吸收损失的次级工具。具体机制可能是转为普通股，也可能是暂时或永久减记本金；触发可以是 CET1 合同比率、不可持续经营决定或其他监管认可条件。只有转股型工具才需要转换价/转换比率，不能把所有 CoCo 写成“按固定比率自动转股”，也不能仅凭 CoCo 名称认定其属于 AT1。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Contingent convertibles or write-down instruments (CoCos)** absorb loss after a contractual or regulatory trigger. Some convert into common equity; others temporarily or permanently write down principal. Only conversion instruments require a conversion price or ratio, and the CoCo label alone does not establish AT1 eligibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**美国Dodd-Frank法案**（2010年通过）旨在防范“太大而不能倒”风险并保护消费者。其核心内容包括：成立**金融稳定监管委员会（FSOC）和金融研究办公室等新机构负责系统性风险监管；引入沃尔克规则**限制银行自营交易和对冲基金、私募基金投资；推动场外衍生品集中清算；要求系统重要机构制定**“生前遗嘱”**（清算计划）；提高系统重要性机构的资本要求；禁止对信用评级的过度依赖；要求资产发行方保留一定的利益（“自留份额”）等 。例如，沃尔克规则规定银行持有的对冲基金和私募基金投资不得超过一级资本的3% ；对大型机构实行更严格的破产清算程序（FDIC接管） 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **U.S. Dodd–Frank Act**, enacted in 2010, sought to address “too big to fail” risk and protect consumers. Its principal measures included creating the Financial Stability Oversight Council (FSOC) and the Office of Financial Research to monitor systemic risk; introducing the **Volcker Rule** to restrict bank proprietary trading and investment in hedge funds and private-equity funds; promoting central clearing of OTC derivatives; requiring systemically important institutions to prepare **“living wills”** or resolution plans; raising capital requirements for such institutions; reducing excessive reliance on credit ratings; and requiring asset issuers to retain an economic interest in securitisations. The slides note, for example, a 3%-of-Tier-1-capital limit associated with bank investment in hedge and private-equity funds and a special FDIC resolution process for large institutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 法域与日期
> 这是美国 2010 年立法及其早期实施的课程概览，不是 Basel 国际标准，也不是 2026 年所有机构的现行规则摘要。Volcker Rule 的覆盖、定义、豁免与基金投资限额须查当日美国法规；不能把这里的历史数字外推到其他法域。

  

**其他国家监管制度**：英、美等国也推出类似改革。英国的Vickers报告（2011）导致2013年的《金融服务（银行改革）法》，要求银行实行业务隔离（ring-fencing）等；欧盟Liikanen报告（2012）建议分拆银行自营业务 。各国监管普遍关注问题包括：推广场外衍生品集中清算、限制自营交易（或至少隔离高风险业务）、制定生前遗嘱、加强金融机构高管薪酬监管等 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Other national regulatory regimes:** The United Kingdom, the European Union, and other jurisdictions introduced related reforms. The United Kingdom's 2011 Vickers Report led to the Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013 and requirements such as retail-bank ring-fencing; the EU's 2012 Liikanen Report proposed separating proprietary-trading activities. Common themes included central clearing of OTC derivatives, restrictions on or separation of proprietary trading, living wills, and stronger oversight of senior-executive compensation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

(a) 巴塞尔风险监管规则；  
(b) 其他国家引入的监管规则。  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Give one example showing how a provision of the **Dodd–Frank Act** conflicts with:
(a) the Basel risk-regulation framework; and
(b) regulatory rules introduced by other countries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


>[!example] 示例
> 禁止使用外部信用评级
> 1. 《多德–弗兰克法案》——第 939A 条  
>    要求美国所有联邦法规 **删除对外部信用评级的引用**，监管机构需改用“适当的内部量化标准”评估信用风险。
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>Ban on the Use of External Credit Ratings
> **1.** **Section 939A of the Dodd–Frank Act**<br>
>It requires U.S. federal regulations to **remove references to external credit ratings** and directs regulators to use suitable internal quantitative standards for assessing credit risk.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

2. 冲突 (a)：巴塞尔风险监管规则  
   - 巴塞尔 II / III 标准法规定按评级决定风险权重  
     - AAA/AA 20 %，A 50 %，BBB 100 %，等  
   - 若美国银行不能引用评级，则无法直接应用这些权重  
   - 结果：同一资产在美国与巴塞尔框架下的 **资本要求计算方法不兼容**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** Conflict with (a): the Basel risk-regulation framework<br>
- Under the Basel II/III standardised approach, credit ratings determine risk weights.
- Illustrative weights are 20% for AAA/AA, 50% for A, and 100% for BBB.
- If U.S. banks cannot rely on ratings, they cannot apply these weights directly.
- Consequently, the **method used to calculate capital requirements** for the same asset differs between U.S. rules and the Basel framework.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

3. 冲突 (b)：其他国家的监管规则  
   - 欧盟、英国、瑞士等沿用巴塞尔标准法并继续 **引用评级**  
   - 美国银行若不用评级，将采用替代模型或监管分级  
   - 导致 **跨境资本比较困难**，美国银行与外国银行在同一资产上需持有的资本可能显著不同，破坏监管一致性与竞争中立原则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** Conflict with (b): other countries' regulatory rules<br>
- The European Union, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other jurisdictions continued to use the Basel standardised approach and to **refer to ratings**.
- U.S. banks instead had to use alternative models or regulatory classifications.
- This makes **cross-border capital comparisons difficult**: U.S. and foreign banks may need materially different capital for the same asset, weakening regulatory consistency and competitive neutrality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**1.** **(a)** Calculate the bank's **[[LCR 与 NSFR|Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)]]** using the factors stipulated in the exercise.<br>
**2.** **(b)** To meet Basel III requirements, the bank decides to attract additional **stable retail deposits** and invest all of the new deposits in **Treasury securities**. How much new deposit funding is required?<br>

(a) 计算净稳定资金比率  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
(a) Calculate the Net Stable Funding Ratio
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\text{NSFR} = \frac{\text{ASF}}{\text{RSF}} = 
\frac{73.75}{74.95} \approx 0.984 < 1
$$  

银行 **未达标**（应 ≥ 1）。  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bank **does not meet the requirement**, which is at least 1, or 100%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 为什么删掉“Tier 2 = 50% ASF”
> 仅凭 Tier 2 标签不能给 50% ASF。当前 Basel NSF30 下，合格监管资本通常为 100% ASF，但剩余期限不足一年的 Tier 2 不进入该资本项；它能否落入其他 ASF 档还要看剩余期限和融资来源。本题为了保留原算术，明确把 3 改成 6–12 个月其他批发债务。若它真是剩余期限至少一年的合格 Tier 2，因子应重新按 100% 处理，答案会改变。详见 [[LCR 与 NSFR 因子分类]]。

(b) 增加稳定零售存款 $x$ 并全部购买国库券（RSF 5 %）

(b) Add stable retail deposits of $x$ and invest the entire amount in Treasury securities, which carry a 5% RSF factor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\text{ASF 增量}=0.95x,\qquad \text{RSF 增量}=0.05x
$$  

设调整后 NSFR = 1：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set the adjusted NSFR equal to 1:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{73.75 + 0.95x}{74.95 + 0.05x} = 1
\Longrightarrow 73.75 + 0.95x = 74.95 + 0.05x
\Longrightarrow 0.90x = 1.20
\Longrightarrow x = 1.33
$$  

**答案**：  
(a) $$
    \boxed{NSFR ≈ 0.984}
    $$
(b) 需新增约$$\boxed{1.33 亿美元}$$稳定零售存款，并以相同金额购入国库券，方可令 NSFR 达到 100 %。

**Answer:** (a) NSFR $\approx0.984$; (b) $x\approx1.33$ in units of USD 100 million, or about **USD 133 million**.
