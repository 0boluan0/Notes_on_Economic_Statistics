# 5. 金融风险管理的过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. The Process of Financial Risk Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 识别风险:风险因素
2. 测度风险:相对度量和绝对度量
	1. 针对债券等利率性金融产品的久期([[债券久期、凸性与收益率曲线风险|Duration]])和凸性([[债券久期、凸性与收益率曲线风险|Convexity]])指标;
	2. 针对股票的Beta()值;
	3. 针对金融衍生产品的Delta(), [[市场风险、Greeks 与动态对冲|Theta]](),[[市场风险、Greeks 与动态对冲|Gamma]](),[[市场风险、Greeks 与动态对冲|Vega]]()和Rho()指标等. 
	4. 风险绝对测度指标：Markovitz(1952)提出的衡量随机变量离散程度的方差(Variance)指标以及目前主流的VaR指标.
3. 管理风险:操作对冲,风险对冲
	1. 风险自留（Risk retention）是指公司对于其在经营业务时自然暴露出的某些风险决定承担而不是试图内部抵消或者是向另外的市场参与者转移所构成的风险集聚。公司自留的风险可能是融资的也可能是非融资的 
		1. 非融资（unfunden）自留风险：公司决定保留特定风险而不打算预备资金，而是在风险损失出现时公司另掏腰包。
		2. 融资的（funden）自留风险是指公司在风险事件转变为现实的损失之前，对此风险已经准备了特定的资金和资金来源。 融资的（funden）自留风险又可分为事前融资和事后融资。
			1. 设置专用准备金以弥补将来的可能损失就是事前融资。
			2. 预先协商一个信用额度，从而使风险损失发生后公司可以提款（贷款）就是事后融资。
	2. 风险抵消是一家公司能够自己减少或消除其风险暴露而不寻求另一家公司的帮助。
	3. 风险转移是通过一定的协议（合同，金融工具）将风险发生的不利影响从一家公司的股东转移到一个或多个人或者是一个或多个公司的股东。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Identify risk: determine the relevant risk factors.<br>
**2.** Measure risk: use relative and absolute measures.<br>
↳ **1.** For bonds and other interest-rate products, use duration (Duration) and convexity (Convexity).<br>
↳ **2.** For equities, use beta.<br>
↳ **3.** For derivatives, use Delta, Theta, Gamma, Vega, Rho, and related Greeks.<br>
↳ **4.** Absolute risk measures include variance (Variance), introduced by Markowitz (1952) to describe the dispersion of a random variable, and the now widely used VaR measure.<br>
**3.** Manage risk: use operational hedging and financial hedging.<br>
↳ **1.** *Risk retention* means that a company chooses to bear risks that arise naturally from its business rather than offsetting them internally or transferring them to another market participant. Retained risk may be unfunded or funded.<br>
↳ ↳ **1.** *Unfunded risk retention*: the company retains a specific risk without setting money aside and pays the loss from its own resources if it occurs.<br>
↳ ↳ **2.** *Funded risk retention*: before the risk becomes an actual loss, the company arranges dedicated funds or a funding source. This can be pre-loss or post-loss financing.<br>
↳ ↳ ↳ **1.** Establishing a dedicated reserve for possible future losses is pre-loss financing.<br>
↳ ↳ ↳ **2.** Negotiating a credit line in advance, so the company can draw a loan after a loss occurs, is post-loss financing.<br>
↳ **2.** *Risk reduction* means that a company reduces or eliminates its own exposure without relying on another company.<br>
↳ **3.** *Risk transfer* uses an agreement—such as a contract or financial instrument—to shift the adverse effect of a risk event from one company's shareholders to one or more people or to the shareholders of one or more other companies.<br>

><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>The market's expected return is 12%, the risk-free rate is 7%, and the standard deviation of the market return is 15%. One investor constructs a portfolio on the efficient frontier with an expected return of 10%; another constructs a portfolio on the efficient frontier with an expected return of 20%. What is the standard deviation of each portfolio?
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->


这个问题实际上是考察[[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|资本市场线]]（[[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|Capital Market Line]], CML）上的投资组合的标准差计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This question asks for portfolio standard deviations on the [[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|capital market line]] (Capital Market Line, CML).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

已知:
- 市场预期回报：$\mu_m = 12\%$
- [[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|无风险利率]]：$r_f = 7\%$
- 市场标准差：$\sigma_m = 15\%$
- 投资组合A的预期回报：$\mu_{p1} = 10\%$
- 投资组合B的预期回报：$\mu_{p2} = 20\%$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given:

- Expected market return: $\mu_m=12\%$
- [[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|Risk-free rate]]: $r_f=7\%$
- Market standard deviation: $\sigma_m=15\%$
- Expected return of Portfolio A: $\mu_{p1}=10\%$

由
$$
\sigma_p = \frac{\mu_p - r_f}{\mu_m - r_f}\sigma_m
$$
 分别代入数值

**Portfolio A ($\mu_{p1}=10\%$)**

设银行初始资产为 $A$，年末盈利为 $X$，$X$ 服从正态分布：
$$
X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)
$$

K + X > 0
$$

## 1.18

>[!question] 1.18
>一个投资组合经理主动地管理某投资组合，投资组合的beta系数为0.2。在去年，无风险利率为5%，主要股指的表现都很差，回报大约为-30%。投资组合经理的回报为-10%，投资组合经理自称在这样的市场条件下，自身表现很好。请对投资组合经理的观点进行讨论，
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>A portfolio manager actively manages a portfolio with a beta of 0.2. Last year, the risk-free rate was 5%, the major stock indices returned approximately -30%, and the manager's portfolio returned -10%. The manager claims to have performed well under those market conditions. Assess that claim.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据[[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|资本资产定价模型]]（CAPM）：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
According to the [[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|capital asset pricing model]] (CAPM):
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

R_p = R_f + \beta (R_m - R_f)

$$
已知
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $R_f = 5\%$
- $R_m = -30\%$
- $\beta = 0.2$

则理论上该投资组合的回报应该为：
$$
R_p^{\text{CAPM}} = 5\% + 0.2 \times (-30\% - 5\%) = 5\% + 0.2 \times (-35\%) = 5\% - 7\% = -2\%
$$
实际回报 $R_p = -10\%$，理论回报 $R_p^{\text{CAPM}} = -2\%$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The portfolio's CAPM-implied return is therefore the value calculated above. Its actual return was $R_p=-10\%$, whereas its CAPM-implied return was $R_p^{\text{CAPM}}=-2\%$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

那么，该投资组合实际回报比理论值低了(比按照风险敞口应取得的回报)低了8个百分点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The portfolio therefore underperformed the return implied by its market-risk exposure by 8 percentage points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|阿尔法值]]是实际回报与CAPM预期回报的差：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|Alpha]] is the difference between the actual return and the return expected under CAPM:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\alpha = R_p - \big[ R_f + \beta(R_m - R_f) \big]
$$

代入数据：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substituting the data:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\alpha = -10\% - (-2\%) = -8\%
$$

- **表面上**：经理在市场极端下跌（-30%）时仅亏损-10%，亏损幅度显著小于市场，看似不错。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **At first glance:** The manager lost only -10% while the market fell -30%, so the result appears comparatively good.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **深入分析**：该投资组合的$\beta$仅为0.2，意味着本身就对市场下跌并不敏感，理论亏损应仅为-2%。结果实际亏损-10%，表现比风险调整后应有回报**更差**，主动管理的“超额收益”为负。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Risk-adjusted analysis:** The portfolio's $\beta$ is only 0.2, so it should be relatively insensitive to a market decline; CAPM implies a loss of only -2%. The actual loss of -10% is therefore worse than the risk-adjusted benchmark, and the active manager generated negative abnormal performance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **结论**：
    - 投资组合经理以“市场暴跌下亏损较少”为优点自夸，其实忽略了组合本身的低$\beta$特性。
    - 若持有$\beta=1$的市场组合，-10%的结果的确非常优秀。但低$\beta$意味着本来风险和损失就应该远小于市场。
    - **正确评判主动管理表现，必须考虑风险调整后的基准（如CAPM或阿尔法）**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Conclusion:**
    - The manager's claim that the portfolio “lost less in a market crash” ignores the portfolio's low $\beta$.
    - A -10% return would indeed be excellent for a market portfolio with $\beta=1$, but a low-$\beta$ portfolio should have much lower market risk and a much smaller loss.
    - **Active management must be evaluated against a risk-adjusted benchmark, such as CAPM or alpha.**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
