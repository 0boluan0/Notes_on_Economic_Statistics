
## **信用VaR简介**  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Introduction to Credit VaR*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信用风险在险价值（[[信用组合风险与 Credit VaR|Credit VaR]]）是在一定置信度下，一个信用组合在规定展望期内可能发生的最大损失 。信用VaR常用于测算监管资本和经济资本，例如根据VaR来确定银行需要留存的资本金。它与市场风险VaR的主要区别在于展望期和模型复杂度：市场风险VaR常以1天为展望期，而非交易用途的信用风险VaR通常取1年（监管资本计量时也可取10天） 。信用VaR的计算依赖于真实世界下的违约概率估计，然后在风险中性假设下计算组合价值分布 。由于信用事件（违约、降级、信用利差变化）之间存在相关性（如违约率与经济周期相关），信用VaR模型通常需要考虑这种信用相关性 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Credit value at risk ([[信用组合风险与 Credit VaR|Credit VaR]]) is a high quantile of the loss distribution of a credit portfolio over a specified horizon. It is used in regulatory and economic-capital analysis to determine how much capital a bank should hold against unexpected credit losses. Compared with market-risk VaR, credit VaR usually has a longer horizon and a more complex loss mechanism: market VaR often uses one day, whereas non-trading credit portfolios commonly use one year; some trading-book credit calculations use ten days. Economic credit-risk measurement normally uses real-world default and migration probabilities. Risk-neutral probabilities and market-consistent spreads enter when future instruments must be repriced. Because defaults, downgrades, and spread moves depend on common economic conditions, credit VaR must also model credit dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，定义随机损失$L$的分布函数为$F_L(x)$，则置信度$\alpha$下的VaR定义为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if random loss $L$ has distribution function $F_L(x)$, VaR at confidence level $\alpha$ is defined as:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

\mathrm{VaR}_\alpha = \inf{x:;P(L \le x)\ge \alpha}.

$$

- 信用VaR计算可区分只考虑违约损失的模型和同时考虑降级、利差变动等更广义损失的模型。违约损失一般为违约时的敞口乘以$(1-\text{回收率})$ 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Credit VaR models may cover only default loss or may also include broader losses from rating migration and credit-spread movements. Default loss is generally exposure at default multiplied by $(1-\text{recovery rate})$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- 信用VaR高分位点常采用更复杂的模型（如Vasicek、CreditMetrics等），且需要用1年期违约概率等较长期参数 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- High-quantile Credit VaR usually requires models such as Vasicek or CreditMetrics and longer-horizon inputs such as one-year default probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

VaR rises to RMB 1 million only above 99% confidence, for example at 99.5%. Specifically:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 95%置信度：$P(L \le 0)=99%>95%$，因此$\mathrm{VaR}_{95%}=0$万元。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- At 95% confidence, $P(L \le 0)=99%>95%$, so $\mathrm{VaR}_{95%}=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- 99%置信度：$P(L \le 0)=99%\ge99%$，因此$\mathrm{VaR}_{99%}=0$万元。

## **信用评级迁移矩阵**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Credit-Rating Transition Matrices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

信用评级迁移矩阵描述了公司信用级别在一定时间内从一个等级迁移到另一个等级的概率 。例如，表格中每一行代表初始评级，每一列代表到期末评级，矩阵元素为对应的转移概率。评级迁移矩阵通常基于历史数据统计得到 。对多期迁移概率，可利用矩阵幂运算：若假设不同年份之间评级变动独立，则2年期迁移矩阵可以用1年期矩阵自乘得到（$P_{2}=P_{1}\times P_{1}$），类似地，5年期迁移矩阵为1年期矩阵的5次方 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A credit-rating transition matrix records the probability that a company moves from one rating to another over a specified period. Each row is the initial rating, each column the ending rating, and each cell the corresponding transition probability. The matrix is normally estimated from historical data. Under a time-homogeneous Markov assumption, multi-period transitions are obtained by matrix powers: the two-year matrix is $P_{2}=P_{1}\times P_{1}$ and the five-year matrix is the fifth power of the one-year matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_{5\text{年}}=(P_{1\text{年}})^5.$$

从矩阵幂的结果可看出，评级保持不变的概率随着时间增长而迅速降低，而发生违约的累积概率会显著增加 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Matrix powers show that the probability of remaining in the original rating generally falls with horizon, while cumulative default probability rises.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

对于短于1年的时间段，可以通过对1年期矩阵开方来近似。例如，6个月期迁移矩阵可近似为1年矩阵的平方根（$P_{0.5\text{年}}=\sqrt{P_{1\text{年}}}$），3个月期为四次方根 。由于时间越短，评级变化的概率越小，所以1个月的信用评级保持不变的概率非常高 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For periods shorter than one year, a matrix root can provide an approximation. A six-month matrix may be approximated by the square root of the annual matrix, $P_{0.5\text{-year}}=\sqrt{P_{1\text{-year}}}$, and a three-month matrix by the fourth root. Because short-horizon transition probabilities are small, the probability of retaining the same rating over one month is very high.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

需要注意的是，迁移矩阵计算通常假设不同时间段之间评级迁移相互独立，但实际情况中存在评级惯性（momentum）：被降级的公司在短期内再被降级的概率较高 。不过在大多数情况下，独立性假设依然是一个合理的近似。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This calculation assumes time-homogeneous Markov transitions. Actual ratings can display momentum: a recently downgraded company may be more likely to be downgraded again soon. The Markov approximation is nevertheless widely used as a tractable baseline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **Vasicek模型**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Vasicek Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

Vasicek模型是巴塞尔II内部评级法（IRB）下用于计算贷款组合高分位违约率分布的模型 。该模型利用一因子高斯Copula，将组合违约率在给定置信度下的最坏情况违约率（Worst-Case Default Rate, WCDR）与组合中单个贷款的违约概率（$PD$）和资产相关性参数$\rho$联系起来 。具体地，对于一笔贷款，一年期信贷违约概率为$PD$，资产相关系数为$\rho$，在置信水平$X$下的最坏违约率给出公式 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Vasicek model underlies the high-quantile portfolio default-rate calculation in the Basel II internal-ratings-based approach. It uses a one-factor Gaussian copula to relate the worst-case default rate (WCDR) at a chosen confidence level to individual-loan [[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|PD]] and asset correlation $\rho$. For a loan with one-year PD, asset correlation $\rho$, and confidence level $X$, WCDR is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

WCDR(T,X) ;=; N!\Bigl(\frac{N^{-1}(PD)+\sqrt{\rho},N^{-1}(X)}{\sqrt{1-\rho}}\Bigr),

$$

其中$N(\cdot)$为标准正态分布函数，$N^{-1}(\cdot)$为其反函数，$T$为展望期（通常取1年），$X$为置信度（如99.9%）。该公式说明在置信水平$X$下，组合的违约率分布上分位点由上述公式计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $N(\cdot)$ is the standard-normal CDF, $N^{-1}(\cdot)$ its inverse, $T$ the horizon—usually one year—and $X$ the confidence level, such as 99.9%. The formula gives the upper $X$-quantile of the limiting portfolio default-rate distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

在大组合（贷款数$M$足够大）假设下，可近似将组合经济资本（意外损失）计算为各贷款风险贡献之和 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an asymptotically fine-grained portfolio with sufficiently many loans, economic capital or unexpected loss can be approximated by summing loan-level contributions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

\text{资本需求} = \sum_{i=1}^M (WCDR_i - PD_i)\times LGD_i \times EAD_i,

$$

其中$LGD_i$为损失率（$1-\text{回收率}$），$EAD_i$为违约时敞口。对于同质组合（所有贷款参数相同），即可用$(WCDR-PD)\times LGD\times EAD$给出一笔贷款的意外损失，再乘以贷款数量。值得注意的是，[[巴塞尔银行资本与流动性监管|Basel]] II规定$\rho$由监管给定，置信度$X$通常取99.9% 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $LGD_i$ is loss given default, or $1-\text{recovery rate}$, and $EAD_i$ is exposure at default. In a homogeneous portfolio, unexpected loss per loan is $(WCDR-PD)\times LGD\times EAD$, multiplied by the number of loans for portfolio loss. Under [[巴塞尔银行资本与流动性监管|Basel II]], the regulatory correlation function is prescribed and confidence level $X$ is generally 99.9%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **CreditRisk Plus模型**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The CreditRisk+ Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

[[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|Credit Risk]] Plus模型由瑞士信贷于1997年提出，是一种信用风险VaR计算模型，借鉴了保险行业的解析近似方法 。该模型假设贷款组合中各笔贷款违约事件相互独立或近似独立：若一个金融机构有$n$笔贷款，每笔贷款一年内违约概率为$q$，则组合中恰好$m$笔贷款违约的概率服从二项分布。当$n$很大且$q$很小时，可近似用泊松分布表示违约个数$m$的概率 ：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|CreditRisk+]], introduced by Credit Suisse in 1997, is an actuarial model for Credit VaR. In its simplest setup, loan defaults are independent or approximately independent. If a portfolio contains $n$ loans, each with one-year default probability $q$, the number $m$ of defaults is binomial. When $n$ is large and $q$ small, the distribution is approximated by a Poisson law:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

P(M=m)\approx e^{-\mu}\frac{\mu^m}{m!},\quad \mu = nq.

$$

其中$\mu$为组合的预期违约数。若各贷款违约概率不同，但都较小且$\mu$定义为全组合的平均违约数，则上述Poisson近似仍然适用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mu$ is the portfolio's expected number of defaults. If individual default probabilities differ but are all small and $\mu$ is their sum, the Poisson approximation can still apply.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

此外，如果对未来整体违约率$q$存在不确定性（将$q$视为随机变量），常假设$\mu=nq$服从Gamma分布，[[泊松过程|Poisson]]-Γ混合后会得到负二项分布 。这反映了违约率随机性增大时尾部风险更重的特点。Credit Risk Plus通常只计算违约频率分布，再结合损失严重度（LGD和敞口）来得到损失分布，进而计算VaR。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the future portfolio-wide default rate $q$ is itself uncertain, $\mu=nq$ is often modelled with a Gamma distribution. A [[泊松过程|Poisson]]–Gamma mixture produces a negative-binomial distribution, capturing the heavier tail created by random default intensity. CreditRisk+ first models default frequency, then combines it with loss severity—LGD and exposure—to obtain a loss distribution and Credit VaR.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **CreditMetrics模型**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The CreditMetrics Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

CreditMetrics模型于1997年由摩根大通提出，用于计算信用组合在考虑信用评级变动（包括降级和违约）下的损失分布 。该模型需要对每个对手方使用信用评级迁移矩阵，并通过Monte Carlo模拟来预测未来各借款人的评级变化情况 。其核心步骤包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CreditMetrics, introduced by J.P. Morgan in 1997, models a credit portfolio's loss distribution when ratings can migrate, including both downgrade and default. It uses a transition matrix for every counterparty and Monte Carlo simulation to generate future rating states. Its main steps are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **模拟评级变化**：根据1年期评级迁移概率，每条模拟路径随机决定每个对手方一年后的信用等级 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Simulate rating transitions:** On each path, draw every counterparty's rating one year ahead from the one-year transition probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **重新定价或违约损失计算**：若某对手方一年后未违约，则对该对手所有未到期交易按当时信用等级对应的信用利差曲线重新定价，计算损失；若发生违约，则损失等于违约时敞口乘以$(1-R)$ 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Reprice or calculate default loss:** If a counterparty survives, reprice all outstanding transactions using the credit-spread curve for its simulated rating. If it defaults, loss equals exposure at default times $(1-R)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **统计损失分布**：重复上述模拟过程多次，得到所有交易对手的总损失分布，然后从中求取所需置信度下的信用VaR 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Construct the loss distribution:** Repeat the simulation many times, aggregate losses across all counterparties on each path, and take the desired Credit VaR quantile.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

CreditMetrics模型可处理降级造成的市值损失，不仅限于违约损失，这区别于仅计算违约损失的模型 。在模拟中，通常需要考虑不同对手信用等级之间的相关性，可通过Gaussian Copula等方法将多家公司评级变动联系起来 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CreditMetrics captures mark-to-market losses from downgrades as well as outright default loss. Simulation should also account for dependence across counterparties, for example through a Gaussian copula linking firms' rating changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**计算题 7：** 某债权对手违约时敞口$EAD=500$万元，当前信用等级违约概率$PD=1%$，回收率$R=40%$。试计算此贷款的一年预期损失（EL）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Exercise 7:** A loan has EAD of RMB 5 million, one-year PD of 1%, and recovery $R=40%$. Calculate one-year expected loss (EL).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**参考答案：** 预期损失可以简单地计算为$\mathrm{EL}=EAD\times PD\times (1-R)$。代入数据：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** Expected loss is $\mathrm{EL}=EAD\timesPD\times(1-R)$. Substituting the values:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

\mathrm{EL}=500\times 0.01 \times (1-0.4)=500\times 0.01\times 0.6 =3\text{ 万元}.

$$

  

## **信用价差风险**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Credit-Spread Risk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

信用价差风险指的是信用敏感产品对信用利差变化的敞口。当计算信用VaR时，应同时考虑信用利差可能的波动。一种简单的做法是沿用市场风险VaR的思路：收集相关企业历史信用利差变化（如过去500天），使用历史模拟法计算1天、99%置信度的信用VaR，然后将结果按$\sqrt{10}$（假设风险随时间平方根扩散）放大至10天VaR 。但此法存在问题：历史数据中未违约公司未来可能违约这一风险未体现，且并非所有公司每日更新信用价差，数据质量受限 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Credit-spread risk is exposure of a credit-sensitive instrument to changes in its spread. A simple Credit VaR calculation can mirror market-risk historical simulation: collect daily spread changes for the relevant issuers, for example over 500 days; calculate one-day 99% Credit VaR; then scale by $\sqrt{10}$ to ten days under the square-root-of-time assumption. This has important limitations. A surviving issuer's historical spread series does not contain its possible future jump to default, many issuers do not have reliable daily spread observations, and square-root scaling may fail under stressed, nonlinear credit dynamics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

另一种更精细的方法类似CreditMetrics：首先利用第21.1节中的方法得到10天期限的信用迁移矩阵，然后通过Monte Carlo模拟未来10天内各公司评级变化 。具体步骤为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A richer approach resembles CreditMetrics. First derive a ten-day transition matrix using the method in Section 21.1, then use Monte Carlo simulation to generate firms' ten-day rating outcomes:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 根据10天迁移矩阵抽样每家公司未来评级（保持原评级、上升、下降或违约） ；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Draw each firm's ten-day rating from the transition matrix: unchanged, upgraded, downgraded, or defaulted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- 对每个评级对应的信用利差变动概率分布进行抽样，确定10天后各评级下的信用利差 ；
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- For each rating, draw a credit-spread change from its conditional distribution to determine the ten-day spread.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- 根据模拟得到的信用利差水平计算每笔资产的价值，并由此得到整个组合10天后的价值，再计算组合损失分布，从而得到10天VaR 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Revalue every asset at the simulated spread, aggregate the portfolio value after ten days, and construct the loss distribution and ten-day VaR.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

  

在信用价差风险模拟中，也需考虑信用等级之间的相关性。可以通过两种方法：一是使用Gaussian Copula关联不同公司的评级变动（如前述）；二是假设所有信用等级的利差变化高度相关，即当一个评级的产品利差上升时，其他评级产品利差也同时上升 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Spread-risk simulation must also model dependence. One method links firms' rating transitions through a Gaussian copula. Another assumes spread changes across rating categories are highly correlated, so spreads for several grades widen together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**计算题 8：** 某产品计算出1天展望、99%置信度的信用VaR为10万元。若假定风险随时间平方根扩散，则10天展望的信用VaR约为多少？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Exercise 8:** A product has one-day 99% Credit VaR of RMB 100,000. Under square-root-of-time scaling, estimate ten-day Credit VaR.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**参考答案：** 根据风险随时间平方根扩散的近似，10天VaR可用1天VaR乘以$\sqrt{10}$估算：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** Multiply one-day VaR by $\sqrt{10}$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

\text{VaR}_{10\text{天}}\approx \sqrt{10}\times \text{VaR}_{1\text{天}} = \sqrt{10}\times 10\text{ 万元} \approx 31.62\text{ 万元}.

$$

  

**计算题 9：** 现有一张2年期零息信用债券，面值$1000，无风险利率3%，当前信用利差为200bp（总到期收益率5%）。若未来信用评级降级导致利差升至500bp（总收益率8%），试计算债券价格的变化及损失（假设连续复利）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Exercise 9:** A two-year zero-coupon credit bond has face value USD 1,000, a 3% risk-free rate, and current spread of 200 bp, giving total yield 5%. If a downgrade widens the spread to 500 bp and total yield to 8%, calculate the price change and loss under continuous compounding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**参考答案：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 当前债券价格：无风险3%+信用利差2%=5%的利率，2年期零息债券价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Current bond price: with 3% risk-free rate plus 2% spread, total yield is 5%, so the two-year zero-coupon price is:<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    $$P_0 = \frac{1000}{(1+0.05)^2} \approx 907.03\text{ 美元}.$$
    
2. 利差升至500bp时，总收益率8%，债券未来价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** After spread widens to 500 bp, total yield is 8%, so the new price is:<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    $$P_1 = \frac{1000}{(1+0.08)^2} \approx 857.34\text{ 美元}.$$
    
3. 价格下跌损失为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** The loss from the price decline is:<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    $$\Delta P = P_0 - P_1 \approx 907.03 - 857.34 = 49.69\text{ 美元}.$$
    

  

该损失即因信用利差扩大而产生的信用价差风险损失。若债券发生违约，则价格将骤降为回收价值（比如400美元），相应损失计算方法同上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the credit-spread loss. If the bond defaults and falls to a recovery value such as USD 400, the loss is calculated in the same way from the pre-default price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
