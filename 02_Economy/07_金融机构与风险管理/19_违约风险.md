[[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|Credit risk]] is the risk that a debtor fails to perform its contractual debt obligations when due. This chapter covers credit ratings, [[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|probability of default]], recovery rates, credit default swaps (CDS), and related measures.

## 1.1**信用评级**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1 Credit Ratings*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评级机构（如穆迪、标普、惠誉）对公司债券的信用质量进行评定，通常从高到低划分等级。这些机构 **“顺周期”评级**（rate through the cycle）以稳定为目标，不会因短期经济波动频繁调整评级 。例如，在短期经济下行可能提高未来6个月违约概率时，评级机构通常不会立刻下调公司长期信用评级，而是关注长期违约趋势 。内部评级方面，银行等金融机构会根据借款人的财务指标（如资产负债比率、[[财务报表、财务比率与财务预测|流动比率]]、盈利能力等）对客户进行打分评级 。这有助于银行对无公开评级的小型企业进行信用评估。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rating agencies (such as Moody's, S&P, and Fitch) assess the credit quality of corporate bonds, typically dividing them from high to low. These agencies conduct a "through-the-cycle" rating aimed at stability and do not frequently adjust ratings in response to short-term economic fluctuations. For instance, when a short-term economic downturn may increase the future six-month probability of default, rating agencies usually do not immediately downgrade a company’s long-term credit rating but instead focus on long-term trends of default. In terms of internal ratings, financial institutions such as banks evaluate customers based on financial metrics (such as debt-to-equity ratio, [[财务报表、财务比率与财务预测|Current Ratio]], profitability, etc.). This helps banks assess the creditworthiness of small businesses without public ratings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**Altman Z-score 模型**是最早的违约预测模型之一。它用五个财务比率构建线性组合来估计上市制造业公司的违约风险。公式为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **Altman Z-score model** is one of the earliest default-prediction models. It combines five financial ratios linearly to estimate default risk for publicly traded manufacturing firms:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$Z = 1.2X_1 + 1.4X_2 + 3.3X_3 + 0.6X_4 + 1.0X_5$$

其中：$X_1$=流动资产净额/总资产，$X_2$=留存收益/总资产，$X_3$=息税前利润/总资产，$X_4$=股权市值/总负债，$X_5$=销售额/总资产 。计算出的$Z$值若大于3，则认为公司违约可能性很低；$2<Z<3$时有警戒意义；$1<Z<2$时违约可能性较高；$Z<1$时违约风险极大 。这种模型优势在于利用公开的会计数据快速评估风险，但也存在假设局限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Where:
$X_1$ = Net working assets / Total assets,
$X_2$ = Retained earnings / Total assets,
$X_3$ = Earnings before interest and taxes (EBIT) / Total assets,
$X_4$ = Equity market value / Total liabilities,
$X_5$ = Sales revenue / Total assets.

The value of $Z$ is considered low risk of default if it is greater than 3; it is a warning sign when $2<Z<3$; high risk of default when $1<Z<2$; and extremely high when $Z<1$. The advantage of this model lies in quickly assessing risk using publicly available financial data. However, it also has assumptions that limit its applicability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2**历史违约概率**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2 Historical Default Probability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==就是泊松分布==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==This is a Poisson-style intensity setup.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

违约概率有**无条件违约概率**和**违约强度（hazard rate）**两种描述方式。定义生存概率$V(t)$为到期前无违约的概率，则$t$时刻的无条件违约概率为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Default can be described through an **unconditional default probability** or a **default intensity (hazard rate)**. Let survival probability $V(t)$ be the probability of no default before time $t$. The unconditional probability of default by time $t$ is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$O(t)=1-V(t),. $$

如果设风险中性条件下的瞬时违约率为$\lambda(t)$，则有微分方程$V’(t)=-\lambda(t)V(t)$。解得累积违约概率：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the instantaneous risk-neutral default intensity is $\lambda(t)$, survival satisfies $V’(t)=-\lambda(t)V(t)$. Hence cumulative default probability is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$O(t)=1-\exp\Bigl(-\int_0^t\lambda(u),du\Bigr),. $$

当违约强度$\lambda$恒定时，此式简化为$O(t)=1-e^{-\lambda t}$ 。例如若$\lambda=1\%/年$，则1年累计违约概率为$1-e^{-0.01}=0.00995\approx0.995\%$ 。违约强度$\lambda(t)$是条件违约率，表示在$t$时刻公司违约的瞬时概率（假定此前未违约）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When default intensity $\lambda$ is constant, the expression becomes $O(t)=1-e^{-\lambda t}$. If $\lambda=1\%$ per year, one-year cumulative default probability is $1-e^{-0.01}=0.00995\approx0.995\%$. The intensity $\lambda(t)$ is a conditional instantaneous default rate: it applies at time $t$ given survival up to that point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**示例题：** 假设某公司每年的违约强度恒定为$\lambda=2\%$。求该公司在5年内累计违约概率，以及第3年出现违约的条件概率（假设前两年无违约）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Example Question:** Assume that a company has a constant default intensity of $\lambda=2\%$ annually. Calculate the cumulative default probability over 5 years, as well as the conditional probability of defaulting in the third year (assuming no defaults in the first two years).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** 5年累计违约概率$=1-e^{-0.02\times5}=1-e^{-0.1}\approx0.0952;(9.52\%)$。第3年在此前未违约的条件下违约的概率为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** The five-year cumulative default probability is $1-e^{-0.02\times5}=1-e^{-0.1}\approx0.0952$ (9.52%). Conditional on survival through the first two years, the probability of default during year three is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac{O(3)-O(2)}{1-O(2)}=\frac{[1-e^{-0.02\times3}]-[1-e^{-0.02\times2}]}{e^{-0.02\times2}}=\frac{e^{-0.04}-e^{-0.06}}{e^{-0.04}}\approx e^{-0.02}-1 \approx 1.98\%,. $$

这里使用了无条件违约概率$O(t)=1-e^{-0.02t}$和条件概率公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unconditional default probability $O(t)=1-e^{-0.02t}$ and conditional probability formulas were used.

## 1.3**回收率**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3 Recovery Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  =1-[[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|LGD]](损失率)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$=1-LGD$, where LGD is the loss rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当公司违约时，债权人在破产清算或重组中能追回的债券价值占债券面值的比例称为**回收率**（$R$）。通常按违约后（如一个月后）债券交易价格与面值之比来定义 。例如某债券面值100元，违约后一个月交易价为30元，则回收率为30%。损失率（LGD）则为$1-R$。历史数据显示，不同债务优先级的回收率差别较大，第一留置权债券平均回收率较高 。此外，回收率通常与经济状况关联：违约率高的年份回收率倾向较低 ，这意味着信用风险加剧时损失率会上升。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a company defaults, the proportion of the bond's face value that a creditor can recover in bankruptcy liquidation or restructuring is known as the recovery rate ($R$). Typically, it is defined by the ratio of the bond's price after default (e.g., one month after) to its face value. For instance, if a bond has a face value of 100 yuan and trades at 30 yuan one month after default, the recovery rate would be 30%. The loss rate (LGD), on the other hand, is $1-R$. Historical data shows significant differences in recovery rates across different levels of debt priority; first-priority collateral bonds generally have higher recovery rates. Additionally, recovery rates are often correlated with economic conditions: years with high default rates tend to see lower recovery rates, implying that credit risk intensifies when loss rates rise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**示例题：** 某公司发行的一张债券面值$1,000，违约后一个月市场价格为$350。计算该债券的回收率及违约损失率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Example:** A bond has face value USD 1,000 and trades at USD 350 one month after default. Calculate its recovery rate and loss given default.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** 回收率$R=350/1000=35\%$，[[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|违约损失率]]$1-R=65\%$。这意味着债权人只收回面值的35%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** Recovery rate is $R=350/1000=35\%$, and [[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|loss given default]] is $1-R=65\%$. The creditor recovers only 35% of face value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1 CDS内容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1 How a CDS Works*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信用违约互换（CDS）是一种场外信贷衍生品，允许投资者买卖对特定参考实体（如公司或国家）违约风险的保护 。CDS的基本结构为：保护买方支付固定的年化利差（以基点表示）给保护卖方，买方获得当参考实体发生**信用事件**（如破产、重组违约等）时的补偿权利 。参考实体的所有债券总面值为名义本金（notional）。若无违约，保护买方定期支付费用但没有任何补偿；若发生违约，卖方按照合同约定进行补偿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A credit default swap (CDS) is an OTC credit derivative through which investors buy or sell protection against a credit event affecting a specified reference entity, such as a company or sovereign. The protection buyer pays a fixed annualised spread, quoted in basis points, to the protection seller. In return, the buyer receives compensation if a contractual **credit event**, such as bankruptcy or a qualifying restructuring, occurs. The CDS notional is the contractually agreed amount of protection; it is not necessarily the total face value of the reference entity's bonds. Without a credit event, the buyer pays periodic premiums and receives no protection payment. After a credit event, the seller settles according to the contract.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **保护买方义务：** 按约定利差定期（通常季度）支付保费；
- **保护卖方义务：** 若信用事件发生，以实物交割或现金结算方式补偿买方。实物交割时，保护买方以面值将参考实体债券卖给卖方 ；现金结算时，卖方向买方支付名义本金与违约后市价差额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Protection buyer's obligation:** Pay the agreed premium periodically, usually quarterly.
- **Protection seller's obligation:** Compensate the buyer after a credit event through physical or cash settlement. Under physical settlement, the buyer delivers an eligible bond and receives par. Under cash settlement, the seller pays the difference between notional and the bond's post-default value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CDS交易通常有标准化合约，例如5年期CDS。举例：某5年期CDS名义$1亿，年费率90bp，买方每季度支付$225,000（=0.009/4×$100,000,000）给卖方。如果第三年发生违约且采用实物交割，保护买方可用$1亿面值债券按面值卖给卖方，获得$1亿现金 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CDS contracts are commonly standardised, with five years a benchmark maturity. Suppose a five-year CDS has USD 100 million notional and a 90 bp annual spread. The buyer pays $0.009/4\times\$100{,}000{,}000=\$225{,}000$ each quarter. If default occurs in year three and settlement is physical, the buyer can deliver USD 100 million face value of eligible bonds and receive USD 100 million in cash.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2 CDX信用指数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2 CDX Credit Index*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**CDX 信用指数：** 为了分散风险，市场上出现了信用违约指数，如北美投资级CDX（CDX NA IG）和欧洲iTraxx投资级指数，每个指数包含125家大型企业的信用风险组合 。这些指数每年定期（3月、9月）调整标的成分 。例如，假设五年期CDX NA IG的标价为买165bp、卖166bp ，表示可以以166bp的年费率买入对所有125家公司各$1保护（每家公司$1名义额）。用户可自定义名义金额，例如要对每家公司买入$80万保护，总名义为125×$80万。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**CDX credit indices:** Credit indices diversify exposure across multiple reference entities. Examples include the North American investment-grade CDX (CDX. NA. IG) and the European investment-grade iTraxx index, each described here as containing 125 large companies. New index series are rolled in March and September. If a five-year CDX. NA. IG market is quoted 165/166 bp, protection can be bought at 166 bp on equal notionals across all 125 names. To buy USD 0.8 million of protection on each name, total index notional is $125\times\$0.8$ million = USD 100 million.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**示例题：** 某5年期CDS名义本金为$2000万，年费率120bp（季度支付）。假设无违约发生，保护买方每季度支付多少保费？若第三年发生违约，采用实物交割，保护买方将获多少补偿？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Example Question:** A 5-year CDS with a notional principal of $20 million has an annual fee rate of 120 bp (quarterly payments). Assuming no default occurs, how much premium does the protection buyer pay each quarter? If a default occurs in the third year and physical settlement is used, how much compensation will the protection buyer receive?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** 年费率120bp对应0.012，季度付则每季度支付$2000万×0.012/4=$60,000。若违约并实物交割，保护买方可按面值将$2000万面值的参考债券卖给保护卖方，从而获得$2000万现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** A 120 bp spread is 0.012 per year, so quarterly premium is $\$20{,}000{,}000\times0.012/4=\$60{,}000$. If default occurs and settlement is physical, the buyer can deliver USD 20 million face value of eligible reference obligations and receive USD 20 million at par.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3 信用利差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3 Credit Spread*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **信用利差（Credit Spread）：** 债券收益率与无风险收益率（同期限国债）之差，反映承受信用风险要求的额外收益 。例如，10年期公司债收益率8%，同期国债5%，则信用利差为300bp。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Credit spread:** The difference between a bond's yield and the yield on a comparable risk-free government bond. It is compensation for credit and related risks. If a ten-year corporate bond yields 8% and a comparable government bond yields 5%, the spread is 300 bp.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.4 CDS利差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4 CDS Spread*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **CDS利差（CDS Spread）：** 购买信用保护的年费率，用基点表示，相当于每年为名义$1支付的保护费百分比 。若某公司5年期CDS利差为2%，即每年为每$100支付$2的保护费。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **CDS spread:** The annual rate paid for credit protection, quoted in basis points. A 2% five-year CDS spread means that the buyer pays USD 2 per year for each USD 100 of notional, subject to the contract's premium schedule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.5 资产互换及基差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.5 Asset Swaps and Basis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **资产互换（Asset Swap Spread）：** 将固定收益债券转换为浮息工具时得到的等效利差。它通常等于公司债券收益率扣除参考浮动利率（如Libor Swap利率）的差值。资产互换价差提供了债券收益率相对于掉期（浮动）基准的直接衡量 。例如：若一个公司债发行面值$100票面9%的年息，市场报价95元，可通过支付$5并支付票息收取Libor+X的掉期来获得损益平衡，则X即为该债的资产互换利差 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Asset-swap spread:** The equivalent spread obtained when a fixed-rate bond is transformed into a floating-rate position. It is commonly measured relative to a swap floating-rate benchmark. For example, suppose a USD 100 face-value corporate bond with a 9% annual coupon trades at 95. If an investor pays the USD 5 price adjustment and enters a swap that pays away the coupon in return for LIBOR plus $X$, the break-even $X$ is the bond's asset-swap spread.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **CDS-债券基差：** 定义为CDS利差与同期限公司债收益率利差之差：
    $$\text{CDS-Bond 基差} = \text{CDS利差} - \text{债券收益率利差} ,. $$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **CDS-bond basis:** defined as the difference between the CDS spread and the yield spread of corporate bonds of the same maturity:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    理论上CDS卖方承担债券违约风险，与债券收益应该一致，因此基差应接近0。然而实际中因为交易成本、流动性和技术性因素（如债券并非在平价交易）等，基差常非零 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Theoretically, the CDS seller assumes the risk of bond default risk, and the basis should be close to zero since the credit spread should match the bond's yield. However, in practice, due to transaction costs, liquidity issues, and technical factors (such as bonds not trading at par), the basis is often non-zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**示例题：** 某公司5年期债券收益率为6%，同期国债收益率为4%，则债券信用利差为200bp。若该公司五年期CDS利差为350bp，则CDS-Bond基差为多少？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Example:** A company's five-year bond yields 6% while a comparable government bond yields 4%, giving a 200 bp bond spread. If its five-year CDS spread is 350 bp, what is the CDS–bond basis?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** 债券利差$=6\%-4\%=2\%=200\text{bp}$，CDS利差$=350\text{bp}$。基差$=350-200=150\text{bp}$。基差为正值表明CDS市场对该信用风险的定价高于债券市场。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** Bond spread $=6\%-4\%=2\%=200\text{bp}$ and CDS spread $=350\text{bp}$. Basis $=350-200=150\text{bp}$. A positive basis means the CDS market charges a larger spread for the credit risk than the cash-bond market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.6**利用信用利差和CDS估计违约概率**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.6 **Estimating Default Probabilities Using Credit Spreads and CDS*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通过信用利差或CDS利差可以近似推导违约强度和违约概率。在连续复利模型下，若假设违约强度恒定为$\lambda$，则信用利差$s$与$\lambda$、回收率$R$的关系可近似表示为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Credit or CDS spreads can be used to approximate default intensity and default probability. Under continuous compounding and a constant default intensity $\lambda$, credit spread $s$, intensity $\lambda$, and recovery rate $R$ are approximately related by:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$s\approx \lambda(1-R),. $$

等价于
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
equivalent to
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\lambda\approx\frac{s}{1-R},,$$

当$s$较小时此近似较准确 。例如，若某债券信用利差为200bp（即$s=0.02$），回收率$R=40\%$，则估计$\lambda\approx0.02/(1-0.4)=0.0333$。在5年内的累计违约概率约为$1-e^{-0.0333\times5}\approx0.154$（15.4%）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The approximation is most accurate when $s$ is small. If a bond's credit spread is 200 bp, so $s=0.02$, and recovery is $R=40\%$, then $\lambda\approx0.02/(1-0.4)=0.0333$. Five-year cumulative default probability is approximately $1-e^{-0.0333\times5}\approx0.154$, or 15.4%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更精确的方法是基于无套利定价，利用CDS定价方程对未来现金流现值进行配平，从而反推出$\lambda$。在CDS定价中，溢价腿与保障腿现值相等。解此方程可得到风险中性违约强度曲线 。对于常数$\lambda$的情形，亦有解析解：如果$Q$为$T$年累计违约概率，则$Q=1-\exp(-\lambda T)$，从中可解得
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A more precise approach is based on no-arbitrage pricing, using the CDS pricing equation to balance future cash flows' present values, thereby deducing $\lambda$. In the CDS pricing, the premium leg equals the present value of the protection leg. Solving this equation yields the risk-neutral default intensity curve. For the constant $\lambda$ case, there is also an analytical solution: if $Q$ is the cumulative probability of default over $T$ years, then $Q=1-\exp(-\lambda T)$, from which $\lambda$ can be derived.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\lambda=-\frac{1}{T}\ln(1-Q),. $$

。CDS市场提供的多期限利差可用于引导出整个违约强度期限结构。  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CDS spreads at several maturities can therefore be bootstrapped into a term structure of default intensities.

## 2.7**利用股价估计违约概率（Merton和KMV模型）**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.7 **Using Stock Prices to Estimate Default Probabilities (Merton and KMV Models)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**[[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|Merton模型]]：** 利用公司资产价值$V_t$作为被试价值，假设负债为固定面额$D$的零息债券，到期时偿还本金。Merton模型将公司股权$E$视为对公司资产的看涨期权：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**[[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|Merton model]]:** Let firm asset value be $V_t$ and represent debt as a zero-coupon bond with fixed face value $D$ due at maturity. The model treats equity $E$ as a call option on firm assets:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$E_0 = V_0 N(d_1) - D e^{-rT} N(d_2),$$

其中$$d_{1}=\frac{\ln(V_0/D)+(r+\tfrac12\sigma_V^2)T}{\sigma_V\sqrt{T}},\quad d_{2}=d_1-\sigma_V\sqrt{T},$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
thereof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$r$为无风险利率，$\sigma_V$为资产价值波动率。违约发生当资产价值低于负债面值，即$V_T<D$，该概率为风险中性违约概率：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $r$ is the risk-free rate and $\sigma_V$ is asset-value volatility. Default at maturity occurs when asset value is below debt face value, $V_T<D$. Under the model, the following expression gives the risk-neutral default probability:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_{\text{违约}} = \Phi(-d_2),, $$

其中$\Phi(\cdot)$为标准正态累积分布 。由于$V_0$和$\sigma_V$不可直接观测，可通过观测股权市值$E_0$和股价波动率$\sigma_E$，联立以上方程解出$V_0$和$\sigma_V$。然后可计算距离违约（Distance to Default, DD）：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Where $\Phi(\cdot)$ denotes the standard normal cumulative distribution function. Since $V_0$ and $\sigma_V$ cannot be directly observed, they can be estimated using observable stock market value $E_0$ and stock price volatility$\sigma_E$. By solving the above equations simultaneously, we can determine $V_0$ and $\sigma_V$. Then, the distance to default (Distance to Default, DD) can be calculated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$DD = d_2 = \frac{\ln(V_0/D)+(r-\tfrac12\sigma_V^2)T}{\sigma_V\sqrt{T}},$$

表示资产价值距违约点的距离（以标准差计） 。距离越小，公司违约可能性越高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The distance between the asset value and the default point, measured in standard deviations. The smaller this distance, the higher the likelihood of the company defaulting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


**KMV模型：** Moody’s KMV模型是对Merton模型的扩展，利用公开市场的股价和负债结构来估计公司资产价值及其波动率，并定义**违约点**（通常为短期债务加长期债务的一定比例）。根据计算出的DD，KMV通过历史违约经验将其转换为**预期违约频率（EDF）**，即实证意义上的违约概率 。简单来说，KMV模型也视股权为资产的看涨期权来反推资产价值，并结合市场数据校准违约概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**KMV model:** Moody's KMV extends the Merton framework. It uses observed equity prices and the liability structure to infer firm asset value and volatility, and defines a **default point**, commonly short-term debt plus a fraction of long-term debt. Historical default experience maps distance to default (DD) into an **expected default frequency (EDF)**, an empirical default probability. Like Merton, KMV treats equity as a call option on assets but calibrates the probability with market and historical data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**示例题：** 某公司总资产市场价值$V_0=100$亿，资产波动率$\sigma_V=30\%$，有一笔2年后到期的零息债务，面值$D=80$亿。[[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|无风险利率]]$r=3\%$，试估计该公司2年后的违约概率（按Merton模型计算）。

## **风险中性违约概率与真实世界违约概率**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Risk-Neutral Default Probability and Real World Default Probability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

违约概率有风险中性和真实世界两种概念。**真实世界违约概率**（或物理违约概率）来源于历史统计和评级机构数据，它反映借款人在实际环境下的违约频率；而**风险中性违约概率**来源于市场价格（如债券利差、CDS利差），反映在风险中性概率度量下的违约概率 。一般而言，风险中性违约概率高于真实世界违约概率 ，因为市场利差除了补偿违约预期损失外，还包含风险溢价和流动性溢价等。在实际运用中，风险中性违约概率用于定价和估值分析（如信用衍生品定价），而真实世界违约概率用于风险管理和情景分析 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Default probabilities have concepts of risk-neutral and real-world. **Real-world default probability** (or physical default probability) originates from historical statistics and rating agency data, reflecting the actual frequency of a borrower's default; whereas **risk-neutral default probability** originates from market prices (such as bond spreads, CDS spreads), reflecting the default probability under risk-neutral probability measures. Generally, risk-neutral default probability is higher than real-world default probability, because market spreads not only compensate for expected loss due to default but also include factors like risk premium and liquidity premiums. In practical applications, risk-neutral default probability is used for pricing and valuation analyses (such as credit derivatives pricing), while real-world default probability is used for risk management and scenario analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**示例题：** 某公司5年期CDS隐含违约概率为10%，同期历史数据估计的真实违约概率为6%。这两个概率的区别是什么？在估值和风险管理时应如何使用？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Example Question:** The implied default probability for a 5-year CDS by a certain company is 10%, while the estimated true default probability from historical data is 6%. What is the difference between these two probabilities? How should they be used in valuation and risk management?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** CDS隐含的10%违约概率是风险中性概率，已反映风险溢价，适用于信用衍生品的定价；6%是真实违约概率，用于衡量实际违约风险和进行风险情景分析。风险中性概率通常高于真实概率 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** The 10% default probability embedded in the CDS is a risk-neutral probability, reflecting risk premium, suitable for pricing credit derivatives; 6% is the actual default probability, used to measure the actual default risk and manage credit risk. Risk-neutral probabilities are typically higher than actual probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 0.5 year default | $q_1$ | 35 | $e^{-0.5 r}$ | $q_1 35 e^{-0.5 r}$ |
| 1.5 year default | $(1-q_1)q_2$ | 35 | $e^{-1.5 r}$ | $(1-q_1)q_2 35 e^{-1.5 r}$ |
| Survive to 2 years | $(1-q_1)(1-q_2)$ | 8 (t=1) & 108 (t=2) | $e^{-r}, e^{-2r}$ | $(1-q_1)8 e^{-r} + (1-q_1)(1-q_2)108 e^{-2r}$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令总现值等于市场价 $P_2$：  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set the total present value equal to the market price $P_2$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P_2 = q_1 35 e^{-0.5 r}
     + (1-q_1) 8 e^{-r}
     + (1-q_1) q_2 35 e^{-1.5 r}
     + (1-q_1)(1-q_2) 108 e^{-2 r}
$$  

已知 $q_1$，数值求解 $q_2$ 得  
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given $q_1$, solve for $q_2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\boxed{q_2 \approx 4.05\%/\text{年}}$$

**Results**

## **一、信用风险 vs. 信贷风险 vs. 交易对手风险 (19 · 1 · 1)**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Credit Risk vs. Lending Risk vs. Counterparty Risk (19 · 1 · 1)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|**概念**|**PPT 关键词**|**场景举例**|**如何“界定 & 监管”**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**信用风险 (Credit Risk)**|总括性“大帽子”|借款人违约、债券不兑付、票据跳票|[[巴塞尔银行资本与流动性监管|Basel]] II/III → [[巴塞尔银行资本与流动性监管|资本充足率]] (8 %)|
|**信贷风险 (Lending Risk)**|“贷款本金 + 利息收不回”|公司贷款、抵押贷|风险权重 + [[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|PD]]/LGD + 担保 & 补救期 (cure period)|
|**交易对手风险 (Counterparty Risk)**|“金融合约在到期前对手违约导致的**正价值**损失”|衍生品、回购、证券借贷|CEM / SA-CCR 计 **[[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|EAD]]**；CDS 对冲；双边保证金|
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| **Concept** | **Slide cue** | **Example** | **Boundary and regulation** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Credit risk** | The broad umbrella | Borrower default, missed bond payment, dishonoured bill | [[巴塞尔银行资本与流动性监管|Basel]] II/III capital rules, including the 8% total-capital baseline |
| **Lending risk** | Loan principal and interest are not recovered | Corporate loans and mortgages | Risk weights, PD, LGD, collateral, and a cure period |
| **Counterparty risk** | Loss of a financial contract's **positive value** because the counterparty defaults before maturity | Derivatives, repos, and securities lending | CEM or SA-CCR for **EAD**, CDS hedges, and bilateral margin |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **直观点**
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Briefly Put**
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- > 公司贷款 100 m → **信贷风险**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- > Company loan 100 m → **Credit risk**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- > 与公司做掉期，市值我浮盈 5 m → **交易对手风险**（就怕它提前破产）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- > To do a swap with the company, my market value would show a 5 million floating profit. → **Counterparty Risk** (fear it might go bankrupt prematurely)
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- > 二者合起来，都属于更大的 **信用风险** 范畴
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- > Both are subsets of the broader category of **[[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|credit risk]]**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **二、CDS 合同：多情景下买卖双方收益 (19 · 2 “CDS 合约收益比较”)**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*II. CDS Contracts: Yield Comparisons for Buyers and Sellers in Multiple Scenarios (19 · 2 "Comparing CDS Contract Yields")*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|**场景**|**买保护方**|**卖保护方**|
|---|---|---|
|**常态 (无违约)**|支付保费 (spread × 名义) → 为“安心”付钱|收取保费 → 赚取收益 (类浮息票息)|
|**债券利差扩大** (信用恶化)|CDS 市值上升，可 **平仓盈利** 或持有等待违约赔付|合约市值变负，要贴现金做 VM；账面亏损|
|**违约发生**|收到 **赔付 = 名义×(1–回收率)**|需支付该赔付；早前赚的保费可能远不足以覆盖|
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
|**Scenario**|**Buy Protection**|**Sell Protection**|
|---|---|---|
|**Normal (No Default)**|Pay premiums (spread × Notional) → Pay for “peace of mind”|Collect premiums → Earn income (similar to floating interest rate coupon yield)|
|**Bond Spread Widens** (Credit Downgrade)|CDS market value increases, allowing for **liquidation profits** or holding until default payout|Contract market value becomes negative, requiring posting cash for VM; accounting loss|
|**Default Occurs**|Receive **payment = Notional × (1 – Recovery Rate)**|Must pay this amount; earlier earned premiums may not be sufficient to cover|
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **买方收益最大化点**：利差刚放大时卖出合约（锁定涨出来的市值），而不一定等正式违约。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Maximizing Buyer Profits Point:** Sell the contract as soon as the spread widens, locking in gains from any subsequent price appreciation, rather than waiting for a formal default.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **四、信用等级下降 & 抵押品不足导致损失 (19 · 3 “担保品与补救期”)**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*IV. Losses Resulting from Downgraded Credit Ratings and Insufficient Collateral (19 · 3 "Collateral and Grace Periods")*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**链条 4 步**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A Four-Step Chain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **评级被下调** → 触发合约条款：需“追加抵押品 (Margin Call)”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Rating is downgraded** → Trigger contract terms: Margin call required.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
2. **对手没钱补** → 出现 **抵押品短缺 (Collateral shortfall)**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **The counterparty cannot meet the call** → a **collateral shortfall** emerges.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
3. 我方暴露增加，若其随后违约 → **损失 = 未补抵押 × (1–回收)**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** Our exposure increases, and if they subsequently default, **Loss = Unsecured Collateral × (1 – Recovery)**.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
4. **补救期 (Cure period)**：通常 1–3 天，若对方仍补不上可宣布违约并动用原抵押品。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**4.** **Cure Period**: Typically ranging from 1 to 3 days. If the counterparty fails to cure within this period, a breach may be declared, and the original collateral may be invoked.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

|**关键词**|**PPT 注释**|**管控手段**|
|---|---|---|
|**Trigger downgrade clause**|评级触发条款|预留流动性或设置 CSAs 缓级梯度|
|**Threshold**|允许无抵押额度|与评级挂钩；降级 → Threshold 下降|
|**Independent Amount**|额外 IM 存款|防快速恶化|
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
|**Keywords**|**PPT Annotations**|**Control Measures**|
|---|---|---|
|**Trigger downgrade clause**|Rating trigger clause|Reserve liquidity or set CSAs with tiered gradients|
|**Threshold**|Allowance for unsecured amount|Linked to ratings; downgrade → Threshold decreases|
|**Independent Amount**|Additional IM deposits|Prevent rapid deterioration|
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **要点**：即便没“真违约”，单纯评级下调也会带来 **流动性压力**；危机中 AIG 就因巨额 margin call 差点断裂。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Key Points:** Even without “actual default,” a downgrade in ratings can still cause **liquidity pressure**; during the crisis, AIG nearly collapsed due to massive margin calls.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **🌟 30 秒复盘**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*🌟 30 Second Reflection*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **信用风险三小弟**：贷款 (Lending) + 对手 (Counterparty) ⊂ 全部信用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Three layers of credit risk:** lending risk and counterparty risk are both subsets of the broader credit-risk category.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
2. **CDS 谁赚谁赔？** 买方给保费，等对手坏；卖方赚保费，赌对手不坏。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **CDS Who Wins and Loses?** The buyer pays the premium and wins if their counterparty defaults; the seller earns the premium and bets that the counterparty will not default.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
3. **Basis 看方向**：大于 0 → CDS 贵，常见危机流动性溢价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** **Read the [[CDS、信用利差与基差|basis]] by its sign:** above zero means CDS is more expensive than ASW, often reflecting a crisis liquidity premium.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
4. **降级 ≠ 违约，但会先逼出现金缺口**；补救期不到位就会转实亏。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**4.** **Downgrading does not equate to default, but it will force a cash shortfall first**; if remediation is inadequate, it will turn into an actual loss.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
