# **1.[[信用风险参数与模型.canvas|信用风险]]**

*1. **[[信用风险参数与模型.canvas|Credit Risk]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信用风险（Credit Risk）是指债务人未能按期履行约定的债务义务（违约）所带来的风险。本章内容涵盖信用评级、[[违约概率口径|违约概率]]、回收率、信用违约互换（CDS）等多个方面。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[信用风险参数与模型.canvas|Credit risk]] is the risk that a debtor fails to perform its contractual debt obligations when due. This chapter covers credit ratings, [[违约概率口径|probability of default]], recovery rates, credit default swaps (CDS), and related measures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.1**信用评级**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1 Credit Ratings*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评级机构（如穆迪、标普、惠誉）对公司债券的信用质量进行评定，通常从高到低划分等级。这些机构 **“顺周期”评级**（rate through the cycle）以稳定为目标，不会因短期经济波动频繁调整评级 。例如，在短期经济下行可能提高未来6个月违约概率时，评级机构通常不会立刻下调公司长期信用评级，而是关注长期违约趋势 。内部评级方面，银行等金融机构会根据借款人的财务指标（如资产负债比率、[[流动比率|流动比率]]、盈利能力等）对客户进行打分评级 。这有助于银行对无公开评级的小型企业进行信用评估。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rating agencies (such as Moody's, S&P, and Fitch) assess the credit quality of corporate bonds, typically dividing them from high to low. These agencies conduct a "through-the-cycle" rating aimed at stability and do not frequently adjust ratings in response to short-term economic fluctuations. For instance, when a short-term economic downturn may increase the future six-month probability of default, rating agencies usually do not immediately downgrade a company’s long-term credit rating but instead focus on long-term trends of default. In terms of internal ratings, financial institutions such as banks evaluate customers based on financial metrics (such as debt-to-equity ratio, [[流动比率|Current Ratio]], profitability, etc.). This helps banks assess the creditworthiness of small businesses without public ratings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**Altman Z-score 模型**是最早的违约预测模型之一。它用五个财务比率构建线性组合来估计上市制造业公司的违约风险。公式为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **Altman Z-score model** is one of the earliest default-prediction models. It combines five financial ratios linearly to estimate default risk for publicly traded manufacturing firms:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$Z = 1.2X_1 + 1.4X_2 + 3.3X_3 + 0.6X_4 + 1.0X_5$$

其中：$X_1$=流动资产净额/总资产，$X_2$=留存收益/总资产，$X_3$=息税前利润/总资产，$X_4$=股权市值/总负债，$X_5$=销售额/总资产 。计算出的$Z$值若大于3，则认为公司违约可能性很低；$2<Z<3$时有警戒意义；$1<Z<2$时违约可能性较高；$Z<1$时违约风险极大 。这种模型优势在于利用公开的会计数据快速评估风险，但也存在假设局限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Where:
$X_1$ = Net working assets / Total assets,
$X_2$ = Retained earnings / Total assets,
$X_3$ = Earnings before interest and taxes (EBIT) / Total assets,
$X_4$ = Equity market value / Total liabilities,
$X_5$ = Sales revenue / Total assets.

The value of $Z$ is considered low risk of default if it is greater than 3; it is a warning sign when $2<Z<3$; high risk of default when $1<Z<2$; and extremely high when $Z<1$. The advantage of this model lies in quickly assessing risk using publicly available financial data. However, it also has assumptions that limit its applicability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2**历史违约概率**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2 Historical Default Probability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==就是泊松分布==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==This is a Poisson-style intensity setup.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

违约概率有**无条件违约概率**和**违约强度（hazard rate）**两种描述方式。定义生存概率$V(t)$为到期前无违约的概率，则$t$时刻的无条件违约概率为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Default can be described through an **unconditional default probability** or a **default intensity (hazard rate)**. Let survival probability $V(t)$ be the probability of no default before time $t$. The unconditional probability of default by time $t$ is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$O(t)=1-V(t),. $$

如果设风险中性条件下的瞬时违约率为$\lambda(t)$，则有微分方程$V’(t)=-\lambda(t)V(t)$。解得累积违约概率：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the instantaneous risk-neutral default intensity is $\lambda(t)$, survival satisfies $V’(t)=-\lambda(t)V(t)$. Hence cumulative default probability is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$O(t)=1-\exp\Bigl(-\int_0^t\lambda(u),du\Bigr),. $$

当违约强度$\lambda$恒定时，此式简化为$O(t)=1-e^{-\lambda t}$ 。例如若$\lambda=1\%/年$，则1年累计违约概率为$1-e^{-0.01}=0.00995\approx0.995\%$ 。违约强度$\lambda(t)$是条件违约率，表示在$t$时刻公司违约的瞬时概率（假定此前未违约）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When default intensity $\lambda$ is constant, the expression becomes $O(t)=1-e^{-\lambda t}$. If $\lambda=1\%$ per year, one-year cumulative default probability is $1-e^{-0.01}=0.00995\approx0.995\%$. The intensity $\lambda(t)$ is a conditional instantaneous default rate: it applies at time $t$ given survival up to that point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**示例题：** 假设某公司每年的违约强度恒定为$\lambda=2\%$。求该公司在5年内累计违约概率，以及第3年出现违约的条件概率（假设前两年无违约）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Example Question:** Assume that a company has a constant default intensity of $\lambda=2\%$ annually. Calculate the cumulative default probability over 5 years, as well as the conditional probability of defaulting in the third year (assuming no defaults in the first two years).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** 5年累计违约概率$=1-e^{-0.02\times5}=1-e^{-0.1}\approx0.0952;(9.52\%)$。第3年在此前未违约的条件下违约的概率为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** The five-year cumulative default probability is $1-e^{-0.02\times5}=1-e^{-0.1}\approx0.0952$ (9.52%). Conditional on survival through the first two years, the probability of default during year three is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac{O(3)-O(2)}{1-O(2)}=\frac{[1-e^{-0.02\times3}]-[1-e^{-0.02\times2}]}{e^{-0.02\times2}}=\frac{e^{-0.04}-e^{-0.06}}{e^{-0.04}}=1-e^{-0.02}\approx 1.98\%.$$

这里使用了无条件违约概率$O(t)=1-e^{-0.02t}$和条件概率公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unconditional default probability $O(t)=1-e^{-0.02t}$ and conditional probability formulas were used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3**回收率**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3 Recovery Rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回收率 $R=1-[[违约损失口径|LGD]]$（损失率）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$=1-LGD$, where LGD is the loss rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当公司违约时，债权人在破产清算或重组中能追回的债券价值占债券面值的比例称为**回收率**（$R$）。通常按违约后（如一个月后）债券交易价格与面值之比来定义 。例如某债券面值100元，违约后一个月交易价为30元，则回收率为30%。损失率（LGD）则为$1-R$。历史数据显示，不同债务优先级的回收率差别较大，第一留置权债券平均回收率较高 。此外，回收率通常与经济状况关联：违约率高的年份回收率倾向较低 ，这意味着信用风险加剧时损失率会上升。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a company defaults, the proportion of the bond's face value that a creditor can recover in bankruptcy liquidation or restructuring is known as the recovery rate ($R$). Typically, it is defined by the ratio of the bond's price after default (e.g., one month after) to its face value. For instance, if a bond has a face value of 100 yuan and trades at 30 yuan one month after default, the recovery rate would be 30%. The loss rate (LGD), on the other hand, is $1-R$. Historical data shows significant differences in recovery rates across different levels of debt priority; first-priority collateral bonds generally have higher recovery rates. Additionally, recovery rates are often correlated with economic conditions: years with high default rates tend to see lower recovery rates, implying that credit risk intensifies when loss rates rise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**示例题：** 某公司发行的一张债券面值$1,000，违约后一个月市场价格为$350。计算该债券的回收率及违约损失率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Example:** A bond has face value USD 1,000 and trades at USD 350 one month after default. Calculate its recovery rate and loss given default.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** 回收率$R=350/1000=35\%$，[[违约损失口径|违约损失率]]$1-R=65\%$。这意味着债权人只收回面值的35%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** Recovery rate is $R=350/1000=35\%$, and [[违约损失口径|loss given default]] is $1-R=65\%$. The creditor recovers only 35% of face value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. **Credit Default Swap (CDS)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1 CDS内容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1 How a CDS Works*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信用违约互换（CDS）是一种场外信用衍生品，允许投资者买卖对特定参考实体（如公司或国家）信用风险的保护。CDS 的基本结构是：保护买方按合约名义本金（notional）支付约定保费，若参考实体发生该交易覆盖并满足认定条件的[[CDS信用事件与结算|信用事件]]，保护卖方再按结算条款给付。**名义本金是双方在合约中约定的保护金额，不是参考实体全部债券的总面值。**若未发生覆盖的信用事件，买方持续支付保费但没有保护腿给付。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A credit default swap (CDS) is an OTC credit derivative through which investors buy or sell protection on the credit risk of a specified reference entity, such as a company or sovereign. The protection buyer pays the agreed premium on the contractual notional. If the reference entity experiences a [[CDS信用事件与结算|credit event]] covered and confirmed under that transaction, the protection seller settles under the contract. **The CDS notional is the protection amount agreed by the parties; it is not the total face value of all bonds issued by the reference entity.** Without a covered credit event, the buyer continues paying premiums and receives no protection-leg payment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **保护买方义务：** 按约定利差定期（通常季度）支付保费；
- **保护卖方义务：** 若发生合同覆盖的信用事件，按约定的实物、拍卖或现金方式结算。实物交割时，买方交付合格可交割义务并收到相应的合同面值现金；这笔现金是**毛收款**，不是净利润。若交割债务当时价值为 $V_d$、交割面值为 $N$，忽略应计、折现、交易与融资成本时，净保护价值约为 $N-V_d$。现金或拍卖结算则直接按合同确定的结算价格计算差额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Protection buyer's obligation:** Pay the agreed premium periodically, usually quarterly.
- **Protection seller's obligation:** Settle after a covered credit event through the agreed physical, auction, or cash method. Under physical settlement, the buyer delivers an eligible obligation and receives the corresponding contractual par amount. That cash receipt is **gross**, not net profit: if the delivered obligation is worth $V_d$ and its delivered face amount is $N$, simplified net protection is about $N-V_d$ before accrual, discounting, transaction, and funding costs. Cash or auction settlement instead pays the contractually determined price difference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CDS 交易通常采用标准化条款，例如五年期 CDS。举例：某五年期 CDS 合约名义本金为 1 亿美元，年费率 90 bp，买方每季度支付 225,000 美元（$0.009/4\times100{,}000{,}000=225{,}000$）。若第三年发生覆盖的信用事件并采用实物交割，买方可交付 1 亿美元面值的合格义务并收到 1 亿美元**毛现金**；但净保护价值还要扣除这批交割义务的取得或持有价值。仅凭合约名义本金，不能把净保护收益写成 1 亿美元。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CDS contracts commonly use standardised terms, with five years a benchmark maturity. Suppose a five-year CDS has USD 100 million contractual notional and a 90 bp annual spread. The buyer pays $0.009/4\times\$100{,}000{,}000=\$225{,}000$ each quarter. If a covered credit event occurs in year three and settlement is physical, the buyer may deliver USD 100 million face value of eligible obligations and receive USD 100 million of **gross cash**. Net protection still subtracts the acquisition or holding value of those obligations; the notional alone does not establish a USD 100 million net gain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.2 CDX信用指数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2 CDX Credit Index*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**CDX 信用指数：** 为了分散风险，市场上出现了信用违约指数，如北美投资级CDX（CDX NA IG）和欧洲iTraxx投资级指数，每个指数包含125家大型企业的信用风险组合 。这些指数每年定期（3月、9月）调整标的成分 。例如，假设五年期CDX NA IG的标价为买165bp、卖166bp ，表示可以以166bp的年费率买入对所有125家公司各$1保护（每家公司$1名义额）。用户可自定义名义金额，例如要对每家公司买入$80万保护，总名义为125×$80万。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**CDX credit indices:** Credit indices diversify exposure across multiple reference entities. Examples include the North American investment-grade CDX (CDX. NA. IG) and the European investment-grade iTraxx index, each described here as containing 125 large companies. New index series are rolled in March and September. If a five-year CDX. NA. IG market is quoted 165/166 bp, protection can be bought at 166 bp on equal notionals across all 125 names. To buy USD 0.8 million of protection on each name, total index notional is $125\times\$0.8$ million = USD 100 million.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**示例题：** 某五年期 CDS 合约名义本金为 2,000 万美元，年费率 120 bp（季度支付）。假设信用事件前正常付费，保护买方每季度支付多少保费？若第三年发生覆盖的信用事件并采用实物交割，合格交割债务当时的市值为 800 万美元，请分别写出买方的毛现金收款和简化净保护价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Example Question:** A five-year CDS has USD 20 million contractual notional and a 120 bp annual premium paid quarterly. How much premium does the buyer pay each quarter before a credit event? If a covered credit event occurs in year three, physical settlement applies, and the eligible delivered obligations are then worth USD 8 million, state separately the buyer's gross cash receipt and simplified net protection value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** 年费率 120 bp 对应 0.012，因此每季度保费为 $20{,}000{,}000\times0.012/4=60{,}000$ 美元。实物交割时，买方交付 2,000 万美元面值的合格义务并收到 2,000 万美元毛现金；扣除市值 800 万美元的交割债务后，忽略应计、融资和交易成本的净保护价值为 $20{,}000{,}000-8{,}000{,}000=12{,}000{,}000$ 美元。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** A 120 bp spread is 0.012 per year, so the quarterly premium is $\$20{,}000{,}000\times0.012/4=\$60{,}000$. Under physical settlement, the buyer delivers USD 20 million face value of eligible obligations and receives USD 20 million gross cash. Subtracting the USD 8 million market value of the delivered obligations gives simplified net protection of USD 12 million before accrual, funding, and transaction costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.3 信用利差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3 Credit Spread*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **信用利差（Credit Spread）：** 债券收益率与无风险收益率（同期限国债）之差，反映承受信用风险要求的额外收益 。例如，10年期公司债收益率8%，同期国债5%，则信用利差为300bp。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Credit spread:** The difference between a bond's yield and the yield on a comparable risk-free government bond. It is compensation for credit and related risks. If a ten-year corporate bond yields 8% and a comparable government bond yields 5%, the spread is 300 bp.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.4 CDS利差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4 CDS Spread*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **CDS 利差（CDS Spread）：** 对可把持续费率直接设为初始价值为零的新合约，平价利差是使 [[CDS两腿定价|保护腿与保费腿]] 的风险中性现值相等的年化费率。若市场合约采用标准固定票息加 upfront，固定票息本身不是平价利差，比较前要换算为平价等价利差。只有在“2% 是持续平价费率”的口径下，才可简写为每年每 100 美元合约名义本金支付 2 美元保费，并仍受具体付费日和应计规则约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **CDS spread:** For a new contract whose running rate can be set to zero initial value, the par spread is the annualised rate that balances the risk-neutral present values of the [[CDS两腿定价|protection and premium legs]]. If a standard contract uses a fixed coupon plus upfront, that fixed coupon is not the par spread and must be converted to a par-equivalent spread before comparison. Only when 2% is the running par rate can it be summarised as USD 2 per year per USD 100 of contractual notional, still subject to the payment schedule and accrual rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.5 资产互换及基差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.5 Asset Swaps and Basis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **资产互换利差（Asset-Swap Spread, ASW）：** 把固定息票债券与利率互换组合为浮动参考利率加一段利差时，使交易在约定价格口径下成立的那段利差。ASW 由债券价格、息票、互换曲线与报价约定共同决定，不能一般化地写成“债券到期收益率减某个浮动利率”。例如：若一只面值 100、年息票 9% 的公司债报价 95，投资者用价格调整并通过互换支付固定息票、收取浮动参考利率加 $X$，使组合损益平衡的 $X$ 才是该债的 ASW。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Asset-swap spread (ASW):** The spread over a floating swap benchmark that makes the agreed package of a fixed-rate bond and an interest-rate swap balance at the quoted price. It depends on the bond price, coupon, swap curve, and market conventions; it is not generally a simple bond-yield-minus-floating-rate subtraction. For example, if a USD 100 face-value corporate bond with a 9% annual coupon trades at 95, the break-even $X$ in a package that pays away the fixed coupon and receives the floating benchmark plus $X$ is the bond's ASW.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **[[债券CDS基差|CDS–ASW 基差]]：** 本页统一定义为同一信用、同期限的 CDS 平价等价利差减 ASW：
    $$B_{\mathrm{ASW}}=s_{\mathrm{CDS}}-s_{\mathrm{ASW}}.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **[[债券CDS基差|CDS–ASW basis]]:** throughout this page, it means the par-equivalent CDS spread minus the same-credit, same-maturity ASW spread:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    只有在融资和做空无摩擦、两边流动性相同、期限与债务层级匹配、信用事件和结算给付可复制且没有对手方或保证金成本的理想环境中，基差才可能接近 0。现实中的融资、流动性、交割、条款、供需和中介资产负债表约束都能让它持续非零。若改用 Z-spread 或相对国债利差相减，必须另标为相应的 **proxy（代理度量）**，不能沿用这里的 CDS–ASW 定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The basis may approach zero only in an idealised setting with frictionless funding and shorting, equal liquidity, aligned maturity and seniority, matched credit-event and settlement payoffs, and no counterparty or margin costs. Funding, liquidity, deliverability, contract terms, supply and demand, and intermediary balance-sheet constraints can keep it away from zero. A subtraction using Z-spread or a government-bond spread must be labelled as that separate **proxy** rather than called the CDS–ASW basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**示例题：** 某公司五年期债券的 ASW 为 200 bp，同一参考实体、同期限 CDS 的平价等价利差为 350 bp。按本页口径，CDS–ASW 基差是多少？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Example:** A company's five-year bond has an ASW spread of 200 bp, while the par-equivalent CDS spread for the same reference entity and maturity is 350 bp. What is the CDS–ASW basis under this page's convention?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** $B_{\mathrm{ASW}}=350-200=+150\text{ bp}$。正值只表示 CDS 利差比同期限 ASW 宽 150 bp；它本身不证明存在可执行的无风险套利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** $B_{\mathrm{ASW}}=350-200=+150\text{ bp}$. The positive sign says only that the CDS spread is 150 bp wider than the same-maturity ASW; it does not by itself establish an executable risk-free arbitrage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.6 **由信用利差和 CDS 反推风险中性违约强度**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.6 **Inferring Risk-Neutral Default Intensity from Credit Spreads and CDS Quotes***
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场利差可在给定定价模型、回收率和贴现曲线后反推[[违约强度模型|风险中性违约强度]] $\lambda^{\mathbb Q}$；它不是历史频率或真实世界 PD。在连续保费、近似常数的 $\lambda^{\mathbb Q}$ 与条件期望合同回收率 $R$、统一贴现体系，并忽略双边违约、流动性和融资等非信用楔子时，简化模型给出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given a pricing model, recovery assumption, and discount curve, a market spread can imply a [[违约强度模型|risk-neutral default intensity]] $\lambda^{\mathbb Q}$; it is not a historical frequency or real-world PD. With continuous premiums, approximately constant $\lambda^{\mathbb Q}$ and conditional expected contractual recovery $R$, one consistent discount system, and no bilateral-default, liquidity, funding, or other non-credit wedge, the simplified model gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
s=\lambda^{\mathbb Q}(1-R).
$$

离散付息、应计以及强度或回收率随期限和状态变化时，它只是一阶近似：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With discrete premium dates, accrual, or intensity and recovery varying over time or state, it is only a first-order approximation:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\lambda^{\mathbb Q}\approx\frac{s}{1-R}.
$$

例如，若**强行把**某债券的 200 bp 市场利差全部当作同一简化模型中的信用补偿，并假设 $R=40\%$，得到的只是定价隐含代理：$\lambda^{\mathbb Q}_{\mathrm{proxy}}\approx0.02/(1-0.4)=0.0333$。常数强度下，五年累计风险中性违约概率代理为 $PD^{\mathbb Q}_{\mathrm{proxy}}(0,5)=1-e^{-0.0333\times5}\approx0.154$（15.4%）。现金债利差还可能包含流动性、融资、税收、嵌入期权和供需成分，所以该结果既不是由债券价格唯一识别的纯信用强度，也不是现实中的五年违约频率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if one **forces the entire** 200 bp market spread on a bond into this simplified credit-pricing model and assumes $R=40\%$, the result is only a pricing-implied proxy: $\lambda^{\mathbb Q}_{\mathrm{proxy}}\approx0.02/(1-0.4)=0.0333$. Under constant intensity, the five-year cumulative risk-neutral default-probability proxy is $PD^{\mathbb Q}_{\mathrm{proxy}}(0,5)=1-e^{-0.0333\times5}\approx0.154$, or 15.4%. A cash-bond spread may also contain liquidity, funding, tax, embedded-option, and supply-demand components, so this is neither a uniquely identified pure-credit intensity nor a real-world five-year default frequency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更完整的 CDS 校准会按 [[CDS两腿定价|保费腿与保护腿]] 的风险中性现值配平，并在给定回收假设与贴现曲线下，用多期限报价引导出 $\lambda^{\mathbb Q}(t)$。一个 CDS 利差不能同时分别识别强度与回收率。若 $\lambda^{\mathbb Q}$ 为常数，$T$ 年累计风险中性违约概率满足
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fuller CDS calibration balances the risk-neutral present values of the [[CDS两腿定价|premium and protection legs]] and, given recovery and discount assumptions, bootstraps $\lambda^{\mathbb Q}(t)$ from several maturities. A single CDS spread cannot separately identify intensity and recovery. With constant $\lambda^{\mathbb Q}$, the cumulative risk-neutral default probability over $T$ years satisfies:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
PD^{\mathbb Q}(0,T)=1-e^{-\lambda^{\mathbb Q}T},
\qquad
\lambda^{\mathbb Q}=-\frac{1}{T}\ln\!\left(1-PD^{\mathbb Q}(0,T)\right).
$$

这条曲线服务定价；用于信用风险预测或资本管理时，还要按 [[违约概率口径|风险中性与真实世界概率的口径边界]] 转换或另行估计。现金债与 CDS 之间的非信用楔子及口径差异另见 [[债券CDS基差]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This curve is a pricing input. Credit forecasting or capital management requires a real-world estimate or an explicit measure conversion; see [[违约概率口径|the boundary between risk-neutral and real-world probabilities]]. Non-credit wedges and measurement differences between cash bonds and CDS are covered in [[债券CDS基差]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**示例题：** 某公司五年期债券的市场利差为 150 bp，回收率假设为 50%。在明确把全部利差视为信用补偿的简化定价假设下，估计平均风险中性强度代理 $\lambda^{\mathbb Q}_{\mathrm{proxy}}$，并计算五年累计风险中性违约概率代理。该结果能否称为真实世界 PD？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Example:** A company's five-year bond has a 150 bp market spread and assumed recovery of 50%. Under the explicit simplifying assumption that the entire spread is credit compensation, estimate the average risk-neutral intensity proxy $\lambda^{\mathbb Q}_{\mathrm{proxy}}$ and the five-year cumulative risk-neutral default-probability proxy. Can the result be called real-world PD?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** $\lambda^{\mathbb Q}_{\mathrm{proxy}}\approx s/(1-R)=0.015/(1-0.50)=0.03$（3%/年），所以 $PD^{\mathbb Q}_{\mathrm{proxy}}(0,5)=1-e^{-0.03\times5}\approx0.1393$，即约 13.93%。不能称为真实世界 PD；它依赖风险中性定价假设，而且现金债利差中的非信用成分会污染该代理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** $\lambda^{\mathbb Q}_{\mathrm{proxy}}\approx s/(1-R)=0.015/(1-0.50)=0.03$, or 3% per year. Hence $PD^{\mathbb Q}_{\mathrm{proxy}}(0,5)=1-e^{-0.03\times5}\approx0.1393$, or about 13.93%. It is not real-world PD: it depends on risk-neutral pricing assumptions and is contaminated by any non-credit component of the cash-bond spread.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.7**利用股价估计违约概率（Merton和KMV模型）**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.7 **Using Stock Prices to Estimate Default Probabilities (Merton and KMV Models)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**[[Merton违约模型|Merton模型]]：** 利用公司资产价值$V_t$作为被试价值，假设负债为固定面额$D$的零息债券，到期时偿还本金。Merton模型将公司股权$E$视为对公司资产的看涨期权：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**[[Merton违约模型|Merton model]]:** Let firm asset value be $V_t$ and represent debt as a zero-coupon bond with fixed face value $D$ due at maturity. The model treats equity $E$ as a call option on firm assets:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$E_0 = V_0 N(d_1) - D e^{-rT} N(d_2),$$

其中$$d_{1}=\frac{\ln(V_0/D)+(r+\tfrac12\sigma_V^2)T}{\sigma_V\sqrt{T}},\quad d_{2}=d_1-\sigma_V\sqrt{T},$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
thereof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$r$为无风险利率，$\sigma_V$为资产价值波动率。违约发生当资产价值低于负债面值，即$V_T<D$，该概率为风险中性违约概率：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $r$ is the risk-free rate and $\sigma_V$ is asset-value volatility. Default at maturity occurs when asset value is below debt face value, $V_T<D$. Under the model, the following expression gives the risk-neutral default probability:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_{\text{违约}} = \Phi(-d_2),, $$

其中$\Phi(\cdot)$为标准正态累积分布 。由于$V_0$和$\sigma_V$不可直接观测，可通过观测股权市值$E_0$和股价波动率$\sigma_E$，联立以上方程解出$V_0$和$\sigma_V$。然后可计算距离违约（Distance to Default, DD）：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Where $\Phi(\cdot)$ denotes the standard normal cumulative distribution function. Since $V_0$ and $\sigma_V$ cannot be directly observed, they can be estimated using observable stock market value $E_0$ and stock price volatility$\sigma_E$. By solving the above equations simultaneously, we can determine $V_0$ and $\sigma_V$. Then, the distance to default (Distance to Default, DD) can be calculated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$DD = d_2 = \frac{\ln(V_0/D)+(r-\tfrac12\sigma_V^2)T}{\sigma_V\sqrt{T}},$$

表示资产价值距违约点的距离（以标准差计） 。距离越小，公司违约可能性越高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The distance between the asset value and the default point, measured in standard deviations. The smaller this distance, the higher the likelihood of the company defaulting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->


**KMV模型：** Moody’s KMV模型是对Merton模型的扩展，利用公开市场的股价和负债结构来估计公司资产价值及其波动率，并定义**违约点**（通常为短期债务加长期债务的一定比例）。根据计算出的DD，KMV通过历史违约经验将其转换为**预期违约频率（EDF）**，即实证意义上的违约概率 。简单来说，KMV模型也视股权为资产的看涨期权来反推资产价值，并结合市场数据校准违约概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**KMV model:** Moody's KMV extends the Merton framework. It uses observed equity prices and the liability structure to infer firm asset value and volatility, and defines a **default point**, commonly short-term debt plus a fraction of long-term debt. Historical default experience maps distance to default (DD) into an **expected default frequency (EDF)**, an empirical default probability. Like Merton, KMV treats equity as a call option on assets but calibrates the probability with market and historical data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**示例题：** 某公司总资产市场价值$V_0=100$亿，资产波动率$\sigma_V=30\%$，有一笔2年后到期的零息债务，面值$D=80$亿。[[无风险利率口径|无风险利率]]$r=3\%$，试估计该公司2年后的违约概率（按Merton模型计算）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Example:** A company has market asset value $V_0=10$ billion, asset volatility $\sigma_V=30\%$, and zero-coupon debt with face value $D=8$ billion due in two years. The [[无风险利率口径|risk-free rate]] is $r=3\%$. Estimate its two-year default probability under the Merton model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** 首先计算$d_2$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** Start by calculating $d_2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$d_2=\frac{\ln(100/80)+(0.03-\tfrac12(0.3)^2)\times2}{0.3\sqrt{2}}

=\frac{\ln(1.25)+(0.03-0.045)\times2}{0.4243}\approx\frac{0.2231-0.03}{0.4243}\approx0.4553.$$

基础 Merton 模型下的风险中性违约概率 $= \Phi(-d_2)=\Phi(-0.4553)\approx0.3245$，即约32.45
\%。（公式与含义参考 ）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[Merton违约模型|Risk-neutral default probability in the base Merton model]] $=\Phi(-d_2)=\Phi(-0.4553)\approx0.3245$, or approximately $32.45\%$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

## **风险中性违约概率与真实世界违约概率**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Risk-Neutral Default Probability and Real World Default Probability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

违约概率有风险中性和真实世界两种概念。**真实世界违约概率**（或物理违约概率）来源于历史统计和评级机构数据，它反映借款人在实际环境下的违约频率；而**风险中性违约概率**来源于市场价格（如债券利差、CDS利差），反映在风险中性概率度量下的违约概率 。一般而言，风险中性违约概率高于真实世界违约概率 ，因为市场利差除了补偿违约预期损失外，还包含风险溢价和流动性溢价等。在实际运用中，风险中性违约概率用于定价和估值分析（如信用衍生品定价），而真实世界违约概率用于风险管理和情景分析 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Default probabilities have concepts of risk-neutral and real-world. **Real-world default probability** (or physical default probability) originates from historical statistics and rating agency data, reflecting the actual frequency of a borrower's default; whereas **risk-neutral default probability** originates from market prices (such as bond spreads, CDS spreads), reflecting the default probability under risk-neutral probability measures. Generally, risk-neutral default probability is higher than real-world default probability, because market spreads not only compensate for expected loss due to default but also include factors like risk premium and liquidity premiums. In practical applications, risk-neutral default probability is used for pricing and valuation analyses (such as credit derivatives pricing), while real-world default probability is used for risk management and scenario analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**示例题：** 某公司5年期CDS隐含违约概率为10%，同期历史数据估计的真实违约概率为6%。这两个概率的区别是什么？在估值和风险管理时应如何使用？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Example Question:** The implied default probability for a 5-year CDS by a certain company is 10%, while the estimated true default probability from historical data is 6%. What is the difference between these two probabilities? How should they be used in valuation and risk management?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**答案：** CDS隐含的10%违约概率是风险中性概率，已反映风险溢价，适用于信用衍生品的定价；6%是真实违约概率，用于衡量实际违约风险和进行风险情景分析。风险中性概率通常高于真实概率 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** The 10% default probability embedded in the CDS is a risk-neutral probability, reflecting risk premium, suitable for pricing credit derivatives; 6% is the actual default probability, used to measure the actual default risk and manage credit risk. Risk-neutral probabilities are typically higher than actual probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **一、信用风险 vs. 信贷风险 vs. 交易对手风险 (19 · 1 · 1)**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*I. Credit Risk vs. Lending Risk vs. Counterparty Risk (19 · 1 · 1)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|**概念**|**PPT 关键词**|**场景举例**|**如何“界定 & 监管”**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**信用风险 (Credit Risk)**|总括性“大帽子”|借款人违约、债券不兑付、票据跳票|Basel 信用 RWA 进入 [[风险加权资本率|风险加权资本率]]；8% 只是总资本国际基线|
|**信贷风险 (Lending Risk)**|“贷款本金 + 利息收不回”|公司贷款、抵押贷|风险权重 + [[违约概率口径|PD]]/[[违约损失口径|LGD]] + 担保 & 补救期 (cure period)|
|**交易对手风险 (Counterparty Risk)**|“金融合约在到期前对手违约导致的**正价值**损失”|衍生品、回购、证券借贷|当前 Basel 标准法用 [[SA-CCR|SA-CCR]] 计 EAD；CEM 只用于历史题，本地生效日另查；保证金另作风险缓释|
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| **Concept** | **Slide cue** | **Example** | **Boundary and regulation** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Credit risk** | The broad umbrella | Borrower default, missed bond payment, dishonoured bill | Basel credit RWA enters the risk-weighted capital ratio; 8% is only the international total-capital baseline |
| **Lending risk** | Loan principal and interest are not recovered | Corporate loans and mortgages | Risk weights, PD, LGD, collateral, and a cure period |
| **Counterparty risk** | Loss of a financial contract's **positive value** because the counterparty defaults before maturity | Derivatives, repos, and securities lending | The current Basel standardised EAD method is SA-CCR; CEM is retained only for historical exercises and local effective dates must still be checked; margin is separate risk mitigation |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **直观点**
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Briefly Put**
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- > 公司贷款 100 m → **信贷风险**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- > Company loan 100 m → **Credit risk**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- > 与公司做掉期，市值我浮盈 5 m → **交易对手风险**（就怕它提前破产）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- > To do a swap with the company, my market value would show a 5 million floating profit. → **Counterparty Risk** (fear it might go bankrupt prematurely)
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- > 二者合起来，都属于更大的 **信用风险** 范畴
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- > Both are subsets of the broader category of **[[信用风险参数与模型.canvas|credit risk]]**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **二、CDS 合同：多情景下买卖双方现金流与价值 (19 · 2 “CDS 合约收益比较”)**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*II. CDS Contracts: Buyer and Seller Cash Flows and Values across Scenarios (19 · 2 "Comparing CDS Contract Returns")*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|**场景**|**买保护方**|**卖保护方**|
|---|---|---|
|**参考实体未发生覆盖信用事件**|按合约名义本金支付保费|收取保费，但仍承担未来保护给付|
|**同一参考信用的 CDS 市场利差扩大**|其他条件相同时，既有保护头寸市值通常上升；可以持有或按当时可执行价格平仓|既有卖保护头寸市值通常下降，且可能产生变动保证金需求|
|**参考实体发生合同覆盖的信用事件**|按[[CDS信用事件与结算\|结算条款]]取得保护给付；金额不必等于自身实际损失|按合同支付保护腿；先前保费可能不足以覆盖给付|
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
|**Scenario**|**Buy Protection**|**Sell Protection**|
|---|---|---|
|**No covered credit event affecting the reference entity**|Pay premiums on the contractual notional|Collect premiums while retaining the contingent protection obligation|
|**Market CDS spread on the same reference credit widens**|All else equal, existing purchased protection usually gains market value; it may be held or unwound at the then-executable price|Existing sold protection usually loses market value and may create variation-margin needs|
|**The reference entity experiences a covered credit event**|Receive the protection payment under the [[CDS信用事件与结算\|settlement terms]]; it need not equal the buyer's actual loss|Pay the protection leg; earlier premiums may be insufficient to cover it|
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **持有还是平仓是一项估值决策。** 利差扩大通常提高既有保护的市值；退出判断还要综合之后的利差路径、剩余保费、流动性、bid–ask、融资、保证金和投资目标，并按当时可执行价格计算。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Holding versus unwinding is a valuation decision.** A wider spread usually raises the value of existing protection; the exit decision also uses the subsequent spread path, remaining premiums, liquidity, bid–ask, funding, margin, the investor's objective, and the executable price at that time.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **三、CDS–ASW 基差方向与抽样表 (19 · 2 同页)**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*III. CDS–ASW Basis Direction and Sample Table (19 · 2 on the Same Page)*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|**指标**|**定义**|**可比口径**|**符号与交易边界**|
|---|---|---|---|
|**CDS spread**|保护腿与保费腿平衡后的平价等价利差；固定票息加 upfront 要先换算|理想化匹配下可与同期限 ASW 比较||
|**ASW spread**|由债券价格、息票与互换曲线隐含的资产互换利差|||
|**[[债券CDS基差\|Basis]] = CDS − ASW**|若 **>0**，CDS 更宽；若 **<0**，ASW 更宽|正基差的理想化方向：做空现金债 + 卖 CDS 保护；负基差：买债 + 买保护|危机期符号不固定：现金债融资/流动性冲击可推向负值，保护需求或 CDS 约束也可推向正值；两种方向都不是无风险套利|
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| **Measure** | **Definition** | **Comparable convention** | **Sign and trading boundary** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CDS spread** | Par-equivalent spread balancing the protection and premium legs; convert fixed coupon plus upfront before comparison | Comparable with same-maturity ASW only under aligned, idealised conditions |  |
| **ASW spread** | Asset-swap spread implied by the bond price, coupon, and swap curve |  |  |
| **[[债券CDS基差\|Basis]] = CDS − ASW** | If positive, CDS is wider; if negative, ASW is wider | Idealised positive-basis direction: short the cash bond and sell CDS protection; negative-basis direction: buy the bond and buy protection | Crisis has no fixed sign: cash-bond funding/liquidity stress can push it negative, while protection demand or CDS constraints can push it positive; neither direction is risk-free arbitrage |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **抽样表用法**：找“日期列 + 五年期 CDS + ASW”，计算差值即 basis，观察它随市场情绪跳动。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Sampling Table Usage**: Look for "Date Column + 5-Year CDS + ASW", calculate the difference to obtain the basis, and observe its fluctuation in response to market sentiment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 交易方向不等于完整策略
> 在本页 $B_{\mathrm{ASW}}=s_{\mathrm{CDS}}-s_{\mathrm{ASW}}$ 的口径下，正基差的理想化镜像是**做空现金债并卖出 CDS 保护**；负基差的经典方向是**买入债券并买入同名 CDS 保护**，通常还要融资债券并处理利率风险。逐腿现金流和融资边界见 [[负基差交易]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under this page's $B_{\mathrm{ASW}}=s_{\mathrm{CDS}}-s_{\mathrm{ASW}}$ convention, the idealised positive-basis direction is to **short the cash bond and sell CDS protection**; the classic negative-basis direction is to **buy the bond and buy protection on the same name**, normally with bond funding and interest-rate risk management. See [[负基差交易]] for leg-by-leg cash flows and funding boundaries.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **四、信用等级下降 & 抵押品不足导致损失 (19 · 3 “担保品与补救期”)**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*IV. Losses Resulting from Downgraded Credit Ratings and Insufficient Collateral (19 · 3 "Collateral and Grace Periods")*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**链条 4 步**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A Four-Step Chain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **评级被下调** → 触发合约条款：需“追加抵押品 (Margin Call)”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Rating is downgraded** → Trigger contract terms: Margin call required.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
2. **对手没钱补** → 出现 **抵押品短缺 (Collateral shortfall)**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **The counterparty cannot meet the call** → a **collateral shortfall** emerges.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
3. 我方暴露增加，若其随后违约 → **损失 = 未补抵押 × (1–回收)**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** Our exposure increases, and if they subsequently default, **Loss = Unsecured Collateral × (1 – Recovery)**.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
4. **补救期 (Cure period)**：通常 1–3 天，若对方仍补不上可宣布违约并动用原抵押品。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**4.** **Cure Period**: Typically ranging from 1 to 3 days. If the counterparty fails to cure within this period, a breach may be declared, and the original collateral may be invoked.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

|**关键词**|**PPT 注释**|**管控手段**|
|---|---|---|
|**Trigger downgrade clause**|评级触发条款|预留流动性或设置 CSAs 缓级梯度|
|**Threshold**|允许无抵押额度|与评级挂钩；降级 → Threshold 下降|
|**Independent Amount**|额外 IM 存款|防快速恶化|
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
|**Keywords**|**PPT Annotations**|**Control Measures**|
|---|---|---|
|**Trigger downgrade clause**|Rating trigger clause|Reserve liquidity or set CSAs with tiered gradients|
|**Threshold**|Allowance for unsecured amount|Linked to ratings; downgrade → Threshold decreases|
|**Independent Amount**|Additional IM deposits|Prevent rapid deterioration|
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **要点**：即便没“真违约”，单纯评级下调也会带来 **流动性压力**；危机中 AIG 就因巨额 margin call 差点断裂。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Key Points:** Even without “actual default,” a downgrade in ratings can still cause **liquidity pressure**; during the crisis, AIG nearly collapsed due to massive margin calls.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### **🌟 30 秒复盘**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*🌟 30 Second Reflection*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **信用风险三小弟**：贷款 (Lending) + 对手 (Counterparty) ⊂ 全部信用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** **Three layers of credit risk:** lending risk and counterparty risk are both subsets of the broader credit-risk category.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
2. **CDS 谁付谁收？** 买方付保费；若**参考实体**发生合同覆盖的信用事件，卖方按条款给付保护。保护卖方自身违约属于交易对手风险，不是参考实体信用事件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**2.** **Who pays and who receives in a CDS?** The buyer pays premiums; if the **reference entity** experiences a covered credit event, the seller pays protection under the contract. Default by the protection seller is counterparty risk, not a credit event of the reference entity.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
3. **Basis 先锁口径再看方向**：按 CDS − ASW，正值表示 CDS 更宽，负值表示 ASW 更宽；危机期没有固定符号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**3.** **Fix the convention before reading the [[债券CDS基差|basis]]:** under CDS minus ASW, positive means CDS is wider and negative means ASW is wider; crisis periods have no fixed sign.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
4. **降级 ≠ 违约，但会先逼出现金缺口**；补救期不到位就会转实亏。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**4.** **Downgrading does not equate to default, but it will force a cash shortfall first**; if remediation is inadequate, it will turn into an actual loss.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
