## 1.1 利率的种类、利率决定理论；利率期限结构与收益率曲线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.1 Types of interest rates, theories of rate determination, the term structure, and the yield curve*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**利率的种类：** 市场上存在许多不同利率，它们由于期限、币种、抵押方式、信用、流动性、税收和市场用途不同而数值各异，且变动并非完全同步。常见利率包括政府公债收益率、隔夜指数互换（OIS）利率、银行同业利率、商业贷款利率、存款利率和优惠利率。不存在跨币种、跨产品和跨用途都无条件正确的单一“无风险基准”：政府债收益率可含流动性、供需和税收效应，OIS 亦取决于币种、合约与抵押品口径。定价、折现、资金转移定价或风险对冲应选择与目的匹配的曲线；头寸与基准的调整不同步会形成 [[IRRBB基差|基差风险]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Types of interest rates:** Market rates differ by maturity, currency, collateralisation, credit, liquidity, tax treatment, and intended use, and they do not move in perfect synchrony. Examples include government-bond yields, overnight-index-swap (OIS) rates, interbank rates, commercial lending rates, deposit rates, and prime rates. There is no single benchmark that is unconditionally risk-free across currencies, products, and purposes. Government yields can contain liquidity, supply-demand, and tax effects, while an OIS rate also depends on its currency, contract, and collateral convention. Pricing, discounting, funds-transfer pricing, and hedging should use a curve suited to the purpose; imperfect adjustment between a position and its benchmark creates [[IRRBB基差|basis risk]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**利率的决定理论：** 关于利率由何决定，经济学中有多种理论学说：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Theory of interest rate determination:** There are many theories in economics about how interest rates are determined:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

此外，还有其他利率决定理论，例如**通货膨胀预期理论**（费雪效应认为名义利率 = 实际利率 + 通胀预期），以及**市场分割理论**等。但总体而言，利率的高低取决于资金供需、央行政策以及投资者预期等多种因素 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Other explanations include **expected-inflation theories**—the Fisher effect states that the nominal rate equals the real rate plus expected inflation—and **market-segmentation theory**. More generally, interest rates reflect the supply of and demand for funds, central-bank policy, and investor expectations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**利率期限结构与收益率曲线：** 同一时点上，在币种、信用、流动性、税收和合同条款可比时，不同到期期限的利率关系称作 [[利率期限结构|利率期限结构或收益率曲线]]。横轴为期限，纵轴还必须注明是零息即期率、远期率、平价率还是具体工具的到期收益率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**The term structure and the yield curve:** At one date, the [[利率期限结构|term structure]] compares rates across maturities while currency, credit, liquidity, tax, and contractual terms remain comparable. Maturity is on the horizontal axis, while the vertical axis must still be identified as a zero-coupon spot, forward, par, or individual-instrument YTM rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收益率曲线有多种典型形状：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A yield curve can take several characteristic shapes:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **正常的正斜率曲线：** 长期利率高于短期利率，是最常见形态，通常反映经济处于正常或扩张状态 。投资者要求对长期投资提供期限溢价，因此期限越长收益率越高，以补偿未来的不确定性和通胀风险。
- **平坦曲线：** 各期限利率差别很小，曲线接近水平 。这通常出现于经济转折时期，市场对未来利率走势不确定或预期短期和长期利率将趋于相近。
- **[[曲线倒挂|倒挂曲线]]：** 指定曲线和期限后，短期利率高于长期利率。它可能反映未来短率下降预期，也可能来自期限溢价和供需变化；历史上的预测相关性不是衰退必然发生或由倒挂造成的证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Normal upward-sloping curve:** long-term rates exceed short-term rates. This common shape is often associated with normal or expanding economic conditions. Investors demand a term premium for committing funds for longer periods and bearing greater uncertainty and inflation risk.
- **Flat curve:** yields differ little across maturities. This often appears near an economic turning point, when the market is uncertain about future rates or expects short- and long-term rates to converge.
- **[[曲线倒挂|Inverted curve]]:** for a specified curve and pair of maturities, short rates exceed long rates. It may reflect expected declines in future short rates or changes in term premia and supply-demand conditions. Historical predictive association does not prove that recession must follow or that inversion causes it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

除了上述三种基本形态外，现实中还可能出现陡峭或驼峰等 [[曲线形变|水平、斜率与曲率变化]]。解释框架包括 [[预期假说|纯粹预期理论]]、[[流动性溢价理论]]、[[市场分割|市场分割理论]] 与 [[期限偏好|期限偏好理论]]。远期率只有在零期限溢价等额外条件下才等于未来短率预期；现实曲线通常混合预期、风险补偿与期限特定供需。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Other shapes include steep and humped [[曲线形变|level, slope, and curvature changes]]. Explanations include the [[预期假说|pure expectations hypothesis]], [[流动性溢价理论|liquidity-premium theory]], [[市场分割|market segmentation]], and [[期限偏好|preferred habitat]]. A forward rate equals an expected future short rate only under additional restrictions such as a zero term premium; observed curves generally combine expectations, risk compensation, and maturity-specific demand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==基准利率必须与币种、抵押、流动性、信用、税收和用途一起说明；不能无条件声称国债低于“无风险利率”或 OIS 始终是最接近无风险的唯一基准。==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==A benchmark must be identified together with its currency, collateral, liquidity, credit, tax, and intended-use conventions. It is not valid to claim unconditionally that Treasury yields lie below "the" risk-free rate or that OIS is always the single closest risk-free benchmark.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.2 利率风险的基本概念及分类
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.2 Basic concepts and categories of interest-rate risk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**利率风险的定义：** 利率风险是指因市场利率变动的不确定性而导致金融机构（如商业银行）收益或价值发生不利变化的可能性 。简单来说，当市场利率上升或下降时，银行的实际利息收益与支出可能偏离原先预期，从而使银行遭受损失。这种风险尤其体现在持有固定利率工具时：如果市场利率上升，固定利率资产的价格会下跌，使持有者蒙受损失 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Definition of interest-rate risk:** Interest-rate risk is the possibility that uncertainty about market-rate movements will adversely affect a financial institution's earnings or economic value. When rates rise or fall, actual interest income and expense may depart from their expected amounts. The exposure is especially visible in fixed-rate instruments: when market yields rise, the value of an existing fixed-rate asset falls.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1.3. 净利息收入的构成，资产负债匹配策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1.3 Components of net interest income and asset–liability matching*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**净利息收入（Net Interest Income, NII）：** 指银行的利息收入减去利息支出后的净额，即贷款等生息资产收到的利息减去存款等付息负债支付的利息 。**净息差**（NIM，net interest margin）则是净利息收入与生息资产平均余额之比，衡量银行运用资金赚取利差收益的能力 。净息收入是商业银行最主要的收入来源之一，其稳定性关系到银行盈利的稳定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Net interest income (NII):** A bank's interest income minus its interest expense—for example, interest received on loans and other earning assets less interest paid on deposits and other interest-bearing liabilities. The **net interest margin (NIM)** is NII divided by average earning assets and measures how effectively a bank earns a spread on the funds it employs. NII is a major source of bank revenue, so its stability matters for the stability of bank profits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净利息收入取决于：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net interest income depends on:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 生息资产规模及收益率（如贷款利率、债券利息等），带来利息收入；
- 付息负债规模及成本率（如存款利率、同业拆借利率等），带来利息支出；
- 二者的差额乘以相应余额即为净利息收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The volume and yield of earning assets, such as loans and bonds, determine interest income.
- The volume and cost of interest-bearing liabilities, such as deposits and interbank borrowing, determine interest expense.
- Applying the relevant rate spread to the corresponding balances gives net interest income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**资产负债的期限匹配策略：** 银行为了管理利率风险，通常要协调资产和负债的利率敏感性，即“**匹配**”它们的重新定价期限和利率条款。这属于资产负债管理（Asset and Liability Management, ALM）部门的重要职责 。主要策略包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Asset–liability maturity matching:** To manage interest-rate risk, a bank coordinates the rate sensitivities of its assets and liabilities by matching their repricing dates, maturities, and rate terms. This is a central responsibility of asset and liability management (ALM). The main approaches include:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **存短贷短 / 存长贷长：** 尽量使负债的期限结构与资产相匹配。例如，用短期资金支持短期贷款，用长期负债支持长期贷款。这样，当市场利率变化时，资产端和负债端会同步反应，净利息差不致大幅波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Fund short-term loans with short-term liabilities and long-term loans with long-term liabilities:** Aligning asset and liability maturities makes both sides reprice in a similar way when market rates change, limiting swings in the net interest margin.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **存短贷长（期限错配）及其风险：** 有时银行会出现“存短借长”的情况，即用短期存款支持长期贷款 。这可能因为短期负债成本低而长期贷款收益高，从而提高当前利差收益。然而，这种策略蕴含利率风险和流动性风险：若未来利率上升，需要续借的短期负债利率提高，但长期贷款利率固定在较低水平，银行利息支出增长快于收入，净息收入将收窄甚至倒挂。此外，依赖短期资金还有**流动性风险**，一旦短期资金市场紧张（如遇金融危机导致融资困难），银行可能无法滚动融资支持长期资产，引发流动性危机。例如2007年英国北岩银行因过度依赖短期批发融资支持长期按揭贷款，在市场动荡时陷入挤兑，就是警示案例 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Funding long-term loans with short-term deposits—maturity transformation and its risks:** Short-term funding can be cheap while long-term loans earn higher yields, which increases the current spread. The strategy nevertheless creates both interest-rate and liquidity risk. If rates rise, short-term liabilities must be rolled over at a higher cost while the return on fixed-rate long-term loans remains low, compressing NII. If short-term markets become stressed, the bank may also be unable to refinance its long-term assets. Northern Rock's 2007 run, after heavy reliance on short-term wholesale funding for long-term mortgages, is a warning example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **利率预测与定价策略：** 银行在制定存贷款利率时，也会考虑市场对未来利率走势的预期。例如，如果市场普遍预计未来短期利率与当前相当且保持平稳，而银行却提供“5年期存款利率=1年期存款利率，5年期贷款利率=1年期贷款利率”的报价 （即对客户长短期利率均相同），会发生什么？理性的储户大多不会被较长期限的存款吸引（因为5年存款利率并未比1年高），而贷款客户则倾向锁定长期低利率贷款（因为5年贷款利率不高于短期） 。结果银行吸收的多为短期存款、发放的却是长期贷款，形成严重的错配。这种定价策略显然不可持续。所以银行通常根据期限结构调整定价，例如长期存款适当提高利率吸引锁定，长期贷款利率亦通常高于短期以补偿风险 。这体现了利率期限结构理论（如流动性偏好理论）的应用：一般情况下，**长期利率应高于短期利率** 。银行根据市场预期调整资产负债的定价，有助于吸引合理期限的资金并鼓励客户选择有利于银行匹配的期限，从而降低利率风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Rate forecasting and pricing:** Suppose the market expects short rates to remain stable, but a bank offers the same rate on one- and five-year deposits and the same rate on one- and five-year loans. Depositors have little reason to lock funds away for five years, while borrowers prefer to lock in the low five-year loan rate. The bank therefore attracts short deposits and makes long loans, creating a severe mismatch. Sustainable pricing normally pays more for longer deposits and charges more for longer loans to compensate for risk. Pricing assets and liabilities in line with the term structure encourages customers to choose maturities that support the bank's desired match.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **动态调整和对冲：** 当短期负债过多支持长期贷款时，银行可提高长期贷款定价，或在需要增加稳定长期融资时**提高长期存款利率**、改善期限条款来吸引客户锁定资金。降低长期存款利率会减弱而非增强客户锁定长期的动机。但提价也会抬高资金成本并压缩 NII，因此必须在稳定融资收益与定价成本之间权衡。银行也可用互换将固定利率资产转为浮动暴露。这些管理行动是动态模型的输入，不应在静态缺口算例中默认已发生。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Dynamic adjustment and hedging:** If too much short-term funding supports long-term loans, a bank may raise long-term loan pricing or, when it needs more stable term funding, **raise the offered rate on long-term deposits** or improve their terms. Cutting that rate weakens rather than strengthens the customer's incentive to lock funds away. A higher offered rate, however, raises funding cost and can compress NII, so management must trade off funding stability against pricing cost. A swap can also convert part of a fixed-rate asset book into floating exposure. These management actions belong in a dynamic model and must not be assumed silently in a static gap example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.1久期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.1 Duration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**久期的概念：** **久期**（[[麦考利久期|Duration]]）又称**持续期**，久期原用于衡量固定收益债券的平均实际回收期，后来扩展为衡量债券对利率变动敏感性的重要指标 。简单来说，久期是债券或其他固定收益资产的所有未来现金流的加权平均到期时间，权重为每期现金流的现值占总现值的比重 。因此，久期体现了一种平均**资金回收期限**：久期越长，说明现金流越晚才能收回，债券价格对利率变化就越敏感。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Concept of duration:** **Duration**, also called *duration*, began as a measure of the average time required to recover a fixed-income investment and later became a central measure of price sensitivity to interest rates. It is the weighted-average time to the asset's future cash flows, where each weight is that cash flow's present value as a share of total present value. A longer duration therefore means that value is recovered later and that price is more sensitive to a change in yield.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- duration-source-note:start DR01 -->
> [!note] 校注：平均现金流时点与价格敏感度分开
> [[麦考利久期]] · [[到期日与久期]]
>
> 本段的现值加权平均时点特指麦考利久期，不是收回原始投资所需的实际回收期，也不是所有“久期”指标的共同定义。非负、固定现金流且总现值为正时，权重才可解释为平均时点的非负权重。比较价格敏感度应使用同一收益率坐标下的修正久期；只有收益率与复利口径等条件一致时，才能直接从麦考利久期大小推断其排序。[OCC 手册印刷 76 页 / PDF 79 页](https://www.occ.treas.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/files/interest-rate-risk/pub-ch-interest-rate-risk.pdf#page=79)分别定义两种久期及固定现金流边界。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->

Nonnegative fixed cash flows and positive total present value make the weights a genuine weighted average. Price sensitivity should be compared using modified duration under one yield coordinate; Macaulay rankings alone require matching yield and compounding conventions. OCC printed page 76 / PDF page 79 distinguishes the measures and their fixed-cash-flow boundary.

## 2.2 [[麦考利久期|马考利久期]]计算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.2 Calculating [[麦考利久期|Macaulay duration]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start NAV -->
[[02_Economy/05_财务管理/06_债券定价与收益率|连续阅读：债券定价与收益率]] · [[债券定价、收益率与信用利差.canvas|关系图]]
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

马考利久期（[[麦考利久期|Macaulay Duration]]）定义为**现金流现值加权的平均到期时间**：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Macaulay duration is the **present-value-weighted average time to the cash flows**:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

D_M = \frac{ \sum_{t=1}^{n} t \cdot \frac{CF_t}{(1+y)^t} }{ \sum_{t=1}^{n} \frac{CF_t}{(1+y)^t} }

$$

- $CF_t$：第$t$期的现金流（付息/还本）
- $y$：[[到期收益率|到期收益率]]（即市场利率）
- $n$：期数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $CF_t$: cash flow at time $t$, including coupon and principal payments
- $y$: [[到期收益率|yield to maturity]], or market yield
- $n$: number of periods
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bond-source-note:start D01 -->
> [!note] 校注：本式的每期收益率与久期单位
> 上式的 $t$ 按付息期编号时，$(1+y)^t$ 中的 $y$ 必须是**每期有效收益率**，算出的 $D_M$ 单位也为期；它是这只债券价格隐含的单一 [[到期收益率]]，不是所有期限共同的“市场利率”。若一年有 $m$ 期，接入下节的名义年收益率 $y_{\mathrm{ann}}$ 时，应令每期率为 $y_{\mathrm{ann}}/m$，并将现值加权时点写成 $t/m$，得到以年计的 $D_M$；若保留本式按期计算的结果，则先除以 $m$。之后才可用以年计的 $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=D_M/(1+y_{\mathrm{ann}}/m)$。转换原则见 [[债券付息周期换算]]，期间权重见 [[麦考利久期|久期定义]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $t$ counts coupon periods, $y$ in $(1+y)^t$ must be an **effective period yield**, and the resulting $D_M$ is measured in periods. It is the single [[到期收益率|YTM]] implied by this bond's price, not one market rate shared by every maturity. With $m$ periods per year, use $y_{\mathrm{ann}}/m$ for the next section's nominal annual yield and weight payment times by $t/m$ to obtain years. Alternatively, divide the period-based result by $m$ first. Only then use $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=D_M/(1+y_{\mathrm{ann}}/m)$ in years. See [[债券付息周期换算|coupon-period conversion]] and [[麦考利久期|the duration definition]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- bond-source-note:end -->

## 2.3[[修正久期|修正久期]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.3 [[修正久期|Modified duration]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

修正久期公式为：
$$
\text{修正久期} = \frac{\text{马考利久期}}{1 + \frac{y}{m}}
$$
- $y$：年到期收益率（按债券报价惯例表示的 nominal annual [[到期收益率|yield to maturity]]）
- $m$：每年复利/付息次数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The modified-duration formula is:
- $y$: annual yield to maturity, stated as a nominal annual rate
- $m$: number of coupon or compounding periods per year
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

==当采用连续复利收益率定价时（或特殊地，收益率 $y=0$ 时），马考利久期与修正久期相等。==
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
==With continuously compounded yields—or, as a special case, when $y=0$—Macaulay duration and modified duration are equal.==
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- duration-source-note:start DR02 -->
> [!note] 校注：导数必须对应明确的利率变量
> [[修正久期]] · [[收益率久期与曲线久期]]
>
> 上式以年为时间单位，$y$ 是每年复利 $m$ 次的名义年收益率，固定现金流的修正久期为 $-P_y/P$。改用连续复利年率 $r$ 时，$P(r)=\sum_i CF_i e^{-rt_i}$ 的敏感度 $-P_r/P$ 等于其现值加权时点；这是换了导数坐标，并非同一个有限复利报价公式凭空消失了分母。单券 YTM 变化也不自动等于整条即期曲线同幅平移；曲线风险须另行指定各期限怎样变化。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With time in years and nominal annual yield $y$ compounded $m$ times, fixed-cash-flow modified duration is $-P_y/P$. Under a continuously compounded annual rate $r$, the sensitivity $-P_r/P$ of $P(r)=\sum_i CF_i e^{-rt_i}$ equals the present-value-weighted payment time. This changes the derivative coordinate; it does not remove a denominator from the same finite-compounding quotation. A bond's YTM change is not automatically a parallel spot-curve shift: curve risk requires an explicit maturity-by-maturity shock.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- duration-source-note:end -->

## 2.4 [[金额久期|绝对额久期]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.4 [[金额久期|dollar duration]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
  
>[!note] 定义
> **绝对额久期（[[金额久期|dollar duration / money duration]]）**是价值对收益率的一阶金额敏感度：$DD=-D_{mod}P$，单位是“货币金额／1.00 收益率变化”。**DV01/PVBP** 才是 1bp（$0.0001$）变化的金额：$DV01=|DD|\times0.0001$（符号另按“加息 1bp 的损益”约定）。
><!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **[[金额久期|Dollar duration or money duration]]** is the first-order monetary sensitivity to a 1.00 change in yield: $DD=-D_{mod}P$, with units of currency per 1.00 yield change. **DV01/PVBP** is the amount for one basis point, $0.0001$: $DV01=|DD|\times0.0001$; a signed convention may instead report P&L for a one-basis-point rise.
><!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**计算公式：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Calculation formula:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$

DD=-D_{mod}\times P,
\qquad
DV01=|DD|\times0.0001

$$

- $DD$ 是每 1.00 收益率变化的金额；$DV01$ 是每 1bp 的金额。报告时还必须写明币种、曲线、利率方向和符号约定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $DD$ is an amount per 1.00 yield change; $DV01$ is an amount per basis point. A report must also identify the currency, curve, rate direction, and sign convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**举例：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Example:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 某债券当前市值：$10,000,000$元
- 修正久期：$5$
- 问：如果市场利率上升$0.01$（即1%），债券价格下跌多少元？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Current market value of the bond: $10{,}000{,}000$ yuan
- Modified duration: $5$
- Question: if the market yield rises by $0.01$, or one percentage point, how many yuan does the bond lose?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**解：**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Solution:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- money duration $DD=-5\times10{,}000{,}000=-50{,}000{,}000$ 元／1.00；
- $DV01=50{,}000{,}000\times0.0001=5{,}000$ 元／bp；
- 收益率上升 1 个百分点（$0.01=100bp$）时，$\Delta P\approx-50{,}000{,}000\times0.01=-500{,}000$ 元。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Money duration is $DD=-5\times10{,}000{,}000=-50{,}000{,}000$ yuan per 1.00.
- $DV01=50{,}000{,}000\times0.0001=5{,}000$ yuan per basis point.
- A one-percentage-point rise, $0.01=100bp$, gives $\Delta P\approx-50{,}000{,}000\times0.01=-500{,}000$ yuan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- duration-source-note:start DR03 -->
> [!note] 校注：本课负号约定与基点单位
> [[金额久期]] · [[DV01]] · [[组合DV01聚合]]
>
> 本课将 $DD=P_y=-D_{mod}P$ 记为“加息方向的金额导数”，所以普通多头固定现金流债券的 $DD$ 为负。另一常用约定把金额久期记成 $-P_y=D_{mod}P$，此时该多头值为正；两种报告不能不换符号就相加。这里的 $|DD|10^{-4}$ 是一阶 DV01 大小，不是任意有限 1bp 冲击的精确重估差。组合应先按同一曲线、币种和符号合计各头寸的有符号敏感度，再按报告目的取绝对值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This course uses $DD=P_y=-D_{mod}P$, the monetary derivative in the rate-rise direction, which is negative for an ordinary long fixed-cash-flow bond. Another common money-duration convention is $-P_y=D_{mod}P$, positive for that long position. Convert signs before combining reports. Here $|DD|10^{-4}$ is a first-order DV01 magnitude, not the exact repricing difference for every finite one-basis-point shock. Aggregate signed position sensitivities under matching curve, currency, and sign conventions before taking an absolute value if required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.5[[有效久期|有效久期]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.5 [[有效久期|Effective duration]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有效久期（[[有效久期|Effective Duration]]）是指**在考虑现金流可随市场利率变化（如含期权、可赎回债券等复杂产品）时，债券价格对利率小幅变化的敏感度**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Effective duration is the **sensitivity of a bond's price to a small change in rates when its cash flows can themselves change with rates**, as with callable bonds, prepayable loans, and other instruments containing embedded options.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它反映了**实际可观察到的价格—利率关系**，是利率风险管理中对复杂债券（如含提前还款权、可赎回债券）的标准敏感性度量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It captures the **observable price–yield relationship** and is the standard duration measure for instruments whose expected cash flows change when rates change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**数值公式（近似数值法）：**
$$
\text{有效久期} = \frac{P_{-} - P_{+}}{2 \Delta y \cdot P_0}
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Numerical approximation:**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $P_{-}$：利率下降$\Delta y$后债券价格
- $P_{+}$：利率上升$\Delta y$后债券价格
- $P_0$：当前价格
- $\Delta y$：利率变动幅度（如0.01）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $P_{-}$: bond price after yields fall by $\Delta y$
- $P_{+}$: bond price after yields rise by $\Delta y$
- $P_0$: current bond price
- $\Delta y$: size of the yield shift, such as $0.01$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- duration-source-note:start DR04 -->
> [!note] 校注：有效久期依赖模型与冲击，不是直接观测常数
> [[有效久期]] · [[有效凸性]]
>
> “实际可观察”应理解为要刻画含选择权的价格响应，不能把这个指标当作无模型的市场观测。须指定被移动的基准曲线、上下冲击 $h=\Delta y>0$、估值日、固定的利差等其他参数，并在每个情景中重估行权或提前还款及折现。以同一基准模型的 $P_0>0$ 归一化，$(P_- -P_+)/(2hP_0)$ 是对称差分估计；应检查不同小步长的稳定性。若仅折现原固定现金流，就没有计入选择权导致的现金流变化。定义边界见 [OCC 印刷 75 页 / PDF 78 页](https://www.occ.treas.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/files/interest-rate-risk/pub-ch-interest-rate-risk.pdf#page=78)。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Observable” describes the intended option-sensitive price response, not a model-free market constant. Specify the benchmark curve, symmetric shock $h=\Delta y>0$, valuation date, and other fixed inputs such as spread; revalue exercise or prepayment and discounting in each scenario. Normalized by the same model's baseline $P_0>0$, $(P_- -P_+)/(2hP_0)$ is a central-difference estimate whose stability should be checked across small bump sizes. Discounting unchanged cash flows omits option-induced payment changes. See OCC printed page 75 / PDF page 78.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- duration-source-note:end -->

## 2.6 [[债券凸性|曲率]]
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.6 [[债券凸性|curvature]]*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

曲率就是凸性.
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Curvature is convexity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**曲率（[[债券凸性|Convexity]]）的概念：** 曲率衡量的是债券价格对利率变化的二阶敏感度，即价格-利率曲线的弯曲程度（[[债券凸性|凸性]]）。定义上，曲率 $C$ 可表示为债券价格关于收益率的二阶导数，标准定义公式为：
$$
C = \frac{1}{B}\frac{d^2B}{dy^2}.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Concept of curvature or [[债券凸性|convexity]]:** Convexity measures the second-order sensitivity of a bond's price to yield—the curvature of its price–yield relationship. If $B$ is the bond price and $y$ the yield, the standard normalized measure is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其计算离散形式通常为各期现金流现值按期限的$t(t+1)$加权求和再除以价格 。直观理解，曲率反映了当利率变化较大时，久期估计产生误差的修正程度。高曲率的债券，其价格-利率关系曲线更加弯曲，上涨下跌不对称性更强。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In discrete time, convexity is obtained by weighting the present value of each cash flow by a maturity term such as $t(t+1)$ and dividing by price. Intuitively, convexity corrects the error in a duration-only estimate when yields move by a material amount. Greater convexity means a more curved and asymmetric price response.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- duration-source-note:start DR05 -->
> [!note] 校注：凸性公式不能省掉复利因子
> [[债券凸性]] · [[曲线形变]]
>
> 对年复利 $P(y)=\sum_t CF_t(1+y)^{-t}$、$y>-1$，正确的归一化凸性为 $C_y=\sum_t t(t+1)CF_t(1+y)^{-t-2}/P$；若使用现值权重，仍须乘 $(1+y)^{-2}$。对每年复利 $m$ 次的名义年率、以年计的时点 $t_i$，则为 $C_y=\sum_i t_i(t_i+1/m)CF_i(1+y/m)^{-mt_i-2}/P$。连续复利年率下才得到 $C_r=\sum_i t_i^2CF_i e^{-rt_i}/P$。这里是价格对利率的归一化二阶导数，不是收益率曲线横截面的“曲率”，也不是未经归一化的 $P''$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For annually compounded $P(y)=\sum_t CF_t(1+y)^{-t}$ with $y>-1$, normalized convexity is $C_y=\sum_t t(t+1)CF_t(1+y)^{-t-2}/P$; a present-value-weighted expression still needs $(1+y)^{-2}$. For a nominal annual yield compounded $m$ times and payment times $t_i$ in years, it is $C_y=\sum_i t_i(t_i+1/m)CF_i(1+y/m)^{-mt_i-2}/P$. Continuous compounding instead gives $C_r=\sum_i t_i^2CF_i e^{-rt_i}/P$. This is normalized price convexity, not cross-sectional yield-curve curvature or unnormalized $P''$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- duration-source-note:end -->

- $C$ 是凸性（曲率），$D$ 是久期，$B$ 是债券价格，$y$ 是到期收益率
- 价格变动近似公式：
    $$
    \frac{\Delta B}{B} = -D \Delta y + \frac{1}{2}C (\Delta y)^2
    $$
- 意思是：价格对利率变动的敏感度，除了久期线性部分，还有二阶的凸性修正项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $C$ is convexity, $D$ is duration, $B$ is the bond price, and $y$ is yield to maturity.
- The approximate price-change formula is:
- In addition to the linear duration effect, it includes a second-order convexity adjustment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- duration-source-note:start DR06 -->
> [!note] 校注：二阶近似的坐标与余项
> [[久期价格近似]] · [[久期凸性价格近似]]
>
> 原式等号按“近似”理解：对当前点附近足够光滑的价格函数，$\Delta P/P\approx-D_y\Delta y+\tfrac12C_y(\Delta y)^2$，其中 $D_y=-P_y/P$、$C_y=P_{yy}/P$ 必须对同一变量求导。有限复利 YTM 下的一阶项用修正久期，不可直接用麦考利久期。连续复利年率下两者的相应数值才相等。二阶项改善局部估计，但不保证任意大冲击都准确；含期权或非平行变动还须使用相应模型和曲线坐标。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Read the original equality as an approximation. For a sufficiently smooth local price function, $\Delta P/P\approx-D_y\Delta y+\tfrac12C_y(\Delta y)^2$, where $D_y=-P_y/P$ and $C_y=P_{yy}/P$ use the same coordinate. The first-order term under a finite-compounding YTM uses modified, not unconverted Macaulay, duration. Their corresponding values coincide under a continuously compounded annual rate. A second-order term improves a local estimate but does not guarantee accuracy for arbitrarily large shocks; options and nonparallel moves require the appropriate model and curve coordinates.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2.9. [[关键利率久期|局部久期]]、局部久期分析与利率曲线扰动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2.9 [[关键利率久期|Key-rate duration]], key-rate analysis, and yield-curve shocks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现实中，利率变动经常不是严格的平行移动，即不同期限的收益率可能变化幅度不同，甚至方向相反（例如短端利率上升而长端下降，形成收益率曲线的扭曲）。为更准确地衡量这种情景下投资组合的利率风险，我们引入**局部久期**（也称**关键期限久期**，[[关键利率久期|Key Rate Duration]]）分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Actual yield-curve movements are rarely perfectly parallel. Yields at different maturities may move by different amounts or even in opposite directions—for example, the short end may rise while the long end falls. **Key-rate duration** measures portfolio sensitivity to localized changes at selected maturities and therefore captures these nonparallel curve movements more accurately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

局部久期的概念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Concept of [[关键利率久期|key-rate duration]]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- duration-source-note:start DR09 -->
> [!note] 校注：节点久期需要完整的曲线扰动规则
> [[关键利率久期]] · [[关键久期加总条件]]
>
> 关键期限不是只改一个孤立报价。须说明节点、插值和端点外推，使每个节点冲击生成一条可重估的曲线，并固定其余风险参数。若各节点敏感度是同一估值模型中对坐标 $s_k$ 的 $KRD_k=-P_{s_k}/P$，则小冲击的一阶近似为 $\Delta P/P\approx-\sum_kKRD_k\Delta s_k$。在该估值模型于当前点可微的前提下，节点扰动的合计确实生成同一平行曲线移动，是保证节点久期之和对应这个平行冲击久期的充分条件；没有这一分割条件时，具体头寸的数字仍可能偶然相等。它不必等于单券 YTM 修正久期。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A key-rate shock changes a defined curve, not an isolated quotation. Specify nodes, interpolation, and endpoint extrapolation so each bump produces a revaluable curve, with other risks held fixed. If $KRD_k=-P_{s_k}/P$ uses coordinates $s_k$ in one valuation model, a small shock gives $\Delta P/P\approx-\sum_kKRD_k\Delta s_k$. With that valuation model differentiable at the current point, node bumps jointly generating the same parallel curve move are sufficient to guarantee that key-rate durations sum to its parallel-shift duration. Without this partition condition, the numbers can still coincide accidentally for a particular position. The sum need not equal a bond's YTM-based modified duration.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

# 3. 利率风险的敏感性分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Sensitivity analysis of interest-rate risk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3.1 [[金额久期|Delta]]

**敏感性分析概述：** 利率风险敏感性分析主要关注**小幅利率变化**对银行收益或价值的影响，即计算利率风险的“Delta”。在利率风险管理中，Delta通常用**久期或其衍生指标**来度量 。有两种常见的Delta定义方法：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Overview:** Interest-rate sensitivity analysis examines how **small changes in rates** affect a bank's earnings or economic value. In this setting, the first-order exposure—its Delta—is usually measured with duration or a related quantity. Two common conventions are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 将 **money duration** 作为每 1.00 收益率变化的一阶金额敏感度；
- 或报告 **DV01**（Dollar Value of 1 Basis Point），其绝对值为 $|DD|\times0.0001$。两者数值相差 10,000 倍，不能混写单位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Use **money duration** for first-order monetary sensitivity per 1.00 yield change.
- Or report **[[DV01|DV01]]**, the dollar value of a one-basis-point move, with magnitude $|DD|\times0.0001$. The numerical units differ by a factor of 10,000 and must not be mixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- duration-source-note:start DR11 -->
> [!note] 校注：这里的金额导数针对价值，不直接等于净利息收入变化
> 本节 $DD$ 与 DV01 衡量当前价值对指定利率变量的敏感度。规划期 NII 是流量，须另用重定价时点、计息期间、传导与业务假设；不能把价格导数直接当成未来利润变化。另，$1\mathrm{bp}=0.01$ 个百分点 $=10^{-4}$ 的小数利率变化，基点单位本身与金额 DV01 是两种对象。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $DD$ and DV01 measure current-value sensitivity to a specified rate variable. Planning-horizon NII is a flow requiring repricing dates, accrual periods, pass-through, and business assumptions; a price derivative is not a direct forecast of profit changes. Also, $1\mathrm{bp}=0.01$ percentage point $=10^{-4}$ in decimal rate units. A basis point is a rate unit, whereas DV01 is a monetary sensitivity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- duration-source-note:end -->

## 3.2 缺口管理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3.2 Gap Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (1)利率敏感性缺口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(1)interest rate sensitivity gap*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**[[重定价缺口|重定价缺口管理]]：** 银行按时间桶统计利率敏感资产（RSA）和利率敏感负债（RSL），$GAP_k=RSA_k-RSL_k$。正负方向只能在两端利率**同幅、同向、同时、完全传导**，业务余额和结构静态，且忽略 [[IRRBB基差|基差]]、floor 和客户选择权时，用于规划期 NII 的一阶近似。若规划期为 $H$，头寸在 $t_i$ 时重定价，实际重定价后计息权重为 $w_i=\max(H-t_i,0)$，则

$$
\Delta NII_H\approx\Delta r\left(\sum_{i\in RSA}A_iw_i-\sum_{j\in RSL}L_jw_j\right).
$$

直接写 $\Delta NII\approx GAP\times\Delta r$ 还隐含全额头寸在规划期初重定价并按新利率计息整个期间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**[[重定价缺口|Repricing-gap management]]:** In bucket $k$, $GAP_k=RSA_k-RSL_k$. Its directional conclusion is only a first-order planning-horizon NII approximation when both sides receive the same simultaneous and fully passed-through rate shock, balances and business mix remain static, and [[IRRBB基差|basis risk]], floors, and customer options are ignored. If the horizon is $H$ and position $i$ reprices at $t_i$, the post-repricing accrual weight is $w_i=\max(H-t_i,0)$, so use the weighted formula above. Writing $\Delta NII\approx GAP\times\Delta r$ additionally assumes a full-horizon post-repricing accrual period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **正缺口：** 在上述条件下，加息通常提高 NII；
- **负缺口：** 在上述条件下，加息通常降低 NII；
- **零缺口：** 只表示桶内归类金额相等。若实际重定价时点、参考利率、beta、lag、floor 或行为现金流不同，NII 仍会受利率影响；它也不说明 EVE 已免疫。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Positive gap:** under those conditions, a rate rise usually increases NII.
- **Negative gap:** under those conditions, a rate rise usually reduces NII.
- **Zero gap:** only the bucketed amounts match. Different actual repricing dates, reference rates, betas, lags, floors, or behavioural cash flows can still change NII, and zero gap does not immunise EVE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (2)持续期缺口管理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(2) Duration Gap Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**[[久期缺口|久期缺口管理]]：** 重定价缺口关注 NII 流量，久期缺口关注权益经济价值（EVE）的一阶变化。规模调整后的定义必须是

$$
DGAP=D_A-\frac{L}{A}D_L,
$$

而不是 $D_A-D_L$。若 $D_A,D_L$ 都是 modified duration，共同小幅平行冲击下 $\Delta E\approx-A\,DGAP\,\Delta y$，**不再除以 $1+y$**。只有两端都是同一年复利收益率口径的 Macaulay duration 时，才有 $\Delta E\approx-A\,DGAP^{Mac}\Delta y/(1+y)$；不同曲线或复利口径应先分别转为 modified duration。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**[[久期缺口|Duration-gap management]]:** Repricing gap concerns NII flow; duration gap concerns the first-order change in EVE. The scale-adjusted definition is $DGAP=D_A-(L/A)D_L$, not $D_A-D_L$. With modified durations, $\Delta E\approx-A\,DGAP\,\Delta y$ and there is no additional division by $1+y$. Divide a Macaulay-based gap by $1+y$ only when both sides use the same annually compounded yield convention; otherwise convert each side separately to modified duration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **$DGAP>0$：** 共同小幅平行加息时，EVE 一阶下降；
- **$DGAP<0$：** 同一冲击下，EVE 一阶上升；
- **$DGAP=0$：** 只对当前久期、小幅共同平行冲击做到一阶匹配；不免疫 [[曲线形变|曲线形变]]、[[IRRBB基差|基差]]、凸性、[[无到期存款行为|存款行为]]或 [[提前还款选择权|提前还款]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **$DGAP>0$:** a small common parallel rate rise lowers EVE to first order.
- **$DGAP<0$:** the same shock raises EVE to first order.
- **$DGAP=0$:** first-order matching holds only for the current durations and a small common parallel shock; it does not immunise [[曲线形变|curve shape]], [[IRRBB基差|basis]], convexity, [[无到期存款行为|deposit behaviour]], or [[提前还款选择权|prepayment]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### (3)基点价值法和对冲
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*(3) Basis Point Value and Hedging*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**基点价值法和对冲：** BPV 或 DV01 是每 1bp 利率变化的金额敏感度。对固定现金流债券，$|DV01|\approx D_{mod}P\times0.0001$；它等于 money duration 的绝对值乘 $0.0001$，而不是与 money duration 同一单位。互换 DV01 是固定腿与浮动腿敏感度的净额。对冲时必须同时匹配绝对量和相反符号，并说明基准曲线与利率方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Basis-point value and hedging:** BPV or DV01 is monetary sensitivity per one basis point. For a fixed-cash-flow bond, $|DV01|\approx D_{mod}P\times0.0001$; it is money duration multiplied by $0.0001$, not the same unit. Swap DV01 is the net sensitivity of the fixed and floating legs. A hedge must match the magnitude with the opposite sign and identify the curve and rate direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通过以上方法，银行可以使用利率衍生品（期货、互换等）的头寸来管理Delta（DV01），从而将整体利率敏感度降至目标水平（例如零，若希望完全对冲）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the above approach, banks can use their positions in interest rate derivatives (futures, swaps, etc.) to manage Delta (DV01), thereby reducing overall interest rate sensitivity to a target level (e.g. zero, if full hedging is desired).
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- duration-source-note:start DR12 -->
> [!note] 校注：零 DV01 是指定因子的一阶抵消
> [[DV01对冲]] · [[关键利率对冲]]
>
> 将总 DV01 调到零，只是对所选共同冲击的一阶价值敏感度做匹配；不等于全部利率风险“完全对冲”。不同期限节点、基准曲线、凸性、期权与后续市场变化仍可产生损益。多个独立因子应逐项匹配，并检查对冲工具对其他因子的交叉暴露。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Zero total DV01 matches first-order value sensitivity to a selected common shock, not every form of interest-rate risk. Other maturity nodes, benchmark curves, convexity, options, and subsequent market changes can still generate P&L. Match multiple independent factors separately and include each hedge instrument's cross-exposures.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
<!-- duration-source-note:end -->

# 4.利用利率衍生工具管理风险的方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Managing risk with interest-rate derivatives*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
  

金融衍生工具为利率风险管理提供了重要手段。西方商业银行广泛使用**利率衍生品**对冲或转移利率风险，包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial derivatives are important tools for hedging or transferring interest-rate risk. Common instruments include:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **利率期货：** 卖出国债期货可对冲加息导致的债券价格下跌。在经典的 $100-隐含短期利率$ 价格报价下，未来短期融资利率上升会使期货价格下跌，因此借款人应**卖出（做空）期货**，而不是买入。Eurodollar 期货在本页只是历史课堂例子；美元市场现行短期利率期货以 SOFR 期货为主，使用时还要检查基差、合约乘数和 convexity adjustment。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Interest-rate futures:** selling government-bond futures can hedge a bond portfolio against a rate-driven price decline. Under the classic $100-$implied short-rate quotation, higher future funding rates mean a lower futures price, so a borrower hedges by **selling futures**, not buying them. Eurodollar futures here are a historical classroom example; current US short-rate futures are principally SOFR futures. Basis, contract multiplier, and convexity adjustment still need to be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **远期利率协议（FRA）：** FRA 是场外合约，按约定的起息日、期间、名义本金和固定利率，对市场参考利率与固定利率之差进行结算。它是**锁定远期借贷利率**，不是保留下行收益的 cap。未来借款人要对冲加息，经济方向是 FRA payer/borrower：支付固定、收取浮动差额，参考利率高于合同利率时收到补偿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Forward rate agreement (FRA):** an OTC contract that settles the difference between a contractual fixed rate and a market reference rate for a stated future period and notional. It **locks a forward borrowing or lending rate**; it is not a cap. A future borrower hedges rising rates in the economic payer/borrower direction—pay fixed and receive floating—so the FRA pays when the reference rate exceeds the contract rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **利率互换（Interest Rate Swaps）：** 若原负债支付浮动利率，要把融资成本转为固定，互换方向必须是 **pay fixed / receive floating（付固定、收浮动）**：收到的浮动腿抵消原浮动负债，剩下固定支出。同一方向也可将收固定的资产转为收浮动。其他条件不变时，市场互换固定利率上升，pay-fixed/receive-floating 旧互换的价值通常上升。历史合约曾使用 LIBOR；现行合约的浮动基准需按币种和合约核对，例如 OIS 使用隔夜指数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Interest-rate swap:** to convert a floating-rate liability into fixed funding cost, the swap must **pay fixed and receive floating**. The received floating leg offsets the original floating liability, leaving the fixed payment. The same direction converts a fixed-rate asset receipt into a floating-rate receipt. Other things equal, a rise in the market swap fixed rate usually increases the value of an existing pay-fixed/receive-floating swap. LIBOR belongs to historical contracts; the current floating benchmark must be checked by currency and contract, while OIS uses an overnight index.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **利率期权**：包括**利率上限期权（Cap）**、**下限期权（Floor）和利率期权债券**等。上限期权为持有者提供在利率高于行权利率时获得补偿的权利，可用于对冲浮动利率负债利率上升的风险；下限期权则保护最低利率，可对冲浮动利率资产利率下降的风险。**利率互换期权（Swaption）**赋予持有者在未来进入一个利率互换的选择权。银行可以买入相应的期权来防范极端利率走势造成的损失，同时保持在有利情形下的收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Interest-rate options:** caps compensate the holder when a reference rate exceeds the strike and can protect a floating-rate borrower against rising rates. Floors protect a minimum rate and can hedge the risk that income on a floating-rate asset will fall. A **swaption** gives its holder the right to enter an interest-rate swap in the future. Options limit losses from adverse extreme moves while preserving gains in favorable states.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **组合策略**： 银行也可以通过组合构造衍生工具策略，如**利率领式（Collar）**策略（买入上限同时卖出下限，限制利率波动区间），或动态对冲策略等，以较低成本达到风险管理目标 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Combination strategies:** a bank may construct an **interest-rate collar** by buying a cap and selling a floor, thereby limiting the relevant rate range at lower net cost. Dynamic hedging is another possibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**套期保值与投机：** 利率衍生工具不仅可用于保值，也可用于主动调整风险敞口、甚至投机获利 。例如，若一家银行判断未来利率将下行，可增加资产久期或利用互换降低付息成本以获取更高利差收益；相反，若预期利率上升，可通过先行卖出期货锁定高利率再买回等方式获利。但监管要求银行主要将衍生品用于风险管理而非纯投机，并建立相应内控防止过度风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Hedging and speculation:** Interest-rate derivatives can hedge existing exposure, actively reshape it, or be used to speculate. A bank expecting rates to fall might lengthen asset duration or use swaps to reduce funding costs; one expecting rates to rise might sell futures and later repurchase them. Regulation and internal controls generally require banks to use derivatives primarily for risk management and to limit excessive speculative positions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## **模拟试题与解答**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Practice questions and answers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**问题1：** 本课程把商业银行的利率风险讲成哪四类？这与 Basel 的三方面归类有什么关系？请分别定义并举例说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Question 1:** Which four teaching categories does this course use for a commercial bank's interest-rate risk, and how do they relate to Basel's three aspects? Define each and give an example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  

**解答：** 本课程为便于学习，把 gap risk 中的重定价与收益率曲线形变分开讲，因此列成四类。Basel SRP98.15 的原文口径是三方面：gap risk（其中包括收益率曲线的期限结构变化）、basis risk 和 option risk；不能把课程四分法冒充 Basel 的逐字分类。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** For teaching, this course separates repricing mismatch from yield-curve shape within gap risk and therefore lists four categories. Basel SRP98.15 instead identifies three aspects: gap risk, including changes in the term structure of rates; basis risk; and option risk. The course's four-part presentation is not Basel's verbatim taxonomy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **[[重定价缺口|重定价风险]]：** 由于资产、负债及表外业务的重定价时点或到期日不匹配而产生。例如，用短期负债支持长期固定利率贷款，加息时资金成本可先于贷款收益上升。实际 NII 还取决于重定价后计息时间、传导幅度和行为变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Repricing risk:** a mismatch between the repricing dates or maturities of assets, liabilities, and off-balance-sheet positions. If a bank funds long-term fixed-rate loans with short-term deposits, higher market rates raise deposit costs before loan income adjusts, compressing the spread and NII.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **[[IRRBB基差|基差风险]]：** 即使金额和重定价时点一致，不同参考利率，或对同一利率的 beta、lag 和 floor 不同，也会使资产收益与负债成本不同幅变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Basis risk:** exposure caused by assets and liabilities repricing from different reference rates, even when their repricing dates match. If loans are linked to one-year LPR while deposit rates are set separately, LPR may fall by more than deposit rates. Loan income then falls faster than funding cost, narrowing the spread. Use of several benchmarks such as LPR and Shibor makes this exposure more complex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **[[曲线形变|收益率曲线风险]]：** 同一指数曲线的不同期限不同幅变动，包括变陡、变平和中段曲率变化，会改变 NII 和 EVE。一个平行冲击或总久期无法覆盖这类暴露。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Yield-curve risk:** exposure to unexpected nonparallel changes in the curve's slope or shape. A bank that borrows short and lends long may benefit from a normal upward-sloping curve but lose NII when the curve inverts and short funding rates exceed long lending rates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **选择权风险：** 一类是市场利率达到条件便自动改变现金流的 automatic options，例如显式或隐含的 cap、floor 及其他嵌入式衍生条款；另一类是客户行为选择权，利率变化会改变 [[提前还款选择权|贷款提前还款]]、定期存款提前支取，以及 [[无到期存款行为|无到期存款的流失与定价行为]]。两类都可能改变现金流或付息率，必须在情景中重估，不能只折现原合同现金流。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Option risk:** automatic options, such as explicit or implicit caps, floors, and other embedded derivative terms, change cash flows mechanically when market conditions trigger them. Behavioural options depend on customer action: falling rates may increase prepayment of high-rate loans, while rising rates may encourage early deposit withdrawal or migration. Both types require scenario-dependent cash flows or rates rather than fixed contractual cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**问题2：** 某银行 0–6 个月的 [[重定价缺口]] 为 +5亿元。假设资产与负债在同一时点重定价，同幅、完全传导 100bp 的平行冲击，余额和业务结构静态，并忽略基差、floor 和客户选择权。请定性分析 NII 方向；负缺口又如何？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Question 2:** A bank has a positive six-month [[重定价缺口|repricing gap]] of CNY 500 million. Assume both sides reprice simultaneously, receive the same fully passed-through 100bp parallel shock, balances and business mix remain static, and basis, floors, and customer options are ignored. Explain the NII direction; what changes for a negative gap?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**解答：** **利率敏感性缺口**（Gap）为正表示在0-6月重定价区间内，银行的利率敏感资产规模大于利率敏感负债规模，即**可变动利率的资产超出负债5亿元**。当市场利率整体上升1%时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** A positive **interest-rate sensitivity gap** means that, within the 0–6 month repricing window, rate-sensitive assets exceed rate-sensitive liabilities by CNY 500 million. If rates rise by 1%:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **对于正缺口（+5亿）：** 银行需要为负债支付更高利息，但也能够从资产获取更高利息。由于资产端规模更大，资产利息收入的增加量将**大于**负债利息支出的增加量。因此，**净利息收入将增加**。换言之，在这一期限段，多出来的5亿元资产因为利率上升获得额外利息，而负债没有对应那么多增量，净收益正贡献。这体现了正缺口银行在利率上行时利差扩大的特征 。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Positive gap of CNY 500 million:** both asset income and liability expense rise, but the larger asset balance produces the greater change. NII therefore **increases**. The unmatched CNY 500 million of assets earns additional interest without an equal increase in funding expense.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
- **若缺口为负（例如 -5亿）：** 则情况相反。负缺口意味着利率敏感负债多于资产。利率上升1%时，银行利息支出增加额**大于**利息收入增加额，导致**净利息收入下降** 。可以这样理解：短期内需要付息的负债比能够重新投资提价的资产多，利率一涨，银行“亏”的部分超过“赚”的部分，净利差收窄甚至可能转负。因此负缺口银行害怕利率上升，但在利率下降时会受益（因为那时负债成本减少比资产收益减少更多，净利息收入反而上升）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Negative gap, for example negative CNY 500 million:** rate-sensitive liabilities exceed assets. A 1% rise in rates increases interest expense by more than interest income, so NII **falls** and the margin narrows. Falling rates have the opposite effect because liability costs then decline by more than asset yields.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    

总之，**只在题设的同幅、同时、静态和一阶条件下**，正缺口在加息时提高 NII，负缺口则降低 NII。若实际重定价时点不同，应用 $w_i=\max(H-t_i,0)$ 分别加权；零缺口也不能推出 NII 或 EVE 不受利率影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In summary, **only under the stated simultaneous, equal, static, first-order conditions** does a positive gap raise NII when rates rise and a negative gap reduce it. If actual repricing dates differ, apply $w_i=\max(H-t_i,0)$ separately. A zero gap does not imply that rates cannot affect NII or EVE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**解答：** 这道题考查利用衍生品的**DV01（[[DV01|基点价值]]）**来进行利率风险对冲的计算方法。核心思路是使对冲工具的总DV01等于组合的DV01，且方向相反，以实现1bp利率变动造成的损益互相抵消。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Answer:** Use the derivatives' **DV01, or [[DV01|basis-point value]]**, to size an opposite-signed sensitivity. The hedge DV01 should offset the portfolio DV01 so that the profit on one side of a one-basis-point move cancels the loss on the other.

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Five-year swap hedge:** The portfolio loses USD 4,000 when the market swap rate rises one basis point. <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    $$

    $$
    
    解得 $N_s = \frac{4000}{400} = 10$（百万美元）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solving gives $N_s=\frac{4000}{400}=10$ million dollars.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
    
    因此需要一笔**名义1000万美元**的5年期 **pay fixed / receive floating（付固定、收浮动）**互换。市场互换利率上升1bp时，该头寸约收益4000美元，抵补原组合损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The required hedge is a **USD 10 million five-year pay-fixed/receive-floating swap**, which gains about USD 4,000 for the stated one-basis-point rise and offsets the portfolio loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

综上，按题设可**卖出20手历史 Eurodollar 期货**，或进入**名义1000万美元、付固定收浮动的5年期互换**。DV01 定量规模，基准与方向决定对冲是否真正相反。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the stated historical exercise, sell **20 Eurodollar futures contracts**, or enter a **USD 10 million five-year pay-fixed/receive-floating swap**. DV01 sizes the hedge; benchmark and direction determine whether it is genuinely offsetting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [[DV01对冲]] · [[关键利率对冲]]

# 附录：数据、模型、行为与监管边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Appendix: data, model, behaviour, and regulatory boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 课堂近似不是精确监管 EVE
> 本页的 gap、duration、DV01 和单一冲击算例用于理解方向和一阶敏感度。它们不等于银行的完整 NII/EVE 模型，也不能把课堂现金流、单一收益率和静态余额伪装成精确监管结果。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The page's gap, duration, DV01, and single-shock examples explain direction and first-order sensitivity. They are not a bank's complete NII/EVE model and must not be presented as precise regulatory EVE based on classroom cash flows, one yield, and static balances.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **数据边界：** 输入要覆盖表内和表外利率敏感头寸，并统一名义本金、账面余额、经济价值、币种、曲线、重定价日、day-count 与复利口径。资产、负债与权益边界必须平衡且可对账。
- **模型边界：** 至少分开 [[IRRBB 的 NII 与 EVE|NII 与 EVE]]，区分静态、恒定资产负债表和动态业务情景；非平行冲击、基差和含期权现金流需用 key-rate 或完整情景重估。
- **行为边界：** [[合同期限、重定价期限与行为期限|合同、重定价与行为期限]]不能混用。[[无到期存款行为|无到期存款]]的核心余额、beta、lag 与流失，以及 [[提前还款选择权|提前还款]]速度，都是可压力测试的假设，不是永久常数。
- **监管与本地规则边界：** Basel Framework 是全球审慎标准，OCC 手册是美国银行监管指引；二者用于定位概念与模型限制，不替代机构所在司法辖区的实施规则、监管口径、内部模型政策和模型验证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Data boundary:** Inputs must cover on- and off-balance-sheet rate-sensitive positions and reconcile notional, book balance, economic value, currency, curve, repricing date, day count, and compounding. Assets, liabilities, and equity must balance under a stated perimeter.
- **Model boundary:** Keep [[IRRBB 的 NII 与 EVE|NII and EVE]] separate and distinguish run-off, constant-balance-sheet, and dynamic-business assumptions. Nonparallel shocks, basis, and option-dependent cash flows require key-rate or full scenario revaluation.
- **Behavioural boundary:** [[合同期限、重定价期限与行为期限|Contractual, repricing, and behavioural horizons]] are different. [[无到期存款行为|NMD]] core balances, betas, lags, and runoff, plus [[提前还款选择权|prepayment]] speeds, are stressable assumptions, not permanent constants.
- **Regulatory and local-rule boundary:** The Basel Framework is a global prudential standard; the OCC handbook is US supervisory guidance. They locate concepts and model limitations but do not replace applicable jurisdictional implementation, supervisory definitions, internal model policy, or independent model validation.
