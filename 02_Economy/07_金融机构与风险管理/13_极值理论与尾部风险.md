## 1. 不拟合整条分布，还能知道什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. What can we learn without fitting the whole distribution?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果有一千天损失，经验分布可以描述这段历史，却很难直接回答万分之一尾部的位置。EVT 的切入点不是凭空补出九千天，而是利用分布在极端区域可能具有的共同结构。先看每块最大的观测，再看超过一个高阈值的观测；这两条路线的对象不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A thousand daily losses describe observed history but cannot directly locate a one-in-ten-thousand tail quantile. EVT uses possible common structure in extremes; it does not invent another nine thousand observations. Block maxima and high-threshold exceedances provide two routes with different modelled objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[极值理论]]

![[分块极大值法]]

![[广义极值分布]]

GEV 给出候选极限的形状，但不是说随便拿一组最大值就已经服从 GEV。必须分清“某个母分布属于哪个吸引域”和“如果非退化极限存在，它只能是什么类型”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
GEV describes candidate limiting shapes; an arbitrary finite sample of maxima is not automatically GEV. Distinguish a parent distribution's domain of attraction from the possible type of a non-degenerate limit, if one exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 从最大值转向所有超额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. From maxima to all threshold excesses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每月只取一天会丢掉同月其他严重损失。POT 改问“超过阈值以后，还会再多损失多少”。先定义这个条件样本，再学 GPD 的分布形式，最后才问哪个定理允许用它逼近真实超额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking one observation per month discards other large losses in that month. POT asks how much additional loss occurs after a threshold is crossed. Define the conditional sample, learn the GPD family, and then examine the theorem supporting the approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[超阈值法]]

![[广义Pareto分布]]

![[超阈值极限定理]]

形状参数不只影响曲线“看起来多厚”。它还决定 GPD 模型是否有有限均值和方差；这会直接影响稍后的 ES。以下条件是精确 GPD 的矩条件，不能不加限定地搬到所有渐近同类的母分布上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Shape affects more than the visual thickness of the tail: it determines whether a GPD has finite moments, which matters for ES. The following exact-GPD moment conditions must not be transferred without qualification to every parent distribution in the same asymptotic class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 用同一组参数算完概率、VaR 和 ES
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Calculate probability, VaR and ES from one parameter set*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用课程计算题：损失阈值为 100 万元，5% 的观测超过阈值，GPD 形状为 0.2、尺度为 10 万元。下面连续做三件事：先问损失超过 150 万元的概率，再反解 99% VaR，最后求超过该分位数后的平均损失。始终保留同一时间单位和损失单位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the course parameters: a CNY 1 million loss threshold, a 5% exceedance rate, GPD shape 0.2 and scale CNY 100,000. Find the probability of exceeding CNY 1.5 million, invert the tail for 99% VaR, then calculate mean loss beyond that quantile. Keep the time horizon and monetary units fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[POT尾概率]]

![[POT分位数]]

算 ES 前还差一步：已经把起算点从阈值移到 VaR，超额分布的尺度也必须跟着变。先理解这一变化，再代入均值；这也为后面的阈值诊断提供依据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ES needs one more step: moving the origin from the threshold to VaR changes excess scale. Understand that change before taking the mean; it also supports threshold diagnostics later.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[GPD阈值稳定性]]

![[POT尾部ES]]

三次计算的结果分别约为 0.15625%、118.9865 万元和 136.2331 万元。第一个是无条件概率，第二个是分位数，第三个是尾部平均损失，不能相互替代。这里是在给定模型下计算，不是已经完成了一次真实数据估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three answers are about 0.15625%, CNY 1,189,865 and CNY 1,362,331. They are respectively an unconditional probability, a quantile and a tail mean, not interchangeable quantities. These calculations use a specified model; they do not constitute an empirical fit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 参数从哪里来，阈值凭什么这样选
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Where do the parameters and threshold come from?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际数据不会把参数附在表头。先在一个给定阈值下估计，再把阈值往上、往下移动，比较形状、修正尺度、均值超额和目标风险量。选择与诊断通常要反复做；不是挑出一张漂亮图就结束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Real data do not arrive with fitted parameters. Estimate at a candidate threshold, then vary it and compare shape, modified scale, mean excess and the target risk measure. Selection and diagnostics are iterative, not a search for one attractive plot.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 金融时间序列还缺哪一层
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. What changes for financial time series?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续几天都很危险，可能反映高波动状态，也可能留下过滤后仍未解释的极端聚集。先明确要预测“明天的条件损失”还是“随机一天的无条件损失分布”；过滤、聚集处理和概率分母都应跟着这个目标走。这里的无条件风险仍可是一日风险，不是更长的持有期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consecutive extremes may reflect a high-volatility state or clustering that remains after filtering. Decide whether the target is tomorrow's conditional loss or the unconditional loss distribution for a randomly selected day. Filtering, cluster treatment and probability denominators must match that target. Unconditional risk can still concern a one-day horizon; it does not imply a longer holding period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
