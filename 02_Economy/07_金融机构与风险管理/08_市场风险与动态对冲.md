# 市场风险与动态对冲
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Market Risk and Dynamic Hedging*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从这里连续读到最后，你应当能解释一笔头寸为何随市场变化、把敏感度换成正确的交易数量，并逐笔算清再平衡与融资后的损益。关系总览见 [[市场风险、Greeks 与动态对冲.canvas|主题图]]；原始课堂讲解与作业保存在 [[08_操作员如何管理风险暴露|第 8 章课程记录]]，其中的数值或条件差异由相邻校注说明。

Read this path to explain a position's response to markets, convert sensitivities into signed trades, and account for rebalancing and financing. ## 1. 先把头寸翻译成价值函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Translate the position into a value function*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先不要背五个字母。面对“持有海外股票”，第一个问题是记账币种；面对“买入远期”，第一个问题是到期收什么、付什么。只有方向、数量和输入明确，后面的正负号才有意义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before memorising the five letters, ask which currency reports the foreign equity's value and what a forward receives and pays. Signs become meaningful only after direction, quantity and inputs are specified.

## 2. 期权价格从哪里来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Where the option value comes from*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直线的斜率容易看出来，期权曲线则需要一个估值模型。先在一期例子里亲手复制两种支付，理解定价概率为什么由无套利决定；再把同一个逻辑用于欧式期权。这里不要求先接受“投资者都是风险中性的”这个故事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A straight line has an obvious slope; an option curve needs a valuation model. First replicate two terminal payments in a one-period example to see how no-arbitrage determines pricing weights, then apply the same idea to a European option.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[风险中性定价]]

![[BSM期权定价]]

合约若允许提前行权，问题就多了一层：不是只给到期支付定价，还要决定在哪个允许的时点领取支付。先学比较方法，再看一个可以简化为欧式的问题，最后用两期看跌例检查“今天等待”和“未来的选择没有价值”为什么不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Early exercise adds a decision to valuation: when should the holder take the payoff? Learn the comparison rule, examine a case that reduces to European valuation, then use a two-period put to distinguish waiting today from having no valuable future exercise opportunities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[美式行权决策]]

![[无股息看涨不提前行权]]

![[当前不行权与提前权利金]]

![[风险模拟P-Q分工]]

回到第 7 章时，区分两个问题：用真实世界模型问“未来损失多大”，用条件定价模型问“到了这个状态，未到期合约值多少”。二者可以接在同一次风险模拟里，不需要各建一套期权知识。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Returning to Chapter 7, distinguish forecasting future losses from valuing an outstanding contract within a future state. Both fit into one risk simulation and reuse the same option-pricing objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 一次只改变一个输入
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Change one input at a time*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把价格函数固定住。每一个 Greek 都是一次带条件的提问：“只改变这个输入，其他输入先不动，会怎样？”理解这一点后，现货、时间、利率和波动率的单位就不能混着用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the valuation function. Each Greek asks a conditional question: what happens when this input changes and the others remain fixed? Spot, time, rate and volatility units must then be kept distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 把敏感度接回实际损益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Turn sensitivities into P&L estimates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

导数本身不是一次市场变化的损益。先乘正确的冲击，再问被省略的项是什么。下面从一个现货变化走向两个因子共同变化：外币股票的例子会直接显示，两个自身 Gamma 都为零，仍然可能存在交叉项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A derivative is not the P&L of a market move. Multiply by the correctly scaled shock, then identify omitted terms. Moving from one spot factor to foreign equities shows why zero own-factor Gammas do not eliminate cross effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

![[Delta-Gamma价格近似]]

![[交叉Gamma]]

![[多因子二阶损益近似]]

![[Greeks损益归因]]

若近似与重估存在差额，不能直接宣判“漏了某个因子”。先查时间、现金流、成交、单位和模型输入是否一致，再按 [[估值近似验证]] 检查它是否足以支持当前风险用途。这里复用已经核验的验证方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An unexplained difference is not by itself proof of a missing factor. Check timing, cash flows, trades, units and inputs, then apply the existing [[估值近似验证|approximation-validation method]] to the intended risk use.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 从一张期权到一笔对冲订单
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. From one option to a hedge order*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一步先聚合已有持仓，再求新增交易；负数量表示空头。对冲的目标是合并后某个敏感度为零，不能只看对冲工具自己的符号。更不能把“最终需要持有多少”当成“现在还应再交易多少”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Aggregate existing positions before solving for new trades, with negative quantities representing shorts. Neutrality concerns the combined position. Distinguish the required final holding from the additional order needed now.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 时间经过后，钱从哪里来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. After time passes, where does the money come from?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一张表里保持 Delta 为零，还不是一条可执行策略。市场先移动，旧持仓先产生损益，现金先计息；然后你才按新价格交易。新增股票的钱必须从现金账户扣除，不能偷偷把账户重置成“刚好复制”的金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keeping a spreadsheet Delta-neutral is not yet an executable strategy. The market moves first, old holdings accrue P&L and cash earns or pays interest; only then do you trade at the new price. Purchases must be debited from cash rather than resetting the account to make replication exact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

The $-36$ in Exercise 8.9 is a second-order spot-only estimate, not complete holding-period P&L with time, interest, volatility changes and trading costs. To study the strategy, retain its path and cash flows using [[风险模拟路径依赖|path-dependent risk simulation]].

## 7. 用一个完整任务检查是否能用了
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Check the whole workflow*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

请用“100 股、欧元股价 50→52、美元/欧元 1.20→1.25”的例子，不看答案完成：写出价值函数，列出两个一阶项与一个交叉项，把 6000→6500 的总变化核齐。再用动态 Delta 对冲卡里的三日路径，逐行解释为什么再平衡不改变当刻净值，但对冲组合不必等于当刻期权价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use 100 shares with euro price 50→52 and USD/EUR 1.20→1.25: write the value function, its two first-order contributions and the cross term, and reconcile 6000→6500. Then explain each row of the three-day dynamic hedge: rebalancing preserves current net value but does not force it to equal the option value.
