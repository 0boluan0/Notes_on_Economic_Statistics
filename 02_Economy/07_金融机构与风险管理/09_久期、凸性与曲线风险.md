# 久期、凸性与曲线风险：从债券价格走到对冲
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Duration, convexity, and curve risk: from a bond price to a hedge*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券定价回答“这组支付今天值多少”，这一章接着问：“利率变了，它会少值多少？”先从一只支付确定的债券开始，算清现金流时间、价格斜率和弯曲程度；然后放开“只有一个收益率在变”的假设，处理曲线扭曲与提前赎回，最后决定怎样对冲。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bond pricing asks what a set of payments is worth today. This chapter asks how that value changes when rates move. We begin with fixed payments, work out their timing and the slope and curvature of price, then move beyond a single yield to curve twists, early redemption, and hedging.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 1. 先看钱在什么时候回来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First, locate the payments in time*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一只面值 100、每年付息 8、三年到期的债券，并不是三年后才一次性收到全部价值。它在第 1、2、3 年分别支付 8、8、108。在年复利到期收益率为 5% 时，按 [[固定现金流债券定价]]：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A three-year bond with face value 100 and annual coupons of 8 does not return all its value at maturity. It pays 8, 8, and 108 at the ends of years one, two, and three. At an annually compounded YTM of 5%, [[固定现金流债券定价|fixed-cash-flow pricing]] gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P=\frac8{1.05}+\frac8{1.05^2}+\frac{108}{1.05^3}=108.169744\ldots.$$

把每笔支付的现值除以总价，就得到它对今天价值的贡献比例。再用这些比例加权支付时间，便是 [[麦考利久期]]：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Divide each payment's present value by the total price to obtain its share of today's value. The weighted average of payment times is [[麦考利久期|Macaulay duration]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$D_{Mac}=\frac{1\times8/1.05+2\times8/1.05^2+3\times108/1.05^3}{P}=2.792046\ldots\text{ 年}.$$

“2.79 年”描述的是支付时间的现值重心，不是某一天累计收款恰好收回本金，也不是已经考虑了真实再投资收益的回本期。零息债只有一个支付日，这个重心才恰好落在到期日；付息债会被较早的息票往前拉。比较两只债券时，不能仅凭到期日排序，具体对照见 [[到期日与久期]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The 2.79 years locate the present-value centre of the payments. They are not the date on which cumulative receipts recover the investment, nor a payback period incorporating realised reinvestment returns. A zero-coupon bond has only one payment date, so its duration equals maturity; earlier coupons pull the centre forward. See [[到期日与久期|maturity versus duration]] before ranking different bonds by maturity alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 为什么平均时间能变成价格敏感度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How payment timing becomes price sensitivity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对上面的价格式求导，每笔支付都会多出一个时间系数和一个贴现因子。将负导数除以当前价格，就得到 [[修正久期]]。若 $y$ 是每年复利 $m$ 次的名义年收益率，支付时间 $t_k$ 以年计，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiating the price equation introduces a time factor and an extra discount factor for each payment. The negative derivative divided by current price is [[修正久期|modified duration]]. With nominal annual yield $y$, $m$ compounding periods per year, and payment times $t_k$ in years:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(y)=\sum_k CF_k(1+y/m)^{-mt_k},\qquad
D_{mod}=-\frac{P'(y)}P=\frac{D_{Mac}}{1+y/m}.
$$

这里的导数关系是精确的；把有限幅度的价格变化替换成“当前斜率乘收益率变化”，才是 [[久期价格近似]]。本例一年付息一次，$D_{mod}=2.792046/1.05=2.659091$。若收益率立即从 5% 升至 5.5%，应代入 $\Delta y=0.005$，而不是 0.5：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivative identity is exact. The [[久期价格近似|duration price approximation]] begins when a finite price change is replaced by the current slope times the yield change. Here, annual coupons give $D_{mod}=2.792046/1.05=2.659091$. A rise from 5% to 5.5% means $\Delta y=0.005$, not 0.5:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\Delta P\approx-PD_{mod}\Delta y=-1.438166,\qquad P_{linear}\approx106.731578.$$

每年付息两次时，要同时把贴现率换成 $y/2$、期数换成 $2t$；不能把按“期”算出的麦考利久期直接当成年数。若改用连续复利率 $z$，价格写成 $\sum CF_ke^{-zt_k}$，负相对导数就是按该价格算出的平均支付时间。分母不是可有可无，而是由所选利率坐标决定的；需要时回到 [[债券付息周期换算]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With semiannual payments, both the period rate and the number of periods must change: use $y/2$ and $2t$. A duration measured in coupon periods is not already in years. Under continuous yield $z$, price is $\sum CF_ke^{-zt_k}$ and its negative relative derivative equals the payment-time average computed from that price. The conversion factor depends on the yield coordinate; see [[债券付息周期换算|coupon-period conversion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 凸性补上切线没有描述的弯曲
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Convexity adds the curvature missing from the tangent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直接以 5.5% 重估，本例价格为 $106.744833$，比刚才的直线估计高约 0.0133。原因可以从 [[债券凸性]] 看出来：固定非负支付的价格曲线向上弯，当前点的切线在曲线下方。再求一次导数，令 $C=P''(y)/P$，本例 $C=9.880406$。按 [[久期凸性价格近似]] 加入二阶项：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Direct repricing at 5.5% gives $106.744833$, about 0.0133 above the tangent estimate. [[债券凸性|Bond convexity]] explains why: with fixed nonnegative payments, the price curve bends upwards and lies above its tangent. Let $C=P''(y)/P$; here $C=9.880406$. The [[久期凸性价格近似|duration-convexity approximation]] adds the second-order term:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac{\Delta P}P\approx-D_{mod}\Delta y+\frac12C(\Delta y)^2.$$

| 同一只债券、同一次 50bp 加息 | 新价格 |
| --- | ---: |
| 当前久期的一阶近似 | 106.731578 |
| 久期加凸性的二阶近似 | 106.744937 |
| 按原支付重新贴现 | 106.744833 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| The same bond after the same 50bp rise | New price |
| --- | ---: |
| First-order duration estimate | 106.731578 |
| Second-order duration-convexity estimate | 106.744937 |
| Direct repricing of the payments | 106.744833 |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二阶估计在这个例子中明显更准，但仍不是精确价格。冲击越大、现金流分布越靠后或行权决策变化越明显，就越需要检查完整重估。读课堂 9.17–9.18 时也要区分“久期相近”“一阶变化相近”和“实际变化相等”：[[同久期不同凸性]] 给出一组久期精确相同、凸性仍不同的对照。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second-order estimate is much better in this example, but it is still an approximation. Larger moves, later payments, or changes in exercise decisions make full repricing more important. In Exercises 9.17–9.18, distinguish similar duration, similar first-order changes, and equal actual changes. [[同久期不同凸性|Equal duration with different convexity]] gives a comparison with exactly equal durations but different curvature.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 把百分比敏感度换成真实金额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Turn relative sensitivity into a monetary exposure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交易台要问的是“加息一个基点亏多少钱”，而不只是“久期有多大”。若头寸当前价值为 $V$，本组原子把 [[金额久期]] 定为 $-\partial V/\partial y$，把 [[DV01]] 定为它乘以 $10^{-4}$。因此，普通固定现金流多头的 DV01 为正，但加息损益为负：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A trading desk needs the monetary loss from a one-basis-point rise, not just a duration number. These atoms define [[金额久期|money duration]] as $-\partial V/\partial y$ and [[DV01]] as that quantity times $10^{-4}$. An ordinary long fixed-cash-flow position therefore has positive DV01 but negative P&L when yields rise:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$DV01=VD_{mod}\times10^{-4},\qquad\Delta V\approx-DV01\times\Delta y_{bp}.$$

市值 1000 万元、修正久期 5 的头寸，DV01 为 5000 元／bp；加息 10bp 的一阶损失是 5 万元。**课堂原记录有些地方直接把“加息 1bp 的损益”称为带符号 DV01，符号与这里相反。** 两种约定都能使用，但必须写清楚，不能看到两个正数就以为风险会抵消。金额应按各自持仓规模正确缩放，并统一币种和利率冲击口径；对冲两腿的本金不必相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A CNY 10 million position with modified duration 5 has DV01 of CNY 5,000 per bp; a 10bp rise produces an approximate CNY 50,000 loss. **Some classroom passages call the signed P&L for a 1bp rise “signed DV01”, using the opposite sign convention.** Either convention works if stated consistently. Equal positive numbers do not by themselves offset. Scale each exposure to its own position size and use a common currency and rate-shock convention; hedge notionals need not be equal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

组合先把同一冲击下的金额导数相加，再除以组合价值，就得到 [[组合久期聚合]] 与 [[组合凸性聚合]]；用持仓市值加权，是这一步推导的结果，不是拿各券久期任意求平均。对于净值很小甚至为零的多空组合，比例可能极大或根本无定义，而 [[组合DV01聚合]] 仍可直接给出有意义的金额暴露。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Add monetary derivatives under one common shock before dividing by portfolio value. This gives [[组合久期聚合|portfolio duration]] and [[组合凸性聚合|portfolio convexity]]; market-value weighting follows from the derivation rather than being an arbitrary averaging rule. A long-short portfolio with very small or zero net value can have an unstable or undefined relative duration, while [[组合DV01聚合|aggregate DV01]] still gives a meaningful monetary exposure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 先说清楚，究竟是哪一个利率在变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Specify which rate is changing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到这里，我们一直改变一只债券自身的 $y$。这个 [[到期收益率]] 是把它的全价和约定支付压成一个数字；现实中却有一整条 [[利率期限结构]]。一年即期率上涨、十年即期率下跌时，不能把两个变化塞进同一个 $\Delta y$。即使各期限都涨 10bp，按新即期率逐笔贴现，也未必等价于把这只债券的 YTM 加 10bp。这就是 [[收益率久期与曲线久期]] 要区分的两个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So far, we have changed one bond's own $y$. That [[到期收益率|YTM]] summarises its full price and specified payments in one number, whereas the market has an entire [[利率期限结构|term structure]]. A rise in the one-year spot rate and a fall in the ten-year rate cannot both be represented by one $\Delta y$. Even a 10bp rise at every spot maturity need not be equivalent to a 10bp increase in this bond's YTM. [[收益率久期与曲线久期|Yield duration versus curve duration]] separates these questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[有效久期]] 的做法是先指定基准曲线、保持不变的利差与模型参数，再将曲线向上、向下各移动 $h$，重新估值。普通固定现金流债券也可以这样测量；若存在 [[可赎回债券|赎回权]] 或 [[提前还款选择权|提前还款]]，重估还必须容许支付路径随利率变化。所得 $V_-$、$V_+$ 是情景模型的结果，不是已经观察到的未来成交价。[[有效凸性]] 则用相同的三个价格估计二阶反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[有效久期|Effective duration]] specifies the benchmark curve and the spreads and model parameters held fixed, shifts the curve up and down by $h$, and reprices the position. This also works for fixed-cash-flow bonds. With a [[可赎回债券|callable bond]] or [[提前还款选择权|prepayable loan]], valuation must allow payment paths to change with rates. The resulting $V_-$ and $V_+$ are modelled scenario values, not observed future transaction prices. [[有效凸性|Effective convexity]] estimates the second-order response from the same three prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

降息可能使发行人更愿意赎回，限制持有人的涨价收益，这解释了为什么含期权债券可能出现 [[负凸性边界|局部负凸性]]。但不是有赎回条款就处处负凸，也不能把收益率横截面的“曲率”当成价格曲线的“凸性”；前者描述 [[曲线形变]]，后者描述价格怎样响应所选冲击。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rate decline may encourage the issuer to call the bond, limiting the holder's price gain and potentially producing [[负凸性边界|local negative convexity]]. A call provision does not imply negative convexity at every rate, however. Nor is curvature across yield maturities the same as convexity of price: the former describes [[曲线形变|curve shape]], while the latter describes the price response to a specified shock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 从曲线节点走到可核对的对冲
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From curve nodes to a checkable hedge*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[关键利率久期]] 把“整条曲线一起动”拆成若干明确的节点扰动。必须说明节点间如何插值、最短和最长期限之外如何处理；这些规则决定实际动到了哪些贴现率。给定一组小幅节点变化，价格的一阶估计为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[关键利率久期|Key-rate duration]] replaces one parallel move with specified local node shocks. Interpolation and treatment beyond the shortest and longest nodes determine which discount rates actually move. For small changes in these coordinates, the first-order price estimate is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac{\Delta V}{V}\approx-\sum_j KRD_j\Delta r_j.$$

当这些局部扰动合起来恰好覆盖同一种平行移动，且估值在当前点可微时，可以保证关键久期在导数意义下加总为平行曲线久期；缺少这一分割条件，不能一般地保证相等，具体见 [[关键久期加总条件]]。有限幅度重估算出的和一般只近似满足这个关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the local shocks jointly reproduce the same parallel move and valuation is differentiable at the current point, key-rate derivatives are guaranteed to add to parallel-curve duration. Without this partition condition, equality is not generally guaranteed; see [[关键久期加总条件|conditions for adding key-rate durations]]. Finite-bump estimates generally satisfy the relation only approximately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课堂表 9.5 的 1、2、3、4、5、7、10 年节点分别为 $2.0,1.6,0.6,0.2,-0.5,-1.8,-1.9$，末尾的 $0.2$ 是**总计**。总久期很小，不说明各段风险很小：若只让一年节点上升 10bp，500 万美元头寸仍约损失 $2\times500万\times0.001=1万美元$。原图首行乘式少了一个零，计算应以题干的 500 万美元为准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Classroom Table 9.5 gives node durations of $2.0,1.6,0.6,0.2,-0.5,-1.8,-1.9$ for years 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, and 10. The final $0.2$ is the **total**. A small total does not mean small local exposures: a 10bp rise at the one-year node still produces an approximate USD 10,000 loss on a USD 5 million position. The first multiplication in the source image drops one zero; use the stated USD 5 million position size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对冲也要逐个因子核对。[[DV01对冲]] 先解决一个共同风险因子的金额抵消；[[关键利率对冲]] 再处理多个节点。假设组合在一年、五年利率各上升 1bp 时，分别损失 500 和 4000 美元。一个只在一年节点提供补偿的工具，无论做多大，都不能消掉五年节点暴露。若工具会同时影响多个节点，就要把每个工具的整列敏感度放进方程组，而不是分别算两个比例以后直接相加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hedging requires the same factor-by-factor check. [[DV01对冲|A DV01 hedge]] offsets one common risk factor; [[关键利率对冲|key-rate hedging]] handles several nodes. Suppose separate 1bp rises at the one-year and five-year rates cost the portfolio USD 500 and USD 4,000. A tool that offsets only the one-year node cannot remove the five-year exposure, regardless of its size. If instruments affect several nodes, include each instrument's full sensitivity column in a system of equations rather than calculating isolated ratios and adding the trades.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把“联立”实际算一次：另取组合节点 DV01 为 $(60,90)^T$ 美元／bp，工具 A、B 每单位多头的两节点敞口分别为 $(20,5)^T$、$(5,20)^T$。工具也会影响另一个节点，所以两行必须同时抵销：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work through a simultaneous hedge: take a separate portfolio with node DV01s $(60,90)^T$ USD per bp. A long unit of instrument A contributes $(20,5)^T$, and B contributes $(5,20)^T$. Each instrument affects both nodes, so both equations must hold:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{pmatrix}20&5\\5&20\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}h_A\\h_B\end{pmatrix}
=-\begin{pmatrix}60\\90\end{pmatrix}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad h_A=-2,\quad h_B=-4.
$$

做空 2 单位 A 和 4 单位 B 后，第一节点剩余 $60-40-20=0$，第二节点剩余 $90-10-80=0$。若只按总 DV01 做空 6 单位 A，总和也为零，却留下 $(-60,60)^T$：平移时互相抵销，扭曲时仍会产生损益。是否对冲成功，要看你原先承诺消掉的是哪几个因素。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Shorting two units of A and four of B leaves $60-40-20=0$ at the first node and $90-10-80=0$ at the second. Shorting six units of A alone also gives zero total DV01, but leaves $(-60,60)^T$: the exposures cancel under a parallel move and still produce P&L under a twist. A hedge succeeds only relative to the factors it was intended to offset.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些都是当前情景附近的匹配。利率移动后，久期、凸性和期权行使概率会变化；对冲还可能留下基差与信用风险。若对象是银行整体，下一步用 [[久期缺口]] 接入资产与负债的规模，再进入 [[银行账簿利率风险.canvas|银行账簿关系图]] 区分经济价值与净利息收入。它们复用这里的敏感度，不再建立另一套久期定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These matches are local to the current valuation. Rate moves change duration, convexity, and exercise probabilities, and basis or credit exposures may remain. For a bank, use [[久期缺口|the duration gap]] to incorporate asset and liability sizes, then the [[银行账簿利率风险.canvas|banking-book map]] to distinguish economic value from net interest income. Those topics reuse these sensitivities rather than defining duration again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
