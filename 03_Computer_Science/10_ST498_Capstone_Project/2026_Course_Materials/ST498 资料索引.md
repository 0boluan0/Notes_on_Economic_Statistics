## 当前课程安排

课程从 11 月持续到次年 8 月，没有常规 lectures 或 seminars。小组与 LSE 导师、行业伙伴定期会面，频率在 11 月 kick-off 时商定。唯一一次全体线下课程说明会要求必须参加：**2026-10-19，10:00–11:30，KSW.G.01**。时间地点来自当前 Moodle 正文，日期也与新版幻灯片封面一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course runs from November to the following August without regular lectures or seminars. Groups meet with their LSE supervisor and industry partner at a frequency agreed during the November kick-off. Attendance at the single in-person course briefing is mandatory: **19 October 2026, 10:00–11:30, KSW.G.01**. The current Moodle text supplies the time and venue, and the date agrees with the new presentation cover.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
当前幻灯片目标为 **16 个三人小组**。组员自行协商，每位学生须对全部项目排序；小组可一致同意同一份偏好表。组队信息和偏好提交是 **10 月 19 日说明会之后**的事项。每组将获得用于项目工作及提交的 GitHub 仓库。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The current slides target **16 groups of three**. Students agree their group membership and each ranks every project; a group may agree to send one common ranking. Submitting membership details and preferences is an action **after the 19 October briefing**. Each group will receive a GitHub repository for project work and submissions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
