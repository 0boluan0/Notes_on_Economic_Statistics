## 4. 写一条 AI-Use Journal

让一个聊天机器人解释 `git fetch`、`git pull` 和 `git merge` 的作用与区别。在 journal 中记录：机器人给出的解释；你在课程 GitHub 仓库中如何逐项检验这些说法；实际测试是支持、反驳还是仅部分支持它们。写清执行的命令及实际结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ask a chatbot of your choice to explain what `git fetch`, `git pull` and `git merge` do and how they differ. In your AI-use journal, note down: (1) the explanation the chatbot gave you, (2) what you then did to test each of its claims yourself in the course's GitHub repository, and (3) whether your tests confirmed, contradicted, or only partly matched what the chatbot told you. Be specific — include the commands you ran and what actually happened.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
