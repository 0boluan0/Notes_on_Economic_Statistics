## AI 使用要求

当前 Assessments 页面采用 Position 2（limited authorised use）：可以辅助解释或调试代码，分析、文字解释与最终图形须由学生完成；获准使用也需披露。页面要求在参考文献之后的 appendix 记录工具名称和版本、提供方、URL、使用情境与反思。完整要求以 [Assessments 原页](https://moodle.lse.ac.uk/course/section.php?id=347353) 为准。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Assessments page applies Position 2, limited authorised use: tools may help explain or debug code, while analysis, written interpretation and final graphics must be the student's own. Permitted use must be disclosed in an appendix after the references, identifying the tool and version, publisher, URL, context and reflection. Consult the [Assessments page](https://moodle.lse.ac.uk/course/section.php?id=347353) for the full requirements.
