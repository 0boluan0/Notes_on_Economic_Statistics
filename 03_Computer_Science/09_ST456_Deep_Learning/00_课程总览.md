# Deep Learning · 深度学习
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A connected reading path through deep learning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

深度学习从一个具体问题出发：怎样用数据调整一个函数，使它对新输入作出有用的预测？先理解模型、损失、优化与泛化，再研究适合不同数据的网络结构，最后扩展到生成与决策。这里以 **Understanding Deep Learning（UDL）** 组织基础阅读；课堂范围和考核要求仍由 ST456 当期材料确定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deep learning starts with a concrete question: how can data be used to adjust a function so that it makes useful predictions for new inputs? Begin with models, losses, optimization, and generalization; then study architectures for different data and extend these ideas to generation and decision-making. This reading path uses **Understanding Deep Learning (UDL)**, while current ST456 materials determine classroom scope and assessment requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从数据到函数：第 1–4 章
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From data to functions: Chapters 1–4*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从第 1 章识别学习任务，再在第 2 章用直线拟合理解输入、输出、参数、训练与推断。直线表达能力有限，因此第 3 章加入非线性激活，第 4 章再把多个计算层复合起来。读这部分时，每个公式都应能回答：输入是什么、哪些量可以调整、输出怎样算出来？矩阵记号用于压缩计算，先逐项算一个小例子再写成矩阵。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify learning tasks in Chapter 1 and use line fitting in Chapter 2 to distinguish inputs, outputs, parameters, training, and inference. Chapter 3 adds nonlinear activations, and Chapter 4 composes layers. For each formula, identify its inputs, adjustable quantities, and output calculation. Matrix notation compresses these operations; work through a small example element by element first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从预测到目标：第 5 章
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From predictions to objectives: Chapter 5*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有了网络，还需要定义怎样的预测算好。第 5 章把输出看成条件分布，再用最大似然构造损失函数。先理解“已知输入以后，输出有哪些可能”，并区分连续变量的密度与概率。下面的条件概率原子解释离散事件中的条件化；读连续输出时，还要结合附录 C 的密度与分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once a network is defined, its predictions need an objective. Chapter 5 models conditional output distributions and derives losses through maximum likelihood. Begin by asking which outputs remain possible after observing the input, distinguishing density from probability for continuous variables. The atom below explains conditioning for events; use Appendix C to extend this to continuous densities and distributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从目标到训练：第 6–7 章
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From objectives to training: Chapters 6–7*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第 6 章讨论怎样利用梯度更新参数；第 7 章解释怎样高效求出梯度以及为什么初始化影响训练。先固定其他参数，理解只改变一个参数时损失如何变化，再把网络中的多层变化连接起来。以下两个原子分别提供偏导数和一元链式法则；真正的网络计算图还需要处理多条路径的贡献求和，并保持矩阵尺寸一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chapter 6 uses gradients to update parameters; Chapter 7 explains efficient gradient computation and the role of initialization. First hold other parameters fixed and examine how the loss changes with one parameter, then connect changes across layers. The following atoms provide partial derivatives and the single-variable chain rule. Full computational graphs additionally require summing contributions across paths and maintaining consistent matrix dimensions.

## 从拟合到泛化：第 8–9 章
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From fitting to generalization: Chapters 8–9*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

损失下降只说明模型更符合当前训练目标。第 8 章用独立数据检验泛化，第 9 章讨论正则化等改进办法。将训练、验证与测试各自的用途说清楚，再比较模型；把数据划分、评价方法和改动理由一起写进小实验，才能解释改进是否可信。完成这里，就能把一个小网络的整个训练流程讲完整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A falling loss establishes better fit to the training objective. Chapter 8 evaluates generalization using separate data, and Chapter 9 introduces regularization and related improvements. Define the roles of training, validation, and test data before comparing models. Record the split, evaluation method, and reason for each change so improvements can be assessed. This completes the basic training workflow for a small network.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从通用网络到数据结构：第 10–13 章
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From generic networks to data structure: Chapters 10–13*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图像具有空间结构，文本需要上下文，图数据带有邻接关系。依次学习 CNN、残差连接、Transformer 与 GNN，始终追问：结构利用了数据的哪种规律，信息怎样流动，哪些参数被共享？这些架构沿用前面的损失与优化思想，不必为每一种名称重新建立一套学习框架。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Images have spatial structure, text needs context, and graphs encode adjacency. Study CNNs, residual connections, Transformers, and GNNs by asking which data properties each exploits, how information flows, and which parameters are shared. These architectures build on the earlier account of losses and optimization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从预测到生成与决策：第 14–19 章
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From prediction to generation and decision-making: Chapters 14–19*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第 14 章先建立生成任务与评估的地图，再比较 GAN、normalizing flow、VAE 与 diffusion 如何从简单随机变量产生复杂数据。每一种方法都要分别解释生成过程、训练目标和可计算的量。VAE 的潜变量与 ELBO 是本书进入 diffusion 的关键衔接。第 19 章另开决策分支：动作影响未来数据与回报，需要重新解释反馈和评价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chapter 14 introduces generative tasks and evaluation before comparing how GANs, normalizing flows, VAEs, and diffusion models turn simple randomness into complex data. Distinguish generation, training objectives, and tractable computations for each family. Latent variables and the ELBO in the VAE chapter prepare the book’s treatment of diffusion. Chapter 19 develops decision-making, where actions influence future observations and rewards.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 回看解释与责任：第 20–21 章
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Revisiting explanation and responsibility: Chapters 20–21*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用第 20 章检查哪些认识来自实验、哪些结论有特定条件、哪些机制仍未充分解释。用第 21 章把优化目标与现实后果联系起来。伦理案例可以穿插到前面的分类、生成和决策练习中；读完整本书后的目标，是能够解释方法、检验结果，也能够说清证据的边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use Chapter 20 to distinguish empirical observations, conditional results, and incompletely explained mechanisms. Chapter 21 connects optimization objectives with real consequences. Ethical cases can accompany earlier classification, generation, and decision exercises. The eventual aim is to explain methods, test results, and state the boundaries of the evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
