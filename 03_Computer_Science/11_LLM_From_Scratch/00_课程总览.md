## 3 Attention scores and weights
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*注意力分数与权重*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The input vector at each position produces a query, a key and a value. For one query, its dot products with the visible keys form a row of raw scores. Scaling and softmax turn this row into the weights used to read the corresponding values. The experiment below isolates this score-to-weight step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个位置的输入向量生成查询（query）、键（key）和值（value）。固定一个查询，它与各个可见键的点积构成一行原始分数。缩放（scaling）与 softmax 把这行分数变成读取各个值的份额。下面的实验专门观察“分数变成权重”这一步。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### 3.4 Exploring square-root scaling and softmax
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拖动观察根号维度缩放与 softmax*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each pair of bars compares the same candidate with and without scaling. The four weights in each series sum to one. Here, d is the query/key width within one attention head. The four sliders represent four candidate positions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个位置的一对条形对比“直接 softmax”和“先除以 √d 再 softmax”。每组四个位置的权重分别合计为 1。这里的 d 是一个注意力头（attention head）内查询与键的向量维度，四个分数滑块分别代表四个候选位置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start in the fixed-score mode. Set A to −8, B to 0.5, and d to 32; leave C and D fixed. Compare B's weight with A's weight. Then change d while keeping all four scores fixed. At d=1, the two sets of bars coincide. Larger divisors make the weight ratios closer to one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先选“固定点积，只改变除数”。把 A 设为 −8、B 设为 0.5、d 设为 32，并保持 C、D 的分数不动。观察 B 的权重是 A 的多少倍，再只拖动 d。d=1 时，两组条形重合；除数增大时，各位置的权重比更接近 1。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let r be the raw dot-product scores and a the attention weights. The shared denominator cancels when comparing any two visible candidates, so their weight ratio depends on their score difference. The formulas below apply to finite scores and positive d.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 r 表示原始点积分数，a 表示注意力权重。比较两个可见候选时，共同分母可以约掉，所以权重比取决于分数差。下面假设分数有限且 d 为正。

$$
a_j=\frac{\exp(r_j/\sqrt d)}{\sum_\ell\exp(r_\ell/\sqrt d)},
\qquad
\frac{a_B}{a_A}=\exp\!\left(\frac{r_B-r_A}{\sqrt d}\right).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For A=−8 and B=0.5, the raw score gap is 8.5. Direct softmax gives a weight ratio of about 4,915. Dividing the scores by √32 before softmax reduces that ratio to about 4.49. These pairwise ratios hold for any finite scores at C and D; the individual percentages also depend on those other candidates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

A=−8、B=0.5 时，原始分数相差 8.5。直接 softmax 后，B 的权重约为 A 的 4,915 倍；先除以 √32，再 softmax，这个比值约为 4.49。只要 C、D 的分数有限，B/A 的比值就满足下面的公式；每项具体占百分之多少，还取决于 C、D。

$$
\text{Direct softmax}\quad \frac{p_B}{p_A}=e^{8.5}\approx4914.77,
\qquad
\text{Scaled softmax}\quad \frac{a_B}{a_A}=e^{8.5/\sqrt{32}}\approx4.49.
$$

> [!info]- Reference · 从分数差推到权重比
> ![[00_Knowledge/Softmax#从分数到权重]]

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding the same constant to every score preserves every score difference and therefore preserves the softmax distribution. Dividing all scores by √d scales each difference by 1/√d. A bar displayed as 0.00% may still have a small positive weight. With finite inputs, mathematical softmax is strictly positive; limited display precision can round small values to zero, and floating-point arithmetic can underflow at more extreme scales.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给所有分数加同一个常数，会保留每一对分数的差，因此 softmax 分布保持相同。把全部分数除以 √d，会把每一对分数差也乘以 1/√d。界面显示为 0.00% 的条形仍可能对应一个很小的正权重。有限输入的数学 softmax 各项严格为正；显示精度会把小数四舍五入为零，更极端的计算还可能出现浮点下溢（floating-point underflow）。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The dimension-growth mode multiplies the baseline scores by √(d/64). Division by √d then cancels this chosen amplitude growth exactly. This mode illustrates the scaling idea. In an actual model, changing the vector dimension also changes the dot products. Under mutually independent, zero-mean, unit-variance query/key components, the dot-product variance is d and its standard deviation is √d. The linked proposition states and derives these assumptions explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“模拟维度增长”模式把基准分数乘以 √(d/64)，随后除以 √d，恰好抵消这个预设的幅度增长。真实模型改变向量维度时，点积也会随之改变。在查询与键各分量相互独立、均值为 0、方差为 1 的假设下，点积的方差为 d，标准差为 √d。具体条件和推导见[[00_Knowledge/点积缩放|点积缩放]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The optional gradient panel shows the derivative of A's weight with respect to A's raw score. Scaling contributes its own factor 1/√d through the chain rule. Compare the displayed derivatives directly; their relative sizes depend on the scores and d.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可展开的梯度（gradient）区域显示“A 的权重对 A 的原始分数的导数”。缩放会通过链式法则（chain rule）额外带来一个 1/√d 因子。两种导数的大小关系取决于分数和 d，可以直接比较界面读数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Vaswani et al., §3.2.1 and its variance footnote, support the scaling formula and its variance motivation. Blanchard, Higham and Higham, §1 equation (1.2), gives the softmax definition; §2 gives its derivatives. The ratios and shift property above follow by algebra, and the numerical example was independently recomputed. The interactive controls compare one query and four visible candidates using explicit scores.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

来源与核验：[Vaswani et al., §3.2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1706.03762v7#S3.SS2.SSS1) 及方差脚注支持缩放公式和方差动机；[Blanchard, Higham & Higham, §1 式 (1.2) 与 §2](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~dhigham/Publications/P149.pdf) 支持 softmax 定义与导数。上述权重比和平移性质由代数推导，数值例子已独立复算。交互实验使用明确给定的分数，比较一个查询对四个可见候选的权重。
