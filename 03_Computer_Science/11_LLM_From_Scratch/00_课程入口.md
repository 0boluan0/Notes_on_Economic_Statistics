# LLMs From Scratch · 从零构建大语言模型

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The main course is **Master and Build Large Language Models**, led by Sebastian Raschka, with prerequisite lessons by Abhinav Kimothi. It accompanies Raschka's **Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)**. Follow the book's chapter order and use the corresponding videos, original notebooks, and exercises together. The publisher lists 17 hours 15 minutes of video; study and practice require additional time.

## Bridge to the papers · 接到论文所需的补充

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first course covers model construction, pretraining, and supervised finetuning. Reinforcement learning needs an additional source. Continue with the relevant chapters of Raschka's **Build a Reasoning Model (From Scratch)**: establish pretrained generation and evaluation from Chapters 2–3, use the sampling and log-probability material needed from Chapters 4–5, then study Chapters 6–7 on reinforcement learning and GRPO. PPO and paper-specific mathematics are checked separately when required by the assigned papers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一套课覆盖模型构建、预训练与监督微调；强化学习（reinforcement learning, RL）需要继续补。接同作者 **Build a Reasoning Model (From Scratch)**：先承接第 2–3 章的已有模型生成与评估，补第 4–5 章中后续所需的采样（sampling）和对数概率（log-probability），再学第 6–7 章的强化学习与组相对策略优化（group relative policy optimization, GRPO）。近端策略优化（proximal policy optimization, PPO）及论文自己的数学论证按实际需要核对。
