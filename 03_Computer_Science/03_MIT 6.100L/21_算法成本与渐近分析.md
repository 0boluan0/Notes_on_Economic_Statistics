## 1. 先把一句含糊的“快”变成可分析的问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*1. Turn “fast” into a question that can be analyzed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[算法复杂度]]描述指定条件下的资源消耗。以列表查找为例，我们可以问：长度为 $n$ 的列表中，为了判断目标是否存在，最多要做多少次相等比较？这句话固定了任务、[[输入规模]]、资源单位与[[最坏情况复杂度|输入情形]]，已经比“这个算法是 $O(n)$”完整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[算法复杂度|Algorithmic complexity]] describes resource use under specified conditions. For list search, ask: on a list of length $n$, what is the maximum number of equality comparisons needed to decide whether a target exists? This fixes the task, [[输入规模|size]], resource unit and [[最坏情况复杂度|input case]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

规模不一定是一个整数的数值。扫描列表时用长度；处理大整数时，编码它的位数也可能才是输入长度。若循环从 $1$ 数到整数 $N\ge1$，它有 $N$ 轮；而 $N$ 的二进制长度只有 $\lfloor\log_2N\rfloor+1$。关于数值 $N$ 线性，不等于关于编码长度线性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Size need not mean numerical value. A list has a length; a large integer has a representation length. Counting from one to integer $N\ge1$ takes $N$ iterations, while $N$ uses only $\lfloor\log_2N\rfloor+1$ binary digits. Linear growth in the value is not linear growth in the encoding length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着规定[[算法成本模型]]。课程常把一次算术、比较、索引和赋值算作常数成本。这个约定适合先看程序结构，但不允许把任意长整数乘法、长度不断增长的字符串比较或列表切片当作免费的一步。后面每个时间界都沿用明确的单位成本；改变模型，就重新计费。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next choose the [[算法成本模型|cost model]]. The course often gives arithmetic, comparison, indexing and assignment constant cost. This is useful for exposing structure, but does not make arbitrary-length multiplication, growing-string comparison or slicing free. The bounds below use specified unit costs; a different model requires new accounting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 2. 先得到成本函数，再选择要说多强的界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*2. Obtain a cost function before choosing the strength of a bound*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设计步得到 $T(n)=3n^2+20n+1$。对所有 $n\ge1$，都有 $3n^2\le T(n)\le24n^2$。左边是常数倍下界，右边是常数倍上界，所以 $T(n)=\Theta(n^2)$。这里的 $3,24$ 一次选定，不能随着 $n$ 改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose counting gives $T(n)=3n^2+20n+1$. For every $n\ge1$, $3n^2\le T(n)\le24n^2$, so $T(n)=\Theta(n^2)$. The constants three and twenty-four are chosen once; they cannot vary with $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[大O记号]]只要求最终上界，因此这个函数也属于 $O(n^3)$；[[大Ω记号]]只要求最终下界；[[大Θ记号]]要求同时夹在同一比较函数的两个正常数倍之间。**O 不是最坏情况的缩写，Ω 也不是最好情况的缩写。** 它们描述的是已经选定的函数；同一个最坏成本函数也可以有上界、下界和紧界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[大O记号|Big O]] requires only an eventual upper bound, so this function is also $O(n^3)$. [[大Ω记号|Big Omega]] requires a lower bound; [[大Θ记号|big Theta]] requires both. **O does not mean worst case, and Omega does not mean best case.** They bound an already selected function; a worst-case cost function can have upper, lower and tight bounds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若还要判断“相对误差是否消失”，那是[[渐近等价]]的问题。$T(n)\sim3n^2$，但不与 $n^2$ 渐近等价，因为比值趋于 $3$ 而非 $1$。算法增长分类常用 Θ；估计一个计数的相对误差时则可能需要更强的 $\sim$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether relative error vanishes is the separate question of [[渐近等价|asymptotic equivalence]]. Here $T(n)\sim3n^2$, but $T(n)\not\sim n^2$, since the ratio tends to three rather than one. Theta often suffices for growth classes; relative-error estimates may need the stronger equivalence relation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 3. 循环不是看层数，而是加总每次真正做的工作
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*3. Count executed work, not just loop levels*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面每次 `count += 1` 按常数成本计，输入为非负整数 $n$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n$ be a nonnegative integer and charge constant cost for each increment:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

```python
count = 0
for i in range(n):
    for j in range(i):
        count += 1
```

总增量是 $\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}i=n(n-1)/2$，所以在 $n\to\infty$ 时是 $\Theta(n^2)$。这就是[[循环成本求和]]：某轮只有零次，另一轮有 $n-1$ 次，但总和能准确说明增长。若内层永远只遍历十个数字，总和才是 $10n=\Theta(n)$；两层循环不保证平方阶。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The increments total $\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}i=n(n-1)/2$, giving $\Theta(n^2)$ as $n\to\infty$. This is [[循环成本求和|summing loop costs]]: individual rounds differ, but their sum determines growth. If the inner loop always scans only ten digits, the total is $10n=\Theta(n)$ instead. Two nested loops do not guarantee quadratic cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

顺序执行两段循环就相加，矩形嵌套且每轮内层同样长才可直接相乘。两个列表分别长 $n,m$ 时，完整比较所有元素对要 $nm$ 次；没有“两表等长”的条件，不应把它改写成 $n^2$。提前退出还要求你确认最坏输入究竟怎样让退出尽可能晚。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sequential loops add. Rectangular nesting multiplies when every inner scan has the same length. Comparing every pair from lists of lengths $n,m$ takes $nm$ comparisons, not $n^2$ unless equal lengths are assumed. With early exits, identify an input that postpones termination before claiming a worst-case lower bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一种常见循环是 `while m > 0: m //= 2`。从正整数 $n$ 开始，[[减半迭代次数]]恰为 $\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor+1$。它先说明轮数；每轮若又复制长列表，总时间仍要把复制算进去。对连续求根，精度所需轮数应回到[[二分误差界]]的误差与计数约定，不能把离散索引公式原样套用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Starting from positive integer $n$, `while m > 0: m //= 2` runs exactly $\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor+1$ times, as shown in [[减半迭代次数|halving iteration counts]]. This counts rounds first; copying a long list in each round adds further cost. For continuous root-finding, use the error and indexing conventions of [[二分误差界|the bisection error bound]] instead of transferring a discrete formula unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 4. 二分搜索把“轮数”和“每轮成本”分得最清楚
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*4. Binary search separates round count from cost per round*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有序随机访问数组上的[[二分查找]]每次用中点比较排除约一半候选。若只更新索引，每轮其他工作是常数，最坏时间为 $\Theta(\log n)$；迭代实现还能只用常数个索引变量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[二分查找|Binary search]] on a sorted random-access array removes about half the candidates per comparison. Updating only indices gives constant additional work per round and $\Theta(\log n)$ worst-case time. An iterative implementation also needs only a constant number of index variables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但 `bisect_search1` 用切片建立下一层子列表。[[切片二分成本]]给出总复制量 $n/2+n/4+\cdots=\Theta(n)$，因此时间变成 $\Theta(n)$。递归仍只有对数层，只是第一层复制半个原列表已经需要线性工作。讲义里的逐层粗计数应视作松上界；紧界要按各层实际长度求和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course's `bisect_search1` creates a slice for each recursive level. [[切片二分成本|Slicing cost analysis]] sums $n/2+n/4+\cdots=\Theta(n)$, producing $\Theta(n)$ time. The recursion still has logarithmic depth, but its first slice alone performs linear work. A coarse per-level estimate is only an upper bound; the tight bound sums the actual lengths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[辅助空间]]计算峰值而非累计工作：切片递归沿活跃路径保留的列表总长为线性；索引递归不复制列表，但有对数层栈；迭代索引版才可使用常数辅助空间。这里要分别报告时间、栈以及保留的数据，而不是只写“空间小”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[辅助空间|Auxiliary space]] measures the peak, not cumulative work. Recursive slicing retains linear total list length along its active path. Index-based recursion avoids copying but keeps a logarithmic stack; iterative index-based search can use constant auxiliary space. Report time, stack and retained data separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 5. 最坏、平均、随机期望和摊还回答四种不同的问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*5. Worst case, average case, randomized expectation and amortization ask different questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定“找到就返回”的线性搜索、长度 $n\ge1$ 与常数成本比较。[[最好情况复杂度]]取同规模输入中最小成本：目标在第一项，只需一次。[[最坏情况复杂度]]取最大成本：目标缺失，检查全部 $n$ 项。这里不能把某几次运行的平均秒数当成其中任何一个定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix early-return linear search, length $n\ge1$, and constant-cost comparisons. [[最好情况复杂度|Best case]] minimizes cost over inputs of that size: a first-position match needs one comparison. [[最坏情况复杂度|Worst case]] maximizes it: an absent target needs $n$. The average seconds from a few measurements define neither of these quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[平均情况复杂度]]需要输入分布。假设目标必定存在，且唯一匹配位置在 $1,\ldots,n$ 上均匀，那么平均比较数为 $(1+\cdots+n)/n=(n+1)/2$；改变缺失概率或位置分布，答案也会改变。[[随机算法期望复杂度]]则可以固定一个输入，只对算法内部的随机选择取期望。两种随机性可以一起出现，但必须分别说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[平均情况复杂度|Average-case complexity]] requires an input distribution. If the target is present and its unique position is uniform over $1,\ldots,n$, expected comparisons are $(1+\cdots+n)/n=(n+1)/2$. Different position or absence probabilities give different answers. [[随机算法期望复杂度|Randomized expected complexity]] can instead fix the input and average only over internal random choices. Both sources can coexist, but must be identified separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[摊还复杂度]]不需要随机性。看一串追加操作：容量不足时把动态数组翻倍，搬移量依次为 $1,2,4,\ldots$。从空结构做到 $N$ 个元素，总搬移量小于 $2N$，再加 $N$ 次写入，总成本线性，故每次摊还常数；触发扩容的那一次仍可能很慢。[[哈希表几何扩容]]复用相同的容量分析，但碰撞处理的期望成本是另外一项条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[摊还复杂度|Amortized complexity]] needs no randomness. In an append sequence, doubling a full array moves $1,2,4,\ldots$ elements. From empty to $N$ elements, moves total less than $2N$; adding $N$ writes gives linear total cost and constant amortized cost per append, although one resizing append can still be expensive. [[哈希表几何扩容|Geometric hash-table resizing]] uses the same capacity argument; expected collision cost needs separate assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 6. 递归的工作可能在调用里，也可能在结果里
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*6. Recursive work can come from calls or from their results*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[递归调用树成本]]要求把所有实际调用的本层工作相加。归并排序产生两个半规模子问题，但每层总工作线性、层数对数，所以[[归并排序复杂度]]是 $\Theta(n\log n)$；这依赖[[分治成本模型]]中的子问题规模与合并成本。只看到“两次递归”不能推出指数级。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[递归调用树成本|Call-tree analysis]] adds the local work of every executed call. Merge sort creates two half-size subproblems, with linear total work per level and logarithmic depth, giving [[归并排序复杂度|merge-sort time]] $\Theta(n\log n)$. This depends on the subproblem sizes and merge work in the [[分治成本模型|divide-and-conquer model]]. Two recursive calls do not by themselves imply exponential time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，课程中的 `gen_subsets` 每层只递归一次，却返回全部子集。对 $n$ 个互异元素，每个元素出现在一半子集中，显式列表一共要写 $n2^{n-1}$ 个引用。[[显式输出下界]]已迫使指数级工作；计入复制后完整成本为 $\Theta(n2^n)$。这说明“调用树没分叉”也不能推出线性时间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, `gen_subsets` makes one recursive call per level but returns every subset. For $n$ distinct elements, each belongs to half the subsets, so explicit lists write $n2^{n-1}$ references. The [[显式输出下界|output-size lower bound]] already forces exponential work; including copying gives $\Theta(n2^n)$. A nonbranching call tree need not mean linear time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 7. 回到真实程序，选择一个值得比较的工作负载
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*7. Return to a concrete workload when comparing implementations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[计时与渐近分析]]互相补充。$n^2$ 和 $1000n\log_2n$ 的渐近阶不同，但仅凭阶数不能确定在某个小 $n$ 上谁更快；还要比较常数、内存分配、缓存和实际输入。计时也应明确是否包含构造输入、排序等预处理，并重复测量同类工作负载。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[计时与渐近分析|Timing and asymptotic analysis]] complement each other. The orders of $n^2$ and $1000n\log_2n$ differ, but orders alone do not rank them at a particular small $n$. Constants, allocation, caches and inputs matter. State whether timings include input construction or sorting, and repeat measurements on comparable workloads.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若固定数据上有 $K$ 次查找，比较的就不再是单次查找。设列表长度 $n\ge2$、$K\ge1$，期间不更新数据；比较与随机访问按常数成本计。使用标准归并排序，再用不切片的索引二分，最坏总成本为 $\Theta(n\log n+K\log n)$；反复线性扫描的最坏总成本为 $\Theta(Kn)$，重复查询缺失目标可达到这些搜索下界。这不表示每组查询都耗费同样多：可在中点提前返回的二分版若每次首次命中，查询部分只需 $\Theta(K)$。数据更新或实现改变时须重新分析。进一步选排序或哈希时，沿用[[排序复杂度口径]]与[[哈希表期望查找复杂度]]中的任务、数据分布和资源条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $K$ queries on fixed data, compare the complete workload. Let the list have length $n\ge2$, with $K\ge1$, no updates, and constant-cost comparisons and random access. Standard merge sort followed by index-based binary search without slices has worst-case total cost $\Theta(n\log n+K\log n)$; repeated linear scans have worst-case total cost $\Theta(Kn)$. Repeated absent-target queries attain these search lower bounds. Not every query sequence costs this much: a binary-search version that can return at the midpoint needs only $\Theta(K)$ query work when every first comparison succeeds. Updates or implementation changes require fresh analysis. Follow the task, distribution and resource conditions in [[排序复杂度口径|sorting complexity conventions]] and [[哈希表期望查找复杂度|expected hash-table lookup costs]] when choosing a structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> 1. 两层循环为什么既可能线性，也可能平方？
> 2. 二分递归只有对数层，为什么切片实现仍需线性时间？
> 3. 一次扩容耗时线性，为什么不反驳摊还常数？
>
> **核对：** 先逐轮求和；再把复制计入每层成本；最后分清单次最坏成本与整个操作序列的总成本。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Check three ideas: why two loops can be linear or quadratic; why logarithmic-depth binary search can take linear copying time; and why a linear-cost resize does not refute constant amortized cost. Sum actual work, charge representation operations, and distinguish one operation from a full sequence.
