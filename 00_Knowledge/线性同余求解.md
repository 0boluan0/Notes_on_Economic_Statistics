# Solving a linear congruence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*线性同余求解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation $6x\equiv8\pmod{14}$ has more than one answer class: six and thirteen both work. Replacing eight by seven makes it unsolvable. The goal is to decide which situation occurs and then describe **every** integer solution, not merely find one successful substitution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方程 $6x\equiv8\pmod{14}$ 不止一个解类：六和十三都可用；把八换成七，方程却无解。这里要解决的是：先判断能否求解，再描述**所有**整数解，而不只是找到一次代入成功。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a [[线性同余方程|linear congruence]], integers $a,b$ and an integer modulus $n>1$ are fixed, while $x$ is unknown. By [[同余|congruence]], $ax\equiv b\pmod n$ means that $n$ divides $ax-b$. Write $d=\gcd(a,n)$ for the [[最大公因数|greatest common divisor]]. Since $n>0$, we have $1\le d\le n$, even when $a$ is zero or negative. A solution class groups solutions differing by a multiple of the original modulus $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[线性同余方程]]中，整数 $a,b$ 和整数模数 $n>1$ 固定，$x$ 是未知整数。按[[同余]]定义，$ax\equiv b\pmod n$ 表示 $n$ 整除 $ax-b$。令 $d=\gcd(a,n)$ 为[[最大公因数]]；因为 $n>0$，总有 $1\le d\le n$，即使 $a$ 为零或负数也一样。一个解类把相差原模数 $n$ 的整数倍的解视为同一类。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Existence is decided by one divisibility test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一次整除检查判定是否有解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the [[线性同余方程|linear congruence]] $ax\equiv b\pmod n$, let $a,b\in\mathbb Z$, let $n>1$ be an integer, and set $d=\gcd(a,n)$. A solution exists exactly when $d\mid b$; when solvable, it has exactly $d$ distinct solution classes modulo $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 [[线性同余方程]] $ax\equiv b\pmod n$，设 $a,b\in\mathbb Z$、整数 $n>1$，并令 $d=\gcd(a,n)$。方程有解当且仅当 $d\mid b$；可解时，恰有 $d$ 个不同的模 $n$ 解类。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Check solvability first.** The congruence is equivalent to $ax+ny=b$ for some integers $x,y$. The left-hand side is divisible by $d$, so $d\mid b$ is necessary. Conversely, [[Bézout等式|Bézout's identity]] gives $sa+tn=d$; if $b=dk$, multiplying by $k$ produces a solution $x=sk,y=tk$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**先判可解性。** 同余等价于 $ax+ny=b$ 对某些整数 $x,y$ 成立。左边总被 $d$ 整除，故 $d\mid b$ 必要；反之，[[Bézout等式]] 给出 $sa+tn=d$，当 $b=dk$ 时乘以 $k$ 即得一组解 $x=sk,y=tk$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $ny$ is the signed multiple of $n$ added to $ax$ to obtain $b$. Necessity follows because $d$ divides both terms of $ax+ny$. For sufficiency, $d\mid b$ makes $k=b/d$ an integer. Multiplying the independently proved Bézout identity by $k$ gives $a(sk)+n(tk)=b$, so $x_*=sk$ is an actual integer solution. No inverse of $a$ modulo $n$ is assumed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 $ny$ 是加到 $ax$ 上以得到 $b$ 的带符号的 $n$ 的整数倍。必要性来自 $d$ 同时整除 $ax+ny$ 的两项。充分性中，$d\mid b$ 保证 $k=b/d$ 为整数；把已独立证明的 Bézout 等式乘以 $k$，得到 $a(sk)+n(tk)=b$，因此 $x_*=sk$ 确实是整数解，没有预先假设 $a$ 在原模数下有逆元。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reduce the equation, then restore the original classes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先缩小方程，再恢复原来的解类*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $d<n$ and $d\mid b$, divide the coefficient, target and modulus by the same positive integer $d$. The reduced coefficient and modulus are coprime: if an integer $h>1$ divided both $a/d$ and $n/d$, then $dh>d$ would be a common divisor of $a,n$, contradicting the definition of $d$. Thus the [[模逆元存在条件|inverse-existence criterion]] applies at the reduced modulus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $d<n$ 且 $d\mid b$ 时，把系数、目标和模数同时除以正整数 $d$。约除后的系数与模数互素：若某个整数 $h>1$ 同时整除 $a/d,n/d$，那么 $dh>d$ 就是 $a,n$ 的公因数，与 $d$ 的定义矛盾。因此可以在缩小后的模数上应用[[模逆元存在条件]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Then find every solution.** If $d\mid b$ and $d<n$, let $a'=a/d$, $b'=b/d$ and $n'=n/d>1$. The original congruence is equivalent to $a'x\equiv b'\pmod{n'}$. Since $\gcd(a',n')=1$, choose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**再求全部解。** 在 $d\mid b$ 且 $d<n$ 时，令 $a'=a/d$、$b'=b/d$、$n'=n/d>1$。原式等价于 $a'x\equiv b'\pmod{n'}$；因为 $\gcd(a',n')=1$，可取

$$
x_0=\operatorname{rem}\!\left((a')^{-1}b',n'\right).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All integer solutions are $x=x_0+kn'$ for $k\in\mathbb Z$. As classes modulo the original modulus $n$, they are exactly
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有整数解是 $x=x_0+kn'$（$k\in\mathbb Z$）。按原模数 $n$ 分类，恰好为

$$
x\equiv x_0+t\frac nd\pmod n,
\qquad t=0,1,\ldots,d-1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Completeness follows from the [[同余消去律|cancellation law]]: the difference between any solution and $x_0$ is divisible by $n/d$. The listed $d$ representatives are distinct modulo $n$, because two are congruent exactly when $d\mid(t_1-t_2)$; within the stated range, this requires $t_1=t_2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完整性来自 [[同余消去律]]：任一解与 $x_0$ 的差都被 $n/d$ 整除。上面 $d$ 个代表互不重复，因为两个代表模 $n$ 相同等价于 $d\mid(t_1-t_2)$；在这个取值范围内只能有 $t_1=t_2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $\operatorname{rem}(z,n')$ above denotes the standard remainder of $z$ in $0,\ldots,n'-1$; here $n'$ means $n/d$. In the displayed formula, $(a')^{-1}$ is a [[模逆元|modular inverse]] modulo $n'$, not a real reciprocal. Dividing the integer equality $ax-b=qn$ by $d$ proves the reduced congruence is equivalent to the original one. Multiplying back by $d$ proves the reverse direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上式的 $\operatorname{rem}(z,n')$ 表示 $z$ 在 $0,\ldots,n'-1$ 中的标准余数，这里 $n'$ 指 $n/d$。公式中的 $(a')^{-1}$ 是模 $n'$ 的[[模逆元]]，不是实数倒数。把整数等式 $ax-b=qn$ 除以 $d$，就得到缩小后的同余；再乘回 $d$ 可得反方向，因此约除前后等价。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why are there no missing integer solutions? Subtract the equation for a known solution $x_0$ from the equation for any other solution $x$. This gives $n\mid a(x-x_0)$. The exact cancellation rule gives $n/d\mid(x-x_0)$, so $x=x_0+k(n/d)$ for an integer $k$. Conversely, every such integer works because $a\,k(n/d)=(a/d)kn$ is a multiple of $n$. This proves both inclusion directions for the whole solution family.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么没有遗漏其他整数解？把已知解 $x_0$ 的式子从另一个解 $x$ 的式子中减去，得到 $n\mid a(x-x_0)$。准确的消去律给出 $n/d\mid(x-x_0)$，所以存在整数 $k$ 使 $x=x_0+k(n/d)$。反过来，任意这种整数都成立，因为 $a\,k(n/d)=(a/d)kn$ 是 $n$ 的整数倍。这证明了解族的两个包含方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To count classes modulo $n$, use [[带余除法|division with remainder]] to divide the integer $k$ by the positive integer $d$: write $k=qd+t$ with $0\le t<d$. Then $x=x_0+qn+t(n/d)$, so every solution belongs to one of the $d$ displayed classes. Two listed classes coincide only if $n$ divides $(t_1-t_2)n/d$, equivalently $d\mid(t_1-t_2)$. Since $|t_1-t_2|<d$, that forces $t_1=t_2$. The finite count is therefore exact, while each class still contains infinitely many integers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了按原模数 $n$ 计数，对整数 $k$ 作正除数 $d$ 的[[带余除法]]，写成 $k=qd+t$，其中 $0\le t<d$。于是 $x=x_0+qn+t(n/d)$，每个解都属于所列的 $d$ 个类之一。两类重合恰当 $n$ 整除 $(t_1-t_2)n/d$，等价于 $d\mid(t_1-t_2)$；因为 $|t_1-t_2|<d$，只能有 $t_1=t_2$。所以有限的类数确实为 $d$，而每个类中仍有无穷多个整数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two classes modulo fourteen
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*模十四的两个解类*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $6x\equiv8\pmod{14}$, we have $d=2\mid8$, and division gives $3x\equiv4\pmod7$. Since $3^{-1}\equiv5\pmod7$, $x_0\equiv20\equiv6\pmod7$. The complete list modulo fourteen is $x\equiv6,13\pmod{14}$; substitution gives remainder eight in both cases. Replacing the right-hand side by seven makes the congruence unsolvable because $2\nmid7$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $6x\equiv8\pmod{14}$，$d=2\mid8$，约除得到 $3x\equiv4\pmod7$。$3^{-1}\equiv5\pmod7$，所以 $x_0\equiv20\equiv6\pmod7$，全部模 14 解类为 $x\equiv6,13\pmod{14}$；代入分别得到余数 8。若把右边改成 7，则 $2\nmid7$，立即无解。

> [!example] One reduced class, two original classes · 缩模一类，原模两类
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The reduced answer $x\equiv6\pmod7$ means $x=6+7k$. Splitting $k$ into even and odd values gives $x=6+14q$ or $x=13+14q$. Direct substitution yields $6\cdot6=36=2\cdot14+8$ and $6\cdot13=78=5\cdot14+8$. Listing only six modulo fourteen would lose the second class.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 缩模后的答案 $x\equiv6\pmod7$ 表示 $x=6+7k$。把 $k$ 分为偶数与奇数，便得到 $x=6+14q$ 或 $x=13+14q$。代回核验为 $6\cdot6=36=2\cdot14+8$、$6\cdot13=78=5\cdot14+8$。若只报“模十四等于六”，就遗漏了第二个类。

## Zero coefficients, signs and the reduced modulus one
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零系数、符号与缩模为一*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The degenerate case $d=n$ means $n\mid a$, including $a=0$. If $n\mid b$, every integer is a solution, giving $n=d$ classes modulo $n$; otherwise there is no solution. No inverse modulo one is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

退化情形 $d=n$ 意味着 $n\mid a$（包含 $a=0$）。此时若 $n\mid b$，所有整数都是解，共有 $n=d$ 个模 $n$ 解类；否则无解。不需要为模数 1 求逆元。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indeed, if $n\mid a$, the left side is zero modulo $n$ for every $x$. For $0x\equiv0\pmod5$, all five classes occur; for $0x\equiv1\pmod5$, none occur. If solvable, choose $x_0=0$: the general family with step $n/d=1$ is all integers. The reduced condition is simply $1\mid(x-x_0)$, not a request to apply the inverse definition at modulus one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

确实，若 $n\mid a$，则对每个 $x$，左边都模 $n$ 余零。对 $0x\equiv0\pmod5$，五个类全是解；对 $0x\equiv1\pmod5$，一个也不是。有解时可取 $x_0=0$，通式的步长 $n/d=1$ 就产生所有整数；缩模后的条件仅为 $1\mid(x-x_0)$，并不是要求把逆元定义套到模一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Negative coefficients and targets are allowed. For example, $-6x\equiv-8\pmod{14}$ is equivalent to the earlier example after multiplying both sides by $-1$, so it has the same two classes. When $d=1$, there is exactly one class modulo the original $n$; when $d>1$, solvability gives multiple classes, not a unique integer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数与目标允许为负。例如 $-6x\equiv-8\pmod{14}$ 两边乘以 $-1$ 就回到前例，仍有同样两个类。当 $d=1$ 时，原模数 $n$ 下恰有一个解类；当 $d>1$ 时，有解就产生多个解类，而不是唯一整数。
