# Evaluating a limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*极限计算流程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A limit calculation asks how **nearby** outputs behave, so a failed substitution is a clue about the next step, not the final answer. A useful process is to establish the allowed inputs, diagnose the expression, then choose an argument that preserves the same approach. This keeps simple algebra effective while making the assumptions of more powerful tools visible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极限计算问的是**附近**输出怎样变化，所以代入失败只是下一步的线索，并不是最终答案。一个实用过程是：先确定合法输入与趋近方式，再诊断表达式，最后选择保持同一趋近问题的论证。这既能用好简单代数，也能看清较强工具所需的条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)$ means the [[函数极限|limit of a function]] as inputs approach $a$ through its domain, excluding $a$ itself. Before manipulating a formula, identify where denominators are nonzero and roots or logarithms are defined. Also read the direction: $x\to a^+$ means from the right, $x\to a^-$ from the left. A domain endpoint may allow only one side.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记号 $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)$ 表示沿定义域内、不同于 $a$ 的输入趋于 $a$ 时的[[函数极限]]。动手变形前，先看分母哪里非零，根号与对数哪里有定义；还要读清方向：$x\to a^+$ 从右侧，$x\to a^-$ 从左侧。定义域端点可能只允许一侧趋近。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A decision sequence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 判断顺序

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First try substitution as a **diagnostic**. If the component functions are continuous at the approached point and every operation remains valid, the [[极限定律适用条件|limit laws]] justify the resulting number. For example, at $x\to3$, $(x^2+x)/(x+1)$ gives $12/4=3$, with a nonzero limiting denominator. The same arithmetic would not justify substitution into a discontinuous piecewise definition.

If both numerator and denominator tend to zero, record the [[零比零未定式|$0/0$ indeterminate form]] and look for structure that can be exposed. If a denominator tends to zero while the numerator does not, examine signs and the allowed direction; do not treat division by zero as a numerical operation. If two formulas apply on opposite sides, evaluate the two approaches separately and use [[双侧极限判定|the two-sided criterion]] to compare the answers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把代入当作**诊断**。若组成函数在目标点连续、每个运算都仍有定义，就能由[[极限定律适用条件]]确认所得数值。例如 $x\to3$ 时，$(x^2+x)/(x+1)$ 得到 $12/4=3$，分母极限非零。但不能用同样的算术，替代对不连续分段定义的分析。

若分子分母都趋于零，就记录为[[零比零未定式|$0/0$ 未定式]]，再看结构能否通过变形显露。若分母趋零而分子不趋零，要检查符号与允许的趋近方向，不能把除零当作数值运算。若两侧采用不同表达式，先分别计算，再用[[双侧极限判定]]比较结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Cancel only where the original expression is defined
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*在原式有定义的输入处约分*

> [!example] A common vanishing factor · 共同趋零的因子
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To evaluate $\lim_{x\to1}(x^2-1)/(x-1)$, the direct form is $0/0$. For every $x\ne1$,
> $$\frac{x^2-1}{x-1}=\frac{(x-1)(x+1)}{x-1}=x+1.$$
> Hence the limit is $2$. The equality holds throughout the punctured neighbourhood, meaning the neighbourhood with $1$ removed. It is sufficient because the limit never tests the excluded input itself.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 求 $\lim_{x\to1}(x^2-1)/(x-1)$ 时，直接形式为 $0/0$。对每个 $x\ne1$，上面的因式分解与约分成立，故极限为2。等式在去心邻域内处处成立；去心邻域就是删去目标点1的邻域。这已经足够，因为极限本来就不检查目标点自身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an instance of [[点值不决定极限|local agreement preserving a limit]]. The simplified formula may have a value at the missing point; that is an extension of the original expression, not a justification for dividing by zero there. Likewise, a rewrite must not discard nearby inputs that the original problem requires.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是[[点值不决定极限|附近处处相等便保留极限]]的应用。化简后的式子可以在缺失点有值，但那是原表达式的一种延伸，不是允许在该点除零。类似地，变形不能丢掉原问题需要检查的附近输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use a conjugate to expose a hidden factor
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用共轭式显露隐藏因子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\sqrt{1+h}-1$, the **conjugate expression** is $\sqrt{1+h}+1$; multiplying them gives $(1+h)-1=h$. Thus, for $h\ge-1$ and $h\ne0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\sqrt{1+h}-1$ 的**共轭式**是 $\sqrt{1+h}+1$；两者相乘得到 $(1+h)-1=h$。因此，对 $h\ge-1$ 且 $h\ne0$，

$$
\frac{\sqrt{1+h}-1}{h}
=\frac{h}{h(\sqrt{1+h}+1)}
=\frac1{\sqrt{1+h}+1}
\longrightarrow\frac12\qquad(h\to0).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The new denominator tends to $2$, so the quotient law now applies. The conjugate is positive near zero and introduces no extra division by zero. Here **rationalization** is useful because it exposes the factor that was preventing direct substitution; it is not an instruction to multiply every radical expression by an arbitrary conjugate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

新分母趋于2，现在商法则可以使用。共轭式在零附近为正，不会引入额外除零。这里**有理化**的用处是显露妨碍直接代入的因子；并不是遇到任何根式都要机械地乘一个共轭式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Let the direction and a bound settle the remaining question
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用趋近方向与界完成判断*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $|x|/x$ near zero, cancelling as though $|x|=x$ everywhere would be wrong. The quotient is $1$ for $x>0$ and $-1$ for $x<0$. Thus the right limit is $1$, the left limit is $-1$, and the two-sided limit does not exist. Similarly, $1/x$ tends to opposite infinities on the two sides, while $1/x^2$ tends to $+\infty$ from both sides; the [[无穷极限与无穷远极限|infinite-limit definitions]] keep these claims separate from finite limits.

Some expressions are better controlled by a bound than by cancellation. For $x\sin(1/x)$, the factor $\sin(1/x)$ has no limit at zero, so the product law cannot split it into two limits. Instead,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对零附近的 $|x|/x$，不能把 $|x|$ 到处当成 $x$ 来约分。$x>0$ 时比值为1，$x<0$ 时为 $-1$，因此右极限为1、左极限为 $-1$，双侧极限不存在。类似地，$1/x$ 在两侧趋于相反的无穷，而 $1/x^2$ 从两侧都趋于正无穷；[[无穷极限与无穷远极限]]区分这些表述与有限极限。

有些表达式适合用界控制，不必强行约分。例如 $x\sin(1/x)$ 中，$\sin(1/x)$ 在零处没有极限，所以不能用积法则拆成两个极限。改用

$$-|x|\le x\sin(1/x)\le |x|,$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
both bounding expressions tend to zero, and the [[夹逼定理|squeeze theorem]] gives the limit zero. If algebra and bounds do not resolve a ratio, [[洛必达法则|L'Hôpital's rule]] may help after its differentiability and other hypotheses are checked. Merely obtaining an indeterminate form does not establish those hypotheses.

Finally, state the result with its direction and meaning: a finite number, divergence to a specified infinity, or failure of a limit. Check that every cancellation was legitimate near the target and that the answer uses the original domain. Numerical values can suggest the answer, but cannot certify behaviour at every sufficiently close input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两边都趋于零，[[夹逼定理]]便给出极限零。若代数与上下界仍不能解决某个比值，核对可导性等全部假设后，可以考虑[[洛必达法则]]。单纯得到一个未定式，并没有证明这些假设已经成立。

最后连同趋近方向说明答案的含义：有限数、趋于指定的无穷，或极限不存在。复查每次约分在目标附近是否有效，结果是否仍使用原定义域。数值可以提示答案，却不能保证所有充分接近的输入都符合结论。
