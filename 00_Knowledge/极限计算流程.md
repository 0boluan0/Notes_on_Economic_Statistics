# 极限计算先代入再判断并选择改写
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Evaluate a limit by substituting, diagnosing, and then choosing a rewrite*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子流程
> 先尝试直接代入；若出现 $0/0$ 等未定式，再用因式分解、有理化、共同主导项或标准极限改写。分段点必须分别求左右极限；只有两者存在且相等，双侧极限才存在。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Try direct substitution first. If it produces an indeterminate form such as $0/0$, rewrite by factorisation, rationalisation, common dominant terms, or a standard limit. At a piecewise boundary, compute the one-sided limits separately; the two-sided limit exists only if both exist and agree.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 判断顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Decision sequence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 直接代入，记录得到有限值、确定的无穷行为、未定式，还是左右行为不明。
2. 先选保持原极限的改写：约因子、有理化、提取主导项、变量替换或标准极限。
3. 只有定理的形式和假设都满足时，才使用夹逼定理或洛必达法则。
4. 最后检查符号、定义域、左右方向和数量级。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

**1.** Substitute directly and record whether the result is a finite value, a definite infinite behaviour, an indeterminate form, or unresolved one-sided behaviour.<br>
**2.** Choose a limit-preserving rewrite: cancel a factor, rationalise, extract dominant terms, substitute a variable, or use a standard limit.<br>
**3.** Apply the squeeze theorem or L'Hôpital's rule only when both its form and hypotheses are satisfied.<br>
**4.** Check the sign, domain, one-sided direction, and order of magnitude at the end.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $\lim_{x\to1}(x^2-1)/(x-1)$ 代入得到 $0/0$ 后，下一步最自然的动作是什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After substitution gives $0/0$ in $\lim_{x\to1}(x^2-1)/(x-1)$, what is the most natural next step?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 在 $x\ne1$ 的去心邻域中因式分解并约去 $x-1$，得到 $x+1$，再取极限为 $2$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Factor and cancel $x-1$ on the punctured neighbourhood where $x\ne1$, obtaining $x+1$, whose limit is $2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
