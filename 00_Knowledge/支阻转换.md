# 支阻转换是突破后原支撑或阻力区域被用于相反角色判断的规则

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Support–resistance role reversal treats a breached zone as a candidate reference with the opposite role.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支阻转换是技术分析的一种角色更新规则：价格向下突破原[[支撑位]]后，若从下方重新接近，该区域被考察为候选[[阻力位]]；向上突破原阻力后，若从上方重新接近，则考察为候选支撑。转换的是同一区域相对于当前价格的参考角色。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After price breaks below [[支撑位|support]], that zone is assessed as potential [[阻力位|resistance]] on an approach from below. After a break above resistance, it is assessed as potential support on an approach from above. The same zone changes its reference role relative to current price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如预先标定100附近为阻力，价格按既定标准上破到105，再回到100附近时，可检验它是否起支撑作用。价格也可能不回测，或回测后继续跌穿；“转换”不保证这些后续步骤一定发生。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a predefined resistance zone near 100 is breached to 105, a later return near 100 can test its support role. Price may never return, or may break below on its return; role reversal guarantees neither a retest nor a successful hold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应用前须固定“突破”是盘中越界、收盘越界还是其他确认标准，以及区域宽度和允许回测时间。仅有事后回测成功的图，不能证明该规则在当时可识别；若使用最终收盘，需遵守[[交易信号时序]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify whether a breach means an intraday crossing, a closing-price crossing or another criterion, together with zone width and the allowed retest interval. A successful retrospective chart is not proof of real-time identification. Closing-price rules must respect [[交易信号时序|signal timing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
