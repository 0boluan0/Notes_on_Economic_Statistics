# 广义脉冲响应对排序不变但不完成结构识别
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Generalized impulse responses are ordering-invariant but do not complete structural identification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 解释边界
> 线性 VAR 的 GIRF 对某一简约型创新作条件冲击并平均其同期相关分量，因而不依赖 Cholesky 排序；但它仍是基于简约型创新与给定信息集的响应，不自动获得经济结构冲击的因果身份。

在 [[广义脉冲响应]] 的线性公式中，$\Sigma_u e_j/\sqrt{\sigma_{jj}}$ 已把其他创新在给定 $u_{jt}$ 变化下的平均伴随变动包含进来。因此 GIRF 避开的是“先选一个 Cholesky 排序来正交化”，而不是“简约型创新可能是多个经济冲击的混合”这个识别问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the linear GIRF formula, $\Sigma_u e_j/\sqrt{\sigma_{jj}}$ already incorporates the average co-movement of the other innovations conditional on a change in $u_{jt}$. GIRF therefore avoids choosing a Cholesky ordering for orthogonalization; it does not remove the possibility that a reduced-form innovation is a mixture of economic shocks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“排序不变”是相对递归正交化的性质，不等于“结构识别完成”。GIRF 结果仍依赖变量的定义、缩放、信息集和模型规格。若 $u_{jt}$ 是多个经济冲击的混合，GIRF 也沿这个混合方向响应，不能只凭方程标签命名为货币、供给或需求冲击。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordering invariance is relative to recursive orthogonalization; it is not completed structural identification. A GIRF still depends on variable definitions, scaling, the information set, and model specification. If innovation $u_{jt}$ mixes several economic shocks, the GIRF follows that same mixed direction and cannot be named monetary, supply, or demand merely from the equation label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在非高斯或非线性系统中，广义响应更一般地定义为两个条件期望路径之差；上面的闭式公式依赖线性条件期望和相应分布矩条件，不能不加说明地套用到所有模型。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In nonlinear or non-Gaussian systems, generalized responses are defined from differences in conditional expected paths, and the linear closed form need not survive. The conditioning history, shock sign, and magnitude can matter, so linear-GIRF interpretation cannot be transferred without stating those assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> GIRF 不随 Cholesky 排序变化，是否意味着它能直接作为“已识别货币政策冲击”的结构 IRF？
>
> **答案：** 不意味着。它避免了递归排序，却仍以简约型创新为条件；经济冲击身份需要独立识别与论证。

## 来源与核验

- [Pesaran & Shin (1998), *Generalized Impulse Response Analysis in Linear Multivariate Models*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0165-1765(97)00214-0)：核对排序不变性只来自避开递归正交化，而 GIRF 仍以简约型创新的条件变化为对象。
- [Kilian & Lütkepohl (2017), *Structural Vector Autoregressive Analysis*](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108164818)：核对简约型响应与结构冲击识别的区别。
