# 投资组合可行集是给定资产与交易约束下能够达到的风险收益组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The portfolio opportunity set contains the risk-return pairs attainable from the specified assets and trading constraints.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在单期均值—方差模型中，设资产收益有有限二阶矩，均值向量为 $\mu$、协方差矩阵为 $\Sigma$。先用 $\mathcal W$ 表示允许的权重集合，再用[[组合均值与方差]]计算风险收益；投资组合可行集就是这些权重在风险收益平面上的像：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a single-period mean-variance model, assume finite second moments and let $\mu$ and $\Sigma$ denote the mean vector and covariance matrix. Let $\mathcal W$ denote the allowed weights, and use the [[组合均值与方差|portfolio mean and variance formulas]] to compute risk and return. The opportunity set is their image in the risk-return plane:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mathcal F=\left\{\left(\sqrt{w^T\Sigma w},\,w^T\mu\right):w\in\mathcal W\right\}.
$$

$\mathcal W$ 中的元素是配置方案，$\mathcal F$ 中的元素是可达到的“标准差、期望收益”二元组。不同权重可能投影到同一点；权重域是凸集也不意味着这个二维像集必为凸集。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An element of $\mathcal W$ is an allocation; an element of $\mathcal F$ is an attainable standard-deviation/expected-return pair. Different weights can map to the same point. Convexity of the weight domain does not by itself make this two-dimensional image convex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有完全投资约束时，$\mathcal W=\{w:\mathbf1^Tw=1\}$，允许负权重。[[禁止卖空的最小方差优化|禁止卖空的优化问题]]还会加上 $w_i\ge0$。加入新资产可能扩大可行集；增加交易约束则只能缩小或保持它，必须先固定比较所用的资产与规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With only full investment, $\mathcal W=\{w:\mathbf1^Tw=1\}$ allows negative weights. [[禁止卖空的最小方差优化|Optimization without short selling]] adds $w_i\ge0$. Adding assets may expand the opportunity set; adding trading restrictions can only shrink it or leave it unchanged. Comparisons must hold the asset universe and rules fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如两资产 $\mu=(0.08,0.12)^T$、$\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}0.04&0.015\\0.015&0.09\end{bmatrix}$。等权配置给出 $(\sigma_p,\mu_p)=(0.20,0.10)$，所以该点在允许等权配置的可行集中。可行只说明“做得到”，是否被别的组合改善由[[均值方差支配]]判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two assets with the stated $\mu$ and $\Sigma$, equal weights give $(\sigma_p,\mu_p)=(0.20,0.10)$, an attainable point whenever equal weighting is allowed. Feasibility means attainable; [[均值方差支配|mean-variance dominance]] determines whether another portfolio improves upon it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Zivot, *Portfolio Theory with Matrix Algebra*, PDF pp.10–11](https://faculty.washington.edu/ezivot/econ424/portfolioTheoryMatrix.pdf#page=10)：支持投资机会集是可达标准差与期望收益的集合；本文明确区分它与权重域。
- [Zivot, *Portfolio Theory with Short Sales Constraints*, §13.3.1–13.3.2](https://bookdown.org/compfinezbook/introcompfinr/Portfolio-Theory-with-1.html)：支持非负权重限制与可行范围变化。数值点由上述两资产输入独立代入核算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Zivot, PDF pp.10–11, supports the opportunity set as attainable standard-deviation/mean pairs; this note explicitly distinguishes it from the weight domain.
- Zivot, §13.3.1–13.3.2, supports nonnegative-weight restrictions and their effect on feasibility. The numerical point is independently calculated from the stated inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
