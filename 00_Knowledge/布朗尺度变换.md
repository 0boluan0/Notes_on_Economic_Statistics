# Brownian scaling
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*布朗尺度变换*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Brownian scaling** tells us how to change the speed of a Brownian clock without changing the kind of random motion we see. If we run the clock four times as fast, the typical displacement doubles. Dividing the observed displacement by two restores standard Brownian motion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**布朗尺度变换**说明怎样改变布朗运动的时钟速度，同时保留同一种随机运动规律。时钟加快到四倍，典型位移扩大到两倍；再把观察到的位移除以二，就恢复为标准布朗运动。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[标准布朗运动|standard Brownian motion]] $W=(W_t)_{t\ge0}$ starts at zero, has continuous sample paths, and has independent increments with $W_t-W_s\sim N(0,t-s)$ for $0\le s<t$. In this [[正态分布|normal-distribution notation]], the second parameter is the variance; its square root is the [[标准差|standard deviation]]. Thus $W_1$ has standard deviation $1$, whereas $W_4$ has standard deviation $2$. The square root, rather than the time multiplier itself, is the spatial correction we need.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[标准布朗运动]] $W=(W_t)_{t\ge0}$ 从零出发，样本路径连续，并具有独立增量；对 $0\le s<t$，有 $W_t-W_s\sim N(0,t-s)$。这个[[正态分布]]记号的第二个参数是方差，开平方才是[[标准差]]。因此 $W_1$ 的标准差为 $1$，$W_4$ 的标准差为 $2$。需要用来修正空间尺度的是时间倍数的平方根。

> [!example] A faster clock, with smaller displayed displacements · 加快时钟，缩小显示位移
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Set $Y_t=W_{4t}/2$. At the new time $t=1$, display half of the old position at time $4$. Between new times $1$ and $3$, the old clock advances from $4$ to $12$: the old increment has variance $8$, and division by two makes its variance $8/4=2$. That is exactly the variance required for a new time interval of length $3-1=2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $Y_t=W_{4t}/2$。新时刻 $t=1$ 显示的是旧时刻 $4$ 的位置的一半。新时刻从 $1$ 到 $3$ 时，旧时钟从 $4$ 走到 $12$：旧增量方差为 $8$，除以二后方差成为 $8/4=2$，恰好等于新区间长度 $3-1=2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, fix a **finite constant $c>0$**, chosen independently of which path is observed, and define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般地，预先固定一个**有限常数 $c>0$**，不根据观察到的路径选择它，定义

$$Y_t=c^{-1/2}W_{ct}=\frac{W_{ct}}{\sqrt c},\qquad t\ge0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $t$ is the new clock reading, $ct$ is the old clock reading, and $c^{-1/2}$ rescales position. Then **$Y$ is itself standard Brownian motion**. Values $c>1$ speed up the clock; values $0<c<1$ slow it down. This property is called self-similarity with exponent $1/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$t$ 是新时钟读数，$ct$ 是旧时钟读数，$c^{-1/2}$ 用来缩放位置。这样得到的 **$Y$ 本身仍是标准布朗运动**。$c>1$ 加快时钟，$0<c<1$ 放慢时钟；这称为指数为 $1/2$ 的自相似性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Checking the defining properties
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 逐条核验定义

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The process still starts at zero: $Y_0=c^{-1/2}W_0=0$ almost surely. For $0\le s<t$, multiplying a normal random variable by $c^{-1/2}$ multiplies its variance by $c^{-1}$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

新过程仍从零出发：$Y_0=c^{-1/2}W_0=0$ 几乎必然成立。对 $0\le s<t$，把正态随机变量乘以 $c^{-1/2}$，会把它的方差乘以 $c^{-1}$，因此

$$Y_t-Y_s=c^{-1/2}(W_{ct}-W_{cs})
\sim N\!\left(0,c^{-1}c(t-s)\right)=N(0,t-s).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finite collection of nonoverlapping new time intervals becomes a collection of nonoverlapping old intervals after multiplication by the same positive $c$. The corresponding old increments are mutually independent, and deterministic rescaling preserves that independence. Finally, on the probability-one event where the whole path of $W$ is continuous, the path $t\mapsto W_{ct}/\sqrt c$ is continuous too. These checks establish the entire Brownian definition, rather than only the distribution at a single time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一组有限个互不重叠的新时间区间，同乘正数 $c$ 后仍是互不重叠的旧区间。相应旧增量相互独立，确定性的缩放保留这种独立性。最后，在 $W$ 整条路径连续的概率一事件上，$t\mapsto W_{ct}/\sqrt c$ 也连续。这样核对的是完整的布朗定义，不只是某个时刻的分布。

## Equality of process laws
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 等分布不是逐路径等式

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalently, with $\overset d=$ meaning equality in distribution,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等价地，用 $\overset d=$ 表示等分布，有

$$(W_{ct})_{t\ge0}\overset d=(\sqrt c\,W_t)_{t\ge0}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This compares the laws of **whole continuous paths**, on $C([0,\infty),\mathbb R)$ with its usual Borel events from uniform convergence on bounded time intervals. In ordinary terms, it preserves the probabilities of measurable questions about the whole curve, not just one-time histograms. For continuous processes, the full [[有限维分布|finite-dimensional family]] determines this path-space law; matching one-time marginals alone would not suffice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它比较的是**整条连续路径**的概率律：路径空间为 $C([0,\infty),\mathbb R)$，使用由有限时间区间上一致收敛产生的通常 Borel 事件族。直观上，它保留的是关于整条曲线的可测问题的概率，不只是单个时刻的直方图。对于连续过程，整族[[有限维分布]]能够确定这个路径空间上的律；只有单时点边际相同则不够。

> [!warning] The same law does not mean the same observed curve · 同分布不表示同一条实际曲线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For one given Brownian motion, the claim does not say $W_4=2W_1$. Indeed, $W_4-2W_1=(W_4-W_1)-W_1$ is normal with mean zero and variance $3+1=4$, since the two terms are independent. Thus these values are equal with probability zero, despite $W_4\overset d=2W_1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对同一个给定的布朗运动，结论没有声称 $W_4=2W_1$。事实上，$W_4-2W_1=(W_4-W_1)-W_1$ 的两个部分独立，所以差值是均值零、方差 $3+1=4$ 的正态变量。这两个实际值以概率零相等，尽管 $W_4\overset d=2W_1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The restriction $c>0$ matters: $c=0$ makes the normalization undefined, and negative $c$ asks for negative old times outside this process's domain. The theorem concerns a fixed constant; a path-dependent random choice of clock is a different problem and is not covered by this proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$c>0$ 是必要的范围限定：$c=0$ 使归一化无定义，负数 $c$ 则会要求原过程定义域之外的负时间。这里讨论固定常数；根据路径随机挑选时钟，是另一个问题，不在这段证明的范围内。

> [!question]- Slowing the clock · 放慢时钟
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Is $Z_t=2W_{t/4}$ standard Brownian motion? Yes: take $c=1/4$, so $c^{-1/2}=2$. The old clock runs four times more slowly, and doubling space compensates for the smaller increment variance.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $Z_t=2W_{t/4}$ 还是标准布朗运动吗？是。取 $c=1/4$，就有 $c^{-1/2}=2$；旧时钟速度降为四分之一，再把空间放大两倍，补偿减小的增量方差。
