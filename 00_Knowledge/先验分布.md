# 先验分布是在纳入当前数据之前对未知量指定的概率分布

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A prior is a probability distribution on an unknown quantity before the current data are incorporated*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 先验 $\pi(\theta)$ 表达纳入当前数据之前对未知量 $\theta$ 的不确定性；可以来自历史证据、领域知识或明确的建模选择。离散先验概率之和为 1，连续先验密度的积分为 1。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A prior describes uncertainty about an unknown quantity before incorporating the current data. It may use previous evidence, domain knowledge, or an explicit modelling choice. A proper discrete prior sums to one; a proper continuous prior integrates to one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

若袋子 A、B 的事前概率都是 $1/2$，这是关于“哪个袋子”的分布，不是关于抽到红球的概率。观察红球后，结合[[似然函数]]得到[[后验分布]]。同一份当前数据不能被无说明地重复计入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal prior probabilities for bags A and B concern which bag is used, not whether a draw is red. Observations update this distribution through the [[似然函数|likelihood]] to produce a [[后验分布|posterior]]. Do not silently count the same evidence twice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
