# uniq只合并相邻相同行而不会自动完成全局去重

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*uniq combines adjacent equal lines; it does not automatically deduplicate globally*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 `LC_ALL=C`、比较完整行且不忽略大小写或部分字符的用法下，`uniq` 把连续相同的行看作一组，默认只输出每组第一行。它不要求输入已排序，但相同行若被别的行隔开，就属于不同组，不能得到全局去重结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With `LC_ALL=C`, full-line comparison, and no options that ignore case or portions of a line, `uniq` treats consecutive equal lines as one group and normally outputs the first line of each group. Input need not be sorted, but equal lines separated by other lines form separate groups rather than being deduplicated globally.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

```bash
printf '%s\n' a b a | LC_ALL=C uniq
printf '%s\n' a a b | LC_ALL=C uniq -c
```

第一条仍输出 `a`、`b`、`a`。第二条输出 `a` 的组计数 `2` 与 `b` 的组计数 `1`，具体前导空白由实现决定。`-d` 只保留重复组的一份代表，`-u` 只保留长度为一的组；在未保证同值相邻时，`-u` 不是“在整个文件中只出现一次的值”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first command still outputs `a`, `b`, `a`. The second reports a group count of `2` for `a` and `1` for `b`; leading padding depends on the implementation. `-d` retains one representative of each repeated group, while `-u` retains singleton groups only. Without a guarantee that equal values are adjacent, `-u` does not mean values occurring exactly once in the entire file.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要通过排序统计全局频数，先用兼容的排序规则使每个值的所有记录连续出现，再调用 `uniq -c`，见[[排序分组频数统计]]。不要无条件把 `sort | uniq` 与 `sort -u` 当成同一操作：比较键、忽略选项或 locale 的等价规则不同，可能造成不同的“相等”判定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For sort-based global frequencies, first make all records of each value contiguous using compatible sorting rules, then apply `uniq -c`; see [[排序分组频数统计|sort-based frequency counting]]. Do not unconditionally identify `sort | uniq` with `sort -u`: comparison keys, ignore options, or locale equivalence rules can produce different notions of equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [POSIX uniq, Description、Options and Application Usage](https://pubs.opengroup.org/onlinepubs/9799919799/utilities/uniq.html)：支持相邻组、`-c/-d/-u` 与 `sort | uniq`、`sort -u` 在非全序排序环境下可能不同的边界。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  This specification supports adjacent groups, `-c/-d/-u`, and the possible difference between `sort | uniq` and `sort -u` under a collation that is not a total order.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [GNU Coreutils, uniq invocation](https://www.gnu.org/software/coreutils/manual/html_node/uniq-invocation.html)：支持不要求预排序、只识别相邻重复及比较选项；例子固定 `C` locale 和完整行比较。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The manual supports unsorted input, adjacent-only duplicate detection, and comparison options. The examples fix the `C` locale and full-line comparison.
