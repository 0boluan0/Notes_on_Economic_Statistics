# Conditional variance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*条件方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Conditional variance** measures the spread still left after specified information is known. A [[条件期望|conditional mean]] gives the average within the selected group. Conditional variance then averages squared distances from **that group’s mean**, using that group’s probabilities. It answers how uncertain the outcome remains, even after the group is identified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**条件方差**衡量知道指定信息以后还剩多少波动。[[条件期望|条件均值]]先给出所选组内的平均值；条件方差再用组内概率，平均结果离**这一组自己的均值**的平方距离。它回答的是：已经知道属于哪一组，结果还有多不确定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two routes can leave different amounts of uncertainty
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*不同线路留下的波动可以不同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider two equally likely bus routes, labelled $X=0$ and $X=1$. Let $Y$ be the waiting time in minutes. Within each route, the two possible waits are equally likely:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑两条等概率出现的公交线路，记作 $X=0$ 和 $X=1$。用 $Y$ 表示等待分钟数。每条线路内的两个可能时间等概率出现：

| Route / 线路 | Waits / 等待时间 | Conditional mean / 条件均值 | Conditional variance / 条件方差 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $X=0$ | $1,3$ | $2$ | $1$ |
| $X=1$ | $4,8$ | $6$ | $4$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For route $0$, the squared distances from $2$ are $(1-2)^2=(3-2)^2=1$, so their average is $1$. For route $1$, the squared distances from $6$ are both $4$. Thus $\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X=0)=1$ and $\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X=1)=4$, in square minutes. Fixing a route produces a number. Before seeing the route, the rule itself is a random variable:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线路 $0$ 的两个时间离均值 $2$ 的平方距离均为 $1$，所以平均为 $1$；线路 $1$ 的两个时间离均值 $6$ 的平方距离均为 $4$。因此 $\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X=0)=1$、$\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X=1)=4$，单位是平方分钟。固定线路得到一个数值；看到线路之前，这条规则本身是随机变量：

$$
\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)=\begin{cases}1,&X=0,\\4,&X=1.\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The route tells us which spread applies. It does not tell us the exact wait. This is why conditional variance can vary across information states.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线路信息告诉我们应该使用哪个波动尺度，却没有告诉我们具体要等多久。这就是条件方差会随信息状态变化的原因。

> [!abstract] Definition · 定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let a [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]] $\mathcal G$ describe the available information, and assume $E[Y^2]<\infty$. This finite second-moment condition is also written $Y\in L^2$. Set $m=E[Y\mid\mathcal G]$. The conditional variance is the conditional average of the squared residual $Y-m$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用 [[σ-代数]] $\mathcal G$ 表示可用信息，并假设 $E[Y^2]<\infty$；这个有限二阶矩条件也记为 $Y\in L^2$。令 $m=E[Y\mid\mathcal G]$，条件方差就是平方残差 $(Y-m)^2$ 的条件平均：

$$
\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid\mathcal G):=E[(Y-m)^2\mid\mathcal G].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is nonnegative, finite almost surely, and $\mathcal G$-measurable: it can be determined from the specified information. Like conditional expectation, it is defined up to changes on a probability-zero set. For information supplied by $X$, take $\mathcal G=\sigma(X)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它非负、几乎处处有限，而且对 $\mathcal G$ 可测，也就是可以由指定信息确定。与条件期望一样，它允许在概率为零的集合上改变版本。若信息由 $X$ 提供，就取 $\mathcal G=\sigma(X)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A convenient computation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*便于计算的等价式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once $\mathcal G$ is known, $m$ is known too. Expand $(Y-m)^2$ and use the [[条件期望取出已知量|known-factor rule]] to take $m$ outside the conditional average:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知道 $\mathcal G$ 后，$m$ 也已知。展开 $(Y-m)^2$，再用[[条件期望取出已知量|提出已知因子的规则]]：

$$
\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid\mathcal G)=E[Y^2\mid\mathcal G]-2mE[Y\mid\mathcal G]+m^2=E[Y^2\mid\mathcal G]-m^2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In route $1$, this reads $(4^2+8^2)/2-6^2=40-36=4$. The assumption $E[Y^2]<\infty$ guarantees that the quantities used here are integrable and the conditional variance has a finite overall average. Mere $E[|Y|]<\infty$ does not give this guarantee; broader definitions require checking the [[矩存在性的使用边界|moment conditions]]. A finite unconditional second moment is a sufficient setting for this card, not a necessary condition for every group to have a finite variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在线路 $1$ 中，上式就是 $(4^2+8^2)/2-6^2=40-36=4$。假设 $E[Y^2]<\infty$ 保证这里涉及的量可积，并保证条件方差的总体平均有限。仅有 $E[|Y|]<\infty$ 不能保证这些结论；更广的定义要另查[[矩存在性的使用边界|矩条件]]。无条件二阶矩有限是本卡采用的充分条件，并不是每个组内方差有限的必要条件。

## 两个边界情形
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Two information extremes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->



<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With no information beyond the whole sample space, $\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid\mathcal G)=\operatorname{Var}(Y)$ almost surely. If the information completely determines $Y$, then its conditional mean is $Y$ and the conditional variance is zero almost surely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果除了整个样本空间以外没有额外信息，条件方差几乎处处就是普通[[方差]]。如果信息已经完全确定 $Y$，条件均值就是 $Y$，条件方差几乎处处为零。

> [!example]- Known signal, remaining noise · 已知信号与剩余噪声
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $Y=Z+\varepsilon$, where $Z$ is square integrable and known from $\mathcal G$. Suppose $\varepsilon$ is square integrable, independent of $\mathcal G$, with mean zero and variance $\sigma^2$. Then $E[Y\mid\mathcal G]=Z$ and $\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid\mathcal G)=\sigma^2$. The information identifies the signal $Z$ but leaves the noise. Here the remaining variance happens to be constant.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $Y=Z+\varepsilon$，其中 $Z$ 平方可积且可由 $\mathcal G$ 确定。设噪声 $\varepsilon$ 平方可积、与 $\mathcal G$ 独立、均值为零、方差为 $\sigma^2$。于是 $E[Y\mid\mathcal G]=Z$，且 $\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid\mathcal G)=\sigma^2$。信息确定了信号 $Z$，却留下噪声；在这个模型中，剩余方差恰好是常数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Averaging conditional variances does not generally recover the overall variance. In the two-route example the average is $(1+4)/2=2.5$, while the overall variance is $6.5$. Different route means account for the missing $4$; the [[全方差公式|law of total variance]] explains that additional contribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把条件方差再平均，一般得不到总体方差。两线路例子中，条件方差的平均为 $(1+4)/2=2.5$，总体方差却为 $6.5$；不同线路均值贡献了其余的 $4$，由[[全方差公式]]解释。
