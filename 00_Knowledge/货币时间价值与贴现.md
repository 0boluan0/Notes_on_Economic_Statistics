# 货币时间价值与贴现
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Time Value of Money and Discounting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把发生在不同日期的现金流换算到同一个时点，使消费、投资、借贷和估值方案可以比较。
> **具体锚点：** 若一年期机会成本为 5%，一年后的 105 元与今天的 100 元等值；不是因为 105 和 100 相等，而是 100 今天可以增长到 105。
> **核心难点：** 现金流时点、复利频率、名义/实际口径和风险必须与贴现率一一匹配；贴现不是机械地除以任意利率。
> **为什么重要：** 债券与股票估值、资本预算、租赁、养老金和任何跨期决策都建立在同一套时间换算上。
> **继续：** 需要从单一贴现率进入按期限变化的利率时，读 [[利率、即期远期与期限结构]]；项目应用见 [[资本预算与投资决策]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It converts cash flows occurring on different dates to one common date so that consumption, investment, borrowing, and valuation alternatives can be compared.
> **Concrete anchor:** If the one-year opportunity cost is 5%, 105 received in one year is equivalent to 100 today—not because 105 equals 100, but because 100 today can grow to 105.
> **Central difficulty:** Cash-flow timing, compounding frequency, nominal-versus-real convention, and risk must each match the discount rate. Discounting is not mechanical division by an arbitrary rate.
> **Why it matters:** Bond and equity valuation, capital budgeting, leasing, pensions, and every intertemporal decision use the same time-conversion logic.
> **Continue:** To move from one discount rate to rates that vary by maturity, read [[利率、即期远期与期限结构|Interest Rates, Spot and Forward Rates, and the Term Structure]]. For a project application, see [[资本预算与投资决策|Capital Budgeting and Investment Decisions]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一条主线：现在值、将来值与机会成本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*One backbone: present value, future value, and opportunity cost*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复利回答“今天的一笔钱到未来会变成多少”，贴现回答同一个等价关系的反方向。若每期利率为 $r$，今天的 $PV$ 在 $t$ 期后成为 $FV_t=PV(1+r)^t$；因此未来现金流的现值为 $PV=FV_t/(1+r)^t$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compounding asks how much a sum today will become in the future; discounting applies the same equivalence in reverse. At a per-period rate $r$, present value $PV$ becomes $FV_t=PV(1+r)^t$ after $t$ periods, so the present value of a future cash flow is $PV=FV_t/(1+r)^t$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 $r$ 是放弃最佳可比选择的机会成本，而不是由公式自己产生的数字。若现金流更晚、更不确定、币种不同或流动性更差，可比投资机会就会变化，所需贴现率也可能变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $r$ is the opportunity cost of forgoing the best comparable alternative, not a number generated by the formula itself. If a cash flow is later, more uncertain, denominated in another currency, or less liquid, the comparable opportunity changes and so may the required discount rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 复利、现值与贴现
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Compounding, present value, and discounting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单笔未来现金流现值为 $PV=CF_t/(1+r)^t$；多笔现金流逐笔贴现后相加。复利频率、年化口径和现金流时点必须一致。贴现率应匹配期限、币种、风险和流动性，不能用一个利率无差别折现所有现金流。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The present value of one future cash flow is $PV=CF_t/(1+r)^t$; multiple cash flows are discounted one by one and then added. Compounding frequency, annualization convention, and cash-flow dates must be consistent. A discount rate must match maturity, currency, risk, and liquidity; one rate cannot indiscriminately discount every cash flow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 年金、永续年金与增长永续年金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Annuities, perpetuities, and growing perpetuities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若每期期末收到固定现金流 $C$，持续 $n$ 期，则普通年金现值为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a fixed cash flow $C$ is received at each period-end for $n$ periods, the present value of an ordinary annuity is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$PV=C\frac{1-(1+r)^{-n}}{r}.$$

若现金流永续且从下一期开始，$PV=C/r$；若下一期现金流为 $C_1$ 且以后按固定率 $g$ 增长，$PV=C_1/(r-g)$，但必须有 $r>g$ 且增长假设长期可持续。期初年金比同额期末年金每笔早一期，因此普通年金现值再乘 $(1+r)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a perpetuity beginning next period, $PV=C/r$. If next period's cash flow is $C_1$ and then grows forever at rate $g$, $PV=C_1/(r-g)$, but only when $r>g$ and the growth assumption is sustainable in the long run. Each payment in an annuity due arrives one period earlier than in an ordinary annuity, so multiply the ordinary-annuity value by $(1+r)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 复利频率与利率口径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Compounding frequency and rate conventions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

名义年利率不能在复利频率不同的方案间直接比较。若名义年利率为 $j$、每年复利 $m$ 次，有效年利率为 $(1+j/m)^m-1$。连续复利下，$FV=PV e^{rt}$。先把利率换成与现金流期间一致的有效利率，再进行折现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nominal annual rates cannot be compared directly across alternatives with different compounding frequencies. If the nominal annual rate is $j$ with $m$ compounding periods per year, the effective annual rate is $(1+j/m)^m-1$. Under continuous compounding, $FV=PV e^{rt}$. Convert the rate to an effective rate matching the cash-flow interval before discounting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 数值锚点：一组现金流怎样被换到今天
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Numerical anchor: bringing a stream of cash flows to today*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设项目今天支出 1,000，未来三年年末分别收到 300、400、500，年贴现率为 8%。未来流入的现值为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a project costs 1,000 today and pays 300, 400, and 500 at the ends of the next three years, with an annual discount rate of 8%. The present value of the future inflows is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{300}{1.08}+\frac{400}{1.08^2}+\frac{500}{1.08^3}=1{,}022.11.
$$

净现值为 22.11。若误把第三年末的 500 当成第二年末，会得到更高现值；若把 8% 名义年率按月复利却仍直接用年度指数，也会发生口径错配。时间线应在按计算器之前画出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net present value is 22.11. Mistaking the 500 at the end of year three for a payment at the end of year two would overstate present value. Treating an 8% nominal annual rate compounded monthly as if it were an effective annual rate would also mismatch conventions. Draw the timeline before using a calculator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把年中现金流当成年末现金流，会系统性低估现值。
- 把名义年率与有效年率混用，会在复利频率较高或期限较长时积累明显误差。
- 用含通胀的名义贴现率折实际现金流，或反过来，会重复加入或遗漏通胀。
- 用企业整体资本成本折现安全税盾、风险项目和不同币种现金流，等于假设它们具有同一风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Treating mid-year cash flows as year-end cash flows systematically understates present value.
- Mixing nominal and effective annual rates creates material errors when compounding is frequent or horizons are long.
- Discounting real cash flows with a nominal rate, or nominal cash flows with a real rate, double-counts or omits inflation.
- Discounting safe tax shields, risky projects, and cash flows in different currencies with one firm-wide cost of capital assumes they all have the same risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：贴现为什么是机会成本而不是“钱自然贬值”？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why does discounting reflect opportunity cost rather than money naturally decaying?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 因为放弃今天的钱也放弃了投资、消费或保持流动性的选择；贴现率衡量最佳可比选择的回报与风险补偿，而不是货币物理上消失。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Giving up money today also gives up the option to invest, consume, or retain liquidity. The discount rate measures the return and risk compensation on the best comparable alternative, not physical decay of money.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 月利率 1% 与名义年利率 12% 的有效年回报是否相同？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does a monthly rate of 1% have the same effective annual return as a nominal annual rate of 12%?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 若 12% 是按月复利的名义年率，两者合同描述一致，但有效年率是 $(1.01)^{12}-1\approx12.68\%$，不是 12%。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If 12% is a nominal annual rate compounded monthly, the two descriptions refer to the same contract, but the effective annual rate is $(1.01)^{12}-1\approx12.68\%$, not 12%.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 什么时候不能把多笔现金流都用同一个贴现率？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When should multiple cash flows not all be discounted at one rate?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 当它们的期限、币种、系统风险、信用风险或流动性实质不同，而且这些差异会改变可比机会成本时，应使用匹配的逐期贴现率或状态价格。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When maturity, currency, systematic risk, credit risk, or liquidity differs materially enough to change the comparable opportunity cost, use matching period-specific rates or state prices.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
