# 市场相对于信息集有效是指价格已充分反映该信息集中的可得信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A market is efficient relative to an information set when prices fully reflect the information available in that set*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 给定时点 $t$ 的信息集 $\mathcal I_t$，如果资产价格已经充分反映 $\mathcal I_t$ 中当时可得的信息，就称市场相对于 $\mathcal I_t$ 有效。有效性因此总是“相对于什么信息”的命题，而不是对市场永久正确、价格不再变化或投资者从不犯错的总评。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Given an information set $\mathcal I_t$ available at time $t$, a market is efficient relative to $\mathcal I_t$ when asset prices already fully reflect that information. Efficiency is therefore always relative to a stated information set; it is not a claim that markets are permanently correct, prices never change, or investors never err.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“充分反映”关心的是旧信息还能否产生系统且可实施的模型相对异常收益。它不要求回报为零：投资者仍可获得时间价值与风险补偿，新信息到来时价格也可以大幅改变。真正检验时还必须指定资产、时期、正常收益模型与信息和交易成本，见 [[市场效率检验口径]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Fully reflect” concerns whether old information can still generate systematic, implementable, model-relative abnormal returns. It does not require returns to be zero: investors may still earn compensation for time and risk, and prices may move sharply when new information arrives. An empirical test must additionally specify the assets, period, normal-return model, and information and trading costs; see [[市场效率检验口径|market-efficiency test scope]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一份公开公告发布后，即使股价上涨，也不能仅凭“价格变了”否定有效性；上涨可能正是价格吸收新增信息。相关问题是：在公告公开之后，交易者能否只用当时已经公开的信息，在合理风险模型和实施成本下继续系统获得异常收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, a price rise after a public announcement does not by itself reject efficiency; the rise may be the incorporation of new information. The relevant question is whether, after disclosure, traders can use information already public at the time to earn systematic abnormal returns under a reasonable risk model and implementable costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

弱式、半强式和强式不是三个不同定义，而是把这个命题应用于逐步扩大的信息集，见 [[有效市场三种形式]]。任何异常收益检验又同时依赖正常收益模型，见 [[市场效率联合假设]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weak, semi-strong, and strong forms are not different definitions; they apply the same claim to progressively larger information sets, as shown in [[有效市场三种形式|the three forms of market efficiency]]. Any abnormal-return test also depends jointly on its normal-return model; see [[市场效率联合假设|the joint hypothesis]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 某股票在盈利公告后立即上涨 8%，这是否证明市场无效？
>
> **答案：** 不能。价格因新增信息立即调整与有效市场相容；要构成反证，需说明公告后已经可得的信息仍能在风险和成本调整后系统预测异常收益。
