# Row space
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **row space** contains every vector you can build by multiplying a matrix's rows by scalars and adding them. It collects all the coefficient lists obtainable from the given rows. A redundant row adds another way to form a combination, but adds no new direction to the space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**行空间**收集把矩阵各行乘以标量再相加所能得到的全部向量，也就是原有各行能够生成的所有系数列表。一行若已能由其他行组合得到，它会增加一种组合方式，却不会给这个空间增加新方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two independent rows describe a plane
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider this real matrix. Write its rows as $r_1,r_2,r_3$, each with three coordinates:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑下面的实矩阵，把三行分别记作 $r_1,r_2,r_3$，每行都有三个坐标：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&0\\2&4&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
c_1r_1+c_2r_2+c_3r_3=(c_1+2c_2,\,2c_1+4c_2,\,c_3).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second row is twice the first. Thus every combination has the form $(s,2s,t)$, and every such vector is obtained by choosing $(c_1,c_2,c_3)=(s,0,t)$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二行是第一行的两倍，所以每个组合都形如 $(s,2s,t)$；反过来，选择 $(c_1,c_2,c_3)=(s,0,t)$ 就能得到每个这样的向量。因此

$$\operatorname{Row}(A)=\{(s,2s,t):s,t\in\mathbb R\}
=\operatorname{span}\{(1,2,0),(0,0,1)\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here [[张成|span]] means all linear combinations. The two displayed generators are [[线性无关|independent]]: their combination can be zero only when both coefficients are zero. They form a [[基|basis]], so the row space is a two-dimensional plane in $\mathbb R^3$. All three original rows span the plane, but are not a basis because the second is redundant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 [[张成|span]] 表示全部线性组合。列出的两个生成向量[[线性无关]]：只有两个系数都为零，组合才会为零。它们构成一组[[基]]，因此行空间是 $\mathbb R^3$ 中的二维平面。原来的三行一起能够张成这个平面，却因第二行冗余而不是一组基。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Rows, coefficients and the transpose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行、组合系数与转置*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$ have rows $r_1,\ldots,r_m$, where $m,n$ are positive integers and the scalar field $\mathbb F$ is $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$ 的各行为 $r_1,\ldots,r_m$，其中 $m,n$ 是正整数，标量域 $\mathbb F$ 为 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。定义

> [!abstract] All row combinations · 各行的全部组合
> $$\boxed{\operatorname{Row}(A)
> =\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{m}c_i r_i:c_1,\ldots,c_m\in\mathbb F\right\}
> =\{c^TA:c\in\mathbb F^m\}.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The superscript $T$ is the ordinary [[矩阵转置|transpose]], with no complex conjugation. The coefficient vector $c$ has $m$ entries, one per row, while every resulting row has $n$ entries. Hence the row space is a [[子空间|subspace]] of the space of $n$-coordinate rows: it contains zero and is closed under linear combinations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上标 $T$ 是普通[[矩阵转置|转置]]，不取复共轭。组合系数向量 $c$ 有 $m$ 个分量，每行对应一个；得到的每一行却有 $n$ 个分量。因此行空间是 $n$ 坐标行向量空间的[[子空间]]：包含零，并且对线性组合封闭。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If we write these same coordinate lists as columns, then $(c^TA)^T=A^Tc$. Under this row-to-column identification, the row space is written $C(A^T)\subseteq\mathbb F^n$, the [[列空间|column space]] of the transpose. This identification changes the orientation of the written vector, not its entries. For complex matrices, conjugating the entries is a further operation; the distinction matters below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若把同一份坐标列表改写成列，则 $(c^TA)^T=A^Tc$。在这种行列写法的对应下，行空间记作 $C(A^T)\subseteq\mathbb F^n$，即转置矩阵的[[列空间]]。这里改变的是向量的书写方向，不是其元素。对复矩阵，给元素取共轭是额外一步，下面会区分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the rows measure over the real numbers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*实数情形下，各行读取什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real matrix and an input column $x\in\mathbb R^n$, the $i$th coordinate of $Ax$ is $r_i x$, the standard [[内积|inner product]] of $r_i^T$ and $x$. Thus $Ax=0$ exactly when $x$ is [[正交|orthogonal]] to every row, and hence to every row combination. The [[零空间|null space]] consists of precisely these invisible input directions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实矩阵与输入列向量 $x\in\mathbb R^n$，$Ax$ 的第 $i$ 个坐标为 $r_i x$，也就是 $r_i^T$ 与 $x$ 的标准[[内积]]。所以 $Ax=0$ 当且仅当 $x$ 与每一行都[[正交]]，进而与全部行组合正交。[[零空间]]恰好收集这些无法从输出看出的输入方向：

$$N(A)=C(A^T)^\perp\qquad(A\text{ real, standard inner product}).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $\perp$ denotes the [[正交补|orthogonal complement]], which tests perpendicularity against every vector of a space. In the opening example, $Ax=0$ requires $x_1+2x_2=0$ and $x_3=0$, giving $x=(-2u,u,0)^T$. Its dot product with every $(s,2s,t)$ is zero. These are homogeneous solutions; the row-space vectors themselves are coefficient lists, not the solutions of $Ax=b$. The wider real-space geometry is developed in the [[基本子空间正交补|fundamental-subspace orthogonality theorem]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

符号 $\perp$ 表示[[正交补]]，要求与一个空间的每个向量都垂直。开头例子中，$Ax=0$ 要求 $x_1+2x_2=0$、$x_3=0$，所以 $x=(-2u,u,0)^T$，与任意 $(s,2s,t)$ 的点积都为零。这些才是齐次解；行空间中的向量是系数列表，不是 $Ax=b$ 的解。完整实数几何见[[基本子空间正交补]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Changing the rows without changing their span
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An invertible row operation replaces the rows with combinations of themselves, and its inverse recovers the old rows as combinations of the new ones. Each row space is therefore contained in the other. More formally, if $R=EA$ with [[逆矩阵|invertible]] $E$, then $A=E^{-1}R$ proves the reverse inclusion. This is why [[行等价|row-equivalent matrices]] have the same row space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可逆行操作用原有各行的组合替换各行，其逆操作又把旧行恢复为新行的组合，因此两个行空间互相包含。具体地，若 $R=EA$ 且 $E$ [[逆矩阵|可逆]]，则 $A=E^{-1}R$ 给出反向包含。这解释了为什么[[行等价]]矩阵具有同一行空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently, the [[RREF读取行空间基|nonzero rows of reduced row echelon form give a row-space basis]]. For the example, they are $(1,2,0)$ and $(0,0,1)$. Zero rows contribute no direction. A zero matrix has row space $\{0\}$ and an empty basis, not an empty row space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此可以[[RREF读取行空间基|用行最简形的非零行作为行空间基]]。对本例，它们就是 $(1,2,0)$ 与 $(0,0,1)$。零行不贡献方向；零矩阵的行空间为 $\{0\}$，基为空列表，而不是行空间为空集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[行秩等于列秩|Row rank equals column rank]] says that the two spaces have the same dimension. They need not be the same space: rows have $n$ coordinates, columns have $m$, and even when $m=n$ their spans may differ. For $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$, the row space is the line through $(1,2)$, while the column space is the line through $(1,0)^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[行秩等于列秩]]说的是两个空间的维数相同，不是空间相同：行有 $n$ 个坐标，列有 $m$ 个；即使 $m=n$，它们张成的空间仍可能不同。例如 $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ 的行空间是 $(1,2)$ 所在直线，列空间却是 $(1,0)^T$ 所在直线。

## Complex rows and Hermitian geometry
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*复数行与 Hermitian 几何*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The algebraic definition above remains $C(A^T)$ in column notation. In the standard complex inner product $u^*x$, however, $^*$ means [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]. The geometric row space used in orthogonality statements is $C(A^*)$, formed by conjugating the transposed rows. It is the entrywise-conjugate space of $C(A^T)$, not necessarily the same set. Under this convention,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的代数定义改写成列后仍是 $C(A^T)$。但标准复内积为 $u^*x$，其中 $^*$ 表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]。用于正交关系的几何行空间是 $C(A^*)$，由各行取共轭转置后张成。它是 $C(A^T)$ 逐元素取共轭得到的空间，两者不一定是同一个集合。在这套约定下，

$$\boxed{N(A)=C(A^*)^\perp.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indeed, $(A^*y)^*x=y^*Ax$ for every $y\in\mathbb C^m$. If $Ax=0$, all these products vanish. Conversely, if they vanish for all $y$, choosing $y=Ax$ gives $(Ax)^*(Ax)=0$, forcing $Ax=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为对每个 $y\in\mathbb C^m$，都有 $(A^*y)^*x=y^*Ax$。若 $Ax=0$，这些内积全为零；反过来，若对所有 $y$ 都为零，取 $y=Ax$ 就得到 $(Ax)^*(Ax)=0$，从而 $Ax=0$。

> [!warning] Transpose and conjugate transpose give different spaces · 两种转置可能给出不同空间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A=(1\ \ i)$ with $i^2=-1$. Then $C(A^T)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{(1,i)^T\}$ but $C(A^*)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{(1,-i)^T\}$. These lines are different: a scalar matching the first coordinate of their displayed generators must be $1$, and then cannot match the second. Moreover, with $u=(1,i)^T$ and $x=(i,1)^T$, we have $u^*x=i-i=0$ but $Ax=2i\ne0$. Thus using the algebraic row space in the Hermitian orthogonality test gives the wrong conclusion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $A=(1\ \ i)$，其中 $i^2=-1$。则 $C(A^T)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{(1,i)^T\}$，而 $C(A^*)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{(1,-i)^T\}$。两条直线不同：要匹配所列生成向量的第一坐标，倍数只能为 $1$，但它无法匹配第二坐标。并且取 $u=(1,i)^T$、$x=(i,1)^T$，有 $u^*x=i-i=0$，却有 $Ax=2i\ne0$。所以不能把代数行空间直接放进 Hermitian 正交检验。
