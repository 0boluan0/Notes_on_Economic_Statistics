# 定积分与微积分基本定理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Definite integrals and the fundamental theorem of calculus*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把不断累积的微小贡献合成总量，并揭示“累积”和“瞬时变化”互为逆过程。
> **具体锚点：** 已知每时刻速度 $v(t)$，区间内位移是 $\int_a^b v(t)dt$，不是简单用某一时刻速度乘时间。
> **核心难点：** 定积分是带符号的极限，不天然等于几何面积；微积分基本定理需要连续性等条件。
> **为什么重要：** 概率密度、消费者剩余、期望、现值和连续时间模型都在做累积。
> **继续：** 先读黎曼和与 FTC；遇到算不出的积分转到 [[积分方法|积分方法、参数曲线与极坐标]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** A definite integral aggregates continuously varying local contributions, while the fundamental theorem identifies accumulation and instantaneous change as inverse operations.
> **Concrete anchor:** If velocity is $v(t)$, displacement over $[a,b]$ is $\int_a^b v(t)dt$, not one arbitrarily chosen speed times elapsed time.
> **Central difficulty:** A definite integral is a signed limit, not automatically geometric area. The fundamental theorem requires conditions such as continuity.
> **Why it matters:** Probability density, surplus, expectation, present value, and continuous-time models all aggregate flows.
> **Continue with:** Understand Riemann sums and both parts of the theorem; for antiderivative techniques see [[积分方法|integration techniques]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从黎曼和到定积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From Riemann sums to the definite integral*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $[a,b]$ 分成小区间，在每段取样并求 $\sum f(x_i^*)\Delta x_i$；当最大网格长度趋于零且极限与分割方式无关时，得到 $\int_a^b f(x)dx$。它累计的是“高度×宽度”的带符号贡献。若要几何面积，函数变号处应分段并积分 $|f|$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Partition $[a,b]$, sample each subinterval, and form $\sum f(x_i^*)\Delta x_i$. If this converges to a partition-independent limit as the largest mesh width tends to zero, the result is $\int_a^b f(x)dx$. It accumulates signed “height times width” contributions. Geometric area instead requires splitting at sign changes and integrating $|f|$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两个基本定理怎样把导数与积分接起来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How the two fundamental theorems connect derivatives and integrals*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 连续，累积函数 $F(x)=\int_a^x f(t)dt$ 满足 $F'(x)=f(x)$；反过来，若 $G'=f$，则 $\int_a^b f(x)dx=G(b)-G(a)$。前者说明累积量的边际变化就是当前流量，后者把极限定义转成原函数计算。换元法本质上是链式法则在积分中的反向使用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f$ is continuous, the accumulation function $F(x)=\int_a^x f(t)dt$ satisfies $F'(x)=f(x)$. Conversely, if $G'=f$, then $\int_a^b f(x)dx=G(b)-G(a)$. The first says that the marginal change in an accumulated stock is the current flow; the second replaces a limit definition by antiderivative evaluation. Substitution is the chain rule used in reverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 平均值、净变化与单位
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Average value, net change, and units*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

函数在区间的平均值是 $\frac1{b-a}\int_a^b f$。若 $r(t)$ 是存量的变化率，$\int_a^b r(t)dt$ 是净变化，单位从“存量/时间”乘以时间回到存量。单位检查常能发现漏掉区间长度或把流量误作存量的错误。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The average value of a function on an interval is $\frac1{b-a}\int_a^b f$. If $r(t)$ is the rate of change of a stock, $\int_a^b r(t)dt$ is the net change. Multiplying “stock per unit time” by time restores stock units. Unit checks often reveal a missing interval length or confusion between a flow and a stock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 积分应用的建模顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A modelling sequence for integral applications*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

体积、功、质心和概率都先确定微元：一小块贡献是什么、用哪个变量切片、边界在哪里。随后才写积分。圆盘法和壳层法不是两套需背诵的公式，而是两种不同切片；选能让半径、高度和边界最简单的表示。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For volume, work, centres of mass, and probability, first identify the infinitesimal contribution: what one slice contributes, which variable indexes slices, and where the bounds lie. Only then write the integral. Disk and shell methods are two slicing choices, not unrelated formula lists; choose the representation that makes radii, heights, and bounds simplest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：从速度到位移与路程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: from velocity to displacement and distance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $v(t)=2t-4$ 且 $0\le t\le4$，位移是 $\int_0^4(2t-4)dt=0$，因为前两秒的负向位移与后两秒的正向位移抵消。总路程则是 $\int_0^4|2t-4|dt=8$。这个区别直接显示了定积分是带符号净累积，而距离必须累积速率的绝对值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $v(t)=2t-4$ for $0\le t\le4$, displacement is $\int_0^4(2t-4)dt=0$ because negative motion during the first two seconds cancels positive motion during the last two. Total distance is $\int_0^4|2t-4|dt=8$. The contrast shows that a definite integral is signed net accumulation, while distance accumulates the absolute speed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么定积分可能为 0，但曲线与横轴围成的面积不为 0？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a definite integral be zero while the geometric area is positive?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 正负区域的带符号贡献可能相互抵消；面积需要对绝对值积分或按变号点分段。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Positive and negative signed contributions may cancel. Geometric area requires integrating the absolute value or splitting at sign changes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明微积分基本定理的第一部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*State the first fundamental theorem of calculus in your own words.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 从起点累积到 $x$ 的总量，当终点再向前移动一点时，新增加的量近似为当前函数值乘这一步，因此累积函数的导数回到原函数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Moving the upper endpoint a small amount adds approximately the current function value times that movement, so the derivative of the accumulated total recovers the original function.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 列积分应用时最常见的结构错误是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is the most common structural error when setting up an integral application?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 没有先定义微元和变量就套公式，导致半径、厚度、密度或边界不属于同一切片。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Applying a formula before defining the slice and variable can make the radius, thickness, density, and bounds refer to incompatible slices.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 如何用单位判断一个积分设置是否可疑？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How can units reveal a suspicious integral setup?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> integrand 的单位乘以积分变量的单位应等于所求总量的单位；不符往往意味着漏了厚度、密度或转换因子。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The integrand's units times the integration variable's units must equal the desired total's units. A mismatch often signals a missing thickness, density, or conversion factor.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
