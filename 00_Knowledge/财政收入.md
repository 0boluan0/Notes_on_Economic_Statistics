# 财政收入在GFS口径下是交易带来的政府净值增加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under GFS, government revenue is an increase in net worth resulting from a transaction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在政府财政统计GFS中，财政收入包括税收、相应口径的社会缴款、赠与以及财产收入、商品服务销售收入等。共同点不是“现金进来了”，而是这笔交易使政府的资产减负债，也就是净值，增加了。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
GFS revenue includes taxes, qualifying social contributions, grants, property income and sales of goods and services. The defining feature is not a cash inflow: the transaction raises assets minus liabilities, or net worth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一笔税在本期形成应收权利，即使下期才收款，按[[现金与权责发生|权责发生口径]]也可能属于本期收入。反过来，借款100同时增加现金100和负债100，净值没有增加；卖出按当前价值记账的金融资产只是换成现金，亦不因此产生收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A tax claim arising this period can be current revenue on an [[现金与权责发生|accrual basis]], even if collected later. Borrowing 100 increases cash and liabilities equally; selling a financial asset at its recorded current value exchanges assets. Neither transaction alone raises net worth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净值上升也不全是收入。政府持有的土地市价上涨属于重估，不是交易收入。阅读“财政收入”数字前，必须确认是这个统计定义，还是课堂、预算表所说的较广筹资口径；同名不能代替口径核对，参见[[财政收入与融资]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Not every increase in net worth is revenue: a rise in the market value of government land is revaluation. Check whether a reported revenue figure uses GFS or a broader budgetary funding convention. The label alone does not establish comparability; see [[财政收入与融资|revenue and financing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
