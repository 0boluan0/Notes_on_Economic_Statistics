# 解释景气信号应先核对对象、基准、变化方向与发布时间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Interpret an activity signal only after checking its target, reference level, direction of change, and release timing.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读到一个景气数字时，先查它衡量什么，以及与什么比较；“高于基准”和“比上月上升”是不同判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When reading an activity statistic, first identify what it measures and its comparison baseline. Being above a reference level differs from rising since the previous month.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 查对象与算法：是产量、价格、库存还是回答比例？水平、同比、环比或偏离趋势？是否季调、加权或逆向处理？
2. 查基准与方向：50 或 100 在该指标里究竟表示什么？分别报告当前位置与变化方向。
3. 查信息时点：调查期、发布时间、修订版本是否相容？领先性针对哪个目标，近期是否仍有证据支持？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Identify the object and construction: output, prices, inventories, or response shares; levels, annual changes, monthly changes, or deviations from trend; seasonal adjustment, weights, or inversion.<br>
**2.** Interpret the reference level specifically, and report position separately from direction.<br>
**3.** Check survey dates, publication dates, and revisions; identify the target and evidence behind any claimed lead.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以生产扩散指数为例，增加、持平、减少回答占 40%、30%、30%，则指数为 $40+0.5\times30=55$，表示增加回答比减少回答多，不表示产量增长 5%。若指数从 55 降至 52，仍在 50 以上，只是净扩张回答优势减弱。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If production responses are 40% higher, 30% unchanged, and 30% lower, the diffusion index is $40+0.5\times30=55$. More respondents report increases than decreases; output has not thereby grown 5%. A fall from 55 to 52 remains above 50 while showing a smaller net balance of expansion responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OECD 标准化综合领先指标的 100 则对应趋势参照：101 降至 100.5，仍发出未来活动水平高于趋势的信号，但方向在减弱。100.5 不表示 GDP 增长 0.5%；更不能把其规则套到任意以基年等于 100 编制的指数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the OECD's standardized CLI, 100 is a trend reference. A fall from 101 to 100.5 still signals future activity above trend while weakening directionally. A value of 100.5 does not mean GDP growth of 0.5%, and this convention cannot be applied to every index rebased to 100.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [国家统计局，采购经理指数编制方法，第一至三节](https://www.stats.gov.cn/zs/tjws/zytjzbqs/cgzlzs/202501/t20250121_1958396.html)：核对扩散公式、综合加权、配送时间逆向处理和季节调整；本卡只用生产分类指数作例。
- [OECD, Interpreting CLIs, PDF 第 1–5 页](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/data/methods/Interpreting_OECD_Composite_Leading_Indicators.pdf#page=1)：核对 100 的趋势含义、位置与方向、不能量化 GDP 增长以及单月信号的局限。两组数值为独立示意。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NBS supports diffusion calculations, weighting, reversed delivery-time components, and seasonal adjustment; the example uses only a production component. OECD supports the trend baseline, level-direction distinction, non-quantitative growth interpretation, and single-month limitations. Both examples are independently constructed.
