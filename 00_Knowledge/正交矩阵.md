# Orthogonal matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正交矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An orthogonal matrix turns or reflects vectors without stretching them. If you apply the same matrix to two nonzero vectors, their lengths and their angle stay the same. The matrix is a real square array, and its columns form a new set of perpendicular unit directions. This makes it useful for changing coordinates without distorting the geometry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正交矩阵可以转动或反射向量，而不把它们拉长或压短。把同一个正交矩阵作用于两个非零向量，它们的长度与夹角保持不变。它是实数方阵，各列给出一组彼此垂直的单位方向，因此适合在不扭曲几何关系的情况下变换坐标。

> [!example] A quarter-turn / 转动四分之一圈
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $Q=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$. It sends $(3,4)^T$ to $(-4,3)^T$: the arrow turns counterclockwise by $90^\circ$ and its length remains 5. Multiplication by $Q^T=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$ turns it back. The superscript $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]]: exchange rows and columns.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $Q=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$，它把 $(3,4)^T$ 变成 $(-4,3)^T$：箭头逆时针转过 $90^\circ$，长度仍为5。再乘 $Q^T=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$ 就转回去。上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，即交换行与列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the matrix identity says
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 矩阵等式在说什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real $n\times n$ matrix $Q=[q_1\ \cdots\ q_n]$, the vectors $q_j$ are its columns. Their [[向量点积|dot products]] fill the entries of $Q^TQ$: entry $(i,j)$ is $q_i^Tq_j$. A [[标准正交组|set of orthonormal vectors]] consists of unit vectors with zero dot product between distinct members. Thus $Q$ is **orthogonal** exactly when
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实数 $n\times n$ 方阵 $Q=[q_1\ \cdots\ q_n]$，$q_j$ 是各列。各列的[[向量点积|点积]]组成 $Q^TQ$：第 $(i,j)$ 项为 $q_i^Tq_j$。[[标准正交组]]要求每个向量长度为1、不同向量的点积为零。因此，$Q$ 为**正交矩阵**恰好意味着

$$
Q^TQ=I_n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $I_n$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]], with ones on the diagonal and zeros elsewhere. The diagonal entries say that every column has squared length 1; the off-diagonal entries say that different columns are perpendicular. Merely perpendicular columns are insufficient: $2I_n$ doubles every length and satisfies $(2I_n)^T(2I_n)=4I_n$, not $I_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$I_n$ 是[[单位矩阵]]，对角线为1，其余位置为0。对角线条件要求每列的长度平方为1；非对角线条件要求不同列垂直。只有列间垂直还不够，例如 $2I_n$ 会把每个长度翻倍，并且 $(2I_n)^T(2I_n)=4I_n$，不是 $I_n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why transposing reverses the transformation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么转置就能反向变换

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Orthonormal columns are [[线性无关|linearly independent]]. Since there are $n$ of them in $\mathbb R^n$, they form a basis and $Q$ has an [[逆矩阵|inverse]]. The equation $Q^TQ=I_n$ then gives $Q^T=Q^{-1}$, and multiplying in the other order also gives $QQ^T=I_n$. Consequently the rows, as well as the columns, are orthonormal. For real square matrices, the following conditions are equivalent:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准正交的列[[线性无关]]；它们在 $\mathbb R^n$ 中共有 $n$ 个，构成一组基，因此 $Q$ 有[[逆矩阵]]。由 $Q^TQ=I_n$ 得 $Q^T=Q^{-1}$，反过来相乘也有 $QQ^T=I_n$。所以不仅列，行也标准正交。对实方阵，下面条件等价：

$$
Q^TQ=I_n\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad QQ^T=I_n\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad Q^{-1}=Q^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For vectors $x,y\in\mathbb R^n$, associating the products gives $(Qx)^T(Qy)=x^TQ^TQy=x^Ty$. Setting $y=x$ proves $\|Qx\|_2^2=\|x\|_2^2$, so [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]] is preserved. Applying the same identity to $x-y$ preserves distances. For nonzero vectors, the angle is determined by the dot product divided by the two lengths, so it is preserved too. Reflections are allowed: $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ flips the second coordinate without changing lengths. An orthogonal matrix need not be symmetric; the quarter-turn example has $Q^T\ne Q$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $x,y\in\mathbb R^n$，结合矩阵乘法有 $(Qx)^T(Qy)=x^TQ^TQy=x^Ty$。取 $y=x$ 得 $\|Qx\|_2^2=\|x\|_2^2$，所以[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]不变。对 $x-y$ 应用同一关系，可见距离也不变。两个非零向量的夹角由点积除以两个长度决定，因此夹角同样保留。反射也可以：$\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ 翻转第二个坐标，却不改长度。正交矩阵不必对称；前面的四分之一圈旋转就满足 $Q^T\ne Q$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A tall matrix has a different role
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 高矩阵的作用不同

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The term “orthogonal matrix” here means a square real matrix. A tall $m\times r$ matrix with $r<m$ may still have orthonormal columns and satisfy $Q^TQ=I_r$. It preserves lengths of input vectors in $\mathbb R^r$, but maps them into an $r$-dimensional part of $\mathbb R^m$ and has no two-sided inverse. For example, $Q=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ sends $(a,b)$ to $(a,b,0)$. Here $QQ^T=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0)$ projects onto the horizontal plane; it is not $I_3$. This is the [[标准正交基投影公式|orthonormal-basis projection formula]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里“正交矩阵”指实数方阵。高的 $m\times r$ 矩阵在 $r<m$ 时仍可有标准正交列，并满足 $Q^TQ=I_r$。它保留 $\mathbb R^r$ 输入向量的长度，但只把它们送进 $\mathbb R^m$ 的一个 $r$ 维部分，没有双侧逆。例如 $Q=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ 把 $(a,b)$ 送到 $(a,b,0)$，而 $QQ^T=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0)$ 是投影到水平面的矩阵，不是 $I_3$。这正是[[标准正交基投影公式]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For complex entries, length preservation uses the conjugate transpose $Q^*$, which transposes and conjugates each entry. The corresponding square matrix satisfies $Q^*Q=I$ and is [[Unitary 矩阵|unitary]]. The real definition above is its real-valued case; replacing $Q^*$ by $Q^T$ for complex data generally changes the condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若矩阵含复数，保长度的条件使用共轭转置 $Q^*$，即转置并对每项取复共轭。对应方阵满足 $Q^*Q=I$，称为[[Unitary 矩阵|酉矩阵]]。上面的实数定义是其特例；处理复数时，通常不能把 $Q^*$ 换成 $Q^T$。
