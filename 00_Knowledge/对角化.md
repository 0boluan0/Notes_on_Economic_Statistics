# Diagonalization
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*对角化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix can mix the coordinates of a vector even when its action is simple along other directions. **Diagonalization** finds a coordinate system in which each coordinate is multiplied by its own number, without receiving contributions from the other coordinates. The transformation of vectors stays the same; the coordinates used to describe it change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵会混合向量的各个坐标，但它沿另一些方向的作用可能很简单。**对角化**寻找这样一套坐标：每个坐标只乘自己的一个数，不接收其他坐标的贡献。向量所经历的变换保持不变，改变的是描述它的坐标。

> [!example] Two directions that do not mix · 两个不互相混合的方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In ordinary coordinates, the matrix $A$ below mixes both entries. However, multiplying the two displayed column vectors gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在通常坐标下，下面的矩阵 $A$ 会混合两个分量。但把它乘在这两个列向量上，得到
>
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad x_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad x_2=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad Ax_1=3x_1,\quad Ax_2=x_2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus $x_1$ is stretched by $3$ and $x_2$ is unchanged. These are [[特征对|eigenvectors]], meaning nonzero vectors for which $Ax=\lambda x$; the multiplier $\lambda$ is the corresponding eigenvalue. Here the two directions form a [[基|basis]]: every vector has a unique expression $v=c_1x_1+c_2x_2$. Consequently, $Av=3c_1x_1+c_2x_2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 所以 $x_1$ 被伸长为原来的 $3$ 倍，$x_2$ 不变。它们是[[特征对|特征向量]]，即满足 $Ax=\lambda x$ 的非零向量；乘数 $\lambda$ 是对应的特征值。这两个方向组成一组[[基]]：每个向量都能唯一写成 $v=c_1x_1+c_2x_2$。因此，$Av=3c_1x_1+c_2x_2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the three matrix factors as coordinate operations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把三个矩阵因子读成坐标操作*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Put the basis vectors into the columns of $S$, and put their multipliers into the matching positions of a [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $\Lambda$. A diagonal matrix has zero entries away from its main diagonal. In this example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把基向量放进 $S$ 的各列，再把对应乘数放进[[对角矩阵]] $\Lambda$ 的相应位置。对角矩阵在主对角线以外的元素全部为零。本例中，

$$S=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad S^{-1}=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad \Lambda=\begin{pmatrix}3&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad A=S\Lambda S^{-1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[逆矩阵|inverse]] $S^{-1}$ recovers the coefficients $c$ from $v=Sc$. Reading the product from right to left, $S^{-1}$ finds the new coordinates, $\Lambda$ multiplies them separately, and $S$ reconstructs the vector in ordinary coordinates. For $v=(4,2)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], the new coordinates are $c=(3,1)^T$. They become $(9,1)^T$, and reconstruction gives $Av=(10,8)^T$. Direct [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] gives the same result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[逆矩阵]] $S^{-1}$ 从 $v=Sc$ 中找回系数 $c$。从右向左读这个乘积：$S^{-1}$ 找出新坐标，$\Lambda$ 分别乘上各自的数，$S$ 再把向量还原成通常坐标。对 $v=(4,2)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，新坐标是 $c=(3,1)^T$；它们变成 $(9,1)^T$，还原后得到 $Av=(10,8)^T$。直接做[[矩阵乘法]]也得到同一结果。

## The definition and the matching columns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定义与列的配对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a field $\mathbb F$, the allowed scalars, such as the real numbers $\mathbb R$ or complex numbers $\mathbb C$. Let $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$ with integer $n\ge1$. We say $A$ is **diagonalizable over $\mathbb F$** if there is an invertible $n\times n$ matrix $S$ over that same field such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定数域 $\mathbb F$，即允许使用的标量，例如实数 $\mathbb R$ 或复数 $\mathbb C$。设 $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$，整数 $n\ge1$。若存在同一数域上的可逆 $n\times n$ 矩阵 $S$，使

$$S^{-1}AS=\Lambda,\qquad \Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n),$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
then $A$ is diagonalizable; finding such $S$ and $\Lambda$ is a diagonalization. This is a [[相似矩阵|similarity transformation]], so the same linear action is expressed in a new basis. Multiplying the equality by $S$ gives $AS=S\Lambda$. If $S=[x_1\ \cdots\ x_n]$, comparison of column $i$ gives $Ax_i=\lambda_i x_i$. Thus the columns of $S$ must be an eigenvector basis, with their eigenvalues in the same order. The [[可对角化判据|diagonalizability criterion]] explains how to decide whether enough independent eigenvectors exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $\Lambda$ 为对角矩阵，就称 $A$ 在该域上可对角化；找出这样的 $S$ 与 $\Lambda$，就是进行对角化。这是[[相似矩阵|相似变换]]：同一个线性作用改用另一组基来表示。把等式左乘 $S$，得到 $AS=S\Lambda$。若 $S=[x_1\ \cdots\ x_n]$，比较第 $i$ 列就得到 $Ax_i=\lambda_i x_i$。因此，$S$ 的列必须是一组特征向量基，特征值也必须按相同顺序排列。怎样判断独立特征向量是否足够，见[[可对角化判据]]。

> [!note] Zero eigenvalues are allowed · 允许零特征值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Only $S$ must be invertible; $A$ need not be. Every diagonal matrix is already diagonalized by the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $S=I_n$. Examples include $\operatorname{diag}(2,-1)$, $\operatorname{diag}(2,0)$, and the zero matrix. The basis vectors need not have unit length or be mutually perpendicular.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 要求可逆的是 $S$，不是 $A$。任何对角矩阵都已经能用[[单位矩阵]] $S=I_n$ 完成对角化，例如 $\operatorname{diag}(2,-1)$、$\operatorname{diag}(2,0)$ 和零矩阵。基向量也不必是单位向量，或彼此垂直。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Swapping two columns of $S$ is allowed if the corresponding two diagonal entries of $\Lambda$ are swapped as well. Rescaling a column by a nonzero scalar is also allowed. These choices explain why a diagonalization need not be unique. Deleting off-diagonal entries of $A$ would instead change the transformation; [[对角化与行化简|row reduction]] also generally changes it and is a different operation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以交换 $S$ 的两列，但也要交换 $\Lambda$ 中对应的两个对角元；也可以把某一列乘上非零标量。这些选择说明对角化结果不必唯一。直接删掉 $A$ 的非对角元会改变原变换；[[对角化与行化简|行化简]]通常也会改变它，是另一种操作。
