# Quesnay 的《经济表》用阶级支付追踪年度再生产
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Quesnay's Tableau traces annual reproduction through payments among classes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 循环模型
> Quesnay 的 *Tableau économique* 把国民分为生产阶级、所有者阶级和“不生产”阶级，用一连串购买与支付展示：农业年度再生产怎样补回耕作预付、产生所有者收入，又怎样通过各阶级支出回到农产品、原料和制成品的购买，使下一年能按假定规模重新开始。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Quesnay's *Tableau économique* divides the nation into a productive class, a proprietor class, and a “sterile” class. A sequence of purchases and payments shows how annual agricultural reproduction restores cultivation advances, generates proprietors' revenue, and circulates through expenditure on food, raw materials, and manufactures so that the next year can begin again at the assumed scale.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **生产阶级：** 耕作者预付农业费用，出售农业产物，补回预付并支付土地收入。
- **所有者阶级：** 地主、君主和什一税领取者取得净产品形成的收入，再把它支出到农产品与制成品。
- **“不生产”阶级：** 手工业者、商人及其他非农业劳动者购买农产品和原料，并出售加工品与服务。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Productive class:** cultivators advance agricultural expenses, sell agricultural produce, restore advances, and pay land revenue.
- **Proprietor class:** landowners, the sovereign, and tithe recipients receive income formed from the net product and spend it on agricultural and manufactured goods.
- **“Sterile” class:** artisans, merchants, and other non-agricultural workers buy food and raw materials and sell transformed goods and services.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

《经济表》不是对法国经济逐笔测量得到的现代投入产出表。它是一个给定产出、价格、支出比例与阶级行为的理想化算术图式，用来检验某种支出路径是否恢复预付和年度再生产。它的贡献在于把孤立交易重排成相互依赖的循环；它的数值结论不能脱离这些假设直接外推。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The *Tableau* is not a modern input–output table estimated transaction by transaction from the French economy. It is a stylised arithmetic scheme with given output, prices, expenditure shares, and class behaviour, used to ask whether a spending path restores advances and annual reproduction. Its contribution is to reorganise isolated exchanges into an interdependent circuit; its numerical conclusions cannot be extrapolated without its assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 所有者阶级收到土地收入后为什么还必须出现在《经济表》的循环中？
>
> **答案：** 它对农产品和制成品的支出把收入送回生产阶级与“不生产”阶级；若支出路径改变，预付与下一轮再生产能否恢复也会改变。
