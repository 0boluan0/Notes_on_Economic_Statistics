# 纯 AK 中永久提高储蓄率会提高长期增长率并减少当期消费
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Permanently increasing the saving rate in pure AK raises long-run growth and reduces current consumption.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在采用外生常储蓄率的纯 [[AK模型]] 中，固定 $A>0$、折旧率 $\delta$ 和人口增长率 $n$，人均增长率为 $g=sA-\delta-n$。若储蓄率从 $s_0$ 永久提高至 $s_1>s_0$，增长率永久提高 $A(s_1-s_0)$；但改变发生时，既有资本和产出没有跳变，人均消费立即从 $(1-s_0)Ak$ 降至 $(1-s_1)Ak$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In pure [[AK模型|AK]] with an exogenous constant saving rate, fixed $A>0$, depreciation $\delta$ and population growth $n$, per-capita growth is $g=sA-\delta-n$. Permanently raising saving from $s_0$ to $s_1>s_0$ permanently raises growth by $A(s_1-s_0)$. Existing capital and output do not jump at the change, but consumption per person immediately falls from $(1-s_0)Ak$ to $(1-s_1)Ak$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键是单位资本的产出 $A$ 不随积累下降，新增储蓄对增长率的作用不会被逐渐降低的回报抵消。这和 [[储蓄率的水平效应|标准 Solow 中储蓄率提高的水平与转型效应]] 不同：标准 Solow 经济到达新的平衡增长路径后，人均增长率仍由外生技术增长率决定。这里的比较以各自模型的条件为前提。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Output per unit of capital remains $A$, so declining returns do not gradually offset the growth effect of additional saving. This differs from [[储蓄率的水平效应|the level and transition effects of saving in standard Solow]]: after reaching its new balanced path, per-capita growth there is again determined by exogenous technological growth. Each comparison is conditional on its own model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用 [[AK人均增长条件]] 的构造例子：$A=0.5$、$\delta=0.05$、$n=0.02$，改变时 $k=10$，所以 $y=5$。把 $s$ 从 $0.20$ 提高至 $0.24$，人均总投资从 $1$ 增至 $1.2$，人均消费从 $4$ 降至 $3.8$，人均增长率从每年 $3\%$ 提高至 $5\%$。这是增加当前投资以换取更快积累的例子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the constructed example in [[AK人均增长条件|the AK growth condition]], let $A=0.5$, $\delta=0.05$, $n=0.02$ and initial $k=10$, giving $y=5$. Raising $s$ from $0.20$ to $0.24$ raises gross investment per person from $1$ to $1.2$, lowers consumption from $4$ to $3.8$, and raises per-capita growth from $3\%$ to $5\%$ annually. More current investment finances faster accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增长更快尚未证明这次改变提高跨期福利：需要评价当前消费损失、未来消费收益以及贴现偏好。外生储蓄版没有给出这样的效用目标。若家庭已经在最优化，储蓄率须由 [[AK闭合方式|偏好与回报共同决定]]，不能再把它作为可任意调高的独立参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Faster growth alone does not establish higher intertemporal welfare. That requires valuing current consumption losses, future gains and discounting, which the exogenous-saving version does not specify. Under household optimisation, [[AK闭合方式|preferences and returns jointly determine saving]]; saving is no longer a freely adjustable independent parameter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
