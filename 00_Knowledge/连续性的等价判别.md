# Equivalent criteria for continuity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*连续性的等价判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To check a function for continuity, we can control small errors, pull back open regions, or follow convergent input sequences. For maps between metric spaces these tests give exactly the same answer. They are useful in different situations: a formula may give an explicit error bound, a set description may make preimages easy, and one badly behaved sequence can disprove continuity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验函数连续，可以控制小误差，可以查看开区域的原像，也可以跟踪收敛输入序列。对于度量空间之间的映射，这些判据给出完全相同的答案。各自适合不同情境：公式可能容易给出误差界，集合描述可能方便求原像，而一条表现异常的序列就能否定连续性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $(X,d_X)$ and $(Y,d_Y)$ be [[度量空间|metric spaces]], and let $f:X\to Y$. The [[连续性|topological definition of continuity]] asks for open preimages. The notation $x_n\to x$ means [[序列收敛|convergence in the specified metric]]: for each positive error tolerance, all sufficiently late terms lie within that distance of $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $(X,d_X)$、$(Y,d_Y)$ 是[[度量空间]]，$f:X\to Y$。[[连续性|拓扑意义下的连续定义]]要求开集的原像开。记号 $x_n\to x$ 表示[[序列收敛|在指定度量下收敛]]：对每个正的误差容限，所有足够靠后的项都落在距 $x$ 小于该容限的范围里。

> [!important] Four equivalent tests · 四种等价判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The following statements are equivalent for $f:X\to Y$ between metric spaces.
>
> **Error control.** For every $x\in X$ and $\varepsilon>0$, there exists $\delta>0$ such that every $z\in X$ satisfies
> $$d_X(z,x)<\delta\quad\Longrightarrow\quad d_Y(f(z),f(x))<\varepsilon.$$
> **Open preimages.** For each open $V\subseteq Y$, $f^{-1}(V)$ is open in $X$.
>
> **Closed preimages.** For each closed $F\subseteq Y$, $f^{-1}(F)$ is closed in $X$.
>
> **Sequences.** For every $x\in X$ and every sequence $(x_n)$ in $X$, $x_n\to x$ implies $f(x_n)\to f(x)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对度量空间之间的 $f:X\to Y$，四个条件等价：每个点、每个输出容限 $\varepsilon>0$ 都有输入容限 $\delta>0$ 满足上式；每个开集的原像开；每个闭集的原像闭；每条收敛到 $x$ 的输入序列都给出收敛到 $f(x)$ 的输出序列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The order is essential: choose the point $x$ and desired output tolerance $\varepsilon$ first, then find $\delta$. The same $\delta$ must work for every input $z$ in that ball, but it may depend on $x$ and $\varepsilon$. Requiring it to work simultaneously for all $x$ is the stronger condition called [[一致连续|uniform continuity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

量词顺序很重要：先确定点 $x$ 和想要的输出容限 $\varepsilon$，再寻找 $\delta$。同一个 $\delta$ 必须对球内每个输入 $z$ 都有效，但它可以依赖 $x$ 与 $\varepsilon$。若要求它同时适用于所有 $x$，就变成更强的“[[一致连续]]”条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the neighbourhood and error tests agree
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*邻域与误差判据为何等价*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume error control. Take an open $V\subseteq Y$ and $x\in f^{-1}(V)$. Because $f(x)\in V$ and $V$ is open, some output ball $B_Y(f(x),\varepsilon)$ lies in $V$. Error control supplies an input ball $B_X(x,\delta)$ mapped into that output ball, hence lying inside $f^{-1}(V)$. Every point of the preimage has such a ball, so the preimage is open.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先假设误差控制成立。取开集 $V\subseteq Y$ 及 $x\in f^{-1}(V)$。由于 $f(x)\in V$ 且 $V$ 开，某个输出球 $B_Y(f(x),\varepsilon)$ 留在 $V$ 内。误差控制给出输入球 $B_X(x,\delta)$，其输出都进入这个输出球，因此输入球留在 $f^{-1}(V)$ 中。原像中的每个点都有这样的球，所以原像开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, suppose open preimages are open. For fixed $x$ and $\varepsilon>0$, the preimage of $B_Y(f(x),\varepsilon)$ is an open set containing $x$. It therefore contains some $B_X(x,\delta)$. This inclusion is precisely the error-control implication. Finally, open and closed preimage tests are equivalent through
$$f^{-1}(Y\setminus F)=X\setminus f^{-1}(F).$$
Taking the complement commutes with taking a preimage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，若开集原像开，对固定的 $x$ 与 $\varepsilon>0$，输出球 $B_Y(f(x),\varepsilon)$ 的原像是包含 $x$ 的开集，因此包含某个 $B_X(x,\delta)$。这个包含关系恰好就是误差控制。最后，开原像与闭原像通过上面的补集恒等式等价：先取补集再取原像，与先取原像再在定义域中取补集，得到相同集合。

## Why checking all convergent sequences is enough
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么检验所有收敛序列就够了*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $f$ is continuous at $x$ and $x_n\to x$. Given an output tolerance $\varepsilon$, continuity gives an input tolerance $\delta$. Convergence puts all terms from some index $N$ onward inside that input tolerance, so their outputs are all within $\varepsilon$ of $f(x)$. This proves $f(x_n)\to f(x)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f$ 在 $x$ 处连续且 $x_n\to x$。给定输出容限 $\varepsilon$，连续性提供输入容限 $\delta$；收敛又保证从某个编号 $N$ 起全部输入项都满足它。因此，从那以后全部输出都与 $f(x)$ 相距小于 $\varepsilon$，这就证明 $f(x_n)\to f(x)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the reverse direction, suppose continuity fails at $x$. Negating the error-control statement gives one fixed $\varepsilon_0>0$ such that every input radius $\delta>0$ admits a point $z$ with
$$d_X(z,x)<\delta\quad\text{but}\quad d_Y(f(z),f(x))\ge\varepsilon_0.$$
For each integer $n\ge1$, use radius $\delta=1/n$ to choose such a point $x_n$. Then $d_X(x_n,x)<1/n$ forces $x_n\to x$, while every output stays at least $\varepsilon_0$ away from $f(x)$. Thus the sequence test fails. The fixed positive output gap is what makes this contradiction work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逆方向用反证：若 $x$ 处连续失败，把误差控制取否定，就得到一个**固定的** $\varepsilon_0>0$，使每个输入半径 $\delta>0$ 都能找到满足上式的点。对每个整数 $n\ge1$，取 $\delta=1/n$ 选出这样的 $x_n$。距离 $d_X(x_n,x)<1/n$ 强迫 $x_n\to x$，输出却始终与 $f(x)$ 至少相距 $\varepsilon_0$，所以序列判据失败。关键是输出偏差有一个固定的正下界。

> [!example] One useful witness · 一条有效的反例序列
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $h(x)=0$ for $x<0$ and $h(x)=1$ for $x\ge0$. Choose $x_n=-1/n$. The inputs converge to $0$, but every output is $0$, whereas $h(0)=1$. This single sequence disproves continuity at $0$. Checking a few well-behaved sequences could not prove continuity: the criterion quantifies over all of them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $x<0$ 时 $h(x)=0$，$x\ge0$ 时 $h(x)=1$。取 $x_n=-1/n$，输入收敛到 $0$，输出却一直为 $0$，而 $h(0)=1$。这一条序列就能否定 $0$ 处连续。相反，仅检查几条表现良好的序列不能证明连续，因为判据要求对所有序列成立。

## Use the actual domain and the correct scope
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*使用真实定义域与正确适用范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f:S\to Y$ on a subset $S\subseteq X$, use the restricted metric or [[子空间拓扑|subspace topology]]. All tested points and sequences must lie in $S$, and the point of continuity must belong to $S$. For example, the identity on $[0,1]$ is continuous at $0$ with $\delta=\varepsilon$; negative inputs are irrelevant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对定义在 $S\subseteq X$ 上的 $f:S\to Y$，使用限制度量或[[子空间拓扑]]。受检点及序列都必须在 $S$ 内，连续点自身也必须属于 $S$。例如 $[0,1]$ 上的恒等函数在 $0$ 处连续，取 $\delta=\varepsilon$ 即可，负输入与此无关。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The open and closed preimage equivalence remains valid in arbitrary topological spaces, and continuity always preserves sequence limits. The reverse sequential implication is not guaranteed for arbitrary topological domains; the proof above uses a metric to choose the shrinking radii $1/n$. Also, these are preimage tests, not image tests. A constant map $\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ need not send an open set to an open set. The continuous inclusion $(0,1)\hookrightarrow\mathbb R$ sends its whole domain, which is closed relative to itself, to a set that is not closed in $\mathbb R$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在任意拓扑空间中，开原像与闭原像仍等价，连续也总会保持序列极限。但对任意拓扑定义域，反方向的序列推连续没有普遍保证；上面的证明用度量选取不断缩小的半径 $1/n$。此外，这些都是原像判据，并非像的判据。常值映射 $\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ 未必把开集映成开集；连续包含映射 $(0,1)\hookrightarrow\mathbb R$ 也把相对于自身闭的整个定义域，映成在 $\mathbb R$ 中不闭的集合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
None of the criteria asks for a derivative. The separate card [[连续不必可导|continuity need not imply differentiability]] explains that distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述判据都不要求导数存在。这个区别另见[[连续不必可导]]。
