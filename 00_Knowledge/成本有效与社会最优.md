# 最低成本实现给定减排量不等于该减排量使社会净收益最大
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Meeting a given abatement target at minimum cost does not establish that the target maximises social net benefits.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

成本有效先把环境目标固定，问怎样用最少资源达到它；社会最优还要问目标本身值不值得扩大或缩小。前者是[[成本效果分析|同一效果下比较成本]]，后者必须把多减排带来的环境收益与资源成本一起纳入评价。达到第一项并不自动达到第二项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cost-effectiveness fixes the environmental target and minimises the resources needed to meet it. Social optimality also asks whether to expand or reduce that target. The first compares [[成本效果分析|costs for a common effect]]; the second values both environmental benefits and resource costs. The first result does not establish the second.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $A$为总减排，$\mathcal A$为可行总减排量集合。先用[[成本有效减排]]定义其最低成本 $C^*(A)$，再以已经完整计入环境效益的 $B(A)$选择总量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be total abatement and $\mathcal A$ its feasible set. First use [[成本有效减排|cost-effective allocation]] to obtain $C^*(A)$; then choose the total using $B(A)$, which explicitly includes environmental benefits:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C^*(A)=\min_{\sum_i a_i=A}\sum_i C_i(a_i),
\qquad
\max_{A\in\mathcal A}\{B(A)-C^*(A)\}.
$$

各企业内点 MAC相等只解决左边；总量选择还要比较 $B'(A)$ 与 $C^{*'}(A)$，并检查[[最优污染边界|可行端点]]。这里假定成本代表[[社会机会成本]]、环境效益未遗漏或重复计算，且没有另需处理的分配权重、其他扭曲和实施限制。若目标依法固定，成本有效仍是有意义的较窄问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal interior MACs solve only the first problem. Choosing the total requires comparing $B'(A)$ with $C^{*'}(A)$ and checking [[最优污染边界|feasible boundaries]]. Costs must represent [[社会机会成本|social opportunity costs]], benefits must be complete and unduplicated, and relevant weights, distortions and implementation constraints must be addressed. A legally fixed target can still make cost-effectiveness a useful narrower question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自造例：两家容量各100，成本 $a_1^2/2$和$3a_2^2/2$。在本次比较区间 $0\le A\le120$中，最省成本分担为$(3A/4,A/4)$，故$C^*(A)=3A^2/8$。设 $B(A)=120A-A^2/4$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With capacities of one hundred per firm and costs $a_1^2/2$ and $3a_2^2/2$, the least-cost allocation on the comparison interval $0\le A\le120$ is $(3A/4,A/4)$, giving $C^*(A)=3A^2/8$. Let $B(A)=120A-A^2/4$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
B'(A)=120-\tfrac12 A,\qquad C^{*'}(A)=\tfrac34 A,\qquad A^*=96.
$$

减排96时净收益为$9216-3456=5760$；减排100虽然已按最低成本分担，净收益仍只有$9500-3750=5750$。把目标从100改为96增加净收益10。为实现100服务的共同价格75，也不同于此例最优目标对应的72。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Abatement of ninety-six gives net benefits $9,216-3,456=5,760$. Abatement of one hundred, even allocated at least cost, gives only $9,500-3,750=5,750$. Changing the target adds ten. The common price seventy-five that implements one hundred also differs from seventy-two at the optimal target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税款或配额购买款不能再机械加到资源成本上：应按[[转移支付核算]]对齐付款和收款。哪个群体承担更多，也不是由上述总额最大化自动判定的公平结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Do not mechanically add tax or allowance payments to resource costs; align payers and recipients through [[转移支付核算|transfer accounting]]. Maximising the aggregate does not settle whether burdens across groups are fair.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Montgomery (1972)，原刊扫描](https://www.mobt3ath.com/uplode/book/book-93471.pdf#page=1)，印刷 pp.395–396：明确许可证市场理论处理的是以最低成本实现外部给定的环境目标，不自动得到完整社会最优。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Printed pp.395–396 distinguish minimum-cost attainment of an externally specified environmental target from overall social optimality.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Hahn and Stavins (2011)，作者原刊 PDF](https://stavins.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum8111/files/stavins/files/hahn_stavins_in_journal_of_law_economics.pdf#page=4)，印刷 p.S269：明确区分目标的社会边际权衡与成本有效实现。两层优化及96/100例由本卡给定函数独立推导和复算。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Printed p.S269 separates the social marginal trade-off from cost-effective implementation. The nested optimisation and numerical comparison are independently derived from the stated functions.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
