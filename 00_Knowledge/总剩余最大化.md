# 在无其他扭曲且边际收益递减、边际成本递增时，总剩余的内点最大量由二者相等确定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With no other distortions, decreasing marginal benefit and increasing marginal cost determine the interior surplus-maximising quantity where they coincide.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择总剩余最大量，要比较再交易一单位的边际收益 $MB$ 与边际机会成本 $MC$：前者较大时增加数量，后者较大时减少数量；若存在内点交点，最优量在二者相等处。这个判断以 [[总剩余]] 的收益减资源成本口径为基础。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare the marginal benefit of another unit with its marginal opportunity cost: expand when MB exceeds MC and contract when MC exceeds MB. An interior optimum is their intersection. The rule uses the benefit-minus-resource-cost definition of [[总剩余|total surplus]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里固定可行数量区间 $[0,\bar Q]$，假定交易按买方估值和生产成本有效排序，$MB,MC$ 连续且前者严格递减、后者严格递增。买方货币收益须可精确解释（例如相关范围内满足 [[剩余与补偿度量|准线性内点条件]]），且没有外部性、其他市场扭曲、遗漏资源成本或随方案改变的固定成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix feasible quantities $[0,\bar Q]$ and an efficient ordering of buyers and production. Assume continuous, strictly decreasing MB and strictly increasing MC. Monetary buyer benefits must be exact, for example under the [[剩余与补偿度量|interior quasilinear conditions]]. Exclude externalities, distortions elsewhere, omitted resource costs and policy-dependent fixed costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为多一小单位的总剩余增量是 $MB-MC$，该差在上述条件下严格递减，所以由正转负的交点给出唯一最大量：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The marginal change in surplus is MB minus MC. Under these assumptions it strictly decreases, so an intersection where its sign changes from positive to negative gives the unique maximum:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
TS'(Q)=MB(Q)-MC(Q),
\qquad
MB(Q^*)=MC(Q^*)\quad(0<Q^*<\bar Q).
$$

若 $MB(0)\le MC(0)$，最优量为 $0$；若 $MB(\bar Q)\ge MC(\bar Q)$，最优量为 $\bar Q$。因此“令二者相等”只适用于内点，不应在没有可行交点时硬解。若上述单调条件失效，局部相等也不足以确认全局最大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $MB(0)\le MC(0)$, choose zero. If $MB(\bar Q)\ge MC(\bar Q)$, choose the capacity bound. Equality is an interior rule, not a reason to select an infeasible intersection. Without the monotonicity assumptions, local equality alone need not establish a global maximum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $MB=12-Q$、$MC=2+Q$、$\bar Q=8$，由 $12-Q=2+Q$ 得 $Q^*=5$。此时 $TS=\int_0^5(10-2q)\,dq=25$；数量 $4$ 或 $6$ 时都只有 $24$。竞争模型中的共同价格为 $7$，两方剩余各 $12.5$；相等条件选的是数量，总剩余最大不决定分配是否公平。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $MB=12-Q$, $MC=2+Q$ and capacity $8$, the optimum is $Q=5$ and surplus is $25$. Quantities $4$ and $6$ each yield only $24$. The competitive common price is $7$, splitting surplus equally at $12.5$ each. The marginal condition chooses quantity; maximising the total does not decide distributive fairness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
