# 存货周转率是期间明确的周转额与存货存量之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inventory turnover divides an explicitly defined period throughput measure by inventory.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评估存货管理时常用 $T=COGS/\overline I$，即营业成本除以平均存货，使成本流量与按成本计量的存量配对。若资料使用营业收入作分子，应标记收入口径，见[[存货周转的收入与成本口径]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inventory management commonly uses $T=COGS/\overline I$: cost of sales divided by average inventory, matching a cost flow with a cost-valued stock. A revenue numerator must be labelled as such; see [[存货周转的收入与成本口径|revenue versus cost inventory turnover]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

期间有 $D$ 天时，折算存货天数为 $D/T$。CPA为简便用期末存货119、年度成本2644，得次数22.2185、折算16.4278天；改用平均存货会得到另一个已声明口径，而非同一答案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $D$ days in the period, implied inventory days are $D/T$. CPA's simplified ending inventory of 119 and annual cost of 2,644 give turnover of 22.2185 and 16.4278 days. Average inventory produces a different declared measure rather than the same numerical answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解释时看原料、在制品、产成品结构及季节订单；少库存可能节约资金，也可能导致缺货。折算天数不是逐件存货年龄，更不是销售后再收回应收的完整现金周期。零存货使次数未定义，零成本使通常天数解释失效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interpret the result alongside raw materials, work in process, finished goods, and seasonal orders. Less stock may save capital or create shortages. Implied days are neither each item's age nor the full cash cycle including collection after sale. Zero inventory makes turnover undefined; zero cost invalidates the usual days interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
