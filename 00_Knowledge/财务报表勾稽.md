# 财务报表勾稽先统一主体期间与计量口径再核对跨表余额和变动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reconcile financial statements by aligning entity, period, and measurement before checking linked balances and movements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

勾稽是在同一套[[财务报表]]之间追踪同一金额及其变动解释的方法。先统一主体、合并范围、期间、币种、金额单位和期初重述口径，再核对余额，而不是靠倒挤“其他”把不一致藏起来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reconciliation traces the same amounts and their movements across a set of [[财务报表|financial statements]]. Align entity, consolidation scope, period, currency, scale, and opening restatements before comparing balances; do not conceal mismatches in an unexplained “other” plug.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先查[[会计恒等式|资产＝负债＋权益]]，再追两条跨表桥：[[现金流量表]]的期初余额加三类净现金流、汇率影响及确有依据的其他调节，应得到同口径期末现金及现金等价物；[[所有者权益变动表]]应将调整后期初权益、损益、其他综合收益、所有者交易及适用的其他直接调整连接到期末权益。比较的是相同范围，不能把现金及现金等价物不经说明等同于资产负债表某一行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check the [[会计恒等式|accounting equation]], then follow two cross-statement bridges. Opening cash plus the three net-flow categories, exchange effects, and any substantiated further adjustments should reach the corresponding closing cash-equivalent balance. The [[所有者权益变动表|equity statement]] should link restated opening equity, performance, owner transactions, and applicable other direct adjustments to closing equity. Reconcile matching scopes rather than assuming one balance-sheet line automatically equals cash and cash equivalents.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面是一家无税、无期初重述的简化公司的完整核对例，所有金额单位一致，现金分配列为筹资活动。期初现金20、设备70、投资10，负债60、权益40。期间现金销售收入100、付现经营费用80、折旧5；购置设备付现8；借款收现3、增资收现6、现金分配4。外币现金产生未实现汇兑损失1并计入损益；投资另有税后其他综合收益损失2，且无其他变动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a simplified company with no taxes or opening restatements, using one currency unit throughout and classifying cash distributions as financing. Opening cash is 20, equipment 70, investments 10, liabilities 60, and equity 40. During the period it records cash sales of 100, cash operating expenses of 80, depreciation of 5, cash equipment purchases of 8, borrowing receipts of 3, new equity receipts of 6, and cash distributions of 4. Foreign-currency cash produces an unrealised exchange loss of 1 in profit or loss; the investment has a separate after-tax other-comprehensive-income loss of 2. There are no other movements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 利润：$100-80-5-1=14$；经营现金流为 $100-80=20$，亦等于 $14+5+1$，其中未实现汇兑损失加回，未伪造现金付款。
- 现金：$20+20-8+(3+6-4)-1=36$。
- 权益：$40+14-2+6-4=54$。
- 期末资产：现金36、设备 $70+8-5=73$、投资 $10-2=8$，合计117；负债 $60+3=63$，于是 $117=63+54$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Profit is $100-80-5-1=14$. Operating cash flow is $100-80=20$, also $14+5+1$: the unrealised exchange loss is reversed without inventing a cash payment.
- Closing cash is $20+20-8+(3+6-4)-1=36$.
- Closing equity is $40+14-2+6-4=54$.
- Closing assets comprise cash of 36, equipment of $70+8-5=73$, and investments of $10-2=8$, totalling 117. Liabilities are $60+3=63$, so $117=63+54$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果现金算成37，先查是否漏掉汇率调节；如果权益只算 $40+14-4=50$，则漏掉增资与其他综合收益。实际报表还可能有并购、非现金融资、重分类等调节，须从[[财务报表附注]]找具体解释。勾稽通过只说明这些关系闭合，并不单独证明估计合理、现金可自由使用或公司值得投资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash of 37 suggests an omitted exchange adjustment. Equity of $40+14-4=50$ omits the contribution and other comprehensive income. Actual reports may also require acquisition, non-cash financing, or reclassification reconciliations supported by the [[财务报表附注|notes]]. Reconciliation establishes consistency of these links, not by itself reasonable estimates, unrestricted cash, or investment merit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
