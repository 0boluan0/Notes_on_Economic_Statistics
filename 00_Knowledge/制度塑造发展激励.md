# 制度通过权利执行交易成本与权力约束塑造发展激励
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Institutions shape development incentives through rights, enforcement, transaction costs, and constraints on power*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心机制
> 制度是规范行为的正式与非正式规则及其执行方式。它们决定投资与创新的收益能否保留、合同能否执行、交易与纠纷成本有多高，以及政治权力能否任意没收资产或排除市场进入。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Institutions are formal and informal rules, together with their enforcement. They shape whether returns to investment and innovation can be retained, contracts enforced, transactions and disputes resolved at tolerable cost, and political power prevented from arbitrarily confiscating assets or blocking entry.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个概念不等于一个治理排名。产权安全要说清保护谁、针对什么风险以及如何执行；[[国家能力]]要区分征税、信息、执法与公共品供给。没有执行能力的权利可能只在纸上；没有权力约束的高能力又可能扩大掠夺。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The concept is not one governance score. Property security must specify whose rights are protected, against which risks, and by what enforcement; [[国家能力|state capacity]] must distinguish taxation, information, law enforcement, and public-goods provision. Rights without enforcement can remain nominal, while capacity without constraints on power can facilitate extraction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

制度还会改变分配，所以“社会总收益更高”不足以预测哪套规则会被选择。既有规则的受益者可能有动机和权力阻止改革。因此制度机制同时需要激励与政治权力两条证据链。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Institutions also redistribute resources, so a rule with higher aggregate benefits need not be selected. Beneficiaries of existing arrangements may have both the motive and the power to block reform. An institutional explanation therefore needs evidence about incentives and political power, not just an efficiency label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 最小自检
> 为什么“法律已承认产权”还不足以说明投资激励已改善？
>
> **答案：** 还要检查登记、法院或其他纠纷解决是否可达，执行是否一致，以及政府或强势主体能否绕过规则。
