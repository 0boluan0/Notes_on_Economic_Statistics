# 相同均值和基尼系数可以对应不同且相交的洛伦兹曲线

两个分布即使平均收入和[[基尼系数]]都相同，收入在不同位置的配置仍可能不同。基尼只保留[[洛伦兹曲线]]与平等线之间的总面积；不同区间的面积差可相互抵消。因此“基尼相同”不能推出“底部各群体的收入份额相同”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two distributions can have the same mean income and [[基尼系数|Gini coefficient]] while allocating income differently across the distribution. The Gini retains only the total area between the [[洛伦兹曲线|Lorenz curve]] and equality. Area differences over separate intervals can offset, so equal Ginis do not imply equal income shares for each lower-income group.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较两个等权四人分布：甲为 $10,20,30,40$，乙为 $8,24,28,40$。总收入都为 100，均值都为 25。各自六个无序配对的绝对差之和都为 100，所以在同一未作有限样本修正的口径下，二者均有 $G=0.25$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare equally weighted four-person distributions A: $10,20,30,40$ and B: $8,24,28,40$. Both total 100 and average 25. In each, the six unordered absolute pair differences sum to 100. Both therefore have $G=0.25$ under the same convention without a finite-sample correction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 累计人口份额 | 甲的累计收入份额 | 乙的累计收入份额 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 最低 25% | 10% | 8% |
| 最低 50% | 30% | 32% |
| 最低 75% | 60% | 60% |
| 全体 | 100% | 100% |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Cumulative population share | A: cumulative income share | B: cumulative income share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bottom 25% | 10% | 8% |
| Bottom 50% | 30% | 32% |
| Bottom 75% | 60% | 60% |
| Everyone | 100% | 100% |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乙的最低四分之一处境更差，但最低一半合计获得的份额更高，曲线因而相交。比较具体群体，应检查相应份额或原始分布；单一基尼值无法替你选择关注的群体。反过来，曲线相交不保证基尼相等，本例只是证明相等仍不足以恢复分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bottom quarter in B is worse off, while its bottom half collectively receives a larger share, so the curves cross. Group-specific questions require the relevant shares or the underlying distribution; one Gini value cannot select the group of interest. Conversely, crossing curves need not have equal Ginis. This example establishes only that equal Ginis cannot recover distribution shape.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
