# 在剩余价值率与周转口径不变时较高的资本价值构成对应较低的利润率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Higher value composition corresponds to a lower profit rate when the surplus-value rate and turnover basis are unchanged.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用[[Marx利润率]]中利润等于该资本生产的剩余价值、一次可变资本周转和统一预付估值的条件，令 $x=s/v_a>0$、$j=c_a/v_a\ge0$，便有 $p'=x/(1+j)$。若 $x$ 不变，$j$ 增大使分母增大，利润率随之降低；这是固定其他比率条件的比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the conditions stated in [[Marx利润率|Marx's profit rate]]: profit equals surplus value produced by the capital, the period is one variable-capital turnover, and advances use consistent valuation. With $x=s/v_a>0$ and $j=c_a/v_a\ge0$, $p'=x/(1+j)$. Holding $x$ fixed, a higher $j$ enlarges the denominator and lowers the rate. This is a comparison conditional on the other ratio remaining unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把可变资本预付统一为20，逐项比较三种构造条件：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set the variable-capital advance at 20 and compare three constructed cases:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 条件 | $c_a$ | $v_a$ | $s$ | $x$ | $j$ | $p'$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 初始比较 | 80 | 20 | 20 | 1 | 4 | 20% |
| 仅提高 $j$ | 180 | 20 | 20 | 1 | 9 | 10% |
| 同时提高 $x$ | 180 | 20 | 40 | 2 | 9 | 20% |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

| Case | $c_a$ | $v_a$ | $s$ | $x$ | $j$ | $p'$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Initial case | 80 | 20 | 20 | 1 | 4 | 20% |
| Higher $j$ only | 180 | 20 | 20 | 1 | 9 | 10% |
| Higher $x$ as well | 180 | 20 | 40 | 2 | 9 | 20% |

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二行说明保持 $x=1$ 时的构成效应；第三行说明 $x$ 同时变化可以抵消它。三行可以比较不同资本或不同条件，不预先声称某一家工厂必然依次经历这三个状态。若比较年利润率，还须保持周转次数相同或把各自的次数计入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second row isolates composition while holding $x=1$; the third shows that a simultaneous change in $x$ can offset the effect. The rows may compare different capitals or conditions and do not assert that one factory necessarily passes through them in order. Annual comparisons also require equal turnover counts or an explicit adjustment for each count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要把这个条件比较变成关于历史变化的论证，还需解释[[资本价值构成]]为什么变化、[[资本有机构成|价值比例怎样反映技术变化]]、剩余价值率与周转怎样响应，以及[[利润率反作用机制]]的力度。单独的分式没有给出这些变量的时间路径，也没有提供制度何时结束的推论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Turning this comparison into an argument about historical change requires explaining why [[资本价值构成|value composition]] changes, [[资本有机构成|how value proportions reflect technical change]], how surplus-value rates and turnover respond, and the strength of [[利润率反作用机制|counteracting mechanisms]]. The fraction alone provides neither those time paths nor a conclusion about when an institutional system ends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
