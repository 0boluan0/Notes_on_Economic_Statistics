# 庇古税是以边际外部损害为校准依据的矫正税
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Pigouvian tax is a corrective tax calibrated to marginal external damage.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

庇古税让造成外部损害的活动承担相应的边际代价，使行动者不再只按自己的收益和成本作选择。其第一最佳基准是：**对同一计量的活动，单位税率取社会最优活动水平处的边际外部损害**。它属于[[外部性内部化|用行为相关价格内部化外部性]]，不是看到污染就任意加一个税。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A Pigouvian tax adds the relevant external cost to the actor's marginal decision. In the first-best benchmark, the unit rate equals marginal external damage at the socially efficient activity level, measured on the same basis. It uses a [[外部性内部化|behaviour-linked price to internalise the externality]], rather than an arbitrary charge on anything polluting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $e$ 是可核实的排放，$B(e)$ 是允许排放带来的净私人收益，$D(e)$ 是外部损害。没有其他遗漏扭曲、货币得失可一致加总、$B$ 严格凹且最优解在内点时，社会条件是 $B'(e^*)=D'(e^*)$。征恒定单位税 $t^*$ 后，私人最大化 $B(e)-t^*e$，因此
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $e$ be verifiable emissions, $B(e)$ the associated net private benefit and $D(e)$ external damage. With no omitted distortions, consistent monetary accounting, strictly concave $B$ and an interior optimum, the social condition is $B'(e^*)=D'(e^*)$. The taxed actor maximises $B(e)-t^*e$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$t^*=D'(e^*),\qquad B'(e)=t^*\ \Longrightarrow\ e=e^*.$$

例如 $B(e)=100e-e^2/2$、$D(e)=e^2/2$、$0\le e\le100$。私人原本选 100；社会条件 $100-e=e$ 给出 50；税率 50 使私人条件变成 $100-e=50$，也选择 50。若这里还把 $e$ 称为产量，必须另加[[产量、排放与减排|一单位产出对应一单位排放]]的假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $B(e)=100e-e^2/2$, $D(e)=e^2/2$ and $0\le e\le100$, the untaxed choice is 100. The social condition gives 50, and a rate of 50 induces that choice. Calling $e$ output additionally requires an explicit [[产量、排放与减排|one-for-one output–emissions relationship]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**最优点对齐不等于整条曲线重合。** 固定税率使私人边际成本增加 $t^*$；若边际损害随排放而变，校准保证在 $e^*$ 处这个增量等于当处损害，不保证在其他数量处也相等。恒定边际损害的课堂图可以整体平移，不能无条件推广。零排放等[[最优污染边界|角点]]、既有税楔或市场势力也需要重写条件，不能机械套用内点公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Matching at the optimum does not make entire marginal-cost curves coincide. A constant rate adds $t^*$ everywhere; calibration guarantees equality with marginal damage at the intended optimum, not at every other quantity. Whole-curve translation applies to the constant-damage classroom case. [[最优污染边界|Corners]], existing taxes or market power require the conditions to be reconsidered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际[[排污费]]可能只为达到某个减排目标定价，未必已按边际损害校准。至于税收总额和受害者是否获赔，另见[[庇古税与损害赔偿]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[排污费|emissions charge]] may be set to meet a target without being calibrated to marginal damage. [[庇古税与损害赔偿|Tax revenue and compensation]] are separate questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
