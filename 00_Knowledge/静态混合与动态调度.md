# 静态数据混合保持抽样规则不变，动态数据调度随训练进程改变规则；只有带有有序学习原则的调度才是课程学习
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A static mixture keeps its sampling rule fixed, while a dynamic schedule changes it during training; only a schedule with an ordered learning principle is curriculum learning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 三个名称
> 静态 mixture 在整个训练中使用同一来源权重和抽样规则。动态 data schedule 让权重、来源集合或样本选择随训练步改变。curriculum learning（课程学习）还要求这种变化承担一个有序学习原则，例如由易到难；并非所有动态调度都是 curriculum。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A static mixture uses one set of source weights and sampling rules throughout training. A dynamic data schedule changes weights, source sets, or sample selection with training step. Curriculum learning adds an ordered learning principle, such as easy-to-hard progression. Not every dynamic schedule is a curriculum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 用分布写出区别
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Writing the distinction as distributions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若来源内分布为 $q_i(x)$，固定权重 $w_i$ 给出

$$
q_t(x)=\sum_i w_iq_i(x),
$$

且右侧不随训练步 $t$ 改变，就是静态混合。若权重或来源内选择变为 $w_i(t)$、$q_i(x,t)$，则

$$
q_t(x)=\sum_i w_i(t)q_i(x,t)
$$

是动态调度。这个公式只说明“变了”，不说明变化是否按难度或学习阶段组织。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With source distributions $q_i(x)$, a fixed $q_t(x)=\sum_iw_iq_i(x)$ is static when its right-hand side does not change with step $t$. A schedule with $w_i(t)$ or $q_i(x,t)$ is dynamic. The notation says that the distribution changes; it does not say that the change follows difficulty or another learning order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

后期提高高质量数学数据、引入新鲜数据或做领域强化，都可能是动态调度。若没有明确的学习顺序，称为 annealing、阶段性上采样或动态 mixture 比笼统称作 curriculum 更准确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Late upweighting of high-quality mathematics, adding fresh data, or domain specialization can all be dynamic schedules. Without an explicit learning order, labels such as annealing, staged upsampling, or dynamic mixture are more precise than calling every case a curriculum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 最小例子
> 前 90% 训练步使用固定通用 mixture，最后 10% 上调高质量数学与代码数据。它确定是动态调度；若论文称最后阶段为 annealing，可以沿用。仅凭这些信息还不能确定它是 curriculum。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first 90% of training uses a fixed general mixture, and the last 10% upweights high-quality mathematics and code. This is certainly a dynamic schedule and may be called annealing when the study defines it so. The description alone does not establish a curriculum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 全程使用 70% web、20% code、10% math 的随机等长 block 抽样，是 curriculum 吗？
>
> **答案：** 不是。它是静态 mixture；来源不同不等于样本呈现按训练进程有序变化。

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Grattafiori et al. (2024), *The Llama 3 Herd of Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21783)：第 3.1.2 节报告主 mixture，第 3.1.3 节把上调特定高质量数据的 annealing 另列为后期阶段。
- [Bengio et al. (2009), *Curriculum Learning*](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2009/papers/119.pdf)：把 curriculum 表述为组织样本选择与顺序，并让训练分布从较易样本逐步接近目标分布。
