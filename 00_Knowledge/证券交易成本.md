# 证券交易成本包括完成交易相对明确基准的显性费用和隐性执行代价

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Securities trading costs include explicit charges and implicit execution costs relative to a stated benchmark.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证券交易成本是实现证券买卖所付出的显性费用和隐性执行代价。佣金、交易所收费和适用交易税是可单列的显性费用；[[买卖价差]]、[[市场冲击]]、延迟及未成交的机会代价属于隐性部分。谈“成本多少”前必须说明基准价格、观察时限和订单是否完全成交。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Securities trading costs comprise explicit charges and implicit costs of implementing a trade. Commissions, venue fees and applicable transaction taxes are separately identifiable charges. [[买卖价差|Spreads]], [[市场冲击|market impact]], delay and missed execution contribute implicit costs. A measurement must specify its benchmark, horizon and whether the order was completed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对完全成交的简单买卖，令 $Q$ 为成交数量，$\bar P$ 为[[逐档成交均价|成交均价]]，$P_0$ 为明确基准，$F$ 为净显性费用。以买入为 $\varepsilon=1$、卖出为 $\varepsilon=-1$，可用下式记录相对该基准的执行成本；它不是含未成交机会成本的完整通式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a simple fully filled trade, let $Q$ be filled quantity, $\bar P$ the [[逐档成交均价|average execution price]], $P_0$ a stated benchmark and $F$ net explicit charges. Set $\varepsilon=1$ for a buy and $\varepsilon=-1$ for a sell. The expression below records execution cost against that benchmark, not a complete formula including unfilled-order opportunity cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C=\varepsilon Q(\bar P-P_0)+F.
$$

例如静态卖盘在 100 元有 50 股、100.20 元有 100 股，买入 120 股依次成交 50 股和 70 股，总价款 12014 元。基准为提交前中间价 99.90 元，费用 4 元，则成本为 $12014-120(99.90)+4=30$ 元。在该静态例子中，价格差的 26 元可以再拆为跨半价差的 12 元与高于原最佳卖价的 14 元；不能在 30 元上把这些项目再加一次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a static ask book offers 50 shares at 100 and 100 shares at 100.20. Buying 120 shares fills 50 and then 70, for a total of 12014. With a pre-submission midpoint benchmark of 99.90 and fees of 4, cost is $12014-120(99.90)+4=30$. In this static example, the 26 price component can be split into 12 for crossing the half-spread and 14 above the original best ask. These components must not be added to the 30 again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际相对基准的价格差可以为负，表示该口径下价格改善；所有差额也未必由自身交易造成。若订单只部分成交，不能丢掉剩余数量后声称已经衡量完整决策的成本。基准选择、市场价格变化与因果归因需要分别说明；[[金融交易成本的规模经济]]则研究规模怎样影响成本，不替代这里的成本定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The realised price difference may be negative, indicating improvement against that benchmark, and need not be entirely caused by one's own trade. Partial fills cannot be treated as the full decision by discarding the residual. Benchmark choice, market changes and causal attribution are separate issues. [[金融交易成本的规模经济|Economies of scale in financial transaction costs]] explain how scale affects costs rather than replacing this definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
