# 银行存款创造与货币供给
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bank Deposit Creation and the Money Supply*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释银行放贷时怎样同时创造贷款资产和存款负债，以及结算、资本、风险、资金成本和需求怎样限制体系扩张。
> **具体锚点：** 银行发放贷款时通常同时记一笔贷款资产和客户存款负债；它不是先把某个储户现金原封不动转给借款人。
> **核心难点：** 能记账创造存款不等于没有约束；结算资金、资本、风险、监管、定价和贷款需求都会限制扩张。
> **为什么重要：** 货币供给、银行危机、政策传导和监管资本只有放回同一资产负债表才清楚。
> **继续：** 先逐笔写贷款、支付和还款的复式记账，再区分单家银行准备金约束与全体系广义货币变化。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Explain how bank lending creates a loan asset and a deposit liability simultaneously and how settlement, capital, risk, funding costs, and demand constrain system expansion.
> **Concrete anchor:** When a bank grants a loan, it normally records a loan asset and a customer deposit liability at the same time; it does not first transfer a particular saver's cash unchanged to the borrower.
> **Central difficulty:** The ability to create deposits by accounting entry does not remove constraints. Settlement funding, capital, risk, regulation, pricing, and credit demand all limit expansion.
> **Why it matters:** Money supply, bank crises, policy transmission, and regulatory capital become clear only when placed on the same balance sheet.
> **Continue with:** Write the double-entry records for lending, payment, and repayment, then distinguish a single bank's reserve needs from changes in broad money across the banking system.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 贷款—存款创造
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Loan-and-deposit Creation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

贷款记账可创造存款，但客户支付给别行时需准备金/融资结算。银行在政策利率、资本、流动性、风险限额和有信用需求的借款人共同约束下扩表。简单存款乘数忽略这些内生反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Booking a loan can create a deposit, but when the customer pays someone at another bank, settlement requires reserves or funding. Banks expand under joint constraints from the policy rate, capital, liquidity, risk limits, and creditworthy demand. The simple deposit multiplier omits these endogenous responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单家银行放贷 100 时，资产端贷款 +100、负债端存款 +100。借款人向别行支付后，本行存款 -100、准备金 -100，收款行准备金和存款各 +100；体系总存款仍增加 100，但准备金只重新分配。若贷款本金用存款偿还，贷款和存款同时减少。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When one bank lends 100, loan assets rise by 100 and deposit liabilities rise by 100. After the borrower pays a customer at another bank, the lending bank's deposits and reserves each fall by 100, while the receiving bank's reserves and deposits each rise by 100. System-wide deposits remain 100 higher, but reserves have only been redistributed. When principal is repaid with a deposit, the loan and deposit contract together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 货币供给与中央银行
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Money Supply and the Central Bank*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中央银行设定准备金价格/数量环境和资产负债表，商业银行与公众共同决定广义货币。现金偏好、银行超额准备和贷款需求使货币乘数时变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The central bank sets the price or quantity environment for reserves and its balance sheet, while commercial banks and the public jointly determine broad money. Cash preferences, banks' reserve demand, and loan demand make the money multiplier time-varying.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在充足准备金框架中，央行可主要用准备金利率控制短期市场利率，准备金数量不再是约束贷款的固定先决投入。央行仍通过利率、资产价格、融资条件和监管环境影响存款与信贷创造，但不是逐笔分配可贷准备金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an ample-reserves framework, the central bank can control short-term market rates mainly through the remuneration of reserves; the quantity of reserves is no longer a fixed input constraining each loan. The central bank still influences deposit and credit creation through interest rates, asset prices, financing conditions, and regulation, but it does not allocate a fixed stock of lendable reserves loan by loan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：贷款、支付与还款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Lending, Payment, and Repayment*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行甲批准 100 贷款：甲的贷款 +100、借款人存款 +100。借款人向银行乙客户买设备：甲准备金 -100、甲存款 -100；乙准备金 +100、乙客户存款 +100。以后借款人从收入获得存款并偿还 20 本金时，贷款资产与存款负债各减 20；利息支付则成为银行收入并影响权益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bank A approves a loan of 100: its loans rise by 100 and the borrower's deposit rises by 100. The borrower buys equipment from a customer of Bank B: Bank A's reserves and deposits each fall by 100, while Bank B's reserves and its customer's deposit each rise by 100. Later, when the borrower obtains deposits from income and repays 20 of principal, loan assets and deposit liabilities each fall by 20; interest payment becomes bank income and affects equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“银行只能贷出已有存款”忽略贷款创造存款；“银行能无限创造钱”忽略资本、结算和风险；“准备金增加十倍所以贷款必增十倍”把条件性教学恒等式当因果机制；“银行创造财富”又混淆新货币负债与借款人同时新增债务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Banks can lend only pre-existing deposits” ignores deposit creation through lending. “Banks can create unlimited money” ignores capital, settlement, and risk. “Ten times more reserves must create ten times more loans” mistakes a conditional teaching identity for a causal mechanism. “Banks create wealth” confuses a new monetary liability with the borrower's simultaneous new debt.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 银行放贷时哪两个项目同时增加？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Which two balance-sheet items rise simultaneously when a bank lends?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 银行的贷款资产与借款人的存款负债同时增加；借款人也同时获得存款资产和贷款债务。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The bank's loan asset and deposit liability to the borrower rise together; the borrower simultaneously receives a deposit asset and a loan obligation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 跨行支付为什么不必消灭体系存款？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does an interbank payment not necessarily destroy system-wide deposits?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 存款从付款行客户转到收款行客户，准备金在两行间结算；体系存款只是换了银行，除非发生还款、取现或其他资产负债变化。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The deposit moves from the payer's bank to the recipient's bank while reserves settle between them; system deposits merely change banks unless repayment, cash withdrawal, or another balance-sheet change occurs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么简单货币乘数不是现代银行放贷的固定因果规律？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is the simple money multiplier not a fixed causal law of modern bank lending?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 准备金需求、资本、风险、利率、借款需求和央行操作都会内生调整，银行不按一笔外生准备金机械逐轮放贷。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Reserve demand, capital, risk, interest rates, credit demand, and central-bank operations adjust endogenously; banks do not mechanically relend an exogenous reserve injection round by round.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
