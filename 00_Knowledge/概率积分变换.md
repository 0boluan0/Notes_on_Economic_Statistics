# 随机变量代入自己的连续 CDF 后服从单位区间均匀分布

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Evaluating a random variable through its own continuous CDF gives a uniform variable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $F_X$ 连续，则 $U=F_X(X)\sim U[0,1]$。不要求 CDF 处处有正导数；连续但有平坦段的情形也成立。若在有关取值范围内严格递增，可用
$$P(F_X(X)\le u)=F_X(F_X^{-1}(u))=u,\quad0<u<1$$
直接证明；一般连续情形用广义分位数处理平坦段。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A variable evaluated through its own continuous CDF is uniform. A positive derivative everywhere is unnecessary. Ordinary inversion proves the strictly increasing case; quantiles handle flat parts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若 $F_X(x)=x^3$（$0\le x\le1$），则 $X^3$ 均匀。离散变量一般不满足结论：若 $X=0,1$ 各一半，$F_X(X)$ 只取 $1/2,1$。这条结果不允许忽略连续性，也不允许把任意估计 CDF 当成真实 CDF 直接套用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The CDF x³ transforms its own variable into a uniform one. A discrete equal-mass variable at zero and one instead transforms into only one half and one. Continuity and use of the true CDF are material conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
