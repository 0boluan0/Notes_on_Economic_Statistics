# Probability integral transform
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*概率积分变换*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **probability integral transform**, or **PIT**, expresses a random value on its own cumulative-probability scale. Instead of reporting a value such as a waiting time, we report the fraction of its modelled distribution at or below that value. When the distribution has a continuous CDF, these transformed values follow a uniform distribution on the unit interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**概率积分变换**（PIT）把随机取值改写到它自己的累计概率尺度上：不再直接报告等待时间之类的原数值，而是报告模型分布中不超过这个值的概率。只要分布的 CDF 连续，变换后的随机变量就在单位区间上均匀分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X$ be a [[随机变量|random variable]], and let its [[累积分布函数|CDF]] be $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$. The number $F_X(x)$ is a cumulative probability attached to a fixed threshold $x$. Substituting the random input $X$ itself gives another random variable, $U=F_X(X)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X$ 是[[随机变量]]，它的[[累积分布函数|CDF]] 为 $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$。固定门槛 $x$ 时，$F_X(x)$ 是一个累计概率数值；把随机取值 $X$ 本身代进去，才得到新的随机变量 $U=F_X(X)$。

> [!abstract] The continuous-CDF theorem · 连续 CDF 下的结论
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $F_X$ is continuous, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果 $F_X$ 连续，则
>
> $$U=F_X(X)\sim\operatorname{Uniform}(0,1),\qquad P(U\le u)=u\quad(0\le u\le1).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The symbol $\sim$ means “has this distribution”. [[连续均匀分布|Uniformity]] means that an interval inside $[0,1]$ receives probability equal to its length: for example, $P(0.2<U\le0.3)=0.1$. It does not give positive probability to any single point. For a CDF, continuity excludes jumps; strict increase and an everywhere-positive density are not required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\sim$ 表示“服从这个分布”。[[连续均匀分布|均匀性]]意味着：$[0,1]$ 内一段区间的概率等于它的长度，例如 $P(0.2<U\le0.3)=0.1$；并不是每个单点都获得正概率。对 CDF 来说，连续性排除跳跃，但不要求严格递增，也不要求密度处处为正。

> [!example] A cubic CDF · 三次方 CDF
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $F_X(x)=x^3$ for $0\le x\le1$, with CDF zero below zero and one above one. Then the transform is $U=X^3$. To check its distribution, choose any $0<u<1$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $0\le x\le1$ 时 $F_X(x)=x^3$，零以下的 CDF 为零，一以上为一。那么变换就是 $U=X^3$。要检查它的分布，任取 $0<u<1$：
>
> $$P(U\le u)=P(X^3\le u)=P(X\le u^{1/3})=F_X(u^{1/3})=u.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For example, $X=0.8$ is mapped to $0.8^3=0.512$. The original values are more concentrated near one, but the CDF transform redistributes them on the uniform probability scale. The computation uses the CDF that actually belongs to $X$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 例如，$X=0.8$ 会映射到 $0.8^3=0.512$。原变量在一附近更集中，但 CDF 变换把它改写成均匀的概率尺度。这里使用的必须是 $X$ 自己实际所属分布的 CDF。

## Why a flat part does not invalidate the result
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么平坦段不会破坏结论*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $F_X$ is continuous and strictly increasing through a probability level $u$, an ordinary inverse gives $P(F_X(X)\le u)=F_X(F_X^{-1}(u))=u$. A CDF with a flat part has no unique ordinary inverse there. Its [[总体分位数|generalised quantile]] still selects the leftmost point reaching $u$. Continuity ensures it reaches $u$ exactly; the extra interval with CDF value $u$ carries zero probability, so including it changes no probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $F_X$ 连续，而且经过概率水平 $u$ 时严格递增，普通逆函数就给出 $P(F_X(X)\le u)=F_X(F_X^{-1}(u))=u$。有平坦段时，普通逆不再唯一，但[[总体分位数|广义分位数]]仍会选出最左边达到 $u$ 的位置。连续性保证那里恰好达到 $u$；之后 CDF 同样等于 $u$ 的额外区间不承载概率，因此是否把它包含进来，不改变事件概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a concrete case, put probability $1/2$ uniformly on $[0,1]$ and probability $1/2$ uniformly on $[2,3]$. Its CDF rises from zero to $1/2$, stays flat at $1/2$ across the gap $[1,2]$, then rises to one. No probability lies in that gap: $P(1<X\le2)=F_X(2)-F_X(1)=0$. The transform is still uniform; the flat segment does not create a mass at $U=1/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，把一半概率均匀放在 $[0,1]$，另一半均匀放在 $[2,3]$。CDF 先从零升到 $1/2$，在空隙 $[1,2]$ 上保持 $1/2$，再升到一。这段空隙不承载概率：$P(1<X\le2)=F_X(2)-F_X(1)=0$。变换后仍然均匀；平坦段不会在 $U=1/2$ 处创造点概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem is about the CDF's continuity, so it also applies when a continuous CDF has no density; see [[连续CDF不保证密度|continuous CDFs without a density]]. No differentiation is needed in the statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条定理要求的是 CDF 连续，因此也适用于 CDF 连续却没有密度的情形，见[[连续CDF不保证密度]]。定理本身不需要对 CDF 求导。

> [!warning] A jump leaves a jump after the transform · 跳跃不会被普通变换自动抹平
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X$ equal zero or one with probability $1/2$ each. Then $F_X(0)=1/2$ and $F_X(1)=1$, so $F_X(X)$ takes only $1/2$ and $1$, again with probability $1/2$ each. In particular $P(F_X(X)\le1/4)=0$, whereas a uniform variable would give $1/4$. The ordinary discrete transform is therefore not uniform.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $X$ 以各 $1/2$ 的概率取零或一，则 $F_X(0)=1/2$、$F_X(1)=1$，所以 $F_X(X)$ 只会取 $1/2$ 与 $1$，各占一半。特别地，$P(F_X(X)\le1/4)=0$，而均匀变量应给出 $1/4$。因此，普通的离散变换并不均匀。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An estimated or fitted CDF is also not automatically the true CDF in this theorem. Applying it can be useful for model checking, but exact uniformity then needs justification for that estimation setup. The result here concerns a variable evaluated through its own continuous population CDF.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估计或拟合出来的 CDF 也不会自动等于定理中的真实 CDF。用它变换数据可以帮助检查模型，但是否具有精确均匀性，需要针对估计方式另作论证。这里的结论针对的是：把变量代入它自己连续的总体 CDF。
