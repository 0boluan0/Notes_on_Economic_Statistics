# 在李嘉图的竞争性差额地租论中高粮价产生地租而不是地租决定粮价

在 Ricardo 的差额地租论中，满足粮食需求需要动用更难生产的土地或追加投入，先使粮食的正常价格提高；优等生产条件随后留下可以支付给地主的差额。这不是农民先把任意高的地租加进成本，再让消费者照单付钱。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Ricardo's differential-rent argument, meeting food demand requires less favourable land or additional inputs, raising grain's normal price. Superior production conditions then leave a differential return available to the landlord. The mechanism does not start with farmers adding an arbitrary rent bill to costs and passing it on to consumers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键在于原模型有一项 [[边际土地零租条件|不付地租而正常覆盖生产成本的边际选择]]。为了得到社会所需的最后一部分粮食，仍必须使用它。较好的土地生产同质粮食，面对同一个价格，才形成 [[级差地租]]；边际选择也可以在 [[土地的集约边际|同一地块的最后一份投入]] 上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The argument relies on a [[边际土地零租条件|rent-free marginal alternative that normally covers production costs]]. That alternative remains necessary to supply the last required portion of grain. Better land sells the same grain at the same price and earns [[级差地租|differential rent]]. The relevant margin may also be [[土地的集约边际|the last input application to an existing plot]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由此可以做一个反事实检验：如果地主放弃地租，但粮食需求、技术、土地使用与边际投入条件都不变，生产最后一吨粮食所需的资源并未减少。因而正常价格不会仅因这项转账取消而下降；原本归地主的差额会先留在经营者手中。Ricardo 正是用这个比较说明价格与地租的因果次序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a counterfactual in which landlords waive rent while demand, technology, land use and marginal production conditions remain unchanged. The resources needed for the last tonne have not fallen. Its normal price therefore does not fall merely because a transfer is waived; the differential initially remains with cultivators. Ricardo uses this comparison to identify the direction of causation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不等于说任意土地收费都不影响任何价格。若租金变化会改变开垦、改良、退出或土地改作他用，上述“生产条件不变”的反事实便不成立；[[土地租金与改良回报|改良资本的使用成本]] 也不能被当成纯地租直接抹去。原命题针对竞争性的差额分配，不是所有土地市场和税费的通用结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This does not make every land charge irrelevant to every price. If changing a payment alters cultivation, improvements, exit or alternative uses, the unchanged-production counterfactual fails. Nor can [[土地租金与改良回报|the cost of using improvement capital]] simply be removed as pure rent. The claim concerns competitive differential distribution, not every land market or tax.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ricardo（1821），[《原理》II.11–15、19](https://www.econlib.org/library/Ricardo/ricP.html?chapter_num=3#2.11)：支持所需边际生产条件、同质粮食价格与差额租的次序；II.15 明确讨论地主放弃全部地租的反事实。
- 适用边界通过对该反事实保持不变的条件逐项说明；不把土地收费或政策变化当作已经由原典解决的现代一般均衡问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chapter II supplies the marginal-production argument and, in paragraph 15, the rent-waiver counterfactual. The scope statement makes its maintained conditions explicit rather than treating the passage as a general-equilibrium result for modern land policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
