# Dominated convergence theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*控制收敛定理（支配收敛定理）*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Seeing a random approximation approach its target on almost every outcome does not by itself guarantee that its average approaches the target's average. Rare values may grow large enough to keep affecting that average. The **dominated convergence theorem** rules out this problem when every approximation fits under the same integrable bound on its absolute size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看到随机近似在几乎每个结果上趋于目标，并不能单凭这一点保证平均值也趋于目标的平均值。罕见数值可能大到持续影响平均。**控制收敛定理**要求所有近似的绝对大小都被同一个可积上界控制，从而排除这个问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here [[期望|integrable]] means finite average magnitude: $E|Y|<\infty$. An **envelope** $G$ is a nonnegative random variable large enough to cover $|X_n|$ for every $n$ on the same outcome. It may vary across outcomes and may be unbounded; the requirement is $E[G]<\infty$. The [[随机变量|variables]] and the target live on one [[概率空间|probability space]], so all comparisons refer to the same experiment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的[[期望|可积]]指平均大小有限，即 $E|Y|<\infty$。**包络** $G$ 是一个非负随机变量，在同一个结果上，它能盖住每个 $n$ 的 $|X_n|$。它可以随结果变化，也可以无界；要求的是 $E[G]<\infty$。这些[[随机变量]]与目标定义在同一个[[概率空间]]上，因此所有比较都针对同一次试验。

> [!important] One integrable envelope for the whole sequence · 全序列共用一个可积包络
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $X_n\to X$ [[几乎必然收敛|almost surely]], and a nonnegative $G$ satisfies $E[G]<\infty$ and $|X_n|\le G$ almost surely for every $n$. Then $X$ and all $X_n$ are integrable, and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $X_n$ [[几乎必然收敛]]到 $X$，并且存在非负 $G$ 满足 $E[G]<\infty$，对每个 $n$ 都有 $|X_n|\le G$ 几乎必然成立。那么 $X$ 和所有 $X_n$ 都可积，且
>
> $$
> E|X_n-X|\longrightarrow0,
> \qquad
> E[X_n]\longrightarrow E[X].
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first conclusion is [[L1收敛|convergence in $L^1$]], meaning that the average absolute error vanishes. It is stronger than convergence of the means alone. No independence, identical distribution or monotonicity is required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一条结论是 [[L1收敛|$L^1$ 收敛]]，即平均绝对误差趋于零；它比仅有均值收敛更强。不要求独立、同分布或单调。

## An unbounded envelope can still work
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*包络无界也可以使用定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $U$ have the [[连续均匀分布|uniform distribution on $(0,1)$]], and set $G=1/\sqrt U$. Very small values of $U$ make $G$ arbitrarily large, but its mean is finite. The density is one, and the [[反常积分|improper integral]] at zero gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $U$ 在 $(0,1)$ 上[[连续均匀分布|均匀分布]]，令 $G=1/\sqrt U$。$U$ 很小时，$G$ 可以任意大，但它的均值有限。由于密度为一，通过零端点处的[[反常积分]]得到

$$
E[G]=\lim_{a\downarrow0}\int_a^1u^{-1/2}\,du
=\lim_{a\downarrow0}2(1-\sqrt a)=2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now choose $X_n=(-1)^nG/n$. Every path tends to zero, but the signs alternate, so this is not a nonnegative increasing sequence. Nevertheless $|X_n|=G/n\le G$, and dominated convergence applies. Direct calculation confirms $E|X_n|=2/n\to0$ and $E[X_n]=(-1)^n2/n\to0$. The common envelope need not be a fixed numerical ceiling.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再取 $X_n=(-1)^nG/n$。每条路径都趋于零，但正负号交替，所以这不是非负递增序列。然而 $|X_n|=G/n\le G$，控制收敛定理仍然适用。直接计算也有 $E|X_n|=2/n\to0$、$E[X_n]=(-1)^n2/n\to0$。共同包络不必是一个固定的数值上限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simpler special case is a common constant bound $|X_n|\le M<\infty$. On a probability space, $E[M]=M$, so this is automatically an integrable envelope. What matters is that the same bound works for every $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更简单的特例是共同常数界 $|X_n|\le M<\infty$。在概率空间上，$E[M]=M$，所以它自动是可积包络。关键是同一个界对所有 $n$ 都适用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why domination controls the average error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*包络如何控制平均误差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is one probability-one event where the convergence and all the bounds hold: only countably many null exceptions must be removed. On that event, taking a pointwise limit gives $|X|\le G$, hence $E|X|\le E[G]<\infty$. Also $|X_n-X|\le2G$. Thus the nonnegative variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以找到同一个概率为一的事件，使收敛与所有上界同时成立：只需去掉可数多个零概率异常集。在这个事件上，逐点取极限得到 $|X|\le G$，因此 $E|X|\le E[G]<\infty$。又有 $|X_n-X|\le2G$，所以可以构造非负变量

$$
H_n=2G-|X_n-X|\ge0,\qquad H_n\longrightarrow2G\quad\text{almost surely}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply [[Fatou引理|Fatou's lemma]] to $H_n$. Because the fixed budget $2E[G]$ is finite, we may subtract it without an undefined infinity-minus-infinity operation:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $H_n$ 应用 [[Fatou引理]]。由于固定总量 $2E[G]$ 有限，可以相减，不会出现没有定义的无穷减无穷：

$$
2E[G]\le\liminf_nE[H_n]
=2E[G]-\limsup_nE|X_n-X|.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The upper limit $\limsup$ is the largest limiting tail value. The inequality forces this upper limit of the nonnegative average errors to be at most zero, so the errors tend to zero. Finally, [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]] and the fact that taking absolute values prevents cancellation give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上极限 $\limsup$ 表示后续尾部最大可能值的极限。这个不等式迫使非负平均误差的上极限不超过零，所以误差趋于零。最后，[[期望线性性]]与绝对值阻止正负抵消的性质给出

$$
|E[X_n]-E[X]|\le E|X_n-X|\longrightarrow0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Remove a cap from an integrable variable
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*恢复被截断的可积变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $X$ is integrable, clip it to the interval $[-n,n]$ by $T_n(X)=\max(-n,\min(X,n))$. Clipping leaves small values unchanged and replaces larger magnitudes by the nearest endpoint. Every finite value is eventually unchanged, so $T_n(X)\to X$ almost surely. Since $|T_n(X)|\le|X|$ and $E|X|<\infty$, the theorem gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $X$ 可积，用 $T_n(X)=\max(-n,\min(X,n))$ 把它截在区间 $[-n,n]$ 内。较小数值保持不变，绝对值更大的数则被替换为最近的端点。每个有限值最终都不再受截断，所以 $T_n(X)$ 几乎必然趋于 $X$。又因为 $|T_n(X)|\le|X|$ 且 $E|X|<\infty$，定理给出

$$
E|T_n(X)-X|\longrightarrow0,
\qquad E[T_n(X)]\longrightarrow E[X].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is why bounded approximations can recover the expectation of a signed integrable variable. For a merely nonnegative variable with possibly infinite expectation, [[单调收敛定理|monotone convergence]] instead justifies increasing caps without requiring an integrable envelope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这解释了为什么可以先做有界近似，再恢复含正负值的可积变量的期望。若变量仅为非负、期望可能无穷，则应由[[单调收敛定理]]保证递增截断的极限，而不必要求可积包络。

> [!warning] Separate bounds do not make a common envelope · 各自有界不等于共同可积控制
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the same uniform $U$, set $Y_n=n\mathbf1_{\{U\le1/n\}}$, where the [[事件指示变量|indicator]] is one when $U\le1/n$ and zero otherwise. Each variable is bounded by its own finite number $n$, and each is integrable. The sequence even has $E|Y_n|=1$ uniformly in $n$. Yet $Y_n\to0$ almost surely while $E[Y_n]=1$. Thus neither individual integrability nor a common bound on expectations can replace a common integrable pointwise envelope.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 仍取同一个均匀变量 $U$，令 $Y_n=n\mathbf1_{\{U\le1/n\}}$；这里的[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]在 $U\le1/n$ 时为一，否则为零。每个变量都有自己的有限上界 $n$，也都可积；甚至所有 $n$ 都有 $E|Y_n|=1$。但 $Y_n$ 几乎必然趋于零，$E[Y_n]$ 却始终等于 $1$。因此，各自可积或期望存在共同上界，都不能替代同一个可积的逐点包络。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No integrable envelope can dominate this entire sequence, since the theorem would then force its means to approach zero. The weaker, more flexible tail condition [[一致可积|uniform integrability]] and its [[一致可积与L1收敛|$L^1$ convergence criterion]] explain the broader setting; domination is a convenient sufficient condition, not a necessary condition for passing every expectation limit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不可能存在一个可积包络盖住整个序列，否则定理就会迫使它的均值趋于零。更弱、更灵活的尾部条件是[[一致可积]]，相应的[[一致可积与L1收敛|$L^1$ 收敛判据]]说明更一般的情形；共同包络是方便的充分条件，并不是每次期望极限交换都必须具备的条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For general integration, replace $E$ by $\int\cdot\,d\mu$, almost sure by almost everywhere, and $E[G]<\infty$ by $\int G\,d\mu<\infty$. The whole space need not have finite measure. However, a constant bound is integrable only when its integral is finite; the probability-space shortcut $E[M]=M$ does not extend to an infinite-measure space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一般积分，把 $E$ 换成 $\int\cdot\,d\mu$，把几乎必然换成几乎处处，并要求 $\int G\,d\mu<\infty$ 即可。整个空间不必具有有限测度。但常数上界也只有在其积分有限时才可积；概率空间中 $E[M]=M$ 的简便结论不能直接搬到无穷测度空间。
