# 交换媒介通过避免物物交换的双重需求巧合降低交易成本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A medium of exchange lowers transaction costs by avoiding barter's double coincidence of wants*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子机制
> 货币作为交换媒介时，卖方接受它不是因为想直接消费它，而是相信之后能用它向别人购买。这样，交易不再要求双方恰好同时想要对方的商品。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> As a medium of exchange, money is accepted not because the seller wants to consume it directly, but because the seller expects others to accept it later. Exchange therefore no longer requires both parties to want each other's goods at the same time.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共同接受的交换媒介把“卖出自己的商品”和“买入想要的商品”拆成两笔交易，减少搜寻、议价和等待成本。其效果依赖接受网络：一种资产可能在某个平台、国家或社群中可支付，却在另一处缺乏交换媒介功能。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A commonly accepted medium separates selling one's own good from buying the desired good, reducing search, bargaining, and waiting costs. Its effectiveness depends on the acceptance network: an asset may serve as payment on one platform, in one country, or within one community while lacking that role elsewhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- “有人愿意接受”不足以说明普遍交换媒介；接受范围和重复可用性很重要。
- 支付工具不是必然独立货币：银行卡通常只是访问银行存款的指令通道。
- 一项资产可以是良好价值储藏，却因价格波动、验证成本或接受范围窄而不是良好交换媒介。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Acceptance by one counterparty is not enough for a generally useful medium of exchange; breadth and repeatability matter.
- A payment instrument is not necessarily separate money: a debit card is normally an instruction channel for a bank deposit.
- An asset may store value well yet be a poor medium of exchange because of price volatility, verification cost, or narrow acceptance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么用共同货币交易不要求面包师正好想要木匠提供的服务？
>
> **答案：** 面包师可以先收下大家都接受的货币，再在别处购买自己需要的东西，卖和买不必由同一对手同时完成。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does a common currency remove the need for a baker to want exactly the service offered by a carpenter?
>
> **Answer:** The baker can first accept money that others also accept and later buy what is wanted elsewhere; sale and purchase need not occur with the same counterparty at the same time.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
