# 全球价值链是产品或服务的增值生产阶段分布在至少两个国家的活动链

全球价值链（global value chain，GVC）把面向最终使用者的产品或服务生产分成相互衔接的增值阶段，其中至少两个阶段位于不同国家。分工可以围绕零部件，也可以围绕设计、数据处理等任务；并不要求一家企业把整条链都拥有。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A global value chain divides the production of a good or service for final users into connected value-adding stages, with at least two stages located in different countries. The division may involve components or tasks such as design and data processing; one firm need not own the entire chain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造例：甲国生产车架，乙国生产刹车，丙国组装自行车并销售。三地承担同一产品的不同阶段，构成全球价值链。丙国只需掌握组装及其配套能力便可参与，不必先学会制造每一种零件；但交付质量、规格和时间仍要衔接。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed example, country A makes bicycle frames, B makes brakes, and C assembles and sells bicycles. The stages form a global value chain. C can participate through assembly and its supporting capabilities without first producing every component, but quality, specifications and timing must remain compatible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

国家、部门或企业只要承担其中一个阶段，就可能参与这条链；“同时直接进口和出口”是某些研究使用的企业识别方式，不是所有参与者都必须满足的定义。只向本地出口商供货的企业，也可能间接嵌入跨国生产。统计上的跨境增值方向见[[全球价值链参与方向]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A country, sector or firm may participate by undertaking one stage. Directly importing and exporting is a useful indicator in some studies, not a requirement for every participant. A supplier serving a domestic exporter can be indirectly connected to international production. See [[全球价值链参与方向|directions of GVC participation]] for value-added accounting across borders.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[价值链]]中的波特框架以企业战略活动及其联结为分析起点；本卡关注生产阶段跨国分布，分析单位不同。全球价值链说明“谁承担哪一段”，本身不说明各方收益多少、是否发生[[价值链功能升级|功能升级]]，也不证明整条链必须采取外资控股形式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Porter's [[价值链|value-chain framework]] starts from a firm's strategic activities and their links; this card concerns stages distributed across countries. A GVC identifies who performs which stage, without establishing the size of gains, [[价值链功能升级|functional upgrading]], or a requirement for foreign ownership.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
