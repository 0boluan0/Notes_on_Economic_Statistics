# 财政赤字是既定期间与口径下财政支出超过收入的差额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fiscal deficit is the excess of expenditure over revenue for a specified period and accounting basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

赤字是一期流量，不是期末债务存量。本卡采用GFS净借出／净借入余额：收入减费用，再减非金融资产净投资。余额为负时，其相反数就是这一口径的赤字；具体国家的现金赤字、预算余额或其他调整指标，需先做口径衔接。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A deficit is a period flow, not an end-period debt stock. Here the GFS balance is revenue minus expense minus net investment in nonfinancial assets. A negative balance represents net borrowing; its magnitude is the deficit on this basis. Cash or jurisdiction-specific measures require reconciliation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设收入为$R$、费用为$E$、非金融资产净投资为$N$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With revenue $R$, expense $E$ and net investment in nonfinancial assets $N$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
OB=R-E-N=\Delta FA_{\mathrm{交易}}-\Delta L_{\mathrm{交易}},\qquad DEF=-OB.
$$

同一范围内收入230、费用220、净投资20，余额为−10，赤字为10。若同期金融资产净增加5，则负债交易净增加15，而不是10。费用包括固定资本消耗，净投资已扣除它，两项相加时不要重复计入折耗。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revenue 230, expense 220 and net investment 20 give a balance of −10 and a deficit of 10. If financial assets increase by 5 through transactions, liabilities must increase by 15 through transactions. Expense includes fixed-capital consumption, while net investment deducts it; avoid counting depreciation twice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这解释了为什么[[财政收入与融资|发债不是收入]]，也为什么“今年赤字10”不保证“债务只增加10”。从交易到期末存量，还需[[债务存量流量调节]]。财政主动扩张与否则要结合[[财政自动稳定器]]和[[结构性预算余额]]判断，不能只凭赤字变大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This explains why [[财政收入与融资|debt issuance is not revenue]] and why a deficit of 10 need not raise debt by exactly 10. [[债务存量流量调节|Stock-flow reconciliation]] links transactions to closing stocks. Assessing discretionary expansion also requires [[财政自动稳定器|automatic stabilisers]] and [[结构性预算余额|structural balances]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
