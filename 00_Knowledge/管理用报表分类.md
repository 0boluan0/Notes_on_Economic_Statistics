# 管理用报表按项目的经营或融资功能重分类并同步匹配损益和现金流
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Management statements classify items by operating or financing function and consistently match their income and cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重分类先问一项资产或负债在这家企业中做什么：它服务于商品、服务及生产性投资，还是用于筹资与运用经营以外的多余资金？再把该项目的损益和现金流放入同一功能类别。这样才能分别考察经营回报和融资成本，而不是只换一张表的栏目。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with the item's role in this particular business: does it support goods, services, and productive investment, or financing and the use of funds surplus to operations? Classify its income and cash flows consistently. Separating operating return from financing cost requires more than relabelling one statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通工商企业的应收账款通常来自销售，虽是会计意义上的金融资产，管理分析中仍属经营资产；设备也属经营资产。经营所需现金可以留在经营侧，多余现金及相应收益则可放在金融侧。应付供应商通常随采购形成，借款则通常服务融资。遇到投资性房地产、长期股权投资、租赁、应计利息等项目，应查实际业务和附注，不能只背科目清单。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nonfinancial company's trade receivables usually arise from sales: they are accounting financial assets but operating assets in this analysis. Equipment is operating too. Required operating cash may stay on the operating side, while surplus cash and its income may be financial. Trade payables usually arise from purchasing, whereas borrowing usually supplies financing. Investment property, equity investments, leases, and accrued interest require business and note-level evidence, not a memorised classification list.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完成后应同时满足[[管理用资产负债表恒等式]]与[[管理用利润表拆分]]，现金流也按[[管理用现金流量]]衔接。经营负债仍是实际义务；即使未单列利息，也可能有放弃现金折扣等经济成本。银行以存贷为主营业务，不能机械沿用制造企业的分类。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result should satisfy the [[管理用资产负债表恒等式|balance-sheet identity]] and [[管理用利润表拆分|income reconciliation]], with cash flows aligned through [[管理用现金流量|the management cash-flow framework]]. Operating liabilities remain real obligations and may carry economic costs such as forgone cash discounts even without explicit interest. Banks cannot mechanically inherit a manufacturer's classification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
