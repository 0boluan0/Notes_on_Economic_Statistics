# Multiplicativity of the determinant
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行列式乘法性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When two square matrices act in succession, you can find the determinant of their combined action by multiplying their two determinants. You do not first need to compute every entry of the combined matrix. Here a [[行列式|determinant]] is the scalar assigned to a square matrix, and [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] records successive actions: in $AB$, $B$ acts first and $A$ acts second.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个方阵先后作用时，只要把各自的行列式相乘，就能得到合成作用的行列式，不必先算出乘积矩阵的每个元素。这里，[[行列式]]是方阵对应的标量；[[矩阵乘法]]记录前后作用：$AB$ 表示先做 $B$，再做 $A$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, take
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，取

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
AB=\begin{pmatrix}2&3\\0&3\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two original determinants are $1$ and $6$. The product has determinant $2\cdot3-3\cdot0=6$, exactly $1\cdot6$. For these real matrices, $B$ stretches the coordinate directions by $2$ and $3$, while $A$ shears without changing signed area. Their combined signed-area factor is therefore $6$. The algebraic theorem also applies to complex matrices, where a determinant need not be a real signed-volume factor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原来两个行列式分别是 $1$ 和 $6$；乘积矩阵的行列式为 $2\cdot3-3\cdot0=6$，恰好等于 $1\cdot6$。对这两个实矩阵，$B$ 沿两个坐标方向分别伸缩 $2$ 倍和 $3$ 倍，$A$ 则作保持有向面积的剪切，所以合成的有向面积因子为 $6$。代数定理也适用于复矩阵，此时行列式不一定是表示有向体积的实数。

> [!important] Same-size square factors · 同阶方阵的乘积
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $n\ge1$ be an integer, and let $A,B\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$ have entries in the same field $\mathbb F$, such as the real or complex numbers. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $n\ge1$ 为整数，$A,B\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$ 的元素来自同一个域 $\mathbb F$，例如实数域或复数域。则
>
> $$\boxed{\det(AB)=\det A\,\det B.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This includes singular factors, whose determinants are zero. Neither invertibility nor $AB=BA$ is required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 定理包含行列式为零的奇异因子，不要求因子可逆，也不要求 $AB=BA$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A proof that also covers singular factors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同时覆盖奇异因子的证明*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix $B$ and regard $H_B(A)=\det(AB)$ as a function of $A$. If row $i$ of $A$ is the row vector $r_i$, then row $i$ of $AB$ is $r_iB$, by [[矩阵乘积逐行作用|the rowwise multiplication rule]]. For row vectors $u,v$ and scalars $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F$, replacing $r_i$ by $\alpha u+\beta v$ replaces that product row by $\alpha(uB)+\beta(vB)$. The determinant is linear in one row while all other rows stay fixed, so $H_B$ has the same separate row linearity. If two rows of $A$ are equal, their product rows are equal, and $H_B(A)=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $B$，把 $H_B(A)=\det(AB)$ 看成关于 $A$ 的函数。若 $A$ 的第 $i$ 行为行向量 $r_i$，则由[[矩阵乘积逐行作用]]，$AB$ 的第 $i$ 行是 $r_iB$。对行向量 $u,v$ 及标量 $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F$，把 $r_i$ 换成 $\alpha u+\beta v$，乘积中的这一行就变成 $\alpha(uB)+\beta(vB)$。行列式在固定其他行时对这一行线性，所以 $H_B$ 也逐行线性。若 $A$ 的两行相同，它们的乘积行也相同，从而 $H_B(A)=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now expand every row of $A$ in coordinate rows. Write $e_j$ for the row with a $1$ in position $j$ and zeros elsewhere; row $i$ is $\sum_{j=1}^n a_{ij}e_j$, where $a_{ij}$ is the entry in row $i$, column $j$. The [[Leibniz 行列式公式|coordinate-row expansion behind the Leibniz formula]] applies to any separately row-linear function that vanishes on equal rows. Repeated choices of $e_j$ give zero, so only permutations remain. A permutation $\sigma$ assigns each row a different label $\sigma(i)$; its sign is $+1$ for an even number of row swaps and $-1$ for an odd number. Sorting the coordinate rows into their natural order gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把 $A$ 的每行按坐标行展开。记 $e_j$ 为第 $j$ 个位置是 $1$、其余位置为零的行，则第 $i$ 行为 $\sum_{j=1}^n a_{ij}e_j$，其中 $a_{ij}$ 是第 $i$ 行、第 $j$ 列的元素。[[Leibniz 行列式公式|Leibniz 公式背后的坐标行展开]]适用于任何逐行线性且重复行归零的函数。重复选到同一个 $e_j$ 的项为零，因此只留下排列：排列 $\sigma$ 给各行分配互不相同的标签 $\sigma(i)$，偶数次换行取符号 $+1$，奇数次取 $-1$。把坐标行排回自然顺序，便得

$$\begin{aligned}
H_B(A)
&=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\left(\prod_{i=1}^n a_{i,\sigma(i)}\right)
 H_B(e_{\sigma(1)},\ldots,e_{\sigma(n)})\\
&=H_B(I_n)\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)
 \prod_{i=1}^n a_{i,\sigma(i)}\\
&=H_B(I_n)\det A.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $S_n$ is the set of all permutations, $\prod$ multiplies the selected coefficients, and the row-list notation means the matrix formed from those rows. The [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I_n$ has rows $e_1,\ldots,e_n$. Because $I_nB=B$, we have $H_B(I_n)=\det B$, completing the proof. We never required $H_B(I_n)=1$ and never divided by $\det B$. Thus the same proof works when $B$ is singular. Equal-row vanishing, rather than division by $2$, also makes the argument valid in characteristic two.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $S_n$ 是全部排列的集合，$\prod$ 表示把选出的系数相乘，按行列出的函数参数表示由这些行组成的矩阵。[[单位矩阵]] $I_n$ 的各行正是 $e_1,\ldots,e_n$。由于 $I_nB=B$，所以 $H_B(I_n)=\det B$，证明完成。全过程没有要求 $H_B(I_n)=1$，也没有除以 $\det B$，因此 $B$ 奇异时仍然成立。证明使用重复行归零，不需要除以 $2$，故也适用于特征二的域。

## The factors need not commute
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么不需要 $AB=BA$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening example, $BA=\begin{pmatrix}2&2\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$ differs from $AB$, but both determinants are $6$. In general, determinants are scalars in a field, where multiplication commutes, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头例子的 $BA=\begin{pmatrix}2&2\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$ 与 $AB$ 不同，但两者的行列式都为 $6$。一般地，行列式是域中的标量，标量乘法可交换，因此

$$\det(AB)=\det A\det B=\det B\det A=\det(BA).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This equality also does not imply that the products are [[相似矩阵|similar matrices]], meaning matrices related by an invertible change of basis. For example, let $P=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ and $N=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$. Then $PN=N\ne0$, while $NP=0$. Both determinants vanish, but the zero matrix remains zero under every invertible change of basis, so it cannot be similar to $N$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个相等也不能推出两个乘积是[[相似矩阵]]，即通过可逆换基相联系的矩阵。例如，取 $P=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$、$N=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$，则 $PN=N\ne0$，而 $NP=0$。两个行列式都为零，但零矩阵经过任何可逆换基仍为零，因此不可能与 $N$ 相似。

## Powers, inverses and singular products
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*幂、逆矩阵与奇异乘积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a positive integer $k$, the power $A^k$ means multiplying $k$ copies of $A$. Repeated use of the product rule gives $\det(A^k)=(\det A)^k$. At exponent zero, $A^0=I_n$ and its determinant is $1$; the scalar convention $z^0=1$ also applies at $z=0$ here. If $A$ has an [[逆矩阵|inverse]] $A^{-1}$, which undoes its action, then $AA^{-1}=I_n$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正整数 $k$，$A^k$ 表示把 $k$ 个 $A$ 相乘。重复使用乘法性，得到 $\det(A^k)=(\det A)^k$。指数为零时，$A^0=I_n$，其行列式为 $1$；这里的标量零次幂也约定为 $z^0=1$，包括 $z=0$。若 $A$ 存在能还原其作用的[[逆矩阵]] $A^{-1}$，则 $AA^{-1}=I_n$ 给出

$$1=\det A\,\det(A^{-1}),\qquad
\det(A^{-1})=\frac1{\det A}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently the power formula also holds for negative integers when $A$ is invertible. Over a field, [[可逆性与非零行列式|a square matrix is invertible exactly when its determinant is nonzero]]. The product rule therefore shows that $AB$ is invertible exactly when both factors are invertible. If either factor is singular, the product is singular too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，当 $A$ 可逆时，幂公式也适用于负整数。域上的[[可逆性与非零行列式|方阵可逆恰好等价于行列式非零]]，所以乘法性还说明：$AB$ 可逆恰好等价于两个因子都可逆；只要一个因子奇异，乘积也奇异。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Boundaries of the rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is no general addition rule $\det(A+B)=\det A+\det B$. For example, over the reals, $\det(I_2+I_2)=4$, while the sum of the two determinants is $2$. Separate row linearity does not make the determinant linear in the whole matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般没有加法规则 $\det(A+B)=\det A+\det B$。例如，在实数域上，$\det(I_2+I_2)=4$，但两个行列式之和为 $2$。逐行线性不意味着对整张矩阵线性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rectangular factors may produce square products, but their own ordinary determinants are undefined. In fact, for $R=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\end{pmatrix}$ and $C=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}$, the two product determinants are different:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩形因子可能相乘得到方阵，但矩形因子自身没有这里的普通行列式。实际上，对 $R=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\end{pmatrix}$、$C=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}$，两个乘积的行列式还可以不同：

$$RC=[1],\qquad CR=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\det(RC)=1,\quad\det(CR)=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus both the product rule and the derived equality $\det(AB)=\det(BA)$ above must retain their same-order square-factor assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，上面的乘法性和由它推出的 $\det(AB)=\det(BA)$，都必须保留“因子为同阶方阵”的假设。

> [!question]- Use the inverse consequence · 使用逆矩阵推论
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Over the reals, if $\det A=-2$ and $\det B=3$ for same-size square matrices, what is $\det(BA^{-1})$? Since $\det A\ne0$, the inverse exists, and the answer is $3(-1/2)=-3/2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在实数域上，同阶方阵满足 $\det A=-2$、$\det B=3$，则 $\det(BA^{-1})$ 是多少？因为 $\det A\ne0$，逆矩阵存在，答案为 $3(-1/2)=-3/2$。
