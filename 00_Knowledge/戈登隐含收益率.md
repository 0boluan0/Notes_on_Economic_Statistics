# 戈登隐含收益率是在稳定增长假设下由市场价反解的下一期股利收益率与增长率之和
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Gordon implied return is the next-period dividend yield plus growth, reverse-calculated from market price under stable-growth assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定市场价 $P_0>0$、下一期预计现金股利 $D_1>0$ 与其后固定永续增长率 $g$，若采用[[戈登增长模型]]，使预计股利现值等于该价格的模型收益率为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given market price $P_0>0$, the next expected cash dividend $D_1>0$, and constant perpetual growth $g$ thereafter, the return that equates projected dividend value to price under the [[戈登增长模型|Gordon model]] is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_0=\frac{D_1}{\rho-g}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\rho=\frac{D_1}{P_0}+g.$$

其中 $D_1/P_0$ 是**前瞻**[[股利收益率]]，不是上一年已付股利除现价；$\rho$ 是给定价格和增长预测后的反解结果。它与从风险要求出发选择的必要报酬率 $k_e$ 职责不同：估值用 $k_e$ 算价值，反解用现价算 $\rho$。只有价格与同一套预测下的估值一致时，两者才相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$D_1/P_0$ is the **forward** [[股利收益率|dividend yield]], not last year's paid dividend divided by today's price. The implied return $\rho$ follows from the supplied price and growth forecast. It differs in role from the required return $k_e$ chosen for risk: valuation uses $k_e$ to calculate value, while inversion uses price to calculate $\rho$. They coincide only when price equals value under the same forecasts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如每股现价20元，下一期股利1元，此后永续增长10%，则 $\rho=1/20+10\%=15\%$。再假定匹配的必要报酬率也一直为15%，而且未来仍按同一股利路径定价，则下一年收到1元股利后的股票价值是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a price of 20, next-period dividend of 1, and perpetual growth of 10%, $\rho=1/20+10\%=15\%$. If the matched required return also remains 15% and future prices continue to reflect the same dividend path, the price after receiving the next dividend of 1 is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_1=\frac{D_2}{k_e-g}
=\frac{1(1.10)}{0.15-0.10}=22.$$

忽略税费，一年[[持有期收益率|总回报]]于是为 $(1+22-20)/20=15\%$：5%来自股利，10%来自预计价差。这时 $P_1/P_0=1+g$，是**同一增长路径与恒定定价条件共同推出**的结果，不是只要股利增长10%，股价就必须增长10%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ignoring taxes and costs, the one-year [[持有期收益率|total return]] is $(1+22-20)/20=15\%$: 5% from dividends and 10% from the projected price gain. Here $P_1/P_0=1+g$ follows jointly from the same growth path and unchanged pricing conditions. Dividend growth of 10% alone does not force price growth of 10%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零增长例更容易分清两种利率：预计每年末永久派2元，若按必要报酬率16%估值，价值为12.5元；现价若只有12元，则同一股利预测反解出的 $\rho=2/12=16.6667\%$。这只是该预测与价格组合隐含的收益率；股利可能改变，市场也未承诺在下一年修正到你算出的价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The zero-growth example separates the rates clearly. A perpetual annual dividend of 2 is worth 12.5 at a required return of 16%. A market price of 12 instead implies $\rho=2/12=16.6667\%$ under the same dividend forecast. That is the return implied by those inputs, not a promise that dividends remain unchanged or that the market corrects to the estimated value next year.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

真实股利、风险溢价与卖价变化时，实际回报应按实际现金与成交价格重新计算。对多期随机现金流，反解一个使**预测**现值等于现价的利率，也不自动等于随机复合收益率的期望；本公式的适用范围仍是指定的稳定增长模型。若股利分阶段变化，先用[[多阶段股利估值]]描述路径，再做相应的数值反解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When realised dividends, risk premia, or sale prices differ, calculate realised return from actual cash and transaction prices. For multiperiod random cash flows, a rate equating **forecast** present value with price is not automatically the expectation of a random compounded return. This formula remains specific to the stated stable-growth model. For changing growth stages, describe the path with [[多阶段股利估值|multistage valuation]] before an appropriate numerical inversion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
