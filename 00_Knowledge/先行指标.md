# 先行指标是相对于指定经济活动基准通常较早转折的时间序列
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A leading indicator is a time series that typically turns before a specified economic-activity benchmark.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先行是相对于特定国家、基准周期和样本的**经验时序属性**。新订单、建筑许可或利差可能提前反映未来生产与融资条件，但没有每次领先固定月数、没有假信号的保证；一种指标在不同经济结构下也可能改变表现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Leading behavior is an **empirical timing property** relative to a country, reference cycle, and sample. Orders, permits, or spreads may signal future production and financing conditions, but lead times vary and false signals occur.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用前先统一指标方向：初次申请失业救济人数上升通常表示活动转弱，不能和新订单上升直接按同方向相加。再检查发布时点；观测月份较早转折不等于投资者当时已收到数据，见[[经济数据的实时口径]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Orient indicators consistently before combining them: rising initial unemployment claims usually signal weakness, unlike rising orders. Also check release dates; an earlier observation-period turning point does not prove that the information was available then. See [[经济数据的实时口径|real-time data conventions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与[[同步指标]]、[[滞后指标]]的分类要围绕同一个基准。单个先行信号可以形成待验证假设，不足以确定衰退开始日或保证某资产下一期收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare [[同步指标|coincident]] and [[滞后指标|lagging]] indicators against the same benchmark. One leading signal is a hypothesis to test, not a recession date or a guaranteed asset return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
