# DV01对冲以共同风险因子的带符号敞口确定抵销头寸

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A DV01 hedge sizes offsetting positions from signed exposures to one common risk factor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先指定双方共同承受的利率因素 $x$，以小数年率计。组合与每单位对冲工具的 [[DV01]] 都按 $d=-10^{-4}\partial V/\partial x$ 计算，并统一币种、估值时点、冲击方式与单位规模。令原组合敞口为 $d_P$，每单位多头工具敞口为 $d_H$，持有数量为 $h$（负数表示空头），要消掉这个因素的一阶敞口，应解：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First specify a common rate factor $x$ in decimal annual-rate units. Compute [[DV01]] for the portfolio and one unit of the hedge as $d=-10^{-4}\partial V/\partial x$, using one currency, valuation time, shock rule, and unit scale. Let the existing exposure be $d_P$, the exposure per long hedge unit be $d_H$, and the hedge holding be $h$ (negative for short). Eliminating this factor's first-order exposure requires:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
d_P+h d_H=0,\qquad h=-\frac{d_P}{d_H}\quad(d_H\ne0).
$$

例如原组合 $d_P=+500$ 美元／bp，每单位多头工具 $d_H=+25$ 美元／bp，则 $h=-20$。共同因素上移 1bp 时，原组合约亏 500，20 单位空头约赚 500。若两笔都做多，金额敏感度相加，不能因绝对值匹配而称为抵销。若目标不是零而是 $d_*$，则用 $h=(d_*-d_P)/d_H$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $d_P=+500$ USD per bp and each long hedge unit has $d_H=+25$, then $h=-20$. A 1bp rise in the common factor causes an approximate 500 loss on the portfolio and 500 gain on twenty short hedge units. Two long positions add exposure; matching absolute values does not offset it. For a nonzero target $d_*$, use $h=(d_*-d_P)/d_H$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键判断不是“名义本金相等”，而是 [[组合DV01聚合|带符号金额导数]] 是否抵销。两只债券各自 YTM 上升 1bp，尚不是共同基准曲线冲击；两个不同参考利率也不必同步。若要把各自收益率 DV01 用于同一个因素，须先给出各收益率对该因素的响应，再换算敞口，不能默认响应都为 1。有关口径区别见 [[收益率久期与曲线久期]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The test is cancellation of [[组合DV01聚合|signed monetary derivatives]], not equal notional amounts. Raising each bond's own YTM by 1bp is not yet a common benchmark-curve shock, and distinct reference rates need not move together. To use own-yield DV01s for one common factor, first specify how each yield responds to that factor and transform the exposures; those responses are not automatically 1. See [[收益率久期与曲线久期|yield versus curve duration]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这只匹配当前点的一个一阶因素。凸性、曲线非平行变化、基差、信用和波动率风险仍可能保留；利率或行权状态改变后应重新估算。如果 $d_H=0$ 而 $d_P\ne0$，该工具根本不能完成此目标；若交易单位只能取整数，应把舍入后的剩余敞口重新算出来，而不是报告精确中性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This matches one first-order factor at the current point. Convexity, nonparallel moves, basis, credit, and volatility exposures may remain; changes in rates or exercise states require reassessment. If $d_H=0$ but $d_P\ne0$, the instrument cannot meet the target. If only integer trading units are available, recalculate the residual after rounding rather than claiming exact neutrality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
