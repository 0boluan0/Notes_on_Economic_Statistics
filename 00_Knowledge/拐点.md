# 拐点是函数图像凹凸方向在该点两侧发生改变的点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An inflection point is a point across which the graph changes its convexity or concavity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 若 $f$ 在 $c$ 连续，并且在 $c$ 左右相邻区间的凹凸性相反，则 $(c,f(c))$ 是拐点。核心是“变号”或“变向”，不是方程 $f''(c)=0$ 本身。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $f$ is continuous at $c$ and has opposite concavity on neighboring intervals to the left and right, then $(c,f(c))$ is an inflection point. The defining event is the change in concavity, not the equation $f''(c)=0$ by itself.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$f(x)=x^3$ 在零点的二阶导数从负变正，所以零点是拐点。$f(x)=x^4$ 也有 $f''(0)=0$，但两侧都凸，因而不是拐点。另一方面，拐点处二阶导数可以不存在；先把 $f''=0$ 或无定义的位置列为候选，再检查两侧凹凸性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x^3$, the second derivative changes from negative to positive at zero, so the origin is an inflection point. For $x^4$, $f''(0)=0$ but the function is convex on both sides, so it is not. The second derivative may also fail to exist at an inflection point. Treat zeros and failures of $f''$ as candidates, then test the two sides.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拐点也不要求切线水平。$x^3+x$ 在零点凹凸改变，但 $f'(0)=1$。把“临界点”“驻点”和“拐点”混为一谈，会把一阶方向变化与二阶曲率变化混在一起。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inflection point need not have a horizontal tangent: $x^3+x$ changes concavity at zero while $f'(0)=1$. Critical points, stationary points, and inflection points describe different first- and second-order events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 求出 $f''(c)=0$ 后还必须做什么？
>
> **答案：** 检查 $c$ 是否在函数图像上且两侧凹凸性是否真正改变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** After finding $f''(c)=0$, what must you still check?
>
> **Answer:** Check that there is a graph point at $c$ and that concavity actually changes across it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
