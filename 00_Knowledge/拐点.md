# Inflection points
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拐点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **inflection point** is a point where a continuous graph changes its direction of bending. It can keep rising while changing from bending down to bending up. This is a change of [[凹凸性|concavity or convexity]], rather than a change from increasing to decreasing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**拐点**是连续图像改变弯曲方向的点。图像可以一直上升，同时从向下弯变成向上弯。这里改变的是[[凹凸性]]，并不是从递增改成递减。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^3$, slopes on the negative side decrease towards zero; slopes on the positive side increase away from zero. The curve is concave on the left and convex on the right, meeting continuously at $(0,0)$. That meeting point is an inflection point, even though the function increases through it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x)=x^3$，负半轴上的斜率逐渐减小至零，正半轴上的斜率又从零逐渐增大。曲线左侧为凹、右侧为凸，在 $(0,0)$ 连续相接。因此原点是拐点，尽管函数经过原点时仍一直递增。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What must change at the point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*在这个点必须发生什么变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We use the continuous-graph convention: $f$ is real-valued on an open interval around an interior input $c$, is [[一点连续|continuous at $c$]], and changes from convex to concave, or from concave to convex, on adjacent intervals on the two sides. Continuity means that the nearby values approach the assigned value $f(c)$. The inflection point is the graph point $(c,f(c))$; $c$ alone is its input coordinate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡采用连续图像的约定：实值函数 $f$ 定义在内点 $c$ 周围的某个开区间上，在 $c$ [[一点连续|连续]]，并在左右相邻区间从凸变凹，或从凹变凸。连续表示附近函数值趋于已赋的点值 $f(c)$。拐点是图像点 $(c,f(c))$；单独的 $c$ 是它的输入坐标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here convex means that the graph lies below its chords; the calculus name is **concave up**. Concave means above the chords, or **concave down**. The change must be genuine arbitrarily close to $c$ on both sides. Because a straight line is both convex and concave, merely assigning different labels to straight pieces does not count: neither side should be a straight line on a smaller interval immediately adjacent to $c$. Strictly opposite bending on the two sides is the usual clear case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的凸是指图像不高于弦，微积分中称为 **concave up**；凹是指图像不低于弦，称为 **concave down**。两侧任意靠近 $c$ 时都要有真正相反的弯曲。直线既凸又凹，所以不能只把直线段分别贴上不同标签：任一侧都不应在紧邻 $c$ 的某个更小区间上完全变成直线。两侧严格反向弯曲，是通常最清楚的情形。

> [!important] A reliable sufficient test · 一个可靠的充分检验
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $f$ is continuous at $c$, and its [[高阶导数|second derivative]] exists on each punctured side interval. If $f''<0$ throughout one side and $f''>0$ throughout the other, the [[导数判凹凸|derivative criteria]] prove a change of concavity and hence an inflection point. The value of $f''(c)$ itself is not needed for this test.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $f$ 在 $c$ 连续，且[[高阶导数|二阶导数]]在两侧去掉 $c$ 的区间上存在，那么一侧处处 $f''<0$、另一侧处处 $f''>0$，就可由[[导数判凹凸]]证明凹凸改变，从而确认拐点。这个检验不需要 $f''(c)$ 本身存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Zero, undefined, and nonhorizontal are different facts
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零、未定义与非水平切线是不同事实*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x^3$, $f''(x)=6x$ has the required opposite signs. For $x^4$, $f''(x)=12x^2$ is positive on both sides of zero. Although both functions have $f''(0)=0$, only the cubic changes concavity. A zero second derivative nominates a candidate; it does not certify it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $x^3$，$f''(x)=6x$ 确实在两侧异号。对 $x^4$，$f''(x)=12x^2$ 在零点两侧都为正。虽然两者都满足 $f''(0)=0$，只有立方函数改变凹凸。二阶导数为零只给候选，不是确认。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inflection can also occur where the second derivative is undefined. Take $g(x)=x|x|$, which equals $-x^2$ for $x<0$ and $x^2$ for $x\ge0$. It is continuous at zero and has $g'(0)=\lim_{h\to0}|h|=0$. Its derivative is $2|x|$, whose left and right slopes at zero are $-2$ and $2$. Thus $g''(0)$ does not exist, while the concave left side and convex right side make $(0,0)$ an inflection point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二阶导数未定义处也可能有拐点。令 $g(x)=x|x|$，则 $x<0$ 时为 $-x^2$，$x\ge0$ 时为 $x^2$。它在零点连续，且 $g'(0)=\lim_{h\to0}|h|=0$。导函数为 $2|x|$，在零点的左右斜率分别是 $-2$、$2$，因此 $g''(0)$ 不存在；但左凹右凸仍使 $(0,0)$ 成为拐点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f''(c)$ does exist at such a change, it must equal zero. Indeed, derivative monotonicity reverses across $c$, so $f'$ has a local extremum there; differentiability of $f'$ forces its derivative to be zero. Conversely, this necessary zero does not establish a change, as $x^4$ shows. When using a second-derivative sign chart, zeros and undefined locations are therefore places to examine, not automatic answers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若这种改变处的 $f''(c)$ 确实存在，则它必须等于零：$f'$ 的单调方向在 $c$ 两侧反转，于是在那里有局部极值；$f'$ 在该点可导便迫使其导数为零。反过来，这个必要的零值并不能证明凹凸改变，$x^4$ 就是反例。因此制作二阶导数符号表时，零点和未定义的位置只是要检查的地点，并非自动成立的答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A horizontal tangent is a separate matter. The function $h(x)=x^3+x$ still has $h''(x)=6x$ and an inflection at zero, but $h'(0)=1$. A [[驻点|stationary point]] has zero first derivative; a [[临界点|critical point]] is a broader first-derivative candidate. Neither term means “inflection point”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

切线水平是另一件事。$h(x)=x^3+x$ 仍满足 $h''(x)=6x$，在零点有拐点，却有 $h'(0)=1$。[[驻点]]要求一阶导数为零，[[临界点]]是范围更广的一阶导数候选概念；两者都不等于“拐点”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check the graph point before accepting a sign change
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*接受变号结论前，先检查图像点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The function $1/x$ is concave for $x<0$ and convex for $x>0$, since $f''(x)=2/x^3$. Yet zero is outside its domain and separates two branches, so there is no inflection point there. Assigning a finite value at zero would not repair its failure of continuity. Likewise, an endpoint with only one side in the domain cannot satisfy this two-sided definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

函数 $1/x$ 满足 $f''(x)=2/x^3$，在 $x<0$ 时凹，在 $x>0$ 时凸；但零点不在定义域中，只是两条分支的分界，并没有那里的拐点。即使在零点补一个有限值，也不能修复其不连续。类似地，定义域只在一侧有输入的端点，不满足这里的双侧定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For [[曲线描绘|curve sketching]], first locate the candidate input, then verify continuity, genuine opposite concavity on adjacent intervals, and finally the coordinate $f(c)$. Checking a single sample on each side is justified only when the derivative formula or another argument establishes its sign throughout the corresponding intervals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用于[[曲线描绘]]时，先定位候选输入，再核对连续性、相邻两侧真正相反的凹凸，最后算出纵坐标 $f(c)$。只有导数公式或其他论证已保证相应区间内符号固定，才可以用两侧各一个代表点来检查。
