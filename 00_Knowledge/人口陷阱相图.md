# 比较总收入与人口增长率可以判断人口陷阱相图中人均收入的变化方向

人口陷阱相图的读法是：在同一个人均收入水平上，比较总收入增长率与人口增长率。总收入增长较快，人均收入向右增加；人口增长较快，人均收入向左减少；两率相等时，人均收入不变。在连续调整的解释下，要判断交点是否稳定，再看它左右两侧的箭头是否都指向它。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read a population-trap phase diagram by comparing total-income growth and population growth at the same income per person. Faster income growth moves income per person rightward; faster population growth moves it leftward; equal rates leave it unchanged. Under the continuous-adjustment interpretation, assess stability by checking whether the arrows on both sides point toward the crossing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先认轴和增长口径

这里使用[[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=24|课堂第 24 页]]的三交点图：横轴是人均收入 $y=Y/P$，纵轴同时画两种增长率。**实线**是总收入增长率 $g_Y$，**虚线**是人口增长率 $g_P$；$P$ 是人口，不是价格。横轴向右只是收入水平更高，不表示时间自动向后推进。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=24|classroom diagram on PDF page 24]] has three crossings. Its horizontal axis is income per person, $y=Y/P$, while its vertical axis carries two growth rates: the **solid** curve is total-income growth $g_Y$ and the **dashed** curve is population growth $g_P$. $P$ denotes population, not prices. Moving right means a higher income level, not automatically a later date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $Y$ 取为同一价格口径的实际总收入，令两率为同一期的简单增长率，且 $Y,P>0$、$1+g_P>0$。由[[人均GDP|人均量的比值恒等式]]：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Measure $Y$ as real aggregate income on a consistent price basis. For simple growth rates over the same period, with positive income and population and $1+g_P>0$, the [[人均GDP|identity for a per-capita ratio]] gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y_{t+1}=y_t\frac{1+g_Y(y_t)}{1+g_P(y_t)},
\qquad
g_y=\frac{g_Y-g_P}{1+g_P}.
$$

因此两曲线的高低差能**精确判断方向**；把 $g_Y-g_P$ 直接当作离散人均增长率则只是小变动近似。例如总收入增长 4%、人口增长 3%，人均增长为 $1.04/1.03-1\approx0.971\%$。若两率指连续时间的瞬时增长率，才有精确的 $\dot y/y=g_Y-g_P$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The vertical gap therefore determines the direction exactly, but treating $g_Y-g_P$ itself as the discrete per-capita growth rate is only a small-change approximation. With income growing by 4% and population by 3%, income per person grows by approximately 0.971%. For continuous-time instantaneous growth rates, $\dot y/y=g_Y-g_P$ is exact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 逐区间读课堂的 A、B、C

把低收入交点 A 的横坐标记为 $y_A$；B 对应 $Y_2$，C 对应 $Y_4$。原出版图低端 $Y_0/Y_1$ 标号拥挤，$Y_1$ 的准确投影在这里不作强行配对：读低端调整时以实际交点 A 为准。$Y_1,\ldots,Y_5$ 都是收入水平标记，不是五个必经的发展阶段。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $y_A$ for the low-income crossing A; B corresponds to $Y_2$ and C to $Y_4$. The original diagram crowds the labels $Y_0$ and $Y_1$, so no exact projection is assigned to $Y_1$ here: use the actual crossing A for low-income adjustment. The labels $Y_1,\ldots,Y_5$ mark income levels, not five inevitable stages of development.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 位置 | 比较两率 | 人均收入怎样动 |
|---|---|---|
| A 左侧邻域 | $g_Y>g_P$ | 向右靠近 A |
| $y_A<y<Y_2$ | $g_Y<g_P$ | 向左靠近 A |
| B：$y=Y_2$ | $g_Y=g_P$ | 精确处在交点时不动；轻微左偏后远离 B，轻微右偏也远离 B |
| $Y_2<y<Y_3$ | $g_Y>g_P$ | 向右，靠近 C |
| $y=Y_3$ | 总收入增长率在峰顶，仍有 $g_Y>g_P$ | 继续向右；增长率峰值不是人均收入均衡 |
| $Y_3<y<Y_4$ | 实线虽已下降，仍有 $g_Y>g_P$ | 继续向右，靠近 C |
| C：$y=Y_4$ | $g_Y=g_P>0$ | 人均收入不变 |
| C 右侧图示区间，含 $Y_5$ | $g_Y<g_P$，两率仍为正 | 人均收入向左减少，靠近 C |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Position | Comparison | Movement of income per person |
|---|---|---|
| Just left of A | $g_Y>g_P$ | Rightward toward A |
| $y_A<y<Y_2$ | $g_Y<g_P$ | Leftward toward A |
| B, at $y=Y_2$ | Equal rates | No movement exactly at B; a small deviation to either side moves away from B |
| $Y_2<y<Y_3$ | $g_Y>g_P$ | Rightward toward C |
| At $Y_3$ | Total-income growth peaks, but still exceeds population growth | Still rightward; a growth-rate peak is not a per-capita-income equilibrium |
| $Y_3<y<Y_4$ | The solid curve is falling, but $g_Y>g_P$ | Still rightward toward C |
| C, at $y=Y_4$ | Equal positive rates | Income per person is unchanged |
| The displayed region right of C, including $Y_5$ | Both rates are positive, with $g_Y<g_P$ | Leftward toward C |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续调整下，A 和 C 的左右箭头都朝内，B 的左右箭头朝外。因此 A、C 是局部吸引的均衡，B 是不稳定门槛。跨过 B 后进入 C 的吸引区，并不等于人均收入从此永久增长：固定这两条曲线时，到了 C 人均增长仍归零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With continuous adjustment, the arrows point inward at A and C and outward at B. A and C are locally attracting equilibria; B is an unstable threshold. Crossing B enters C's basin of attraction, but does not establish permanently positive per-capita growth: with both curves fixed, that growth reaches zero at C.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 稳定性判断适用于什么动态

上表的连续解释取 $\dot y=y[g_Y(y)-g_P(y)]$，假设曲线在交点附近足够光滑。看交点两侧高低关系就能判断局部吸引或排斥；示意图没有给出经济需要多久才靠近交点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The continuous interpretation uses $\dot y=y[g_Y(y)-g_P(y)]$, with sufficiently smooth curves near each crossing. Their ordering on either side determines local attraction or repulsion. The schematic diagram does not specify how long adjustment takes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若把一整期当作离散跳步，不能仅凭“两侧朝内”就保证收敛。令 $H(y)=y(1+g_Y(y))/(1+g_P(y))$，在交点 $y^*$ 附近线性化：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a discrete jump of a full period, inward directions alone do not guarantee convergence. Set $H(y)=y(1+g_Y(y))/(1+g_P(y))$ and linearize near a crossing $y^*$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\lambda=H'(y^*)
=1+\frac{y^*[g_Y'(y^*)-g_P'(y^*)]}{1+g_P(y^*)}.
$$

$|\lambda|<1$ 保证局部渐近稳定，$|\lambda|>1$ 则不稳定；$|\lambda|=1$ 时线性化不能决定。这里复用的是[[离散系统谱稳定性|线性化误差每期缩小的判据]]。A/C 的曲线差从正变负，不排除粗跳步反复越过均衡甚至越跳越远，所以课堂连续箭头不能代替离散调整的幅度检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$|\lambda|<1$ guarantees local asymptotic stability, while $|\lambda|>1$ implies instability; at $|\lambda|=1$, linearization is inconclusive. This applies the [[离散系统谱稳定性|contraction criterion for the linearized error]]. At A or C, the gap changes from positive to negative, but a coarse step may overshoot repeatedly and even diverge. Continuous arrows cannot replace checking the size of discrete adjustments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与零人口增长的区别

A 和 C 相等的是**总收入增长率与人口增长率**，不是出生率与死亡率；图中两者都在正增长位置。因此它们的人均收入均衡不能直接认作[[马尔萨斯生存收入|零人口净增长阈值]]。[[马尔萨斯技术冲击|固定技术与土地的人口反馈模型]]把生产与人口方程一起写出，稳态人口可以不变；本图另给了一条总收入增长曲线，允许总收入与人口共同增长。两种表达共享反馈直觉，但须分别检查方程和均衡条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A and C equate **total-income growth with population growth**, not births with deaths; both crossings lie at positive growth rates. Their per-capita-income equilibria cannot simply be identified with a [[马尔萨斯生存收入|zero-net-population-growth threshold]]. The [[马尔萨斯技术冲击|fixed-technology, fixed-land feedback model]] specifies production and population together and can have a constant population in steady state. This diagram supplies a separate total-income growth curve and permits income and population to grow together. The feedback intuition is related, but the equations and equilibrium conditions require separate checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
