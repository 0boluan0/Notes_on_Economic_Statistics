# Dot product
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*向量点积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **dot product** combines two real vectors into one number by multiplying matching coordinates and adding the results. It is useful when we want to measure how two vectors align: the result depends on both their directions and their lengths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**点积**把两个实向量合成一个数：对应坐标相乘，再把结果相加。它可以衡量两个向量的方向配合，但结果同时受方向和长度影响。

> [!example] Multiply matching coordinates · 对应坐标相乘
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $u=(3,4)$ and $v=(2,-1)$. Their first coordinates contribute $3\times2=6$, and their second coordinates contribute $4\times(-1)=-4$. Adding gives $u\cdot v=6-4=2$. The answer is a scalar, meaning a single real number. Keeping the two products as the pair $(6,-4)$ would be a different operation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $u=(3,4)$、$v=(2,-1)$。第一对坐标贡献 $3\times2=6$，第二对贡献 $4\times(-1)=-4$，相加得到 $u\cdot v=6-4=2$。答案是标量，即一个实数。如果把两个乘积保留成 $(6,-4)$，那是另一种运算。

## From coordinates to the general rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从坐标到一般规则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mathbb R^n$ means lists of $n$ real coordinates, with a fixed positive integer $n$. These lists form a [[向量空间|vector space]]: they can be added coordinate by coordinate and multiplied by a real number. For $u=(u_1,\ldots,u_n)$ and $v=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ in that same space, define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $\mathbb R^n$ 表示由 $n$ 个实坐标组成的列表，$n$ 是固定的正整数。这些列表构成[[向量空间]]，可以逐坐标相加，也可以乘以一个实数。对同一空间中的 $u=(u_1,\ldots,u_n)$、$v=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$，定义

$$
u\cdot v=\sum_{i=1}^n u_iv_i=u_1v_1+\cdots+u_nv_n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The index $i$ selects the same coordinate position in both vectors; the summation includes every position once. Their numbers of coordinates must match. If vectors are written as columns, [[矩阵转置|transposing]] $u$ makes a $1\times n$ row, so the [[矩阵乘法|matrix product]] $u^Tv$ gives the same scalar. Writing $uv^T$ instead produces an $n\times n$ matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下标 $i$ 选择两个向量中相同位置的坐标，求和把每个位置计入一次；两个向量的坐标个数必须相同。若写成列向量，$u$ 的[[矩阵转置|转置]]是 $1\times n$ 行向量，因此[[矩阵乘法|矩阵乘积]] $u^Tv$ 给出同一个标量。写成 $uv^T$ 则会得到 $n\times n$ 矩阵。

## What the sign and size tell us
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*符号与大小分别告诉我们什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A vector dotted with itself gives a sum of squares: $u\cdot u=\sum_i u_i^2=\|u\|_2^2$. Here $\|u\|_2$ is its [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]]. For $u=(3,4)$, this is $25$, so its length is $5$. A dot product between different vectors may be positive, zero or negative; only the self-product is always nonnegative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

向量与自身点积得到平方和：$u\cdot u=\sum_i u_i^2=\|u\|_2^2$，其中 $\|u\|_2$ 是[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]。对 $u=(3,4)$，自身点积为 $25$，长度因此为 $5$。不同向量之间的点积可以为正、零或负；只有自身点积总是非负。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two nonzero vectors in ordinary two- or three-dimensional geometry, let $\theta$ be their angle, between $0$ and $\pi$ radians. The coordinate calculation is connected to geometry by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对普通二维或三维几何中的两个非零向量，令 $\theta$ 为它们的夹角，范围是 $0$ 到 $\pi$ 弧度。坐标计算与几何由下式连接：

$$
u\cdot v=\|u\|_2\|v\|_2\cos\theta,
\qquad
\cos\theta=\frac{u\cdot v}{\|u\|_2\|v\|_2}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cosine is positive for an angle below $90^\circ$, zero at $90^\circ$, and negative above $90^\circ$. For the example $u=(3,4)$, $v=(2,-1)$, it equals $2/(5\sqrt5)>0$. Replacing $v$ by $-v$ reverses the dot-product sign. With $w=(4,-3)$, the two contributions cancel: $u\cdot w=12-12=0$, so $u$ and $w$ are [[正交|orthogonal]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

夹角小于 $90^\circ$ 时余弦为正，等于 $90^\circ$ 时为零，大于 $90^\circ$ 时为负。原例 $u=(3,4)$、$v=(2,-1)$ 的余弦为 $2/(5\sqrt5)>0$。把 $v$ 换成 $-v$ 会翻转点积的符号。若取 $w=(4,-3)$，则两项抵消：$u\cdot w=12-12=0$，所以 $u$ 与 $w$ [[正交]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The angle formula follows by expanding $\|u-v\|_2^2=\|u\|_2^2+\|v\|_2^2-2u\cdot v$ and comparing it with the cosine rule for the triangle formed by the two arrows. In higher-dimensional $\mathbb R^n$, the same ratio defines the Euclidean angle; [[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式|Cauchy–Schwarz]] guarantees that it lies in $[-1,1]$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

夹角公式可以这样理解：展开 $\|u-v\|_2^2=\|u\|_2^2+\|v\|_2^2-2u\cdot v$，再与两个箭头构成的三角形的余弦定理比较。在更高维的 $\mathbb R^n$ 中，用同一个比值定义欧氏夹角；[[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式]]保证比值属于 $[-1,1]$。

> [!warning] An alignment score still contains scale · 点积仍然带着长度尺度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Doubling $u$ doubles $u\cdot v$ even though its direction is unchanged. To compare directions alone, divide by both nonzero lengths. If either vector is zero, its dot product with every vector is zero, but the angle formula divides by zero and cannot be used: the zero vector has no direction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把 $u$ 加倍，方向不变，$u\cdot v$ 却会加倍。若只想比较方向，需要除以两个非零长度。若任一向量为零，它与任何向量的点积都为零，但夹角公式会除以零，不能使用：零向量没有方向。

## How dot products behave under addition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*点积如何处理加法与缩放*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Coordinate arithmetic gives symmetry and linearity in either argument. For real numbers $a,b$ and vectors of the same dimension,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逐坐标运算给出对称性，以及对每一个自变量的线性性。对实数 $a,b$ 和同维向量，有

$$
u\cdot v=v\cdot u,\qquad
(au+bw)\cdot v=a(u\cdot v)+b(w\cdot v).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the second identity, expand each term $(au_i+bw_i)v_i=au_iv_i+bw_iv_i$ and add over $i$. Symmetry gives the corresponding rule in the second argument. These are scalar-weighted sums, as in a [[张成|linear combination]]. They do not make three-vector dot products associative: after one dot product the result is a number, so the next operation must be specified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二条只需把每一项展开为 $(au_i+bw_i)v_i=au_iv_i+bw_iv_i$，再对 $i$ 求和。对称性给出第二个自变量中的对应规则；这里的标量加权相加与[[张成|线性组合]]相同。这并不赋予三个向量点积结合律：做完一次点积已经得到一个数，下一步是什么运算必须另外说明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card uses the standard dot product on real coordinate vectors. Its length-and-angle interpretation assumes perpendicular unit coordinate axes. General inner products can assign other weights and geometry. For complex coordinates, an ordinary transpose does not supply the usual positive inner product: for $z=(i)$, $z^Tz=-1$, while the squared length is $1$. The complex version needs conjugation; the broader conventions are discussed in [[正交|orthogonality]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡使用实坐标向量的标准点积；其长度和夹角解释采用彼此垂直、长度为一的坐标轴。一般内积可以赋予不同权重与几何。复坐标下，普通转置不能提供通常的正定内积：对 $z=(i)$，有 $z^Tz=-1$，但长度平方应为 $1$。复数版本需要共轭；更广的约定见[[正交]]。
