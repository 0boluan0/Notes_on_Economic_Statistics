# Recurrence and holding times
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*常返性与停留时间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A route tells us whether a state will be revisited. A clock tells us how long that revisit takes. For a [[CTMC定义|continuous-time Markov chain]], these are different questions: [[嵌入跳链|the embedded jump chain]] determines recurrence, but its average number of steps cannot be used as the average calendar return time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

路线告诉我们会不会再次访问某状态，时钟告诉我们等多久。对于[[CTMC定义|连续时间 Markov 链]]，这是两个不同问题：[[嵌入跳链]]决定常返性，但平均回返步数不能当作平均日历回返时间。

> [!example] Two jumps, but two different waits · 两次跳跃与两段不同等待
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose the path must alternate $0\to1\to0\to1\to\cdots$, with exit rates $q_0=2$ and $q_1=1$ per hour. The first return to 0 always takes two jumps. In calendar time, it includes a mean $1/2$ hour at 0 and a mean $1$ hour at 1, for a total mean $3/2$ hours. A particular return could be much faster or slower.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设路径必定按 $0\to1\to0\to1\to\cdots$ 交替，出口率为每小时 $q_0=2$、$q_1=1$。首次返回 0 一定经过两跳；但日历时间包括在 0 平均等 $1/2$ 小时、在 1 平均等 $1$ 小时，总均值为 $3/2$ 小时。某次具体回返可能更快，也可能更慢。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A return must follow a departure

Use a finite or countable, time-homogeneous [[最小CTMC|minimal CTMC]] with a [[生成矩阵约束|stable conservative generator]] $Q$. Write $q_j=-q_{jj}<\infty$ for the [[出口率|exit rate]] at state $j$. Start at a nonabsorbing state $i$, so $q_i>0$. Let $T_0=0,T_1,T_2,\ldots$ be genuine jump times and $J_n$ the state after jump $n$. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*回返必须发生在离开之后*

考虑有限或可数、时间齐次的[[最小CTMC|最小 CTMC]]，生成矩阵 $Q$ [[生成矩阵约束|稳定且保守]]。记 $q_j=-q_{jj}<\infty$ 为状态 $j$ 的[[出口率]]。从非吸收状态 $i$ 出发，所以 $q_i>0$。令 $T_0=0,T_1,T_2,\ldots$ 为真实跳时，$J_n$ 为第 $n$ 跳之后的状态，定义

$$\tau_i^+=\inf\{n\ge1:J_n=i\},\qquad
C_i=T_{\tau_i^+}\quad\text{if }\tau_i^+<\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The variable $\tau_i^+$ counts jumps until first return; $C_i$ measures elapsed time from the initial entry at time zero to that return. If no return occurs, set both to $\infty$, even if the path explodes earlier. Do not replace $C_i$ on that event by the explosion time. The first holding period at $i$ is included in $C_i$.

Using $\inf\{t>0:X_t=i\}$ instead would give zero, because the chain initially stays at $i$ for a positive interval. Equivalently, the intended return is $\inf\{t\ge T_1:X_t=i\}$, after the first departure. If one measures only the excursion after leaving, the mean differs by the finite initial holding mean $1/q_i$; finiteness is unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\tau_i^+$ 计数首次回返所需跳数，$C_i$ 从时刻零的初始进入开始，计量到此次回返为止的时间。不回返时，两者都设为 $\infty$，即使路径更早爆炸也如此，不能把此时的 $C_i$ 换成爆炸时间。$C_i$ 包含最初在 $i$ 的停留。

若改用 $\inf\{t>0:X_t=i\}$，结果会是零，因为链最初就在 $i$ 停留一段正时间。这里真正要的是第一次离开后的 $\inf\{t\ge T_1:X_t=i\}$。若只计离开后在外游走的时长，其均值与本卡的均值相差有限的 $1/q_i$，所以是否有限的分类不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Recurrence is preserved

For a nonabsorbing state, return occurs in the CTMC exactly when it occurs at a finite index of the jump chain. A finite returning route has finitely many finite exponential waits. Therefore the [[常返与暂态|recurrent/transient classification]] agrees: recurrence means $\Pr_i(\tau_i^+<\infty)=\Pr_i(C_i<\infty)=1$; transience means this probability is less than one. Here $\Pr_i$ denotes probability when started at $i$.

This also gives the calendar-time meaning of recurrence: visits to $i$ occur at arbitrarily large times almost surely. Indeed, a recurrent jump chain visits $i$ infinitely often. Each visit contributes an independent exponential wait of the same positive mean $1/q_i$. By the [[强大数定律|strong law of large numbers]], their sum diverges almost surely, so these visits cannot be compressed into a finite explosion time. If the jump chain is transient, there are only finitely many visits to $i$ almost surely, each of finite duration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*常返性保持不变*

对非吸收状态，CTMC 发生回返，当且仅当跳链在有限下标回返。一条有限的回返路线，只包含有限个几乎必定有限的指数等待。因此，两者的[[常返与暂态|常返／暂态分类]]相同：常返表示 $\Pr_i(\tau_i^+<\infty)=\Pr_i(C_i<\infty)=1$，暂态表示这个概率小于一；$\Pr_i$ 表示从 $i$ 出发时的概率。

这也给出日历时间中的常返含义：几乎必然会在任意晚的时刻再次访问 $i$。因为常返跳链会无限次访问 $i$，每次都贡献一个独立、相同正均值 $1/q_i$ 的指数等待，由[[强大数定律]]，其总和几乎必然发散，所以这些访问不能挤进有限的爆炸时间。跳链若暂态，则几乎必然只访问 $i$ 有限次，每次时长也有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $q_i=0$, the state is absorbing and remains occupied forever. It is recurrent under this arbitrarily-late-occupation convention, as is the jump chain completed with a self-loop. There is no genuine departure cycle; formulas that divide by $q_i$ do not apply. The single absorbing state supports the stationary point mass there and is treated separately from the return-cycle definitions below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $q_i=0$，状态吸收，永远被占据，按“任意晚仍会处于这里”的口径属于常返；补自环后的跳链也常返。但它没有真实的离开—返回周期，不能套用除以 $q_i$ 的公式。单个吸收态上的点质量是平稳概率；它需要与下面的回返周期定义分开处理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A finite jump count need not give a finite mean duration

Now suppose the nonabsorbing state $i$ is recurrent. The [[正常返与零常返|positive/null recurrence distinction]] asks whether the relevant mean return is finite. In the jump chain it uses $E_i\tau_i^+$; in the CTMC it uses $E_iC_i$. Here $E_i$ is expectation for the chosen start. In particular, the CTMC state is positive recurrent if $E_iC_i<\infty$, and null recurrent if $E_iC_i=\infty$.

Conditional on a returning state route, each visit to $J_n$ contributes mean holding time $1/q_{J_n}$. Since all durations are nonnegative, summing them and then taking expectation gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有限回返步数不保证有限平均时长*

现在假设非吸收状态 $i$ 常返。[[正常返与零常返|正常返／零常返]]关注相应平均回返量是否有限：跳链看 $E_i\tau_i^+$，CTMC 看 $E_iC_i$；$E_i$ 是从指定状态出发的期望。具体而言，CTMC 的 $E_iC_i<\infty$ 称正常返，$E_iC_i=\infty$ 称零常返。

给定一条回返状态路线，每次访问 $J_n$ 都贡献平均停留 $1/q_{J_n}$。因为时长非负，可以先求和再取期望，得到

$$E_iC_i=E_i\!\left[\sum_{n=0}^{\tau_i^+-1}\frac1{q_{J_n}}\right],$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
with infinity allowed. This formula is being used for a recurrent start, where the return index is finite almost surely. It replaces “one unit per jump” by a state-dependent duration. Multiplying one state's positive rate by $100$ divides its contribution per visit by $100$, without changing whether the route returns. In a finite closed irreducible class with at least two states, all positive rates have a positive minimum, and the finite jump chain has finite mean returns; hence the CTMC is positive recurrent. Countably infinite classes have no such automatic bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中允许结果为无穷。本式用于常返起点，此时回返下标几乎必定有限。它把“每跳算一单位”换成了随状态变化的时长。把某状态的正速率乘 $100$，便把每次访问该状态的时间贡献除以 $100$，不改变路线能否回返。在至少含两个状态的有限闭不可约类中，所有正率有共同正下界，有限跳链的平均回返步数也有限，因此 CTMC 正常返；可数无限类没有这种自动保证。

> [!example] Always two jumps, but infinite mean calendar time · 总是两跳，平均日历时间却无穷
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> From state 0, choose a positive integer $j$ with probability $2^{-j}$; from $j$, return directly to 0. These probabilities sum to one. The jump chain returns to 0 in exactly two jumps. Give state 0 rate $q_0=1$, and state $j\ge1$ rate $q_j=2^{-j}$. Every chosen wait is finite almost surely, but its conditional mean at $j$ is $2^j$. Thus
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$E_0C_0=1+\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}2^{-j}2^j=\infty.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The CTMC returns with probability one but is null recurrent, whereas the jump chain is positive recurrent. All exit rates are at most 1, so [[有界率非爆炸|a common finite rate bound prevents explosion]]. If instead every exit rate is 1, the same jump chain gives $E_0C_0=2$. The difference comes entirely from the clock.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 0 以概率 $2^{-j}$ 选择正整数 $j$，从 $j$ 直接返回 0；这些概率总和为一，所以跳链总是恰好两跳回到 0。令 $q_0=1$、$q_j=2^{-j}$。每次选中的等待几乎必然有限，但在 $j$ 的条件均值为 $2^j$，于是得到上式的无穷均值。CTMC 必然回返，却是零常返；跳链则正常返。所有出口率不超过 1，由[[有界率非爆炸|统一有限率上界]]可知不会爆炸。如果把全部出口率改为 1，同一跳链便有 $E_0C_0=2$。差别完全来自时钟。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same conversion explains why stationary jump-visit proportions differ from stationary calendar-time proportions: the latter must count the duration of each visit. That calculation is developed in [[跳链频率时间加权|holding-time weighting]]. The discrete [[Kac回返公式|Kac formula]] uses a step-count return and should not be transferred unchanged to $C_i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样的换算解释了为何平稳的跳链访问比例与日历时间占比不同：后者还要计入每次停留的时长，具体计算见[[跳链频率时间加权]]。离散[[Kac回返公式]]采用回返步数，不能不改就套给 $C_i$。
