# 腾落线是市场每日上涨家数减下跌家数的累积序列
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The advance-decline line cumulatively sums the daily difference between advancing and declining issue counts.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

腾落线（advance-decline line，课程缩写 ADL）把市场每天上涨股票家数减去下跌家数，再累加到前一期数值，用来描述市场参与面的变化。本卡的 ADL 指市场宽度腾落线，与使用价量计算的 accumulation/distribution line 是不同对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The advance-decline line, abbreviated ADL in the course, adds each day's advancing-minus-declining issue count to its previous value to describe market participation. Here ADL refers to market breadth, distinct from the price-and-volume accumulation/distribution line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定统计股票池、交易日和可比前收价格口径，令 $A_t$ 为上涨家数、$D_t$ 为下跌家数；持平不计入两者。以指定基期 $ADL_0=0$ 开始：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify the universe, trading dates, and comparable prior-close basis. Let $A_t$ and $D_t$ count advances and declines, excluding unchanged issues from both. Starting at a specified base with $ADL_0=0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
ADL_t=ADL_{t-1}+A_t-D_t
=\sum_{i=1}^{t}(A_i-D_i).
$$

例如三期上涨家数 $6,2,7$、下跌家数 $3,6,2$，净家数依次为 $3,-4,5$，因此腾落线依次为 $3,-1,4$。若基期改为 $100$，同段曲线变为 $103,99,104$；增量和转向不变，绝对水平改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For advancing counts $6,2,7$ and declining counts $3,6,2$, net advances are $3,-4,5$, giving line values $3,-1,4$. A starting value of $100$ instead gives $103,99,104$: increments and turning directions stay the same, while levels shift.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每家证券对当日净家数只贡献 $+1$、$-1$ 或 $0$；涨跌幅度和市值权重没有进入公式。因此腾落线与市值加权指数可能方向不同，仅此不能推出指数即将反转。使用历史序列时还须一致处理成分变化、拆股等导致的前收不可比与缺失报价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each issue contributes only $+1$, $-1$, or $0$ to daily net advances; return magnitudes and market-cap weights do not enter the formula. The line may therefore move differently from a capitalization-weighted index, which alone does not establish an imminent reversal. Historical comparisons also require consistent treatment of universe changes, corporate actions affecting prior-close comparability, and missing quotes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[OBOS]] 取固定窗口的涨跌家数累计差，腾落线则从基期持续累加。相同股票池、涨跌分类和时间口径下，$N$ 期 OBOS 等于腾落线当期值减去 $N$ 期前值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[OBOS|OBOS]] accumulates net advances over a fixed window, while the advance-decline line accumulates from its base date. With matching universes, classifications, and periods, $N$-period OBOS equals the current line value minus its value $N$ periods earlier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
