# 偏好数据中的长度相关可能被奖励模型学成评分捷径，并被 RL 或 DPO 放大为输出变长；评审器的长度偏好必须另行检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Length correlations in preference data can become reward-model shortcuts and be amplified into longer outputs by RL or DPO; evaluator length bias must be tested separately*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] “更长”可能是质量信号，也可能只是捷径
> 若训练数据中 chosen 比 rejected 系统性更长，长度就同时携带两种信息：答案可能真的更完整，也可能只是评估者、采样器或模板留下的代理特征。奖励模型可以用这个容易识别的特征预测标签；DPO 也可以直接提高带有该特征的回答优势。优化后长度差可能大于原始配对中的差异，因此总胜率上升不能单独证明内容质量上升。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If chosen responses are systematically longer than rejected responses, length carries both genuine completeness and a possible shortcut left by evaluators, samplers, or templates. A reward model can use the shortcut to predict labels, and DPO can directly favour responses carrying it. Optimisation may amplify the original length difference, so a higher aggregate win rate does not by itself establish better content.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 数据相关、奖励捷径、优化放大与评审偏差是四层问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Data correlation, reward shortcut, optimisation, and evaluator bias are four different layers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一层是**偏好数据相关**：chosen 与 rejected 的长度分布不同。第二层是**奖励模型捷径**：在显式奖励模型的流程中，模型可能借长度预测偏好标签，而不是只识别任务质量。第三层是**优化放大**：RL 可沿着奖励模型的长度捷径改变输出；DPO 虽然不训练显式奖励模型，也可能直接利用偏好对中的长度相关。第四层是**评审器偏差**：特定自动评审器可能在应该选较短回答时仍更常选较长回答，使表面胜率进一步受长度影响。四层可以同时出现，但不能只由其中一层推断其余三层。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first layer is a correlation in preference data: chosen and rejected responses have different length distributions. The second is a reward-model shortcut: in pipelines with an explicit reward model, length may help predict preference labels without faithfully tracking task quality. The third is optimisation amplification: RL can follow that reward shortcut, while DPO, despite having no explicit reward model, can directly exploit length correlations in preference pairs. The fourth is evaluator bias: a particular automatic judge may still choose the longer response when the human label favours the shorter one. The four layers can coexist, but no single layer proves the other three.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长度本身也不是错误。复杂证明、完整风险说明或逐步操作通常需要更多 token；简短答案也可能遗漏关键条件。真正要排除的是“在任务信息量不变时，仅靠拉长文字就获得更高偏好分”。因此不能把正则化目标写成“越短越好”，也不能把所有 RLHF 或 DPO 的改进都归因于长度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Length is not itself an error. A proof, a risk explanation, or a procedure may legitimately require more tokens, while a short answer can omit essential conditions. The concern is gaining preference score merely by expanding text while task-relevant information is held fixed. The remedy is therefore not “shorter is always better,” and empirical length effects do not reduce every RLHF or DPO improvement to verbosity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 有效比较要固定或显式调整长度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A valid comparison holds length fixed or adjusts for it explicitly*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可用的证据至少包括两种互补比较：

- 在近似同长度回答中比较正确性、相关性、覆盖条件与无依据主张；
- 把同一内容改写成长短版本，检查评估器是否在事实与任务完成度不变时仍系统偏向长版。

报告时应并列给出原始胜率、长度控制后的胜率、平均长度差和按质量维度拆开的误差。只把长度作为普通协变量塞进一次回归，可以描述条件相关，却不能自动识别“若把同一回答变短会怎样”的因果效应；匹配、受控重写或随机化展示能提供更接近目标的证据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Useful evidence combines comparisons among responses of similar length with controlled long and short rewrites of the same content. Reports should show raw win rate, length-controlled win rate, mean length differences, and rubric-level errors. Adding length to an observational regression describes conditional association; it does not by itself identify what would happen if the same answer were shortened. Matching, controlled rewriting, or randomised presentation provides stronger evidence for that question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> DPO 模型对基线的自动胜率从 50% 升至 62%，平均回答长度同时翻倍。现有结果最多支持什么？下一步怎样区分长度捷径与真实质量提升？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A DPO model's automatic win rate over a baseline rises from 50% to 62%, while its mean response length doubles. What does this establish, and how should length shortcuts be separated from genuine quality gains?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 它只支持“在当前评估器与输出分布下，较长的 DPO 输出更常获胜”。应补同长度匹配或长度控制胜率，并用事实性、相关性和完整性分项的盲评比较；若可以，再随机展示同一内容的长短改写。长答案不因此必然更差，62% 也不因此必然全是长度造成。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** It establishes only that the longer DPO outputs win more often under the current evaluator and response distribution. Add matched-length or length-controlled win rates and blinded rubric scores for factuality, relevance, and completeness; ideally randomise long and short rewrites of the same content. Longer answers are not thereby always worse, nor is the entire 62% necessarily caused by length.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Singhal et al. (2024), “A Long Way to Go: Investigating Length Correlations in RLHF”](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=G8LaO1P0xv)：核对三个公开任务设定中偏好数据的长度相关、奖励提升与输出长度增长的关系。该实验说明这些数据与模型中的显著机制，不证明所有任务或所有 RLHF 必然偏好长答案。
- [Park et al. (2024), “Disentangling Length from Quality in Direct Preference Optimization”](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.297/)：核对 DPO 在其摘要与对话数据上的长度利用、分布外自举解释及长度正则实验。论文范围不支持“DPO 的所有收益都是长度”或“长回答总是低质”。
- [Saito et al. (2023), “Verbosity Bias in Preference Labeling by Large Language Models”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10076)：核对其问题设置中，在人类标签偏好较短回答的配对里，GPT-4 仍更常选择较长回答。该结果限定于论文使用的 judge、候选回答和评价设计，不代表所有自动或人工评审。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Singhal et al. (2024) was checked for correlations between preference labels, reward gains, and response length across its three public experimental settings. These experiments identify a substantial mechanism in those data and models, not an inevitability for every RLHF task.
- Park et al. (2024) was checked for length exploitation by DPO in the paper's summarisation and dialogue settings, its out-of-distribution bootstrapping account, and its length-regularisation experiments. The paper does not show that every DPO gain is caused by length or that long answers are always low quality.
- Saito et al. (2023) was checked for cases in which GPT-4 more often selected the longer response even when the human preference label selected the shorter one. The result is bounded by its judge, candidates, and evaluation design and does not characterise every automatic or human evaluator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
