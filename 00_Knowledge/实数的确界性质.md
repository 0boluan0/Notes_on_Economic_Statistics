# Least-upper-bound property of the real numbers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*实数的确界性质*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing that a set fits below some ceiling does not, by definition alone, produce its tightest ceiling. The real numbers provide an existence guarantee: every nonempty set of real numbers that is bounded above has a real [[上确界|supremum]], its least upper bound. This is the **least-upper-bound property**, also called order completeness of the real numbers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知道一个集合能放在某个上界之下，并不能仅凭定义就得到最紧的上界。实数给出一个存在保证：每个非空、有上界的实数集合，都存在实数[[上确界]]，即最小上界。这就是**实数的确界性质**，也称实数的序完备性。

> [!important] The existence guarantee · 存在保证
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A\subseteq\mathbb R$ be nonempty. If a real number $M$ satisfies $a\le M$ for every $a\in A$, then a unique real number $s=\sup A$ exists. It bounds all members of $A$ and lies below or at every other upper bound. The property does not require $s\in A$ or a lower bound on $A$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $A\subseteq\mathbb R$ 非空。若存在实数 $M$，使每个 $a\in A$ 都满足 $a\le M$，则存在唯一实数 $s=\sup A$。它界住所有集合成员，并且不高于任何其他上界。这个性质不要求 $s\in A$，也不要求 $A$ 有下界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $A=\{x\in\mathbb R:x<1\}$ extends indefinitely to the left, but has supremum $1$. Its supremum is not a maximum because $1\notin A$. Existence is the substantive guarantee here; uniqueness follows directly from the definition of a least upper bound. The guarantee is a completeness property of $\mathbb R$, often included among its defining axioms, rather than a consequence of merely having an ordered number system.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$A=\{x\in\mathbb R:x<1\}$ 向左无限延伸，却有上确界 $1$；由于 $1\notin A$，它没有最大值。这里实质性的保证是存在性；唯一性直接来自最小上界的定义。这是 $\mathbb R$ 的完备性质，通常作为定义实数的公理之一，而不是任何有大小次序的数系都会自动具备的性质。

## Why rational numbers leave a gap
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么有理数会留下缺口*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\mathbb Q$ denote the rational numbers, fractions of integers with nonzero denominator. Consider
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $\mathbb Q$ 表示有理数，即分母非零的整数分数。考虑

$$B=\{q\in\mathbb Q: q>0,\ q^2<2\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This set is nonempty because $1\in B$, and $2$ is an upper bound. Numbers such as $1.4$ and $1.41$ belong to it, with squares $1.96$ and $1.9881$. Nevertheless, **there is no least upper bound within $\mathbb Q$**. Any rational candidate falls on one side of the gap: if its square is below $2$, it can be increased while staying in $B$; if its square is above $2$, it can be lowered while remaining an upper bound. Equality would require a rational square root of $2$, which does not exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $1\in B$，这个集合非空；$2$ 是它的上界。$1.4$ 和 $1.41$ 等数属于它，平方分别为 $1.96$ 和 $1.9881$。然而，**在 $\mathbb Q$ 内找不到最小上界**。任何有理数候选都落在缺口的一侧：平方小于 $2$，就还能增大并留在 $B$ 内；平方大于 $2$，就还能降低并保持上界身份。若平方恰为 $2$，则需要有理数形式的 $\sqrt2$，而这样的数不存在。

> [!example]- Check every rational candidate · 逐类排除有理数候选
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose a rational $s$ were the least upper bound. Since $1\in B$ and $2$ is an upper bound, $1\le s\le2$.
>
> If $s^2<2$, choose the positive rational $h=(2-s^2)/(2(2s+1))$. Here $h\le1/6<1$, so
> $$(s+h)^2=s^2+2sh+h^2<s^2+h(2s+1)=\frac{s^2+2}{2}<2.$$
> Thus $s+h\in B$ is larger than the proposed upper bound.
>
> If $s^2>2$, put $h=(s^2-2)/(2s)>0$ and $u=s-h=(s^2+2)/(2s)>0$. Both are rational, and
> $$u^2=2+h^2>2.$$
> Every $q\in B$ is positive with $q^2<2<u^2$, hence $q<u$. This gives the smaller rational upper bound $u<s$.
>
> Finally, if $s^2=2$, write $s=m/n$ in lowest terms, with positive integers $m,n$. The equation $m^2=2n^2$ forces $m$ to be even. Writing $m=2k$ then gives $n^2=2k^2$, so $n$ is also even, contradicting lowest terms. All possibilities are excluded.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设有理数 $s$ 是最小上界。因为 $1\in B$，且 $2$ 是上界，所以 $1\le s\le2$。
>
> 若 $s^2<2$，选正有理数 $h=(2-s^2)/(2(2s+1))$。此时 $h\le1/6<1$，由上面的平方展开可得 $(s+h)^2<2$，于是比 $s$ 更大的 $s+h$ 仍在 $B$ 中，否定了 $s$ 是上界。
>
> 若 $s^2>2$，令 $h=(s^2-2)/(2s)>0$，并取 $u=s-h=(s^2+2)/(2s)>0$。它们都是有理数，且 $u^2=2+h^2>2$。每个 $q\in B$ 都为正，并有 $q^2<2<u^2$，故 $q<u$。于是找到了更小的有理数上界 $u<s$。
>
> 最后，若 $s^2=2$，把 $s=m/n$ 写成正整数的最简分数。$m^2=2n^2$ 迫使 $m$ 为偶数；再写 $m=2k$，得到 $n^2=2k^2$，迫使 $n$ 也为偶数，与最简分数矛盾。所有情况均被排除。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over the real numbers, the analogous set $A=\{x\in\mathbb R:x>0,\ x^2<2\}$ does have a real supremum $s$, by completeness. It again lies between $1$ and $2$. The same two perturbations above, now using real $h$, exclude $s^2<2$ and $s^2>2$. Thus $s^2=2$: the property supplies the positive real square root of $2$. This shows how an existence guarantee can produce a number that the rational system lacks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换成实数集合 $A=\{x\in\mathbb R:x>0,\ x^2<2\}$，完备性就保证存在实数上确界 $s$，且仍在 $1$ 与 $2$ 之间。沿用上面的两种微调，只是现在允许 $h$ 为实数，就能排除 $s^2<2$ 和 $s^2>2$。因此 $s^2=2$：确界性质提供了 $2$ 的正实数平方根。这展示了存在保证如何给出有理数系缺少的数。

## Keep the assumptions visible
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*不能省略的条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The empty set has every real number as an upper bound, because it has no member that could violate the bound. There is no least such bound: for any candidate $u$, $u-1$ is another, smaller one. A nonempty set can instead fail by being unbounded above. For $A=\mathbb R$, any proposed bound $M$ is exceeded by the member $M+1$. Neither case has a real supremum. Conventions such as $\sup\varnothing=-\infty$ or $\sup\mathbb R=+\infty$ belong to the extended real numbers and do not remove the theorem's assumptions for a **real-valued** supremum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

空集的每个实数都是上界，因为没有成员能够违反这个界。但这些上界没有最小者：任何候选 $u$ 之下，都还有上界 $u-1$。非空集合也可能因为无上界而不适用：对 $A=\mathbb R$，任取候选上界 $M$，成员 $M+1$ 就超过它。这两种情况都没有实数上确界。$\sup\varnothing=-\infty$、$\sup\mathbb R=+\infty$ 等约定属于扩展实数，并不取消本性质对**实数值**上确界的条件要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reflecting a nonempty, bounded-below set through zero gives a nonempty, bounded-above set. Therefore completeness also guarantees a real infimum, through the [[上确界#The lower boundary by reflection|reflection identity]] $\inf A=-\sup(-A)$. Neither guarantee says the bound is attained; deciding attainment requires more information about the set or the function producing its values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把非空、有下界的集合关于零反射，就得到非空、有上界的集合。因此，完备性也通过[[上确界#The lower boundary by reflection|反射恒等式]] $\inf A=-\sup(-A)$ 保证实数下确界存在。这两个存在保证都不表示界一定能取到；判断是否取到，还要了解集合本身或产生这些值的函数。
