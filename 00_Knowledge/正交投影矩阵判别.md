# Recognising an orthogonal projection matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*正交投影矩阵判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[正交投影|orthogonal projection]] keeps a vector's component in a chosen subspace and discards a perpendicular remainder. A matrix describing this operation must do two things: leave an already projected vector unchanged, and separate the retained and discarded directions at right angles. This criterion turns those geometric requirements into two matrix equalities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[正交投影]]保留向量在指定子空间中的分量，去掉与该子空间垂直的余量。描述这项操作的矩阵必须同时做到两件事：已经投影过的向量再投影时不变；保留与丢弃的方向彼此垂直。本判据把这两个几何要求变成两条矩阵等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A projection that averages two coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把两个坐标变成平均值的投影*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the real plane with the usual [[内积|inner product]], take
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在采用通常[[内积]]的实平面中，取

$$P=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
x=\begin{pmatrix}3\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
Px=\begin{pmatrix}2\\2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
x-Px=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The output lies on the line $S=\{(t,t)^T:t\in\mathbb R\}$. The residual $x-Px$ is perpendicular to every vector on that line because $1\cdot t+(-1)\cdot t=0$. Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], used to write coordinates as a column. Applying $P$ to $(2,2)^T$ keeps it unchanged. Direct multiplication gives $P^2=P$, and reflection of the entries across the diagonal gives $P^T=P$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输出落在直线 $S=\{(t,t)^T:t\in\mathbb R\}$ 上。残差 $x-Px$ 与这条线上的每个向量垂直，因为 $1\cdot t+(-1)\cdot t=0$。这里 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，用来把坐标写成列。再把 $P$ 作用于 $(2,2)^T$，结果不变。直接相乘得到 $P^2=P$；沿对角线交换元素，则有 $P^T=P$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The criterion fixes the inner product
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*判据必须先固定内积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $m\ge1$ be an integer and $P\in\mathbb F^{m\times m}$, where $\mathbb F$ is $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. Use the **standard coordinate inner product** $\langle x,y\rangle=x^*y$. The star denotes [[Hermitian 矩阵#Transpose, then conjugate|conjugate transpose]]: transpose and conjugate each entry; conjugation sends $a+bi$ to $a-bi$, where $i^2=-1$. For real entries, $P^*=P^T$. Perpendicularity means that this inner product is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $m\ge1$ 为整数，$P\in\mathbb F^{m\times m}$，其中 $\mathbb F$ 为 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。使用**标准坐标内积** $\langle x,y\rangle=x^*y$。星号表示[[Hermitian 矩阵#Transpose, then conjugate|共轭转置]]：先转置，再对各元素取共轭；共轭把 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$，其中 $i^2=-1$。对实数元素，$P^*=P^T$。这里的垂直表示该内积为零。

> [!important] Two conditions, together · 两个条件缺一不可
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The matrix $P$ is the orthogonal projector onto its range exactly when
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $P$ 是到自身值域的正交投影矩阵，当且仅当
>
> $$\boxed{P^2=P\quad\text{and}\quad P^*=P.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first condition is [[幂等矩阵|idempotence]]: two applications equal one. The second is self-adjointness in these coordinates, called [[实对称矩阵|symmetry]] over the reals and [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian symmetry]] over the complex numbers. Thus the real test is $P^2=P$ and $P^T=P$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一条是[[幂等矩阵|幂等性]]：作用两次等于一次。第二条是在这些坐标下的自伴条件，实数情形称为[[实对称矩阵|对称]]，复数情形称为 [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian 对称]]。因此实数判据就是 $P^2=P$ 与 $P^T=P$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The range $S=\operatorname{range}(P)=\{Px:x\in\mathbb F^m\}$ is the [[列空间|column space]], the set of possible outputs. The kernel $\ker(P)=\{x:Px=0\}$ is the [[零空间|nullspace]], containing the inputs discarded entirely. The criterion determines the subspace from $P$; to certify projection onto a separately prescribed subspace $S_0$, also check $\operatorname{range}(P)=S_0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

值域 $S=\operatorname{range}(P)=\{Px:x\in\mathbb F^m\}$ 就是[[列空间]]，即全部可能输出。核 $\ker(P)=\{x:Px=0\}$ 就是[[零空间]]，收集被全部丢弃的输入。判据从 $P$ 本身确定目标子空间；如果题目另外指定了 $S_0$，还要检查 $\operatorname{range}(P)=S_0$，才能确认投到的是它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why every orthogonal projector satisfies the test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*必要性：几何投影为何满足两条等式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $P$ is already known to project orthogonally onto a subspace $S$. A vector in $S$ is retained unchanged. Since $Px\in S$, applying $P$ again gives $P(Px)=Px$ for every $x$, hence $P^2=P$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先假设已知 $P$ 是到子空间 $S$ 的正交投影。位于 $S$ 中的向量会保持不变；因为 $Px\in S$，再次作用得到 $P(Px)=Px$。这对每个 $x$ 成立，所以 $P^2=P$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove the second condition, split two arbitrary vectors into their retained and perpendicular parts:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为证明第二条，把任意两个向量分成保留部分与垂直部分：

$$x=s+r,\qquad y=t+q,\qquad s,t\in S,\quad r,q\in S^\perp.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the [[正交补|orthogonal complement]] $S^\perp$ consists of vectors perpendicular to every member of $S$. Because $Px=s$ and $Py=t$, the cross terms vanish:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的[[正交补]] $S^\perp$ 由与 $S$ 中每个向量都垂直的向量组成。由于 $Px=s$、$Py=t$，交叉项为零：

$$\langle Px,y\rangle=\langle s,t+q\rangle
=\langle s,t\rangle
=\langle s+r,t\rangle=\langle x,Py\rangle.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In standard coordinates this says $x^*P^*y=x^*Py$ for all $x,y$. Taking $x$ and $y$ in turn to be the coordinate vectors with a single entry $1$ checks every matrix entry, so $P^*=P$. The argument works for both real and complex coordinates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在标准坐标下，这表示对所有 $x,y$ 都有 $x^*P^*y=x^*Py$。让 $x,y$ 依次取“一个坐标为 $1$、其余为 $0$”的坐标向量，就能检查每个矩阵元素，因此 $P^*=P$。这个论证同时适用于实数与复数坐标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the two matrix conditions are sufficient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 两个条件各自做什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now start only from $P^2=P$ and $P^*=P$. Let $I=I_m$ denote the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]], which leaves every input unchanged. For any $x$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在只假设 $P^2=P$ 与 $P^*=P$。令 $I=I_m$ 表示保持所有输入不变的[[单位矩阵]]。对任意 $x$，

$$x=Px+(I-P)x,\qquad P(I-P)x=(P-P^2)x=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first part is in $S=\operatorname{range}(P)$, and the second is in $\ker(P)$. Idempotence also fixes every vector $s=Pu\in S$, since $Ps=P^2u=Pu=s$. Self-adjointness supplies the perpendicularity: for arbitrary $u,v$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一部分在 $S=\operatorname{range}(P)$ 中，第二部分在 $\ker(P)$ 中。幂等性还保证每个 $s=Pu\in S$ 都固定不动，因为 $Ps=P^2u=Pu=s$。自伴条件则提供垂直关系：对任意 $u,v$，

$$\langle Pu,(I-P)v\rangle
=u^*P^*(I-P)v=u^*P(I-P)v=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $Px$ lies in $S$ and the whole residual $(I-P)x$ lies in $S^\perp$. This is precisely the defining decomposition for orthogonal projection. It is unique: if $x=s+r=s'+r'$ with the two retained parts in $S$ and the two remainders in $S^\perp$, then $s-s'=r'-r$ belongs to both spaces. Its inner product with itself is zero, forcing it to be the zero vector. Therefore the matrix rule really selects the unique orthogonal component.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 $Px$ 在 $S$ 中，而整个残差 $(I-P)x$ 在 $S^\perp$ 中，这正是正交投影的定义分解。分解也唯一：若 $x=s+r=s'+r'$，其中两个保留部分在 $S$ 中、两个余量在 $S^\perp$ 中，那么 $s-s'=r'-r$ 同时属于两个空间。它与自身的内积为零，只能是零向量。因此这个矩阵规则确实选出了唯一的正交分量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This also identifies the **entire** kernel. If $Px=0$, then $x=(I-P)x\in S^\perp$. Conversely, if $x\in S^\perp$, the decomposition $x=0+x$ already has the required form; uniqueness forces $Px=0$. Hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这还确定了**整个**核：若 $Px=0$，则 $x=(I-P)x\in S^\perp$；反过来，若 $x\in S^\perp$，分解 $x=0+x$ 已符合要求，唯一性迫使 $Px=0$。所以

$$\operatorname{range}(P)=S,\qquad \ker(P)=S^\perp.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The complementary matrix $I-P$ retains the removed component. It satisfies $(I-P)^2=I-P$ and $(I-P)^*=I-P$, and its range is $S^\perp$: it produces only perpendicular remainders and fixes every vector of $S^\perp$. Therefore $I-P$ is the orthogonal projector onto $S^\perp$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

互补矩阵 $I-P$ 保留原先被去掉的分量。它满足 $(I-P)^2=I-P$ 与 $(I-P)^*=I-P$，值域为 $S^\perp$：它只产生垂直余量，并固定 $S^\perp$ 中每个向量。因此 $I-P$ 是到 $S^\perp$ 的正交投影。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Idempotence alone allows a slanted projection
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小反例

$$Q=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
Q^2=Q,\qquad Q^T\ne Q.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This matrix sends $(x_1,x_2)^T$ to $(x_1+x_2,0)^T$. Its range is the horizontal axis, but its kernel is $\operatorname{span}\{(-1,1)^T\}$, meaning all scalar multiples of that vector. It projects along this slanted line. For the input $(0,1)^T$, the output is $(1,0)^T$ and the residual is $(-1,1)^T$; their dot product is $-1$, not zero. The perpendicular projection onto the horizontal axis would instead output zero. Thus $Q$ is an oblique projection, not an orthogonal one under the standard inner product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该矩阵把 $(x_1,x_2)^T$ 送到 $(x_1+x_2,0)^T$。值域是水平轴，但核为 $\operatorname{span}\{(-1,1)^T\}$，即该向量的全部标量倍数；投影沿这条斜线进行。对输入 $(0,1)^T$，输出为 $(1,0)^T$，残差为 $(-1,1)^T$，两者点积为 $-1$ 而非零。投到水平轴的垂直投影应当输出零。因此 $Q$ 是斜投影，在标准内积下并非正交投影。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Symmetry alone is also insufficient: $2I$ equals its transpose but $(2I)^2=4I\ne2I$. In contrast, both $P=0$ and $P=I$ satisfy the full test. They project onto $\{0\}$ and the whole space, respectively; the criterion does not require a nonzero or proper target subspace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只要求对称也不够：$2I$ 等于自身转置，但 $(2I)^2=4I\ne2I$。相反，$P=0$ 与 $P=I$ 都满足完整判据，分别投到 $\{0\}$ 和整个空间。因此，判据不要求目标子空间非零，也不要求它是严格小于整个空间的真子空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The eigenvalue check is useful as a consequence, not as a substitute for the test. If $Pv=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$, an [[特征对|eigenpair]], then idempotence gives $\lambda^2v=P^2v=Pv=\lambda v$, so $\lambda\in\{0,1\}$. This reasoning needs only idempotence. The oblique example $Q$ also has eigenvalues $0$ and $1$, so those values alone cannot certify perpendicularity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特征值检查是一个有用的推论，却不能替代完整判据。若 $Pv=\lambda v$ 且 $v\ne0$，即构成一个[[特征对]]，幂等性给出 $\lambda^2v=P^2v=Pv=\lambda v$，所以 $\lambda\in\{0,1\}$。这个推导只用幂等性；斜投影例子 $Q$ 也有特征值 $0,1$，所以只看这些值不能确认垂直关系。

## Complex coordinates and the choice of geometry
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*复坐标与几何约定*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A complex orthogonal projector need not equal its ordinary transpose. For example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复数正交投影矩阵不必等于普通转置。例如，

$$P_{\mathbb C}=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&-i\\i&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
P_{\mathbb C}^2=P_{\mathbb C},\quad
P_{\mathbb C}^*=P_{\mathbb C},\quad
P_{\mathbb C}^T\ne P_{\mathbb C}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Its columns span the complex line through $u=(1,i)^T$. For $x=(1,0)^T$, the output is $u/2$ and the residual is $(1,-i)^T/2$. The residual’s orthogonality to the target line is checked by $u^*(x-P_{\mathbb C}x)=(1+(-i)(-i))/2=0$. Ordinary transpose would use the wrong geometry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它的列张成经过 $u=(1,i)^T$ 的复直线。取 $x=(1,0)^T$，输出为 $u/2$，残差为 $(1,-i)^T/2$。残差与目标直线的垂直关系由 $u^*(x-P_{\mathbb C}x)=(1+(-i)(-i))/2=0$ 核验；只用普通转置就使用了错误的几何。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The condition $P^*=P$ is a statement about matrices in standard coordinates, or in an [[标准正交组|orthonormal basis]] for the chosen inner product: a basis of mutually perpendicular unit vectors. Changing the inner product or using nonorthonormal coordinates changes how an adjoint is represented. The geometric condition is still self-adjointness together with idempotence, but ordinary symmetry of the displayed entries is not then the general test. Fix the inner product and coordinates before applying this card.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$P^*=P$ 针对标准坐标，或所选内积的一组[[标准正交组|标准正交基]]，即由两两垂直的单位向量组成的基。改变内积或使用非标准正交坐标，会改变伴随的矩阵表示。几何上仍需自伴与幂等，但此时一般不能直接以显示元素的普通对称性作判别。应用本卡前，要先固定内积与坐标约定。
