# 正交投影矩阵当且仅当对称且幂等
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A matrix is an orthogonal projector exactly when it is symmetric and idempotent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 判别
> 对实方阵 $P\in\mathbb R^{m\times m}$，下列条件等价：
>
> 1. $P$ 是到子空间 $S=\operatorname{range}(P)$ 的正交投影；
> 2. $P^T=P$ 且 $P^2=P$。
>
> 此时
> $$
> \operatorname{range}(P)=S,\qquad \ker(P)=S^\perp,
> $$
> 且 $I-P$ 是到 $S^\perp$ 的正交投影。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a real square matrix $P$, the following are equivalent: $P$ is the orthogonal projector onto a subspace $S$; and $P^T=P$ together with $P^2=P$. In that case $\operatorname{range}(P)=S$, $\ker(P)=S^\perp$, and $I-P$ is the orthogonal projector onto $S^\perp$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两个条件各自做什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What each condition contributes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$P^2=P$ 表示投影一次后再投影不会改变结果，因此 $P$ 在其值域上等于恒等映射。对任意 $x$，
$$
x=Px+(I-P)x,\qquad P(I-P)x=0,
$$
所以第一项在 $\operatorname{range}(P)$，第二项在 $\ker(P)$。对称性再给出
$$
\langle Pu,(I-P)v\rangle=u^TP(I-P)v=0,
$$
迫使值域与核正交，因而这个幂等映射是**正交**而非斜投影。

以上矩阵写法针对实内积空间。在复内积空间中，转置必须换成共轭转置：正交投影矩阵当且仅当 $P^*=P$ 且 $P^2=P$；此时值域与核仍互为正交补。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Idempotence means that projecting an already projected vector changes nothing, so $P$ acts as the identity on its range. Every $x$ decomposes as $Px+(I-P)x$, with the second term in the kernel. Symmetry makes these two components orthogonal because $u^TP(I-P)v=0$, turning an idempotent map into an orthogonal rather than oblique projection. Over a complex inner-product space, transpose is replaced by conjugate transpose: the characterisation is $P^*=P$ and $P^2=P$, with range and kernel still orthogonal complements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小反例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal counterexample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix}
$$
满足 $P^2=P$，却不对称。它把向量沿 $\operatorname{span}\{(-1,1)^T\}$ 投到 $x$ 轴；这个核并不与 $x$ 轴正交，所以它只是斜投影。只检查幂等性，不能断言“正交投影”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matrix $\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix}$ is idempotent but not symmetric. It projects onto the $x$-axis along $\operatorname{span}\{(-1,1)^T\}$, which is not perpendicular to that axis. Idempotence alone therefore identifies a projection, not necessarily an orthogonal one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 若 $P^T=P$ 且 $P^2=P$，它的特征值为什么只能是 $0$ 或 $1$？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can a symmetric idempotent matrix have only eigenvalues zero and one?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 若 $Pv=\lambda v$，则 $P^2v=\lambda^2v$，而 $P^2=P$ 给 $\lambda^2=\lambda$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** From $Pv=\lambda v$ and $P^2=P$, one obtains $\lambda^2v=\lambda v$, hence $\lambda\in\{0,1\}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
