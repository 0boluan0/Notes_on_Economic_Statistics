# 效用可能集是可由某个可行配置同时达到或超过的个人效用水平向量集合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The utility possibility set is the set of utility vectors that some feasible allocation can attain or exceed for every individual*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

效用可能集（utility possibility set）把可行配置映射到个人效用空间。本卡采用“向下完备”的约定：若某个可行配置能使每个人至少达到向量 $v$ 指定的效用，$v$ 就属于该集合。设 $F$ 是给定资源、技术和制度约束下的可行配置集，$u_i(x)$ 是个人 $i$ 在配置 $x$ 下的效用，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The utility possibility set maps feasible allocations into individual utility space. This card uses the downward-comprehensive convention: a vector belongs to the set if some feasible allocation gives each person at least the utility specified by that vector. Let $F$ be the feasible allocation set under the specified resource, technology, and institutional constraints, and let $u_i(x)$ be person $i$'s utility from allocation $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mathcal U=\{v\in\mathbb R^n:\exists x\in F,\ u_i(x)\ge v_i\ \text{for every }i\}.
$$

这里必须是同一个配置同时满足所有人的效用要求，不能把每个人各自最喜欢的配置中的最高效用拼在一起。改变资源、技术或允许的制度安排，会改变 $F$，也就可能改变 $\mathcal U$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One and the same allocation must satisfy everyone's utility requirements. Combining each person's maximum from a different allocation does not establish feasibility. Changes in resources, technology, or admissible institutions can change $F$ and hence $\mathcal U$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有些教材把“效用可能集”专指精确实现的效用向量集 $E=\{(u_1(x),\ldots,u_n(x)):x\in F\}$。本卡的 $\mathcal U$ 是 $E$ 的向下完备扩张：它记录可同时保障的效用下界，不声称每个下界都恰好由某配置实现。读图和套用定理前先核对这两种约定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some texts reserve the term for the exact attainable utility image $E=\{(u_1(x),\ldots,u_n(x)):x\in F\}$. Here $\mathcal U$ is its downward-comprehensive extension: it records jointly attainable lower bounds, not necessarily utility vectors realised exactly. Check the convention before interpreting a diagram or applying a theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小自设例：两人分完一单位商品，$x_1+x_2=1$、$x_i\ge0$，且 $u_i=x_i$。精确实现集 $E$ 是 $u_1+u_2=1$ 的线段；$\mathcal U$ 的非负部分则是 $v_1+v_2\le1$ 的三角区域。$(0.3,0.3)$ 可由分配 $(0.5,0.5)$ 同时超过，但不是这个“必须分完”模型的精确效用结果；$(0.7,0.7)$ 连同时达到也不可能。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Minimal constructed example: two people divide one unit, with $x_1+x_2=1$, $x_i\ge0$, and $u_i=x_i$. The exact image $E$ is the line segment $u_1+u_2=1$; the nonnegative part of $\mathcal U$ is the triangle $v_1+v_2\le1$. Allocation $(0.5,0.5)$ exceeds the target $(0.3,0.3)$ for both people, although that target is not an exact outcome when the whole unit must be allocated. Target $(0.7,0.7)$ cannot even be attained jointly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

效用空间图的坐标是一人一个效用值；[[交换经济可行配置|Edgeworth box 的坐标]]则是商品数量。[[契约曲线]]上的有效配置可以映射为效用组合，但两种空间不能直接当作同一张图；效用空间中哪些点不受支配，见[[效用可能性边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The axes here measure one utility value per person, whereas [[交换经济可行配置|Edgeworth-box coordinates]] measure quantities of goods. Efficient allocations on the [[契约曲线|contract curve]] can be mapped to utility vectors, but the two spaces are not the same diagram. See the [[效用可能性边界|utility possibility frontier]] for the dominance test in utility space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
