# 资本积累是把剩余价值重新转化为追加资本的过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Capital accumulation converts surplus value into additional capital.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Marx 的分析中，资本积累是把已经产生的[[剩余价值]]的一部分重新用作资本，购买生产资料和[[Marx劳动力|劳动力]]，使生产在扩大的规模上继续。它回答的是剩余怎样进入下一轮生产；剩余的产生本身还不等于积累。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Marx's analysis, accumulation employs part of the [[剩余价值|surplus value]] already produced as additional capital, purchasing means of production and [[Marx劳动力|labour power]] so that production continues on an expanded scale. It concerns the use of surplus in subsequent production; producing a surplus is not yet accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先区分三笔用途：补回本期耗用的原有资本，维持原规模的再生产；把剩余用于资本家的个人消费；把剩余用于追加生产资本。只有第三笔是这里的资本积累。设补回原有投入后剩余为100个货币单位，个人消费60，实际追加生产投入40，则本次资本化为40。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish three uses: replacing capital consumed in current production to reproduce the existing scale; consuming surplus personally; and employing surplus as additional productive capital. Only the third is accumulation in this sense. If surplus after replacement is 100 monetary units, personal consumption is 60 and additional productive investment is 40, the amount capitalised is 40.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一笔40必须进一步转成能结合起来的生产要素，具体见[[积累的物质条件]]。把货币留在账户里，可以是为未来购买作准备；它尚未完成本卡所说的剩余向追加生产资本的转化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The 40 must become productive elements that can actually be combined; see [[积累的物质条件|the material conditions of accumulation]]. Holding money can prepare for a future purchase, but does not yet complete the conversion into additional productive capital described here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
