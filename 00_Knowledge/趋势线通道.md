# 趋势线通道是由趋势线及其平行边界界定的走势区间

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A trend-line price channel is the interval bounded by a trend line and a parallel boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

趋势线通道先以同侧转折点确定一条[[趋势线]]，再经过另一侧的选定极值画平行边界，两线之间形成走势区间。上涨时通常以低点连线为下界，过对应高点画上界；下降时以高点连线为上界，过对应低点画下界。课程把平行辅助边界称为轨道线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This entry specifically defines a trend-line channel: establish a [[趋势线|trend line]] from turning points on one side, then draw a parallel boundary through a selected opposite extreme. Rising channels commonly start from lows and add an upper parallel through a high; falling channels reverse the construction. The course calls the auxiliary parallel a channel line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一线性坐标下，两边界可写成 $L_1(t)=a+bt$ 与 $L_2(t)=a+bt+d$，其中 $d>0$ 是固定垂直价差。这不是滚动最高最低价通道，也不是按统计残差拟合的回归通道；不能把同名工具的算法互相替代。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a common linear scale, the boundaries can be written $L_1(t)=a+bt$ and $L_2(t)=a+bt+d$, with a fixed vertical price difference $d>0$. This is neither a rolling-high/low channel nor a regression-residual channel; those algorithms are not interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

边界是观察参照，不是价格被强制限制的走廊。未触另一边界便回头、或越过边界，可以提示复查原结构，但不能单独保证反转。锚点及通道修订也须保留其当时可得版本，见[[预测时点与信息集]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The boundaries are references, not constraints forcing prices to remain inside. An early turn or a breach may motivate reassessment but does not by itself guarantee reversal. Anchor and channel revisions must retain their contemporaneous versions under the [[预测时点与信息集|information-set boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
