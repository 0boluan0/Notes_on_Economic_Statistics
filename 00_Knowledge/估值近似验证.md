# 风险模拟中的估值近似必须在覆盖目标尾部的验证情景上与全重估比较
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Valuation approximations in risk simulation must be compared with full revaluation on validation scenarios that cover the target tail*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 平均定价误差小，尾部分位仍可能错
> Delta–Gamma、回归代理、插值或 surrogate 可以降低每个情景的估值成本，但必须在包含 VaR 或 ES 目标尾部的验证情景上与全重估比较，而不只检查普通情景的全样本平均误差。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Delta–Gamma, regression proxies, interpolation, or surrogates can reduce valuation cost per scenario, but they must be compared with full revaluation on validation scenarios that include the VaR or ES target tail, rather than being judged only by mean error over ordinary scenarios.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 [[风险模拟估值层]] 为了降低计算成本而采用敏感度展开、回归代理、插值或其他 surrogate 时，验证样本必须覆盖模型实际服务的冲击范围，尤其是决定 VaR 或 ES 的尾部。全重估或经批准的基准估值器提供比较对象；误差应按普通区间、压力区间和目标尾部分层查看。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the [[风险模拟估值层|valuation layer]] uses a sensitivity expansion, regression proxy, interpolation, or another surrogate to reduce cost, validation must cover the shock range in which the model will be used, especially the tail that determines VaR or ES. Full revaluation or an approved benchmark pricer supplies the comparator; errors should be examined separately in ordinary, stress, and target-tail regions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如代理在中央 95% 情景的误差接近零，却在最坏 1% 系统性高估组合价值 5；平均误差看似很小，99% VaR 仍会低估约 5。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A proxy can be nearly exact in the central 95% of scenarios but overvalue the portfolio by 5 in the worst 1%. Average error looks small while 99% VaR is understated by roughly 5.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 代理模型的全样本平均误差为零，是否足以用于 99% VaR？
>
> **答案：** 不足。正负误差可以在中心抵消，而尾部仍系统偏差；必须单独检查目标尾部。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** Is zero full-sample mean error sufficient to use a proxy model for 99% VaR?
>
> **Answer:** No. Positive and negative errors can cancel in the centre while the tail remains systematically biased; the target tail must be checked separately.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- 全重估仍依赖基础定价模型与实现正确。
- 代理验证情景必须覆盖普通、压力和目标尾部区域。
- 嵌套模拟要分开记录外层风险抽样与内层估值误差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Full revaluation still depends on the pricing model and implementation.
- Proxy validation scenarios must cover ordinary, stress, and target-tail regions.
- Nested simulation should separate outer risk sampling from inner valuation error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Federal Reserve, FEDS 2008-21](https://www.federalreserve.gov/pubs/feds/2008/200821/index.html)：核对嵌套模拟中的外层风险与内层估值误差。
- Glasserman, [*Monte Carlo Methods in Financial Engineering*](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-387-21617-1)：核对代理、嵌套模拟与数值误差。
- [Basel Framework, MAR30](https://www.bis.org/basel_framework/chapter/MAR/30.htm)：仅支持独立于开发过程的验证职能，以及该职能对估值与风险转换计算准确性的检查要求；目标尾部验证情景的设计不据此条文归因。
