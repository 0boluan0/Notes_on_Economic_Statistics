# 对同一冲击参数求二阶导数且组合价值非零时组合凸性等于市值加权凸性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For a common shock parameter and nonzero portfolio value, portfolio convexity is market-value-weighted component convexity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一估值币种和共同标量冲击 $x$ 下，固定数量 $q_j$，正单位全价 $P_j(x)$ 二次可导。若 $V=\sum_jq_jP_j\ne0$，各资产凸性为 $\mathcal C_j=P_j''/P_j$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With one valuation currency and common scalar shock $x$, hold quantities $q_j$ fixed and let positive unit full prices $P_j(x)$ be twice differentiable. If $V=\sum_jq_jP_j\ne0$ and $\mathcal C_j=P_j''/P_j$, then:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\boxed{\mathcal C_V=\frac{V''}V=\sum_jw_j\mathcal C_j},\qquad w_j=\frac{q_jP_j}{V}.$$

理由是 $V''=\sum_jq_jP_j''$。金额二阶敏感度 $V''=V\mathcal C_V$ 可以直接相加；它与以净值归一化的凸性不同，$V=0$ 时前者仍可存在。多头 $60$、$40$ 的凸性若分别为 $6$、$30$，组合凸性为 $0.6\times6+0.4\times30=15.6$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This follows from $V''=\sum_jq_jP_j''$. The currency second-order sensitivity $V''=V\mathcal C_V$ adds directly and can exist at zero net value even when normalized convexity cannot. Long market values of 60 and 40 with convexities 6 and 30 give $0.6\times6+0.4\times30=15.6$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若有空头，市值权重可为负，组合凸性不必是非负权重平均。若使用不同收益率单位、不同曲线或不同冲击方向，不能把凸性直接放在这个式子中；多因子变化还可能产生交叉二阶项。本页也不在冲击中重新调整仓位，避免把交易规则的导数混入固定组合的风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Short positions allow negative market-value weights. Convexities using different yield units, curves or shock directions do not belong in the same sum; multifactor changes may also introduce cross-second-order terms. Holdings are not rebalanced through the shock, so derivatives of a trading rule are not confused with fixed-portfolio risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
