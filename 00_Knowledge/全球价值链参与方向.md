# 后向GVC参与看本国出口中的外国增加值而前向参与看本国增加值进入他国出口

在增值贸易口径下，后向参与追踪本国出口所包含的、此前从国外进口的增加值；前向参与追踪本国创造的增加值如何经另一国继续生产，并进入该国向第三国的出口。方向以正在考察的经济体为参照，不是给企业贴“先进”或“落后”的标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In value-added trade accounting, backward participation traces foreign value added embodied in a country's exports. Forward participation traces its own value added through further production abroad into another country's exports to a third country. Direction is relative to the economy being examined, not a ranking of sophistication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设甲国用本地投入生产价值100的零件，出口给乙国；乙国再增加50的价值，将150的成品出口给丙国最终使用。甲国的100进入乙国出口，是甲国的前向参与；同一笔100是乙国出口里的外国增加值，属于乙国的后向参与。在这个没有其他投入和重复回流的例子中，乙国出口的外国增加值占比为 $100/150=2/3$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose A produces components worth 100 using domestic inputs and exports them to B. B adds 50 and exports the finished product for 150 to final users in C. A's 100 embodied in B's exports is forward participation for A and backward participation for B. With no other inputs or returning flows, foreign value added represents $100/150=2/3$ of B's exports.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个经济体可以同时有前向和后向参与。比较“参与率”时必须核对分子、分母与数据库的计数方法，不能把两个来源中名称相似的比例随意相加。海关总额不能直接揭示增加值来源，投入产出表的分解还依赖部门聚合和投入配置假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An economy can participate in both directions. Comparing participation rates requires matching numerators, denominators and database counting conventions; similarly named ratios from different sources cannot be added indiscriminately. Customs values do not directly reveal value-added origins, while input-output decompositions depend on aggregation and allocation assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“后向”不同于[[外资溢出的传递方向|FDI后向联系]]：后者描述外资下游客户与本地上游供应商之间的可能影响，不要求该供应商的产出最终出口，也不是一个进口含量比例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This use of “backward” differs from [[外资溢出的传递方向|backward FDI linkages]], which concern possible effects from foreign downstream customers to local upstream suppliers. Those links need not end in exports and are not an imported-content ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
