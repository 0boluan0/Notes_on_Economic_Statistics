# 连续时间马尔可夫链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Continuous-time Markov chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 描述系统在连续时间中随机等待后跳转状态，并从瞬时转移率推导任意时长的转移概率。
> **具体锚点：** 机器在“正常—故障—维修”状态间随机跳转，等待时间和下一状态由当前状态决定。
> **核心难点：** 生成矩阵 Q 的非对角元是速率而非概率，对角元为负且使每行和为 0。
> **为什么重要：** 排队、可靠性、信用迁移和化学反应模型需要连续等待时间。
> **继续：** 先由 Q 读停留时间和跳转链，再用 Kolmogorov 方程与稳态；仅关心到达计数可回到 [[泊松过程|泊松过程与更新过程]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It describes systems that wait for random continuous durations before jumping to new states, using transition rates rather than fixed-step probabilities.
> **Concrete anchor:** A machine switches between working and failed states. It fails at rate $\lambda$ while working and is repaired at rate $\mu$ while failed, giving exponential holding times.
> **Central difficulty:** Off-diagonal entries of the generator $Q$ are instantaneous rates, not probabilities. Diagonal entries are negative so that every row sums to zero.
> **Why it matters:** Queueing, reliability, credit migration, chemical reactions, and epidemic models all use continuous-time jumps.
> **Continue with:** Construct $Q$ from holding times, then compute with matrix exponentials, Kolmogorov equations, or uniformization. For fixed-step models, see [[离散时间马尔可夫链|discrete-time Markov chains]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 指数停留时间与跳转
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Exponential holding times and jumps*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

齐次 CTMC 在状态 i 的停留时间服从 $Exp(q_i)$，离开后跳到 j 的概率为 $q_{ij}/q_i$。指数无记忆性使给定当前状态后，未来与过去独立。若实际停留时间明显非指数，可考虑半 Markov 或扩充状态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the total exit rate from state $i$ is $q_i$, the holding time is Exp$(q_i)$. Conditional on leaving, the next state is $j$ with probability $q_{ij}/q_i$. Exponential memorylessness makes the future independent of the elapsed holding time once the current state is known. Clearly non-exponential durations call for phase expansion or a semi-Markov model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把“等多久”和“跳去哪”分开是构造模型的可靠方法。多个独立指数时钟竞争时，最先响的时钟时间仍为指数，速率是各速率之和，而某个时钟先响的概率等于其速率除以总速率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separating “how long to wait” from “where to jump” is a reliable modelling strategy. When several independent exponential clocks compete, the first ringing time is exponential with rate equal to the sum of the rates, and a particular clock rings first with probability equal to its rate divided by the total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 生成矩阵
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The generator matrix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $i\ne j$，$q_{ij}\ge0$ 是瞬时跳转率，$q_{ii}=-\sum_{j\ne i}q_{ij}$。短时间 h 内 $P_{ij}(h)=q_{ij}h+o(h)$，而非 $q_{ij}$ 本身。生成矩阵单位是“每单位时间”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $i\ne j$, $q_{ij}=\lim_{h\downarrow0}P(X_{t+h}=j\mid X_t=i)/h\ge0$, while $q_{ii}=-\sum_{j\ne i}q_{ij}$. Over a short time, $P(h)=I+hQ+o(h)$; for a finite-state chain, $P(t)=e^{Qt}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

生成矩阵的行和为零，因此 Q 本身不是转移矩阵。把 $q_{ij}$ 当“一步概率”会同时违反非负对角和行和为一。单位也不同：转移概率无量纲，生成率的单位是每单位时间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rows of the generator sum to zero, so $Q$ is not a transition matrix. Treating $q_{ij}$ as a one-step probability violates both non-negative diagonals and unit row sums. The units differ as well: transition probabilities are dimensionless, whereas generator entries are rates per unit time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Kolmogorov 前向与后向方程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Kolmogorov forward and backward equations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

转移矩阵 $P(t)$ 满足后向方程 $P'(t)=QP(t)$ 和前向方程 $P'(t)=P(t)Q$，初值 $P(0)=I$；有限状态下解为 $e^{Qt}$。前向从到达状态流量平衡解释，后向从起始状态第一步解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the row-stochastic convention, the backward equation $P'(t)=QP(t)$ decomposes the first infinitesimal step, while the forward equation $P'(t)=P(t)Q$ decomposes the last one. For a finite time-homogeneous chain, both are solved by $e^{Qt}$. With time-varying generators, matrices generally do not commute, so an ordinary matrix exponential cannot be written mechanically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 稳态与详细平衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stationarity and detailed balance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平稳分布满足 $\pi Q=0$ 和归一化。不可约有限 CTMC 有唯一平稳分布；详细平衡 $\pi_iq_{ij}=\pi_jq_{ji}$ 给可逆性和简便求解。平稳概率与平均停留时间、访问频率共同决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stationary distribution satisfies $\pi Q=0$ and $\sum_i\pi_i=1$. Detailed balance, $\pi_iq_{ij}=\pi_jq_{ji}$, implies stationarity. Existence and convergence still require conditions such as irreducibility, positive recurrence, and non-explosion; solving the linear equations alone is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## uniformization 与计算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Uniformization and computation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选 $\nu\ge\max_iq_i$，构造离散链 $R=I+Q/\nu$，再按 Poisson($\nu t$) 步数混合，可计算 $P(t)$。它把连续时间问题转为 Poisson 时钟下的离散跳转。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose $\nu\ge\max_iq_i$ and define the discrete transition matrix $R=I+Q/\nu$. Then $P(t)=\sum_{n\ge0}e^{-\nu t}(\nu t)^nR^n/n!$. This represents a CTMC as a Poisson number of discrete transitions, permits self-loops, and is useful for numerical computation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：两状态维修系统
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: a two-state repair system*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正常状态 0 以速率 $\lambda$ 故障到 1，故障状态 1 以速率 $\mu$ 修复到 0：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
State 0 is operational and fails to state 1 at rate $\lambda$; state 1 is failed and is repaired to state 0 at rate $\mu$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Q=\begin{pmatrix}-\lambda&\lambda\\ \mu&-\mu\end{pmatrix}.
$$

稳态解为 $\pi_0=\mu/(\lambda+\mu)$、$\pi_1=\lambda/(\lambda+\mu)$。这也可由平均周期解释：正常时间均值 $1/\lambda$，故障时间均值 $1/\mu$，长期可用率是正常时间除以周期总时间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The stationary solution is $\pi_0=\mu/(\lambda+\mu)$ and $\pi_1=\lambda/(\lambda+\mu)$. The same result follows from a cycle argument: mean uptime is $1/\lambda$, mean downtime is $1/\mu$, and long-run availability is uptime divided by total cycle time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若故障后修复时间不具有无记忆性，二状态 CTMC 不能同时保持准确的状态定义和指数停留；可把修复拆成多个阶段或转向 semi-Markov/更新模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If repair time is not memoryless, a two-state CTMC cannot preserve both the stated state definition and exponential holding times. One can split repair into several phases or move to a semi-Markov or renewal model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 建模检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Modelling checks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

确认状态定义能让等待时间近似无记忆；合并过粗状态常造成停留时间非指数和路径依赖。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First check that the state contains all current information determining future rates, then examine exponential holding times, calendar-time variation in rates, and the possibility of explosion. Estimate $Q$ from both transition counts and exposure time in each state; proportions from the embedded jump chain alone discard holding-time information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 生成矩阵的 $q_{ij}$ 为什么不是一步转移概率？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is a generator entry $q_{ij}$ not a one-step transition probability?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它是单位时间的瞬时速率，可大于 1；短时间概率约为 $q_{ij}h$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It is an instantaneous rate per unit time and may exceed one. The short-interval probability is approximately $q_{ij}h$, while finite-time transition probabilities come from $e^{Qt}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Q 的对角元为什么为负？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why are the diagonal entries of $Q$ negative?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它记录离开当前状态的总速率之负值，使每行和为 0，从而保持概率总量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Each diagonal entry is minus the total exit rate, making every row sum to zero and preserving total probability in the first-order approximation $I+hQ$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 若某状态停留时间显著不是指数分布，说明什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does a clearly non-exponential holding time indicate?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 给定当前状态后等待时间仍依赖已停留时长，Markov 状态定义可能过粗，需扩充状态或用半 Markov 模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The current state does not satisfy memorylessness. Add age or phase information, or use a semi-Markov or renewal model rather than retaining the same CTMC.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
