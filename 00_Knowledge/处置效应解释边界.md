# 损失厌恶本身不能直接推出卖赢持亏的处置效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Loss aversion alone does not imply the disposition effect*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[损失厌恶]]规定同幅得失的价值不对称，却没有单独规定是否卖出一个已有头寸。推出[[处置效应]]还须指定参考点、未来结果与信念、价值曲率、权重、交易机会，以及评价的是期末总得失还是实现当下的得失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[损失厌恶|Loss aversion]] specifies asymmetry between equal-sized gains and losses, not a sale decision for an existing position. Deriving the [[处置效应|disposition effect]] also requires a reference point, future outcomes and beliefs, curvature, weighting, trading opportunities, and whether evaluation concerns terminal total gains or gains realized at the moment of sale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小反例只检验“损失厌恶必然让人保留输家”：参考点为买入价 $100$，现价 $90$。卖出后现金以零利率持有至共同评价日；继续持有则届时各半概率值 $80$ 或 $100$。无税费，用实际概率加权，并以同一终值基准评价；取 $v(x)=x$（$x\ge0$）、$v(x)=2x$（$x<0$）。这有严格的同幅损失厌恶，但
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A minimal counterexample tests only whether loss aversion forces retention of a loser. The purchase-price reference is $100$ and the current price is $90$. Sale proceeds earn zero until a common evaluation date; holding gives $80$ or $100$ with equal probabilities. Ignore taxes and costs, use actual probabilities, and evaluate terminal values against the same reference. Let $v(x)=x$ for gains and $v(x)=2x$ for losses. This exhibits strict equal-sized loss aversion, yet
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$V_{\rm sell}=v(-10)=-20,\qquad
V_{\rm hold}=\tfrac12v(-20)+\tfrac12v(0)=-20.$$

两者无差异，故这种损失厌恶没有产生严格的“持亏”偏好。反例不否定某个完整前景模型可能生成处置效应；它说明仅给出损失厌恶不够，损失区间的曲率或实现效用等额外假设会改变选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The alternatives tie, so this loss aversion does not produce a strict preference to retain the loser. This does not deny that a fully specified prospect-theory model can generate the effect. It shows why loss aversion alone is insufficient: curvature within losses or utility from realization can change the choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经验上发现 PGR 高于 PLR，也不是损失厌恶的直接测量。Barberis–Xiong 的正式模型表明，把偏好定义在年度得失还是已实现得失上会改变处置效应预测；某些设定还会给出相反模式。因此从心理特征走向交易行为，需要可检验的完整机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Empirically finding PGR above PLR is not a direct measurement of loss aversion. Barberis and Xiong show that evaluating annual versus realized gains and losses changes disposition-effect predictions, with some specifications yielding the opposite pattern. Moving from a psychological feature to trading behavior requires a testable, fully specified mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Barberis & Xiong (2009), pp. 751–754，PDF 1–4](https://www.princeton.edu/~wxiong/papers/disposition.pdf#page=1)：支持前景偏好到交易决策需要正式建模、年度与实现得失实现方式不同，以及相反处置模式的可能；已目视 p. 753。$90$ 头寸反例为自拟，逐项计算共同终值下的评价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paper supports the need for formal trading models, the distinction between annual and realized gain/loss utility, and possible opposite disposition patterns. Page 753 was visually checked. The $90$ position counterexample is independently evaluated at a common terminal date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Odean (1998), p. 1795，PDF 21](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/Papers%20current%20versions/AreInvestorsReluctant.pdf#page=21)：支持已观察的行为结果仍不能单独区分前景偏好与均值回复信念等解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Odean supports the limitation that observed trading patterns alone do not distinguish prospect preferences from explanations such as mean-reversion beliefs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
