# Principal-minor test for positive semidefiniteness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*半正定的主子式判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix is [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefinite]] when its quadratic value never becomes negative. For a real symmetric matrix, the value is $x^TAx$ for a real column $x$, where $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. The principal-minor test replaces this question about every vector with a collection of determinant inequalities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵[[半正定矩阵|半正定]]，表示它的二次型值永不为负。实对称情形的值为 $x^TAx$，$x$ 是实列向量，$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。主子式判据把“每个向量”的问题转换为一组行列式不等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[主子式|principal minor]] is a determinant obtained by retaining the **same** indices for rows and columns. For $A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix}$ there are three: $a$, $c$, and $ac-b^2$. The complete two-dimensional test is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[主子式]]是用**同一组**指标保留行、列以后得到的行列式。对 $A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix}$，共有三个：$a$、$c$ 和 $ac-b^2$。完整的二维判据为

$$A\succeq0\iff a\ge0,\quad c\ge0,\quad ac-b^2\ge0.$$

> [!example] A zero determinant is allowed · 允许行列式为零
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$ the three minors are $1,1,0$. It passes the test, and direct multiplication confirms $x^TAx=(x_1+x_2)^2\ge0$. At $(1,-1)^T$ the value is zero, so this matrix is semidefinite but not definite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$，三个主子式为 $1,1,0$，因此通过判据。直接相乘也得到 $x^TAx=(x_1+x_2)^2\ge0$。在 $(1,-1)^T$ 上取零，所以它半正定但非正定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The full statement
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*完整条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n\ge1$ be a finite integer, and let $A$ be an $n\times n$ [[实对称矩阵|real symmetric]] or [[Hermitian 矩阵|complex Hermitian]] matrix. In the complex case the quadratic value is $x^*Ax$ for complex $x$, with $*$ the conjugate transpose. Write $A_{S,S}$ for the submatrix retaining the same nonempty index set $S$ on both sides. Its determinant is real under these symmetry assumptions. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n\ge1$ 为有限整数，$A$ 为 $n\times n$ 的[[实对称矩阵]]或复 [[Hermitian 矩阵]]。复数情形对复向量 $x$ 使用 $x^*Ax$，$*$ 为共轭转置。记 $A_{S,S}$ 为行、列同时保留同一非空指标集 $S$ 的子矩阵；在这些对称性条件下，其行列式为实数。则

$$A\succeq0\iff
\det A_{S,S}\ge0\quad
\text{for every nonempty }S\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are $2^n-1$ such index sets. The $n$ [[顺序主子式|leading principal minors]] use only $S=\{1,\ldots,k\}$ and are a smaller collection when $n\ge2$. For a Hermitian two-by-two matrix $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\\overline b&c\end{pmatrix}$, the test becomes $a,c\ge0$ and $ac-|b|^2\ge0$, where $a,c$ are real, $\overline b$ denotes the complex conjugate, and $|b|^2=b\overline b$ is the squared complex magnitude. For $n=1$, the test is simply $a_{11}\ge0$; the zero matrix passes in every dimension, and positive-definite matrices also pass.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这样的指标集有 $2^n-1$ 个。$n$ 个[[顺序主子式]]只使用 $S=\{1,\ldots,k\}$，在 $n\ge2$ 时只是其中一小部分。对 Hermitian 二阶矩阵 $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\\overline b&c\end{pmatrix}$，条件改为 $a,c\ge0$ 且 $ac-|b|^2\ge0$，其中 $a,c$ 为实数，$\overline b$ 表示复共轭，$|b|^2=b\overline b$ 是复数模平方。一维时只需 $a_{11}\ge0$；零矩阵在任何维数都通过，正定矩阵同样通过。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why every minor must be nonnegative
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么每个主子式都必须非负*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume $A$ is semidefinite and choose any $S$. Take a vector whose coordinates lie in $S$, and fill every omitted coordinate with zero. Its quadratic value under $A$ is precisely the value under $A_{S,S}$. The smaller matrix is therefore semidefinite too. By the [[正定与半正定的谱判据|spectral sign criterion]], all its eigenvalues are nonnegative, so their [[行列式与特征值乘积|product, its determinant]], is nonnegative. This establishes necessity for every choice of $S$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $A$ 半正定，任选 $S$。取一个仅在 $S$ 的坐标上有分量的向量，把其他坐标补零，它在 $A$ 下的二次型值就等于在 $A_{S,S}$ 下的值。因此较小的矩阵也半正定。由[[正定与半正定的谱判据]]，它的特征值全非负，所以这些特征值的[[行列式与特征值乘积|乘积，也就是行列式]]非负。这就对每个 $S$ 证明了必要性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the complete collection is sufficient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么整组条件又足够*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now assume every principal minor is nonnegative. Add a small positive number $\varepsilon$ to each diagonal entry, giving $A+\varepsilon I_n$, where $I_n$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. We will show that this new matrix is positive definite for **every** $\varepsilon>0$, then let the added number tend to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在假设全部主子式非负。给每个对角元加上一个正数 $\varepsilon$，得到 $A+\varepsilon I_n$，其中 $I_n$ 为[[单位矩阵]]。先证明：对**任意** $\varepsilon>0$，新矩阵都正定；再让加上的数趋于零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the leading block of size $k$. Its perturbed determinant is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考察大小为 $k$ 的左上角块，加数后的行列式为

$$\det(A_{1:k,1:k}+\varepsilon I_k)
=\sum_{J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k\}}
\varepsilon^{k-|J|}\det A_{J,J}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $|J|$ counts the selected indices, and the empty-set term uses the convention $\det A_{\varnothing,\varnothing}=1$. To see the identity, use the [[行列式|determinant’s separate row linearity]]: in row $j$, choose either the original row or the added row with $\varepsilon$ in position $j$ and zeros elsewhere. If original rows are chosen exactly at indices $J$, expanding along the other coordinate rows leaves $\det A_{J,J}$ and the factor $\varepsilon^{k-|J|}$. No sign change appears because the same rows and columns are removed. For $k=2$ this is just
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$|J|$ 是选出的指标个数；空集项约定 $\det A_{\varnothing,\varnothing}=1$。恒等式可以用[[行列式|行列式逐行线性]]展开：第 $j$ 行选择原来的行，或新增的那一行，后者仅在第 $j$ 个位置为 $\varepsilon$、其余为零。若恰在 $J$ 中选择原行，沿其他坐标行展开，就留下 $\det A_{J,J}$ 及因子 $\varepsilon^{k-|J|}$；删掉的是相同行、列，所以没有额外负号。$k=2$ 时就是

$$\det\begin{pmatrix}a+\varepsilon&b\\\overline b&c+\varepsilon\end{pmatrix}
=(ac-|b|^2)+\varepsilon(a+c)+\varepsilon^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every term in the general sum is nonnegative, and the empty-set term $\varepsilon^k$ is strictly positive. Thus **all leading minors** of $A+\varepsilon I_n$ are positive. [[Sylvester 正定判据|Sylvester's strict criterion]] makes it positive definite. For each fixed nonzero $x$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般求和式的每一项都非负，空集项 $\varepsilon^k$ 还严格为正。因此 $A+\varepsilon I_n$ 的**全部顺序主子式**严格正，[[Sylvester 正定判据]]说明它正定。对每个固定非零 $x$，

$$x^*(A+\varepsilon I_n)x=x^*Ax+\varepsilon\sum_i|x_i|^2>0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As $\varepsilon$ decreases to zero, the right-hand expression tends to $x^*Ax$, which must be nonnegative. At $x=0$ it is zero. In the real case use $T$ and ordinary squares instead. This proves semidefiniteness and explains why the proof needs minors from all index sets, not only the leading sequence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

让 $\varepsilon$ 下降趋于零，右边趋于 $x^*Ax$，其极限必须非负；$x=0$ 时值为零。实数情形使用 $T$ 和普通平方即可。这证明了半正定，也解释了证明为什么要用全部指标集的主子式，而非只有顺序一串。

> [!warning] The collection matters · 要看检查了哪一组
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $\operatorname{diag}(0,-1)$, both leading minors are zero, yet the omitted principal minor $a_{22}=-1$ detects failure. See [[顺序主子式不推半正定|the leading-only counterexample]]. The theorem also requires symmetry: $\begin{pmatrix}1&4\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ has all principal minors positive, but its quadratic value at $(1,-1)^T$ is $-2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $\operatorname{diag}(0,-1)$，两个顺序主子式都为零，漏掉的主子式 $a_{22}=-1$ 却会否定半正定，详见[[顺序主子式不推半正定]]。对称性也不可省略：$\begin{pmatrix}1&4\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ 的全部主子式都正，但在 $(1,-1)^T$ 上二次型值为 $-2$。
