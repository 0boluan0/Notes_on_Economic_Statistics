# 实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵半正定当且仅当全部主子式非负
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A real symmetric or Hermitian matrix is positive semidefinite if and only if every principal minor is nonnegative*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 主子式判据
> 对实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵 $A$，
> $$A\succeq0\iff \det A_{I,I}\ge0\quad\text{对每个非空指标集 }I\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}.$$
> 这里 $A_{I,I}$ 同时选择同一组行和列；它的行列式称为 principal minor（主子式）。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrix is positive semidefinite if and only if every principal minor $\det A_{I,I}$ is nonnegative, where the same index set selects rows and columns.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必要性来自每个主子矩阵仍半正定，故其特征值非负、行列式非负。充分性可以通过一个小扰动看清。假设 $A$ 的全部主子式非负。对任意 $\varepsilon>0$，$A+\varepsilon I$ 的每个顺序主子式都可写成
$$
\det(A_{1:k,1:k}+\varepsilon I)
=\sum_{J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k\}}
\varepsilon^{k-|J|}\det A_{J,J}>0.
$$
求和中的每项非负，而 $J=\varnothing$ 这一项是 $\varepsilon^k>0$。因此 Sylvester 判据给出 $A+\varepsilon I\succ0$。令 $\varepsilon\downarrow0$，对每个 $x$ 都得到 $x^*Ax\ge0$，所以 $A\succeq0$。

这也说明了为什么半正定时必须检查**所有**指标集，而不能只检查左上角 $1:k$。只查顺序主子式的最小反例见 [[顺序主子式不推半正定]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Necessity follows because every principal submatrix remains positive semidefinite. For sufficiency, assume every principal minor of $A$ is nonnegative. Each leading principal minor of $A+\varepsilon I$ is a positive sum of principal minors of $A$, with the strictly positive empty-index term $\varepsilon^k$. Sylvester's criterion therefore gives $A+\varepsilon I\succ0$ for every $\varepsilon>0$. Letting $\varepsilon$ tend to zero yields $x^*Ax\ge0$ for every $x$. This proof uses every index set; [[顺序主子式不推半正定|the counterexample to using only leading principal minors]] is the smallest case where the weaker check fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 对 $A=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{bmatrix}$，全部主子式非负具体要求什么？
>
> **答案：** $a\ge0$、$c\ge0$ 且 $ac-b^2\ge0$；三个 principal minors 都要检查。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Question:** For $A=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{bmatrix}$, what exactly does nonnegativity of every principal minor require?
>
> **Answer:** It requires $a\ge0$, $c\ge0$, and $ac-b^2\ge0$; all three principal minors must be checked.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
