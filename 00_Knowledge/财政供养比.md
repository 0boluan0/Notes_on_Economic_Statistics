# 财政供养比是按年龄公共转移流出加权的有效缴款人数与按公共转移流入加权的有效受益人数之比

国民转移账户的财政供养比用各年龄对公共转移的缴付和受益模式给人口加权，比较有效缴款人与有效受益人。这里“流出／流入”均从个人一侧看：缴纳税费等是流出，收到公共现金或实物给付是流入；不是政府账户中相反方向的记法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The NTA fiscal support ratio weights people by age profiles of contributions to and benefits from public transfers. It compares effective contributors with effective beneficiaries. Inflows and outflows are viewed from individuals: taxes and related contributions flow out, while public cash and in-kind benefits flow in—not the opposite government-account perspective.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设基期各年龄人均公共转移流出、流入分别为 $\tau_a^0,b_a^0$，分别除以选定参照年龄组的相应均值，得到权重 $w_a^T,w_a^B$。对时期 $t$ 的年龄人口 $N_{a,t}$，定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Normalize base-year age-specific public transfer outflows $\tau_a^0$ and inflows $b_a^0$ by their respective reference-age means to obtain weights $w_a^T,w_a^B$. For age-specific population $N_{a,t}$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
FSR_t=\frac{\sum_a w_a^T N_{a,t}}{\sum_a w_a^B N_{a,t}},
\qquad I_t=\frac{FSR_t}{FSR_0}.
$$

固定这些收支年龄权重后，基期为 1 的 $I_t$ 显示人口结构变化怎样改变相对筹资条件。它不是实际税收／GDP、实际预算余额或简单的“劳动年龄人口／老年人口”；教育给付、老年缴税与劳动年龄者领取给付都可以进入权重。NTA 的公共转移流出还可能含分配的转移赤字，不能逐项等同于当期实际缴税。若未来政策、就业、健康或收入模式改变，必须另设情景，不能把固定权重外推当作无条件预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding contribution and benefit weights fixed, the base-one index $I_t$ shows how population composition changes relative financing conditions. It is not actual tax revenue/GDP, the budget balance or working-age people divided by older people. Education benefits, taxes paid by older people and benefits received at working ages can all enter the profiles. NTA public transfer outflows may also include an allocated transfer deficit and are not item-for-item identical to actual current taxes. Changes in policy, employment, health or income patterns require alternative scenarios, not an unconditional fixed-profile forecast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $I_t=0.8$，意味着相对基期，同样年龄收支模式下每单位有效受益人对应的有效缴款能力低 20%。在只准统一调整给付或缴款强度、并要恢复原收支比例的简化情景中，可以把给付乘 0.8，或把缴款乘 $1/0.8=1.25$；下降 20% 与上调 25% 不是相同百分比，也不是唯一政策选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $I_t=0.8$, effective contribution capacity per effective beneficiary is 20% below its base-year level under unchanged age profiles. In a simplified scenario allowing only uniform adjustments to benefits or contributions to restore the original financing ratio, benefits could be multiplied by 0.8 or contributions by $1/0.8=1.25$. A 20% cut and a 25% increase are not the same percentage and are not the only policy options.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[年龄抚养比]]先描述年龄人口，[[经济供养比]]描述劳动收入与消费的年龄模式，本指标则只聚焦公共转移一侧。完整的老年资源支持还包括[[老年消费的筹资|私人转移与资产筹资]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[年龄抚养比|age dependency ratio]] describes population counts; the [[经济供养比|economic support ratio]] uses labour-income and consumption profiles. This ratio focuses on public transfers. Complete old-age financing also includes [[老年消费的筹资|private transfers and assets]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [UN，NTA Manual（2013）](https://ntaccounts.org/doc/repository/NTA%20manual%202013.pdf#page=36)，印刷第19页 Fiscal support ratio、第113–114页公共转移流出与转移赤字、第136页公共账户应用、第198页术语 Fiscal support ratio：有效缴款／受益者、参照年龄权重与基期归一化、年龄模式情景。0.8 与 1.25 的对照为独立代数核验，仅适用于正文限定的调整情景。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The NTA Manual defines effective contributors and beneficiaries, reference-age weights, base-year normalization and age-profile scenarios. The 0.8/1.25 comparison is independently derived for the explicitly restricted adjustment scenario.
