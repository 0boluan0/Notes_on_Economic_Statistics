# 政府公债、预算制度与财政规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Public Debt, Budget Institutions, and Fiscal Rules*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释政府怎样在时间上安排税收、支出和融资，并用预算制度与财政规则管理偿债、周期和政治承诺。
> **具体锚点：** 衰退会自动扩大赤字；若规则要求当年机械平衡预算，政府可能在需求疲弱时加税减支并放大衰退。
> **核心难点：** 债务可持续性同时取决于利率、增长、初级余额、币种和期限；headline deficit 又混合周期、一次性项目与表外风险。
> **为什么重要：** 公债不是免费资源，预算数字也不是完整财政状态；二者决定代际分配、危机空间与政策信誉。
> **继续：** 先写债务动态与完整财政口径，再检查预算执行、或有负债和规则的逃逸条款。
> 政府可在时间上重排税收和支出，但不能永久逃离跨期预算约束。本卡连接公债的代际与风险分配、预算周期的问责，以及财政规则在纪律与灵活性之间的权衡。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Explain how government allocates taxes, spending, and finance over time and uses budget institutions and fiscal rules to manage solvency, the cycle, and political commitments.
> **Concrete anchor:** A recession automatically widens the deficit; a rule requiring immediate annual balance may force tax increases or spending cuts during weak demand and deepen the downturn.
> **Central difficulty:** Debt sustainability depends jointly on interest rates, growth, the primary balance, currency, and maturity, while the headline deficit mixes cyclical effects, one-offs, and off-balance-sheet risks.
> **Why it matters:** Public debt is not free resources, and budget figures are not the full fiscal position; both shape intergenerational distribution, crisis capacity, and policy credibility.
> **Continue with:** Write debt dynamics and a comprehensive fiscal perimeter first, then inspect budget execution, contingent liabilities, and escape clauses in fiscal rules.
> Government can rearrange taxes and spending across time, but it cannot permanently escape its intertemporal budget constraint. This card links the intergenerational and risk allocation of public debt, accountability over the budget cycle, and the trade-off between discipline and flexibility in fiscal rules.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 财政收入、公债与代际
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fiscal Revenue, Public Debt, and Generations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税、费、国有资产收入和公债筹资的经济含义不同。债务把部分税负和风险移至未来，但何时由谁承担取决于利率、增长、财政反应和持有结构；政府跨期预算约束不能长期被忽略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taxes, fees, state-asset income, and debt finance have different economic meanings. Debt shifts some tax burden and risk into the future, but who bears it and when depends on interest rates, growth, fiscal responses, and the ownership structure. The government's intertemporal budget constraint cannot be ignored indefinitely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用债务动态近似可写成债务率变化取决于初始债务、实际利率与增长率之差以及 primary balance。当 $r>g$ 时，既有债务率更容易自行上升；当 $r<g$ 时压力较小，但汇率、期限、风险溢价和未来初级赤字仍决定可持续性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Debt dynamics can be approximated as depending on initial debt, the difference between the real interest rate and growth, and the primary balance. When $r>g$, an existing debt ratio tends to grow more easily; when $r<g$, pressure is lower, but exchange rates, maturity, risk premia, and future primary deficits still determine sustainability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 预算原则与周期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Budget Principles and the Budget Cycle*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预算包含编制、审议、执行、调整和决算。统一、完整、透明与绩效信息帮助问责；现金口径、权责发生和或有负债揭示不同风险。预算赤字数字需结合周期和一次性项目。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The budget includes preparation, legislative review, execution, adjustment, and final accounts. Unity, comprehensiveness, transparency, and performance information support accountability. Cash accounting, accrual accounting, and contingent liabilities reveal different risks. A reported deficit must be interpreted alongside the economic cycle and one-off items.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预算不是只有批准数字。执行偏差、补充预算、地方与国企表外融资、养老金承诺和担保会改变真实财政立场。结构性余额试图区分周期，但依赖潜在产出和收入弹性估计，不能当无误差事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A budget is more than an approved number. Execution gaps, supplementary budgets, off-balance-sheet financing by local governments or state firms, pension promises, and guarantees change the true fiscal position. Structural balances try to remove cyclical effects, but they depend on estimates of potential output and revenue elasticities and are not error-free facts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 财政规则与灵活性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fiscal Rules and Flexibility*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

赤字/债务规则约束时间不一致和政治偏差，但过硬规则会放大衰退或诱发会计规避。良好规则需透明逃逸条款、独立监督和中期框架。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deficit and debt rules constrain time inconsistency and political bias, but excessively rigid rules can amplify recessions or induce accounting avoidance. Good rules require transparent escape clauses, independent oversight, and a medium-term framework.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

规则选择应匹配控制变量：支出规则较接近政府年度决策，债务规则连接长期锚但短期可控性弱，结构性余额规则更逆周期却依赖不可观测估计。多重规则并存可能互补，也可能使责任模糊。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rule should match the controlled variable. Expenditure rules are close to annual government decisions; debt rules provide a long-run anchor but are weakly controllable in the short run; structural-balance rules are more countercyclical but rely on unobservable estimates. Multiple rules may complement one another or blur accountability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：衰退中的赤字上限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: A Deficit Ceiling in Recession*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经济衰退会自动降低税收并提高失业给付，使 headline deficit 上升。若规则要求当年立即回到固定赤字上限，政府可能在需求疲弱时加税减支，放大衰退。允许明确、可监督的周期调整或逃逸条款，可保留长期锚而避免机械顺周期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A recession automatically reduces revenue and raises unemployment benefits, widening the headline deficit. If a rule forces immediate return to a fixed annual ceiling, government may raise taxes or cut spending while demand is weak, deepening the recession. A transparent, supervised cyclical adjustment or escape clause can preserve the long-run anchor without mechanical procyclicality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只看债务率不看币种、期限和利息负担会错估风险；只看当年赤字不看周期、资产出售和或有负债会错估财政立场；规则频繁“达标”但表外债务增长，说明指标可被规避而非纪律真正改善。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Looking only at the debt ratio while ignoring currency, maturity, and interest burden misstates risk. Looking only at the current deficit while ignoring the cycle, asset sales, and contingent liabilities misstates the fiscal stance. If rules are repeatedly met while off-balance-sheet debt grows, the metric is being avoided rather than discipline genuinely improving.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 公债是否等于免费筹资？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Is public debt free financing?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不是。它形成未来利息和再融资义务，并通过税收、通胀、支出或增长路径分配成本与风险。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. It creates future interest and refinancing obligations and distributes costs and risks through taxes, inflation, spending, or the growth path.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 headline deficit 不能单独表示财政立场？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can the headline deficit not by itself represent the fiscal stance?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 周期、一次性交易、会计口径和或有负债都会改变数字，需要区分自动稳定器、主动政策与表外风险。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The cycle, one-off transactions, accounting conventions, and contingent liabilities all affect the number, so automatic stabilizers, discretionary policy, and off-balance-sheet risks must be separated.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 财政规则为什么需要逃逸条款？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why do fiscal rules need escape clauses?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 极端冲击下机械遵守可能放大危机；透明触发、限期恢复和独立监督使规则在保留纪律的同时允许必要应对。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Mechanical compliance during extreme shocks can amplify a crisis. Transparent triggers, time-limited restoration plans, and independent oversight allow necessary responses while preserving discipline.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
