# 稳态是递推的不动点而稳定性描述偏离该点后的动态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A steady state is a fixed point, whereas stability describes the dynamics of deviations from it*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 对固定输入下的递推 $y_t=F(y_{t-1})$，稳态 $y^*$ 满足
> $$y^*=F(y^*).$$
> 它回答“若已经在那里，下一期是否仍在那里”。稳定性则问：从附近偏离后，轨迹会回去、维持偏离，还是离得更远。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A steady state is a fixed point of the recurrence. Stability asks a different question: what happens after a perturbation away from that point?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存在稳态不保证收敛。$y_t=-y_{t-1}$ 有稳态 $0$，但任意非零初值会在正负之间永久交替；$y_t=1.1y_{t-1}$ 同样有稳态 $0$，偏离却会放大。反过来，一条轨迹若恰好从稳态开始就保持不动，也不能证明系统对其他初值稳定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Existence of a fixed point does not imply convergence. The recurrence $y_t=-y_{t-1}$ oscillates forever around the fixed point zero, while $y_t=1.1y_{t-1}$ moves away from it. A trajectory started exactly at a fixed point also reveals nothing about nearby initial states.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机递推还要多分一层。确定性初始偏离可以衰减，而新的随机冲击仍会每期到达；此时过程可能在不变分布下围绕某个均值波动，却不会逐路径停在一个常数上。因此“均值回复”“协方差平稳”“样本路径收敛到稳态”不是同义句。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stochastic recurrences add another distinction. The effect of an initial condition may decay while new shocks keep arriving. The process can then fluctuate under an invariant distribution without any sample path settling at a constant. Mean reversion, covariance stationarity, and pathwise convergence are different claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $y_t=-0.8y_{t-1}+2$ 的稳态存在是否已经证明轨迹会收敛？
>
> **答案：** 还没有；要再检查偏离系数。这里 $|{-0.8}|<1$，所以偏离会交替衰减，才得到收敛。
