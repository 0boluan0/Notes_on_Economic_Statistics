# 扩展欧几里得算法在求最大公因数时同时求出Bézout系数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The extended Euclidean algorithm computes Bézout coefficients together with the gcd*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对不全为零的整数输入 $a,b$，普通[[欧几里得算法]]只保留余数；扩展版本还保留“每个余数是原输入的哪种整数线性组合”。它返回 $g,s,t$，满足 $g=\gcd(a,b)=sa+tb$。MIT 课程把它称为 **Pulverizer**。实际手算可以先列除法式再反向代入，这与逐步记录系数做的是同一件事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For integer inputs $a,b$ not both zero, the ordinary [[欧几里得算法|Euclidean algorithm]] tracks remainders; its extended version also tracks each remainder as an integer linear combination of the original inputs. It returns $g,s,t$ with $g=\gcd(a,b)=sa+tb$. MIT calls it the **Pulverizer**. For hand calculations, writing the division equations and then substituting backward performs the same task as tracking coefficients forward.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从一个能复算的例子看更新规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The update rule through a checkable example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以 $a=26,b=7$ 为例，先得到 $26=3\cdot7+5$、$7=1\cdot5+2$、$5=2\cdot2+1$、$2=2\cdot1+0$。从最后一个非零余数反代：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a=26,b=7$, division gives $26=3\cdot7+5$, $7=1\cdot5+2$, $5=2\cdot2+1$, and $2=2\cdot1+0$. Substitute backward from the last nonzero remainder:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
1&=5-2\cdot2\\
 &=5-2(7-5)=3\cdot5-2\cdot7\\
 &=3(26-3\cdot7)-2\cdot7=3\cdot26-11\cdot7.
\end{aligned}
$$

若用系数表向前算，设 $x=s_xa+t_xb$、$y=s_ya+t_yb$，本轮商为 $q$，则新余数 $r=x-qy$ 的系数就是 $(s_x-qs_y,\ t_x-qt_y)$。初始系数为 $(1,0)$、$(0,1)$；每轮同时更新余数和这对系数，最后返回非零行。负输入先取绝对值，返回时调整对应系数的符号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For forward coefficient tracking, suppose $x=s_xa+t_xb$ and $y=s_ya+t_yb$. If the quotient is $q$, then the new remainder $r=x-qy$ has coefficients $(s_x-qs_y,\ t_x-qt_y)$. Initialize with $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$, update remainders and coefficients together, and return the last nonzero row. For negative inputs, first take absolute values and adjust coefficient signs on return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用原始输入验算 $sa+tb=g$ 是最直接的检查。在这个例子中，模 $26$ 后 $-11\cdot7\equiv1$，所以 $7$ 的[[模逆元]]可取 $15$；若算出的 gcd 不是 $1$，就不能把返回的系数直接叫作逆元，须按[[模逆元存在条件]]判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The direct check is to substitute the original inputs into $sa+tb=g$. Here, reducing modulo $26$ gives $-11\cdot7\equiv1$, so $15$ represents a [[模逆元|modular inverse]] of $7$. If the gcd is not $1$, the returned coefficient is not automatically an inverse; apply the [[模逆元存在条件|inverse-existence criterion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*

  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Section 8.2.2 supports tracking remainders and coefficients together and returning the Bézout representation in the last nonzero row. The back-substitution for $26,7$ and the check $7\cdot15\equiv1\pmod{26}$ are recomputed step by step.
