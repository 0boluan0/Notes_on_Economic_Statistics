# 限制性条款是在融资合同中约定借款人行为、信息提供或财务条件的约束

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Covenants are financing-contract provisions that constrain borrower conduct, information provision, or financial conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

限制性条款（covenants）把保护出资人的要求写进合同，例如限制新增债务和股利分配、维持约定财务指标、提供财务报告。它既可以禁止某些行为，也可以要求持续履行某些义务；不只是一句笼统的“不得损害银行利益”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Covenants put investor protections into a contract: restrictions on additional debt or dividends, maintenance of specified financial measures, or delivery of financial reports. They can prohibit actions or impose continuing duties, rather than merely request that the borrower not harm the lender.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如约定未经同意不得以核心设备为另一笔债务设立优先担保，可以限制把既有债权人的回收基础转给后来债权人的行为。判断是否违约时要读清允许事项、阈值、计算日期和补救安排；提前到期、停止后续拨款或重新谈判等后果，取决于合同和适用规则，不是每项条款违反后的统一自动结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A restriction on pledging core equipment with priority to a new lender without consent can limit transfers of existing creditors' recovery base. Testing compliance requires the permitted exceptions, thresholds, measurement dates, and cure provisions. Acceleration, suspension of future funding, or renegotiation depends on the contract and applicable rules, not one automatic consequence of every breach.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

写入条款还需要有人取得信息并执行，因而与[[委托筛选与监督]]互补。条款过细可能成本高、妨碍有益调整，也无法事先穷尽所有经营状态；其价值应扣除这类[[代理成本|治理成本与剩余损失]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Terms require information and enforcement, making them complementary to [[委托筛选与监督|delegated screening and monitoring]]. Very detailed provisions can be costly, restrict beneficial adjustments, and still fail to cover every operating state. Their benefit must be assessed against [[代理成本|governance costs and residual loss]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
