# Boundary of a set
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*集合边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **boundary point** is a point around which no open neighbourhood stays entirely on one side of a set: every such neighbourhood meets both the set and its complement. For $A=[0,1)$ in the real line, the boundary is $\{0,1\}$. The first endpoint belongs to $A$ and the second does not. Being a boundary point and being a member of the set are separate questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**边界点**是这样的位置：无论取怎样的开邻域，都不能让邻域完全留在集合的一侧；它总会同时碰到集合与补集。例如实线中的 $A=[0,1)$，边界为 $\{0,1\}$。前一个端点属于 $A$，后一个不属于。因此，“是不是边界点”与“是不是集合成员”是两个问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the ambient [[拓扑|topological space]] $X$ and a subset $A\subseteq X$. Its complement is $X\setminus A$, the points of $X$ outside $A$. An open neighbourhood of $x$ is an [[开集|open set]] containing $x$. In a [[度量空间|metric space]], the same test can use every positive-radius open ball centred at $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定环境[[拓扑|拓扑空间]] $X$ 和子集 $A\subseteq X$。补集 $X\setminus A$ 是 $X$ 中不属于 $A$ 的点。$x$ 的开邻域就是包含 $x$ 的[[开集]]；在[[度量空间]]内，也可以检查以 $x$ 为中心、所有正半径的开球。

> [!abstract] Both sides in every neighbourhood · 每个邻域都碰到两侧
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Write the boundary as $\partial_X A$, or $\partial A$ when $X$ is understood. Then
> $$x\in\partial_X A\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad U\cap A\ne\varnothing\ \text{and}\ U\cap(X\setminus A)\ne\varnothing\quad\text{for every open }U\ni x.$$
> The word “every” matters. One large interval meeting both sides is insufficient; the test must hold however small the neighbourhood.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 将边界记为 $\partial_X A$，环境明确时简写 $\partial A$。判据要求每个开邻域都同时与 $A$ 和 $X\setminus A$ 相交。“每个”至关重要：一个很大的区间碰到两侧还不够，再小的邻域也必须如此。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A=[0,1)$, a point $x$ strictly between $0$ and $1$ has a sufficiently small interval lying inside $A$, so it is not on the boundary. A point below $0$ or above $1$ has a sufficiently small interval outside $A$. At $0$ and $1$, every open interval reaches both sides. Changing $A$ to $(0,1)$ or $[0,1]$ leaves the boundary unchanged; only membership of the endpoints changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $A=[0,1)$，严格处在 $0$ 与 $1$ 之间的点 $x$ 有足够小的区间完全位于 $A$ 内，所以不是边界点。小于 $0$ 或大于 $1$ 的点则有足够小的区间完全位于外部。在 $0$ 与 $1$ 处，每个开区间都会到达两侧。把 $A$ 换成 $(0,1)$ 或 $[0,1]$，边界仍不变，变的只是端点是否属于集合。

## Read the boundary from closure and interior
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从闭包与内部读出边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[集合闭包|closure]] $\operatorname{cl}_X A$ consists of points every neighbourhood of which meets $A$. The [[集合内部|interior]] $\operatorname{int}_X A$ consists of points with some open neighbourhood wholly inside $A$. Requiring contact with both sides gives
$$\partial_X A=\operatorname{cl}_X A\cap\operatorname{cl}_X(X\setminus A)=\operatorname{cl}_X A\setminus\operatorname{int}_X A.$$
For the second equality, a point is outside the closure of the complement exactly when it has an open neighbourhood avoiding that complement, which means it is interior to $A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[集合闭包]] $\operatorname{cl}_X A$ 收集每个邻域都碰到 $A$ 的点；[[集合内部]] $\operatorname{int}_X A$ 收集存在某个开邻域完全落在 $A$ 内的点。要求同时接触两侧，得到上式。第二个等号的理由是：不在补集的闭包内，恰好表示有一个开邻域避开补集，也就是属于 $A$ 的内部。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The intersection formula is symmetric in $A$ and its complement, so they have the same boundary. It also shows the boundary is [[闭集|closed]], since it is the intersection of two closed sets. This does not imply that the boundary has empty interior. For instance, in the usual real line every open interval contains both rational and irrational numbers, so the boundary of the rational numbers is the whole real line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交集公式对 $A$ 与补集完全对称，因此它们的边界相同。边界也是[[闭集]]，因为它是两个闭集之交。但边界未必内部为空。例如通常实线的每个开区间都同时含有有理数和无理数，所以有理数集的边界是整个实线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rational-number example is not a picture of two spatially separated regions. Arbitrarily fine fraction grids supply rational points in every interval; once a rational point is inside, a sufficiently small irrational shift keeps it inside and supplies an irrational point. The neighbourhood test, rather than a drawn outline, determines the boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有理数这个例子并不是两块彼此分开的区域。任意细的分数网格能在每个区间提供有理点；选定区间内部的有理点后，加上足够小的无理偏移，仍在区间内部，就得到无理点。决定边界的是邻域判据，而不是画出来的轮廓。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the boundary says about openness and closedness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用边界判断开闭*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A point of $A$ that is not on the boundary must have an open neighbourhood inside $A$. Therefore $A$ is open exactly when $A\cap\partial_X A=\varnothing$. Also, the closure consists of $A$ together with any missing boundary points:
$$\operatorname{cl}_X A=A\cup\partial_X A.$$
Thus $A$ is closed exactly when $\partial_X A\subseteq A$. These are conditions relative to $X$; a set can satisfy both.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

属于 $A$ 却不是边界点的点，必有完全位于 $A$ 内的开邻域。因此 $A$ 开，当且仅当 $A\cap\partial_X A=\varnothing$。闭包则由 $A$ 加上尚未包含的边界点组成，故有上式；于是 $A$ 闭，当且仅当 $\partial_X A\subseteq A$。这些判别都相对于 $X$，同一个集合可能同时满足两条。

> [!example] The ambient space changes the boundary · 环境空间会改变边界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $A=[0,1]$. In $X=\mathbb R$, its boundary is $\{0,1\}$. In the [[子空间拓扑|subspace]] $Y=[0,1]$, the same set is the entire space and its complement is empty. No relative neighbourhood can meet that empty complement, so $\partial_Y A=\varnothing$. More generally, the boundary of the empty set and of the whole ambient space is empty.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $A=[0,1]$。在 $X=\mathbb R$ 中，边界为 $\{0,1\}$；在带[[子空间拓扑]]的 $Y=[0,1]$ 中，同一个 $A$ 就是整个空间，补集为空。任何相对邻域都不可能碰到这个空补集，因此 $\partial_Y A=\varnothing$。一般地，空集和整个环境空间的边界都为空。
