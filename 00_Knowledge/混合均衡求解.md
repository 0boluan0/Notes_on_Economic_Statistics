# 求有限博弈的混合策略均衡必须先提出支持集，再用对手概率制造支持内无差异，并检查概率合法与支持外策略不更优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*To solve a finite game's mixed-strategy equilibrium, first posit supports, use each opponent's probabilities to make supported strategies indifferent, and then check probability feasibility and every excluded strategy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无差异方程不是一台“输入矩阵就输出均衡”的机器。它只在你已经猜对哪些纯策略获得正概率后成立。完整做法是：**猜支持集 → 解支持内无差异 → 验证概率 → 检查支持外收益 → 双方同时确认最佳反应**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indifference equations are not a machine that turns any payoff matrix directly into equilibrium. They apply only after the strategies receiving positive probability have been identified. The full method is: posit supports, solve the within-support indifference equations, validate probabilities, test excluded strategies, and finally verify mutual best response for every player.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 支持集枚举法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Support Enumeration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每组候选支持 $T_i\subseteq S_i$：

1. 令 $\sigma_i(s_i)>0$ 对 $s_i\in T_i$，支持外概率为 0；
2. 用 $\sigma_{-i}$ 让玩家 $i$ 的所有 $s_i\in T_i$ 获得同一个期望收益；
3. 加上各概率非负且和为 1；
4. 检查每个 $s_i\notin T_i$ 的收益不高于支持内收益；
5. 对所有玩家同时重复，并把退化支持也算进去，以免漏掉纯策略均衡。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**1.** Assign positive probability to each strategy in a candidate support and zero outside it.<br>
**2.** Use opponents' probabilities to equalize the payoffs of all strategies in that support.<br>
**3.** Impose nonnegativity and probabilities summing to one.<br>
**4.** Verify that no excluded strategy earns more than the supported payoff.<br>
**5.** Do this simultaneously for every player and include singleton supports so that pure equilibria are not omitted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 概率是交叉决定的
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mixing Probabilities Are Determined Crosswise*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设行玩家以概率 $p$ 选上方策略，列玩家以概率 $q$ 选左方策略。行玩家是否愿意混合取决于 $q$；列玩家是否愿意混合取决于 $p$。因此：

- 解行玩家无差异方程得到的是**列玩家的 $q$**；
- 解列玩家无差异方程得到的是**行玩家的 $p$**。

把方程里的概率认成“正在计算收益的那个人自己的概率”，是最常见的方向错误。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the row player mixes with probability $p$ and the column player with probability $q$, the row player's indifference is controlled by $q$, while the column player's indifference is controlled by $p$. The equation for one player's payoff therefore solves the opponent's mixing probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 配对硬币：没有纯均衡但有唯一混合均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Matching Pennies: No Pure Equilibrium but One Mixed Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下表中列玩家希望两面相同，行玩家希望不同：

|  | $H$ | $T$ |
|---|---|---|
| $H$ | $(-1,1)$ | $(1,-1)$ |
| $T$ | $(1,-1)$ | $(-1,1)$ |

列玩家以概率 $q$ 选 $H$ 时，行玩家两项收益为

$$
U_1(H\mid q)=1-2q,
\qquad
U_1(T\mid q)=2q-1.
$$

无差异给出 $q=1/2$；对称地，列玩家无差异要求 $p=1/2$。两边同时成立，因此唯一 Nash 均衡是 $(p,q)=(1/2,1/2)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The column player wants the coins to match and the row player wants them to differ. The row player's displayed indifference condition gives $q=1/2$; symmetry gives $p=1/2$ from the column player's condition. Both best-response requirements therefore hold at the unique Nash equilibrium $(1/2,1/2)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 性别博弈：混合均衡不能取代两个纯均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Battle of the Sexes: The Mixed Equilibrium Does Not Replace the Pure Ones*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|  | Cafe | Pub |
|---|---|---|
| **Cafe** | $(4,3)$ | $(1,1)$ |
| **Pub** | $(0,0)$ | $(3,4)$ |

若行玩家以 $x$ 选 Cafe，列玩家以 $y$ 选 Cafe，则行玩家无差异要求

$$
4y+1(1-y)=3(1-y)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
y=\frac13,
$$

列玩家无差异要求

$$
3x=x+4(1-x)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
x=\frac23.
$$

所以 $(x,y)=(2/3,1/3)$ 是一个内点混合均衡；$(Cafe,Cafe)$ 与 $(Pub,Pub)$ 两个纯均衡仍然存在。求“所有均衡”时，不能在找到内点解后停下。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The row player's indifference equation determines $y=1/3$, while the column player's determines $x=2/3$. This gives one interior mixed equilibrium. The two coordinated pure equilibria remain valid, so finding the interior solution does not complete a request for all equilibria.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 三个失败信号
> - 解出的概率小于 0 或大于 1：候选支持通常错了；
> - 支持外策略收益更高：候选不是均衡；
> - 只让一方无差异：只求出了一条最佳反应条件，还没有求出策略组合。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A probability outside $[0,1]$ usually rejects the proposed support. A more profitable excluded strategy rejects the candidate. Making only one player indifferent supplies one best-response condition, not an equilibrium profile.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
