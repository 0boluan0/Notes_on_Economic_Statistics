# 每个大于一的整数都有唯一的素因数分解但因子顺序可变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Every integer greater than one has a unique prime factorization up to the order of factors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个整数 $n>1$，可以写成有限个[[素数]]的乘积；忽略排列顺序后，素数有哪些、每个出现几次都唯一。例如 $60=2^2\cdot3\cdot5$，不能再有另一组不同的素因子。$1$ 可用空乘积表示，但它本身不是素数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every integer $n>1$ is a finite product of [[素数|primes]], with the primes and their multiplicities uniquely determined once order is ignored. For example, $60=2^2\cdot3\cdot5$ has no different collection of prime factors. The integer $1$ is represented by the empty product and is not itself prime.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 存在性与唯一性分别证明
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Prove existence and uniqueness separately*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存在性用[[强数学归纳法]]：$2$ 已是素数。对 $n>2$，若 $n$ 是素数就结束；否则 $n=ab$，其中 $2\le a,b<n$，由归纳假设把两个更小因子各自分成素数，再相乘即可。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Existence follows by [[强数学归纳法|strong induction]]: $2$ is prime. For $n>2$, a prime needs no further factorization; otherwise $n=ab$ with $2\le a,b<n$. Factor both smaller integers by the induction hypothesis and combine their prime factors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

唯一性从两种分解 $p_1\cdots p_r=q_1\cdots q_s$ 出发。由[[素数整除乘积]]，$p_1$ 必整除某个 $q_j$，而 $q_j$ 是素数，所以两者相等。把这个因子移到前面、在整数等式中约去，再重复。不会有一边先用完而另一边还有素因子，因为剩下的素数乘积不可能等于 $1$。因此两边的素因子连同重复次数完全一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For uniqueness, start with $p_1\cdots p_r=q_1\cdots q_s$. [[素数整除乘积|Euclid's lemma]] makes $p_1$ divide some prime $q_j$, hence $p_1=q_j$. Move that factor to the front, cancel it in the integer equality, and repeat. One side cannot run out while the other retains prime factors, whose product cannot equal $1$. Thus both multisets of factors agree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个定理证明分解存在且唯一，并没有提供对大整数快速找出分解的保证。[[RSA安全边界]]恰恰需要区分“答案在数学上确定”与“攻击者能否有效算出答案”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem establishes existence and uniqueness, not an efficient factorization method for large integers. [[RSA安全边界|RSA's security boundary]] requires distinguishing a mathematically determined answer from one an attacker can efficiently compute.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
