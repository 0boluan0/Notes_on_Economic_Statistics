<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Unique prime factorization
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*唯一素因数分解 · 算术基本定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A positive integer can be split into factors in several ways: $60=6\cdot10=12\cdot5$. Continue splitting until each factor is a [[素数|prime]], an integer greater than $1$ with no positive divisors other than $1$ and itself. Both routes then give two copies of $2$, one $3$ and one $5$. Unique prime factorization says that this final collection is determined by the integer, even when the route and order differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
一个正整数可以用不同方式拆成因子，例如 $60=6\cdot10=12\cdot5$。继续拆到每个因子都是[[素数]]——大于 $1$、正因子只有 $1$ 与自身的整数——两条路线都会得到两个 $2$、一个 $3$ 和一个 $5$。唯一素因数分解说的是：即使拆法和排列不同，最终这些素因子及其出现次数仍由原整数确定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every integer $n>1$ is a finite product of [[素数|primes]], with the primes and their multiplicities uniquely determined once order is ignored. For example, $60=2^2\cdot3\cdot5$ has no different collection of prime factors. The integer $1$ is represented by the empty product and is not itself prime.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对每个整数 $n>1$，可以写成有限个[[素数]]的乘积；忽略排列顺序后，素数有哪些、每个出现几次都唯一。例如 $60=2^2\cdot3\cdot5$，不能再有另一组不同的素因子。$1$ 可用空乘积表示，但它本身不是素数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What exactly is unique?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 唯一的究竟是什么？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an integer $n>1$, a prime factorization is an equality $n=p_1\cdots p_r$, where $r\ge1$ is finite and every $p_i$ is prime. The subscript identifies a position in the list, so the same prime may appear in several positions. If another prime list $q_1,\ldots,q_s$ has the same product, the theorem says $r=s$ and the lists can be reordered to agree entry by entry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对整数 $n>1$，素因数分解是等式 $n=p_1\cdots p_r$，其中 $r\ge1$ 有限，每个 $p_i$ 都是素数。下标表示列表中的位置，因此同一素数可以出现在多个位置。如果另一个素数列表 $q_1,\ldots,q_s$ 的乘积也等于 $n$，定理断言 $r=s$，并且重新排列后两个列表能够逐项相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $2\cdot2\cdot3$ and $3\cdot2\cdot2$ are the same factorization of $12$ up to order. Equivalently, require the primes to be listed in nondecreasing order; that ordered list is unique. Grouping repeated primes gives powers: $2\cdot2\cdot3\cdot5=2^2\cdot3\cdot5$, where the exponent $2$ records two occurrences, not two different primes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
所以 $2\cdot2\cdot3$ 与 $3\cdot2\cdot2$ 是 $12$ 的同一种分解，只是顺序不同。也可以要求素数按不减顺序排列，此时得到的有序列表唯一。把重复素数合并就得到幂：$2\cdot2\cdot3\cdot5=2^2\cdot3\cdot5$；指数 $2$ 表示出现两次，不是两种不同素数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Prove existence and uniqueness separately
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 存在性与唯一性分别证明

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Existence asks whether the splitting process can always finish with primes. Uniqueness asks whether another completed splitting could have different primes or multiplicities. The two claims need different arguments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
存在性问的是：能否总把整数拆成有限个素数？唯一性问的是：另一种已经拆完的结果，会不会含有不同的素数或不同次数？这两个问题需要分别论证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### Existence by strong induction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*用强归纳证明存在性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Existence follows by [[强数学归纳法|strong induction]]: $2$ is prime. For $n>2$, a prime needs no further factorization; otherwise $n=ab$ with $2\le a,b<n$. Factor both smaller integers by the induction hypothesis and combine their prime factors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
存在性用[[强数学归纳法]]：$2$ 已是素数。对 $n>2$，若 $n$ 是素数就结束；否则 $n=ab$，其中 $2\le a,b<n$，由归纳假设把两个更小因子各自分成素数，再相乘即可。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More explicitly, the induction hypothesis for a fixed $n>2$ covers every integer $m$ with $2\le m<n$, not just $n-1$. If $n$ is composite, choose a positive divisor $a$ with $1<a<n$ and write $n=ab$. Then $b$ is an integer, $b\ge2$, and $b=n/a\le n/2<n$; also $a\ge2$. Both factors lie in the hypothesis range. Each has a finite prime list, and concatenating the two lists gives one for $n$. A prime uses its one-element list. Together with the base case $2$, these branches cover every integer $n\ge2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
更具体地，对固定 $n>2$，归纳假设覆盖每个满足 $2\le m<n$ 的整数 $m$，不只覆盖 $n-1$。若 $n$ 为合数，取正因子 $a$ 满足 $1<a<n$，写成 $n=ab$。此时 $b$ 是整数，$b\ge2$，且 $b=n/a\le n/2<n$；同时 $a\ge2$。两因子都落在假设范围内，各有有限素数列表，把列表接起来就得到 $n$ 的分解。若 $n$ 为素数，就用只含自身的列表。加上基例 $2$，便覆盖全部整数 $n\ge2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
### Uniqueness by matching one prime at a time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*逐个匹配素因子证明唯一性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For uniqueness, start with $p_1\cdots p_r=q_1\cdots q_s$. [[素数整除乘积|Euclid's lemma]] makes $p_1$ divide some prime $q_j$, hence $p_1=q_j$. Move that factor to the front, cancel it in the integer equality, and repeat. One side cannot run out while the other retains prime factors, whose product cannot equal $1$. Thus both multisets of factors agree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
唯一性从两种分解 $p_1\cdots p_r=q_1\cdots q_s$ 出发。由[[素数整除乘积]]，$p_1$ 必整除某个 $q_j$，而 $q_j$ 是素数，所以两者相等。把这个因子移到前面、在整数等式中约去，再重复。不会有一边先用完而另一边还有素因子，因为剩下的素数乘积不可能等于 $1$。因此两边的素因子连同重复次数完全一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The finite-product step follows by repeatedly applying the two-factor lemma: from $p\mid q_1(q_2\cdots q_s)$, either $p\mid q_1$ or $p$ divides the remaining product. Repeating this argument reaches a single factor after finitely many steps. A prime divisor $p>1$ of a prime $q_j$ must equal $q_j$, because the only positive divisors of $q_j$ are $1$ and $q_j$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
有限乘积这一步来自反复使用两个因子的引理：由 $p\mid q_1(q_2\cdots q_s)$，或者 $p\mid q_1$，或者 $p$ 整除剩余乘积。反复使用，有限步后一定到达某个单独因子。素数 $q_j$ 的素因子 $p>1$ 必等于 $q_j$，因为 $q_j$ 的正因子只有 $1$ 与 $q_j$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cancellation is valid in this integer equality because the matched prime is nonzero. Each cancellation removes one prime from each finite list, so the matching process terminates. If one list became empty first, its product would be $1$ while the other, still containing a prime, would have product at least $2$, a contradiction. Both lists therefore end together, with every occurrence matched. Euclid's lemma has an independent proof using [[Bézout等式|Bézout's identity]]; it need not assume the unique factorization being proved here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
匹配到的素数非零，因此可以在这个整数等式中约去。每次从两个有限列表各删除一个素数，所以过程有限终止。如果一个列表先空了，其乘积为 $1$，另一边仍含素数则乘积至少为 $2$，矛盾。因此两边同时耗尽，每次出现都已匹配。Euclid 引理可由[[Bézout等式]]独立证明，不需要预先假设这里正在证明的唯一分解。

> [!example] Two routes, one prime list · 两条路线，同一素数列表
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The route $60=6\cdot10$ gives $(2\cdot3)(2\cdot5)$; the route $60=12\cdot5$ gives $(2\cdot2\cdot3)\cdot5$. In a comparison, match a $2$ and cancel to obtain $30$ on both sides; the remaining matches are another $2$, then $3$ and $5$. The intermediate composite factors were different, but the prime multiplicities agree.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 路线 $60=6\cdot10$ 得到 $(2\cdot3)(2\cdot5)$；路线 $60=12\cdot5$ 得到 $(2\cdot2\cdot3)\cdot5$。比较时先匹配一个 $2$，约去后两边都为 $30$；再匹配另一个 $2$，接着匹配 $3$ 与 $5$。中间的合数因子不同，但素因子的次数相同。

## The cases one, zero and negative integers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 一、零与负整数的边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The empty product is defined to be $1$. This extends the statement to all positive integers: $1$ has the empty prime list, while each prime has a one-element list. Keeping $1$ out of the prime definition matters: otherwise $15=3\cdot5=1\cdot3\cdot5=1\cdot1\cdot3\cdot5$ would have arbitrary extra prime occurrences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
空乘积约定为 $1$。这样可把陈述扩展到所有正整数：$1$ 对应空素数列表，每个素数对应一个元素的列表。不把 $1$ 定为素数非常重要；否则 $15=3\cdot5=1\cdot3\cdot5=1\cdot1\cdot3\cdot5$ 就能任意增加所谓素因子的次数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The integer $0$ is outside the theorem: no finite product of positive primes is $0$, and the empty product is $1$. For a negative integer, first separate its sign and factor its positive absolute value. For example, $-60=(-1)\,2^2\cdot3\cdot5$; the factor $-1$ records the sign and is not a positive prime. In particular, $-1$ is its sign times an empty prime product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
整数 $0$ 不在定理范围内：有限个正素数相乘不可能为 $0$，空乘积则为 $1$。对于负整数，先分离负号，再分解其正的绝对值。例如 $-60=(-1)\,2^2\cdot3\cdot5$，其中 $-1$ 记录符号，不是正素数。特别地，$-1$ 本身就是负号乘上空素数乘积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem establishes existence and uniqueness, not an efficient factorization method for large integers. [[RSA安全边界|RSA's security boundary]] requires distinguishing a mathematically determined answer from one an attacker can efficiently compute.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这个定理证明分解存在且唯一，并没有提供对大整数快速找出分解的保证。[[RSA安全边界]]恰恰需要区分“答案在数学上确定”与“攻击者能否有效算出答案”。

> [!question]- What does the theorem rule out? · 定理排除了什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Is $60=4\cdot15$ a counterexample to uniqueness?
>
> **Answer:** No. Those factors are composite. Refining them gives $(2\cdot2)(3\cdot5)$, the same prime list. The uniqueness statement compares completed prime factorizations, not arbitrary products of composite factors.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> $60=4\cdot15$ 是否反驳唯一性？
>
> **答案：** 不反驳。这两个因子是合数，继续分解得到 $(2\cdot2)(3\cdot5)$，仍是同一素数列表。唯一性比较的是已经全部分成素数的结果，不是任意合数乘积。
