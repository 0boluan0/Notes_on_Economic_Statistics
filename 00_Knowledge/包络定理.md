# The envelope theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*包络定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a price, technology parameter, or resource limit changes, the best achievable payoff changes too. The **envelope theorem** computes that value response without first solving for every movement of the chosen quantities. In an unconstrained interior optimum, a small adjustment of the choice has no first-order payoff effect. With constraints, the objective gradient need not vanish: the multipliers account for the first-order cost of keeping the moving constraints satisfied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价格、技术参数或资源上限变化时，能够获得的最好收益也会变化。**包络定理**计算这种最优价值的响应，不必先求每一个选择变量怎样移动。无约束内部最优处，选择的小幅调整没有一阶收益效应；有约束时，目标梯度未必为零，而乘子会计入继续满足移动约束所需的一阶调整。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[值函数|value function]] $v(\theta)$ records the globally best objective value, not the maximizing choice. The [[多元链式法则|chain rule]] normally includes both the parameter's direct effect and the indirect effect through the choice. The envelope identity cancels the indirect terms using the [[KKT条件|KKT equations]]. A partial derivative $\mathcal L_\theta$ holds $x$, $\lambda$, and $\nu$ fixed while changing $\theta$; only afterwards do we substitute their optimal values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[值函数]] $v(\theta)$ 记录全局最优目标值，而不是实现它的选择。[[多元链式法则]]通常同时包含参数的直接作用和通过选择调整产生的间接作用；包络恒等式利用 [[KKT条件]]消去后者。偏导 $\mathcal L_\theta$ 的意思是改变 $\theta$ 时暂时固定 $x$、$\lambda$、$\nu$，求完偏导后才代入它们的最优值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a scalar parameter $\theta$ in an open interval $I$ and choices $x$ in a common open set $U$. Assume $f,g,h$ are $C^1$ jointly on $U\times I$, and include all choice restrictions among the displayed constraints. Suppose a $C^1$ triple $(x^*(\theta),\lambda^*(\theta),\nu^*(\theta))$ exists throughout $I$: $x^*$ attains the finite global maximum, the triple satisfies KKT with $\lambda^*\ge0$, and the active inequality indices remain fixed. These are sufficient hypotheses for the following classical branch version. It does not establish that such a branch exists; [[隐函数比较静态|implicit comparative statics]] explains one route to constructing it, with optimality verified separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令标量参数 $\theta$ 位于开区间 $I$，选择 $x$ 位于共同开集 $U$。假设 $f,g,h$ 在 $U\times I$ 上共同为 $C^1$，并把所有选择限制列入显示的约束。进一步假设整个 $I$ 上存在 $C^1$ 三元组 $(x^*(\theta),\lambda^*(\theta),\nu^*(\theta))$：$x^*$ 取得有限的全局最大值，三元组满足 KKT 且 $\lambda^*\ge0$，活动不等式索引保持固定。这些是下面经典分支版本的充分前提；定理本身不负责证明分支存在。[[隐函数比较静态]]介绍一种构造途径，其中最优性仍须单独核实。

> [!important] Differentiate the optimized value · 最优价值的导数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $v(\theta)$ be the maximum of $f(x,\theta)$ subject to $g(x,\theta)\le0$ and $h(x,\theta)=0$, with $\mathcal L=f-\lambda^Tg-\nu^Th$. If a regular differentiable optimum branch with a fixed active set exists near the reference parameter, then $dv/d\theta=\partial\mathcal L/\partial\theta$ evaluated at the optimal choice and multipliers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑
> $$
> v(\theta)=\max_x f(x,\theta)
> \quad\text{s.t.}\quad
> g(x,\theta)\le0,\ h(x,\theta)=0,
> $$
> 并取
> $$
> \mathcal L=f-\lambda^Tg-\nu^Th.
> $$
> 若参考点附近存在正则、活动集固定且可微的最优分支 $(x^*(\theta),\lambda^*(\theta),\nu^*(\theta))$，则
> $$
> \frac{dv}{d\theta}
> =\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\theta}
> (x^*,\lambda^*,\nu^*;\theta).
> $$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No convexity assumption is needed for the chain-rule cancellation once this optimizing branch and its multipliers are already given. Convexity can instead help certify that a KKT branch is globally optimal. If $x(\theta)$ is only a local optimum or a stationary branch, the same calculation may describe its branch payoff $f(x(\theta),\theta)$, but that payoff cannot be labeled the global $v(\theta)$ without proving it is the best across all feasible choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一旦已经给出上述最优分支及乘子，链式法则的消项本身不需要凸性；凸性可以在另一步帮助认证 KKT 分支的全局最优。若 $x(\theta)$ 只是一条局部最优或驻点分支，同样的计算可能得到该分支收益 $f(x(\theta),\theta)$ 的导数，但未证明它优于全部可行选择，就不能把该收益称为全局 $v(\theta)$。

## How the indirect term disappears in a constrained problem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 约束问题中间接项怎样消失

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the next derivation only, write $g$ and $\lambda$ for the subvector of constraints in the fixed active set $\mathcal A$ and their multipliers. Thus $g(x^*(\theta),\theta)=0$ is an identity on $I$. The vector $h$ contains all equalities, and each gradient $\nabla_xf$ is a column. This active-row convention is needed for the displayed differentiated constraint equation; a slack inequality is not an equality to differentiate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仅在接下来的推导中，用 $g$、$\lambda$ 简记固定活动集 $\mathcal A$ 中的约束子向量及其乘子。因此 $g(x^*(\theta),\theta)=0$ 在 $I$ 上恒成立；$h$ 则保留全部等式，$\nabla_xf$ 按列向量书写。下面显示的约束微分等式必须采用这一活动行约定，不能把松弛不等式也当成恒等式求导。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The chain rule gives $dv/d\theta=f_\theta+\nabla_xf^T(dx^*/d\theta)$. Constrained stationarity replaces $\nabla_xf$ by $D_xg^T\lambda+D_xh^T\nu$. Total differentiation of every active constraint and equality gives $D_xg(dx^*/d\theta)+g_\theta=0$ and $D_xh(dx^*/d\theta)+h_\theta=0$. Substitution yields $dv/d\theta=f_\theta-\lambda^Tg_\theta-\nu^Th_\theta=\mathcal L_\theta$.

The cancellation in a constrained problem is not the claim that $\nabla_xf=0$ at the optimum; it generally is not. The cancellation comes from **Lagrangian stationarity plus total differentiation of the active constraints**. Slack constraints have zero multipliers and therefore contribute no first-order term even if left in the notation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿最优分支使用链式法则：
$$
\frac{dv}{d\theta}
=f_\theta+\nabla_xf^T\frac{dx^*}{d\theta}.
$$
约束驻点给
$$
\nabla_xf=D_xg^T\lambda+D_xh^T\nu.
$$
对每条活动约束和等式约束全微分，又有
$$
D_xg\frac{dx^*}{d\theta}+g_\theta=0,
\qquad
D_xh\frac{dx^*}{d\theta}+h_\theta=0.
$$
代回便得
$$
\frac{dv}{d\theta}
=f_\theta-\lambda^Tg_\theta-\nu^Th_\theta
=\mathcal L_\theta.
$$

因此，约束情形的消项并不是简单地说“最优点上 $\nabla_xf=0$”。约束最优时 $\nabla_xf$ 通常不为零；真正的消项来自 **Lagrangian 驻点 + 活动约束的全微分**。松弛约束的乘子为零，所以把它们保留在式子里也不贡献一阶项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To restore the original full vector of inequalities in stationarity and in the final expression $f_\theta-\lambda^Tg_\theta-\nu^Th_\theta$, pad inactive multiplier entries with zeros. This leaves those weighted expressions unchanged. It does **not** assert $D_xg_i\,x^{*\prime}+g_{i,\theta}=0$ for an inactive index. The proof also never differentiates a multiplier, which explains why the final formula requires only its current value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把驻点式和最终的 $f_\theta-\lambda^Tg_\theta-\nu^Th_\theta$ 恢复为原来的全不等式向量时，只需给不活动乘子补零，相应加权表达式保持不变。这**不表示**不活动索引也满足 $D_xg_i\,x^{*\prime}+g_{i,\theta}=0$。证明也从未对乘子求导，所以最终公式只需要乘子的当前值。

> [!example] A moving resource limit · 资源上限在移动
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $v(b)=\max_{0\le x\le b}(4x-x^2)$ and restrict attention to $0<b<2$. The payoff increases up to $x=2$, so the constrained maximizer is $x^*=b$. The lower bound is slack. With $\mathcal L=4x-x^2-\lambda(x-b)$, stationarity gives $\lambda^*=4-2b$. Holding $x$ and $\lambda$ fixed gives $\mathcal L_b=\lambda$, so the envelope theorem yields $v'(b)=4-2b$.
>
> Direct calculation agrees: $v(b)=4b-b^2$. At $b=1$, $x^{*\prime}=1$ and $f_x(x^*)=2$, so the indirect objective term is not zero; it is exactly the multiplier-weighted effect of the moving constraint. Setting $f_x=0$ would give the wrong answer.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $v(b)=\max_{0\le x\le b}(4x-x^2)$，先限于 $0<b<2$。目标在 $x=2$ 之前递增，故受约束最优为 $x^*=b$，下限松弛。取 $\mathcal L=4x-x^2-\lambda(x-b)$，驻点给出 $\lambda^*=4-2b$。固定 $x$ 和 $\lambda$ 求偏导得 $\mathcal L_b=\lambda$，于是包络公式给出 $v'(b)=4-2b$。
>
> 直接代入也得到 $v(b)=4b-b^2$。在 $b=1$，$x^{*\prime}=1$、$f_x(x^*)=2$，通过选择变化产生的目标项并不为零；它恰好等于移动约束的乘子加权效应。若错误地设 $f_x=0$，就得不到正确答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the equality example in [[隐函数比较静态|implicit comparative statics]], $f=-(x_1-\theta)^2-x_2^2$ and $h=x_1+x_2-1=0$. Its optimizer is $((1+\theta)/2,(1-\theta)/2)$. Since $h_\theta=0$, the envelope calculation is simply $v'(\theta)=f_\theta=2(x_1^*-\theta)=1-\theta$. Independently, the value is $v(\theta)=-(1-\theta)^2/2$, with the same derivative. The choice components move, but their net first-order contribution cancels under the equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[隐函数比较静态]]的等式例子中，$f=-(x_1-\theta)^2-x_2^2$、$h=x_1+x_2-1=0$，最优选择是 $((1+\theta)/2,(1-\theta)/2)$。因为 $h_\theta=0$，包络计算直接给出 $v'(\theta)=f_\theta=2(x_1^*-\theta)=1-\theta$。另外直接求值，得到 $v(\theta)=-(1-\theta)^2/2$，其导数一致。选择分量都在移动，但在等式约束下，它们的一阶净贡献相消。

## Parallel role with implicit-function comparative statics
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与隐函数比较静态的并列关系

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[隐函数比较静态|Implicit-function comparative statics]] solves for $dx^*/d\theta$ and asks how the optimal choice changes. The envelope formula gives $dv/d\theta$ directly and asks how the optimal value changes. If the target is the marginal parameter effect on profit, indirect utility, or cost, computing the full choice derivative is often unnecessary.

An unconstrained interior optimum is the special case with no constraint terms: stationarity gives $\nabla_xf=0$, so $dv/d\theta=f_\theta$. With multiple optimizers, active-set changes, or a nondifferentiable value function, inspect one-sided or directional value derivatives. [[Danskin定理|Danskin's theorem]] gives one precise fixed compact-set route, while [[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微|the optimizer-jump boundary]] prevents an invalid smoothness converse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[隐函数比较静态]] 要求解 $dx^*/d\theta$，回答“最优选择怎么变”。包络公式直接给 $dv/d\theta$，回答“最优价值怎么变”。如果只关心利润、间接效用或成本的边际参数效应，先求完整的选择导数往往是多余工作。

无约束内部最优是特例：没有约束项，驻点为 $\nabla_xf=0$，于是 $dv/d\theta=f_\theta$。但多最优解、活动集切换或价值函数不可微时，必须直接检查值函数的单侧或方向导数。固定紧选择集上的一条精确路线见 [[Danskin定理]]；选择路径与价值光滑性不能相互逆推，见 [[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The smooth-branch hypotheses are a convenient sufficient route, not a necessary characterization of value differentiability. In the capacity example, the active set changes at $b=2$: the value becomes $4$ for $b\ge2$, while its left derivative $4-2b$ tends to zero. Thus $v$ is differentiable there even though $x^*(b)=\min\{b,2\}$ is not. That point falls outside the fixed-active-set proof above; its derivative was established by direct calculation. The cited general envelope results have their own hypotheses rather than a blanket prohibition on switching.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

光滑分支前提是方便的充分路线，不是值函数可微性的必要刻画。在资源上限例子里，$b=2$ 处活动集切换：$b\ge2$ 后价值恒为 $4$，而左侧导数 $4-2b$ 趋于零。因此 $v$ 在这里仍可微，尽管 $x^*(b)=\min\{b,2\}$ 不可微。这个点不属于上面固定活动集的证明范围，其导数是通过直接计算得到的；更一般的包络结果各有自己的前提，不会一概禁止切换。

> [!question] Check the cancellation · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At a constrained optimum, $\nabla_xf$ is generally nonzero. Why can the terms containing $dx^*/d\theta$ still be eliminated?
>
> **Answer:** Lagrangian stationarity writes the objective gradient as a multiplier-weighted combination of active-constraint gradients. Total differentiation of those constraints then replaces the choice-derivative terms by the constraints' direct parameter derivatives.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 约束最优处 $\nabla_xf$ 往往不为零，为什么仍能消掉包含 $dx^*/d\theta$ 的项？
>
> **答案：** Lagrangian 驻点把目标梯度写成活动约束梯度的乘子组合；活动约束的全微分再把这些选择变化项换成约束对参数的直接偏导。
