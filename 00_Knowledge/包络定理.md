# 在正则且可微的约束最优分支上，价值函数对参数的导数等于 Lagrangian 对该参数的偏导数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Along a regular differentiable constrained optimum branch, the derivative of the value function equals the Lagrangian partial derivative with respect to the parameter*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 价值的一阶变化不需要先求选择的一阶变化
> 考虑
> $$
> v(\theta)=\max_x f(x,\theta)
> \quad\text{s.t.}\quad
> g(x,\theta)\le0,\ h(x,\theta)=0,
> $$
> 并取
> $$
> \mathcal L=f-\lambda^Tg-\nu^Th.
> $$
> 若参考点附近存在正则、活动集固定且可微的最优分支 $(x^*(\theta),\lambda^*(\theta),\nu^*(\theta))$，则
> $$
> \frac{dv}{d\theta}
> =\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\theta}
> (x^*,\lambda^*,\nu^*;\theta).
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $v(\theta)$ be the maximum of $f(x,\theta)$ subject to $g(x,\theta)\le0$ and $h(x,\theta)=0$, with $\mathcal L=f-\lambda^Tg-\nu^Th$. If a regular differentiable optimum branch with a fixed active set exists near the reference parameter, then $dv/d\theta=\partial\mathcal L/\partial\theta$ evaluated at the optimal choice and multipliers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 约束问题中间接项怎样消失
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How the indirect term disappears in a constrained problem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿最优分支使用链式法则：
$$
\frac{dv}{d\theta}
=f_\theta+\nabla_xf^T\frac{dx^*}{d\theta}.
$$
约束驻点给
$$
\nabla_xf=D_xg^T\lambda+D_xh^T\nu.
$$
对每条活动约束和等式约束全微分，又有
$$
D_xg\frac{dx^*}{d\theta}+g_\theta=0,
\qquad
D_xh\frac{dx^*}{d\theta}+h_\theta=0.
$$
代回便得
$$
\frac{dv}{d\theta}
=f_\theta-\lambda^Tg_\theta-\nu^Th_\theta
=\mathcal L_\theta.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The chain rule gives $dv/d\theta=f_\theta+\nabla_xf^T(dx^*/d\theta)$. Constrained stationarity replaces $\nabla_xf$ by $D_xg^T\lambda+D_xh^T\nu$. Total differentiation of every active constraint and equality gives $D_xg(dx^*/d\theta)+g_\theta=0$ and $D_xh(dx^*/d\theta)+h_\theta=0$. Substitution yields $dv/d\theta=f_\theta-\lambda^Tg_\theta-\nu^Th_\theta=\mathcal L_\theta$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，约束情形的消项并不是简单地说“最优点上 $\nabla_xf=0$”。约束最优时 $\nabla_xf$ 通常不为零；真正的消项来自 **Lagrangian 驻点 + 活动约束的全微分**。松弛约束的乘子为零，所以把它们保留在式子里也不贡献一阶项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cancellation in a constrained problem is not the claim that $\nabla_xf=0$ at the optimum; it generally is not. The cancellation comes from **Lagrangian stationarity plus total differentiation of the active constraints**. Slack constraints have zero multipliers and therefore contribute no first-order term even if left in the notation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与隐函数比较静态的并列关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Parallel role with implicit-function comparative statics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[隐函数比较静态]] 要求解 $dx^*/d\theta$，回答“最优选择怎么变”。包络公式直接给 $dv/d\theta$，回答“最优价值怎么变”。如果只关心利润、间接效用或成本的边际参数效应，先求完整的选择导数往往是多余工作。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[隐函数比较静态|Implicit-function comparative statics]] solves for $dx^*/d\theta$ and asks how the optimal choice changes. The envelope formula gives $dv/d\theta$ directly and asks how the optimal value changes. If the target is the marginal parameter effect on profit, indirect utility, or cost, computing the full choice derivative is often unnecessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无约束内部最优是特例：没有约束项，驻点为 $\nabla_xf=0$，于是 $dv/d\theta=f_\theta$。但多最优解、活动集切换或价值函数不可微时，必须直接检查值函数的单侧或方向导数。固定紧选择集上的一条精确路线见 [[Danskin定理]]；选择路径与价值光滑性不能相互逆推，见 [[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An unconstrained interior optimum is the special case with no constraint terms: stationarity gives $\nabla_xf=0$, so $dv/d\theta=f_\theta$. With multiple optimizers, active-set changes, or a nondifferentiable value function, inspect one-sided or directional value derivatives. [[Danskin定理|Danskin's theorem]] gives one precise fixed compact-set route, while [[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微|the optimizer-jump boundary]] prevents an invalid smoothness converse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 约束最优处 $\nabla_xf$ 往往不为零，为什么仍能消掉包含 $dx^*/d\theta$ 的项？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At a constrained optimum, $\nabla_xf$ is generally nonzero. Why can the terms containing $dx^*/d\theta$ still be eliminated?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** Lagrangian 驻点把目标梯度写成活动约束梯度的乘子组合；活动约束的全微分再把这些选择变化项换成约束对参数的直接偏导。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Lagrangian stationarity writes the objective gradient as a multiplier-weighted combination of active-constraint gradients. Total differentiation of those constraints then replaces the choice-derivative terms by the constraints' direct parameter derivatives.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
