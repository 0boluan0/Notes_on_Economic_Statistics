# 始终只用第一个观察可以无偏而不一致

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Using only the first observation can be unbiased but inconsistent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 若 $X_i\overset{iid}{\sim}N(\theta,1)$，取 $T_n=X_1$。每个 $n$ 都有 $E[T_n]=\theta$，但 $T_n\sim N(\theta,1)$ 不随 $n$ 收缩，因此无偏不推出一致。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For iid normal observations with variance one, the first observation is unbiased for the mean at every sample size. Its distribution never contracts, so unbiasedness does not imply consistency.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

取 $\varepsilon=1$，$P(|T_n-\theta|>1)=P(|Z|>1)\approx0.3173$，其中 $Z\sim N(0,1)$。概率始终不趋于 0，直接违反[[估计量一致性]]。失败来自估计规则没有使用新增数据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At tolerance one, the error probability stays about 0.3173. It fails the definition of [[估计量一致性|consistency]] because the estimator ignores additional observations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
