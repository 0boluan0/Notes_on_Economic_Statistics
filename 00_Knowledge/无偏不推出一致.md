# Unbiasedness without consistency
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*无偏不推出一致*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rule can be centred on the right answer across repeated samples and still fail to become more accurate when more data arrive. [[估计量无偏性|Unbiasedness]] asks whether its average answer equals the target. [[估计量一致性|Consistency]] asks whether the chance of a noticeable error disappears as sample size grows. Keeping only the first observation separates these two requirements particularly clearly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条规则在重复抽样时，平均答案可以正好等于目标，却仍不能随着数据增加而变得更准确。[[估计量无偏性|无偏性]]检查平均答案是否等于目标；[[估计量一致性|一致性]]检查样本量增大时，明显误差的概率是否消失。始终只用第一个观察，就能清楚地区分这两项要求。

^core

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the first measurement, however many arrive
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*后来测了多少次，都只报第一次*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ are repeated measurements of an unknown real mean $\theta$. Assume they are [[独立同分布|independent and identically distributed]], each with [[正态分布|normal distribution]] $N(\theta,1)$: mean $\theta$ and variance $1$. In particular, their first and second moments are finite. For every positive integer sample size $n$, use the estimation rule $T_n=X_1$. The subscript $n$ records how much data are available, even though this rule discards observations $2$ through $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ 是对未知实数均值 $\theta$ 的重复测量，假设它们[[独立同分布]]，每个都服从[[正态分布]] $N(\theta,1)$，即均值为 $\theta$、方差为 $1$，所以一阶、二阶矩都有限。对每个正整数样本量 $n$，使用规则 $T_n=X_1$。下标 $n$ 表示手里已有多少数据，尽管这条规则丢弃了第 $2$ 至第 $n$ 个观察。

> [!example] The answer never uses the extra readings · 新读数没有进入答案
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the observed measurements begin $10.8,9.6,10.2$, the reported estimates for sample sizes $1,2,3$ are all $10.8$. On another run, the first measurement and hence all three reported estimates may change. This distinguishes the random [[估计对象三分|estimation rule from its realised answer]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果实际读数开头是 $10.8,9.6,10.2$，样本量为 $1,2,3$ 时，报告的估计值全是 $10.8$。重新做一次实验，第一次读数可能不同，三个答案也会一起改变。这就是随机的[[估计对象三分|估计规则与已经实现的答案]]的区别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $E_\theta$ and $P_\theta$ for expectation and probability under a fixed value of the model parameter. Since $X_1$ has mean $\theta$, every $n$ satisfies
$$E_\theta[T_n]=E_\theta[X_1]=\theta.$$
This holds for every real $\theta$, so the rule is unbiased throughout this model. Its [[抽样分布|sampling distribution]] nevertheless remains $N(\theta,1)$ for every $n$: additional observations do not change this rule's distribution at all.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $E_\theta,P_\theta$ 表示固定模型参数值下的期望与概率。因为 $X_1$ 的均值是 $\theta$，每个 $n$ 都有上面的期望恒等式。它对每个实数 $\theta$ 都成立，所以规则在整个模型中无偏。可是，它的[[抽样分布]]始终为 $N(\theta,1)$；增加观察并没有改变这条规则的分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check a fixed error tolerance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把“不集中”落实为一个超差概率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To disprove consistency, it is enough to find **one fixed** positive tolerance whose error probability does not tend to zero. Choose tolerance $1$. By [[正态标准化|normal standardisation]], $Z=X_1-\theta$ has distribution $N(0,1)$. Let $\Phi(z)=P(Z\le z)$ be its [[累积分布函数|cumulative distribution function]]. Symmetry of this distribution gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要否定一致性，只需找到**一个固定**的正容忍值，使超差概率不趋零。取容忍值 $1$；由[[正态标准化]]，$Z=X_1-\theta$ 服从 $N(0,1)$。用 $\Phi(z)=P(Z\le z)$ 表示它的[[累积分布函数]]。由该分布的对称性，得到

> [!important] A probability that never decreases · 始终不变的概率
> $$P_\theta(|T_n-\theta|>1)=P(|Z|>1)=2[1-\Phi(1)]\approx0.3173.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is the same probability for $n=1$, $n=100$, and every larger sample size. About $31.73\%$ of repeated samples produce an error exceeding one unit, however many observations the rule is given. It is therefore not consistent for $\theta$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $n=1$、$n=100$ 或任何更大样本量，都得到同一个概率。无论给这条规则多少观察，重复抽样时约有 $31.73\%$ 的答案偏离目标超过一个单位，所以它对 $\theta$ 不一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The decisive evidence is this tail probability, not merely the fact that variance stays at $1$. In general, [[均方收敛推出概率收敛|probability convergence can coexist with rare large errors that prevent second moments from shrinking]]. Here the full normal distribution stays unchanged, so there is no such ambiguity. Averaging all observations would instead have variance $1/n$ in this model. The failure belongs to the rule that ignores new information, even though the observations themselves are independent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

决定性证据是这个尾部概率，而不只是方差一直为 $1$。一般而言，[[均方收敛推出概率收敛|依概率收敛仍可伴随罕见大误差，使二阶矩不收缩]]。这里整个正态分布都不变，所以结论没有这层歧义。若改为平均全部观察，在此模型下方差会是 $1/n$。失败来自忽略新信息的规则，即使数据本身相互独立也一样。
