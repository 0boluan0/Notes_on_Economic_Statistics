# 梯形法逐段积分端点间的线性插值并等于同网格左右矩形和的平均
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The composite trapezoidal rule integrates the straight line between each pair of endpoints and averages the left and right sums on the same grid*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限区间 $a<b$，取 $N$ 个等宽小区间，$h=(b-a)/N$、$x_i=a+ih$。梯形法在每段连接两个端点的函数值，以这条直线下的带符号积分近似 $f$ 的积分；$f$ 连续时，网格加细后趋于 [[定积分]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite interval with $a<b$, use $N$ equal subintervals, $h=(b-a)/N$, and $x_i=a+ih$. On each subinterval, integrate the line joining the two endpoint values to approximate the signed integral of $f$. For continuous $f$, refinement converges to the [[定积分|definite integral]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
T_N=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\frac h2[f(x_{i-1})+f(x_i)]
=\frac h2\left[f(x_0)+2\sum_{i=1}^{N-1}f(x_i)+f(x_N)\right]
=\frac{L_N+R_N}{2}.
$$

每个内部端点同时属于左右两个梯形，所以权重为 $2$；最外端点只出现一次。最后一个等式中的 $L_N,R_N$ 必须采用同一网格的 [[左右端点矩形法]]。$N$ 个小区间需要 $N+1$ 个不同的端点函数值，一次函数可被精确积分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An interior endpoint belongs to two neighbouring trapezoids, giving weight $2$; each outer endpoint appears once. Here $L_N,R_N$ are the [[左右端点矩形法|endpoint rectangle rules]] on the same grid. The method requires $N+1$ distinct endpoint values and integrates affine functions exactly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\int_1^2dx/x$ 取 $N=2$，节点是 $1,3/2,2$，所以 $T_2=\tfrac14(1+2\cdot\tfrac23+\tfrac12)=17/24\approx0.708333$。如何利用二阶导数判断误差方向和上界，见 [[梯形积分误差界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\int_1^2dx/x$ with $N=2$, the nodes are $1,3/2,2$, giving $T_2=\tfrac14(1+2\cdot\tfrac23+\tfrac12)=17/24\approx0.708333$. See [[梯形积分误差界|the trapezoidal error bound]] for second-derivative control of the error's sign and size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 把 $f(x)=1$ 代入梯形公式，为何必须得到 $b-a$？
>
> **答案：** 加权和是 $1+2(N-1)+1=2N$，乘 $h/2$ 后为 $Nh=b-a$；这能检查外系数和端点权重是否记错。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why must the trapezoidal formula return $b-a$ for $f(x)=1$? **Answer:** The weights sum to $1+2(N-1)+1=2N$. Multiplying by $h/2$ gives $Nh=b-a$, checking both the prefactor and endpoint weights.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
