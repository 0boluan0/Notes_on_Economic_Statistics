# 提高国产比例并不自动提高供应链韧性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A higher domestic production share does not automatically improve supply chain resilience.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

国产比例描述生产发生在哪里；[[供应链韧性]]描述冲击后能否恢复稳定运作。把生产迁回国内可能减少某些跨境风险，却不自动消除上游依赖、国内灾害、质量事故或共同基础设施中断。判断国产化是否提高韧性，要比较具体风险暴露和可执行的恢复路径，而不是把地理标签当成能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The domestic production share describes where production occurs; [[供应链韧性|supply chain resilience]] concerns recovery after disruption. Reshoring may reduce particular cross-border risks without eliminating upstream dependencies, domestic disasters, quality failures or common infrastructure outages. Its contribution must be assessed through specific exposures and feasible recovery paths rather than inferred from a geographic label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个反例是：两家国内装配厂都依赖同一家海外芯片厂，而且没有经过验证的替代芯片。增加国内装配比例，并没有消除这一个上游停供点；即使改用两个国内芯片供应商，若二者共用会同时停摆的电网或关键投入，风险也未必真正分散。反过来，跨国供应商若受到不同冲击并能在所需时间内切换，可能提供恢复选项。这里比较的是共同依赖与替代能力，不是断言国内或国外总是更安全。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider two domestic assembly plants that both depend on the same overseas chip producer and have no validated substitute chip. Increasing domestic assembly does not remove that upstream failure point. Even two domestic chip suppliers may not diversify risk if both depend on an electricity network or critical input that fails together. Conversely, suppliers in different countries may provide recovery options when their exposures differ and switching is feasible within the required time. The relevant comparison is shared dependence and substitutability, not an assertion that domestic or foreign production is always safer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评估一个替代方案时，应检查替代品是否兼容、备用产能是否可用、运输和认证需要多久，以及库存能支撑多久。还要计入增加供应商、保有库存、重复产能和失去专业化收益的成本。冗余和多元化可以有帮助，但数量更多、距离更近或全链集中于本国，都不是无条件的改进；应针对明确的服务目标和冲击情景比较方案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An alternative must be checked for compatibility, available spare capacity, transport and qualification lead times, and the duration of inventory cover. Additional suppliers, inventories, duplicated capacity and lost specialization benefits also have costs. Redundancy and diversification can help, but larger counts, shorter distances or concentrating the whole chain domestically are not unconditional improvements. Alternatives should be compared against a specified service objective and shock scenario.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
