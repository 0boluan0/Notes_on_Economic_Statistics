# 回购协议约定一方先出售资产并在未来按约定条件购回等价资产
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A repurchase agreement combines an initial asset sale with an agreement to repurchase equivalent assets later on agreed terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回购协议把两次相反方向的交易连在一起：今天卖方交付证券、收到现金，未来卖方支付约定的购回价、取回等价证券。经济作用类似以证券支持的短期融资，但合同采用资产转让与购回安排，不能只凭“像抵押贷款”就认定所有法律细节相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The seller delivers securities for cash today, then pays the agreed repurchase price for equivalent securities later. Economically it resembles secured funding, but the contractual transfer-and-repurchase arrangement need not share every legal feature of a collateralised loan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在不发生证券付息、无费用、采用简单利息和ACT/360的教学例子中，借入现金100万元、年化回购利率3.6%、期限10天，到期支付为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With no intervening coupon or fees and simple ACT/360 interest, financing of 1,000,000 at 3.6% annualised for ten days produces:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P_{\mathrm{repurchase}}=1{,}000{,}000\left(1+0.036\frac{10}{360}\right)=1{,}001{,}000.
$$

证券市值不必等于借入现金；折扣、追加保证金、违约处置与是否允许再次使用证券，都要看合同。上式只计算这组条件下的现金腿，不是全部风险分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Collateral value need not equal the cash borrowed. Haircuts, margin calls, default close-out, and reuse depend on the contract. The formula covers the cash leg under the stated assumptions, not the complete risk assessment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场惯例通常把卖方一侧称为repo、买方一侧称为reverse repo；央行文件可能采用特定操作命名，分析投放或回笼方向时用[[央行回购方向|现金流向]]确认。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market usage generally calls the seller's side a repo and the buyer's side a reverse repo. Central-bank operations can use specific naming conventions; establish [[央行回购方向|cash direction]] before interpreting liquidity provision or absorption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
