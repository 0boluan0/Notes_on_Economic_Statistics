# 覆盖左侧的匹配给出单射但不一定是完美匹配

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A matching that covers the left side defines an injection but need not be perfect*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区别
> 在二分图 $G=(L\cup R,E)$ 中，matching 若覆盖 $L$ 的每个顶点，就为每个 $\ell\in L$ 选择了一个互不重复的右侧伙伴，因此定义一个单射 $f:L\to R$。perfect matching 则要求图中每个顶点都被覆盖。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a bipartite graph $G=(L\cup R,E)$, a matching that covers every vertex of $L$ assigns each $\ell\in L$ a distinct partner in $R$, thereby defining an injection $f:L\to R$. A perfect matching, by contrast, covers every vertex of the graph.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $|L|=|R|$，覆盖 $L$ 的 matching 含 $|L|$ 条边，也必然覆盖全部 $R$，所以它是 perfect。若 $|R|>|L|$，覆盖 $L$ 仍会留下 $|R|-|L|$ 个右点；这个分配已经完成左侧任务，却不是整张图的 perfect matching。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $|L|=|R|$, a matching covering $L$ has $|L|$ edges and must also cover all of $R$, so it is perfect. If $|R|>|L|$, covering $L$ leaves $|R|-|L|$ right vertices unmatched. The left-side assignment is complete, but the matching is not perfect on the whole graph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，两名学生对应三个项目，只要两名学生各获一个不同项目，matching 就覆盖了学生侧；剩下一个空项目不会使分配失败。把“覆盖指定一侧”误说成 “perfect”，会把问题原本没有要求的覆盖目标偷偷加进去。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, with two students and three projects, assigning distinct projects to both students covers the student side; one unused project does not make the assignment fail. Calling this “perfect” would silently impose a coverage requirement that the problem never asked for.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，开始求解前应先写清目标是 covers $L$、covers $R$、covers all vertices，还是仅最大化 $|M|$。[[Hall 定理|Hall's theorem]] 通常刻画的是覆盖指定左侧 $L$，而不是默认两侧都被覆盖。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before solving, state whether the objective is to cover $L$, cover $R$, cover every vertex, or merely maximize $|M|$. [[Hall 定理|Hall's theorem]] normally characterizes coverage of a designated left side $L$, not automatic coverage of both sides.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与辨别
> 若 $|L|=2$、$|R|=3$，一个大小为 $2$ 且覆盖 $L$ 的 matching 是否 perfect？
>
> **答案：** 不是。它覆盖全部左点，却必然留下一个右点；只有覆盖图中所有顶点才叫 perfect。
