# 边际效用递减不等于总效用下降
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diminishing marginal utility does not mean falling total utility*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 若总效用写成数量的可微函数 $U(q)$，边际效用是 $MU(q)=U'(q)$。边际效用递减说的是 $MU$ 随 $q$ 增加而下降，并不决定 $MU$ 的正负：在 $MU(q)>0$ 的区间，总效用仍在增加，只是增加得越来越慢；在 $MU(q)<0$ 的区间，总效用才随数量增加而下降。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If total utility is represented by a differentiable function $U(q)$, marginal utility is $MU(q)=U'(q)$. Diminishing marginal utility means that $MU$ falls as quantity rises; it does not determine the sign of $MU$. Total utility still rises, though more slowly, on an interval where $MU(q)>0$, and falls as quantity rises on an interval where $MU(q)<0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Jevons 所说的 total utility 与 final degree of utility 是两个不同对象：前者对应整段消费带来的总量，后者对应现有数量附近再增加一小单位所带来的增量。水可以有极高的总用途，同时在供给充足时拥有接近零的最后一单位效用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Jevons distinguished total utility from the final degree of utility. The former concerns the whole quantity consumed; the latter concerns the increment generated by one more small unit at the current stock. Water can have immense total usefulness while an additional unit has nearly zero marginal utility when water is abundant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在光滑模型中，$MU'(q)=U''(q)<0$ 表示局部凹性。它并不保证边际效用在所有数量上都为正，也不等于现代需求理论必须把效用当作可跨人比较的基数。现代消费者理论通常只要求效用函数表示偏好排序；换一个严格递增变换不会改变选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a smooth model, $MU'(q)=U''(q)<0$ expresses local concavity. It neither guarantees positive marginal utility at every quantity nor requires modern demand theory to treat utility as interpersonally comparable cardinal measurement. Modern consumer theory normally uses utility to represent an ordering of preferences; a strictly increasing transformation leaves choices unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若 $U(q)=10\sqrt q$ 且 $q>0$，随着 $q$ 增加，边际效用怎样变化？总效用是否下降？
>
> **答案：** $MU(q)=5/\sqrt q>0$ 且递减，所以总效用继续上升，但斜率越来越小。

## 来源与核验

- [Jevons, *The Theory of Political Economy*, Chapter III](https://www.econlib.org/library/YPDBooks/Jevons/jvnPE.html?chapter_num=7)：核对 total utility、final degree of utility、水的例子以及最后增量随数量增加而下降的原始表述。
- [Menger, *Principles of Economics*](https://cdn.mises.org/Principles%20of%20Economics_5.pdf)：交叉核对可用数量、被满足需要的重要性与具体部分价值之间的关系。
- [Stanford Econ 11, Lecture 3](https://web.stanford.edu/~jay/micro_class/lecture3.pdf)：核对现代序数效用表示与严格递增变换保留选择的边界。
