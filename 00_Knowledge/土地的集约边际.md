# 土地的集约边际是在已耕土地上决定是否继续追加投入的边际

**土地的集约边际**是对已有耕地再增加一份资本和劳动时所面对的追加产出与成本。它考察“这块地还要不要继续加投入”；开垦另一块土地则是在广延边际上扩张。粮食需求增加后，经营者应比较这两种办法，并不必然先去开垦最差的空地。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **intensive margin of land use** concerns the additional output and cost of applying more capital and labour to land already cultivated. It asks whether to intensify existing use; bringing another plot into cultivation expands the extensive margin. Higher food demand requires comparing these alternatives rather than automatically cultivating the poorest unused land.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，旧地上再投同一份资本可多得 85 单位粮食，新开劣地只能多得 80；在其他条件相同时，追加旧地更有利。Ricardo 因而把无租边际不仅放在“最后一块土地”，也放在“最后一份资本”。这里比较的是新增的 85，而不是旧地加总之后的产量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an equal additional capital outlay yields 85 units of grain on existing land but only 80 on a new inferior plot, intensification is preferable with other conditions equal. Ricardo therefore locates the rent-free margin in the last application of capital as well as in the last grade of land. The relevant comparison uses the extra 85, not total output on the old plot.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原书接着设同一地块上三次相等资本投入的产出分别为 100、85、75。若只用前两次，第二次是无租边际，第一次的生产率优势为 15。若第三次也有必要，前两次相对它的优势变成 25 和 10，差额共 35；第三次本身仍是比较基准。这个例子把 [[级差地租]] 的逻辑从“地块之间”推进到“追加投入之间”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ricardo then considers three equal capital applications yielding 100, 85 and 75. With only the first two used, the second is the rent-free margin and the first has an advantage of 15. If the third becomes necessary, the earlier advantages are 25 and 10, totalling 35. The third remains the benchmark. This extends [[级差地租|differential rent]] from differences between plots to differences between input applications.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种分解要求投入可比较、资本能寻找较优用途、利润率具有竞争性趋同压力。收益优势转为地主实际收到的租金还取决于租约能否调整；Ricardo 的例子明确提到租期届满。不能把每期追加投入的产出差直接说成当期租约必然改价，也不能把整个已出租农场说成“无租”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The comparison requires comparable inputs, alternative uses of capital and competitive pressure toward a common profit rate. Translating a productive advantage into an actual rent payment also depends on contractual adjustment; Ricardo explicitly refers to the lease expiring. An output difference need not reprice an existing lease immediately, and a rent-free marginal application does not make the whole rented farm rent-free.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ricardo（1821），[《原理》II.7–10](https://www.econlib.org/library/Ricardo/ricP.html?chapter_num=3#2.7)：支持旧地追加投入与新地开垦的比较、100／85／75 的相等资本例、最后投入无租以及租约届满的条件。15、25、10、35 已依原例逐项复算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chapter II, paragraphs 7–10, supports the two margins, equal-capital example, rent-free last application and lease-expiry condition. All reported differences and their sum have been recomputed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
