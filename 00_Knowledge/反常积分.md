# 反常积分把无界区间或奇点附近的累积定义为有限截断积分的极限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An improper integral defines accumulation on an unbounded interval or near a singularity as a limit of finite truncations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 反常积分不是把无穷或奇点当作普通端点，而是先在每个合法的有限区间上做定积分，再让截断边界趋向问题点。例如
> $$\int_a^\infty f(x)\,dx:=\lim_{b\to\infty}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx.$$
> 右侧极限存在且为有限实数时，反常积分才称为收敛。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> An improper integral does not treat infinity or a singularity as an ordinary endpoint. It first forms proper integrals on valid finite intervals and then lets the truncation boundary approach the problematic point. For example, $\int_a^\infty f(x)\,dx:=\lim_{b\to\infty}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$. The improper integral converges only when this limit exists as a finite real number.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 在有限左端点 $a$ 附近无界，则定义改为
$$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx:=\lim_{\varepsilon\to0^+}\int_{a+\varepsilon}^b f(x)\,dx.$$
形式虽然不同，核心动作相同：不在函数失去普通可积性的地方直接运算，而是从定义良好的截断积分取得极限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f$ is unbounded near the finite left endpoint $a$, the definition becomes $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx:=\lim_{\varepsilon\to0^+}\int_{a+\varepsilon}^b f(x)\,dx$. The notation changes, but the operation is the same: never calculate directly at a point where ordinary integrability fails; take a limit of well-defined truncated integrals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## “发散”不只表示趋于正无穷

若截断积分趋于 $+\infty$ 或 $-\infty$、在不同子序列间振荡，或根本没有唯一极限，普通反常积分都不收敛。写出截断极限能区分这些失败，而一句“面积太大”只能解释非负函数的一部分情形。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If truncated integrals tend to $+\infty$ or $-\infty$, oscillate among subsequences, or have no unique limit, the ordinary improper integral diverges. Writing the truncation limit distinguishes these failures; “the area is too large” explains only part of the nonnegative case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 判断 $\int_1^\infty e^{-x}\,dx$ 时，第一行为什么必须保留一个有限上限？
>
> **答案：** 因为积分的定义对象是 $\int_1^b e^{-x}dx$ 的极限；只有有限截断积分先有普通意义，才能再令 $b\to\infty$。
