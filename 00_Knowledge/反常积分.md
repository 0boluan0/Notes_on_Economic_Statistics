# 反常积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Improper integrals*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当积分区间无界或 integrand 在端点/内部发散时，用极限给“总累积”一个严格定义。
> **具体锚点：** $\int_1^\infty x^{-2}dx=1$，但 $\int_1^\infty x^{-1}dx$ 发散；被积函数都趋零不足以决定累积。
> **核心难点：** 每个无穷端点或奇点都要分别取极限；不能把两个发散量用对称截断假装抵消。
> **为什么重要：** 概率密度的可积性、尾部矩、连续时间现值和近似误差都可能依赖它。
> **继续：** 先拆分所有问题端点，再用 $p$-积分、比较或极限比较判断。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Improper integrals define total accumulation through limits when an interval is unbounded or the integrand diverges at an endpoint or interior point.
> **Concrete anchor:** $\int_1^\infty x^{-2}dx=1$, whereas $\int_1^\infty x^{-1}dx$ diverges. Merely tending to zero does not guarantee finite accumulation.
> **Central difficulty:** Every infinite endpoint or singularity needs its own limit. Symmetric truncation cannot manufacture cancellation between two divergent pieces.
> **Why it matters:** Integrability of densities, tail moments, continuous-time present value, and approximation error can all depend on these limits.
> **Continue with:** Split at every problematic point, then compare with a benchmark such as a $p$-integral.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反常积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Improper integrals*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无穷区间或 integrand 在端点无界时，积分必须定义为极限，例如 $\int_1^\infty f=\lim_{b\to\infty}\int_1^b f$。若内部有奇点，要在奇点两侧分别收敛，不能让正负无穷“相消”。$p$-积分给出基准：$\int_1^\infty x^{-p}dx$ 当且仅当 $p>1$ 收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On an infinite interval, or when the integrand is unbounded at an endpoint, define the integral as a limit; for example, $\int_1^\infty f=\lim_{b\to\infty}\int_1^b f$. An interior singularity requires convergence on both sides separately; positive and negative infinities cannot be allowed to “cancel.” The benchmark $p$-integral $\int_1^\infty x^{-p}dx$ converges exactly when $p>1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 判别方法与边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Tests and boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非负 integrand，若 $0\le f(x)\le g(x)$ 且 $\int g$ 收敛，则 $\int f$ 收敛；若 $f(x)\ge g(x)\ge0$ 且 $\int g$ 发散，则 $\int f$ 发散。若 $f/g\to c\in(0,\infty)$，两者同收敛或同发散。比较必须在问题端点附近成立，而不是根据中间一段图像判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nonnegative integrands, $0\le f\le g$ plus convergence of $\int g$ implies convergence of $\int f$; conversely, $f\ge g\ge0$ plus divergence of $\int g$ implies divergence of $\int f$. If $f/g\to c\in(0,\infty)$, the two have the same convergence behaviour. The comparison must hold near the problematic endpoint, not merely on a visually convenient middle interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：内部奇点不能对称抵消
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: an interior singularity cannot be cancelled symmetrically*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\int_{-1}^{1}x^{-1}dx$ 不是普通意义下的收敛反常积分，因为 $\int_{-1}^{0}x^{-1}dx=-\infty$ 而 $\int_0^1x^{-1}dx=+\infty$。对称截断 $\int_{-a}^{-\varepsilon}+\int_{\varepsilon}^{a}$ 恒为零，给的是 Cauchy principal value，不是两侧各自收敛的反常积分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\int_{-1}^{1}x^{-1}dx$ does not converge as an ordinary improper integral because $\int_{-1}^{0}x^{-1}dx=-\infty$ and $\int_0^1x^{-1}dx=+\infty$. Symmetric truncation makes $\int_{-a}^{-\varepsilon}+\int_{\varepsilon}^{a}$ equal zero, but that is a Cauchy principal value, not convergence of the two one-sided improper integrals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 反常积分在内部奇点处为什么必须拆开？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must an improper integral be split at an interior singularity?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 定义要求两侧极限分别存在；若只对称取极限，可能把两个不存在的无穷量人为抵消。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The definition requires the two one-sided limits to exist separately. A symmetric limit can artificially cancel two divergent quantities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 $f(x)\to0$ 不足以保证 $\int_1^\infty f(x)dx$ 收敛？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does $f(x)\to0$ not guarantee convergence of $\int_1^\infty f(x)dx$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它只控制局部高度不控制尾部累积速度；$1/x\to0$ 但调和型尾部的积分仍发散。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It controls local height but not the rate of tail accumulation; $1/x\to0$, yet its harmonic-type tail integral diverges.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 使用比较判别时，不等式方向为什么重要？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does the inequality direction matter in a comparison test?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 比一个已知收敛函数更小才能推出收敛，比一个已知发散函数更大才能推出发散；反向不提供所需结论。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Being smaller than a convergent benchmark proves convergence, while being larger than a divergent benchmark proves divergence. Reversing either inequality gives no such conclusion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话区分反常积分和 Cauchy principal value。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distinguish an improper integral from a Cauchy principal value in your own words.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 反常积分要求每个问题端点的极限独立收敛；principal value 用协调的对称截断取一个组合极限，可在普通积分发散时存在。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An improper integral requires independent convergence at every problematic endpoint. A principal value coordinates symmetric truncations and can exist even when those separate limits diverge.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
