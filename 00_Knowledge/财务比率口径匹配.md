# 财务比率口径匹配先统一主体期间和计量范围再选择适当存量与分母解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Match financial-ratio bases by aligning entity, period, and measurement scope before choosing a representative stock and interpreting the denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算前依次确认：是合并还是母公司；利润属于全部股东还是普通股股东；流量覆盖多久；资产或权益取期初、期末还是平均；债务是否含经营负债；金额是否扣减值、含税或经过年化。分子分母必须回答同一个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before calculating, identify consolidated versus parent-only reporting, the shareholders entitled to the profit, the flow period, beginning/end/average stocks, inclusion of operating liabilities, impairment or tax treatment, and annualisation. Numerator and denominator must address the same question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

周转率和收益率把期间流量除以存量时，期间平均资产或权益通常更能代表投入；季节性明显时，两端平均仍可能不足。现金流/债务若问“本期现金对期末尚欠债务的覆盖”，则使用期末债务有不同的解释目的，不能机械改成平均。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For turnover and return ratios, average assets or equity usually better represent resources employed during the flow period; a two-point average may still be inadequate under seasonality. A cash-flow/debt ratio addressing coverage of debt outstanding at period-end has a different purpose and should not mechanically substitute average debt.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CPA本章为简化，除特别说明外使用期末存量。例如净利润136、期末资产2000，期末口径ROA为6.8%；若期初资产1680，改用两端平均得到 $136/1840\approx7.3913\%$。这是两个已声明口径，不是把一个答案纠正为另一个。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CPA uses period-end stocks for simplicity unless stated otherwise. Profit of 136 divided by end assets of 2,000 gives 6.8%. With beginning assets of 1,680, the two-point average gives $136/1840\approx7.3913\%$. These are two declared bases, not a correction of one answer into the other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后检查分母：零时比率未定义；很小时对微小变化敏感；负权益、负营运资本等情形虽可能算出值，却不能照正分母的通常高低排序。跨公司/跨期必须同时说明口径是否一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally inspect the denominator: a zero denominator makes the ratio undefined, and a small one makes it sensitive. Negative equity or working capital may yield numbers without the usual positive-denominator ranking. State whether measurement bases are comparable across companies or periods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
