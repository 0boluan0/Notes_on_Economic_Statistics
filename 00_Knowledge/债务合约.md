# 债务合约约定借款人向债权人偿还资金的义务及其条件

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A debt contract specifies a borrower's obligation to repay a creditor and the conditions governing repayment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债务合约形成债权人的偿付请求权和借款人的对应义务，约定本金、利息或其他计息规则、支付时间以及适用的担保、优先顺序和违约安排。贷款和债券是常见形式；有偿付承诺不代表实际支付必然兑现。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A debt contract creates a creditor's payment claim and a corresponding borrower obligation, specifying principal, interest or other accrual rules, payment dates, and relevant security, priority, and default arrangements. Loans and bonds are familiar forms; a promise to pay is not a guarantee of payment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若借出 100、约定一年后还 110，则 110 是约定到期支付，不是债权人已实现的回报。违约时实际回收可能更少。债务也不必都是固定利率或一次到期还款；分析[[股债到期分配]]时采用的单期固定债额，是为了看清权利分配而加的模型条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If 100 is lent in exchange for 110 due in one year, 110 is a promised terminal payment, not an already realized return. Default may reduce recovery. Debt need not always have a fixed rate or a single repayment date; the one-period fixed amount in [[股债到期分配|terminal debt–equity allocation]] is a simplifying model assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
