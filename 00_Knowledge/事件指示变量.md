# Event indicator
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*事件指示变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **event indicator** records the answer to a yes-or-no question as a number: $1$ if the event occurs and $0$ if it does not. On a die roll, we can ask “is the result even?” and record only the answer, without keeping the exact face value. This lets us use numerical operations to study events. When used to record membership of a category, such a $0/1$ variable is also called a **dummy variable**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**事件指示变量**把一个“是或否”的问题记成数字：事件发生记为 $1$，没有发生记为 $0$。掷骰子时，可以只问“点数是不是偶数”，只记录这个答案，不保留具体点数。这样，就可以用数值运算研究事件。用来记录是否属于某个类别时，这种 $0/1$ 变量也常称为**虚拟变量**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[随机变量|random variable]] is a rule assigning a number to each outcome. In a [[概率空间|probability space]], the sample space $\Omega$ contains the possible outcomes, and an event $A$ is a set of outcomes to which the model assigns a probability. Write $\omega$ for the outcome that happens. The indicator of $A$ is the particular recording rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[随机变量]]是一条给每个结果指定数字的规则。在[[概率空间]]中，样本空间 $\Omega$ 装着可能结果，事件 $A$ 是模型允许分配概率的一组结果。用 $\omega$ 表示实际出现的结果。事件 $A$ 的指示变量，就是下面这条记录规则：

$$
I_A(\omega)=\mathbf 1_A(\omega)=
\begin{cases}
1,&\omega\in A,\\
0,&\omega\notin A.
\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both $I_A$ and $\mathbf 1_A$ name the same function. The subscript tells us which event is being recorded, and $\omega\in A$ means that the outcome satisfies that event. Specifying “success is $1$” is therefore incomplete until we have said which outcomes count as success.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$I_A$ 与 $\mathbf 1_A$ 是同一个函数的两种写法。下标说明正在记录哪个事件；$\omega\in A$ 表示这个结果符合事件 $A$。因此，只说“成功记作 $1$”还不够，还要先说明哪些结果算成功。

> [!example] Keeping only whether the die is even · 只记录点数是否为偶数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For one six-sided die, let $A=\{2,4,6\}$. The indicator gives the following values, whether or not the die is fair:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对一枚六面骰子，令 $A=\{2,4,6\}$。无论骰子是否公平，指示变量都按下表取值：
>
> | $\omega$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ | $5$ | $6$ |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | $I_A(\omega)$ | $0$ | $1$ | $0$ | $1$ | $0$ | $1$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus $I_A(2)=1$ and $I_A(5)=0$. Seeing the value $1$ tells us the result was even; it does not distinguish $2$, $4$ and $6$. If the die is fair, the probability of seeing $1$ is $3/6=1/2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 所以 $I_A(2)=1$，而 $I_A(5)=0$。看到记录值为 $1$，只能知道点数是偶数，无法区分 $2$、$4$、$6$。若骰子公平，看到 $1$ 的概率才是 $3/6=1/2$。

## The event and its numerical record
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*事件与数值记录怎样对应*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The event “the indicator equals $1$” is exactly $A$. The event “the indicator equals $0$” is its complement $A^c$, meaning all outcomes in which $A$ does not occur:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“指示变量等于 $1$”这个事件恰好就是 $A$；“指示变量等于 $0$”则是它的补事件 $A^c$，即所有没有发生 $A$ 的结果：

$$\{I_A=1\}=A,\qquad \{I_A=0\}=A^c.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently, if $p=P(A)$, then $P(I_A=1)=p$ and $P(I_A=0)=1-p$. This is a [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli distribution]], written $I_A\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(p)$. The indicator supplies a concrete event-based way of constructing that distribution; $p$ may also be $0$ or $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，若 $p=P(A)$，就有 $P(I_A=1)=p$、$P(I_A=0)=1-p$。这正是 [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli 分布]]，记为 $I_A\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(p)$。指示变量提供了从具体事件构造这个分布的方法；$p$ 也可以等于 $0$ 或 $1$。

> [!warning] A record is not its probability · 记录值与概率不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On a fair die, $P(A)=1/2$ is fixed, but the observed indicator is either $0$ or $1$, never $1/2$. The [[指示变量期望|expected value of the indicator]] is $P(A)$; that is a statement about its average, not its value on one outcome.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对公平骰子，$P(A)=1/2$ 是一个确定的数，但实际记录的指示变量只能是 $0$ 或 $1$，不会是 $1/2$。[[指示变量期望|指示变量的期望]]等于 $P(A)$，说的是平均值，而不是某次结果上的记录值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once an event is recorded numerically, [[随机计数的指标和|adding indicators counts occurrences]], and [[指示变量的交事件乘积|multiplying indicators records joint occurrence]]. These operations keep their meaning because each indicator first specifies precisely what its $1$ represents.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把事件记成数字以后，[[随机计数的指标和|相加可以计数]]，[[指示变量的交事件乘积|相乘可以记录事件是否同时发生]]。这些运算之所以有明确含义，是因为每个指示变量都先说明了自己的 $1$ 代表什么。

> [!question]- Try a different event · 换一个事件试试
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $B$ mean “the die is greater than $4$”. What are $I_B(3)$, $I_B(5)$ and $\{I_B=1\}$?
>
> **Answer:** $I_B(3)=0$, $I_B(5)=1$, and $\{I_B=1\}=\{5,6\}=B$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $B$ 表示“点数大于 $4$”。$I_B(3)$、$I_B(5)$ 和 $\{I_B=1\}$ 分别是什么？
>
> **答案：** $I_B(3)=0$，$I_B(5)=1$，$\{I_B=1\}=\{5,6\}=B$。
