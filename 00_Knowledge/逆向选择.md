# 逆向选择是私人类型信息使交易选择偏向另一方较不利类型的问题

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Adverse selection occurs when private type information makes transaction selection favour types less favourable to the other side*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逆向选择是[[信息不对称]]的一种问题：参与者了解自己的风险、质量或其他类型特征，交易对方却不能充分区分；在给定价格或契约下，不同类型的参与或合约选择不同，使实际成交对象的构成偏向对方较不利的类型。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adverse selection is an [[信息不对称|asymmetric-information]] problem. Participants know their risk, quality, or other type characteristics, while the other side cannot adequately distinguish them. Types then differ in participation or contract choice at the offered terms, shifting the composition of transactions toward types less favourable to the other side.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，同一保费下，知道自己患病风险较高的人更愿意购买保险；若保险人不能据此分别定价，实际参保者就可能比用于定价的总体风险更高。关键是风险类型影响“谁进入或选哪份合约”，不是投保后某个人改变了预防行为；后者属于[[道德风险]]的诊断范围。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a common premium, people who privately know that their illness risk is high may be more willing to buy insurance. If insurers cannot price that difference, enrollees may be riskier than the population used for pricing. The mechanism concerns who enters or selects a contract, not an insured person's later change in precaution, which calls for a [[道德风险|moral-hazard]] diagnosis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不能从“积极申请贷款”直接推出借款人差，也不能从存在差项目直接推出市场最终只剩差项目。必须交代私人信息、类型如何影响选择，以及交易条件如何回应；信息生产和[[委托筛选与监督|中介筛选]]可能使部分好坏类型得以区分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Actively seeking a loan does not establish that a borrower is poor quality, nor does the presence of poor projects prove that only those projects will remain. Specify the private information, its effect on selection, and the response of contract terms. Information production and [[委托筛选与监督|intermediary screening]] may distinguish some types.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
