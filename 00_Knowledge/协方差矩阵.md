# Covariance matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*协方差矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When one observation contains several measurements, we need to describe both how much each measurement varies and how pairs vary together. A **covariance matrix** collects those quantities in one square table. Its row and column labels name the variables; each entry compares the variable named by its row with the one named by its column.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一次观察包含多个测量值时，需要同时描述每个量自身怎样波动，以及两个量怎样共同变化。**协方差矩阵**把这些量收集在一张方形表里。行和列的标签都是变量名称，每个元素比较所属行、列对应的两个变量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let a [[随机向量|random vector]] $X=(X_1,X_2)^T$ record two measurements from the same outcome. The superscript $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]], so this notation makes $X$ a column. The [[均值向量|mean vector]] $\mu=(E[X_1],E[X_2])^T$ gives their separate average values. Subtracting it centers each coordinate: $X_j-\mu_j$ is the deviation of measurement $j$ from its own mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用[[随机向量]] $X=(X_1,X_2)^T$ 记录同一个结果中的两个测量值。上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，因此这里把 $X$ 写成列向量。[[均值向量]] $\mu=(E[X_1],E[X_2])^T$ 给出各自的平均值；减去它就是逐坐标中心化，$X_j-\mu_j$ 表示第 $j$ 个测量值偏离自身均值多少。

> [!example] Four possible pairs, each with probability one quarter · 四个等可能的测量组合
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose the model assigns probability $1/4$ to each row below. These are the possible outcomes and their probabilities, not four observations being used to estimate an unknown population. The mean is $\mu=(2,4)^T$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设模型为下表每一行指定概率 $1/4$。这些是模型的可能结果及其概率，并不是用来估计未知总体的四个观测样本。均值为 $\mu=(2,4)^T$。
>
> | $X_1$ | $X_2$ | $X_1-2$ | $X_2-4$ | $(X_1-2)(X_2-4)$ |
> |---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
> | $1$ | $2$ | $-1$ | $-2$ | $2$ |
> | $1$ | $4$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $0$ |
> | $3$ | $4$ | $1$ | $0$ | $0$ |
> | $3$ | $6$ | $1$ | $2$ | $2$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Averaging the squared deviations gives [[方差|variances]] $1$ and $2$. Averaging the last column gives [[协方差|covariance]] $1$: the two deviations tend to have the same sign. Thus the table of pairwise comparisons is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 平均两列偏移的平方，得到[[方差]] $1$ 和 $2$；平均最后一列，得到[[协方差]] $1$，说明两个偏移倾向于同号。因此，两两比较组成的方形表为
>
> $$
> \Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix}.
> $$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reading the diagonal and the other entries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 对角与非对角分别回答什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A diagonal entry compares a coordinate with itself, so it averages a square and is that coordinate's variance. An off-diagonal entry averages the product of two different centered coordinates and is their covariance. Swapping the pair leaves the product unchanged, which explains why the matrix is [[实对称矩阵|symmetric]]. Covariance may be negative: deviations that tend to point in opposite directions contribute negative products.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对角元素把一个坐标与自己比较，因此平均的是平方，得到该坐标的方差。非对角元素平均两个不同坐标中心化后的乘积，得到它们的协方差。交换这两个坐标不会改变乘积，所以矩阵是[[实对称矩阵|对称的]]。协方差也可以为负：倾向于反向偏移的两个坐标会产生负的乘积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The units follow the product. If the coordinates are height in centimetres and weight in kilograms, their variances have units $\mathrm{cm}^2$ and $\mathrm{kg}^2$, while their covariance has units $\mathrm{cm}\cdot\mathrm{kg}$. A covariance is therefore not a unit-free correlation coefficient; [[相关矩阵|a correlation matrix]] uses a separate standardization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位也随乘积而来。如果两个坐标分别是以厘米计的身高、以千克计的体重，方差单位就是 $\mathrm{cm}^2$ 和 $\mathrm{kg}^2$，协方差单位是 $\mathrm{cm}\cdot\mathrm{kg}$。因此协方差不是无量纲的相关系数；[[相关矩阵]]还需要另做标准化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One definition for any finite number of coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*推广到任意有限个坐标*

> [!important] Population covariance · 总体协方差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $p\ge1$ be the number of coordinates, and let $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$ be a real random vector on one probability space. Assume **finite second moments**, $E[X_j^2]<\infty$ for every $j$. With $\mu=E[X]$, define the $p\times p$ covariance matrix by
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$
> \boxed{\Sigma=\operatorname{Cov}(X)
> =E[(X-\mu)(X-\mu)^T]},\qquad
> \Sigma_{jk}=E[(X_j-\mu_j)(X_k-\mu_k)].
> $$
>
> 令 $p\ge1$ 表示坐标数，$X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$ 是同一概率空间上的实随机向量。假设每个坐标都有**有限二阶矩**，即 $E[X_j^2]<\infty$。记 $\mu=E[X]$，上式定义了 $p\times p$ 的协方差矩阵；$\Sigma_{jk}$ 是第 $j$ 行、第 $k$ 列的元素。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product inside the expectation is an **outer product**: a $p\times1$ column multiplied by a $1\times p$ row. Ordinary [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] puts every centered pair product in the correct position. For two coordinates, writing $d_j=X_j-\mu_j$ makes this visible:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

期望内部的乘积叫作**外积**：一个 $p\times1$ 的列乘以一个 $1\times p$ 的行。普通[[矩阵乘法]]会把每对中心化乘积放到对应位置。二维时，记 $d_j=X_j-\mu_j$，就能直接看见：

$$
\begin{bmatrix}d_1\\d_2\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}d_1&d_2\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}d_1^2&d_1d_2\\d_2d_1&d_2^2\end{bmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expectation is taken **entry by entry**, producing a fixed population matrix. Finite second moments ensure that the means and all entries are finite; the [[期望的Cauchy–Schwarz不等式|Cauchy–Schwarz bound for expectations]] controls the absolute expectation of each product. No independence or normal distribution is assumed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取期望是**逐元素**进行的，得到一张固定的总体矩阵。有限二阶矩保证均值和所有元素都有限，[[期望的Cauchy–Schwarz不等式]]控制每个乘积的绝对期望。定义不要求坐标独立，也不要求正态分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matrix records second-order linear variation, not the full joint law. In particular, [[零协方差不推独立|zero covariance need not mean independence]], and the entries do not identify a causal direction. A [[样本协方差矩阵|sample covariance matrix]] is instead computed from observed data and estimates this population object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这张矩阵记录二阶线性波动，而不是完整的联合分布。特别是，[[零协方差不推独立]]，矩阵元素也不能确定因果方向。[[样本协方差矩阵]]则由观测数据计算，用来估计这里的总体对象。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To use the matrix, [[线性投影方差|the linear-combination variance identity]] turns fixed weights $a$ into a variance $a^T\Sigma a$. Its nonnegativity explains [[协方差矩阵半正定性|why covariance matrices are positive semidefinite]]. Changing units or mixing coordinates is handled separately by [[协方差仿射变换|the affine covariance transformation rule]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用这张矩阵时，[[线性投影方差|线性组合的方差恒等式]]把固定权重 $a$ 转成方差 $a^T\Sigma a$；方差的非负性解释了[[协方差矩阵半正定性]]。改变单位或者混合坐标，则由[[协方差仿射变换]]单独说明。
