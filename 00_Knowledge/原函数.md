# Antiderivative
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*原函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **antiderivative** recovers a function from its rate of change. If you know that a quantity changes at the rate $2x$, you are looking for a function whose slope at every input $x$ equals $2x$. The function $F(x)=x^2$ works because $F'(x)=2x$. So do $x^2+5$ and $x^2-3$: moving a graph vertically changes its height but leaves its slopes unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**原函数**是从变化率反过来寻找原来的函数。假如已知一个量的变化率是 $2x$，现在要找的就是：哪个函数在每个输入 $x$ 处的斜率都等于 $2x$？$F(x)=x^2$ 可以，因为 $F'(x)=2x$；$x^2+5$ 和 $x^2-3$ 也可以。把图像整体向上或向下平移，会改变高度，却不会改变各处的斜率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here [[导数|the derivative]] $F'(x)$ means the instantaneous rate of change of $F$ at $x$. We work on a nonempty open interval $I$, a stretch of real inputs with no gaps. A differentiable function $F$ is an antiderivative of $f$ on $I$ when **$F'(x)=f(x)$ at every $x\in I$**. Uppercase $F$ names the function being recovered; lowercase $f$ names its given derivative. The equality must hold throughout the interval, not merely at one test point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的[[导数]] $F'(x)$，表示 $F$ 在 $x$ 处的瞬时变化率。先在非空开区间 $I$ 上讨论，也就是一段没有缺口的实数输入。可导函数 $F$ 是 $f$ 在 $I$ 上的一个原函数，意思是 **每个 $x\in I$ 都满足 $F'(x)=f(x)$**。大写 $F$ 表示要恢复的函数，小写 $f$ 表示已知的导数。只在一个测试点碰巧相等，还不足以成为原函数。

## Reading the integral notation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*怎样读不定积分记号*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once one antiderivative $F$ has been found, [[原函数相差常数|uniqueness up to a constant]] says that all the others on the same interval are $F+C$, with $C$ any real constant. We write this family as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

找到一个原函数 $F$ 后，[[原函数相差常数]]告诉我们：同一区间上的其他原函数全都形如 $F+C$，其中 $C$ 是任意实常数。把这一族函数记为

$$
\int f(x)\,dx=F(x)+C.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $\int$ is the integral sign; $f(x)$ is the **integrand**; $dx$ tells us that $x$ is the integration variable. This expression is an **indefinite integral**, representing a family of functions. For example, $\int 2x\,dx=x^2+C$. A definite integral with fixed bounds represents a number instead; the [[Newton-Leibniz公式|Newton–Leibniz formula]] connects that number to endpoint values of an antiderivative under its hypotheses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\int$ 是积分号，$f(x)$ 叫**被积函数**，$dx$ 指明积分变量是 $x$。这种记号叫**不定积分**，表示一族函数，例如 $\int 2x\,dx=x^2+C$。固定上下限的定积分表示的是一个数；在相应条件下，[[Newton-Leibniz公式]]把这个数与原函数的端点值联系起来。不定积分并不是一个还没补写上下限的定积分。

> [!example] Check by differentiating · 用求导验算
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To find an antiderivative of $6x^2$, try $x^3$. Its derivative is $3x^2$, so multiply by $2$: $F(x)=2x^3$ gives $F'(x)=6x^2$. Thus $\int 6x^2\,dx=2x^3+C$. Checking the derivative verifies the proposed formula; the constant-difference theorem justifies that the family contains every answer on the interval.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 求 $6x^2$ 的原函数时，可以先试 $x^3$。它的导数是 $3x^2$，还差一倍，因此乘上 $2$：$F(x)=2x^3$ 就满足 $F'(x)=6x^2$。所以 $\int 6x^2\,dx=2x^3+C$。求导验算说明候选公式正确；相差常数定理则说明同一区间上的答案没有遗漏。

## Read differentiation rules backward, with their domains
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*反向使用求导规则，同时保留定义域*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since the derivative of $-\cos x$ is $\sin x$, the first formula below holds on the real line. For the second, let $r$ be a fixed real exponent, take $x>0$, and require $r\ne-1$. The [[实数幂求导|real power rule]] differentiates the numerator and produces the factor $r+1$, which the denominator cancels:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $-\cos x$ 的导数是 $\sin x$，下面第一式在实数轴上成立。第二式中，令 $r$ 为固定实指数，先取 $x>0$，并要求 $r\ne-1$。用[[实数幂求导]]得到分子的导数时，产生的系数 $r+1$，恰好被分母抵消：

$$
\int\sin x\,dx=-\cos x+C,
\qquad
\int x^r\,dx=\frac{x^{r+1}}{r+1}+C\quad(r\ne-1).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For exponents whose real powers are also defined and differentiable on other intervals, the power formula applies there too. For instance, a nonnegative integer exponent allows the whole real line. The stated positive-input restriction makes the formula unambiguous for arbitrary real $r$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于在其他区间上也有定义且可导的实幂，幂公式在那些区间上同样适用。例如，非负整数次幂可以取整个实数轴。先限制正输入，是为了让任意实指数 $r$ 的含义都明确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $r=-1$, dividing by $r+1$ is impossible: the proposed expression is undefined. Instead, $\int dx/x=\ln|x|+C$ on each interval avoiding zero. For $x>0$ this follows from [[对数函数求导|the logarithm derivative]] $(\ln x)'=1/x$; for $x<0$, the [[链式法则|chain rule]] gives $(\ln(-x))'=(1/(-x))(-1)=1/x$. Zero remains excluded, and constants on the two half-lines may differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $r=-1$ 时，分母 $r+1$ 为零，所得表达式没有定义，不能把它当作“形式答案”。这时应使用 $\int dx/x=\ln|x|+C$，每次在不经过零点的区间上理解。$x>0$ 时由[[对数函数求导]] $(\ln x)'=1/x$ 得到；$x<0$ 时用[[链式法则]]：$(\ln(-x))'=(1/(-x))(-1)=1/x$。零点始终不在定义域内，两条半轴上的常数也可以分别选取。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finding an antiderivative can require more than spotting a familiar derivative; [[换元积分|substitution]] handles certain composite expressions. Moreover, existence and an elementary formula are separate questions. For continuous $f$, the [[积分求导定理|fundamental theorem for accumulation]] constructs an antiderivative from integrals. Some such functions have [[无初等原函数的积分|no elementary antiderivative]], meaning no finite expression from the usual algebraic, exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric functions and their inverses. That distinction does not follow merely from failing to find a formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

寻找原函数未必总能靠认出一条熟悉的求导公式完成；[[换元积分]]能处理某些复合结构。另外，“原函数存在”与“原函数能写成初等公式”是不同问题。对连续的 $f$，[[积分求导定理]]可以用积分构造原函数；其中有些函数[[无初等原函数的积分|没有初等原函数]]，也就是无法用通常的代数运算、指数、对数、三角函数及其反函数的有限组合写出。这一结论不能仅凭自己暂时没找到公式来判断。
