# 简单移动平均是固定长度滚动窗口内观测值的等权算术平均
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A simple moving average is the equally weighted arithmetic mean of observations in a fixed-length rolling window.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

简单移动平均（simple moving average，SMA）在每个时点取最近 $N$ 个观测值的算术平均；时间向前移动一期时，窗口加入最新值并移除最旧值。用于证券价格时，先固定价格字段、观测频率与窗口长度，才有可复算的一条均线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simple moving average (SMA) takes the arithmetic mean of the most recent $N$ observations. Each new period adds the latest observation and removes the oldest. A reproducible price SMA requires a specified price field, observation frequency, and window length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以每个交易期的收盘价 $C_t$ 为输入，$N$ 为正整数，并将当期包括在窗口内：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $C_t$ be the closing price of trading period $t$ and $N$ a positive integer. The window includes the current period:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
SMA_{N,t}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}C_{t-j}.
$$

本卡在取得完整 $N$ 期有效观测之前不输出值；缺失观测不当作零填入。这里的“期”指所选行情频率中的观测期，日线的 $N$ 期通常是 $N$ 个交易日，并非 $N$ 个自然日。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This definition produces no value until a complete window of $N$ valid observations is available; missing values are not filled with zero. A period follows the selected data frequency: $N$ daily bars normally represent $N$ trading days, not calendar days.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若连续四期收盘价为 $10,12,11,15$，则第三期 $SMA_3=11$，第四期为 $(12+11+15)/3=12.6667$。均线增加 $5/3$，来自新进入的 $15$ 替换移出的 $10$，并非只由最后一期的涨跌决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For four closes of $10,12,11,15$, the three-period SMA is $11$ in period three and $12.6667$ in period four. Its increase of $5/3$ comes from replacing $10$ with $15$, rather than solely from the latest price change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
SMA_{N,t}-SMA_{N,t-1}=\frac{C_t-C_{t-N}}{N}.
$$

SMA 是已观察价格的平滑变换。若使用当期收盘价，必须等该值可得后才能计算；均线穿越如何变成订单属于另外的交易规则。它也不等于时间序列 MA 模型中的随机误差结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An SMA smooths observed prices. If it uses the current close, it can be calculated only after that value becomes available; turning a crossover into an order requires a separate trading rule. It is also distinct from the random-error structure of a time-series MA model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
