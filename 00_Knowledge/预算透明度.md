# 预算透明度是及时公开清晰、可靠、全面且可比较的预算与执行信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Budget transparency is the timely public disclosure of clear, reliable, comprehensive and comparable budget and execution information.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预算透明度让公众和监督者能够理解政府的资金计划、所依据的假设及实际执行，而不是只让报表“可以下载”。信息需要在相应决策之前及时提供，并在执行中和期末持续更新，清楚说明范围、单位、记账基础和修订。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Budget transparency enables the public and oversight bodies to understand fiscal plans, assumptions and implementation, rather than merely download reports. Information should be available before relevant decisions and updated during and after execution, with clear coverage, units, accounting basis and revisions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小可用对照是同一范围、同一基础的初始预算、调整预算与执行结果，以及差异解释。如果年度报告扩大了机构范围，就提供口径衔接，而不能直接把新增覆盖额解释为支出增长。具体预算比较复用[[预算支出与决算支出]]，范围变化复用[[财政合并口径]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful minimum compares the original budget, revised budget and outturn on a common basis, with explanations of differences. Expanded reporting coverage requires reconciliation before interpreting growth. See [[预算支出与决算支出|budget/outturn comparisons]] and [[财政合并口径|fiscal consolidation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[预算完整性]]回答信息是否覆盖了应关注的资金活动；透明度进一步要求这些信息可理解、可取得和可核对。公开预测误差或审计发现也有助于透明度，不能把“报告里没有坏消息”当作质量标准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[预算完整性|Comprehensiveness]] concerns coverage; transparency also requires accessibility, intelligibility and verifiability. Disclosing forecast errors and audit findings can improve transparency; the absence of adverse news is not a quality criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
