# 在单一外部性与通常的竞争内点条件下负外溢使活动过多而正外溢使活动不足
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With a single externality and standard interior competitive conditions, negative spillovers create excessive activity and positive spillovers insufficient activity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个数量结论适用于一个明确基准：活动只有所列的外部影响，其他市场按竞争条件运作，货币收益和成本可以一致加总，且边际曲线使私人和社会问题各有唯一内点解。**不能只看到“负外部性”四个字，就给任何现实市场判定产量过多。**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The quantity conclusion uses a specific benchmark: the stated spillover is the only distortion, other markets are competitive, monetary benefits and costs are comparable, and marginal curves yield unique interior choices. A negative externality alone does not diagnose excessive output in every real market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $B(q)$ 为扣除私人资源成本后的净私人收益，$D(q)$ 为外部损害，$B''<0$、$D'\ge0$、$D''\ge0$。私人选择满足 $B'(q_m)=0$，社会目标为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $B(q)$ be private benefit net of private resource costs and $D(q)$ external damage, with $B''<0$, $D'\ge0$ and $D''\ge0$. The private choice solves $B'(q_m)=0$, while the social objective is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$W(q)=B(q)-D(q),\qquad B'(q^*)=D'(q^*).$$

若 $D'(q^*)>0$，递减的 $B'$ 必须在更小的数量上才等于这个正值，所以 $q^*<q_m$。从 $q_m$ 减到 $q^*$，避免的损害大于牺牲的私人净收益，净改善为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $D'(q^*)>0$, decreasing $B'$ reaches that positive value at a smaller quantity, so $q^*<q_m$. The gain from reducing activity is avoided damage minus forgone net private benefit:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$W(q^*)-W(q_m)=\int_{q^*}^{q_m}[D'(q)-B'(q)]\,dq.$$

例：产品按固定价格100出售，私人生产成本为 $q^2/2$，于是 $B(q)=100q-q^2/2$；另有损害 $D(q)=q^2/2$，则 $q_m=100$、$q^*=50$，净改善为2500。物理产量下降，但扣除损害的净价值上升；它不是“社会总产出增加2500单位”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a fixed product price of 100 and private production cost $q^2/2$, net private benefit is $B(q)=100q-q^2/2$. With $D(q)=q^2/2$, $q_m=100$, $q^*=50$ and the gain is 2,500. Physical output falls while net value rises; 2,500 is not an increase in physical output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正外溢把社会目标改为 $B(q)+E(q)$。若 $E'>0$ 且总目标仍严格凹、解在内点，则社会条件 $B'(q^*)=-E'(q^*)<0$ 给出更高的 $q^*$。若另有垄断、税楔、容量限制或其他外部效应，应重新写出完整边际条件；偏离方向要由合起来的模型决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For positive spillovers, maximise $B(q)+E(q)$. With $E'>0$, strict concavity and an interior solution, $B'(q^*)=-E'(q^*)<0$ implies higher activity. Monopoly, taxes, capacity or additional spillovers require the full marginal condition to be rebuilt.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
