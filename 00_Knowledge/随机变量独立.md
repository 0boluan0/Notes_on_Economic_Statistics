# Independent random variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*随机变量独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two **random variables are independent** when information about one does not change the probability rules for the other. For example, after recording the first of two independent coin tosses, the second still has the same chance of heads. This is a statement about how all possible values are distributed together, rather than a judgement about one observed pair of numbers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个**随机变量独立**，意思是关于其中一个变量的信息，不会改变另一个变量的概率规则。例如，记录两次独立抛硬币中的第一次结果后，第二次出现正面的概率仍和原来一样。这个性质描述的是所有可能取值怎样共同分布，不能仅凭观察到的一对数字来判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[随机变量|random variable]] is a rule for recording an uncertain outcome as a number. A statement such as $X=1$ or $X\leq2$ selects a set of outcomes and therefore describes an event. Independence of variables applies [[事件独立|independence of events]] to every event described by $X$ and every event described by $Y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[随机变量]]是把不确定结果记成数字的规则。$X=1$ 或 $X\leq2$ 这样的陈述会选出一组基本结果，因此对应一个事件。变量独立，就是把[[事件独立]]的要求应用于由 $X$ 描述的每个事件，以及由 $Y$ 描述的每个事件。

## 直觉
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What “no extra information” means*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose someone tells us that $X$ belongs to a set $A$, for example $X>0$. Provided that event has positive probability, independence says that the chance of $Y$ belonging to any set $B$ remains unchanged. We do not need to learn the exact value of $X$: even partial information about it must leave these probabilities unchanged. The same requirement works in the other direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假如有人告诉我们 $X$ 落在集合 $A$ 里，例如 $X>0$。只要这个事件的概率为正，独立性就要求：$Y$ 落在任何集合 $B$ 里的概率仍不改变。不必知道 $X$ 的精确值；即使只知道关于它的部分信息，也不能改变这些概率。反过来，关于 $Y$ 的信息也一样。

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Two coin records*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] Checking a given model · 检查一个给定模型
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider two coin tosses with four equally likely outcomes: $HH,HT,TH,TT$, each of probability $1/4$. Here $H$ means heads and $T$ tails. Let $X$ record the first result and $Y$ the second, using $1$ for heads and $0$ for tails.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑一个两次抛硬币的模型：$HH,HT,TH,TT$ 四种结果各有 $1/4$ 的概率，其中 $H$ 表示正面，$T$ 表示反面。用 $X$ 记录第一次、$Y$ 记录第二次，正面记 $1$，反面记 $0$。
>
> | $X\backslash Y$ | $Y=0$ | $Y=1$ |
> |---|---|---|
> | $X=0$ | $1/4$ | $1/4$ |
> | $X=1$ | $1/4$ | $1/4$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each variable is $0$ or $1$ with probability $1/2$. Every pair of values has probability $1/4=(1/2)(1/2)$. Learning $X=1$ leaves the two equally likely possibilities $(1,0)$ and $(1,1)$, so the chance of $Y=1$ stays $1/2$. The same is true when $X=0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个变量取 $0$ 或 $1$ 的概率都为 $1/2$，每一对取值的概率都是 $1/4=(1/2)(1/2)$。知道 $X=1$ 后，剩下等可能的 $(1,0)$ 和 $(1,1)$，所以 $Y=1$ 的概率仍为 $1/2$；知道 $X=0$ 时也是如此。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because these variables have only two values, checking all four value pairs establishes their independence; sums of those cells also give the required product for larger value sets. The [[随机变量独立的分布判据|distribution criteria for independence]] explain how to carry out this check with a PMF, CDF, or density.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这两个变量都只有两个取值，因此检查全部四对取值就能确定它们独立；把相应格子相加，也能得到更大取值集合所需的乘积关系。[[随机变量独立的分布判据]]说明如何用 PMF、CDF 或密度完成这样的检查。

> [!abstract] The definition for real-valued variables · 实值随机变量的定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Random variables $X$ and $Y$ on the same probability space are independent if, for every pair of Borel sets $A,B\subseteq\mathbb R$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 同一概率空间上的实值随机变量 $X$、$Y$ 独立，是指对任意 Borel 集 $A,B\subseteq\mathbb R$，都有
>
> $$P(X\in A,\ Y\in B)=P(X\in A)P(Y\in B).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The comma means both events happen. Borel sets are the standard numerical sets, including points and intervals, used in the [[随机变量|definition of a real random variable]]. The left side is the actual joint probability; independence requires it to equal the product on the right for **every** choice of the two sets.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 逗号表示两个事件同时发生。Borel 集是[[随机变量|实随机变量定义]]中使用的标准数值集合，包括单点和区间。左边是实际联合概率；独立性要求：**每一种**集合选择都让它等于右边的概率乘积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $P(X\in A)>0$, dividing this equality by $P(X\in A)$ gives $P(Y\in B\mid X\in A)=P(Y\in B)$. This is the [[条件概率|conditional-probability]] version of the intuition. The product definition also covers zero-probability events, where this elementary division is unavailable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $P(X\in A)>0$ 时，把等式两边除以 $P(X\in A)$，就得到 $P(Y\in B\mid X\in A)=P(Y\in B)$。这就是前面直觉的[[条件概率]]形式。乘积定义还适用于零概率事件，而那里不能进行这种初等的除法。

> [!warning] One failed event pair is enough · 一组反例就能否定独立
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If we copy the first fair coin's result and set $Y=X$, then $P(X=1,Y=1)=1/2$, while $P(X=1)P(Y=1)=1/4$. The variables have matching individual distributions, but they fail the independence definition. Finding one pair of value sets that fails is enough; finding one pair that works is not enough to prove independence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果只抛一次公平硬币，再把第一次的结果复制成 $Y=X$，则 $P(X=1,Y=1)=1/2$，而 $P(X=1)P(Y=1)=1/4$。两个变量各自的分布一样，却不满足独立定义。找到一组失败的取值集合就能否定独立；仅找到一组成立的集合不能证明独立。
