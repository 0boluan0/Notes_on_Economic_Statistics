# 国内生产总值是在一定时期内一国或地区常住生产单位创造的最终产出价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gross domestic product measures the value of final output created by resident producers during a specified period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

国内生产总值（GDP）是**期间生产流量**，不是国家财富存量或股票市值。按生产法汇总各生产环节增加值，可避免把中间品重复计入；按支出法，则汇总本期国内最终产出的使用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
GDP is a **flow of production over a period**, not a stock of national wealth or equity market value. Summing value added avoids counting intermediate inputs twice; the expenditure approach records the uses of domestic final output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在本课简化口径下，$Y=C+I+G+X-M$：消费、实际投资、政府购买与净出口。$I$ 包含资本形成和存货变化，不是购买已有股票；转移支付本身不是政府购买。进口先可能计入 $C,I,G$，再扣回以排除国外生产，并非“进口一笔必让 GDP 下降一笔”的因果式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the course's simplified expenditure convention, $Y=C+I+G+X-M$: consumption, real investment, government purchases, and net exports. Investment includes capital formation and inventory changes, not purchases of existing shares. Transfers are not themselves government purchases. Imports are subtracted to remove foreign production already included in expenditure, not to assert a one-for-one causal reduction in GDP.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如农户生产并售出价值 80 的小麦，面包店用其生产最终售出价值 150 的面包：最终产出为 150，增加值也是 $80+(150-80)=150$，不能相加成 230。比较不同时期产出量，应转到[[实际GDP增长]]，而非直接比较现价金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If wheat worth 80 becomes bread sold for 150, final output is 150 and value added is $80+(150-80)=150$, not 230. Comparing production volumes over time requires [[实际GDP增长|real GDP growth]], rather than changes in current-price amounts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
