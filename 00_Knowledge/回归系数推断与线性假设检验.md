# 回归系数推断与线性假设检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Regression coefficient inference and linear hypothesis testing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 在样本会随机变化的前提下，量化回归系数的不确定性，并检验一个或一组线性限制。
> **具体锚点：** 教育系数为 0.08 不是完整结论；还要知道标准误、区间、抽样设计和效应单位。
> **核心难点：** p 值是假设成立时数据有多极端，不是原假设为真的概率；统计显著不等于实际重要。
> **为什么重要：** 它阻止我们把样本噪声、模型选择和实质效果混成一个星号。
> **继续：** 先从 [[#点估计为什么还不够]] 建立 sampling distribution，再分别读 [[#单个系数：t 检验与置信区间]] 和 [[#多个限制：F 与 Wald 联合检验]]。标准误依赖误差结构；怀疑异方差或相关时转到 [[异方差与自相关]]，数据驱动地挑过模型则进入 [[回归模型比较与选择]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Regression inference quantifies coefficient uncertainty when samples vary randomly and tests one or several linear restrictions.
> **Concrete anchor:** An education coefficient of 0.08 is not a complete result. Its standard error, interval, sampling design, and units of effect are also needed.
> **Central difficulty:** A p-value measures how extreme the data are under the null hypothesis; it is not the probability that the null is true. Statistical significance is not practical importance.
> **Why it matters:** Inference prevents sampling noise, model selection, and substantive effect size from being collapsed into a single significance star.
> **Continue with:** Begin with [[#点估计为什么还不够|why a point estimate is not enough]], then study [[#单个系数：t 检验与置信区间|single-coefficient inference]] and [[#多个限制：F 与 Wald 联合检验|joint linear restrictions]]. If the error variance or dependence structure is doubtful, continue to [[异方差与自相关|heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation]]. For data-driven model choice, use [[回归模型比较与选择|regression model comparison and selection]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 点估计为什么还不够
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why a point estimate is not enough*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在线性模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the linear model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y=X\beta+u,
\qquad
\hat\beta=(X'X)^{-1}X'y,
$$

$\hat\beta$ 是当前样本给出的数，$\beta$ 是想了解的 population parameter。若重新从同一 data-generating process 抽样，$\hat\beta$ 会变化；inference 研究的正是这种重复抽样变化，而不是给已经观测到的数字再加一个装饰。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\hat\beta$ is the number produced by the observed sample, whereas $\beta$ is the population parameter of interest. Repeated samples from the same data-generating process would produce different estimates. Inference studies this repeated-sampling variation rather than decorating an already observed number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $E(u\mid X)=0$、$\operatorname{Var}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2I$ 且 $X$ full column rank 的经典条件下，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the classical conditions $E(u\mid X)=0$, $\operatorname{Var}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2I$, and full column rank of $X$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(\hat\beta\mid X)=\beta,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta\mid X)=\sigma^2(X'X)^{-1}.
$$

未知的 $\sigma^2$ 通常用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The unknown $\sigma^2$ is commonly estimated by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
s^2=\frac{RSS}{n-k},
$$

其中 $k$ 是包括 intercept 在内的 estimated parameters 数。estimated covariance matrix 的第 $j$ 个 diagonal element 开平方，就是 $se(\hat\beta_j)$。因此 standard error 同时取决于三件事：noise 有多大、样本提供多少独立 variation、以及采用的 error covariance model 是否正确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $k$ is the number of estimated parameters, including the intercept. The square root of the $j$th diagonal element of the estimated covariance matrix is $se(\hat\beta_j)$. A standard error therefore depends jointly on the noise level, the independent variation supplied by the sample, and whether the chosen error-covariance model is appropriate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数估计会随重复抽样变化，标准误估计其标准差。经典同方差公式、heteroskedasticity-robust、cluster-robust 和 HAC 回答不同依赖结构；不能把“稳健”当作无条件更好，聚类层级必须对应共同冲击/处理分配层级。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Coefficient estimates vary across repeated samples, and the standard error estimates their standard deviation. Classical homoskedastic, heteroskedasticity-robust, cluster-robust, and HAC formulas address different dependence structures. “Robust” is not unconditionally better; the clustering level must match the level at which shocks are shared or treatment is assigned.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!important] 点估计与标准误可能分别失败
> 若 $E(u\mid X)\neq0$，问题是 estimand/identification，standard error 再精确也救不了 biased or inconsistent coefficient；若外生性仍成立但 $\operatorname{Var}(u\mid X)$ 设错，point estimate 可能仍可用，先坏掉的是 uncertainty estimate。前者见 [[内生性、识别、IV、2SLS 与 GMM]]，后者见 [[异方差与自相关]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $E(u\mid X)\neq0$, the failure concerns the estimand or identification; a precise standard error cannot rescue a biased or inconsistent coefficient. If exogeneity survives but $\operatorname{Var}(u\mid X)$ is misspecified, the point estimate may remain usable while its uncertainty estimate fails. See [[内生性、识别、IV、2SLS 与 GMM|endogeneity and identification]] for the first case and [[异方差与自相关|heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation]] for the second.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 单个系数：t 检验与置信区间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A single coefficient: t tests and confidence intervals*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验 $H_0:\beta_j=\beta_{j,0}$ 时，先把 estimate 与 null value 的距离换成 standard-error units：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To test $H_0:\beta_j=\beta_{j,0}$, first express the distance between the estimate and the null value in standard-error units:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
t=\frac{\hat\beta_j-\beta_{j,0}}{se(\hat\beta_j)}.
$$

在 normal, homoskedastic finite-sample model 下，它使用 $t_{n-k}$ reference distribution；使用 robust covariance 或更一般抽样结构时，结论通常来自 asymptotic approximation，small-sample degrees of freedom 和 cluster 数会变得重要。不能只抄一个 $t$ 临界值而不说明 standard error 是怎样得到的。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under a normal, homoskedastic finite-sample model, this statistic uses a $t_{n-k}$ reference distribution. With a robust covariance estimator or a more general sampling structure, validity is usually asymptotic, so small-sample degrees of freedom and the number of clusters become important. A critical value should not be copied without stating how the standard error was obtained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$1-\alpha$ two-sided confidence interval 通常写成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A two-sided $1-\alpha$ confidence interval is commonly written as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat\beta_j\pm c_{1-\alpha/2}\,se(\hat\beta_j),
$$

其中 critical value 必须匹配相同的 reference distribution。$t=(\hat\beta_j-\beta_{j,0})/se(\hat\beta_j)$。在相应小样本或渐近条件下，用参考分布计算 p 值。$1-\alpha$ 置信区间是重复抽样覆盖程序的性质；当前区间得到后，频率学派不把参数当随机变量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where the critical value must match the same reference distribution. Under the relevant finite-sample or asymptotic conditions, the statistic $t=(\hat\beta_j-\beta_{j,0})/se(\hat\beta_j)$ yields a p-value. A $1-\alpha$ confidence interval describes the repeated-sampling coverage of the procedure. Once the present interval is calculated, frequentist inference does not treat the parameter itself as random.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一个完整读法：教育系数 0.08
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A complete reading of an education coefficient of 0.08*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设结果变量是 $\log(wage)$，education coefficient 为 $0.08$，standard error 为 $0.03$。检验 $H_0:\beta_{educ}=0$ 得 $t=2.67$；用 1.96 作大样本近似，95% interval 是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the response is $\log(wage)$, the education coefficient is $0.08$, and its standard error is $0.03$. Testing $H_0:\beta_{educ}=0$ gives $t=2.67$. Using 1.96 as a large-sample approximation, the 95% interval is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
0.08\pm1.96(0.03)=[0.0212,0.1388].
$$

这说明在既定 specification、sampling assumptions 与 standard-error choice 下，数据与零效应不太相容；它并没有证明 education 因果提高工资，也没有说明“原假设只有某个概率为真”。若按 log-level model 精确换算，point estimate 对应约 $100(e^{0.08}-1)=8.3\%$ 的条件均值差，而不是机械写成绝对 0.08 个工资单位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conditional on the stated specification, sampling assumptions, and standard-error choice, the data are not very compatible with a zero coefficient. This does not prove that education causally raises wages, nor does it assign a probability to the null hypothesis. In a log-level model, the exact transformation of the point estimate is approximately $100(e^{0.08}-1)=8.3\%$, rather than an absolute difference of 0.08 wage units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 任意线性组合：不要孤立读取一个系数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Arbitrary linear combinations: do not read one coefficient in isolation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

许多真正关心的量不是单个 coefficient，而是 $a'\beta$。例如含有 `female`、`experience` 与 `female × experience` 时，10 年经验处的 group difference 是 $\beta_{female}+10\beta_{interaction}$。它的 variance 不是两个 variances 简单相加，而是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Many quantities of substantive interest are linear combinations $a'\beta$, not individual coefficients. With `female`, `experience`, and `female × experience`, for example, the group difference at ten years of experience is $\beta_{female}+10\beta_{interaction}$. Its variance is not the simple sum of two variances; it is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(a'\hat\beta)=a'\widehat V(\hat\beta)a.
$$

展开后包含 covariance。只看 interaction term 的 p 值，或者把两个 confidence intervals 分别相加，都会回答错问题。模型中类别与交互的具体解释见 [[虚拟变量与交互项#交互项如何改变斜率与组差]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The expansion contains covariance terms. Looking only at the interaction term's p-value, or adding two confidence intervals endpoint by endpoint, answers the wrong question. See [[虚拟变量与交互项#交互项如何改变斜率与组差|how interactions change slopes and group differences]] for the substantive interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 多个限制：F 与 Wald 联合检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Several restrictions: joint F and Wald tests*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $q$ 个 linear restrictions 写成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $q$ linear restrictions as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_0:R\beta=r.
$$

多个限制 $R\beta=r$ 应联合检验。逐个 t 检验不能保证整个假设的错误率，也不能利用系数协方差。有限样本同方差下 F 检验可比较受限与不受限残差平方和；稳健语境用相应 Wald 统计量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiple restrictions $R\beta=r$ should be tested jointly. Separate $t$ tests neither control the error rate for the composite hypothesis nor use covariance among the coefficient estimates. Under finite-sample homoskedastic conditions, an $F$ test can compare restricted and unrestricted residual sums of squares; robust settings require the corresponding Wald statistic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经典 nested-model $F$ statistic 是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The classical nested-model $F$ statistic is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
F=
\frac{(RSS_R-RSS_U)/q}{RSS_U/(n-k_U)},
$$

其中 restricted model 必须能由 unrestricted model 施加这些限制得到，且两个模型使用同一 response 和同一 observations。$RSS_R-RSS_U$ 衡量限制强迫模型放弃多少 fit；除以 $q$ 与 unrestricted error variance 后，才可与 $F_{q,n-k_U}$ 比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where the restricted model must be obtainable from the unrestricted model by imposing the stated restrictions, and both models must use the same response and observations. The difference $RSS_R-RSS_U$ measures how much fit the restrictions force the model to surrender. Scaling by $q$ and the unrestricted error variance produces a statistic compared with $F_{q,n-k_U}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用一般 covariance estimator 时，Wald statistic 可写为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a general covariance estimator, the Wald statistic can be written as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
W=(R\hat\beta-r)'
\left[R\widehat V(\hat\beta)R'\right]^{-1}
(R\hat\beta-r),
$$

并在相应条件下与 $\chi_q^2$ 或经过 degrees-of-freedom adjustment 的 $F$ reference 比较。只有一个 restriction 时，使用同一 covariance estimator 的 $F=t^2$；这也是检查软件输出是否一致的简单办法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
and, under the relevant conditions, compared with a $\chi_q^2$ reference or a degrees-of-freedom-adjusted $F$ reference. With one restriction and the same covariance estimator, $F=t^2$. This is a simple consistency check on software output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 联合显著不等于每个变量都重要
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Joint significance does not make every variable important*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若检验 $H_0:\beta_2=\beta_3=0$ 被拒绝，只能说这一组 restrictions 与数据不相容；可能是一个系数远离零，也可能是某个 linear combination 被精确识别。反过来，两个 individual tests 都未拒绝，也不保证 joint test 不拒绝，因为 covariance 会改变联合方向上的 uncertainty。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rejecting $H_0:\beta_2=\beta_3=0$ means only that this set of restrictions is incompatible with the data. One coefficient may be far from zero, or a particular linear combination may be precisely identified. Conversely, failure to reject both individual hypotheses does not guarantee failure of the joint test, because covariance changes uncertainty along joint directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 大样本推断、功效与模型选择后的不确定性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Large-sample inference, power, and uncertainty after model selection*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一致性和渐近正态让样本量大时使用正态近似，但有效样本数取决于聚类/时间依赖。弱识别、厚尾、极端杠杆和少量聚类可让常规近似很差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consistency and asymptotic normality justify normal approximations in large samples, but the effective sample size depends on clustering and temporal dependence. Weak identification, heavy tails, extreme leverage, and few clusters can make conventional approximations poor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

significance level $\alpha$ 控制 $H_0$ 成立时错误拒绝的概率；power 是某个 alternative 下正确拒绝的概率。未拒绝 $H_0$ 可能意味着 effect 接近零，也可能意味着 standard error 太大。判断“证据支持接近零”时，应预先给出 substantively meaningful margin，并检查 confidence interval 是否整体落在这个范围内，而不是把 $p>0.05$ 翻译成“没有效应”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The significance level $\alpha$ controls the probability of a false rejection when $H_0$ is true. Power is the probability of rejection under a specified alternative. Failure to reject may reflect an effect near zero or merely a large standard error. To argue that the evidence supports a negligible effect, specify a substantively meaningful margin and check whether the confidence interval lies inside it instead of translating $p>0.05$ into “no effect.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果先在许多 specifications 中寻找显著结果，再把获胜模型当作预先给定，nominal p-values 和 intervals 会忽略 selection uncertainty。预先设定、清楚区分 exploratory 与 confirmatory analysis、报告 specification sensitivity，或使用专门的 post-selection method，才与这类问题对应。模型选择指标本身见 [[回归模型比较与选择]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If many specifications are searched for significance and the winning model is then treated as prespecified, nominal p-values and intervals omit selection uncertainty. Prespecification, a clear separation between exploratory and confirmatory analysis, specification-sensitivity reporting, or an appropriate post-selection method addresses this problem. See [[回归模型比较与选择|regression model comparison and selection]] for the selection criteria themselves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 报告与诊断顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Reporting and diagnostic order*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同时给估计值、单位、标准误/区间、样本和控制、误差结构及主要规格。不要把不显著写成“没有效应”，而应看区间排除了哪些实质大小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Report the estimate and its units, standard error or interval, sample and controls, error structure, and main specification together. Do not translate insignificance into “no effect”; examine which substantively important effect sizes the interval rules out.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个可靠顺序是：先写 estimand 与 null restriction；确认 coefficient 或 linear combination 的单位；选择与 sampling/dependence structure 相符的 covariance estimator；计算 estimate、standard error、interval 与 test；检查 identification、specification 和 influential observations；最后同时报告 statistical 与 substantive interpretation。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A reliable order is to state the estimand and null restriction, determine the units of the coefficient or linear combination, select a covariance estimator matched to the sampling and dependence structure, calculate the estimate, standard error, interval, and test, inspect identification, specification, and influential observations, and finally report both statistical and substantive interpretations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 失败读法 | 为什么错 | 应怎样修复 |
|---|---|---|
| “$p=0.03$，所以 $H_0$ 为真的概率是 3%” | 频率学派 p 值以 $H_0$ 和模型条件为前提 | 写成“在这些条件下，至少同样极端结果的概率为 3%” |
| “不显著，所以没有效应” | test 可能 power 不足 | 读 confidence interval 与 meaningful effect range |
| 对多个 coefficients 分别做 t tests | 没有直接检验 joint restriction，且忽略 covariance | 写成 $R\beta=r$ 并做 F/Wald test |
| 换 robust SE 就解决内生性 | covariance 修正不改变 biased point estimate | 回到 identification 与 research design |
| 在 30 个模型中挑最显著的一个后照常报告 | 忽略 model-search uncertainty | 预先设定、验证或使用适合 selection 的推断 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Failed interpretation | Why it fails | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| “$p=0.03$, so the probability that $H_0$ is true is 3%” | A frequentist p-value conditions on $H_0$ and the model assumptions | Say that equally or more extreme results have probability 3% under those conditions |
| “It is insignificant, so there is no effect” | The test may have low power | Read the confidence interval against a meaningful effect range |
| Test several coefficients one at a time | This does not test the joint restriction and ignores covariance | Express $R\beta=r$ and use an F or Wald test |
| Replace the standard error with a robust one to solve endogeneity | A covariance correction does not change a biased point estimate | Return to identification and research design |
| Search thirty models, select the most significant one, and report ordinary inference | Model-search uncertainty is omitted | Prespecify, validate, or use inference designed for selection |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：standard error 与 residual standard deviation 分别描述什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: what do a coefficient standard error and a residual standard deviation describe?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> residual standard deviation 描述 observations 围绕 regression function 的 noise scale；coefficient standard error 描述在既定 sampling process 下 $\hat\beta_j$ 跨重复样本的变化尺度。后者同时取决于 noise、design matrix 和 covariance estimator。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The residual standard deviation describes the noise scale of observations around the regression function. A coefficient standard error describes the repeated-sampling variation of $\hat\beta_j$ under the stated sampling process. It depends on the noise, the design matrix, and the covariance estimator.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### p 值 0.03 表示原假设只有 3% 概率为真吗？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does a p-value of 0.03 mean that the null hypothesis has only a 3% probability of being true?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不是。它表示在原假设和模型条件成立时，观察到至少同样极端统计量的概率约为 3%。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. It means that, if the null hypothesis and the model conditions hold, the probability of observing a statistic at least as extreme is about 3%.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么多个系数共同为 0 要用联合检验？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why should the hypothesis that several coefficients are jointly zero be tested jointly?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 单独检验忽略多重性和系数间协方差；联合检验直接针对整个限制集合。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Separate tests ignore multiplicity and covariance among coefficients. A joint test targets the entire set of restrictions directly.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 系数不显著时应怎样判断证据？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How should the evidence be assessed when a coefficient is insignificant?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 查看置信区间是否仍包含实质上重要的正负效应，并评估功效、识别和标准误结构，而不是宣布零效应。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Check whether the confidence interval still contains substantively important positive or negative effects, and assess power, identification, and the standard-error structure instead of declaring a zero effect.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 只有一个 restriction 时，同一 covariance estimator 下 t test 与 F test 应是什么关系？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With one restriction and the same covariance estimator, how should a t test and an F test be related?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 应有 $F=t^2$。若不一致，先检查两处是否使用了不同 standard errors、degrees-of-freedom corrections、samples 或模型。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> They should satisfy $F=t^2$. If they do not, first check whether the two calculations used different standard errors, degrees-of-freedom corrections, samples, or models.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
