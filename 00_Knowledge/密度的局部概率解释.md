# 连续密度在一点的高度是一小段区间概率对长度的一阶比率

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*At a continuity point, density is the first-order probability per unit interval length*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若分布有密度且 $f_X$ 在 $x$ 连续，则
$$P(x-h/2<X\le x+h/2)=f_X(x)h+o(h),\quad h\downarrow0.$$
密度的单位是取值单位的倒数；高度不是单点概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a continuity point, probability in a short centred interval is density times length plus a smaller-order error. Density has inverse units and is not point probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对两个等宽小区间，分母位置密度为正时，概率比趋于密度比。密度高度本身也有尺度意义，并非只有比值才有意义。由于密度可在零测集上任意修改而不改变分布，任意指定单点的高度不能脱离连续性条件使用这个解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For equal small intervals, probability ratios approach density ratios when the denominator is positive. Absolute density has a scale-dependent meaning too. The continuity qualification matters because density values can be changed on null sets without changing the law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
