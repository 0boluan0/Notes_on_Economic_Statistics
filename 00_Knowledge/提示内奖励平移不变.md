# 在固定参考策略与 KL 系数的单步回答优化中，给同一提示的所有回答奖励加上同一个仅依赖提示的函数，不会改变 Bradley–Terry 偏好概率或 KL 正则最优策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In one-step response optimization with a fixed reference policy and KL coefficient, adding the same prompt-only function to every response reward changes neither Bradley–Terry preference probabilities nor the KL-regularized optimal policy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 不变的是什么
> 固定参考策略 $\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}$ 与 $\beta>0$，对每个提示 $x$ 考虑
> $$
> \max_\pi\;\mathbb E_{y\sim\pi(\cdot\mid x)}[r(x,y)]
> -\beta D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(\pi(\cdot\mid x)\|\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(\cdot\mid x)\right).
> $$
> 若 $r'(x,y)=r(x,y)+f(x)$，其中 $f(x)$ 对同一提示的所有回答都相同，那么 $r$ 与 $r'$ 给出同一个最优回答分布。它们属于同一个“只差提示常数”的奖励等价类。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fix a reference policy $\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}$ and $\beta>0$. In the one-step response objective that maximizes expected reward minus $\beta D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi\|\pi_{\mathrm{ref}})$ for each prompt, rewards $r'(x,y)=r(x,y)+f(x)$ produce the same optimal response distribution because $f(x)$ is shared by every response to that prompt. They belong to the same prompt-shift reward equivalence class.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么平移会消掉
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the shift cancels*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该目标的最优策略可写成
$$
\pi_r^*(y\mid x)
=\frac{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(y\mid x)\exp(r(x,y)/\beta)}
{Z_r(x)},
\qquad
Z_r(x)=\sum_y\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(y\mid x)\exp(r(x,y)/\beta).
$$
把奖励换成 $r+f(x)$ 后，分子和归一化常数都乘上 $\exp(f(x)/\beta)$；约掉这个共同因子，$\pi_{r+f}^*=\pi_r^*$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The optimizer has the form $\pi_r^*(y\mid x)\propto\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(y\mid x)\exp(r(x,y)/\beta)$. Replacing $r$ by $r+f(x)$ multiplies both numerator and normalizer by $\exp(f(x)/\beta)$, so the common factor cancels and the optimal policy is unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一平移也不会改变 [[Bradley–Terry模型|Bradley–Terry]] 的成对选择概率，因为
$$
[r(x,y_a)+f(x)]-[r(x,y_b)+f(x)]
=r(x,y_a)-r(x,y_b).
$$
因此，成对偏好与这类 KL 正则策略目标都不能识别每个提示的奖励绝对零点。DPO 的推导正是利用了这个等价类，而不是先找出唯一的“真实奖励函数”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same shift leaves Bradley–Terry choice probabilities unchanged because it cancels from every within-prompt score difference. Pairwise preferences and this KL-regularized policy objective therefore do not identify a prompt-specific absolute reward origin. The DPO derivation works with this equivalence class rather than recovering a unique true reward function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 结论只到这里
> 这不是“奖励怎样改都无所谓”。若改动依赖回答 $y$，排序和最优策略通常会变；若把奖励整体乘以常数而不同时调整 $\beta$，策略温度也会变；若参考策略、KL 方向、$\beta$ 或决策结构改变，也必须重新推导。本卡不把单步、整段回答的结果外推到任意多步 MDP。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is not invariance to arbitrary reward changes. A response-dependent change generally alters rankings and the optimizer. Scaling reward without correspondingly changing $\beta$ changes the policy temperature. A different reference, KL direction, coefficient, or decision structure requires a new derivation. The one-step whole-response result is not asserted for arbitrary multi-step MDPs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 对同一提示，三个回答的奖励是 $-1,0,2$。把它们都加上 $7$，同时保持参考策略和 $\beta$ 不变。Bradley–Terry 概率与 KL 正则最优策略会怎样？若只给第三个回答加 $7$ 呢？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For one prompt, three responses have rewards $-1,0,2$. Add $7$ to all three while keeping the reference and $\beta$ fixed. What happens to Bradley–Terry probabilities and the KL-regularized optimizer? What if only the third response receives $7$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 共同加 $7$ 时，所有差值不变，归一化中的共同因子也约掉，所以两者都不变。只改第三个回答是依赖 $y$ 的改动，会改变其差值，通常也会改变选择概率和最优策略。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Adding $7$ to all responses preserves every difference and cancels from the policy normalizer, so both objects are unchanged. Changing only the third response is response-dependent; it changes score differences and generally changes both choice probabilities and the optimal policy.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Rafailov et al. (2023), *Direct Preference Optimization*](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.18290#A5)：附录 A.5 把奖励等价关系写成 $r'(x,y)=r(x,y)+f(x)$，并证明等价奖励产生相同的偏好分布与相同的 KL 正则最优策略。
- [Rafailov et al. (2023), §3](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.18290#S3)：给出固定参考策略与 $\beta$ 下的闭式最优策略，以及该等价关系在 DPO 参数化中的作用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Rafailov et al. (2023), Appendix A.5, defines reward equivalence by $r'(x,y)=r(x,y)+f(x)$ and proves equality of the induced preference distribution and KL-regularized optimal policy.
- Rafailov et al. (2023), §3, gives the closed-form optimizer for a fixed reference policy and $\beta$ and uses the equivalence class in the DPO parameterization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
