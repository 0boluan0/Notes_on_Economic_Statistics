# 固定权重组合的期望收益是均值的线性组合而方差是协方差矩阵的二次型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A fixed-weight portfolio has a linear mean and a covariance-quadratic variance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要把持仓 $w$ 放到风险—收益图上，设同一计价单位、同一期间的收益向量 $R$ 有有限二阶矩，$\mu=E[R]$、$\Sigma=\operatorname{Cov}(R)$，并把期初已确定的 $w$ 视为常数。那么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To locate a portfolio on a risk–return diagram, use returns with a common unit and horizon, finite second moments, mean vector $\mu$, covariance matrix $\Sigma$, and fixed initial weights $w$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\mu_p=w^\top\mu,\qquad \sigma_p^2=w^\top\Sigma w,\qquad \sigma_p=\sqrt{w^\top\Sigma w}.$$

第一式直接用[[期望线性性]]；第二式把 $R_p-\mu_p=w^\top(R-\mu)$ 平方后取期望，就是统计课程的[[线性投影方差]]。这里应用同一个数学对象，不另设一套“金融协方差”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mean follows from [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]]. Squaring and averaging $w^\top(R-\mu)$ gives the existing [[线性投影方差|linear-projection variance identity]]; portfolio theory uses the same covariance object as statistics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两项资产时，二次型展开为

$$\sigma_p^2=w_A^2\sigma_A^2+w_B^2\sigma_B^2+2w_Aw_B\operatorname{Cov}(R_A,R_B).$$

设 $\mu=(0.08,0.12)^\top$，$\Sigma=\begin{pmatrix}0.04&0.015\\0.015&0.09\end{pmatrix}$。等权组合的期望收益为 $10\%$，方差为 $0.25(0.04)+0.25(0.09)+0.5(0.015)=0.04$，标准差为 $20\%$。不能把标准差直接平均成 $25\%$；缺掉的恰是[[两资产分散化|共同波动对组合风险的作用]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the stated means and covariance matrix, equal weights give a mean of $10\%$, variance $0.04$, and standard deviation $20\%$. Averaging individual standard deviations would instead give $25\%$ and would miss the [[两资产分散化|effect of dependence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若二阶矩不存在，方差目标本身就不适用，见[[矩存在性的使用边界]]。这些恒等式不要求联合正态；正态等更强条件是否需要，取决于随后采用的[[均值方差偏好边界|偏好或分布解释]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without finite second moments the variance objective is unavailable. The identities themselves need no joint normality; stronger assumptions concern the subsequent [[均值方差偏好边界|preference interpretation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
