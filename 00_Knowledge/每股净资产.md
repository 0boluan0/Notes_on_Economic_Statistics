# 每股净资产是归属于普通股股东的账面权益除以同一时点发行在外普通股数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Book value per share divides book equity attributable to ordinary shareholders by ordinary shares outstanding at the same date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每股净资产把时点上的普通股账面权益换算为每股金额。设该权益为 $B_c$、同刻发行在外普通股数为 $N>0$，则 $BVPS=B_c/N$。合并报表中的全部权益还可能含少数股东权益及非普通股权益，不能不经归属调整就全部除以母公司普通股数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BVPS expresses ordinary book equity at a date as a per-share amount: $BVPS=B_c/N$, where $N>0$ is ordinary shares outstanding at that date. Consolidated total equity can include non-controlling interests and non-ordinary equity; it cannot automatically be divided by the parent's ordinary shares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如总账面权益 1200 万元，其中少数股东权益 100 万元、归母权益中的优先股账面权益 200 万元；若其余全部归属于 300 万股同类普通股，则 $B_c=900$ 万元，BVPS 为 3 元／股。这里扣的是对应账面权益，不是优先股市场价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose total book equity is 12 million, including 1 million of non-controlling interests and 2 million of preference equity within parent equity. If the remainder belongs to 3 million identical ordinary shares, ordinary book equity is 9 million and BVPS is 3. The deduction is the corresponding book equity, not the market price of preference shares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它与[[基本每股收益]]不同：一个使用时点权益和同刻股数，另一个使用期间利润和加权股数。BVPS 可以为负；它既不是每股现金，也不是变现承诺，市场价格边界见[[账面净资产不是股价下限]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unlike [[基本每股收益|basic EPS]], BVPS uses a stock of equity and shares at a date rather than period profit and weighted shares. BVPS may be negative. It is neither cash per share nor a liquidation promise; see [[账面净资产不是股价下限|why book equity is not a share-price floor]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
