# 大模型分布式训练
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distributed Training for Large Models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当模型、激活和优化器状态无法高效放进一台设备时，把训练计算与状态拆到多台加速器，同时保持与目标优化过程等价或近似等价。
> **具体锚点：** data parallel 给各卡不同样本并同步梯度；tensor parallel 把一次大矩阵乘拆到多卡；pipeline parallel 把不同层放到不同阶段。
> **核心难点：** 节省某类显存通常会增加通信、重算或 pipeline 空泡；最优方案由模型形状、batch、网络拓扑和容错要求共同决定。
> **为什么重要：** 理论 FLOPs 不等于实际训练速度，扩更多卡甚至可能因通信与空泡而变慢。
> **继续：** 先用“拆数据、拆算子、拆层、拆状态”四个维度定位瓶颈；稀疏 expert 通信见 [[Mixture of Experts（MoE）]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Problem addressed:** When model weights, activations, and optimizer states cannot fit or run efficiently on one device, partition training computation and state across accelerators while preserving the intended optimization process exactly or approximately.
> **Concrete anchor:** Data parallelism gives devices different examples and synchronizes gradients; tensor parallelism splits one large matrix multiplication; pipeline parallelism places different layers in different stages.
> **Central difficulty:** Saving one type of memory usually adds communication, recomputation, or pipeline bubbles. The best design jointly depends on model shape, batch size, network topology, and fault-tolerance needs.
> **Why it matters:** Theoretical FLOPs are not achieved training speed, and adding devices can even slow training through communication and idle bubbles.
> **Continue with:** Locate the bottleneck using four dimensions—shard data, operators, layers, and state—then see [[Mixture of Experts（MoE）|Mixture of Experts]] for sparse expert communication.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!source] 本节依据
> - Megatron-LM、Megatron pipeline 研究与 ZeRO 原论文：核验 tensor/pipeline/data parallel、状态分片和通信—显存取舍。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> - The Megatron-LM, Megatron pipeline, and ZeRO papers verify tensor, pipeline, and data parallelism, state sharding, and communication–memory trade-offs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 四种基本切分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Four Basic Partitioning Dimensions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

data parallel 复制模型、分数据并同步梯度；tensor parallel 拆单个算子；pipeline parallel 拆层并处理 micro-batch；参数/梯度/优化器状态还可分片。实际方案同时受显存、网络带宽、延迟、利用率和容错约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Data parallelism replicates the model, partitions data, and synchronizes gradients. Tensor parallelism partitions individual operators. Pipeline parallelism partitions layers and processes micro-batches. Parameters, gradients, and optimizer states can also be sharded. Practical designs are jointly constrained by memory, network bandwidth, latency, utilization, and fault tolerance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这四种切分可以组合成多维并行。选择顺序通常从硬约束出发：模型是否能放入单卡、单层矩阵是否能放入单卡、全局 batch 是否足够填满 data-parallel replicas、跨节点网络是否承受高频 collective。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four dimensions can be combined into multidimensional parallelism. Selection usually begins with hard constraints: whether the model fits on one device, whether a single layer fits, whether the global batch can fill data-parallel replicas, and whether the inter-node network can sustain frequent collectives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Data parallel 与状态分片
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Data Parallelism and State Sharding*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

传统 data parallel 在每张卡保存完整参数、梯度和优化器状态，每张卡处理不同 micro-batch，再用 all-reduce 得到相同梯度。它实现简单且计算扩展良好，但模型状态在每卡重复，不能解决单卡显存不足。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Traditional data parallelism stores full parameters, gradients, and optimizer states on every device, processes different micro-batches, and uses all-reduce to obtain identical gradients. It is simple and compute-scalable but duplicates model state on every device, so it cannot solve per-device memory overflow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ZeRO/FSDP 逐步分片优化器状态、梯度和参数，在需要计算时 gather、之后再释放或 reduce-scatter。显存从复制转为按设备数分摊，但通信更细碎；参数 offload 到 CPU/NVMe 进一步省显存，却可能把瓶颈移到互连和存储。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ZeRO or FSDP progressively shards optimizer states, gradients, and parameters, gathering them for computation and releasing or reduce-scattering them afterward. Memory changes from replicated to distributed across devices, but communication becomes more granular. Offloading parameters to CPU or NVMe saves more accelerator memory while potentially moving the bottleneck to interconnects and storage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Tensor 与 pipeline parallel
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Tensor and Pipeline Parallelism*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

tensor parallel 把线性层的行或列分片，每层都要 collective，因此适合高带宽低延迟的设备组。pipeline parallel 把连续层分为 stage，micro-batch 像流水线经过各 stage；stage 负载不均或 micro-batch 太少会形成 bubble。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tensor parallelism shards rows or columns of linear layers and requires collectives at each layer, so it suits groups connected by high-bandwidth, low-latency links. Pipeline parallelism partitions consecutive layers into stages and sends micro-batches through them; imbalanced stages or too few micro-batches create bubbles.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

activation checkpointing 不保存所有中间激活，而在反向传播前重算部分前向过程，以额外 FLOPs 换显存。它不是并行维度，却常与上述策略一起决定可用 micro-batch 和通信隐藏程度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Activation checkpointing omits selected intermediate activations and recomputes their forward operations before backpropagation, trading extra FLOPs for memory. It is not a parallelism dimension, but it often determines feasible micro-batch size and how well communication can be hidden alongside the other strategies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：为什么八卡不是八倍快
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Why Eight Devices Are Not Eight Times Faster*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设单卡每步计算 800 ms。八路 data parallel 把本地计算降到约 100 ms，但梯度同步需 30 ms，输入等待 10 ms，则一步约 140 ms，速度提升约 $800/140=5.7$ 倍而非八倍。若全局 batch 不变导致每卡 micro-batch 太小，kernel 利用率下降后还会更低。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose one training step takes 800 ms on one device. Eight-way data parallelism reduces local compute to about 100 ms, but gradient synchronization takes 30 ms and input waiting 10 ms, yielding roughly 140 ms per step and a $800/140=5.7$-fold speedup rather than eightfold. Keeping global batch fixed may make per-device micro-batches too small and reduce kernel utilization further.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对四个 pipeline stage 和一个 micro-batch，执行近似串行，几乎没有流水线收益；增加 micro-batch 数可摊薄填充与排空时间，却增加 activation 存储、调度复杂度，并可能改变有效 batch。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With four pipeline stages and one micro-batch, execution is nearly serial and gains little from pipelining. More micro-batches amortize fill and drain time but increase activation storage and scheduling complexity and may alter effective batch size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- GPU 利用率低且通信时间高：按 collective、节点内/节点间链路和消息尺寸分解，重新布置高频 tensor parallel 组。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  GPU utilization is low and communication time high: decompose by collective, intra-node versus inter-node links, and message size, then remap high-frequency tensor-parallel groups.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 显存仍溢出：区分参数、优化器、梯度、激活和临时 buffer，再决定 state sharding、checkpointing 或更小 micro-batch。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Memory still overflows: separate parameters, optimizer state, gradients, activations, and temporary buffers before choosing state sharding, checkpointing, or a smaller micro-batch.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 扩卡后 tokens/s 不升：检查强扩展下每卡工作量、pipeline bubble、数据加载、同步尾延迟和网络拓扑。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Tokens per second do not rise after adding devices: inspect per-device work under strong scaling, pipeline bubbles, data loading, synchronization tail latency, and network topology.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 重启后结果漂移：核对优化器、scheduler、随机数、data sampler 和分片状态是否全部进入 checkpoint。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Results drift after restart: verify that optimizer, scheduler, random-number, data-sampler, and sharded states are all included in checkpoints.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum Self-Check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### data、tensor、pipeline parallel 分别拆什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does data, tensor, and pipeline parallelism partition?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> data parallel 拆样本并同步梯度；tensor parallel 拆单层算子；pipeline parallel 拆连续层并让 micro-batch 流过 stage。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Data parallelism partitions samples and synchronizes gradients; tensor parallelism partitions a layer's operators; pipeline parallelism partitions consecutive layers and moves micro-batches through stages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### ZeRO/FSDP 节省显存的代价是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is the cost of the memory saved by ZeRO or FSDP?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 状态不再每卡完整复制，但计算前后需要 gather、reduce-scatter 等更细粒度通信；offload 还增加主机或存储传输。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> State is no longer fully replicated on each device, but computation requires more granular collectives such as gather and reduce-scatter; offloading adds host or storage transfers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 pipeline micro-batch 太少会浪费设备？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why do too few pipeline micro-batches waste devices?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 流水线需要填充和排空；micro-batch 太少时，许多 stage 在开始与结束阶段等待，bubble 占总时间比例很高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A pipeline must fill and drain. With too few micro-batches, many stages wait near the beginning and end, so bubbles occupy a large fraction of total time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
