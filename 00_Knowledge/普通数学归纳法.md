# Ordinary mathematical induction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*普通数学归纳法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary induction turns one starting case and one reusable successor argument into a proof about every integer from that starting point onward. Imagine positions numbered $0,1,2,\ldots$: position $0$ is reached, and from any reached position the next one can be reached. Then any particular position, even a very distant one, is reached after finitely many steps. The mathematical proof must establish that the same successor argument works at **every** required position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
普通归纳把一个起始情形与一个可重复使用的相邻递推论证，组成对起点以后每个整数都成立的证明。想象位置编号为 $0,1,2,\ldots$：先到达 $0$，而且从任何已经到达的位置都能走到下一个位置。那么任意指定的位置，即使很远，也能经过有限步到达。数学证明必须保证：同一个递推论证在**每个**所需的位置都能使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## State the claim and its starting point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先说明要证明什么，从哪里开始*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $P(n)$ for the statement about index $n$. This is a [[命题与谓词|predicate]]: once an allowed integer is substituted for $n$, the statement has a truth value. Fix an integer starting index $n_0$. The intended conclusion concerns all **integers** $n\ge n_0$, not every real number above $n_0$. Here is the proof rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
用 $P(n)$ 表示关于下标 $n$ 的陈述。它是一个[[命题与谓词|谓词]]：代入论域中的具体整数后，陈述就具有真值。先固定一个整数起点 $n_0$；目标是证明所有**整数** $n\ge n_0$ 的情形，并不是证明大于等于 $n_0$ 的每个实数。证明规则如下。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove $P(n)$ for every $n\ge n_0$, first establish $P(n_0)$. Then, for an arbitrary $n\ge n_0$, explicitly assume $P(n)$ and derive $P(n+1)$. The base case supplies the starting point, and the inductive step propagates the property from each reached integer to its successor; neither part can be omitted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
要证明性质 $P(n)$ 对所有 $n\ge n_0$ 成立，先证明 $P(n_0)$，再对任意 $n\ge n_0$，在明确假设 $P(n)$ 的条件下推出 $P(n+1)$。基例提供起点，归纳步把已经到达的每个整数传递到下一个；两部分缺一不可。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **base case** proves the first statement directly. The **inductive step** proves the [[蕴含真值条件|implication]] $P(n)\Rightarrow P(n+1)$ for an arbitrary index in the stated range; $\Rightarrow$ means “if the statement on the left is true, then the statement on the right is true.” The temporary assumption $P(n)$ is the induction hypothesis. It is used to prove an implication, rather than announced as an already proved universal conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**基例**直接证明起始陈述。**归纳步**对所声明范围中的任意下标证明[[蕴含真值条件|蕴含]] $P(n)\Rightarrow P(n+1)$；$\Rightarrow$ 表示“若左侧为真，则右侧为真”。临时使用的 $P(n)$ 叫归纳假设；它用于证明条件命题，不是在未经证明时宣告所有情形都成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed target $m\ge n_0$, the base case and $m-n_0$ applications of the successor implication reach $P(m)$. No final largest integer is needed: $m$ was arbitrary. If the starting point is not zero, the change of index $k=n-n_0$ places the same argument on $k=0,1,2,\ldots$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对任意指定的 $m\ge n_0$，从基例出发，应用 $m-n_0$ 次相邻递推就能得到 $P(m)$。这里不需要一个最大的末尾整数，因为 $m$ 是任取的。起点不是零时，令 $k=n-n_0$，就把同一论证改写到 $k=0,1,2,\ldots$ 上。

## A complete sum proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把和式例子完整写出来*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, to prove $0+1+\cdots+n=n(n+1)/2$, verify the case $n=0$. In the inductive step, add $n+1$ to the sum in the hypothesis and obtain $(n+1)(n+2)/2$, exactly the statement $P(n+1)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如证明 $0+1+\cdots+n=n(n+1)/2$：基例 $n=0$ 成立；归纳步把假设中的和式加上 $n+1$，得到 $(n+1)(n+2)/2$，恰是 $P(n+1)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In detail, let $S_n=0+1+\cdots+n$ for a nonnegative integer $n$; thus $S_0=0$. Our predicate is $P(n):S_n=n(n+1)/2$. The base case is $0=0(0+1)/2$. Now fix an arbitrary integer $n\ge0$ and assume $P(n)$. Since the next sum has exactly one extra term,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
具体地，对非负整数 $n$，记 $S_n=0+1+\cdots+n$，所以 $S_0=0$。要证的谓词为 $P(n):S_n=n(n+1)/2$。基例是 $0=0(0+1)/2$。现在任取整数 $n\ge0$，假设 $P(n)$ 成立；下一个和式只多一项，因此

$$
\begin{aligned}
S_{n+1}&=S_n+(n+1)\\
&=\frac{n(n+1)}2+(n+1)\\
&=\frac{(n+1)(n+2)}2.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second line is the precise place where the induction hypothesis is used. The final line is the desired formula with $n+1$ in place of $n$. Because the calculation works for arbitrary $n\ge0$, induction proves the formula for every such integer. Checking $n=0,1,2,3$ alone would only check four instances; it would not establish the reusable step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
第二行就是使用归纳假设的位置；最后一行则把目标公式中的 $n$ 换成了 $n+1$。计算对任意 $n\ge0$ 都成立，因而归纳法给出全部非负整数情形。只代入 $n=0,1,2,3$，至多检查四个实例，并没有证明可重复使用的归纳步。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inductive step does not assume the result being proved. It temporarily assumes the current case $P(n)$ while the obligation remains to derive the next case $P(n+1)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
归纳步不是重新假设结论；只能暂时假设当前层 $P(n)$，目标仍是推出下一层 $P(n+1)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The base cases must connect to the declared domain and step pattern. Proving only $P(0)$ with a step $P(n)\Rightarrow P(n+2)$ reaches only the even indices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
基例必须与声明的论域和递推链相接。若只证 $P(0)$，却只建立 $P(n)\Rightarrow P(n+2)$，便只能覆盖偶数下标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For that two-step pattern, a second starting case $P(1)$ would start the odd chain. Likewise, if a successor argument is valid only from index $2$, a proof at index $1$ does not bridge the missing step. Check the smallest index at which every division, overlap or subobject used in the step is legitimate. For claims about all objects of size $n$, $P(n)$ must quantify over all those objects, not one chosen example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对这种跨两步的递推，另证 $P(1)$ 才能启动奇数链。同样，若相邻递推只从下标 $2$ 开始有效，仅证明下标 $1$ 并不能填补缺失的一步。归纳步中的除法、交叠或子对象，从哪个最小下标起才合法，都要检查。若结论涉及大小为 $n$ 的所有对象，$P(n)$ 就必须涵盖所有这些对象，不能只指某个选定实例。

> [!question]- Find the missing link · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A proof establishes $P(1)$ and shows that for every $n\ge2$, $P(n)$ implies $P(n+1)$. Does it prove $P(n)$ for all $n\ge1$?
>
> **Answer:** No. The step cannot be used until $n=2$, so $P(1)$ does not start the chain. One must also prove $P(2)$ or make the step valid for every $n\ge1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 某证明验证了 $P(1)$，并证明“对每个 $n\ge2$，若 $P(n)$ 则 $P(n+1)$”。它能推出所有 $n\ge1$ 的 $P(n)$ 吗？
>
> **答案：** 不能。递推从 $n=2$ 才可使用，$P(1)$ 无法启动它；还缺少 $P(2)$，或需要把归纳步改为对所有 $n\ge1$ 成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a step naturally needs several smaller indices rather than just its immediate predecessor, [[强数学归纳法|strong induction]] makes those assumptions explicit. For a process, [[不变量原理|the invariant principle]] applies the same successor reasoning to the number of transitions already taken. These are applications and variants of the proof pattern, not evidence that checking a finite sample is enough.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若一步自然地需要多个较小下标，而不只是紧邻的上一层，[[强数学归纳法]]能把这些假设明确列出。对于过程，[[不变量原理]]把同一相邻递推应用到已经发生的转移步数。这些是证明模式的应用或变体，并不意味着有限次抽查已经足够。
