# 支撑位是技术分析中被用于判断下跌可能停顿或回升的价格区域

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Support is a price zone used in technical analysis to assess where a decline might pause or reverse upward.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支撑位是观察者根据既有价格结构选出的参考区域，用于判断价格从上方向其接近时，下跌是否可能减缓、停止或转为回升。常见参照包括先前谷位和上升[[趋势线]]；把区域画成一条“支撑线”是图表表达，并不意味着价格必须精确停在一个点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Support is a reference zone selected from existing price structure to assess whether a decline approaching from above might slow, stop or reverse. Prior troughs and rising [[趋势线|trend lines]] are common references. Drawing a single line does not imply that price must stop at one exact point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如先前多次低点在99至101附近，可先把该区间作为候选支撑，再观察后续接近时的表现。若价格跌破预定边界，应按事先规则记录失效或[[支阻转换]]，而不是因为它“是支撑”就否认跌破。先看见反弹再回头挑出的支撑，与事前标定的支撑不是同一种证据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated prior lows around 99–101 may motivate a candidate support zone whose later tests can be observed. A breach must be recorded under predefined invalidation or [[支阻转换|role-reversal]] rules. A level selected only after observing a rebound is not equivalent to one specified beforehand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支撑是可能反应的位置，不是不可跌破的价格下界。与上涨可能停顿的[[阻力位]]相对，二者都需要固定价格口径、区域宽度及观察时点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Support is a possible reaction area, not an inviolable lower bound. It contrasts with [[阻力位|resistance]] to an advance; both require explicit price conventions, zone widths and observation times.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
