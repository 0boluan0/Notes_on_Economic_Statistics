# Lewis二元经济模型以资本主义部门从生计部门吸收劳动解释资本积累驱动的扩张

Lewis 二元经济模型研究这样的发展过程：资本主义部门将部分剩余用于扩大生产能力，在一定阶段能够按近乎不变的工资从生计部门获得额外劳动，从而扩大资本主义生产和就业。它把劳动供给条件、收入分配与资本积累联系起来，不只是描述农村人口进入城市。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Lewis dual-economy model studies expansion in which the capitalist sector reinvests part of its surplus and, during a particular phase, recruits additional labour from the subsistence sector at an approximately unchanged wage. It connects labour supply, income distribution and capital accumulation, rather than merely describing rural people moving to cities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Lewis 原文按生产组织划分部门：资本主义部门使用**可再生产的资本**，并向资本提供者支付使用报酬；其通常情形是资本所有者雇佣劳动。生计部门则是这一抽象模型中未使用这类资本的其余部分。“生计”不是产品毫无价值，也不是政府登记或劳动合同的一种统计状态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lewis distinguishes sectors by production organization. The capitalist sector uses reproducible capital and remunerates its providers, typically with capital owners hiring labour. The subsistence sector is the remainder not using such capital in this abstraction. “Subsistence” does not mean that its output has no value, nor does it define a registration or employment-contract status.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，按资本雇工经营的种植园可以属于资本主义部门，城市中的小规模生计活动则不因位于城市而自动属于它。现实农户也可能使用工具、资本或雇工；应用模型时要说明抽象如何对应实际单位，不能把农业／工业、农村／城市和[[非正规部门|正规性]]直接当成同一对部门。产业结构的统计描述另见[[结构转型]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A plantation organized around capital and hired labour can belong to the capitalist sector, while an urban subsistence activity does not become capitalist merely through its location. Real farmers may use tools, capital or hired workers, so applications must explain the mapping from the abstraction to actual units. Agriculture versus industry, rural versus urban, and [[非正规部门|formality]] are not interchangeable sector divisions. [[结构转型|Structural transformation]] addresses the statistical composition of industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模型的关键条件是[[无限劳动供给]]，而不是所有劳动者的边际产出都为零。课堂用[[剩余劳动力]]展示来源部门低产出损失的情形；工资基准还取决于[[边际产出与退出收入|劳动者在原部门可得的收入]]。增长过程则要检查[[利润再投与劳动吸收|剩余是否真的再投并增加劳动需求]]，不能由部门名称直接推出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The key condition is [[无限劳动供给|unlimited labour supply]], not zero marginal product for every worker. The classroom case of [[剩余劳动力|surplus labour]] illustrates a small output loss in the source sector, while the wage benchmark also depends on [[边际产出与退出收入|income available to workers outside capitalist employment]]. Expansion requires checking whether [[利润再投与劳动吸收|surplus is actually reinvested and raises labour demand]], not inferring growth from sector labels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
