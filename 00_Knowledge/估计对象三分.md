# Estimand 是研究问题定义的目标量，estimator 是对可能样本使用的计算规则，estimate 是该规则在当前样本上的数值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An estimand is the target quantity defined by the research question, an estimator is a rule applied to possible samples, and an estimate is that rule's value in the observed sample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 先固定“想知道什么”，再讨论“怎么算”
> **Estimand（目标量）**是研究问题指定的总体对象，例如某个目标总体的平均处理效应 $\tau=E[Y(1)-Y(0)]$。**Estimator（估计量）**是把可能样本映到数值的规则，
> $$
> \hat\tau=\delta(Z_1,\ldots,Z_n),
> $$
> 所以在重复抽样或随机分配下它是随机变量。**Estimate（估计值）**是把当前数据代入 $\delta$ 后得到的这一个数。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An **estimand** is the population object fixed by the research question, such as the average treatment effect $\tau=E[Y(1)-Y(0)]$ in a stated target population. An **estimator** is a rule $\hat\tau=\delta(Z_1,\ldots,Z_n)$ mapping every possible sample to a value, so it is a random variable under repeated sampling or random assignment. An **estimate** is the single numerical value produced when the observed data are inserted into that rule.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 混淆三者会悄悄换掉问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Confusing the three can silently change the question*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“教育的平均效果”还不是完整的 estimand。至少要说清楚：哪一种教育干预、观察什么结果、在什么时候观察、面向哪个总体，以及目标是全体的 ATE、实际接受干预者的 ATT，还是被工具变量改变处理状态者的 LATE。这里每换一次人群或效应口径，换的都是 estimand，而不只是计算方法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“The average effect of education” is not yet a complete estimand. At minimum, one must specify the intervention, outcome, horizon, target population, and whether the target is an ATE, an ATT among those who actually receive treatment, or a LATE among compliers whose treatment status is changed by the instrument. Changing the population or causal contrast changes the estimand, not merely the calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个 estimand 可以使用不同的 estimator，例如均值差、回归调整或加权估计。反过来，同一种 estimator 形式在不同数据生成过程中可能收敛到不同的统计对象；即使统计对象相同，能否把它解释为因果效应仍取决于识别假设。软件输出的 “coefficient = 0.12” 只是 estimate。它估计什么，要由目标定义、数据生成过程和识别论证共同决定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One estimand can be approached with different estimators, such as a difference in means, regression adjustment, or weighting. Conversely, the same estimator form may converge to different statistical objects under different data-generating processes; even when the statistical object is the same, its causal interpretation still depends on the identifying assumptions. A reported “coefficient = 0.12” is only an estimate. What it estimates is determined jointly by the target, the data-generating process, and the identification argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准误、偏差和一致性是 estimator 在重复样本中的性质，不是当前 estimate 本身的固定标签。一个 estimate 可以偶然接近真值，即使 estimator 有偏；也可以离真值很远，即使 estimator 无偏。[[识别与估计]] 还要进一步问：这个 estimand 是否已由总体分布与假设唯一确定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Standard error, bias, and consistency are repeated-sample properties of an estimator, not fixed properties of the realised estimate. A biased estimator can occasionally produce a close estimate, and an unbiased estimator can produce a distant one. [[识别与估计|Identification and estimation]] add a prior question: is the estimand uniquely determined by the population distribution and assumptions?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一篇论文先研究所有合格学生的培训 ATE，后来改为研究实际参加者的 ATT。即使两次最后恰好报告同一个数，estimand、estimator、estimate 中哪一层一定发生了变化？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A paper first studies the training ATE for all eligible students and later studies the ATT for actual participants. Even if both analyses happen to report the same number, which of the estimand, estimator, and estimate must have changed?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** Estimand 一定变了。Estimator 是否改变取决于计算规则是否改变；两次 estimate 则可能碰巧相同。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The estimand must have changed. Whether the estimator changed depends on whether the computational rule changed; the two realised estimates may happen to be equal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [UC Berkeley Stat 210A, “Estimation in statistical models”](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~wfithian/courses/stat210a/models.html)：直接核对 estimand、estimator 与 estimate 的对象层级及 estimator 的随机性。
- [Angrist, Imbens and Rubin (1996), “Identification of Causal Effects Using Instrumental Variables”](https://users.nber.org/~rdehejia/%21%40%24AEM/Topic%2004%20IV/readings/angrist_imbens_rubin_JASA_1996.pdf)：核对因果 estimand 必须说明目标总体与效应口径，尤其是 ATE、ITT 与 LATE 的区分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- UC Berkeley Stat 210A was checked directly for the distinction among an estimand, an estimator, and its realised estimate, including the estimator's randomness across possible samples.
- Angrist, Imbens, and Rubin (1996) was checked for the need to state the population and causal-effect target, especially the distinction among ATE, intention-to-treat effects, and LATE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
