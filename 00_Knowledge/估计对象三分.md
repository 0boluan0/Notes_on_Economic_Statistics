# Estimand, estimator and estimate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*估计目标、估计量与估计值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An estimation problem has three parts: **what we want to know**, **the rule we use to learn about it**, and **the answer that rule gives for the data we observed**. These are the estimand, estimator and estimate. A number in a software output tells us the third part; we still need the first two to understand it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估计问题有三层：**想知道什么**、**用什么规则去估计**、**这份数据代入规则后得到什么**。它们分别是估计目标 estimand、估计量 estimator 和估计值 estimate。软件输出的一个数只告诉我们第三层，还要知道前两层才能解释它。

> [!example] One target, one rule, one observed answer · 目标、规则、本次答案
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose we want the average number of books borrowed last month by students in a specified university. That [[总体与样本|population]] mean $\mu$ is the **estimand**. We decide to average the measurements from $n$ sampled students: the **estimator** is $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$. If the observed values are $2,5,8$, the **estimate** is $\bar x_3=5$ books.
>
> The result $5$ does not redefine the target to be $5$, nor does it prove that the population mean is $5$. Another sample might give a different answer while the target and calculation rule stay the same.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 想了解某所大学学生上月借书量的平均值，这个[[总体与样本|总体]]均值 $\mu$ 是**估计目标**。决定对抽中 $n$ 名学生的数值求平均，规则 $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_iX_i$ 是**估计量**。这次数据若为 $2,5,8$，算出的 $\bar x_3=5$ 本就是**估计值**。结果为 $5$ 并没有把目标重新定义为 $5$，也没有证明总体均值为 $5$；换样本后结果可变，目标与规则仍可相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The estimator varies before the data are observed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*观察前，规则的输出会随样本变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X$ denote the full random dataset, $x$ its observed value, and $\delta$ a specified [[统计量|statistical rule]] using no unknown input. If the target is $\tau$, then $T=\delta(X)$ is the estimator as a random quantity, and $t=\delta(x)$ is its realized estimate. For example, $\delta$ might compute a mean or a difference of group means. Authors sometimes use one symbol, such as $\hat\tau$, for both $T$ and $t$; ask whether the discussion ranges over possible samples or has already fixed the data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $X$ 为整个随机数据集，$x$ 为实际观察，$\delta$ 为不需要未知输入的[[统计量|计算规则]]。目标记为 $\tau$，则 $T=\delta(X)$ 是作为随机量的估计量，$t=\delta(x)$ 是本次估计值。$\delta$ 可以是平均数或两组均值之差。书中有时用同一个 $\hat\tau$ 同时指两者；判断上下文是在遍历可能样本，还是已经固定了数据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The estimator's [[抽样分布|sampling distribution]] describes how $T$ varies. Its [[估计量偏差|bias]] compares its expectation with the target, and its [[标准误含义|standard error]] describes its sampling spread. [[估计量一致性|Consistency]] concerns a sequence of rules as the sample size grows. These properties are evaluated under a sampling model; a single observed estimate cannot establish them. A biased rule can give a close answer once, and an unbiased rule can give a distant answer once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估计量的[[抽样分布]]描述 $T$ 怎样波动；[[估计量偏差|偏差]]比较其期望与目标，[[标准误含义|标准误]]衡量抽样分散程度，[[估计量一致性|一致性]]则研究样本量增长时的一串规则。这些性质需要在抽样模型下评价，单个估计值不能证明它们。有偏规则也可能偶然估得很近，无偏规则也可能某次估得很远。

## Changing the target can change the question
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 混淆三者会悄悄换掉问题

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The target must name the population, measurement and time period. “Average effect of training” also needs a specified intervention and comparison. In the [[潜在结果反事实|potential-outcome framework]], $Y(1)$ is a person's outcome under training and $Y(0)$ the same person's outcome without it. The [[平均处理效应|average treatment effect (ATE)]] averages $Y(1)-Y(0)$ over the target population; the [[已处理者平均处理效应|effect on the treated (ATT)]] averages over those who actually receive treatment. They specify different averaging groups, even when their numerical values happen to agree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

目标要说明总体、测量内容和时间。“培训的平均效果”还须指定培训方案与比较对象。在[[潜在结果反事实|潜在结果框架]]里，$Y(1)$ 是一个人接受培训时的结果，$Y(0)$ 是同一人不接受时的结果。[[平均处理效应|ATE]]在目标总体中平均 $Y(1)-Y(0)$，[[已处理者平均处理效应|ATT]]只在实际接受者中平均。两者指定的平均人群不同，即使数值可能恰好相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A further example is the [[IV异质性边界|local average treatment effect (LATE)]] for people whose participation is changed by a particular instrument, under its identification assumptions. This is another target group, not merely another name for the population ATE. Different valid instruments can select different groups. The detailed instrumental-variable conditions belong to that linked card; the point here is to state whose effect is being estimated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一个例子是[[IV异质性边界|局部平均处理效应 LATE]]：在相应识别假设下，针对参与状态被某个工具改变的人群。它规定了另一目标人群，不是全体 ATE 的另一个名字。不同有效工具可能推动不同人群；工具变量的详细条件见链接，这里要抓住“究竟在估计谁的效果”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One target may be approached with several estimators: for example, a difference in means, regression adjustment or weighting, when the corresponding assumptions justify that use. Conversely, a fixed computational rule may approach different quantities under different data-generating models. [[识别与估计|Identification]] asks whether the observable population distribution and assumptions uniquely determine the chosen target. Calling a regression coefficient an estimate of a causal effect does not supply that argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一目标可以尝试不同估计规则，例如均值差、回归调整或加权，但各自需要支持这种用途的条件。反过来，同一计算规则在不同数据生成模型下可能趋近不同对象。[[识别与估计|识别]]问的是：可观察总体分布与假设能否唯一确定所选目标。把回归系数叫作“因果效应估计值”，本身没有提供识别论证。

> [!question]- The same number can answer a different question · 相同数字也可能回答不同问题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A study changes its target from the training ATE to the ATT but again reports $0.12$. Which layer certainly changed? The **estimand's definition** changed. The estimator changed only if its rule changed; the two numerical estimates happen to agree. Equality of answers does not establish equality of questions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 研究从培训 ATE 改问 ATT，却仍报告 $0.12$。必然改变的是**估计目标的定义**；规则变了才说估计量变了，两次估计值则可能碰巧相同。答案相等不能证明问题相同。
