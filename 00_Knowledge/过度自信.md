# 过度自信是相对于可校准基准高估判断准确性或能力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Overconfidence overstates judgment accuracy or ability relative to a calibratable benchmark*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

过度自信是对自身能力、判断正确性或信息精度的评价高于相应基准。[[DHS私人信息模型|金融模型中的一种具体化]]是低估私人信号的噪声方差，即把自己的信息看得过于精确；它不是“表达得自信”的同义词，也不等于总是预测价格上涨。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Overconfidence overstates one's ability, correctness, or information precision relative to an appropriate benchmark. [[DHS私人信息模型|One financial-model implementation]] understates the noise variance of a private signal. It is not synonymous with confident presentation and need not mean always predicting rising prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如真实信号 $s=\theta+\varepsilon$ 的噪声方差为 $4$，决策者却认为方差为 $1$。在该已指定模型中，他把精度 $1/\operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon)$ 从真实的 $1/4$ 高估为 $1$。这说明的是精度误判，并没有改变信号的真实生成过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the actual noise variance in $s=\theta+\varepsilon$ is four, but the decision maker believes it is one. Within this specified model, precision $1/\operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon)$ is overstated from one quarter to one. The belief is mistaken; the actual signal-generating process is unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际识别需要比较事先记录的置信程度与相应准确率、误差分布或能力基准。一次盈利不证明能力高，一次亏损也不证明过度自信；高换手率还可能有流动性或信息等其他原因。[[有偏自我归因]]可以影响自信如何随结果变化，但它是另一项判断机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Empirical identification compares recorded confidence with corresponding accuracy, error distributions, or ability benchmarks. One gain does not establish high ability, and one loss does not establish overconfidence; high turnover may have liquidity or information explanations. [[有偏自我归因|Biased self-attribution]] can influence how confidence changes after outcomes, but is a distinct mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Daniel, Hirshleifer & Subrahmanyam (1998), pp. 1844–1846，PDF 6–8](https://www.kentdaniel.net/papers/published/jf98.pdf#page=6)：支持过度自信的能力与精度含义，并在 p. 1845 式 (1) 附近明确主观噪声方差低于真实值；已目视该页。方差 $4$ 与 $1$ 为自拟实例；单次结果不足以识别偏差是由定义所需比较基准推出的使用边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The source supports ability and precision interpretations and explicitly models understated noise variance around equation (1) on p. 1845, which was visually checked. Variances four and one are illustrative. The limitation of single-outcome evidence follows from the definition's need for a comparison benchmark.
