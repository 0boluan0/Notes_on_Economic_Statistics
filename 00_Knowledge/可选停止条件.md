<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Optional stopping conditions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*可选停止条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fair game remains fair for any predetermined number of rounds. Can we replace that number by “stop when a desired event happens”? **Optional stopping gives conditions under which the value at this random time still has the initial expectation.** The issue is not just whether the stopping rule avoids future information: we must also control the values that can accumulate before it stops.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公平游戏进行一个事先固定的轮数后，期望收益仍然不变。如果把轮数换成“等某个事件发生就停止”，结论还成立吗？**可选停止给出一组条件，保证随机停止时的值仍具有初始期望。**需要检查的不仅是规则是否偷看未来，还包括停止之前可能累积的数值是否受到足够控制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Throughout this card, $M_n$ is a real, discrete-time [[鞅定义|martingale]] for a [[滤过定义|filtration]] $\mathcal F_n$: each $M_n$ is observable by $n$, $E|M_n|<\infty$, and its next conditional mean is its current value. $S,T$ are [[停时定义|stopping times]] taking values in $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}\cup\{\infty\}$. The symbol $n\wedge T$ means $\min(n,T)$; [[停时信息集|$\mathcal F_S$]] is the information available when $S$ occurs. The qualification “a.s.” means outside an event of probability zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡始终讨论实值、离散时间[[鞅定义|鞅]] $M_n$ 及其[[滤过定义|滤过]] $\mathcal F_n$：每个 $M_n$ 到时刻 $n$ 可观测，$E|M_n|<\infty$，且下一步的条件均值等于当前值。$S,T$ 为取值于 $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}\cup\{\infty\}$ 的[[停时定义|停时]]。$n\wedge T$ 表示 $\min(n,T)$；[[停时信息集|$\mathcal F_S$]] 是 $S$ 发生时已有的信息；“a.s.”表示除一个概率为零的事件外成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A bounded stopping rule needs no limiting step
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 有界停止为什么简单

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bounded stopping time has one deterministic finite upper bound: $T\le N$ for all paths, or almost surely after choosing equivalent versions. It can still depend on observations. For example, stop at the first head or at toss $10$, whichever comes first. The [[停止过程保持鞅|stopped-process theorem]] gives $E[M_{n\wedge T}]=E[M_0]$ for every finite $n$. Taking $n=N$ already gives $E[M_T]=E[M_0]$. There is no exchange of an infinite limit with expectation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有界停时是指存在同一个确定的有限上界：所有路径都有 $T\le N$，或在选择等价版本后几乎必然如此。停止规则仍可依赖观察结果，例如首次出现正面就停止，但最迟第10次后停止。[[停止过程保持鞅|停止过程定理]]给出每个有限 $n$ 都有 $E[M_{n\wedge T}]=E[M_0]$。直接取 $n=N$ 就得到 $E[M_T]=E[M_0]$，不需要交换无穷极限与期望。

> [!important] Conditional optional sampling · 条件可选抽样
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $S\le T\le N$ are stopping times with a deterministic finite bound $N$, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若停时满足 $S\le T\le N$，其中 $N$ 是确定的有限上界，则
> $$E[M_T\mid\mathcal F_S]=M_S\quad\text{a.s.}$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus $E[M_T]=E[M_S]=E[M_0]$. This conditional statement says more than equality of two unconditional means: at the earlier stopping time, the later value is predicted by the value already held.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因而 $E[M_T]=E[M_S]=E[M_0]$。条件式比两个无条件期望相等更强：在较早停止时预测较晚的值，预测结果正是当时已有的值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see why, write $\Delta M_k=M_k-M_{k-1}$ and use [[事件指示变量|indicators]] $\mathbf1_A$. For every event $A\in\mathcal F_S$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为说明理由，记 $\Delta M_k=M_k-M_{k-1}$，并用 $\mathbf1_A$ 表示[[事件指示变量]]。对任意事件 $A\in\mathcal F_S$，

$$
\mathbf1_A(M_T-M_S)=\sum_{k=1}^{N}
\mathbf1_{A\cap\{S\le k-1\}\cap\{T\ge k\}}\Delta M_k.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficient of each difference is known at $k-1$: $A\cap\{S\le k-1\}\in\mathcal F_{k-1}$ by stopped information, and $\{T\ge k\}\in\mathcal F_{k-1}$ by the stopping rule. The product of this coefficient and the martingale difference therefore has expectation zero, using [[条件期望取出已知量|known factors]] and [[塔式法则|iterated expectations]]. Hence $E[\mathbf1_A M_T]=E[\mathbf1_A M_S]$ for every such $A$. Both stopped values are integrable finite selections, and $M_S$ is $\mathcal F_S$-measurable. This verifies the definition of [[条件期望|conditional expectation]]. The [[停时信息单调性|inclusion $\mathcal F_S\subseteq\mathcal F_T$]] alone would not prove this equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个增量的系数在 $k-1$ 时已知：停时信息保证 $A\cap\{S\le k-1\}\in\mathcal F_{k-1}$，停时规则保证 $\{T\ge k\}\in\mathcal F_{k-1}$。利用[[条件期望取出已知量|提出已知因子]]和[[塔式法则]]，该系数乘以鞅差的期望就是零。因此每个这样的 $A$ 都满足 $E[\mathbf1_A M_T]=E[\mathbf1_A M_S]$。两个停止值是有限个可积变量中的选择，因而可积；$M_S$ 又对 $\mathcal F_S$ 可测，这就验证了[[条件期望|条件期望的定义]]。仅有[[停时信息单调性|$\mathcal F_S\subseteq\mathcal F_T$]]，不能单独推出这个等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An unbounded stopping rule needs control of the limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 无界停止真正多出哪一步

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now suppose $T<\infty$ almost surely, but no fixed deadline bounds it. On every path where $T$ is finite, $M_{n\wedge T}$ eventually equals $M_T$. Thus $M_{n\wedge T}\to M_T$ almost surely. To preserve expectation, we need enough control to pass from the finite identities to the limit. Each of the following is a sufficient route, not a list of conditions that must all hold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在设 $T$ 几乎必然有限，但没有固定截止时间能限制它。对每条 $T$ 有限的路径，$M_{n\wedge T}$ 最终恰好等于 $M_T$，所以 $M_{n\wedge T}\to M_T$ 几乎必然成立。要保持期望，还需足够控制把有限截断的等式传到极限。下面每一种都是充分路线，不是要求同时满足的条件清单。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Uniform integrability of the stopped family.** If $\{M_{n\wedge T}:n\ge0\}$ is [[一致可积|uniformly integrable]], its absolute tail expectations become small uniformly in $n$. The [[一致可积与L1收敛|UI criterion for $L^1$ convergence]] then makes $M_T$ integrable and gives $E|M_{n\wedge T}-M_T|\to0$. Consequently,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**停止族一致可积。** 若 $\{M_{n\wedge T}:n\ge0\}$ [[一致可积]]，其绝对尾部期望便可对所有 $n$ 统一控制。由[[一致可积与L1收敛|UI与 $L^1$ 收敛判据]]，$M_T$ 可积且 $E|M_{n\wedge T}-M_T|\to0$，因此

$$E[M_T]=\lim_{n\to\infty}E[M_{n\wedge T}]=E[M_0].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**One integrable envelope.** It is enough that a nonnegative random variable $Y$ satisfies $E[Y]<\infty$ and $|M_{n\wedge T}|\le Y$ almost surely for every $n$. The [[控制收敛定理|dominated convergence theorem]] supplies the same limit passage. In particular, the stronger condition $|M_n|\le Y$ for all $n$ also suffices. The envelope may be random; it is its expectation that must be finite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**一个可积包络。** 若存在非负随机变量 $Y$，满足 $E[Y]<\infty$，且每个 $n$ 都有 $|M_{n\wedge T}|\le Y$ 几乎必然成立，也已足够。[[控制收敛定理|支配收敛定理]]保证同样的极限交换。特别地，更强的条件“全部 $|M_n|\le Y$”也足够。包络可以是随机的，但其期望必须有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Bounded increments and finite mean stopping time.** Suppose a deterministic constant $C<\infty$ bounds every increment, $|M_{k+1}-M_k|\le C$ almost surely for all $k$, and $E[T]<\infty$. The latter also guarantees $T<\infty$ almost surely. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**有界增量与有限平均停时。** 若存在确定常数 $C<\infty$，使每个 $k$ 都有 $|M_{k+1}-M_k|\le C$ 几乎必然成立，并且 $E[T]<\infty$，则后者也保证 $T$ 几乎必然有限，而且

$$|M_{n\wedge T}|\le |M_0|+C(n\wedge T)\le |M_0|+CT.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last expression is an integrable envelope, so the preceding route applies. A separate finite bound for each time step is not enough: one common $C$ is required. These routes concern the discrete-time setup stated above; they are not a continuous-time theorem obtained by replacing the index $n$ by a real time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一项是可积包络，因此适用上一条路线。每一步各有一个有限上界还不够，这里需要所有步骤共用的 $C$。以上路线都在开头的离散时间设定内，不能仅把 $n$ 改成实数时刻就当作连续时间定理。

> [!warning] A harmless-looking terminal value can hide large interim losses · 终值温和，途中仍可能失控
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Even $E[T]<\infty$ together with a bounded $M_T$ is insufficient. In [[无限翻倍破坏可选停止|the doubling strategy]], the first win has $E[T]=2$ and terminal gain $M_T=1$, but the initial value is $0$. Before a win, increasingly rare losses have increasing size. The stopped family fails UI, and there is no common increment bound. This is why terminal information cannot replace control of the whole stopped family.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 即使 $E[T]<\infty$ 且 $M_T$ 有界，也仍不够。在[[无限翻倍破坏可选停止|无限翻倍策略]]中，首次获胜满足 $E[T]=2$，终值收益 $M_T=1$，初始值却是 $0$。获胜前，越来越罕见的亏损越来越大；停止族不一致可积，增量也没有共同上界。因此，终值看似温和不能代替对整个停止族的控制。

> [!note]- A UI martingale may also be sampled at infinity · 整体一致可积时可允许无限停时
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the original martingale $(M_n)$ is UI, it has an almost-sure and $L^1$ limit $M_\infty$, and $M_n=E[M_\infty\mid\mathcal F_n]$. Define $M_U$ to use this limit on $\{U=\infty\}$ for any stopping time $U$. Then $M_U=E[M_\infty\mid\mathcal F_U]$. To check this, split a test event $A\in\mathcal F_U$ into the countably many branches $A\cap\{U=n\}$ and the branch $A\cap\{U=\infty\}$; each finite branch lies in $\mathcal F_n$. Conditional absolute-value bounds give $E|M_U|\le E|M_\infty|$, so the branch expectations can be summed. For $S\le T\le\infty$, information monotonicity and the tower property therefore give $E[M_T\mid\mathcal F_S]=M_S$ a.s. This version controls the entire original martingale and explicitly defines its value at infinity; it does not assign such a value to an arbitrary martingale.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若原鞅 $(M_n)$ 整体一致可积，它存在几乎必然且 $L^1$ 的极限 $M_\infty$，并有 $M_n=E[M_\infty\mid\mathcal F_n]$。对任意停时 $U$，在 $\{U=\infty\}$ 分支用该极限定义 $M_U$，便有 $M_U=E[M_\infty\mid\mathcal F_U]$。验证时，把检验事件 $A\in\mathcal F_U$ 分成可数个 $A\cap\{U=n\}$ 以及 $A\cap\{U=\infty\}$ 分支；每个有限分支属于 $\mathcal F_n$。条件绝对值估计给出 $E|M_U|\le E|M_\infty|$，因而可以相加这些分支的期望。对 $S\le T\le\infty$，再用停时信息单调性和塔式法则，得到 $E[M_T\mid\mathcal F_S]=M_S$ 几乎必然成立。此版本控制的是整个原鞅，并明确规定无穷处的取值，不是给任意鞅自动添加一个终值。

## A fair walk between two absorbing endpoints
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小应用：公平随机游走的吸收概率

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $N\ge1$ be an integer. Start a walk at $M_0=i\in\{0,\ldots,N\}$ and add independent $+1$ or $-1$ steps with probability $1/2$ each. This is a martingale for the information generated by its steps. Stop at the [[离散首次到达停时|first visit]] to $0$ or $N$, namely $T=\inf\{n\ge0:M_n\in\{0,N\}\}$. Until then, the walk remains between the endpoints, so $0\le M_{n\wedge T}\le N$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $N\ge1$ 为整数。从 $M_0=i\in\{0,\ldots,N\}$ 出发，每步独立地以各 $1/2$ 的概率加 $1$ 或减 $1$。相对于各步生成的信息，它是鞅。取[[离散首次到达停时|首次到达]] $0$ 或 $N$ 的时刻 $T=\inf\{n\ge0:M_n\in\{0,N\}\}$。停止前路径处于两个端点之间，因此 $0\le M_{n\wedge T}\le N$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We also need to justify that it stops. From any interior point, the next $N$ positive steps would hit $N$ within that block, an event of probability $2^{-N}$. Independent future steps give $\Pr(T>kN)\le(1-2^{-N})^k\to0$, so $T<\infty$ almost surely. If the starting point is already an endpoint, $T=0$. The constant envelope $N$ now permits optional stopping. Writing $p=\Pr(M_T=N)$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要说明确实会停止。从任一内部点出发，只要之后连续 $N$ 步都向上，就会在这一段内到达 $N$，该事件的概率为 $2^{-N}$。未来步骤独立，故 $\Pr(T>kN)\le(1-2^{-N})^k\to0$，于是 $T$ 几乎必然有限。若一开始就在端点，则 $T=0$。现在常数包络 $N$ 使可选停止适用。记 $p=\Pr(M_T=N)$，则

$$i=E[M_0]=E[M_T]=Np+0(1-p),\qquad p=\frac{i}{N}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, starting at $i=2$ between $0$ and $5$ gives probability $2/5$ of reaching $5$ first. This is a statement about eventual exit, not about which endpoint is reached by a predetermined deadline. The same probability can be checked with [[命中概率第一步方程|first-step hitting equations]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如从 $0$ 与 $5$ 之间的 $i=2$ 出发，先到 $5$ 的概率是 $2/5$。这描述最终从哪个端点退出，不是某个固定截止时间之前到达哪个端点。同一概率也可用[[命中概率第一步方程]]核对。
