# Zero gradient and tangent-space ambiguity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零梯度不定切空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a differentiable function, a zero gradient means that its first-order linear approximation detects no change in any direction. It does **not** tell us the shape of a level set. The same zero gradient can occur where the level set is one isolated point, two crossing lines, or a perfectly smooth line. To distinguish these, we must examine the set itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对可微函数而言，梯度为零表示其一阶线性近似在任何方向上都没有检测到变化。它**并没有**告诉我们水平集的形状：同样的零梯度，可以对应一个孤立点、两条交叉直线，也可以对应一条完全光滑的直线。要区分它们，必须查看集合本身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f:U\to\mathbb R$ be [[全微分|differentiable]] at $a$, with $U$ an [[开集|open subset]] of real $\mathbb R^n$ and finite integer $n\ge2$. Put $c=f(a)$ and $L_c=\{x\in U:f(x)=c\}$. The gradient $\nabla f(a)$ is the column of partial derivatives; $T$ below means [[矩阵转置|transpose]], so $\nabla f(a)^Tv$ is the standard [[向量点积|dot product]] with a velocity $v$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $U$ 是实坐标空间 $\mathbb R^n$ 的[[开集]]，$n\ge2$ 为有限整数，$f:U\to\mathbb R$ 在 $a$ [[全微分|可微]]。记 $c=f(a)$，$L_c=\{x\in U:f(x)=c\}$。梯度 $\nabla f(a)$ 是偏导数组成的列；下式中的 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，所以 $\nabla f(a)^Tv$ 是它与速度 $v$ 的标准[[向量点积|点积]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As shown in [[梯度与水平集|gradients and level sets]], any curve $\gamma$ in $L_c$ with $\gamma(0)=a$, differentiable at $0$, has velocity $v=\gamma'(0)$ satisfying
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正如[[梯度与水平集]]所证明的，任何留在 $L_c$ 内、经过 $\gamma(0)=a$ 且在 $0$ 可微的曲线，其速度 $v=\gamma'(0)$ 都满足

$$Df(a)v=\nabla f(a)^Tv=0.$$

> [!warning] A necessary condition can lose all information · 必要条件可能失去全部筛选能力
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $\nabla f(a)=0$, the displayed equation becomes $0=0$ for every $v\in\mathbb R^n$. Its solution space is all of $\mathbb R^n$, but that does not prove that every such velocity is attainable along the level set. Nor does it prove that the level set has no tangent space.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $\nabla f(a)=0$，上式对每个 $v\in\mathbb R^n$ 都化为 $0=0$。方程的解空间是整个 $\mathbb R^n$，但这不能证明每个速度都能沿水平集实现，也不能证明水平集没有切空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One point: every admissible velocity is zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小反例：方程给出全空间，水平集却只有一点

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $f(x,y)=x^2+y^2$ and $a=(0,0)$. Its gradient is $(2x,2y)^T$, so $\nabla f(a)=0$. Nevertheless, the equation $x^2+y^2=0$ forces both real coordinates to be zero:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $f(x,y)=x^2+y^2$，$a=(0,0)$。梯度为 $(2x,2y)^T$，所以 $\nabla f(a)=0$。但实数平方都非负，$x^2+y^2=0$ 迫使两个坐标同时为零：

$$L_0=\{(0,0)\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every curve staying in this set is constant, hence has velocity zero. Viewed as a zero-dimensional smooth set, its tangent space is $\{0\}$. The kernel of $Df(a)$ is instead $\mathbb R^2$. It has included directions that the actual set cannot support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

留在这个集合里的任何曲线都是常曲线，因此速度只能为零。把它看成零维光滑集合时，其切空间为 $\{0\}$；导数 $Df(a)$ 的核却是 $\mathbb R^2$，包含了实际集合根本无法支持的方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two crossing lines: the velocities do not form one tangent line
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两条交叉直线：速度不组成单一切线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now take $g(x,y)=x^2-y^2$. Again $\nabla g(0,0)=0$, but its zero level set is the union of $y=x$ and $y=-x$. Velocities along these two branches include $(1,1)^T$ and $(1,-1)^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再取 $g(x,y)=x^2-y^2$。仍有 $\nabla g(0,0)=0$，但零水平集是 $y=x$ 与 $y=-x$ 的并集。沿这两个分支的速度分别可以取 $(1,1)^T$ 和 $(1,-1)^T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are all the possibilities, up to arbitrary real speeds on the two lines. Indeed, if $\gamma(t)=(x(t),y(t))$ stays in the set and $\gamma(0)=0$, then for $t\ne0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

除去沿这两条直线选择任意实数速度外，没有其他可能。因为若 $\gamma(t)=(x(t),y(t))$ 留在集合内且 $\gamma(0)=0$，则当 $t\ne0$ 时

$$\left(\frac{x(t)}t\right)^2-\left(\frac{y(t)}t\right)^2=0.
\qquad\xrightarrow{t\to0}\qquad v_1^2-v_2^2=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, every $v$ satisfying $v_2=\pm v_1$ is realized by the straight curve $\gamma(t)=tv$. But the sum of the two displayed velocities is $(2,0)^T$, which is not on either line. The attainable velocities therefore do not form a vector space. At this crossing there is no single tangent line of a regular curve representing the whole level set; each branch has its own tangent line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，每个满足 $v_2=\pm v_1$ 的 $v$ 都能由直线曲线 $\gamma(t)=tv$ 实现。但前面两个速度的和是 $(2,0)^T$，不在任一分支上。因此可实现的速度并不构成向量空间。在这个交叉点，没有一条正则曲线的单一切线能够代表整个水平集；每个分支各有自己的切线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A smooth line can also have a zero defining gradient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*光滑直线也可能由零梯度的函数定义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $h(x,y)=y^3$. At the origin its gradient $(0,3y^2)^T$ vanishes, yet $h=0$ is simply the line $y=0$. All curves in that line have velocities $(v_1,0)^T$, and every such velocity is attained by $\gamma(t)=(tv_1,0)$. Its tangent space is precisely the horizontal axis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $h(x,y)=y^3$。原点处的梯度 $(0,3y^2)^T$ 为零，但 $h=0$ 就是直线 $y=0$。该直线内所有曲线的速度都形如 $(v_1,0)^T$，而每个这样的速度都可由 $\gamma(t)=(tv_1,0)$ 实现。它的切空间恰好是水平轴。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same set is the zero level set of $k(x,y)=y$. This time $\nabla k=(0,1)^T\ne0$, and the regular formula correctly gives $v_2=0$. Thus a zero gradient can be a limitation of the chosen **defining function**, without being a geometric singularity of its level set. Even the kernel can happen to be correct: for the constant function $f\equiv0$ on $\mathbb R^2$, the level set is the whole plane and every velocity is attainable. The zero gradient alone does not distinguish any of these cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个集合也可以写成 $k(x,y)=y$ 的零水平集。这次 $\nabla k=(0,1)^T\ne0$，正则公式正确给出 $v_2=0$。可见零梯度有时只是所选**定义函数**的局限，并不表示水平集自身有几何奇点。核甚至可能碰巧就是正确答案：若 $f$ 在 $\mathbb R^2$ 上恒为零，水平集是整个平面，每个速度都可实现。单凭零梯度无法区分这些情况。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use the theorem in its stated direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 正确的使用方式

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f$ is $C^1$ near $a$ and $\nabla f(a)\ne0$, the [[隐函数定理|implicit function theorem]] and [[梯度与水平集|regular-level-set result]] guarantee a local graph and identify its tangent space with $\ker Df(a)$. These are sufficient hypotheses, not necessary conditions for a set to have a tangent space. When they fail, analyze the actual local equation or a suitable alternative defining function; higher-order terms may help, but the first-order identity alone is no conclusion about the shape.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 在 $a$ 附近为 $C^1$ 且 $\nabla f(a)\ne0$，[[隐函数定理]]与[[梯度与水平集|正则水平集结论]]保证局部图形，并把切空间确定为 $\ker Df(a)$。这些是充分假设，不是集合有切空间的必要条件。假设不成立时，应分析实际的局部方程，或选择合适的另一种定义函数；高阶项有时能帮助分析，但单独的一阶恒等式并未给出形状结论。

> [!question]- Same derivative, different geometry · 同一导数，不同几何
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At the origin, what distinguishes $x^2+y^2=0$, $x^2-y^2=0$, and $y^3=0$? Their gradients are all zero. Their attainable velocities are respectively $\{0\}$, the union of two crossing lines, and one line. Only examining the level sets reveals that difference.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在原点处，$x^2+y^2=0$、$x^2-y^2=0$ 与 $y^3=0$ 有何区别？三者梯度都为零，但可实现的速度分别是 $\{0\}$、两条交叉直线的并集，以及一条直线。查看水平集本身才能得到这一区别。
