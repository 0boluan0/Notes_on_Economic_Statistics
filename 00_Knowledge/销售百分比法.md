# 销售百分比法用稳定的项目收入比例预测经营资产负债和融资需求
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The percentage-of-sales method forecasts operating balance-sheet items and financing needs using stable item-to-sales ratios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果增加销售通常需要相应增加应收账款、存货和产能，也会自然增加应付账款，就可以先用这些项目占收入的比例估计扩张需要的资金。**只对有理由随销售变化的项目应用比例**；债务与权益如何补足缺口，是后续融资决策。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If higher sales require more receivables, inventory, and capacity while generating more supplier credit, item-to-sales ratios can provide a first estimate of expansion funding. Apply ratios only where proportionality is defensible. How debt and equity close the remaining gap is a separate financing decision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设基期收入为 $S_0$，预测收入为 $S_1$，经营资产、经营负债的收入比分别为 $a$、$l$。两者保持不变时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let sales be $S_0$ initially and $S_1$ in the forecast. With constant operating-asset and operating-liability ratios $a$ and $l$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$OA_1=aS_1,\quad OL_1=lS_1,\quad \Delta NOA=(a-l)(S_1-S_0).$$

例如收入从 1,000 增至 1,200，经营资产为收入的 60%，经营负债为 10%，净经营资产需增加 $(0.6-0.1)\times200=100$。若预测净利率 5%、利润全部留存、没有可动用金融资产，新增留存为 60，[[外部融资需求]]为 40。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If sales rise from 1,000 to 1,200, operating assets equal 60% of sales and operating liabilities 10%, net operating assets increase by 100. With a 5% forecast net margin, full retention, and no available financial assets, retained income is 60 and [[外部融资需求|external financing needed]] is 40.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比例只是模型假设。设备有闲置产能时，销售增长未必立即增加固定资产；满负荷后的整批扩建则可能跳跃增加投资。客户付款变慢会提高应收收入比，供应商缩短账期会降低经营负债收入比。发生这些变化时，应改用相应业务驱动，而不是把旧比例当作会计恒等式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Proportionality is an assumption. Spare capacity may absorb sales growth without new fixed assets; expansion at full capacity can require lumpy investment. Slower collection raises receivables relative to sales, while shorter supplier terms reduce spontaneous financing. Revise the business drivers instead of treating historical ratios as accounting identities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净利率还必须与借款后的利息一致。若把它先固定，就应明确这是已包含新增利息的预计利润率；否则需解[[融资利息循环]]。保留足够计算精度，最后才舍入；逐项舍入的合计可能与先合计再舍入不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The margin must also be consistent with interest after borrowing. A fixed margin is assumed to include that interest; otherwise solve [[融资利息循环|the financing feedback]]. Retain precision until the final presentation, since sums of individually rounded items can differ from a rounded aggregate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
