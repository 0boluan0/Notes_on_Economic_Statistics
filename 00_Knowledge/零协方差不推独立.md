# Zero covariance does not imply independence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*零协方差不推出独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two variables can have **zero covariance** even when one tells us exactly how to calculate the other. [[协方差|Covariance]] checks one average: the product of deviations from the two means. [[随机变量独立|Independence]] is a much stronger requirement about all their probabilities. A positive contribution and a negative contribution can cancel in that one average while substantial dependence remains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使一个变量能让我们精确算出另一个，它们的**协方差仍可能为零**。[[协方差]]检查的是一个平均值：两个变量相对各自均值的偏差乘积；[[随机变量独立|独立]]则对全部概率关系提出更强的要求。正负贡献可以在这一个平均值中抵消，依赖关系却仍存在。

> [!example] A number and its square · 一个数及其平方
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X$ take $-1,0,1$ with equal probability, and define $Y=X^2$. These are all the possible pairs:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $X$ 等概率取 $-1,0,1$，并定义 $Y=X^2$。全部可能的取值组合为
>
> | $X$ | $Y=X^2$ | Probability / 概率 | $XY=X^3$ |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | $-1$ | $1$ | $1/3$ | $-1$ |
> | $0$ | $0$ | $1/3$ | $0$ |
> | $1$ | $1$ | $1/3$ | $1$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Using [[期望|probability-weighted averages]],
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 按[[期望|概率加权平均]]，
>
> $$E[X]=0,\qquad E[Y]=\frac23,\qquad E[XY]=0.$$
> $$\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=E[XY]-E[X]E[Y]=0-0\cdot\frac23=0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The $-1$ and $1$ contributions to $E[XY]$ cancel. Directly in the centred-product definition, the contributions are $-1/3,0,1/3$ before probability weighting, and cancel too.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $E[XY]$ 中的 $-1$ 与 $1$ 贡献抵消。直接看中心化乘积定义，三个取值组合的偏差乘积依次为 $-1/3,0,1/3$，按概率加权后也抵消。

## Checking dependence with a probability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*用具体概率检查依赖*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before observing $X$, the chance of $Y=0$ is $1/3$. After observing $X=0$, the chance is $1$, because squaring zero gives zero. Thus the [[条件概率|conditional probability]] has changed:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

观察 $X$ 之前，$Y=0$ 的概率是 $1/3$。知道 $X=0$ 之后，这个概率变成 $1$，因为零的平方必为零。因此[[条件概率]]发生了变化：

$$P(Y=0\mid X=0)=1\ne\frac13=P(Y=0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalently, the [[随机变量独立的分布判据|joint-probability product criterion]] fails: $P(X=0,Y=0)=1/3$, but $P(X=0)P(Y=0)=1/9$. This one failure proves dependence. It also makes the practical limitation clear: despite zero covariance, observing $X$ determines $Y$ completely in this example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等价地，[[随机变量独立的分布判据|联合概率的乘积判据]]不成立：$P(X=0,Y=0)=1/3$，而 $P(X=0)P(Y=0)=1/9$。只要这一处失败，就能证明不独立。实际含义也很清楚：在这个例子中，尽管协方差为零，观察 $X$ 仍能完全确定 $Y$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both variances are positive and finite: $\operatorname{Var}(X)=2/3$ and $\operatorname{Var}(Y)=2/9$. Hence the [[相关系数|Pearson correlation]] is also well defined and zero. This is a counterexample to “zero correlation means no dependence” as well as to the covariance version.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个方差都有限且为正：$\operatorname{Var}(X)=2/3$、$\operatorname{Var}(Y)=2/9$。因此 [[相关系数|Pearson 相关系数]]也有定义并等于零。这个例子既否定“零协方差就独立”，也否定“零相关就没有依赖”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The implication that does hold
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*哪一个方向确实成立*

> [!abstract] Independence implies zero covariance under the stated moments · 有相应矩条件时，独立推出零协方差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $X$ and $Y$ are independent and have finite second moments, then $E[XY]=E[X]E[Y]$, so $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0$. The square example shows why we cannot reverse this implication. Finite second moments ensure the covariance exists; independence alone does not manufacture missing moments.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $X$、$Y$ 独立且二阶矩有限，则 $E[XY]=E[X]E[Y]$，因此 $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0$。平方反例说明不能反推。有限二阶矩保证协方差存在；独立本身不能让原本不存在的矩凭空存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Additional distributional assumptions can change the reverse direction. For a **jointly Gaussian** pair, zero covariance does imply independence; the [[联合高斯独立判据|joint Gaussian independence criterion]] states the precise setting. Joint Gaussian structure is the needed assumption, not merely normal-looking individual distributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

额外的分布假设可以改变反方向的结论。对于**联合 Gaussian** 的一对变量，零协方差确实推出独立，具体条件见[[联合高斯独立判据]]。需要的是联合 Gaussian 结构，不能只看两个变量各自像正态分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same square example also has $E[Y\mid X=x]=x^2$, which changes with $x$ rather than staying at $E[Y]=2/3$. Thus zero covariance does not even require the [[条件期望|conditional mean]] to stay fixed. That separate distinction is developed in [[零协方差不推均值独立|zero covariance does not imply mean independence]]; [[独立性强弱关系|the hierarchy of independence restrictions]] connects the three levels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个平方例子还满足 $E[Y\mid X=x]=x^2$：它随 $x$ 改变，并非一直等于 $E[Y]=2/3$。所以零协方差甚至不要求[[条件期望|条件均值]]保持不变。这个独立的区别在[[零协方差不推均值独立]]中展开，[[独立性强弱关系]]则串联这三个层次。
