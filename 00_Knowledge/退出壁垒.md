# 退出壁垒是使企业难以停止或撤出某项经营活动的障碍
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Exit barriers are obstacles that make stopping or withdrawing from a business activity difficult.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

退出壁垒包括退出时新增的解约、安置或修复义务，资产难以转作他用，以及战略关联或组织阻力。它们可能使供给能力在需求下降后仍滞留，加剧[[行业内竞争]]。退出困难与[[进入壁垒|进入困难]]不是同一维度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exit barriers include additional termination, severance, or restoration obligations, assets with poor alternative uses, and strategic or organizational obstacles. They can keep capacity in a shrinking market and intensify [[行业内竞争|rivalry]]. Difficulty exiting is distinct from [[进入壁垒|difficulty entering]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断是否继续经营仍应比较未来增量现金流。例如，已经不可收回的 100 万建厂费不因退出而重新发生，不能单独作为继续亏损的理由；退出才新增的 10 万解约费则会改变继续与退出的比较。专用资产低转售价值会影响可回收金额，但不能把历史账面损失当成新的现金支付。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuation should still be assessed using future incremental cash flows. An irrecoverable one-million construction cost is not incurred again upon exit and cannot alone justify continuing losses. A new 100,000 termination payment does alter the comparison. Low resale value affects recoveries, but a historical accounting loss is not a new cash payment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体决策口径复用[[项目增量现金流]]。从现有企业角度，高进入壁垒与低退出壁垒可能分别减轻新增供给与存量过剩压力；不能把两类壁垒合成“越低越有吸引力”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
See [[项目增量现金流|incremental project cash flows]] for the decision convention. For incumbents, high entry barriers and low exit barriers may respectively limit new supply and ease persistent excess capacity. The two cannot be combined into a general claim that lower barriers always improve attractiveness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
