# 余弦函数的Maclaurin级数由交错偶次幂组成并在整个实轴成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Maclaurin series for cosine has alternating even powers and is valid on the whole real line*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以弧度为自变量，对所有实数 $x$，
$$
\cos x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^nx^{2n}}{(2n)!}
=1-\frac{x^2}{2!}+\frac{x^4}{4!}-\cdots,
\qquad R=\infty.
$$
它可由 [[正弦函数幂级数]] 逐项求导得到：$(2n+1)/(2n+1)!=1/(2n)!$，而 [[幂级数逐项求导]] 保持无限半径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With angles in radians, the displayed expansion holds for every real $x$. [[幂级数逐项求导|Differentiating]] the [[正弦函数幂级数|sine series]] cancels each factor $2n+1$ against its factorial and preserves the infinite radius.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

恢复公式时只需抓住“从 $1$ 开始、偶次幂、符号交错、幂次对应阶乘”。例如 $1-x^2/2$ 也是三次 Taylor 多项式；用 [[Taylor余项]] 可得误差不超过 $|x|^4/4!$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To reconstruct the formula, start from one and use even powers, alternating signs, and matching factorials. For example, $1-x^2/2$ is also the third-degree Taylor polynomial, with [[Taylor余项|remainder]] bounded by $|x|^4/4!$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
