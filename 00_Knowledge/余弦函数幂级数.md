# The cosine power series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*余弦函数幂级数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cosine power series starts from the value $\cos0=1$ and adds corrections in even powers of the angle. It explains why $1-x^2/2$ approximates cosine near zero and how to improve that approximation by adding $x^4/24$, then subtracting $x^6/720$, with a justified error bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

余弦函数幂级数从 $\cos0=1$ 出发，用角度的偶次幂逐步修正。它解释了为什么零点附近可以用 $1-x^2/2$ 近似余弦，以及如何加上 $x^4/24$、再减去 $x^6/720$ 来提高精度，并给出有依据的误差界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The input $x$ is in radians, meaning arc length divided by radius; cosine is the horizontal coordinate of the corresponding point on the unit circle. These are the angle units used by [[正弦余弦求导|sine and cosine differentiation]]. For a degree measurement $d$, use $x=\pi d/180$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入 $x$ 以弧度计量，即弧长除以半径；余弦是单位圆上对应点的横坐标。[[正弦余弦求导]]也使用这一角度单位。若角用度数 $d$ 表示，应换算为 $x=\pi d/180$。

> [!important] Even powers beginning with one / 从一开始的偶次幂
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every real $x$ in radians, the following series converges absolutely to $\cos x$ and has infinite [[幂级数收敛半径|radius]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对以弧度计量的每个实数 $x$，下面的级数绝对收敛到 $\cos x$，[[幂级数收敛半径|半径]]为无穷。

$$\cos x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^n x^{2n}}{(2n)!}=1-\frac{x^2}{2!}+\frac{x^4}{4!}-\cdots.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The index $n=0,1,2,\ldots$ selects powers $0,2,4,\ldots$. The factorial $k!=1\cdot2\cdots k$ for $k\ge1$, with $0!=1$, matches the power of $x$. The zero-degree term is the constant $1$, even when $x=0$; all odd-power coefficients vanish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

索引 $n=0,1,2,\ldots$ 依次选择幂次 $0,2,4,\ldots$。阶乘在 $k\ge1$ 时为 $k!=1\cdot2\cdots k$，约定 $0!=1$，阶乘的指标与 $x$ 的幂次相同。零次项是常数 $1$，在 $x=0$ 处也如此；所有奇次项的系数为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Deriving it from sine
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*由正弦推导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[正弦函数幂级数|sine expansion]] is already known to equal $\sin x$ for every real $x$ and to have infinite radius. The theorem on [[幂级数逐项求导|termwise differentiation]] therefore permits differentiation at every real input. Differentiating its term of power $2n+1$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[正弦函数幂级数]]已证明在每个实数输入处等于 $\sin x$，且半径为无穷。因此[[幂级数逐项求导]]定理允许在每个实数输入处求导。对其中幂次为 $2n+1$ 的项求导，得到

$$\frac{d}{dx}\left(\frac{(-1)^n x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}\right)=\frac{(-1)^n(2n+1)x^{2n}}{(2n+1)!}=\frac{(-1)^n x^{2n}}{(2n)!}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The factor $2n+1$ cancels against $(2n+1)!=(2n+1)(2n)!$. Since the derivative of sine is cosine, this proves the displayed identity; the differentiation theorem also preserves the infinite radius. It establishes equality with the function, not just a proposed coefficient pattern.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $(2n+1)!=(2n+1)(2n)!$，因子 $2n+1$ 可以约掉。正弦的导数是余弦，于是得到上面的恒等式；求导定理也保持无限半径。这证明的是与函数相等，而不只是写出一个候选系数规律。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficients can also be recovered from the [[Taylor级数|Maclaurin rule]]: derivatives cycle through $\cos x,-\sin x,-\cos x,\sin x$, whose values at zero are $1,0,-1,0$. Dividing each order's value by its factorial gives the same even-power terms. For $x\ne0$, the displayed term-magnitude ratio is $|x|^2/[(2n+1)(2n+2)]\to0$, confirming absolute convergence; at zero the series is $1+0+0+\cdots$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也可以从 [[Taylor级数|Maclaurin 规则]]恢复系数：各阶导数按 $\cos x,-\sin x,-\cos x,\sin x$ 循环，在零处的值为 $1,0,-1,0$；每阶值除以对应阶乘，就得到同一偶次幂展开。$x\ne0$ 时，上式相邻项的绝对值比为 $|x|^2/[(2n+1)(2n+2)]\to0$，也验证了绝对收敛；零处则直接为 $1+0+0+\cdots$。

## How many powers are needed?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*需要保留多少次幂？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an integer $m\ge0$, let $Q_m(x)=\sum_{n=0}^m(-1)^n x^{2n}/(2n)!$. This polynomial has degree $2m$ and also equals the Taylor polynomial through order $2m+1$, because the next odd coefficient is zero. All derivatives of cosine have magnitude at most one. The [[Taylor余项|Taylor remainder bound]] consequently gives, for every real $x$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对整数 $m\ge0$，令 $Q_m(x)=\sum_{n=0}^m(-1)^n x^{2n}/(2n)!$。这个多项式次数为 $2m$，但因下一奇数阶系数为零，它也等于保留到 $2m+1$ 阶的 Taylor 多项式。余弦所有阶导数绝对值都不超过一，因此由 [[Taylor余项]]可知，对每个实数 $x$，

$$|\cos x-Q_m(x)|\le\frac{|x|^{2m+2}}{(2m+2)!}.$$

> [!example] A quadratic approximation with a fourth-order error / 二次近似与四阶误差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $m=1$, $Q_1(x)=1-x^2/2$, and the bound is $|x|^4/24$. On $|x|\le0.2$, the error is at most $1/15000\approx0.00006667$. At $x=0.2$, the estimate is $49/50=0.98$. Including $x^4/24$ gives $Q_2(0.2)=14701/15000$, with error at most $1/11250000$. The degree of the polynomial and the order through which it matches Taylor coefficients need not coincide.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $m=1$，$Q_1(x)=1-x^2/2$，误差界为 $|x|^4/24$。在 $|x|\le0.2$ 上，误差至多 $1/15000\approx0.00006667$；$x=0.2$ 时近似值为 $49/50=0.98$。再加入 $x^4/24$，得到 $Q_2(0.2)=14701/15000$，误差至多 $1/11250000$。多项式本身的次数，与它匹配 Taylor 系数直到哪一阶，并不一定相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An infinite radius concerns convergence as $m$ grows for a fixed input. For a fixed small $m$, the bound increases with $|x|$, so the short approximation is most effective near zero. Differentiating this cosine series likewise gives the negative of the sine series, consistently with the derivative cycle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无限半径讨论的是固定输入后令 $m$ 增大时的收敛。固定较小的 $m$，误差上界随 $|x|$ 增大，因此短近似在零点附近最有效。对余弦级数继续逐项求导，会得到正弦级数的负值，与导数循环一致。
