# Misspecified control means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*控制变量均值误设*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[控制变量|control variate]] removes random variation by subtracting a quantity whose average should be zero. If its reference mean is wrong, that quantity has a systematic offset. The estimate can then become very stable around the wrong answer. This card separates a fixed erroneous reference from a reference that is itself estimated with uncertainty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[控制变量]]通过减去本应平均为零的量来消除随机波动。如果参照均值错误，这个量就带有系统偏移，估计可能非常稳定，却围绕着错误答案。本卡区分固定填错的参照值与本身带有估计不确定性的参照值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Follow the sign of the displacement
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先追踪偏移的方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Y$ be the target output and $C$ the control, both with finite absolute expectations. Write $\mu=E[Y]$ and $m_0=E[C]$ for their true means. The algorithm instead uses a fixed number $m$ and a fixed real coefficient $\beta$. For a fixed integer $N\ge1$ of paired outputs with these common means, let $\bar Y,\bar C$ be their sample averages and set $\widehat\mu_{\beta,m}=\bar Y-\beta(\bar C-m)$. Define the reference error $\delta=m-m_0$. By [[期望线性性|linearity]], its [[估计量偏差|bias]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令目标输出为 $Y$、控制量为 $C$，两者绝对期望有限。真实均值记为 $\mu=E[Y]$、$m_0=E[C]$，算法却使用固定数 $m$ 和固定实数系数 $\beta$。对固定整数 $N\ge1$ 次、具有这些共同均值的配对输出，记各自样本平均为 $\bar Y,\bar C$，令 $\widehat\mu_{\beta,m}=\bar Y-\beta(\bar C-m)$，参照误差为 $\delta=m-m_0$。由[[期望线性性]]，其[[估计量偏差|偏差]]为

$$
E[\widehat\mu_{\beta,m}]-\mu= -\beta(m_0-m)=\beta\delta.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This expectation calculation needs no independence. If the true control mean is $5$ but the supplied number is $4.8$, then $\delta=-0.2$. With $\beta=2$, the estimate is biased downward by $0.4$. If $\beta<0$ and $m$ overstates the true mean, the bias is also downward: $\beta\delta<0$. A wrong mean creates no bias when $\beta=0$, because the control is then not used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一步不需要独立性。若真实控制均值为 $5$，填入 $4.8$，则 $\delta=-0.2$；取 $\beta=2$，估计向下偏 $0.4$。若 $\beta<0$ 且高估均值，偏差也向下，因为 $\beta\delta<0$。当 $\beta=0$ 时，即便均值填错也不产生这项偏差，因为此时根本没有使用控制量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## More simulations shrink variance, not a fixed offset
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*更多模拟减少波动，不消除固定偏移*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume now that the complete pairs are [[独立同分布|iid]] with finite second moments, and keep $m,\beta$ fixed as $N$ increases. Let $v_\beta=\operatorname{Var}[Y-\beta(C-m_0)]$. Replacing $m_0$ by $m$ adds the constant $\beta\delta$, which leaves variance unchanged. The [[MSE偏差方差分解|bias–variance identity]] therefore gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在假设完整配对[[独立同分布]]且二阶矩有限，随 $N$ 增加仍保持 $m,\beta$ 固定。记 $v_\beta=\operatorname{Var}[Y-\beta(C-m_0)]$。把 $m_0$ 换成 $m$，只加了常数 $\beta\delta$，所以方差不变。由 [[MSE偏差方差分解|偏差—方差分解]]，

$$
\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\mu_{\beta,m})=\frac{v_\beta}{N},\qquad \operatorname{MSE}(\widehat\mu_{\beta,m};\mu)=\frac{v_\beta}{N}+\beta^2\delta^2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[均方误差|MSE]] is the expected squared distance from the intended target $\mu$. Its variance term shrinks, while its squared-bias term stays. By the [[弱大数定律|iid law of large numbers]], the estimate approaches $\mu+\beta\delta$ in probability. Unless $\beta\delta=0$, it is not consistent for $\mu$. A small reported Monte Carlo standard error only describes the remaining simulation spread.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[均方误差|MSE]]衡量与原目标 $\mu$ 之间平方距离的期望。方差项缩小，偏差平方项保持不变。由[[弱大数定律]]，估计依概率趋向 $\mu+\beta\delta$；除非 $\beta\delta=0$，否则对 $\mu$ 不一致。很小的 Monte Carlo 标准误只描述剩余的模拟波动。

> [!example] A lower variance but a larger MSE · 方差更低，MSE 却更高
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let independent $A,\varepsilon$ each take $-1$ or $1$ equally likely. Put $C=5+A$ and $Y=10+2A+\varepsilon$. Then $m_0=5$, $\mu=10$, $\operatorname{Var}(Y)=5$, and the optimal fixed coefficient is $\beta=2$. The correct correction leaves $10+\varepsilon$, with variance $1$. Using $m=4.8$ leaves $9.6+\varepsilon$. At $N=100$, the uncorrected mean has MSE $5/100=0.05$, whereas the miscentred estimate has MSE $1/100+0.4^2=0.17$. The variance gain is real, but it does not outweigh the bias at this sample size.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设相互独立的 $A,\varepsilon$ 都等概率取 $-1,1$，令 $C=5+A$、$Y=10+2A+\varepsilon$。此时 $m_0=5$、$\mu=10$、$\operatorname{Var}(Y)=5$，最优固定系数为 $\beta=2$。正确修正后为 $10+\varepsilon$，方差 $1$；填入 $m=4.8$ 后却成为 $9.6+\varepsilon$。当 $N=100$，普通均值 MSE 为 $5/100=0.05$，错误中心化估计的 MSE 却为 $1/100+0.4^2=0.17$。方差收益确实存在，但在这个样本量下不足以抵消偏差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## If the reference mean is estimated
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*如果参照均值也是估出来的*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An independent estimate $\widehat m$ is random, not a fixed truth. For fixed $\beta$, suppose it is independent of the production pairs, has $E[\widehat m]=m_0$ and finite variance $\tau^2$. Using $\bar Y-\beta(\bar C-\widehat m)$ is then unbiased over both the reference estimate and the production simulation. Its variance is $v_\beta/N+\beta^2\tau^2$. Conditional on one realised reference estimate, however, its shift is $\beta(\widehat m-m_0)$. Reusing that same estimate while increasing only the production count does not remove its uncertainty. If the two data sources are dependent, their cross-covariance must also be included.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

独立估计值 $\widehat m$ 是随机量，不是固定真值。固定 $\beta$，假设它独立于正式配对，满足 $E[\widehat m]=m_0$、有限方差 $\tau^2$。使用 $\bar Y-\beta(\bar C-\widehat m)$，对参照估计与正式模拟共同平均后无偏，方差则为 $v_\beta/N+\beta^2\tau^2$。但给定一次参照估计后，其条件偏移为 $\beta(\widehat m-m_0)$；一直重复使用它、只增加正式模拟次数，不能消除其不确定性。若两份数据依赖，还必须计入交叉协方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the other extreme, setting $\widehat m=\bar C$ from the very same production run makes the correction exactly zero, leaving $\bar Y$. An estimated mean therefore needs its own sampling and accuracy argument. Similarly, a deterministic numerical approximation needs an error assessment: if $|m-m_0|\le\eta$, then the absolute bias is at most $|\beta|\eta$. Strong correlation by itself supplies neither assessment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一个极端是直接把同一轮正式样本的 $\bar C$ 当作 $\widehat m$，修正项就恰好为零，答案回到 $\bar Y$。因此估计均值需要独立说明抽样与精度；确定性的数值近似也需要误差评估：若 $|m-m_0|\le\eta$，绝对偏差至多为 $|\beta|\eta$。仅仅相关很强，不能提供这些保证。
