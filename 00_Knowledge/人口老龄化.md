# 人口老龄化是人口年龄分布向较高年龄移动并表现为老年人口份额上升的过程

人口老龄化是人口分布向较高年龄移动、老年人口份额上升的过程，而不是某个人年纪增长。用老年人口份额衡量时，应明确选用的年龄界限；例如选择 65 岁，就观察 $N_{65+}/N$，不能与使用 60 岁界限的数据直接拼接。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Population ageing is a shift towards older ages with a rising older-population share, not an individual growing older. An older-population share requires an explicit age threshold: choosing 65 gives $N_{65+}/N$. It cannot be directly spliced with a series using age 60.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

老年人数增加不一定意味着老龄化：若总人口增长得更快，老年份额反而可以下降。老龄化也不等于总人口减少，例如总人口从 100 增至 120、老年人口从 10 增至 18，则总人口增长而老年份额从 10% 升至 15%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rising number of older people need not imply population ageing if total population grows faster. Ageing does not imply population decline either: total population can rise from 100 to 120 while older people rise from 10 to 18, increasing their share from 10% to 15%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

老年人口份额与[[年龄抚养比]]的老年分项分母不同；两者都不是实际需要多少财政支持的直接答案。要判断劳动收入、消费与公共预算的含义，应继续看[[经济供养比]]和[[财政供养比]]，并核实际就业、健康与制度安排。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The older-population share has a different denominator from the old-age component of the [[年龄抚养比|age dependency ratio]]. Neither directly measures fiscal support needs. For labour income, consumption and public budgets, use the [[经济供养比|economic support ratio]] and [[财政供养比|fiscal support ratio]], with actual employment, health and institutional arrangements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
