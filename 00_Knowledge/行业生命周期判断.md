# 判断行业生命周期阶段应交叉核对需求、供给与盈利证据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Assess an industry life-cycle stage by comparing evidence on demand, supply, and profitability.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定业务、地域与观察期，再把阶段当作待检验解释：初创看采用和商业化；成长看渗透与持续扩张；成熟看需求饱和、替换需求及产能调整；衰退看需求是否持续收缩及退出。不能只凭行业年龄或一年的收入增速定阶段。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the business, geography, and period, then treat the stage as a hypothesis: examine adoption and commercialization at introduction, penetration and sustained expansion during growth, saturation and replacement demand at maturity, and persistent contraction and exit in decline. Age or one year's revenue growth is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同时核对产能、进入退出、竞争方式和利润。收入增长须分量价；利润总额与利润率分开。若销量稳定、企业数量减少而利润率上升，可能是成熟中的整合，不能只因利润增长就判为成长。证据冲突时，应说明业务分部、地域差异或转型，而不是强行归类。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Also examine capacity, entry and exit, competitive behavior, and profit. Separate volume from price and total profit from margins. Stable sales volumes, fewer firms, and rising margins may indicate consolidation within maturity rather than a growth stage. Conflicting evidence may reflect segments, geography, or transition and should not be forced into one label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阶段判断解释经营环境，并不决定证券回报；[[增长不等于增值]]。技术、政策和社会变化可使阶段跳跃、延长或重新出现，预测应列出能推翻判断的新证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stage explains operating conditions but does not determine security returns: [[增长不等于增值|growth need not create value]]. Technology, policy, and social change can interrupt, extend, or renew stages; a forecast should specify evidence that would overturn it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
