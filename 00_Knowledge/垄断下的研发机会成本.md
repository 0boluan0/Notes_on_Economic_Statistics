# 制造业垄断使研发工资低于同一劳动转出制造业时放弃的边际产出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Manufacturing monopoly makes the research wage lower than the marginal output forgone when the same labour leaves manufacturing.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 [[Aghion–Howitt稳态研发|熟练劳动可用于制造或研发的基本模型]] 中，多用一单位研发劳动，会少用一单位制造劳动。私人研发企业支付的是市场工资；社会放弃的是这单位劳动原本能生产的最终消费品。若制造业存在垄断，两者可以不同，因此不能无条件把研发工资当作 [[社会机会成本]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[Aghion–Howitt稳态研发|basic model with shared skilled labour]], an extra unit of research displaces one manufacturing worker. The researcher pays a market wage; society forgoes the final consumption that worker could produce. Manufacturing monopoly can separate the two, so research wages need not equal [[社会机会成本|social opportunity cost]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保持同一个生产率 $A$ 与同一个制造劳动量 $x$ 来比较。最终品为 $y=Ax^\alpha$，$0<\alpha<1$；制造垄断者让边际收入等于工资。由其定价条件和物质生产函数，分别得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare at the same productivity $A$ and manufacturing employment $x$. Final output is $y=Ax^\alpha$, with $0<\alpha<1$, and the manufacturing monopolist equates marginal revenue with the wage. Its pricing condition and the physical production function give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\underbrace{MC_{\mathrm{private}}}_{\text{研发企业支付}}
=w=A\alpha^2x^{\alpha-1},
\qquad
\underbrace{MC_{\mathrm{social}}}_{\text{被放弃的边际消费产出}}
=A\alpha x^{\alpha-1}=\frac w\alpha>w.
$$

例如 $A=1,\alpha=0.5,x=0.72$，市场工资约为 $0.294628$，但在这一分配点转移一小单位劳动的边际产出损失约为 $0.589256$。这是边际比较；若一次转移大量劳动，需计算整个产出变化，不能用起点边际量无限外推。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $A=1,\alpha=0.5,x=0.72$, the wage is about $0.294628$, while the marginal output sacrifice is about $0.589256$. This is a marginal comparison. A large reallocation requires the full output change, not indefinite extrapolation of the initial marginal value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个楔子单独看会让私人研发低估资源成本，从而倾向于研发过多；它与 [[创新的跨期知识外溢|漏掉社会收益]] 的方向相反。工资支付同时是劳动者的收入，不能在已经按失去的产出计量成本后再全额加扣工资，见 [[财务与社会评价]]。完整净方向仍由 [[私人与社会研发]] 判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This wedge makes private research understate resource cost and, on its own, encourages excessive research. It works against [[创新的跨期知识外溢|omitted social benefits]]. Wages are also workers' income, so they cannot be deducted again after counting the forgone output; see [[财务与社会评价|financial versus social appraisal]]. [[私人与社会研发|The full welfare comparison]] determines the net direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paper's monopoly conditions and fourth welfare wedge establish the distinction. The wage ratio and numerical comparison are independently evaluated at one common allocation.
