# 推理显存预算必须同时容纳驻留权重、物理缓存和执行期间的其他峰值占用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An inference memory budget must fit resident weights, physical caches, and other peak execution allocations together*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断一次部署能否装下，必须按设备核算同一时刻需要存在的内存。权重装得下只解决其中一项；[[KV缓存容量|有效 KV 数据]]还要经过 [[分页KV占用|实际分配规则]]，执行过程中也会出现激活、临时工作区和运行时开销。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deployment feasibility requires per-device accounting of memory that must coexist. Fitting weights addresses only one component. [[KV缓存容量|Effective KV payload]] is subject to [[分页KV占用|physical allocation rules]], while execution also needs activations, workspace, and runtime allocations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一台设备，先划定可供本进程使用的容量 $C$，用互不重复的类别记账：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one device, first establish capacity $C$ available to the process, then use non-overlapping accounting categories:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\max_t\{M_W(t)+M_{\rm KV}(t)+M_{\rm other}(t)\}+R\leq C.
$$

$M_W$ 是本设备实际驻留的权重及其必要编码信息，$M_{\rm KV}$ 是物理缓存分配，$M_{\rm other}$ 包含未在前两项计入的激活、临时 workspace、通信缓冲、图执行私有池和运行时分配；$R$ 是预留余量。实现不使用某项就不计它。同一块内存复用给不同阶段时，也不能把两阶段的峰值当作同时存在。用“各项独立最大值之和”可作保守上界，却可能高估真实同时峰值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$M_W$ is resident weight storage including required encoding information; $M_{\rm KV}$ is physical cache allocation. $M_{\rm other}$ covers remaining activations, workspace, communication buffers, graph-private pools, and runtime allocations; $R$ is headroom. Count only components actually used. Summing separate maxima is conservative and can overestimate a peak when buffers are reused across phases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

观测时也要保持口径互斥。例如 PyTorch 的 `memory_allocated` 统计活跃 tensor 使用量，`memory_reserved` 统计分配器管理的总量，后者包含前者及空闲缓存，不能把两个数相加。如果已经按总量计入图私有池，就不能再把池内 tensor 全额重复计入其他类别。空闲缓存可能被复用；活跃 tensor 统计也不涵盖全部进程占用，仍需结合分配器状态核对实际峰值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Observed counters also overlap. In PyTorch, `memory_allocated` measures live tensor storage, while `memory_reserved` measures the allocator's total managed memory, including those tensors and cached free blocks. Do not add the two. Likewise, counting an entire graph-private pool and then counting all its tensors again double-counts memory. Cached blocks may be reusable, and live tensor counters do not cover every process allocation; inspect allocator state alongside actual peaks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如假定可用容量 24 GiB，驻留权重 14 GiB，另有 3 GiB 的其他占用上界，并预留 1 GiB，那么给 KV 分配的预算最多是 6 GiB。[[KV缓存容量]]中的 4 GiB 有效负载仍须确认物理占用是否不超过 6 GiB；继续生成会增长历史，不能只在读完提示时检查一次。这些数字是预算练习，不是特定 7B 模型的内存实测。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose usable capacity is 24 GiB, resident weights occupy 14 GiB, other allocations have a 3 GiB bound, and 1 GiB is reserved. At most 6 GiB remains for KV allocation. The 4 GiB effective payload in [[KV缓存容量|KV payload accounting]] must still fit after physical allocation and later context growth. These are budget assumptions, not measurements of a particular model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[量化对象|只量化权重]]不会自动缩小未改动的 KV；[[注意力计算与显存|避免完整注意力矩阵物化]]也不会消除权重或缓存。多设备部署应逐项检查切分和复制，不能把总字节一律除以设备数。MoE 还需区分本次激活专家与 [[MoE 全量权重驻留|整个模型必须可访问的专家权重]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[量化对象|Weight-only quantization]] does not automatically shrink unchanged KV storage, and [[注意力计算与显存|avoiding attention-matrix materialization]] removes neither weights nor caches. Multi-device budgets must follow actual partitioning and replication. For MoE, active experts differ from the [[MoE 全量权重驻留|expert weights the model must keep accessible]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Kwon et al. (2023), *PagedAttention*](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.06180v1)，§3–4：支持权重、临时激活、KV 分配及缓存增长/碎片的区分。
- [PyTorch 2.14, *CUDA semantics — Memory management / Graph memory management*](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/notes/cuda.html#memory-management)：支持活跃 tensor 与分配器总保留量的区分、图私有池及其存活期。是否存在各项占用须以具体执行栈为准。
- 本卡不等式是同时驻留条件的记账表达，24 GiB 例子为独立预算推演；它们不替代进程峰值与分配器观测。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
PagedAttention supports the allocation categories and growth behavior; PyTorch distinguishes live tensor storage from allocator-reserved memory and documents graph-private pools. The inequality and budget example are accounting derivations, not replacements for observing the actual process and allocator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
