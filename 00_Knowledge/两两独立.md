# Pairwise independence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两两独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A collection is **pairwise independent** when every two distinct members, considered on their own, are independent. The word “pairwise” specifies the size of the groups we check: choose any two, check their independence, then repeat for every other pair. It does not make a claim about three or more members considered together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一组成员**两两独立**，意思是从中任取两个不同成员，单独看这一对时，它们都独立。“两两”指定了检查的组别大小：选两个检查，再把其他各对都检查一遍。它本身不对三个或更多成员放在一起时的关系作出保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For events, each pair uses [[事件独立|the two-event product rule]]. An event is a set of possible outcomes, and $A_i\cap A_j$ means that both selected events occur. The subscripts $i,j$ are simply labels, not numerical values of the events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果成员是事件，每一对都使用[[事件独立|两个事件的乘积规则]]。事件是一组可能结果，$A_i\cap A_j$ 表示选出的两个事件同时发生。下标 $i,j$ 只是事件的编号，不是事件本身的数值。

> [!abstract] Definition for events · 事件族的定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The family $\{A_i\}_{i\in I}$ is pairwise independent if, for every pair of distinct labels $i\ne j$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 事件族 $\{A_i\}_{i\in I}$ 两两独立，是指对每一对不同编号 $i\ne j$，都有
>
> $$P(A_i\cap A_j)=P(A_i)P(A_j).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $I$ is the collection of labels. With three events, the pairs are $(A_1,A_2)$, $(A_1,A_3)$ and $(A_2,A_3)$; checking only adjacent labels would miss a pair.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $I$ 是编号集合。若有三个事件，要检查 $(A_1,A_2)$、$(A_1,A_3)$ 和 $(A_2,A_3)$；只检查相邻编号会漏掉一对。

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Independent coin tosses give pairwise independence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider three fair coin tosses modelled by eight equally likely outcome strings. Let $A_i$ mean “toss $i$ is heads”. Each event has probability $1/2$. For any selected pair of tosses, two of the eight strings have heads at both selected positions, so the joint probability is $2/8=1/4=(1/2)(1/2)$. All three pairs pass, so the events are pairwise independent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑三次公平硬币投掷的模型，八种结果组合等可能。令 $A_i$ 表示“第 $i$ 次是正面”，每个事件的概率为 $1/2$。任取两次，八种组合中都有两种在所选位置上同时为正面，所以联合概率为 $2/8=1/4=(1/2)(1/2)$。三对都通过检查，因此这些事件两两独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This particular model is also [[相互独立|mutually independent]]. Mutual independence checks every finite group, so it automatically includes all the pairs. The reverse implication is false: there are models in which every pair passes but a triple fails. The complete construction is given in [[两两独立不推出相互独立|pairwise independence without mutual independence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个特定模型也满足[[相互独立]]。相互独立检查每一组有限个成员，所以自然包含了所有逐对条件。反过来则不成立：有些模型中，每一对都通过，但三个一起时失败；完整构造见[[两两独立不推出相互独立]]。

## Pairwise independence of random variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*成员是随机变量时*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a collection of [[随机变量|random variables]] $\{X_i\}_{i\in I}$ on the same probability space, require every pair $X_i,X_j$ to satisfy [[随机变量独立|the definition of independent random variables]]. This means checking every pair of numerical events, not just one chosen pair of values:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果成员是定义在同一概率空间上的[[随机变量]] $\{X_i\}_{i\in I}$，就要求每对 $X_i,X_j$ 都满足[[随机变量独立]]的定义。这意味着检查这两个变量的所有数值事件组合，而不是只选一对取值：

$$
P(X_i\in B_i,\ X_j\in B_j)
=P(X_i\in B_i)P(X_j\in B_j),\qquad i\ne j.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sets $B_i,B_j$ range over all Borel sets of real numbers, including points and intervals. Once two variables have been selected, their independence must be complete. The restriction “pairwise” concerns how many variables we select, not how many value combinations we test within that pair.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$B_i,B_j$ 遍历所有 Borel 实数集合，包括单点和区间。选中两个变量以后，它们之间的独立性仍须完整成立。“两两”的限制针对选取几个变量，不是说在这两个变量内部只抽查几个取值组合。

> [!example] Four events, six pairs · 四个事件要查六对
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A_1,A_2,A_3,A_4$, check the label pairs $(1,2),(1,3),(1,4),(2,3),(2,4),(3,4)$. There are $\binom42=6$ pairs. Passing these six checks establishes pairwise independence, while triple and quadruple intersections remain untested.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $A_1,A_2,A_3,A_4$，应检查编号对 $(1,2),(1,3),(1,4),(2,3),(2,4),(3,4)$，共 $\binom42=6$ 对。六项都通过，就建立了两两独立；三重和四重交集仍没有被检查。
