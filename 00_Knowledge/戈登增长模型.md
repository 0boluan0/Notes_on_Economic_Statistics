# 戈登增长模型是以恒定股权必要报酬率折现永续稳定增长现金股利的估值模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Gordon growth model values perpetually and steadily growing cash dividends using a constant required equity return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

戈登增长模型是[[股利贴现模型]]的稳定增长版本：从下一期末的每股现金股利 $D_1>0$ 开始，以后每期按同一增长率 $g$ 延续，使用同期间、同币种并匹配股权风险的恒定必要报酬率 $k_e$。在通常的 $k_e>0$、$g>-1$ 且 $k_e>g$ 的设定下：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Gordon model is the stable-growth version of the [[股利贴现模型|DDM]]. Start with a positive cash dividend per share $D_1$ at the end of next period; each subsequent dividend grows at the same rate $g$. Use a constant required equity return $k_e$ matched to the period, currency, and equity risk. Under the usual assumptions $k_e>0$, $g>-1$, and $k_e>g$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$D_t=D_1(1+g)^{t-1},\qquad
V_0=\frac{D_1}{k_e-g}.$$

分子是**还没有收到的下一期股利**。若刚支付 $D_0$，且从 $D_0$ 到 $D_1$ 也按同一 $g$ 增长，才可写成 $D_1=D_0(1+g)$。若题目直接给了 $D_1$，不必再乘一次增长率；在[[多阶段股利估值]]的交界处，首笔稳定尾段股利与前一笔也不必按尾段增长率相连。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The numerator is the **next dividend not yet received**. If $D_0$ has just been paid and the same growth rate also links it to $D_1$, then $D_1=D_0(1+g)$. A directly supplied $D_1$ is not grown again. At a [[多阶段股利估值|multistage valuation]] boundary, the first terminal-stream dividend need not grow from the preceding dividend at the terminal growth rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就是把现金股利代入[[增长永续年金]]：每期折现后的增长倍数为 $(1+g)/(1+k_e)$，小于 1 才能把无限级数相加。正股利下若 $k_e\le g$，不能把分母为零或负数所得的结果当成有限股票价值。若 $g=0$，则退化为[[固定永续年金]]的 $V_0=D_1/k_e$，不需要另建一套零增长数学。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This applies the [[增长永续年金|growing-perpetuity]] calculation to cash dividends. The discounted growth factor $(1+g)/(1+k_e)$ must be below one for the infinite series to sum. With positive dividends, $k_e\le g$ does not yield a finite share value by accepting a zero or negative denominator. When $g=0$, the model reduces to the [[固定永续年金|level-perpetuity]] value $V_0=D_1/k_e$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

教材例中刚付 $D_0=2$ 元，永久增长率为 12%，股权必要报酬率为 16%。先得到 $D_1=2(1.12)=2.24$，再得 $V_0=2.24/(0.16-0.12)=56$ 元。直接用2作分子会得50元，错在把已付股利当成下一期。零增长例每年末股利2元、必要报酬率16%，则为 $2/0.16=12.5$ 元。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the textbook example, a dividend of 2 has just been paid, perpetual growth is 12%, and the required equity return is 16%. First calculate $D_1=2(1.12)=2.24$, then $V_0=2.24/(0.16-0.12)=56$. Using 2 directly gives 50 by mistaking the paid dividend for the next dividend. With a constant annual dividend of 2 and a 16% required return, the zero-growth value is $2/0.16=12.5$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $D_1$ 时，较高 $k_e$ 降低价值，较高 $g$ 提高价值；分母越小，结果越敏感。但不能脱离盈利、留存和再投资能力任意上调永久 $g$，也不能把短期高增长直接延伸到无穷。若增长或风险尚在转变，先用[[多阶段股利估值]]；若已知市场价而要反推模型收益率，则改用[[戈登隐含收益率]]，不要把估值输入和反解结果混为一谈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding $D_1$ fixed, a higher $k_e$ lowers value and a higher $g$ raises it; sensitivity increases as the denominator shrinks. Perpetual growth must remain consistent with earnings, retention, and reinvestment capacity, rather than extending short-term high growth indefinitely. Use [[多阶段股利估值|multistage valuation]] while growth or risk is changing. If market price is given and the model-implied return is sought, use [[戈登隐含收益率|the Gordon implied return]] instead of confusing a valuation input with a reverse-calculated result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Footnote 1 on CPA PDF121, printed page 113, explains that the first future dividend and subsequent stable growth suffice; the transition from the previous dividend need not use that same rate. Long-run plausibility follows the shared growing-perpetuity conditions. The example's 12% is not prescribed as a realistic perpetual forecast for an actual company.
