# Martingale difference sequences
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*鞅差序列*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **martingale difference** is a new change whose conditional average, using the information available before that change, is zero. It may turn out positive or negative, and its size can depend on the past. The requirement is that the past does not give a nonzero conditional mean for the next change. A sequence of such changes is a martingale difference sequence, often abbreviated MDS.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**鞅差**是一项新的变化：利用变化发生以前的信息计算，它的条件平均为零。实际结果可以为正或负，大小也可以依赖过去；要求的是过去信息不能给这一步带来非零的条件均值。一串这样的变化组成鞅差序列，常缩写为 MDS。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[滤过定义|filtration]] $(\mathcal F_n)_{n\ge0}$ describes what is known after each step. Let $D_n$ be the real change observed at step $n$, with $n\ge1$. Being [[适应过程|adapted]] means the information at that step determines $D_n$. Being integrable means $E|D_n|<\infty$, so its [[条件期望|conditional expectation]] is well defined as an integrable random variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[滤过定义|滤过]] $(\mathcal F_n)_{n\ge0}$ 描述每一步之后已知什么。用实数 $D_n$ 表示第 $n$ 步观察到的变化，编号从 $n\ge1$ 开始。[[适应过程|适应]]表示当期信息足以确定 $D_n$；可积表示 $E|D_n|<\infty$，从而可以把它的[[条件期望]]定义为可积随机变量。

> [!important] The preceding information is the key · 关键是上一期信息
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every $n\ge1$, an MDS must satisfy all three conditions: $D_n$ is $\mathcal F_n$-measurable, $E|D_n|<\infty$, and the displayed conditional mean is zero almost surely. This means zero outside a probability-zero exceptional set; it does not mean that every observed $D_n$ is zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每个 $n\ge1$，鞅差序列同时要求 $D_n$ 对 $\mathcal F_n$ 可测、$E|D_n|<\infty$，以及下面的条件均值几乎必然为零。几乎必然允许零概率异常集，并不是说每次观察到的 $D_n$ 都等于零。

$$E[D_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]=0,\qquad n\ge1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conditioning uses $\mathcal F_{n-1}$, before the new change. Conditioning instead on $\mathcal F_n$ would reveal $D_n$ itself and give $E[D_n\mid\mathcal F_n]=D_n$. Also, $\mathcal F_0$ can already contain initial information: the first increment must have conditional mean zero given that information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件信息是新变化发生之前的 $\mathcal F_{n-1}$。如果改用 $\mathcal F_n$，就已经知道 $D_n$ 本身，只会得到 $E[D_n\mid\mathcal F_n]=D_n$。此外，$\mathcal F_0$ 可以包含初始信息，第一项也必须在给定这些信息后保持条件均值为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Dependence is allowed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*允许依赖过去*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\xi_1,\xi_2,\ldots$ be independent fair signs, each equal to $-1$ or $1$ with probability $1/2$. Use $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$ and a trivial $\mathcal F_0$. Set $D_1=\xi_1$, and for $n\ge2$ choose a multiplier $H_n=1+\mathbf1_{\{\xi_{n-1}=1\}}$, then put $D_n=H_n\xi_n$. The [[事件指示变量|indicator]] adds one after a positive previous result, so the next change has size two after a win and size one after a loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $\xi_1,\xi_2,\ldots$ 是相互独立的公平符号，取 $-1$ 或 $1$ 的概率各为 $1/2$。采用 $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$，$\mathcal F_0$ 为平凡信息。令 $D_1=\xi_1$；对 $n\ge2$，选择倍数 $H_n=1+\mathbf1_{\{\xi_{n-1}=1\}}$，再令 $D_n=H_n\xi_n$。这里的[[事件指示变量]]在上一轮为正时加一，所以前一轮赢后，新变化的大小为二；输后为一。

> [!example] The size can be predictable while the sign is fair · 大小已知，符号仍公平
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $D_1=1$, then $D_2$ is equally likely to be $-2$ or $2$, with conditional mean zero. If $D_1=-1$, it is equally likely to be $-1$ or $1$, again with conditional mean zero. The conditional variances for these two histories are $4$ and $1$, respectively. Indeed, $|D_2|=2$ exactly when $D_1=1$, so these increments are dependent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $D_1=1$，$D_2$ 等概率取 $-2$ 或 $2$，条件均值为零；若 $D_1=-1$，它等概率取 $-1$ 或 $1$，条件均值仍为零。两种历史下的条件方差分别为 $4$ 和 $1$。事实上，$|D_2|=2$ 恰好等价于 $D_1=1$，因此这些增量并不独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every later step, $H_n$ is [[离散可预测过程|predictable]] because it is known at time $n-1$, and $|D_n|\le2$. Independence of the fresh sign gives $E[D_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]=H_nE[\xi_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]=0$. Thus the whole sequence is an MDS. Independence and identical distributions of the increments are not required by the definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以后每一步的 $H_n$ 都[[离散可预测过程|可预测]]，因为在 $n-1$ 时已经知道它，而且 $|D_n|\le2$。利用新符号的独立性，有 $E[D_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]=H_nE[\xi_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]=0$，所以整串都是鞅差。定义不要求这些增量独立，也不要求同分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Zero overall mean is weaker
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*总体均值为零更弱*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking expectations gives $E[D_n]=0$ by the [[全期望公式|law of total expectation]]. The reverse fails. Let a fair sign $Z$ be revealed at time $1$, and use $D_n=Z$ for every $n\ge1$, with $\mathcal F_0$ trivial and $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(Z)$ thereafter. Each increment has mean zero, but $E[D_2\mid\mathcal F_1]=Z\ne0$: after the first observation, its sign is already known.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[全期望公式]]再取一次期望，得到 $E[D_n]=0$，但反方向不成立。让公平符号 $Z$ 在时刻 $1$ 揭示，并对每个 $n\ge1$ 使用 $D_n=Z$；$\mathcal F_0$ 平凡，此后 $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(Z)$。每项总体均值都为零，但 $E[D_2\mid\mathcal F_1]=Z\ne0$：第一次观察之后，符号就已经知道了。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The word “difference” comes from martingales: if $M$ is a [[鞅定义|martingale]], its increments $M_n-M_{n-1}$ satisfy this definition. Conversely, the [[鞅差部分和|partial-sum theorem]] explains how these changes, added to an integrable initial value known at time zero, produce a martingale. That theorem concerns the accumulated level; this definition concerns each new change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“差”这个名字来自鞅：若 $M$ 是[[鞅定义|鞅]]，它的增量 $M_n-M_{n-1}$ 就符合这里的定义。反过来，[[鞅差部分和]]说明怎样把这些变化加到一个在零时刻已知的可积初值上，构成鞅。那条定理讨论累计水平，这里定义的是每次新变化。
