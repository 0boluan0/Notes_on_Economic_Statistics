# 最小方差边界由每个可达目标收益下方差最小的组合构成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The minimum-variance frontier consists of the least-variance portfolios at each attainable target expected return.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定具有有限二阶矩的资产收益与权重可行域 $\mathcal W$。对一个可达目标收益 $r$，在 $w^T\mu=r$ 的可行组合中寻找最小方差；当最小值能取到时，相应组合属于**最小方差边界**。其风险收益坐标由下式给出：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix asset returns with finite second moments and an allowed weight set $\mathcal W$. At an attainable target mean $r$, minimize variance among feasible portfolios satisfying $w^T\mu=r$. When the minimum is attained, its minimizers belong to the minimum-variance frontier, with the following risk-return coordinates:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
v(r)=\min_{w\in\mathcal W,\;w^T\mu=r}w^T\Sigma w,
\qquad (\sigma,\mu)=(\sqrt{v(r)},r).
$$

这一步只比较**同一目标收益**的组合。如果某个目标不可达，就没有对应的可行点；如果只存在未取到的下确界，也不能凭空指定一个最优权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This optimization compares portfolios at the same target mean. An infeasible target supplies no feasible point. An unattained infimum likewise does not identify an optimal allocation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小方差边界不自动等于[[均值方差有效前沿]]：另一目标收益的组合可能在收益更高的同时风险不增加。例如 $\mu=(0.08,0.12)^T$、$\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}0.04&0.015\\0.015&0.09\end{bmatrix}$，只施加 $w_1+w_2=1$。目标 $8\%$ 强制 $w=(1,0)$，方差为 $0.04$；目标 $10\%$ 强制等权，方差同为 $0.04$。前者虽是其目标下的最小方差解，却被后者支配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The minimum-variance frontier is not automatically the [[均值方差有效前沿|mean-variance efficient frontier]]: a different target can offer higher return without greater risk. With the stated two-asset inputs and only $w_1+w_2=1$, target $8\%$ forces $w=(1,0)$ and variance $0.04$, while target $10\%$ forces equal weights and the same variance. The former minimizes variance at its own target but is dominated by the latter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

允许卖空且条件满足时，[[目标收益最小方差解]]给出闭式；其中哪些点真正有效，由[[最小方差边界的有效上支]]进一步筛选。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under unrestricted short selling and the required regularity conditions, the [[目标收益最小方差解|target-return solution]] gives a closed form. The [[最小方差边界的有效上支|efficient-upper-branch result]] then identifies which points are efficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Sources and verification*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Sigman, p.6, §1.5, equations (12)–(14), supports variance minimization subject to a fixed target mean and budget.
- Zivot, pp.16–17, Proposition 9, distinguishes efficient and inefficient parts of the minimum-variance frontier. The example is independently verified from the budget and target equations.
