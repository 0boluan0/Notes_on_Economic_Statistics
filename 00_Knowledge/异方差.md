# 回归误差的条件方差随观测信息改变时，误差具有异方差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Regression errors are heteroskedastic when their conditional variance changes with observed information*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 在线性条件均值模型
> $$Y_i=X_i'\beta+u_i,\qquad E(u_i\mid X)=0$$
> 中，若不同观测的误差散布大小不同，
> $$\operatorname{Var}(u_i\mid X)=\sigma_i^2,$$
> 就存在异方差。它改变的是**给定解释变量后尚未解释部分的尺度**，不是误差的条件均值。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a linear conditional-mean model, heteroskedasticity means that the dispersion of the unexplained component differs across observations: $\operatorname{Var}(u_i\mid X)=\sigma_i^2$. It concerns conditional scale, not the conditional mean of the error.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，用收入解释家庭消费时，高收入家庭可选择的消费组合更多，消费偏离回归均值的幅度也可能更大。回归线仍可正确描述每个收入水平的条件均值，但点云会随着收入上升逐渐张开。这个“张开”才是异方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, in a regression of household consumption on income, higher-income households may have more varied consumption choices. The regression line can still describe the conditional mean correctly while the cloud of observations fans out as income rises. That changing spread is heteroskedasticity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

异方差是协方差结构中的**对角线问题**：误差协方差矩阵 $\Omega=\operatorname{Var}(u\mid X)$ 的对角元不再都等于同一个 $\sigma^2$。它本身不要求不同观测之间相关；若非对角元也不为零，则还存在[[误差序列相关]]或组内相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Heteroskedasticity is a diagonal feature of the error covariance matrix: the diagonal entries of $\Omega=\operatorname{Var}(u\mid X)$ are not all the same. It does not itself imply correlation across observations; nonzero off-diagonal entries represent serial or within-group dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要把横截面异方差与[[ARCH(q)模型|条件波动动态]]分开。前者只说条件方差不是常数；ARCH/GARCH 进一步规定当前方差怎样由过去冲击递推。收益率的波动聚集可以同时属于两者，但不能因为“高波动与低波动交替”就把普通异方差检验当作完整波动模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cross-sectional heteroskedasticity must also be distinguished from conditional-volatility dynamics. ARCH and GARCH do more than allow a nonconstant variance: they specify how current variance evolves from past shocks. Volatility clustering may satisfy both descriptions, but an ordinary heteroskedasticity test is not a complete volatility model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 某回归中 $E(u_i\mid X)=0$，但 $\operatorname{Var}(u_i\mid X)=4X_i^2$。这是内生性还是异方差？
>
> **答案：** 是异方差。条件均值仍为零，变化的是条件方差；它首先影响效率与常规标准误，而不是自动制造内生性。
