# Heteroskedasticity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*异方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A regression describes how an outcome changes with information such as income. Even after fixing that information, different outcomes remain possible. **Heteroskedasticity means that the size of this remaining variation depends on the information.** The average can be correctly described while the observations are tightly grouped around it in one group and widely spread in another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归描述结果如何随收入等信息改变。即使把这些信息固定下来，结果仍可能有多种取值。**异方差指的就是：这部分剩余变化的大小随所给信息而改变。** 平均水平可以描述正确，但一组观测紧贴平均水平，另一组却分散得更开。

> [!example] Two income groups, two different spreads · 两个收入组，两种分散程度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider a deliberately small consumption model. Let $X=0$ denote the lower-income group and $X=1$ the higher-income group, each with probability $1/2$. Independently, a sign $\varepsilon$ is $-1$ or $1$, each with probability $1/2$. Define consumption $Y$ and its error $U$ by
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑一个刻意简化的消费模型。$X=0$ 表示较低收入组，$X=1$ 表示较高收入组，两组各占概率 $1/2$。另有独立的符号 $\varepsilon$，以相同概率取 $-1$ 或 $1$。定义消费 $Y$ 及其误差 $U$：
>
> $$Y=20+10X+U,\qquad U=(1+2X)\varepsilon.$$
>
> | Group / 组别 | Possible $Y$ values / 可能的消费 | Conditional mean / 条件均值 | Possible $U$ values / 可能的误差 | Conditional variance / 条件方差 |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | $X=0$ | $19,\ 21$ | $20$ | $-1,\ 1$ | $1$ |
> | $X=1$ | $27,\ 33$ | $30$ | $-3,\ 3$ | $9$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Within each row the two outcomes are equally likely. Both rows have mean error zero, but the second row has larger squared deviations: $(9+9)/2=9$ instead of $(1+1)/2=1$. The change from mean $20$ to mean $30$ describes the mean relationship. The change from error variance $1$ to $9$ is the heteroskedasticity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每一行的两个结果都等可能。两组误差的均值均为零，但第二组的平方偏差更大：$(9+9)/2=9$，而第一组是 $(1+1)/2=1$。均值由 $20$ 变为 $30$ 描述的是均值关系；误差方差由 $1$ 变为 $9$ 才是异方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What is being held fixed?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*究竟固定了什么信息？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real outcome $Y$ with [[矩存在性的使用边界|finite second moment]], meaning $E[Y^2]<\infty$, let $m(X)=E[Y\mid X]$ be its [[条件期望|conditional mean]]: the average outcome after learning $X$. Here $X$ may contain several variables or categories. The error $U=Y-m(X)$ is the deviation from that average, so $E[U\mid X]=0$. Its [[条件方差|conditional variance]] measures the average squared deviation remaining after $X$ is known:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实数结果 $Y$ 的[[矩存在性的使用边界|二阶矩有限]]，即 $E[Y^2]<\infty$，令 $m(X)=E[Y\mid X]$ 为[[条件期望|条件均值]]，也就是知道 $X$ 后的平均结果。这里的 $X$ 可以包含多个变量或类别。误差 $U=Y-m(X)$ 是相对该平均水平的偏差，因此 $E[U\mid X]=0$。它的[[条件方差]]衡量知道 $X$ 后还剩下多大的平均平方偏差：

$$\sigma^2(X)=\operatorname{Var}(U\mid X)=E[U^2\mid X].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If this quantity equals one fixed nonnegative number $\sigma^2$ almost surely, the errors are **homoskedastic** relative to $X$. If no such constant works, they are **heteroskedastic**. “Almost surely” allows exceptions on events of probability zero; changing a formula only at an impossible value of $X$ does not create heteroskedasticity. The definition concerns the conditional variance, not whether the mean function is straight. A [[条件均值与线性投影|linear conditional-mean model]] is the special case $m(X)=X^\top\beta$, where the fixed coefficient vector $\beta$ weights the entries of $X$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若这个量几乎必然等于某个固定的非负数 $\sigma^2$，就称误差相对于 $X$ **同方差**；若不存在这样的常数，就称其**异方差**。“几乎必然”允许概率为零的例外：只在不可能出现的 $X$ 取值上改写公式，不会制造异方差。定义说的是条件方差，不要求均值函数一定是直线。[[条件均值与线性投影|线性条件均值模型]]只是其中的特殊情况 $m(X)=X^\top\beta$，固定系数向量 $\beta$ 对 $X$ 的各分量加权。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conditioning information matters. In a time series, [[条件异方差|conditional heteroskedasticity given the past]] concerns the variation remaining after observing the history. An [[ARCH(q)模型|ARCH model]] goes further by specifying how past shocks determine that variance. It is one model for changing conditional variance, not the definition of every heteroskedastic regression.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件信息本身很重要。时间序列中的[[条件异方差|给定过去信息的条件异方差]]，关心看过历史之后还剩多少变化。[[ARCH(q)模型|ARCH 模型]]进一步规定过去冲击如何决定当前方差。它是变化条件方差的一类模型，不是所有异方差回归的定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why an iid sample can still be heteroskedastic
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么 iid 样本仍然可能异方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Draw independent copies of the entire pair $(X,Y)$ in the opening example. These observations are [[独立同分布|independent and identically distributed]], and every error has the same unconditional variance:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把开头的整个 $(X,Y)$ 对象独立重复抽取，就得到[[独立同分布|独立同分布（iid）]]的观测；每个误差的无条件方差也都相同：

$$\operatorname{Var}(U)=E[U^2]=\frac12\cdot1+\frac12\cdot9=5.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Yet after fixing the group, the variance is $1$ or $9$. There is no contradiction: iid describes how complete observations are sampled, while heteroskedasticity describes how the conditional spread varies inside their common distribution. Likewise, mean error zero does not require a constant error variance. In this example the mean equation is correct despite the changing spread.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但固定组别之后，方差是 $1$ 或 $9$。这并不矛盾：iid 描述完整观测怎样抽样，异方差描述它们共同分布内部的条件分散程度怎样变化。同样，误差条件均值为零也不要求误差条件方差为常数。本例均值方程是正确的，分散程度仍然变化。

## Variance across groups versus covariance across observations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*组别间的方差变化与观测间的协方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n$ observations, collect the errors into $u=(u_1,\ldots,u_n)^\top$ and write $X_{\mathrm{all}}$ for the full design information. Their conditional [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] is $\Omega=\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X_{\mathrm{all}})$. Its diagonal entries are individual variances; its off-diagonal entries are covariances between different errors. These are separate features. For example, conditional on a fixed two-row design, the matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $n$ 个观测，把误差排成 $u=(u_1,\ldots,u_n)^\top$，用 $X_{\mathrm{all}}$ 表示全部设计信息。条件[[协方差矩阵]]为 $\Omega=\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X_{\mathrm{all}})$。对角元是单个误差的方差，非对角元是不同误差之间的协方差。这是两个不同的特征。例如，给定固定的两行设计后，下面两种矩阵分别说明：

$$
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&9\end{pmatrix}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\begin{pmatrix}1&1/2\\1/2&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first has unequal variances and zero cross-covariance. The second has equal variances but correlated errors. If the observations are time-ordered, that cross-covariance is [[误差序列相关|serial correlation]]. Thus homoskedasticity alone does not imply $\Omega=\sigma^2 I_n$: that familiar form also requires zero off-diagonal covariances, with $I_n$ the $n$-dimensional identity matrix. Independence is stronger still.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一种方差不等，但交叉协方差为零；第二种方差相等，但误差相关。若这些观测按时间排序，该交叉协方差就是[[误差序列相关]]。所以同方差本身不推出 $\Omega=\sigma^2 I_n$；这个常见形式还要求非对角协方差为零，$I_n$ 是 $n$ 维单位矩阵。独立又是更强的要求。

## What the pattern changes in a regression
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*这种模式会改变回归的什么部分？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fan-shaped residual plot can suggest changing variance, but the fitted [[残差协方差诊断|residuals are proxies for unobserved errors]]. A misspecified mean can also produce patterns. The probabilistic definition is about the error distribution under a specified mean and information set; a plot by itself does not verify that model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

张开的残差图可以提示方差变化，但拟合[[残差协方差诊断|残差只是不可观测误差的代用品]]，均值模型设错也可能产生图案。概率定义针对的是指定均值与信息集之下的误差分布；仅凭一张图不能核实整个模型。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the correctly specified linear mean model, with a fixed [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full-column-rank design]] and mean-zero errors, [[普通最小二乘|OLS]] coefficients remain unbiased even if the errors are heteroskedastic. Their uncertainty, however, depends on the full $\Omega$. The conventional homoskedastic covariance formula is no longer guaranteed to be correct; it need not be wrong for every special covariance pattern. [[协方差失效下的OLS|OLS under a changed error covariance]] develops this distinction. [[异方差稳健协方差|Heteroskedasticity-consistent covariance estimation]] requires its own sampling and large-sample conditions, and does not by itself handle arbitrary correlation across observations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在线性均值模型正确、设计固定且[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]的条件下，只要误差均值为零，即使异方差，[[普通最小二乘|OLS]] 系数仍无偏；但其不确定性依赖完整的 $\Omega$。常规同方差协方差公式不再保证正确，并不意味着它对每一种特殊协方差结构都一定错误。[[协方差失效下的OLS]]展开这个区别。[[异方差稳健协方差]]估计也有自己的抽样与大样本条件，不能单凭“异方差稳健”四个字处理观测间任意相关。
