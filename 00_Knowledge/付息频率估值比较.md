# 提高付息频率对债券价值的影响取决于固定的是名义年收益率还是有效年收益率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The value effect of increasing coupon frequency depends on whether nominal or effective annual yield is held fixed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较不同付息频率时，必须说明保持哪些条件不变。固定面值 $F$、年息票总额 $Fc$、剩余期限 $T$，不等于同时固定了名义年收益率和有效年收益率；两者之间的换算本身依赖付息／复利频率 $m$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A coupon-frequency comparison must state what is held fixed. Fixing face value $F$, total annual coupons $Fc$, and maturity $T$ does not fix both nominal and effective annual yields: their conversion itself depends on frequency $m$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若固定同一个正的名义年收益率数值 $y>0$，每期率取 $y/m$，且每种频率都有完整付息期，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the same positive nominal annual yield $y>0$ is held fixed, the period rate is $y/m$, and each schedule has complete coupon periods, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_m=\frac{Fc}{y}+F\left(1-\frac{c}{y}\right)\left(1+\frac{y}{m}\right)^{-mT}.$$

频率上升使 $(1+y/m)^m$ 上升、到期贴现因子下降。因此在这组条件下，$c<y$ 的折价债价格下降，$c>y$ 的溢价债价格上升，$c=y$ 的平价债不变。这里不仅改变了支付时间，也改变了有效年收益率，不能称为只改变付息频率而保持经济贴现率不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A higher frequency raises $(1+y/m)^m$ and lowers the maturity discount factor. Under these conditions, a discount bond with $c<y$ loses value, a premium bond with $c>y$ gains value, and a par bond with $c=y$ is unchanged. Both payment timing and the effective annual yield have changed; this does not hold the economic discount rate constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以下两组均取 $F=100,c=5\%,T=2$，从每年付 $5$ 改为每半年付 $2.5$，无违约且按面值偿还：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both comparisons below use $F=100,c=5\%,T=2$, replacing annual coupons of 5 with semiannual coupons of 2.5, with no default and redemption at par:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 固定口径 / Fixed convention | 年付价格 / Annual price | 半年每期率 / Half-year rate | 半年付价格 / Semiannual price |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 名义年收益率 6% / Nominal annual yield 6% | 98.166607 | 3% | 98.141451 |
| 有效年收益率 6% / EAR 6% | 98.166607 | $\sqrt{1.06}-1$ | 98.302109 |

第二组固定 EAR 时，本金的现值不变，只把原本年末息票的一半提前半年支付；正贴现率使提前的支付更值钱，所以价格反而上升。一般把每个规则付息期细分、平均提前支付，固定正 EAR 时也有这个逻辑。零 EAR 下时间提前不改变现值；负 EAR 的比较须重新按公式判断，不能套正利率口号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a fixed EAR in the second row, principal PV is unchanged and half of each annual coupon is paid six months earlier. At a positive discount rate, earlier payment is more valuable, so price rises. The same reasoning applies when each regular period is subdivided evenly at a fixed positive EAR. At zero EAR, timing alone leaves PV unchanged; negative-EAR comparisons must be checked from the formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
