# 货币性与流动性应按面值兑付结算速度接受范围价格风险和转换成本判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Moneyness and liquidity require a multidimensional test of par redemption, settlement speed, acceptance, price risk, and conversion cost*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子判据
> 资产“有多像货币”不是单一的是非标签。至少要问：能否按面值兑付或结算、多久完成、谁接受、价格是否会在使用前波动、转成支付资产要付多少费用或价差，以及压力期这些条件是否仍成立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> How money-like an asset is cannot be reduced to one yes-or-no label. At minimum ask whether it redeems or settles at par, how quickly, who accepts it, whether its price may move before use, what fees or spreads conversion incurs, and whether those properties survive stress.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“流动性高”也必须说明是哪一种流动性。市场流动性强调能否迅速低成本卖出；支付流动性强调能否及时完成到期付款；货币性还加入面值单一性、接受网络和结算终局性。三者相关，但不能用“容易卖”替代“可以直接按面值支付”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Highly liquid” must also identify the kind of liquidity. Market liquidity concerns rapid sale at low cost; funding or payment liquidity concerns meeting obligations on time; moneyness additionally involves singleness at par, the acceptance network, and settlement finality. These ideas are related, but “easy to sell” cannot substitute for “directly usable at par.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 货币性是连续谱，且会随持有人、制度、技术和压力状态变化。
- 同属某个货币总量不表示成分的风险、收益和可用速度相同。
- 货币市场基金份额可能高度流动，却仍有赎回、结算或净值边界；不能仅凭名称把它等同于银行存款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Moneyness is a continuum and can vary by holder, institution, technology, and state of stress.
- Membership in one monetary aggregate does not make components identical in risk, return, or speed of use.
- A money-market fund share may be highly liquid yet retain redemption, settlement, or net-asset-value boundaries; its name alone does not make it a bank deposit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一只债券可在几分钟内卖出，但价格可能下跌且卖出后资金次日才到账。它为什么未必和活期存款一样“像货币”？
>
> **答案：** 它虽有市场流动性，却不能保证按面值即时结算，还承担价格和到账时间风险；货币性需要同时考察多个维度。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A bond can be sold within minutes, but its price may fall and cash arrives the next day. Why is it not necessarily as money-like as a demand deposit?
>
> **Answer:** It has market liquidity but not guaranteed immediate settlement at par, and it bears price and timing risk. Moneyness requires several dimensions at once.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
