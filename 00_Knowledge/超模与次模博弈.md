# 超模博弈以收益对自身策略与对手策略的递增差异刻画战略互补，递减差异则给出次模方向与战略替代，而最佳反应单调性还依赖有序策略空间和正则条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A supermodular game expresses strategic complementarity through increasing differences between own and opponents' strategies; decreasing differences give the submodular direction of strategic substitution, while monotone best responses also require ordered strategy spaces and regularity conditions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在标量有序策略中，收益 $u_i(s_i,s_{-i})$ 对 $(s_i,s_{-i})$ 具有**递增差异**，若对 $s_i'>s_i$ 和 $s_{-i}'\ge s_{-i}$，

$$
u_i(s_i',s_{-i}')-u_i(s_i,s_{-i}')
\ge
u_i(s_i',s_{-i})-u_i(s_i,s_{-i}).
$$

意思是：对手选择更高时，自己把策略从低提高到高的增量收益不会变小。递减差异把不等号反过来，表示对手选择更高会削弱自己提高策略的诱因。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Increasing differences mean that the incremental payoff from raising one's own action does not fall when opponents choose higher actions. Reversing the inequality gives decreasing differences. These payoff comparisons—not the visual slope of one plotted curve—are the primitive concepts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 平滑标量情形的快速检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Quick Test in Smooth Scalar Games*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若收益二阶连续可微，则

$$
\frac{\partial^2u_i}{\partial s_i\partial s_j}\ge0
$$

对应递增差异；小于等于零对应递减差异。在紧致有序策略集、连续性和最优解存在等条件下，递增差异使最大与最小最佳反应随对手策略单调上升；递减差异给出向下的反应方向。由此才能连接到[[战略替代与互补]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For twice differentiable scalar payoffs, a nonnegative cross-partial characterises increasing differences and a nonpositive one characterises decreasing differences. With compact ordered strategy sets and the regularity needed for maximizers to exist, increasing differences make extremal best responses nondecreasing; decreasing differences lead in the opposite direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## “反应函数向上”不是完整定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An Upward-Sloping Reaction Curve Is Not the Full Definition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

超模博弈的正式定义还包含策略集的格结构、收益的连续性或上半连续性，以及多维自身策略时的超模性。最佳反应可能是集合，单值曲线的斜率只是这些条件在简单模型里的投影。反过来，一张局部向上的曲线也不能证明整个博弈在全域超模。

线性 Cournot 利润的交叉偏导为负，产量表现为战略替代；Hotelling 内点定价分支的交叉偏导为正，价格表现为战略互补。[[广告竞赛反应函数|广告份额竞赛]]的反应先升后降，正说明同一博弈不能在整个区间被草率归入一个方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A formal supermodular game also requires lattice strategy spaces, appropriate continuity, and supermodularity in multidimensional own actions. A single-valued reaction-curve slope is only a simple projection of that structure. Cournot quantities have a negative cross effect; the interior Hotelling pricing branch has a positive one; and the advertising contest changes sign across regions, so it is not globally classified by one label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 某反应函数在均衡附近向上，但在别处向下。能否据此称整个博弈为超模博弈？
>
> **答案：** 不能。超模性是对整个相关策略域的收益差异与格结构的条件；局部斜率只说明局部反应方向。
