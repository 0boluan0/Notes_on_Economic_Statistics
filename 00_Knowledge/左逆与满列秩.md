# 矩阵有左逆当且仅当它满列秩
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A matrix has a left inverse if and only if it has full column rank*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心等价
> 设 $\mathbb F\in\{\mathbb R,\mathbb C\}$ 且 $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$。存在 $L\in\mathbb F^{n\times m}$ 使
> $$
> LA=I_n
> $$
> 当且仅当 $\operatorname{rank}(A)=n$。因此左逆只可能在 $m\ge n$ 时存在。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $\mathbb F\in\{\mathbb R,\mathbb C\}$ and $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$. A matrix $L\in\mathbb F^{n\times m}$ satisfying $LA=I_n$ exists if and only if $\operatorname{rank}(A)=n$. Hence a left inverse can exist only when $m\ge n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么两边等价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the two conditions are equivalent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $LA=I_n$ 且 $Ax=0$，则 $x=L(Ax)=0$。所以 $N(A)=\{0\}$，$A$ 的列线性无关，因而 $\operatorname{rank}(A)=n$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $LA=I_n$ and $Ax=0$, then $x=L(Ax)=0$. Thus $N(A)=\{0\}$, the columns of $A$ are linearly independent, and $\operatorname{rank}(A)=n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，若 $A$ 满列秩，则限制映射 $A:\mathbb F^n\to C(A)$ 是双射。先在 $C(A)$ 上把 $L$ 定义为这个限制映射的逆，再把 $C(A)$ 的一组基扩充为 $\mathbb F^m$ 的基，并令 $L$ 在补充的基向量上取零，就得到满足 $LA=I_n$ 的线性映射。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, if $A$ has full column rank, then the restricted map $A:\mathbb F^n\to C(A)$ is a bijection. Define $L$ on $C(A)$ as the inverse of this restricted map, extend a basis of $C(A)$ to a basis of $\mathbb F^m$, and set $L$ to zero on the added basis vectors. The resulting linear map satisfies $LA=I_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 解的含义边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary for solutions of $Ax=b$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满列秩保证 $Ax=b$ **至多**有一个精确解，但不保证每个 $b\in\mathbb F^m$ 都可达；后者要求满行秩。若 $m>n$，左逆通常不唯一，因为它只需在 $C(A)$ 上正确撤销 $A$，在其他输出方向上仍有自由度。标准内积进一步选出的规范左逆见 [[满列秩伪逆公式]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full column rank makes an exact solution to $Ax=b$ unique whenever it exists, but it does not make every $b\in\mathbb F^m$ attainable; that requires full row rank. When $m>n$, left inverses are generally non-unique because they need only undo $A$ on $C(A)$ and may act differently on the remaining output directions. The canonical left inverse selected by the standard inner product is given in [[满列秩伪逆公式|the full-column-rank pseudoinverse formula]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 一个 $5\times3$ 满列秩矩阵有左逆。它能保证对每个 $b\in\mathbb R^5$ 都有解吗？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A full-column-rank $5\times3$ matrix has a left inverse. Does this guarantee a solution for every $b\in\mathbb R^5$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。列空间只有 3 维；满列秩只保证一旦有解就唯一。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. The column space has dimension three; full column rank guarantees uniqueness only when a solution exists.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
