# Chromatic number
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*色数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **chromatic number** is the smallest number of color labels needed to color every vertex while keeping adjacent vertices different. A particular [[Proper 着色与独立集划分|proper coloring]] is one assignment; the chromatic number asks for the best possible number across all valid assignments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**色数**是在给全部顶点着色、同时让相邻顶点异色的前提下，所需颜色标签数的最小值。某个[[Proper 着色与独立集划分|合法着色]]是一种具体分配；色数则要在全部合法分配中寻找最少用色数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the chain $a-b-c$, assigning different colors to all three vertices works. But assigning one color to $a,c$ and a second to $b$ also works, and the edge $ab$ rules out one color. Its chromatic number is therefore two, even when the displayed drawing happens to use three colors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对链 $a-b-c$，三个顶点各用一种颜色当然合法。但也可以让 $a,c$ 同色、让 $b$ 用另一色；而边 $ab$ 又排除了一色方案。因此它的色数为二，即使眼前的图画恰好用了三种颜色。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The minimum exists for finite simple graphs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有限简单图的最小值确实存在*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a finite simple undirected graph $G=(V,E)$ with at least one vertex. A proper coloring always exists: give every vertex its own label. Hence some positive integer $k\le |V|$ works, and the least working integer is well defined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取至少有一个顶点的有限简单无向图 $G=(V,E)$。给每个顶点单独一种标签，总能得到合法着色。因此至少有一个正整数 $k\le |V|$ 可行，其中最小的整数确实存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonempty graph,
$$
\chi(G)=\min\{k:G\text{ admits a proper }k\text{-coloring}\}.
$$
Thus $k$-colorability says only $\chi(G)\le k$; it does not by itself determine $\chi(G)$. A nonempty edgeless graph has chromatic number one. The displayed minimum is intentionally defined only for nonempty graphs; the common convention for the null graph is $\chi(G)=0$, and any different convention must be stated explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非空图，chromatic number 定义为
$$
\chi(G)=\min\{k:G\text{ admits a proper }k\text{-coloring}\}.
$$
因此“$G$ 可用 $k$ 色”只表示 $\chi(G)\le k$，而不是已经算出了 $\chi(G)$。若图非空但没有边，所有顶点可以共享一种颜色，所以 $\chi(G)=1$。上面的 minimum 特意只对非空图下定义；对没有顶点的 null graph，常见约定是 $\chi(G)=0$，但使用其他约定时必须明说。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the number does and does not describe
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*它衡量什么，又不衡量什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonempty graph, $1\le\chi(G)\le |V|$. At least one edge forces at least two colors, because its endpoints must differ. A complete graph $K_r$, meaning $r$ vertices with every pair joined, needs $r$ colors. Renaming colors or vertices cannot change the minimum: it only renames the same valid assignments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非空图满足 $1\le\chi(G)\le |V|$。只要至少有一条边，就至少需要两色，因为这条边两端必须异色。完全图 $K_r$ 指 $r$ 个顶点两两相连的图，因而需要 $r$ 色。重命名颜色或顶点不会改变这个最小值，只是在给同样的合法分配换名字。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The value does not count vertices, edges, colorings, or the number of colors used by a particular algorithm. A graph is **$k$-colorable** when $\chi(G)\le k$; it is **$k$-chromatic** when $\chi(G)=k$. To establish the latter, see [[精确色数的上下界|matching upper and lower bounds]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

色数不是顶点数、边数、着色方案数，也不是某次算法运行实际用的颜色数。“可 $k$ 着色”表示 $\chi(G)\le k$；“色数为 $k$”表示 $\chi(G)=k$。要证明后一等式，需要[[精确色数的上下界|让上、下界相遇]]。

> [!warning] Empty vertices and loops are different boundaries / 空顶点集与自环是不同边界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The convention $\chi(\varnothing)=0$ means that no vertices need colors. It is not obtained by minimizing over positive $k$ in the displayed nonempty-graph definition: the empty graph is colorable with every positive palette, whose minimum would be 1. A graph with a loop is different again: the proper-coloring rule admits no coloring at all. The finite numerical definition here is for loop-free graphs; no value for looped graphs is imposed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 约定 $\chi(\varnothing)=0$ 表示没有顶点需要颜色，不能把它说成上面非空图定义中对正整数 $k$ 取最小值的结果：空图对任意正整数调色板都可着色，正整数中的最小值会是1。含自环又是另一种情形：合法着色条件根本没有解。本卡的有限数值定义针对无自环图，不另规定含自环图的色数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Parallel edges between different vertices impose repeated copies of one inequality, so deleting their duplicates does not change the chromatic number. Infinite graphs can require infinitely many colors and need a broader definition; they are outside the finite scope here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不同顶点间的平行边只是重复同一个异色约束，删除重复边不会改变色数。无限图可能需要无限多种颜色，必须采用更广的定义，不属于本卡的有限范围。
