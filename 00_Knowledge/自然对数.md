# The natural logarithm
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*自然对数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **natural logarithm** answers an inverse question: “What exponent on $e$ produces this positive number?” If [[自然指数函数|the natural exponential]] takes an input $y$ to $e^y$, then $\ln x$ takes its positive output $x$ back to the input that produced it. For example, $\ln(e^2)=2$, and $\ln1=0$ because $e^0=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**自然对数**回答一个反向问题：“$e$ 要取多少次幂，才得到这个正数？”如果[[自然指数函数]]把输入 $y$ 变成 $e^y$，那么 $\ln x$ 就把正的输出 $x$ 还原成产生它的输入。例如 $\ln(e^2)=2$；$\ln1=0$，因为 $e^0=1$。

> [!example] Recovering a time · 把时间解出来
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the model $Q(t)=100e^{0.05t}$, when does the quantity reach $200$? Dividing by $100$ gives $e^{0.05t}=2$. Applying $\ln$ recovers $0.05t=\ln2$, so $t=\ln2/0.05\approx13.863$. Here $\ln2\approx0.693147$ is the exponent that makes $e$ produce a factor of two.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在模型 $Q(t)=100e^{0.05t}$ 中，什么时候数量达到 $200$？除以 $100$ 得 $e^{0.05t}=2$。取自然对数，把指数还原出来：$0.05t=\ln2$，因此 $t=\ln2/0.05\approx13.863$。这里 $\ln2\approx0.693147$ 是“使 $e$ 的幂得到倍数二”的指数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the inverse statement includes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*反函数关系包含什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exponential is strictly increasing from all real inputs onto all positive outputs. Every $x>0$ therefore has exactly one corresponding exponent. The formal definition and the two undoing identities are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自然指数函数从全部实数输入严格递增地取遍全部正输出。因此每个 $x>0$ 都对应唯一的指数。正式定义与两个互相还原的恒等式是

$$y=\ln x\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad e^y=x,\qquad x>0,$$

$$e^{\ln x}=x\quad(x>0),\qquad \ln(e^y)=y\quad(y\in\mathbb R).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The domain of $\ln$ is $(0,\infty)$ and its range is $\mathbb R$. It is negative between zero and one, zero at one, and positive above one; in particular $\ln e=1$ and $\ln(1/e)=-1$. As its positive input approaches zero, it decreases without bound. That limit does not assign a real value to $\ln0$. No real exponent on $e$ produces zero or a negative number, so these are excluded inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\ln$ 的定义域是 $(0,\infty)$，值域是全部实数。输入在零与一之间时结果为负，输入为一时结果为零，大于一时结果为正；特别地，$\ln e=1$、$\ln(1/e)=-1$。正输入趋近零时，对数向负方向无界下降，但这个极限并没有给 $\ln0$ 指定一个实数值。$e$ 的任何实数次幂都不会产生零或负数，所以它们不在定义域中。

## Multiplication becomes addition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把乘法变成加法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For positive $u,v$ and any real $r$, the exponent laws give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正数 $u,v$ 与任意实数 $r$，指数法则给出

$$\ln(uv)=\ln u+\ln v,\qquad\ln(u/v)=\ln u-\ln v,\qquad\ln(u^r)=r\ln u.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the first identity, write $u=e^p$ and $v=e^q$. Then $uv=e^{p+q}$, so the exponent recovered by $\ln$ is $p+q$. A product of growth factors becomes a sum of log changes: $\ln(uv)-\ln u=\ln v$ depends on the multiplying factor, not the starting size. This is also why [[对数求导|logarithmic differentiation]] can turn products and powers into simpler sums.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看第一个恒等式，写成 $u=e^p$、$v=e^q$。于是 $uv=e^{p+q}$，自然对数还原出来的指数就是 $p+q$。增长倍数的乘积就这样变成对数变化量的和：$\ln(uv)-\ln u=\ln v$ 只由乘上的倍数决定，与起始规模无关。这也是[[对数求导]]可以把乘积与幂拆成较简单的和式的原因。

> [!warning] Keep the input conditions · 保留输入条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The product rule requires positive factors, not merely a positive product. Although $(-2)(-3)=6$ has a real logarithm, $\ln(-2)+\ln(-3)$ does not. Also $\ln(u+v)$ generally does not equal $\ln u+\ln v$: with $u=v=1$, the two sides are $\ln2$ and $0$. Writing $\ln|u|$ creates a function on $u\ne0$; it does not define $\ln u$ for negative $u$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 乘积法则要求各因子为正，而不只是乘积为正。虽然 $(-2)(-3)=6$ 可以取实对数，$\ln(-2)+\ln(-3)$ 却无定义。$\ln(u+v)$ 一般也不等于 $\ln u+\ln v$：取 $u=v=1$，两边分别是 $\ln2$ 与 $0$。写成 $\ln|u|$ 是另造一个在 $u\ne0$ 上有定义的函数，并没有把负数处的 $\ln u$ 定义出来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a general logarithm, $\log_a x$ asks for the exponent on a fixed base $a>0$, $a\ne1$. The identity $a^y=e^{y\ln a}$ gives $\log_a x=\ln x/\ln a$ for $x>0$. Thus changing bases rescales the answer. Base one is excluded because $1^y$ cannot recover different positive outputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般对数 $\log_a x$ 问的是固定底数 $a>0$、$a\ne1$ 应取多少次幂。利用 $a^y=e^{y\ln a}$，对 $x>0$ 有 $\log_a x=\ln x/\ln a$，所以换底只是把答案换一个尺度。底数一必须排除，因为 $1^y$ 无法还原不同的正输出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The construction in [[自然指数函数|the exponential card]] begins with an integral and builds an inverse exponential, so this inverse definition does not depend on proving logarithm rules circularly. The same function has the [[自然对数积分表示|integral representation]] $\ln x=\int_1^x dt/t$. Its rate of change is developed separately in [[对数函数求导|logarithmic derivatives]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[自然指数函数]]卡先从积分构造出指数函数，因此这里的反函数定义不需要循环借用待证的对数规则。同一个函数还有[[自然对数积分表示|积分表示]] $\ln x=\int_1^x dt/t$；它的变化率则在[[对数函数求导]]中单独说明。
