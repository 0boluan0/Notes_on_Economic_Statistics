# 自然对数是自然指数函数从正实数到实数的反函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The natural logarithm is the inverse of the natural exponential from positive reals to real numbers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> [[自然指数函数]] $e^x$ 把 $\mathbb R$ 一一映到 $(0,\infty)$。它的反函数称为自然对数，记作 $\ln x$：
> $$
> y=\ln x\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad e^y=x,\qquad x>0.
> $$
> 因此 $\ln$ 的定义域是正实数、值域是全体实数，并有 $\ln1=0$、$\ln e=1$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]] maps $\mathbb R$ one-to-one onto $(0,\infty)$. Its inverse is the natural logarithm: $y=\ln x$ exactly when $e^y=x$, for $x>0$. Thus the domain of $\ln$ is the positive reals, its range is all real numbers, and $\ln1=0$, $\ln e=1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指数加法律翻转成乘积法则：
$$
\ln(xy)=\ln x+\ln y,\qquad
\ln(x^r)=r\ln x
$$
（在实值表达式有定义的条件下）。对数因此把乘法变成加法、把幂变成乘法；这正是 [[对数求导]] 能拆开复杂乘积和移动指数的原因。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exponential addition law becomes $\ln(xy)=\ln x+\ln y$, and powers become $\ln(x^r)=r\ln x$ wherever the real-valued expressions are defined. Logarithms therefore turn products into sums and exponents into factors, which is why logarithmic differentiation can simplify moving powers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“$\ln x$”不是对非正数暂时没算出来，而是在实数体系中没有定义。写 $\ln|u(x)|$ 可在 $u\ne0$ 的局部区间处理负值，但不能据此跨过零点或忽略分支。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In real calculus, $\ln x$ is not merely unevaluated for nonpositive $x$; it is undefined there. The expression $\ln|u(x)|$ can handle a locally nonzero negative function, but it does not permit crossing a zero or ignoring branches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
