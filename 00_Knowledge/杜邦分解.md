# 同口径权益净利率等于营业净利率乘总资产周转率再乘权益乘数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a consistent basis, ROE equals net margin times total asset turnover times the equity multiplier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

杜邦分解用销售和资产作中间桥梁，把[[权益净利率]]拆成“销售留下多少利润、资产支持多少销售、每元权益支持多少资产”。设同一主体和期间的利润、收入为 $NI,S$，选定存量口径的资产、权益为 $A^*,E^*$，且三个分母不为零：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DuPont decomposition bridges [[权益净利率|ROE]] through sales and assets: profit per sale, sales per unit of assets, and assets per unit of equity. Let $NI,S$ refer to the same entity and period, and $A^*,E^*$ to specified stock measures, with all three denominators nonzero:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac{NI}{E^*}=\underbrace{\frac{NI}{S}}_{\text{净利率}}\times\underbrace{\frac{S}{A^*}}_{\text{资产周转率}}\times\underbrace{\frac{A^*}{E^*}}_{\text{权益乘数}}.$$

右侧的 $S$ 和 $A^*$ 精确抵消，所以这是恒等式，不是统计估计。若[[总资产周转率]]用平均资产，[[权益乘数]]也必须用同一个平均资产；不能混入期末资产。以 ABC 的期末口径计算，$(136/3000)(3000/2000)(2000/960)=136/960\approx14.17\%$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$S$ and $A^*$ cancel exactly, making this an identity rather than a statistical estimate. Average assets in [[总资产周转率|turnover]] must match average assets in the [[权益乘数|multiplier]], not ending assets. ABC's ending-stock calculation yields approximately 14.17%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由此可以沿[[营业净利率]]追收入与费用，沿周转率追资产占用，沿乘数追融资结构。若要分摊两期 ROE 差额，使用[[连环替代法]]并说明顺序；恒等式本身没有给出唯一的因果贡献。增加债务还可能改变利息和净利润，不能把三个比率当成彼此独立的旋钮，见[[ROE与杠杆的解释边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Follow [[营业净利率|net margin]] into income and expenses, turnover into asset use, and the multiplier into financing structure. To allocate a change in ROE, use [[连环替代法|chain substitution]] with a stated order; the identity supplies no unique causal allocation. Additional debt may change interest and net income, so the factors are not independent controls; see [[ROE与杠杆的解释边界|the leverage boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
