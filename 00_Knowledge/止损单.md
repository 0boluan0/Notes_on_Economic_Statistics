# 止损单是在达到约定触发条件后转为市价单的委托

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stop order becomes a market order when its specified trigger condition is met.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

止损单是先以约定价格条件等待触发，触发后转为[[市价单]]的条件委托。本卡采用普通 stop order 的含义：卖出止损通常在价格向下达到或越过止损价时触发，买入止损通常在价格向上达到或越过止损价时触发；具体比较使用成交价、报价还是其他指标，由场所和经纪商规则决定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stop order is a conditional instruction that waits for a specified price condition and then becomes a [[市价单|market order]]. In the ordinary stop-order sense used here, a sell stop normally triggers when the price reaches or crosses the stop price downward, and a buy stop when it reaches or crosses upward. Whether the test uses trades, quotations or another specified indicator depends on venue and broker rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

止损价是启动执行的门槛，不是承诺成交的价格。例如，持有的股票原价为 100 元，卖出止损价为 95 元；若适用触发指标跳到 90 元，止损单已触发，而当时可执行买价只有 89.80 元，则在数量足够且无其他限制的假设下可能按 89.80 元卖出，并非 95 元。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The stop price starts execution; it does not promise an execution price. Suppose a held share was at 100 and a sell stop is set at 95. If the applicable trigger indicator jumps to 90, the order triggers. With sufficient size and no other restrictions, an available bid of only 89.80 can result in a sale at 89.80, not 95.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，“止损”这个名称不保证最大亏损、成交价格或全部成交。短暂价格波动也可能触发委托。若同时要求成交价格不劣于某一限价，需区分[[止损限价单]]，而后者又会带来不成交的可能。有效期、适用交易时段和撤单条件仍须另行确认。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The name does not guarantee a maximum loss, price or full execution. A brief price fluctuation can trigger the order. Requiring a price no worse than a limit instead calls for a [[止损限价单|stop-limit order]], which can remain unfilled. Validity, eligible sessions and cancellation conditions must still be checked separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
