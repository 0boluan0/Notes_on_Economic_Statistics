# Row equivalence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行等价*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Row equivalence means that one matrix can be obtained from another by reversible elementary changes to its rows. When the rows record equations, these operations replace the equations with convenient combinations. Reversibility matters: multiplying an entire row by zero may discard information, so it is not an allowed operation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行等价是指：一个矩阵可以通过对行作可逆的初等变换得到另一个矩阵。当各行记录方程时，这些操作把方程换成更方便的组合。可逆性很重要：整行乘零可能丢失信息，因此不属于允许的操作。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The allowed operations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*允许哪些操作*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A,R\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$ be matrices of the same size over the same field $\mathbb F$, usually $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. The positive integers $m,n$ count rows and columns. Write $R_i$ for row $i$ when describing an operation. The three [[初等矩阵|elementary row operations]] and their inverses are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A,R\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$ 是同一域 $\mathbb F$ 上同样大小的矩阵，通常取 $\mathbb F=\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。正整数 $m,n$ 分别表示行数和列数，描述操作时用 $R_i$ 表示第 $i$ 行。三种[[初等矩阵|初等行操作]]及其逆操作是：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Swap two rows; swap them again to undo the operation.
- Multiply row $i$ by a scalar $c\ne0$; multiply it by $1/c$ to undo it.
- Replace row $i$ by $R_i+cR_j$, where $i\ne j$; replace it by $R_i-cR_j$ to undo it. The source row $j$ stays unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 交换两行；再次交换即可恢复。
- 第 $i$ 行乘非零标量 $c$；再乘 $1/c$ 即可恢复。
- 用 $R_i+cR_j$ 替换第 $i$ 行，其中 $i\ne j$；再用 $R_i-cR_j$ 替换即可恢复。作为来源的第 $j$ 行保持不变。

> [!important] Definition · 定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $A$ and $R$ are **row equivalent** if a finite sequence of these operations transforms $A$ into $R$. Zero operations are allowed. Each operation is left multiplication by an elementary $m\times m$ matrix. If the successive matrices are $E_1,\ldots,E_k$, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果有限次上述操作能把 $A$ 变成 $R$，就称两者**行等价**；允许零次操作。每一步都是左乘一个 $m\times m$ 初等矩阵。若依次使用的初等矩阵为 $E_1,\ldots,E_k$，则
>
> $$
> R=EA,\qquad E=E_k\cdots E_2E_1.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every factor is [[逆矩阵|invertible]], hence so is $E$. Conversely, such a product specifies a finite row-operation sequence. With zero operations, $E$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I_m$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个因子均[[逆矩阵|可逆]]，因此 $E$ 可逆。反过来，这样的乘积就给出有限串行操作。零次操作对应[[单位矩阵]] $I_m$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The order in this [[矩阵乘法|matrix product]] records time from right to left: $E_1$ acts first. Row equivalence is an equivalence relation. Doing nothing proves reflexivity; reversing the order and undoing every step proves symmetry; concatenating two sequences proves transitivity. Any echelon form obtained from $A$ by row operations belongs to this same class. The [[RREF存在唯一性|RREF theorem]] selects one unique reduced form from each class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个[[矩阵乘法|矩阵乘积]]从右向左记录操作先后：$E_1$ 最先作用。行等价是一种等价关系：不作操作证明自反性；倒序撤销每一步证明对称性；接续两串操作证明传递性。由 $A$ 经行操作得到的任何阶梯形都属于同一行等价类。[[RREF存在唯一性|RREF 存在唯一性定理]]从每个类中选出一个唯一的行最简形。

## A small reversible change
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use real entries in this example and in the numerical comparisons below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本例及下文的数值比较均取实数元素。

$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\3&7\end{bmatrix}
\xrightarrow{R_2\leftarrow R_2-3R_1}
R=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
E=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\-3&1\end{bmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The new second row is $(3,7)-3(1,2)=(0,1)$, so $R=EA$. Row $1$ has not changed. Adding three copies of it back to row $2$ recovers $(3,7)$. In matrix form,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

新的第二行为 $(3,7)-3(1,2)=(0,1)$，所以 $R=EA$。第一行未变，把它的三倍加回第二行，就恢复了 $(3,7)$。用矩阵表示为

$$
E^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\3&1\end{bmatrix},
\qquad A=E^{-1}R.
$$

## What must be transformed together
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonhomogeneous system $Ax=b$, $x$ is the unknown column vector and $b$ is the given right-hand side. Multiplying the equation by $E$ gives $Rx=Eb$, not generally $Rx=b$. In the example, take $b=(1,0)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非齐次方程组 $Ax=b$，$x$ 是未知列向量，$b$ 是给定右端。两侧左乘 $E$ 得到 $Rx=Eb$，通常不是 $Rx=b$。在前例中取 $b=(1,0)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，则

$$
Ax=b\ \Longrightarrow\ x=(7,-3)^T,
\qquad
Rx=b\ \Longrightarrow\ x=(1,0)^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The unchanged right-hand side has produced different solutions. The correct transformed right-hand side is $Eb=(1,-3)^T$; solving $Rx=Eb$ again gives $(7,-3)^T$. This is why [[行操作的解集不变性|solution preservation requires operating on the entire augmented matrix]]. In the homogeneous case $b=0$, the transformed right-hand side remains zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

右端不动，解就不同。正确变换后的右端为 $Eb=(1,-3)^T$，解 $Rx=Eb$ 又得到 $(7,-3)^T$。这就是[[行操作的解集不变性|保持解集必须对增广矩阵整体操作]]的原因。齐次情形 $b=0$ 时，变换后右端仍为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reversible row combinations preserve the [[行空间|row space]], since each old row can also be recovered as a combination of the new ones. Equal [[矩阵秩|rank]], the dimension of the column space, alone is weaker: the one-row matrices $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ both have rank $1$, but with only one row the only nontrivial allowed change is nonzero scaling, which cannot turn one into the other. The effects on the other fundamental spaces are distinguished in [[行操作与四个子空间|row operations and the four subspaces]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可逆的行组合保持[[行空间]]，因为旧行也能写成新行的组合。但[[矩阵秩|秩]]即列空间维数，相同的秩更弱：单行矩阵 $(1,0)$ 与 $(0,1)$ 的秩都是 $1$，只有一行时，唯一非平凡的允许变换是乘非零数，无法把其中一个变成另一个。对其他基本子空间的作用，见[[行操作与四个子空间]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Row equivalence also differs from [[相似矩阵|similarity]]. Row operations use a left factor $E$ and can apply to rectangular matrices. Similarity has the square-matrix form $S^{-1}AS$, with the same change of coordinates on both sides. For example, $I_2$ is row equivalent to $\operatorname{diag}(2,1)$ by scaling its first row, but every matrix similar to $I_2$ is $S^{-1}I_2S=I_2$. Here $\operatorname{diag}(2,1)$ has $2,1$ on its main diagonal and zeros elsewhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行等价也不同于[[相似矩阵|相似]]。行操作只使用左因子 $E$，也适用于矩形矩阵；相似具有方阵形式 $S^{-1}AS$，两侧配合同一次坐标变换。例如，第一行乘 $2$ 就把 $I_2$ 变为 $\operatorname{diag}(2,1)$，两者行等价，但任何与 $I_2$ 相似的矩阵都是 $S^{-1}I_2S=I_2$。这里 $\operatorname{diag}(2,1)$ 表示主对角线为 $2,1$，其余位置为零的矩阵。

> [!question]- How do you reverse a sequence? · 怎样撤销整串操作？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Undo the last operation first, then proceed backwards. Each inverse is again an allowed elementary operation, so reachability by row operations works in both directions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 先撤销最后一步，再依次往前撤销。每个逆操作仍是允许的初等操作，所以能够通过行操作相互到达是双向的。
