# Exponential distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*指数分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **exponential distribution** describes a nonnegative waiting time measured on a continuous clock. It is used when, while we are still waiting, the event's instantaneous rate stays constant. The event might be a component failing or a service completing. This is a modeling assumption about the event mechanism, not something every waiting time automatically satisfies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**指数分布**描述按连续时钟计量的非负等待时间。它适用于这样的模型：只要还在等待，事件发生的瞬时速率就保持不变。事件可以是零件失效，也可以是服务完成。这是关于事件机制的一项建模假设，不是所有等待时间自动满足的规律。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, suppose a toy service model has rate $\lambda=2$ per hour. The symbol $\lambda$ controls how quickly the wait ends; a larger rate means a shorter typical wait. Let the [[随机变量|random variable]] $T$ record the waiting time in hours. Unlike the [[几何分布|geometric distribution]], which counts attempts $1,2,3,\ldots$, $T$ can take fractional times such as $0.4$ hours.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一个示意服务模型的速率为每小时 $\lambda=2$。参数 $\lambda$ 控制等待结束得有多快，速率越大，通常等待越短。令[[随机变量]] $T$ 记录以小时计的等待时长。[[几何分布]]统计的是 $1,2,3,\ldots$ 次尝试，而这里的 $T$ 可以取 $0.4$ 小时这样的非整数时间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the law as a probability of still waiting
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先把分布读成“还在等”的概率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The clearest description is the **survival probability**, meaning the chance that the wait lasts beyond a specified time. With a finite positive rate $\lambda$, it decreases exponentially:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最直接的描述是**生存概率**，即等待超过指定时长的概率。对有限的正速率 $\lambda$，它按指数函数下降：

> [!abstract] Rate parameterization · 速率参数约定
> $$0<\lambda<\infty,\qquad
> T\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda),\qquad
> P(T>t)=e^{-\lambda t}\quad(t\ge0).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $\sim$ means “has this distribution”; [[自然指数函数|$e^x$]] is the natural exponential function. The product $\lambda t$ is dimensionless: if $t$ is in hours, $\lambda$ is in inverse hours. Since the survival probability tends to zero as $t\to\infty$, $T$ is finite with probability one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\sim$ 表示“服从这个分布”，[[自然指数函数|$e^x$]] 是自然指数函数。乘积 $\lambda t$ 没有单位：若 $t$ 以小时计，$\lambda$ 的单位就是每小时。由于生存概率在 $t\to\infty$ 时趋于零，$T$ 以概率一为有限值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[累积分布函数|cumulative distribution function]] $F_T(t)=P(T\le t)$ gives the complementary probability that waiting has finished by $t$. The [[概率密度函数|density]] $f_T(t)$ gives probabilities by integration over intervals:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[累积分布函数]] $F_T(t)=P(T\le t)$ 给出补事件的概率：截至 $t$ 时等待已经结束。[[概率密度函数]] $f_T(t)$ 则通过对区间积分来给出概率：

$$
F_T(t)=
\begin{cases}
0,&t<0,\\
1-e^{-\lambda t},&t\ge0,
\end{cases}
\qquad
f_T(t)=
\begin{cases}
0,&t<0,\\
\lambda e^{-\lambda t},&t\ge0.
\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indeed, $\int_0^t\lambda e^{-\lambda u}\,du=1-e^{-\lambda t}$ for $t\ge0$, and integrating to infinity gives total probability one. At any single specified time, $P(T=t)=0$; a density value is not a point probability. The density can exceed one when measured in some time units, as explained in [[PMF不等于密度|point probabilities versus densities]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

的确，$t\ge0$ 时，$\int_0^t\lambda e^{-\lambda u}\,du=1-e^{-\lambda t}$；把积分上限取到无穷，总概率就是一。在任意单个指定时点，都有 $P(T=t)=0$，密度值不是点概率。采用某些时间单位时，密度可以大于一；这个区别见[[PMF不等于密度|点概率与密度]]。

> [!example] Half an hour versus an interval · 半小时与一段区间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the rate-$2$-per-hour model, the chance of still waiting after half an hour is $e^{-2(1/2)}=e^{-1}\approx0.368$. The chance of finishing within that half-hour is $1-e^{-1}\approx0.632$. Finishing between half an hour and one hour has probability
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每小时速率为 $2$ 时，半小时后仍在等待的概率是 $e^{-2(1/2)}=e^{-1}\approx0.368$；半小时内结束的概率是 $1-e^{-1}\approx0.632$。在半小时到一小时之间结束的概率为
>
> $$P(1/2<T\le1)=F_T(1)-F_T(1/2)=e^{-1}-e^{-2}\approx0.233.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These are interval probabilities, not the density height at either endpoint.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这些都是区间概率，不是两个端点处的密度高度。

## The average wait is the reciprocal of the rate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*平均等待时间是速率的倒数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[期望|expectation]] weights each possible duration by its density. For an exponential variable, [[分部积分|integration by parts]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[期望]]用密度为各个可能时长加权。对指数变量，使用[[分部积分]]可得

$$
E[T]=\int_0^\infty t\lambda e^{-\lambda t}\,dt
=\left[-t e^{-\lambda t}\right]_0^\infty
+\int_0^\infty e^{-\lambda t}\,dt
=\frac1\lambda.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The boundary term vanishes because [[幂函数慢于指数函数|exponential decay dominates the linear factor]] $t$. Thus rate $2$ per hour gives a mean wait of $1/2$ hour, or thirty minutes. This average is not a scheduled completion time: some waits are much shorter, and some are much longer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

边界项为零，因为[[幂函数慢于指数函数|指数衰减压过了线性因子]] $t$。所以每小时速率为 $2$，平均等待为 $1/2$ 小时，也就是三十分钟。平均值并不是预先排好的结束时间：有些等待短得多，有些长得多。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some sources use a **scale** parameter $\theta=1/\lambda$ instead. In that convention the density is $f_T(t)=\theta^{-1}e^{-t/\theta}$ for $t\ge0$, and the mean is $\theta$. Always check whether the argument of an exponential-distribution function is a rate or a scale. Saying only “parameter $2$” does not resolve the convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有些资料改用**尺度**参数 $\theta=1/\lambda$。此时 $t\ge0$ 上的密度写成 $f_T(t)=\theta^{-1}e^{-t/\theta}$，均值为 $\theta$。使用指数分布函数时，要先检查输入的是速率还是尺度，仅说“参数为 $2$”还不够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Units change the numerical parameter. The same service model expressed in minutes has rate $2/60=1/30$ per minute. For a thirty-minute wait, the exponent is still $(1/30)\cdot30=1$. We changed the numerical clock, not the event mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位也会改变参数的数值。同一个服务模型若用分钟计时，速率为每分钟 $2/60=1/30$。对三十分钟的等待，指数中的乘积仍为 $(1/30)\cdot30=1$。改变的是计时单位，不是事件机制。

## What “constant rate” does and does not mean
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*恒定速率究竟意味着什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the start, the probability of finishing in a short interval of length $h$ is $1-e^{-\lambda h}$. Its ratio to $h$ tends to $\lambda$ as $h\downarrow0$, by the [[指数函数求导|derivative of the exponential function]]. Thus $\lambda h$ is a short-time approximation, not the exact probability for arbitrary $h$. In the service example, using $h=1$ hour would give $\lambda h=2$, while the actual probability is $1-e^{-2}<1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

刚开始时，在长为 $h$ 的短区间内结束的概率是 $1-e^{-\lambda h}$。由[[指数函数求导]]，当 $h\downarrow0$ 时，这个概率除以 $h$ 的比值趋于 $\lambda$。所以 $\lambda h$ 只是短时间近似，不是任意 $h$ 下的精确概率。服务例子若取 $h=1$ 小时，$\lambda h=2$，但真实概率是 $1-e^{-2}<1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same short-time rate applies after any elapsed wait, conditional on still waiting. That stronger statement follows from [[指数分布无记忆性|exponential memorylessness]]: the remaining wait has the original distribution. It is why this distribution appears in [[CTMC指数停留时间|holding times of homogeneous continuous-time Markov chains]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

已经等待一段时间后，只要条件是“仍未结束”，短时速率仍然相同。这个更强的结论来自[[指数分布无记忆性]]：剩余等待具有原来的分布。这也解释了它为什么出现在[[CTMC指数停留时间|时间齐次连续时间 Markov 链的停留时间]]中。

> [!warning] A rate of zero is a different boundary · 零速率是另一种边界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Setting $\lambda=0$ in the density gives the zero function, whose integral is zero. It is not an ordinary probability density. A model that never finishes can instead assign $T=\infty$ with probability one, as at an absorbing state. A fixed deadline or an age-dependent completion rate also needs a different waiting-time model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把 $\lambda=0$ 代入密度会得到恒零函数，其积分是零，不是普通概率密度。若模型规定永不结束，可以另行令 $T$ 以概率一等于无穷，例如吸收态中的等待。固定截止时间或随已等待时长变化的完成速率，也需要别的等待时间模型。
