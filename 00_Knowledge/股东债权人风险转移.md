# 债务条件已定且项目切换难以约束时股东可能偏好损害债权人与总价值的风险选择

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once debt terms are fixed, an inadequately constrained project switch can benefit shareholders at the expense of creditors and total value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股东在偿债后取得上行剩余，而有限责任限制其下行损失。这种[[股债到期分配]]可能使股东与债权人对项目排序相反。作为融资后的[[道德风险]]机制，需要股东方能够选择或切换项目，而债权人无法用观察、重新定价或可执行条款充分控制这种选择；外生灾害造成风险上升本身不是该机制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Shareholders receive upside after debt service, while limited liability bounds their downside. This [[股债到期分配|terminal payoff allocation]] can reverse shareholders' and creditors' project rankings. As a post-financing [[道德风险|moral-hazard]] mechanism, it requires a project choice that creditors cannot adequately control through observation, repricing, or enforceable terms. An external disaster raising risk is not itself this mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设双方风险中性，两个项目投资成本相同，到期债额均已定为 80，无其他请求权和处置成本。安全项目必得 100；风险项目以各 50% 概率得到 180 或 0。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume risk-neutral parties, equal project investment costs, debt already fixed at 80, and no other claims or resolution costs. The safe project pays 100 for sure; the risky project pays 180 or zero with equal probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 项目 / Project | 期望资产 / Expected assets | 期望偿债 / Expected debt payment | 期望股权剩余 / Expected equity payoff |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 安全 / Safe | 100 | 80 | 20 |
| 风险 / Risky | 90 | 40 | 50 |

风险切换让股东期望剩余增加 30，债权人期望收回减少 40，总期望产出减少 10。这证明这样的利益冲突可能存在，不证明只要方差上升股权价值就必然增加。理性债权人若事前预见这种选择，会把它反映在放款价格和条件中；“风险转移激励”也不要求债权人一定受骗。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The switch raises expected equity payoff by 30, lowers expected debt payment by 40, and reduces total expected output by 10. This demonstrates a possible conflict, not a theorem that any variance increase raises equity value. Creditors anticipating the choice can price it into lending terms; risk-shifting incentives do not require creditors to be deceived.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
