# Tracking a left-nullspace basis through elimination
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*通过消元记录追踪左零空间基*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When two equations cancel during elimination, the resulting zero row tells us there was a relation among the original equations. To recover the coefficients of that relation, we must keep a record of the operations. Appending an [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] makes that record part of the calculation. The recorded combinations that produce zero give an entire basis of the original [[左零空间|left nullspace]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

消元中两个方程相消，得到的零行说明原方程之间存在关系。要恢复这条关系的系数，就需要保留操作记录。在右侧附上[[单位矩阵]]，就能让记录随计算一同进行。记录中产生零的组合，能给出原矩阵[[左零空间]]的一整组基。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A zero row records a cancellation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*零行记录了怎样的抵消*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with $A=(1,1)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Subtracting row $1$ from row $2$ produces a zero second row. Apply that same operation to both blocks:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看 $A=(1,1)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。用第 $2$ 行减第 $1$ 行，会得到零的第二行。对左右两块同步进行该操作：

$$
\left[\begin{array}{c|cc}1&1&0\\1&0&1\end{array}\right]
\longrightarrow
\left[\begin{array}{c|cc}1&1&0\\0&-1&1\end{array}\right],
\qquad
R=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix},\quad
E=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second row of $E$ says “minus original row $1$, plus original row $2$”. Its product with $A$ is zero. Thus the column $y=(-1,1)^T$ satisfies $y^TA=0$, equivalently $A^Ty=0$. It describes the same one-dimensional left nullspace as the basis vector $(1,-1)^T$. The zero row's position alone does not supply these original coefficients; the right-hand block does.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$E$ 的第二行表示“减去原第 $1$ 行，加上原第 $2$ 行”，它与 $A$ 的乘积为零。因此列向量 $y=(-1,1)^T$ 满足 $y^TA=0$，等价于 $A^Ty=0$。它与基向量 $(1,-1)^T$ 描述同一个一维左零空间。仅看零行的位置，得不到这些原始系数；右侧记录块才给出系数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Record the operations on a square identity block
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*用方形单位块记录全部操作*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$, with positive integers $m,n$. Reduce the left block to its [[行最简形|RREF]] $R$, applying each row swap, nonzero row scaling, or row addition to the whole augmented matrix. Each such [[初等矩阵|elementary operation]] is invertible. Their product $E$ therefore satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，$m,n$ 为正整数。将左侧矩阵化为[[行最简形]] $R$ 时，对整个增广矩阵同步进行换行、非零倍乘行或倍加行。每个这样的[[初等矩阵|初等操作]]都可逆，其乘积 $E$ 因而满足

$$
[A\mid I_m]\longrightarrow[R\mid E],\qquad
E[A\mid I_m]=[EA\mid E],\qquad R=EA.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both $I_m$ and $E$ are $m\times m$, even if $A$ is rectangular. The right block records the operations that reduce the **left block**; we do not continue reducing the augmented matrix merely to simplify $E$. In general $E$ is not an inverse of $A$: only when $A$ is square and invertible does its RREF equal $I_m$, giving $E=A^{-1}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使 $A$ 为矩形，$I_m$ 和 $E$ 也都是 $m\times m$。右侧记录的是化简**左侧矩阵**所用的操作，不要为了再化简 $E$ 而继续对整个增广矩阵做无关消元。通常 $E$ 不是 $A$ 的逆；只有 $A$ 是可逆方阵时，其行最简形才是 $I_m$，从而 $E=A^{-1}$。

> [!important] Select rows by the zero positions in R · 按 R 的零行位置取行
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $r=\operatorname{rank}(A)$, the [[矩阵秩|rank]] or number of pivots. The bottom $m-r$ rows of $R$ are zero. Write $E_{i:}$ for the entire $i$th row of $E$. For each zero-row index $i$, transpose that recorded row to a column:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $r=\operatorname{rank}(A)$，即[[矩阵秩|秩]]或主元数。$R$ 最下面 $m-r$ 行为零，用 $E_{i:}$ 表示 $E$ 的第 $i$ 个完整行。对每个零行编号 $i$，将对应记录行转置为列：
>
> $$y_i=(E_{i:})^T,\qquad i=r+1,\ldots,m.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These $m-r$ columns form a [[基|basis]] of $N(A^T)$. If $r=m$, there are no such rows: the left nullspace is $\{0\}$ and the basis is empty.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这 $m-r$ 个列向量构成 $N(A^T)$ 的一组[[基]]。若 $r=m$，没有这样的行；此时左零空间为 $\{0\}$，其基为空向量组。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why these directions give the whole left nullspace
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么这一步给出左零方向

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, a selected row satisfies $y_i^TA=E_{i:}A=R_{i:}=0$, so it really is a left-null direction. A nonzero row of $R$ does not give such a direction. Second, the selected rows of the invertible matrix $E$ are independent: a dependence among a subset would be a dependence among all its rows. Transposing preserves that independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

首先，被选中的行满足 $y_i^TA=E_{i:}A=R_{i:}=0$，确实给出左零方向；$R$ 的非零行所对应的记录行则不满足这一条件。其次，可逆矩阵 $E$ 中被选出的行彼此独立，因为一个子集中的线性关系也会成为全部行之间的线性关系；转置保持这种独立性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove **spanning**, take any column $y$ with $y^TA=0$ and define the row $c^T=y^TE^{-1}$. Then $c^TR=y^TA=0$. The first $r$ rows of $R$ are independent: in pivot column $p_j$, this last equation reads $c_j=0$. Hence $c_1=\cdots=c_r=0$. Multiplying $c^T=y^TE^{-1}$ by $E$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了证明**张成**，取任意满足 $y^TA=0$ 的列向量 $y$，定义行向量 $c^T=y^TE^{-1}$。于是 $c^TR=y^TA=0$。$R$ 的前 $r$ 个行彼此独立：观察第 $j$ 个主元列 $p_j$，这个等式直接给出 $c_j=0$，因此 $c_1=\cdots=c_r=0$。将 $c^T=y^TE^{-1}$ 右乘 $E$，得到

$$
y^T=c^TE=\sum_{i=r+1}^{m}c_iE_{i:},
\qquad y=\sum_{i=r+1}^{m}c_i y_i.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So every left-null vector is a combination of the selected vectors. This proves completeness rather than merely checking that several vectors multiply to zero. The count also agrees with $\dim N(A^T)=m-r$, as described by the [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity theorem]] and the [[四个基本子空间|four fundamental subspaces]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此每个左零向量都是所选向量的组合。这里证明了完整性，而不只是核对几个向量相乘得到零。向量个数也与 $\dim N(A^T)=m-r$ 一致，可对照[[秩零度定理]]及[[四个基本子空间]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A larger example keeps the same meaning
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*更大的例子仍在记录相同的关系*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the matrix used in [[RREF读取零空间基|the right-nullspace construction]], the recorded product is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[RREF读取零空间基]]中使用的矩阵，记录的乘积为

$$
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}-1&2&0\\1&-1&0\\-1&0&1\end{pmatrix}}_{E}
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3&1\\1&1&2&1\\1&2&3&1\end{pmatrix}}_{A}
=
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}1&0&1&1\\0&1&1&0\\0&0&0&0\end{pmatrix}}_{R}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One way to obtain it is to subtract row $1$ from rows $2$ and $3$, negate row $2$, and then subtract twice the new row $2$ from row $1$, always updating the identity block too. Only the third row of $R$ is zero. Thus $(-1,0,1)^T$ is a left-nullspace basis: original row $3$ equals original row $1$. There are three equations and two pivots, so precisely one independent relation remains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一种实现方式是：第 $2,3$ 行分别减去第 $1$ 行，再将第 $2$ 行乘以 $-1$，最后用第 $1$ 行减去新第 $2$ 行的两倍；每步也同步作用于单位块。$R$ 只有第三行为零，所以左零空间的一组基为 $(-1,0,1)^T$，它表示原第 $3$ 行等于原第 $1$ 行。三个方程中有两个主元，因此恰好剩下一条独立关系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the record preserves
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*记录保留了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Row reduction need not preserve the original left-null directions. In the opening example, $N(A^T)$ is the span of $(1,-1)^T$, whereas $N(R^T)$ is the span of $(0,1)^T$. Moreover, $A'=(1,2)^T$ has the same RREF $R$, but its left nullspace is spanned by $(-2,1)^T$. Thus $R$ alone cannot determine the original directions. Some operations leave them unchanged; [[行操作与四个子空间|preservation cannot be assumed in general]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

消元未必保持原来的左零方向。开头的例子中，$N(A^T)$ 由 $(1,-1)^T$ 张成，而 $N(R^T)$ 由 $(0,1)^T$ 张成。另外，$A'=(1,2)^T$ 具有相同的行最简形 $R$，左零空间却由 $(-2,1)^T$ 张成。因此仅凭 $R$ 不能确定原来的方向。有些操作会使它保持不变，但[[行操作与四个子空间|一般不能假定保持]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the original $A$ remains available, solving $A^Ty=0$ directly also works. Tracking $E$ reuses the elimination and identifies relations among the original equations. Different valid operation sequences can give different bases of the same left nullspace. For a nonhomogeneous system $Ax=b$, these relations become the [[左零空间相容性|compatibility tests]] $y_i^Tb=0$; checking this entire basis tests every left-null relation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果还保留原矩阵 $A$，直接求解 $A^Ty=0$ 也可以。追踪 $E$ 的价值在于复用消元，并直接读出原方程之间的关系。不同的合法操作序列可以给出同一左零空间的不同基。对非齐次系统 $Ax=b$，这些关系成为[[左零空间相容性|相容性检验]] $y_i^Tb=0$；检验这整组基，就覆盖了全部左零关系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Complex entries require a conjugation choice
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*复数情形须区分是否取共轭*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over $\mathbb C$, the same elimination gives $R=EA$. If a selected recorded row is $w^T$, then $w^TA=0$, so its ordinary transpose $w$ belongs to the algebraic kernel $N(A^T)$. For the standard complex [[内积|inner product]], however, the geometric left nullspace is $N(A^*)$, where $*$ means [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]. Its basis is obtained by taking the **conjugate transpose of each selected row**: use $\overline w$, since $(\overline w)^*=w^T$. A bar conjugates each complex entry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复数域上同样有 $R=EA$。若选中的记录行为 $w^T$，则 $w^TA=0$，所以普通转置得到的 $w$ 属于代数上的核 $N(A^T)$。但在标准复[[内积]]下，几何左零空间为 $N(A^*)$，其中 $*$ 表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]。此时应将每条被选中的记录行作**共轭转置**：使用 $\overline w$，因为 $(\overline w)^*=w^T$；横线表示对每个复数元素取共轭。

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1\\i\end{pmatrix},\quad
E=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-i&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
EA=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad i^2=-1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the recorded row is $(-i,1)$. Ordinary transpose gives $(-i,1)^T\in N(A^T)$; conjugate transpose gives $(i,1)^T\in N(A^*)$, because $(1,-i)(i,1)^T=0$. The same algebraic proof works over $\mathbb C$; conjugating the resulting basis gives independence and spanning for this Hermitian convention as well; see [[基本子空间正交补|the complex orthogonal-complement identities]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

此时记录行为 $(-i,1)$。普通转置得到 $(-i,1)^T\in N(A^T)$，共轭转置则得到 $(i,1)^T\in N(A^*)$，因为 $(1,-i)(i,1)^T=0$。上述代数证明在复数域上同样成立；对所得基取共轭，也能得到 Hermitian 约定下的独立与张成；可对照[[基本子空间正交补|复数正交补恒等式]]。
