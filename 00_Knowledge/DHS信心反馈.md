# 有偏自我归因可让确认消息进一步强化私人信号权重并产生持续过度反应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Biased self-attribution can reinforce private-signal weights and generate continuing overreaction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 DHS 的动态扩展中，投资者把确认原判断的结果当作能力证据，却不因反面结果同等程度地降低信心。于是新公告不仅提供新信息，还可能放大他对旧私人信号的相信程度，使价格沿原方向继续偏离。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The dynamic extension lets confirming outcomes increase confidence more than disconfirming ones reduce it. An announcement can therefore amplify belief in an earlier private signal, extending the initial price deviation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

想象投资者已因私人研究买入，随后公告与其方向一致。他可以一边更新基本面，一边更相信“我的研究特别准”。后一个变化就是此卡的反馈，而非单纯获知好消息。后续信息足够纠偏时，早先偏离又会逆转；这不是每条价格路径必然呈驼峰。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A buyer receiving confirming news may update fundamentals and also become more convinced of their research skill. The latter is the feedback. Later correction can reverse the deviation; no individual realized path is required to be hump-shaped.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，持续上涨未必表示价格还没有到达合理水平：它也可能表示过度反应仍在积累。单看收益同向延续分不清二者，见[[动量识别边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuation can reflect accumulating overreaction rather than incomplete adjustment; see [[动量识别边界|the identification limit of momentum]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sections III–IV establish the model-conditional dynamic mechanism, not an unconditional trading rule.
