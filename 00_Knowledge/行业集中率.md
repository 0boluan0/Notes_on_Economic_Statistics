# 行业集中率是界定市场中份额最大的若干经营者的合计份额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A concentration ratio is the combined market share of a specified number of the largest operators in a defined market.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将同口径的份额由大到小排列为 $s_{(1)}\geq\cdots\geq s_{(N)}\geq0$，总和为 1。前 $n$ 家的行业集中率为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Order consistently measured shares as $s_{(1)}\geq\cdots\geq s_{(N)}\geq0$, summing to one. The concentration ratio of the largest $n$ firms is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$CR_n=\sum_{i=1}^ns_{(i)},\qquad 1\leq n\leq N.$$

例如，六家份额为 30%、25%、20%、10%、10%、5%，则 $CR_4=85\%$。须同时报告 $n$；$CR_4$ 与 $CR_8$ 不是可直接混用的同一数字。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For shares of 30%, 25%, 20%, 10%, 10%, and 5%, $CR_4=85\%$. Report $n$: $CR_4$ and $CR_8$ are not interchangeable statistics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集中率不完整记录组内份额分布。例如，两家合占 60%，可以是 30% 与 30%，也可以是 50% 与 10%；同一个 $CR_2$ 不区分这两种结构。需要全体分布时可另算[[HHI指数]]，仍不能跳过[[集中度与竞争强度|竞争行为和进入条件]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A concentration ratio does not fully describe shares within the selected group. A combined 60% may mean 30% plus 30%, or 50% plus 10%; the same $CR_2$ cannot distinguish them. An [[HHI指数|HHI]] uses the full distribution, but [[集中度与竞争强度|conduct and entry conditions]] still require analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [OpenStax, Principles of Microeconomics 3e, §11.1, The Four-Firm Concentration Ratio / The Herfindahl-Hirschman Index](https://openstax.org/books/principles-microeconomics-3e/pages/11-1-corporate-mergers)：核对最大企业份额相加的定义及集中率相同而分布不同的局限。$CR_n$ 记号和本卡数例为通用定义的直接整理，已复算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
OpenStax supports adding the largest firms' shares and shows that identical concentration ratios can conceal different distributions. The $CR_n$ notation directly generalizes that definition; the examples were independently checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
