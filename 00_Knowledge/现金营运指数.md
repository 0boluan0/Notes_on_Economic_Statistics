# 现金营运指数是经营活动现金流量净额与明确调整口径下经营所得现金的比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cash operating index divides net operating cash flow by cash generated from operations under an explicitly stated adjustment basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金营运指数用实际 CFO 对照利润经过非经营和非现金调整后、尚未扣除营运资本占用的金额。沿本课程的简化口径，设净利润为 $NI$、非经营净收益为 $G$、尚未在 $G$ 中处理的非付现费用净调整为 $NC$，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This index compares actual CFO with profit adjusted for non-operating and non-cash items before working-capital absorption. In the course's simplified convention, let $NI$ be net income, $G$ net non-operating gains, and $NC$ the net non-cash expense adjustment not already dealt with in $G$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$C^*=NI-G+NC,\qquad I_C=\frac{CFO}{C^*},\quad C^*\ne0.$$

这里的“经营所得现金” $C^*$ 是分析调节额，不是银行已经收到的另一笔现金。应按[[现金流量表]]的调节表逐项界定，避免非现金处置损益同时在 $G$ 和 $NC$ 中调整两次；准则分类或税项处理不同，也可能需要其他调节，不能把这条简式当完整现金流量表。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Cash generated” $C^*$ is an analytical reconciliation amount, not a separate bank receipt. Define adjustments from the [[现金流量表|cash-flow reconciliation]], avoiding double adjustment of disposal gains in both $G$ and $NC$. Classification and tax differences can require further items; the simple expression does not replace a complete statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $NI=100$，含处置收益 20，另有折旧 30；没有其他调节时，$C^*=110$。若按 CFO 调节范围计算的非现金经营营运资本增加 40，则 CFO 为 70，指数为 $70/110\approx0.636$。此处只计入已调节非现金交易和重分类的存货、经营应收减经营应付等净变动，排除现金及现金等价物本身与融资性债务，不直接采用管理用报表可能包含经营现金的[[经营营运资本]]口径。这说明本期有现金被营运资本占用；应收能否收回、备货是否合理，还需[[盈利质量核查]]。指数小于 1 本身不能证明利润虚假，大于 1 也可能来自收回旧应收或延迟付款。分母为零时不定义；分母为负时，不沿用“越大越好”的排序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $NI=100$, disposal gains of 20, and depreciation of 30 with no other adjustments, $C^*=110$. An increase of 40 in non-cash operating working capital within the CFO reconciliation leaves CFO of 70 and an index of approximately 0.636. This change concerns inventories and operating receivables less operating payables after non-cash and reclassification adjustments, excluding cash, cash equivalents, and financing debt, unlike management-reporting definitions of [[经营营运资本|operating working capital]] that may include operating cash. This identifies cash absorption, not whether receivables or inventory are sound; apply [[盈利质量核查|earnings-quality checks]]. Values below one do not prove false earnings, and values above one may reflect old receivable collections or delayed payments. Zero and negative denominators do not support ordinary ratio ranking.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
