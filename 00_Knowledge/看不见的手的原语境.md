# 《国富论》的看不见的手说明私人资本配置可能产生非意图的公共结果，而非保证一切自利行为最优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The invisible hand in The Wealth of Nations concerns unintended public consequences of private capital allocation, not the optimality of every self-interested action*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

《国富论》使用“看不见的手”时，正在讨论**进口限制与私人资本投向**：为了保护本国产业而给予垄断地位，是否真的让整个社会的产业和收入增加？Smith 的回答通过投资者的具体选择展开，不是从一句“人会自利”直接跳到“所有市场结果都最好”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The phrase appears in a discussion of **import restrictions and the direction of private capital**: does granting domestic producers protection from foreign competition actually increase society's industry and revenue? Smith develops his answer through investors' particular choices rather than deriving universal optimality from self-interest alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

他的论证包含两步。第一，在利润相等或差别不大时，投资者为了资金安全、便于观察和寻求法律保护，更倾向把资本留在熟悉的本国用途。第二，资本既然投向本国产业，追求利润又使经营者寻找产出交换价值较高的用途。Smith 把这两步与本国年产出的交换价值及年收入联系起来：投资者首先打算增加自己的收入，却可能同时促成并未刻意追求的公共结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The argument has two steps. First, at equal or nearly equal profits, investors favour familiar domestic uses for the security of their funds, ease of supervision, and access to legal remedies. Second, within domestic industry, the pursuit of profit directs capital toward uses whose output has greater exchangeable value. Smith connects these choices with the exchangeable value of national annual output and annual revenue: a person pursuing private gain may help bring about a public consequence that was not intended.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这段话的直接语境很具体，但 Smith 也说类似的非意图结果会出现在其他场合。因此，既不必把它限制成一个孤立的贸易小故事，也不应删掉论证条件后把它扩成“任何自利行为都增进所有人的福利”。本国年收入的判断，本身还没有回答收入归谁、每个人是否受益以及分配是否公正。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The immediate setting is specific, though Smith also says unintended consequences occur in other cases. The passage need not be reduced to an isolated trade anecdote, but neither should its conditions be removed to make it say that every self-interested action improves everyone's welfare. A claim about national annual revenue does not by itself establish who receives that income, whether everyone gains, or whether its distribution is just.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把它放回全书还会看到：Smith 分析[[垄断与自然价格|垄断如何阻碍竞争调整]]，也为[[Smith公共职责|国防、司法和公共工程]]保留明确职责。用“看不见的手”替代这些制度分析，反而会把他的解释删掉。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Elsewhere in the book, Smith examines [[垄断与自然价格|how monopoly obstructs competitive adjustment]] and assigns explicit responsibilities for [[Smith公共职责|defence, justice, and public works]]. Replacing that institutional analysis with the slogan would remove part of his explanation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现代[[第一福利定理]]则是另一个精确问题：在模型条件下，给定的竞争均衡是否 Pareto 有效？它有自己的假设和证明，也不保证分配公平或现实价格会收敛。它可以与 Smith 的问题相互参照，但不是那句比喻的同义改写。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The modern [[第一福利定理|First Welfare Theorem]] asks a different, precise question: under the model's conditions, is a given competitive equilibrium Pareto efficient? It has its own assumptions and proof, and guarantees neither distributive justice nor convergence of actual prices. It can be compared with Smith's question without being treated as a synonymous restatement of the metaphor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
