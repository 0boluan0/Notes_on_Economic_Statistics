# Linear independence versus spanning
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*线性无关与张成*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When we build a vector by multiplying given vectors by numbers and adding the results, there are two separate questions: **can we reach the desired vector, and can two different coefficient lists reach the same vector?** [[张成|Spanning]] answers the first question for every vector in a specified target space. [[线性无关|Linear independence]] rules out different coefficient lists producing the same result. Knowing one answer does not settle the other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把给定向量分别乘上数字，再把结果相加时，有两个不同的问题：**想要的向量能不能拼出来，同一个向量会不会有两套不同的系数？** [[张成]]回答指定目标空间中的每个向量是否都能拼出；[[线性无关]]排除不同系数列表产生同一结果的情况。知道其中一个答案，并不能确定另一个。

## 最小对照
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*One example separates coverage from uniqueness*

Take $u_1=(1,0,0)$ and $u_2=(0,1,0)$ in $\mathbb R^3$. A coefficient multiplies a whole vector: $c_1$ determines the contribution from $u_1$, and $c_2$ the contribution from $u_2$. Their combinations are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\mathbb R^3$ 中取 $u_1=(1,0,0)$、$u_2=(0,1,0)$。一个系数乘的是一个完整向量：$c_1$ 决定使用多少份 $u_1$，$c_2$ 决定使用多少份 $u_2$。它们的组合为

$$c_1u_1+c_2u_2=(c_1,c_2,0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To obtain $(2,3,0)$, the coefficients must be $c_1=2,c_2=3$; there is no second choice. More generally, every reachable vector $(a,b,0)$ forces $c_1=a,c_2=b$. Thus the pair is independent. But $(0,0,1)$ is unreachable because every combination has last coordinate zero. The pair spans the $xy$-plane $S=\{(a,b,0):a,b\in\mathbb R\}$, but it does **not** span $\mathbb R^3$. Always state which space must be covered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要得到 $(2,3,0)$，只能取 $c_1=2,c_2=3$，没有第二种选择。更一般地，每个能到达的 $(a,b,0)$ 都迫使 $c_1=a,c_2=b$，所以这对向量无关。但 $(0,0,1)$ 无法到达，因为任何组合的最后一个坐标都是零。这对向量张成 $xy$ 平面 $S=\{(a,b,0):a,b\in\mathbb R\}$，却**不**张成 $\mathbb R^3$。必须说明要覆盖哪个空间。

> [!example] Add a redundant vector · 加入一个冗余向量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Keep the target space $S$ and add $u_3=(1,1,0)=u_1+u_2$. The three vectors still span $S$, since the first two already reach every $(a,b,0)$. However, the target $(1,1,0)$ now has coefficient lists $(1,1,0)$ and $(0,0,1)$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 保持目标空间为 $S$，再加入 $u_3=(1,1,0)=u_1+u_2$。三个向量仍张成 $S$，因为前两个已经能到达每个 $(a,b,0)$。可是目标 $(1,1,0)$ 现在有两套系数 $(1,1,0)$ 和 $(0,0,1)$：
>
> $$1u_1+1u_2+0u_3=0u_1+0u_2+1u_3.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Coverage survives, but independence fails. The third vector adds another way to make existing targets, without adding a new reachable direction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 覆盖性保留了，无关性却消失了。第三个向量增加了拼出已有目标的方法，没有增加新的可达方向。

## The guarantees in general
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一般情况下各自保证什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $v_1,\ldots,v_k$ lie in a vector space $S$ over a fixed scalar field $\mathbb F$, such as the real numbers. We ask for coefficients $c_1,\ldots,c_k\in\mathbb F$ satisfying $b=c_1v_1+\cdots+c_kv_k$. The target is $b$; the unknowns are the coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $v_1,\ldots,v_k$ 属于固定标量域 $\mathbb F$（例如实数）上的向量空间 $S$。我们寻找系数 $c_1,\ldots,c_k\in\mathbb F$，使 $b=c_1v_1+\cdots+c_kv_k$。$b$ 是目标，系数是未知量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Property of the list | Guarantee | What remains undecided |
|---|---|---|
| Linearly independent | Every target has **at most one** coefficient representation | Some targets may have none |
| Spans $S$ | Every $b\in S$ has **at least one** representation | A target may have several |
| Both: a basis of $S$ | Every $b\in S$ has **exactly one** representation | Both questions are settled |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无关保证每个目标**至多一种**表示，允许根本没有；张成 $S$ 保证每个 $b\in S$ **至少一种**表示，允许不唯一；两者同时成立，就是 $S$ 的一组[[基]]，每个目标**恰好一种**表示。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why does independence give uniqueness? If two coefficient lists produce $b$, subtract their representations. The coefficient differences then combine the vectors to zero. Independence forces every difference to be zero, so the lists were identical. This argument never produces a representation for an unreachable target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无关为什么能给出唯一性？假如两套系数都产生 $b$，把两种表示相减，系数之差就把这些向量组合成零。无关性迫使每个差值为零，所以两套系数原本相同。这个论证不会替无法到达的目标创造出一种表示。

## Reading the distinction in a matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把区别读成矩阵方程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For vectors with $m$ coordinates, place the $k$ vectors in the columns of an $m\times k$ matrix $A$, and collect the coefficients into $c=(c_1,\ldots,c_k)^T$. Then the same question is $Ac=b$. The [[零空间|nullspace]] $N(A)$ contains the coefficient vectors producing zero; the [[列空间|column space]] $C(A)$ contains all reachable outputs. Independence is $N(A)=\{0\}$, while spanning $S$ is $C(A)=S$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于具有 $m$ 个坐标的向量，把这 $k$ 个向量放成 $m\times k$ 矩阵 $A$ 的列，把系数收集为 $c=(c_1,\ldots,c_k)^T$，同一个问题就写成 $Ac=b$。[[零空间]] $N(A)$ 收集产生零的系数向量；[[列空间]] $C(A)$ 收集所有可达输出。无关对应 $N(A)=\{0\}$，张成 $S$ 对应 $C(A)=S$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In [[矩阵秩|rank]] language, full column rank means rank $k$ and gives uniqueness whenever a solution exists. Full row rank means rank $m$ and makes every $b\in\mathbb F^m$ reachable. A tall matrix ($m>k$) can have full column rank while missing output directions, as the two three-coordinate columns above do. A wide matrix ($m<k$) can have full row rank but has dependent columns; for example, the columns $(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)$ cover $\mathbb R^2$ with redundancy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用[[矩阵秩]]表达，满列秩表示秩为 $k$，保证有解时唯一；满行秩表示秩为 $m$，保证每个 $b\in\mathbb F^m$ 都可达。高矩阵（$m>k$）可以满列秩，却缺少输出方向，前面那两个三坐标列向量就是例子。宽矩阵（$m<k$）可以满行秩，但列必定相关；例如 $(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)$ 三列覆盖 $\mathbb R^2$，同时存在冗余。

> [!question]- Check the missing guarantee · 检查缺少哪项保证
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The columns of a $3\times2$ matrix are independent. Must $Ac=b$ have a solution for every $b\in\mathbb R^3$? **No.** Independence prevents repeated coefficient representations; two columns cannot span all of $\mathbb R^3$. The first example gives a target that is missed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个 $3\times2$ 矩阵的列无关，是否每个 $b\in\mathbb R^3$ 都使 $Ac=b$ 有解？**不是。** 无关性排除重复的系数表示；两列无法张成整个 $\mathbb R^3$。第一个例子就给出了无法到达的目标。
