# 跨行支付用准备金在银行间结算并通常只重分配体系余额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An interbank payment is settled through reserve or correspondent balances and normally redistributes rather than creates system balances*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子机制
> 在最简单的直接准备金结算中，付款人与收款人在不同银行时，付款行减少客户存款负债和自身准备金资产；收款行增加准备金资产和客户存款负债。忽略费用、信用新增、现金转换及其他交易时，公众存款与既定准备金持有人集合内的准备金总量不变，只是持有人改变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the simplest direct reserve settlement, when payer and recipient use different banks, the payer's bank reduces its customer's deposit liability and its own reserve asset; the recipient's bank increases its reserve asset and the customer's deposit liability. Ignoring fees, new credit, cash conversion, and other transactions, public deposits and total reserves within the specified set of reserve holders are unchanged; only their holders change.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Fedwire Funds Service 是逐笔实时全额结算：每条支付指令分别借记和贷记 Reserve Bank 账户，并在处理时完成最终结算。National Settlement Service 则由清算安排先计算多边净头寸，再把一份结算文件中的净借贷记入 master accounts，且入账为 final and irrevocable。若经 correspondent 结算，参与银行可能先改变其对 correspondent 的债权或负债，只有 correspondent 层面的央行账户需要入账。操作路径与汇总时点不同，因此不能假定每笔客户支付都立刻造成两家客户银行等额准备金移动；但公众存款与最终央行结算资产属于不同负债层级。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fedwire Funds Service is real-time gross settlement: each payment instruction separately debits and credits Reserve Bank accounts and becomes final when processed. The National Settlement Service instead lets a clearing arrangement calculate multilateral net positions before a settlement file posts net debits and credits to master accounts, with entries that are final and irrevocable. With correspondent settlement, participant banks may first change claims on or liabilities to the correspondent, and only the correspondent-level central-bank accounts need post. Because the route and aggregation time differ, one must not assume that every customer payment immediately moves equal reserves between the two customer banks; public deposits and the ultimate central-bank settlement asset nevertheless remain liabilities at different institutional layers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 在最简单的直接准备金结算中，单家付款行准备金下降不表示体系准备金被销毁；收款行等额增加。经净额或 correspondent 结算时，应改看实际入账机构和净额。
- “体系总量不变”只适用于先固定的 reserve-balance holder 集合与观察时点，并要求支付没有跨到 Federal Reserve 的 Treasury、foreign official 或其他非准备金负债，也没有央行操作、现金转换或结算失败等伴随事件。
- 总存款不变是对纯支付的结论；若支付来自新贷款、还贷、手续费或取现，体系存款可以变化。
- 使用 correspondent 时，客户银行之间可以先在 correspondent 的账簿上结算；央行准备金的移动发生在哪一层、何时发生，必须沿实际结算链识别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- In the simplest direct reserve settlement, a fall in the payer bank's reserves does not mean system reserves were destroyed; the recipient bank gains the same amount. Under net or correspondent settlement, inspect the institutions and net amounts actually posted.
- “Unchanged system reserves” applies only after fixing the reserve-balance-holder set and observation time, and only when the payment does not cross into Treasury, foreign-official, or other non-reserve Federal Reserve liabilities and is not accompanied by a central-bank operation, currency conversion, or settlement failure.
- Unchanged total deposits is a conclusion for a pure transfer. New lending, repayment, fees, or cash withdrawal can change system deposits.
- With a correspondent, the customer banks may first settle on the correspondent's books. Trace the actual chain to identify at which layer, and when, reserve balances move.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 在最简单的直接准备金结算中，纯跨行工资支付后，为什么付款行“缺了一笔准备金”却不能说既定持有人集合的总准备金下降？
>
> **答案：** 同额准备金被贷记给收款行；付款行的减少与收款行的增加在体系层面抵消。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the simplest direct reserve settlement, why does the payer bank's reserve outflow after a pure interbank salary payment not mean reserves fell across the specified holder set?
>
> **Answer:** The same reserve amount is credited to the recipient bank, so the fall at one bank is offset at system level by the rise at the other.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
