# 固定正权重和边际波动下较低相关性使两资产组合的方差降低
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With fixed positive weights and marginal volatilities, lower correlation reduces two-asset portfolio variance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两项资产权重为 $w$ 和 $1-w$，且 $0<w<1$、$\sigma_A,\sigma_B>0$ 时，保持权重与边际波动不变，相关系数 $\rho$ 越低，组合方差越小：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two assets with weights strictly between zero and one and positive standard deviations, holding weights and marginal volatilities fixed makes portfolio variance increase with correlation:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\sigma_p^2=w^2\sigma_A^2+(1-w)^2\sigma_B^2+2w(1-w)\rho\sigma_A\sigma_B,$$
$$\frac{\partial\sigma_p^2}{\partial\rho}=2w(1-w)\sigma_A\sigma_B>0.$$

因此只要 $\rho<1$，组合标准差就严格小于**标准差的加权平均** $w\sigma_A+(1-w)\sigma_B$。注意比较基准：这并不保证它低于两项资产中较小的标准差，也不保证提高期望收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $\rho<1$, portfolio standard deviation is strictly below the weighted average of the two standard deviations. That comparison does not guarantee less risk than the safer asset, or a higher expected return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如两项波动均为 $20\%$、各持一半，$\rho=1,0,-1$ 时标准差依次为 $20\%,14.14\%,0$。若改成 $\sigma_A=10\%,\sigma_B=30\%,\rho=0$，等权波动约 $15.81\%$，仍高于只持 A 的 $10\%$。分散化优势要说明“相对谁”，再用[[均值方差支配]]比较收益是否也合适。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal positions in two assets with $20\%$ volatility have portfolio volatility $20\%,14.14\%,0$ at correlations $1,0,-1$. But uncorrelated assets with volatilities $10\%$ and $30\%$ produce about $15.81\%$ at equal weights—more than holding the safer asset alone. Use [[均值方差支配|mean–variance dominance]] to compare return as well as risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一多一空，$w(1-w)<0$，上述导数变号：此时降低相关性反而可能提高风险。完全相关时哪些权重能精确抵消波动，由[[完全相关的风险抵消]]单独处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a long–short position the weight product is negative and the derivative changes sign. Lower correlation can then increase risk. [[完全相关的风险抵消|Exact cancellation under perfect correlation]] requires its own weight calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
