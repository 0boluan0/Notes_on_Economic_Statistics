# 交易成本是为寻找交易方形成协议并监督执行交易而付出的资源
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Transaction costs are resources used to find counterparties, reach agreement, and monitor and enforce transactions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交易成本是为了使一项交易能够发生并按约执行而耗用的时间、信息和其他资源。寻找受影响的人、核实谁造成损害、讨价还价、写合同、测量履约情况与处理违约，都可能属于这类成本。它不同于协议本身约定转给对方的款项，也不同于实施减排所需的设备资源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Transaction costs are the time, information and other resources needed to make and enforce a transaction. They can include identifying affected parties, verifying causation, negotiating, contracting, monitoring and addressing breach. They differ from the transfer agreed between parties and from the equipment used to abate emissions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如一项调整能避免30的损害，但让工厂损失20，原本有10的共同增益。如果为这份协议核实信息、谈判和执行要再耗用12，即使能找到[[外部性谈判区间|表面上双方可接受的付款]]，实施后的总增益也为负。若其他条件相同且交易成本降至3，才留下7供双方分配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Avoiding damage of thirty at a forgone private benefit of twenty creates ten of gains before transaction costs. Spending twelve on verification, negotiation and enforcement makes the agreement unprofitable jointly. Reducing those costs to three leaves seven to share.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这解释了为什么[[科斯定理|无成本交易的基准]]不能直接代替制度选择。集中登记和共同代理可能节约重复谈判，却也可能有自己的组织成本；是否值得应与可行替代相比。采用外包服务时，支付金额和其背后的社会资源耗用还要按[[转移支付核算]]分别处理，不能只把所有付款相加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[科斯定理|costless benchmark]] does not rank real institutions. Registries or collective representation may reduce repeated negotiation but have organisational costs of their own. Compare feasible alternatives and use [[转移支付核算|transfer accounting]] to distinguish purchased services' payments from their social resource costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
