# 外部融资需求是经营扩张扣除自然负债融资和内部可用来源后仍需筹集的资金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
External financing needed is the funding gap after spontaneous operating liabilities and available internal sources have supported the operating plan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在销售百分比融资模型中，外部融资需求（EFN）是企业为满足预计经营资金占用，在自然增加的经营负债、当期利润留存及可动用金融资产之外，还需要从借款或股权筹资获得的金额。这里“外部”**不包括已扣除的应付账款等自然经营融资**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the sales-based financing model, EFN is the additional borrowing or equity funding required after spontaneous operating liabilities, retained current income, and available financial assets support the operating plan. “External” here excludes the spontaneous operating financing already deducted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $U$ 为不影响经营及最低现金需要、确实可以动用的金融资产，$NI_1$ 为预测净利润，$b$ 为[[利润留存率]]。在没有其他权益变动和资金用途的简化模型中：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $U$ be financial assets genuinely available without compromising operations or minimum cash, $NI_1$ forecast net income, and $b$ the [[利润留存率|retention ratio]]. With no other equity changes or funding uses:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$EFN=\Delta OA-\Delta OL-U-bNI_1=\Delta NOA-U-bNI_1.$$

经营资产增加 150、经营负债增加 30、可用金融资产 10、留存利润 40 时，EFN 为 70。新增借款 70 或借款与增资合计 70，都能在这个模型里填补缺口，但融资成本、契约和股权变化并不相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An operating-asset increase of 150, operating-liability increase of 30, available financial assets of 10, and retained income of 40 produce EFN of 70. Borrowing 70 or combining debt and equity can close the accounting gap, but their costs, covenants, and ownership effects differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

算出负值表示在既定假设下有融资剩余，并非必须借入负数；可据此研究还债、保留流动性或分配。算出年度 EFN 为零也不保证全年随时有钱：[[留存收益不是现金]]，年度资金平衡还要经过[[现金预算]]的收付时间检查。EFN 是资金需要，不是银行已经批准的额度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A negative result means a modelled funding surplus, not a requirement to borrow a negative amount. It can inform repayment, liquidity, or distribution choices. Zero annual EFN does not ensure liquidity throughout the year: [[留存收益不是现金|retained earnings are not cash]], and a [[现金预算|cash budget]] must check timing. A funding requirement is not an approved credit facility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
