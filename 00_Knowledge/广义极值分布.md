# 广义极值分布是由位置、尺度和形状参数刻画的三类极值分布族
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The generalized extreme-value family describes three extremal types through location, scale, and shape parameters*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

广义极值分布（GEV）是下列 CDF 定义的分布族，其中位置参数 $\mu\in\mathbb R$、尺度参数 $\sigma>0$、形状参数 $\xi\in\mathbb R$。令 $z=(x-\mu)/\sigma$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The generalized extreme-value distribution is the family defined by the following CDF, with location $\mu\in\mathbb R$, scale $\sigma>0$, and shape $\xi\in\mathbb R$. Write $z=(x-\mu)/\sigma$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_{\xi,\mu,\sigma}(x)=
\begin{cases}
\exp\!\left[-(1+\xi z)^{-1/\xi}\right],&\xi\ne0,\quad1+\xi z>0,\\
\exp[-e^{-z}],&\xi=0,\quad x\in\mathbb R.
\end{cases}
$$

完整 CDF 还包括支持边界：$\xi>0$ 时，对 $x\le\mu-\sigma/\xi$ 置 0；$\xi<0$ 时，对 $x\ge\mu-\sigma/\xi$ 置 1。三种形状分别是 Fréchet 型（$\xi>0$）、Gumbel 型（$\xi=0$）和反向 Weibull 型（$\xi<0$，右端点有限）。这里的反向 Weibull 是最大值极限的类型，不等同于通常定义在非负半轴的寿命 Weibull 分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The complete CDF includes its support boundaries: set it to zero for $x\le\mu-\sigma/\xi$ when $\xi>0$, and to one for $x\ge\mu-\sigma/\xi$ when $\xi<0$. The types are Fréchet ($\xi>0$), Gumbel ($\xi=0$), and reversed Weibull ($\xi<0$, finite right endpoint). The reversed-Weibull extremal type is not the usual nonnegative Weibull lifetime distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\mu=0,\sigma=2,\xi=-1/2$ 时，右端点是 4，$H(0)=e^{-1}$，而 $H(4)=1$。这是一条具体分布定义；母分布的样本最大值是否能归一化到该类型，则是[[极大值吸引域]]与[[极大值极限定理]]的问题。母分布属于 Gumbel 吸引域，也不意味着母分布本身是 Gumbel 或指数分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\mu=0,\sigma=2,\xi=-1/2$, the right endpoint is 4, with $H(0)=e^{-1}$ and $H(4)=1$. This defines a particular distribution. Whether a parent distribution's normalised maxima approach that type is a question about its [[极大值吸引域|maximum domain of attraction]] and the [[极大值极限定理|extremal types theorem]]. Membership in the Gumbel domain does not make the parent distribution Gumbel or exponential.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Sources and verification*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Haugh's notes, PDF pp. 4–5 and 12, support the three-parameter CDF, types, and endpoints. The normal and lognormal examples on p. 12 support distinguishing the parent law from its extremal limit. CDF values outside the support are completed by endpoint limits; the numerical example is checked by substitution.
