# 有效联邦基金利率是申报隔夜联邦基金交易利率的成交量加权中位数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The effective federal funds rate is the volume-weighted median of reported overnight federal funds transaction rates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

纽约联储依据FR 2420申报的隔夜[[联邦基金利率|联邦基金交易]]计算有效联邦基金利率（EFFR），用一个统计指标概括前一营业日的实际交易。它是成交量加权中位数，不是简单平均，也不是央行目标区间的中点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The New York Fed computes EFFR from overnight [[联邦基金利率|federal funds trades]] reported on FR 2420, summarising actual transactions for the previous business day. It is a volume-weighted median, not a simple mean or target-range midpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设申报样本按利率从低到高为：3.80%成交20，4.00%成交60，4.10%成交20。总量100，累计量在4.00%越过50，所以加权中位数为4.00%。同一组的成交量加权平均却是3.98%；两种统计方法回答的问题不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose volumes are 20 at 3.80%, 60 at 4.00%, and 20 at 4.10%. Total volume is 100, and cumulative volume crosses 50 at 4.00%, the weighted median. The volume-weighted mean is instead 3.98%. The two summaries answer different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子只说明中位数的计算，刻意不让累计量恰好落在一半。使用实际官方时间序列时应沿其完整发布方法，而不是把教学样本算法当成官方所有边界处理。如果EFFR在目标区间内，也只能说明这个汇总统计量的位置，不能保证每笔交易都在区间内。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example isolates a median without a halfway tie. Actual official series follow the full publication methodology, not every edge case of this teaching calculation. An EFFR inside the target range does not ensure every transaction lies inside it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Federal Reserve Bank of New York，Effective Federal Funds Rate](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/effr)：FR 2420隔夜样本、成交量加权中位数与前一营业日指标；口径核验于2026-09-06。教学数例独立构造。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The New York Fed specifies the sample and weighted-median statistic. The numerical example is constructed and does not reproduce a historical fixing.
