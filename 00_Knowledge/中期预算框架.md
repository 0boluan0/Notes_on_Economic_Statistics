# 中期预算框架是在多年财政约束下连接政策成本预测、支出安排与年度预算的制度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A medium-term budget framework links policy-cost projections, expenditure plans and annual budgets within multi-year fiscal constraints.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中期预算框架使本年度选择与未来几年的成本和可用资源相衔接：先明确总体财政约束，再预测现行政策成本、识别政策空间，并将支出安排落实到年度预算。不同制度中，年度预算之后的年份可以采用指示性计划，也可以受较强的支出上限约束，需要明确其效力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A medium-term budget framework links today's choices to future costs and resources. Aggregate fiscal constraints guide current-policy projections, available policy space and annual allocations. Outer-year plans may be indicative or subject to stronger ceilings; their force must be specified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，新医院建设当年支付建设款，未来还要承担人员、维护与设备更新成本。只列建设年份会低估持续负担；把这些成本纳入多年基线，才能与未来上限对照。[[支出基线与支出上限]]分别承担成本预测和总量控制，不应混为一个数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A new hospital entails construction costs and subsequent staffing, maintenance and equipment costs. A multi-year baseline brings these commitments into comparison with future ceilings. [[支出基线与支出上限|Baselines and ceilings]] serve distinct forecasting and control functions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多年框架需要定期更新经济假设、现行政策成本和新增政策影响，同时解释与上一版的差异。更新预测不等于每年取消既定约束；较强的上限也不等于自动取得支出授权，具体执行仍通过[[政府预算]]及适用的调整程序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The framework requires updated assumptions, policy costs and explanations of revisions. Updating forecasts does not itself cancel constraints, and even a binding ceiling does not grant spending authority. Implementation follows the [[政府预算|budget]] and applicable amendment procedures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [OECD, Recommendation on Budgetary Governance](https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/public/doc/319/body-text.en.html)，原则 2、3：支持现实基线、边界、政策衔接、多年管理及投资的后续成本。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Principles 2 and 3 support realistic baselines, expenditure boundaries, policy alignment, multi-year management and recurrent costs of investment.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [OECD, Multi-year expenditure baselines](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/quality-budget-institutions_8e811202-en/full-report/multi-year-expenditure-baselines_cdff6aca.html)，§§3.1、3.3.1–3.3.2；[Top-down expenditure ceilings](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/quality-budget-institutions_8e811202-en/full-report/top-down-expenditure-ceilings_26af86f0.html)，§§4.2.2、4.4.1：支持滚动预测、多年成本与后续年份计划的指示性/约束性之别。医院例为构造说明。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  These sections support rolling projections, policy costs over time and the distinction between indicative and binding outer-year ceilings. The hospital is an illustrative example.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
