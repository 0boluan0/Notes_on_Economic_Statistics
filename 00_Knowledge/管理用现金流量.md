# 管理用现金流量是按经营与金融活动重组的现金流量，其中经营活动包含生产性投资
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Management cash flows group cash generation and distribution into operating and financial activities, with productive investment included in operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

管理用现金流量从经营业务与资本提供者两个角度阅读同一资金运动。经营侧不仅包括销售收付，还扣除生产性投资以及约定的经营资金增加；金融侧说明这笔净现金如何在债务、金融资产和股东之间分配。普通工商企业在一致口径下，经营侧的实体现金流可与[[企业自由现金流]]衔接。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This framework reads the same movement of funds from the operating-business and capital-provider perspectives. Operations include trading cash flows less productive investment and the specified increase in operating funds. The financial side explains the net distribution through debt, financial assets, and equity. For an ordinary nonfinancial business on a consistent basis, the operating residual connects to [[企业自由现金流|FCFF]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它不是把法定[[现金流量表]]的经营、投资、筹资三栏直接合并改名。传统经营现金流 CFO 通常不扣购置生产设备的投资现金，而管理口径要扣；融资相关利息、金融收益、税效应和现金范围也须重新匹配。因此，不能把 CFO 直接当作这里的“实体现金流”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is not a simple relabelling of the three sections of a statutory [[现金流量表|cash-flow statement]]. Reported CFO generally excludes cash spent acquiring productive equipment, whereas the management operating measure deducts it. Financing interest, financial income, tax effects, and cash scope also require consistent treatment. CFO cannot simply be substituted for the operating residual.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在已完成现金与账面变动调节的简化模型里，若“向资本提供者净分配”为正，可写成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simplified model after reconciling cash with book movements, sign net distributions to capital providers positively:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$FCFF=\underbrace{NFE_{after}-\Delta NFD}_{\text{净债权侧分配}}+\underbrace{Div-NetIssue}_{\text{股权侧分配}}.$$

其中净发行 $NetIssue$ 为发行收款减回购付款。ABC 的 $70.72-196+56-0=-69.28$，负值表示资本提供者净投入，支持本期经营再投资。该例中资本变动已按教材简化处理；实际汇兑、非现金交易或重估需另调节，不能把任何账面负债增加都说成借到现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$NetIssue$ is cash issuance proceeds less repurchase payments. ABC gives $70.72-196+56=-69.28$, meaning a net inflow from capital providers to support operating reinvestment. The example uses the textbook's simplified capital movements. Actual exchange effects, non-cash transactions, or revaluations require reconciliation; a larger book liability is not automatically cash borrowing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
