# 买卖价差是同口径最佳卖价减最佳买价

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bid–ask spread is the best ask minus the best bid measured on the same basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

买卖价差是同一证券、同一时点和明确市场范围内，最佳卖价 $a$ 与最佳买价 $b$ 之差。普通连续交易报价中，最佳买价是买方愿付的最高价，最佳卖价是卖方愿收的最低价；两者必须使用相同币种、计价单位和报价基础。它不是两个不同资产之间的相对估值价差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bid–ask spread is the difference between best ask $a$ and best bid $b$ for the same security, time and specified market scope. In ordinary continuous quotations, the best bid is the highest buying price and the best ask the lowest selling price. Currency, unit and quotation basis must match. This is not a relative-value gap between different assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
s=a-b,\qquad m=\frac{a+b}{2},\qquad
s_{\mathrm{rel}}=\frac{a-b}{m}\quad(m>0).
$$

例如 $b=99.80$、$a=100.00$，价差为 0.20，中间价为 99.90，相对价差约 0.2002%。若数量足够且报价保持不变，按卖价买入相对中间价多付 0.10；先按卖价买入再立即按买价卖出，每单位损失 0.20，未计费用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $b=99.80$ and $a=100.00$, the spread is 0.20, midpoint 99.90 and relative spread about 0.2002%. With sufficient size and unchanged quotes, buying at the ask costs 0.10 above the midpoint. Buying at the ask and immediately selling at the bid loses 0.20 per unit before fees.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这只是报价口径的成本示意。真实执行可能获得价格改善，也可能因[[市场深度]]不足而跨档成交；相对中间价的单边价差代价也不是经纪佣金。较窄价差不保证能成交大数量，报价价差更不等于[[做市商]]的无风险利润。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a quotation-based cost illustration. Execution may improve on a quote or cross levels when [[市场深度|depth]] is insufficient. The one-way cost relative to the midpoint is not a brokerage commission. A narrow spread guarantees neither large-size execution nor riskless [[做市商|market-maker]] profit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
