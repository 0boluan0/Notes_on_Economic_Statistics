# 美国现行货币基础与 M1 M2 覆盖不同部门资产不能当作同一嵌套序列
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Under current U.S. definitions, the monetary base and M1/M2 cover different sectoral assets and are not one nested sequence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 按现行美国官方定义，货币基础是流通中通货加准备金余额；这里的 currency in circulation 排除美国财政部和 Federal Reserve Banks 持有的通货，但包括存款机构库存现金。H.6 的 M1 currency 还会排除存款机构金库中的通货，因此主要捕捉公众持有的通货。M1 与 M2 另包含不同范围的存款或近货币工具；三者有重叠，却覆盖不同持有人和发行主体的资产。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under the current U.S. official definitions, the monetary base is currency in circulation plus reserve balances. Base currency in circulation excludes currency held by the U.S. Treasury and Federal Reserve Banks but includes depository institutions' vault cash. H.6 M1 currency additionally excludes currency in depository-institution vaults and therefore mainly captures currency held by the public. M1 and M2 also include different ranges of deposits or near-money instruments; the three measures overlap but cover assets with different holders and issuers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，不能把 monetary base、M1、M2 排成“同一批公众资产从窄到宽”的三层。准备金进入货币基础但通常不进入公众 M1/M2；商业银行存款进入 M1/M2，却不是央行负债，也不按同一方式进入货币基础。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The monetary base, M1, and M2 therefore cannot be treated as three increasingly broad layers of the same public asset set. Reserve balances enter the base but normally not public M1/M2; commercial-bank deposits enter M1/M2 but are not central-bank liabilities and do not enter the base in the same way.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- base 扩张不等于 M1 或 M2 同额扩张。
- 同一张纸币从银行金库交给公众时，仍在货币基础的 currency in circulation 中，却从 M1 排除项变成 M1 currency；所以两种口径不能只凭“通货”二字判断集合关系。
- M1 大于或小于 base 的数值关系本身没有稳定结构含义，必须看各自成分。
- “高能货币”是历史理论标签，不保证存在固定、稳定的存款乘数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Expansion of the base does not imply an equal expansion of M1 or M2.
- A note handed from a bank vault to the public remains in base currency in circulation but moves from an M1 exclusion into M1 currency. The shared word “currency” therefore does not establish a simple set relation.
- Whether M1 is numerically above or below the base has no stable structural meaning without inspecting components.
- “High-powered money” is a historical theoretical label; it does not guarantee a fixed, stable deposit multiplier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么银行准备金大量上升时，货币基础会升而公众 M1 未必同额上升？
>
> **答案：** 准备金属于货币基础，是央行对合格机构的负债；公众 M1 的存款是商业银行负债，只有相应结算链或私人记账事件才改变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can a large rise in bank reserves raise the monetary base without an equal rise in public M1?
>
> **Answer:** Reserves are central-bank liabilities to eligible institutions and belong to the base. Public deposits are commercial-bank liabilities and change only through the corresponding settlement chain or private accounting event.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
