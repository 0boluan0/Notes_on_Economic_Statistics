# 在正向区间上对可积函数积分会保留逐点大小关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Integration over an increasing interval preserves pointwise order between integrable functions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $a<b$，$f,g$ 在 $[a,b]$ 可积且处处 $f(x)\le g(x)$，则
$$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx\le\int_a^b g(x)\,dx.$$
理由很直接：每个小区间的宽度为正，相同采样下每项保持不等式，再取[[黎曼和]]极限。若把上下限反过来，两边都取负，不等号也要反向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With increasing bounds and integrable functions ordered pointwise, their integrals have the same order. Positive subinterval widths preserve each sampled inequality, and limits preserve the result. Reversing the bounds negates both integrals and reverses the inequality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $0\le x\le1$ 上 $x^2\le x$，所以 $\int_0^1x^2\,dx\le\int_0^1x\,dx$。它不要求 $f$ 或 $g$ 自身单调；比较的是两条函数的高低，不是同一函数沿 $x$ 的增减。怎样挑选容易积分的比较函数，见[[积分上下界估计]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $x^2\le x$ on the unit interval, the same inequality holds between their integrals. Neither function must itself be monotone: the comparison concerns heights across functions, not changes along one function. Choosing useful comparators is the method in [[积分上下界估计|integral bounds]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
