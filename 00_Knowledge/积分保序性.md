# Order preservation of the integral
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*积分的保序性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose one tap’s flow never exceeds another’s over the same forward time interval. Its total contribution cannot exceed the other’s. **Order preservation** turns a point-by-point comparison of two functions into a comparison of their [[定积分|definite integrals]]. This can estimate a difficult integral without finding its exact value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一段正向时间内，一只水龙头的流量始终不超过另一只，那么它的总贡献也不会更多。**保序性**把两个函数逐点的大小关系，变成两个[[定积分]]的大小关系，因此可以用来估计难以精确求出的积分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a<b$ be finite. Suppose real-valued $f,g$ are Riemann integrable on $[a,b]$ and $f(x)\le g(x)$ at every input in that interval. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设有限端点满足 $a<b$。若实值函数 $f,g$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上黎曼可积，并且区间内每个输入都满足 $f(x)\le g(x)$，则

$$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx\le\int_a^b g(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The functions may take negative values. The premise compares their heights at the same input; it does not require either function to increase or decrease with $x$. For example, $-2\le-1$ gives $\int_0^1(-2)\,dx=-2\le-1=\int_0^1(-1)\,dx$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

函数值可以为负。前提比较的是同一输入下两函数的高度，不要求任一函数随 $x$ 单调增减。例如 $-2\le-1$，对应 $\int_0^1(-2)\,dx=-2\le-1=\int_0^1(-1)\,dx$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the comparison survives accumulation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么累积之后仍能比较*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the same partition and sample points in the two [[黎曼和|Riemann sums]]. Every piece has positive width $\Delta x_i$, so $f(\xi_i)\Delta x_i\le g(\xi_i)\Delta x_i$. Adding preserves this inequality. As the largest width tends to zero, integrability makes the two sums approach their respective integrals, and the weak inequality survives the limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给两个[[黎曼和]]使用相同分割与采样点。每段宽度 $\Delta x_i$ 为正，所以 $f(\xi_i)\Delta x_i\le g(\xi_i)\Delta x_i$。相加仍保持不等式；最大段宽趋于零时，可积性使两边分别趋于各自的积分，非严格不等式在极限中保留。

> [!example] Compare simple functions · 比较容易处理的函数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $0\le x\le1$, $x^2\le x$. Therefore $\int_0^1x^2\,dx\le\int_0^1x\,dx$, consistent with $1/3\le1/2$. Taking $f=0$ shows that a nonnegative integrable function has a nonnegative integral. Choosing useful lower and upper comparators leads to [[积分上下界估计|integral bounds]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $0\le x\le1$ 上，$x^2\le x$，所以 $\int_0^1x^2\,dx\le\int_0^1x\,dx$，与 $1/3\le1/2$ 一致。取 $f=0$，还可得非负可积函数的积分非负。如何挑选合适的上下比较函数，见[[积分上下界估计]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Direction and equality matter
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方向与等号都需要留意*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reversing the bounds negates both integrals, so the inequality reverses: $\int_b^a f\ge\int_b^a g$. In the example above, $-1/3\ge-1/2$. Equal bounds make both integrals zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反向端点使两个积分同时取负，因此不等号反向：$\int_b^a f\ge\int_b^a g$。上例反向后为 $-1/3\ge-1/2$。相同端点则使两个积分都为零。

> [!warning] A difference at one point may contribute no area · 单个点的差异可以不改变积分
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $f(x)=0$ on $[0,1]$, and let $g(x)=0$ except $g(1/2)=1$. Then $f\le g$ everywhere and they differ at one point, yet both integrals are zero. For a partition whose largest width is $\delta$, only the one or two pieces touching $1/2$ can contribute to a sampled sum for $g$; its value is between $0$ and $2\delta$. Hence every sufficiently fine sum tends to zero. Equality of integrals therefore does not imply equality of functions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $[0,1]$ 上 $f(x)=0$，而 $g$ 除 $g(1/2)=1$ 外均为零。虽然处处 $f\le g$ 且一个点上有差异，但两者积分均为零。最大段宽为 $\delta$ 时，$g$ 的采样和只有接触 $1/2$ 的一段或两段可能有贡献，故其值介于 $0$ 与 $2\delta$ 之间。所有充分细的和都趋于零。因此，积分相等并不推出函数相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful stronger case adds continuity: if both functions are continuous on $[a,b]$, $f\le g$ everywhere, and $f(c)<g(c)$ at some interior point $c$, then their integrals are strictly ordered. Indeed, the positive gap $d=g(c)-f(c)$ remains at least $d/2$ on a short interval around $c$ by [[一点连续|continuity at that point]]. This interval has positive width and contributes a positive amount; [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]] combines it with the nonnegative contribution elsewhere. [[定积分线性性|Linearity]] identifies the integral of the gap with the difference of the integrals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个实用的加强情形需要连续性：若两函数在 $[a,b]$ 上连续、处处 $f\le g$，且某个内部点 $c$ 满足 $f(c)<g(c)$，那么积分严格不等。因为[[一点连续|该点的连续性]]保证正差值 $d=g(c)-f(c)$ 在附近一小段上至少为 $d/2$；这段有正宽度，贡献正量；[[定积分区间可加性]]把它与其他地方的非负贡献合起来。再由[[定积分线性性|线性性]]，差函数的积分就是两积分之差。
