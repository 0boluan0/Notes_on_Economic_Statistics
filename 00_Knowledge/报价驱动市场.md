# 报价驱动市场主要围绕交易商的买卖报价组织成交

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A quote-driven market organises trading primarily around dealers' bid and ask quotations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

报价驱动市场是以交易商报出的买价、卖价及可交易数量组织成交的市场机制。投资者可向一家或多家交易商询价，再按可接受的报价与其成交；承担报价交易对手角色的[[做市商]]通常以自己的账户买卖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A quote-driven market organises transactions around dealers' bids, asks and available quantities. An investor may request quotes from one or several dealers and trade against an acceptable quote. A [[做市商|market maker]] acting as the quoted counterparty normally trades on its own account.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，某交易商愿意按 99.80 买入、按 100.00 卖出各不超过 50 单位。投资者向它买入 30 单位，使用的是卖价 100.00；买入 120 单位则不能仅凭这条报价推定全部按 100.00 成交。还须确认报价是否可执行、有效时间和适用数量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a dealer offers to buy at 99.80 and sell at 100.00, up to 50 units on each side. An investor buying 30 units from the dealer uses its ask of 100.00. A 120-unit purchase cannot be assumed to execute entirely at that price. Quote firmness, validity and size must also be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个分类回答“交易围绕谁的报价组织”，不是“交易是否使用电脑”或“是否在交易所进行”。[[订单驱动市场]]也会显示买卖报价，做市商也可以向订单簿提交订单；同一平台可能结合不同机制。不能把所有债券永久归为报价驱动，或把报价驱动直接等同于不透明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The classification concerns whose quotations organise trading, not whether trading is electronic or exchange-based. An [[订单驱动市场|order-driven market]] also displays bid and ask prices, and market makers can submit orders to a book. Platforms may combine mechanisms. Neither all bonds nor all quote-driven trading should be assigned a timeless venue or transparency classification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
