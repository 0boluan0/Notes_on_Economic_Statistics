# 零息债券在到期前不支付息票并承诺在到期日支付约定金额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A zero-coupon bond pays no interim coupons and promises one payment at maturity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零息债券（zero-coupon bond）的合同现金流是在到期前没有息票支付，在到期日承诺支付约定金额。普通按面值偿还的零息债券只承诺在 $T$ 支付面值 $F$；“承诺”不意味着发行人不会违约。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero-coupon bond has no contractual interim coupon payments and promises one payment at maturity. An ordinary zero-coupon bond redeeming at par promises only its face value $F$ at $T$. A promise does not eliminate issuer default risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若该支付无违约风险，采用与其期限匹配的年有效即期利率 $s_T>-1$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the payment is default-free and its maturity-matched effective annual spot rate is $s_T>-1$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P=F\,DF(0,T)=\frac{F}{(1+s_T)^T}.$$

因此零息不等于零收益：$F=100,T=1,s_1=5\%$ 时价格约为 $95.24$。也不能把“折价”当作无条件定义：$s_1=-1\%$ 时价格约为 $101.01$，高于到期款；若此负收益率保持不变，价格随到期临近向下靠近 $100$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero coupon does not mean zero return: with $F=100,T=1,s_1=5\%$, price is about 95.24. Nor is a discount an unconditional defining property: at $s_1=-1\%$, price is about 101.01, above the maturity payment. If that negative yield stays constant, price falls toward 100 as maturity approaches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The CPA textbook, printed pp. 105–106 / PDF pp. 113–114, supports the pure-discount cash-flow structure and lump-sum present-value formula. Positive- and negative-yield cases are direct substitutions into that formula.
- OpenStax Section 10.1 supports the absence of interim coupons and the single maturity payment; its discount description is read as the positive-yield case.
