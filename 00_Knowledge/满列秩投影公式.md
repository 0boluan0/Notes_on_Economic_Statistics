# Projection from a full-column-rank matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*满列秩投影公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The columns of a matrix can describe a line, a plane or a higher-dimensional subspace without being perpendicular. To project a vector onto that space, first find the combination of columns that best approximates it, then construct the fitted vector. The Gram-matrix inverse corrects for the lengths and overlap of the chosen columns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵的列可以描述直线、平面或更高维子空间，而不必彼此垂直。要把一个向量投影到这个空间，先找到最接近它的列组合，再合成拟合向量。Gram矩阵的逆会校正所选列的长度与相互重叠。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ have [[线性无关|independent columns]], so $m\ge n$ and $A$ has full column rank. Its [[列空间|column space]] $C(A)=\{Ax:x\in\mathbb R^n\}$ contains every attainable fitted vector. For an input $b\in\mathbb R^m$, let $p$ denote its [[正交投影|orthogonal projection]] onto $C(A)$. With $T$ denoting [[矩阵转置|transpose]], the formula is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ 的列[[线性无关]]，因此 $m\ge n$ 且 $A$ 满列秩。它的[[列空间]] $C(A)=\{Ax:x\in\mathbb R^n\}$ 包含全部可能的拟合向量。对输入 $b\in\mathbb R^m$，用 $p$ 表示它到 $C(A)$ 的[[正交投影]]；$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。公式为

$$
\hat x=(A^TA)^{-1}A^Tb,\qquad p=A\hat x=Pb,\qquad P=A(A^TA)^{-1}A^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficient vector $\hat x$ has $n$ entries; it tells how much of each column to use. The projection $p$ has $m$ entries and lies in the original space. The [[Gram矩阵正定判据|Gram matrix]] $G=A^TA$ is $n\times n$ and records all column dot products. Thus the steps are $b\mapsto A^Tb\mapsto G^{-1}A^Tb\mapsto p$: measure dot products, correct the coordinates, rebuild the vector. When the columns are orthonormal, $G=I_n$, the [[单位矩阵|$n$-by-$n$ identity matrix]], and this reduces to [[标准正交基投影公式|$P=AA^T$]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数向量 $\hat x$ 有 $n$ 项，告诉我们每列取多少；投影 $p$ 有 $m$ 项，位于原来的空间。[[Gram矩阵正定判据|Gram矩阵]] $G=A^TA$ 为 $n\times n$，记录所有列间点积。因此步骤为 $b\mapsto A^Tb\mapsto G^{-1}A^Tb\mapsto p$：量点积、校正坐标、合成向量。列标准正交时 $G=I_n$，即 $n\times n$ 的[[单位矩阵]]，便化成[[标准正交基投影公式|$P=AA^T$]]。

> [!example] Two overlapping basis directions / 两条相互重叠的基方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $a_1=(1,0,0)^T$, $a_2=(1,1,0)^T$, $A=[a_1\ a_2]$, and $b=(2,3,4)^T$. The columns span the horizontal plane but are not orthogonal. Here $A^Tb=(2,5)^T$ and $G=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$, with $G^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}$. Therefore $\hat x=(-1,3)^T$ and $p=-a_1+3a_2=(2,3,0)^T$. The residual $(0,0,4)^T$ is perpendicular to the plane. Treating the raw dot products $(2,5)^T$ as coefficients would instead give $(7,5,0)^T$, which is not the nearest point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $a_1=(1,0,0)^T$、$a_2=(1,1,0)^T$、$A=[a_1\ a_2]$、$b=(2,3,4)^T$。两列张成水平面，却不正交。此时 $A^Tb=(2,5)^T$、$G=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$，且 $G^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}$。于是 $\hat x=(-1,3)^T$、$p=-a_1+3a_2=(2,3,0)^T$，残差 $(0,0,4)^T$ 垂直于平面。若直接把点积 $(2,5)^T$ 当系数，则会得到 $(7,5,0)^T$，并不是最近点。

## Why this is the orthogonal projection
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么这是正交投影

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full column rank ensures $A^TA$ is invertible: for every nonzero coefficient vector $v$, one has $Av\ne0$, and hence $v^TA^TAv=\|Av\|_2^2>0$. This is the Gram positive-definiteness criterion. The candidate $p=A(A^TA)^{-1}A^Tb$ lies in $C(A)$, and its residual obeys
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满列秩保证 $A^TA$ 可逆：对每个非零系数向量 $v$，都有 $Av\ne0$，所以 $v^TA^TAv=\|Av\|_2^2>0$，这就是Gram正定判据。候选向量 $p=A(A^TA)^{-1}A^Tb$ 位于 $C(A)$，其残差满足

$$
A^T(b-p)=A^Tb-A^TA(A^TA)^{-1}A^Tb=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This means $b-p$ is perpendicular to every column, hence to the whole column space. The [[最佳逼近与正交残差|nearest-point theorem]] then identifies $p$ as the unique closest vector. Equivalently, the coordinates solve the [[最小二乘正规方程|normal equations]] $A^TA\hat x=A^Tb$. The resulting $P$ is symmetric and idempotent, fixes every vector in $C(A)$, and sends its orthogonal complement to zero. Its rank is $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这表示 $b-p$ 垂直于每一列，也就垂直于整个列空间。由[[最佳逼近与正交残差|最近点定理]]，$p$ 正是唯一最近向量。等价地，系数解满足[[最小二乘正规方程|正规方程]] $A^TA\hat x=A^Tb$。所得 $P$ 对称且幂等，保留 $C(A)$ 中每个向量，把其正交补送到零，秩为 $n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The projection depends on the subspace, not on which independent columns describe it. If an invertible $n\times n$ matrix $B$ gives a new basis $AB$, then $C(AB)=C(A)$ and both projector formulas give the same $P$. Coordinates change to $B^{-1}\hat x$, while the fitted vector stays fixed. For square invertible $A$, the column space is all of $\mathbb R^m$ and $P=I_m$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

投影取决于子空间，而不是用哪几条独立列描述它。若可逆 $n\times n$ 矩阵 $B$ 给出新基 $AB$，则 $C(AB)=C(A)$，两个投影公式产生同一个 $P$。坐标改为 $B^{-1}\hat x$，拟合向量却不变。若 $A$ 是可逆方阵，列空间就是整个 $\mathbb R^m$，故 $P=I_m$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Where the inverse formula stops applying
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失效边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the columns are dependent, $A^TA$ is singular and this ordinary inverse formula is undefined. The subspace and its unique nearest-point projection still exist; only the independent-coordinate formula has failed. For example, two identical columns $a,a$ with $a=(1,0)^T$ give a singular Gram matrix, but projection of $(2,3)^T$ onto their span is still $(2,0)^T$. Every coefficient pair with sum 2 gives that fit. Use an independent basis for the same space, or the [[AA+列空间投影|pseudoinverse projector $AA^+$]], where $A^+$ denotes the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若列线性相关，$A^TA$ 奇异，通常意义下的逆矩阵公式没有定义。子空间及其唯一最近点投影仍存在，失效的是这套独立坐标公式。例如把 $a=(1,0)^T$ 重复两次作为列，Gram矩阵就奇异；但 $(2,3)^T$ 到其张成空间的投影仍为 $(2,0)^T$，系数和等于2的每一对都给出该拟合。可改用同一空间的一组独立基，或用[[AA+列空间投影|伪逆投影 $AA^+$]]，其中 $A^+$ 表示Moore–Penrose伪逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This formula specifies the exact projector, not a requirement to form an inverse in software. Even with independent columns, forming $A^TA$ squares the two-norm condition number; see [[正规方程条件数平方|normal-equation conditioning]]. A suitable [[薄QR最小二乘|QR least-squares solve]] or SVD can compute the fitted vector without explicitly forming the inverse. Numerical stability and the mathematical existence of the inverse are separate questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个公式描述精确投影，并不要求软件显式计算逆矩阵。即使列独立，形成 $A^TA$ 也会使二范数条件数平方，见[[正规方程条件数平方]]。适当的[[薄QR最小二乘|QR最小二乘求解]]或SVD可在不显式求逆的情况下计算拟合向量；数值稳定与数学上是否存在逆，是不同问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For complex $A$ of full column rank under the usual Euclidean inner product, use $P=A(A^*A)^{-1}A^*$, with $*$ denoting conjugate transpose. Weighted distances require a different formula and an explicitly chosen inner product; the expression here uses ordinary Euclidean distance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对通常欧氏内积下满列秩的复矩阵，使用 $P=A(A^*A)^{-1}A^*$，其中 $*$ 表示共轭转置。加权距离需要另一套公式，并明确所选内积；这里使用普通欧氏距离。
