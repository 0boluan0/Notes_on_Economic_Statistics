<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Doubling and optional stopping
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*无限翻倍与可选停止*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine a fair coin game: stake one unit, double the stake after every loss, and stop at the first win. A win then repays all earlier losses and leaves one unit of profit. The mathematical model permits arbitrarily large stakes and arbitrarily long play. It produces a useful counterexample: the first win has finite expected waiting time, and the final profit is always one almost surely, yet these facts do not justify applying the usual optional-stopping equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设想一个公平硬币游戏：先下注一个单位，每次输了就把下一笔翻倍，首次赢时停止。获胜这一笔恰好补回此前全部亏损，再剩一个单位利润。数学模型允许任意大的下注额与任意长的游戏时间。它给出一个重要反例：首次获胜的平均等待时间有限，最终利润几乎必然为一，但这些事实仍不足以直接使用可选停止的期望等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The apparent puzzle is resolved by separating a fixed deadline from an eventual stopping time. At every fixed deadline there is a small chance of a very large accumulated loss. As the deadline grows, that chance shrinks, but the loss grows fast enough that its contribution to the expectation does not disappear.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理解它的关键，是区分固定截止时刻与最终停止时刻。到每个固定截止时刻，仍有很小的概率累积出巨大亏损。截止时间推后，这个概率减小，亏损却增大得足够快，以至于它对期望的贡献并没有消失。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Build the fair process
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 构造

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\xi_1,\xi_2,\ldots$ be independent fair signs, with $P(\xi_n=1)=P(\xi_n=-1)=1/2$; $1$ means a win and $-1$ a loss. The [[自然滤过|natural filtration]] $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$ records the first $n$ outcomes, and $\mathcal F_0$ is trivial. Define the first win by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $\xi_1,\xi_2,\ldots$ 为相互独立的公平符号，$P(\xi_n=1)=P(\xi_n=-1)=1/2$，其中 $1$ 表示赢，$-1$ 表示输。[[自然滤过]] $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$ 记录前 $n$ 次结果，$\mathcal F_0$ 为平凡信息。首次获胜时刻定义为

$$T=\inf\{n\ge1:\xi_n=1\},\qquad\inf\varnothing=\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a [[停时定义|stopping time]]: by round $n$ we can tell whether any of the observed signs was positive. It is the [[离散首次到达停时|first-entry rule]] applied from step $1$. Let $\mathbf1_A$ be the [[事件指示变量|indicator]] equal to $1$ on event $A$ and $0$ otherwise. The stake at step $n$ is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它是[[停时定义|停时]]：到第 $n$ 轮，只看已出现的符号，就知道是否已经赢过。这是从第 $1$ 步开始使用的[[离散首次到达停时|首次进入规则]]。用[[事件指示变量]] $\mathbf1_A$ 表示事件 $A$ 发生时取 $1$、否则取 $0$，则第 $n$ 步下注额为

$$H_n=2^{n-1}\mathbf1_{\{T\ge n\}},\qquad n\ge1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The event $T\ge n$ says that the first $n-1$ rounds were all losses. Thus the stake is [[离散可预测过程|predictable]]: it is fixed before seeing the current coin, and becomes zero after a win. Define the cumulative net gain by $M_0=0$ and $M_n=\sum_{k=1}^nH_k\xi_k$. This includes both wins and losses, not just the amount received on the final bet.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$T\ge n$ 表示前 $n-1$ 轮全输，所以下注额[[离散可预测过程|可预测]]：看到本轮硬币以前已经确定，赢后归零。令累计净收益为 $M_0=0$、$M_n=\sum_{k=1}^nH_k\xi_k$。这个数同时计算收益与亏损，不只是最后一笔获胜时收到的金额。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each fixed $n$, $|H_n\xi_n|\le2^{n-1}$, so the increment is integrable. Its conditional mean is zero because $H_n$ is known at time $n-1$ and the next sign is independent and fair:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个固定 $n$，都有 $|H_n\xi_n|\le2^{n-1}$，所以本期增量可积。$H_n$ 在 $n-1$ 时已知，新符号又独立且公平，因此条件均值为零：

$$E[H_n\xi_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]=H_nE[\xi_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These increments are a [[鞅差序列|martingale difference sequence]], so their [[鞅差部分和|finite partial sums]] form a [[鞅定义|martingale]] for this filtration. The bound $2^{n-1}$ is finite for each step, which proves per-step integrability; there is no single finite bound covering all steps.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些增量构成[[鞅差序列]]，所以它们的[[鞅差部分和|有限部分和]]是相对于该滤过的[[鞅定义|鞅]]。$2^{n-1}$ 对每一期都有限，足以证明逐期可积；但没有一个有限常数能同时约束所有期。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The waiting time and final gain look harmless
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 停时与终值都很“温和”

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To win for the first time on round $k$, we need $k-1$ losses followed by a win. Independence gives $P(T=k)=2^{-k}$ and $P(T>n)=2^{-n}\to0$, so $T$ is almost surely finite. Its mean is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要在第 $k$ 轮首次赢，必须先输 $k-1$ 轮、再赢一轮。独立性给出 $P(T=k)=2^{-k}$，并有 $P(T>n)=2^{-n}\to0$，所以 $T$ 几乎必然有限。其期望为

$$E[T]=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}k2^{-k}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}P(T>n)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}2^{-n}=2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tail sum counts one unit of waiting for each round still to be completed. On $T=k$, the previous loss is $1+2+\cdots+2^{k-2}=2^{k-1}-1$, interpreted as zero if $k=1$. The winning stake pays $2^{k-1}$, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

尾概率求和，是把每个尚未完成的等待轮次各计一个单位。在 $T=k$ 分支上，此前损失为 $1+2+\cdots+2^{k-2}=2^{k-1}-1$；$k=1$ 时解释为空和、取零。本轮获胜收益为 $2^{k-1}$，因此

$$M_T=-(2^{k-1}-1)+2^{k-1}=1\quad\text{almost surely}.$$

> [!example] Winning on the third round · 第三轮首次赢
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The three stakes are $1,2,4$, and the net changes are $-1,-2,+4$. The cumulative gains are $-1,-3,1$. Had the third round also lost, the cumulative gain would instead be $-7$, and the next required stake would be $8$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 三次下注为 $1,2,4$，净变化为 $-1,-2,+4$，累计收益依次为 $-1,-3,1$。若第三轮仍输，累计收益就成为 $-7$，下一轮又需要下注 $8$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore $E[M_T]=1\ne0=E[M_0]$, even though $E[T]=2$ and $M_T$ is a bounded constant. This does not contradict [[可选停止条件|optional stopping]]: finite expected waiting and an integrable terminal value are not, by themselves, one of its sufficient control conditions. The exceptional event of never winning has probability zero; no payoff on that event affects these claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

于是 $E[M_T]=1\ne0=E[M_0]$，尽管 $E[T]=2$，$M_T$ 甚至是有界常数。这没有违反[[可选停止条件]]：平均等待有限与终值可积，本身并不构成一组足够的控制条件。永不获胜的异常事件概率为零，在该事件上怎样规定终值，都不影响以上结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Rare losses retain a large contribution to the mean
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 缺失的质量藏在越来越罕见的巨额亏损里

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The strategy already freezes after its first win, so $M_n=M_{n\wedge T}$, where $n\wedge T=\min(n,T)$. At a fixed deadline $n\ge0$, either it has won and the gain is $1$, or every round so far has lost:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

策略在首次赢后已经冻结，所以 $M_n=M_{n\wedge T}$，其中 $n\wedge T=\min(n,T)$。到固定截止时刻 $n\ge0$，要么已经赢过、收益为 $1$，要么此前全部轮次都输：

$$M_{n\wedge T}=
\begin{cases}
1,&T\le n,\quad\text{with probability }1-2^{-n},\\
-(2^n-1),&T>n,\quad\text{with probability }2^{-n}.
\end{cases}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first case has probability zero when $n=0$. At every finite deadline the two contributions exactly cancel:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$n=0$ 时第一种情况概率为零。对每个有限截止时刻，两种情况对期望的贡献恰好抵消：

$$\begin{aligned}
E[M_{n\wedge T}]&=(1-2^{-n})-(2^n-1)2^{-n}=0,\\
E|M_{n\wedge T}|&=(1-2^{-n})+(2^n-1)2^{-n}=2-2^{1-n}\le2.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, after ten rounds the all-loss event has probability $1/1024$, but its loss is $1023$. Its contribution to expected absolute loss is $1023/1024$, nearly one. A small probability alone cannot make an expectation small when the associated value is increasing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如第十轮后，全部输的概率只有 $1/1024$，亏损却是 $1023$；它对绝对损失期望的贡献为 $1023/1024$，几乎仍是一个单位。所对应的数值不断增大时，概率小并不自动使期望贡献小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[一致可积|Uniform integrability]] would require the expected absolute contribution beyond a large threshold $K$ to become small uniformly over every $n$. Here, for $K\ge1$, the positive value $1$ is outside that tail. Whenever $2^n-1>K$, the negative tail contributes $1-2^{-n}$, and therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[一致可积]]要求：超过大阈值 $K$ 的绝对值期望贡献，能够对所有 $n$ 一起变小。这里取 $K\ge1$，正的取值 $1$ 已不属于尾部；只要 $2^n-1>K$，负尾就贡献 $1-2^{-n}$，于是

$$\sup_{n\ge0}E\!\left[|M_{n\wedge T}|\mathbf1_{\{|M_{n\wedge T}|>K\}}\right]=1,\qquad K\ge1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The supremum is one, even though each finite contributing term is less than one: arbitrarily large $n$ make it arbitrarily close. Thus the stopped family is not UI. The bound on $E|M_{n\wedge T}|$ above shows concretely why [[L1有界不推一致可积|a uniform first-moment bound does not imply UI]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个有限项虽然都小于一，上确界仍为一，因为把 $n$ 取大就能任意接近一。因此停止族不一致可积。上面 $E|M_{n\wedge T}|$ 的统一上界，具体展示了为什么[[L1有界不推一致可积|一阶矩统一有界仍不推出一致可积]]。

> [!important] Almost-sure convergence does not remove the expected loss · 几乎必然收敛仍不足以消去平均损失
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On almost every path a win eventually occurs, so $M_{n\wedge T}\to1=M_T$. Yet for every finite $n$, $E|M_{n\wedge T}-1|=2^n2^{-n}=1$. Thus there is no [[L1收敛|$L^1$ convergence]]. Each truncated expectation is zero, while the limit has expectation one; the rare large losses explain the failed passage to the limit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 几乎每条路径最终都会赢，所以 $M_{n\wedge T}\to1=M_T$。但每个有限 $n$ 都有 $E|M_{n\wedge T}-1|=2^n2^{-n}=1$，因此没有 [[L1收敛|$L^1$ 收敛]]。每个截断期望为零，极限的期望却为一；罕见的大额亏损解释了为什么不能交换极限与期望。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[停止过程保持鞅|finite-time stopped-martingale theorem]] remains valid at every deadline. What fails is the extra control needed to replace those finite values by the eventual terminal value. Imposing a finite bankroll or a fixed maximum number of rounds changes the model and removes the unconditional promise of a final one-unit gain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[停止过程保持鞅|有限时刻的停止过程定理]]对每个截止时刻都成立；失败的是把这些有限值换成最终停止值所需的额外控制。若加入有限本金或固定的最多轮数，模型就改变了，最终保证赚一个单位的结论也随之消失。
