# Birth–death chains
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*生灭链*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A birth–death chain tracks a count that changes one unit at a time. An arrival can raise the count from $1$ to $2$; a completion can lower it from $1$ to $0$. The words “birth” and “death” describe these upward and downward moves. They can represent customers, jobs, or individuals, rather than literal births and deaths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

生灭链描述每次只增减一个单位的数量：一次到达可以使人数从 $1$ 变成 $2$，一次完成可以使人数从 $1$ 变成 $0$。“生”和“灭”是向上、向下跳转的名称，可以表示顾客、任务或个体，不限于生物出生与死亡。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A count with two available places
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先看最多两个名额的计数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the possible counts are $0,1,2$. Potential arrivals follow a [[齐次泊松过程|homogeneous Poisson process]] of rate $2$ per hour; a job is admitted only while there is room. Each admitted job has an [[指数分布|exponential processing time]] of rate $1$ per hour. All processing times are independent of each other and of arrivals, and admitted jobs are processed simultaneously. The upward rates are therefore $\lambda_0=\lambda_1=2$, and the downward rates are $\mu_1=1$, $\mu_2=2$. At $0$ there is nothing to finish; at $2$ no further job is admitted.

In the [[生成矩阵约束|generator matrix]], row $n$ describes moves out of state $n$. In the order $0,1,2$, this model has

$$Q=\begin{pmatrix}-2&2&0\\1&-3&2\\0&2&-2\end{pmatrix}.$$

For example, from state $1$, the [[出口率|total exit rate]] is $1+2=3$ per hour. The next change is upward with probability $2/3$ and downward with probability $1/3$. The [[CTMC指数停留时间|holding time]] has an exponential distribution of rate $3$, hence mean $1/3$ hour. Rates determine both the clock and the probabilities of the competing destinations; they are not themselves probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设可能的数量为 $0,1,2$。潜在到达服从每小时速率 $2$ 的[[齐次泊松过程]]，只有有空位时才接收新任务。每个接收任务的处理时长服从速率为每小时 $1$ 的[[指数分布]]；所有处理时长彼此独立，也与到达过程独立，接收的任务同时处理。因此向上速率为 $\lambda_0=\lambda_1=2$，向下速率为 $\mu_1=1$、$\mu_2=2$。在 $0$ 没有任务可完成，在 $2$ 不再接收新任务。

[[生成矩阵约束|生成矩阵]]的第 $n$ 行表示从状态 $n$ 出发的转移；按 $0,1,2$ 排列，就得到上面的 $Q$。例如在状态 $1$，[[出口率|总出口率]]是每小时 $3$，下一次改变向上的概率为 $2/3$，向下为 $1/3$；[[CTMC指数停留时间|停留时间]]服从速率 $3$ 的指数分布，均值为 $1/3$ 小时。速率同时决定等待的时钟和各竞争去向的概率，本身并不是概率。

> [!important] Nearest-neighbour rates · 相邻状态的速率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A time-homogeneous **birth–death chain** is a [[CTMC定义|continuous-time Markov chain]] on $S=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, or on a finite interval $S=\{0,\ldots,N\}$, whose only possible genuine jumps are
>
> $$n\longrightarrow n+1\quad\text{at rate }\lambda_n,\qquad
> n\longrightarrow n-1\quad\text{at rate }\mu_n.$$
>
> The subscript is the **state being left**. All rates are nonnegative and finite at each state. Set $\mu_0=0$; in the finite case also set $\lambda_N=0$. Then
>
> $$q_{n,n+1}=\lambda_n,\qquad q_{n,n-1}=\mu_n,\qquad
> q_{nn}=-(\lambda_n+\mu_n),$$
>
> with other off-diagonal entries zero. Only entries whose destination lies in $S$ are included. $N$ is the largest allowed count, not a rate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 时间齐次**生灭链**是状态空间为 $S=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ 或有限区间 $S=\{0,\ldots,N\}$ 的[[CTMC定义|连续时间 Markov 链]]，真实跳跃只能从 $n$ 到 $n+1$，速率为 $\lambda_n$，或从 $n$ 到 $n-1$，速率为 $\mu_n$。下标表示**正在离开的状态**。各速率非负且在每个状态有限。下边界设 $\mu_0=0$；有限情形再设 $\lambda_N=0$。生成矩阵满足上式，其余非对角元为零；只写终点仍在 $S$ 中的元素。$N$ 是允许的最大数量，不是速率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The diagonal entry makes each row sum to zero; it is not a negative transition probability. If $\lambda_n+\mu_n>0$, the next-jump probabilities are $\lambda_n/(\lambda_n+\mu_n)$ and $\mu_n/(\lambda_n+\mu_n)$. If both rates vanish, the state is [[吸收状态|absorbing]] and the process stays there. A direct jump from $n$ to $n+2$ violates this nearest-neighbour definition, even if a two-jump route can reach $n+2$ later.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对角元用于使每行之和为零，不是负的转移概率。若 $\lambda_n+\mu_n>0$，下一跳向上、向下的概率分别为两个速率除以总速率；若两率都为零，该状态就是[[吸收状态|吸收态]]，过程一直停在那里。直接从 $n$ 跳到 $n+2$ 不符合相邻跳转定义，尽管两次连续向上跳可以在之后到达 $n+2$。

## Boundaries change the model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*边界和零速率会改变模型*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If every allowed adjacent pair has positive rates in both directions, all states communicate and the chain is [[不可约链|irreducible]]. A zero rate removes a directed route. In the three-state example, changing only $\lambda_1$ to zero makes $\{0,1\}$ a [[闭沟通类|closed communicating class]]: state $2$ can enter it, but cannot be reached from it. It would be wrong to treat all three states as one irreducible class after that change.

Setting every death rate to zero gives a [[纯生链|pure-birth chain]]; setting every birth rate to zero gives a pure-death chain. On a finite interval the finite rates imply non-explosion. On an infinite interval, finiteness at each state does not give one bound valid for all states: the pure-birth rates $\lambda_n=2^n$ already give an [[CTMC爆炸|explosive example]]. If $\sup_n(\lambda_n+\mu_n)<\infty$, the [[有界率非爆炸|bounded-rate criterion]] does guarantee non-explosion.

Thus “birth–death” specifies the allowed state changes. Whether stationary probabilities exist is a separate question; [[生灭链平稳递推|adjacent-rate recursion]] computes their candidate weights and explains the remaining checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若每对允许的相邻状态都有正的双向速率，所有状态互相沟通，链[[不可约链|不可约]]。零速率会删掉一个方向的路径。在三状态例中，仅把 $\lambda_1$ 改为零，就使 $\{0,1\}$ 成为[[闭沟通类]]：状态 $2$ 能进入它，但从它无法回到 $2$；这时不能仍把三状态当作一个不可约类。

把所有死亡率设为零，就得到[[纯生链]]；把所有出生率设为零，就得到纯灭链。有限区间上的有限速率保证非爆炸。但在无限区间，每个状态的速率有限，不等于存在对所有状态统一有效的上界：纯生率 $\lambda_n=2^n$ 已提供[[CTMC爆炸|爆炸例子]]。若 $\sup_n(\lambda_n+\mu_n)<\infty$，则可用[[有界率非爆炸]]保证过程不爆炸。

所以“生灭”规定允许怎样改变状态。是否存在平稳概率是另一问题；[[生灭链平稳递推]]负责计算候选权重并说明后续检查。
