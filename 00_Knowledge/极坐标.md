# Polar coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*极坐标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Polar coordinates locate a point by choosing a direction from the origin and then moving along that direction. They are useful when a circle, a rotating ray, or a distance from a centre describes the geometry more simply than horizontal and vertical offsets. We allow a **signed** radial coordinate: a negative value moves along the opposite ray.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极坐标先从原点选一个方向，再沿这个方向定位点。描述圆、旋转射线或到中心的距离时，这种方式常比横向与纵向位移更直接。这里允许径向坐标**带正负号**：负值表示沿反向射线取点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From a direction and a signed radius to a point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*由方向和有向半径确定点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the polar pair as $(r,\theta)$, where $r$ is a real number and $\theta$ is an angle measured from the positive horizontal axis. Positive angles turn counterclockwise. We use **radians**: its signed measure is the directed arc length divided by a positive circle radius, so one full turn is $2\pi$. The unit direction at angle $\theta$ has horizontal and vertical components $\cos\theta$ and $\sin\theta$. Multiplying it by $r$ gives the defining conversion:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把极坐标对写作 $(r,\theta)$，其中 $r$ 是实数，$\theta$ 从水平轴正方向开始计量，正角沿逆时针方向旋转。这里使用**弧度**：有向角度等于有向圆弧长除以正半径，因此一整圈为 $2\pi$。角度 $\theta$ 对应的单位方向，其水平、竖直分量分别为 $\cos\theta,\sin\theta$。将它乘以 $r$，就得到定义性的换算式：

$$x=r\cos\theta,\qquad y=r\sin\theta.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The pair $(r,\theta)$ is an instruction in the polar system; $(x,y)$ gives the resulting Cartesian coordinates. These pairs are not usually the same numbers. Since $\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta=1$, the distance from the origin is $\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=|r|$. Thus $r>0$ follows the selected ray, $r<0$ follows the opposite ray, and $r=0$ gives the origin at every angle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$(r,\theta)$ 是极坐标系中的定位指令，$(x,y)$ 则是所得点的直角坐标；这两组数通常不同。由 $\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta=1$，点到原点的距离为 $\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=|r|$。因此 $r>0$ 时沿所选射线取点，$r<0$ 时沿反向射线取点，$r=0$ 时无论角度怎样都在原点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $(r,\theta)=(2,\pi/3)$ gives $(x,y)=(1,\sqrt3)$. Keeping the angle and changing the radius to $-2$ gives $(-1,-\sqrt3)$, on the opposite side of the origin. A negative radius does not mean a negative geometric distance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$(r,\theta)=(2,\pi/3)$ 给出 $(x,y)=(1,\sqrt3)$。保持角度不变而把半径改成 $-2$，就得到原点另一侧的 $(-1,-\sqrt3)$。负半径并不是负的几何距离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Recovering one representation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从直角坐标恢复一组表示*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a non-origin point $(x,y)$, first choose the nonnegative radius $R=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}>0$. Then choose an angle satisfying **both** $\cos\theta=x/R$ and $\sin\theta=y/R$. These two equations retain the quadrant. If $x\ne0$, their quotient gives $\tan\theta=y/x$, but that quotient alone cannot distinguish opposite directions. When $x=0$, use the vertical direction directly instead of dividing by zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非原点 $(x,y)$，先选非负半径 $R=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}>0$，再选一个**同时**满足 $\cos\theta=x/R$ 和 $\sin\theta=y/R$ 的角度。这两个等式保留了象限信息。若 $x\ne0$，相除可得 $\tan\theta=y/x$，但只看这个比值无法区分相反方向。若 $x=0$，直接判断竖直方向，不要除以零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The point $(-1,\sqrt3)$ has distance $2$ and lies in the second quadrant, so $(2,2\pi/3)$ is one representation. The point $(0,-3)$ can be written $(3,3\pi/2)$. At the origin the radius is zero and no particular angle is selected by the point. Even away from the origin, full turns and sign reversal give other valid pairs; [[极坐标表示不唯一|non-uniqueness of polar coordinates]] explains exactly which ones.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

点 $(-1,\sqrt3)$ 到原点距离为 $2$，位于第二象限，所以 $(2,2\pi/3)$ 是一组表示。点 $(0,-3)$ 可写作 $(3,3\pi/2)$。原点的半径为零，而点本身不能确定一个特别的角度。即使不在原点，转整圈或改变半径符号也会产生别的合法坐标对；[[极坐标表示不唯一]]会准确说明哪些表示等价。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A coordinate pair is not yet a whole curve
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*坐标对与整条曲线的区别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding $r=R>0$ fixed while allowing a full turn of angles describes the circle of radius $R$. Holding $\theta=c$ fixed gives a ray if $r\ge0$, but an entire line through the origin if all real $r$ are allowed. The admissible values therefore matter even for these simple descriptions.

To draw a continuous $r=f(\theta)$ over a specified interval, let the angle vary and use the Cartesian map above at every input. This produces a [[参数曲线|parametric curve]], an ordered map into the plane. Its actual points and any repeated tracing are the separate subject of [[极坐标曲线的覆盖与重描|tracing a polar curve]]. A plot with horizontal coordinate $\theta$ and vertical coordinate $r$ is instead a graph of the function $f$; it is not the polar curve in the physical $xy$-plane.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $r=R>0$，让角度转一整圈，得到半径为 $R$ 的圆。固定 $\theta=c$ 时，若只允许 $r\ge0$，得到一条射线；若允许全部实数 $r$，则得到穿过原点的一整条直线。即使这些简单描述，也必须说明允许的取值。

要画指定区间上连续的 $r=f(\theta)$，就让角度变化，在每个输入处使用上面的直角坐标映射。这给出一条[[参数曲线]]，即按参数排序的平面映射。实际覆盖哪些点、是否重复描画，由[[极坐标曲线的覆盖与重描]]单独解释。以 $\theta$ 为横坐标、$r$ 为纵坐标画出的只是函数 $f$ 的图像，不是物理 $xy$ 平面内的极坐标曲线。
