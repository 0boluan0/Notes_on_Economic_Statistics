# Integrating powers of sine and cosine
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*正弦余弦幂积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An integral such as $\int\sin^3x\cos^2x\,dx$ asks for an [[原函数|antiderivative]]: a function whose derivative is the whole product $\sin^3x\cos^2x$. The useful move is to keep one factor that matches a derivative, then rewrite the rest in one variable. **Odd and even exponents tell us whether this direct route is available.**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

像 $\int\sin^3x\cos^2x\,dx$ 这样的积分，要求寻找一个[[原函数]]：对它求导，要得到整个乘积 $\sin^3x\cos^2x$。一个有效做法是留下与导数配对的因子，再把其余部分改写成同一个变量。**指数的奇偶性帮助我们判断，能否直接采用这条路线。**

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $x$ is a real angle measured in radians. Sine and cosine are the vertical and horizontal coordinates on the unit circle, giving $\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$. Their [[正弦余弦求导|derivatives]] give the two factors we want: $(\sin x)'=\cos x$ and $(\cos x)'=-\sin x$. The notation $\sin^3x$ means $(\sin x)^3$, not $\sin(x^3)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $x$ 是以弧度计量的实数角。正弦与余弦是单位圆上的纵、横坐标，因此 $\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$。由[[正弦余弦求导]]，有 $(\sin x)'=\cos x$、$(\cos x)'=-\sin x$；这正好给出可配对的两个因子。记号 $\sin^3x$ 表示 $(\sin x)^3$，不是 $\sin(x^3)$。

> [!example] Keep one sine factor · 留下一个正弦因子
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the opening example, write $\sin^3x=(\sin^2x)\sin x$. Keep the last $\sin x\,dx$ together, and replace $\sin^2x$ by $1-\cos^2x$. Then the [[换元积分|substitution]] $u=\cos x$ gives $du=-\sin x\,dx$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在开头的例子中，写成 $\sin^3x=(\sin^2x)\sin x$。把最后一个 $\sin x\,dx$ 留在一起，将剩下的 $\sin^2x$ 换成 $1-\cos^2x$。再作[[换元积分]] $u=\cos x$，便有 $du=-\sin x\,dx$：
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> \int\sin^3x\cos^2x\,dx
> &=\int(1-\cos^2x)\cos^2x\sin x\,dx\\
> &=-\int(1-u^2)u^2\,du\\
> &=-\frac{u^3}{3}+\frac{u^5}{5}+C\\
> &=-\frac{\cos^3x}{3}+\frac{\cos^5x}{5}+C.
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The new integrand is an ordinary polynomial. The minus sign comes from the cosine derivative. Substitute back at the end; $C$ is an arbitrary real constant. Differentiating the answer gives $\cos^2x\sin x-\cos^4x\sin x=\sin^3x\cos^2x$, which checks both the sign and the powers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 新的被积函数是普通多项式。负号来自余弦的导数，最后要把变量换回；$C$ 是任意实常数。对答案求导，得到 $\cos^2x\sin x-\cos^4x\sin x=\sin^3x\cos^2x$，同时核对了负号和次数。

## Choose a route from the exponents
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三种路由

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $I_{m,n}=\int\sin^m x\cos^n x\,dx$, where $m,n$ are nonnegative integers. They count repeated factors; an exponent of zero means that factor is absent. Thus $m=n=0$ simply gives $\int1\,dx=x+C$. In all other cases, inspect the exponents as follows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $I_{m,n}=\int\sin^m x\cos^n x\,dx$，其中 $m,n$ 是非负整数，表示相应因子重复出现的次数；指数为零，表示这一因子不出现。因此，$m=n=0$ 就是 $\int1\,dx=x+C$。其余情形可按下面的方式检查指数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If **$m$ is odd**, write $m=2r+1$ with an integer $r\ge0$. Reserving one sine factor leaves only pairs of sine factors, each replaceable by $1-\cos^2x$. With $u=\cos x$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 **$m$ 为奇数**，写成 $m=2r+1$，其中整数 $r\ge0$。留下一个正弦因子以后，其余正弦全都成对，每一对都能换成 $1-\cos^2x$。令 $u=\cos x$，则

$$I_{2r+1,n}=-\int(1-u^2)^r u^n\,du.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If **$n$ is odd**, write $n=2s+1$ with an integer $s\ge0$. Reserve $\cos x\,dx$, replace the remaining cosine pairs by $1-\sin^2x$, and set $u=\sin x$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 **$n$ 为奇数**，写成 $n=2s+1$，其中整数 $s\ge0$。留下 $\cos x\,dx$，把其余成对的余弦换成 $1-\sin^2x$，再令 $u=\sin x$：

$$I_{m,2s+1}=\int u^m(1-u^2)^s\,du.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both expressions are polynomials because the exponents are nonnegative integers. If both original exponents are odd, either route works; compare the amount of expansion. These substitutions are chain-rule compositions, so they do not require sine or cosine to be one-to-one on the whole interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于指数都是非负整数，两个新表达式都是多项式。如果原来的两个指数都为奇数，两条路线均可采用，可以比较哪一种展开更少。这些换元使用的是链式法则的复合关系，不要求正弦或余弦在整个区间上一一对应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If **both exponents are even**, removing just one sine or cosine leaves an unpaired factor. Instead use the power-reducing identities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果**两个指数都为偶数**，只拿走一个正弦或余弦，就会留下一个无法配对的因子。此时改用降幂恒等式：

$$\sin^2x=\frac{1-\cos2x}{2},\qquad
\cos^2x=\frac{1+\cos2x}{2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These follow by combining $\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$ with $\cos2x=\cos^2x-\sin^2x$. After substitution and expansion, repeat a suitable route for any remaining powers. The angle may double, but the powers become lower; the resulting angle multiplier must be kept when integrating.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$ 与 $\cos2x=\cos^2x-\sin^2x$ 联立即可得到这两式。代入、展开后，对仍然存在的幂再选择合适路线。角度可能加倍，但次数降低；积分时必须保留角度倍数带来的系数。

> [!example] Both powers are even · 两个指数都是偶数
>
> $$\sin^2x\cos^2x
> =\frac{(1-\cos2x)(1+\cos2x)}4
> =\frac{1-\cos4x}{8}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus the integral becomes
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因而积分变成
>
> $$\int\sin^2x\cos^2x\,dx
> =\frac{x}{8}-\frac{\sin4x}{32}+C.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The denominator $32$ contains the extra factor $4$ because $(\sin4x)'=4\cos4x$. Reserving just one $\cos x\,dx$ and setting $u=\sin x$ would leave another $\cos x$ to express; that is not the immediate polynomial route. An inverse-branch substitution can still be valid, but introduces a square root and a sign choice instead of simplifying this example.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 分母 $32$ 中额外的因子 $4$ 来自 $(\sin4x)'=4\cos4x$。若只留下一个 $\cos x\,dx$ 并令 $u=\sin x$，还剩另一个 $\cos x$ 要处理，因此不会直接得到多项式积分。选定反函数分支后，换元仍可能合法，却会引入根号和符号选择，反而没有简化这个例子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The method above concerns nonnegative integer powers. Negative exponents introduce denominators, and fractional exponents require real-domain and sign checks; the same parity instructions cannot simply be applied to them. For [[正切正割幂积分|tangent and secant powers]], the useful derivative factors are different, so their route selection is treated separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里处理的是非负整数幂。负指数会引入分母，分数指数需要检查实数定义域与符号，不能直接套用同一套奇偶步骤。[[正切正割幂积分]]中可配对的导数因子不同，因此另行讨论其选择方法。
