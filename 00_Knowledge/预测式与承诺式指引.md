# 预测式指引解释预期政策而承诺式指引约束未来政策选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Delphic guidance explains expected policy, whereas Odyssean guidance commits future policy choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预测式（Delphic）指引说明央行对经济和相应政策路径的预测，不据此单独锁定未来行动；承诺式（Odyssean）指引则对未来行动作出承诺，试图通过公众相信会履行的未来选择影响今天。区别是未来选择受到什么约束，不是声明里有没有一个具体日期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Delphic guidance forecasts the economy and the associated policy path without independently binding future action. Odyssean guidance commits to future action and seeks to influence today's decisions through belief in that commitment. The distinction concerns constraints on future choices, not merely whether a date is stated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一句“未来利率将保持较低”有两种可能：一是央行预见经济更弱，现有反应方式因而给出低利率；二是在相同经济条件下，央行承诺比原有反应方式维持更久的宽松。两者都可能降低未来利率预期，但前者还可能传递较差的基本面消息，不能将观察到的利率下降直接当作额外刺激效果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Low future rates may mean a weaker forecast under an unchanged reaction function, or a commitment to maintain accommodation longer under the same economic conditions. Both can lower expected rates, but the former may reveal worse fundamentals. A fall in yields alone does not identify additional stimulus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

承诺式指引面临[[政策时间不一致]]：当未来时点到来，先前承诺已产生的益处成为过去，央行可能想改选。是否可信，要具体检查[[承诺与可信性|执行或声誉机制]]。沟通、[[央行透明与问责|公开解释与监督]]有助于公众判断，但宣布承诺不等于已经建立了不可反悔的约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Odyssean guidance faces [[政策时间不一致|time inconsistency]]: past benefits from anticipation are sunk when action becomes due. Credibility requires examining [[承诺与可信性|enforcement or reputation]]. [[央行透明与问责|Explanation and oversight]] help the public assess policy but do not automatically make a promise irrevocable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

日期式、状态依存式都可能包含不同程度的预测和承诺；现实公告也可能混合两者。做区分时固定同一组经济条件，问央行是在解释原有选择，还是改变了今后会做什么；具体门槛另见[[指引门槛与触发条件]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Date-based and state-contingent guidance can contain different degrees of forecasting and commitment, and announcements may mix them. Hold economic conditions fixed and ask whether policy is being explained or changed. See [[指引门槛与触发条件|guidance thresholds and triggers]] for the conditional logic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
