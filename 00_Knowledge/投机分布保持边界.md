# 投机解码保持的是指定目标采样器的分布，逐次文本和浮点结果相同需要额外条件

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Speculative decoding preserves the specified target sampler's distribution; identical text on each run and identical floating-point results require additional conditions.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

严格 [[投机解码]]的分布保证针对同一前缀下指定的目标采样器。[[投机采样修正]]中的 $p$ 必须是目标真正希望采样的分布，$q$ 必须是草稿真正用来抽候选的分布；验收和修正使用它们在同一 token 空间、同一条件前缀下的概率。数学上保持这个分布，不会自动使两种程序的每次随机输出或浮点中间结果完全一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The guarantee for strict [[投机解码|speculative decoding]] concerns a specified target sampler at the same prefix. In [[投机采样修正|speculative sampling correction]], $p$ must be the intended target sampling distribution and $q$ the distribution actually used to draw draft candidates. Acceptance and correction use their probabilities over the same token space and conditioning prefix. Preserving this distribution mathematically does not automatically make every random output or floating-point intermediate identical between programs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先明确目标是哪一个分布

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First specify the target distribution.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[语言模型输出头]]产生词表 logits 与基础概率，实际生成还可能设置温度、top-k 或 top-p 筛选及重新归一化。这些操作后的有效概率才是算法中的 $p$；草稿的 $q$ 也要对应其真实采样过程。两边可以采用不同的合法采样设置，正确性的关键是公式里使用的分布与实际抽样一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[语言模型输出头|language-model output head]] produces vocabulary logits and base probabilities, while generation may additionally apply temperature, top-k or top-p filtering, and renormalization. The resulting effective probabilities define $p$ in the algorithm; $q$ must likewise match the actual draft sampling procedure. The two sides may use different valid sampling settings. Correctness requires the distributions used in the formulas to match the actual draws.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如三个 token 的 logits 为 $(\log2,0,0)$，正温度 $\tau$ 下通过 [[Softmax]] 计算 $p_\tau=\operatorname{softmax}(\ell/\tau)$，则

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, take logits $(\log2,0,0)$ for three tokens. At positive temperature $\tau$, [[Softmax|softmax]] gives $p_\tau=\operatorname{softmax}(\ell/\tau)$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p_1=(1/2,1/4,1/4),\qquad
p_{1/2}=(2/3,1/6,1/6).
$$

若直接做 greedy，即按固定规则选最大 logit，此例最大项唯一，采样分布就是 $(1,0,0)$。保持这三种分布对应三个不同基线；不能在验收公式中用未经温度或筛选处理的概率，却宣称保持另一种采样设置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For greedy selection with a fixed rule for choosing the largest logit, the maximum is unique here and the distribution is $(1,0,0)$. Preserving these three distributions corresponds to three different baselines. Using probabilities before temperature or filtering in the acceptance formula does not establish preservation of a different sampling setup.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同分布与逐次输出的区别

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equal distributions and identical individual outputs.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个随机过程可以有相同输出分布，却在同一次运行中给出不同结果。取 $U\sim\operatorname{Uniform}(0,1)$，定义

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two random procedures can have the same output distribution while producing different results on a particular run. Let $U\sim\operatorname{Uniform}(0,1)$ and define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
X=\mathbf1\{U<1/2\},\qquad Y=\mathbf1\{U\ge1/2\}.
$$

$X$ 与 $Y$ 都以 $1/2$ 的概率取 1，却每次取相反的值。这是“同分布不推出样本相同”的构造反例。投机过程会把随机数用于草稿、验收和修正，消费次序和数量可与普通采样不同；单独设置相同 seed，不能由分布定理推出逐 token 文本相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both $X$ and $Y$ equal 1 with probability $1/2$, yet their realized values always differ. This constructed counterexample shows that equal distributions do not imply equal samples. Speculation uses random draws for drafting, acceptance, and correction, potentially changing their order and count relative to ordinary sampling. Setting the same seed alone does not let the distribution theorem imply identical token sequences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

greedy 是特殊情形：若每个真实前缀上的目标 argmax 结果、并列最大值处理和停止规则都相同，确定的输出序列就相同。这里的保证来自相同的确定决策；随机采样的分布保证没有自动附带这项逐次一致性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Greedy decoding is a special case: if the target argmax at every real prefix, tie-breaking, and stopping rules agree, the deterministic output sequence agrees. This follows from identical deterministic decisions; distribution preservation in stochastic sampling does not automatically add that per-run identity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 浮点计算还要满足什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Additional requirements for floating-point computation.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数学上相同的计算，采用不同 batch 形状、kernel、运算次序或精度时，浮点结果不保证逐 bit 一致。若目标的批量验证实际得到 $\tilde p$，理想修正证明针对的是 $\tilde p$；它本身不会证明 $\tilde p$ 等于普通生成路径的 $p$，也不会消除实现中的舍入误差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mathematically equivalent computations need not produce bitwise-identical floating-point results when batch shapes, kernels, operation order, or precision differ. If batched target verification actually produces $\tilde p$, the ideal correction proof concerns $\tilde p$. The proof itself neither establishes that $\tilde p$ equals the ordinary generation path's $p$ nor removes implementation rounding error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，改变草稿只改变提议分布 $q$，使用正确的新 $q$ 时仍可保持既定目标；改变目标模型的权重或数值计算则可能改变 $p$，须先重新明确参照分布。某实现的数值一致性必须在其实际计算路径下检验，不能仅由算法名称认证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Likewise, changing the draft changes the proposal distribution $q$, and using the correct new $q$ can still preserve the specified target. Changing the target's weights or numerical computation may change $p$, requiring the reference distribution to be specified again. Numerical consistency of an implementation must be checked along its actual computation paths; the algorithm's name does not certify it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Leviathan, Kalman & Matias (2023), *Fast Inference from Transformers via Speculative Decoding*](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/leviathan23a/leviathan23a.pdf)，§2.2、§3.6、Appendix A.1：支持把采样设置转为有效分布，以及对实际 $p,q$ 的分布保持保证；Appendix A.5 则明确允许宽松验收的变体会改变分布。温度计算与同分布反例为由定义直接构造的说明。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Sections 2.2 and 3.6 and Appendix A.1 establish effective sampling distributions and the preservation guarantee for the actual $p,q$. Appendix A.5 explicitly distinguishes variants that allow lenient acceptance and change the distribution. The temperature calculation and equal-distribution counterexample are constructed directly from the definitions.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [PyTorch, *Numerical accuracy*](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/notes/numerical_accuracy.html)，正文开头及 “Batched computations or slice computations”：支持有限精度下运算次序和批量计算不保证逐 bit 相同。这里据此限定概率定理的实现结论，不声称已经测试任何具体推理系统。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The opening discussion and “Batched computations or slice computations” establish that operation ordering and batching under finite precision do not guarantee bitwise identity. This limits what can be inferred from the probability theorem about implementations; no particular inference system is claimed to have been tested.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
