# 产品价值包含耗用生产资料的转移价值与活劳动新创造的价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The product's value combines transferred value from consumed means of production with new value created by living labour*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Marx 的一次生产期价值分析中，产品价值包含两部分：本期正常耗用的生产资料所转移的旧价值，以及本期活劳动新创造的价值。令 $c_u$ 表示前者，$v$ 表示本期劳动力价值的等价物，$s$ 表示 [[剩余价值]]，则产品价值 $W$ 与新创造价值 $N$ 分别为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Marx's value analysis of a production period, the product contains transferred value from means of production normally used up during that period and new value created by current living labour. Let $c_u$ denote the former, $v$ the equivalent of labour-power's value for the period, and $s$ [[剩余价值|surplus value]]. The value of the product $W$ and newly created value $N$ are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
W=c_u+v+s,\qquad N=v+s,\qquad s=W-c_u-v.
$$

$c_u$ 里的下标提醒我们只计当期耗用。若一台机器仅磨损了自身价值的一小部分，其未耗用余额仍留在机器里，不进入本期产品价值。若改为比较全部预付资本与生产结束后的总价值，就须在产品之外，把期末仍持有的设备余额一并列入结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The subscript in $c_u$ marks consumption during the period. If only a fraction of a machine's value is worn away, the unused balance remains in the machine and does not enter the current product. A comparison of total capital advanced with the total value held after production must include the remaining machinery value alongside the product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

教学例沿用8小时工作日：在相同社会劳动折算、正常且不变的强度和固定货币价值下，每小时新创100价值单位；劳动力按其一天400的价值购买。因此新价值为800，其中 $v=400$、$s=400$。若另有1200的生产资料价值正常耗用并转移，则产品价值为2000。2000、800与400分别回答产品含有多少价值、当期新创多少价值、其中有多少剩余。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use an eight-hour day as a constructed example. With consistent social-labour conversion, normal unchanged intensity and a fixed value of money, each hour creates 100 value units. Labour-power is purchased at its daily value of 400. New value is therefore 800, divided into $v=400$ and $s=400$. If 1,200 units of means-of-production value are normally consumed and transferred, the product is worth 2,000. The figures 2,000, 800 and 400 answer respectively how much value the product contains, how much is newly created, and how much of the latter is surplus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这套分解依赖 [[不变资本]] 与 [[可变资本]] 在价值形成中的不同作用。此例只考察一天、一次劳动力使用期；跨多次周转时，期间工资支出总额可能超过一次预付的可变资本。应用到利润率前，应另行统一 [[预付与耗用资本|全部预付资本、期间耗用与周转]] 的口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This decomposition depends on the different roles of [[不变资本|constant capital]] and [[可变资本|variable capital]] in forming value. This example covers one day and one period of labour-power's use. Across multiple turnovers, total wage expenditure for a period may exceed the variable capital advanced once. Before applying a profit rate, align [[预付与耗用资本|total advances, period consumption and turnover]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Marx，《资本论》卷I，[第9章第1节](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch09.htm#S1)，从 $C=c+v$ 与 $C'=(c+v)+s$ 到机器£1000余额、£54耗损和“value-product”的区分：支持当期耗用口径及产品价值与新价值的分解。MIA 所载 Moore / Aveling 英译、Engels 编，1887英文初版底本，标示处纳入德文第四版改动。本卡用 $c_u$ 显示原文该处 $c$ 的耗用限定；数例为教学构造。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volume I, Chapter 9, Section 1 moves from $C=c+v$ and $C'=(c+v)+s$ through the £1,000 machinery balance and £54 wear to the distinction between the product's value and the value-product. It supports the period-consumption basis and the decomposition. The MIA text uses the Moore and Aveling translation edited by Engels, based on the 1887 first English edition with indicated fourth German edition changes. The notation $c_u$ makes the passage's consumption restriction explicit; the numerical illustration is constructed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
