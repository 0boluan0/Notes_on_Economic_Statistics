# Sufficient Markov states
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*Markov 充分状态*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **sufficient state** is a summary of the available history that loses no information needed for the future prediction under consideration. For a machine, “working” may be enough in one model; in another, an old working machine fails more often than a new one, so the state may need to include its age. The question is whether two histories assigned the same state give the same forecast, not how many numbers the state contains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**充分状态**是对已有历史的一份摘要，它没有丢掉当前预测任务所需要的信息。对一台机器，“正常工作”在一个模型里可能已经够用；在另一个模型里，旧机器比新机器更容易坏，状态就可能还需要记录年龄。关键是被归入同一状态的两段历史是否给出同样的预测，而不是状态里写了多少个数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[随机过程|stochastic process]] records a variable at successive times. Write $Z_0,\ldots,Z_n$ for the observations so far and $S_n=f_n(Z_0,\ldots,Z_n)$ for a proposed summary at time $n$. The function $f_n$ is simply the rule that turns the record into a state. A [[条件分布|conditional distribution]] describes the probabilities after information is supplied. Sufficiency asks whether supplying the whole record improves that distribution after $S_n$ is already known.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[随机过程]]记录一个变量在不同时点的取值。把目前的观测写成 $Z_0,\ldots,Z_n$，候选摘要写成 $S_n=f_n(Z_0,\ldots,Z_n)$；$f_n$ 就是把记录整理成状态的规则。[[条件分布]]描述在已知信息下的各个概率。充分性问的是：已经知道 $S_n$ 以后，再提供全部记录，是否还会改变这份分布。

> [!abstract] The same forecast from either record · 两份信息给出相同预测
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $\mathcal H_n$ denote the available history, mathematically the [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]] generated by the observations up to $n$. A state $S_n$ constructed from that history is sufficient for the selected future process when, for every event $A$ concerning that future,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用 $\mathcal H_n$ 表示已有历史，在数学上是截至 $n$ 的观测生成的[[σ-代数]]。由这份历史构造的状态 $S_n$，若对所选未来过程的每个事件 $A$ 都满足下式，就对该未来的预测充分：
>
> $$\Pr(A\mid\mathcal H_n)=\Pr(A\mid S_n).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For example, $A$ could be “the next value is $1$” or “the next two values are both $1$”. This is equality of the whole future law, not just equality of its mean. With discrete histories, compare histories of positive probability; in general, conditional identities hold almost surely, meaning that a set of histories of total probability zero may be excluded. Do not divide by the probability of a zero-probability history.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 例如，$A$ 可以是“下一值为 $1$”，也可以是“接下来两个值都为 $1$”。要求相同的是完整的未来分布，而不只是未来的均值。离散情形比较正概率历史；一般的条件等式按几乎必然成立理解，即允许排除总概率为零的一组历史。不能拿零概率历史的概率作分母。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the predictive content of the [[Markov性|Markov property]]: once the state is known, the retained history and the future are [[条件独立|conditionally independent]]. A sufficient summary need not be the smallest possible one. Keeping extra historical information does not destroy predictive sufficiency, provided that the enlarged state is still constructed from the same available history.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就是 [[Markov性]]所表达的预测含义：知道状态后，已有历史与未来[[条件独立]]。充分摘要不一定最小；只要新增信息仍来自同一份已有历史，多保留一些历史信息不会破坏这种预测充分性。

> [!example] Position in a random walk · 随机游走的当前位置
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Start at $Z_0=0$ and set $Z_{n+1}=Z_n+\varepsilon_{n+1}$, where the increments $\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2,\ldots$ are [[独立同分布|independent and identically distributed]], each equal to $1$ or $-1$ with probability $1/2$. At position $s$, the next position is $s+1$ or $s-1$, each with probability $1/2$. Two steps later the probabilities at $s+2,s,s-2$ are $1/4,1/2,1/4$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $Z_0=0$ 出发，令 $Z_{n+1}=Z_n+\varepsilon_{n+1}$，其中增量 $\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2,\ldots$ [[独立同分布]]，各以 $1/2$ 的概率等于 $1$ 或 $-1$。目前位于 $s$ 时，下一步到 $s+1$ 或 $s-1$ 的概率各为 $1/2$；两步后在 $s+2,s,s-2$ 的概率分别为 $1/4,1/2,1/4$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every future position is the current position plus future increments. Those increments are independent of the observed past, so the route taken to reach $s$ changes none of these forecasts. Thus $S_n=Z_n$ is sufficient for the future positions. The same position can summarise many different routes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个未来位置都等于当前位置加上之后的增量。未来增量与已观察的过去独立，因此到达 $s$ 所走的路线不再改变预测，$S_n=Z_n$ 就足以预测未来位置。同一个位置可以概括很多条不同路线。

## State the history and the prediction target
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先说明以哪份历史预测什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sufficiency is relative to the information and the future being predicted. For example, in the walk above, $Z_n$ need not remain sufficient if the information set is enlarged to reveal the next increment in advance. Nor does sufficiency for the next position automatically mean sufficiency for some additional unmodelled outcome. Missing inventory, elapsed duration or an unobserved regime can matter in other models; each proposed state must be checked against that model's conditional laws.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

充分性取决于所用信息和预测对象。例如，上面的随机游走若提前告诉你下一次增量，信息集合扩大后，仅凭 $Z_n$ 就未必仍然充分。对下一位置充分，也不自动表示对另一个未纳入模型的结果充分。其他模型中的库存、已持续时间或未观测制度，都可能影响预测；必须按该模型的条件律核对候选状态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A direct way to disprove sufficiency is to find two positive-probability histories with the same summary but different forecasts. Let $U,V$ be independent fair bits, set $Z_0=U$, $Z_1=V$, and $Z_n=U$ for all $n\ge2$. At time $1$, the summary $S_1=Z_1=V$ gives probability $1/2$ that $Z_2=1$. The fuller record knows $U$ and gives probability $0$ or $1$. Keeping $(Z_1,Z_0)$ supplies the missing information. Checking one realised path would not reveal all such comparisons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要否定充分性，可以找两段正概率历史：摘要相同，预测却不同。令 $U,V$ 为独立公平二元结果，设 $Z_0=U$、$Z_1=V$，以后 $n\ge2$ 都令 $Z_n=U$。在时点 $1$，摘要 $S_1=Z_1=V$ 给出的 $Z_2=1$ 概率为 $1/2$；完整记录知道 $U$，给出的概率却是 $0$ 或 $1$。保留 $(Z_1,Z_0)$ 才补上这份信息。只观察一条实现路径，并不能完成所有这样的比较。

## Make the summary usable at the next step
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*还要能把摘要更新到下一步*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To use summaries as a state process, check how they update. A useful sufficient construction has $S_{n+1}=G_n(S_n,Z_{n+1})$: the current summary and the next observation determine the next summary, without retrieving discarded history. If the conditional law of $Z_{n+1}$ given the full history depends only on $S_n$, applying this update makes the law of $S_{n+1}$ depend only on $S_n$ and the time $n$. The resulting state process is Markov. Applying that argument at every step also gives forecasts for future blocks. [[状态扩充|State augmentation]] implements this construction by keeping the needed recent observations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把摘要用作状态过程，还需要检查怎样更新。一种可靠的充分构造是 $S_{n+1}=G_n(S_n,Z_{n+1})$：只用当前摘要与新观测，就能得到下一摘要，不必重新取回已经丢弃的历史。若在完整历史下 $Z_{n+1}$ 的条件律只依赖 $S_n$，经过这种更新，$S_{n+1}$ 的分布便只依赖 $S_n$ 和时点 $n$，所得状态过程就具有 Markov 性。每一步都这样推下去，也能给出未来片段的预测。[[状态扩充]]通过保留所需的近期观测来实现这一构造。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The update rule can still depend on time. A sufficient state does not by itself give a [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|time-homogeneous kernel]]. Also, predicting only one chosen outcome correctly is weaker than retaining the whole future law required in the definition above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更新规则仍可能随时间改变，因此状态充分并不自动产生[[DTMC时间齐次转移核]]。另外，只能正确预测某一个指定结果，也弱于上面定义要求的保留完整未来分布。
