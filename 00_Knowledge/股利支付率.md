# 股利支付率是同一权益范围与期间口径的股利相对于净利润的比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The dividend payout ratio is dividends relative to net income on a matched equity and period basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股利支付率衡量所选期间利润有多大比例用于相应股利分配。若同一权益范围的净利润为 $NI\ne0$、对应股利为 $Div$，则 $d=Div/NI$。分析普通股时，两者都应归属于普通股，不能用普通股股利除包含其他权益持有者收益的利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The payout ratio measures the share of selected period earnings used for corresponding dividends: $d=Div/NI$ for $NI\ne0$. For ordinary shares, both amounts must belong to ordinary shareholders rather than combining their dividends with other holders' earnings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如普通股利润 100、按该利润分配现金股利 30，支付率为 30%，[[利润留存率]]为 70%。若股数和分配口径完全一致，也可用每股股利／每股收益；但 [[基本每股收益|EPS 的期间加权股数]]与股利登记日股数可能不同，不能不核对就把每股式当总额式的恒等变形。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary profit of 100 and corresponding cash dividends of 30 give a 30% payout and a 70% [[利润留存率|retention ratio]]. Dividends per share divided by EPS is equivalent only with matched share and distribution bases. [[基本每股收益|Weighted EPS shares]] may differ from dividend-entitled shares, so the per-share expression need not equal the aggregate ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要写清是“本期利润对应的分配”还是“本期实际付现”：去年利润的分红今年支付，现金时点不同。净利润为零时不定义；亏损期或使用以前年度积累派发特别股利时，负值或超过 100% 都不能按通常分配份额解释。它也不是投资者按买价得到的[[股利收益率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish distributions associated with current earnings from cash paid this period: last year's dividend may be paid this year. Zero income makes the ratio undefined; losses or special distributions from prior accumulations make negative or above-100% ratios unsuitable as ordinary earnings shares. Payout is not the investor's price-based [[股利收益率|dividend yield]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
