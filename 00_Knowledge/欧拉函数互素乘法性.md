# Coprime multiplicativity of Euler's totient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*欧拉函数互素乘法性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The four remainders coprime to twelve are $1,5,7,11$. Instead of checking all twelve candidates, we can record each candidate by its remainder modulo three and its remainder modulo four. The useful fact is that every pair of coprime remainders on those two sides comes from exactly one coprime remainder modulo twelve. Counting pairs then turns a larger count into a product of two smaller counts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与十二互素的余数是 $1,5,7,11$。我们可以不逐个检查十二个候选，而是用“模三的余数”和“模四的余数”共同记录它。关键在于：两侧各取一个互素余数，每个这样的余数对都恰好来自一个与十二互素的余数。因此，数余数对就把大模数下的计数变成两个较小计数的乘积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a positive integer $m$, [[欧拉函数|Euler's totient]] $\varphi(m)$ counts the representatives $r=0,\ldots,m-1$ with [[最大公因数|gcd]] $\gcd(r,m)=1$. Such integers are called **coprime**. When $m\ge2$, the [[模逆元存在条件|inverse criterion]] says that these are exactly the classes having a [[模逆元|modular inverse]], a multiplier $u$ for which $ru\equiv1\pmod m$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正整数 $m$，[[欧拉函数]] $\varphi(m)$ 统计代表 $r=0,\ldots,m-1$ 中满足[[最大公因数]] $\gcd(r,m)=1$ 的个数；这时称两整数**互素**。当 $m\ge2$ 时，[[模逆元存在条件]]说明，这恰好数出了有[[模逆元]]的类：存在乘数 $u$ 使 $ru\equiv1\pmod m$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Multiply counts only after checking coprimality
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先检查互素，再把计数相乘*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For coprime positive integers, the displayed multiplicative identity holds. Coprimality is essential: the totient of four is two, while the square of the totient of two is one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $a,b$ 为正整数且 $\gcd(a,b)=1$，则
$$
\varphi(ab)=\varphi(a)\varphi(b).
$$
这里的互素前提不可省略：$\varphi(2\cdot2)=2$，但 $\varphi(2)\varphi(2)=1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $a,b$ are positive integer inputs to the totient, rather than arbitrary real numbers. The word “multiplicative” in this statement refers to products of **coprime** inputs. It does not permit splitting any product into factors and multiplying their totients. We will first prove the case $a,b\ge2$, where the linked modular-inverse and CRT statements apply, then handle a factor equal to one directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $a,b$ 是欧拉函数的正整数输入，不是任意实数。本结论中的“乘法性”针对**互素**输入，不允许任取一种因数拆法就把欧拉函数值相乘。先证明 $a,b\ge2$ 的情形，以便使用所链逆元与中国剩余定理，再直接处理某个因子为一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Pair every large remainder with two smaller remainders
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把一个大模数余数配成两个小模数余数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a,b\ge2$ with $\gcd(a,b)=1$, let $x$ range over $0,\ldots,ab-1$. Send it to the ordered pair of its standard remainders:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a,b\ge2$ 且 $\gcd(a,b)=1$，让 $x$ 遍历 $0,\ldots,ab-1$，把它送到其两个标准余数组成的有序对：

$$F(x)=\bigl(\operatorname{rem}(x,a),\operatorname{rem}(x,b)\bigr).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first coordinate lies in $0,\ldots,a-1$ and the second in $0,\ldots,b-1$; $\operatorname{rem}$ means the remainder given by [[带余除法|division with remainder]]. The [[中国剩余定理|Chinese remainder theorem]] says that for every such pair there is exactly one $x$ in the chosen range. Thus $F$ is a **bijection**: it neither misses a pair nor assigns the same pair to two different candidates. This is the reversible correspondence used in [[双射计数|counting by bijection]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一坐标落在 $0,\ldots,a-1$，第二坐标落在 $0,\ldots,b-1$；$\operatorname{rem}$ 表示[[带余除法]]给出的余数。[[中国剩余定理]]保证：每个这样的有序对，都恰好来自所选范围内的一个 $x$。因此 $F$ 是**双射**，既不遗漏余数对，也不把两个不同候选送到同一个余数对；这就是[[双射计数]]使用的可逆对应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bijection on all remainders is not yet enough: the totient counts only the coprime ones. We must prove that the correspondence takes exactly those candidates to pairs whose two components are coprime to their respective moduli.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全部余数之间有双射还不够，因为欧拉函数只数其中互素的部分。还要证明：恰好这些候选对应“两坐标分别与各自模数互素”的余数对。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a,b\ge2$, the [[中国剩余定理|Chinese remainder theorem]] pairs a residue modulo $ab$ with residues modulo $a$ and $b$. An inverse modulo the product gives inverses in both components. Conversely, CRT combines component inverses into one inverse modulo the product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $a,b\ge2$ 时，[[中国剩余定理]] 给出模 $ab$ 的余数类与“一个模 $a$ 余数类、一个模 $b$ 余数类”的双射。这个对应还保持可逆性：若 $xy\equiv1\pmod{ab}$，则在两个小模数下也成立；反过来，若两个分量各有逆元，CRT 将它们的逆元合成一个 $y$，使 $xy$ 同时模 $a,b$ 为 $1$，于是模 $ab$ 也为 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To expand the forward direction, suppose $x$ has an inverse $y$ modulo $ab$. Then $ab\mid(xy-1)$ implies both $a\mid(xy-1)$ and $b\mid(xy-1)$. Reducing $x$ and $y$ to either smaller modulus preserves the product, so both components of $F(x)$ have inverses. By the inverse criterion, both components are counted by their own totients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把正方向展开：设 $x$ 有模 $ab$ 的逆元 $y$。由 $ab\mid(xy-1)$，可得 $a\mid(xy-1)$ 且 $b\mid(xy-1)$。把 $x,y$ 分别换成任一小模数下的余数，不改变该模数下的乘积，因此 $F(x)$ 的两坐标各有逆元。按逆元判据，两坐标各自都被其欧拉函数计入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the reverse direction, start with any pair $(r,s)$ counted on the two sides. Let $u$ satisfy $ru\equiv1\pmod a$ and let $v$ satisfy $sv\equiv1\pmod b$. First use CRT to obtain the unique $x$ with residues $(r,s)$. Use CRT again to obtain an integer $y$ with residues $(u,v)$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反方向从任意一个两侧都被计入的余数对 $(r,s)$ 出发。取 $u$ 满足 $ru\equiv1\pmod a$，取 $v$ 满足 $sv\equiv1\pmod b$。先用 CRT 找到余数对为 $(r,s)$ 的唯一代表 $x$；再用一次 CRT 找到余数对为 $(u,v)$ 的整数 $y$。于是

$$xy\equiv ru\equiv1\pmod a,\qquad xy\equiv sv\equiv1\pmod b.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both $xy$ and $1$ satisfy the same two remainder conditions. CRT uniqueness modulo $ab$ therefore gives $xy\equiv1\pmod{ab}$. So $x$ has an inverse modulo the product and is counted by $\varphi(ab)$. The first CRT use joins the chosen residues; the second joins their inverses. This proves that no permitted pair is lost when we restrict the correspondence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$xy$ 与 $1$ 满足同样的两条余数条件，因此 CRT 的模 $ab$ 唯一性给出 $xy\equiv1\pmod{ab}$。所以 $x$ 有模乘积的逆元，被 $\varphi(ab)$ 计入。第一次 CRT 合并选定的余数，第二次合并它们的逆元；这就证明，把对应限制到互素部分时，没有遗漏任何允许的余数对。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are $\varphi(a)$ choices for the first component, and for each of them all $\varphi(b)$ second components are allowed. The [[乘法法则|product rule of counting]] gives $\varphi(a)\varphi(b)$ pairs. The restricted bijection gives the same count for the coprime classes modulo $ab$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一坐标有 $\varphi(a)$ 种选择；无论选哪一种，第二坐标都可以取全部 $\varphi(b)$ 种。按[[乘法法则]]，余数对共有 $\varphi(a)\varphi(b)$ 个。限制后的双射说明，模 $ab$ 的互素类也恰好有这么多个。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## See all four pairs for twelve
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*看清十二对应的四个余数对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The invertible classes therefore correspond bijectively, so their counts multiply. If one factor is one, the identity follows directly from $\varphi(1)=1$. For example, the coprime factorization $12=3\cdot4$ gives a totient of four.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此可逆余数类也一一对应，按乘法计数就有 $\varphi(ab)=\varphi(a)\varphi(b)$。若某个因子为 $1$，结论直接由 $\varphi(1)=1$ 得到。例如 $12=3\cdot4$ 且 $3,4$ 互素，所以 $\varphi(12)=\varphi(3)\varphi(4)=2\cdot2=4$。

> [!example] The complete pairing · 完整配对
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $a=3,b=4$, the coprime remainders are $1,2$ on the first side and $1,3$ on the second. The four candidates below give all four ordered pairs exactly once. For $x=5$, its component inverses are $2$ modulo three and $1$ modulo four; the integer $y=5$ combines them, and $5\cdot5=25\equiv1\pmod{12}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $a=3,b=4$，第一侧的互素余数为 $1,2$，第二侧为 $1,3$。下面四个候选把全部四个有序对各给出一次。对 $x=5$，分量逆元分别为模三的 $2$ 和模四的 $1$；整数 $y=5$ 合并这两个逆元，且 $5\cdot5=25\equiv1\pmod{12}$。

$$1\longmapsto(1,1),\quad5\longmapsto(2,1),\quad7\longmapsto(1,3),\quad11\longmapsto(2,3).$$

## Keep the factor-one case separate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*因子为一时单独处理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $a=1$, then $ab=b$ and $\varphi(a)\varphi(b)=1\cdot\varphi(b)=\varphi(ab)$; the case $b=1$ is the same, including $a=b=1$. The value $\varphi(1)=1$ follows from its counting definition: the sole candidate is zero, and $\gcd(0,1)=1$. We do not apply the linked $n>1$ inverse definition or CRT theorem at modulus one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $a=1$，则 $ab=b$，而 $\varphi(a)\varphi(b)=1\cdot\varphi(b)=\varphi(ab)$；$b=1$ 时同理，也包括 $a=b=1$。$\varphi(1)=1$ 来自计数定义：唯一候选是零，且 $\gcd(0,1)=1$。这里没有把所链的 $n>1$ 逆元定义或 CRT 套到模一。

> [!warning] A factorization need not be coprime · 因数拆分未必互素
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Writing $12=2\cdot6$ is a valid integer factorization, but $\gcd(2,6)=2$, so this theorem does not justify multiplying $\varphi(2)=1$ by $\varphi(6)=2$. Their product is two, whereas $\varphi(12)=4$. The useful split $12=3\cdot4$ is coprime. For a general positive integer, the [[欧拉函数乘积公式|totient product formula]] uses powers of distinct primes to obtain valid coprime factors.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $12=2\cdot6$ 是正确的整数因数分解，但 $\gcd(2,6)=2$，所以本定理不能用来把 $\varphi(2)=1$ 与 $\varphi(6)=2$ 相乘；所得二并不是 $\varphi(12)=4$。可用的拆法 $12=3\cdot4$ 才互素。对一般正整数，[[欧拉函数乘积公式]]使用不同素数的幂，得到符合条件的互素因子。
