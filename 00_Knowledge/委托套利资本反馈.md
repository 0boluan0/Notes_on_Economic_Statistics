# 受约束纠偏头寸的亏损与赎回可通过被迫减仓放大错误定价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Losses and redemptions on constrained corrective positions can amplify mispricing through forced position reductions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个反馈机制从已经建立的纠偏头寸出发：错误定价进一步扩大造成亏损，[[套利资本随业绩变动|业绩敏感的外部资金]]随之撤出；若现金和新融资不足，经理必须减仓，而承接需求又不足以在原价格吸收出售，价格便可能进一步偏离基本价值。每一步都有条件，不能把“亏损”直接改写为“必然撤资、必然火售”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mechanism starts from an existing corrective position: worsening mispricing causes losses, and [[套利资本随业绩变动|performance-sensitive outside capital]] withdraws. If cash and new funding are insufficient, the manager must reduce holdings; if buyers cannot absorb the sale at the old price, price can move still further from fundamental value. Each step is conditional: a loss does not automatically imply redemption or a fire sale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Shleifer–Vishny 的一个可复算情形是：基本价值 $V$ 固定，总供给为 1，悲观需求为 $(V-S_t)/p_t$。若第 1 期已经全额投入 $F_1$，第 2 期仍低估且把剩余资金 $F_2$ 全部投入，则市场出清和线性资金规则为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A reproducible Shleifer–Vishny case fixes fundamental value at $V$, total supply at 1, and pessimistic demand at $(V-S_t)/p_t$. If resources $F_1$ are fully invested initially and remaining resources $F_2$ are fully invested in the still-underpriced asset next period, market clearing and the linear funding rule give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p_1=V-S_1+F_1,\qquad
p_2=V-S_2+F_2,\qquad
F_2=F_1-aF_1\left(1-\frac{p_2}{p_1}\right).
$$

$$
p_2=\frac{V-S_2-aF_1+F_1}{1-aF_1/p_1}.
$$

这里限定 $a>1$、$0<aF_1<p_1$，并检查 $0<p_2<p_1<V$ 和 $F_2>0$；这些条件把我们留在正价格、剩余资本为正的稳定分支。固定第 1 期状态，在这一分支有 $\partial p_2/\partial S_2=-1/(1-aF_1/p_1)<-1$：价格对悲观冲击的反应超过一比一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Restrict attention to $a>1$ and $0<aF_1<p_1$, checking $0<p_2<p_1<V$ and $F_2>0$. These conditions keep the calculation on the stable branch with positive price and remaining capital. Holding the first-period state fixed, $\partial p_2/\partial S_2=-1/(1-aF_1/p_1)<-1$ on this branch: price responds more than one-for-one to pessimism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将原论文例子按金额放大 100 倍，取 $V=100,S_1=30,S_2=40,F_1=20,a=1.2$，并给定初期满仓。可得 $p_1=90$、$p_2=76.3636$、$F_2=16.3636$。原持仓为 $20/90=0.222222$ 单位，第 2 期流量发生前市值为 16.9697，净赎回为 $16.9697-16.3636=0.6061$；剩余持仓为 $F_2/p_2=0.214286$，确实减少，而不仅是市值下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Scale the paper's monetary example by 100: set $V=100,S_1=30,S_2=40,F_1=20,a=1.2$ and take full initial investment as given. Then $p_1=90$, $p_2=76.3636$, and $F_2=16.3636$. Initial holdings are $20/90=0.222222$ units, worth 16.9697 before second-period flows. Net redemption is $16.9697-16.3636=0.6061$, leaving $F_2/p_2=0.214286$ units: holdings actually fall, not merely their marked value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满仓是该模型中配合 $a>1$ 和不利冲击而保证减仓的一个充分条件，不是减仓的必要条件。若 $a=1$，在此满仓设定中只有投资损失、没有额外赎回，单位持仓不变。充足现金、不随近期回报撤出的资金，或有能力承接的新买家，都可能削弱乃至切断出售反馈。一般火售如何侵蚀偿付能力见[[火售流动性偿付反馈]]；本卡关注价格纠偏资本为何反而退出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full initial investment, together with $a>1$ and an adverse shock, is a sufficient condition for liquidation in this model, not a necessary one. With $a=1$ in this fully invested setup, investment losses occur without extra redemption and unit holdings remain unchanged. Ample cash, funding insensitive to recent returns, or capable new buyers can weaken or interrupt the selling feedback. [[火售流动性偿付反馈|Fire-sale liquidity–solvency feedback]] covers general solvency effects; this card concerns the withdrawal of price-correcting capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也不能从这个坏状态推出“资金越看业绩，市场在所有情形都越无效”。原论文指出，若价格先部分回归，业绩改善带来的流入可能加快纠偏；事前整体效果取决于冲击过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nor does this adverse state establish that stronger performance sensitivity makes markets less efficient in every setting. The paper notes that inflows after partial recovery can accelerate correction; the overall ex-ante effect depends on the shock process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
