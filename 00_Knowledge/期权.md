# 期权是赋予持有人按约定条件选择行权并使卖方承担相应履约义务的合约
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An option gives its holder an exercise choice under specified terms and imposes the corresponding obligation on its writer*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通期权的买方支付权利金，取得在约定期限内按执行价买入或卖出标的的权利，也可以不行权；卖方收取权利金，若被指派履约则须按合同执行。买入权利是[[看涨期权]]，卖出权利是[[看跌期权]]。“看跌”说的是权利种类，不代表持有人在卖出这份期权。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The buyer of a plain option pays a premium for the right to buy or sell the underlying at a strike within specified exercise terms, and may choose not to exercise. The writer receives the premium and must perform if assigned. The buying right is a [[看涨期权|call]]; the selling right is a [[看跌期权|put]]. A put holder owns a selling right, rather than necessarily being short the option.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

欧式期权只允许在约定到期时行权，美式期权允许在约定有效期内提前行权；这两个名称不指交易地点。合约还要写明实物交割或现金结算、覆盖数量、到期和操作截止时间。是否值得提前行权是估值问题，不能由“允许”直接推成“应该”；一个具体结论见[[无股息看涨不提前行权]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
European exercise is restricted to expiry; American exercise permits earlier exercise within the specified period. These labels do not identify geography. Delivery or cash settlement, quantity, expiry, and instruction deadlines also matter. Permission to exercise early does not imply that doing so is optimal; see [[无股息看涨不提前行权|the no-early-exercise result for a non-dividend-paying call]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全额支付权利金且单独持有普通期权的买方，期权本身可以跌至零。若行权后取得股票、建立空头或承担交割付款，则后续头寸另有风险与资金要求；不能把“期权买方损失有限”扩展到行权后的整个账户。实值期权也可能按经纪商流程自动行权，不能靠“不主动操作”来假定没有交割。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fully paid standalone plain option can expire worthless. Exercising may create stock ownership, a short position, or a delivery payment, each with separate funding and risk. The option's limited loss does not extend to the entire post-exercise account. Broker procedures may automatically exercise an in-the-money option.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
