# 行业的长期成长与短期周期敏感性是可以同时存在的不同维度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Long-run industry growth and short-run cyclical sensitivity are different dimensions that can coexist.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

成长回答需求或业务规模是否有持续扩张动力；周期敏感性回答它对整体经济波动反应多强。一个行业可以长期扩张，同时在衰退中大幅减速或下降。因此“成长型”与“周期型”不必互斥。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Growth asks whether demand or business scale has a persistent expansion driver. Cyclical sensitivity asks how strongly it responds to aggregate fluctuations. An industry can expand over the long run yet slow sharply or contract in a recession, so growth and cyclicality need not be mutually exclusive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个纯示意分解说明：正常趋势带来 10% 的年增长，衰退冲击贡献 −15 个百分点，则当年增长为 −5%。这不是估计模型或行业预测，只说明正趋势不排除周期性下降。原课的“成长周期型”正提醒同时保留这两个维度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a purely illustrative additive decomposition, trend growth contributes 10% and a recession shock subtracts 15 percentage points, giving −5% growth that year. This is not an estimated model or forecast; it demonstrates that a positive trend does not preclude cyclical contraction. The course's growth-cyclical category makes the same distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
