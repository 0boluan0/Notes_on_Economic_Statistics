# 离散执行连续复制策略可能留下追踪误差且增加频率不能消除所有风险来源
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Discrete implementation of a continuous replication strategy can leave tracking error, and greater frequency does not remove every risk source*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在两次交易之间，实际持仓不随连续变化的理论 Delta 更新。令 $C_t$ 为目标合约价值、$X_t$ 为实际自融资对冲资产，取追踪误差 $E_t=C_t-X_t$；对于“卖出合约、持有对冲资产”的组合，按相同价格平仓的净值是 $-E_t$。沿用初始 $X_0=C_0$、无中间外部现金流的账时，合计初始净投入为零，该净值也等于损益。到期时才可将 $C_T$ 换成合约支付。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Between trades, actual holdings do not update with the continuously changing theoretical delta. With target value $C_t$ and self-financing hedge assets $X_t$, define tracking error $E_t=C_t-X_t$. The short-contract, long-hedge book has closeout net value $-E_t$ under the same prices. With initial $X_0=C_0$ and no interim external cash flows, the combined initial investment is zero, so this net value also equals P&L. Only at maturity does $C_T$ become the contractual payoff.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不表示每个合约、每条路径的误差必非零。例如零利率下无分红股票的到期支付 $aS_T+b$，可一直持有 $a$ 股和现金 $b$ 精确复制，不需要连续调仓；非线性合约在某条特定路径上的误差也可能恰好抵销。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Error need not be nonzero for every claim or path. At zero interest with no stock dividends, the terminal payoff $aS_T+b$ is replicated exactly by holding $a$ shares and cash $b$, without continuous rebalancing. Errors on a nonlinear claim can also happen to offset along a particular path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[动态Delta对冲]] 的零利率算例在第一次再平衡前有 $E_1=0.0472071631$。按新的 Delta 交易只改变股票与现金的组成，不改变当时的 $X_1$，所以不能立即抹去已经发生的误差。以后误差可能增加、减少或改变符号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The zero-rate example in [[动态Delta对冲|dynamic delta hedging]] has $E_1=0.0472071631$ before its first rebalance. Trading to the new delta changes stock/cash composition, not current $X_1$, so it cannot immediately erase the error already incurred. Later errors may grow, shrink, or change sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必须区分三种问题：一是理论复制模型正确但只在有限时点交易；二是跳跃、随机波动或其他未被工具覆盖的风险使连续极限也不完整；三是买卖价差、费用和市场冲击使每次调整都有成本。更频繁交易在合适的无摩擦扩散模型中可改善误差的统计尺度，但不是对每一条已实现路径的单调损益保证，也不是有成本市场里的通用最优策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish finite trading times under a correct replication model, incompleteness from jumps or other unspanned risks, and costs from spreads, fees, or market impact. More frequent trading can improve statistical error measures in suitable frictionless diffusion models. It does not guarantee monotonically better P&L on every realised path or provide a universally optimal strategy with costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[路径模拟离散化误差]] 是电脑近似连续模型时的数值问题；本卡关注真实策略本来就按离散规则交易。若策略规定每日对冲，把模拟网格改成每小时并不能擅自改成每小时交易。增加路径数也不会消除该策略的实际对冲误差，只会更精确地估计其分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[路径模拟离散化误差|Simulation time-discretisation error]] is a numerical approximation issue. This card concerns a strategy that actually trades discretely. Refining a daily hedge simulation to hourly model steps does not authorise hourly trades. More simulated paths estimate the strategy's error distribution more precisely rather than eliminating its trading error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
