# 绝对收敛的实数或复数级数在任意一一重排后仍绝对收敛且和不变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Every bijective rearrangement of an absolutely convergent real or complex series has the same sum and remains absolutely convergent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\sum a_n=S$ 绝对收敛，且 $\sigma:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ 是双射，则 $\sum a_{\sigma(n)}=S$，并且重排后仍绝对收敛。双射意味着每一项恰好用一次：没有漏项，也没有重复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\sum a_n=S$ converges absolutely and $\sigma$ is a bijection of the positive integers, then $\sum a_{\sigma(n)}=S$ absolutely. Bijection means that each term appears exactly once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么远处的项不会把答案改掉？给定误差要求，先选一个有限前缀，使它后面的绝对值总和很小。重排总会在某个时刻收齐这个前缀；此后，重排部分和与原和的差，只可能来自原来那个很小的尾部。取更长前缀就能把差压到任意小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a finite initial block leaving a tiny absolute tail. A bijective rearrangement eventually includes every term of that block. From then on, its difference from the original sum consists only of omitted terms from the tiny tail. Longer initial blocks make this discrepancy arbitrarily small.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $1-1/2+1/4-1/8+\cdots$ 可以按另一顺序逐项相加，和仍为 $2/3$。这为 [[幂级数乘法]] 内部的绝对收敛运算提供支撑；仅有 [[条件收敛]] 时则要检查 [[条件收敛重排|另一条边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sum of $1-1/2+1/4-1/8+\cdots$ stays $2/3$ under any bijective rearrangement. This supports operations on absolutely convergent [[幂级数乘法|power-series products]]. [[条件收敛重排|Conditional convergence has a different rearrangement boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
