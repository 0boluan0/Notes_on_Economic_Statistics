# Rearranging an absolutely convergent series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*绝对收敛级数重排*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite sums do not change when their terms are reordered. An [[无穷级数|infinite series]], however, is a limit of partial sums, so changing the order changes the sequence whose limit is being taken. **Absolute convergence** provides enough control to prove that every ordering using every original term exactly once still has the same sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限和改变加法顺序后，结果不变。但[[无穷级数]]是部分和的极限；改变顺序，也改变了正在取极限的那列部分和。**绝对收敛**提供足够强的控制，保证只要每个原项恰好使用一次，任意排列仍得到同一个和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What counts as a rearrangement?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*什么才是重排？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Index the original finite real or complex terms by $n\in\mathbb N=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$. A rearrangement chooses the original index $\sigma(k)$ for the new position $k$. The map $\sigma:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ must be a **bijection**: no original index is repeated, and each original index occurs at some finite position. Even when two terms have equal numerical values, their positions remain distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把原来有限的实数或复数项按 $n\in\mathbb N=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$ 编号。重排用 $\sigma(k)$ 指出新位置 $k$ 所取的原下标。映射 $\sigma:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ 必须是**双射**：原下标不重复，而且每个原下标都在某个有限位置出现。即使两个项数值相等，它们的原位置仍是不同的。

> [!important] Same sum, still absolute / 同一个和，且仍绝对收敛
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ is [[绝对收敛|absolutely convergent]], with sum $S$. For every bijection $\sigma$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ [[绝对收敛]]，和为 $S$，则对任意双射 $\sigma$，
>
> $$
> \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}a_{\sigma(k)}=S,
> \qquad
> \sum_{k=1}^{\infty}|a_{\sigma(k)}|
> =\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|<\infty.
> $$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original sum has a finite value $S$ by [[绝对收敛推出收敛|absolute convergence implies convergence]]. The present theorem adds invariance under reordering, including permutations that move infinitely many positions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原级数的有限值 $S$ 由[[绝对收敛推出收敛]]保证存在。本定理进一步说明，即使无穷多个位置都发生移动，只要满足重排条件，和也不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why every sufficiently long new partial sum is close
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么每个足够长的新部分和都接近原和*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $S_M=\sum_{n=1}^{M}a_n$ and let $A=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|$. Given an error tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, choose an original cutoff $M$ so large that both
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $S_M=\sum_{n=1}^{M}a_n$，并令 $A=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|$。任给误差要求 $\varepsilon>0$，先把原顺序的截断点 $M$ 取到足够大，使

$$
|S-S_M|<\frac{\varepsilon}{2},
\qquad
\sum_{n=M+1}^{\infty}|a_n|<\frac{\varepsilon}{2}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original convergence supplies the first bound and absolute convergence supplies the second. Because the finite set of original indices $1,\ldots,M$ all eventually appear, there is a finite new cutoff $K$ by which they have all appeared. Consequently **every** new partial sum $T_N=\sum_{k=1}^{N}a_{\sigma(k)}$ with $N\ge K$ consists of $S_M$ plus a finite selection of distinct original terms with indices larger than $M$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原级数收敛保证第一个界，绝对收敛保证第二个界。原下标 $1,\ldots,M$ 只有有限多个，且每个都会出现，所以存在一个新的有限门槛 $K$：加到新位置 $K$ 时，已经把它们全部收齐。因此，**每个** $N\ge K$ 对应的新部分和 $T_N=\sum_{k=1}^{N}a_{\sigma(k)}$，都由 $S_M$ 加上一些原下标大于 $M$、互不重复的项组成。

$$
\begin{aligned}
|T_N-S|
&\le |S_M-S|+
\sum_{\substack{1\le k\le N\\\sigma(k)>M}}|a_{\sigma(k)}|\\
&\le |S_M-S|+\sum_{n=M+1}^{\infty}|a_n|<\varepsilon.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finite selection of distinct nonnegative magnitudes cannot exceed the whole magnitude tail. Thus $T_N\to S$. This controls all cutoffs $N\ge K$, not merely a subsequence ending at conveniently chosen groups. Complex terms cause no change: only their real, nonnegative magnitudes are compared.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从非负幅度尾部取有限多个互不重复的项，其和不可能超过整个幅度尾和。于是 $T_N\to S$。这个论证控制所有 $N\ge K$，而非只控制恰好停在某些分组末端的部分和子列。复数项也没有问题，因为比较大小时使用的始终是它们非负的实数模。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To verify absolute convergence after rearranging, put $B_N=\sum_{k=1}^{N}|a_{\sigma(k)}|$ and $A_M=\sum_{n=1}^{M}|a_n|$. For $N\ge K$, the same finite-prefix inclusion gives $A_M\le B_N\le A$. Hence $0\le A-B_N\le A-A_M$. Making $M$ arbitrarily large proves $B_N\to A$, including equality of the two magnitude sums.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后还须核对重排后仍绝对收敛。令 $B_N=\sum_{k=1}^{N}|a_{\sigma(k)}|$、$A_M=\sum_{n=1}^{M}|a_n|$。对 $N\ge K$，同样的“已收齐前缀”事实给出 $A_M\le B_N\le A$，所以 $0\le A-B_N\le A-A_M$。把 $M$ 取到任意大，就得到 $B_N\to A$，并证明两种排列下的幅度总和相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Swap every adjacent pair
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把每一对相邻项都交换*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $a_n=(-1/2)^{n-1}$, whose [[几何级数|geometric sum]] is $2/3$ and whose magnitude sum is $2$. Exchange positions $1,2$, then $3,4$, and so on: $\sigma(2j-1)=2j$, $\sigma(2j)=2j-1$ for $j\ge1$. The reordered series begins
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $a_n=(-1/2)^{n-1}$，其[[几何级数]]的和为 $2/3$，幅度总和为 $2$。交换位置 $1,2$，再交换 $3,4$，如此继续，即对 $j\ge1$ 令 $\sigma(2j-1)=2j$、$\sigma(2j)=2j-1$。重排后开头为

$$
-\frac12+1-\frac18+\frac14-\frac1{32}+\frac1{16}-\cdots.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first four new partial sums are $-1/2,\ 1/2,\ 3/8,\ 5/8$. The theorem gives the same limit $2/3$ despite these changed intermediate values. Directly, $T_{2m}=S_{2m}$ after $m$ complete pairs; the intervening sum is $T_{2m-1}=S_{2m}-a_{2m-1}$. Since $a_{2m-1}\to0$, these intervening cutoffs approach $2/3$ too. Checking the latter is what turns the paired calculation into convergence of the full sequence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前四个新部分和是 $-1/2,\ 1/2,\ 3/8,\ 5/8$。虽然中途值变了，定理保证极限仍为 $2/3$。也可以直接核验：完成 $m$ 对后，$T_{2m}=S_{2m}$；而中间的部分和为 $T_{2m-1}=S_{2m}-a_{2m-1}$。由于 $a_{2m-1}\to0$，这些中间截断也趋于 $2/3$。补上后一项检查，才从“每对完成时的计算”得到整个部分和数列收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keeping only even-indexed terms would instead give $-1/2-1/8-1/32-\cdots=-2/3$; this omits terms and is not a bijective rearrangement. Likewise, “all positive terms first, then all negative terms” is not an ordering of all terms when both sets are infinite: no negative term would get a finite position. For a genuinely [[条件收敛|conditionally convergent]] real series, [[条件收敛重排|bijective rearrangements can change the sum]]. Absolute convergence is what supports order-independent operations used in [[幂级数乘法|power-series multiplication]], subject to that theorem's own conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果只保留偶数下标项，则得到 $-1/2-1/8-1/32-\cdots=-2/3$；这漏掉了项，并不是双射重排。同样，当正负项各有无穷多项时，“先放完全部正项，再放全部负项”也不是全部项的有效排序，因为负项永远得不到有限位置。对真正[[条件收敛]]的实数级数，[[条件收敛重排|双射重排可以改变和]]。绝对收敛为[[幂级数乘法]]中的顺序无关运算提供支撑，但仍须满足该运算本身的条件。
