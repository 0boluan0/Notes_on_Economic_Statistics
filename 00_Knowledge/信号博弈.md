# 信号博弈中，拥有私人类型的发送者先选择可观察且影响收益的行动，接收者观察行动而非类型后再回应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a signaling game, a privately informed sender first chooses an observable, payoff-relevant action, and the receiver observes that action rather than the sender's type before responding.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心结构
> [[博弈类型|私人类型]]先影响发送者怎样选择信号；信号随后被接收者观察，并同时影响收益与接收者对类型的推断。这里的“信号”是博弈中的行动，不是天然可信的标签。它能否传递信息，取决于不同类型的成本、收益与偏离激励。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Core structure*
>
> A private type first shapes the sender's choice of signal. The receiver then observes the signal, which affects both payoffs and inferences about type. The signal is an action in the game, not an inherently credible label; whether it conveys information depends on type-specific costs, benefits, and deviation incentives.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 标准两阶段结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Standard two-stage structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令发送者类型为 $\theta\in\Theta$，标准模型给定一份对类型有 full support 的先验 $\mu\in\Delta(\Theta)$、信号集合 $S$、接收者行动集合 $A$，以及收益函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the sender's type be $\theta\in\Theta$. The standard model specifies a full-support prior $\mu\in\Delta(\Theta)$ over types, a signal set $S$, a receiver action set $A$, and payoff functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
u_1:\Theta\times S\times A\to\mathbb R,
\qquad
u_2:\Theta\times S\times A\to\mathbb R.
$$

行动顺序是：

1. [[Harsanyi转换|Nature 按 $\mu$ 抽取 $\theta$]]，发送者观察自己的类型；
2. 发送者选择 $s\in S$；
3. 接收者观察 $s$、但不观察 $\theta$，再选择 $a\in A$；
4. 收益由 $(\theta,s,a)$ 决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The order of play is:

&nbsp;

**1.** [[Harsanyi转换|Nature draws $\theta$ according to $\mu$]], and the sender observes their own type;<br>
**2.** the sender chooses $s\in S$;<br>
**3.** the receiver observes $s$ but not $\theta$, and then chooses $a\in A$;<br>
**4.** payoffs are determined by $(\theta,s,a)$.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

MIT 的入门定义先用有限 $\Theta,S,A$ 说明结构；教育信号等应用可使用连续信号。有限性是该讲义这一版模型的方便假设，不是“信号博弈”一词在所有文献中的必要定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
MIT's introductory definition uses finite sets $\Theta,S,A$ to present the structure, while applications such as education signaling may use continuous signals. Finiteness is a convenient assumption in that version of the model, not a necessary part of the term “signaling game” throughout the literature.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 完整策略必须覆盖所有类型与所有观察
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Complete strategies must cover every type and every observation.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

发送者与接收者面对的是两种不同的条件计划：前者按自身类型选择信号，后者按观察到的信号选择行动，而且两份计划都必须覆盖均衡路径外的情形。完整的数学对象与最小检查清单见[[信号博弈策略]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sender and receiver face different contingent plans: the sender chooses a signal as a function of type, while the receiver chooses an action as a function of the observed signal. Both plans must also cover contingencies off the equilibrium path. See [[信号博弈策略|strategies in a signaling game]] for the complete mathematical object and a minimal checklist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 信号不是 cheap talk，也不因昂贵而自动可信
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A signal is not cheap talk, and costliness alone does not make it credible.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信号是否直接进入收益，区分了标准信号博弈与[[信号与廉价谈话|廉价谈话]]；信号是否可信，则取决于不同类型的偏离激励，而不是成本的绝对大小，见[[昂贵信号不必然可信]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether the observable choice enters payoffs directly distinguishes a standard signaling game from [[信号与廉价谈话|cheap talk]]. Whether a signal is credible depends on the deviation incentives of different types rather than on its absolute cost; see [[昂贵信号不必然可信|a costly signal need not be credible]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] Beer–Quiche 只是结构锚点
> Nature 先决定发送者是 weak 还是 strong；发送者知道自己的类型后选择 Beer 或 Quiche；接收者只观察食物，再决定 Fight 或 Don't Fight。食物既影响发送者偏好，又传递类型信息，因此它是信号。哪种类型选择哪种食物仍须由收益和 PBE 条件推出。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Beer–Quiche as a structural anchor*
>
> Nature first determines whether the sender is weak or strong. After learning their type, the sender chooses Beer or Quiche; the receiver observes only the food choice and then chooses Fight or Don't Fight. The food affects the sender's preferences and can convey information about type, so it is a signal. Which type chooses which food must still be derived from payoffs and the PBE conditions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一名求职者知道自己的生产率，先选择教育程度；企业只观察教育程度后决定工资。为什么这构成信号博弈，而不是同时行动的贝叶斯博弈？
>
> **答案：** 求职者先知道私人类型并选择一个可观察、影响收益的行动，企业随后观察该行动而不观察类型再回应；因此行动同时影响收益和企业对类型的推断。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Self-check*
>
> A job applicant knows their productivity and first chooses an education level; a firm observes only education before setting a wage. Why is this a signaling game rather than a simultaneous Bayesian game?
>
> **Answer:** The applicant observes a private type and first chooses an observable, payoff-relevant action. The firm then observes that action but not the type before responding, so the action affects both payoffs and the firm's inference about type.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
