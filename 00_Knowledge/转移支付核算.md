# 转移支付应在同一福利账户内抵消而真实资源成本只计一次
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Transfers cancel within a common welfare account; real resource costs are counted once.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

转移支付核算先确定纳入哪些人和政府，再把同一笔支付的付款与收款两端对齐。在同一货币单位、同一时期且每元权重相同的加总中，边界内的纯转移相互抵消；支付金额本身不能再当作额外资源损失。政策引起的行为扭曲、资源占用和管理成本则须另查，且只计一次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First define the included people and government, then match each payment to its receipt. Internal pure transfers cancel when units, timing and monetary weights agree. Behavioural distortions, resource use and administration require separate checks without duplication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个可操作的顺序是：固定比较基准；列消费者、生产者、政府三方的变化；核对税款或补贴是否已进入私人剩余；最后加入尚未包含的真实成本。对无其他遗漏项目的从量税模型，政府净收入为 $R$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the counterfactual; record changes for consumers, producers and government; check whether private surplus already reflects taxes or subsidies; then include any omitted real costs. For a unit tax with no other missing items, let net government receipts be $R$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta W_{\mathrm{money}}
=\Delta CS+\Delta PS+\Delta R.
$$

用 $P_D=12-Q$、$P_S=2+Q$ 的自造市场：无税时 $Q=5,p=7$；征每单位 $2$ 的税后 $Q=4$、买方价 $8$、卖方价 $6$。同一账户为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the constructed market $P_D=12-Q$, $P_S=2+Q$, the untaxed outcome is $Q=5,p=7$. A unit tax of $2$ gives $Q=4$, buyer price $8$ and seller price $6$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 项目 | 无税 | 有税 | 变化 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 消费者剩余 | 12.5 | 8 | −4.5 |
| 生产者剩余 | 12.5 | 8 | −4.5 |
| 政府税收 | 0 | 8 | +8 |
| 合计 | 25 | 24 | −1 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Item | No tax | Tax | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Consumer surplus | 12.5 | 8 | −4.5 |
| Producer surplus | 12.5 | 8 | −4.5 |
| Government revenue | 0 | 8 | +8 |
| Total | 25 | 24 | −1 |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

私人剩余减少 $9$ 已包含向政府转移的 $8$，不能再扣 $8$；净损失是未发生交易的 $1$，即此模型中的 [[税收超额负担]]。若另有尚未入账、价值为 $1$ 的管理资源耗用，合计才进一步降至 $23$。直接积分 $\int_0^4(10-2q)\,dq=24$ 已经抵消税款，不能在此基础上再加 $8$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The private loss of $9$ already includes the $8$ tax transfer. The net loss is $1$ from forgone trade, the model's [[税收超额负担|excess burden]]. An additional, previously uncounted administration cost of $1$ would reduce the total to $23$. The direct integral already cancels taxes, so adding revenue to it would double count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

纯现金转移 $100$ 的双方合计是 $-100+100=0$；这不意味着分配没有价值。若收款人不在核算边界内，或采用不同人的福利权重，就不能套用这个零和结论；也不能据此假定筹款和实施不耗费资源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pure internal cash transfer of $100$ nets to zero. This does not make distribution irrelevant. The cancellation does not apply unchanged when recipients are excluded or welfare weights differ, nor does it establish costless financing or implementation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
