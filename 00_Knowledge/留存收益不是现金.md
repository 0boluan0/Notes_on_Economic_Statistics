# 留存收益记录累计未向所有者分配的利润而不表示企业另存着等额现金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Retained earnings record accumulated undistributed profit, not a separate cash reserve of the same size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

留存收益属于权益：它记录企业累计盈利中未向所有者分配的部分，并可能受以前年度调整等事项影响。在中国会计语境中，留存收益通常包括**盈余公积和未分配利润**；从未分配利润提取盈余公积，是权益内部的转列，不等于向股东付现，也不会因此减少留存收益总额。现金属于资产。两者位于资产负债表不同位置，不能因为企业有 100 的留存收益，就推断银行账户还有 100 可用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Retained earnings are equity: accumulated profit not distributed to owners, potentially adjusted for prior-period matters. In Chinese accounting terminology, they generally comprise surplus reserves and undistributed profit. Appropriating profit to a surplus reserve reclassifies equity internally; it neither pays cash to shareholders nor reduces total retained earnings. Cash is an asset. These are different balance-sheet categories; retained earnings of 100 do not imply 100 remains available in a bank account.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看一个无税、无股利的最小例子：企业把成本 60 的存货以 100 赊销，收入与成本均符合确认条件。净利润及留存收益增加 40，存货减少 60、应收账款增加 100，**现金没有增加**。之后收款 100 是资产从应收转成现金，不再确认第二次利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a minimal example with no tax or dividends, inventory costing 60 is sold on credit for 100, with recognition conditions satisfied. Profit and retained earnings rise by 40; inventory falls by 60 and receivables rise by 100. Cash does not increase. Later collection converts receivables into cash without recognising profit again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以把留存利润视为[[外部融资需求|内部融资来源]]，是在说企业没有把这一部分权益分配出去；不是再向现金流量表加入一笔“留存收益收款”。利润已经通过相关资产负债变化反映在资金中，重复加入会把同一来源算两次。具体可用现金仍需结合回款、投资、受限资金和[[现金预算]]判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Calling retained income an [[外部融资需求|internal financing source]] means that equity has not been distributed. It does not justify adding a “retained-earnings receipt” to cash flow. Profit is already reflected through the associated asset and liability changes; adding it again double counts. Collections, investment, restrictions, and the [[现金预算|cash budget]] determine available cash.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [财政部《会计基础》考试大纲（2014），印刷第 16 页（PDF 28）](https://m.mof.gov.cn/czxw/201404/P020140421323840971505.pdf)：仅用于核对留存收益包含盈余公积、未分配利润这一术语关系，不作为现行确认与计量准则的来源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Ministry of Finance's 2014 accounting outline supports only the terminology connecting retained earnings, surplus reserves, and undistributed profit; it is not used as a source for current recognition or measurement requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [[02_Economy/05_财务管理/2023年注册会计师全国统一考试辅导教材---财务成本管理 (中国注册会计师协会) (Z-Library).pdf#page=72|CPA《财务成本管理》第二章，印刷第 64 页（PDF 72）]]：支持留存利润增加权益、参与融资缺口的会计含义；赊销例以会计分录逻辑独立构造并检查，现金为零的结论不依赖估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The CPA text supports retained profit's role in equity and financing. The credit-sale example is independently constructed and checked using accounting entries; its zero cash effect does not rely on an estimate.
