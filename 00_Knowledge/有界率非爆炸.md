# Bounded rates and non-explosion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有界率非爆炸*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A continuous-time chain can make many jumps very quickly. **Non-explosion** asks for something more basic than long-run stability: on any finite time interval, can only finitely many genuine state changes occur? A common finite ceiling on all exit rates guarantees that they cannot accumulate infinitely fast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续时间链可能在很短时间内跳很多次。**非爆炸**问的是一个比长期稳定更基础的问题：任意有限时间区间内，真实状态变化是否只能发生有限次？如果所有出口率都有同一个有限上界，就能保证跳跃不会无限快地积聚。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We consider a finite or countable state set $S$ and a [[生成矩阵约束|stable conservative Q-matrix]]: $q_{ij}\ge0$ for $j\ne i$, and the [[出口率|total exit rate]] $q_i=\sum_{j\ne i}q_{ij}=-q_{ii}$ is finite at each state. “Conservative” here means zero row sums. The [[最小CTMC|minimal pure-jump process]] follows these rates up to its possible [[CTMC爆炸|explosion time]] $\zeta$, the accumulation time of infinitely many jumps; it is killed if explosion occurs. This theorem rules that event out.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑有限或可数状态集合 $S$，以及[[生成矩阵约束|稳定且代数保守的 Q 矩阵]]：当 $j\ne i$ 时 $q_{ij}\ge0$，每个状态的[[出口率|总出口率]] $q_i=\sum_{j\ne i}q_{ij}=-q_{ii}$ 都有限。这里“保守”指每行和为零。[[最小CTMC|最小纯跳过程]]按这些速率演化到可能的[[CTMC爆炸|爆炸时刻]] $\zeta$，也就是无限次跳跃的积聚时刻；若爆炸就终止。本定理排除这种事件。

> [!important] One bound for every state / 所有状态共用一个上界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If there is a finite constant $M\ge0$ such that $q_i\le M$ for every $i\in S$, the minimal process is non-explosive from every initial state: $P_i(\zeta=\infty)=1$. Here $P_i$ means probability when starting at $i$. A finite state space automatically supplies such a bound when each exit rate is finite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若存在有限常数 $M\ge0$，使每个 $i\in S$ 都满足 $q_i\le M$，则最小过程从任一初态出发都非爆炸：$P_i(\zeta=\infty)=1$。$P_i$ 表示从 $i$ 出发时的概率。状态集合有限且各出口率有限时，自动存在这样的共同上界。

## Compare every wait with a rate-M clock
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每次等待下面都有一个统一的较短等待*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First separate $M=0$: then every $q_i=0$, so every state is absorbing and there are no genuine jumps. Suppose $M>0$. Use [[独立同分布|iid]] unit-rate [[指数分布|exponential variables]] $E_0,E_1,\ldots$, independent of the [[嵌入跳链|jump chain]] $Y_0,Y_1,\ldots$. If the current exit rate is positive, the usual [[生成矩阵分解|holding-time construction]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先单独处理 $M=0$：这时全部 $q_i=0$，每个状态都吸收，根本没有真实跳跃。以下设 $M>0$。取[[独立同分布]]的单位速率[[指数分布|指数变量]] $E_0,E_1,\ldots$，并使它们独立于[[嵌入跳链]] $Y_0,Y_1,\ldots$。若当前出口率为正，标准[[生成矩阵分解|停留时间构造]]给出

$$H_n=\frac{E_n}{q_{Y_n}}\ge\frac{E_n}{M}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$H_n$ is the time spent at the state visited after $n$ jumps. Dividing a unit-rate exponential by $q_{Y_n}$ gives exactly the required rate. If a zero-rate state is reached, its holding time is infinite and the path stops jumping, so it is already non-explosive. Otherwise, on a path with infinitely many jumps, their total elapsed time satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$H_n$ 是完成 $n$ 次跳跃后，在当前状态停留的时间。单位速率指数变量除以 $q_{Y_n}$，恰好得到所需的速率。若进入零速率状态，停留时间为无穷，路径不再跳跃，已经非爆炸。否则，在无限跳跃的路径上，总经过时间满足

$$\zeta=\sum_{n=0}^\infty H_n\ge\frac1M\sum_{n=0}^\infty E_n=\infty\qquad\text{almost surely}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why does the last sum diverge? Each $E_n$ has mean $1$, so the [[强大数定律|strong law of large numbers]] says that the average of the first $m$ terms tends to $1$ almost surely. Their sum therefore grows without bound. The argument uses one common $M$; choosing a different upper bound at each state would not give this comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后那个和为什么发散？每个 $E_n$ 的均值为 $1$，[[强大数定律]]告诉我们，前 $m$ 项的平均几乎必然趋于 $1$，因此总和无界增长。论证使用的是同一个 $M$；每到一个状态就另选不同上界，无法得到这个比较。

## Counting candidate events
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用候选事件数理解上界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is an equivalent [[CTMC均匀化|uniformization]] picture when $M>0$: use a [[齐次泊松过程|Poisson clock]] of rate $M$, and at each candidate event either change state or keep it unchanged. The number $J(t)$ of genuine jumps by time $t$ is at most the candidate count $N(t)$, whose distribution is Poisson$(Mt)$. Since that count is finite almost surely, genuine jumps cannot explode.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $M>0$ 时，还可以从等价的[[CTMC均匀化|均匀化]]角度理解：使用速率为 $M$ 的[[齐次泊松过程|Poisson 时钟]]，每次候选事件发生时，或者改变状态，或者保持原状态。截至 $t$ 的真实跳数 $J(t)$ 不超过候选计数 $N(t)$，后者服从 Poisson$(Mt)$。候选计数几乎必然有限，因此真实跳跃不能爆炸。

> [!example] A probability bound is not a fixed jump budget / 随机计数不是固定额度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If every exit rate is at most $5$ per hour, two hours can be covered by a Poisson candidate count of mean $10$. Thus $E[J(2)]\le10$. This does not say that there are always at most ten jumps: a Poisson count can exceed its mean. Non-explosion means finitely many jumps almost surely, not a deterministic finite cap shared by every path.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果各出口率都不超过每小时 $5$，两小时内可以用均值为 $10$ 的 Poisson 候选计数覆盖，因此 $E[J(2)]\le10$。这不表示每条路径都最多跳十次：Poisson 计数可能超过自己的均值。非爆炸表示跳数几乎必然有限，不表示所有路径共享同一个确定的有限次数上限。

## A sufficient condition, not a necessary one
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*充分条件，不是必要条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unbounded rates do not by themselves imply explosion. For a direct example, let state $i_n$ jump only to its absorbing partner $a_n$, at rate $n$, for $n=1,2,\ldots$. The global exit-rate supremum is infinite, but every path makes at most one jump. The rates encountered along a path matter, not only the largest rates somewhere in the state space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

出口率无界本身不能推出爆炸。一个直接例子是：对每个 $n=1,2,\ldots$，状态 $i_n$ 只能以速率 $n$ 跳到对应吸收态 $a_n$。全局出口率上确界为无穷，但每条路径最多跳一次。关键还在于路径实际会遇到哪些速率，而不只是状态空间某处存在多大的速率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[纯生链|pure-birth]] example gives a less trivial case: from $n\ge1$, jump to $n+1$ at rate $\lambda_n=n$. Its rates grow without bound, yet the [[纯生链爆炸判据|pure-birth criterion]] gives non-explosion because $\sum_{n\ge1}1/n=\infty$. In contrast, rates $\lambda_n=2^n$ have $\sum_{n\ge1}2^{-n}=1$ and produce explosion almost surely. Every individual rate is finite in both models. Thus “finite at each state”, “uniformly bounded over all states”, and “non-explosive” are three distinct assertions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个[[纯生链]]例子给出更有内容的情形：从 $n\ge1$ 以速率 $\lambda_n=n$ 跳到 $n+1$。速率不断增大，但[[纯生链爆炸判据]]仍因 $\sum_{n\ge1}1/n=\infty$ 而给出非爆炸。相反，速率 $\lambda_n=2^n$ 满足 $\sum_{n\ge1}2^{-n}=1$，会几乎必然爆炸。这两个模型中，每个单独的速率都有限。因此，“每个状态分别有限”“所有状态一致有界”“过程非爆炸”是三个不同断言。
