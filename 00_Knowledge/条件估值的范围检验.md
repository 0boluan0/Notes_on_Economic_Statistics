# 条件估值应检查回答是否对被估变化的范围和问卷语境作合理响应

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Contingent valuation should test whether responses sensibly reflect the scope of the valued change and the survey context.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

范围检验考察[[条件估值]]回答是否随所估改善的数量、范围或持续时间作出可解释的响应。若两组调查在其他条件可比时分别描述较小和较大的改善，要检查受访者是否确实理解了差别，以及回答是否在估计具体改善，而非只是表达支持某项善举。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A scope test examines whether [[条件估值|contingent-valuation]] responses react intelligibly to the quantity, extent or duration of an improvement. Comparing otherwise comparable surveys for smaller and larger changes requires checking that respondents understood the distinction and valued the specific improvement rather than merely expressing support for a worthy cause.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更多好处通常应在尚未饱和等条件下带来更高支付意愿，但不要求严格同比增长。自造函数 $W(s)=10\sqrt{s}$ 表示对范围 $s\ge0$ 的总支付意愿：范围从1变为4时，价值从10变为20而不是40；边际支付意愿递减与合理范围响应可以并存。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More of a desirable outcome generally raises willingness to pay under conditions such as nonsatiation, but proportional growth is not required. For the constructed function $W(s)=10\sqrt{s}$, increasing scope from 1 to 4 raises value from 10 to 20, not 40. Diminishing marginal willingness to pay is compatible with a sensible scope response.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还应区分范围变化与“单独询问一项改善”或“把它嵌入更大组合”的语境变化；替代品、预算与问题顺序可能影响解释。范围响应不足会削弱该调查的可靠性，需要检查设计与理解，不能直接推断真实环境价值为零，也不能靠暗示受访者必须报更大数字来通过检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish changes in scope from asking about an improvement separately or within a larger package. Substitutes, budgets and question order affect interpretation. Inadequate scope response weakens survey reliability and calls for design and comprehension checks; it neither proves zero environmental value nor justifies prompting respondents to report larger numbers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [NOAA Panel，Report on Contingent Valuation，印刷第6–8、15、21页（PDF第7–9、16、22页）](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/60900/noaa_60900_DS1.pdf)：核对范围、边际价值递减、情境与可靠性边界。平方根函数是反驳“必须同比”的独立数例，不是经验估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The NOAA report supports the scope, diminishing-marginal-value, context and reliability boundaries. The square-root function independently illustrates why proportionality is unnecessary; it is not an empirical estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
