# 任意固定底数大于一的对数都比任意正幂增长得慢
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A logarithm with any fixed base greater than one grows more slowly than every positive power*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $b>1$ 与 $p>0$，当 $x\to\infty$ 时，$\log_b x=o(x^p)$，即
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For fixed $b>1$ and $p>0$, as $x\to\infty$, $\log_b x=o(x^p)$; that is,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\log_b x}{x^p}\longrightarrow0.
$$

关键不是对数增长得“小一点”，而是它相对于每个固定正幂的比重都趋零。$p$ 可以非常小，但必须先固定；这不是允许 $p=p(x)\to0$ 的统一结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The logarithm is not merely a little smaller: its proportion relative to every fixed positive power tends to zero. The exponent may be arbitrarily small, but must be fixed first. This is not a uniform claim allowing $p=p(x)\to0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明取 $\delta=p/2>0$。由 [[自然对数积分表示]]，对 $x\ge1$，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a proof, choose $\delta=p/2>0$. By the [[自然对数积分表示|integral representation of the natural logarithm]], for $x\ge1$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
0\le\ln x=\int_1^x\frac{dt}{t}
\le\int_1^x t^{\delta-1}\,dt
=\frac{x^\delta-1}{\delta}.
$$

因此 $0\le\ln x/x^p\le x^{-p/2}/\delta\to0$。换底公式 $\log_bx=\ln x/\ln b$ 只引入固定正常数，故结论不变。例如 $\log_2n=o(\sqrt n)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $0\le\ln x/x^p\le x^{-p/2}/\delta\to0$. Changing the base by $\log_bx=\ln x/\ln b$ introduces only a fixed positive constant. For example, $\log_2n=o(\sqrt n)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一比较还给出：对固定 $k>0$，$(\ln x)^k=o(x^p)$。把已证结论用于指数 $p/k$，再将比值取 $k$ 次幂即可。不要把“对数慢”理解成在每一个有限输入上都更小；渐近结论允许很远的交叉点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same comparison gives $(\ln x)^k=o(x^p)$ for fixed $k>0$: apply the result with exponent $p/k$ and raise the ratio to the power $k$. Slower asymptotic growth does not mean a smaller value at every finite input; the crossover may occur far out.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
