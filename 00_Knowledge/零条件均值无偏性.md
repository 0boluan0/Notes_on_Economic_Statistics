# 零条件均值使 OLS 条件无偏
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Zero conditional mean makes OLS conditionally unbiased*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子命题
> 在线性参数模型且设计矩阵满列秩时，若对完整样本有
> $$
> E(u\mid X)=0,
> $$
> 则
> $$
> E(\hat\beta\mid X)=\beta.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a model linear in parameters with a full-column-rank design matrix, full-sample zero conditional mean, $E(u\mid X)=0$, implies $E(\hat\beta\mid X)=\beta$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why it holds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 $y=X\beta+u$ 和 OLS 公式，
$$
\hat\beta=\beta+(X'X)^{-1}X'u.
$$
给定 $X$ 取条件期望后，附加误差项的条件期望为零，所以估计量的条件中心落在 $\beta$ 上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From $y=X\beta+u$ and the OLS formula, $\hat\beta=\beta+(X'X)^{-1}X'u$. Conditional on $X$, the added error term has expectation zero, so the conditional centre of the estimator is $\beta$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 含义边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Meaning boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 $X$ 是整个样本设计矩阵，$u$ 是对应的误差向量。独立随机抽样下，逐个观测的 $E(u_i\mid x_i)=0$ 可推出 $E(u\mid X)=0$；时间序列或其他依赖数据不能省略把逐期条件扩展到完整设计矩阵所需的额外论证。无偏性是重复抽样分布的性质，不保证某一个样本估计恰好等于真值。同方差和正态性都不是这条无偏结论所必需；若零条件均值因 $E(x_i u_i)\neq0$ 的内生性而失败，增加样本通常不会消除这种系统偏差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $X$ is the full sample design matrix and $u$ the corresponding error vector. Under independent random sampling, the observation-level condition $E(u_i\mid x_i)=0$ implies $E(u\mid X)=0$; time-series and other dependent data require an additional argument before an observation-level condition can be extended to the full design. Unbiasedness is a repeated-sampling property and does not guarantee that one sample estimate equals the truth. Homoskedasticity and normality are not required. If zero conditional mean fails through endogeneity with $E(x_i u_i)\neq0$, increasing the sample ordinarily does not remove that systematic bias.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 同方差失败时，OLS 是否因此自动有偏？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Does heteroskedasticity automatically make OLS biased?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不会。零条件均值等条件仍成立时，OLS 可保持无偏；同方差主要关系常规方差公式和 Gauss–Markov 有效率结论。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. OLS can remain unbiased when zero conditional mean and the other conditions hold. Homoskedasticity primarily affects the conventional variance formula and Gauss–Markov efficiency.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
