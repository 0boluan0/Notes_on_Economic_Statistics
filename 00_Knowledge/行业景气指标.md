# 行业景气指标是用于观察行业经营活跃程度及其变化的统计量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Industry activity indicators are statistics used to observe operating conditions and their changes within an industry.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行业景气指标用订单、产量、销售、库存、产能利用率或调查回答等资料，观察行业经营活动的强弱与变化。可以是单项指标，也可以是按明确方法编制的综合指数；“景气指标”不是一条具有统一中性值的通用序列。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industry activity indicators use orders, output, sales, inventories, capacity utilization, or survey responses to track operating conditions. They may be individual measures or explicitly constructed composite indices. There is no universal activity series with one common neutral value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，生产调查扩散指数回答“报告增加的企业是否较多”，库存量回答“库存有多少”，两者单位与经济含义不同。领先、同步或滞后必须相对于某个目标序列和时期界定；一个指标也可能在突发冲击中失去原有领先性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A production diffusion index asks whether more firms report increases, whereas an inventory level measures how much stock is held. Their units and meanings differ. Leading, coincident, or lagging status must be defined relative to a target series and period; a sudden shock can disrupt historical lead times.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此先读[[景气指标口径核对|指标说明]]，再把信号接到收入、成本或现金流，不能由一个“高于 50 或 100”的读数直接推出股票收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read the [[景气指标口径核对|indicator's measurement conventions]] before translating its signal into revenue, cost, or cash flow. A reading above 50 or 100 alone does not establish stock returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
