# Characteristic polynomial under similarity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*相似变换下的特征多项式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A change of coordinates can change every entry of a matrix without changing its characteristic polynomial. This polynomial packages the eigenvalues and how often they occur as roots. The point of the theorem is that this information belongs to the underlying linear action, rather than the particular basis used to write it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换坐标可以改变矩阵的所有元素，却不改变它的特征多项式。这个多项式把特征值及各自在多项式中出现的重复次数装在一起。本定理说明，这些信息属于底层线性作用，而不是临时选择的坐标基。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recall two definitions. [[相似矩阵|Similarity]] means $B=M^{-1}AM$ for an invertible coordinate conversion $M$. The [[特征多项式|characteristic polynomial]] is $p_A(t)=\det(tI_n-A)$: $t$ is a polynomial variable, $I_n$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]], and $\det$ is the [[行列式|determinant]]. Here $A,B,M$ are $n\times n$ matrices over the same field $\mathbb F$, with $n\ge1$, and $M^{-1}$ exists over that field.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先回顾两个定义。[[相似矩阵|相似]]表示 $B=M^{-1}AM$，其中 $M$ 是可逆的坐标转换矩阵。[[特征多项式]]为 $p_A(t)=\det(tI_n-A)$：$t$ 是多项式变量，$I_n$ 是[[单位矩阵]]，$\det$ 表示[[行列式]]。这里 $A,B,M$ 都是同一域 $\mathbb F$ 上的 $n\times n$ 矩阵，$n\ge1$，并且 $M^{-1}$ 存在于这个域上。

> [!example] Different entries, the same polynomial · 元素不同，多项式相同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With the matrices below, multiplication verifies $B=M^{-1}AM$. The first matrix is diagonal; the second has a nonzero off-diagonal entry. Nevertheless both determinants give $(t-2)(t-3)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 下面的矩阵相乘可验证 $B=M^{-1}AM$。第一个矩阵是对角矩阵，第二个有非零的非对角元，但两个行列式都给出 $(t-2)(t-3)$。
>
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix},\quad M=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad B=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\0&3\end{pmatrix}.$$
> $$p_B(t)=\det\begin{pmatrix}t-2&1\\0&t-3\end{pmatrix}=(t-2)(t-3)=p_A(t).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the coordinate factors cancel
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么坐标转换因子会消去*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The scalar identity matrix is unchanged by the conversion: $M^{-1}(tI_n)M=tI_n$. Thus the entire matrix inside the determinant can be factored, without assuming that $A$ commutes with $M$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标量乘单位矩阵在这个转换下不变：$M^{-1}(tI_n)M=tI_n$。因此可以对行列式里的整个矩阵作如下分解，不需要假设 $A$ 与 $M$ 可交换：

$$tI_n-B=M^{-1}(tI_n-A)M.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now use [[行列式乘法性|multiplicativity of the determinant]], which turns the determinant of a product of square matrices into a product of scalar determinants. Since $M^{-1}M=I_n$, the first and last factors multiply to one:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再使用[[行列式乘法性]]：方阵乘积的行列式等于各个标量行列式的乘积。因为 $M^{-1}M=I_n$，第一个与最后一个因子相乘等于一：

$$\begin{aligned}
p_B(t)&=\det\bigl(M^{-1}(tI_n-A)M\bigr)\\
&=\det(M^{-1})\,\det(tI_n-A)\,\det(M)\\
&=p_A(t).
\end{aligned}$$

> [!important] The complete polynomial is invariant · 整个多项式保持不变
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Similar matrices over the same field have identical characteristic polynomials, coefficient by coefficient. The proof does not require diagonalization or even invertibility of $A$; only the change-of-basis matrix $M$ must be invertible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 同一域上的相似矩阵，其特征多项式的每个系数都相同。证明不要求可对角化，也不要求 $A$ 可逆；必须可逆的只是换基矩阵 $M$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the roots and the coefficients carefully
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样读取根与系数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A root $\lambda$ is an [[特征对|eigenvalue]]: there is a nonzero vector $v$ with $Av=\lambda v$. If $(t-\lambda)^d$ divides $p_A(t)$ but $(t-\lambda)^{d+1}$ does not, the integer $d$ is its [[代数重数与几何重数|algebraic multiplicity]]. Identical polynomials preserve both the roots and these counts. A real matrix may have no real roots; the polynomial identity still holds. When considering complex roots, view both matrices over $\mathbb C$ and compare them in that same field.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根 $\lambda$ 是[[特征对|特征值]]，即存在非零向量 $v$ 使 $Av=\lambda v$。若 $(t-\lambda)^d$ 整除 $p_A(t)$，而 $(t-\lambda)^{d+1}$ 不整除，整数 $d$ 就是其[[代数重数与几何重数|代数重数]]。多项式完全相同，就保留所有根及这些重复次数。实矩阵可能没有实根，但多项式恒等式仍成立；讨论复根时，应把两个矩阵都放到 $\mathbb C$ 上，在同一域中比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficient of $t^{n-1}$ is $-\operatorname{tr}(A)$, where the [[迹与特征值总和|trace]] is the sum of diagonal entries. The constant coefficient is $(-1)^n\det(A)$. Hence similarity also preserves trace and determinant. In the example, $p_A(t)=t^2-5t+6$, giving trace $5$ and determinant $6$. With the alternative convention $\det(A-tI_n)$, the whole polynomial is multiplied by $(-1)^n$; its roots and their multiplicities are unchanged, but coefficient signs must be read consistently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$t^{n-1}$ 的系数是 $-\operatorname{tr}(A)$，其中[[迹与特征值总和|迹]]是主对角元之和；常数项是 $(-1)^n\det(A)$。因此相似也保留迹与行列式。例子中 $p_A(t)=t^2-5t+6$，给出迹5、行列式6。若另一教材采用 $\det(A-tI_n)$，整个多项式会乘上 $(-1)^n$；根和重数不变，但读取系数符号时必须始终使用同一种约定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a three-dimensional check over $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, if one matrix in a similarity class has polynomial $(t-1)^2(t+4)$, every matrix in that class has the same polynomial. The root $1$ occurs twice and $-4$ once; the trace is $-2$ and determinant is $-4$. Expanding gives $t^3+2t^2-7t+4$, whose constant term is the negative of the determinant because $n=3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再在实数或复数域上做一个三维检查：若某个相似类中的矩阵具有多项式 $(t-1)^2(t+4)$，同类中的每个矩阵都具有这个多项式。根1出现两次，根 $-4$ 出现一次；迹为 $-2$，行列式为 $-4$。展开为 $t^3+2t^2-7t+4$，由于 $n=3$，常数项恰为行列式的相反数。

> [!warning] Equal polynomials do not establish similarity · 多项式相同还不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The matrices $2I_2$ and $\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ both have polynomial $(t-2)^2$, but they are not similar. The [[同谱不必相似|counterexample]] depends on different numbers of independent eigenvector directions. Polynomial coefficients do not supply those dimensions; their preservation under similarity requires the separate [[相似保持特征空间维数|eigenspace argument]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $2I_2$ 与 $\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ 的特征多项式都是 $(t-2)^2$，却不相似。这个[[同谱不必相似|反例]]依靠的是线性无关特征方向数量不同。多项式系数不提供这些维数；相似变换也保留这些维数，需要另用[[相似保持特征空间维数|特征空间的论证]]。
