# 创造性破坏是创新创造新生产机会并替代旧产品、技术或经营活动的过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Creative destruction is the process through which innovation creates new productive opportunities while displacing existing products, technologies or activities.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

创造性破坏把创新的创造与替代放在同一个过程里理解。在 Aghion–Howitt（1992）的基本模型中，具体形式是**质量更高的中间品使最终品生产更有效率，并取代上一代中间品**。新创新者获得市场，旧创新者失去原来的垄断利润；技术进步因此同时产生收益和损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Creative destruction joins creation and displacement in one process. In the basic Aghion–Howitt model, a better intermediate input improves final production and replaces the previous vintage. The new innovator gains the market while the previous innovator loses monopoly profits, so technological progress generates both gains and losses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，同一生产任务原来使用第一代设备；第二代设备使相同投入能生产更多最终品。在模型所设的充分大质量改进下，新设备供应者能够取代旧供应者。**旧知识仍可存在、旧专利仍可合法有效，但旧供应者已无法靠它取得原有利润。** 被破坏的是其经济地位与租金，未必是物理设备被砸毁；利润持续多久见 [[创新租金的替代风险]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider successive generations of equipment for the same production task. A sufficiently large quality improvement lets the new supplier displace the old one. The old knowledge and legal patent can survive while their former profit opportunity disappears. This need not involve physically destroying equipment; see [[创新租金的替代风险|replacement risk and innovation rents]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与增加可同时使用的新品种有所不同：品种扩展增加选择集合，本例则在同一投入功能上更换质量代际。质量与品种都可由有目的的研发产生，但只有明确写入替代关系，才能据此推出旧利润消失以及 [[商业抢夺效应]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expanding variety adds inputs that can coexist; this example replaces quality generations serving the same function. Both can arise from purposeful research, but disappearing incumbent rents and the [[商业抢夺效应|business-stealing effect]] require an explicit displacement mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一基本模型把劳动在制造与研发之间即时分配，没有失业、再培训或转岗摩擦。因此可以用它研究 [[Aghion–Howitt稳态研发|创新激励与资源分配]]，但不能把现实劳动者的调整成本当成已经由该模型计算出的结果。总体增长也不自动说明每个受影响者都获益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The basic model reallocates labour immediately between manufacturing and research, without unemployment, retraining or adjustment frictions. It studies [[Aghion–Howitt稳态研发|innovation incentives and resource allocation]], but does not calculate workers' real-world adjustment costs. Aggregate growth does not imply gains for everyone affected.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
