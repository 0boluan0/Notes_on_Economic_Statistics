# Smith 以使用价值表示物品的用途，以交换价值表示它购买其他物品的能力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Smith uses value in use for an object's usefulness and value in exchange for its power to purchase other goods*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在《国富论》中，**使用价值（value in use）**回答“这个东西有什么用”，**交换价值（value in exchange）**回答“拥有它，能换到多少别的东西”。前者说物品的用途，后者说它在交换中的购买能力；用途很重要，并不保证它能换来很多商品。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In *The Wealth of Nations*, **value in use** concerns what an object is useful for, whereas **value in exchange** concerns how much of other goods its owner can obtain for it. Important uses do not by themselves guarantee great purchasing power in exchange.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Smith 用水与钻石说明两种判断可以分开：水对生活极有用，在他的例子里却几乎换不到什么；钻石的直接用途远小于水，却可以换取许多其他商品。这里先提出一个需要解释的反差，并没有从“水很有用”直接推出水应该卖什么价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith illustrates the distinction with water and diamonds. Water is enormously useful but, in his example, purchases very little; a diamond has far less direct usefulness yet can command many other goods. The example identifies a contrast to explain rather than deriving a particular price from water's usefulness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也规定了后面的问题：既然用途和交换能力不是一回事，交换能力该用什么衡量，又由什么因素决定？Smith 随后讨论[[劳动支配与劳动投入|劳动的计量口径]]，再讨论价格的构成及其变动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The distinction sets up the next questions: how should purchasing power be measured, and what determines it? Smith next examines [[劳动支配与劳动投入|labour as a measure]], followed by the components and movements of price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到了边际主义，分析转向“在已有数量下，再得到一小单位能满足多迫切的需要”。这属于后来的[[边际效用与市场价格|边际评价解释]]，不是 Smith 在本段已经给出的解答。比较这两种问法，比把“使用价值”直接换写成“边际效用”更能看清思想史上的变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Marginalist analysis later asks how urgent a want an additional small unit would satisfy at the existing stock. That is a later [[边际效用与市场价格|explanation through marginal valuation]], not the answer Smith supplies in this passage. Comparing the questions preserves the historical change that would disappear if value in use were simply renamed marginal utility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
