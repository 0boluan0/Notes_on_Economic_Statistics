# 按目标与草稿概率比验收并从正残差重采样，可以恢复目标 token 分布

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Acceptance by the target-to-draft probability ratio, followed by positive-residual resampling on rejection, recovers the target token distribution.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定同一个已提交前缀，令 $p(v)$ 为目标采样分布，$q(v)$ 为实际草稿分布，二者定义在同一有限词表上。草稿抽取 $X\sim q$ 后，以 $\min(1,p(X)/q(X))$ 的概率保留它；拒绝时从归一化的正残差 $(p-q)_+$ 重采样。按精确概率运算，这个过程最终输出的 token 服从 $p$。它是 [[投机解码]]保持分布的单步依据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix one committed prefix and let $p(v)$ be the target sampling distribution and $q(v)$ the actual draft distribution over the same finite vocabulary. After drawing $X\sim q$, retain it with probability $\min(1,p(X)/q(X))$; on rejection, resample from the normalized positive residual $(p-q)_+$. Under exact probability arithmetic, the resulting token follows $p$. This is the one-step basis for distribution preservation in [[投机解码|speculative decoding]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 接受分支已经分配了多少概率

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Probability mass already allocated by acceptance.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

独立抽取 $U\sim\operatorname{Uniform}(0,1)$，当 $U\le a(X)$ 时接受，其中

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Draw $U\sim\operatorname{Uniform}(0,1)$ independently and accept when $U\le a(X)$, where
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
a(v)=
\begin{cases}
\min\!\left(1,\dfrac{p(v)}{q(v)}\right),&q(v)>0,\\
1,&q(v)=0.
\end{cases}
$$

比值只在 $q(X)>0$ 的实际候选上求值。$q(v)=0$ 的 token 不会由草稿抽中，此时约定 $a(v)=1$ 只为把函数补全，不改变任何采样结果。这些 token 仍可由修正补回，无需要求草稿覆盖目标的全部支持集。通过接受分支输出 $v$ 的**无条件概率质量**为

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ratio is evaluated only for an actual candidate with $q(X)>0$. A token with $q(v)=0$ cannot be proposed; setting $a(v)=1$ there merely completes the function's definition without changing any sample. Such tokens can still be supplied by correction, so the draft need not cover the target's entire support. The **unconditional probability mass** assigned to output $v$ by the acceptance branch is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Pr(X=v,\text{接受})=q(v)a(v)=\min(p(v),q(v)),
\qquad
\beta=\Pr(\text{接受})=\sum_v\min(p(v),q(v)).
$$

## 拒绝分支恰好补齐缺额

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The rejection branch exactly fills the deficit.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接受分支给 $v$ 分配的质量不超过 $p(v)$，尚缺 $p(v)-\min(p(v),q(v))=(p(v)-q(v))_+$，其中 $(z)_+=\max(z,0)$。所有缺额相加为 $1-\beta$，也正好是发生拒绝的概率。因此拒绝后采用

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Acceptance allocates no more than $p(v)$ to token $v$, leaving a deficit of $p(v)-\min(p(v),q(v))=(p(v)-q(v))_+$, where $(z)_+=\max(z,0)$. The deficits sum to $1-\beta$, exactly the probability of rejection. The conditional resampling distribution is therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
r(v)=\frac{(p(v)-q(v))_+}{\sum_u(p(u)-q(u))_+}
=\frac{(p(v)-q(v))_+}{1-\beta}.
$$

若 $\beta=1$，则 $p=q$ 且拒绝不会发生，不应调用分母为零的残差归一化。其他情形中，记最终输出为 $Y$，合并接受和拒绝两条分支便得到

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\beta=1$, then $p=q$ and rejection never occurs, so the zero-denominator residual normalization must not be invoked. Otherwise, writing $Y$ for the final output and combining the two branches gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Pr(Y=v)=\min(p(v),q(v))+(1-\beta)r(v)=p(v).
$$

此外，由 $\min(a,b)=(a+b-|a-b|)/2$ 可得 $\beta=1-\frac12\sum_v|p(v)-q(v)|$。接受率取决于两分布重叠的概率质量；这个等式不涉及两个模型的运行时间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity $\min(a,b)=(a+b-|a-b|)/2$ also gives $\beta=1-\frac12\sum_v|p(v)-q(v)|$. Acceptance depends on the overlapping probability mass of the two distributions; this identity says nothing about either model's runtime.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三个 token 的完整算例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A complete example with three tokens.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造词表 $\{A,B,C\}$，取 $p=(0.2,0.5,0.3)$、$q=(0.5,0.3,0.2)$。下表依次列出目标概率、草稿概率、验收概率、接受分支的无条件质量、拒绝后的条件分布及最终输出概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a constructed vocabulary $\{A,B,C\}$, take $p=(0.2,0.5,0.3)$ and $q=(0.5,0.3,0.2)$. The columns show target probability, draft probability, acceptance probability, unconditional accepted mass, the conditional distribution after rejection, and final output probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| $v$ | $p(v)$ | $q(v)$ | $a(v)$ | $q(v)a(v)$ | $r(v)$ | $\Pr(Y=v)$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 0.2 | 0.5 | 0.4 | 0.2 | 0 | 0.2 |
| B | 0.5 | 0.3 | 1 | 0.3 | 2/3 | 0.5 |
| C | 0.3 | 0.2 | 1 | 0.2 | 1/3 | 0.3 |

总接受率 $\beta=0.7$，拒绝率 $0.3$。A 被草稿提得过多，验收把多余质量截去；B、C 尚缺的 $0.2$、$0.1$，由拒绝分支按 $2:1$ 补回。例如 B 的最终概率为 $0.3+0.3\times\frac23=0.5$，不是残差表中的 $\frac23$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The total acceptance rate is $\beta=0.7$, leaving rejection probability $0.3$. The draft proposes A too often, so acceptance trims its excess mass. The remaining deficits of $0.2$ for B and $0.1$ for C are supplied in a $2:1$ ratio by the rejection branch. For example, B's final probability is $0.3+0.3\times\frac23=0.5$, rather than the residual table's $\frac23$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用上述验收规则，如果拒绝后误从原始 $p$ 重采样，最终分布会成为

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keeping this acceptance rule but incorrectly resampling from the original $p$ after rejection would instead produce
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
(0.2,0.3,0.2)+0.3(0.2,0.5,0.3)=(0.26,0.45,0.29)\ne p.
$$

问题在于接受分支已经偏向两分布重叠的部分，修正必须补缺额。这个反例只针对当前验收规则，并未否定使用另一种验收比例的经典拒绝采样。多 token 如何按真实前缀组合本定理，见 [[投机前缀验收]]；精确概率与实际实现的关系，见 [[投机分布保持边界]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Acceptance has already allocated the overlapping mass, so correction must fill the deficits. This counterexample concerns the present acceptance rule; it does not invalidate classical rejection sampling with a different acceptance ratio. [[投机前缀验收|Speculative prefix acceptance]] explains how to compose the result over real prefixes, and [[投机分布保持边界|the distribution-preservation boundary]] relates the probability theorem to implementations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
