# 财政规则的存在、实际合规与经济效果是需要分别验证的三个判断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The existence of a fiscal rule, actual compliance and economic effectiveness require separate evidence.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“有规则”意味着可以识别一项生效中的制度约束；“合规”意味着实际行为和结果满足该规则及其合法调整程序；“有效”还要回答它是否改善了希望改善的财政或经济表现。三者的证据不同，不能从第一项直接推到第三项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Existence means an operative institutional constraint can be identified. Compliance concerns observed conduct and results under that rule and its authorised adjustment procedures. Effectiveness asks whether desired fiscal or economic performance improved. Evidence for the first does not establish the third.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验合规要固定[[财政规则覆盖边界]]，核对预算时与执行后的数值，以及获批的例外和回归安排。检验效果则先写清目标：债务风险、稳定性、透明度或支出质量。仅看到采用规则后赤字下降，还不能排除景气、税基或其他改革的影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compliance checks require a fixed [[财政规则覆盖边界|measurement perimeter]], planned and realised figures, and authorised deviations and return arrangements. Effectiveness requires a stated objective such as debt risk, stabilisation, transparency or spending quality. A deficit decline after adoption alone cannot exclude the cycle, tax-base changes or other reforms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[独立财政机构]]可以提供预测、合规分析与公开解释，但机构报告仍需按其证据范围阅读。规则达标不保证每项支出值得做，未达某个政治目标也不单独证明[[政府失灵]]；都需要明确评价标准与可行的对照。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[独立财政机构|Independent fiscal institutions]] can supply forecasts, compliance analysis and public explanations, which must still be read within their evidential scope. Rule compliance does not prove every expenditure worthwhile; missing a political target alone does not establish [[政府失灵|government failure]]. Both require criteria and feasible comparisons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [IMF, Fiscal Rules Dataset](https://www.imf.org/en/topics/fiscal-policies/fiscal-rules-dataset)，定义与覆盖说明：支持制度设计、实际执行与效果之别；制度字段和数据库另列的合规指标应分别解释。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The definition and coverage distinguish institutional design, adherence and effectiveness; institutional fields and separately included compliance measures require their own interpretation.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [IMF, How to Select Fiscal Rules: A Primer](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/howtonotes/howtonote1809.pdf)，印刷页 1–4；[OECD, Recommendation on Budgetary Governance](https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/public/doc/319/body-text.en.html)，原则 8、10：支持目标权衡、评价及监督。正文将这些目标与不同类型的评价证据相区分。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The IMF discussion and OECD Principles 8 and 10 support design objectives, evaluation and oversight. The text distinguishes those objectives from the evidence required to assess them.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
