# 多重共线性描述设计矩阵各列之间的依赖，内生性描述回归量与结构误差的关系，二者不会互相推出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Multicollinearity concerns dependence among design columns, whereas endogeneity concerns the relation between regressors and the structural error; neither implies the other*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 两条不同的轴
> **多重共线性**问的是 $X$ 的不同列能否被数据分开；**内生性**问的是目标方程所需的 $E[Xu]=0$ 或更强外生条件是否成立。前者主要决定系数是否唯一、是否精确；后者决定 OLS 是否围绕目标结构参数取值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Multicollinearity** asks whether the columns of $X$ can be separated by the observed design. **Endogeneity** asks whether the orthogonality condition required by the target structural equation, such as $E[Xu]=0$, holds. The former governs uniqueness and precision; the latter governs whether OLS is centred on the structural target.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 问题 | 关系发生在哪里 | 典型后果 |
|---|---|---|
| 完全共线 | $X$ 的列之间 | 相关单个系数不唯一 |
| 近似共线 | $X$ 的列之间 | 单个系数方差大、对样本和设定敏感 |
| 内生性 | 回归量与目标方程的结构误差之间 | 结构系数可能有偏且不一致 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Problem | Where the relation occurs | Typical consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Exact collinearity | Among columns of $X$ | Affected individual coefficients are not unique |
| Near collinearity | Among columns of $X$ | Individual coefficients are imprecise and specification-sensitive |
| Endogeneity | Between regressors and the target equation's structural error | Structural coefficient estimates may be biased and inconsistent |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个问题可以分别存在。随机实验中两个处理变量若总是一起分配，可能外生却无法分别识别；反过来，一个回归量可与误差相关但和其他回归量近乎正交，此时共线性不严重，内生性仍然存在。高 VIF 因而不是内生性检验，低 VIF 也不是因果识别证书。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The problems can occur separately. Two treatments may be exogenous but always assigned together, making their separate effects unidentified. Conversely, a regressor may be correlated with the structural error while nearly orthogonal to every other regressor. A high VIF is therefore not a test of endogeneity, and a low VIF is not a certificate of causal identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当设计满列秩且[[零条件均值无偏性|零条件均值]]成立时，近似共线性不会单独制造系统偏差；它会让可用于分开系数的独立变异变少。为了降低 VIF 而删掉真正的混杂控制变量，反而可能把精度问题变成内生性问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With full column rank and zero conditional mean, near multicollinearity does not by itself create systematic bias; it reduces the independent variation available to separate coefficients. Deleting a genuine confounder merely to lower a VIF can instead turn a precision problem into an endogeneity problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个解释变量的 VIF 等于 1，能否据此认定它外生？
>
> **答案：** 不能。VIF=1 只说明它不能被其余设计列线性解释；它仍可能与目标方程的结构误差相关。
