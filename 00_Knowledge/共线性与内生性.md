# Multicollinearity and endogeneity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*共线性与内生性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A regression can struggle for two different reasons: the observed predictors move together so closely that their separate contributions are hard to distinguish, or a predictor moves with the unobserved error in the equation we actually want to estimate. The first is [[多重共线性|multicollinearity]], a relationship among design columns. The second is [[内生性|endogeneity]], a relationship between predictors and the target equation’s error. Diagnosing either one does not diagnose the other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归可能遇到两种不同困难：观察到的解释变量几乎总是一起变化，难以分开各自贡献；或者某个解释变量与真正要估计的方程中未观察到的误差一起变化。前者是[[多重共线性]]，关系发生在设计列之间；后者是[[内生性]]，关系发生在解释变量与目标方程误差之间。判断其中一种，不能代替判断另一种。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What each question needs

Write the target equation as $y=D\beta+u$. The [[设计矩阵|design matrix]] $D$ stacks one observation per row and one included feature per column, including a column of ones when there is an intercept. The coefficient vector $\beta$ is the target, and $u$ is its unobserved error, not the fitted [[回归残差|residual]].

Collinearity can be inspected using $D$ alone. Exact column dependence makes at least some coefficients nonunique; near dependence can make particular coefficient combinations imprecise, depending on design scale and the error covariance. The mechanism and its conditions are in [[近似共线性精度|precision under near collinearity]].

Exogeneity instead requires a claim about the target equation and the data-generating process. For full column rank, $E(u\mid D)=0$ gives [[零条件均值无偏性|conditional unbiasedness of OLS]]. Let $d_i$ be observation $i$’s feature vector. With finite second moments, a weaker population moment, such as $E[d_i u_i]=0$ with nonsingular $E[d_id_i']$, identifies the population linear-projection coefficient with the target; consistency additionally needs valid sample-average limits. These are different guarantees. A nonzero conditional error mean need not produce bias in every coefficient, since the relevant projected component can be zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两个问题分别需要什么

把目标方程写成 $y=D\beta+u$。[[设计矩阵]] $D$ 每行对应一个观测，每列对应一个纳入的特征；有截距时包含一列全 1。系数向量 $\beta$ 是目标，$u$ 是该方程未观察到的误差，不是拟合后算出的[[回归残差]]。

共线性可以只看 $D$ 来检查。精确列依赖使至少部分系数不唯一；近似依赖可能使某些系数组合不精确，具体还取决于设计尺度和误差协方差。机制与条件见[[近似共线性精度]]。

外生性则需要关于目标方程和数据生成过程的论证。满列秩时，$E(u\mid D)=0$ 给出 [[零条件均值无偏性|OLS 的条件无偏性]]。令 $d_i$ 是第 $i$ 个观测的特征向量。在二阶矩有限时，较弱的总体矩条件，例如 $E[d_i u_i]=0$ 且 $E[d_id_i']$ 非奇异，使总体线性投影系数等于目标；一致性还需要样本平均的有效极限。这些保证并不相同。条件误差均值非零，也不意味着每个系数都有偏，因为相关投影分量可能为零。

## Two examples that separate them
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两个把它们分开的例子

> [!example] Exogenous but inseparable / 外生却分不开
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Randomly assign a package containing both tutoring and study materials, with positive probabilities of assignment and nonassignment. Let $T$ mark package assignment, so the two recorded treatment columns are both $T$. Suppose the target equation is $Y=\alpha+\beta_1T+\beta_2T+u$ with $E(u\mid T)=0$. Random assignment can support this exogeneity condition, while the duplicate columns identify only the sum $\gamma=\beta_1+\beta_2$. Replacing $(\beta_1,\beta_2)$ by $(\beta_1+c,\beta_2-c)$ leaves every fitted value unchanged. The package effect does not reveal the separate tutoring and materials effects.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 随机分配同时包含辅导与学习材料的套餐，分配与不分配都有正概率。用 $T$ 表示是否分配到套餐，两列处理记录因此都等于 $T$。假设目标方程为 $Y=\alpha+\beta_1T+\beta_2T+u$，且 $E(u\mid T)=0$。随机分配可以支持这条外生条件，但重复列只能识别总和 $\gamma=\beta_1+\beta_2$。把系数改成 $(\beta_1+c,\beta_2-c)$，全部拟合值仍相同。套餐效应不能分别告诉我们辅导和材料的效应。

> [!example] No predictor correlation, but an endogenous target equation / 解释变量不相关，目标方程却内生
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X,Z,\varepsilon$ be mutually independent, each taking $-1$ and $1$ with equal probability. Specify the target equation $Y=2X+u$, where $u=X+\varepsilon$, and allow $Z$ as another regressor with target coefficient zero. Then $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)=0$, but $E(Xu)=1$. In the equally weighted eight-cell design containing every $(X,Z,\varepsilon)$ combination, the predictor columns $1,X,Z$ are orthogonal and the sample VIFs are 1. OLS nevertheless gives an $X$ slope of 3, not the specified target 2. The slope 3 correctly describes $E(Y\mid X,Z)=3X$; that is a different target.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $X,Z,\varepsilon$ 相互独立，各以相同概率取 $-1$ 或 $1$。指定目标方程 $Y=2X+u$，其中 $u=X+\varepsilon$，再允许纳入目标系数为零的 $Z$。此时 $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)=0$，却有 $E(Xu)=1$。在等权包含全部 $(X,Z,\varepsilon)$ 组合的八格设计中，$1,X,Z$ 三列正交，样本 VIF 都是 1；OLS 的 $X$ 斜率仍为 3，而不是指定的目标 2。斜率 3 正确描述了 $E(Y\mid X,Z)=3X$，但它回答的是另一个目标。

## What follows for diagnosis

A high [[方差膨胀因子|VIF]] is not an endogeneity test, and a low VIF does not certify causal identification. Likewise, the normal equations force fitted residuals to be orthogonal to the included columns; this is [[样本正交与总体外生性|sample fitting algebra, not a check of target-model exogeneity]].

With full column rank and the stated zero-conditional-mean condition, near collinearity alone does not bias OLS. Removing a control merely to lower its VIF can change the coefficient being estimated and introduce [[遗漏变量偏差|omitted-variable bias]] relative to the original target. The response should therefore follow [[共线性补救原则|the research target and the source of the design problem]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 诊断时能据此做什么

高[[方差膨胀因子|VIF]] 不是内生性检验，低 VIF 也不能证明因果识别成立。同样，正规方程强制拟合残差与纳入列正交；这是[[样本正交与总体外生性|样本拟合代数，不能检查目标模型的外生性]]。

在满列秩及上述零条件均值条件下，近似共线性本身不会使 OLS 有偏。仅为降低 VIF 而删控制项，可能改变所估计的系数，并相对于原目标引入[[遗漏变量偏差]]。应根据[[共线性补救原则|研究目标与设计问题的来源]]选择处理。
