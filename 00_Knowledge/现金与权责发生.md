# 现金口径按收付时点记录而权责发生口径按经济事项发生时点记录
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash recording follows receipts and payments; accrual recording follows the timing of economic events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金口径问“钱什么时候到账或付出”，权责发生口径问“服务何时提供、资产何时转移、无条件的权利义务何时形成”。预算获批或签订待履行合同，不自动等于费用已经发生；还要区分承诺、交付、到期与支付。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash recording asks when money moves; accrual recording asks when services are supplied, ownership changes or unconditional claims arise. Budget approval or an unperformed contract does not automatically establish an incurred expense. Commitment, delivery, due date and payment are distinct stages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

某项维护服务在12月完成并验收，费用12，合同允许次年1月底支付，政府在1月20日付款。权责发生报表在12月记费用12与应付款12；1月付款时结清应付，不再记一次费用。现金报表则在1月记流出12。12月底虽然尚未付款，却没有逾期，不能直接称[[财政拖欠]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A maintenance service costing 12 is completed and accepted in December, payable by the end of January and paid on 20 January. Accrual accounts recognise expense and a payable in December; January payment settles the liability without another expense. Cash accounts record the January outflow. The unpaid December balance is not yet [[财政拖欠|an arrear]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两种口径提供不同信息：现金用于判断近期支付能力，权责发生帮助看已经发生但尚未支付的义务和资源使用。比较[[预算支出与决算支出]]或[[财政赤字]]，必须保持口径一致；不能靠推迟付款就说经济负担消失了。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash data inform immediate payment capacity; accrual data reveal incurred obligations and resource use. Compare [[预算支出与决算支出|budgets and outturns]] or [[财政赤字|deficits]] on a consistent basis. Delaying payment does not erase the underlying burden.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
