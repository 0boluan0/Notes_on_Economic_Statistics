# 加入真正无关变量通常不制造偏差但可能损失精度而完全共线另破坏唯一性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A truly irrelevant regressor usually does not create bias but may reduce precision, while exact collinearity separately destroys coefficient uniqueness*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 在扩展模型中对所有回归量的外生性等条件仍成立、且新增变量的真实系数为零时，把它加入 OLS 通常不会系统移动原系数估计的中心。它会消耗自由度；在经典同方差、给定设计的方差比较下，若新增列与既有变量共享用于识别焦点系数的变异，该系数的方差会增大，而新增列的控制后剩余部分与焦点列的控制后剩余部分正交时可以不变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If exogeneity and the other assumptions hold for every regressor in the expanded model, and the added regressor's true coefficient is zero, including it in OLS does not usually shift the centre of the existing coefficient estimates. It consumes degrees of freedom. In the classical homoskedastic fixed-design variance comparison, the focal coefficient's variance rises when the new column shares variation used to identify that coefficient, and can remain unchanged when their residualised columns are orthogonal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三种不能混称的情形
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three cases that should not be conflated*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **无关且条件正交：** 主要代价是复杂度和自由度；在上述经典方差比较下，原焦点系数的方差可保持不变。
2. **无关但高度相关：** 原系数仍可无偏，却可能因可用于分开两列的独立变异很少而标准误显著增大。
3. **完全线性组合：** 设计矩阵秩亏，单个系数不再唯一；这不是单纯“标准误大”的极端版本，而是参数化本身未被数据识别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. **Irrelevant and conditionally orthogonal:** the main costs are complexity and degrees of freedom; under the classical variance comparison above, the focal coefficient's variance can remain unchanged.
2. **Irrelevant but highly correlated:** coefficients can remain unbiased while standard errors rise because little independent variation separates the columns.
3. **An exact linear combination:** the design matrix is rank deficient and individual coefficients are not unique. This is an identification failure of the parameterisation, not merely a very large standard error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

统计上“不显著”不等于实质上无关。尤其在因果模型中，控制变量的保留与删除还取决于它对目标比较和识别假设的作用；不能只按一次样本的 p 值删掉理论上必要的控制项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Statistical insignificance is not substantive irrelevance. In a causal model, retaining a control also depends on its role in the target comparison and identification assumptions; a theoretically required control should not be deleted solely because of one sample p-value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 新增无关变量与现有 $X$ 高度相关时，最典型的代价是什么？
>
> **答案：** 在外生性仍成立时通常不是制造系统偏差，而是减少分开系数的独立变异，从而降低精度。
