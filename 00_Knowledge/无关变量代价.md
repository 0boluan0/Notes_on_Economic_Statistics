# The cost of an irrelevant regressor
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*加入无关变量的代价*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A truly irrelevant regressor has a zero coefficient in the specified larger model. Adding it asks the sample to estimate an extra number even though its population contribution, under that model, is zero. This can reduce precision without creating bias. It does not follow that every coefficient becomes less precise or that the reported standard error must rise in every dataset.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

真正无关的回归变量，是在指定的较大模型中系数为零的变量。加入它，相当于让样本额外估计一个数，而在该模型下它的总体贡献为零。这可能降低精度，却不制造偏差；但并不意味着每个系数都变得不精确，或每份数据中报告的标准误都必定上升。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the same $n$ observations and let $C$ contain the controls retained in both fits, including an intercept. Let $x$ be the focal predictor and $z$ the proposed extra column. Suppose the true conditional mean is $C\gamma+x\beta$, so the larger model $C\gamma+x\beta+z\delta$ has $\delta=0$. Here $y$ is the vector of outcomes, $u=y-C\gamma-x\beta$ is its disturbance vector, $\gamma$ collects control coefficients, and $I_n$ is the identity matrix. Require the full design $D=[C,x,z]$ to have [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full column rank]], $E(u\mid D)=0$ and $\operatorname{Var}(u\mid D)=\sigma^2 I_n$ with $\sigma^2>0$. Thus errors have zero conditional mean, equal variance and zero conditional cross-covariances; normality is not needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定同样的 $n$ 个观测。令 $C$ 包含两次拟合都保留的控制项，并包括截距；$x$ 是关注的解释变量，$z$ 是拟增加的一列。假定真实条件均值是 $C\gamma+x\beta$，所以较大模型 $C\gamma+x\beta+z\delta$ 中 $\delta=0$。这里 $y$ 是结果向量，$u=y-C\gamma-x\beta$ 是扰动向量，$\gamma$ 汇集控制项系数，$I_n$ 是单位矩阵。要求完整设计 $D=[C,x,z]$ [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]、$E(u\mid D)=0$，且 $\operatorname{Var}(u\mid D)=\sigma^2 I_n$、$\sigma^2>0$。也就是说，误差的条件均值为零，方差相同，条件交叉协方差为零；不要求正态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under these assumptions, both OLS estimates of $\beta$ are [[零条件均值无偏性|conditionally unbiased]], even though their values in a particular sample can differ. To compare their variances, first regress $x$ and $z$ separately on $C$. Call the remaining [[回归残差|residual vectors]] $v$ and $w$. They are the variation in the two columns that the common controls do not already explain. [[FWL残差化定理|FWL]] expresses the focal coefficient using precisely these remaining directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这些假设下，两种 OLS 对 $\beta$ 的估计都[[零条件均值无偏性|条件无偏]]，但在某一份样本中，数值可以不同。要比较方差，先分别把 $x,z$ 对 $C$ 回归，将剩下的[[回归残差|残差向量]]记为 $v,w$：它们代表共同控制项尚未解释的两列变动。[[FWL残差化定理|FWL]] 正是用这些剩余方向表示关注的系数。

$$
r=\frac{v'w}{\sqrt{(v'v)(w'w)}},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_{\rm short}\mid D)=\frac{\sigma^2}{v'v},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_{\rm long}\mid D)=\frac{\sigma^2}{v'v(1-r^2)}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The prime means transpose, so $v'v$ is the sum of squared residualized $x$ values. Because the common controls include an intercept, $r$ is the ordinary sample [[相关系数|correlation]] of the two residual vectors. Full rank makes both squared lengths positive and $r^2<1$. Adding $z$ leaves only $v'v(1-r^2)$ of the variation available to separate the $x$ coefficient. The variance multiplier is $1/(1-r^2)\ge1$; equality occurs at $r=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

撇号表示转置，所以 $v'v$ 是残差化后 $x$ 各分量的平方和。共同控制项包含截距，因此 $r$ 就是两组残差的普通样本[[相关系数]]。满列秩保证两组平方和为正且 $r^2<1$。加入 $z$ 后，只剩 $v'v(1-r^2)$ 的变动可用于分开估计 $x$ 的系数。方差倍数为 $1/(1-r^2)\ge1$；$r=0$ 时相等。

## Orthogonal, overlapping and exactly redundant columns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三种不能混称的情形

> [!example] Four rows show when the cost is zero or positive / 四行数据看清何时损失精度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With an intercept as the only common control, take $x=(-1,-1,1,1)'$ and $w=(-1,1,-1,1)'$. Both are centered, their squared lengths are $4$, and $x'w=0$.
>
> If the irrelevant added column is $z=w$, the two slope variances both equal $\sigma^2/4$. If instead $z=x+w=(-2,0,0,2)'$, its squared length is $8$ and $x'z=4$, giving $r^2=1/2$. The slope variance doubles to $\sigma^2/2$.
>
> In both full-rank cases, the number of coefficients rises from two to three and residual degrees of freedom fall from two to one. If $z=x$ exactly, there is no unique way to separate their coefficients: only their sum determines the fitted line. That last case is rank deficiency, not a finite variance inflation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 共同控制项只有截距，取 $x=(-1,-1,1,1)'$、$w=(-1,1,-1,1)'$。两列均值为零、平方和均为 $4$，且 $x'w=0$。
>
> 若新增无关列为 $z=w$，两次拟合的斜率方差都是 $\sigma^2/4$。若改为 $z=x+w=(-2,0,0,2)'$，其平方和为 $8$，且 $x'z=4$，于是 $r^2=1/2$，斜率方差翻倍至 $\sigma^2/2$。
>
> 两个满列秩情形中，系数数目都从二增至三，残差自由度从二减至一。若恰好 $z=x$，则无法唯一分开两列的系数，只有系数之和决定拟合直线。最后这种情况是秩亏，并非一个有限的方差膨胀。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed comparison concerns the true repeated-sample variance conditional on the design, with the same $\sigma^2$. A reported [[OLS经典协方差估计|standard error]] also uses an estimated error variance, usually $s^2=SSR/(n-p)$ when $n>p$ and $p$ counts the fitted independent coefficients. Adding a column [[训练拟合单调性|cannot increase training SSR]] on the same rows, while reducing the denominator. Their ratio, and hence a realized standard error, need not move in one direction. “The true variance is weakly larger” and “this reported standard error increased” are different claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的比较针对给定设计、同一个 $\sigma^2$ 下重复抽样的真实方差。报告的[[OLS经典协方差估计|标准误]]还会使用估计的误差方差，通常在 $n>p$ 时取 $s^2=SSR/(n-p)$，其中 $p$ 数拟合中的独立系数。相同行上增加一列，[[训练拟合单调性|训练 SSR 不会上升]]，分母也减少，因此比值以及某次样本的标准误不一定只朝一个方向变化。“真实方差弱增”和“这次报告的标准误上升”是两个判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This variance ordering depends on the spherical error covariance assumed above. With heteroskedasticity or correlated errors, there is no general ordering for ordinary OLS after adding an irrelevant variable. Nor does the result apply if conditioning on the new column breaks zero conditional mean. Zero contribution, exogeneity and rank are distinct assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个方差排序依赖于上面假定的球形误差协方差。存在异方差或误差相关时，普通 OLS 加入无关变量后的方差没有这种普遍排序。若对新增列取条件后零条件均值不再成立，结论同样不能直接套用。零贡献、外生性和秩是不同的假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, “truly irrelevant” is a statement about the specified population model, not a label established by one nonsignificant coefficient. A wide interval can include both zero and substantively important values. Variable retention should follow [[回归设定起点|the target and research design]], while prediction requires separate performance evidence. An irrelevant added term is an example of overspecification; predictive overfitting requires evidence about performance beyond the fitting sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后，“真正无关”是关于指定总体模型的陈述，不是一次系数不显著就能确定的标签。很宽的区间可以同时包含零和实质上重要的数值。变量保留应服从[[回归设定起点|目标与研究设计]]，预测还需要单独的表现证据。加入无关项属于过度设定的例子；预测意义的过拟合需要拟合样本之外的表现证据。
