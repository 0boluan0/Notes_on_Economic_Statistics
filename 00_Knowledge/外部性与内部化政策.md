# 外部性与内部化政策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Externalities and Internalization Policies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 分析个人决策没有计入对他人影响，或无法有效排除未付费者时，市场数量为何偏离社会最优。
> **具体锚点：** 工厂只面对生产成本而不承担居民污染损害时，会把产量推到私人边际成本等于价格，而不是社会边际成本等于价格。
> **核心难点：** 必须在社会最优数量处比较边际损害与边际收益；平均损害、总损害和“零污染”都不是正确税率条件。
> **为什么重要：** 排污税、排放交易、疫苗补贴、拥堵费和产权谈判都在选择怎样让外部影响进入决策。
> **继续：** 先画出私人和社会边际曲线，再根据监测能力与不确定性比较税、数量、交易和协商。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Explain why market quantities depart from the social optimum when private decisions omit effects on others or when non-payers cannot be effectively excluded.
> **Concrete anchor:** When a factory faces production cost but not residents' pollution damage, it expands until price equals private rather than social marginal cost.
> **Central difficulty:** Marginal damage and marginal benefit must be compared at the efficient quantity; average damage, total damage, and “zero pollution” are not the correct tax condition.
> **Why it matters:** Emissions taxes, cap-and-trade, vaccine subsidies, congestion charges, and property-rights bargaining all choose how to bring external effects into decisions.
> **Continue with:** Draw private and social marginal curves first, then compare taxes, quantity limits, trading, and bargaining in light of monitoring and uncertainty.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

外部性问题的核心不是“某项活动有坏处或好处”，而是行动者面对的边际成本或收益与全社会面对的边际量不一致，而且这部分影响没有通过市场价格或契约被计入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The core of an externality is not simply that an activity has harmful or beneficial consequences. It is that the marginal cost or benefit faced by the decision-maker differs from the marginal cost or benefit faced by society, with the difference left outside market prices or contracts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 外部性的边际楔子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Marginal Wedge Created by an Externality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

负外部性下社会边际成本 $SMC=PMC+MD$，市场数量通常过高；正外部性下社会边际收益高于私人收益，数量过低。政策目标是让决策者面对社会边际成本/收益，而非简单“消灭所有外部性”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a negative externality, social marginal cost is $SMC=PMC+MD$, so the market quantity is usually excessive. With a positive externality, social marginal benefit exceeds private marginal benefit, so the market quantity is too low. The policy objective is to make decision-makers face social marginal costs or benefits, not simply to eliminate every external effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确定外部影响随活动量怎样变化，再找竞争市场数量 $Q_m$ 与社会最优 $Q^*$。最优通常仍保留一部分污染或风险，因为把活动降到零也有机会成本；正确边际条件是额外一单位社会收益等于额外一单位社会成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First determine how the external effect varies with the activity level, then identify the competitive quantity $Q_m$ and the social optimum $Q^*$. The optimum often retains some pollution or risk because reducing the activity to zero also has an opportunity cost. The correct marginal condition equates the social benefit and social cost of one additional unit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Pigouvian 税、补贴与管制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pigouvian Taxes, Subsidies, and Regulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理想 Pigouvian 税等于最优数量处边际损害，使私人一阶条件与社会条件一致。数量标准在损害陡、成本不确定时可能更合适；价格工具在减排成本差异大时给灵活性。补贴也可能扩大行业规模或产生财政成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ideal Pigouvian tax equals marginal damage at the efficient quantity, aligning the private first-order condition with the social condition. A quantity standard may be preferable when damages are steep and costs uncertain, while a price instrument provides flexibility when abatement costs differ greatly. Subsidies can also expand the size of the industry or impose fiscal costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税率不是简单等于当前平均损害，也不是对受害者全部损失逐元赔偿。它针对边际行为：在 $Q^*$ 处，每增加一单位活动造成的外部损害。若排放无法准确监测，可对投入、产出或技术征税/设标，但替代指标会引入新的扭曲。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tax rate is neither current average damage nor a dollar-for-dollar reimbursement of total harm. It targets behavior at the margin: the external damage caused by one more unit of activity at $Q^*$. If emissions cannot be measured accurately, policy may target inputs, output, or technology, but proxy instruments introduce additional distortions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 排放交易与产权
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Emissions Trading and Property Rights*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

cap-and-trade 固定总量并允许许可交易，使边际减排成本趋同。初始配额影响分配，标准条件下不影响成本有效配置。监测、额外性、市场力量和价格波动影响现实效果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cap-and-trade fixes the aggregate quantity and permits allowance trading, tending to equalize marginal abatement costs. Initial allowance allocation affects distribution but, under standard conditions, not the cost-effective allocation of abatement. Monitoring, additionality, market power, and price volatility matter in practice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总量交易和税都可使不同企业选择各自低成本减排路径。税固定边际价格而让排放量调整；交易固定排放总量而让许可价格调整。混合机制如价格地板、价格上限或稳定储备可管理极端不确定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both emissions trading and taxation allow heterogeneous firms to choose their lower-cost abatement paths. A tax fixes the marginal price and lets emissions adjust; trading fixes aggregate emissions and lets the allowance price adjust. Hybrid mechanisms such as price floors, ceilings, or market-stability reserves can manage extreme uncertainty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Coase 机制及边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Coase Mechanism and Its Boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

产权清晰、交易成本低、参与者少且信息充分时，协商可内部化外部性；初始权利仍影响财富分配。大规模污染、信息不对称和执行困难使条件常失败。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When property rights are clear, transaction costs are low, the number of parties is small, and information is adequate, bargaining can internalize an externality. Initial rights still affect wealth distribution. Large-scale pollution, asymmetric information, and enforcement difficulties often make these conditions fail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Coase 分析的用途是提醒我们比较交易制度，而不是证明政府永远无须介入。政府可能通过界定权利、建立监测和法院、降低谈判成本来使协商成为可能；也可能因信息和执行失败产生更差结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The value of Coasean analysis is to direct attention to alternative transactional institutions, not to prove that government is never needed. Government may make bargaining possible by defining rights, building monitoring and judicial systems, and reducing negotiation costs; it may also perform poorly because of information and enforcement failures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：排污税
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: An Emissions Tax*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若企业每多生产一单位获得的私人边际净收益为 $100-Q$，边际外部损害恒为 20，则无政策时企业在 $Q=100$ 停止；社会条件为 $100-Q=20$，得到 $Q^*=80$。每单位 20 的税使企业面对相同条件。若损害随数量上升，税率应取 $Q^*$ 处的边际损害，而非常数平均值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the firm's private marginal net benefit from another unit is $100-Q$ and marginal external damage is a constant 20. Without policy the firm stops at $Q=100$; the social condition is $100-Q=20$, giving $Q^*=80$. A tax of 20 per unit makes the firm face the same condition. If damage rises with quantity, the correct tax is marginal damage evaluated at $Q^*$ rather than a constant average.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 工具选择与失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Instrument Choice and Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

依次检查：外部性是否真实位于边际；受影响者与行动者能否识别；排放/行为能否监测；成本和损害哪一侧更不确定；工具是否引起规避、转移或进入退出。若政策只改变账面指标而影响转移到未监管渠道，说明靶向变量选错。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check in order whether the externality is genuinely marginal, whether affected parties and actors can be identified, whether emissions or behavior can be monitored, which side—costs or damages—is more uncertain, and whether the instrument induces avoidance, relocation, entry, or exit. If policy improves only the reported metric while the harm shifts to an unregulated channel, the target variable is wrong.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Pigouvian 税率应等于哪里的边际损害？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*At what quantity should the marginal damage underlying a Pigouvian tax be evaluated?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 理想上等于社会最优数量处的边际外部损害，使私人边际成本对齐社会边际成本。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Ideally, it equals marginal external damage at the socially efficient quantity, aligning private marginal cost with social marginal cost.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Coase 定理为什么不是‘政府永远不用管’？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does the Coase theorem not mean that government should never intervene?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 其有效性依赖产权、低交易成本、信息和执行；现实大规模外部性常不满足。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Its effectiveness depends on property rights, low transaction costs, information, and enforcement; large-scale real-world externalities often do not satisfy these conditions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么最优污染通常不等于零？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is the efficient level of pollution usually not zero?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 消除最后一单位污染也有减排成本；最优点比较边际损害与边际减排或产出收益，而不是把任何损害机械降为零。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Eliminating the final unit of pollution also has an abatement cost. The optimum compares marginal damage with marginal abatement cost or production benefit rather than mechanically reducing every harm to zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
