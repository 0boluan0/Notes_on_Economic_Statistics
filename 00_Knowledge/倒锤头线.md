# 倒锤头线是下降走势后小实体靠近区间下端且上影线较长的K线

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inverted hammer is a candle after a decline with a small body near the bottom of its range and a long upper shadow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

倒锤头线是下降走势之后出现的一根[[K线]]：小实体处于本期价格区间下端，上影线较长，下影线没有或很短，实体可阴可阳。相同轮廓在上涨后属于[[射击之星]]的识别背景；“倒锤头”不能只凭长上影线命名。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inverted hammer is a [[K线|candlestick]] after a decline with a small body near the bottom of its range, a long upper shadow and little or no lower shadow. Either body direction is allowed. The same outline after an advance belongs to the [[射击之星|shooting-star]] context; a long upper shadow alone is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本课要求等待下一期间的看涨确认，例如下一期开盘高于倒锤头实体，或随后形成较高水平的阳线。若选用收盘确认，必须等该期结束；当期曾冲高又回落本身不证明买方已取得持续优势。形态和确认都只是学派的识别约定，不保证后续上涨。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course requires bullish confirmation in the next period, such as an opening above the inverted hammer's body or a subsequent up candle at a higher level. A closing confirmation must wait until that period ends. An intraperiod rally followed by a retreat does not itself establish lasting buying strength. Neither the shape nor its confirmation guarantees an advance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
