# 比较货币贫困之前须对齐福利内容、家庭换算、人口权重与价格期间

测量货币贫困时，先说清 $y_i$ 代表什么，再与[[贫困线]]比较。工资、家庭可支配收入、人均消费和国家人均产出不是可互换的“收入”；统一显示成一个货币单位，并不能消除它们的内容差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before measuring monetary poverty, establish what $y_i$ represents and then compare it with the [[贫困线|poverty line]]. Wages, household disposable income, per-person consumption, and national output per person are not interchangeable income measures. A common currency label does not remove differences in content.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先检查福利内容。收入法可能包括劳动收入、经营收入、转移收入等，并扣除相应税费；消费法可包括购买、自给生产、受赠消费及住房服务等。收入会随收款和经营冲击波动，消费也受调查问卷、回忆期和估价方法影响，因此不能假定换一种变量只会给出同一个贫困率。住房服务流量也不能直接用当期购房总价替代。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First inspect the aggregate's components. Income measures may include earnings, business income, and transfers net of relevant taxes; consumption may include purchased, own-produced, and gifted items and housing services. Income responds to payment timing and business shocks, while consumption estimates depend on questionnaires, recall periods, and valuation. Switching the variable need not preserve the poverty rate. The flow of housing services is not the full purchase price of a home.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再检查家庭怎样变为个人。若家庭 $h$ 总福利为 $C_h$、有 $s_h$ 个成员，人均法令每位成员的福利为 $C_h/s_h$；这是均分处理，不是已观察到每个人实际得到相同份额。成人等价尺度则按需要差异和可能的家庭规模经济调整分母，不能在比较中默默切换两种方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next inspect the household-to-person conversion. With household welfare $C_h$ and $s_h$ members, the per-capita method assigns $C_h/s_h$ to each member. Equal assignment is a convention, not observed equal sharing. Adult-equivalence scales instead adjust for needs and possible household economies of scale; the two methods must not be switched silently across comparisons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一条记录是一户，其抽样权重 $a_h$ 表示该户代表多少户，则计算人口贫困率时的权重应为 $a_hs_h$。这是[[贫困人口比例]]中“数人”与“数户”的区别。最后统一调查期间和地区价格，检查极低值、零值及负收入的处理；国际价格转换再接[[购买力平价]]。调查问卷或构造方法变更造成的序列断点，也不能当作生活改善；发布与修订版本的检查复用[[经济数据的实时口径]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a household record has sampling weight $a_h$, representing that many households, its population weight is $a_hs_h$. This is the distinction between counting people and households in the [[贫困人口比例|headcount ratio]]. Finally align survey periods and regional prices and inspect the treatment of very low, zero, and negative observations. [[购买力平价|Purchasing power parity]] supplies the international price conversion. A break caused by questionnaire or construction changes is not evidence of improved living conditions; use [[经济数据的实时口径|data-vintage checks]] for releases and revisions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述变量仍只是货币福利的代理。教育、健康或基本服务方面的剥夺，需另外观察；[[多维贫困]]进一步讨论多种剥夺的联合识别，不能从是否高于一条收入线直接推断这些剥夺已经消失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These aggregates remain proxies for monetary welfare. Deprivations in education, health, and basic services require additional evidence; [[多维贫困|multidimensional poverty]] examines their joint identification. Being above an income line does not establish their absence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
