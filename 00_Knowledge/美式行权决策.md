# 美式行权决策比较即时支付与保留后续行权选择的继续价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An American exercise decision compares immediate payoff with continuation value that retains future exercise choices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

美式期权允许持有人按合约约定在到期前行权。判断某一允许行权的时点是否应行权，须比较**立即取得的支付**与**本次不行权、但以后仍可最优行权的继续价值**；只看是否实值，或只与欧式到期支付比较，都不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An American option permits its holder to exercise before expiry under the contract's terms. At an eligible exercise time, compare the immediate payoff with the value of continuing now while retaining optimal future exercise choices. Being in the money, or comparing only with a European terminal payoff, is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在无摩擦、可复制且具有一致风险中性定价的离散模型中，设允许决策的日期为 $t_0<\cdots<t_N=T$，$H_i$ 为立即行权支付，$A_i$ 为尚未行权合约的价值。对普通看涨、看跌，分别用 $H_i=(S_i-K)^+$、$H_i=(K-S_i)^+$；$x^+=\max(x,0)$。设现金账户为 $B_i>0$，支付具有所需可积性，从到期向前计算：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a frictionless, replicable discrete model with consistent risk-neutral valuation, let $t_0<\cdots<t_N=T$ be the decision dates, $H_i$ the immediate exercise payoff, and $A_i$ the value of the unexercised contract. A vanilla call and put use $(S_i-K)^+$ and $(K-S_i)^+$ respectively, where $x^+=\max(x,0)$. With positive cash account $B_i$ and integrable payoffs, work backward from expiry:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
A_N=H_N,\qquad
C_i=E^Q\!\left[\left.\frac{B_i}{B_{i+1}}A_{i+1}\right|\mathcal F_i\right],
\qquad A_i=\max(H_i,C_i).
$$

这里 $\mathcal F_i$ 是当时已知的信息；$C_i$ 是继续价值，不是“看涨价格”的固定记号。$H_i>C_i$ 时行权，$H_i<C_i$ 时继续，等号时模型价值上无差别。关键是期望内放入 $A_{i+1}$：未来节点也有选择权，不能一概换成相同到期日的欧式价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mathcal F_i$ is current information, and $C_i$ denotes continuation value rather than a call price. Exercise if $H_i>C_i$, continue if $H_i<C_i$, and regard equality as value indifference within the model. The expectation contains $A_{i+1}$ because future nodes retain exercise choices; it cannot generally be replaced by the corresponding European value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 单节点核算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A one-node calculation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

某看跌的执行价为 $100$，当前股票为 $80$，下一决策日就是到期日，股票可能为 $96$ 或 $64$。股票无股息，每期上、下变动因子为 $u=1.2,d=0.8$，现金增长因子为 $R=1.1$。因 $d<R<u$，与股票定价一致的风险中性上行概率为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A put has strike 100 and current stock price 80; its next decision date is expiry, with stock price 96 or 64. The stock pays no dividends, its up/down factors are $u=1.2,d=0.8$, and cash grows by $R=1.1$ per period. Since $d<R<u$, the stock-consistent risk-neutral up probability is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$p_Q=\frac{R-d}{u-d}=\frac34,\qquad
\frac{p_Q\,96+(1-p_Q)64}{1.1}=80.$$

两种到期支付是 $4$ 和 $36$，所以
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The terminal payoffs are 4 and 36, giving:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$H=100-80=20,\qquad C=\frac{\tfrac34\cdot4+\tfrac14\cdot36}{1.1}
=\frac{120}{11}\approx10.909091,\qquad A=20.$$

这一节点应行权，因为立即支付大于继续价值。这里计算的是指定离散行权日期上的模型；把连续可行权的美式合约离散到有限日期，会得到近似而非自动得到连续时间精确价格。交易费用、结算限制和估值误差也须另行判断。完整的两期对照见 [[当前不行权与提前权利金]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exercise at this node because immediate payoff exceeds continuation value. This calculation uses specified discrete exercise dates; restricting a continuously exercisable American contract to finitely many dates gives an approximation, not automatically its exact continuous-time price. Transaction costs, settlement restrictions and valuation error require separate assessment. See [[当前不行权与提前权利金|current non-exercise versus the early-exercise premium]] for the full two-period comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
