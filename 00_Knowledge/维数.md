# Dimension
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*维数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **dimension** of a finite-dimensional vector space counts how many independent scalar choices are needed to specify one of its vectors. A [[基|basis]] supplies exactly such choices: every vector has a unique coefficient for each basis vector. Dimension counts those basis vectors, not the number of entries used to write an object in a surrounding space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限维向量空间的**维数**，计算描述其中一个向量需要多少个独立的标量选择。[[基]]恰好提供这种选择：每个向量都对每个基向量有唯一的系数。维数数的是基向量的个数，而不是对象写在外部空间中时有几个数字。

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A plane with three written coordinates but two independent choices*

Consider the $xy$-plane $S=\{(x,y,0):x,y\in\mathbb R\}$ inside $\mathbb R^3$. The third entry is always zero, while $x$ and $y$ can be chosen freely. With $e_1=(1,0,0)$ and $e_2=(0,1,0)$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $\mathbb R^3$ 内的 $xy$ 平面 $S=\{(x,y,0):x,y\in\mathbb R\}$。第三个数恒为零，$x$、$y$ 可以自由选择。令 $e_1=(1,0,0)$、$e_2=(0,1,0)$，则

$$(x,y,0)=xe_1+ye_2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficient $x$ tells us how much of the first vector to use; $y$ does the same for the second. Every vector in the plane has exactly one such pair, so these two vectors are a basis and $\dim S=2$. The surrounding space $\mathbb R^3$ has dimension $3$, but the plane uses only two independent choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数 $x$ 决定使用多少份第一个向量，$y$ 决定第二个。平面中每个向量都恰好对应一对这样的系数，所以这两个向量是一组基，$\dim S=2$。外部空间 $\mathbb R^3$ 的维数为 $3$，平面却只有两个独立选择。

> [!example] The entries need not be individually fixed · 坐标都在变，也可能只有一个自由选择
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The line $L=\{(t,2t,3t):t\in\mathbb R\}$ also lives in $\mathbb R^3$. All three coordinates vary, but one number $t$ determines all of them. Its basis is the single vector $(1,2,3)$, so $\dim L=1$. Counting nonzero or changing entries would give the wrong answer.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 直线 $L=\{(t,2t,3t):t\in\mathbb R\}$ 也位于 $\mathbb R^3$。三个坐标都在变，但一个数 $t$ 就确定了全部坐标。它的基只有 $(1,2,3)$ 一个向量，所以 $\dim L=1$。数非零坐标或会变化的坐标，都会算错。

> [!abstract] Basis length defines dimension · 基的长度定义维数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A space is finite-dimensional when a finite list of vectors spans it. Such a space has a basis, and **any two bases have the same number of vectors**. If $B=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ is a basis of $V$, define $\dim_{\mathbb F}V=n$, where $\mathbb F$ is the scalar field. We usually omit the subscript when the field is understood.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个空间如果存在有限的张成组，就称为有限维空间。这样的空间有基，且**任意两组基所含向量数都相同**。若 $B=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ 是 $V$ 的基，就定义 $\dim_{\mathbb F}V=n$，其中 $\mathbb F$ 是标量域。标量域明确时，通常省略下标。

## 直觉
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Changing the basis does not change the count*

The plane above also has basis $u_1=(1,0,0),u_2=(1,1,0)$: any $(x,y,0)$ equals $(x-y)u_1+yu_2$ uniquely. The two coefficient lists $(x,y)$ and $(x-y,y)$ differ, but both contain two numbers. The theorem that all bases have equal length makes dimension a property of the space rather than of the basis we happen to choose.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的平面还有另一组基 $u_1=(1,0,0),u_2=(1,1,0)$，因为任意 $(x,y,0)$ 唯一地等于 $(x-y)u_1+yu_2$。两套系数 $(x,y)$ 与 $(x-y,y)$ 不同，却都含两个数。“所有基长度相同”这一定理，使维数成为空间的属性，而不是临时选了哪组基的属性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same counting works for objects other than coordinate arrows. A diagonal real $2\times2$ matrix has the form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样的计数也适用于坐标箭头以外的对象。一个实数 $2\times2$ 对角矩阵可以写成

$$\begin{bmatrix}a&0\\0&b\end{bmatrix}=a\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}+b\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each whole matrix on the right is one basis vector. The diagonal-matrix space has dimension $2$, although each matrix is written with four entries. The space of **all** real $2\times2$ matrices has dimension $4$, because all four entries can be chosen independently. These are different spaces under the same entrywise addition and scaling.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

右边每个完整矩阵是一个基向量。对角矩阵空间的维数为 $2$，虽然每个矩阵写出来有四个元素；**所有**实数 $2\times2$ 矩阵组成的空间则是四维，因为四个元素可以各自独立选择。它们使用同样的逐元素加法与数乘，却是两个不同的空间。

## 零维边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Zero dimension and the empty basis*

The zero vector space $\{0\}$ contains exactly one vector. It needs no basis vectors: the empty list is [[线性无关|independent]], and its [[张成|span]] is $\{0\}$. Therefore $\dim\{0\}=0$. The list containing one zero vector is dependent and is not a basis; counting that one element would incorrectly give dimension one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零向量空间 $\{0\}$ 恰好包含一个向量，但不需要任何基向量：空组[[线性无关|无关]]，其[[张成]]又是 $\{0\}$，所以 $\dim\{0\}=0$。只含一个零向量的组是相关的，不是基；如果把这个成员数成一个基向量，就会错算成一维。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, a zero-dimensional space has the empty basis and thus can contain only zero. Do not confuse the zero vector space with a matrix's [[零空间|nullspace]] $N(A)=\{x:Ax=0\}$. A nullspace can contain a whole line or plane; its dimension, called [[零度|nullity]], is zero only when $N(A)=\{0\}$. Matrix [[矩阵秩|rank]] measures the dimension of the column space, while [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity]] connects the two counts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，零维空间只有空基，因此只能包含零。不要把零向量空间与矩阵的[[零空间]] $N(A)=\{x:Ax=0\}$ 混同：后者可能包含一整条直线或一个平面；其维数叫[[零度]]，仅当 $N(A)=\{0\}$ 时才为零。矩阵的[[矩阵秩|秩]]衡量列空间维数，[[秩零度定理]]则联系这两个计数。

> [!warning] State the scalar field · 说明标量域
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Over real scalars, $1,i$ form a basis of $\mathbb C$, so $\dim_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C=2$. Over complex scalars, $1$ alone is a basis, so $\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C=1$. The field determines what counts as one coefficient. Also, the finite number used here does not cover spaces with no finite spanning list; such spaces are called infinite-dimensional.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 允许实标量时，$1,i$ 是 $\mathbb C$ 的基，所以 $\dim_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C=2$；允许复标量时，单个 $1$ 就是基，所以 $\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C=1$。标量域决定“一份系数”允许装什么数。此外，这里的有限计数不涵盖没有有限张成组的空间；后者称为无限维空间。
