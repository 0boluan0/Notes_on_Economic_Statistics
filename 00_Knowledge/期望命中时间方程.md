# Expected hitting times by first-step analysis
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*期望命中时间方程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
You may be certain of eventually reaching a target, yet the average waiting time can be large or even infinite. To calculate the average, charge one unit for each step taken **before the first hit**. The first-step equation says: pay for the next step, then add the average remaining wait from the state reached.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

目标可能最终一定会到达，但平均等待时间仍可能很长，甚至无穷。计算这个平均数时，为**第一次命中以前**走的每一步记一单位成本。第一步方程的意思是：先付下一步的时间成本，再加上从所到状态出发的平均剩余等待。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a finite- or countable-state [[离散时间Markov链|Markov chain]] with a [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|time-homogeneous rule]], in the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]] $p_{ij}=\Pr(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_n=i)$. For a fixed target set $A$, let $\tau_A=\inf\{n\ge0:X_n\in A\}$. It is zero when we start in $A$, and infinity when the path never enters $A$. Write
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用有限或可数状态的[[离散时间Markov链]]，假设转移规则[[DTMC时间齐次转移核|时间齐次]]，采用[[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]] $p_{ij}=\Pr(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_n=i)$。固定目标集合 $A$，令 $\tau_A=\inf\{n\ge0:X_n\in A\}$。从 $A$ 内出发时它为零，从不进入 $A$ 时为无穷。记

$$
m_i=E_i[\tau_A]\in[0,\infty].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$E_i$ is [[期望|expectation]] with starting state fixed at $i$. Because the waiting time is nonnegative, its expectation is defined even when it is infinite; no subtraction of infinities is involved. This is an **unconditional** mean. It includes paths that never hit, rather than averaging only over successful paths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$E_i$ 是固定起点为 $i$ 时的[[期望]]。等待时间非负，所以期望即使为无穷仍有定义，不涉及无穷减无穷。这是**无条件**平均：它也计入永远没有命中的路径，不是只对成功路径求平均。

> [!important] Zero on the target, one step outside · 目标上为零，目标外加一步
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The equations hold in the extended nonnegative numbers:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 下面的等式在非负扩展数中成立：
>
> $$
> m_i=0\quad(i\in A),\qquad m_i=1+\sum_{j:p_{ij}>0}p_{ij}m_j\quad(i\notin A).
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> From outside $A$, one step must be spent even if that step reaches $A$. Conditional on a possible next state $j$, the [[Markov性|Markov property]] restarts the remaining problem from $j$. For finite means, this weighting is the [[全期望公式|law of total expectation]]; the capped-time argument below extends the calculation to nonnegative infinite means. Summing only over positive transition probabilities avoids writing $0\cdot\infty$. An infinite answer must not be rearranged by subtracting infinities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $A$ 外出发，下一步即使直接进入 $A$，也已经用了一步。给定一个可能的下一状态 $j$，[[Markov性]]使剩余问题从 $j$ 重新开始。均值有限时，这种加权就是[[全期望公式]]；下方的封顶时间论证把计算推广到非负但可能无穷的均值。仅对正转移概率求和，可以避免写出 $0\cdot\infty$；含无穷的等式不能通过无穷相减来移项。

> [!example] Two stages before completion · 完成之前的两个阶段
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $a$ be completion. From $s$, the probabilities of $s,c,a$ are $1/2,1/4,1/4$. From $c$, stay at $c$ or finish at $a$, each with probability $1/2$. The target is $A=\{a\}$. Starting outside $A$, the probability of completing on the next step is always at least $1/4$, so $\Pr_i(\tau_A>N)\le(3/4)^N$ and the means are finite. Now solve
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $a$ 表示完成。从 $s$ 到 $s,c,a$ 的概率依次是 $1/2,1/4,1/4$；从 $c$ 留在 $c$ 或完成到 $a$ 的概率各为 $1/2$。目标为 $A=\{a\}$。在目标外，无论当前在哪个状态，下一步完成的概率至少为 $1/4$，所以 $\Pr_i(\tau_A>N)\le(3/4)^N$，均值有限。现在解
>
> $$
> m_a=0,\qquad m_c=1+\tfrac12m_c,\qquad m_s=1+\tfrac12m_s+\tfrac14m_c.\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad m_c=2,\ m_s=3.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Solving the $c$ equation first gives two steps, then substitution into the $s$ equation gives three steps. Starting at $a$ costs zero, not one. Changing transitions after completion would not change these answers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 先解 $c$ 的方程，得到两步；再代入 $s$ 的方程，得到三步。从 $a$ 出发成本是零，不是一。改变完成以后的转移，不会改变这些答案。

## Build the mean from capped waiting times
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从有上限的等待时间构造均值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If finiteness is not yet known, cap the waiting time at an integer $N\ge0$: $m_i^{(N)}=E_i[\min(\tau_A,N)]$. Even on a path that never hits, this capped cost is just $N$. Start with $m_i^{(0)}=0$ at every state, and iterate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

尚不清楚均值是否有限时，先用整数 $N\ge0$ 封顶等待时间：$m_i^{(N)}=E_i[\min(\tau_A,N)]$。即使某条路径永不命中，封顶成本也只是 $N$。从每个状态的 $m_i^{(0)}=0$ 开始，迭代

$$
m_i^{(N+1)}=\begin{cases}0,&i\in A,\\1+\sum_jp_{ij}m_j^{(N)},&i\notin A.\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The capped variables increase to $\tau_A$. The [[单调收敛定理|monotone convergence theorem]] therefore gives $m_i^{(N)}\uparrow m_i$, allowing infinity. For any other nonnegative extended-valued solution $y$ of the original equations, $y\ge m^{(0)}$ and the iteration preserves the inequality. Thus $y\ge m^{(N)}$ for all $N$, and $y\ge m$. The true mean is the **pointwise minimal nonnegative solution**, not an arbitrary algebraic solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些封顶变量递增到 $\tau_A$，所以由[[单调收敛定理]]，$m_i^{(N)}\uparrow m_i$，极限可以为无穷。对原方程任意另一个非负扩展值解 $y$，起初有 $y\ge m^{(0)}$，且每次迭代都保持这个不等式。因此对所有 $N$ 有 $y\ge m^{(N)}$，进而 $y\ge m$。真实均值是**逐点最小非负解**，不是随便一个代数解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same truncation explains a useful tail formula. The [[事件指示变量|indicator]] $\mathbf1_{\{\tau_A>n\}}$ records whether the $(n+1)$st waiting step is still needed. Counting those steps and then using monotone convergence gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样的截断还能说明一个有用的尾概率公式。[[事件指示变量|指示变量]] $\mathbf1_{\{\tau_A>n\}}$ 记录是否还需要等待第 $n+1$ 步。把这些步数加起来，再用单调收敛，得到

$$
\min(\tau_A,N)=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\mathbf1_{\{\tau_A>n\}},\qquad E_i\tau_A=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\Pr_i(\tau_A>n).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This formula justifies the geometric-tail bound in the example. It also shows why knowing only that the tail probabilities tend to zero is not enough: their sum may diverge.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这解释了例子中几何尾界为何能保证均值有限，也说明只知道尾概率趋于零还不够：它们的和仍可能发散。

## What an infinite answer means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*无穷答案意味着什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\Pr_i(\tau_A=\infty)>0$, then $E_i\tau_A=\infty$. For example, if $s$ moves in one step to target $a$ or to an absorbing failure state $d$, each with probability $1/2$, then hitting occurs with probability $1/2$ and the unconditional mean is infinite. The successful paths all take one step, but their conditional average answers a different question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\Pr_i(\tau_A=\infty)>0$，则 $E_i\tau_A=\infty$。例如，从 $s$ 一步以各 $1/2$ 的概率到目标 $a$ 或到吸收故障状态 $d$，命中概率是一半，无条件平均等待则是无穷。成功路径都只用一步，但只对它们取条件平均，回答的是另一个问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, hitting with probability one still need not give a finite mean on an infinite state space; [[必达不推有限期望|almost-sure hitting versus finite mean]] gives a complete random-walk example. On a **finite time-homogeneous chain**, however, almost-sure hitting from a given state does imply a finite mean from that state. Among the finitely many states reachable before a hit, each has a positive-probability finite path to $A$. Choose one such path for each state. The largest path length $L$ is finite and the smallest path probability $\delta$ is positive, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，在无限状态空间上，命中概率为一也未必有有限均值；[[必达不推有限期望]]给出完整随机游走例子。但对**有限时间齐次链**，从某个给定状态几乎必然命中，确实推出从该状态出发的均值有限。在命中前可能到达的有限多个状态中，每个状态都有一条正概率的有限路径通向 $A$。各选一条路径后，最大长度 $L$ 有限，最小路径概率 $\delta$ 为正，于是

$$
\Pr_i(\tau_A>kL)\le(1-\delta)^k,\qquad E_i\tau_A\le L/\delta<\infty.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The case $i\in A$ is already handled by $m_i=0$. Matrix-inverse formulas such as [[期望吸收时间为N1|the fundamental-matrix formula for absorption time]] need their own finite transient-block assumptions. They are not a way to bypass these boundary and finiteness checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$i\in A$ 的情形已经由 $m_i=0$ 处理。[[期望吸收时间为N1|吸收时间的基本矩阵公式]]这类矩阵求逆方法，还需要相应的有限暂态块条件，不能用来跳过边界与有限性的检查。
