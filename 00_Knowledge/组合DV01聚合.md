# 同一利率因素与币种下组合DV01等于各头寸带符号DV01之和
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Portfolio DV01 is the sum of signed position DV01s for the same rate factor and valuation currency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

各头寸用同一币种、同一利率因素 $x$ 和同一基点尺度估值，数量在冲击中固定。若 $V(x)=\sum_jV_j(x)$ 且各项可导，则按本主题 [[DV01]] 的负导数约定，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use one valuation currency, one rate factor $x$ and one basis-point scale, with holdings fixed through the shock. If $V(x)=\sum_jV_j(x)$ and each term is differentiable, this topic's [[DV01|negative-derivative convention]] gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\boxed{\mathrm{DV01}_{V,x}=-10^{-4}V'(x)=\sum_j\left[-10^{-4}V_j'(x)\right]=\sum_j\mathrm{DV01}_{j,x}}.$$

这里直接加货币敏感度，不再乘一次市值权重。若已拿到“每手 DV01”，则先乘带正负号的手数；若拿到的是整个头寸的 DV01，则不再乘数量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Add the currency sensitivities directly, without multiplying by market-value weights again. Multiply per-contract DV01 by signed contract quantities first; do not multiply by quantity again if the number already covers the whole position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如某因素的两笔头寸 DV01 为 $+500$ 和 $-200$ 元／bp，合计为 $+300$ 元／bp。该因素升 $1$ bp，一阶损益约为 $-300$ 元。把两项先取绝对值会得到 $700$，那是总幅度而不是净风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Position DV01s of +500 and −200 currency units per bp sum to +300. A one-basis-point increase in that factor gives first-order P&L of approximately −300. Adding absolute values gives 700, a gross magnitude rather than net exposure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个恒等式不除以净值，因此净值恰为零的多空组合仍可有非零 DV01。相反，两个名字相同却冲击不同曲线的 DV01 不能据此相抵；跨币种折算也必须声明汇率是否固定，否则价值导数还会包含汇率变化项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity does not divide by net value, so a zero-net-value long-short portfolio can still have nonzero DV01. Two similarly named DV01s on different curves cannot be netted on this basis. Currency conversion must also state whether exchange rates are held fixed; otherwise their changes contribute to the value derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
