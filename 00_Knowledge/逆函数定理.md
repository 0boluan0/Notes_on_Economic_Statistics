# The inverse function theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*逆函数定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inverse recovers an input from its output. For example, the map $F(x,y)=(x+y^2,y)$ mixes the first input with the square of the second. Given output $(u,v)$, recover $y=v$ and then $x=u-v^2$. The inverse is $G(u,v)=(u-v^2,v)$. More complicated maps may have no convenient inverse formula. The inverse function theorem can still guarantee that nearby outputs determine unique nearby inputs, with a differentiable recovery map.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逆映射从输出找回输入。例如，$F(x,y)=(x+y^2,y)$ 把第一个输入与第二个输入的平方混合起来。给定输出 $(u,v)$，先找回 $y=v$，再得 $x=u-v^2$，所以逆映射是 $G(u,v)=(u-v^2,v)$。更复杂的映射可能没有方便的反解公式；逆函数定理仍能保证：附近输出对应唯一的附近输入，而且这个恢复输入的映射可微。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The relevant local model is the [[全微分|total derivative]]: one linear map approximating all small input displacements. Its matrix is the [[Jacobian矩阵|Jacobian]], with output components in rows and input variables in columns. When there are $n$ inputs and $n$ outputs, this matrix is square. An [[逆矩阵|inverse matrix]] can undo its linear change exactly. The theorem explains when that reversible linear model also guarantees a reversible nonlinear map nearby.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里使用的局部模型是[[全微分]]：用同一个线性映射近似所有小输入位移。它的矩阵是[[Jacobian矩阵]]，行对应输出分量、列对应输入变量。当输入、输出都有 $n$ 个分量时，矩阵为方阵；[[逆矩阵]]可以精确撤销其线性变化。定理说明，这种可逆的线性模型在什么条件下也能保证附近的非线性映射可逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the theorem guarantees
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定理具体保证什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $U$ be an [[开集|open subset]] of $\mathbb R^n$, where $n\ge1$ is an integer, and let $F:U\to\mathbb R^n$. Take $a\in U$ and write $b=F(a)$. Assume $F$ is $C^k$ on $U$, for an integer $k\ge1$: its component partial derivatives through order $k$ exist and are continuous. In particular, $C^1$ means continuous first partial derivatives, which ensure a total derivative. Assume $DF(a)$ is invertible; equivalently, the [[可逆性与非零行列式|square Jacobian has nonzero determinant]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $U$ 是 $\mathbb R^n$ 的[[开集]]，维数 $n\ge1$ 有限，$F:U\to\mathbb R^n$。取 $a\in U$，记 $b=F(a)$。假设 $F$ 在 $U$ 上为 $C^k$，其中 $k\ge1$ 为整数：各分量直到 $k$ 阶的偏导都存在且连续。特别地，$C^1$ 表示一阶偏导连续，因而存在全微分。再假设 $DF(a)$ 可逆，等价地，[[可逆性与非零行列式|方阵 Jacobian 的行列式非零]]。

> [!important] Unique nearby inputs, with the same smoothness · 唯一附近输入与相同光滑阶数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> There are open neighborhoods $U_0\subseteq U$ of $a$ and $V_0$ of $b$ such that $F$ maps $U_0$ bijectively onto $V_0$. Thus every $y\in V_0$ has exactly one preimage in $U_0$. The inverse $G:V_0\to U_0$ is $C^k$, with $G(b)=a$. A bijection whose map and inverse are both $C^k$ is called a **$C^k$ diffeomorphism**.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 存在 $a$ 的开邻域 $U_0\subseteq U$ 和 $b$ 的开邻域 $V_0$，使 $F$ 将 $U_0$ 双射到 $V_0$。也就是说，每个 $y\in V_0$ 在 $U_0$ 中恰有一个原像。逆映射 $G:V_0\to U_0$ 也为 $C^k$，且 $G(b)=a$。映射及其逆都为 $C^k$ 的双射，称为 **$C^k$ 微分同胚**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Local” refers to both neighborhoods. An output in $V_0$ could still have other preimages outside $U_0$. The notation $F|_{U_0}$ means restricting $F$ to this smaller input set. The two inverse identities are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“局部”同时涉及输入和输出的这两个邻域。$V_0$ 中的输出仍可能在 $U_0$ 以外有其他原像。记号 $F|_{U_0}$ 表示只在这个较小输入集合上考察 $F$。互逆的两个恒等式是

$$G(F(x))=x\quad(x\in U_0),\qquad F(G(y))=y\quad(y\in V_0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Differentiating the inverse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*逆映射的导数怎样计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem first supplies an inverse with the required differentiability. Apply the [[多元链式法则|multivariable chain rule]] to $F(G(y))=y$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先由定理保证逆映射存在并且可微，再对 $F(G(y))=y$ 使用[[多元链式法则]]：

$$DF(G(y))\,DG(y)=I_n,\qquad
\boxed{DG(y)=[DF(G(y))]^{-1}.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $I_n$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]], which leaves every vector unchanged. Evaluate the forward derivative at the recovered input $G(y)$, then take its matrix inverse. This is a matrix inverse, not reciprocals of its individual entries. In particular, $DG(b)=[DF(a)]^{-1}$. The identity determines the derivative of an existing differentiable inverse; by itself, it does not prove that the inverse exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $I_n$ 是使每个向量保持不变的[[单位矩阵]]。先在找回的输入 $G(y)$ 处计算正向导数，再求它的矩阵逆；不是把每个矩阵元素分别取倒数。特别地，$DG(b)=[DF(a)]^{-1}$。这个恒等式确定的是已有可微逆映射的导数，单独写它并没有证明逆映射存在。

> [!example] Check the triangular example · 核对开头的三角形式例子
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $F(x,y)=(x+y^2,y)$, the forward Jacobian is $\begin{pmatrix}1&2y\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$, with determinant $1$. At output $(u,v)$, the inverse Jacobian is $\begin{pmatrix}1&-2v\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$. Multiplying the two matrices at corresponding input/output points gives $I_2$, just as differentiating $G(u,v)=(u-v^2,v)$ directly does. Here the explicit formula proves global invertibility as well; the theorem alone guarantees a neighborhood.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $F(x,y)=(x+y^2,y)$，正向 Jacobian 为 $\begin{pmatrix}1&2y\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$，行列式是 $1$。在输出 $(u,v)$ 处，逆映射的 Jacobian 为 $\begin{pmatrix}1&-2v\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$。在相对应的输入、输出处将二者相乘得到 $I_2$，与直接对 $G(u,v)=(u-v^2,v)$ 求导一致。本例的显式反解还证明了全局可逆；定理本身只保证一个邻域。

## Why nearby behavior matters
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么还要控制附近的变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a single point, invertibility concerns only the linear model. Continuity of the derivative keeps nearby models sufficiently close to that invertible one. In the proof this prevents nearby inputs from folding onto one output, and supplies solutions for all outputs in a small open neighborhood. Thus the $C^1$ hypothesis does substantive work; checking only that a derivative exists and is invertible at one point is not the stated theorem. The full existence proof is in the sources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只看某一点时，可逆性还只属于线性模型。导数的连续性让附近的模型保持足够接近这个可逆模型；证明利用这一点阻止附近输入折叠成同一个输出，并为一个小开邻域中的所有输出提供解。因此 $C^1$ 假设承担实质作用；只检查某一点的导数存在且可逆，还没有满足本卡的定理。完整的存在性证明见来源。

> [!warning] Everywhere locally invertible can still fail globally · 处处局部可逆仍可能全局失败
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $\mathbb R^2\setminus\{(0,0)\}$, let $H(x,y)=(x^2-y^2,2xy)$. Its determinant is $4(x^2+y^2)>0$, so the theorem applies at every point. Yet $H(-x,-y)=H(x,y)$: two distinct inputs give the same output. Neighborhoods can separate them; the whole domain cannot.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\mathbb R^2\setminus\{(0,0)\}$ 上令 $H(x,y)=(x^2-y^2,2xy)$。它的行列式为 $4(x^2+y^2)>0$，所以每个点都适用本定理。但 $H(-x,-y)=H(x,y)$，两个不同输入给出同一输出。小邻域能把它们分开，整个定义域却不能。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $DF(a)$ is singular, [[Jacobian奇异不推局部非单射|local injectivity is still a separate question]]. A differentiable local inverse is impossible at the corresponding output, but a continuous inverse may exist. When instead only some variables must be recovered from equations, the relevant result is the [[隐函数定理|implicit function theorem]]. The one-input case connects to the [[反函数导数|ordinary inverse-derivative formula]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $DF(a)$ 奇异，[[Jacobian奇异不推局部非单射|局部单射性仍要另行判断]]。相应输出处不可能有可微局部逆，但可能有连续逆。若问题是从方程中只解出部分变量，则使用[[隐函数定理]]；只有一个输入时，可衔接[[反函数导数]]的普通公式。
