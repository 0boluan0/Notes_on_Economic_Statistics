# 股票价格指数是按既定样本权重和调整规则汇总股票价格变动的指标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A stock price index aggregates stock-price changes using specified constituents, weights and adjustment rules.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股票价格指数把一组股票的价格变化压缩成一条可比较的序列。它代表的是**规则选中的那组股票及其权重**，不一定代表所有公司、平均投资者或整个经济。设基期为 100，指数后来为 120，表示该指数口径下价格累计上涨 20%，而非每只股票都上涨 20%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stock price index condenses price changes into a comparable series. It represents the stocks and weights selected by its rules, not necessarily every company, the average investor or the economy. A rise from a base of 100 to 120 means a 20% increase under that index's convention, not a 20% rise in every constituent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先核对样本，再问“谁的涨跌影响更大”：答案分别由[[价格加权指数]]、[[市值加权指数]]或[[等权指数]]等规则决定。指数本身是计算结果；[[指数基金]]才是试图追踪它的投资产品，实际收益还受费用和跟踪偏离影响。股息是否计入，另看[[价格指数与全收益指数]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After checking the constituents, ask whose price move matters most: [[价格加权指数|price weighting]], [[市值加权指数|capitalization weighting]] and [[等权指数|equal weighting]] provide different answers. An index is a calculated measure; an [[指数基金|index fund]] is an investment product attempting to track it. Dividend treatment is a separate issue, explained in [[价格指数与全收益指数|price and total-return indices]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
