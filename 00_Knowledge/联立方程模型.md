# 联立方程模型由一组相互约束并共同决定多个内生变量的结构方程组成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A simultaneous-equations model is a system of structural equations that jointly constrain and determine several endogenous variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 单方程把右侧变量视为给定后解释一个结果；联立方程模型则把若干结果放在同一个系统里共同求解。每条结构方程描述一类行为、技术或制度关系，而某个内生变量可以同时出现在另一条方程的右侧。观察到的是整套结构与外生冲击共同决定的均衡结果，不是把其中一条关系单独运行后的轨迹。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A single equation explains one outcome conditional on its regressors. A simultaneous-equations model instead solves several endogenous outcomes jointly. Each structural equation represents a behavioral, technological, or institutional relation, and an endogenous outcome from one equation may appear on the right-hand side of another. The observed data are equilibrium outcomes of the whole system.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 供给与需求的最小系统

考虑
$$
Q^d=\alpha P+Z_d'\gamma+u_d,
\qquad
Q^s=\beta P+Z_s'\delta+u_s,
\qquad
Q^d=Q^s=Q.
$$
$P$ 与 $Q$ 由需求、供给和市场出清共同决定。需求方程里的 $\alpha$ 描述沿需求曲线改变价格时数量怎样变化；但样本中的均衡点同时受到供给移动影响，所以直接把 $Q$ 对 $P$ 做 OLS，通常不会沿着需求曲线识别 $\alpha$。这条失败机制由[[同时性内生]]单独解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price and quantity are jointly determined by demand, supply, and market clearing. The demand coefficient $\alpha$ describes movement along a demand curve, whereas observed equilibrium points also move when supply shifts. An OLS regression of equilibrium quantity on equilibrium price therefore need not trace the demand curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 结构式与约化式回答不同问题

结构式保留系统内部“谁约束谁”的参数与冲击。把系统联立求解后，可以把每个内生变量写成全部外生变量与结构冲击的函数；这称为**约化式**。约化式适合描述外生移动最终怎样改变均衡结果，却不会仅凭代数自动告诉我们某个结构参数。要从约化式恢复结构式，仍需排除限制、秩条件或其他识别假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Structural equations retain the within-system behavioral parameters and shocks. Solving the system expresses each endogenous variable as a function of all exogenous shifters and structural disturbances; this is the reduced form. It describes how exogenous changes move equilibrium outcomes, but recovering structural parameters still needs exclusion, rank, or other identifying restrictions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，只移动供给而不直接进入需求误差的 $Z_s$，会使均衡点沿需求曲线移动，因而可能成为需求方程中价格的[[工具变量]]；只移动需求的 $Z_d$ 则可能帮助识别供给斜率。工具是否有效取决于目标方程，不能给 $Z_s$ 或 $Z_d$ 贴一个跨方程不变的标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A shifter $Z_s$ that moves supply without entering the demand error can move equilibrium points along demand and may instrument for price in the demand equation. A demand shifter can play the corresponding role for supply. Validity is tied to the target equation, not to a permanent label on the shifter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用边界

- “有多条方程”不等于联立决定。若多个结果方程只共享相关误差、却没有内生结果互相进入右侧，那更接近 seemingly unrelated regressions，而不是这里的反馈系统。
- 单方程也可能因遗漏变量、选择或测量误差而出现[[内生性]]；联立方程只是内生性的一种生成机制。
- 把系统写成矩阵并不自动赋予结构或因果含义。结构解释来自方程、时序、排除限制和冲击假设，而不是方程数量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Several equations do not by themselves imply simultaneity: equations may merely have correlated disturbances without endogenous outcomes feeding back into one another. Conversely, a single equation can be endogenous through omitted variables, selection, or measurement error. Structural meaning comes from the maintained behavioral, timing, exclusion, and shock assumptions—not from writing a system in matrix notation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 观察到价格与成交量正相关，为什么不能直接把这条回归线叫作供给曲线？
>
> **答案：** 因为均衡点同时由供给与需求移动决定；同一条正相关轨迹可能混合两条曲线的变化。必须找到只移动另一侧、且不直接进入目标方程误差的外生变化，才可能识别目标结构斜率。
