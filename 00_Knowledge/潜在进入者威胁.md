# 潜在进入者威胁是可能进入的竞争者对行业现有盈利条件施加的压力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The threat of new entrants is pressure on incumbent profitability from competitors that may enter the industry.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

潜在进入者可能带来新供给并争夺客户，使现有企业难以维持高价格，或必须增加投入来守住业务。压力来自可行的进入可能性，不要求已经发生实际进入；[[进入壁垒]]与进入者的能力会影响威胁大小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Potential entrants can add supply and compete for customers, limiting prices or requiring incumbents to spend more to defend their business. A credible possibility of entry can exert pressure before entry occurs. [[进入壁垒|Entry barriers]] and entrants' capabilities influence the threat.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，若经营者一涨价就可能吸引邻近地区供应商以较低运输成本扩张，现有价格就受到约束，即使对方暂未开设本地网点。反过来，多年无人进入也可能是需求差、利润低，不能只凭“没有新企业”断定壁垒很高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An incumbent's price increase may invite a nearby supplier to expand at low transport cost, constraining prices even without a local outlet today. Conversely, prolonged absence of entry may reflect weak demand or low profits, not necessarily high barriers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
