# 开放式基金允许按合同约定申购或赎回份额且份额总额不固定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An open-end fund permits subscriptions or redemptions under its contractual terms, so its total units are not fixed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开放式基金以份额可依约新增或注销的安排运作。申购是向基金投入对价取得新份额，赎回是向基金交回份额取得约定对价；两者不同于投资者之间转让已有份额。这里“开放”不意味着每一时刻、任何数量、无需确认就能按原价退出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An open-end fund operates with units that can be created or cancelled under its terms. Subscription contributes consideration to the fund for new units; redemption returns units for the specified consideration. Neither is the same as transferring existing units between investors. Open-endedness does not mean immediate, unlimited, unconditional exit at the original price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在中国《证券投资基金法》的定义中，份额总额不固定，申赎遵守合同约定的时间和场所。普通非交易所交易的开放式共同基金通常按适用日[[基金份额净值]]加减费用处理，而不是按提交订单时已经知道的任意盘中报价成交；初始封闭期、暂停或延期安排另受法律和合同约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the Chinese Fund Law, total units are variable and dealing follows contractual times and locations. Ordinary non-exchange-traded open-end mutual funds generally use the applicable day's [[基金份额净值|per-unit NAV]], adjusted for fees, rather than an arbitrary intraday quote known when the order is placed. Initial lockups, suspensions, or deferrals are separately governed by law and the contract.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如原有 100 万份，基金确认赎回并注销 10 万份，且没有其他份额变化，则余额为 90 万份。若只是甲把 10 万份卖给乙，基金总份额不会因此变化。能否及时兑现赎回，还取决于资产端的[[流动性转换|流动性安排]]，而非净值核算本身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Starting from 1 million units, confirmed redemption and cancellation of 0.1 million units leaves 0.9 million if nothing else changes. A transfer of 0.1 million units from one investor to another does not itself change outstanding units. Timely payment also depends on [[流动性转换|asset-side liquidity arrangements]], not NAV arithmetic alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“开放式／封闭式”描述资本及赎回安排，“上市交易／不上市”描述交易渠道。两轴不能混为互斥类别：[[上市开放式基金]]同时具有上市交易和申赎渠道；美国许多[[ETF]]在法律上也是开放式投资公司，但个人投资者通常在交易所买卖，由授权参与者办理大额直接申赎。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Open-end versus closed-end concerns capital and redemption arrangements; listed versus unlisted concerns trading channels. The axes are not mutually exclusive categories. A [[上市开放式基金|LOF]] has both exchange trading and subscription/redemption channels. Many U.S. [[ETF|ETFs]] are legally open-end investment companies, while retail investors typically trade on exchanges and authorized participants perform large direct creations or redemptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
