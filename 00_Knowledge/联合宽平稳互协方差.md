# 联合宽平稳还要求互协方差只依赖滞后
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Joint wide-sense stationarity additionally requires cross-covariance to depend only on lag*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 两个实值过程 $X_t,Y_t$ 联合宽平稳，要求二者各自宽平稳，并且
> $$
> \gamma_{XY}(h)=\operatorname{Cov}(X_{t+h},Y_t)
> $$
> 只依赖 $h$，不依赖日历时间 $t$。分别宽平稳不能自动推出联合宽平稳，因为两过程之间的关系仍可能随时间改变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Two processes are jointly WSS when each is WSS and their cross-covariance $\gamma_{XY}(h)=\operatorname{Cov}(X_{t+h},Y_t)$ depends only on lag. Separate WSS does not guarantee joint WSS because their cross-relationship may still vary with calendar time.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实值过程满足 $\gamma_{XY}(-h)=\gamma_{YX}(h)$，但单个互协方差一般不是偶函数；必须在反转滞后时交换下标。对复值过程，若定义
$$
\gamma_{XY}(h)=E[(X_{t+h}-\mu_X)\overline{(Y_t-\mu_Y)}],
$$
则反转关系是 $\gamma_{XY}(-h)=\overline{\gamma_{YX}(h)}$。Cauchy–Schwarz 还给出
$$
|\gamma_{XY}(h)|^2\le\gamma_X(0)\gamma_Y(0).
$$
若 $W_t=X_t+Y_t$，只有联合宽平稳时才能直接把四个 lag-only covariance 项合成 $W$ 的自协方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real processes, $\gamma_{XY}(-h)=\gamma_{YX}(h)$; a single cross-covariance need not be even. With the conjugated convention for complex processes, the reversal is $\gamma_{XY}(-h)=\overline{\gamma_{YX}(h)}$. Cauchy–Schwarz gives $|\gamma_{XY}(h)|^2\le\gamma_X(0)\gamma_Y(0)$. Joint WSS is what permits the four covariance terms of $X+Y$ to remain lag-only.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $X$ 和 $Y$ 各自宽平稳，是否已经足以让 $X+Y$ 宽平稳？
>
> **答案：** 不足。还要检查两者的互协方差只依赖滞后；否则 $X+Y$ 的交叉项仍可以随日历时间变化。
