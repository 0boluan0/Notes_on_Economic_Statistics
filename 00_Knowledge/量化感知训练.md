# 量化感知训练在训练或微调中模拟目标量化数值以调整模型参数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Quantization-aware training simulates target quantization numerics during training or fine-tuning to adjust model parameters*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

量化感知训练（quantization-aware training，QAT）在训练或微调的前向计算中模拟目标量化的舍入与截断，让损失反映这些数值变化，再据此调整模型参数。常见实现使用 fake quantization：数值先按目标规则量化再反量化，但仍用浮点张量承载，训练后才转换成实际部署表示。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quantization-aware training (QAT) simulates the target quantizer's rounding and clipping in training or fine-tuning forward passes. The loss therefore reflects these numerical changes and guides parameter updates. A common implementation uses fake quantization: values are quantized and dequantized under the target rules while remaining in floating-point tensors, then converted to the deployment representation after training.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 前向离散，更新可以保持连续

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Discrete forward values with continuous parameter updates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[数值量化|固定的均匀量化器]]，前向可以用 $\widehat w=s(\operatorname{clip}(\operatorname{round}(w/s)+z,q_{\min},q_{\max})-z)$ 代替原权重 $w$，其中 $[q_{\min},q_{\max}]$ 是合法整数码范围。浮点潜在权重 $w$ 仍被保留，以累积小于一个量化间距的更新。因此 QAT 不要求训练过程中的全部存储和算术都是整数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a [[数值量化|fixed uniform quantizer]], the forward pass can replace weight $w$ with $\widehat w=s(\operatorname{clip}(\operatorname{round}(w/s)+z,q_{\min},q_{\max})-z)$, where $[q_{\min},q_{\max}]$ is the valid integer-code range. A latent floating-point weight $w$ is retained to accumulate updates smaller than one quantization step. QAT therefore does not require all training storage and arithmetic to be integer-valued.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最近舍入函数的真实导数几乎处处为零，在跳点不可导，不能直接提供这里需要的学习信号。常见 straight-through estimator（STE）在未截断区间内近似采用 $\partial\widehat w/\partial w\approx1$。这是用于反向传播的替代规则，不是阶梯函数的真实导数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nearest rounding has zero derivative almost everywhere and is nondifferentiable at jumps, so its true derivative cannot supply the desired learning signal. A common straight-through estimator (STE) approximates $\partial\widehat w/\partial w\approx1$ inside the unclipped range. This is a surrogate backward rule, not the staircase function's true derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 算一次更新

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Computing one update*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令单参数模型的前向输出为 $y=\widehat w$，目标为 $1$，损失 $\ell=(y-1)^2$。固定量化间距 $s=0.5$、$z=0$、合法码 $0,1,2,3$，实数范围为 $[0,1.5]$。当前潜在权重为 $w=0.24$，所以前向得到 $\widehat w=0$、$\ell=1$。采用上述 STE 和学习率 $\eta=0.01$：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let a one-parameter model output $y=\widehat w$, with target one and loss $\ell=(y-1)^2$. Fix spacing $s=0.5$, zero-point $z=0$, and valid codes $0,1,2,3$, representing $[0,1.5]$. At latent weight $w=0.24$, the forward value is $\widehat w=0$, giving $\ell=1$. Using the STE above and learning rate $\eta=0.01$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\widetilde{\frac{\partial\ell}{\partial w}}
=2(\widehat w-1)\cdot1=-2,
\qquad
w_{\mathrm{new}}=0.24-0.01(-2)=0.26.
$$

下一次前向把 $0.26$ 舍入到等级 $0.5$，损失变成 $(0.5-1)^2=0.25$。潜在权重只移动 $0.02$，前向却跨过了等级边界。这个小例解释 QAT 的数值机制，不证明所有训练都会收敛或恢复原模型质量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The next forward pass rounds $0.26$ to level $0.5$, giving loss $(0.5-1)^2=0.25$. The latent weight moved only $0.02$ but crossed a level boundary in the forward computation. This example explains the mechanism without proving convergence or recovery of original model quality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与[[训练后量化|PTQ]]相比，QAT 增加了训练或微调成本；最终导出的量化模型仍要独立评测。fake quantization 阶段没有真正压缩成目标存储类型，不能把它的训练显存或速度直接视为部署收益。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compared with [[训练后量化|PTQ]], QAT adds training or fine-tuning cost, and the exported quantized model still requires evaluation. Fake quantization has not yet compressed values into the target storage type, so its training memory use or speed is not a deployment result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Jacob et al. (2018), *Quantization and Training of Neural Networks*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.05877)，§3：支持前向模拟量化与保留浮点权重进行小步更新。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Section 3 supports simulated quantization in the forward pass and floating-point weights that permit small updates.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [PyTorch TorchAO, *Quantization-Aware Training*](https://docs.pytorch.org/ao/stable/workflows/qat.html)，开头定义及 prepare/convert 流程：支持 fake quantization 的浮点载体与训练后的真实量化转换，不依赖某个 API 版本的调用语法。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The opening definition and prepare/convert workflow distinguish floating-point fake quantization from conversion to actual quantization after training. This claim does not depend on a particular API version's syntax.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Nagel et al. (2021), *A White Paper on Neural Network Quantization*](https://arxiv.org/html/2106.08295v1)，§4.1 式(36)–(37)：支持未截断区间内 STE 的替代梯度。单步训练例按明确给定的损失、步长和替代梯度独立计算。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Section 4.1, equations (36)–(37), gives the STE surrogate gradient inside the unclipped interval. The single update is independently calculated using the stated loss, learning rate, and surrogate gradient.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
