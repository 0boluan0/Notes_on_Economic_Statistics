# 债务存量变化须由赤字、金融资产交易和估值等调节共同解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changes in the debt stock require reconciliation with deficits, financial-asset transactions, valuation and other adjustments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

期末债务减期初债务，通常不等于当期[[财政赤字]]。政府可能一边借钱一边增加现金，外币债务可能因本币贬值升值，统计范围也可能改变。先保持单位范围、币种、估值和记账基础可比，再解释差额，不能将剩余差额直接命名为“隐性债务”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Closing minus opening debt need not equal the [[财政赤字|fiscal deficit]]. Government may borrow to build cash, currency depreciation may revalue foreign-currency debt, and coverage may change. Reconcile definitions before interpreting a residual; it is not automatically hidden debt.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把赤字取正数$DEF_t$，债务存量为$B_t$，定义存量流量调节$SFA_t$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the deficit positive as $DEF_t$ and debt stock $B_t$, define the stock-flow adjustment:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
B_t-B_{t-1}=DEF_t+SFA_t.
$$

在GFS账户中，负债交易净增加等于赤字加金融资产交易净增加。若全部相关负债均为债务工具，则$SFA$由金融资产净交易、债务重估和其他数量变化组成；存在非债务负债交易时，还要扣除那部分，不能把总负债变化都当债务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In GFS, net liability transactions equal the deficit plus net financial-asset acquisitions. If all relevant liabilities are debt instruments, the adjustment comprises financial-asset transactions, debt revaluation and other volume changes. Subtract non-debt liability transactions where present.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例：期初债务600、赤字10、现金资产净增加5、外币债务重估增加12，没有其他项目，则期末债务为627，$SFA=17$。其中15是净债务交易，12不是本期新增借款。若某担保兑现已列入费用和赤字，再把同额加进$SFA$会重复计算；应看它实际进入了哪张账。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Opening debt 600, deficit 10, a cash-asset increase of 5 and currency revaluation of 12 give closing debt 627 and $SFA=17$. Net debt transactions are 15; revaluation is not borrowing. A called guarantee already recorded in expense and the deficit must not be added again as an adjustment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
