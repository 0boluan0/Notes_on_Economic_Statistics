# 充分分散可以压低可抵消的个别冲击而不会自动消除共同因子暴露
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Broad diversification can reduce cancellable individual shocks without automatically eliminating common-factor exposure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

持有更多资产能否降低风险，要看**权重有没有真正分散，以及资产是否一起波动**。把相似资产的名字增加到几百个，并不会自动消除共同冲击。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Risk reduction depends on genuinely dispersed weights and on joint movements, not the number of asset names. Hundreds of similar positions can retain the same common shock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在单因子模型 $R_i=\alpha_i+\beta_iF+\varepsilon_i$ 中，设固定因子的方差满足 $0<\operatorname{Var}(F)<\infty$、残差有有限方差，并假定残差与因子正交、不同资产的残差互不相关，便有
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a single-factor model with fixed positive finite factor variance and finite residual variances, assume residuals are orthogonal to the factor and mutually uncorrelated across assets. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\operatorname{Var}(R_p)=\Bigl(\sum_iw_i\beta_i\Bigr)^2\operatorname{Var}(F)+\sum_iw_i^2\operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon_i).$$

如果残差方差一致有界，且随着资产数增加 $\sum_iw_i^2\to0$，第二项趋于零。第一项则取决于共同因子暴露是否也趋于零。例如各 $\beta_i=1$、等权、残差方差相同为 $s^2$ 时，总方差为 $\operatorname{Var}(F)+s^2/n$，而非 $s^2/n$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Uniformly bounded residual variances and $\sum_iw_i^2\to0$ make the residual term vanish. The factor term disappears only if the aggregate factor exposure does. Equal weights and unit betas give $\operatorname{Var}(F)+s^2/n$, not merely $s^2/n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使不采用因子模型，等权组合也恒有 $\operatorname{Var}(R_p)=\overline{\sigma_i^2}/n+(n-1)\overline{\sigma_{ij}}/n$，其中第二个平均遍历所有 $i\ne j$ 的协方差。这只是一种代数分组：不能把原始协方差矩阵的对角项统称“非系统风险”，把所有非对角项统称“系统风险”。共同因子也进入每项资产自己的方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without a factor model, equal-weight variance still equals average individual variance divided by $n$, plus $(n-1)/n$ times average off-diagonal covariance. This algebraic grouping is not a systematic/idiosyncratic decomposition: common factors also enter diagonal variances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述结论回答“哪些风险能被分散”。市场是否为剩余暴露支付均衡溢价，是另一个需要定价假设的问题，见[[特有风险定价边界]]；不能单凭方差分解推出 CAPM。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These statements concern diversification, not equilibrium risk premia. [[特有风险定价边界|CAPM pricing of idiosyncratic risk]] needs separate pricing assumptions; variance decomposition alone does not establish CAPM.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
