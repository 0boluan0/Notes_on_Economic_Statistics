# Proxy SVAR 的外部工具必须关联目标冲击并排除其他冲击
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Proxy SVAR instrument must be relevant for the target shock and exclude the other shocks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 适用条件
> 外部工具 $z_t$ 至少要与目标结构冲击相关，并与同期其他结构冲击正交。样本相关性不能自动验证这两条不可观测的结构条件；动态响应的传播还会受可恢复性、控制集与领先—滞后排除条件影响。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Conditions for use
> An external instrument $z_t$ must at least be correlated with the target structural shock and orthogonal to the other contemporaneous shocks. Sample correlations cannot verify these unobserved structural conditions on their own, and dynamic-response identification also depends on recoverability, controls, and the required lead-lag exclusions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设结构冲击为 $\varepsilon_t=(\varepsilon_{1t},\ldots,\varepsilon_{Kt})'$，目标冲击为 $\varepsilon_{1t}$。核心同期矩条件通常写为
$$
E(z_t\varepsilon_{1t})\ne0
\quad\text{（相关性／relevance）},
$$
以及
$$
E(z_t\varepsilon_{jt})=0,\qquad j=2,\ldots,K
\quad\text{（外生性／exogeneity）}.
$$
第一条使工具携带目标冲击信号；第二条排除工具同时代理其他冲击。叙事日期、高频意外或外部预测误差都只是工具的来源，不会因名称而自动满足矩条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For target shock $\varepsilon_{1t}$, the core contemporaneous moments are $E(z_t\varepsilon_{1t})\neq0$ for relevance and $E(z_t\varepsilon_{jt})=0$ for every $j\neq1$ for exogeneity. Narrative dates, high-frequency surprises, and external forecast errors are possible sources of instruments, not guarantees that these moment conditions hold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些同期条件还没有自动识别所有期限的动态响应。传统 Proxy SVAR 先从 $E(z_tu_t)$ 识别目标冲击在简约型创新中的当期方向，再借 VAR 动态向前传播。若目标冲击无法由当前和过去的简约型创新恢复，即不满足方法所需的 invertibility/recoverability，这一传播解释会失效。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The contemporaneous moments do not automatically identify responses at every horizon. A conventional Proxy SVAR first uses $E(z_tu_t)$ to recover the target shock's impact direction in the reduced-form innovations and then propagates it through the VAR. This interpretation fails when the target shock is not recoverable from current and past reduced-form innovations under the method's maintained assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

局部投影 IV（LP-IV）可以避免先拟合整个 VAR 传播递推，但并非因此不需要动态假设。它需要更明确的期限别排除条件：在所给控制下，工具不能与会影响目标期结果的其他领先或滞后冲击相关。增加滞后简约型创新等控制有时能改变所需排除，但会重新引入可恢复性条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
LP-IV avoids propagating a shock through a previously fitted full VAR, but it does not remove dynamic assumptions. It needs horizon-specific lead-lag exclusions conditional on the chosen controls. Adding lagged reduced-form innovations can alter those exclusions but may reintroduce recoverability requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

工具往往只识别冲击方向或相对响应。要报告“加息 25 个基点”之类的水平效应，还需要经济单位归一化。若 $E(z_t\varepsilon_{1t})$ 很小，弱识别会使常规渐近正态区间严重失真；因而相关性必须作弱工具诊断，外生性则需制度叙事、信息泄漏与共同新闻审计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An instrument often identifies only a direction or relative responses. Economic-unit effects require an additional normalization. If relevance is weak, conventional normal approximations can be badly distorted; relevance calls for weak-instrument diagnostics, while exogeneity requires institutional reasoning about timing, information leakage, and common news.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个高频意外与利率方程残差高度相关，是否已足以证明它能用于 Proxy SVAR 并识别全部期限的货币政策响应？
>
> **答案：** 不足。还要论证它关联的确是目标结构冲击，对其他冲击外生，并检查弱工具、尺度归一化和方法所需的动态可恢复或排除条件。

## 来源与核验

- [Stock & Watson (2018), *Identification and Estimation of Dynamic Causal Effects in Macroeconomics Using External Instruments*](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24216)：核对外部工具的有效性、可逆性／可恢复性与 VAR-IV、LP-IV 条件的差别。
- [Mertens & Ravn (2013), *The Dynamic Effects of Personal and Corporate Income Tax Changes in the United States*](https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.103.4.1212)：核对叙事工具的应用与经济排除论证。
- [Montiel Olea, Stock & Watson (2021), *Inference in Structural VARs with External Instruments*](https://www.princeton.edu/~mwatson/papers/JOE_Publication_SVARIV.pdf)：核对弱工具诊断及弱识别下的推断风险。
