<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Binary search
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*二分查找*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Binary search finds a value in an ordered sequence by repeatedly checking a middle position and ruling out the side where the value cannot occur. Looking for 17 in an ascending list does not require checking every smaller entry: a middle value of 13 tells us that all entries at or before it are too small. The useful information is the ordering, not merely the choice of a midpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二分查找在有序序列中寻找目标值：每轮查看中间位置，再排除目标不可能出现的一侧。在升序列表中找 17，并不需要逐个检查更小的元素；若中间值是 13，就知道它及它前面的值都太小。起作用的是顺序提供的信息，并非“选了一个中点”这个动作本身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unlike [[枚举验证|exhaustive enumeration]], which tests candidates individually, this method proves that many untested candidates can be discarded together. An [[序列共同接口|index]] names a position, starting at zero in a Python list. We will search positions in a half-open interval $[lo,hi)$: include `lo`, exclude `hi`. Thus `[0,8)` contains indices 0 through 7; `hi=8` is a boundary, not an index to read.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[枚举验证]]逐个测试候选，二分查找则证明许多尚未测试的候选可以一并排除。[[序列共同接口|索引]]是位置的编号，Python 列表从 0 开始。下面使用半开索引区间 $[lo,hi)$：包含 `lo`，不包含 `hi`。因此 `[0,8)` 包含索引 0 到 7；`hi=8` 是边界，不能当作元素索引读取。

> [!example] Find 17 by following the remaining interval · 沿剩余区间找 17
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For `a = [2, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23]`, start with all eight positions. Here `mid` is an index and `a[mid]` is the stored value.
>
> | Remaining indices | Middle index | Value | What follows |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | `[0,8)` | 4 | 13 | Too small: keep `[5,8)` |
> | `[5,8)` | 6 | 19 | Too large: keep `[5,6)` |
> | `[5,6)` | 5 | 17 | Return index 5 |
>
> The first update removes indices 0 through 4 because every value there is at most 13. The second removes index 6 and all later candidates because their values are at least 19.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 `a = [2, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23]`，初始保留全部八个位置。`mid` 是索引，`a[mid]` 才是该位置的值。
>
> | 剩余索引 | 中点索引 | 元素值 | 下一步 |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | `[0,8)` | 4 | 13 | 太小，保留 `[5,8)` |
> | `[5,8)` | 6 | 19 | 太大，保留 `[5,6)` |
> | `[5,6)` | 5 | 17 | 返回索引 5 |
>
> 第一次删除索引 0 到 4，因为这些值都不超过 13。第二次删除索引 6 及其后仍在候选范围内的位置，因为这些值都不小于 19。

> [!important] Why a whole side can be discarded · 为什么可以排除一整侧
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On an ascending discrete sequence, binary search maintains a half-open index interval $[lo,hi)$ that can still contain the target. Comparing the midpoint with the target proves that one side is impossible, reducing the number of remaining candidates by about half on every iteration.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对按升序排列的离散序列，二分查找维护一个仍可能含有目标的半开索引区间 $[lo,hi)$。检查中点后，`小于`、`等于` 或 `大于` 的比较反馈能证明其中一侧不可能含有目标，于是每轮把候选数缩小约一半。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep every possible match inside the interval
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 半开区间不变量决定更新方向

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[循环不变量与终止|loop invariant]] is a statement that remains true before each iteration. Here it connects the original search question to the positions still under consideration. Let $n$ be the number of entries, $a_i$ the value at index $i$, and $t$ the target. Assume that values and target obey a consistent total ordering: each pair is smaller, equal, or larger, and these comparisons agree across pairs. The sequence is nondecreasing under that same comparison, and neither the sequence nor its comparison rule changes during the search. Index access and each comparison must finish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[循环不变量与终止|循环不变量]]是在每次迭代开始前都成立的陈述，这里把原来的查找问题与尚未排除的位置联系起来。设 $n$ 是元素数，$a_i$ 是索引 $i$ 处的值，$t$ 是目标。假设值与目标使用一致的全序比较：每对值必为小于、等于或大于之一，不同值对之间的比较也保持一致。序列按这个比较非递减排列，而且搜索中序列及比较规则都不改变；每次索引读取与比较都必须能够完成。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an ascending array, initialize $[lo,hi)=[0,n)$. The invariant is that if the target occurs in the original array, at least one matching index remains inside this half-open interval; comparisons have safely eliminated the positions outside it. At the midpoint, $a_{mid}<t$ eliminates indices through `mid`, while $a_{mid}>t$ eliminates `mid` and everything to its right. Equality supplies a match.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设数组满足

$$
a_0\le a_1\le\cdots\le a_{n-1},
$$

目标值为 $t$。初始化 $lo=0,hi=n$，并保持不变量：

> 若 $t$ 出现在原数组中，则至少有一个满足 $a_i=t$ 的索引仍位于 $[lo,hi)$；区间外的位置已经被比较结果安全排除。

令

$$
mid=lo+\left\lfloor\frac{hi-lo}{2}\right\rfloor.
$$

- 若 $a_{mid}<t$，由升序性知 $i\le mid$ 的位置都不可能等于 $t$，所以更新 $lo=mid+1$。
- 若 $a_{mid}>t$，由升序性知 $i\ge mid$ 的位置都不可能等于 $t$，所以更新 $hi=mid$。
- 若 $a_{mid}=t$，则已经找到一个匹配位置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The floor symbol $\lfloor u\rfloor$ means the greatest integer no larger than $u$. With $lo<hi$, the displayed midpoint lies in $[lo,hi)$, so it can be read safely. A failed comparison excludes the midpoint too: `lo=mid+1` skips a value proved too small, whereas `hi=mid` makes a value proved too large the excluded upper boundary. Alongside the matching-index statement, maintain $0\le lo\le hi\le n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下取整符号 $\lfloor u\rfloor$ 表示不大于 $u$ 的最大整数。只要 $lo<hi$，上式中点就在 $[lo,hi)$ 内，可以安全读取。未命中时，中点本身也已被排除：`lo=mid+1` 跳过已经证明太小的值；`hi=mid` 把已经证明太大的值放到不包含的右边界。除了“匹配索引仍在其中”，还应一直保持 $0\le lo\le hi\le n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The half-open convention represents an empty search space by $lo=hi$, and every unsuccessful update strictly decreases $hi-lo$, proving termination. With duplicates, this version finds some match only. Finding the first match requires a lower-bound invariant and is a different specification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半开区间让空搜索空间自然表示为 $lo=hi$。每次未命中的更新都严格缩短 $hi-lo$，所以循环会终止。若序列允许重复值，上述版本只承诺找到某一个匹配；寻找第一个匹配位置需要改用 lower-bound 不变量，不能把“任意命中”和“最左命中”当成同一个规格。

## A complete find-any implementation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*完整的任意命中实现*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The following Python version returns a matching index, or `None` if none exists. The preconditions above are part of its contract; it does not scan the list again to verify sortedness. Its final comparison branch rejects an incomparable pair, but is not a substitute for those global preconditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面的 Python 版本返回一个匹配索引；不存在匹配时返回 `None`。上面的前提属于调用约定；函数不会再扫描整个列表来检查是否有序。最后一个比较分支会拒绝当前不可比较的一对值，但不能替代这些整体前提。

```python
def binary_search(a, target):
    lo, hi = 0, len(a)
    while lo < hi:
        mid = lo + (hi - lo) // 2
        value = a[mid]
        if value < target:
            lo = mid + 1
        elif value > target:
            hi = mid
        elif value == target:
            return mid
        else:
            raise ValueError("Values do not obey the required ordering")
    return None
```

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Initially every possible match lies in `[0,n)`. The two strict-comparison branches preserve this fact by the ordered-array argument above. Equality returns an actual matching entry. Otherwise the nonnegative integer `hi-lo` strictly decreases. When it reaches zero, no possible match remains; therefore `None` is justified. This proves correctness and termination separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

初始时，所有可能的匹配都在 `[0,n)` 内。两个严格比较分支通过上面的有序性论证保持这个事实；相等分支返回实际匹配的元素。若未返回，非负整数 `hi-lo` 严格减小。它变成 0 时，没有任何可能的匹配仍被保留，因此可以返回 `None`。这分别说明了结果正确与程序终止的理由。

> [!example] Absent, empty, and repeated values · 未命中、空表与重复值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Search for 16 in the same eight-entry list. The intervals are `[0,8)`, `[5,8)`, `[5,6)`, then `[5,5)`: the values checked are 13, 19, and 17, so the result is `None`. An empty list starts with `[0,0)` and returns `None` without indexing anything. On `[7]`, target 7 returns index 0; target 8 returns `None`. Index 0 is a valid answer, so test `result is None`, not merely `if not result`.
>
> On `[1,2,2,2,4]`, target 2 is found at index 2 immediately. Index 1 is the first occurrence, but this algorithm did not promise that one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在同一个八元素列表中找 16，区间依次为 `[0,8)`、`[5,8)`、`[5,6)`、`[5,5)`；检查的值依次为 13、19、17，所以返回 `None`。空列表从 `[0,0)` 开始，不读取任何元素就返回 `None`。对 `[7]`，目标 7 返回索引 0，目标 8 返回 `None`。索引 0 是合法答案，因此应检查 `result is None`，不能只写 `if not result`。
>
> 对 `[1,2,2,2,4]` 查找 2，会立即返回索引 2；第一次出现的位置是索引 1，但当前算法并未承诺返回它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Python’s `bisect_left` has a different specification: it returns a left insertion boundary, including when the value is absent. To use that boundary for exact membership, first check that it is less than the sequence length and then check equality at that index. An insertion position alone is not a found-value certificate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Python 的 `bisect_left` 使用另一种规格：返回左侧插入边界，即使值不存在也会返回位置。若要据此判断精确成员关系，必须先检查返回位置小于序列长度，再检查该位置元素是否等于目标。插入位置本身不是已经找到目标的证据。

## Count remaining candidates, then account for work
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 对数复杂度针对剩余候选数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After $k$ halving steps, at most about $\lceil n/2^k\rceil$ candidates remain. Reducing the search to constant size therefore takes $O(\log_2 n)$ iterations. With random-access array indexing and constant-cost comparisons, the worst-case comparison count is $\Theta(\log n)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若初始候选数为 $n$，经过 $k$ 轮减半后，剩余候选数至多约为

$$
m_k\le \left\lceil\frac{n}{2^k}\right\rceil.
$$

要把候选缩到常数个，只需 $k=O(\log_2 n)$ 轮；在随机访问数组、一次取中点和比较均为常数成本的模型下，最坏比较次数为 $\Theta(\log n)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For this particular midpoint rule, the displayed ceiling bound is valid but loose. If a nonempty interval has $m=hi-lo$ positions, a non-hit leaves either $\lfloor m/2\rfloor$ positions on the left or $m-\lfloor m/2\rfloor-1$ on the right. Both are at most $\lfloor m/2\rfloor$. Thus, after $k$ non-hit steps, $m_k\le\lfloor n/2^k\rfloor$. By [[减半迭代次数|integer floor-halving]], for $n\ge1$ the maximum number of midpoint checks is $\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor+1$. This bound is attained, for example, by searching for a target below every entry: each step retains the left interval. For $n=8$, the candidate counts can be $8,4,2,1,0$, requiring four checks. For $n=0$, there are zero checks and no logarithm is evaluated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对当前中点规则，上面的向上取整界成立，但较松。若非空区间含有 $m=hi-lo$ 个位置，未命中后，左侧保留 $\lfloor m/2\rfloor$ 个，右侧保留 $m-\lfloor m/2\rfloor-1$ 个；两者都不超过 $\lfloor m/2\rfloor$。因此，经过 $k$ 次未命中更新，有 $m_k\le\lfloor n/2^k\rfloor$。由[[减半迭代次数|正整数向下取整减半]]，当 $n\ge1$ 时，中点检查次数最多为 $\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor+1$。例如目标小于所有元素时，每轮保留左半边，就能达到这个界。$n=8$ 时，候选数可以依次是 $8,4,2,1,0$，共检查四次。$n=0$ 时检查零次，不计算对数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This exact count is for midpoint checks, not individual Python comparison calls. The code uses up to three such calls in an iteration; their bounded number preserves the asymptotic result when each call has constant cost. An immediate middle match takes one iteration, so the logarithmic claim is a worst-case claim, not a statement that every query takes that long.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个精确次数数的是中点检查，而不是 Python 单个比较调用。代码一轮至多进行三个这样的调用；若每次调用的成本为常数，有界的调用数不会改变渐近结论。第一次就命中中点只需一轮，所以对数结论针对最坏情形，并非每次查询都需要那么久。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This bound concerns candidate count under a stated [[算法成本模型|cost model]]. Sorting an unsorted input is preprocessing rather than part of one binary-search query, and a structure without constant-time midpoint access need not achieve $\Theta(\log n)$ elapsed time. [[切片二分成本|Slicing binary-search costs]] explains how logarithmically many comparisons can coexist with more expensive copying. The [[03_Computer_Science/03_MIT 6.100L/21_算法成本与渐近分析#7. 回到真实程序，选择一个值得比较的工作负载|course path's query-cost comparison]] accounts for preprocessing across multiple queries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个结论是关于候选数和[[算法成本模型|成本模型]]的。若数据尚未排序，预先排序的成本不属于一次二分查找本身；若容器不能常数时间访问中点，实际运行时间也未必是 $\Theta(\log n)$。[[切片二分成本]]具体说明“比较次数对数级，但复制成本更高”的实现差异；多次查询时如何分摊预处理成本，见[[03_Computer_Science/03_MIT 6.100L/21_算法成本与渐近分析#7. 回到真实程序，选择一个值得比较的工作负载|课程路径中的查询总成本比较]]。

## Order must justify the discarded side
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 没有顺序或方向反馈就不能安全减半

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without sorted order, the midpoint comparison cannot justify discarding a side; treating `[1, 100, 2, 3]` as sorted can eliminate the half containing `100`. Likewise, equality-only feedback on a PIN does not reveal which side contains the target. Binary search requires both ordered candidates and directional comparison feedback, not merely a midpoint guess.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若序列未排序，中点值与目标的大小关系不能排除某一侧。例如在 `[1, 100, 2, 3]` 中查找 `100`，把它当成升序数组会错误地丢弃可能含有答案的一半。

即使候选有编号，若每次反馈只有“对/错”，也无法知道目标落在中点哪一侧；猜四位 PIN 时通常不能根据一次失败排除前半或后半。二分查找需要的是有序候选以及能给出方向的比较反馈，而不只是“选择了一个中点”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In that unsorted example, the first midpoint is index 2 with value 2. Since $2<100$, the ordered-array rule would set `lo=3` and discard index 1, which is exactly where 100 occurs. The invalid premise, not the midpoint arithmetic, caused the wrong answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这个无序反例中，第一次中点是索引 2，值为 2。由于 $2<100$，有序数组规则会令 `lo=3`，把实际存放 100 的索引 1 删除。错误来自不成立的前提，而不是中点计算。

> [!warning] Keep comparisons and the data consistent · 比较与数据须保持一致
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An IEEE floating-point NaN is neither less than, greater than, nor equal to an ordinary number. It does not obey the ordering required here; the final branch in the example code raises rather than treating “neither smaller nor larger” as equality. Likewise, reordering or mutating the sequence during a search can invalidate an earlier elimination. This is a search algorithm for a fixed ordered input, not a sorting or input-validation procedure.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> IEEE 浮点 NaN 与普通数比较时，小于、大于、等于都不成立，不满足这里的顺序前提。示例代码的最后分支会报错，不会把“既不小也不大”直接当作相等。同样，搜索期间修改元素或重排列表，可能让先前的排除结论失效。这是对固定有序输入的查找算法，不是排序或输入验证程序。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[二分求根|Bisection root-finding]] also shrinks an interval, but it concerns a continuous variable. The standard bracketing argument requires continuity and opposite endpoint signs; some monotone inversion problems additionally use monotonicity to determine the update direction. The discrete array invariant does not replace these conditions for a continuous problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[二分求根]]也会反复缩小区间，但它处理连续变量。标准括区间论证依赖函数连续且端点异号；某些单调反演问题还可用单调性提供更新方向。离散数组的索引不变量不能直接替代这些连续问题条件。

> [!question]- Check the elimination argument · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A list has fixed indices but its values are unsorted. Why can a midpoint value below the target not justify deleting the entire left side?
>
> **Answer:** Index order carries no information about value order. The left side may still contain values below, equal to, or above the target, so the comparison supplies no valid elimination certificate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个列表虽然有固定索引，但元素没有排序。为什么仍不能根据中点元素小于目标，就删除中点左侧的全部元素？
>
> **答案：** 索引顺序没有传递到元素值；左侧仍可能出现任意大于、等于或小于目标的值，因此比较结果没有提供安全排除一半的证据。
