# Density scaling under a change of variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*密度换元的尺度因子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **probability density** measures probability per unit of length. If a transformation stretches a small interval, the same probability is spread over more length, so the density falls. The scale correction in a density transformation measures **old length per unit of new length**. This is why it uses the inverse derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**概率密度**表示每单位长度上分配多少概率。变换把一个小区间拉长时，同一份概率铺在更长的区间上，密度就降低。密度换元的尺度校正计算的是**每单位新长度对应多少旧长度**，所以使用逆变换的导数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If this density interpretation is new, [[概率密度函数|the density definition]] explains why probabilities come from areas under a curve. For $X$ [[连续均匀分布|uniform]] on $(0,1)$, its density is $1$ there. Let $Y=2X+1$. The new range is $(1,3)$, twice as long, so its density is $1/2$. For example, $1<Y<1.2$ is the same event as $0<X<0.1$; both probabilities are $0.1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若还不熟悉这种含义，[[概率密度函数]]解释了概率为什么是曲线下的面积。令 $X$ 在 $(0,1)$ 上[[连续均匀分布|均匀分布]]，该区间内密度为 $1$。取 $Y=2X+1$，新范围是 $(1,3)$，长度翻倍，密度变为 $1/2$。例如 $1<Y<1.2$ 与 $0<X<0.1$ 是同一事件，两者概率均为 $0.1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The inverse identifies both the point and the length scale
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*逆函数同时找回位置与长度尺度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume $X$ has a density $f_X$ and lies in an open interval $I$ with probability one. Let $g:I\to J=g(I)$ be strictly monotone, continuously differentiable, and satisfy $g'(x)\ne0$ throughout $I$. Then its inverse $h=g^{-1}:J\to I$ is continuously differentiable. A sufficient regular setting for the density rule is therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $X$ 有密度 $f_X$，并以概率一落在开区间 $I$ 中。设 $g:I\to J=g(I)$ 严格单调、连续可微，且在整个 $I$ 上满足 $g'(x)\ne0$。于是逆函数 $h=g^{-1}:J\to I$ 连续可微。密度公式的一个充分正则情形为

> [!important] Evaluate the old density and correct the scale · 原位置取密度，再校正尺度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The inverse $h(y)$ finds the input that becomes $y$. The absolute derivative $|h'(y)|$ converts a unit of output length back to input length. Outside $J$, set the density to zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 逆函数 $h(y)$ 找到变成 $y$ 的原输入；导数绝对值 $|h'(y)|$ 把单位输出长度换回输入长度。在 $J$ 之外把密度设为零。
>
> $$\boxed{f_Y(y)=f_X(h(y))\,|h'(y)|=\frac{f_X(g^{-1}(y))}{|g'(g^{-1}(y))|},\quad y\in J.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiating $g(h(y))=y$ by the [[链式法则|chain rule]] gives $g'(h(y))h'(y)=1$, which explains the reciprocal. Do not replace $f_X(h(y))$ with $f_X(y)$: densities must be evaluated in their original coordinates. Density functions describe a distribution through integrals and may differ on sets of length zero; the formula gives a version of the density, not a prescribed value at every endpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $g(h(y))=y$ 使用[[链式法则]]，得到 $g'(h(y))h'(y)=1$，这说明了倒数来自哪里。不要把 $f_X(h(y))$ 换成 $f_X(y)$：原密度必须在原坐标中取值。密度通过积分描述分布，可以在长度为零的集合上取不同值；公式给出一个密度版本，并不规定每个端点必须取什么值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Probability conservation gives the formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从概率保持不变推导公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$ for the [[累积分布函数|CDF]]. If $g$ is increasing, $F_Y(y)=F_X(h(y))$. At points where the CDF derivatives agree with the chosen density functions, which holds almost everywhere, differentiation gives $f_Y(y)=f_X(h(y))h'(y)$, with $h'(y)>0$. If $g$ is decreasing, the event becomes $X\ge h(y)$. Since a variable with a density has no point masses, $F_Y(y)=1-F_X(h(y))$; differentiating adds a minus sign, cancelling the negative inverse derivative. Both cases give $|h'(y)|$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记[[累积分布函数]]为 $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$。若 $g$ 递增，则 $F_Y(y)=F_X(h(y))$；在 CDF 导数与所选密度函数一致的位置（这在几乎处处成立）求导，得到 $f_Y(y)=f_X(h(y))h'(y)$，而 $h'(y)>0$。若 $g$ 递减，事件改写为 $X\ge h(y)$；有密度的变量没有点质量，故 $F_Y(y)=1-F_X(h(y))$。求导多出的负号与负的逆导数相消，两种情况都得到 $|h'(y)|$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exact statement behind the small-interval intuition is an integral identity. For an interval $B\subseteq J$, the source event is $X\in g^{-1}(B)$. The probability-integral version of [[定积分换元|substitution]] applies to nonnegative densities even when they are discontinuous; this uses the general Lebesgue change-of-variables theorem cited below, beyond the elementary continuous-integrand version. It gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

小区间直觉背后的精确陈述是积分恒等式。对区间 $B\subseteq J$，对应的原事件为 $X\in g^{-1}(B)$。用于概率积分的[[定积分换元|换元法]]也适用于不连续的非负密度；这里使用来源所列的一般 Lebesgue 换元定理，范围超过初等的连续被积函数版本。于是

$$P(Y\in B)=\int_{g^{-1}(B)}f_X(x)\,dx=\int_B f_X(h(y))|h'(y)|\,dy.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus the displayed candidate density produces the correct interval probabilities, including total probability $1$. The informal relation $f_Y(y)|dy|\approx f_X(x)|dx|$ summarizes this mechanism; it should not be read as an exact finite-interval formula at one sample point. For a decreasing transformation of a distribution with atoms, the CDF instead needs the [[递减变换的CDF|left-limit correction]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，所给候选密度算出了正确的区间概率，包括总概率 $1$。直观关系 $f_Y(y)|dy|\approx f_X(x)|dx|$ 概括这个机制，但不能把它理解为仅在某一点取密度就能精确计算有限区间概率。若递减变换作用于带点质量的分布，CDF 还需要[[递减变换的CDF|左极限修正]]。

## Stretching and reflection give the same nonnegative factor
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拉伸与反向都使用非负尺度因子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening example, $h(y)=(y-1)/2$ and $h'(y)=1/2$. Hence $f_Y(y)=1/2$ on $(1,3)$ and zero outside. The normalization check is $(3-1)/2=1$. If instead $Y=3-2X$, then $h(y)=(3-y)/2$, $h'(y)=-1/2$, and the same output interval again has density $1/2$. The minus sign reverses order; it does not create negative probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头的例子有 $h(y)=(y-1)/2$、$h'(y)=1/2$，因此 $(1,3)$ 内 $f_Y(y)=1/2$，区间外为零。归一化检验为 $(3-1)/2=1$。若改成 $Y=3-2X$，则 $h(y)=(3-y)/2$、$h'(y)=-1/2$；同一输出区间仍具有密度 $1/2$。负号改变排列方向，不会产生负概率。

## Check the domain before using one inverse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*使用单个逆函数前先检查范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $X$ uniform on $(0,1)$ and $Y=X^2$, the positive branch is enough. On $0<y<1$, $h(y)=\sqrt y$ and $f_Y(y)=1/(2\sqrt y)$. It becomes unbounded near zero, but $\int_0^1(2\sqrt y)^{-1}dy=1$ and $P(Y=0)=0$. The critical endpoint is outside the open interval where the formula is used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $X$ 在 $(0,1)$ 均匀分布且 $Y=X^2$，正分支就够了。对 $0<y<1$，$h(y)=\sqrt y$，密度为 $f_Y(y)=1/(2\sqrt y)$。它在零附近无界，但 $\int_0^1(2\sqrt y)^{-1}dy=1$，且 $P(Y=0)=0$。这个临界端点不在套用公式的开区间内。

> [!warning] One inverse need not cover the source distribution · 一个逆函数未必覆盖原分布
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $X$ can be positive and negative, squaring may have two relevant preimages. Their contributions must both be included. A flat piece of $g$ can collapse an interval with positive probability into a point mass. Critical points, flat pieces and multiple branches need the broader [[随机变量的分布变换|CDF or branchwise method]]; losing these cases is not repaired by taking an absolute value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $X$ 可正可负，平方变换可能有两个有效原像，两者贡献都要计入。$g$ 的一段平坦区间还可能把正概率压成点质量。临界点、平坦部分与多个分支要用更一般的[[随机变量的分布变换|CDF 法或分支法]]；单纯加绝对值不能弥补这些遗漏。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let a random vector $X$ have a density and lie with probability one in an open set $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$. For a $C^1$ diffeomorphism $T:U\to V$ between open sets of the same dimension and $Y=T(X)$, the probability-integral extension of [[多元换元公式|multivariable substitution]] gives $f_Y(y)=f_X(T^{-1}(y))|\det D(T^{-1})(y)|$. This expression applies on $V$; set it to zero outside $V$. The inverse determinant converts output volume to input volume. The forward determinant in an integral written over input coordinates and the inverse determinant in an output density are therefore two uses of the same volume rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设随机向量 $X$ 有密度，并以概率一落在开集 $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 中。若 $T:U\to V$ 是同维开集间的 $C^1$ 微分同胚，且 $Y=T(X)$，则[[多元换元公式]]的概率积分推广给出 $f_Y(y)=f_X(T^{-1}(y))|\det D(T^{-1})(y)|$。这个表达式用于 $V$ 内，$V$ 外取零。逆行列式把输出体积换回输入体积。因此，以输入坐标写积分时的正向行列式，与输出密度里的逆行列式，是同一体积规则的两种用法。
