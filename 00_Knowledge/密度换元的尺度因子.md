# 密度换元乘以逆变换导数的绝对值以保持概率质量

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Density transformation uses the absolute inverse derivative to conserve probability mass*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $g$ 严格单调、连续可微且导数非零的区间内，若 $X$ 有密度，$Y=g(X)$ 满足
$$f_Y(y)=f_X(g^{-1}(y))|(g^{-1})'(y)|.$$
相应微小区间代表同一事件，$f_Y(y)|dy|\approx f_X(x)|dx|$；因而旧密度必须乘旧长度与新长度之比。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a continuously differentiable monotone change of variable with nonzero derivative, the inverse scale factor conserves probability between corresponding intervals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
拉长两倍的 $Y=2X+1$ 使密度减半；递减变换的逆导数为负也需取绝对值。临界点、平坦段与多根不能机械套一个逆函数，应回到 [[随机变量的分布变换]]。多维对应 [[多元换元公式]] 中逆 Jacobian 行列式的绝对值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Doubling interval lengths halves density. Decreasing orientation requires an absolute value. Critical points, flat pieces, and multiple roots require the broader [[随机变量的分布变换|CDF or branchwise method]]. In multiple dimensions the factor becomes the absolute inverse [[多元换元公式|Jacobian determinant]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
