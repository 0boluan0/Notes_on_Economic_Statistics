# Integration by partial fractions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*部分分式积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Partial fractions rewrites one complicated fraction as a sum of simpler fractions, so that its integral can be found term by term. It applies to a **rational function** $P(x)/Q(x)$: both $P$ and $Q$ are polynomials with real coefficients, meaning finite linear combinations of nonnegative integer powers of $x$, and the denominator polynomial is not identically zero. The fraction is defined only where $Q(x)\ne0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

部分分式法把一个复杂分式改写成几个简单分式之和，再逐项积分。它适用于**有理函数** $P(x)/Q(x)$：$P,Q$ 都是实系数多项式，即 $x$ 的非负整数次幂的有限线性组合，且分母多项式不能恒为零。这个分式只在 $Q(x)\ne0$ 的位置有定义。

> [!example] Undo adding fractions · 把通分倒过来做
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Factor $x^2+x-2=(x-1)(x+2)$ and look for constants $A,B$ such that
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把 $x^2+x-2$ 分解成 $(x-1)(x+2)$，寻找常数 $A,B$，使
> $$\frac{4x-1}{(x-1)(x+2)}=\frac A{x-1}+\frac B{x+2}.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Multiplying by the common denominator gives the polynomial identity $4x-1=A(x+2)+B(x-1)$. Substituting $x=1$ gives $3=3A$, and $x=-2$ gives $-9=-3B$. Thus $A=1$, $B=3$. These substitutions are made into the polynomial identity, not into the undefined original fraction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 乘以共同分母，得到多项式恒等式 $4x-1=A(x+2)+B(x-1)$。代入 $x=1$ 得 $3=3A$，代入 $x=-2$ 得 $-9=-3B$，所以 $A=1$、$B=3$。这里代入的是多项式恒等式，不是原来在这些点无定义的分式。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $\ln$ denotes the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]]. The [[对数函数求导|logarithmic derivative]] $d\ln|x-a|/dx=1/(x-a)$ for $x\ne a$ gives the antiderivatives of these two simple fractions. Thus
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $\ln$ 表示[[自然对数]]。利用[[对数函数求导|对数求导公式]]，在 $x\ne a$ 时有 $d\ln|x-a|/dx=1/(x-a)$，两个简单分式的原函数便随之得到：
>
> $$\int\frac{4x-1}{x^2+x-2}\,dx
> =\ln|x-1|+3\ln|x+2|+C.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The result holds on each interval avoiding $-2$ and $1$. Combining the two fractions checks the decomposition; differentiating the answer checks the integral.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 结果分别适用于避开 $-2,1$ 的各个区间。把两个分式重新通分可以检查分解，对答案求导可以检查积分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## First separate any polynomial part
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先把多项式部分分离出来*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **degree** of a nonzero polynomial is its largest power with a nonzero coefficient. If the numerator's degree is at least the denominator's, first divide the polynomials to write $P/Q=S+R/Q$, where $S$ is a polynomial and either $R=0$ or $R$ has smaller degree than $Q$. Only the remaining proper fraction needs decomposition. For example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非零多项式的**次数**，是系数不为零的最高次幂。如果分子的次数不小于分母，先做多项式除法，把 $P/Q$ 写成 $S+R/Q$：$S$ 是多项式，余式 $R$ 或者为零，或者次数小于 $Q$。只有剩下的真分式需要分解。例如

$$\frac{x^2+1}{x-1}=x+1+\frac2{x-1},\qquad
\int\frac{x^2+1}{x-1}\,dx=\frac{x^2}{2}+x+2\ln|x-1|+C.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This preliminary step also handles a nonzero constant denominator: division leaves just a polynomial, so no partial fractions are needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果分母是非零常数，这一步直接得到多项式，也就不需要部分分式分解了。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use every factor and every repeated power
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*每个因子、每一重幂都要列项*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over the real numbers, a polynomial factors into linear factors and irreducible quadratic factors, up to an overall nonzero constant. “Irreducible quadratic” means a degree-two polynomial with no real root; it cannot be split into real linear factors. After factoring the proper fraction's denominator, use the following complete forms, with all displayed capital letters as unknown constants:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实系数多项式可以分解成一次因子和实数范围不可约的二次因子，再乘一个非零常数。“不可约二次因子”指没有实根的二次多项式，不能再拆成实一次因子。把真分式的分母因式分解后，按下面的完整形式列项，所有大写字母都表示待求常数：

$$\begin{aligned}
(x-a)^k &: \quad \frac{A_1}{x-a}+\frac{A_2}{(x-a)^2}+\cdots+\frac{A_k}{(x-a)^k},\\
q(x)^r &: \quad \frac{B_1x+C_1}{q(x)}+\frac{B_2x+C_2}{q(x)^2}+\cdots+\frac{B_rx+C_r}{q(x)^r}.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $k,r$ are positive integers and $q$ is irreducible quadratic. Include a term for **every power**, even if some coefficients later turn out to be zero. The numerator over each quadratic power must allow both an $x$ term and a constant. For a denominator $(x-1)^2(x^2+1)$, the required form is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $k,r$ 是正整数，$q$ 是不可约二次因子。**每一重幂**都要列项，即使最后某些系数可能为零；每个二次因子的分子都必须允许一次项和常数项。若分母是 $(x-1)^2(x^2+1)$，应写成

$$\frac{A_1}{x-1}+\frac{A_2}{(x-1)^2}+\frac{Bx+C}{x^2+1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiply through by the entire denominator and compare coefficients of equal powers of $x$, or substitute enough suitable values into the resulting polynomial identity. Solve for the constants and recombine the fractions to check. Choosing only $A/(x-1)^2+B/(x^2+1)$ omits terms needed for a general numerator; a convenient-looking incomplete template is not a valid general method.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘掉整个共同分母后，可以比较两边 $x$ 各次幂的系数，也可以向所得多项式恒等式代入足够多的适当数值。解出常数后，重新通分检查。只写 $A/(x-1)^2+B/(x^2+1)$ 会漏掉表示一般分子所需的项，不能作为一般方法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Integrate the pieces and keep their domains
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*逐项积分，并保留定义域*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a linear factor, the first power gives a logarithm; higher powers use the power rule:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一次因子的分母若为一重，积分给出对数；更高重数则用幂函数积分公式：

$$\int\frac{dx}{x-a}=\ln|x-a|+C,\qquad
\int\frac{dx}{(x-a)^j}=\frac{(x-a)^{1-j}}{1-j}+C\quad(j\ge2).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a term with irreducible quadratic denominator $q(x)^j$, write its linear numerator $Bx+C$ as $\lambda q'(x)+\mu$. The derivative part uses [[换元积分|substitution]] $u=q(x)$. For the constant part, complete the square in $q$: a first power leads to an arctangent, while higher powers can be handled by [[三角代换|trigonometric substitution]] and [[正弦余弦幂积分|trigonometric power integrals]]. For instance,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于不可约二次因子幂 $q(x)^j$ 上方的一次式 $Bx+C$，先把它写成 $\lambda q'(x)+\mu$。含分母导数的部分用 [[换元积分]] $u=q(x)$；常数部分先对 $q$ 配方，一重分母会给出反正切，更高重数可以借助 [[三角代换]] 和 [[正弦余弦幂积分]]。例如

$$\int\frac{dx}{x^2+2x+3}
=\int\frac{dx}{(x+1)^2+2}
=\frac1{\sqrt2}\arctan\!\left(\frac{x+1}{\sqrt2}\right)+C.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The shift is $x+1$ because that is what completing the square produces. The [[反三角函数求导|arctangent derivative]] checks the answer directly. Partial fractions therefore reduces every real rational-function integral to polynomial, logarithmic, rational and inverse-tangent pieces in principle; factoring and solving for coefficients can still be laborious.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平移量是 $x+1$，因为配方得到的正是这个表达式；利用 [[反三角函数求导|反正切求导公式]]可以直接检查答案。原则上，部分分式能够把每个实有理函数的积分化成多项式、对数、有理式和反正切等部分，但因式分解及求系数仍可能很费计算。

> [!warning] Algebra does not fill holes or cancel divergent integrals · 代数运算不自动补定义域
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Record the original domain before cancellation. For example, $(x^2-1)/(x-1)=x+1$ only when $x\ne1$; cancelling the common factor does not define the original fraction at $1$. An uncancelled real pole separates intervals, and integration constants may differ on the separate intervals. A definite integral crossing a genuine pole must be checked by [[跨奇点积分拆分|separate one-sided improper limits]]; endpoint subtraction across the pole can hide divergence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 约分前先记录原式定义域。例如 $(x^2-1)/(x-1)=x+1$ 只在 $x\ne1$ 时成立，约掉公因子并没有替原分式定义 $x=1$ 处的值。未消去的实极点把区间分开，各区间的积分常数可以不同。定积分若跨过真正的极点，必须按 [[跨奇点积分拆分|左右两侧分别取反常极限]]；直接跨极点做原函数端点相减，可能把发散掩盖掉。
