# 四分位距是第三四分位数与第一四分位数之差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The interquartile range is the third quartile minus the first quartile*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 四分位距 $\mathrm{IQR}=q_{0.75}-q_{0.25}$ 衡量分布中间部分的跨度。样本 IQR 用同一种[[样本分位数]]约定算出的两个四分位数相减。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The interquartile range measures the span between the first and third quartiles. A sample IQR uses two quartiles calculated under the same [[样本分位数|quantile convention]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

若两个四分位数为 7 和 15，则 IQR 为 $15-7=8$。它通常比极差对尾部极端值更稳健，但不描述整条分布，且有限样本结果仍受分位数约定影响。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quartiles of 7 and 15 give an IQR of 8. It is typically less sensitive to extremes than the range, but does not describe the whole distribution and depends on the finite-sample quantile convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
