# 半分离均衡是满足 PBE、既非合并也非分离并只部分揭示发送者类型的信号博弈均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A semi-separating equilibrium is a signaling-game equilibrium that satisfies PBE, is neither pooling nor separating, and reveals the sender's type only partially.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 定义
> 半分离不是“差一点算出分离”，而是[[信号博弈]]中一种完整的 PBE 分类：不同类型的信号分布并不相同，但其支持又有重叠。某些观察仍留下多个可能类型，另一些观察可能识别类型。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Definition*
>
> Semi-separation is not an incomplete attempt at separation; it is a complete PBE class in a [[信号博弈|signaling game]]. Type-contingent signal distributions differ but have overlapping supports. Some observations leave several types possible, while others may identify the type.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它在三分法中的位置
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Its place in the three-way classification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半分离要求类型条件信号分布并不完全相同，同时至少有一个正概率信号被多个类型共同使用。也就是说，它既不是[[合并均衡|合并]]，也不是[[分离均衡|分离]]。三个及以上类型时，见证“分布不同”与“支持重叠”的类型对不必相同；完整的互斥判定见[[混合信号均衡分类]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Semi-separation requires type-contingent signal distributions that are not identical, together with at least one positive-probability signal used by more than one type. It is therefore neither [[合并均衡|pooling]] nor [[分离均衡|separating]]. With three or more types, the pair witnessing different distributions need not be the pair witnessing overlapping supports. See [[混合信号均衡分类|classification of mixed signaling equilibria]] for the complete mutually exclusive test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Bayes 更新产生部分信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bayesian updating produces partial information.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以两个类型 $H,L$ 和两个信号 $s_1,s_2$ 为例。若
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider two types $H,L$ and two signals $s_1,s_2$. If
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sigma_1(s_1\mid H)=1,
\qquad
\sigma_1(s_1\mid L)=q,
\qquad
\sigma_1(s_2\mid L)=1-q,
\quad 0<q<1,
$$

则 $s_1$ 由两种类型共同发送，观察它后的 posterior 为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
then both types send $s_1$, so the posterior after observing it is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p(H\mid s_1)
=
\frac{\mu_0(H)}{\mu_0(H)+\mu_0(L)q},
$$

既不是先验、也不是 0 或 1；$s_2$ 只由 $L$ 发送，所以 $p(L\mid s_2)=1$。这正是“部分揭示”：一部分观察缩小但不消除类型不确定性，另一部分观察可能完全识别类型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
which is neither the prior nor zero or one. Only $L$ sends $s_2$, so $p(L\mid s_2)=1$. This is partial revelation: some observations reduce but do not eliminate type uncertainty, while others may identify the type completely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 成为均衡还需要什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What else is required for equilibrium?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正概率混合必须由支持内收益相等支撑，见[[混合支持内无差异]]。不过在有限标准信号博弈中，[[混合合并可纯化|mixed pooling]] 与[[混合分离可纯化|mixed separating]] PBE 都能纯化；因此若纯合并与纯分离 PBE 均不存在，存在性与三分法会保证至少有一份半分离 PBE，见[[纯端点缺失迫使半分离]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive-probability mixing must be supported by equal payoffs within the support; see [[混合支持内无差异|support indifference]]. In a finite standard signaling game, however, every [[混合合并可纯化|mixed pooling]] or [[混合分离可纯化|mixed separating]] PBE can be purified. Thus, if no pure pooling or pure separating PBE exists, equilibrium existence and the trichotomy guarantee at least one semi-separating PBE; see [[纯端点缺失迫使半分离|absence of pure endpoints forces semi-separation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] “两个类型都混合”不自动是半分离
> 若 $H$ 与 $L$ 使用完全相同的 Beer/Quiche 混合概率，这仍是 pooling，因为观察不改变类型 posterior。只有两种分布不同且至少共享一个正概率信号，才是 semi-separating。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *“Both types mix” does not automatically imply semi-separation.*
>
> If $H$ and $L$ use exactly the same mixing probabilities over Beer and Quiche, the equilibrium is still pooling because the observation does not change the type posterior. It is semi-separating only when the two distributions differ and share at least one signal used with positive probability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $H$ 只发送 $s_1$，$L$ 只发送 $s_2$，但接收者在两个信号后都混合行动。这是否是半分离？
>
> **答案：** 不是。分类看发送者各类型的信号策略；两类信号支持互不相交，所以它仍是 separating。接收者是否混合不会改变这一分类。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Self-check*
>
> $H$ sends only $s_1$ and $L$ sends only $s_2$, but the receiver mixes actions after both signals. Is this semi-separating?
>
> **Answer:** No. The classification depends on each sender type's signaling strategy. The two supports are disjoint, so the equilibrium remains separating. Whether the receiver mixes does not change that classification.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
