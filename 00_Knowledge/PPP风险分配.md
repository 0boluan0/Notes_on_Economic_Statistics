# PPP风险应按控制减缓与承受能力分配而不是尽量转给私人方
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Allocate PPP risks according to the ability to control, mitigate and bear them, rather than maximizing transfer to the private party.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给 [[政府和社会资本合作|PPP]] 分配风险时，对每项风险依次问：谁能降低它发生的概率，谁能减轻发生后的损失，谁能以较低成本承受剩余损失？可用合同激励、保险或分担规则落实这个判断。风险分得越多并不一定越有效；让私人方承担难以管理的风险，可能只换来更高报价、融资困难或违约。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each [[政府和社会资本合作|PPP]] risk, ask who can reduce its probability, mitigate its impact and bear the remaining loss at lower cost. Use incentives, insurance or sharing rules to implement the allocation. Transferring unmanageable risks may raise prices, obstruct financing or cause default.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，承包方通常能通过施工组织影响延误风险；地震发生本身不可由它控制，但抗震设计可以降低损失。两者不能仅凭“都是建设风险”就用同一规则分配。无法有效控制的剩余风险，还要比较保险、分散和承担成本，不能预设总由政府或私人承担。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A contractor may control construction scheduling but cannot prevent an earthquake, although design can reduce damage. These risks need different treatment. For residual risks, compare insurance, diversification and bearing costs rather than assigning them automatically to either sector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要检查承担能力是否真实。在有限追索项目融资且没有额外股东担保等义务时，股东可能只损失已承诺投入，不能据合同中的“私人承担”假定其愿意无限填补亏损。项目失败后政府仍可能需要维持关键公共服务，因而保留残余服务风险；这与政府是否依法或依合同偿还私人债务是两个问题，不能自动画等号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check actual loss-bearing capacity. With limited-recourse financing and no additional sponsor guarantees or obligations, shareholder exposure may be limited to committed capital. Government may still need to sustain essential services after project failure. That residual service responsibility does not itself establish an obligation to repay private debt.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，风险矩阵应写清风险事件、承担方和合同处理方式；泛写“风险转移给社会资本”不够。收入不足时谁承受损失，也要把 [[项目融资与付费来源|合同付费来源]] 与融资追索权分开检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A risk matrix should specify the event, bearer and contractual treatment. Check [[项目融资与付费来源|contractual payment sources]] separately from financing recourse when assessing revenue-shortfall exposure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
