# Fixed shifts and consistency
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*固定偏移与一致性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose an estimating rule becomes concentrated near the right target as more data arrive. Adding five to every answer moves that concentration five units away. The answers can become very stable while remaining wrong for the original target. This is the effect of a **fixed shift**: its size does not change with sample size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设一条估计规则随着数据增加，逐渐集中到正确目标附近。若给每个答案都加五，这个集中位置也会向上移动五个单位。答案可以变得十分稳定，却仍偏离原目标。这就是**固定偏移**的作用：它的大小不随样本量变化。

^core

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The probability limit moves by exactly the same amount
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*概率极限随常数平移*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $T_n$ be real-valued estimators and let the finite real target be $\theta$. Assume [[估计量一致性|consistency]]: $T_n\xrightarrow{p}\theta$, meaning $P_\theta(|T_n-\theta|>\varepsilon)\to0$ for every fixed $\varepsilon>0$. Here $P_\theta$ is probability in the sampling model with parameter fixed at $\theta$. For a known finite real constant $c$, independent of $n$, define $U_n=T_n+c$. The error relative to the shifted target is exactly the old error:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $T_n$ 是实值估计量，有限实数目标为 $\theta$，并假设它[[估计量一致性|一致]]，即 $T_n\xrightarrow{p}\theta$：对每个固定 $\varepsilon>0$，都有 $P_\theta(|T_n-\theta|>\varepsilon)\to0$。$P_\theta$ 表示参数固定为 $\theta$ 时抽样模型中的概率。对不随 $n$ 变化的已知有限实常数 $c$，定义 $U_n=T_n+c$。相对平移后目标的误差，恰好就是原误差：

> [!important] A shifted probability limit · 平移后的概率极限
> $$U_n-(\theta+c)=T_n-\theta,\qquad U_n\xrightarrow p\theta+c.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The event that either absolute error exceeds a fixed tolerance is the same event. This proves the limit directly from [[依概率收敛|convergence in probability]]. No moment or independence assumptions are needed for this step.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个误差超过同一个固定容忍值，是完全相同的事件。因此由[[依概率收敛]]定义直接得到极限，这一步不需要矩条件或独立性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $c=0$, the original rule is unchanged. If $c\ne0$, $U_n$ is consistent for $\theta+c$, but not for $\theta$. [[估计对象三分|The specified target]] is essential to this statement: the same sequence is suitable for the shifted target and unsuitable for the original one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$c=0$ 时，原规则没有改变。$c\ne0$ 时，$U_n$ 对 $\theta+c$ 一致，却对 $\theta$ 不一致。这里必须说明[[估计对象三分|要估计的目标]]：同一个序列，对平移后的目标合适，对原目标则不合适。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why an error away from the original target becomes almost certain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*超过一个固定误差的概率反而趋于一*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $c\ne0$, choose any fixed tolerance satisfying $0<\varepsilon<|c|$, and set $\delta=(|c|-\varepsilon)/2>0$. Whenever the original error is at most $\delta$, the triangle inequality gives
$$|U_n-\theta|=|c+(T_n-\theta)|\ge |c|-|T_n-\theta|\ge |c|-\delta>\varepsilon.$$
The event $|T_n-\theta|\le\delta$ has probability tending to one. Therefore $P_\theta(|U_n-\theta|>\varepsilon)\to1$. Finding one such tolerance already disproves consistency; the argument works for every fixed tolerance strictly smaller than $|c|$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$c\ne0$ 时，选任意满足 $0<\varepsilon<|c|$ 的固定容忍值，再令 $\delta=(|c|-\varepsilon)/2>0$。只要原误差不超过 $\delta$，由三角不等式就有上式：新误差至少为 $|c|-\delta$，严格超过 $\varepsilon$。而 $|T_n-\theta|\le\delta$ 的概率趋于一，所以 $P_\theta(|U_n-\theta|>\varepsilon)\to1$。找到一个这样的容忍值已经足以否定一致性；这里对每个严格小于 $|c|$ 的固定容忍值都成立。

> [!example] A sample mean plus five · 样本均值加五
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take [[独立同分布|i.i.d.]] observations with [[正态分布|normal distribution]] $N(\theta,1)$, where $\theta$ is the mean and $1$ the variance. Let $\bar X_n$ be their average and $U_n=\bar X_n+5$. The average has mean $\theta$ and variance $1/n$, so its [[均方收敛推出概率收敛|vanishing squared error ensures consistency]]. Adding five gives
> $$E_\theta[U_n]=\theta+5,\qquad \operatorname{Var}_\theta(U_n)=\frac1n,\qquad E_\theta[(U_n-\theta)^2]=25+\frac1n.$$
> At $n=100$, the sampling centre is still five units above the target, although the [[标准误含义|standard error]] is only $0.1$. Whenever $|\bar X_n-\theta|\le1$, the shifted error is at least $4$, hence exceeds $2$. Since that event has probability tending to one, $P_\theta(|U_n-\theta|>2)\to1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取[[独立同分布]]的[[正态分布]] $N(\theta,1)$ 观察，$\theta$ 为均值、$1$ 为方差。记平均为 $\bar X_n$，令 $U_n=\bar X_n+5$。原平均的均值为 $\theta$、方差为 $1/n$，因此[[均方收敛推出概率收敛|平方误差趋零保证其一致]]。加五以后，期望为 $\theta+5$，方差仍为 $1/n$，相对原目标的均方误差为 $25+1/n$。$n=100$ 时，抽样中心仍比目标高五个单位，尽管[[标准误含义|标准误]]已经只有 $0.1$。只要 $|\bar X_n-\theta|\le1$，新误差就至少为 $4$，必然超过 $2$；前一事件概率趋于一，所以后一事件概率也趋于一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The variance measures spread around the rule's own mean; it does not check whether that mean is at the target. When the variance exists, a constant shift leaves it unchanged by the [[方差的仿射变换|variance shift rule]]. The [[MSE偏差方差分解|squared-bias term]] records the error that remains in the example. By contrast, the [[一致不推出无偏|sample mean plus $1/n$]] loses its shift as $n$ grows. A known fixed correction can also be removed: $U_n-c=T_n$ restores the original consistent rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方差衡量规则围绕自身均值的分散程度，不负责检查这个均值是否位于目标。方差存在时，常数平移根据[[方差的仿射变换|方差平移规则]]保持它不变；本例中持续存在的误差，由[[MSE偏差方差分解|偏差平方项]]记录。相比之下，[[一致不推出无偏|样本均值加 $1/n$]]中的偏移会随 $n$ 消失。已知的固定修正也可以减回去：$U_n-c=T_n$ 恢复原来的一致规则。
