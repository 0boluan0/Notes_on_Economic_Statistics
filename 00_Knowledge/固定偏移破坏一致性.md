# 给一致估计量加非零固定常数会把概率极限移离原目标

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Adding a nonzero fixed constant shifts a consistent estimator away from its original target*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 若 $T_n\xrightarrow{p}\theta$，则 $T_n+c\xrightarrow{p}\theta+c$。当 $c\ne0$ 时，新序列对原目标 $\theta$ 不一致，即使它的方差趋零。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Adding a fixed nonzero constant shifts the probability limit, so the resulting estimator is inconsistent for its original target even if its variance vanishes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

在方差为 1 的 iid 正态模型中，$\bar X_n+5$ 的偏差为 5、方差为 $1/n$，MSE 为 $25+1/n$。它越来越集中在 $\theta+5$ 附近。若容许误差取 2，远离 $\theta$ 的概率趋于 1。对比[[一致不推出无偏]]中趋零的偏移。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The shifted sample mean $\bar X_n+5$ has bias five and MSE $25+1/n$. It concentrates at the wrong centre. Its probability of an error exceeding two tends to one, unlike the [[一致不推出无偏|vanishing-shift example]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
