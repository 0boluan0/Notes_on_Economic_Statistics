# 风险估计窗口及其自适应规则必须在最终评估期之前冻结，不能用同一时期同时选择并验证窗口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Estimation-window and adaptation rules must be frozen before final evaluation; the same period cannot both select and validate the window*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 窗口选择需要自己的验证数据
> 固定窗口长度、扩展窗口、日期加权和自适应触发规则都属于模型选择。它们可以在按时间排序的 validation origins 上比较，但在进入最终评估期之前必须冻结；否则最终评估期已经被用于调参。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fixed length, expanding windows, date weighting, and adaptive triggers are all model choices. They may be compared on time-ordered validation origins, but must be frozen before the final evaluation period begins; otherwise that period has already been used for tuning.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设一组历史预测起点用于选择窗口制度，随后保留一段从未参与选择的最终时期。每个起点只能使用当时可得的信息；窗口长度、衰减参数、缺失值处理和重新估计频率都应在该起点内部执行。确定方案后，最终时期只用于一次诚实的后续评估。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use one sequence of historical forecast origins to select the window design, then preserve a later period that has not influenced that choice. At every origin, window length, decay parameters, missing-data treatment, and re-estimation frequency must use only information then available. Once selected, the final period is reserved for honest subsequent evaluation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自适应窗口并不被禁止。可以预先规定“当某个只使用过去数据的变化检测量越过阈值时缩短窗口”，但检测量、阈值、候选窗口与冲突处理都要事先写清。若看到最终损失或 VaR 例外后才决定何时缩窗，就是用结果反向选择规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adaptive windows are allowed when the adaptation rule is specified in advance. A rule may shorten the window when a past-data change statistic crosses a fixed threshold, but the statistic, threshold, candidate windows, and conflict handling must be predetermined. Choosing when to shorten only after seeing final losses or VaR exceptions is outcome-driven tuning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看过最终时期后仍可继续研究，但该时期必须改称 validation data；若要重新声称最终样本外表现，就需要新的、未参与修订的后续时期。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Research may continue after the final period has been inspected, but that period then becomes validation data. A new untouched future period is required for another claim of final out-of-sample performance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 分析者试过 250、500 和 1000 日窗口，选择在 2024–2025 回测中例外最少的 500 日窗口，又用同一回测宣称该窗口已通过最终验证。问题是什么？
>
> **答案：** 2024–2025 已参与窗口选择，因此只是 validation period；不能再把同一批例外当作未使用的最终证据。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** An analyst tries 250-, 500-, and 1,000-day windows, chooses the 500-day window because it has the fewest exceptions in 2024–2025, and then calls that same backtest final validation. What is wrong?
>
> **Answer:** The 2024–2025 period influenced window selection and is therefore validation data, not untouched final evidence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- 冻结的是选择与适应规则，不是要求预测期内永远不更新数据或参数。
- 监管规定的窗口可以直接形成合规方案，但若另外比较多个内部实现，比较过程仍需与最终评估分开。
- 一次最终评估不证明窗口永久适用；后续制度变化仍需持续监控，但改变规则后要重新建立独立评估证据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The selection and adaptation rule is frozen; data and parameters may still update exactly as that rule prescribes.
- A regulatory window may define the compliant design directly, but comparing alternative internal implementations still requires separation from final evaluation.
- One final evaluation does not prove permanent suitability. Regime changes require ongoing monitoring, and a changed rule requires new independent evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Tashman, [*Out-of-sample tests of forecasting accuracy: an analysis and review*](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169207000000650)：支持按时间组织拟外样本评估、滚动与固定窗口设计，以及模型构造和最终结果评估需要区分的纪律。
- Hyndman & Athanasopoulos, [*Forecasting: Principles and Practice*, §5.10](https://otexts.com/fpp3/tscv.html)：支持 rolling forecasting origin 在每个起点只使用此前数据，并在时间序列上执行窗口化评估。
- [[滚动起点评估]]：核对本库采用的 validation origins 与最终 test period 分工；本卡只把该纪律具体应用于风险估计窗口。
