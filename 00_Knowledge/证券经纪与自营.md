# 证券经纪为客户代理交易而证券自营以机构自身账户承担交易头寸
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Brokerage executes as an agent for clients, whereas principal dealing takes positions in the firm's own account.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区分经纪与自营，要问“这笔交易是谁的账户、谁承担证券价格变化”。经纪商替客户寻找或执行交易，客户持有买到的头寸；自营商自己买入或卖出，成为交易的一方，并承担自身库存的盈亏。[[做市商]]持续提供买卖报价时，通常是在自营角色下提供流动性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ask whose account is used and who bears changes in the security's price. An agent arranges or executes a client's trade; a principal buys or sells for its own account and bears inventory gains or losses. A [[做市商|market maker]] ordinarily supplies liquidity in a principal capacity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一证券公司可以开展两种业务，因此不能只凭机构名称判定角色。甲要求券商买入 100 股：券商代甲在市场成交，是经纪；券商直接把自己库存的 100 股卖给甲，是自营成交。角色不同会改变收费、利益冲突与交易确认中需要核对的事项，但不意味着代理业务没有操作风险或自营业务不受客户保护规则约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One firm may perform both functions. Buying 100 shares in the market on A's behalf is agency execution; selling 100 shares from the firm's inventory to A is principal dealing. The distinction changes fees, conflicts and disclosures to check, without eliminating operational risk or applicable customer protections.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
