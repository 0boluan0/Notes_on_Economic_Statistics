# Smith 用熟练化、减少任务切换和机器改进解释分工如何提高劳动生产率

Smith 要解释的不是“人多所以产量大”，而是采用 [[分工]] 后，为什么同样人数可以生产更多。他提出三条相互配合的机制：反复做同一类工作使动作更熟练；少换工作场所和工具，减少转换中的时间损失；注意力集中在具体操作上，有助于发现省力的方法和改进机器。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith asks why the same number of workers can produce more after adopting [[分工|division of labour]], rather than why a larger workforce produces more. He identifies three complementary mechanisms: practice improves dexterity; fewer changes of location, tools and task save time; and attention to a particular operation helps reveal labour-saving methods and machinery.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设想一位工人每做一枚针都要依次拉丝、切断、磨尖、装针头。即使各个动作本身不变，来回换工具也会占用时间。如果几个人分别连续完成这些操作，转换次数就减少了。熟练化还可能缩短每次操作；机器改进则会改变完成操作的方法。这是三种不同的增产来源，不只是把已经存在的工作重新分配一遍。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine a worker drawing wire, cutting it, sharpening it and attaching a head for every pin. Even if each operation remains unchanged, switching tools takes time. Assigning workers to perform the operations repeatedly reduces switching. Practice may also shorten each operation, while improved machinery changes the method itself. These are distinct sources of higher output, not merely a relabelling of an unchanged process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Smith 描述的工场中，10 人一天合计生产约48,000枚大头针；按这个数量计算，人均约4,800枚。这个数字展示了他所说的“适当分工并结合操作”的效果。它不是把训练、工具和组织分别控制住的实验，因而不能据此把全部增产精确归给某一个机制，更不能把这个倍数套到所有行业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith describes ten workers producing roughly 48,000 pins in a day, or about 4,800 per worker at that output. The example illustrates the combined effect of divided and coordinated operations. It is not an experiment separately controlling training, equipment and organisation, so it cannot identify the exact contribution of one mechanism or establish a multiplier for every industry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

机器改进也不全来自操作机器的工人。Smith 同时提到专门的机器制造者，以及能够把不同事物联系起来的研究者。分工可以让生产和知识工作都更加专门化，但更细的分工仍受 [[分工的市场限制|交换范围]] 约束；专门熟练的提高与更广泛能力的发展也并不完全一致，见 [[分工的能力代价]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Machine improvements need not come from their operators. Smith also discusses specialist machine makers and researchers who connect different objects and ideas. Productive and intellectual work can both become more specialised, but finer division remains constrained by [[分工的市场限制|the scope for exchange]]. Greater specialised dexterity also need not mean broader intellectual development; see [[分工的能力代价|the capability costs of narrow division of labour]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Smith，[《国富论》第一篇第一章](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=4)，第3段的制针工场、第5–9段的三条机制及发明者来源：核对熟练、转换时间、机器改进和观察例的口径。约4,800枚/人日由48,000枚÷10人计算；逐枚换工具的叙述是用来解释机制的构造情境。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Book I, Chapter I, paragraph 3 supplies the pin-factory description; paragraphs 5–9 explain the three mechanisms and different sources of invention. The per-worker figure is calculated as 48,000 divided by ten. The tool-switching scenario is constructed to illustrate the mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
