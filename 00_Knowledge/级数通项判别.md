# The nth-term divergence test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*级数通项判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If running totals settle at a finite value, the change made by one more addition must approach zero. The **nth-term divergence test** turns this observation into a quick way to rule out convergence of an [[无穷级数|infinite series]]. It checks the individual term $a_n$, not the partial sum $S_n=a_1+\cdots+a_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果累计总量趋于一个有限数，再加一项造成的变化就必须趋于零。**级数通项判别**把这个观察变成快速排除[[无穷级数]]收敛的办法：检查单项 $a_n$，而不是直接计算部分和 $S_n=a_1+\cdots+a_n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, consider $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n/(n+1)$. The terms begin $1/2,2/3,3/4,\ldots$ and approach $1$, not $0$. Each addition remains substantial, so the total cannot settle. Indeed, every term is at least $1/2$, giving $S_N\ge N/2\to+\infty$. The general test reaches the divergence conclusion without needing this particular lower bound or a formula for $S_N$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，考察 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n/(n+1)$。各项从 $1/2,2/3,3/4,\ldots$ 开始，趋于 $1$ 而不是 $0$；每次仍增加相当大的量，所以累计值不可能稳定。这里甚至每项都至少为 $1/2$，从而 $S_N\ge N/2\to+\infty$。一般通项判别不必找到这个特殊下界，也不必求出 $S_N$ 的公式，就能判断发散。

> [!important] A necessary condition, used in reverse · 用必要条件排除收敛
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a series of finite real terms, convergence implies $a_n\to0$. Therefore, if $a_n$ does **not** tend to zero, the series diverges. This includes a nonzero limit, unbounded terms, or terms with no limit. No positivity or monotonicity assumption is required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每项均为有限实数的级数，收敛必然推出 $a_n\to0$。因此，若 $a_n$ **不趋于零**，级数就发散，包括趋于非零数、通项无界或通项根本没有极限的情况。不要求各项非负，也不要求单调。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why adjacent totals reveal the new term
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*相邻部分和之差就是新增项*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set $S_0=0$, the sum before any terms are added. Finite subtraction gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $S_0=0$，表示还未加入任何项时的总量。有限次相减得到

$$a_n=S_n-S_{n-1},\qquad n\ge1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now suppose the series converges to a finite $S$. Both $S_n$ and the one-step-shifted sequence $S_{n-1}$ approach $S$, so their difference approaches $S-S=0$. More explicitly, once both totals lie within $\varepsilon/2$ of $S$, the triangle inequality gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在假设级数收敛到有限数 $S$。$S_n$ 与错开一步的 $S_{n-1}$ 都趋于 $S$，所以差趋于 $S-S=0$。更明确地，只要两个部分和与 $S$ 的距离都小于 $\varepsilon/2$，三角不等式就给出

$$|a_n|=|S_n-S_{n-1}|\le|S_n-S|+|S_{n-1}-S|<\varepsilon.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This proves $a_n\to0$ by the definition of [[序列收敛|sequence convergence]]. The finiteness of $S$ is essential to this proof: subtracting two divergent expressions as “$\infty-\infty$” is not a limit argument. The divergence test is the contrapositive of what we have proved, so it cannot be reversed into a convergence test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这按[[序列收敛]]的定义证明了 $a_n\to0$。证明需要 $S$ 有限，不能把两个发散表达式写成“$\infty-\infty$”再相减。发散判别只是这条结论的逆否命题，不能反过来变成收敛判别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Small terms can still accumulate without bound
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*小项仍然可以累积无界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The harmonic series $1+1/2+1/3+\cdots$ has $a_n=1/n\to0$, but its totals grow without bound. To see why, collect finite blocks with twice as many terms each time:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

调和级数 $1+1/2+1/3+\cdots$ 的通项 $a_n=1/n\to0$，累计值却无界增长。把有限的项按每次翻倍的个数分组，就能看出原因：

$$H_8=1+\left(\frac12\right)
+\left(\frac13+\frac14\right)
+\left(\frac15+\frac16+\frac17+\frac18\right).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $H_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}1/n$ is the $N$th harmonic partial sum. Continue these blocks up to $2^m$, where $m$ is any positive integer. The block ending at $2^k$ has $2^{k-1}$ terms, each at least $1/2^k$, so its sum is at least $1/2$. Thus $H_{2^m}\ge1+m/2$. Given any desired total, enough such blocks exceed it; because every term is positive, all later partial sums exceed it too. Hence $H_N\to+\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $H_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}1/n$ 是第 $N$ 个调和部分和。按同一规则分组直到 $2^m$，其中 $m$ 为任意正整数。以 $2^k$ 结束的一组有 $2^{k-1}$ 项，每项至少为 $1/2^k$，所以整组至少贡献 $1/2$。因此 $H_{2^m}\ge1+m/2$。给定任何想超过的总量，累积足够多组就会超过；由于每项为正，所有更晚的部分和也会超过它。于是 $H_N\to+\infty$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The more quantitative [[调和数对数增长|harmonic-number bound]] gives $H_N\ge\ln(N+1)\to\infty$, where $\ln$ is the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]]. Both arguments describe the accumulation, not just the size of one late term. In contrast, $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}2^{-n}=1$ is a convergent [[几何级数|geometric series]], also with terms tending to zero. Thus the same term limit $0$ is compatible with either result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更定量的[[调和数对数增长|调和数界]]给出 $H_N\ge\ln(N+1)\to\infty$，其中 $\ln$ 是[[自然对数]]。两种证明检查的都是累积方式，而不只是某个晚期项的大小。相比之下，$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}2^{-n}=1$ 是收敛的[[几何级数]]，通项同样趋于零。因此，相同的通项极限 $0$ 可以对应完全不同的敛散结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What to conclude after checking the terms
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*检查通项之后能说什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the terms fail to tend to zero, the test is decisive. For example, $a_n=(-1)^{n-1}$ alternates between $1$ and $-1$, so the series diverges. Its totals alternate between $1$ and $0$: the conclusion “diverges” does not by itself mean “tends to $+\infty$.” This example also shows why signed terms are allowed in the test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若通项不趋于零，判别就已给出确定结论。例如 $a_n=(-1)^{n-1}$ 在 $1$ 与 $-1$ 之间交替，所以级数发散；它的部分和却只在 $1$ 与 $0$ 之间交替。因此，“发散”本身不等于“趋于 $+\infty$”，这个例子也说明判别允许有正有负的项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $a_n\to0$, record only that the necessary condition holds. A [[级数判别法选择|suitable further convergence argument]] is still needed. Failure to calculate the term limit is not the same as proving that it fails to be zero. The parallel warning for integrals is [[趋零不推尾积分收敛|a vanishing integrand with an infinite accumulated tail]]; one must check the correct accumulated object in each setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $a_n\to0$，只能记录必要条件已满足，仍需[[级数判别法选择|进一步适用的收敛论证]]。“没算出通项极限”也不同于“已证明通项不趋于零”。积分中相应的提醒是[[趋零不推尾积分收敛|函数趋零仍可有无限尾部累积]]；每种问题都要检查它真正累加的对象。

> [!question]- What has been proved by $a_n\to0$? · 通项趋零已经证明了什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It has ruled out this particular reason for divergence. It has not proved convergence or divergence of the series. The harmonic and geometric examples above have the same term limit and opposite answers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 它排除了这里这一种发散理由，却没有证明级数收敛，也没有证明级数发散。上面的调和与几何例子，通项极限相同，结论相反。
