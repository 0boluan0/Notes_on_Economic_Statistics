# Multiple and multivariate regression
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*多解释变量回归与多响应回归*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Many variables” can mean two different things in a regression: many pieces of information used to predict, or many outcomes to be predicted. **Multiple regression** usually means one response and several predictors. **Multivariate regression**, in this series of cards, means several responses measured on the same observational units. Count the outcomes before deciding which meaning is intended.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归中的“变量多”可能有两种含义：用于预测的信息很多，或者想要预测的结果很多。**Multiple regression** 通常指一个响应、多个预测变量；本系列的 **multivariate regression** 指同一批观测单位具有多个响应。判断术语前，要先数清结果变量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A response is the outcome whose mean or prediction we want to describe. A predictor is information placed on the other side of the model to help describe that outcome. These roles come from the research question, not the variable's name. A reading score could be a predictor of a mathematics score in one study, or one of two responses in another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

响应是我们要描述其均值或预测值的结果；预测变量是放在模型另一侧、用于描述结果的信息。这些角色由研究问题决定，不由变量名字决定：阅读成绩既可以在一项研究中预测数学成绩，也可以在另一项研究中成为两个响应之一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same students, two different questions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*同一批学生，两种不同问题*

> [!example] Several predictors for one score · 多个预测变量，一个成绩
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For student $i$, let $y_i$ be mathematics score, $t_i$ study time, $a_i$ attendance and $b_i$ a prior assessment score. A multiple linear regression is
> $$y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1t_i+\beta_2a_i+\beta_3b_i+u_i.$$
> There is one response, three predictors and an intercept $\beta_0$. Write $X$ for all predictor information arranged in the design matrix. The model's [[条件期望|conditional mean]] is the expression before $u_i$ when $E(u_i\mid X)=0$. Each student contributes one response value; having three predictors does not make that response multivariate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对学生 $i$，令 $y_i$ 为数学成绩，$t_i$ 为学习时间，$a_i$ 为出勤，$b_i$ 为既往测验成绩。上述 multiple linear regression 有一个响应、三个预测变量，以及截距 $\beta_0$。用 $X$ 表示按设计矩阵排列的全部预测信息。在 $E(u_i\mid X)=0$ 时，$u_i$ 之前的表达式给出[[条件期望|条件均值]]。每名学生只贡献一个响应值，三个预测变量不会把结果变成多响应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now ask about both mathematics and reading, using only study time. A [[多响应线性回归|multivariate linear regression]] may specify
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在改为同时研究数学与阅读，只使用学习时间作为预测变量。一个[[多响应线性回归]]可以写为

$$
Y_{i1}=\alpha_0+\alpha_1t_i+U_{i1},\qquad
Y_{i2}=\gamma_0+\gamma_1t_i+U_{i2}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The labels $1,2$ select the two responses. Each has its own intercept and slope; sharing $t_i$ does not force $\alpha_1=\gamma_1$. Each student now contributes a response pair. One non-intercept predictor is already enough for this to be a multivariate response model. Adding attendance and prior assessment would give several predictors **and** several responses; the two counts are separate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下标 $1,2$ 选择两个响应，每个都有自己的截距和斜率；共用 $t_i$ 并不要求 $\alpha_1=\gamma_1$。每名学生现在贡献一对响应值。因此，只有一个非截距预测变量，也足以构成多响应模型；再加入出勤与既往测验，就同时具有多个预测变量和多个响应。这是两项独立的计数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Matrix dimensions reveal the distinction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*矩阵尺寸直接显示区别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[多元数据矩阵|data-matrix convention]] places observations in rows. Let $n$ be their number, $q$ the number of responses, and $k$ the number of encoded design columns, including an intercept column if used. Then the shared-design equation is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[多元数据矩阵]]把观测排在行上。令 $n$ 为观测数，$q$ 为响应数，$k$ 为编码后的设计列数，有截距时也把截距列计入。共享设计方程为

$$Y_{n\times q}=X_{n\times k}B_{k\times q}+U_{n\times q}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$X$ contains predictor information, $B$ holds one coefficient column per response, and $U$ holds the deviations from the modeled means. For $80$ students, the two examples have the following shapes. An intercept adds a column to $X$, not a response column to $Y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$X$ 装预测信息，$B$ 为每个响应保存一列系数，$U$ 装相对于模型均值的偏移。若有 $80$ 名学生，前面两个例子的尺寸如下。截距在 $X$ 中增加一列，不会给 $Y$ 增加一个响应。

| Model / 模型 | Responses / 响应数 | Non-intercept predictors / 非截距预测变量数 | $Y$ | $X$ | $B$ |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| One score / 一个成绩 | $1$ | $3$ | $80\times1$ | $80\times4$ | $4\times1$ |
| Two scores / 两个成绩 | $2$ | $1$ | $80\times2$ | $80\times2$ | $2\times2$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The general matrix formula also works when $q=1$; that algebraic specialization is the single-response case. “Multivariate” usually highlights $q>1$, not a different rule of matrix multiplication. Encoded columns can include transformations or group indicators, so the number of named predictors and the number of design columns need not coincide. The response count still comes from $Y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通用矩阵公式在 $q=1$ 时也成立，此时退化为单响应情形。“Multivariate”通常强调 $q>1$，并不是使用另一种矩阵乘法规则。设计列还可以包括变量变换或组别指示，因此“有几个命名的预测变量”与“有几列设计”未必相同；响应个数仍然看 $Y$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why putting responses together can matter
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把响应放在一起，有什么作用？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mathematics and reading errors from the same student may move together. Their [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] records both individual variances and their cross-covariance. A joint question—whether study time has zero slope for **both** responses—concerns $(\alpha_1,\gamma_1)$ together. This differs from asking two separate one-response questions and treating their answers as automatically one calibrated test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一名学生的数学与阅读误差可能共同波动，其[[协方差矩阵]]同时记录各自方差与交叉协方差。一个联合问题——学习时间对**两个**响应的斜率是否都为零——需要一起考虑 $(\alpha_1,\gamma_1)$。它不同于分别提出两个单响应问题，再把答案自动当成一个已经正确校准的检验。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the same observations, a full-column-rank design (its columns are [[线性无关|linearly independent]]), ordinary unweighted least squares and unrestricted coefficient columns, [[共享设计的逐响应OLS|separate OLS fits give the same coefficient estimates as the multivariate fit]]. With a rank-deficient design, the fitted values still agree, but the [[最小二乘解唯一性|coefficients need not be unique]]. Joint inference still needs the relevant response dependence and sampling assumptions. Correlated responses are common, but correlation is not part of the definition of “multivariate”; two independent outcomes are still two responses. Likewise, the name alone does not impose a Gaussian distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在相同观测、共同满列秩设计（各列[[线性无关]]）、普通未加权最小二乘和无跨响应系数约束下，[[共享设计的逐响应OLS|逐响应分别拟合与多响应拟合给出相同系数估计]]。设计秩亏时，拟合值仍然相同，但[[最小二乘解唯一性|系数未必唯一]]。联合推断仍需相应的响应依赖和抽样假设。响应相关很常见，却不是“多响应”的定义条件：两个相互独立的结果仍是两个响应；这个名字本身也不会强加 Gaussian 分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[MANOVA|MANOVA]] uses a multivariate linear-model setup to compare group mean vectors, encoding group membership in the design. General multivariate regression can also include continuous predictors. Software may expose its joint-hypothesis machinery under a MANOVA label even in that broader setting; inspect the specified model and hypothesis rather than inferring them solely from the command name.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[MANOVA]]使用多响应线性模型比较组均值向量，将组别编码进设计矩阵。一般多响应回归也可以包含连续预测变量；软件可能在更宽泛的情形下，仍以 MANOVA 命令提供联合假设检验。应查看实际模型和假设，不能只从命令名称推断。

> [!tip] Read the course's variables, not only its title · 读课程变量，不只看标题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The Chinese term used in the course title often names single-response multiple regression. The permanent [[01_Math/04_多元统计分析/07_多元线性回归multivariate linear regression.md#1.1. 模型形式|Chapter7 model]] uses an $n\times1$ response and an $n\times(k+1)$ design, with $k$ there counting predictors before the intercept. It is therefore chiefly a multiple-regression chapter, despite its English title. Its coefficient interpretation belongs to [[多元回归系数|multiple-regression coefficients]]; the several-response extension belongs to [[多响应线性回归|the multivariate model card]]. Both usages are readable once their dimensions are explicit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 中文“多元线性回归”常指单响应 multiple regression。永久[[01_Math/04_多元统计分析/07_多元线性回归multivariate linear regression.md#1.1. 模型形式|课程第7章模型]]使用 $n\times1$ 响应、$n\times(k+1)$ 设计；其中 $k$ 是不含截距的预测变量个数。因此，该章主要讲多解释变量回归，不能仅凭英文标题判为多响应。其系数含义见[[多元回归系数]]，多个响应的扩展见[[多响应线性回归]]；明确维度后，两种用法就不会混淆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Penn State STAT501, Lesson5, overview and §§5.1,5.3–5.4](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat501/Lesson05): one response with several predictors, an intercept, and the single-response matrix formulation. Its course assumptions are not treated as definitions of the terminology.
- [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/MIT_OCW_18.06SC_PDF/05_Session_Summaries/MIT18_06SCF11_Ses2.3sum.pdf#page=3|MIT 18.06SC Session2.3, p.3]]: full column rank makes the normal-equation coefficients unique; without it, fitted values and coefficient uniqueness must be distinguished.
- [statsmodels, *Multivariate Linear Model — MultivariateLS*, introduction and `mv_test`](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/examples/notebooks/generated/multivariate_ls.html): multiple responses, shared predictors, matrix shapes, separate OLS estimates and joint hypotheses; its MANOVA interface also allows continuous predictors.
- [[01_Math/04_多元统计分析/07_多元线性回归multivariate linear regression.md#1.1. 模型形式|Permanent course Chapter7 §§1.1–1.3]]: the local single-response convention and intercept count. The two student models and shape comparison are explicit examples of those definitions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Penn State 第5课概述、5.1及5.3–5.4节核对单响应多预测变量、截距和矩阵形式，没有把该课的分布假设当作术语定义。
- MIT 18.06SC 第2.3节第3页核对满列秩与正规方程系数唯一性；秩亏时应区分唯一拟合值与不唯一系数。
- statsmodels 文档核对多响应共享预测变量、维度、逐响应 OLS 等价和联合检验，并核对其 MANOVA 接口可含连续预测变量。
- 永久课程第7章1.1–1.3节核对本地单响应约定与截距计数；两个学生模型及尺寸比较直接按定义构造。
