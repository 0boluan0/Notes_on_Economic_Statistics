# 上市开放式基金是在交易所买卖份额并保留按约申购赎回渠道的开放式基金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A listed open-ended fund combines exchange trading of units with contractual subscription and redemption channels*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上市开放式基金（LOF）在中国基金市场中指可上市交易的[[开放式基金]]：投资者既可通过交易所买卖已有份额，也可按基金与销售渠道的规则申购、赎回。以深证 LOF 为例，合资格场内证券经营机构可办理相关交易或申赎，银行、证券公司和基金公司直销等场外渠道也可办理申赎。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the Chinese fund market, a listed open-ended fund (LOF) is an [[开放式基金|open-end fund]] whose existing units can trade on an exchange while subscriptions and redemptions remain available under fund and distribution-channel rules. For a Shenzhen LOF, qualified exchange-side securities firms can handle the relevant trading or dealing activities, while off-exchange channels can include banks, securities firms, and direct fund sales.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“买卖”与“申赎”不是同一订单。买卖按市场成交价格转让份额，可能出现[[基金折溢价]]；申赎按适用的[[基金份额净值]]和费用等合同规则计算新增份额或赎回金额。上市并不把申赎价格改成盘中撮合价格，也不把每笔场内买卖变成基金新发或注销份额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trading and subscription/redemption are different orders. Trading transfers units at market prices, potentially with a [[基金折溢价|premium or discount]]. Subscriptions and redemptions determine new units or proceeds using the applicable [[基金份额净值|NAV]], fees, and contractual rules. Listing neither turns dealing prices into intraday matched prices nor makes every exchange trade create or cancel units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，适用净值为 1 元而可买市价为 0.98 元时，买 1 万份的成交金额为 9,800 元；这并不保证立刻按 1 元赎回并净赚 200 元。还须检查持有份额所在登记系统、申赎开放安排、必要的转托管、适用估值时点与费用，等待期间价格也可能变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the applicable NAV is 1 and an executable purchase price is 0.98, buying 10,000 units costs 9,800 before fees. This does not guarantee immediate redemption at 1 and a net gain of 200. The registration system, dealing availability, any required custody transfer, applicable valuation time, and fees matter, and prices may change while waiting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

LOF 与 [[ETF]] 都可能有交易所市场和申赎渠道，但不是同义词。应分别核对各产品的申赎资格、金额或单位、现金或实物对价以及登记交收安排；不能把美国 ETF 的 AP 批量机制直接套给所有中国 LOF，也不能仅因能上市就断言该基金追踪指数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An LOF and an [[ETF]] can both have exchange trading and primary dealing channels, but they are not synonyms. Eligibility, cash amounts or unit sizes, cash or in-kind consideration, and registration and settlement must be checked separately. The U.S. AP block mechanism cannot be assigned to all Chinese LOFs, nor does listing imply index tracking.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
