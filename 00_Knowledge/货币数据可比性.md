# 跨国跨期比较货币数据必须先固定口径发行主体地域日期和调整方式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cross-country and intertemporal monetary comparisons must first fix the measure, issuer, geography, date, and adjustment method*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子程序
> 引用或比较任何货币数字前，至少写清：统计口径及成分、每项资产的发行或负债主体、持有人部门与地域、观察日期或期间、单位与频率、是否季调，以及定义修订或序列断点。缺一项都可能把分类差异误写成经济变化。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Before citing or comparing any monetary number, state at least the measure and components, the issuer or obligor for each asset, holder sector and geography, observation date or period, units and frequency, seasonal-adjustment status, and any definitional revision or series break. Omitting one can turn a classification difference into a false economic change.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可操作顺序是：先打开发布机构的定义与脚注；再核对系列 notes 和最后更新时间；然后只在相容区间计算增长率或比率；若定义改变，分段报告、使用官方回溯序列或明确说明不可直接比较。名称相同从来不是可比性的充分证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An operational sequence is: open the publisher's definition and footnotes; check series notes and the last update; calculate growth rates or ratios only over compatible spans; and, if the definition changes, report segments, use an official backcast, or state that direct comparison is invalid. Identical names are never sufficient evidence of comparability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- “美国 M1”必须同时说明版本和日期；“M1”本身不足以固定口径。
- monetary base 的发行主体和持有人集合不同于 M2，不能只因单位都是货币就直接相减解释。
- 实时发布值可能修订；观察日期、发布日期和检索日期是三个不同时间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- “U.S. M1” still requires a definition vintage and date; “M1” alone does not fix the measure.
- The monetary base has different issuers and holder sectors from M2, so a subtraction is not economically interpretable merely because both use currency units.
- Real-time observations may be revised; observation date, release date, and retrieval date are distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两国都公布“M2/GDP”，为什么还不能直接比较？
>
> **答案：** 必须先核对两国 M2 的成分、持有人与地域覆盖、GDP 期间、季调和日期；相同标签与比率形式不保证分子分母口径相容。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Two countries both publish “M2/GDP.” Why is that not yet enough for comparison?
>
> **Answer:** Check each M2's components, holder and geographic coverage, the GDP period, seasonal adjustment, and dates. Identical labels and ratio forms do not guarantee compatible numerators and denominators.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Federal Reserve Board, H.6 Money Stock Measures](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h6/current/)：核对当前脚注、频率、季调与发布更新时间。
- [FRED, M1SL series notes](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/M1SL)：核对 observation、units、frequency、update 和 series notes 是不同字段。
- [Federal Reserve Board, H.6 Technical Q&As](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h6/h6_technical_qa.htm)：核对定义变更必须作为断点处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The current H.6 release was checked for definitions, frequency, seasonal adjustment, and release date.
- FRED M1SL was checked for separate observation, unit, frequency, update, and series-note fields.
- The H.6 Technical Q&As were checked for the need to treat definitional changes as breaks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
