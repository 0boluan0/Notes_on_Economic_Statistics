# Variance under affine transformations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*方差的仿射变换*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **affine transformation** changes a numerical variable by multiplying every value by a fixed number and then adding a fixed number. For example, $Y=3X-10$ triples each value of $X$ and then subtracts ten. To find the new [[方差|variance]], the key is to track distances from the mean: a shift moves the values and their mean together, while scaling changes those distances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**仿射变换**就是把一个数值变量的每个取值乘以固定数，再加上固定数。例如，$Y=3X-10$ 把 $X$ 的每个值乘以三，再减去十。要判断新的[[方差]]，关键是跟踪“离均值多远”：平移会让取值与均值一起移动，缩放则会改变这些距离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $X$ has finite variance. Let $a$ and $b$ be fixed real numbers, with $a$ the multiplier and $b$ the shift, and define $Y=aX+b$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $X$ 的方差有限。设 $a,b$ 是固定实数，其中 $a$ 为乘数，$b$ 为平移量，并定义 $Y=aX+b$，则

> [!abstract] The multiplier is squared · 乘数要平方
> $$\operatorname{Var}(Y)=\operatorname{Var}(aX+b)=a^2\operatorname{Var}(X).$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The shift $b$ does not appear. The multiplier enters as $a^2$ because variance averages **squared** deviations from the mean.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 结果中没有平移量 $b$。乘数以 $a^2$ 出现，因为方差平均的是偏离均值的**平方**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Watch the values and the centre move together
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同时看取值和中心怎样移动*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X$ take $2$ or $6$, each with probability $1/2$. Its mean is $4$ and its variance is $4$. Apply $Y=3X-10$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

让 $X$ 以各 $1/2$ 的概率取 $2$ 或 $6$，均值为 $4$，方差为 $4$。现在应用 $Y=3X-10$：

| Quantity · 量 | Original $X$ · 原变量 | Transformed $Y$ · 变换后 |
|---|---|---|
| Possible values · 取值 | $2,\ 6$ | $-4,\ 8$ |
| Mean · 均值 | $4$ | $2$ |
| Deviations from the mean · 偏离均值 | $-2,\ 2$ | $-6,\ 6$ |
| Variance · 方差 | $4$ | $36$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The final subtraction moves the two values and their mean by the same amount, so it leaves the deviations unchanged. Multiplication by three triples each deviation, and squaring makes their average nine times as large: $36=3^2\times4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后的减十同时移动了两个取值和均值，因此不改变偏离均值的量。乘三使每个偏离变成三倍，平方后平均便变成九倍：$36=3^2\times4$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A negative multiplier also obeys the rule. Multiplying by $-3$ reverses which side of the mean a value lies on, but still triples its distance, so the variance is again multiplied by nine. If $a=0$, then $Y=b$ is constant and its variance is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

负乘数也遵循这条规则。乘以 $-3$ 会把取值翻到均值的另一侧，但距离仍变成三倍，因此方差同样变成九倍。如果 $a=0$，则 $Y=b$ 是常数，方差为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The calculation follows the same idea
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*公式推导就是把刚才的过程写出来*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $\mu=E[X]$ for the mean of $X$. By [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]], the new mean is $E[Y]=a\mu+b$. Subtracting this mean from $Y$ cancels the shift:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $\mu=E[X]$ 表示 $X$ 的均值。根据[[期望线性性]]，新均值为 $E[Y]=a\mu+b$。从 $Y$ 中减去新均值，平移量便抵消了：

$$Y-E[Y]=(aX+b)-(a\mu+b)=a(X-\mu).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Variance now squares this deviation and takes its expectation. Since $a^2$ is constant, it can be taken outside the expectation:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方差接下来对偏离平方并取期望。由于 $a^2$ 是常数，可以把它移到期望之外：

$$\operatorname{Var}(Y)=E[a^2(X-\mu)^2]
=a^2E[(X-\mu)^2]=a^2\operatorname{Var}(X).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Units and standard deviation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*换单位时，方差与标准差缩放不同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Standard deviation is the square root of variance. Writing $\sigma_X$ and $\sigma_Y$ for the two standard deviations gives $\sigma_Y=|a|\sigma_X$, where $|a|$ is the nonnegative magnitude of the multiplier. The absolute value is needed because a standard deviation cannot be negative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准差是方差的平方根。用 $\sigma_X,\sigma_Y$ 表示两个标准差，就有 $\sigma_Y=|a|\sigma_X$，其中 $|a|$ 是乘数的非负大小。这里要取绝对值，因为标准差不能为负。

> [!example] Seconds to milliseconds · 从秒改成毫秒
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $X$ records time in seconds and $Y=1000X$ records the same time in milliseconds, the numerical variance is multiplied by $1000^2$. Thus $4\,\mathrm{s}^2$ becomes $4{,}000{,}000\,\mathrm{ms}^2$, while a standard deviation of $2\,\mathrm{s}$ becomes $2000\,\mathrm{ms}$. These describe the same physical spread in different units.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $X$ 用秒记录时间，$Y=1000X$ 用毫秒记录同一时间，方差的数值就要乘以 $1000^2$。因此 $4\,\mathrm{s}^2$ 变成 $4{,}000{,}000\,\mathrm{ms}^2$，而标准差 $2\,\mathrm{s}$ 变成 $2000\,\mathrm{ms}$。它们描述的是同一个实际散布程度，只是单位不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rule concerns fixed transformations of one variable. It does not justify adding the variances of arbitrary summands. For instance, $X+X=2X$ has variance $4\operatorname{Var}(X)$, not $2\operatorname{Var}(X)$. For a sum involving two variables, use the [[和的方差协方差项|formula that includes their covariance]] to account for their joint variation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条规则处理的是一个变量的固定变换，不能据此把任意相加项的方差直接相加。例如，$X+X=2X$ 的方差为 $4\operatorname{Var}(X)$，而不是 $2\operatorname{Var}(X)$。若要处理两个变量的和，应使用包含二者[[和的方差协方差项|协方差项的公式]]，把它们共同变动的部分计入。
