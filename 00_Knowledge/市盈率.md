# 市盈率是普通股市场价格与明确期间每股收益口径的比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The price-to-earnings ratio compares an ordinary share's market price with EPS on a specified period and measurement basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市盈率表示所选股价相当于多少倍每股会计收益：$PE=P/EPS$，其中 $EPS\ne0$。报出“20 倍”之前，要说明盈利是最近年度、过去十二个月还是下一年预测，以及使用[[基本每股收益]]、[[稀释每股收益]]还是有明确调节的盈利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
P/E expresses the share price as a multiple of accounting EPS: $PE=P/EPS$ for non-zero EPS. A figure such as 20 times requires a stated fiscal-year, trailing, or forecast period and a choice of [[基本每股收益|basic]], [[稀释每股收益|diluted]], or explicitly adjusted earnings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如当前价格 40 元，过去十二个月基本 EPS 为 2 元，则历史基本市盈率为 20 倍；下一年预测 EPS 为 2.5 元，对应预测市盈率 16 倍。两者并不矛盾：后一数值依赖预测实现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A price of 40 and trailing basic EPS of 2 give a historical basic P/E of 20. Forecast EPS of 2.5 gives a forward P/E of 16. These are different questions, and the latter depends on the forecast being realized.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

20 倍不是“保证二十年回本”：利润不一定全部分红，也不保证不变。比较高低时，还要看增长、风险、利润持续性及会计口径；低市盈率可能对应周期高点或预期利润下滑。零盈利时不定义，负盈利时负倍数不能沿用“越低越便宜”的普通排序。将它改算为市值／总利润时，股数与利润归属也须匹配，不能混入少数股东利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A P/E of 20 does not guarantee a twenty-year payback: earnings need not remain constant or be fully distributed. Growth, risk, persistence, and accounting basis matter; a low multiple may reflect peak-cycle earnings or expected decline. Zero and negative earnings do not support ordinary cheapness ranking. An aggregate market-cap-to-profit expression also requires matched shares and attribution, excluding non-controlling profit from an ordinary-parent multiple.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
