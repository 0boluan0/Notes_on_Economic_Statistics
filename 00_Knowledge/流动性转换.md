# 流动性转换向客户发行易变现索取权并持有难变现资产
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Liquidity transformation issues readily redeemable claims while holding less-saleable assets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子机制
> 中介向客户提供能随时支付、赎回或在较稳定价格下变现的索取权，同时把资产配置到定制贷款、抵押贷款或交易不活跃的证券。客户获得的流动性高于其按比例直接持有底层资产时的流动性；中介则承担在赎回到来时筹集现金的责任。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An intermediary offers claims that customers can use for payment, redeem, or sell at a relatively stable price, while investing in customised loans, mortgages, or thinly traded securities. Customers receive more liquidity than they would from proportionate direct holdings of the underlying assets; the intermediary assumes responsibility for raising cash when redemption arrives.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行活期存款是典型例子：客户期待按面值使用，银行资产中的贷款却不能像现金一样立刻出售。开放式基金也可能提供每日赎回，同时持有成交稀少的公司债或银行贷款。两者合同保障、净值规则和公共安全网不同，但都可能发生“负债端比资产端更容易变现”的结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A demand deposit is a standard example: customers expect to use it at par, while bank loans cannot be sold instantly like cash. An open-end fund may likewise offer daily redemption while holding thinly traded corporate bonds or bank loans. Their contractual protections, valuation rules, and public backstops differ, but both can make investor claims more liquid than portfolio assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这项服务依赖常态下并非所有客户同时要现金、资产能产生现金流、以及中介持有缓冲或能借到短期资金。它没有把底层资产“物理上变流动”；它把变现的不便和时点风险集中到中介资产负债表上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The service relies on withdrawals not all arriving at once in ordinary times, on cash flows from assets, and on buffers or short-term borrowing capacity. It does not physically make the underlying assets liquid. It concentrates the inconvenience and timing risk of liquidation on the intermediary's balance sheet.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 流动性转换与期限转换相关但不等同；完整区分见 [[期限转换不等于流动性转换]]。
- “可赎回”不一定承诺固定面值。浮动净值基金可能把资产价格变化传给投资者，银行存款则通常有不同的面值承诺和制度保护。
- 市场流动性在压力期会内生恶化；依据平静期成交量判断能否快速出售，可能严重低估赎回风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Liquidity transformation is related to but distinct from [[期限转换|maturity transformation]]. The former concerns the price impact of obtaining cash now; the latter concerns contractual payment dates.
- “Redeemable” does not necessarily mean redeemable at a fixed par value. A floating-net-asset-value fund can pass asset-price changes to investors, whereas bank deposits carry different par promises and institutional protections.
- Market liquidity can deteriorate endogenously under stress. Normal-times trading volume can seriously understate the difficulty of meeting redemptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么一只持有十年期国债的基金，可能比一只持有三个月定制贷款的基金更容易满足今日赎回？
>
> **答案：** 到期更远不必然更难变现。若十年期国债有深厚市场，而三个月贷款缺少买家，前者的市场流动性反而更高。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why might a fund holding ten-year government bonds meet today's redemption more easily than one holding customised three-month loans?
>
> **Answer:** A later maturity does not necessarily mean lower saleability. Deep trading can make the ten-year government bond more liquid than a short customised loan with few buyers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
