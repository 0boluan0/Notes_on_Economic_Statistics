# Higher derivatives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*高阶导数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[导数|derivative]] tells us how fast a quantity changes. A **second derivative** asks how fast that rate itself changes. Repeating the question gives higher derivatives. For motion, position leads to velocity, and velocity leads to acceleration; each step answers a new question about the same motion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[导数]]告诉我们一个量变化得多快，**二阶导数**进一步问这个变化率本身变化得多快。继续重复这个问题，就得到高阶导数。对运动而言，位置求导得到速度，速度求导得到加速度；每一阶回答同一段运动中的不同问题。

> [!example] Position, velocity, acceleration · 位置、速度、加速度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose position in metres is numerically $s(t)=t^3$ when time $t$ is measured in seconds. By the [[整数幂求导|integer power rule]],
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设以秒计的时间为 $t$，以米计的位置数值满足 $s(t)=t^3$。由[[整数幂求导]]，
>
> $$s'(t)=3t^2,\qquad s''(t)=6t,\qquad s^{(3)}(t)=6.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $t=2$, position is $8$ m, velocity is $12$ m/s, and acceleration is $12$ m/s². The third derivative is $6$ m/s³, the rate at which acceleration changes. Although two numerical values happen to equal $12$, their units and meanings differ.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $t=2$ 处，位置为 $8$ 米，速度为每秒 $12$ 米，加速度为 $12$ 米/秒²。三阶导数为 $6$ 米/秒³，描述加速度的变化率。虽然其中两个数值恰好都是 $12$，单位与含义却不同。

## Read the order as repeated operations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把阶数读成求导操作的次数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $f'$ is itself differentiable, define $f''=(f')'$. If $f''$ is differentiable, define $f^{(3)}=(f'')'$, and continue. For a positive integer $n$, the standard notations are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $f'$ 本身可导，就定义 $f''=(f')'$；如果 $f''$ 也可导，就定义 $f^{(3)}=(f'')'$，如此继续。对正整数 $n$，常用记号为

$$f^{(n)}(x)=D^nf(x)=\frac{d^nf}{dx^n}(x).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $D$ means “differentiate with respect to $x$”, and $D^n$ means apply this operation $n$ times. For example, $d^2f/dx^2$ abbreviates $d(df/dx)/dx$; it is not a quotient of squared ordinary numbers. The parentheses in $f^{(n)}$ distinguish derivative order from a power of the function. For $f(x)=x^3$, the second derivative is $6x$, while the squared function is $f(x)^2=x^6$ and the squared first derivative is $[f'(x)]^2=9x^4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $D$ 表示“对 $x$ 求导”，$D^n$ 表示把这项操作做 $n$ 次。例如 $d^2f/dx^2$ 是 $d(df/dx)/dx$ 的简写，不是两个普通数平方后的商。$f^{(n)}$ 中的括号把导数阶数与函数值的乘方区分开。若 $f(x)=x^3$，二阶导数为 $6x$，而函数的平方为 $f(x)^2=x^6$，一阶导数的平方为 $[f'(x)]^2=9x^4$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f$ has units $Y$ and its input has units $X$, each differentiation divides the units by $X$, so $f^{(n)}$ has units $Y/X^n$. For an ordinary graph, $f''$ describes the change in slope: positive $f''$ throughout an interval makes the slope increase there. This is the information used in [[二次近似|quadratic approximation]], where the second derivative supplies the quadratic correction to a tangent-line approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 的单位为 $Y$，输入的单位为 $X$，每求一次导数就多除以一个 $X$，所以 $f^{(n)}$ 的单位为 $Y/X^n$。对一般图像，$f''$ 描述斜率怎样变化：若一个区间内处处有 $f''>0$，斜率就在该区间内递增。[[二次近似]]正是利用这类信息，在切线近似上加上由二阶导数决定的二次修正。

> [!warning] One derivative does not promise the next · 一阶存在不保证下一阶存在
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider $f(x)=x|x|$, which equals $x^2$ for $x\ge0$ and $-x^2$ for $x<0$. Away from zero, differentiation gives $f'(x)=2|x|$. At zero, the difference quotient is $h|h|/h=|h|\to0$, so the same formula holds there too. Thus $f$ is differentiable everywhere. But at zero the difference quotient for $f'$ is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑 $f(x)=x|x|$：在 $x\ge0$ 时等于 $x^2$，在 $x<0$ 时等于 $-x^2$。非零处求导得到 $f'(x)=2|x|$。零点的差商为 $h|h|/h=|h|\to0$，所以这一公式在零点也成立；$f$ 因而处处可导。可是 $f'$ 在零点的差商为
>
> $$\frac{f'(h)-f'(0)}h=\frac{2|h|}h
> =\begin{cases}2,&h>0,\\-2,&h<0.\end{cases}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The two sides disagree, so $f''(0)$ does not exist. Having a derivative, or even having a continuous first derivative, does not establish the next order. Check each required order before using a higher-derivative formula.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 左右两侧不同，因此 $f''(0)$ 不存在。有一阶导数，甚至一阶导数连续，也不能保证下一阶存在。使用高阶导数公式以前，必须检查所需阶数。

## Patterns can save repeated work
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*识别规律可以减少重复计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[正弦余弦求导|sine and cosine derivatives]], with angles in radians, give a four-step cycle:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在弧度制下，[[正弦余弦求导]]给出四步循环：

$$D\sin x=\cos x,\quad D^2\sin x=-\sin x,
\quad D^3\sin x=-\cos x,\quad D^4\sin x=\sin x.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A polynomial behaves differently. For a positive integer $n$, repeatedly differentiating $x^n$ multiplies by $n,n-1,\ldots,1$, so $D^nx^n=n!$, where $n!=n(n-1)\cdots1$. The next derivative and all later ones are zero by the [[导数线性法则|constant rule]]. When the function is a product, the [[高阶乘积法则|higher-order product rule]] organizes the repeated differentiations. These patterns simplify calculation; the definition remains successive differentiation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多项式则有另一种规律。对正整数 $n$，连续求导 $x^n$ 会依次乘上 $n,n-1,\ldots,1$，因此 $D^nx^n=n!$，其中 $n!=n(n-1)\cdots1$。由[[导数线性法则|常数法则]]，下一阶及更高阶都为零。如果函数是乘积，[[高阶乘积法则]]能整理重复求导产生的项。这些规律让计算更省事，但高阶导数的定义始终是逐次求导。
