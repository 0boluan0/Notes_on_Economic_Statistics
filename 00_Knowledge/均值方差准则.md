# 均值方差准则是只以期望收益与收益方差评价可行组合的决策规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The mean–variance criterion evaluates feasible portfolios using only expected return and return variance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值方差准则把每个可行组合压缩为 $(\mu_p,\sigma_p^2)$：其他条件相同时偏好更高期望收益、更低方差。它先排除存在[[均值方差支配|至少一项更优且另一项不差的可行替代]]的组合，再用投资者愿意作出的收益—风险取舍，在[[均值方差有效前沿]]上选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The criterion represents each feasible portfolio by its mean and variance, preferring higher mean or lower variance with the other held fixed. It first excludes [[均值方差支配|dominated portfolios]], then uses the investor's trade-off to select on the [[均值方差有效前沿|efficient frontier]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一种常用的具体评分是

$$J(w)=\mu_p-\frac{\gamma}{2}\sigma_p^2,\qquad\gamma>0.$$

这里 $\gamma$ 是这个评分中对方差的惩罚强度；只有配合明确的效用和分布假设，才可解释为特定风险厌恶参数。不要把所有均值方差偏好都限成这一种函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common score is $J=\mu_p-\gamma\sigma_p^2/2$, with positive variance penalty $\gamma$. Identifying that coefficient with a particular risk-aversion measure requires explicit utility and distribution assumptions. Mean–variance preferences need not all have this functional form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如组合 P 的 $(\mu,\sigma)=(9\%,15\%)$，Q 为 $(11\%,25\%)$。当 $\gamma=1$，两者得分恰好同为 $0.07875$；$\gamma=2$ 时 P 得分 $0.0675$，Q 得分 $0.0475$，于是这个准则选 P。收益和风险都较高时，不能仅凭两个点本身宣布谁“最好”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For P at $(9\%,15\%)$ and Q at $(11\%,25\%)$, the score ties at $0.07875$ when $\gamma=1$. At $\gamma=2$, P scores $0.0675$ and Q $0.0475$, so this criterion selects P. A higher-return, higher-risk point is not intrinsically the best choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方差将上涨和下跌偏离都计入风险。这个选择规则何时能代表一般期望效用、何时漏掉尾部差异，见[[均值方差偏好边界]]；输入的 $\mu,\Sigma$ 是否可信则由[[组合优化的输入误差]]处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Variance counts deviations on both sides. [[均值方差偏好边界|Preference limitations]] address when the rule represents expected utility and when it misses tail differences; [[组合优化的输入误差|input error]] concerns the reliability of the moments supplied to it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
