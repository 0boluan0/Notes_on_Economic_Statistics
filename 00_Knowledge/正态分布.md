# 正态分布是由位置 μ 与正尺度 σ 决定的高斯密度族

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The normal family has Gaussian densities determined by location and positive scale*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$（$\sigma>0$）指密度
$$f_X(x)=\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-(x-\mu)^2/(2\sigma^2)},\quad x\in\mathbb R.$$
其均值为 $\mu$，方差为 $\sigma^2$。第二个参数是方差，标准差是其正平方根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A normal variable has the displayed Gaussian density, with mean μ and variance σ². The second parameter denotes variance rather than standard deviation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
标准正态 $N(0,1)$ 的密度与 CDF 分别记为 $\phi,\Phi$；一般正态 CDF 为 $\Phi((x-\mu)/\sigma)$，见 [[正态标准化]]。正态分布对称，偏度为 0，峰度为 3；改变尺度并不改变这两个标准化形状矩。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Standard normal density and CDF are denoted by φ and Φ. [[正态标准化|Standardisation]] supplies the general normal CDF. Normal symmetry gives zero skewness and its fourth standardised moment is three.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
