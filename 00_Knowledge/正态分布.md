# Normal distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*正态分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **normal distribution**, also called a **Gaussian distribution**, is a continuous probability model with a symmetric bell-shaped density. Values near its centre have higher density than values far away. Two numbers determine the whole curve: the mean locates its centre, and the standard deviation determines its width.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**正态分布**，也称**高斯分布**，是一种具有对称钟形密度的连续概率模型。靠近中心的位置密度较高，离中心较远的位置密度较低。两个数决定整条曲线：均值确定中心，标准差确定宽度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[期望|mean]] is the probability-weighted centre. The [[标准差|standard deviation]] measures spread in the same units as the variable. Write these as $\mu$ (“mu”) and $\sigma$ (“sigma”), where $\mu$ is real and $\sigma$ is a positive finite number. We use the notation $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$, where $\sim$ means “has the distribution” and the second parameter is the **variance** $\sigma^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[期望|均值]]是按概率加权的中心，[[标准差]]则用与变量相同的单位衡量散布程度。分别记作 $\mu$（mu）和 $\sigma$（sigma），其中 $\mu$ 为实数，$\sigma$ 是有限正数。记号 $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ 中，$\sim$ 表示“服从……分布”，第二个参数是**方差** $\sigma^2$。

> [!example] Read the parameters before using the formula · 先读懂参数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $X\sim N(10,4)$, the mean is $10$, the variance is $4$, and the standard deviation is $\sqrt4=2$. The locations $8$ and $12$ are both one standard deviation from the mean, on opposite sides, so they have the same density. Equal-width intervals reflected around $10$ also have equal probabilities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $X\sim N(10,4)$，均值为 $10$，方差为 $4$，标准差为 $\sqrt4=2$。$8$ 与 $12$ 分别位于均值两侧，离均值都相差一个标准差，所以密度相同。把等宽区间围绕 $10$ 镜像翻转，得到的两个区间也有相同概率。

## What makes this particular bell curve normal
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*哪一种钟形曲线才是正态密度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bell-like appearance alone does not define a normal law; it has a particular [[概率密度函数|probability density]]. For a numerical value $x$, the difference $x-\mu$ is its displacement from the mean. In the formula below, $\pi\approx3.14159$ is pi and $e\approx2.71828$ is the base of the natural exponential function. The formula applies to every real number $x$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仅仅看起来像钟形，还不足以定义正态分布；它具有特定的[[概率密度函数]]。对数值 $x$，$x-\mu$ 表示它相对于均值的偏离。下式中，$\pi\approx3.14159$ 是圆周率，$e\approx2.71828$ 是自然指数函数的底数；公式适用于每个实数 $x$：

$$f_X(x)=\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}\,
 e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Squaring $x-\mu$ gives the same value at equal distances on either side, producing symmetry. The negative exponent makes the density decrease as the distance grows. Increasing $\sigma$ spreads the curve horizontally; the factor $1/\sigma$ reduces its height so the total area remains one. Changing $\mu$ shifts the curve without changing its shape or width.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$(x-\mu)^2$ 在中心两侧等距离处取相同值，因此产生对称性。指数中的负号使密度随距离增大而下降。增大 $\sigma$ 会把曲线横向拉宽，前面的 $1/\sigma$ 同时降低高度，使总面积保持为一；改变 $\mu$ 则只平移曲线，不改变形状和宽度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The density is positive everywhere on the real line, so every interval of positive length has positive probability, however far it lies from the mean. There is no hard lower or upper cutoff. Nevertheless, any single exact value has probability zero, as for other variables described by a density. Probabilities come from areas over intervals, not from density heights at points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

密度在整条实数线上都为正，所以任何正长度区间都有正概率，无论离均值多远；正态分布没有硬性的上下截止点。不过，与其他有密度的变量一样，任何一个精确值的概率都为零。概率来自区间下的面积，而不是某一点的密度高度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed density requires $\sigma>0$. If the variance is zero, the variable is constant at its mean; it does not have this density, and zero cannot be substituted into the denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的密度要求 $\sigma>0$。若方差为零，变量以概率一等于均值，不具有这个密度，不能在分母中直接代入零。

## One standard curve for all locations and scales
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*所有位置与尺度共用一条标准曲线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **standard normal distribution** has mean zero and variance one: $Z\sim N(0,1)$. Its density is written $\phi(z)$, using lowercase phi. Its [[累积分布函数|CDF]] is written $\Phi(z)=P(Z\le z)$, using uppercase Phi; it records the probability accumulated to the left of the threshold $z$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**标准正态分布**的均值为零、方差为一，记作 $Z\sim N(0,1)$。其密度用小写 phi 记为 $\phi(z)$，[[累积分布函数|CDF]]用大写 Phi 记为 $\Phi(z)=P(Z\le z)$，表示累积到阈值 $z$ 左侧的概率。

$$\phi(z)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-z^2/2},\qquad
\Phi(z)=\int_{-\infty}^{z}\phi(t)\,dt.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $t$ is just the running variable of integration. Lowercase $\phi$ gives density height; uppercase $\Phi$ gives cumulative probability. By symmetry, $\Phi(0)=1/2$. [[正态标准化|Normal standardisation]] converts the threshold $x$ into the number of standard deviations from the mean, giving the general CDF
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $t$ 只是积分过程中使用的变量。小写 $\phi$ 给密度高度，大写 $\Phi$ 给累计概率。由对称性，$\Phi(0)=1/2$。[[正态标准化]]把阈值 $x$ 换成“离均值多少个标准差”，从而给出一般正态的 CDF：

$$F_X(x)=\Phi\!\left(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Normal probabilities can therefore be computed using one standard normal table or numerical CDF routine. For orientation, about $68.27\%$ of the probability lies within one standard deviation of the mean, and about $95.45\%$ within two. For $N(10,4)$ these intervals are $[8,12]$ and $[6,14]$. They are probability intervals, not absolute limits on possible values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，可以用同一张标准正态表或数值 CDF 程序计算正态概率。作为量级参照，约 $68.27\%$ 的概率在均值左右一个标准差内，约 $95.45\%$ 在两个标准差内。对 $N(10,4)$，对应区间是 $[8,12]$ 与 $[6,14]$；它们是概率区间，不是取值的绝对边界。

> [!note]- Standardised shape summaries · 标准化的形状指标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every nondegenerate normal distribution has [[偏度|skewness]] zero and [[峰度|kurtosis]] three, hence excess kurtosis zero. These quantities use powers of deviations divided by the corresponding powers of standard deviation, so shifting or positively rescaling the normal curve does not change them. Their values alone do not establish that an arbitrary distribution is normal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个非退化正态分布的[[偏度]]为零、[[峰度]]为三，因此超额峰度为零。这些指标把偏离的幂除以标准差的相应次幂，所以平移或按正数缩放正态曲线不会改变它们。但任意分布仅有这两个指标值，并不足以证明其正态性。
