# 事后价格变化不能证明此前价格未有效反映当时信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A later price change does not prove that the earlier price failed to reflect information available at the time*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 有效价格是相对于当时信息集与风险模型的条件判断，不是以后永不改变的“真值”。新的现金流消息、风险溢价、无风险利率或贴现率信息到来时，价格改变正是及时吸收信息可以产生的结果。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An efficient price is conditional on the information set and risk model available at that time; it is not a “true value” that can never change. When new information arrives about cash flows, risk premia, the risk-free rate, or discount rates, a price change can be exactly what prompt information incorporation predicts.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从“股价后来跌了”倒推“此前一定高估”使用了事后才知道的信息。要检验此前是否低效，应证明当时可得的某个信息集能够事前、系统且在风险与成本调整后预测该变化，而不是挑中已经发生的跌幅讲故事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inferring “the earlier price must have been overvalued” from a later fall uses information known only ex post. A test of earlier inefficiency must show that an information set available at the time predicted the move ex ante, systematically, after risk and cost adjustment, rather than selecting a realised decline and narrating it backward.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也不意味着任何价格变化都证明市场有效。缓慢反应、可预测反转或同一公开信息后的持续漂移可能构成证据，但仍要通过联合假设、时间戳、可实施成本和样本外复核。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This does not mean that every price change proves efficiency. Slow adjustment, predictable reversal, or persistent drift after the same public information can provide evidence, but it must still pass the joint-hypothesis, timestamp, implementable-cost, and out-of-sample checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 某股票一年后跌 40%，这是否证明一年前市场价格无效？
>
> **答案：** 不能。先区分一年间出现的新信息；只有当一年前可得信息能在风险和成本调整后事前预测该跌幅，才构成相关有效性证据。

## 来源与核验

- [Fama (1991), “Efficient Capital Markets: II”](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1991.tb04636.x)：核对效率是价格对信息的条件反映，而回报可预测性必须与预期收益模型共同解释。
- [Fama et al. (1969), “The Adjustment of Stock Prices to New Information”](https://doi.org/10.2307/2525569)：核对检验对象是价格对新增信息的调整速度。
- [[意外信息定价]]：复用事前预期与事后结果的时间边界。
