# Smoothness does not imply analyticity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*光滑不代表解析*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing a function's value and every derivative at one point can still miss its behaviour arbitrarily close to that point. There is a smooth function that is positive everywhere except zero, yet every derivative at zero is zero. Its Taylor series is therefore the zero series, which converges everywhere and represents the function only at its centre.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使知道函数在一点的函数值和所有阶导数，仍可能遗漏它在任意邻近位置的行为。有一个光滑函数，除了零点之外处处为正，但零点的每阶导数都是零。因此它的 Taylor 级数就是处处收敛的零级数，却只在中心处表示原函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here **smooth**, or $C^\infty$, means that [[高阶导数|derivatives of every order]] exist and are continuous on an open interval. A real function is **analytic at a point $a$** if it equals a convergent power series centred at $a$ on some open interval around $a$. By the coefficient argument in [[Taylor级数|Taylor series]], that local representation, if it exists, must be its Taylor series. Smoothness supplies derivatives; analyticity requires local equality with their series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里，**光滑**，也记作 $C^\infty$，指每一阶[[高阶导数]]都在开区间上存在且连续。实函数**在点 $a$ 解析**，指它在 $a$ 附近某个开区间上等于一个以 $a$ 为中心的收敛幂级数。根据 [[Taylor级数]]中的系数论证，这种局部表示若存在，就必定是它的 Taylor 级数。光滑保证导数存在，解析还要求函数在附近等于这些导数构成的级数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A function that becomes extremely flat at zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*在零点变得极平的函数*

$$
f(x)=\begin{cases}
e^{-1/x^2},&x\ne0,\\
0,&x=0.
\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]] is always positive, so $f(1)=e^{-1}$ and $f(1/2)=e^{-4}$ are positive. The same values occur at $-1$ and $-1/2$, because only $x^2$ enters the formula. As $x$ approaches zero from either side, $1/x^2$ grows without bound and the function becomes very small. The assigned value $f(0)=0$ fills in that limiting value. We must still prove that all derivatives fit smoothly across zero; the off-zero formula alone does not establish this.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[自然指数函数]]总为正，因此 $f(1)=e^{-1}$、$f(1/2)=e^{-4}$ 都是正数。$-1$ 与 $-1/2$ 处分别取相同值，因为公式中出现的是 $x^2$。当 $x$ 从任意一侧趋于零，$1/x^2$ 无界增大，函数变得极小；规定 $f(0)=0$ 正好补上这一极限值。但还须证明所有阶导数都能在零点平滑衔接，不能只凭非零处的公式宣布这一点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The key fact is that the exponential beats every fixed inverse power:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键是这个指数因子能够压过任意固定的负幂：

$$\lim_{x\to0}|x|^{-m}e^{-1/x^2}=0\qquad\text{for every fixed real }m\ge0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove it on both sides, put $u=1/x^2$. Then $u\to+\infty$ whether $x\to0^+$ or $x\to0^-$. Choose an integer $k>m/2$. The finite [[Taylor余项|Taylor remainder theorem]] applied to $e^u$ at zero gives $e^u\ge u^k/k!$ for $u>0$: all terms of its degree-$k$ Taylor polynomial and the Lagrange remainder are positive. Consequently,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为同时证明左右两侧，令 $u=1/x^2$。无论 $x\to0^+$ 还是 $x\to0^-$，都有 $u\to+\infty$。选整数 $k>m/2$。对 $e^u$ 在零点使用有限阶 [[Taylor余项]]定理，得到 $u>0$ 时 $e^u\ge u^k/k!$，因为其 $k$ 阶 Taylor 多项式各项及 Lagrange 余项都为正。因此

$$0\le |x|^{-m}e^{-1/x^2}=u^{m/2}e^{-u}
\le k!\,u^{m/2-k}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The final power tends to zero because its exponent is negative. This proof uses only a finite Taylor formula, not equality with an infinite Taylor series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后这个幂的指数为负，所以趋于零。此证明只使用有限阶 Taylor 公式，没有预先假设无穷 Taylor 级数等于函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Prove every derivative, including at the joining point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*逐阶证明导数，包括拼接点本身*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Away from zero, the [[链式法则|chain rule]], [[乘积法则|product rule]] and [[指数函数求导|exponential derivative]] give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在零点之外，由[[链式法则]]、[[乘积法则]]与[[指数函数求导]]得到

$$f'(x)=2x^{-3}e^{-1/x^2},\qquad
f''(x)=(4x^{-6}-6x^{-4})e^{-1/x^2}\qquad(x\ne0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each derivative remains a polynomial in $1/x$ times the same exponential. To keep track of all orders, let $P_0(t)=1$ and define polynomials recursively by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每次求导后，仍是“$1/x$ 的多项式乘同一个指数因子”。为统一记录所有阶，令 $P_0(t)=1$，并递归定义多项式

$$P_{n+1}(t)=-t^2P_n'(t)+2t^3P_n(t)\qquad(n\ge0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $t$ is the polynomial's input; when differentiating with respect to $x$ we substitute $t=1/x$, with $dt/dx=-t^2$. The two terms in the recurrence come from differentiating the polynomial factor and the exponential factor respectively. In particular, $P_1(t)=2t^3$ and $P_2(t)=4t^6-6t^4$, matching the displayed calculations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $t$ 是多项式的输入；对 $x$ 求导时，代入 $t=1/x$，其导数为 $dt/dx=-t^2$。递推式两项分别来自对多项式因子和指数因子求导。特别地，$P_1(t)=2t^3$、$P_2(t)=4t^6-6t^4$，与刚才两次计算一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define $g_n(x)=P_n(1/x)e^{-1/x^2}$ for $x\ne0$, and $g_n(0)=0$. Each $g_n$ is continuous at zero: expand the finite polynomial, apply the inverse-power limit to the absolute value of each term, and add finitely many limits. It is smooth away from zero by the ordinary differentiation rules. At zero, the [[导数|difference quotient]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义 $x\ne0$ 时 $g_n(x)=P_n(1/x)e^{-1/x^2}$，并补 $g_n(0)=0$。每个 $g_n$ 都在零点连续：把有限多项式展开，对每项绝对值使用刚才的负幂极限，再相加即可。零点以外则由普通求导规则得到光滑性。零点处的[[导数|差商]]为

$$\frac{g_n(h)-g_n(0)}h=\frac{P_n(1/h)}h e^{-1/h^2}\longrightarrow0
\qquad(h\to0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dividing by $h$ adds only one more inverse power, so the same absolute-value estimate proves this two-sided limit. Thus $g_n'(0)=0=g_{n+1}(0)$, while the recurrence gives $g_n'=g_{n+1}$ off zero. Starting with $g_0=f$, induction proves $f^{(n)}=g_n$ on the whole real line. Each of these derivatives is continuous and satisfies $f^{(n)}(0)=0$, including $n=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

除以 $h$ 只会再增加一个负幂，因此同一个绝对值估计证明了这个双侧极限。于是 $g_n'(0)=0=g_{n+1}(0)$；非零处又由递推式得到 $g_n'=g_{n+1}$。从 $g_0=f$ 开始归纳，便知整个实轴上都有 $f^{(n)}=g_n$。这些导数每一阶都连续，并满足 $f^{(n)}(0)=0$，其中也包括 $n=0$。

> [!warning] Infinite radius still does not identify the function / 无穷半径仍不能确定原函数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every Taylor coefficient at zero is $0/n!=0$, so every partial sum is the zero polynomial. This series has [[幂级数收敛半径|infinite radius]] and sum zero everywhere. For a nonnegative order $N$, the remainder is $R_{N,0}(x)=f(x)-P_{N,0}(x)$. Thus, for every fixed $x\ne0$, it equals the same positive number $f(x)$ at every order. The [[Taylor级数等于函数|remainder criterion]] therefore fails there. Every open interval around zero contains such a point, so $f$ is not analytic at zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 零点处每个 Taylor 系数都是 $0/n!=0$，所有部分和都是零多项式。这个级数的[[幂级数收敛半径|半径为无穷]]，处处和为零。对非负阶数 $N$，余项定义为 $R_{N,0}(x)=f(x)-P_{N,0}(x)$。于是每个固定非零 $x$ 处的每阶余项都等于同一个正数 $f(x)$，因此该处不满足[[Taylor级数等于函数|余项判据]]。零点的每个开邻域都包含这样的点，所以 $f$ 在零点不解析。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This does not contradict uniqueness of power-series coefficients: that theorem assumes an actual representation on a neighbourhood. The zero function and this $f$ have the same derivatives at zero, but only the zero function is represented there by their common zero Taylor series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不违背幂级数系数的唯一性，因为该定理的前提是在邻域内确实存在幂级数表示。零函数与这里的 $f$ 在零点具有完全相同的各阶导数，但共同的零 Taylor 级数在零点附近只能表示零函数。
