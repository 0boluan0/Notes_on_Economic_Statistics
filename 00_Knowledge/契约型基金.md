# 契约型基金是通过基金契约组织独立基金财产而不另设基金公司的集合投资形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A contractual fund organizes separate fund property through a fund contract rather than incorporating a fund company*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

契约型基金的投资者、管理人和托管人等依基金契约与适用法律形成权利义务关系，基金财产独立于管理人、托管人的自有财产；基金本身不另作为一家公司发行公司股份。投资者取得基金份额所代表的受益权，而不是基金管理公司的股权。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A contractual fund organizes the rights and duties of investors, the manager, and the custodian through the fund contract and applicable law. Fund property is separate from the manager's and custodian's own property, and the fund itself is not incorporated as a share-issuing company. Investors acquire beneficial interests represented by fund units, not equity in the fund management company.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在中国证券投资基金的合同安排中，管理人依职责作投资管理，托管人依职责保管基金财产等，持有人依份额与合同分享收益、承担风险。不能因为财产由托管人保管就当作托管人的自有资产，也不能因为管理人下单就把基金财产当作其资产负债表中可任意使用的财产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under China's contractual securities-fund framework, the manager manages investments, the custodian safeguards fund property within its duties, and holders share returns and risks according to their units and the contract. Custody does not make the assets the custodian's own property, and placing investment orders does not give the manager unrestricted use of fund assets as its own.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

契约形式也不等于持有人“没有发言权”。例如中国《证券投资基金法》规定公开募集基金持有人可依法参与持有人大会表决，并对更换管理人等特定事项行使权利；这与持有人每天直接指定基金买哪只股票是两回事。具体权利来自法律和基金文件，而不能照搬[[公司型基金]]的董事会结构，也不能一概删去集体治理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Contractual form does not mean investors have no voice. China's Fund Law, for example, grants holders of publicly offered funds meeting and voting rights over specified matters, including manager replacement. That differs from directing daily stock purchases. Rights come from law and fund documents: neither the board structure of a [[公司型基金|corporate fund]] nor an absence of collective governance should be assumed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小识别问题是：投资者拿到的是独立基金财产的份额权益，还是一间基金投资公司的股份？前者若由契约而非基金公司组织，就是本卡所说的契约型安排；“购买某基金公司管理的产品”本身没有回答这个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identifying question is whether the investor receives a unit interest in separately organized fund property or shares in an incorporated investment fund. The former, when organized by contract rather than a fund company, is the contractual form described here. Buying a product managed by a named fund manager does not by itself answer that question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [香港投委会 IFEC, What is an open-ended fund company?](https://www.ifec.org.hk/web/en/investment/investment-products/funds/open-ended-fund-company/what-is-open-ended-fund-company.page)与[Who manages funds?](https://www.ifec.org.hk/web/en/investment/investment-products/funds/basics/who-manage-funds.page)：已重开相关正文，支持信托式与公司式组织的区别，以及管理、托管和资产隔离；不把香港信托的法律细节直接等同于每一法域的契约型基金。

- The reopened IFEC explanations support the distinction between trust and corporate organization and the separation of management, custody, and assets. Hong Kong trust-law details are not equated with contractual funds in every jurisdiction.
