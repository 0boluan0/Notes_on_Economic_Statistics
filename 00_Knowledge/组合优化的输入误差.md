# 输入估计误差可能放大最优权重变化并破坏样本内风险优势
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Input estimation error can amplify changes in optimal weights and undermine in-sample risk advantages.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值—方差优化使用 $\widehat\mu,\widehat\Sigma$ 求出的权重，只对这些**输入**最优。样本估计不等于真实参数，所以较小的 $w^T\widehat\Sigma w$ 不自动意味着较小的真实方差 $w^T\Sigma w$，也不保证实际实现收益达到目标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weights optimized with $\widehat\mu,\widehat\Sigma$ are optimal for those inputs. Estimates need not equal the true parameters: smaller $w^T\widehat\Sigma w$ does not automatically mean smaller true variance $w^T\Sigma w$, nor does it guarantee that realized returns meet the target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 误差怎样进入权重和风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How errors enter weights and risk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[目标收益最小方差解]]含 $\Sigma^{-1}$ 和 $D=AC-B^2$ 的分母。协方差接近奇异、或资产均值使两条约束接近相关时，输入的小变化可能对应很大的权重变化。[[样本协方差秩边界]]还说明：资产维数不小于观测数时，通常的中心化样本协方差必奇异，不能直接使用普通逆闭式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[目标收益最小方差解|target-return solution]] contains $\Sigma^{-1}$ and denominator $D=AC-B^2$. Near-singular covariance or nearly dependent budget/return constraints can turn small input changes into large allocation changes. The [[样本协方差秩边界|sample covariance rank bound]] also shows that centered sample covariance is necessarily singular when the asset dimension is at least the number of observations, precluding the ordinary-inverse formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意固定权重，设 $\varepsilon_\mu=\max_i|\widehat\mu_i-\mu_i|$、$\varepsilon_\Sigma=\max_{i,j}|\widehat\Sigma_{ij}-\Sigma_{ij}|$，逐项取绝对值得
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any fixed weights, let $\varepsilon_\mu=\max_i|\widehat\mu_i-\mu_i|$ and $\varepsilon_\Sigma=\max_{i,j}|\widehat\Sigma_{ij}-\Sigma_{ij}|$. Termwise absolute-value bounds give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
|w^T(\widehat\mu-\mu)|\le\|w\|_1\varepsilon_\mu,
\qquad
|w^T(\widehat\Sigma-\Sigma)w|\le\|w\|_1^2\varepsilon_\Sigma.
$$

这是固定输入误差下的确定性界，不是置信区间。它说明大额多空对冲会放大最坏方向的误差影响；但大额多空头寸也可能是**真实**协方差结构的最优结果，不能只凭极端权重断定估计出错。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are deterministic bounds for given input errors, not confidence intervals. Large offsetting long and short positions magnify worst-direction error exposure. Yet extreme positions can also be optimal under the true covariance structure; their presence alone does not prove estimation failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如只有两资产、预算为 1、目标收益 $10\%$。估计均值 $(8\%,12\%)$ 强制等权；仅把第二项估计改成 $10\%$，权重就变成 $(0,1)$。若真实协方差始终为 $\begin{bmatrix}0.04&0.015\\0.015&0.09\end{bmatrix}$，这两种配置的真实方差分别是 $0.04$ 与 $0.09$。这是输入敏感性演示，不是对第二组估计真假的判定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With two assets, unit budget, and target mean $10\%$, estimated means $(8\%,12\%)$ force equal weights. Changing only the second estimate to $10\%$ changes the weights to $(0,1)$. If true covariance remains the stated matrix, the true variances are $0.04$ and $0.09$. This demonstrates input sensitivity without deciding which mean estimate is correct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应用时要分别检查输入敏感性、估计窗口、样本外表现与可实施约束。限制权重或收缩估计可能减轻某些估计误差，也可能引入约束或模型偏差；必须在指定数据和评价口径下比较，不能把某种修正宣布为普遍更优。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check input sensitivity, estimation windows, out-of-sample performance, and implementation constraints separately. Weight restrictions or shrinkage may reduce some estimation errors while introducing restriction or model bias. Compare them under specified data and evaluation conventions rather than declaring any correction universally superior.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Jagannathan and Ma, *Risk Reduction in Large Portfolios*, NBER Working Paper 8922 (2002), PDF pp.3–6 / 正文 pp.1–4](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w8922/w8922.pdf#page=3)：支持样本矩优化的极端权重、真实协方差结构也可能导致极端头寸，以及限制权重时估计误差与模型限制的权衡。此处不调用该文后续命题或将经验结论普遍化。
- [[目标收益最小方差解]]、[[样本协方差秩边界]]：核对闭式中的逆矩阵、约束退化和样本秩限制。两个绝对误差界由逐项三角不等式独立推出，算例直接解预算与目标等式并复算方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Jagannathan and Ma, NBER WP 8922 (2002), PDF pp.3–6 / text pp.1–4, supports extreme estimated portfolios, extreme weights arising from true covariance structure, and the estimation-versus-restriction trade-off. No later proposition or universalized empirical claim is invoked here.
- [[目标收益最小方差解|The target-return solution]] and [[样本协方差秩边界|the sample rank bound]] support the inverse, constraint-degeneracy, and rank checks. The two deterministic bounds follow independently by the triangle inequality; the example is solved directly from the constraints and covariance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
