# 正的社会净现值只支持已纳入影响的净收益判断而不自动决定公共方案
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Positive social NPV supports a net-gain judgement for included impacts, not an automatic public decision.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

社会净现值为正，说明在给定范围、估值、贴现和风险假设下，**已经计入的**效益现值大于成本现值。要据此选择公共方案，还须检查遗漏影响、受益与受损分布，以及其他可行方案和实施约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive social NPV means included discounted benefits exceed included costs under the specified scope, valuation, discounting and risk assumptions. Selecting a public option additionally requires omitted impacts, distribution, alternatives and implementation constraints to be considered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如甲得益 100、乙损失 60，等权货币净额为 40。这个总数没有告诉我们乙是否得到补偿；在可转移货币收益等条件下，它可与 [[潜在补偿准则]] 相连，却不是“人人已经变好”的证明。若环境损害尚未估值，也不能默认为它已包含在 60 中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If one person gains 100 and another loses 60, the equal-weight net amount is 40. It does not establish actual compensation. Under suitable transferable money-metric conditions it connects to [[潜在补偿准则|potential compensation]], not proof that everyone is already better off. Unvalued environmental damage must not be silently treated as included.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若两个互斥且可实施的方案净收益分别为 40 和 55，仅证明前者大于零还不够：它可能输给后者。比较升级所增加的价值，沿用 [[互斥项目的增量 NPV|增量 NPV 的选择逻辑]]；本课把评价对象换成社会影响。硬预算下还须检查 [[资本限额下的项目组合|可行组合]]，不能只按最高比率选择方案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An option worth 40 may lose to a mutually exclusive feasible option worth 55. Apply [[互斥项目的增量 NPV|incremental-NPV comparison]] to the social account. Under a hard budget, examine [[资本限额下的项目组合|feasible combinations]] rather than selecting solely by the highest ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个完整结论可以是：“在这些估值与折现假设下，方案净收益为正；受损群体、未货币化影响和预算安排见附表；与可行替代方案相比仍占优。”如果关键假设稍变就翻转排序，则应报告 [[敏感性、情景与模拟|临界值和相关情景]]，让选择依赖什么一目了然。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A complete judgement states the valuation assumptions, net gain, distribution, unmonetised impacts, affordability and comparison with feasible alternatives. If plausible assumption changes reverse the ranking, report [[敏感性、情景与模拟|switching values and coherent scenarios]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
