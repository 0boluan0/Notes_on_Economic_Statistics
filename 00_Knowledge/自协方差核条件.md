# 合法自协方差核必须 Hermitian 有界且半正定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A valid autocovariance kernel must be Hermitian, bounded at zero, and positive semidefinite*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子判据
> 对复值宽平稳过程的自协方差 $\gamma(h)=E[(X_{t+h}-\mu)\overline{(X_t-\mu)}]$：
> - $\gamma(-h)=\overline{\gamma(h)}$；实值过程因此有 $\gamma(-h)=\gamma(h)$；
> - $|\gamma(h)|\le\gamma(0)$；
> - 对任意时点 $t_1,\ldots,t_n$ 和系数 $a_1,\ldots,a_n$，
> $$
> \sum_{i,j}a_i\overline{a_j}\gamma(t_i-t_j)\ge0.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A complex WSS autocovariance is Hermitian, bounded in magnitude by its zero-lag value, and positive semidefinite on every finite set of times. For a real process, Hermitian symmetry reduces to even symmetry.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半正定性来自方差恒非负：上述二次型正是 $\operatorname{Var}(\sum_i a_iX_{t_i})$。因此“偶函数、零点最大”仍不够；若某个有限 Toeplitz 协方差矩阵有负二次型，该候选函数就不可能来自任何二阶过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive semidefiniteness follows because the quadratic form is the variance of a linear combination. Evenness and a maximum at zero are not sufficient: one negative quadratic form in a finite covariance matrix invalidates the candidate kernel.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个实偶函数满足 $|g(h)|\le g(0)$，能否据此断定它是某个宽平稳过程的自协方差？
>
> **答案：** 不能。还必须对每个有限时点集都生成半正定的协方差矩阵；一个负二次型就能否定该候选核。
