# Why endpoint subtraction can fail at a singularity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*跨奇点使用原函数的边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finding an [[原函数|antiderivative]] on each side of a missing point does not automatically allow one endpoint subtraction across it. The expression $F(b)-F(a)$ can hide what happens as the integration approaches that point. For an [[反常积分|improper integral]], the missing check is whether each one-sided accumulation has a finite limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在缺失点两侧各自找到[[原函数]]，并不自动允许跨过它做一次端点相减。$F(b)-F(a)$ 可能把逼近这个位置时发生的事情藏起来。对于[[反常积分]]，还缺少一个检查：每一侧的累积是否分别趋向有限值。

> [!example] A positive integrand giving a negative answer · 正函数竟算出负结果
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On either side of zero, $F(x)=-1/x$ has derivative $1/x^2$. Substituting only the outer endpoints would give
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在零点的任一侧，$F(x)=-1/x$ 的导数确实是 $1/x^2$。如果只代入外侧端点，就会得到
>
> $$F(1)-F(-1)=-1-1=-2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is **not** the value of $\int_{-1}^{1}dx/x^2$. A nonnegative function cannot have a negative integral over increasing bounds, by [[积分保序性|order preservation]]. That contradiction detects a mistake; it does not yet prove the correct result.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这**不是** $\int_{-1}^{1}dx/x^2$ 的值。由[[积分保序性]]，非负函数在正向区间上的积分不可能为负。这个矛盾能发现错误，但还没有证明正确结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Split at zero and use positive independent cutoffs $\varepsilon,\delta<1$. On each remaining finite interval the [[Newton-Leibniz公式|endpoint formula]] is valid, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在零点拆开，用相互独立的正截断量 $\varepsilon,\delta<1$。剩余的每个有限区间都能合法使用 [[Newton-Leibniz公式|原函数端点公式]]，得到

$$\int_{-1}^{-\varepsilon}\frac{dx}{x^2}=\frac1\varepsilon-1,
\qquad
\int_{\delta}^{1}\frac{dx}{x^2}=\frac1\delta-1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both quantities tend to $+\infty$, so the improper integral diverges. There are no finite inner endpoint values to cancel. Even symmetric truncation gives $2/\varepsilon-2\to+\infty$, not $-2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两者都趋于 $+\infty$，所以反常积分发散，并没有两个有限的内侧端点值可以抵消。即使对称截断，也得到 $2/\varepsilon-2\to+\infty$，而不是 $-2$。

## What would justify joining the pieces?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*什么条件允许把两段连接起来？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $c$ be the only possible singularity between finite ordinary endpoints $a<b$. Suppose $f$ is continuous on each side, with antiderivatives $F_-$ on the left and $F_+$ on the right. Suppose also that the finite limits $L_-=\lim_{x\to c^-}F_-(x)$ and $L_+=\lim_{x\to c^+}F_+(x)$ exist. [[反常积分逐坏点拆分|Separate-limit evaluation]] then gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $c$ 是有限普通端点 $a<b$ 之间唯一可能的奇点。假设 $f$ 在两侧分别连续，左侧原函数是 $F_-$、右侧原函数是 $F_+$，并且有限极限 $L_-=\lim_{x\to c^-}F_-(x)$、$L_+=\lim_{x\to c^+}F_+(x)$ 都存在。按照[[反常积分逐坏点拆分|各侧独立取极限]]，有

$$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx
=\bigl(L_--F_-(a)\bigr)+\bigl(F_+(b)-L_+\bigr).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two inner values cancel only when the chosen primitives have matching limits. Their [[原函数相差常数|additive constants are independent on disconnected intervals]]. One can align finite limits by adjusting these constants, but this works **after** convergence is proved. An infinite or oscillating limit cannot be repaired by adding a finite constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有所选原函数的内侧极限相等，两个内侧值才会抵消。[[原函数相差常数|彼此断开的区间允许独立选择加法常数]]。当极限都有限时，可以调整常数，让它们对齐；但这是在**已经证明收敛之后**才有意义的操作。无穷或振荡的极限不能靠加一个有限常数修复。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a simple illustration, let $f(x)=1$ on $[-1,1]\setminus\{0\}$. The separate integrals each equal $1$, so the total is $2$. Yet choosing $F_-(x)=x$ and $F_+(x)=x+100$ would give the unjustified outer subtraction $101-(-1)=102$. The correct formula subtracts the inner limit $100$ too, recovering $2$. Correct derivatives on the two pieces alone do not specify how the pieces join.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个简单例子是 $[-1,1]\setminus\{0\}$ 上的 $f(x)=1$。左右积分各为 $1$，总值为 $2$。但若选 $F_-(x)=x$、$F_+(x)=x+100$，不加检查地只做外端点差，就得到 $101-(-1)=102$。正确公式还要减去内侧极限 $100$，恢复为 $2$。两段求导都对，本身并没有规定两段应怎样连接。

## Checks before evaluating
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 一套先于计算的检查

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First locate denominator zeros, endpoints and real-domain restrictions. Decide whether the issue is a genuine unbounded singularity, a removable hole, or an ordinary discontinuity. Then [[反常积分逐坏点拆分|separate every required limit]] and evaluate primitives only on valid truncations. Add the results after their finite limits are established; finally compare signs and, where available, a simple integral bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先定位分母零点、端点和实数定义域限制，辨认它究竟是真正的无界奇点、可去的孔点，还是普通间断。然后[[反常积分逐坏点拆分|把每个需要的极限分开]]，只在合法截断区间使用原函数；各侧有限极限建立后再相加，最后核对符号，以及能得到的简单积分界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A hole does not by itself imply divergence. For $(x^2-1)/(x-1)$, the [[可去间断的唯一补值|continuous extension]] is $x+1$ at the missing point too; after explicitly justifying that extension, the interval can be integrated normally. A bounded function with finitely many jump discontinuities may also be Riemann integrable. The diagnostic is a demand to check the theorem's hypotheses, not a rule that every discontinuity makes integration impossible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有孔点并不意味着发散。对 $(x^2-1)/(x-1)$，[[可去间断的唯一补值|连续延拓]]让缺失点也按 $x+1$ 取值；明确说明这种延拓以后，就可以按普通区间积分。只有有限个跳跃间断的有界函数也可能黎曼可积。这里的诊断要求检查定理前提，并不是说每种间断都让积分无法进行。
