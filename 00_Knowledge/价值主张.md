# 价值主张是企业面向目标客户提出的需求满足与价值提供
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A value proposition specifies the needs a firm addresses and the value it offers its target customers.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价值主张回答“服务哪些客户、满足什么需求、相对其他选择提供什么价值”。在竞争分析中，还应把价格放入比较：客户获得的额外便利、质量或性能，是否值得相应付出。它面向客户需求，而[[价值链]]说明企业怎样交付这种价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A value proposition states which customers are served, which needs are met, and what value is offered relative to alternatives. Competitive analysis also considers price: are added convenience, quality, or performance worth the required payment? The proposition faces customer demand; the [[价值链|value chain]] explains delivery.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“高品质、以客户为中心”不足以识别一个具体价值主张。自拟对照：“为营业时间长的小餐馆提供每周自动排班，减少店主手工协调时间，按门店收取可预先知道的月费”，明确了对象、需求和收费体验，但仍须用实际节省时间及客户选择验证承诺。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“High quality and customer focus” does not identify a specific proposition. A constructed alternative—“weekly automated scheduling for small restaurants with long opening hours, reducing manual coordination at a predictable monthly fee per location”—identifies customers, needs, and payment experience. Actual time savings and customer choices must still validate the promise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价值主张不必是高端溢价，也可通过舍弃客户不需要的功能提供低价的适用方案。只有与竞争者形成有意义差异并能交付时，它才有助于竞争定位；一句宣传口号不自动构成[[护城河证据]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A proposition need not promise a premium offering: omitting unwanted features can provide an adequate lower-priced solution. It contributes to positioning when the difference matters and can be delivered; a slogan is not automatically [[护城河证据|evidence of a durable advantage]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
