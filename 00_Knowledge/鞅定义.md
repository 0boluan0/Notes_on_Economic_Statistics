# Martingale
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*鞅*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **martingale** is a process whose current value is also its conditional average future value. After using all the information currently available, the model predicts no average gain or loss from the current level. The actual next value can still go up or down. “Fair” here describes a conditional average, not a promise that every outcome stays unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**鞅**是一种过程：当前值同时也是利用当前全部信息计算出的未来条件平均值。在模型中，掌握现有信息后，相对于当前水平并没有平均收益或损失。下一次实际取值仍可上升或下降；这里的“公平”说的是条件平均，而不是每次结果都不变。

> [!example] A fair sequence of gains and losses · 一串公平的盈亏
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Start at $M_0=0$. At step $k$, an independent fair coin produces a gain $\xi_k=1$ or a loss $\xi_k=-1$, each with probability $1/2$. Let $M_n=\xi_1+\cdots+\xi_n$ be the total after $n$ steps. If the current total is $3$, the next total is $4$ or $2$, with conditional average $(4+2)/2=3$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $M_0=0$ 开始。第 $k$ 步用一枚独立的公平硬币决定收益 $\xi_k=1$ 或损失 $\xi_k=-1$，概率各为 $1/2$。令 $M_n=\xi_1+\cdots+\xi_n$ 为前 $n$ 步累计值。如果当前累计值为 $3$，下一步可能是 $4$ 或 $2$，条件平均为 $(4+2)/2=3$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The information at time $n$ consists of the tosses already seen. The next coin is independent of that history, so its conditional mean is zero. This remains true after every possible past history, not merely after averaging all histories together.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 时刻 $n$ 的信息是已经看见的投掷结果。下一枚硬币独立于这些历史，所以条件均值为零。这个结论在每种已知历史下都成立，而不是把所有历史混在一起平均后才成立。

## Naming the process and its information
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先说明过程，以及观察者知道什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[随机过程|stochastic process]] $M=(M_t)$ is a collection of random variables on one probability space, indexed here by time $t$. A [[滤过定义|filtration]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ describes the available information: $\mathcal F_s\subseteq\mathcal F_t$ when $s\le t$, so information accumulates. Each $\mathcal F_t$ is a collection of observable events, formally a [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]]. In the coin model, $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$ means the information generated by the first $n$ tosses; $\mathcal F_0$ initially contains no toss results.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[随机过程]] $M=(M_t)$ 是同一概率空间上的一组随机变量，这里用时间 $t$ 编号。[[滤过定义|滤过]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ 描述可用信息：$s\le t$ 时有 $\mathcal F_s\subseteq\mathcal F_t$，表示信息逐步累积。每个 $\mathcal F_t$ 是能被观察到的事件集合，正式称为[[σ-代数]]。硬币模型里，$\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$ 表示前 $n$ 次投掷生成的信息，$\mathcal F_0$ 起初还没有投掷结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The process is [[适应过程|adapted]] when $M_t$ can be determined from the information $\mathcal F_t$ available at that time; formally, $M_t$ is $\mathcal F_t$-measurable. The [[条件期望|conditional expectation]] $E[M_t\mid\mathcal F_s]$ averages the future value while retaining what is known at time $s$. It is itself a random variable: different known histories can give different averages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

过程[[适应过程|适应]]于这个滤过，意思是时刻 $t$ 的信息 $\mathcal F_t$ 已足以确定 $M_t$，正式说法是 $M_t$ 对 $\mathcal F_t$ 可测。[[条件期望]] $E[M_t\mid\mathcal F_s]$ 则保留时刻 $s$ 的已知信息，对未来值求平均。它本身仍是随机变量，因为不同已知历史可以给出不同的平均值。

> [!important] Definition relative to a filtration · 相对于滤过的定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real process $M=(M_t)$ is a martingale with respect to $(\mathcal F_t)$ if it is adapted, every time value is integrable, $E|M_t|<\infty$, and for every fixed pair of times $s\le t$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$\boxed{E[M_t\mid\mathcal F_s]=M_s\quad\text{almost surely}.}$$
>
> 实值过程 $M=(M_t)$ 相对于 $(\mathcal F_t)$ 是鞅，是指：它适应于这个滤过，每个时刻都可积，即 $E|M_t|<\infty$，并且对每一对固定的 $s\le t$ 都满足上式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Integrability means that the [[期望|expected absolute value]] is finite; it makes the conditional means well-defined and finite almost surely. It is required separately at every time, without requiring one uniform bound over all times or a finite second moment. “Almost surely” permits exceptions on a probability-zero event. The equation is asserted for each fixed pair of times; in continuous time this wording alone does not assert one common exceptional set for every possible pair.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可积表示[[期望|绝对值的期望]]有限，保证条件均值有定义且几乎必然有限。要求逐时点成立，并不要求所有时刻共用一个统一上界，也不要求二阶矩有限。“几乎必然”允许零概率事件上的例外。等式针对每对固定时刻陈述；连续时间时，这本身不额外断言存在一个对所有时刻对通用的例外零集。

## Why one step is enough in discrete time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 离散时间的一步判据

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For integer times $n=0,1,\ldots$, once adaptedness and integrability hold, it is enough to check $E[M_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=M_n$ for each $n$. The [[塔式法则|tower property]] averages through nested information. For two steps,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对整数时间 $n=0,1,\ldots$，只要已经满足适应性与可积性，逐期检查 $E[M_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=M_n$ 就够了。[[塔式法则]]允许通过嵌套信息逐层平均。例如两步时，

$$
E[M_{n+2}\mid\mathcal F_n]
=E\!\left[E[M_{n+2}\mid\mathcal F_{n+1}]\mid\mathcal F_n\right]
=E[M_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=M_n
\quad\text{a.s.}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeating the same finite argument gives every later integer time $m>n$. The reverse direction is immediate: the definition already includes $m=n+1$. Equivalently, the next increment has conditional mean zero, $E[M_{n+1}-M_n\mid\mathcal F_n]=0$; [[鞅差序列|martingale differences]] study those increments separately. Independence was a convenient way to check the coin example, not part of the definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限次重复同样的步骤，就得到任意更晚整数时刻 $m>n$。反方向直接成立，因为定义本来就包含 $m=n+1$。也可以等价地说，下一步增量的条件均值为零：$E[M_{n+1}-M_n\mid\mathcal F_n]=0$；这些增量由[[鞅差序列]]单独研究。独立性只是验证硬币例子的一种方便方法，并不是鞅定义的一部分。

> [!warning] More information can change the answer · 增加信息可能改变结论
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a single fair toss, let $M_0=0$ and $M_1=\pm1$. With no result known at time $0$, $E[M_1\mid\mathcal F_0]=0$. If a larger information set $\mathcal G_0$ already reveals that toss, then $E[M_1\mid\mathcal G_0]=M_1$, which is not $0$. The same values remain adapted when information is enlarged, but the martingale property can fail. See [[适应不推鞅|why adaptedness alone does not imply a martingale]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对单次公平投掷，令 $M_0=0$、$M_1=\pm1$。若时刻 $0$ 尚不知道结果，则 $E[M_1\mid\mathcal F_0]=0$；若更大的信息集 $\mathcal G_0$ 已经揭示这次投掷，则 $E[M_1\mid\mathcal G_0]=M_1$，不等于 $0$。同样的过程在扩大信息后仍然适应，但可能不再是鞅，详见[[适应不推鞅]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking ordinary expectations gives $E[M_t]=E[M_s]$, but [[恒定均值不推鞅|constant unconditional means do not establish the conditional property]]. Replacing equality by $\ge$ gives a [[次鞅|submartingale]], and by $\le$ a [[超鞅|supermartingale]]; every martingale satisfies both. Nonlinear transformations need their own argument: [[凸变换产生次鞅|an integrable convex transformation of a martingale]] generally yields a submartingale, rather than preserving equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再取普通期望，可得 $E[M_t]=E[M_s]$，但[[恒定均值不推鞅|无条件均值恒定不足以证明条件性质]]。把等号换成 $\ge$ 得到[[次鞅]]，换成 $\le$ 得到[[超鞅]]；每个鞅都同时满足两者。非线性变换需要另行论证：[[凸变换产生次鞅|可积的凸变换]]一般给出次鞅，不保证继续保持等号。
