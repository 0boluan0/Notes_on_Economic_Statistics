# Centre of mass
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*质心*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **centre of mass** is the average position of an object's mass. A location containing more mass contributes more to this average. In a uniform gravitational field, it gives the balance position: the turning effects of mass on opposite sides cancel there. This connects an everyday question—where should a support go?—to a [[加权平均|weighted average of positions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**质心**是物体质量的平均位置：某个位置所含质量越大，它对平均位置的贡献越大。在均匀重力场中，质心给出平衡位置，两侧质量产生的转动作用在那里抵消。这把“支点应该放在哪里”这个日常问题，变成了位置的[[加权平均]]。

> [!example] A heavier mass pulls the average towards it · 较重的质量把平均位置拉向自己
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Put a $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ point mass at $x=0\,\mathrm m$ and a $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ point mass at $x=4\,\mathrm m$ on a weightless rigid rod. Their centre of mass is
> $$\bar x=\frac{1\times0+3\times4}{1+3}=3\,\mathrm m.$$
> At this support position, the first mass is $3\,\mathrm m$ to the left and the second is $1\,\mathrm m$ to the right. The mass-times-distance products are both $3\,\mathrm{kg\,m}$, so the gravitational turning effects balance. The unweighted midpoint $2\,\mathrm m$ would ignore the unequal masses.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在不计质量的刚性细杆上，$x=0\,\mathrm m$ 处放 $1\,\mathrm{kg}$ 质点，$x=4\,\mathrm m$ 处放 $3\,\mathrm{kg}$ 质点，质心为 $3\,\mathrm m$。支在这里时，两质点分别位于左侧 $3\,\mathrm m$ 和右侧 $1\,\mathrm m$；质量乘距离都为 $3\,\mathrm{kg\,m}$，重力产生的转动作用抵消。直接取中点 $2\,\mathrm m$ 会忽略质量差别。

## Averaging discrete positions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*平均离散位置*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For finitely many point masses $m_i\ge0$ at positions $x_i$, let $M=\sum_i m_i>0$ be their total mass. The index $i$ labels the masses, and the bar in $\bar x$ marks the average coordinate. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于有限多个质点，设第 $i$ 个质点的质量 $m_i\ge0$、位置为 $x_i$，总质量 $M=\sum_i m_i>0$。下标 $i$ 用来区分质点，$\bar x$ 上的横线表示平均坐标。于是

$$\bar x=\frac{\sum_i m_i x_i}{M},\qquad
\sum_i m_i(x_i-\bar x)=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second equality follows by expanding the sum and substituting the first. It says that the signed mass-weighted offsets from the centre cancel. In a plane, average the two coordinates using the same masses:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

展开第二个和，再代入第一个公式，就得到零：相对于质心，按质量加权的带符号偏移相互抵消。在平面上，分别平均两个坐标，并使用同一组质量：

$$(\bar x,\bar y)=\left(\frac{\sum_i m_i x_i}{M},\frac{\sum_i m_i y_i}{M}\right).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sum $\sum_i m_i x_i$ is a **first moment**: it accumulates mass times a coordinate. Dividing its units, mass times length, by the total mass leaves a length. Coordinates may be negative; mass weights remain nonnegative. With all masses on a bounded interval, their weighted mean lies between the smallest and largest positions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\sum_i m_i x_i$ 称为**一阶矩**，把“质量乘坐标”累加起来。它的单位是质量乘长度，除以总质量后留下长度。坐标可以为负，质量权重仍须非负。若全部质点位于一个有界区间内，加权平均位置必然在最左与最右位置之间。

## From individual masses to a continuous body
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从质点到连续物体*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a continuous distribution, $dm$ means the mass assigned to a small piece, and integration replaces the sum over pieces. For example, a thin rod with linear density $\lambda(x)$, measured in mass per unit length, has $dm=\lambda(x)\,dx$. In a plane the definition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于连续分布，$dm$ 表示一小份材料的质量，积分取代对各份质量的求和。例如，细杆的线密度为 $\lambda(x)$、单位为“质量／长度”时，有 $dm=\lambda(x)\,dx$。平面中的定义是

$$M=\int dm,\qquad
(\bar x,\bar y)=\left(\frac{1}{M}\int x\,dm,\frac{1}{M}\int y\,dm\right).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The integrals run over the whole mass distribution. We require nonnegative mass, $0<M<\infty$, and finite absolute first moments:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些积分覆盖整个质量分布。要求质量非负、$0<M<\infty$，并且绝对一阶矩有限：

$$\int |x|\,dm<\infty,\qquad\int |y|\,dm<\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These conditions make each average an ordinary finite number, rather than an undefined cancellation of infinite positive and negative contributions. For a bounded body of finite mass, they hold automatically. A zero total mass leaves nothing to average. The separate card [[质心积分计算|calculating centres of mass by integration]] explains how to express the mass and moment integrals using slices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些条件保证平均坐标是通常意义下的有限数，而不是无穷正负贡献之间无定义的抵消。对于有界且质量有限的物体，绝对一阶矩条件自动成立；总质量为零则没有可平均的质量。怎样通过切片写出质量与一阶矩，见[[质心积分计算]]。

## When the geometric centre is the mass centre
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*形心何时等于质心*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a bounded planar region with well-defined positive area $A$, its **area centroid** averages position by area $dA$. A lamina is a thin sheet modelled by such a region. If its mass per unit area is the same positive constant $\rho$ everywhere, then $dm=\rho\,dA$ and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于面积有定义且为正的有界平面区域，**面积形心**以面积 $dA$ 为权重平均位置。薄片是用这种平面区域表示的很薄的材料。若每单位面积的质量处处等于同一个正常数 $\rho$，则 $dm=\rho\,dA$，并且

$$\bar x=\frac{\int x\rho\,dA}{\rho A}=\frac{\int x\,dA}{A},\qquad
\bar y=\frac{\int y\rho\,dA}{\rho A}=\frac{\int y\,dA}{A}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus the mass centre equals the area centroid for uniform density. With varying density, they can differ: the denser side receives greater mass weight even if both sides have equal area. To infer the mass centre’s position from a line of symmetry, check that the mass distribution respects the symmetry as well as the shape. Pairing equal masses on opposite sides explains the cancellation. A uniform circular annulus has its centre of mass at the centre of the hole, where there is no material; a centre of mass need not lie inside the material itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，均匀密度时质心等于面积形心。密度变化时，二者可以不同：即使两边面积相同，较密一侧也获得更大的质量权重。根据一条对称轴推断质心位置时，要检查质量分布和形状都遵守该对称性；把两侧相等质量配对，就能看出偏移如何抵消。均匀圆环的质心在圆孔中心，那里没有材料，所以质心不一定落在材料内部。

> [!tip] Which centre does a volume formula need? · 体积公式需要哪一种中心
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[Pappus体积定理|Pappus's volume theorem]] uses the **area centroid** of the rotating region. It is a geometric statement. If the actual plate has nonuniform density, using its mass centre in place of the area centroid can give the wrong volume.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[Pappus体积定理]]使用旋转区域的**面积形心**，因为它计算的是几何体积。实际薄片若密度不均匀，用质量质心代替面积形心，可能得到错误的体积。
