# 质心是物体各位置按质量加权平均得到的位置
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The centre of mass is the mass-weighted average position of a body*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

质心是把物体各位置按其质量份额平均得到的位置。设质量非负、总质量 $M=\int dm$ 严格为正且有限，并且位置的一阶绝对矩有限，则平面物体的质心为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The centre of mass averages a body's positions according to their shares of its mass. With nonnegative mass, finite positive total mass $M=\int dm$, and finite absolute first moments of position, a planar body's centre of mass is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
(\bar x,\bar y)=\left(\frac1M\int x\,dm,\;\frac1M\int y\,dm\right).
$$

直线上离散质点的定义相应为 $\bar x=\sum_i m_ix_i/\sum_i m_i$。例如在 $x=0$ 放质量 $1$、在 $x=4$ 放质量 $3$，质心为 $\bar x=3$；较重的位置贡献更多权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For discrete point masses on a line, the definition is $\bar x=\sum_i m_ix_i/\sum_i m_i$. Mass $1$ at $x=0$ and mass $3$ at $x=4$ give $\bar x=3$: the heavier location receives more weight.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有界且面积严格为正的平面区域，其形心是按面积 $dA$ 平均的位置。密度均匀时 $dm=\rho\,dA$，常数 $\rho$ 在分子分母中约去，因此质心等于形心；密度不均匀时必须保留质量权重，不能直接假定两者相等。在均匀重力场中，质心对应物体的平衡位置，但它不一定落在材料内部，例如均匀圆环的质心在中间孔内。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bounded planar region with positive area has a centroid defined by area-weighted position. Uniform density gives $dm=\rho\,dA$, so the constant density cancels and the centre of mass equals the centroid. Nonuniform density requires mass weighting. In a uniform gravitational field the centre of mass gives the balance position, but it need not lie within the material; a uniform ring has its centre of mass in the hole.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算时需要把这些质量矩转成可求的一元积分，具体切片选择见 [[质心积分计算]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To calculate these moments using one-variable integrals, choose appropriate slices as described in [[质心积分计算|calculating centres of mass by integration]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
