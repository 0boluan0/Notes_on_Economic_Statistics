# Positive-definite matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正定矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **positive-definite matrix** assigns a strictly positive quadratic value to every nonzero vector. Think of a displacement with several coordinates: however its coordinates combine, the associated quadratic cost is positive unless the displacement is zero. The condition concerns the value of a whole expression, rather than the signs of the matrix's individual entries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**正定矩阵**使每个非零向量对应的二次型值都严格为正。可以把向量看成有多个坐标的位移：无论这些坐标怎样组合，只要位移不为零，对应的二次代价就为正。条件检查的是整个表达式的值，而不是矩阵各个元素的正负号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $D=\operatorname{diag}(2,5)$: the listed numbers occupy the main diagonal and the other entries are zero. For a real column $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], its [[二次型|quadratic form]] is the scalar
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看[[对角矩阵]] $D=\operatorname{diag}(2,5)$：列出的数放在主对角线上，其余元素为零。对实列向量 $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$，$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，它的[[二次型]]是一个数：

$$x^TDx=\begin{pmatrix}x_1&x_2\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&5\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}x_1\\x_2\end{pmatrix}=2x_1^2+5x_2^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $x\ne0$, at least one coordinate is nonzero, so at least one term is positive and the other is nonnegative. This proves positivity for every nonzero $x$, not just for a few numerical trials. At $x=0$ the value is necessarily zero, which explains the exclusion of the zero vector in the definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只要 $x\ne0$，至少一个坐标非零，于是至少一项为正，另一项非负。这证明了每个非零 $x$ 都满足要求，而不只是试算几个向量。$x=0$ 时结果必为零，所以定义必须排除零向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The real definition tests every direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*实数定义检查每个方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n\ge1$ be a finite integer. A [[实对称矩阵|real symmetric]] matrix has size $n\times n$, real entries, and $A=A^T$. Under this symmetry convention, positive definiteness means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n\ge1$ 为有限整数。[[实对称矩阵]]是具有实数元素的 $n\times n$ 方阵，并满足 $A=A^T$。在这个对称性约定下，正定定义为

$$\boxed{A\succ0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
A=A^T\ \text{and}\ x^TAx>0\ \text{for every }x\in\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $A\succ0$ abbreviates this entire condition. For a $1\times1$ matrix $[a]$, it reduces to $a>0$, since the expression is $ax^2$. In higher dimensions, different coordinates can interact. Scaling a real vector by $c\ne0$ gives $(cx)^TA(cx)=c^2x^TAx$, so the sign is unchanged along the same direction. Numerical trials alone leave untested directions; a proof must also account for those directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记号 $A\succ0$ 是整个条件的简写。对 $1\times1$ 矩阵 $[a]$，二次型为 $ax^2$，因此定义退化为 $a>0$。维数更高时，不同坐标之间还可能相互作用。把实向量乘以非零实数 $c$，有 $(cx)^TA(cx)=c^2x^TAx$，所以同一方向上的正负号不变；单靠数值试算会留下尚未检查的方向，证明还必须覆盖它们。

> [!example] Negative entries can occur in a positive-definite matrix · 正定矩阵可以含负元素
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}$. It is symmetric, and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}$。它对称，并且
>
> $$x^TAx=2x_1^2-2x_1x_2+2x_2^2
> =x_1^2+x_2^2+(x_1-x_2)^2>0\quad(x\ne0).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first two squares already give a positive sum for a nonzero vector. Thus $A$ is positive definite even though its off-diagonal entries are negative.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 非零向量的前两个平方项之和就已为正。因此，尽管非对角元是负数，$A$ 仍然正定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive diagonal entries alone do not suffice. With $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix}$, the coordinate directions give positive values, but $x=(1,-1)^T$ gives $x^TBx=1-4+1=-2$. The diagonal entries test only the coordinate axes; the cross term can change the sign on a combined direction. This is the [[正对角元的正定边界|positive-diagonal boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仅有正对角元也不够。取 $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix}$，坐标轴方向的二次型都为正，但 $x=(1,-1)^T$ 给出 $x^TBx=1-4+1=-2$。对角元只检查坐标轴；交叉项可以改变组合方向上的符号。这就是[[正对角元的正定边界]]。

## Complex vectors require conjugation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*复向量需要取共轭*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a complex $n\times n$ matrix, use [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian symmetry]] $A=A^*$. The star denotes [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]: transpose, then replace each $a+bi$ by $a-bi$, where $i^2=-1$. The definition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对复 $n\times n$ 矩阵，使用 [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian 对称性]] $A=A^*$。星号表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]：先转置，再把每个 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$，其中 $i^2=-1$。定义为

$$\boxed{A\succ0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
A=A^*\ \text{and}\ z^*Az>0\ \text{for every }z\in\mathbb C^n\setminus\{0\}.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hermitian symmetry makes the scalar real: $\overline{z^*Az}=z^*A^*z=z^*Az$, where the bar denotes complex conjugation. Its sign can therefore be compared with zero. For example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Hermitian 对称性保证这个数为实数：$\overline{z^*Az}=z^*A^*z=z^*Az$，横线表示复共轭。因此可以比较它与零的大小。例如，

$$H=\begin{pmatrix}2&i\\-i&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
z^*Hz=|z_1+iz_2|^2+|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2>0\quad(z\ne0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $|w|^2=\bar w w$ is the squared magnitude of a complex number, always nonnegative. The last two terms guarantee strict positivity. Ordinary transpose cannot replace the star: even for the positive-definite scalar matrix $[1]$, the nonzero scalar $z=i$ gives $z^T[1]z=-1$, whereas $z^*[1]z=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $|w|^2=\bar w w$ 是复数模的平方，总是非负；最后两项保证严格为正。普通转置不能代替星号：即使取正定的一阶矩阵 $[1]$，非零复数 $z=i$ 也会给出 $z^T[1]z=-1$，而 $z^*[1]z=1$。

## Keep the convention and its consequences separate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*区分定义约定与后续结论*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card reserves “positive definite” for real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrices. For a nonsymmetric real matrix, the quadratic expression depends only on its [[二次型对称化|symmetric part]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡把“正定矩阵”保留给实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵。对非对称实矩阵，二次表达式只取决于它的[[二次型对称化|对称部分]]：

$$x^TAx=x^T\frac{A+A^T}{2}x.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For instance, $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}$ gives $x_1^2+x_2^2$, but it is not symmetric, so it is not called positive definite under this convention. Its symmetric part is $\operatorname{diag}(1,1)$ and is positive definite. A source using a broader convention must not be used to transfer symmetric spectral tests to the original nonsymmetric matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}$ 给出 $x_1^2+x_2^2$，但自身不对称，因此按本卡约定不称为正定矩阵；它的对称部分 $\operatorname{diag}(1,1)$ 才是正定矩阵。即使某资料采用较宽的叫法，也不能据此把对称矩阵的谱判据套到原非对称矩阵上。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every positive-definite matrix is [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefinite]], because strict positivity away from zero implies nonnegativity everywhere. Positive definiteness also guarantees an [[逆矩阵|inverse]], an operation that reverses multiplication by the matrix. For the [[正定与半正定可逆性|invertibility argument]], $Ax=0$ would force $x^*Ax=0$, so only the zero vector can be lost. The converse requires additional assumptions; $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ is invertible but not positive definite. The [[正定与半正定的谱判据|spectral criterion]] converts the all-directions condition into positive eigenvalues, and the [[正定谱下界|uniform lower-bound theorem]] quantifies positivity relative to squared [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]]. For the opening $D$, $x^TDx\ge2(x_1^2+x_2^2)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个正定矩阵都[[半正定矩阵|半正定]]，因为非零处严格为正蕴含处处非负。正定性也保证[[逆矩阵]]存在，也就是矩阵的乘法作用可以撤销。[[正定与半正定可逆性|可逆性的论证]]从以下观察开始：若 $Ax=0$，就会有 $x^*Ax=0$，因此只有零向量会被映成零。反向需要额外条件；$\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ 可逆，却不正定。[[正定与半正定的谱判据|谱判据]]把所有方向的条件转换为特征值全正；[[正定谱下界|统一下界定理]]进一步按[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]的平方量化正性。开头的 $D$ 就满足 $x^TDx\ge2(x_1^2+x_2^2)$。
