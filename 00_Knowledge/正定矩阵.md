# 实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵的二次型在每个非零方向严格为正时称为正定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrix is positive definite when its quadratic form is strictly positive in every nonzero direction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 定义
> 实矩阵 $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ 称为正定，记作 $A\succ0$，当且仅当
> $$A=A^T,\qquad x^TAx>0\quad\text{对每个 }x\ne0.$$
> 复数版本要求 $A=A^*$ 且 $x^*Ax>0$ 对每个非零复向量成立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real matrix is positive definite, written $A\succ0$, when it is symmetric and $x^TAx>0$ for every nonzero real vector. Over $\mathbb C$, require Hermitian symmetry and $x^*Ax>0$ for every nonzero complex vector.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

量词“每个非零方向”不可弱化为“坐标轴方向”或“试过几个向量”。例如
$$
A=\operatorname{diag}(2,5)
$$
满足 $x^TAx=2x_1^2+5x_2^2>0$ 对每个 $x\ne0$ 成立，所以 $A\succ0$。正定怎样进一步给出相对于 $\|x\|_2^2$ 的统一下界，见 [[正定谱下界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The quantifier over every nonzero direction is essential. For example, $A=\operatorname{diag}(2,5)$ is positive definite because $x^TAx=2x_1^2+5x_2^2>0$ for every nonzero $x$. The uniform lower bound supplied by positive definiteness is developed in [[正定谱下界|the spectral lower-bound result]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里沿用线性代数中的标准约定，把“正定矩阵”保留给实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵。有些资料也对非对称 $A$ 说“$x^TAx>0$”；那一性质其实只属于它的对称部分，应先明确替换，避免把对称矩阵的谱判据误用于 $A$ 本身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the term positive-definite matrix is reserved for a real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrix. If another convention calls a nonsymmetric $A$ positive definite from $x^TAx>0$, that property belongs to its symmetric part and does not justify applying symmetric spectral tests to $A$ itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 只知道所有 $a_{ii}>0$ 为什么还没有验证定义？
>
> **答案：** 这只检查了 $x=e_i$ 的坐标轴方向，交叉项仍可能在斜方向造成负值。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Question:** Why does knowing that every diagonal entry $a_{ii}$ is positive not yet verify positive definiteness?
>
> **Answer:** It checks only the coordinate directions $x=e_i$; cross terms may still make the quadratic form negative in an oblique direction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
