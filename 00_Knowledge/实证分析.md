# 实证分析借助模型与证据描述和解释经济现象并预测变化的后果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Positive analysis uses models and evidence to describe and explain economic phenomena and predict consequences*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实证分析回答“是什么、为什么、如果改变某个条件会怎样”。这里的“实证”对应 positive，不限于统计回归：在明确假设下推导一个模型的行为预测，也是实证分析。数据则帮助检验预测、辨别机制和估计效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive analysis asks what happens, why, and what would follow from a change in conditions. It is not limited to statistical estimation: deriving a behavioural prediction from a model with stated assumptions is also positive analysis. Data help test predictions, distinguish mechanisms, and estimate effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，“对工资征税后，工时会怎样变化？”是实证问题。先写出税制怎样改变预算约束，再分析[[劳动供给效应|劳动与闲暇选择]]；若模型给不出唯一方向，就需要说明还缺哪些参数或证据。“应该征多少税？”则还需要[[规范分析|评价标准]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“How will hours worked respond to a wage tax?” is a positive question. First specify the change in the budget constraint, then analyse [[劳动供给效应|labour–leisure choice]]. If the model does not determine a unique direction, identify the parameters or evidence still needed. “How much should we tax?” additionally requires [[规范分析|an evaluative criterion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实证结论不直接评价结果好坏，但研究仍有问题选择、测量和模型假设。对政策的因果解释，要交代[[因果研究设计检查|反事实与识别依据]]；不能把“看到了相关性”写成“已经知道政策造成什么”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A positive conclusion does not itself judge an outcome desirable, but the research still involves choices of questions, measurements, and assumptions. A causal policy claim needs an account of its [[因果研究设计检查|counterfactual and identification strategy]]; observed association alone does not establish what the policy caused.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
