# Exit rates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*出口率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **exit rate** measures how quickly a continuous-time chain leaves its present state, regardless of which destination it chooses. If a state has two possible outgoing directions with rates $2$ and $3$ per hour, its total exit rate is $5$ per hour. This combines the possible ways to leave; it is not a probability and does not count jumps during time spent in other states.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**出口率**衡量连续时间链离开当前状态的快慢，暂不区分它接下来去哪。如果一个状态有两个离开方向，速率分别为每小时 $2$ 和 $3$，总出口率就是每小时 $5$。它合并了各种离开方式，不是一个概率，也不是把待在其他状态时发生的跳跃算进来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work with a [[CTMC定义|time-homogeneous pure-jump CTMC]] and a [[生成矩阵约束|stable conservative generator]] $Q=(q_{ij})$. In the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]], off-diagonal entry $q_{ij}$ is the rate from $i$ to $j$. The exit rate of $i$ is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑时间齐次的[[CTMC定义|纯跳 CTMC]]，使用[[生成矩阵约束|稳定、保守的生成矩阵]] $Q=(q_{ij})$。在[[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]]下，非对角元 $q_{ij}$ 是从 $i$ 到 $j$ 的速率。状态 $i$ 的出口率为

$$q_i=\sum_{j\ne i}q_{ij}=-q_{ii},\qquad 0\le q_i<\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sum excludes the diagonal. The nonpositive entry $q_{ii}$ balances the outgoing rates; it is not an additional route out of the state. If a model uses a subgenerator that omits killing outside its listed states, $-q_{ii}$ also includes that omitted rate. The displayed equality with the listed off-diagonal sum then needs the cemetery state to be included.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求和时不包含对角元。非正的对角元 $q_{ii}$ 用来抵消各方向的速率，并不是另一条离开路线。若模型采用没有列出外部终止状态的子生成矩阵，$-q_{ii}$ 还包含未列出的杀死率；这时需要把墓地状态纳入，才能与全部非对角率之和相等。

## How long do we wait before leaving?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*离开之前等待多久？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $H_i$ be the time from entry into $i$ until the first genuine change of state. For $q_i>0$, the [[CTMC指数停留时间|holding-time law]] is an [[指数分布|exponential distribution]] with rate $q_i$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $H_i$ 表示进入 $i$ 后，到第一次真实改变状态为止的时间。若 $q_i>0$，[[CTMC指数停留时间|停留时间]]服从速率为 $q_i$ 的[[指数分布]]：

$$\Pr(H_i>t)=e^{-q_i t}\quad(t\ge0),\qquad
\mathbb E[H_i]=\frac1{q_i}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[期望|mean]] $1/q_i$ is an average waiting time, not a scheduled departure time. The product $q_i t$ is dimensionless: a rate measured per hour gives $1/q_i$ in hours. The probability of at least one departure by time $h$ is $1-e^{-q_i h}$, whose [[CTMC短时概率展开|first-order approximation]] is $q_i h$. A rate by itself may be larger than one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[期望|均值]] $1/q_i$ 是平均等待时间，不是规定必须在那个时刻离开。乘积 $q_i t$ 没有单位；若速率按每小时计，$1/q_i$ 就以小时计。在时长 $h$ 内至少离开一次的概率为 $1-e^{-q_i h}$，它的[[CTMC短时概率展开|一阶近似]]才是 $q_i h$。速率这个数本身完全可以大于一。

> [!example] A total rate of five · 总速率为五
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose state $i$ has rates $q_{ia}=2$ and $q_{ib}=3$ per hour, with no other exits. Then $q_i=5$, $q_{ii}=-5$, and the mean holding time is $1/5$ hour, or $12$ minutes. In $0.01$ hour, the probability of a departure is $1-e^{-0.05}\approx0.0487706$, close to $0.05$ but not equal to it. Conditional on making the next genuine jump, the destination probabilities are $2/5$ and $3/5$, respectively.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若从 $i$ 到 $a,b$ 的速率分别为每小时 $2,3$，没有其他离开方向，那么 $q_i=5$、$q_{ii}=-5$，平均停留时间为 $1/5$ 小时，即 $12$ 分钟。$0.01$ 小时内离开的概率为 $1-e^{-0.05}\approx0.0487706$，接近但不等于 $0.05$。下一次真实跳跃去往 $a,b$ 的概率分别为 $2/5,3/5$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Normalizing the separate rates by the total describes the [[嵌入跳链|embedded jump chain]]; the [[生成矩阵分解|generator decomposition]] separates “how long?” from “where next?”. Knowing only $q_i=5$ does not distinguish a split $2+3$ from a split $4+1$. Both give the same waiting-time law but different next-state probabilities. Those probabilities concern the first jump, not the state after an arbitrary fixed time, when further jumps may have occurred.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把各方向的速率除以总速率，就得到[[嵌入跳链]]的概率；[[生成矩阵分解]]把“等多久”和“下一站去哪”分开。只知道 $q_i=5$，无法区分 $2+3$ 与 $4+1$ 两种分配：它们的等待时间分布一样，下一站的概率却不同。这里的概率描述第一次真实跳跃，不是任意固定时长后的状态；固定时长内可能已经又跳了几次。

## Zero rate and endpoints after possible returns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零速率与可能返回后的终点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $q_i=0$, nonnegativity forces every $q_{ij}$ for $j\ne i$ to be zero. The state is absorbing and $H_i=\infty$ almost surely. Handle it separately: there is no finite mean $1/q_i$ and no next-destination distribution obtained by dividing by $q_i$. A diagonal entry of $-4$ instead means exit rate $4$ and mean holding time $1/4$ in the chosen time unit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $q_i=0$，由非负性可知所有 $j\ne i$ 的 $q_{ij}$ 都为零。该状态是吸收态，$H_i=\infty$ 几乎必然成立，需要单独处理：没有有限的均值 $1/q_i$，也不能除以 $q_i$ 来定义下一站的分布。相反，对角元为 $-4$ 时，出口率为 $4$，按选定的时间单位，平均停留时间就是 $1/4$。

> [!note] Staying throughout is different from being back · 始终没走与最后又在这里不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Write $p_{ii}(t)=\Pr_i(X_t=i)$ for the probability of being in $i$ at time $t$ when starting there. The event $H_i>t$ means no departure has happened. The event $X_t=i$ can also occur after leaving and returning. Consequently $p_{ii}(t)$ need not equal $e^{-q_i t}$. Exit rate controls the first departure, whereas a finite-time transition probability combines all allowed paths.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 记 $p_{ii}(t)=\Pr_i(X_t=i)$，表示从 $i$ 出发，时刻 $t$ 处于 $i$ 的概率。事件 $H_i>t$ 表示始终没有离开；事件 $X_t=i$ 还可能由先离开、后返回产生。因此，$p_{ii}(t)$ 不一定等于 $e^{-q_i t}$。出口率控制第一次离开，而有限时长的转移概率汇总了所有允许的路径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, finiteness of each $q_i$ is only a local condition. It does not rule out visiting states with ever larger rates and making infinitely many jumps in finite time; that is [[CTMC爆炸|explosion]]. A single bound on all exit rates is a separate [[有界率非爆炸|sufficient non-explosion condition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后，每个 $q_i$ 分别有限只是局部条件。它不能排除不断进入速率更大的状态，并在有限时间内跳跃无限次，即[[CTMC爆炸|爆炸]]。对全部出口率给出同一个有限上界，是另一条[[有界率非爆炸|非爆炸充分条件]]。
