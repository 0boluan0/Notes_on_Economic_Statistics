# 多元换元公式用 Jacobian 行列式的绝对值校正局部体积
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The multivariable change-of-variables formula corrects local volume by the absolute Jacobian determinant*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 定理
> 设 $U,V\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 为开集，$T:U\to V$ 是 $C^1$ 微分同胚，$g:V\to\mathbb R$ 连续且可积。则
> $$
> \int_V g(y)\,dy
> =
> \int_U g(T(x))\,|\det DT(x)|\,dx.
> $$
> 绝对值把 Jacobian 的有向体积缩放转成积分所需的非负普通体积缩放。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $U,V\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be open, let $T:U\to V$ be a $C^1$ diffeomorphism, and let $g:V\to\mathbb R$ be continuous and integrable. The displayed formula transforms the integral using the absolute Jacobian determinant.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定理的条件是整体的一一对应与 $C^1$ 正则性，不是“算出一个 Jacobian”就自动成立。$\det DT$ 的符号记录坐标取向；普通体积元不带取向，因此必须使用绝对值。有向积分或微分形式属于另一种语境，不能与本式混用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem requires a global one-to-one correspondence between the stated domains and $C^1$ regularity; merely computing a Jacobian does not make the formula applicable. The sign of $\det DT$ records coordinate orientation, whereas an ordinary volume element is unoriented and therefore uses the absolute value. Oriented integration and differential forms belong to a different setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么普通体积换元使用 $|\det DT|$ 而不是 $\det DT$？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does an ordinary-volume change of variables use $|\det DT|$ rather than $\det DT$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** determinant 的负号只记录坐标取向翻转，而普通体积元不能因此变成负数。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** A negative determinant records a reversal of coordinate orientation, but an ordinary volume element cannot become negative for that reason.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
