# Multivariable change of variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*多元换元公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **change of variables** computes the same integral using different coordinates. An integral adds local values weighted by small lengths, areas or volumes; [[定积分|the one-variable definite integral]] is the length-based starting point. The new coordinates may make a curved region simpler, but equal-sized coordinate boxes need not represent equal-sized pieces of the original region. The Jacobian factor corrects their volume before the contributions are added.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**换元**是用另一套坐标计算同一个积分。积分把局部数值乘以小段长度、面积或体积后累加；一元的[[定积分]]以长度为权重，是理解这个过程的起点。新坐标可能让弯曲的区域变简单，但新坐标里同样大小的小方块，未必对应原区域里同样大的部分。Jacobian 因子先校正这些部分的体积，再把贡献相加。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with a rectangle: put $T(u,v)=(2u,3v)$ on $U=(0,1)^2$. Its image is $V=(0,2)\times(0,3)$. The parameter square has area $1$, while the image has area $6$. Its [[Jacobian矩阵|derivative matrix]] is $DT=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$, with determinant $6$. Integrating the constant function $1$ over $V$ therefore becomes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看矩形：在 $U=(0,1)^2$ 上令 $T(u,v)=(2u,3v)$，像为 $V=(0,2)\times(0,3)$。参数正方形面积为 $1$，像矩形面积为 $6$。它的[[Jacobian矩阵|导数矩阵]]是 $DT=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$，行列式为 $6$，所以在 $V$ 上积分常数函数 $1$ 变成

$$\int_V1\,dx\,dy=\int_0^1\int_0^1 6\,du\,dv=6.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without the factor, the calculation would measure the parameter square instead of the requested rectangle. For a nonlinear map, this correction generally varies with position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果漏掉因子，算出的就是参数正方形的面积，而不是题目要求的矩形面积。非线性映射的校正因子通常还会随位置改变。

## Read the map from new coordinates to old coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先辨认新坐标怎样映到原坐标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n\ge1$, and let $U,V\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be [[开集|open sets]]. Write $y=T(x)$ with $x\in U$ the coordinates used on the right side of the integral and $y\in V$ the original coordinates. Assume $T$ is a **$C^1$ diffeomorphism**: it is one-to-one and onto $V$, and both $T$ and its inverse have continuous first derivatives. This is a correspondence over the stated domains, stronger than merely having a local inverse near one point; see the [[逆函数定理|inverse function theorem]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n\ge1$，$U,V\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 为[[开集]]。写 $y=T(x)$，其中 $x\in U$ 是积分右侧使用的坐标，$y\in V$ 是原坐标。假设 $T$ 是 **$C^1$ 微分同胚**：它一一且映满 $V$，并且 $T$ 与逆映射的一阶导数都连续。这是整个所述区域间的对应，比只在某点附近存在局部逆更强；局部结论见[[逆函数定理]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Jacobian矩阵|Jacobian matrix]] $DT(x)$ records how a small input displacement changes all outputs. Its [[Jacobian行列式|determinant]] scales signed first-order volume; the absolute value gives ordinary volume. Let $g:V\to\mathbb R$ be continuous and absolutely integrable, meaning $\int_V|g(y)|\,dy<\infty$. A standard form of the theorem is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Jacobian矩阵]] $DT(x)$ 记录小的输入位移怎样改变全部输出；其 [[Jacobian行列式|行列式]]缩放一阶有向体积，绝对值给出普通体积。设 $g:V\to\mathbb R$ 连续且绝对可积，即 $\int_V|g(y)|\,dy<\infty$。定理的一个标准版本为

> [!important] The same total in two coordinates · 两套坐标中的同一个总量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Evaluate the old integrand at the corresponding point, multiply by the local volume factor, and integrate over the new domain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在对应位置计算原被积函数，乘局部体积因子，再在新区域上积分。
>
> $$\boxed{\int_V g(y)\,dy=\int_U g(T(x))\,|\det DT(x)|\,dx.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $dx$ and $dy$ denote $n$-dimensional volume elements, not scalar derivatives. For $n=2$, for example, $dx$ abbreviates $dx_1\,dx_2$. The composition $g(T(x))$ and the domain $U=T^{-1}(V)$ are as essential as the determinant. This is the volume-integral counterpart of [[定积分换元|one-variable substitution]]; signed one-variable limits can instead account for orientation by reversing the bounds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $dx$、$dy$ 表示 $n$ 维体积元，不是标量导数。例如 $n=2$ 时，$dx$ 是 $dx_1\,dx_2$ 的简写。复合函数 $g(T(x))$ 与新区域 $U=T^{-1}(V)$ 和行列式同样不可遗漏。这是[[定积分换元|一元换元]]的体积积分版本；一元有向积分也可以通过调换积分上下限处理取向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the absolute determinant appears
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么出现绝对行列式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Near each input point, differentiability approximates $T$ by its derivative matrix. A small parameter box is therefore approximated by a parallelepiped with volume multiplied by $|\det DT|$. Its contribution to the original integral is approximately its volume times $g$ at the corresponding output. Adding increasingly fine pieces motivates the formula; controlling the limiting errors is part of the theorem, not an exact claim about each finite box.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在每个输入点附近，可微性让导数矩阵近似 $T$。因此，小参数方块的像近似为平行多面体，体积乘以 $|\det DT|$；它对原积分的贡献近似为该体积乘对应输出处的 $g$。把越来越细的部分相加，可以理解公式的来由；极限误差的控制属于定理内容，不能把每个有限方块的近似当成精确等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $T(u,v)=(-2u,3v)$ instead, the determinant is $-6$ while the image rectangle still has area $6$. The sign records an orientation reversal. Ordinary area, volume and probability integrals use the absolute value. Oriented differential forms use a different convention and cannot be substituted into this formula without accounting for orientation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若改用 $T(u,v)=(-2u,3v)$，行列式为 $-6$，像矩形的面积仍为 $6$。负号记录取向翻转。普通面积、体积与概率积分使用绝对值；有向微分形式采用另一种约定，不能不处理取向就与本式混用。

## Polar coordinates: both the factor and the domain change
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*极坐标：因子与区域一起改变*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a disk of radius $R>0$, use $T(r,\theta)=(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)$ on $0<r<R$, $0<\theta<2\pi$. Removing the center and one radial cut makes this a one-to-one smooth coordinate chart with a smooth inverse. Its derivative and determinant are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对半径 $R>0$ 的圆盘，在 $0<r<R$、$0<\theta<2\pi$ 上使用 $T(r,\theta)=(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)$。去掉圆心与一条径向切口后，这是具有光滑逆的一一坐标图。其导数与行列式为

$$DT(r,\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&-r\sin\theta\\ \sin\theta&r\cos\theta\end{pmatrix},\qquad\det DT=r.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The omitted center, radial cut and outer boundary have area zero, so including them does not change these ordinary area integrals. The disk area is $\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^R r\,dr\,d\theta=\pi R^2$. For a nonconstant integrand, the coordinates inside it must change too:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

被省略的圆心、径向切口和外边界的面积都为零，所以是否包含它们不改变这里的普通面积积分。圆盘面积为 $\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^R r\,dr\,d\theta=\pi R^2$。被积函数不是常数时，它内部的坐标也必须改变：

$$\int_{x^2+y^2<R^2}(x^2+y^2)\,dx\,dy=\int_0^{2\pi}\int_0^R r^2\,r\,dr\,d\theta=\frac{\pi R^4}{2}.$$

> [!warning] A local determinant does not prove one-to-one coverage · 局部行列式不证明一一覆盖
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Extending the same polar parameter range to $0<\theta<4\pi$ keeps $r>0$ and a nonzero determinant, but covers almost every point twice. The integral of $r$ over that parameter region is $2\pi R^2$, not the disk area. A nonzero determinant gives local regularity; global coverage must be checked separately. Splitting into charts must avoid double counting their overlaps.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若把同一极坐标范围扩为 $0<\theta<4\pi$，仍有 $r>0$ 与非零行列式，但几乎每个像点都覆盖两次。在这个参数区域积分 $r$ 得到 $2\pi R^2$，不是圆盘面积。非零行列式给出局部正则性；整体覆盖次数必须另查。分成多个坐标图计算时，也要避免重复计算重叠部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the input and output dimensions differ, the ordinary determinant in this theorem is unavailable; [[矩形Jacobian体积因子|the Gram volume factor]] addresses parametrized lower-dimensional volume. For probability densities with $Y=T(X)$, the output density instead uses the derivative of the inverse, because it converts output volume back to input volume; see [[密度换元的尺度因子|density scaling]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入与输出维数不同时，本定理中的普通行列式无法使用；参数化低维体积由[[矩形Jacobian体积因子]]处理。若 $Y=T(X)$ 是概率变换，输出密度使用逆映射的导数，因为要把输出体积换回输入体积，见[[密度换元的尺度因子]]。
