# Design matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*设计矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A design matrix arranges the numerical features used by a model into a rectangular table: **one row per observation, one column per included feature**. It tells a linear regression which quantities receive coefficients. It is built from the chosen model and data; it is not necessarily a copy of every column in the original spreadsheet.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设计矩阵把模型使用的数值特征排成一张矩形表：**每行一个观测，每列一个纳入的特征**。它告诉线性回归哪些量各自配一个系数。它由选定的模型与数据共同构造，不一定等于原始表格中的全部列。

> [!example] From three observations to a matrix / 从三个观测到矩阵
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose the predictor values are $x_1=0,x_2=1,x_3=2$ and the model is $y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1x_i+u_i$. Here $y_i$ is the observed response, $\beta_0$ the intercept, $\beta_1$ the slope, and $u_i$ the model error for observation $i$. The vectors $y$ and $u$ below stack the three responses and three errors. To express the same equation for all three observations, include a column of ones for the intercept and a column of predictor values:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设解释变量值为 $x_1=0,x_2=1,x_3=2$，模型是 $y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1x_i+u_i$。其中 $y_i$ 是观测响应，$\beta_0$ 是截距，$\beta_1$ 是斜率，$u_i$ 是第 $i$ 个观测的模型误差。下面的向量 $y$、$u$ 分别把三个响应、三个误差排成一列。要一次写出三个观测的同一方程，就为截距放一列全 1，再放一列解释变量值：
>
> $$
> D=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> \beta=\begin{pmatrix}\beta_0\\\beta_1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> y=D\beta+u.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first row gives $\beta_0$, the second gives $\beta_0+\beta_1$, and the third gives $\beta_0+2\beta_1$. For the illustrative coefficient vector $(2,3)'$, the model’s linear predictions are $(2,5,8)'$. Here a prime denotes transpose, turning a listed row into a column. No estimation rule has been applied yet.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一行给出 $\beta_0$，第二行给出 $\beta_0+\beta_1$，第三行给出 $\beta_0+2\beta_1$。若用示意系数向量 $(2,3)'$，模型的线性预测就是 $(2,5,8)'$；这里的撇号表示转置，把列出的行变为一列；还没有使用任何估计规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reading the rows and columns

With $n$ observations and $k$ included features, $D$ is $n\times k$, the coefficient vector is $k\times1$, and $D\beta$ is $n\times1$. This is [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]]: each row is combined with the same coefficients to produce that observation’s prediction. The response vector $y$ is written separately from $D$.

A feature need not be an untouched raw variable. Adding $x^2$ adds the column $(0,1,4)'$ in the example. A categorical predictor may produce indicator columns, such as a column equal to 1 for one category and 0 otherwise. Interactions produce product columns. These transformations can curve the fitted relationship while leaving it [[参数线性|linear in the unknown coefficients]], provided the feature values do not themselves depend on those unknown coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样读行和列

有 $n$ 个观测、$k$ 个纳入特征时，$D$ 为 $n\times k$，系数向量为 $k\times1$，$D\beta$ 为 $n\times1$。这就是[[矩阵乘法]]：每行使用同一组系数进行组合，得到该观测的预测。响应向量 $y$ 与 $D$ 分开写。

特征不必是未经处理的原变量。例子中加入 $x^2$，就增加一列 $(0,1,4)'$。类别变量可以产生指示列，例如属于某类别取 1，否则取 0；交互项产生乘积列。只要特征值本身不依赖这些未知系数，这些变换可以让拟合关系弯曲，同时仍然[[参数线性|对未知系数线性]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The matrix follows the specification

Including an intercept means including a constant feature; fitting through the origin omits it. Including an intercept and indicators for every category creates an exact redundancy, because those indicators sum to the constant column. That is a [[多重共线性|column-dependence problem]], not a reason to delete a data row. [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|Full column rank]] determines whether the OLS coefficient vector is unique.

For prediction at a new observation, build its row with the same feature definitions, units, category coding and training-defined transformations. The row order must also stay aligned with the outcomes during fitting. Calling $D$ “given” in a conditional calculation means conditioning on its realized values; it does not by itself say whether the observations were experimentally fixed or randomly sampled.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 矩阵跟着模型设定走

包含截距意味着包含常数特征；过原点拟合则省略它。若既含截距，又含所有类别的指示列，就产生精确冗余，因为这些指示列相加等于常数列。这是[[多重共线性|列依赖问题]]，不能靠删某个观测行来解决。[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]决定 OLS 系数向量是否唯一。

预测新观测时，应使用同样的特征定义、单位、类别编码与训练阶段确定的变换构造新的一行。拟合时，各行也必须与响应值正确对应。条件计算中把 $D$ 当作“给定”，只是对已经实现的取值做条件分析，本身不说明数据是实验中固定的，还是随机抽样得到的。

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Hansen, *Econometrics*, §3.5](https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~xshi28/Econometrics_Hansen.pdf): stacking observation vectors into $y=X\beta+e$ and the dimensions of the regressor matrix; §4.3 explains conditioning on the realized matrix.
- [NIST, Linear Least Squares Regression](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmd/section1/pmd141.htm): known functions of predictors as features and linearity in parameters. The three-row example is a direct matrix calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Hansen §3.5 核对把观测堆叠成 $y=X\beta+e$ 及解释变量矩阵的维度；§4.3 支持对实现的矩阵取条件。
- NIST 的 Linear Least Squares Regression 核对已知解释变量函数与参数线性；三行例由矩阵乘法直接计算。
