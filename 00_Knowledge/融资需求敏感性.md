# 融资需求敏感性是在其他假设固定时衡量单个驱动变化对融资缺口的影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financing-need sensitivity measures how a single driver changes the funding gap while other assumptions remain fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把[[外部融资需求]]写成驱动的函数，再问净利率、分红政策或销售分别改变时，需要多筹多少资金。敏感性分析适合识别影响较大的假设和临界值；它回答“如果单独改变”，不提供该变化发生的概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Express [[外部融资需求|EFN]] as a function of its drivers, then vary margin, dividends, or sales separately. Sensitivity analysis identifies influential assumptions and thresholds. It answers a conditional “what if,” not the probability of that change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在销售比例模型中，令 $c=a-l$ 为净经营资产收入比，$d$ 为股利支付率，$U$ 为可用金融资产：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the sales-ratio model, let $c=a-l$ be the net-operating-asset ratio, $d$ the payout ratio, and $U$ available financial assets:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$EFN=c(S_1-S_0)-m(1-d)S_1-U.$$

固定其他输入，且预计销售 $S_1>0$ 时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding the other inputs fixed, with forecast sales $S_1>0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac{\partial EFN}{\partial m}=-(1-d)S_1,\qquad \frac{\partial EFN}{\partial d}=mS_1.$$

所以在 $d<1$ 时，提高净利率会减少融资需求；在 $m>0$ 时，提高支付率会增加融资需求。这些结论带着符号条件：利润为负、分红超过当期利润时，不能照搬直观方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Higher margin reduces the funding need when $d<1$; higher payout increases it when $m>0$. The sign conditions matter: losses or distributions exceeding current profit do not support the same automatic interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $S_0=3000$、$S_1=4000$、$c=0.605$、$U=0$。净利率 4.5%、支付率 30% 时，EFN 为 479。只把支付率改为零，EFN 降为 425；只把净利率改为 10%、支付率仍为 30%，EFN 降为 325。两次实验不能同时算作原基准下独立可实现的改善。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With sales rising from 3,000 to 4,000, $c=0.605$, and $U=0$, a 4.5% margin and 30% payout give EFN of 479. Changing only payout to zero reduces it to 425; changing only margin to 10% while retaining 30% payout reduces it to 325. These separate experiments are not automatically jointly achievable improvements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若研究销售边际变化，在 $S_0,c,m,d,U$ 固定时，$\partial EFN/\partial S_1=c-m(1-d)$。这与[[外部融资销售增长比|总缺口除以销售增量]]不同。现实中提价可能减少销量、延长回款或改变成本；这些共同变化应进入[[情景预测]]，而不是继续假装其他变量没变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For marginal sales changes with $S_0,c,m,d,U$ fixed, $\partial EFN/\partial S_1=c-m(1-d)$. This differs from [[外部融资销售增长比|total EFN divided by incremental sales]]. If a price increase also changes volume, collection, or costs, model their joint movement in a [[情景预测|scenario]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
