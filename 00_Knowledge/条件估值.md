# 条件估值在明确假想变化与支付安排下询问支付意愿

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Contingent valuation elicits willingness to pay under a specified hypothetical change and payment arrangement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件估值是一种陈述偏好方法：用调查描述“没有项目”的基准、项目带来的具体变化以及支付安排，再询问个人或家庭愿付多少，或是否愿按给定金额支持该方案。估值取决于这个明确情境，而不是对“环境好不好”的一般态度调查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Contingent valuation is a stated-preference method. A survey specifies the no-project baseline, the proposed change and the payment arrangement, then asks how much a person or household would pay or whether they support the proposal at a stated price. It values that scenario, not general approval of environmental protection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如调查某湖在未来一年内恢复到明确水质标准的项目，说明没有项目时的水质、可替代湖泊、实现概率、一次性家庭收费20元，并提醒这会挤出其他支出。单个“愿意”回答只是这一金额下的选择，不是最大支付意愿恰等于20元；从样本推向总体还需要合适抽样与无回答处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, specify a lake's one-year water-quality improvement, the baseline, substitute lakes, delivery probability and a one-off household charge of 20, reminding respondents that payment reduces other spending. One “yes” indicates acceptance at that price, not a maximum willingness to pay of exactly 20. Population inference also requires appropriate sampling and treatment of nonresponse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预调查和理解检查应确认回答者接受了方案及预算约束；[[条件估值的范围检验]]进一步检查被估变化是否真的影响回答。支付工具、时间范围、家庭或个人单位变更后，不能继续把答案当成同一估值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Piloting and comprehension checks should establish that respondents understand the scenario and budget constraint. [[条件估值的范围检验|Scope testing]] further checks whether the valued change affects answers. Altering the payment mechanism, horizon or household–individual unit changes the valuation object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
