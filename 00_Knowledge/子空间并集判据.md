# When is a union of subspaces a subspace?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两个子空间的并集什么时候仍是子空间？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **union** $U\cup V$ keeps every vector that belongs to $U$, to $V$, or to both. It does not automatically add new vectors obtained by combining the two sides. That is the difficulty: a [[子空间|subspace]] must stay closed under addition and scalar multiplication, so a sum of one vector from each side must also be included.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**并集** $U\cup V$ 保留属于 $U$、属于 $V$ 或同时属于两者的全部向量，但不会自动补入把两边向量组合后得到的新向量。问题就出在这里：[[子空间]]必须对加法与数乘封闭，所以从两边各取一个向量相加，结果也必须包含在内。

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Two axes show exactly what can fail*

In $\mathbb R^2$, let $U=\{(a,0):a\in\mathbb R\}$ be the horizontal axis and $V=\{(0,b):b\in\mathbb R\}$ the vertical axis. Each is a subspace. Their union is just the two axes: a vector belongs when at least one coordinate is zero. Yet
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\mathbb R^2$ 中，让 $U=\{(a,0):a\in\mathbb R\}$ 为横轴，$V=\{(0,b):b\in\mathbb R\}$ 为纵轴。两者都是子空间。并集只有这两条轴：至少一个坐标为零，向量才在其中。然而

$$(1,0)\in U,\qquad (0,1)\in V,\qquad (1,0)+(0,1)=(1,1)\notin U\cup V.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sum is on neither axis, so addition leaves the union. Containing zero and allowing every scalar multiple are not enough here; the missing operation is addition across the two sets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相加结果不在任何一条轴上，因此加法离开了并集。这里虽然含零，也允许每个向量乘任意标量，却仍然不够；缺的是跨两个集合的加法封闭性。

> [!abstract] The containment criterion · 包含关系判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $U,V$ be subspaces of the same vector space over the same scalar field. Their union is a subspace **if and only if one contains the other**:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $U,V$ 是同一标量域上同一向量空间的子空间。它们的并集是子空间，**当且仅当其中一个包含另一个**：
>
> $$U\cup V\text{ is a subspace}\iff U\subseteq V\text{ or }V\subseteq U.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $U\subseteq V$ means every vector in $U$ already belongs to $V$; equality is allowed. In that case $U\cup V=V$, so taking the union has added nothing outside the larger subspace.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $U\subseteq V$ 表示 $U$ 的每个向量本来就在 $V$ 中，允许两者相等。此时 $U\cup V=V$，取并集没有向较大的子空间之外添加任何东西。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why containment is necessary
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么不互相包含就必定失败*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose neither subspace contains the other. Then there is a vector $u$ belonging to $U$ but not $V$, and a vector $v$ belonging to $V$ but not $U$. The notation $U\setminus V$ means precisely “in $U$ and outside $V$”:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设两边互不包含。于是可以找到向量 $u$ 属于 $U$ 却不属于 $V$，也可以找到 $v$ 属于 $V$ 却不属于 $U$。记号 $U\setminus V$ 的意思就是“在 $U$ 中且不在 $V$ 中”：

$$u\in U\setminus V,\qquad v\in V\setminus U.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now inspect $u+v$. If it belonged to $U$, we could subtract $u\in U$ and obtain $v=(u+v)-u\in U$, contrary to our choice of $v$. Subtraction is allowed because a subspace permits multiplication by $-1$ and addition. If $u+v$ belonged to $V$, subtracting $v$ would similarly put $u$ in $V$, another contradiction. Therefore $u+v$ is in neither subspace and hence outside their union. The union fails addition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在检查 $u+v$。若它在 $U$ 中，就可以减去 $u\in U$，得到 $v=(u+v)-u\in U$，与选择 $v$ 时的要求矛盾。这里可以相减，是因为子空间允许乘 $-1$ 再相加。若 $u+v$ 在 $V$ 中，同理减去 $v$ 就会得到 $u\in V$，也矛盾。因此 $u+v$ 两边都不属于，必然在并集之外，并集不对加法封闭。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This proves the difficult direction. The other direction is immediate: if $U\subseteq V$, the union is $V$; if $V\subseteq U$, it is $U$. Either way it is already a subspace. The proof does not require finite dimension.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就证明了需要说明的一侧。另一侧直接成立：若 $U\subseteq V$，并集就是 $V$；若 $V\subseteq U$，并集就是 $U$。无论哪种情况，它原本就是子空间。这个证明不要求空间有限维。

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Union, intersection, and sum answer different questions*

The [[子空间的交集|intersection]] retains vectors that satisfy both membership conditions and is always a subspace. If instead we want a subspace containing everything in both sets, their [[子空间之和|sum]] allows every $u+v$ with $u\in U,v\in V$. It is the [[子空间和与并集的张成|span of the union]], the smallest subspace containing both. For the two axes, that space is the whole plane; for a line already inside a plane, the union and sum are both the plane. The criterion proved here concerns **two** subspaces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[子空间的交集|交集]]只保留同时属于两边的向量，并且总是子空间。如果想得到一个包含两边所有向量的子空间，就取[[子空间之和]]，允许所有 $u\in U,v\in V$ 相加得到 $u+v$。它等于[[子空间和与并集的张成|并集的张成]]，是同时包含两者的最小子空间。对于两条坐标轴，这个空间是整个平面；对于本来就包含在平面中的一条直线，并集与和都等于那个平面。本卡证明的判据针对的是**两个**子空间。

> [!question]- A line inside a plane · 直线本来就在平面内
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $U=\{(a,0,0):a\in\mathbb R\}$ and $V=\{(a,b,0):a,b\in\mathbb R\}$. Is $U\cup V$ a subspace? **Yes:** every vector of $U$ is already in $V$, so the union equals the $xy$-plane $V$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $U=\{(a,0,0):a\in\mathbb R\}$、$V=\{(a,b,0):a,b\in\mathbb R\}$，$U\cup V$ 是子空间吗？**是：** $U$ 的每个向量已经在 $V$ 中，并集就是 $xy$ 平面 $V$。
