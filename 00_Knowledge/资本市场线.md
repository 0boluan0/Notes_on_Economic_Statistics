# 资本市场线是CAPM均衡下无风险资产与市场组合构成的有效射线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The capital market line is the efficient ray formed by the risk-free asset and the market portfolio in CAPM equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本市场线（CML）是在标准 [[CAPM假设|CAPM 均衡]] 下，将无风险资产与理论市场组合 $M$ 相结合形成的有效期望收益—总波动关系。它是市场组合对应的特殊 [[资本配置线]]，不是任意风险组合的配置线，也不是单项证券按自身标准差定价的公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The capital market line (CML) is the efficient expected-return–total-volatility relation obtained by combining the risk-free asset with the theoretical market portfolio $M$ in standard [[CAPM假设|CAPM equilibrium]]. It is the special [[资本配置线|capital allocation line]] associated with the market, not the allocation line of an arbitrary risky portfolio or a pricing formula based on each security's own standard deviation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在市场为正 Sharpe 切点、$\sigma_M>0$、同率借贷可行的情形，对市场组合取权重 $y\ge0$，有 $\sigma_C=y\sigma_M$，因此
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the market is the positive-Sharpe tangency portfolio, $\sigma_M>0$, and common-rate borrowing and lending are feasible, a market weight $y\ge0$ gives $\sigma_C=y\sigma_M$, hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(R_C)=r_f+\frac{E(R_M)-r_f}{\sigma_M}\sigma_C,
\qquad \sigma_C\ge0.
$$

截距为 $r_f$，斜率为市场 [[Sharpe比率]]。例如 $r_f=3\%$、$E(R_M)=9\%$、$\sigma_M=20\%$，CML 斜率为 0.30；波动为 10% 的线上组合有期望收益 6%，波动为 30% 的线上组合有期望收益 12%。这些是模型期望值，不是逐期保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The intercept is $r_f$ and the slope is the market [[Sharpe比率|Sharpe ratio]]. For $r_f=3\%$, $E(R_M)=9\%$, and $\sigma_M=20\%$, the slope is 0.30. A portfolio on the line with 10% volatility has expected return 6%; one with 30% volatility has expected return 12%. These are model expectations, not period-by-period guarantees.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

起点全持无风险资产，虽在线上，其自身 Sharpe 是 $0/0$、未定义；有正风险配置时才与市场有相同 Sharpe。若 $y<0$，做空市场的组合不属于这条有效上射线。是否可把现实指数代作 $M$，另见 [[市场代理联合检验]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The entirely risk-free starting point lies on the line but has an undefined $0/0$ Sharpe ratio. Positive risky allocations share the market's Sharpe. A negative $y$ shorts the market and lies off this efficient upper ray. Whether an observed index represents $M$ is a separate [[市场代理联合检验|market-proxy question]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
