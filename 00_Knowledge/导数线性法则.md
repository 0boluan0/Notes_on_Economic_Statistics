# 有限线性组合的导数等于各项导数的同一线性组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The derivative of a finite linear combination is the same linear combination of the component derivatives*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子规则
> 若 $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ 在 $x$ 可导，$a_1,\ldots,a_n$ 是常数，则
> $$
> \left(\sum_{j=1}^n a_jf_j\right)'(x)=\sum_{j=1}^n a_jf_j'(x).
> $$
> 常数函数的导数为 $0$，所以多项式可以逐项求导。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ are differentiable at $x$ and $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ are constants, then $(\sum_j a_jf_j)'(x)=\sum_j a_jf_j'(x)$. Constant functions have derivative zero, so polynomials may be differentiated term by term.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明只需把线性组合放进 [[导数|差商]]：
$$
\frac{\sum_j a_jf_j(x+h)-\sum_j a_jf_j(x)}{h}
=\sum_j a_j\frac{f_j(x+h)-f_j(x)}{h},
$$
再用有限和的极限定律。这里的“线性”指对函数做加法和常数倍；它不说明乘积、商或复合可以逐项处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Insert the linear combination into the [[导数|difference quotient]], distribute the finite sum, and pass each term to its derivative. Linearity concerns addition and scalar multiplication; it does not make products, quotients, or compositions termwise operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如
$$
\frac{d}{dx}(3x^4-2x+7)=12x^3-2.
$$
但 $(fg)'$ 通常不等于 $f'g'$。同样，这条有限和规则不能无条件推广到无穷级数；逐项求导需要额外的收敛控制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $d(3x^4-2x+7)/dx=12x^3-2$. In contrast, $(fg)'$ is generally not $f'g'$. Nor may the finite-sum rule be extended automatically to an infinite series; termwise differentiation requires additional convergence conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么 $(2f-3g)'=2f'-3g'$，却不能由同一理由写成 $(fg)'=f'g'$？
>
> **答案：** 差商对加法和常数倍保持线性；乘积的增量同时含两项一阶变化，必须使用乘积法则。
