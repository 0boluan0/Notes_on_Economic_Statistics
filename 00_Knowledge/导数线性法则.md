# Linearity of differentiation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*导数线性法则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When quantities are added, their rates of change add too. If one part of a cost is changing at $4$ pounds per hour and another at $-1$ pound per hour, the total is changing at $3$ pounds per hour. Multiplying a quantity by a fixed number multiplies its rate by that number. **Linearity of differentiation** combines these two facts and lets us differentiate a finite sum one term at a time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

几个量相加时，它们的变化率也相加。如果一项费用以每小时 $4$ 英镑的速度变化，另一项以每小时 $-1$ 英镑的速度变化，总费用就以每小时 $3$ 英镑的速度变化。把一个量乘以固定倍数，其变化率也乘以相同倍数。**导数线性法则**把这两件事合在一起，让我们能对有限项的和逐项求导。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[导数|derivative]] $f'(x)$ is the local change in $f$ per unit change in the input $x$. Here “linear” describes what differentiation does to **functions**: it preserves addition and constant multiples. It does not mean that the graphs of those functions must be straight lines.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[导数]] $f'(x)$ 表示输入 $x$ 每改变一个单位时，$f$ 的局部变化率。这里“线性”描述求导怎样作用于**函数**：它保持加法和常数倍运算，并不是说这些函数的图像必须是直线。

> [!abstract] Keep the same constant weights · 保留原来的常数系数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ are differentiable at $x_0$ and $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ are constants. The finite linear combination $F(x)=\sum_{j=1}^n a_jf_j(x)$ means “multiply each function by its weight, then add”. It is differentiable at $x_0$, with
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $f_1,\ldots,f_n$ 在 $x_0$ 可导，$a_1,\ldots,a_n$ 是常数。有限线性组合 $F(x)=\sum_{j=1}^n a_jf_j(x)$ 的意思是“把每个函数乘以相应系数，再相加”。它在 $x_0$ 可导，且
>
> $$F'(x_0)=\sum_{j=1}^n a_jf_j'(x_0).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For example, $(2f-3g)'=2f'-3g'$. A separate constant term has derivative zero: its value does not change when $x$ changes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 例如 $(2f-3g)'=2f'-3g'$。额外加上的常数项导数为零，因为 $x$ 变化时它的值始终不变。

## Why taking the limit preserves the sum
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么取极限以后仍能逐项相加*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start from the [[导数|difference quotient]], which measures change over an input increment $h\ne0$. Ordinary algebra separates the change of $F$ into the changes of its components:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从[[导数|差商]]出发，它衡量输入增加 $h\ne0$ 时的平均变化率。普通代数就能把 $F$ 的变化拆成各分量的变化：

$$\frac{F(x_0+h)-F(x_0)}h
=\sum_{j=1}^n a_j\frac{f_j(x_0+h)-f_j(x_0)}h.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As $h\to0$, each quotient tends to the finite number $f_j'(x_0)$. The rules for a [[函数极限|limit]] allow a **finite** sum of convergent quantities to pass to the sum of their limits. That is the whole reason the weights survive unchanged. For a constant function $c$, the quotient is $(c-c)/h=0$, giving the constant rule directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $h\to0$ 时，每个差商都趋于有限数 $f_j'(x_0)$。[[函数极限]]的运算法则允许把**有限个**收敛量之和的极限写成各自极限之和，因此所有系数原样保留。常数函数 $c$ 的差商为 $(c-c)/h=0$，直接得到常数求导法则。

> [!example] A polynomial, one component at a time · 多项式逐项处理
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use the [[整数幂求导|integer power rule]] for the individual powers and linearity for their combination:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个幂项使用[[整数幂求导]]，各项组合使用线性法则：
>
> $$\frac d{dx}(3x^4-2x+7)
> =3\cdot4x^3-2\cdot1+0=12x^3-2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $x=1$, the three contributions to the slope are $12$, $-2$, and $0$, so the total slope is $10$. The constant $7$ shifts the graph vertically but contributes no change in slope.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $x=1$ 处，三项对斜率的贡献分别是 $12$、$-2$、$0$，总斜率就是 $10$。常数 $7$ 把图像竖直平移，却不会改变斜率。

> [!warning] A changing weight needs its own derivative · 系数变化时也要求导
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $x f(x)$, the multiplier $x$ is not constant. The [[乘积法则|product rule]] gives $(xf)'=f+xf'$, including the extra term from the changing multiplier. For instance, $x\cdot x=x^2$ has derivative $2x$, not $x$. Products and [[链式法则|compositions]] therefore need their own rules. Likewise, a finite-sum argument does not justify differentiating an infinite series term by term; that requires additional convergence conditions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $x f(x)$ 中，乘数 $x$ 不是常数。[[乘积法则]]给出 $(xf)'=f+xf'$，其中包含乘数变化带来的额外项。例如 $x\cdot x=x^2$ 的导数是 $2x$，不是 $x$。所以乘积和[[链式法则|复合函数]]都需要各自的规则。同样，有限和的证明不能直接用于无穷级数；无穷级数逐项求导还需要额外的收敛条件。
