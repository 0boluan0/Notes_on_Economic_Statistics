# 除非理论明确要求相应零约束，含交互项的模型应保留其组成变量的低阶主效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Unless theory explicitly requires the corresponding zero restrictions, an interaction model should retain the lower-order main effects of its component variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 层级原则在防什么
> 在 $Y=\beta_0+\beta_XX+\beta_DD+\beta_{XD}XD+u$ 中，保留 $X$ 和 $D$ 让两组都可有自己的截距和斜率。只保留 $XD$ 不是“更简洁地写交互”，而是额外限制某些基准截距或斜率恰好为零。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $Y=\beta_0+\beta_XX+\beta_DD+\beta_{XD}XD+u$, retaining $X$ and $D$ allows both groups to have their own intercepts and slopes. Keeping $XD$ while deleting its components is not merely a shorter way to write an interaction; it imposes additional zero restrictions on reference intercepts or slopes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 删掉 $X$ 会强制 $D=0$ 基准组的 $X$ 斜率为零。
- 删掉 $D$ 会强制两组在 $X=0$ 时有相同截距。
- 两者都删会同时施加这两项限制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Omitting $X$ forces the $X$ slope in the $D=0$ reference group to zero.
- Omitting $D$ forces the groups to share an intercept at $X=0$.
- Omitting both imposes both restrictions at once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

层级原则还有一个重参数化理由。把 $X$ 改成 $X_c=X-c$ 时，$DX=D(X_c+c)=DX_c+cD$；若模型包含交互却没有 $D$，平移 $X$ 会改变模型所能表示的函数集合。保留完整低阶项后，换单位原点或基准编码只会重写系数，不会悄悄换模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hierarchy also protects reparameterisation invariance. If $X_c=X-c$, then $DX=D(X_c+c)=DX_c+cD$. A model with the interaction but without $D$ changes its representable functions when the origin of $X$ is shifted. Retaining the lower-order hierarchy lets a change of origin or reference coding rewrite coefficients without silently changing the model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是一条强默认而非逻辑禁令。若物理机制、实验设计或制度规则真正要求某条基准线穿过零，可以明确施加并检验相应限制；理由应来自问题本身，而不是某个主效应样本 $p$ 值较大，或为了把 VIF 压到阈值以下。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a strong default, not a logical prohibition. A physical mechanism, experimental design, or institutional rule may genuinely require a reference line to pass through zero, in which case the restriction can be stated and assessed explicitly. The justification should come from the problem, not from a large sample p-value on a main effect or an attempt to push VIF below a cutoff.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 模型保留 `training × experience` 却删掉 `training`，实际强加了什么？
>
> **答案：** 两组在 `experience=0` 时必须有相同截距；这是一项实质限制，不是无害的变量筛选。

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Penn State STAT 462, §7.6](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat462/node/157/)：明确给出含交互时通常保留组成主效应的 hierarchy principle，并说明例外需要领域理由。
- [NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook, §5.4.7.3](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section4/pri473.htm)：交叉核对保留显著高阶项所含低阶项的层级原则及其并非人人同意的使用边界。
- [Stata, “Factor variables”](https://www.stata.com/features/overview/factor-variables/)：核对 full-factorial `##` 同时展开主效应与交互项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Penn State explicitly states the hierarchy principle and its domain-specific exceptions. NIST independently documents the principle and its non-universal status. Stata's official factor syntax verifies that a full factorial expands to main effects plus interactions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
