# Hierarchy in interaction models
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*交互模型的层级原则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a model contains a product of predictors, the **hierarchy principle** recommends retaining the corresponding lower-order terms. For a group indicator $D$ and experience $X$, retaining $DX$ normally goes with retaining $D$ and $X$. This keeps a flexible group comparison and makes ordinary changes of reference easier to interpret. It is a modelling principle, not a theorem that every scientifically justified model must contain every component.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模型包含解释变量乘积时，**层级原则**建议同时保留相应低阶项。对于组别指示 $D$ 与经验 $X$，保留 $DX$ 通常也保留 $D$、$X$。这样既能灵活比较组别，也让通常的参照变化更容易解释。这是建模原则，不是“任何有科学依据的模型都必须包含全部组成项”的定理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Omitting a term imposes a restriction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*删项会施加什么限制*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $D=0$ denote a reference group and $D=1$ a comparison group. In the [[类别连续交互|complete two-line mean model]]
$$m(x,d)=\beta_0+\beta_Xx+\beta_Dd+\beta_{XD}xd,$$
$m(x,d)$ is the modelled conditional mean of an integrable outcome $Y$. The terms $X$ and $D$ are lower order because they appear individually rather than multiplied. Substitution gives $m(x,0)=\beta_0+\beta_Xx$ and $m(x,1)=\beta_0+\beta_D+(\beta_X+\beta_{XD})x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $D=0$ 为基准组，$D=1$ 为比较组。在上式[[类别连续交互|完整两直线均值模型]]中，$m(x,d)$ 是对可积结果 $Y$ 的条件均值建模。$X$、$D$ 单独出现，没有彼此相乘，因此是较低阶项。分别代入后，基准线是 $\beta_0+\beta_Xx$，比较线是 $\beta_0+\beta_D+(\beta_X+\beta_{XD})x$。

> [!important] Read the constraint in the group lines · 在组别直线中读约束
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Keeping the intercept and $XD$ but omitting $X$ imposes $\beta_X=0$: the reference-group line must be flat. Omitting $D$ instead imposes $\beta_D=0$: the two lines must meet at $x=0$. Omitting both imposes both restrictions. The common intercept itself need not be zero. These are restrictions on the allowed mean functions, even if the remaining coefficients are re-estimated.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 保留截距和 $XD$ 却删掉 $X$，相当于施加 $\beta_X=0$，强制基准组直线水平。改为删掉 $D$，相当于施加 $\beta_D=0$，强制两条线在 $x=0$ 相遇。两者都删，就同时施加两项限制；共同截距本身不必为零。即使重新估计剩余系数，对允许均值函数的这些限制仍存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, the rule $12+0.80x-1.50d-0.25xd$ gives a rising reference line and different group intercepts, $12$ and $10.50$. A model omitting $X$ cannot represent that rising reference line at multiple experience values. A model omitting $D$ cannot represent those unequal intercepts. Re-fitting a reduced model can change every remaining estimate; it does not undo the restriction introduced by the deletion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $12+0.80x-1.50d-0.25xd$ 给出上升的基准线，且两组截距为 $12$ 与 $10.50$。删掉 $X$ 的模型无法在多个经验取值上表示这条上升基准线；删掉 $D$ 的模型无法表示这两个不同截距。重新拟合可能改变全部剩余估计值，却不能消除删项带来的限制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the origin of a predictor matters
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么零点位置会影响删项的含义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a reduced model keeps $1,X,DX$ but omits $D$, writing $m=a+bX+hDX$. Its three coefficients $a,b,h$ are fixed. Centre experience at a fixed $c$ by setting $X_c=X-c$. Expanding gives
$$m=(a+bc)+bX_c+hcD+hDX_c.$$
The term $hcD$ appears automatically. If one now fits only $1,X_c,DX_c$ and again omits $D$, one generally fits a different family of functions: the old model forced the group lines to meet at $X=0$, while the new one forces them to meet at $X=c$. The issue disappears in special cases such as $c=0$ or $h=0$, but not in general.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设简化模型保留 $1,X,DX$ 而删去 $D$，写成 $m=a+bX+hDX$，其中 $a,b,h$ 是固定系数。取固定 $c$，令 $X_c=X-c$，展开就得到上式，自动出现 $hcD$。如果此时仍只拟合 $1,X_c,DX_c$、再次删去 $D$，通常已经换了函数集合：旧模型强制两线在 $X=0$ 相遇，新模型强制在 $X=c$ 相遇。在 $c=0$ 或 $h=0$ 等特殊情况下这个问题消失，但一般并不消失。

> [!example] The same shape needs a new lower-order term · 保持同一形状需要新低阶项
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $m=12+0.80X-0.25DX$, the two lines meet at zero. With $X_c=X-10$, exactly the same function is $20+0.80X_c-2.50D-0.25DX_c$. At ten years its group predictions are $20$ and $17.50$. Dropping the newly produced $-2.50D$ term would force them to agree there, so it would change the model rather than merely relabel experience.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 模型 $m=12+0.80X-0.25DX$ 的两条线在零点相遇。令 $X_c=X-10$，同一函数必须写成 $20+0.80X_c-2.50D-0.25DX_c$；十年经验处两组预测为 $20$、$17.50$。删掉新产生的 $-2.50D$ 会强制它们在十年处相同，因此是在改模型，而不是仅更换经验标签。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The full model contains the terms needed for both centring and ordinary [[基准组重编码|reference recoding]]. For example, reversing the binary reference with $D^*=1-D$ turns $XD$ into $X-XD^*$, which produces an $X$ term. With the intercept and both components retained, these are invertible re-expressions of the same [[列空间|design-column space]]. For the same data, weights and unconstrained least-squares criterion, fitted values are unchanged. [[交互主效应基准|The coefficients’ reference meanings]] can still change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完整模型包含中心化及通常[[基准组重编码|基准重编码]]所需的项。例如换成 $D^*=1-D$，则 $XD=X-XD^*$，会产生一个 $X$ 项。保留截距与两个组成项后，这些变化只是同一[[列空间|设计列空间]]的可逆改写；数据、权重与无约束最小二乘准则相同，拟合值就不变，而[[交互主效应基准|系数的参照含义]]仍可能改变。

## A deliberate constrained model can still be appropriate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*明确的受限模型仍可以合适*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A scientific hypothesis might specify equal group means at a physically meaningful zero exposure. Then $m=a+bX+hDX$, with no separate $D$ term, is a legitimate constrained model for that question: the two groups may respond differently to exposure while sharing the zero-exposure baseline. For instance, $m=5+2X+3DX$ gives baseline $5$ in both groups and slopes $2$ and $5$. If the origin is moved, preserve the same substantive constraint through the induced $D$ term instead of silently replacing it with equality at the new zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

科学假设可能规定：在有实际意义的零暴露处，两组均值相同。此时没有单独 $D$ 项的 $m=a+bX+hDX$，就是针对该问题的合法受限模型：两组可有不同暴露反应，却共享零暴露基线。例如 $m=5+2X+3DX$ 让两组基线都为 $5$，斜率分别为 $2$、$5$。如果移动零点，应通过随之产生的 $D$ 项保留同一实质约束，不能悄悄改成在新零点相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The constraint should have a stated substantive or design justification, or be assessed as an explicit modelling hypothesis. A large p-value for one lower-order coefficient does not establish that it is zero, and that coefficient’s hypothesis can change with the reference. Dropping a term merely to reduce a [[VIF阈值边界|VIF]] value can likewise change the scientific model. A deliberate restricted model, a predictive simplification and an unrestricted descriptive model have different [[模型选择目标|modelling goals]]; the hierarchy principle makes those choices visible rather than prohibiting them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

约束应有明确的实质或设计依据，或作为清楚提出的建模假设加以评估。某个低阶系数 p 值较大，并不证明它为零，而且该系数的假设会随参照改变；仅为降低 [[VIF阈值边界|VIF]] 而删项，也可能改变科学模型。明确受限模型、预测简化与不受限描述模型有不同的[[模型选择目标]]；层级原则帮助看清这些选择，并不禁止它们。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Retaining a complete hierarchy does not establish the right functional form, adequate support, a full-rank design or a causal interpretation. Those remain separate checks. It primarily prevents an apparently small term deletion from introducing an unnoticed constraint tied to arbitrary coding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保留完整层级也不证明函数形式正确、支持充足、设计满秩或具有因果解释，这些仍需分别检查。它主要防止一次看似小的删项，偷偷引入与任意编码相绑定的约束。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Penn State STAT 462, §7.6, opening discussion](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat462/node/157/): the hierarchy recommendation for interactions, including exceptions justified by the subject area.
- [NIST/SEMATECH, §5.4.7.3, model-fitting step 3 and the stepwise example](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section4/pri473.htm): retaining lower-order terms involved in higher-order terms despite their individual p-values, while explicitly acknowledging that the convention is not universal.
- [Stata, “Factor variables”](https://www.stata.com/features/overview/factor-variables/): full-factorial syntax includes the components and interaction; this is an implementation convention, not proof that every restricted model is invalid.
- All omitted-term restrictions, reparameterisations and numerical examples above are verified by direct substitution into the displayed functions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Penn State STAT 462 第 7.6 节开头核对交互层级建议与由领域理由支持的例外。
- NIST 第 5.4.7.3 节建模步骤 3 及逐步回归例子核对：低阶项单独 p 值较大时仍可按层级保留，并明确该惯例不是所有分析者都赞同的普遍规则。
- Stata 的 Factor variables 页面核对 full-factorial 语法包含组成项与交互；软件约定不构成“所有受限模型都不成立”的证明。
- 删项约束、重参数化及数值例子均直接代入文中函数核验。
