# 到期价格收敛不能保证交易者有资金持仓到收敛时点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Convergence at maturity does not guarantee that a trader can fund the position until convergence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

终点支付相抵只回答“若能持有到终点，合约怎样结算”。中途若有保证金调用、融资到期或赎回要求，交易者还必须及时取得现金；资金不足可能迫使他在价差尚未收敛时退出并实现亏损。最终相等与沿途可融资是两个不同的条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Offsetting terminal payments answer how contracts settle if held to the endpoint. Interim margin calls, funding maturity, or redemptions additionally require timely cash. Insufficient funding can force exit at an uncorrected spread and realize a loss. Terminal equality and funding along the path are different conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑到期均按同一标的结算价 $x$ 结算、单位乘数为 1 的两份期货：以报价 98 做多 A，以报价 100 做空 B，开仓时合约净价值均为零。简化设定初始保证金每份 5、另外可用现金 3，忽略利息与费用；保证金被占用，不能直接充作随后调用的可用现金。初始外部资金为 $5+5+3=13$，不是零资金交易。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider two futures that settle against the same terminal underlying price $x$, each with unit multiplier 1: enter long A at quote 98 and short B at quote 100, each initially having zero contract value. Assume initial margin 5 per contract and additional available cash 3, ignoring interest and costs. Posted margin is encumbered and cannot directly serve as cash for a subsequent call. Initial outside funds are $5+5+3=13$, so this is not a capital-free trade.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随后 A 跌到 95、B 升到 105，两边分别亏损 3 和 5，合计变动保证金现金需求为 8。可用现金只有 3，缺口为 5；若不能融资且必须在该价格平仓，则交易损益为 −8。若本来能够继续持有，最终两边报价都为 $x$，累计期货损益却是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If A falls to 95 and B rises to 105, the positions lose 3 and 5, producing a total variation-margin cash need of 8. Available cash is only 3, leaving a gap of 5. If funding is unavailable and closing at those prices is required, trading P&L is −8. Had continued holding been feasible, equal final quotes $x$ would instead have produced cumulative futures P&L
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
(x-98)+(100-x)=2.
$$

这里 −8 是已经形成的期货损益，现金调用是其结算要求；不能再把 8 作为独立新损失扣一次。初始保证金也不因提交就自动变成费用。不同账户能否净额、抵押品是否可动用须按具体安排判断，见[[净额与抵押品]]；VM／IM 如何形成资金压力见[[保证金顺周期]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The −8 is the existing futures P&L, and the cash call settles it; subtracting another 8 as a separate loss would double-count. Posting initial margin is not automatically an expense either. Netting across accounts and collateral availability depend on the arrangements, as explained in [[净额与抵押品|netting and collateral]]; [[保证金顺周期|margin procyclicality]] covers VM/IM funding pressure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个反例需要提前现金需求与融资不足同时出现。若拥有可执行且足够的融资、能承受沿途风险并确实持有到相同交割，不能仍断言一定被迫亏损。反过来，现实中若连终点支付也不同，就还要加入合同匹配及基本面风险，而不能把所有损失都归为“只是时间不够”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The counterexample requires both interim cash needs and insufficient funding. Executable, adequate funding and the ability to bear interim risk through identical settlement do not imply a forced loss. Conversely, if terminal payments also differ, contract matching and fundamental risk must be considered; not every loss is merely a shortage of waiting time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
