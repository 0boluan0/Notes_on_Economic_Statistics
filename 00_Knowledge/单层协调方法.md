# 自底向上、自顶向下与中层展开只保留一个选定聚合层级的协调前预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bottom-up, top-down, and middle-out reconciliation retain base forecasts from one selected aggregation level*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 三种方法怎样不同
> **自底向上**只预测最底层，再通过 $S$ 向上加总；**自顶向下**只预测最高总量，再按比例分到底层；**中层展开**先预测一个选定的中间层，向上加总、向下分摊。三者都能产生相干结果，但都舍弃了其他层级的 base forecasts。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Bottom-up forecasts only the bottom level and aggregates upward. Top-down forecasts only the total and disaggregates it by proportions. Middle-out forecasts one chosen intermediate level, aggregating above it and allocating below it. All are coherent but discard base forecasts from the other levels.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自底向上保留局部结构，不需要估计分摊比例；当底层有充足数据和独特信号时很自然。但底层序列可能短、稀疏或噪声很大，单独预测后再相加也会累计误差。它“使用最多细节”不等于自动最准确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bottom-up preserves local structure and avoids allocation proportions, but bottom series may be short, sparse, or noisy. Using the finest detail does not guarantee the best forecast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自顶向下可让总量模型利用较平滑的聚合信号，却把底层变化压进历史或预测比例。新产品、地区结构变化和不同季节性会使比例失真；标准 top-down 也不满足“任意无偏 base forecasts 经协调后仍无偏”的保持条件。中层展开只是把取舍放在中间，并未使用所有节点的信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Top-down exploits a smoother aggregate signal but forces local dynamics through allocation proportions, which can fail after product or regional change. Standard top-down rules do not preserve unbiasedness for arbitrary unbiased base forecasts. Middle-out relocates rather than removes the single-level trade-off.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此选择哪一层不能凭组织图直觉决定。应在相同 origins、horizons 和逐层损失上与 [[MinT协调|利用全部层级的协调]]比较；若某层对决策特别关键，还应单独报告该层是否被改善或拉坏。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The chosen level should be validated under common origins and horizons against reconciliation methods that use all levels. Decision-critical levels need their own accuracy report rather than being hidden inside an aggregate score.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 底层序列最多，因此 bottom-up 一定利用了“全部信息”。这句话哪里错？
>
> **答案：** bottom-up 只保留底层 base forecasts；中间层和总量模型可能识别到被底层噪声掩盖的结构，但它们的预测被舍弃了。
