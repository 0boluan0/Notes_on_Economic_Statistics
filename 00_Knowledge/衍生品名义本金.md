# 衍生品名义本金是合约约定的本金或用于计算支付的参考金额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A derivative's notional amount is its contractual principal or a reference amount used to calculate payments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

名义本金（notional）回答“按多大的基数计算”，不直接回答“合约今天值多少钱”。在普通同币种利率[[金融互换|互换]]中，本金通常仅用于计算利息；在实物交割的外汇合约中，约定货币本金可以正是到期必须交付的金额。因此，也不能反过来说所有名义本金都只是纸面数字、从不实际支付。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Notional identifies the calculation base, not the contract's current value. In a plain same-currency interest-rate [[金融互换|swap]], it normally only scales interest payments. In a physically settled FX contract, the currency principal can be an actual delivery obligation. Notional is therefore not universally a fictitious amount that never changes hands.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个本金 100 万、半年按两种利率之差结算的互换，若利差为 1 个百分点，本期净支付为 $1{,}000{,}000\times0.5\times0.01=5{,}000$。100 万是计算基数，5,000 是这一期的支付；剩余所有支付的现值才决定合约市值。三个数字回答不同问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A swap with a notional of 1 million and a semiannual rate difference of one percentage point pays 5,000 for that period. The million is the base, 5,000 is one payment, and the present value of all remaining payments determines the contract's market value. These answer different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较规模时还要写明币种和报告口径。指数期货常用“报价点数 × 每点乘数 × 手数”表示名义金额；不同统计口径也可能用当前标的价格计量。保证金、合约市值、名义金额和[[对手方敞口指标|未来信用敞口]]不能互相替代。相同名义本金而到期日、敏感度或净额条款不同，风险也不相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify currency and reporting convention. Index futures often use quote points times the point multiplier and contract count; some statistical definitions use the current underlying price. Margin, market value, notional, and [[对手方敞口指标|future credit exposure]] are distinct. Equal notionals with different maturities, sensitivities, or netting terms need not carry equal risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
