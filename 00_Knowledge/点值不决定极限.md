# Limits and point values
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*点值不决定极限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[函数极限|function limit]] asks what happens at inputs close to a target. A **point value** is the output at the target itself. These answer different questions: changing the one value at the target does not alter all the nearby values that determine the limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[函数极限]]问的是：输入靠近目标点时，输出怎样变化。**点值**则是目标点本身对应的输出。这是两个不同的问题；只修改目标点的一个取值，并不会改变决定极限的那些附近取值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same nearby rule, three choices at zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

> [!example] A missing point or a moved point · 缺一个点，或挪动一个点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Keep $f(x)=x+1$ for every $x\ne0$. At $0$, we may set $f(0)=1$, set $f(0)=100$, or leave the function undefined. In all three cases the limit is $1$: for every nonzero $x$, the output error from $1$ is $|f(x)-1|=|x|$, so $0<|x|<\varepsilon$ forces output error below $\varepsilon$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对所有 $x\ne0$ 保持 $f(x)=x+1$。在 $0$ 处，可以设 $f(0)=1$、设 $f(0)=100$，或者不定义。不论哪种选择，极限都是 $1$：对每个非零 $x$，输出与 $1$ 的误差都是 $|f(x)-1|=|x|$，所以 $0<|x|<\varepsilon$ 就保证输出误差小于 $\varepsilon$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a graph, these choices fill the hole on the line, place one point far above it, or leave the hole empty. None changes the line away from $0$. However, they do change [[一点连续|continuity at the point]], which requires a defined point value and local agreement with it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在图像上，这三种选择分别是补上线上的洞、把一个点放到很高的位置、保留空洞。它们都没有改变 $0$ 以外的直线。但它们确实会影响[[一点连续]]，因为连续要求点值有定义，并且与附近的行为接得上。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The local agreement that preserves a limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*保持极限所需的局部一致性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **punctured neighbourhood** of $a$ means a neighbourhood with $a$ removed: $0<|x-a|<r$ for some radius $r>0$. Only domain points inside that region are tested. The two functions must have the same allowed inputs there, as well as the same outputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$a$ 的**去心邻域**就是拿掉 $a$ 本身的邻近范围：对某个半径 $r>0$，有 $0<|x-a|<r$。只检查这个范围内的定义域点；两个函数在这里既要有相同的合法输入，也要给出相同的输出。

> [!important] Local agreement theorem · 局部一致性定理
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $f:D_f\to\mathbb R$ and $g:D_g\to\mathbb R$. Suppose some $r>0$ makes the two local input sets identical,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $f:D_f\to\mathbb R$、$g:D_g\to\mathbb R$。假设存在 $r>0$，使两者的局部输入集合相同：
>
> $$D_f\cap\{x:0<|x-a|<r\}
> =D_g\cap\{x:0<|x-a|<r\}=E.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $E$ names this common set. Assume it has points arbitrarily close to $a$, and that $f(x)=g(x)$ for every $x\in E$. Then, for every finite real $L$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $E$ 表示这个共同集合。再假设它包含任意靠近 $a$ 的点，并且对每个 $x\in E$ 都有 $f(x)=g(x)$。那么，对每个有限实数 $L$，
>
> $$\lim_{\substack{x\to a\\x\in D_f}}f(x)=L
> \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> \lim_{\substack{x\to a\\x\in D_g}}g(x)=L.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus finite-limit existence and value are unchanged. In particular, adding, deleting or changing only the value at $a$ preserves the limit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因此，有限极限的存在性和值都不变。特别地，只增加、删除或改变 $a$ 处的点值，不会改变极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see why, suppose the limit of $f$ is $L$. For an output tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, obtain a radius $\delta_f>0$ from its limit definition. For $g$, use the smaller radius $\delta=\min\{r,\delta_f\}$. Every permitted non-target input for $g$ in this radius also belongs to $D_f$, and its output equals $f(x)$, so it has the same error bound. Interchanging $f$ and $g$ proves the reverse implication. Values farther away than $r$ play no role.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理由是：假设 $f$ 的极限为 $L$。对输出容差 $\varepsilon>0$，从它的极限定义取得半径 $\delta_f>0$。给 $g$ 使用较小的半径 $\delta=\min\{r,\delta_f\}$。这个范围内，$g$ 的每个合法非目标输入也属于 $D_f$，输出又等于 $f(x)$，因此满足相同的误差界。交换 $f$ 与 $g$ 就得到反方向。距离超过 $r$ 的取值不参与这个论证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Agreement on only some nearby points is insufficient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep $f(x)=0$ on all of $\mathbb R$. Let $g(x)=0$ for $x<0$ and $g(x)=1$ for $x\ge0$. The functions agree at infinitely many inputs arbitrarily close to $0$ on the left, yet $g$ has unequal one-sided limits and no two-sided limit. “Many nearby points” is not the same as **every** input in a common punctured neighbourhood; the [[双侧极限判定|two-sided limit criterion]] detects the missing right-side agreement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $f(x)=0$ 对所有实数成立；令 $x<0$ 时 $g(x)=0$，$x\ge0$ 时 $g(x)=1$。两个函数在左侧任意靠近 $0$ 的无穷多个输入上相等，但 $g$ 的左右极限不相等，因而没有双侧极限。“很多附近点相等”不等于共同去心邻域内的**每个**输入都相等；[[双侧极限判定]]能发现右侧没有一致性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nor is agreement only on the intersection of two different domains enough. A function equal to $0$ on $(-\infty,0)$ has domain-relative limit $0$ at $0$, while one equal to $1$ on $(0,\infty)$ has domain-relative limit $1$. Their local domains have no common inputs, so saying they agree wherever both are defined supplies no useful information. The theorem requires the local domains themselves to coincide.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只要求两个不同定义域的交集上取值相等也不够。定义在 $(-\infty,0)$ 上的常值 $0$ 函数，在 $0$ 处相对于自身定义域的极限为 $0$；定义在 $(0,\infty)$ 上的常值 $1$ 函数，相应极限为 $1$。它们的局部定义域没有共同输入，因此“在两者都有定义的地方相等”没有提供有效信息。定理要求局部定义域本身一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cluster-point assumption supplies nearby inputs to test. At an isolated point, the punctured-limit definition used here is not applied. On the other hand, changing the point value cannot create a finite limit where nearby behaviour already prevents one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

聚点前提保证附近确实有输入可供检查。在孤立点处，这里采用的去心极限定义不适用。另一方面，如果附近的行为本来就使有限极限不存在，修改点值也不能创造出有限极限。
