# 央行购买资产可在不完全替代条件下改变私人持有的风险供给并推动组合与价格调整
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With imperfect asset substitution, central-bank purchases can alter privately held risk supply and induce portfolio and price adjustments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果长期债券和货币不是完全替代品，[[量化宽松]]把私人持有的部分长期债券换成存款与准备金，就改变了私人部门需要承担的久期和资产组合。原卖方可能转买其他相近资产；价格随供求调整，直到投资者愿意持有新的总供给。受到影响的不只被买入的那只债券。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If long bonds and money are imperfect substitutes, [[量化宽松|QE]] replaces some privately held long bonds with deposits and reserves, changing private duration exposure and portfolios. Sellers may buy substitutes; prices adjust until investors willingly hold the new aggregate supply. Effects can extend beyond the purchased security.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么不能说“这笔新钱最终必须全部花掉”？甲买乙的债券时，存款只是转到乙；新的资产供给必须由私人部门整体持有，价格和收益率共同调整，才可能让大家愿意接受不同的组合。记账恒等式没有保证哪一位投资者一定买股票，也没有保证消费按购买金额同幅增加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
New money need not be spent away in aggregate: when A buys B's bond, the deposit moves to B. The private sector must collectively hold the new supplies, with prices and yields adjusting to make that allocation acceptable. Accounting guarantees neither equity purchases by each seller nor equal-sized consumption growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用[[期限溢价]]的低利率近似作一个价格分解：预期短率平均为3%、期限溢价为1%，长期收益率约4%。若购买减少私人需承担的久期风险，模型中的溢价降到0.6%，而短率预期不变，收益率约为3.6%。这说明一个可能渠道，不是QE每次必降40bp的估计；若预期短率也改变，应分开记录。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the low-rate [[期限溢价|term-premium approximation]], average expected short rates of 3% plus a 1% premium give a long yield near 4%. If purchases reduce private duration exposure and the model premium falls to 0.6% with expectations fixed, the yield is near 3.6%. This illustrates a channel, not a universal 40-basis-point QE effect; expectation changes must be recorded separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若资产完全替代且购买不传递新信息，这条相对供给渠道可以很弱或消失。市场修复、政策信号和风险承载能力会改变实际效果；不能把一次收益率下降全部归给组合再平衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With perfect substitution and no new information, this relative-supply channel can be weak or absent. Market repair, policy signals, and risk-bearing capacity affect observed outcomes, so not every yield decline identifies portfolio rebalancing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
