# 贷款利率提高可能通过借款参与约束筛走低风险类型

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Higher loan rates can screen out lower-risk types through the borrowing participation constraint*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定贷款额、抵押和其他条款，若银行看不清类型，利率就可能改变申请人构成。考虑风险中性、无抵押、[[有限责任]]的借款人：项目总收入为 $Y$，到期应付额为 $D$，不借款的外部机会收益为 $u_0$。申请须比较预期剩余与外部机会，而不是只看项目能否偿付全部合同额。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding loan size, collateral, and other terms fixed, rates can change applicant composition when the bank cannot distinguish types. Consider a risk-neutral borrower with no collateral and [[有限责任|limited liability]]: project gross revenue is $Y$, promised repayment is $D$, and the outside option yields $u_0$. Participation compares expected residual income with the outside option, not merely whether the project can repay the contractual amount in full.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
U(D)=\mathbb E[(Y-D)_+],\qquad
U(D)>u_0\ \Rightarrow\ \text{strictly prefers borrowing}.
$$

$U(D)<u_0$ 时不申请；相等时无差异，必须另说明选择规则。特别是无抵押时剩余不会低于零，因此“剩余为零”在 $u_0=0$ 时不能自动推出退出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $U(D)<u_0$, the borrower does not apply. Equality implies indifference and needs a tie-breaking rule. With no collateral the residual is nonnegative, so a zero residual does not automatically imply exit when $u_0=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Stiglitz–Weiss 的一种风险排序先固定项目均值，再比较保均值扩散：更分散的收入对凸函数 $(Y-D)_+$ 的期望不低。它说明高风险类型可能保留更强的借款意愿，但“方差更大”本身不足以替代该排序，严格退出也仍取决于参与约束是否跨过阈值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One Stiglitz–Weiss ordering fixes mean project returns and compares mean-preserving spreads. Greater dispersion weakly raises the expectation of the convex residual $(Y-D)_+$. Riskier types can therefore retain a stronger willingness to borrow. A larger variance alone does not establish this ordering, and strict exit still requires crossing the participation threshold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小例子：每人借款 100；安全型确定获得 120，风险型以 $0.8$ 概率获得 150、否则为零，两者均值都是 120。两型可观察特征相同、项目类型不可改变且不可被银行区分，均有外部机会收益 8。到期额从 110 增至 115 时，安全型预期剩余从 10 降至 5，因 $5<8$ 退出；风险型则从 32 降至 28，仍因 $28>8$ 申请。这里改变的是谁借款，不是同一个人改选项目。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a minimal example, each borrower needs 100. The safe type receives 120 for certain; the risky type receives 150 with probability $0.8$ and zero otherwise. Both means are 120. Their observable characteristics coincide, their project types are fixed and indistinguishable to the bank, and both have an outside option of 8. Raising promised repayment from 110 to 115 lowers the safe type's residual from 10 to 5, causing exit because $5<8$. The risky type's residual falls from 32 to 28 but remains above 8. The change concerns who borrows, not a given person's project choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
