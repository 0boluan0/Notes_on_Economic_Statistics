# 概率建模先定义一次试验与基本结果再选择计算法则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Probability modelling defines one trial and its elementary outcomes before choosing a calculation rule*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子程序
> 1. 明确一次随机试验的单位；
> 2. 写出基本结果与样本空间；
> 3. 把问题翻译成事件，并检查基本结果是否等可能；
> 4. 有新信息时先改写条件空间，再选择计数、补事件、全概率或 Bayes 法则。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> **1.** Specify what counts as one random trial.<br>
> **2.** List the elementary outcomes and the sample space.<br>
> **3.** Translate the question into events and check whether the outcomes are equiprobable.<br>
> **4.** If new information is given, first restrict the model to the conditional space; only then choose counting, a complement, total probability, or Bayes' rule.<br>
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个程序不保证模型正确；它只是让试验单位、结果集合、事件和所用公式都可检查。把多次试验混成一个基本结果，或在没有等可能性的情况下直接数个数，仍会得到错误模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This procedure does not guarantee a correct model. It makes the trial unit, outcome set, events, and chosen formula inspectable. A model is still wrong if several trials are mixed into one elementary outcome or if outcomes are counted as though they were equiprobable when they are not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 算后检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Post-calculation checks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结果必须落在 $[0,1]$；完备分区的权重之和必须为 $1$；后验概率的变化方向应与证据相符。这些检查能发现模型或分母错误，但不能取代建模本身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The answer must lie in $[0,1]$; weights over an exhaustive partition must sum to $1$; and the posterior should move in a direction consistent with the evidence. These checks can expose a modelling or denominator error, but they cannot replace the model itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 收到“已知事件 $B$ 发生”这一新信息后，为什么不能先套 Bayes 公式再想样本空间？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After learning that event $B$ occurred, why should you not plug into Bayes' rule before reconsidering the sample space?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 先把概率限制到 $B$ 才能确认各事件、分区和分母代表什么；否则公式中的条件方向或归一化集合可能从一开始就错了。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Restricting the model to $B$ first establishes what the events, partition, and denominator mean. Otherwise the conditioning direction or normalising set may already be wrong.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
