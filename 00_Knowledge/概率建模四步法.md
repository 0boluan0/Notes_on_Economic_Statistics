# A four-step method for probability modelling
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*概率建模四步法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before choosing a probability formula, make clear what experiment the formula describes. A **probability model** connects possible outcomes, events we care about, and assumptions about their probabilities. The four-step method makes those choices explicit: define the sample space, identify the events, assign probabilities, then calculate. A small coin example shows why each step matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择概率公式之前，要先说清楚它描述的是哪一次试验。**概率模型**把可能结果、我们关心的事件，以及各结果的概率假设联系起来。四步法依次把这些选择写明：定义样本空间、确定事件、分配概率，再进行计算。用一个小型硬币例子就能看出每一步为什么重要。

> [!example] A question with extra information · 一个带有已知信息的问题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Toss a fair coin twice, assuming the tosses are independent. We are told precisely that **at least one toss was heads**. Given this information, what is the probability that both were heads? Write $H$ for heads and $T$ for tails.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设两次投掷相互独立，把一枚公平硬币抛两次。现在明确知道的信息是：**至少有一次正面**。在这个条件下，两次都是正面的概率是多少？用 $H$ 表示正面，$T$ 表示反面。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 1. Decide what one outcome records
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先决定一个结果记录什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here one complete experiment consists of **two tosses**, so one outcome records both results in order. The [[概率空间|sample space]]—the set of possible outcomes—is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里一次完整试验包括**两次投掷**，所以一个结果要按顺序记录两次的正反面。[[概率空间|样本空间]]就是全部可能结果组成的集合：

$$\Omega=\{HH,HT,TH,TT\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The outcomes must be mutually exclusive and exhaustive: every completed experiment belongs to exactly one entry. $HT$ and $TH$ are different records. An outcome may legitimately be a whole sequence of physical actions; what matters is that the record preserves the information required by the question. Recording only the total number of heads is another possible model, but its three values $0,1,2$ are not equally likely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些结果必须互斥且穷尽：每次完成试验，都恰好对应其中一个条目。$HT$ 和 $TH$ 是不同的记录。一个结果完全可以合法地记录一整串实际操作；关键是它要保留问题需要的信息。只记正面总次数也是一种模型，但这时的三个取值 $0,1,2$ 并不等可能。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 2. Separate the target event from the known information
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*区分目标事件与已知信息*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An event is a set of outcomes satisfying a description. Let $A$ be the target “both heads” and $B$ the known event “at least one head”:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

事件是一组满足某个描述的结果。用 $A$ 表示目标“两次都是正面”，用 $B$ 表示已知事件“至少有一次正面”：

$$A=\{HH\},\qquad B=\{HH,HT,TH\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We want the [[条件概率|conditional probability]] $P(A\mid B)$, which asks about $A$ within the outcomes allowed by $B$. Writing the sets prevents a change of question: “the first toss was heads” would instead give $B'=\{HH,HT\}$. If information comes from a selection or reporting procedure, that procedure must also be specified; different ways of obtaining information can lead to different conditional models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要求的是[[条件概率]] $P(A\mid B)$，也就是在 $B$ 所允许的结果内，$A$ 的概率。写出集合可以防止悄悄换题：“第一次是正面”会得到另一个条件 $B'=\{HH,HT\}$。若信息来自某种抽取或报告过程，还要说清楚这个过程；获取信息的方式不同，可能对应不同的条件模型。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 3. Assign probabilities before counting
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先分配概率，再判断能否数个数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fairness gives each toss a $1/2$ chance of each face. [[事件独立|Independence]] allows us to multiply those probabilities, so each complete record has probability $(1/2)(1/2)=1/4$. The four probabilities sum to one, as required in a [[离散概率空间|discrete probability space]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公平性意味着每次投掷出现正面或反面的概率都是 $1/2$。[[事件独立|独立性]]让我们可以把它们相乘，所以每条完整记录的概率都是 $(1/2)(1/2)=1/4$。四条记录的概率之和为一，符合[[离散概率空间]]的要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equiprobability comes from these assumptions, not from having written four entries. If the coin were biased or the second result were copied from the first, the probabilities would change. Only after checking the [[古典概型等可能条件|equal-probability condition]] may we replace weighted sums with counts. In a probability tree, branch probabilities are conditional on reaching the branch; the [[概率乘法法则|multiplication rule]] combines them along a complete path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等可能性来自这些假设，不是来自“列出了四项”。如果硬币有偏，或第二次的记录只是抄了第一次，概率分配就会改变。只有检查过[[古典概型等可能条件|等可能条件]]，才能把按概率加权的求和换成数个数。在概率树中，每条边的概率都以到达该分支为条件；沿完整路径，用[[概率乘法法则]]把这些概率组合起来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## 4. Calculate in the correct reference group
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*在正确的范围内计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The target $A$ has probability $1/4$, while the known event $B$ has probability $3/4$. Because $A$ lies entirely inside $B$, their intersection $A\cap B$ equals $A$. Restricting to $B$ and renormalising gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

目标 $A$ 的概率是 $1/4$，已知事件 $B$ 的概率是 $3/4$。由于 $A$ 完全包含在 $B$ 中，交集 $A\cap B$ 就是 $A$。把考虑范围限制到 $B$，再重新归一化，得到

$$P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}=\frac{1/4}{3/4}=\frac13.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We could also count one qualifying record among the three equally likely remaining records. If the information had instead been “the first toss was heads”, only $HH,HT$ would remain and the answer would be $1/2$. The denominator must describe the information actually given.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也可以直接数：剩下的三条等可能记录中，一条满足目标。若条件改成“第一次是正面”，就只剩 $HH,HT$，答案会是 $1/2$。分母必须对应实际给出的信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For other problems, choose the calculation that fits the event: [[补事件法|a complement]] helps when “the target never happens” is simpler; [[全概率公式|total probability]] combines disjoint cases; [[Bayes法则|Bayes' rule]] reverses a conditioning direction. The model tells us what their terms mean before a formula is applied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

遇到别的问题，再根据事件选择计算方式：“目标没有发生”更简单时，可以用[[补事件法]]；需要合并互斥情况时，用[[全概率公式]]；需要反转条件方向时，用[[Bayes法则]]。模型先说明公式中各项代表什么，公式才有明确的用途。

> [!tip] Check what the answer refers to · 检查答案对应什么
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A probability must lie between $0$ and $1$, and the probabilities of an exhaustive disjoint partition must sum to one. Check the conditioning direction and whether the denominator is positive. In this example, learning $B$ removes only a failure of $A$, so increasing its probability from $1/4$ to $1/3$ is consistent. These checks can reveal errors, but a number in range does not prove that the assumptions fit the experiment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 概率必须在 $0$ 与 $1$ 之间，互斥完备分区的概率之和必须为一；还要检查条件方向，以及分母是否为正。本例中，知道 $B$ 只排除了一个不满足 $A$ 的结果，因此 $A$ 的概率从 $1/4$ 升到 $1/3$ 是一致的。这些检查能发现错误，但一个处于合法范围的数字，并不能证明模型假设符合实际试验。
