# Accumulation function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*累积函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **accumulation function** records how much has built up from a fixed starting point to a changing endpoint. If water enters a tank at three litres per minute, the amount added after $x$ minutes is $G(x)=3x$ litres. The amount after four minutes, $G(4)=12$, is one number; the rule $G$ supplies an amount for every allowed duration $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**累积函数**记录从固定起点到不断变化的终点，一共累积了多少。假如水以每分钟三升的速率流入水箱，经过 $x$ 分钟增加的水量就是 $G(x)=3x$ 升。四分钟后的水量 $G(4)=12$ 是一个数；函数 $G$ 则为每个允许的结束时刻 $x$ 给出一个水量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the rate varies, a [[定积分|definite integral]] adds the small contributions. Keep its starting input $a$ fixed and make its ending input $x$ variable:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

速率变化时，用[[定积分]]把各小段贡献相加。保持起点输入 $a$ 不变，让终点输入 $x$ 变化：

$$G(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $f$ is the function being accumulated, $t$ is the **dummy integration variable**, and $x$ is the input of the new function $G$. Renaming $t$ to $u$ leaves the result unchanged. Changing $x$ changes which interval is accumulated. Thus $\int_a^b f(t)\,dt$ with fixed $a,b$ is a number, whereas $x\mapsto\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$ is a function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $f$ 是被累积的函数，$t$ 是**积分中的哑变量**，$x$ 才是新函数 $G$ 的输入。把 $t$ 改名为 $u$，结果不变；改变 $x$，累积的区间就变了。因此，固定 $a,b$ 后的 $\int_a^b f(t)\,dt$ 是一个数，而 $x\mapsto\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$ 是一个函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choose a domain on which every required integral exists
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先保证每个输入对应的积分都存在*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A precise setting is a real-valued function $f$ that is Riemann integrable on a finite interval $[L,R]$, with $L<R$. Fix $a\in[L,R]$. Then $G(x)$ is defined for every $x\in[L,R]$: restriction to a smaller interval preserves integrability, and reversed bounds use a minus sign. The letters $L,R$ specify the domain's endpoints; $a$ is the chosen reference point, which may lie inside that domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个精确的设定是：实值函数 $f$ 在有限区间 $[L,R]$ 上黎曼可积，其中 $L<R$，并固定 $a\in[L,R]$。那么每个 $x\in[L,R]$ 都能定义 $G(x)$：缩小积分区间仍然可积，反向上下限则按负号处理。$L,R$ 表示定义域的端点，$a$ 是选定的参考起点，也可以位于定义域内部。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In particular, $G(a)=0$, and for $x<a$ we have $G(x)=-\int_x^a f(t)\,dt$. If $f$ is defined on a larger interval and is integrable on each finite segment used, the same definition works there as well. Knowing that $f$ exists only at $a$ and $x$ is insufficient: the **whole intervening interval** must permit the integral.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特别地，$G(a)=0$；当 $x<a$ 时，$G(x)=-\int_x^a f(t)\,dt$。如果 $f$ 定义在更大的区间上，并且每个用到的有限线段都可积，同样可以在那里定义累积函数。只知道 $f$ 在 $a,x$ 两点有定义还不够：**连接两点的整段区间**都必须允许积分。

> [!example] The same rule on both sides of zero · 零点两侧使用同一个规则
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(t)=2t$ and starting point $a=0$, triangle areas give $G(x)=\int_0^x2t\,dt=x^2$. At $x=2$ this is $4$. At $x=-2$, the integral from $-2$ to $0$ is $-4$, so reversing its direction gives $G(-2)=4$ as well. The two signs—negative heights and reversed bounds—both matter.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $f(t)=2t$，取起点 $a=0$，由三角形面积得到 $G(x)=\int_0^x2t\,dt=x^2$。$x=2$ 时为 $4$；$x=-2$ 时，从 $-2$ 到 $0$ 的积分为 $-4$，反向后同样得到 $G(-2)=4$。被积函数的负号与积分方向的负号都不能漏掉。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What changes when the endpoint moves?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*终点移动时，增加了哪一部分？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any two allowed inputs $x,y$, [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]] cancels their common accumulated part:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意两个允许的输入 $x,y$，[[定积分区间可加性]]让它们共同累积的部分相消：

$$G(y)-G(x)=\int_x^y f(t)\,dt.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So the change in $G$ depends on the contribution between the two endpoints. This also explains why changing the reference point only shifts the graph vertically. If $H(x)=\int_c^x f(t)\,dt$ for another allowed starting point $c$, then $H(x)=G(x)-G(c)$; the subtracted quantity is constant with respect to $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，$G$ 的变化取决于两个终点之间的贡献。这也说明，改变参考起点只会使图像竖直平移。如果改取 $H(x)=\int_c^x f(t)\,dt$，其中 $c$ 是另一个允许的起点，那么 $H(x)=G(x)-G(c)$；被减去的量不随 $x$ 变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Riemann integrability on $[L,R]$ includes boundedness: some finite number $M\ge0$ satisfies $|f(t)|\le M$ everywhere. The [[积分绝对值界|integral absolute-value bound]] therefore limits each extra piece to its length times $M$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $[L,R]$ 上黎曼可积包含有界性：存在有限的 $M\ge0$，使每一点都满足 $|f(t)|\le M$。由[[积分绝对值界]]，新增部分的绝对贡献不会超过它的长度乘以 $M$：

$$|G(y)-G(x)|\le M|y-x|.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This bound makes $G$ [[一点连续|continuous]]: a small change in the endpoint produces a small change in the accumulated total. It does not yet say that $G$ has a derivative everywhere. The [[积分求导定理|theorem on differentiating accumulation]] adds continuity of $f$ at the point in question and then proves $G'(x)=f(x)$. That conclusion makes $G$ an [[原函数|antiderivative]] on the interior of an interval where $f$ is continuous; it is a theorem about the defined function, not the definition itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个界保证 $G$ [[一点连续|连续]]：终点改变很少，累积总量也改变很少。但它还没有保证 $G$ 处处可导。[[积分求导定理]]在所考察点补上 $f$ 的连续性，才证明 $G'(x)=f(x)$。因此，在 $f$ 连续的区间内部，$G$ 成为一个[[原函数]]；这是对已经定义的函数所证明的结论，不是累积函数的定义本身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f\ge0$, increasing the endpoint cannot decrease $G$, because every added integral is nonnegative. With signed values, this monotonicity can disappear: $G(x)=x^2$ decreases as $x$ moves from $-2$ to $0$. Accumulation means adding signed contributions, not necessarily an always-increasing total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $f\ge0$，终点向右移动不会使 $G$ 减少，因为新增积分非负。允许带符号的函数值后，这种单调性就未必成立：$G(x)=x^2$ 在 $x$ 从 $-2$ 移动到 $0$ 时反而减少。累积表示把带符号的贡献加起来，不一定表示总量始终增加。
