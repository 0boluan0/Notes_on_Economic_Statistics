# 使用价值是物品满足某种人类需要的有用性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Use-value is an object's usefulness in satisfying a human want*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Marx 的分析中，**使用价值（use-value）**回答物品“有什么用”：它凭自身性质满足某种人类需要，这种有用性在实际使用或消费中实现。需要可以由生活必需引起，也可以由偏好引起；物品可以直接供消费，也可以作为生产资料发挥作用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Marx's analysis, **use-value** concerns what an object is useful for. Its properties satisfy a human want, and that usefulness is realised in use or consumption. Wants may concern subsistence or personal preferences; an object may serve consumption directly or function as a means of production.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一件外套的保暖用途，不由裁制它花了两小时还是四小时来定义。讨论这种用途，与讨论它能按什么比例换取麻布，是不同的判断；后一个问题进入[[Marx交换价值|交换价值]]。这延续了 [[Smith的价值区分|Smith 对用途与交换能力的区分]]，而 Marx 还进一步追问交换比例中表现的价值是什么。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A coat's usefulness for warmth is not defined by whether making it took two hours or four. That judgment differs from asking how much linen it exchanges for, which concerns [[Marx交换价值|exchange-value]]. This continues [[Smith的价值区分|Smith's distinction between usefulness and purchasing power]], while Marx further asks what value is expressed in the exchange relation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用价值的范围大于[[Marx商品|商品]]：空气有用，其自然提供的有用性却不以人的劳动为前提，因而在这里的分析中可以有使用价值而没有价值。反过来，毫无用处的产品不能仅凭生产者耗费了时间就获得价值；劳动必须成为有用产品中的社会劳动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use-value extends beyond [[Marx商品|commodities]]. Air is useful, but its naturally supplied usefulness does not require human labour; in this analysis it can therefore have use-value without value. Conversely, time spent making a useless product does not by itself confer value. The labour must count as social labour embodied in a useful product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
