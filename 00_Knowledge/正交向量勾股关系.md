# 正交向量的平方范数按勾股关系相加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The squared norm of a sum of orthogonal vectors is the sum of their squared norms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心结论
> 在实或复内积空间中，若 $x\perp y$，则
> $$
> \|x+y\|^2=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2.
> $$
> 更一般地，有限个两两正交向量满足
> $$
> \left\|\sum_{i=1}^r x_i\right\|^2=\sum_{i=1}^r\|x_i\|^2.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a real or complex inner-product space, $x\perp y$ implies $\|x+y\|^2=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2$. More generally, the squared norm of a finite sum of pairwise orthogonal vectors equals the sum of their squared norms.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么交叉项消失
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the cross terms vanish*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

展开内积得到
$$
\begin{aligned}
\|x+y\|^2
&=\langle x+y,x+y\rangle\\
&=\|x\|^2+\langle x,y\rangle+\langle y,x\rangle+\|y\|^2.
\end{aligned}
$$
正交使两个交叉项都为零。有限多个向量的公式可反复使用同一展开得到。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expanding the inner product produces the two cross terms $\langle x,y\rangle$ and $\langle y,x\rangle$. Orthogonality makes both zero, and the finite-family formula follows by the same expansion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\mathbb R^2$ 中取 $x=(3,0)^T$、$y=(0,4)^T$。两者正交，并且
$$
\|x+y\|_2^2=25=9+16=\|x\|_2^2+\|y\|_2^2.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $\mathbb R^2$, take $x=(3,0)^T$ and $y=(0,4)^T$. They are orthogonal and their squared norms satisfy $25=9+16$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正向结论在实、复内积空间中都成立。勾股等式能否反推出正交则取决于数域；复数域中的独立反例与精确边界见 [[复空间勾股不推正交]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The forward implication holds over both real and complex inner-product spaces. Whether the equality conversely implies orthogonality depends on the field; the exact complex boundary is treated in [[复空间勾股不推正交|the complex Pythagorean converse boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 最近点证明为什么能把总误差拆成“无法消除的残差”和“候选点偏离投影的距离”？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can the nearest-point proof split total error into an unavoidable residual and the candidate's displacement from the projection?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 两个误差分量分别位于子空间及其正交补中，因此平方范数可以按勾股关系相加。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The two error components lie in a subspace and its orthogonal complement, so their squared norms add by the Pythagorean identity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
