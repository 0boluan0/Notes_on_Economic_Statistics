# Pythagorean identity for orthogonal vectors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正交向量的勾股关系*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Perpendicular components contribute separately to **squared length**. Add the vectors first and square the resulting length, or square each component's length and add: orthogonality makes the answers equal. This lets us keep track of how much of a vector lies along different perpendicular directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相互垂直的分量各自贡献一部分**长度平方**。先把向量相加再求长度平方，或者把各分量的长度平方相加，正交性使两种算法得到同一答案。这让我们能分别衡量一个向量沿不同垂直方向的贡献。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A right triangle in coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $x=(3,0)^T$ and $y=(0,4)^T$ in $\mathbb R^2$, where $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]] so these are columns. Their [[向量点积|dot product]] is zero, hence they are [[正交|orthogonal]]. Their [[欧氏范数|Euclidean lengths]] are $3$ and $4$, while $x+y=(3,4)^T$ has length $5$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\mathbb R^2$ 中取 $x=(3,0)^T$、$y=(0,4)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，所以它们是列向量。两者[[向量点积|点积]]为零，因此[[正交]]。它们的[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]分别为 $3$、$4$，而 $x+y=(3,4)^T$ 长度为 $5$：

$$\|x+y\|_2^2=25=9+16=\|x\|_2^2+\|y\|_2^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lengths themselves do not add: $5\ne3+4$. The theorem concerns their squares, and neither vector needs length one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长度本身不能直接相加：$5\ne3+4$。定理说的是平方，而且两个向量都不必有单位长度。

> [!important] Use the norm induced by the same inner product · 使用同一内积给出的范数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a real or complex space with an [[内积|inner product]], set $\|v\|^2=\langle v,v\rangle$. If $x\perp y$, meaning $\langle x,y\rangle=0$, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在具有[[内积]]的实或复空间中，令 $\|v\|^2=\langle v,v\rangle$。若 $x\perp y$，即 $\langle x,y\rangle=0$，则
>
> $$\|x+y\|^2=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Zero vectors are allowed. Finite dimension is not needed for this two-vector identity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 允许零向量，也不需要空间有限维。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Expand the squared length
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么交叉项消失

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inner product expands over addition. Conjugate symmetry gives $\langle y,x\rangle=\overline{\langle x,y\rangle}$, where the bar takes complex conjugation. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内积可以对加法展开。共轭对称性给出 $\langle y,x\rangle=\overline{\langle x,y\rangle}$，横线表示复共轭。因此

$$\begin{aligned}
\|x+y\|^2
&=\langle x+y,x+y\rangle\\
&=\|x\|^2+\langle x,y\rangle+\langle y,x\rangle+\|y\|^2\\
&=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2+2\operatorname{Re}\langle x,y\rangle.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\operatorname{Re}$ means the real part; for real numbers it changes nothing. Orthogonality makes both cross terms zero. Without it, the cross terms can increase or decrease the squared length. For example, $x=y=(1,0)^T$ gives $\|x+y\|^2=4$, while $\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2=2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\operatorname{Re}$ 表示实部，实数取实部后不变。正交时两个交叉项都为零；不正交时，它们可能增大或减小总长度平方。例如 $x=y=(1,0)^T$ 时，$\|x+y\|^2=4$，而 $\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2=2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Several perpendicular components
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*多个垂直分量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $x_1,\ldots,x_r$ be a finite family of **pairwise** orthogonal vectors: every two with different indices have zero inner product. Expanding the finite sum eliminates every cross term, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $x_1,\ldots,x_r$ 是有限个**两两**正交向量，即任意两个不同下标的向量内积都为零。展开有限和时，每个交叉项都消失，得到

$$\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{r}x_j\right\|^2=\sum_{j=1}^{r}\|x_j\|^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $r=0$, both sides are zero by the empty-sum convention. Multiplying the components by arbitrary scalars $c_j$ preserves pairwise orthogonality. Since $\|c_jx_j\|^2=|c_j|^2\|x_j\|^2$, the weighted version is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$r=0$ 时按空和约定，两边均为零。给每个分量乘任意标量 $c_j$，两两正交性仍然成立。由于 $\|c_jx_j\|^2=|c_j|^2\|x_j\|^2$，带系数的版本为

$$\left\|\sum_{j=1}^{r}c_jx_j\right\|^2
=\sum_{j=1}^{r}|c_j|^2\|x_j\|^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real coefficients, $|c_j|^2=c_j^2$; for complex coefficients, the modulus is essential. If the vectors form an [[标准正交组|orthonormal family]], each $\|x_j\|^2=1$, so only the squared coefficient magnitudes remain. This statement concerns finite sums; an infinite series additionally needs a convergence argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实系数满足 $|c_j|^2=c_j^2$，复系数则必须取模。如果这些向量组成[[标准正交组]]，每个 $\|x_j\|^2=1$，右边就只剩系数的模平方。本结论讨论有限和；无限级数还需要另外处理收敛。

> [!warning] Equality is not always a test for pairwise orthogonality · 等式未必能检验两两正交
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For two real vectors, equality forces their inner product to be zero. For two complex vectors it only forces the real part to vanish; see the [[复空间勾股不推正交|complex converse boundary]]. For three or more vectors, even real cross terms can cancel: $(1,0)^T,(1,1)^T,(-1,1)^T$ have total squared length $5=1+2+2$, although the first two have dot product $1$. The theorem's pairwise-orthogonality condition is sufficient; a single total equality does not replace it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对两个实向量，等式迫使内积为零；对两个复向量，它只迫使实部为零，见[[复空间勾股不推正交]]。三个及以上向量即使是实数，交叉项也可能相互抵消：$(1,0)^T,(1,1)^T,(-1,1)^T$ 的总长度平方为 $5=1+2+2$，但前两个向量的点积为 $1$。两两正交是定理的充分条件，不能用一次总量相等来替代。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The norm must match the inner product used to test orthogonality. If we instead measure $(a,b)^T$ by $|a|+|b|$, the opening perpendicular pair gives $7^2=49$, not $3^2+4^2=25$. This does not contradict the theorem: that length rule is not the norm induced by the ordinary dot product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

范数必须与检验正交时的内积相匹配。若改用 $|a|+|b|$ 衡量 $(a,b)^T$，开头那对垂直向量就给出 $7^2=49$，而不是 $3^2+4^2=25$。这不反驳定理，因为该长度规则不是普通点积给出的范数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One important use is a [[正交分解定理|projection–residual decomposition]] $b=p+e$, where $p$ lies in a subspace $S$ and $e$ is perpendicular to $S$. For any candidate $s\in S$, $b-s=e+(p-s)$ has perpendicular components, so $\|b-s\|^2=\|e\|^2+\|p-s\|^2$. This is the calculation behind [[最佳逼近与正交残差|the nearest-point proof]]: changing $p$ to another candidate adds a nonnegative squared error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个重要用途是[[正交分解定理|投影与残差的分解]] $b=p+e$，其中 $p$ 位于子空间 $S$，$e$ 垂直于 $S$。任取候选 $s\in S$，$b-s=e+(p-s)$ 的两部分垂直，所以 $\|b-s\|^2=\|e\|^2+\|p-s\|^2$。这就是[[最佳逼近与正交残差|最近点证明]]背后的计算：把 $p$ 换成别的候选，会额外增加一个非负的平方误差。
