# 产业政策是政府为促进所选择的战略性经营活动而采取的行动

产业政策（industrial policy）是政府预期能够增加其所选择的战略性经营活动的行动。“战略性”表示政府赋予某类活动特别的重要性，不表示该选择已经被证明能增进社会福利。这里的“产业”不限于制造业，也可以涉及农业、服务业、技术或生产任务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industrial policy comprises government actions expected to expand business activities it regards as strategic. “Strategic” identifies a government priority, not an established welfare gain. The activities can be in agriculture or services as well as manufacturing, and may involve particular technologies or production tasks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别一项产业政策，要同时指出“希望扩大什么活动”和“政府具体改变什么条件”。工具可以是专门的基础设施、技能培训或质量认证，也可以是补贴、信贷和贸易措施；是否属于产业政策，不取决于工具名称是否含有“产业”。名义上面向所有企业的工具，也可能因资格、地点和技术要求而特别有利于某些活动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify both the activity to be expanded and the condition the government changes. Instruments include tailored infrastructure, training and certification, as well as subsidies, credit and trade measures. The label on an instrument is not decisive: formally broad measures can favor particular activities through eligibility, location or technical requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，为发展易腐农产品出口而建设特定产区的冷链与检验能力，是围绕一项经营活动配置公共投入的教学例子。它是否值得做，还要检查企业究竟缺什么、私人安排能否解决、服务是否被使用，以及相对于可行替代的收益和成本；“政府支持了它”不是这些问题的答案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A teaching example is cold-chain and inspection capacity in a production area intended to expand exports of perishable crops. Its merit depends on the actual constraint, possible private arrangements, service use, and benefits and costs relative to feasible alternatives. Government support itself settles none of these questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，政策的定义与政策的理由要分开：[[学习外部性]]、[[成本发现外部性]]或[[产业投资协调失灵]]是需要具体证据的候选机制，不是所有产业政策都自动具备的属性。一般的[[市场失灵]]判断、[[实施能力诊断]]和[[产业支持的停止与延期|支持复核]]分别回答不同问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The policy category and its justification are distinct. [[学习外部性|Learning externalities]], [[成本发现外部性|cost-discovery externalities]] and [[产业投资协调失灵|investment coordination failures]] are candidate mechanisms requiring evidence, not automatic features of every industrial policy. [[市场失灵|Market-failure assessment]], [[实施能力诊断|implementation-capacity diagnosis]] and [[产业支持的停止与延期|support review]] answer different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
