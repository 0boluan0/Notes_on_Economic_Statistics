# 自注意力是查询、键和值来自同一表示序列的注意力读取

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Self-attention draws its queries, keys, and values from the same representation sequence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自注意力是一种[[注意力读取]]：查询、键和值都由当前层接收的同一表示序列产生，使序列位置可以从本序列中的可见位置汇总信息。“自”说明信息来源相同，不表示每个位置只读取它自己。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Self-attention is an [[注意力读取|attention read]] whose queries, keys, and values are derived from the same representation sequence supplied to the layer. Positions aggregate information from visible positions within that sequence. “Self” identifies a shared source; it does not restrict each position to reading itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同源表示经过不同投影

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A shared source with different projections*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

省略 batch，以行排列 token。给定 $X\in\mathbb R^{n\times d_{\mathrm{model}}}$，一个标准 Transformer attention head 通过三个可学习投影得到

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Omitting the batch dimension and placing tokens in rows, let $X\in\mathbb R^{n\times d_{\mathrm{model}}}$. A standard Transformer attention head uses three learned projections:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Q=XW_Q,\qquad K=XW_K,\qquad V=XW_V,
$$

$$
W_Q,W_K\in\mathbb R^{d_{\mathrm{model}}\times d_k},
\qquad W_V\in\mathbb R^{d_{\mathrm{model}}\times d_v}.
$$

三者共享 X，但投影矩阵承担不同角色，参数一般不同，所以投影后的 Q、K、V 不必相等。代入[[缩放点积注意力]]后，权重矩阵为 $n\times n$：行对应发起读取的位置，列对应提供 key–value 的位置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All three projections share X, but their parameter matrices serve different roles and generally differ, so Q, K, and V need not be equal. In [[缩放点积注意力|scaled dot-product attention]], the resulting weight matrix is $n\times n$: rows identify reading positions, and columns identify positions supplying key–value pairs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 可见性与同源是两个判断

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Visibility and a shared source are separate choices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑三个位置的序列，位置 2 发出 query。在全可见的自注意力中，它可以读取位置 1、2、3；加入[[因果注意力掩码]]后，它只能读取位置 1、2。两者都从同一序列构造 Q/K/V，因此都是自注意力；“自注意力”本身不规定单向还是双向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a three-position sequence, with position 2 issuing a query. In fully visible self-attention it can read positions 1, 2, and 3. With a [[因果注意力掩码|causal attention mask]], it can read only positions 1 and 2. Both derive Q/K/V from the same sequence and therefore remain self-attention. The term itself does not prescribe one-way or bidirectional visibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 query 来自一个表示序列，而 key/value 来自另一个表示来源，则进入[[交叉注意力]]。若要用因果自注意力训练 next-token 预测，还需检查 logit 与标签的错位；完整的联合约束见[[Transformer自回归约束]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When queries come from one representation sequence and keys and values from another source, the operation is [[交叉注意力|cross-attention]]. Training next-token prediction with causal self-attention also requires correct logit–label alignment; the combined requirements are explained in [[Transformer自回归约束|Transformer autoregressive constraints]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
