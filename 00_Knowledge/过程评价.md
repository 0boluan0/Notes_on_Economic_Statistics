# 过程评价是考察干预如何交付、如何发挥作用及情境如何影响这些过程的系统评价

过程评价（process evaluation）研究一项干预实际怎样运行：交付了什么、怎样交付、谁接触和使用了它，以及参与者反应和周围情境怎样影响其作用。它既记录实施情况，也解释哪些环节运行顺利、哪些需要调整；不是把预算执行率重新命名为评价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Process evaluation systematically examines how an intervention operates: what is delivered, how, who encounters and uses it, and how participants' responses and context shape its operation. It documents delivery and helps explain what works well or needs adjustment, rather than merely relabelling budget execution as evaluation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以[[变革理论]]为起点，选出需要检查的环节。常见观测包括交付与设计是否一致、提供了多少服务、接触到哪些目标人群、出现了哪些改动，以及人员、资源或组织环境怎样影响这些过程。行政记录、现场观察、访谈和问卷可以相互补充；“过程评价”不专指某一种定性方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the [[变革理论|theory of change]] to select the processes that need investigation. Common observations concern consistency with design, the amount delivered, reach, adaptations, and the influence of staff, resources and organizational context. Administrative records, observation, interviews and surveys can complement one another; process evaluation is not synonymous with a single qualitative method.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，计划为100名学生提供各10次辅导。实际80人至少参加一次，60人完成10次，则触达目标人群的比例为80%，完整剂量覆盖为60%，参与者中的完成比例为 $60/80=75\%$。三者分母不同；它们也未告诉我们教学质量如何、未完成者为什么退出，或学习是否因辅导而改善。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose 100 students are each intended to receive ten tutoring sessions. Eighty attend at least once and sixty complete all ten. Reach is 80%, full-dose coverage is 60%, and completion among participants is $60/80=75\%$. The denominators differ. These measures alone reveal neither instructional quality nor reasons for withdrawal, and do not establish a causal improvement in learning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[项目投入产出与结果]]负责区分测量层次；过程评价追查这些层次之间实际发生了什么。它可帮助解释影响评价的结果，但单独的交付记录不能代替[[公共项目反事实|有无干预的反事实比较]]，也不能说明效益是否值得成本。怎样据证据区分交付问题和因果解释问题，见[[实施与理论失败]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[项目投入产出与结果|Inputs, outputs and outcomes]] distinguish measurement levels; process evaluation investigates what actually happens between them. It can help interpret impact findings, but delivery records alone do not replace a [[公共项目反事实|counterfactual comparison]] or establish that benefits justify costs. See [[实施与理论失败|implementation and theory failure]] for the separate diagnostic judgment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评价不要求所有地点机械复制每个细节。应先说清需要保留的核心功能，再判断地方改动是在适应情境还是破坏机制；改动本身不是失败的充分证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Evaluation does not require mechanical replication of every detail. Specify the core functions that must be retained, then assess whether local changes improve contextual fit or undermine the mechanism. Adaptation alone is not sufficient evidence of failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
