# 极值理论研究样本极端值与分布尾部的渐近规律
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Extreme-value theory studies asymptotic laws of sample extremes and distribution tails*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极值理论（EVT）是研究最大值、最小值及高阈值超额等极端事件在适当极限下如何分布的概率理论。金融损失通常取数值越大越不利的方向，于是主要研究右尾；最小值问题可通过变量取负转换为最大值问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Extreme-value theory studies limiting probability laws for maxima, minima, and excesses over high thresholds. Financial losses are usually oriented so that larger values are worse, making the right tail the main object. Negating a variable converts a minimum problem into a maximum problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两条常用建模路线处理不同的数据对象：[[分块极大值法]]每块保留一个最大值，用[[广义极值分布]]描述其适当归一化后的可能极限；[[超阈值法]]保留超过阈值的观测，使用[[广义Pareto分布]]近似超额的条件分布。两条路线由[[极大值吸引域]]条件与[[超阈值极限定理]]连接，而不是两个可以任意互换的分布名称。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[分块极大值法|block-maxima approach]] retains one maximum per block and uses the [[广义极值分布|GEV family]] for possible limits after appropriate normalisation. The [[超阈值法|peaks-over-threshold approach]] retains exceedances and approximates their conditional excess distribution with a [[广义Pareto分布|GPD]]. The [[极大值吸引域|maximum domain of attraction]] and the [[超阈值极限定理|threshold-excess limit theorem]] connect the approaches; their distributions are not interchangeable labels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

EVT 可以在尾部模型成立时外推样本最大损失以外的风险，但极限定理不保证任意有限样本已经接近极限，也不自动消除时间依赖、结构变化或参数不确定性。它补充[[历史模拟法]]的样本尾部信息，而不把尚未观察到的损失变成已知事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
EVT permits extrapolation beyond the largest observed loss when its tail model is appropriate. A limit theorem does not guarantee an accurate finite-sample approximation or remove serial dependence, structural change, or parameter uncertainty. It supplements the empirical tail information used by [[历史模拟法|historical simulation]] rather than turning unobserved losses into known facts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Martin Haugh，Columbia IEOR E4602，[Extreme Value Theory，PDF 第 2–3、6、18 页](https://www.columbia.edu/~mh2078/QRM/EVT_MasterSlides.pdf#page=3)：核对研究对象、两条路线与经验分位数无法越过样本最大值的动机；不将讲义中的数据效率概述理解为 POT 在所有场景必然更优。
- McNeil–Frey，[作者稿，1999-06-19，PDF／文内第 7、9 页](https://statmath.wu.ac.at/~frey/publications/evt-garch.pdf#page=7)：核对尾部近似的条件及原始金融收益的非独立同分布问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Haugh's Columbia notes, PDF pp. 2–3, 6, and 18, support the objects, the two approaches, and the empirical-quantile limitation; the efficiency discussion is not treated as universal superiority of POT.
- The McNeil–Frey author draft dated 19 June 1999, PDF and internal pp. 7 and 9, supports the approximation conditions and the dependence issue for raw financial returns.
