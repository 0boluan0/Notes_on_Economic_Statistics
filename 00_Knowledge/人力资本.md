# 人力资本是个人承载并影响经济活动的知识技能与能力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Human capital consists of knowledge, skills and capabilities embodied in people that affect economic activity.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

人力资本指个人身上与经济活动有关的知识、技能、能力及其他属性。它描述人能怎样有效地完成工作、解决问题与适应任务，而不是单纯计算劳动者人数。在增长模型里，这些不同维度常被简化成一个技能或效率指标 $h$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Human capital comprises knowledge, skills, capabilities and other attributes embodied in individuals that matter for economic activity. It concerns people's ability to perform work, solve problems and adapt to tasks, rather than simply counting workers. Growth models often compress these dimensions into a skill or efficiency index $h$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知识与技能紧密相连，但各有侧重：知识包括某领域的事实、原理和方法；技能侧重运用知识和经验完成任务。例如，了解编程语言的语法属于知识，能够用它定位错误并修复程序体现技能。人力资本可包含两者，不能只用背下多少信息衡量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowledge and skills are related but emphasise different things. Knowledge includes a field's facts, principles and methods; skills concern applying knowledge and experience to tasks. Knowing a programming language's syntax is knowledge, while using it to diagnose and repair a program demonstrates skill. Human capital can include both and is not measured simply by information memorised.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

教育、培训与实践是可能的积累过程，现有能力是存量，两者不能混用。受教育年数或学历可作为人力资本的代理指标，却不能完整表达教育质量、实践所得技能与后续变化。实践导致的能力改善可用 [[学习效应]] 描述；它不要求每次改善都创造一种新的通用技术。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Education, training and practice are potential accumulation processes; existing capability is a stock. Years of schooling and qualifications can proxy human capital, but do not fully capture educational quality, skills gained through practice or subsequent changes. Practice-driven improvements can be described as [[学习效应|learning effects]] without requiring each improvement to create a new general-purpose technology.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Lucas（1988）的简化把 $h$ 解释为个人一般技能水平：在该模型的效率单位口径下，一名技能为 $2h$ 的劳动者提供一小时劳动，等同于两名技能为 $h$ 的劳动者各提供一小时。这个线性换算是模型表示，不是所有职业和任务的经验定律；技能怎样随投入变化还需另写 [[人力资本线性积累|积累技术]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lucas (1988) interprets $h$ as a general skill level. In that model's efficiency units, one hour from a worker with skill $2h$ is equivalent to one hour each from two workers with skill $h$. This linear conversion is a modelling representation, not an empirical law for every occupation. A separate [[人力资本线性积累|accumulation technology]] is needed to determine how skills change with investment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，“人力资本重要”本身还不能推出长期人均增长：需要说明当期资源怎样形成未来能力、回报是否随积累衰减，以及谁承担成本和获得收益。定义也没有自动设定知识外溢或创新激励。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The importance of human capital alone does not establish sustained per-capita growth. The model must explain how current resources create future capability, whether returns diminish with accumulation, and who bears costs and receives benefits. The definition itself specifies neither spillovers nor innovation incentives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [OECD，Survey of Adult Skills – Reader's Companion，第1章](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/survey-of-adult-skills-2023_3639d1e2-en/full-report/the-survey-of-adult-skills-and-the-measurement-of-human-capital_f320884e.html)，“Defining human capital”、Table 1.1 及 “Educational attainment as a measure of human capital”：支持个人承载的多维定义、知识与技能侧重以及学历作为代理的边界；编程例子为独立构造。
- Lucas（1988），[On the Mechanics of Economic Development](https://web.stanford.edu/~klenow/Lucas_Mechanics.pdf#page=15)，*Journal of Monetary Economics* 22，3–42，印刷第17–19页（PDF第15–17页）：支持个人一般技能 $h$、效率劳动表示及生产与技能积累必须分别建模。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The OECD definition, Table 1.1 and measurement discussion supply the multidimensional concept and proxy boundary; the programming example is constructed. Lucas supplies the skill index, efficiency-labour interpretation and separation of production from accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
