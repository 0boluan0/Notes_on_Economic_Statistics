# 摊还复杂度用任意合法操作序列的总成本界约束每次操作的分摊成本

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Amortized complexity controls charges per operation through total-cost bounds for every legal sequence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

摊还复杂度先固定数据结构的初始状态与允许的操作，再给每一条合法操作序列的总成本建立上界，用它控制每次操作的分摊成本。它研究昂贵操作如何被整段序列吸收，不需要假设输入或操作序列来自随机分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Amortized complexity fixes an initial state and allowed operations, then bounds the total cost of every legal operation sequence to control the cost charged per operation. It accounts for expensive operations across the sequence without assuming a random distribution of inputs or sequences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，若存在与序列长度和内容无关的常数 $C,B$，使从规定初始状态出发的每条长度为 $m$ 的合法序列都满足

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if constants $C,B$, independent of the sequence's length and contents, satisfy the following for every legal length-$m$ sequence from the specified initial state,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{m}c_i\le Cm+B,
$$

则每次操作具有摊还 $O(1)$ 成本。这不要求每个实际成本 $c_i$ 都是 $O(1)$。若初始结构本来很大，建构成本或初始存量项必须另外交代，不能无声地套用“从空结构开始”的界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
then each operation has amortized $O(1)$ cost. Individual costs $c_i$ need not be constant. A large initial structure requires a separate construction or initial-state allowance; a bound proved from an empty structure cannot be transferred silently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

容量按固定比例增长的动态数组是典型例子：在搬移一个固定大小引用为常数成本的模型下，从空数组连续追加，偶尔一次要搬移全部现有引用，但全部追加及搬移的总成本仍为线性。[[哈希表几何扩容]]给出同一几何级数机制在表容量管理中的证明；它仅控制容量管理，并未同时证明哈希碰撞处理的成本。整个操作若还依赖随机哈希，可以出现“期望摊还”界，两个限定各有自己的职责。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A geometrically growing dynamic array illustrates this: with constant-cost moves of fixed-size references, appending from empty occasionally moves every current reference, yet total work remains linear. [[哈希表几何扩容|Geometric hash-table resizing]] uses the same mechanism for capacity management, not collision costs. Random hashing may additionally yield an expected amortized bound; the qualifiers address different issues.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
