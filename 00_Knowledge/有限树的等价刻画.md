# 有限 simple graph 是 tree 当且仅当任意两点间有唯一 simple path
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A finite simple graph is a tree exactly when every vertex pair has a unique simple path*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 对有限无向 simple graph $T$，以下条件等价：
> 1. $T$ connected 且 acyclic；
> 2. 任意两顶点间恰有一条 simple path；
> 3. $T$ connected，且每条边都是 bridge；等价地，$T$ 是 edge-minimal connected；
> 4. $T$ acyclic，且加入任意一条缺失的边都会产生 cycle；等价地，$T$ 是 edge-maximal acyclic。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a finite undirected simple graph $T$, the following are equivalent: it is connected and acyclic; every vertex pair has exactly one simple path; it is connected and every edge is a bridge, equivalently edge-minimal connected; it is acyclic and adding any missing edge creates a cycle, equivalently edge-maximal acyclic.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

unique-path 条件把 connected 与 acyclic 压成一句话。connected 保证至少一条 path；若有两条不同 simple paths，从第一次分叉到第一次重会的两段会闭合成 cycle。反过来，cycle 上两个顶点可沿 cycle 的两个方向得到不同 paths。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The unique-path condition combines connectivity and acyclicity. Connectivity supplies at least one path. Two distinct simple paths contain, between their first divergence and next reunion, two internally disjoint segments that form a cycle. Conversely, two vertices on a cycle are joined by two routes around it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

bridge 与 minimal/maximal 两种说法回答同一结构的不同操作问题：tree 中删任意边都会让其两端失去唯一 path；在两个不相邻顶点间加边，则新边与原先唯一 path 恰好闭合一个 cycle。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bridge and minimal/maximal formulations describe the same structure operationally. Deleting any tree edge destroys the unique path between its endpoints. Adding an edge between nonadjacent vertices closes a cycle with their unique original path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一张 connected graph 中每条边都是 bridge，为什么它不可能含 cycle？
>
> **答案：**cycle 上任意一条边都可由 cycle 的其余部分绕行；删掉它不会破坏连通，因此它不是 bridge，与前提矛盾。
