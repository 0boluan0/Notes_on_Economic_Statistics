# Random walks on graphs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图上随机游走

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A random walk on a graph describes a moving object whose current position is a vertex. At each step, a probability rule chooses its next position among the allowed destinations. The graph gives the connections; the rule says how likely each move is. Here we use a fixed graph and a rule depending only on the current vertex, so the positions form a [[离散时间Markov链|discrete-time Markov chain]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
图上随机游走描述一个在顶点之间移动的对象。每走一步，就按某个概率规则选择下一位置：图给出连接关系，规则给出各次移动的概率。本卡使用固定的图，而且选择规则只依赖当前顶点，因此各时刻的位置构成一条 [[离散时间Markov链]]。

> [!example] Choosing a neighbor / 选择一个邻居
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider three vertices joined as $A-B-C$, with each connection usable in both directions. From $B$, choose $A$ or $C$ with probability $1/2$ each. From either endpoint, the only neighbor is $B$, so the next move goes to $B$ with probability $1$.
>
> In the order $(A,B,C)$, the transition matrix is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 三个顶点连成 $A-B-C$，每条连接都可以双向走。从 $B$ 出发，以各 $1/2$ 的概率选 $A$ 或 $C$；从任一端点出发，唯一邻居都是 $B$，所以下一步以概率 $1$ 到达 $B$。按 $(A,B,C)$ 排列状态，转移矩阵是
> $$
> P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\[2pt]1/2&0&1/2\\[2pt]0&1&0\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A row describes where the walker goes **from** one vertex. If it starts at $B$, the successive probability rows are $(0,1,0)$, $(1/2,0,1/2)$, and $(0,1,0)$. Returning to a vertex is allowed; a random walk is not a search that marks visited vertices and avoids them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 一行描述从某个顶点**出发**后去往哪里。从 $B$ 开始，前三个时刻的概率行依次是 $(0,1,0)$、$(1/2,0,1/2)$、$(0,1,0)$。随机游走可以重复到访顶点，不是把访问过的点标记后避开的搜索过程。

## From a graph to transition probabilities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
从图到转移概率

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $G=(V,E)$: $V$ is the set of vertices, and $E$ records the edges. A [[图类型的建模边界|directed edge]] $u\to v$ permits a move from $u$ to $v$ but does not itself permit the reverse move. An undirected edge permits both directions. For the uniform rule below, assume a finite graph with no parallel edges or self-loops. The [[度数、入度与出度|out-degree]] $d^+(u)$ counts the outgoing neighbors of $u$; for an undirected graph, use its ordinary neighbor count $d(u)$ instead.

If $d^+(u)>0$, choosing each outgoing neighbor equally gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
记 $G=(V,E)$：$V$ 是顶点集合，$E$ 记录边。[[图类型的建模边界|有向边]] $u\to v$ 允许从 $u$ 走到 $v$，但本身不允许反向移动；无向边则允许双向移动。下面的均匀规则先限定在没有平行边或自环的有限图上。[[度数、入度与出度|出度]] $d^+(u)$ 数的是 $u$ 的出邻居数；无向图则用普通邻居数 $d(u)$。

当 $d^+(u)>0$ 时，等概率选择每个出邻居得到

$$
P_{uv}=\begin{cases}
1/d^+(u),&(u,v)\in E,\\
0,&(u,v)\notin E.
\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first index $u$ is the current vertex and the second $v$ is the next vertex. Each row has nonnegative entries summing to one, as required of a [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|time-homogeneous transition kernel]]. With the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]], a probability row $\mu_t$ evolves by $\mu_{t+1}=\mu_tP$. Its entry $\mu_t(u)$ is the probability of being at $u$ at time $t$; specifying $P$ still leaves the initial distribution $\mu_0$ to be chosen.

An incoming edge counts a way to **reach** $u$, so the in-degree is not the denominator when choosing where to go next. An [[邻接表与邻接矩阵|adjacency matrix]] records the connections; it becomes a transition matrix only after the move probabilities have been assigned.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
第一个下标 $u$ 是当前位置，第二个下标 $v$ 是下一位置。每行元素非负、总和为 $1$，才满足 [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|时间齐次转移核]] 的要求。采用 [[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]] 时，概率行 $\mu_t$ 按 $\mu_{t+1}=\mu_tP$ 演化，其中 $\mu_t(u)$ 是时刻 $t$ 位于 $u$ 的概率。指定 $P$ 后，还需要选择初始分布 $\mu_0$。

入边数的是**到达** $u$ 的途径，所以选择下一步去向时，分母不是入度。[[邻接表与邻接矩阵|邻接矩阵]] 只记录连接；分配好移动概率后，才得到转移矩阵。

## Weights and staying in place
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
权重与原地停留

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Uniform choice is one possible model. To prefer some moves, give each ordered pair a nonnegative move weight $w_{uv}$, with zero weight for a prohibited move. Let $s_u=\sum_{v\in V}w_{uv}$ be the total outgoing weight. Whenever $0<s_u<\infty$, normalize the weights within that row:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
均匀选择只是一种模型。若要偏好某些移动，可以给每个有序顶点对一个非负移动权重 $w_{uv}$，不允许的移动取权重 $0$。令 $s_u=\sum_{v\in V}w_{uv}$ 为总出权重；只要 $0<s_u<\infty$，就可以在这一行内归一化：

$$
P_{uv}=\frac{w_{uv}}{s_u}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, weights $w_{BA}=3$ and $w_{BC}=1$ give probabilities $3/4$ and $1/4$, rather than $1/2$ and $1/2$. We normalize by the outgoing weight at $B$, not by the weight of the whole graph. A negative cost or a signed similarity score cannot be used directly as a probability weight.

For an undirected weighted walk, choose symmetric weights $w_{uv}=w_{vu}$. Nevertheless, $P_{uv}$ need not equal $P_{vu}$, because $s_u$ and $s_v$ may differ. The first example already has $P_{AB}=1$ and $P_{BA}=1/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，$w_{BA}=3$、$w_{BC}=1$ 给出 $3/4$ 与 $1/4$ 的移动概率，而不是各 $1/2$。分母是 $B$ 的总出权重，不是全图权重。带负号的成本或相似度不能直接当作概率权重。

无向加权游走使用对称权重 $w_{uv}=w_{vu}$，但 $s_u$ 与 $s_v$ 可能不同，所以 $P_{uv}$ 不一定等于 $P_{vu}$。开头的例子就有 $P_{AB}=1$、$P_{BA}=1/2$。

> [!note] State the loop convention / 写清自环约定
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A positive diagonal weight $w_{uu}$ allows the walker to stay at $u$ for a step. In the formula here, that matrix entry is counted once in $s_u=\sum_v w_{uv}$. The usual combinatorial degree of an undirected graph counts a loop twice. Do not mix that degree convention with a row-weight formula without specifying how the weights represent the loop. With parallel edges, choosing a distinct edge uniformly need not agree with choosing a distinct neighbor uniformly; probabilities to the same destination must be added.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 正的对角权重 $w_{uu}$ 允许游走者在一步之内留在 $u$。本卡公式把这个矩阵元素在 $s_u=\sum_v w_{uv}$ 中计一次；无向图常见的组合度数约定则把自环计两次。使用行权重公式时，要明确自环如何编码，不能混用度数口径。若存在平行边，均匀选一条可区分的边，不一定等同于均匀选一个不同邻居；通向同一目的地的概率应相加。

## What the rule does not settle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
还需要区分什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A walk need not make a random choice at every vertex. On the directed cycle $1\to2\to3\to1$, each vertex has one outgoing edge, so the uniform rule follows $1,2,3,1,\ldots$ deterministically once the starting vertex is fixed. Successive positions also need not be independent: the present vertex restricts the next destination.

If $s_u=0$, the normalization is undefined. In an unweighted directed graph this is a vertex with no outgoing edges; an isolated undirected vertex has the same problem. An explicit [[悬空节点转移|dangling-node rule]] is needed if the process is to continue. The same weighted construction works on a countable graph when every outgoing total is finite and positive, but a uniform distribution over infinitely many outgoing neighbors does not exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
随机游走不要求每到一个顶点都真的出现随机选择。有向三环 $1\to2\to3\to1$ 的每点只有一条出边，固定起点后，均匀规则就确定地沿 $1,2,3,1,\ldots$ 循环。连续时刻的位置也不必独立：当前位置限制了下一步的去向。

若 $s_u=0$，归一化没有定义。无权有向图中，这对应没有出边的顶点；无向图中的孤立顶点也有同样问题。若过程还要继续，就需要显式的 [[悬空节点转移]] 规则。相同的加权构造可以用于可数图，但每个顶点的总出权重必须有限且为正；不能在可数无穷多个出邻居上各给相同概率而仍使总和为 $1$。
