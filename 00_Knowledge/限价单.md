# 限价单限定买入最高成交价或卖出最低成交价

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A limit order sets the highest acceptable purchase price or the lowest acceptable sale price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

限价单是指定价格边界的买卖委托。若限价为 $L$，买单只允许以 $P\le L$ 成交，卖单只允许以 $P\ge L$ 成交；成交仍取决于对手订单、排队位置、有效期和其他执行条件。限价约束成交价格，不保证成交数量或成交本身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A limit order specifies an execution-price boundary. With limit $L$, a buy can execute only at $P\le L$ and a sell only at $P\ge L$. Opposing orders, queue position, validity and other conditions still determine execution. The limit constrains the price, not whether or how much the order fills.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在允许以不劣于限价的对手报价成交的简化[[连续竞价]]中，假设卖盘为 100.00 元 50 股、100.20 元 100 股。限价 100.10 元买入 120 股，可以立即买到 50 股，每股 100.00 元；不能为补足数量而跨到 100.20 元。若允许剩余委托留簿，70 股可等待后续对手单；若附即时成交后撤余单的条件，余量则取消。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simplified [[连续竞价|continuous auction]] permitting execution against opposing quotes no worse than the limit, suppose the asks are 50 shares at 100.00 and 100 at 100.20. A 120-share buy limited to 100.10 can immediately take 50 at 100.00, but cannot cross to 100.20 to finish. If residual orders may rest, 70 shares can wait; an immediate-or-cancel condition instead cancels the remainder.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，限价单不等于“只能恰好按限价成交”，也不等于“必须等未来价格穿越限价”。已经能与对手报价成交的限价单可以主动取走流动性。不同场所的专有订单类型还可能限制允许跨越的价位、残单处理或接纳范围，不能仅凭中文名称推定完全相同的行为。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A limit order does not necessarily execute exactly at its limit or wait for a future crossing of that limit. A marketable limit order can actively consume liquidity. Venue-specific types may further restrict price levels, residual handling or acceptance, so matching names do not imply identical behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
