# 观测组均值差等于 ATET 加选择偏差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The observed population mean contrast equals ATET plus selection bias*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 设 $\beta=E[Y\mid D=1]-E[Y\mid D=0]$，两组概率均为正且潜在结果可积。由观察结果与潜在结果的一致性，$\beta=\mathrm{ATET}+\mathrm{SB}$。进一步有 $\beta-\mathrm{ATE}=\mathrm{SB}+(\mathrm{ATET}-\mathrm{ATE})$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With positive group probabilities, integrable potential outcomes, and consistency, the observed population contrast decomposes into ATET plus selection bias. Its difference from ATE also contains the ATET–ATE gap.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

先用[[潜在结果反事实|切换关系]]替换观察结果，再加减同一个反事实均值：
$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta
&=E[Y(1)\mid D=1]-E[Y(0)\mid D=0]\\
&=\{E[Y(1)\mid D=1]-E[Y(0)\mid D=1]\}\\
&\quad+\{E[Y(0)\mid D=1]-E[Y(0)\mid D=0]\}\\
&=\mathrm{ATET}+\mathrm{SB}.
\end{aligned}
$$
消除 SB 只保证 $\beta=\mathrm{ATET}$；若目标是 [[平均处理效应|ATE]]，还要处理 ATET 与 ATE 的差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replace observed outcomes using the [[潜在结果反事实|potential-outcome switching relation]], then add and subtract the treated group’s untreated mean. The first bracket is ATET; the second is selection bias. Eliminating the latter alone does not ensure equality with the [[平均处理效应|ATE]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
