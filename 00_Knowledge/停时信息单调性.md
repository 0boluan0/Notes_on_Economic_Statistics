# 若离散停时 $S\le T$，则 $\mathcal F_S\subseteq\mathcal F_T$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If discrete stopping times satisfy $S\le T$, then $\mathcal F_S\subseteq\mathcal F_T$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 停时信息的单调性
> 设 $S$ 与 $T$ 是相对同一滤过的离散停时。若对每个样本点都有 $S\le T$，则
> $$
> \mathcal F_S\subseteq\mathcal F_T.
> $$
> 较晚停止不会丢掉较早停止时已经能判断的事件。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For two discrete stopping times relative to the same filtration, $S\le T$ implies that every event knowable at $S$ is also knowable at $T$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 证明
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Proof*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取任意 $A\in\mathcal F_S$。对每个 $n\ge0$，由 $S\le T$ 可知

$$
\{T\le n\}\subseteq\{S\le n\}.
$$

因此

$$
A\cap\{T\le n\}
=(A\cap\{S\le n\})\cap\{T\le n\}.
$$

$A\in\mathcal F_S$ 保证 $A\cap\{S\le n\}\in\mathcal F_n$；$T$ 是停时则保证 $\{T\le n\}\in\mathcal F_n$。所以右边属于 $\mathcal F_n$。这对每个 $n$ 成立，故 $A\in\mathcal F_T$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $A\in\mathcal F_S$. Since $S\le T$, the event $\{T\le n\}$ is contained in $\{S\le n\}$. The defining condition for $\mathcal F_S$ and the stopping-time measurability of $\{T\le n\}$ therefore make $A\cap\{T\le n\}$ an event in $\mathcal F_n$ for every $n$. Hence $A\in\mathcal F_T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 在可选抽样中的作用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Role in optional sampling*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $S\le T$ 时，$\mathcal F_S\subseteq\mathcal F_T$ 使“在较早停时已有信息上条件化较晚停时的值”有明确的信息方向。在满足相应条件时，[[可选停止条件|可选抽样]]才能写成

$$
E(M_T\mid\mathcal F_S)=M_S.
$$

信息集的包含关系只说明条件化的方向；它本身不保证可选抽样等式所需的有界性或一致可积性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inclusion establishes the direction of conditioning in optional sampling: information available at the earlier stopping time is contained in that available at the later one. It does not by itself supply the boundedness or uniform-integrability controls needed for the optional-sampling identity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若 $S\le T$，为什么包含方向是 $\mathcal F_S\subseteq\mathcal F_T$，而不是反过来？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $S\le T$, why is the inclusion $\mathcal F_S\subseteq\mathcal F_T$ rather than the reverse?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 到较晚停时 $T$ 时，不会丢失在较早停时 $S$ 已能判断的事件；较晚的信息集可以更大。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** By the later stopping time, every event decidable at the earlier stopping time remains decidable; additional information may also have arrived.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
