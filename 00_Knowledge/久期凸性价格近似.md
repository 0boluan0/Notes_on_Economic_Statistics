# 久期凸性价格近似在一阶价格变化上加入同一收益率坐标的二阶修正
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Duration-convexity pricing adds a second-order correction in the same yield coordinate to the first-order price change*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若全价函数在当前收益率附近足够光滑，保持现金流和估值日不变，将 [[二次近似]] 中的导数替换为修正久期和凸性：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a sufficiently smooth full-price function near the current yield, hold cash flows and valuation date fixed and substitute modified duration and convexity into the [[二次近似|quadratic approximation]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac{P(y+\Delta y)-P(y)}{P(y)}\approx-D_{\mathrm{mod}}\Delta y+\frac12\mathcal C_y(\Delta y)^2.$$

三个输入 $D_{\mathrm{mod}},\mathcal C_y,\Delta y$ 必须使用同一收益率变量。此处 $y$ 是小数年名义收益率、每年复利 $m$ 次；若改用连续复利 $c$，就同时改用对 $c$ 求导的久期、凸性和 $\Delta c$。不能把年复利凸性与连续复利久期拼在一起。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All three inputs must use the same yield variable. Here $y$ is decimal nominal annual yield compounded $m$ times a year. Switching to continuous yield $c$ requires duration and convexity differentiated with respect to $c$, together with $\Delta c$. Annual-compounding convexity cannot be mixed with continuous-compounding duration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取每年支付 $8,8,108$ 的三年债券，当前年复利收益率 $5\%$。按未截断现金流算得 $P=108.169744$、$D_{\mathrm{mod}}=2.659091$、$\mathcal C_y=9.880406$。收益率升 $50$ bp 时，一阶估价为 $106.731578$，凸性修正额为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the three-year bond paying $8,8,108$ annually at a current 5% annually compounded yield, unrounded calculations give $P=108.169744$, $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=2.659091$ and $\mathcal C_y=9.880406$. A 50 bp increase gives a first-order value of 106.731578 and a convexity adjustment of:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac12P\mathcal C_y(0.005)^2\approx0.0133595.$$

二阶估价为 $106.744937$，直接重估为 $106.744833$。本例二阶误差约 $0.000104$，明显小于一阶误差；这仍是本次冲击的验证结果，而非“任何大幅变动加一项就够准”的保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second-order value is 106.744937 versus an exact repricing of 106.744833. Its error of about 0.000104 is much smaller here than the first-order error. That is a checked result for this shock, not a guarantee that one extra term handles every large move accurately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若三阶导数在连接两收益率的区间连续，价格余项为 $P'''(\xi)(\Delta y)^3/6$，其中 $\xi$ 在二者之间。误差取决于冲击大小与区间内高阶导数；实际应用可用[[估值近似验证|完整重估作对照]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the third derivative is continuous between the two yields, the price remainder is $P'''(\xi)(\Delta y)^3/6$ for some intermediate $\xi$. Error depends on both shock size and higher derivatives over that interval; [[估值近似验证|full repricing provides a practical check]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
