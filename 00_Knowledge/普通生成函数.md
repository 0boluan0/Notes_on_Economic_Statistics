# Ordinary generating function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*普通生成函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ordinary generating function stores an entire sequence of numbers in one algebraic expression. The power of a symbol records the index; its coefficient records the corresponding sequence value. This lets us use algebra to organise a counting problem across all sizes at once.

For example, make words using the letters A and B, with repetition allowed and order recorded. There is one empty word, two words of length one (`A`, `B`), and four of length two (`AA`, `AB`, `BA`, `BB`). The counts begin $1,2,4,8,\ldots$. We store them as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
普通生成函数把一整列数放进一个代数表达式中：符号的次数记录索引，前面的系数记录相应的数列项。这样，就可以用代数同时整理一个计数问题的所有规模。

例如，用 A、B 两个字母组成字符串，允许重复，并记录顺序。有一个空字符串；长为一的有 `A`、`B` 两个；长为二的有 `AA`、`AB`、`BA`、`BB` 四个。计数从 $1,2,4,8,\ldots$ 开始，把它们写成

$$A(x)=1+2x+4x^2+8x^3+\cdots.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The term $4x^2$ says “four objects of size two.” It does not say that $x$ is a probability, or that the count is obtained by substituting $x=2$. First fix [[计数对象|what constitutes one object and its size]]; then put that count in the corresponding coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
其中 $4x^2$ 表示“规模为二的对象有四个”。这里没有说 $x$ 是概率，也不是要代入 $x=2$ 才得到数量。应先明确[[计数对象|什么算一种对象、它的规模如何定义]]，再把对应数量放进系数。

> [!important] A sequence written as coefficients / 把数列写进系数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a sequence of real or complex numbers $a_0,a_1,a_2,\ldots$, its ordinary generating function (OGF) is the formal power series
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 对实数或复数数列 $a_0,a_1,a_2,\ldots$，其普通生成函数（OGF）是形式幂级数
>
> $$\boxed{A(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_nx^n}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $n$ runs over nonnegative integers. The notation $[x^n]A(x)$ means “take the coefficient of $x^n$,” so $[x^n]A(x)=a_n$. For example, $[x^2]A(x)=4$ in the word-counting series above. Two formal power series are equal when all their coefficients agree.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 这里 $n$ 遍历非负整数。记号 $[x^n]A(x)$ 表示“取出 $x^n$ 的系数”，因此 $[x^n]A(x)=a_n$。上面的字符串例子就有 $[x^2]A(x)=4$。两个形式幂级数相等，指的是每个次数的系数都相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a counting application, each $a_n$ must be a finite nonnegative integer. For an arbitrary sequence it need not be an integer, positive, or a probability. The constant term $a_0$ records index zero: it is one for our empty-word example, but there is no universal rule that every generating function must start with one. If $A(x)=2+5x+7x^2+\cdots$, the first three sequence entries are simply $2,5,7$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
用于计数时，每个 $a_n$ 都应是有限的非负整数；但若编码任意数列，它不必是整数、正数或概率。常数项 $a_0$ 对应索引零：空字符串例子里它是 1，却不能要求所有生成函数都以 1 开始。若 $A(x)=2+5x+7x^2+\cdots$，前三个数列项就是 $2,5,7$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the coefficients are the probabilities of a nonnegative integer-valued random variable, this encoding is a [[概率生成函数|probability generating function]]. Its nonnegative coefficients must sum to one; that extra requirement belongs to the probability model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若系数是一个非负整数值随机变量的各项概率，这种编码就是[[概率生成函数]]。其非负系数必须加起来等于 1；这是概率模型额外要求的条件。

## Recording allowed amounts
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把允许的数量写出来*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose one labelled box holds identical tokens and we record only how many it contains. If its capacity is two, the allowed amounts are zero, one and two, each in one way. Its OGF is the polynomial $1+x+x^2$: all later coefficients are zero. If any nonnegative amount is allowed, the series becomes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设一个有标号的盒子装相同的筹码，只记录其中的数量。若容量为二，允许零个、一个或两个，每个数量都只有一种方式，因此 OGF 是多项式 $1+x+x^2$，后面的系数都为零。若允许任意非负数量，则得到

$$G(x)=1+x+x^2+\cdots=\frac{1}{1-x}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As a formal identity, the fraction means that multiplying this series by $1-x$ gives the series $1+0x+0x^2+\cdots$: the constant coefficient is one, and every positive-degree coefficient cancels as $1-1$. It is a compact name for the all-ones sequence.

Combining boxes uses a separate fact: the [[生成函数乘积与卷积|product rule for generating functions]] adds their sizes and sums the counts over all possible size splits. The definition here tells us how to encode each box; the product rule explains what multiplying those encodings counts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
作为形式恒等式，这个分式表示：把该级数乘以 $1-x$，得到 $1+0x+0x^2+\cdots$。常数项是 1，每个正次数的系数都以 $1-1$ 抵消。它是全为 1 的数列的一种紧凑记法。

组合多个盒子时，还要用[[生成函数乘积与卷积|生成函数乘积规则]]：把部件规模相加，并对所有可能的规模拆分累计数量。本卡的定义说明怎样编码每个盒子；乘积规则说明相乘后究竟数什么。

> [!warning] A formal series is not automatically a numerical sum / 形式级数不自动等于可求值的和
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the formal interpretation, $x$ is an index marker and no number is substituted. Taking one coefficient, adding series, and multiplying two series use finite calculations at each fixed degree. No numerical convergence is needed for those operations.
>
> If we instead substitute a real or complex number and interpret the series as a function value, convergence becomes a separate question. The [[几何级数|geometric series]] $\sum_{n\ge0}x^n$ equals $1/(1-x)$ numerically only for $|x|<1$; at $x=2$ its terms do not even tend to zero, although the fraction alone has the value $-1$. General numerical evaluation is governed by the [[幂级数收敛半径|radius of convergence]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 在形式解释中，$x$ 只是索引标记，不代入数值。取出一个系数、级数相加，以及两个级数相乘，在每个固定次数上都只做有限次计算，不需要数值收敛。
>
> 如果改为代入实数或复数，并把级数当作函数值，收敛性就是另一件需要检查的事。[[几何级数]] $\sum_{n\ge0}x^n$ 只有在 $|x|<1$ 时，才在数值意义上等于 $1/(1-x)$；$x=2$ 时各项甚至不趋于零，尽管单看分式可算出 $-1$。一般的数值求值要检查[[幂级数收敛半径]]。
