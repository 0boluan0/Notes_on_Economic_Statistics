# 泰勒原则要求名义政策利率对持续通胀上升作超过一比一的反应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Taylor principle calls for a more-than-one-for-one nominal policy-rate response to a persistent rise in inflation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

泰勒原则强调“名义加息”与“实际融资条件收紧”不同：若持续通胀上升同时提高相关期限的预期通胀，名义利率必须上升得更多，才会提高近似事前实际利率。原则描述规则的响应强度，不要求央行对每次暂时价格跳升立即机械加息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nominal tightening differs from real tightening. When persistent inflation raises expected inflation over the relevant horizon, nominal rates must rise by more to increase the approximate ex-ante real rate. The principle concerns a rule's response strength, not an immediate mechanical response to every temporary price jump.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同期限近似式 $r_t^e\approx i_t-E_t\pi_{t+1}$ 下，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the same-horizon approximation $r_t^e\approx i_t-E_t\pi_{t+1}$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\Delta r_t^e\approx\Delta i_t-\Delta E_t\pi_{t+1}.$$

若预期通胀从 2% 升至 3%，名义利率从 4% 升至 5.5%，近似实际利率从 2% 升至 2.5%；若名义利率仅升至 4.5%，近似实际利率反而降至 1.5%。这是给定预期变化的计算，不假定实际通胀和预期通胀永远同步；近似与随机通胀下的精确期望须区分，见[[事前实际利率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If expected inflation rises from 2 to 3 percent and the nominal rate from 4 to 5.5 percent, the approximate real rate rises from 2 to 2.5 percent. A nominal rise only to 4.5 percent instead lowers it to 1.5 percent. This calculation conditions on expectations; actual and expected inflation need not move together. See [[事前实际利率|the ex-ante real rate]] for the distinction between this approximation and an exact expectation under uncertain inflation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[泰勒规则]]中若写 $i=r^*+\pi+\alpha(\pi-\pi^*)+\beta x$，固定其余输入后的通胀总系数为 $1+\alpha$；相应“超过一”要求的是 $1+\alpha>1$。若改写为直接以通胀缺口乘 $\phi_\pi$ 的形式，要求才记作 $\phi_\pi>1$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[泰勒规则|Taylor-rule]] form $i=r^*+\pi+\alpha(\pi-\pi^*)+\beta x$, the total inflation coefficient is $1+\alpha$. The condition is $1+\alpha>1$. In a form multiplying the inflation gap directly by $\phi_\pi$, it is written $\phi_\pi>1$ instead.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从这一代数判断到“整个经济存在唯一稳定均衡”，还须给定预期形成、利率平滑、其他政策规则与模型结构。不能把超过一比一当成所有模型、所有冲击下稳定的充分保证；供给冲击的具体权衡见[[供给冲击下的政策权衡]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Moving from this algebra to a unique stable macroeconomic equilibrium requires assumptions about expectations, smoothing, other policies and model structure. A more-than-one-for-one response is not a universal sufficient stability condition; see [[供给冲击下的政策权衡|policy trade-offs under supply shocks]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
