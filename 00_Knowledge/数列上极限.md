# Limit superior of a sequence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*数列上极限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sequence $-2,\ 3/2,\ -4/3,\ 5/4,\ -6/5,\ 7/6,\ldots$ keeps visiting two different levels. Its positive terms approach $1$, while its negative terms approach $-1$, so the whole sequence has no ordinary limit. Its **limit superior**, written $\limsup$, records the limiting upper level that remains after any finite beginning has been discarded. Here that value is $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数列 $-2,\ 3/2,\ -4/3,\ 5/4,\ -6/5,\ 7/6,\ldots$ 不断靠近两个不同水平：正项趋于 $1$，负项趋于 $-1$，因此整列没有普通极限。**上极限**记作 $\limsup$，记录不断去掉有限开头后，剩下尾部的上方水平最终趋向哪里。这个例子的上极限是 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Look at a whole tail, then move its starting point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先看整个尾部，再移动起点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $x_n=(-1)^n(1+1/n)$ for the example, with $n=1,2,\ldots$. For each starting index $N$, consider every later term $x_N,x_{N+1},\ldots$ together. Their [[上确界|supremum]] is their least upper bound: the smallest ceiling above the entire tail, whether or not any term reaches it. Denote it by $M_N$. In this example the ceiling is attained by the first even-indexed term in the tail:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把例子写成 $x_n=(-1)^n(1+1/n)$，其中 $n=1,2,\ldots$。对每个起点索引 $N$，把之后的全部项 $x_N,x_{N+1},\ldots$ 放在一起。它们的[[上确界]]是最小上界，即压住整个尾部的最低高度，不要求某项一定达到它。记这个值为 $M_N$。本例中，它恰好由尾部的第一个偶数索引项取到：

$$M_1=M_2=\frac32,\qquad M_3=M_4=\frac54,\qquad M_5=M_6=\frac76,\quad\ldots$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $M_N\to1$. We took two separate operations: a supremum over all $n\ge N$, followed by a limit as $N$ increases. Taking only $\sup_{n\ge1}x_n=3/2$ would retain the early peak and answer a different question. Nor must a tail supremum be a maximum: for $x_n=1-1/n$, every tail has supremum $1$, although no term equals $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以 $M_N\to1$。这里做了两步：先对所有 $n\ge N$ 取上确界，再让 $N$ 不断增大。若只取 $\sup_{n\ge1}x_n=3/2$，就会保留早期峰值，回答的是另一个问题。尾部上确界也未必是最大值：对 $x_n=1-1/n$，每个尾部的上确界都是 $1$，但没有一项等于 $1$。

> [!important] Definition on the real line · 实数数列的定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $(x_n)_{n\ge1}$ be a sequence of finite real numbers. For each integer $N\ge1$, set
> $$M_N=\sup\{x_n:n\ge N\},\qquad
> \limsup_{n\to\infty}x_n=\inf_{N\ge1}M_N=\lim_{N\to\infty}M_N.$$
> A tail with no finite upper bound has $M_N=+\infty$. The infimum means the greatest lower bound of the tail ceilings. The final value lies in the extended real line $[-\infty,+\infty]$; the last limit is understood with these infinite values allowed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $(x_n)_{n\ge1}$ 是每项都为有限实数的数列。对每个整数 $N\ge1$，令 $M_N=\sup\{x_n:n\ge N\}$，并把 $\inf_{N\ge1}M_N=\lim_{N\to\infty}M_N$ 定义为上极限。尾部没有有限上界时，约定 $M_N=+\infty$；这里的下确界指这些尾部上界的最大下界。最终值允许在扩展实数 $[-\infty,+\infty]$ 中，最后的极限也按允许无穷值理解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why does this limit always make sense? Removing the first term of a tail cannot increase its supremum, so $M_{N+1}\le M_N$. If the original sequence is unbounded above, every tail is unbounded above: removing finitely many finite values cannot create an upper bound. All the $M_N$ are then $+\infty$. Otherwise they are real numbers forming a nonincreasing sequence. If they have a finite lower bound, the [[实数的确界性质|least-upper-bound property of the real numbers]], applied to their negatives, supplies their infimum $L$; for every $\varepsilon>0$ some $M_N<L+\varepsilon$, and all later ceilings lie between $L$ and $L+\varepsilon$. If they have no finite lower bound, they eventually fall below every real threshold, giving limit $-\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么这个极限总能解释？从尾部删掉第一项，不会使其上确界增加，所以 $M_{N+1}\le M_N$。若原数列上方无界，每个尾部也上方无界，因为删除有限多个有限数不能创造一个上界；此时所有 $M_N$ 都是 $+\infty$。否则，$M_N$ 是单调不增的实数数列。若它们有有限下界，对它们的相反数应用[[实数的确界性质]]，便得到下确界 $L$；对每个 $\varepsilon>0$，总有某个 $M_N<L+\varepsilon$，之后的上界都夹在 $L$ 与 $L+\varepsilon$ 之间。若没有有限下界，则最终会低于每个实数阈值，极限为 $-\infty$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What a finite upper limit tells us
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有限上极限提供的两条信息*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite real number $L$, the equality $\limsup x_n=L$ is equivalent to the following two statements for **every** $\varepsilon>0$: eventually every term is below $L+\varepsilon$, and terms above $L-\varepsilon$ still occur arbitrarily late. In symbols,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限实数 $L$，$\limsup x_n=L$ 等价于：对**每个** $\varepsilon>0$，最终所有项都低于 $L+\varepsilon$；同时，无论看到多晚，之后仍能找到高于 $L-\varepsilon$ 的项。写成量词是

$$
\exists N\ \forall n\ge N:\ x_n<L+\varepsilon,
\qquad
\forall N\ \exists n\ge N:\ x_n>L-\varepsilon.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first statement follows because some tail ceiling is below $L+\varepsilon$. For the second, every tail ceiling is at least $L$, so $L-\varepsilon$ cannot bound a whole tail from above. Conversely, the first statement forces the infimum of the ceilings to be at most $L+\varepsilon$, and the second forces it to be at least $L-\varepsilon$; letting the tolerance shrink identifies it as $L$. Notice the different quantifiers: the lower inequality need not hold for every late term.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一条来自某个尾部上界已经低于 $L+\varepsilon$。第二条则因为每个尾部上界都至少为 $L$，所以 $L-\varepsilon$ 不可能压住整个尾部。反过来，第一条使尾部上界的下确界至多为 $L+\varepsilon$，第二条使它至少为 $L-\varepsilon$；容许误差任意缩小时，只能得到 $L$。注意两侧量词不同：下方不等式并不要求每个靠后的项都满足。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This gives the two rules often used in the [[级数根值判别|root test]]. If $\limsup x_n<q$ for a real number $q$, then $x_n<q$ eventually. If $\limsup x_n>q$, then $x_n>q$ for infinitely many indices. These statements also cover an infinite upper limit in the corresponding direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这给出[[级数根值判别]]常用的两条规则：若实数 $q$ 满足 $\limsup x_n<q$，则最终有 $x_n<q$；若 $\limsup x_n>q$，则有无穷多个索引满足 $x_n>q$。相应方向上的无穷上极限也满足这些结论。

## Ordinary limits and infinite upper limits
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*普通极限与无穷上极限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $x_n$ [[序列收敛|converges]] to a finite real $L$, then all sufficiently late terms lie between $L-\varepsilon$ and $L+\varepsilon$, so their tail ceilings tend to $L$ as well. Therefore $\limsup x_n=L$. The converse fails: the opening alternating example has upper limit $1$ but no ordinary limit. Changing finitely many terms changes neither conclusion about the upper limit, since sufficiently late tails remain identical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $x_n$ [[序列收敛|收敛]]到有限实数 $L$，则足够靠后的全部项都在 $L-\varepsilon$ 与 $L+\varepsilon$ 之间，它们的尾部上界也趋于 $L$，因此 $\limsup x_n=L$。反过来不成立：开头的交替例子上极限为 $1$，整列却没有普通极限。修改有限多项也不改变上极限，因为足够靠后的尾部完全相同。

> [!warning] An infinite upper limit is not an infinite term · 无穷上极限不是某项取无穷
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $x_n=0$ at odd indices and $x_n=n$ at even indices, every tail is unbounded above, so $\limsup x_n=+\infty$. Yet $x_n$ does not tend to $+\infty$: zeros keep returning. In general, $\limsup x_n=+\infty$ means that every tail contains values exceeding any prescribed real threshold.
>
> By contrast, $\limsup x_n=-\infty$ means that eventually **every** term lies below each prescribed real threshold; it is equivalent to $x_n\to-\infty$. The example $x_n=-n$ has tail ceiling $M_N=-N$. These are statements about tails of finite numbers, not ordinary real-valued limits at a point called infinity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令奇数索引项为 $0$、偶数索引项为 $n$，每个尾部都上方无界，因此 $\limsup x_n=+\infty$。但 $x_n$ 并不趋于 $+\infty$，因为零项不断出现。一般而言，$\limsup x_n=+\infty$ 表示每个尾部中都有超过任意给定实数阈值的项。
>
> 相反，$\limsup x_n=-\infty$ 表示对每个给定实数阈值，最终**所有项**都低于它；这等价于 $x_n\to-\infty$。例如 $x_n=-n$ 的尾部上界是 $M_N=-N$。这些都是对有限数列项的尾部所作的陈述，不是把无穷当成普通实数极限点。
