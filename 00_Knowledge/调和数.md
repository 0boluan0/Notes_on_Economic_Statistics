# Harmonic numbers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*调和数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $n$-th harmonic number is the total obtained by adding the reciprocals of the first $n$ positive integers. The contributions get smaller: first add $1$, then $1/2$, then $1/3$, and so on. For instance, a process whose first three steps contribute these amounts accumulates $1+1/2+1/3=11/6$, not $1/3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第 $n$ 个调和数，就是把前 $n$ 个正整数的倒数相加所得的总量。每次贡献逐渐变小：先加 $1$，再加 $1/2$、$1/3$，依此类推。例如，一个过程的前三步分别贡献这些数量，累计值就是 $1+1/2+1/3=11/6$，而不是最后一步的 $1/3$。

> [!important] Definition / 定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For an integer $n\ge1$, write
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对整数 $n\ge1$，定义
>
> $$
> \boxed{H_n=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\frac1k=1+\frac12+\cdots+\frac1n.}
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The index $k$ runs from one to $n$; there is no $1/0$ term. Define $H_0=0$ as the empty sum. For every $n\ge1$, adding the newest term gives the recurrence $H_n=H_{n-1}+1/n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 索引 $k$ 从 1 到 $n$，没有 $1/0$ 这一项。另约定空和 $H_0=0$。对每个 $n\ge1$，加上最新一项就得到递推 $H_n=H_{n-1}+1/n$。

$$
H_1=1,\qquad H_2=\frac32,\qquad H_3=\frac{11}{6},\qquad H_4=\frac{25}{12}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each $H_n$ is one finite number. The sequence $(H_n)$ consists of the partial sums of the [[无穷级数|infinite series]] $1+1/2+1/3+\cdots$, called the harmonic series. Whether those partial sums have a finite limit is an additional question, answered by [[调和数对数增长|the growth and divergence result]]. The fact that the individual term $1/n$ tends to zero does not answer it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个 $H_n$ 都是一个有限的数。数列 $(H_n)$ 是[[无穷级数]] $1+1/2+1/3+\cdots$（调和级数）的部分和数列。这些部分和是否有有限极限，是另一件事，由[[调和数对数增长|调和数的增长与发散结论]]回答；单项 $1/n$ 趋于零，并不能回答这个问题。

## Why reciprocals appear in book stacking
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*书本堆叠为何出现倒数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the classical idealised construction, use identical uniform books of length $L>0$, with one book in each layer. Each upper substack is placed at the limiting balance point over the book below; the whole stack is then balanced at the table edge. Adding the $j$-th book underneath contributes $L/(2j)$ to the possible top-book overhang. Summing those contributions gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经典的理想化构造使用长度为 $L>0$、质量分布均匀且彼此相同的书，每层只放一本。上方每组书都在下一本书上达到临界平衡，再把整体平衡在桌边。向底下加入第 $j$ 本书时，可使最上方书的伸出量增加 $L/(2j)$。累计这些贡献，得到

$$
B_n=\frac L2+\frac L4+\cdots+\frac L{2n}=\frac L2 H_n.
$$

> [!example] Four books / 四本书
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The construction gives $B_4=(L/2)(25/12)=25L/24$, a little more than one book length. With unit-length books this is the familiar $H_n/2$ formula. The harmonic number is dimensionless; the factor $L$ supplies the length unit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 四本书时，$B_4=(L/2)(25/12)=25L/24$，略超过一本书的长度。把书长设为 1，就得到熟悉的 $H_n/2$。调和数本身没有长度单位，长度单位来自因子 $L$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This example illustrates a finite reciprocal sum. It is a specified one-book-per-layer balance model, not a claim about every possible pile arrangement. The question of how slowly its overhang increases belongs to [[调和数对数增长|logarithmic growth]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子说明有限的倒数和怎样出现。它限定了每层一本的平衡模型，不是在断言任意堆法都满足同一最大伸出量。伸出量增长有多慢，要进一步看[[调和数对数增长]]。
