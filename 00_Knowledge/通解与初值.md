# General solutions and initial data
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*通解与初值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An equation can determine how a curve changes without fixing which curve it is. For $y'=2x$, all functions $y=x^2+C$ have the required slope; the real constant $C$ shifts the graph vertically. The extra requirement $y(2)=7$ gives $7=4+C$, hence $C=3$. The family describes the possibilities, while the value at one input chooses a member.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方程可以规定曲线怎样变化，却不确定究竟是哪一条曲线。对 $y'=2x$，所有 $y=x^2+C$ 都有要求的斜率；实常数 $C$ 改变图像的整体高度。再加上 $y(2)=7$，便得到 $7=4+C$，所以 $C=3$。解族描述可能性，一个输入处的函数值则在其中选取成员。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the claim that these are **all** solutions on an interval follows from [[原函数相差常数|antiderivatives differing by a constant]]. In a more complicated [[常微分方程|ODE]], writing down a parameterised family does not by itself prove that no other solutions have been missed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里能断言它们是一个区间上的**全部**解，依据是[[原函数相差常数]]。对更复杂的[[常微分方程]]，写出一个带参数的解族，本身还不能证明没有遗漏其他解。

> [!abstract] Three roles to keep separate · 分清三个角色
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A **general solution** describes solutions in a specified region using free constants, with any separately needed branches accounted for. A **particular solution** is one specified member. An **initial-value problem** combines the differential equation with data such as $y(x_0)=y_0$: $x_0$ is the chosen input, and $y_0$ the required value there. The solution must satisfy both the equation on an interval containing $x_0$ and the data at $x_0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **通解**用自由常数描述指定范围内的解，并交代需要另列的解支；**特解**是其中一条确定的解。**初值问题**把微分方程与 $y(x_0)=y_0$ 这样的数据放在一起：$x_0$ 是指定输入，$y_0$ 是该处要求的函数值。解既要在包含 $x_0$ 的区间上满足方程，也要在 $x_0$ 满足数据。

## A constant need not be an additive height
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*常数不一定表现为相加的高度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course example $y'+xy=0$ has the family $y=Ae^{-x^2/2}$, with real parameter $A$. By the [[链式法则|chain rule]], its derivative is $-xAe^{-x^2/2}=-xy$, so every $A$ works. The initial condition $y(0)=3$ gives $A=3$; $y(0)=0$ gives the zero solution. The free parameter records unspecified starting information, not an unfinished calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程例子 $y'+xy=0$ 有解族 $y=Ae^{-x^2/2}$，参数 $A$ 为实数。由[[链式法则]]，它的导数是 $-xAe^{-x^2/2}=-xy$，所以每个 $A$ 都成立。初值 $y(0)=3$ 给出 $A=3$，$y(0)=0$ 给出零解。自由参数记录尚未指定的起始信息，不表示计算没有做完。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We can also prove completeness here without dividing by $y$, so zero is not lost. For any differentiable solution on an interval, the [[乘积法则|product rule]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里还能不除以 $y$ 就证明解族完整，因而不会遗漏零解。对任意区间上的可导解，[[乘积法则]]给出

$$\frac d{dx}\bigl(e^{x^2/2}y(x)\bigr)=e^{x^2/2}\bigl(y'(x)+xy(x)\bigr)=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A function with zero derivative throughout one interval is constant. Thus $e^{x^2/2}y=A$, proving that every solution there has the displayed form. This additional argument justifies uniqueness for the specified initial value in this example; solving $A=3$ alone would only choose a member of the proposed family.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一区间内处处导数为零的函数是常数，因此 $e^{x^2/2}y=A$，每个解都必须有上面的形式。这段额外论证才使本例中指定初值的唯一性成立；单独解出 $A=3$，只是在已经提出的解族内部完成选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Initial data do not guarantee uniqueness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有初值不等于自动唯一*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a second-order equation, starting position alone may leave the starting slope undetermined. The equation $y''=0$ has $y=A+Bx$; $y(0)=2$ fixes $A=2$ but leaves $B$ free. Adding $y'(0)=-1$ gives $y=2-x$. For an equation of order $n$, values of $y,y',\ldots,y^{(n-1)}$ at one input are the usual $n$ initial data. This count is a guide under suitable hypotheses, not a theorem for every written equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对二阶方程，只给起始位置还可能留下起始斜率。$y''=0$ 有解 $y=A+Bx$；$y(0)=2$ 只确定 $A=2$，$B$ 仍自由。再加 $y'(0)=-1$ 才得到 $y=2-x$。对 n 阶方程，通常在同一个输入处给定 $y,y',\ldots,y^{(n-1)}$ 这 n 个初始数据。这种计数是在适当条件下的指引，不是对任意书面方程无条件成立的定理。

> [!warning] Two solutions can share the same starting value · 两个解可以有同一个初值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider $y'=3|y|^{2/3}$ with $y(0)=0$. Both $y(x)=0$ and $y(x)=x^3$ solve it on the whole real line. For the second, $y'=3x^2=3|x^3|^{2/3}$, including at zero. The initial values agree but the functions differ. Continuity of the right-hand side alone therefore does not guarantee uniqueness.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑 $y'=3|y|^{2/3}$，并给定 $y(0)=0$。$y(x)=0$ 与 $y(x)=x^3$ 都在整个实数轴上解它。第二个函数满足 $y'=3x^2=3|x^3|^{2/3}$，零点也成立。两者初值相同，却是不同的函数。因此右端连续本身并不能保证唯一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One common sufficient regularity assumption for $y'=f(x,y)$ is that $f$ is continuous near $(x_0,y_0)$ and is **locally Lipschitz in $y$** there: one finite constant $L$ bounds $|f(x,u)-f(x,v)|$ by $L|u-v|$ for all nearby $x,u,v$. This controls how much the prescribed rate changes when the state changes. The usual local existence-and-uniqueness theorem then gives a unique solution near $x_0$, not automatically for all real inputs. A continuously differentiable right-hand side satisfies such a local bound; failure of a sufficient condition alone proves neither nonexistence nor nonuniqueness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $y'=f(x,y)$，一种常用的充分正则条件是：$f$ 在 $(x_0,y_0)$ 附近连续，并且对 $y$ **局部 Lipschitz**，即存在一个有限常数 $L$，使附近所有 $x,u,v$ 都满足 $|f(x,u)-f(x,v)|\le L|u-v|$。它限制了状态改变时，规定的变化率能改变多少。通常的局部存在唯一性定理于是保证 $x_0$ 附近有唯一解，却不会自动保证全部实数输入上都有解。连续可微的右端满足这种局部界；某项充分条件失效，本身也不能反推出无解或多解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Existence can fail at an inadmissible starting point: $y'=1/x$ cannot be satisfied on an interval containing $x=0$. Even a smooth right-hand side need not give a global solution: $y'=y^2$, $y(0)=1$ has $y=1/(1-x)$ on $(-\infty,1)$ and blows up as $x\to1^-$. A smaller interval containing zero is also a valid solution domain. Seeking the [[微分方程最大区间|maximal solution interval]] is the further question of how far the same solution can be extended.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不合法的起始点可能使解不存在：$y'=1/x$ 无法在包含 $x=0$ 的区间上成立。即使右端光滑，也不一定得到全局解：$y'=y^2$、$y(0)=1$ 有解 $y=1/(1-x)$，定义在 $(-\infty,1)$，但在 $x\to1^-$ 时爆破。包含零的更小区间也可以是合法解区间。寻找[[微分方程最大区间]]，才进一步追问同一条解能延拓多远。
