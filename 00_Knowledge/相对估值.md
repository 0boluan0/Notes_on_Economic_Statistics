# 相对估值是依据可比资产的市场定价与一致价值驱动因素推断目标价格的方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Relative valuation infers a target's price from comparable assets' market pricing and consistently measured value drivers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相对估值先看市场怎样给可比对象定价，再把相应倍数应用于目标公司的同口径指标。它回答“按这些可比对象的定价，目标应对应多少价格”，不直接证明这个价格等于全部未来现金流的内在价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Relative valuation applies a market-derived multiple to the target's corresponding measure. It answers what price is consistent with those comparables, not whether that price equals intrinsic value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若可比倍数为 $m$、目标指标为 $X$，则相对价格估计为 $\widehat V=mX$。例如三家经过筛选的可比公司，同年度预测[[市盈率]]为12、15、18倍；若选中位数15倍、目标同年度预测EPS为2元，则每股估计为30元。选择中位数是本例的方法选择，不会自动使三家公司可比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With comparable multiple $m$ and matched target measure $X$, the estimate is $\widehat V=mX$. Suppose screened comparables have same-year forward [[市盈率|P/E ratios]] of 12, 15, and 18. Using their median of 15 and target EPS of 2 gives 30 per share. The median is a choice in this example, not proof of comparability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“可比”需要检查增长、风险、再投资及现金分配特征，也要统一估值日、预测期间、会计调整和权利范围。行业相同只是筛选起点。[[市盈率]]乘每股盈利得到每股价格；[[EV-EBITDA倍数]]乘匹配的EBITDA得到经营企业价值，不能把两者的输出混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare growth, risk, reinvestment and distributions, while matching valuation dates, forecast periods, accounting adjustments and claims. Industry membership is only a starting point. [[市盈率|P/E]] times EPS produces a share price; [[EV-EBITDA倍数|EV/EBITDA]] times matched EBITDA produces operating enterprise value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果整个可比组已经被市场高估，“比同行便宜”仍可能贵于独立现金流估值。倍数背后的条件可继续看[[市盈率基本面约束]]和[[市净率基本面约束]]；增加倍数数量不能代替对这些条件的判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A target can look cheap relative to an overpriced peer group yet exceed an independent cash-flow value. See the [[市盈率基本面约束|fundamental constraints on P/E]] and [[市净率基本面约束|P/B]]. More multiples do not replace judgement about those conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
