# 直接套用试点效应预测规模化结果需要说明人群、实施和系统反馈的可比性

试点扩张的效应外推，是用试点证据预测覆盖更多对象或更大地区后的政策效果。它是一个需要条件的[[外部效度]]判断，不是把试点平均效果机械乘以全国人数。即使试点内部识别可信，扩张时改变了谁接受、谁交付或周围人的反应，所预测的效果就可能不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Extrapolating a pilot to scale uses its evidence to predict effects under broader coverage. This is a conditional [[外部效度|external-validity]] judgment, not mechanical multiplication by the national population. Credible identification within the pilot does not keep effects fixed when recipients, implementers or surrounding responses change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把扩张方案与试点逐项对照：

- **对象与地点：** 试点是否偏向主动报名、条件较好或更容易合作的人群？目标总体的差异是否会改变作用机制？
- **实施组织：** 扩张后由谁招聘、培训、监督和维护？人员供给、成本与监督能力能否维持试点中的关键交付？
- **市场与外溢：** 更多处理对象是否会改变价格、工资、拥挤程度或未参与者的结果？试点的对照比较覆盖了哪些反馈？
- **政治和行为反应：** 收益与损失变大后，参与者、执行者或利益群体是否会调整配合、规避、支持或阻挠行为？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare four features: **recipients and sites**, including selection into the pilot; **delivery organizations**, staff, costs and supervision; **markets and spillovers**, including prices and nonparticipants; and **political or behavioral responses** as stakes change. Each item is a question to investigate, not a prediction that expansion must worsen it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些是可能改变效果的通道，不是“扩大必然失败”的清单。人员培训可扩充供给，组织学习可改善交付，外溢也可能为正或很小；利益反应可能支持政策，也可能阻碍它。判断需依照具体证据，不能预先写成工资必涨、质量必降或政治阻力必增。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These channels can change effects without guaranteeing failure. Training can expand supply, organizational learning can improve delivery, spillovers may be positive or small, and stakeholders may support or resist a policy. Evidence must determine which responses occur.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个独立构造的本地辅导员市场说明价格反馈。设同质辅导服务的供给为 $S(w)=2(w-50)$，私人需求为 $D(w)=300-2w$，$w$ 是同一期间每单位服务的工资；在所用区间两者均非负，没有跨地流动或其他供给变化。政府额外购买 $q$ 单位服务时，均衡满足：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a constructed local market for homogeneous tutoring services. Supply is $S(w)=2(w-50)$ and private demand $D(w)=300-2w$, where $w$ is the wage per unit of service over the same period. Quantities are nonnegative over the relevant range, with no migration or other supply shifts. If government additionally purchases $q$ units, market clearing requires:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$2(w-50)=300-2w+q,\qquad w=100+\frac q4.$$

没有新增购买时工资100、私人使用100单位；政府购买20单位后，工资105、私人使用90单位、总供给110单位。政府获得20单位不等于全市场净增20单位，也不能按原工资计算实际购买支出。这个结果来自指定的供需斜率；供给若在相关范围内完全弹性，工资反馈可以不同。例子既不是实际估计，也没有从服务数量推出学习效果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Initially the wage is 100 and private use 100 units. With government purchasing 20, the wage becomes 105, private use 90 and total supply 110. Public purchases therefore do not equal the net increase in total services, and expenditure cannot be calculated at the old wage. These results depend on the specified slopes; perfectly elastic supply could produce a different wage response. No empirical estimate or learning gain is claimed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应分别扩大或检验三个维度：样本所代表的人群范围、真正接受干预的实施规模、随机化单位所覆盖的相互作用范围。只增加受访人数，不能自动检验组织扩张或价格外溢；采用更大的随机化区域，也必须说明相关外溢是否越过边界。小试点仍可提供机制和可行性证据，必要时结合目标情境的[[过程评价]]、分阶段扩张或明确假设的模型预测；不存在保证普遍外推成功的固定规模门槛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish the represented population, actual implementation scale, and the interaction area covered by the randomization unit. More respondents do not automatically test delivery at scale or market spillovers; larger randomized areas still require checking cross-boundary effects. Small pilots can inform mechanisms and feasibility, complemented by [[过程评价|process evidence]] in the target setting, phased expansion or explicitly conditional model forecasts. There is no universal scale threshold guaranteeing generalization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Muralidharan & Niehaus（2017），Experimentation at Scale，NBER WP23957](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w23957/w23957.pdf)，正文第2–7页（PDF第3–8页），§1.1–1.3：支持代表性、组织实施与外溢三个规模维度；第6页对教师工会反应写作一种推测，不构成普遍政治定律。
- 同文§2.3与§3.1，正文第12–16页（PDF第13–17页）：支持外溢取决于情境、随机化边界与成本权衡，以及小试点、目标实施协议和模型外推的互补作用。本文供需式为独立构造的一市场反馈例，不冒充全文一般均衡模型或原文实证数值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The NBER manuscript distinguishes three scale dimensions and treats organizational, market and political responses conditionally. Later sections discuss spillover boundaries and complementary approaches. The supply–demand example is independently constructed, not an empirical finding or a full general-equilibrium model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
