# Mahalanobis distance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*马氏距离*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Mahalanobis distance** measures separation after accounting for the scales of the variables and the directions in which they vary together. A difference of two units can be ordinary for a variable that varies widely, yet large for one that barely varies. Ordinary [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]] adds squared coordinate differences directly; Mahalanobis distance first changes the measuring scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**马氏距离**在测量两点相隔多远时，同时考虑各变量的尺度以及它们共同变化的方向。同样差两个单位，对变化很大的变量可能很普通，对变化很小的变量却可能很大。普通[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]直接累加坐标差的平方；马氏距离先调整衡量差异的尺度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a fixed real symmetric [[正定矩阵|positive-definite matrix]] $\Sigma$ of size $p\times p$, with $p\ge1$. Positive definiteness means $v^T\Sigma v>0$ for every nonzero vector $v$; it ensures that all directions have positive scale and that $\Sigma^{-1}$ exists. Often $\Sigma$ is a population or sample [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]], but the geometric definition only needs the chosen positive-definite matrix. Let $x,y\in\mathbb R^p$ be two finite coordinate vectors and put $\delta=x-y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选定一个固定的 $p\times p$ 实对称[[正定矩阵]] $\Sigma$，其中 $p\ge1$。正定指每个非零向量 $v$ 都满足 $v^T\Sigma v>0$：所有方向都有正尺度，并且逆矩阵 $\Sigma^{-1}$ 存在。$\Sigma$ 常取总体或样本[[协方差矩阵]]，但几何定义只要求选定矩阵正定。令 $x,y\in\mathbb R^p$ 为两个有限坐标向量，$\delta=x-y$ 是它们的位移。

> [!important] Distance and squared distance · 距离与平方距离
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $$d_\Sigma(x,y)=\sqrt{\delta^T\Sigma^{-1}\delta},
> \qquad d_\Sigma^2(x,y)=\delta^T\Sigma^{-1}\delta.$$
> The superscript $T$ means transpose: a column becomes a row so that the matrix product yields one number. The square root is part of the distance. If the quadratic form equals $9$, the distance is $3$ and the squared distance is $9$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上标 $T$ 表示转置，把列变成行，使矩阵乘积得到一个数。平方根属于距离定义的一部分；如果二次型等于 $9$，距离是 $3$，平方距离才是 $9$。

> [!example] A difference measured in standard-deviation units · 用标准差单位衡量差异
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(4,1)$, meaning diagonal entries $4,1$ and zero off-diagonal entries, and $\delta=(2,1)^T$. When these entries are variances, the standard deviations are $2$ and $1$. The displacement is therefore one standard deviation in each coordinate:
> $$d_\Sigma^2=\frac{2^2}{4}+\frac{1^2}{1}=2,\qquad d_\Sigma=\sqrt2.$$
> Its ordinary Euclidean length is $\sqrt5$. These answers use different scales; neither is an arithmetic correction of the other.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\operatorname{diag}(4,1)$ 表示对角为 $4,1$、其余位置为零。若它们是方差，标准差就是 $2$ 和 $1$。位移 $(2,1)^T$ 在两个坐标上都恰好是一个标准差，因此平方马氏距离为 $2$、马氏距离为 $\sqrt2$。普通欧氏长度则是 $\sqrt5$；它们使用不同尺度。

## Euclidean distance after whitening
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 白化后的 Euclidean 距离

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\Sigma=LL^T$ with $L$ invertible, for example a [[Cholesky 正定判据|Cholesky factor]]. Since $\Sigma^{-1}=L^{-T}L^{-1}$, where $L^{-T}$ means $(L^{-1})^T$,
$$d_\Sigma(x,y)=\|L^{-1}(x-y)\|_2.$$
Thus transform both points by the same invertible map $L^{-1}$ and measure their Euclidean separation. This is **whitening** when $\Sigma$ is the covariance: applying this map to a centered random vector gives identity covariance. Whitening alone does not make its coordinates independent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取分解 $\Sigma=LL^T$，其中 $L$ 可逆，例如 [[Cholesky 正定判据|Cholesky 分解的因子]]。由于 $\Sigma^{-1}=L^{-T}L^{-1}$，$L^{-T}$ 指 $(L^{-1})^T$，距离便等于上式：对两点应用同一个可逆变换 $L^{-1}$，再计算欧氏距离。若 $\Sigma$ 是协方差，这叫**白化**：对中心化随机向量这样变换后，协方差成为单位矩阵。白化本身不保证各坐标独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The principal-direction description uses an [[实对称矩阵谱定理|orthogonal eigenbasis]] explicitly. Write $\Sigma=Q\Lambda Q^T$, where the columns of $Q$ are perpendicular unit eigenvectors and $\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_p)$ has positive eigenvalues. If $u=Q^T\delta$ gives the displacement in these directions, then
$$d_\Sigma^2=\sum_{j=1}^p\frac{u_j^2}{\lambda_j}.$$
A direction with large variance receives less weight. Correlations generally rotate these directions away from the original coordinate axes; simply dividing each original coordinate by its standard deviation does not in general reproduce this distance. The [[正定逆平方根|symmetric inverse square root]] gives another whitening map.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要解释“沿主方向旋转、缩放”，可明确使用[[实对称矩阵谱定理|正交特征分解]] $\Sigma=Q\Lambda Q^T$。$Q$ 的列是两两垂直的单位特征向量，$\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_p)$ 中各特征值为正。$u=Q^T\delta$ 是位移在这些方向上的坐标；上式说明方差大的方向权重较小。存在相关性时，主方向通常不再等于原坐标轴，所以只把每个原坐标除以自身标准差，通常不等于马氏距离。[[正定逆平方根|对称逆平方根]]给出另一种白化变换。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because the whitening map is invertible, this is a genuine [[度量空间|metric]]: it is nonnegative, symmetric, zero exactly when the two points coincide, and obeys the triangle inequality inherited from Euclidean distance. The squared distance generally fails the triangle inequality: on the real line with $\Sigma=1$, the points $0,1,2$ give $4>1+1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

白化变换可逆，因此这里的距离是真正的[[度量空间|度量]]：非负、对称，只在两点相同时为零，并继承欧氏距离的三角不等式。平方距离通常不满足三角不等式；例如实数轴上 $\Sigma=1$，三个点 $0,1,2$ 的平方距离给出 $4>1+1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To decide whether a measured distance is statistically unusual, extra distributional assumptions are needed; see [[马氏距离不要求正态|geometry versus probability calibration]]. If the scale matrix is singular, the ordinary inverse is unavailable and [[奇异尺度马氏伪距离|using a pseudoinverse changes the geometry]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要判断某个距离在统计上是否罕见，还需要分布条件，见[[马氏距离不要求正态|几何与概率校准的区别]]。尺度矩阵奇异时，普通逆不存在；[[奇异尺度马氏伪距离|改用伪逆会改变几何含义]]。
