# Degree, indegree and outdegree
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*度数、入度与出度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A vertex's **degree** counts how many edge ends meet there. On the undirected path $a-b-c$, the middle vertex $b$ has degree $2$, while $a$ and $c$ each have degree $1$. If an isolated vertex $d$ is added, its degree is $0$: being a vertex does not itself contribute an edge.

With arrows, the question splits into two: how many edges arrive, and how many leave? These are **indegree** and **outdegree**. First specify the [[图类型的建模边界|graph model]]: whether edges have directions, whether a self-loop may join a vertex to itself, and whether distinct parallel edges may have the same endpoints. All graphs here have finite vertex and edge sets, denoted by $V$ and $E$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

顶点的**度数**统计有多少个边的端点落在这里。在无向路径 $a-b-c$ 中，中间顶点 $b$ 的度数为 $2$，而 $a,c$ 的度数各为 $1$。若另加一个孤立顶点 $d$，它的度数是 $0$：仅仅成为顶点，并不会自动贡献一条边。

有箭头时，问题分成两个：有多少条边进入这里，又有多少条边从这里离开？分别称为**入度**和**出度**。应先说明[[图类型的建模边界|图的模型]]：边是否有方向，是否允许连接顶点自身的自环，以及是否允许端点相同但彼此不同的平行边。本文的顶点集合 $V$ 与边集合 $E$ 都有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Counting undirected edge ends

Write $\deg(v)$ for the degree of vertex $v$. Count one for each end of an edge located at $v$. An ordinary edge joining distinct vertices contributes one at each endpoint. A self-loop has both ends at the same vertex and therefore contributes **two** there. Each parallel edge is counted separately.

In a **simple undirected graph**, loops and parallel edges are absent. Each incident edge then leads to one different neighbour, so degree equals the number of neighbours. That shortcut does not extend unchanged to multigraphs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*无向边按端点计数*

用 $\deg(v)$ 表示顶点 $v$ 的度数。每个落在 $v$ 上的边端点计一次。连接不同顶点的普通边给两端各贡献一次；自环的两端都在同一个顶点，因此给该点贡献**两次**。每条平行边分别计数。

在**简单无向图**中，没有自环和平行边，每条关联边恰好通向一个不同的邻居，所以度数等于邻居数。多重图不能直接照搬这个简化计数法。

> [!example] Two parallel edges and a loop · 两条平行边与一个自环
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take vertices $a,b,c,d$. Include two distinct edges between $a$ and $b$, a loop at $a$, and one edge between $b$ and $c$. There are four edges in total, and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取顶点 $a,b,c,d$，在 $a,b$ 之间放两条不同的边，再放一个 $a$ 上的自环和一条 $b,c$ 之间的边。共四条边，各点度数为
>
> $$
> \deg(a)=2+2=4,\qquad \deg(b)=2+1=3,\qquad
> \deg(c)=1,\qquad \deg(d)=0.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The two $a$–$b$ edges contribute twice at $a$ even though they lead to the same neighbour. The loop contributes twice for a different reason: both of its ends are at $a$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两条 $a$–$b$ 边虽然通向同一个邻居，仍给 $a$ 贡献两次；自环也贡献两次，但原因不同：它自身的两个端点都落在 $a$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Separating arrival from departure

For a directed edge $e:u\to v$, its **tail** is the starting vertex $u$ and its **head** is the ending vertex $v$. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把到达与离开分开*

对有向边 $e:u\to v$，起点 $u$ 是边尾（tail），终点 $v$ 是边头（head）。定义

$$
\deg^-(v)=\bigl|\{e\in E:\operatorname{head}(e)=v\}\bigr|,
\qquad
\deg^+(v)=\bigl|\{e\in E:\operatorname{tail}(e)=v\}\bigr|.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The vertical bars mean the number of edges in the indicated set. The minus superscript denotes indegree, and the plus superscript denotes outdegree; neither is a negative or positive sign on the value. These are counts of **edges**, not necessarily counts of distinct neighbouring vertices. A directed loop contributes one to each count at its vertex.

For example, include two edges $a\to b$, one $b\to a$, one loop $b\to b$, and one $a\to c$, with $d$ isolated. Reading the arrows gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

竖线表示所列集合中的边数。上标减号表示入度，上标加号表示出度，不是说数值为负或正。这里统计的是**边**，不一定是不同邻居的数量。有向自环给所在顶点的入度、出度各贡献一次。

例如取两条 $a\to b$、一条 $b\to a$、一个自环 $b\to b$ 和一条 $a\to c$，另有孤立点 $d$。逐个读箭头得到

| Vertex · 顶点 | $\deg^-$ | $\deg^+$ |
|---|---:|---:|
| $a$ | $1$ | $3$ |
| $b$ | $3$ | $2$ |
| $c$ | $1$ | $0$ |
| $d$ | $0$ | $0$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In particular, $c$ has no outgoing edge but is not isolated: an edge arrives there. A directed vertex is isolated exactly when **both** counts are zero. Counts always refer to the graph currently specified. For example, the indegrees maintained by [[Kahn 拓扑排序与环证书|Kahn's algorithm]] refer to its remaining graph after processed vertices have been removed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $c$ 没有出边，却不是孤立点，因为仍有边进入它。有向顶点孤立，当且仅当两种度数**都**为零。计数始终针对当前指定的图。例如，[[Kahn 拓扑排序与环证书|Kahn 拓扑排序]]维护的入度，是已经移除处理完的顶点之后，剩余图中的入度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What degree does and does not record
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界约定

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Degree is a local count, not a description of the entire graph. A vertex of degree $2$ may be the middle of a three-vertex path or a vertex of a triangle. More strongly, a six-vertex cycle and two disjoint triangles both have six degree-$2$ vertices, yet their numbers of [[无向图连通分量|connected components]] differ. Degree data alone do not determine connectivity, cycles or distances.

Edge weights also answer another question. A weight may represent cost or capacity; it does not replace the count of edges. An edge with weight zero still contributes to degree if it is present in $E$. A **weighted degree**, often called strength, sums weights with an explicitly chosen incidence convention. With loops, that convention must say how often a loop's weight is included. The unweighted degree here always counts an undirected loop twice.

If $E$ is empty, every existing vertex has degree zero. We also allow $V=\varnothing$ as an explicit extension of the course's nonempty-vertex convention; then $E=\varnothing$ and there is no vertex whose degree needs evaluating. How vertex counts combine over the whole graph is the separate [[无向图握手定理|handshaking lemma]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

度数是局部计数，不是整张图的描述。度数为 $2$ 的顶点既可以是三顶点路径的中点，也可以位于三角形中。更明显地，六顶点环和两个互不连接的三角形都有六个度数为 $2$ 的顶点，但[[无向图连通分量|连通分量]]数不同。只知道度数，不能确定连通性、环或距离。

边权回答的又是另一类问题。权重可以代表成本或容量，却不取代边数；只要一条零权边仍在 $E$ 中，它就仍参与度数计数。**加权度数**也称强度（strength），按明确选定的关联约定累加权重；允许自环时，必须说明自环权重计入几次。本文的普通度数始终把无向自环计两次。

若 $E$ 为空，所有已有顶点的度数都为零。本文还明确允许 $V=\varnothing$，作为课程“顶点集非空”约定的扩展；此时 $E=\varnothing$，也没有需要计算度数的顶点。把全图各顶点的计数合起来，会得到独立的[[无向图握手定理]]。

> [!question]- Incoming links or outgoing choices? · 收到的链接与离开的选择
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A webpage receives $50$ links and contains $3$ outgoing links. Its indegree is $50$ and its outdegree is $3$. These equal numbers of distinct referring or destination pages only if the model has at most one corresponding link per page pair. Merging the two counts loses the direction of the links.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个网页收到 $50$ 条链接，并提供 $3$ 条出链，则入度为 $50$、出度为 $3$。只有模型限制相应页面对至多一条链接时，这些数才分别等于不同引用页面数或目标页面数。把两种计数混成一个数，会丢掉链接的方向。
