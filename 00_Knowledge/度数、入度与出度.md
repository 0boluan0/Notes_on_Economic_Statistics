# 顶点的度数统计与它关联的边而有向图要分入度与出度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Vertex degree counts incident edges, while directed graphs separate in-degree from out-degree*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 在有限无向 simple graph 中，顶点 $v$ 的 degree $\deg(v)$ 是以 $v$ 为端点的边数，也等于 $v$ 的邻居数。在 digraph 中要分开计数：
> $$
> \deg^-(v)=\#\{u:(u,v)\in E\},\qquad
> \deg^+(v)=\#\{w:(v,w)\in E\}.
> $$
> 前者是 in-degree，后者是 out-degree。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a finite undirected simple graph, the degree $\deg(v)$ is the number of edges incident to $v$, equivalently its number of neighbors. A digraph needs two counts:
> $$
> \deg^-(v)=\#\{u:(u,v)\in E\},\qquad
> \deg^+(v)=\#\{w:(v,w)\in E\},
> $$
> the in-degree and out-degree respectively.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

degree 是局部关联数，不直接说明邻居之间怎样连接。两个顶点可以 degree 相同，却分别处在 triangle 和 tree 中；整张图也可以有相同 degree multiset 而不连通结构、cycle 或距离完全不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Degree is a local incidence count and does not describe how the neighbors connect to one another. Equal-degree vertices may lie in a triangle and a tree respectively. Entire graphs may share a degree multiset while differing in components, cycles, or distances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界约定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary conventions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 对 digraph，每条普通边 $u\to v$ 给 $u$ 的 out-degree 与 $v$ 的 in-degree 各贡献 $1$；self-loop 对同一点的两种 degree 各贡献 $1$。
- 对允许 loop 的无向 multigraph，handshaking 约定通常让一个 loop 对 degree 贡献 $2$，因为它在同一顶点有两个端点 incidence；必须先声明本课程采用的定义。
- weighted degree（strength）是 incident weights 的和，不是这里的边数 degree；两者名称接近但回答不同问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

**1.** A directed edge $u\to v$ contributes one to the out-degree of $u$ and one to the in-degree of $v$; a self-loop contributes one to each count at its vertex.<br>
**2.** In an undirected multigraph that permits loops, the usual handshaking convention counts a loop twice in the degree because it has two endpoint incidences at the same vertex. The adopted definition must be stated.<br>
**3.** Weighted degree, or strength, sums incident weights. It is not the edge-count degree defined here.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 一个网页有 50 个链接指向它，并从自身链接到 3 个网页。哪个数更直接反映“受多少页面引用”，哪个数更直接反映“它提供多少下一跳”？
>
> **答案：**in-degree $50$ 反映收到的链接，out-degree $3$ 反映可沿该页离开的链接。把二者合成一个 degree 会丢掉方向语义。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A webpage receives links from 50 pages and links out to three pages. Which count reflects incoming references, and which reflects the number of available next steps?
>
> **Answer:** The in-degree 50 counts incoming links; the out-degree three counts outgoing choices. Combining them into one undirected degree would discard direction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
