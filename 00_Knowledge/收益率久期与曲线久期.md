# 单券收益率久期与基准曲线久期衡量不同的利率扰动

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bond's yield duration and its benchmark-curve duration measure different interest-rate perturbations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[修正久期]] 对一只债券自身的 [[到期收益率]] $y$ 求价格导数，固定用于定义 YTM 的支付。[[有效久期]] 则改变选定基准曲线，按指定规则重新估价。即使支付完全固定，“所有即期率各加 1bp”也未必使该债券反解出的 YTM 恰好增加 1bp；一个标量报价不等于整条 [[利率期限结构]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[修正久期|Modified duration]] differentiates price with respect to one bond's own [[到期收益率|yield to maturity]], holding its specified payments fixed. [[有效久期|Effective duration]] changes a chosen benchmark curve and reprices under stated rules. Even with entirely fixed payments, adding 1bp to every spot rate need not increase the bond's implied YTM by exactly 1bp. A scalar yield quotation is not the entire [[利率期限结构|term structure]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个最小对照：一年后收 5，两年后收 105；对应年有效即期率为 4%、6%。同一价格 $P_0$ 可以由曲线贴现，也可反解单一年复利收益率 $y_0$：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a minimal comparison, receive 5 in one year and 105 in two years, with annual effective spot rates of 4% and 6%. The same price can be obtained from the spot curve or represented by one annually compounded yield $y_0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P_0=\frac5{1.04}+\frac{105}{1.06^2}=98.257319\ldots,
\qquad
P_0=\frac5{1+y_0}+\frac{105}{(1+y_0)^2},\quad y_0=0.059498549\ldots.
$$

但对应扰动函数分别是 $P_y(\delta)=5/(1+y_0+\delta)+105/(1+y_0+\delta)^2$ 与 $P_c(\delta)=5/(1.04+\delta)+105/(1.06+\delta)^2$。在 $\delta=0$ 求负相对导数，得到 $D_y\approx1.842354$、$D_c\approx1.841520$。初始价格相同，并没有迫使两条价格路径的斜率相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Their perturbation functions are $P_y(\delta)=5/(1+y_0+\delta)+105/(1+y_0+\delta)^2$ and $P_c(\delta)=5/(1.04+\delta)+105/(1.06+\delta)^2$. Negative relative derivatives at zero give $D_y\approx1.842354$ and $D_c\approx1.841520$. Matching the initial price does not force the slopes of these two price paths to match.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两者可以在模型条件对齐时一致，例如无期权、固定支付、平坦即期曲线恰等于 $y_0$，且两边使用同一复利坐标与相同加法冲击。更一般地，若固定支付下 $y(x)$ 是曲线重估价格对应的可微 YTM，则链式法则给出 $D_{\mathrm{curve}}=D_{\mathrm{mod}}y'(0)$；不能省略 $y'(0)$ 而默认它等于 1。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The measures can coincide when assumptions align: for example, fixed option-free payments, a flat spot curve equal to $y_0$, and the same compounding coordinate and additive shock. More generally, if $y(x)$ is the differentiable YTM implied by curve repricing of fixed payments, the chain rule gives $D_{\mathrm{curve}}=D_{\mathrm{mod}}y'(0)$. The factor $y'(0)$ cannot simply be assumed to equal 1.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用曲线久期还须说清移动的是零息率、平价率还是其他节点报价，以及插值、重建曲线与保持不变的利差。这也解释了为什么 [[关键久期加总条件|关键久期加总]] 即使成立，得到的仍是同一冲击口径的平行曲线久期，不能自动替换为单券 YTM 久期。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Curve duration additionally requires the shocked quote type—zero rates, par rates, or other node quotes—plus interpolation, curve rebuilding, and frozen spreads. Thus, even when [[关键久期加总条件|key-rate durations add up]], the result is parallel duration under that same shock convention, not automatically the bond's YTM duration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
