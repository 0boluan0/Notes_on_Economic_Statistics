# 当强迫模式也是特征根时特解试探式必须乘以足够次数的 t
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When the forcing mode is also a characteristic root, the particular-solution trial must be multiplied by a sufficient power of t*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 若强迫项含 $q_d(t)\lambda^t$，通常试同形函数；但若 $\lambda$ 同时是齐次特征根且重数为 $m$，原试探族会被齐次算子消掉或失去可解性，必须改试
> $$
> t^m\tilde q_d(t)\lambda^t.
> $$
> 乘多少次 $t$ 由重根次数决定，不是看到分母为零后随意补丁。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the forcing mode is also a characteristic root of multiplicity $m$, the ordinary trial overlaps the homogeneous space. Multiplying by $t^m$ moves the trial to an independent generalized mode.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小例子是
$$y_t-y_{t-1}=1.$$
齐次根为 $1$，所以常数试探 $C$ 代入后只得到 $0=1$。改试 $Ct$，便有 $Ct-C(t-1)=C=1$，得到特解 $y_t^p=t$；通解是 $y_t=t+A$。这正是单位根对常数输入的累积。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y_t-y_{t-1}=1$, a constant trial fails because the homogeneous root is one. The adjusted trial $Ct$ yields $C=1$, so the full solution is $t+A$. A unit root accumulates a constant input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\lambda$ 不是根，就不要多乘 $t$；那会扩大未知系数却不增加必要信息。若 $\lambda$ 是二重根，则只乘一次仍有重叠，要乘 $t^2$。在复数记号下，这条规则也覆盖与系统固有频率相同的正弦强迫。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No time factor is needed without overlap. A double root requires $t^2$, and the same rule covers sinusoidal forcing when its complex frequency coincides with a system mode.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 强迫项为 $2(0.8)^t$，而 $0.8$ 是特征方程的二重根。试探式应怎样改？
>
> **答案：** 从 $C(0.8)^t$ 改为 $Ct^2(0.8)^t$。
