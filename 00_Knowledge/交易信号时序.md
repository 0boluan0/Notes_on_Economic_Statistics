# 交易信号时序按信息可得、规则计算、订单提交、成交和持仓收益的先后衔接
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Trading signal timing connects information availability, rule calculation, order submission, execution and subsequent holding returns.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验一条交易规则的时序，要分别记录所需数据何时真正可得、计算何时完成、订单何时提交并被接纳、何时成交，以及由该次成交产生的持仓从何时开始承担收益。[[预测时点与信息集]]限定“当时能知道什么”；交易时序进一步限定“据此产生的订单何时能够改变持仓”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check when the required data became available, calculation finished, the order was submitted and accepted, execution occurred, and the resulting position began earning returns. [[预测时点与信息集|The forecast information set]] determines what could be known; trading timing additionally determines when an order based on it could change holdings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若规则需要 $t$ 日最终收盘价，信号只能在该价格可得且计算完成后产生。对只交易常规时段的日线示例，可以安排在下一合格交易时段提交，但不能倒填在 $t$ 日同一收盘成交。若希望参加收盘竞价，必须另外给出提交截止前已可得的输入；最终收盘价不能同时充当事先已知信号和执行承诺。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rule requiring day $t$'s final closing price can generate a signal only after that price is available and calculation is complete. A daily example restricted to regular hours may submit in the next eligible session, but cannot backfill execution at the same day-$t$ close. Participation in a closing auction instead requires inputs available before its submission deadline; the final closing price cannot serve as both advance information and a promised fill.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“下一开盘成交”仍须解释为某个执行模型。[[市价单]]按届时可得价格执行；[[限价单]]还受价格边界、数量和排队条件约束。跳空、停牌、价格保护或流动性不足可以改变价格、减少成交量或导致未成交。仅有一根 OHLC 日线也未必能确定盘中各阈值的先后与自己能取得的数量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Execute at the next open” still names an execution model. A [[市价单|market order]] faces available prices; a [[限价单|limit order]] also faces its price bound, quantity and queue conditions. Gaps, halts, protection rules or insufficient liquidity can change the price or prevent a complete fill. One OHLC bar need not identify the order of intraday threshold hits or the quantity available to a particular order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $t$ 日收盘为 100，收盘后产生买入信号；下一次允许提交时可得卖价为 105.20。若实际成交于 105.20、随后标记价为 106，则新增这一股的未扣费持仓损益为 0.80。收盘 100 到成交 105.20 之间的上涨不属于这笔新持仓；已有旧仓位则仍按其实际持有区间计价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a buy signal follows a close of 100 and the available ask at the next permitted submission is 105.20. If one share fills at 105.20 and is later marked at 106, its holding gain before fees is 0.80. The rise from 100 to the fill is not earned by that new position; an existing position is valued over its own actual holding interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
