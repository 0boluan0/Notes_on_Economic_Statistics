# Inference for linear combinations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*线性组合的推断*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A regression table lists coefficients one at a time, but a substantive question often combines several of them. We might want the difference between two group effects, or the group gap at ten years of experience. **Inference for a linear combination** first writes that target as a weighted sum, then calculates the uncertainty of the whole sum. Estimates from the same data can move together, so their separate standard errors are not enough.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归表逐行列出系数，但实际问题经常把几项系数组合起来，例如两个组效应之差，或十年经验处的组间差。**线性组合的推断**先把目标写成加权和，再计算整个和的不确定性。同一份数据估计出的系数可能一起波动，因此各行标准误本身不够。

> [!example] The same two estimates, two different uncertainties · 同两项估计，不同组合的不确定性
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose two estimates, measured in the same units, are $\hat\beta_1=5$ and $\hat\beta_2=3$. Their estimated [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] is
> $$\widehat V=\begin{bmatrix}4&3\\3&9\end{bmatrix}.$$
> The diagonal entries are estimated variances, giving [[标准误含义|standard errors]] $2$ and $3$. The off-diagonal $3$ estimates how the errors in the two estimates move together. Their estimated sum is $8$, with variance $4+9+2(3)=19$. Their estimated difference is $2$, with variance $4+9-2(3)=7$. The respective standard errors are $\sqrt{19}$ and $\sqrt7$.
>
> Positive covariance increases uncertainty in the sum but reduces uncertainty in the difference: shared upward or downward estimation errors partly cancel when we subtract. Treating the two estimates as unrelated would assign variance $13$ to both and miss this distinction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设两个单位相同的估计为 $\hat\beta_1=5$、$\hat\beta_2=3$，估计[[协方差矩阵]]如上。对角元是估计方差，给出[[标准误含义|标准误]] $2$、$3$；非对角的 $3$ 估计两项估计误差怎样共同波动。估计和为 $8$，方差 $4+9+2(3)=19$；估计差为 $2$，方差 $4+9-2(3)=7$，标准误分别为 $\sqrt{19}$、$\sqrt7$。
>
> 正协方差增加和的不确定性，却减少差的不确定性：相减时，共同向上或向下的估计误差部分抵消。若把两项估计当作互不相关，就会给两种组合都配上方差 $13$，漏掉这种区别。

## Write the target before its standard error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先写目标，再算它的标准误*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\beta=(\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_k)^\top$ contain $k\ge1$ fixed model coefficients, and let $\hat\beta$ be their [[估计对象三分|random estimator]]. A hat distinguishes the estimate from the parameter; $\top$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Choose a fixed weight vector $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_k)^\top$. The target and its estimate are
$$\theta=a^\top\beta=\sum_{j=1}^k a_j\beta_j,\qquad
\hat\theta=a^\top\hat\beta.$$
For the two-coefficient example, $a=(1,1)^\top$ selects the sum and $a=(1,-1)^\top$ the difference. Units belong to the weights as well: a coefficient measured per year needs a weight measured in years if the resulting target is a wage difference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $\beta=(\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_k)^\top$ 包含 $k\ge1$ 个固定模型系数，$\hat\beta$ 是相应的[[估计对象三分|随机估计量]]。帽子区分估计与参数，上标 $\top$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。选定固定权重向量 $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_k)^\top$ 后，目标是 $\theta=a^\top\beta$，估计是 $\hat\theta=a^\top\hat\beta$。

两系数例子里，$a=(1,1)^\top$ 取和，$a=(1,-1)^\top$ 取差。权重也带单位：若某系数的单位是每年工资变化，要组成工资差，相应权重就应带年数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume each coordinate of $\hat\beta$ has a finite second moment, meaning $E[\hat\beta_j^2]<\infty$ for every coordinate $j$, so its true covariance matrix $V=\operatorname{Cov}(\hat\beta)$ exists. The [[协方差仿射变换|fixed linear transformation rule]] gives the exact identity
$$\operatorname{Var}(\hat\theta)=a^\top Va
=\sum_j a_j^2V_{jj}+2\sum_{j<\ell}a_ja_\ell V_{j\ell}.$$
The first sum contains each coefficient's variance; the second contains every relevant pairwise covariance. This identity does not require independent estimates, normality, or an unbiased estimator. In inference conditional on a fixed or observed design $X$, use the corresponding conditional covariance throughout instead.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $\hat\beta$ 每个坐标的二阶矩有限，即对每个 $j$ 都有 $E[\hat\beta_j^2]<\infty$，使真实协方差矩阵 $V=\operatorname{Cov}(\hat\beta)$ 存在。[[协方差仿射变换|固定线性变换规则]]给出上面的精确恒等式。第一项求和包含各系数方差，第二项包含所有相关的两两协方差。这条恒等式不要求估计量彼此独立、正态或无偏。若在固定或已观察到的设计 $X$ 下做条件推断，则应始终使用相应的条件协方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Usually $V$ is unknown. Replacing it with one complete covariance estimate $\widehat V$ gives an **estimated** variance $a^\top\widehat Va$ and, when this is nonnegative, estimated standard error
$$\widehat{\operatorname{SE}}(\hat\theta)=\sqrt{a^\top\widehat Va}.$$
This substitution does not make the estimate equal to the unknown true variance, or guarantee that it is unbiased. Use the same [[标准误口径匹配|covariance method]] for every entry. Mixing robust diagonal entries with classical off-diagonal entries is not justified by either method. A zero variance estimate supplies no positive denominator for an ordinary studentized test; a negative result is not a real standard error and calls for checking the covariance estimate or calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通常 $V$ 未知。用一整张协方差估计 $\widehat V$ 替换，得到**估计**方差 $a^\top\widehat Va$；它非负时，开平方得到估计标准误。这种代入不表示估计值等于未知真实方差，也不自动保证无偏。所有元素须采用同一个[[标准误口径匹配|协方差口径]]；把稳健对角元与经典非对角元拼起来，不受任何一套原方法的保证。方差估计为零，不能给普通学生化检验提供正分母；若为负，更不能开出实数标准误，应检查估计或计算。

## A group gap at ten years of experience
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*十年经验处的组间差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a [[类别连续交互|group-by-experience interaction model]], let $D$ be $0$ for a reference group and $1$ for the group labelled `female`, and let $x$ be years of experience. The proposed wage mean is
$$m(x,D)=\beta_0+\beta_fD+\beta_xx+\beta_{fx}Dx.$$
The coefficients describe an intercept, a group offset, an experience slope and a slope difference. Subtracting the two mean lines at the same prespecified $x=10$ gives
$$\delta_{10}=\beta_f+10\beta_{fx},\qquad
\hat\delta_{10}=\hat\beta_f+10\hat\beta_{fx}.$$
With coefficients ordered as $(\beta_0,\beta_f,\beta_x,\beta_{fx})$, the weights are $a=(0,1,0,10)^\top$. Thus
$$\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(\hat\delta_{10})
=\widehat V_{ff}+100\widehat V_{fx,fx}+20\widehat V_{f,fx}.$$
The subscripts identify coefficient entries, not new observations. The factor $20$ comes from the two cross terms, each weighted by $10$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[类别连续交互|组别与经验的交互模型]]中，令 $D=0$ 表示基准组，$D=1$ 表示标记为 `female` 的组，$x$ 是经验年数。提出的工资均值如上，系数分别描述截距、组别偏移、经验斜率与斜率差。在事先指定的同一个 $x=10$ 处相减，得到组差 $\delta_{10}=\beta_f+10\beta_{fx}$，相应估计为 $\hat\delta_{10}=\hat\beta_f+10\hat\beta_{fx}$。

若系数顺序为 $(\beta_0,\beta_f,\beta_x,\beta_{fx})$，则权重是 $a=(0,1,0,10)^\top$，方差展开如上。下标标记系数位置，不是新增观测；$20$ 来自两个各带权重 $10$ 的交叉项。

> [!example] Compute the whole ten-year contrast · 完整计算十年对比
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $\hat\beta_f=2$, $\hat\beta_{fx}=0.3$, and their covariance submatrix is $\begin{bmatrix}1&-0.04\\-0.04&0.01\end{bmatrix}$. The estimated gap is $2+10(0.3)=5$. Its variance is $1+100(0.01)+20(-0.04)=1.2$, so its standard error is $\sqrt{1.2}\approx1.095$.
>
> The separate standard errors $1$ and $0.1$ do not answer this question. Ignoring their covariance would instead give variance $2$. Reading two separate p-values, or adding endpoints of their separate intervals, does not construct the calibrated interval for the ten-year gap. This calculation concerns the specified mean model; a causal interpretation and support for comparison at ten years require separate arguments.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 例如 $\hat\beta_f=2$、$\hat\beta_{fx}=0.3$，这两项的协方差子矩阵为 $\begin{bmatrix}1&-0.04\\-0.04&0.01\end{bmatrix}$。估计组差是 $5$，估计方差为 $1+100(0.01)+20(-0.04)=1.2$，标准误约为 $1.095$。
>
> 单独的标准误 $1$、$0.1$ 没有回答组合问题；忽略协方差会得到方差 $2$。读取两个单独的 p 值，或把两个单独区间的端点相加，都没有构造十年组差的匹配区间。这是在指定均值模型中的计算；因果解释，以及十年处是否有比较支持，仍需各自论证。

## Several combinations share one transformed matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*多个组合共用一张变换后的矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Put $q\ge1$ fixed weight rows into a $q\times k$ matrix $R$. The vector $R\beta$ contains the $q$ targets, and their estimated covariance is
$$\widehat{\operatorname{Cov}}(R\hat\beta)=R\widehat V R^\top.$$
For the opening example, $R=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}$ reports sum and difference together. Their estimated covariance matrix is $\begin{bmatrix}19&-5\\-5&7\end{bmatrix}$. The off-diagonal $-5$ records how those two new estimates move together; it is not discarded merely because each has its own standard error.

Subtracting a fixed proposed vector $r$ leaves the covariance unchanged, so the same matrix describes $R\hat\beta-r$ in a test of $R\beta=r$. Covariance propagation remains valid with redundant rows of $R$. An ordinary [[Wald联合检验|joint Wald test]] additionally needs independent restrictions and positive-definite restriction covariance; the formula above alone does not establish either condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $q\ge1$ 行固定权重堆成 $q\times k$ 矩阵 $R$，$R\beta$ 就包含这 $q$ 个目标，估计协方差是 $R\widehat V R^\top$。开头例子取 $R=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}$，同时报告和与差，得到矩阵 $\begin{bmatrix}19&-5\\-5&7\end{bmatrix}$。非对角的 $-5$ 描述两个新估计量怎样共同波动，不能因为各自已有标准误就丢掉。

减去固定的假设向量 $r$ 不改变协方差，所以检验 $R\beta=r$ 时，$R\hat\beta-r$ 也使用这张矩阵。即使 $R$ 含冗余行，协方差传播仍有效；普通[[Wald联合检验]]则还需要独立限制和正定的限制协方差，上式本身没有证明这些条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A standard error measures a scale; a [[回归t检验|single-contrast test]] or [[置信区间覆盖率|confidence interval]] also needs a justified reference distribution and critical value. Fix $a$ or $R$ with the scientific question, or justify inference conditional on a design that determines them. Choosing weights from the same response data to maximise significance makes them random and changes the procedure. Treating the selected weights as fixed ignores [[选择后推断|selection uncertainty]]; the fixed-matrix variance identity cannot simply be applied to such random weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准误描述尺度；[[回归t检验|单个组合的检验]]或[[置信区间覆盖率|置信区间]]还需要有依据的参考分布和临界值。应按问题固定 $a$ 或 $R$，或者论证条件于确定这些权重的设计时如何推断。若用同一响应数据挑选最显著的权重，权重就变成随机的，程序也改变了；把选出的权重继续当作固定量，会遗漏[[选择后推断|选择不确定性]]，不能直接沿用固定矩阵的方差恒等式。

> [!question]- Two standard errors do not supply a covariance · 两个标准误不提供协方差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the two standard errors are $2$ and $3$, must the sum's standard error be $\sqrt{13}$? No. Its estimated variance is $13+2\widehat V_{12}$, so $\sqrt{13}$ follows only when the estimated covariance is zero. The opening example gives $\sqrt{19}$ instead.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若两个标准误是 $2$、$3$，和的标准误一定是 $\sqrt{13}$ 吗？不一定。其估计方差是 $13+2\widehat V_{12}$；只有估计协方差为零，才得到 $\sqrt{13}$。开头例子得到的是 $\sqrt{19}$。

## 来源与核验

- [Geyer, Stat 5101 notes, Theorem 5.3 and Corollary 5.4, PDF pp.141–142](https://www.stat.umn.edu/geyer/old/5102/n.pdf#page=141): exact covariance propagation for fixed rectangular matrices and fixed scalar combinations. The finite-second-moment condition is stated explicitly here.
- [Hansen, *Econometrics* (2010), §5.7, PDF pp.84–86](https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~xshi28/Econometrics_Hansen.pdf#page=84): functions of regression parameters, linear restrictions and their estimated covariance. Hansen's asymptotic matrix is on the $\sqrt n$ scale; $\widehat V$ in this card estimates the covariance of $\hat\beta$ itself.
- [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/03_多元线性回归.md#3.2. 多参数检验：F 检验|Local linear restrictions]], [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/05_多元回归模型的矩阵表达.md#4.2. Wald 检验|matrix-form inference]], and [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/10_虚拟变量.md#1.3 类别与连续变量交互：把一条线放宽为两条线|the permanent interaction section]] preserve course scope. The sum/difference and ten-year numerical examples are direct calculations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Geyer 定理 5.3、推论 5.4 核对固定长方形矩阵及固定标量组合的精确协方差传播；本卡明确有限二阶矩前提。
- Hansen 第 5.7 节核对参数函数、线性限制及其估计协方差。来源的渐近矩阵采用 $\sqrt n$ 尺度；本卡 $\widehat V$ 估计 $\hat\beta$ 本身的协方差。
