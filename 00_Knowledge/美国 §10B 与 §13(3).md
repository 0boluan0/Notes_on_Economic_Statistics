# 美国 §10B 贴现窗口与 §13(3) 紧急便利有不同对象和授权边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In the United States, section 10B discount-window lending and section 13(3) emergency facilities have different counterparties and legal boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子法律边界
> 截至 2026-08-29，Federal Reserve Act §10B(a) 明文授权 Reserve Banks 向 member banks 提供有抵押 advances；Regulation A 则依据多项 Federal Reserve Act 放贷授权，为 depository institutions 实施 primary、secondary 和 seasonal credit。§10B(b) 另列资本不足机构限制和“没有必须放贷的义务”。§13(3) 只在 unusual and exigent circumstances 下授权具有 broad-based eligibility 的项目或便利，并附财政部长事先批准、抵押与偿付能力等限制。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> As checked on 2026-08-29, Federal Reserve Act section 10B(a) expressly authorises secured advances to member banks. Regulation A implements primary, secondary, and seasonal credit for depository institutions under several Federal Reserve Act lending authorities. Section 10B(b) separately imposes limits for undercapitalised institutions and states that a Reserve Bank has no obligation to lend. Section 13(3) instead authorises broad-based programmes or facilities only in unusual and exigent circumstances, subject to prior Treasury Secretary approval, collateral, and solvency restrictions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

13(3) 项目必须面向可识别的金融市场或部门；Regulation A 还规定，若少于五个主体具有资格，或项目旨在帮助一个或多个特定公司逃避破产或处置，就不构成 broad-based eligibility。放贷前还须取得参与者无法从其他银行取得 adequate credit accommodations 的证据，并取得其非 insolvent 的证据；抵押品须被赋予 lendable value。它因此不是“任何非银行缺钱都可找 Fed”的一般窗口，实际参与者也不会仅因项目存在就获得贷款权利。

## 易错边界

- 2008 年前后的具体做法不能不经核验地投射到 Dodd–Frank 修法后的制度。
- 不能把 §10B(a) 的 member bank 对象、§10B(b) 的 depository-institution 限制条款与 Regulation A 的实际窗口范围压成一句“§10B 覆盖所有存款机构”。
- “有资格申请”不等于 Reserve Bank 必须批准或续作。
- 13(3) 的“至少五个”是 Regulation A 对 eligible persons/entities 的最低广泛性条件，不表示必须已有五个实际借款人。

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么一家特定非银行公司的流动性困难不能单凭 13(3) 得到定制救助？
>
> **答案：** 现行条文要求广泛资格项目，并排除为特定单一公司避免破产而设的便利，还需财政部长批准等条件。

- [Federal Reserve Board, Federal Reserve Act §13(3)](https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/section13.htm)：核对 unusual and exigent、broad-based、Treasury approval、solvency 与报告边界。
- [Federal Reserve Board, Regulation A §201.3](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/regulations/section-2013-extensions-of-credit-generally.htm) 与 [§201.4](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/regulations/section-2014-availability-and-terms-of-credit.htm)：分别核对一般放贷与无贷款权利边界，以及 primary、secondary、seasonal 和 emergency credit 的对象与实施规则。
