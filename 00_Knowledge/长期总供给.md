# 长期总供给在标准AD-AS模型中把充分调整后的产出置于潜在产出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Long-run aggregate supply places fully adjusted output at potential in the standard AD-AS model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长期总供给 LRAS（课堂写 LAS）是工资、价格和预期充分调整后的一般价格水平与实际产出关系。标准模型将其画为位于[[潜在产出]] $Y^*$ 的垂线：在资源、技术和制度不变时，单纯改变名义价格水平不会增加可持续实际生产能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
LRAS, called LAS in the course, relates the price level to output after wages, prices, and expectations adjust. The standard model draws it vertically at [[潜在产出|potential output]] $Y^*$: changing nominal prices alone does not increase sustainable capacity when resources, technology, and institutions are unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

垂直不等于位置永远固定。资本、劳动供给、技术或制度变化可移动 LRAS；持续冲击是否伤及潜能，要单独建模。也不能把“最终回到潜能”的模型闭合条件解释为现实必在固定几年内自动复原。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A vertical curve need not stay in the same position. Changes in capital, labour supply, technology, or institutions can shift LRAS. Whether a persistent shock damages potential requires separate modeling, and long-run closure does not guarantee recovery within a fixed number of years.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课堂中“需求增加只提高长期价格”的结论，以原 LRAS 不变为条件；若同一政策也改变生产能力，就不能只移动 AD，详见[[AD-AS长期调整]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The classroom conclusion that higher demand raises only long-run prices holds conditional on unchanged LRAS. A policy that also changes capacity requires more than an AD shift; see [[AD-AS长期调整|long-run AD-AS adjustment]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
