# 在有限标准信号博弈的 weak-PBE 口径下，每个 mixed separating PBE 都可逐类型选取一个支持内信号纯化为 pure separating PBE
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a finite standard signaling game under weak PBE, every mixed separating PBE can be purified into a pure separating PBE by selecting one supported signal for each type.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 mixed separating PBE 中，不同发送者类型的信号支持两两不交。对每个类型 $\theta$，任选

$$
s_\theta\in\operatorname{supp}\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta),
$$

再让 $\theta$ 改为以概率 1 发送 $s_\theta$。支持互不相交保证不同类型选出的信号仍然不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a mixed separating PBE, the signal supports of distinct sender types are pairwise disjoint. For each type $\theta$, choose one $s_\theta\in\operatorname{supp}\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta)$ and let that type send $s_\theta$ with probability one. Disjoint supports ensure that different types still select different signals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么接收者仍然最优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the receiver remains optimal*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个保留信号仍只可能来自原来的类型，因此其退化 posterior 与接收者反应不变。对类型 $\theta$ 原支持中其余现已离轨的信号，指定 belief $\delta_\theta$ 并沿用原反应；该反应原本就在同一退化 posterior 下最优。原本就在路径外的信号继续沿用原 belief 与反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every retained signal can still come only from its original type, so its degenerate posterior and receiver response are unchanged. At any other signal formerly in type $\theta$'s support and now off path, assign belief $\delta_\theta$ and retain the original response, which was already optimal under that same degenerate posterior. Signals that were already off path keep their original beliefs and responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么不会出现新的发送者偏离
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why no new sender deviation appears*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

类型 $\theta$ 原本在自己支持内的每个信号上都取得相同的最大收益，也已经把其他类型使用的信号和全部支持外信号纳入偏离比较。纯化只从其原最优支持中保留 $s_\theta$，而所有信号后的接收者反应保持不变。因此没有类型获得新的有利偏离，所得 assessment 是 pure separating weak PBE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Type $\theta$ originally obtained the same maximal payoff from every signal in its own support and had already compared signals used by other types and all signals outside its support. Purification keeps one signal $s_\theta$ from that original optimal support while leaving the receiver's response after every signal unchanged. No type therefore acquires a profitable deviation, and the resulting assessment is a pure separating weak PBE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个构造依赖有限信号集合、类型支持两两不交，以及 weak PBE 的离轨 belief 口径。若多个类型共享任何正概率信号，候选就不是 mixed separating，本构造不能直接使用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The construction relies on a finite signal set, pairwise disjoint type supports, and the weak-PBE treatment of off-path beliefs. If any positive-probability signal is shared by more than one type, the candidate is not mixed separating and this construction does not apply directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
