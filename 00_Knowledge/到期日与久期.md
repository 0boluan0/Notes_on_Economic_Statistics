# 相同到期期限不保证债券具有相同久期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equal time to maturity does not guarantee equal bond duration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到期期限只给最后一笔支付的位置；[[麦考利久期]]还取决于此前的现金流及其贴现权重。因此不能只看“都是三年债券”，就认为利率敏感度相同。比较久期时还要统一收益率口径、付息安排和估值时点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maturity locates only the final payment. [[麦考利久期|Macaulay duration]] also depends on earlier cash flows and their discount weights, so two three-year bonds need not have equal interest-rate sensitivity. Compare durations using consistent yield conventions, payment schedules and valuation dates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小对照：都按面值 $100$ 偿还、剩余三年、年复利收益率 $5\%$，在一个完整年度开始时估值。零息债券的全部权重在第三年，$D_M=3$；年息票 $4$ 的 $D_M\approx2.884380$；年息票 $8$ 的 $D_M\approx2.792046$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A minimal comparison uses par repayment of 100, three years remaining and a 5% annually compounded yield, valued at the start of a full annual period. The zero-coupon bond has $D_M=3$; annual coupons of 4 give about 2.884380, and coupons of 8 give about 2.792046.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个票息比较的条件很重要。保持面值 $F>0$、共同收益率和规则付息日 $t_1<\cdots<t_N=T$ 不变，每期息票同为 $c\ge0$。令 $d_k>0$ 为固定的贴现因子，$A=\sum_kd_k$、$B=\sum_kt_kd_k$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coupon comparison needs its conditions. Hold positive face value $F$, the common yield and regular payment dates $t_1<\cdots<t_N=T$ fixed, with the same coupon amount $c\ge0$ at each date. For fixed positive discount factors $d_k$, let $A=\sum_kd_k$ and $B=\sum_kt_kd_k$. Then:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$D_M(c)=\frac{cB+TFd_N}{cA+Fd_N},\qquad \frac{dD_M}{dc}=\frac{Fd_N(B-TA)}{(cA+Fd_N)^2}\le0.$$

若至少有一个到期前付息日，$B<TA$，不等式严格：提高票息让较早付款获得更多现值权重。只有一笔最终支付时，无论这笔款被叫作本金还是利息，平均支付时间都仍是 $T$。这段推导不允许同时改变收益率、期权或摊还安排；同样，“期限更长”也不能脱离这些条件自动推出久期更大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With at least one earlier coupon date, $B<TA$, so the inequality is strict: higher coupons give earlier payments more present-value weight. If there is only one final payment, the average time remains $T$ regardless of whether part is called interest. This argument does not allow simultaneous changes in yield, options or amortization. Nor does longer maturity alone establish higher duration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
