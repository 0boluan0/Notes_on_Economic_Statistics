# Real skew-symmetric matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*实反对称矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **real skew-symmetric matrix** pairs each off-diagonal entry with its negative in the reflected position. For example, the $2$ above the main diagonal below is paired with $-2$ below it:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**实反对称矩阵**把每个非对角元与镜像位置上的相反数配对。例如，下面主对角线上方的 $2$，对应下方的 $-2$：

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}0&2\\-2&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A^T=\begin{pmatrix}0&-2\\2&0\end{pmatrix}=-A.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[矩阵转置|transpose]] swaps rows and columns. Here swapping the two off-diagonal positions has the same effect as negating every entry. That is the defining relation: a real square matrix $A$ is **skew-symmetric** when
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[矩阵转置]]交换行列。在这里，交换两个非对角位置，效果恰好与把全部元素变号相同。这就是定义关系：实方阵 $A$ 称为**反对称矩阵**，当且仅当

$$A^T=-A\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a_{ji}=-a_{ij}\text{ for every }i,j.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The indices $i,j$ specify row and column positions. A square shape is needed because transposition exchanges the numbers of rows and columns. All entries in this card are real, and $A^T$ is the ordinary transpose.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指标 $i,j$ 指明行列位置。必须是方阵，因为转置会交换行数与列数。本卡的元素均为实数，$A^T$ 使用普通转置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why every diagonal entry is zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么对角元全为零？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A diagonal position is unchanged by transposition. At that position, the definition demands that a real number equal its own negative:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对角位置转置后仍在原处。定义因此要求该处的实数等于自己的相反数：

$$a_{ii}=-a_{ii}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad 2a_{ii}=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a_{ii}=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently every real $2\times2$ skew-symmetric matrix has the form $\begin{pmatrix}0&t\\-t&0\end{pmatrix}$ with one freely chosen real number $t$. In larger matrices the same rule applies to every reflected pair. The number of independent choices is explained in [[反对称矩阵的自由参数|the parameter count for skew-symmetric matrices]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以每个实 $2\times2$ 反对称矩阵都形如 $\begin{pmatrix}0&t\\-t&0\end{pmatrix}$，只需自由选择一个实数 $t$。更大的矩阵对每一对镜像位置都使用同一规则。独立选择的数量见[[反对称矩阵的自由参数]]。

> [!warning] Skew-symmetric is not the same as nonsymmetric · 反对称不等于“不对称”
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The matrix $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ is not [[实对称矩阵|symmetric]], because its reflected entries $1$ and $0$ differ. It is not skew-symmetric either: those entries are not negatives of each other, and its first diagonal entry is nonzero. Skew-symmetry requires the specific equality $B^T=-B$; failing $B^T=B$ is not enough. The zero matrix satisfies both equalities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ 不满足[[实对称矩阵|对称]]，因为镜像位置的 $1$ 与 $0$ 不同；它也不反对称，因为两者不是相反数，而且第一个对角元不为零。反对称要求特定等式 $B^T=-B$，仅仅不满足 $B^T=B$ 还不够。零矩阵则同时满足两种等式。
