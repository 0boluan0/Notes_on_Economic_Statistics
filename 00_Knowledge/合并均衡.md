# 合并均衡是满足 PBE 且所有发送者类型采用同一信号策略的信号博弈均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A pooling equilibrium is a signaling-game equilibrium that satisfies PBE and in which all sender types use the same signaling strategy.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 定义
> “合并”描述[[信号博弈]]中不同类型在路径上表现相同，因而观察信号不能区分类型。它只是候选策略的分类；只有路径内外 beliefs、接收者最佳反应与每种类型的不偏离条件全部满足，才是[[完美贝叶斯均衡|pooling PBE]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Definition*
>
> “Pooling” means that different types behave identically on the path in a [[信号博弈|signaling game]], so the observed signal cannot distinguish their types. It is only a classification of a candidate strategy. The outcome is a [[完美贝叶斯均衡|pooling PBE]] only when on-path and off-path beliefs, receiver best responses, and the no-deviation conditions for every type all hold.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 纯策略与混合策略下的定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Definition under pure and mixed strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

MIT Lecture 3 的纯策略基准是：存在 $s^P\in S$，使所有 $\theta\in\Theta$ 都满足
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The pure-strategy benchmark in MIT Lecture 3 is that there exists $s^P\in S$ such that every $\theta\in\Theta$ satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sigma_1(s^P\mid\theta)=1.
$$

在混合策略下采用一项清楚且互斥的扩展：存在同一分布 $q\in\Delta(S)$，使
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under mixed strategies, use the following clear and mutually exclusive extension: there exists a common distribution $q\in\Delta(S)$ such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta)=q(\cdot)
\qquad\text{对所有 }\theta.
$$

纯 pooling 是 $q$ 退化在 $s^P$ 的特例。若不同类型虽然共享某些信号、但使用的整个分布不相同，则不属于完全 pooling；三类混合信号策略的统一口径见[[混合信号均衡分类]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pure pooling is the special case in which $q$ is degenerate at $s^P$. If different types share some signals but use different overall distributions, the strategy is not fully pooling. See [[混合信号均衡分类|classification of mixed signaling equilibria]] for the unified treatment of the three classes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 路径上 posterior 不改变先验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The on-path posterior does not change the prior.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设先验为 $\mu_0(\theta)$。对任一 $q(s)>0$ 的路径上信号，[[路径上一致性]]给出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the prior be $\mu_0(\theta)$. For any on-path signal with $q(s)>0$, [[路径上一致性|on-path consistency]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p(\theta\mid s)
=\frac{\mu_0(\theta)q(s)}
{\sum_{\theta'}\mu_0(\theta')q(s)}
=\mu_0(\theta).
$$

因此，合并策略下信号在路径上不提供类型信息。这里的结论依赖“各类型 likelihood 完全相同”，不是依赖接收者主观上选择忽略信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus, under a pooling strategy, an on-path signal provides no information about type. The conclusion relies on the likelihood being identical across types, not on the receiver subjectively choosing to ignore information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 成为 PBE 仍要过三类检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three checks are still required for PBE.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **接收者路径上最优。** 给定 posterior 等于先验，接收者在所有 $q(s)>0$ 的信号后必须作最佳反应；
2. **路径外可支撑。** 对 $q(s)=0$ 的信号，要明确[[路径外信念]]并通过[[路径外反应可支持性]]检查；
3. **逐类型不偏离。** 每个发送者类型都必须宁愿遵循 $q$，而不是改用另一个信号以诱导不同的接收者反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;

**1. Receiver optimality on path.** Given that the posterior equals the prior, the receiver must best respond after every signal with $q(s)>0$;<br>
**2. Off-path supportability.** For every signal with $q(s)=0$, specify an [[路径外信念|off-path belief]] and apply the [[路径外反应可支持性|off-path response supportability]] test;<br>
**3. No deviation by any type.** Every sender type must prefer following $q$ to switching to another signal in order to induce a different receiver response.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只写“大家选一样”没有验证均衡。特别是路径外反应会决定某个类型是否有动力打破 pooling；同一条 pooling 路径可能由不同路径外 beliefs 支撑多个 PBE，也可能没有任何 belief 能支撑。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Writing only that “everyone chooses the same signal” does not verify equilibrium. In particular, off-path responses determine whether a type wants to break the pooling pattern. The same pooling path may be supported by different off-path beliefs in several PBEs, or no belief may support it at all.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] Beer–Quiche 的合并候选
> weak 与 strong 都选 Beer。看到 Beer 后，接收者的 posterior 等于类型先验；看到 Quiche 后的 belief 是路径外的。必须用该 belief 推出 Fight/Don't Fight，再分别检查 weak 和 strong 是否都不愿改选 Quiche。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *A pooling candidate in Beer–Quiche*
>
> The weak and strong types both choose Beer. After Beer, the receiver's posterior equals the prior over types; the belief after Quiche is off path. Use that belief to derive the best response between Fight and Don't Fight, then check separately that neither the weak nor the strong type wants to switch to Quiche.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 所有类型都使用同一混合分布 $q$，其中 Beer 与 Quiche 都有正概率。看到 Beer 后 posterior 是否一般会偏向更爱 Beer 的类型？
>
> **答案：** 不会。各类型对 Beer 的 likelihood 都是同一个 $q(Beer)$，Bayes 公式中它会抵消，所以 posterior 等于先验。若类型使用不同分布，就不是 pooling。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Self-check*
>
> Every type uses the same mixed distribution $q$, with both Beer and Quiche assigned positive probability. After Beer, will the posterior generally favor the type that likes Beer more?
>
> **Answer:** No. Every type has the same likelihood $q(Beer)$ for Beer, so it cancels in Bayes' formula and the posterior equals the prior. If the types use different distributions, the strategy is not pooling.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
