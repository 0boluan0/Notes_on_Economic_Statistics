# Distributive laws for matrix multiplication
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵乘法分配律*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **distributive laws** let us multiply a sum of matrices one term at a time. To calculate $A(B+C)$, we can first add $B$ and $C$, then multiply by $A$; or calculate $AB$ and $AC$ separately, then add their results. Matrix addition means adding corresponding entries of matrices with the same dimensions. The law changes how we organize the calculation, while keeping every factor on its original side.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**分配律**允许把矩阵的和逐项相乘。计算 $A(B+C)$ 时，可以先把 $B,C$ 相加，再乘 $A$；也可以分别算出 $AB,AC$，最后把结果相加。矩阵相加是把尺寸相同的矩阵对应位置的元素相加。分配律改变计算的组织方式，但每个因子原来在哪一侧，展开后仍留在哪一侧。

> [!example] Two routes to the same product · 两条计算路径
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use the [[矩阵乘法|row-by-column multiplication rule]] with
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对下面的矩阵使用[[矩阵乘法|行乘列规则]]：
>
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
> B=\begin{pmatrix}3&0\\4&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
> C=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Adding first gives $B+C=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\4&3\end{pmatrix}$, hence $A(B+C)=\begin{pmatrix}10&7\\4&3\end{pmatrix}$. Multiplying separately gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 先相加得到 $B+C=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\4&3\end{pmatrix}$，再乘得 $A(B+C)=\begin{pmatrix}10&7\\4&3\end{pmatrix}$。分别相乘则得到
>
> $$AB=\begin{pmatrix}11&2\\4&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> AC=\begin{pmatrix}-1&5\\0&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> AB+AC=\begin{pmatrix}10&7\\4&3\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For instance, the upper-left entry is $1(3-1)+2(4+0)=10$ on the first route, and $(1\cdot3+2\cdot4)+(1\cdot(-1)+2\cdot0)=11-1=10$ on the second. Ordinary scalar distribution explains why the entries agree.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 例如左上角：第一条路径算 $1(3-1)+2(4+0)=10$；第二条路径算 $(1\cdot3+2\cdot4)+(1\cdot(-1)+2\cdot0)=11-1=10$。两者相等的原因就是普通数的分配律。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Match the dimensions before expanding
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*展开前先匹配尺寸*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $m,n,p$ be positive integers. If $A$ has $m$ rows and $n$ columns, and $B,C$ both have $n$ rows and $p$ columns, then $B+C$ is defined and every resulting product below has size $m\times p$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $m,n,p$ 为正整数。若 $A$ 有 $m$ 行、$n$ 列，而 $B,C$ 都有 $n$ 行、$p$ 列，那么 $B+C$ 可以相加，下面各个乘积的尺寸都为 $m\times p$：

$$A(B+C)=AB+AC.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is also a right-side version. If $B,C$ both have size $m\times n$ and $A$ has size $n\times p$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也有共同因子留在右侧的版本。若 $B,C$ 都为 $m\times n$，$A$ 为 $n\times p$，则

$$(B+C)A=BA+CA.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both statements work for real or complex entries. They also run backward: $AB+AC$ can be factored as $A(B+C)$, whereas $BA+CA$ can be factored as $(B+C)A$. The factor stays on the side it occupies in both terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两条规律对实数和复数元素都成立，也可以倒过来提取公因子：$AB+AC=A(B+C)$，而 $BA+CA=(B+C)A$。共同因子在哪一侧，提取以后就留在哪一侧。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why every entry agrees
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么所有元素都一致*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The subscripts $i,j$ select an output row and column; $k$ runs through the shared inner dimension. Write $a_{ik},b_{kj},c_{kj}$ for the corresponding entries. Distributing inside their finite sum gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下标 $i,j$ 选取结果的行和列，$k$ 遍历共同的内侧维度。相应元素记为 $a_{ik},b_{kj},c_{kj}$。在有限求和中展开，就有

$$\begin{aligned}
\bigl(A(B+C)\bigr)_{ij}
&=\sum_{k=1}^n a_{ik}(b_{kj}+c_{kj})\\
&=\sum_{k=1}^n a_{ik}b_{kj}+\sum_{k=1}^n a_{ik}c_{kj}
=(AB+AC)_{ij}.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same calculation for $(B+C)A$ expands $(b_{ik}+c_{ik})a_{kj}$. Since each position agrees, the matrices agree. The numerical example illustrates the law; this entrywise argument proves it for all compatible matrices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $(B+C)A$ 做相同计算，只需展开 $(b_{ik}+c_{ik})a_{kj}$。每个位置的值相同，整个矩阵便相同。数值例子展示规律，逐元素论证才证明它适用于所有尺寸相容的矩阵。

> [!warning] Distribution does not swap factors · 分配不会交换因子
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the example, $BA+CA=(B+C)A=\begin{pmatrix}2&5\\4&11\end{pmatrix}$, different from $A(B+C)$. Thus even when both orders are defined, moving $A$ from the left to the right changes the result in general. With rectangular matrices, the swapped products may not even be defined.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上例中 $BA+CA=(B+C)A=\begin{pmatrix}2&5\\4&11\end{pmatrix}$，与 $A(B+C)$ 不同。即使两个次序都能相乘，把 $A$ 从左边挪到右边通常也会改变结果；对于矩形矩阵，交换后甚至可能无法相乘。
