# Design-matrix condition number
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*设计矩阵条件数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a regression, two coefficient changes can almost cancel in the predictions: raise one coefficient and lower another, and the fitted values barely move. The **condition number of the design matrix** compares the strongest and weakest coefficient directions, using a specified measure of length. It describes how unevenly the design distinguishes directions. To interpret its size, keep the column units and scaling explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在回归中，两项系数变化可能几乎相互抵消：提高一个系数、降低另一个，拟合值却几乎不动。**设计矩阵条件数**在指定长度度量下，比较最强与最弱的系数方向，描述设计区分不同方向的能力有多不均衡。解释数值时，必须说明列的单位和缩放方式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Measure the strongest and weakest stretch
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*比较最强与最弱伸缩*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X$ be a real $n\times p$ [[设计矩阵|design matrix]]: rows are observations and columns are encoded regression terms, including an intercept column if the model uses one. Assume $n\ge p\ge1$ and [[线性无关|linearly independent columns]], which give [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|unique OLS coefficients]]. A coefficient vector $b$ gives fitted values $Xb$. Changing it by $v$ changes those values by $Xv$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X$ 为实数 $n\times p$ [[设计矩阵]]：行是观测，列是编码后的回归项；模型有截距时，也把截距列计入。假定 $n\ge p\ge1$，各列[[线性无关]]，从而 [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|OLS 系数唯一]]。系数向量 $b$ 对应拟合值 $Xb$，把系数改变 $v$，拟合值就改变 $Xv$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the [[欧氏范数|Euclidean norm]] $\|v\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_jv_j^2}$ to measure vector length. Among coefficient changes of length one, the largest and smallest output lengths are the extreme [[奇异值|singular values]] $\sigma_1$ and $\sigma_p$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用[[欧氏范数]] $\|v\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_jv_j^2}$ 测量向量长度。在长度为一的系数变化中，输出长度的最大值、最小值，就是两个极端[[奇异值]] $\sigma_1,\sigma_p$：

$$\sigma_1=\max_{\|v\|_2=1}\|Xv\|_2,\qquad
\sigma_p=\min_{\|v\|_2=1}\|Xv\|_2>0.$$

> [!important] The matrix 2-norm condition number · 矩阵二范数条件数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With singular values ordered $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_p>0$,
>
> $$\kappa_2(X)=\frac{\sigma_1}{\sigma_p}\ge1.$$
>
> The subscript $2$ specifies Euclidean lengths and the associated [[谱范数|matrix spectral norm]]. It does not refer to two predictors. A ratio of $100$ means that equal-length coefficient changes in the strongest and weakest directions produce output changes whose lengths differ by a factor of $100$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 将奇异值排列为 $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_p>0$，则 $\kappa_2(X)=\sigma_1/\sigma_p\ge1$。下标 $2$ 指定欧氏长度及相应[[谱范数|矩阵谱范数]]，并非表示有两个预测变量。比值为 $100$，表示最强和最弱方向上同样长的系数变化，使输出长度相差 $100$ 倍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[奇异值分解|singular value decomposition]] supplies unit right and left singular vectors $v_j,u_j$ satisfying $Xv_j=\sigma_j u_j$. Thus $v_p$ identifies the weakest coefficient combination, while $u_p$ is its direction in observation space. The ratio alone does not identify those variables. If columns are dependent, a nonzero change can leave $Xb$ unchanged; for this coefficient-identification diagnostic we report an infinite condition number. A wide design with $p>n$ likewise cannot identify all coefficients, even if a routine reports a finite ratio of its listed nonzero singular values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[奇异值分解]]给出单位右、左奇异向量 $v_j,u_j$，满足 $Xv_j=\sigma_j u_j$。因此 $v_p$ 指出最弱的系数组合，$u_p$ 则是它在观测空间中的方向；比值本身并没有指出具体变量。若各列线性相关，某个非零系数变化可使 $Xb$ 完全不变；对这个系数识别诊断，约定报告无穷条件数。当 $p>n$ 时，也无法识别所有系数，即使某个程序对其列出的非零奇异值报告有限比值。

> [!example] Two nearly indistinguishable columns · 两列几乎难以区分
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider the two-feature design
>
> $$X=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0.01&-0.01\end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> Writing a column with superscript $T$ uses [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. The unit directions $v_+=(1,1)^T/\sqrt2$ and $v_-=(1,-1)^T/\sqrt2$ produce $(\sqrt2,0)^T$ and $(0,0.01\sqrt2)^T$. These output directions are orthogonal, so the singular values are $\sqrt2$ and $0.01\sqrt2$, giving $\kappa_2(X)=100$. The first observation strongly reveals the sum of the coefficients; the second only weakly reveals their difference. This is a geometry example with no intercept or residual degrees of freedom, not an estimate of error variance from two data points.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上标 $T$ 使用[[矩阵转置|转置]]把坐标写成列。对上述两个特征列，单位方向 $v_+=(1,1)^T/\sqrt2$、$v_-=(1,-1)^T/\sqrt2$ 分别产生 $(\sqrt2,0)^T$、$(0,0.01\sqrt2)^T$。两个输出方向正交，故奇异值为 $\sqrt2$ 和 $0.01\sqrt2$，条件数为 $100$。第一个观测有力地区分系数之和，第二个却只能微弱地区分系数之差。这是没有截距、没有残差自由度的几何例子，并非用两个点估计误差方差。

## Relative geometry and absolute perturbations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*相对几何与绝对扰动*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep $X$ fixed and change the response from $y$ to $y+\delta y$. The [[满列秩伪逆公式|least-squares map]] is $X^+=(X^TX)^{-1}X^T$, so the coefficient change is $\delta\hat\beta=X^+\delta y$. The SVD gives $X^+u_j=v_j/\sigma_j$, hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保持 $X$ 不变，把响应由 $y$ 改为 $y+\delta y$。[[满列秩伪逆公式|最小二乘映射]]为 $X^+=(X^TX)^{-1}X^T$，因此系数改变 $\delta\hat\beta=X^+\delta y$。SVD 给出 $X^+u_j=v_j/\sigma_j$，所以

$$\|\delta\hat\beta\|_2\le\frac{1}{\sigma_p}\|\delta y\|_2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This **absolute** amplification bound is $1/\sigma_p$, not $\kappa_2$ itself; see [[小奇异值放大噪声|directional amplification]]. For $X=\operatorname{diag}(100,1)$, meaning diagonal entries $100,1$ and zero elsewhere, the condition number is $100$ but this bound is only $1$. For $X=0.01I_2$, where $I_2$ is the two-dimensional identity matrix, the condition number is $1$ but the bound is $100$. A condition number compares scales; it does not specify their absolute size. If $X$ itself is perturbed in a least-squares problem, relative error bounds also depend on the residual's size relative to the fitted signal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个**绝对**放大上界是 $1/\sigma_p$，并不是条件数本身，见[[小奇异值放大噪声|方向性放大]]。若 $X=\operatorname{diag}(100,1)$，即对角元为 $100,1$、其余为零，条件数是 $100$，但该上界只有 $1$；若 $X=0.01I_2$，其中 $I_2$ 为二阶单位矩阵，条件数是 $1$，上界却为 $100$。条件数比较不同尺度，却没有指定尺度本身的绝对大小。最小二乘问题中若连 $X$ 也受到扰动，相对误差界还会依赖残差相对拟合信号的大小。

## How it differs from VIF and numerical stability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与 VIF 的分工

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[方差膨胀因子|VIF]] compares one predictor with the other columns. The condition number compares directions of the whole matrix. Neither chooses a variable for deletion, and a large raw condition number need not mean that every VIF is large: unequal column units can create a large ratio even for orthogonal columns. Inspect the weak vector under a stated [[条件数缩放边界|scaling convention]], then interpret the combination in the model's terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[方差膨胀因子|VIF]]把一个预测变量与其他列比较，条件数则比较整个矩阵的不同方向。两者都不负责决定删哪一列；较大的原始条件数也不表示每个 VIF 都很大，因为列的单位差异，即使在列正交时也能制造大比值。应在明确的[[条件数缩放边界|缩放口径]]下检查弱向量，再解释它在模型中的含义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[近似共线性精度|Sampling precision]] additionally requires an error model. For fixed $X$ in $y=X\beta+u$, let $u$ denote the random error vector. With zero [[条件期望|conditional error mean]] and $\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X)=\tau^2I_n$ for finite $\tau^2>0$ and identity matrix $I_n$, the variance of the estimated coefficient combination $v_j^T\hat\beta$ is $\tau^2/\sigma_j^2$. Here $\tau^2$ is error variance, not a singular value. A numerical ratio alone cannot supply it. [[条件性与算法稳定性|Algorithmic stability]] instead concerns rounding and the computation: a stable QR or SVD solver can reduce numerical damage but cannot add missing statistical information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[近似共线性精度|抽样精度]]还需要误差模型。对固定 $X$ 的 $y=X\beta+u$，以 $u$ 表示随机误差向量。若误差[[条件期望|条件均值]]为零、$\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X)=\tau^2I_n$，其中 $I_n$ 为单位矩阵、$\tau^2$ 有限并为正，估计系数组合 $v_j^T\hat\beta$ 的方差为 $\tau^2/\sigma_j^2$。这里 $\tau^2$ 是误差方差，不是奇异值，数值比值本身不能提供它。[[条件性与算法稳定性|算法稳定性]]则关乎舍入与计算：稳定的 QR 或 SVD 求解器可以减少数值损伤，却无法增加缺失的统计信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, the Gram matrix $X^TX$ records pairwise column dot products. It has [[特征对|eigenvalues]] $\sigma_j^2$, so at full column rank $\kappa_2(X^TX)=\kappa_2(X)^2$. Forming and solving the [[正规方程条件数平方|normal equations]] therefore uses a matrix with the squared condition number. This algebraic identity explains an avoidable computational difficulty; it does not mean that every computed coefficient always loses exactly that many digits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后，Gram 矩阵 $X^TX$ 记录各列两两的点积，其[[特征对|特征值]]为 $\sigma_j^2$，所以满列秩时 $\kappa_2(X^TX)=\kappa_2(X)^2$。显式形成并求解[[正规方程条件数平方|正规方程]]，就会使用条件数已经平方的矩阵。这个代数恒等式解释了一种可以避免的计算困难，但并不是说每个计算出来的系数必然损失某个固定数量的有效数字。

## 来源与核验

- [LAPACK Users' Guide, Singular Value Decomposition](https://netlib.org/lapack/lug/node32.html) and [Norms and Condition Numbers](https://netlib.org/lapack/lug/node75.html): singular values, singular directions and the specified norm. The extreme-stretch and directional perturbation formulas here follow directly from that decomposition.
- [LAPACK, Further Details: Error Bounds for Linear Least Squares Problems](https://netlib.org/lapack/lug/node83.html): the full-rank rectangular ratio, backward error and the additional residual-angle dependence of least-squares sensitivity.
- [MIT 18.335J, Week 4, Lecture 9](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-335j-introduction-to-numerical-methods-spring-2019/pages/week-4/): normal-equation condition-number squaring and the QR alternative. The coefficient-variance identity follows by applying the displayed SVD to $\tau^2(X^TX)^{-1}$.
- [NIST Dataplot, Regression Diagnostics, Multi-collinearity](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman1/auxillar/regrdiag.htm): the role of column scaling in regression diagnostics; the condition-number ratio above is verified against LAPACK. [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/06_多重共线性.md#4.5. 设计矩阵的整体弱方向|Course §4.5]] fixes the local regression scope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- LAPACK 的 SVD 与范数章节核对奇异值、奇异方向及范数口径；极端伸缩和方向扰动公式由该分解直接推得。
- LAPACK 的最小二乘误差界章节核对满秩矩形矩阵比值、后向误差，以及最小二乘敏感性还依赖残差角度的边界。
- MIT 第 9 讲核对正规方程使条件数平方及 QR 替代路线；系数组合的方差公式由 SVD 代入 $\tau^2(X^TX)^{-1}$ 推得。
- NIST 的共线性部分核对列缩放在回归诊断中的角色；本卡条件数比值另以 LAPACK 核验。本地课程第 4.5 节提供回归范围。
