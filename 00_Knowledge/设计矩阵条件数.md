# 设计矩阵的二范数条件数用最大与最小奇异值之比衡量最强和最弱列组合方向的相对敏感性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The two-norm condition number of a design matrix measures relative sensitivity by the ratio of its largest to smallest singular value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 若 $X$ 满列秩，奇异值满足 $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_p>0$，则
> $$
> \kappa_2(X)=\frac{\sigma_1}{\sigma_p}.
> $$
> $\sigma_1$ 描述最强的列组合方向，$\sigma_p$ 描述最弱方向；比率很大表示某些系数组合会把很小的数据或数值扰动放大。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $X$ has full column rank with singular values $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_p>0$, then $\kappa_2(X)=\sigma_1/\sigma_p$. The largest singular value describes the strongest design direction and the smallest the weakest. A large ratio signals that some coefficient combination strongly amplifies small data or numerical perturbations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 SVD $X=U\Sigma V'$，右奇异向量 $v_p$ 给出最弱的**系数组合**：沿 $v_p$ 改变系数，只会使拟合向量改变 $\sigma_p$ 倍。若 $\sigma_p=0$，设计精确秩亏，条件数为无穷；若它只是相对很小，就对应近似共线方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the SVD $X=U\Sigma V'$, the right singular vector $v_p$ identifies the weakest coefficient combination: moving the coefficients along $v_p$ changes the fitted vector by only $\sigma_p$ times that movement. If $\sigma_p=0$, the design is exactly rank deficient and the condition number is infinite; a merely small value gives a near-collinear direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与 VIF 的分工
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How it differs from VIF*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[方差膨胀因子]]逐列问“第 $j$ 个系数的方差被其余列放大多少”；条件数从整个矩阵问“最弱的联合方向有多敏感”。大条件数不能单独指出应删哪一列，也不等于每个 VIF 都很大。最有用的做法是继续查看弱奇异向量由哪些经过同一缩放的列组成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
VIF asks column by column how much the other regressors inflate the variance of coefficient $j$. A condition number asks how sensitive the weakest joint direction of the whole matrix is. A large condition number does not identify a variable to delete and need not imply that every VIF is large. Its useful follow-up is to inspect which consistently scaled columns load on the weak singular vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

统计上的系数方差与数值算法的舍入误差都可能沿小奇异方向放大，但它们不是同一个问题。即使软件使用稳定 QR 或 SVD，样本仍可能缺少区分系数的真实信息；反过来，显式形成 $X'X$ 还会额外把二范数条件数平方，见[[正规方程条件数平方]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sampling uncertainty and numerical roundoff can both be amplified in a small-singular-value direction, but they are not the same problem. A stable QR or SVD solver cannot create sample information that the design lacks. Conversely, explicitly forming $X'X$ adds a numerical penalty by squaring the two-norm condition number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么只报告“条件数很大”仍没有指出是哪组经济变量无法被数据分开？
>
> **答案：** 条件数只给最强与最弱尺度之比；还要在明确缩放后查看最小奇异值对应的右奇异向量及变量含义。
