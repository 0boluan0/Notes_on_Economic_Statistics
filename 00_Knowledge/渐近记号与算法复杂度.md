# 渐近记号与算法复杂度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Asymptotic Notation and Algorithmic Complexity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 估计输入变大时算法所需时间/空间怎样增长，并在顺序、比较和键查找任务中选择合适结构。
> **具体锚点：** 1,000 个元素的 O(n²) 排序约做百万级比较，而 O(n log n) 约万级；增长阶在规模大时主导。
> **核心难点：** Big O 是上界，Theta 才是紧确阶；平均、最坏和摊销复杂度回答不同情景。
> **为什么重要：** 先选正确算法/数据结构，通常比微优化语句收益大得多。
> **继续：** 先定义输入规模和基本操作，再分析循环/递归；排序还要看稳定性、原地性和数据分布。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Problem addressed:** Estimate how an algorithm's time or space grows with input size and use that information to choose suitable algorithms and data structures.
> **Concrete anchor:** Sorting 1,000 elements with an O(n²) method can require about a million comparisons, whereas O(n log n) is around ten thousand; growth rate dominates at scale.
> **Central difficulty:** Big O is an upper bound, while Theta is a tight order; average, worst-case, and amortized analyses answer different questions.
> **Why it matters:** Choosing the right algorithm or data structure usually matters more than micro-optimizing statements.
> **Continue with:** Define input size and a basic operation before analyzing loops or recursion; use [[排序算法|Sorting Algorithms]] and [[哈希表|Hash Tables]] for concrete design trade-offs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 计时与计步
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Timing and Counting Operations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

墙钟时间受机器和输入影响，操作计数抽象增长。基准测试应 warm-up、重复并使用代表输入；理论分析给跨机器趋势，实测验证常数、缓存和实现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Wall-clock time depends on machine and input, while operation counting abstracts growth. A benchmark should warm up, repeat, and use representative inputs. Theory predicts cross-machine trends; measurement reveals constants, cache behavior, and implementation effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## O、Omega 与 Theta
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*O, Omega, and Theta*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$O(g)$ 给最终上界，$\Omega(g)$ 给下界，$\Theta(g)$ 给同阶。忽略常数和低阶项只适合渐近；小 n、昂贵操作和内存局部性仍重要。明确最好/平均/最坏。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$O(g)$ gives an eventual upper bound, $\Omega(g)$ a lower bound, and $\Theta(g)$ a tight order. Ignoring constants and lower-order terms is asymptotic; small inputs, expensive primitive operations, and memory locality still matter. Always state whether the case is best, average, or worst.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 常见复杂度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Common Growth Rates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常数、log、linear、n log n、quadratic、exponential 依次增长。嵌套循环不总是相乘，要看总迭代；每次把问题减半常为 log n。递归用调用树或 recurrence 汇总。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Constant, logarithmic, linear, n log n, quadratic, and exponential growth become progressively larger. Nested loops do not automatically multiply; count total iterations. Repeated halving often yields logarithmic depth. Analyze recursion with a call tree or recurrence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 摊销与空间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Amortized and Space Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

动态数组 append 单次扩容 O(n)，但一系列 append 摊销 O(1)。报告时间时也看辅助空间、递归栈和数据复制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One dynamic-array expansion costs O(n), but a sequence of appends has amortized O(1) cost per append. Alongside time, report auxiliary space, recursion stack usage, and data copying.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：三角形循环不是 O(n²)
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: A Triangular Loop Is Still Theta(n²), but Count It Correctly*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若内层执行 `0..i-1`，总次数是 $0+1+\cdots+(n-1)=n(n-1)/2$，所以是 $\Theta(n^2)$；结论来自求和，而不是看到两层循环便机械相乘。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the inner loop runs from `0` to `i-1`, total iterations are $0+1+\cdots+(n-1)=n(n-1)/2$, hence $\Theta(n^2)$. The conclusion comes from summation, not mechanically multiplying because two loops are visible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若内层每次把索引乘 2，则每个外层只做 $\Theta(\log n)$ 次，总复杂度是 $\Theta(n\log n)$。相同嵌套层数可以有不同增长阶。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the inner index doubles each time, it performs $\Theta(\log n)$ iterations per outer iteration, for $\Theta(n\log n)$ total time. The same nesting depth can therefore have a different growth order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 搜索复杂度入口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Entry Point to Search Complexity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有序数组二分查找保持“目标若存在则仍在当前半开区间”的不变量，每步把候选区间减半，因此比较次数为 $\Theta(\log n)$。它不同于连续函数的二分求根，后者见 [[数值求根：二分法与 Newton–Raphson]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Binary search on a sorted array maintains the invariant that any existing target remains inside the current half-open interval. Each step halves that interval, giving $\Theta(\log n)$ comparisons. This differs from bisection root-finding for a continuous function, covered in [[数值求根：二分法与 Newton–Raphson|Numerical Root-Finding]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 只写 O(n) 却没有 n：先定义输入规模和计数的基本操作。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  A claim says only O(n) without defining n: state the input-size parameter and counted primitive operation first.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 平均 O(1) 被当成保证：区分期望、摊销和最坏情形，并写明随机性或输入假设。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Average O(1) is treated as a guarantee: distinguish expected, amortized, and worst-case bounds and state randomness or input assumptions.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 理论更优却实测更慢：检查规模是否进入渐近区、常数、分配与缓存，不要否定正确的渐近结论。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  A theoretically better method is slower in a benchmark: inspect whether input size reached the asymptotic regime, as well as constants, allocation, and cache behavior.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum Self-Check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 O(n) 不等于‘正好执行 n 次’？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does O(n) not mean “exactly n operations”?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它只给足够大 n 下由常数倍 n 上界控制，可含常数和低阶项。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It states only that a constant multiple of n eventually bounds the function above; constants and lower-order terms may be present.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 动态数组 append 为什么可说摊销 O(1)？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can dynamic-array append be described as amortized O(1)?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 偶尔昂贵扩容的成本分摊到许多便宜 append 上，长序列平均每次常数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The occasional expensive resize is spread over many cheap appends, producing constant average cost across a long sequence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 二分查找的前提和不变量是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What are the precondition and invariant of binary search?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 数据按比较规则有序；若目标存在，它始终位于当前候选半开区间内，每步依据中点比较安全排除一半。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Data must be ordered under the comparison rule; if the target exists, it remains in the current half-open candidate interval, and each midpoint comparison safely excludes half.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
