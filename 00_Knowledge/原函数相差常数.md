# Antiderivatives differ by a constant
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*原函数相差常数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing every slope of a curve still leaves its vertical position undetermined. The functions $x^2$ and $x^2+5$ both have derivative $2x$, but one graph is always $5$ units above the other. **On one interval, a constant vertical shift is the only freedom left:** two functions with the same derivative cannot drift farther apart at some inputs and closer together at others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使知道曲线上每一点的斜率，图像整体的高度仍然没有确定。$x^2$ 和 $x^2+5$ 的导数都是 $2x$，但后一条图像始终比前一条高 $5$。**在同一个区间上，常数平移就是剩下的全部自由度：**两个函数如果处处导数相同，它们的高度差就不能时大时小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recall that an [[原函数|antiderivative]] of $f$ is a function whose derivative is $f$ throughout the interval. Let $I$ be a nonempty open interval, and suppose differentiable functions $F$ and $G$ both satisfy $F'(x)=G'(x)=f(x)$ there. Then there is a real constant $C$ such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回顾[[原函数]]：它是一个在整个区间上求导后都得到 $f$ 的函数。设 $I$ 是非空开区间，可导函数 $F,G$ 在其中都满足 $F'(x)=G'(x)=f(x)$。那么存在实常数 $C$，使

$$
F(x)=G(x)+C\qquad(x\in I).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same $C$ works for every input in $I$. This conclusion does not require $f$ to be continuous. If $F$ and $G$ also extend continuously to an included endpoint, their constant difference extends to that endpoint by continuity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里同一个 $C$ 对 $I$ 中所有输入都有效，并不要求共同导数 $f$ 连续。如果 $F,G$ 还连续延伸到某个被包含的端点，这个常数差也会由连续性延伸到那个端点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the difference cannot vary
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么两者之差不能变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Measure the vertical gap by $H(x)=F(x)-G(x)$. Its derivative is $H'(x)=F'(x)-G'(x)=0$. A zero derivative at just one point says little about the rest of a graph; here it is zero **at every point of the interval**. The [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]] turns that fact into a comparison of any two heights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $H(x)=F(x)-G(x)$ 表示两条图像的高度差。它的导数是 $H'(x)=F'(x)-G'(x)=0$。仅在一点导数为零，无法说明整条图像怎样；这里是**区间内每一点**的导数都为零。[[拉格朗日中值定理]]把这一事实转化为任意两个函数值之间的比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take any $u<v$ in $I$. Because $I$ is an interval, the whole segment $[u,v]$ lies inside $I$. Differentiability makes $H$ continuous on $[u,v]$, and it is differentiable on $(u,v)$. Thus the mean value theorem gives some $c\in(u,v)$ with
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任取 $I$ 中的 $u<v$。因为 $I$ 是区间，整段 $[u,v]$ 都在 $I$ 内。$H$ 的可导性保证它在 $[u,v]$ 上连续，并且在 $(u,v)$ 内可导。因此中值定理给出某个 $c\in(u,v)$，满足

$$
H(v)-H(u)=H'(c)(v-u)=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So $H(v)=H(u)$. The two points were arbitrary, hence $H$ is constant throughout $I$. This is the zero-derivative case of the [[导数单调性判据|derivative criterion for monotonicity]], with the reasoning made explicit here. Conversely, adding a constant to an antiderivative preserves its derivative. Together, these two directions justify the entire family $\int f(x)\,dx=G(x)+C$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以 $H(v)=H(u)$。这两点是任意取的，说明 $H$ 在整个 $I$ 上恒定。这也是[[导数单调性判据]]中“导数恒为零”的情形，这里把推理完整展开了。反过来，给原函数加上任意常数，导数当然保持不变。两个方向合起来，才说明 $\int f(x)\,dx=G(x)+C$ 恰好给出了全部原函数。

> [!example] A value fixes the remaining constant · 一个函数值确定常数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On the real line, $F'(x)=2x$ gives $F(x)=x^2+C$. If we also know $F(0)=5$, substituting $x=0$ gives $C=5$, so $F(x)=x^2+5$. This is how an [[通解与初值|initial condition]] selects one member of this family: the derivative specifies changes, and one value specifies the height.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在实数轴上，$F'(x)=2x$ 给出 $F(x)=x^2+C$。如果还知道 $F(0)=5$，代入 $x=0$ 就得到 $C=5$，因此 $F(x)=x^2+5$。这就是[[通解与初值|初值条件]]从这一族函数中选出一条的方式：导数确定变化，一个函数值确定高度。

## A gap in the domain permits separate constants
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*定义域断开时，每一段可以有自己的常数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The function $f(x)=1/x$ is defined on $(-\infty,0)\cup(0,\infty)$, which consists of two separate intervals. Compare $G(x)=\ln|x|$ with
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$f(x)=1/x$ 的定义域是 $(-\infty,0)\cup(0,\infty)$，由两个分开的区间组成。比较 $G(x)=\ln|x|$ 与下面的函数：

$$
F(x)=
\begin{cases}
\ln|x|,&x<0,\\
\ln x+7,&x>0
\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At every allowed input, both derivatives equal $1/x$. Yet $F-G$ equals $0$ on the negative half-line and $7$ on the positive half-line. No single constant describes the gap on the whole domain. The mean value proof cannot connect a negative input to a positive input: their connecting segment passes through zero, where the functions are undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在每个允许的输入处，两个函数的导数都等于 $1/x$。但是，$F-G$ 在负半轴上等于 $0$，在正半轴上等于 $7$，没有一个常数能描述整个定义域上的差。中值定理的证明无法连接一个负输入和一个正输入，因为连接它们的线段经过零点，而函数在那里没有定义。

> [!important] State the interval · 写清所讨论的区间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> All antiderivatives of $1/x$ on its full domain use two independent constants: $\ln|x|+C_-$ for $x<0$, and $\ln x+C_+$ for $x>0$. A condition imposed at one positive input fixes $C_+$ only. The theorem gives one constant **per connected interval**, not automatically one constant across a disconnected domain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $1/x$ 的完整定义域上，全部原函数要用两个独立常数表示：$x<0$ 时为 $\ln|x|+C_-$，$x>0$ 时为 $\ln x+C_+$。只在一个正输入处给定函数值，只能确定 $C_+$。定理保证的是**每个连通区间上**有一个常数，不能自动把它们合并成跨越断开定义域的一个常数。
