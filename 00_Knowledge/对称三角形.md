# 对称三角形是高点降低与低点抬高形成的收敛价格区间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A symmetrical triangle is a converging price range formed by lower highs and higher lows.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对称三角形是由相继降低的高点构成向下上界、相继抬高的低点构成向上下界，两条 [[趋势线]]朝前收敛的价格区间。价格在其中往返时，区间总体变窄。“对称”不要求两侧几何斜率绝对值完全相等，也不要求恰好六个触点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A symmetrical triangle has a falling upper boundary through lower highs and a rising lower boundary through higher lows. The two [[趋势线|trendlines]] converge forward in time as the trading range narrows. Symmetry does not require identical absolute slopes or exactly six touches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

形状本身不决定离开方向：向上和向下突破都可能，既可延续也可逆转进入形态前的方向。若检验突破，先固定锚点识别、边界容差及确认条件，再把当期价格与当期边界值比较；不能用最终画好的三角倒填早期信号，见 [[交易信号时序]]。形态定义也不包含某个保证可达到的目标价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The shape does not determine the exit direction: either an upward or a downward break may continue or reverse the preceding move. A breakout test needs predetermined anchor identification, boundary tolerance and confirmation, comparing price with the boundary value at that time. A triangle completed later cannot backfill earlier signals; see [[交易信号时序|execution timing]]. The definition includes no guaranteed price target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如某线性时间坐标下，已用可得锚点固定上界 $U(t)=120-2t$、下界 $L(t)=100+t$。在 $t=4$，区间为 $[104,112]$；在交点以前宽度 $20-3t$ 递减。若选择无缓冲收盘确认，$t=4$ 收盘 113 或 103 分别是上破或下破的两个可能结果，不能从收敛形状预先断定其中一个。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose available anchors fix $U(t)=120-2t$ and $L(t)=100+t$ on linear axes. At $t=4$ the range is $[104,112]$, with width $20-3t$ decreasing before the intersection. Under an unbuffered closing test, a close at 113 or 103 would respectively be an upward or downward break. Convergence alone does not select one outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
