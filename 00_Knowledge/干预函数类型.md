# 脉冲、阶跃与斜坡干预分别编码一次、永久与渐进的输入路径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pulse, step, and ramp interventions encode one-off, permanent, and gradual input paths*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 若事件从 $T_0$ 开始，脉冲 $P_t=\mathbf1\{t=T_0\}$ 只在一期为 1；阶跃 $S_t=\mathbf1\{t\ge T_0\}$ 此后一直为 1；斜坡 $R_t=(t-T_0+1)\mathbf1\{t\ge T_0\}$ 随事件后时间递增。它们分别询问一次异常、永久水平输入和逐步累积输入，不能只因发生日期相同就互换。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A pulse equals one only at the event date, a step remains one thereafter, and a ramp grows with time since the event. They encode a one-off anomaly, a permanent level input, and a gradually accumulating input, respectively.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入函数的形状不等于结果响应的形状。即使 $P_t$ 只有一期为 1，若模型含自回归或 transfer-function 动态，$y$ 的响应仍可衰减若干期；反过来，一个永久阶跃也可能在差分模型中主要表现为一次水平变化。报告时应把“输入怎样编码”和“模型怎样传播”分成两句话。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The intervention input is not the same as the outcome response. A one-period pulse can generate a multi-period decay through autoregressive or transfer-function dynamics, while a permanent step in levels may appear as a one-time change after differencing. State the input path and the propagation law separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还可以编码有限持续脉冲、逐渐饱和、季节性变化或提前预期，但每一种都应由事件机制和可观测时间线决定。看到图形先上升后趋稳，并不足以倒推出应使用斜坡；候选形状需要在事件发生前确定或通过诚实的样本外比较约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite-duration pulses, saturating changes, seasonal interventions, and anticipation effects are possible extensions. Their forms should follow the event mechanism and observable timeline, not be selected retrospectively merely because one shape fits the realised path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 一次事故与制度改革
> 一次停电可先用 pulse 编码；从某日起永久实施的新收费制度可先用 step 编码。两者都可能通过动态模型影响多期，但输入反事实并不相同。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A one-off outage is naturally encoded as a pulse, while a pricing regime that remains in force is naturally encoded as a step. Either may affect several outcome periods through model dynamics, but their input counterfactuals differ.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> pulse 变量只有一期为 1，是否意味着结果只能受影响一期？
>
> **答案：** 不意味着。输入只有一期；结果可经自回归或传递函数在以后各期继续响应。
