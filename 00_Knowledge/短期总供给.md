# 短期总供给是在工资投入成本和预期等短期条件给定时价格与实际产出的关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Short-run aggregate supply relates prices to real output while short-run wage, input-cost, and expectation conditions are held fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

短期总供给曲线 SRAS（课堂写 SAS）表示给定短期成本、技术和预期下，各一般价格水平对应的生产量。标准模型中，产品价格相对尚未充分调整的投入成本上升，会使增加生产更有利，因而曲线通常向上倾斜。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
SRAS, called SAS in the course, shows production at different price levels under given short-run costs, technology, and expectations. When output prices rise relative to incompletely adjusted input costs, additional production becomes more attractive, giving the standard curve an upward slope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿曲线移动与曲线移动不同：只改变一般产品价格是在原曲线上移动；工资、能源成本或生产率改变，会改变同一价格下的供给并移动曲线，见[[总供给冲击]]。斜率和调整速度是模型与经济状态的属性，不由“短期”二字唯一确定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing only the general output price moves along SRAS. Changes in wages, energy costs, or productivity alter supply at a given price and [[总供给冲击|shift the curve]]. Its slope and adjustment speed depend on the model and economic conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如示意式 $P=Y+c$ 中 $c$ 代表成本条件。$c$ 从 0 升到 20 后，同一 $P=100$ 下产量从 100 降为 80；实际均衡还要与[[总需求]]共同求解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the illustrative relation $P=Y+c$, raising the cost term $c$ from 0 to 20 reduces output at $P=100$ from 100 to 80. Equilibrium still requires solving jointly with [[总需求|aggregate demand]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
