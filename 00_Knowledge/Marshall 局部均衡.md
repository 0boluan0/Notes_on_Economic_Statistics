# Marshall 局部均衡暂时固定跨市场反馈以研究一个市场
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Marshallian partial equilibrium temporarily holds cross-market feedback fixed to study one market*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子方法
> 局部均衡选择一个市场作为焦点，把收入、相关商品价格、投入市场和制度环境等暂时视为给定，再研究该市场的需求、供给、价格与数量如何相容。它是一种有意识的隔离近似，不是声称其他市场事实上不存在。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Partial equilibrium selects one focal market, temporarily treating income, related-goods prices, input markets, and institutions as given while studying the consistency of demand, supply, price, and quantity in that market. It is a deliberate isolating approximation, not a claim that other markets do not exist.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当焦点市场相对整体很小、收入反馈弱、替代与投入联动有限，或这些反馈已经被清楚参数化时，局部均衡通常更透明。若政策同时改变多个大市场、显著重分配收入，或投入价格和预算反馈会改变结论，就需要扩展为多市场或一般均衡分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Partial equilibrium is often transparent when the focal market is small relative to the economy, income feedback is weak, substitution and input linkages are limited, or those feedbacks are explicitly parameterised. A policy that changes several large markets, materially redistributes income, or generates decisive input-price and budget feedback calls for a multi-market or general-equilibrium extension.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般均衡并非天然“更高级”。加入大量无关反馈会增加建模和解释负担。方法选择应由省略的反馈是否足以改变问题答案决定，而不是由模型覆盖的市场数量决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
General equilibrium is not automatically superior. Adding immaterial feedback creates modelling and interpretation costs. The right method depends on whether omitted feedback can materially change the answer, not on the number of markets represented.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 对一个极小商品征税且收入效应可忽略时，为什么局部均衡可能比完整一般均衡更合适？
>
> **答案：** 关键跨市场反馈很小，局部模型能直接展示税如何移动该市场的价格和数量；额外联立可能只增加噪声而不改变答案。
