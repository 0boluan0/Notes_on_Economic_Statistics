# Generalized eigenvectors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*广义特征向量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ordinary [[特征对|eigenvector]] is a nonzero vector that a matrix only scales: $Av=\lambda v$. Subtract that scaling from the matrix action, and the vector goes straight to zero. A **generalized eigenvector** may need several applications of this same adjusted action before reaching zero. It describes an exact sequence of linked directions when ordinary eigenvectors do not supply enough directions for a basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通[[特征对|特征向量]]是只被矩阵缩放的非零向量：$Av=\lambda v$。从矩阵作用中减去这份缩放，它就直接归零。**广义特征向量**则可能需要重复施加几次这个调整后的作用，才会归零。当普通特征向量不足以提供一组基时，它帮助描述相互连接的精确方向。

> [!example] One step versus two steps / 一步与两步
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider the two-coordinate matrix $J$ below. Subtracting twice the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I_2$ removes the diagonal scaling and leaves $N$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑下列作用于两个坐标的矩阵 $J$。减去两倍[[单位矩阵]] $I_2$，就去掉了对角缩放，留下 $N$：
>
> $$J=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> N=J-2I_2=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The action is $N(x,y)^T=(y,0)^T$; the superscript $T$ turns the written coordinate row into a [[矩阵转置|column vector]]. Let $e_1=(1,0)^T$ and $e_2=(0,1)^T$. Then $Ne_1=0$, while $Ne_2=e_1\ne0$ and $N^2e_2=0$. The first vector needs one step; the second needs two. In particular, $Je_2=(1,2)^T$ is not $2e_2$, although $e_2$ is a generalized eigenvector for $2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $N(x,y)^T=(y,0)^T$；上标 $T$ 表示把写成一行的坐标[[矩阵转置|转置为列向量]]。令 $e_1=(1,0)^T$、$e_2=(0,1)^T$，则 $Ne_1=0$，而 $Ne_2=e_1\ne0$、$N^2e_2=0$。前者一步归零，后者两步归零。尤其 $Je_2=(1,2)^T$ 并不等于 $2e_2$，但 $e_2$ 仍是对应于 $2$ 的广义特征向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The order is the first power that works
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*阶数取第一次归零所需的次数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ matrix over $\mathbb F=\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, with $n\ge1$, and let $\lambda\in\mathbb F$ be an eigenvalue. Here $\mathbb F$ specifies the allowed scalars. Set $N=A-\lambda I_n$, so $Nv=Av-\lambda v$. A vector $v\in\mathbb F^n$ is a generalized eigenvector for $\lambda$ when $v\ne0$ and, for some positive integer $r$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 是 $\mathbb F=\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$ 上的 $n\times n$ 方阵，$n\ge1$，$\lambda\in\mathbb F$ 是一个特征值；$\mathbb F$ 指定允许使用的标量。令 $N=A-\lambda I_n$，即 $Nv=Av-\lambda v$。若 $v\in\mathbb F^n$ 非零，且存在正整数 $r$ 使

$$N^r v=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The power means repeated matrix application: $N^2v=N(Nv)$, not squaring each coordinate. Its **order** is the smallest such positive integer $r$. Equivalently,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

就称 $v$ 是对应于 $\lambda$ 的广义特征向量。幂表示重复施加矩阵：$N^2v=N(Nv)$，不是逐个坐标平方。使等式成立的最小正整数 $r$ 称为它的**阶**，等价地要求

$$N^rv=0,\qquad N^{r-1}v\ne0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The convention $N^0=I_n$ makes this work for $r=1$ as well. Order one means $Nv=0$, exactly the ordinary eigenvector condition. We use **proper generalized eigenvector** for order at least two. Thus “generalized” includes ordinary eigenvectors; it does not mean “not an eigenvector”. The zero vector is excluded even though every power sends it to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

约定 $N^0=I_n$，因此 $r=1$ 时同样适用。阶为一表示 $Nv=0$，正是普通特征向量条件。阶至少为二时，本卡称为**真广义特征向量**。所以“广义”包含普通特征向量，并不表示“不是特征向量”。零向量虽然被每个幂送到零，仍被定义排除。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once one power sends $v$ to zero, every higher power does too, since $N0=0$. Therefore $N^3v=0$ alone does not establish order three: an ordinary eigenvector also satisfies it. The additional check $N^2v\ne0$ is what makes the order exactly three.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一旦某个幂把 $v$ 送到零，所有更高次幂也会如此，因为 $N0=0$。因此仅有 $N^3v=0$ 不能确定阶为三：普通特征向量也满足它。还要检查 $N^2v\ne0$，才能确定阶恰为三。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Higher powers collect more directions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*更高次幂可以收集更多方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[零空间|null space]] $\ker N^k$ is the set of all vectors sent to zero by $N^k$, including zero. These spaces are nested:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[零空间]] $\ker N^k$ 收集被 $N^k$ 送到零的全部向量，包括零向量。这些空间逐层包含：

$$\ker N\subseteq\ker N^2\subseteq\ker N^3\subseteq\cdots.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening example, $\ker N=\{(x,0)^T:x\in\mathbb F\}$ is only the horizontal line, whereas $\ker N^2=\mathbb F^2$. Every nonzero vector with second coordinate zero has order one; every vector with second coordinate nonzero has order two. The ordinary [[特征空间|eigenspace]] is the first of these null spaces. This explains how generalized directions can fill a gap left by ordinary ones; it does not guarantee that any arbitrarily chosen vectors form a basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头例子中，$\ker N=\{(x,0)^T:x\in\mathbb F\}$ 只有水平直线，而 $\ker N^2=\mathbb F^2$。第二坐标为零的非零向量阶为一，第二坐标非零的向量阶为二。普通[[特征空间]]就是第一个零空间。这说明广义方向怎样补充普通方向的不足，但不保证随意挑选的向量就能组成一组基。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $v$ has order $r$, its last nonzero image $w=N^{r-1}v$ satisfies $Nw=0$, so $Aw=\lambda w$. Thus repeated adjusted action reaches an ordinary eigenvector before zero. Reading these nonzero images in reverse order gives a [[Jordan链|Jordan chain]], whose vectors and order are explained separately. In an $n$-dimensional space the order cannot exceed $n$: that chain contains $r$ [[Jordan链向量线性无关|independent vectors]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $v$ 的阶为 $r$，最后一个非零像 $w=N^{r-1}v$ 满足 $Nw=0$，即 $Aw=\lambda w$。因此重复施加调整后的作用，会先到达普通特征向量，再归零。把这些非零像反向排列就得到 [[Jordan链]]，其向量顺序另有说明。在 $n$ 维空间中，阶不能超过 $n$，因为那条链包含 $r$ 个[[Jordan链向量线性无关|线性无关向量]]。

> [!warning] Exact relations, not near eigenvectors / 精确关系，不是近似特征向量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A small nonzero $Av-\lambda v$ does not establish generalized-eigenvector status. The definition requires an exact zero after a finite power. It also concerns a chosen eigenvalue in the chosen field: enlarging the field can make new eigenvalues and vectors available, as discussed in the [[Jordan标准形的域条件|field condition for Jordan form]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $Av-\lambda v$ 很小但非零，并不能证明它是广义特征向量；定义要求有限次作用后精确归零。它还针对所用数域中的特定特征值；扩充数域可能带来新的特征值与向量，见[[Jordan标准形的域条件]]。
