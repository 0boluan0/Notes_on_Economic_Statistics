# Stationary uniqueness and convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*稳态唯一与逐步收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A unique balanced distribution need not attract other distributions. A system can have exactly one probability allocation that stays unchanged, while every unbalanced allocation keeps oscillating. The two-state switching example makes the distinction visible without a long calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

唯一的平衡分布未必会吸引其他分布。系统可能恰好只有一种概率分配保持不变，而其他不平衡分配始终振荡。只需看两个状态不断交换，就能把这一区别看清楚。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A switch that never settles
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*不断交换的两个状态*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the states be $1$ and $2$. At each step the system certainly switches to the other state. Under the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|column convention]], the [[Markov矩阵|transition matrix]] and probability update are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设状态为 $1$、$2$，每一步都确定地切换到另一个状态。采用[[Markov矩阵左右约定|列随机约定]]，[[Markov矩阵|转移矩阵]]与概率更新为

$$
P=\begin{bmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{bmatrix},\qquad p_{k+1}=Pp_k.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The column $p_k$ lists the two state probabilities at step $k=0,1,2,\ldots$. A [[Markov稳态分布|stationary distribution]] $\pi$ must satisfy $P\pi=\pi$, with nonnegative entries summing to one. If $\pi=(a,b)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], the equation swaps the entries and therefore requires $a=b$. There is only one normalized answer: $\pi=(1/2,1/2)^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

列 $p_k$ 列出第 $k=0,1,2,\ldots$ 步的两个状态概率。[[Markov稳态分布|稳态分布]] $\pi$ 必须满足 $P\pi=\pi$，分量非负且总和为一。若写 $\pi=(a,b)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置]]，方程交换两个分量，因此要求 $a=b$。归一化后只有一个答案：$\pi=(1/2,1/2)^T$。

> [!example] Unique balance, persistent alternation · 平衡唯一，交替不止
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Start with certainty in state $1$, so $p_0=(1,0)^T$. For every integer $k\ge0$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若确定从状态 $1$ 出发，即 $p_0=(1,0)^T$，则对每个整数 $k\ge0$，
>
> $$p_{2k}=(1,0)^T,\qquad p_{2k+1}=(0,1)^T.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The even and odd subsequences have different values forever, so the distributions do not converge. Starting at $\pi$ itself does stay at $\pi$: the failed claim is convergence **from every initial distribution**, not the existence of any convergent start.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 偶数步与奇数步始终取不同的值，所以分布没有极限。从 $\pi$ 本身出发当然会保持 $\pi$；失败的是“**每个初始分布**都收敛”的断言，而不是“存在某个收敛初态”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The fixed part and the oscillating part
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*固定部分与振荡部分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[特征对|matrix eigenvector]] is a nonzero direction that multiplication merely scales. Here the balanced direction $(1,1)^T$ has eigenvalue $1$, whereas the signed direction $(1,-1)^T$ has eigenvalue $-1$. The latter is not a probability vector; it describes a deviation that moves probability from one state to the other. Every initial probability column has the decomposition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[特征对|矩阵特征向量]]是一个非零方向，乘矩阵只会把它乘以某个标量。这里平衡方向 $(1,1)^T$ 的特征值为 $1$，带正负号的方向 $(1,-1)^T$ 的特征值为 $-1$。后者不是概率向量，而是把概率从一个状态移到另一个状态的偏离。任意初始概率列都可写成

$$
p_0=(a,1-a)^T
=\pi+(a-\tfrac12)(1,-1)^T,\qquad 0\le a\le1,
$$
$$
p_k=P^kp_0=\pi+(-1)^k(a-\tfrac12)(1,-1)^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The factor $(-1)^k$ changes the sign but not the size of the deviation. Unless $a=1/2$, it cannot decay. [[Markov稳态唯一判据|Stationary uniqueness]] restricts the fixed eigenspace at $1$; it does not remove eigenvalue $-1$ or other eigenvalues of modulus one. In probabilistic language the switching chain has [[状态周期|period]] $2$: returns to the starting state occur only at positive even times. It is also [[不可约链|irreducible]], since each state can reach the other. Thus even finite irreducibility does not by itself supply convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因子 $(-1)^k$ 只改变偏离的符号，不缩小偏离的大小。除非 $a=1/2$，否则偏离不会衰减。[[Markov稳态唯一判据|稳态唯一性]]限制的是特征值 $1$ 的固定空间，没有排除 $-1$ 或其他模为一的特征值。用概率语言说，这条交换链的[[状态周期|周期]]为 $2$：只有在正偶数时刻才能回到出发状态。它还是[[不可约链|不可约的]]，因为两个状态能互相到达。因此，有限不可约本身也没有提供收敛保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The extra condition needed for convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*收敛还需要什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|time-homogeneous Markov chain]], using the same matrix at every step, with a unique stationary distribution $\pi$, convergence $P^kp_0\to\pi$ for every initial probability column holds exactly when **every eigenvalue other than $1$ has modulus strictly less than one**. The modulus is the absolute value for a real eigenvalue and the distance from zero for a complex one. The [[Markov矩阵幂收敛判据|matrix-power convergence criterion]] explains why this also covers matrices without a full eigenvector basis: the remaining [[Jordan块|Jordan-block]] terms decay, while eigenvalue $1$ is already semisimple for a finite Markov matrix. No diagonalizability assumption is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每一步使用同一矩阵、具有唯一稳态 $\pi$ 的有限[[DTMC时间齐次转移核|时间齐次 Markov 链]]，从每个初始概率列出发都有 $P^kp_0\to\pi$，恰好需要**除 $1$ 外的所有特征值模都严格小于一**。模对实特征值就是绝对值，对复特征值则是到原点的距离。[[Markov矩阵幂收敛判据]]解释了为什么没有完整特征向量基的矩阵也适用：其余 [[Jordan块]]的项仍会衰减，而有限 Markov 矩阵的特征值 $1$ 已经半单。这里不必额外假设可对角化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common probabilistic sufficient condition is [[有限链逐步收敛|finite, irreducible and aperiodic]]. Aperiodic means that the greatest common divisor of the positive return times is $1$. This is a convenient route to the conclusion; irreducibility is not necessary for convergence from every initial distribution to the same stationary distribution. For example, $\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix}$ sends every starting distribution to $(1,0)^T$ in one step despite being reducible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

概率论中常用的充分条件是[[有限链逐步收敛|有限、不可约且非周期]]。非周期是指正返回时刻的最大公约数为 $1$。这是一条方便的保证路线；对于一般链，“全体初态收敛到同一稳态”并不要求不可约。例如 $\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix}$ 虽然可约，却把任何初始分布在一步内送到 $(1,0)^T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see what damping looks like, change the switch: stay with probability $\varepsilon$ and switch with probability $1-\varepsilon$, where $0<\varepsilon<1$. With $I_2$ the [[单位矩阵|identity]], the new matrix is $P_\varepsilon=\varepsilon I_2+(1-\varepsilon)P$. It has the same unique $\pi$, but the deviation eigenvalue is now $2\varepsilon-1$, whose modulus is below one. Hence, for integer $k\ge1$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要看衰减是什么样，可以修改交换规则：以概率 $\varepsilon$ 留下，以概率 $1-\varepsilon$ 交换，其中 $0<\varepsilon<1$。记 $I_2$ 为[[单位矩阵]]，新矩阵是 $P_\varepsilon=\varepsilon I_2+(1-\varepsilon)P$。它保持相同的唯一稳态 $\pi$，但偏离方向的特征值变成 $2\varepsilon-1$，模小于一。因此，对整数 $k\ge1$，

$$
P_\varepsilon^kp_0
=\pi+(2\varepsilon-1)^k(a-\tfrac12)(1,-1)^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\varepsilon=1/4$ and $p_0=(1,0)^T$, the first-state probability is $1,1/4,5/8,7/16,\ldots$: it alternates around $1/2$ with shrinking deviations. At the excluded endpoint $\varepsilon=0$ we recover the original oscillation; at $\varepsilon=1$ the matrix is $I_2$ and uniqueness fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $\varepsilon=1/4$、$p_0=(1,0)^T$ 时，第一状态的概率依次为 $1,1/4,5/8,7/16,\ldots$：它在 $1/2$ 两侧交替，但偏离不断缩小。被排除的端点 $\varepsilon=0$ 恢复原先振荡；$\varepsilon=1$ 则得到 $I_2$，此时稳态不唯一。

> [!note] Averaging changes the question · 取平均是在改变问题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the original switch from $(1,0)^T$, average the first $N\ge1$ distributions: $\bar p_N=N^{-1}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}p_k$. If $N$ is even, this equals $\pi$ exactly. If $N$ is odd, it equals $\pi+(1/(2N),-1/(2N))^T$. Thus $\bar p_N\to\pi$ although $p_k$ does not converge. This is a [[周期链Cesaro平均|Cesàro average of distributions]], not a claim that the unaveraged distributions settle, nor a calculation of an observed sample-path average.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对从 $(1,0)^T$ 开始的原交换链，把前 $N\ge1$ 个分布平均：$\bar p_N=N^{-1}\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}p_k$。若 $N$ 为偶数，结果恰好等于 $\pi$；若 $N$ 为奇数，结果为 $\pi+(1/(2N),-1/(2N))^T$。所以 $\bar p_N\to\pi$，但 $p_k$ 本身不收敛。这是[[周期链Cesaro平均|分布的 Cesàro 平均]]，没有声称未平均的分布已经稳定，也不是在计算一条实际观测样本路径的平均。
