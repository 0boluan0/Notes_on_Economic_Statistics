# 期限与流动性转换在集中撤资时会转化为挤兑火售与系统性反馈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Maturity and liquidity transformation can turn concentrated withdrawals into runs, fire sales, and systemic feedback*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子诊断
> 同一结构在平时提供有价值的流动性，在压力时也会制造脆弱性：客户能迅速撤资，底层资产却不能按面值迅速变现。若出资者担心别人先走，提前撤资可能成为理性反应；中介为筹现金折价出售资产，价格下跌又削弱其他机构的资本和抵押品，触发保证金、进一步出售与信贷收缩。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The same structure that supplies valuable liquidity in normal times creates fragility under stress: customers can withdraw quickly, while underlying assets cannot be realised quickly at par. If funders expect others to leave first, early withdrawal can become individually rational. The intermediary sells assets at distressed prices to raise cash; lower prices then erode other institutions' capital and collateral, triggering margin calls, further sales, and contraction of credit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以把反馈链写成：

$$
\text{撤资或拒绝续作}
\rightarrow \text{现金缺口}
\rightarrow \text{折价出售资产}
\rightarrow \text{市场价格下降}
\rightarrow \text{资本与抵押品价值下降}
\rightarrow \text{更多撤资、去杠杆和信贷收缩}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The feedback chain is:

$$
\text{withdrawal or refusal to roll}
\rightarrow \text{cash shortfall}
\rightarrow \text{distressed asset sales}
\rightarrow \text{lower market prices}
\rightarrow \text{weaker capital and collateral}
\rightarrow \text{further withdrawals, deleveraging, and credit contraction}.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不意味着每一次提款都是挤兑，也不意味着任何期限缺口都会导致危机。风险取决于撤资是否集中、资产折价程度、杠杆、共同持仓、透明度、资本与流动性缓冲，以及可信后备融资。[[协调性挤兑]]隔离“为什么单个存款人会抢跑”的多重均衡机制；[[金融加速器]]另行解释借款人净值与外部融资条件怎样把冲击传到实体经济。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Not every withdrawal is a run, and not every maturity gap causes a crisis. Risk depends on the concentration of withdrawals, liquidation discounts, leverage, common holdings, transparency, capital and liquidity buffers, and credible backup funding. [[协调性挤兑|Coordination runs]] isolates the multiple-equilibrium mechanism behind an individual depositor's incentive to run; [[金融加速器|the financial accelerator]] separately explains how borrower net worth and external financing conditions transmit the shock to the real economy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 哪些环节可以截断反馈

稳定融资和流动性缓冲可减少“资金先走”的现金缺口，资本可吸收火售造成的资产损失，存款保险可降低受保储户抢跑动机，合格的流动性支持可缓解资产尚未到期而现金先到期的时点问题。但这些工具的对象、条件和风险不同；流动性工具不会把信用损失变没。这些制度分工已由 [[流动性与偿付能力]] 和 [[银行危机工具分工]] 单独承担，本卡只保留反馈链本身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stable funding and liquidity buffers reduce cash shortfalls, capital absorbs asset losses, deposit insurance can reduce insured depositors' incentive to run, and eligible liquidity support can bridge a timing mismatch. These tools have different users, conditions, and risks; liquidity cannot erase a credit loss. [[流动性与偿付能力|Liquidity versus solvency]] and [[银行危机工具分工|the division of crisis tools]] own those institutional distinctions, while this atom owns the feedback chain itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么一家机构的被迫售卖会伤害没有向它放款的另一家机构？
>
> **答案：** 若两家持有相似资产，火售价格会成为市场估值并压低另一家的资产和抵押品价值；杠杆、保证金和资本约束随后迫使后者也出售，形成价格外部性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can one institution's forced sale harm another institution that never lent to it?
>
> **Answer:** If both hold similar assets, fire-sale prices can mark down the second institution's assets and collateral. Leverage, margin, and capital constraints can then force it to sell as well, creating a price externality.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Federal Reserve Board, Financial Stability Monitoring](https://www.federalreserve.gov/pubs/feds/2013/201321/index.html)：核对期限转换、批发短期融资、杠杆和共同敞口如何通过火售与负反馈放大冲击。
- [Federal Reserve Board, Financial Stability Regulation](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/tarullo20121010a.htm)：核对影子中介遭撤资后出售长期资产，进而触发价格下跌、保证金和其他投资者去杠杆的链条。
- [Federal Reserve Board, Monetary Policy and Financial Stability, 2 October 2023](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/barr20231002a.htm)：核对中介转换支持信贷却也积累脆弱性，以及这些脆弱性会放大不利冲击。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The Federal Reserve monitoring framework was checked for amplification through maturity transformation, wholesale short-term funding, leverage, common exposures, fire sales, and negative feedback.
- Governor Tarullo's Federal Reserve speech was checked for the chain from shadow-intermediary withdrawals to sales of longer-term assets, price declines, margin calls, and deleveraging by others.
- Governor Barr's Federal Reserve speech was checked for intermediation supporting credit while accumulating vulnerabilities that amplify adverse shocks.
