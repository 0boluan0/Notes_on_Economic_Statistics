# 基金折溢价是份额市价相对于同口径份额净值的偏离
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A fund premium or discount is the deviation of its share price from a comparable per-share NAV*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于有交易市价的基金份额，市价高于[[基金份额净值]]称为溢价，低于净值称为折价。设同一类别、同一计价货币和明确时点口径的每份价格为 $P$、每份净值为 $v>0$，带符号折溢价率为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fund share trades at a premium when its market price exceeds [[基金份额净值|per-share NAV]], and at a discount when the price is lower. For the same share class and currency, with an explicit timing convention, let price be $P$ and positive per-share NAV be $v$. The signed premium/discount rate is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\delta=\frac{P-v}{v}.
$$

例如 $v=10$ 元、$P=10.20$ 元，溢价率是 $2\%$；$P=9.70$ 元时，带符号偏离为 $-3\%$，也可表述为折价幅度 $3\%$。若一处用正数表示折价幅度，另一处用带符号率，比较前须统一符号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With NAV of 10 and price of 10.20, the premium is 2%. A price of 9.70 gives a signed deviation of −3%, or a discount magnitude of 3%. Comparisons must distinguish a positive discount magnitude from the signed convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必须标明 $P$ 是收盘价、买价、卖价还是其他报价，以及 $v$ 是哪次估值。把盘中价格与上一交易日净值相比，所得是这一披露口径的偏离，不能直接声称是此刻可执行的套利收益；底层市场休市、报价滞后或估值不确定性都可能使两者并非同步可交易价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify whether $P$ is a closing price, bid, ask, or another quote, and identify the NAV valuation time. An intraday price compared with the previous day's NAV measures that reporting convention, not an immediately executable arbitrage profit. Closed underlying markets, stale quotations, or valuation uncertainty can prevent the two numbers from representing simultaneous tradable values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

折价也不是安全垫的保证。即使偏离缩小，净值下跌仍可造成亏损：忽略分配和费用，买入时 $v=10,P=9$，后来 $v=8,P=8$，折价消失但份额价格亏损 $1$ 元。[[封闭式基金]]的折价未必有直接赎回渠道来消除；[[ETF]]则须另看[[ETF申赎价格联结|申赎交易的可执行条件]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A discount does not guarantee protection. Even narrowing deviations can accompany a loss: ignoring distributions and fees, buying at NAV 10 and price 9, then exiting at NAV and price both 8, loses 1 despite eliminating the discount. A [[封闭式基金|closed-end fund]] may lack a direct redemption channel to close the gap; an [[ETF]] requires a separate assessment of [[ETF申赎价格联结|executable creation and redemption]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [SEC, Publicly Traded Closed-End Funds, 2020，Paying a Premium or Discount](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/investor-bulletin-publicly-traded-closed-end-funds)：已重开，支持市价与 NAV 的折溢价定义及折价可能持续。公式是该比较的带符号百分比表达，数字例为自拟并复算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The reopened SEC section supports the price–NAV definition and potentially persistent discounts. The formula expresses the comparison as a signed percentage; the numerical examples are independently constructed and recomputed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [SEC, Updated Investor Bulletin: ETFs, 2023，Trading Prices / Bid-Ask Spread](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-24)：已重开，支持日度净值与盘中市价的区别及买卖价差。同步、同类别及可执行价值要求是据这些不同计量口径作出的比较条件，不把公布净值当作兑现保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The reopened ETF bulletin distinguishes daily NAV from intraday prices and explains bid–ask spreads. Timing, class consistency, and executable-value requirements are comparison conditions drawn from these different measures, not a guarantee that published NAV can be realized.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [SEC 2019 ETF 最终规则说明，PDF／印刷第 62–63 页](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2019/33-10695.pdf#page=62)：已实际读取，支持不同时区、低频交易和发布延迟使盘中参考估值失真，以及参与者另行估算资产价值；本卡不把参考净值、正式 NAV 与成交价格混成同一个数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The final-rule explanation, PDF / printed pp. 62–63, was read for stale intraday estimates caused by market timing, infrequent trading, and publication lags, and for participants' separate valuation estimates. Indicative values, official NAV, and trade prices remain distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
