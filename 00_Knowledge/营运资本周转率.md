# 营运资本周转率是营业收入与所声明营运资本占用之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Working capital turnover is revenue divided by the specified working-capital measure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

营运资本周转率为 $S/WC$。必须先声明用[[营运资本|流动资产减流动负债]]，还是只含经营项目的[[经营营运资本]]，以及采用期末还是平均存量；两种WC不能无说明互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Working capital turnover is $S/WC$. State whether WC means [[营运资本|current assets minus current liabilities]] or [[经营营运资本|operating working capital]], and whether stocks are ending or averaged. These definitions cannot be exchanged silently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CPA普通营运资本例 $S=3000,WC=400$，次数7.5，365天下折算48.6667天。该例的经营营运资本却为472，若改用此分母，次数约6.3559；差额不是计算失误，而是融资与经营项目范围不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CPA's standard working-capital example has $S=3000,WC=400$, giving turnover of 7.5 and 48.6667 implied days. Its operating working capital is 472, giving about 6.3559 instead. The difference reflects the treatment of financing and operating items, not arithmetic error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

WC接近零会使比率剧烈放大；WC为负时可算出负次数，却不能按“越高越好”或真实循环天数理解。分子、分母变化都要回到经营现金时序解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Near-zero WC magnifies the ratio; negative WC can produce negative turnover without the usual higher-is-better or literal-cycle interpretation. Interpret changes in both numerator and denominator through operating cash timing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
