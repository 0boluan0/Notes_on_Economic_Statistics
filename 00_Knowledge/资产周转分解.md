# 共用收入分母的资产收入比与折算周转天数可以加总而周转次数不能直接加总
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Asset-to-revenue ratios and implied turnover days add on a common revenue base; turnover multiples do not add directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若总资产由不重叠、完整且同口径的项目构成，$A=\sum_i A_i$，并共用收入 $S\ne0$ 与期间长度 $D$，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If assets comprise exhaustive, non-overlapping components on a consistent basis, $A=\sum_i A_i$, using common nonzero revenue $S$ and period length $D$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac AS=\sum_i\frac{A_i}{S},\qquad\frac{DA}{S}=\sum_i\frac{DA_i}{S}.$$

反之，$S/(\sum_iA_i)$ 一般不等于 $\sum_i S/A_i$。例如收入3000、流动资产700、非流动资产1300，总次数1.5，不等于4.2857+2.3077；但365天口径下243.3333=85.1667+158.1667。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By contrast, $S/(\sum_iA_i)$ generally differs from $\sum_i S/A_i$. Revenue of 3,000, current assets of 700, and noncurrent assets of 1,300 give total turnover of 1.5, not 4.2857+2.3077. Yet implied days satisfy 243.3333=85.1667+158.1667.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此可用各项目资产/收入比的跨期变化解释总变化。不得把总额和其组成重复相加，也不得把库存成本天数与其他收入天数相加；[[存货周转的收入与成本口径]]必须统一。这里是金额比例恒等式，不是把各资产实际变现等待时间相加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changes in each component's assets/revenue can therefore explain the total change. Do not double-count totals and components or combine cost-based inventory days with revenue-based days; align [[存货周转的收入与成本口径|inventory numerator conventions]]. This is an amount-ratio identity, not a sum of actual liquidation waiting times.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
