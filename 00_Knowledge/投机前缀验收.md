# 投机解码只保留首个拒绝之前的候选，并在该位置修正或在全接收后追加一个 token

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Speculative decoding keeps candidates only up to the first rejection, then corrects that position or adds one token after full acceptance.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在严格单草稿链的 [[投机解码]]中，目标可以并行计算候选前缀的概率，输出却只能沿真实前缀顺序提交。首个被拒绝的候选及其后续全部丢弃，再在拒绝位置抽一个修正 token；如果全部候选被接受，就从完整候选前缀的目标分布再抽一个额外 token。[[投机采样修正]]给出每个位置的正确验收和修正规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In strict single-draft-chain [[投机解码|speculative decoding]], the target may evaluate candidate-prefix probabilities in parallel, but outputs must be committed in real-prefix order. The first rejected candidate and every later candidate are discarded, then one correction token is sampled at the rejected position. If all candidates are accepted, one additional token is sampled from the target distribution after the complete candidate prefix. [[投机采样修正|Speculative sampling correction]] supplies the valid per-position acceptance and correction rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一轮如何提交

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How a round commits tokens.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $h$ 为已提交前缀，$\gamma$ 为候选数。草稿按自身条件分布依次抽取 $x_1,\ldots,x_\gamma$；目标计算同一组前缀下的概率，并多计算完整候选前缀后的一个分布：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $h$ be the committed prefix and $\gamma$ the candidate count. The draft sequentially draws $x_1,\ldots,x_\gamma$ from its own conditionals. The target evaluates the same prefixes and one extra distribution after the complete candidate prefix:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
q_i(v)=q(v\mid h,x_1,\ldots,x_{i-1}),\quad x_i\sim q_i,
\qquad i=1,\ldots,\gamma,
$$
$$
p_i(v)=p(v\mid h,x_1,\ldots,x_{i-1}),
\qquad i=1,\ldots,\gamma+1.
$$

从第一个候选起，用独立于草稿抽样、且彼此独立的 $U_i\sim\operatorname{Uniform}(0,1)$ 检查 $U_i\le\min(1,p_i(x_i)/q_i(x_i))$。若首个拒绝在位置 $j$，保留 $x_1,\ldots,x_{j-1}$，并抽取

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Starting with the first candidate, use $U_i\sim\operatorname{Uniform}(0,1)$ independent of the draft draws and of one another to check $U_i\le\min(1,p_i(x_i)/q_i(x_i))$. If the first rejection occurs at position $j$, retain $x_1,\ldots,x_{j-1}$ and draw
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
y\sim\operatorname{normalize}\bigl((p_j-q_j)_+\bigr).
$$

本轮提交 $x_1,\ldots,x_{j-1},y$。后面的 $p_{j+1},\ldots$ 都以已拒绝的 $x_j$ 为条件，不能继续当作真实输出的分布。如果所有候选均通过，则额外抽 $y\sim p_{\gamma+1}$，提交 $x_1,\ldots,x_\gamma,y$；这时没有拒绝事件，使用完整目标分布而非正残差。额度充足且未遇 EOS 时，连续接受 $n$ 个候选的一轮交付 $n+1$ 个 token。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The round commits $x_1,\ldots,x_{j-1},y$. Later distributions $p_{j+1},\ldots$ condition on the rejected $x_j$ and cannot continue to represent the real output. If every candidate passes, draw an additional $y\sim p_{\gamma+1}$ and commit $x_1,\ldots,x_\gamma,y$; with no rejection event, this uses the full target distribution rather than the positive residual. Given sufficient allowance and no EOS, a round with $n$ consecutively accepted candidates delivers $n+1$ tokens.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 第二个候选被拒绝的算例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An example where the second candidate is rejected.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造词表 $\{A,B,C\}$，草稿提出 A、B、C。第一位置取 $p_1=(.2,.5,.3)$、$q_1=(.5,.3,.2)$。候选 A 的接受率为 $.2/.5=.4$，本次 $U_1=.25$，于是接受 A。在新前缀 $hA$ 下，取 $p_2=(.5,.2,.3)$、$q_2=(.2,.6,.2)$；候选 B 的接受率为 $1/3$，本次 $U_2=.8$，因此拒绝 B。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a constructed vocabulary $\{A,B,C\}$, suppose the draft proposes A, B, C. At the first position, let $p_1=(.2,.5,.3)$ and $q_1=(.5,.3,.2)$. Candidate A has acceptance probability $.2/.5=.4$ and is accepted for this draw $U_1=.25$. At prefix $hA$, let $p_2=(.5,.2,.3)$ and $q_2=(.2,.6,.2)$. Candidate B has acceptance probability $1/3$ and is rejected for this draw $U_2=.8$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拒绝位置的正残差为 $(.3,0,.1)$，归一化后是 $(.75,0,.25)$。假设此次修正抽到 C，则本轮提交 A、C，下一轮从 $hAC$ 继续。原第三候选虽然也叫 C，但它和相应目标概率基于 $hAB$ 生成，不能因为 token 字面相同就保留原来的后续状态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The positive residual at rejection is $(.3,0,.1)$, normalized to $(.75,0,.25)$. If correction draws C, the round commits A, C and the next round starts from $hAC$. Although the original third candidate was also C, it and its target probability were generated under $hAB$; the matching token name does not make the old continuation state valid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一次循环若三个候选 A、B、C 均被接受，就可从 $p(\cdot\mid hABC)$ 再抽一个 token。例如该分布为 $(.1,.7,.2)$，此次抽到 B，便一次交付 A、B、C、B。最后这个 bonus token 来自目标分布；这个例子以“前三项已通过”为条件，并未假定它们总能通过。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In another round, if A, B, C are all accepted, one more token can be drawn from $p(\cdot\mid hABC)$. For example, if that distribution is $(.1,.7,.2)$ and the draw is B, the round delivers A, B, C, B. The bonus token comes from the target distribution. This example conditions on the first three candidates having passed; it does not assume they always pass.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 提交后的状态与停止规则

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*State and stopping rules after commitment.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据 [[KV复用条件]]，拒绝位置及其后面的推测 KV 已不对应真实前缀，应当截断或失效；已接受前缀对应的目标 [[KV cache]] 仍然有效。新采到的修正或 bonus token 在用于产生它的那次 forward 中尚未作为输入，因此其 KV 尚未生成。继续解码时，要将它送入需要沿真实前缀继续运行的模型，不能仅因“token 已输出”就把它算进已有缓存。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the [[KV复用条件|KV reuse conditions]], speculative KV entries at and after rejection no longer correspond to the real prefix and must be truncated or invalidated; the target [[KV cache|KV cache]] for the accepted prefix remains valid. The newly sampled correction or bonus token was not an input to the forward pass that produced it, so its KV entries have not yet been generated. Continued decoding must feed it into models that need to advance along the real prefix; an emitted token does not automatically count as cached state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设输出上限还允许 $R$ 个 token。$R=0$ 时结束，$R=1$ 时可直接做一次目标采样；$R\ge2$ 时使用 $\gamma_{\mathrm{eff}}=\min(\gamma,R-1)$，确保全接受后追加一个 token 仍不超额。也可以从正确生成的流中只取剩余额度允许的前缀，但不能对外提交越界 token。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $R$ tokens remain under the output cap. Stop when $R=0$, use one direct target draw when $R=1$, and for $R\ge2$ set $\gamma_{\mathrm{eff}}=\min(\gamma,R-1)$ so that an additional token after full acceptance still fits. Equivalently, retain only the allowed prefix of a correctly generated stream, without exposing tokens beyond the cap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

EOS 是结束 token。若已接受候选或修正/bonus token 为 EOS，就在该处停止，同轮预计算的 EOS 后内容不再输出。按相同额度和 EOS 规则截断，保持的是基线对应的前缀及停止过程分布；它不表示每个请求必定生成到指定上限。序列保证来自在每个真实前缀上应用单步修正定理，再组合条件概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
EOS is the end token. If an accepted candidate or a correction/bonus token is EOS, stop there and do not emit later content precomputed in that round. Applying the same cap and EOS rules preserves the baseline's corresponding prefix and stopping-process distributions; it does not mean every request reaches the cap. The sequence guarantee follows by applying the one-step correction theorem at each real prefix and composing the conditional probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
