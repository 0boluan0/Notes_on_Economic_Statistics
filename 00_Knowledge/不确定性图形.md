# 不确定性图形必须标明估计对象、区间口径与适用条件；涉及个体散布时还要呈现数据分布，均值置信区间不能代替预测区间或数据范围
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Uncertainty graphics must identify the estimand, interval definition, and conditions; when individual spread matters they must also show the data distribution, and a mean confidence interval cannot replace a prediction interval or data range*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 先解释估计对象与区间；问题涉及个体时再呈现分布
> 误差棒或带状区间首先要说明中心估计什么、区间如何构造及依赖哪些条件。若结论涉及个体散布、分布形状、离群点或模型诊断，还应展示原始点或合适的分布摘要。均值的标准误或置信区间描述估计精度，不描述个体散布；数据范围描述已观测极值，也不等于未来个体的预测区间。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An error bar or ribbon must first identify what the centre estimates, how the interval was constructed, and the conditions it relies on. If the claim concerns individual spread, distribution shape, outliers, or model diagnostics, also show raw observations or a suitable distribution summary. A standard error or confidence interval for a mean describes estimation precision, not individual dispersion. The observed range describes realised extremes, not a prediction interval for a future individual.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三个对象不能混用

设 $Y_1,\ldots,Y_n$ 独立同分布，均值为 $\mu$，方差为 $\sigma^2$。样本均值和样本标准差为

$$
\bar Y=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nY_i,
\qquad
s^2=\frac1{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^n(Y_i-\bar Y)^2.
$$

在正态总体且方差未知的经典模型下：

1. **均值的置信区间**

$$
\bar Y\pm t_{n-1,1-\alpha/2}\frac{s}{\sqrt n}
$$

针对固定但未知的 $\mu$；它的频率学解释是重复抽样覆盖率，见 [[置信区间覆盖率]]。

2. **一个未来独立观测的预测区间**

$$
\bar Y\pm t_{n-1,1-\alpha/2}s\sqrt{1+\frac1n}
$$

针对未来 $Y_{n+1}$。它包含个体变异，通常远宽于均值区间。

3. **已观测数据范围**

$$
[Y_{(1)},Y_{(n)}]=[\min_iY_i,\max_iY_i]
$$

只报告这批样本的极值；其宽度随样本量和尾部偶然性变化，没有自动的 95% 覆盖含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For iid observations from a normal population with unknown variance, the classical confidence interval $\bar Y\pm t s/\sqrt n$ targets the fixed unknown mean and has a repeated-sampling coverage interpretation. The prediction interval $\bar Y\pm ts\sqrt{1+1/n}$ targets a future independent observation and includes individual variation, so it is usually much wider. The observed range $[\min Y_i,\max Y_i]$ reports only realised sample extremes; it changes with sample size and tail luck and carries no automatic 95% meaning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些公式依赖独立性、正态模型及抽样设计。非正态、小样本、聚类、时间依赖、加权、模型拟合或多重比较都可能需要不同方法。本卡的重点不是把一个公式套到所有图上，而是要求图例写清“估计对象 + 区间口径 + 条件”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These formulas rely on independence, a normal model, and the sampling design. Non-normality, small samples, clustering, temporal dependence, weighting, fitted models, or multiplicity may require different methods. The point is not to apply one formula universally but to label every uncertainty display with its estimand, interval construction, and conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 例子：样本量只缩窄均值区间

若 $s=10$ 且近似使用 $1.96$：

- $n=25$ 时，均值 95% 区间的半宽约为 $1.96\times10/5=3.92$；
- $n=100$ 时，半宽约为 $1.96\times10/10=1.96$；
- 但单个观测的典型散布仍约为 $s=10$，并未因为样本量从 25 增至 100 而减半。

因此只画“均值 ± 95% 置信区间”会让大样本组看起来非常稳定，却不能告诉读者组内个体是否高度分散。在这个例子里，问题正涉及个体散布，所以还应叠加原始点、透明抖动、箱线/小提琴或其他合适的分布表示；若点太多，则给出样本量和清楚的分布摘要。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $s=10$ and a rough multiplier of 1.96, increasing $n$ from 25 to 100 reduces the mean-interval half-width from about 3.92 to 1.96. Yet the typical individual spread remains about 10. A plot of means with 95% confidence intervals can therefore make a large group look highly stable without showing heterogeneous individuals. Because this example asks about individual spread, add raw observations, transparent jitter, a box or violin display, or another suitable distribution summary; if there are too many points, report sample size and a clear distribution summary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 图例必须回答什么

一个可审计的不确定性图形应明确：

- 点或线估计的是均值、中位数、比例、分位数、回归预测还是别的对象；
- 区间是标准误、标准差、置信区间、可信区间、预测区间、分位范围还是最小—最大值；
- 置信水平或分位数，例如 95% 或第 10–90 百分位；
- 区间来自解析公式、bootstrap、模型后验还是别的方法，以及关键独立性/聚类条件；
- 必要时报告每组样本量和缺失处理；若结论涉及个体散布、形状、离群点或模型诊断，展示原始分布或合适摘要。

“error bars” 不是一个口径。即使两张图长度相同，`±1 SD`、`±1 SE` 和 `95% CI` 也回答不同问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An auditable uncertainty graphic states the estimand; whether the interval is an SE, SD, confidence interval, credible interval, prediction interval, quantile range, or min–max range; its level or quantiles; and how it was constructed under the relevant dependence assumptions. Report group sample sizes and missing-data handling when material. If the claim concerns individual spread, shape, outliers, or model diagnostics, show raw observations or a suitable distribution summary. “Error bars” is not a definition. Equal-looking bars labelled $\pm1$ SD, $\pm1$ SE, and 95% CI answer different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检
> A 组和 B 组的样本标准差都为 12，样本量分别为 16 和 144。图中只画各组均值及 `±1 SE`。B 组误差棒约是 A 组的几分之一？能否据此说 B 组个体更集中？
>
> **答案：**$SE=s/\sqrt n$，所以 A 组为 $12/4=3$，B 组为 $12/12=1$，B 组误差棒是三分之一。不能据此说 B 组个体更集中，因为两组样本标准差同为 12；变窄的是均值估计的不确定性。图还应展示或说明个体分布，并把误差棒明确标成 `±1 SE`。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Since $SE=s/\sqrt n$, the bars are 3 for group A and 1 for group B, so B's bar is one third as wide. This does not show that B's individuals are more concentrated: both sample standard deviations are 12. Only uncertainty about the group mean is smaller. Show or summarise the individual distribution and label the bars explicitly as $\pm1$ SE.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与原文定位

- Douglas G. Altman and J. Martin Bland (2005), [“Standard deviations and standard errors,” *BMJ* 331:903](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.331.7521.903)：定位标准差描述样本个体变异、标准误描述样本统计量精度的区分。
- Geoff Cumming, Fiona Fidler and David L. Vaux (2007), [“Error bars in experimental biology,” *Journal of Cell Biology* 177(1), pp. 7–11](https://doi.org/10.1083/jcb.200611141)：定位 SD、SE、CI 误差棒含义与图注必须明确口径的说明。
- NIST/SEMATECH, [e-Handbook §1.3.5.2 “Confidence Limits for the Mean”](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda352.htm) 与 [Dataplot “Prediction Limits (mean)”](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman1/auxillar/predlimi.htm)：分别定位未知方差时总体均值的 $t$ 区间，以及一个未来独立观测对应的 $\sqrt{1+1/n}$ 预测因子。
