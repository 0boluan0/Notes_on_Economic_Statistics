# 样本均值方差趋零等价于加权协方差平均趋零
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Vanishing sample-mean variance is equivalent to a vanishing weighted covariance average*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子判据
> 对实值宽平稳序列 $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_{t=1}^nX_t$，
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(\bar X_n)
> =\frac1n\left[\gamma(0)+2\sum_{h=1}^{n-1}\left(1-\frac hn\right)\gamma(h)\right].
> $$
> 因 $E[\bar X_n]=\mu$，样本均值按 [[均方收敛]] 到 $\mu$ 当且仅当这个三角权重协方差平均趋于 0。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a real WSS sequence, the variance of the sample mean is the triangularly weighted average of its autocovariances shown above. Since the sample mean is unbiased for $\mu$, mean-square mean ergodicity is equivalent to this variance tending to zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\sum_{h=-\infty}^{\infty}|\gamma(h)|<\infty$，上式必趋于 0，所以绝对可和是充分条件；它不是必要条件。比如 $\gamma(h)=\cos(\omega_0h)$（$\omega_0\not\equiv0$）不绝对可和，但三角平均仍趋于 0，因而可以对均值遍历。也不能把“相关衰减”误写成独立或 mixing。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Absolute summability of $\gamma$ is sufficient because it bounds the bracketed term, but it is not necessary. For nonzero $\omega_0$, $\gamma(h)=\cos(\omega_0h)$ is not absolutely summable while the triangular average still vanishes. Correlation decay must not be relabelled as independence or mixing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $\sum_h|\gamma(h)|=\infty$ 是否就能否定均方意义的均值遍历？
>
> **答案：** 不能。绝对可和只是一个方便的充分条件；真正的必要充分检验是三角加权协方差平均是否趋于 0。
