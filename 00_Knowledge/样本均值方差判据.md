# Variance criterion for a sample mean
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*样本均值方差判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A long time record can contain many measurements but little new information: neighbouring values may move together. To decide whether its average estimates the population mean accurately, we can calculate the average’s variance, including the dependence between different times. For a wide-sense stationary sequence this calculation simplifies to a weighted sum of autocovariances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一段很长的时间记录可能包含很多测量，却没带来多少新信息，因为相邻数值可能一起变动。要判断其平均值能否准确估计总体均值，可以计算平均值的方差，并把不同时点的依赖一起算进去。对于宽平稳序列，这个计算能化成自协方差的加权和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work with a **real, discrete-time [[宽平稳定义|wide-sense stationary (WSS) sequence]]** $X_t$. This means finite second moments, a common mean $\mu$, and [[自协方差与ACF|autocovariance]] $\gamma(h)=\operatorname{Cov}(X_{t+h},X_t)$ depending only on the lag $h$, not the date $t$. In particular $\gamma(0)=\operatorname{Var}(X_t)$ and $\gamma(-h)=\gamma(h)$. We average the first $n$ values: $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_{t=1}^nX_t$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡讨论**实值、离散时间的[[宽平稳定义|宽平稳序列]]** $X_t$：各项二阶矩有限，均值都是 $\mu$，[[自协方差与ACF|自协方差]] $\gamma(h)=\operatorname{Cov}(X_{t+h},X_t)$ 只依赖间隔 $h$，不依赖日期 $t$。特别地，$\gamma(0)=\operatorname{Var}(X_t)$，且 $\gamma(-h)=\gamma(h)$。取前 $n$ 项的平均 $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_{t=1}^nX_t$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the weights are triangular
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三角权重从哪里来

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[和的方差协方差项|The variance of a sum]] includes every ordered pair of observations. At lag zero there are $n$ pairs. At lag $h=1$ there are $n-1$ pairs in each direction; at lag $h=2$ there are $n-2$, and so on. Grouping by lag gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[和的方差协方差项|和的方差展开]]会包含每一对有序观测。零滞后有 $n$ 对；滞后 $h=1$ 时每个方向各有 $n-1$ 对；滞后 $h=2$ 时各有 $n-2$ 对，依此类推。按滞后分组得到

$$
\begin{aligned}\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_n)&=\frac1{n^2}\sum_{s=1}^n\sum_{t=1}^n\gamma(t-s)\\&=\frac1{n^2}\left[n\gamma(0)+2\sum_{h=1}^{n-1}(n-h)\gamma(h)\right]\\&=\frac1n\left[\gamma(0)+2\sum_{h=1}^{n-1}\left(1-\frac hn\right)\gamma(h)\right].\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The weights $1-h/n$ decrease linearly with the lag, hence “triangular”. For $n=3$ the formula is $[3\gamma(0)+4\gamma(1)+2\gamma(2)]/9$: three diagonal pairs, four neighbouring ordered pairs and two end-to-end pairs. Some autocovariances can be negative, but the complete expression is a variance and is nonnegative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

权重 $1-h/n$ 随滞后线性下降，所以称为“三角权重”。例如 $n=3$ 时为 $[3\gamma(0)+4\gamma(1)+2\gamma(2)]/9$，分别对应三对自身、四对相邻有序观测和两对首尾观测。部分自协方差可以为负，但完整表达式是方差，必定非负。

> [!important] An exact mean-square criterion · 精确的均方判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> By [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]], $E[\bar X_n]=\mu$. Therefore the following are equivalent:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 由[[期望线性性]]，$E[\bar X_n]=\mu$，因此以下三个条件等价：
>
> $$
> \bar X_n\xrightarrow{L^2}\mu\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad E[(\bar X_n-\mu)^2]=\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_n)\to0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\frac1n\left[\gamma(0)+2\sum_{h=1}^{n-1}\left(1-\frac hn\right)\gamma(h)\right]\to0.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is [[均方收敛|mean-square convergence]] of the time average to the population mean, called [[均值遍历|mean ergodicity]] **in the mean-square sense**. It also implies convergence in probability via Chebyshev. The displayed equivalence concerns this specified second-moment setting; it does not assert almost-sure convergence or independence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这就是时间平均对总体均值的[[均方收敛]]，称为**均方意义的[[均值遍历]]**。经 Chebyshev 不等式，它还能推出依概率收敛。上述等价关系属于这个明确的二阶矩设定，不是在断言几乎必然收敛或独立性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Outside this setting, do not forget the target: for any square-integrable estimator $T_n$ and finite constant $\mu$, $E[(T_n-\mu)^2]=\operatorname{Var}(T_n)+(E[T_n]-\mu)^2$. Vanishing variance alone does not remove bias. Nor does general probability convergence alone force second moments to converge; rare large errors can prevent that, as the [[均方收敛|mean-square convergence examples]] explain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

离开这个设定时，不能忘记目标：对任意平方可积估计量 $T_n$ 和有限常数 $\mu$，有 $E[(T_n-\mu)^2]=\operatorname{Var}(T_n)+(E[T_n]-\mu)^2$。仅方差趋零不会消除偏差；一般的依概率收敛也不能单独保证二阶矩收敛，罕见的大误差可能阻止它，见[[均方收敛|均方收敛中的例子]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A convenient sufficient condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 方便使用的充分条件

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the autocovariances are absolutely summable, meaning $A=\sum_{h=-\infty}^{\infty}|\gamma(h)|<\infty$, each triangular weight is between zero and one. Hence $0\le\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_n)\le A/n\to0$. This is a convenient sufficient condition, not a necessary one. Even the weaker condition $\gamma(h)\to0$ as $|h|\to\infty$ suffices: split the sum at a fixed lag; its finite initial part divided by $n$ vanishes, and its remaining part is bounded by an arbitrarily small tail supremum times a bounded total weight.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若自协方差绝对可和，即 $A=\sum_{h=-\infty}^{\infty}|\gamma(h)|<\infty$，由于每个三角权重都在零和一之间，有 $0\le\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_n)\le A/n\to0$。这是方便的充分条件，不是必要条件。更弱的 $\gamma(h)\to0$（$|h|\to\infty$）也足够：在固定滞后处分开求和，有限前段除以 $n$ 后趋零；后段由任意小的尾部上界乘上有界的总权重控制。

> [!example] No decay, but cancellation · 不衰减也能相互抵消
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $U$ and $V$ be independent, each taking $-1$ and $1$ equally likely. For a fixed angle $\omega\notin2\pi\mathbb Z$, define $X_t=U\cos(\omega t)+V\sin(\omega t)$. Its mean is zero and direct covariance expansion gives $\gamma(h)=\cos(\omega h)$. The shift-and-subtract argument for a [[几何和|finite geometric sum]] works for complex numbers too: if $r=e^{i\omega}\ne1$, then $(1-r)\sum_{t=1}^n r^t=r(1-r^n)$. Taking absolute values gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $U,V$ 独立，各自等概率取 $-1$ 和 $1$。固定角度 $\omega\notin2\pi\mathbb Z$，定义 $X_t=U\cos(\omega t)+V\sin(\omega t)$。均值为零，直接展开协方差可得 $\gamma(h)=\cos(\omega h)$。[[几何和|有限几何和]]的错位相减论证对复数也成立：若 $r=e^{i\omega}\ne1$，则 $(1-r)\sum_{t=1}^n r^t=r(1-r^n)$。取绝对值得到
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(\bar X_n)=\frac{\left(\sum_{t=1}^n\cos(\omega t)\right)^2+\left(\sum_{t=1}^n\sin(\omega t)\right)^2}{n^2}=\frac{\sin^2(n\omega/2)}{n^2\sin^2(\omega/2)}\le\frac1{n^2\sin^2(\omega/2)}\to0.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $i^2=-1$ and $e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$ combine the two real sums; the final variance formula is real. In the especially simple case $\omega=\pi$, $X_t=(-1)^tU$, so even-length averages are exactly zero and odd-length averages have variance $1/n^2$. Yet $|\gamma(h)|=1$ for every lag. Thus absolute summability, and even decay of autocovariance, are unnecessary. The exclusion of multiples of $2\pi$ matters: at those frequencies $X_t=U$ is a random constant and averaging does not reduce its variance.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $i^2=-1$，$e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$ 把两个实数和合在一起，最终方差公式仍是实数。特别简单的情况是 $\omega=\pi$：$X_t=(-1)^tU$，偶数长度的平均恰好为零，奇数长度的平均方差为 $1/n^2$。但每个滞后都有 $|\gamma(h)|=1$。所以绝对可和、甚至自协方差衰减都不是必要条件。排除 $2\pi$ 的整数倍很重要：这些频率下 $X_t=U$ 是随机常数，平均不会降低方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The criterion checks how all covariances combine, rather than attaching a label such as “independent” to a decaying curve. For uncorrelated observations it reduces to $\gamma(0)/n$, the stationary special case of the [[有界方差弱大数定律|bounded-variance weak law]]. For a common shock $X_t=Z$ with positive finite variance, every covariance is $\operatorname{Var}(Z)$ and the displayed sum stays equal to that variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个判据检查全部协方差如何合在一起，而不是因为曲线衰减就贴上“独立”等标签。观测不相关时，它退化为 $\gamma(0)/n$，即[[有界方差弱大数定律]]的平稳特例。若共同冲击 $X_t=Z$ 的方差有限且为正，每个协方差都等于 $\operatorname{Var}(Z)$，完整加权和始终等于该方差。
