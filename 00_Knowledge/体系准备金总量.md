# 体系准备金总量由央行资产负债表和非银行央行账户交易决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*System-wide reserve balances change through the central-bank balance sheet and transactions with non-bank central-bank accounts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子不变量
> 银行之间的支付和同业交易通常只能重新分配已有准备金，不能让合并后的银行体系凭空增加或消灭央行负债。体系总准备金改变，需要央行资产负债表操作，或准备金与央行其他负债之间的转换。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Payments and transactions among banks normally redistribute existing reserves; the consolidated banking system cannot create or extinguish a central-bank liability by itself. Aggregate reserve balances change through central-bank balance-sheet operations or conversion between reserves and other central-bank liabilities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Federal Reserve 通过存款机构准备金账户结算的证券买入或向存款机构贷款，在其他条件不变时增加准备金；证券卖出或贷款偿还则减少。财政部从 Treasury General Account 支出会把央行负债从财政存款转为存款机构准备金，收税则反向吸收；银行取得纸币又会把准备金转为 currency。关键是先确定交易是否跨过“银行体系—央行或央行其他账户”边界，以及实际被记入哪一类央行负债。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Federal Reserve securities purchases settled through a depository institution's reserve account, or lending to a depository institution, raise reserves all else equal; securities sales or loan repayment reduce them. Treasury spending from the Treasury General Account shifts a central-bank liability from the Treasury deposit into depository-institution reserves, while tax receipts reverse the flow; banks obtaining notes convert reserves into currency. The diagnostic questions are whether the transaction crosses the boundary between the banking system and the central bank or another central-bank account, and which central-bank liability is actually posted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 单家银行可以借入或流失准备金；这不等于合并体系总量改变。
- 这里的“体系总准备金”特指同一时点、同一统计制度下 reserve-balances 类别内所有合格持有人的余额之和，不是 Federal Reserve 所有存款负债之和，也不是所有支付机构流动资产之和。
- 同业贷款把准备金从贷款行转给借款行，并不由商业银行创造新准备金。
- “只有央行操作影响准备金”过于窄：财政部及其他央行账户与银行体系之间的流量也会改变存款机构准备金，虽然仍在央行负债端重分配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- One bank can borrow or lose reserves without changing the consolidated system total.
- Here “aggregate system reserves” means the sum, at one observation time and under one statistical regime, of balances inside the reserve-balances category across all eligible holders. It is not the sum of all Federal Reserve deposit liabilities or all payment institutions' liquid assets.
- An interbank loan transfers reserves from lender to borrower; a commercial bank does not create new reserves.
- “Only central-bank operations affect reserves” is too narrow: flows between the banking system and Treasury or other central-bank accounts can also change depository-institution reserves by reallocating central-bank liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 甲银行向乙银行支付后，什么证据才能说明体系总准备金真的下降，而不只是转移？
>
> **答案：** 必须找到对应余额转到银行体系之外的央行负债项目、转为通货，或被央行资产出售／贷款偿还吸收；只看到甲行下降不够。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After Bank A pays Bank B, what evidence would show that aggregate reserves truly fell rather than merely moved?
>
> **Answer:** Identify the balance moving to a central-bank liability outside the banking system, converting into currency, or being absorbed by a central-bank asset sale or loan repayment. A fall at Bank A alone is insufficient.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
