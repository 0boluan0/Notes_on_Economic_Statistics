# 通货膨胀是一般价格水平随时间上涨的现象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inflation is an increase in the general price level over time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通货膨胀描述一组广泛商品和服务的价格总体上涨，不要求超过某个“较高幅度”才成立。实际测量使用明确的价格指数 $P_t$；期间通胀率为 $\pi_t=P_t/P_{t-1}-1$，同比与环比对应不同基期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Inflation concerns a broad set of goods and services, not a minimum threshold of “high” price growth. For a specified price index $P_t$, the inflation rate is $\pi_t=P_t/P_{t-1}-1$; year-on-year and period-on-period rates use different comparison dates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CPI 衡量居民消费篮子，GDP 平减指数对应国内产出，二者覆盖、权重和进口处理不同。不能用某一商品涨价证明所有价格普遍上涨，也不能把任意一个指数的变化不加说明地当成所有主体面对的通胀。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CPI measures a consumer basket, while the GDP deflator covers domestic output; their coverage, weights, and treatment of imports differ. One rising price does not establish economy-wide inflation, and one index need not measure every agent's inflation experience.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若消费指数由 100 到 102，该期间通胀率为 2%。相同名义金额能买到的该篮子数量减少，但名义收入若也变化，实际购买力须同时比较收入与价格。后续先区分[[通胀率与价格水平]]，再讨论企业售价、成本和贴现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A consumer index rising from 100 to 102 implies 2% inflation. A fixed nominal amount buys less of that basket; changing nominal income must also be considered when assessing purchasing power. Distinguish [[通胀率与价格水平|inflation rates from price levels]] before analyzing firms' prices, costs, and discounting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
