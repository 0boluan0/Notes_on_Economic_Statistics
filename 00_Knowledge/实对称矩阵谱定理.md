# The spectral theorem for real symmetric matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*实对称矩阵的谱定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix can mix the original coordinates while acting very simply along a different set of directions. The spectral theorem says that a [[实对称矩阵|real symmetric matrix]] always has a full set of mutually perpendicular directions along which it only multiplies by real numbers. Once we express a vector in those directions, applying the matrix becomes a separate scalar multiplication in each coordinate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个矩阵可能混合原来的坐标，却沿另一组方向表现得非常简单。谱定理说，每个[[实对称矩阵]]都有一整组彼此垂直的方向；沿这些方向，它只做实数倍的缩放。把向量改用这些方向表示后，矩阵作用就变成各坐标分别乘一个数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[特征对|eigenvector]] is a nonzero vector $q$ satisfying $Aq=\lambda q$; the eigenvalue $\lambda$ is that directional multiplier. An [[标准正交组|orthonormal family]] consists of perpendicular unit vectors. Calling it a [[基|basis]] adds the essential promise that every input can be expressed in these directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[特征对|特征向量]]是满足 $Aq=\lambda q$ 的非零向量 $q$，特征值 $\lambda$ 是沿它的乘数。[[标准正交组]]由彼此垂直的单位向量组成；称它为[[基]]，还额外保证每个输入都能用这些方向表示。

> [!example] Mixed coordinates, separate directions · 坐标混合，方向分开
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> q_1=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> q_2=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix}.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Direct multiplication gives $Aq_1=3q_1$ and $Aq_2=q_2$. The vectors have length one and dot product zero. Thus, if $x=c_1q_1+c_2q_2$, then $Ax=3c_1q_1+c_2q_2$: the two new coordinates do not mix. For $x=(2,0)^T$, both coefficients are $\sqrt2$, and this produces $Ax=(4,2)^T$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 直接相乘得 $Aq_1=3q_1$、$Aq_2=q_2$。两个向量长度均为一，点积为零。因此若 $x=c_1q_1+c_2q_2$，就有 $Ax=3c_1q_1+c_2q_2$，两个新坐标互不混合。取 $x=(2,0)^T$，两个系数均为 $\sqrt2$，得到 $Ax=(4,2)^T$。

## The matrix form records a change of coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵公式记录换坐标的过程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a real $n\times n$ matrix, with finite $n\ge1$. Symmetry means $A^T=A$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. The theorem gives an [[正交矩阵|orthogonal matrix]] $Q=[q_1\ \cdots\ q_n]$, whose columns form the orthonormal eigenbasis, and a real [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $\Lambda$ such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为实 $n\times n$ 矩阵，$n\ge1$ 且有限。对称指 $A^T=A$，其中 $T$ 为[[矩阵转置|转置]]。定理给出[[正交矩阵]] $Q=[q_1\ \cdots\ q_n]$，其列构成标准正交特征基，以及实[[对角矩阵]] $\Lambda$，满足

$$Q^TQ=QQ^T=I_n,\qquad
\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n),\qquad
A=Q\Lambda Q^T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $I_n$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity]], and $\operatorname{diag}$ places the listed numbers on the diagonal. Read $Ax=Q\Lambda(Q^Tx)$ from right to left: $Q^T$ finds the new coordinates, $\Lambda$ multiplies them separately, and $Q$ converts the result back. The equality $Q^TQ=I_n$ makes $Q^T$ the inverse coordinate change. In the example, $Q=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$ and $\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(3,1)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$I_n$ 是[[单位矩阵]]；$\operatorname{diag}$ 把列出的数放到对角线上。把 $Ax=Q\Lambda(Q^Tx)$ 从右往左读：$Q^T$ 求出新坐标，$\Lambda$ 分别缩放，$Q$ 再换回原坐标。$Q^TQ=I_n$ 保证 $Q^T$ 正是反向换坐标。上例中 $Q=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$，$\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(3,1)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The converse holds too: if a real matrix has this form with orthogonal $Q$ and real diagonal $\Lambda$, then $A^T=Q\Lambda^TQ^T=A$. Thus orthogonal diagonalization characterizes real symmetry. This is stronger than [[可对角化判据|ordinary diagonalizability]], which allows an eigenvector basis that is not perpendicular.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来也成立：若实矩阵具有上述形式，$Q$ 正交、$\Lambda$ 为实对角矩阵，则 $A^T=Q\Lambda^TQ^T=A$。因此，正交对角化恰好刻画实对称性。它比[[可对角化判据|一般的可对角化]]更强；后者允许特征向量基不垂直。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why symmetry supplies enough directions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*对称性为什么能提供足够多的方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, every eigenvalue is real, even if we initially allow a complex eigenvector $v$. Write $v^*=\overline v^{\,T}$ for conjugate transpose. Since $A$ is real symmetric, it is also [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]], so the scalar $v^*Av$ equals its complex conjugate. From $Av=\lambda v$ we obtain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

首先，即使暂时允许复特征向量 $v$，每个特征值仍是实数。记 $v^*=\overline v^{\,T}$ 为共轭转置。实对称 $A$ 也是 [[Hermitian 矩阵]]，因此标量 $v^*Av$ 等于自身的复共轭。由 $Av=\lambda v$ 得

$$\lambda=\frac{v^*Av}{v^*v}\in\mathbb R,\qquad
v^*v=\sum_j|v_j|^2>0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The modulus square $|v_j|^2$ is nonnegative, and the denominator is positive because $v\ne0$. For real eigenvectors $u,v$ with distinct eigenvalues $\lambda,\mu$, symmetry also gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模平方 $|v_j|^2$ 非负，且 $v\ne0$ 保证分母严格正。对分别对应不同特征值 $\lambda,\mu$ 的实特征向量 $u,v$，对称性又给出

$$\lambda u^Tv=(Au)^Tv=u^TAv=\mu u^Tv.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $\lambda-\mu\ne0$, their dot product must be zero. These two observations alone do not yet prove that we have a full basis. The missing step is to find a direction and repeat the argument on all the directions perpendicular to it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $\lambda-\mu\ne0$，两个向量的点积必须为零。但这两条观察还没有证明能找齐一整组基。缺少的一步是：先找一个方向，再对与它垂直的全部方向重复论证。

> [!note]- Completing the existence proof · 补全存在性证明
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[特征多项式底层域|characteristic polynomial over the complex numbers]] has a root by the fundamental theorem of algebra, so an eigenpair exists. Its eigenvalue is real by the argument above. If its eigenvector is $v=a+ib$ with real vectors $a,b$, then $Aa=\lambda a$ and $Ab=\lambda b$; at least one of $a,b$ is nonzero. Normalize that real eigenvector to obtain $q$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 由代数基本定理，[[特征多项式底层域|复数域中的特征多项式]]至少有一个根，所以存在特征对。上面的论证说明其特征值为实数。若特征向量为 $v=a+ib$，$a,b$ 为实向量，则 $Aa=\lambda a$、$Ab=\lambda b$，且二者至少一个非零。把这个实特征向量除以长度，得到单位向量 $q$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Its [[正交补|orthogonal complement]] $q^\perp=\{x:q^Tx=0\}$ contains all remaining perpendicular directions and has dimension $n-1$. For $x\in q^\perp$, $q^TAx=(Aq)^Tx=\lambda q^Tx=0$. Thus $A$ keeps this subspace inside itself. In an orthonormal basis of that subspace, the restricted matrix is again real symmetric. Apply the same argument there. Induction, starting with a one-dimensional scalar, produces $n$ mutually perpendicular unit eigenvectors. This proves completeness without assuming distinct eigenvalues.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[正交补]] $q^\perp=\{x:q^Tx=0\}$ 收集其余全部垂直方向，维数为 $n-1$。对其中的 $x$，有 $q^TAx=(Aq)^Tx=\lambda q^Tx=0$，所以 $A$ 把这个子空间保持在自身内部。用该子空间的标准正交基表示时，限制后的矩阵仍实对称。于是可以在其中重复论证；从一维标量开始归纳，得到 $n$ 个彼此垂直的单位特征向量。这样便在不假设特征值互异的前提下，证明了基的完整性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A repeated eigenvalue therefore causes no shortage of directions. Within its [[特征空间|eigenspace]], linear combinations remain in that same space, and [[Gram-Schmidt正交化|Gram–Schmidt]] turns a basis into an orthonormal one. Arbitrarily selected eigenvectors for the same eigenvalue need not be perpendicular: for $A=I_2$, both $(1,0)^T$ and $(1,1)^T$ are eigenvectors. The theorem says that an orthonormal basis **can be chosen**, not that every choice has that property.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，重特征值不会导致方向不足。在它的[[特征空间]]内，线性组合仍留在同一空间，可以用 [[Gram-Schmidt正交化]]把一组基变成标准正交基。不过，同一特征值下随意选出的特征向量未必垂直：$A=I_2$ 时，$(1,0)^T$ 和 $(1,1)^T$ 都是特征向量。定理说的是**可以选出**标准正交基，并非每种选择都如此。

## The projection form and the limits of the theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*投影形式与适用边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a unit vector $q_i$, the [[正交投影|orthogonal projection]] $q_iq_i^T$ sends $x$ to its component $(q_i^Tx)q_i$. Expanding the matrix product therefore gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对单位向量 $q_i$，[[正交投影]] $q_iq_i^T$ 把 $x$ 变成其沿该方向的分量 $(q_i^Tx)q_i$。展开矩阵乘积便得到

$$A=\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_iq_iq_i^T,\qquad
Ax=\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i(q_i^Tx)q_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each direction contributes its projected component multiplied by its eigenvalue. The eigenvalues may be positive, zero or negative; symmetry alone is not positive definiteness. The [[正定与半正定的谱判据|spectral sign criterion]] supplies that additional classification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个方向先取投影分量，再乘其特征值，最后相加。特征值允许正、零、负；对称本身不等于正定，额外的分类由[[正定与半正定的谱判据]]完成。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nonsymmetric matrix such as $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ has real eigenvalues $1,2$ and independent eigenvectors $(1,0)^T,(1,1)^T$, but these eigendirections are not perpendicular. Real eigenvalues alone therefore do not justify $Q\Lambda Q^T$. Other nonsymmetric matrices may lack a full eigenbasis or have nonreal eigenvalues. The theorem makes its stronger promise because of symmetry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非对称矩阵 $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ 有实特征值 $1,2$ 和线性无关特征向量 $(1,0)^T,(1,1)^T$，但两个特征方向不垂直。因此，只有实特征值不足以使用 $Q\Lambda Q^T$。其他非对称矩阵还可能缺少完整特征基，或有非实特征值。定理之所以能作出更强保证，依靠的是对称性。
