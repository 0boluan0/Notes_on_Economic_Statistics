# Dimension formula for a sum of subspaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*子空间和的维数公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When two subspaces contribute directions to their [[子空间之和|sum]], directions already shared by both must not be counted twice. Their [[子空间的交集|intersection]] contains exactly those shared directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个子空间各自为它们的[[子空间之和|和]]提供方向，其中双方共有的方向不能重复计数。[[子空间的交集|交集]]恰好收集这些共同方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the $xy$-plane $S=\{(x,y,0):x,y\in\mathbb R\}$ and the $xz$-plane $T=\{(x,0,z):x,z\in\mathbb R\}$. Each needs two independent directions. Together they reach every $(x,y,z)$, since $(x,y,z)=(x,y,0)+(0,0,z)$. Their sum therefore has three dimensions, not four: both planes already contain the $x$-axis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $xy$ 平面 $S=\{(x,y,0):x,y\in\mathbb R\}$ 与 $xz$ 平面 $T=\{(x,0,z):x,z\in\mathbb R\}$。每个平面都需要两个独立方向；合起来能到达任意 $(x,y,z)$，因为 $(x,y,z)=(x,y,0)+(0,0,z)$。和空间因此是三维，而非四维：两个平面原本就共享 $x$ 轴。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, let $S,T$ be finite-dimensional subspaces of the same vector space over the same scalar field. With $\dim$ denoting [[维数|the number of vectors in a basis]],
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，设 $S,T$ 是同一数域上、同一向量空间的有限维子空间。$\dim$ 表示[[维数|一组基中向量的数量]]，则

> [!important] Count shared directions once · 共同方向只数一次
> $$
> \dim(S+T)=\dim S+\dim T-\dim(S\cap T).
> $$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $S+T$ contains every $s+t$ with $s\in S,t\in T$. It is generally larger than the union $S\cup T$, which only retains members already in one of the two sets. The union may fail to be a vector space, so the formula cannot simply replace $+$ with $\cup$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $S+T$ 包含全部 $s+t$，其中 $s\in S,t\in T$；它通常比只收集两边已有成员的并集 $S\cup T$ 更大。并集可能不是向量空间，公式中的 $+$ 不能随意换成 $\cup$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why subtract the intersection once?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么交集的维数只减一次？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a [[基|basis]] $w_1,\ldots,w_k$ of $S\cap T$: these are $k$ independent shared directions. Keep them and add enough new independent directions to cover $S$, giving a basis $(w_1,\ldots,w_k,s_1,\ldots,s_a)$ of $S$. Similarly obtain $(w_1,\ldots,w_k,t_1,\ldots,t_b)$ for $T$. Here $a$ and $b$ count the directions added to the shared list on the two sides. Such extensions exist in finite dimensions: add a vector outside the current span until the entire space is covered. Their combined list, with the $w$ vectors included once, spans $S+T$, because it can build both summands in every $s+t$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取交集的一组[[基]] $w_1,\ldots,w_k$，即 $k$ 个独立的共同方向。保留它们，再补足新的独立方向，分别得到 $S$ 的基 $(w_1,\ldots,w_k,s_1,\ldots,s_a)$ 和 $T$ 的基 $(w_1,\ldots,w_k,t_1,\ldots,t_b)$。$a,b$ 分别数两边在共同部分之外补了多少个方向。有限维下可以这样扩充：只要还没张成整个空间，就加入一个当前张成之外的向量。合并两组并只保留一份 $w$，便能构造任意 $s+t$ 的两部分，因此张成 $S+T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To check independence, suppose a combination of the $w,s,t$ vectors is zero. The combination of the $t$ vectors then equals a vector in $S$ and also lies in $T$, hence is in the intersection. That combination can therefore also be expressed using only $w$ vectors. Moving the two expressions to one side gives a zero combination of the $w,t$ basis of $T$, so all $t$ coefficients must vanish. Independence of the $w,s$ basis then forces the remaining coefficients to vanish. The combined basis has $k+a+b=(k+a)+(k+b)-k$ vectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验无关性：假设 $w,s,t$ 的一个组合为零，则其中 $t$ 的组合等于 $S$ 中的向量，同时也属于 $T$，因此属于交集。于是这个组合又能只用 $w$ 表示。把两种表示移到同一边，就得到 $T$ 的 $w,t$ 基的一次零组合，所以所有 $t$ 系数只能为零；再由 $w,s$ 的无关性，其余系数也只能为零。合并后的基有 $k+a+b=(k+a)+(k+b)-k$ 个向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The plane example has $k=1$ shared $x$-direction, $a=1$ extra $y$-direction and $b=1$ extra $z$-direction. Thus the abstract count gives $1+1+1=2+2-1=3$. If the intersection is only $\{0\}$, its basis is empty and $k=0$; the formula still applies. In that case the sum is a [[直和|direct sum]], with unique components from the two subspaces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面的平面例子中，$k=1$ 数共同的 $x$ 方向，$a=1$ 数新增的 $y$ 方向，$b=1$ 数新增的 $z$ 方向，所以计数为 $1+1+1=2+2-1=3$。若交集只有 $\{0\}$，它的基为空、$k=0$，公式照样成立；此时和是[[直和]]，来自两边的分量唯一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real $3\times3$ matrices, the [[实对称矩阵|symmetric space]] has [[对称矩阵的自由参数|$6$ free entries]]: three diagonal entries and three entries above the diagonal, which determine their reflected partners. The upper triangular space also has $6$: the three diagonal and three upper entries, with zeros below. Their dimensions are therefore both $6$. Their [[上三角与对称矩阵的交集|intersection is the diagonal space]] of dimension $3$, so their sum has dimension $6+6-3=9$ and equals the full [[矩阵空间|matrix space]]: a subspace with the same finite dimension as its ambient space must be the whole space. The decomposition into a symmetric part and an upper triangular part need not be unique: a shared diagonal matrix can be shifted from one part to the other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实 $3\times3$ 矩阵，[[实对称矩阵|对称空间]]有 [[对称矩阵的自由参数|$6$ 个自由元素]]：三个对角元与三个上方元素，后者决定镜像位置；上三角空间也有三个对角元和三个上方元素，下方全为零。因此两边维数都是 $6$，[[上三角与对称矩阵的交集|交集是维数为 $3$ 的对角空间]]，因此和的维数为 $6+6-3=9$，恰为全部[[矩阵空间]]：子空间若与所在的有限维空间维数相同，就必是全空间。分解成对称部分与上三角部分未必唯一：公共的对角矩阵可以从一部分移到另一部分。
