# 回归模型设定与函数形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Regression model specification and functional form*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把研究问题翻译成一个可解释的 conditional-mean equation，并检查 variables、transformations、interactions 与 dynamics 是否遗漏或写错。
> **具体锚点：** 工资随 experience 往往先快速增长后趋缓；只放一条直线会把不同经验阶段压成一个平均 slope，而加入 $experience^2$ 后，marginal effect 会随经验变化。
> **核心难点：** 好 fit 不能证明 specification 正确；residual pattern 与 RESET 可以发现某些问题，却不能告诉你唯一应该加入什么。变量选择还必须服从 estimand、时间顺序与 mechanism。
> **为什么重要：** 均值函数若写错，coefficient interpretation、prediction、standard error 和 subsequent tests 都可能针对错误对象。
> **继续：** 先用 [[#从研究问题到 conditional-mean specification]] 明确目标，再看 [[#函数形式不是装饰：它决定 coefficient 的含义]] 与 [[#怎样发现设定不足]]；若问题只是 models 之间怎样选，进入 [[回归模型比较与选择]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Model specification translates a research question into an interpretable conditional-mean equation and checks whether variables, transformations, interactions, or dynamics are missing or misspecified.
> **Concrete anchor:** Wages often rise quickly with experience and then flatten. A straight line compresses all career stages into one average slope, whereas adding $experience^2$ lets the marginal effect vary with experience.
> **Central difficulty:** Good fit cannot prove that a specification is correct. Residual patterns and RESET may reveal some failures but cannot identify the unique missing term. Variable choice must also respect the estimand, temporal order, and mechanism.
> **Why it matters:** If the mean function is wrong, coefficient interpretation, prediction, standard errors, and subsequent tests may all concern the wrong object.
> **Continue with:** Begin by moving [[#从研究问题到 conditional-mean specification|from the research question to a conditional-mean specification]], then study [[#函数形式不是装饰：它决定 coefficient 的含义|how functional form determines coefficient meaning]] and [[#怎样发现设定不足|how to diagnose inadequacy]]. For comparison among already admissible models, continue to [[回归模型比较与选择|regression model comparison and selection]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从研究问题到 conditional-mean specification
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From the research question to a conditional-mean specification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模型设定不是“把能找到的 columns 全放入 software”。先写清 population、outcome、focal variables、需要保持不变的 comparison、time index 与 intended use，再决定一个可解释的 conditional mean：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Model specification is not the act of putting every available column into software. First state the population, outcome, focal variables, comparison to be held fixed, time index, and intended use; then formulate an interpretable conditional mean:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(Y\mid X)=m(X;\beta).
$$

“linear regression”要求对 parameters linear，不要求 $m$ 对原始 predictors 都是一条直线。$Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2X^2+u$ 仍然是 linear-in-parameters model；$\log Y$、$\log X$、splines 和 interactions 也可以通过扩展 design matrix 在 OLS framework 中估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Linear regression requires linearity in the parameters, not a straight-line relation in every raw predictor. The model $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2X^2+u$ is still linear in parameters. Logarithms, splines, and interactions can likewise be represented through an expanded design matrix and estimated within the OLS framework.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

遗漏相关且与已含 X 相关的变量会产生遗漏变量偏差；加入无关变量通常不偏但降低精度。错误函数形式、错误时间顺序、样本选择和测量误差也属于设定问题。设定来自研究问题与数据生成过程，不能靠自动逐步回归完全决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Omitting a relevant variable that is correlated with included $X$ creates omitted-variable bias. Adding an irrelevant variable usually does not create bias, but it reduces precision. An incorrect functional form or temporal ordering, sample selection, and measurement error are also specification problems. Specification follows from the research question and data-generating process; automated stepwise regression cannot determine it completely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不过这些失败不全属于同一层。函数形式错主要是 $E(Y\mid X)$ 的 representation 问题；measurement error、selection 与 simultaneity 可能直接令 regressors 与 structural error 相关，属于 [[内生性、识别、IV、2SLS 与 GMM|identification failure]]；heteroskedasticity 与 serial dependence 则是 conditional variance/covariance 问题。先分层才能选对修复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These failures do not all belong to one layer. An incorrect functional form primarily misrepresents $E(Y\mid X)$. Measurement error, selection, and simultaneity may make regressors correlated with the structural error and therefore create an [[内生性、识别、IV、2SLS 与 GMM|identification failure]]. Heteroskedasticity and serial dependence concern the conditional variance or covariance. The layer must be identified before choosing a remedy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 遗漏、冗余与目标 estimand
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Omission, redundancy, and the target estimand*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑真实 conditional mean 含 $X$ 与 $Z$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the true conditional mean contains $X$ and $Z$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2Z+u,
\qquad E(u\mid X,Z)=0.
$$

若错误地只回归 $Y$ on $X$，simple-regression slope 的 probability limit 可写成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $Y$ is incorrectly regressed on $X$ alone, the probability limit of the simple-regression slope can be written as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{plim}\tilde\beta_1
=\beta_1+\beta_2\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)}{\operatorname{Var}(X)}.
$$

这条 omitted-variable-bias formula 给出两个条件：$Z$ 必须影响 $Y$，且 $Z$ 必须与 $X$ 相关，才会污染 $X$ slope。若目标是 causal $\beta_1$，variables 应依据 confounding paths 与 temporal order 决定，而不是先按 p-value 或 AIC 删除；若目标只是 prediction，遗漏的后果则表现为 generalisation error，而不必等同于 causal bias。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This omitted-variable-bias formula identifies two requirements: $Z$ must affect $Y$, and it must be related to $X$, for the slope on $X$ to be contaminated. If the target is the causal $\beta_1$, variables should be chosen from confounding paths and temporal order rather than deleted by p-value or AIC. For a prediction target, omission instead appears through generalisation error and need not be described as causal bias.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

加入一个真正 irrelevant regressor，在原 assumptions 下不会系统移动 $\hat\beta_1$ 的中心，但会消耗 degrees of freedom，并可能因与 $X$ 共享 variation 而增大 standard error。若它是 exact linear combination，则甚至使 coefficient 不可识别；这属于 [[多重共线性与设计矩阵诊断]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding a truly irrelevant regressor does not systematically move the centre of $\hat\beta_1$ under the original assumptions, but it consumes degrees of freedom and may increase the standard error by sharing variation with $X$. If it is an exact linear combination, the coefficient is not identified at all; see [[多重共线性与设计矩阵诊断|multicollinearity and design-matrix diagnostics]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 函数形式不是装饰：它决定 coefficient 的含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Functional form is not decoration: it determines coefficient meaning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Level 与 log forms
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Level and logarithmic forms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| Model | $\beta_1$ 的近似解释 | 需要注意 |
|---|---|---|
| $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+u$ | $X$ 增 1 单位，conditional mean of $Y$ 增 $\beta_1$ units | slope 被约束为 constant |
| $\log Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+u$ | $X$ 增 1，$Y$ 约变 $100\beta_1\%$ | exact percentage 是 $100(e^{\beta_1}-1)\%$；prediction 回 level 需处理 retransformation |
| $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1\log X+u$ | $X$ 增 1%，$Y$ 约变 $0.01\beta_1$ units | $X$ 必须为正；effect depends on percentage change |
| $\log Y=\beta_0+\beta_1\log X+u$ | $\beta_1$ 是 elasticity | log zero/negative values 没有定义 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Model | Approximate interpretation of $\beta_1$ | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+u$ | A one-unit increase in $X$ raises the conditional mean of $Y$ by $\beta_1$ units | The slope is constrained to be constant |
| $\log Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+u$ | A one-unit increase in $X$ changes $Y$ by approximately $100\beta_1\%$ | The exact percentage is $100(e^{\beta_1}-1)\%$; level prediction requires retransformation care |
| $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1\log X+u$ | A one-percent increase in $X$ changes $Y$ by approximately $0.01\beta_1$ units | $X$ must be positive and the effect is defined for a percentage change |
| $\log Y=\beta_0+\beta_1\log X+u$ | $\beta_1$ is an elasticity | The logarithm is undefined for zero or negative values |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

transforming $Y$ 不只是让 graph 更直；它改变 modeled conditional quantity 与 error structure。例如 $E(\log Y\mid X)$ exponentiate 后一般不等于 $E(Y\mid X)$，因为 $E(e^u\mid X)\neq1$。若最终决策在 level scale 上，应明确预测的是 median-like quantity 还是 retransformed mean，并验证 original-scale loss。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Transforming $Y$ does more than straighten a graph; it changes the modelled conditional quantity and the error structure. Exponentiating $E(\log Y\mid X)$ generally does not yield $E(Y\mid X)$ because $E(e^u\mid X)\neq1$. If decisions are made on the level scale, state whether the target is median-like or a retransformed mean and validate loss on the original scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Polynomials 与 marginal effects
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Polynomials and marginal effects*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 quadratic model
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the quadratic model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E(Y\mid X,Z)=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2X^2+Z'\gamma,
$$

不能把 $\beta_1$ 当作所有 $X$ 上的 effect；marginal effect 是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\beta_1$ is not the effect at every value of $X$. The marginal effect is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\partial E(Y\mid X,Z)}{\partial X}=\beta_1+2\beta_2X.
$$

若 $\beta_2<0$，turning point 是 $-\beta_1/(2\beta_2)$，但只有落在 observed support 且 model 在该处可信时才有实质意义。polynomial hierarchy 通常要求放 $X^2$ 时保留 $X$；center $X-c$ 可让 lower-order coefficient 对应 $X=c$ 处 slope，并缓解由构造产生的 numerical collinearity，但不会增加样本中的独立信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\beta_2<0$, the turning point is $-\beta_1/(2\beta_2)$, but it is substantively meaningful only when it lies in the observed support and the model is credible there. Polynomial hierarchy normally retains $X$ when $X^2$ is included. Centering to $X-c$ makes the lower-order coefficient the slope at $X=c$ and may reduce constructed numerical collinearity, but it does not add independent information to the sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Interactions 改变“保持其他不变”的含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Interactions change the meaning of holding other variables fixed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有 $X\times Z$ 时，$X$ 的 effect 是 $\beta_X+\beta_{XZ}Z$，所以 main effect 只描述 $Z=0$ 的 comparison。若 0 不在 meaningful range，应 center $Z$ 或直接报告几个 substantive values 上的 linear combinations 与 intervals。完整编码、解释和 joint tests 见 [[虚拟变量与交互项]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With an $X\times Z$ term, the effect of $X$ is $\beta_X+\beta_{XZ}Z$, so the main effect refers only to the comparison at $Z=0$. If zero is not meaningful, centre $Z$ or report linear combinations and intervals at substantively relevant values. See [[虚拟变量与交互项|dummy variables and interactions]] for coding, interpretation, and joint tests.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样发现设定不足
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How to detect an inadequate specification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Residual plots 是有方向的诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Residual plots are directional diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OLS sample residuals 与 included regressors orthogonal 是 normal equations 的机械结果，不证明 population conditional mean 正确。应画 residuals against fitted values、每个 focal predictor、time/order 与可能遗漏的 candidate variables：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
OLS sample residuals are orthogonal to included regressors by the normal equations; this mechanical fact does not prove that the population conditional mean is correct. Plot residuals against fitted values, each focal predictor, time or order, and plausible omitted candidate variables:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| Pattern | 首先提示 | 还不能单独证明 |
|---|---|---|
| U-shape / systematic curve | mean function 缺 nonlinear term 或 transformation | 唯一正确函数形式 |
| Funnel / changing spread | heteroskedasticity 或 response scale 问题 | coefficient 已 biased |
| Runs over time | serial dependence、trend 或 dynamics 遗漏 | 具体 lag structure |
| isolated vertical residual | possible outlier | influence；还需结合 leverage |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Pattern | First suggestion | What it cannot establish alone |
|---|---|---|
| A U-shape or systematic curve | A missing nonlinear term or transformation in the mean function | The unique correct functional form |
| A funnel or changing spread | Heteroskedasticity or a response-scale problem | That coefficients are already biased |
| Runs over time | Serial dependence, a trend, or omitted dynamics | The precise lag structure |
| An isolated vertical residual | A possible outlier | Influence, which also requires leverage |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

NIST 与 Penn State 的共同原则是 iterative model building：formulate、estimate、evaluate，再回到 formulation。每次 transformation 后要重新检查所有 assumptions；修复 curvature 的 change 可能同时改变 variance、interpretation 与 extrapolation。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The shared NIST and Penn State principle is iterative model building: formulate, estimate, evaluate, and return to formulation. Every transformation requires a fresh check of all assumptions. A change that repairs curvature may also alter variance, interpretation, and extrapolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### RESET、LM 与 specific alternatives
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*RESET, score tests, and specific alternatives*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

RESET 用拟合值幂等扩展检查某类非线性遗漏，但拒绝只说明当前规格不足，不能定位缺哪一项。LM 检验从受限模型残差检验新增方向。非嵌套模型比较需明确候选理论；检验结果不是机械模型选择器。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
RESET augments the model with powers of fitted values to detect certain omitted nonlinearities, but rejection only indicates that the current specification is inadequate; it does not identify the missing term. An LM test uses residuals from a restricted model to test added directions. Comparing nonnested models requires explicit candidate theories, and a test result is not an automatic model selector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个常见 RESET implementation 在原模型后加入 $\hat y^2,\hat y^3,\ldots$ 并联合检验其 coefficients 为 0。因为这些 powers 汇总了 included regressors 的 nonlinear combinations，拒绝可能来自 missing square、interaction、wrong transformation 或其他 omitted structure。failure to reject 也只说明这组 probes 没发现足够证据，不等于模型已被证明正确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common RESET implementation augments the original model with $\hat y^2,\hat y^3,\ldots$ and jointly tests whether their coefficients are zero. Because these powers summarise nonlinear combinations of included regressors, rejection may reflect a missing square, interaction, incorrect transformation, or another omitted structure. Failure to reject means only that these probes found insufficient evidence; it does not prove the model correct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若已经有明确 alternative，例如“遗漏 $X^2$ 与 $XZ$”，直接加入这些 terms 并做 joint test 通常比 generic RESET 更容易解释。若两个 models nonnested，比较框架应移到 [[回归模型比较与选择]]，不能把普通 nested F formula 硬套上去。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a specific alternative such as omitted $X^2$ and $XZ$ terms is available, adding those terms and testing them jointly is usually easier to interpret than a generic RESET. For nonnested models, move to [[回归模型比较与选择|regression model comparison and selection]] rather than forcing an ordinary nested F formula onto the problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 完整例子：experience 的 concavity 怎样改变问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: how concavity in experience changes the question*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设先估计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the initial model is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\log(wage_i)=\beta_0+\beta_1education_i+\beta_2experience_i+u_i.
$$

residual-versus-experience plot 显示：低 experience residuals 偏正，中段偏负，高段又偏正。这种 systematic curve 表示 constant slope 没捕捉 conditional mean。依据 human-capital intuition，加入 $experience^2$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The residual-versus-experience plot is systematically positive at low experience, negative in the middle, and positive again at high experience. This curvature indicates that a constant slope misses the conditional mean. Guided by human-capital reasoning, add $experience^2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\log(wage_i)
=\beta_0+\beta_1education_i
+\beta_2experience_i+\beta_3experience_i^2+u_i.
$$

现在“多一年 experience”的 approximate percentage effect 是 $100(\beta_2+2\beta_3experience)\%$。若 $\beta_3<0$，return declines with experience；因此不能只报告 $\beta_2$。应联合检验 $\beta_2=\beta_3=0$，画 observed-support 内的 fitted curve 与 confidence band，并重新检查 residuals。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The approximate percentage effect of one more year of experience is now $100(\beta_2+2\beta_3experience)\%$. If $\beta_3<0$, the return declines with experience, so reporting $\beta_2$ alone is wrong. Test $\beta_2=\beta_3=0$ jointly, plot the fitted curve and confidence band over the observed support, and inspect the residuals again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使曲线改善 fit，也不能据此把 education coefficient 解释为 causal return；unobserved ability 仍可能与 education 相关。functional-form repair 解决的是 mean representation，不自动解决 identification。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even if the curve improves fit, this does not turn the education coefficient into a causal return. Unobserved ability may still be related to education. Repairing the functional form addresses mean representation, not identification automatically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 修复顺序与边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Order of repair and scope boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先修数据错误，再回到研究设计和函数形式；必要时报告多规格、非线性或正则化。每次删变量、变换或删点都应说明目标和后果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Correct data errors first, then revisit the research design and functional form. Report alternative specifications, nonlinearities, or regularization when appropriate. Every variable removal, transformation, or observation deletion should state its purpose and consequences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更具体的 sequence 是：确认 coding、units 与 sample construction；写 estimand 和 temporal order；画 raw relationship 与 partial residual patterns；依据 mechanism 提出少量 candidate forms；用 interpretable restrictions、residuals 与 appropriate validation 比较；检查 collinearity 和 influence；最后在 intended scale 上报告 effects。不要在发现一个 diagnostic failure 后同时变换 outcome、删 observations、换 controls，再无法知道哪一步改变了结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A more specific sequence is to verify coding, units, and sample construction; state the estimand and temporal order; inspect raw relationships and partial residual patterns; propose a small set of mechanism-guided forms; compare them with interpretable restrictions, residuals, and appropriate validation; inspect collinearity and influence; and report effects on the intended scale. After one diagnostic failure, do not simultaneously transform the outcome, delete observations, and change controls, because the source of any change then becomes unknowable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 失败做法 | 为什么不够 | 修复 |
|---|---|---|
| 高 $R^2$ 就宣布 specification 正确 | fit 不检验遗漏结构、识别或 residual behaviour | 结合 research design、residuals、tests 与 validation |
| RESET 拒绝后自动加入 $\hat y^2$ 当最终模型 | probe 不等于 substantive variable | 用 theory 和 residual pattern 提出可解释 terms |
| 为让 residuals 漂亮而 log-transform $Y$ | outcome scale 与 estimand 被改变 | 先说明目标量并检查 retransformation |
| 放 $X^2$ 却孤立解释 $\beta_X$ | marginal effect 随 $X$ 变化 | 报告 derivative 与 relevant values |
| 逐步回归决定 causal controls | prediction association 不等于 confounding logic | 先按 temporal/causal structure 定 controls |
| 函数形式修好后就忽略内生性 | mean representation 与 identification 是不同层 | 分别诊断并报告 assumptions |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Failed practice | Why it is insufficient | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| Declare the specification correct because $R^2$ is high | Fit does not test omitted structure, identification, or residual behaviour | Combine research design, residuals, tests, and validation |
| After RESET rejects, add $\hat y^2$ as the final substantive model | A probe is not a substantive variable | Use theory and residual patterns to propose interpretable terms |
| Log-transform $Y$ merely to make residuals look attractive | The outcome scale and estimand change | State the target quantity and check retransformation |
| Include $X^2$ but interpret $\beta_X$ alone | The marginal effect varies with $X$ | Report the derivative at relevant values |
| Let stepwise regression determine causal controls | Predictive association is not confounding logic | Determine controls from temporal and causal structure first |
| Ignore endogeneity after repairing functional form | Mean representation and identification are different layers | Diagnose and report the assumptions separately |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么 quadratic model 仍是 linear regression？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why is a quadratic model still a linear regression?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 因为 model 对 unknown parameters $\beta_0,\beta_1,\beta_2$ 仍是 linear；$X^2$ 只是 design matrix 的另一列。对 raw $X$ 的 conditional mean 可以是 curved。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The model remains linear in the unknown parameters $\beta_0,\beta_1,\beta_2$; $X^2$ is simply another column of the design matrix. The conditional mean may still be curved as a function of raw $X$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### RESET 拒绝后能否知道应该加入哪个变量？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*After RESET rejects, does it reveal which variable should be added?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不能。它提示某类函数形式或遗漏问题，却不提供唯一结构修复；仍需理论和数据生成过程。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. It signals a possible functional-form or omission problem but does not supply a unique structural repair. Theory and the data-generating process are still required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### $\log Y$ model 的 fitted value exponentiate 后，为什么通常不是 $E(Y\mid X)$？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is the exponentiated fitted value from a $\log Y$ model generally not $E(Y\mid X)$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 因为 $Y=e^{X\beta+u}$，所以 $E(Y\mid X)=e^{X\beta}E(e^u\mid X)$；第二个 factor 通常不等于 1。需要 distributional assumption、smearing 或直接在 level scale 验证。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Since $Y=e^{X\beta+u}$, $E(Y\mid X)=e^{X\beta}E(e^u\mid X)$, and the second factor is generally not one. A distributional assumption, a smearing correction, or direct validation on the level scale is needed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 在 quadratic model 中，$\beta_1$ 何时等于 $X$ 的 marginal effect？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a quadratic model, when does $\beta_1$ equal the marginal effect of $X$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 在未中心化的 $X+X^2$ model 中，它是 $X=0$ 时的 marginal effect；一般位置是 $\beta_1+2\beta_2X$。若用 $X-c$，lower-order coefficient 对应 $X=c$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In an uncentred $X+X^2$ model, it is the marginal effect at $X=0$; at a general value the effect is $\beta_1+2\beta_2X$. With $X-c$, the lower-order coefficient refers to $X=c$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### residual-versus-fitted plot 没有明显 pattern，是否证明模型正确？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does a pattern-free residual-versus-fitted plot prove that the model is correct?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不证明。它只是没有暴露该 plot 能看见的明显 mean/variance pattern；omitted confounding、measurement error、selection、局部 misspecification 或样本外 instability 仍可能存在。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. It means only that the plot did not reveal an obvious visible mean or variance pattern. Omitted confounding, measurement error, selection, local misspecification, or out-of-sample instability may still remain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
