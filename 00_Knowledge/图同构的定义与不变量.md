# 图同构是保持邻接关系的顶点双射而不是画法相似
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Graph isomorphism is an adjacency-preserving vertex bijection, not visual resemblance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> simple graphs $G,H$ 之间的 isomorphism 是双射 $f:V(G)\to V(H)$，使所有 $u,v$ 都满足
> $$
> \{u,v\}\in E(G)\iff \{f(u),f(v)\}\in E(H).
> $$
> 两图 isomorphic 是指存在这样的 $f$。它允许重命名与重新画图，但不允许增加、删除或改变邻接关系。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An isomorphism between simple graphs $G$ and $H$ is a bijection $f:V(G)\to V(H)$ such that, for all $u,v$,
> $$
> \{u,v\}\in E(G)\iff \{f(u),f(v)\}\in E(H).
> $$
> The graphs are isomorphic when such an $f$ exists. Vertex names and the drawing may change, but adjacency may not.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

`iff` 的两边都重要：edge 必须映成 edge，non-edge 也必须映成 non-edge。对顶点数相同的 simple graphs，双射且“每条 edge 映成 edge”再结合边数相同可推出反向，但直接使用完整定义更安全，不会把一个 graph 只嵌入另一个 graph 的情形误叫同构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both directions of `iff` matter: edges map to edges and non-edges to non-edges. For equal-size simple graphs, a bijection that maps edges to edges plus equality of edge counts can imply the converse, but using the full definition avoids confusing an embedding into a denser graph with an isomorphism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同构不变量用于排除，不总用于确认
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Isomorphism invariants rule mappings out but may not confirm them*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同构保留 vertex/edge counts、每个对应顶点的 degree、degree multiset、path/cycle 结构、components 与 chromatic number。若其中任一不同，可立即证明 non-isomorphic。反向一般不成立：相同 degree sequence 只说明局部计数相同，不能替代一份完整的 adjacency-preserving bijection。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Isomorphism preserves vertex and edge counts, corresponding degrees, the degree multiset, paths and cycles, components, and chromatic number. Any mismatch proves non-isomorphism. The converse generally fails: an equal degree sequence matches only local counts and does not replace a complete adjacency-preserving bijection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图纸上的顶点坐标、边长、交叉数量与标签字体通常不是 graph structure。两张画得很像的图可能因一条邻接不同而不 isomorphic；同一个 cycle 也能画成圆、星形或弯曲多边形而保持同构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Vertex coordinates, drawn edge lengths, crossing counts, and label typography are usually not graph structure. Two similar-looking drawings may differ by one adjacency and fail to be isomorphic; the same cycle may be drawn as a circle, star-shaped polygon, or curved loop without changing its isomorphism class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 两图都有六个顶点、六条边，degree sequence 都是 $(2,2,2,2,2,2)$。能否推出同构？
>
> **答案：**不能。一个图可以是六边形 $C_6$，另一个可以是两个不相交的 triangles；degree sequence 相同，但前者 connected、后者有两个 components，所以不同构。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Two graphs have six vertices, six edges, and degree sequence $(2,2,2,2,2,2)$. Must they be isomorphic?
>
> **Answer:** No. One may be the cycle $C_6$, while the other is the disjoint union of two triangles. Their degrees agree, but one is connected and the other has two components.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
