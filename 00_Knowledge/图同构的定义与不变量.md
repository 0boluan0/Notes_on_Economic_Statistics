# Graph isomorphism
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*图同构*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two networks can have the same connections even when their vertices have different names and occupy different places on a page. A **graph isomorphism** is a complete matching of their vertices that preserves exactly which pairs are connected by an edge. It tells us that the two graphs have the same structure after relabeling.

Here the main model is a finite [[图类型的建模边界|simple undirected graph]]: an edge joins two different vertices, has no direction, and occurs at most once for each pair. Write $G=(V_G,E_G)$ and $H=(V_H,E_H)$, where $V$ is a vertex set and $E$ is its edge set. The names in $V_G$ and $V_H$ need not coincide.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两张网络可以有同样的连接关系，即使顶点名字不同、在纸上的位置也不同。**图同构**就是把两图顶点完整配对，并且准确保留哪些顶点对之间有边。它表达的是：重新命名以后，两图的结构相同。

本卡主要讨论有限[[图类型的建模边界|简单无向图]]：每条边连接两个不同顶点，没有方向，每对顶点之间至多一条边。记 $G=(V_G,E_G)$、$H=(V_H,E_H)$，其中 $V$ 是顶点集，$E$ 是边集。两个顶点集中的名字不必相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A complete matching of two paths
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把两条路径完整配对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $G$ have vertices $a,b,c,d$ and exactly the edges $ab,bc,cd$. Here $ab$ abbreviates the unordered pair $\{a,b\}$. Let $H$ have vertices $1,2,3,4$ and exactly the edges $\{1,3\},\{1,4\},\{2,4\}$. They are the paths $a-b-c-d$ and $3-1-4-2$. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $G$ 的顶点为 $a,b,c,d$，边恰好是 $ab,bc,cd$；其中 $ab$ 简写无序对 $\{a,b\}$。令 $H$ 的顶点为 $1,2,3,4$，边恰好是 $\{1,3\},\{1,4\},\{2,4\}$。它们分别是路径 $a-b-c-d$ 与 $3-1-4-2$。定义

$$
f(a)=3,\qquad f(b)=1,\qquad f(c)=4,\qquad f(d)=2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every vertex of $G$ receives one image; the four images are different and include every vertex of $H$. This is a **bijection**, meaning every target vertex is matched by exactly one source vertex. Now check all six pairs of different vertices:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$G$ 的每个顶点都有一个像；四个像彼此不同，而且覆盖 $H$ 的全部顶点。这是一个**双射**，意思是每个目标顶点恰好与一个源顶点配对。接着核对全部六对不同顶点：

| Pair in $G$ · $G$ 中的顶点对 | Image pair in $H$ · $H$ 中的像对 | Adjacency · 邻接情况 |
|---|---|---|
| $ab$ | $\{3,1\}$ | Edge in both · 两边都有边 |
| $bc$ | $\{1,4\}$ | Edge in both · 两边都有边 |
| $cd$ | $\{4,2\}$ | Edge in both · 两边都有边 |
| $ac$ | $\{3,4\}$ | Nonedge in both · 两边都无边 |
| $ad$ | $\{3,2\}$ | Nonedge in both · 两边都无边 |
| $bd$ | $\{1,2\}$ | Nonedge in both · 两边都无边 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This matching is an isomorphism. Placing $1,2,3,4$ around a square would not change the answer: their positions do not add edges. The complete matching, rather than a similar-looking drawing, is the certificate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个配对就是同构。即使把 $1,2,3,4$ 画在正方形四个角上，答案也不变，因为位置不会自动增加边。能够证明同构的是这份完整配对，而不是图画看起来相似。

> [!definition] Preserve edges and nonedges · 同时保留有边与无边
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An **isomorphism** from $G$ to $H$ is a bijection $f:V_G\to V_H$ satisfying, for every $u,v\in V_G$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $G$ 到 $H$ 的**同构**是双射 $f:V_G\to V_H$，并且对任意 $u,v\in V_G$ 都满足
>
> $$
> \{u,v\}\in E_G\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> \{f(u),f(v)\}\in E_H.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The graphs are **isomorphic**, written $G\cong H$, when at least one such $f$ exists. The double arrow means “if and only if”: adjacency holds on one side exactly when it holds on the other. For $u=v$, both sides are false in this loopless model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 至少存在一个这样的 $f$ 时，称两图**同构**，记为 $G\cong H$。双箭头表示“当且仅当”：一边有邻接，另一边也恰好有邻接。在这里的无自环模型中，$u=v$ 时两边都为假。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the reverse implication matters

Map the path $a-b-c$ to a triangle on $1,2,3$ using $a\mapsto1$, $b\mapsto2$, $c\mapsto3$. This is a vertex bijection and both path edges become triangle edges. But the nonedge $ac$ becomes the edge $\{1,3\}$. The map does not satisfy the reverse implication, so it is not an isomorphism.

There is a useful finite check: if a vertex bijection sends every edge of $G$ to an edge of $H$ **and the two finite edge sets have equal size**, then the reverse implication follows. Distinct unordered vertex pairs have distinct images under a bijection, so the image edges already fill $E_H$; there is no extra edge left to be the image of a nonedge. Without that edge-count condition, the path-to-triangle example shows the problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么反方向也必须成立*

把路径 $a-b-c$ 映到顶点为 $1,2,3$ 的三角形，令 $a\mapsto1$、$b\mapsto2$、$c\mapsto3$。这是顶点双射，两条路径边也都变成三角形的边。但是原来的非边 $ac$ 变成了边 $\{1,3\}$。它不满足反向蕴含，因此不是同构。

有限情形有一个方便的核对方式：顶点双射把 $G$ 的每条边都送到 $H$ 的边，且**两张图的有限边集大小相同**时，反向蕴含就会成立。双射把不同的无序顶点对送到不同的像对，因此这些像边已经占满 $E_H$，不会再剩一条额外的边作为非边的像。若没有边数相同的条件，路径到三角形的例子就说明了问题。

## Structural checks before searching for a matching
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同构不变量用于排除，不总用于确认

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A candidate matching must send each vertex to one with the same [[度数、入度与出度|degree]], the number of incident edges in this simple graph. Such preserved measurements can reject candidates or show that no matching exists. However, agreeing on selected measurements does not by itself produce an isomorphism. The precise one-way rule, its proof and the six-cycle versus two-triangles example are explained in [[图同构不变量的判别边界|Isomorphism invariant tests]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个候选配对必须把每个顶点送到相同[[度数、入度与出度|度数]]的顶点；在简单图中，度数就是与该点关联的边数。这些保持不变的量可以排除候选，或证明根本不存在同构。但所选测量值相同，本身不会给出同构。准确的单向规则、证明以及六边形与两个三角形的例子，见[[图同构不变量的判别边界]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Which features belong to the graph?

Vertex coordinates, drawn edge lengths, crossings and label typography are not part of an ordinary abstract simple graph. A cycle can be drawn as a polygon, a curved loop or a star-shaped polygon with crossing edge strokes. A crossing creates no new vertex unless the model explicitly places one there. Relabeling also need not be unique: reversing either path in the example gives another valid matching.

For a directed graph, preserve ordered arrows instead of unordered pairs; reversing an arrow is generally a structural change. If parallel edges or loops are allowed, preserve their multiplicities and incidences as well. If weights or colors are part of the object being compared, a weight- or color-preserving isomorphism must preserve them too. An isomorphism of the underlying unweighted graph does not assert equality of those extra data.

The course's basic graph definition uses a nonempty vertex set. If empty graphs are explicitly admitted, the unique empty map makes the two empty graphs isomorphic; an empty graph and a nonempty graph cannot have a vertex bijection. Edgeless graphs with the same finite number of vertices are isomorphic under any vertex bijection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*哪些特征属于图本身？*

普通抽象简单图不把顶点坐标、画出的边长、交叉数量和标签字体作为结构。一个环可以画成多边形、弯曲的闭合线，或者边线相交的星形多边形；除非模型明确在交叉处放置顶点，否则交叉不会产生新顶点。同构配对也不必唯一：把例子中的一条路径反向配对，就能得到另一份合法匹配。

有向图应保留有序箭头，而不是无序顶点对；把一条箭头反向通常会改变结构。允许平行边或自环时，还要保留重数与关联方式。若边权或颜色本来就是比较对象的一部分，那么保持权重或颜色的同构也必须保留这些额外数据；底层无权图同构，并不表示额外数据相同。

课程的基本图定义要求顶点集非空。如果明确允许空图，唯一的空映射使两个空图同构；空图与非空图之间则不可能有顶点双射。顶点数相同的有限无边图，在任意顶点双射下都同构。
