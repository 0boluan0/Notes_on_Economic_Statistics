# Matrix transpose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵转置*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **transpose** reorganises a matrix by turning its rows into columns. It keeps every number, but exchanges the number's row position and column position. For example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**转置**把矩阵的行变成列。每个数本身不变，改变的是位置：原来的行号与列号交换。例如，

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3\\4&5&6\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A^T=\begin{pmatrix}1&4\\2&5\\3&6\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first row $(1,2,3)$ becomes the first column. The second row $(4,5,6)$ becomes the second column. In particular, the $6$ moves from row $2$, column $3$, to row $3$, column $2$. A $2\times3$ array has become a $3\times2$ array. The superscript $T$ names this operation; it is not a numerical exponent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一行 $(1,2,3)$ 变为第一列，第二行 $(4,5,6)$ 变为第二列。具体看数字 $6$：它从第 $2$ 行、第 $3$ 列，移到第 $3$ 行、第 $2$ 列。于是 $2\times3$ 数表变为 $3\times2$ 数表。上标 $T$ 是这个操作的名称，不是数值指数。

> [!important] Exchange the two indices · 交换两个指标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For an $m\times n$ matrix $A=(a_{ij})$, the transpose $A^T$ has size $n\times m$, with
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $m\times n$ 矩阵 $A=(a_{ij})$，转置 $A^T$ 的尺寸为 $n\times m$，并且
>
> $$(A^T)_{ij}=a_{ji},\qquad 1\le i\le n,\quad1\le j\le m.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An entry $a_{ij}$ means row $i$, column $j$. Equivalently, the old entry at $(i,j)$ appears at $(j,i)$ in the new matrix. The sizes $m,n$ are positive integers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $a_{ij}$ 表示第 $i$ 行、第 $j$ 列的元素。等价地，原来位于 $(i,j)$ 的元素会出现在新矩阵的 $(j,i)$ 处；尺寸 $m,n$ 为正整数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What changes, and what stays the same?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*哪些位置改变，哪些不变？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a square matrix, this is reflection across the main diagonal, which runs from the top-left corner to the bottom-right. Diagonal entries stay in place; the entries at $(i,j)$ and $(j,i)$ exchange places. A real square matrix that is unchanged by this reflection is a [[实对称矩阵|real symmetric matrix]]. Rectangular matrices can be transposed too, as the opening example shows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对方阵，这相当于沿左上至右下的主对角线反射：对角元留在原位，$(i,j)$ 与 $(j,i)$ 两处的元素交换。反射后完全不变的实方阵称为[[实对称矩阵]]。矩形矩阵同样能转置，开头的例子就是如此。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Doing the operation twice moves each entry from $(i,j)$ to $(j,i)$ and back again, so $(A^T)^T=A$. A row becomes a column, and a column becomes a row. This is why a column vector is often written compactly as $(x_1,\ldots,x_n)^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

做两次转置，每个元素就从 $(i,j)$ 到 $(j,i)$，再回到原处，因此 $(A^T)^T=A$。行变列，列变行；所以列向量常简写成 $(x_1,\ldots,x_n)^T$。

> [!warning] Transposing does not undo a matrix's action · 转置不是撤销矩阵的作用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An [[逆矩阵|inverse matrix]] undoes a matrix's action through multiplication. Transposition only rearranges entries; it does not require an inverse. For instance, $\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}$ has no inverse, yet its transpose is perfectly well defined and equals itself. Do not replace $A^{-1}$ by $A^T$ without an additional condition that makes them equal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[逆矩阵]]通过乘法撤销原矩阵的作用。转置只重新排列元素，并不要求逆矩阵存在。例如，$\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}$ 没有逆矩阵，却完全可以转置，而且转置等于自身。没有额外条件时，不能把 $A^{-1}$ 换成 $A^T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With complex entries, the ordinary transpose still moves entries without changing their values. Thus $\begin{pmatrix}1&i\end{pmatrix}^T=\begin{pmatrix}1\\i\end{pmatrix}$, where $i^2=-1$. The **conjugate transpose** additionally conjugates every entry, so that same row becomes $\begin{pmatrix}1\\-i\end{pmatrix}$. These operations coincide on real matrices; [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian symmetry]] uses the conjugate transpose. The separate [[乘积的转置|transpose-of-a-product rule]] explains how transposition interacts with multiplication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对复数元素，普通转置仍然只移动位置，不改变元素值。因此 $\begin{pmatrix}1&i\end{pmatrix}^T=\begin{pmatrix}1\\i\end{pmatrix}$，其中 $i^2=-1$。**共轭转置**还会把每个元素取共轭，所以同一行会变成 $\begin{pmatrix}1\\-i\end{pmatrix}$。两种操作对实矩阵相同；[[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian 对称性]]使用共轭转置。转置与乘法如何配合，由[[乘积的转置]]单独说明。
