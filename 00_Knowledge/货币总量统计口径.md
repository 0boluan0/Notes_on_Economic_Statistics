# 货币总量是制度化统计口径而不是自然分类
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Monetary aggregates are institutional statistical measures, not natural kinds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子分类
> M1、M2 等货币总量把若干金融工具按特定统计制度归组。它们服务于监测和分析，但边界由法律、产品设计、报告体系和政策用途决定，不是所有国家和年代都共享的自然分类。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Monetary aggregates such as M1 and M2 group specified financial instruments under a statistical regime. They support monitoring and analysis, but their boundaries depend on law, product design, reporting systems, and policy use rather than a natural classification shared by every country and period.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“monetary aggregate”指一项聚合指标及其成分，不是把不同层级重复相加。在当前美国 H.6 口径中，M1 已包含在 M2 中，因此写成“M1 与 M2 的总和”会重复计算。其他国家或时期仍必须先读发布机构的定义与脚注，再讨论增速、相关性或政策含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A monetary aggregate is one aggregate measure and its components; it is not the sum of overlapping levels. Under the current U.S. H.6 definition, M1 is included in M2, so adding M1 and M2 double-counts the narrower component. For any other country or period, read the issuing agency's definition and footnotes before interpreting growth, correlations, or policy significance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 名称相同不保证定义相同；不同国家都叫 M1，也可能纳入不同工具。
- 一个产品被重新分类可使统计量跳变，而经济主体的资产负债表未发生同额交易。
- 口径越广不表示每个成分都能以相同速度、成本和风险用于支付。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Identical labels do not guarantee identical definitions; two countries may both publish M1 while including different instruments.
- Reclassification can make an aggregate jump without an equal transaction on private balance sheets.
- A broader aggregate does not make every component equally fast, cheap, or safe to use in payment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 在当前美国 H.6 口径中，为什么不能把 M1 和 M2 相加后称为“货币总量”？
>
> **答案：** 该口径下 M2 已包含 M1；二者相加会重复计算 M1，且没有对应的官方统计口径。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under the current U.S. H.6 definition, why is M1 plus M2 not a valid measure of “total money”?
>
> **Answer:** Under that definition, M2 already contains M1, so the sum double-counts M1 and does not correspond to the official statistical measure.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
