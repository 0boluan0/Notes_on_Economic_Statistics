# 平面区域在同平面旋转轴一侧转一周的体积等于面积乘形心路程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A planar region lying on one side of a coplanar axis generates a volume equal to its area times the distance travelled by its centroid in one full revolution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设有界平面区域 $D$ 的面积为 $0<A<\infty$，边界分段光滑，且整个区域位于同平面直线轴的一侧；轴不穿过区域内部。令 $d$ 为区域形心到轴的距离，则区域绕轴旋转一周所生成的几何体积为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let a bounded planar region $D$ have finite positive area and a piecewise smooth boundary, and lie entirely on one side of a coplanar axis that does not pass through its interior. If its centroid is distance $d$ from the axis, the volume generated by one full revolution is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
V=A\,(2\pi d).
$$

这里用的是按面积平均的形心，即均匀薄片的 [[质心]]；实际材料若密度不均匀，不能以其质量质心代替。轴可沿边界接触区域，例如三角形绕一条边旋转。分段光滑的边界本身面积为零，不改变面积矩或该体积公式；区域面积为零时则不使用本定理，因为面积形心的分母为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the area centroid, equivalently the [[质心|centre of mass]] of a uniform lamina. A nonuniform material's mass centre cannot replace it. The axis may touch the boundary, as when a triangle rotates about one side. A piecewise smooth boundary has zero area and does not alter the area moments or formula. A zero-area region is excluded because the centroid's area denominator vanishes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明的核心是 [[圆柱壳法]]。把轴取为 $y$ 轴且区域放在 $x\ge0$，形心距离为 $d=\bar x=A^{-1}\int_Dx\,dA$。每块面积生成的壳层贡献为 $2\pi x\,dA$，没有跨轴的重复覆盖，于是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[圆柱壳法|shell method]] gives the proof. Put the axis on the $y$-axis and the region in $x\ge0$. Its centroid distance is $d=\bar x=A^{-1}\int_Dx\,dA$. Each area element contributes $2\pi x\,dA$, with no overlap from opposite sides of the axis, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
V=2\pi\int_Dx\,dA=2\pi A\bar x=2\pi Ad.
$$

例如顶点为 $(0,0),(r,0),(0,h)$ 的均匀直角三角形，$r,h>0$，面积为 $rh/2$，形心为 $(r/3,h/3)$。绕 $y$ 轴旋转得圆锥，体积为 $(rh/2)(2\pi r/3)=\pi r^2h/3$。若让轴穿过圆盘中心，形心路程会是零而旋转体体积仍为正，说明外轴条件不可删除。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A uniform right triangle with vertices $(0,0),(r,0),(0,h)$, where $r,h>0$, has area $rh/2$ and centroid $(r/3,h/3)$. Rotation about the $y$-axis gives cone volume $(rh/2)(2\pi r/3)=\pi r^2h/3$. An axis through a disk's centre instead gives zero centroid travel but positive solid volume, showing why the external-axis condition is necessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
