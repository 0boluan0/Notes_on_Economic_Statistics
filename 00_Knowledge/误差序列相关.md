# Serial correlation in regression errors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*回归误差的序列相关*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a model predicts monthly sales from information available to it. The **error** is the part of sales left unexplained by that model. Serial correlation means that errors at different dates have nonzero [[协方差|covariance]]: their deviations from their means tend to move together, or in opposite directions. The question is about these remaining errors, not simply whether sales themselves rise or fall in runs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设一个模型根据给定信息预测月销售额，**误差**就是模型没有解释的那部分销售额。序列相关指不同时点的误差具有非零[[协方差]]：它们偏离各自均值的方向倾向于一起变化，或反向变化。问题针对的是这些剩余误差，不只是销售额本身是否连续上涨或下跌。

> [!example] Equal variances, correlated errors · 方差相同，误差仍相关
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Hold the regression design fixed. Let the two errors $u_1,u_2$ have the following joint distribution:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定回归设计，设两个误差 $u_1,u_2$ 的联合分布如下：
>
> | $u_1$ | $u_2$ | Probability / 概率 | $u_1u_2$ |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | $1$ | $1$ | $3/8$ | $1$ |
> | $1$ | $-1$ | $1/8$ | $-1$ |
> | $-1$ | $1$ | $1/8$ | $-1$ |
> | $-1$ | $-1$ | $3/8$ | $1$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each error has mean zero and variance one. The two have the same sign with probability $3/4$, so their product averages to
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个误差的均值为零、方差为一。它们以 $3/4$ 的概率同号，因此乘积的平均值为
>
> $$\operatorname{Cov}(u_1,u_2)=E[u_1u_2]=\frac38-\frac18-\frac18+\frac38=\frac12.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Equal error variances have not prevented serial correlation. Using the [[和的方差协方差项|variance-of-a-sum formula]], the average error has variance
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 误差方差相同，并没有阻止序列相关。按[[和的方差协方差项|和的方差公式]]，平均误差的方差为
>
> $$\operatorname{Var}\!\left(\frac{u_1+u_2}{2}\right)
> =\frac{1+1+2(1/2)}4=\frac34.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Ignoring the covariance would give $1/2$ instead. Replacing $u_2$ by $-u_2$ changes the covariance to $-1/2$ and the average's variance to $1/4$. Correlation can therefore increase or decrease the uncertainty of a particular average; its sign and the weights matter.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 忽略协方差会错算成 $1/2$。若把 $u_2$ 换成 $-u_2$，协方差变成 $-1/2$，平均误差的方差变成 $1/4$。所以相关性对某个平均值的不确定性可以增大也可以减小，必须看相关方向及权重。

## Specify the regression information
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先说明给定了哪些回归信息*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write a time-ordered linear model as $Y_t=x_t^\top\beta+u_t$, where $t$ is the time index, $x_t$ contains the predictors, $\beta$ is the coefficient vector, and $u_t$ is the error. Let $X_{\mathrm{all}}$ denote the full design information. In a [[条件均值与线性投影|conditional-mean interpretation]], assume $E[u_t\mid X_{\mathrm{all}}]=0$. Suppose each error has [[矩存在性的使用边界|finite second moment]], $E[u_t^2]<\infty$, so the conditional covariances exist. Then the absence of serial correlation, relative to that information, means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把按时间排序的线性模型写为 $Y_t=x_t^\top\beta+u_t$：$t$ 是时间下标，$x_t$ 包含解释变量，$\beta$ 是系数向量，$u_t$ 是误差。令 $X_{\mathrm{all}}$ 表示全部设计信息。在[[条件均值与线性投影|条件均值解释]]下，假设 $E[u_t\mid X_{\mathrm{all}}]=0$。再假设每个误差的[[矩存在性的使用边界|二阶矩有限]]，即 $E[u_t^2]<\infty$，从而条件协方差存在。相对于这些信息，没有序列相关意味着

$$\operatorname{Cov}(u_t,u_s\mid X_{\mathrm{all}})=0
\quad\text{almost surely for every }t\ne s.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Serial correlation occurs when this condition fails for some pair of distinct dates. With mean-zero errors, the conditional covariance equals $E[u_tu_s\mid X_{\mathrm{all}}]$. For fixed predictors there is no random design to condition on, and ordinary error covariances describe the same calculation. A condition given only past information is a different condition from one given the full design; this matters especially when predictors include lagged outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若某一对不同日期不满足这个条件，就存在序列相关。误差条件均值为零时，条件协方差就是 $E[u_tu_s\mid X_{\mathrm{all}}]$。若解释变量固定，就没有随机设计需要条件化，用普通误差协方差做同样的计算即可。只给定过去信息与给定全部设计，是不同的条件；解释变量含滞后结果时尤其不能混用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an off-diagonal feature of the error [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]]. [[异方差|Heteroskedasticity]] concerns its diagonal variances; either feature can occur without the other, or both can occur together. Zero serial covariance is also weaker than independent errors: [[零协方差不推独立|nonlinear dependence can remain when covariances are zero]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是误差[[协方差矩阵]]的非对角特征；[[异方差]]关心的是对角方差。两种情况可以分别出现，也可以同时出现。序列协方差为零还弱于误差独立：[[零协方差不推独立|协方差为零时，仍可能有非线性依赖]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One model that carries a shock forward
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一种让冲击延续下去的模型*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an AR(1) error model, a fraction $\rho$ of the previous error is carried into the next one, together with a new shock $\varepsilon_t$. For a fixed design, take
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 AR(1) 误差模型中，上期误差的一部分 $\rho$ 延续到下一期，再加上新的冲击 $\varepsilon_t$。对固定设计，设

$$u_t=\rho u_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t,\qquad t\ge1,\qquad |\rho|<1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the shocks are [[独立同分布|independent and identically distributed (iid)]] with mean zero and variance $\tau^2>0$, and the entire shock sequence is independent of the initial error $u_0$. Set $E[u_0]=0$ and $\operatorname{Var}(u_0)=v=\tau^2/(1-\rho^2)$. These assumptions imply constant mean zero and variance $v$: each step has variance $\rho^2v+\tau^2=v$. The [[相关系数|correlation]] is covariance divided by the two standard deviations. For a lag $h\ge0$ and $t\ge h$, these assumptions give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里新冲击[[独立同分布|独立同分布（iid）]]、均值为零、方差为 $\tau^2>0$，且整个新冲击序列独立于初始误差 $u_0$。取 $E[u_0]=0$，$\operatorname{Var}(u_0)=v=\tau^2/(1-\rho^2)$。这些假设保证均值一直为零、方差一直为 $v$，因为每步的方差是 $\rho^2v+\tau^2=v$。[[相关系数]]是协方差除以两个标准差的乘积。对滞后期数 $h\ge0$ 及 $t\ge h$，这些假设给出

$$\operatorname{Cov}(u_t,u_{t-h})=\rho^h v,\qquad
\operatorname{Corr}(u_t,u_{t-h})=\rho^h.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the covariance mechanism, expand the recursion over $h$ steps. It becomes $\rho^h u_{t-h}$ plus newer shocks independent of $u_{t-h}$. Only the first term contributes to the covariance. With $\rho=1/2$ and $\tau^2=3/4$, the variance is $1$, the lag-one covariance is $1/2$, and the lag-two covariance is $1/4$. A negative $\rho$ makes the covariances alternate in sign across lags; it does not force every realised error to alternate in sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把递推展开 $h$ 步就能看清协方差的来源：$u_t$ 等于 $\rho^h u_{t-h}$，再加上一些独立于 $u_{t-h}$ 的较新冲击，因此只有第一项贡献协方差。取 $\rho=1/2$、$\tau^2=3/4$，方差为 $1$，一阶滞后协方差为 $1/2$，二阶为 $1/4$。负的 $\rho$ 会让不同滞后期的协方差正负交替，并不强迫每次实现的误差都交替变号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This construction is [[宽平稳定义|weakly stationary]]: its mean and variance are constant, and covariance depends only on the lag. Normal distributions are unnecessary for these moment formulas. The initialization matters: starting instead at $u_0=0$ gives $\operatorname{Var}(u_t)=\tau^2\sum_{j=0}^{t-1}\rho^{2j}$ for $t\ge1$, which generally changes during the initial periods. AR(1) and stationarity are examples of additional structure, not requirements in the definition of serial correlation. The [[自协方差与ACF|autocovariance and autocorrelation functions]] organise lag-dependent moments when that structure is appropriate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个构造是[[宽平稳定义|宽平稳]]的：均值与方差恒定，协方差只依赖滞后期数。上述矩公式不需要正态分布。初始化很重要：若改从 $u_0=0$ 开始，则 $t\ge1$ 时 $\operatorname{Var}(u_t)=\tau^2\sum_{j=0}^{t-1}\rho^{2j}$，通常在开始几个时期会变化。AR(1) 与平稳性是可以另加的结构，不是序列相关定义必需的条件；结构适用时，可以用[[自协方差与ACF|自协方差函数与自相关函数]]整理各滞后的矩。

## Errors, fitted residuals, and a persistent outcome
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*误差、拟合残差和持续变化的结果*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A model such as $Y_t=2t+\varepsilon_t$ with independent mean-zero shocks has a rising deterministic trend but no serially correlated errors. Looking at the raw outcome alone does not decide the error question. In data we normally estimate the mean, so we observe fitted residuals $\hat u_t$, not the true errors $u_t$. Fitting itself can create relations among the residuals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $Y_t=2t+\varepsilon_t$，其中冲击独立且均值为零：结果有确定的上升趋势，但误差没有序列相关。只看原始结果，不能回答误差的问题。实际数据通常需要估计均值，因此我们看见的是拟合残差 $\hat u_t$，不是实际误差 $u_t$；拟合本身就会给残差加入相互约束。

> [!note] A two-observation residual calculation · 两个观测的残差计算
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fit an intercept to $Y_1=\mu+u_1$ and $Y_2=\mu+u_2$, where the two errors are independent, mean zero, and have variance one. Subtracting their sample mean gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $Y_1=\mu+u_1$、$Y_2=\mu+u_2$ 拟合一个截距，设两个误差独立、均值为零、方差为一。减去样本均值后，
>
> $$\hat u_1=\frac{u_1-u_2}{2},\qquad
> \hat u_2=-\hat u_1,\qquad
> \operatorname{Cov}(\hat u_1,\hat u_2)=-\frac12.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The residuals are correlated even though the errors are independent. This is a calculation of fitting-induced dependence, not a useful two-observation diagnostic test. [[残差协方差诊断|Residual diagnostics]] must account for how the residuals were produced and for the calibration of any test.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 误差独立，残差却相关。这是在计算拟合引入的依赖，并不是建议用两个观测做有用的诊断检验。[[残差协方差诊断]]必须考虑残差怎样生成，以及检验的校准条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Serial correlation can change the sampling uncertainty of coefficient estimates, as the opening average already illustrates. With a fixed [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full-column-rank design]] and mean-zero errors, it need not destroy [[普通最小二乘|ordinary least squares (OLS)]] unbiasedness; [[OLS一致性条件|consistency still needs separate assumptions]]. The conventional independent-error covariance formula is not generally guaranteed. Choosing an appropriate [[标准误口径匹配|standard-error calculation]] requires a justified dependence structure; even [[HAC协方差|HAC estimation]] needs weak-dependence, moment, and bandwidth conditions rather than allowing arbitrary persistent dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

序列相关可能改变系数估计的抽样不确定性，开头的平均值例子已经说明这一点。在固定[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩设计]]与误差均值为零的条件下，它不必破坏[[普通最小二乘|普通最小二乘（OLS）]]的无偏性；[[OLS一致性条件|一致性仍需另外的假设]]。常规独立误差协方差公式一般不再保证适用。选择合适的[[标准误口径匹配|标准误计算方式]]，必须说明依赖结构；即使 [[HAC协方差|HAC 估计]]也需要弱依赖、矩和带宽条件，不能容纳任意持续依赖。
