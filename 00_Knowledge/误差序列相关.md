# 给定回归信息后，不同时点的误差仍有非零协方差时，误差具有序列相关
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Regression errors are serially correlated when errors at different dates retain nonzero conditional covariance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 在时间有序的回归
> $$Y_t=X_t'\beta+u_t$$
> 中，若某些 $t\ne s$ 满足
> $$\operatorname{Cov}(u_t,u_s\mid X)\ne0,$$
> 误差就具有序列相关，也常被称为误差自相关。它表示一个时期没有被均值方程解释的冲击，会与其他时期的未解释冲击共同变化。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a time-ordered regression, serial correlation means that the unexplained errors at some distinct dates have nonzero conditional covariance. A shock left outside the conditional-mean equation therefore co-moves with unexplained shocks at other dates.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最常见的教学模型是一阶自回归误差：
$$
u_t=\rho u_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t,
\qquad |\rho|<1,
$$
其中新息 $\varepsilon_t$ 跨期不相关且条件均值为零。$\rho>0$ 时，正误差往往接着正误差、负误差往往接着负误差；$\rho<0$ 时，误差更容易交替变号。这个 AR(1) 只是可能的协方差模型，不是“序列相关”的定义本身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common teaching model is the AR(1) error process $u_t=\rho u_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t$. Positive $\rho$ produces runs of errors with the same sign, while negative $\rho$ favours alternation. AR(1) is one possible covariance model, not the definition of serial correlation itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不要把“$Y_t$ 自己有惯性”和“回归误差有序列相关”混为一谈。若均值方程已经正确放入必要的趋势、季节或滞后项，$Y_t$ 可以高度持久而误差近似白噪声；反过来，残差相关常在提醒我们均值方程漏掉了动态结构。它也可能来自持续但不可观测的冲击，此时问题确实留在误差协方差层。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Persistence in the outcome is not the same as serial correlation in regression errors. A correctly specified conditional mean may absorb trend, seasonality, and lagged dynamics even when the outcome is highly persistent. Conversely, residual dependence can expose omitted dynamics, although persistent unobserved shocks may genuinely remain in the error covariance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“自相关”一词还常指原始序列的样本 ACF。这里的对象更窄：它是**回归误差**的跨期协方差。实际数据中只能看见估计残差，因此诊断必须经过[[残差协方差诊断]]这一步，不能声称直接观察到了 $u_t$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The word autocorrelation also describes the ACF of an observed series. Here the object is narrower: cross-time covariance in regression errors. Because only fitted residuals are observed, diagnosis must treat them as generated proxies rather than direct observations of $u_t$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 月销售额本身相关很强，但加入趋势、季节项和必要滞后后，残差近似白噪声。回归误差仍必然序列相关吗？
>
> **答案：** 不必然。结果序列的持久性可由条件均值解释；误差序列相关问的是模型解释之后剩余冲击之间是否还相关。
