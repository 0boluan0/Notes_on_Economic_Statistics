# 节点冲击共同构成平行位移且价格可微时关键利率久期之和等于相同口径的平行曲线久期

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Key-rate durations sum to parallel curve duration under the same convention when their shocks partition a parallel shift and price is differentiable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把曲线用同一组利率坐标 $\mathbf r$ 表示，令 $P(\mathbf r)$ 是固定估值规则下的可微全价函数，$P_0>0$。第 $i$ 个 [[关键利率久期]] 沿方向 $\mathbf b_i$ 定义。如果这些单位冲击加起来，在所有相关曲线坐标上恰好是一单位平行位移，即 $\sum_i\mathbf b_i=\mathbf1$，那么：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Represent the curve in one common rate coordinate vector $\mathbf r$. Let $P(\mathbf r)$ be differentiable dirty price under fixed valuation rules, with $P_0>0$. Define [[关键利率久期|key-rate duration]] $i$ along direction $\mathbf b_i$. If the unit shocks jointly give a unit parallel shift at every relevant curve coordinate, so that $\sum_i\mathbf b_i=\mathbf1$, then:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sum_i KRD_i
=-\frac1{P_0}\sum_i\nabla P(\mathbf r_0)^T\mathbf b_i
=-\frac1{P_0}\nabla P(\mathbf r_0)^T\mathbf1
=D_{\mathrm{parallel}}.
$$

理由是 [[多元链式法则]]：沿方向的价格导数对方向向量是线性的。这就是“partition of unity”在这里的用途，而不是“每张卡都叫久期，所以自然相加”。以分段线性曲线为例，相邻两节点在其间某期限上的插值权重相加为 1；最短与最长期限外还要有一致的端点延伸，不能漏掉价函数仍在使用的期限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[多元链式法则|multivariable chain rule]] makes the directional price derivative linear in the direction vector. That is why a partition of unity matters; the measures do not add merely because they share a name. With piecewise-linear interpolation, the two adjacent node weights sum to 1 between nodes. Endpoint extensions must also cover the maturities still used by the valuation model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小反例：用两项零息支付的价函数 $P=50e^{-r_1}+50e^{-2r_2}$，在 $r_1=r_2=0$ 时 $P_0=100$。完整两节点久期为 0.5、1，平行久期为 1.5；若只报第一节点，它的 0.5 不能代表整体。反过来，冲击不构成平行位移时，某只头寸的数字仍可能偶然相等，但没有上述一般保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a minimal counterexample, let two zero-coupon payments have price $P=50e^{-r_1}+50e^{-2r_2}$. At $r_1=r_2=0$, price is 100, the two complete node durations are 0.5 and 1, and parallel duration is 1.5. Reporting only the first node cannot replace the total. Conversely, numbers may coincide accidentally for a particular position even without a partition; the general guarantee is what fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是相同初始价格与模型下的局部导数恒等式。分别做有限步长中央差分后相加，通常只近似等于一次整体位移的差分，因为多变量非线性可能引入交叉和高阶项。若节点冲击的是平价报价，而整体冲击的是零息率，也已换了方向，不能比较后硬凑相等。加总结果更不自动等于单券 YTM 的 [[修正久期]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an identity between local derivatives evaluated at the same price and model. A sum of finite-bump central differences generally only approximates the finite difference for a simultaneous shift, because nonlinear multivariable pricing can introduce cross and higher-order terms. Bumping par quotes locally but zero rates in the aggregate changes the direction being compared. Nor does the sum automatically equal a bond's YTM-based [[修正久期|modified duration]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
