# 一般政府覆盖政府及受控非市场单位而公共部门还包括公共公司
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
General government covers government and controlled nonmarket units; the public sector additionally includes public corporations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“政府欠了多少”先要回答统计谁。GFS的一般政府包括中央、地方等政府单位、相应社会保障基金及政府控制的非市场非营利机构；公共部门更广，还包括政府控制的公共公司。国有商业银行或其他市场性公共公司，不能仅凭国有身份直接并入一般政府。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before measuring government debt, identify the reporting units. GFS general government includes government units, relevant social security funds and government-controlled nonmarket nonprofit institutions. The public sector also includes public corporations. State ownership alone does not place a commercial bank or market corporation inside general government.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断受控机构是否属于一般政府，要看实际控制与市场／非市场性质，而不是名称中有没有“公司”，也不是是否出现在主预算上。统计一般政府时，属于该范围的预算外单位仍应纳入。法定年度预算的授权范围则是[[政府预算|另一个制度问题]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Classification depends on control and market status, not the corporate label or inclusion in the main budget. Extrabudgetary units belonging to general government remain within that statistical perimeter. The coverage of annual legal authorisation is a [[政府预算|separate budgetary question]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，中央债务100、地方40、国有市场公司30，不能直接说“一般政府债务170”。先排除不属一般政府的30，再用[[财政合并口径]]消除中央与地方之间的债权债务。公共公司的风险仍可通过担保或救助传回政府，应另看[[财政或有负债]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With central debt 100, local debt 40 and public market-corporation debt 30, general government debt is not automatically 170. Establish coverage first, then [[财政合并口径|consolidate]] claims within it. Corporate risks can still reach government through guarantees or support; see [[财政或有负债|contingent fiscal liabilities]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
