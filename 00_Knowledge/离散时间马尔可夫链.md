# 离散时间马尔可夫链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Discrete-time Markov chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 描述每一步状态如何只依赖当前状态而非完整历史，并分析首达、常返和长期分布。
> **具体锚点：** 今天的天气状态决定明天天气概率；若给定今天后，昨天不再增加预测信息，可用一阶 Markov 链。
> **核心难点：** 常返不等于正常返；有限不可约链状态均正常返，但无限状态空间需分别判断。平稳分布存在也不自动保证收敛。
> **为什么重要：** 排队、信用评级、随机算法、PageRank 和离散动态模型都使用转移结构。
> **继续：** 先画沟通类与周期，再求平稳分布；连续等待时间见 [[连续时间马尔可夫链]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** When the next step of a system can be described from its current state alone, transition probabilities can be used to analyse long-run occupancy, first passage, and absorption.
> **Concrete anchor:** Daily weather moves between sunny and rainy states. Once today's state is known, the model assumes that earlier weather adds no further information about tomorrow.
> **Central difficulty:** The existence of a stationary distribution does not guarantee convergence. Irreducibility, periodicity, and recurrence determine long-run behaviour.
> **Why it matters:** Queueing, inventory, reliability, credit migration, randomised algorithms, and MCMC all rely on state-transition structure.
> **Continue with:** Draw the state graph and classify communicating classes before solving for stationary distributions and first-passage quantities. For continuous time, see [[连续时间马尔可夫链|continuous-time Markov chains]].

## Markov 性与转移矩阵
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Markov property and transition matrices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$P(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_n=i,X_{n-1},\ldots)=P(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_n=i)=p_{ij}$。齐次链的 P 不随 n 变。n 步转移由 $P^n$ 给出，Chapman–Kolmogorov 方程把中间时点求和连接起来。矩阵行/列约定必须固定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$P(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_n=i,X_{n-1},\ldots)=P(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_n=i)=p_{ij}$. Under the row-stochastic convention, each row of $P=(p_{ij})$ sums to one, and $n$-step transitions are given by $P^n$. The Markov property depends on how the state is defined. If the current state omits predictive history, enlarge the state instead of asserting memorylessness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Chapman–Kolmogorov 方程 $P^{m+n}=P^mP^n$ 对中间状态求和。矩阵乘法的顺序由行向量或列向量约定决定；写平稳方程前先声明概率分布是行向量 $\pi=\pi P$ 还是列向量 $P\pi=\pi$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Chapman–Kolmogorov equation $P^{m+n}=P^mP^n$ sums over intermediate states. Matrix order depends on whether probability distributions are represented as row or column vectors. Before writing a stationary equation, state whether the convention is $\pi=\pi P$ or $P\pi=\pi$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 沟通类与不可约性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Communicating classes and irreducibility*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

i 可达 j 表示某个 n 使 $p_{ij}^{(n)}>0$；互相可达形成沟通类。不可约链只有一个沟通类。有限链的闭沟通类包含常返状态，暂态状态最终可能离开；分类应从图结构开始而非先解方程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $i\to j$ if state $j$ can eventually be reached from $i$ with positive probability. Mutual accessibility defines communicating classes. An irreducible chain has a single communicating class. Identifying closed and transient classes is more important than immediately solving equations, because probability cannot flow back out of a closed class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 周期、常返与正常返
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Periodicity, recurrence, and positive recurrence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

状态周期是所有返回时间的最大公约数；同一不可约类周期相同。常返表示最终返回概率为 1，正常返还要求平均返回时间有限，零常返则平均返回时间无限。有限不可约链全部正常返，不能把这一结论无条件推广到无限链。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The period of state $i$ is the greatest common divisor of all possible return times, and every state in an irreducible class has the same period. Recurrence means eventual return with probability one; positive recurrence additionally requires a finite mean return time. Every state in a finite irreducible chain is positive recurrent, but countably infinite chains require separate analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

周期性不是“不稳定”。两状态确定性交替链有唯一平稳分布 $(1/2,1/2)$，但从某个确定状态出发的分布在两个端点间循环，不逐点收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Periodicity is not the same as instability. A deterministic two-state alternating chain has the unique stationary distribution $(1/2,1/2)$, yet a distribution started from one definite state oscillates between the endpoints rather than converging pointwise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 平稳分布与长期收敛
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stationary distributions and long-run convergence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平稳分布满足 $\pi=pi P$、$\sum_i\pi_i=1$。不可约正常返链有唯一平稳分布；若再非周期，$P^n$ 的各行收敛到 $\pi$。周期链可有唯一平稳分布却逐步分布振荡，时间平均仍可能收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stationary distribution satisfies $\pi=\pi P$ and $\sum_i\pi_i=1$. A finite irreducible chain has a unique stationary distribution; with aperiodicity added, distributions from arbitrary initial states generally converge to it. For a positive recurrent state, Kac's relation gives $\pi_i=1/E_iT_i^+$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必须区分三件事：方程有解、解唯一、从给定初态收敛。可约链可能有许多平稳分布；周期链可能有唯一解却不逐步收敛；无限链甚至可能无可归一化平稳概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three questions must be separated: does the stationary equation have a solution, is it unique, and does the chain converge to it from a given initial state? A reducible chain may have many stationary distributions; a periodic chain may have a unique one without stepwise convergence; an infinite chain may have no normalisable stationary probability at all.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 首达、吸收与方程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First passage, absorption, and recursive equations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

首达概率和期望首达时间通过“第一步分析”递归：从当前状态先走一步，再对下一状态条件化。吸收链可把暂态块写成 Q，基本矩阵 $(I-Q)^{-1}$ 汇总预期访问次数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First-passage probabilities and expected hitting times use first-step analysis: move one step from $i$ to $j$, then append the unknown quantity starting from $j$. An absorption probability $h$ satisfies boundary values and $h_i=\sum_jp_{ij}h_j$; an expected time $m$ satisfies $m_i=1+\sum_jp_{ij}m_j$. Set boundary conditions before solving the linear system.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 详细平衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Detailed balance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\pi_i p_{ij}=\pi_jp_{ji}$，链在 $\pi$ 下可逆且自动平稳。详细平衡是求平稳分布的充分条件，不是所有平稳链都必须满足。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\pi_ip_{ij}=\pi_jp_{ji}$, the chain is reversible with respect to $\pi$, and summing these equations yields stationarity. Detailed balance is sufficient, not necessary: a non-reversible chain with net circulation may still possess a stationary distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：两状态天气链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: a two-state weather chain*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令状态为晴 S、雨 R，转移矩阵为 $P=\begin{pmatrix}0.8&0.2\\0.4&0.6\end{pmatrix}$。平稳方程给 $0.2\pi_S=0.4\pi_R$ 与 $\pi_S+\pi_R=1$，所以 $\pi=(2/3,1/3)$。矩阵所有元素为正，因此链不可约且非周期，任意初态分布收敛到该长期占比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the states be sunny $S$ and rainy $R$, with $P=\begin{pmatrix}0.8&0.2\\0.4&0.6\end{pmatrix}$. The stationary equations give $0.2\pi_S=0.4\pi_R$ and $\pi_S+\pi_R=1$, so $\pi=(2/3,1/3)$. Every entry is positive, making the chain irreducible and aperiodic; distributions from any initial state converge to these long-run proportions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若将两行改成 $(0,1)$ 和 $(1,0)$，平稳分布仍是 $(1/2,1/2)$，但链周期为 2，展示了“求出 $\pi$”与“收敛到 $\pi$”之间的差别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the rows are changed to $(0,1)$ and $(1,0)$, the stationary distribution remains $(1/2,1/2)$, but the chain has period two. This isolates the difference between solving for $\pi$ and converging to $\pi$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 常返与正常返的区别是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is the difference between recurrence and positive recurrence?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 常返只要求最终返回概率为 1；正常返还要求返回时间期望有限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Recurrence requires eventual return with probability one. Positive recurrence additionally requires a finite expected return time, which is what supports a positive normalisable long-run share.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 链有唯一平稳分布，为什么仍可能不收敛到它？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why might a chain fail to converge even when it has a unique stationary distribution?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 周期性可使分布在几个子类间循环；还需非周期性等条件才有逐步分布收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Periodicity can make the distribution cycle through several phases. A stationary distribution remains unchanged when used as the initial distribution, but arbitrary initial distributions need not approach it step by step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 第一步分析的通用思路是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is the general idea of first-step analysis?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 对下一步状态条件化，把目标量写成一步成本/概率加上从下一状态重新开始的目标量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Condition on the destination of the first transition, express the original quantity as the one-step cost or probability plus the same unknown quantity restarted from the new state, and impose boundary conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
