# 劳动强度与其他价值条件不变时社会生产率提高会降低单位商品价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Higher social productivity lowers unit commodity value when labour intensity and other value conditions remain unchanged*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Marx 的分析中，生产率提高意味着一定时间的[[具体劳动]]能生产更多使用价值。当这种改进成为社会正常生产条件、劳动强度与复杂劳动折算不变，并且其他投入的单位转移价值不变时，每件商品所需的[[社会必要劳动时间]]减少，单位商品价值随之下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Marx's analysis, higher productivity means that a given duration of [[具体劳动|concrete labour]] produces more use-values. When the improvement becomes socially normal, with unchanged labour intensity, skilled-labour conversion and transferred input value per unit, less [[社会必要劳动时间|socially necessary labour time]] is required per commodity, reducing its unit value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个只计算本工序新增劳动的例子：同质简单劳动在相同强度下工作八小时，原来生产八件，现在普遍能够生产十六件。每件分摊本工序劳动从 $8/8=1$ 小时降到 $8/16=0.5$ 小时，全日新增价值仍对应八小时社会劳动。实物数量翻倍与新增价值翻倍是两个不同判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider only the new labour added in one production operation. Eight hours of homogeneous simple labour at unchanged intensity originally produce eight units and now normally produce sixteen. Labour added per unit falls from $8/8=1$ hour to $8/16=0.5$ hour, while the day's new value still corresponds to eight hours of social labour. Doubling physical output and doubling new value are different claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[产品价值分解|完整商品价值]]还含原料和设备耗用等投入所转移的价值。因此，以上半小时是该工序新增部分；若新技术同时改变每件耗用的投入或其价值，必须把这些变化一并计算，才能得到完整单位价值。若只有一家企业率先采用新技术，还须区别它的个别生产条件与尚未改变的社会标准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[产品价值分解|Total commodity value]] also includes value transferred from inputs such as materials and equipment used up. The half-hour above therefore concerns the newly added component. If the technology changes input use or input values per unit, those changes must also be included to determine total unit value. When only one firm adopts an innovation, distinguish its individual conditions from the social standard that has not yet changed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
