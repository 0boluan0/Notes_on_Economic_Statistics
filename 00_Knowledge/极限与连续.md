# 极限与连续
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Limits and continuity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 极限描述输入不断靠近某点时输出的趋势；连续要求这个趋势恰好等于函数在该点的值。
> **具体锚点：** $f(x)=(x^2-1)/(x-1)$ 在 $x=1$ 没定义，但当 $x\to1$ 时极限仍是 2；极限与点值是两件事。
> **核心难点：** 必须区分左右极限、无穷极限与无穷远处极限，并且不能在前提不成立时套用极限运算规则。
> **为什么重要：** 导数、积分、收敛、渐近近似和概率极限都以它为逻辑起点。
> **继续：** 先判断极限是否存在，再检查是否等于点值；要表达局部变化率时进入 [[导数与求导规则|导数与求导规则]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** A limit describes the value approached as the input approaches a point; continuity additionally requires that approached value to equal the function's value there.
> **Concrete anchor:** $f(x)=(x^2-1)/(x-1)$ is undefined at $x=1$, yet its limit as $x\to1$ is $2$. A limit and a point value are distinct objects.
> **Central difficulty:** One-sided limits, infinite limits, and limits at infinity answer different questions. Limit laws may be used only when their hypotheses hold.
> **Why it matters:** Derivatives, integrals, convergence, asymptotic approximation, and probability limits all begin here.
> **Continue with:** Establish existence first and continuity second; then use [[导数与求导规则|derivatives and differentiation rules]] to encode local rates of change.

## 极限与连续
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Limits and continuity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极限问的是靠近一点时的趋势，不要求函数在该点取什么值；连续则要求极限存在且等于函数值。极限定律只有在相关极限存在时才能机械使用。夹逼定理处理被上下界锁住的量；单侧极限用来检查分段函数和边界。可导必连续，但连续未必可导，例如 $|x|$ 在零点连续却有尖点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A limit concerns the trend near a point and does not require any particular function value there. Continuity requires the limit to exist and equal the point value. Limit laws are mechanical only after the relevant limits are known to exist. The squeeze theorem handles quantities trapped between common bounds, and one-sided limits diagnose piecewise functions and boundaries. Differentiability implies continuity, but continuity does not imply differentiability: $|x|$ is continuous at zero but has a corner there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 极限存在的检查顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A diagnostic sequence for limit existence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先尝试直接代入；若出现 $0/0$ 等未定式，再用因式分解、有理化、共同主导项或标准极限改写。分段点必须分别求左右极限；只有两者存在且相等，双侧极限才存在。“值变得很大”要与“趋于正或负无穷”分开，后者是明确的发散方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Try direct substitution first. If it yields an indeterminate form such as $0/0$, rewrite by factorisation, rationalisation, dominant terms, or a standard limit. At a piecewise boundary, compute left and right limits separately; the two-sided limit exists only when both exist and agree. “Becoming large” must also be distinguished from tending specifically to positive or negative infinity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 连续性能推出什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What continuity allows us to conclude*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

函数在 $a$ 连续需要三件事：$f(a)$ 有定义、$\lim_{x\to a}f(x)$ 存在、两者相等。连续函数在区间上保持“不跳过中间值”，因此介值定理能证明方程有根，却不自动给出根的唯一性或计算方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity at $a$ requires three facts: $f(a)$ is defined, $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)$ exists, and the two are equal. A continuous function on an interval cannot skip intermediate values. The intermediate value theorem can therefore establish existence of a root, but it gives neither uniqueness nor a computational procedure by itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：可去间断与跳跃间断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: removable and jump discontinuities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x)=(x^2-1)/(x-1)$，$x\ne1$ 时化简为 $x+1$，所以 $\lim_{x\to1}f(x)=2$。若定义 $f(1)=2$，间断可被“补洞”；若定义为其他值，极限不变但仍不连续。相反，若某分段函数在 $a$ 的左极限为 1、右极限为 3，则不管怎样重定义 $f(a)$ 都无法使其连续。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=(x^2-1)/(x-1)$, cancellation gives $x+1$ whenever $x\ne1$, so $\lim_{x\to1}f(x)=2$. Defining $f(1)=2$ fills the removable hole; assigning another value leaves the limit unchanged but the function discontinuous. By contrast, if a piecewise function has left limit $1$ and right limit $3$ at $a$, no choice of $f(a)$ can restore continuity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 函数在一点连续，能否断定它在那里可导？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does continuity at a point imply differentiability there?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不能。连续排除了跳跃，但没有排除尖点、尖峰或竖直切线；$|x|$ 在 0 点就是反例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> No. Continuity rules out a jump but not a corner, cusp, or vertical tangent; $|x|$ at zero is a counterexample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 $0/0$ 不是极限的答案？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is $0/0$ not an answer to a limit problem?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> $0/0$ 是未定式，只说明直接代入无法决定比值的趋势；化简后的极限可能是有限值、无穷或不存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> $0/0$ is an indeterminate form. It says only that direct substitution does not determine the ratio's behaviour; after rewriting, the limit may be finite, infinite, or nonexistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 使用介值定理证明 $f(x)=0$ 有根时需要哪两项信息？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What two facts are needed to use the intermediate value theorem to prove that $f(x)=0$ has a root?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> $f$ 在一个闭区间 $[a,b]$ 上连续，且 $f(a)$ 与 $f(b)$ 异号或有一个已经为零；这保证 0 是两个端点值之间的中间值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The function must be continuous on $[a,b]$, and the endpoint values must have opposite signs or one must already be zero. Then zero lies between the endpoint values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话区分“极限存在”与“函数连续”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distinguish limit existence from continuity in your own words.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 极限只看附近点怎样走；连续还要求目标点本身有定义，并且点值与附近趋势接上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A limit concerns the nearby trend. Continuity also requires the function to be defined at the target point and for that value to meet the nearby trend without a break.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
