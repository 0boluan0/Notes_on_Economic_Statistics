# Sample covariance matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*样本协方差矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **sample covariance matrix** describes how the variables in a data table vary around their own sample averages. Its diagonal records the spread of each variable; its off-diagonal entries record whether two variables tend to deviate in the same or opposite directions. It is computed from the observed rows, so a new sample generally gives a different matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**样本协方差矩阵**描述数据表中各变量围绕自身样本平均值的变化。对角记录每个变量的离散程度，非对角记录两个变量的偏差倾向于同向还是反向。它由观测行计算，因此换一批样本，结果通常也会改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a real [[多元数据矩阵|data matrix]] with $n\ge2$ rows of observations and $p\ge1$ columns of variables, with a complete finite numerical value in every cell. Write observation $i$ as the column $x_i\in\mathbb R^p$. Its [[样本均值向量|sample mean vector]] is $\bar x=n^{-1}\sum_i x_i$: one average for each column. Subtracting $\bar x$ gives the deviation $d_i=x_i-\bar x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用实数[[多元数据矩阵]]：$n\ge2$ 行观测、$p\ge1$ 列变量，每个单元格都有完整、有限的数值。把第 $i$ 个观测写成列向量 $x_i\in\mathbb R^p$。[[样本均值向量]] $\bar x=n^{-1}\sum_i x_i$ 给出每列的平均值；减去它便得到偏差 $d_i=x_i-\bar x$。

> [!important] The convention used here · 本卡的分母约定
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The observed sample covariance is the $p\times p$ matrix
> $$s=\frac1{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^n d_i d_i^T,\qquad
> s_{jk}=\frac1{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^n(x_{ij}-\bar x_j)(x_{ik}-\bar x_k).$$
> The outer product $d_i d_i^T$ multiplies every coordinate deviation by every other one. The transpose $T$ turns the second column into a row. Thus each observation contributes a whole table of pairwise deviation products.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 样本协方差是上式中的 $p\times p$ 矩阵。外积 $d_i d_i^T$ 把每个坐标偏差与每个其他坐标偏差相乘；转置 $T$ 将第二个列向量变成行。因此每个观测贡献一整张两两偏差乘积表。

> [!example] Three observations, two variables · 三次观测、两个变量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $x_1=(1,2)^T$, $x_2=(2,4)^T$, $x_3=(3,3)^T$, the mean is $(2,3)^T$. The deviations are $(-1,-1)^T,(0,1)^T,(1,0)^T$, so
> $$\sum_i d_i d_i^T=
> \begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix}
> +\begin{bmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}
> +\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}
> =\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix},\qquad
> s=\begin{bmatrix}1&1/2\\1/2&1\end{bmatrix}.$$
> Both sample variances are $1$, and the sample covariance is $1/2$. This positive covariance summarizes same-direction deviations; it does not establish a causal relationship.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 三次观测的均值为 $(2,3)^T$，减去均值得到列出的三个偏差。逐个计算外积、相加，再除以 $n-1=2$，就得到右侧矩阵。两个样本方差均为 $1$，样本协方差为 $1/2$；正协方差概括同向偏移，不证明因果关系。

## Matrix form and entry meanings
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 矩阵写法与元素解释

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X_{\mathrm{data}}$ contain rows $x_i^T$. The [[中心化矩阵|centering matrix]] $H=I_n-\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^T/n$ subtracts the column averages, where $I_n$ is the identity and $\mathbf1_n$ is the column of $n$ ones. Thus $X_c=HX_{\mathrm{data}}$ contains rows $d_i^T$ and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $X_{\mathrm{data}}$ 的各行为 $x_i^T$。[[中心化矩阵]] $H=I_n-\mathbf1_n\mathbf1_n^T/n$ 负责减去各列平均值；$I_n$ 是单位矩阵，$\mathbf1_n$ 是由 $n$ 个一组成的列向量。因此 $X_c=HX_{\mathrm{data}}$ 的各行为 $d_i^T$，并且

$$s=\frac1{n-1}X_c^TX_c.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the same calculation: the $(j,k)$ entry of $X_c^TX_c$ is the dot product of the two deviation columns. Each diagonal of $s$ is a sum of squared deviations divided by $n-1$, hence a sample variance; each off-diagonal of $s$ is a sample covariance, with units equal to the product of the two variables’ units. Symmetry follows because the product is unchanged when $j,k$ are exchanged. The [[外积和的半正定性|sum-of-outer-products theorem]] also makes $s$ positive semidefinite; [[样本协方差秩边界|invertibility needs more]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与逐行累加完全相同：$X_c^TX_c$ 的 $(j,k)$ 元就是两列偏差的点积。对角为平方和，经除法后成为样本方差；非对角为样本协方差，其单位是两变量单位的乘积。交换 $j,k$ 不改变乘积，因此矩阵对称。[[外积和的半正定性]]还说明 $s$ 半正定，但[[样本协方差秩边界|可逆需要额外条件]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every deviation column sums to zero. Using the raw matrix $X_{\mathrm{data}}^TX_{\mathrm{data}}$ omits centering and instead includes the contribution of the mean. Nor is a covariance matrix a [[相关矩阵|correlation matrix]]: correlations additionally divide by both standard deviations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每列偏差之和为零。直接使用原始 $X_{\mathrm{data}}^TX_{\mathrm{data}}$ 会漏掉中心化，把均值的贡献也算进去。协方差矩阵也不是[[相关矩阵]]：相关系数还要除以双方的标准差。

## Before and after observing the sample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*观察样本之前与之后*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before collecting data, model the rows as random vectors $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ and define the random statistic
$$S=\frac1{n-1}\sum_i(X_i-\bar X)(X_i-\bar X)^T,\qquad \bar X=\frac1n\sum_iX_i.$$
In a repeated-sampling question we can take $E[S]$. After substituting the actual observations, $s$ is one fixed realization. This distinction separates both objects from the population [[协方差矩阵|covariance]] $\Sigma$, which is defined by an expectation under the population law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采集数据之前，把各行建模为随机向量 $X_1,\ldots,X_n$，由上式定义随机统计量 $S$。在重复抽样问题中可以讨论 $E[S]$；代入当前观测后，$s$ 就是一个固定实现值。二者还要与总体[[协方差矩阵]] $\Sigma$ 区分：总体协方差由总体概率律下的期望定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The arithmetic definition does not require independent observations or a Gaussian model. To conclude $E[S]=\Sigma$, however, one needs sampling and moment assumptions; the reason for the divisor is in [[样本协方差无偏分母|the unbiased-divisor theorem]]. Another convention divides the same sum by $n$. Its likelihood role and existence conditions belong to [[Gaussian协方差MLE边界|Gaussian covariance estimation]]. With missing entries, pairwise deletion uses different row sets for different entries, so it is not the single complete-data Gram construction above and does not automatically inherit its PSD guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

算术定义不要求观测独立，也不要求 Gaussian 模型。但要断言 $E[S]=\Sigma$，就需要抽样与矩条件；分母的理由见[[样本协方差无偏分母]]。另一个约定将相同外积和除以 $n$，其似然角色与存在条件见[[Gaussian协方差MLE边界]]。存在缺失值时，逐对删除会让不同元素使用不同观测行，因此不再是这里同一份完整数据的 Gram 构造，也不能自动继承其半正定保证。
