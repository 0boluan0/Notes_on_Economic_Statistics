# Multicollinearity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*多重共线性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a wage regression includes age, years of education, and work experience. If experience has been calculated as age minus education minus six, it supplies no new linearly independent column of numbers once the other columns and an intercept are included. The data cannot separately determine all four coefficients. **Multicollinearity** concerns this overlap among the predictor columns: one column is an exact, or close, linear combination of others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设工资回归同时包含年龄、受教育年数和工作经验。如果经验直接按“年龄减教育年数再减六”计算，那么在已有其他列和截距之后，它没有提供新的线性独立数列，数据不能分别确定全部四个系数。**多重共线性**讨论的就是预测列之间这种重叠：某一列等于或接近其他列的线性组合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In [[普通最小二乘|ordinary least squares]], a design matrix $X$ collects the included predictors: $n$ rows represent observations and $p$ columns represent the chosen features, including a column of ones for an intercept. A coefficient vector $\beta$ turns them into fitted values $X\beta$. The question is whether different choices of coefficients can give identical, or very similar, fitted values. It is a question about the whole collection of columns, not just the correlation of one pair.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[普通最小二乘]]中，设计矩阵 $X$ 收集纳入的预测变量：$n$ 行对应观测，$p$ 列对应选定特征，包含截距时加一列全为 $1$ 的数。系数向量 $\beta$ 将它们组合为拟合值 $X\beta$。现在要问：不同系数选择，是否能产生完全相同或非常相近的拟合值？这是关于整组设计列的问题，不只是某一对变量的相关。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Exact dependence removes uniqueness; near dependence does not
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 精确依赖与近似依赖不是同一种后果

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exact dependence means there is a **nonzero** coefficient vector $a$ with $Xa=0$. Thus adding $ca$ to any coefficient vector, for any scalar $c$, leaves the fitted values unchanged: $X(\beta+ca)=X\beta$. This is failure of [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full column rank]]. The affected coefficients are not unique, although the OLS fitted vector is still unique. A particular coefficient or combination unaffected by the redundant direction can remain identifiable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

精确依赖指存在**非零**系数向量 $a$，使 $Xa=0$。因此，对任意标量 $c$，把系数改成 $\beta+ca$ 都不改变拟合值：$X(\beta+ca)=X\beta$。这意味着设计不满足[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]。受冗余方向影响的系数不唯一，但 OLS 拟合向量仍然唯一；不受该方向影响的某个系数或系数组合仍可能可以识别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With near dependence, the columns need not satisfy an exact identity. After fixing the units and a meaningful scale for coefficient changes, there is a direction $a$ along which a substantial coefficient change produces only a small fitted-value change $Xa$. Full rank and a unique OLS solution may remain. “Near” has no universal yes/no cutoff: a small raw number can arise simply from the choice of units, and multiplying $a$ by a tiny number would make $Xa$ tiny for any matrix. A meaningful diagnostic must fix the scale before comparing directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

近似依赖时，各列不必满足精确恒等式。在固定单位和有意义的系数变化尺度后，存在一个方向 $a$，使系数发生明显变化时，拟合值的变化 $Xa$ 却很小。设计仍可能满列秩，OLS 解仍可能唯一。“接近”没有通用的是非阈值：原始数值小可能只因单位不同，而把 $a$ 任意缩小，对任何矩阵都能使 $Xa$ 很小。因此，比较方向前必须先说明尺度。

> [!note] Two different concerns · 两种不同的问题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[设计矩阵条件数|Numerical conditioning]] asks how sensitive a calculation is to input or rounding perturbations, under a stated scaling convention. [[近似共线性精度|Statistical precision]] asks how much an estimator varies under an error or sampling model. The same weak design direction can matter for both, but the questions differ. A more stable numerical solver cannot create missing information, and a large numerical condition number alone does not specify a coefficient’s sampling variance.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[设计矩阵条件数|数值条件性]]在指定缩放口径下，讨论输入扰动或舍入误差对计算的影响；[[近似共线性精度|统计精度]]则在误差或抽样模型下，讨论估计量的波动。相同的薄弱设计方向可能同时影响两者，但问题不同。更稳定的数值算法不能创造缺失的信息，而较大的数值条件数本身也没有指定系数的抽样方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An identity hidden in the variable definitions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to $\mathrm{experience}=\mathrm{age}-\mathrm{education}-6$. For example, age $30$ and education $16$ force recorded experience to be $8$ under that rule. Write the fitted wage equation with coefficients $b_0,b_A,b_E,b_T$. Substitution gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回到 $\mathrm{experience}=\mathrm{age}-\mathrm{education}-6$。例如，年龄 $30$、教育 $16$ 年时，这条规则强制把经验记为 $8$ 年。用 $b_0,b_A,b_E,b_T$ 表示工资拟合方程的四个系数，代入得到

$$
b_0+b_A\mathrm{age}+b_E\mathrm{education}+b_T\mathrm{experience}
=(b_0-6b_T)+(b_A+b_T)\mathrm{age}+(b_E-b_T)\mathrm{education}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The observed fitted values depend on three combinations, not four separately determined coefficients. Even infinitely precise arithmetic cannot recover all four from these columns. If experience is instead independently recorded and the relation only approximately holds, the fourth column may add a small amount of new variation. Then changing age while holding both education and experience fixed has little support in this sample; whether the resulting coefficient is sufficiently precise also depends on the noise and the amount of data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

观察到的拟合值依赖三个组合，而不是四个分别确定的系数。即使用无限精确的计算，也不能从这些列中恢复全部四个系数。如果经验来自独立记录，上式只近似成立，第四列可能提供少量新变动。此时，保持教育和经验均不变而改变年龄的比较，在样本中缺少支持；相应系数是否足够精确，还取决于噪声和数据量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The relation can involve several columns even when no pair has correlation close to $1$ or $-1$; see [[两两相关诊断边界|the limit of pairwise correlations]]. A [[方差膨胀因子|variance inflation factor]] examines one nonconstant predictor against all the others. The [[设计矩阵条件数|design condition number]] instead examines the relative strength of directions in the whole matrix, with scaling made explicit. These are diagnostics of the same design from different viewpoints, not interchangeable verdicts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使没有任何一对变量的相关接近 $1$ 或 $-1$，若干列仍可能共同形成依赖，见[[两两相关诊断边界]]。[[方差膨胀因子]]把一个非常数预测列与全部其他列比较；[[设计矩阵条件数]]则在说明缩放后，考察整个矩阵各方向的相对强弱。它们从不同角度诊断同一设计，不能当成可互换的判决。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Column dependence is not a claim about the outcome or the unobserved error. It does not itself establish [[共线性与内生性|endogeneity]], nor is it the same as an unusual observation row. [[异常杠杆影响区分|Outliers, leverage and influence]] concern different diagnostic questions. How a dependence arose is treated in [[结构性与数据性共线|structural versus data-based collinearity]]; this card defines the phenomenon rather than choosing a variable to remove.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

列依赖并不是关于结果或未观察误差的结论。它本身不能认定[[共线性与内生性|内生性]]，也不等同于某行观测异常；[[异常杠杆影响区分]]处理不同的诊断问题。依赖怎样产生，由[[结构性与数据性共线]]讨论；本卡定义现象，并不据此决定删哪个变量。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Hansen, *Econometrics* (2010), §4.11](https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~xshi28/Econometrics_Hansen.pdf#page=65), printed pp.58–59 / PDF65–66: exact and near column dependence, scaling ambiguity, numerical sensitivity and the separate precision issue. The rank-deficient case here preserves the distinction between unique fits and nonunique coefficients.
- [NIST Dataplot, Regression Diagnostics, “Multi-collinearity”](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman1/auxillar/regrdiag.htm): dependence on several columns, limits of pairwise screening, and scaled matrix diagnostics. Only these qualitative and geometric claims are used here.
- [Penn State STAT 501, Lesson12, §§12.1 and 12.4](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat501/Lesson12): predictor overlap and auxiliary-regression diagnostics. [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/06_多重共线性.md#1.3. 一个贯穿全章的例子|The permanent course example]] supplies the age–education–experience identity, verified by direct substitution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Hansen §4.11 核对精确与近似列依赖、尺度问题、数值敏感性与统计精度的分工；精确秩亏仍保留“拟合唯一、系数不唯一”的区别。
- NIST 的 Multi-collinearity 小节用于多列依赖、两两筛查限制和缩放诊断的定性与几何内容。
- Penn State 第12.1、12.4节支持预测列重叠及辅助回归；永久课程的年龄、教育、经验恒等式已直接代入核算。
