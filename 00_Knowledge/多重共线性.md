# 多重共线性是设计矩阵的列之间存在精确或近似线性依赖
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Multicollinearity is exact or approximate linear dependence among columns of the design matrix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 在线性回归的设计矩阵 $X$ 中，若某一列可以由其他列精确线性表示，就存在**完全共线性**；若某个列组合虽不为零、却只剩很小的样本变异，就存在**近似共线性**。两者都在问：数据有没有足够独立的解释变量变异，把不同系数分别识别出来？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a regression design matrix $X$, exact multicollinearity means that one column is an exact linear combination of others. Near multicollinearity means that some nonzero column combination has very little sample variation. Both ask whether the design contains enough independent regressor variation to distinguish the coefficients.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 精确依赖与近似依赖不是同一种后果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Exact and approximate dependence have different consequences*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若存在非零向量 $a$ 使 $Xa=0$，则 $X$ 不满列秩。$\beta$ 与 $\beta+ca$ 对任意 $c$ 都产生同一拟合值，因此相关单个系数没有唯一答案；这由[[满列秩与OLS唯一性]]处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a nonzero vector $a$ satisfies $Xa=0$, then $X$ lacks full column rank. The coefficient vectors $\beta$ and $\beta+ca$ produce the same fitted values for every $c$, so the affected individual coefficients are not unique; see [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|the OLS rank condition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $Xa$ 只是接近零，$X$ 仍可满列秩，系数也仍可计算；问题变成数据只在这个方向提供很少信息。小的样本改动、测量误差或合理的设定变化，便可能引起较大的系数变化，见[[近似共线性精度]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $Xa$ is merely close to zero, $X$ may still have full column rank and the coefficients remain computable. The design then contains little information in that direction, so small sample, measurement, or specification changes can cause large coefficient movements; see [[近似共线性精度|precision under near multicollinearity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若样本中按定义有
$$
experience=age-education-6,
$$
则同时放入截距、`age`、`education` 与 `experience` 会产生精确线性依赖。若 `experience` 是实际记录、上式只近似成立，模型可能仍可估计，但“保持教育和经验不变而只改变年龄”的比较几乎没有样本支持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $experience=age-education-6$ holds by construction, an intercept together with age, education, and experience is exactly collinear. If experience is observed and the identity holds only approximately, estimation may remain possible, but the comparison that changes age while holding education and experience fixed has almost no sample support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共线性诊断的对象是 $X$ 的**列**。它不等于解释变量与结构误差相关，也不等于某一行是异常观测；前者见[[共线性与内生性]]，后者见[[异常值、杠杆与影响诊断.canvas|异常值、杠杆与影响诊断]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multicollinearity concerns the columns of $X$. It is neither correlation between a regressor and the structural error nor an unusual observation row; those are addressed by [[共线性与内生性|the collinearity–endogeneity distinction]] and [[异常值、杠杆与影响诊断.canvas|influence diagnostics]], respectively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
