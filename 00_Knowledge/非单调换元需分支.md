# Inverse branches in substitution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*换元积分中的反函数分支*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The substitution $u=x^2$ forgets the sign of $x$: both $x=-1$ and $x=1$ give $u=1$. This matters if a calculation needs to recover $x$ from $u$. A **branch** restricts the original inputs so that this recovery is unambiguous: on $x>0$ use $x=\sqrt u$, and on $x<0$ use $x=-\sqrt u$. Choosing a branch also determines the sign of its [[反函数导数|inverse derivative]], hence the transformed integration factor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换元 $u=x^2$ 丢掉了 $x$ 的正负号：$x=-1$ 与 $x=1$ 都得到 $u=1$。如果计算确实需要由 $u$ 还原 $x$，这就很重要。选择一个**分支**，就是限制原输入范围，让还原结果唯一：$x>0$ 时用 $x=\sqrt u$，$x<0$ 时用 $x=-\sqrt u$。分支也决定[[反函数导数]]的符号，从而决定换元后的积分因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first question is therefore whether the calculation actually needs an inverse. The [[定积分换元|complete substitution formula]] can often be used without recovering $x$ at all.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，首先应判断计算是否真的需要反解。[[定积分换元|完整的定积分换元公式]]往往根本不需要把 $x$ 还原出来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When no inverse is used
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*不需要反解时*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a<b$ be finite real endpoints, let $g$ be continuously differentiable on $[a,b]$, and let $f$ be continuous on an interval containing **every value** of $g$ on $[a,b]$. Continuous differentiability here includes a continuous extension of the interior derivative to the endpoints. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<b$ 是有限实数，$g$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上连续可微，$f$ 在包含 $g$ 于整段 $[a,b]$ 上**所有取值**的区间上连续。这里连续可微包括内点导数能够连续延拓到端点。于是

$$\int_a^b f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx=\int_{g(a)}^{g(b)}f(u)\,du.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $g$ is the inner expression, $g'$ is its derivative, and $f$ describes the remaining dependence on the new variable $u$. The [[链式法则|chain rule]] and the [[Newton-Leibniz公式|fundamental theorem of calculus]] prove this identity without an inverse. Thus $g$ may turn around, have zero derivative, or even be constant. The bounds keep their direction; equal image bounds give zero for this complete integrand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $g$ 是内层表达式，$g'$ 是其导数，$f$ 表示剩余部分对新变量 $u$ 的依赖。[[链式法则]]与[[Newton-Leibniz公式|微积分基本定理]]证明这条恒等式时没有使用反函数。因此，$g$ 可以中途折返、导数为零，甚至是常数。换后的端点保留方向；像端点相同时，这种完整被积式的积分为零。

> [!example] Turning around can cancel correctly · 折返可以正确抵消
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With $g(x)=x^2$, the entire factor $2x\,dx$ is $du$. Consequently
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $g(x)=x^2$，整个 $2x\,dx$ 就是 $du$。因此
>
> $$\int_{-1}^{1}2xe^{x^2}\,dx=\int_1^1e^u\,du=0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The contribution on the negative half is negative and the positive half cancels it. Even an additional factor $x^2$ creates no inverse problem: $2x\,x^2e^{x^2}\,dx$ becomes $u e^u\,du$ directly. A remaining expression in $x$ may already be a single-valued expression in $u$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 负半段贡献为负，正半段恰好抵消它。即使再乘一个 $x^2$，也不需要反解：$2x\,x^2e^{x^2}\,dx$ 可以直接写成 $u e^u\,du$。还出现含 $x$ 的表达式，不代表它不能直接改写为 $u$ 的单值表达式。

## When the inverse really changes the calculation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*反解何时真正影响计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now consider the ordinary [[定积分|definite integral]] $\int_{-1}^{1}x^2\,dx$. The original integrand is nonnegative, so it should not cancel to zero. If we insist on $u=x^2$, the differential relation $du=2x\,dx$ gives $dx=du/(2x)$ only for $x\ne0$. Replacing that $x$ by $\sqrt u$ on the whole interval would silently discard the negative branch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再看普通[[定积分]] $\int_{-1}^{1}x^2\,dx$。原被积函数非负，不应正负抵消为零。如果坚持令 $u=x^2$，微分关系 $du=2x\,dx$ 只能在 $x\ne0$ 时写成 $dx=du/(2x)$。把整个区间上的这个 $x$ 都替换成 $\sqrt u$，就悄悄丢掉了负分支。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]] to separate the negative and positive halves. On the negative half, $x=-\sqrt u$ and $u$ travels from $1$ down to $0$; on the positive half, $x=\sqrt u$ and $u$ travels from $0$ up to $1$. The two transformed expressions are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用[[定积分区间可加性]]分开负半段与正半段。负半段上 $x=-\sqrt u$，$u$ 从 $1$ 降到 $0$；正半段上 $x=\sqrt u$，$u$ 从 $0$ 升到 $1$。对应的两个改写为

$$
\begin{aligned}
x<0:&\quad x^2\,dx=u\frac{-1}{2\sqrt u}\,du=-\frac{\sqrt u}{2}\,du,\\
x>0:&\quad x^2\,dx=u\frac{1}{2\sqrt u}\,du=\frac{\sqrt u}{2}\,du.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inverse derivative is undefined at $u=0$, so justify the calculation first on $[-1,-\varepsilon]$ and $[\varepsilon,1]$, where $0<\varepsilon<1$, and then let $\varepsilon$ decrease to zero. The original omitted integral is $2\varepsilon^3/3\to0$, and each simplified transformed integrand extends continuously to zero. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反函数导数在 $u=0$ 没有定义，所以先在 $[-1,-\varepsilon]$ 与 $[\varepsilon,1]$ 上计算，其中 $0<\varepsilon<1$，再让 $\varepsilon$ 趋于零。原式省去的中间积分为 $2\varepsilon^3/3\to0$；化简后的两个新被积函数都能连续延拓到零。因此

$$
\int_{-1}^{1}x^2\,dx
=\int_1^0-\frac{\sqrt u}{2}\,du+\int_0^1\frac{\sqrt u}{2}\,du
=\frac13+\frac13=\frac23.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both contributions are positive: on the first branch, the negative inverse derivative is accompanied by reversed bounds. Directly using the antiderivative $x^3/3$ is shorter here; the substitution exposes why a branch and its direction must be kept together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两段贡献都为正：第一条分支中，反函数导数的负号与倒序端点同时出现。这道题直接用原函数 $x^3/3$ 更短；换元计算展示的是，反解分支必须与方向配套保留。

## A practical branch check
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样检查分支*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a general original integrand $H(x)$, an inverse substitution uses $x=\phi(u)$ and transforms it to $H(\phi(u))\phi'(u)$. On a closed branch interval, a continuously differentiable $g$ with derivative nowhere zero, together with continuous $H$, supplies a differentiable inverse and the corresponding definite-integral formula. Zeros of $g'$ or singular inverse endpoints need separate justification, as in the square example. Merely being monotone does not guarantee a differentiable inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一般原被积函数 $H(x)$，反解换元使用 $x=\phi(u)$，把它改写为 $H(\phi(u))\phi'(u)$。在一个闭分支区间上，若 $g$ 连续可微且导数处处非零，$H$ 连续，就能获得可导反函数及相应的定积分公式。$g'$ 的零点或反函数的奇异端点需要另行说明，平方代换就是一例。仅仅单调，并不保证反函数可导。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before splitting, simplify the whole expression and see whether it is already $f(g(x))g'(x)$. If recovery of $x$ is still needed and different original inputs give the same $u$, select valid inverse branches and add their separately transformed integrals. A finite split is useful when such a split exists; an arbitrary oscillating function need not admit finitely many monotone pieces. Branch selection also does not make a divergent [[反常积分|improper integral]] converge: any singular limit still needs its own check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分段之前，先化简整个表达式，检查它是否已经是 $f(g(x))g'(x)$。若仍然确实需要还原 $x$，而不同原输入给出同一个 $u$，再选择合法的反解分支，把各段换元后的积分相加。有合适的有限分段时才使用有限分段；任意振荡函数未必能分成有限个单调段。选择分支也不会使发散的[[反常积分]]变得收敛，奇异极限仍须单独核查。
