# Root test for series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*级数根值判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The root test measures the exponential size of each term. For a geometric term $q^n$ with $q\ge0$, its $n$th root is exactly $q$. If the late terms of a series have $n$th-root magnitudes uniformly below a fixed number less than one, their magnitudes fit under a convergent geometric series. Taking a limit superior lets this test work even when the root magnitudes oscillate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根值判别衡量每项的指数尺度。对 $q\ge0$ 的几何项 $q^n$，取 $n$ 次根正好得到 $q$。如果级数足够靠后各项的模，取 $n$ 次根后都不超过某个固定的小于一的数，就能用收敛的几何级数压住这些项。使用上极限，还允许这些根值本身不断振荡。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A series with zeros between its positive terms
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正项之间插着零的级数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a_n=0$ when $n$ is odd and $a_n=2^{-n}$ when $n$ is even. The first terms of the series are
$$0+\frac14+0+\frac1{16}+0+\frac1{64}+\cdots.$$
Adjacent ratios repeatedly require division by zero. Instead set $b_n=|a_n|^{1/n}$, the nonnegative $n$th root of the magnitude. Then $b_n$ alternates between $0$ and $1/2$. It has no ordinary limit, but every tail has supremum $1/2$, so its [[数列上极限|limit superior]] is $1/2$. In particular $0\le a_n\le(1/2)^n$ for every $n$, which already proves convergence by [[级数直接比较判别|comparison]] with a [[几何级数|geometric series]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令奇数 $n$ 时 $a_n=0$，偶数 $n$ 时 $a_n=2^{-n}$，级数开头是 $0+1/4+0+1/16+0+1/64+\cdots$。相邻比值会不断遇到零分母。换成 $b_n=|a_n|^{1/n}$，即通项模的非负 $n$ 次根，则 $b_n$ 在 $0$ 与 $1/2$ 之间交替，没有普通极限；但每个尾部的上确界都为 $1/2$，所以[[数列上极限|上极限]]为 $1/2$。事实上，对每个 $n$ 都有 $0\le a_n\le(1/2)^n$，直接与[[几何级数]]作[[级数直接比较判别|比较]]就已证明收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The nonzero terms are $4^{-k}$ for integers $k\ge1$, so the sum is $1/3$. More explicitly, partial sums through $2K$ and $2K+1$ both equal $\sum_{k=1}^{K}4^{-k}\to1/3$. Thus no zero term has been silently divided out or skipped in taking the limit of the original partial sums.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非零项为 $4^{-k}$，其中整数 $k\ge1$，所以总和为 $1/3$。具体说，前 $2K$ 项和与前 $2K+1$ 项和都等于 $\sum_{k=1}^{K}4^{-k}\to1/3$。这里并未在求原部分和极限时偷偷除去零项或跳过原索引。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The upper-limit criterion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*上极限判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ have finite real or complex terms. For $a_n=u_n+iv_n$, with $i^2=-1$, the magnitude is $|a_n|=\sqrt{u_n^2+v_n^2}$; complex partial sums converge when both coordinates converge. Recall that [[绝对收敛|absolute convergence]] means convergence of the real nonnegative series $\sum |a_n|$. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ 的各项为有限实数或复数。若 $a_n=u_n+iv_n$，其中 $i^2=-1$，则模为 $|a_n|=\sqrt{u_n^2+v_n^2}$；复数部分和收敛指两个坐标分别收敛。[[绝对收敛]]则是指非负实数级数 $\sum|a_n|$ 收敛。定义

$$
b_n=|a_n|^{1/n},\qquad
\rho=\limsup_{n\to\infty}b_n
=\lim_{N\to\infty}\sup_{n\ge N}b_n\in[0,+\infty].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The index $n$ is a positive integer, so the root of a zero term is simply zero. The inner supremum is the least ceiling over the entire tail starting at $N$; then the starting point moves to infinity. This is the [[数列上极限|sequence limit superior]], not an assumption that $b_n$ itself converges. If an ordinary root limit does exist, it equals $\rho$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

索引 $n$ 是正整数，所以零项的根值直接等于零。内层上确界是起点为 $N$ 的整个尾部的最小上界，再让起点不断后移。这就是[[数列上极限]]，并没有要求 $b_n$ 自己收敛。如果普通根值极限确实存在，它就等于 $\rho$。

> [!important] Three outcomes · 三种结果
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $\rho<1$, the series converges absolutely. If $\rho>1$, including $+\infty$, it diverges. If $\rho=1$, the test is inconclusive. Zero terms are allowed anywhere; a sequence that is eventually zero has $\rho=0$ and gives a finite sum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $\rho<1$，级数绝对收敛；若 $\rho>1$，包括 $+\infty$，级数发散；若 $\rho=1$，无法由本判别作出结论。任意位置都可以出现零项；最终全为零的数列有 $\rho=0$，其级数只是有限和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A geometric ceiling or infinitely many large terms
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*几何上界，或者无穷多个大项*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $\rho<1$. Choose a real number $q$ with $\rho<q<1$. The tail-ceiling definition gives an integer $N$ such that every $n\ge N$ has $b_n<q$. Raising to the positive integer power $n$ preserves the inequality: $|a_n|<q^n$. Therefore every finite tail partial sum satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $\rho<1$。选实数 $q$，使 $\rho<q<1$。由尾部上界的定义，存在整数 $N$，使每个 $n\ge N$ 都有 $b_n<q$。两侧取正整数 $n$ 次幂保留不等号，得到 $|a_n|<q^n$，所以任意有限尾部部分和满足

$$0\le\sum_{n=N}^{M}|a_n|\le\sum_{n=N}^{M}q^n\le\frac{q^N}{1-q}\qquad(M\ge N).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The nonnegative partial sums are increasing and bounded; adding the finite prefix proves absolute convergence. [[绝对收敛推出收敛|Absolute convergence implies convergence]] of the original series, even if signs or complex phases vary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些非负部分和递增且有界，加上有限前缀便证明绝对收敛。再由[[绝对收敛推出收敛]]，即使符号或复数相位不断变化，原级数仍然收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now suppose $\rho>1$. Choose a fixed real number $q$ with $1<q<\rho$; if $\rho=+\infty$, any fixed $q>1$ works. Infinitely many indices must satisfy $b_n>q$. Otherwise one whole late tail would have ceiling at most $q$, contradicting its upper limit. At those indices $|a_n|>q^n>1$, so the terms cannot tend to zero. The [[级数通项判别|term test]] proves divergence. Its necessity also holds for complex sums because $a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}$ for consecutive partial sums.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再假设 $\rho>1$。选固定实数 $q$ 满足 $1<q<\rho$；若 $\rho=+\infty$，任意固定 $q>1$ 都可以。一定有无穷多个索引满足 $b_n>q$，否则某个完整尾部的上界就会至多为 $q$，与上极限矛盾。在这些索引处 $|a_n|>q^n>1$，所以通项不可能趋于零，由[[级数通项判别]]知级数发散。复数级数也有同一必要条件，因为相邻部分和满足 $a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}$。

> [!warning] Upper limit does not mean every late term is large · 上极限大不等于每项都大
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $a_n=0$ at odd indices and $a_n=2^n$ at even indices, the root magnitudes alternate between $0$ and $2$. Their upper limit is $2$, and the series diverges although infinitely many terms are zero. The proof needs arbitrarily late large terms, not growth of every term.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若奇数索引处 $a_n=0$、偶数索引处 $a_n=2^n$，根值在 $0$ 与 $2$ 间交替，上极限为 $2$。尽管有无穷多个零项，级数仍发散。证明只需要任意靠后还能出现大项，不需要每一项都增长。

## The value one leaves the question open
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*等于一时仍须另作判断*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both $a_n=1/n$ and $a_n=1/n^2$ have $|a_n|^{1/n}\to1$, yet the [[p级数判别|p-series test]] gives divergence for the first and convergence for the second. For example, for either exponent $p=1$ or $2$, writing $n^{-p/n}=\exp(-p\log n/n)$ shows the root limit, since $\log n/n\to0$. Here $\log$ is the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] and $\exp$ is its inverse, the [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]]. The logarithmic limit follows from [[洛必达法则|L’Hôpital’s rule]] for real $x\to+\infty$: the derivative ratio for $\log x/x$ is $1/x\to0$. Thus $\rho<1$ is sufficient for absolute convergence, not necessary. The test estimates an exponential scale; it does not settle every slower rate of decay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$a_n=1/n$ 与 $a_n=1/n^2$ 都有 $|a_n|^{1/n}\to1$，但[[p级数判别]]给出前者发散、后者收敛。例如对指数 $p=1$ 或 $2$，写成 $n^{-p/n}=\exp(-p\log n/n)$，再用 $\log n/n\to0$，即可看出根值极限。这里 $\log$ 表示[[自然对数]]，$\exp$ 是其反函数[[自然指数函数]]。对实数 $x\to+\infty$，$\log x/x$ 的导数之比为 $1/x\to0$，由[[洛必达法则]]得到所用的对数极限。因此 $\rho<1$ 是绝对收敛的充分条件，不是必要条件。根值判别衡量指数尺度，并不能判定所有更慢的衰减速度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compared with the [[级数比值判别|ratio test]], taking roots is often simpler when the entire term is an $n$th power, and it avoids zero denominators. Taking adjacent ratios is often simpler for factorials. Neither critical value of one is a verdict about the series itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与[[级数比值判别]]相比，整项本身是某表达式的 $n$ 次幂时，开根往往更简单，而且不会遇到零分母；阶乘相邻相除则往往更方便。两种方法算出的临界值一，都不是对级数本身的敛散判决。
