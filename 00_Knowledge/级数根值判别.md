# 通项绝对值的n次根上极限小于一保证绝对收敛而大于一保证发散
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A limsup of nth-root magnitudes below one gives absolute convergence and a limsup above one gives divergence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实数或复数级数 $\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n$，令
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real or complex series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n$, set
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\rho=\limsup_{n\to\infty}|a_n|^{1/n}
=\lim_{N\to\infty}\sup_{n\ge N}|a_n|^{1/n}\in[0,+\infty].
$$

若 $\rho<1$，级数绝对收敛；若 $\rho>1$（包括 $+\infty$），级数发散；若 $\rho=1$，无结论。上极限取每个尾部的上确界再取极限，所以不要求 $|a_n|^{1/n}$ 本身收敛，也允许 $a_n=0$。若普通极限存在，它就等于这里的上极限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\rho<1$, the series converges absolutely; if $\rho>1$, including $+\infty$, it diverges; if $\rho=1$, the test is inconclusive. The limsup is the limit of tail suprema, so the roots need not converge and zero terms are allowed. When an ordinary limit exists, it equals the limsup.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\rho<1$，取 $\rho<q<1$，则最终 $|a_n|^{1/n}\le q$，所以 $|a_n|\le q^n$，可与 [[几何级数]] 比较。若 $\rho>1$，则有无穷多个 $n$ 满足 $|a_n|^{1/n}>1$，从而 $|a_n|>1$；通项不趋于零，由 [[级数通项判别]] 发散。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\rho<1$, choose $q$ between $\rho$ and one. Eventually $|a_n|\le q^n$, giving comparison with a [[几何级数|geometric series]]. If $\rho>1$, infinitely many terms have magnitude above one, so the [[级数通项判别|term test]] proves divergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如奇数 $n$ 时取 $a_n=0$，偶数 $n$ 时取 $a_n=2^{-n}$。根值在 0 与 $1/2$ 间交替，没有普通极限，但 $\rho=1/2$，所以级数收敛；相邻项比值则会遇到零分母。边界例子 $\sum1/n$ 与 $\sum1/n^2$ 都有 $\rho=1$，却一发散一收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $a_n=0$ for odd $n$ and $a_n=2^{-n}$ for even $n$. The roots alternate between zero and one half, so no ordinary limit exists, but the limsup is one half and the series converges. Adjacent ratios encounter zero denominators. Both $\sum1/n$ and $\sum1/n^2$ have limsup one, despite different convergence outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
