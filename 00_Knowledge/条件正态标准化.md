# 条件正态变量按各组均值和正标准差标准化后与组变量独立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A conditionally normal variable standardised within groups is independent of the grouping variable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若对 $P_X$ 几乎处处的 $x$，$Y\mid X=x\sim N(\mu(x),\sigma^2(x))$，其中位置与尺度函数可测、$0<\sigma(x)<\infty$，令
$$Z=\frac{Y-\mu(X)}{\sigma(X)}.$$
则每组都有 $Z\mid X=x\sim N(0,1)$，由 [[条件分布不变推出独立]]得到 $Z\perp X$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A measurable conditional normal location-scale model with positive finite scale becomes standard normal in every group after within-group standardisation. A [[条件分布不变推出独立|common full conditional law]] implies independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若只知道每组均值与方差，标准化只能保证它们变成 0 和 1；组间仍可有不同偏度、峰度或离散结构。条件正态假设使“两个参数相同”足以代表“整张分布相同”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing only group means and variances guarantees only zero means and unit variances after standardisation. Shapes may still differ. Conditional normality makes the two parameters determine the entire law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
