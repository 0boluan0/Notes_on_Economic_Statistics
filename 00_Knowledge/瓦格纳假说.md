# 瓦格纳假说认为经济发展会通过政府职能与公共服务需求扩展推动支出相对增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Wagner's hypothesis links economic development to a relative expansion of expenditure through broader government functions and demand for public services.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

瓦格纳假说讨论长期发展中的政府活动扩张：工业化使经济关系更复杂，行政、司法和公共服务的需求随之改变，公共支出因而可能比收入增长得更快。它试图解释“为什么政府的相对规模会扩大”，而不只是观察政府每年花的钱更多。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Wagner's hypothesis concerns long-run government expansion. Industrialisation makes economic relationships more complex and changes demand for administration, legal institutions and public services. Expenditure may consequently grow faster than income. The question is relative expansion, not merely a larger monetary total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以沿着一条具体机制理解：交易分工加深，需要更复杂的合同执行和纠纷处理；城市化与新技术又改变公共服务需求。只有这些需求最终被制度安排转化为政府活动和财政支出，才会推高支出规模。需求增加本身，并没有规定必须由政府直接生产全部服务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deeper specialisation can require more elaborate contract enforcement, while urbanisation and technology change service needs. Spending expands only insofar as institutions translate these needs into government activity. Greater demand does not itself require government to produce every service directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程常用“人均收入提高，[[财政支出占比]] 上升”概括这一假说。设人口 $N>0$，以 $y=Y/N>0$ 表示人均收入、$e=G/N$ 表示人均支出，在口径一致的可微长期关系 $e=f(y)>0$ 中：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course summarises the hypothesis as a rising [[财政支出占比|expenditure share]] with income per person. Let population $N>0$, income $y=Y/N>0$ and expenditure $e=G/N$. For a consistently measured, differentiable long-run relationship $e=f(y)>0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
s=\frac{G}{Y}=\frac{f(y)}{y},\qquad
\frac{d\ln s}{d\ln y}
=\frac{y f'(y)}{f(y)}-1.
$$

于是，长期人均支出收入弹性大于 1 才对应这一定义下的占比上升。它不同于一次年度波动算出的 [[财政支出弹性|有限变化比值]]；还需要明确价格、人口和政府覆盖范围。两个名义总量一起上涨，不能单独证明发展引起了政府扩张。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A long-run per-capita income elasticity above one implies a rising share in this formulation. It differs from a one-year [[财政支出弹性|finite-change ratio]] and requires explicit prices, population and coverage. Co-movement of nominal totals alone does not establish causation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把它当作待检验的历史解释，而不是每个国家、每个时期的必然规律。融资约束、政治选择和服务提供方式，都可能使支出路径偏离上述机制；也不能由“会增长”推出“增长就是福利最优”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Treat it as a testable historical explanation, not a universal law or a welfare optimum. Financing constraints, political choices and delivery arrangements can produce different expenditure paths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
