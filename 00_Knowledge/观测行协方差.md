# Covariance across observational rows
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*观测行协方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two measurements from the same classroom, patient, or time period may share an unexpected influence. Once the modeled means have been removed, their errors can still move together. **Row covariance** describes this association between observational units. In a multi-response table, it must also say how a response in one row relates to every response in another row.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

来自同一教室、患者或时段的测量可能共享意外影响。即使减去模型均值，它们的误差仍可能共同变化。**观测行协方差**描述不同观测单位之间的这种联系。对于多响应表，还必须说明某一行的响应怎样与另一行的各个响应共同变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card explains a particular, **separable covariance model**: every pair of rows uses the same response-covariance pattern, multiplied by a row-pair coefficient. It is a useful model, not a consequence of arranging observations into a [[多元数据矩阵|data matrix]]. General cross-covariance blocks need not have this form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡解释一种特定的**可分离协方差模型**：每对观测行共用同一响应协方差模式，再乘上这一行对的系数。它是一种建模选择，不是把观测排进[[多元数据矩阵]]后就自动成立的事实；一般交叉协方差块未必具有这种形式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Begin with two whole error vectors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先看两个完整的误差向量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use [[多响应线性回归|the model]] $Y=XB+U$ with $n\ge1$ observational rows and $q\ge1$ response columns. Hold the design $X$ fixed, or explicitly work conditional on its realised value. Assume $E[U\mid X]=0$ and finite conditional second moments. Write $u_i=(U_{i1},\ldots,U_{iq})^T$ for row $i$ as a column vector. The cross-covariance block is the $q\times q$ matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用[[多响应线性回归|模型]] $Y=XB+U$，设有 $n\ge1$ 个观测行、$q\ge1$ 个响应列。把设计 $X$ 固定，或者明确给定其实现值讨论。假设 $E[U\mid X]=0$，各元素的条件二阶矩有限。将第 $i$ 行写成列向量 $u_i=(U_{i1},\ldots,U_{iq})^T$，则交叉协方差块是下面的 $q\times q$ 矩阵：

$$C_{ij}=\operatorname{Cov}(u_i,u_j\mid X)=E[u_i u_j^T\mid X],\qquad
(C_{ij})_{ab}=\operatorname{Cov}(U_{ia},U_{jb}\mid X).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The entry $(a,b)$ compares response $a$ in row $i$ with response $b$ in row $j$. When $i=j$, this is the ordinary [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] within one row. In general $C_{ji}=C_{ij}^T$, but $C_{ij}$ itself need not be symmetric when $i\ne j$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第 $(a,b)$ 项比较的是第 $i$ 行的响应 $a$ 与第 $j$ 行的响应 $b$。当 $i=j$ 时，就是同一行内部通常的[[协方差矩阵]]。一般有 $C_{ji}=C_{ij}^T$；但 $i\ne j$ 时，$C_{ij}$ 自身不必对称。

> [!important] The separable restriction · 可分离限制
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A separable model specifies fixed real symmetric [[半正定矩阵|positive-semidefinite]] factors $\Omega\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ and $\Sigma\in\mathbb R^{q\times q}$ such that every block has the form
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 可分离模型规定固定的实对称[[半正定矩阵]]因子 $\Omega\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ 与 $\Sigma\in\mathbb R^{q\times q}$，使每个块都满足
>
> $$\boxed{C_{ij}=\Omega_{ij}\Sigma.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $\Omega$ is the **row-covariance factor** and $\Sigma$ is the response-covariance factor. “Fixed” is within the stated conditional model. This restriction uses second moments only; it does not require Gaussian errors. Since every block is a scalar multiple of the same symmetric $\Sigma$, it is stronger than merely allowing some row dependence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 其中 $\Omega$ 是**观测行协方差因子**，$\Sigma$ 是响应协方差因子。“固定”指所述条件模型内固定。这个限制只用二阶矩，不要求 Gaussian 误差。由于每个块都是同一个对称 $\Sigma$ 的标量倍数，它比“允许行间存在一些依赖”强得多。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[矩阵正态误差结构|matrix-normal model]] adds a joint Gaussian law to this structure. Its column-stacking notation writes the same covariance as $\operatorname{Cov}(\operatorname{vec}U\mid X)=\Sigma\otimes\Omega$: $\operatorname{vec}$ places one full column after another, and the Kronecker product replaces each $\Sigma_{ab}$ by the block $\Sigma_{ab}\Omega$. This is just another ordering of all the entry covariances above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[矩阵正态误差结构]]在这套结构上进一步加入联合 Gaussian 分布。采用逐列堆叠，整个协方差可写成 $\operatorname{Cov}(\operatorname{vec}U\mid X)=\Sigma\otimes\Omega$：$\operatorname{vec}$ 把完整的一列接在另一列之后，Kronecker 积则把每个 $\Sigma_{ab}$ 替换成整块 $\Sigma_{ab}\Omega$。它只是把上面的逐元素协方差按另一种顺序排好。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the diagonal and off-diagonal differently
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*对角与非对角分别告诉我们什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The diagonal $\Omega_{ii}$ scales the entire covariance of row $i$: $C_{ii}=\Omega_{ii}\Sigma$. An off-diagonal $\Omega_{ij}$ scales the entire cross-covariance between two rows. It is not automatically their correlation coefficient, nor a covariance in the units of every response. For response $a$ with $\Sigma_{aa}>0$ and rows with positive diagonal scales, the [[相关系数|correlation]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对角元素 $\Omega_{ii}$ 缩放第 $i$ 行的整张协方差，即 $C_{ii}=\Omega_{ii}\Sigma$；非对角元素 $\Omega_{ij}$ 缩放两行之间整张交叉协方差。它本身不自动等于相关系数，也不能同时带有每个响应的协方差单位。对 $\Sigma_{aa}>0$ 的响应 $a$，若两行对角尺度都为正，其[[相关系数]]为

$$\operatorname{Corr}(U_{ia},U_{ja}\mid X)
=\frac{\Omega_{ij}}{\sqrt{\Omega_{ii}\Omega_{jj}}}.$$

> [!example] The second row is noisier · 第二行的噪声更大
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $\Omega=\begin{bmatrix}1&1/2\\1/2&4\end{bmatrix}$ and $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}4&2\\2&9\end{bmatrix}$. Row 1 has covariance $\Sigma$; row 2 has covariance $4\Sigma$, so each response's variance is four times as large there. Between rows, $C_{12}=\tfrac12\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&9/2\end{bmatrix}$. In particular, the first response has variances $4$ and $16$, covariance $2$, and correlation $2/\sqrt{4\cdot16}=1/4$, not $\Omega_{12}=1/2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $\Omega=\begin{bmatrix}1&1/2\\1/2&4\end{bmatrix}$、$\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}4&2\\2&9\end{bmatrix}$。第 1 行的协方差为 $\Sigma$，第 2 行为 $4\Sigma$，所以各响应的方差在第二行都是第一行的四倍。两行之间，$C_{12}=\tfrac12\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\1&9/2\end{bmatrix}$。特别地，第一个响应的两行方差分别为 $4,16$，协方差为 $2$，相关系数为 $2/\sqrt{4\cdot16}=1/4$，并不是 $\Omega_{12}=1/2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity choice $\Omega=I_n$ gives mutually uncorrelated error rows with a common covariance $\Sigma$. A diagonal $\Omega$ allows different row scales while keeping cross-covariances zero. If the whole error table is jointly Gaussian, zero cross-blocks give mutual independence by the [[联合高斯独立判据|Gaussian independence criterion]]; with $\Omega=I_n$, the zero-mean error rows are then i.i.d. With unequal positive diagonal entries they are independent but generally differently distributed. Response rows can have different model means even in the i.i.d.-error case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $\Omega=I_n$，就得到互不相关且具有共同协方差 $\Sigma$ 的误差行。对角 $\Omega$ 允许行尺度不同，同时保持交叉协方差为零。若整张误差表还联合 Gaussian，[[联合高斯独立判据]]使零交叉块推出各行相互独立；$\Omega=I_n$ 时，零均值误差行进一步独立同分布。若正对角元素不等，则行独立但一般不同分布。即便误差独立同分布，响应行也可以具有不同模型均值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without Gaussianity, zero covariance alone cannot establish independence. For a one-response example, let $S$ take $-1,0,1$ equally likely and set $u_1=S$, $u_2=S^2-2/3$. Both have mean zero, their covariance is $E[S^3]-(2/3)E[S]=0$, and their variances are $2/3$ and $2/9$. Thus a diagonal factor $\Omega=\operatorname{diag}(2/3,2/9)$ with $\Sigma=[1]$ fits their covariance. Yet $u_2$ is determined by $u_1$: whenever $u_1=0$, $u_2=-2/3$. This is [[零协方差不推独立|uncorrelated but dependent]] data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有 Gaussian 假设时，零协方差不能单独保证独立。取单响应例子：$S$ 等概率取 $-1,0,1$，令 $u_1=S$、$u_2=S^2-2/3$。两者均值为零，协方差为 $E[S^3]-(2/3)E[S]=0$，方差分别为 $2/3$、$2/9$，因此 $\Omega=\operatorname{diag}(2/3,2/9)$、$\Sigma=[1]$ 确实符合其协方差。然而 $u_2$ 完全由 $u_1$ 决定：只要 $u_1=0$，就必有 $u_2=-2/3$。这是[[零协方差不推独立|互不相关但有依赖]]的数据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Only the combined scale is identified
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*只有合并后的尺度能由协方差确定*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying one factor by a positive number and dividing the other by that number leaves every entry covariance unchanged. For any $c>0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把一个因子乘以正数，再把另一个因子除以同一个数，不会改变任何逐元素协方差。对任意 $c>0$，

$$(\Sigma/c)\otimes(c\Omega)=\Sigma\otimes\Omega.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the example, $(\Omega,\Sigma)$ and $(2\Omega,\Sigma/2)$ describe exactly the same covariances. Under a Gaussian model they describe the same distribution if the mean is unchanged. A model therefore needs a scale convention before it reports the two factors separately. For nonzero positive-semidefinite factors, one option is $\operatorname{tr}(\Omega)=n$, where [[迹与特征值总和|trace]] means the sum of diagonal entries. Another is to fix one specified positive diagonal entry. These conventions remove a shared scalar ambiguity; they do not change correlations or supply missing evidence for separability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在例子中，$(\Omega,\Sigma)$ 与 $(2\Omega,\Sigma/2)$ 描述完全相同的协方差；在 Gaussian 模型内，若均值不变，它们也描述同一分布。因此，分别报告两个因子前，模型需要一个尺度规范。对非零半正定因子，一种选择是 $\operatorname{tr}(\Omega)=n$，其中[[迹与特征值总和|迹]]指对角元素之和；也可固定某个指定的正对角元素。这些规范消除了共同标量歧义，却不会改变相关性，也不能替可分离假设提供证据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The trace convention does not force every $\Omega_{ii}$ to equal one. Making all diagonal entries one is an additional equal-row-scale restriction, not generally something that one scalar can accomplish. If an entire factor is zero, the overall covariance is zero and the other factor is not recoverable at all. Positive-definite factors avoid that degenerate boundary; singular factors still describe valid second moments, but inverse-based procedures require separate conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

迹规范并不强迫每个 $\Omega_{ii}$ 都等于一。让全部对角元素都等于一，是额外的行尺度相同限制，通常不能只靠一个标量完成。若整个因子为零，总协方差为零，另一个因子完全无法从中恢复。正定因子避开这种退化边界；奇异因子仍可描述合法二阶矩，但需要逆矩阵的程序必须另查条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Some dependence cannot be separated this way
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有些依赖结构不能这样拆开*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider two independent, zero-mean Gaussian rows with covariances $\operatorname{diag}(1,4)$ and $\operatorname{diag}(4,1)$. In row 1, the first response's variance is one quarter of the second's; in row 2, it is four times the second's. If both matrices were scalar multiples of a common $\Sigma$, those ratios would be equal. They are not, so no single pair $(\Omega,\Sigma)$ describes this table, even though all four entries together are Gaussian.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑两个独立、零均值的 Gaussian 行，协方差分别为 $\operatorname{diag}(1,4)$ 与 $\operatorname{diag}(4,1)$。第 1 行中，第一个响应方差是第二个的四分之一；第 2 行中却是四倍。若两张矩阵都是同一 $\Sigma$ 的标量倍数，这两个比率必须相等，但事实并非如此。因此，即使四个元素整体 Gaussian，也不存在一对 $(\Omega,\Sigma)$ 能描述这张表。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nonzero $\Omega_{ij}$ with nonzero $\Sigma$ entails some nonzero cross-covariance, but covariance does not establish a causal connection or identify the source of a shared shock. Conversely, a plausible classroom or time-series story does not prove a particular separable formula. The general [[样本均值协方差|covariance of a sample mean]] can use arbitrary cross-covariance blocks. [[相关行下的多响应GLS|GLS for correlated rows]] uses a specified row model for estimation, and [[相关行不容独立推断|the dependent-row inference boundary]] explains why an independent-row standard error cannot simply be retained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\Omega_{ij}\ne0$ 且 $\Sigma\ne0$ 会给出某些非零交叉协方差，但协方差不能证明因果联系，也不能识别共同冲击的来源。反过来，一个合理的教室或时序故事，也不能证明某个具体可分离公式。一般的[[样本均值协方差]]可以使用任意交叉协方差块；[[相关行下的多响应GLS]]使用指定的行模型估计，而[[相关行不容独立推断]]解释了为什么不能直接保留独立行标准误。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Hoff, *Separable covariance arrays via the Tucker product*, §2.2, PDF p.4; §3.2, p.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1008.2169#page=4): covariance-only separable construction and its distinction from a Gaussian model; non-identifiability under reciprocal rescaling of factors. The singular extension and concrete examples above follow directly from their entry covariances.
- [SciPy `matrix_normal`, Notes](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.matrix_normal.html): dimensions and column-stacked Kronecker order for the positive-definite Gaussian specialization. Its software restriction does not impose Gaussianity or invertibility on the second-moment definition here.
- [Boehm et al. (2019), Methods, printed p.2318 / PDF p.2](https://pages.stat.wisc.edu/~yandell/doc/2019/g3.2317.full.pdf#page=2): two indexed covariance factors and the independent-row identity special case.
- [Fan et al., *Statistical Foundations of Data Science*, §2.2.2, printed p.28 / PDF p.44](https://fan.princeton.edu/sites/g/files/toruqf5476/files/documents/chapters1-3.pdf#page=44): fixed-design versus conditional interpretation. [[联合高斯独立判据|The Gaussian independence criterion]] supplies the additional distributional condition; the finite three-point dependence example is computed explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Hoff 第4页核对可分离二阶矩与 Gaussian 分布的区分，第7页核对互逆缩放造成的尺度不可识别；奇异延伸及数值例子按逐元素协方差直接验证。
- SciPy 核对 Gaussian 特例的维度与逐列堆叠顺序，Boehm 等第2318页给出两个索引方向和单位行因子的实例；软件的正定限制没有被误当作一般二阶矩定义的条件。
- Fan 等第28印刷页核对设计条件的解释；Gaussian 独立性由对应定理衔接，三点分布反例直接计算。
