# 既有经营回报不变时利润增长由再投资比例和新增资本回报共同决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With unchanged earnings from existing operations, earnings growth depends on reinvestment and the return on new capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设本年 NOPAT 为 $N_t>0$，年末再投资 $I_t=b_tN_t$，新增投资下一年贡献 $r_{new}I_t$ 的税后经营利润。若原有业务下一年的利润仍是 $N_t$，且不计其他变化，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let current NOPAT be $N_t>0$, year-end reinvestment be $I_t=b_tN_t$, and new investment generate next year's after-tax operating profit $r_{new}I_t$. If existing operations still earn $N_t$ next year and nothing else changes:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$N_{t+1}=N_t+r_{new}I_t,\qquad g_{t+1}=\frac{N_{t+1}-N_t}{N_t}=b_t r_{new}.$$

因此，同样希望利润增长 6%，若新增投资回报为 15%，需再投资利润的 40%；若回报只有 10%，需再投资 60%。给增长率加一个零不需要多少字，但现实中的增长必须有相应资本、产能或效率来源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For 6% earnings growth, a 15% return on new investment requires reinvesting 40% of profit; a 10% return requires 60%. A growth assumption therefore needs a corresponding source in capital, capacity, or efficiency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 $b$ 是经营再投资占 NOPAT 的比例，不是净利润留存率；$r_{new}$ 是新增资本回报，不必等于历史[[投入资本回报率|平均 ROIC]]。若老业务提效或衰退、税率变化、投资多年后才投产，必须把那些利润变化和时间另写出来，不能机械套这条单期关系。是否增值另由[[增长不等于增值]]判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $b$ is operating reinvestment divided by NOPAT, not the retention ratio of net income. The new-investment return need not equal historical [[投入资本回报率|average ROIC]]. Efficiency changes, legacy decline, tax changes, or delayed commissioning require explicit additional terms and timing. [[增长不等于增值|Growth and value creation]] are separate questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Damodaran, The Fundamental Determinants of Growth](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/valquestions/growth.htm)：核对稳定回报下再投资—增长关系及新增/既有回报差异；本卡用明确投资时点独立推导，避免含混的同期公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Damodaran supports the reinvestment-growth relation and the distinction between new and existing investment returns. The equation here is independently derived with an explicit year-end investment and next-year earnings timeline.
