# 收入驱动预测把收入拆成可核验的数量、价格和业务组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Driver-based revenue forecasting decomposes revenue into verifiable volume, price, and business mix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先找业务真正的计费单位，再预测收入。单一产品可用销量乘平均实现价格；多产品或多地区业务应先分别预测再加总，避免把组合变化误认成统一提价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the actual billing unit before forecasting revenue. For one product, use volume times realised average price. Forecast products or regions separately before adding them, so a mix shift is not mistaken for a uniform price increase.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$R_t=\sum_j Q_{j,t}P_{j,t}.$$

订阅业务则需要口径匹配的平均付费用户与每用户收入；交易平台的成交总额不等于营业收入，若按佣金确认收入，还要乘抽成率。先从[[商业模式]]判断谁付钱、为什么付钱。[[市场份额]]给数量预测加外部约束，产能和渠道给实现速度加约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A subscription model needs consistently measured average paying users and revenue per user. Platform transaction value is not revenue; commission revenue also depends on the take rate. Start from the [[商业模式|business model]]. [[市场份额|Market share]], capacity, and distribution constrain the forecast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如同口径市场销量从 1,000 增至 1,100，公司份额从 10% 增至 11%，公司销量便从 100 增至 121。售价从 10 降至 9.5 后，收入是 1,149.5，比原来的 1,000 增长 14.95%，不是把“市场增长 10%”直接填进公司收入。份额扩大若依赖降价，还要检查[[经营杠杆|利润对量价的反应]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If market volume rises from 1,000 to 1,100 and share rises from 10% to 11%, company volume rises from 100 to 121. At a price of 9.5 instead of 10, revenue is 1,149.5, up 14.95% from 1,000—not simply the market's 10% growth. If price cuts drive share gains, also check [[经营杠杆|the profit response]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
