# 四类间断由连续条件的失败方式区分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Four types of discontinuity are distinguished by how continuity fails*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子分类
> 1. **跳跃间断：**有限左右极限都存在但不相等。
> 2. **可去间断：**有限左右极限相等，但函数未定义或点值不等于极限。
> 3. **无穷间断：**至少一个单侧极限为 $+\infty$ 或 $-\infty$。
> 4. **振荡间断：**靠近目标点时无限振荡，单侧极限不存在。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> **1. Jump:** Both finite one-sided limits exist but are unequal.<br>
> **2. Removable:** The finite one-sided limits agree, but the function is undefined or its point value differs from the limit.<br>
> **3. Infinite:** At least one one-sided limit is $+\infty$ or $-\infty$.<br>
> **4. Oscillatory:** The function oscillates indefinitely near the target point, so a one-sided limit does not exist.<br>
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 修复边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Repair boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可去间断处若重新定义函数值，可修复连续性；跳跃、无穷、振荡间断不能只改一个点修复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A removable discontinuity can be repaired by redefining the point value. Jump, infinite, and oscillatory discontinuities cannot be repaired by changing only one point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $\sin(1/x)$ 在 $x=0$ 附近无限振荡，属于哪一类间断？为什么不是无穷间断？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The function $\sin(1/x)$ oscillates indefinitely near $x=0$. Which type of discontinuity is this, and why is it not an infinite discontinuity?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 振荡间断；函数值保持有界，却没有单侧极限，并非趋于 $+\infty$ 或 $-\infty$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** It is oscillatory. The values remain bounded but have no one-sided limit; they do not tend to $+\infty$ or $-\infty$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
