# Classifying discontinuities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*间断类型判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before naming a discontinuity, ask what outputs do near the target input, and only then compare that behaviour with the value at the target itself. Two functions may both be undefined at zero but have completely different prospects: $\sin x/x$ has a [[正弦基本极限|finite limit]] there, whereas $1/x$ runs in opposite infinite directions. The missing denominator value alone does not classify the problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给间断命名之前，先看输入靠近目标点时输出怎样变化，再把这种行为与目标点本身的值比较。两个函数即使都在零处未定义，情况也可能完全不同：$\sin x/x$ 在那里有[[正弦基本极限|有限极限]]，$1/x$ 却沿左右两侧趋向相反的无穷方向。仅凭分母在某点为零，无法判断是哪种问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Fix the domain and inspect the two sides
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先固定定义域，再检查两侧*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two-sided discussion below concerns a real-valued function defined for $0<|x-a|<r$ for some $r>0$: there are inputs on both sides of the finite target $a$, which may itself be defined or missing. A [[函数极限|finite limit]] $L$ means all sufficiently close permitted outputs are within every prescribed tolerance of $L$. The [[双侧极限判定|left and right limits]] apply that test separately with $x<a$ and $x>a$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面的双侧讨论针对实值函数：存在某个 $r>0$，使 $0<|x-a|<r$ 时都有定义。因此有限目标点 $a$ 两侧都有输入，而 $a$ 本身可以有值，也可以缺失。[[函数极限|有限极限]] $L$ 意味着，所有足够近的合法输入，其输出都落在 $L$ 的任意给定容差内。[[双侧极限判定|左、右极限]]分别对 $x<a$ 与 $x>a$ 作此检验。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If both side limits are the same finite number $L$, check the point value. If $f(a)=L$, the function is [[一点连续|continuous at $a$]]. If the point value is missing or differs from $L$, it is a [[可去间断的唯一补值|removable defect]]: the only successful filling is $L$. If both side limits are finite but unequal, there is a [[跳跃间断不可补值|jump]], and no single point value can meet both sides.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若左右极限都等于同一个有限数 $L$，再检查点值。若 $f(a)=L$，函数就在[[一点连续|该点连续]]；若点值缺失或不等于 $L$，就是[[可去间断的唯一补值|可去的缺口]]，唯一成功的补值为 $L$。若两侧极限都有限却不相等，则是[[跳跃间断不可补值|跳跃]]，不存在同时接上两侧的单个点值。

> [!example] The finite cases side by side · 比较有限极限的两种情形
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $a=1$, $(x^2-1)/(x-1)=x+1$ for $x\ne1$, so both sides tend to $2$: fill the missing value with $2$. At $a=0$, the rule $1$ for $x<0$ and $3$ for $x>0$ has side limits $1,3$: no filling works. In each case the nearby rule is fixed before considering the target value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $a=1$，$x\ne1$ 时有 $(x^2-1)/(x-1)=x+1$，因此两侧都趋于 $2$，把缺失值补成 $2$ 即可。在 $a=0$，若左侧恒为 $1$、右侧恒为 $3$，两侧极限就是 $1,3$，没有成功的补值。两种情形都先固定附近规则，再考虑目标点值。

## Infinite growth is more specific than unboundedness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*趋于无穷比无界更具体*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A side limit of $+\infty$ says that, for every real threshold $M$, all sufficiently close inputs on that side give $f(x)>M$. The meaning of $-\infty$ reverses the inequality. These are [[无穷极限与无穷远极限|infinite output limits at a finite input]], not real values available to fill in. Here **infinite discontinuity** records that at least one side has such an infinite limit; it is useful to state which side and which sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单侧极限为 $+\infty$，指任取实数门槛 $M$，只要从该侧靠得足够近，所有输出都满足 $f(x)>M$；$-\infty$ 则把不等号反向。这是[[无穷极限与无穷远极限|输入靠近有限点时的输出无穷极限]]，不是可以补入的实数点值。这里用**无穷间断**记录至少一侧具有这种无穷极限，并应说明具体侧别与正负方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $1/x$ at zero, the right side tends to $+\infty$ and the left to $-\infty$. For $1/x^2$, both sides tend to $+\infty$. The former has no common two-sided extended limit; the latter does, but neither has a finite real limit permitting continuous filling. To check the positive right limit of $1/x$, for example, choose $\delta=1/(|M|+1)$. Then $0<x<\delta$ implies $1/x>|M|+1>M$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对零附近的 $1/x$，右侧趋于 $+\infty$，左侧趋于 $-\infty$；对 $1/x^2$，两侧都趋于 $+\infty$。前者没有共同的双侧扩展极限，后者有，但二者都没有能用来连续补值的有限实数极限。例如检验 $1/x$ 的右极限为正无穷，取 $\delta=1/(|M|+1)$，便有 $0<x<\delta$ 推出 $1/x>|M|+1>M$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Oscillation and mixed side behaviour
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*振荡与两侧混合的行为*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An oscillatory failure does not settle to one finite value or to one infinite direction on the affected side. For $f(x)=\sin(1/x)$, values stay between $-1$ and $1$, but arbitrarily close positive inputs attain both extremes. With $n=1,2,\ldots$ and angles measured in radians, take
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

振荡型失败，是相关一侧的输出既不稳定到一个有限值，也不朝某个固定无穷方向离开。对 $f(x)=\sin(1/x)$，值始终在 $-1$ 与 $1$ 之间，却能在任意靠近零的正输入处分别取到两个极端。令 $n=1,2,\ldots$，角度用弧度表示，取

$$x_n=\frac1{\pi/2+2\pi n},\qquad y_n=\frac1{3\pi/2+2\pi n}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both input sequences decrease to zero, but $f(x_n)=1$ and $f(y_n)=-1$ for every $n$. A right limit would have to accommodate both, which is impossible. Reflecting the inputs through zero gives the same obstruction from the left. Boundedness alone therefore does not produce a limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两条输入序列都递减到零，但对每个 $n$ 都有 $f(x_n)=1$、$f(y_n)=-1$。若右极限存在，就必须同时容纳这两个不同的趋势，不可能成立。把输入关于零反射，也能得到左侧的同样障碍。因此，仅有界并不能保证极限存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Oscillation need not be bounded: $\sin(1/x)/x$ on the positive side is zero along $x=1/(n\pi)$, yet along $x_n$ above it grows to $+\infty$. Thus it is unbounded near zero without tending to $+\infty$ or $-\infty$. Conversely, oscillating signs need not cause discontinuity: $|x\sin(1/x)|\le|x|$ shows that $x\sin(1/x)$ has limit zero and becomes continuous after filling with zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

振荡也未必有界：正侧的 $\sin(1/x)/x$ 沿 $x=1/(n\pi)$ 始终为零，沿上面的 $x_n$ 却趋于 $+\infty$。所以它在零附近无界，却不趋于 $+\infty$ 或 $-\infty$。反过来，符号来回变化也未必造成间断：$|x\sin(1/x)|\le|x|$ 说明 $x\sin(1/x)$ 的极限为零，补成零后连续。

> [!warning] Describe both sides when labels overlap · 标签重叠时要写明两侧
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A function can equal $1/x^2$ for $x<0$ and $\sin(1/x)$ for $x>0$. Then the left side tends to $+\infty$, while the right side oscillates. Under the “at least one infinite side” convention, it has both infinite and oscillatory behaviour. The four familiar names are useful descriptions, not a universal list of four mutually exclusive boxes. Record each side's actual limit or failure before choosing a label.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个函数可以在 $x<0$ 时等于 $1/x^2$，在 $x>0$ 时等于 $\sin(1/x)$。这样左侧趋于 $+\infty$，右侧振荡。按“至少一侧无穷”的约定，它同时具有无穷与振荡行为。因此，常见四个名称是有用的描述，不是一套普遍适用且互不重叠的四格表。先记录两侧实际极限或失败方式，再选择标签。

## What a one-point change can repair
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 修复边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the nearby domain and values fixed and $a$ a domain cluster point, a continuous real-valued filling exists exactly when the domain-relative finite limit exists. This makes the removable case the repairable one; a jump, infinite side or nonconvergent side prevents a one-point repair. If the point is missing, the term “discontinuity” here follows the calculus convention for an extendable or nonextendable hole; continuity at the point itself only applies after the point belongs to the function's domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定附近定义域与函数值，并且 $a$ 是定义域聚点时，存在连续的实数补值，当且仅当相对于定义域的有限极限存在。因此可去情形能够修复，跳跃、无穷侧或不收敛的一侧都阻止改单点修复。若目标点缺失，这里的“间断”沿用微积分对可补或不可补缺口的称呼；讨论该点本身连续，须先让它属于函数定义域。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an endpoint, use only the side available in the domain. For an arbitrary domain with gaps, use its actual permitted inputs rather than a full interval. An isolated domain point is automatically continuous and does not fit the two-sided classification. These checks prevent a missing side from being mistaken for a failed side limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在定义域端点，只检验定义域允许的那一侧；对有缺口的一般定义域，只用实际允许的输入，而不是假设整段区间都有定义。孤立的定义域点自动连续，不属于上述双侧分类。这样才能避免把不存在的输入侧误认为单侧极限失败。
