# 马尔萨斯生存收入是给定人口行为与死亡条件下使人口净增长为零的收入水平

马尔萨斯生存收入（subsistence income）在这里是人口平衡的模型阈值：固定婚育行为、死亡条件并排除净迁移时，收入处于这个水平，人口既不增加也不减少。它不等于每个人刚好不饿死所需的一笔钱，也不是各时代、各社会共同的固定金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here, Malthusian subsistence income is a model threshold for population balance. With family-formation behaviour and mortality conditions fixed, and no net migration, population neither increases nor decreases at this income. It is not an individual's bare survival budget or a universal amount across societies and periods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $y$ 表示收入水平，$n(y)$ 表示人口净增长率，阈值 $\bar y$ 满足

$$
n(\bar y)=0.
$$

零点定义了人口平衡；要得到[[马尔萨斯陷阱|马尔萨斯式反馈]]，还须说明零点两侧的反应，例如 $y>\bar y$ 时人口增加，$y<\bar y$ 时人口减少，并配上人口增加压低人均产出的生产关系。单写 $n(\bar y)=0$，没有证明系统会回到这里。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The zero defines population balance. [[马尔萨斯陷阱|Malthusian feedback]] additionally needs responses on either side—such as population expanding above the threshold and contracting below it—and a production relationship that lowers output per person as population rises. The equation alone does not prove convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CORE's definition and population-equilibrium discussion support the zero-growth meaning and directional feedback. The equation expresses that definition in notation.
