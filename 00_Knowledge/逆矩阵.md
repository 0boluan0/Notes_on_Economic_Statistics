# Inverse matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*逆矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **inverse matrix** reverses another matrix's action. If a matrix changes an input vector into an output vector, its inverse recovers the input from that output. Such a reversal has to work for every vector, not just for one successful example; some matrices lose information and cannot be reversed this way.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**逆矩阵**用来撤销另一个矩阵的作用。一个矩阵把输入向量变成输出向量，逆矩阵再从输出恢复输入。这种撤销必须对每个向量都有效，不能只在一个例子上凑巧成功；有些矩阵会丢掉信息，因而无法这样撤销。

## 最小例子

> [!example] Undo two independent scalings · 撤销两个坐标的缩放
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $A$ doubles the first coordinate and triples the second. The matrix $B$ reverses both scalings:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[对角矩阵]] $A$ 把第一个坐标乘二、第二个坐标乘三；$B$ 撤销这两次缩放：
>
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> B=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&0\\0&1/3\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For example, $A(4,6)^T=(8,18)^T$, then $B(8,18)^T=(4,6)^T$. More generally, [[矩阵乘法|multiplication]] gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 例如 $A(4,6)^T=(8,18)^T$，再算 $B(8,18)^T=(4,6)^T$。更一般地，按[[矩阵乘法]]计算，
>
> $$BA=AB=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}=I_2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $I_2$ is the identity matrix: multiplying by it leaves every two-coordinate vector unchanged. Both orders restore the original vector, so $B$ is an inverse of $A$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $I_2$ 是单位矩阵：乘以它以后，每个二维向量都保持原样。两个作用次序都能恢复原向量，所以 $B$ 是 $A$ 的逆矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Require recovery in both orders
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*要求两个次序都能恢复*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ square matrix over the real or complex numbers, with $n$ a positive integer. The identity $I_n$ has $1$ in each diagonal position and $0$ elsewhere, so $I_nx=x$ for every $n$-coordinate column $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为实数或复数的 $n\times n$ 方阵，$n$ 为正整数。单位矩阵 $I_n$ 的对角元为 $1$、其余位置为 $0$，因此对每个有 $n$ 个坐标的列向量 $x$，都有 $I_nx=x$。

> [!abstract] Invertible and singular · 可逆与奇异
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A matrix $B$ of the same size is an **inverse** of $A$ if
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若同尺寸矩阵 $B$ 满足
>
> $$BA=AB=I_n,$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> then $A$ is **invertible** (or **nonsingular**), and its inverse is written $A^{-1}$. If no such $B$ exists, $A$ is **singular**, meaning noninvertible. An inverse, when it exists, is unique; that fact is proved in [[逆矩阵唯一性|uniqueness of the inverse]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 则称 $B$ 为 $A$ 的**逆矩阵**，$A$ **可逆**或**非奇异**，其逆记为 $A^{-1}$。若不存在这样的 $B$，$A$ 就**奇异**，也就是不可逆。逆矩阵存在时只有一个，证明见[[逆矩阵唯一性]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation $BA=I_n$ says $B(Ax)=x$: do $A$, then undo it with $B$. The equation $AB=I_n$ says $A(By)=y$: do $B$, then undo it with $A$. Matrix products act from right to left, so the two equations describe two possible starting orders. In particular, if $Ax=b$ and the inverse exists, multiplying by $A^{-1}$ recovers $x=A^{-1}b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$BA=I_n$ 表示 $B(Ax)=x$：先做 $A$，再用 $B$ 撤销。$AB=I_n$ 表示 $A(By)=y$：先做 $B$，再用 $A$ 撤销。矩阵乘积从右往左作用，所以两个等式描述两种起始次序。特别地，如果 $Ax=b$ 且逆矩阵存在，左乘 $A^{-1}$ 就能恢复 $x=A^{-1}b$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Mixing coordinates can still be reversible
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*混合坐标以后也可以撤销*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nondiagonal example makes clear why inversion is not taking a reciprocal of each entry. Let
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个非对角的例子能说明，求逆为什么不是把每个元素各自取倒数。令

$$S=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
T=\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$S$ sends $(u,v)^T$ to $(u+2v,v)^T$. The second coordinate remains available, so $T$ can subtract twice that coordinate from the first, recovering $(u,v)^T$. Direct multiplication in either order gives $ST=TS=I_2$, hence $T=S^{-1}$. The entry $2$ has become $-2$, and the zero entry causes no problem; entrywise reciprocals would not produce this inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$S$ 把 $(u,v)^T$ 变成 $(u+2v,v)^T$。第二个坐标仍然保留着，所以 $T$ 可以从第一个坐标中减去第二个坐标的两倍，恢复 $(u,v)^T$。直接按两个次序相乘，均有 $ST=TS=I_2$，因此 $T=S^{-1}$。原来的元素 $2$ 在逆中变成了 $-2$，零元素也没有妨碍求逆；逐元素取倒数得不到这个结果。

## 边界

> [!warning] Lost information cannot be recovered · 丢掉的信息无法恢复
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The square matrix $K=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}$ sends both $z=(-2,1)^T$ and the zero vector to zero. If an inverse existed, applying it to that one output would have to return two different inputs, which is impossible. Algebraically, $Kz=0$ and $K^{-1}K=I$ would imply $z=K^{-1}0=0$, contradicting $z\ne0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 方阵 $K=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}$ 把 $z=(-2,1)^T$ 和零向量都变成零。如果存在逆，对同一个输出施加它，就必须恢复成两个不同输入，这是不可能的。代数上，$Kz=0$ 与 $K^{-1}K=I$ 会推出 $z=K^{-1}0=0$，与 $z\ne0$ 矛盾。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a nonzero vector in the [[零空间|null space]], the set of inputs sent to zero. It shows why a square shape alone does not guarantee invertibility. General ways to decide include pivots, [[矩阵秩|rank]], and the [[可逆性与非零行列式|determinant criterion]]; an explicit computational method is [[Gauss-Jordan求逆|Gauss–Jordan inversion]]. Those criteria and procedures are separate from the definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里出现了[[零空间]]中的非零向量；零空间就是被送到零的输入集合。它说明方阵这一形状本身不保证可逆。一般判断方法包括主元、[[矩阵秩|秩]]以及[[可逆性与非零行列式|行列式判据]]，具体计算方法可用[[Gauss-Jordan求逆]]。这些判据与算法和逆矩阵的定义各自承担不同作用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For rectangular matrices, distinguish a [[左逆|left inverse]], which satisfies $LA=I$, from a [[右逆|right inverse]], which satisfies $AR=I$; the identity sizes depend on the side. They are not automatically ordinary two-sided inverses. For finite square matrices, however, a one-sided inverse does imply the other identity. That stronger result is the [[方阵单侧逆|one-sided inverse theorem for square matrices]], not an omitted part of the definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于矩形矩阵，要区分满足 $LA=I$ 的[[左逆]]与满足 $AR=I$ 的[[右逆]]，两侧的单位矩阵尺寸也要分别匹配。它们不会自动成为普通双侧逆。不过，对有限阶方阵，只存在一侧逆就能推出另一侧也成立。这是更强的[[方阵单侧逆|方阵单侧逆定理]]，不是定义里省略的条件。
