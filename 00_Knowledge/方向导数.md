# Directional derivatives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方向导数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **directional derivative** measures how an output initially changes as the input moves along a specified straight line. On a temperature map $T(x,y)$, moving diagonally can change both coordinates at once. The directional derivative gives the local rate along that chosen route.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**方向导数**测量输入沿指定直线移动时，输出在起点附近的变化率。对温度图 $T(x,y)$，斜着走会同时改变两个坐标；方向导数给出沿这条选定路线的局部变化率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First specify a starting point $a$ and a nonzero vector $v$. The path $a+tv$ starts at $a$ when $t=0$; increasing $t$ moves along $v$, while negative $t$ moves the other way. This card uses the **vector convention**: the rate is per unit of the parameter $t$. Many calculus texts instead use a unit vector to report the rate per unit distance. We will connect the two conventions explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先指定起点 $a$ 和非零向量 $v$。路径 $a+tv$ 在 $t=0$ 时位于 $a$；$t$ 增大时沿 $v$ 前进，负 $t$ 则走向另一侧。本卡采用**向量约定**，即每单位参数 $t$ 的变化率。许多微积分教材则先使用单位向量，报告每单位距离的变化率；下面会明确两种约定的联系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Restrict the function to one line
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把函数限制到一条直线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f:U\to\mathbb R$, where $U$ is an [[开集|open set]] containing $a$: a sufficiently small ball around $a$ stays in the domain. Thus $a+tv$ is available for both positive and negative $t$ close to zero. Substitute the path into $f$ to get the one-variable function $\phi(t)=f(a+tv)$. Its ordinary [[导数|derivative]] at zero defines
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f:U\to\mathbb R$，其中 $U$ 是包含 $a$ 的[[开集]]：$a$ 周围足够小的球仍在定义域内。因此零附近的正、负 $t$ 都能使 $a+tv$ 留在定义域中。把路径代入 $f$，得到一元函数 $\phi(t)=f(a+tv)$；它在零点的普通[[导数]]定义了

$$D_vf(a)=\phi'(0)
=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{f(a+tv)-f(a)}{t},$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
when the two-sided limit is finite. Here $D_v$ names differentiation along the specified vector; $t$ is the single variable being differentiated, and the point $a$ and vector $v$ stay fixed. A one-sided limit as $t\downarrow0$ is a different convention and must be labelled as such.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件是双侧极限为有限值。$D_v$ 表示沿指定向量求导；真正参与求导的单一变量是 $t$，起点 $a$ 与向量 $v$ 都保持固定。若只取 $t\downarrow0$ 的单侧极限，那是另一个约定，必须标明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Minimal example: write the line first, then differentiate one variable
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子：先写直线，再做一元求导

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x,y)=x^2y$, start at $a=(1,2)$ and choose $v=(3,4)$. Each parameter step changes $x$ by three times that step and $y$ by four times it. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x,y)=x^2y$，取起点 $a=(1,2)$、向量 $v=(3,4)$。每次参数增量都使 $x$ 改变其三倍、$y$ 改变其四倍，因此

$$(x(t),y(t))=(1+3t,2+4t),\qquad
\phi(t)=(1+3t)^2(2+4t)=2+16t+42t^2+36t^3.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $\phi(0)=2$, the difference quotient is $16+42t+36t^2$. Letting $t\to0$ gives $D_vf(1,2)=16$. For a small step $t$, the leading output change is $16t$. This direct substitution needs no gradient formula; it is exactly the defining one-variable derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $\phi(0)=2$，差商为 $16+42t+36t^2$。令 $t\to0$，得到 $D_vf(1,2)=16$；小参数步长 $t$ 对应的主导输出变化为 $16t$。这个代入法不需要先用梯度公式，直接就是定义中的一元导数。

> [!note] Per unit parameter or per unit distance? · 按参数还是按距离
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]] of $v=(3,4)$ is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$. For a rate per unit distance in the input plane, use $u=v/5=(3/5,4/5)$, which has length $1$. Substitution gives $D_uf(1,2)=16/5$. The numbers $16$ and $16/5$ describe the same ray using different parameter speeds; neither contradicts the other.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $v=(3,4)$ 的[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]为 $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$。若要输入平面中每单位距离的变化率，就用长度为 $1$ 的 $u=v/5=(3/5,4/5)$；代入得到 $D_uf(1,2)=16/5$。$16$ 和 $16/5$ 使用不同参数速度描述同一射线，二者并不矛盾。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, [[方向导数尺度|scaling the vector]] changes the rate by the same factor. With a nonzero vector and the Euclidean unit choice $u=v/\|v\|_2$, this gives $D_uf(a)=D_vf(a)/\|v\|_2$ whenever the derivative exists. “Per unit distance” assumes that the input coordinates use a specified common length scale; changing units can change that interpretation. The zero vector would make a constant path and produce zero, but it selects no geometric direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，[[方向导数尺度|把向量缩放]]会让变化率按同一比例缩放。对非零向量，取欧氏单位向量 $u=v/\|v\|_2$，只要导数存在，就有 $D_uf(a)=D_vf(a)/\|v\|_2$。“每单位距离”预先采用了输入坐标的共同长度尺度；改变单位也会改变这种解释。零向量只给出恒定路径和零变化率，没有选择任何几何方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A partial derivative is the coordinate-direction case
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 偏导数是坐标方向的特例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The vector $e_i$, with a $1$ in coordinate $i$ and zeros elsewhere, moves only that coordinate. Therefore $D_{e_i}f(a)=\partial_i f(a)$: a [[偏导数|partial derivative]] is the coordinate-axis case. For the example at $(1,2)$, these rates are $D_{(1,0)}f=4$ and $D_{(0,1)}f=1$. The vector $(3,4)$ gives $3(4)+4(1)=16$ because this polynomial is differentiable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

向量 $e_i$ 的第 $i$ 个坐标为 $1$、其余为零，因此只改变这一坐标。所以 $D_{e_i}f(a)=\partial_i f(a)$：[[偏导数]]就是坐标轴方向的特例。对本例的点 $(1,2)$，两个坐标变化率为 $D_{(1,0)}f=4$、$D_{(0,1)}f=1$。由于这个多项式可微，向量 $(3,4)$ 给出 $3(4)+4(1)=16$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
That combination is justified in general by [[方向导数梯度公式|the gradient formula]] when a [[全微分|total differential]] exists: one linear approximation then controls all small input changes. Directional derivatives alone do not supply that assumption. A function may have a derivative along one line but not another, or even [[方向导数不可推可微|have all directional derivatives without being differentiable]]. The linked boundary card explains the failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般情形中，在[[全微分]]存在、同一个线性近似控制所有小输入变化时，[[方向导数梯度公式]]保证这种组合。方向导数本身没有提供这个前提。函数可能沿某条线有导数、另一条线没有，甚至[[方向导数不可推可微|所有方向导数都存在却仍不可微]]；链接卡解释了其中缺口。

> [!example] Check the two sides of the line · 检查直线两侧
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x,y)=|x|$ at $(0,0)$, moving along $(0,1)$ leaves the output zero, so that directional derivative is zero. Along $(1,0)$, the quotient is $|t|/t$, with right limit $1$ and left limit $-1$. Its two-sided directional derivative does not exist, even though its forward one-sided rate exists.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对原点处的 $f(x,y)=|x|$，沿 $(0,1)$ 移动时输出始终为零，所以该方向导数为零；沿 $(1,0)$ 的差商则为 $|t|/t$，右极限为 $1$、左极限为 $-1$，双侧方向导数不存在，尽管向前的单侧变化率存在。
