# 净现值把全部增量现金流折到同一时点后按正负判断价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Net present value discounts all incremental cash flows to one date and uses its sign to judge value creation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子决策
> 净现值是项目全部增量现金流按匹配机会成本折到同一评价时点后的带符号总和：
> $$NPV=\sum_{t=0}^{T}CF_tDF_t.$$
> 独立项目在估计与贴现率都一致时：$NPV>0$ 增加价值，应接受；$NPV<0$ 减少价值，应拒绝；$NPV=0$ 恰好赚取必要回报。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> Net present value is the signed sum of all incremental project cash flows discounted to one valuation date at the matching opportunity cost:
> $$NPV=\sum_{t=0}^{T}CF_tDF_t.$$
> For an independent project with consistent estimates and rates: $NPV>0$ creates value and should be accepted; $NPV<0$ destroys value and should be rejected; $NPV=0$ earns exactly the required return.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如今天支出 1,000，未来三年年末收入 300、400、500，年有效贴现率 8%：
$$NPV=-1000+\frac{300}{1.08}+\frac{400}{1.08^2}+\frac{500}{1.08^3}=17.63.$$
正的 17.63 是在补偿 8% 机会成本以后仍新增的今天价值，不是未来现金流的简单会计利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, spend 1,000 today and receive 300, 400, and 500 at the next three year-ends at an 8% effective annual discount rate:
$$NPV=-1000+\frac{300}{1.08}+\frac{400}{1.08^2}+\frac{500}{1.08^3}=17.63.$$
The positive 17.63 is value added today after compensating the 8% opportunity cost, not the simple accounting surplus of future cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个项目未来总流入大于总流出，为什么仍可能 NPV 为负？
>
> **答案：** 未折现总额忽略了现金流日期和机会成本。若流入太晚或不足以补偿匹配风险的必要回报，折到今天后仍会小于初始与后续投入。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can a project have total future inflows above total outflows and still have negative NPV?
>
> **Answer:** Undiscounted totals ignore timing and opportunity cost. If inflows arrive too late or fail to compensate the required return for their risk, their present value can remain below the present value of investment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
