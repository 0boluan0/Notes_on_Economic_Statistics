# The complex Pythagorean converse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*复空间中的勾股逆命题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[正交向量勾股关系|Pythagorean identity]] says that orthogonal vectors have additive squared lengths. Can we run that test backwards: if the squared lengths add, must the vectors be orthogonal? For two real vectors, yes. For two complex vectors, one such equality checks only part of the inner product and can miss a nonzero imaginary part.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[正交向量勾股关系]]说明，正交向量的长度平方可以相加。能否反过来检验：如果长度平方相加成立，向量就一定正交吗？对两个实向量可以；对两个复向量，一条这样的等式只检查了内积的一部分，可能漏掉非零虚部。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One complex coordinate already gives a counterexample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小反例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work in $\mathbb C$, treating each complex number as a one-coordinate vector. Let $i^2=-1$, $x=1$ and $y=i$. Use the usual [[内积|inner product]] $\langle x,y\rangle=\overline{x}y$, conjugate-linear in its first argument, and the norm $\|x\|=|x|$, the complex modulus. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\mathbb C$ 中，把每个复数看成只有一个坐标的向量。令 $i^2=-1$，取 $x=1$、$y=i$。使用通常的[[内积]] $\langle x,y\rangle=\overline{x}y$，即对第一个自变量共轭线性；范数 $\|x\|=|x|$ 是复数的模。于是

$$\|x+y\|^2=|1+i|^2=2=|1|^2+|i|^2=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
But $\langle1,i\rangle=i\ne0$. The vectors fail the definition of [[正交|orthogonality]], even though the displayed squared lengths add perfectly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

然而 $\langle1,i\rangle=i\ne0$，不符合[[正交]]的定义，尽管上面的长度平方确实相加。

> [!note] A right angle in the picture uses a different scalar field · 图中的直角使用了不同数域
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On the ordinary complex-plane picture, $1$ and $i$ appear along perpendicular real axes. That picture treats $\mathbb C$ as the two-dimensional **real** space $\mathbb R^2$, with real dot product $\operatorname{Re}(\overline{x}y)$. Complex-inner-product orthogonality requires the whole number $\overline{x}y$ to vanish. The same norm can therefore accompany different orthogonality questions when the scalar field changes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在通常的复平面图里，$1$ 与 $i$ 位于彼此垂直的实坐标轴上。那张图把 $\mathbb C$ 看成二维**实**空间 $\mathbb R^2$，实点积为 $\operatorname{Re}(\overline{x}y)$。复内积的正交要求整个复数 $\overline{x}y$ 为零。因此，改变标量数域后，同样的范数也可能对应不同的正交问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What one equality actually tests
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一条等式究竟检验了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now let $x,y$ be vectors in any complex inner-product space, with $\|v\|^2=\langle v,v\rangle$. Write $z=\langle x,y\rangle=a+ib$, where $a,b$ are real numbers. Thus $a=\operatorname{Re}z$ is the real part and $b=\operatorname{Im}z$ the imaginary part. Conjugate symmetry gives $\langle y,x\rangle=\bar z=a-ib$. Expanding yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在令 $x,y$ 为任意复内积空间中的向量，并采用 $\|v\|^2=\langle v,v\rangle$。把 $z=\langle x,y\rangle$ 写成 $a+ib$，其中 $a,b$ 为实数。因此 $a=\operatorname{Re}z$ 为实部，$b=\operatorname{Im}z$ 为虚部。共轭对称性给出 $\langle y,x\rangle=\bar z=a-ib$。展开得

$$\begin{aligned}
\|x+y\|^2
&=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2+z+\bar z\\
&=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2+2a.
\end{aligned}$$

> [!important] The exact converse · 精确的反向结论
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The equality $\|x+y\|^2=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2$ is equivalent to $\operatorname{Re}\langle x,y\rangle=0$. Orthogonality requires both real and imaginary parts to vanish. A nonzero purely imaginary inner product survives the first test.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 等式 $\|x+y\|^2=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2$ 等价于 $\operatorname{Re}\langle x,y\rangle=0$。正交却要求实部和虚部同时为零。非零纯虚内积能够通过第一次检验。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the real case is different
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 实数域边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a real inner-product space, the inner product is already real. Its real part vanishes exactly when the whole inner product vanishes. Therefore, for two real vectors with the norm induced by that same inner product,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在实内积空间中，内积本来就是实数，实部为零就等于整个内积为零。因此，对两个实向量，若范数由同一内积给出，则

$$\|x+y\|^2=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x\perp y.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero vectors cause no exception. Over complex spaces the forward implication from orthogonality to equality remains valid; only the converse from this single equality fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零向量不会造成例外。复数情形下，“正交推出等式”的正向结论仍然成立，失败的只是从这一条等式反推正交。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A second phase checks the missing part
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用第二个相位检查漏掉的部分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the first-argument-conjugate convention, multiplying $y$ by $i$ gives $\langle x,iy\rangle=iz=-b+ia$. Also $\|iy\|=\|y\|$, because $|i|=1$. Applying the same expansion to $x$ and $iy$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用第一个自变量共轭线性的约定，把 $y$ 乘以 $i$，就有 $\langle x,iy\rangle=iz=-b+ia$。又因 $|i|=1$，故 $\|iy\|=\|y\|$。把同样的展开用于 $x$ 和 $iy$，得到

$$\|x+iy\|^2=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2-2\operatorname{Im}\langle x,y\rangle.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus, if **both** $\|x+y\|^2$ and $\|x+iy\|^2$ equal $\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2$, then $a=b=0$ and $x\perp y$. Conversely, orthogonality makes both equalities hold. In the counterexample, $x+iy=1+i^2=0$, so the second equality fails: its left side is $0$, not $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，若 $\|x+y\|^2$ 与 $\|x+iy\|^2$ **都**等于 $\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2$，就有 $a=b=0$，从而 $x\perp y$。反过来，正交也保证两条等式都成立。在反例中，$x+iy=1+i^2=0$，所以第二条等式不成立：左边是 $0$，并不是 $2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Books that conjugate the second argument instead obtain the opposite sign on the imaginary cross term. The conclusion is unchanged: testing the ordinary sum detects the real part, and testing a quarter-turn of one vector detects the imaginary part. Computing the full inner product directly is usually the simplest test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若教材改为对第二个自变量取共轭，虚部交叉项的符号会相反，但结论不变：普通和检验实部，把一个向量乘以 $i$ 再检验则能检测虚部。通常直接计算完整内积最简单。
