# 复内积空间中的勾股等式只推出内积实部为零而不一定推出正交
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a complex inner-product space, the Pythagorean equality forces only the real part of the inner product to vanish and need not imply orthogonality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 反向边界
> 在复内积空间中，
> $$
> \|x+y\|^2=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2
> $$
> 等价于 $\operatorname{Re}\langle x,y\rangle=0$，但正交要求更强的 $\langle x,y\rangle=0$。因此勾股等式不能反推出 $x\perp y$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Over a complex inner-product space, the Pythagorean equality says only that the real part of $\langle x,y\rangle$ is zero. Orthogonality requires the entire complex inner product to be zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

展开平方范数得到
$$
\|x+y\|^2
=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2
+\langle x,y\rangle+\overline{\langle x,y\rangle}
=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2+2\operatorname{Re}\langle x,y\rangle.
$$
所以等式只消掉交叉项的实部，纯虚内积仍可存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two cross terms sum to twice the real part of the inner product. The equality therefore removes only that real part; a nonzero purely imaginary inner product may remain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小反例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal counterexample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\mathbb C$ 中取 $x=1$、$y=i$。则
$$
|1+i|^2=2=|1|^2+|i|^2,
$$
但在通常 Hermitian 内积约定下 $\langle1,i\rangle=i\ne0$，所以 $x$ 与 $y$ 不正交。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $\mathbb C$, take $x=1$ and $y=i$. The Pythagorean equality holds, yet their Hermitian inner product is nonzero, so they are not orthogonal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 实数域边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Real-field boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在实内积空间中，内积本身就是实数，所以 $\operatorname{Re}\langle x,y\rangle=0$ 等于 $\langle x,y\rangle=0$；此时勾股等式确实反推正交。反例只阻断复数域中的反向蕴含，不影响“正交推出勾股”的正向定理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over a real inner-product space the inner product is already real, so the equality does imply orthogonality. The counterexample blocks only the converse over complex spaces; it does not affect the forward implication from orthogonality to Pythagoras.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 复内积空间中已知 $\|x+y\|^2=\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2$，最多能推出关于 $\langle x,y\rangle$ 的什么结论？
>
> **答案：** 只能推出 $\operatorname{Re}\langle x,y\rangle=0$；虚部可能非零。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Question:** In a complex inner-product space, what is the strongest conclusion about $\langle x,y\rangle$ from the Pythagorean equality alone?
>
> **Answer:** Only $\operatorname{Re}\langle x,y\rangle=0$; the imaginary part may be nonzero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
