# 贝叶斯博弈中，混合策略分布在完整纯策略上，行为策略则按类型指定行动分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a Bayesian game, a mixed strategy is a distribution over complete pure strategies, whereas a behavioral strategy assigns an action distribution to each type*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两种随机化回答的是不同问题。令完整纯策略集合为 $S_i=A_i^{T_i}$。normal-form **混合策略**是

$$
\mu_i\in\Delta(S_i),
$$

即在得知自身类型前，对“每个类型分别做什么”的完整计划随机化；**行为策略**则是

$$
\beta_i:T_i\to\Delta(A_i),
$$

即观察到类型后，只在该类型可选的行动之间随机化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two forms of randomization answer different questions. Let $S_i=A_i^{T_i}$ be the set of complete pure type-contingent plans. A normal-form mixed strategy randomizes over those complete plans before the player learns their type, whereas a behavioral strategy randomizes among the actions available after a particular type is observed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么不能把二者写成同一个对象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the two objects should not be identified*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

混合策略 $\mu_i$ 还能规定不同反事实类型下行动之间的相关性；行为策略 $\beta_i$ 只给出每个已观察类型下的行动分布。因此，看到 $\beta_i(a_i\mid t_i)$ 不能反推出唯一的完整计划分布。有限一次性模型中二者何时实现相同结果、为何仍不一一对应，单独见[[贝叶斯随机化结果等价]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A mixed strategy can additionally correlate the prescriptions attached to different counterfactual types, whereas a behavioral strategy records only the action distribution after each observed type. The type-by-type marginals therefore do not identify a unique distribution over complete plans. See [[贝叶斯随机化结果等价|Bayesian randomization outcome equivalence]] for the finite one-shot equivalence and its non-uniqueness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
