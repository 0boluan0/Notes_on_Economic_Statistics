# Convergence to a constant
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*常数极限下，依分布收敛也能控制误差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a limiting distribution puts all its probability at one number $c$, getting close to that distribution means putting nearly all probability near $c$. There is no random target value to pair differently. In this special case, [[依分布收敛|convergence in distribution]] is enough to guarantee [[依概率收敛|convergence in probability]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当极限分布把全部概率放在一个数 $c$ 上，分布接近这个极限，就意味着几乎全部概率都集中到 $c$ 附近。这里没有一个仍然随机的目标值可以被重新配对。在这个特例中，[[依分布收敛]]足以保证[[依概率收敛]]。

> [!important] A deterministic limit · 确定的极限值
> $$X_n\Rightarrow c\quad\Longrightarrow\quad X_n\xrightarrow p c.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $X_n$ is real-valued and $c$ is a fixed finite real number. No independence or moment assumption is required. The condition only involves each $X_n$'s distance from $c$, so it remains meaningful even if the variables are originally on different probability spaces, using their respective probabilities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $X_n$ 实值，$c$ 是固定的有限实数。不要求独立或矩条件。结论只涉及各个 $X_n$ 到 $c$ 的距离；即使变量原来定义在不同概率空间上，分别使用各自的概率，这个条件也仍有意义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the two tails of the CDF
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*直接看分布函数的两侧尾部*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[累积分布函数|CDF]] of the constant $c$ is zero to its left and one at $c$ and to its right. It jumps at $c$, so distributional convergence need not give $F_n(c)\to1$. Instead choose a fixed tolerance $\varepsilon>0$: the two points $c-\varepsilon$ and $c+\varepsilon$ are both continuity points of the limiting CDF.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常数 $c$ 的[[累积分布函数]]在 $c$ 左边为 0，在 $c$ 及右边为 1。它在 $c$ 处跳跃，因此依分布收敛未必保证 $F_n(c)\to1$。应当固定容忍值 $\varepsilon>0$，观察 $c-\varepsilon$ 与 $c+\varepsilon$：这两点都是极限 CDF 的连续点。

$$F_n(c-\varepsilon)\longrightarrow0,\qquad F_n(c+\varepsilon)\longrightarrow1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Being more than $\varepsilon$ away from $c$ means lying in one of the two tails. The left strict tail is contained in the left non-strict tail counted by the CDF, hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

距 $c$ 超过 $\varepsilon$，就是落入两侧尾部之一。左侧严格小于事件包含在 CDF 计算的小于等于事件中，因此

$$\begin{aligned}
P(|X_n-c|>\varepsilon)
&=P(X_n<c-\varepsilon)+P(X_n>c+\varepsilon)\\
&\le F_n(c-\varepsilon)+1-F_n(c+\varepsilon)\longrightarrow0.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the probability-convergence definition for every fixed positive tolerance. There is no need to make a claim about the CDF at its jump.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这正是对每个固定正容忍值的依概率收敛定义，无须对跳点上的 CDF 作出额外断言。

> [!example] A rare nonzero value · 偶尔出现的非零值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $P(X_n=1)=1/n$ and $P(X_n=0)=1-1/n$. For $t<0$, the CDF is zero; for $0<t<1$ it is $1-1/n$; for $t\ge1$ it is one. Thus $X_n\Rightarrow0$. The theorem gives $X_n\xrightarrow p0$; directly, the error probability is $1/n$ for $0<\varepsilon<1$ and zero for $\varepsilon\ge1$. No joint construction is needed for these calculations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $P(X_n=1)=1/n$、$P(X_n=0)=1-1/n$，则 CDF 在 $t<0$ 为 0，在 $0<t<1$ 为 $1-1/n$，在 $t\ge1$ 为 1，所以 $X_n\Rightarrow0$。本定理给出 $X_n\xrightarrow p0$；直接计算，$0<\varepsilon<1$ 时超差概率为 $1/n$，$\varepsilon\ge1$ 时为零。这些计算不需要指定变量之间的联合构造。

## The closed-set proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## Portmanteau 证明

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Portmanteau定理|Portmanteau theorem]] gives another useful proof: for any fixed [[闭集|closed set]] $K$, distributional convergence implies $\limsup_nP(X_n\in K)\le P(X\in K)$. This bounds every possible limiting upper value of the probabilities. Take the closed region outside the $\varepsilon$-neighbourhood of $c$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Portmanteau定理]]提供另一种常用证明：对任意固定[[闭集]] $K$，依分布收敛保证 $\limsup_nP(X_n\in K)\le P(X\in K)$，也就是控制这些概率可能达到的上极限。取 $c$ 的 $\varepsilon$ 邻域外的闭区域：

$$K_\varepsilon=(-\infty,c-\varepsilon]\cup[c+\varepsilon,\infty).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The limiting law $\delta_c$ means unit probability at $c$. Since $K_\varepsilon$ excludes $c$, it has $\delta_c$-probability zero, and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极限分布 $\delta_c$ 表示概率一集中于 $c$。因为 $K_\varepsilon$ 不含 $c$，其极限概率为零，故

$$0\le\limsup_{n\to\infty}P(|X_n-c|\ge\varepsilon)
=\limsup_{n\to\infty}P(X_n\in K_\varepsilon)
\le\delta_c(K_\varepsilon)=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The nonnegative probabilities therefore tend to zero, which also bounds the strictly larger-than-$\varepsilon$ event. For a nonconstant random limit, this fixed closed region cannot generally represent the event $|X_n-X|\ge\varepsilon$; that event depends on a joint pairing. See the [[分布收敛不推概率收敛|coupling counterexample]]. Together with the [[概率收敛推出分布收敛|forward implication]], we obtain equivalence of the two convergence modes for a constant limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非负概率因此趋于零，也就控制了严格大于 $\varepsilon$ 的事件。若目标 $X$ 仍然随机，一个固定闭区域一般不能表示 $|X_n-X|\ge\varepsilon$ 事件，因为后者依赖两者的联合配对，见[[分布收敛不推概率收敛|耦合反例]]。结合[[概率收敛推出分布收敛|正向蕴含]]，常数极限下这两种收敛方式等价。
