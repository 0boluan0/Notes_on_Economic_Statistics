# 必要劳动是在工作日中生产劳动力价值等价物的劳动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Necessary labour produces an equivalent of labour-power's value within the working day*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必要劳动（necessary labour）是工人在工作日中创造 [[劳动力价值]] 等价物的那部分劳动；进行这部分劳动所需的时长，称为必要劳动时间。在劳动力按其价值出售的分析条件下，这部分新价值等于购买该劳动力所用的可变资本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Necessary labour is the part of a worker's labour during the working day that creates an equivalent of the [[劳动力价值|value of labour-power]]. Its duration is necessary labour time. Under the assumption that labour-power sells at its value, this new value equals the variable capital used to purchase that labour-power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“再生产”等价物不要求工人亲手生产自己消费的生活资料。社会分工中，工人可以生产纱；只要这部分纱所体现的新增价值等于维持劳动力所需生活资料的价值，就满足此处的价值关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Producing this equivalent does not require the worker to make their own means of subsistence. Within the social division of labour, a worker may produce yarn. The relevant relation holds when the new value represented by this part of the yarn equals the value of the means of subsistence required to maintain labour-power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设一天劳动力价值为400单位，劳动力按价值支付；同样的平均社会劳动在正常且不变的强度下，每小时新创100价值单位，那么必要劳动时间为4小时。工作日若有8小时，其余4小时对应 [[剩余劳动]]。这是教学构造中的价值分割，并不要求现实生产活动在第4小时突然改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a day's labour-power is worth 400 units and is paid at its value. If the same average social labour at normal, unchanged intensity creates 100 value units an hour, necessary labour time is four hours. In an eight-hour working day, the remaining four hours correspond to [[剩余劳动|surplus labour]]. This is a value division in a constructed example; the actual production activity need not suddenly change after the fourth hour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阅读一般商品价值时遇到的“社会必要劳动时间”，则衡量特定商品按社会正常条件生产所需的劳动。两种用法的联系与判断见 [[两种必要劳动时间]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the analysis of commodity value, “socially necessary labour time” measures the labour needed to produce a particular commodity under normal social conditions. See [[两种必要劳动时间|the distinction between the two uses of necessary labour time]] for how these usages connect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Marx，《资本论》卷I，[第9章第1节](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch09.htm#S1)，从工人通过社会分工生产生活资料的价值等价物，到必要劳动及必要劳动时间的命名：支持定义与分工条件；该章注5说明两种“必要劳动时间”用法。MIA 所载 Moore / Aveling 英译、Engels 编，1887英文初版底本，标示处纳入德文第四版改动。4小时例为教学构造。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volume I, Chapter 9, Section 1 defines necessary labour and its duration through the production of an equivalent of subsistence value under the social division of labour. Note 5 explains the two uses of “necessary labour time.” The MIA text uses the Moore and Aveling translation edited by Engels, based on the 1887 first English edition with indicated fourth German edition changes. The four-hour illustration is constructed.
