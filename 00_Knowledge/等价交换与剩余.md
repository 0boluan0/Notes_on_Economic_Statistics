# 按价值购买劳动力后使用它超过必要劳动时间可以产生剩余价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Labour-power purchased at its value can produce surplus value when used beyond necessary labour time*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本按 [[劳动力价值]] 购买一定期间内 [[Marx劳动力|劳动力]] 的使用权，在生产中使它发挥作用。购买价格支付的是劳动力这个商品的价值；其使用形成的活劳动却可以创造更大的新价值。劳动过程越过再生产劳动力价值等价物的时点，便由 [[必要劳动]] 延续为 [[剩余劳动]]，形成由资本占有的 [[剩余价值]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Capital purchases the use of [[Marx劳动力|labour-power]] for a period at its [[劳动力价值|value]] and puts it to work in production. The purchase price pays for the value of this commodity, while its use as living labour can create more new value. When production continues beyond the point at which an equivalent of labour-power's value has been produced, [[必要劳动|necessary labour]] extends into [[剩余劳动|surplus labour]], creating [[剩余价值|surplus value]] appropriated by capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把这一机制放进一个可算的教学构造：劳动力一天价值400单位，按价值购买；一天工作8小时，在正常且不变的强度下，平均社会劳动每小时表现为100价值单位。再假设当天正常耗用的生产资料按其1200单位价值购买，并且货币价值不变。活劳动创造800新价值，产品包含的总价值因此为 $1200+800=2000$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a constructed numerical example. A day's labour-power is worth 400 units and is purchased at that value. The worker works eight hours at normal, unchanged intensity, with each hour of average social labour expressed in 100 value units. Assume that the means of production normally used up that day are also purchased at their value of 1,200 units, with the value of money unchanged. Living labour creates 800 units of new value, making the product's total value $1200+800=2000$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本家拥有这一生产过程的产品；若按2000单位的价值售出，在补回当期耗用的生产资料1200和劳动力支出400后，取得400的剩余。投入和产出都按价值交换，剩余的生成环节仍发生在生产中的劳动力使用上。销售使产品中的剩余价值实现为货币，衔接 [[Marx资本|资本的价值增殖运动]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The capitalist owns the product of this process. If it sells at its value of 2,000 units, replacing 1,200 of consumed means of production and 400 spent on labour-power leaves a surplus of 400. Inputs and output have all exchanged at their values; surplus was generated through the use of labour-power in production. Sale realises the surplus contained in the product as money, connecting the process to [[Marx资本|capital's movement of valorisation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的1200只代表当期耗用。若生产还使用有未耗用余额的机器，该余额仍留在机器中；完整比较货币预付与期末结果时，应一并计算这项资产，见 [[产品价值分解]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The 1,200 represents only current consumption. If production also uses machinery with an unconsumed balance, that value remains in the machine. A complete comparison of the money advance with the end result must also include that asset; see [[产品价值分解|product-value decomposition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Marx，《资本论》卷I，[第7章第1–2节](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch07.htm)：第1节结尾支持资本控制生产过程与产品归属；第2节从劳动力自身价值及其使用价值的区分，到27先令转为30先令的例子，再到等价交换、生产和流通的关系，支持完整机制。该章继而以是否越过劳动力价值等价物的再生产时点区分价值形成与价值增殖。MIA 所载 Moore / Aveling 英译、Engels 编，1887英文初版底本，标示处纳入德文第四版改动。本卡将原文机制改用统一8小时教学例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volume I, Chapter 7, Sections 1–2 supports the mechanism. The end of Section 1 establishes control of production and ownership of the product. Section 2 distinguishes the value and use value of labour-power, develops the 27-to-30-shilling example, and relates equivalent exchange to production and circulation. It then distinguishes value formation from valorisation by whether production continues beyond the reproduction of labour-power's equivalent. The MIA text uses the Moore and Aveling translation edited by Engels, based on the 1887 first English edition with indicated fourth German edition changes. This note applies the mechanism to the common eight-hour illustration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
