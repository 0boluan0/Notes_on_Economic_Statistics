# Detailed balance and reversibility
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*详细平衡与可逆性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[DTMC详细平衡|Detailed balance]] tests each pair of states. [[DTMC可逆性|Reversibility]] compares entire stationary recordings with their reverses. For a homogeneous Markov chain, these two tests are equivalent relative to the same probability distribution. The connection is useful because a collection of two-state equations can settle a question about paths of every length.

Throughout, the state space is finite or countable. In the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]], $p_{ij}$ is the probability of moving from $i$ to $j$ and $\sum_jp_{ij}=1$. The vector $\pi$ is a probability distribution: its entries are nonnegative and sum to one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[DTMC详细平衡|详细平衡]]逐对检查状态；[[DTMC可逆性|可逆性]]则比较整段平稳录像与它的倒放。对齐次 Markov 链，相对于同一个概率分布，这两个检查等价。因此一组两状态方程就能解决所有长度的路径问题。

本页状态空间有限或可数。采用[[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]]：$p_{ij}$ 表示从 $i$ 到 $j$，每行和为一；$\pi$ 非负且各条目总和为一。

> [!important] The exact relationship · 准确的关系
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $$\text{detailed balance relative to }\pi
> \iff\text{reversibility relative to }\pi
> \implies\pi P=\pi.$$
>
> The first condition does not need stationarity as an extra assumption: it implies it. Reversibility refers to the chain actually started in $\pi$. No irreducibility or aperiodicity is required, and zero entries in $\pi$ are allowed. The last implication cannot in general be reversed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 相对于同一 $\pi$，详细平衡与可逆性等价，并都推出 $\pi$ 平稳。详细平衡不必另加平稳假设，因为它会推出平稳；可逆性描述实际从 $\pi$ 启动的链。不要求不可约或非周期，允许 $\pi$ 含零条目。最后的箭头一般不能倒过来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Sum the pairwise equations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先把逐对方程求和*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a destination $j$. Detailed balance gives

$$(\pi P)_j=\sum_i\pi_i p_{ij}
=\sum_i\pi_jp_{ji}=\pi_j\sum_i p_{ji}=\pi_j.$$

In the last sum, $j$ is the fixed starting state and $i$ runs over destinations, so this is row $j$ summing to one. Nonnegative sums remain valid on a countable state space. Thus $\pi$ is a [[Markov稳态分布|stationary distribution]]: pairwise cancellation guarantees total mass conservation at each state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定目的状态 $j$，对流入项求和并使用详细平衡，就得到上面的等式。最后一个和里，$j$ 是固定的起点，$i$ 遍历终点，因此它是第 $j$ 行的和，等于一。可数状态空间下，这些非负和同样有效。于是 $\pi$ 是[[Markov稳态分布|平稳分布]]：逐对抵消保证每个状态的总质量守恒。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Move the starting mass along a path
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*沿着路径传递起始质量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a two-step path $a\to b\to c$, the forward probability is $\pi_ap_{ab}p_{bc}$. Apply detailed balance to the first pair, then the second:

$$\pi_ap_{ab}p_{bc}
=\pi_bp_{ba}p_{bc}
=\pi_cp_{cb}p_{ba}.$$

The last expression is the reversed path's probability. We moved the factor $\pi$ from the first state to the last while reversing each transition. Repeating the same operation for $m$ steps gives

$$\pi_{i_0}p_{i_0i_1}\cdots p_{i_{m-1}i_m}
=\pi_{i_m}p_{i_mi_{m-1}}\cdots p_{i_1i_0}.$$

These are the path probabilities supplied by the [[Markov性|Markov property]]. Equality for every finite state sequence proves equality of the corresponding joint distributions, hence reversibility. The calculation only multiplies equalities; it never divides by $\pi_i$, so it also covers paths with zero probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两步路径 $a\to b\to c$ 的概率是 $\pi_ap_{ab}p_{bc}$。先对第一对、再对第二对用详细平衡，得到 $\pi_cp_{cb}p_{ba}$，即倒序路径的概率。这个操作把 $\pi$ 因子从起点传到终点，同时逐步反向每条转移；重复便得到任意 $m$ 步的等式。

[[Markov性]]让这些乘积正是路径概率。每个有限状态序列都等概率，便证明相应联合分布相同，也就是可逆性。计算只乘等式，没有除以 $\pi_i$，因此也覆盖零概率路径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, reversibility applied to just $(X_0,X_1)$ gives

$$\Pr_\pi(X_0=i,X_1=j)=\Pr_\pi(X_0=j,X_1=i),$$

which is exactly $\pi_ip_{ij}=\pi_jp_{ji}$. This proves the reverse direction without inspecting longer paths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，把可逆性仅用于 $(X_0,X_1)$，上面的联合概率相等就是 $\pi_ip_{ij}=\pi_jp_{ji}$，无需再检查更长路径就得到详细平衡。

> [!example] Stationary mass with a circulating current · 总量平衡，流量仍循环
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take
>
> $$P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&1\\1&0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad\pi=(1/3,1/3,1/3).$$
>
> Each state receives mass $1/3$ from its predecessor, so $\pi P=\pi$. But the forward pair $(1,2)$ has probability $1/3$, while $(2,1)$ has probability zero. The chain is stationary from $\pi$ and not reversible: mass circulates around the ring instead of balancing each pair.
>
> Periodicity is not the reason for failure. Replace $P$ by $L=(I+P)/2$, where $I$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. Each state now stays put with probability $1/2$, making this finite irreducible chain aperiodic. Uniform $\pi$ remains stationary, yet $\pi_1L_{12}=1/6$ and $\pi_2L_{21}=0$. It still fails detailed balance, despite satisfying the [[有限链逐步收敛|finite-chain convergence theorem]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对上面的单向环，每个状态从前驱收到 $1/3$ 的质量，所以均匀分布平稳。但配对 $(1,2)$ 的概率为 $1/3$，反向配对为零；质量沿环循环，没有逐对平衡，因此不可逆。
>
> 失败并非由周期性造成。改成 $L=(I+P)/2$，其中 $I$ 为[[单位矩阵]]，就给每个状态加上概率 $1/2$ 的自环，使这条有限不可约链非周期。均匀分布仍平稳，但正向流量 $1/6$ 与反向零依旧不同。它满足[[有限链逐步收敛|有限链收敛定理]]，仍不满足详细平衡。

## Why the same distribution matters
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么必须说清“相对于哪个分布”*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $P=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ and $\pi=(1,0)$, all detailed-balance equations hold. The only stationary recording stays at state 1 forever, so it is reversible. The one-way transition from state 2 to state 1 is not observed under this initial law. Thus zeros in $\pi$ are legitimate; they do not assert symmetry for experiments started outside its positive-mass states.

If asked only to find a stationary distribution, detailed balance is a useful sufficient method. Failure to solve its equations does not show that no stationary distribution exists: the ring above has one. Solve $\pi P=\pi$ with probability constraints when pairwise balance is unavailable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $P=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ 和 $\pi=(1,0)$，全部详细平衡方程成立。唯一的平稳录像永远停在状态一，因此可逆；状态二到一的单向转移在这个初始律下不会出现。允许 $\pi$ 含零是合理的，但没有断言从正质量状态之外启动的实验也具有对称性。

若题目只要求平稳分布，详细平衡是一种有用的充分方法。它的方程无概率解，不表示平稳分布不存在，单向环就是反例。不能逐对平衡时，应带着概率约束解 $\pi P=\pi$。
