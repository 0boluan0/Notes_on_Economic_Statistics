# 路径依赖头寸的风险模拟必须保留风险因子的时间顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Risk simulation for path-dependent positions must preserve the time ordering of risk factors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 终点相同不等于损益相同
> 障碍、亚式平均、提前行权、累计现金流和动态对冲取决于到达终点的过程。只匹配持有期末分布，会丢失触碰、平均与再平衡信息。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Barriers, Asian averages, early exercise, accumulated cash flows, and dynamic hedging depend on the route to the terminal state. Matching only the horizon distribution loses touching, averaging, and rebalancing information.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

需要模拟有序路径

$$
x_{t_0}^{(i)},x_{t_0+\Delta}^{(i)},\ldots,x_{t_0+H}^{(i)}.
$$

例如两条路径都以价格 100 结束，但一条曾上穿障碍 120，另一条从未触碰；障碍期权的终值或提前失效状态可以不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two paths can both end at price 100 while only one crossed a barrier at 120; a barrier option can therefore have a different terminal or knock-out state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 只要模拟出的终点分布正确，就能给障碍期权做风险模拟吗？
>
> **答案：** 不能。还要模拟并验证持有期内的触碰顺序。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** Is a correct terminal distribution sufficient for risk simulation of a barrier option?
>
> **Answer:** No. The order of barrier touches within the holding period must also be simulated and validated.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- 只依赖终点状态的头寸不必保存完整路径。
- 保存有序路径仍不保证时间网格足够细；该数值边界见 [[路径模拟离散化误差]]。
- 动态对冲还需明确再平衡规则、交易成本和信息时点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- A terminal-state-only position does not require full paths.
- Preserving an ordered path does not prove that its time grid is sufficiently fine; see [[路径模拟离散化误差|path-simulation discretisation error]].
- Dynamic hedging also requires rebalancing rules, transaction costs, and information timing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
