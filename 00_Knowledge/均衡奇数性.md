# 每个有限非退化两人策略式博弈都有有限且为奇数个的纳什均衡，但退化博弈可以出现均衡连续统
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Every finite nondegenerate two-player strategic-form game has a finite odd number of Nash equilibria, while a degenerate game may have a continuum of equilibria*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**均衡奇数性**是对全部 Nash 均衡——纯的和混合的——一起计数的结构结论。它不是“随便一张矩阵都只能有 1、3、5 个均衡”的口诀；两人、有限策略和非退化缺一不可。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **oddness result** counts all Nash equilibria, pure and mixed together. It is not a rule that every payoff matrix must have exactly 1, 3, or 5 equilibria. Finiteness, two players, and nondegeneracy are part of the statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 非退化的操作含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Operational Meaning of Nondegeneracy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在两人有限博弈中，若一名玩家使用支持大小为 $k$ 的混合策略，对手至多有 $k$ 项纯策略同时成为最佳反应，则博弈是非退化的。若一个小支持意外制造了“过多”并列最佳反应，就出现退化；最佳反应对应可能重合成线段，均衡也可能不再是彼此孤立的点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a finite two-player game, nondegeneracy means that a mixed strategy with support size $k$ gives the opponent at most $k$ pure best responses. Too many tied best responses signal degeneracy. Best-response correspondences can then overlap along a segment rather than meet at isolated points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 性别博弈为什么常见三个均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why Battle of the Sexes Commonly Has Three Equilibria*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准性别博弈通常有两个协调的纯策略均衡和一个内点混合均衡，总数为 3。这里的“第三个”不是偶然遗漏：在非退化两人博弈中，孤立均衡的总数必须为奇数。不过这项定理只能帮助检查“是否可能漏解”，不能替代[[混合均衡求解|支持集枚举和逐项验证]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The standard Battle of the Sexes has two coordinated pure equilibria and one interior mixed equilibrium, for a total of three. The third equilibrium is consistent with the parity theorem. Oddness can flag a potentially incomplete solution, but it cannot replace [[混合均衡求解|support enumeration and direct verification]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 退化博弈可以有无穷多个均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Degenerate Game Can Have Infinitely Many Equilibria*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $p=\Pr_1(T)$、$q=\Pr_2(L)$，收益为

|  | $L$ | $R$ |
|---|---|---|
| $T$ | $(1,1)$ | $(2,2)$ |
| $B$ | $(3,3)$ | $(2,0)$ |

玩家 1 在 $q=0$ 时对所有 $p\in[0,1]$ 都是最佳反应；玩家 2 在 $p=3/4$ 时对所有 $q\in[0,1]$ 都是最佳反应。两条对应产生

$$
(p,q)=(0,1)
\quad\text{以及}\quad
q=0,\ p\in[3/4,1]
$$

这一整段均衡。存在连续统正是退化信号，所以不能再套奇数计数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $q=0$, every $p$ is a best response for Player 1; at $p=3/4$, every $q$ is a best response for Player 2. Their intersections are the isolated equilibrium $(0,1)$ and the entire segment $q=0$, $p\in[3/4,1]$. A continuum is a sign of degeneracy, so the odd finite count no longer applies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] “一般位置”要翻译成条件
> 口头上说“generic games have an odd number of equilibria”时，实际依赖的是退化收益关系只占参数空间中的特殊集合。做具体题不能凭“看起来一般”跳过检验；一旦出现收益并列或最佳反应线重合，就应先按退化情形处理。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Saying that the result holds generically means that degeneracy occupies a special set of payoff parameters. In a concrete problem, ties or overlapping best-response segments must be treated as possible degeneracy rather than dismissed as “generic.”
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
