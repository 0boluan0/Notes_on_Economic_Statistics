# 技术设计可以同时被多项活动使用，其排他程度取决于用途与制度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A technological design can serve several activities simultaneously, while its excludability depends on its use and the institutions governing access.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一份机器设计被甲厂使用，不会因此减少乙厂可使用的设计内容；这种使用上的**非竞争性**，与能否禁止乙厂未经许可使用的**排他性**是不同的问题，通用分类见 [[物品的竞争性与排他性]]。Romer（1990）把设计理想化为可低成本复制、可同时使用的知识；这不表示设计容易发明，也不表示掌握和应用它不需要投入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Plant A's use of a machine design does not reduce the design available to Plant B. This nonrivalry in use is distinct from the ability to exclude unauthorized users; see [[物品的竞争性与排他性|rivalry and excludability]]. Romer idealizes designs as knowledge that can be copied cheaply and used simultaneously. This says nothing about how cheaply they can be invented, learned or applied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要分清**设计、机器、人的时间**：两厂可以同时参考一种设计，但同一台实体机器有使用容量，工程师同一小时也不能被两项工作完整占用。[[人力资本|人力资本所提供的个人服务]]因此与可重复使用的设计不同。Romer 模型中的非竞争投入是设计知识，而非每一种机器的实物数量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish the design, the machine and the person's time. Two factories can consult the same design, but a physical machine has limited capacity and an engineer cannot devote the same full hour to two jobs. [[人力资本|The personal services supplied by human capital]] therefore differ from reusable designs. The nonrival input is design knowledge, not the physical quantity of each machine variety.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Romer品种扩张模型]]对两种用途规定不同权利：专利持有人可以阻止别人未经许可制造或销售该设计对应的机器；其他研究者却可以学习公开设计，发明新的不同品种。前一种使用被排他，后一种研究收益未被排他，这就是原文“部分可排他”的具体含义。它是模型设定，不能替代对现实专利制度、商业秘密和许可范围的调查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Romer品种扩张模型|Romer expanding-variety model]] assigns different rights to two uses. A patent holder can exclude unauthorized manufacture or sale of its machine, while other researchers can learn from the disclosed design to invent different varieties. Partial excludability refers to this division. It is a model assumption, not a description of every patent, trade secret or licensing regime.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，甲开发一种专门切削机的设计。乙复制设计文件并不会让甲失去文件内容，但生产该机器仍可能需要甲许可；乙从设计中学到的原理又可能帮助它开发另一种专门机器。最后这项未获补偿的后续收益，在 [[Romer研发知识外溢]] 中进入研发生产函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose A designs a specialized cutting machine. B's copy does not remove A's information, although making that machine may require permission. The principles B learns may also help it design a different machine. The resulting uncompensated benefit to subsequent research enters the production of ideas in [[Romer研发知识外溢|Romer's research knowledge spillover]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Romer（1990），[Endogenous Technological Change，Stanford托管原文](https://web.stanford.edu/~klenow/Romer_1990.pdf#page=5)，印刷S74–S75（PDF第5–6页）：设计的非竞争性、复制的理想化、与个人技能的区别。
- 同文，印刷S84–S85（PDF第15–16页）：设计用于机器制造与用于后续研究的两种排他边界。切削机例为按这两种用途构造的教学例子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pages S74–S75 support nonrival use and the distinction from personal skills; S84–S85 specify the two uses and their different exclusion rights. The cutting-machine example is constructed to illustrate that distinction.
