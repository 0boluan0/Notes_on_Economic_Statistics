# 若候选与目标根之间满足平均值定理且导数绝对值有正下界，则求根残差给出线性位置误差界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If the Mean Value Theorem applies between a candidate and the target root and the derivative magnitude has a positive lower bound, then the residual gives a linear positional-error bound*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子条件
> 设 $f(r)=0$，候选为 $x$。若 $f$ 在连接 $x$ 与目标根 $r$ 的闭区间上连续、在内部可导，并且整个区间都满足
> $$
> |f'(t)|\ge m>0,
> $$
> 则平均值定理给出
> $$
> \boxed{|x-r|\le\frac{|f(x)|}{m}}.
> $$
> 若没有另行证明的转换关系，[[求根残差|残差]]只能报告方程缺口，不能直接改名为根的位置误差。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $f(r)=0$ and let $x$ be a candidate. If $f$ is continuous on the closed segment joining $x$ and the target root $r$, differentiable in its interior, and satisfies $|f'(t)|\ge m>0$ throughout that segment, then the Mean Value Theorem yields $|x-r|\le |f(x)|/m$. Without a separately proved conversion relation, a [[求根残差|residual]] reports only equation defect and cannot be relabelled directly as positional root error.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 平均值定理提供转换因子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Mean Value Theorem Supplies the Conversion Factor*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平均值定理保证在 $x$ 与 $r$ 之间存在某个 $\xi$，使

$$
f(x)-f(r)=f'(\xi)(x-r).
$$

由 $f(r)=0$，

$$
|f(x)|=|f'(\xi)|\,|x-r|.
$$

若整个连接区间都有 $|f'|\ge m>0$，便得到

$$
|x-r|
=\frac{|f(x)|}{|f'(\xi)|}
\le\frac{|f(x)|}{m}.
$$

$1/m$ 把函数值尺度转换成位置尺度。$m$ 越小，同样的残差只能给出越松的位置界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Mean Value Theorem gives a point $\xi$ between $x$ and $r$ such that $f(x)-f(r)=f'(\xi)(x-r)$. Since $f(r)=0$, $|f(x)|=|f'(\xi)|\,|x-r|$. A positive lower bound $|f'|\ge m$ on the entire connecting segment therefore yields $|x-r|\le |f(x)|/m$. The factor $1/m$ converts function-value scale into positional scale; a smaller $m$ gives a weaker certificate for the same residual.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 下界必须覆盖候选到目标根的整段
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Lower Bound Must Cover the Entire Segment*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只知道候选点处 $|f'(x)|$ 很大还不够，因为平均值定理中的 $\xi$ 未必等于 $x$。还必须先有证据说明候选与**目标根**位于同一个可核验区间，并在该区间上统一控制 $|f'|$。若根的位置、连续可导区间或导数下界都未知，就不能先假定它们成立，再用残差宣布候选靠近某个根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A large value of $|f'(x)|$ at the candidate alone is insufficient because the Mean Value Theorem point $\xi$ need not equal $x$. Evidence must first place the candidate and the intended root in one verified interval and control $|f'|$ throughout it. Unknown root location, smoothness, or derivative bounds cannot be assumed merely to turn a small residual into a proximity claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 缩放反例：很小的残差仍可对应很大的位置误差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Scaling Counterexample: A Tiny Residual Can Still Accompany a Large Positional Error*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令

$$
f(x)=10^{-12}(x-1),
\qquad r=1.
$$

在 $x=0$，

$$
|f(0)|=10^{-12},
\qquad
|0-r|=1.
$$

方程乘以非零常数不会改变根，却会同比改变残差。因此不存在脱离函数尺度的通用残差阈值。这里 $m=10^{-12}$，正确的条件界为

$$
|0-r|
\le\frac{10^{-12}}{10^{-12}}
=1,
$$

并没有把位置误差误报成 $10^{-12}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=10^{-12}(x-1)$ with root $r=1$, the candidate $x=0$ has residual $10^{-12}$ but positional error $1$. Multiplying an equation by a nonzero constant leaves its roots unchanged while scaling its residuals, so no scale-free universal residual threshold exists. Here $m=10^{-12}$ and the valid bound is $1\le10^{-12}/10^{-12}=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 重根边界：导数下界可能消失
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Multiple-Root Boundary: The Derivative Lower Bound May Vanish*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对

$$
f(x)=(x-1)^2,
\qquad r=1,
$$

有 $f'(r)=0$。当 $x=1+10^{-6}$ 时，

$$
|f(x)|=10^{-12},
\qquad
|x-r|=10^{-6}.
$$

此处正确关系是 $|x-r|=\sqrt{|f(x)|}$，不是线性关系 $|x-r|=|f(x)|$。根附近不存在统一的导数正下界，所以上面的线性转换条件不能使用。这也与 [[Newton局部收敛|Newton 在重根处失去简单根二次收敛保证]] 属于同一局部几何边界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=(x-1)^2$ at the multiple root $r=1$, $f'(r)=0$. At $x=1+10^{-6}$ the residual is $10^{-12}$ while the positional error is $10^{-6}$, with $|x-r|=\sqrt{|f(x)|}$ rather than a linear equality. No uniform positive derivative lower bound exists near the root, so the linear conversion cannot be applied. The same local geometry removes the usual simple-root quadratic guarantee for [[Newton局部收敛|Newton iteration]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样用于停止与验收
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How to Use the Bound in Stopping and Validation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 若已证明 $|f'|\ge m>0$，所需位置容差 $\varepsilon_x$ 可转成残差条件 $|f(x)|\le m\varepsilon_x$。
- 若已有有效括区间，[[二分误差界|区间宽度]]能直接给出位置证书，不需要先用导数把残差换算成距离。
- 若没有转换证据，应把结果如实报告为残差，并保留 [[数值迭代停止条件|步长、括区间、迭代上限与失败状态]]。
- 相对残差或归一化残差可以改善跨尺度解释，但归一化本身不自动证明位置误差界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- With a proved bound $|f'|\ge m>0$, a positional tolerance $\varepsilon_x$ can be enforced through $|f(x)|\le m\varepsilon_x$.
- With a valid bracket, [[二分误差界|bracket width]] directly certifies position without first converting a residual through a derivative.
- Without conversion evidence, report the residual as a residual and retain the [[数值迭代停止条件|step, bracket, iteration limit, and failure state]].
- Relative or normalised residuals can improve scale-aware reporting, but normalisation alone does not prove a positional-error bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检
> 某程序报告 $|f(x)|=10^{-10}$。只凭这个数，能否断言 $|x-r|\le10^{-10}$？
>
> **答案：** 不能。还要明确目标根 $r$ 所在的有效区间、平均值定理所需的连续与可导条件，以及整段上的已知下界 $|f'|\ge m>0$。能推出的是 $|x-r|\le10^{-10}/m$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A program reports $|f(x)|=10^{-10}$. Does this alone prove $|x-r|\le10^{-10}$?
>
> **Answer:** No. One still needs a verified interval containing the candidate and intended root, the continuity and differentiability required by the Mean Value Theorem, and a known lower bound $|f'|\ge m>0$ throughout that interval. The conclusion is $|x-r|\le10^{-10}/m$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
