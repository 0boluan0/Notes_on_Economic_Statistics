# From residual to root error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从残差估计根误差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A small [[求根残差|residual]] says the equation is nearly satisfied in output units. To know whether the input is close to a root, ask how much the function must change when the input moves. If the slope cannot become too flat anywhere between the candidate and a known root, even a small output defect limits the input distance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

较小的[[求根残差]]表示方程在输出单位下接近成立。要知道输入是否接近某个根，还要问：输入移动时，函数值至少会改变多少？如果候选与已知存在的根之间任何位置的斜率都不会太平，即使很小的输出缺口也能限制输入距离。

> [!important] A conditional position bound · 有条件的位置误差界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $f(r)=0$ and let $x$ be a candidate. If $f$ is continuous on the closed segment joining $x$ and the target root $r$, differentiable in its interior, and satisfies $|f'(t)|\ge m>0$ throughout that segment, then the Mean Value Theorem yields $|x-r|\le |f(x)|/m$. Without a separately proved conversion relation, a [[求根残差|residual]] reports only equation defect and cannot be relabelled directly as positional root error.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $f(r)=0$，候选为 $x$。若 $f$ 在连接 $x$ 与目标根 $r$ 的闭区间上连续、在内部可导，并且整个区间都满足
> $$
> |f'(t)|\ge m>0,
> $$
> 则平均值定理给出
> $$
> \boxed{|x-r|\le\frac{|f(x)|}{m}}.
> $$
> 若没有另行证明的转换关系，[[求根残差|残差]]只能报告方程缺口，不能直接改名为根的位置误差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $r$ is an actual root, $x$ is the candidate, and $m$ is a **known positive lower bound on the magnitude of the derivative**. The [[导数|derivative]] measures output change per input unit; hence $|f(x)|/m$ has input units. This theorem assumes the root exists. An existence argument, such as [[介值定理|continuity with a sign-changing interval]], comes first. The stated whole-segment derivative bound is a convenient stronger hypothesis; the proof only needs differentiability and the bound at interior points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $r$ 是实际存在的根，$x$ 是候选，$m$ 是**已知的导数绝对值正下界**。[[导数]]的单位是每单位输入对应的输出变化，所以 $|f(x)|/m$ 的单位与输入相同。本定理把根的存在当作前提；应先有存在性论证，例如[[介值定理|连续函数在异号端点之间存在根]]。所列整段导数界是一项方便的较强条件；证明实际上只需内部可导并在内部有这个下界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the mean value theorem gives the bound
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 平均值定理提供转换因子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $x=r$, both errors are zero and the result is immediate. For the rest of this proof assume $x\ne r$, so the segment has positive length. The [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]] equates the average slope over that segment to a derivative at some interior point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $x=r$，两种误差都为零，结论直接成立。下面的证明假设 $x\ne r$，使区间具有正长度。[[拉格朗日中值定理]]说明，整段的平均斜率等于某个内部位置的导数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Mean Value Theorem gives a point $\xi$ between $x$ and $r$ such that $f(x)-f(r)=f'(\xi)(x-r)$. Since $f(r)=0$, $|f(x)|=|f'(\xi)|\,|x-r|$. A positive lower bound $|f'|\ge m$ on the entire connecting segment therefore yields $|x-r|\le |f(x)|/m$. The factor $1/m$ converts function-value scale into positional scale; a smaller $m$ gives a weaker certificate for the same residual.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平均值定理保证在 $x$ 与 $r$ 之间存在某个 $\xi$，使

$$
f(x)-f(r)=f'(\xi)(x-r).
$$

由 $f(r)=0$，

$$
|f(x)|=|f'(\xi)|\,|x-r|.
$$

若整个连接区间都有 $|f'|\ge m>0$，便得到

$$
|x-r|
=\frac{|f(x)|}{|f'(\xi)|}
\le\frac{|f(x)|}{m}.
$$

$1/m$ 把函数值尺度转换成位置尺度。$m$ 越小，同样的残差只能给出越松的位置界。

> [!example] A complete certificate for √2 · 平方根的完整误差证明
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $f(t)=t^2-2$ and $x=1.4$. Since $f(1)=-1$ and $f(2)=2$, continuity first supplies a root $r\in[1,2]$. On this interval $f'(t)=2t\ge2$, so use $m=2$. The residual is 0.04 and the position bound is $|1.4-r|\le0.04/2=0.02$. Thus an input tolerance of 0.02 is certified without knowing the decimal expansion of $r$. At $x=1.414$, the exact residual is $|1.414^2-2|=0.000604$, giving $|1.414-r|\le0.000302<0.001$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $f(t)=t^2-2$、$x=1.4$。由于 $f(1)=-1$、$f(2)=2$，先由连续性得到 $[1,2]$ 内存在根 $r$。整段上 $f'(t)=2t\ge2$，可取 $m=2$。残差为 0.04，所以位置界为 $|1.4-r|\le0.04/2=0.02$；无需知道 $r$ 的小数展开，就能保证输入容差 0.02。若候选为 $x=1.414$，精确残差是 $|1.414^2-2|=0.000604$，得到 $|1.414-r|\le0.000302<0.001$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Control the entire candidate-to-root segment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 下界必须覆盖候选到目标根的整段

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A large value of $|f'(x)|$ at the candidate alone is insufficient because the Mean Value Theorem point $\xi$ need not equal $x$. Evidence must first place the candidate and the intended root in one verified interval and control $|f'|$ throughout it. Unknown root location, smoothness, or derivative bounds cannot be assumed merely to turn a small residual into a proximity claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只知道候选点处 $|f'(x)|$ 很大还不够，因为平均值定理中的 $\xi$ 未必等于 $x$。还必须先有证据说明候选与**目标根**位于同一个可核验区间，并在该区间上统一控制 $|f'|$。若根的位置、连续可导区间或导数下界都未知，就不能先假定它们成立，再用残差宣布候选靠近某个根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A small residual cannot supply the missing existence assumption. For example, $f(t)=e^t$ is positive for every real $t$, but $f(-20)\approx2.06\times10^{-9}$. There is no real root to measure a distance to. A large derivative only at a candidate is likewise not a valid choice of $m$: for $f(t)=t^3$, $x=1$ and $r=0$, dividing the residual 1 by $|f'(1)|=3$ would incorrectly claim an error at most $1/3$, while the true error is 1. The derivative drops to zero near the root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

较小的残差不能补出缺失的存在性前提。例如，$f(t)=e^t$ 对每个实数 $t$ 都为正，但 $f(-20)\approx2.06\times10^{-9}$；根本没有实根可供测量距离。同样，候选处导数很大也不能直接把它当作 $m$：对 $f(t)=t^3$、$x=1$、$r=0$，若把残差 1 除以 $|f'(1)|=3$，会错误声称误差至多 $1/3$，而真实误差是 1；靠近根时导数趋于零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Scaling can hide a large positional error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 缩放反例：很小的残差仍可对应很大的位置误差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=10^{-12}(x-1)$ with root $r=1$, the candidate $x=0$ has residual $10^{-12}$ but positional error $1$. Multiplying an equation by a nonzero constant leaves its roots unchanged while scaling its residuals, so no scale-free universal residual threshold exists. Here $m=10^{-12}$ and the valid bound is $1\le10^{-12}/10^{-12}=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令

$$
f(x)=10^{-12}(x-1),
\qquad r=1.
$$

在 $x=0$，

$$
|f(0)|=10^{-12},
\qquad
|0-r|=1.
$$

方程乘以非零常数不会改变根，却会同比改变残差。因此不存在脱离函数尺度的通用残差阈值。这里 $m=10^{-12}$，正确的条件界为

$$
|0-r|
\le\frac{10^{-12}}{10^{-12}}
=1,
$$

并没有把位置误差误报成 $10^{-12}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conditional bound respects that scaling: if $g=c f$ with $c\ne0$, a valid bound for $|g'|$ is $m_g=|c|m$. Therefore $|g(x)|/m_g=|f(x)|/m$. The residual alone changes, while the certified distance does not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个有条件的界会正确处理缩放：若 $g=c f$ 且 $c\ne0$，则 $|g'|$ 的一个有效下界为 $m_g=|c|m$。因此 $|g(x)|/m_g=|f(x)|/m$；残差的数值变了，但能保证的距离没有变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The multiple-root boundary
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 重根边界：导数下界可能消失

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=(x-1)^2$ at the multiple root $r=1$, $f'(r)=0$. At $x=1+10^{-6}$ the residual is $10^{-12}$ while the positional error is $10^{-6}$, with $|x-r|=\sqrt{|f(x)|}$ rather than a linear equality. No uniform positive derivative lower bound exists near the root, so the linear conversion cannot be applied. The same local geometry removes the usual simple-root quadratic guarantee for [[Newton局部收敛|Newton iteration]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对

$$
f(x)=(x-1)^2,
\qquad r=1,
$$

有 $f'(r)=0$。当 $x=1+10^{-6}$ 时，

$$
|f(x)|=10^{-12},
\qquad
|x-r|=10^{-6}.
$$

此处正确关系是 $|x-r|=\sqrt{|f(x)|}$，不是线性关系 $|x-r|=|f(x)|$。根附近不存在统一的导数正下界，所以上面的线性转换条件不能使用。这也与 [[Newton局部收敛|Newton 在重根处失去简单根二次收敛保证]] 属于同一局部几何边界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use the bound to set and check a tolerance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样用于停止与验收

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- With a proved bound $|f'|\ge m>0$, a positional tolerance $\varepsilon_x$ can be enforced through $|f(x)|\le m\varepsilon_x$.
- With a valid bracket, [[二分误差界|bracket width]] directly certifies position without first converting a residual through a derivative.
- Without conversion evidence, report the residual as a residual and retain the [[数值迭代停止条件|step, bracket, iteration limit, and failure state]].
- Relative or normalised residuals can improve scale-aware reporting, but normalisation alone does not prove a positional-error bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 若已证明 $|f'|\ge m>0$，所需位置容差 $\varepsilon_x$ 可转成残差条件 $|f(x)|\le m\varepsilon_x$。
- 若已有有效括区间，[[二分误差界|区间宽度]]能直接给出位置证书，不需要先用导数把残差换算成距离。
- 若没有转换证据，应把结果如实报告为残差，并保留 [[数值迭代停止条件|步长、括区间、迭代上限与失败状态]]。
- 相对残差或归一化残差可以改善跨尺度解释，但归一化本身不自动证明位置误差界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A program supplies an evaluated value $\widetilde f(x)$ rather than an exact $f(x)$. If a separate evaluation-error analysis guarantees $|\widetilde f(x)-f(x)|\le\eta$, the triangle inequality gives $|f(x)|\le|\widetilde f(x)|+\eta$. Under the same root-existence and derivative assumptions, the safe position bound is therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

程序提供的是求值结果 $\widetilde f(x)$，未必是精确的 $f(x)$。若另有求值误差分析保证 $|\widetilde f(x)-f(x)|\le\eta$，三角不等式给出 $|f(x)|\le|\widetilde f(x)|+\eta$。在相同的根存在与导数条件下，安全的位置界因此为

$$\displaystyle |x-r|\le\frac{|\widetilde f(x)|+\eta}{m}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The number $\eta$ must be justified; machine precision by itself is not a bound on an arbitrarily evaluated expression. If it is unknown, report a computed residual and distinguish that observation from a rigorous position certificate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\eta$ 必须有依据；机器精度本身不是任意表达式的求值误差界。若它未知，应如实报告计算残差，并把这个观察与严格的位置误差保证区分开。

> [!question]- Apply the bound · 可核验自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A program reports $|f(x)|=10^{-10}$. Does this alone prove $|x-r|\le10^{-10}$?
>
> **Answer:** No. One still needs a verified interval containing the candidate and intended root, the continuity and differentiability required by the Mean Value Theorem, and a known lower bound $|f'|\ge m>0$ throughout that interval. The conclusion is $|x-r|\le10^{-10}/m$.
>
> If $10^{-10}$ is only the computed residual magnitude and the evaluation error is bounded by $\eta$, the bound is instead $(10^{-10}+\eta)/m$. With no justified evaluation-error bound, the program output alone is not a rigorous position certificate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 某程序报告 $|f(x)|=10^{-10}$。只凭这个数，能否断言 $|x-r|\le10^{-10}$？
>
> **答案：** 不能。还要明确目标根 $r$ 所在的有效区间、平均值定理所需的连续与可导条件，以及整段上的已知下界 $|f'|\ge m>0$。能推出的是 $|x-r|\le10^{-10}/m$。
>
> 若 $10^{-10}$ 只是计算残差的大小，且求值误差至多为 $\eta$，位置界应改为 $(10^{-10}+\eta)/m$；若没有有依据的求值误差界，就不能把程序输出本身当作严格的位置误差保证。
