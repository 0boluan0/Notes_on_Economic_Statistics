# 判断正定矩阵应先确认实对称或复 Hermitian，再按已知信息选择完整判据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Test positive definiteness by checking symmetry first and then choosing the criterion matched to the available information*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 先分清问题
> 先分清题目是在判断“正定矩阵”，还是只问某个矩阵产生的二次型是否为正。判断正定矩阵时先确认实对称或复 Hermitian；随后只需选择一个与已知信息匹配的完整判据：二次型、特征值、$LDL^*$ 主元（实数情形为 $LDL^T$）、顺序主子式或 Cholesky 分解 $A=LL^*$（实数情形为 $LL^T$）。不要把几个不完整的迹象拼成结论。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> First distinguish classifying a positive-definite matrix from testing positivity of a quadratic form represented by an arbitrary matrix. For a matrix classification, verify real symmetry or the Hermitian condition, then choose one complete criterion matched to the available information: quadratic values, eigenvalues, $LDL^*$ pivots ($LDL^T$ over the reals), leading principal minors, or a Cholesky factorisation $A=LL^*$ ($LL^T$ over the reals). Do not combine several incomplete clues into a verdict.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“先确认对称”只针对“这个矩阵是否正定”这类分类问题。若题目问的是一个非对称实矩阵产生的二次型是否严格为正，应先换成对称部分 $(A+A^T)/2$ 再判断，不能仅因 $A\ne A^T$ 就停止。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Checking symmetry first applies to the classification question “is this matrix positive definite?” If the question instead asks whether the quadratic form generated by a nonsymmetric real matrix is strictly positive, first replace $A$ by $(A+A^T)/2$ and test that representative; do not stop merely because $A\ne A^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择方法应服从已知信息，而不是每题全算一遍。已有谱分解时看特征值；小型符号矩阵常用 Sylvester；题目给出平方和或消元结构时看 $LDL^*$（实数情形为 $LDL^T$）；数值求解时，实对称正定系统用 $LL^T$，复 Hermitian 正定系统用 $LL^*$；需要证明定义时回到任意非零 $x$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose the test that matches the information already available. Use eigenvalues when a spectral decomposition is known, Sylvester for small symbolic matrices, $LDL^*$ for elimination or completed squares ($LDL^T$ over the reals), Cholesky as $LL^T$ for real symmetric positive-definite systems or $LL^*$ for complex Hermitian positive-definite systems, and the quadratic definition when the proof must control every direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些判据彼此等价，因而确认其中一条就够了。方法选择取决于题目已经给了什么，不应反过来增加不必要的计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These criteria are equivalent, so establishing one of them is enough. The best choice depends on what the problem has already supplied; computing every criterion only adds unnecessary work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不能混淆半正定边界：把这些严格正号全部改成非负不会得到一组平行的充分判据。PSD 可用全非负特征值、直接二次型，或全部 principal minors 非负；只看 leading minors 非负不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Do not mechanically replace every strict inequality by a nonstrict one for the semidefinite boundary. PSD can be established by nonnegative eigenvalues, the quadratic definition, or nonnegative values of every principal minor; nonnegative leading minors alone are insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 已知一个 $4\times4$ 实对称矩阵的 $LDL^T$ 主元为 $2,1,0.3,4$，还需要再求特征值吗？
>
> **答案：** 不需要；全部主元正已经由等价判据证明正定。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A $4\times4$ real symmetric matrix has $LDL^T$ pivots $2,1,0.3,4$. Must you also compute its eigenvalues?
>
> **Answer:** No. The positivity of every pivot already proves positive definiteness by an equivalent criterion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
