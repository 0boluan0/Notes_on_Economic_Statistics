# Choosing a positive-definiteness test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*正定判据的选择*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Testing positive definiteness means deciding whether a matrix gives a strictly positive quadratic value in **every nonzero direction**. A good test turns that requirement over infinitely many directions into information already available: a sum of squares, a spectrum, or a factorization. The aim is to establish one complete criterion, not to collect several encouraging signs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断正定，是判断矩阵在**每个非零方向**上是否都给出严格正的二次值。合适的判据把这个涉及无穷多个方向的要求，转成题目已有的平方和、特征值或分解信息。目标是建立一个完整判据，而不是搜集几个看起来有利的迹象。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $A=\begin{pmatrix}5&2\\2&3\end{pmatrix}$ is real symmetric. Its first leading minor is $5$, and its determinant is $15-4=11$. These two positive numbers already establish positive definiteness by [[Sylvester 正定判据|Sylvester's criterion]]. Solving a characteristic polynomial as well would not strengthen that exact conclusion. The same example can instead be understood through [[二维二次型配方|completing the square]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $A=\begin{pmatrix}5&2\\2&3\end{pmatrix}$ 为实对称矩阵。第一个顺序主子式为 $5$，行列式为 $15-4=11$；这两个正数已经通过 [[Sylvester 正定判据]]证明正定，无须再解特征多项式才能确认。同一个例子也可以通过[[二维二次型配方]]理解：

$$x^TAx=5\left(x_1+\frac25x_2\right)^2+\frac{11}{5}x_2^2>0\qquad(x\ne0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$ is a real column vector and $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Both squares can vanish together only when $x_2=0$ and then $x_1=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$ 为实列向量，$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。两项平方同时为零，必须先有 $x_2=0$，再有 $x_1=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Identify the matrix and the question first
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先确认矩阵类型与所问对象*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Throughout these tests, $A$ is a finite $n\times n$ matrix, $n\ge1$. The vault's [[正定矩阵|positive-definite matrix]] convention requires real [[实对称矩阵|symmetry]] or complex [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian symmetry]]: $A=A^T$ over the reals, or $A=A^*$ over the complexes. The star means conjugate transpose. The corresponding requirement is $x^TAx>0$ for all nonzero real $x$, or $z^*Az>0$ for all nonzero complex $z$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以下判据针对有限 $n\times n$ 矩阵，$n\ge1$。本库的[[正定矩阵]]约定要求实数域中的[[实对称矩阵|对称性]]，或复数域中的 [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian 性]]：分别为 $A=A^T$、$A=A^*$；星号表示共轭转置。对应要求是每个非零实向量满足 $x^TAx>0$，或每个非零复向量满足 $z^*Az>0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An arbitrary real matrix can still generate a positive quadratic expression without itself being symmetric. If the question concerns that expression, use [[二次型对称化|its symmetric representative]] $S=(A+A^T)/2$, because $x^TAx=x^TSx$. For instance $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\-2&1\end{pmatrix}$ is not symmetric, but its quadratic expression is $x_1^2+x_2^2$ and $S=I_2$, the [[单位矩阵|identity]]. This is a statement about the represented form, not a reason to skip the matrix convention. For complex vectors, the algebraic identity $z^TAz=z^T((A+A^T)/2)z$ still holds. Its values can be nonreal, so it does not justify applying real-valued positive-definiteness tests to this bilinear expression. A Hermitian form instead uses $z^*Az$ and requires the corresponding Hermitian assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任意实矩阵即使不对称，也可能产生恒正的二次表达式。如果题目问的是该表达式，应按[[二次型对称化]]改用 $S=(A+A^T)/2$，因为 $x^TAx=x^TSx$。例如 $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\-2&1\end{pmatrix}$ 不对称，但其二次表达式为 $x_1^2+x_2^2$，对称代表是[[单位矩阵]] $I_2$。这是关于所表示二次型的结论，不应因此省略矩阵分类的约定。对复向量，代数恒等式 $z^TAz=z^T((A+A^T)/2)z$ 仍成立，但其值可能不是实数，不能因此把实值正定的符号检验直接用于这个双线性表达式。Hermitian 型改用 $z^*Az$，并须检查相应的 Hermitian 前提。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choose the certificate supplied by the problem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*按已有信息选择一份完整证明*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The following are alternative exact certificates under the symmetry assumptions above. The linked cards give their full theorem statements and proofs.

- **An expression with transparent signs:** prove the [[正定矩阵|quadratic definition]] for every nonzero input. In a sum of positively weighted squares, also show that simultaneous vanishing forces the input to be zero; a square sum alone can be only semidefinite.
- **A known spectral decomposition:** use [[正定与半正定的谱判据|all eigenvalues being strictly positive]]. An eigenvalue is the multiplier in an [[特征对|eigenvector direction]]. The largest eigenvalue being positive is insufficient; every direction must pass.
- **A small symbolic matrix:** use [[Sylvester 正定判据|all $n$ leading principal minors being positive]]. A [[顺序主子式|leading principal minor]] is the determinant of the first $k$ rows and columns, for each $k=1,\ldots,n$. Checking only the full determinant omits the smaller restrictions.
- **A supplied elimination or square-completion factorization:** verify $A=LDL^*$ and use [[LDL正定判别|all diagonal entries of $D$ being positive]]. Here $L$ is unit lower triangular, meaning ones on its diagonal and zeros above it; $D$ is real diagonal, with diagonal entries $d_i$. The number $|y_i|^2$ is the squared absolute value of coordinate $y_i$. These are symmetry-preserving pivots in the stated order, not arbitrary pivots after unrelated row swaps. For $y=L^*x$, invertibility gives $x\ne0\iff y\ne0$ and $x^*Ax=\sum_i d_i|y_i|^2$.
- **A factorization to use in a solve:** a [[Cholesky 正定判据|Cholesky factorization]] $A=LL^*$ with $L$ lower triangular and strictly positive real diagonal certifies positive definiteness. Indeed $x^*Ax=\|L^*x\|_2^2>0$ for $x\ne0$; $\|\cdot\|_2$ is [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]]. A singular factor would only guarantee nonnegative values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在前述对称性条件下，以下是不同的精确证明路线；链接卡片分别给出完整定理与证明。

- **表达式的符号直接可见：** 对每个非零输入证明[[正定矩阵|二次型定义]]。若写成正权平方和，还须说明各平方同时为零只能发生在零输入；只有平方和，可能仅能证明半正定。
- **已有谱分解：** 按[[正定与半正定的谱判据]]检查全部特征值严格正。特征值是沿[[特征对|特征向量方向]]的乘数，只知道最大特征值正不够。
- **小型符号矩阵：** 用 [[Sylvester 正定判据]]检查全部 $n$ 个顺序主子式正。[[顺序主子式]]是前 $k$ 行与前 $k$ 列子矩阵的行列式，$k=1,\ldots,n$；只算整个行列式会漏掉较小的限制。
- **已有消元或配方分解：** 核对 $A=LDL^*$，再按 [[LDL正定判别]]检查 $D$ 的全部对角元正。$L$ 为单位下三角矩阵，即对角元全一、对角线上方全零；$D$ 为实对角矩阵，其对角元记为 $d_i$；$|y_i|^2$ 是坐标 $y_i$ 的模平方。这里使用既定次序下保持对称结构的主元，不是任意换行后的主元。令 $y=L^*x$，可逆性保证 $x\ne0\iff y\ne0$，且 $x^*Ax=\sum_i d_i|y_i|^2$。
- **后续需要用分解求解：** [[Cholesky 正定判据|Cholesky 分解]] $A=LL^*$ 中，若下三角 $L$ 的对角元为严格正实数，就能证明正定。因为 $x^*Ax=\|L^*x\|_2^2>0$，其中 $\|\cdot\|_2$ 为[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]；若因子不可逆，只能保证二次值非负。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the real case, replace every $*$ above by $T$. For a verified $4\times4$ factorization with diagonal pivots $2,1,3/10,4$, the positive-pivot certificate already finishes the question. No eigenvalue calculation is required. Conversely, a zero scalar pivot can stop that particular elimination without making the whole matrix singular: $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ has determinant $-1$. It fails positive definiteness, but remains invertible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实数情形把上述 $*$ 换成 $T$。如果已经核对一个 $4\times4$ 分解，其对角主元为 $2,1,3/10,4$，正主元判据就已完成任务，无须再求特征值。反过来，零标量主元可能使该次序的消元停住，却不说明整个矩阵奇异：$\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ 的行列式为 $-1$。它不是正定矩阵，但仍可逆。

> [!warning] A semidefinite question needs the right criterion · 半正定须使用对应判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefiniteness]], direct quadratic values or all nonnegative eigenvalues work. The minor criterion instead requires **every [[主子式|principal minor]]** to be nonnegative. Merely relaxing the leading-minor test fails: the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $\operatorname{diag}(0,-1)$ has diagonal entries $0,-1$ and zeros elsewhere. Both leading minors are zero, but its second coordinate direction gives $-1$. See [[半正定主子式判别|the full criterion]] and [[顺序主子式不推半正定|the leading-minor boundary]]. Positive diagonal entries or a positive determinant are also incomplete clues; their separate [[正对角元的正定边界|coordinate-axis]] and [[正行列式的正定边界|determinant]] boundaries explain why.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 判断[[半正定矩阵]]，可直接检查二次值非负或全部特征值非负；主子式路线则要求**全部[[主子式]]**非负。只放宽顺序主子式判据会失败：[[对角矩阵]] $\operatorname{diag}(0,-1)$ 的对角元为 $0,-1$，其余位置为零；它的两个顺序主子式均为零，第二坐标方向的值却为 $-1$。参见[[半正定主子式判别]]与[[顺序主子式不推半正定]]。正对角元或正行列式同样只是局部迹象，分别见[[正对角元的正定边界]]与[[正行列式的正定边界]]。

## Separate exact structure from numerical uncertainty
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*精确结构与数值不确定性分开判断*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An exact theorem concerns the stated matrix. For $A_\varepsilon=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1+\varepsilon\end{pmatrix}$, its determinant is exactly $\varepsilon$: positive $\varepsilon$ gives positive definiteness, zero gives singular semidefiniteness, and negative $\varepsilon$ gives an indefinite form. Rounding $1+\varepsilon$ to $1$ changes this example's matrix. A tiny positive computed pivot or eigenvalue therefore needs an error or tolerance assessment before it is treated as a certified positive quantity. This does not invalidate the exact criteria; it distinguishes what the arithmetic has established from what the theorem requires.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

精确定理针对给定矩阵。对 $A_\varepsilon=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1+\varepsilon\end{pmatrix}$，行列式恰为 $\varepsilon$：$\varepsilon>0$ 时正定，等于零时奇异半正定，小于零时不定。若把 $1+\varepsilon$ 舍入成 $1$，就改变了该例的矩阵。因此，数值算出的极小正主元或特征值，在被当作有保证的正量前，须结合误差或容差判断。这不否定精确判据，而是区分算术实际建立的结论与定理所需条件。
