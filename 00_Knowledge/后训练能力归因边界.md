# 后训练前后的一次行为差异不能单独区分能力新增、能力唤起、格式适配与能力压制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*One behavioral difference before and after post-training cannot by itself distinguish newly learned capability, capability elicitation, format adaptation, and capability suppression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 输出变了，只说明从能力到可见行为的链条中至少有一环变了
> 后训练模型答对而基座模型答错，至少有四种解释：训练教会了新的求解能力；基座已有相关表示但原提示没有唤起；两者能力相近但后训练模型学会了要求的回答格式；或者基座与后训练模型中的某个能力被拒答、迎合或其他策略行为遮住。一次 prompt、一次采样和最终答案不能识别是哪一种。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a post-trained model answers correctly while its base model does not, at least four explanations remain: fine-tuning taught a new capability; the base model represented relevant information but the prompt failed to elicit it; capabilities were similar but post-training taught the required format; or refusal, sycophancy, or another policy behaviour suppressed a capability in one condition. One prompt, one sample, and one final answer cannot identify which explanation is correct.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 四个解释对应不同证据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The four explanations require different evidence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **能力新增：** 在训练未出现的任务变体上，后训练模型仍能执行新的中间操作；改格式、换提示和去掉表面模板后优势仍在。学习曲线随相关训练数据增加而出现系统性迁移。
2. **能力唤起：** 基座模型在少量示例、明确任务说明、替代答案标签或合适解码下能够稳定恢复同一能力，且不是碰巧猜中。
3. **格式适配：** 两个模型的内部或自由形式答案同样正确，但只有后训练模型遵守 JSON、选项字母、角色边界或停止符。修正答案解析器（parser）或允许等价答案后，能力差异显著缩小。
4. **能力压制：** 受控改变拒答激励、用户立场、被训练状态或输出通道后，原先不表现的正确行为再次出现；同时要排除提示直接泄露答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. **New capability:** The post-trained model performs new intermediate operations on held-out task variants, and the advantage survives changes in wording and format.
2. **Elicitation:** The base model reliably recovers the same capability under demonstrations, explicit task descriptions, alternative verbalizers, or suitable decoding—not as a lucky sample.
3. **Format adaptation:** Free-form content is equally correct, but only the post-trained model obeys JSON, option-letter, role, or stopping conventions; accepting equivalent answers or fixing the parser removes much of the gap.
4. **Suppression:** Correct behaviour reappears when refusal incentives, user stance, perceived training status, or output channel is controlled, without the prompt revealing the answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“后训练只是在唤起预训练能力”与“后训练给了模型全新知识”都是过度概括。SFT 的梯度会改变参数，当然可能教会新映射；另一方面，默认聊天输出也不是模型表示内容的完整清单。诊断目标不是选一个漂亮故事，而是设计能让不同解释给出不同预测的对照。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both “post-training only elicits pretrained capabilities” and “post-training supplies entirely new knowledge” are overgeneralisations. SFT updates parameters and can teach new mappings, while default chat outputs are not a complete inventory of represented information. The diagnostic task is to design contrasts on which the explanations make different predictions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两类存在性结果不能被扩写成普遍规律
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Two existence results must not be expanded into universal claims*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Burns et al. 在六个模型、十个是非问答数据集上，用未标注激活和逻辑一致性约束寻找方向；其方法平均超过 zero-shot（不给示例）输出，并降低提示敏感性。这证明在那些设置中，模型激活可以支持默认输出未稳定表达的信息。它不证明所有失败背后都有正确的隐藏知识，也不证明该方法能识别任意能力。

Greenblatt et al. 构造密码锁模型（password-locked model）：模型只有在提示中出现密码时才展示某项已知能力，否则模仿较弱模型。高质量示范微调常能在没有密码时恢复这些能力，只有评价信号时的强化学习也常能恢复一部分。这给“仅靠提示得到的表现只是可访问能力下界”提供了受控证据；但它是研究者刻意锁住的实验模型（model organism），不证明自然训练的模型普遍隐藏能力。更重要的是，微调本身会改参数，也可能教会新能力，所以普通模型在微调后成功仍不能自动归为“纯唤起”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Burns et al. used unlabeled activations and logical-consistency constraints across six models and ten yes/no datasets. Their method beat zero-shot outputs on average and reduced prompt sensitivity, showing that activations supported information not reliably expressed by default outputs in those settings. It does not show that every failure hides correct knowledge or that the method identifies arbitrary capabilities. Greenblatt et al. constructed password-locked models that reveal known capabilities only when a password is present and otherwise imitate a weaker model. Fine-tuning on high-quality demonstrations, and often reinforcement learning from evaluations, recovered locked performance without the password. This controlled model organism shows that ordinary prompting can underestimate accessible capability. It does not establish that naturally trained models generally hide capabilities; because fine-tuning changes parameters and may teach new skills, success after fine-tuning is not automatically pure elicitation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小诊断矩阵
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A minimal diagnostic matrix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较基座与后训练模型时，固定题目、模型可见信息和主要解码预算，再交叉改变三件事：任务表述与少量示例、允许的输出格式、会影响策略行为的条件。对每个条件保留多次采样和过程可验证的中间结果，并在真正未参与训练或调参的变体上复测。若只换提示就宣布“发现隐藏能力”，提示也可能提供了新算法或答案线索；若只看最终字符串，答案解析器错误又可能被误判为能力差异。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hold the task, model-visible information, and main decoding budget fixed while crossing three interventions: task wording and demonstrations, permitted output format, and conditions affecting policy behaviour. Retain repeated samples and verifiable intermediate results, then retest on variants untouched by training or tuning. A prompt can teach an algorithm or leak an answer, so prompt recovery alone does not prove latent capability; a final-string comparison can likewise mistake parser failure for capability failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 基座模型在自由回答中给出正确数值，但没有按要求输出 JSON；后训练模型输出了可解析且数值正确的 JSON。这个样本能证明后训练新增了数学能力吗？下一步最小对照是什么？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A base model gives the correct number in free-form text but not in the required JSON, while the post-trained model returns valid JSON with the same correct number. Does this sample establish a newly learned mathematical capability? What is the smallest next comparison?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能；现有证据更直接支持格式适配。先用接受等价自由答案的评分器比较两者数学正确率，再分别给出清楚的 JSON schema 和少量格式示例。只有后训练模型在未见过的数学变体上仍展现基座无法恢复的求解步骤，才更支持能力新增。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. The sample more directly supports format adaptation. First compare mathematical accuracy with a scorer accepting equivalent free-form answers, then provide both models with a clear JSON schema and a few formatting examples. Persistent advantages in solution steps on held-out mathematical variants would provide stronger evidence for new capability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Burns et al. (2022), “Discovering Latent Knowledge in Language Models Without Supervision”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.03827)：核对六个模型、十个是非问答数据集、未标注激活与逻辑一致性方法，以及相对 zero-shot 输出和提示敏感性的实验。该结果是受限设置中的存在性证据，不支持“隐藏能力普遍存在”。
- [Greenblatt et al. (2024), “Stress-Testing Capability Elicitation With Password-Locked Models”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19550)：核对 password-locked model、示范微调与仅有评价时的强化学习唤起实验。该 model organism 的隐藏能力由研究者刻意构造；普通模型上的微调还可能增加能力，因此成功表现只给可访问能力下界，不能单独识别纯唤起。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Burns et al. (2022) was checked for six models, ten yes/no datasets, the unlabeled-activation and logical-consistency method, and results relative to zero-shot outputs and prompt sensitivity. These are bounded existence results, not evidence that hidden capability is universal.
- Greenblatt et al. (2024) was checked for password-locked models and elicitation through demonstration fine-tuning or reinforcement learning from evaluations alone. The hidden capability was deliberately constructed in this model organism; on ordinary models, fine-tuning may also add capability, so successful post-fine-tuning performance does not by itself identify pure elicitation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
