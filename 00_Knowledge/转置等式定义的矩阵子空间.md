# Symmetric and skew-symmetric matrix subspaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*对称与反对称矩阵为什么构成子空间？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding two symmetric matrices preserves their matching pairs; scaling one changes both entries of each pair by the same factor. These are exactly the operations needed for a [[子空间|subspace]]: a collection containing zero that stays closed when we add its members or multiply them by scalars. Here a whole matrix is one vector, as in a [[矩阵空间|matrix space]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个对称矩阵相加后，镜像位置仍相等；给一个对称矩阵数乘时，每对元素也都乘上同一个因子。这恰好对应[[子空间]]需要的运算：集合含零，而且把成员相加、数乘后仍留在其中。这里像[[矩阵空间]]中一样，把整个矩阵当作一个向量。

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
B=\begin{pmatrix}3&-1\\-1&4\end{pmatrix},\quad
A+B=\begin{pmatrix}4&1\\1&4\end{pmatrix},\quad
2A=\begin{pmatrix}2&4\\4&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both results are [[实对称矩阵|symmetric]], meaning that reflection across the diagonal preserves entries. For [[实反对称矩阵|skew-symmetric matrices]], the entries in each reflected pair are negatives instead; addition and scaling preserve that relation too. The calculation below proves these statements for arbitrary real coefficients, not just the displayed examples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个结果都[[实对称矩阵|对称]]，即主对角线两侧的镜像元素仍相同。[[实反对称矩阵|反对称矩阵]]则要求每对镜像元素互为相反数；加法与数乘同样保持这个关系。下面的计算会对任意实系数证明这些结论，而不只检验上面的几个例子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One proof for either fixed condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*对每一种固定条件，使用同一个证明*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a positive integer $n$ and work in $M_n(\mathbb R)$, the space of all real $n\times n$ matrices. Choose a sign $\varepsilon$: use $+1$ for symmetry or $-1$ for skew-symmetry. Keep this sign fixed throughout. The collection under consideration is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定正整数 $n$，在全部实 $n\times n$ 矩阵构成的空间 $M_n(\mathbb R)$ 中讨论。选择一个符号 $\varepsilon$：对称时取 $+1$，反对称时取 $-1$，并始终保持这个选择不变。要研究的集合是

$$S_\varepsilon=\{A\in M_n(\mathbb R):A^T=\varepsilon A\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The zero matrix satisfies the equation. Now let $A,B$ both satisfy it, and let $\alpha,\beta$ be any real scalars. The [[矩阵转置|transpose]] adds and scales entry by entry, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零矩阵满足这个等式。再设 $A,B$ 都满足它，$\alpha,\beta$ 为任意实数。[[矩阵转置]]与逐项相加、数乘相容，因此

> [!important] The same condition survives a linear combination · 线性组合保持同一个条件
> $$
> (\alpha A+\beta B)^T
> =\alpha A^T+\beta B^T
> =\alpha\varepsilon A+\beta\varepsilon B
> =\varepsilon(\alpha A+\beta B).
> $$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result satisfies exactly the same defining equation, so it is again in $S_\varepsilon$. Together with the zero matrix, this proves that each of the two collections is a real vector subspace. The two cases are separate: taking one matrix from each collection is not what the closure assertion says.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结果满足原来的同一个定义等式，所以仍在 $S_\varepsilon$ 中。结合零矩阵，便证明两类集合各自都是实向量子空间。这是分别对两种情况作断言，并不是说从两类里各取一个矩阵也保持原条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, adding the symmetric matrix $\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ to the skew-symmetric matrix $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$ gives $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$, which is neither symmetric nor skew-symmetric. This is why the sign must be fixed before checking closure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，对称矩阵 $\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ 加上反对称矩阵 $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$，得到 $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$，它既不对称也不反对称。这说明检验封闭性前，为什么必须先固定符号。

> [!warning] An inequality gives a different collection · 改成不等式就换了集合
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The nonsymmetric collection $\{A:A^T\ne A\}$ is not a subspace. Zero is excluded. Moreover, a nonsymmetric $A$ and its negative $-A$ both belong to this collection, but their sum is zero and leaves it. This collection is the set-theoretic complement of the symmetric subspace inside $M_n(\mathbb R)$; it is not a complementary subspace in a [[直和|direct-sum decomposition]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 非对称矩阵集合 $\{A:A^T\ne A\}$ 不是子空间：零矩阵被排除；而且非对称的 $A$ 与 $-A$ 都在其中，相加却得到不在其中的零。这是对称子空间在 $M_n(\mathbb R)$ 中的集合论补集，并不是[[直和|直和分解]]意义的补子空间。
