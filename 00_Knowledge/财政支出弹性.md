# 财政支出弹性是可比口径下支出增长率与GDP增长率的比值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fiscal expenditure elasticity is expenditure growth divided by GDP growth on a comparable basis.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财政支出弹性比较支出与 GDP 的相对变动速度。按本课程使用的同基期有限变化定义，设 $G_0>0$、$Y_0>0$ 且 $\Delta Y\ne0$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The measure compares proportional changes in spending and GDP. Under the course's common-base finite-change definition, with $G_0>0$, $Y_0>0$ and $\Delta Y\ne0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E_G=\frac{\Delta G/G_0}{\Delta Y/Y_0}
=\frac{MGP}{G_0/Y_0}.
$$

第二个等号说明，弹性等于 [[财政支出边际倾向]] 除以基期 [[财政支出占比]]。若 $G:200\to220$、$Y:1000\to1080$，支出增长 10%、GDP 增长 8%，所以 $E_G=1.25$；同样有 $0.25/0.20=1.25$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Elasticity equals the [[财政支出边际倾向|marginal propensity]] divided by the initial [[财政支出占比|expenditure share]]. The stated changes give growth of 10% and 8%, hence $E_G=1.25=0.25/0.20$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 GDP 增长率为正时，$E_G>1$ 表示支出增长更快，占比上升。更一般且不受增长率正负误导的判断是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With positive GDP growth, $E_G>1$ means expenditure grows faster and its share rises. The more general comparison, valid regardless of the sign of GDP growth, is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{s_1}{s_0}=\frac{1+g_G}{1+g_Y},\qquad
s_1>s_0\iff g_G>g_Y,
\quad Y_1>0.
$$

例如，GDP 下降 10% 而支出不变，弹性为 $0/(-10\%)=0$，支出占比却上升。因此不能在衰退时照搬“弹性小于 1，所以占比下降”。GDP 不变时增长率之比没有定义；观察值也不是已识别的因果收入弹性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If GDP falls 10% and expenditure stays constant, measured elasticity is zero but the spending share rises. With zero GDP growth the ratio is undefined. An observed finite-change ratio is not an identified causal income elasticity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

研究 [[瓦格纳假说]] 时常关心长期关系中的收入弹性；那需要明确人口、价格和估计模型，不能用一次景气波动的这个比值直接证明长期假说。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tests of [[瓦格纳假说|Wagner's hypothesis]] often concern a long-run income elasticity, requiring explicit population, price and model choices. One cyclical observation cannot establish that long-run relationship.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
