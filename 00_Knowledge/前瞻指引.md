# 前瞻指引是央行就未来政策路径及其条件向公众提供的沟通
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Forward guidance is central-bank communication about the future policy path and its conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前瞻指引让公众了解未来政策可能怎样变化，以及判断依赖什么条件。家庭、企业和投资者的当前决策取决于未来融资条件，因此即使今天的政策利率不变，可信的新指引仍可能改变当前金融条件。它可以提供预测，也可以包含承诺，不能把所有指引当成无条件保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Forward guidance describes likely policy changes and the conditions behind them. Current decisions depend on future financing conditions, so credible new guidance can matter even when today's policy rate is unchanged. Guidance may communicate a forecast or a commitment; it is not invariably an unconditional guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例：当前利率仍为 3%，但央行说明在已列明的经济状态下，未来维持低利率的时间可能比市场原先预计更长。如果公众相信这一信息，[[未来短率预期]]可以变化；期限更长的融资价格还受[[期限溢价]]等影响，不能照搬“当前利率未动，所以政策消息无作用”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose today's rate remains 3 percent, but guidance indicates a longer low-rate period under specified conditions than markets had expected. If believed, it can change [[未来短率预期|expected future short rates]]. Longer borrowing rates also reflect [[期限溢价|term premia]], so an unchanged current rate does not imply irrelevant policy news.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读一段指引，先认定主体、政策工具、时间或经济条件，再问相对于此前预期增加了什么信息。预测与承诺的区别见[[预测式与承诺式指引]]；条件语句的逻辑见[[指引门槛与触发条件]]；公告效果按[[意外信息定价]]判断，而不是按措辞是否听起来宽松判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the speaker, instrument, horizon or conditions, then ask what changed relative to prior beliefs. Distinguish [[预测式与承诺式指引|forecasts from commitments]], interpret [[指引门槛与触发条件|threshold conditions]], and assess [[意外信息定价|news relative to expectations]] rather than the tone alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
