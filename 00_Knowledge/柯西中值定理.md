# Cauchy's mean value theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*柯西中值定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose two quantities depend on the same input: $f(x)=x^2$ and $g(x)=x^3$. As $x$ moves from $1$ to $2$, the first quantity increases by $3$ and the second by $7$. Their total-change ratio is $3/7$. Can that ratio also occur among their instantaneous rates of change at one common input? Here $f'(x)/g'(x)=2/(3x)$, and at $c=14/9$, which lies between $1$ and $2$, it is exactly $3/7$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设两个量依赖同一个输入：$f(x)=x^2$、$g(x)=x^3$。当 $x$ 从 $1$ 走到 $2$，前者增加 $3$，后者增加 $7$，总变化量之比为 $3/7$。这个比值能否也等于某个共同输入处的瞬时变化率之比？本例 $f'(x)/g'(x)=2/(3x)$，在位于 $1,2$ 之间的 $c=14/9$ 处，它恰好等于 $3/7$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[导数|derivative]] measures instantaneous change. Cauchy's mean value theorem relates **two total changes** to **two derivatives evaluated at the same interior point**. Its most general statement uses multiplication, so it still makes sense when some of those quantities are zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[导数]]衡量瞬时变化。柯西中值定理把**两个总变化量**与**同一个内部点处的两个导数**联系起来。最一般的陈述采用乘法，因此即使某些量为零，等式仍然有意义。

> [!important] A relation without division · 不需要除法的关系
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $a<b$ be finite real numbers. Let $f,g:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ be [[一点连续|continuous]] at every point of the closed interval and differentiable at every point of $(a,b)$. Write $\Delta f=f(b)-f(a)$ and $\Delta g=g(b)-g(a)$ for their endpoint changes. Then some $c\in(a,b)$ satisfies
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $a<b$ 是有限实数，实值函数 $f,g:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ 在闭区间每一点[[一点连续|连续]]，并在开区间 $(a,b)$ 每一点可导。用 $\Delta f=f(b)-f(a)$、$\Delta g=g(b)-g(a)$ 表示两端之间的变化量。则存在 $c\in(a,b)$ 满足
>
> $$\Delta f\,g'(c)=\Delta g\,f'(c).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Neither endpoint differentiability nor continuity of the derivatives is assumed. The point $c$ is an existence conclusion; it need not be unique or equal to the interval midpoint.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不要求端点可导，也不要求导数连续。$c$ 是一个存在性结论；它不必唯一，也不必是区间中点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Make the endpoint values agree
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把端点变成等高，再用罗尔定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define an auxiliary real function $H$ by subtracting two scaled changes:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造辅助实函数 $H$，把两个经过缩放的变化量相减：

$$H(x)=\Delta g\,[f(x)-f(a)]-\Delta f\,[g(x)-g(a)].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The numbers $\Delta f,\Delta g$ are fixed while $x$ varies. Thus $H$ inherits closed-interval continuity and interior differentiability. At the endpoints, $H(a)=0$ and $H(b)=\Delta g\Delta f-\Delta f\Delta g=0$. [[罗尔定理|Rolle's theorem]] says that a function with these properties and equal endpoint values has a zero derivative somewhere inside. At such a point $c$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$x$ 变化时，$\Delta f,\Delta g$ 是固定数。因此 $H$ 同样在闭区间连续、在内部可导。两端满足 $H(a)=0$、$H(b)=\Delta g\Delta f-\Delta f\Delta g=0$。[[罗尔定理]]说明：具有这些性质且端点值相等的函数，在内部某处导数为零。对这样的 $c$，

$$0=H'(c)=\Delta g\,f'(c)-\Delta f\,g'(c),$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
which is the required identity. This proof never divides by an endpoint change or a derivative. It therefore includes constant functions and zero endpoint changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就是所需等式。证明没有除以任何端点变化量或导数，因此也包含常值函数与端点变化量为零的情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When a ratio is legitimate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*何时才能写成比值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful sufficient extra assumption is $g'(x)\ne0$ for **every** $x\in(a,b)$. Then $\Delta g\ne0$: otherwise Rolle's theorem applied to $g$ would force an interior zero derivative. Also $g'(c)\ne0$ at the point supplied above. Division is now justified:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个常用的充分附加条件是：**每个** $x\in(a,b)$ 都满足 $g'(x)\ne0$。此时 $\Delta g\ne0$，因为若端点值相等，对 $g$ 用罗尔定理就会得到内部零导数。同时，上面得到的 $c$ 也满足 $g'(c)\ne0$。于是可以合法地相除：

$$\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{g(b)-g(a)}=\frac{f'(c)}{g'(c)}.$$

> [!warning] Different endpoint values alone do not suffice · 仅有端点差非零还不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $f(x)=x^2$ and $g(x)=x^3$ on $[-1,1]$. Here $\Delta f=0$ and $\Delta g=2$. The multiplication identity forces $c=0$, because $0=2(2c)$. But $g'(0)=0$, so the derivative ratio is undefined there. At every $c\ne0$ the ratio $2/(3c)$ is nonzero and cannot equal $\Delta f/\Delta g=0$. The division-free theorem remains true; the ratio conclusion is unavailable.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[-1,1]$ 上取 $f(x)=x^2$、$g(x)=x^3$。此时 $\Delta f=0$、$\Delta g=2$。交叉乘积等式要求 $0=2(2c)$，所以只能取 $c=0$；但 $g'(0)=0$，导数比在这里没有定义。对每个 $c\ne0$，$2/(3c)$ 都不为零，不能等于 $\Delta f/\Delta g=0$。不含除法的定理仍然成立，却不能据此得到比值结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choosing $g(x)=x$ gives $g'(c)=1$ and $\Delta g=b-a$, recovering the [[拉格朗日中值定理|ordinary mean value theorem]]. Choosing an interval with one endpoint approaching the other is the key step in the $0/0$ proof of [[洛必达法则|L'Hôpital's rule]]. The derivative ratio there is evaluated at an intermediate point, not automatically at the original input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $g(x)=x$，便有 $g'(c)=1$、$\Delta g=b-a$，从而得到[[拉格朗日中值定理]]。让区间的一端逐渐靠近另一端，则是[[洛必达法则]]中 $0/0$ 情形的关键证明步骤；其中导数比在一个中间点处取值，并不是自动在原输入处取值。
