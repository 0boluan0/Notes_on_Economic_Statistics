# 工资提高同时改变家庭资源与育儿时间成本，因此不能等同于非劳动收入增加

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A higher wage changes both household resources and the opportunity cost of childcare time, so it is not equivalent to an increase in non-labour income.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断收入变化如何影响生育，先问改变的是工资、非劳动收入，还是育儿所需的时间与现金支出。在家长以减少市场工作来提供照护的模型中，工资提高既增加可赚取的收入，也提高一小时亲自育儿时间的机会成本；在其他价格固定时，一笔非劳动收入增加没有后一项变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First identify whether the change concerns the wage, non-labour income, or childcare requirements. When parental care displaces paid work, a higher wage raises both earning capacity and the opportunity cost of care. With other prices fixed, additional non-labour income does not create the latter change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把每名子女的教育投入暂时固定，设家长可用时间为 $T$，每名子女需要家长亲自照护时间 $\tau>0$ 和现金支出 $a\ge0$，工资为 $w>0$，非劳动收入为 $R$，其他消费为 $c$，子女数量为 $n$。若没有其他时间用途，且 $0\le\tau n\le T$，教学预算为

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hold educational investment per child fixed. Let $T$ be available parental time, $\tau>0$ care time per child, $a\ge0$ cash cost per child, $w>0$ the wage, $R$ non-labour income, $c$ other consumption, and $n$ the number of children. With no other time uses and $0\le\tau n\le T$, the teaching budget is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
c+an=w(T-\tau n)+R
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
c+n(a+w\tau)=wT+R.
$$

$R$ 增加只提高右边的资源；若对子女数量的需求有正的纯收入效应，生育数量会增加。$w$ 增加则同时提高资源 $wT+R$ 与每名子女的完整成本 $a+w\tau$。对原先仍在市场工作的家长，收入效应可以支持更多子女，时间变贵的替代效应却推动相反方向，净效应不能只靠“收入更高”确定。这与[[价格效应分解]]及[[劳动供给效应]]使用相同的分析工具，但生育选择需要自己的预算与偏好条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rise in $R$ increases resources without changing the cost per child; fertility rises if demand for the number of children has a positive pure income effect. A rise in $w$ increases both full resources and the full cost per child. For a parent initially doing paid work, income and substitution effects can oppose each other. [[价格效应分解|Price-effect decomposition]] and [[劳动供给效应|labour-supply effects]] provide the analytical tools, but fertility requires its own budget and preference assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造例子：令 $T=100$、$\tau=10$、$a=20$、$w=10$、$R=0$。完整资源为 $1000$，每名子女完整成本为 $120$。若只让 $R$ 增加到 $100$，两者变为 $1100$ 与 $120$；若只让 $w$ 增加到 $12$，则变为 $1200$ 与 $140$。这只比较预算变化，尚未给出偏好，不能据此算出最优子女数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed example, $T=100$, $\tau=10$, $a=20$, $w=10$, and $R=0$ give full resources of $1000$ and a full cost of $120$ per child. Raising only $R$ to $100$ changes these to $1100$ and $120$; raising only $w$ to $12$ changes them to $1200$ and $140$. Preferences have not been specified, so these budget comparisons do not determine optimal fertility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若再允许[[生育数量质量权衡|数量—质量共同选择]]，还要区分谁的工资变化。在 Doepke 等的对数效用、正教育投入的内部解中，家长工资 $w$ 上升而教师价格 $p$ 固定时，最优教育投入上升、生育数量下降；如果 $p$ 与 $w$ 同比例上升、其他参数不变，该模型中的两项选择均不变。市场照护能否替代家长时间，也会改变工资与育儿成本的联系。因此，个体工资差异的比较静态不能直接冒充全国收入增长的历史规律。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With [[生育数量质量权衡|joint quantity–quality choice]], whose wage changes also matters. In the positive-education interior solution of Doepke et al.'s log-utility model, higher parental wages at a fixed teacher price raise education and lower fertility. If teacher prices and parental wages rise proportionally while other parameters remain fixed, both choices remain unchanged. Purchased care can also change the link between wages and childcare costs. Household comparative statics are therefore not automatically an economy-wide historical law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Doepke、Hannusch、Kindermann、Tertilt（2022），The Economics of Fertility: A New Era](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w29948/w29948.pdf)，NBER Working Paper 29948，2022 年 4 月版，§2.2，印刷 pp. 10–12（PDF pp. 12–14）：支持纯财富变化与工资变化的区分，以及固定教师价格和工资同比变化的不同预测；其内部解需 $0<\gamma<1$、$\gamma\phi w>p\theta$，其中 $\gamma$ 为教育回报参数、$\phi$ 为每名子女照护时间、$\theta>0$ 为基础人力资本参数。§4.2，印刷 pp. 35–37（PDF pp. 37–39），式 (5)–(7)：支持市场照护改变时间成本通道。本卡加入 $R$ 与固定现金成本 $a$ 的预算及数例是教学改写，未把内部解公式外推到零教育角点。

Section 2.2 supplies the distinction between wealth and wages and the wage/teacher-price comparison; the quoted interior result requires positive education and the stated parameter conditions. Section 4.2 models purchased childcare. The budget with $R$ and fixed cash cost $a$ is a teaching adaptation, not a reported estimate.
