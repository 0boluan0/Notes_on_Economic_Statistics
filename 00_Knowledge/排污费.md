# 排污费是按污染排放量征收以提高继续排放边际代价的费用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An emissions charge is a payment assessed on pollution released that raises the marginal cost of continuing to emit.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

排污费按仍然排出的污染数量收费。每单位费率为 $t$ 时，少排一单位就少付 $t$；企业因此把“继续排放付费”和“花资源减排”作比较。这里讨论的是经济学上的按排放收费工具，不是某一国家现行法律中名为“排污费”的特定制度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An emissions charge applies to pollution still released. At rate $t$, emitting one unit less saves $t$, so the firm compares paying for emissions with spending resources to abate. The term here denotes an economic instrument, not a particular jurisdiction's current legal category.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定企业、产出等其他选择与基准排放 $e_0$，令[[产量、排放与减排|减排量]]为 $a\in[0,e_0]$，剩余排放为 $e_0-a$。$C(a)$ 是净私人减排成本，连续可微且凸；收费完整执行、无逃避渠道时，企业解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hold the firm, output and other choices fixed, with baseline emissions $e_0$. Let [[产量、排放与减排|abatement]] be $a\in[0,e_0]$ and remaining emissions $e_0-a$. For differentiable convex net private abatement cost $C(a)$ and an enforced charge without evasion, the firm solves
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\min_{0\le a\le e_0}\{C(a)+t(e_0-a)\}.$$

若最优在内点，$C'(a)=t$：下一单位减排成本低于费率就值得继续减排，高于费率则宁愿付费。角点也要检查：若 $C'(0)\ge t$，零减排可最优；若 $C'(e_0)\le t$，完全减排可最优。严格凸时解唯一；不能把区间外的交点当答案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an interior optimum, $C'(a)=t$: abate further when the next unit costs less than the charge, and pay instead when it costs more. Zero abatement can be optimal if $C'(0)\ge t$, and complete abatement if $C'(e_0)\le t$. Strict convexity gives uniqueness; an intersection outside the feasible interval is not a solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设一个独立的减排技术例：$e_0=100$、$C(a)=a^2/2$、$t=50$。企业减排 50、剩余排放 50，减排成本 1250、缴费 2500。成本函数与产出均在本例中固定；这些数字本身没有告诉我们污染损害是多少，因而还不能判定这个费率是否是[[庇古税|社会最优的矫正税率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a separate fixed-output abatement-technology example, take $e_0=100$, $C(a)=a^2/2$ and $t=50$. The firm abates fifty and emits fifty, spending 1,250 on abatement and paying 2,500. These assumptions do not specify damage and therefore do not establish that fifty is the [[庇古税|efficient corrective rate]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多家企业面对同一费率时，何时能以最低总成本达到给定减排目标，见[[共同排放价格与减排分担]]。实际排放是否可核实，可参考[[排放监测与履约|排放核算、缺值与质量检查]]；具体税费征收仍须按适用规则核定，不能直接照搬配额缴回程序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[共同排放价格与减排分担|A common emissions price]] explains when this incentive implements a least-cost allocation across firms. For credible measurement, consult [[排放监测与履约|emissions accounting, missing-data and quality checks]]. Charge assessment follows the applicable collection rules, not the allowance-surrender procedure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
