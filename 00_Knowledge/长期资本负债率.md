# 长期资本负债率是非流动负债占非流动负债与股东权益合计的比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The long-term-capital liability ratio is noncurrent liabilities divided by noncurrent liabilities plus equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CPA口径为 $NCL/(NCL+E)$，衡量本时点长期资本中非流动负债的占比。ABC为 $740/(740+960)\approx43.5294\%$，教材舍入为44%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CPA defines the ratio as $NCL/(NCL+E)$, the share of long-term capital represented by noncurrent liabilities. ABC gives $740/(740+960)\approx43.5294\%$, rounded to 44% in the text.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它按流动/非流动分类，而非按有息/无息分类；不包括短期借款及已转入流动部分的到期债务，非流动负债也不全是有息借款。因此它不等于全部债务负担，更不能替代[[资产负债率]]或到期现金流分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The classification is current versus noncurrent, not interest-bearing versus non-interest-bearing. The numerator excludes short-term loans and current maturities, while noncurrent liabilities need not all bear interest. It therefore does not measure the entire debt burden or replace [[资产负债率|liabilities-to-assets]] and maturity analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比例的通常解释要求分母为正且权益有正常含义；零分母不可计算、负权益情况下不能以低值简单判安全。比较不同资料时检查“long-term debt”是否改用了有息债务范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The usual interpretation requires positive long-term capital and meaningful equity. A zero denominator is undefined, and a low value with negative equity does not establish safety. Check whether another source restricts “long-term debt” to interest-bearing obligations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
