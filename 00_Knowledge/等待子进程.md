# wait等待本shell可管理的子进程而PID存在性轮询不能替代退出状态回收
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*wait handles children known to the current shell; polling PID existence does not replace collecting exit status.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Bash 中，后台启动命令后及时保存 `$!`，再用 `wait "$child_pid"` 等待相应子进程并取得其状态。Shell 的 `wait` 不是“等待任意系统 PID”接口；换到另一个 shell，即使知道数字，也不能因此获得原 shell 的子进程退出状态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Bash, save `$!` after launching a background command, then use `wait "$child_pid"` to wait for that child and collect its status. Shell `wait` is not a wait-for-any-system-PID interface. Another shell does not acquire the original parent's child status simply by knowing the number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在启用作业控制时，暂停等状态变化或捕获的信号可能使等待返回；需要严格等终止时应核对所用 Bash 版本与 `wait -f` 的条件，且不能把任何一次返回都自动当成成功完成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With job control, a stop or a trapped signal can affect when waiting returns. When termination rather than a state change is required, check the Bash version and the conditions for `wait -f`. A return from waiting is not automatically successful task completion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课堂中的 `pidwait` 轮询是不同问题：`kill -0` 不发送信号，但检查 PID 和权限；非零结果可能是无该进程，也可能是无权限。PID 重用、已退出未回收进程与检查之间的竞争意味着“还查得到／查不到”不是原任务成功与否的可靠证明。轮询应有间隔与错误边界，不能空转。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The classroom `pidwait` polling exercise addresses a different problem. `kill -0` sends no signal but checks PID validity and permission; failure can mean either absence or insufficient permission. PID reuse, unreaped exited processes, and races between checks mean presence or absence does not establish success of the original task. Poll with an interval and explicit error boundaries rather than busy-waiting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

[GNU Bash Manual, Job Control Builtins — wait](https://www.gnu.org/software/bash/manual/html_node/Job-Control-Builtins.html)：支持子进程、jobspec、`-f` 与返回状态；本机 `help wait` 用于核对基础版本行为。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[GNU Bash Manual: Job Control Builtins — wait](https://www.gnu.org/software/bash/manual/html_node/Job-Control-Builtins.html) supports children, jobspecs, `-f`, and status; installed `help wait` corroborates the basic version behavior.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[OpenBSD kill(2)](https://man.openbsd.org/kill.2) 的 signal 0、ESRCH、EPERM 与 [wait(2)](https://man.openbsd.org/wait.2)：支持存在性检查与退出状态回收的区别；[MIT 练习](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/2020/command-line/#job-control-1) 提供 `pidwait` 语境。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[OpenBSD kill(2)](https://man.openbsd.org/kill.2), including signal 0, ESRCH, and EPERM, and [wait(2)](https://man.openbsd.org/wait.2) distinguish existence checks from status collection. The [MIT exercise](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/2020/command-line/#job-control-1) provides the `pidwait` context.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
