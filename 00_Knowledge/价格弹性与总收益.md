# 沿同一需求曲线的价格变化对总收益的局部影响由需求价格弹性绝对值是否超过一决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Along a fixed demand curve, the local revenue effect of a price change depends on whether absolute price elasticity exceeds one.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同一市场、同一期限中的可微需求 $q(p)>0$，设 $p>0$、$q'(p)\le0$，销售总收益为 $R(p)=p q(p)$。沿这条固定需求曲线小幅降价时，若需求缺乏弹性，总收益下降；若需求富有弹性，总收益上升。这里的弹性使用绝对值，定义和区间限制见 [[价格弹性的测量边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For differentiable demand $q(p)>0$ with $p>0$ and $q'(p)\le0$ in a fixed market and horizon, revenue is $R(p)=p q(p)$. A small price reduction lowers revenue when demand is inelastic and raises it when demand is elastic. Elasticity here is absolute; see [[价格弹性的测量边界|elasticity measurement and scope]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘积法则把价格和数量两种影响分开：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product rule separates the price and quantity effects:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
R'(p)=q(p)+p q'(p)
=q(p)(1-\eta),\qquad
\eta=-\frac{p q'(p)}{q(p)}.
$$

所以 $\eta<1$ 时收益随价格同向变动，$\eta>1$ 时反向变动。$\eta=1$ 只说明该点一阶变化为零；要断言一整段价格变化都保持收益不变，须在该段持续满足单位弹性等相应条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revenue moves locally with price when $\eta<1$ and against it when $\eta>1$. At $\eta=1$, only the first-order change vanishes at that point. Constancy over an interval requires the corresponding condition throughout it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如固定需求为 $q=120-2p$。价格从 10 降到 8，数量从 100 增到 104，总收益却从 1000 降到 832；这个区间需求缺乏弹性。若原因是丰收使供给外移，这可以解释在此模型中“增产而销售收入下降”。但这不是所有农产品的必然结局：需求移动、贸易、库存、价格支持都会改变比较；销售收入也不是扣除成本后的利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With fixed demand $q=120-2p$, lowering price from ten to eight raises quantity from 100 to 104 but reduces revenue from 1,000 to 832. Demand is inelastic over this interval. A harvest-induced supply expansion can generate this outcome in the model, but demand shifts, trade, storage and price support change the comparison. Sales revenue is not profit net of costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
