# 金融体系与金融中介
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Financial System and Financial Intermediation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释储蓄者与融资者为何既通过证券市场直接连接，也通过银行、基金和其他中介完成信息生产、期限转换和风险分担。
> **具体锚点：** 一个储户很难独立评估并持续监督数百家借款企业，银行可集中筛选和监控，再向储户提供可随时支取的存款。
> **核心难点：** 中介既降低信息与交易成本，也因期限和流动性转换创造挤兑与系统风险；“去中介化”往往只是中介形式改变。
> **为什么重要：** 同样的储蓄规模通过不同市场和中介结构，会产生不同融资可得性、风险分配与政策传导。
> **继续：** 银行的具体资产负债约束见 [[商业银行资产负债管理]]；信息摩擦见 [[金融市场中的信息不对称与契约]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Explain why savers and borrowers connect both directly through securities markets and indirectly through banks, funds, and other intermediaries that produce information, transform maturities, and share risk.
> **Concrete anchor:** A depositor cannot cheaply evaluate and monitor hundreds of borrowing firms; a bank can pool screening and monitoring while offering the depositor an on-demand claim.
> **Central difficulty:** Intermediation reduces information and transaction costs but creates run and systemic risk through maturity and liquidity transformation. “Disintermediation” often changes the form rather than eliminating intermediation.
> **Why it matters:** The same volume of saving can produce different access to finance, risk allocation, and policy transmission under different market and intermediary structures.
> **Continue with:** For banks' concrete balance-sheet constraints see [[商业银行资产负债管理|commercial-bank balance-sheet management]]; for information frictions see [[金融市场中的信息不对称与契约|asymmetric information and contracts in financial markets]].

## 直接融资与金融中介
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Direct Finance and Financial Intermediation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金融体系把储蓄转为投资、分散风险、提供支付和信息生产。市场通过证券直接连接资金双方，中介把资产重新打包并承担流动性和信用转换。交易成本、信息不对称和规模经济解释中介存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The financial system channels saving into investment, distributes risk, provides payments, and produces information. Securities markets connect funding parties directly, while intermediaries repackage assets and perform liquidity and credit transformation. Transaction costs, asymmetric information, and economies of scale explain why intermediaries exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“直接”融资仍依赖承销、评级、交易、托管和清算等机构；“间接”融资则让储户持有中介负债而中介持有最终借款人资产。区分的关键是资产负债表上谁直接面对最终信用风险，而不是有没有金融机构参与。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even “direct” finance relies on underwriters, ratings, trading, custody, and clearing institutions. In “indirect” finance, savers hold an intermediary's liabilities while the intermediary holds the ultimate borrower's assets. The key distinction is whose balance sheet directly bears the ultimate credit exposure, not whether institutions participate at all.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 中介的四项转换
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Four Transformations Performed by Intermediaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中介进行规模转换、期限转换、流动性转换和风险转换：汇集小额资金形成大额贷款，以短期负债支持长期资产，向客户提供可赎回负债，并用筛选、分散和资本吸收损失。每项服务也生成对应脆弱性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Intermediaries transform scale, maturity, liquidity, and risk: they pool small balances into large loans, fund long assets with shorter liabilities, issue redeemable claims, and absorb losses through screening, diversification, and capital. Each service creates a corresponding vulnerability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：企业债与银行贷款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Corporate Bonds versus Bank Loans*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

大型透明企业可发行标准化债券，由众多投资者持有；信息较少的中小企业可能依赖银行关系贷款，由银行持续获取软信息并重谈条款。前者通常流动性更高、融资来源更分散，后者更适合定制和监督，却把信用与流动性风险集中到银行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A large transparent firm may issue standardized bonds held by many investors. A smaller opaque firm may rely on a relationship bank that gathers soft information and renegotiates terms over time. The first route is usually more liquid and diversifies funding sources; the second better supports customization and monitoring but concentrates credit and liquidity risk in the bank.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把中介只描述成“把储户的钱转借出去”会漏掉资产创造、信息生产和流动性服务；把证券市场称为无中介会漏掉基础设施；只看到分散收益而不看共同敞口和期限错配，会低估系统风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Describing an intermediary merely as relending savers' money omits asset creation, information production, and liquidity services. Calling securities markets intermediary-free omits their infrastructure. Focusing on diversification while ignoring common exposures and maturity mismatch understates systemic risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 金融中介为什么在证券市场存在时仍有价值？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why do financial intermediaries remain valuable when securities markets exist?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它们集中筛选和监督、定制契约、转换期限与流动性，并让小额储户持有较简单的索取权。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> They pool screening and monitoring, customize contracts, transform maturity and liquidity, and let small savers hold simpler claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 期限转换为什么既创造价值又创造风险？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does maturity transformation create both value and risk?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它让储户持有短期可用资金、借款人获得长期融资，但集中赎回时长期资产无法无损迅速变现，形成挤兑和火售风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It gives savers short-term usable claims and borrowers long-term finance, but mass redemption cannot be met by liquidating long assets quickly without loss, creating run and fire-sale risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 判断直接与间接融资时应看什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What distinguishes direct from indirect finance?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 看储户是否直接持有最终融资者的证券，还是持有中介负债并由中介持有最终资产和承担信用转换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Ask whether savers directly hold the ultimate borrower's security or instead hold an intermediary's liability while the intermediary holds the ultimate asset and performs credit transformation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
