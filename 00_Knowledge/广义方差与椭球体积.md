# Generalized variance and ellipsoid volume
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*广义方差与椭球体积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A covariance matrix describes spread using **squared** units. An ellipse's axes, however, are lengths. Taking square roots converts the directional variances into axis lengths, which is why covariance-ellipsoid volume depends on the square root of [[广义方差|generalized variance]].

For example, let $\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(4,9)$ and center the ellipse at the origin. The region $x_1^2/4+x_2^2/9\le1$ has semiaxes $2$ and $3$, so its area is $\pi(2)(3)=6\pi$. The covariance determinant is $36$: the area is $\pi\sqrt{36}$, not $36\pi$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

协方差矩阵用**平方单位**描述散布，椭圆的轴却是长度。把各方向的方差开平方，才得到轴长，因此协方差椭球的体积取决于[[广义方差]]的平方根。

例如 $\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(4,9)$，中心在原点。区域 $x_1^2/4+x_2^2/9\le1$ 的半轴长为 $2$ 和 $3$，面积为 $\pi(2)(3)=6\pi$。协方差行列式为 $36$，所以面积是 $\pi\sqrt{36}$，不是 $36\pi$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Specify the region before measuring it
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先明确测量的是哪个区域*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $p\ge1$ be an integer, let $\mu\in\mathbb R^p$ be a fixed center, and let the real symmetric [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] $\Sigma$ be [[正定矩阵|positive definite]], meaning every nonzero direction has positive variance. For a finite $r\ge0$, define the **filled** ellipsoid
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设整数 $p\ge1$，$\mu\in\mathbb R^p$ 是固定中心，实对称[[协方差矩阵]] $\Sigma$ [[正定矩阵|正定]]，即每个非零方向都有正方差。对有限的 $r\ge0$，定义包含内部的**实心**椭球

$$E_r=\{x\in\mathbb R^p:(x-\mu)^T\Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu)\le r^2\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The expression on the left is squared [[马氏距离|Mahalanobis distance]] from the center. The inequality includes the interior; replacing it by equality would describe only the boundary surface, whose $p$-dimensional volume is zero. Here volume means length when $p=1$, area when $p=2$, and the corresponding $p$-dimensional measure in general.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左边是离中心的[[马氏距离]]平方。不等号将内部包括在内；若改成等号，就只描述边界表面，其 $p$ 维体积为零。这里的体积在 $p=1$ 时指长度，$p=2$ 时指面积，一般指相应的 $p$ 维测量。

> [!important] Fix dimension and radius when comparing · 比较时固定维度与半径
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $\kappa_p$ be the volume of the Euclidean unit ball in $p$ dimensions. Then
> $$\operatorname{Vol}_p(E_r)=\kappa_p r^p\sqrt{\det(\Sigma)}.$$
> In dimensions $1,2,3$, the constants are $2,\pi,4\pi/3$. For the same $p$ and $r>0$, changing the determinant from $9$ to $36$ doubles the volume. Changing the radius or dimension would also change the other factors. At $r=0$, the region is the single center and has zero volume.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $\kappa_p$ 为 $p$ 维欧氏单位球的体积，便得到上面的公式。维度 $1,2,3$ 时，常数分别为 $2,\pi,4\pi/3$。固定相同的 $p$ 与 $r>0$，广义方差从 $9$ 变成 $36$，体积放大两倍；若改变半径或维度，其他因子也会改变。$r=0$ 时只剩中心一点，体积为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Square roots convert variance to length
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 平方根来自线性长度尺度

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[实对称矩阵谱定理|spectral theorem]] gives $\Sigma=Q\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_p)Q^T$, with positive eigenvalues and an orthogonal matrix $Q$. Its columns are perpendicular unit directions. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[实对称矩阵谱定理]]，$\Sigma=Q\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_p)Q^T$，特征值均为正，正交矩阵 $Q$ 的各列给出相互垂直的单位方向。定义

$$L=\Sigma^{1/2}=Q\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt{\lambda_1},\ldots,\sqrt{\lambda_p})Q^T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This square root satisfies $LL^T=\Sigma$. Set $x=\mu+Lz$. Substituting into the quadratic expression gives $z^Tz=\|z\|_2^2$, the squared [[欧氏范数|ordinary Euclidean length]]. Thus the ball $\|z\|_2\le r$ is mapped exactly onto $E_r$. Translation changes its position, and the perpendicular coordinate changes preserve volume. The axis stretches multiply it by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个矩阵平方根满足 $LL^T=\Sigma$。令 $x=\mu+Lz$，代入二次型后得到 $z^Tz=\|z\|_2^2$，即[[欧氏范数|普通欧氏长度]]的平方。因此球 $\|z\|_2\le r$ 恰好被映射为 $E_r$。平移只改变位置，正交换基保留体积；沿各轴的拉伸则将体积乘以

$$|\det L|=\prod_{j=1}^p\sqrt{\lambda_j}=\sqrt{\det\Sigma}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the [[行列式体积与取向|determinant volume factor]]. A radius-$r$ ball already has volume $\kappa_p r^p$, so multiplying gives the result. Equivalently, the [[正定二次型的椭球|principal semiaxes]] are $r\sqrt{\lambda_j}$. They use eigenvalues of the covariance $\Sigma$; the quadratic form uses $\Sigma^{-1}$, whose eigenvalues are their reciprocals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就是[[行列式体积与取向|行列式的体积缩放因子]]。半径为 $r$ 的球原有体积 $\kappa_p r^p$，乘上它即得结论。也可以直接看[[正定二次型的椭球|主半轴]] $r\sqrt{\lambda_j}$：这里的特征值属于协方差 $\Sigma$，而二次型使用的是 $\Sigma^{-1}$，其特征值恰为倒数。

> [!note]- The constant in any dimension · 任意维度的常数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The closed formula is $\kappa_p=\pi^{p/2}/\Gamma(p/2+1)$. For a positive real input $a$, the Gamma function is defined by the convergent [[反常积分|improper integral]] $\Gamma(a)=\int_0^\infty t^{a-1}e^{-t}\,dt$, where the infinite interval is interpreted through limits of finite integrals. It agrees with factorials through $\Gamma(k+1)=k!$ for nonnegative integers $k$. This supplies the dimension-only constant; the dependence on $\Sigma$ is already fully explained by the axis stretches.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一般公式为 $\kappa_p=\pi^{p/2}/\Gamma(p/2+1)$。对正实数 $a$，Gamma函数由收敛[[反常积分]] $\Gamma(a)=\int_0^\infty t^{a-1}e^{-t}\,dt$ 定义，无穷区间通过有限积分的极限解释。非负整数 $k$ 满足 $\Gamma(k+1)=k!$，与阶乘衔接。这只提供依赖维度的常数；对 $\Sigma$ 的依赖已由轴拉伸完整说明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The volume statement is geometric and requires no Gaussian model. Calling $E_r$ a probability region requires an additional distribution and a calibrated $r$. For a Gaussian vector with its true mean and positive-definite covariance, the [[Gaussian马氏平方律|squared-distance chi-square law]] provides that calibration. Substituting estimated parameters changes the probability question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个体积结论是几何结论，不要求Gaussian模型。若要把 $E_r$ 称为具有指定概率的区域，还须另给分布并校准 $r$。Gaussian向量使用真实均值与正定协方差时，[[Gaussian马氏平方律|距离平方的卡方律]]提供这种校准；换成估计参数会改变概率问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\Sigma$ is singular, the ordinary inverse in the displayed definition does not exist. The linear image $\mu+\Sigma^{1/2}\{z:\|z\|_2\le r\}$ still makes sense and has zero ambient $p$-volume, although it may have nonzero lower-dimensional volume. Simply replacing the inverse by a pseudoinverse without restricting to the covariance's range instead leaves null directions unbounded; see [[奇异尺度马氏伪距离|singular-scale distance]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\Sigma$ 奇异，定义里的普通逆不存在。线性像 $\mu+\Sigma^{1/2}\{z:\|z\|_2\le r\}$ 仍有意义，其环境 $p$ 维体积为零，但较低维体积可能非零。若只把逆换成伪逆，却不把点限制在协方差的值域平移内，零方向会不受约束而无界，见[[奇异尺度马氏伪距离]]。
