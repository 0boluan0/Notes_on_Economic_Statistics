# Covariance under an affine transformation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*仿射变换下的协方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing measurement units, adding a fixed offset, or mixing several measurements changes the coordinates in which we describe data. **The covariance transformation rule** tells us how their joint variation changes. A fixed offset moves every observation equally, so it disappears when we measure deviations from the new mean. Scaling or mixing coordinates changes those deviations and therefore their covariance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

改变单位、添加固定偏移，或者把多个测量值混合，都会改变描述数据的坐标。**协方差的变换规则**说明共同波动怎样随之变化。固定偏移把所有观测等量移动，计算相对于新均值的偏移时就抵消了；缩放或混合坐标会改变偏移，因此也改变协方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, suppose the first of two measurements changes from metres to centimetres. Its deviations multiply by $100$, so its variance multiplies by $100^2$. Its covariance with the unchanged second measurement multiplies by $100$, because only one factor in the centered product has changed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，二维测量中的第一项从米改成厘米，其偏移乘 $100$，方差就乘 $100^2$；它与保持原单位的第二项之间的协方差乘 $100$，因为中心化乘积中只有一个因子缩放。

> [!important] Fixed matrices and offsets · 固定矩阵与偏移
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X\in\mathbb R^p$ have finite second moments, mean $\mu=E[X]$, and [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] $\Sigma$. Let $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times p}$ and $b\in\mathbb R^m$ be fixed, with positive integers $m,p$. The transformed vector $Y=AX+b$ has
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $X\in\mathbb R^p$ 的各坐标二阶矩有限，均值为 $\mu=E[X]$，[[协方差矩阵]]为 $\Sigma$。取固定矩阵 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times p}$ 与固定向量 $b\in\mathbb R^m$，其中 $m,p$ 为正整数。变换后的向量 $Y=AX+b$ 满足
>
> $$
> E[Y]=A\mu+b,\qquad
> \boxed{\operatorname{Cov}(Y)=A\Sigma A^T.}
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is an affine transformation: $AX$ mixes coordinates linearly and $b$ adds an offset. The result is $m\times m$, since it compares the $m$ output coordinates. $A$ may be rectangular or rank deficient; neither independence nor normality is required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这叫仿射变换：$AX$ 线性混合坐标，$b$ 再加偏移。结果尺寸为 $m\times m$，因为比较的是 $m$ 个输出坐标。$A$ 可以是长方形，也可以秩亏；不要求独立或正态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the offset disappears
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 平移为何消失

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[均值向量仿射变换|mean transformation rule]] follows by taking the expectation of each output coordinate. Subtract this mean from $Y$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[均值向量仿射变换]]由逐个输出坐标取期望得到。再用 $Y$ 减去这个均值：

$$Y-E[Y]=AX+b-(A\mu+b)=A(X-\mu).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The offset cancels exactly. Substituting into the definition of covariance, and using [[乘积的转置|the reversed-order transpose rule]], gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

偏移项恰好抵消。将其代入协方差定义，再用[[乘积的转置|乘积转置时颠倒次序的规则]]：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Cov}(Y)
&=E\!\left[(Y-E[Y])(Y-E[Y])^T\right]\\
&=E\!\left[A(X-\mu)(X-\mu)^TA^T\right]\\
&=A\Sigma A^T.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last step uses the fact that $A$ is fixed. Dimension checks explain where the transpose goes: $(m\times p)(p\times p)(p\times m)$ produces the required $m\times m$ matrix. The finite second moments ensure the matrix expectations exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一步使用了 $A$ 固定这一条件。维度也能帮助判断转置位置：$(m\times p)(p\times p)(p\times m)$ 正好产生 $m\times m$ 矩阵。有限二阶矩保证这些矩阵期望存在。

> [!example] Report the sum and difference together · 同时报告和与差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}4&3\\3&9\end{bmatrix}$ and $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}$, then $AX=(X_1+X_2,X_1-X_2)^T$. Multiplying on both sides gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}4&3\\3&9\end{bmatrix}$，$A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{bmatrix}$，则 $AX=(X_1+X_2,X_1-X_2)^T$。两侧相乘得到
>
> $$
> A\Sigma A^T=\begin{bmatrix}19&-5\\-5&7\end{bmatrix}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The diagonal gives the two [[线性投影方差|linear-combination variances]]. The off-diagonal is their covariance: $\operatorname{Cov}(X_1+X_2,X_1-X_2)=4-9=-5$. Adding any fixed $b$ leaves this entire matrix unchanged.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对角给出两个[[线性投影方差|线性组合的方差]]。非对角是两者的协方差：$\operatorname{Cov}(X_1+X_2,X_1-X_2)=4-9=-5$。再添加任意固定 $b$，整张矩阵都不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the metres-to-centimetres example, $A=\operatorname{diag}(100,1)$ instead gives $\begin{bmatrix}40000&300\\300&9\end{bmatrix}$. A single output row $A=a^T$ recovers the scalar variance rule. Centering followed by scaling each coordinate by its own standard deviation gives the [[相关矩阵|correlation matrix]]; the effect of units is developed in [[相关矩阵尺度不变性|its scale-change rule]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在米改厘米的例子中，改用 $A=\operatorname{diag}(100,1)$ 得到 $\begin{bmatrix}40000&300\\300&9\end{bmatrix}$。只保留一个输出行 $A=a^T$，就恢复标量方差规则。先中心化，再除以每个坐标自己的标准差，会得到[[相关矩阵]]；单位变换的具体影响由[[相关矩阵尺度不变性]]继续说明。

> [!warning] Random coefficients are a different problem · 随机系数需要另行处理
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The displayed rule requires deterministic $A,b$. If $X$ is equally likely to be $-1$ or $1$ and we choose the random scalar coefficient $A=X$, then $AX=1$ has variance zero, while $A^2\operatorname{Var}(X)=1$. A coefficient depending on the observation cannot be pulled through expectation as if it were fixed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上式要求 $A,b$ 是确定的。若 $X$ 等概率取 $-1$ 或 $1$，再选随机标量系数 $A=X$，则 $AX=1$ 的方差为零，而 $A^2\operatorname{Var}(X)=1$。依赖观测的系数不能当作固定量移到期望之外。
