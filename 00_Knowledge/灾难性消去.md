# 近数相减会暴露并放大操作数已有的相对误差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Subtracting nearby values exposes and amplifies pre-existing relative error*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子失效模式
> 当两个接近的量相减时，共同的高位被消去，真实差值远小于各操作数。操作数中原本很小的绝对舍入误差因此可能占结果的很大比例，使有效数字骤减；这就是灾难性消去的核心。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When two nearby quantities are subtracted, their common leading digits cancel and the true difference is much smaller than either operand. Small absolute rounding errors already present in the operands can then become a large fraction of the result, abruptly destroying significant digits. This is the core of catastrophic cancellation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键边界是：近数相减不一定在“减法这一步”产生大舍入。Goldberg 的例子中，两个先前乘法得到的近似量相减时，最后的减法甚至可以精确，但它把先前隐藏在高位后的误差暴露出来。若操作数本身精确且硬件提供合适的保护位，近数相减可以是良性的。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The key boundary is that subtraction itself need not introduce a large new rounding error. In Goldberg's example, the final subtraction can be exact while exposing errors created by earlier multiplications. If the operands are exact and the arithmetic supplies appropriate guard digits, cancellation can be benign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

诊断时应追踪操作数从哪里来，并寻找代数等价但避免近数相减的公式、专用函数（如面向小量的 `log1p`）或更高精度中间计算。仅把结果打印出更多小数位不能恢复已经消失的信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diagnosis must trace where the operands came from and look for an algebraically equivalent formula that avoids nearby subtraction, a specialized function such as `log1p` for small arguments, or higher-precision intermediate work. Printing more decimal places cannot recover information already lost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 为什么“减法本身是精确的”仍不能排除灾难性消去？
>
> **答案：** 两个被减数可能已经带有舍入误差；高位相消后，剩下的差主要由这些旧误差构成，相对误差仍会很大。

## 来源与核验

- David Goldberg, [*What Every Computer Scientist Should Know About Floating-Point Arithmetic*](https://doi.org/10.1145/103162.103163)，§2.4：区分 catastrophic 与 benign cancellation，并说明减法可精确却暴露先前误差。
- MIT 18.S096, [*Matrix Calculus for Machine Learning and Beyond — Full Lecture Notes*](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-s096-matrix-calculus-for-machine-learning-and-beyond-january-iap-2023/mit18_s096iap23_lec_full.pdf)：核验有限差分中步长过小时的 catastrophic cancellation 例子。
- Python Software Foundation, [`math.log1p`](https://docs.python.org/3/library/math.html#math.log1p)：核验 `log(1+x)` 在小 $x$ 下需要专用实现以保持精度。
