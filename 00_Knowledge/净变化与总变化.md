# 变化率的积分给净变化而变化率绝对值的积分给总变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Integrating a rate gives net change; integrating its absolute value gives total change*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若位置 $s(t)$ 连续可微、速度 $v(t)=s'(t)$，且 $a<b$，则
$$\text{位移}=s(b)-s(a)=\int_a^b v(t)\,dt,\qquad
\text{路程}=\int_a^b|v(t)|\,dt.$$
位移允许前进和后退相消；路程把两个方向的运动量都算进去。这是同一变化过程的两个不同问题，不能只凭“总共”二字决定用哪个积分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a continuously differentiable position, the velocity integral gives displacement and the speed integral gives distance. Displacement cancels motion in opposite directions; distance counts both. They answer different questions about the same process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $v(t)=2t-2$，$0\le t\le2$。它在 $t=1$ 变号，故
$$\int_0^2v(t)\,dt=0,\qquad
\int_0^2|v(t)|\,dt=-\int_0^1v(t)\,dt+\int_1^2v(t)\,dt=2.$$
物体回到起点，但确实走了路。面积题也一样：曲线下的带符号积分与几何面积的差别由正负贡献决定；两曲线之间的面积使用[[曲线间面积]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The velocity changes sign at one. Its integral is zero, whereas splitting the absolute-value integral at that sign change gives distance two. Returning to the start does not erase travel. The same distinction separates signed area from geometric area; between two graphs use [[曲线间面积|area between curves]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
