# Net change and total change
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*净变化与总变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A return journey can end where it began and still cover a positive distance. **Net change** compares the final value with the initial value. **Total change** counts movement in both directions positively. An integral can answer either question, depending on whether the rate keeps its sign or is replaced by its magnitude.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一趟往返运动可以回到起点，却仍走过一段路。**净变化**比较终点与起点的值；**总变化**把两个方向的变化都按正数累计。积分可以回答这两个问题，关键在于保留变化率的正负号，还是先取它的大小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $s(t)$ be a position on a line at time $t$, and let its [[导数|derivative]] $v(t)=s'(t)$ be velocity: positive and negative signs indicate the two directions. Assume finite times $a<b$ and a continuously differentiable position on $[a,b]$, meaning that its velocity is continuous. Speed is $|v(t)|$, which ignores direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $s(t)$ 是时刻 $t$ 的直线位置，它的[[导数]] $v(t)=s'(t)$ 是速度；正负号表示两个方向。设有限时刻 $a<b$，位置在 $[a,b]$ 上连续可微，也就是速度连续。速率是 $|v(t)|$，只记录大小。

> [!important] Two totals from one motion · 同一运动的两种总量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> By [[Newton-Leibniz公式|antiderivative evaluation]], displacement is the signed velocity integral. Distance accumulates speed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 由[[Newton-Leibniz公式]]，位移等于带符号的速度积分；路程则累计速率。
>
> $$\text{displacement}=s(b)-s(a)=\int_a^b v(t)\,dt,\qquad\text{distance}=\int_a^b|v(t)|\,dt.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a small time interval of width $\Delta t$, the signed movement is approximately $v(t)\Delta t$, and its distance is approximately $|v(t)|\Delta t$. The [[定积分|definite integral]] takes the limit of these small contributions. Velocity measured in metres per second multiplied by time in seconds gives metres for both answers; matching units alone cannot decide which answer is wanted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在宽为 $\Delta t$ 的短时间里，带符号的移动近似为 $v(t)\Delta t$，走过的距离近似为 $|v(t)|\Delta t$。[[定积分]]取这些小贡献之和的极限。米每秒乘以秒，两种结果的单位都是米；仅检查单位，还不能决定题目要的是哪一个。

## Split where the direction changes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*在方向改变处切开*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $v(t)=2t-2$ on $[0,2]$, velocity is negative before $t=1$ and positive after it. One position with this velocity is $s(t)=t^2-2t$, starting at zero. It reaches $s(1)=-1$, then returns to $s(2)=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $[0,2]$ 上取 $v(t)=2t-2$。$t=1$ 之前速度为负，之后为正。一个对应的位置是 $s(t)=t^2-2t$，从零出发，先到达 $s(1)=-1$，再回到 $s(2)=0$。

$$\int_0^1v(t)\,dt=-1,\qquad\int_1^2v(t)\,dt=1.$$

> [!example] The return does not erase the journey · 回到起点不会抹掉路程
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The displacement is $-1+1=0$. To find distance, reverse the negative contribution before adding: $-(-1)+1=2$. Equivalently, use $|2t-2|=2-2t$ before $1$ and $|2t-2|=2t-2$ after $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 位移为 $-1+1=0$。求路程时，先把负方向的贡献变成正数再相加：$-(-1)+1=2$。也就是在 $1$ 之前用 $|2t-2|=2-2t$，之后用 $|2t-2|=2t-2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[积分绝对值界|integral triangle inequality]] guarantees $|s(b)-s(a)|\le\int_a^b|v(t)|\,dt$. Taking one absolute value after integration gives only the magnitude of displacement. It cannot restore motion already cancelled in the integral. If velocity stays nonnegative throughout, distance equals displacement; if it stays nonpositive, distance is the negative of displacement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[积分绝对值界]]保证 $|s(b)-s(a)|\le\int_a^b|v(t)|\,dt$。积分后只取一次绝对值，得到的仍只是位移的大小，无法补回已相互抵消的运动。速度全程非负时，路程等于位移；全程非正时，路程是位移的相反数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For hand calculation, find the zeros of velocity and check its sign on the intervening intervals. A zero alone does not mean reversal: $v(t)=(t-1)^2$ touches zero and remains nonnegative. [[定积分区间可加性|Interval additivity]] justifies adding the contributions of the sign intervals. The integral of speed remains meaningful even when a finite list of reversals is unavailable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

手算时先找速度的零点，再检查中间各段的符号。速度为零不一定反向：$v(t)=(t-1)^2$ 只是接触零，前后仍非负。[[定积分区间可加性]]允许把各符号区间的贡献相加。即使无法用有限列表列出全部反向时刻，速率的积分仍有意义。

## The same distinction beyond motion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*运动以外的同一种区别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any continuously differentiable scalar quantity $F$, integrating $F'$ gives its final-minus-initial change; integrating $|F'|$ gives its [[函数总变差|total variation]], the accumulated up-and-down movement. For a graph, $\int f$ is signed area, whereas $\int|f|$ counts area on both sides of the horizontal axis positively. For the area between two graphs, apply the difference of their heights as explained in [[曲线间面积|area between curves]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意连续可微的实值量 $F$，积分 $F'$ 给末值减初值，积分 $|F'|$ 则给它的[[函数总变差]]，即累计上下变化量。对函数图像，$\int f$ 是带符号面积，$\int|f|$ 则把横轴两侧的面积都按正数累计。两条曲线之间的面积要处理两者高度之差，见[[曲线间面积]]。

> [!note]- Why the total-variation formula holds · 总变差公式为什么成立
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For any partition, each $|F(t_i)-F(t_{i-1})|$ is at most the integral of $|F'|$ on that piece, so their sum is at most $\int_a^b|F'|$. Conversely, the [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]] writes each increment as $F'(c_i)\Delta t_i$ for a point inside its piece. Their absolute values form a tagged [[黎曼和|Riemann sum]] of the continuous function $|F'|$. As the largest width tends to zero, these sums approach the integral, proving equality with the supremum over partitions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对任意分割，每个 $|F(t_i)-F(t_{i-1})|$ 都不超过该段上 $|F'|$ 的积分，因此相加后不超过 $\int_a^b|F'|$。反过来，[[拉格朗日中值定理]]把每段增量写成某个内部点处的 $F'(c_i)\Delta t_i$。取绝对值后，它们构成连续函数 $|F'|$ 的带采样点[[黎曼和]]。最大段宽趋于零时，这些和趋于该积分，因此所有分割所得和的上确界正好等于它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These formulas concern a finite time interval with the stated regularity. Continuous differentiability is a sufficient condition, not a necessary one; a continuous path split into finitely many pieces can be handled piece by piece if each restriction is continuously differentiable up to its piece endpoints. A position jump cannot be counted by simply integrating an ordinary velocity that ignores the jump.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些公式针对满足上述正则条件的有限时间区间。连续可微是充分条件，并非必要条件；若位置连续，能分成有限段且每段直到端点都连续可微，也可逐段计算。若位置本身发生跳跃，不能只积分一个忽略跳跃的普通速度函数，就声称已数清全部移动。
