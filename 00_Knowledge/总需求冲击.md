# 总需求冲击是使同一价格水平下计划总支出发生变化的扰动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An aggregate demand shock changes planned aggregate spending at a given price level.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总需求冲击改变消费、投资、政府购买或净出口的决定条件，从而移动[[总需求|AD 曲线]]。信心变化、外部需求变化和某些政策变化都可能产生该扰动；一般价格自身变化引起的沿 AD 移动不叫 AD 冲击。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A demand shock changes determinants of consumption, investment, government purchases, or net exports, shifting [[总需求|AD]]. Confidence, external demand, and policy may cause such a disturbance. A price-level movement along an unchanged AD curve is not an AD shock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在给定向上倾斜的[[短期总供给]]时，正向需求冲击使均衡产出与价格水平同升，负向冲击使二者同降。这是单独移动 AD 的比较静态；若同时有供给变化，不能只凭观察到的 GDP 与 CPI 就唯一识别冲击来源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With upward-sloping [[短期总供给|SRAS]] fixed, a positive demand shock raises equilibrium output and prices, while a negative shock lowers both. This comparative static shifts only AD. Simultaneous supply changes prevent unique identification from GDP and CPI alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如出口订单突然减少可压低计划支出；但进口也可能因国内衰退更快下降，使净出口反而增加。因此“贸易顺差扩大”本身不能替代完整的需求诊断。长期结局另由[[AD-AS长期调整]]处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fewer export orders can reduce planned spending, yet imports may fall even faster in a domestic downturn, increasing net exports. A larger trade surplus alone is therefore not a complete demand diagnosis. [[AD-AS长期调整|Long-run adjustment]] is a separate step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
