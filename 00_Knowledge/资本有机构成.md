# 资本有机构成是由技术构成决定并反映其变化的资本价值构成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Organic composition is value composition insofar as it is determined by technical composition and reflects its changes.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Marx 把[[资本价值构成]]中由[[资本技术构成]]决定、并反映后者变化的方面称为资本有机构成。这个定义把价值比例与生产技术联系起来：相同劳动运用更多生产资料，可能使投入生产资料的价值相对于投入劳动力的价值上升。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Marx calls the [[资本价值构成|value composition]] organic insofar as it is determined by the [[资本技术构成|technical composition]] and reflects its changes. The definition connects value proportions to production techniques: employing more means of production with the same labour may raise the value invested in those means relative to labour power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“由技术构成决定并反映其变化”是定义的一部分。因此，单独观察到 $c/v$ 变大，首先只确定价值构成变化，还须解释它与生产资料和劳动的实物关系有什么联系。工资变化或相同原料的价值变化，都能改变价值比例而未必改变技术组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Determined by technical composition and reflecting its changes” is part of the definition. Observing a higher $c/v$ first establishes a change in value composition; its relation to the physical combination of means of production and labour still needs explaining. Changed wages or the value of unchanged materials can alter the ratio without changing that combination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实物增长与价值增长也不一定同幅。若同类机器数量翻倍而每台价值减半，这部分机器的总价值保持不变；还要检查其他生产资料与劳动力，才能判断全部 $c/v$ 怎样变化。把“机器更多”连到[[资本构成与利润率|利润率比较]]之前，需要先补上这一步价值计量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Physical and value growth need not have the same magnitude. If the number of identical machines doubles while each machine's value halves, their total value is unchanged. Other productive inputs and labour power must also be examined to determine the overall $c/v$. This valuation step is required before connecting more machinery to [[资本构成与利润率|a profit-rate comparison]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
