# 更高ROE不能单独证明经营改善或股东价值增加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Higher ROE alone does not establish improved operations or greater shareholder value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ROE 上升时，先区分净利润改善和权益分母变薄，再判断两者背后的交易与风险。[[杜邦分解]]允许我们定位利润率、资产周转和杠杆的变化，但它没有证明“借得越多，经营越好”。借款回购、亏损冲减权益或其他权益调整，都可能改变这个分母。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When ROE rises, distinguish higher net income from a thinner equity denominator, then examine the transactions and risks behind each. [[杜邦分解|DuPont decomposition]] locates margin, turnover, and leverage changes; it does not prove that borrowing more improves operations. Debt-funded repurchases, losses, and other equity adjustments can change the denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个明确简化的全年模型中，期初经营资产均为 100、全年融资前利润均为 10，无税及其他损益，ROE 统一采用期初权益。甲全由权益支持，ROE 为 $10/100=10\%$；乙由全年维持的债务 50、期初权益 50 支持，债务利率 4%，净利润为 $10-2=8$，ROE 却为 $8/50=16\%$。乙经营利润没有增加，股东面对的固定付息义务增加了。若经营利润降到 1，甲 ROE 为 1%，乙则为 −2%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simplified full-year model, both firms start with operating assets of 100 and earn pre-financing profit of 10, with no tax or other items; ROE uses opening equity consistently. Firm A is entirely equity-financed and earns 10% ROE. Firm B maintains debt of 50 at 4% throughout the year and starts with equity of 50; net income is 8 but ROE is 16%. Operating profit did not improve, while fixed interest obligations increased. If operating profit falls to 1, A earns 1% ROE and B earns −2%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，高 ROE 是继续调查的线索，不是估值结论。要分开经营回报与融资成本，可用[[管理用ROE分解]]；要判断价值，则还需未来现金流与承担风险所要求的回报。银行的具体读法复用[[ROA、ROE 与杠杆]]，其中还要结合资本充足与资产风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
High ROE is a reason to investigate, not a valuation conclusion. [[管理用ROE分解|The operating-financing decomposition]] separates operating return from financing cost; valuation also needs future cash flows and the return required for their risk. For banks, use [[ROA、ROE 与杠杆|the bank-specific ROA/ROE/leverage diagnostic]], including capital adequacy and asset risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
