# 组合计数原理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Principles of combinatorial counting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 不逐个枚举地计算有限结构数量，并用渐近语言比较输入变大时的增长速度。
> **具体锚点：** 长度 n 的二进制串有 $2^n$ 个；恰有 k 个 1 的串有 $\binom nk$ 个，因为只需选择 1 的位置。
> **核心难点：** 加法原理要求情形互斥，乘法原理要求每一步选择数在条件下正确；重复、顺序和不可区分性决定公式。
> **为什么重要：** 概率分母、算法复杂度、离散模型和随机结构都依赖可靠计数。
> **继续：** 先写对象和双射，再选排列组合、隔板、容斥或鸽巢，而不是按关键词套公式。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Counting principles determine the number of finite structures without listing them one by one. Growth-rate notation is a related comparison tool, but its full treatment belongs in [[渐近记号与算法复杂度|asymptotic notation and algorithmic complexity]].
> **Concrete anchor:** There are $2^n$ binary strings of length $n$, and $\binom nk$ with exactly $k$ ones because the object is determined by choosing their positions.
> **Central difficulty:** Addition requires disjoint cases; multiplication requires the correct conditional number of choices at each stage. Order, repetition, and distinguishability determine the formula.
> **Why it matters:** Probability denominators, discrete models, random structures, and complexity arguments depend on reliable counting.
> **Continue with:** Define the objects and seek a bijection before choosing permutations, combinations, stars and bars, inclusion–exclusion, or the pigeonhole principle.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 加法、乘法与双射
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Addition, multiplication, and bijections*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

互斥类别的数量相加，连续选择阶段的数量相乘。若两个集合能建立一一对应，它们等势；双射常把难计数对象转换为熟悉对象，并自动避免重复或漏计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Add the sizes of mutually exclusive classes and multiply the numbers of choices across successive stages. A bijection shows that two sets have the same size; it often converts unfamiliar objects into familiar ones while preventing duplication and omission by construction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用乘法原理时，第二步的选择数可以依赖第一步的具体结果，但必须对每个可能前缀都是同一数量，或者将不同前缀分类后分别计数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the product rule, the available second-stage choices may depend on the particular first-stage outcome, but their number must be constant across all possible prefixes. Otherwise, partition the prefixes into cases and count each case separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 排列、组合与重复
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Permutations, combinations, and repetition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 n 个不同对象有序选 k 个是下降阶乘，无序选是 $\binom nk$。有重复对象的排列要除以重复阶乘；可重复、无序选择对应 stars and bars，但前提是盒子可区分且对象只按数量区分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choosing $k$ ordered objects from $n$ distinct objects gives a falling factorial; ignoring order gives $\binom nk$. Permutations of a multiset divide by factorials of repeated multiplicities. Repeated unordered choices invite stars and bars only when boxes are distinguishable and objects matter solely through their counts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 二项式、鸽巢与容斥
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The binomial theorem, pigeonhole principle, and inclusion–exclusion*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二项式系数既计数选择也给 $(x+y)^n$ 展开。鸽巢原理用容量矛盾证明至少一个盒子过载。容斥先相加单集合，再减交集、加三重交集；截断时 Bonferroni 不等式给界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Binomial coefficients both count selections and give the expansion of $(x+y)^n$. The pigeonhole principle proves overload through a capacity contradiction. Inclusion–exclusion adds single-set sizes, subtracts pairwise intersections, and adds triple intersections; truncated sums yield Bonferroni bounds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 求和与渐近
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sums and asymptotics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

大 O 给上界、Omega 给下界、Theta 给同阶双边界；常数和低阶项在增长比较中被忽略，但有限规模性能仍可能重要。证明界要给常数和阈值或用极限比较，不应只凭图形。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Big O gives an upper bound, Omega a lower bound, and Theta matching upper and lower growth bounds. Constants and lower-order terms are ignored in asymptotic comparison, although they can matter at finite sizes. A proof must exhibit constants and a threshold or use a valid limit comparison; a graph alone is not a proof. See [[渐近记号与算法复杂度|asymptotic notation and algorithmic complexity]] for the full retrieval unit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 生成函数入口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Entry point to generating functions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把序列 $a_n$ 编码为幂级数 $\sum a_nx^n$，乘法对应卷积，递推可转成代数方程。它是计数工具，不一定把 x 当实际概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Encode a sequence $a_n$ as the power series $\sum a_nx^n$. Multiplication represents convolution, and recurrences can become algebraic equations. This is a counting device; $x$ need not represent a real probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：至少选一个的计数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: counting selections with at least one item*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 10 名学生中选一个非空委员会，直接按人数求和是 $\sum_{k=1}^{10}\binom{10}{k}$。更短的方法是用补集：每人都有“选”或“不选”两种状态，共 $2^{10}$ 个子集，再去掉空集，得 $2^{10}-1$。两种方法同时给出二项式恒等式 $\sum_{k=0}^{10}\binom{10}{k}=2^{10}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To choose a nonempty committee from ten students, summing by committee size gives $\sum_{k=1}^{10}\binom{10}{k}$. A shorter complement argument gives each student two states, selected or not, for $2^{10}$ subsets; remove the empty subset to obtain $2^{10}-1$. The two counts also establish $\sum_{k=0}^{10}\binom{10}{k}=2^{10}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 常见诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Common diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每次计数都问：对象是否可区分、顺序是否重要、能否重复、是否恰好/至多、各情形是否重叠。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every count, ask whether objects are distinguishable, whether order matters, whether repetition is allowed, whether the condition is exact or bounded, and whether the cases overlap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Minimum self-check*

### 为什么加法原理要求情形互斥？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does the addition rule require disjoint cases?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 若同一对象落入多个类别，直接相加会重复计数；需改成不交分解或用容斥修正。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If an object belongs to several classes, direct addition counts it repeatedly. Replace the classes by a disjoint partition or correct the overlap with inclusion–exclusion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 隔板法适用前要检查什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What must be checked before using stars and bars?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 盒子可区分、对象只按数量不可区分、允许的非负/正数约束已通过变量变换正确处理。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The boxes must be distinguishable, the objects indistinguishable except through counts, and any nonnegative or positive lower bounds must be handled by the correct variable shift.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### $O(n)$ 和 $\Theta(n)$ 的信息差别是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How does the information in $O(n)$ differ from that in $\Theta(n)$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> $O(n)$ 只给不超过线性量级的上界，常数函数也属于；$\Theta(n)$ 同时给线性上、下界。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $O(n)$ supplies only a linear upper bound and therefore includes constant functions; $\Theta(n)$ supplies matching linear upper and lower bounds.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明：为什么双射是一种“不用枚举的计数证明”？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why a bijection proves a count without enumeration.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 双射让目标集合的每个对象恰好对应已知集合的一个对象；既无重复也无遗漏，所以数量相同。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A bijection pairs each target object with exactly one object in a set of known size. Because nothing is duplicated or omitted, the two sets have the same cardinality.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
