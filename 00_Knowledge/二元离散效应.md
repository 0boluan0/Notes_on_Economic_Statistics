# 二元或离散解释变量的概率效应用两种完整预测之差计算，而不是用不存在的无穷小导数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The probability effect of a binary or discrete explanatory variable is the difference between two complete predictions, not a nonexistent infinitesimal derivative*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 状态对比
> 对二元解释变量 $D$，给定其他协变量 $Z=z$ 的模型内离散效应是
> $$
> \Delta_D(z)=G[\eta(D=1,z)]-G[\eta(D=0,z)].
> $$
> 两边都要重算完整指数；$D$ 的主效应、交互项和其他依赖 $D$ 的变换必须一起改变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a binary explanatory variable $D$, the model-implied discrete effect at other covariates $Z=z$ is $\Delta_D(z)=G[\eta(D=1,z)]-G[\eta(D=0,z)]$. The complete index must be recomputed on both sides: the main effect of $D$, its interactions, and every other transformation that depends on $D$ must all change together.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二元变量没有可观察的“增加一个无穷小单位”，所以在 $D=0$ 处报告导数不能替代从 0 到 1 的真实状态切换。多类别变量同理：先说明基准类别和目标类别，再比较相应完整预测。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A binary variable has no observable change of “one infinitesimal unit,” so a derivative reported at $D=0$ cannot replace the actual switch from 0 to 1. The same principle applies to a multicategory variable: state the reference and target categories first, then compare the corresponding complete predictions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\eta=-1+0.7D-0.4DZ+0.2Z$。在 $Z=0$ 时，切换 $D$ 使指数增加 0.7；在 $Z=1$ 时只增加 0.3。概率差还会受两处基准指数影响，因此既不能只报 0.7，也不能把 $G(0.7)-G(0)$ 当成所有人的离散效应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, let $\eta=-1+0.7D-0.4DZ+0.2Z$. At $Z=0$, switching $D$ raises the index by 0.7; at $Z=1$, it raises the index by only 0.3. The probability difference also depends on the baseline index in both states. One therefore cannot report only 0.7 or treat $G(0.7)-G(0)$ as the discrete effect for everyone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\Delta_D(z)$ 是拟合模型下的条件对比。把它解释为“把同一个人从 $D=0$ 干预到 $D=1$ 的因果效应”，还需要 $D$ 的干预定义、共同支持和可信识别条件；非线性链接本身不提供反事实。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\Delta_D(z)$ is a conditional contrast under the fitted model. Interpreting it as the causal effect of intervening on the same person to change $D$ from 0 to 1 additionally requires a definition of that intervention, common support, and credible identification conditions. A nonlinear link does not itself supply the counterfactual.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
