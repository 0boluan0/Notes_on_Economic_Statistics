# 公债再融资风险是到期债务只能以异常高成本续借或无法续借的风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Public debt refinancing risk is the risk that maturing debt can be renewed only at unusually high cost, or cannot be renewed at all.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公债再融资风险关注旧债到期时能否取得替代资金，以及取得资金的成本。[[政府公债]]的存量相同，若到期日集中程度、币种或持有人续投意愿不同，续借压力也可以不同；嵌入回售权等条款还可能提前现金需求。不能只看债务总额而忽略具体[[债券|偿付日程]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Refinancing risk concerns access to replacement funding when old debt matures and the cost of that funding. Equal [[政府公债|public debt]] stocks may imply different pressures because of maturity concentration, currency or investors' willingness to renew. Put options can bring funding needs forward. The stock alone does not describe the [[债券|payment schedule]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，存量本金 $100$ 中有 $20$ 明年到期、其余 $80$ 第五年到期，明年还须付息 $4$。假设扣除其他支出后的可用财政现金为 $6$，另有确定可动用的现金缓冲 $8$，且没有其他支付，则明年须筹的新资金为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose principal of $100$ includes $20$ due next year and $80$ in year five, with interest of $4$ also payable next year. Assume available fiscal cash after other outlays is $6$, a usable cash buffer is $8$, and there are no other payments. Required new funding is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
(20+4)-(6+8)=10.
$$

若只能融到 $7$，仍有现金缺口 $3$；若能融到 $10$ 但只能接受异常高利率，也发生了再融资风险中的成本冲击。这只是给定日期的现金核对，不是由债务本金 $100$ 单独得出的结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Funding of only $7$ leaves a cash gap of $3$. Raising the full $10$ only at an unusually high rate realises the cost component of refinancing risk. This is a dated cash reconciliation, not a conclusion inferred from the debt stock of $100$ alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到期日错开、预先融资和适当现金缓冲可以降低集中续借压力，但有持有现金和融资成本等取舍，也不能把未落实的备用授信当成无条件资金。较长期债务未必在每种风险上都更安全：长期浮息债可以减少近期到期本金，同时保留[[再融资与利率重定价|利率重置风险]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Staggering maturities, prefinancing and cash buffers can reduce concentrated rollover pressure, with funding and carry costs. Unconfirmed standby credit should not be treated as unconditional cash. Longer maturity does not reduce every risk: long-term floating-rate debt can reduce near-term principal maturities while retaining [[再融资与利率重定价|repricing risk]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

到期资金能否续接，是[[债务可持续性]]评估的一部分，不能替代整体判断。具有长期偿付能力不保证每个到期日都有买家；暂时能续借，也不证明未来所有偿付安排可行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Access to funding at maturity is one part of [[债务可持续性|debt sustainability]] assessment, not a substitute for the whole assessment. Long-run repayment capacity does not guarantee buyers at every maturity, and temporary rollover access does not establish that all future debt service is feasible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
