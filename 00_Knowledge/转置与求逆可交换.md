# Inverse of a transpose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*转置与求逆可交换*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are two ways to combine [[矩阵转置|transposition]], which swaps rows and columns, with [[逆矩阵|inversion]], which reverses a matrix action. For an invertible square matrix, transposing first and then inverting gives the same matrix as inverting first and then transposing. This lets us reuse an inverse we already know.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[矩阵转置|转置]]交换行与列，[[逆矩阵|求逆]]寻找撤销矩阵作用的矩阵。这两个操作可以按两种顺序进行。对可逆方阵，先转置再求逆，与先求逆再转置，结果相同，因此可以复用已经知道的逆矩阵。

> [!example] Turn an upper shear into a lower shear · 从上方剪切变成下方剪切
> $$
> A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad A^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $A$ adds twice the second coordinate to the first; its inverse subtracts that amount. After transposing,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $A$ 把第二个坐标的两倍加到第一个坐标上，逆矩阵则把它减掉。转置以后，
>
> $$
> A^T=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\2&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad (A^{-1})^T=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-2&1\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Now $A^T$ adds twice the first coordinate to the second, and $(A^{-1})^T$ subtracts it. Multiplying these two lower triangular matrices in either order gives $I_2$, so the transposed inverse is indeed the inverse of the transpose.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 此时 $A^T$ 把第一个坐标的两倍加到第二个坐标上，$(A^{-1})^T$ 则减掉它。把这两个下三角矩阵按任一顺序相乘，都得到 $I_2$，所以逆矩阵的转置确实就是转置矩阵的逆。

## Transpose both inverse identities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把两条逆矩阵等式转置*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be an invertible finite $n\times n$ real or complex matrix. The claim is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 是有限阶 $n\times n$ 的实或复可逆方阵。结论为

$$
(A^T)^{-1}=(A^{-1})^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By definition, $AA^{-1}=A^{-1}A=I_n$. The [[乘积的转置|transpose-of-a-product rule]] reverses the order of factors, and $I_n^T=I_n$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据定义，$AA^{-1}=A^{-1}A=I_n$。[[乘积的转置|乘积转置规则]]会反转因子次序，而 $I_n^T=I_n$。因此

$$
(AA^{-1})^T=(A^{-1})^TA^T=I_n,\qquad (A^{-1}A)^T=A^T(A^{-1})^T=I_n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These equations show both that $A^T$ is invertible and that its inverse is $(A^{-1})^T$. The [[逆矩阵唯一性|uniqueness theorem]] then justifies the displayed equality. For complex entries, $T$ still means ordinary transpose: exchange row and column positions without conjugating the entries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这两个等式既证明 $A^T$ 可逆，也证明 $(A^{-1})^T$ 是它的逆，再由[[逆矩阵唯一性]]得到所写的等式。对于复数元素，$T$ 仍表示普通转置：只交换行列位置，不对元素取共轭。

> [!warning] Two commuting operations are not the same operation · 两个操作可交换，不代表它们相同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The equality compares two successive operations; it does not say $A^T=A^{-1}$. For $D=\operatorname{diag}(2,3)$, transposition leaves $D$ unchanged, while $D^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/3)$. The stronger equality $A^T=A^{-1}$ holds for real [[正交矩阵|orthogonal matrices]]. If $A$ is singular, the ordinary inverse expressions in this rule are undefined.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 等式比较的是两次操作的先后次序，并没有说 $A^T=A^{-1}$。例如 $D=\operatorname{diag}(2,3)$ 转置后不变，但 $D^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1/3)$。更强的 $A^T=A^{-1}$ 对实[[正交矩阵]]成立。若 $A$ 奇异，本规律中的普通逆表达式就没有定义。
