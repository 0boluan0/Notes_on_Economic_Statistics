# Transposition and inversion commute for an invertible matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*对可逆矩阵，先转置后求逆与先求逆后转置结果相同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an invertible square matrix $A$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对可逆方阵 $A$，

$$
(A^T)^{-1}=(A^{-1})^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply the [[乘积的转置|transpose-product rule]] to the two inverse identities:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对两条逆矩阵恒等式应用[[乘积的转置|乘积转置规则]]：

$$
(AA^{-1})^T=(A^{-1})^TA^T=I,
\qquad
(A^{-1}A)^T=A^T(A^{-1})^T=I.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $(A^{-1})^T$ is the two-sided inverse of $A^T$. This does not say that $A^T=A^{-1}$; that equality is an additional property, satisfied for example by real [[正交矩阵|orthogonal matrices]]. If $A$ is singular, the ordinary inverse expressions are undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以 $(A^{-1})^T$ 是 $A^T$ 的双侧逆。此结论不表示 $A^T=A^{-1}$；后者是额外性质，例如实[[正交矩阵]]满足它。若 $A$ 奇异，这里的普通逆矩阵表达式无定义。
