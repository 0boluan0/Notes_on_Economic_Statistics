# 独立财政机构是以非党派和专业独立方式提供财政分析与监督的公共机构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An independent fiscal institution is a public body providing non-partisan, professionally independent fiscal analysis and oversight.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

独立财政机构通过预测评估、政策成本分析、规则监测等工作，为财政决策提供可以公开核查的专业信息。财政委员会和议会预算办公室是可能的组织形式；具体职责取决于其法定授权，不能假设每个机构都承担全部职能。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Independent fiscal institutions provide verifiable analysis through functions such as forecast assessment, policy costing and rule monitoring. Fiscal councils and parliamentary budget offices are possible forms; mandates differ, so no institution should be assumed to perform every function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的独立主要是分析与工作安排不受党派指令左右，需要相应的任用保障、足够资源、及时信息取得权以及公开方法和报告。把机构命名为“独立”并不能单独证明这些条件已经具备。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Independence concerns analysis and work free from partisan direction, supported by appointment safeguards, resources, timely information access and published methods and reports. The label “independent” does not establish those conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这类机构可以解释预算假设是否可信、[[财政规则合规与效果]]分别有什么证据，也可依授权评估[[财政逃逸条款]]。它通常通过公开分析和声誉发挥影响，而不是代替民选机构决定税收和支出；其事前分析职责也不等于事后财务审计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Such an institution can assess budget assumptions, distinguish evidence on [[财政规则合规与效果|rule compliance and effectiveness]], and review [[财政逃逸条款|escape clauses]] where mandated. Its influence is generally analytical and reputational, not a substitute for elected fiscal decisions. Ex-ante analysis is also distinct from ex-post financial audit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [OECD, Recommendation on Principles for Independent Fiscal Institutions](https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/public/doc/301/body-text.en.html)，前言及原则 1–9，尤其 2–8：支持定义、国别适配、非党派性、法定职责、资源、数据获取、透明度和说服性影响。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The preamble and Principles 1–9, especially 2–8, support the definition, country-specific mandates, non-partisanship, resources, data access, transparency and persuasive influence.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
