# 显示偏好估值利用实际选择中的可识别权衡恢复非市场属性的价值

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revealed-preference valuation recovers non-market attribute values from identifiable trade-offs in actual choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

显示偏好估值使用人们真正作出的选择，而不是只询问假想回答：先确定可行选项、货币差额与所关心属性的差异，再说明为什么其他差异不足以解释选择。例如实际选择较贵而省时的路线，可以为[[时间节约价值]]提供证据；复合商品的连续属性价格见[[享乐价格估值]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revealed-preference valuation uses actual choices. Establish the feasible alternatives, monetary difference and relevant attribute change, then explain why other differences do not account for the choice. Choosing a costlier, faster route can inform [[时间节约价值|time value]]; [[享乐价格估值|hedonic valuation]] treats continuous attributes in bundled goods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若两条路线除多付2元与节省10分钟外完全相同，而且决策者知情、可自由选择并按稳定偏好作出选择，选择收费路线意味着他对这段节时的支付意愿至少为2元。比值12元/小时是这一离散变化平均价值的下界，不是人人通用的精确边际估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If informed travellers choose freely according to stable preferences between otherwise identical routes differing only by a charge of 2 and a saving of 10 minutes, the paid choice implies willingness to pay of at least 2. The ratio of 12 per hour bounds average value for this discrete saving; it is not a universal exact marginal estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此必须检查预算、选择集、信息、被遗漏的舒适度等属性，以及谁选择进入样本。实际行为减少了一类假想回答问题，却不自动消除混杂或选择偏差；价格相关性不等于所求属性的因果福利效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check budgets, choice sets, information, omitted amenities and selection into the sample. Actual behaviour avoids one class of hypothetical-response problems but does not automatically eliminate confounding or selection bias. Price correlation alone is not a causal welfare effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
