# 成本不确定时价格与数量工具的局部福利比较取决于边际收益与边际成本的相对斜率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Under cost uncertainty, the local welfare comparison of prices and quantities depends on the relative slopes of marginal benefits and marginal costs.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

监管者在不知道真实减排成本时必须提前定政策：价格工具固定单位排放收费、让减排量调整；数量工具固定总减排量、让实施它的边际成本调整。在已知效益、局部线性边际曲线的基准中，边际减排收益相对更陡时数量工具损失较小；边际减排成本相对更陡时价格工具损失较小。关键是同一数量轴上的**相对斜率**，不是企业间成本差异大不大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before learning actual abatement costs, a regulator must choose a fixed emissions price with adjustable abatement, or fixed total abatement with adjustable marginal implementation costs. With known benefits and locally linear marginal curves, quantities perform better when marginal benefits are relatively steeper; prices perform better when marginal costs are relatively steeper. Compare slopes on the same quantity axis, not cost differences across firms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a$为总减排、$a_0$为按期望曲线选定的计划量，$x=a-a_0$。各状态下都按[[成本有效减排|最低成本分担]]治理；总边际成本和边际效益为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a$ be total abatement, $a_0$ the planned amount selected from expected curves, and $x=a-a_0$. Assume [[成本有效减排|least-cost allocation]] in every state. Aggregate marginal cost and marginal benefit are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
MB(a)=p_0-bx,\qquad MAC(a,\theta)=p_0+cx+\theta,
\qquad b\ge0,\quad c>0,\quad E[\theta]=0,\quad
\operatorname{Var}(\theta)=\sigma^2.
$$

企业在工具固定后知道成本状态 $\theta$；效益已知、完全履约、所有响应均在可行内点，没有另加风险厌恶、收益冲击或治理外部效应。用货币效益减真实资源成本评价福利。这里 $b$是减排边际效益下降的斜率绝对值，$c$是总边际减排成本上升的斜率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Firms observe the cost state after the instrument is fixed. Benefits are known, compliance is complete, all responses are interior, and the model excludes risk aversion, benefit shocks and external effects of abatement. Welfare is monetary benefit minus real resource cost. The parameters $b$ and $c$ measure the absolute downward slope of marginal abatement benefit and the upward slope of aggregate marginal abatement cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

事后最优、固定数量和固定价格 $t=p_0$分别给出下列偏离。福利相对事后最优的损失，可从两条边际曲线之间的面积直接算出：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ex-post optimum, fixed quantity and fixed price $t=p_0$ imply the following deviations. Integrating the gap between the marginal curves gives losses relative to the ex-post optimum:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
x^*=-\frac{\theta}{b+c},\quad x_Q=0,\quad x_P=-\frac{\theta}{c},
$$
$$
L_Q=\frac{\theta^2}{2(b+c)},\qquad
L_P=\frac{b^2\theta^2}{2c^2(b+c)},\qquad
E[W_P-W_Q]=\frac{\sigma^2(c-b)}{2c^2}.
$$

因此在这个模型中，$c>b$有利于价格，$b>c$有利于数量；$b=c$时本项二次比较打平，$\sigma^2=0$时没有这一不确定性损失。横轴若改为排放量，应比较边际损害上升斜率与边际减排成本下降斜率的绝对值，不能把图轴反转后仍照搬斜率符号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $c>b$ favours prices and $b>c$ favours quantities in this model. Equal slopes tie in this quadratic comparison; zero cost variance removes this uncertainty loss. On an emissions axis, compare the upward slope of marginal damage with the absolute downward slope of marginal abatement cost, taking care with the reversed axis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自造例：$a_0=10,p_0=20,c=2$，$\theta$以相等概率为$-2$或$+2$。固定数量总是10，固定价格使减排为11或9。若$b=1$，每状态数量损失为$2/3$、价格损失为$1/6$，价格平均好$1/2$；若仅改为$b=4$，两损失变成$1/3$和$4/3$，数量平均好1。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $a_0=10$, $p_0=20$, $c=2$ and equally likely shocks of minus or plus two. Quantity remains ten, while a fixed price gives abatement eleven or nine. At $b=1$, losses are $2/3$ for quantities and $1/6$ for prices, favouring prices by $1/2$. Changing only $b$ to four gives losses $1/3$ and $4/3$, favouring quantities by one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[共同排放价格与减排分担|企业间异质性]]解释为什么共同价格能改进分担；本卡的不确定性则是监管者事前不知道哪条成本曲线会实现。若收益和成本冲击相关、响应触及角点、存在大范围非线性、存量污染动态或不可逆阈值，须重做福利比较，不能把这一局部判据当作污染物的固定分类。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[共同排放价格与减排分担|Heterogeneity across firms]] explains how a common price improves allocation. Here uncertainty means the regulator does not know which cost curve will occur. Correlated benefit and cost shocks, corners, substantial nonlinearities, pollution stocks or irreversible thresholds require a fresh welfare comparison rather than a fixed classification of pollutants.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
