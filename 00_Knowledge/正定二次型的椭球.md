# Ellipsoids from positive-definite quadratic forms
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
正定二次型的椭球

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ellipse can describe all points with the same quadratic cost. For example, the equation $4x_1^2+x_2^2=1$ allows a displacement of only $1/2$ along the first coordinate axis, but a displacement of $1$ along the second. The first direction is more expensive: its square is multiplied by $4$. This is why a larger quadratic coefficient produces a shorter axis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
椭圆可以表示二次代价相同的所有点。例如，方程 $4x_1^2+x_2^2=1$ 允许沿第一条坐标轴移动 $1/2$，沿第二条坐标轴则能移动 $1$。第一种方向的平方被乘以 $4$，代价更高，因此对应的轴更短。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matrix expression packages these squared terms into a [[二次型|quadratic form]]: $x^TAx$, where $x$ is a real column vector and $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. For this example, $A=\operatorname{diag}(4,1)$ is the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] with diagonal entries $4$ and $1$. A general symmetric matrix also contains cross terms such as $x_1x_2$; these can tilt the ellipse. Eigenvectors identify the tilted axes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
矩阵表达式把这些平方项收进一个[[二次型]] $x^TAx$：$x$ 是实列向量，$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。本例 $A=\operatorname{diag}(4,1)$ 是对角元为 $4,1$ 的[[对角矩阵]]。一般对称矩阵还会产生 $x_1x_2$ 这样的交叉项，使椭圆相对坐标轴倾斜；特征向量会指出倾斜后的轴在哪里。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Directions and semiaxis lengths
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 主轴方向与半轴长度

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume $n\ge1$ and $A$ is a real symmetric $n\times n$ [[正定矩阵|positive-definite matrix]]: $x^TAx>0$ whenever $x\ne0$. The [[实对称矩阵谱定理|real spectral theorem]] supplies perpendicular unit eigenvectors $q_1,\ldots,q_n$ and positive eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$. Each [[特征对|eigenpair]] satisfies $Aq_i=\lambda_iq_i$: the matrix scales the direction $q_i$ by $\lambda_i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $n\ge1$，$A$ 是实对称的 $n\times n$ [[正定矩阵]]，即只要 $x\ne0$ 就有 $x^TAx>0$。[[实对称矩阵谱定理]]给出相互垂直的单位特征向量 $q_1,\ldots,q_n$ 以及正特征值 $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$。每个[[特征对]]满足 $Aq_i=\lambda_iq_i$，表示矩阵在 $q_i$ 方向上按 $\lambda_i$ 缩放。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Put these vectors into the columns of $Q$ and the eigenvalues on the diagonal of $\Lambda$. Then $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$. Express a point using the new axes:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
把这些向量放进 $Q$ 的各列，把特征值放在 $\Lambda$ 的对角线上，就有 $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$。用这些新轴描述一个点：

$$
x=\sum_{i=1}^n y_iq_i=Qy,\qquad y=Q^Tx,\qquad y_i=q_i^Tx.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The number $y_i$ is the signed coordinate of $x$ along the unit direction $q_i$. Since $Q$ is [[正交矩阵|orthogonal]], this change of coordinates rotates or reflects the picture without changing lengths. Substitution removes the cross terms:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
数 $y_i$ 是 $x$ 沿单位方向 $q_i$ 的带符号坐标。由于 $Q$ 是[[正交矩阵]]，这种换坐标只旋转或反射图形，不改变长度。代入后，交叉项消失：

$$
x^TAx=\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i y_i^2,
\qquad
x^TAx=1\ \Longleftrightarrow\
\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{y_i^2}{(1/\sqrt{\lambda_i})^2}=1.
$$

> [!important] Principal axes / 主轴
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The surface $x^TAx=1$ is centred at the origin. Its principal directions are $q_i$, and its semiaxis lengths are $1/\sqrt{\lambda_i}$. A semiaxis runs from the centre to the boundary; the full axis has twice that length. In two dimensions the surface is an ellipse; in three it is an ellipsoid surface.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 曲面 $x^TAx=1$ 以原点为中心，主轴方向是 $q_i$，半轴长度是 $1/\sqrt{\lambda_i}$。半轴从中心走到边界，整条轴的长度是它的两倍。二维时这条边界是椭圆，三维时是椭球面。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The length formula can also be checked directly. On the line $x=tq_i$, the quadratic form is $\lambda_i t^2$. To reach level $1$, one needs $t=\pm1/\sqrt{\lambda_i}$. If an eigenvalue increases from $1$ to $9$, the corresponding semiaxis shrinks from $1$ to $1/3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
长度公式也可以直接检查：沿直线 $x=tq_i$，二次型等于 $\lambda_i t^2$；达到高度 $1$ 时，$t=\pm1/\sqrt{\lambda_i}$。因此，特征值从 $1$ 增大到 $9$ 时，相应半轴从 $1$ 缩短为 $1/3$。

> [!example] A tilted ellipse / 倾斜的椭圆
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A=\begin{bmatrix}5&4\\4&5\end{bmatrix}$, the equation is $5x_1^2+8x_1x_2+5x_2^2=1$. The unit directions $q_+=(1,1)^T/\sqrt2$ and $q_-=(1,-1)^T/\sqrt2$ have eigenvalues $9$ and $1$. Thus the ellipse is shortest along the upward diagonal, with semiaxis $1/3$, and longest along the downward diagonal, with semiaxis $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $A=\begin{bmatrix}5&4\\4&5\end{bmatrix}$，方程为 $5x_1^2+8x_1x_2+5x_2^2=1$。单位方向 $q_+=(1,1)^T/\sqrt2$、$q_-=(1,-1)^T/\sqrt2$ 分别对应特征值 $9,1$，因此沿向上的对角线最短，半轴为 $1/3$；沿向下的对角线最长，半轴为 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated eigenvalues give equal semiaxis lengths. Within their shared eigenspace, any perpendicular unit axes describe the same shape; the individual axis directions need not be unique. If every eigenvalue equals $\lambda$, the surface is a sphere of radius $1/\sqrt\lambda$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
重复特征值会给出相等的半轴长度。在它们共同的特征空间内，任取一组相互垂直的单位轴都会描述同一形状，因此每条轴的方向未必唯一。若所有特征值都等于 $\lambda$，曲面就是半径 $1/\sqrt\lambda$ 的球面。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Centre, level, and the filled region
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 中心、等值高度与内部区域

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed centre $c\in\mathbb R^n$ and a finite level $h>0$, replacing $x$ by $x-c$ translates the surface, and replacing $1$ by $h$ multiplies every semiaxis by $\sqrt h$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
给定中心 $c\in\mathbb R^n$ 和有限高度 $h>0$，把 $x$ 换成 $x-c$ 会平移曲面，把等式右边的 $1$ 换成 $h$ 会把每条半轴乘以 $\sqrt h$：

$$
(x-c)^TA(x-c)=h,
\qquad \text{semiaxis length}=\sqrt{h/\lambda_i}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equality describes the boundary; $\le h$ includes the interior. At $h=0$, positive definiteness leaves only the centre, and at $h<0$ there are no points. No probability model is required for these geometric statements. When the quadratic matrix is $A=\Sigma^{-1}$ for a positive-definite [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] $\Sigma$, its eigenvalues are the reciprocals of those of $\Sigma$: the semiaxes are then $\sqrt{h\lambda_i(\Sigma)}$. The associated volume calculation is developed separately in [[广义方差与椭球体积|generalized variance and ellipsoid volume]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
等号描述边界，$\le h$ 还包括内部。$h=0$ 时正定性只留下中心；$h<0$ 时没有点满足条件。这些都是几何结论，不需要概率模型。若二次型矩阵为正定[[协方差矩阵]] $\Sigma$ 的逆 $A=\Sigma^{-1}$，其特征值是 $\Sigma$ 特征值的倒数，半轴便成为 $\sqrt{h\lambda_i(\Sigma)}$。相应的体积计算见[[广义方差与椭球体积]]。

## What fails at the boundary of positive definiteness?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 不再严格正定时发生什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A$ is positive semidefinite with both a zero and a positive eigenvalue, the equation $x^TAx=1$ leaves a direction unrestricted. For example, $\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0)$ gives $x_1^2+x_2^2=1$ with arbitrary $x_3$: a cylinder extending indefinitely. This is different from flattening a solid ellipsoid by a singular linear map, which produces a bounded set in a lower-dimensional subspace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若 $A$ 半正定，同时有零特征值和正特征值，$x^TAx=1$ 会放任某个方向自由移动。例如 $\operatorname{diag}(1,1,0)$ 给出 $x_1^2+x_2^2=1$，而 $x_3$ 任意，是无限延伸的圆柱面。这与用奇异线性映射把实心椭球压扁不同：后者在较低维子空间内仍然有界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mixed positive and negative eigenvalues produce indefinite quadratic level sets; $x_1^2-x_2^2=1$ is a hyperbola. If all eigenvalues are nonpositive, level $1$ is empty. The useful conclusion is therefore precise: real symmetry and strict positivity give a nonempty, bounded ellipsoid surface at every positive level.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若特征值有正有负，就得到不定二次型的等值面，例如 $x_1^2-x_2^2=1$ 是双曲线；若所有特征值都不正，等于 $1$ 的集合则为空。准确结论是：实对称性与全部特征值严格为正，保证每个正高度上都有非空、有界的椭球面。
