# 正定二次型的单位等值面是以其矩阵特征向量为主轴方向的椭球
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The unit level set of a positive-definite quadratic form is an ellipsoid whose principal axes are eigenvectors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 几何解释
> 若实对称 $A\succ0$ 且 $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$，则等值面
> $$x^TAx=1$$
> 是以原点为中心的椭球。第 $i$ 条主轴方向是特征向量 $q_i$，对应半轴长度为 $1/\sqrt{\lambda_i}$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $A\succ0$ is real symmetric, the level set $x^TAx=1$ is a centred ellipsoid. Its principal axes are the eigenvectors $q_i$, and the semiaxis in direction $q_i$ has length $1/\sqrt{\lambda_i}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $y=Q^Tx$。正交换基只旋转或反射坐标，方程变为
$$
\lambda_1y_1^2+\cdots+\lambda_ny_n^2=1
=\sum_i\frac{y_i^2}{(1/\sqrt{\lambda_i})^2}.
$$
较大特征值表示该方向惩罚更强，所以椭球半轴更短。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In spectral coordinates the equation is $\sum_i\lambda_i y_i^2=1$. Orthogonal change of coordinates preserves lengths, and a larger eigenvalue produces a shorter semiaxis because the quadratic form grows faster in that direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

边界改变几何类型。若 $A\succeq0$ 有零特征值且至少一个正特征值，$x^TAx=1$ 沿零空间方向不受约束，是无界圆柱而非椭球；若有正负特征值，则通常得到双曲型等值面。只有全部特征值严格正才保证有界椭球。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the semidefinite boundary, a zero eigenvalue leaves a nullspace direction unconstrained and produces an unbounded cylinder when positive directions remain. Mixed signs lead to hyperbolic level sets. Strict positivity of every eigenvalue is what makes the ellipsoid bounded.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若特征值从 $1$ 增大到 $9$，对应单位等值面的半轴怎样变化？
>
> **答案：** 从 $1$ 缩短为 $1/3$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If an eigenvalue increases from $1$ to $9$, how does the corresponding semiaxis of the unit level set change?
>
> **Answer:** Its length decreases from $1$ to $1/3$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
