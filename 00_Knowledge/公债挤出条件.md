# 给定购买与收入时公债经储蓄渠道挤出投资须检查私人储蓄抵消与经济开放性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With purchases and income given, assess saving-mediated crowding out through the private-saving offset and the economy's openness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公债通过储蓄渠道挤出投资，是财政融资降低可用于资本积累的储蓄，使私人投资低于比较基准的机制；不能仅凭“政府发了债”就断言投资等额减少。先固定政府购买与收入框架，再问私人储蓄是否抵消了政府储蓄下降，以及剩余调整落在国内投资还是对外资产积累。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Saving-mediated crowding out occurs when fiscal financing lowers saving available for capital accumulation and thereby reduces private investment relative to a counterfactual. Bond issuance alone does not establish an equal investment decline. Hold purchases and the income framework fixed, then ask how private saving responds and whether adjustment falls on domestic investment or foreign asset accumulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在封闭经济、忽略折旧并统一总额口径的简化一期核算中，令 $Y$ 为收入、$C$ 为私人消费、$T$ 为净税收、$G$ 为政府购买，私人储蓄为 $S_p=Y-T-C$，政府储蓄为 $S_g=T-G$。商品市场出清给出：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simplified closed economy, ignoring depreciation and using consistent gross measures, let $Y$ be income, $C$ private consumption, $T$ net taxes and $G$ government purchases. Private and government saving are $S_p=Y-T-C$ and $S_g=T-G$. Goods-market clearing implies:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
I=S_p+S_g,
\qquad \Delta I=\Delta S_p+\Delta S_g.
$$

例如，起初 $S_p=20,S_g=0,I=20$。在 $Y,G$ 不变且没有其他变化的比较中，减税 $10$ 并举债融资，使 $S_g$ 降至 $-10$；若私人储蓄只增加 $4$，则 $I=24-10=14$，投资减少 $6$。若私人储蓄恰好增加 $10$，总储蓄不变，就没有这个挤出渠道；[[李嘉图等价]]给出完全抵消的一组充分条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Initially let $S_p=20,S_g=0,I=20$. Holding $Y,G$ and other factors fixed, a debt-financed tax cut of $10$ lowers government saving to $-10$. If private saving rises by only $4$, investment becomes $14$, a fall of $6$. A private saving increase of $10$ instead fully offsets public dissaving. [[李嘉图等价|Ricardian equivalence]] supplies sufficient conditions for that offset.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开放经济可从国外获得资金。进一步假定没有跨国净要素收入和转移、忽略资产估值变化时，同一简化模型为 $S_p+S_g=I+NFI$，其中 $NFI$ 是净对外投资。储蓄减少 $6$ 可以表现为国内投资不变、$NFI$ 减少 $6$；因此，不能把封闭经济的投资变化直接搬到开放经济。对外净资产较少也可能影响未来可支配收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An open economy can obtain foreign funding. With no net cross-border factor income or transfers and ignoring asset revaluations, the simplified model gives $S_p+S_g=I+NFI$, where $NFI$ is net foreign investment. Saving can fall by $6$ while domestic investment is unchanged and $NFI$ falls by $6$. The closed-economy investment result therefore does not transfer directly; lower net foreign assets can still affect future disposable income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述恒等式没有决定私人储蓄、产出或利率的行为反应。短期存在闲置产能、价格黏性或货币政策配合时，[[财政政策]]可能改变 $Y$；公共投资也可能改变私人投资回报。利率上升不是挤出的定义，[[利率与储蓄反应|储蓄随利率变化的方向]]也不能无条件预设。长期较少的私人资本降低未来产出，还需要相应生产技术与其他条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identities do not determine behavioural responses of saving, output or interest rates. With slack, sticky prices or monetary accommodation, [[财政政策|fiscal policy]] may change $Y$; public investment may alter private returns. A rate rise is not the definition of crowding out, and the [[利率与储蓄反应|saving response to interest rates]] is conditional. Lower future output from reduced capital also requires suitable production assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
