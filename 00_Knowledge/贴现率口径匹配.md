# 贴现率应取与现金流风险币种及索取权匹配的机会成本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The discount rate should be the opportunity cost matching the cash flow's risk, currency, and claim*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子原则
> 贴现率不是公式自动给出的数字，而是放弃最佳可比投资机会的成本。可比意味着现金流的风险、币种、流动性与索取权口径一致：股权现金流配股权成本，企业自由现金流配资本成本；不同币种用相应币种的回报率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> A discount rate is not generated by the formula; it is the cost of forgoing the best comparable investment. Comparability requires matching risk, currency, liquidity, and claim: equity cash flow with cost of equity, free cash flow to the firm with cost of capital, and each currency with a return estimated in that currency.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用企业统一 WACC 折现安全税盾、高风险项目和不同币种现金流，等于强行假设这些现金流具有同一可比机会。正确做法是先定义现金流口径，再选择同口径的必要回报率；风险越高不意味着任意加一个“保守溢价”，而是必须有与风险暴露相对应的定价依据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using one firm-wide WACC for safe tax shields, risky projects, and cash flows in different currencies assumes that all are comparable opportunities. Define the cash-flow claim first, then select a required return on the same basis. Higher risk does not license an arbitrary “conservative premium”; the adjustment must correspond to the priced exposure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若现金流取决于未来状态，不同状态中的一元钱价值可能不同；此时可以逐状态使用状态价格或随机贴现因子，而不能把所有状态粗暴压成一个任意平均贴现率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If cash flow depends on the future state, one unit paid in different states can have different values. State prices or a stochastic discount factor can then price each state rather than forcing every payoff through an arbitrary average discount rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么不能用 WACC 折现 FCFE，再把结果称为股权价值？
>
> **答案：** FCFE 只属于股东，应配股权资本成本；WACC 对应向债权人与股东共同提供的企业现金流。现金流索取权与贴现率口径不一致会改变估值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can you not discount FCFE at WACC and call the result equity value?
>
> **Answer:** FCFE belongs only to equity holders and should be paired with cost of equity. WACC corresponds to cash flow available to both debt and equity claimants. Mismatching claim and rate changes the valuation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
