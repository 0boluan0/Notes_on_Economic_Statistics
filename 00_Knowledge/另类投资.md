# 另类投资是相对传统公开股票债券和现金划分的非传统资产或投资策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Alternative investments are nontraditional assets or strategies classified relative to conventional public equities, bonds, and cash*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“另类”是一个分类用语，不是一种统一的现金流合约。它通常包括私募股权和私募债务、房地产和基础设施等实物资产，以及对冲基金等策略。边界随用途而异：一家对冲基金可以持有普通股票，之所以被归为另类，可能是因为卖空、杠杆或其他投资安排，而不是底层股票突然变成了另一种权利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Alternative is a classification, not one cash-flow contract. It commonly covers private equity and debt, real assets such as property and infrastructure, and strategies such as hedge funds. Boundaries depend on purpose. A hedge fund can hold ordinary shares while being classified as alternative because of its strategy or structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分析时同时看**底层资产**和**持有方式**。直接买一栋楼，主要面对租金、维护、空置和出售价格；买投资房地产的基金份额，还多了费用、治理和赎回条款。[[证券投资基金|基金包装]]并没有替投资者消除房产风险，上市交易也不会使底层资产本身变得随时可卖。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Examine both the underlying and the holding structure. Direct property ownership brings rent, maintenance, vacancy, and resale-price exposure. A property fund adds fees, governance, and redemption terms. [[证券投资基金|Fund packaging]] does not remove the underlying exposure or make the property itself instantly saleable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不能从“另类”三字推出高收益、低相关或保值。相关性必须对应具体资产、估值频率和时期；不常估值的资产即使账面价格很平稳，也可能难以按那个价格出售。能否分散现有组合的风险，要回到共同风险因子、可实现退出价格和资金锁定期限判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The label does not establish high returns, low correlation, or capital protection. Correlation requires a specified asset, valuation frequency, and period. Smooth infrequent appraisals need not be executable sale prices. Diversification depends on shared risk factors, realizable exit prices, and capital lockups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
