# 家庭可通过异地收入与可信的收入共享安排分担收入风险

迁移可以是家庭共同安排的一部分：有人外出工作，有人留在原地，双方在收入好时支持收入差的一方。它能提供多少保险，取决于两地收入是否同时受冲击、收入能否实际共享，以及迁移和转移资金的成本。**换一个工作地点本身并不等于分散风险。**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Migration can form part of a household arrangement in which some members leave, others remain, and each supports the other when earnings fall. Its insurance value depends on how incomes move together, whether sharing is feasible and credible, and the costs of moving and transferring resources. A different location alone does not establish diversification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个构造例子看清这条机制。两名成员各有年收入 $100+\varepsilon$，同地务农时受到同一个冲击 $\varepsilon$：以各半概率取 $-40$ 或 $40$。全家收入于是各以一半概率为120或280，均值为200。暂设收入全部合并、消费价格相同，迁移成本和汇款成本均为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider two members each earning $100+\varepsilon$ a year. When both farm locally, they share the same shock, equally likely to be $-40$ or $40$. Household income is therefore 120 or 280 with equal probability and mean 200. Assume full income pooling, identical consumption prices, and no moving or remittance costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一名成员外出后仍有相同的个人收入分布，但其冲击 $\eta$ 与原地的 $\varepsilon$ 独立，总收入变为 $200+\varepsilon+\eta$：120、200、280的概率分别为 $1/4,1/2,1/4$。均值未变，但极端结果各自发生的概率减半。按[[和的方差协方差项]]：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the migrant retains the same individual earnings distribution but faces a shock $\eta$ independent of $\varepsilon$, total income becomes $200+\varepsilon+\eta$. Outcomes 120, 200 and 280 have probabilities $1/4$, $1/2$ and $1/4$. The mean is unchanged, while each extreme outcome becomes half as likely. Using the [[和的方差协方差项|variance-of-a-sum identity]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}(200+2\varepsilon)=6400,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(200+\varepsilon+\eta)=3200.
$$

这个例子说明，即使外出者的预期收入没有提高，家庭仍可能看重收入分布的改善。它不证明任何两种分布只比较方差就能排出福利高低；有成本、背景收入或不同偏好时，需要重新评价，见[[风险的确定性等价]]。若两地收入受到完全相同的冲击，本例的保险收益就消失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus a household may value migration even without a higher expected income for the migrant. This does not make variance alone a universal welfare ranking. Costs, background income and preferences require an updated evaluation; see [[风险的确定性等价|certainty equivalents]]. If both locations face the same shock, this example's insurance gain disappears.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收入共享也需要具体安排。外出成功时的汇款可以支持留守者，求职困难时也可能由家里支持外出者；谁承担费用、谁收到多少，不能由“家庭总收入更稳定”直接推出。因此，这条机制解释的是**异地收入如何与相互支持结合**，不是每个成员都必然受益，也不单独解释是否会[[循环迁移|反复迁回原地]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sharing requires an arrangement: remittances may support those who remain, while the family may support the migrant during unsuccessful job search. More stable total income does not determine who pays or receives how much. The mechanism links geographically diversified earnings to mutual support; it guarantees neither a gain for every member nor [[循环迁移|repeated return movements]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Stark、Bloom（1985），[The New Economics of Labor Migration](https://newdoc.nccu.edu.tw/teasyllabus/1102265813001/5_NewEconomicsOfLaborMigratin.pdf)，*American Economic Review* 75(2)，印刷第174–175页（含JSTOR封面的PDF第3–4页）：支持迁移者与留守家庭的费用/收益共享、隐含契约和不完全同向收入冲击下的相互保险。
- 二状态冲击及方差数值为教学构造；使用有限二阶矩下的共享方差恒等式，不是该论文的参数估计。成本与分配条件用于界定机制，不从家庭总量改善推断每个成员的福利。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stark and Bloom support contractual sharing and coinsurance between migrants and nonmigrants. The two-state example and variance calculations are constructed, not estimates from the paper. The conditions separate an aggregate insurance mechanism from individual welfare claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
