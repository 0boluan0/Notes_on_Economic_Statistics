# Expectation of an indicator
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*指示变量的期望*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **indicator's expectation** is the probability of the event it records. The reason is simple: an event that happens contributes $1$, and an event that does not happen contributes $0$. Its probability-weighted average therefore measures how likely the event is to happen.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**指示变量的期望**就是它所记录事件的概率。原因很直接：事件发生时贡献 $1$，没有发生时贡献 $0$。把这两个记录值按概率加权平均，得到的就是事件发生的可能性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, roll a fair six-sided die and let $A$ mean “the result is even”. The [[事件指示变量|event indicator]] $I_A$ records $1$ for $2,4,6$ and $0$ for $1,3,5$. Each record has probability $1/2$, so its [[期望|expectation]], written $E[I_A]$, is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，掷一枚公平六面骰，令 $A$ 表示“点数是偶数”。[[事件指示变量]] $I_A$ 遇到 $2,4,6$ 时记录 $1$，遇到 $1,3,5$ 时记录 $0$。两个记录值的概率各是 $1/2$，因此它的[[期望]]，记为 $E[I_A]$，等于

$$E[I_A]=1\times\frac12+0\times\frac12=\frac12=P(A).$$

> [!warning] An average is not an extra possible value · 平均值不是额外的取值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The die's indicator can only be $0$ or $1$. The value $1/2$ describes its expectation; no single roll produces the record $1/2$. Likewise, an event with probability $0.3$ has an indicator with expectation $0.3$, while the indicator still records only zero or one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 骰子的指示变量只能取 $0$ 或 $1$。$1/2$ 描述的是期望，某一次投掷不会产生 $1/2$ 这个记录。同样，概率为 $0.3$ 的事件，其指示变量的期望为 $0.3$，但记录值仍然只有零和一。

## The calculation for any event
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*任意事件都适用的计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be an event in a probability model, and write $p=P(A)$ for its probability. By definition, the event $I_A=1$ is exactly $A$, while $I_A=0$ means that $A$ did not occur. Thus $I_A$ has the [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli distribution]] with success probability $p$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 是概率模型中的一个事件，用 $p=P(A)$ 表示它的概率。按定义，$I_A=1$ 恰好就是事件 $A$，而 $I_A=0$ 表示 $A$ 没有发生。所以 $I_A$ 服从成功概率为 $p$ 的 [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli 分布]]：

$$P(I_A=1)=p,\qquad P(I_A=0)=1-p.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiply each possible value by its probability and add:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将每个可能取值乘以对应概率，再相加：

$$\boxed{E[I_A]=1\cdot p+0\cdot(1-p)=p=P(A).}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This finite expectation always exists because the indicator is bounded between zero and one. The calculation also works at $p=0$ and $p=1$. It needs no independence assumption and no repeated experiment: it concerns one event and its indicator in the given model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于指示变量始终介于零和一之间，这个有限期望总是存在。$p=0$ 和 $p=1$ 时也适用。计算不要求独立性，也不要求真的重复试验：它只涉及给定模型中的一个事件及其指示变量。

## Why the identity is useful
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么值得把概率改写成期望*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity is a bridge between events and numerical averages. Once a count has been expressed as a sum of indicators, we can replace each indicator's expectation with the corresponding event probability. [[期望线性性|Linearity of expectation]] then adds these probabilities. The separate [[随机计数的指标和|indicator-sum method]] explains how to make sure that the sum really counts the intended objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条恒等式把事件与数值平均连接起来。当一个计数被写成指示变量之和以后，每一项的期望就可以换成对应事件的概率，再由[[期望线性性]]把这些概率相加。[[随机计数的指标和|指示变量计数法]]进一步说明，怎样确认这个和确实数到了目标对象。

> [!note]- The average of repeated records is a further result · 重复记录的平均涉及另一个结论
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the experiment is repeated so that the indicators are [[独立同分布|independent and identically distributed]], their sample average is the fraction of trials in which $A$ occurs. The [[弱大数定律|weak law of large numbers]] says that the probability of this fraction differing from $p$ by more than any fixed positive tolerance tends to zero as the number of trials grows. That is an additional statement about repeated sampling; $E[I_A]=p$ already holds for one indicator.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果重复试验产生的指示变量[[独立同分布]]，它们的样本平均就是事件 $A$ 发生的试验比例。[[弱大数定律]]说明：随着试验次数增加，这个比例偏离 $p$ 超过任意固定正误差的概率趋于零。这是关于重复抽样的额外结论；单个指示变量已经满足 $E[I_A]=p$。
