# 证券投资基金是将投资者资金汇集为独立资产池并按基金份额分享投资结果的集合投资安排
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A securities investment fund pools investors' money into a separate asset pool and allocates investment results through fund units*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证券投资基金通过发行份额汇集资金，由管理人依基金目标进行证券投资，持有人取得对基金投资结果的权利。基金财产与管理人、托管人的自有财产区分；买基金份额不是买管理公司的股票，也不是直接向管理人提供一笔承诺还本的贷款。具体权利由组织形式、适用法律与基金文件确定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A securities investment fund issues units to pool capital, which a manager invests in securities under the fund's mandate. Investors acquire rights to the fund's investment results. Fund property is distinct from the manager's and custodian's own property. Buying fund units is neither buying shares in the management company nor lending it money with a repayment promise. Legal form, governing law, and fund documents determine the specific rights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若某基金只有一种同权份额、共 1 万份，持有 100 份就是持有该份额权益的 1%。这不是可以单独取走每只底层股票 1% 的权利；分配、转让及赎回须服从基金安排。多类别份额可能有不同费用或分配条件，不能跨类别只按数量比较权益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With 10,000 equally entitled units in a single class, 100 units represent 1% of that class's fund interest. This does not authorize the holder to withdraw 1% of each underlying stock individually: distributions, transfers, and redemptions follow fund arrangements. Different classes may have different fees or distribution terms, so unit counts alone do not establish equal rights across classes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产隔离和托管保护的是财产归属与管理秩序，不保证投资本金或收益。资产池可以分散，也可以集中；实际风险仍取决于底层持仓、杠杆和交易条款，见[[分散化与共同风险]]。管理费等费用依约计提，基金亏损时也可能照收；并不存在管理费必与规模反比、与风险正比的通用公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Segregation and custody protect property rights and administration, not principal or returns. A pool can be diversified or concentrated; its risk still depends on holdings, leverage, and contractual terms, as explained in [[分散化与共同风险|diversification and common risk]]. Contractual fees may accrue even when the fund loses money. There is no universal formula making management fees inversely proportional to size and directly proportional to risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
