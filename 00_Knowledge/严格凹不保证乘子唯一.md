# 严格凹可以使最优选择唯一，却不能保证约束乘子唯一；乘子唯一性取决于活动约束法向量的识别
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strict concavity can make the primal optimizer unique but cannot guarantee unique constraint multipliers, whose identification depends on active constraint normals*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 选择唯一与法向量系数唯一是两件事
> 严格凹控制的是不同可行点能否同时最大化目标，因此最多确定一个最优选择 $x^*$。KKT 乘子则是驻点方程中活动约束法向量的系数；若这些法向量相关，同一个 $\nabla f(x^*)$ 可以有多组系数表示。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Strict concavity prevents two distinct feasible choices from both maximizing the objective. KKT multipliers are instead coefficients representing the objective gradient by active constraint normals. Dependent normals can admit several coefficient representations at the same unique optimizer.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反例：唯一选择，多组乘子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Counterexample: one choice, many multipliers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑
$$
\max_x -(x-1)^2
\quad\text{s.t.}\quad
x\le0,
\qquad
2x\le0.
$$
目标严格凹，可行域为 $(-\infty,0]$，所以唯一最优点是 $x^*=0$。取
$$
\mathcal L=-(x-1)^2-\lambda_1x-\lambda_2(2x).
$$
驻点条件为
$$
2-\lambda_1-2\lambda_2=0.
$$
它允许 $\lambda_1,\lambda_2\ge0$ 的多组解，例如 $(2,0)$ 与 $(0,1)$。两条重复活动约束的梯度相关，正是乘子不唯一的来源。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maximising $-(x-1)^2$ subject to the duplicate constraints $x\le0$ and $2x\le0$ has the unique optimizer zero by strict concavity. Stationarity is $2-\lambda_1-2\lambda_2=0$, admitting several nonnegative multiplier pairs such as $(2,0)$ and $(0,1)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样保证乘子唯一
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How multiplier uniqueness is obtained*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若活动不等式梯度与等式梯度满足 LICQ，则 [[LICQ保证乘子唯一]]。这项结论来自法向量线性无关，与目标严格凹承担的选择唯一性不同。还要另外记住：严格凹连最优选择的存在都不负责，见 [[严格凹不保证解存在]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
LICQ makes multipliers unique by making active and equality normals linearly independent. That is separate from strict concavity's control of the primal choice. Strict concavity does not even establish existence of that choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 已知凸问题有唯一最优选择，为什么仍不能直接把每条约束的影子价格当成唯一数值？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a convex problem has a unique optimizer, why can the shadow price of each constraint still fail to be unique?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 唯一选择不保证活动法向量线性无关；重复或相关约束仍可让驻点方程有多组乘子。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** A unique optimizer does not make active normals linearly independent. Duplicated or dependent constraints can still admit several multiplier vectors.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
