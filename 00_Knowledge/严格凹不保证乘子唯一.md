# Primal and multiplier uniqueness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*最优选择与乘子唯一性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An optimization problem may identify exactly one best choice and still leave several ways to assign its multipliers. The choice $x^*$ answers “where should we act?” The multiplier vector answers a different algebraic question: “which coefficients balance the objective gradient with the listed constraint gradients at that point?” Repeated constraints can give different coefficient lists for the same balance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

优化问题可以准确确定一个最佳选择，却仍有多组乘子。选择 $x^*$ 回答“应该选哪里”，乘子向量则回答另一个代数问题：“在这个点，用哪些系数把目标梯度表示成所列约束梯度的组合？”重复约束可以让同一平衡有不同系数列表。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First keep the scope of [[凹函数|strict concavity]] precise. Let $C$ be a nonempty [[凸集|convex feasible set]], and let the real objective $f$ be strictly concave on $C$. If two distinct points $x,y$ both attained the maximum value $v$, their feasible midpoint would satisfy $f((x+y)/2)>(f(x)+f(y))/2=v$, contradicting maximality. Thus there is **at most one attained maximizer**. Convexity keeps the midpoint feasible; existence still needs a separate argument, as in [[凸优化全局性|convex optimization]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先限定[[凹函数|严格凹性]]的作用范围：设 $C$ 是非空[[凸集|凸可行集]]，实值目标 $f$ 在 $C$ 上严格凹。若两个不同点 $x,y$ 都取得最大值 $v$，其可行中点会满足 $f((x+y)/2)>(f(x)+f(y))/2=v$，与最大性矛盾。因此，**取得最大值的点至多一个**。凸性保证中点可行；是否取得仍需另证，见[[凸优化全局性]]中的条件说明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For differentiable constrained problems, [[KKT条件|KKT stationarity]] writes the objective gradient as a combination of equality and active inequality gradients. The following distinction uses the convex feasible-set scope just established; it concerns multipliers at one fixed point for the same listed constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对可微约束问题，[[KKT条件|KKT驻点式]]把目标梯度写成等式与活动不等式梯度的组合。下面的区别沿用刚才的凸可行集范围，比较的是同一个点、同一组所列约束的乘子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strict concavity prevents two distinct feasible choices from both maximizing the objective. KKT multipliers are instead coefficients representing the objective gradient by active constraint normals. Dependent normals can admit several coefficient representations at the same unique optimizer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

严格凹控制的是不同可行点能否同时最大化目标，因此最多确定一个最优选择 $x^*$。KKT 乘子则是驻点方程中活动约束法向量的系数；若这些法向量相关，同一个 $\nabla f(x^*)$ 可以有多组系数表示。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One optimum and a whole interval of multiplier pairs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反例：唯一选择，多组乘子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maximising $-(x-1)^2$ subject to the duplicate constraints $x\le0$ and $2x\le0$ has the unique optimizer zero by strict concavity. Stationarity is $2-\lambda_1-2\lambda_2=0$, admitting several nonnegative multiplier pairs such as $(2,0)$ and $(0,1)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑
$$
\max_x -(x-1)^2
\quad\text{s.t.}\quad
x\le0,
\qquad
2x\le0.
$$
目标严格凹，可行域为 $(-\infty,0]$，所以唯一最优点是 $x^*=0$。取
$$
\mathcal L=-(x-1)^2-\lambda_1x-\lambda_2(2x).
$$
驻点条件为
$$
2-\lambda_1-2\lambda_2=0.
$$
它允许 $\lambda_1,\lambda_2\ge0$ 的多组解，例如 $(2,0)$ 与 $(0,1)$。两条重复活动约束的梯度相关，正是乘子不唯一的来源。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The strict concavity assertion can be checked without relying on a picture. For $f(x)=-(x-1)^2$, distinct real $x,y$ and $0<t<1$, expanding gives $f(tx+(1-t)y)-tf(x)-(1-t)f(y)=t(1-t)(x-y)^2>0$. The two constraints describe the same half-line $C=(-\infty,0]$. For $x<0$, $(x-1)^2>1$, so $f(x)<-1=f(0)$; this establishes attainment and uniqueness directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

严格凹性可以直接展开核对，无需依赖图形。对 $f(x)=-(x-1)^2$、不同实数 $x,y$ 与 $0<t<1$，有 $f(tx+(1-t)y)-tf(x)-(1-t)f(y)=t(1-t)(x-y)^2>0$。两条约束描述同一个半直线 $C=(-\infty,0]$。当 $x<0$ 时，$(x-1)^2>1$，所以 $f(x)<-1=f(0)$，直接证明了取得性和唯一性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At zero both constraints bind, and their gradients are $1$ and $2$. The objective derivative is $f^{\prime}(0)=2$. All nonnegative pairs solving stationarity are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零点两条约束都绑定，梯度分别是 $1$ 和 $2$，目标导数为 $f^{\prime}(0)=2$。驻点式的全部非负解为

$$
(\lambda_1,\lambda_2)=(2-2t,t),\qquad 0\le t\le1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indeed, set $t=\lambda_2$. Nonnegativity gives $t\ge0$ and $\lambda_1=2-2t\ge0$, hence $t\le1$; every such $t$ also satisfies the equation. The primal point is feasible, both multipliers are nonnegative, and every product $\lambda_i g_i(0)$ is zero. Therefore all these pairs satisfy the complete KKT system, not just stationarity. The relation $2\cdot1-1\cdot2=0$ permits the coefficients to change without changing the gradient balance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $t=\lambda_2$，非负性给出 $t\ge0$，而 $\lambda_1=2-2t\ge0$ 又给出 $t\le1$；反过来，每个这样的 $t$ 都满足方程。原选择可行，两个乘子均非负，所有乘积 $\lambda_i g_i(0)$ 都为零。因此这些系数对满足完整 KKT 系统，不仅仅满足驻点式。关系 $2\cdot1-1\cdot2=0$ 使系数可以改变，而梯度平衡保持不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Which additional information identifies the coefficients?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样保证乘子唯一

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[LICQ条件|LICQ]] requires all equality gradients and all active inequality gradients to be linearly independent as an indexed family. If two KKT multiplier vectors already exist at the same point, inactive multipliers are zero by complementarity. Subtracting their stationarity equations leaves a zero linear combination of that family. Independence forces every coefficient difference to vanish: this is [[LICQ保证乘子唯一|the multiplier-uniqueness theorem]]. It is an at-most-one result with existence supplied separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[LICQ条件|LICQ]]要求全部等式梯度和全部活动不等式梯度组成的带索引族线性无关。若同一点已有两组 KKT 乘子，非活动乘子由互补性均为零；两组驻点式相减后，便得到这个梯度族的零线性组合。线性无关迫使每个系数差为零，这就是[[LICQ保证乘子唯一]]。它是“至多一组”的结论，存在性须另有依据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
LICQ makes multipliers unique by making active and equality normals linearly independent. That is separate from strict concavity's control of the primal choice. Strict concavity does not even establish existence of that choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若活动不等式梯度与等式梯度满足 LICQ，则 [[LICQ保证乘子唯一]]。这项结论来自法向量线性无关，与目标严格凹承担的选择唯一性不同。还要另外记住：严格凹连最优选择的存在都不负责，见 [[严格凹不保证解存在]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dependence permits nonuniqueness but does not force it, because inequality multipliers must also obey their signs. With the same $x\le0,2x\le0$ constraints and objective $-x^2$, the unique optimum is still zero but stationarity becomes $\lambda_1+2\lambda_2=0$. The only nonnegative pair is $(0,0)$, as checked in [[LICQ不是KKT必要条件|LICQ failure with valid multipliers]]. This contrast keeps a sufficient uniqueness condition from becoming an incorrect equivalence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

梯度相关允许乘子不唯一，却不强迫它不唯一，因为不等式乘子还要满足符号条件。同样取 $x\le0,2x\le0$，若目标改成 $-x^2$，唯一最大点仍是零，驻点式却变为 $\lambda_1+2\lambda_2=0$，唯一非负解是 $(0,0)$，详见[[LICQ不是KKT必要条件|LICQ失败但乘子存在的例子]]。这个对比避免把充分唯一性条件误读为等价条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even a unique multiplier becomes a [[影子价格|shadow-price derivative]] only under suitable sensitivity assumptions. In a redundant description, assigning a separate marginal value to every listed constraint is especially delicate: independently perturbing different copies changes different problems. The counterexample establishes nonuniqueness of the KKT coefficients themselves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使乘子唯一，把它解释成[[影子价格|影子价格导数]]也需要适当的敏感度前提。在冗余约束描述中，给每条所列约束分别赋边际价值尤其要谨慎：独立扰动不同副本，改变的是不同问题。本例直接证明的是 KKT 系数本身不唯一。

> [!question]- Check the implication · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a convex problem has a unique optimizer, why can the shadow price of each constraint still fail to be unique?
>
> **Answer:** A unique optimizer does not make active normals linearly independent. Duplicated or dependent constraints can still admit several multiplier vectors.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 已知凸问题有唯一最优选择，为什么仍不能直接把每条约束的影子价格当成唯一数值？
>
>
>
> **答案：** 唯一选择不保证活动法向量线性无关；重复或相关约束仍可让驻点方程有多组乘子。
