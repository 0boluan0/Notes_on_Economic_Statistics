# When do matrix powers tend to zero?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*矩阵幂趋零判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated multiplication can erase every initial vector, preserve some directions, or amplify them. The question here is when the matrices $A,A^2,A^3,\ldots$ themselves approach the zero matrix. Then every fixed initial vector is eventually sent arbitrarily close to zero. The answer depends on the magnitudes of all complex eigenvalues, even when there are too few eigenvectors to diagonalize $A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反复相乘可能逐渐消去所有初值，也可能保留或放大某些方向。本卡问的是矩阵 $A,A^2,A^3,\ldots$ 本身何时趋近零矩阵；这样每个固定初始向量最终都会被送得任意接近零。答案取决于全部复特征值的模，即使特征向量不够、不能对角化也适用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A small radius can coexist with an early increase
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*谱半径小，初期仍可能先增长*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $A=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&3\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix}$. Its two eigenvalues are both $1/2$, but it has only one independent eigenvector direction. Direct [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] gives, for integers $k\ge1$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $A=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&3\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix}$，两个特征值都是 $1/2$，却只有一个独立特征方向。直接按[[矩阵乘法]]计算，对整数 $k\ge1$ 有

$$A^k=\begin{pmatrix}2^{-k}&3k\,2^{-(k-1)}\\0&2^{-k}\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The upper-right entries at $k=1,2,3$ are $3,3,9/4$. Eventually both $2^{-k}$ and $k2^{-k}$ tend to zero, so every entry disappears. Yet the initial unit vector $(0,1)^T$ first becomes $(3,1/2)^T$, whose length is $\sqrt{37}/2>3$; $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. The theorem will guarantee eventual disappearance, not monotone shrinkage at every step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$k=1,2,3$ 时，右上角依次为 $3,3,9/4$。最终 $2^{-k}$ 与 $k2^{-k}$ 都趋于零，所以每个元素都消失；但初始单位向量 $(0,1)^T$ 第一步变成 $(3,1/2)^T$，长度为 $\sqrt{37}/2>3$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。定理保证的是最终消失，不是每步单调缩小。

> [!important] The criterion includes defective matrices / 不可对角化也适用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A$ be a fixed $n\times n$ real or complex matrix, with positive integer $n$. Its [[谱半径|spectral radius]] $\rho(A)$ is the largest modulus of its complex eigenvalues. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $A$ 为固定的 $n\times n$ 实矩阵或复矩阵，$n$ 为正整数。[[谱半径]] $\rho(A)$ 是其全部复特征值模的最大值，则
>
> $$\boxed{A^k\longrightarrow0\ (k\to\infty)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\rho(A)<1.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The index $k$ runs through nonnegative integers and $A^0=I_n$, the [[单位矩阵|identity]]. Convergence means every entry tends to zero. Equivalently, $\|A^k\|\to0$ in any fixed norm on the finite-dimensional [[矩阵空间|matrix space]]: all such norms give the same convergence notion. One concrete choice is the [[Frobenius范数|Frobenius norm]], the square root of the sum of squared absolute entries. The matrix size and the matrix itself stay fixed as $k$ increases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$k$ 取非负整数，$A^0=I_n$ 为[[单位矩阵]]。收敛表示每个元素都趋于零；等价地，在有限维[[矩阵空间]]上的任一固定范数下都有 $\|A^k\|\to0$，因为这些范数给出同一种收敛概念。一个具体选择是[[Frobenius范数]]：所有元素的绝对值平方求和，再开平方。这里随着 $k$ 增加，矩阵尺寸和矩阵本身都不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why no eigenvalue may reach the unit circle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么任何特征值都不能碰到单位圆*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an [[特征对|eigenpair]] $Av=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$, repeated multiplication gives $A^kv=\lambda^kv$. If $A^k\to0$, its product with the fixed vector $v$ tends to zero. Taking vector lengths gives $\|\lambda^kv\|=|\lambda|^k\|v\|$. Since $\|v\|>0$, this tends to zero only when $|\lambda|<1$. The argument applies to every eigenvalue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[特征对]] $Av=\lambda v$、$v\ne0$，反复相乘得到 $A^kv=\lambda^kv$。若 $A^k\to0$，它乘以固定向量 $v$ 也趋于零。取向量长度，有 $\|\lambda^kv\|=|\lambda|^k\|v\|$；由于 $\|v\|>0$，只有 $|\lambda|<1$ 才能趋于零。这个论证适用于每个特征值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real matrix with a nonreal eigenvalue, apply the same real matrix to complex vectors. If its entries tend to zero, the complex products do too, so no eigenvalue is omitted. The unit circle is the set of complex numbers with modulus one; the condition requires every eigenvalue strictly inside it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实矩阵若有非实特征值，就把同一个实矩阵作用在复向量上。元素趋零仍保证复向量乘积趋零，因此不能漏掉非实特征值。单位圆是模等于1的复数集合；条件要求每个特征值都严格在圆内。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why missing eigenvectors do not prevent decay
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*缺少特征向量为什么仍不妨碍衰减*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A$ is diagonalizable, [[对角化计算矩阵幂|the power formula]] reduces the question to $\lambda_i^k\to0$. For a general real or complex matrix, work over $\mathbb C$, where its characteristic polynomial splits into linear factors. The [[Jordan标准形的域条件|Jordan-form existence condition]] then gives $A=SJS^{-1}$ with invertible $S$. The matrix $J$ consists of finitely many Jordan blocks, so it is enough to show that each block's powers tend to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可对角化时，[[对角化计算矩阵幂|矩阵幂公式]]把问题化为 $\lambda_i^k\to0$。一般的实矩阵或复矩阵则放到复数域上：特征多项式在那里分解成一次因子，满足[[Jordan标准形的域条件]]，所以有 $A=SJS^{-1}$，$S$ 可逆。$J$ 由有限多个Jordan块组成，只需证明每个块的幂都趋零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a positive integer $r$, write a size-$r$ block as $J_r(\lambda)=\lambda I_r+N$, where $N$ shifts along the chain and $N^r=0$. For $k\ge r-1$, its [[Jordan块的幂|finite power formula]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $r$ 为正整数，把大小为 $r$ 的块写成 $J_r(\lambda)=\lambda I_r+N$，其中 $N$ 沿链移位且 $N^r=0$。当 $k\ge r-1$ 时，[[Jordan块的幂|有限幂公式]]为

$$J_r(\lambda)^k=\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}\binom{k}{j}\lambda^{k-j}N^j.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\binom{k}{j}$ is a [[组合数|binomial coefficient]], a polynomial in $k$ of fixed degree $j$. If $0<|\lambda|<1$, each coefficient is bounded by a constant times $k^j|\lambda|^k$, which tends to zero because [[幂函数慢于指数函数|exponential decay dominates any fixed polynomial]]. If $\lambda=0$, treat it separately: the block is $N$, and $N^k=0$ for all $k\ge r$. Thus every block vanishes. Finally $A^k=SJ^kS^{-1}\to0$, since multiplying by fixed matrices only forms finite linear combinations of entries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\binom{k}{j}$ 是[[组合数]]，也是关于 $k$ 的固定 $j$ 次多项式。若 $0<|\lambda|<1$，每个系数的模都被常数乘 $k^j|\lambda|^k$ 控制；由[[幂函数慢于指数函数|固定多项式最终被指数衰减压过]]，它趋于零。若 $\lambda=0$，单独处理：块就是 $N$，当 $k\ge r$ 时 $N^k=0$。因此所有块都消失；再由 $A^k=SJ^kS^{-1}$，固定矩阵相乘只形成元素的有限线性组合，也得到 $A^k\to0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exponent-zero scalar factors in the finite formula are empty products equal to $1$; handling $\lambda=0$ via nilpotence avoids any negative-power expression. In particular, even a defective matrix whose eigenvalues all have modulus $0.99$ has vanishing powers: each block contributes only a fixed-degree polynomial factor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限公式中的标量零次幂按空乘积取1；$\lambda=0$ 时直接使用幂零性，不会引入负次幂表达。特别地，即使矩阵不可对角化、全部特征值模为 $0.99$，其幂仍趋零，因为每个块只多出固定次数的多项式因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the quantifiers and the strict inequality
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*看清“每个初值”和严格不等号*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The condition $\rho(A)\le1$ is insufficient: $[1]^k=[1]$, while $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}^k=\begin{pmatrix}1&k\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ is unbounded. A rotation may remain bounded without converging to zero. These are different failures; the theorem asks specifically for a zero limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\rho(A)\le1$ 不够：$[1]^k=[1]$；而 $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}^k=\begin{pmatrix}1&k\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ 甚至无界。旋转也可能有界却不趋零。这是不同的失败方式，本定理专门判断零极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Also, $A^kx\to0$ for one selected vector $x$ is insufficient. For $A=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,2)$, the vector $(1,0)^T$ decays, but $(0,1)^T$ grows. Requiring decay for every fixed initial vector is equivalent to entrywise matrix decay: test the finitely many coordinate basis vectors to recover the columns of $A^k$. The corresponding dynamical interpretation is [[离散系统谱稳定性|discrete-system spectral stability]]. Early growth, as in the opening example, remains a separate [[非正规矩阵瞬态|transient question]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另外，只检查一个选定向量 $x$ 满足 $A^kx\to0$ 也不够。例如 $A=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,2)$ 时，$(1,0)^T$ 衰减，$(0,1)^T$ 却增长。要求每个固定初值都趋零，才等价于矩阵逐元素趋零：代入有限个坐标基向量，就得到 $A^k$ 的全部列。动力系统中的相应解释见[[离散系统谱稳定性]]；开头那样的初期增长仍属于另一个[[非正规矩阵瞬态|瞬态问题]]。
