# Negative-definite matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*负定矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **negative-definite matrix** gives a strictly negative quadratic value for every nonzero input vector. Imagine choosing a displacement with several coordinates: every nonzero choice must make the quadratic expression negative, including choices that change several coordinates together. The word “negative” describes that whole scalar expression.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**负定矩阵**使每个非零输入向量对应的二次型值都严格为负。可以把输入看成有多个坐标的位移：每一种非零位移都必须使二次表达式为负，包括几个坐标同时变化的情况。“负”描述的是整个表达式最后得到的那个数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with $A=-I_2$, where the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I_2$ has diagonal entries $1,1$ and zero off-diagonal entries. For a real column $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$, with $T$ denoting [[矩阵转置|transpose]], the [[二次型|quadratic form]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看 $A=-I_2$；[[单位矩阵]] $I_2$ 的两个对角元是1，非对角元是0。对实列向量 $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，对应的[[二次型]]为

$$x^T(-I_2)x=-x_1^2-x_2^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $x=(1,2)^T$ gives $-5$. More importantly, if $x\ne0$, at least one coordinate is nonzero, so at least one square is positive and their negative sum is strictly negative. This proves the condition for all nonzero inputs. Some coordinates may be zero; only the vector with every coordinate zero is excluded.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $x=(1,2)^T$ 给出 $-5$。更重要的是，只要 $x\ne0$，至少一个坐标就非零，因此至少一个平方项为正，它们的负和就严格为负。这证明了所有非零输入都满足条件。有些坐标可以为零；排除的只是所有坐标都为零的向量。

## The definition includes symmetry
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*定义包含对称性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n\ge1$ be a finite integer. This card uses the convention that a real negative-definite matrix is [[实对称矩阵|symmetric]]: $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ and $A=A^T$. Its defining sign condition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n\ge1$ 为有限整数。本卡采用实负定矩阵必须[[实对称矩阵|对称]]的约定：$A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ 且 $A=A^T$。它的符号条件为

$$\boxed{A\prec0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A=A^T\ \text{and}\ x^TAx<0\ \text{for every }x\in\mathbb R^n\setminus\{0\}.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $A\prec0$ abbreviates the matrix condition. At $x=0$ the quadratic value is always zero, so strict negativity cannot be required there. For a one-entry matrix $[a]$, the condition reduces to $a<0$. Multiplying a nonzero real vector by a nonzero real number $c$ gives $(cx)^TA(cx)=c^2x^TAx$: reversing or rescaling a direction does not change its sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记号 $A\prec0$ 是上述矩阵条件的简写。$x=0$ 时二次型必为零，不能在那里要求严格为负。对只有一个元素的矩阵 $[a]$，条件就是 $a<0$。若把非零实向量乘以非零实数 $c$，则 $(cx)^TA(cx)=c^2x^TAx$；反向或改变同一方向的尺度，都不会改变符号。

> [!example] Matrix entries need not all be negative · 不要求所有元素都负
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}-2&1\\1&-2\end{pmatrix}$ has positive off-diagonal entries, yet
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 矩阵 $A=\begin{pmatrix}-2&1\\1&-2\end{pmatrix}$ 的非对角元为正，但
>
> $$x^TAx=-2x_1^2+2x_1x_2-2x_2^2=-x_1^2-x_2^2-(x_1-x_2)^2<0\quad(x\ne0).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first two negative squares already force a strict inequality. Conversely, every entry of $B=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-2\\-2&-1\end{pmatrix}$ is negative, but the column $v=(1,-1)^T$ gives $v^TBv=2>0$. Thus even making every entry negative does not ensure negative definiteness.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 前两个负平方项就已保证严格小于零。反过来，$B=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-2\\-2&-1\end{pmatrix}$ 的每个元素都负，却对列向量 $v=(1,-1)^T$ 给出 $v^TBv=2>0$。可见即使所有元素都负，也不能保证负定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For that input, the calculation is $v^TBv=-1+4-1=2$. Testing just the coordinate axes would miss this positive value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对这个输入，计算就是 $v^TBv=-1+4-1=2$。只检查两个坐标轴会漏掉这个正值。

## Negation connects the positive and negative definitions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*取负号连接正定与负定*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Negating $A$ reverses every quadratic value: $x^T(-A)x=-x^TAx$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $A$ 取负，会使每个二次型值变号：$x^T(-A)x=-x^TAx$。因此

$$A\prec0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad -A\succ0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $-A\succ0$ means [[正定矩阵|positive definiteness]]. This allows positive-definite results to be applied to $-A$ with the signs translated carefully. Every negative-definite matrix is also [[半负定矩阵|negative semidefinite]], because strict negativity away from zero implies nonpositivity everywhere. The converse fails: $\operatorname{diag}(-1,0)$, a [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]], gives zero at the nonzero vector $(0,1)^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $-A\succ0$ 表示[[正定矩阵|正定]]。这样就可以把正定结论用于 $-A$，再认真转换符号。每个负定矩阵也都[[半负定矩阵|半负定]]，因为非零处严格为负，蕴含所有向量处非正。反向不成立：[[对角矩阵]] $\operatorname{diag}(-1,0)$ 在非零向量 $(0,1)^T$ 上给出零值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symmetry convention matters. A nonsymmetric real matrix may still give a strictly negative quadratic value for every nonzero real vector, but we then describe its [[二次型对称化|symmetric part]], $(A+A^T)/2$, as negative definite rather than assigning that name to the original matrix. For the pure function $q(x)=x^TAx$ with $A\prec0$, the origin is its unique global maximum, since $q(0)=0$ and $q(x)<0$ everywhere else. A general nonlinear function needs the separate assumptions in the [[Hessian 局部极大判据|Hessian local-maximum test]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对称性约定不可省略。非对称实矩阵也可能对每个非零实向量给出严格负的二次型值，但此时应说它的[[二次型对称化|对称部分]] $(A+A^T)/2$ 负定，而不是直接把这个名称用于原矩阵。对纯二次函数 $q(x)=x^TAx$，若 $A\prec0$，原点就是唯一的全局最大点，因为 $q(0)=0$，其余各点均有 $q(x)<0$。一般非线性函数还需要[[Hessian 局部极大判据]]中的独立条件。

## The complex version uses a real-valued Hermitian form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*复数版本使用实值 Hermitian 二次型*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a complex matrix, replace real symmetry by [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian symmetry]] $A=A^*$ and test $z^*Az<0$ for every nonzero $z\in\mathbb C^n$. The star is [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]: transpose and conjugate each entry, replacing $a+bi$ by $a-bi$, where $i^2=-1$. Hermitian symmetry makes $z^*Az$ real, so comparison with zero is meaningful. The equivalence $A\prec0\iff -A\succ0$ still holds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对复矩阵，用 [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian 对称性]] $A=A^*$ 代替实对称性，并要求每个非零 $z\in\mathbb C^n$ 都满足 $z^*Az<0$。星号表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]：转置并把每个元素取共轭，即把 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$，其中 $i^2=-1$。Hermitian 对称性使 $z^*Az$ 为实数，因此能够比较它与零的大小；$A\prec0\iff -A\succ0$ 仍成立。

$$z^*(-I_n)z=-\sum_{j=1}^n|z_j|^2<0\quad(z\ne0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $|a+bi|^2=a^2+b^2$. Ordinary transpose is unsuitable for this sign test: for the scalar matrix $[-1]$ and $z=i$, it gives $z^T[-1]z=1$, whereas conjugate transpose gives $z^*[-1]z=-1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $|a+bi|^2=a^2+b^2$。这个符号判别不能用普通转置代替：对一阶矩阵 $[-1]$ 与 $z=i$，普通转置给出 $z^T[-1]z=1$，共轭转置却给出正确的 $z^*[-1]z=-1$。
