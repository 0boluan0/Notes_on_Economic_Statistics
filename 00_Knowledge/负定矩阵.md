# 实对称矩阵负定是指其二次型在每个非零向量上都严格为负

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A real symmetric matrix is negative definite when its quadratic form is strictly negative on every nonzero vector*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实对称矩阵 $A$，负定记作 $A\prec0$，定义为 $x^TAx<0$ 对每个 $x\ne0$ 成立。它等价于 $-A$ [[正定矩阵|正定]]，因此可把正定工具用于 $-A$。

$A=-I$ 给出 $x^TAx=-\|x\|^2<0$。负定不是每个元素都负：对角矩阵 $-I$ 的非对角元为零。负定必[[半负定矩阵|半负定]]；反过来不成立，因为半负定允许非零零方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real symmetric matrix, negative definiteness means strict negativity of the quadratic form on every nonzero vector. Equivalently, its negative is [[正定矩阵|positive definite]]. The example $-I$ shows that entrywise negativity is unnecessary. Negative definiteness implies [[半负定矩阵|negative semidefiniteness]], but the converse fails when a nonzero zero direction exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
