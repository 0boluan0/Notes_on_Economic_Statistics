# 避免的外部损害超过行动者损失时存在可使双方受益的减排行为补偿区间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When avoided external damage exceeds the actor's forgone benefit, a range of compensation payments can make both parties better off*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定无协议时谁有权做什么，再计算**这一个行为变化**对各方的影响。假设工厂有权按原水平生产；渔民希望它减产。若减产使工厂净收益减少 $L$、使渔民避免损害 $A$，且双方能自由作货币转移、估值不随转移改变、没有谈判执行成本，那么支付额 $T$ 须满足
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify the entitlement and behaviour without agreement before valuing the proposed change. Suppose the factory may maintain its original output. A reduction costs it net benefit $L$ and avoids damage $A$ to fishers. With feasible transfers, transfer-independent valuations and no negotiation or enforcement costs, a payment $T$ must satisfy
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$L\le T\le A.$$

当 $A>L$，取严格位于区间内的金额可让双方都严格受益；端点让一方刚好不受损。区间说明交易可以创造多少共同收益，不唯一确定谁拿多少，也不保证协议一定谈成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A>L$, an interior payment makes both parties strictly better off; an endpoint leaves one indifferent. The interval identifies available gains, not their unique division or guaranteed agreement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $B(q)=100q-q^2/2$、$D(q)=q^2/2$ 比较100与50：工厂少得1250，渔民少损失3750。因此 $1250\le T\le3750$。若支付2000，工厂比无协议多得750，渔民净改善1750；共同增益2500不因如何分账而变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $B(q)=100q-q^2/2$ and $D(q)=q^2/2$, reducing output from 100 to 50 sacrifices 1,250 and avoids 3,750 of damage. At a payment of 2,000, the factory gains 750 and fishers gain 1,750 relative to no agreement. Total gain remains 2,500.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图上 $MD(q)>B'(q)$ 只说明在这个点附近再少生产一点有共同收益。跨过一大段数量，要像上例一样累计损害与收益变化，不能把某一点的边际差额当总补偿。如果权利改为渔民可禁止排污，无协议基准和付款方向都要重新计算；参见[[科斯产权不变性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The marginal inequality $MD(q)>B'(q)$ identifies a local gain from reducing activity. A finite change requires accumulated damage and benefit differences, not one marginal gap used as total compensation. Changing the entitlement requires a new baseline and payment direction; see [[科斯产权不变性|entitlement invariance]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
