# n 阶线性常系数差分方程对输出滞后作固定系数线性组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An nth-order linear constant-coefficient difference equation combines output lags with fixed coefficients*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 课程采用的标准形式是
> $$
> y_t=a_0+a_1y_{t-1}+\cdots+a_ny_{t-n}+x_t,\qquad a_n\ne0.
> $$
> 未知序列及其滞后都只以一次幂、加法和固定系数出现；$n$ 是输出递推的阶数，$x_t$ 是从方程外部给定的强迫过程。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the course form, the unknown sequence and its lags enter only through first powers, addition, and fixed coefficients. The furthest nonzero output lag gives the order, and $x_t$ is an externally specified forcing process.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“线性常系数”描述作用在未知序列上的左侧算子，不要求 $x_t$ 看起来简单。强迫项可以是常数、时间趋势、季节项、当前或滞后的其他变量，也可以是随机冲击；左侧算子满足叠加，而固定强迫下任意两条解之差满足齐次方程。若系数随 $t$ 变化，或出现 $y_{t-1}^2$、$y_{t-1}y_{t-2}$，就离开了本卡的模型类。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Linear constant-coefficient” describes the operator acting on the unknown sequence rather than requiring a simple input. That operator obeys superposition, and two solutions under the same forcing differ by a homogeneous solution. Time-varying coefficients, powers of lagged outputs, or products between them fall outside this class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把方程写出来还没有选定唯一轨迹。通常需要给出 $n$ 个相邻初值，例如 $y_0,\ldots,y_{n-1}$，才可以逐期计算以后各项。若目标改为寻找一条从无限过去生成的平稳随机解，则“初值”会被因果性、收敛性和分布条件替代；那是另一种解的选择原则，不应悄悄混入有限初值问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation alone describes a family of trajectories. A regular nth-order forward recurrence generally needs n adjacent initial values. Selecting a stationary solution generated from the infinite past is a different problem governed by causality, convergence, and distributional assumptions rather than by silently discarding the initial state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $y_t=(0.5+t^{-1})y_{t-1}+u_t$ 是本卡意义下的线性常系数差分方程吗？
>
> **答案：** 它对 $y$ 仍是线性的，但系数随 $t$ 变化，因此不是常系数方程。
