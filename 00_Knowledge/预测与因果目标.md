# 预测优化给定部署分布下的样本外损失，因果分析定义干预下的反事实目标；预测准确不能替代因果识别
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Prediction minimises out-of-sample loss under a target deployment distribution, whereas causal analysis defines counterfactual intervention targets; predictive accuracy cannot replace causal identification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 两类任务先问的不是同一个问题
> 预测先固定部署分布、可用信息和损失函数，再寻找
> $$
> f^*\in\arg\min_f E_{P_{\mathrm{deploy}}}[L\{Y,f(X)\}].
> $$
> 平方损失下，最优预测是 $f^*(x)=E[Y\mid X=x]$。因果分析先固定干预、结果、时间范围与目标总体，例如
> $$
> ATE=E[Y(1)-Y(0)].
> $$
> 观察条件分布 $P(Y\mid A,X)$ 一般不等于干预分布；低样本外误差不能补上缺失的识别条件。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Prediction fixes a deployment distribution, available information, and a loss function, then minimises expected out-of-sample loss. Under squared loss the target is $E[Y\mid X=x]$. Causal analysis instead fixes an intervention, outcome, horizon, and target population, for example $ATE=E[Y(1)-Y(0)]$. Observational conditional distributions are generally not interventional distributions, so low predictive error cannot supply missing identification assumptions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预测问“遇到类似的新数据，结果会是什么”，所以可以使用在真实预测时点可得、相对稳定且能降低部署损失的强代理变量。评价时要按任务选择样本外损失、校准或排序指标；上线后还要另行监控部署分布是否漂移。因果分析问“若改变处理会怎样”，变量能否进入调整集取决于它是处理前混杂因素、中介、碰撞点还是其他后处理结果，而不是它的变量重要性或对 $R^2$ 的贡献。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Prediction asks what outcome will occur for similar future observations, so stable information available at deployment—including strong proxies—may be useful. Evaluation should use task-appropriate out-of-sample loss, calibration, or ranking metrics, while deployment monitoring should separately check for distribution shift. Causal analysis asks what would happen under a changed treatment, so adjustment depends on whether a variable is a pretreatment confounder, mediator, collider, or another post-treatment consequence, not on variable importance or its contribution to $R^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交叉验证只能检验模型在所模拟部署分布上的预测表现，不能检验无未测混杂、排除限制、无干扰或平行趋势。反过来，一个设计清楚的随机试验即使几乎无法准确预测个体结果，仍可识别平均处理效应；但若效应很小而结果噪声很大，当前样本中的 estimate 仍可能很不精确。预测误差和因果估计的不确定性属于不同证据标准。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cross-validation evaluates prediction under its simulated deployment distribution; it does not test no unmeasured confounding, an exclusion restriction, no interference, or parallel trends. Conversely, a well-designed randomised trial may identify an average treatment effect even when individual outcomes are poorly predictable; if the effect is small and outcome noise is large, however, the estimate in the observed sample may still be imprecise. Predictive error and uncertainty about a causal estimate are different standards of evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两类任务可以合作。若基线 $X$ 满足一致性、条件交换性与正值性，则
$$
E[Y(a)]=E_X\{E[Y\mid A=a,X]\}.
$$
结果回归或倾向得分可以由机器学习估计，异质效应和政策价值也可以使用预测工具；但因果解释来自研究设计和这些识别条件，不来自算法的排行榜分数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tasks can cooperate. Under consistency, conditional exchangeability, and positivity given baseline $X$, standardisation identifies $E[Y(a)]$ by averaging $E[Y\mid A=a,X]$. Machine learning may estimate outcome regressions, propensity scores, heterogeneous effects, or policy values, but the causal interpretation comes from the design and identification assumptions rather than a predictive leaderboard score.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个模型对餐馆违规风险的排序很准。若检查资源只能覆盖 10% 的餐馆，而目标是最大限度减少违规，除风险排序外，至少还需要知道什么？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A model ranks restaurant violation risk accurately. If inspectors can visit only 10% of restaurants and the goal is to reduce violations as much as possible, what must be known in addition to the risk ranking?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 还需要知道检查对不同餐馆违规概率的因果影响，以及检查成本和部署约束。高风险餐馆不一定是最容易被检查改变的餐馆，因此 AUC 不能直接决定最优检查顺序。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** One also needs the causal effect of inspection on each type of restaurant, plus inspection costs and deployment constraints. The highest-risk restaurants need not be the most responsive to inspection, so AUC cannot determine the optimal inspection order by itself.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Mullainathan and Spiess (2017), “Machine Learning: An Applied Econometric Approach”](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257%2Fjep.31.2.87)：核对机器学习的样本外预测目标及其与因果识别的分工。

- [[条件均值因果边界]]：复用一致性、条件交换性、正值性和标准化把结果回归连接到反事实均值的已核验条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Mullainathan and Spiess (2017) was checked for out-of-sample prediction as machine learning's central econometric target and for its division of labour with causal identification.
- Shmueli (2010) was checked for differences in goals, variable selection, and evaluation between explanatory or causal modelling and prediction.
- [[条件均值因果边界|The conditional-mean causal boundary]] supplies the verified consistency, conditional-exchangeability, positivity, and standardisation conditions linking an outcome regression to counterfactual means.
