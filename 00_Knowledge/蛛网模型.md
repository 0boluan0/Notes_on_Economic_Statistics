# 蛛网模型让本期供给依赖上一期价格而本期价格通过市场出清决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The cobweb model makes current supply depend on last period's price while market clearing determines the current price*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 线性蛛网模型写成
> $$
> d_t=a-\gamma p_t,\qquad
> s_t=b+\beta p_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t,\qquad
> d_t=s_t,
> $$
> 其中 $\gamma,\beta>0$。生产者用上一期价格形成本期供给，本期价格再调整到使市场出清；供给冲击 $\varepsilon_t$ 在本期改变可售数量。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the linear cobweb model, producers base current supply on the previous price, while the current price clears demand and supply. A contemporaneous supply shock shifts the amount available.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

联立三式得到一阶差分方程
$$
p_t=\frac{a-b}{\gamma}-\frac{\beta}{\gamma}p_{t-1}-\frac{1}{\gamma}\varepsilon_t.
$$
在无冲击且价格不再变化时，长期出清价格满足
$$
p^*=\frac{a-b}{\gamma+\beta}.
$$
负的递推系数来自供给滞后：上期高价诱发本期高供给，本期出清价因而下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market clearing reduces the model to a first-order price recurrence. Its negative coefficient creates alternating adjustment: a high previous price raises current supply and depresses the current clearing price. The no-shock fixed point is $(a-b)/(\gamma+\beta)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是一套明确的预期与时序假设，不是所有农产品市场的自然定律。若生产者使用理性预期、库存平滑、生产周期超过一期，或价格并非每期完全出清，递推结构和稳定条件都会改变。模型的价值在于把“滞后反应如何制造振荡”隔离出来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The model is a specific timing and expectations mechanism, not a universal law of commodity markets. Rational expectations, inventories, longer production lags, or incomplete price adjustment would change the recurrence. Its value is to isolate how delayed response creates oscillation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么蛛网价格递推中的 $p_{t-1}$ 系数是负的？
>
> **答案：** 上期价格越高，本期供给越多；给定向下倾斜的本期需求，市场出清要求本期价格更低。
