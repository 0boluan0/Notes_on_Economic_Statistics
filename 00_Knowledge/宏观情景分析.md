# 宏观情景分析把一致的宏观假设逐项传到公司经营现金流和贴现结果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Macro-to-firm scenario analysis maps coherent macro assumptions into operating cash flows and discounted values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复用[[情景预测]]的条件性定义：先写数据时点和宏观冲击，再写公司暴露与合同如何改变销量、售价、成本、税收、投资和营运资金，最后匹配现金流、币种、期限与贴现率。每个箭头都应能解释或重算；只写“衰退→看空”不构成完整情景。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the conditional definition of [[情景预测|scenario forecasting]], specify the data date and macro shock, map company exposures and contracts to volume, prices, costs, taxes, investment, and working capital, and then match cash flows with currency, horizon, and discount rates. Each link must be explainable or reproducible; “recession means bearish” is incomplete.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一期教学企业假设全部销售和成本当期收付，无利息、折旧、资本开支或营运资金变化，期末无剩余价值，所得税率为 20%。基准销量 100、单价 10、单位成本 6、固定成本 100，税前经营现金为 $100(10-6)-100=300$，[[企业自由现金流|FCFF]] 为 240；以 10% 贴现得到 218.18。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a one-period teaching firm, assume all sales and costs are paid within the period, no interest, depreciation, capital expenditure, or working-capital change, no terminal value, and a 20% tax rate. Volume 100, price 10, unit cost 6, and fixed cost 100 give operating cash before tax of 300 and [[企业自由现金流|FCFF]] of 240. At 10%, value is 218.18.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

需求转弱且投入涨价的情景设销量 90、单价仍为 10、单位成本 6.5，固定成本与税率不变，所需回报升至 12%。经营现金变为 $90(10-6.5)-100=215$，税后为 172，价值为 $172/1.12\approx153.57$。这组数字展示闭合计算，不声称宏观数据必然推出这些参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A weaker-demand and higher-input-cost scenario sets volume to 90, price to 10, unit cost to 6.5, fixed cost and tax unchanged, and required return to 12%. Operating cash is 215, after-tax cash 172, and value $172/1.12\approx153.57$. The example demonstrates a closed calculation, not parameters mechanically implied by macro data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后检查哪些参数有证据、哪些是假设，检验合理替代情景及最敏感驱动。情景未给概率就不能加权成“预期价值”；未包含全部风险的情景集合也不是预测区间。对政策两端的区别，另见[[政策传导的估值渠道]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally distinguish evidence from assumptions and test plausible alternatives and sensitive drivers. Scenarios without probabilities cannot simply be averaged into expected value, and an incomplete scenario set is not a prediction interval. See [[政策传导的估值渠道|policy valuation channels]] for the cash-flow and discount-rate distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
