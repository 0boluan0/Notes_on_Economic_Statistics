# Work by integration
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*功的积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Work** measures energy transferred by a particular force as an object moves. A stronger force or a longer displacement can make a larger contribution, but direction matters too. Pulling a spring and lifting water both involve work; integration is useful because the force or the distance travelled by each piece is not constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**功**描述物体移动时，某个特定的力传递了多少能量。力更大或位移更长，贡献可能更大，但方向同样重要。拉伸弹簧和提升水都涉及做功；当力的大小变化，或不同部分移动的距离不同时，就需要积分来累加。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin with motion along a straight line, choosing the rightward direction as positive. A constant force of $5$ newtons pointing right does $5\times2=10$ joules of work when the object moves $2$ metres right. If the object instead moves $2$ metres left while that **same force still points right**, its work is $5\times(-2)=-10$ joules. The sign refers to the work of this force, not necessarily the sum of the work done by all forces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看直线运动，规定向右为正。一股始终向右、大小为 $5$ 牛顿的力，在物体向右移动 $2$ 米时做功 $5\times2=10$ 焦耳。若物体改为向左移动 $2$ 米，而**这股力仍向右**，它做的功就是 $5\times(-2)=-10$ 焦耳。这个符号描述的是所研究的这一股力，不一定是所有力做功的总和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From small displacements to an integral
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从小位移累加成积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $x$ denote position along the chosen line, and let $F(x)$ be the force's **signed component** in the positive direction. Positive $F$ points toward increasing $x$, and negative $F$ points toward decreasing $x$. Assume the force depends only on position and is Riemann integrable on the finite interval traversed. Continuity is a sufficient condition. For one movement from $a$ to $b$, without a reversal along the way, its work is the [[定积分|definite integral]]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $x$ 表示沿所选直线的位置，$F(x)$ 表示这股力沿正方向的**带符号分量**：正值指向正向，负值指向反向。假设力只依赖位置，在经过的有限区间上黎曼可积；连续性是一个充分条件。物体从 $a$ 移到 $b$，中途不反向时，这股力做的功是[[定积分]]：

$$W=\int_a^b F(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a<b$, split the interval into small pieces. In piece $i$, choose a sample position $\xi_i$ and use $F(\xi_i)$ as an approximately constant force. If the piece has width $\Delta x_i$, its work is approximately $F(\xi_i)\Delta x_i$. Adding these terms gives a [[黎曼和|Riemann sum]]. As the largest piece width tends to zero, integrability makes the sums approach the displayed integral. The force need not be nonnegative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $a<b$ 时，把区间分成小段。在第 $i$ 段选一个采样位置 $\xi_i$，用 $F(\xi_i)$ 近似这一段内的力。若段宽是 $\Delta x_i$，这一小段的功近似为 $F(\xi_i)\Delta x_i$；相加后就是[[黎曼和]]。最大段宽趋于零时，可积性保证这些和趋向上面的积分。力并不需要处处非负。

> [!important] Keep direction and units · 保留方向与单位
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $b<a$, retain the reversed integration limits. A force opposing motion contributes negative work. Do not insert an absolute value merely to obtain a positive answer. Force in newtons times displacement in metres gives joules: $1\,\mathrm J=1\,\mathrm N\cdot\mathrm m$. The “area” in a force–position graph therefore has units of energy, not square metres.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $b<a$ 时，保留反向的积分上下限。力与运动方向相反，贡献就是负功；不能只为了得到正数而添加绝对值。牛顿乘以米得到焦耳：$1\,\mathrm J=1\,\mathrm N\cdot\mathrm m$。因此，力—位置图像中的“面积”单位是能量，而不是平方米。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the motion reverses finitely many times and the same position-only force applies on each leg, add the oriented integrals. [[定积分区间可加性|Interval additivity]] cancels retraced contributions and leaves $\int_a^bF(x)\,dx$ between the final endpoints. This cancellation would fail for a force whose direction changes with the motion, such as sliding friction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果运动有限次反向，但每一段都适用同一个只依赖位置的力函数，就把各段的有向积分相加。[[定积分区间可加性]]使往返经过的贡献相互抵消，最终仍得到起终点之间的 $\int_a^bF(x)\,dx$。若力随运动方向改变，例如滑动摩擦力，就不能这样抵消。

## A spring: whose work is being calculated?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*弹簧：计算的是谁做的功？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an ideal horizontal spring, take $x=0$ at its natural length and let $x$ measure the change in length, not the entire spring length. In the range where Hooke's law applies, the restoring force is $F_{\mathrm{spring}}(x)=-kx$, where $k>0$ is the spring constant, measured in newtons per metre. A stretched spring pulls back; a compressed spring pushes outward.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一根理想水平弹簧，以自然长度的位置为 $x=0$；$x$ 表示长度的改变量，而不是弹簧的总长度。在胡克定律适用的范围内，回复力为 $F_{\mathrm{spring}}(x)=-kx$，其中 $k>0$ 是弹簧劲度系数，单位为牛顿每米。伸长时它向回拉，压缩时它向外推。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To evaluate the integral, use $-kx^2/2$, whose [[导数|derivative]] is $-kx$. The [[Newton-Leibniz公式|antiderivative evaluation rule]] subtracts its value at the starting position from its value at the ending position:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算时，可以用[[导数]]为 $-kx$ 的函数 $-kx^2/2$。[[Newton-Leibniz公式]]让我们用它在终点的值减去在起点的值：

$$W_{\mathrm{spring}}
=\int_a^b(-kx)\,dx
=-\frac{k}{2}(b^2-a^2).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an ideal slow stretching process with friction neglected and negligible change in kinetic energy, the external force balances the spring: $F_{\mathrm{ext}}(x)=kx$. Its work is therefore $k(b^2-a^2)/2$, the opposite of the spring's work. This describes the ideal quasistatic model; a prescribed acceleration would generally require a different applied force.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在忽略摩擦、动能变化可忽略的理想缓慢拉伸过程中，外力与弹簧力平衡：$F_{\mathrm{ext}}(x)=kx$。所以外力功是 $k(b^2-a^2)/2$，与弹簧做的功互为相反数。这是理想准静态模型；若要求物体以某种加速度运动，所需外力通常就不同了。

> [!example] Stretching from 10 cm to 30 cm · 从伸长 10 厘米拉到 30 厘米
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $k=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$, $a=0.10\,\mathrm m$, and $b=0.30\,\mathrm m$. Convert both lengths to metres before integrating:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $k=200\,\mathrm{N/m}$、$a=0.10\,\mathrm m$、$b=0.30\,\mathrm m$。积分前先把两个长度都换成米：
>
> $$W_{\mathrm{ext}}=\frac{200}{2}(0.30^2-0.10^2)=8\,\mathrm J,\qquad
> W_{\mathrm{spring}}=-8\,\mathrm J.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The applied force rises from $20$ to $60$ newtons. Because it varies linearly, its average is $40$ newtons, and $40\times0.20=8$ joules checks the integral. Using the final force throughout would overestimate the work.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 外力从 $20$ 牛顿增加到 $60$ 牛顿。因为它线性变化，平均值为 $40$ 牛顿，而 $40\times0.20=8$ 焦耳，正好核对积分。若全程都用末端的力，就会高估所做的功。

## Lifting water: each layer travels a different distance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*提水：每一层移动的距离不同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now the pieces are different portions of material, not successive positions of one object. Measure height $y$ upward. Suppose water initially occupies heights $\ell\le y\le u$ in a regular tank, where $\ell<u$ are finite. Let $A(y)\ge0$ be its horizontal cross-sectional area, assumed Riemann integrable, and let the uniform mass density be $\rho>0$ kilograms per cubic metre. The outlet is at height $H\ge u$, and gravitational acceleration $g>0$ is treated as constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这次分割的是不同的材料，而不是同一个物体先后到达的位置。以向上为高度 $y$ 的正方向。设规则水箱中的水最初位于有限高度区间 $\ell\le y\le u$，其中 $\ell<u$；水平截面积为 $A(y)\ge0$，假设它黎曼可积。水的均匀质量密度为 $\rho>0$ 千克每立方米，出口高度为 $H\ge u$，重力加速度 $g>0$ 视为常数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A thin layer has [[切片体积|volume]] approximately $A(y)\Delta y$. Multiplying by $\rho$ gives its mass, and multiplying again by $g$ gives its weight. Lifting that layer to the outlet raises it by $H-y$. In the ideal model that counts work against gravity, with losses and changes in kinetic energy neglected, its contribution is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一薄层水的[[切片体积|体积]]近似为 $A(y)\Delta y$，乘以 $\rho$ 得质量，再乘以 $g$ 得重量。把这一层提升到出口，需要升高 $H-y$。在只计算克服重力、忽略损耗和动能变化的理想模型中，这一层的贡献为：

$$\Delta W\approx
\underbrace{\rho g A(y)\Delta y}_{\text{layer weight}}
\underbrace{(H-y)}_{\text{lifting distance}},
\qquad
W=\int_\ell^u \rho g A(y)(H-y)\,dy.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\Delta y$, or $dy$ in the integral, specifies how much water belongs to a layer; it is **not** that layer's travel distance. The factor $H-y$ supplies that distance. If a problem instead gives **weight density**, meaning weight per unit volume in $\mathrm{N/m^3}$, use that value directly in place of $\rho g$; multiplying it by $g$ again would count gravity twice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 $\Delta y$，以及积分中的 $dy$，决定这一层包含多少水，**不是**这一层移动的距离；移动距离来自因子 $H-y$。如果题目给的是**重量密度**，也就是每单位体积的重量，单位为 $\mathrm{N/m^3}$，就直接用它代替 $\rho g$，不能再乘一次 $g$。

> [!example] A tank with a constant cross-section · 截面积恒定的水箱
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A(y)=1\,\mathrm{m^2}$ for $0\le y\le2\,\mathrm m$, with outlet height $H=3\,\mathrm m$, density $\rho=1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, and $g=9.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$. Using metres for $y$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设水位从 $0$ 到 $2$ 米，截面积始终为 $1\,\mathrm{m^2}$；出口高 $3$ 米，水的密度为 $1000\,\mathrm{kg/m^3}$，取 $g=9.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}$。用米表示 $y$，有：
>
> $$W=9800\int_0^2(3-y)\,dy=39\,200\,\mathrm J.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The $2\,\mathrm{m^3}$ of water weighs $19\,600$ newtons. Its layers rise by distances from $1$ to $3$ metres; with constant cross-section and density, their average lift is $2$ metres. Thus $19\,600\times2=39\,200$ joules. Treating every layer as if it starts at the bottom would give an incorrect $58\,800$ joules.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这 $2\,\mathrm{m^3}$ 的水重 $19\,600$ 牛顿，各层提升距离从 $1$ 米到 $3$ 米。截面积和密度都恒定，所以平均提升距离为 $2$ 米，核对得 $19\,600\times2=39\,200$ 焦耳。若把所有水都当成从箱底出发，就会错误地得到 $58\,800$ 焦耳。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The units also check the construction: $(\mathrm{kg/m^3})(\mathrm{m/s^2})(\mathrm{m^2})(\mathrm m)(\mathrm m)=\mathrm{kg\,m^2/s^2}=\mathrm J$. Integrating only $A(y)$ gives volume; integrating $\rho A(y)$ gives mass; work additionally requires gravitational force and the lifting distance. If only part of the water is removed, the integral must describe the layers actually lifted to the specified outlet.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

量纲也能核对建模：$(\mathrm{kg/m^3})(\mathrm{m/s^2})(\mathrm{m^2})(\mathrm m)(\mathrm m)=\mathrm{kg\,m^2/s^2}=\mathrm J$。只积分 $A(y)$ 得体积，只积分 $\rho A(y)$ 得质量；功还需要把质量变为重力，并乘以提升距离。若只抽出一部分水，积分范围必须对应实际被提升到指定出口的那些水层。

## When position alone is not enough
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*仅有起终点何时不够*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the force depends on time or on the direction of motion, describe the actual trajectory. Let $x(t)$ be a continuously differentiable position during finite times $t_0<t_1$, let its [[导数|derivative]] $x'(t)$ be the signed velocity, and let $\mathcal F(t)$ be the signed force evaluated along that motion. Assuming the product is integrable—for example, when both factors are continuous—the work is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若力还依赖时间或运动方向，就要描述实际轨迹。设在有限时段 $t_0<t_1$ 内，位置 $x(t)$ 连续可微，[[导数]] $x'(t)$ 是带符号速度，$\mathcal F(t)$ 是沿实际运动取到的带符号力。假设两者乘积可积，例如两者都连续时，就有：

$$W=\int_{t_0}^{t_1}\mathcal F(t)x'(t)\,dt.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same formula applies piece by piece to a continuous trajectory with finitely many continuously differentiable pieces. For a fixed surface and constant sliding-friction magnitude $c>0$, friction opposes each movement, so its work is $-c\int|x'(t)|\,dt$: negative $c$ times the [[净变化与总变化|distance travelled]]. A trip from $0$ to $L>0$ and back has zero displacement but frictional work $-2cL$. The endpoints alone therefore cannot determine this work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于连续、可分成有限个连续可微部分的轨迹，也可以逐段使用同一公式。对固定表面上的滑动摩擦，若摩擦力大小恒为 $c>0$，它总与移动方向相反，因此摩擦功为 $-c\int|x'(t)|\,dt$，也就是负的 $c$ 乘以[[净变化与总变化|所走路程]]。从 $0$ 到 $L>0$ 再回来的位移为零，摩擦功却为 $-2cL$。所以仅有起终点，不能确定这种力做了多少功。
