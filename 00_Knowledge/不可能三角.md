# 高资本流动下可信固定汇率与独立利率路径不能同时完全维持
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With high capital mobility, a credible fixed exchange rate and an independent interest-rate path cannot both be fully maintained*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子政策约束
> 不可能三角的三个目标是：高度开放的资本流动、可信而稳定的汇率承诺、面向国内目标独立设定货币条件。靠近三个“角点”的制度不能把三项目标同时完全实现；选择其中两项会约束第三项。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The trilemma's three objectives are high capital mobility, a credible commitment to exchange-rate stability, and independent monetary conditions directed at domestic objectives. A regime near the idealized corners cannot fully achieve all three at once; choosing two constrains the third.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在可信固定汇率下，预期汇率变动空间被压缩。若资本高度流动且本外币资产足够可替代，国内利率持续偏离锚货币的风险调整回报会引发资本流动、远期和储备压力，迫使政策利率、汇率承诺或资本开放程度之一调整。[[无抵补利率平价]]提供利差与预期汇率变化之间的一个理论接口，但三角是更广的制度约束，不能由一条 CIP 等式机械推出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under a credible peg, expected exchange-rate movement is compressed. If capital is highly mobile and domestic and foreign assets are sufficiently substitutable, a persistent gap between the domestic rate and the anchor currency's risk-adjusted return creates capital-flow, forward-market, and reserve pressure. The interest-rate path, the exchange-rate commitment, or capital openness must then adjust. UIP provides one theoretical interface between interest differentials and expected currency movement, but the trilemma is a broader institutional constraint and is not mechanically derived from one CIP equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## “三选二”是角点速记而非绝对二元法则

现实中的资本流动并非只有完全自由或完全封闭，汇率制度也有区间、爬行、管理浮动和货币联盟。有限且有效的资本流动管理、较宽汇率波动或不完全可信的承诺，可能给货币政策留下部分空间；代价和可持续性取决于执行、市场发展、资产替代、外币负债和全球金融周期。少数目标型限制若容易规避，不足以长期同时保住硬盯住与独立利率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Pick two” is a corner-solution mnemonic, not an absolute binary law. Capital mobility can be partial, and exchange-rate regimes include bands, crawls, managed floats, and monetary unions. Effective capital-flow management, wider exchange-rate movement, or an incomplete commitment can leave some monetary-policy room, with durability depending on enforcement, market development, asset substitutability, foreign-currency liabilities, and the global financial cycle. A few easily evaded controls cannot generally preserve both a hard peg and an independent rate path for long.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡中的“货币政策独立”指相对于锚货币独立选择国内货币条件，不等同于 [[央行目标与工具独立性|央行在法律和治理上的目标或工具独立性]]。三角也不声称财政政策、监管或外汇储备完全无用，只限制三项目标的可持续组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here, monetary-policy independence means choosing domestic monetary conditions independently of the anchor currency. It is not the same as a central bank's legal goal or instrument independence. The trilemma also does not claim that fiscal policy, regulation, or reserves are useless; it restricts the sustainable combination of the three named objectives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 有少量资本管制的固定汇率经济体是否因此自动获得完全独立的货币政策？
>
> **答案：** 不会。关键是资本流动管理是否全面、可执行和可持续，以及汇率承诺和资产替代程度；三项目标在现实中常表现为程度上的权衡。

- [IMF, *Choice of Exchange Rate Arrangement*, pp. 17–18](https://www.imf.org/en/-/media/files/publications/miscellaneous/english/2022/mcm-technical-assistance-handbook/choice-of-exchange-rate-arrangement.pdf)：核对三项目标、固定汇率下的利率约束以及资本流动管理必须全面有效的边界。

- [R. A. Mundell, “Capital Mobility and Stabilization Policy Under Fixed and Flexible Exchange Rates” (1963)](https://doi.org/10.2307/139336)：核对高资本流动这一关键条件。
- [Obstfeld, Shambaugh and Taylor, “The Trilemma in History” (2005)](https://direct.mit.edu/rest/article/87/3/423/57557/The-Trilemma-in-History-Tradeoffs-Among-Exchange)：核对三项目标在历史数据中的制度权衡。
