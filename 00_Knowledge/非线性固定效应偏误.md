# 在短 T 的一般非线性面板中，逐单位估计固定效应的 MLE 具有附带参数偏误，增加 N 本身不能消除

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a general nonlinear panel with short $T$, an MLE that estimates a fixed effect for every unit has incidental-parameter bias, which increasing $N$ alone cannot remove*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 附带参数问题
> 在 $N$ 个单位、每单位约 $T$ 期的非线性模型中，unconditional fixed-effects MLE 同时估计共同斜率 $\beta$ 和 $N$ 个单位参数 $\alpha_i$。若 $T$ 很短，每个 $\alpha_i$ 始终只由少量观测估计；让 $N\to\infty$ 会同时增加观测和待估固定效应，因而一般不能靠更大的 $N$ 消除斜率偏误。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a nonlinear model with $N$ units and about $T$ periods per unit, an unconditional fixed-effects MLE estimates both the common slope $\beta$ and $N$ unit parameters $\alpha_i$. When $T$ is short, each $\alpha_i$ is always estimated from only a few observations. Letting $N\to\infty$ adds observations and fixed effects to be estimated at the same time, so a larger $N$ generally cannot eliminate the slope bias.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与静态线性 FE 不同：线性加性模型可用组内变换精确消去 $\alpha_i$；logit/probit 的非线性使“先估错一点 $\alpha_i$”会传进共同斜率和平均部分效应。在一维个体效应的大 $N,T$ 展开中，常见领先偏误量级为 $1/T$；同时加入个体和时间效应时，领先项通常含 $1/T$ 与 $1/N$ 两部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This differs from static linear fixed effects: a linear additive model can eliminate $\alpha_i$ exactly with the within transformation, whereas the nonlinearity of logit or probit transmits estimation error in $\alpha_i$ into the common slope and average partial effects. In a large-$N,T$ expansion with one-dimensional individual effects, the leading bias is commonly of order $1/T$. With both individual and time effects, the leading term usually contains components of order $1/T$ and $1/N$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可选处理包括：在静态 logit 且目标为共同斜率时使用 [[条件固定效应Logit|条件似然]]；在适用的大 $N,T$ 条件下使用分析偏误修正或 split-panel jackknife；或重新评估随机效应、相关随机效应及其他结构假设。修正方法依赖模型、缺失模式、时间依赖和目标量，不能只因软件提供一个选项就视为通用修复。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Possible responses include using [[条件固定效应Logit|the conditional likelihood]] for a static logit when the common slope is the target; applying an analytical bias correction or split-panel jackknife under suitable large-$N,T$ conditions; or reconsidering random effects, correlated random effects, and other structural assumptions. A correction depends on the model, missing-data pattern, time dependence, and target quantity. The existence of a software option does not make it a universal repair.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 不要与 Nickell 偏误混同
> 本卡的附带参数偏误在静态非线性模型中也会出现，不需要滞后因变量。[[动态固定效应偏误|Nickell 偏误]] 来自动态线性 FE 中去均值滞后结果与去均值误差相关，是另一种机制。动态二元面板还涉及初始条件和真实状态依赖，本批不处理，也不能从本卡外推。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The incidental-parameter bias discussed here also appears in static nonlinear models and does not require a lagged dependent variable. [[动态固定效应偏误|Nickell bias]] is a different mechanism: in a dynamic linear fixed-effects model, the demeaned lagged outcome is correlated with the demeaned error. Dynamic binary panels additionally involve initial conditions and genuine state dependence; this atom set does not cover them, and they cannot be inferred from this card.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Hahn and Newey (2004), [“Jackknife and Analytical Bias Reduction for Nonlinear Panel Models”](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-0262.2004.00533.x), *Econometrica* 72(4): 1295–1319：直接证明非线性面板 fixed-effects estimators 的附带参数偏误，并推导 large-$T$ 分析修正与 panel jackknife；不是从 Nickell 偏误类推。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Hahn and Newey (2004), [“Jackknife and Analytical Bias Reduction for Nonlinear Panel Models”](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-0262.2004.00533.x), *Econometrica* 72(4): 1295–1319, directly establishes incidental-parameter bias in nonlinear panel fixed-effects estimators and derives large-$T$ analytical corrections and a panel jackknife; the result is not extrapolated from Nickell bias.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- Fernández-Val and Weidner (2016), [“Individual and Time Effects in Nonlinear Panel Models with Large N, T”](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1473260/), *Journal of Econometrics* 192(1): 291–312：直接处理含个体与时间效应的 logit、probit 等非线性面板，给出参数与平均部分效应的分析及 jackknife 偏误修正。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Fernández-Val and Weidner (2016), [“Individual and Time Effects in Nonlinear Panel Models with Large N, T”](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1473260/), *Journal of Econometrics* 192(1): 291–312, directly studies nonlinear panels such as logit and probit with individual and time effects and provides analytical and jackknife bias corrections for parameters and average partial effects.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- StataCorp, [*clogit*](https://www.stata.com/manuals/rclogit.pdf), “Fixed-effects logit”：交叉核对固定 $T$ 下直接估计全部 $\alpha_i$ 与 $\beta$ 的 full MLE 不一致，以及条件 logit 的特殊绕开方式。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  StataCorp, [*clogit*](https://www.stata.com/manuals/rclogit.pdf), “Fixed-effects logit,” cross-checks the inconsistency, at fixed $T$, of the full MLE that directly estimates every $\alpha_i$ together with $\beta$, and the special conditional-logit workaround.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
