# 代表性启发式依据与典型模式的相似性判断类别或事件概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The representativeness heuristic judges categories or event probabilities by similarity to a typical pattern*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

代表性启发式是依据观察对象与某类典型特征、某种过程或模式的相似程度，判断它属于该类或由该过程产生的可能性。相似性可以包含有用信息；问题在于把“很像”直接当成“很可能”，从而忽略基础发生率、样本量或证据的实际区分能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The representativeness heuristic assesses category membership or origin from a process by resemblance to its characteristic features or pattern. Similarity can be informative. The error arises when resemblance substitutes for probability while base rates, sample size, or the evidence's actual diagnosticity are neglected.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个自拟对比：类别 $A$ 的先验概率仅为 $0.1$；观察到特征 $E$，且 $P(E\mid A)=0.4$、$P(E\mid A^c)=0.2$。该特征在 $A$ 中确实更常见，但[[Bayes法则]]给出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an illustrative contrast, let category $A$ have prior probability $0.1$, with $P(E\mid A)=0.4$ and $P(E\mid A^c)=0.2$. Feature $E$ is genuinely more common in $A$, but [[Bayes法则|Bayes' rule]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P(A\mid E)=\frac{0.4\times0.1}{0.4\times0.1+0.2\times0.9}=\frac2{11}\approx0.1818.$$

“更像 $A$”提高了后验概率，却没有使 $A$ 成为大概率类别。使用相似性本身不是偏差；需要比较实际判断与同一信息下的适当基准。短期走势看起来像持续趋势，也不足以单凭这个形状确定生成过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Resemblance raises the posterior, but does not make $A$ the probable category. Using similarity is not itself a bias; the judgment must be compared with an appropriate benchmark under the same information. Likewise, a short run resembling a persistent trend does not identify its generating process by appearance alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
