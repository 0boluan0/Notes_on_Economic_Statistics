# 行业是按相近产品或服务及其竞争关系界定的一组经营活动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An industry is a group of business activities defined by related products or services and their competitive relationships.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行业把提供相近产品或服务、面对相似需求与竞争条件的经营活动归在一起。边界取决于分析目的；应说明产品、地域和时期。行业不是天然固定的公司名单，一家多业务公司可以参与多个行业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An industry groups activities supplying related products or services under similar demand and competitive conditions. Its boundary depends on the analytical purpose and should specify products, geography, and period. It is not an immutable list of companies: a diversified company can operate in several industries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，分析某城市咖啡店的客流时，附近咖啡店可能是直接竞争者，居家冲泡可能是替代方式；分析全球咖啡豆采购时，市场边界又不同。分类编码有助于整理资料，但不能替代[[行业可比样本选择]]，也不自动等于反垄断分析中的相关市场。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a city coffee shop's customer traffic, nearby shops may be direct rivals and home brewing a substitute. Global coffee-bean procurement has a different boundary. Classification codes help organize data but do not replace [[行业可比样本选择|peer-group selection]] or automatically define an antitrust relevant market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
