# Double counting
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*双重计数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ana belongs to both the chess and drama clubs, Ben belongs only to chess, and Cara belongs only to drama. There are three people, but four **memberships**. A membership is a pair consisting of one person and one club that person belongs to:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Ana 同时参加棋社和戏剧社，Ben 只参加棋社，Cara 只参加戏剧社。这里有三个人，却有四份**成员关系**。一份关系是“一个人，以及这个人参加的一个社团”组成的有序对：

$$
(A,C),\ (A,D),\ (B,C),\ (K,D),
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $A,B,K$ stand for Ana, Ben and Cara, and $C,D$ for chess and drama. Counting by person gives $2+1+1=4$ memberships. Counting by club gives $2+2=4$. These expressions agree because they count the same four pairs.

**Double counting** proves an identity by counting one finite collection in two complete ways. The first task is to identify the [[计数对象|objects]] that both sides count. Here they are memberships, not distinct people and not clubs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $A,B,K$ 分别代表 Ana、Ben、Cara，$C,D$ 分别代表棋社、戏剧社。按人统计，成员关系有 $2+1+1=4$ 份；按社团统计，则有 $2+2=4$ 份。两个式子相等，是因为它们数的是同样的四个配对。

**双重计数**用两种完整的计数方式计算同一个有限集合，从而证明两个表达式相等。第一步是明确两边共同数的[[计数对象]]；这里是成员关系，而不是不同的人数或社团数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Rows and columns count the same pairs

Let $P$ be a finite set of people, $C$ a finite set of clubs, and $R$ the set of valid membership pairs $(p,c)$. For a person $p$, count the clubs paired with that person. For a club $c$, count the people paired with that club. The notation $\#\{\cdots\}$ below means “the number of elements satisfying the condition.” Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*按行、按列数的是同一批配对*

令 $P$ 为有限的人员集合，$C$ 为有限的社团集合，$R$ 为有效成员关系 $(p,c)$ 的集合。固定一个人 $p$，数与他配对的社团；固定一个社团 $c$，数与它配对的人。下式中 $\#\{\cdots\}$ 表示“满足括号内条件的元素个数”：

$$
\sum_{p\in P}\#\{c\in C:(p,c)\in R\}
=|R|
=\sum_{c\in C}\#\{p\in P:(p,c)\in R\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each pair has exactly one person and exactly one club. Grouping by either coordinate therefore counts every pair once. The group sizes may differ, and some groups may be empty; the [[加法法则|sum rule]] still applies. If no memberships exist, both sums are zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个配对恰有一个人员坐标和一个社团坐标，因此按任一坐标分组，都把每个配对恰好数一次。各组不必一样大，也可以有空组，[[加法法则]]仍然适用；如果没有成员关系，两边都为零。

> [!example]- A graph counts edge ends · 图中数边的端点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a finite undirected simple graph, vertices are points and each edge joins two different vertices. A vertex–edge **incidence** is a pair $(v,e)$ saying that vertex $v$ is an endpoint of edge $e$. Let $I$ be the set of these pairs and $E$ the edge set. The [[度数、入度与出度|degree]] $\deg(v)$ is the number of edges incident to $v$.
>
> Counting incidences by vertex gives $|I|=\sum_v\deg(v)$. Counting by edge gives $|I|=2|E|$, because every edge has two endpoints. Thus the [[无向图握手定理|handshaking lemma]] follows:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在有限无向简单图中，顶点是点，每条边连接两个不同顶点。一份**顶点—边关联**是有序对 $(v,e)$，表示顶点 $v$ 是边 $e$ 的端点。令 $I$ 为这些配对的集合，$E$ 为边集合。顶点的[[度数、入度与出度|度数]] $\deg(v)$ 是与它关联的边数。
>
> 按顶点数关联，得到 $|I|=\sum_v\deg(v)$；按边数关联，每条边有两个端点，得到 $|I|=2|E|$，于是有[[无向图握手定理]]：
>
> $$
> \sum_v\deg(v)=2|E|.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the path $a-b-c$, the degrees are $1,2,1$: their sum is $4$, twice the two edges. We count endpoint–edge pairs, not each edge once on the vertex side.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 例如路径 $a-b-c$ 的度数为 $1,2,1$，总和为 $4$，恰好是两条边的两倍。按顶点统计时数的是端点与边的配对，并不是让每条边只出现一次。

## Distinguishing neighbouring methods
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与相邻方法的区别

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[双射计数|A bijection]] pairs two collections reversibly. The [[除法法则|division rule]] corrects a fixed number of representations per object. Double counting obtains two expressions for the size of the **same** collection. These approaches can be combined, but seeing two sums in a formula does not identify a double-counting proof; the common objects and why each count is complete must be explained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[双射计数]]把两个集合可逆配对；[[除法法则]]修正每个对象固定数量的表示；双重计数则为**同一个**集合的大小写出两个表达式。方法可以组合使用，但式子里出现两个求和号，不足以构成双重计数证明；必须说清共同对象是什么，以及两种方法为什么都完整地数了它们。

> [!example]- A subset with a marked member · 选一个子集，并标出其中一人
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> From $n\ge1$ distinct people, form a subset $S$ and mark one of its members $x$. The counted object is the pair $(S,x)$ with $x\in S$. If $S$ has size $k$, there are $\binom nk$ choices of subset and $k$ choices of marked member, giving $k\binom nk$ pairs. Here $\binom nk$ is the [[组合数|number of $k$-element subsets]]. Sum over $k=0,\ldots,n$; the empty subset contributes zero.
>
> Alternatively, choose the marked person in $n$ ways, then decide separately whether each of the remaining $n-1$ people joins the subset. The [[乘法法则|product rule]] gives $2^{n-1}$ choices. Both counts yield
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $n\ge1$ 个不同的人中选一个子集 $S$，再标出其中一人 $x$。共同计数对象是满足 $x\in S$ 的配对 $(S,x)$。若子集有 $k$ 人，先有 $\binom nk$ 种子集可选，再有 $k$ 种标记方式，共 $k\binom nk$ 个配对；其中 $\binom nk$ 是[[组合数|$k$ 元子集数]]。对 $k=0,\ldots,n$ 相加，空子集因没有人可标记而贡献零。
>
> 另一种数法是先从 $n$ 人中选被标记者，再分别决定剩余 $n-1$ 人是否入选，由[[乘法法则]]得到 $2^{n-1}$ 种。两种计数给出
>
> $$
> \sum_{k=0}^{n}k\binom nk=n2^{n-1}\qquad(n\ge1).
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $n=3$, counting by subset size gives $0+3+6+3=12$, while counting by the marked person gives $3\times4=12$. If $n=0$, there are no marked pairs; handle that empty case directly rather than using a construction with $n-1$ remaining people.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $n=3$ 时，按子集大小得到 $0+3+6+3=12$；先选被标记者则得到 $3\times4=12$。$n=0$ 时没有可标记的配对，直接计为零即可，不必套用“剩余 $n-1$ 人”的构造。
