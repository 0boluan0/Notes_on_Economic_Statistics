# 满足 $AGA=A$ 的矩阵称为 $A$ 的广义逆（{1}-逆）
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A matrix $G$ satisfying $AGA=A$ is a one-condition generalized inverse of $A$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 对 $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$，若 $G\in\mathbb F^{n\times m}$ 满足
> $$
> AGA=A,
> $$
> 则称 $G$ 为 $A$ 的广义逆；更精确地说，它是满足第一条 Penrose 方程的 $\{1\}$-inverse。这个等式表示：$A$ 原本能够产生的每个输出，经过 $G$ 再由 $A$ 映回后保持不变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$, a matrix $G\in\mathbb F^{n\times m}$ satisfying $AGA=A$ is called a generalized inverse of $A$, more precisely a $\{1\}$-inverse satisfying the first Penrose equation. Every output originally attainable through $A$ remains unchanged after passing through $G$ and then through $A$ again.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么它通常不唯一
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why it is generally non-unique*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如令
$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
G=\begin{bmatrix}1&a\\b&c\end{bmatrix}.
$$
对任意 $a,b,c$ 都有 $AGA=A$，所以同一个 $A$ 可以有许多广义逆。条件 $AGA=A$ 只约束 $G$ 如何处理 $A$ 真正使用的方向，并不固定其余自由度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$, every matrix $G=\begin{bmatrix}1&a\\b&c\end{bmatrix}$ satisfies $AGA=A$. Thus one matrix can have many generalized inverses. The equation constrains how $G$ acts on directions actually used by $A$ but does not determine its remaining degrees of freedom.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

此时
$$
AG=\begin{bmatrix}1&a\\0&0\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
GA=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\b&0\end{bmatrix}.
$$
两者都幂等，但在实数或复数的标准内积下未必是 Hermitian，因此可以表示斜投影而不是正交投影。一个 [[左逆]] 或 [[右逆]] 自动满足 $AGA=A$，但一般广义逆不必是任何一侧的逆。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed products $AG$ and $GA$ are both idempotent, but under the standard real or complex inner product they need not be Hermitian and may therefore represent oblique rather than orthogonal projections. A [[左逆|left inverse]] or [[右逆|right inverse]] automatically satisfies $AGA=A$, whereas a general $\{1\}$-inverse need not be a one-sided inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与 Moore–Penrose 伪逆的区别
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Difference from the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“广义逆”在不同文献中可能泛指满足若干逆性质的矩阵，因此使用时必须说明方程口径。[[Moore–Penrose 伪逆]] 在 $AGA=A$ 之外还要求反身条件 $GAG=G$ 以及两个 Hermitian 条件，从这个不唯一家族中选出与标准内积几何相容的规范矩阵。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The term “generalized inverse” can cover matrices satisfying different subsets of inverse-like conditions, so the defining equations must be stated. In addition to $AGA=A$, the [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse]] satisfies the reflexive condition $GAG=G$ and two Hermitian conditions, selecting the canonical member compatible with standard inner-product geometry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 只验证 $AGA=A$ 能否确定 $G$ 就是 Moore–Penrose 伪逆？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Does checking only $AGA=A$ establish that $G$ is the Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。它只说明 $G$ 是一个 $\{1\}$-inverse；这类矩阵通常不唯一，也不保证 $AG$ 与 $GA$ 是正交投影。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. It establishes only that $G$ is a $\{1\}$-inverse. Such matrices are generally non-unique and need not make $AG$ and $GA$ orthogonal projectors.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
