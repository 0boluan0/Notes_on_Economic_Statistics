<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Generalized inverses
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*广义逆：保住原本可达的输出*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix can lose information: the map $(x_1,x_2)^T\mapsto(x_1,0)^T$ forgets the second input. No inverse can recover that forgotten number from the output alone. A **generalized inverse** makes a weaker promise: given an output the matrix can actually produce, it chooses an input that produces that output again. It need not recover the original input or choose the shortest possible one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵可能丢失信息：映射 $(x_1,x_2)^T\mapsto(x_1,0)^T$ 会忘掉第二个输入，仅凭输出无法恢复那个数。**广义逆**作出一个较弱的承诺：给它一个原矩阵确实能够产生的输出，它会选一个能再次产生该输出的输入；既不必恢复原输入，也不必选最短的输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ have $m$ rows and $n$ columns, with real or complex entries and $m,n\ge1$. Inputs have $n$ coordinates and outputs have $m$, so a reverse map $G$ has size $n\times m$. This card uses the **one-condition**, or **$\{1\}$-inverse**, convention:

$$AGA=A.$$

The equality concerns matrix composition. If $b=Ax$ is any attainable output, then $AGb=AGAx=Ax=b$. Thus $Gb$ solves $Az=b$ whenever that equation has a solution. Conversely, preserving every attainable output gives $AGAx=Ax$ for every input $x$, hence the matrix identity. The set of attainable outputs is the [[列空间|column space]] $C(A)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 有 $m$ 行、$n$ 列，元素可为实数或复数，$m,n\ge1$。输入有 $n$ 个坐标，输出有 $m$ 个，所以反向映射 $G$ 为 $n\times m$。本卡采用**单条件广义逆**，也称 **$\{1\}$-逆**的口径，即上式 $AGA=A$。

该等式描述映射的复合。若 $b=Ax$ 是可达输出，则 $AGb=AGAx=Ax=b$，因此只要方程 $Az=b$ 有解，$Gb$ 就是一个解。反过来，若每个可达输出都被保住，那么对每个输入 $x$ 都有 $AGAx=Ax$，也就得到矩阵恒等式。全部可达输出组成[[列空间]] $C(A)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the reverse map is usually not unique
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么它通常不唯一

> [!example] The forgotten coordinate remains a choice / 被忘掉的坐标仍可自由选择
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the opening map, take
>
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> G=\begin{pmatrix}1&\alpha\\\beta&\gamma\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> The scalars $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ may be arbitrary real or complex numbers: direct multiplication gives $AGA=A$ for every choice. An attainable output is $b=(s,0)^T$. The chosen input is $Gb=(s,\beta s)^T$, and $A$ sends it back to $(s,0)^T$. The requirement fixes the first coordinate but lets the second vary. Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]] for writing a column vector; it does not conjugate $s$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对开头的映射，取上面的 $A,G$。标量 $\alpha,\beta,\gamma$ 可任取实数或复数，直接相乘始终得到 $AGA=A$。可达输出为 $b=(s,0)^T$；$G$ 选出的输入是 $(s,\beta s)^T$，再经 $A$ 仍得到 $(s,0)^T$。条件固定第一坐标，却允许第二坐标变化。这里 $T$ 是把坐标排成列的[[矩阵转置|转置]]，不对 $s$ 取共轭。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every finite real or complex matrix has a generalized inverse: its [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse]] is one particular choice. If $A$ is square and invertible, multiplying $AGA=A$ on both sides by the ordinary [[逆矩阵|inverse]] shows $G=A^{-1}$, so the choice is unique. At the other extreme, if $A=0$, every $n\times m$ matrix $G$ satisfies the equation. A [[左逆|left inverse]] ($GA=I_n$) or [[右逆|right inverse]] ($AG=I_m$) also satisfies $AGA=A$, where $I$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]], but neither one-sided identity is required in general.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个有限维实或复矩阵都有广义逆：其 [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆]]就是其中一个。若 $A$ 是可逆方阵，在 $AGA=A$ 左右分别乘普通[[逆矩阵]]便得 $G=A^{-1}$，此时唯一。另一个极端是 $A=0$，任何 $n\times m$ 的 $G$ 都满足条件。[[左逆]] $GA=I_n$ 或[[右逆]] $AG=I_m$ 也自动满足 $AGA=A$，其中 $I$ 是[[单位矩阵]]；但一般广义逆不要求任何一侧乘积等于单位矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The products $AG$ and $GA$ always satisfy $(AG)^2=AG$ and $(GA)^2=GA$: applying either twice is the same as applying it once. They are [[幂等矩阵|idempotent matrices]]. In the displayed family,

$$AG=\begin{pmatrix}1&\alpha\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
GA=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\\beta&0\end{pmatrix}.$$

They need not equal their conjugate transposes, so these projections need not be [[正交投影|orthogonal projections]]. In geometric language they can be oblique: the discarded direction need not be perpendicular to the retained one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘积 $AG$ 和 $GA$ 总满足 $(AG)^2=AG$、$(GA)^2=GA$：连续作用两次等于一次，因此是[[幂等矩阵]]。本例两个乘积如上，并不必等于各自的共轭转置，所以不必是[[正交投影]]。从几何上说，它们可以是斜投影：被舍去的方向未必垂直于保留方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the extra Moore–Penrose conditions accomplish
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与 Moore–Penrose 伪逆的区别

> [!warning] Solving an attainable equation is a limited promise / 能解可解方程，不等于具有两种最优性
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Choose $G=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ in the family above. For $b=(1,0)^T$, it returns $(1,1)^T$, an exact solution with length $\sqrt2$, while $(1,0)^T$ is an exact solution of length 1. It has not selected minimum norm.
>
> For the unattainable target $b=(0,1)^T$, it returns $(1,0)^T$. Its fitted output is $(1,0)^T$, leaving residual length $\sqrt2$. The zero input gives fitted output zero and residual length 1, so $Gb$ is not even a least-squares minimizer here. Length means the usual [[欧氏范数|Euclidean norm]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在上述家族中选 $G=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$。对 $b=(1,0)^T$，它返回长度为 $\sqrt2$ 的精确解 $(1,1)^T$，而 $(1,0)^T$ 也是精确解，长度仅为1，因此没有选最小范数。
>
> 对不可达目标 $b=(0,1)^T$，它返回 $(1,0)^T$，拟合输出也为 $(1,0)^T$，残差长度为 $\sqrt2$。零输入的拟合输出为零，残差长度仅为1，所以此时 $Gb$ 连最小二乘解都不是。这里长度使用通常[[欧氏范数]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|Moore–Penrose inverse]] adds $GAG=G$ and the requirements that $AG$ and $GA$ be [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]], meaning equal to their conjugate transposes. These four conditions select a unique matrix. It returns the [[最小范数最小二乘解|minimum-norm least-squares solution]] for every target, including unattainable ones. For $A=0$ that distinguished matrix is $G=0$, although every $G$ satisfies the single condition. Because authors sometimes use “generalized inverse” for different collections of conditions, always state which equations are required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Moore–Penrose 伪逆]]还增加 $GAG=G$，以及 $AG$、$GA$ 为 [[Hermitian 矩阵]]的要求，即等于各自共轭转置。四个条件共同选出唯一矩阵；它对每个目标，包括不可达目标，都返回[[最小范数最小二乘解]]。$A=0$ 时这一规范矩阵为 $G=0$，尽管任意 $G$ 都满足单条件。不同文献有时以“广义逆”指不同条件组合，因此使用时应说明要求哪些等式。
