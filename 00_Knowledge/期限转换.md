# 期限转换用较短期负债支持较长期资产并承担续融资风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Maturity transformation funds longer-term assets with shorter-term liabilities and bears refinancing risk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子机制
> 储蓄者往往不愿长期锁定全部资金，而住房、设备和企业投资需要多年融资。中介可以发行可随时支取或较短期到期的负债，同时持有较长期贷款和证券，让两边不必接受同一期限；代价是负债先到期或被撤回时，中介必须续借、寻找新资金或出售资产。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Savers often do not want to lock away all funds for years, while housing, equipment, and business investment need long-term finance. An intermediary can issue demandable or shorter-maturity liabilities while holding longer-maturity loans and securities, so the two sides need not accept the same horizon. The cost is refinancing risk: liabilities can mature or leave before assets repay, forcing the intermediary to renew funding, find replacements, or sell assets.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

期限转换的价值来自把不同人的时间偏好拼接起来。储户获得较早取回资金的合同权利，借款人则得到不必每天续借的长期资金。中介能否稳定完成这项服务，取决于资金来源是否分散稳定、资产现金流、可用抵押品、流动性储备以及在压力下能否获得后备融资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maturity transformation creates value by reconciling different time preferences. Savers receive a contractual ability to recover funds sooner, while borrowers obtain long-term finance without rolling it over every day. Stability depends on the diversity and persistence of funding, asset cash flows, available collateral, liquidity buffers, and access to backup funding under stress.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与流动性转换分开

本卡只定义合同到期日之间的错配。“今天能否迅速、低损失地变现”是另一个问题；两者的系统区分和反例见 [[期限转换不等于流动性转换]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This atom owns only the mismatch between contractual payment dates. Whether an asset can be turned into cash quickly and cheaply today is a separate question; see [[期限转换不等于流动性转换|the maturity-versus-liquidity distinction]] for the full comparison and counterexamples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一家机构用隔夜回购为五年期贷款融资。即使贷款最终全部还清，为什么机构仍可能在下周失败？
>
> **答案：** 资产的终局信用结果良好，不代表中途资金不断档。若隔夜出资者拒绝续作，机构必须立刻找到替代融资或折价出售尚未到期的贷款。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An institution funds five-year loans with overnight repos. Why can it fail next week even if every loan will ultimately repay?
>
> **Answer:** Good final credit outcomes do not ensure continuous funding. If overnight lenders refuse to roll over, the institution must immediately find replacement funding or sell unmatured loans at a discount.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Federal Reserve Board, Monetary Policy and Financial Stability, 2 October 2023](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/barr20231002a.htm)：核对金融中介以短期流动负债支持长期、低流动性资产，并由此在资产负债表上形成脆弱性。
- [Federal Reserve Board, Financial Stability Monitoring](https://www.federalreserve.gov/pubs/feds/2013/201321/index.html)：核对批发短期融资、期限转换与火售反馈之间的关系。
- [Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis, Traditional Versus Shadow Banking](https://www.stlouisfed.org/publications/page-one-economics/2012/02/01/traditional-versus-shadow-banking)：核对传统银行与影子银行都可能以短期资金支持长期贷款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Governor Barr's Federal Reserve speech was checked for short, liquid liabilities funding longer, less-liquid assets and the resulting balance-sheet vulnerabilities.
- The Federal Reserve's monitoring framework was checked for the link among wholesale short-term funding, maturity transformation, and fire-sale feedback.
- The St. Louis Fed note was checked for maturity transformation in both traditional and shadow banking.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
