# 金融中介集中筛选与监督能减少信息生产成本和搭便车
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Financial intermediaries reduce information-production costs and free riding through delegated screening and monitoring*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子机制
> 借款人通常比外部资金提供者更了解自己的质量和行动。若每位小额储蓄者都独立调查同一借款人，会重复付费；若调查结果必须公开，未付费者又能跟随已付费者交易，削弱生产信息的激励。中介把许多资金集中起来，由一个机构筛选借款人、设计契约并持续监督，再由该机构持有贷款，因而能够把信息收益保留在自己的资产组合中。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Borrowers usually know more than outside fund suppliers about their quality and actions. If every small saver investigates the same borrower, effort is duplicated; if the result must be public, non-paying investors can trade on information produced by others, weakening the incentive to produce it. An intermediary pools funds, screens borrowers, designs contracts, monitors them over time, and retains the loans, allowing the value of private information to remain inside its portfolio.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这项功能同时处理两类问题：放款前的筛选帮助缓解[[逆向选择]]，放款后的监督与[[限制性条款]]帮助缓解[[道德风险]]。它不要求中介“知道一切”，而是让专业调查、持续接触和执行合同的成本低于每个投资者各做一遍。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mechanism addresses two different problems. Pre-loan screening mitigates [[逆向选择|adverse selection]]; post-loan monitoring and [[限制性条款|covenants]] mitigate [[道德风险|moral hazard]]. It does not require the intermediary to know everything. It requires professional investigation, repeated contact, and enforcement to be cheaper than having every investor duplicate the work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

委托监督只在特定条件下具有净优势：中介必须比分散投资者更低成本地获取和使用信息；它还必须因持有足够风险敞口、声誉、契约或监管而有动机认真监督；委托人监督中介所新增的代理成本，也必须小于节省的重复调查成本和搭便车导致信息生产不足的损失。若发起人能迅速卖出贷款而几乎不留风险，或客户无法约束中介，这项优势可能缩小甚至反转。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Delegated monitoring has a net advantage only under conditions. The intermediary must acquire and use information more cheaply than dispersed investors; it must have incentives to monitor through sufficient retained exposure, reputation, contract, or regulation; and the new agency cost of monitoring the monitor must be smaller than the duplicated investigation cost and the loss from information underproduction caused by free riding. If an originator can rapidly sell a loan while retaining almost no exposure, or if customers cannot discipline the intermediary, the advantage can shrink or reverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中介并不会因此天然可信。储户或基金份额持有人也无法完整观察中介如何选资产，于是原来的“借款人—投资者”信息问题部分转化为“中介—客户、管理层、监管者”之间的代理问题。资本、治理、披露、审慎监管和中介自身声誉，都是约束这第二层问题的制度安排。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This does not make an intermediary inherently trustworthy. Depositors or fund shareholders cannot fully observe how it selects assets, so part of the original borrower-investor information problem becomes an agency problem among the intermediary, its customers, management, and regulators. Capital, governance, disclosure, prudential supervision, and the intermediary's own reputation constrain this second layer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与邻近概念的边界

- [[金融市场中的信息不对称与契约.canvas|信息不对称与契约总图]]展示问题及一般工具；本原子只解释“由中介代替大量资金提供者进行信息生产”这一组织机制。
- 公开评级、审计和证券研究也生产信息，但容易被其他投资者观察并利用；中介持有贷款时更容易把私有信息的收益内部化。
- 自动评分可扩展硬信息处理，却不必然替代依赖现场接触、经营背景和借款人关系的软信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The [[金融市场中的信息不对称与契约.canvas|asymmetric-information and contracts map]] shows the problem and the broader menu of responses; this atom isolates the organisational mechanism in which an intermediary produces information on behalf of many fund suppliers.
- Ratings, audits, and securities research also produce information, but other investors can more readily observe and use their output. Retaining a loan makes it easier for an intermediary to internalise the return on proprietary information.
- Automated scoring scales the processing of hard information but does not necessarily replace soft information gathered through context, site contact, and relationships.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么由一家银行调查并持有一笔贷款，可能比 1,000 名债券投资者各自调查同一企业更可持续？
>
> **答案：** 调查成本只需承担一次，银行还能通过自己持有的贷款保留调查收益；分散投资者既会重复付费，也可能等待别人生产可免费利用的信息。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can one bank investigating and retaining a loan be more sustainable than 1,000 bond investors investigating the same firm separately?
>
> **Answer:** The investigative cost is incurred once, and the bank can capture its benefit through the loan it holds. Dispersed investors would duplicate the cost and may instead wait to free-ride on information produced by others.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
