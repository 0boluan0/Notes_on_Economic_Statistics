# Affine hull
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*仿射包*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **affine hull** of a collection of points is the smallest flat space that contains them all. Two distinct points determine a whole line; three points that do not lie on one line determine a whole plane. “Flat space” here includes a single point and higher-dimensional versions of lines and planes. It need not pass through the origin.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一群点的**仿射包**，就是包含它们的最小平直空间。两个不同的点确定一整条直线；三个不共线的点确定一整片平面。“平直空间”也包括单个点，以及直线、平面的高维版本；它不必经过原点。

> [!example] Extend the segment · 把线段延长
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $p=(0,1)$ and $q=(2,1)$ in the plane. The line segment between them consists of $(t,1)$ with $0\le t\le2$. Its affine hull is the entire horizontal line $y=1$, including points such as $(-3,1)$ and $(5,1)$. Both the two endpoints alone and the filled segment have this same affine hull: the hull records their supporting flat space, not which parts of it were originally present.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在平面中取 $p=(0,1)$、$q=(2,1)$。两点之间的线段由 $0\le t\le2$ 时的 $(t,1)$ 构成；它的仿射包却是整条水平线 $y=1$，包括 $(-3,1)$、$(5,1)$ 等点。只取两个端点，或者取填满的线段，仿射包都相同：它记录的是承载这些点的平直空间，不是原集合填了其中哪些部分。

## Weights that add to one
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*系数之和等于一*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work in $\mathbb R^n$, the space of points with $n$ real coordinates. A set is **affine** when, together with any two of its points, it contains the whole line through them. Algebraically, it contains $\theta x+(1-\theta)y$ for every real $\theta$. Compare this with a [[凸集|convex set]], which only requires $0\le\theta\le1$ and therefore only the connecting segment. An affine combination of several points likewise has real coefficients whose sum is one; negative coefficients are allowed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在由 $n$ 个实坐标组成的空间 $\mathbb R^n$ 中讨论。一个集合称为**仿射集**，是指只要包含两点，就包含穿过它们的整条直线：对每个实数 $\theta$，都包含 $\theta x+(1-\theta)y$。[[凸集]]只要求 $0\le\theta\le1$，因此只要求中间线段。多个点的**仿射组合**同样使用总和为一的实系数，允许其中有负数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a set $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, its affine hull, written $\operatorname{aff}(C)$, consists of all finite affine combinations:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对集合 $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n$，它的仿射包记为 $\operatorname{aff}(C)$，由全部有限仿射组合构成：

$$
\operatorname{aff}(C)=
\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{k}\theta_i x_i:
 k\ge1,\ x_i\in C,\ \theta_i\in\mathbb R,\quad
 \sum_{i=1}^{k}\theta_i=1\right\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $k$ is a positive integer counting the chosen points; points may repeat. In the example, $(1-\theta)p+\theta q=(2\theta,1)$. Allowing every real $\theta$ gives the whole line. The restriction $0\le\theta\le1$ would give only the segment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的正整数 $k$ 计数所选点的个数，允许重复选点。在前面的例子中，$(1-\theta)p+\theta q=(2\theta,1)$；让 $\theta$ 遍历全部实数，就得到整条直线。若只允许 $0\le\theta\le1$，得到的才是线段。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This construction really is the smallest affine set containing $C$. Choosing one point with coefficient one shows $C\subseteq\operatorname{aff}(C)$. An affine combination of two finite affine combinations is again a finite combination with total coefficient one, so the hull is affine. Any affine set containing $C$ must contain all these combinations, so it must contain the entire hull. “Smallest” means inclusion in every such containing set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个构造确实是包含 $C$ 的最小仿射集。只取一个点、系数取一，说明 $C\subseteq\operatorname{aff}(C)$。两个有限仿射组合再做仿射组合，展开后仍是系数和为一的有限组合，所以仿射包本身是仿射集。任何包含 $C$ 的仿射集，又都必须包含这些组合，因此必须包含整个仿射包。“最小”指它包含在每一个合格的仿射集中。

## Choose a base point, then span the directions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先选基点，再张成位移方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nonempty $C$, choose a base point $x_0\in C$ and form the displacement vectors $x-x_0$. Their [[张成|linear span]] contains all finite linear combinations with unrestricted coefficients. The hull can be written as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $C$ 非空，选一个基点 $x_0\in C$，再形成各个位移向量 $x-x_0$。这些向量的[[张成|线性张成]]包含系数不受限制的全部有限线性组合。仿射包可以写成

$$
\operatorname{aff}(C)=x_0+\operatorname{span}\{x-x_0:x\in C\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see why, subtract $x_0$ from an affine combination: because the coefficients sum to one, the result is a linear combination of the displacements. Conversely, adding $x_0$ to $\sum_i a_i(x_i-x_0)$ gives $\sum_i a_i x_i+(1-\sum_i a_i)x_0$, whose coefficients sum to one. Thus the hull is a [[子空间|linear subspace]] shifted to a base point. Changing the chosen base point within $C$ does not change the resulting set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从一个仿射组合中减去 $x_0$，由于系数和为一，结果就是位移向量的线性组合。反过来，把 $x_0$ 加到 $\sum_i a_i(x_i-x_0)$ 上，得到 $\sum_i a_i x_i+(1-\sum_i a_i)x_0$，各系数的和仍为一。因此，仿射包是一个平移到基点上的[[子空间|线性子空间]]；在 $C$ 中换选基点，不会改变最终集合。

> [!warning] The origin is not automatically added · 不会自动补入原点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $p=(0,1)$ and $q=(2,1)$, the affine hull is $y=1$, which excludes the origin. Their linear span is instead all of $\mathbb R^2$: any $(u,v)$ equals $(v-u/2)p+(u/2)q$. A linear span has no sum-to-one restriction, so it answers a different question.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $p=(0,1)$、$q=(2,1)$，仿射包是 $y=1$，不含原点；它们的线性张成却是整个 $\mathbb R^2$，因为任意 $(u,v)$ 都等于 $(v-u/2)p+(u/2)q$。线性张成不限制系数之和，因此回答的是另一个问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single point has itself as its affine hull: all coefficients sum to one, so every combination returns that point. With the finite-combination definition above, $\operatorname{aff}(\varnothing)=\varnothing$, since there is no point to choose; the base-point formula is used only for nonempty sets. Once the hull is known, [[相对内部|relative interior]] asks which points have a small surrounding region inside $C$ when motion is restricted to this hull.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单点集的仿射包还是该单点，因为系数和为一时，每个组合都返回同一点。按上面的有限组合定义，$\operatorname{aff}(\varnothing)=\varnothing$，因为无点可选；基点公式只用于非空集合。确定仿射包之后，[[相对内部]]进一步问：把活动范围限制在这个仿射包内，哪些点周围仍有一小块区域完全留在 $C$ 中？
