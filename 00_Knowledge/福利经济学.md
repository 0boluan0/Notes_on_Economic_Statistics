# 福利经济学研究如何按明确的福利标准评价经济配置与政策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Welfare economics evaluates economic allocations and policies using explicit welfare criteria*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

福利经济学问的是：在可行的资源配置或政策之间，哪些结果更值得选择，依据是什么？它先了解每个人受到的影响，再按明确的评价标准比较结果。这里的“福利”是人的境况或福祉，不专指政府发放的福利金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Welfare economics asks which feasible allocations or policies are more desirable, and why. It identifies how people are affected and compares outcomes using an explicit evaluative criterion. “Welfare” means well-being, not specifically government benefit payments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一政策可以降低一些人的支出、提高另一些人的成本。预测这些变化属于[[实证分析]]；判断这样做是否值得属于[[规范分析]]。福利经济学把两者接起来，而不把某一个可观察指标当作社会目标本身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A policy can lower some people's expenses while raising others' costs. Predicting those changes is [[实证分析|positive analysis]]; deciding whether they are desirable is [[规范分析|normative analysis]]. Welfare economics connects the two without treating a single observed indicator as the social objective itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，[[Pareto改进与有效|Pareto 标准]]能认可“至少一人更好、无人更差”的变化，却不能替有赢家和输家的所有方案排序。要作进一步比较，就需要说明采用的[[社会福利函数|福利评价规则]]或其他规范标准。不同标准可能对同一组实证后果给出不同判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, the [[Pareto改进与有效|Pareto criterion]] endorses a change that benefits someone without harming anyone, but does not rank every pair of policies with winners and losers. Further comparisons require a [[社会福利函数|social welfare rule]] or another stated normative criterion. Different criteria can evaluate the same positive consequences differently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
