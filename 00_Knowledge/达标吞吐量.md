# 达标吞吐量是在指定时间窗口内满足服务条件的有效工作量除以窗口时长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Goodput is the amount of work meeting declared service conditions divided by the observation-window duration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

达标吞吐量（goodput）只把满足已声明服务条件的工作计入有效产出。这里先按**完整请求**计数：在长度为 $\Delta t$ 的观测窗口内，令 $R$ 为该窗口完成的请求集合，$g(r)=1$ 表示请求成功完成且满足事先规定的条件，否则为 0，则

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Goodput counts work that meets declared service conditions. Use **complete requests** as the unit here. For completed requests $R$ in a window of duration $\Delta t$, let $g(r)=1$ only when request $r$ successfully meets the predefined conditions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$G_{\rm req}=\frac{\sum_{r\in R}g(r)}{\Delta t}\quad\text{requests/s}.$$

条件可以是客户端 [[首Token延迟|TTFT]] 不超过 1 秒，且请求 [[平均输出Token时间|TPOT]] 不超过 50 ms/token。要逐请求检查两项的**交集**：TTFT 有 90% 达标、TPOT 也有 90% 达标，并不保证同一批 90% 请求同时达标。条件使用哪种时间点、是否包含只有一个输出 token 的请求，都应先确定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conditions might require client TTFT at most one second and request TPOT at most 50 ms/token. Check their **intersection per request**: separate 90% attainment rates do not imply that the same 90% meet both. Define timing boundaries and the treatment of single-token outputs beforehand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 10 秒完成 100 个请求，其中 80 个同时满足条件，则总 [[推理吞吐率|请求吞吐]]是 10 requests/s，达标吞吐量为 8 requests/s。另一配置完成 120 个，却只有 60 个达标，其总吞吐更高、达标吞吐却只有 6。两个构造结果说明必须同时看交付数量和服务约束。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If 100 requests finish in ten seconds and 80 meet all conditions, total [[推理吞吐率|request throughput]] is 10 requests/s and goodput is 8. Another configuration finishing 120 with only 60 qualifying has higher total throughput but goodput of only 6. These constructed counts distinguish volume from usable delivery.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“goodput”的论文口径不完全统一。有的统计达标请求的输出 token 数，公式分子变为 $\sum_r g(r)N_r$，单位也变为 token/s；DistServe 则用满足指定达标比例时的**最大可承受请求率**衡量容量，再按 GPU 数归一化。它们都考虑服务条件，但不是上面一次窗口内的请求计数，比较前必须核对定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Papers use different conventions. Token-weighted goodput replaces the numerator with $\sum_r g(r)N_r$ and has units token/s. DistServe instead measures the maximum request rate supportable at a specified attainment target and normalizes by GPU count. These concern service constraints but are not the same as a window's qualifying-request count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个指标也不包含全部体验：稍微超时但完整回答的请求仍可能有价值，丢弃慢请求则可能损害用户。报告时应一起给出提交量、失败/取消/超时及延迟分布，不能通过少接或丢掉难请求制造表面改善。答案是否正确还需单独的质量验收，见 [[推理性能可比性]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This metric does not capture all experience: a late complete answer can remain valuable, while dropping slow requests can harm users. Report submitted load, failures, cancellations, timeouts, and latency distributions. Answer quality requires separate acceptance under [[推理性能可比性|matched performance evaluation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Wang et al., *Revisiting Service Level Objectives and System Level Metrics in Large Language Model Serving*](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.14257v2)，§1、§4.1–4.2：支持按服务条件筛选有效产出及丢弃慢请求的指标局限。原文文字说请求数，而 Eq.7 含 $N_r$ 为 token 加权；本卡显式区分两种计数，未混用其单位。
- [Zhong et al. (2024), *DistServe*](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/osdi24-zhong-yinmin.pdf)，§1–2、§4.1：支持达标比例约束下的最大请求率及 per-GPU 容量口径。窗口算例与交集提醒为定义的直接应用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first paper motivates qualifying output and documents metric limitations; its prose and token-weighted equation use different units, distinguished here. DistServe supplies the attainment-constrained capacity convention. The counting examples and intersection check follow directly from the definitions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
