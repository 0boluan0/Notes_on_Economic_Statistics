# Congruence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*同余*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Seventeen and five leave the same remainder when divided by twelve: $17=1\cdot12+5$ and $5=0\cdot12+5$. **Congruence** records this agreement of remainders. It lets us group integers by a fixed repeating cycle while still remembering that the integers themselves can be different.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

十七和五除以十二时余数相同：$17=1\cdot12+5$，$5=0\cdot12+5$。**同余**记录的就是这种余数相同的关系。它按固定周期把整数归类，同时并不把不同整数说成同一个数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A difference that is a whole multiple
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*差恰好是模数的整数倍*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix an integer $n>1$, called the **modulus**, and let $a,b$ be any integers, including negative integers or zero. The notation $n\mid(a-b)$ uses [[整除|divisibility]]: the difference is an exact integer multiple of $n$. Read $a\equiv b\pmod n$ as “$a$ is congruent to $b$ modulo $n$”; the modulus belongs to the comparison of both sides.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定一个整数 $n>1$，称为**模数**；$a,b$ 可以是任意整数，包括负数和零。记号 $n\mid(a-b)$ 使用[[整除]]关系，表示差恰好是 $n$ 的整数倍。$a\equiv b\pmod n$ 读作“$a$ 与 $b$ 模 $n$ 同余”；模数作用于两边之间的比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For integers $a,b$ and an integer modulus $n>1$, **congruence** $a\equiv b\pmod n$ means $n\mid(a-b)$, or equivalently $a-b=kn$ for some integer $k$. Congruence compares residue classes; it does not assert equality of the integers themselves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对整数 $a,b$ 和整数模数 $n>1$，**同余** $a\equiv b\pmod n$ 表示 $n\mid(a-b)$，也就是 $a-b=kn$ 对某个整数 $k$ 成立。同余比较的是整数所属的余数类，不是说两个整数本身相等。

> [!example] Equality and congruence · 相等与同余
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $17\equiv5\pmod{12}$ because $17-5=12$, although $17\ne5$. But $17$ and $5$ are not congruent modulo $7$, because $12$ is not an integer multiple of $7$. Equal integers are congruent for every allowed modulus, since their difference is zero; the converse depends on the modulus and is generally false.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $17\equiv5\pmod{12}$，因为 $17-5=12$，但 $17\ne5$。它们模 $7$ 却不同余，因为 $12$ 不是 $7$ 的整数倍。相等的两个整数对每个允许的模数都同余，因为差为零；反方向一般不成立，不能省略模数。

## Why equal remainders give exactly this relation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么它恰好表示余数相同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By the [[带余除法|division algorithm]], write the standard remainder as $\operatorname{rem}(a,n)\in\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 [[带余除法]]，把标准余数记为 $\operatorname{rem}(a,n)\in\{0,\ldots,n-1\}$，则

$$
a\equiv b\pmod n
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{rem}(a,n)=\operatorname{rem}(b,n).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[带余除法|division theorem]] gives unique integers $q_1,r_1,q_2,r_2$ with $a=q_1n+r_1$, $b=q_2n+r_2$ and $0\le r_1,r_2<n$. Here the $q$ symbols are quotients and the $r$ symbols are remainders. Subtracting removes the parts that are already multiples of $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[带余除法]]给出唯一的整数商与余数，使 $a=q_1n+r_1$、$b=q_2n+r_2$，且 $0\le r_1,r_2<n$。其中 $q$ 表示商，$r$ 表示余数；把两式相减，就把已经是 $n$ 的整数倍的部分分离出来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indeed, writing $a=q_1n+r_1$ and $b=q_2n+r_2$ gives $a-b=(q_1-q_2)n+(r_1-r_2)$. Since $-n<r_1-r_2<n$, the remainder difference is divisible by $n$ exactly when it is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理由是写 $a=q_1n+r_1$、$b=q_2n+r_2$ 后，$a-b=(q_1-q_2)n+(r_1-r_2)$。因为 $-n<r_1-r_2<n$，这个余数差能被 $n$ 整除当且仅当它为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More explicitly, if $r_1=r_2$, then $a-b=(q_1-q_2)n$, proving congruence. Conversely, if $n\mid(a-b)$, subtracting $(q_1-q_2)n$ shows $n\mid(r_1-r_2)$. A nonzero integer multiple of positive $n$ has absolute value at least $n$, whereas $|r_1-r_2|<n$. Thus this difference must be zero. These two directions cover all integer inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体地说，若 $r_1=r_2$，则 $a-b=(q_1-q_2)n$，所以同余。反过来，若 $n\mid(a-b)$，减去 $(q_1-q_2)n$ 后可得 $n\mid(r_1-r_2)$。正数 $n$ 的非零整数倍绝对值至少为 $n$，但这里 $|r_1-r_2|<n$，因此差只能为零。这两个方向覆盖所有整数输入。

> [!example] Negative inputs use the same remainder range · 负数也用同一余数范围
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $-1=(-1)\cdot7+6$, $6=0\cdot7+6$ and $13=1\cdot7+6$. Their quotients differ, but their standard remainder is $6$. In contrast, $-1$ and $1$ are not congruent modulo $7$: their remainders are $6$ and $1$. Congruence is not a comparison of absolute values.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $-1=(-1)\cdot7+6$、$6=0\cdot7+6$、$13=1\cdot7+6$，商不同，但标准余数都是 $6$。相反，$-1$ 与 $1$ 模 $7$ 不同余，余数分别是 $6$ 和 $1$。同余不是比较绝对值。

## One relation, and the classes it creates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个关系及其形成的类*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A relation is a rule comparing two objects. An [[等价关系|equivalence relation]] has three properties that make grouping consistent: each object belongs with itself, the comparison can be reversed, and two comparisons can be joined. For a fixed modulus $n$, congruence has all three. Reflexivity follows from $a-a=0\cdot n$. If $a-b=kn$, then $b-a=(-k)n$, proving symmetry. If also $b-c=\ell n$, then $a-c=(k+\ell)n$, proving transitivity. The same $n$ is used throughout.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关系是一条比较两个对象的规则。[[等价关系]]有三项保证分类一致的性质：每个对象与自己同类，比较可以反向，两次比较可以接起来。固定模数 $n$ 后，同余满足这三条。由 $a-a=0\cdot n$ 得自反性；若 $a-b=kn$，则 $b-a=(-k)n$，得到对称性；若还有 $b-c=\ell n$，则 $a-c=(k+\ell)n$，得到传递性。整个推理必须使用同一个 $n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Congruence is reflexive, symmetric and transitive, so it partitions $\mathbb Z$ into exactly $n$ residue classes: $[r]_n=\{r+kn:k\in\mathbb Z\}$, with $0\le r<n$. For example, $-1\equiv6\equiv13\pmod7$: all three have standard remainder six, although they are distinct integers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同余具有自反性、对称性和传递性，因而把 $\mathbb Z$ 分成恰好 $n$ 个余数类：$[r]_n=\{r+kn:k\in\mathbb Z\}$，其中 $0\le r<n$。例如 $-1\equiv6\equiv13\pmod7$；三者的标准余数都是 6，但它们是不同的整数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mathbb Z$ is the set of all integers. The brackets $[r]_n$ denote the **whole set** of integers congruent to $r$, called its residue class; $r$ is one representative. Each integer has exactly one standard remainder, so every integer belongs to one of $[0]_n,\ldots,[n-1]_n$. Two different standard remainders cannot be congruent by the remainder test, so these $n$ classes are distinct and do not overlap. For example, $[-1]_7=[6]_7=[13]_7$, even though $-1,6,13$ are different representatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\mathbb Z$ 表示全体整数。方括号 $[r]_n$ 表示与 $r$ 同余的**整组整数**，称为同余类；$r$ 只是其中一个代表。每个整数恰有一个标准余数，因此必属于 $[0]_n,\ldots,[n-1]_n$ 中的一类。不同的标准余数由余数判别可知不可能同余，所以这 $n$ 个类不同且互不重叠。例如 $[-1]_7=[6]_7=[13]_7$，虽然 $-1,6,13$ 是不同的代表。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish the objects: $a\equiv b\pmod n$ is a relational statement, whereas $\operatorname{rem}(a,n)$ is a particular integer. [[模运算|Modular arithmetic]] explains why calculations may use remainder representatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区分两个对象：$a\equiv b\pmod n$ 是一个关系命题；$\operatorname{rem}(a,n)$ 是一个确定的整数值。[[模运算]] 说明为何可以用余数代表元进行计算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The restriction $n>1$ is the convention used here and in the linked course. The divisibility rule could also define congruence modulo $1$, in which case every integer belongs to the single class; that is outside this card’s main scope. Modulus zero does not give the standard remainder range $0\le r<n$. Keeping the convention explicit prevents these different boundary cases from being silently mixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里及所链课程采用 $n>1$ 的约定。若把整除规则扩展到模 $1$，所有整数都会落入同一个类，但这不属于本卡主范围。模数为零则没有标准余数范围 $0\le r<n$。明确约定，可以避免把这些不同边界悄悄混在一起。
