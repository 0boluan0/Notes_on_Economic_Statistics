# 正规化政策应按相对反事实的企业与劳动者净改善评价而不只看登记数

评价正规化政策，先说明改变的是企业登记、税务身份还是[[非正规就业|岗位保护]]，再区分[[项目投入产出与结果|行政交付与最终结果]]。登记增加可以证明某项手续完成，却不能单独证明企业生产率、收入或劳动者保障改善；判断净改善还须比较[[公共项目反事实|没有该政策时的结果]]以及实施、合规等资源成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify whether a formalization policy changes business registration, tax status, or [[非正规就业|job protection]], then distinguish [[项目投入产出与结果|administrative outputs from outcomes]]. More registrations document completed procedures, not necessarily higher productivity, income, or worker protection. Net improvement requires comparison with the [[公共项目反事实|without-policy counterfactual]] and resource costs of implementation and compliance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个有边界的例子是 de Mel、McKenzie 与 Woodruff 的斯里兰卡登记实验：研究对象为科伦坡和康提已有、尚未在 Divisional Secretariat 登记的企业，筛选范围包括 1–14 名有薪雇员、男性或夫妻共同所有，排除季节性农业和渔业。提供信息并报销直接登记费未显著增加登记，而附加现金激励提高了登记。这检验的是该地既有企业的一种登记安排，不是所有正规化政策。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In de Mel, McKenzie, and Woodruff's Sri Lankan experiment, eligible existing firms in Colombo and Kandy were not registered with the Divisional Secretariat, had 1–14 paid employees, and were male-owned or jointly owned by spouses; seasonal agriculture and fisheries were excluded. Information plus reimbursement of registration fees did not significantly increase registration; additional cash incentives did. This tests a specific registration arrangement for existing firms, not formalization policies universally.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

后续利润分布的主要变化集中在少数高增长企业，不能把均值增益读成每家企业都受益；现金本身还可能影响资本和利润，因此“鼓励登记方案的效果”与“纯粹登记的效果”不能无条件等同。研究对登记因果效应使用了额外识别假设，并讨论这一限制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changes in the subsequent profit distribution were concentrated among a few fast-growing firms, so a mean gain does not imply gains for every firm. Cash may itself affect capital and profits; the registration-encouragement package and registration alone therefore need not have identical effects. The study uses additional identification assumptions and discusses this limitation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

推广时还应检查[[非正规标签与经营约束|谁进入样本、谁响应政策]]，以及企业存续、就业与保障、收入分布和公共资源使用的变化。该实验没有据此识别新企业进入或完整社会净收益；某个结果不显著，也不等于已经证明效应为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For broader application, check [[非正规标签与经营约束|sample composition and policy responsiveness]], alongside survival, employment and protection, income distribution, and public resource use. The experiment does not thereby identify effects on new-firm entry or complete social net benefits. A statistically insignificant result is not proof of a zero effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
