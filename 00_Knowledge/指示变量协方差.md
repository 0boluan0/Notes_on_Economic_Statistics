# Covariance of event indicators
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*指示变量协方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two yes-or-no events, **indicator covariance** compares how often they happen together with how often they would happen together under independence. Positive covariance means more overlap than that benchmark; negative covariance means less. For two indicators specifically, equality with the benchmark is enough to establish independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对两个“是或否”的事件，**指示变量协方差**把它们实际同时发生的概率，与独立时应有的同时发生概率相比较。实际重叠更多，协方差为正；更少则为负。对两个指示变量而言，恰好等于这个基准就足以建立独立性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $I_A,I_B$ be the [[事件指示变量|indicators]] of events $A,B$. Write $p=P(A)$, $q=P(B)$ and $r=P(A\cap B)$, where $A\cap B$ means both events occur. [[协方差|Covariance]] can be calculated as a product average minus the product of the two averages. Here the [[指示变量期望|individual averages]] are $p,q$, and the [[指示变量的交事件乘积|product average]] is $r$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $I_A,I_B$ 是事件 $A,B$ 的[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]。记 $p=P(A)$、$q=P(B)$、$r=P(A\cap B)$，其中 $A\cap B$ 表示同时发生。[[协方差]]可以用“乘积的平均减去两个平均的乘积”计算；这里[[指示变量期望|各自的平均]]为 $p,q$，[[指示变量的交事件乘积|乘积的平均]]为 $r$，所以

> [!abstract] Actual overlap minus the independent benchmark · 实际重叠减去独立基准
> $$\operatorname{Cov}(I_A,I_B)=E[I_AI_B]-E[I_A]E[I_B]=r-pq.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Indicators are bounded, so all expectations in this formula are finite. The benchmark $pq$ is a comparison value, not an assumption that the events really are independent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 指示变量有界，因此这里所有期望都有限。$pq$ 是用于比较的基准，并不是先假定事件真的独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For fixed marginal probabilities $p=0.4$ and $q=0.5$, the benchmark is $0.2$. The following are three different valid joint models with those same marginals:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定边际概率 $p=0.4$、$q=0.5$，独立基准就是 $0.2$。下面三个不同的联合模型都具有这两个边际概率：

| $r=P(A\cap B)$ | $pq$ | $\operatorname{Cov}(I_A,I_B)$ |
|---|---|---|
| $0.3$ | $0.2$ | $0.1$ |
| $0.2$ | $0.2$ | $0$ |
| $0.1$ | $0.2$ | $-0.1$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the last model, both events occur only half as often as under the independence benchmark. The negative covariance is $0.1-0.4(0.5)=-0.1$; it does not mean that the intersection has a negative probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一个模型中，同时发生的概率只有独立基准的一半。负协方差为 $0.1-0.4(0.5)=-0.1$，并不是说交事件有负概率。

## Why zero covariance is enough for two indicators
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么两个指标的零协方差足以推出独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero covariance is equivalent to $r=pq$, which is exactly the [[事件独立|independence condition for the two events]]. It also makes the two zero-or-one variables independent. To see the whole joint distribution, the four cell probabilities are determined by $p,q,r$. When $r=pq$, they become
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零协方差等价于 $r=pq$，这正是[[事件独立|两个事件的独立条件]]，同时也使这两个零一变量独立。完整联合分布的四个格子都由 $p,q,r$ 决定；当 $r=pq$ 时，它们变为

| $(I_A,I_B)$ | Joint probability / 联合概率 | When $r=pq$ / 此时 |
|---|---|---|
| $(1,1)$ | $r$ | $pq$ |
| $(1,0)$ | $p-r$ | $p(1-q)$ |
| $(0,1)$ | $q-r$ | $(1-p)q$ |
| $(0,0)$ | $1-p-q+r$ | $(1-p)(1-q)$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every cell factors into its two marginal probabilities, giving the [[随机变量独立的分布判据|discrete independence criterion]]. This reasoning includes probabilities zero or one. For general random variables, a single covariance does not determine all their joint probabilities, so [[零协方差不推独立|zero covariance need not imply independence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个格子都分解为两个边际概率的乘积，满足[[随机变量独立的分布判据|离散独立性判据]]。概率为零或一时，这套推理也成立。对一般随机变量，一个协方差无法决定全部联合概率，因此[[零协方差不推独立|零协方差未必推出独立]]。

> [!warning] Pairwise conclusions stay pairwise · 逐对结论仍然只约束逐对关系
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If every pair in a collection of indicators has zero covariance, every pair is independent. The collection need not be [[相互独立|mutually independent]]. For example, independently toss two fair coins and record whether the first is heads, the second is heads, and the two results match. Each indicator has mean $1/2$ and each pair has covariance zero, but the three indicators are all one with probability $1/4$, not $1/8$. The complete table is in [[两两独立不推出相互独立|the pairwise-independence counterexample]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果一组指标每对的协方差都为零，那么每对都独立，但整组未必[[相互独立]]。例如，独立抛两次公平硬币，分别记录第一次是否正面、第二次是否正面、两次是否相同。每个指标均值为 $1/2$，每对协方差都为零，但三个指标同时为一的概率是 $1/4$，而非 $1/8$。完整表格见[[两两独立不推出相互独立|两两独立反例]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a [[随机计数的指标和|count built from indicators]], pairwise zero covariances are enough to remove all cross terms in the [[和的方差协方差项|variance-of-a-sum formula]]. They do not generally make the count binomial: in the coin example, the count is three with probability $1/4$ and one with probability $3/4$, rather than having a $\operatorname{Binomial}(3,1/2)$ law. Sharing a person or another underlying object also does not determine a covariance; that modelling issue is explained in [[共享索引不决定协方差|shared indices and covariance]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[随机计数的指标和|由指标组成的计数]]，逐对零协方差已足以消去[[和的方差协方差项|和的方差公式]]里的交叉项，却一般不能保证计数服从二项分布：上述硬币模型中，计数以 $1/4$ 的概率为三、以 $3/4$ 的概率为一，并不服从 $\operatorname{Binomial}(3,1/2)$。两个指标涉及同一个人或底层对象，也不能据此确定协方差；这个建模问题见[[共享索引不决定协方差]]。
