# Information at a stopping time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*停时信息集*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **information at a stopping time** records everything that can be decided when an observation rule tells us to stop. If we stop tossing coins at the first head, the time of that head and all tosses seen so far are known; later tosses are still unknown. The stopping time changes from one outcome to another, but the information available under that rule must still be described by one mathematical object, written $\mathcal F_T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**停时信息集**记录的是：当观察规则让我们停止时，已经能够判断的事情。例如在第一次出现正面时停止抛硬币，此时知道首次正面出现的时间，也知道已经看到的全部结果，但还不知道之后的抛掷。停止时刻随结果而变，但“按这个规则停止时可用的信息”仍需要由同一个数学对象 $\mathcal F_T$ 描述。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[滤过定义|filtration]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ is the growing collection of information available up to each fixed time $t$. Each $\mathcal F_t$ is a [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]]: a collection of events closed under complements and countable unions. A [[停时定义|stopping time]] $T$ satisfies $\{T\le t\}\in\mathcal F_t$: by time $t$ we can tell whether stopping has already happened.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[滤过定义|滤过]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ 是截至各个固定时刻 $t$ 可用、随时间增加的信息。每个 $\mathcal F_t$ 都是一个[[σ-代数]]，即对补集和可数并封闭的事件集合。[[停时定义|停时]] $T$ 满足 $\{T\le t\}\in\mathcal F_t$：到了 $t$，能够判断停止是否已经发生。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check an event on the paths that have already stopped
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*只在已经停止的路径上检查事件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work on a probability space $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\Pr)$, where $\Omega$ contains possible outcomes and $\mathcal F$ is the ambient collection of events. Time may be discrete, $t\in\mathbb N_0$, or continuous, $t\in[0,\infty)$. Allow $T=\infty$ when stopping never occurs. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在概率空间 $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\Pr)$ 上，$\Omega$ 是所有可能结果，$\mathcal F$ 是环境事件集合。时间可以是离散的 $t\in\mathbb N_0$，也可以是连续的 $t\in[0,\infty)$；永不停止时允许 $T=\infty$。定义

$$
\mathcal F_T
=\left\{A\in\mathcal F:
A\cap\{T\le t\}\in\mathcal F_t
\text{ for every time }t\right\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $A$ is the event we want to know. The intersection $A\cap\{T\le t\}$ says both “the rule has stopped by $t$” and “$A$ is true”. The definition asks that this combined event can be decided using information available by $t$, for every $t$. It does not demand that $A$ itself be known at every early time, when the rule may not yet have stopped.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $A$ 是想判断的事件。交集 $A\cap\{T\le t\}$ 同时表示“规则已在 $t$ 前停止”和“$A$ 发生”。定义要求：对每个 $t$，都能用截至 $t$ 的信息判断这个合并事件。它没有要求在尚未停止的早期时刻就已经知道整个 $A$。

> [!example] Stop after one or three tosses · 抛一次或三次后停止
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Toss a coin up to three times, and let $\mathcal F_n$ reveal the first $n$ results. Stop at $T=1$ if the first toss is heads; otherwise stop at $T=3$. The event “the first toss is heads, or it is tails and the third toss is heads” belongs to $\mathcal F_T$: on the early-stop branch we already know it is true, and on the late-stop branch we know the third result. In contrast, “the third toss is heads” does not belong to $\mathcal F_T$, because on paths stopping after an initial head the third result has not been observed. Assume every three-toss sequence is possible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 最多抛三次硬币，$\mathcal F_n$ 显示前 $n$ 次结果。若第一次为正面就令 $T=1$，否则令 $T=3$。“第一次为正面，或者第一次为反面且第三次为正面”属于 $\mathcal F_T$：提前停止的分支上已经知道它为真，晚停止的分支上已经看到第三次。但“第三次为正面”不属于 $\mathcal F_T$，因为第一次为正面就停止的路径还没有看到第三次。这里假设八种三次抛掷序列均可能发生。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why it is a sigma-algebra
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为何它确实是一个σ代数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The whole sample space belongs to $\mathcal F_T$ because $\Omega\cap\{T\le t\}=\{T\le t\}\in\mathcal F_t$. If $A$ passes the test, its complement does too, since $A^c\cap\{T\le t\}$ is $\{T\le t\}$ minus $A\cap\{T\le t\}$. Finally, intersections with $\{T\le t\}$ distribute over countable unions, so the test is preserved under countable unions. These are exactly the sigma-algebra requirements. This definition needs no extra right-continuity assumption on the filtration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

整个样本空间属于 $\mathcal F_T$，因为 $\Omega\cap\{T\le t\}=\{T\le t\}\in\mathcal F_t$。若 $A$ 通过检验，其补集也通过：$A^c\cap\{T\le t\}$ 就是从 $\{T\le t\}$ 中减去 $A\cap\{T\le t\}$。最后，与 $\{T\le t\}$ 相交可以分配到可数并中，因此可数并也保留检验条件。这恰好是σ代数的要求。这个定义本身不需要额外假设滤过右连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The discrete branch form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 离散分支的等价写法

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In discrete time, the definition is equivalent to requiring $A\in\mathcal F$ and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在离散时间中，定义等价于要求 $A\in\mathcal F$ 且

$$
A\cap\{T=n\}\in\mathcal F_n\qquad\text{for every }n\ge0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To obtain an exact stopping branch from the original definition, subtract $A\cap\{T\le n-1\}$ from $A\cap\{T\le n\}$; at $n=0$ use the first event directly. Conversely, the union of the branches $k=0,\ldots,n$ gives $A\cap\{T\le n\}$. Thus for $T\in\{1,3\}$, check $A\cap\{T=1\}\in\mathcal F_1$ and $A\cap\{T=3\}\in\mathcal F_3$. Different branches use the information available when that branch stops.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从原定义得到恰好停止的分支，只需用 $A\cap\{T\le n\}$ 减去 $A\cap\{T\le n-1\}$；$n=0$ 时直接用第一项。反过来，对 $k=0,\ldots,n$ 的分支取有限并即可得到 $A\cap\{T\le n\}$。因此当 $T\in\{1,3\}$ 时，分别检查 $A\cap\{T=1\}\in\mathcal F_1$ 和 $A\cap\{T=3\}\in\mathcal F_3$。不同分支使用各自停止时已有的信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a deterministic time $T\equiv m$, this reduces to $\mathcal F_T=\mathcal F_m$. For continuous time, keep the $\{T\le t\}$ definition: a union over all exact-time branches $\{T=t\}$ may be uncountable, and sigma-algebras guarantee closure only under countable unions. The discrete branch test cannot simply be copied with a real number replacing $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $T\equiv m$ 是确定时刻，定义退化为 $\mathcal F_T=\mathcal F_m$。连续时间仍使用 $\{T\le t\}$ 的定义：对所有实数停止时刻分别取分支再合并，可能涉及不可数并，而σ代数只保证对可数并封闭。因此不能简单地把离散分支检验中的 $n$ 换成实数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We use the ambient condition $A\in\mathcal F$. Some texts instead work inside $\mathcal F_\infty=\sigma(\bigcup_t\mathcal F_t)$, the information generated by all finite times. If $\mathcal F=\mathcal F_\infty$ these conventions agree. If $\mathcal F$ is larger, its version permits any ambient event on the never-stop branch: finite-time intersections do not inspect that branch. In particular, when $\Pr(T=\infty)>0$, the two ambient choices can allow different events there. Keep the ambient space explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里采用环境条件 $A\in\mathcal F$。有些教材直接在 $\mathcal F_\infty=\sigma(\bigcup_t\mathcal F_t)$ 中工作，即全部有限时刻生成的信息。若 $\mathcal F=\mathcal F_\infty$，两种约定一致。若 $\mathcal F$ 更大，它的版本会在永不停止的分支上允许任意环境事件，因为有限时刻的交集根本没有检查那个分支。特别是 $\Pr(T=\infty)>0$ 时，两种环境选择可能在那里允许不同事件，因而应说清楚使用哪一个环境空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One fixed information object
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不能逐路径写成 $\mathcal F_{T(\omega)}$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The expression $\mathcal F_{T(\omega)}$ changes with the sample point $\omega$. It is useful intuition for which fixed-time information we consult on one path, but it is not itself a fixed sigma-algebra on $\Omega$. The definition of $\mathcal F_T$ checks the same event across all relevant stopping branches and collects all events that pass.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\mathcal F_{T(\omega)}$ 随样本点 $\omega$ 改变，可以提示某条路径上需要查看哪一层固定时刻信息，却不能直接充当 $\Omega$ 上一个固定的σ代数。$\mathcal F_T$ 的定义跨所有相关停止分支检查同一个事件，再收集所有通过检验的事件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The stopping time itself is known at stopping: for each $a\ge0$, $\{T\le a\}\cap\{T\le t\}=\{T\le\min(a,t)\}\in\mathcal F_t$, so $T$ is $\mathcal F_T$-measurable. Statements such as the [[CTMC强Markov性|strong Markov property]] condition the future on this single information object. The separate [[停时信息单调性|discrete-time information monotonicity theorem]] explains the comparison at two ordered stopping times in the discrete setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

停止发生时也知道停止时刻本身：对每个 $a\ge0$，$\{T\le a\}\cap\{T\le t\}=\{T\le\min(a,t)\}\in\mathcal F_t$，所以 $T$ 对 $\mathcal F_T$ 可测。[[CTMC强Markov性|强 Markov 性]]等结论，就是用这同一个信息对象来条件化未来。两个有先后顺序的停时如何比较信息，其离散版本见单独的[[停时信息单调性]]。
