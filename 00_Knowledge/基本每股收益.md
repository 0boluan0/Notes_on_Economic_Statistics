# 基本每股收益是归属于普通股股东的期间损益除以加权平均发行在外普通股数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Basic earnings per share divides period profit or loss attributable to ordinary shareholders by the weighted average number of ordinary shares outstanding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本每股收益把一个期间的损益换算为每股[[普通股]]的会计盈利。按 IAS 33 的合并报表口径，分子是归属于母公司普通股股东的损益 $NI_c$，不是包含少数股东损益的集团全部净利润；分母是期间加权平均发行在外普通股数 $\bar N>0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Basic EPS expresses period accounting earnings per [[普通股|ordinary share]]. Under IAS 33 for consolidated statements, the numerator $NI_c$ belongs to ordinary equity holders of the parent, excluding non-controlling interests; the denominator is weighted average ordinary shares outstanding, $\bar N>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$EPS_b=\frac{NI_c}{\bar N},\qquad \bar N=\sum_j N_j\frac{\Delta t_j}{T}.$$

若只有一类普通股，全年归属利润 120 万元，上半年发行在外 100 万股、下半年现金增发后为 200 万股，则按两个等长期间计算 $\bar N=150$ 万股，基本 EPS 为 0.80 元／股，而非按年末股数算出的 0.60 元／股。它不是百分数，也不是承诺支付的股利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With one ordinary share class, attributable profit of 1.2 million, 1 million shares for the first half-year, and 2 million after a cash issue for the second equal half, weighted shares are 1.5 million and basic EPS is 0.80, not the 0.60 obtained from year-end shares. EPS is neither a percentage nor a promised dividend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若股数全年不变，期末数可以直接代表该平均数；股数变化时则应按规则计算，而不能默认期末数仍然合适。权益分类优先股的股利等可能还需调整归母损益；拆股、送股等不带来相应新增资源的股数变化有追溯调整要求，不能一律照现金增发的时间权重处理。潜在普通股的稀释影响由[[稀释每股收益]]另行计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Year-end shares directly represent the average when shares are unchanged throughout the period. Equity-classified preference dividends and similar items may require adjustments to parent profit. Splits and bonus issues without corresponding additional resources require retrospective share adjustments, rather than the cash-issue treatment. [[稀释每股收益|Diluted EPS]] separately incorporates dilutive potential ordinary shares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
