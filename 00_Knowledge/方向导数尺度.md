# Scaling a directional derivative
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方向导数的尺度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A movement vector specifies both a direction and a speed for the line parameter. Replacing $(3,4)$ by $(6,8)$ follows the same ray but moves twice as far for the same change in the parameter. The [[方向导数|directional derivative]], which measures change per unit of that parameter, therefore doubles whenever the original finite, two-sided derivative exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

运动向量同时指定方向和直线参数的运动速度。把 $(3,4)$ 换成 $(6,8)$，仍沿同一条射线运动，但参数变化相同时，位移变为两倍。[[方向导数]]测量每单位参数的变化率，所以只要原来的有限双侧导数存在，它也会变为两倍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f:U\to\mathbb R$ on an [[开集|open subset]] $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, with $n\ge1$ and $a\in U$. Fix $v\ne0$ and assume the finite, two-sided limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f:U\to\mathbb R$，其中 $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 是[[开集]]，$n\ge1$，$a\in U$。固定 $v\ne0$，假设有限的双侧极限

$$
D_vf(a)=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{f(a+tv)-f(a)}t
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
exists. For any real scalar $c\ne0$, the derivative along $cv$ then exists and obeys
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存在。对任意实标量 $c\ne0$，沿 $cv$ 的导数也存在，并且

$$
\boxed{D_{cv}f(a)=cD_vf(a).}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive $c$ changes speed along the same ray. Negative $c$ also reverses the ray, so the sign changes as well: in particular, $D_{-v}f(a)=-D_vf(a)$. For $c=0$, the constant path has rate $D_0f(a)=0$; with this natural extension the identity also holds at zero, although the zero vector has no geometric direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正的 $c$ 改变沿同一射线的速度；负的 $c$ 还会翻转射线，因此变化率的符号也相应改变，特别是 $D_{-v}f(a)=-D_vf(a)$。当 $c=0$ 时，恒定路径的变化率为 $D_0f(a)=0$；采用这个自然延拓，等式在零处也成立，但零向量并没有几何方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reparametrize the same line
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 缩放公式来自重新参数化

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $c\ne0$, set $s=ct$. As $t$ approaches zero from both sides, so does $s$, even when $c<0$. Substitution in the difference quotient gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $c\ne0$，令 $s=ct$。当 $t$ 从两侧趋于零时，$s$ 也从两侧趋于零，即使 $c<0$ 也是如此。代入差商得到

$$
\begin{aligned}
D_{cv}f(a)
&=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{f(a+tcv)-f(a)}t\\
&=c\lim_{s\to0}\frac{f(a+sv)-f(a)}s
=cD_vf(a).
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only the stated directional limit is needed; [[全微分|full differentiability]] is not required. If full differentiability does hold, the [[方向导数梯度公式|gradient formula]] gives the same result by linearity of the dot product. Scaling along one line is a weaker property than combining different directions by addition: the former alone does not make all directional rates one linear map.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里只需要所述方向极限存在，不要求[[全微分|全可微]]。如果确实全可微，[[方向导数梯度公式]]也能利用点积的线性性得到同一个结果。沿一条线缩放，比把不同方向按加法组合起来更弱；仅有前者，并不能保证所有方向变化率来自同一个线性映射。

> [!warning] Reversing a one-sided limit changes its side · 单侧极限换向会改变趋近侧
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Some conventions use only $t\downarrow0$ and denote the resulting rate by $D_v^+f(a)$. For $f(x)=|x|$ at $0$, both $D_1^+f(0)$ and $D_{-1}^+f(0)$ equal $1$: moving away from zero raises the value in either direction. They are not negatives of one another. Here the two-sided derivative does not exist. Positive scaling still preserves the chosen side, but the negative-scaling rule above must not be transferred to one-sided derivatives without changing sides.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 有些约定只取 $t\downarrow0$，把所得变化率记为 $D_v^+f(a)$。对 $f(x)=|x|$ 在 $0$ 处，$D_1^+f(0)$ 和 $D_{-1}^+f(0)$ 都等于 $1$：无论朝哪边离开零点，函数值都会增加。它们并不互为相反数；这里双侧导数不存在。正倍数仍保留所选的趋近侧，但不能把上面的负倍数规则直接搬到单侧导数上而不改变趋近侧。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Separate direction from speed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 比较方向必须先规定单位长度

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]] is $\|v\|_2=\sqrt{v_1^2+\cdots+v_n^2}$. For $v\ne0$, the vector $u=v/\|v\|_2$ has length $1$ and points along the same ray. Scaling gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]为 $\|v\|_2=\sqrt{v_1^2+\cdots+v_n^2}$。对 $v\ne0$，向量 $u=v/\|v\|_2$ 长度为 $1$，并沿同一射线指向。由缩放规则，

$$
D_uf(a)=\frac{D_vf(a)}{\|v\|_2}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The left side is change per unit of Euclidean input distance; the original $D_vf(a)$ is change per unit of $t$. This distance interpretation uses a chosen common scale for the input coordinates. If coordinates measure different physical quantities, their units or a separate notion of length must first be specified; changing that choice can change which direction is steepest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左边是每单位欧氏输入距离的变化率；原来的 $D_vf(a)$ 是每单位 $t$ 的变化率。这种距离解释预先采用了输入坐标的共同尺度。若不同坐标测量不同物理量，就需要先规定单位或另选长度的含义；改变这种选择，也可能改变最陡方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without any length restriction, a vector with $D_vf(a)>0$ can be multiplied by arbitrarily large positive $c$, producing arbitrarily large rates on the same ray. Fixing length removes this speed ambiguity. With differentiability, it also makes the [[梯度最陡方向|Euclidean steepest-direction problem]] a finite maximization of $\nabla f(a)^Tu$ over unit vectors. The full differentiability assumption supplies that linear formula; normalization alone does not supply it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若完全不限制长度，只要某个向量满足 $D_vf(a)>0$，就可以乘以任意大的正数 $c$，在同一条射线上得到任意大的变化率。固定长度排除了这种速度混淆。在可微前提下，[[梯度最陡方向|欧氏最陡方向问题]]就成为在单位向量上最大化 $\nabla f(a)^Tu$ 的有限最大值问题。提供该线性公式的是全可微假设，归一化本身并没有提供它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same ray at three parameter speeds
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the differentiable polynomial $f(x,y)=x^2y$ at $(1,2)$, the gradient is $(4,1)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Let $v=(3,4)^T$, so $\|v\|_2=5$ and $u=(3/5,4/5)^T$. The gradient formula gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对可微多项式 $f(x,y)=x^2y$ 在 $(1,2)$ 处，梯度为 $(4,1)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。令 $v=(3,4)^T$，则 $\|v\|_2=5$，$u=(3/5,4/5)^T$。由梯度公式，

$$
D_vf(1,2)=4(3)+1(4)=16,\qquad
D_uf(1,2)=\frac{16}{5},\qquad
D_{2v}f(1,2)=32.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The function, starting point and ray are the same. The three vectors move at different speeds per unit of $t$. Reversing and doubling the original vector instead gives $D_{-2v}f(1,2)=-32$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

函数、起点和射线都没有改变，三个向量只是让每单位 $t$ 对应不同的运动速度。若把原向量反向并加倍，则得到 $D_{-2v}f(1,2)=-32$。

> [!question]- Compare the rates on one ray · 比较同一射线上的变化率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $D_vf(a)=3$, then $D_{4v}f(a)=12$. This does not make $4v$ a steeper geometric direction: it travels four times as far per unit of the parameter. Normalize both nonzero vectors and their per-distance rates agree.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $D_vf(a)=3$，则 $D_{4v}f(a)=12$。这不表示 $4v$ 是更陡的几何方向，它只是每单位参数移动了四倍距离。把两个非零向量都归一化后，每单位距离的变化率相同。
