# Characteristic function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*概率分布的特征函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **characteristic function** is another complete way to describe a probability distribution. It turns each possible value into a unit-length arrow in the plane, then averages those arrows using the probabilities as weights. Changing a parameter changes the directions of the arrows and gives a whole function. This encoding is useful for identifying distributions and working with sums of independent variables, even when ordinary means or variances do not exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**特征函数**是完整描述一个概率分布的另一种方式：把每个可能值变成平面上一支单位长度的箭头，再按概率加权平均。改变参数，就会改变各支箭头的方向，从而得到一整个函数。这个记法可以用来确定分布、研究独立变量之和，即使通常的均值或方差不存在也能使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the complex exponential records
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*复指数记录了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X$ be a real [[随机变量|random variable]], a number recorded from an uncertain outcome, and choose a fixed real parameter $t$. The random quantity is $X$; $t$ is an input we control. To interpret the transform, we need a little complex-number notation. A complex number $a+ib$ has real part $a$ and imaginary part $b$, where $i^2=-1$. Think of it as the point $(a,b)$ in a plane. Its modulus, or length, is $|a+ib|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X$ 是实[[随机变量]]，即把不确定结果记录成一个数；再选择固定实参数 $t$。随机的是 $X$，$t$ 则是我们控制的函数输入。理解变换需要一点复数记号：复数 $a+ib$ 的实部为 $a$、虚部为 $b$，其中 $i^2=-1$；可以把它看成平面上的点 $(a,b)$。它的模，也就是长度，为 $|a+ib|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Euler's identity $e^{iu}=\cos u+i\sin u$, with real angle $u$ measured in radians, represents a point on the unit circle. For an observed value $x$, the number $e^{itx}$ therefore has angle $tx$ and length one. Its direction changes with $x$ and $t$, but its length does not grow with $|x|$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

欧拉恒等式 $e^{iu}=\cos u+i\sin u$ 表示单位圆上的一个点，实角度 $u$ 以弧度计。对观测值 $x$，$e^{itx}$ 因而具有角度 $tx$、长度一；其方向随 $x,t$ 改变，长度却不会随 $|x|$ 增大。

> [!important] Definition for every real parameter · 对每个实参数都成立的定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The characteristic function of $X$ is
>
> $$\boxed{\varphi_X(t)=E[e^{itX}]
> =E[\cos(tX)]+iE[\sin(tX)],\qquad t\in\mathbb R.}$$
>
> The symbol $\varphi$ is phi. A complex [[期望|expectation]] here simply averages the real and imaginary parts separately. Thus the formula uses two ordinary real expectations. For fixed $t$, $\varphi_X(t)$ is a fixed complex number, not a random observation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $X$ 的特征函数由上式定义，符号 $\varphi$ 读作 phi。这里的复数[[期望]]，就是分别平均实部与虚部，因此只用到了两个普通实数期望。固定 $t$ 后，$\varphi_X(t)$ 是确定的复数，不是一次随机观测值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both sine and cosine lie between $-1$ and $1$. Their expectations are therefore finite for **every** real random variable, with no density or finite-moment assumption. Also $|e^{itX}|=1$, so the length of the average is at most the average length:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正弦与余弦都在 $-1$ 到 $1$ 之间，因此对**每个**实随机变量，这两个期望都有限，不需要密度，也不需要有限矩。又因为 $|e^{itX}|=1$，平均后的长度不会超过长度的平均：

$$|\varphi_X(t)|=|E[e^{itX}]|\le E[|e^{itX}|]=1,
\qquad \varphi_X(0)=1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The modulus bound is a statement about distance from zero in the complex plane. It does not say that $\varphi_X(t)$ is a probability between zero and one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个模长界描述的是复平面中到零点的距离，并不是说 $\varphi_X(t)$ 是介于零和一之间的概率。

> [!example] A fair random sign gives a cosine · 公平随机符号得到余弦
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $P(X=1)=P(X=-1)=1/2$. The [[LOTUS|rule for averaging a function of a variable]] gives
>
> $$\varphi_X(t)=\tfrac12e^{it}+\tfrac12e^{-it}
> =\tfrac12(\cos t+i\sin t)+\tfrac12(\cos t-i\sin t)
> =\cos t.$$
>
> The two imaginary parts cancel. At $t=0$ the answer is $1$; at $t=\pi/2$ it is $0$; at $t=\pi$ it is $-1$. These are transform values. In particular, the value zero at $\pi/2$ is not the probability $P(X=\pi/2)$, and negative transform values are perfectly valid.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设 $P(X=1)=P(X=-1)=1/2$。由 [[LOTUS|随机变量函数的期望公式]]，按各半概率平均两个复指数，得到上式中的 $\cos t$，因为两个虚部恰好抵消。$t=0$ 时答案为 $1$，$t=\pi/2$ 时为 $0$，$t=\pi$ 时为 $-1$。这些是变换的函数值；特别地，在 $\pi/2$ 处取零，不是在计算 $P(X=\pi/2)$，负的特征函数值也完全合法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a discrete variable with [[概率质量函数|PMF]] $p_X(x)$, the general calculation is $\varphi_X(t)=\sum_x e^{itx}p_X(x)$. If the variable has a [[概率密度函数|density]] $f_X$, it becomes $\varphi_X(t)=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{itx}f_X(x)\,dx$. The expectation definition still works when there is no density. Both displayed calculations are absolutely convergent: the absolute value of the exponential is one and the total probability is one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若离散变量的[[概率质量函数|PMF]]为 $p_X(x)$，一般计算式是 $\varphi_X(t)=\sum_x e^{itx}p_X(x)$；若变量有[[概率密度函数|密度]] $f_X$，则变成 $\varphi_X(t)=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{itx}f_X(x)\,dx$。没有密度时，期望定义仍然适用。上述求和、积分都绝对收敛，因为指数的模为一，总概率也是一。

## A fingerprint of the law, not one summary number
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*整个分布的指纹，而不只是一个概括数字*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **uniqueness theorem** says that two real random variables have the same distribution if and only if their characteristic functions agree for every real $t$. One direction follows immediately from averaging against the same distribution. The other is a substantive inversion theorem: the complete transform determines all distribution probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**唯一性定理**说明：两个实随机变量同分布，当且仅当它们的特征函数对每个实数 $t$ 都相等。一个方向很直接——按相同分布平均，就会得到相同变换；反方向则是实质性的反演定理：完整变换能够确定全部分布概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Every $t$” matters. All distributions already have $\varphi(0)=1$, so that one value cannot distinguish them. The theorem concerns equality of **laws**, not equality of observations or independence of the variables. It is also stronger than comparing finitely many moments. No general density formula is implied: a distribution consisting of point masses has a characteristic function too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“每个 $t$”很重要。所有分布都满足 $\varphi(0)=1$，单看这一点当然不能区分分布。定理说的是**概率律**相同，不是两次观测值相同，也不是变量彼此独立；它还强于比较有限个矩。存在特征函数不意味着存在密度，全由单点质量组成的分布也有特征函数。

## Independent addition becomes multiplication
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*独立变量相加，特征函数相乘*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $X$ and $Y$ are [[随机变量独立|independent]] real variables, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果实变量 $X,Y$ [[随机变量独立|相互独立]]，则

$$\varphi_{X+Y}(t)
=E[e^{itX}e^{itY}]
=E[e^{itX}]E[e^{itY}]
=\varphi_X(t)\varphi_Y(t).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first step is the exponential rule; the second uses independence to factor the expectation of the bounded transformed variables. No means or variances need to exist. For two independent fair signs, the sum is $-2,0,2$ with probabilities $1/4,1/2,1/4$. Its transform is both $(\cos t)^2$ and $\tfrac14e^{-2it}+\tfrac12+\tfrac14e^{2it}$, two forms of the same function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一步使用指数运算法则，第二步利用独立性，把两个有界变换变量的乘积期望分解；不要求原变量有均值或方差。两个独立公平符号之和以 $1/4,1/2,1/4$ 的概率取 $-2,0,2$，所以其特征函数既可写成 $(\cos t)^2$，也可写成 $\tfrac14e^{-2it}+\tfrac12+\tfrac14e^{2it}$，二者是同一个函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The independence assumption is essential to this calculation. If instead $Y=X$ for the same fair sign, the sum is $2X$ and its transform is $\cos(2t)$, which is not generally $(\cos t)^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个计算需要独立假设。若把同一个公平符号复制成 $Y=X$，和就是 $2X$，特征函数为 $\cos(2t)$，通常不等于 $(\cos t)^2$。

## Gaussian variables and several coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Gaussian 变量与多个坐标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a [[正态分布|normal variable]] $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[正态分布|正态变量]] $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$，有

$$\varphi_X(t)=\exp\!\left(i\mu t-\tfrac12\sigma^2t^2\right).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This formula also covers the degenerate convention $\sigma^2=0$: then $X=\mu$ almost surely and the transform is $e^{i\mu t}$ directly from the definition. The Gaussian formula is not valid for an arbitrary variable merely because its mean and variance are known.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公式也适用于 $\sigma^2=0$ 的退化约定：此时 $X=\mu$ 几乎必然，从定义直接得到 $e^{i\mu t}$。任意变量即使已知均值、方差，也不能据此套用 Gaussian 公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real [[随机向量|random vector]] $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$, choose a real vector $t$ of the same dimension. Replace the scalar product $tX$ by the [[向量点积|dot product]] $t^TX=\sum_j t_jX_j$ and define $\varphi_X(t)=E[e^{it^TX}]$. This whole function determines the **joint** law. When $X$ is [[联合Gaussian|jointly Gaussian]] with mean $\mu$ and covariance $\Sigma$, the scalar Gaussian formula applied to each projection gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实[[随机向量]] $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$，选择同维实向量 $t$，把标量乘积 $tX$ 换成[[向量点积]] $t^TX=\sum_jt_jX_j$，定义 $\varphi_X(t)=E[e^{it^TX}]$。这个完整函数确定的是**联合**分布。若 $X$ [[联合Gaussian|联合 Gaussian]]，均值为 $\mu$、协方差为 $\Sigma$，把标量 Gaussian 公式应用于每个投影，就得到

$$\varphi_X(t)=\exp\!\left(it^T\mu-\tfrac12t^T\Sigma t\right).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No inverse or determinant appears, so this remains valid for singular covariance. It is the transform used in the [[联合高斯独立判据|Gaussian independence criterion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公式中没有逆或行列式，因此奇异协方差下也成立；[[联合高斯独立判据]]使用的就是这个变换。

## Keep three nearby names distinct
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*区分三个相近名称*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[矩母函数|moment-generating function]] uses $M_X(s)=E[e^{sX}]$ for a real parameter $s$. This real exponential can grow rapidly, so finiteness away from zero is not guaranteed. The characteristic function instead uses the bounded oscillation $e^{itX}$ and always exists. Writing a formal substitution $s=it$ in a real MGF formula does not by itself justify that formula for complex arguments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[矩母函数]]使用 $M_X(s)=E[e^{sX}]$，参数 $s$ 为实数；这个实指数可能快速增长，所以零以外的有限性没有自动保证。特征函数则使用有界振荡 $e^{itX}$，始终存在。在实 MGF 公式里形式上代入 $s=it$，本身并不能证明该公式对复参数也成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[概率生成函数|probability generating function]] is for a nonnegative integer-valued count $N$: $G_N(z)=E[z^N]$, with absolute convergence for $|z|\le1$. For such a count, $\varphi_N(t)=G_N(e^{it})$ because $|e^{it}|=1$. The PGF's count restriction does not apply to characteristic functions, which also handle negative and noninteger values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[概率生成函数]]针对非负整数值计数 $N$，写成 $G_N(z)=E[z^N]$，在 $|z|\le1$ 时绝对收敛。对这种计数，由于 $|e^{it}|=1$，有 $\varphi_N(t)=G_N(e^{it})$。PGF 的计数取值限制不适用于特征函数；特征函数也处理负值和非整数值。

> [!warning] “Characteristic function of a set” is a different term · “集合的特征函数”是另一个用法
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In analysis, the characteristic function of a set $A$ may mean its indicator $\mathbf1_A(x)$: one if $x\in A$, zero otherwise. For an event this is the [[事件指示变量|event indicator]]. It records membership and has input $x$ or an outcome. The probability characteristic function $\varphi_X(t)$ instead averages complex exponentials and has input $t$. Read the definition, not just the shared name.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在分析学中，“集合 $A$ 的特征函数”可能指指示函数 $\mathbf1_A(x)$：$x\in A$ 时为一，否则为零；对事件来说，这就是[[事件指示变量]]。它记录是否属于集合，输入是 $x$ 或一个基本结果。概率论中的 $\varphi_X(t)$ 则平均复指数，输入是参数 $t$。应根据定义辨认，不能只看名称。
