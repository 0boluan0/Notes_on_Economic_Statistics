# Left and right singular vectors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*左右奇异向量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[奇异值|singular value]] tells us a stretch amount, but it does not say where to point the input. **Singular vectors** supply that missing information. A right singular vector is a unit input direction; its matched left singular vector is a unit output direction. For a positive stretch, the matrix sends the first direction to a positive multiple of the second.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[奇异值]]告诉我们伸缩的大小，却没有告诉我们应该把输入指向哪里。**奇异向量**补上方向信息：右奇异向量是单位输入方向，配对的左奇异向量是单位输出方向。正伸缩对应的矩阵作用，会把前者变成后者的正数倍。

> [!example] The same index, different spaces / 相同下标，不同空间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With
> $$A=\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix},$$
> choose $v_1=(1,0)^T$, $v_2=(0,1)^T$ in the two-dimensional input space and $u_1=(1,0,0)^T$, $u_2=(0,1,0)^T$ in the three-dimensional output space. Then $Av_1=3u_1$ and $Av_2=u_2$. The symbols $v_1,u_1$ carry the same index because they are paired, not because their coordinates or ambient dimensions must agree. $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 输入空间为二维，可取 $v_1=(1,0)^T$、$v_2=(0,1)^T$；输出空间为三维，对应 $u_1=(1,0,0)^T$、$u_2=(0,1,0)^T$。于是 $Av_1=3u_1$、$Av_2=u_2$。$v_1,u_1$ 下标相同表示配对，并不意味着坐标或所在空间维数相同。$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。

## A pair must satisfy both equations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一对方向必须同时满足两个方程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite real or complex $m\times n$ matrix, $m,n\ge1$, a full [[奇异值分解|SVD]] writes $A=U\Sigma V^*$. The columns $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ of $V$ are input directions; the columns $u_1,\ldots,u_m$ of $U$ are output directions. Each family is an [[标准正交组|orthonormal basis]]: every vector has [[欧氏范数|length]] one and distinct vectors in the same family are perpendicular. The star means [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]], replacing $a+bi$ by $a-bi$ after transposition, with $i^2=-1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限维实或复 $m\times n$ 矩阵，$m,n\ge1$，完整[[奇异值分解]]写成 $A=U\Sigma V^*$。$V$ 的列 $v_1,\ldots,v_n$ 是输入方向，$U$ 的列 $u_1,\ldots,u_m$ 是输出方向。两组分别为[[标准正交组|标准正交基]]：每个向量[[欧氏范数|长度]]为1，同组不同向量互相垂直。星号是[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]，转置后把 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$，其中 $i^2=-1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $r$ be the [[矩阵秩|rank]], or number of positive singular values. For each $1\le i\le r$, the positive pair satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $r$ 为[[矩阵秩|秩]]，即正奇异值个数。对每个 $1\le i\le r$，正方向配对满足

$$
Av_i=\sigma_i u_i,\qquad A^*u_i=\sigma_i v_i,\qquad
\|v_i\|_2=\|u_i\|_2=1,\qquad \sigma_i>0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first equation gives the forward action. The second says that the adjoint maps the output direction back to the same input direction with the same stretch; the adjoint is not generally an inverse. These equations follow by multiplying the SVD by the corresponding columns. Conversely, unit vectors satisfying both equations form a positive singular-vector pair.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一式描述正向作用；第二式说明伴随映射把这个输出方向送回同一个输入方向，伸缩量仍相同，但伴随通常不是逆。将 SVD 乘以对应列即可得到这两式。反过来，单位向量若同时满足两式，就组成一对正奇异方向。

> [!warning] Normalizing an arbitrary image is not enough / 任取输入再归一化输出还不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{bmatrix}$ and choose $v=u=(1,0)^T$. The equation $Av=u$ holds and both vectors have length one, but $A^*u=(1,1)^T\ne v$. They are not a singular-vector pair with value 1. The [[奇异向量与Gram特征向量|Gram-eigenvector construction]] supplies input directions that satisfy both equations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上述矩阵与 $v=u=(1,0)^T$ 确实满足 $Av=u$，两向量也都长为1；但 $A^*u=(1,1)^T\ne v$，所以它们不是奇异值1的一对奇异向量。[[奇异向量与Gram特征向量|Gram 特征向量构造]]会选择同时满足两个方程的输入方向。

## Signs, phases and repeated values
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*符号、相位与重复值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $v_i$ is replaced by $-v_i$, its positive partner must also become $-u_i$. For complex vectors, both can be multiplied by the same complex number of magnitude one. This [[奇异向量共同相位|common phase freedom]] leaves their contribution to $A$ unchanged. For example, with $A=[-3]$, one valid pair is $v=1,u=-1,\sigma=3$; choosing $u=1$ while keeping $v=1$ would give the wrong sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若把 $v_i$ 换成 $-v_i$，正值对应的伙伴也必须换成 $-u_i$。复数时，两者可同乘一个绝对值为1的复数。这种[[奇异向量共同相位|共同相位自由]]不改变它们对 $A$ 的贡献。例如 $A=[-3]$ 可取 $v=1,u=-1,\sigma=3$；若保持 $v=1$ 却单独取 $u=1$，符号就错了。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A positive singular value that occurs only once fixes its pair up to this common phase. For a [[重奇异值下方向不唯一|repeated positive value]], an entire subspace is fixed, while orthonormal bases within it can change. Once the right basis is chosen, $u_i=Av_i/\sigma_i$ fixes the matching left basis. Choosing two bases independently merely because they have the same eigenvalue need not give a valid pair.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仅出现一次的正奇异值，把其方向对确定到共同相位。若为[[重奇异值下方向不唯一|重复正奇异值]]，确定的是整个子空间，其中的标准正交基仍可改变。一旦选定右侧基，$u_i=Av_i/\sigma_i$ 就确定配对左基；不能仅凭特征值相同，就把两侧任意独立选出的基直接配起来。

## Zero directions complete the two bases separately
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零方向分别补齐两侧的基*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The remaining $n-r$ input directions form a basis of the [[零空间|nullspace]] $N(A)=\{v:Av=0\}$. The remaining $m-r$ output directions form a basis of the [[左零空间|left nullspace]] $N(A^*)=\{u:A^*u=0\}$. They are often called zero singular directions or nullspace completion vectors. The two bases can be chosen independently: a zero output from $Av$ does not identify a preferred unit vector on the left.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

余下 $n-r$ 条输入方向组成[[零空间]] $N(A)=\{v:Av=0\}$ 的基；余下 $m-r$ 条输出方向组成[[左零空间]] $N(A^*)=\{u:A^*u=0\}$ 的基。它们常称为零奇异方向或零空间补基向量。两侧基可独立选择，因为 $Av=0$ 并不能指定左侧应该是哪条单位方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first $p=\min(m,n)$ columns on each side occupy the paired diagonal positions of a full SVD, including any zero entries. If one space is larger, its remaining completion vectors have no counterpart among those $p$ positions. In the opening $3\times2$ example, $u_3=(0,0,1)^T$ completes the output basis and satisfies $A^*u_3=0$; there is no $v_3$. For $A=0$, every orthonormal input basis and every orthonormal output basis can be chosen independently. The detailed freedom is described in [[SVD零块补基不唯一|nullspace completion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两侧前 $p=\min(m,n)$ 列对应完整 SVD 的配对对角位置，包括其中的零项。若某一空间更大，额外补基向量在这 $p$ 个位置中没有对应伙伴。开头的 $3\times2$ 例子中，$u_3=(0,0,1)^T$ 补齐输出基并满足 $A^*u_3=0$，却没有 $v_3$。若 $A=0$，两侧任意标准正交基都可独立选择；详细边界见 [[SVD零块补基不唯一]]。
