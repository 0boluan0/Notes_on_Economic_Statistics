# Cluster points and convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*序列聚点与整列收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the list $-1,1,-1,1,\ldots$. It returns to $1$ as late as we like, but never stays near $1$ permanently. It does the same at $-1$. This separates two questions: “Can we find later and later terms close to this point?” and “Are all sufficiently late terms close to it?” A **cluster point of a sequence** answers the first; [[序列收敛|convergence of the full sequence]] answers the second.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看列表 $-1,1,-1,1,\ldots$。无论看多晚，它还会回到 $1$，但不会从此一直留在 $1$ 附近；对 $-1$ 也是如此。这里有两个不同问题：“能否找到越来越靠后的项接近这个点？”以及“是否足够靠后的所有项都接近它？”**序列的聚点**回答前者，[[序列收敛|整列收敛]]回答后者。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Arbitrarily late visits versus an entire tail
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*任意晚的访问与整个尾部*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $(x_n)_{n\ge1}$ be a sequence in a [[度量空间|metric space]] $(X,d)$, and let $x\in X$. The ball $B_\varepsilon(x)$ contains points whose distance from $x$ is less than $\varepsilon>0$. We call $x$ a cluster point of the sequence when every such ball contains terms at arbitrarily large indices:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $(x_n)_{n\ge1}$ 是[[度量空间]] $(X,d)$ 中的序列，$x\in X$。球 $B_\varepsilon(x)$ 包含距 $x$ 小于 $\varepsilon>0$ 的点。若每个这样的球都包含索引任意大的序列项，就称 $x$ 为序列的聚点：

$$\forall\varepsilon>0\quad\forall N\ge1\quad\exists n\ge N:\quad d(x_n,x)<\varepsilon.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $N$ and $n$ are integers. After any proposed cutoff $N$, at least one suitable term remains. Equivalently, each ball contains terms at infinitely many indices: a finite collection of indices would have a largest one. Compare this with convergence, which requires that for each $\varepsilon$ there be one cutoff after which **every** term lies in the ball. A limit is always a cluster point, but a cluster point need not be a limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $N,n$ 为整数。在任意给定门槛 $N$ 后面，至少还能找到一个合适的项。等价地，每个球都对应无穷多个索引，因为有限多个索引会有最大值。整列收敛则要求：对每个 $\varepsilon$，存在一个门槛，之后**每一项**都在球中。极限一定是聚点，聚点却不一定是极限。

> [!example] Two clusters, no limit · 两个聚点，没有极限
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $x_n=(-1)^n$, every even term is $1$ and every odd term is $-1$. Each ball around $1$ contains all even terms, and each ball around $-1$ contains all odd terms, so both points are cluster points. A radius-$1/2$ ball around either one misses infinitely many terms. The sequence cannot converge to some third point either: any limit of the full sequence would also be the limit of its constant even and odd subsequences, contradicting [[序列极限唯一|uniqueness of metric limits]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $x_n=(-1)^n$，每个偶数项为 $1$，每个奇数项为 $-1$。$1$ 周围每个球都含全部偶数项，$-1$ 周围每个球都含全部奇数项，因此两者都是聚点。以任何一者为中心、半径 $1/2$ 的球都会漏掉无穷多项。整列也不可能收敛到第三个点：若整列有极限，两个常值奇偶子列都必须有同一极限，这与[[序列极限唯一|度量极限唯一性]]矛盾。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A cluster point gives a convergent subsequence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*聚点可以选出收敛子列*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **subsequence** keeps selected positions in their original order. Write it as $x_{n_1},x_{n_2},\ldots$, with positive integer indices $n_1<n_2<\cdots$. The indices must increase; repeatedly selecting one early occurrence is not a subsequence. In particular, $n_k\ge k$, so a subsequence eventually passes any fixed index of the original list. This also proves that every subsequence of a convergent sequence converges to the same limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**子列**从原列表中选取一些位置，并保持原有顺序。记为 $x_{n_1},x_{n_2},\ldots$，其中正整数索引满足 $n_1<n_2<\cdots$。索引必须递增，不能反复选同一个早期位置冒充子列。特别地，$n_k\ge k$，所以子列最终会越过原序列中任意固定索引。这也证明了：收敛序列的每个子列都收敛到同一极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $x$ is a cluster point, choose $n_1\ge1$ with $d(x_{n_1},x)<1$. Having chosen $n_{k-1}$, apply the cluster-point condition with radius $1/k$ and cutoff $n_{k-1}+1$. It supplies an index $n_k>n_{k-1}$ satisfying
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $x$ 是聚点，先选 $n_1\ge1$ 使 $d(x_{n_1},x)<1$。已经选好 $n_{k-1}$ 后，用半径 $1/k$ 和门槛 $n_{k-1}+1$ 应用聚点条件，就能找到 $n_k>n_{k-1}$，并且

$$d(x_{n_k},x)<1/k.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, choose an integer $K>1/\varepsilon$. Then $k\ge K$ gives $d(x_{n_k},x)<1/k\le1/K<\varepsilon$, proving $x_{n_k}\to x$. Conversely, a subsequence converging to $x$ supplies terms arbitrarily far along the original list in every ball about $x$, because its indices increase without bound. Thus in a metric space, being a cluster point of the sequence is equivalent to being the limit of some subsequence. The construction controls selected terms only; it says nothing about the terms left out.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意容许误差 $\varepsilon>0$，取整数 $K>1/\varepsilon$。当 $k\ge K$ 时，$d(x_{n_k},x)<1/k\le1/K<\varepsilon$，所以 $x_{n_k}\to x$。反过来，若某个子列收敛到 $x$，它不断增大的索引便能在原列表任意靠后的位置，提供落入每个球的项。因此，在度量空间中，序列聚点等价于某个子列的极限。这个构造只控制选中的项，对被略去的项没有任何保证。

> [!warning] Count positions, not different values · 数的是索引，不是不同取值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the constant sequence $x_n=0$ in $\mathbb R$, every ball about $0$ contains infinitely many terms, one at each index. Thus $0$ is a sequence cluster point, even though the set of distinct values is only $\{0\}$. A set accumulation point is a different notion: it requires every neighbourhood to meet the set at a point **other than the proposed point itself**. The singleton $\{0\}$ has no such accumulation point in the usual real line. When using the word “cluster”, specify whether the object is a sequence with indexed repetitions or a set of distinct points.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对实数中的常值序列 $x_n=0$，$0$ 周围每个球都包含无穷多项，每个索引对应一项。因此 $0$ 是序列聚点，尽管不同取值组成的集合只有 $\{0\}$。集合的聚积点是另一个概念：每个邻域都要遇到集合中**异于候选点自身**的点。通常实数线中的单点集 $\{0\}$ 没有这样的聚积点。使用“聚点”时，要说清对象是允许重复的带索引序列，还是不同点构成的集合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The subsequence equivalence above is asserted for metric spaces. The countable radii $1,1/2,1/3,\ldots$ are what make the construction possible. Do not transfer that proof to an arbitrary topology merely by replacing the word “ball” with “neighbourhood”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的子列等价性限定在度量空间。可数的一列半径 $1,1/2,1/3,\ldots$ 是构造得以进行的依据，不能只把“球”换成“邻域”，就把这个证明照搬到任意拓扑。
