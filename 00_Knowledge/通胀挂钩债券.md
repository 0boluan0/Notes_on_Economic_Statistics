# 通胀挂钩债券是按指定物价指数调整本金或息票现金流的债券

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inflation-linked bond adjusts principal or coupon cash flows using a specified price index.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通胀挂钩债券是将本金或息票支付与约定物价指数联系起来的[[债券]]，目的是按该指数调整名义现金流。它不是单纯把息票率换成某个市场利率；后者是[[浮动利率债券]]。所用指数、基期、公布滞后、调整日期和下限条款，都属于具体合同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inflation-linked bond is a [[债券|bond]] linking principal or coupon payments to an agreed price index, thereby adjusting nominal cash flows to that index. It does not merely reset its coupon rate to a market interest rate, as a [[浮动利率债券|floating-rate bond]] does. The index, base period, publication lag, adjustment dates and any floor are contractual details.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一种常见结构先把原始本金 $F_0$ 乘以适用的指数比率 $I_k/I_0$，再以固定年息票率 $c$ 对调整后本金计算利息。忽略具体插值并设一年付息 $m$ 次、每次等长时，其该期调整后本金及息票为：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One common structure multiplies original principal $F_0$ by the applicable index ratio $I_k/I_0$, then applies a fixed annual coupon rate $c$ to the adjusted principal. Ignoring product-specific interpolation and assuming $m$ equal coupon periods per year gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
F_k=F_0\frac{I_k}{I_0},\qquad C_k=\frac{c}{m}F_k.
$$

若 $F_0=100$，本次适用指数比率为 $1.03$，固定年息票率 $c=2\%$，每半年付息，则调整后本金为 103，本次息票为 $103\times2\%/2=1.03$。息票率仍为 2%，变化的是计息本金和名义息票金额；不能因此称其为“基准利率加点”的浮息券。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $F_0=100$, the applicable index ratio is $1.03$, the fixed annual coupon rate is $2\%$ and payments are semiannual, adjusted principal is 103 and the coupon is $103\times2\%/2=1.03$. The rate remains 2%; the interest-bearing principal and nominal coupon amount change. This is not a benchmark-plus-spread floater.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，美国 TIPS 的本金随指定 CPI 调整，固定息票率作用于调整后的本金，并有到期原始本金保护；该到期下限不意味着途中市价或每期调整本金都不下降，更不是所有通胀挂钩债券都必须有的条款。购买力保护相对于指定指数和现金流规则，不保证覆盖个人消费篮子、税费或提前出售损失。其贴现须匹配[[名义与实际贴现|名义／实际口径]]；市场收益率差的解释另见[[盈亏平衡通胀]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
US TIPS, for example, adjust principal using a specified CPI, apply a fixed coupon rate to adjusted principal and protect original principal at maturity. That maturity floor does not prevent interim market prices or adjusted principal from falling, and is not a universal requirement for inflation-linked debt. Purchasing-power protection is relative to the specified index and cash-flow rules, not every personal consumption basket, tax, fee or early-sale loss. Discounting must match [[名义与实际贴现|nominal and real conventions]]; interpretation of market yield differences belongs to [[盈亏平衡通胀|breakeven inflation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
