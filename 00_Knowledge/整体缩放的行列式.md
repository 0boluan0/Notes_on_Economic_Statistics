# Determinant under scalar multiplication
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*整体缩放的行列式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying an entire matrix by a scalar means multiplying **every entry** by that scalar. For a square matrix with $n$ rows, this changes its [[行列式|determinant]] by $n$ scalar factors, one from each row. It differs from changing just one row.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把整张矩阵乘以一个标量，意思是把**每个元素**都乘以这个数。对有 $n$ 行的方阵，其[[行列式]]会带上 $n$ 个标量因子，每行贡献一个；这与只改变一行不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{pmatrix}$, whose determinant is $1\cdot4-2\cdot3=-2$. Doubling only the first row gives determinant $-4$. Doubling the entire matrix doubles both rows and gives determinant $-8$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看 $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{pmatrix}$，其行列式为 $1\cdot4-2\cdot3=-2$。只把第一行加倍，行列式变成 $-4$；把整张矩阵加倍，则两行都加倍，行列式变成 $-8$：

$$\det\begin{pmatrix}2&4\\3&4\end{pmatrix}=8-12=-4,\qquad
\det(2A)=\det\begin{pmatrix}2&4\\6&8\end{pmatrix}=16-24=-8=2^2\det A.$$

> [!important] One factor from every row · 每行各贡献一个因子
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $n\ge1$ be an integer, $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$, and $c\in\mathbb F$. All entries and the scalar belong to the same field, for example the real or complex numbers. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $n\ge1$ 为整数，$A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$，且 $c\in\mathbb F$。矩阵元素与标量属于同一个域，例如实数域或复数域。则
>
> $$\boxed{\det(cA)=c^n\det A.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The exponent is the matrix order $n$, not its rank or its number of nonzero entries. Neither $A$ nor $c$ needs to be invertible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 指数是矩阵阶数 $n$，不是矩阵的秩，也不是非零元素的数量；不要求 $A$ 或 $c$ 可逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Extract the factors one row at a time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*按行依次提出因子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the rows of $A$ as $r_1,\ldots,r_n$. The [[行操作对行列式的影响|single-row scaling rule]] says that scaling one row by $c$, with all other rows fixed, multiplies the determinant by $c$. Use it on each row of $cA$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $A$ 的各行记为 $r_1,\ldots,r_n$。[[行操作对行列式的影响|单行缩放规则]]说明：固定其他行，只把某行乘以 $c$，行列式就乘以 $c$。对 $cA$ 的每一行依次使用这条规则：

$$\begin{aligned}
\det(cA)&=\det(cr_1,cr_2,\ldots,cr_n)\\
&=c\det(r_1,cr_2,\ldots,cr_n)\\
&=c^2\det(r_1,r_2,cr_3,\ldots,cr_n)\\
&=\cdots=c^n\det(r_1,\ldots,r_n)=c^n\det A.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The row-list notation means the matrix with the displayed rows; for $n=1$, there is only the first extraction, and for $n=2$ the second extraction is already the last. We are using linearity, not dividing rows by $c$, so the proof remains valid when $c=0$. Scaling by zero is not an invertible elementary row operation, but the determinant identity is still valid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按行列出的记号表示由这些行组成的矩阵；$n=1$ 时只提出一次因子，$n=2$ 时第二次已是最后一次。这里使用线性性，并没有把行除以 $c$，所以 $c=0$ 时证明仍然成立。乘以零不是可逆的初等行操作，但行列式恒等式仍然成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is also a product-rule interpretation. The [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I_n$ has ones on its diagonal and zeros elsewhere. Applying the same row argument to $cI_n$ gives $\det(cI_n)=c^n\det I_n=c^n$. Since $cA=(cI_n)A$, [[行列式乘法性|multiplicativity]] then yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还可以从乘法性理解这条公式。[[单位矩阵]] $I_n$ 的对角元为一，其余元素为零。对 $cI_n$ 使用刚才的逐行论证，得到 $\det(cI_n)=c^n\det I_n=c^n$。又因为 $cA=(cI_n)A$，由[[行列式乘法性]]得到

$$\det(cA)=\det(cI_n)\det A=c^n\det A.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Examples and boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 例子与边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over the reals, if $A$ is $4\times4$ and $\det A=3$, then $\det(2A)=2^4\cdot3=48$. A concrete example is the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] with diagonal entries $3,1,1,1$ and every other entry zero. Scaling it by two makes the diagonal $6,2,2,2$, whose product is $48$. Only doubling one row would instead give $2\cdot3=6$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在实数域上，若 $A$ 为 $4\times4$ 且 $\det A=3$，则 $\det(2A)=2^4\cdot3=48$。具体可取主对角元为 $3,1,1,1$、其余元素为零的[[对角矩阵]]；整体乘二后，对角元变成 $6,2,2,2$，乘积为 $48$。若只把其中一行乘二，行列式则是 $2\cdot3=6$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formula also covers zero and singular matrices. If $c=0$, the scaled matrix is zero and its determinant is zero because $n\ge1$. If $\det A=0$, every scalar multiple has determinant zero. Hence an unchanged determinant does not by itself imply $c=1$. Zero-order matrices, whose determinant convention is $1$, are outside the positive-order scope stated above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公式也包含零标量与奇异矩阵。若 $c=0$，缩放后为零矩阵；因为 $n\ge1$，其行列式为零。若 $\det A=0$，任何标量倍数的行列式也都为零。因此，行列式保持不变不能单独推出 $c=1$。零阶矩阵的行列式约定为 $1$，不属于上面明确限定的正阶范围。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a negative real scalar, the parity of $n$ matters: $\det(-A)=(-1)^n\det A$. In three dimensions, scaling by $-2$ contributes $(-2)^3=-8$; in four dimensions it contributes $16$. For real matrices, ordinary [[行列式体积与取向|volume]] scales by the absolute factor $|c|^n$, while the signed determinant is multiplied by $c^n$, including its sign. This geometric interpretation should not turn a complex scalar into a real signed length: algebraically, with $A=I_2$ and $c=i$, where $i^2=-1$, we have $\det(iI_2)=i^2=-1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标量为负实数时，$n$ 的奇偶性很重要：$\det(-A)=(-1)^n\det A$。三维中整体乘以 $-2$，因子是 $(-2)^3=-8$；四维中因子则为 $16$。对实矩阵，普通[[行列式体积与取向|体积]]按绝对因子 $|c|^n$ 缩放，有向行列式则乘以包含符号的因子 $c^n$。不能把这种几何解释直接套成复标量的“有向实长度”：代数上，取 $A=I_2$、$c=i$，其中 $i^2=-1$，便有 $\det(iI_2)=i^2=-1$。

> [!question]- Count the changed rows · 数清改变了几行
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does multiplying an entire $3\times3$ matrix by $-2$ multiply its determinant by $-8$, rather than by $-2$? All three rows contribute a factor $-2$, giving $(-2)^3=-8$. Scaling just one row contributes only one factor.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 为什么 $3\times3$ 矩阵整体乘以 $-2$，会让行列式乘以 $-8$，而不是 $-2$？三行各贡献一个因子 $-2$，相乘得到 $(-2)^3=-8$；只缩放一行时才只贡献一个因子。
