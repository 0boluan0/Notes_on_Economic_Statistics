# 价格反应取决于结果相对事前预期的意外成分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Price reactions depend on the unexpected component of an outcome relative to prior expectations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 新闻的绝对水平不决定价格方向；事件前已经进入价格的预期才是比较基准。结果高于预期是正面意外，低于预期是负面意外，价格还会同时反映未来现金流、贴现率与风险判断怎样被修订。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The absolute level of news does not determine the direction of a price reaction. The relevant benchmark is the expectation already incorporated before the event. An outcome above expectation is a positive surprise and one below expectation a negative surprise, while prices also reflect revisions to future cash flows, discount rates, and risk.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公司利润同比增长但低于分析师与市场已定价的更高预期，股价可以下跌；亏损公司若亏损远小于预期，股价可以上涨。事件研究若按“新闻看起来好不好”分组，而不构造事前预期或信息意外，就会混淆已知水平与新增信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A firm can report year-on-year profit growth yet fall because the result missed a still higher expectation already priced in. A loss-making firm can rise when the loss is much smaller than expected. An event study that classifies news by appearance without measuring the ex ante expectation or surprise confounds the known level with new information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预期代理也有测量边界。分析师平均预测、期货价格或统计模型只是在目标时点可得信息的代理；若代理在公告后更新、遗漏系统性预期或只覆盖部分投资者，所谓意外成分会带入测量误差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expectation proxies also have measurement boundaries. Consensus forecasts, futures prices, and statistical models merely proxy the information available at the target time. If a proxy is updated after the announcement, omits systematic expectations, or covers only some investors, the measured surprise contains error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 每股收益为正且增长，是否足以把公告标成“好消息”？
>
> **答案：** 不足。要与公告前可得的市场预期比较，并说明该预期怎样测量；价格反应对应的是意外修订，而不是新闻形容词。

## 来源与核验

- [MacKinlay (1997), “Event Studies in Economics and Finance”](https://www.bu.edu/econ/files/2011/01/MacKinlay-1996-Event-Studies-in-Economics-and-Finance.pdf)，pp. 16–17：核对盈利公告示例以实际盈利减事前预测构造信息意外。
- [Fama et al. (1969), “The Adjustment of Stock Prices to New Information”](https://doi.org/10.2307/2525569)：核对价格调整研究针对新增信息而非结果绝对水平。
