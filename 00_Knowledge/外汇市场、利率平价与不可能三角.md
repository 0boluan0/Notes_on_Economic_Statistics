# 外汇市场、利率平价与不可能三角
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Foreign-exchange Markets, Interest Parity, and the Impossible Trinity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 比较跨币种资产回报，说明即期、远期、利差、预期和风险溢价怎样共同决定汇率，并理解开放经济政策约束。
> **具体锚点：** 英镑存款利率较高不表示英镑资产必然回报更高；若英镑预期贬值或需支付远期贴水，利差会被抵消。
> **核心难点：** 必须先声明汇率报价方向；CIP 是远期锁定后的近无套利关系，UIP 则依赖预期并含风险溢价。
> **为什么重要：** 跨币种投资、套期保值、外汇干预和固定汇率政策都会因忽略报价或风险溢价而得出反向结论。
> **继续：** 先把两种货币回报写到同一计价货币，再判断资本流动、汇率承诺和货币政策独立性能否共存。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Compare returns across currencies, explain how spot and forward rates, interest differentials, expectations, and risk premia jointly determine exchange rates, and understand open-economy policy constraints.
> **Concrete anchor:** A higher interest rate on sterling deposits does not guarantee a higher sterling-asset return; expected depreciation or a forward discount can offset the differential.
> **Central difficulty:** The exchange-rate quotation must be stated first. CIP is an approximate no-arbitrage relation after locking the exchange rate forward, while UIP depends on expectations and contains a risk premium.
> **Why it matters:** Cross-currency investment, hedging, foreign-exchange intervention, and fixed-rate policy can produce reversed conclusions when quotation direction or risk premia are ignored.
> **Continue with:** Write both currency returns in the same numeraire first, then test whether capital mobility, an exchange-rate commitment, and monetary-policy independence can coexist.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 汇率报价与回报
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Exchange-rate Quotations and Returns*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先声明本币/外币报价。即期和远期汇率连接跨币种现金流；升值/贬值的语言随报价方向反转。投资回报需同时计算利息和汇率变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
State whether the exchange rate is quoted as domestic currency per unit of foreign currency. Spot and forward rates connect cash flows across currencies; appreciation and depreciation reverse when the quotation is inverted. Investment returns must include both interest and the exchange-rate change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $S_t$ 表示每单位外币的本币价格，$S$ 上升是本币贬值、外币升值；若用倒数报价，语言相反。跨币种收益应先把期末外币本息乘期末汇率，或用远期预先锁定，再与本币投资比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $S_t$ is domestic currency per unit of foreign currency, a rise in $S$ means domestic depreciation and foreign appreciation; the language reverses under the reciprocal quotation. Compare cross-currency returns by converting foreign principal and interest at the terminal exchange rate or by locking the rate forward in advance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 利率平价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Interest Parity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

covered interest parity 用远期合约锁定汇率，近似无套利关系；uncovered parity 用预期未来即期率替代远期，包含风险溢价且实证偏离常见。不要把 UIP 当精确短期预测公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Covered interest parity locks the exchange rate with a forward contract and is approximately a no-arbitrage relation. Uncovered parity replaces the forward rate with the expected future spot rate, includes a risk premium, and often fails empirically. UIP is not an exact short-term forecasting formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以本币为基准，CIP 比较 $(1+i_d)$ 与 $(1+i_f)F/S$。若扣除交易成本后不相等，可借入低回报路径、投资高回报路径并用远期锁汇；现实偏离可来自资产负债表、信用、监管和市场分割，而不一定是免费套利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using domestic currency as the numeraire, CIP compares $(1+i_d)$ with $(1+i_f)F/S$. If they differ beyond transaction costs, one can borrow along the lower-return route, invest along the higher-return route, and lock the exchange rate forward. Real deviations may reflect balance-sheet constraints, credit, regulation, and market segmentation rather than free arbitrage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不可能三角与干预
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Impossible Trinity and Intervention*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本自由流动、固定汇率和独立货币政策不能三者同时完全实现。外汇干预是否改变货币条件取决于是否冲销，效果还受储备、信誉和市场深度影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Free capital mobility, a fixed exchange rate, and independent monetary policy cannot all be fully achieved at once. Whether foreign-exchange intervention changes monetary conditions depends on sterilization, while reserves, credibility, and market depth affect effectiveness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定汇率且资本高度流动时，本币利率需与锚货币回报相容，否则资本流动迫使央行买卖外汇并改变准备金。资本管制、浮动汇率或放弃独立政策分别放松不同边约束，但都带来成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a fixed exchange rate and highly mobile capital, the domestic interest rate must be compatible with returns in the anchor currency; otherwise capital flows force the central bank to trade foreign exchange and change reserves. Capital controls, a floating rate, or surrendering monetary independence each relax a different edge of the constraint and each carries costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：有套保和无套保美元投资
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Hedged and Unhedged Dollar Investment*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

英国投资者可获得英镑利率 4%，美元利率 5%。若美元一年远期相对即期贴水约 1%，套保美元回报约被降回 4%，符合 CIP；若不套保，实际英镑回报取决于一年后美元即期汇率，5% 利息可能被美元贬值超过抵消。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A UK investor can earn 4% in sterling or 5% in dollars. If the dollar trades at roughly a 1% one-year forward discount relative to spot, the hedged dollar return is brought back to about 4%, consistent with CIP. Without hedging, the sterling return depends on the future dollar spot rate, and dollar depreciation can more than offset the 5% interest return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有声明报价就谈升贬值容易把符号反转；用远期汇率直接当市场“预测”忽略套保利差与风险；用 UIP 做确定性套利混淆预期与锁定价格；讨论固定汇率独立政策而不写资本流动条件，会误用不可能三角。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Discussing appreciation without stating the quotation reverses signs easily. Treating the forward rate as a pure market forecast ignores hedging differentials and risk. Using UIP as deterministic arbitrage confuses an expectation with a locked price. Applying the impossible trinity without stating capital mobility misuses the result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### covered 与 uncovered interest parity 的关键差别是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is the key difference between covered and uncovered interest parity?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> CIP 用远期锁定汇率接近无套利；UIP 依赖未来即期汇率预期并含风险溢价。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> CIP uses a forward contract to lock the exchange rate and is close to a no-arbitrage condition; UIP depends on the expected future spot rate and contains a risk premium.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么讨论本币升值前要声明汇率报价？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must the exchange-rate quotation be stated before discussing domestic appreciation?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 本币/外币和外币/本币互为倒数，同一市场变化在两个报价中方向相反。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Domestic-per-foreign and foreign-per-domestic quotations are reciprocals, so the same market move has opposite directions under the two quotations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 高利率货币为什么不保证未套保高回报？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does a high-interest currency not guarantee a high unhedged return?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 汇率变化和风险溢价共同决定本币回报；高利差可能被持有期内货币贬值抵消。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Exchange-rate changes and the risk premium jointly determine the return in the investor's currency; depreciation during the holding period can offset the higher interest rate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
