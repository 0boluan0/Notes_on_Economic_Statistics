<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Choosing the unit of deletion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*删点诊断中的删除单位*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A deletion diagnostic asks what happens when a specified piece of data is removed and the model is refitted. The piece may be one row, one person's repeated records or a whole school. These are different perturbations. [[删点影响定义|Influence on a chosen coefficient or prediction]] must therefore be accompanied by the deletion unit: “no single row changes the slope much” does not imply “no school matters much.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

删点诊断问的是：移除指定的一部分数据并重拟合后，会发生什么。被移除的可以是一行、一个人的多次记录，或整所学校的数据；它们施加的是不同扰动。因此，说[[删点影响定义|某个系数或预测受多大影响]]时，还要交代删除单位。“没有单行显著改变斜率”，不意味着“没有哪所学校对斜率重要”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why one-at-a-time deletion can hide a joint effect
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 成组掩蔽

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Masking occurs when other observations keep supporting a fitted pattern after one candidate is omitted. The remaining support makes the one-case change look small. It is a statement about a diagnostic and a target, not proof that any of the supporting records is erroneous.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

掩蔽（masking）指的是：删去一个候选点后，其他观测仍在支撑同一个拟合形状，使单点删除带来的变化看起来很小。它描述诊断与目标之间的关系，不证明这些相互支持的记录有错。

> [!example] Two distant cases supporting each other / 两个远端观测相互支撑
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fit an intercept and slope by [[普通最小二乘|OLS]] to five observations:
> $(-1,-1),(0,1),(1,1),(10,30),(10,30)$.
> The last two are distinct observational units with identical measured values, not accidental duplicate records.
>
> With all five cases the slope is $179/61\approx2.934$. Delete either far case and it becomes $449/154\approx2.916$, a change of only about $0.019$. Delete both and the remaining three-point slope is $1$, a change of about $1.934$ from the full fit. Their individual full-fit [[Cook距离|Cook distances]] are only about $0.013$ each.
>
> These are three different refits. Adding the two small single-deletion changes does not recover the joint change: after the first deletion, the other far case has a new leverage and a different influence. The calculation establishes masking of the joint slope sensitivity; it does not establish which model describes a population correctly.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 对五个观测 $(-1,-1),(0,1),(1,1),(10,30),(10,30)$ 用[[普通最小二乘|OLS]]拟合截距与斜率。末尾两个来自不同观察单位，只是测量值相同，并非误重复的记录。
>
> 全部保留时斜率为 $179/61\approx2.934$；删去任意一个远点后为 $449/154\approx2.916$，只改变约 $0.019$；同时删去两点后，剩余三点斜率是 $1$，相对全样本改变约 $1.934$。它们各自在全样本下的单点 [[Cook距离]]仅约 $0.013$。
>
> 这是三次不同的重拟合。两次很小的单点变化相加，并不能还原联合变化：先删一个后，另一个点的杠杆与影响已经变了。计算证明联合斜率敏感性被逐点诊断掩蔽，不证明哪个模型正确描述总体。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Match the unit to the question and fitted model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 依赖数据中的删除单位

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A panel may contain ten yearly rows for each firm. Deleting one firm-year asks about that measurement while retaining nine records from the same firm. Deleting the firm asks about losing its entire observed trajectory. Which question matters depends on the sampling scheme, treatment assignment, dependence structure and target. The [[总体与样本|target population and observational units]] need to be explicit; no level becomes “the genuinely independent unit” merely because it has been chosen for deletion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一份面板数据可能每家公司有十年记录。删一个公司年份，是在保留其余九条记录时考察该次测量；删整家公司，则是在考察失去整条已观察轨迹。哪种问题重要，取决于抽样、处理分配、依赖结构与分析目标。[[总体与样本|目标总体和观察单位]]需要明确；某层级不会仅仅因为被选作删除单位，就成为“真正独立的单位”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For ordinary OLS, a joint deletion must leave enough independent design information to estimate the same coefficients. Removing the only observations in a category may make a coefficient unidentifiable. Then an undefined coefficient change is not zero influence; report that the specified comparison cannot be estimated. In other models, deleting a group may also change which effects or variance components can be estimated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于普通 OLS，联合删除后仍须留下足够的独立设计信息，才能估计同一组系数。若移除了某类别仅有的全部观测，某个系数可能不再可识别。此时未定义的系数变化不等于零影响，应报告指定比较无法估计。在其他模型中，删组也可能改变哪些效应或方差分量能够估计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing to [[聚类标准误边界|cluster-robust standard errors]] adjusts an uncertainty calculation under its own assumptions; it does not perform a group deletion or refit the point estimate. Conversely, a group-deletion comparison is not itself a valid confidence interval. Describe the refitting rule and compare the same named target on a compatible scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换用[[聚类标准误边界|聚类稳健标准误]]是在其自身假设下调整不确定性计算，不是在删组，也不替你重新拟合点估计。反过来，删组比较本身也不是有效的置信区间。需要说明重拟合规则，并在相容的尺度上比较同一个明确目标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Software details matter for correlated-data models. SAS GEE diagnostics distinguish observation deletion from cluster deletion and provide one-step approximations. In R's `lme4`, `influence(..., groups=...)` refits after removing each specified group; omitting `groups` treats individual rows as deletion groups. A restricted iteration budget may give an approximation, so check the returned convergence information. Fixed-effect coefficients, variance–covariance components and predictions are distinct comparison targets. Ordinary OLS closed-form single-case formulas cannot simply be substituted for these procedures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相关数据模型还需要核对软件实现。SAS 的 GEE 诊断区分逐观测与逐簇删除，并提供一步近似。R 的 `lme4` 中，`influence(..., groups=...)` 按指定组逐次移除并重拟合；省略 `groups` 时把每一行当作删除组。限制迭代次数可能只得到近似结果，因此要检查返回的收敛信息。固定效应系数、方差—协方差分量与预测，是不同的比较目标；不能直接用普通 OLS 单点闭式公式替代这些过程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose groups for an interpretable question, not by searching for a deletion that produces a preferred answer. If a joint perturbation reveals sensitivity, follow [[异常观测处理原则|the evidence-based handling procedure]] and report it. The evidence that a group matters and the justification for permanently excluding it are separate decisions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应围绕可解释的问题选择分组，而不是搜索能产生理想答案的删除组合。联合扰动若揭示敏感性，就按[[异常观测处理原则]]核查证据并报告。证明一组观测影响结果，与论证永久排除它，是两件不同的事。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Penn State STAT501, Lesson11 §11.6, Hospital Infection example](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat501/Lesson11.html): one-at-a-time deletion can miss two observations supporting the same fit. The five-point example above is an independent construction, calculated by separate OLS refits.
- [SAS, Case Deletion Diagnostic Statistics, GEE section](https://support.sas.com/documentation/cdl/en/statug/65328/HTML/default/statug_genmod_details41.htm): separate observation/cluster deletion and one-step approximations.
- [R lme4, influence.merMod, Arguments/Details/Value](https://search.r-project.org/CRAN/refmans/lme4/html/influence.merMod.html): group specification, row default, limited refitting, coefficient/covariance outputs and convergence flags.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- PSU 医院例核对逐点删除可能漏掉相互支撑的两点；本文五点例另行构造并逐次重拟合。
- SAS GEE 诊断核对逐观测/逐簇删除与一步近似的区分。
- R lme4 文档核对指定组、默认逐行、有限迭代、系数/协方差输出与收敛标记。
