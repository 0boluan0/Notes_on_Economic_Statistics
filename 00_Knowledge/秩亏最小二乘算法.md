# Solving rank-deficient least squares
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*秩亏最小二乘算法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When two coefficient choices give the same best fit, a solver needs a rule for choosing between them. Here the rule has two stages: first minimize the fitting residual, then choose the shortest coefficient vector among all tied minimizers. This is the [[最小范数最小二乘解|minimum-norm least-squares solution]]. Complete orthogonal factorization (COD) and singular value decomposition (SVD) are two ways to compute it while keeping track of the directions the data cannot identify.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当不同系数给出同样好的拟合时，求解器需要选解规则。这里分两步：先最小化拟合残差，再从并列最优解中选出最短的系数向量。这就是[[最小范数最小二乘解]]。完整正交分解（COD）与奇异值分解（SVD）是两条计算路线，都显式处理数据无法辨别的方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A repeated column leaves a free choice
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

> [!example] The fit fixes a sum / 拟合只确定一个和
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take
>
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\2&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad b=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> Each column supplies the same direction. The output is $Ax=(x_1+x_2)(1,2)^T$, so every exact solution satisfies $x_1+x_2=1$. Both $(1,0)^T$ and $(1/2,1/2)^T$ fit perfectly. Writing every solution as $x=(1/2+t,1/2-t)^T$ gives $\|x\|_2^2=1/2+2t^2$. The unique shortest one is $(1/2,1/2)^T$. Here $\|x\|_2$ is [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]] and $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个列提供相同方向，输出为 $Ax=(x_1+x_2)(1,2)^T$，所以精确解只需满足 $x_1+x_2=1$。$(1,0)^T$ 和 $(1/2,1/2)^T$ 都完全拟合；将全部解写成 $x=(1/2+t,1/2-t)^T$，长度平方为 $1/2+2t^2$，故唯一最短解为 $(1/2,1/2)^T$。$\|x\|_2$ 表示[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]，$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, $A$ has $m$ rows and $n$ columns, the target $b$ has $m$ entries, and the coefficients $x$ have $n$ entries. Its [[矩阵秩|rank]] $r$ counts independent column directions. Rank deficiency here means $r<\min(m,n)$; a wide full-row-rank matrix is a different case, explained in [[欠定不等于秩亏|underdetermined versus rank deficient]]. All [[线性最小二乘|least-squares]] minimizers have the same fitted output. They differ by a [[零空间|null-space]] vector $z$ satisfying $Az=0$. The shortest minimizer has no component in this invisible direction.

The following formulas use real matrices and ordinary Euclidean norms. For complex entries, transpose becomes conjugate transpose and orthogonal transformations become unitary; lengths use squared absolute values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，$A$ 有 $m$ 行、$n$ 列，目标 $b$ 有 $m$ 项，系数 $x$ 有 $n$ 项。[[矩阵秩|秩]] $r$ 计数独立列方向；此处“秩亏”指 $r<\min(m,n)$，宽且满行秩是另一情形，见[[欠定不等于秩亏]]。全部[[线性最小二乘]]最优解产生同一拟合输出，解之差属于满足 $Az=0$ 的[[零空间]]；最短解不含这种不可见方向的分量。

以下公式使用实矩阵和普通欧氏范数。复数时把转置改为共轭转置、正交变换改为酉变换，长度平方使用坐标绝对值的平方和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Complete orthogonal factorization
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 完整正交分解路线

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CPQR first reorders columns and uses [[QR分解|QR]] to expose a leading independent block. Merely solving for those columns and setting other original coordinates to zero can give $(1,0)^T$ in the example. That is a [[CPQR回代不保最小范数|basic solution]], not a general minimum-norm procedure. COD adds a change of coordinates in the **input** space, so that its free coordinates are genuinely perpendicular null-space directions.

For an exact rank-$r$ matrix with $r\ge1$, write the completed factorization as

$$A=Q\begin{pmatrix}T&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}V^T.$$

Here $Q$ is $m\times m$, $V$ is $n\times n$, both are [[正交矩阵|orthogonal]], and $T$ is an invertible $r\times r$ [[三角矩阵|upper-triangular]] block. The column permutation is absorbed into $V$. Let $Q_1,V_1$ be the first $r$ columns of these matrices. Solve $Ty=Q_1^Tb$ by triangular back substitution, then return $x_\star=V_1y$. There is no need to form $T^{-1}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CPQR 先重排列，再用 [[QR分解]]显出领先的独立块。只解这些列、把其他原坐标设为零，可能在开头例子中得到 $(1,0)^T$；这是[[CPQR回代不保最小范数|基本解]]，并非一般的最小范数步骤。COD 再对**输入**空间换坐标，使自由坐标真正对应正交的零空间方向。

对精确秩为 $r\ge1$ 的矩阵，可将完成后的分解写成上式。$Q$ 为 $m\times m$、$V$ 为 $n\times n$，两者都是[[正交矩阵]]，$T$ 是可逆的 $r\times r$ [[三角矩阵|上三角]]块，列置换已经合并进 $V$。令 $Q_1,V_1$ 为它们的前 $r$ 列；三角回代解 $Ty=Q_1^Tb$ 后返回 $x_\star=V_1y$，无须显式形成 $T^{-1}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see why, split the transformed coefficient $V^Tx=(y,z)$ and transformed target $Q^Tb=(c_1,c_2)$ after their first $r$ coordinates. Then

$$\|Ax-b\|_2^2=\|Ty-c_1\|_2^2+\|c_2\|_2^2,\qquad
\|x\|_2^2=\|y\|_2^2+\|z\|_2^2.$$

The equation $Ty=c_1$ gives the best residual. The remaining coordinate $z$ cannot change that residual, so setting $z=0$ makes the coefficients shortest. In the opening example these input coordinates are $y=(x_1+x_2)/\sqrt2$ and $z=(x_1-x_2)/\sqrt2$: fitting fixes the sum, and minimum norm removes the difference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把变换后的系数 $V^Tx=(y,z)$ 和目标 $Q^Tb=(c_1,c_2)$ 都在前 $r$ 项后分块，就得到上面两个平方和。解 $Ty=c_1$ 使残差最小；剩余坐标 $z$ 不能改变残差，因此再令 $z=0$ 使系数最短。开头例子中的输入坐标可取 $y=(x_1+x_2)/\sqrt2$、$z=(x_1-x_2)/\sqrt2$：拟合固定和，最小范数再去掉差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
LAPACK `DGELSY` follows this route. It chooses an effective rank using CPQR and a leading-block condition estimate controlled by `RCOND`, regards the remaining block as negligible, and applies right-side transformations before returning to the original coordinates. If a nonzero block was discarded, the displayed exact factorization belongs to that approximating matrix, not exactly to the original $A$. Ordinary CPQR's rank evidence has the [[CPQR秩揭示边界|separate guarantee boundary]] discussed there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

LAPACK `DGELSY` 采用这条路线：结合 CPQR、领先块条件估计和 `RCOND` 选有效秩，把剩余块视为可忽略，再从右侧作变换并还原原坐标。若舍去了非零块，上面的精确分解针对该近似矩阵，而非严格针对原 $A$。普通 CPQR 的秩证据另有[[CPQR秩揭示边界|保证边界]]。

## Singular value decomposition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## SVD 路线

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[奇异值分解|SVD]] writes the rank-$r$ matrix as $A=\sum_{i=1}^r\sigma_i u_i v_i^T$. The unit vector $v_i$ is an input direction, $u_i$ its output direction, and the positive [[奇异值|singular value]] $\sigma_i$ is the stretch between them. Different input directions, and different output directions, are orthonormal. Read the target coordinate $u_i^Tb$, divide by its stretch, and rebuild the coefficient:

$$x_\star=\sum_{i=1}^r\frac{u_i^Tb}{\sigma_i}\,v_i=A^+b.$$

$A^+$ denotes the [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse]]. Components of $b$ outside the column space remain as residual; unused null-space coordinates of $x$ are set to zero. This also works when no exact fit exists. For the opening $A$ but target $b=(1,0)^T$, fitting the scalar sum $s=x_1+x_2$ minimizes $(s-1)^2+4s^2$, giving $s=1/5$ and $x_\star=(1/10,1/10)^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[奇异值分解|SVD]]把秩为 $r$ 的矩阵写成 $A=\sum_{i=1}^r\sigma_i u_i v_i^T$。单位向量 $v_i$ 是输入方向，$u_i$ 是对应输出方向，正的[[奇异值]] $\sigma_i$ 是伸缩倍数；输入方向之间、输出方向之间各自标准正交。读取目标坐标 $u_i^Tb$，除以伸缩倍数，再沿 $v_i$ 合成系数，便得上式，其中 $A^+$ 是 [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆]]。$b$ 在列空间之外的部分留作残差，$x$ 中没有用到的零空间坐标设为零。这也适用于无法精确拟合的情形：开头的 $A$ 若配 $b=(1,0)^T$，拟合只需令 $s=x_1+x_2$ 最小化 $(s-1)^2+4s^2$，故 $s=1/5$，最短系数为 $(1/10,1/10)^T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the usual range `0 < RCOND < 1`, LAPACK `DGELSD` uses an SVD-based solve and counts singular values greater than `RCOND` times the largest singular value; values at or below the cutoff are treated as zero. A negative `RCOND` requests machine precision as the relative cutoff. It exposes the stretch information directly. Its cost relative to a QR-based solve depends on dimensions, implementation and requested output; it may require more computation or storage. Both routes also handle the zero matrix: all fits are zero, and the shortest coefficient is zero without any division by a singular value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在通常范围 `0 < RCOND < 1` 内，LAPACK `DGELSD` 使用 SVD 路线，把大于 `RCOND` 乘最大奇异值的方向计入有效秩，小于或等于阈值的方向视为零。`RCOND` 为负时则采用机器精度作为相对阈值。该路线直接提供伸缩尺度信息。它与 QR 路线的成本比较取决于尺寸、实现和输出要求，可能需要更多计算或存储。两条路线都可处理零矩阵：拟合只能为零，最短系数为零，无须除以任何奇异值。

## State the rank model before reporting the answer
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 决策时必须记录什么

> [!warning] Thresholding changes the problem / 截断会改变问题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,10^{-8})$ and $b=(1,10^{-8})^T$, the exact solution is $(1,1)^T$. If a singular-value threshold $\tau=10^{-6}$ discards the second direction, the new matrix is $A_\tau=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ and its minimum-norm least-squares answer is $(1,0)^T$. It has residual length $10^{-8}$ for the original data, rather than zero. This may be a justified noise decision, but it is not the exact solution of the original full-rank problem.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,10^{-8})$、$b=(1,10^{-8})^T$，精确解为 $(1,1)^T$。奇异值阈值 $\tau=10^{-6}$ 若删除第二方向，新矩阵成为 $A_\tau=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$，其最小范数最小二乘解为 $(1,0)^T$。对原数据它留下 $10^{-8}$ 的残差长度，而非零；这可以是有理由的噪声处理，但不是原满秩问题的精确解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Record the exact or effective rank, the [[数值秩|rank test and tolerance]], the matrix/variable scaling, and the norm used to select the solution. CPQR-based truncation and SVD truncation need not produce the same approximating matrix or rank even with the same `RCOND`. For an ordinary full-column-rank problem, [[薄QR最小二乘|thin QR]] is usually sufficient; for full-row-rank wide systems, use the separate underdetermined route. Minimum norm selects one fit-equivalent coefficient vector; it does not establish the true hidden coefficients, and [[小奇异值放大噪声|small retained singular values can still amplify noise]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

报告应包括精确秩或有效秩、[[数值秩|判秩方法与容差]]、矩阵及变量的缩放方式，以及选解所用范数。即使 `RCOND` 相同，CPQR 截断与 SVD 截断也未必产生同一个近似矩阵或秩。普通满列秩问题通常用[[薄QR最小二乘]]即可；宽且满行秩系统则走独立的欠定分支。最小范数只是在拟合无法区分的系数之间选一个，并不证明它是真实隐含系数，而且[[小奇异值放大噪声|保留的小奇异值仍会放大噪声]]。
