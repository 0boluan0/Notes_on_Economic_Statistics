# 完整正交分解或 SVD 可在选定秩模型下求出秩亏最小二乘的最小范数解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Complete orthogonal factorization or the SVD can compute a minimum-norm solution to rank-deficient least squares under the selected rank model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 怎样选方法
> 已确认 $\operatorname{rank}(A)<\min(m,n)$，或已按明确阈值采用较低有效秩时，使用完整正交分解（COD）或 SVD 路线显式处理零空间自由度。两条路线都能返回所选秩模型下的最小范数最小二乘解。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Once $\operatorname{rank}(A)<\min(m,n)$ has been established, or a lower effective rank has been adopted under an explicit threshold, use a complete orthogonal factorisation or an SVD-based route that handles null-space freedom explicitly. Either route can return the minimum-norm least-squares solution for the selected rank model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开始前先把形状与秩分类分开。欠定满行秩不属于秩亏分支，见 [[欠定不等于秩亏]]；过定满列秩则通常直接使用 [[薄QR最小二乘]]。这里只处理已经确认的秩亏或阈值降秩问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separate matrix shape from rank classification before choosing the algorithm. A full-row-rank underdetermined system is not rank deficient, while an overdetermined full-column-rank problem normally uses thin QR least squares. The rule here starts only after exact rank deficiency or thresholded rank reduction has been established.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 完整正交分解路线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Complete orthogonal factorization route*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

LAPACK `xGELSY` 先对 $A$ 做列主元 QR，再通过 `RCOND` 和领先三角块的条件估计选择有效秩。随后它从右侧施加正交变换，得到完整正交分解，并在变量空间中删除零空间分量。为什么仅做主元回代仍不够，见 [[CPQR回代不保最小范数]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
LAPACK `xGELSY` first performs column-pivoted QR, then uses `RCOND` and a condition estimate of the leading triangular block to choose an effective rank. Right orthogonal transformations complete the factorisation and remove the null-space component in variable space. [[CPQR回代不保最小范数|The pivot-back-substitution boundary]] explains why the first stage alone is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CPQR 是重要的起点，但它不自动拥有强秩揭示的全部保证，见 [[CPQR秩揭示边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CPQR is an important starting point, but it does not automatically carry every strong rank-revealing guarantee; see [[CPQR秩揭示边界|the CPQR rank-revealing boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## SVD 路线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*SVD route*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

SVD 直接列出奇异方向与奇异值。对原矩阵的所有严格正奇异值取倒数，得到精确的 $A^+b$；若按阈值把小但非零的奇异值也截成零，则得到截断模型的 $A_\tau^+b$。LAPACK `xGELSD` 正是按 `RCOND` 选择有效秩，再求该数值秩模型下的最小范数解。SVD 通常比 QR 路线提供更直接的敏感性信息，但计算与存储成本可能更高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The SVD displays singular directions and singular values directly. Reciprocating every strictly positive singular value gives the exact $A^+b$; truncating small but nonzero singular values instead gives $A_\tau^+b$ for a thresholded model. LAPACK `xGELSD` uses `RCOND` to select an effective rank and solves the minimum-norm problem for that numerical-rank model. The SVD usually exposes sensitivity more directly than a QR route, but may require more computation and storage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 决策时必须记录什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What the decision must record*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“精确秩亏”与“按容差视为秩亏”不是同一句话。凡是用 `RCOND` 或奇异值阈值选择有效秩，都应记录 [[数值秩]] 的阈值和缩放口径。否则“最小范数解”究竟是针对原矩阵还是截断模型，无法复核。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exact rank deficiency and rank deficiency declared under a tolerance are different statements. Whenever `RCOND` or a singular-value threshold selects an effective rank, record the threshold and scaling convention from [[数值秩|the numerical-rank definition]]. Otherwise one cannot tell whether the reported minimum-norm solution belongs to the original matrix or to a truncated model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 一个矩阵只是接近秩亏，却仍在代数上满列秩，能否不声明阈值就把它当成低秩矩阵？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a matrix is nearly rank deficient but still has full algebraic column rank, may it be treated as lower rank without stating a threshold?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。截断一个非零方向是建模与数值决策，必须报告有效秩阈值及其缩放方式。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. Truncating a nonzero direction is a modelling and numerical decision, so the effective-rank threshold and its scaling must be reported.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令
$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\2&2\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
b=\begin{bmatrix}1\\2\end{bmatrix}.
$$
$A$ 的秩为 $1<2$，全部精确解满足 $x_1+x_2=1$。COD 或 SVD 会选出 $x_\star=(1/2,1/2)^T$，它在这条解直线上具有最小 Euclidean 范数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the displayed rank-one system, every exact solution satisfies $x_1+x_2=1$. A complete orthogonal or SVD route returns $(1/2,1/2)^T$, the unique point of minimum Euclidean norm on that solution line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
