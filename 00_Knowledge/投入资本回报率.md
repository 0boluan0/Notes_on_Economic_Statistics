# 投入资本回报率是税后经营利润相对于匹配经营投入资本的比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return on invested capital relates after-tax operating profit to consistently measured operating invested capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

投入资本回报率（ROIC）衡量经营业务用投入资本创造税后经营利润的能力。分子通常是 NOPAT，即融资前的税后经营利润；分母是为该经营业务投入的资本，不能直接拿包含大量闲置现金的总资产来代替。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ROIC measures how operating invested capital generates after-tax operating profit. Its numerator is normally NOPAT, after-tax profit before financing. The denominator is capital invested in those operations, not automatically total assets including surplus cash.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡为与[[再投资增长关系]]配对，采用期初资本口径，通常要求经营投入资本 $IC_{t-1}>0$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To match [[再投资增长关系|the reinvestment-growth relation]], this atom uses opening invested capital, normally requiring $IC_{t-1}>0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$ROIC_t=\frac{NOPAT_t}{IC_{t-1}}.$$

例如期初经营投入资本 500、当年税后经营利润 60，ROIC 为 12%。资本为零时比率无定义；资本为负时不能按通常的“越高越好”解释。实务也可能用期初期末平均资本；横向比较时必须说明日期，并一致处理商誉、租赁与研发。会计投入资本不是企业市场价格，所以 ROIC 不是买入股票后能获得的收益率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Opening invested capital of 500 and NOPAT of 60 give ROIC of 12%. Zero capital makes the ratio undefined; negative capital invalidates its usual higher-is-better interpretation. Practice may use average opening and closing capital instead; comparisons must specify timing and consistently treat goodwill, leases, and R&D. Accounting invested capital is not the market purchase price, so ROIC is not a shareholder's investment return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

过去所有资产的平均 ROIC 也不等于下一笔投资的边际回报。老项目非常赚钱时，新项目的低回报可能暂时被掩盖；预测增长应问新增资本能带来什么，而非无条件沿用历史均值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Average ROIC on existing assets need not equal the marginal return on the next investment. Profitable legacy projects can mask weak new projects; growth forecasts need the return on additional capital rather than an unqualified historical average.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
