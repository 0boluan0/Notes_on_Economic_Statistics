# 政府和社会资本合作是由私人方承担公共资产或服务的重大管理责任和风险并按绩效获酬的长期合同安排
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A public–private partnership is a long-term public-asset or service contract with substantial private management and risk responsibilities and performance-linked remuneration.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政府和社会资本合作（PPP）在本卡采用世界银行《PPP Reference Guide》的通用定义：政府与私人方订立提供公共资产或服务的长期合同，由私人方承担重要管理责任和风险，并使其报酬与履约绩效相联系。各国法律对 PPP 的范围可能另有规定；不是只要“政府与企业一起出钱”就满足这个定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card follows the World Bank PPP Reference Guide: a government and a private party enter a long-term arrangement for a public asset or service, with substantial private management and risk responsibilities and remuneration linked to performance. National legal definitions can differ. Public and private co-investment alone is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，合同把一座污水处理设施的建设、维护和多年处理服务交给私人方，并把付款与可用性、处理量和水质要求相联系。这里关键的是跨期服务责任与激励，不只是买到一座设施。PPP 也可以涉及既有资产的改造、维护和运营，而不必每次都新建资产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, a contract can combine construction, maintenance and multi-year wastewater treatment, with payment linked to availability, volume and water-quality requirements. The distinguishing feature is sustained service responsibility and incentives, not merely purchasing a facility. PPPs can also upgrade, maintain and operate existing assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，BOT、TOT 等名称只能提供线索，仍要检查合同期限、承担的职能、风险和报酬安排；不能由缩写自动认定是不是 PPP。ABS 把贷款等资产集合后形成证券，属于证券化融资安排；这个名称本身没有说明公共服务合同和绩效责任，因而也不能单独证明存在 PPP。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Labels such as BOT or TOT are clues, not a sufficient classification: inspect duration, functions, risk and payment terms. Asset-backed securities pool assets such as loans into securities. Securitization alone does not establish a public-service contract or performance obligations and therefore does not establish a PPP.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

PPP 的私人方可以先筹集资金，但项目最后仍需要收入来维持服务并偿付资金提供者；这由 [[项目融资与付费来源]] 区分。具体风险交给谁，应按 [[PPP风险分配]] 判断。公共服务目标、生产者和资金来源也不必重合，见 [[公共品的筹资、生产与治理]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Private participants may raise initial capital, but the project still needs revenue to sustain services and repay capital providers; distinguish [[项目融资与付费来源|financing from ultimate payment sources]]. Apply [[PPP风险分配|risk-allocation criteria]] to individual risks, and separate [[公共品的筹资、生产与治理|public objectives, production and funding roles]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [World Bank PPP Reference Guide, What is a PPP?](https://ppp.worldbank.org/what-ppp-defining-public-private-partnership)：支持长期合同、公共资产或服务、重大私人管理责任与风险、绩效联系报酬这组定义条件。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Supports the defining conditions used in this card.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [World Bank PPP Reference Guide, PPP Contract Types and Terminology](https://ppp.worldbank.org/ppp-contract-types-and-terminology)，Functions Transferred、Payment Mechanism 与 PPP Contract Types：支持新建与既有资产、职能和付费方式，以及名称跨国不一致的边界；污水处理例子是据此构造的说明例。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Supports asset scope, contracted functions, payment mechanisms and inconsistent terminology across countries; the wastewater contract is an illustrative example.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [U.S. SEC, Asset-Backed Securities, Background](https://www.sec.gov/spotlight/dodd-frank/assetbackedsecurities.shtml)：仅支持 ABS 的资产集合与证券化性质；其本身不足以认定 PPP 的判断来自上面的 PPP 定义条件。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Supports the nature of securitization; the PPP classification follows separately from the defining contractual conditions above.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
