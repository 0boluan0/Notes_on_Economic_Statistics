# 权益净利率是归属于所选股东范围的净利润与其账面权益的比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return on equity is net income attributable to a specified shareholder group divided by that group's book equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

权益净利率（ROE）衡量每一元账面权益对应多少期间净利润。设归属于所选股东范围的净利润为 $NI_E$、匹配的权益存量为 $E^*$，则 $ROE=NI_E/E^*$。全年表现常配平均权益；CPA 例题用期末权益，ABC 为 $136/960\approx14.17\%$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ROE measures period net income per unit of book equity. For income $NI_E$ attributable to the selected shareholders and matched equity stock $E^*$, $ROE=NI_E/E^*$. Average equity is common for annual performance; CPA uses ending equity, giving ABC approximately 14.17%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“匹配”包括股东范围：集团全部净利润要配包括非控股权益的全部权益；归属于母公司股东的净利润要配相应母公司股东权益。若计算普通股 ROE，分子还须按权利扣除不属于普通股的收益，分母也只取相应普通股权益。日期与范围都要在[[财务比率口径匹配]]中说清。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Matching includes ownership scope. Total consolidated income pairs with total equity including non-controlling interests; income attributable to parent shareholders pairs with their equity. Common-equity ROE excludes income and equity belonging to other claims as required by their rights. Both timing and ownership scope belong in [[财务比率口径匹配|ratio-basis matching]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

账面权益是资产减负债后的会计余值，见[[净值]]，并不等于股东买股票时支付的市价。因而 ROE 不是股票持有期收益率。$E^*=0$ 时比率无定义；权益接近零或为负时，常规的“越高越好”解释也不可靠。利润与融资结构的关系见[[杜邦分解]]，评价边界见[[ROE与杠杆的解释边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Book equity is the accounting residual of assets less liabilities; see [[净值|net worth]]. It is not the stock's market purchase price, so ROE is not a holding-period stock return. Zero equity makes the ratio undefined; tiny or negative equity undermines a routine higher-is-better interpretation. [[杜邦分解|DuPont decomposition]] relates profitability to financing structure, with the [[ROE与杠杆的解释边界|interpretive boundary]] treated separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
