# 0 除以 0 只说明直接代入未定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Zero over zero says only that direct substitution is indeterminate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> $0/0$ 是未定式，只说明直接代入无法决定比值的趋势；化简后的极限可能是有限值、无穷或不存在。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $0/0$ is an indeterminate form. It says only that direct substitution cannot determine the ratio's behaviour; after rewriting, the limit may be finite, infinite, or nonexistent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么未定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the form is indeterminate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样代入得到 $0/0$，$x/x$ 在 $x\to0$ 时趋于 $1$，$x^2/x$ 趋于 $0$，而 $x/x^2=1/x$ 的双侧极限不存在。形式相同，趋势不同，所以 $0/0$ 本身不是答案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Direct substitution gives $0/0$ in all three cases, yet $x/x\to1$, $x^2/x\to0$, and $x/x^2=1/x$ has no two-sided limit as $x\to0$. The same form can conceal different behaviours, so $0/0$ is not itself an answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 直接代入得到 $0/0$ 后，已经知道极限不存在了吗？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If direct substitution gives $0/0$, do you already know that the limit does not exist?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不知道。它只说明必须继续化简、改写或选用有条件的极限工具。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. It tells you only that further simplification, rewriting, or an applicable limit theorem is needed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
