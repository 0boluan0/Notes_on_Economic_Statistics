# The zero-over-zero indeterminate form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零比零未定式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a numerator and a denominator both approach zero. Their ratio depends on how they approach zero relative to each other. Knowing only the two limiting values therefore does not determine the ratio's behaviour. This situation is called the **$0/0$ indeterminate form**. It is a description of a [[函数极限|limit problem]], not a new value assigned to division by zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分子与分母都趋于零时，比值取决于它们相对以怎样的方式靠近零。仅知道两个极限值都为零，并不能确定比值的趋势。这种情形叫 **$0/0$ 未定式**。它描述的是一个[[函数极限]]问题，不是给除以零分配了一个新数值。

> [!important] What the notation records · 记号记录了什么
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Along the specified approach, $f(x)\to0$ and $g(x)\to0$, while $f(x)/g(x)$ is evaluated only at allowed inputs where $g(x)\ne0$. We call the ratio's limiting form $0/0$. The [[极限定律适用条件|quotient limit law]] cannot decide it, because that law requires a nonzero limit for the denominator. A finite limit, divergence to infinity and oscillation are all possible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 沿指定趋近方向，$f(x)\to0$、$g(x)\to0$，而 $f(x)/g(x)$ 只在分母非零的合法输入处计算。此时称比值具有 $0/0$ 的极限形式。[[极限定律适用条件|商的极限定律]]要求分母极限非零，因此不能据此给出答案。有限极限、趋于无穷、持续振荡都有可能。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The numerator and denominator need not even be defined at the approached point. Conversely, obtaining the symbols $0/0$ by plugging in the point is not enough if the component functions are discontinuous there: the form is established by their **nearby limits**, not merely their point values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分子与分母甚至不必在目标点有定义。反过来，如果它们在该点不连续，仅把点代进去得到两个零也还不够：未定式由**附近的极限**确定，而不只看点值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the form is indeterminate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么未定

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $x\to0$ through nonzero real inputs. Each numerator and denominator below tends to zero, but the ratios behave differently:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $x$ 沿非零实数趋于0。下列每行的分子、分母都趋于零，但比值行为不同：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Ratio | Equal expression for $x\ne0$ | Behaviour as $x\to0$ |
|---|---|---|
| $x/x$ | $1$ | Limit $1$ |
| $x^2/x$ | $x$ | Limit $0$ |
| $x/x^3$ | $1/x^2$ | Tends to $+\infty$ |
| $x/x^2$ | $1/x$ | Opposite infinite behaviours on the two sides |
| $x\sin(1/x)/x$ | $\sin(1/x)$ | Oscillates without a limit |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前两行分别趋于1与0；第三行从两侧都趋于正无穷；第四行右侧趋于正无穷、左侧趋于负无穷；第五行不停振荡。相同的 $0/0$ 形式，不能区分这些结果。更一般地，任意固定实数 $c$ 都可由 $cx/x=c$ 得到，所以形式本身连有限答案的大小都不限制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the oscillating example, the numerator really tends to zero: $|x\sin(1/x)|\le |x|$, so any small enough $|x|$ forces it close to zero. But the ratio takes value $1$ at $x_n=1/(\pi/2+2\pi n)$ and value $-1$ at $y_n=1/(3\pi/2+2\pi n)$, for positive integers $n$. Both input sequences approach zero, so their incompatible output values rule out a single limit. A table or plot of a few inputs can miss such oscillations; the two explicit sequences give a mathematical reason.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

振荡例中的分子确实趋于零，因为 $|x\sin(1/x)|\le|x|$，让 $|x|$ 足够小便能控制分子。但对正整数 $n$，比值在 $x_n=1/(\pi/2+2\pi n)$ 处等于1，在 $y_n=1/(3\pi/2+2\pi n)$ 处等于 $-1$。两列输入都趋于零，输出却互不相容，因此不可能有一个共同极限。只试少量数值或看图可能漏掉振荡；这两列明确的输入给出了数学理由。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A hole in the formula may be removable
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*表达式的缺口可能可以补上*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $(x^2-1)/(x-1)$ as $x\to1$, both component limits are zero. For $x\ne1$, factor the numerator and cancel the nonzero factor:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于 $x\to1$ 时的 $(x^2-1)/(x-1)$，分子分母极限都为零。但在 $x\ne1$ 时可以因式分解并约去非零因子：

$$\frac{x^2-1}{x-1}=\frac{(x-1)(x+1)}{x-1}=x+1\longrightarrow2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cancellation is valid at every nearby input that the original limit tests. It does not claim that the original fraction has a value at $x=1$. The [[点值不决定极限|point value is separate from the limit]]; if we wish to extend the function continuously to that point, the limit tells us to choose the new value $2$.

After identifying $0/0$, use the expression's structure: factorization, rationalization or a bound may resolve it. [[洛必达法则|L'Hôpital's rule]] is another possible tool only after its own hypotheses are checked; the symbols $0/0$ alone are not permission to differentiate a numerator and denominator. The broader decision process is in [[极限计算流程|evaluating a limit]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个约分对原极限检查的每个附近输入都有效，却没有声称原分式在 $x=1$ 处有值。[[点值不决定极限|点值与极限是两回事]]；若希望把函数连续地延伸到该点，极限告诉我们应补值2。

识别出 $0/0$ 后，继续看表达式结构：因式分解、有理化或上下界可能解决问题。[[洛必达法则]]是另一种可选工具，但要先检查它自己的假设；仅看到 $0/0$，并不能直接对分子分母分别求导。完整的判断过程见[[极限计算流程]]。
