# 实时宏观判断须同时固定指标定义、观测期间、发布日期和数据版本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Real-time macroeconomic analysis must fix the measure, observation period, release date, and data vintage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先写决策时点，再为每个输入记录：国家与覆盖范围、单位及名义/实际口径、同比或环比与季调方式、数据所指期间、首次及修订发布日期。只用决策时点已经发布的版本；事后修订值可用来评价误差，不能悄悄代入当时的判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the decision date. For each input record its country and coverage, units and nominal/real basis, growth and seasonal-adjustment convention, observation period, and release or revision dates. Use only vintages available then; later revisions may evaluate errors but must not silently replace real-time inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如教学数据在 1 月首次报告上季度增长 0.2%，3 月修订为 −0.1%。模拟 1 月决策若使用 −0.1%，就是使用未来信息。又如年度实际 GDP 增长与单月 CPI 同比不具有相同频率，不能直接当作两个同时到达的即时状态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a constructed example reports quarterly growth of 0.2% in January, revised to −0.1% in March. A January backtest using −0.1% uses future information. Annual real GDP growth and monthly CPI inflation also have different frequencies and availability dates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

合成指标还需统一方向、标准化和权重，并记录缺值处理；比较时保留同一版本规则。中国货币统计的专门变更复用[[货币数据可比性]]与[[货币总量统计口径]]，而不是把 M1/M2 当成跨国跨年固定不变的自然分类。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Composite indicators also require consistent orientation, scaling, weights, and missing-value treatment. For monetary-series changes, reuse [[货币数据可比性|monetary-data comparability]] and [[货币总量统计口径|monetary-aggregate definitions]] rather than treating M1 and M2 as immutable categories.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
