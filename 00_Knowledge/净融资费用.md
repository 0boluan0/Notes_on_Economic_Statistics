# 净融资费用是归入金融侧的费用扣除金融收益后的净成本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net financing expense is the net cost of financing expenses less financial income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净融资费用把按[[管理用报表分类]]归入金融侧的利息等费用，与金融资产收益相抵。采用“费用为正”的符号，税前净融资费用 $NFE_{pre}=\text{金融费用}-\text{金融收益}$；若金融收益更多，这个净成本可以为负。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net financing expense offsets expenses such as interest against financial income, following [[管理用报表分类|operating-financing classification]]. With expenses signed positively, pre-tax NFE is financial expenses less financial income. It can be negative when financial income exceeds expenses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CPA 将 ABC 的财务费用 110 与金融投资收益 6 相抵，得到税前净融资费用 104；按 32% 分配抵税效应 33.28，税后净融资费用为 70.72。因此管理表里的“税后利息费用”并不是简单的 $110(1-32\%)$，也不是本期实际支付利息现金的同义词。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CPA offsets ABC's finance expense of 110 against financial investment income of 6, giving pre-tax NFE of 104. Allocating a 32% tax benefit yields after-tax NFE of 70.72. The management statement's “after-tax interest expense” is therefore neither simply gross interest times one minus tax nor necessarily cash interest paid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，若 $B_F$ 是一致分配给金融侧的税收利益，则 $NFE_{after}=NFE_{pre}-B_F$。只有税率和税效应条件允许时，才写成 $NFE_{pre}(1-\tau)$；不能先假定所有利息都能立即省下同额税款。这个税后净成本与[[税后经营净利润]]配对，组成[[管理用利润表拆分]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, let $B_F$ be the tax benefit consistently allocated to financing: $NFE_{after}=NFE_{pre}-B_F$. The shortcut $NFE_{pre}(1-\tau)$ requires suitable tax-rate and tax-benefit conditions; interest does not automatically deliver an immediate proportional tax saving. Pair this after-tax cost with [[税后经营净利润|NOPAT]] in the [[管理用利润表拆分|income reconciliation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
