# 有限责任将投资者对企业债务的责任限制在规定的出资范围内

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Limited liability restricts an investor's responsibility for business debts to a specified capital commitment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限责任把企业的债务与投资者其他个人财产分开：在已足额履行出资义务、没有额外个人担保等责任的通常公司模型中，股东可以损失全部投入，但不因企业还不上债就必须以其他个人财产补足。它限制的是责任范围，不是承诺投资不会亏损，也不是免除企业本身的债务。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Limited liability separates business debts from an investor's other personal assets. In the usual corporate model, with capital commitments fully paid and no additional personal guarantee or other liability, shareholders can lose their entire investment without having to cover the firm's shortfall from other personal assets. It limits liability; it neither guarantees the investment nor cancels the firm's debt.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如股东投入 20，公司最终可供清偿的资产只有 30、债务为 50。若没有其他追索来源，股东的投资归零；缺口 20 不自动变成股东的个人债务。实际案件仍须检查组织形式、出资义务、担保和适用法律，不能从一个收益模型推定全部法律责任。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose an investor contributes 20 and the company ends with 30 of assets available against debt of 50. Without another source of recourse, the equity investment is wiped out; the shortfall of 20 does not automatically become the shareholder's personal debt. Actual liability depends on organizational form, capital obligations, guarantees, and applicable law, not just a payoff model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
