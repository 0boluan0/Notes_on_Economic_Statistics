# 事件独立等价于联合概率分解为边际概率乘积
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Event independence is equivalent to factorising the joint probability into marginal probabilities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 事件 $A$ 与 $B$ 独立，当且仅当
> $$P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B).$$
> 若 $P(B)>0$，这等价于 $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$：得知 $B$ 不改变 $A$ 的概率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> Events $A$ and $B$ are independent exactly when
> $$P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B).$$
> If $P(B)>0$, this is equivalent to $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$: learning $B$ does not change the probability of $A$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 判断依据
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Basis for the judgement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

独立性必须由联合概率或生成机制推出，不能由事件名称“看起来无关”推出。乘积定义对零概率事件仍有意义；条件概率等价式只在条件事件概率为正时可用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Independence must follow from the joint probabilities or the generating mechanism, not from labels that merely sound unrelated. The product definition remains meaningful for zero-probability events; the conditional-probability equivalence requires a conditioning event with positive probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若 $P(A)=0.4$、$P(B)=0.5$、$P(A\cap B)=0.2$，$A$ 与 $B$ 是否独立？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $P(A)=0.4$, $P(B)=0.5$, and $P(A\cap B)=0.2$, are $A$ and $B$ independent?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 是，因为 $0.2=0.4\times0.5$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Yes, because $0.2=0.4\times0.5$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
