# Independent events
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*事件独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two events are **independent** when learning that one occurred does not change the probability of the other, whenever that conditional probability is defined. Imagine two coin tosses: knowing the first result need not help us predict the second. Independence makes this intuition precise and tells us when we may multiply two individual probabilities to obtain the probability that both events occur.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个事件**独立**，意思是在相应条件概率有定义时，知道其中一个事件发生，不会改变另一个事件的概率。想象两次抛硬币：知道第一次的结果，不一定能帮助我们预测第二次。独立性把这个想法写成精确条件，也告诉我们，什么时候可以把两个事件各自的概率相乘，得到它们同时发生的概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **event** is a collection of possible outcomes: for example, all outcomes in which the first toss is heads. A [[离散概率空间|discrete probability model]] specifies these outcomes and their probabilities. Write $P(A)$ for the probability of event $A$, and $A\cap B$ for the event that **both** $A$ and $B$ occur.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**事件**是一组可能结果，例如“第一次抛出正面”包含的所有结果。[[离散概率空间|离散概率模型]]规定这些结果及其概率。用 $P(A)$ 表示事件 $A$ 的概率，用 $A\cap B$ 表示 $A$ 与 $B$ **同时发生**的事件。

> [!abstract] The test for two events · 两个事件的独立性条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Events $A$ and $B$ are independent exactly when
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 事件 $A$、$B$ 独立，当且仅当
>
> $$P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The left side is their actual joint probability in the model. The right side multiplies their individual probabilities. Independence is the assertion that these two quantities agree.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 左边是模型中两个事件实际同时发生的概率，右边是它们各自概率的乘积。独立性说的是这两个量相等。

> [!example] Checking two coin tosses · 检查两次抛硬币
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider a model in which $HH,HT,TH,TT$ each have probability $1/4$, with $H$ for heads and $T$ for tails. Let $A$ mean “the first toss is heads” and $B$ mean “the second toss is heads”. Two outcomes satisfy each event, but only $HH$ satisfies both:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑一个模型：$HH,HT,TH,TT$ 四种结果的概率都为 $1/4$，其中 $H$ 表示正面，$T$ 表示反面。令 $A$ 表示“第一次是正面”，$B$ 表示“第二次是正面”。每个事件都包含两种结果，同时满足两个事件的只有 $HH$：
>
> $$P(A)=\frac12,\qquad P(B)=\frac12,\qquad P(A\cap B)=\frac14=\frac12\times\frac12.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus these events are independent in this model. If we learn that $B$ occurred, only $HH$ and $TH$ remain; exactly half of that remaining probability belongs to $A$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因此，在这个模型中两个事件独立。知道 $B$ 已发生后，只剩下 $HH$ 与 $TH$；其中恰好一半的剩余概率属于 $A$。

## Why the product means “no change after learning”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*乘积条件为什么表示信息不改变概率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[条件概率|Conditional probability]] $P(A\mid B)$ measures the chance of $A$ after we restrict attention to the known event $B$. When $P(B)>0$, its definition gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[条件概率]] $P(A\mid B)$ 描述把考虑范围限制在已知事件 $B$ 内以后，$A$ 的概率。当 $P(B)>0$ 时，根据定义，

$$P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the events are independent, substitute $P(A)P(B)$ for the numerator and cancel $P(B)$; the result is $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$. Conversely, multiplying that equality by $P(B)$ recovers the product condition. The product condition is symmetric in $A$ and $B$, so independence works in both directions; $P(B\mid A)=P(B)$ also holds when $P(A)>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果两个事件独立，就把分子换成 $P(A)P(B)$，约去 $P(B)$ 后得到 $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$。反过来，把这个等式两边乘以 $P(B)$，就能得到乘积条件。乘积条件中 $A$ 与 $B$ 的地位对称，所以独立性是双向的：当 $P(A)>0$ 时，也有 $P(B\mid A)=P(B)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product definition still works when a probability is zero. If $P(B)=0$, then $P(A\cap B)=0=P(A)P(B)$ for every event $A$. Thus a zero-probability event is independent of every event according to this definition, even though the elementary conditional probability $P(A\mid B)$ is undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当某个事件概率为零时，乘积定义仍然适用。若 $P(B)=0$，则对任何事件 $A$ 都有 $P(A\cap B)=0=P(A)P(B)$。因此，按这个定义，零概率事件与任何事件都独立，尽管初等条件概率 $P(A\mid B)$ 此时没有定义。

> [!warning] Mutually exclusive is a different property · 互斥是另一种关系
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On one fair die roll, “the result is $1$” and “the result is $2$” are mutually exclusive: they cannot both occur. Their joint probability is $0$, whereas the product is $(1/6)(1/6)=1/36$. They are therefore dependent. Learning that the result is $1$ changes the chance of $2$ from $1/6$ to $0$. In general, [[正概率互斥不独立|mutually exclusive events with positive probabilities cannot be independent]]; the positive-probability qualification matters.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 同一次公平骰子投掷中，“点数是 $1$”与“点数是 $2$”互斥，也就是不能同时发生。它们的联合概率为 $0$，各自概率的乘积却是 $(1/6)(1/6)=1/36$，因此不独立。知道点数为 $1$，会把出现 $2$ 的概率从 $1/6$ 变成 $0$。一般地，[[正概率互斥不独立|两个正概率事件互斥时不可能独立]]，其中“正概率”这个条件不能省略。

## Checking the model before multiplying
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*相乘之前先检查模型*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To establish independence in a given model, calculate the joint probability and compare it with the product. For example, $P(A)=0.4$, $P(B)=0.5$, and $P(A\cap B)=0.2$ satisfy the test. Knowing only the first two numbers would not be enough: the joint probability must come from the model, not from assuming the conclusion we want to check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要在给定模型中判定独立，应先算出联合概率，再与各自概率的乘积比较。例如，$P(A)=0.4$、$P(B)=0.5$、$P(A\cap B)=0.2$ 就满足独立性条件。只知道前两个数还不够：联合概率必须从模型得到，不能先假设独立来相乘，再用这个结果“证明”独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real experiment, independence is often a modelling assumption justified by how observations are generated. Descriptions such as “different people” or “different times” do not by themselves establish it. With three or more events, checking each pair only establishes [[两两独立|pairwise independence]]; [[相互独立|mutual independence]] also controls larger combinations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面对实际实验，独立性常常是一项建模假设，需要依据观测怎样产生来判断是否合理。“不同的人”或“不同时间”这样的描述，本身不能证明独立。有三个及以上事件时，逐对检查只能建立[[两两独立]]；[[相互独立]]还要求更大组合也满足相应的乘积条件。
