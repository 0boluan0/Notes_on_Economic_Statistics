# 经营周转或融资比例变化可能使实际销售增长偏离留存权益支持的增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changes in operating turnover or financing ratios can make actual sales growth differ from growth supported by retained equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[可持续增长率]]保持经营与财务政策不变，实际销售增长却可能发生在政策正在改变的年份。要解释两者差异，先沿销售、资产、权益三个数走一遍，而不是把一个增长率当作另一个的预测误差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[可持续增长率|Sustainable growth]] holds operating and financial policies fixed; actual growth may occur in a year when they change. Explain the difference through sales, assets, and equity rather than automatically treating one rate as a forecast error of the other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $S_t$ 为当期销售，$A_t,E_t$ 为对应期末资产和权益，所有分母为正。由 $S_t=(S_t/A_t)(A_t/E_t)E_t$，可得：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $S_t$ denote period sales and $A_t,E_t$ the corresponding ending assets and equity, with positive denominators. Since $S_t=(S_t/A_t)(A_t/E_t)E_t$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac{S_1}{S_0}=\frac{S_1/A_1}{S_0/A_0}\times\frac{A_1/E_1}{A_0/E_0}\times\frac{E_1}{E_0}.$$

若资产周转率和权益乘数都保持不变，销售增长就与权益增长相同。这是充分条件，不是必要条件：周转率提高 20%、权益乘数同时除以 1.2，也会相互抵消而保留相同增长率。周转改善、增加杠杆或发行股权都可能让销售增长超过原政策所支持的速度；它们分别改变了不同环节，不能统称为经营效率提高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Constant asset turnover and a constant equity multiplier are sufficient, not necessary, for sales and equity to grow at the same rate. A 20% rise in turnover can be offset by dividing the equity multiplier by 1.2. Better turnover, greater leverage, or new equity can support faster sales growth, but they affect different parts of the identity and are not all improvements in operating efficiency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CPA 例 2-4 中，20×2 到 20×3 年销售从 1,100 增至 1,650，增长 50%；权益从 363 增至 412.5，只增长约 13.64%。资产周转率不变，但负债从 66 增至 231，权益乘数由 $429/363$ 上升至 $643.5/412.5$。一次提高杠杆帮助完成了 50% 的扩张；13.64% 则是当期留存权益增长，不能据此把实际销售增长改写成 13.64%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In CPA Example 2-4, sales rise from 1,100 to 1,650, or 50%, while equity rises from 363 to 412.5, about 13.64%. Turnover stays constant, but liabilities rise from 66 to 231 and the equity multiplier increases. The one-time leverage increase supports the larger expansion; 13.64% remains retained-equity growth rather than actual sales growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把更高杠杆继续维持下去，与每年再提高一次杠杆，是两件事。同样，恢复旧杠杆可能要求还债并缩减资产，但收缩多少还取决于利润、分配、周转和新增资本，不能说高速增长之后必然按固定比例衰退。分析某个比率改善的效果时，也必须检查其余比率是否发生抵消变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maintaining higher leverage differs from increasing it again each year. Returning to lower leverage may require repayment and asset reduction, but the extent depends on profits, distributions, turnover, and capital raised. Rapid growth does not imply a universal subsequent contraction. An improvement in one ratio must also be checked against offsetting changes in others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
