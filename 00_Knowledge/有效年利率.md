# 名义年利率必须结合复利频率换算为有效年利率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A nominal annual rate must be combined with compounding frequency to obtain an effective annual rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子换算
> 名义年利率 $j$ 若每年复利 $m$ 次，每期利率为 $j/m$，一年实际积累因子为 $(1+j/m)^m$，所以
> $$EAR=\left(1+\frac{j}{m}\right)^m-1.$$
> 不说明复利频率的名义年率是不完整的，也不能与另一复利频率的名义年率直接比较。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> If a nominal annual rate $j$ compounds $m$ times per year, the period rate is $j/m$ and the one-year accumulation factor is $(1+j/m)^m$. Therefore
> $$EAR=\left(1+\frac{j}{m}\right)^m-1.$$
> A nominal annual rate without its compounding frequency is incomplete and cannot be compared directly with a nominal rate using another frequency.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

月利率 $1\%$ 与“名义年率 $12\%$、按月复利”描述同一合同，但一年后的有效回报是 $(1.01)^{12}-1\approx12.68\%$，不是 $12\%$。折现前还要让有效利率期间与现金流间隔一致：月现金流用月有效率，年度现金流可用 EAR。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A monthly rate of $1\%$ and “12% nominal annual, compounded monthly” describe the same contract, but the effective annual return is $(1.01)^{12}-1\approx12.68\%$, not $12\%$. Before discounting, the effective-rate period must match the cash-flow interval: monthly cash flows use a monthly effective rate; annual cash flows may use EAR.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两笔贷款都标“年利率 8%”，一笔按年复利，一笔按月复利。哪笔有效年成本更高？
>
> **答案：** 按月复利者更高：$(1+0.08/12)^{12}-1\approx8.30\%$，而按年复利者 EAR 正好为 8%。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Two loans both quote “8% per year”; one compounds annually and the other monthly. Which has the higher effective annual cost?
>
> **Answer:** The monthly-compounded loan: $(1+0.08/12)^{12}-1\approx8.30\%$, while annual compounding has an EAR of exactly 8%.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
