# 远期合约是双方约定在未来按确定价格交付标的或结算相应差额的合约
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A forward commits two parties to future delivery at an agreed price or settlement of the corresponding difference*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

远期合约（forward）今天约定标的、数量、交割日和交割价格 $K$，将来依约履行。多头是约定买入的一方，空头是约定卖出的一方。双方通常在场外协商条款；价格后来变得不利，并不会像[[期权]]那样给一方任意放弃履约的权利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A forward specifies the underlying, quantity, delivery date, and delivery price $K$ today. The long commits to buying and the short to selling. Terms are usually negotiated over the counter. An adverse price move does not grant the unilateral right to abandon performance that an [[期权|option]] provides.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设合约覆盖一单位标的，双方均履约，到期现货价格为 $S_T$。多头付 $K$ 得到价值 $S_T$ 的资产，因此到期净支付价值为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one unit, with both parties performing, the long pays $K$ for an asset worth the terminal spot price $S_T$. Its net terminal payoff is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$X_T^{\mathrm{long}}=S_T-K,\qquad X_T^{\mathrm{short}}=K-S_T.$$

若 $K=100$，到期标的值 120，则多头得到的资产比支付多 20；若值 80，则差额为 −20。现金结算可以只交换这个差额。数量为 $q$ 时乘以 $q$，但必须先确认价格和数量的单位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $K=100$, an asset worth 120 leaves the long 20 ahead at settlement; an asset worth 80 leaves it 20 behind. Cash settlement can exchange the difference directly. Multiply by $q$ for quantity $q$, after checking the units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交割价格 $K$ 是未来交换的约定价格；合约今天的**市值**是另一个量。按公平条件新签的普通远期可有零初始市值，但旧合约随着市场变化会变成正值或负值。外汇情形的具体贴现见[[外汇远期估值]]。是否抵押、追加保证金或允许提前终止，另由合同决定；“远期”不等于绝无中途现金要求。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The delivery price $K$ is not the contract's current market value. A newly agreed fair forward may have zero initial value, while an existing contract acquires positive or negative value as markets change. [[外汇远期估值|FX forward valuation]] gives the discounted calculation. Collateral, margin, and early-termination provisions are separate contractual terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
