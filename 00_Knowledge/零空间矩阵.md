# A nullspace matrix has a nullspace basis as its columns and parameterizes every homogeneous solution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*零空间矩阵以零空间的一组基为列并参数化全部齐次解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ of rank $r$, a nullspace matrix $N\in\mathbb R^{n\times(n-r)}$ has a [[基|basis]] of [[零空间|$N(A)$]] as its columns. Consequently,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对秩为 $r$ 的矩阵 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，零空间矩阵 $N\in\mathbb R^{n\times(n-r)}$ 以[[零空间|$N(A)$]] 的一组[[基]]为列，因此

$$
AN=0,\qquad N(A)=\{Nc:c\in\mathbb R^{n-r}\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each fixed coefficient vector $c$ gives one solution; allowing all coefficients gives the whole space. The matrix $N$ is a representation of the solution directions, whereas $N(A)$ is a set. The columns may be obtained by [[RREF读取零空间基|activating free variables one at a time]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个固定的系数向量 $c$ 给出一个解，允许系数任意变化才得到整个空间。矩阵 $N$ 表示解方向，$N(A)$ 则是一个集合。各列可通过[[RREF读取零空间基|逐个激活自由变量]]得到。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If pivot coordinates already come first and $R=\begin{bmatrix}I_r&F\\0&0\end{bmatrix}$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若主元坐标已经排在前面，且 $R=\begin{bmatrix}I_r&F\\0&0\end{bmatrix}$，则

$$
N=\begin{bmatrix}-F\\I_{n-r}\end{bmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity block holds different free-variable choices in separate columns. It proves independence and lets every free-coordinate vector appear. If columns were reordered, [[列置换后的解向量|restore the original coordinate order]] before using $N$ with $A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位矩阵块在不同列放置不同的自由变量取值，既保证线性无关，也允许任意自由坐标向量出现。若曾重排列，必须先[[列置换后的解向量|恢复原坐标顺序]]，才能把所得 $N$ 用于原矩阵 $A$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation $AN=0$ alone does not prove completeness or independence. For example, a zero column satisfies it but cannot span a nontrivial nullspace. If $r=n$, the nullspace is $\{0\}$ and the basis has no vectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仅有 $AN=0$ 不能证明解方向完整或独立。例如零列也满足该式，却无法张成非平凡零空间。若 $r=n$，则零空间为 $\{0\}$，其基为空向量组。
