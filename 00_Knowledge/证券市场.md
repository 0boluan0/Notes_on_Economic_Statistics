# 证券市场是组织证券发行与交易的制度和交易网络
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A securities market is the institutional and trading network through which securities are issued and traded.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证券市场把证券的供给者、投资者及中介连接起来，使[[股票]]、[[债券]]等权利能够发行、定价和转让。“市场”不必是一座交易大厅，也不意味着所有交易集中在同一个系统。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A securities market connects issuers, investors and intermediaries so that claims such as [[股票|shares]] and [[债券|bonds]] can be issued, priced and transferred. A market need not occupy a trading floor or concentrate all trading in a single system.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分类时先说清楚按什么分：[[一级市场]]与[[二级市场]]区分发行和转让；[[证券交易所]]与[[场外市场]]区分交易组织安排；股票、债券和基金市场则按交易对象区分。同一笔债券转让可以同时属于“债券市场、二级市场、场外市场”，这些标签不互斥。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
State the classification criterion first: [[一级市场|primary]] versus [[二级市场|secondary markets]] separates issuance from resale; [[证券交易所|exchanges]] versus [[场外市场|OTC markets]] concerns trading arrangements; equity, bond and fund markets are classified by instrument. A bond resale may simultaneously belong to the bond, secondary and OTC markets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
