# 每股账面净资产不构成普通股市场价格的必然下限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Book value per share is not a necessary lower bound on an ordinary share's market price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[每股净资产]]依据会计确认与计量；股价反映市场对未来股东现金流及风险的判断。普通股通常不附带“随时按账面净资产兑付”的权利，所以账面值与市场值之间没有这种自动价格约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[每股净资产|BVPS]] follows accounting recognition and measurement, while share prices reflect market assessments of future shareholder cash flows and risk. Ordinary shares generally carry no right to immediate redemption at book value, so book value does not impose an automatic price bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个简化反例：公司没有负债，只有账面净额 100 的设备和 10 股普通股。若该设备用途受限、不能立即按账面额出售，市场估计全部未来股东现金流的现值只有 60，则每股账面值为 10、市场估值为 6。账面值不是随时可交易的设备售价，这一差异本身不构成买股后必赚 4 的套利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a debt-free company with equipment carried at 100 and 10 ordinary shares. If the equipment has restricted uses and cannot immediately be sold at carrying value, while the market values all future shareholder cash flows at 60, BVPS is 10 but the market valuation is 6 per share. Carrying value is not an executable sale price; the difference alone does not provide a certain arbitrage gain of 4.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

清算也需另估资产回收、费用与偿付顺序；账面权益为正不保证清算分配等额。反过来，未确认资源与未来盈利机会也可能使价格高于账面值。因此[[市净率]]低于 1 是待解释的现象，不是单独的低估证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Liquidation requires separate estimates of recoveries, costs, and payment priority; positive book equity does not guarantee an equal distribution. Conversely, unrecognized resources and future earning opportunities may support prices above book value. A [[市净率|P/B ratio]] below one therefore requires explanation rather than proving undervaluation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
