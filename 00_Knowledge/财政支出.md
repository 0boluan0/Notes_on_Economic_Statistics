# 财政支出是政府在一定时期内为履行职能而按预算安排使用的资金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fiscal expenditure is the funding that government allocates and uses through its budget to perform its functions over a specified period.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财政支出是政府把公共资金用于履行职能的活动；说某年的“财政支出规模”时，指按指定预算和统计口径计量的金额。给公立学校支付教师工资、购买设备和发放助学补助，都是这一活动的具体形式。它既包含购买资源，也包含向其他主体转移购买力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fiscal expenditure is the government's use of public funds to perform its functions. Its annual scale is the amount measured within a specified budgetary and statistical perimeter. Paying public-school teachers, purchasing equipment and providing student grants illustrate purchases of resources and transfers of purchasing power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读到一个支出数字，先问清：哪个政府层级、哪个预算范围、哪一年，以及是计划数还是实际数。例如，“中央本级支出”与“全国一般公共预算支出”不能直接互换；年初批准的 100 与年末实际执行的 96，也属于不同的 [[预算支出与决算支出|计划和执行口径]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the government level, budget perimeter, period and whether the figure is planned or actual. Central-government own expenditure is not interchangeable with a nationwide budget aggregate; an approved 100 and an outturn of 96 belong to different [[预算支出与决算支出|planning and execution measures]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个概念告诉我们政府安排了多少资金，尚未告诉我们产生了多少 [[福利经济学|社会福利]]。比较资金的用途看 [[财政支出分类]]；读取 IMF 等跨国统计时，还要核对 [[GFS费用与支出|费用、支出和融资交易的边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The concept measures public funding, not the amount of [[福利经济学|social welfare]] produced. Use [[财政支出分类|expenditure classifications]] to examine its uses, and check [[GFS费用与支出|expense, expenditure and financing boundaries]] when reading international statistics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
