# 随机效应要求未观测单位效应与整条回归量路径正交而不是只要求随机抽样

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Random effects require the unobserved unit effect to be orthogonal to the entire regressor path, not merely random sampling*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子命题
> 标准线性 RE 的关键附加限制通常写成
> $$E(c_i\mid X_i)=0,$$
> 或至少对每个时期写成 $\operatorname{Cov}(c_i,x_{it})=0$，并同时要求 idiosyncratic shocks 对 $X_i$ 外生。这里的 “random” 描述怎样把 $c_i$ 纳入随机误差结构，不是说单位从总体随机抽样后该限制就自动成立。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A key additional restriction in the standard linear RE model is $E(c_i\mid X_i)=0$, or at least $\operatorname{Cov}(c_i,x_{it})=0$ for every period, together with exogeneity of idiosyncratic shocks. “Random” describes how $c_i$ enters the stochastic error structure; random sampling of units does not imply this orthogonality.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机抽取工人可以让样本代表总体，却不阻止总体中稳定能力同时影响教育和工资。此时 $c_i$ 与 $X_i$ 相关，RE 用组间信息的部分就会把稳定差异混入斜率。关键问题应是“机制上为什么 $c_i$ 与整条 $X_i$ 路径正交”，而不是“样本是不是随机的”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Randomly sampling workers may make the sample representative, but it does not prevent stable ability from affecting both education and wages in the population. Then $c_i$ correlates with $X_i$, and the between component of RE contaminates the slope. The substantive question is why orthogonality should hold over the full regressor path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使 $E(c_i\mid X_i)=0$ 成立，RE 仍需正确的条件均值、足够的二阶矩与适合其 GLS 权重的方差结构来获得通常宣称的效率。正交限制和 [[随机效应GLS|协方差结构]] 是不同假设，不能统称为“随机效应成立”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even when $E(c_i\mid X_i)=0$ holds, the usual efficiency claim for RE also requires a correct conditional mean, sufficient second moments, and a covariance structure appropriate for its GLS weights. Orthogonality and the covariance structure used by GLS are distinct assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 为什么随机抽样不能推出 $\operatorname{Cov}(c_i,x_{it})=0$？
>
> **答案：** 随机抽样复制总体联合分布；若总体中能力与教育相关，样本会保留这项相关，而不会把它消掉。
