# 零概率到达的信息集上，弱 PBE 的路径上 Bayes 规则不确定信念，但所指定信念仍须支持序贯最优的续局策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*At a zero-probability information set, the on-path Bayes rule of weak PBE does not determine beliefs, but the specified belief must still support a sequentially optimal continuation strategy.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心边界
> 若候选策略使信息集 $I$ 的到达概率为零，$\mu(x\mid I)=P_\sigma(x)/P_\sigma(I)$ 出现 $0/0$。weak PBE 因而不从候选策略唯一推出该处 belief；但 assessment 仍须写出 belief，并据此检查所有路径外行动和偏离。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Core boundary*
>
> If a candidate strategy gives information set $I$ zero reach probability, $\mu(x\mid I)=P_\sigma(x)/P_\sigma(I)$ becomes $0/0$. Weak PBE therefore does not derive a unique belief there from the candidate strategy. The assessment must still specify a belief and use it to check every off-path action and deviation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## “Bayes 不确定”不等于“路径外什么都可以”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*“Not determined by Bayes” does not mean “anything goes off path.”*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $P_\sigma(I)=0$ 的信息集，[[路径上一致性]]无法直接计算[[信息集信念]]。在 weak-PBE 定义下，可从 $\Delta(I)$ 中指定一份 $\mu(\cdot\mid I)$。指定之后仍有三项约束：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an information set with $P_\sigma(I)=0$, [[路径上一致性|on-path consistency]] cannot directly calculate the [[信息集信念|information-set belief]]. Under the weak-PBE definition, one may specify a distribution $\mu(\cdot\mid I)$ from $\Delta(I)$. Once it is specified, three constraints remain:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. belief 必须是 $I$ 内节点上的合法概率分布；
2. 给定这份 belief，候选续局策略必须满足[[序贯理性]]；
3. 预期到这份路径外反应后，较早行动的每个玩家或每种类型仍不得有有利偏离。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;

**1.** The belief must be a valid probability distribution over the nodes in $I$;<br>
**2.** given that belief, the candidate continuation strategy must satisfy [[序贯理性|sequential rationality]];<br>
**3.** anticipating the resulting off-path response, every player or type that acts earlier must still have no profitable deviation.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以自由的是“Bayes 没有唯一钉住哪份 posterior”，不是“可以任意指定行动来支撑想要的答案”。若不存在任何 belief 能让规定的路径外行动成为最佳反应，该候选就不是 PBE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The freedom is that Bayes' rule does not uniquely pin down the posterior, not that actions may be chosen arbitrarily to support a desired answer. If no belief makes the prescribed off-path action a best response, the candidate is not a PBE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 信号博弈中的存在性写法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Existence formulation in a signaling game*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在标准两阶段[[信号博弈]]里，若信号 $s$ 从未被任何类型发送，接收者在 $s$ 后的行动必须是某份 $p(\cdot\mid s)\in\Delta(\Theta)$ 下的最佳反应。怎样判断一项规定反应能否由某份 belief 支撑，以及这项反应能否阻止发送者偏离，见[[路径外反应可支持性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a standard two-stage [[信号博弈|signaling game]], if no type ever sends signal $s$, the receiver's action after $s$ must be a best response under some $p(\cdot\mid s)\in\Delta(\Theta)$. See [[路径外反应可支持性|supportability of off-path responses]] for how to determine whether some belief supports a prescribed response and whether that response deters sender deviations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 更强均衡怎样处理这项自由度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How stronger equilibrium concepts handle this freedom*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

路径外 belief 在 weak PBE 下可选，不代表所有均衡概念都给予同样自由。[[PBE定义口径]]解释不同 PBE 强度，[[序贯均衡]]则要求所有 belief 来自同一列完全混合策略的 Bayes 极限；[[直观准则]]与[[D1准则]]又从偏离类型的激励出发筛选离轨信念。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ability to choose off-path beliefs under weak PBE does not mean that every equilibrium concept grants the same freedom. [[PBE定义口径|PBE definition conventions]] explains the different strengths of PBE. [[序贯均衡|Sequential equilibrium]] requires all beliefs to arise as Bayesian limits of one sequence of completely mixed strategies, while the [[直观准则|intuitive criterion]] and [[D1准则|D1 criterion]] screen off-path beliefs using the incentives of types that might deviate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] pooling 后的未采用信号
> weak 与 strong 都选 Beer，Quiche 从未出现。可以先指定看到 Quiche 后 weak 的概率 $q$，据此算 Fight/Don't Fight 的最佳反应，再检查 weak 与 strong 是否都不愿改发 Quiche。若接收者反应在任何 $q\in[0,1]$ 下都不是规定行动，则该候选无法由路径外 belief 支撑。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *An unused signal after pooling*
>
> The weak and strong types both choose Beer, so Quiche is never observed. One may first specify the probability $q$ of the weak type after Quiche, use it to calculate the best response between Fight and Don't Fight, and then check whether both types refrain from deviating to Quiche. If the prescribed receiver action is not optimal for any $q\in[0,1]$, no off-path belief can support the candidate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 路径外 posterior 无法由 weak PBE 的 Bayes 条件唯一算出。是否可以直接写“任意”，并跳过接收者最佳反应？
>
> **答案：** 不可以。可选择的 belief 必须明确到足以验证接收者续局最优，并且相应反应还要阻止所有较早的有利偏离。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Self-check*
>
> Weak PBE's Bayes condition does not uniquely determine an off-path posterior. May one simply write “arbitrary” and skip the receiver's best-response check?
>
> **Answer:** No. The chosen belief must be specified precisely enough to verify the receiver's continuation optimality, and the resulting response must also deter all profitable earlier deviations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
