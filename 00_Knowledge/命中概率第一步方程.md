# Hitting probabilities by first-step analysis
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*命中概率第一步方程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a machine can keep running, reach a desired operating state, or enter a permanent failure state. We want the probability that it **ever reaches the desired state**, however long that takes. First-step analysis replaces this question about a whole future path with a weighted average of the same question from each possible next state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设机器可能继续运行、进入目标工作状态，也可能进入永久故障状态。我们想知道的是：无论等多久，它**最终曾经到达目标状态**的概率有多大。第一步分析把关于整条未来路径的问题，改写为从各个下一状态出发的同一个问题，再按转移概率加权。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Specify the event before writing equations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先明确事件，再写方程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $(X_n)_{n\ge0}$ be a finite- or countable-state [[离散时间Markov链|Markov chain]] with a [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|time-homogeneous transition rule]]. Write $p_{ij}$ for the probability of going from $i$ to $j$ in one step; this is the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]], so $\sum_jp_{ij}=1$. Fix a target set $A$. Define its **first hitting time** and its eventual hitting probability by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $(X_n)_{n\ge0}$ 是有限或可数状态的[[离散时间Markov链]]，转移规则[[DTMC时间齐次转移核|时间齐次]]。用 $p_{ij}$ 表示从 $i$ 一步到 $j$ 的概率；这是[[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]]，所以 $\sum_jp_{ij}=1$。固定目标集合 $A$，定义**首次命中时刻**和最终命中概率：

$$
\tau_A=\inf\{n\ge0:X_n\in A\},\qquad h_i=\Pr_i(\tau_A<\infty).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\Pr_i$ means “start at $X_0=i$.” If the path never enters $A$, set $\tau_A=\infty$; if it starts in $A$, then $\tau_A=0$. Thus $h_i$ is a probability, not an average waiting time or the probability of occupying $A$ at one particular step. The latter distinction is explained in [[n步转移非首达|occupancy versus first passage]]. The target need not be absorbing: behavior after the first hit cannot undo that hit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\Pr_i$ 表示“固定从 $X_0=i$ 出发”。若整条路径从不进入 $A$，约定 $\tau_A=\infty$；若起点已经在 $A$ 内，则 $\tau_A=0$。因此 $h_i$ 是一个概率，不是平均等待时间，也不是某个指定时刻位于 $A$ 的概率；后一项区别见[[n步转移非首达]]。目标不必是吸收状态：第一次到达以后即使离开，也不会撤销已经发生的命中。

> [!important] Boundary values and the first step · 边界与第一步
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The true probabilities satisfy
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 真实命中概率满足
>
> $$
> h_i=1\quad(i\in A),\qquad h_i=\sum_jp_{ij}h_j\quad(i\notin A).
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $i\notin A$, split according to $X_1=j$ using the [[全概率公式|law of total probability]]. For each $j$ with $p_{ij}>0$, the [[Markov性|Markov property]] and time homogeneity make the remaining chance of a hit equal to $h_j$. States with $p_{ij}=0$ contribute zero; no conditional probability on a zero-probability event is needed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $i\notin A$，按第一步 $X_1=j$ 用[[全概率公式]]分解。对每个 $p_{ij}>0$ 的 $j$，[[Markov性]]与时间齐次性保证剩余命中概率就是从 $j$ 重新出发的 $h_j$。$p_{ij}=0$ 的状态贡献为零，无须给零概率事件上的条件概率赋值。

> [!example] A finite chain can already have extra algebraic solutions · 有限链也会有多余代数解
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take states $s,a,d$. From $s$, stay at $s$ with probability $1/2$, go to the target $a$ with probability $1/4$, and go to $d$ with probability $1/4$. Both $a$ and $d$ are [[吸收状态|absorbing]]. For $A=\{a\}$, starting at $d$ can never succeed, so $h_d=0$ and $h_a=1$. Therefore
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取状态 $s,a,d$。从 $s$ 出发，以 $1/2$ 的概率留在 $s$，以 $1/4$ 到目标 $a$，以 $1/4$ 到 $d$。$a,d$ 都是[[吸收状态]]。对 $A=\{a\}$，从 $d$ 出发永远不可能成功，因此 $h_d=0$、$h_a=1$，于是
>
> $$
> h_s=\tfrac12h_s+\tfrac14,\qquad h_s=\tfrac12.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> But the displayed general equations at $d$ say only $h_d=h_d$. They also admit $x_d=1,x_s=1,x_a=1$. All these numbers lie in $[0,1]$, yet they are not the hitting probabilities. Finiteness of the state space alone does not make the equations unique.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 然而上面的一般方程在 $d$ 处只给出 $h_d=h_d$，也允许 $x_d=1,x_s=1,x_a=1$。这些数全在 $[0,1]$ 内，却并非真实命中概率。状态空间有限，本身并不保证方程解唯一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the smallest nonnegative solution is the right one
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么要取最小非负解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Smallest” means **no larger at every state**, not the smallest sum of coordinates. To construct the answer, first allow only $N$ steps: $h_i^{(N)}=\Pr_i(\tau_A\le N)$. At horizon zero, $h_i^{(0)}=1$ on $A$ and $0$ outside it. Increase the horizon with
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“最小”指**在每个状态上都不大于其他解**，不是坐标总和最小。先只允许等待 $N$ 步，令 $h_i^{(N)}=\Pr_i(\tau_A\le N)$。时限为零时，$A$ 内的 $h_i^{(0)}=1$，外面的为零。随后按下式延长时限：

$$
h_i^{(N+1)}=\begin{cases}1,&i\in A,\\ \sum_jp_{ij}h_j^{(N)},&i\notin A.\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These finite-horizon probabilities increase to $h_i$, because any finite hit occurs within some finite horizon. If a nonnegative vector $x$ satisfies the same boundary and first-step equations, then $x\ge h^{(0)}$. Nonnegative transition probabilities preserve this comparison at each iteration, so $x\ge h^{(N)}$ for every $N$, and finally $x\ge h$. This proves the **minimal nonnegative solution** rule and gives an increasing sequence of lower bounds for computation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些有限时限概率递增到 $h_i$，因为任何有限时刻发生的命中，总会被某个有限时限包含。若非负向量 $x$ 满足同样的边界与第一步方程，则 $x\ge h^{(0)}$。转移概率非负，使每次迭代都保持这个比较，因此对每个 $N$ 都有 $x\ge h^{(N)}$，最后得到 $x\ge h$。这就证明了**最小非负解**规则，也给出一列递增的计算下界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the example, $h_s^{(N)}=\frac12(1-2^{-N})$, giving $0,1/4,3/8,7/16,\ldots$ and limit $1/2$. The iteration assigns no invented success probability to $d$. A finite stopping horizon gives a lower bound; an accuracy guarantee requires a separate bound on the remaining chance of a later hit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上例有 $h_s^{(N)}=\frac12(1-2^{-N})$，依次为 $0,1/4,3/8,7/16,\ldots$，极限为 $1/2$。迭代不会凭空给 $d$ 分配成功概率。有限时限给出的是下界；要保证误差足够小，还需另外控制更晚命中的剩余概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Boundary choices change the question
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*边界选择会改变问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To ask whether a disjoint target $B$ is hit **before** another target $C$, use value $1$ on $B$, value $0$ on $C$, and the first-step equation outside $B\cup C$. The event is $\{\tau_B<\tau_C\}$; a path reaching neither target does not count as success. Again use the minimal nonnegative solution if the equations are not unique. This differs from eventually hitting $B$ when visits to $C$ are allowed along the way.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要问是否**先到达**互不相交的目标 $B$ 而不是 $C$，就在 $B$ 上设一、$C$ 上设零，在 $B\cup C$ 外写第一步方程。事件为 $\{\tau_B<\tau_C\}$；两个目标都不到的路径不算成功。方程不唯一时仍取最小非负解。这与允许途中经过 $C$、只问最终是否到达 $B$ 不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, a nearest-neighbor walk on $0,1,2,\ldots$ moves up with probability $3/5$ and down with probability $2/5$, with target $0$. On the finite interval $0,\ldots,M$, the probability of hitting $0$ before $M$, starting at $0<i<M$, is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，在 $0,1,2,\ldots$ 上每步向上走一格的概率为 $3/5$、向下为 $2/5$，目标是 $0$。先限制在有限区间 $0,\ldots,M$；从 $0<i<M$ 出发，先到 $0$ 而不是 $M$ 的概率是

$$
\frac{(2/3)^i-(2/3)^M}{1-(2/3)^M}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Its boundary values and recurrence can be checked by substitution. As $M\to\infty$, the events increase to eventual hitting of $0$: a finite path that hits $0$ has a finite maximum. Hence $h_i=(2/3)^i$. The constant solution $1$ also satisfies the infinite recurrence and the boundary at $0$, but overstates the true ruin probability. Average waiting time is a separate question handled by [[期望命中时间方程|expected-hitting-time equations]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直接代入可以核对它的两个边界和递推式。当 $M\to\infty$，这些事件递增到最终命中 $0$ 的事件，因为一条在有限时刻命中 $0$ 的路径有有限的最大高度。因此 $h_i=(2/3)^i$。常数解一也满足无限递推和零点边界，却高估了真实破产概率。平均等待多久是另一个问题，由[[期望命中时间方程]]处理。
