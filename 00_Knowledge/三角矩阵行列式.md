# Determinant of a triangular matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*三角矩阵行列式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A triangular matrix lets us read its determinant without expanding a long sum: multiply the entries on the main diagonal. For example, changing the entries above the diagonal in the following matrix would leave its determinant unchanged:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
三角矩阵让我们不必展开很长的和，就能读出行列式：把主对角线上的元素相乘。例如，下面矩阵中主对角线上方的元素不影响这个结果：

$$
T=\begin{bmatrix}2&7&-1\\0&-3&4\\0&0&5\end{bmatrix},
\qquad \det T=2\cdot(-3)\cdot5=-30.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recall that an [[三角矩阵|upper-triangular matrix]] has zeros below its main diagonal; a lower-triangular matrix has zeros above it. The [[行列式|determinant]] assigns a scalar to a square matrix. Here the zero pattern makes that scalar especially simple, even if some diagonal entries are zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
回顾[[三角矩阵|三角矩阵]]的定义：上三角矩阵在主对角线下方全为零，下三角矩阵在主对角线上方全为零。[[行列式]]给方阵赋予一个标量。三角形的零元素分布使这个标量特别容易计算，即使某些对角元为零也不例外。

> [!important] The diagonal-product rule / 对角元乘积公式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $n\ge1$ be an integer and let $T=(t_{ij})$ be an upper- or lower-triangular $n\times n$ matrix over one field $\mathbb F$, such as $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. The entry $t_{ij}$ is in row $i$, column $j$. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 设 $n\ge1$ 为整数，$T=(t_{ij})$ 是同一域 $\mathbb F$ 上的 $n\times n$ 上三角或下三角矩阵，例如实矩阵或复矩阵；$t_{ij}$ 表示第 $i$ 行、第 $j$ 列的元素。则
>
> $$
> \det T=\prod_{i=1}^{n}t_{ii}=t_{11}t_{22}\cdots t_{nn}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The product symbol means multiplying all $n$ diagonal entries. None is required to be nonzero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 连乘符号表示把全部 $n$ 个对角元相乘；定理不要求它们非零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the rule also works when a diagonal entry is zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么对角元为零时证明仍然成立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We prove the upper-triangular case by induction on the size. A $1\times1$ matrix satisfies $\det[t_{11}]=t_{11}$. Now suppose the rule holds in size $n-1$. Write $d=t_{nn}$. The last row of $T$ is $(0,\ldots,0,d)$. Let $T'$ be the matrix with the same first $n-1$ rows but last row $(0,\ldots,0,1)$. [[行列式|Linearity in one row]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对上三角情形，按矩阵阶数归纳。$1\times1$ 时有 $\det[t_{11}]=t_{11}$。现在假设结论对 $n-1$ 阶成立，记 $d=t_{nn}$。$T$ 的最后一行是 $(0,\ldots,0,d)$。令 $T'$ 的前 $n-1$ 行与 $T$ 相同，最后一行改为 $(0,\ldots,0,1)$。由[[行列式|对单独一行的线性性]]，

$$
\det T=d\det T'.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a multilinearity identity, not an instruction to divide a row by $d$. It remains valid when $d=0$. In $T'$, subtract $t_{in}$ times the last row from row $i$, for each $i<n$. These [[行操作对行列式的影响|row replacements preserve the determinant]] and clear the last column above its final entry, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这是多线性恒等式，并不是让我们把某一行除以 $d$；因此 $d=0$ 时也有效。在 $T'$ 中，对每个 $i<n$，从第 $i$ 行减去最后一行的 $t_{in}$ 倍。这些[[行操作对行列式的影响|倍加行操作保持行列式不变]]，并清除最后一列中末项上方的元素，得到

$$
\begin{bmatrix}B&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix},
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $B$ is the upper-left $(n-1)\times(n-1)$ triangular block; the displayed zeros denote zero blocks of the required sizes. Its determinant is $\det B$: as a function of the rows of $B$, this larger determinant is multilinear, alternating (zero when two rows agree), and equals $1$ at the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. The [[行列式|uniqueness property defining the determinant]] therefore identifies it with $\det B$. The induction hypothesis now gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
其中 $B$ 是左上角的 $(n-1)\times(n-1)$ 三角块，式中的零表示相应尺寸的零块。这个大矩阵的行列式等于 $\det B$：把它看作 $B$ 的各行的函数，它仍然多线性、交替（两行相同时为零），并且在[[单位矩阵]]处等于 $1$。因此，[[行列式|定义行列式的唯一性]]表明它就是 $\det B$。利用归纳假设便得到

$$
\det T=d\det B=t_{nn}\prod_{i=1}^{n-1}t_{ii}
=\prod_{i=1}^{n}t_{ii}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a lower-triangular matrix, start with its first row $(t_{11},0,\ldots,0)$. Extract $t_{11}$ by row linearity, replace that row by $(1,0,\ldots,0)$, and use it to clear the first column below it. The remaining lower-right block has size $n-1$, so the same induction applies. This proof uses neither division by a diagonal entry nor the assumption that the matrix is invertible; it can precede the [[Leibniz 行列式公式|Leibniz expansion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对于下三角矩阵，从第一行 $(t_{11},0,\ldots,0)$ 开始：用行线性性提出 $t_{11}$，把该行写成 $(1,0,\ldots,0)$，再用它消去第一列下方的元素。剩下的右下角块为 $n-1$ 阶，因此同样可以归纳。这个证明既不除以对角元，也不假设矩阵可逆，还可以放在 [[Leibniz 行列式公式]]之前。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Direct consequences
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 直接后果

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over a field, a product is nonzero exactly when every factor is nonzero. Thus a triangular matrix has nonzero determinant exactly when every diagonal entry is nonzero. Combining this with [[可逆性与非零行列式|the determinant criterion for invertibility]] gives the corresponding invertibility test. In this nonzero-diagonal case, triangular back substitution has a nonzero diagonal coefficient at each step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
在域中，一个乘积非零当且仅当每个因子非零。因此，三角矩阵的行列式非零当且仅当全部对角元非零。结合[[可逆性与非零行列式|行列式与可逆性的判别]]，便得到对应的可逆性判据。在对角元全部非零时，三角系统回代的每一步都有非零的对角系数。

> [!warning] A diagonal entry need not be a pivot / 对角元未必是主元
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A [[主元|pivot]] is a nonzero leading entry of a row in echelon form. A triangular matrix can have a zero diagonal entry without having a zero row. For example,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> [[主元]]是阶梯形矩阵一行的非零首项。三角矩阵可以有零对角元，却没有零行。例如，
>
> $$
> \begin{bmatrix}0&1\\0&2\end{bmatrix}
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> has determinant $0\cdot2=0$, although neither row is zero. Its triangular shape alone does not make it row echelon form. The diagonal-product rule applies directly anyway.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 的行列式是 $0\cdot2=0$，但两行都非零。仅有三角形结构并不能保证它已是行阶梯形；对角元乘积公式仍可直接使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This theorem reads the determinant once a triangular matrix is available. Turning a general matrix into that form, while accounting for every sign and scale change, is the separate procedure [[消元计算行列式|determinant computation by elimination]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
本定理负责在矩阵已经是三角形时读取行列式。把一般矩阵化成三角形，并追踪每次变号与缩放，是另一项操作：[[消元计算行列式]]。
