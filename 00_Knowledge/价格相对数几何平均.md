# 价格相对数几何平均通过平均对数价格变化汇总各项价格比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A geometric average of price relatives aggregates price ratios by averaging their logarithms.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当所有价格比 $R_i=p_{it}/p_{i0}>0$，且权重非负、总和为 1，可写为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For positive price relatives $R_i=p_{it}/p_{i0}$ and nonnegative weights summing to one:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$I_G=B\prod_iR_i^{w_i}=B\exp\!\left(\sum_iw_i\log R_i\right).$$

等权时 $w_i=1/n$，就是价格比乘积的 $n$ 次方根。两股分别涨 20% 和跌 10%，基值 100 的指数为 $100\sqrt{1.2\times0.9}\approx103.923$。[[价格相对数算术平均|算术平均]]则为 105；同一组权重下，正数的几何平均不超过算术平均。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With equal weights this is the $n$th root of the product. Returns of +20% and -10% give $100\sqrt{1.2\times0.9}\approx103.923$, versus 105 for the [[价格相对数算术平均|arithmetic average]]. For the same weights and positive observations, the geometric mean does not exceed the arithmetic mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里平均的是同一期间不同成分的价格比，不能直接当作等金额持仓的简单收益率。它也不等于课堂另一个“加权几何平均”公式：后者实际把拉氏和派氏**两个汇总指数**再作几何平均，见[[Fisher价格指数]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This averages different constituents' price relatives over the same period, not the simple return on equal cash holdings. It also differs from the course's separate formula taking the geometric mean of the two aggregate Laspeyres and Paasche indices; see the [[Fisher价格指数|Fisher price index]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
