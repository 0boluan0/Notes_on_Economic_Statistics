# Graph modeling semantics
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*图模型语义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **graph model** represents objects by vertices and a chosen relationship by edges. The important first step is to say what those objects and relationships mean. A line between two dots might mean “can travel between them,” “cannot schedule them together,” or “may assign one to the other.” Those meanings lead to different questions even when the drawing looks the same.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**图模型**用顶点表示对象，用边表示选定的关系。第一步是说清楚这些对象和关系的含义。两个点之间的一条线，可以表示“可以往返”“不能排在同一时段”，也可以表示“允许把一个分配给另一个”。即使画法相同，这些含义也会引出不同的问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $G=(V,E)$, where $V$ is the set of vertices, also called **nodes**, and $E$ is the set of edges. A set is a collection of distinct elements: two vertices are different because the model distinguishes their identities, not because the drawing places them far apart. For each edge, specify its endpoints and the rule that makes that edge exist. Direction, parallel edges, self-loops and weights require the further choices explained in [[图类型的建模边界|graph-type boundaries]]. Here the examples use finite sets of vertices and edges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
记 $G=(V,E)$，其中 $V$ 是顶点集合，顶点也称**节点**，$E$ 是边集合。集合收集彼此不同的元素：两个顶点是否不同，取决于模型是否区分它们的身份，而不是图上画得多远。每条边都要说明端点，以及什么条件成立时才连这条边。方向、平行边、自环和权重还需要作进一步选择，见[[图类型的建模边界]]。本卡的例子都使用有限的顶点集和边集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Decode an edge before following a path
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先解释边，再解释路径*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $V=\{A,B,C\}$ consists of three webpages, with one hyperlink from $A$ to $B$ and another from $B$ to $C$. Then $E=\{(A,B),(B,C)\}$ records two directed edges. The [[游走路径与环|path]] $A\to B\to C$ means that two successive clicks can reach $C$ from $A$. It does not say that $A$ contains a direct link to $C$, or that $C$ can reach $A$. Each of those would require additional edges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $V=\{A,B,C\}$ 表示三个网页，$A$ 有超链接指向 $B$，$B$ 又有超链接指向 $C$。于是 $E=\{(A,B),(B,C)\}$ 记录两条有向边。[[游走路径与环|路径]] $A\to B\to C$ 表示连续点击两次能从 $A$ 到达 $C$。它没有说 $A$ 直接链接到 $C$，也没有说从 $C$ 能返回 $A$；这些结论都需要额外的边。

> [!example] The same shape can encode a conflict / 相同形状可以表示冲突
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Now let $A,B,C$ denote course exams. Join two vertices exactly when the exams share a student. If the only edges are $\{A,B\}$ and $\{B,C\}$, then $A$ and $C$ may share one time slot while $B$ uses another, as far as these conflicts are concerned. A [[Proper 着色与独立集划分|proper coloring]] assigns slot labels so adjacent vertices receive different labels. The chain $A-B-C$ does not make $A$ and $C$ directly conflicting: a conflict relation need not be transitive. Room capacity or exam duration would require additional constraints.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 现在让 $A,B,C$ 表示课程考试，当且仅当两门考试有共同学生时连边。若仅有 $\{A,B\}$ 与 $\{B,C\}$，那么只考虑这些冲突时，$A,C$ 可以同场次安排，$B$ 另占一个时段。[[Proper 着色与独立集划分|合法着色]]用不同时段标签分开相邻顶点。链 $A-B-C$ 不意味着 $A,C$ 直接冲突：冲突关系不必有传递性。考场容量或考试时长还需要另加约束。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even the same courses can support another graph: draw $u\to v$ when $u$ is a prerequisite for $v$. That graph records precedence, not timetable conflict. If instead the arrow means “$u$ depends on $v$,” its direction is reversed. The words defining the arrow determine how a [[图距离与可达性|reachability]] result is translated back into a statement about courses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
即使对象仍是同一批课程，也可以另建一张图：当 $u$ 是 $v$ 的先修课时画 $u\to v$。这张图表达先后依赖，而不是排课冲突。若改用“$u$ 依赖 $v$”作为箭头含义，方向就反过来了。箭头的文字定义决定了怎样把[[图距离与可达性|可达性]]结论翻译回课程问题。

## Check what the model preserves
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 建模检查

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Object identity.** Decide what information must be part of a vertex. In a timetable problem, “at station $S$ at 09:00” and “at station $S$ at 10:00” may need to be different states. Collapsing them into one station vertex may create a route whose connections cannot actually be caught. The same issue arises with versions, locations or other state information.

**Edge criterion.** State whether an edge records something observed, permitted, possible or conflicting. In the hyperlink example, an edge means a link exists, not that someone clicked it. A possible transition is not evidence that the transition has occurred.

**Direction and value.** Explain the arrow and any attached number in words and units. A travel time of five minutes and a capacity of five passengers are different quantities. Neither the number nor the arrow determines its own interpretation.

**Objective.** Decide whether the question asks for existence, minimum cost, a partition into groups, or an assignment. A graph records the modeled relations; a solution is an additional object selected or constructed subject to the stated rules. Proving an algorithm correct for the graph does not by itself prove that the graph captured every constraint of the original problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**对象身份。** 决定哪些信息必须进入顶点。例如时刻表问题中，“09:00 在 $S$ 站”和“10:00 在 $S$ 站”可能必须是不同状态。把它们压成一个车站顶点，可能产生实际上赶不上的换乘路线。版本、地点或其他状态信息也有同样的问题。

**连边判据。** 明确边表示已观察到的关系、允许关系、可能转换，还是冲突。超链接例子的边表示链接存在，不表示有人已经点击。可能发生的转换不等于已经发生的转换。

**方向与数值。** 用文字和单位解释箭头及附带数值。五分钟的行程时间和五人的容量是不同的量；数值与箭头都不会自行确定自己的含义。

**目标对齐。** 问题究竟要求存在性、最小成本、分组还是分配？图记录被建模的关系；解则是在所述规则下额外选出或构造的对象。证明算法在这张图上正确，并不自动证明这张图已表达现实问题的全部约束。

## Candidate edges and a selected assignment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*候选关系与最终分配*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For student–project assignment, use a [[二分图配对关系|bipartite graph]]: students form one side, projects form the other, and edges only join opposite sides. An edge means that a student is eligible for that project. Assume each student may receive at most one project and each project at most one student. Then a [[匹配定义|matching]], a set of edges sharing no endpoint, is a feasible selection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
给学生分配项目时，可以用[[二分图配对关系|二分图]]：一侧是学生，另一侧是项目，边只连接不同侧。边表示学生有资格选择该项目。假设每名学生至多获得一个项目，每个项目至多分配给一名学生，那么一个[[匹配定义|匹配]]，即端点互不重复的边集，才是合法选择。

> [!question]- Which edges have actually been chosen? / 哪些边已经被选中？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Students $s_1,s_2$ and projects $p_1,p_2$ have eligibility edges $s_1p_1,s_1p_2,s_2p_2$. Do these three edges already form an assignment?
>
> No. The pair $\{s_1p_1,s_2p_2\}$ assigns both students without conflict; selecting both $s_1p_2$ and $s_2p_2$ would use $p_2$ twice. All three original edges remain valid candidate relations even though only some are selected. Projects with multiple places need an explicit capacity model rather than this one-place matching assumption.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 学生 $s_1,s_2$ 与项目 $p_1,p_2$ 之间有资格边 $s_1p_1,s_1p_2,s_2p_2$。这三条边已经是一份分配结果吗？
>
> 不是。选择 $\{s_1p_1,s_2p_2\}$ 可以不冲突地分配两名学生；同时选择 $s_1p_2,s_2p_2$ 则重复使用了项目 $p_2$。原来的三条边都可以是有效候选，即使最终只选一部分。有多个名额的项目需要明确容量模型，不能直接沿用每个项目仅一个名额的匹配假设。
