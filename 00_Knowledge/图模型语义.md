# 建图先规定顶点代表什么以及边代表什么关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Graph modeling begins by stating what vertices represent and what an edge means*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子模型
> 图写成 $G=(V,E)$，但符号本身没有替你完成建模。必须先用一句完整的话规定：**一个顶点是什么对象，一条边在什么条件下存在，边的方向与权重分别表达什么。** 后续的可达、最短路、着色或匹配结论，只对这套语义成立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A graph is written $G=(V,E)$, but the notation does not choose the model for you. State in a complete sentence **what one vertex represents, exactly when an edge exists, and what any direction or weight means**. Later claims about reachability, shortest paths, coloring, or matching are valid only for that semantics.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一组对象会因问题不同产生不同的图。课程可以把网页作为顶点、hyperlink 作为 $u\to v$；也可以把课程作为顶点、时间冲突作为无向边。前者的 path 表示可以连续点击，后者的 edge 却表示两门课**不能**放在同一时段，不能把“走一条路”的直觉原封不动搬过去。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same objects can yield different graphs for different questions. One model may use webpages as vertices and hyperlinks as directed edges $u\to v$; another may use courses as vertices and timetable conflicts as undirected edges. A path in the first model represents successive clicks, while an edge in the second means that two courses **cannot** share a time slot. The travel metaphor does not transfer automatically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 建模检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Modeling check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **对象边界：**两个看似相同的名字是否真是同一个顶点？时间、版本或地点是否应进入顶点状态？
2. **连边判据：**边记录的是实际发生、允许发生、可能发生，还是冲突约束？
3. **方向语义：**$u\to v$ 是“从 $u$ 能到 $v$”，还是“$u$ 依赖 $v$”？方向写反会把可达性整体反转。
4. **目标对齐：**问题问的是 existence、minimum cost、partition 还是 assignment？这些目标可能使用同一批顶点，却需要不同的边语义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

**1. Object identity:** Do two identical-looking labels denote the same vertex, or should time, version, or location be part of the state?<br>
**2. Edge criterion:** Does an edge record an observed relation, a permitted transition, a possible transition, or a conflict?<br>
**3. Direction:** Does $u\to v$ mean “one can move from $u$ to $v$,” or “$u$ depends on $v$”? Reversing this choice reverses reachability.<br>
**4. Objective:** Is the problem asking for existence, minimum cost, a partition, or an assignment? The same vertices may require different edge semantics for different objectives.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> “学生”和“项目”都是对象。若目标是给每名学生分配一个合格项目，顶点和边应怎样定义？边是否表示已经完成的分配？
>
> **答案：**把学生与项目放在二分图两侧；当且仅当某学生有资格选择某项目时连边。边表示**允许的候选关系**，最终分配是从这些边中另选出的 matching，不能把候选边误当成已经完成的分配。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both students and projects are objects. If the objective is to assign every student an eligible project, how should vertices and edges be defined? Does an edge mean that an assignment has already been made?
>
> **Answer:** Put students and projects on the two sides of a bipartite graph, joining a student to a project exactly when that assignment is eligible. An edge records an **allowed candidate relation**. The final assignment is a matching selected from those edges, not the edge set itself.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
