# 新增城市岗位若诱致更多新增城市劳动力就会增加失业人数但不必提高失业率

在[[Harris–Todaro模型]]的工资固定、自由迁移基准中，新增城市现代岗位先提高获聘机会，可能吸引更多农村劳动者进入城市竞争池。**若每新增一个岗位诱致超过一名新增城市劳动者，失业人数反而增加**；这类岗位创造的反直觉结果是Todaro悖论的核心。它不等于[[城市工资与迁移反应|工资提高的效应]]，也不意味着所有就业创造都会增加失业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a fixed wage and free migration in the [[Harris–Todaro模型|Harris–Todaro benchmark]], additional modern urban jobs improve hiring chances and may attract more rural workers. Unemployment headcounts rise if each new job draws in more than one additional urban worker. This is the core job-creation paradox, distinct from [[城市工资与迁移反应|a wage change]] and not a universal result of employment policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先分别写人数与失业率

令现代就业为 $M$，迁移调整后的城市劳动力为 $S(M)$。以下假定城市只有这类就业和公开失业，没有其他岗位、劳动参与变化或城市自然人口变化；因此 $U=S-M$。从[[失业率|劳动力分母]]出发：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let modern employment be $M$ and the adjusted urban labour pool $S(M)$. Assume urban workers are either in these jobs or openly unemployed, with no other jobs, participation changes or urban demographic changes. Then $U=S-M$ and the [[失业率|labour-force denominator]] gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{dU}{dM}=\frac{dS}{dM}-1,\qquad
\frac{du}{dM}=\frac{M(dS/dM)-S}{S^2}.
$$

因此失业**人数**上升的条件是 $dS/dM>1$；失业**率**上升却需要更强的 $dS/dM>S/M$。在有失业的起点，$S/M>1$，所以完全可能出现人数增加、比例下降。有限变化同样先核 $\Delta U=\Delta S-\Delta M$，再用调整后的分母计算比例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The headcount rises when $dS/dM>1$, whereas the rate rises only when $dS/dM>S/M$. Since $S/M>1$ initially with unemployment, the count can rise while the rate falls. For finite changes, first calculate the headcount change and then use the new denominator for the rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么迁移响应不是任意给定的

用固定产品价格的教学特例说明。设城市工资 $\bar w_M$ 固定、总劳动力固定，迁移使农村就业减少并提高农业收入；将这一收入写成城市劳动力的函数 $b(S)>0$，且 $b'(S)>0$。它反映的是农业边际产出递减，而非城市人口直接创造农业收入。内点迁移均衡要求：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a constructed fixed-price case with a fixed urban wage and total labour. As migration reduces agricultural employment, rural marginal earnings rise. Write these earnings as $b(S)>0$ with $b'(S)>0$; this captures diminishing agricultural marginal product, not a direct productive effect of urban population. Interior equilibrium requires:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\bar w_M M=S b(S),\qquad
\frac{dS}{dM}=\frac{\bar w_M}{b(S)+Sb'(S)}.
$$

只有 $\bar w_M>b(S)+Sb'(S)$ 时，新增岗位诱致的城市劳动力超过新增岗位。农村收入随离农上升越快，吸引进一步迁移的空间越小。在这组严格递增的 $b$ 条件下：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More than one worker is induced per new job only if the urban wage exceeds $b(S)+Sb'(S)$. Faster increases in rural earnings restrain further migration. Under this strictly increasing rural-earnings schedule:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{du}{dM}=-\frac{b'(S)}{b(S)+Sb'(S)}<0.
$$

这给出一个人数与比例方向不同的机制，不是普遍人口统计定理。Harris与Todaro原文的完整模型还包含相对产品价格反馈；其附录II同样推出在相应设定下就业增加时城市失业率下降，而失业人数是否增加仍取决于迁移量。固定农村收入的特例 $b'=0$ 则可保持失业率不变，不能沿用严格下降号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a mechanism for diverging count and rate movements, not a universal demographic theorem. The full original model also includes relative-price feedback; Appendix II likewise obtains a lower urban unemployment rate, while the headcount depends on induced migration. With constant rural earnings, $b'=0$, the fixed-price special case instead permits an unchanged rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个完整调整的数例

复用[[Harris–Todaro模型|模型入口的教学起点]]：$L=100$、$\bar w_M=120$、$b(S)=20+S$，初始 $M=20,S=40,U=20,u=50\%$。若在同一工资下外生多提供一个岗位，令 $M=21$，新均衡解 $2520=S(20+S)$，得 $S\approx41.186$。因此新增城市劳动者约1.186人，失业人数变为20.186，但失业率降至约49.012%。人数在这个连续模型中可理解为可细分劳动量或放大后的规模单位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reuse the [[Harris–Todaro模型|constructed benchmark]] with total labour 100, wage 120 and rural earnings $20+S$. Initially jobs number 20, the urban pool 40, unemployment 20 and its rate 50%. With 21 jobs at the same wage, equilibrium solves $2520=S(20+S)$, giving a pool of about 41.186. Unemployment rises to 20.186, while its rate falls to 49.012%. Fractional counts denote divisible labour or scaled population units in this continuous model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

新增岗位是否提高总福利是另一个问题：还要看新增产出、离农机会成本、筹资与分配，不能从失业人数单独得出。若新增城市劳动者进入[[非正规就业]]，则必须重写 $U=S-M$ 的状态核算和预期收入，不能把非正规就业冒充失业来制造“悖论”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Welfare requires separate analysis of output, agricultural opportunity cost, financing and distribution; the unemployment headcount alone does not decide it. If entrants obtain [[非正规就业|informal jobs]], both state accounting and expected earnings must change. Informal employment cannot be relabelled unemployment to produce the paradox.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
