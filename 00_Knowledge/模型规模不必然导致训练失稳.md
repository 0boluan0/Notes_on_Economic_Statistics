# 模型规模会通过具体机制改变预训练的风险面，但不是某次训练失稳的充分根因
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Model scale can change the risk surface of pretraining through specific mechanisms, but it is not a sufficient root cause of a particular training failure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心命题
> 模型变大时，数值敏感性、内存压力与所需的并行方式都可能改变；伴随的设备规模和运行时长还会改变故障暴露。这些变化共同改写训练的**风险面**。但看到“参数更多”，并不能直接解释一次 loss divergence 或 job interruption。要把规模写成某次事故的原因，还必须指出规模通过哪个可检验机制造成了什么近端异常。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> As a model grows, numerical sensitivity, memory pressure, and the required parallel configuration may change; the accompanying device count and run duration also change failure exposure. Together these changes reshape the training **risk surface**. But “more parameters” does not by itself explain a particular loss divergence or job interruption. To treat scale as a cause of an incident, one must identify a testable mechanism through which scale produced a specific proximal abnormality.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先把三种“规模”分开
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Separate three meanings of scale first*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **模型规模**：参数量、深度、宽度和激活尺寸，可能通过初始化、精度、范数增长或超参数安全区间影响优化。
2. **系统规模**：设备、主机和同步参与者的数量，会改变组件故障的暴露机会与恢复复杂度。
3. **运行规模**：训练时长、step 或 token 数，会改变观察到低频事件的机会。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. **Model scale** means parameter count, depth, width, and activation size. It may affect optimization through initialization, precision, norm growth, or safe hyperparameter ranges.
2. **System scale** means the number of devices, hosts, and synchronous participants. It changes exposure to component failures and the complexity of recovery.
3. **Run scale** means duration, steps, or processed tokens. It changes the opportunity to observe rare events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这三种量常常一起增长，但不是同一个变量。如果只记一个“大模型”标签，就无法分辨事故是来自优化机制、集群组件，还是更长观察窗口带来的暴露。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These quantities often grow together, but they are not the same variable. A single “large model” label cannot distinguish an optimization mechanism, a cluster component failure, and the greater exposure created by a longer observation window.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 风险因子不等于事故根因
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A risk factor is not an incident root cause*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

规模可以出现在这条因果链的上游：

$$
\text{规模维度}
\rightarrow \text{暴露或安全区间变化}
\rightarrow \text{具体机制}
\rightarrow \text{可观测异常}
\rightarrow \text{失稳事件}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Scale may sit upstream in a causal chain: a scale dimension changes exposure or a safe operating region; a specific mechanism then produces an observable abnormality and, finally, an instability event.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但是，不能跳过中间三环，把“模型很大”直接接到“这次发散”。优化事故需要 learning rate、warmup、初始化、精度与 loss scaling、gradient clipping、异常 batch、optimizer state 等证据；系统事故则需要 GPU/HBM、主机、网络、存储、软件或维护日志。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One cannot skip the middle of the chain and connect “the model is large” directly to “this run diverged.” Optimization incidents need evidence about learning rate, warmup, initialization, precision and loss scaling, gradient clipping, anomalous batches, or optimizer state. Systems incidents need GPU or HBM, host, network, storage, software, or maintenance evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## OPT 与 Llama 3 给出的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The boundary illustrated by OPT and Llama 3*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OPT-175B 的报告把 hardware failures 与 loss divergences 分开记录。前者至少导致 35 次人工 restart，并估计还有 70 次以上自动 restart；后者则与 dynamic loss scalar 降到 0、最后一层 activation norm 激增相关。较低 learning rate 与更早 checkpoint 帮助训练恢复，而早期调低 gradient clipping 阈值被报告为有助于稳定。这些记录把失稳落到了可观测的近端机制，而不是停在参数量上。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The OPT-175B report records hardware failures separately from loss divergences. Hardware failures caused at least 35 manual restarts and an estimated 70-plus automatic restarts. Loss divergences were instead associated with the dynamic loss scaler reaching zero and a spike in the final layer's activation norm. A lower learning rate and an earlier checkpoint helped training recover, while an early reduction in the gradient-clipping threshold was reported to improve stability. The report locates instability in observable proximal mechanisms rather than stopping at parameter count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Llama 3 405B 提供了另一个关键对照。训练配方只观察到少量 loss spike，并且不需要为 model-training divergence 进行干预；同一项目在 54 天快照期内却记录了 466 次 job interruption，其中 47 次为计划中断，419 次为意外中断，意外中断约 78% 被归于已确认或疑似硬件问题。所以，405B 并没有必然带来优化发散；而作业中断的近端原因也要从同步集群的具体组件中寻找。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Llama 3 405B supplies a complementary contrast. Its recipe produced only a few loss spikes and required no intervention for model-training divergence. Yet the same project recorded 466 job interruptions in a 54-day snapshot: 47 planned and 419 unexpected, with about 78% of unexpected interruptions attributed to confirmed or suspected hardware issues. Thus 405B scale did not make optimization divergence inevitable, while proximal causes of job interruptions still had to be identified in specific components of the synchronous cluster.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 这两个案例不证明“规模没有影响”
> OPT 与 Llama 3 不是只改变参数规模的受控实验，它们的数据、架构、精度、超参数、软硬件和恢复系统都不同。这些证据能否定“大模型必然发散”或“中断数就是发散数”，却不能单独估计参数规模对发散概率的因果效应。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> OPT and Llama 3 are not controlled experiments that vary only parameter count. Their data, architecture, precision, hyperparameters, hardware, software, and recovery systems differ. The evidence rejects claims that large models must diverge or that interruption counts equal divergence counts, but it does not by itself estimate the causal effect of parameter scale on divergence probability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 对一次失稳做因果诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnosing the cause of one instability event*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 先按 [[预训练稳定性]] 区分优化轨迹事件与作业连续性事件。
2. 明确说的是参数规模、系统规模，还是运行时长。
3. 写出一条可被推翻的机制，例如“某精度下 activation norm 增长使 loss scaler 持续下降”。
4. 用事故前后的日志、checkpoint 对照和干预结果检验这条机制。
5. 如果要评估“规模让风险增加了多少”，需要可比运行或专门消融，而不是一次事后归因。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Use [[预训练稳定性|the two-axis definition of pretraining stability]] to distinguish an optimization event from a job-continuity event.
2. State whether “scale” means parameter count, system size, or run duration.
3. Formulate a falsifiable mechanism, such as activation-norm growth at a given precision driving the loss scaler downward.
4. Test the mechanism with before-and-after logs, checkpoint comparisons, and intervention outcomes.
5. Estimating how much scale changes risk requires comparable runs or a dedicated ablation, not one post-hoc attribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一份事故报告只写“这是 405B 模型，所以 loss 发散是模型太大造成的”。它至少还缺什么？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An incident report says only, “This is a 405B model, so its loss diverged because the model was too large.” What is still missing?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 它没有指出参数规模通过什么机制导致了哪个近端异常，也没有 loss、loss scaler、norm、精度、超参数、异常 batch 或干预结果等证据。“405B”是背景变量，不是已经验证的事故根因。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** It gives no mechanism connecting parameter count to a proximal abnormality and no evidence from loss, the loss scaler, norms, precision, hyperparameters, anomalous batches, or intervention outcomes. “405B” is a background variable, not a verified incident root cause.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Zhang et al. (2022), *OPT: Open Pre-trained Transformer Language Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.01068)，§2.5：核对硬件故障导致的人工/自动 restart，loss divergence 与 loss scaler、activation norm 的关联，较低 learning rate 与更早 checkpoint 的恢复作用，以及 gradient clipping 调整的稳定性记录。
- [Grattafiori et al. (2024), *The Llama 3 Herd of Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21783)，§§3.3.4、3.4.1：核对 54 天内 466 次 job interruption（47 计划、419 意外）、意外中断的硬件归因，以及 405B 配方只有少量 loss spike、无需 divergence 干预的记录。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Zhang et al. (2022), §2.5, was checked for manual and automatic restarts caused by hardware failures, the association of loss divergence with loss scaling and activation norms, recovery through a lower learning rate and an earlier checkpoint, and the reported stability effect of adjusted gradient clipping.
- Grattafiori et al. (2024), §§3.3.4 and 3.4.1, was checked for 466 job interruptions over 54 days—47 planned and 419 unexpected—hardware attribution among unexpected interruptions, and a 405B recipe with few loss spikes and no required divergence intervention.
