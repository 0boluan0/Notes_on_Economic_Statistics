# 上升三法是涨势中长阳线内部短暂回调整理后再由阳线突破的组合

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rising three methods is an uptrend formation with a pause inside an initial long up candle's range followed by a renewed upward breakout.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上升三法是已有涨势中的三阶段[[K线]]组合：先有一根长阳线，中间是数根较小、通常向下回调的K线，最后出现恢复上涨的阳线。中间小K线的整个高低价格范围保持在第一根的高低区间内；本课最终阳线须突破前两阶段高点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rising three methods is a three-stage [[K线|candlestick]] formation within an uptrend: a long up candle, several small candles usually drifting down, and a renewed up candle. The intermediate candles' full high-low ranges stay within the first candle's range; the course requires the final up candle to break the preceding stages' highs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“三法”指推进、休整、再推进三个阶段，中间休整不必恰好三根。本课常见图为五根，但仅看到长阳线后的三根小阴线时还没有完整形态。若把最终突破规定为收盘超过前高，必须等待最后一根收盘，不能把休整区中的某一天当作已确认信号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three methods refer to advance, pause and renewed advance; the pause need not contain exactly three candles. The common diagram has five, but the initial up candle plus three small down candles is incomplete. If the rule requires a close above the preceding high, confirmation must wait for the final close.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

学派将完整形态视为持续整理候选，而不是上涨保证；成交量在推进时放大、休整时收缩属于课程辅助解释。下跌方向的对应结构见[[下降三法]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The completed formation is interpreted as possible continuation, not a guaranteed advance. Greater volume during advances and lower volume during the pause are supporting course heuristics. See [[下降三法|falling three methods]] for the downward counterpart.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
