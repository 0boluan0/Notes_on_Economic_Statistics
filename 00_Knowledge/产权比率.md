# 产权比率是同一时点总负债与股东权益之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The liabilities-to-equity ratio divides total liabilities by equity at the same date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本课产权比率为 $L/E$，$L$ 包含全部报表负债而非只有付息借款。$E>0$时，它表示每一元账面权益配套多少负债；ABC的 $1040/960\approx1.0833$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course ratio is $L/E$, where $L$ includes all reported liabilities, not just interest-bearing borrowing. With positive equity it measures liabilities per unit of book equity; ABC gives $1040/960\approx1.0833$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[会计恒等式]]，$L/E=A/E-1$，所以产权比率等于[[权益乘数]]减1。它也可由[[资产负债率]] $d=L/A$ 换算为 $d/(1-d)$，前提是各分母非零、口径相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[会计恒等式|accounting identity]] gives $L/E=A/E-1$: the ratio equals the [[权益乘数|equity multiplier]] minus one. It also equals $d/(1-d)$ for the [[资产负债率|liabilities-to-assets ratio]] $d$, provided denominators are nonzero and measurement bases match.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它是账面杠杆指标，不是资本使用效率；$E=0$不可除，$E<0$时的负比率不代表低负债或安全。若资料把debt限为有息债务，应重新标明定义，不能与本课总负债版直接排名。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is book leverage, not capital-use efficiency. Division is undefined at zero equity, while a negative ratio under negative equity does not mean low debt or safety. Definitions restricting debt to interest-bearing obligations must be distinguished from this total-liability version.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
