# Statistical power
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*检验功效*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A test's **power** is its chance of detecting a specified departure from the null when that departure really is present. Imagine repeating the entire study many times under the same true effect and design. Power counts the fraction of those repetitions in which the test rejects. It describes the procedure across possible samples, not how convincing this one observed sample feels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**检验功效**描述：某个指定的偏离确实存在时，检验有多大概率发现它。想象在同一个真实效应和设计下反复完成整项研究，功效计数其中有多少比例会拒绝零假设。它描述程序在各种可能样本中的表现，不是本次样本“看起来多有说服力”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with a [[零假设与备择假设|null hypothesis]] $H_0:\theta\in\Theta_0$ and an alternative $H_1:\theta\in\Theta_1$. The parameter $\theta$ records the model's true values, including nuisance parameters when needed; $\Theta_0,\Theta_1$ are the corresponding sets. Fix a sample size, sampling design and rejection rule. Its **rejection region** $\mathcal R$ is the set of all datasets for which the rule rejects. The power function is
$$\pi(\theta)=P_\theta\{\text{data}\in\mathcal R\}.$$
Here $P_\theta$ means generate new data under parameter $\theta$ while leaving the rule fixed. At an alternative value, $1-\pi(\theta)$ is the probability of missing that alternative, a Type II error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先写[[零假设与备择假设|零假设]] $H_0:\theta\in\Theta_0$ 与备择 $H_1:\theta\in\Theta_1$。参数 $\theta$ 记录模型的真实取值，需要时也包括干扰参数；$\Theta_0,\Theta_1$ 是相应取值集合。固定样本量、抽样设计和拒绝规则。**拒绝域** $\mathcal R$ 收集所有会使规则拒绝的数据集。功效函数如上：$P_\theta$ 表示按参数 $\theta$ 重新产生数据，同时保持规则不变。在某个备择值处，$1-\pi(\theta)$ 是漏掉这个备择的概率，即第二类错误。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same function also records false alarms
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*同一个函数也记录误报*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a null value, rejection is a Type I error. The test's **size** is its worst false-rejection probability over the null:
$$\operatorname{size}=\sup_{\theta\in\Theta_0}\pi(\theta).$$
The supremum means the least upper bound, allowing a worst case that is approached but not attained. A **level-$\alpha$** test has size at most $\alpha$. Calling a test “5%” does not force the actual error probability to equal 5%, especially for discrete data. Likewise, “80% power” needs an alternative value and design: it is not a guarantee at every nonzero effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在零假设取值处，拒绝就是第一类错误。检验的**实际大小**是零假设范围内最坏的错误拒绝概率，写成上述上确界；上确界是最小上界，允许最坏情形只能趋近而不能取到。**水平 $\alpha$** 的检验要求实际大小至多为 $\alpha$。称作“5% 检验”并不强迫实际错误概率恰为 5%，离散数据尤其如此。同样，“80% 功效”必须交代备择值与设计，并不是对每个非零效应都保证 80%。

> [!example] Ten tosses may miss a strongly biased coin · 十次可能漏掉明显偏向的硬币
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $K$ count heads in ten [[独立同分布|iid]] tosses with heads probability $\theta$, so $K$ is [[二项分布|binomial]]. Test $H_0:\theta\le1/2$ against $H_1:\theta>1/2$ by rejecting only when $K\ge9$. Then
> $$\pi(\theta)=10\theta^9(1-\theta)+\theta^{10}.$$
> This function increases on $[0,1]$ because its [[导数|derivative]], the local rate of change, is $90\theta^8(1-\theta)\ge0$. Thus the null worst case is $\theta=1/2$:
> $$\operatorname{size}=\frac{11}{1024}\approx0.01074<0.05.$$
> At $\theta=0.8$, the power is $0.3758096384$: the rule misses this biased coin about $62.4\%$ of the time. The same conservative rule can have low false alarms and low detection probability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 十次[[独立同分布|iid]]抛掷的正面概率为 $\theta$，用 $K$ 计数正面，则 $K$ 服从[[二项分布]]。检验 $H_0:\theta\le1/2$ 对 $H_1:\theta>1/2$，仅当 $K\ge9$ 时拒绝，功效就是九次与十次正面的概率和。其[[导数]]（局部变化率）为 $90\theta^8(1-\theta)\ge0$，所以零假设下最坏点是 $\theta=1/2$，实际大小为 $11/1024\approx0.01074$，低于 5%。但在 $\theta=0.8$ 时，功效只有 $0.3758096384$，约 $62.4\%$ 的研究会漏掉这个偏向明显的硬币。同一个保守规则可以同时具有低误报和低发现概率。

## A larger signal relative to noise shifts the rejection probability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*信号相对噪声变大，拒绝概率怎样变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an exact continuous example, take $n$ iid observations from $N(\mu,10^2)$ with **known** standard deviation $10$ and fixed positive integer $n$. Test $H_0:\mu\le0$ against $H_1:\mu>0$ using
$$T=\frac{\bar X}{10/\sqrt n},\qquad \text{reject if }T>c,\qquad c=\Phi^{-1}(0.95)\approx1.645.$$
The denominator is the mean's [[标准误含义|standard error]], and $\Phi$ is the [[正态标准化|standard normal CDF]]. Since $(\bar X-\mu)/(10/\sqrt n)\sim N(0,1)$,
$$\pi_n(\mu)=1-\Phi\!\left(c-\frac{\mu\sqrt n}{10}\right).$$
At $\mu=0$ the probability is $0.05$; at smaller null values it is lower. The model therefore gives exact size $0.05$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再看一个精确的连续例子：从 $N(\mu,10^2)$ 取固定正整数 $n$ 个 iid 观察，标准差 $10$ **已知**。检验 $H_0:\mu\le0$ 对 $H_1:\mu>0$，用上式统计量，超过 $c=\Phi^{-1}(0.95)\approx1.645$ 时拒绝。分母是均值的[[标准误含义|标准误]]，$\Phi$ 是[[正态标准化|标准正态 CDF]]。把真实均值减掉后得到标准正态变量，因而推出上面的功效公式。在 $\mu=0$ 处拒绝概率恰为 $0.05$，更小的零假设取值对应更低概率，所以实际大小为 $0.05$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| True mean | Sample size | Mean SE | Power, rounded |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $1$ | $25$ | $2$ | $0.126$ |
| $5$ | $25$ | $2$ | $0.804$ |
| $5$ | $100$ | $1$ | $0.9996$ |

The first two rows change the assumed true effect while holding the procedure fixed. The last row changes the sample size and hence the procedure. These calculations rely on the stated known-variance normal model; replacing the variance by an estimate or introducing dependence requires the corresponding new sampling calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上表第一行：真实均值 $1$、样本量 $25$、均值标准误 $2$，功效约 $0.126$。第二行保持程序不变，把真实均值改为 $5$，功效约 $0.804$。第三行真实均值仍为 $5$，样本量增至 $100$、标准误降至 $1$，功效约 $0.9996$。前两行只改变假定真实效应，最后一行则改变了研究程序。这些数依赖声明的已知方差正态模型；估计方差或引入依赖后，应重新使用相应抽样分布计算。

> [!warning] A design probability is not a posterior probability · 设计概率不是后验概率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Power is not the probability that the alternative is true after rejection. Nor does a non-rejection establish a small effect. Choose plausible, scientifically meaningful alternatives when planning, and report uncertainty about noise or other design inputs. Substituting the observed estimate for the unknown true effect after the study creates a plug-in calculation, not a known actual detection probability. For interpreting this study's magnitude, return to the estimate, interval and [[显著性解释边界|practical threshold]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 功效不是“拒绝之后备择为真”的概率，不拒绝也不等于效应很小。规划时应选有科学含义、可信的备择值，并交代噪声等设计输入的不确定性。研究结束后用观察估计值替代未知真实效应，得到的是代入计算，不是已知的实际发现概率。解释本次研究的量级，要回到估计值、区间与[[显著性解释边界|实际阈值]]。
