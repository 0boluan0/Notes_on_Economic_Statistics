# 介值定理只保证中间值至少存在，不保证唯一性或提供求解算法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Intermediate Value Theorem guarantees only existence, not uniqueness or a solution algorithm*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> [[介值定理]] 的结论是“至少存在一个”满足 $f(c)=y$ 的点。它不排除多个解，也不告诉我们解在哪里或怎样计算出来；唯一性和算法都需要额外结构。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[介值定理|Intermediate Value Theorem]] concludes that at least one point satisfies $f(c)=y$. It neither excludes multiple solutions nor locates or computes one. Uniqueness and an algorithm require additional structure.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 存在不等于唯一
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Existence is not uniqueness*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $f(x)=x^3-x$ 于 $[-2,2]$。端点值为 $-6$ 与 $6$，而中间值 $y=0$ 同时在 $c=-1,0,1$ 取得。介值定理的“至少一个”完全允许这种情况。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^3-x$ on $[-2,2]$, the endpoint values are $-6$ and $6$, while the intermediate value $y=0$ is attained at $c=-1,0,1$. “At least one” is fully compatible with multiple solutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 定理不自动变成算法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A theorem does not automatically become an algorithm*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知道某个解存在，并没有给出有限步骤去定位它。[[二分求根]] 还要额外规定端点异号、取中点、按符号保留一半和停止条件，才成为可执行方法；即使如此，多根时也未必能指定最终得到哪一个。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing that a solution exists does not provide finite steps for locating it. [[二分求根|Bisection]] adds an opposite-sign bracket, midpoint updates, a sign-based invariant, and a stopping rule; even then, several roots may prevent control over which one is selected.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 介值定理证明区间内有根后，能否据此断言根唯一？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After the theorem proves that a root exists in an interval, may uniqueness be inferred?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。还需要严格单调等额外条件；介值定理本身只给存在性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. An additional condition such as strict monotonicity is needed; the theorem itself gives only existence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
