# What the intermediate value theorem guarantees
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*介值定理的结论范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a continuous real function has a negative value at one endpoint of an interval and a positive value at the other. The [[介值定理|intermediate value theorem]] certifies that at least one input gives output zero. It answers an existence question. It does not yet tell us whether there is exactly one such input, or return a numerical approximation to it. Those are different tasks, requiring their own arguments or instructions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设一个连续实函数在区间一个端点取负值，在另一个端点取正值。[[介值定理]]保证至少有一个输入使输出为零。这回答的是存在性问题，还没有说明这样的输入是否恰好只有一个，也没有返回它的数值近似。这些是不同任务，需要各自的论证或操作规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Existence is not uniqueness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 存在不等于唯一

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the continuous polynomial $f(x)=x^3-x$ on $[-2,2]$. Its endpoint values are $f(-2)=-6$ and $f(2)=6$, so the theorem guarantees a root, meaning an input where the output is zero. Factoring the polynomial reveals exactly how many roots this example has:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $[-2,2]$ 上的连续多项式 $f(x)=x^3-x$。端点值为 $f(-2)=-6$、$f(2)=6$，所以定理保证有根，即有输入使输出为零。因式分解进一步揭示本例究竟有几个根：

$$f(x)=x(x-1)(x+1),\qquad f(x)=0\ \Longleftrightarrow\ x\in\{-1,0,1\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All three roots lie inside the interval. This fully satisfies “at least one”; it contradicts an attempted inference of uniqueness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个根都在区间内，完全符合“至少一个”；它们反驳的是把定理结论擅自加强为唯一性。

> [!tip] Add a separate uniqueness argument · 单独证明唯一性
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A strictly increasing function satisfies $g(u)<g(v)$ whenever $u<v$ in its domain. It cannot take the same value at two different inputs; strict decrease works similarly. For $g(x)=x^2-2$ on $[1,2]$, if $u<v$ then $g(v)-g(u)=(v-u)(v+u)>0$. The intermediate value theorem gives existence, and this additional calculation gives uniqueness.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 严格递增的意思是：定义域内 $u<v$ 时总有 $g(u)<g(v)$。这样的函数不可能在两个不同输入处取得同一值；严格递减同理。对 $[1,2]$ 上的 $g(x)=x^2-2$，若 $u<v$，则 $g(v)-g(u)=(v-u)(v+u)>0$。介值定理给出存在性，这一步额外计算才给出唯一性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strict monotonicity is a sufficient uniqueness condition, not a necessary one. The function $x^2$ on $[-1,1]$ has only the root $0$, even though it decreases before $0$ and increases after $0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

严格单调是唯一性的充分条件，并非必要条件。$[-1,1]$ 上的 $x^2$ 只有根 $0$，但它在 $0$ 左侧递减、右侧递增，并不在整个区间上严格单调。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Turn existence into a specified computation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 定理不自动变成算法

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem's statement supplies no sequence of computational instructions. A proof can nevertheless suggest an algorithm: the nested-interval proof leads to [[二分求根|bisection]]. Assuming a continuous function and reliable evaluations of its sign, first handle any endpoint root. Otherwise start with opposite endpoint signs, evaluate the midpoint, and either return an exact zero or keep the half whose endpoint signs are still opposite. Specify a stopping tolerance as well. These rules turn the existence certificate into a procedure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定理的陈述没有列出计算指令，但证明仍然可以启发算法：嵌套区间证明就引出了[[二分求根]]。在函数连续且能够可靠判断函数值符号的前提下，先处理端点根；否则从端点异号的区间出发，计算中点，遇到精确零点就返回，否则保留端点仍异号的一半，并指定停止容差。这些规则才把存在性保证变成操作过程。

> [!example] Two updates for a square root · 平方根的两次更新
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $g(x)=x^2-2$ on $[1,2]$, the midpoint $3/2$ gives $g(3/2)=1/4>0$, so retain $[1,3/2]$. Its midpoint $5/4$ gives $g(5/4)=-7/16<0$, so retain $[5/4,3/2]$. This final interval has width $1/4$. Its midpoint $11/8$ is within $1/8$ of a root in the interval. This is a position guarantee from the bracket, not a claim that $11/8$ is the exact root.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[1,2]$ 上的 $g(x)=x^2-2$，中点 $3/2$ 给出 $g(3/2)=1/4>0$，故保留 $[1,3/2]$。它的中点 $5/4$ 给出 $g(5/4)=-7/16<0$，故保留 $[5/4,3/2]$。最后区间宽度为 $1/4$，其中点 $11/8$ 与区间内某个根的距离不超过 $1/8$。这是由括区间得到的位置保证，并不是说 $11/8$ 就是精确根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The general width and midpoint guarantees are given in [[二分误差界|bisection error bounds]]. A small output residual $|g(x)|$ alone need not certify a small input error; the extra assumptions for that inference are explained in [[残差控制根误差|residual-based root-error bounds]]. With several roots, ordinary bisection retains at least one but does not generally let us prescribe which root it will approach. Under the stated evaluation assumptions, it can reach a requested positive bracket tolerance in finitely many iterations without finding an exact root in finitely many iterations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般的区间宽度及中点保证见[[二分误差界]]。仅有较小的输出残差 $|g(x)|$，未必能保证输入位置误差小；这个推断需要的额外假设见[[残差控制根误差]]。有多个根时，普通二分法保留至少一个，却通常不能让我们预先指定最终逼近哪一个。在上述函数值判断前提下，它能在有限次迭代内达到给定的正括区间容差，但未必能在有限次迭代内找到精确根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A failed sufficient test does not prove nonexistence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*充分条件未满足，不等于解不存在*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity together with opposite endpoint signs is sufficient to certify an interior root. It is not a necessary condition for a particular function to have a root. For example, $x^2$ on $[-1,1]$ has positive values at both endpoints and still has the root $0$. Likewise, the discontinuous function equal to $-1$ for $x<0$, $0$ at $x=0$, and $1$ for $x>0$ has a root at $0$. Neither example licenses applying the sign-change theorem without its assumptions; each root is known by direct evaluation instead.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续性配合端点异号，是保证内部有根的充分条件，并不是某个具体函数有根的必要条件。例如 $[-1,1]$ 上的 $x^2$ 两个端点值都为正，却仍有根 $0$。同样，令 $x<0$ 时函数为 $-1$、$x=0$ 时为 $0$、$x>0$ 时为 $1$，这个不连续函数也在 $0$ 处有根。两个例子都不允许省略假设而使用异号介值结论；这里的根是直接代入得知的。

## Sources and verification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Strang, Calculus, §2.7, PDF p.47](https://ocw.mit.edu/ans7870/textbooks/Strang/Edited/Calculus/2.pdf#page=47): the existence conclusion for an intermediate output, without a uniqueness conclusion.
- [Lebl, Basic Analysis I, §3.3.2, PDF pp.120–123, especially Theorem 3.3.8 and Example 3.3.9](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-100a-real-analysis-fall-2020/mit18_100af20_basic_analysis.pdf#page=121): existence, possibly many solutions, and the constructive connection between the proof and bisection. The cubic, monotonicity and failure-of-converse examples above are checked directly.
- [MIT 18.330, Chapter 4, §4.1, PDF pp.1–2](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-330-introduction-to-numerical-analysis-spring-2012/5b325bfa56a599794c7196de926844b0_MIT18_330S12_Chapter4.pdf#page=1): explicit sign-based bisection updates, termination at an exact midpoint root, and the lack of control over which of several roots is selected. The midpoint position bound above follows directly from half the bracket width.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Strang《Calculus》第2.7节、PDF47页：对中间输出给出存在结论，没有唯一性结论。
- Lebl《Basic Analysis I》第3.3.2节、PDF120–123页，特别是定理3.3.8与例3.3.9：存在性、可能的多解以及证明与二分法的构造性联系；三次多项式、单调性和逆命题失败的例子在正文直接核算。
- MIT 18.330第4章第4.1节、PDF1–2页：按符号进行二分更新、中点精确为根时终止，以及多根时无法任意指定所得根；正文中点位置界直接来自括区间宽度的一半。
