# Delete-one influence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*删点影响*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Delete-one influence** asks how much a specified analysis result changes when one observation is left out and the model is fitted again. It is a sensitivity comparison. The result might be a slope, a prediction at a particular predictor value, or an uncertainty estimate. Naming that target comes before calling a point “influential.”

An observation can move a fitted line while leaving its prediction at one location unchanged. It can also change estimated uncertainty without moving the coefficients much. Influence is therefore a property of an observation **in this model, sample and comparison**, not a permanent label attached to the observation itself. The distinction from an unusual outcome or predictor position is explained in [[异常杠杆影响区分|outliers, leverage and influence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**删点影响**问的是：留出一条观测并重新拟合后，某个指定分析结果改变了多少？它是一种敏感性比较。结果可以是斜率、某个解释变量位置上的预测，或不确定性估计。在说某点“有影响”之前，应先说清目标。

一个观测可能移动整条拟合直线，却让某个位置的预测保持不变；也可能主要改变不确定性估计，而没有明显移动系数。因此，影响是某条观测**在这套模型、样本和比较中**的属性，不是贴在它身上的永久标签。它与结果值特殊、解释变量位置特殊的区别，见[[异常杠杆影响区分]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Compare the same target before and after deletion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*删点前后比较同一个目标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\hat\beta$ be the coefficient vector fitted to all $n$ rows, and $\hat\beta_{(i)}$ the vector obtained after deleting row $i$. Parentheses indicate the omitted row; they are not a coefficient index. Keep the outcome definition, predictor terms and fitting rule explicit. For unweighted [[普通最小二乘|OLS]], assume the complete and deleted designs both have [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full column rank]] whenever comparing their unique coefficient vectors.

The vector $\hat\beta-\hat\beta_{(i)}$ records coefficient changes. To compare predictions, choose the **same** predictor vector $x_*$ before and after deletion. If an intercept is included, its coordinate in $x_*$ is one. The fitted mean changes by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把全部 $n$ 行拟合得到的系数向量记为 $\hat\beta$，删去第 $i$ 行后得到的记为 $\hat\beta_{(i)}$。括号标明被删的观测，不是系数编号。应明确保持的结果定义、解释变量项和拟合规则。使用不加权[[普通最小二乘|OLS]] 比较唯一系数向量时，要求完整设计与删点设计均[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]。

向量 $\hat\beta-\hat\beta_{(i)}$ 记录系数改变。若比较预测，则前后应选取**同一个**解释变量向量 $x_*$；若含截距，$x_*$ 的对应坐标为一。拟合均值的变化为

$$\hat y(x_*)-\hat y_{(i)}(x_*)=x_*'(\hat\beta-\hat\beta_{(i)}).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A prime means transpose. Different choices of $x_*$ weight the coefficient changes differently; large individual changes can even cancel at a particular location. This is a comparison of fitted outputs, not a causal effect of removing a person from a population.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

撇号表示转置。不同 $x_*$ 对系数变化赋予不同权重；即使单个系数改变很大，也可能在某个位置恰好抵消。这是在比较拟合输出，不是在估计“从总体中移除某个人”的因果效应。

> [!example] A changed slope with one unchanged prediction / 斜率变了，一个位置的预测却没变
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fit an intercept and slope to $x=(0,1,2,3)$ and $y=(0,1,2,8)$. The complete fit is $\hat y=-1+2.5x$. Delete the last row and refit: the remaining three points give $\hat y_{(4)}=x$. The intercept changes by $-1$ and the slope by $1.5$, comparing complete minus deleted.
>
> At $x_*=3$, the predictions are $6.5$ and $3$, a change of $3.5$. At $x_*=2/3$, both predict $2/3$, because $-1+1.5(2/3)=0$. The point is influential for the slope and the first prediction, but gives zero change for the second prediction. The deleted fit has zero residual variance here; these comparisons are valid algebra, but a diagnostic requiring a positive deleted variance cannot be calculated from this example.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $x=(0,1,2,3)$、$y=(0,1,2,8)$ 拟合截距与斜率，完整拟合为 $\hat y=-1+2.5x$。删去最后一行重新拟合，其余三点给出 $\hat y_{(4)}=x$。按“完整减删点”比较，截距改变 $-1$，斜率改变 $1.5$。
>
> 在 $x_*=3$ 处，两个预测分别为 $6.5$ 和 $3$，相差 $3.5$；在 $x_*=2/3$ 处却都预测 $2/3$，因为 $-1+1.5(2/3)=0$。该点影响斜率及第一个位置的预测，却没有改变第二个位置的预测。本例删点后残差方差为零；这些拟合比较仍是有效代数，但不能据此计算要求删点方差为正的诊断量。

## Different summaries answer different questions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不同诊断量回答不同问题

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A diagnostic can scale a change to remove measurement units or combine many changes into one number. Such a summary retains a particular target:

| Diagnostic | Direct target | What it does not identify by itself |
|---|---|---|
| [[Cook距离\|Cook's distance]] | Combined change in fitted values at the original sample's design points; equivalently a weighted coefficient change | Which individual coefficient accounts for the change |
| [[DFFITS\|DFFITS]] | Scaled fitted-value change at the deleted case's own design point | Change at every other prediction point |
| [[DFBETAS\|DFBETAS]] | Scaled change in one named coefficient | Stability of all other coefficients and predictions |
| COVRATIO | Change in the determinant of the estimated coefficient covariance matrix | Direction or size of the coefficient vector's displacement |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

诊断量可以缩放变化以消除单位，或把多个变化合成一个数；但每种摘要仍保留自己的目标：

| 诊断量 | 直接针对的目标 | 不能单独告诉你什么 |
|---|---|---|
| [[Cook距离\|Cook 距离]] | 原样本设计点上整组拟合值的变化，等价于系数变化的加权汇总 | 究竟哪个系数承担了变化 |
| [[DFFITS]] | 被删观测自身设计点的标准化拟合值变化 | 其他全部位置的预测变化 |
| [[DFBETAS]] | 某个指定系数的标准化变化 | 其他系数与预测是否稳定 |
| COVRATIO | 系数协方差估计矩阵的行列式变化 | 系数向量位移的方向和大小 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus a large standardized change in one coefficient and a less prominent global Cook summary are not automatically contradictory: they aggregate and scale changes differently. Compare each quantity with its own [[影响诊断阈值|screening convention]], rather than treating their numerical magnitudes as interchangeable.

COVRATIO illustrates a target that is not a point-estimate change. Let $\widehat V$ and $\widehat V_{(i)}$ estimate the coefficient [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrices]] before and after deletion, using the same estimable parameterization. Assume both are [[正定矩阵|positive definite]], meaning positive variance in every nonzero coefficient direction. Their [[行列式|determinants]] summarize joint spread as [[广义方差|generalized variance]]. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，某个系数的标准化变化很大，而全局 Cook 摘要不突出，并不自动矛盾：二者汇总和缩放变化的方式不同。应按各自的[[影响诊断阈值|筛查惯例]]理解，不能把数值大小直接互换。

COVRATIO 展示的是另一类目标：它不测量点估计移动。令 $\widehat V$、$\widehat V_{(i)}$ 分别估计完整与删点拟合的系数[[协方差矩阵]]，并使用同一个可估参数化。假设二者都[[正定矩阵|正定]]，即每个非零系数方向都有正方差；矩阵的[[行列式]]以[[广义方差]]概括联合散布。于是

$$
\operatorname{COVRATIO}_i
=\frac{\det(\widehat V_{(i)})}{\det(\widehat V)}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A ratio above one means the deleted fit has a larger estimated generalized variance; below one means smaller. It does not mean every coefficient variance changed in the same direction. Nor is it the literal ratio of confidence-region volumes: with dimension and the same positive ellipsoid threshold held fixed, the [[广义方差与椭球体积|volume ratio]] is $\sqrt{\operatorname{COVRATIO}_i}$. For example, $\widehat V=\operatorname{diag}(1,4)$ and $\widehat V_{(i)}=\operatorname{diag}(4,4)$ give determinant ratio $4$, but fixed-threshold ellipse area ratio $2$. Here $\operatorname{diag}(a,b)$ puts $a,b$ on the diagonal and zeros elsewhere. Actual confidence regions may also use different critical values after the sample size changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比值大于一，表示删点后的估计广义方差较大；小于一表示较小，但不代表每个系数方差都同方向改变。它也不是置信区域体积比本身：固定维数并使用相同的正椭球阈值时，[[广义方差与椭球体积|体积比]]为 $\sqrt{\operatorname{COVRATIO}_i}$。例如，$\widehat V=\operatorname{diag}(1,4)$、$\widehat V_{(i)}=\operatorname{diag}(4,4)$ 给出行列式比 $4$，固定阈值椭圆面积比却是 $2$。其中 $\operatorname{diag}(a,b)$ 把 $a,b$ 放在对角线上，其余元素为零。真正的置信区域在样本量改变后，还可能使用不同的临界值。

## Keep the comparison well defined
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If deletion removes the only observations identifying a coefficient, the remaining design loses rank. Do not report an arbitrary new coefficient vector as a comparable unique estimate; investigate which fitted targets remain estimable. If covariance estimates are singular, the ordinary determinant ratio can be zero over zero or otherwise fail to describe full-dimensional uncertainty. Choose a diagnostic that matches the estimable target and covariance rule.

Deleting a row also changes the sample, and can change the represented population. A flag is evidence of sensitivity, not permission to delete a valid observation. Check recording errors, population membership and model form, and document results with and without a justified exclusion; see [[异常观测处理原则|handling unusual observations]].

Single-case deletion can miss a group of observations that jointly support one another. For clustered, panel or jointly assigned data, choose a meaningful [[删点单位边界|deletion unit]] as well as a target. Finally, changing the fitting rule or reselecting a model during deletion answers a different sensitivity question from refitting the same fixed specification. State which procedure is being compared. None of these diagnostics establishes the data-generating mechanism or causal identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若删点移除了识别某个系数的唯一观测，剩余设计就会失去秩。不能把任意选出的新系数向量当作可比较的唯一估计；应检查哪些拟合目标仍可估。协方差估计奇异时，普通行列式比可能成为零比零，或无法描述完整维度的不确定性，应选择与可估目标和协方差口径匹配的诊断。

删去一行也改变了样本，有时会改变所代表的总体。标记反映敏感性，不授权删除有效观测。应检查记录错误、总体归属和模型形式；有理由排除时，记录保留与排除后的结果，见[[异常观测处理原则]]。

单点删除可能漏掉共同支撑彼此的一组观测。聚类、面板、共同分配数据中，除了目标，还须选择有意义的[[删点单位边界|删除单位]]。最后，删点时改变拟合规则或重新选择模型，回答的是另一种敏感性问题，不等同于重拟合同一固定设定，应明确比较了哪套程序。任何这些诊断都不能确立数据生成机制或因果识别。
