# 正归一化分母下逆协方差加权超额均值给出唯一最大Sharpe组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With a positive normalization denominator, inverse-covariance-weighted excess means give the unique maximum-Sharpe portfolio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设同一单期的风险资产均值向量为 $\mu$，协方差矩阵 $\Sigma$ 正定，收益有有限二阶矩；允许无限卖空，风险资产权重仅受 $\mathbf1^\top w=1$ 约束。给定确定的 $r_f$，令 $a=\mu-r_f\mathbf1$、$d=\mathbf1^\top\Sigma^{-1}a$。若 $d>0$，下式给出唯一的 [[切点组合]]，其最大 Sharpe 为正。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the one-period risky-return mean vector be $\mu$ and the covariance matrix $\Sigma$ be positive definite, with finite second moments. Allow unrestricted short sales and impose only $\mathbf1^\top w=1$. For a certain $r_f$, define $a=\mu-r_f\mathbf1$ and $d=\mathbf1^\top\Sigma^{-1}a$. If $d>0$, the following is the unique [[切点组合|tangency portfolio]], attaining a positive maximum Sharpe ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
w_T=\frac{\Sigma^{-1}a}{d},\qquad
S_T=\sqrt{a^\top\Sigma^{-1}a}.
$$

证明不只依赖一阶条件。把 $\Sigma^{1/2}w$ 与 $\Sigma^{-1/2}a$ 应用 Cauchy–Schwarz，得下式上界。达到正的等号要求 $w=c\Sigma^{-1}a$ 且 $c>0$；预算约束给 $c=1/d$。正定性保证分母的风险范数为正，而 $d>0$ 保证这个方向可以按正倍数归一化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof does not rely only on first-order conditions. Apply Cauchy–Schwarz to $\Sigma^{1/2}w$ and $\Sigma^{-1/2}a$ to obtain the upper bound below. Positive equality requires $w=c\Sigma^{-1}a$ with $c>0$; the budget constraint fixes $c=1/d$. Positive definiteness ensures a positive risk norm, while $d>0$ permits normalization in the positive direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
S(w)=\frac{a^\top w}{\sqrt{w^\top\Sigma w}}
\le\sqrt{a^\top\Sigma^{-1}a}.
$$

又设 $A=\mathbf1^\top\Sigma^{-1}\mathbf1>0$，由 [[全局最小方差解]] 得 $\mu_{GMV}=(\mathbf1^\top\Sigma^{-1}\mu)/A$。于是 $d=A(\mu_{GMV}-r_f)$，所以这里的正分母条件等价于 $r_f<\mu_{GMV}$。禁止卖空或设置权重上限后，可行解必须重新求，不能把违反约束的闭式权重直接保留。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A=\mathbf1^\top\Sigma^{-1}\mathbf1>0$. The [[全局最小方差解|global minimum-variance solution]] gives $\mu_{GMV}=(\mathbf1^\top\Sigma^{-1}\mu)/A$, so $d=A(\mu_{GMV}-r_f)$. A positive denominator is therefore equivalent here to $r_f<\mu_{GMV}$. Short-sale bans or weight caps require solving the constrained problem instead of retaining infeasible closed-form weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\mu=(0.08,0.12)^\top$、$r_f=0.02$、$\Sigma=\begin{pmatrix}0.04&0.015\\0.015&0.09\end{pmatrix}$，可得 $\Sigma^{-1}a=(52/45,124/135)^\top$、$d=56/27$，故 $w_T=(39/70,31/70)^\top$。组合均值约为 9.77143%，方差约为 0.0374694，波动约 19.3570%，Sharpe 约 0.401479。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\mu=(0.08,0.12)^\top$, $r_f=0.02$, and $\Sigma=\begin{pmatrix}0.04&0.015\\0.015&0.09\end{pmatrix}$, we obtain $\Sigma^{-1}a=(52/45,124/135)^\top$ and $d=56/27$, giving $w_T=(39/70,31/70)^\top$. Its mean is approximately 9.77143%, variance 0.0374694, volatility 19.3570%, and Sharpe 0.401479.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
