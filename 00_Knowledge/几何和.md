# 有限几何和是连续公比幂项的有限和
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A finite geometric sum adds a finite run of successive powers of a common ratio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 几何和把固定公比的有限项加在一起。首项为 $1$、共有 $n+1$ 项时，$G_n=1+r+\cdots+r^n$；对非负整数 \(n\) 与 \(r\ne1\)，
> \[
> \sum_{i=0}^{n}r^i=\frac{1-r^{n+1}}{1-r}.
> \]
> 当 \(r=1\) 时原和为 \(n+1\)。首项为 $c$ 时，把以上结果乘以 $c$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A finite geometric sum adds finitely many terms with a common ratio. With first term one and $n+1$ terms, $G_n=(1-r^{n+1})/(1-r)$ for $r\ne1$, and $G_n=n+1$ for $r=1$. Multiply by $c$ when the first term is $c$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 \(S_n=1+r+\cdots+r^n\)。把 \(rS_n=r+\cdots+r^{n+1}\) 与原式相减，大部分项消去：\((1-r)S_n=1-r^{n+1}\)。这里除以 \(1-r\) 已明确排除 \(r=1\)。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplication by $r$ shifts the finite sum by one power. Subtraction cancels the middle terms and gives $(1-r)S_n=1-r^{n+1}$. Dividing by $1-r$ therefore excludes $r=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $1+2+4+8=(1-2^4)/(1-2)=15$，有限和不要求 $|r|<1$。当项数趋于无穷时，问题转交给 [[几何级数]]，其收敛条件需要另行检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $1+2+4+8=15$ even though the ratio exceeds one. A finite sum does not require $|r|<1$. Letting the number of terms grow without bound instead leads to a [[几何级数|geometric series]], whose convergence must be checked separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限几何和还可以逐项求导，但所得的带权求和是另一条可独立复习的恒等式，由 [[加权几何和]] 承接。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiating a finite geometric sum leads to the separate [[加权几何和|weighted geometric-sum identity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么把 \(r=1\) 代入闭式得到 \(0/0\) 不能说明原和未定义？
>
> **答案：** 未定义的是通过除以 \(1-r\) 得到的闭式，不是原来的有限和；回到定义可得 \(n+1\)。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why does substitution of $r=1$ into the quotient not make the original sum undefined? **Answer:** Only the quotient requires division by zero; the finite sum itself is $n+1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
