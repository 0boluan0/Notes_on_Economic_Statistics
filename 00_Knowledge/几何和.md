# Finite geometric sum
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有限几何和*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finite geometric sum adds amounts made by repeatedly multiplying by the same factor. For example, four layers containing $1,2,4,8$ items have $1+2+4+8=15$ items altogether. Each layer doubles the previous one; the multiplier $2$ is the common ratio. The sum records the total, not just the final layer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限几何和把按同一个倍数逐次变化的数量加起来。例如，四层分别有 $1,2,4,8$ 个物品，总数是 $1+2+4+8=15$。每层都是前一层的两倍，倍数 $2$ 称为公比；求和得到的是总量，不是最后一层的数量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $c$ be the first term, $r$ the common multiplier, and $n$ a nonnegative integer. Here $c,r$ are real numbers. Starting the index at zero gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设首项为 $c$，公比为 $r$，$n$ 是非负整数；本卡中 $c,r$ 都为实数。从索引零开始，写成

$$
S_n(c,r)=\sum_{i=0}^{n}cr^i=c+cr+\cdots+cr^n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The summation symbol $\sum$ means add the terms for every integer $i$ from zero through $n$, including both ends. There are **$n+1$ terms**. The zero-th power is the constant factor one, including when $r=0$; thus the first term is always $c$. Multiplying one term by $r$ produces the next, even if the terms are zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求和符号 $\sum$ 表示把整数索引 $i$ 从零到 $n$ 的各项都加起来，两个端点都包括，所以共有 **$n+1$ 项**。零次幂按常数因子 1 处理，$r=0$ 时也如此，因此首项始终为 $c$。后一项由前一项乘 $r$ 得到，零项也遵守这条规则。

> [!important] The finite formula / 有限求和公式
> $$
> \boxed{S_n(c,r)=\begin{cases}\displaystyle c\frac{1-r^{n+1}}{1-r},&r\ne1,\\[4pt]c(n+1),&r=1.\end{cases}}
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No condition $|r|<1$ is needed. At $n=0$ the sum has one term $c$; at $r=0$ only that first term remains. If instead there are $m$ terms, use indices $0,\ldots,m-1$. For $m=0$, the empty sum is zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不需要 $|r|<1$。$n=0$ 时只有首项 $c$；$r=0$ 时也只剩首项。若题目说共有 $m$ 项，就使用索引 $0,\ldots,m-1$；$m=0$ 时为空和，取值为零。

## Why shifting the sum helps
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*乘一次公比，再错位相减*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First set $c=1$ and write $G_n=1+r+\cdots+r^n$. Multiplying by $r$ shifts every power one place to the right. When the two sums are subtracted, all middle powers cancel:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先令 $c=1$，记 $G_n=1+r+\cdots+r^n$。乘以 $r$ 会把所有次数向后移动一位，两式相减时，中间各次幂都抵消：

$$
\begin{aligned}G_n&=1+r+\cdots+r^n,\\rG_n&=\phantom{1+{}}r+\cdots+r^n+r^{n+1},\\(1-r)G_n&=1-r^{n+1}.\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $r\ne1$, divide by $1-r$ and then multiply by $c$. For $r=1$, division is unavailable, but the original sum is simply $n+1$ copies of $c$. The quotient becoming $0/0$ does not make the finite sum undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$r\ne1$ 时可以除以 $1-r$，最后再乘 $c$。$r=1$ 时不能做这一步除法，但原和就是 $n+1$ 个 $c$；分式出现 $0/0$ 并不表示有限和没有定义。

> [!example] Check the first and last terms / 先检查首尾项
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $1+2+4+8$, use $c=1$, $r=2$, $n=3$: $(1-2^4)/(1-2)=15$. For $3+6+12$, use $c=3$, $r=2$, $n=2$: $3(1-2^3)/(1-2)=21$. The exponent in the numerator is one beyond the last power.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $1+2+4+8$ 对应 $c=1,r=2,n=3$，得到 $(1-2^4)/(1-2)=15$；$3+6+12$ 对应 $c=3,r=2,n=2$，得到 $3(1-2^3)/(1-2)=21$。分子中的指数比最后一项的次数多一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Letting the number of terms grow without bound is a different question, handled by [[几何级数|geometric series]] and its convergence conditions. Giving the $i$-th power an extra weight $i$ instead produces the separate [[加权几何和|weighted geometric-sum identity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果让项数无限增加，就进入[[几何级数]]的收敛问题，需要另查条件；若给第 $i$ 次幂再乘权重 $i$，则得到另一条[[加权几何和|带权求和恒等式]]。
