# 直接融资仍依赖承销交易托管与清算但服务机构未必承担最终信用风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Direct finance still relies on underwriting, trading, custody, and settlement even when service providers do not bear the ultimate credit risk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> “直接融资”只说明，在被分类的那一段融资链中，资金提供者直接持有资金使用者的证券；它不说明交易可以在没有金融机构和基础设施的情况下完成。发行、定价、经纪、做市、评级、托管、记录、清算和结算都可能由专业机构完成；它们提供市场服务，却未必把最终借款人的信用资产留在自己的资产负债表上。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Direct finance” means that, on the financing leg being classified, the supplier of funds directly holds the user's security; it does not mean that the transaction can occur without financial institutions or infrastructure. Specialists may provide issuance, pricing, brokerage, market making, ratings, custody, recordkeeping, clearing, and settlement without retaining the ultimate borrower's credit asset on their own balance sheets.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，投资银行帮助企业选择债券条款并承销发行，券商撮合买卖，中央证券存管记录所有权，清算与结算设施交换证券和资金。家庭或基金若在这一段直接持有企业债，企业信用风险就直接落在证券持有人身上；这些服务机构承担的更多是承销、市场、操作、托管或结算风险。若家庭是通过基金份额出资，家庭到基金之间仍另有一层中介索取权。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, an investment bank advises on bond terms and underwrites the issue, brokers arrange trades, a central securities depository records ownership, and clearing and settlement systems exchange securities and funds. If a household or fund directly holds the corporate bond on this leg, the firm's credit risk falls directly on the security holder; service providers bear underwriting, market, operational, custody, or settlement risks instead. If the household supplies funds through a fund share, the household-to-fund leg still adds a separate layer of intermediary claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条区分很重要，因为“没有中介负债夹在中间”和“没有中介服务”是两个不同命题。金融市场基础设施能降低匹配和结算成本，也会集中操作、信用与流动性风险；因此直接融资并非天然去中心化或天然更安全。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The distinction matters because “no intermediary liability lies between the parties” and “no intermediary service is used” are different claims. Financial market infrastructure lowers matching and settlement costs but can also concentrate operational, credit, and liquidity risk. Direct finance is therefore neither inherently decentralised nor inherently safer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 判断步骤

1. 先固定要分类的融资段，确认这一段的资金提供者持有什么索取权；
2. 再确认服务机构是否只是代办、撮合、托管或结算；
3. 若某机构长期买入最终资产，并以自己的负债向投资者融资，才出现资产负债表中介层。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Fix the financing leg being classified and identify the claim held by its supplier of funds.
2. Determine whether each institution merely arranges, brokers, safeguards, or settles the transaction.
3. A balance-sheet intermediation layer appears when an institution retains the ultimate asset and finances it by issuing its own liability to investors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么中央清算机构介入一笔债券交易，并不必然把企业的直接融资变成银行式间接融资？
>
> **答案：** 清算机构负责履约和结算安排，不必长期持有企业债并向储蓄者发行自己的融资负债。在被分类的债券融资段中，资金提供者仍直接持有企业索取权。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does the involvement of a central clearing institution not necessarily turn a corporate bond issue into bank-style indirect finance?
>
> **Answer:** The clearing institution manages performance and settlement without necessarily retaining the corporate debt and issuing its own funding liability to savers. On the bond-financing leg being classified, the supplier of funds still directly holds the firm's claim.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
