# 偏好模型在既定标注协议上预测准确，只说明它复现了该协议下的相对选择，不能单独证明分数代表事实性、安全性或广泛用户效用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Accurate prediction under a specified annotation protocol shows only that a preference model reproduces relative choices under that protocol; it does not by itself establish that its score represents factuality, safety, or broad user utility*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 预测对象先由标注协议定义
> 一条偏好记录不是脱离环境的“人类价值”。它来自某组标注者，在某套说明下，从某个候选集合中，对某类提示作出的相对选择。模型在同分布留出对上预测准确，证明它能复现这些选择；只有另外验证过，才可以把分数解释成“更真实”“更安全”或“对广泛用户更有用”。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A preference record is not context-free human value. It is a relative choice made by a particular annotator population, under particular instructions, among particular candidates, on a particular prompt distribution. Accuracy on an in-distribution holdout shows that the model reproduces those choices. Interpreting the score as factuality, safety, or utility for a broader population requires separate validation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同一个分数会随协议改变含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The same score changes meaning when the protocol changes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

至少有五个环节共同决定观察到的偏好：

1. **谁在判断：** 标注者的语言、经验、风险承受和用户群体；
2. **按什么判断：** 说明要求综合考虑哪些维度，冲突时怎样取舍；
3. **看到了什么：** 候选由哪些策略生成，是否允许平局或弃权；
4. **在哪里判断：** 提示类型、领域和难度分布；
5. **怎样汇总：** 多名标注者的冲突如何裁决或加权。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Observed preferences are jointly determined by who judges, the instructions and trade-offs they use, which candidates they see, the prompt distribution, and the rule for aggregating disagreement. Changing any of these can change the construct represented by an otherwise identical scalar score.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，若候选主要在“短而正确”与“长而正确”之间变化，模型可能学到长度线索；若错误回答写得更自信或更迎合，标注协议也可能把这些风格与正确性混在一起。[[长度代理偏差]] 与 [[迎合偏好偏差]] 是具体失真，不是这张卡要重新展开的全部机制。这里的总边界是：**预测被记录的选择，不等于识别选择背后的每个规范维度。**

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If candidates vary mainly between short-correct and long-correct answers, length can become a cue. Confident or agreeable wording can likewise become entangled with correctness. [[长度代理偏差|Length bias]] and [[迎合偏好偏差|sycophancy bias]] are concrete mechanisms. The general boundary is that predicting recorded choices does not identify every normative dimension behind them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三种证据不能互相代替
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three kinds of evidence are not interchangeable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **选择预测效度：** 在同一协议的新比较中，模型能否预测被选项；
- **构念效度：** 分数变化是否真的对应声称的维度，例如事实正确或危险性，而不是长度、语气等代理；
- **外部效度：** 换标注群体、候选生成器、提示领域或真实使用环境后，关系是否仍成立。

一个高 held-out accuracy 主要支持第一项。若要把分数命名为事实性，至少要和独立事实标准对照；若要声称安全性，要在明确威胁模型和危险任务上验证；若要声称广泛用户效用，要报告不同用户群体和任务分层，而不是只沿用训练标注者的平均选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choice-prediction validity asks whether the model predicts new choices under the same protocol. Construct validity asks whether score changes track the claimed property rather than a proxy. External validity asks whether the relationship survives new annotator groups, candidate generators, domains, and use settings. High holdout accuracy primarily supports the first. Factuality, safety, and broad utility each require their own independent criteria and stratified evaluation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 正确说法
> 可以说“该模型在这套标注协议和候选分布上预测相对选择”。若还没有相应验证，不要把它缩写成“模型学会了人类价值”，也不要把一个标量直接命名为真值、安全分或社会福利。若系统最终必须聚合多种目标，另见 [[对齐目标聚合]]；若策略开始主动寻找代理分数漏洞，另见 [[奖励代理过优化]]。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A defensible statement is that the model predicts relative choices under the stated annotation protocol and candidate distribution. Without further validation, do not shorten this to “the model learned human values” or rename the scalar as truth, safety, or social welfare. [[对齐目标聚合|Objective aggregation]] and [[奖励代理过优化|proxy overoptimization]] are separate downstream problems.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个奖励模型在同一批标注者、同一说明和同类候选的留出比较上达到 90% 准确率。能否据此断言它的分数是“事实正确率”？下一条最直接的验证是什么？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A reward model reaches 90% accuracy on held-out comparisons from the same annotators, instructions, and candidate type. Does this establish that its score is a probability of factual correctness? What is the most direct next validation?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。结果只支持它预测该协议下的选择。应先在独立判定事实真假的样本上检验分数与事实错误的关系，同时控制长度、语气等可能代理；若要外推到其他用户，还要换群体和任务复测。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. The result supports prediction of choices under that protocol. Test the score against independently established factual outcomes while controlling plausible proxies such as length and tone; generalization to other users also requires new groups and tasks.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Ouyang et al. (2022), *Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback*](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.02155#S5.SS2)：第 5.2 节明确把对齐限定为特定参考群体与应用，并讨论标注者群体、研究者指令和模型行为之间的关系。
- [Stiennon et al. (2020), *Learning to summarize from human feedback*](https://arxiv.org/html/2009.01325)：第 3.3–3.4 节给出比较数据和奖励模型，第 4.3 节展示奖励模型在分布变化与过度优化下不能被直接当作真实目标。
- [Rafailov et al. (2023), *Direct Preference Optimization*](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.18290#S5.SS1)：第 5.1 节把奖励评估明确限制在给定偏好数据和生成分布的实验口径内。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Ouyang et al. (2022), §5.2, explicitly scopes alignment to a particular reference group and application and discusses the roles of labelers and researcher instructions.
- Stiennon et al. (2020), §§3.3–3.4 and 4.3, documents comparison data, reward modeling, distribution shift, and overoptimization of the learned proxy.
- Rafailov et al. (2023), §5.1, evaluates reward behavior within a specified preference dataset and generation setting rather than treating it as an unrestricted human-utility measure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
