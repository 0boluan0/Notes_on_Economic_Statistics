# 优化器不连续或不可微并不能推出值函数不可微
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A discontinuous or nondifferentiable optimizer does not imply that the value function is nondifferentiable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 选择路径和最优价值是两个函数
> [[最优解对应]] $S(\theta)$ 可以多值，从中挑出的某条优化器路径 $x^*(\theta)$ 可以跳跃。[[值函数]] $v(\theta)$ 只记录这些选择共享的最优目标值。一条选择路径不光滑，不足以判定价值也不光滑；必须直接检查 $v$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The solution correspondence may be multivalued, and a selected optimizer path may jump. The value function records only the optimal objective value shared by those choices. Nonsmoothness of a selected path is therefore insufficient to establish nonsmoothness of the value itself.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反例：选择跳跃，价值仍可微
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Counterexample: the choice jumps while the value remains differentiable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令
$$
X=\{-1,1\},
\qquad
f(x,\theta)=x\theta^3.
$$
当 $\theta<0$ 时，唯一最优选择是 $x=-1$；当 $\theta>0$ 时，唯一最优选择是 $x=1$。因此任何在两侧选取唯一最优点的路径都在零点跳跃。但
$$
v(\theta)=\max_{x\in\{-1,1\}}x\theta^3=|\theta|^3,
$$
而 $v$ 在零点可微，且 $v'(0)=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X=\{-1,1\}$ and $f(x,\theta)=x\theta^3$. The unique optimizer switches from $-1$ to $1$ as the parameter crosses zero, so the selected optimizer jumps. Yet $v(\theta)=|\theta|^3$ is differentiable at zero with derivative zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同样的跳跃也可以伴随折点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The same optimizer switch can also accompany a kink*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把目标改成 $f(x,\theta)=x\theta$。优化器仍在零点从 $-1$ 切换到 $1$，但这次
$$
v(\theta)=|\theta|,
$$
右导数为 $1$，左导数为 $-1$，普通导数不存在。两个例子的优化器都跳跃，值函数的光滑性却不同，证明两者之间没有这个逆推。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $f(x,\theta)=x\theta$, the optimizer switches in the same way, but $v(\theta)=|\theta|$ has right derivative one and left derivative minus one. The optimizer's jump alone therefore does not determine the value function's smoothness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 应当检查什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What should be checked instead*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若最优解多值或选择分支跳跃，先检查值函数本身的左、右或方向导数。在紧且不随参数变化的选择集等适当条件下，[[Danskin定理]] 可以直接由当前最优解的参数梯度给出方向导数。若选择集随参数变化、不紧或最优值不取得，则需要匹配这些条件的更一般包络定理，不能原样套用固定紧集公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the optimizer is multivalued or jumps, inspect one-sided or directional derivatives of the value itself. Under the compact parameter-independent choice-set assumptions of [[Danskin定理|Danskin's theorem]], active optimizer gradients give those directional derivatives. Parameter-dependent constraints, noncompactness, or nonattainment require a theorem matched to that setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 观察到 $x^*(\theta)$ 在 $\theta_0$ 跳跃时，最稳妥的下一步是什么？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When $x^*(\theta)$ jumps at $\theta_0$, what is the safest next step?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 直接检查 $v(\theta)$ 在 $\theta_0$ 的左、右或方向导数，而不是由选择跳跃自动断言价值不可微。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Inspect the value function's left, right, or directional derivatives at $\theta_0$ instead of inferring nondifferentiability from the optimizer's jump.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Milgrom and Segal, “Envelope Theorems for Arbitrary Choice Sets,” *Econometrica* 70(2)](https://web.stanford.edu/~milgrom/publishedarticles/Envelope%20Theorems.pdf)：核对包络结论并不要求先存在一条光滑的最优选择分支，以及价值单侧导数的一般边界。
- LSE EC400，*SOFP Lecture Notes*（课程讲义）：核对经典可微包络公式与多最优解时的课程警示；正文的两个可直接计算反例分离了选择光滑性与价值光滑性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Milgrom and Segal, “Envelope Theorems for Arbitrary Choice Sets,” was checked for envelope results without a smooth optimizer selection and for one-sided value derivatives.
- The EC400 SOFP notes were checked for the classical smooth envelope formula and its warning about multiple optima. The two explicit examples directly separate smoothness of the optimizer from smoothness of the value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
