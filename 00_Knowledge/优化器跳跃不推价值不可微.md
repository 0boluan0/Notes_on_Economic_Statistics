<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Can an optimizer jump while the value remains differentiable?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*优化选择跳跃时，价值还能可微吗？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A small change in a parameter can abruptly change which choice is best, while the best score still changes without a corner. The reason is that the **optimizer** is an input, whereas the **value** is its score. We can test the distinction with only two available choices and elementary derivatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参数稍有变化，最优选择可能突然换到另一个位置，但最优分数仍能平滑地变化。关键在于：**优化器**是输入，**价值**是它的分数。只用两个可选点和基本导数，就能直接检验这一区别。

> [!important] Choice and value · 选择路径和最优价值是两个函数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The solution correspondence may be multivalued, and a selected optimizer path may jump. The value function records only the optimal objective value shared by those choices. Nonsmoothness of a selected path is therefore insufficient to establish nonsmoothness of the value itself.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[最优解对应]] $S(\theta)$ 可以多值，从中挑出的某条优化器路径 $x^*(\theta)$ 可以跳跃。[[值函数]] $v(\theta)$ 只记录这些选择共享的最优目标值。一条选择路径不光滑，不足以判定价值也不光滑；必须直接检查 $v$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here a selection $x^*(\theta)$ means one member of [[最优解对应|the optimizer set]] at each parameter where a solution exists. A [[导数|derivative]] at zero tests whether the difference quotient $[v(h)-v(0)]/h$ has one limit as nonzero $h$ approaches zero from both sides. We will compute that quotient directly, without assuming a differentiable optimizer selection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的选择路径 $x^*(\theta)$，是在有最优解的每个参数处，从最优解集合中挑出的一个成员。零点的[[导数]]要检查：当非零 $h$ 从左右两侧趋近零时，差商 $[v(h)-v(0)]/h$ 是否趋向同一个数。下面直接计算这个差商，无需先假设最优选择路径可微。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Counterexample: the choice jumps while the value remains differentiable
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反例：选择跳跃，价值仍可微

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The feasible set below contains exactly two points. At parameter $\theta$, their scores are $f(-1,\theta)=-\theta^3$ and $f(1,\theta)=\theta^3$. Since $\theta^3$ has the same sign as $\theta$, we can identify the better choice just by comparing these two numbers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令
$$
X=\{-1,1\},
\qquad
f(x,\theta)=x\theta^3.
$$
当 $\theta<0$ 时，唯一最优选择是 $x=-1$；当 $\theta>0$ 时，唯一最优选择是 $x=1$。因此任何在两侧选取唯一最优点的路径都在零点跳跃。但
$$
v(\theta)=\max_{x\in\{-1,1\}}x\theta^3=|\theta|^3,
$$
而 $v$ 在零点可微，且 $v'(0)=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X=\{-1,1\}$ and $f(x,\theta)=x\theta^3$. The unique optimizer switches from $-1$ to $1$ as the parameter crosses zero, so the selected optimizer jumps. Yet $v(\theta)=|\theta|^3$ is differentiable at zero with derivative zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在零参数处，两种选择都给出零分数，所以 $S(0)=\{-1,1\}$。两侧的最优解分别唯一，因此每条最优选择路径的左极限均为 $-1$，右极限均为 $1$；无论在零点选哪一个，都无法消除跳跃。这是最优选择被迫切换，不只是任意打破平局造成的跳变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At zero both choices score zero, so $S(0)=\{-1,1\}$. The unique choices on the two sides force left limit $-1$ and right limit $1$ for every optimizer selection, regardless of its value at zero. To verify the value derivative, use $v(0)=0$ and, for $h\ne0$, compute
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

验证价值的导数时，利用 $v(0)=0$，对 $h\ne0$ 计算

$$
\frac{v(h)-v(0)}{h}=\frac{|h|^3}{h}=h|h|\longrightarrow0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The absolute value of this quotient is $h^2$, which tends to zero from either side. Away from zero, differentiating the two polynomial pieces gives $v'(\theta)=3\theta|\theta|$; this also tends to zero at zero. Thus the value has a continuous first derivative, even though every optimizer selection jumps.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个差商的绝对值为 $h^2$，从哪一侧趋近零，结果都为零。离开零点后，分别对两侧多项式求导，得到 $v'(\theta)=3\theta|\theta|$，它在趋近零时也趋于零。因此，价值有连续的一阶导数，而每条最优选择路径却都发生跳跃。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same optimizer switch can also accompany a kink
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同样的跳跃也可以伴随折点

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $f(x,\theta)=x\theta$, the optimizer switches in the same way, but $v(\theta)=|\theta|$ has right derivative one and left derivative minus one. The optimizer's jump alone therefore does not determine the value function's smoothness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把目标改成 $f(x,\theta)=x\theta$。优化器仍在零点从 $-1$ 切换到 $1$，但这次
$$
v(\theta)=|\theta|,
$$
右导数为 $1$，左导数为 $-1$，普通导数不存在。两个例子的优化器都跳跃，值函数的光滑性却不同，证明两者之间没有这个逆推。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the same two-point feasible set. The scores are now $-\theta$ and $\theta$, with the same optimizer set at every parameter as in the cubic example. However, the value quotient is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保持同一个两点可行集。两种选择的分数变为 $-\theta$ 和 $\theta$，每个参数处的最优解集合都与三次例子相同。但价值的差商变为

$$
\frac{v(h)-v(0)}{h}=\frac{|h|}{h}
=\begin{cases}-1,&h<0,\\1,&h>0.\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two limits disagree. In the cubic example the two score curves meet at zero with the same slope, zero; in this linear example they meet with slopes $-1$ and $1$. Choosing the higher score therefore produces a differentiable join in the first example and a corner in the second. The optimizer switch by itself misses this slope information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左右极限不同。在三次例子中，两条分数曲线在零点相遇时的斜率都为零；在线性例子中，斜率却分别为 $-1$ 和 $1$。因此，逐点取较高分数，在前者产生可微的接合，在后者产生折角。只观察优化器切换，会漏掉这些斜率信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What should be checked instead
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 应当检查什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the optimizer is multivalued or jumps, inspect one-sided or directional derivatives of the value itself. Under the compact parameter-independent choice-set assumptions of [[Danskin定理|Danskin's theorem]], active optimizer gradients give those directional derivatives. Parameter-dependent constraints, noncompactness, or nonattainment require a theorem matched to that setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若最优解多值或选择分支跳跃，先检查值函数本身的左、右或方向导数。在紧且不随参数变化的选择集等适当条件下，[[Danskin定理]] 可以直接由当前最优解的参数梯度给出方向导数。若选择集随参数变化、不紧或最优值不取得，则需要匹配这些条件的更一般包络定理，不能原样套用固定紧集公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the two finite examples above, the direct calculations already prove the conclusions; no envelope theorem is being invoked. For a new problem, verify the full hypotheses of the particular linked theorem, including its regularity assumptions, rather than inferring them from compactness alone. Ordinary chain-rule differentiation of $f(x^*(\theta),\theta)$ through a discontinuous selection is unavailable; the value or an applicable envelope theorem must be analyzed instead.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面两个有限选择例子的直接计算已经证明结论，没有调用包络定理。遇到新问题，应逐项核对所用版本的完整假设，包括正则性条件，不能单凭紧性推定其他前提。最优选择路径不连续时，不能通过普通链式法则对 $f(x^*(\theta),\theta)$ 求导；应分析价值本身，或使用确实适用的包络定理。

> [!question]- Which derivative? · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When $x^*(\theta)$ jumps at $\theta_0$, what is the safest next step?
>
> **Answer:** Inspect the value function's left, right, or directional derivatives at $\theta_0$ instead of inferring nondifferentiability from the optimizer's jump.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 观察到 $x^*(\theta)$ 在 $\theta_0$ 跳跃时，最稳妥的下一步是什么？
>
> **答案：** 直接检查 $v(\theta)$ 在 $\theta_0$ 的左、右或方向导数，而不是由选择跳跃自动断言价值不可微。

## Sources and verification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Milgrom and Segal, “Envelope Theorems for Arbitrary Choice Sets,” was checked for envelope results without a smooth optimizer selection and for one-sided value derivatives.
- The EC400 SOFP notes were checked for the classical smooth envelope formula and its warning about multiple optima. The two explicit examples directly separate smoothness of the optimizer from smoothness of the value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Milgrom and Segal, “Envelope Theorems for Arbitrary Choice Sets,” *Econometrica* 70(2)](https://web.stanford.edu/~milgrom/publishedarticles/Envelope%20Theorems.pdf)：核对包络结论并不要求先存在一条光滑的最优选择分支，以及价值单侧导数的一般边界。
- LSE EC400，*SOFP Lecture Notes*（课程讲义）：核对经典可微包络公式与多最优解时的课程警示；正文的两个可直接计算反例分离了选择光滑性与价值光滑性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Precise locations: Milgrom–Segal, printed pp. 584–586 / PDF pp. 3–5, introduces values as upper envelopes, defines the optimizer set separately, and states Theorem 1 without requiring a smooth optimizer selection. Its conditional derivative statement does not itself prove differentiability. EC400 notes PDF pp. 37–38 and 40 give the classical differentiable-branch route and its scope. The two examples here are derived directly, including their optimizer sets and the two-sided difference quotients; the cubic example is not justified by assuming parameter convexity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

精确位置：Milgrom–Segal 印刷第584–586页／PDF 第3–5页从上包络解释价值，将最优解集合单独定义，并在定理1中不要求先有光滑选择分支；该条件性导数结论本身不证明价值可微。EC400 讲义 PDF 第37–38、40页给出经典可微分支路线及其范围。本卡两个例子均直接推导，包括最优解集合与双侧差商，没有通过假设参数凸性来证明三次例子。
