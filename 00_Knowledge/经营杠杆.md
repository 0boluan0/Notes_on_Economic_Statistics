# 经营杠杆是固定经营成本使经营利润对销售变化更敏感的机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operating leverage is the mechanism by which fixed operating costs amplify operating profit's response to sales changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经营杠杆（operating leverage）来自成本结构：在相关产量范围内，销售增减时一部分经营成本不随之同比变化，于是利润的百分比变化可能大于销售的百分比变化。它不要求借债，不能与债务利息造成的财务杠杆混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operating leverage comes from cost structure. Within the relevant output range, some operating costs do not change proportionately with sales, so profit can change by a larger percentage. It does not require borrowing and differs from financial leverage created by debt financing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在单产品、销量 $Q>0$、经营利润非零，且售价 $p$、单位变动成本 $v$、固定成本 $F$ 保持不变时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one product with positive volume $Q$, fixed price $p$, unit variable cost $v$, and fixed operating cost $F$, and nonzero operating profit:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$EBIT=(p-v)Q-F,\qquad DOL=\frac{dEBIT/EBIT}{dQ/Q}=\frac{(p-v)Q}{(p-v)Q-F}.$$

设 $p=10,v=6,F=300,Q=100$，经营利润为 100，经营杠杆系数为 4。销量增加 10% 至 110，利润变成 140，增加 40%。销量下降同样被放大；接近盈亏平衡点时，分母趋近零，比例指标尤其不稳定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $p=10,v=6,F=300,Q=100$, EBIT is 100 and DOL is 4. A 10% volume rise to 110 raises EBIT to 140, a 40% increase. Downturns are amplified too; near break-even, the percentage measure becomes unstable as its denominator approaches zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若为了多卖而降价，或跨过产能门槛要加一座工厂，就应重新算完整利润，不能沿用旧 DOL。这里的固定成本是指定期限与产量范围内的假设，不表示永久不变。[[收入驱动预测]]先提供量价情景，本卡解释给定成本结构后的利润反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If extra sales require a price cut or a new plant, recompute total profit instead of reusing the old DOL. Fixed costs are fixed only over a specified horizon and output range. [[收入驱动预测|Revenue forecasting]] supplies the volume-price scenario; this atom explains the profit response under the stated cost structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
