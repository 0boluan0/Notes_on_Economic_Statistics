# 创新的正负福利楔子共同决定市场研发高于还是低于社会最优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Opposing welfare wedges jointly determine whether market research exceeds or falls short of the social optimum.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Aghion–Howitt 的市场研发可以偏少，也可以偏多。判断需要让私人均衡与社会规划者面对**同一生产技术、同一研发技术和同一劳动总量**，再比较它们选择的研发劳动。单看创新增加产出、存在知识外溢或某家企业利润下降，都不能决定总福利方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market research in Aghion–Howitt can be too low or too high. Compare private equilibrium and a social planner under the same production technology, research technology and labour endowment. Higher output, a knowledge spillover or an individual firm's lost profits alone cannot determine the overall welfare direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用 [[Aghion–Howitt稳态研发]] 的线性 Cobb–Douglas 特例：$N$ 是熟练劳动总量，$n$ 是研发劳动，$N-n$ 用于制造；$\lambda n$ 是创新强度，每次质量提升乘以 $\gamma$，$0<\alpha<1$。人口与劳动总量固定；这里的 $n$ 不是人口增长率。风险中性的社会目标为以 $r$ 贴现的期望消费现值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the linear Cobb–Douglas case in [[Aghion–Howitt稳态研发|stationary research equilibrium]]: $N$ is total skilled labour, $n$ research labour, $N-n$ manufacturing labour, $\lambda n$ innovation intensity, and $\gamma$ the quality multiplier, with $0<\alpha<1$. Population and labour endowments are fixed; $n$ is not population growth. The risk-neutral planner discounts expected consumption at rate $r$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参数与劳动总量固定，每个新质量状态只同比例放大所有消费，规划者的最优研发分配可取常数 $n$。由 [[随机创新的增长口径|期望产出的增长公式]]，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Parameters and labour endowments are fixed, and each new quality state scales all consumption proportionately, so the planner can choose an optimal constant research allocation $n$. The [[随机创新的增长口径|expected-output growth formula]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E[Y(\tau)]=A_0(N-n)^\alpha e^{\lambda n(\gamma-1)\tau},
$$
$$
U(n)=E\left[\int_0^\infty e^{-r\tau}Y(\tau)\,d\tau\right]
=\frac{A_0(N-n)^\alpha}{r-\lambda n(\gamma-1)}.
$$

现值公式需要分母为正。本卡使用充分条件 $r>\lambda N(\gamma-1)$，使整个 $0\le n\le N$ 比较范围的现值有限。分母中的 $\gamma-1$ 来自期望产出水平，不能换成平均对数增长中的 $\ln\gamma$；$r-\lambda n(\gamma-1)$ 是增长消费流的资本化项，也不是现实公共项目应直接采用的社会贴现率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The value formula requires a positive denominator. The sufficient condition $r>\lambda N(\gamma-1)$ ensures finite values throughout $0\le n\le N$. The term $\gamma-1$ comes from expected output levels and cannot be replaced by $\ln\gamma$. The adjusted denominator capitalises a growing consumption stream; it does not prescribe a real-world public-project discount rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $U(n)$ 求导，内点最优满足
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiating $U(n)$ gives the interior condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\alpha}{N-n^*}
=\frac{\lambda(\gamma-1)}{r-\lambda n^*(\gamma-1)},
\qquad
n^*=\frac{\lambda(\gamma-1)N-\alpha r}
{\lambda(\gamma-1)(1-\alpha)}.
$$

在上述充分条件下，若分子为正，这个解在 $(0,N)$ 内且为最大值；若分子非正，最优为 $n^*=0$。它与市场正研发条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the sufficient condition above, a positive numerator gives an interior maximum in $(0,N)$; a nonpositive numerator gives $n^*=0$. Compare it with the positive-research market condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
1=\frac{\lambda\gamma(1-\alpha)(N-\bar n)}{\alpha(r+\lambda\bar n)}.
$$

原文把两个条件的差异拆为四个机制。[[创新的跨期知识外溢]] 使私人忽略后续代际收益；私人只取得利润而非全部消费产出价值，这是收益不能完全占有的差距。它们倾向使研发偏少。[[商业抢夺效应]] 使私人忽略旧租金损失，[[垄断下的研发机会成本]] 使私人低估被挤出的制造产出；它们倾向使研发偏多。这是同一均衡中的机制比较，**不是四笔可直接机械相加的福利金额**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Four mechanisms separate these conditions. [[创新的跨期知识外溢|Intertemporal spillovers]] leave later benefits outside the innovator's reward; incomplete appropriability also limits the reward to profits rather than all consumption value. These encourage too little research. [[商业抢夺效应|Business stealing]] omits incumbent rent loss, while [[垄断下的研发机会成本|the manufacturing-monopoly cost wedge]] understates displaced output, encouraging too much. These are mechanisms within one equilibrium, not four monetary amounts to add mechanically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $\alpha=0.5,\gamma=1.5,N=1,\lambda=0.1,A_0=1$。两个贴现率都满足 $r>0.05$，质量步长满足 $\gamma>\sqrt2$ 的充分大创新条件：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix $\alpha=0.5,\gamma=1.5,N=1,\lambda=0.1,A_0=1$. Both discount rates below satisfy $r>0.05$, and the quality step satisfies the drastic-innovation condition $\gamma>\sqrt2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 贴现率 $r$ | 市场研发 $\bar n$ | 社会研发 $n^*$ | 市场现值 $U(\bar n)$ | 最优现值 $U(n^*)$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $0.08$ | $0.28$ | $0.40$ | $12.856487$ | $12.909944$ |
| $0.095$ | $0.22$ | $0.10$ | $10.514001$ | $10.540926$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Discount rate $r$ | Market research $\bar n$ | Optimal research $n^*$ | Market value $U(\bar n)$ | Optimal value $U(n^*)$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $0.08$ | $0.28$ | $0.40$ | $12.856487$ | $12.909944$ |
| $0.095$ | $0.22$ | $0.10$ | $10.514001$ | $10.540926$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一行市场研发不足；第二行市场研发过多，减少研发反而提高消费现值。因此增长更快不是这两个可行分配之间的充分福利准则。这些数字是教学参数，不是对现实研发最优比例的估计；效用总量提高也不表示旧企业自动得到补偿，见 [[财务与社会评价]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market research is too low in the first row and too high in the second, where reducing research raises consumption value. Faster growth is therefore not a sufficient welfare criterion across these allocations. The figures are teaching parameters, not estimates of optimal real-world research shares. Higher aggregate welfare does not automatically compensate incumbents; see [[财务与社会评价|financial versus social appraisal]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

进入政策判断时，[[正外部性补贴]] 的单一外溢基准不能直接替代这里多个楔子的均衡。现实资助还需要相对 [[公共项目反事实|未实施方案时的研究路径]] 核对额外效果，再用 [[成本—收益分析]] 处理资源与受益人范围。固定 $\gamma$ 的本例没有求创新幅度、专利长度或劳动转岗政策的最优选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For policy appraisal, the single-externality benchmark in [[正外部性补贴|corrective subsidies]] does not replace this equilibrium with multiple wedges. Actual funding requires additionality relative to the [[公共项目反事实|research path without the proposal]], followed by [[成本—收益分析|social cost-benefit analysis]]. This fixed-$\gamma$ example does not optimise innovation size, patent duration or worker-adjustment policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
