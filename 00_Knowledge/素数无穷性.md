# Infinitude of primes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*素数无穷性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No finite list can contain every [[素数|prime]]. Even if a list is very long, one can prove that some prime is missing. For the short list $2,3,5$, multiply the entries and add $1$: $2\cdot3\cdot5+1=31$. None of the listed primes divides $31$, since division by each leaves remainder $1$. The general proof uses this same idea, but does not require the constructed number itself to be prime.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
任何有限列表都不能包含全部[[素数]]。列表再长，也能证明还有素数没有列进去。例如先列 $2,3,5$，把它们相乘再加 $1$，得到 $2\cdot3\cdot5+1=31$。列表中的素数都不整除 $31$，因为用每一个去除，余数都是 $1$。一般证明使用相同想法，但不要求构造出来的数本身就是素数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Escape any finite list
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 从任意有限列表找到遗漏

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take any finite nonempty list of primes $p_1,\ldots,p_k$, where $k\ge1$ counts its entries. Put $P=p_1\cdots p_k$ and $N=P+1$. The product $P$ is divisible by every listed prime, whereas $N$ is exactly one larger. The [[整除|divisibility]] notation $d\mid m$ means $m=dt$ for an integer $t$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
任取有限非空素数列表 $p_1,\ldots,p_k$，其中 $k\ge1$ 是列表长度。令 $P=p_1\cdots p_k$，再令 $N=P+1$。乘积 $P$ 能被每个列出的素数整除，$N$ 则恰好多 $1$。[[整除]]记号 $d\mid m$ 表示存在整数 $t$ 使 $m=dt$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $N>1$, the [[唯一素因数分解#Existence by strong induction|existence of a prime factorization]] supplies at least one prime divisor $q$ of $N$. Only existence is used here, not uniqueness. If $q$ were one of the listed primes, then $q\mid P$ as well as $q\mid N$. Subtracting these two integer multiples would give $q\mid(N-P)=1$, impossible for $q>1$. Therefore $q$ is outside the list.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
因为 $N>1$，[[唯一素因数分解#Existence by strong induction|素因数分解的存在性]]保证 $N$ 至少有一个素因子 $q$。这里只用存在性，不用唯一性。如果 $q$ 已在列表中，那么除了 $q\mid N$ 以外，还会有 $q\mid P$。两个整数倍相减便得到 $q\mid(N-P)=1$；这与 $q>1$ 矛盾。因此 $q$ 不在原列表中。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To spell out that last impossibility, $q\mid1$ would mean $1=qt$ for an integer $t$. If $t\le0$ the product is nonpositive, and if $t\ge1$ it is at least $q\ge2$. Neither equals $1$. An empty list is also incomplete, since it omits the prime $2$. Thus every finite list omits a prime, which proves infinitude.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
最后的矛盾也可以直接展开：$q\mid1$ 会要求某个整数 $t$ 满足 $1=qt$。若 $t\le0$，乘积非正；若 $t\ge1$，乘积至少为 $q\ge2$，都不可能等于 $1$。空列表同样不完整，因为它没有包含素数 $2$。所以每个有限列表都会漏掉某个素数，素数因此有无穷多个。

> [!important] What the construction guarantees · 构造真正保证什么
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The number $P+1$ has a **prime factor outside the list**. That conclusion is enough. It is stronger than merely finding a new integer, but it does not say that $P+1$ is always prime.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> $P+1$ 有一个**不在原列表中的素因子**，这就足够了。结论不只是找到另一个整数，也没有断言 $P+1$ 总是素数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The constructed number may be composite
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 构造数可能是合数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the first six primes $2,3,5,7,11,13$. The construction gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
取前六个素数 $2,3,5,7,11,13$，构造得到

$$2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13+1=30031=59\cdot509.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both factors on the right exceed $1$, so $30031$ is composite. Nevertheless $59$ is a prime divisor outside the list. To check its primality, a composite $59=ab$ with $1<a\le b$ would have $a\le\sqrt{59}<8$ and hence a prime divisor among $2,3,5,7$. None divides $59$. This example confirms the distinction: a new prime factor is guaranteed, while a prime value of $P+1$ is not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
右边两个因子都大于 $1$，所以 $30031$ 是合数；但 $59$ 确实是一个不在列表中的素因子。检验它是素数：若合数 $59=ab$ 且 $1<a\le b$，则 $a\le\sqrt{59}<8$，于是它必有 $2,3,5,7$ 中的某个素因子。然而这四个数都不整除 $59$。这个例子说明，保证的是得到新的素因子，并非保证 $P+1$ 本身为素数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What “infinitely many” does and does not say
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 无穷性与大小的关系

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every nonnegative integer bound $B$, there is a prime greater than $B$. Indeed, only finitely many positive integers are at most $B$; list all primes among them and apply the argument above (or take $2$ if the list is empty). A prime outside this complete bounded list must exceed $B$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对每个非负整数界 $B$，都有比 $B$ 更大的素数。因为不超过 $B$ 的正整数只有有限多个，可以把其中全部素数列出，再用上面的论证；若列表为空，直接取 $2$。不在这个完整有界列表中的素数必然大于 $B$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an arbitrary incomplete list, “outside the list” need not mean “larger than its largest entry.” For example, $2\cdot5\cdot11+1=111=3\cdot37$ has the new prime factor $3$, which is smaller than $11$. The proof also supplies no bound on the work needed to find a prime factor of a large constructed number. It is a proof that a finite list cannot be complete, not a claim about fast factorization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
如果列表只是任意选出的不完整列表，“不在列表中”不一定表示“比列表最大项还大”。例如 $2\cdot5\cdot11+1=111=3\cdot37$ 的新素因子 $3$ 就小于 $11$。证明也没有给出分解一个很大的构造数所需工作的上界；它证明有限列表不可能完整，并不声称素因数分解很快。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof depends only on primes being positive integers greater than $1$, elementary divisibility, and the existence argument by strong induction. No step assumes in advance that infinitely many primes are available. The claim concerns the positive integer primes, not a change of convention that would count negative signs as new prime values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这个证明只依赖素数是大于 $1$ 的正整数、基本整除性质，以及强归纳给出的素因子存在性。没有哪一步预先假设可以使用无穷多个素数。结论针对正整数素数，不是通过把负号另算成新的素数来改变计数约定。

> [!question]- Where is the new prime? · 新素数在哪里？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the construction gives a composite $N$, does Euclid's proof fail?
>
> **Answer:** No. Choose a prime divisor $q$ of $N$. Every listed prime fails to divide $N$, so $q$ must be missing from the list. The prime-factor existence theorem supplies $q$ even when $N$ is composite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 如果构造得到的是合数 $N$，Euclid 的证明是否失败？
>
> **答案：** 不失败。取 $N$ 的一个素因子 $q$。列表中的素数都不能整除 $N$，因此 $q$ 必在列表之外；即使 $N$ 为合数，素因子存在定理仍能提供这个 $q$。
