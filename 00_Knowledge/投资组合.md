# 投资组合是按确定权重共同持有的一组资产头寸
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An investment portfolio is a collection of asset positions held together with specified weights*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

投资组合不仅说明“持有什么”，也说明“各持有多少”。在单期模型中，设期初净投资财富 $W_0>0$，第 $i$ 项资产的有符号持仓价值为 $V_{i0}$，则它的组合权重为 $w_i=V_{i0}/W_0$。所有资产和融资头寸都计入时，$\sum_iw_i=1$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A portfolio specifies both its assets and the size of each position. In a one-period model with positive initial net wealth $W_0$, the weight of position $i$ is its signed initial value divided by that wealth. Including all assets and financing positions gives $\sum_iw_i=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如用 100 元自有资金买入 A 60 元、B 40 元，权重是 $(0.6,0.4)$。若允许卖空，并把卖空 B 所得的 30 元全部用于买 A，那么 A 为 130 元多头、B 为 30 元空头，净财富仍为 100 元，权重是 $(1.3,-0.3)$。这里忽略交易成本和保证金约束；实际是否可做，要放进[[投资组合可行集]]判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Investing 60 of 100 units in A and 40 in B gives weights $(0.6,0.4)$. If shorting is allowed and all proceeds are usable, a 130-unit long position in A financed partly by a 30-unit short position in B gives $(1.3,-0.3)$. Trading costs and margin constraints are omitted in this example; admissibility belongs to the [[投资组合可行集|feasible set]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“权重加起来等于 1”是净预算核算，不等于“每个权重都在 0 到 1 之间”。后者是额外的禁止卖空约束。确定了持仓，下一步才是[[单期组合收益|把资产回报合成组合回报]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weights summing to one express the net budget, not a ban on negative weights. Nonnegative weights require an additional long-only constraint. Given the positions, [[单期组合收益|one-period aggregation]] determines the portfolio return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
