# Marx 的价值、剩余与资本积累
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Marx on Value, Surplus, and Capital Accumulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释在劳动力按契约出售、商品按价值交换的前提下，资本为什么仍能取得剩余价值，并把剩余的一部分转成扩大再生产。
> **具体锚点：** 若工人一天创造 8 小时的新增价值，其中 4 小时等价于维持劳动力所需的工资，余下 4 小时就是剩余劳动；这里不是少付已完成劳动，而是劳动力这一商品的使用能创造超过其自身价值的新价值。
> **核心难点：** 劳动与劳动力、价值与价格、剩余价值与利润是不同层次；把它直接翻成“企业按成本加价”会丢掉生产关系和积累机制。
> **为什么重要：** 它把分配、工作日、技术、失业和资本积累放进同一个动态体系，而不只是在市场交换中解释价格。
> **继续：** 先掌握劳动力商品与必要/剩余劳动，再看剩余价值怎样资本化；对比价值分析如何转向边际选择，进入 [[边际革命与 Marshall 综合]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It explains how capital can obtain surplus value even when labor power is sold by contract and commodities exchange at their values, and how part of that surplus becomes expanded reproduction.
> **Concrete anchor:** Suppose a worker creates eight hours of new value in a day. Four hours reproduce the wage needed to maintain labor power, and the remaining four are surplus labor. The mechanism is not underpayment for labor already performed; it is that using the commodity labor power can create more value than the value of labor power itself.
> **Central difficulty:** Labor and labor power, value and price, and surplus value and profit belong to different analytical levels. Translating the argument directly into “the firm adds a markup to cost” loses the production relation and accumulation mechanism.
> **Why it matters:** The framework places distribution, the working day, technology, unemployment, and capital accumulation in one dynamic system rather than explaining price only through market exchange.
> **Continue:** First master labor power and the split between necessary and surplus labor, then trace how surplus value is capitalized. To compare the shift toward marginal choice, see [[边际革命与 Marshall 综合|The Marginal Revolution and Marshallian Synthesis]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从商品交换到资本循环
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From Commodity Exchange to the Circuit of Capital*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通商品流通可写成 $C-M-C$：卖出一种商品，是为了购买另一种使用价值。资本循环写成 $M-C-M'$：货币预付后回到更多货币，$M'=M+\Delta M$。问题不是把 $\Delta M$ 当作定义，而是解释它怎样在等价交换的前提下产生。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary commodity circulation can be written as $C-M-C$: one commodity is sold to obtain another use value. The circuit of capital is $M-C-M'$: money is advanced and returns as more money, with $M'=M+\Delta M$. The task is not merely to define $\Delta M$, but to explain how it can arise under exchange of equivalents.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

答案转向生产领域。资本购买生产资料和劳动力；生产资料把已有价值转移到产品，活劳动既保存这些价值，又创造新增价值。只有把交换和生产连接起来，才能区分一次交易获利、垄断加价与整个资本主义生产中可重复的剩余机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The answer moves into production. Capital purchases means of production and labor power. Means of production transfer existing value into the product, while living labor both preserves those values and creates new value. Connecting exchange to production distinguishes a gain in one transaction or a monopoly markup from a reproducible surplus mechanism across capitalist production.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 劳动与劳动力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Labor and Labor Power*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

劳动是实际发生的活动，劳动力是人在一定时间内劳动的能力。工资购买的是劳动力在约定期间的使用权；劳动力的价值与再生产这种能力所需的生活资料及历史社会条件相关，而它的使用价值是在生产中创造价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Labor is the activity actually performed; labor power is a person's capacity to work for a period. The wage purchases the use of that capacity for the contracted time. The value of labor power is related to the means of subsistence and historically specific social conditions required to reproduce it, while its use value is its capacity to create value in production.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个区分解释了为何“工资等于劳动力价值”与“工人创造超过工资的价值”可以同时成立。若把工资理解为每一单位已完成劳动的完整价值，就会把 Marx 正在解释的机制预先排除。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This distinction explains how “the wage equals the value of labor power” and “the worker creates more value than the wage” can both hold. Treating the wage as the full value of every unit of labor already performed assumes away the mechanism Marx is trying to explain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 必要劳动、剩余劳动与剥削率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Necessary Labor, Surplus Labor, and the Rate of Surplus Value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必要劳动时间再生产劳动力价值，剩余劳动时间创造剩余价值。若 $v$ 表示可变资本、$s$ 表示剩余价值，则剩余价值率为

$$\frac{s}{v}=\frac{\text{surplus labour time}}{\text{necessary labour time}}.$$

它衡量的是 Marx 理论中的生产关系，不是利润率；利润率还把机器、原料等不变资本 $c$ 放入分母，写作 $s/(c+v)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Necessary labor time reproduces the value of labor power, while surplus labor time creates surplus value. If $v$ denotes variable capital and $s$ surplus value, the rate of surplus value is

$$\frac{s}{v}=\frac{\text{surplus labour time}}{\text{necessary labour time}}.$$

This measures a production relation in Marx's framework, not the profit rate. The profit rate also places constant capital $c$, such as machinery and materials, in the denominator and is written $s/(c+v)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Worked example：八小时工作日
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: An Eight-hour Working Day*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设八小时劳动创造 800 元新增价值，维持一天劳动力所需工资为 400 元。必要劳动为 4 小时，剩余劳动为 4 小时，$s/v=400/400=100\%$。若生产率提高使工资商品只需 3 小时生产，而工作日不变，必要劳动降至 3 小时、剩余劳动升至 5 小时；这属于相对剩余价值机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose eight hours of labor create 800 units of new value and the daily wage required to reproduce labor power is 400. Necessary labor is four hours, surplus labor is four hours, and $s/v=400/400=100\%$. If productivity in wage-goods production rises so that reproduction requires only three hours while the working day stays fixed, necessary labor falls to three hours and surplus labor rises to five; this is the mechanism of relative surplus value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

延长工作日而必要劳动不变，会增加绝对剩余价值；降低劳动力再生产所需时间而工作日不变，会增加相对剩余价值。两者都要说明制度、技术和工资商品条件，不能只观察会计利润后倒推。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Extending the working day while necessary labor is unchanged increases absolute surplus value. Reducing the time needed to reproduce labor power while the working day is fixed increases relative surplus value. Both require an account of institutions, technology, and wage-goods conditions; neither can be inferred backward from accounting profit alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 积累、扩大再生产与技术
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Accumulation, Expanded Reproduction, and Technology*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本家可把剩余价值用于个人消费，也可购买更多生产资料和劳动力，使下一轮生产扩大。积累因此不是简单的“少消费、多储蓄”，而是剩余产品必须包含可作为新增生产资料和生活资料的物质要素，并能与新增劳动力结合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The capitalist can consume surplus value or use it to purchase additional means of production and labor power, expanding the next production cycle. Accumulation is therefore not merely “consume less and save more.” The surplus product must contain material elements usable as additional means of production and subsistence and must be combined with additional labor power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

技术提高可降低商品价值并重组劳动需求，也可能使资本构成提高、旧设备贬值和部分劳动者被排出。于是生产率进步、就业不安全和竞争性积累在同一机制中出现；它不等于“技术必然让所有工人永久失业”，也不等于调整没有冲突。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Technical improvement can lower commodity values, reorganize labor demand, raise the composition of capital, devalue old equipment, and displace some workers. Productivity growth, employment insecurity, and competitive accumulation therefore appear in the same mechanism. This means neither that technology permanently eliminates all work nor that adjustment is conflict-free.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 解释边界与失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Interpretive Boundaries and Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把价值直接等同于每一时点观察到的市场价格，因而要求每笔交易逐项验证劳动时间。
- 把剩余价值等同于欺诈或少发工资，遗漏劳动力商品的特殊使用价值。
- 把 $s/v$ 当作会计利润率，忽略不变资本与价值向生产价格的转化层次。
- 只用道德赞成或反对代替机制重建，既不说明必要/剩余劳动，也不说明积累怎样发生。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Equating value directly with every observed market price and demanding transaction-by-transaction labor-time verification.
- Equating surplus value with fraud or unpaid wages while omitting the special use value of labor power.
- Treating $s/v$ as the accounting profit rate and ignoring constant capital and the later transformation from values toward prices of production.
- Replacing reconstruction of the mechanism with moral approval or disapproval, without explaining necessary and surplus labor or how accumulation occurs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话区分劳动与劳动力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distinguish labor from labor power in your own words.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 劳动力是被工资契约购买的一段时间内的劳动能力，劳动是这种能力在生产中的实际运用；前者有自身再生产成本，后者能创造新增价值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Labor power is the capacity to work for a contracted period and is what the wage purchases; labor is the actual exercise of that capacity in production. The former has a reproduction cost, while the latter creates new value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么等价交换并不自动排除剩余价值？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does exchange of equivalents not automatically rule out surplus value?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 因为资本按价值购买劳动力后，在生产中使用它；劳动力的使用能创造超过劳动力自身价值的新价值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Capital can purchase labor power at its value and then use it in production. The use of labor power can create more new value than the value of labor power itself.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 八小时中必要劳动 3 小时、剩余劳动 5 小时，剩余价值率是多少？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If an eight-hour day contains three hours of necessary labor and five hours of surplus labor, what is the rate of surplus value?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> $s/v=5/3\approx166.7\%$；这不是把五小时除以整个八小时。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $s/v=5/3\approx166.7\%$; the denominator is necessary labor, not the full eight-hour day.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 剩余价值资本化为什么不只是一个金融账户变化？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is capitalization of surplus value more than a change in a financial account?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 扩大再生产还需要新增生产资料、生活资料和劳动力在下一轮生产中实际结合；货币余额本身不会自动扩大实物生产。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Expanded reproduction requires additional means of production, means of subsistence, and labor power to be combined in the next production cycle. A larger money balance does not by itself expand physical production.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
