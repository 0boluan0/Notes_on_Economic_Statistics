# 均衡信贷配给需要超额需求与银行无盈利偏离而不只是回报局部下降

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equilibrium credit rationing requires excess demand and no profitable feasible bank deviation, not merely a local fall in returns*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要把[[信贷配给|数量限制]]解释成均衡，必须说明：既定契约下仍有愿意且可能获得贷款的需求未被满足，而银行不能通过可行地调整条款、吸引申请者与融资来提高利润。暂时缺款、行政限价或某一次加息后回收下降，都不足以完成这个论证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Explaining [[信贷配给|a quantity restriction]] as an equilibrium requires unmet borrowing demand at the prevailing terms and no feasible adjustment of terms, applicant acquisition, and funding that would raise bank profits. A temporary shortage, an administered price cap, or a single observed fall in recovery after a rate increase is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Stiglitz–Weiss 图 4 的单一利率情形中，固定贷款规模及所考察的其他条款：贷款需求取决于向借款人收取的利率 $i$，可贷资金供给则取决于每单位贷款的预期回收 $\rho(i)$ 所支持的资金回报。若可实行的银行最优利率 $i^*$ 下仍有

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the single-rate case in Stiglitz–Weiss Figure 4, loan size and the other terms under consideration are fixed. Loan demand depends on the charged rate $i$, whereas fund supply depends on the return to funds supported by expected recovery per unit lent, $\rho(i)$. Suppose the implementable bank-optimal rate $i^*$ leaves
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
L^D(i^*)>L^S(\rho(i^*)),
$$

且进一步提价会降低单位回报、没有其他盈利的可行偏离，那么提价就不能像普通固定质量商品那样自动清空超额需求。这说明某些供给条件下可能出现均衡配给，不是只要存在信息不对称就必然配给。资金供给改变后，配给也可能消失。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
and higher rates reduce per-unit return, with no other profitable feasible deviation, raising the price does not automatically clear excess demand as for an ordinary fixed-quality good. This establishes the possibility of rationing under suitable supply conditions, not its inevitability whenever information is asymmetric. A change in fund supply can remove rationing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“局部下降”尤其不能代替完整比较。沿用[[贷款利率筛选效应|外部机会为 8 的两类型例子]]：$D=110$ 的银行回收为 105.6，$D=115$ 降至 92；但 $D=135$ 时风险型剩余为 $0.8(150-135)=12>8$，仍会申请，银行回收为 $0.8\times135=108$，反而高于 105.6。因此前两个数并没有证明 110 是最优值；这些数也没有提供完整的资金供给和竞争条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A local decline does not replace a complete comparison. In the [[贷款利率筛选效应|two-type example with outside option 8]], recovery falls from 105.6 at $D=110$ to 92 at $D=115$. Yet at $D=135$, the risky type's residual is $0.8(150-135)=12>8$, so it still applies and recovery is $0.8\times135=108$, exceeding 105.6. The first two values therefore do not establish 110 as optimal, and these numbers do not specify the full funding and competitive environment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原文定理 6 还允许多峰回报下的双利率均衡，不能把单一函数的无条件全局最大值当作所有配给均衡的必要条件。若允许调整抵押、贷款额或契约菜单，也必须把这些可行偏离纳入分析；核心始终是给定市场选择与资金条件下没有盈利偏离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paper's Theorem 6 also allows two-rate equilibria with multiple return peaks. An unconditional global maximum of one return function is therefore not necessary for every rationing equilibrium. If collateral, loan size, or menus can vary, those feasible deviations must enter the analysis. The central requirement remains no profitable deviation given applicant choices and funding conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
