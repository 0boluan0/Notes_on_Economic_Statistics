# 市场冲击是交易行为造成的执行价格或市场价格变化

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market impact is the change in execution or market prices caused by trading activity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场冲击是订单执行、买卖压力及交易引发的市场反应所造成的价格变化。在交易成本语境中，通常关心它是否使买入更贵或卖出更便宜。关键词是“由交易造成”：执行期间出现的全部价格变动，不自动都是该笔订单的冲击。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market impact is a price change caused by order execution, buying or selling pressure, and market responses to trading. Cost analysis commonly focuses on making purchases dearer or sales cheaper. Causation matters: not every price move during execution is impact caused by that order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最简单的机械渠道是消耗有限[[市场深度]]。静态卖盘为 100.00 元 50 股、100.20 元 100 股，买入 120 股的[[逐档成交均价|均价]]约为 100.1167 元，比原最佳卖价高约 0.1167 元。这个受控推演说明跨档取量的代价，但不能仅凭它断言下一时刻价格会永久上升同样幅度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The simplest mechanical channel consumes finite [[市场深度|depth]]. With static asks of 50 shares at 100.00 and 100 at 100.20, buying 120 shares produces an [[逐档成交均价|average fill]] of about 100.1167, roughly 0.1167 above the original best ask. This controlled calculation illustrates walking-the-book cost, not a permanent future price increase of that magnitude.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

真实市场还会补单、撤单和重新估值；新闻引起的价格变化属于需要区分的执行期间风险。估计冲击要说明比较基准、订单量、时间跨度以及如何区分自身交易与其他变化，不能仅凭“下单后价格变了”作因果归因，也不能把固定线性或平方根公式当成所有市场的定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Real markets replenish, cancel and revalue orders; news-driven price changes are execution-period risk that must be distinguished. Impact estimation must state its benchmark, order size, horizon and treatment of other price changes. A move after submission alone does not identify causation, and no fixed linear or square-root formula defines impact in every market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
