# Proof by contradiction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*反证法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **proof by contradiction** temporarily assumes that the desired statement is false. It then follows that assumption through valid steps until it conflicts with a fact that must hold. The conflict explains why the temporary assumption cannot describe a possible situation under the original hypotheses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**反证法**先暂时假设待证命题是假的，再沿可靠推理追踪这个假设，直到它与必须成立的事实冲突。这个冲突说明：在原题设下，临时反设描述的情形不可能出现。

> [!important] Assume the negation, then justify the contradiction · 反设后仍须逐步论证
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To prove a proposition $P$, assume $\neg P$ and combine that assumption with definitions, axioms, or established results to derive an impossibility. Only when the contradiction genuinely depends on $\neg P$ does it show that the assumption is untenable and hence that $P$ holds.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 要证明命题 $P$，先假设 $\neg P$，再与定义、公理或已证结果一起推出某个不可能同时成立的结论。只有当矛盾确实用到了 $\neg P$，才说明这个反设不能成立，从而得到 $P$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here we work in ordinary classical logic with the original domain and background assumptions fixed. The symbol $\neg P$ means “$P$ is false.” A contradiction may be a statement $R$ together with its negation $\neg R$, or a known impossibility such as $0=1$. The symbol $\bot$ is a short name for such an impossibility.

If the background assumptions and $\neg P$ lead by [[数学证明的有效推理|sound inference]] to $\bot$, they cannot all hold together. Under the background assumptions, $\neg P$ is therefore excluded; classical logic gives $P$. This is a test of an assumption, not permission to make an invalid deduction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里使用通常的经典逻辑，并保持原论域与背景假设不变。$\neg P$ 表示“$P$ 为假”。矛盾可以是某陈述 $R$ 与其否定 $\neg R$ 同时成立，也可以是 $0=1$ 这样的已知不可能事实；符号 $\bot$ 是这类不可能情形的简记。

若背景假设连同 $\neg P$ 通过[[数学证明的有效推理|可靠推理]]得到 $\bot$，它们就不能同时成立。因此，在背景假设下可以排除 $\neg P$，并由经典逻辑得到 $P$。这是检验假设的方法，不是允许错误推理的方法。

## Negate the entire target
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先确认否定的究竟是什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an [[蕴含真值条件|implication]] $A\Rightarrow B$, the target is false precisely when $A$ is true and $B$ is false. A contradiction proof therefore assumes **both $A$ and $\neg B$**, not just $\neg A$. For a universal statement “every object in $D$ has property $Q$,” the [[量词否定规则|negation]] is “there exists an object in the same $D$ for which $Q$ fails.” It is not “every object fails.”

For example, to refute the possibility of an integer whose square is even but which is not even, keep the integer domain, assume those two properties of a hypothetical integer, and derive their incompatibility. The domain does not change when the target is negated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若目标是[[蕴含真值条件|蕴含]] $A\Rightarrow B$，它为假恰好意味着 $A$ 真而 $B$ 假，所以反证时应**同时假设 $A$ 与 $\neg B$**，不是只假设 $\neg A$。若目标是“$D$ 中每个对象都满足性质 $Q$”，其[[量词否定规则|否定]]是“同一个 $D$ 中存在不满足 $Q$ 的对象”，不是“每个对象都不满足”。

例如，要排除“存在一个平方为偶数、自身却不是偶数的整数”，应保持整数论域，给假想整数加上这两个条件，再推出二者不相容。否定目标不会改变论域。

## Why the square root of two is irrational
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把最简分数中的冲突完整写出*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove that $\sqrt 2$ is irrational, assume it can be written as a fraction $n/d$ in lowest terms. The argument forces both $n$ and $d$ to be even, contradicting the lowest-terms condition. Because the contradiction arises from the representation introduced by the assumption, it defeats that assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
证明 $\sqrt 2$ 无理时，反设它可写成最简分数 $n/d$。推理会迫使 $n$ 与 $d$ 都是偶数，这与“最简”冲突。冲突来自反设所引入的最简分数，因此能否定反设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A real number is **rational** if it equals $n/d$ for integers $n,d$ with $d\ne0$; it is **irrational** if no such representation exists. The number $\sqrt2$ denotes the positive real number whose square is $2$. Suppose, for contradiction, that it is rational. We may choose $d>0$ and a fraction in lowest terms: $n$ and $d$ have no common positive divisor greater than $1$.

Why is the lowest-terms choice allowed? Starting from any integer fraction with nonzero denominator, change both signs if necessary to make the denominator positive. Divide numerator and denominator by their [[最大公因数|greatest common divisor]] $g$. If the resulting integers still had a common divisor $h>1$, then $gh>g$ would have divided the original pair, contradicting the choice of $g$. Thus the simplification is justified by an established property, not by the result we are proving.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
实数若能写成整数之比 $n/d$，其中 $d\ne0$，就称为**有理数**；不存在这种表示就称为**无理数**。$\sqrt2$ 表示平方为 $2$ 的正实数。现在反设它是有理数，可以取 $d>0$，并把分数化为最简：$n,d$ 没有大于 $1$ 的正公因子。

为什么允许选最简表示？从任意分母非零的整数分数出发，必要时同时改变分子分母的符号，使分母为正；再把二者同时除以其[[最大公因数]] $g$。若所得两个整数仍有公因子 $h>1$，则 $gh>g$ 会成为原分子分母的公因子，违背 $g$ 最大。因此约分有依据，并未预设待证结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Squaring $\sqrt2=n/d$ and multiplying by $d^2$ gives $n^2=2d^2$, so $n^2$ is even. The [[逆否证明|integer-parity argument]] proves that an integer with even square is even: an odd integer $2k+1$ has odd square $2(2k^2+2k)+1$. Hence write $n=2k$ for an integer $k$. Substitution gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
将 $\sqrt2=n/d$ 平方，再乘以 $d^2$，得到 $n^2=2d^2$，所以 $n^2$ 为偶数。[[逆否证明|整数奇偶论证]]已经证明平方为偶数的整数自身也为偶数：奇整数 $2k+1$ 的平方是奇数 $2(2k^2+2k)+1$。于是存在整数 $k$ 使 $n=2k$。代回得到

$$4k^2=2d^2,\qquad d^2=2k^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now $d^2$ is even, so the same parity result makes $d$ even. Both $n$ and $d$ therefore have the common divisor $2$. But the chosen fraction had no common divisor greater than $1$. These are the two incompatible claims. The assumed rational representation is impossible, so $\sqrt2$ is irrational.

The nonzero denominator matters: it licenses the fraction and multiplication by $d^2$. The parity lemma matters too; one cannot replace it with the false claim that every divisor of $n^2$ divides $n$. For instance, $4$ divides $2^2$, but $4$ does not divide $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
现在 $d^2$ 为偶数，由同一个奇偶结论得到 $d$ 也为偶数。于是 $n,d$ 都有公因子 $2$，但所选最简分数没有大于 $1$ 的公因子；这就是两个不能同时成立的陈述。因此，反设的有理数表示不可能存在，$\sqrt2$ 是无理数。

分母非零保证分数有意义，乘以 $d^2$ 合法。奇偶引理也不可省略，更不能把它偷换成“$n^2$ 的每个因子都整除 $n$”；例如 $4$ 整除 $2^2$，却不整除 $2$。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- An erroneous equation unrelated to the assumption proves nothing. The argument must show how the contradiction follows from the assumption together with sound facts.
- A contrapositive proof of $P\Rightarrow Q$ directly proves the equivalent implication $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$. It need not derive a contradiction and should not be conflated with contradiction proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 单独写出一个与反设无关的错误等式，不会证明目标；必须标明矛盾怎样由反设与可靠事实共同产生。
- 对蕴含 $P\Rightarrow Q$ 做逆否证明，是直接证明等价命题 $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$；它不要求导出矛盾，因此不要与反证法混写。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The dependency warning is understood with reliable, consistent background facts. If those facts alone already produce a contradiction, the problem is in the background or the derivation; adding an unrelated temporary assumption has not repaired it. This is not a requirement that every line visibly mention $\neg P$. What must be checked is the full chain of justifications.

For an implication, [[逆否证明|contraposition]] starts from $\neg B$ and derives $\neg A$, while contradiction starts from $A$ and $\neg B$ and seeks an impossibility. These methods can be nested. Conversely, an unsuccessful search for a contradiction does not show that the original statement is false or that the temporary assumption is true.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
关于依赖关系的提醒，以可靠、一致的背景事实为前提。如果这些事实本身已经推出矛盾，问题出在背景或推导中；另加一个无关反设并不能修好证明。这也不是要求每一行都在字面上出现 $\neg P$，而是要求检查整条推理链的依据。

对蕴含而言，[[逆否证明]]从 $\neg B$ 出发推出 $\neg A$；反证则同时假设 $A$ 与 $\neg B$，寻找不可能情形。两种方法可以嵌套。反过来，暂时没找到矛盾，并不能说明原命题为假，也不能说明临时反设为真。

> [!question]- Audit the dependency · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An argument assumes that $P$ is false and then derives $0=1$ without ever using that assumption. Has it proved $P$?
>
> **Answer:** No. Because the contradiction does not depend on $\neg P$, it only exposes an error in the later reasoning or in some other premise; it does not rule out $\neg P$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 某论证反设 $P$ 为假，随后在完全没有使用这个反设的情况下推出 $0=1$。它是否已经证明了 $P$？
>
> **答案：** 没有。这个矛盾没有依赖 $\neg P$，只说明后续推理或其他前提有问题；它不能排除 $\neg P$。
