# 最大回撤是指定净值路径从此前峰值到随后谷值的最大跌幅
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Maximum drawdown is the largest decline from an earlier peak to a subsequent trough on a specified wealth path.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最大回撤在给定观察区间内，寻找账户净值相对其此前最高值的最大下降。本卡采用“峰值为分母、损失记正”的百分比口径。先固定币种、费用、分配处理和观察频率；令 $V_0,\ldots,V_T>0$ 为无外部入金或提款的净值路径，定义：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maximum drawdown finds the greatest fall in wealth relative to a preceding high within a stated observation interval. This card reports a positive percentage loss with the peak as denominator. Fix currency, costs, distributions and sampling frequency. For a positive wealth path $V_0,\ldots,V_T$ without external deposits or withdrawals, define:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$H_t=\max_{0\le s\le t}V_s,\qquad
D_t=1-\frac{V_t}{H_t},\qquad
MDD_{0:T}=\max_{0\le t\le T}D_t.$$

$H_t$ 只能取当时及以前的高点，初始值 $V_0$ 也要纳入。最大回撤的起点是对应峰值，终点是随后谷值；若全程不下降，结果为零。若另用金额损失 $H_t-V_t$、对数差或带负号的曲线，必须另报单位与符号，不能直接与这里的百分比混排。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$H_t$ includes only current and earlier values, including $V_0$. The drawdown interval begins at the relevant peak and ends at a later trough; a nondecreasing path has zero drawdown. Amount losses $H_t-V_t$, log differences and negatively signed curves require their own units and signs and cannot be ranked interchangeably with this percentage convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如净值依次为 $100,120,90,130,104$，对应回撤为 $0,0,25\%,0,20\%$，所以最大回撤是从 120 到 90 的 25%，尽管期末仍比期初高 4%。用全样本最高 130 与最低 90 算出的约 30.77% 不合法，因为 90 出现在 130 之前。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For wealth $100,120,90,130,104$, drawdowns are $0,0,25\%,0,20\%$. Maximum drawdown is therefore 25%, from 120 to 90, although terminal wealth exceeds initial wealth by 4%. Combining the overall maximum of 130 with the minimum of 90 gives about 30.77% but violates time order: 90 occurs first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

历史最大回撤是这条已观察路径的统计量，不是未来亏损上限，也不是 [[止损单]]能保证的损失界。它不完整描述回撤持续多久或多频繁。只观察收盘净值可能漏掉盘中深谷；有外部资金流时应先消除其对绩效路径的机械影响。零或负权益的破产／杠杆情形超出本卡正净值口径，应单独报告。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Historical MDD describes the observed path; it is neither a future loss ceiling nor a loss bound guaranteed by a [[止损单|stop order]]. It does not fully describe drawdown duration or frequency. Closing observations can miss intraday troughs, and external cash flows require adjustment before interpreting performance. Insolvency or leveraged paths with zero or negative equity fall outside this card's positive-wealth convention and require separate reporting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
