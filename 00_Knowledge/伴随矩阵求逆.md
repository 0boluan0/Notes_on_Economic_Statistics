# The adjugate inverse formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*伴随矩阵求逆*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[逆矩阵|inverse matrix]] undoes a matrix's action. The **adjugate inverse formula** constructs that inverse from cofactors: form the [[伴随矩阵|adjugate]], then divide every entry by the original matrix's [[行列式|determinant]]. The division is available only when that determinant is nonzero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[逆矩阵]]用来撤销一个矩阵的作用。**伴随矩阵求逆公式**从代数余子式构造逆：先形成[[伴随矩阵]]，再把其中每个元素都除以原矩阵的[[行列式]]。只有行列式非零时，这一步除法才有意义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the real matrix $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&3\end{pmatrix}$. Its determinant is $2\cdot3-1\cdot1=5$. Its [[余子式与代数余子式|cofactor]] matrix is $C=\begin{pmatrix}3&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}$, and its adjugate is the ordinary [[矩阵转置|transpose]] $C^T$. Here $C^T=C$ because this particular cofactor matrix is symmetric. Multiplication gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从实矩阵 $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&3\end{pmatrix}$ 开始。行列式为 $2\cdot3-1\cdot1=5$；[[余子式与代数余子式|代数余子式]]矩阵为 $C=\begin{pmatrix}3&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}$，伴随矩阵是它的普通[[矩阵转置|转置]] $C^T$。这里恰好 $C^T=C$，因为这张代数余子式矩阵对称。相乘得到

$$
AC^T=\begin{pmatrix}6-1&-2+2\\3-3&-1+6\end{pmatrix}=5I_2,
\qquad
C^TA=\begin{pmatrix}6-1&3-3\\-2+2&-1+6\end{pmatrix}=5I_2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I_2$ leaves every two-coordinate vector unchanged. Dividing $C^T$ by $5$ therefore removes the extra factor $5$ in both multiplication orders:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[单位矩阵]] $I_2$ 使每个二维向量保持原样。因此，把 $C^T$ 除以 $5$，就能在两个相乘次序中都消去多出的因子 $5$：

$$A^{-1}=\frac15\begin{pmatrix}3&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why division gives the inverse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么除以行列式就得到逆*

> [!important] Scale the two-sided identity · 缩放双侧恒等式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$, where $n\ge1$ is an integer and $\mathbb F$ is a field, for example the real or complex numbers. If $\det A\ne0$, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$，其中整数 $n\ge1$，$\mathbb F$ 是一个域，例如实数域或复数域。若 $\det A\ne0$，则
>
> $$A^{-1}=\frac{1}{\det A}\operatorname{adj}(A).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The scalar $1/\det A$ multiplies every entry of the adjugate; this is not entrywise reciprocation of $A$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 标量 $1/\det A$ 乘伴随矩阵中的每个元素；这不是把 $A$ 的各元素分别取倒数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[伴随矩阵恒等式|adjugate identity]], proved using cofactor expansion without assuming an inverse exists, says
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[伴随矩阵恒等式]]由余子式展开证明，不预先假设逆矩阵存在；它给出

$$A\operatorname{adj}(A)=\operatorname{adj}(A)A=(\det A)I_n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every nonzero scalar in a field has a multiplicative inverse. Set $B=(\det A)^{-1}\operatorname{adj}(A)$. Scaling the two products yields $AB=BA=I_n$. Thus $B$ satisfies the definition of the inverse in both orders, proving existence directly. The [[逆矩阵唯一性|uniqueness of an inverse]] then identifies it as $A^{-1}$. The proof never cancels an arbitrary matrix factor or presumes that arbitrary matrices commute.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

域中每个非零标量都有乘法逆。令 $B=(\det A)^{-1}\operatorname{adj}(A)$，对上述两个乘积缩放，便有 $AB=BA=I_n$。因此 $B$ 在两个次序都满足逆矩阵定义，直接证明了逆的存在；再由[[逆矩阵唯一性]]，它就是 $A^{-1}$。证明没有随意约去矩阵因子，也没有假设任意矩阵都可交换。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a general two-dimensional matrix, the transposition is visible:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一般二阶矩阵，转置这一步可以明确看出来：

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix},\qquad
C=\begin{pmatrix}d&-c\\-b&a\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A^{-1}=\frac{1}{ad-bc}\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}d&-b\\-c&a\end{pmatrix}}_{C^T},
\quad ad-bc\ne0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cofactor $C_{ij}$ moves to position $(j,i)$ before division. This remains ordinary transpose over the complex numbers: no complex conjugation is inserted. At $n=1$, the [[余子式与代数余子式#一阶矩阵与空行列式|empty-determinant convention]] gives $\operatorname{adj}([a])=[1]$, so the formula reduces to $[a]^{-1}=[1/a]$ for $a\ne0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

代数余子式 $C_{ij}$ 先移到 $(j,i)$ 位置，再做除法。即使在复数域上，这里仍是普通转置，不加入复共轭。$n=1$ 时，[[余子式与代数余子式#一阶矩阵与空行列式|空行列式约定]]给出 $\operatorname{adj}([a])=[1]$，所以公式退化为 $a\ne0$ 时的 $[a]^{-1}=[1/a]$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the formula does and does not provide
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 适用边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\det A=0$, the adjugate still exists and the identity still holds, but its right side is the zero matrix. Division by the determinant is unavailable. This is exactly the singular case in the [[可逆性与非零行列式|determinant criterion for invertibility]]; having a nonzero adjugate does not supply an inverse. For example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\det A=0$，伴随矩阵仍然存在，恒等式仍成立，但右侧变成零矩阵，不能再除以行列式。这正是[[可逆性与非零行列式|行列式可逆性判据]]中的奇异情形；伴随矩阵非零也不意味着有逆。例如，

$$
S=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\operatorname{adj}(S)=\begin{pmatrix}4&-2\\-2&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
S\operatorname{adj}(S)=\operatorname{adj}(S)S=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For small matrices and symbolic arguments, the formula shows explicitly how inverse entries depend on the entries of $A$. It also leads to [[Cramer 法则|Cramer's rule]] for a single solution component. For large dense numerical systems, computing every cofactor repeats work. Solvers normally use factorizations such as [[消元与LU分解|LU]] or [[QR分解|QR]], and solving $Ax=b$ need not form the entire inverse. LAPACK's real square-system solver `DGESV`, for example, factors the matrix and solves with those factors. An exact algebraic formula alone is not a numerical error guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对小矩阵和符号推导，这个公式明确显示逆矩阵元素如何依赖 $A$ 的元素，也导向逐个求解分量的 [[Cramer 法则]]。大型稠密数值系统中，计算全部代数余子式会重复工作，通常使用[[消元与LU分解|LU]]或[[QR分解|QR]]等分解；只解 $Ax=b$ 不必先形成整个逆矩阵。例如 LAPACK 的实方阵求解程序 `DGESV` 先分解矩阵，再用分解结果求解。精确代数公式本身并不提供数值误差保证。

> [!question]- Why does the adjugate not rescue a singular matrix? · 为什么奇异矩阵的伴随矩阵不能救回求逆？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The adjugate identity gives zero when $\det A=0$, whereas an inverse must give $I_n$. The rescaling step that would turn the determinant into $1$ requires division by a nonzero scalar.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\det A=0$ 时伴随恒等式给出零矩阵，逆却必须给出 $I_n$；要把行列式缩放成 $1$，必须能除以这个非零标量。
