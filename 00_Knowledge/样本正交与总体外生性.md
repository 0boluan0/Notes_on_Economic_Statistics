# Sample orthogonality and population exogeneity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*样本正交与总体外生性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After an OLS regression with an intercept, software can report zero sample correlation between an included predictor and the fitted residuals. This is an outcome of the fitting rule. It does not show that unobserved influences were unrelated to that predictor before fitting. The distinction is between a relation constructed in one sample and an assumption about how data are generated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

含截距的 OLS 拟合之后，软件可能显示已纳入的解释变量与拟合残差的样本相关为零。这来自拟合规则，并不说明拟合前的未观测影响因素就与该解释变量无关。这里要区分的是：在一个样本中构造出来的关系，与关于数据怎样产生的假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[回归残差|residual]] is the observed outcome minus its fitted value. Let $y$ collect $n$ outcomes, $X$ be the $n\times p$ design matrix of included features, and $\hat\beta$ an unconstrained [[普通最小二乘|OLS]] solution. The [[OLS正规方程|normal equations]] give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[回归残差]]是观察值减拟合值。令 $y$ 收集 $n$ 个结果，$X$ 是包含所选特征的 $n\times p$ 设计矩阵，$\hat\beta$ 是无约束[[普通最小二乘|OLS]]解。[[OLS正规方程]]给出

$$\hat u=y-X\hat\beta,\qquad X'\hat u=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The prime means [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Each entry of $X'\hat u$ is an [[向量点积|inner product]]: multiply a design column by the residuals row by row and sum. OLS makes all these sums zero. This holds for every OLS minimiser, even if redundant columns make the coefficient vector nonunique; no probability model is needed for the identity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

撇号表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。$X'\hat u$ 的每个分量都是一个[[向量点积|内积]]：把某个设计列与残差逐行相乘后求和。OLS 让这些和全部为零。即使冗余列导致系数不唯一，每个 OLS 最小化解仍满足该式；这个恒等式不需要概率模型。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The population assumption has a different object
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用条件与含义边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now suppose a specified population model is $y=X\beta+u$, where $\beta$ is the target coefficient vector and $u$ its model error. One useful **exogeneity condition** is zero conditional mean, $E[u\mid X]=0$: after the complete design is given, each integrable error still has average zero across repeated samples. [[条件期望|Conditional expectation]] formalises that average, and integrability means $E|u_i|<\infty$. This condition concerns $u$, not the fitted $\hat u$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在考虑指定的总体模型 $y=X\beta+u$，其中 $\beta$ 是目标系数向量，$u$ 是对应的模型误差。一种有用的**外生性条件**是零条件均值 $E[u\mid X]=0$：给定完整设计之后，在重复抽样中，每个可积误差的平均仍为零。[[条件期望]]刻画这种平均，可积指 $E|u_i|<\infty$。条件约束的是 $u$，不是拟合残差 $\hat u$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full column rank]] (no exact redundancy among design columns) and the relevant conditional expectations well defined, zero conditional mean yields [[零条件均值无偏性|conditional unbiasedness of OLS]]. But $X'\hat u=0$ holds whether that assumption is true or false. For dependent observations, conditioning on one row’s predictors is not automatically the same as conditioning on the complete design. Exogeneity must be stated for the target and sampling setting actually used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在设计[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]（设计列之间没有精确冗余）、相关条件期望有定义时，零条件均值可推出 [[零条件均值无偏性|OLS 条件无偏]]。但 $X'\hat u=0$ 不论这项假设真假都成立。对相互依赖的观测，只给定某一行的解释变量，并不自动等同于给定完整设计。必须针对实际目标和抽样情境说明外生性条件。

> [!note] Orthogonality is not always correlation · 正交不总等于相关为零
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An included intercept gives $\sum_i\hat u_i=0$. Together with $\sum_i x_{ij}\hat u_i=0$, it makes the sample covariance zero for each included column $j$. The sample correlation is zero only when that column and the residuals both have positive sample variance. Correlation is undefined for a constant column or an all-zero residual vector. Without an intercept, uncentred orthogonality alone need not give zero centred covariance. Weighted or constrained fits have their own fitting conditions; the identity above describes unconstrained ordinary least squares.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 含截距时有 $\sum_i\hat u_i=0$，加上 $\sum_i x_{ij}\hat u_i=0$，使每个纳入列 $j$ 与残差的样本协方差为零。只有该列和残差的样本方差均为正时，样本相关才是零；常数列或全零残差的相关没有定义。没有截距时，未中心化的正交本身不必给出零中心化协方差。加权或受约束拟合有各自的条件；上式针对无约束普通最小二乘。

## A fitted slope can absorb a systematic model error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小反例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Construct an observation by drawing a predictor $z$ and a noise $\varepsilon$ independently, each taking $-1$ and $1$ with probability $1/2$. Specify the target model as $Y=2z+u$, with $u=z+\varepsilon$. Then $E[u\mid z]=z$, so its zero-conditional-mean assumption fails. The observed outcome is $Y=3z+\varepsilon$: a regression can absorb the extra $z$ into its fitted slope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造一个观测：解释变量 $z$ 和噪声 $\varepsilon$ 相互独立，各以 $1/2$ 概率取 $-1$、$1$。指定目标模型为 $Y=2z+u$，其中 $u=z+\varepsilon$。此时 $E[u\mid z]=z$，所以该模型的零条件均值条件失败。观察结果实际为 $Y=3z+\varepsilon$，回归可以把额外的 $z$ 吸收到拟合斜率中。

> [!example] All four possibilities, one misleading reassurance · 四种组合与一个错误的安心理由
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider a four-row sample containing each pair once. Regress $y$ on an intercept and $z$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑每种组合恰出现一次的四行样本，用截距和 $z$ 回归 $y$。
>
> | $z$ | $\varepsilon$ | $u=z+\varepsilon$ | $y=3z+\varepsilon$ | OLS residual · OLS 残差 |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | $-1$ | $-1$ | $-2$ | $-4$ | $-1$ |
> | $-1$ | $1$ | $0$ | $-2$ | $1$ |
> | $1$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $2$ | $-1$ |
> | $1$ | $1$ | $2$ | $4$ | $1$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> OLS gives intercept $0$ and slope $3$, with residuals $(-1,1,-1,1)$. Their sum and their inner product with $z$ are both zero. Nevertheless, the target slope in the specified model was $2$, and the model errors have $z'u=4$, not zero. Residual orthogonality has not recovered the target slope or removed its endogeneity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> OLS 得到截距 $0$、斜率 $3$，残差为 $(-1,1,-1,1)$。残差之和为零，与 $z$ 的内积也为零。但指定模型的目标斜率是 $2$，模型误差满足 $z'u=4$ 而不是零。残差正交没有找回目标斜率，也没有消除该模型的内生性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This example does not say that the fitted slope $3$ is meaningless. It is the slope of the population conditional mean $E[Y\mid z]=3z$ and of its [[条件均值与线性投影|best linear predictor]]. Redefining the target as that descriptive projection changes the question. In general, a population projection error is orthogonal to the included regressors by construction, but that weaker condition need not give zero conditional mean and does not itself identify a causal effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子不是说拟合斜率 $3$ 没有意义。它正是总体条件均值 $E[Y\mid z]=3z$ 及其[[条件均值与线性投影|最佳线性预测]]的斜率。把目标改为该描述性投影，是换了问题。一般而言，总体投影误差按构造与纳入的回归量正交，但这个较弱条件不必推出零条件均值，也不自行识别因果效应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same distinction matters in a wage regression: if unobserved ability is associated with education and also affects wages, fitting OLS still forces the education column and residuals to be orthogonal. It does not remove the omitted influence. [[残差图边界|Residual diagnostics]] may reveal remaining shape, variance or dependence patterns; they cannot certify every population assumption or the [[条件均值因果边界|causal interpretation]] of a coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

工资回归也有同样区别：若未观测能力与教育相关，又影响工资，拟合 OLS 后，教育列与残差仍被强制正交，却没有消除遗漏因素的影响。[[残差图边界|残差诊断]]可以揭示留下的形状、方差或依赖模式，却不能验证全部总体假设，也不能证明系数的[[条件均值因果边界|因果解释]]。
