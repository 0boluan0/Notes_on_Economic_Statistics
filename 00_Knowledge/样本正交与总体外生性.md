# 样本残差正交不能证明总体外生性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sample residual orthogonality does not prove population exogeneity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> $X'\hat u=0$ 是 OLS 对当前样本构造出来的代数恒等式；$E(u\mid X)=0$ 是关于不可观测总体误差和数据生成过程的假设。前者即使在后者完全失败时也会成立，因此不能用残差与 $X$ 的样本正交来验证外生性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $X'\hat u=0$ is an algebraic identity that OLS constructs in the current sample. $E(u\mid X)=0$ is an assumption about the unobserved population error and the data-generating process. The first holds even when the second fails completely, so sample orthogonality of residuals and $X$ cannot verify exogeneity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用条件与含义边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Use condition and meaning boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

残差 $\hat u=y-X\hat\beta$ 可由样本计算；总体误差 $u=Y-X\beta$ 依赖未知参数和模型，通常不可观测。残差诊断可以发现某些函数形式、方差或相关结构的失败迹象，却不能证明所有遗漏因素都与回归变量无关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The residual $\hat u=y-X\hat\beta$ is computable from the sample. The population error $u=Y-X\beta$ depends on unknown parameters and the model and is ordinarily unobserved. Residual diagnostics can reveal signs of functional-form, variance, or dependence failures, but they cannot prove that every omitted factor is unrelated to the regressors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小反例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal counterexample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在工资对教育的回归中，未观测能力若同时影响教育和工资，结构误差就与教育相关。OLS 拟合后仍会机械地产生“教育列与样本残差内积为零”，但这并没有消除能力造成的内生性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a wage regression on education, unobserved ability may affect both education and wages, making the structural error related to education. OLS will still mechanically produce a zero inner product between the education column and the sample residuals; that identity does not remove ability-driven endogeneity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 为什么含截距回归的输出里 `corr(X, residual)=0` 不能当作外生性检验通过？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can `corr(X, residual)=0` in a regression with an intercept not be treated as a successful exogeneity test?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 因为这正是 OLS 一阶条件强制出来的样本关系，而外生性约束的是不可观测总体误差。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Because the OLS first-order conditions force that sample relationship, whereas exogeneity constrains the unobserved population error.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
