# 数字签名用私钥生成，并由对应公钥验证签名与消息是否匹配
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A digital signature is generated with a private key and verified against the message with the corresponding public key*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 数字签名方案包含密钥生成、签名和验证。签名者用私钥对消息生成签名；验证者用对应公钥、消息与签名得到“有效”或“无效”。在算法假设成立且私钥未泄露时，有效结果为“消息签名后未被修改，并由对应私钥持有者生成”提供证据。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A digital-signature scheme contains key generation, signing, and verification. The signer uses a private key to produce a signature on a message; a verifier supplies the corresponding public key, message, and signature and obtains a valid or invalid result. Under the algorithm's assumptions and an uncompromised private key, a valid result is evidence that the message has not changed since signing and that the corresponding private-key holder generated the signature.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公钥可以分发给不持有私钥的验证者，这使签名不同于共享密钥 MAC；但数学验证只接到输入的那把公钥。要把它解释成“某个人或机构签署”，还必须另外证明该公钥属于那个身份，见 [[数字签名与身份绑定]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The public key can be distributed to verifiers who do not possess the private key, which distinguishes signatures from shared-key MACs. Mathematical verification, however, reaches only the supplied public key. Interpreting it as “this person or organisation signed” additionally requires a trusted identity binding; see [[数字签名与身份绑定|digital signatures and identity binding]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

签名通常不隐藏消息；任何拿到消息的人仍可读它。若还需要机密性，应由加密或协议的其他部分提供，而不能从“signature valid”推出来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A signature normally does not hide the message; anyone who receives the message can still read it. Confidentiality, when required, must be supplied by encryption or another part of the protocol and cannot be inferred from “signature valid.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么验证签名只需要公钥，而生成新签名必须持有私钥？
>
> **答案：** 签名方案故意把生成能力留给私钥持有者，同时让公钥持有者检查签名与消息是否匹配；公开验证能力不应带来伪造能力。

## 来源与核验

- [NIST FIPS 186-5, *Digital Signature Standard*](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/186-5/final)：核对数字签名算法、私钥生成、公钥验证，以及签名用于检测未授权修改并认证签名者。
- [NIST SP 800-89, *Recommendation for Obtaining Assurances for Digital Signature Applications*](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/89/final)：交叉核对公钥有效性、私钥持有和密钥对所有者身份是验证签名时彼此不同的保证。
