# 比较优势是相对于另一生产者以更低机会成本生产某种产品的能力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Comparative advantage is the ability to produce a good at a lower opportunity cost than another producer.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较优势问的是：**为了多生产一单位这个产品，我放弃的其他产品是否比对方少？** 它比较取舍，不比较谁在所有工作上都更快。绝对优势看同样投入能生产多少；即使一个国家两种产品都更高效，另一个国家仍可能在其中一种上有比较优势。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Comparative advantage compares what producers forgo to make one more unit, not who is faster at every task. Absolute advantage concerns output from the same inputs. A country can be less productive in both goods yet have comparative advantage in one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设只有劳动、产品同质、单位劳动生产率固定。A 每小时能生产 10 单位布或 5 单位酒，B 能生产 6 单位布或 4 单位酒。生产 1 酒需要的劳动，原本能分别生产：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume homogeneous goods, labour as the only input, and constant labour productivity. A produces ten units of cloth or five units of wine per hour; B produces six or four. The labour needed for one wine could instead produce:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
OC_A(1\text{ 酒})=\frac{10}{5}=2\text{ 布},\qquad
OC_B(1\text{ 酒})=\frac{6}{4}=1.5\text{ 布}.
$$

B 在酒上有比较优势；取倒数，A 的 1 布只需放弃 0.5 酒，B 要放弃 $2/3$ 酒，所以 A 在布上有比较优势。若无运输成本且能按 1 酒换 1.75 布交易，A 用布换酒比自己酿酒省资源，B 用酒换布也比自己织布省资源。这个交换比率是用来演示互利区间的假设，不是仅凭生产率就能算出的市场均衡价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
B has comparative advantage in wine. Taking reciprocals, one cloth costs A half a wine and B two-thirds, so A has comparative advantage in cloth. With costless trade at an assumed rate of one wine for 1.75 cloth, both save resources by importing the good they produce at higher opportunity cost. This illustrative rate lies within the mutually beneficial interval; productivity alone does not determine the equilibrium terms of trade.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是给定技术下的比较。技术、技能和组织经验改变后，机会成本也会改变，见 [[学习效应]]。至于贸易收益怎样分给不同人，要另外检查 [[贸易收益与分配]]；比较优势本身没有回答这两个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The comparison holds technology fixed. Technology, skills and organisational experience can change opportunity costs; see [[学习效应|learning effects]]. Distribution is a separate question addressed in [[贸易收益与分配|Trade gains and distribution]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
