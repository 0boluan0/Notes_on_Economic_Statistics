# 平稳性、遍历性与谱
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stationarity, ergodicity, and spectra*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 描述一个随机对象如何随时间演化，并区分“跨许多重复世界的分布”与“单条长时间路径”的性质。
> **具体锚点：** 每天气温是一条时间路径；平稳性问整体统计规律是否随日历平移不变，遍历性问一条足够长的路径能否代表总体平均。
> **核心难点：** 严平稳、宽平稳和遍历性互不等同；平稳不表示样本路径恒定。
> **为什么重要：** 时间序列估计常只有一条路径，能否用时间平均推断总体取决于这些条件。
> **继续：** 计数事件见 [[泊松过程与更新过程]]；状态跳转见 [[离散时间马尔可夫链]] 与 [[连续时间马尔可夫链]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It describes the time evolution of a random object and separates properties of an ensemble of possible worlds from properties of one long observed path.
> **Concrete anchor:** Daily temperature forms one time path. Stationarity asks whether its overall statistical law is unchanged by shifting the calendar; ergodicity asks whether one sufficiently long path can represent an ensemble average.
> **Central difficulty:** Strict stationarity, weak stationarity, and ergodicity are different properties. Stationarity does not mean that a sample path is constant.
> **Why it matters:** Time-series analysis often observes only one path, and using its time averages to infer population quantities depends on these conditions.
> **Continue with:** For arrival counts see [[泊松过程|Poisson processes]] and [[更新过程|renewal processes]]. For state transitions see [[离散时间马尔可夫链|discrete-time Markov chains]] and [[连续时间马尔可夫链|continuous-time Markov chains]].

## 严平稳与宽平稳
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strict and weak stationarity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

严平稳要求任意有限时点联合分布在共同平移后不变。宽平稳只要求均值常数、协方差仅依赖时差且二阶矩有限。严平稳在二阶矩存在时推出宽平稳；宽平稳一般不推出严平稳，Gaussian 过程是重要例外。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strict stationarity requires every finite-dimensional distribution to remain unchanged after a common time shift. Weak stationarity requires only a constant mean, finite second moments, and covariance depending on time lag alone. Strict stationarity implies weak stationarity when second moments exist; the converse generally fails, with Gaussian processes providing an important exception.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断宽平稳时必须同时检查均值和协方差。仅看到序列围绕某个水平波动不够；趋势、季节变化、结构突变或随时间变化的方差都破坏相应条件。对变换后的过程成立也不等于对原序列成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both the mean and covariance conditions must be checked for weak stationarity. Merely seeing a series fluctuate around a level is insufficient. Trends, changing seasonality, structural breaks, or time-varying variance violate the relevant conditions. Stationarity after transformation does not imply stationarity of the original series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 自协方差、相关与谱
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Autocovariance, correlation, and spectra*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

宽平稳过程的自协方差 $\gamma(h)$ 描述相隔 h 的线性依赖，$\rho(h)=\gamma(h)/\gamma(0)$ 无量纲。功率谱密度在频域分解方差，和自协方差通过 Fourier 变换对应；周期峰值不自动证明确定性周期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a weakly stationary process, the autocovariance $\gamma(h)$ describes linear dependence at lag $h$, while the dimensionless autocorrelation is $\rho(h)=\gamma(h)/\gamma(0)$. The power spectral density decomposes variance by frequency and is linked to autocovariance through a Fourier transform. A spectral peak does not by itself prove a deterministic cycle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于协方差矩阵必须半正定，并非任意看似合理的函数都能成为自协方差。谱密度必须非负；这一事实常比逐个检验滞后更方便地验证模型是否合法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because every covariance matrix must be positive semidefinite, not every plausible-looking function can be an autocovariance function. A spectral density must be non-negative, which can be a more convenient way to verify that a proposed model is valid than checking lags one by one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 遍历性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Ergodicity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均值遍历要求时间平均收敛到总体均值。平稳过程仍可能缺乏遍历性，例如每条路径先随机抽一个常数后永远保持该常数：总体平稳，但单条路径的时间平均只揭示本路径常数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mean ergodicity requires the time average to converge to the ensemble mean. A stationary process need not be ergodic. For example, if each path first draws a random constant and then remains at that constant forever, the ensemble is stationary, but the time average of one path reveals only that path's constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

遍历性不是“样本很长”的同义词，而是分布结构。更长的非遍历路径仍只揭示所在的潜在成分。实践中通常依赖混合、相关衰减或相应遍历定理，而不能从一条图形直接证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ergodicity is not synonymous with “a long sample”; it is a property of the distribution. A longer non-ergodic path still reveals only its latent component. Applications usually rely on mixing, decay of dependence, or an appropriate ergodic theorem rather than proving ergodicity from a single plot.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Gaussian 与二阶结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Gaussian processes and second-order structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Gaussian 过程的任意有限维向量联合正态，因此均值与协方差完全决定其分布。非 Gaussian 过程即使均值和协方差相同，也可能有不同尾部和高阶依赖。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every finite-dimensional vector of a Gaussian process is jointly normal, so its mean and covariance determine the full distribution. Non-Gaussian processes can share the same mean and covariance while having different tails and higher-order dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一例外解释了为什么二阶方法在 Gaussian 模型中格外强，但也提醒我们：对金融收益、计数或极端风险，仅匹配 ACF 不能保证模型复制偏度、厚尾或尾部依赖。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This exception explains the particular power of second-order methods under Gaussian models. It also warns that matching an ACF for financial returns, counts, or extremes does not guarantee reproduction of skewness, heavy tails, or tail dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：平稳但不遍历
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: stationary but non-ergodic*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先抽 $Z$，以相同概率取 1 或 2，再令所有 t 上 $X_t=Z$。任意共同平移不改变联合分布，所以过程严格平稳；总体均值为 1.5。但任一实际路径的时间平均永远是 1 或 2，不会趋于 1.5，因此不是均值遍历。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First draw $Z$, taking values 1 and 2 with equal probability, then set $X_t=Z$ for every $t$. A common time shift changes no joint distribution, so the process is strictly stationary and its ensemble mean is 1.5. Yet the time average of any realised path is always either 1 or 2 and never approaches 1.5, so the process is not mean ergodic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子也说明同一路径内的长期数据量不能替代独立重复世界；是否可以替代，正是遍历性要回答的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example also shows that more observations along a single path cannot automatically replace independent replications across possible worlds. Whether such a replacement is valid is precisely the question answered by ergodicity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 宽平稳是否表示每条样本路径看起来平坦？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does weak stationarity mean that every sample path looks flat?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不是。它只约束总体均值和协方差随时间平移的性质，单条路径仍可剧烈波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> No. It constrains only how the ensemble mean and covariance behave under time shifts; an individual path may still fluctuate violently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么只有一条时间序列时遍历性重要？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is ergodicity important when only one time series is observed?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它为用该路径的长期时间平均估计总体平均提供依据；没有遍历性，一条路径可能不代表总体。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It justifies estimating an ensemble mean with the long-run time average of that path. Without ergodicity, one path may not represent the ensemble.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 均值和协方差何时足以确定整个过程分布？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When are the mean and covariance sufficient to determine the entire process distribution?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 当过程是 Gaussian 时；一般过程还需要高阶和非线性依赖信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> When the process is Gaussian. A general process also requires information about higher-order and nonlinear dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 频谱出现峰值是否证明存在确定周期？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does a peak in the spectrum prove the existence of a deterministic cycle?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不证明。它说明相应频率附近方差集中，仍可能来自随机振荡、滤波或有限样本波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> No. It shows concentration of variance near that frequency, which may still arise from stochastic oscillation, filtering, or finite-sample variation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
