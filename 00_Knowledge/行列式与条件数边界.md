# Determinants and conditioning
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*行列式与条件数边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A determinant with small absolute value says that a real matrix compresses total volume. It does not tell us whether solving its equations is sensitive to **relative** errors. To see the difference, compare two [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrices]], which act on each coordinate separately:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实矩阵的行列式绝对值很小，说明它把总体积压缩得很小，却不能直接说明求解方程是否对**相对**误差敏感。先比较两个[[对角矩阵]]，它们分别缩放各个坐标：

$$A=\begin{bmatrix}10^{-6}&0\\0&10^{-6}\end{bmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{bmatrix}10^6&0\\0&10^{-6}\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$A$ shrinks both coordinate directions equally and has determinant $10^{-12}$. $B$ stretches one direction and compresses the other, yet has determinant $1$. Multiplying the two stretches hides how different they are. A condition number compares them instead.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$A$ 把两个坐标方向同样缩小，行列式为 $10^{-12}$；$B$ 把一个方向拉长、另一个方向压缩，行列式却是 $1$。把两个伸缩量相乘，会掩盖它们之间相差多大；条件数正是要比较这个差距。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A product and a ratio answer different questions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*乘积与比率回答不同的问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a real or complex $n\times n$ matrix, $n\ge1$. Its [[奇异值|singular values]] $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_n\ge0$ are the stretch magnitudes along perpendicular input directions. They are nonnegative, including when entries of $A$ are negative or complex. Their product gives the determinant’s magnitude:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 是实或复 $n\times n$ 方阵，$n\ge1$。它的[[奇异值]] $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_n\ge0$ 是一组相互垂直输入方向上的伸缩量。即使 $A$ 含负数或复数元素，这些伸缩量仍是非负实数。它们的乘积给出行列式的绝对值：

$$|\det A|=\prod_{i=1}^n\sigma_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A$ is invertible, then $\sigma_n>0$. Its **two-norm condition number** is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A$ 可逆，则 $\sigma_n>0$，它的**二范数条件数**为

$$\kappa_2(A)=\|A\|_2\,\|A^{-1}\|_2=\frac{\sigma_1}{\sigma_n}\ge1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The subscript $2$ specifies Euclidean vector length, $\|x\|_2=(\sum_i|x_i|^2)^{1/2}$. The matrix [[谱范数|spectral norm]] $\|A\|_2$ is the largest stretch of a unit input; it equals $\sigma_1$. The [[逆矩阵|inverse]] undoes the stretches, so its largest stretch is $1/\sigma_n$. Thus the condition number compares the strongest and weakest directions. A large ratio indicates ill-conditioning for relative, normwise sensitivity. For a singular square matrix we conventionally set $\kappa_2(A)=\infty$; the inverse formula is then unavailable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下标 $2$ 指定向量使用欧氏长度 $\|x\|_2=(\sum_i|x_i|^2)^{1/2}$。矩阵的[[谱范数]] $\|A\|_2$ 是单位输入的最大伸缩量，等于 $\sigma_1$；[[逆矩阵]]把这些伸缩还原，所以其最大伸缩量为 $1/\sigma_n$。条件数因此比较最强和最弱方向；比率很大，表示按整个向量长度衡量的相对敏感性很高。对奇异方阵，约定 $\kappa_2(A)=\infty$，这时不能使用含逆矩阵的公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To justify the product, use a full [[奇异值分解|SVD]] $A=U\Sigma V^*$, where $\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_n)$ and the [[Unitary 矩阵|unitary matrices]] $U,V$ preserve lengths. The star denotes conjugate transpose, or ordinary transpose for real matrices. These unitary factors have determinant magnitude one, so [[行列式乘法性|multiplying determinants]] leaves exactly the product of the diagonal stretches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘积公式可由完整[[奇异值分解|SVD]] $A=U\Sigma V^*$ 得到，其中 $\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_n)$，[[Unitary 矩阵|酉矩阵]] $U,V$ 保持长度。星号表示共轭转置，实矩阵时就是普通转置。两个酉因子的行列式绝对值都是 $1$，所以用[[行列式乘法性]]后，只剩对角伸缩量的乘积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two counterexamples
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两个反例把两个方向都排除

> [!example] Uniform shrinkage · 整体等比例缩小
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $0<\varepsilon<1$, let $A=\varepsilon I_n$, where $I_n$ leaves every coordinate unchanged. Every singular value of $A$ is $\varepsilon$, so
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $0<\varepsilon<1$，令 $A=\varepsilon I_n$，其中 $I_n$ 保持所有坐标不变。$A$ 的每个奇异值都等于 $\varepsilon$，所以
>
> $$\det A=\varepsilon^n,\qquad\kappa_2(A)=1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The determinant can be arbitrarily small while relative conditioning is as good as possible. With $\varepsilon=10^{-6}$ and $n=2$, this is the first matrix above.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 行列式可以任意小，相对条件性却处在最好的情况。取 $\varepsilon=10^{-6}$、$n=2$ 就是开篇第一个矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, let $B=\operatorname{diag}(M,M^{-1})$ with $M>1$. Its singular values are $M,M^{-1}$, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，令 $B=\operatorname{diag}(M,M^{-1})$，其中 $M>1$。奇异值为 $M,M^{-1}$，于是

$$\det B=1,\qquad \kappa_2(B)=M^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The determinant stays at one while the condition number grows without bound. For $M=10^6$, the ratio is $10^{12}$. Therefore a tiny determinant is neither necessary nor sufficient for ill-conditioning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行列式一直为 $1$，条件数却能无限增大。取 $M=10^6$，比率就是 $10^{12}$。因此，行列式很小既不是病态的必要条件，也不是充分条件。

## What “relative sensitivity” means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*相对敏感性究竟在比较什么？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix invertible $A$ and a nonzero right-hand side $b$ in $Ax=b$. Change only $b$ to $b+\delta b$, and let the exact solution change to $x+\delta x$. The symbol $\delta$ denotes a change. Subtraction gives $A\delta x=\delta b$, hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $Ax=b$ 中固定可逆的 $A$，并取非零的右端 $b$。只把 $b$ 改为 $b+\delta b$，精确解相应变成 $x+\delta x$；符号 $\delta$ 表示改变量。两式相减得到 $A\delta x=\delta b$，所以

$$\|\delta x\|_2\le\|A^{-1}\|_2\|\delta b\|_2,\qquad
\|b\|_2\le\|A\|_2\|x\|_2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Combining these inequalities yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

合并这两个不等式得到

$$\frac{\|\delta x\|_2}{\|x\|_2}
\le\kappa_2(A)\frac{\|\delta b\|_2}{\|b\|_2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The fractions compare changes with the sizes of the original vectors. This is an upper bound, not a claim that every perturbation produces the maximum error. For $A=\varepsilon I_n$, absolute changes are amplified by $1/\varepsilon$, but the original solution is enlarged by the same factor; relative changes remain equal. This is why a small overall scale and poor relative conditioning are different questions. [[条件性与算法稳定性|Algorithmic stability]] separately concerns errors introduced by the computation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个分数都是把改变量与原向量的大小比较。这是上界，并不是说每次扰动都会造成最大的误差。对 $A=\varepsilon I_n$，绝对改变量会放大 $1/\varepsilon$ 倍，但原来的解也同样放大，所以相对改变量保持相等。这解释了为何整体尺度小与相对条件性差是两件事。[[条件性与算法稳定性|算法稳定性]]则另行讨论计算过程引入的误差。

## A practical diagnostic order
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 实际判断顺序

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In exact algebra, $\det A=0$ is a valid [[可逆性与非零行列式|singularity test]]. A small nonzero number is a different matter. For a nonzero real or complex scalar $\alpha$ and invertible $A$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在精确代数中，$\det A=0$ 是可靠的[[可逆性与非零行列式|奇异性判据]]；很小但非零的数则是另一回事。对非零实或复标量 $\alpha$ 与可逆矩阵 $A$，

$$|\det(\alpha A)|=|\alpha|^n|\det A|,\qquad
\kappa_2(\alpha A)=\kappa_2(A).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So a fixed determinant cutoff cannot diagnose numerical rank across different overall scales or dimensions. Inspect singular values and the intended tolerance: $\sigma_n$ measures an absolute weak stretch, whereas $\sigma_n/\sigma_1=1/\kappa_2(A)$ measures it relative to the largest stretch. State the norm, units and scaling convention. Rescaling individual rows or columns can change the condition number; the invariance above is only for multiplying the whole matrix by one scalar, not for arbitrary changes of units across variables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以，一个固定的行列式阈值不能跨整体尺度、跨维数地判断数值秩。应查看奇异值及所采用的容差：$\sigma_n$ 衡量绝对的最弱伸缩量，$\sigma_n/\sigma_1=1/\kappa_2(A)$ 衡量它相对于最大伸缩量有多小。要说明范数、单位和缩放口径。单独缩放某些行或列会改变条件数；上面的不变性只针对整个矩阵乘同一个标量，并不适用于任意变量单位变换。

> [!note]- Complex entries and real volume · 复矩阵的实体积
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For real $A$, $|\det A|$ is the [[行列式体积与取向|real $n$-dimensional volume factor]]. A complex coordinate has two real coordinates. Viewing complex $A$ as a map on $\mathbb R^{2n}$ gives the real volume factor $|\det_{\mathbb C}A|^2=\prod_i\sigma_i^2$. For example, $z\mapsto2z$ on $\mathbb C$ doubles both real coordinates, so its real area factor is $4$, although its complex determinant is $2$. This distinction changes the volume interpretation, not the condition-number comparison.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对实矩阵 $A$，$|\det A|$ 是[[行列式体积与取向|实 $n$ 维体积因子]]。一个复坐标包含两个实坐标；把复矩阵 $A$ 看成 $\mathbb R^{2n}$ 上的映射，实体积因子为 $|\det_{\mathbb C}A|^2=\prod_i\sigma_i^2$。例如复平面上的 $z\mapsto2z$ 把两个实坐标都放大两倍，因此实面积因子是 $4$，复行列式却是 $2$。这个区别改变体积的解释，不改变条件数的比较。
