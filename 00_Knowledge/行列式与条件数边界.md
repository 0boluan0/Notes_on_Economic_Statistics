# 行列式很小不能单独说明矩阵的数值条件很差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A small determinant alone does not establish numerical ill-conditioning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 诊断边界
> 对实数或复数方阵，
> $$
> |\det A|=\prod_{i=1}^n\sigma_i(A),
> $$
> 它把所有方向的缩放乘在一起，并随整体单位改变。满秩方阵的 2-范数条件数却是
> $$
> \kappa_2(A)=\frac{\sigma_{\max}(A)}{\sigma_{\min}(A)},
> $$
> 它比较最强与最弱方向。因此 determinant 很小既不是病态的充分条件，也不是必要条件。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a real or complex square matrix, $|\det A|$ is the product of all singular values and changes with overall scale. The full-rank 2-norm condition number is the ratio $\sigma_{\max}/\sigma_{\min}$. A small determinant is therefore neither sufficient nor necessary for ill-conditioning.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 SVD $A=U\Sigma V^*$ 可见，正交或酉矩阵的 determinant 绝对值为 1，于是 $|\det A|=|\det\Sigma|=\prod_i\sigma_i$。对实矩阵，这个乘积正是单位立方体的 $n$ 维体积缩放；对复矩阵，若把 $A$ 视为 $\mathbb R^{2n}$ 上的实线性映射，真实的 $2n$ 维体积因子是 $|\det_{\mathbb C}A|^2=\prod_i\sigma_i^2$。无论在哪一种语境中，只看乘积都不能分辨“每个方向都同样缩小”和“一个方向被极度压扁、另一个方向被极度拉长”。数值求逆对后者尤其敏感。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From $A=U\Sigma V^*$, the orthogonal or unitary factors have determinant of absolute value one, so $|\det A|=\prod_i\sigma_i$. For a real matrix, this product is the $n$-dimensional volume scale factor. A complex matrix viewed as a real map on $\mathbb R^{2n}$ instead scales real $2n$-volume by $|\det_{\mathbb C}A|^2=\prod_i\sigma_i^2$. In either setting, the product cannot distinguish uniform shrinkage from severe compression in one direction offset by expansion in another. Numerical inversion is especially sensitive to the latter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两个反例把两个方向都排除

取 $0<\varepsilon<1$。矩阵 $A=\varepsilon I_n$ 满足
$$
\det A=\varepsilon^n\quad\text{很小},
\qquad
\kappa_2(A)=1.
$$
它只是把所有方向等比例缩小；相对条件性并不差。

反过来，对 $M>1$，令
$$
B=\operatorname{diag}(M,M^{-1}).
$$
则 $\det B=1$，看起来一点也不小，但
$$
\kappa_2(B)=\frac{M}{M^{-1}}=M^2,
$$
可任意大。一个方向的巨大拉伸恰好掩盖了另一个方向的巨大压缩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A=\varepsilon I_n$, the determinant $\varepsilon^n$ can be tiny while $\kappa_2(A)=1$: every direction is scaled equally. Conversely, $B=\operatorname{diag}(M,M^{-1})$ has determinant one but condition number $M^2$, which can be arbitrarily large. Expansion in one direction hides compression in the other when only the product is inspected.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 实际判断顺序

- 精确代数问题中，$\det A=0$ 可以判定奇异；这是一条可靠的结构结论。
- 浮点数据中，不要用“$|\det A|$ 小于某个固定阈值”判断数值秩。单位改变、矩阵阶数和整体缩放都会改变这个数。
- 要判断近奇异与求解敏感性，应查看奇异值谱、$\sigma_{\min}$、条件数，并说明采用的范数与缩放约定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- In exact algebra, $\det A=0$ reliably establishes singularity.
- With floating-point data, a fixed determinant threshold is not a numerical-rank test; units, dimension, and global scaling all change the value.
- Diagnose near-singularity and sensitivity using the singular spectrum, the smallest singular value, and a condition number with its norm and scaling convention stated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么 $\det\operatorname{diag}(10^6,10^{-6})=1$ 不能说明这个矩阵条件良好？
>
> **答案：** determinant 只看两个奇异值的乘积；它们的比率是 $10^{12}$，所以不同方向的相对缩放极不均衡。
