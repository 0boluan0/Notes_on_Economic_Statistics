# 同一样本上的嵌套 OLS 增加回归量不会提高训练 RSS，也不会降低带截距模型的训练 R²
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Adding regressors to nested OLS on the same sample cannot raise training RSS or lower training R-squared when an intercept is present*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心命题
> 设较小模型的设计矩阵为 $X$，较大模型为 $[X\ Z]$，两者对同一个 $y$ 和同一批观测做未惩罚 OLS。较大模型可以把 $Z$ 的系数设为零，从而精确复制较小模型；它还允许更多候选拟合，所以最小训练残差平方和只能更小或相等。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let the smaller design matrix be $X$ and the larger one $[X\ Z]$, fitted by unpenalised OLS to the same outcome and observations. The larger model can reproduce the smaller one by setting the coefficients on $Z$ to zero, while permitting additional fits. Its minimum training residual sum of squares can therefore only fall or remain unchanged.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

形式上，

$$
\min_{b,c}\lVert y-Xb-Zc\rVert^2
\le
\min_b\lVert y-Xb\rVert^2,
$$

因为左边的可行集合包含所有 $(b,0)$。若两模型都含截距且结果与样本相同，$TSS=\sum_i(y_i-\bar y)^2$ 固定，于是

$$
R^2=1-\frac{RSS}{TSS}
$$

也只能上升或不变。这个结论是优化集合的代数事实，不需要新增变量真的有解释力。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inequality follows because every $(b,0)$ is feasible for the larger problem. With an intercept and the same outcome observations, TSS is fixed, so $R^2=1-RSS/TSS$ can only rise or remain unchanged. This is an algebraic fact about nested optimisation sets; the added variables need not contain genuine signal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它为什么不等于泛化改善
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why it does not imply better generalisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

训练 RSS 用的正是估计系数时见过的数据。额外自由度可能追随这批样本的噪声，因此出现“training RSS 更低、validation loss 更高”。这不与上面的命题矛盾：两个指标评价的是不同观测。要判断预测表现，应进入[[训练验证测试划分]]和[[K折交叉验证]]，而不是期待训练 $R^2$ 自行惩罚复杂度。

结论还依赖精确边界：相同响应、相同行、嵌套列空间、同一种未惩罚 OLS。换了样本、加了正则化或约束，或者比较的不是嵌套模型，就不能直接套用。无截距回归还会让通常的中心化 $R^2$ 解释失去这条简单对应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Training RSS uses the same observations that estimated the coefficients. Extra flexibility can follow sample noise, producing lower training RSS but higher validation loss. The proposition also requires the same outcome, rows, nested column spaces, and unpenalised OLS. Different samples, regularisation, constraints, or nonnested models fall outside it; without an intercept, the usual centred-$R^2$ interpretation also needs separate care.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 在工资模型中加入 20 个无关交互项后，训练 $R^2$ 上升。这个现象是否证明至少一个交互项具有稳定预测价值？
>
> **答案：** 否。较大模型本来就包含较小模型，训练 $R^2$ 不下降是机械结果；稳定预测价值需要独立验证证据。
