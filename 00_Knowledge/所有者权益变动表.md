# 所有者权益变动表是解释期间权益各组成部分从期初到期末变化的报表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A statement of changes in equity explains how each equity component moves from its opening to closing balance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有者权益变动表（statement of changes in equity）把投入资本、留存收益和其他权益组成部分的期初、期间变动与期末联系起来。它区分损益和其他综合收益、与所有者以其所有者身份进行的交易，以及需单独解释的调整；因此比只比较两期[[资产负债表|权益合计]]多回答一个问题：权益为什么变了？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A statement of changes in equity connects opening balances, period movements, and closing balances of contributed capital, retained earnings, and other equity components. It distinguishes performance, owner transactions, and separately explained adjustments, answering why equity changed rather than merely comparing two [[资产负债表|balance-sheet totals]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如忽略交易费用、无期初重述及其他调整时，期初总权益40，本期净利润10、其他综合收益−2、新增所有者投入6、已确认向所有者分配4，则期末总权益为 $40+10-2+6-4=50$。净利润只解释其中一项；其他综合收益是按准则未列入本期净利润的相关收益费用，不应再次加进净利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With no transaction costs, opening restatement, or other adjustments, take opening total equity of 40, net profit of 10, other comprehensive income of −2, owner contributions of 6, and recognised distributions of 4. Closing equity is $40+10-2+6-4=50$. Net profit explains only one component. Other comprehensive income contains relevant income and expense excluded from current profit under the accounting rules; it should not be added into profit again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

留存收益内部转入其他储备可能改变权益构成而不改变总权益；追溯更正也可能调整期初余额而非本期利润。因此先看“调整后期初”与各列，再看合计。确认股利分配与实际付出现金还可能处于不同时间，后者应对照[[现金流量表]]，不能机械把本表分配额当作本期现金流出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A transfer from retained earnings to another reserve can change equity composition without changing total equity. Retrospective corrections may adjust opening balances rather than current profit. Inspect the restated opening balances and individual columns before the total. A recognised distribution and its cash payment may also occur at different times, so compare the latter with the [[现金流量表|cash-flow statement]] instead of assuming they coincide.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sources and verification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [AASB 101，第7、96、106—110段；PDF第8、24、26页](https://www.aasb.gov.au/admin/file/content105/c9/AASB101_07-15_COMPdec22_01-24.pdf#page=26)：支持权益各组成部分调节、损益/其他综合收益/所有者交易分开、期初重述与内部转移。40至50的例子为限定项目后的自拟算例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
AASB 101 supports component reconciliation, the separation of performance and owner transactions, opening restatement, and internal transfers. The 40-to-50 illustration is original under its stated exclusions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [AASB 107，第6、43—44段；PDF第7、12页](https://www.aasb.gov.au/admin/file/content105/c9/AASB107_08-15_COMPjun23_01-24.pdf#page=12)：核对现金流以实际现金及等价物收付为对象，非现金融资不列当期现金流；据此区分权益确认与现金支付时点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
AASB 107 verifies that cash flows concern cash and cash-equivalent movements and exclude non-cash financing, supporting the distinction between equity recognition and payment timing.
