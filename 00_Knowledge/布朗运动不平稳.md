# Brownian levels and stationary increments
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*布朗运动的水平与平稳增量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A moving object's **position** and its **displacement during the next minute** answer different questions. For [[标准布朗运动|standard Brownian motion]] $W=(W_t)_{t\ge0}$, uncertainty about the position accumulates as time passes. Yet a fresh interval of a given length always contributes the same displacement distribution. This is why Brownian motion has stationary increments while its level process is not stationary.

Here $W_t$ is the random position at time $t$, and $W_{s+h}-W_s$ is the increment over an interval starting at $s$ with length $h$. The notation [[正态分布|$N(m,v)$]] gives a normal law with mean $m$ and [[方差|variance]] $v$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个运动物体的**当前位置**和它在**接下来一分钟内的位移**回答不同问题。对[[标准布朗运动]] $W=(W_t)_{t\ge0}$，位置的不确定性随时间累积，但新增一段固定长度的时间，总会贡献相同分布的位移。因此，布朗运动有平稳增量，水平过程本身却不平稳。

这里 $W_t$ 是时刻 $t$ 的随机位置，$W_{s+h}-W_s$ 是从 $s$ 开始、长度为 $h$ 的区间增量。[[正态分布|$N(m,v)$]] 的两个参数分别是均值 $m$ 和[[方差]] $v$。

> [!example] Compare positions, then displacements · 先比位置，再比位移
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $W_2\sim N(0,2)$ and $W_{101}\sim N(0,101)$ have different spreads. But $W_2-W_1$ and $W_{101}-W_{100}$ both have law $N(0,1)$: both measure motion over one time unit. They are also independent because their intervals do not overlap. Equal displacement laws do not imply equal position laws.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $W_2\sim N(0,2)$ 与 $W_{101}\sim N(0,101)$ 的分散程度不同。但 $W_2-W_1$ 和 $W_{101}-W_{100}$ 都服从 $N(0,1)$，因为两者都记录一个时间单位内的位移。两个区间不重叠，所以这两个增量还独立。位移同分布不能推出位置同分布。

## Why strict stationarity fails
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么不严平稳

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[严平稳定义|Strict stationarity]] requires the joint law at every finite selection of times to remain unchanged after a common time shift. We use the usual one-sided version: all original and shifted times must lie in $[0,\infty)$. Even the one-time law must therefore be unchanged.

Brownian motion starts at $W_0=0$ almost surely, whereas $W_t\sim N(0,t)$ for $t>0$. At zero the distribution is a point mass, with no ordinary normal density; writing $N(0,0)$ is only a convention for that degenerate law. For any two distinct positive times, the variances also differ. Thus failure of stationarity is not just an issue at the initial endpoint: the one-time laws already fail the shift test throughout positive time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[严平稳定义|严平稳]]要求：任意有限组时刻的联合分布，在所有时刻共同平移后保持不变。这里采用单侧时间上的通常口径，即原时刻和平移后的时刻都必须属于 $[0,\infty)$。因此，单时点分布也必须保持不变。

布朗运动在零时刻几乎必然满足 $W_0=0$，而 $t>0$ 时有 $W_t\sim N(0,t)$。零时刻的分布是单点质量，没有普通正态密度；$N(0,0)$ 只是表示这个退化分布的约定。任意两个不同的正时刻，其方差也不同。因此，问题并不只出在起始端点：即使只看正时刻，单时点分布仍通不过平移检验。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why weak stationarity also fails
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么也不宽平稳

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[宽平稳定义|Weak stationarity]] asks for finite second moments, a constant mean, and a covariance depending only on the separation between times. In particular, the variance must be constant. Brownian motion has a constant mean zero, but

$$\operatorname{Var}(W_t)=t,\qquad \operatorname{Cov}(W_s,W_t)=\min(s,t).$$

The [[协方差|covariance]] here measures the shared fluctuation in the two positions; $\min(s,t)$ is the earlier time. The later position includes all motion accumulated before that time. For example, the pairs $(0,1)$ and $(1,2)$ have the same separation of one, but their covariances are $0$ and $1$. The variance and covariance requirements both fail. A constant mean alone cannot establish weak stationarity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[宽平稳定义|宽平稳]]要求有限二阶矩、常均值，以及只依赖两时刻间隔的协方差；特别地，方差必须为常数。布朗运动的均值虽然恒为零，但上式中的方差等于 $t$，随时间增长。

这里的[[协方差]]衡量两个位置共有的波动，$\min(s,t)$ 是两个时刻中较早的一个：后来的位置包含了此前积累的全部运动。例如，$(0,1)$ 与 $(1,2)$ 都相隔一个时间单位，协方差却分别为 $0$ 与 $1$。常方差和仅依赖间隔的协方差这两个要求都失败。均值恒定本身不能推出宽平稳。

## What remains stationary
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## “增量平稳”不是“过程平稳”

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For deterministic $s\ge0$ and $h>0$,

$$W_{s+h}-W_s\sim N(0,h).$$

Changing $s$ while keeping $h$ fixed leaves this law unchanged. More strongly, shifting any finite configuration of Brownian increments together preserves its joint law: split the intervals at all their endpoints into nonoverlapping pieces, whose independent normal laws depend only on the piece lengths. Their sums give the original increments. The model with a deterministic linear drift has the corresponding [[带漂移布朗增量|increment law]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对预先固定的 $s\ge0$、$h>0$，增量服从上式的 $N(0,h)$。固定长度 $h$ 而改变起点 $s$，分布不变。更强地，任意有限组布朗增量一起平移后，联合分布也不变：按所有端点把区间拆成不重叠的小段，各小段的独立正态分布只由段长决定，再把小段相加还原各增量即可。加入确定性线性漂移后，对应结果见[[带漂移布朗增量]]。

> [!note] A moving window can be stationary without being independent · 滑动窗口可以平稳但不独立
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fix $h>0$ and define $Y_t=W_{t+h}-W_t$ for $t\ge0$. This new process records a displacement rather than a position. Its finite vectors are [[联合Gaussian|jointly Gaussian]], its mean is zero, and
>
> $$\operatorname{Cov}(Y_s,Y_t)=\max\{h-|t-s|,0\}.$$
>
> The covariance is the length of the shared part of the two windows: only that part contributes to both increments. It depends only on $t-s$, so the Gaussian joint laws are unchanged by common shifts and $Y$ is strictly stationary. With $h=1$, the windows starting at $0$ and $1/2$ have covariance $1/2$ and are not independent. Independence applies to nonoverlapping intervals, including intervals whose endpoints merely touch.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定 $h>0$，令 $Y_t=W_{t+h}-W_t$，$t\ge0$。这个新过程记录位移而非位置，其有限维向量[[联合Gaussian|联合正态]]、均值为零，协方差由上式给出。该协方差就是两窗口重叠部分的长度，因为只有这一段同时贡献给两个增量。它只依赖 $t-s$，所以 Gaussian 联合分布在共同平移后不变，$Y$ 严平稳。当 $h=1$ 时，从 $0$ 和 $1/2$ 开始的窗口协方差为 $1/2$，并不独立。独立性适用于不重叠的区间，端点仅相接的区间也包括在内。
