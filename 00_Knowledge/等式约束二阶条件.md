# Second-order test under equality constraints
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*等式约束二阶判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Lagrange必要条件|Lagrange first-order condition]] finds points where no feasible velocity changes the objective to first order. It does not tell us whether the objective then bends upward or downward. The **second-order test under equality constraints** examines that bending along the constraint surface. Strict downward curvature in every nonzero tangent direction certifies a strict local maximum; strict upward curvature certifies a strict local minimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Lagrange必要条件]]找出一阶上没有可行速度改变目标的点，却还没有判断目标随后向上弯还是向下弯。**等式约束二阶判据**检验沿约束曲面的这种弯曲。每个非零切方向都严格向下弯，可以确认严格局部最大；都严格向上弯，则确认严格局部最小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A curved constraint changes the curvature to inspect
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*约束弯曲时，应该检查哪种曲率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maximise $f(x,y)=-y$ subject to $h(x,y)=y-x^2=0$. The feasible set is a parabola, and on it the objective equals $-x^2$. Thus $(0,0)$ is a strict maximum, although the [[Hessian矩阵|Hessian matrix]] of $f$—its matrix of second partial derivatives—is zero everywhere. The curvature comes from moving on the parabola.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $h(x,y)=y-x^2=0$ 下最大化 $f(x,y)=-y$。可行集是抛物线，其上的目标等于 $-x^2$，所以 $(0,0)$ 是严格最大点；但 $f$ 的[[Hessian矩阵]]——二阶偏导组成的矩阵——处处为零。曲率来自沿抛物线移动。

$$\nabla f(0,0)=(0,-1)^T,\quad \nabla h(0,0)=(0,1)^T,\quad
\nu^*=-1,\qquad \mathcal L=f-\nu h=-x^2\quad\text{at }\nu=-1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the origin the tangent space is $T=\{(a,0)^T:a\in\mathbb R\}$, because $Dh=(0,1)$. Holding the multiplier at $\nu^*=-1$, the Lagrangian Hessian is $H=\operatorname{diag}(-2,0)$. For a nonzero tangent vector, $d^THd=-2a^2<0$. This [[二次型|quadratic form]] measures curvature in direction $d$. The zero normal curvature does not obstruct a constrained maximum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原点处 $Dh=(0,1)$，所以切空间为 $T=\{(a,0)^T:a\in\mathbb R\}$。把乘子固定在 $\nu^*=-1$，Lagrangian Hessian 为 $H=\operatorname{diag}(-2,0)$。对非零切向量，$d^THd=-2a^2<0$。这个[[二次型]]衡量沿 $d$ 方向的曲率。法向曲率为零并不妨碍约束最大点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A sufficient test at a feasible stationary point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*可行驻点上的充分判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the sign convention $\mathcal L(x,\nu)=f(x)-\nu^Th(x)$. Here $x\in\mathbb R^n$, $h=(h_1,\ldots,h_m)$ and each constraint equals zero. Assume all functions are $C^2$ on an open neighbourhood of the candidate: first and second partial derivatives are continuous. Feasibility $h(x^*)=0$ is essential as well as stationarity $\nabla_x\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)=0$. Full row rank of the [[Jacobian矩阵|constraint Jacobian]] gives the regular surface and its tangent space described in the first-order card.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用 $\mathcal L(x,\nu)=f(x)-\nu^Th(x)$ 的号约定。这里 $x\in\mathbb R^n$，$h=(h_1,\ldots,h_m)$，每个约束都等于零。假设所有函数在候选点的开邻域内为 $C^2$，即一阶、二阶偏导连续。除了驻点条件 $\nabla_x\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)=0$，还必须有可行性 $h(x^*)=0$。[[Jacobian矩阵|约束 Jacobian]] 行满秩，才给出一阶条件卡中描述的正则曲面及其切空间。

> [!important] Strict curvature along the tangent space · 切空间上的严格曲率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Optimise $f(x)$ subject to $h(x)=0$. Suppose $f$ and $h$ are $C^2$ near $x^*$, $Dh(x^*)$ has full row rank, and $(x^*,\nu^*)$ satisfies Lagrange stationarity. Let $T=\{d:Dh(x^*)d=0\}$. If $d^T\nabla_{xx}^2\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)d<0$ for every nonzero $d\in T$, then $x^*$ is a strict local maximum; reversing the sign gives a strict local minimum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑 $h(x)=0$ 下优化 $f(x)$。设 $f,h$ 在 $x^*$ 附近为 $C^2$，$Dh(x^*)$ 行满秩，且 $(x^*,\nu^*)$ 满足 Lagrange 驻点条件。令
> $$T=\{d\in\mathbb R^n:Dh(x^*)d=0\}.$$
> 若对每个非零 $d\in T$ 都有 $d^T\nabla_{xx}^2\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)d<0$，则 $x^*$ 是严格局部最大点；把 $<0$ 改成 $>0$，则得到严格局部最小点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the whole space is not tested
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么不是检查整个空间

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equality constraints restrict feasible motion to a local surface. The equation $Dh(x^*)d=0$ describes its tangent directions; a normal displacement leaves the feasible set to first order and is irrelevant to constrained optimality. The Lagrangian Hessian combines objective and constraint curvature, so inspecting $\nabla^2f(x^*)$ alone is generally wrong.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等式约束把可行移动限制在一张局部曲面上。$Dh(x^*)d=0$ 描述该曲面的切方向；法向移动会在一阶上离开约束集，因此与约束最优无关。Lagrangian Hessian
$$
\nabla_{xx}^2\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)
=\nabla^2f(x^*)-\sum_{j=1}^m\nu_j^*\nabla^2h_j(x^*)
$$
同时计入目标曲率与约束曲率，不能只看 $\nabla^2f(x^*)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the columns of $Z$ form a basis for $T$, the test becomes strict negative or positive definiteness of the reduced Hessian $Z^T\nabla_{xx}^2\mathcal L Z$. A bordered Hessian is an equivalent computational device in some low-dimensional problems, but its minor-sign mnemonic varies with maximisation versus minimisation, the number of constraints, and the Lagrangian sign convention. Writing the tangent-space condition first is safer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $Z$ 的列构成 $T$ 的一组基，条件可写成约化 Hessian $Z^T\nabla_{xx}^2\mathcal L Z$ 严格负定或严格正定。边框 Hessian 是某些低维题目的等价计算工具，但其主子式符号规则随最大/最小、约束个数与 Lagrangian 号约定而变；先写切空间条件，再做代数检查更可靠。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The columns of $Z$ are independent spanning vectors for $T$, so every tangent direction has the unique form $d=Zv$. Then $d^THd=v^T(Z^THZ)v$. No orthonormality is required for this sign test. A different basis can change the matrix's eigenvalues, but cannot change whether the quadratic form is positive or negative on every nonzero tangent vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$Z$ 的列是张成 $T$ 的线性无关向量，因此每个切方向唯一写成 $d=Zv$，且 $d^THd=v^T(Z^THZ)v$。这个定号判据不要求基向量正交归一。换基可能改变矩阵的特征值，却不会改变二次型是否在每个非零切向量上恒正或恒负。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the Lagrangian appears
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么出现 Lagrangian*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take a twice differentiable feasible curve $\gamma(t)$ through $x^*$, with velocity $d=\gamma'(0)$. Differentiating each identity $h_j(\gamma(t))=0$ twice gives $d^T\nabla^2h_jd+\nabla h_j^T\gamma''(0)=0$. Meanwhile the second derivative of $f(\gamma(t))$ is $d^T\nabla^2f\,d+\nabla f^T\gamma''(0)$. At stationarity $\nabla f=\sum_j\nu_j^*\nabla h_j$, so the terms involving the curve's acceleration cancel:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取经过 $x^*$ 的二次可微可行曲线 $\gamma(t)$，速度为 $d=\gamma'(0)$。对每个恒等式 $h_j(\gamma(t))=0$ 求两次导数，得到 $d^T\nabla^2h_jd+\nabla h_j^T\gamma''(0)=0$。另一方面，$f(\gamma(t))$ 的二阶导数是 $d^T\nabla^2f\,d+\nabla f^T\gamma''(0)$。驻点处 $\nabla f=\sum_j\nu_j^*\nabla h_j$，所以关于曲线加速度的项抵消为

$$\left.\frac{d^2}{dt^2}f(\gamma(t))\right|_{t=0}
=d^T\left(\nabla^2f(x^*)-\sum_j\nu_j^*\nabla^2h_j(x^*)\right)d=d^THd.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This also explains the weaker necessary condition: at a regular local maximum every such curvature is $\le0$; at a minimum it is $\ge0$. Regularity makes every tangent vector attainable by a feasible curve. Strict inequalities give a sufficient test, while weak inequalities alone can leave the answer undecided.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也解释了较弱的必要条件：在正则局部最大点，每个这样的曲率都 $\le0$；最小点则都 $\ge0$。正则性让每个切向量都能由可行曲线实现。严格不等式给出充分判据，而只有弱不等式时仍可能无法判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why strict curvature controls all nearby feasible points
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么严格曲率能控制全部附近可行点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A curve calculation gives intuition; [[多元Taylor近似|Taylor's formula]] makes the sufficient claim uniform over nearby feasible points. Let $H=\nabla^2_{xx}\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)$, fix $\nu^*$, and consider a feasible displacement $s=x-x^*$. Because both endpoints satisfy $h=0$ and $\nabla_x\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)=0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

曲线计算给出直觉；[[多元Taylor近似|Taylor 公式]]则让充分性同时控制附近所有可行点。令 $H=\nabla^2_{xx}\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)$，固定 $\nu^*$，考虑可行位移 $s=x-x^*$。由于两个端点都满足 $h=0$，且 $\nabla_x\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)=0$，

$$f(x^*+s)-f(x^*)
=\mathcal L(x^*+s,\nu^*)-\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)
=\frac12s^THs+o(\|s\|^2).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose negative definiteness on $T$ did not give a strict maximum. There would be distinct feasible $x_k\to x^*$ with $f(x_k)\ge f(x^*)$. Put $s_k=x_k-x^*$ and $d_k=s_k/\|s_k\|$. A subsequence of these unit vectors converges to a unit vector $d$. Differentiability of $h$ gives $0=As_k+o(\|s_k\|)$, hence $Ad=0$. Dividing the Taylor formula by $\|s_k\|^2$ and taking the limit would give $0\le\frac12d^THd<0$, a contradiction. Replacing $f$ by $-f$ proves the minimum version. The term $o(\|s\|^2)$ means its ratio to $\|s\|^2$ tends to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设在 $T$ 上负定却没有严格最大点，就存在不同于 $x^*$ 的可行点 $x_k\to x^*$，使 $f(x_k)\ge f(x^*)$。令 $s_k=x_k-x^*$、$d_k=s_k/\|s_k\|$。这些单位向量有一个收敛子列，其极限 $d$ 仍为单位向量。$h$ 可微给出 $0=As_k+o(\|s_k\|)$，因此 $Ad=0$。将 Taylor 公式除以 $\|s_k\|^2$ 再取极限，将得到 $0\le\frac12d^THd<0$，矛盾。以 $-f$ 代替 $f$ 就得到最小值版本。$o(\|s\|^2)$ 表示该项与 $\|s\|^2$ 的比值趋于零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem above keeps full rank to give the usual tangent-space interpretation and the necessary-condition comparison. The sufficient argument itself only needs feasibility, a stationary multiplier and strict definiteness on $\ker A$; even without full rank those hypotheses suffice. If $m=n$ and $A$ is invertible, the feasible point is locally isolated and $T=\{0\}$: the nonzero-direction test is vacuous, and it is both a strict local maximum and minimum relative to the feasible set. With no constraints, the test reduces to the ordinary Hessian criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的定理保留满秩，是为了使用通常的切空间解释，并与必要条件比较。充分性论证本身只需要可行性、一个驻点乘子以及在 $\ker A$ 上严格定号；即使不满秩，这些假设仍然足够。若 $m=n$ 且 $A$ 可逆，可行点局部孤立，$T=\{0\}$：没有非零方向需要检查，相对于可行集它同时是严格局部最大与最小。没有约束时，判据退化为普通 Hessian 判据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## This is a one-way sufficient condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 这是一条单向的充分条件

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result runs only from strict definiteness on the tangent space to a strict local optimum. The failed converse from strict optimality to a nonsingular Hessian, reduced Hessian, or optimality-system Jacobian is explained in [[严格最优不推系统非奇异|the strict-optimality boundary]], while [[半定Hessian无结论|the semidefinite-Hessian boundary]] handles the general inconclusive case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条条件的方向是“切空间上严格定号 $\Rightarrow$ 严格局部最优”。不能反向从严格最优推出 Hessian、约化 Hessian 或最优性系统 Jacobian 非奇异；高阶反例及其对比较静态的后果见 [[严格最优不推系统非奇异]]。半定时的一般无结论边界见 [[半定Hessian无结论]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Do distinguish that failed converse from what the full sufficient hypotheses give: full row rank together with strict definiteness on the tangent space also implies a nonsingular bordered optimality system. The [[严格最优不推系统非奇异|boundary card]] proves this by checking its kernel. It is strict optimality by itself that cannot supply the missing hypotheses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要把这个失败的逆推与完整充分假设的后果分开：满行秩加上切空间严格定号，还能推出边框最优性系统非奇异。[[严格最优不推系统非奇异]]通过检查系统的核给出证明。不能补上缺失假设的是严格最优本身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conclusion is local. For $f(x,y)=-x^2+x^4$ subject to $y=0$, the origin passes the negative-curvature test, and $-x^2+x^4<0$ for $0<|x|<1$. But the objective tends to $+\infty$ along the feasible line as $|x|\to\infty$, so there is no global maximum. The signs at one point do not compare distant feasible choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结论是局部的。在 $y=0$ 下取 $f(x,y)=-x^2+x^4$，原点通过负曲率判据，且 $0<|x|<1$ 时 $-x^2+x^4<0$。但沿可行直线令 $|x|\to\infty$，目标趋于 $+\infty$，所以没有全局最大值。一个点处的曲率符号不会比较远处的可行选择。

> [!question] Choose the directions · 选对检查方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why is the Lagrangian Hessian tested on the tangent space rather than required to be negative definite on all of $\mathbb R^n$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 等式约束下为什么应检查 $\nabla_{xx}^2\mathcal L$ 在切空间的定号，而不是要求它在整个 $\mathbb R^n$ 上负定？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Only tangent directions are first-order feasible. Normal directions leave the constraint surface, so their curvature does not determine local optimality on that surface.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 只有切方向对应一阶可行移动；法向方向会离开约束集，其曲率符号不决定约束曲面上的局部最优。
