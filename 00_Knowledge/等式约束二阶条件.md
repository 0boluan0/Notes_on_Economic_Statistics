# 正则等式约束驻点处的 Lagrangian Hessian 若在切空间上严格定号，就足以给出严格局部最优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*At a regular equality-constrained stationary point, a strictly signed Lagrangian Hessian on the tangent space is sufficient for a strict local optimum*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 只检查可行的二阶方向
> 考虑 $h(x)=0$ 下优化 $f(x)$。设 $f,h$ 在 $x^*$ 附近为 $C^2$，$Dh(x^*)$ 行满秩，且 $(x^*,\nu^*)$ 满足 Lagrange 驻点条件。令
> $$T=\{d\in\mathbb R^n:Dh(x^*)d=0\}.$$
> 若对每个非零 $d\in T$ 都有 $d^T\nabla_{xx}^2\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)d<0$，则 $x^*$ 是严格局部最大点；把 $<0$ 改成 $>0$，则得到严格局部最小点。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Optimise $f(x)$ subject to $h(x)=0$. Suppose $f$ and $h$ are $C^2$ near $x^*$, $Dh(x^*)$ has full row rank, and $(x^*,\nu^*)$ satisfies Lagrange stationarity. Let $T=\{d:Dh(x^*)d=0\}$. If $d^T\nabla_{xx}^2\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)d<0$ for every nonzero $d\in T$, then $x^*$ is a strict local maximum; reversing the sign gives a strict local minimum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么不是检查整个空间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the whole space is not tested*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等式约束把可行移动限制在一张局部曲面上。$Dh(x^*)d=0$ 描述该曲面的切方向；法向移动会在一阶上离开约束集，因此与约束最优无关。Lagrangian Hessian
$$
\nabla_{xx}^2\mathcal L(x^*,\nu^*)
=\nabla^2f(x^*)-\sum_{j=1}^m\nu_j^*\nabla^2h_j(x^*)
$$
同时计入目标曲率与约束曲率，不能只看 $\nabla^2f(x^*)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equality constraints restrict feasible motion to a local surface. The equation $Dh(x^*)d=0$ describes its tangent directions; a normal displacement leaves the feasible set to first order and is irrelevant to constrained optimality. The Lagrangian Hessian combines objective and constraint curvature, so inspecting $\nabla^2f(x^*)$ alone is generally wrong.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $Z$ 的列构成 $T$ 的一组基，条件可写成约化 Hessian $Z^T\nabla_{xx}^2\mathcal L Z$ 严格负定或严格正定。边框 Hessian 是某些低维题目的等价计算工具，但其主子式符号规则随最大/最小、约束个数与 Lagrangian 号约定而变；先写切空间条件，再做代数检查更可靠。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the columns of $Z$ form a basis for $T$, the test becomes strict negative or positive definiteness of the reduced Hessian $Z^T\nabla_{xx}^2\mathcal L Z$. A bordered Hessian is an equivalent computational device in some low-dimensional problems, but its minor-sign mnemonic varies with maximisation versus minimisation, the number of constraints, and the Lagrangian sign convention. Writing the tangent-space condition first is safer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 这是一条单向的充分条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*This is a one-way sufficient condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条条件的方向是“切空间上严格定号 $\Rightarrow$ 严格局部最优”。不能反向从严格最优推出 Hessian、约化 Hessian 或最优性系统 Jacobian 非奇异；高阶反例及其对比较静态的后果见 [[严格最优不推系统非奇异]]。半定时的一般无结论边界见 [[半定Hessian无结论]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result runs only from strict definiteness on the tangent space to a strict local optimum. The failed converse from strict optimality to a nonsingular Hessian, reduced Hessian, or optimality-system Jacobian is explained in [[严格最优不推系统非奇异|the strict-optimality boundary]], while [[半定Hessian无结论|the semidefinite-Hessian boundary]] handles the general inconclusive case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 等式约束下为什么应检查 $\nabla_{xx}^2\mathcal L$ 在切空间的定号，而不是要求它在整个 $\mathbb R^n$ 上负定？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why is the Lagrangian Hessian tested on the tangent space rather than required to be negative definite on all of $\mathbb R^n$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 只有切方向对应一阶可行移动；法向方向会离开约束集，其曲率符号不决定约束曲面上的局部最优。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Only tangent directions are first-order feasible. Normal directions leave the constraint surface, so their curvature does not determine local optimality on that surface.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
