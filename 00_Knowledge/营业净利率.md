# 营业净利率是同一期间净利润占营业收入的比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net profit margin is net income divided by revenue for the same period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

营业净利率回答：每取得一元营业收入，最终确认了多少净利润？设同一报告主体、同一期间的净利润为 $NI$、营业收入为 $S>0$，则净利率 $m=NI/S$。ABC 公司收入 3000、净利润 136，净利率为 $136/3000\approx4.53\%$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net margin asks how much net income is recognised per unit of revenue. For the same reporting entity and period, let net income be $NI$ and revenue be $S>0$; then $m=NI/S$. ABC's revenue of 3000 and net income of 136 give approximately 4.53%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“净”是扣完进入损益的成本、费用和所得税后的结果，不是仅扣商品成本的[[毛利率]]，也不是[[营业利润率]]。净利润还可能包含投资收益或偶发损益，因此营业净利率并不天然等于主营业务的持续盈利水平。追问其变化时，可把[[利润表]]各项目除以收入，沿[[结构百分比报表]]找出成本率、费用率或其他损益占比的变化，再查附注。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Net” means after the costs, expenses, and tax recognised in profit or loss. It differs from [[毛利率|gross margin]] and [[营业利润率|operating margin]]. Investment income and unusual items may enter net income, so net margin need not represent sustainable core-business profitability. Divide [[利润表|income-statement]] items by revenue in a [[结构百分比报表|common-size statement]], identify the changing components, and investigate the notes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它的分母是销售收入，不是投入本金，不能直接与存款年利率比较。若要把销售利润联系到资产占用，需再乘[[总资产周转率]]；在同口径下，这得到[[总资产净利率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The denominator is sales, not invested principal, so net margin cannot be ranked directly against a deposit interest rate. Multiplying by matched [[总资产周转率|total asset turnover]] connects sales profitability to asset use and yields [[总资产净利率|net-income return on assets]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
