# 基尼系数是随机两人收入绝对差的期望除以两倍平均收入

基尼系数（Gini coefficient）用随机两人收入绝对差的期望除以两倍平均收入，衡量收入分布的相对不平等；几何上，它等于[[洛伦兹曲线]]与平等线之间面积的两倍。这里采用收入非负、平均收入严格为正的通常口径；消费也可作为变量，但不能把不同[[贫困测量的福利口径|福利口径]]的数字直接当成可比观察。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Gini coefficient measures relative income inequality as the expected absolute difference between two independently drawn incomes divided by twice mean income. Geometrically, it is twice the area between the [[洛伦兹曲线|Lorenz curve]] and the equality line. This definition uses nonnegative incomes with a strictly positive mean. Consumption may replace income, but observations using different [[贫困测量的福利口径|welfare measures]] are not automatically comparable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $n$ 人等权的完整有限人口中，独立抽取允许抽到同一个人。令 $\mu=\sum_i y_i/n>0$，则：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a complete finite population of $n$ equally weighted people, independent draws may select the same person. With $\mu=\sum_i y_i/n>0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
G=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}|y_i-y_j|}{2n^2\mu}
=1-2\int_0^1L(p)\,dp.
$$

四人收入为 $10,20,30,40$，$\mu=25$；六个不同无序配对的绝对差为 $10,20,30,10,20,10$，合计 100。双重求和把它们计两遍，故 $G=200/(2\times16\times25)=0.25$；若按世界银行常见的 0–100 标度报告，则为 25，而不是贫困人口占比 25%。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For incomes $10,20,30,40$, the mean is 25. The six distinct unordered pair differences are $10,20,30,10,20,10$, totaling 100. The double sum counts them twice, so $G=200/(2\times16\times25)=0.25$. On the World Bank's usual 0–100 reporting scale, this is 25; it does not mean that 25% of people are poor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

此式完全平等时为 0；固定 $n$ 的最大值为 $(n-1)/n$，在一人拥有全部正收入时取得。因此四人例的上界是 $0.75$，不能一面使用此式、一面声称其四人上界为 1。另乘 $n/(n-1)$ 是不同的有限样本归一化约定，必须明示。抽样调查还须按调查权重计算，不能把每条记录自动等同于一个等权居民。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This formula is zero at equality. For fixed $n$, its maximum is $(n-1)/n$, attained when one person receives all positive income. The four-person maximum is therefore $0.75$, not 1. Multiplying by $n/(n-1)$ adopts a different finite-sample normalization and must be stated. Survey estimates also require survey weights rather than treating every record as an equally weighted resident.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基尼不因所有收入同比例变化而改变。判断再分配效果须看[[基尼与转移位置]]；从一个基尼值不能倒推出完整分布，见[[相同基尼与分布形状]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Proportional changes in all incomes leave the Gini unchanged. For redistribution, see [[基尼与转移位置|the Gini's response to transfer location]]. A Gini value cannot recover a full distribution; see [[相同基尼与分布形状|different distributions with the same Gini]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
