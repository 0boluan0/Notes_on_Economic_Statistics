# 看涨期权赋予持有人按执行价买入标的的权利
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A call gives its holder the right to buy the underlying at the strike price*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一份普通看涨期权（call）让买方选择是否按 $K$ 买入约定数量的标的。到期时，若市价 $S_T>K$，用 $K$ 买入价值 $S_T$ 的资产有差额价值；若 $S_T\le K$，放弃行权即可。按一单位标的、无履约障碍的到期经济支付写为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A plain call lets its buyer choose whether to buy the underlying at $K$. At expiry, buying an asset worth $S_T>K$ creates value; if $S_T\le K$, the holder can decline. Per underlying unit, with performance available, the terminal economic payoff is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$X_T=\max(S_T-K,0).$$

若 $K=100$，$S_T$ 为 80、100、120 时，支付依次为 0、0、20。这个函数描述**到期支付**，不是今天的价格；距离到期尚有时间时，需要另做估值，例如满足相应假设时采用[[BSM期权定价|BSM 模型]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $K=100$, terminal underlying prices of 80, 100, and 120 give payoffs of 0, 0, and 20. These are terminal payoffs, not today's option prices. Before expiry, valuation needs additional inputs and assumptions; the [[BSM期权定价|BSM model]] is one choice when its conditions hold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若期初权利金为 8，忽略利息和费用，即使标的到期涨到 105，支付也只有 5，净损益仍为 −3。盈亏平衡价为 108。对应的裸卖方收取 8，却要承担相反支付；在股票价格无上界的模型中，其损失无上界。这里的买卖方向与“看涨”这个合约名称必须分别判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With an initial premium of 8 and no interest or fees, a terminal price of 105 produces a payoff of 5 but a loss of 3. Breakeven is 108. The uncovered writer receives 8 and owes the opposite payoff; its loss is unbounded in a model with no upper bound on the stock price. Contract type and position direction are separate choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
