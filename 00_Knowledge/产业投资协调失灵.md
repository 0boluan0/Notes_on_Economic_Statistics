# 产业投资协调失灵是相互依赖的可盈利投资因缺乏协调而不能共同启动

产业投资协调失灵，是若干相互补充的投资只有共同实现才有利可图，但在分散决策下，各方因缺少可信的共同安排而停留在不投资的局面。固定成本、本地不可贸易投入和足够的使用规模可以使一项投资的回报依赖其他投资；并非所有缺投资、所有上下游联系都属于这种失灵。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industrial investment coordination failure occurs when complementary investments are profitable together, but decentralized decisions remain stuck without investment because a credible joint arrangement is missing. Fixed costs, locally supplied nontradable inputs and sufficient utilization can make returns interdependent. Not every investment shortfall or supply-chain link fits this mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个教学构造是生产项目甲与配套服务乙。两者不投时，各得0；任一方单独投资，其净收益为−4，未投资者为0；双方共同投资时，各得6。这里的数值均已扣除各自全部投资与经营成本。若双方都不投，任何一方独自改变决定都会亏损；但共同启动能使两方都受益。因此，“等待别人先行动”可以维持一个双方都不满意的结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a teaching construction, production project A and complementary service B each receive 0 if neither invests. A sole investor receives −4 while the non-investor receives 0. Joint investment yields 6 to each, net of their respective investment and operating costs. From mutual non-investment, neither benefits from acting alone, although joint action improves both payoffs. Waiting can therefore sustain an inferior outcome for both parties.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

诊断时必须具体指出：哪项投资需要哪项配套，为什么普通采购或合同尚不能促成共同启动，以及共同投资后是否真的覆盖全部成本。如果双方联合投资仍亏损，缺口就不能仅靠“协调”解释；如果私人联合体、长期购买合同或共同出资已经解决问题，也不能再把政府介入当成必需。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diagnosis must specify the complementarity, why ordinary procurement or contracts have not secured joint action, and whether joint investment really covers all costs. If it remains loss-making, coordination alone cannot explain the gap. If a private consortium, long-term purchase contract or joint financing resolves it, government intervention is not necessary on that ground.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若[[学习外部性]]持续存在，其矫正理由可能持续；而若障碍只是共同启动，项目协调成功后可能无需长期补贴。政府可以帮助形成共同承诺或提供配套投入，但担保也有风险和激励成本，不能因为成功时无需付款就视为免费。应针对相互依赖的活动选择工具，并用[[实施能力诊断]]检查承诺、信息与监督是否可执行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unlike a persistent [[学习外部性|learning externality]], a pure start-up coordination problem may require no continuing subsidy after successful coordination. Government may facilitate commitments or provide complementary inputs, but guarantees still create risk and incentive costs even if success involves no payout. Instruments should target the interdependent activities, with [[实施能力诊断|implementation-capacity diagnosis]] checking commitments, information and oversight.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Rodrik（2004），[Industrial Policy for the Twenty-First Century](https://downloads.unido.org/ot/47/88/4788272/20001-_23093.pdf#page=14)，UNIDO2004年9月版，印刷12–14页（PDF14–16）：支持固定成本与本地互补投入的协调机制、私人协调的可能性、成功协调不必依赖持续补贴、担保的激励问题，以及应针对相关活动选择工具。
- 0、−4、6的双项目收益是教学构造，仅展示个别行动与共同行动的差别，不是实证估计，也未加入项目以外的社会成本与收益。由该构造不能推出具体政府方案的社会净收益。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rodrik supplies the coordination mechanism and boundaries concerning private solutions, continuing subsidies, guarantees and instrument targeting. The payoff construction is illustrative, omits effects outside the two projects, and does not establish the social net benefit of a government intervention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
