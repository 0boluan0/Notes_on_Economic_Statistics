# 市净率是普通股市场价格与同口径每股账面净资产的比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price-to-book compares an ordinary share's market price with book value per share on a matched equity basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市净率以[[每股净资产]]为账面参照：$PB=P/BVPS$，其中 $BVPS\ne0$。分子和分母须对应同一普通股权益，写清市价日期与账面权益报告日；用归母普通股市值除集团全部权益，会把少数股东权益混入分母。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
P/B uses [[每股净资产|book value per share]] as its accounting reference: $PB=P/BVPS$ for non-zero BVPS. Match ordinary equity scope and state the price and reporting dates. Parent ordinary market capitalization over total group equity incorrectly includes non-controlling interests in the denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如普通股价格 12 元、对应 BVPS 为 8 元，市净率为 1.5 倍。只有在股数与权益归属一致时，才能约去每股式中的股数，得到“普通股市值／普通股账面权益”。多股类、回购或财报日期后增发都需要额外核对。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A price of 12 and BVPS of 8 give P/B of 1.5. Shares cancel to produce ordinary market capitalization over ordinary book equity only with matched shares and attribution. Multiple classes, repurchases, and post-report issues require additional checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市净率比较的是市场评价与会计计量，不是市场价与保证兑付额。[[账面净资产不是股价下限]]，所以低于 1 仍可能反映未来低盈利或资产损失；高于 1 也可能反映未入账资源和盈利机会。负权益时负倍数不能按通常贵贱排序；跨公司比较还需统一计量、合并与权益范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
P/B compares market assessment with accounting measurement, not a guaranteed redemption amount. [[账面净资产不是股价下限|Book value is not a price floor]]: values below one may reflect weak expected earnings or losses, and values above one may reflect unrecognized resources or opportunities. Negative equity invalidates ordinary cheapness ranking; measurement and consolidation scope also affect comparisons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
