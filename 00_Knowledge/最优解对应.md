<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Solution correspondence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*最优解对应*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **solution correspondence** answers “which choices are best?” for every parameter setting. Its output contains all the choices that achieve the [[值函数|optimal value]], so a tie is kept as several answers rather than broken silently. This differs from a value function: the value is a score; the solution correspondence returns the inputs that produce that score.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每给定一个参数条件，**最优解对应**回答“哪些选择最好”。它收集所有达到[[值函数|最优值]]的选择，因此遇到并列最优会保留全部答案，而不悄悄替你挑一个。值函数的输出是分数；最优解对应的输出是产生该分数的输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use $\theta$ for the parameter, $X(\theta)$ for its feasible choices, and $f(x,\theta)$ for a real-valued score at each feasible pair. The word **correspondence** means a rule with a set as its output. The notation $\operatorname*{argmax}$ asks for the arguments, or inputs, attaining the largest value; it does not mean the largest value itself. The comparison is with every feasible choice, not merely nearby choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $\theta$ 表示参数、$X(\theta)$ 表示该参数下的可行选择、$f(x,\theta)$ 表示每个可行点的实数分数。**对应**就是输出为集合的规则。$\operatorname*{argmax}$ 中的 arg 指输入：要找的是取得最优值的输入，而不是最优值这个数；比较范围是全部可行选择，不只是邻近选择。

> [!definition] Keep every maximizing choice · 保留全部最优选择
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Given $v(\theta)=\sup_{x\in X(\theta)}f(x,\theta)$, the solution correspondence is $S(\theta)=\arg\max_{x\in X(\theta)}f(x,\theta)=\{x\in X(\theta):f(x,\theta)=v(\theta)\}$. Its value is a set that may be empty, a singleton, or multivalued.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 给定
> $$
> v(\theta)=\sup_{x\in X(\theta)} f(x,\theta),
> $$
> 最优解对应（solution correspondence）定义为
> $$
> S(\theta)
> =\operatorname*{argmax}_{x\in X(\theta)} f(x,\theta)
> =\{x\in X(\theta):f(x,\theta)=v(\theta)\}.
> $$
> $S(\theta)$ 的值是一个集合，可以为空、单点或含有多个选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why it is a correspondence rather than automatically an ordinary function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么叫“对应”而不默认是普通函数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A parameter need not identify a unique optimizer. Maximising the zero function on $[-1,1]$ makes every feasible point optimal. Forcing this set into one selected path discards multiplicity and may create artificial jumps.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参数不一定唯一决定一个最优选择。例如在 $[-1,1]$ 上最大化零函数，每个可行点都最优，所以 $S=[-1,1]$。若把这个集合强行写成某一条单值路径，会丢掉多解信息，并且人为选择出可能跳跃的分支。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **selection** chooses one member $s(\theta)\in S(\theta)$ at each parameter where it is defined. It can only be defined on parameters with nonempty solution sets. For the parameter-independent zero objective on $[-1,1]$, choosing zero for every parameter is continuous; choosing $-1$ for negative parameters and $1$ otherwise is discontinuous at zero. Both selections are optimal, while the full solution correspondence stays $[-1,1]$ throughout. A selection therefore includes an extra tie-breaking rule and need not inherit continuity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条**选择函数**在每个定义了它的参数处，只挑一个成员 $s(\theta)\in S(\theta)$；它只能定义在最优解集合非空的参数上。对于 $[-1,1]$ 上不随参数变化的零目标，始终选零是连续的；参数为负时选 $-1$、否则选 $1$，则在零点不连续。这两种选择都最优，但完整的最优解对应始终是 $[-1,1]$。因此，选择函数额外包含了一个打破平局的规则，不自动具有连续性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now let the score depend on the parameter. Positive $\theta$ rewards larger $x$, negative $\theta$ rewards smaller $x$, and zero makes all scores equal. This produces the following simple family.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个最小的参数例子是
$$
\max_{x\in[-1,1]}\theta x.
$$
它的最优解对应为
$$
S(\theta)=
\begin{cases}
\{-1\},&\theta<0,\\
[-1,1],&\theta=0,\\
\{1\},&\theta>0.
\end{cases}
$$
零点的多值不是记号故障；它正确保留了该参数下的全部最优选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\max_{x\in[-1,1]}\theta x$, the solution set is $\{-1\}$ for negative $\theta$, all of $[-1,1]$ at zero, and $\{1\}$ for positive $\theta$. The multivalued point is genuine information, not a notation failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正的 $\theta$ 使目标随 $x$ 增大，故最大值在右端点；负的 $\theta$ 使目标随 $x$ 减小，故最大值在左端点；$\theta=0$ 时每个点的分数都是零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The corresponding value is $v(\theta)=|\theta|$. For example, at $\theta=-2$ the optimal score is $2$ but the solution set is $\{-1\}$; at $\theta=0$ the score is $0$ and the solution set is the whole interval. Writing $S(\theta)=\{1\}$ keeps the output a set even when it has only one member. No choice of the zero-parameter member can make a selection of this family continuous across zero: the two side limits are $-1$ and $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对应的值函数为 $v(\theta)=|\theta|$。例如，$\theta=-2$ 时最优分数是 $2$，最优解集合却是 $\{-1\}$；$\theta=0$ 时分数是 $0$，最优解集合却是整个区间。即使只有一个成员，写 $S(\theta)=\{1\}$ 也仍是在输出一个集合。在这个例子里，无论零参数处挑哪个成员，选择函数都不能跨过零点连续，因为左右极限分别是 $-1$ 和 $1$。

## What empty, singleton, and multivalued solution sets mean
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 空集、单点与多点各自说明什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An empty solution set may reflect nonattainment or infeasibility. A singleton means a unique optimizer. A multivalued set means several choices share the same optimal value; the value function remains single-valued.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $S(\theta)=\varnothing$：上确界可能有限，但没有被取得；也可能根本没有可行点。
- $S(\theta)$ 是单点：该参数下的最优选择唯一。
- $S(\theta)$ 含多点：多个选择共享同一最优值；这不妨碍 [[值函数]] 本身是单值的。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a real-valued objective, unboundedness also gives an empty solution set: maximizing $x$ on $\mathbb R$ has value $+\infty$, but no real $x$ attains it. Thus an empty $S(\theta)$ alone does not tell us whether the problem is infeasible, unbounded, or has a finite unattained supremum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实值目标向上无界时，最优解集合也为空：在 $\mathbb R$ 上最大化 $x$，值为 $+\infty$，却没有实数输入取得它。因此，只看 $S(\theta)$ 为空，不能分辨不可行、无界，还是有限上确界未取得。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nonemptiness requires an attainment result, and uniqueness requires separate structure such as strict concavity. Existence of a value does not prove that the solution correspondence is nonempty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非空性需要取得性定理，唯一性需要严格凹性等另外的结构。“值函数存在”不能代替“最优解对应非空”的证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These claims can be proved directly in an example or supplied by a theorem. For a fixed parameter, continuity of $f(\cdot,\theta)$ on a nonempty compact $X(\theta)$ gives an attained maximum by [[极值定理|the extreme-value theorem]]. If the feasible set is also convex and the objective strictly concave there, it has at most one maximizer; see [[严格凹不保证解存在|strict concavity and attainment]]. Strict concavity supplies the “at most one” part, not nonemptiness. Smoothness of a chosen optimizer and smoothness of the value are separate again, as [[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微|the optimizer-jump examples]] show.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体例子中可以直接证明这些结论，也可以调用定理。固定一个参数后，若 $X(\theta)$ 非空且紧，$f(\cdot,\theta)$ 在其上连续，[[极值定理]]保证最大值取得。若再有可行集凸、目标严格凹，则最大点至多一个，见[[严格凹不保证解存在|严格凹性与取得性]]；严格凹只负责“至多一个”，不负责非空。所选优化器与价值是否光滑，又是另外的问题，见 [[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微]]。

> [!question]- A value without a solution · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When maximising $x$ over $(0,1)$, what are the optimal value and the solution correspondence?
>
> **Answer:** The optimal value is the supremum one, but it is unattained, so the solution correspondence is empty.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $(0,1)$ 上最大化 $x$ 时，值函数和最优解对应分别是什么？
>
> **答案：** 最优值是上确界 $1$，但该值不被取得，所以最优解对应为空集。
