# 选择行业可比样本应先固定分析目的再核对业务与经济驱动因素
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Select industry peers by fixing the analytical purpose and then comparing business activities and economic drivers.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先写清要比较什么，再决定哪些业务能放在同一组。统计分类给出候选样本，经济可比性决定保留哪些样本；两者不是同一步。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First specify what is being compared, then decide which businesses belong together. Statistical classifications supply candidates; economic comparability determines which candidates remain. These are different steps.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 固定目的与边界，例如比较某地区零售业务利润率，而不是比较整家跨国公司的估值。
2. 核对主要产品、客户需求、地域、渠道，以及成本和资本密集程度；多业务公司优先取相关分部。
3. 记录分类体系、版本及纳入排除理由；需要时保留窄组和宽组，检验结论对选样的敏感性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Fix the purpose and boundary, such as comparing regional retail margins rather than whole multinational-company valuations.<br>
**2.** Compare products, customer needs, geography, channels, cost drivers, and capital intensity; use relevant segments for diversified firms.<br>
**3.** Record the classification system, version, and inclusion decisions. Where useful, compare narrow and broad peer groups to test sensitivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同属“食品饮料”的原料供应商与品牌零售商，可能面对不同毛利率、营运资本和议价关系。反过来，分类编码不同的业务也可能争夺同一种客户需求。不能为了得到期望的平均数而事后改组。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ingredient supplier and a branded retailer may share a food-and-beverage label yet have different margins, working-capital needs, and bargaining relationships. Businesses with different codes may compete for the same customer need. Peer groups should not be revised after the fact merely to obtain a desired average.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
