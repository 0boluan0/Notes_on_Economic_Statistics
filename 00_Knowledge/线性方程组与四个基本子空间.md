# 线性方程组与四个基本子空间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Linear systems and the four fundamental subspaces*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 判断 $Ax=b$ 是否有解、有多少解，以及矩阵把输入空间中的哪些方向送到输出空间哪里。
> **具体锚点：** 三个方程不一定给出三条独立信息；消元后出现自由变量，意味着解沿某些零空间方向移动而不改变 $Ax$。
> **核心难点：** 行空间、列空间、零空间和左零空间位于不同维度，不能只凭图形印象混用。
> **为什么重要：** 最小二乘、回归识别、Markov 链、优化和数值计算都以秩与子空间为骨架。
> **继续：** 无精确解时进入 [[正交投影与最小二乘]]；研究反复作用时进入 [[特征值、对角化与线性动力系统|行列式、特征值与线性动力系统]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** This card determines whether $Ax=b$ is solvable, whether its solution is unique, and which input directions a matrix preserves or erases.
> **Concrete anchor:** Three equations need not contain three independent restrictions. A free variable moves a solution along a null-space direction without changing $Ax$.
> **Central difficulty:** Column and left-null spaces live in $\mathbb R^m$, while row and null spaces live in $\mathbb R^n$; their roles cannot be interchanged.
> **Why it matters:** Least squares, regression identification, Markov chains, optimisation, and numerical computation all depend on rank and subspaces.
> **Continue with:** Use [[正交投影与最小二乘|orthogonal projection and least squares]] when an exact solution fails, and [[特征值、对角化与线性动力系统|eigenvalues and linear dynamics]] for repeated applications.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 消元、主元与 LU
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Elimination, pivots, and LU factorisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

高斯消元用可逆行操作把系统化成阶梯形。主元列给出约束方向，非主元变量成为自由变量。若不换行，消元矩阵的逆积形成下三角 $L$，得到 $A=LU$；需要换行时写 $PA=LU$。计算过程中应把“对方程做行操作”和“改变列空间中的原列”区分开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gaussian elimination uses invertible row operations to produce echelon form. Pivot columns encode constraints, and nonpivot variables are free. Without row exchanges, inverses of the elimination matrices form a lower-triangular $L$, giving $A=LU$; with exchanges, $PA=LU$. Distinguish row operations on equations from replacing the original columns used to describe the column space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 可解性与完整解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Solvability and the complete solution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$Ax=b$ 有解当且仅当 $b\in C(A)$，等价于 $b$ 与左零空间 $N(A^T)$ 中每个向量正交。若有一个特解 $x_p$，全部解为 $x=x_p+x_n$，其中 $x_n\in N(A)$。唯一解要求列向量独立；对方阵这与满秩、非零行列式和可逆等价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$Ax=b$ is solvable exactly when $b\in C(A)$, equivalently when $b$ is orthogonal to every vector in the left null space $N(A^T)$. Given one particular solution $x_p$, every solution is $x=x_p+x_n$ with $x_n\in N(A)$. Uniqueness requires independent columns; for a square matrix this is equivalent to full rank, a nonzero determinant, and invertibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 四个基本子空间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The four fundamental subspaces*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $m\times n$ 矩阵，$C(A)\subseteq\mathbb R^m$、$N(A^T)\subseteq\mathbb R^m$；$C(A^T)\subseteq\mathbb R^n$、$N(A)\subseteq\mathbb R^n$。前两者互为正交补，后两者也互为正交补。若秩为 $r$，维数分别为 $r,m-r,r,n-r$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an $m\times n$ matrix, $C(A)$ and $N(A^T)$ are subspaces of $\mathbb R^m$, while $C(A^T)$ and $N(A)$ lie in $\mathbb R^n$. The first pair and second pair are orthogonal complements. If the rank is $r$, their respective dimensions are $r,m-r,r,n-r$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 基、维数与秩
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bases, dimension, and rank*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基必须同时满足张成和线性无关。秩是矩阵真正传递的独立方向数，既等于列空间维数也等于行空间维数。rank–nullity 定理 $\operatorname{rank}(A)+\dim N(A)=n$ 把约束数和自由度精确连接起来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A basis must both span and be linearly independent. Rank is the number of independent directions genuinely transmitted by a matrix and equals both column-space and row-space dimension. The rank–nullity theorem, $\operatorname{rank}(A)+\dim N(A)=n$, connects independent restrictions to degrees of freedom.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 矩阵乘法的结构读法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Structural readings of matrix multiplication*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$Ax$ 是 A 的列向量按 $x$ 加权；$AB$ 的每一列是 A 作用在 B 对应列上的结果；外积和秩一分解把矩阵看作若干简单映射之和。选择哪种读法取决于要解释线性组合、复合变换还是低秩结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$Ax$ is a linear combination of the columns of $A$ with weights $x$. Each column of $AB$ is $A$ applied to the corresponding column of $B$. Outer products and rank-one decompositions view a matrix as a sum of simple maps. Choose the reading that matches linear combination, composition, or low-rank structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：无解来自左零空间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: inconsistency detected by the left null space*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$，则 $y=(1,1,-1)^T\in N(A^T)$。任何可解的 $b$ 都必须满足 $y^Tb=b_1+b_2-b_3=0$。所以 $b=(1,1,3)^T$ 不在 $C(A)$ 中，无需完整消元就知道 $Ax=b$ 无解。这个条件也是最小二乘中残差落在左零空间的来源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$. Then $y=(1,1,-1)^T\in N(A^T)$. Every solvable right-hand side must satisfy $y^Tb=b_1+b_2-b_3=0$. Thus $b=(1,1,3)^T$ is not in $C(A)$, so $Ax=b$ is inconsistent without performing full elimination. The same orthogonality explains why least-squares residuals lie in the left null space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*

### 用自己的话解释为什么全部解是“特解 + 零空间”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why every solution is “a particular solution plus the null space.”*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 两个解之差必被 A 映到 0，因此所有解只会沿零空间方向偏离任一特解；反过来加上任何零空间向量也不改变右端 b。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The difference between any two solutions is mapped to zero, so all solutions differ from one particular solution only along null-space directions; adding any null vector also leaves $b$ unchanged.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 如何不求解就判断某个 $b$ 不可能属于 $C(A)$？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How can one show without solving that $b$ is not in $C(A)$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 找到 $y\in N(A^T)$ 使 $y^Tb\ne0$；列空间中的向量都必须与左零空间正交。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Find $y\in N(A^T)$ with $y^Tb\ne0$; every vector in the column space must be orthogonal to the left null space.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一个 $m\times n$ 矩阵满列秩意味着什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does full column rank mean for an $m\times n$ matrix?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 秩为 n、零空间只有 0，因此若 $Ax=b$ 有解就唯一；但当 $m>n$ 时并不保证每个 b 都有解。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The rank is $n$ and the null space is trivial, so any solution is unique. If $m>n$, however, not every right-hand side need be attainable.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 行空间与零空间为什么位于同一个环境空间？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why do the row space and null space live in the same ambient space?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> A 的每一行有 n 个坐标，而零空间由 n 维输入 $x$ 组成；方程 $Ax=0$ 正好说 x 与每一行正交。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each row of $A$ has $n$ coordinates, and null-space inputs are also $n$-dimensional. The equation $Ax=0$ says precisely that $x$ is orthogonal to every row.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
