# Nonnormal transient growth
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*非正规矩阵的瞬态放大*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An evolving state can grow at first and still end up at zero. **Transient growth** describes that temporary amplification. Here we compare the length of the state at a finite step with its initial length, using the same coordinates and the standard Euclidean length throughout. The [[谱半径|spectral radius]] controls whether repeated powers eventually vanish; it does not, by itself, tell us how large the intervening states can become.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个变化中的状态可以先变大，最后仍回到零。**瞬态放大**指这种暂时的增长。本卡在相同坐标下，始终使用标准欧氏长度，把某个有限步的状态长度与初始长度比较。[[谱半径]]控制反复相乘最终是否趋零，却不能单独告诉我们中途会变得多大。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A decaying second coordinate keeps feeding the first
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*第二个分量在衰减，却不断向第一个输送*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the fixed real matrix below, with a real coupling parameter $M$. The state $x_k$ is a two-coordinate column at integer step $k\ge0$, and $x_{k+1}=A_Mx_k$ means one [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] per step. A superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]] when writing a column inline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑下面的固定实矩阵，$M$ 是实数，表示两个分量之间的传递强度。状态 $x_k$ 是整数步 $k\ge0$ 时的二维列向量；$x_{k+1}=A_Mx_k$ 表示每步做一次[[矩阵乘法]]。行内写列向量时，上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。

$$A_M=\begin{pmatrix}0.9&M\\0&0.9\end{pmatrix},\qquad
x_{k+1,1}=0.9x_{k,1}+Mx_{k,2},\qquad x_{k+1,2}=0.9x_{k,2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both eigenvalues are $0.9$, so $\rho(A_M)=0.9<1$ for every fixed $M$. The [[矩阵幂趋零判据|vanishing-power criterion]] therefore gives $A_M^k\to0$. But the second coordinate contributes to the first at every step. Multiplication, or the [[Jordan块的幂|finite block-power calculation]], gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个特征值都是 $0.9$，所以对每个固定 $M$ 都有 $\rho(A_M)=0.9<1$。[[矩阵幂趋零判据]]保证 $A_M^k\to0$。不过第二个分量每步都会向第一个分量作贡献；直接相乘，或使用[[Jordan块的幂|块矩阵的有限幂计算]]，得到

$$A_M^k=\begin{pmatrix}0.9^k&kM\,0.9^{k-1}\\0&0.9^k\end{pmatrix}\quad(k\ge1),\qquad A_M^0=I_2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matrix $I_2$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity]], representing zero steps. To see the off-diagonal factor, start at $x_0=(0,1)^T$. At step $k$, each of the $k$ transfers from the second coordinate has accumulated the same total factor $M\,0.9^{k-1}$; adding them gives $kM\,0.9^{k-1}$. The geometric decay eventually beats the factor $k$, but not necessarily in the first few steps.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$I_2$ 是[[单位矩阵]]，表示零步。要理解右上角的因子，从 $x_0=(0,1)^T$ 出发：到第 $k$ 步时，从第二个分量发生的 $k$ 次传递，每次累计的因子都是 $M\,0.9^{k-1}$；相加便得到 $kM\,0.9^{k-1}$。几何衰减最终会压过 $k$，但前几步未必如此。

> [!example] Growth before decay / 先放大，再衰减
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Choose $M=1$ and the unit initial state $(0,1)^T$. The first two states are $(1,0.9)^T$ and $(1.8,0.81)^T$. Their [[欧氏范数|Euclidean lengths]] are $\sqrt{1.81}\approx1.345$ and $\sqrt{3.8961}\approx1.974$, both larger than the initial length $1$. Nevertheless, both coordinates eventually vanish. Increasing $|M|$ makes the first output length $\sqrt{M^2+0.81}$ arbitrarily large without changing either eigenvalue.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $M=1$、单位初值 $(0,1)^T$，前两步状态为 $(1,0.9)^T$、$(1.8,0.81)^T$，[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]分别为 $\sqrt{1.81}\approx1.345$、$\sqrt{3.8961}\approx1.974$，都大于初始长度1；但两个分量最终都趋零。增大 $|M|$ 会让第一步长度 $\sqrt{M^2+0.81}$ 任意大，却不改变任何特征值。

## Specify the length and the input being tested
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先明确长度，也明确正在检查哪个输入*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed $n\times n$ real or complex matrix $A$, with integer $n\ge1$, write $\|x\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_{j=1}^n|x_j|^2}$. This is the length from the standard coordinate [[内积|inner product]]; $|x_j|$ is the real or complex modulus. The [[谱范数|spectral norm]] of $A^k$ is the largest gain over all unit initial states:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对固定的 $n\times n$ 实矩阵或复矩阵 $A$，整数 $n\ge1$，记 $\|x\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_{j=1}^n|x_j|^2}$，这是标准坐标[[内积]]给出的长度；$|x_j|$ 表示实数或复数坐标的模。$A^k$ 的[[谱范数]]是全部单位初值中最大的放大倍数：

$$\|A^k\|_2=\max_{\|x_0\|_2=1}\|A^kx_0\|_2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $\|A^k\|_2>1$ means that some initial state is longer at step $k$ than at the start. It need not be the state used in one experiment. If the application supplies a particular $x_0$, examine $\|A^kx_0\|_2$ as well. Fix the norm and coordinate units before comparing gains: reweighting coordinates changes the length being measured.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 $\|A^k\|_2>1$ 表示存在一个初值，其第 $k$ 步长度超过起始长度；它未必是某次实验实际使用的初值。若应用指定了 $x_0$，还应检查 $\|A^kx_0\|_2$。比较放大倍数前，要固定范数和坐标单位；重新给各坐标加权，会改变所测量的长度。

## Why normal matrices provide a useful comparison
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么正规矩阵是有用的对照*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[Normal 矩阵|normal matrix]] satisfies $A^*A=AA^*$. The star means conjugate transpose: transpose the matrix and conjugate each complex entry; for a real matrix this is just $A^T$. Nonnormal means that these two products differ. For the real family above, direct multiplication gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Normal 矩阵|正规矩阵]]满足 $A^*A=AA^*$。星号表示共轭转置：先转置，再对每个复元素取共轭；实矩阵的 $A^*$ 就是 $A^T$。非正规表示这两个乘积不同。对上面的实矩阵族，直接相乘得到

$$A_M^TA_M=\begin{pmatrix}0.81&0.9M\\0.9M&0.81+M^2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A_MA_M^T=\begin{pmatrix}0.81+M^2&0.9M\\0.9M&0.81\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
They differ when $M\ne0$. For a normal matrix, the [[Normal 矩阵谱定理|normal spectral theorem]] instead supplies $A=U\Lambda U^*$, where the [[Unitary 矩阵|unitary matrix]] $U$ preserves this length and $\Lambda$ is [[对角矩阵|diagonal]], containing the eigenvalues. Consequently, for integers $k\ge1$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只要 $M\ne0$，二者就不同。正规矩阵则由[[Normal 矩阵谱定理]]得到 $A=U\Lambda U^*$：[[Unitary 矩阵|酉矩阵]] $U$ 保持这里的长度，[[对角矩阵]] $\Lambda$ 放各特征值。因此，对整数 $k\ge1$，

$$\|A^k\|_2=\|U\Lambda^kU^*\|_2=\max_j|\lambda_j|^k=\rho(A)^k.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $k=0$, the norm is $\|I_n\|_2=1$. If $A$ is normal and $\rho(A)<1$, every step contracts every nonzero state in this length: $\|Ax\|_2\le\rho(A)\|x\|_2<\|x\|_2$. The normality assumption is what makes the eigenvalue magnitudes sufficient for this stronger statement. A general nonnormal matrix can mix directions instead of acting along an orthonormal eigenbasis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$k=0$ 时直接取 $\|I_n\|_2=1$。若 $A$ 正规且 $\rho(A)<1$，每步都会使每个非零状态在这个长度下缩小：$\|Ax\|_2\le\rho(A)\|x\|_2<\|x\|_2$。正是正规性让特征值的模足以支持这个更强结论；一般非正规矩阵会混合方向，而不是沿标准正交特征基逐项作用。

> [!warning] Nonnormality permits growth; it does not force it / 非正规允许放大，并不强迫放大
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $C=\begin{pmatrix}0.5&0.1\\0&0.5\end{pmatrix}$. It is nonnormal by the same product comparison, but $\|C\|_2\le\|C\|_F=\sqrt{0.51}<1$, where the [[Frobenius范数|Frobenius norm]] takes the square root of the sum of squared entry magnitudes. The bound follows by applying the [[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式|Cauchy–Schwarz inequality]] to each row. Therefore $\|Cx\|_2\le\sqrt{0.51}\|x\|_2$, so every nonzero real state shrinks at every step. Nonnormality alone is not a growth test.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $C=\begin{pmatrix}0.5&0.1\\0&0.5\end{pmatrix}$，同样比较两个乘积可知它非正规；但 $\|C\|_2\le\|C\|_F=\sqrt{0.51}<1$。其中[[Frobenius范数]]把全部元素模平方求和再开平方；对每一行应用[[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式]]可得上述界。因此 $\|Cx\|_2\le\sqrt{0.51}\|x\|_2$，每个非零实状态每一步都会缩小。仅知道非正规，并不能判定一定发生放大。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite time window, inspect the powers or the specified response; [[Schur分解|Schur factorization]] can provide coordinates without requiring an eigenvector basis. In continuous time, the analogous object is $e^{tA}$, with an example in [[连续系统谱稳定性|continuous-time asymptotic stability]]. Conversely, a single decaying response cannot certify the whole system: [[单轨迹与系统稳定性|unexcited or unobserved modes]] are a different diagnostic limitation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在有限时间窗口内，应检查矩阵幂或指定响应；[[Schur分解]]能提供不要求特征向量基的坐标表示。连续时间中的对应对象是 $e^{tA}$，相关例子见[[连续系统谱稳定性]]。反过来，一条衰减响应也不能单独认证整个系统：[[单轨迹与系统稳定性|未被激活或未被观测的模式]]是另一层诊断边界。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Arbenz–Kressner, ETH Zürich, *Solving Large Scale Eigenvalue Problems*, §6.2, Theorem 6.1 and Remark 6.3, printed pp.113–114 / PDF pp.119–120](https://people.inf.ethz.ch/arbenz/ewp/Lnotes/lsevp2012.pdf#page=119): eventual decay can coexist with early growth; its triangular example motivates the preserved $0.9,M$ family. The powers, transfers and contraction counterexample above are derived directly.
- [Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*, fourth edition, definition 7.18 and theorem 7.31, printed pp.235–236/246–247 / PDF pp.249–250/260–261](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=249): normality and the complex orthonormal eigenbasis. The power-norm identity follows from this basis and length preservation.
- [[03_Positive Definite Matrices and Applications#Jordan块的幂与Jordan块的指数|Permanent Jordan-block calculation]]: the finite $k\lambda^{k-1}$ factor; [LAPACK95, Nonsymmetric Eigenproblems](https://www.netlib.org/lapack95/lug95/node34.html) supports the Schur computational pointer, not a blanket bound on transients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- ETH讲义印刷113–114页核对最终衰减与早期增长并存；其三角矩阵例子支持保留的 $0.9,M$ 矩阵族。矩阵幂、分量传递及收缩反例均直接推导。
- Axler定义7.18与定理7.31核对正规性和复标准正交特征基；幂的范数等式再由该基及长度保持推出。
- 永久Jordan块计算保留 $k\lambda^{k-1}$ 的来源语境；LAPACK95原文支持Schur计算入口，不被用作瞬态大小的统一上界。
