# Does Mahalanobis distance require normality?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*马氏距离需要正态性吗？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
You can measure a [[马氏距离|Mahalanobis distance]] without assuming that the observations follow a normal distribution. The distance answers **how far apart two points are under a chosen scale**. A probability statement such as “only 5% of observations lie farther away” answers a further question about the population that generates the points. That question requires a probability model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算[[马氏距离]]不需要先假设观测服从正态分布。距离回答的是**在选定尺度下，两点相隔多远**。“总体中只有5%的观测会离得更远”则进一步涉及产生这些点的总体，需要概率模型才能回答。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For finite points $x,y\in\mathbb R^p$ and a fixed real symmetric [[正定矩阵|positive-definite scale matrix]] $\Sigma$, the number
$$d_\Sigma(x,y)=\sqrt{(x-y)^T\Sigma^{-1}(x-y)}$$
is well-defined. Positive definiteness supplies the inverse and a positive scale in every direction. No density, independence or Gaussian assumption occurs in this definition. A scale matrix may be chosen even when it is not a population covariance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限坐标点 $x,y\in\mathbb R^p$，只要选定固定的实对称[[正定矩阵|正定尺度矩阵]] $\Sigma$，上式便有定义。正定性保证逆矩阵存在、各方向尺度为正；这里没有密度、独立性或 Gaussian 条件。选定的尺度矩阵也不一定是总体协方差。

## Geometry and probability calibration
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 几何计算与概率校准是两层问题

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose now that $X$ is a [[联合Gaussian|jointly Gaussian random vector]] with true mean $\mu$ and positive-definite covariance $\Sigma$, written $X\sim N_p(\mu,\Sigma)$. Whitening gives $Z=\Sigma^{-1/2}(X-\mu)$ with **independent standard normal coordinates**, not merely coordinates with variance one. Therefore
$$D^2=(X-\mu)^T\Sigma^{-1}(X-\mu)=\sum_{j=1}^pZ_j^2\sim\chi_p^2.$$
The [[卡方分布|chi-square distribution]] $\chi_p^2$ is the law of a sum of $p$ independent standard-normal squares. The [[Gaussian马氏平方律|Gaussian squared-distance theorem]] explains the derivation and how its quantiles give coverage probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

进一步假设 $X$ 是[[联合Gaussian|联合 Gaussian 随机向量]]，真实均值为 $\mu$、正定协方差为 $\Sigma$，记作 $X\sim N_p(\mu,\Sigma)$。白化后 $Z=\Sigma^{-1/2}(X-\mu)$ 的坐标是**相互独立的标准正态变量**，不仅仅是方差为一。因此平方距离是 $p$ 个独立标准正态平方之和，服从[[卡方分布]] $\chi_p^2$。[[Gaussian马氏平方律]]说明推导及分位数怎样给出覆盖概率。

> [!example] A distance exists but its distribution is different · 距离存在，分布却不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X_1,X_2$ be independent, each equal to $-1$ or $1$ with probability $1/2$. Then $\mu=(0,0)^T$ and $\Sigma=I_2$: both variances are $1$ and the covariance is $0$. Every possible point is at the same squared distance from the mean:
> $$D^2=X_1^2+X_2^2=2.$$
> This is a constant, whereas $\chi_2^2$ has variance $4$. The distance calculation is valid; the chi-square reference distribution is not valid for this population.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个坐标独立，分别以一半概率取 $-1$ 或 $1$，于是均值为零、协方差为单位矩阵。四个可能的点到均值的平方距离全是 $2$，没有随机变化；$\chi_2^2$ 的方差却是 $4$。距离计算完全有效，但这一个总体不满足卡方校准。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For skewed, heavy-tailed or discrete data, a chosen positive-definite scale still defines geometry. If a heavy-tailed population has no finite covariance, one must not call an arbitrarily selected finite matrix “its covariance.” Choosing another scale is possible, but neither that choice nor whitening supplies a Gaussian law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对偏斜、重尾或离散数据，选定正定尺度仍能定义几何距离。若重尾总体的有限协方差不存在，就不能把任意选定的有限矩阵称作“它的协方差”。可以选择其他尺度，但选尺度或做白化都不会自动产生 Gaussian 分布。

> [!warning] Estimated parameters change the question · 估计参数后要重新核查
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Replacing $\mu,\Sigma$ by the sample mean and sample covariance changes the statistic. Even under Gaussian sampling, distances computed from the same fitted sample do not automatically have the exact $\chi_p^2$ law above. An independent new observation with parameters estimated from training data also needs a separate calculation. See the examples in [[Gaussian马氏平方律|the squared-distance theorem]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用样本均值和样本协方差代替真实参数，会改变统计量。即使总体 Gaussian，同一批数据拟合后再计算的距离，也不会自动遵守上面的精确卡方分布。对独立新观测使用训练数据估计的参数，同样需要另外推导，例子见[[Gaussian马氏平方律]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The usable distinction is therefore: **positive definiteness justifies the distance; a verified distributional argument justifies a tail probability**. A [[边际与马氏图不证联合Gaussian|normal-looking diagnostic plot]] may provide evidence for a model, but cannot prove that its exact finite-sample reference law holds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，**正定性支持距离计算，经过核实的分布推导才支持尾概率判断**。[[边际与马氏图不证联合Gaussian|看起来正常的诊断图]]可以提供模型线索，却不能证明精确的有限样本参考分布成立。
