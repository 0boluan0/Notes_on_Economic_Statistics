# 合同重定价决定利率变化何时进入既有现金流但不决定主体何时开始调整行为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Contract repricing determines when rate changes enter existing cash flows, not when people begin adjusting their behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一次政策变化对不同主体的传导，先取决于实际合同利率跟随了多少、何时重置，再取决于主体怎样反应。[[合同期限、重定价期限与行为期限]]已经定义三种时间尺度；本卡把它们应用到政策传导，不把“固定利率”解释为“整个家庭暂时不受影响”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Transmission depends first on how much a contract rate changes and when it resets, then on the holder's response. [[合同期限、重定价期限与行为期限|Contractual, repricing and behavioural horizons]] provide the underlying distinctions. Fixed interest does not make an entire household temporarily immune to policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设在期初发生政策利率上升 1 个百分点。只隔离随后一年的利息现金流，余额均固定为 100，无摊还、费用、违约、期权行使或对冲，并作如下合同假设：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the policy rate rises by one percentage point at the start of a year. Isolate that year's interest cash flows on unchanged balances of 100, without amortisation, fees, default, option exercise or hedging, under the following contractual assumptions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 对象 | 假设的实际合同反应 | 一年利息变化 |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 期初重置的浮息借款 | 利率从 3% 到 4% | 支付增加 1 |
| 固定期覆盖整年的借款 | 利率仍为 3% | 支付不变 |
| 可调整报价的存款 | 本例仅从 2% 到 2.4% | 收入增加 0.4 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The floating-rate borrower pays 1 more, the fixed-rate borrower has no change in contractual interest, and the depositor receives 0.4 more. These are assumed pass-through rates on separate positions, not a consolidated-sector account.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二行只说本年合同利息不变。家庭仍可能预见固定期结束后偿付增加而先少消费，也可能因收入前景、资产价格或新增贷款报价变化而调整。新贷款是否获批又是另一个问题，不能把观察到的报价视作保证可借的金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second row only fixes current contractual interest. A household may already cut consumption in anticipation of higher payments after fixation ends, or respond to income prospects, asset prices and new-loan quotes. A quote also does not guarantee loan approval or an available amount.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还须追踪利率风险由谁承担。未对冲的银行若持有固定收益资产而融资成本重置，可能经[[银行贷款渠道]]改变新增信贷；对冲会改变风险承担者。由此，固定合同占比上升不自动意味着总传导一定更弱或更慢。借款人与储蓄者现金流合在一起怎样影响支出，见[[现金流再分配渠道]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trace who bears the rate exposure. An unhedged bank holding fixed-yield assets with repricing funding may change new credit through the [[银行贷款渠道|bank lending channel]]; hedging shifts the exposure. More fixed-rate contracts need not mechanically weaken or delay aggregate transmission. See the [[现金流再分配渠道|cash-flow redistribution channel]] for aggregation across borrowers and savers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
