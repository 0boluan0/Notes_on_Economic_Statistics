# 在双方奖品价值均为正、投入连续且成本等于投入的两人全支付竞赛中，公平并列规则下不存在纯策略纳什均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A continuous two-player all-pay contest with positive prize values, unit bid costs, and a fair tie rule has no pure-strategy Nash equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑[[全支付竞赛]]中两位玩家分别以 $v_i>0$ 评价奖品、选择连续投入 $b_i\ge 0$，并按投入一比一承担成本的模型。最高投入者取得奖品，并列时双方各以 $1/2$ 的概率获胜。要证明不存在纯策略均衡，不能只说“竞争太激烈”，而要穷尽任意纯投入组合 $(b_1,b_2)$ 的两类可能。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let player $i$ value the prize at $v_i>0$ and choose a continuous nonnegative bid that costs one unit per unit bid. The nonexistence result follows by exhausting the two possible shapes of a pure profile—equal or unequal bids—not from a loose intuition about intense competition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 情形一：双方并列
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Case One: Tied Bids*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $b_1=b_2=b$，玩家 $i$ 的收益为 $v_i/2-b$。任取 $0<\varepsilon<v_i/2$，玩家 $i$ 把投入提高到 $b+\varepsilon$ 后确定性获胜，收益为

$$
v_i-b-\varepsilon>\frac {v_i}2-b.
$$

所以任何并列都能被略微加价打破，包括 $b=0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At any tie, player $i$ can overbid by an amount smaller than $v_i/2$ and replace a half chance of winning with certainty. Positivity of $v_i$, rather than equality of the two valuations, is what makes this deviation available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 情形二：一方严格领先
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Case Two: One Bid Is Strictly Higher*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不妨设 $b_1>b_2$。若 $b_2>0$，输家把投入降到零，可把收益从 $-b_2$ 提高到 $0$。若 $b_2=0$，赢家可以选择任意 $b_1'\in(0,b_1)$，仍然获胜，却把收益从 $v_1-b_1$ 提高到 $v_1-b_1'$。因此任何严格领先组合也都存在有利偏离。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the losing bid is positive, the loser improves by dropping it to zero. If the losing bid is zero, the winner can retain the win with any smaller positive bid and save cost. Thus no unequal bid profile survives either.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两类组合已经覆盖全部纯策略组合，因此不存在纯策略纳什均衡。问题不是没有均衡，而是均衡必须进入[[全支付竞赛混合均衡|混合策略]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since ties and strict rankings exhaust all pure profiles, no pure-strategy Nash equilibrium exists. The game does have an equilibrium, but it must use mixed strategies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 结论的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary of the Result*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明准确地用到四项条件：双方价值都严格为正、投入可在非负实数上连续调整、成本等于自身投入，以及并列时公平分配奖品。价值不对称本身不会恢复纯均衡；它改变的是混合均衡的支持、原子点和收益。若存在离散投入、零价值玩家、预算约束、非线性成本或不同的分配与并列规则，则必须重新分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof uses positive valuations, a continuous nonnegative bid space, unit bid costs, and a fair tie rule. Valuation asymmetry alone does not restore a pure equilibrium, although it changes the mixed equilibrium. Discrete bids, zero valuations, budgets, nonlinear costs, or alternative allocation and tie rules require a fresh analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 在任意候选 $(b_1,b_2)=(b,0)$、$b>0$ 中，赢家为什么不能被“已经获胜”说服而停止检查偏离？
>
> **答案：** 因为获胜不等于最优。赢家可把投入降到任意更小的正数，仍获胜并严格提高收益。
