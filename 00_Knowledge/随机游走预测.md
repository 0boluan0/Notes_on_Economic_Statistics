# 随机游走的水平预测延续当前值加漂移且预测误差方差随期限累积
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A random walk's level forecast extends the current value by its drift, while forecast uncertainty accumulates with the horizon*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子推导
> 对
> $$y_t=\mu+y_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t,$$
> 向前递推 $h$ 期得到
> $$y_{t+h}=y_t+h\mu+\sum_{j=1}^{h}\varepsilon_{t+j}.$$
> 若未来创新相对 $\mathcal F_t$ 为鞅差，则
> $$E_t(y_{t+h})=y_t+h\mu.$$
> 无漂移时令 $\mu=0$，点预测就是当前水平 $y_t$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Iterating the random walk forward shows that its point forecast is the current level plus accumulated drift. With zero drift, every horizon has the same point forecast $y_t$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预测误差是未来创新之和：
$$
y_{t+h}-E_t(y_{t+h})=\sum_{j=1}^{h}\varepsilon_{t+j}.
$$
若这些未来创新在条件上互不相关且条件方差均为 $\sigma^2$，则
$$
\operatorname{Var}_t\!\left(y_{t+h}-E_t(y_{t+h})\right)=h\sigma^2.
$$
所以方差随 $h$ 线性增长，标准差只按 $\sqrt h$ 增长。这个结论依赖条件正交与同方差；如果只有二阶白噪声假设，置未来创新为零可给出最佳线性预测，却不必等于完整条件期望。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The forecast error is the sum of future innovations. Conditional orthogonality and common variance $\sigma^2$ make its variance $h\sigma^2$, so its standard deviation grows with $\sqrt h$, not with $h$. Under second-order white noise alone, the same recursion supports the best linear forecast but need not identify the full conditional expectation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 预测期从 4 增到 9 时，随机游走预测误差的标准差按什么比例变化？
>
> **答案：** 方差从 $4\sigma^2$ 增到 $9\sigma^2$，标准差从 $2\sigma$ 增到 $3\sigma$，所以乘以 $3/2$，而不是按期限线性增长。
