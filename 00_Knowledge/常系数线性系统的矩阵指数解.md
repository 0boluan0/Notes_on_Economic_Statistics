# Matrix-exponential solution of a linear system
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[常微分方程|differential equation]] specifies how a quantity changes; an initial value specifies where it starts. In a linear system, several coordinates can affect one another, but a fixed matrix $A$ describes all these effects. The [[矩阵指数|matrix exponential]] turns the initial vector into the vector at time $t$. The theorem below explains both why this formula works and why there is no second trajectory with the same start.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[常微分方程]]规定一个量如何变化，初值规定它从哪里出发。线性系统中的几个坐标可以互相影响，但这些影响都由固定矩阵 $A$ 描述。[[矩阵指数]]把初始向量变成时刻 $t$ 的向量。下面的定理同时解释公式为什么成立，以及为什么同一起点不会有第二条轨迹。

> [!example] One coordinate drives another · 一个坐标影响另一个
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider two real functions with $u_1(0)=0$ and $u_2(0)=1$. The second coordinate decays at a rate equal to its current value; the first also decays but receives a contribution from the second:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑两个实函数，初值为 $u_1(0)=0$、$u_2(0)=1$。第二个坐标按当前值的速率衰减；第一个坐标也衰减，同时收到第二个坐标的贡献：
>
> $$\begin{pmatrix}u_1'(t)\\u_2'(t)\end{pmatrix}=\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}}_A\begin{pmatrix}u_1(t)\\u_2(t)\end{pmatrix},\qquad u(0)=\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The solution will be $u_2(t)=e^{-t}$ and $u_1(t)=te^{-t}$. Differentiating gives $u_2'=-u_2$ and $u_1'=e^{-t}-te^{-t}=-u_1+u_2$, and the values at zero are correct. Thus we already have a candidate. To establish that it is the only solution, we need the uniqueness argument below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解将是 $u_2(t)=e^{-t}$、$u_1(t)=te^{-t}$。求导得到 $u_2'=-u_2$、$u_1'=e^{-t}-te^{-t}=-u_1+u_2$，零时刻的值也正确。因此已经找到了候选解；要说明它是唯一解，还需要后面的唯一性论证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## State exactly what is fixed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*明确哪些量保持固定*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$ be a constant matrix and $u_0\in\mathbb F^n$ an initial column vector, where $\mathbb F$ is $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$ and $n\ge1$. Time $t$ is real. The unknown $u(t)$ is a vector-valued function; $u'(t)$ means taking the [[导数|derivative]] of each coordinate. The [[矩阵乘法|product $Au$]] makes every derivative a linear combination of the current coordinates. There is no additional forcing term.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$ 是常矩阵，$u_0\in\mathbb F^n$ 是初始列向量，其中 $\mathbb F$ 为 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$，$n\ge1$；时间 $t$ 为实数。未知量 $u(t)$ 是向量值函数，$u'(t)$ 表示对各坐标分别求[[导数]]。[[矩阵乘法|乘积 $Au$]]让每个坐标的导数成为当前各坐标的线性组合；方程没有额外的外力项。

> [!important] Existence and uniqueness · 存在与唯一
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For all real $t$, the initial-value problem has exactly one continuously differentiable solution:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对全部实时间 $t$，初值问题恰有一个连续可微的解：
>
> $$u'(t)=Au(t),\quad u(0)=u_0\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad u(t)=e^{tA}u_0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## First check the equation and the initial value
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先检查方程与初值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each entry of the defining matrix series is a power series convergent for every $t$. Thus [[幂级数逐项求导|termwise differentiation of power series]] applies. Differentiating $t^k/k!$ gives $t^{k-1}/(k-1)!$ for $k\ge1$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵定义级数的每个元素都是对全部 $t$ 收敛的幂级数，因此可以使用[[幂级数逐项求导]]。当 $k\ge1$ 时，$t^k/k!$ 的导数为 $t^{k-1}/(k-1)!$，于是

$$\frac{d}{dt}e^{tA}=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{t^{k-1}A^k}{(k-1)!}=Ae^{tA}=e^{tA}A,\qquad e^{0A}=I.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The factor $A$ can be taken on either side because every power of $A$ commutes with $A$. Since $u_0$ is constant, the proposed trajectory satisfies $u'(t)=Ae^{tA}u_0=Au(t)$ and $u(0)=Iu_0=u_0$. The series exists for all $t$, so this constructs a global solution rather than merely a short-time one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个 $A$ 的幂都与 $A$ 可交换，所以可以从左边或右边提出 $A$。由于 $u_0$ 不随时间变，候选轨迹满足 $u'(t)=Ae^{tA}u_0=Au(t)$，且 $u(0)=Iu_0=u_0$。级数对所有 $t$ 存在，因此这里构造的是全局解，而非仅在一小段时间内存在的解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Then rule out every other trajectory
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*再排除其他轨迹*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $v(t)$ is any solution with $v(0)=u_0$ on an interval containing zero. Multiply it by $e^{-tA}$ and differentiate, using the [[乘积法则|product rule]] coordinate by coordinate. The order of the matrix factors is retained:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $v(t)$ 是包含零点的某个区间上的任意解，并且 $v(0)=u_0$。将其左乘 $e^{-tA}$，再逐坐标使用[[乘积法则]]求导；保持矩阵因子的顺序：

$$\frac{d}{dt}\bigl(e^{-tA}v(t)\bigr)=-Ae^{-tA}v(t)+e^{-tA}Av(t)=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two terms cancel because $A$ commutes with $e^{-tA}$. A [[导数单调性判据|real function with derivative zero throughout an interval is constant]]. Apply this to each real coordinate, or to each real and imaginary coordinate part for complex vectors. Hence $e^{-tA}v(t)=v(0)=u_0$. Moreover, the same derivative identity gives $\frac{d}{dt}(e^{tA}e^{-tA})=0$; this product is $I$ at zero, hence is $I$ for all $t$. Multiplying the preceding equality by $e^{tA}$ now forces $v(t)=e^{tA}u_0$. Any solution therefore agrees with the constructed one throughout its interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $A$ 与 $e^{-tA}$ 可交换，两项相消。[[导数单调性判据|实函数在整个区间上导数为零，就在该区间保持常数]]。对每个实坐标使用这条结论；复向量则对每个坐标的实部、虚部分别使用。因此 $e^{-tA}v(t)=v(0)=u_0$。同一个导数恒等式还给出 $\frac{d}{dt}(e^{tA}e^{-tA})=0$；乘积在零点为 $I$，所以始终为 $I$。再把前一个等式左乘 $e^{tA}$，得到 $v(t)=e^{tA}u_0$。因此，任何解在自己的整个区间上都与构造出的解一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use the formula without adding unnecessary assumptions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*按公式计算时需要的条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening example, $A=-I+N$ with $N^2=0$. The [[Jordan块的指数|Jordan-block formula]] gives $e^{tA}=e^{-t}\begin{pmatrix}1&t\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$, which produces the two displayed coordinates after multiplication by $u_0$. If $A=0$, the result is simply $u(t)=u_0$. [[对角化计算矩阵指数|Diagonalization]] is another way to compute the exponential when available; diagonalizability is not an assumption of the solution theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头例子中 $A=-I+N$，且 $N^2=0$。[[Jordan块的指数|Jordan 块公式]]给出 $e^{tA}=e^{-t}\begin{pmatrix}1&t\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$；再乘 $u_0$，就得到已经展示的两个坐标。若 $A=0$，则直接得到 $u(t)=u_0$。可用时，[[对角化计算矩阵指数|对角化]]提供另一种计算指数的方式；但可对角化不是解定理的前提。

> [!note] A different starting time · 改变起始时刻
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the prescribed initial value is $u(t_0)=u_0$ for a real time $t_0$, replace elapsed time $t$ by $t-t_0$: $u(t)=e^{(t-t_0)A}u_0$. The theorem requires a constant matrix and the homogeneous equation $u'=Au$. A varying matrix $A(t)$ or an added term $f(t)$ changes the problem; the formula above cannot simply be reused unchanged.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若初值给在实时间 $t_0$，即 $u(t_0)=u_0$，就把经过的时间换成 $t-t_0$：$u(t)=e^{(t-t_0)A}u_0$。本定理要求常矩阵及齐次方程 $u'=Au$。矩阵改为 $A(t)$，或加入 $f(t)$ 项后，问题已经改变，不能原样套用上述公式。
