# 证券交易所是依规则组织证券交易并提供相应设施与市场管理的机构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A securities exchange organizes securities trading under rules and provides the associated facilities and market oversight.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证券交易所规定哪些证券和参与者可以进入、如何申报和成交、如何发布交易信息及处理违规行为。它可以主要依靠电子系统运行；“集中、有组织”指规则与交易组织，不要求投资者聚在一间大厅里。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An exchange establishes eligibility, order and execution rules, information dissemination and procedures for violations. It can operate electronically: organized trading does not require investors to gather on a physical floor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交易所回答“怎样形成交易”，[[证券清算]]回答“成交后有哪些履行义务”，[[证券结算]]回答“证券与资金是否完成交付”。这些服务可能属于同一集团，却不能据此当成同一种法律角色。会员制和公司制描述治理与所有权安排；仅凭组织形式不能推出永不破产、公平定价或无条件担保履约。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exchange governs execution; [[证券清算|clearing]] establishes post-trade obligations; [[证券结算|settlement]] discharges them through transfers. Common group ownership does not merge these legal functions. Mutual and corporate forms concern governance and ownership; neither alone guarantees solvency, fair prices or performance of every trade.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
