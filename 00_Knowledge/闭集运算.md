# Operations on closed sets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*闭集的交与并*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose one condition restricts a real number to $[0,2]$ and another to $[1,3]$. Satisfying both gives their intersection $[1,2]$; satisfying at least one gives their union $[0,3]$. Both are closed. When many closed conditions are combined, the distinction between “all” and “at least one” becomes important: arbitrary intersections stay closed, but unions are guaranteed to stay closed only for finitely many sets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设一个条件把实数限制在 $[0,2]$，另一个限制在 $[1,3]$。同时满足两者，得到交集 $[1,2]$；至少满足一个，得到并集 $[0,3]$，两者都闭。组合很多闭条件时，“全部满足”和“至少满足一个”的差别就很重要：任意交仍闭，而并集只有在集合数有限时才普遍保证闭。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work in one fixed [[拓扑|topological space]] $X$. A [[闭集|closed set]] is a subset whose complement in $X$ is open. Let $I$ be an index set, simply a collection of labels, and let $F_i$ be closed in $X$ for each $i\in I$. The set $I$ can be finite, countably infinite or uncountable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

始终在同一个[[拓扑|拓扑空间]] $X$ 中讨论。[[闭集]]是补集在 $X$ 中开的子集。用 $I$ 作指标集，即用于编号的一组标签；每个 $i\in I$ 对应一个在 $X$ 中闭的集合 $F_i$。$I$ 可以有限、可数无限，也可以不可数。

> [!important] Which operations preserve closedness? · 哪些运算保持闭性？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The intersection $\bigcap_{i\in I}F_i$ is closed for any index set $I$. The union $\bigcup_{i=1}^{m}F_i$ is closed for any finite integer $m\ge1$. An infinite union of closed sets can be closed, but need not be.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对任意指标集 $I$，交集 $\bigcap_{i\in I}F_i$ 都闭。对任意有限整数 $m\ge1$，并集 $\bigcup_{i=1}^{m}F_i$ 都闭。无限多个闭集的并可以闭，但不保证闭。

## Complements exchange “all” and “at least one”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 用补集解释交并规则

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A point fails to belong to the intersection exactly when it fails at least one of the conditions. A point fails to belong to a union exactly when it fails every condition. These are De Morgan's set identities, with every complement taken inside the same $X$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一点不属于交集，恰好表示至少有一个条件没有满足；一点不属于并集，恰好表示所有条件都没有满足。这就是 De Morgan 集合恒等式，所有补集都在同一个 $X$ 中取：

$$
X\setminus\bigcap_{i\in I}F_i=\bigcup_{i\in I}(X\setminus F_i),
\qquad
X\setminus\bigcup_{i=1}^{m}F_i=\bigcap_{i=1}^{m}(X\setminus F_i).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each $X\setminus F_i$ is open. The [[拓扑|topology axioms]] say that an arbitrary union of open sets and a finite intersection of open sets are open. Thus each displayed complement is open, which proves the two closedness claims. This argument uses no distance or sequence: it works in every topological space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个 $X\setminus F_i$ 都开。[[拓扑|拓扑公理]]保证任意开集之并以及有限开集之交仍开。因此，上面两个补集都开，便证明了对应集合闭。论证不使用距离或序列，适用于每个拓扑空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an empty family, the conventions are $\bigcap_{i\in\varnothing}F_i=X$ and $\bigcup_{i\in\varnothing}F_i=\varnothing$. With no conditions to satisfy, every point belongs to the intersection; with no sets to supply a point, none belongs to the union. Both resulting sets are closed, so the rules cover these cases too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

空集合族采用约定 $\bigcap_{i\in\varnothing}F_i=X$、$\bigcup_{i\in\varnothing}F_i=\varnothing$。没有任何需要满足的条件时，每个点都属于交集；没有任何集合可以提供成员时，并集就没有点。两者都闭，因此规则也覆盖这些情况。

## Why an infinite union can lose an endpoint
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 无限并为什么可能丢掉端点

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $\mathbb R$ with the usual distance, define a closed interval for each integer $n\ge2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在带通常距离的 $\mathbb R$ 中，对每个整数 $n\ge2$ 定义闭区间：

$$F_n=[-1+1/n,\,1-1/n].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first three are $[-1/2,1/2]$, $[-2/3,2/3]$ and $[-3/4,3/4]$. They expand towards $-1$ and $1$, without ever including either. Every point $x$ strictly between those endpoints eventually belongs: choose $n\ge2$ large enough that $1/n<1-|x|$. Hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前三个是 $[-1/2,1/2]$、$[-2/3,2/3]$、$[-3/4,3/4]$。它们向 $-1$ 与 $1$ 扩张，却始终没有包括这两个点。任何严格位于两端之间的 $x$ 最终都会进入某个区间：取足够大的 $n\ge2$，使 $1/n<1-|x|$ 即可。因此

$$\bigcup_{n=2}^{\infty}F_n=(-1,1).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This union is not closed: its complement contains $1$, but every open ball around $1$ contains points of $(-1,1)$. The obstruction is the infinite collection, not an error in any individual interval. In contrast, the infinite union $\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}[-n,n]=\mathbb R$ is closed. “Not guaranteed” does not mean “always fails”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个并集不闭：补集包含 $1$，但 $1$ 周围每个开球都包含 $(-1,1)$ 中的点。问题来自无限组合，并不是某一个区间出了错。相反，无限并 $\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}[-n,n]=\mathbb R$ 就闭。“不能保证”不等于“必然失败”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Arbitrary intersection is what makes the definition of [[集合闭包|closure]] work: intersecting all closed supersets of a set still gives a closed set. That use needs all closed supersets to be taken in the same ambient space, just as in the formulas above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任意交保持闭，正是[[集合闭包]]定义能够成立的原因：把一个集合的所有闭超集相交，结果仍闭。和上面的公式一样，所有闭超集必须相对于同一个环境空间选取。
