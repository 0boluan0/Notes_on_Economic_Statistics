# 价格趋势是在明确观察尺度下由价格方向与高低点结构识别的走势

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A price trend is a directional movement identified from prices and their sequence of highs and lows at a specified observation scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

技术分析中的价格趋势，是在选定时间范围和采样尺度下描述价格整体方向的结构。相继峰值与谷值都抬高，构成上升趋势的典型结构；都降低则为下降趋势；价格在大致稳定区间内往返则可描述为横向走势。不能只凭某一期涨跌替代对整段结构的判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In technical analysis, a price trend describes overall direction over a chosen interval and sampling scale. Successively higher highs and lows characterize an uptrend; lower highs and lows characterize a downtrend. Movement within a broadly stable range is sideways. One period's gain or loss does not establish the direction of the whole structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一序列可以在较大尺度上升、在较小尺度回落；这不是矛盾，而是观察范围不同。[[道氏趋势层级]]给出一种主要、次级与微小运动的分层方式。此处“趋势”是图表分析用语，不等同于统计模型中特定的确定性趋势或随机趋势。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same series can rise at a larger scale while declining at a smaller one. [[道氏趋势层级|Dow's trend hierarchy]] is one way to organize these movements. This chart-analysis usage is not a definition of a deterministic or stochastic trend in a statistical model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用于可重复识别时，应先规定窗口、价格口径、峰谷识别及容差。如果一个峰值须等右侧数期价格出现后才能确认，它的识别时点晚于峰值所在期；必须遵守[[预测时点与信息集]]。已识别方向也不保证未来延续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A reproducible detector must specify its window, price convention, turning-point rule and tolerances. If a peak requires later observations for confirmation, its recognition time is later than its plotted date; the [[预测时点与信息集|forecast information boundary]] applies. An identified direction does not guarantee continuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
