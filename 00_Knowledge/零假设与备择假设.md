# Null and alternative hypotheses
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零假设与备择假设*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A statistical test starts with two statements about how the data could have been generated. The **null hypothesis**, written $H_0$, is the reference claim whose compatibility with the observations we assess. The **alternative hypothesis**, written $H_1$ or $H_A$, specifies the departure we want to detect. These statements concern the [[总体与样本|population or its probability distribution]], not merely a numerical pattern in one observed sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

统计检验先写出两个关于“数据可能怎样产生”的说法。**零假设**（也叫原假设）记为 $H_0$，是要用观测结果审视的基准说法；**备择假设**记为 $H_1$ 或 $H_A$，说明希望识别哪一种偏离。这些说法针对[[总体与样本|总体或其概率分布]]，不只是描述手头某一份样本的数值。

> [!example] A fair coin can produce seven heads · 公平硬币也可能十次出七次正面
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose ten tosses are independent and each has the same unknown probability $\theta$ of heads. Here $\theta$ is a **parameter**: a number describing the data-generating model. To ask whether this probability is one half, write
> $$H_0:\theta=\tfrac12,\qquad H_1:\theta\ne\tfrac12.$$
> Let $X$ count heads. Under this model $X$ has a [[二项分布|binomial distribution]] with ten trials and success probability $\theta$. If we observe seven heads, $X/10=0.7$ is a [[统计量|statistic]] calculated from this sample. It does not make the population parameter equal to $0.7$.
>
> Under $H_0$, the $2^{10}=1024$ head–tail sequences are equally likely and $\binom{10}{7}=120$ of them contain seven heads. Thus exactly seven heads has probability $\binom{10}{7}2^{-10}=15/128>0$. Thus the null does **not** predict exactly five heads in every ten tosses. A test needs a rule for how unusual a result is under the null; observing a sample proportion different from one half is not, by itself, a logical disproof.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设十次抛掷相互独立，每次正面概率都是同一个未知数 $\theta$。$\theta$ 是描述数据生成模型的**参数**。要问正面概率是否为二分之一，就写 $H_0:\theta=1/2$，$H_1:\theta\ne1/2$。正面次数 $X$ 服从十次试验、成功概率 $\theta$ 的[[二项分布]]。如果观察到七次正面，$X/10=0.7$ 是从这份样本算出的[[统计量]]，并不因此把总体参数变成 $0.7$。
>
> 零假设下，$2^{10}=1024$ 种正反面序列等可能，其中 $\binom{10}{7}=120$ 种恰含七次正面，所以恰好七次正面的概率为 $\binom{10}{7}2^{-10}=15/128>0$。所以零假设**没有**要求每十次都必须恰好五次正面。检验还需要规定结果在零假设下有多反常；样本比例不等于二分之一，本身不是逻辑反证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Specify the direction of the question
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先明确要识别哪个方向的偏离*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The alternative determines which departures count as evidence. For the same coin model, these are different questions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

备择假设决定哪些方向的偏离算作证据。同一个硬币模型，可以有不同问题：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Intended question | Null $H_0$ | Alternative $H_1$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is the probability different from one half? | $\theta=1/2$ | $\theta\ne1/2$ |
| Is it greater than one half? | $\theta\le1/2$ | $\theta>1/2$ |
| Is it less than one half? | $\theta\ge1/2$ | $\theta<1/2$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 想问的问题 | 零假设 $H_0$ | 备择假设 $H_1$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 是否偏离二分之一？ | $\theta=1/2$ | $\theta\ne1/2$ |
| 是否大于二分之一？ | $\theta\le1/2$ | $\theta>1/2$ |
| 是否小于二分之一？ | $\theta\ge1/2$ | $\theta<1/2$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first is a two-sided question; the other two are one-sided. In each row the hypotheses are mutually exclusive and exhaust the possibilities in the chosen model, $0\le\theta\le1$. A null can therefore specify a range, not just a single value. Some presentations write only the boundary equality for a one-sided test because that boundary supplies its reference calculation; this shortcut needs justification for the particular test. It does not turn the scientific claim “at most one half” into “exactly one half.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一行是双侧问题，后两行是单侧问题。每一行的两个假设互斥，并覆盖所选模型 $0\le\theta\le1$ 内的所有可能。因此零假设可以是一个范围，不一定只有一个数值。有些单侧检验只把边界等号写出来，因为具体检验在边界上进行参考计算；这个简写需要该检验本身的依据，不能因此把科学问题里的“至多二分之一”理解成“恰好二分之一”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose the question from the scientific aim before looking for a favorable direction in the same data. Switching to a right-sided alternative only after seeing a large positive estimate changes the procedure and may require [[选择后推断|selection-aware calibration]]. “Null” also does not mean “zero”: a reference lifetime of $500$ hours or a coin probability of $1/2$ is perfectly legitimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应根据科学目的确定问题，而不是先看同一份数据往哪边有利，再选择方向。看到很大的正估计后才改用右侧备择，会改变检验程序，可能需要[[选择后推断|选择后的校准]]。“零假设”中的“零”也不要求数值为零：基准寿命 $500$ 小时或正面概率 $1/2$ 都可以成为假设内容。

## The hypothesis and the sampling model do different jobs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*假设内容与抽样模型各自解决什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A hypothesis specifies the feature being questioned. The surrounding model says how observations vary, which is needed to judge evidence. In the coin example, $H_0:\theta=1/2$ together with independent tosses and a common probability determines the null distribution of $X$. Without those sampling assumptions, the same marginal probability of one half need not produce a binomial count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设规定要审视哪项特征，周围的模型则说明观测怎样波动，后者是判断证据的依据。硬币例子中，$H_0:\theta=1/2$ 加上独立抛掷、共同正面概率，才确定 $X$ 在零假设下的分布。没有这些抽样条件，即使每次的边际正面概率都是二分之一，次数也未必服从二项分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Similarly, a claim that a population mean $\mu$ equals zero does not specify the variance, the shape of the distribution or dependence between observations. Unknown model parameters not targeted by this question, such as the variance when testing a mean, are called **nuisance parameters**; “nuisance” means they are not the target of this particular test, not that they can be ignored. A valid test must handle the allowed null distributions and its stated sampling conditions. Defining $H_0$ is the starting point, not a complete testing method.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，总体均值 $\mu$ 为零的说法，并没有规定方差、分布形状或观测之间的依赖。模型需要、却不是本次问题目标的未知参数，例如检验均值时的未知方差，称为**干扰参数**；这表示它们不是本次检验的目标，不表示可以忽略。有效检验必须处理零假设允许的分布以及声明的抽样条件。写出 $H_0$ 是开始，还不是一套完整检验方法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the decision as evidence under assumptions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把决定读成有条件的证据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A testing rule chooses when to **reject** $H_0$ using a statistic. A **Type I error** is rejecting a true null; a **Type II error** is failing to reject when the specified alternative is true. These are repeated-sampling properties of the rule under particular data-generating distributions. They are not posterior probabilities that the hypotheses are true after the observed result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验规则利用统计量决定何时**拒绝** $H_0$。**第一类错误**是零假设真实却被拒绝；**第二类错误**是零假设为假、所设备择成立，却没有拒绝零假设。这些讨论的是规则在特定数据生成分布下反复取样时的表现，并不是看到这次结果后各假设为真的后验概率。

> [!warning] Failure to reject is not proof of equality · 不拒绝不等于证明相等
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A small or noisy sample may not distinguish a modest departure from chance variation. Conversely, even a valid test can reject a true null. Report “reject” or “fail to reject” relative to the stated model and rule, not “proved false” or “proved true.” A [[p值条件解释|p-value]] measures an appropriate tail probability under the null model; it is not $P(H_0\text{ is true}\mid\text{data})$. A small value also does not by itself establish a large or useful effect; see [[显著性解释边界|the interpretation of significance]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 样本少或噪声大时，可能无法把较小的真实偏离与随机波动区分开；反过来，有效检验也可能拒绝真实的零假设。因此应相对于声明的模型和规则报告“拒绝”或“不拒绝”，而不是“已经证伪”或“已经证真”。[[p值条件解释|p 值]]是零假设模型下适当的尾部概率，不是 $P(H_0\text{ 为真}\mid\text{数据})$；数值很小也不直接说明效应很大或很有用，见[[显著性解释边界]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a model with several response coefficients, “both coefficients are zero” has the alternative “at least one is nonzero,” not “both are nonzero.” Writing the two hypotheses first prevents a joint rejection from being over-interpreted. [[多响应一般线性假设|General linear hypotheses]] extend this same idea to specified combinations of coefficient rows and response columns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于含多个响应系数的模型，“两个系数都为零”的备择是“至少一个非零”，而不是“两个都非零”。先把两边写清楚，就不容易把联合拒绝解释过头。[[多响应一般线性假设]]把同样的思想推广到指定的系数行与响应列组合。
