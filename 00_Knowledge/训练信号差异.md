# 预训练、SFT 与偏好优化分别从观测 token、示范回答和相对偏好获得训练信号
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pretraining, SFT, and preference optimization obtain training signals respectively from observed tokens, demonstration responses, and relative preferences*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 先问“什么东西被当成正确信号”
> 预训练把语料中已观测到的下一个 token 当作目标；监督微调（SFT）把给定提示下的示范回答当作目标；偏好优化则利用“对同一提示，回答 A 比回答 B 更受偏好”这种相对判断。三者都会改变模型的输出概率，但监督事件不同，所以不能用“继续喂数据”把它们混成一件事。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Pretraining treats the next observed token in a corpus as the target. Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) treats a demonstration response to a given prompt as the target. Preference optimization uses a relative judgment such as “for this prompt, response A is preferred to response B.” All three alter output probabilities, but the supervised event differs, so they should not be collapsed into the vague description “more training on more data.”
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同样是 token，在数据中承担的角色不同
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The tokens may look alike, but their roles in the data differ*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在自回归预训练中，[[自回归目标|一段观测文本对标准 decoder-only 目标]]通常贡献
$$
-\sum_{t=1}^{T}\log p_\theta(z_t\mid z_{<t}).
$$
这里写的是单条序列对 NLL 分子的贡献，不是在指定 batch reduction；全局 token 均值与逐序列均值的权重差别单见 [[Token损失均值不等价]]。无论明确选择哪一种归一化，这个目标都在拟合**按该归一化规则赋权后**的经验训练分布；它不会因为一段文本被观测到，就把其中的事实、价值判断或行为都认定为真实且理想。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In autoregressive pretraining, an observed sequence $z_{1:T}$ commonly contributes $-\sum_{t=1}^{T}\log p_\theta(z_t\mid z_{<t})$ to the NLL numerator; this does not yet specify batch reduction. The weighting difference between a global token mean and a mean of sequence means is isolated in [[Token损失均值不等价|Token-loss means are not interchangeable]]. Either explicit reduction fits the empirical training distribution reweighted by that reduction; neither certifies every observed factual claim, value judgment, or behavior as true or desirable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 SFT 中，数据明确给出提示 $x$ 和示范回答 $y^*$，目标是让模型在 $x$ 之后更可能产生 $y^*$ 的 token。同一串字如果出现在普通网页里，只是“被观测到的后续”；如果被放在指令—回答样本的回答位置，就成了“希望模型模仿的示范”。区别在数据建模方式，不在 token 的物理形式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In SFT, the data explicitly pair a prompt $x$ with a demonstration response $y^*$, and training makes the tokens of $y^*$ more likely after $x$. The same string can be merely an observed continuation when found on a web page, yet become a response to imitate when placed in the answer field of an instruction–response example. The distinction lies in the data's statistical role, not in the physical form of the tokens.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

偏好数据不必给出唯一标准答案，而是给出 $(x,y_w,y_l)$：对提示 $x$，$y_w$ 被选中，$y_l$ 未被选中。显式奖励模型管线会先学这个排序，再优化策略；DPO 类目标则直接用配对调整策略。两条路线的共同点是，信号只声明配对中的相对选择，不是一份自带真理保证的答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Preference data need not provide a unique reference answer. Instead, a record $(x,y_w,y_l)$ says that for prompt $x$, $y_w$ was chosen over $y_l$. An explicit reward-model pipeline first learns this ordering and then optimizes a policy; DPO-style objectives use the pair to adjust the policy directly. In either case, the observation is a relative choice within the pair, not an answer carrying an automatic guarantee of truth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 边界
> 这是按训练信号做的区分，不是说现实系统只能按三个严格分隔的阶段运行。示范可以由人或模型生成；一个工程管线也可以混合继续预训练、SFT、拒绝采样和偏好优化。判断某个 loss 在学什么，应看单个训练样本给目标函数提供了什么，而不是只看项目给阶段起了什么名字。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This distinction classifies training signals; it does not claim that real systems must run three strictly isolated stages. Demonstrations may be written by people or generated by models, and a pipeline may mix continued pretraining, SFT, rejection sampling, and preference optimization. To determine what a loss learns, inspect what one training record contributes to the objective rather than relying on the project's stage label.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 对同一道问题，数据集 A 只给一个编辑过的理想回答；数据集 B 给两个回答，并记录标注者选了哪个。两者分别提供什么信号？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the same question, dataset A provides one edited ideal answer. Dataset B provides two answers and records which one an annotator chose. What signal does each dataset provide?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** A 提供示范目标，可用于 SFT 模仿；B 提供相对偏好，可用于奖励模型或直接偏好目标。B 没有因为记录了选择，就自动证明被选回答在绝对意义上正确。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** A supplies a demonstration target for SFT imitation. B supplies a relative preference for reward modeling or a direct preference objective. Recording a choice in B does not by itself establish that the chosen answer is correct in an absolute sense.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Ouyang et al. (2022), *Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.02155), §3：对应 demonstration SFT、成对比较、奖励模型与 PPO 三步数据和目标。
- [Touvron et al. (2023), *Llama 2: Open Foundation and Fine-Tuned Chat Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.09288), §3：对应预训练模型经 SFT 与人类偏好数据优化为对话模型的公开实例。
- [Rafailov et al. (2023), *Direct Preference Optimization*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290), §3–4：对应从成对偏好直接构造策略目标的路线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Ouyang et al. (2022), §3, provides the public example of demonstration SFT, pairwise comparisons, reward modeling, and PPO as distinct data and optimization stages.
- Touvron et al. (2023), §3, provides a public case in which a pretrained model is turned into a chat model through SFT and optimization using human preference data.
- Rafailov et al. (2023), §3–4, provides the direct route from paired preferences to a policy objective.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
