# 射击之星是上涨走势后小实体靠近区间下端且上影线较长的K线

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A shooting star is a candle after an advance with a small body near the bottom of its range and a long upper shadow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

射击之星是上涨走势之后出现的一根[[K线]]：小实体靠近本期价格区间下端，上影线长，下影线没有或很短。相同轮廓出现在下降走势后称为[[倒锤头线]]；实体为阳或为阴都不能单独决定形态名称。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A shooting star is a [[K线|candlestick]] after an advance with a small body near the bottom of its range, a long upper shadow and little or no lower shadow. The same outline after a decline is an [[倒锤头线|inverted hammer]]. Body direction alone does not determine the name.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本课主讲页采用“向上跳空开盘、上影线至少3倍实体”的较严口径；补充表另列至少2倍。因此复习主讲识别时保留3倍要求，跨资料识别或写程序时则须先声明采用哪一组标准，不能把它们混称为唯一数值定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The main course slide uses a stricter convention: a gap-up opening and an upper shadow at least three times the body. Its supplementary table instead uses twice the body. Retain the three-times criterion for the main lesson, and specify the chosen convention in comparisons or code rather than treating both as one universal numerical definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

射击之星在该期收盘后才完整可见。它表达上涨后冲高回落的形态，不意味着已经知道这里是最终顶部，也不保证下一期下跌。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The candle is complete only after its period closes. It describes a rally and retreat after an advance, without identifying a final market top or guaranteeing a decline in the next period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
