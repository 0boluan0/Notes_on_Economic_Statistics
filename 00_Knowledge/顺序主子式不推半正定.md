# Nonnegative leading minors and semidefiniteness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*非负顺序主子式与半正定性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero determinant can erase information about later coordinate directions. This is why changing every strict $>0$ in [[Sylvester 正定判据|Sylvester's positive-definite test]] to $\ge0$ does not give a valid test for [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefiniteness]]. In a principal-minor test for semidefiniteness, the collection of submatrices must also change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行列式里的零因子，可能抹掉后续坐标方向的信息。因此，不能把 [[Sylvester 正定判据]]中的每个 $>0$ 换成 $\ge0$，就用来判断[[半正定矩阵|半正定]]；使用主子式判断半正定时，要检查的子矩阵集合也得改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[顺序主子式|leading principal minor]] of order $k$ is the [[行列式|determinant]] of the top-left $k\times k$ block. For a real symmetric $n\times n$ matrix with $n\ge2$, the conditions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第 $k$ 阶[[顺序主子式]]，是左上角 $k\times k$ 块的[[行列式]]。对 $n\ge2$ 的实对称 $n\times n$ 矩阵，条件

$$\det A_{1:k,1:k}\ge0\qquad(k=1,\ldots,n)$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
are necessary but not sufficient for semidefiniteness, which requires $x^TAx\ge0$ for every real column $x$. Here $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. In the Hermitian complex setting use $x^*Ax$, with $*$ the conjugate transpose; the same failure occurs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是半正定的必要条件，却不是充分条件。半正定要求对每个实列向量 $x$ 都有 $x^TAx\ge0$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。复 Hermitian 情形使用 $x^*Ax$，$*$ 为共轭转置；同样会出现此失败。

> [!example] The smallest counterexample · 最小反例
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $A=\operatorname{diag}(0,-1)$: the listed numbers are on the diagonal and the other entries are zero. The first leading minor is $0$, and the second is $0\cdot(-1)=0$. Both pass a nonnegative test.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取[[对角矩阵]] $A=\operatorname{diag}(0,-1)$：列出的数放在对角线上，其余元素为零。第一个顺序主子式是 $0$，第二个是 $0\cdot(-1)=0$，都通过非负检查。
>
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> e_2=\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad e_2^TAe_2=-1<0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second coordinate therefore gives a negative value. The matrix is not positive semidefinite, and the missed [[主子式|principal minor]] is the one-by-one block with index set $\{2\}$, namely $a_{22}=-1$. This is minimal in dimension: for a one-entry real/Hermitian matrix $[a]$, the only leading minor is $a$, and $a\ge0$ is sufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二坐标方向给出负值，所以矩阵不半正定。漏掉的[[主子式]]就是指标集 $\{2\}$ 对应的一阶块 $a_{22}=-1$。二维是最小可能维数：对实数或 Hermitian 一阶矩阵 $[a]$，唯一顺序主子式为 $a$，此时 $a\ge0$ 已经充分。

## What the zero has hidden
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*零把什么遮住了*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, $\operatorname{diag}(0,c)$ has the same leading sequence $0,0$ for every real $c$. If $c\ge0$, it is semidefinite; if $c<0$, it is not. This sequence cannot distinguish the two outcomes. For the counterexample, swapping the two coordinates gives $\operatorname{diag}(-1,0)$ with leading sequence $-1,0$, exposing the failure. Reordering can expose a failure, but one favorable ordering does not certify semidefiniteness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般地，$\operatorname{diag}(0,c)$ 对每个实数 $c$ 都有相同的顺序序列 $0,0$。$c\ge0$ 时半正定，$c<0$ 时却不是；这串数无法区分两种结果。把反例的两个坐标交换，得到 $\operatorname{diag}(-1,0)$，顺序序列变为 $-1,0$，失败便显露出来。换序可能暴露问题，但找到一个能通过的排列并不能证明半正定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the strict Sylvester test, write $\Delta_k$ for the order-$k$ leading determinant and $d_k$ for the corresponding symmetric-elimination pivot. Every $\Delta_k$ is positive, so the ratios $d_k=\Delta_k/\Delta_{k-1}$ are defined and positive, with $\Delta_0=1$. In this example the attempted second ratio is $0/0$, which supplies no information. The identity for products of pivots cannot justify dividing by a zero preceding determinant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

严格 Sylvester 判据中，记第 $k$ 阶顺序行列式为 $\Delta_k$，对应的对称消元主元为 $d_k$。每个 $\Delta_k$ 为正，因此比值 $d_k=\Delta_k/\Delta_{k-1}$ 有定义且为正，约定 $\Delta_0=1$。本例试图计算第二个比值时却得到 $0/0$，它不提供任何信息。主元乘积恒等式不能用来支持除以零的步骤。

## Use the complete condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*使用完整条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrix, the [[半正定主子式判别|complete principal-minor criterion]] checks every nonempty index set, not just $\{1,\ldots,k\}$. In real dimension two that means $a_{11}\ge0$, $a_{22}\ge0$, and $\det A\ge0$. All of these conditions are needed. Other complete methods, such as the [[正定与半正定的谱判据|eigenvalue sign test]], are also available; the counterexample rules out only the shortened leading-minor test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵，[[半正定主子式判别|完整主子式判据]]检查每个非空指标集，而非只有 $\{1,\ldots,k\}$。实二维时就是 $a_{11}\ge0$、$a_{22}\ge0$ 和 $\det A\ge0$，三项都要满足。也可以使用[[正定与半正定的谱判据|特征值符号判据]]等其他完整方法；这里的反例只是否定缩短后的顺序主子式检验。
