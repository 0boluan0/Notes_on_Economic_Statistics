# 变革理论是说明干预如何在给定条件下产生预期结果的因果解释

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A theory of change explains how an intervention is expected to produce its intended outcomes under specified conditions.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**变革理论（theory of change）**把一项干预的做法、参与者反应和预期结果连起来，并说明每一步成立需要什么条件。它回答的不是“准备花多少钱、完成多少工程”，而是“这些行动为什么能改变我们关心的问题”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A theory of change connects an intervention, participants' responses and intended outcomes, making the conditions behind each link explicit. It explains why the planned actions could change the problem, rather than merely listing budgets and deliverables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，设想为果农修一条通往集市的道路。我们希望看到的链条是：雨季仍能通行 → 运送更快、更稳定 → 腐损和交易成本降低 → 可售数量或农户净收入改善。这是一个**待检验的解释**：若货运服务仍然不可得，第一步就未必带来第二步；若运费没有下降、买方压价或新增销售成本很高，收入改善也不能直接从通车推出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a proposed road linking fruit growers to a market: all-weather access could make transport more reliable, reduce spoilage and transaction costs, and improve saleable output or net income. This is a hypothesis to test. Missing transport services, unchanged freight charges, buyer pricing or additional selling costs could interrupt different links.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[项目投入产出与结果]]帮助给每个观测量找准位置；变革理论则解释位置之间的箭头。写箭头时，至少指出谁的行为或约束改变、依赖什么配套条件、用什么证据检查。配套条件应随具体机制判断，并非给每个项目套上同一张“必须拥有”的清单。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[项目投入产出与结果|Inputs, outputs and outcomes]] classify what is measured; the theory of change explains the arrows between them. Each arrow should identify a behavioural response or constraint, its enabling conditions, and evidence that could test it. Complementary requirements are mechanism-specific.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

画出合理链条还没有证明项目有效。通车后收入增加，仍可能来自同期果价上涨；净作用须回到 [[公共项目反事实]]。评价时也应寻找与链条不相容的证据，而不只挑选支持它的观察。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A plausible chain does not establish effectiveness: rising fruit prices could raise income after a road opens. Attribution requires a [[公共项目反事实|counterfactual]]. Evaluation should look for conflicting evidence as well as support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> “道路修好，所以农户收入一定增加”省略了什么？
>
> **答案：** 省略了实际使用、运输与损耗变化、价格和成本传导等机制，以及无项目时收入如何变化的反事实。每个环节都需要条件与证据。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> What is missing from “the road is complete, so farm income must rise”? Actual use, transport and spoilage changes, price and cost transmission, and income without the project. Each link needs conditions and evidence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
