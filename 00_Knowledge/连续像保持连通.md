# Continuous images of connected spaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*连续像保持连通*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A continuous map may stretch a set, identify different points, or collapse the entire set to one point. It cannot turn a connected domain into a disconnected image. For example, $f(t)=t^2$ takes the interval $[-1,2]$ onto $[0,4]$. The inputs $-1$ and $1$ are identified, but the image still contains every value between its endpoints. The theorem explains this without requiring the map to be one-to-one or the spaces to be Euclidean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续映射可以拉伸集合、把不同点映到同一点，甚至把整个集合压成一个点，但不能把连通的定义域变成不连通的像。例如 $f(t)=t^2$ 把区间 $[-1,2]$ 映成 $[0,4]$；输入 $-1$ 和 $1$ 被映到同一点，像却仍包含端点之间的全部数值。定理不要求映射一一对应，也不要求空间是欧氏空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Distinguish the image from the codomain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*区分像集与陪域*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f:X\to Y$, the **image** $f(X)=\{f(x):x\in X\}$ contains the outputs actually attained, whereas the codomain $Y$ may also contain unused points. Give $f(X)$ the [[子空间拓扑|subspace topology]] inherited from $Y$. Recall that [[连通性|connectedness]] excludes two disjoint nonempty relatively open pieces covering the set, and [[连续性|continuity]] says that open output sets have open preimages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于 $f:X\to Y$，**像集** $f(X)=\{f(x):x\in X\}$ 是实际取到的输出组成的集合；陪域 $Y$ 还可能包含从未取到的点。给 $f(X)$ 配上从 $Y$ 继承的[[子空间拓扑]]。[[连通性]]排除两个不交、非空、相对开且覆盖全体的部分；[[连续性]]则要求开输出集合的原像是开集。

> [!important] The image remains connected · 像仍然连通
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $X,Y$ are topological spaces, $X$ is connected, and $f:X\to Y$ is continuous, then $f(X)$ is connected in its subspace topology. There is no assumption that $f(X)=Y$. More generally, every connected subset $A\subseteq X$ has connected image $f(A)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $X,Y$ 为拓扑空间，$X$ 连通，且 $f:X\to Y$ 连续，则 $f(X)$ 在其子空间拓扑中连通。不要求 $f(X)=Y$。更一般地，每个连通子集 $A\subseteq X$ 的像 $f(A)$ 都连通。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Pull a proposed separation back to the domain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把像中的假想分离拉回定义域*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose, for a contradiction, that $f(X)=U\cup V$ is a separation. The sets $U,V$ are nonempty, disjoint and open in $f(X)$. Relative openness gives ambient open sets $O,P\subseteq Y$ such that $U=f(X)\cap O$ and $V=f(X)\cap P$. The ambient sets $O,P$ need not be disjoint outside the image.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反设 $f(X)=U\cup V$ 是一个分离。$U,V$ 都非空、互不相交，并在 $f(X)$ 中开。由相对开性，存在 $Y$ 中的开集 $O,P$，使 $U=f(X)\cap O$、$V=f(X)\cap P$。环境开集 $O,P$ 在像集之外可以相交。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **preimage** $f^{-1}(U)=\{x\in X:f(x)\in U\}$ collects all inputs landing in $U$; the notation does not require an inverse function. Since every output already lies in $f(X)$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**原像** $f^{-1}(U)=\{x\in X:f(x)\in U\}$ 收集落入 $U$ 的全部输入；该记号不要求存在反函数。由于所有输出本来就在 $f(X)$ 中，

$$f^{-1}(U)=f^{-1}(O),\qquad f^{-1}(V)=f^{-1}(P).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity therefore makes both preimages open in $X$. They are disjoint because no output belongs to both $U$ and $V$, and they cover $X$ because every output belongs to one of them. They are also nonempty: a point of $U$ or $V$ is, by definition of the image, attained by some input. Thus they form a separation of $X$, a contradiction. If $X$ is empty, its image is empty and connected by the stated convention, so that case also holds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，连续性使两个原像都在 $X$ 中开。它们不交，因为没有输出同时属于 $U,V$；它们覆盖 $X$，因为每个输出都属于其中一边。它们也都非空，因为 $U,V$ 中的点属于像集，按定义必由某个输入取到。于是两个原像构成 $X$ 的分离，矛盾。若 $X$ 为空，其像也为空，并按既定约定连通，因此这一路边界也成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the subset version, restrict the map to $A$. If $O$ is open in $Y$, its preimage under the restricted map is $A\cap f^{-1}(O)$, open in $A$. The restriction is continuous, so the same proof applies to $f(A)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对子集版本，把映射限制到 $A$。若 $O$ 在 $Y$ 中开，它在限制映射下的原像为 $A\cap f^{-1}(O)$，在 $A$ 中开。因此限制映射连续，对 $f(A)$ 可使用同一证明。

## What can and cannot be inferred
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*结论的方向与适用条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The converse fails. On the disconnected domain $X=(-1,0)\cup(0,1)$, the constant map $f(x)=0$ is continuous: every open set has preimage either $X$ or $\varnothing$. Its image is the connected singleton $\{0\}$. A connected image therefore does not reveal whether the original domain was connected. Likewise, the theorem only concerns the image, not unused parts of $Y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反向不成立。在不连通的定义域 $X=(-1,0)\cup(0,1)$ 上，常值映射 $f(x)=0$ 连续，因为每个开集的原像不是 $X$ 就是 $\varnothing$。其像却是连通的单点 $\{0\}$。因此，仅从像连通无法判断原定义域是否连通；定理也只讨论像，不讨论 $Y$ 中未使用的部分。

> [!warning] Continuity is doing essential work · 连续性是关键条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On the connected interval $[-1,1]$, let $h(t)=0$ for $t<0$ and $h(t)=1$ for $t\ge0$. Its image is the disconnected two-point set $\{0,1\}$ in the usual real line. The failure occurs at input $0$: the open output interval $(1/2,3/2)$ has preimage $[0,1]$, which is not open relative to $[-1,1]$. This map is not continuous, so it does not contradict the theorem.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在连通区间 $[-1,1]$ 上，令 $t<0$ 时 $h(t)=0$，$t\ge0$ 时 $h(t)=1$。像为通常实线中不连通的两点集 $\{0,1\}$。问题出在输入 $0$：开输出区间 $(1/2,3/2)$ 的原像是 $[0,1]$，它不相对开于 $[-1,1]$。这个映射不连续，因此不违反定理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the outputs are real numbers with their usual topology, [[实线连通集是区间|connected subsets are intervals]]. Hence a continuous real-valued function on a connected domain cannot omit a value strictly between two values it attains. Applied to a closed input interval, this gives the familiar [[介值定理|intermediate value theorem]]. It promises attainment of intermediate values, not a unique input attaining each value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若输出为带通常拓扑的实数，[[实线连通集是区间|连通子集就是区间]]。因此，连通定义域上的连续实值函数，不会遗漏两个已取值之间的数。应用到闭的输入区间，就得到熟悉的[[介值定理]]。它保证中间值能取到，不保证每个中间值只有唯一输入与之对应。
