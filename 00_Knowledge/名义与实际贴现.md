# 名义现金流配名义贴现率而实际现金流配实际贴现率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Match nominal cash flows with nominal discount rates and real cash flows with real discount rates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子一致性
> 含预期通胀的当期货币现金流是名义现金流，应使用名义贴现率；按不变购买力表示的现金流是实际现金流，应使用实际贴现率。两套口径可以得到相同价值，但不能交叉配对。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> Cash flows stated in money of the date and containing expected inflation are nominal and require a nominal discount rate. Cash flows stated in constant purchasing power are real and require a real discount rate. Either consistent basis can give the same value; crossing the two bases cannot.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在把同期间通胀作为给定情景路径的贴现计算中，名义／实际口径的精确换算关系是
$$1+r_{nominal}=(1+r_{real})(1+\pi),$$
其中 $\pi$ 是该情景下相同期间的通胀率。若 $\pi$ 只是随机通胀的均值，这一换算不等于实现实际回报的精确期望，区别见[[事前实际利率]]。用名义率折实际现金流会把通胀影响放入分母却不放入分子，通常低估价值；反向错配则通常高估价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When same-period inflation is treated as a given scenario path in discounting, the exact nominal-real conversion is
$$1+r_{nominal}=(1+r_{real})(1+\pi),$$
where $\pi$ is inflation over that period in the scenario. If it is merely the mean of uncertain inflation, this conversion is not the exact expected realised real return; see [[事前实际利率|the ex-ante real rate]]. Discounting real cash flows at a nominal rate puts inflation in the denominator but not the numerator and generally understates value; the reverse mismatch generally overstates it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 预测表按今天购买力保持每年 100 不变，但贴现率含 3% 通胀。错在哪里？
>
> **答案：** 分子是实际现金流，分母却是名义贴现率。要么把现金流按通胀转成名义金额，要么把贴现率转成实际率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A forecast keeps cash flow at 100 each year in today's purchasing power, but the discount rate includes 3% inflation. What is wrong?
>
> **Answer:** The numerator is real while the denominator is nominal. Either inflate the cash flows into nominal amounts or convert the discount rate into a real rate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
