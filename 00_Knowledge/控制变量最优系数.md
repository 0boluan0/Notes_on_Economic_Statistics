# Optimal control-variate coefficient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*单个控制变量的最优固定系数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[控制变量|control variate]] adjusts a noisy output by subtracting a multiple of a centred auxiliary output. The coefficient answers “how much should we subtract?” Too small a correction leaves removable noise; too large a correction can overshoot. The population-optimal coefficient minimises the variance among these fixed linear adjustments. It is a property of the joint distribution, not automatically the coefficient fitted in one finite run.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[控制变量]]从带噪输出中，减去中心化辅助输出的某个倍数。系数回答“究竟减多少”：太小会留下可消除的噪声，太大又可能修正过头。总体最优系数在这类固定线性修正中使方差最小；它是联合分布的性质，不自动等于某次有限运行拟合出的系数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Remove the part that moves with the control
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*去掉随控制量一起变动的部分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the delay example: $C$ is $0$ or $1$ equally likely, $\varepsilon$ is independently $-1$ or $1$ equally likely, and $Y=10+2C+\varepsilon$. The correctly known mean is $m=1/2$. Here $C$ increases the output by two units when it rises by one, so try $\beta=2$. Then $Y-2(C-1/2)=11+\varepsilon$: the delay variation disappears while independent noise remains. Choosing $\beta=4$ instead gives $12-2C+\varepsilon$, which overcorrects the delay and has the same variance as the original output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用延误例：$C$ 等概率取 $0,1$，$\varepsilon$ 独立地等概率取 $-1,1$，$Y=10+2C+\varepsilon$，正确已知均值 $m=1/2$。$C$ 每增加一单位，输出相应增加二单位，所以试取 $\beta=2$。此时 $Y-2(C-1/2)=11+\varepsilon$，延误波动消失，独立噪声仍在。若改取 $\beta=4$，则得到 $12-2C+\varepsilon$，延误部分被修正过头，方差回到原输出的大小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, let $Y,C$ be real outputs with $E[Y^2],E[C^2]<\infty$. Write $v_Y=\operatorname{Var}(Y)$, $v_C=\operatorname{Var}(C)>0$, and $c=\operatorname{Cov}(Y,C)$. [[方差|Variance]] measures spread, while [[协方差|covariance]] measures their linear co-movement. Keep $m=E[C]$ correct and $\beta$ fixed. The [[和的方差协方差项|variance-of-a-sum formula]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，设实数输出 $Y,C$ 满足 $E[Y^2],E[C^2]<\infty$，记 $v_Y=\operatorname{Var}(Y)$、$v_C=\operatorname{Var}(C)>0$、$c=\operatorname{Cov}(Y,C)$。[[方差]]衡量分散程度，[[协方差]]衡量线性共同变化。保持 $m=E[C]$ 正确、$\beta$ 固定，按[[和的方差协方差项|求和方差公式]]得到

$$
v_\beta:=\operatorname{Var}[Y-\beta(C-m)] =v_Y-2\beta c+\beta^2v_C =v_Y-\frac{c^2}{v_C}+v_C\left(\beta-\frac{c}{v_C}\right)^2.
$$

> [!important] The squared penalty identifies the optimum · 平方惩罚直接给出最优值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The last term is nonnegative and, since $v_C>0$, vanishes at exactly one coefficient: $\beta^*=c/v_C$. Thus the minimum is $v_Y-c^2/v_C$. Choosing a coefficient $d$ away from $\beta^*$ adds exactly $v_Cd^2$ to the per-output variance.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 最后一项非负，且因 $v_C>0$，只在唯一系数 $\beta^*=c/v_C$ 处为零。因此最小方差为 $v_Y-c^2/v_C$。系数偏离最优值 $d$ 时，单次输出方差恰好多出 $v_Cd^2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the example, $v_C=1/4$, $c=1/2$ and $v_Y=2$, so $\beta^*=2$ and the minimum is $1$. The formula becomes $v_\beta=1+(\beta-2)^2/4$: $\beta=0,2,4,6$ give variances $2,1,2,5$. A useful control can therefore increase variance when its coefficient is chosen badly. For fixed $N$ [[独立同分布|iid]] complete pairs, divide each per-output variance by $N$ to get the variance of the corrected mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本例 $v_C=1/4$、$c=1/2$、$v_Y=2$，故 $\beta^*=2$，最小方差为 $1$。公式成为 $v_\beta=1+(\beta-2)^2/4$：$\beta=0,2,4,6$ 分别给出方差 $2,1,2,5$。所以好控制量若配上不合适的系数，也会增加方差。对固定 $N$ 个[[独立同分布]]完整配对，把单次方差除以 $N$，就是修正均值的方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Correlation, units and degenerate cases
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*相关方向、单位与零方差边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $v_Y>0$ as well, the [[相关系数|Pearson correlation]] is $\rho=c/\sqrt{v_Yv_C}$, and the minimum is $v_Y(1-\rho^2)$. Negative correlation is useful too: it gives a negative $\beta^*$, so subtracting $\beta^*(C-m)$ adds the appropriately signed correction. The coefficient has units of $Y$ divided by units of $C$; unlike correlation, its numerical value depends on the chosen scales. Perfect $|\rho|=1$ gives zero variance because the centred output is an exact linear function of the centred control with probability one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $v_Y>0$，[[相关系数|Pearson 相关系数]]为 $\rho=c/\sqrt{v_Yv_C}$，最小方差可写为 $v_Y(1-\rho^2)$。负相关也有用：它使 $\beta^*<0$，减去 $\beta^*(C-m)$ 就是按合适符号加回。系数单位为 $Y$ 的单位除以 $C$ 的单位，数值会随尺度改变，不像相关系数那样无单位。$|\rho|=1$ 时，中心化目标以概率一恰为中心化控制量的线性函数，所以最小方差为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $v_Y=0$ while $v_C>0$, then $c=0$, $\beta^*=0$ and the minimum is already zero; $\rho$ is undefined. If $v_C=0$, the correctly centred control $C-m$ is zero with probability one, so every fixed coefficient gives the same output. There is no unique optimum and no valid division by $v_C$. Infinite second moments also fall outside this finite quadratic calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $v_Y=0$ 而 $v_C>0$，则 $c=0$、$\beta^*=0$，最小方差已是零，但 $\rho$ 无定义。若 $v_C=0$，正确中心化的 $C-m$ 以概率一为零，所有固定系数给出同样输出，既没有唯一最优值，也不能除以 $v_C$。二阶矩无限同样不属于这套有限二次式计算的范围。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the population optimum does not promise
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*总体最优不直接保证什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The best fixed coefficient is generally unknown. Replacing moments by sample moments is a regression calculation, but a coefficient fitted and used on the same production sample can introduce [[同样本估计控制系数偏差|finite-sample bias]]. The displayed optimum is neither an exact finite-sample guarantee for that fitted procedure nor a promise about its confidence interval. An independent pilot with the integrability conditions described in [[控制变量|the construction]] preserves unbiasedness, but its random coefficient still pays the nonnegative squared penalty above on average; finite average variance additionally requires a finite second moment for that coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最优固定系数通常未知。用样本矩替换总体矩是回归计算，但在同一正式样本上拟合并应用系数，可能产生[[同样本估计控制系数偏差|有限样本偏差]]。这里的最优值不是那个拟合程序的精确有限样本保证，也不保证其区间覆盖率。按[[控制变量|构造卡]]的独立预试与可积条件可以保留无偏性，但随机估计系数平均仍要支付上面的非负平方惩罚；要使平均方差有限，还需该系数具有有限二阶矩。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, $1-\rho^2$ compares variance at the same number of complete simulations and the correct control mean. A costly control can lose a [[方差缩减比较口径|precision-versus-cost comparison]]. A [[控制变量均值误设|wrong control mean]] introduces bias, so minimising variance alone need not minimise squared error for the intended target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后，$1-\rho^2$ 比较的是完整模拟次数相同、控制均值正确时的方差。昂贵控制量可能输在[[方差缩减比较口径|精度与成本比较]]上；[[控制变量均值误设|控制均值错误]]则会引入偏差，使单独最小化方差未必等于对原目标最小化平方误差。
