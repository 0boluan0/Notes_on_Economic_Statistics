# Removing detours from a walk
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*游走去环得到路径*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a journey returns to a vertex it has already visited, that detour can be omitted without changing where the journey starts or ends. Repeating this deletion turns any finite [[游走路径与环|walk]] into a path. The removed segment need only be a **closed walk**: it need not be a cycle in the stricter sense.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
一段行程如果返回已经访问过的顶点，就可以略去这次绕行，而不改变整个行程的起点和终点。反复删除这样的片段，可以把任何有限[[游走路径与环|游走]]变成路径。被删掉的片段只需要是**闭游走**，不要求它本身是严格意义的环。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a concrete directed example, take four distinct vertices $s,a,b,t$ and suppose the graph contains $s\to a$, $a\to b$, $b\to a$ and $a\to t$. The walk $s,a,b,a,t$ has length four. Deleting the segment $a,b,a$ leaves the path $s,a,t$, of length two. The edge $a\to t$ already existed; the deletion creates no shortcut edge and reverses no arrow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，取四个不同顶点 $s,a,b,t$，有向图含有 $s\to a$、$a\to b$、$b\to a$、$a\to t$。游走 $s,a,b,a,t$ 的长度为四。删掉片段 $a,b,a$ 后，留下长度二的路径 $s,a,t$。边 $a\to t$ 原本就存在，这一步没有新造捷径边，也没有倒转箭头。

> [!important] A path with the same endpoints / 保留相同端点的路径
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every finite walk from $u$ to $v$ contains, after deleting closed segments, a path from $u$ to $v$ with no greater length. This holds in directed and undirected graphs, including permitted loops and parallel edges. Length means the **number of edge traversals**. The graph itself need not be finite; only the given walk must be finite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 每个从 $u$ 到 $v$ 的有限游走，都能通过删除闭合片段，得到端点仍为 $u,v$、长度不更大的路径。有向图、无向图以及允许自环或平行边的图都适用。这里长度指**走边次数**。不要求整张图有限，只要求给定游走有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Delete a repeated segment without changing a step
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 删除论证

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the walk as $v_0,e_1,v_1,\ldots,e_k,v_k$, where $e_i$ is the edge from the preceding vertex $v_{i-1}$ to $v_i$ and $k$ is its finite length. If all vertices are distinct, it already is a path. Otherwise choose positions $i<j$ with $v_i=v_j=x$. The segment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
把游走写成 $v_0,e_1,v_1,\ldots,e_k,v_k$，其中 $e_i$ 是前一个顶点 $v_{i-1}$ 到 $v_i$ 所用的边，有限长度为 $k$。若所有顶点都不同，它已经是路径。否则选择两个位置 $i<j$，使 $v_i=v_j=x$。片段

$$v_i,e_{i+1},v_{i+1},\ldots,e_j,v_j$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
is a positive-length closed walk. Remove its $j-i$ edges. Keep the prefix ending at $v_i=x$, followed by the original suffix starting at $v_j=x$. At the join, keep this vertex once. If the suffix has a next edge $e_{j+1}$, it still starts at $x$ and reaches the same next vertex $v_{j+1}$. Thus every retained edge remains legal, including its direction in a digraph and its identity in a multigraph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
是正长度闭游走。删去其中的 $j-i$ 条走边步骤，保留止于 $v_i=x$ 的前缀，再接上原来始于 $v_j=x$ 的后缀；拼接处只保留一次顶点 $x$。若后缀还有下一条边 $e_{j+1}$，它仍然从 $x$ 出发，通向原来的 $v_{j+1}$。因此保留的每一步仍合法，有向图中的方向以及多重图中的边身份都不改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The new length is $k-(j-i)<k$. The start and end remain $v_0$ and $v_k$, even when the deleted segment includes the beginning or end of the original walk. Length is a nonnegative integer and decreases by at least one each time, so after at most the original $k$ deletions the procedure stops. At that point no vertex repeats, which is precisely the path condition.

When $u=v$, the resulting path is the single vertex $u$, of length zero. Deleting an entire positive-length closed walk is allowed in this case. This is why the procedure is not a method that must leave a nonempty cycle behind.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
新长度为 $k-(j-i)<k$。即使删除片段碰到原游走的开头或结尾，起点和终点仍分别是 $v_0,v_k$。长度是非负整数，每次至少减少一，所以至多进行原长度 $k$ 次删除就会停止。停止时没有重复顶点，恰好满足路径定义。

若 $u=v$，所得路径就是单个顶点 $u$，长度为零。这时可以删去整个正长度闭游走，所以本过程并不是必须留下一个非空环的提取方法。

## Why shortest walks are paths
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么最短游走一定是路径*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a walk from $u$ to $v$ exists, its possible lengths form a nonempty set of nonnegative integers and therefore have a smallest member by [[良序原理适用域|the well-ordering principle]]. A walk with that length cannot repeat a vertex: the deletion just proved would make it shorter. Hence every shortest walk is a path, and minimizing over walks or paths gives the same [[图距离与可达性|edge-count distance]].

The converse fails. In a triangle, the two-edge path $a,b,c$ is longer than the one-edge path $a,c$. “Path” excludes repeated vertices; it does not mean “shortest.” Also, fewer edges does not necessarily mean lower total weight. Deleting a negative-cost detour can increase cost, so this theorem alone does not solve a weighted shortest-route problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若存在从 $u$ 到 $v$ 的游走，其可能长度构成非空的非负整数集合，由[[良序原理适用域|良序原理]]可知存在最小值。达到这个长度的游走不能重复顶点，否则刚才的删除会让它更短。所以每条最短游走都是路径，分别在游走或路径中最小化，得到相同的[[图距离与可达性|按边数计算的距离]]。

反过来不成立。三角形中的两边路径 $a,b,c$ 就比一边路径 $a,c$ 长。“路径”排除重复顶点，不表示“最短”。而且边数更少也不必意味着总权重更小：删去负成本绕行可能增加成本，因此本定理不能单独解决加权最短路线问题。

## Path reduction and cycle extraction are different
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 不能过度推出

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an undirected graph with just one edge $e$ between $u$ and $v$, the closed walk $(u,e,v,e,u)$ has positive length but contains no cycle: it reuses the only edge, and the graph has no other edges. It reduces to the zero-length path at $u$. Thus a positive-length **undirected** closed walk need not contain a cycle.

For a **directed** positive-length closed walk, a directed cycle does exist among its segments. Choose a closed segment of minimum positive length. If it had an additional repeated vertex, a shorter positive closed segment would exist, contradicting the choice. Apart from its equal endpoints, its vertices are distinct, so its directed edges do not repeat either. This different selection keeps a positive closed segment; it is not the endpoint-preserving reduction to a path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
无向图若在 $u,v$ 间只有一条边 $e$，正长度闭游走 $(u,e,v,e,u)$ 并不包含环：它重复使用唯一的一条边，图中也没有其他边。它可以缩成 $u$ 处的零长度路径。因此，正长度**无向**闭游走不一定包含环。

对于正长度**有向**闭游走，其片段中确实存在有向环：选择长度最短的正闭合片段；如果它还有额外的重复顶点，就会出现更短的正闭合片段，与选择矛盾。因此它除首尾外没有重复顶点，有向边也不会重复。这个选择保留的是正长度闭合片段，不是保持整个原游走端点、最后得到路径的删除过程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even in a directed graph, a cycle extracted from a closed walk need not contain all of its vertices. For example, take only the arrows $u\to x$, $x\to u$, $x\to v$, $v\to x$, with three distinct vertices. Both $u$ and $v$ can reach the other through $x$. But any closed trip through both must revisit $x$, so no directed cycle contains both. The cycles $u,x,u$ and $v,x,v$ lie on the two sides of that shared vertex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
即使在有向图中，从闭游走提取出的环也不必包含它的全部顶点。例如取三个不同顶点，只放入 $u\to x$、$x\to u$、$x\to v$、$v\to x$ 四个箭头。$u,v$ 都可以通过 $x$ 到达对方，但任何同时经过两者的闭合行程都必须再次访问 $x$，所以不存在同时含 $u,v$ 的有向环。两侧分别有环 $u,x,u$ 与 $v,x,v$。

> [!question]- What supplies the proof? / 证明依靠什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why cannot “a shortest walk is a path” be justified by defining a path to be shortest? The definition only forbids repeated vertices. The missing argument is that a repeated vertex supplies a deletable positive-length closed segment, contradicting minimum length.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 为什么不能把“路径就是最短游走”当作证明？因为路径定义只排除重复顶点。真正需要的论证是：重复顶点提供一个可以删去的正长度闭合片段，从而与长度最小矛盾。
