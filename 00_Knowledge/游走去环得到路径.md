# 删去游走中的闭合重复段可得到一条不更长的路径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Deleting closed repeated segments from a walk yields a path no longer than the walk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 若图中存在从 $u$ 到 $v$ 的 walk，就存在一条从 $u$ 到 $v$、长度不超过原 walk 的 path。特别地，从 $u$ 到 $v$ 的 shortest walk 必为 path。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a graph contains a walk from $u$ to $v$, it contains a path from $u$ to $v$ no longer than that walk. In particular, every shortest walk from $u$ to $v$ is a path.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 删除论证
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Deletion argument*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 walk 的顶点序列中某个 $x$ 在位置 $i<j$ 重复出现，那么从第一次 $x$ 到第二次 $x$ 的部分是一段 closed subwalk。删除这段的边和内部顶点，把删除前后的两段仍在同一个 $x$ 接起来，起点 $u$、终点 $v$ 与每一步合法性都不变，而长度严格减少。有限次重复后不再有重复顶点，所得 walk 就是 path。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a vertex $x$ occurs twice in a walk at positions $i<j$, the segment from the first occurrence to the second is a closed subwalk. Delete its edges and internal vertices. The remaining prefix and suffix still meet at the same vertex $x$, so the endpoints and validity of every step are preserved, while the length strictly decreases. Repetition must stop after finitely many deletions, leaving a walk with no repeated vertex—a path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这项证明不需要给图额外添加 shortcut edge。删除的是“走过的一圈”，不是把 $x$ 前后的不同顶点强行相连；两段在同一个 $x$ 拼接，因此合法性来自原 walk 本身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof does not add a shortcut edge to the graph. It deletes a completed detour; the retained prefix and suffix meet at the same occurrence of $x$, so their merge is legal using only original edges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不能过度推出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does not follow*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正长度 closed walk 也能通过类似删除提取出某个 cycle，但原 closed walk 本身未必是 cycle，提取出的 cycle 也未必经过原 walk 的每个顶点。两点互相可达同样不保证存在一个同时经过两点的 simple directed cycle：两条方向相反的 paths 可能共享必须重复的瓶颈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A similar deletion argument extracts some cycle from a positive closed walk, but the original closed walk need not itself be a cycle, and the extracted cycle need not contain every original vertex. Mutual reachability of two vertices also need not put both on one simple directed cycle: the two directed routes may share a bottleneck that must be repeated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 为什么“shortest walk 必为 path”不能用“path 的定义就是最短 walk”来证明？
>
> **答案：**path 的定义只排除重复顶点，并不包含最短性；一条 path 可以比另一条 path 长。需要删除重复段的反证：若 shortest walk 重复顶点，就能构造更短 walk，与 shortest 矛盾。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can “a shortest walk is a path” not be proved by saying that a path is defined as a shortest walk?
>
> **Answer:** A path is defined by absence of repeated vertices, not by minimality; one path may be longer than another. The proof needs the deletion contradiction: a repeated vertex in a shortest walk would produce a strictly shorter walk.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
