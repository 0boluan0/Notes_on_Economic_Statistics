# Left-nullspace compatibility
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*左零空间相容性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before solving $Ax=b$, we can ask whether the equations contradict one another. If a weighted sum of their left sides is zero, the same weighted sum of their right sides must also be zero. The **left-nullspace compatibility criterion** says that checking all such row relations is sufficient as well as necessary for a solution to exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求解 $Ax=b$ 之前，可以先检查这些方程是否互相矛盾。如果把各方程左侧按某些权重相加得到零，右侧按同样权重相加也必须为零。**左零空间相容性判据**说明：检验全部这样的行关系，既是有解的必要条件，也是充分条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A third equation constrained by the first two
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*第三个方程受到前两个方程的约束*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with a real system having two unknowns and three equations:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从一个有两个未知数、三个方程的实数方程组开始：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
Ax=b\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\begin{cases}x_1=b_1,\\x_2=b_2,\\x_1+x_2=b_3.\end{cases}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The third row equals the first plus the second. Thus a solution requires $b_3=b_1+b_2$. Here this also suffices: choose $x=(b_1,b_2)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. For $b=(1,1,2)^T$, $x=(1,1)^T$ works. For $b=(1,1,3)^T$, the first two equations demand $x_1+x_2=2$, while the third demands $3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第三行等于前两行之和，所以有解必须满足 $b_3=b_1+b_2$。这里它也足够：取 $x=(b_1,b_2)^T$ 即可，上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。当 $b=(1,1,2)^T$ 时，$x=(1,1)^T$ 是解；当 $b=(1,1,3)^T$ 时，前两式要求 $x_1+x_2=2$，第三式却要求等于 $3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The complete test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*完整判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ and $b\in\mathbb R^m$, with positive integers $m,n$. The unknown $x$ has $n$ coordinates. The [[列空间|column space]] $C(A)=\{Ax:x\in\mathbb R^n\}$ is the set of reachable outputs. The [[左零空间|left nullspace]] $N(A^T)=\{y\in\mathbb R^m:A^Ty=0\}$ collects weights on the $m$ equations whose weighted row sum is zero, equivalently $y^TA=0$. Use the standard real [[内积|inner product]] $y^Tb$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$、$b\in\mathbb R^m$，其中 $m,n$ 为正整数，未知向量 $x$ 有 $n$ 个坐标。[[列空间]] $C(A)=\{Ax:x\in\mathbb R^n\}$ 是可达输出的集合。[[左零空间]] $N(A^T)=\{y\in\mathbb R^m:A^Ty=0\}$ 收集施加在 $m$ 个方程上的权重，使对应行的加权和为零，等价地 $y^TA=0$。这里使用标准实[[内积]] $y^Tb$。

> [!important] All row constraints must hold · 必须满足全部行约束
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The system is **consistent**, meaning it has at least one solution, exactly under the following equivalent conditions:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 方程组**相容**指至少存在一个解；它恰好等价于下面的条件：
>
> $$Ax=b\text{ has a solution}
> \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad b\in C(A)
> \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad y^Tb=0\ \text{for every }y\in N(A^T).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Necessity follows directly: if $b=Ax$ and $A^Ty=0$, then $y^Tb=y^TAx=0$. For sufficiency, passing every test means $b\in N(A^T)^\perp$, the [[正交补|orthogonal complement]] of the left nullspace. The full [[基本子空间正交补|fundamental-subspace complement theorem]] gives $N(A^T)^\perp=C(A)$, so $b=Ax$ for some $x$. Mere perpendicularity of the two spaces would not establish this reverse implication; their complete complementarity does.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必要性直接来自：若 $b=Ax$ 且 $A^Ty=0$，则 $y^Tb=y^TAx=0$。对充分性，通过全部检验意味着 $b$ 属于左零空间的[[正交补]] $N(A^T)^\perp$。完整的[[基本子空间正交补]]定理给出 $N(A^T)^\perp=C(A)$，所以存在某个 $x$ 使 $b=Ax$。仅知道两个空间互相垂直，还不能推出这个反方向；完整的正交补关系才足够。

## An inconsistency certificate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 无解证书

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening matrix, take $y=(1,1,-1)^T$. Direct calculation gives $A^Ty=(0,0)^T$. It encodes “first equation plus second equation minus third equation”. With the incompatible right side $b=(1,1,3)^T$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对开头的矩阵，取 $y=(1,1,-1)^T$，直接计算得 $A^Ty=(0,0)^T$。它表示“第一式加第二式减第三式”。对不相容的右端 $b=(1,1,3)^T$，

$$y^Tb=1+1-3=-1\ne0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a solution existed, the same weighted sum would say $0=-1$. Thus the pair of checks $A^Ty=0$, $y^Tb\ne0$ is a **certificate of inconsistency**: it proves nonexistence without finding every possible $x$. Any nonzero multiple of this $y$ gives the same conclusion. The zero vector cannot certify inconsistency because its inner product with every $b$ is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假如存在解，同样加权就会得到 $0=-1$。因此 $A^Ty=0$、$y^Tb\ne0$ 这两项检验构成一份**无解证书**：无需枚举所有可能的 $x$，就能证明无解。把这个 $y$ 乘任意非零常数，结论不变。零向量不能作无解证书，因为它与任何 $b$ 的内积都是零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A basis makes the universal test finite
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用一组基完成全部检验*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Find a [[基|basis]] $y_1,\ldots,y_k$ of $N(A^T)$: these vectors are independent and span every left-null vector. Here $k=m-r$, where $r$ is the [[矩阵秩|rank]] of $A$. It is enough to check $y_j^Tb=0$ for each $j$. Indeed, if $y=\sum_j c_jy_j$, then $y^Tb=\sum_j c_j(y_j^Tb)=0$. The basis need not be orthonormal; any spanning list is enough for this test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求出 $N(A^T)$ 的一组[[基]] $y_1,\ldots,y_k$：它们线性无关，并张成每个左零向量。这里 $k=m-r$，$r$ 是 $A$ 的[[矩阵秩|秩]]。只需逐个检验 $y_j^Tb=0$。因为任意 $y=\sum_jc_jy_j$ 都满足 $y^Tb=\sum_jc_j(y_j^Tb)=0$。这组基不必标准正交；对这项检验，任何能张成整个左零空间的列表都足够。

> [!warning] One successful test can miss a constraint · 通过一次检验可能漏掉约束
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A=(1,0,0)^T$ and $b=(0,0,1)^T$. Then $N(A^T)=\{(0,s,t)^T:s,t\in\mathbb R\}$. Testing $y_1=(0,1,0)^T$ gives $y_1^Tb=0$, but testing the other basis direction $y_2=(0,0,1)^T$ gives $y_2^Tb=1$. The system is inconsistent. In the opening example, one nonzero test does suffice because the left nullspace is the one-dimensional span of $(1,1,-1)^T$; its dimension has been accounted for.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $A=(1,0,0)^T$、$b=(0,0,1)^T$，则 $N(A^T)=\{(0,s,t)^T:s,t\in\mathbb R\}$。检验 $y_1=(0,1,0)^T$ 得 $y_1^Tb=0$，但检验另一基方向 $y_2=(0,0,1)^T$ 得 $y_2^Tb=1$，所以方程组无解。开头例子只检验一个非零方向就够，是因为左零空间恰为 $(1,1,-1)^T$ 张成的一维空间，全部维数已经覆盖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $N(A^T)=\{0\}$, the basis is empty and there are no nontrivial constraints; every $b\in\mathbb R^m$ is reachable. This does not by itself guarantee uniqueness: $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\end{pmatrix}$ reaches every scalar $b$, but $x_2$ is free. For $A=0$, the left nullspace is all of $\mathbb R^m$, so only $b=0$ passes every test. In general, uniqueness is controlled by $N(A)$, as explained in [[特解加零空间|the particular-solution plus nullspace description]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $N(A^T)=\{0\}$，基为空，没有非平凡约束，每个 $b\in\mathbb R^m$ 都可达。但这不保证解唯一：$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\end{pmatrix}$ 能产生任意标量 $b$，而 $x_2$ 可以任意取值。若 $A=0$，左零空间就是整个 $\mathbb R^m$，只有 $b=0$ 能通过全部检验。一般而言，解的唯一性由 $N(A)$ 控制，见[[特解加零空间]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The test vector $y$ has $m$ coordinates because it weights the equations and pairs with the output $b$; $A^T$ also has $m$ columns. It is not an unknown vector $x\in\mathbb R^n$. If $b$ fails the test, [[最佳逼近与正交残差|least-squares approximation]] may still provide a useful approximate output, but it does not turn the original equation into an exact solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验向量 $y$ 有 $m$ 个坐标，因为它给各方程加权，并与输出 $b$ 做内积；$A^T$ 也有 $m$ 列。它不是未知向量 $x\in\mathbb R^n$。若 $b$ 未通过检验，[[最佳逼近与正交残差|最小二乘逼近]]仍可能提供有用的近似输出，但这不会让原方程突然存在精确解。

## The complex version uses a Hermitian pairing
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*复数版本使用 Hermitian 内积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A\in\mathbb C^{m\times n}$ and $b\in\mathbb C^m$, let $A^*=\overline A^T$ denote conjugate transpose; the bar means complex conjugation. With $y^*b=\sum_j\overline{y_j}b_j$, the criterion becomes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $A\in\mathbb C^{m\times n}$、$b\in\mathbb C^m$，用 $A^*=\overline A^T$ 表示共轭转置，横线表示复共轭。采用 $y^*b=\sum_j\overline{y_j}b_j$，判据变为

$$Ax=b\text{ has a solution}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 y^*b=0\ \text{for every }y\in N(A^*).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof uses $y^*Ax=(A^*y)^*x$ and $C(A)=N(A^*)^\perp$. Testing a complex basis still suffices: if $y=\sum_jc_jy_j$, then $y^*b=\sum_j\overline{c_j}\,y_j^*b$. There is also a valid algebraic version using every ordinary row-relation vector $w\in N(A^T)$ and testing $w^Tb=0$. The correspondence is $w=\overline y$, so it gives the same constraints; mixing $N(A^T)$ with the Hermitian test $y^*b$ would generally be wrong.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明使用 $y^*Ax=(A^*y)^*x$ 与 $C(A)=N(A^*)^\perp$。复数基同样足够：若 $y=\sum_jc_jy_j$，则 $y^*b=\sum_j\overline{c_j}\,y_j^*b$。也可采用有效的代数版本：对每个普通行关系向量 $w\in N(A^T)$ 检验 $w^Tb=0$。两者由 $w=\overline y$ 对应，给出相同约束；把 $N(A^T)$ 与 Hermitian 检验 $y^*b$ 混用，则通常错误。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $A=(1,i)^T$, where $i^2=-1$, has $y=(i,1)^T\in N(A^*)$. Its test is $-ib_1+b_2=0$, exactly the condition for $b=(x,ix)^T$. The corresponding algebraic weights are $w=(-i,1)^T$. Both formulations test the same reachable outputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $A=(1,i)^T$，其中 $i^2=-1$，则 $y=(i,1)^T\in N(A^*)$。它的检验是 $-ib_1+b_2=0$，恰好要求 $b=(x,ix)^T$。对应的代数权重是 $w=(-i,1)^T$。两种形式检验的是相同的可达输出。
