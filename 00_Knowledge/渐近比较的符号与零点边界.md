# 带符号或有零点的渐近比较必须先明确幅度约定与除法条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Asymptotic comparison with signs or zeros requires explicit magnitude conventions and division conditions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增长比较的共同主域取最终严格正函数，这使 $f/g$、$g/f$ 都最终有定义。若允许带符号函数，就不能一边用 $|f|\le Cg$，一边直接交换 $f,g$ 却仍假设右侧为正。若允许无限多个零点，也不能把除法得到的比值条件当作总能使用的定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The common domain for growth comparisons consists of eventually strictly positive functions, so both $f/g$ and $g/f$ are eventually defined. For signed functions, one cannot use $|f|\le Cg$ and then freely swap $f,g$ while silently retaining a positive right-hand side. With infinitely many zeros, ratio conditions obtained by division are not always available either.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意实值 $f,g$，一种一致的**幅度扩展**是用 $|f|\le C|g|$ 定义大 O，用反向大 O 定义 $\Omega$，并用双侧幅度界定义 $\Theta$。其中每个不等式都要求对整个充分大的尾段成立：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For arbitrary real-valued $f,g$, a consistent **magnitude extension** defines big O by $|f|\le C|g|$, Omega by reversed big O, and Theta by two-sided magnitude bounds. Every inequality must hold throughout an eventual tail:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
f=O(g)&:\quad |f(n)|\le C|g(n)|,\qquad C>0,\\
f=\Theta(g)&:\quad c|g(n)|\le|f(n)|\le C|g(n)|,\qquad 0<c\le C.
\end{aligned}
$$

例如 $f=-2n$、$g=n$ 的幅度同阶，但 $f/g=-2$，所以它们并不 [[渐近等价|渐近等价]]。$\sim$ 保留带符号比值趋 1 的含义；把它偷偷改成 $|f|/|g|\to1$ 会丢失符号信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $f=-2n$ and $g=n$ have the same magnitude order, but their ratio is $-2$, so they are not [[渐近等价|asymptotically equivalent]]. The symbol $\sim$ retains the signed ratio-one definition; silently replacing it with $|f|/|g|\to1$ would discard sign information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $g(n)=0$，大 O 的幅度不等式强制相应的 $f(n)=0$。例如令 $f$ 在偶数处为 1、奇数处为 0，$g$ 反过来取值；在无限多个偶数处 $|f|=1>C|g|=0$，因此 $f\ne O(g)$。奇数处同理破坏反向大 O。这是直接违反界，而不是仅仅“比值无极限”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $g(n)=0$, the big O magnitude inequality forces $f(n)=0$ at that input. For example, let $f$ be one on even inputs and zero on odd inputs, with $g$ taking the opposite values. Infinitely many even inputs have $|f|=1>C|g|=0$, disproving $f=O(g)$. Odd inputs similarly disprove the reverse bound. These are direct violations of bounds, not merely absent ratio limits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对小 o，若 $g$ 最终非零，可以用 $|f|/|g|\to0$；这包括带符号余项和 $0=o(1)$。若还要扩到无限零点，可**另行约定**“对每个 $\varepsilon>0$，最终 $|f|\le\varepsilon|g|$”，再把小 $\omega$ 定义为反向小 o。但这种全域扩展使 $0=o(0)$ 成立，因而小 o 不再非自反。把小 o 称为严格序、把 $\sim$ 称为等价关系的课程分类，应限定在共同的最终正函数域；不能原封不动搬到所有非负函数上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For little o with eventually nonzero $g$, use $|f|/|g|\to0$, including signed remainders and $0=o(1)$. To admit persistent zeros as well, one may **separately stipulate** that for every $\varepsilon>0$, eventually $|f|\le\varepsilon|g|$, and define little omega by reversal. However, this unrestricted extension makes $0=o(0)$ true, destroying irreflexivity. Course classifications of little o as a strict order and $\sim$ as an equivalence relation should therefore use a common eventually-positive domain, not all nonnegative functions unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

The domain restriction and extension are not misattributed as the textbook's original definition.
