# 防御型行业是需求和经营表现对经济周期相对不敏感的行业
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A defensive industry has demand and operating performance that are relatively insensitive to the business cycle.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

防御型行业通常满足较难推迟的基本需求，因此经济转弱时需求和经营表现相对稳定。“防御”是相对于其他行业的周期敏感性判断，不是完全免受周期、成本、监管或竞争影响的保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Defensive industries often satisfy basic needs that are difficult to postpone, making demand and operating performance relatively stable in a downturn. Defensive describes comparative cyclical sensitivity, not immunity to the cycle, costs, regulation, or competition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，基本食品的购买量可能比旅游消费稳定，但投入成本上涨仍可能压低食品生产商利润。需求稳定也不等于股价稳定、只产生股息回报或投资必然保本；购买价格与公司风险仍须另行分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Basic-food purchases may be steadier than tourism spending, yet input-cost inflation can reduce food producers' profits. Stable demand does not imply stable stock prices, dividend-only returns, or capital protection. Purchase price and company-specific risks still require analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
