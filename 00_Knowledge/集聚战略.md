# 集聚战略是面向狭窄细分市场追求成本或差异化优势的竞争战略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A focus strategy pursues a cost or differentiation advantage within a narrow market segment.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集聚战略把竞争范围限定在一个或一组细分市场，并为其特殊需求或服务条件配置活动，而非面向整个行业提供统一方案。成本集聚在目标范围内追求低成本；差异化集聚在目标范围内追求客户重视的独特价值。它改变的是竞争范围，不是发明第三种利润来源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A focus strategy limits competitive scope to one segment or a set of segments and tailors activities to their needs or service conditions. Cost focus pursues lower costs within that scope; differentiation focus pursues distinctive customer value there. Focus changes the scope of competition rather than introducing a third source of advantage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自拟例：只服务冷链药品运输的企业，可以围绕温控可靠性设计设备、培训和追踪服务，追求差异化集聚；只在紧密相连的少数配送区域提供标准运输，可尝试成本集聚。是否真有优势需要比较同一目标市场内的竞争者，而不能只因“公司规模小”就认定采用集聚战略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fictional carrier serving only refrigerated pharmaceuticals could organize equipment, training, and tracking around temperature reliability, pursuing differentiation focus. Another carrier might pursue cost focus with standardized delivery in a small, dense service area. Advantage must be assessed against rivals serving that segment; a small firm is not automatically a focused competitor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集中服务不会自动隔绝竞争：目标需求变化、广泛经营者针对性进入或原先服务差异缩小，都可能削弱定位。与[[成本领先]]和[[差异化战略]]相比，使用这一名称时要明确“聚焦在哪里”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Narrow scope does not isolate the firm from competition: changing needs, targeted entry by broad competitors, or shrinking service differences may weaken its position. When distinguishing focus from [[成本领先|broad cost leadership]] or [[差异化战略|differentiation]], identify exactly where the firm focuses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
