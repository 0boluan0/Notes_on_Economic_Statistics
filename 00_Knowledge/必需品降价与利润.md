# 必需品降价只有经工资成本渠道才构成李嘉图提高一般利润率的机制

在 Ricardo 的分配论中，进口和技术改善若降低劳动者必需品的生产或取得成本，就能降低维持劳动家庭所需的工资成本，从而缓解资本收益的压力。关键不是“贸易扩大”这四个字，而是**变便宜的商品是否进入工资所维持的生活条件，以及工资成本是否随之调整**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Ricardo's distributional argument, imports or improvements that cheapen workers' necessities can reduce the wage cost of maintaining labouring households and relieve pressure on capital returns. What matters is **whether the cheaper goods enter the living standard supported by wages, and whether wage costs adjust**, not trade expansion by itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按 [[李嘉图自然工资]] 的固定篮子例，每期需要两单位食品和一单位工业品，工业品价格为 1。若食品价格从 1.5 降到 1，自然工资要求从 4 降到 3，家庭仍消费同一篮子。假如实际工资随之调整，工业生产条件和收入又未改变，[[工资利润的分配约束|留给资本的剩余]] 就会增加。如果实际工资暂时不降，工人先得到购买力改善，同一时期的雇主工资账单则不会自动下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the fixed basket in [[李嘉图自然工资|Ricardo's natural wage]]: two food units and one manufactured unit per period, with the manufactured price at one. Reducing the food price from 1.5 to one lowers the natural-wage requirement from four to three while preserving consumption. If actual wages adjust and industrial production and revenue remain unchanged, [[工资利润的分配约束|the capital residual]] rises. If actual wages remain unchanged for a time, workers gain purchasing power without an automatic reduction in employers' current wage bills.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与此对照，只供富人消费、未进入劳动者维持篮子的丝绸或名酒降价，可以增加消费者的享受，或使他们在保持消费时节省支出；但它没有同一条降低自然工资成本的通道。Ricardo 因而区分了贸易增加可消费的商品、促进储蓄，与贸易提高一般利润率。某个商人暂时获得高利润，也不能证明全经济的正常利润率已提高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By contrast, cheaper luxury silk or wine outside workers' maintenance basket can increase consumers' enjoyment or permit savings at unchanged consumption, but it lacks that natural-wage-cost channel. Ricardo therefore distinguishes more consumption opportunities and saving from a higher general profit rate. An individual merchant's temporary high profit also does not establish a higher normal return across the economy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[比较优势]] 解释国家怎样通过相对成本差异获得贸易的消费机会；本卡解释的是其中一种特定的分配和积累通道。前者的潜在总收益不能直接用来证明后者必然发生，更不能证明每个人都获益，见 [[贸易收益与分配]]。应用到现实政策时，还须检查食品价格传递、工资调整、生产者损益以及其他受影响的成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[比较优势|Comparative advantage]] explains consumption opportunities from relative cost differences; this card concerns a particular distribution-and-accumulation channel. Potential aggregate gains do not prove that this channel operates or that everyone benefits; see [[贸易收益与分配|trade gains and distribution]]. Policy applications also require evidence on food-price transmission, wage adjustment, producer losses or gains and other affected costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ricardo（1821），[《原理》VII.5–8](https://www.econlib.org/library/Ricardo/ricP.html?chapter_num=8#7.5)：支持增加收入与节省支出的区别，以及必需品和奢侈品降价对一般利润率的不同通道；VII.7 明确以劳动者工资购买的商品为条件。
- [V.3、5–8](https://www.econlib.org/library/Ricardo/ricP.html?chapter_num=6#5.3)：支持便宜进口与自然工资成本，以及自然工资和实际市场工资不能静默等同。
- 4→3 是固定生活篮子的独立计价例，已按价格和数量复算。工资暂不调整的比较是对原机制实施条件的说明，不是估计的工资粘性结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chapters VII and V support the necessities channel, distinction from saving and consumption gains, and separation of natural from market wages. The basket calculation is constructed and verified. The unchanged-current-wage comparison clarifies the mechanism's requirements rather than estimating wage rigidity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
