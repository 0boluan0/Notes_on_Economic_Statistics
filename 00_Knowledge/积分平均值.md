# Average value of a function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*积分平均值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **average value of a function** is the constant level that would give the same accumulated total over the same interval. For example, if a room is at $2$ degrees for one hour and $8$ degrees for the next three hours, its average over those four hours is $(2\times1+8\times3)/4=6.5$ degrees. The longer period counts more. Simply averaging the two temperature readings gives $5$, which answers a different question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**积分平均值**是这样一个恒定水平：让它持续整个区间，得到的累积总量与原来变化的函数相同。例如房间前一小时为 $2$ 度，接下来三小时为 $8$ 度，那么四小时的平均温度是 $(2\times1+8\times3)/4=6.5$ 度。持续更久的温度占更大权重；只把两个温度读数相加除以二得到 $5$，回答的不是同一个问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real-valued Riemann-integrable function $f$ on a finite interval $[a,b]$ with $a<b$, its average with respect to the input variable $x$ is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限区间 $[a,b]$（$a<b$）上的实值黎曼可积函数 $f$，相对于输入变量 $x$ 的平均值定义为

$$f_{\mathrm{avg}}=\frac{1}{b-a}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[定积分|definite integral]] is the signed accumulated total; $b-a$ is the positive interval length. Division restores the units of $f$. If $f$ is a flow in litres per minute and $x$ is minutes, the integral is litres and the average is litres per minute. A constant function $f(x)=k$ has average $k$, since its integral is $k(b-a)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[定积分]]给出带符号的累积总量，$b-a$ 是正的区间长度；相除后恢复 $f$ 的单位。例如 $f$ 是每分钟多少升的流量，$x$ 以分钟计，则积分单位是升，平均值单位又是升每分钟。常数函数 $f(x)=k$ 的积分为 $k(b-a)$，所以平均值仍为 $k$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## How ordinary averages become this integral
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*普通平均数怎样变成这个积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Split $[a,b]$ into $n$ equal pieces of width $\Delta x=(b-a)/n$, and sample $f$ once in each piece at $\xi_i$. The arithmetic mean of these $n$ readings is exactly a normalised [[黎曼和|Riemann sum]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $[a,b]$ 等分为 $n$ 段，每段宽 $\Delta x=(b-a)/n$，并在第 $i$ 段选一点 $\xi_i$ 读取 $f$。这 $n$ 个读数的算术平均，恰好是除以总宽度的[[黎曼和]]：

$$\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n f(\xi_i)=\frac1{b-a}\sum_{i=1}^n f(\xi_i)\Delta x.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As $n$ grows, the common width tends to zero. Integrability ensures that the right side tends to the integral average for every choice of one sample per piece. For unequal pieces, use weights $\Delta x_i/(b-a)$ instead of $1/n$; otherwise a small piece counts as much as a large one. Merely adding more arbitrarily clustered readings need not approximate the uniform average.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $n$ 增大时，公共段宽趋于零；可积性保证无论每段怎样选取一个采样点，右侧都趋于积分平均值。如果分段不等宽，应使用权重 $\Delta x_i/(b-a)$，而不是 $1/n$，否则短段会与长段占相同份量。仅仅增加任意聚集的读数，不保证逼近均匀平均。

> [!example] An equivalent constant height · 等效的恒定高度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,2]$, the integral is $8/3$, so $f_{\mathrm{avg}}=(8/3)/2=4/3$. A rectangle of width $2$ and height $4/3$ has the same area as the region under the curve. For functions with negative values, the same identity $\int_a^b f=f_{\mathrm{avg}}(b-a)$ preserves the signed total, not the total geometric area.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[0,2]$ 上的 $f(x)=x^2$，积分为 $8/3$，所以 $f_{\mathrm{avg}}=(8/3)/2=4/3$。宽为 $2$、高为 $4/3$ 的矩形，与曲线下方区域面积相同。函数允许取负值时，同一等式 $\int_a^b f=f_{\mathrm{avg}}(b-a)$ 保留的是带符号的总量，不是全部几何面积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This definition requires integrability, not continuity or an elementary antiderivative. Whether the function actually takes the value $f_{\mathrm{avg}}$ somewhere is a separate question: the [[积分中值定理|mean value theorem for integrals]] supplies that guarantee for continuous functions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个定义要求可积，不要求连续或存在初等原函数。函数是否真的在某处取到 $f_{\mathrm{avg}}$，则是另一个问题；[[积分中值定理]]为连续函数提供这个保证。

## State what receives uniform weight
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*说明相对于什么均匀平均*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the upper half of a unit circle, equal horizontal distances and equal distances along the arc do not select heights with the same weights. Averaging the height $y=\sqrt{1-x^2}$ uniformly over horizontal position gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在单位圆的上半圆上，按水平方向等距取点，与沿圆弧等距取点，赋予各个高度的权重不同。把高度 $y=\sqrt{1-x^2}$ 按水平位置均匀平均，得到

$$\frac12\int_{-1}^1\sqrt{1-x^2}\,dx=\frac12\cdot\frac\pi2=\frac\pi4.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For uniform arc length, use the angle $0\le\theta\le\pi$, measured in radians. The radius is one, so an arc segment has length $d\theta$, and its height is $\sin\theta$. The total arc length is $\pi$, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若按弧长均匀平均，使用以弧度计的角度 $0\le\theta\le\pi$。半径为一，所以小圆弧长度为 $d\theta$，高度为 $\sin\theta$；总弧长为 $\pi$，因此得到

$$\frac1\pi\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\,d\theta=\frac2\pi.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both answers are correct for their stated averaging rules. A [[加权平均|weighted average]] makes such differences explicit by stating how much each region counts. Changing coordinates while transforming all weights correctly preserves an average; declaring the new coordinate uniformly weighted generally changes the question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个答案在各自说明的平均规则下都正确。[[加权平均]]通过明确每个区域占多少份量来表达这种区别。换坐标时若正确变换全部权重，平均值保持不变；若直接宣布对新坐标均匀加权，通常就改变了所问的问题。

> [!warning] The interval must have positive length · 区间必须有正长度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The displayed definition does not apply to $a=b$, where division by $b-a$ would be division by zero, or directly to an infinite interval. A limiting average over expanding intervals is a separate construction. With finite unequal bounds, reversing both the integral and the signed denominator preserves their ratio; using a reversed integral with a positive length would incorrectly negate the average.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上述定义不适用于 $a=b$，因为此时要除以零；也不能直接用于无穷区间。沿扩张区间取平均的极限是另一个构造。对有限且不同的两个端点，若同时反转积分方向和带符号的分母，比例保持不变；若反向积分却仍除以正长度，就会错误地把平均值变号。
