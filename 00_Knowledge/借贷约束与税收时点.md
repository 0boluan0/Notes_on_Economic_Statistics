# 当借贷上限约束当前消费时延期征税可在现值财富不变下扩大可行消费
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tax deferral can expand feasible current consumption under a binding borrowing limit without changing present-value wealth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当家庭不能把未来收入借到今天使用时，当前可支配收入本身可以限制消费。延期征税即使由同一家承担等现值的未来增税，也可能放松这个限制。因此，税款现值不变还不足以推出[[李嘉图等价]]；必须检查跨期预算线之外的借贷约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a household cannot borrow against future income, current disposable income can bind consumption. Deferring taxes may relax this limit even when the same household faces an equal-present-value future tax. Equal tax present values therefore do not alone establish [[李嘉图等价|Ricardian equivalence]]; borrowing constraints must also be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设两期家庭收入为 $(10,100)$，利率为零、初始资产为零，税款从 $(5,0)$ 改为 $(0,5)$；政府购买路径不变。家庭可以储蓄但不能借款，即 $s\geq0$，两期消费满足 $c_0=10-T_0-s$、$c_1=100-T_1+s$。政府可向其他不受此约束的储蓄者借款，所以这只是受约束家庭的局部比较，不是假设整个经济无人提供资金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let two-date income be $(10,100)$, with zero interest and initial assets, and taxes change from $(5,0)$ to $(0,5)$ while public purchases are fixed. Saving is allowed but borrowing is prohibited: $s\geq0$, with $c_0=10-T_0-s$ and $c_1=100-T_1+s$. The government can borrow from other unconstrained savers; this is a partial comparison for the constrained household.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两方案都有 $c_0+c_1=105$。若效用为 $\ln c_0+\ln c_1$，不受约束的最优选择是 $(52.5,52.5)$，但两方案都无法达到：当期税收方案要求 $c_0\leq5$，延期税收方案要求 $c_0\leq10$。在这两个区间内，增加当期消费的边际效用净收益为正：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both schedules give $c_0+c_1=105$. With utility $\ln c_0+\ln c_1$, the unconstrained optimum is $(52.5,52.5)$, but it is infeasible under either schedule: current consumption is bounded by $5$ or $10$. On both feasible intervals, its net marginal utility gain is positive:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{d}{dc_0}\left[\ln c_0+\ln(105-c_0)\right]
=\frac{1}{c_0}-\frac{1}{105-c_0}>0
\quad (0<c_0\leq10).
$$

因此，最优消费从 $(5,100)$ 变为 $(10,95)$，两方案都选 $s=0$。减税 $5$ 全部进入当期消费，但一生资源没有增加；变化来自政府提供了家庭自身无法取得的跨期资金安排。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Optimal consumption changes from $(5,100)$ to $(10,95)$, with $s=0$ in both cases. The tax cut of $5$ is fully consumed now without increasing lifetime resources. The government has supplied intertemporal funding that the household could not obtain privately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本例把借贷上限固定为零，并验证它确实约束选择。若原来能够自由借贷，或政策同时改变了银行愿意提供的额度，结果要重新求解；“存在信用市场不完美”本身不是当期消费必升的充分条件。这里的外生额度限制，也不等同于需要另行证明均衡机制的[[信贷配给]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example fixes the borrowing limit at zero and verifies that it binds. Unrestricted credit or a policy-induced change in available credit requires a new solution. Credit-market imperfections alone do not guarantee higher current consumption. This exogenous limit is also distinct from [[信贷配给|equilibrium credit rationing]], whose mechanism requires separate justification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
