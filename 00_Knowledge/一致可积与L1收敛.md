# Uniform integrability and L¹ convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一致可积与L¹收敛

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Convergence in probability says that errors beyond any fixed tolerance become rare. It does not stop the remaining rare errors from being enormous. **Uniform integrability supplies precisely the missing tail control** that turns this kind of convergence into vanishing average absolute error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

依概率收敛说的是：超过任意固定容差的误差越来越少见。但剩余的罕见误差仍可能极大。**一致可积恰好补上所缺的尾部控制**，从而把这种收敛升级为平均绝对误差趋零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read [[依概率收敛|convergence in probability]] as $P(|X_n-X|>\varepsilon)\to0$ for each fixed $\varepsilon>0$; [[L1收敛|$L^1$ convergence]] as $E|X_n-X|\to0$; and [[一致可积|uniform integrability]] as one cutoff controlling every member’s absolute tail expectation. The criterion below connects these three different statements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里[[依概率收敛]]指每个固定$\varepsilon>0$都有$P(|X_n-X|>\varepsilon)\to0$；[[L1收敛|$L^1$收敛]]指$E|X_n-X|\to0$；[[一致可积]]指用同一个阈值控制所有成员的绝对尾部期望。以下判据连接这三件不同的事。

> [!important] The criterion · 收敛判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X_n$ be integrable real random variables on one probability space, and let $X$ be a finite real random variable there. Suppose $X_n\to X$ in probability. Then $\{X_n:n\ge1\}$ is uniformly integrable if and only if $X$ is integrable and $X_n\to X$ in $L^1$. In particular, the UI condition also guarantees that the limit has a finite first absolute moment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设同一概率空间上的实随机变量$X_n$都可积，$X$是其上的有限实随机变量，并且$X_n$依概率收敛到$X$。则$\{X_n:n\ge1\}$一致可积，当且仅当$X$可积且$X_n$在$L^1$中收敛到$X$。特别地，UI条件还自动保证极限的一阶绝对矩有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Almost-sure convergence is a useful special case because it [[几乎必然收敛推出概率收敛|implies convergence in probability]]. No independence or martingale assumption is needed for the criterion itself. The rare spikes in [[L1有界不推一致可积|the bounded-first-moment counterexample]] converge almost surely to zero but fail UI, leaving average error equal to one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

几乎必然收敛[[几乎必然收敛推出概率收敛|能推出依概率收敛]]，所以是一个常用特例。判据本身不要求独立性，也不要求鞅结构。[[L1有界不推一致可积|一阶矩有界的尖峰反例]]虽然几乎必然趋于零，却不一致可积，平均误差因此始终等于1。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why removing the tails makes the limit work
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

去掉尾部后，为什么可以取极限

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof has two jobs: establish that the limit is integrable, then control the error. UI first gives a common bound $C$ on $E|X_n|$. For any fixed truncation level $K$, the bounded continuous function $x\mapsto\min(|x|,K)$ carries convergence in probability into convergence of its expectations. This bounded step follows from splitting an error into the events where it is at most $\delta$ and greater than $\delta$, as in the estimate below. Hence $E\min(|X|,K)\le C$. Increasing $K$ exhausts the absolute value; the [[单调收敛定理|monotone convergence theorem]] identifies the limit of these nonnegative increasing truncated expectations as $E|X|$. Therefore $E|X|\le C<\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明先要保证极限可积，再控制误差。UI首先给所有$E|X_n|$一个共同上界$C$。固定截断高度$K$，有界连续函数$x\mapsto\min(|x|,K)$把依概率收敛转成其期望收敛；这个有界步骤可按误差是否超过$\delta$分成两部分，具体估计与下文相同。因此$E\min(|X|,K)\le C$。增大$K$会逐渐恢复完整绝对值；[[单调收敛定理]]保证这些非负递增截断的期望极限就是$E|X|$，故$E|X|\le C<\infty$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now clip a real number into $[-K,K]$ by $T_K(x)=\max(-K,\min(x,K))$. Clipping changes it only in the tail and never enlarges differences. The triangle inequality gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在用$T_K(x)=\max(-K,\min(x,K))$把数值截在$[-K,K]$内：它只改动尾部，而且不会放大两数之差。三角不等式给出

$$
E|X_n-X|\le
E[|X_n|\mathbf1_{\{|X_n|>K\}}]
+E|T_K(X_n)-T_K(X)|
+E[|X|\mathbf1_{\{|X|>K\}}].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose $K$ large. UI makes the first term small for every $n$; integrability of $X$ makes the last term small. For this fixed $K$, the middle term obeys, for every $\delta>0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先选足够大的$K$。UI使第一项对全部$n$都小，$X$可积使最后一项小。固定这个$K$，中间项对任意$\delta>0$满足

$$E|T_K(X_n)-T_K(X)|\le\delta+2K\,P(|X_n-X|>\delta).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Where the original error is at most $\delta$, clipping leaves an error at most $\delta$; elsewhere the two clipped values differ by at most $2K$. Convergence in probability removes the probability term as $n$ increases. Then let $\delta$ be small. This establishes $L^1$ convergence without exchanging an uncontrolled expectation and limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原误差不超过$\delta$的地方，截断后也不超过$\delta$；其余地方，两个截断值最多相差$2K$。固定$\delta$后，依概率收敛让概率项随$n$趋零，再取很小的$\delta$即可。这就证明了$L^1$收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why L¹ convergence also forces UI
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么反向也成立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $E|X_n-X|\to0$ and $X$ is integrable, then the late terms are close in average absolute error to one integrable variable. The following bound separates that approximation error from the tail of $X$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若$E|X_n-X|\to0$且$X$可积，后面的项便在平均绝对误差上接近同一个可积变量。下面的界把近似误差与$X$自己的尾部分开：

$$|X_n|\mathbf1_{\{|X_n|>K\}}\le2|X_n-X|+2|X|\mathbf1_{\{|X|>K/2\}}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the tail of $X_n$, either $|X|\le K/2$, in which case the difference is at least half of $|X_n|$, or $|X|>K/2$, where the ordinary triangle inequality suffices. First choose $n$ large to reduce the first expectation, then $K$ large to reduce the second. The finitely many earlier $X_n$ are integrable, so their tails can be covered by enlarging the same cutoff once more. This gives one bound for the entire sequence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在$X_n$的尾部上，若$|X|\le K/2$，两者之差至少为$|X_n|$的一半；若$|X|>K/2$，普通三角不等式就足够。先让$n$足够大以压低第一项期望，再增大$K$压低第二项。剩下有限多个较早的$X_n$都可积，再放大同一个阈值就能同时覆盖它们，于是得到全序列的一致尾部控制。

## Passing a stopped process to its terminal value
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 可选停止中的作用

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a discrete-time [[鞅定义|martingale]] $M_n$ and a [[停时定义|stopping time]] $T<\infty$ almost surely, $n\wedge T=\min(n,T)$ stops the process by time $n$. On every path with finite $T$, once $n\ge T$ the value $M_{n\wedge T}$ is exactly $M_T$. Thus the stopped sequence converges almost surely to $M_T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设$M_n$是离散时间[[鞅定义|鞅]]，[[停时定义|停时]]$T<\infty$几乎处处成立。$n\wedge T=\min(n,T)$表示最迟在$n$时截断。对每条$T$有限的路径，一旦$n\ge T$，$M_{n\wedge T}$就恰好等于$M_T$，故停止序列几乎必然趋于$M_T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the entire stopped family $\{M_{n\wedge T}:n\ge0\}$ is UI, the criterion makes $M_T$ integrable and gives $E|M_{n\wedge T}-M_T|\to0$. Consequently $E[M_{n\wedge T}]\to E[M_T]$. This is the limit step needed in [[可选停止条件|optional stopping]], after the finite-truncation identities have been justified. An almost surely finite $T$ by itself does not supply UI.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若整个停止族$\{M_{n\wedge T}:n\ge0\}$一致可积，判据保证$M_T$可积且$E|M_{n\wedge T}-M_T|\to0$，进而$E[M_{n\wedge T}]\to E[M_T]$。这是[[可选停止条件|可选停止]]在先证明有限截断等式后，走向终值所需的极限步骤；仅有$T$几乎处处有限并不自动保证UI。
