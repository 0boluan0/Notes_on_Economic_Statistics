# 连续复利用指数函数连接现值与终值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Continuous compounding links present and future value with the exponential function*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子公式
> 当名义复利频率趋于无穷，积累因子趋于指数函数。以连续复利率 $r$ 计息 $t$ 年：
> $$FV_t=PV e^{rt},\qquad PV=FV_t e^{-rt}.$$
> 对应的有效年利率是 $e^r-1$，不能把连续复利率 $r$ 直接当作 EAR。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> As nominal compounding frequency tends to infinity, the accumulation factor tends to the exponential function. At a continuously compounded rate $r$ for $t$ years,
> $$FV_t=PV e^{rt},\qquad PV=FV_t e^{-rt}.$$
> The corresponding effective annual rate is $e^r-1$; the continuous rate $r$ is not itself the EAR.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续复利只是另一种利率报价与换算约定，不会改变“先匹配现金流日期与利率口径”的原则。若一道题明确给出连续复利收益率，就使用 $e^{-rt}$；若给的是离散每期有效率，则使用 $(1+r)^{-t}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuous compounding is another rate convention; it does not remove the need to match cash-flow dates and rate bases. Use $e^{-rt}$ when the quoted yield is continuously compounded and $(1+r)^{-t}$ when $r$ is a discrete effective rate per period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 连续复利率为 10% 时，一年贴现因子为什么不是 $1/1.10$？
>
> **答案：** 10% 是连续复利报价，对应贴现因子是 $e^{-0.10}$；$1/1.10$ 对应 10% 的年度离散有效率，是另一种约定。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With a continuously compounded rate of 10%, why is the one-year discount factor not $1/1.10$?
>
> **Answer:** The 10% is a continuous quote, so the factor is $e^{-0.10}$. The factor $1/1.10$ corresponds to a 10% discrete effective annual rate, a different convention.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
