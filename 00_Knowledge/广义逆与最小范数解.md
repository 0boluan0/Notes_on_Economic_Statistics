# 广义逆与最小范数解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pseudoinverses and minimum-norm solutions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 在矩阵非方阵、秩亏或 $Ax=b$ 无精确解/有多解时，用一个唯一规则选出最小二乘与最小范数解。
> **具体锚点：** 过定方程无法精确拟合时，$A^+b$ 先让残差最小；如果有多个同样好的系数，再选 Euclidean 范数最小者。
> **核心难点：** 左逆、右逆、普通逆和 Moore–Penrose 伪逆对应不同秩条件；伪逆不是把零奇异值也取倒数。
> **为什么重要：** 它连接最小二乘、秩亏回归、逆问题、SVD 和数值稳定性。
> **继续：** 先用 SVD 读清哪些方向被保留或消除，再区分最小残差与最小范数两层选择。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** For rectangular or rank-deficient matrices, or systems with no exact or many exact solutions, the pseudoinverse selects a unique least-squares, minimum-norm answer.
> **Concrete anchor:** If an overdetermined system cannot fit exactly, $A^+b$ minimises the residual; among tied coefficient vectors, it chooses the one with smallest Euclidean norm.
> **Central difficulty:** Left, right, ordinary, and Moore–Penrose inverses have different rank conditions. Zero singular values are left at zero rather than inverted.
> **Why it matters:** It links least squares, rank-deficient regression, inverse problems, SVD, and numerical stability.
> **Continue with:** Read the retained singular directions through [[奇异值分解与低秩近似|SVD]], then distinguish residual minimisation from norm minimisation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 左逆、右逆与可逆
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Left inverses, right inverses, and invertibility*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满列秩矩阵可有左逆 L 使 $LA=I$，对应一一映射；满行秩矩阵可有右逆 R 使 $AR=I$，对应满射。只有方阵满秩时左右逆合一并唯一。非方阵不应被要求拥有普通双侧逆。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A full-column-rank matrix has a left inverse $L$ with $LA=I$, corresponding to injectivity. A full-row-rank matrix has a right inverse $R$ with $AR=I$, corresponding to surjectivity. Only a full-rank square matrix has a unique two-sided inverse. A rectangular matrix should not be expected to have an ordinary two-sided inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Moore–Penrose 伪逆
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 $A=U\Sigma V^T$ 把非零奇异值取倒数得到 $A^+=V\Sigma^+U^T$。若方程无精确解，$A^+b$ 给最小二乘解中范数最小者；若有多个精确解，它给最小范数解。四个 Moore–Penrose 条件使该矩阵对任意 A 唯一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From $A=U\Sigma V^T$, invert the nonzero singular values to obtain $A^+=V\Sigma^+U^T$. If no exact solution exists, $A^+b$ is the minimum-norm member of the least-squares solutions; if many exact solutions exist, it is the minimum-norm exact solution. The four Moore–Penrose equations make $A^+$ unique for every matrix $A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么它选最小范数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the pseudoinverse selects minimum norm*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任何系数向量都可分解为行空间成分与零空间成分。两者正交，而零空间成分不改变 $Ax$，只会增大系数范数。$A^+b$ 丢掉这一无法由数据区分的成分，因此在相同拟合中范数最小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every coefficient vector decomposes into a row-space component and a null-space component. These components are orthogonal, while the null component does not change $Ax$ and only increases coefficient norm. $A^+b$ removes this unidentified component and therefore has minimum norm among equally fitting solutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：一个方程、两个未知数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: one equation and two unknowns*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方程 $x_1+x_2=2$ 有无穷多解 $(1+t,1-t)$。令 $A=(1\ 1)$，则 $A^+=A^T(AA^T)^{-1}=(1/2,1/2)^T$，所以 $A^+b=(1,1)^T$。其他解都加上 $t(1,-1)^T\in N(A)$，范数平方变为 $2+2t^2$，因此 $t=0$ 确实最小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation $x_1+x_2=2$ has infinitely many solutions $(1+t,1-t)$. With $A=(1\ 1)$, $A^+=A^T(AA^T)^{-1}=(1/2,1/2)^T$, so $A^+b=(1,1)^T$. Every other solution adds $t(1,-1)^T\in N(A)$ and has squared norm $2+2t^2$, which is minimised at $t=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 诊断与数值边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnostics and numerical boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

很小但非零的奇异值取倒数后会放大噪声，因此数值实现需声明秩容差。截断 SVD 或 ridge 正则化会改变估计目标：它们通过引入偏差来抑制不稳定方向，不应被当成与精确伪逆完全同义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking reciprocals of very small but nonzero singular values amplifies noise, so numerical implementations need an explicit rank tolerance. Truncated SVD and ridge regularisation change the estimation target by introducing bias to suppress unstable directions; they are not exact synonyms for the pseudoinverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*

### 满列秩的高矩阵为什么可能有左逆却没有右逆？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a tall full-column-rank matrix have a left inverse but no right inverse?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它是一一映射但未必覆盖整个输出空间；左逆可恢复输入，右逆要求对每个输出都能找到原像。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It is injective but need not cover the output space. A left inverse recovers inputs; a right inverse would require every output to have a preimage.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 伪逆在无解和多解两种情况下分别选择什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does the pseudoinverse select in the inconsistent and underdetermined cases?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 无解时先最小化残差，再在并列解中取最小范数；有精确多解时直接取最小范数精确解。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With no exact solution, it first minimises residual norm and then coefficient norm among ties. With many exact solutions, it chooses the minimum-norm exact solution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么伪逆不对零奇异值取倒数？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does the pseudoinverse not invert zero singular values?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 零奇异值对应被 A 完全消除的输入方向，数据中没有信息可恢复它；取倒数既无定义也会伪造识别。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A zero singular value is an input direction erased completely by $A$, so the data contain no information with which to recover it. Its reciprocal is undefined and would fabricate identification.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明最小残差与最小范数是哪两层问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distinguish residual minimisation from norm minimisation in your own words.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 最小残差决定拟合向量要离 b 多近；若多个系数都生成同样好的拟合，最小范数再从中选离原点最近的系数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Residual minimisation chooses the attainable fit closest to $b$. If several coefficient vectors yield that fit, norm minimisation chooses the coefficient vector closest to the origin.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
