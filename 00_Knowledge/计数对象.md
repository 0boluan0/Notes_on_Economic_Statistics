# What are we counting?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*计数对象*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A counting problem asks how many **different valid outcomes** there are. Before we can count them, we need to know what one outcome records and when two descriptions mean the same outcome. Choosing a pair of people, assigning two jobs, and recording two draws can involve the same names but produce different answers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计数问题问的是：一共有多少个**不同的合法结果**。要数清楚，先得知道一个结果记录什么，以及两份描述在什么时候算同一个结果。选两个人、分配两个职位、记录两次抽取，虽然可以用到同一批名字，答案却会不同。

> [!example] Three people, three questions · 三个人，三种问题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A,B,C$ name three distinct people. In an ordered pair $(A,B)$, the first and second positions have different meanings. Braces such as $\{A,B\}$ record only membership, so $\{A,B\}=\{B,A\}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用 $A,B,C$ 表示三个不同的人。有序对 $(A,B)$ 的第一、第二位置各有含义；花括号 $\{A,B\}$ 只记录成员，因此 $\{A,B\}=\{B,A\}$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> | What one outcome records | All valid outcomes | Count |
> |---|---|---:|
> | A two-person committee, with no distinct roles | $\{A,B\},\{A,C\},\{B,C\}$ | $3$ |
> | A captain and a deputy, held by different people | $(A,B),(A,C),(B,A),(B,C),(C,A),(C,B)$ | $6$ |
> | Two successive draws, with the name returned after the first | $(A,A),(A,B),(A,C),(B,A),(B,B),(B,C),(C,A),(C,B),(C,C)$ | $9$ |
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一行只选成员，调换书写顺序不产生新委员会；第二行记录“队长、助理”，交换人选就改变职位分配；第三行记录两次抽取的先后结果，放回后允许同一个人出现两次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first question uses an unordered selection without repetition, counted by a [[组合数|binomial coefficient]]. The second uses an ordered selection without repetition, a [[排列|permutation]]. The third allows repetition. These are different collections of outcomes, even before any formula is applied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一问是无序、无重复的选择，由[[组合数]]计数；第二问是有序、无重复的选择，即[[排列]]；第三问则允许重复。它们在使用任何公式之前，就已经是三个不同的结果集合。

## Define one outcome before choosing a method
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 先写对象，再选方法

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful way to specify the model is to finish the sentence “One outcome is …”. Say what is chosen, how many items or positions it has, which labels distinguish objects, whether an object may be used again, and which restrictions make it valid. “Exactly three” and “at most three” describe different collections. Then compare two concrete descriptions: would swapping their order or renaming an otherwise identical item change the outcome?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以先补全一句话：“一个结果是……”。说明选什么、有几个成员或位置、哪些标签区分对象、同一对象能否再次使用，以及满足什么限制才合法。“恰好三个”和“至多三个”对应不同的集合。再拿两份具体描述比较：交换顺序，或者只给原本无法区分的对象改个名字，会不会改变结果？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $S$ denote the finite set of valid outcomes. The notation $|S|$ means its number of elements. A method succeeds when every element of $S$ is accounted for exactly once, or when a deliberate overcount is corrected by a justified factor. An empty set of valid outcomes has count zero; a single valid empty arrangement has count one. “Choose no members” can be one valid choice, even though “choose two different members from one person” has none.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $S$ 表示所有合法结果组成的有限集合，$|S|$ 表示其中的元素个数。一个计数方法需要恰好覆盖每个结果一次；如果有意重计，就要有理由说明怎样修正。没有合法结果时，数量为零；如果空安排本身是一个合法结果，数量则为一。“一个成员也不选”可以是一种合法选择，而“从一个人中选两个不同成员”没有合法选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once $S$ is clear, its structure suggests a method. Disjoint cases can be added using the [[加法法则|sum rule]]. Successive choices with a fixed number of extensions at each step use the [[乘法法则|product rule]]. A reversible encoding transfers the count by [[双射计数|bijection]]. If every outcome has the same number of extra descriptions, the [[除法法则|division rule]] removes that overcount. Overlapping categories require an explicit correction such as [[容斥原理|inclusion–exclusion]]. Each method must fit the specified objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

明确 $S$ 之后，它的结构会提示方法。不交的类别用[[加法法则]]相加；每一步延伸数量固定的逐步选择用[[乘法法则]]；可逆的编码用[[双射计数]]转移数量；每个结果都有同样多份额外描述时，用[[除法法则]]消去重计；类别重叠则需要[[容斥原理]]这样的修正。方法必须对应已经定义好的对象。

## A position can carry information
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*位置也可能是结果的一部分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A three-bit string records a zero or one in each of three labelled positions. Its eight possibilities are $000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111$. The strings with exactly two ones are $011,101,110$: choosing the two positions that contain one determines the entire string. For length $n$, this gives $2^n$ strings in total and $\binom nk$ with exactly $k$ ones, for integers $0\le k\le n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个三位二进制串，在三个有区别的位置各记录零或一。八种可能为 $000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111$；恰有两个一的是 $011,101,110$。只要选定哪两个位置放一，整个串就确定了。推广到长度 $n$，共有 $2^n$ 个串；对整数 $0\le k\le n$，恰有 $k$ 个一的串有 $\binom nk$ 个。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If instead we record only the number of ones, the possible records for length three are $0,1,2,3$. For example, three different strings now share the record $2$. We changed what counts as one outcome. When $n=0$, there is one empty string; it has zero ones.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果只记录“一的个数”，长度三的记录就只有 $0,1,2,3$ 四种。例如，三个不同的串现在都归入记录 $2$。这里改变了什么算作一个结果。长度 $n=0$ 时，有唯一的空串，其中一的个数也是零。

> [!warning] Counting does not assign probabilities · 计数不会自动分配概率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The fraction “favourable outcomes / all outcomes” gives probabilities when the finite underlying outcomes are [[古典概型等可能条件|equally likely]]. If a bag contains three equally likely balls labelled $A_1,A_2,B$, recording only the letter gives two labels, $A$ and $B$, but their probabilities are $2/3$ and $1/3$. Merging descriptions can change their weights.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> “有利结果数 / 总结果数”要变成概率，需要有限底层结果[[古典概型等可能条件|等可能]]。袋中三个球标为 $A_1,A_2,B$，若每个球等可能被抽到，只记录字母虽只有 $A,B$ 两类，概率却是 $2/3$ 和 $1/3$。合并描述后，各类的权重可能不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A count also differs from a growth comparison: [[大Θ记号|Theta notation]] describes the scale of a quantity as an input size grows. We must first specify which objects that quantity counts; changing the model can change both the exact number and its rate of growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计数与增长比较也有区别：[[大Θ记号]]描述某个数量随输入规模增长时的量级。先要确定这个数量在数什么；改变模型，既可能改变精确数量，也可能改变增长速度。
