# 总体是研究对象的集合或分布，样本是按某种机制取得的有限观察

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A population is the target collection or distribution; a sample is a finite set of observations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 总体（population）规定研究面向哪些单位及其分布；样本（sample）是实际取得的有限观察。总体均值用 $E[X]$ 表示，样本均值用 $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$ 表示。样本是否能代表目标总体取决于抽样与观测机制。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The population specifies the units and distribution of interest; the sample contains the observations obtained. The population mean is $E[X]$, whereas the sample mean averages the observed units. Representativeness depends on how units and outcomes enter the data.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

调查 100 所学校不能仅凭数量推断全国学校；若只调查自愿参加的学校，还要检查选择机制。随机抽样负责“谁进入样本”，[[随机分配识别|随机分配]]负责“样本中的谁进入处理组”。两种随机化回答不同问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Surveying 100 schools does not by itself justify a national conclusion. Volunteer recruitment may change the relevant population. Random sampling selects the units observed; [[随机分配识别|random assignment]] allocates treatment. These are different design questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
