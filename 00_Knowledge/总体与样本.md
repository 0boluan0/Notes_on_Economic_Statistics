# Population and sample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*总体与样本*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **population** is what we want to learn about; the **sample** is what we actually observe. To ask about all schools in a country and to collect records from a hundred schools are two different steps. We first define the target, then explain how the observed cases are connected to it. Having many rows of data does not supply that connection by itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**总体**是想要了解的对象，**样本**是实际取得的观察。想研究全国学校，与拿到一百所学校的记录，是两个不同步骤：先明确研究目标，再解释这些观察怎样与目标相连。数据行数很多，本身不会建立这条联系。

^core

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Name the units, measurement and time period
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先明确单位、测量内容与时间范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[观察单位|observational unit]] is the person, school, item or other entity to which a measurement belongs. “All students” is usually too vague: students at which institution, enrolled when, and measured for what outcome? A population definition fixes those boundaries. The sample records the selected units and their observed measurements. In a repeated-measurement study, one person may contribute several rows, so the number of rows need not be the number of distinct people.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[观察单位]]是测量所对应的人、学校、物品或其他实体。“所有学生”通常还不够明确：哪所学校、何时在读、测量什么？总体定义要把这些范围说清。样本则记录被选中的单位及其观测值；重复测量中，一个人可以贡献多行，所以行数未必等于不同人数。

> [!example] Four students, two selected records · 四名学生，两条抽取记录
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Imagine a target population of four students, A–D. Their numbers of library books borrowed last month are $0,2,4,6$. For this teaching example we reveal all four values, so the population mean is
> $$\mu=\frac{0+2+4+6}{4}=3.$$
> If the sample contains B and D, we observe $2,6$ and its mean is $(2+6)/2=4$. The **parameter** $\mu$ summarizes the target population; the sample mean is a [[统计量|statistic]], computed from the selected records. Their difference here is $4-3=1$; taking a sample does not require its mean to equal the population mean.
>
> In an actual study we would usually not know all the population values. Revealing them here lets us see exactly what the sample omits.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设目标总体只有 A–D 四名学生，上月借阅图书数分别为 $0,2,4,6$。教学例子故意展示全部数值，所以总体均值是 $3$。若样本抽到 B、D，就观察到 $2,6$，样本均值为 $4$。描述目标总体的 $\mu$ 是**参数**，从抽中记录计算的均值是[[统计量]]；这次相差 $4-3=1$。样本不要求自己的均值恰好等于总体均值。真实研究通常不知道全部总体数值，这里全部列出只是为了看清样本遗漏了什么。

## A probability model makes the sampling mechanism explicit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*概率模型把抽取机制说清楚*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite population of $N$ members with fixed numerical values $x_1,\ldots,x_N$, an equal-weight population mean is $N^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^N x_j$. If $X$ is the value of one uniformly selected member, its [[期望|expectation]] is this same mean. A weighted target would require its own specified weights; uniform selection is what connects the equal-weight average to this particular probability model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对包含 $N$ 名成员、数值固定为 $x_1,\ldots,x_N$ 的有限总体，等权总体均值是 $N^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^N x_j$。若 $X$ 表示均匀选一名成员得到的数值，它的[[期望]]恰好等于这个均值。若研究目标本来使用不同权重，就需要另行规定；这里是均匀抽取把等权平均与概率模型连起来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Statistics also uses “population” for the distribution of possible observations from a process, such as measurements from future items made under specified production conditions. Then $\mu=E[X]$ names the distribution's mean when $E[|X|]<\infty$. It need not refer to an already enumerated list. Keep the real-world target and the chosen [[随机变量|random-variable model]] connected; neither a distribution name nor the observed sample defines the target for us.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

统计学也用“总体”指某过程可能产生的观测分布，例如指定生产条件下未来产品的测量结果。这时，在 $E[|X|]<\infty$ 条件下，$\mu=E[X]$ 表示分布的均值，不必对应一份已经列全的名单。现实目标与采用的[[随机变量|随机变量模型]]仍须相互对应，不能让分布名称或已拿到的样本替我们决定目标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before observing $n$ draws, write them as random variables $X_1,\ldots,X_n$; after observing them, write their values as $x_1,\ldots,x_n$. The sample-mean rule is $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$, and the observed mean is $\bar x_n=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^nx_i$. Saying “a sample” alone does not say the $X_i$ are [[独立同分布|independent and identically distributed]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还没观察的 $n$ 次抽取可以写成随机变量 $X_1,\ldots,X_n$，已观察的数值写成 $x_1,\ldots,x_n$。样本均值的规则是 $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_iX_i$，实际算出的数值是 $\bar x_n=n^{-1}\sum_ix_i$。只说“样本”，并没有声明这些变量[[独立同分布]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the four-student example, independently and uniformly drawing a name twice **with replacement** gives $4^2=16$ equally likely ordered samples; the same student can appear twice. Drawing two distinct names uniformly **without replacement** gives $4\cdot3=12$ equally likely ordered samples. Each individual draw still has the same marginal distribution over the four values, but the draws are dependent: after drawing A first, the chance of A second is zero, instead of its marginal chance $1/4$. The mechanism therefore affects the [[抽样分布|distribution of sample summaries]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在四学生例子里，独立、均匀地**放回抽取**两次，有 $4^2=16$ 个等可能有序样本，同一人可以重复出现；均匀地**不放回抽取**两人，则有 $4\cdot3=12$ 个等可能有序样本。后者每一次抽取的边际分布仍相同，但两次有依赖：第一位抽到 A 后，第二位为 A 的概率是零，而不是边际概率 $1/4$。因此机制会影响[[抽样分布|样本摘要的概率分布]]。

## Sampling and treatment assignment answer different questions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*抽样与处理分配回答不同问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Random sampling concerns **who enters the observed sample**. [[随机分配识别|Random assignment]] concerns **which treatment an enrolled unit receives**. Randomly assigning tutoring among volunteers does not automatically make those volunteers a random sample of all schools. Conversely, a random sample of schools does not by itself randomize their tutoring participation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机抽样解决**谁进入观察样本**，[[随机分配识别|随机分配]]解决**已经进入研究的单位接受哪种处理**。在自愿学校中随机分配补习，并不会自动让这些学校成为全国学校的随机样本；反过来，随机抽一些学校来调查，也不会自动随机化它们是否参加补习。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A larger sample can reduce chance variation under an appropriate design, but repeating the same selective recruitment need not repair a mismatch with the target population. Check the sampling frame, participation and measurement process before generalizing. Even a random sample need not reproduce every population characteristic exactly in a particular realization. These distinctions explain why “100 schools observed” is a description of the data, not yet a justification for a national conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在合适设计下，扩大样本可以减少随机波动，但不断重复同一种选择性招募，不一定修复与目标总体的错位。推广前要检查抽样名单、参与机制与测量过程；即使随机抽样，一次具体样本也不保证每项特征都与总体精确相同。因此，“观察了一百所学校”是在描述数据，还不是推广至全国的充分依据。
