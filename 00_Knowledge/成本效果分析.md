# 成本效果分析用货币成本比较达到可比结果的不同方案
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cost-effectiveness analysis compares the monetary costs of alternatives that produce comparable outcomes.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

成本效果分析（CEA）把成本用货币表示，把效果保留在可比较的结果单位中，例如减少的事故数或达到规定服务标准的人数。它适合回答“怎样以较少资源取得给定结果”，不必先给结果本身标出货币价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CEA measures costs in money while retaining a comparable outcome unit, such as accidents avoided or people receiving a specified service standard. It can ask how to achieve a given result with fewer resources without monetising the result itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设两套方案都能在同一时期、同一人群中可靠减少 10 起同类事故，且其他效果相同。若折现后的社会资源成本分别为 80 和 100，第一套实现同一结果所需资源更少。这个结论不需要先估计一次事故价值多少。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose two schemes each reliably prevent ten comparable accidents over the same period and population, with identical other effects. Present social costs of 80 and 100 favour the first scheme for delivering that result, without requiring a monetary accident value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

效果不同时，要继续检查比较含义。若 A 花 80 得到 10 单位效果，B 花 100 得到 11 单位效果，从 A 升到 B 多花 20 才多得 1 单位；应讨论这项**增量权衡**是否值得，而不是仅凭两个平均成本比就宣称选出了最优方案。是否能部分实施、能否扩张以及是否互斥，也会改变选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With different effects, examine the incremental trade-off. If A costs 80 for ten units and B costs 100 for eleven, upgrading costs 20 for one additional unit. Average ratios alone do not settle whether that increment is worthwhile. Divisibility, scalability and mutual exclusivity also matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

减少事故、提高识字率和缩短通勤时间不是同一种效果，不能把“每单位成本”直接放在一个榜单里。CEA 还需要明确结果的质量、持续时间及副作用；若这些差异无法用一个共同指标合理表达，应并列呈现，或在可靠估值后使用 [[成本—收益分析]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Accidents avoided, literacy gained and minutes saved are not interchangeable units. Specify outcome quality, duration and side effects. Present material differences separately when one common measure is inadequate, or use [[成本—收益分析|CBA]] if reliable monetary valuation is available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
