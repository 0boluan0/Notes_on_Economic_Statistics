# 左右端点矩形法用每个小区间的左端或右端函数值乘区间宽度近似定积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The left and right endpoint rectangle rules approximate an integral using an endpoint height times each subinterval's width*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限区间 $a<b$，将 $[a,b]$ 等分成 $N$ 个小区间，令 $h=(b-a)/N$、$x_i=a+ih$。左右端点矩形法分别在每段取左端点或右端点作为 [[黎曼和]] 的取样点；连续函数的这两种近似在 $N\to\infty$ 时都趋于 [[定积分]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite interval with $a<b$, use $N$ equal subintervals, $h=(b-a)/N$, and $x_i=a+ih$. These rules choose the left or right endpoint as the sample in each [[黎曼和|Riemann sum]]. For a continuous function, both approximations converge to the [[定积分|definite integral]] as $N\to\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
L_N=h\sum_{i=1}^{N}f(x_{i-1}),\qquad
R_N=h\sum_{i=1}^{N}f(x_i).
$$

每段的近似贡献都是“取样高度乘宽度”；$f$ 为负时贡献也为负。$N$ 个小区间共有 $N+1$ 个端点，但 $L_N$ 和 $R_N$ 各使用 $N$ 个函数值：前者不取 $b$，后者不取 $a$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each contribution is sample height times width, with its sign retained when $f<0$. There are $N+1$ endpoints but each rule uses $N$ function values: $L_N$ omits $b$, while $R_N$ omits $a$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 在整个区间单调递增，则每段左端高度不超过实际高度，右端高度不低于实际高度；由 [[积分保序性]] 得 $L_N\le\int_a^b f\le R_N$。单调递减时不等号反向；函数并不单调时，端点取样本身不保证整体高估或低估。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f$ is increasing throughout the interval, the left endpoint height is no larger than any height in its subinterval, while the right endpoint height is no smaller. [[积分保序性|Order preservation of integration]] gives $L_N\le\int_a^b f\le R_N$. The inequalities reverse for a decreasing function; without monotonicity, endpoint sampling alone gives no general direction of error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $f(x)=x^2$、$[a,b]=[0,1]$、$N=2$ 时，$h=1/2$，所以 $L_2=\tfrac12(0+\tfrac14)=\tfrac18$、$R_2=\tfrac12(\tfrac14+1)=\tfrac58$，确有 $1/8\le1/3\le5/8$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$ with $N=2$, $h=1/2$. Thus $L_2=\tfrac12(0+\tfrac14)=\tfrac18$ and $R_2=\tfrac12(\tfrac14+1)=\tfrac58$, bracketing the exact value $1/3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 在 $[1,3]$ 上用两个小区间近似 $\int_1^3x\,dx$，左右和分别取哪些高度，结果是多少？
>
> **答案：** $h=1$；左和取 $1,2$ 得 $L_2=3$，右和取 $2,3$ 得 $R_2=5$；真值 $4$ 位于二者之间。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Which heights do the two endpoint rules use for $\int_1^3x\,dx$ with two subintervals, and what are the results? **Answer:** With $h=1$, the left heights are $1,2$, giving $L_2=3$; the right heights are $2,3$, giving $R_2=5$. The exact value $4$ lies between them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
