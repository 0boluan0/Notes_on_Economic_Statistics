# Left and right endpoint rules
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*左右端点矩形法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a changing quantity is known only at selected times, one way to estimate its accumulated total is to hold each measured value constant until the next time. The **left endpoint rule** uses the value at the beginning of each short interval; the **right endpoint rule** uses the value at its end. Each contribution is “value × interval width”, and the contributions are added. These are two particular [[黎曼和|Riemann sums]], the sampled sums used to define a [[定积分|definite integral]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个量不断变化，而我们只知道某些时刻的值时，可以先假设它在每小段时间内保持不变，再估计累计总量。**左端点法**用每段开始处的值，**右端点法**用每段结束处的值；把各段的“取样值 × 区间宽度”相加。这是两种特定的 [[黎曼和]]，也就是定义 [[定积分]] 时使用的取样求和。

> [!example] Two rectangles for a changing height · 两个矩形近似变化的高度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$, split the interval at $1/2$. Both widths are $1/2$. Left endpoints give heights $0,1/4$; right endpoints give heights $1/4,1$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[0,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x^2$，在 $1/2$ 处分成两段，每段宽 $1/2$。左端高度是 $0,1/4$，右端高度是 $1/4,1$：
> $$L_2=\frac12\left(0+\frac14\right)=\frac18,
> \qquad R_2=\frac12\left(\frac14+1\right)=\frac58.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The exact integral is $1/3$. The left rectangles lie below this increasing curve and the right rectangles lie above it, so the two estimates bracket the answer.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 积分真值是 $1/3$。曲线一直上升，左端矩形在曲线下方，右端矩形在曲线上方，因此两个近似把答案夹在中间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Writing the sums
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把取样写成公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a<b$ be finite real numbers, let $f$ be real-valued on $[a,b]$, and let the positive integer $N$ count equal subintervals. Their width is $h=(b-a)/N$ and their endpoints are $x_i=a+ih$, for $i=0,\ldots,N$. The subscript $N$ counts **intervals**, not the number of endpoints:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<b$ 为有限实数，$f$ 是 $[a,b]$ 上的实值函数，正整数 $N$ 表示等宽小区间的数量。步长为 $h=(b-a)/N$，端点为 $x_i=a+ih$，其中 $i=0,\ldots,N$。下标 $N$ 数的是**小区间**，不是端点：

$$
L_N=h\sum_{i=1}^{N} f(x_{i-1}),\qquad
R_N=h\sum_{i=1}^{N} f(x_i).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are $N+1$ endpoints altogether, but each sum uses only $N$ values: $L_N$ omits $b$ and $R_N$ omits $a$. A negative function value gives a negative contribution. Thus these rules approximate signed accumulation; they do not automatically give geometric area when the curve crosses the axis. For unequal widths, replace each occurrence of $h$ by that interval's own width $x_i-x_{i-1}$ before adding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总共有 $N+1$ 个端点，但每个和只使用 $N$ 个函数值：$L_N$ 不取 $b$，$R_N$ 不取 $a$。函数值为负时，该段贡献也为负；近似的是带符号的累计量，曲线跨过横轴时不能直接把结果当成几何面积。不等宽分割则要先给每一项乘上它自己的宽度 $x_i-x_{i-1}$，再求和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When the two values bound the answer
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*何时能夹住真值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f$ is nondecreasing throughout $[a,b]$, then on each small interval $f(x_{i-1})\le f(x)\le f(x_i)$. [[积分保序性|Order preservation of integration]] allows these inequalities to be integrated and added. A nonincreasing function reverses the roles:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $f$ 在整个 $[a,b]$ 上单调不减，那么每小段都有 $f(x_{i-1})\le f(x)\le f(x_i)$。利用 [[积分保序性]] 对每段积分再相加，就能得到夹界；单调不增时两者角色对调：

$$
\begin{aligned}
f\text{ nondecreasing}:&\quad L_N\le I\le R_N,\\
f\text{ nonincreasing}:&\quad R_N\le I\le L_N,
\qquad I=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These statements also hold for negative values. On the equal grid, all shared sample terms cancel when the sums are subtracted, giving $R_N-L_N=h[f(b)-f(a)]$. For a monotone function, the guaranteed bracket therefore has width $|R_N-L_N|=h|f(b)-f(a)|$. Merely having a small difference between the two sums supplies no such guarantee without additional information about the function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

函数值为负也不影响这些不等式。在等宽网格上，相减时共同的取样项全部消去，得到 $R_N-L_N=h[f(b)-f(a)]$。单调函数的保证区间宽度因此为 $|R_N-L_N|=h|f(b)-f(a)|$；缺少额外的函数信息时，两种取样结果接近本身并不能保证误差小。

> [!warning] Agreement can miss the middle · 两端一致可能漏掉中间的变化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x(1-x)$ on $[0,1]$ with $N=1$, both endpoint values are zero, so $L_1=R_1=0$. Nevertheless, the integral is $1/6$. The curve rises and then falls between the samples. Endpoint choice alone therefore does not determine the direction or size of the error for a nonmonotone function.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[0,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x(1-x)$，若只取一段，两端值均为零，所以 $L_1=R_1=0$；积分却是 $1/6$。曲线在两个采样点之间先升后降。因此，对非单调函数，仅凭选择左端还是右端，无法判断误差方向或大小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a [[定积分|Riemann-integrable function]], making $h\to0$ makes both sums converge to $I$. In particular, [[连续函数可积|continuity on a finite closed interval]] ensures this. Convergence describes the limit, not an accuracy certificate for one chosen $N$. On the same grid, averaging the two endpoint sums gives the [[梯形法|trapezoidal rule]], $T_N=(L_N+R_N)/2$; sampling the middle of each interval instead gives the [[中点法|midpoint rule]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 [[定积分|黎曼可积函数]]，当 $h\to0$ 时，两种和都会趋于 $I$；特别地，[[连续函数可积|有限闭区间上的连续性]] 足以保证这一点。收敛说的是极限，不是对某个给定 $N$ 的精度认证。同一网格上，左右和的平均值就是 [[梯形法]] 的 $T_N=(L_N+R_N)/2$；改取每小段中间的高度，则得到 [[中点法]]。

> [!question]- Which values are used? · 取哪些高度？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Approximate $\int_1^3x\,dx$ with two intervals. **Answer:** $h=1$; left heights $1,2$ give $L_2=3$, and right heights $2,3$ give $R_2=5$. The exact value $4$ is between them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用两段近似 $\int_1^3x\,dx$。**答案：** $h=1$；左端高度 $1,2$ 得 $L_2=3$，右端高度 $2,3$ 得 $R_2=5$，真值 $4$ 被夹在中间。
