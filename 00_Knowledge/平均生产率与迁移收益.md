# 部门平均劳动生产率之差不能直接充当一名劳动者迁移的增产收益

部门的[[劳动生产率]] $Y/L$ 是已经形成的产出除以就业人数；“多来一名劳动者能增产多少”则是边际问题。原有资本、劳动者技能、工时和工作安排会影响这两者，单凭平均值的大小不能确定迁移造成的产出变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sector's [[劳动生产率|labour productivity]], $Y/L$, divides existing output by employment. How much an additional worker produces is a marginal question. Capital, skills, hours and work organization affect the two measures; average productivity alone does not determine the output effect of migration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个固定资本的 Cobb–Douglas 例子就能看出差别。设部门 $i$ 的产出按共同价格计量，技术为 $Y_i=A_iK_i^{\alpha_i}L_i^{1-\alpha_i}$，其中 $0<\alpha_i<1$。保持 $A_i,K_i$ 不变，对劳动求导：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fixed-capital Cobb–Douglas example makes the distinction explicit. Value output at common prices and let sector $i$ have technology $Y_i=A_iK_i^{\alpha_i}L_i^{1-\alpha_i}$, with $0<\alpha_i<1$. Holding $A_i$ and $K_i$ fixed, differentiate with respect to labour:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
MP_{L,i}=(1-\alpha_i)\frac{Y_i}{L_i}.
$$

假设两个部门的平均产出分别是 2 和 8，但资本指数分别为 0.2 和 0.9，则劳动边际产出分别为 1.6 和 0.8。把少量同质劳动从第一个部门移向第二个部门，在这些固定投入条件下，总产出的一阶变化反而为 $(0.8-1.6)\,dL<0$。这是构造的反例：较高的平均产出没有保证较高的边际增产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose average products are 2 and 8, while capital exponents are 0.2 and 0.9. Marginal products of labour are then 1.6 and 0.8. Moving a small amount of homogeneous labour from the first sector to the second, under the stated fixed-input conditions, gives a first-order total-output change of $(0.8-1.6)\,dL<0$. This constructed counterexample shows that a higher average product need not mean a higher marginal product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，[[离农与生产率重配|固定各部门平均生产率的重配实验]]是有条件的核算反事实。实际迁移可能同时改变资本配置、生产率、就业状态和生活成本，个人工资收益也不是总产出变化的同义词。判断迁移机制，需要额外的生产、就业和选择条件；[[剩余劳动力|Lewis 的剩余劳动力条件]]正是在约束移出劳动造成的边际产出损失，不能由农业平均收入低直接推出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[离农与生产率重配|reallocation experiment with fixed average products]] is therefore a conditional accounting counterfactual. Actual migration can change capital allocation, productivity, employment and living costs; a person's wage gain is not the same as a change in total output. A migration mechanism needs additional production, employment and selection conditions. The [[剩余劳动力|surplus-labour condition in the Lewis model]] restricts the marginal output lost when workers leave; low average agricultural income alone does not establish it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- McMillan、Rodrik、Verduzco-Gallo（2014），[Globalization, Structural Change, and Productivity Growth, with an Update on Africa](https://drodrik.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum7106/files/dani-rodrik/files/globalization_structural_change_productivity_growth_with_africa_update.pdf)，印刷第15–16页：明确区分平均与边际生产率，给出 Cobb–Douglas 下劳动份额的作用，并说明固定部门生产率的反事实条件。
- 偏导公式和两部门反例由上述生产函数直接推导；资本指数是构造参数，不是农业、制造业或任一国家的估计值。工资和生活成本提示用于界定“产出收益”的问题范围，并非迁移回报估计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paper distinguishes average from marginal productivity and states the fixed-sector-productivity assumption. The derivative and counterexample are derived from the specified production functions; the capital exponents are illustrative, not empirical estimates. The wage and living-cost discussion clarifies the outcome being studied rather than estimating migration returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
