# Cancellation in congruences
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*同余消去律*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplication can hide a difference between remainder classes. Modulo ten, multiplying either $3$ or $8$ by $2$ gives remainder $6$. Seeing equal remainders after multiplication therefore does not always let us recover equal remainders before multiplication. A **cancellation law** specifies exactly what we can conclude when the same factor occurs on both sides.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘法可能把原本不同的余数类变成同一个类。模十时，$3$ 和 $8$ 分别乘以 $2$，余数都会变成 $6$。因此，乘完以后余数相同，并不总能反推出乘以前余数也相同。**同余消去律**说明：两边有同一个因子时，准确地能够得到什么结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The modulus may have to shrink
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*模数可能需要缩小*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The statement $u\equiv v\pmod n$ uses [[同余|congruence]]: it means that $n$ [[整除|divides]] $u-v$. The number $\gcd(c,n)$ is the [[最大公因数|greatest common divisor]] of $c$ and $n$. Because $n>1$ is positive, this gcd is a positive integer even when $c$ is zero or negative. It divides $n$, so $n/d$ below is a positive integer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$u\equiv v\pmod n$ 使用[[同余]]关系，意思是 $n$ [[整除]] $u-v$。$\gcd(c,n)$ 是 $c,n$ 的[[最大公因数]]。因为 $n>1$ 为正，这个最大公因数总是正整数，即使 $c$ 为零或负数也一样。它整除 $n$，所以下面的 $n/d$ 是正整数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a,b,c\in\mathbb Z$, let $n>1$ be an integer, and set $d=\gcd(c,n)$. The general cancellation statement is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a,b,c\in\mathbb Z$、整数 $n>1$，并令 $d=\gcd(c,n)$。消去共同系数的一般结论是

$$
ca\equiv cb\pmod n
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\frac nd\mid(a-b).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $n/d>1$, the right-hand side is $a\equiv b\pmod{n/d}$. When $d=n$, the coefficient $c$ is divisible by $n$, so the original congruence holds for all $a,b$. Cancellation leaves only $1\mid(a-b)$ and provides no restriction; this includes $c=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $n/d>1$ 时，右边就是 $a\equiv b\pmod{n/d}$。当 $d=n$ 时，$c$ 本来就被 $n$ 整除，原同余对所有 $a,b$ 都成立，消去后只剩 $1\mid(a-b)$，不给任何限制；这也包含 $c=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $2\cdot8\equiv2\cdot3\pmod{10}$. Since $\gcd(2,10)=2$, correct cancellation gives $8\equiv3\pmod5$, not modulo ten. The ordinary equality rule that any nonzero factor may be cancelled does not carry over to congruences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $2\cdot8\equiv2\cdot3\pmod{10}$。因为 $\gcd(2,10)=2$，正确消去得到 $8\equiv3\pmod5$，而不是模 10。不能把普通等式中“非零即可约掉”的规则照搬到同余式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In that example, the original difference is $16-6=10$, which is divisible by $10$. After removing the factor $2$, the difference is $8-3=5$. It is divisible by $5$ but not by $10$. The reduced modulus records exactly the information that remains. For a negative factor such as $c=-2$, the gcd is still $2$ and the same reduced modulus applies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这个例子中，原来的差是 $16-6=10$，能被 $10$ 整除；去掉因子 $2$ 后，差变成 $8-3=5$，能被 $5$ 整除，却不能被 $10$ 整除。缩小后的模数准确记录了剩下的信息。若共同因子为 $c=-2$，最大公因数仍是 $2$，缩模结论也相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the reduced-modulus rule is exact
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么缩模规则是充要条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Proof: write $c=dc'$ and $n=dn'$, so $\gcd(c',n')=1$. The condition $n\mid c(a-b)$ is equivalent to $n'\mid c'(a-b)$. By [[Bézout等式|Bézout's identity]], some $u,v$ satisfy $uc'+vn'=1$. Multiplying by $a-b$ shows that $n'\mid(a-b)$. The reverse implication follows by multiplying back by $c$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明：写 $c=dc'$、$n=dn'$，则 $\gcd(c',n')=1$，而 $n\mid c(a-b)$ 等价于 $n'\mid c'(a-b)$。由 [[Bézout等式]]，存在 $u,v$ 使 $uc'+vn'=1$；乘以 $a-b$ 后可见 $n'\mid(a-b)$。反方向直接乘回 $c$ 即得。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here is the divisibility argument in detail. Since $d$ divides both inputs, write $c=dc^{\prime}$ and $n=dn^{\prime}$ with integers $c^{\prime},n^{\prime}$ and $n^{\prime}>0$. These new integers are coprime: if a positive integer $h>1$ divided both, then $dh>d$ would divide both $c$ and $n$, contradicting the definition of $d$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把整除论证展开来看：由于 $d$ 整除两个输入，可以写成 $c=dc^{\prime}$、$n=dn^{\prime}$，其中 $c^{\prime},n^{\prime}$ 都是整数且 $n^{\prime}>0$。这两个新整数互素；否则某个正整数 $h>1$ 同时整除它们，$dh>d$ 就会同时整除 $c,n$，与 $d$ 是最大公因数矛盾。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next, $n\mid c(a-b)$ says that $dc^{\prime}(a-b)=qdn^{\prime}$ for some integer $q$. Cancelling the nonzero integer $d$ in this ordinary equality gives $c^{\prime}(a-b)=qn^{\prime}$. Thus $n^{\prime}\mid c^{\prime}(a-b)$. This step cancels in an exact integer equality; it does not assume the congruence cancellation rule we are proving.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着，$n\mid c(a-b)$ 表示存在整数 $q$，使 $dc^{\prime}(a-b)=qdn^{\prime}$。在这个普通整数等式中约去非零整数 $d$，得到 $c^{\prime}(a-b)=qn^{\prime}$，即 $n^{\prime}\mid c^{\prime}(a-b)$。这一步是在精确整数等式中约去因子，没有预先假定正在证明的同余消去律。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $\gcd(c^{\prime},n^{\prime})=1$, [[Bézout等式|Bézout’s identity]] supplies integers $u,v$ with $uc^{\prime}+vn^{\prime}=1$. Multiplication by $a-b$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $\gcd(c^{\prime},n^{\prime})=1$，[[Bézout等式]]给出整数 $u,v$，使 $uc^{\prime}+vn^{\prime}=1$。两边乘以 $a-b$ 得

$$a-b=u\,c^{\prime}(a-b)+v\,n^{\prime}(a-b).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term on the right is divisible by $n^{\prime}$ by the preceding step, and the second visibly contains $n^{\prime}$. Their sum is therefore divisible by $n^{\prime}$. Conversely, if $a-b=kn^{\prime}$ for an integer $k$, then $c(a-b)=dc^{\prime}kn^{\prime}=kc^{\prime}n$, which is divisible by $n$. This proves both directions, including $c=0$ and $n^{\prime}=1$; it never requires an inverse modulo one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

右边第一项由前一步可知能被 $n^{\prime}$ 整除，第二项本身含有因子 $n^{\prime}$，所以它们的和也能被 $n^{\prime}$ 整除。反过来，若 $a-b=kn^{\prime}$，其中 $k$ 为整数，则 $c(a-b)=dc^{\prime}kn^{\prime}=kc^{\prime}n$，能被 $n$ 整除。这样两个方向都已证明，也覆盖 $c=0$、$n^{\prime}=1$；并不需要引入模一的逆元。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When the original modulus can be kept
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*何时能够保留原模数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In particular, the implication from $ca\equiv cb\pmod n$ to $a\equiv b\pmod n$ is valid for all $a,b$ exactly when $\gcd(c,n)=1$. Sufficiency also follows by multiplying by a [[模逆元|modular inverse]]. If $d>1$, take $a=n/d$ and $b=0$: the original congruence holds, but $0<a<n$, so the conclusion fails. This concerns a universally valid rule; an individual congruence with a noncoprime coefficient may still happen to satisfy the stronger conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特别地，“从 $ca\equiv cb\pmod n$ 对任意 $a,b$ 都能推出 $a\equiv b\pmod n$”当且仅当 $\gcd(c,n)=1$：充分性也可由乘 [[模逆元]] 看出；若 $d>1$，取 $a=n/d,b=0$，原式成立但 $0<a<n$，故结论不成立。这里说的是普遍有效的规则；非互素时，某个具体同余式仍可能碰巧满足更强结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The quantifiers matter: for fixed $c,n$, the rule must work for **every** integer pair $a,b$. If $d>1$, the pair $a=n/d,b=0$ defeats it because $ca=(c/d)n$, yet $a$ lies strictly between $0$ and $n$. On the other hand, $2\cdot1\equiv2\cdot11\pmod{10}$ happens to have $1\equiv11\pmod{10}$ as well. A noncoprime factor invalidates a universal inference, not every individual conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的量词很重要：固定 $c,n$ 后，规则要求对**每一对**整数 $a,b$ 都有效。若 $d>1$，取 $a=n/d,b=0$ 就能否定这条规则，因为 $ca=(c/d)n$，但 $a$ 严格介于 $0$ 与 $n$ 之间。另一方面，$2\cdot1\equiv2\cdot11\pmod{10}$ 的确也满足 $1\equiv11\pmod{10}$。非互素因子使全称推理规则失效，并不使每个具体结论都为假。

> [!warning] Nonzero is not enough · 非零还不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For ordinary integer equality, a nonzero common factor can be cancelled. For congruences with the **same modulus**, the universal criterion is coprimality. The [[模逆元存在条件|modular inverse criterion]] gives the same condition, but the general reduced-modulus proof above also works when no inverse exists.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 普通整数等式只要共同因子非零，就可以约去。同余式若要求**保持同一模数**，普遍有效的条件则是互素。[[模逆元存在条件]]给出相同条件，但上面的普遍缩模证明在逆元不存在时也能使用。
