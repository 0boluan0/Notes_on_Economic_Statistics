# 同余式消去系数 c 后模数须除以 gcd(c,n)，互素时才普遍保留原模数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cancelling c in a congruence divides the modulus by gcd(c,n); coprimality permits unrestricted cancellation with the original modulus*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a,b,c\in\mathbb Z$、整数 $n>1$，并令 $d=\gcd(c,n)$。消去共同系数的一般结论是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a,b,c\in\mathbb Z$, let $n>1$ be an integer, and set $d=\gcd(c,n)$. The general cancellation statement is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
ca\equiv cb\pmod n
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\frac nd\mid(a-b).
$$

当 $n/d>1$ 时，右边就是 $a\equiv b\pmod{n/d}$。当 $d=n$ 时，$c$ 本来就被 $n$ 整除，原同余对所有 $a,b$ 都成立，消去后只剩 $1\mid(a-b)$，不给任何限制；这也包含 $c=0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $n/d>1$, the right-hand side is $a\equiv b\pmod{n/d}$. When $d=n$, the coefficient $c$ is divisible by $n$, so the original congruence holds for all $a,b$. Cancellation leaves only $1\mid(a-b)$ and provides no restriction; this includes $c=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明：写 $c=dc'$、$n=dn'$，则 $\gcd(c',n')=1$，而 $n\mid c(a-b)$ 等价于 $n'\mid c'(a-b)$。由 [[Bézout等式]]，存在 $u,v$ 使 $uc'+vn'=1$；乘以 $a-b$ 后可见 $n'\mid(a-b)$。反方向直接乘回 $c$ 即得。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Proof: write $c=dc'$ and $n=dn'$, so $\gcd(c',n')=1$. The condition $n\mid c(a-b)$ is equivalent to $n'\mid c'(a-b)$. By [[Bézout等式|Bézout's identity]], some $u,v$ satisfy $uc'+vn'=1$. Multiplying by $a-b$ shows that $n'\mid(a-b)$. The reverse implication follows by multiplying back by $c$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特别地，“从 $ca\equiv cb\pmod n$ 对任意 $a,b$ 都能推出 $a\equiv b\pmod n$”当且仅当 $\gcd(c,n)=1$：充分性也可由乘 [[模逆元]] 看出；若 $d>1$，取 $a=n/d,b=0$，原式成立但 $0<a<n$，故结论不成立。这里说的是普遍有效的规则；非互素时，某个具体同余式仍可能碰巧满足更强结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In particular, the implication from $ca\equiv cb\pmod n$ to $a\equiv b\pmod n$ is valid for all $a,b$ exactly when $\gcd(c,n)=1$. Sufficiency also follows by multiplying by a [[模逆元|modular inverse]]. If $d>1$, take $a=n/d$ and $b=0$: the original congruence holds, but $0<a<n$, so the conclusion fails. This concerns a universally valid rule; an individual congruence with a noncoprime coefficient may still happen to satisfy the stronger conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $2\cdot8\equiv2\cdot3\pmod{10}$。因为 $\gcd(2,10)=2$，正确消去得到 $8\equiv3\pmod5$，而不是模 10。不能把普通等式中“非零即可约掉”的规则照搬到同余式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $2\cdot8\equiv2\cdot3\pmod{10}$. Since $\gcd(2,10)=2$, correct cancellation gives $8\equiv3\pmod5$, not modulo ten. The ordinary equality rule that any nonzero factor may be cancelled does not carry over to congruences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
