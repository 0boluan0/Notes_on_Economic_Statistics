# 算术平均收益概括单期回报而几何平均收益复现跨期复合增长
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The arithmetic mean summarizes one-period returns, while the geometric mean reproduces compounded growth across periods*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对等长的 $n$ 期简单总回报 $R_1,\ldots,R_n$，设每期 $1+R_i>0$。算术平均把各期回报相加再平均；几何平均则找一个恒定回报 $g$，使连续获得它与原回报序列有相同的累计增长：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n$ equal-length simple total returns with $1+R_i>0$, the arithmetic mean averages the returns. The geometric mean is the constant return $g$ that produces the same compounded growth as the original sequence:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\bar R=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^{n}R_i,
\qquad g=\left[\prod_{i=1}^{n}(1+R_i)\right]^{1/n}-1.$$

同一笔钱没有外部流入流出，且分配已经按约定计入总回报时，$W_n=W_0\prod_i(1+R_i)=W_0(1+g)^n$。例如财富 100→150→100，两期回报为 $1/2$ 与 $-1/3$；算术平均为 $1/12\approx8.33\%$，几何平均为 0%，准确复现“回到原点”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For wealth without external flows, with distributions included consistently, $W_n=W_0(1+g)^n$. A path of 100→150→100 has returns of $1/2$ and $-1/3$: the arithmetic mean is about 8.33%, while the geometric mean is zero, reproducing the unchanged terminal wealth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正增长因子满足算术—几何平均不等式，所以 $g\le\bar R$，只有所有期回报相同时才取等号。这不是两种算法谁“更真实”：描述或估计单期回报均值用算术平均；描述这条已实现路径的复合增长用几何平均。历史均值能否预测下一期，还需要可比性和稳定性假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The arithmetic–geometric mean inequality gives $g\le\bar R$, with equality only for identical period returns. The statistics answer different questions: one-period mean versus realized compound growth. Using a historical mean to predict the next period additionally requires comparability and stability assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多期组合若再平衡，先按[[单期组合收益|各期权重]]算出每期组合回报，再复合；不能用期初权重把各资产的多年几何平均直接加成组合增长率。若净资产变负、回报分母为零或外部现金流未处理，上式也不能直接套用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a rebalanced portfolio, calculate each period's return from that period's [[单期组合收益|weights]] before compounding. Initial-weight averages of asset geometric returns do not generally give portfolio growth. Negative wealth, zero denominators, or untreated external flows fall outside this setup.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
