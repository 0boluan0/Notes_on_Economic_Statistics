# 标准推动浪是由三个同向驱动子浪与两个逆向修正子浪组成的非重叠五浪结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A standard impulse has five subwaves: three aligned motive waves and two countertrend corrections, with the prescribed non-overlap.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准推动浪（impulse）是 [[艾略特波浪]] 中的一类驱动结构：同一级别的 $1,3,5$ 浪沿整体方向推进，$2,4$ 浪逆向修正；第四浪不进入第一浪价格区间。它不是所有驱动浪的统称，对角线结构另有重叠约定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A standard impulse is one motive structure in the [[艾略特波浪|Elliott framework]]. Subwaves $1,3,5$ advance in the overall direction, while $2,4$ correct it, and wave 4 does not enter wave 1's price territory. It is not synonymous with every motive wave; diagonals have different overlap conventions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见简写为 $5-3-5-3-5$：它描述五个同级子浪内部进一步细分的模式，不能理解为顶层共有二十一根K线。这里“3”包含修正模式的变化形式，具体看 [[艾略特修正浪]]；第3浪自身必须是推动结构。只有六个转折端点呈锯齿状，还不足以确认内部细分和整套计数都有效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The common shorthand $5-3-5-3-5$ describes subdivisions inside the five same-degree waves, not twenty-one candlesticks at the top level. Here “3” includes corrective variations, described in [[艾略特修正浪|corrective waves]], and wave 3 must itself be an impulse. Six zigzag endpoints alone do not establish the required internal subdivisions or validate the full count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体排除错误计数的方法见 [[推动浪计数约束]]；对第二与第四浪外形的经验比较见 [[艾略特交替原则]]。一项是有效计数的条件，一项是学派指引，不能互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use [[推动浪计数约束|impulse-count constraints]] to reject invalid labels, and [[艾略特交替原则|alternation]] to compare typical shapes of waves 2 and 4. Validity conditions and guidelines have different roles.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
