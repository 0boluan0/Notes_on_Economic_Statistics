# Algebra of limits
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*极限定律及其适用条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If one quantity approaches $2$ and another approaches $3$ along the same inputs, their sum approaches $5$ and their product approaches $6$. **Limit laws** make this arithmetic valid. They let us combine already established [[函数极限|function limits]], provided the limiting values and the operation satisfy the stated conditions. They do not say that every expression can be split into limits before we know those limits exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿着同一批输入，一个量趋于2，另一个量趋于3，那么它们的和趋于5，积趋于6。**极限定律**使这种算术推理成立：把已经确定的[[函数极限]]组合起来，但必须满足极限值与运算的条件。它并不是说，在尚不知道各部分极限是否存在时，也能任意拆开表达式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Combine limits along the same approach
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*沿同一趋近方向组合极限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f,g$ be real-valued functions on the same domain $D$, and let $a$ be a cluster point of $D$: allowed inputs distinct from $a$ occur arbitrarily close to it. Suppose $f(x)\to L$ and $g(x)\to M$ as $x\to a$ through $D$, where $L,M$ are **finite real numbers**. For any fixed real number $c$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实值函数 $f,g$ 定义在同一集合 $D$ 上，$a$ 是 $D$ 的聚点，即不同于 $a$ 的合法输入能任意接近它。假设沿 $D$ 中输入趋于 $a$ 时，$f(x)\to L$、$g(x)\to M$，其中 $L,M$ 都是**有限实数**。对任意固定实数 $c$，

$$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\to a}(f(x)+g(x))&=L+M, &
\lim_{x\to a}(f(x)-g(x))&=L-M,\\
\lim_{x\to a}cf(x)&=cL, &
\lim_{x\to a}f(x)g(x)&=LM.
\end{aligned}
$$

> [!important] The quotient needs a nonzero limiting denominator · 商法则要求分母极限非零
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $M\ne0$, then $g(x)$ is nonzero for every sufficiently close allowed input other than $a$, and
> $$\lim_{x\to a}\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=\frac{L}{M}.$$
> The limit only needs the quotient to be defined near $a$. Zeros of $g$ far from $a$, or a missing value at $a$ itself, do not prevent this conclusion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $M\ne0$，则对所有充分接近 $a$、又不同于 $a$ 的合法输入，$g(x)$ 都非零，并有上式。极限只要求商在所需的附近输入处有定义；远处的零点，或 $a$ 处没有定义，本身不会妨碍结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, as $x\to2$, the numerator $x^2+1$ approaches $5$ and the denominator $x+1$ approaches $3$. Thus $(x^2+1)/(x+1)\to5/3$. Polynomial limits follow by repeated sums and products of the basic limit $x\to a$, and a rational function can be evaluated this way wherever its denominator's limiting value is nonzero. For one-sided limits, use the same laws after restricting $D$ to the chosen side; do not combine a left-hand limit of one function with a right-hand limit of another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $x\to2$ 时，分子 $x^2+1\to5$，分母 $x+1\to3$，所以 $(x^2+1)/(x+1)\to5/3$。多项式极限来自基本极限 $x\to a$ 的反复求和与相乘；有理函数在分母极限非零时也可这样代入。单侧极限先把 $D$ 限制到指定一侧，再用同样定律；不能把一个函数的左极限与另一个函数的右极限混合运算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why nearby errors remain controlled
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么组合后的误差仍可控制*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the sum, the triangle inequality gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对和，由三角不等式有

$$|(f(x)+g(x))-(L+M)|\le |f(x)-L|+|g(x)-M|.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given a requested error $\varepsilon>0$, make each error smaller than $\varepsilon/2$. Each function supplies an input tolerance; taking the smaller tolerance makes both estimates hold for the same inputs. This proves the sum rule. Subtraction and multiplication by a fixed scalar follow from the same error control.

For the product, add and subtract $f(x)M$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定输出误差要求 $\varepsilon>0$，让两个误差各小于 $\varepsilon/2$ 即可。两个函数各自给出一个输入容差，取其中较小者，就能对同一批输入同时保证两条估计。这证明和法则；差与固定常数倍也用同样的误差控制。

对积，加上再减去 $f(x)M$，得到

$$|f(x)g(x)-LM|\le |f(x)|\,|g(x)-M|+|M|\,|f(x)-L|.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $f(x)\to L$ is finite, nearby values satisfy $|f(x)|<|L|+1$ once $|f(x)-L|<1$. Now require, in the same neighbourhood, $|g(x)-M|<\varepsilon/[2(|L|+1)]$ and $|f(x)-L|<\varepsilon/[2(|M|+1)]$. The displayed error is then less than $\varepsilon$. The extra bound on $f$ is what prevents a small error from being multiplied by an uncontrolled quantity.

For the quotient, $M\ne0$ lets us impose $|g(x)-M|<|M|/2$. Then $|g(x)|>|M|/2$, so the denominator stays away from zero. In this neighbourhood,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $f(x)$ 趋于有限的 $L$，只要 $|f(x)-L|<1$，就有 $|f(x)|<|L|+1$。再在同一个邻域要求 $|g(x)-M|<\varepsilon/[2(|L|+1)]$、$|f(x)-L|<\varepsilon/[2(|M|+1)]$，上式误差便小于 $\varepsilon$。额外限制 $f$ 的大小，正是为了避免“小误差”乘上一个失控的量。

对商，$M\ne0$ 允许要求 $|g(x)-M|<|M|/2$，进而有 $|g(x)|>|M|/2$，把分母与零隔开。在这个邻域中，

$$
\left|\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}-\frac LM\right|
\le \frac{2}{|M|}|f(x)-L|+\frac{2|L|}{|M|^2}|g(x)-M|.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two coefficients are fixed finite numbers, and both errors tend to zero. This proves the quotient rule and explains its nonzero-denominator condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个系数都是固定有限数，两个误差都趋于零，于是商法则成立，也解释了为什么分母极限必须非零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Boundaries of these rules
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $g(x)\to0$, the quotient rule gives no conclusion. For $x\to0$, both $x/x\to1$ and $x^2/x\to0$, while $x/x^2=1/x$ has different infinite behaviours on the two sides. All have numerator and denominator tending to zero; [[零比零未定式|the form $0/0$]] does not decide among them.

The rules also cannot be reversed. On $D=\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$, the functions $f(x)=1/x$ and $g(x)=-1/x$ have no finite limits at zero, but their sum is identically zero and has limit zero. Computing the combined expression is valid; splitting it into two nonexistent finite limits is not. Expressions involving $\infty$ require separate results, because infinity is not a finite real number to which these algebraic statements apply.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $g(x)\to0$，商法则不作结论。例如 $x\to0$ 时，$x/x\to1$、$x^2/x\to0$，而 $x/x^2=1/x$ 在左右两侧有不同的无穷行为。这些分子分母都趋于零，[[零比零未定式|$0/0$ 形式]]却不能决定它们是哪一种结果。

这些定律也不能反向使用。在 $D=\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$ 上，$f(x)=1/x$、$g(x)=-1/x$ 在零处都没有有限极限，但它们的和恒为零，极限就是零。先化简整体是合法的；拆成两个不存在的有限极限再相加则不合法。涉及 $\infty$ 时需要另外的结论，因为无穷不是上述算术命题中的有限实数。
