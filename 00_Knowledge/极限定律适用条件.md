# 极限定律要求各部分极限存在且运算有定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Limit laws require the component limits to exist and the operation to be defined*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子命题
> 若 $\lim f=L$、$\lim g=M$，则在相应极限存在且运算有定义时，
> $$
> \lim(f+g)=L+M,\qquad
> \lim(fg)=LM,\qquad
> \lim\frac fg=\frac LM\quad(M\ne0).
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $\lim f=L$ and $\lim g=M$, then, provided the component limits exist and the displayed operation is defined,
> $$
> \lim(f+g)=L+M,\qquad
> \lim(fg)=LM,\qquad
> \lim\frac fg=\frac LM\quad(M\ne0).
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极限定律只有在相关极限存在时才能机械使用。分母极限为零时，商法则不能直接使用；表达式仍可能有极限，但必须换一种论证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Limit laws become mechanical only after the relevant limits are known to exist. If the denominator tends to zero, the quotient law does not apply directly; the expression may still have a limit, but another argument is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 已知 $g(x)\to0$，能否把 $\lim f(x)/g(x)$ 直接写成 $L/0$？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $g(x)\to0$, may $\lim f(x)/g(x)$ be written directly as $L/0$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。商法则要求分母极限非零；必须另查左右行为、未定式或适用的定理。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. The quotient law requires a nonzero denominator limit; inspect the one-sided behaviour, indeterminate form, or another applicable theorem instead.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
