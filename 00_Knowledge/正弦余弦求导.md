# Derivatives of sine and cosine
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正弦余弦求导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sine and cosine describe the vertical and horizontal coordinates of a point moving around a unit circle. They also describe oscillations. Differentiating them tells us how fast those coordinates or oscillating quantities change. When the input angle is measured in radians, sine changes at the rate given by cosine, and cosine changes at the rate given by negative sine.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正弦与余弦描述单位圆上运动点的纵坐标和横坐标，也能描述振动。对它们求导，就是问这些坐标或振动量改变得有多快。当输入角以弧度计量时，正弦的变化率是余弦，余弦的变化率是负正弦。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[导数|derivative]] is a limit of “output change divided by input change”. Here the input $x$ is an angle: $x=\pi/2$ means a quarter-turn, and $x=2\pi$ means a full turn. The [[正弦基本极限|fundamental sine limit]] explains why radians give the scale factor $1$ needed below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[导数]]是“输出变化量除以输入变化量”的极限。这里输入 $x$ 是角度：$x=\pi/2$ 是四分之一圈，$x=2\pi$ 是一整圈。[[正弦基本极限]]解释了为什么弧度制恰好给出下面所需的比例因子 $1$。

> [!abstract] The two derivative rules · 两条求导公式
> $$
> \frac{d}{dx}\sin x=\cos x,
> \qquad
> \frac{d}{dx}\cos x=-\sin x,
> \qquad x\in\mathbb R.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $x=0$, the sine curve passes through $0$ with slope $1$. The cosine curve passes through $1$ with slope $0$, consistent with its local maximum there. A slope of zero concerns instantaneous change, not the value of the function.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $x=0$，正弦曲线经过 $0$，斜率为 $1$；余弦曲线经过 $1$，斜率为 $0$，与它在这里取得局部最大值相符。斜率为零说的是瞬时变化率，而不是函数值为零。

## Move a small extra angle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*让角度再增加一点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix an angle $x$ and add a small nonzero angle $h$. The angle-addition identity separates the known angle $x$ from the small change $h$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定角度 $x$，再增加一个很小的非零角 $h$。和角公式把已有角度 $x$ 与小变化 $h$ 分开：

$$
\sin(x+h)=\sin x\cos h+\cos x\sin h.
$$
$$
\frac{\sin(x+h)-\sin x}{h}
=\sin x\frac{\cos h-1}{h}+\cos x\frac{\sin h}{h}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As $h\to0$, the two small-angle quotients tend to $0$ and $1$ respectively. The coefficients $\sin x$ and $\cos x$ stay fixed, so the limit is $\sin x\cdot0+\cos x\cdot1=\cos x$. For cosine, its angle-addition identity contains a minus sign:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $h\to0$，两个小角差商分别趋于 $0$ 和 $1$。系数 $\sin x$、$\cos x$ 保持不变，因此极限为 $\sin x\cdot0+\cos x\cdot1=\cos x$。对余弦而言，和角公式中带有一个负号：

$$
\cos(x+h)=\cos x\cos h-\sin x\sin h,
$$
$$
\frac{\cos(x+h)-\cos x}{h}
=\cos x\frac{\cos h-1}{h}-\sin x\frac{\sin h}{h}
\longrightarrow-\sin x.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This also explains the negative sign in the cosine derivative. Both arguments use established geometric limits rather than assuming the derivatives in advance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也解释了余弦导数中的负号。两段推导都使用先前已经建立的几何极限，没有提前假定待证明的求导公式。

> [!example] A faster oscillation · 更快的振动
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $s(t)=3\sin(2t)$, with $t$ measured in seconds and $s$ in metres. The phase $2t$ changes at $2$ radians per second. The [[链式法则|chain rule]] multiplies the derivative of the outer sine by the derivative of this inner phase:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $s(t)=3\sin(2t)$，$t$ 以秒计，$s$ 以米计。相位 $2t$ 以每秒 $2$ 弧度变化。[[链式法则]]要求把外层正弦的导数，乘以内层相位的导数：
>
> $$
> s'(t)=3\cos(2t)\cdot2=6\cos(2t),\qquad s'(0)=6.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The initial velocity is therefore $6$ metres per second. More generally, for a differentiable inner function $u$, the derivatives are $(\sin u)'=\cos(u)u'$ and $(\cos u)'=-\sin(u)u'$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 初始速度因此是每秒 $6$ 米。一般地，对可导的内层函数 $u$，有 $(\sin u)'=\cos(u)u'$ 和 $(\cos u)'=-\sin(u)u'$。

## If the input is a number of degrees
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*输入若是度数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define $S(\theta)=\sin(\pi\theta/180)$ and $C(\theta)=\cos(\pi\theta/180)$. These functions take a numerical degree value as their input. The chain rule then gives $S'(\theta)=(\pi/180)\cos(\pi\theta/180)$ and $C'(\theta)=-(\pi/180)\sin(\pi\theta/180)$. The extra factor converts a rate per radian into a rate per degree; omitting it changes the numerical derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义 $S(\theta)=\sin(\pi\theta/180)$、$C(\theta)=\cos(\pi\theta/180)$，这两个函数以数值度数作为输入。链式法则给出 $S'(\theta)=(\pi/180)\cos(\pi\theta/180)$、$C'(\theta)=-(\pi/180)\sin(\pi\theta/180)$。额外因子把“每弧度的变化率”换成“每度的变化率”；漏掉它会改变导数的数值。
