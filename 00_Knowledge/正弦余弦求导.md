# 弧度制下正弦的导数是余弦而余弦的导数是负正弦
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In radians, the derivative of sine is cosine and the derivative of cosine is negative sine*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 当自变量以弧度计量时，
> $$
> \frac{d}{dx}\sin x=\cos x,
> \qquad
> \frac{d}{dx}\cos x=-\sin x.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With the input measured in radians, $d(\sin x)/dx=\cos x$ and $d(\cos x)/dx=-\sin x$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以正弦为例，把和角公式放入差商：
$$
\frac{\sin(x+h)-\sin x}{h}
=\sin x\frac{\cos h-1}{h}+\cos x\frac{\sin h}{h}.
$$
由 [[正弦基本极限]]，两项分别趋于 $0$ 与 $\cos x$。余弦同理；负号来自 $\cos(x+h)=\cos x\cos h-\sin x\sin h$ 中的第二项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For sine, insert the angle-addition formula into the difference quotient. The two small-angle limits send the first term to zero and the second to $\cos x$. The cosine calculation is analogous; its minus sign comes from the $-\sin x\sin h$ term in the addition formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图像提供快速验算：$sin x$ 在 $0$ 处上升且斜率为 $1$；$cos x$ 在 $0$ 处达到局部最大且斜率为 $0$。若角度用度数，必须再乘 $\pi/180$，不能把弧度公式原样搬过去。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The graphs provide a quick check: sine rises through zero with slope 1, while cosine has a local maximum and slope 0 at zero. Degree-valued inputs require the extra factor $\pi/180$; the radian formulas cannot be copied unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么 $d(\cos x)/dx$ 的负号不是记忆上的偶然？
>
> **答案：** 余弦和角公式本身含 $-\sin x\sin h$；同时图像在第一象限向右下降，也要求导数为负。
