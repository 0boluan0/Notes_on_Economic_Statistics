# The trapezoidal error bound
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*梯形积分误差界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[梯形法|trapezoidal rule]] replaces a curve by short chords. Knowing the resulting number does not tell us how far it lies from the true [[定积分|integral]]. An error bound answers that second question: a limit on how quickly the slope changes controls how far the curve can bend away from its chords, and hence how much area the replacement can gain or lose.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[梯形法]]用许多短弦替代曲线。算出一个数，还不知道它离真实[[定积分|积分]]有多远。误差界回答第二个问题：如果能限制斜率变化得多快，就能控制曲线偏离弦的程度，进而控制替换后多算或少算的累积量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $I=\int_a^bf(x)\,dx$ for the true integral and $T_N$ for the value from $N$ equal strips. The signed error is $I-T_N$; the absolute error $|I-T_N|$ measures its size. The [[高阶导数|second derivative]] $f''=(f')'$ measures change of slope. We will use a number $K_2\ge0$ bounding $|f''|$ over the entire interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把真积分记为 $I=\int_a^bf(x)\,dx$，把 $N$ 个等宽小区间得到的梯形值记为 $T_N$。带符号误差是 $I-T_N$，绝对误差 $|I-T_N|$ 只看大小。[[高阶导数|二阶导数]] $f''=(f')'$ 描述斜率的变化；下面用非负数 $K_2$ 控制整个区间内的 $|f''|$。

> [!important] A guaranteed absolute error · 可保证的绝对误差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $a<b$ be finite, and suppose the real-valued function $f\in C^2([a,b])$. Here $f$ is continuous on the interval, twice differentiable inside, and its first two derivatives extend continuously to the endpoints. If $|f''(x)|\le K_2$ throughout $[a,b]$, then for every positive integer $N$, with $h=(b-a)/N$,
> $$\boxed{|I-T_N|\le\frac{K_2(b-a)h^2}{12}
> =\frac{K_2(b-a)^3}{12N^2}.}$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $a<b$ 是有限实数，实值函数 $f\in C^2([a,b])$，即 $f$ 在闭区间连续、内部二次可导，前两阶导数能连续延伸到端点。若全区间上都有 $|f''(x)|\le K_2$，那么对每个正整数 $N$，取 $h=(b-a)/N$，就有上面的绝对误差界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are convenient sufficient conditions. The number $K_2$ need not be the smallest possible bound, but it must be justified everywhere, not merely at the sampled endpoints. For example, $f(x)=1/x$ on $[1,2]$ has $f''(x)=2/x^3$, so $K_2=2$ is valid because $x\ge1$ throughout the interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些是方便检查的充分条件。$K_2$ 不必是最小上界，但必须确实覆盖每一点，不能只查看若干采样端点。例如 $[1,2]$ 上的 $f(x)=1/x$ 满足 $f''(x)=2/x^3$；由于全区间上 $x\ge1$，所以 $K_2=2$ 是有效上界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From one strip to the whole interval
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从单段误差到整体误差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On one strip $[u,v]$ of width $h=v-u$, introduce the weight
$$g(x)=(x-u)(v-x).$$
This weight is nonnegative, vanishes at both ends, and has $g'(u)=h$, $g'(v)=-h$, $g''(x)=-2$. Two uses of [[分部积分|integration by parts]] give
$$\begin{aligned}
\int_u^v g(x)f''(x)\,dx
&=[gf']_u^v-\int_u^v g'(x)f'(x)\,dx\\
&=-[g'f]_u^v+\int_u^v g''(x)f(x)\,dx\\
&=h[f(u)+f(v)]-2\int_u^v f(x)\,dx.
\end{aligned}$$
The bracket means the value at $v$ minus the value at $u$. Rearranging isolates the exact local error $E_i$ of this strip:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在宽度 $h=v-u$ 的一段 $[u,v]$ 上，引入权函数 $g(x)=(x-u)(v-x)$。它非负，两端为零，而且 $g'(u)=h$、$g'(v)=-h$、$g''=-2$。连续应用两次[[分部积分]]，得到上面的等式；方括号表示 $v$ 处的值减去 $u$ 处的值。移项后，就分离出这一段的精确误差 $E_i$：

$$E_i=\int_u^v f(x)\,dx-\frac h2[f(u)+f(v)]
=-\frac12\int_u^v(x-u)(v-x)f''(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[积分绝对值界|integral triangle inequality]] bounds the magnitude of an integral by the integral of the magnitude. Using $|f''|\le K_2$ and substituting $t=x-u$ gives
$$|E_i|\le\frac{K_2}{2}\int_u^v(x-u)(v-x)\,dx
=\frac{K_2}{2}\int_0^h t(h-t)\,dt
=\frac{K_2h^3}{12}.$$
There are $N$ strips. Their signed errors add to $I-T_N$, so the ordinary triangle inequality yields
$$|I-T_N|\le\sum_{i=1}^N|E_i|\le\frac{NK_2h^3}{12}
=\frac{K_2(b-a)h^2}{12}.$$
The local bound involves $h^3$; summing over all $N=(b-a)/h$ strips gives the global $h^2$ dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[积分绝对值界]]允许用“绝对值的积分”控制“积分的绝对值”。利用 $|f''|\le K_2$，再令 $t=x-u$，就得到每段 $K_2h^3/12$ 的界。共有 $N$ 段，带符号误差相加等于 $I-T_N$；再用普通三角不等式，把各段绝对误差相加，便得到全区间的界。单段是 $h^3$ 量级，但段数为 $(b-a)/h$，所以总界是 $h^2$ 量级。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The sign tells us which side the estimate lies on
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*误差符号告诉我们高估还是低估*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the exact identity, the weight $(x-u)(v-x)$ is nonnegative. If $f''\ge0$ throughout, every $E_i\le0$, so $T_N\ge I$. If $f''\le0$, the inequality reverses. The [[凹凸性|geometric explanation]] is that a convex graph lies below each chord, while a concave graph lies above it. These comparisons concern signed integrals and do not require $f$ itself to be positive. Affine functions have zero error; if the curvature changes sign, local errors can cancel and there is no universal direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

精确恒等式中的权函数 $(x-u)(v-x)$ 非负。若全区间 $f''\ge0$，每段 $E_i\le0$，所以 $T_N\ge I$；若 $f''\le0$，方向相反。[[凹凸性|几何解释]]是：凸图像位于每条弦下方，凹图像位于每条弦上方。这比较的是带符号积分，不要求 $f$ 本身为正。仿射函数的误差为零；若弯曲方向改变，各段误差可能抵消，不能统一断言高估或低估。

> [!example] The bound can be attained · 上界可以恰好达到
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$ with $N=2$, take $K_2=2$. Then
> $$T_2=\frac38,\qquad I=\frac13,\qquad I-T_2=-\frac1{24},
> \qquad\frac{K_2(b-a)^3}{12N^2}=\frac1{24}.$$
> The estimate is too large and its absolute error attains the bound. Thus the constant $1/12$ cannot be reduced in a bound intended to cover every function under these same assumptions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[0,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x^2$，取 $N=2$、$K_2=2$。梯形值为 $3/8$，真积分为 $1/3$，所以 $I-T_2=-1/24$；上界也正好是 $1/24$。这里确实高估，而且绝对误差达到界。因此，若要对同样条件下的所有函数都保证成立，就不能把常数 $1/12$ 再缩小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choose a grid from a desired tolerance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从目标精度反推网格*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To guarantee absolute error at most $\varepsilon>0$, choose a positive integer satisfying
$$N\ge\sqrt{\frac{K_2(b-a)^3}{12\varepsilon}}.$$
Round upward, and keep $N\ge1$ even if $K_2=0$. For $x^2$ on $[0,1]$ and $\varepsilon=0.001$, this requires $N\ge\sqrt{1/(6\cdot0.001)}\approx12.91$, so $N=13$ suffices. It uses $14$ endpoint locations. The theorem controls integration error for exact function values; measurement and rounding errors require their own accounting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要保证绝对误差不超过 $\varepsilon>0$，选满足上式的正整数 $N$，并向上取整；即使 $K_2=0$，也保留 $N\ge1$。对 $[0,1]$ 上的 $x^2$，若 $\varepsilon=0.001$，就需要 $N\ge12.91\ldots$，因此取 $N=13$ 即可，对应 $14$ 个端点位置。定理控制的是使用精确函数值时的积分误差，测量误差和舍入误差需要另外核算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unequal widths or local derivative bounds can be handled by the same proof. If strip $i$ has width $h_i$ and a valid bound $K_{2,i}$ there, then
$$|I-T|\le\frac1{12}\sum_i K_{2,i}h_i^3.$$
This explains why narrower strips can be useful where the second derivative is large. For reversed integration limits, first work on the increasing interval and negate both the integral and approximation; the absolute bound is unchanged, while the signed comparison reverses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个证明也能处理不同段宽或局部导数界：若第 $i$ 段宽为 $h_i$，该段上的有效界为 $K_{2,i}$，则绝对误差不超过 $\sum_iK_{2,i}h_i^3/12$。这说明二阶导数较大的地方为何适合分得更细。反向积分时，先在递增区间应用结论，再把积分与近似同时取负；绝对误差界不变，带符号的大小关系反向。

> [!warning] A bound is not an exact error law · 上界不是实际误差的等式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With the same interval and $K_2$, doubling $N$ divides this guaranteed bound by four; it does not force the actual error to fall by exactly four. Likewise, comparison with the [[中点积分误差界|midpoint bound]] compares guarantees, not every pair of actual errors. For $f(x)=|x|$ on $[-1,1]$, continuity still gives trapezoidal convergence, but $f''(0)$ does not exist, so this $C^2$ theorem cannot be applied directly. Splitting at the corner produces two affine pieces that can instead be integrated exactly.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在同一区间、同一个 $K_2$ 下，把 $N$ 加倍，保证的上界缩为四分之一，并不强迫实际误差恰好缩小四倍。同样，与[[中点积分误差界]]比较的是保证值，不是每个函数的实际误差比。$[-1,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=|x|$ 因连续而仍有梯形收敛，但 $f''(0)$ 不存在，不能直接套用这个 $C^2$ 定理；若在尖角处分开，两侧都是仿射函数，反而可以分别精确积分。
