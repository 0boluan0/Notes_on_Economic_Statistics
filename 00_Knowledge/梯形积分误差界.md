# 二阶导数绝对值有界时复合梯形法的绝对误差不超过区间长度乘步长平方乘该界的十二分之一
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With a bound on the second derivative, composite trapezoidal error is at most one twelfth of that bound times interval length times squared step size*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<b$、$f\in C^2([a,b])$，并在整个区间上有 $|f''(x)|\le K_2$。令 $I=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$，用 $N\ge1$ 个等宽小区间、$h=(b-a)/N$ 计算 [[梯形法]] 的 $T_N$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a<b$, $f\in C^2([a,b])$, and $|f''(x)|\le K_2$ throughout the interval. Set $I=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$, and compute the [[梯形法|composite trapezoidal value]] $T_N$ using $N\ge1$ equal subintervals with $h=(b-a)/N$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\boxed{|I-T_N|\le\frac{K_2(b-a)h^2}{12}
=\frac{K_2(b-a)^3}{12N^2}.}
$$

这里 $C^2$ 表示直到二阶的导数连续，是方便检查的充分条件。$K_2$ 可以是已证明有效的上界，不必恰好等于最大值；只比较少数节点的 $|f''|$ 不能证明它控制整个区间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $C^2$ means continuous derivatives through order two, a convenient sufficient assumption. $K_2$ may be any proved uniform upper bound, not necessarily the exact maximum. Checking $|f''|$ at a few sample nodes alone does not establish a bound over the interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从单段误差到整体误差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From one subinterval to the total error*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在一段 $[u,v]$ 上，$v-u=h$。对 $(x-u)(v-x)f''(x)$ 连续做两次分部积分，得到精确恒等式；再用 [[积分绝对值界]]，有
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On one subinterval $[u,v]$ of width $h$, two integrations by parts applied to $(x-u)(v-x)f''(x)$ give an exact identity. The [[积分绝对值界|absolute integral bound]] then gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\int_u^v f(x)\,dx-\frac h2[f(u)+f(v)]
=-\frac12\int_u^v(x-u)(v-x)f''(x)\,dx,
$$
$$
|E_i|\le\frac{K_2}{2}\int_u^v(x-u)(v-x)\,dx
=\frac{K_2h^3}{12}.
$$

把 $N$ 段相加并取绝对值，得到 $|I-T_N|\le\sum_i|E_i|\le NK_2h^3/12$，即所述公式。核函数 $(x-u)(v-x)$ 非负，所以 $f''\ge0$ 时 $I-T_N\le0$、梯形法高估；$f''\le0$ 时低估。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Summing the $N$ local errors and applying the triangle inequality gives $|I-T_N|\le\sum_i|E_i|\le NK_2h^3/12$, yielding the stated bound. The kernel $(x-u)(v-x)$ is nonnegative, so $f''\ge0$ implies $I-T_N\le0$ and overestimation; $f''\le0$ implies underestimation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $f(x)=x^2$、$[0,1]$、$N=2$，$K_2=2$，界为 $1/24$。直接算得 $T_2=3/8$、$I=1/3$，绝对误差恰为 $1/24$，核对了常数与误差方向。若要求误差不超过 $\varepsilon>0$，可取任意正整数 $N\ge\sqrt{K_2(b-a)^3/(12\varepsilon)}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$ with $N=2$, take $K_2=2$, giving a bound of $1/24$. Direct calculation gives $T_2=3/8$ and $I=1/3$, so the absolute error is exactly $1/24$, checking both the constant and sign. For tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, choose any positive integer $N\ge\sqrt{K_2(b-a)^3/(12\varepsilon)}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 在同一个区间、同一个 $K_2$ 下，把 $N$ 加倍能断言什么？
>
> **答案：** 该误差上界变为原来的 $1/4$；实际误差可能更小，甚至为零，不能仅由这个界断言实际误差恰好缩小四倍。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> What follows when $N$ doubles with the same interval and $K_2$? **Answer:** This error bound becomes one quarter as large. Actual error may be smaller or zero; the bound alone does not establish an exact fourfold reduction in actual error.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
