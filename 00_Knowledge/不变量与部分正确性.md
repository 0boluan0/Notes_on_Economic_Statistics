# Partial correctness from invariants
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*不变量与部分正确性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fact that stays true during a computation is useful only if it helps answer the final question. **Partial correctness** says: if the computation finishes from an allowed input, its result satisfies the specification. “Partial” leaves termination unproved; it does not mean that the returned answer may be partly wrong. An invariant supplies information at the end, and the exit condition tells us how to turn that information into the required result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
计算过程中一直为真的事实，需要能帮助回答最终问题才有用。**部分正确性**说的是：从允许输入出发，若计算结束，结果就满足规格。“部分”表示尚未证明会结束，不是说返回答案可以部分错误。不变量提供结束时仍成立的信息，退出条件则说明怎样把这些信息转化为要求的结果。

> [!example] Moving a counter · 转移计数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Start at $(x,y)=(3,0)$. While $x>0$, make the simultaneous update $(x,y)\to(x-1,y+1)$. The states are $(3,0),(2,1),(1,2),(0,3)$. Their sum stays $x+y=3$. At exit, $x=0$, so the preserved sum gives $y=3$, the desired output. “Both registers are integers” is also preserved, but that fact alone would not determine the output.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $(x,y)=(3,0)$ 开始，只要 $x>0$，就同时更新为 $(x-1,y+1)$。状态依次为 $(3,0),(2,1),(1,2),(0,3)$，始终满足 $x+y=3$。退出时 $x=0$，与不变的和合起来得到目标输出 $y=3$。“两个寄存器都是整数”也被保持，但仅凭这件事不能确定输出。

## Three proof obligations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*三个证明义务*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a [[状态机与可达性|state machine]] whose state records the variables and, where necessary, the current instruction. The **precondition** describes allowed inputs and starting states. The **postcondition** $R(q)$ describes the required result at a final state $q$. An invariant $P(q)$ and exit condition $E(q)$ are also [[命题与谓词|predicates]]: statements that become true or false once the state is specified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
使用[[状态机与可达性|状态机]]表示过程，状态记录变量，并在必要时记录当前指令。**前置条件**描述允许的输入和起始状态；**后置条件** $R(q)$ 描述最终状态 $q$ 必须满足的结果。不变量 $P(q)$ 与退出条件 $E(q)$ 也是[[命题与谓词|谓词]]：给定具体状态后，就成为具有真值的陈述。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each allowed start $s_0$, prove:

- **Initialization:** $P(s_0)$ holds.
- **Preservation:** every allowed transition $q\to r$ satisfies $P(q)\Rightarrow P(r)$.
- **Exit reasoning:** every final state under consideration satisfies $E$, and $P(q)\land E(q)\Rightarrow R(q)$.

The symbol $\land$ means “and”; $\Rightarrow$ means “implies.” The first two obligations are the [[不变量原理|invariant principle]]. They show that $P$ holds at every reachable state, including every reachable final state. Such a state also satisfies $E$, so the third obligation yields $R$. Since the final state was arbitrary, this proves partial correctness for every terminating execution from an allowed input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对每个允许起点 $s_0$，证明：

- **初始化：** $P(s_0)$ 成立。
- **保持性：** 每条允许转移 $q\to r$ 都满足 $P(q)\Rightarrow P(r)$。
- **退出推理：** 所讨论的每个最终状态都满足 $E$，并且 $P(q)\land E(q)\Rightarrow R(q)$。

符号 $\land$ 表示“并且”，$\Rightarrow$ 表示“推出”。前两项就是[[不变量原理]]：它们保证 $P$ 对每个可达状态成立，包括每个可达最终状态。这样的状态还满足 $E$，所以第三项给出 $R$。由于最终状态是任取的，这便证明了从允许输入出发的每条终止执行都满足规格。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The input condition matters: a proof for nonnegative integer inputs does not automatically cover negative inputs or finite-machine overflow. A final state must really be an exit of the model. If a program has several return sites, each needs its corresponding exit argument; checking only the usual loop exit does not justify another return or an unmodelled error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
输入条件不能省略：对非负整数输入的证明，不会自动覆盖负输入或有限机器上的溢出。最终状态也必须真的是模型中的出口；若程序有多个返回位置，每个都需要相应的退出论证，只检查常见的循环出口不能证明其他返回或未建模错误的正确性。

## The invariant at a loop checkpoint
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*循环检查点上的不变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a loop “while $B$ do the body,” take states at the loop head, immediately before testing the Boolean condition $B$. Preservation then means that whenever $P$ and $B$ hold, every completed body execution returns to the loop head with $P$ still true. Normal exit occurs with $B$ false, so its exit condition is $\neg B$. The invariant need not hold between every two individual assignments inside the body; it must hold at the stated checkpoints. If individual instructions are the transitions instead, the invariant must cover those finer states too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对“当 $B$ 成立时执行循环体”的循环，可以把状态取在循环头，即测试真假条件 $B$ 之前。此时保持性要求：当 $P$ 与 $B$ 成立时，每次完成的循环体执行都返回满足 $P$ 的循环头。正常退出时 $B$ 为假，因此退出条件为 $\neg B$。不变量不一定在循环体内部任意两条赋值之间都成立，而须在约定的检查点成立；若把每条指令分别建模为转移，就必须覆盖那些更细的状态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the counter example, let the input be any $N\in\mathbb N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ and use nonnegative-integer states. Initially $(x,y)=(N,0)$, so $x+y=N$. A step with $x>0$ gives $(x-1)+(y+1)=x+y=N$ and stays in the state space. At a normal exit, $x>0$ is false; because $x\ge0$, this means $x=0$. Substituting into $x+y=N$ gives $y=N$. For $N=0$, the initial state is already an exit and the same argument applies without an iteration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
在计数例中，允许输入为任意 $N\in\mathbb N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$，状态中的两个数都为非负整数。初态 $(x,y)=(N,0)$ 满足 $x+y=N$；在 $x>0$ 时，一步更新给出 $(x-1)+(y+1)=x+y=N$，且仍处于状态空间。正常退出时 $x>0$ 为假，又因 $x\ge0$，所以 $x=0$；代入 $x+y=N$ 就有 $y=N$。若 $N=0$，初态已是出口，无须迭代，同一论证仍成立。

## Fast exponentiation: preserve a product, then use zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*快速幂：保持乘积，再使用零指数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the input be a positive real number $a$ and a nonnegative integer $b$; the goal is to return $a^b$. At a loop checkpoint, $x$ is the current base, $y$ the accumulated factor, and $z$ the remaining exponent. Start with $(x,y,z)=(a,1,b)$. If $z=0$, return $y$. Otherwise one complete iteration makes the simultaneous transition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
输入为正实数 $a$ 和非负整数 $b$，目标是返回 $a^b$。在循环检查点，$x$ 表示当前底数，$y$ 表示已累积因子，$z$ 表示剩余指数。初始为 $(x,y,z)=(a,1,b)$；若 $z=0$，就返回 $y$，否则一次完整迭代作如下同时转移：

$$
(x,y,z)\longrightarrow
\begin{cases}
(x^2,y,z/2),&z>0\text{ is even},\\
(x^2,xy,(z-1)/2),&z>0\text{ is odd}.
\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All expressions on the right use the old register values. The invariant is $z\in\mathbb N$ and $yx^z=a^b$. It holds initially because $1\cdot a^b=a^b$. In the even case, the next product is $y(x^2)^{z/2}=yx^z$. In the odd case, it is $(xy)(x^2)^{(z-1)/2}=yx^{1+z-1}=yx^z$. Both cases keep the exponent a nonnegative integer, so the invariant is preserved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
右侧所有表达式都使用更新前的寄存器值。不变量是 $z\in\mathbb N$ 且 $yx^z=a^b$。初态成立，因为 $1\cdot a^b=a^b$。偶数分支更新后的乘积为 $y(x^2)^{z/2}=yx^z$；奇数分支则为 $(xy)(x^2)^{(z-1)/2}=yx^{1+z-1}=yx^z$。两分支都保持指数为非负整数，因此不变量得到保持。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a=2,b=5$, the checkpoint states are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如 $a=2,b=5$ 时，检查点状态依次为

$$
(2,1,5)\to(4,2,2)\to(16,2,1)\to(256,32,0).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At every checkpoint, $yx^z=32$. At exit, $z=0$, so $yx^0=y=a^b$. The invariant preserves the relation between the input and current registers, while the exit condition specializes that relation to the final answer. This verifies the output **if** an exit is reached. The [[秩函数与终止证明|strictly decreasing exponent rank]] is the separate reason that the exit must eventually be reached.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
每个检查点都满足 $yx^z=32$。程序退出时 $z=0$，所以 $yx^0=y=a^b$。不变量负责保留输入与当前寄存器之间的关系，退出条件负责把这条关系收束成最终答案。这里验证的是**若**到达出口，输出便正确；出口必定会到达，则由单独的[[秩函数与终止证明|指数秩严格下降]]论证负责。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An invariant need not equal the postcondition, but it must be strong enough to imply the postcondition under the exit condition. For example, “$x,y$ are integers” together with $x=0$ also permits $(x,y)=(0,7)$; with target $y=3$, that proposed invariant cannot prove the target. A true but weak invariant does not certify the required answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
不变量不必与后置条件相同，但必须强到足以在退出条件下推出后置条件。例如，“$x,y$ 都为整数”与 $x=0$ 合起来也允许 $(x,y)=(0,7)$；若目标是 $y=3$，这个不变量就无法推出目标。一个真实但过弱的不变量，不能证明要求的答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Partial correctness can hold even when no output is ever produced. Starting at $x=1$, a loop that repeatedly performs $x:=x$ while $x>0$ never reaches its exit. Therefore the conditional claim about its final outputs imposes no result on this execution. This is why [[全正确性构成|total correctness]] needs both partial correctness and a separate termination proof. Neither one supplies the other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
即使始终没有输出，部分正确性也可能成立。例如从 $x=1$ 开始，当 $x>0$ 时不断执行 $x:=x$，永远到不了出口；于是关于最终输出的条件断言，不会对这条执行给出任何结果保证。这正是[[全正确性构成|全正确性]]还需要另证终止的原因，两个义务互不替代。

> [!question]- Use the exit condition · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A loop preserves $x+y=N$, exits when $x=0$, and requires $y=N$ upon exit. If the invariant is initialized and preserved, does this establish partial correctness? **Yes:** every reachable terminal state satisfies both equations, hence $y=N$. This still does not prove that the loop terminates.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 某循环保持 $x+y=N$，退出条件是 $x=0$，规格要求退出时 $y=N$。若不变量已经初始化且每步保持，能证明部分正确性吗？**可以：**每个可达终止状态同时满足两个等式，因此 $y=N$；但仍未证明循环必定退出。
