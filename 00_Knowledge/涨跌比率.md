# 涨跌比率是给定期间上涨家数合计与下跌家数合计之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The advance-decline ratio divides total advancing issue counts by total declining issue counts over a specified period.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

涨跌比率（advance-decline ratio，课程缩写 ADR）比较给定期间内上涨股票家数合计与下跌家数合计，描述市场参与面的相对数量。这里 ADR 指市场宽度比率，不指同缩写的存托凭证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The advance-decline ratio, abbreviated ADR in the course, compares total advancing and declining issue counts over a specified period. It describes relative market participation. Here ADR denotes a breadth ratio, not the depositary receipt sharing that abbreviation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡采用包含当前期的 $N$ 期滚动窗口。先固定股票池、交易期与相邻可比收盘价口径，$A_t$ 为上涨家数，$D_t$ 为下跌家数，持平不计入两者；拆股调整、覆盖变化和缺失报价须按一致规则处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card uses an $N$-period rolling window including the current period. Specify the universe, trading periods, and comparable close-to-close basis. Let $A_t$ and $D_t$ count advances and declines, excluding unchanged issues from both. Treat split adjustments, coverage changes, and missing quotes consistently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P_{1,t}=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}A_{t-j},\qquad
P_{2,t}=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}D_{t-j},\qquad
ADR_{N,t}=\frac{P_{1,t}}{P_{2,t}},\quad P_{2,t}>0.
$$

计算时先分别汇总，再相除；$N=1$ 时就是当天上涨家数除以下跌家数。它不是每日比率的算术平均，也不是上涨家数占上涨与下跌合计的比例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sum each count separately before dividing. With $N=1$, the formula is the day's advances divided by declines. It is neither the arithmetic average of daily ratios nor advancing issues as a share of advances plus declines.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如三期上涨家数 $6,2,7$、下跌家数 $3,6,2$，$ADR_3=15/11\approx1.3636$。若先算每日比率再平均，则为 $(2+1/3+3.5)/3\approx1.9444$，结果不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For advancing counts $6,2,7$ and declining counts $3,6,2$, $ADR_3=15/11\approx1.3636$. Averaging daily ratios instead gives $(2+1/3+3.5)/3\approx1.9444$, a different result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分母为正时比率非负；若上涨合计为零，比率可以是 $0$。分母为零时，普通实数比值未定义，本卡记 NA 并说明“窗口无下跌家数”；两种家数合计都为零同样记 NA。若软件显示正无穷，必须识别这是其显示约定。首个完整窗口之前不输出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a positive denominator the ratio is nonnegative, and zero total advances give a ratio of $0$. A zero denominator leaves the real-valued ratio undefined; this card records NA and notes that the window contains no declining issues. Both totals being zero also yields NA. A platform's positive-infinity display is a convention that must be recognized. No output appears before a complete window exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比率大于 $1$ 只说明窗口内上涨家数合计更多，没有包含各家涨跌幅度或指数权重。原课 $0.5/1.5/0.75$ 等阈值属于待指定与检验的应用规则，不改变本卡的计数比例定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A ratio above $1$ states only that advancing counts exceed declining counts over the window; it includes neither return magnitudes nor index weights. The course's thresholds such as $0.5/1.5/0.75$ belong to application rules requiring specification and evaluation, rather than to the ratio's definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
