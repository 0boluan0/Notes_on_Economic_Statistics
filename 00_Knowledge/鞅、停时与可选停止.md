# 鞅、停时与可选停止
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Martingales, stopping times, and optional stopping*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 用条件期望表达“在当前信息下没有可预测增益”，并判断自适应停止是否保持公平性。
> **具体锚点：** 公平赌博的累计财富可能是鞅；等待第一次赢钱是停时，但无限翻倍下注不一定满足可选停止的条件。
> **核心难点：** 鞅相对于过滤定义；恒定无条件均值远弱于条件公平，可选停止也必须核验有界性或一致可积等条件。
> **为什么重要：** 首达概率、序贯检验、金融定价、浓缩不等式和随机积分都依赖鞅方法。
> **继续：** 先复习 [[条件期望与独立性]]，再按“适应—可积—条件期望”三步验证；具体连续过程见 [[布朗运动]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It expresses “no predictable gain given current information” through conditional expectation and determines when adaptive stopping preserves fairness.
> **Concrete anchor:** The cumulative wealth in a fair gamble may be a martingale. Waiting until the first win is a stopping time, but an unlimited doubling strategy need not satisfy optional-stopping conditions.
> **Central difficulty:** A martingale is defined relative to a filtration. Constant unconditional mean is much weaker than conditional fairness, and optional stopping requires conditions such as boundedness or uniform integrability.
> **Why it matters:** Hitting probabilities, sequential tests, financial pricing, concentration inequalities, and stochastic integration all use martingale methods.
> **Continue with:** Review [[条件期望与独立性|conditional expectation and independence]], then verify a candidate in the order “adapted, integrable, conditional expectation”. For a concrete continuous process, see [[布朗运动|Brownian motion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 信息流与鞅
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Information flows and martingales*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

过滤 $\mathcal F_t$ 表示时刻 t 可用信息。过程 M 是鞅若可积、适应且 $E[M_t\mid\mathcal F_s]=M_s$（$s\le t$）。标准布朗运动自身是鞅，$W_t^2-t$ 也是；“均值不变” alone 不足以成为鞅。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A filtration $\mathcal F_t$ represents the information available at time $t$. A process $M$ is a martingale if it is integrable, adapted, and satisfies $E[M_t\mid\mathcal F_s]=M_s$ for $s\le t$. Standard Brownian motion is a martingale, as is $W_t^2-t$. An unchanged unconditional mean alone is not enough.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

适应性排除“偷看未来”的过程；可积性保证条件期望有定义；条件公平要求在全部当前信息下未来期望等于当前值。次鞅将等号改为不小于，超鞅改为不大于，分别表达正漂移和负漂移的条件方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adaptedness rules out looking into the future, integrability makes conditional expectation well-defined, and conditional fairness requires the expected future value given all current information to equal the present value. A submartingale replaces equality with a lower bound and a supermartingale with an upper bound, representing conditional upward and downward drift respectively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样构造与验证鞅
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How to construct and verify a martingale*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见构造是从累计和中减去可预测漂移。若 $X_i$ 独立、$E[X_i]=\mu$，则 $M_n=\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\mu)$ 相对于自然过滤是鞅。验证时不要只算 $E[M_n]$；必须计算 $E[M_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]$ 并使用增量的条件均值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common construction subtracts predictable drift from a cumulative sum. If the $X_i$ are independent with $E[X_i]=\mu$, then $M_n=\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\mu)$ is a martingale relative to its natural filtration. Verification must not stop at $E[M_n]$; compute $E[M_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]$ and use the conditional mean of the increment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若改变过滤，鞅性质可能改变。提前暴露某个未来增量后，原本均值为零的下一步可能变得可预测。因此必须在结论旁写清信息集，而不是把“某过程是鞅”当作无条件标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing the filtration can change the martingale property. If a future increment is revealed early, a formerly mean-zero next step may become predictable. The information set must therefore be stated with the conclusion rather than treating “is a martingale” as an unconditional label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 停时与可选停止的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stopping times and the limits of optional stopping*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

停时由当前和过去信息决定，例如首次到达阈值。可选停止定理在有界停时、可积性或一致可积等条件下才允许 $E[M_\tau]=E[M_0]$；赌博策略不能靠任意无限等待打败条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stopping time is determined by present and past information, such as the first time a threshold is reached. The optional stopping theorem permits $E[M_\tau]=E[M_0]$ only under conditions such as bounded stopping, suitable integrability, or uniform integrability. A gambling strategy cannot defeat those conditions by waiting arbitrarily long.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“最后一次达到阈值的时间”通常不是停时，因为在当下无法知道未来是否再次达到。检验方法是问事件 $\{\tau\le t\}$ 是否只用 $\mathcal F_t$ 的信息即可判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last time a process reaches a threshold is generally not a stopping time, because one cannot know at the present moment whether the threshold will be reached again. The diagnostic is whether the event $\{\tau\le t\}$ can be determined using only information in $\mathcal F_t$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：公平随机游走的吸收概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: absorption probability for a fair random walk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令公平随机游走从 i 开始，在 0 或 N 停止，$\tau$ 为首次到达边界。$M_n=X_n$ 是鞅，且停止后的值有界；可选停止给 $E[X_\tau]=i$。若 $p=P(X_\tau=N)$，则 $E[X_\tau]=Np+0(1-p)$，故 $p=i/N$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let a fair random walk start at $i$ and stop on reaching 0 or $N$, with $\tau$ the first boundary-hitting time. The process $M_n=X_n$ is a martingale, and the stopped value is bounded, so optional stopping gives $E[X_\tau]=i$. If $p=P(X_\tau=N)$, then $E[X_\tau]=Np+0(1-p)$ and therefore $p=i/N$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里不能只写“鞅所以期望不变”；必须说明 $\tau$ 是停时并核验适用条件。若边界被移除而允许无限翻倍下注，停止时间和财富可能失去可积/有界控制，结论就不再自动成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is not enough to write “the expectation is unchanged because this is a martingale”. One must show that $\tau$ is a stopping time and verify the theorem's conditions. If the boundaries are removed and unlimited doubling is allowed, the stopping time and wealth may lose the necessary integrability or boundedness, so the conclusion no longer follows automatically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Doob 分解入口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Entry point to the Doob decomposition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在离散时间，适当可积的次鞅可写成“鞅 + 可预测递增过程”。这把不可预测波动与系统性条件漂移分开，是许多估计、控制和金融分解的原型；当前卡只保留结构入口，不展开完整证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In discrete time, a suitably integrable submartingale can be written as a martingale plus a predictable increasing process. This separates unpredictable fluctuation from systematic conditional drift and is a prototype for many decompositions in estimation, control, and finance. This card retains the structural entry point without presenting a full proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一个过程期望恒定，是否必为鞅？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Is a process necessarily a martingale if its expectation is constant?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不是。鞅要求相对于给定信息流的条件期望等于当前值，恒定无条件均值不足。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> No. A martingale requires the conditional expectation relative to a specified information flow to equal the current value. A constant unconditional mean is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么可选停止不能无条件用于任意赌博停止策略？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can optional stopping not be applied unconditionally to any gambling strategy?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 无限或重尾停止时间可能破坏可积性/一致可积等条件，使交换极限和期望不合法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> An infinite or heavy-tailed stopping time may violate integrability or uniform-integrability conditions, making the interchange of limits and expectations invalid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 怎样快速检查一个随机时间是否为停时？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How can one quickly check whether a random time is a stopping time?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 对每个 t，检查“是否已经在 t 前停止”能否只凭 t 时刻及以前的信息判断；若必须知道未来，就不是停时。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> For every $t$, ask whether “stopping has occurred by time $t$” can be decided using information available by $t$. If future information is required, it is not a stopping time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
