# 判断央行回购投放或回笼准备金必须先确认现金流向与文件命名口径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Determine the cash direction and naming convention before classifying a central-bank repo as reserve provision or absorption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个[[回购协议]]有出借现金和借入现金两侧，repo与reverse repo的称谓又可能依文件而变。判断操作方向时先问：今天谁支付现金、谁交付证券、未来谁把现金还回去。只翻译“正回购／逆回购”容易把资金方向读反。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[回购协议|repo]] has cash-lending and cash-borrowing sides, while terminology can vary by document. First establish who pays cash today, who delivers securities, and who returns the cash later. A label-only translation can reverse the liquidity direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

纽约联储Trading Desk的官方命名是：其**repo operation**先买入证券并约定将来卖回，央行付出现金，在标准准备金结算下暂时增加准备金；其**reverse repo operation**先卖出证券并约定将来买回，央行收进现金，暂时减少准备金。这与从证券原卖方／买方描述普通市场交易的词序不能不加说明地混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the New York Fed Desk's terminology, a **repo operation** buys securities with an agreement to resell, pays cash, and temporarily adds reserves under standard settlement. A **reverse repo operation** sells with an agreement to repurchase, receives cash, and temporarily reduces reserves. Do not silently substitute ordinary seller/buyer terminology for the Desk's operation names.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，央行向交易对手支付100、约定次日收回现金并返还证券，是提供融资；央行今天收100、次日还款，则是吸收融资。无论名称如何，先画两天的现金箭头，方向就确定了。到期反转、续作与当日其他结算还会影响准备金净变化；“临时回笼”也不自动等于政策收紧。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Paying 100 today and receiving repayment tomorrow provides funding; receiving 100 today and repaying tomorrow absorbs it. The two-day cash arrows determine the direction. Maturity reversals, rollovers, and other settlements affect net reserves; temporary absorption is not automatically policy tightening.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
