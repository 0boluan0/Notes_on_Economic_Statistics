# 营运资本是同一时点流动资产减去流动负债的余额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Working capital is current assets minus current liabilities at the same date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以流动资产 $CA$、流动负债 $CL$ 表示，营运资本 $WC=CA-CL$，衡量流动资产超过流动负债的账面余额。这里包含所用报表范围内的现金、短期借款等；不是只含经营项目的[[经营营运资本]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With current assets $CA$ and current liabilities $CL$, $WC=CA-CL$ is their net book balance. It includes cash and short-term borrowing within the stated reporting scope, unlike [[经营营运资本|operating working capital]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $CA=700,CL=300$，营运资本400。它可由[[营运资本融资恒等式]]解释为长期资本支持流动资产的净额，但不是金库里有400现金，也不是当期经营现金流。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Current assets of 700 and current liabilities of 300 give working capital of 400. The [[营运资本融资恒等式|financing identity]] interprets this as net long-term funding supporting current assets; it is neither 400 in cash nor current-period operating cash flow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正值可以提供账面缓冲，却不保证资产及时变现；负值提示部分非流动资产由短期资金支持，但不能脱离回款速度、供应商信用与续融资条件判定违约。比较规模不同的企业还需结合[[流动比率]]和现金到期安排。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A positive balance can provide a book cushion without guaranteeing timely cash conversion. A negative balance indicates some noncurrent assets rely on short-term funding, but default cannot be inferred without collections, supplier credit, and refinancing conditions. Compare differently sized firms using the [[流动比率|current ratio]] and cash timing as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
