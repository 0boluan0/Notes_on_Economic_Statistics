# 套利是在给定交易约束下无需净初始投入且终值非负并有正概率盈利的可行自融资策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Arbitrage is a feasible self-financing strategy that requires no net initial investment, has nonnegative terminal wealth, and makes a profit with positive probability under the stated trading constraints*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

套利（arbitrage）不是“看起来便宜”，而是一项现金流性质。把买卖、融资、费用和履约义务计入同一个组合后，在允许的策略集合中，以零净初始财富开始、期末几乎必然不亏且有正概率盈利的[[自融资策略]]就是套利。给定市场和策略集合中不存在这样的机会，称为无套利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Arbitrage is a cash-flow property, not a judgment that something looks cheap. After including trades, financing, costs, and obligations in one portfolio, an allowed [[自融资策略|self-financing strategy]] starting from zero net wealth that never loses at the horizon almost surely and earns a profit with positive probability is an arbitrage. No-arbitrage means that no such opportunity exists in the specified market and strategy set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $V_t$ 表示包含现金／借款账户的净财富，固定期限 $T$ 的常用形式是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Writing $V_t$ for net wealth including cash and borrowing, a common fixed-horizon formulation is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
V_0=0,\qquad \Pr(V_T\ge0)=1,\qquad \Pr(V_T>0)>0.
$$

若交易一开始就净收款、以后无负支付，可以在可用的正价值现金账户中保留这笔收入，改写为上述零成本形式。反过来，花自己的 100 买入无风险资产而获得利息，不是零初始成本套利。“有正概率盈利”也不要求每个状态都严格盈利，但仅有正期望收益远远不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An immediate net receipt with no later negative payment can be retained in an available positive-value cash account to obtain the zero-cost form above. Investing one's own 100 in a risk-free asset and earning interest is not zero-cost arbitrage. Positive-probability profit need not mean strictly positive profit in every state, but positive expected return alone is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小例子：单期、零利率、无费用且可卖空，A 与 B 在每个期末状态都支付相同金额，当前价格分别为 98 和 100。买一份 A、卖空一份 B 并保留现金 2，初始净值 $98-100+2=0$，期末两项支付抵消而现金仍为 2。若交割权利不同、卖空不可行或费用耗尽 2，就必须重新检查，不能沿用这个证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a one-period, zero-interest, costless market with short sales available, let A and B pay exactly the same amount in every terminal state but cost 98 and 100. Buy A, short B, and retain cash 2: initial net value is $98-100+2=0$, while the terminal claims cancel and cash 2 remains. Different delivery rights, unavailable short sales, or costs consuming the 2 require a new check; the proof no longer applies unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多期与连续时间还要明确可实施性：持仓不能使用未来信息，融资必须可行，并需适当的可容许策略限制；例如以统一下界约束折现财富，排除依赖无限信用的加倍下注。单看一个正的终值公式，不能替代这些条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiperiod and continuous-time settings also require implementability: holdings cannot use future information, financing must be feasible, and an appropriate admissibility restriction is needed. A common restriction bounds discounted wealth below, excluding doubling schemes requiring unlimited credit. A positive terminal formula alone does not establish these conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Sources and verification*

- [Haugh，Forwards, Swaps, Futures and Options，§4.2，PDF／印刷第 12 页，Definitions 4–5](https://martin-haugh.github.io/files/FoundationsFE/for_swap_fut-options.pdf#page=12)：已重开并目视，支持初始净收款与正概率期末盈利两种套利表述，以及单期借贷、卖空条件。98／100 是本卡自拟的逐状态现金流检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Haugh, §4.2, PDF / printed p. 12, Definitions 4–5, reopened and visually checked, supports the immediate-receipt and positive-probability terminal-profit formulations and the one-period borrowing and short-sale assumptions. The 98/100 example is an independently constructed state-by-state cash-flow check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Kohn，PDE for Finance（1999），Section 7，PDF／印刷第 11 页](https://math.nyu.edu/~kohn/pde.finance/1999/section7.pdf#page=11)：已重开并目视，支持零初始财富、自融资、几乎必然非负终值、正概率严格盈利及折现财富下界限制；本卡不把该页简略论述扩写为一般连续时间资产定价定理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Kohn, Section 7, PDF / printed p. 11, reopened and visually checked, supports zero initial wealth, self-financing, almost-sure nonnegative terminal wealth, positive-probability gain, and a lower bound on discounted wealth. Its abbreviated discussion is not extended here into a general continuous-time asset-pricing theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
