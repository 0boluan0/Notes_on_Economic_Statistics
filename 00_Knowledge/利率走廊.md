# 利率走廊以央行存款和借款便利的利率为隔夜市场交易提供上下参照
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An interest-rate corridor uses central-bank deposit and lending rates as lower and upper reference options for overnight trading.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

走廊的下侧来自央行向合格余额或存款支付的利率，上侧来自合格机构向央行借款的成本。在人人能按相同条件使用便利、没有额外成本的简化模型里，机构不愿以低于存款收益的价格出借，也不愿以高于央行借款成本的价格融资。两项外部选择把市场利率夹在中间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lower side comes from reserve or deposit remuneration and the upper side from central-bank borrowing. In a stylised model with equal access and no extra costs, institutions will neither lend below the deposit return nor borrow above the facility cost. These outside options bracket the market rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设存款利率为3%、借款利率为5%，目标隔夜利率为4%。当准备金需求处于向下倾斜区间、两项管理利率不变时，央行增加准备金供给可降低成交利率；减少供给可提高它。央行也可以同时移动上下两项管理利率，使整个走廊上移或下移，不必把每次加息都解释成数量紧缩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the deposit rate is 3%, the lending rate 5%, and the overnight target 4%. On downward-sloping reserve demand with these rates fixed, additional reserves can lower the traded rate and a reduction can raise it. Moving both administered rates can instead shift the whole corridor, so a rate increase need not always require reserve drainage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是操作框架的模型，不是任何时期所有交易都严格满足的不等式。资格、抵押和污名会改变便利的实际可用性，见[[管理利率的市场边界]]。若准备金已多到市场主要沿近乎平坦的需求区间运行，就进入[[充足准备金框架|地板型实施的逻辑]]，不再靠日常微调数量定位走廊中点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an implementation model, not an invariant inequality for every trade. Access, collateral, and stigma qualify the bounds; see [[管理利率的市场边界|market limits]]. With sufficiently abundant reserves, the near-flat demand segment instead leads to [[充足准备金框架|floor-type implementation]], without daily quantity fine-tuning to the corridor midpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
