# 一致分类和分税后净利润等于税后经营净利润减税后净融资费用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After consistent classification and tax allocation, net income equals NOPAT less after-tax net financing expense.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把本期全部损益完整分成经营和金融两侧，再把所得税作一致分配，就能在保留净利润总额的同时，看清经营赚了多少、净融资成本扣了多少：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Classify all period profit-or-loss items into operating and financial components and allocate tax consistently. Total net income is preserved while operating profit and net financing cost become visible:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$NI=NOPAT-NFE_{after}.$$

例如 ABC 的[[税后经营净利润]]为 206.72，[[净融资费用]]税后为 70.72，差额正是净利润 136。分税也要相合：经营税 97.28 减融资抵税 33.28，等于报表所得税 64。不能为让利润式凑平，遗漏一项投资收益或把同一税盾计入两次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ABC's [[税后经营净利润|NOPAT]] of 206.72 less after-tax [[净融资费用|NFE]] of 70.72 equals net income of 136. Tax reconciles too: operating tax of 97.28 less the financing tax benefit of 33.28 equals reported tax of 64. Omitting investment income or counting a tax shield twice is not a valid reconciliation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般地，设 $NI=(OP-NFE_{pre})-T$，且 $T=T_O-B_F$，直接整理便得到上式。这是分类后的会计恒等关系；不意味着融资决策不会影响未来经营，也不意味着税后利润已经变成现金。它与[[管理用资产负债表恒等式]]一起构成[[管理用ROE分解]]的出发点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, if $NI=(OP-NFE_{pre})-T$ and $T=T_O-B_F$, rearrangement yields the identity. It is an accounting reconciliation, not a claim that financing never affects future operations or that after-tax profit is already cash. Together with the [[管理用资产负债表恒等式|balance-sheet identity]], it underpins [[管理用ROE分解|the ROE decomposition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
