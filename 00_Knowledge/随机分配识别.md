# 有效随机分配加上一致性、正值性与稳定干预，使组间均值差识别试验人群中的平均分配效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Valid random assignment together with consistency, positivity, and a stable intervention lets a group-mean contrast identify the average assignment effect in the trial population*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 随机化提供的是可比的分配机制
> 令 $A\in\{0,1\}$ 表示随机分配，$Y(1),Y(0)$ 表示两种分配下的潜在结果。若
> $$
> \{Y(1),Y(0)\}\perp A,
> \qquad 0<P(A=a)<1,
> $$
> 并且干预版本定义清楚；在当前只写成 $Y(a)$ 的表示中，单位之间没有干扰；观察结果满足一致性 $Y=Y(A)$，则
> $$
> E[Y\mid A=1]-E[Y\mid A=0]
> =E[Y(1)-Y(0)].
> $$
> 这里识别的是试验人群中“被分配到方案”的平均效应。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A\in\{0,1\}$ denote random assignment and $Y(1),Y(0)$ the potential outcomes under the two assignments. If assignment is independent of the potential outcomes, both arms have positive assignment probability, the intervention is well defined, there is no interference under the present $Y(a)$ representation, and consistency $Y=Y(A)$ holds, then the difference in arm means identifies the average effect of assignment in the trial population.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机化保证的是分配机制，不保证一次实现中每个基线变量恰好平衡。因此，基线检验不显著不能证明随机化成功；单个显著差异也不能单独推翻一份事先规定且执行过程可核验的随机分配记录，但它应促使研究者检查随机化执行、数据质量和偶然不平衡。预先规定的基线协变量调整可以提高精度，但不能把处理后的变量当作普通基线控制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Randomisation governs the assignment mechanism; it does not guarantee exact balance of every baseline variable in one realised sample. Nonsignificant balance tests do not prove successful randomisation, and one significant difference alone does not overturn a prespecified and auditable assignment procedure, although it should prompt checks of implementation, data quality, and chance imbalance. Prespecified baseline adjustment may improve precision, but post-assignment variables are not ordinary baseline controls.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机的也许只是邀请、资格或方案分配，而不是实际接受处理。若存在不依从，按原分配比较通常保留 intention-to-treat effect（ITT，分配意向效应）的解释；把人事后改分为“实际接受者”和“未接受者”，则会重新引入选择。要把随机分配当作工具变量解释实际处理效应，还需第一阶段、排除限制、单调性等额外条件，识别对象通常也会变成局部平均处理效应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
What is random may be an invitation, eligibility, or assignment rather than treatment received. With noncompliance, comparison by original assignment retains an intention-to-treat interpretation; regrouping units by treatment received reintroduces selection. Using assignment as an instrument for receipt requires additional first-stage, exclusion, and monotonicity assumptions and usually changes the estimand to a local average treatment effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机分配也不会自动解决差异失访、选择性结果缺失、处理扩散或外部推广。它能为试验内平均分配效应建立反事实可比性；从被分配到某方案推到实际接受处理的效应、从有结果记录者推回原试验人群、再从试验人群推广到另一个总体，每一步都需要新的论证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Random assignment does not by itself solve differential attrition, selective outcome missingness, treatment spillovers, or external transport. It establishes counterfactual comparability for an assignment effect within the trial. Moving from assignment to treatment received, from units with observed outcomes back to the original trial population, and from the trial population to another target population each requires a new argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一项培训实验随机发送邀请，但受邀者只有 60% 参加，未受邀者中也有 20% 自行参加。直接比较“参加者”和“未参加者”还能保留随机化识别吗？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A training experiment randomises invitations, but only 60% of invitees attend and 20% of non-invitees also attend through other channels. Does comparing attendees with non-attendees preserve randomised identification?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。随机的是邀请，不是实际参加。按邀请组比较可识别邀请的 ITT；参加本身的效应需要额外的工具变量或依从性假设。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. Invitation, not attendance, was randomised. Comparing invitation arms identifies the invitation ITT; the effect of attendance needs additional instrumental-variable or compliance assumptions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
