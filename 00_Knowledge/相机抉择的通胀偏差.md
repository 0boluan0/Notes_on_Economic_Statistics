# 在理性预期且政策试图把产出推到自然水平以上的基准模型中相机抉择带来通胀偏差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Discretion creates inflation bias in a rational-expectations benchmark where policy targets output above its natural level.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当私人部门预见政府想用意外通胀刺激产出时，通胀可在均衡中高于承诺基准，却没有持续产出收益。这个结论需要明确的产出目标楔子、时序与约束；它不是“任何相机抉择都必然高通胀”的定律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If private agents anticipate an incentive to stimulate output through surprise inflation, equilibrium inflation can exceed the commitment benchmark without a lasting output gain. The result needs an output-target wedge, a timing structure and specified constraints; it is not a law about every discretionary policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用无冲击的一期教学模型：私人先形成 $\pi^e$，央行随后直接选通胀 $\pi$。$x$ 是相对自然产出的缺口，$a>0$ 是意外通胀的产出效应；央行希望缺口达到 $k>0$，损失权重 $\lambda>0$。以通胀目标零作为归一化基准：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a deterministic one-period teaching model, private agents form $\pi^e$ before the central bank directly chooses $\pi$. The output gap relative to natural output is $x$, surprise inflation has coefficient $a>0$, and the preferred gap is $k>0$. The output-loss weight is $\lambda>0$; the inflation target is normalised to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$x=a(\pi-\pi^e),\qquad L=\pi^2+\lambda(x-k)^2.$$

央行把已经形成的预期当作既定，代入约束最小化损失：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking already-formed expectations as given and minimising the loss gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\pi+\lambda a[a(\pi-\pi^e)-k]=0
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\pi=\frac{\lambda a^2\pi^e+\lambda ak}{1+\lambda a^2}.
$$

二阶导数 $2(1+\lambda a^2)>0$。无冲击的理性预期均衡满足 $\pi^e=\pi$，因此 $\pi=\lambda ak>0$、$x=0$。若政策能在私人形成预期之前可信承诺，所有被预见的通胀都只给出 $x=0$，最优承诺便是 $\pi=0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second derivative is positive. Rational expectations in this deterministic equilibrium imply $\pi^e=\pi$, hence $\pi=\lambda ak>0$ and $x=0$. With credible commitment before expectations are formed, all anticipated inflation leaves $x=0$, so the optimal commitment is zero inflation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a=\lambda=k=1$，通胀与缺口按百分点记录。若公众相信零通胀，央行事后选择 $\pi=0.5$，损失从 1 降到 0.5，确有偏离诱因；预见此诱因的均衡却是 $\pi^e=\pi=1$、$x=0$、$L=2$。可信承诺下 $\pi=0$、$x=0$、$L=1$。不可把一次“欺骗成功”的 0.5 当成理性预期均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $a=\lambda=k=1$ and rates and gaps in percentage-point units, expectations of zero invite inflation of 0.5, lowering loss from 1 to 0.5. The anticipated equilibrium is instead $\pi^e=\pi=1$, $x=0$, and loss 2. Credible commitment yields zero inflation and loss 1. A successful one-off surprise is not the rational-expectations equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $k=0$，这个基准模型的通胀偏差消失；若引入冲击、信誉、惩罚或不同信息结构，须重新求解。央行直接控制通胀、无其他实施成本也是简化，不代表现实操作工具。定义入口与制度联系分别见[[政策时间不一致]]、[[承诺与可信性]]和[[央行目标与工具独立性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Setting $k=0$ eliminates this benchmark bias. Shocks, reputation, penalties or different information require a new solution. Direct inflation control without further implementation costs is also a simplification. See [[政策时间不一致|policy time inconsistency]], [[承诺与可信性|commitment and credibility]] and [[央行目标与工具独立性|central-bank independence]] for the distinct concepts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
