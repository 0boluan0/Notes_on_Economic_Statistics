# Geometric series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*几何级数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A geometric series keeps adding terms obtained by multiplying by one fixed ratio. The question is whether the running totals approach a finite number when the process continues indefinitely. For example, the partial totals of $1+1/2+1/4+\cdots$ begin $1,3/2,7/4,15/8$; they approach $2$. Every total is still finite, and the infinite sum means their limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

几何级数不断相加按固定公比变化的项，问题在于：一直加下去时，累计总量是否趋近某个有限数。例如，$1+1/2+1/4+\cdots$ 的前几个累计值是 $1,3/2,7/4,15/8$，它们趋近 $2$。每个累计值都仍是有限和；无穷和指的是这些累计值的极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $c,r$ be fixed real numbers. The first term is $c$, and each next term is obtained by multiplying by $r$. The series and its $N$-th partial sum are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $c,r$ 是固定实数，首项为 $c$，每次乘以 $r$ 得到下一项。级数及其第 $N$ 个部分和写为

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}cr^n=c+cr+cr^2+\cdots,\qquad S_N=\sum_{n=0}^{N}cr^n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $n$ indexes individual terms; $N$ marks the last included index, so $S_N$ has $N+1$ terms. As for any [[无穷级数|infinite series]], convergence means that $S_N$ approaches a finite limit as $N\to\infty$. It is a statement about the totals, not merely about individual terms becoming small.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $n$ 是单项索引，$N$ 表示最后纳入的索引，所以 $S_N$ 有 $N+1$ 项。与一般[[无穷级数]]一样，收敛指 $N\to\infty$ 时 $S_N$ 趋于有限极限；它说的是总和的行为，不只是每一项变小。

> [!important] Convergence and sum / 收敛条件与和
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $c\ne0$, the series converges exactly when $|r|<1$, and then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $c\ne0$ 时，级数收敛当且仅当 $|r|<1$，此时
>
> $$
> \boxed{\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}cr^n=\frac{c}{1-r}.}
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $c=0$, every term is zero and the series sums to zero for every real $r$, including $r=1$. The zero-th power is interpreted as the constant factor one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $c=0$，每一项都是零，因此任意实数 $r$（包括 $r=1$）都得到和零。零次幂按常数因子 1 理解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The partial sum leaves a visible tail
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从部分和看剩余尾部*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[几何和|finite geometric-sum formula]] gives $S_N=c(1-r^{N+1})/(1-r)$ when $r\ne1$. For $|r|<1$, the power $r^{N+1}$ tends to zero, leaving $c/(1-r)$. In fact, the difference between the limit and the partial sum is exactly
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$r\ne1$ 时，[[几何和|有限几何和公式]]给出 $S_N=c(1-r^{N+1})/(1-r)$。$|r|<1$ 使 $r^{N+1}$ 趋于零，留下 $c/(1-r)$。更具体地，极限与当前部分和的差恰为

$$
\frac{c}{1-r}-S_N=\frac{cr^{N+1}}{1-r},\qquad\left|\frac{c}{1-r}-S_N\right|=\frac{|c|\,|r|^{N+1}}{|1-r|}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $c=1,r=1/2$, after the four terms ending in $1/8$, the total is $15/8$ and the remaining gap is $1/8$. For $r=-1/2$, the sum is $2/3$; the partial totals alternate around it, but the gap magnitude still shrinks. For nonzero $c$, a convergent geometric series is also [[绝对收敛|absolutely convergent]], since the absolute terms have ratio $|r|<1$. The zero series is absolutely convergent as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$c=1,r=1/2$ 时，加完以 $1/8$ 结尾的四项，总和是 $15/8$，剩余差距为 $1/8$。$r=-1/2$ 时和为 $2/3$；部分和会在极限两侧交替，但差距大小仍缩小。$c\ne0$ 时，收敛的几何级数也[[绝对收敛]]，因为绝对值项的公比为 $|r|<1$；恒零级数同样绝对收敛。

> [!warning] Check convergence before using the fraction / 使用分式前先查收敛
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For nonzero $c$ and $|r|\ge1$, the terms $cr^n$ do not tend to zero. The [[级数通项判别|necessary zero-term condition]] therefore rules out convergence. With $c=1$, ratio $1$ gives totals $1,2,3,\ldots$; ratio $-1$ gives $1,0,1,0,\ldots$; ratio $2$ gives $1,3,7,15,\ldots$. Substituting $r=-1$ or $2$ into $1/(1-r)$ does not give the ordinary infinite sum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $c\ne0$ 且 $|r|\ge1$，通项 $cr^n$ 不趋于零，因而不满足[[级数通项判别|收敛所必需的通项趋零条件]]。取 $c=1$：$r=1$ 的部分和为 $1,2,3,\ldots$；$r=-1$ 为 $1,0,1,0,\ldots$；$r=2$ 为 $1,3,7,15,\ldots$。把 $-1$ 或 $2$ 代入 $1/(1-r)$，不能得到通常意义的无穷和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an [[普通生成函数|ordinary generating function]], $1/(1-x)=1+x+x^2+\cdots$ can instead be a formal coefficient identity with no number substituted. That algebraic use and the numerical limit considered here have different requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[普通生成函数]]中，$1/(1-x)=1+x+x^2+\cdots$ 还可以表示不代入数值的形式系数恒等式。那种代数用途，与本卡讨论的数值极限，使用条件不同。
