# 双重底是下跌后两次近同低试探并突破中间峰位的形态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A double bottom forms after a decline when two tests of a similar low are followed by a break above the intervening peak.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

双重底是在先前下跌之后，价格两次到达近似同一低位、两谷之间有一次反弹，并在第二谷之后向上突破中间峰位的反转形态。W 形轮廓只给出候选；突破中间 [[阻力位]]才满足这里采用的完成条件，与 [[双重顶]]的方向相反。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A double bottom follows a decline: two troughs reach approximately the same low, separated by a rebound, and price later breaks above the intervening peak. A W-shaped outline is only a candidate; clearing that [[阻力位|resistance]] completes this convention. It is the directional counterpart of a [[双重顶|double top]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两谷不要求数值完全相等，但允许差多少、如何确认局部低点以及怎样判断突破须先规定。若低点要等右侧若干期行情后才确认，它在那些期完成前尚不可用；图上回标不能提前产生订单，见 [[交易信号时序]]。突破后是否回试以及之后是否上涨都不是定义保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The troughs need not be numerically identical, but the tolerance, local-low confirmation and breakout test must be specified beforehand. A low requiring later observations becomes available only after those observations; plotting it back at the low cannot create an earlier order. See [[交易信号时序|signal and execution timing]]. Neither a retest nor subsequent gains are guaranteed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，已识别两谷均为 90，中间峰为 96。采用“收盘严格高于中间峰、无额外缓冲”的事前规则，之后收盘 96 仍未完成，97 才完成。两次触及 90 本身不能据此认定已经买入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two identified troughs at 90 and an intervening peak at 96, a preselected strict-close breakout rule without a buffer is not satisfied by a close at 96, but is by 97. Two tests of 90 alone do not mean a position has been bought.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
