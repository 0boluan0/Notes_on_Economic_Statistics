# 基本面风险是经济现金流或基本价值变化使纠偏头寸发生损失的风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fundamental risk is the risk that changes in economic cash flows or fundamental value cause losses on a price-correcting position*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本面风险（fundamental risk）来自资产经济现金流或基本价值的不确定变化。在套利限制分析中，交易者买入自认为低估的资产后，企业盈利、偿付能力或其他基本因素仍可能恶化；如果没有完全匹配的对冲，最终价值变化就可能使交易亏损。它不只是“市场暂时不认同我的估值”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fundamental risk comes from uncertain changes in an asset's economic cash flows or fundamental value. A trader buying an apparently undervalued asset can still face deteriorating earnings, repayment capacity, or other fundamentals. Without a perfectly matched hedge, the eventual value change can make the trade lose. This is not merely the market temporarily disagreeing with a valuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，忽略利息与费用，交易者以 90 买入一项当时估值为 100 的资产；后来新的经营坏消息使其最终支付变为 70，持有到期仍损失 20。即使最终市场价格准确反映 70，原先的纠偏交易也没有得到保证收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ignoring interest and costs, a trader buys at 90 an asset initially valued at 100. New adverse operating information subsequently reduces its terminal payment to 70, so holding to maturity still loses 20. Even a terminal price accurately reflecting 70 does not guarantee profit on the earlier correction trade.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与之相对，若基本价值保持 100，而价格在情绪影响下从 90 跌到 80，则这个设定里的进一步偏离属于[[噪声交易者风险]]，不是基本价值下降。现实中两者可同时出现；仅观察价格跌了，不能直接识别是哪一种。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In contrast, if fundamental value stays at 100 while sentiment pushes price from 90 to 80, the additional deviation in that setup is [[噪声交易者风险|noise-trader risk]], not deteriorating fundamental value. Both can occur together in practice; a price decline alone does not identify which caused it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本面风险也不等于“两个合约不完全相同”的基差或匹配误差。现金流差异可能留下基本面敞口，但两种名称回答的问题不同：前者问经济价值是否变化，后者问所用对冲关系是否吻合。系统性与公司特有的基本面冲击都可能重要；专门化且不充分分散的交易者并不会自动消除后一类风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fundamental risk is also not synonymous with basis or matching error between nonidentical contracts. Cash-flow differences can leave fundamental exposure, but the labels answer different questions: whether economic value changes, versus whether the chosen hedge relationship matches. Both systematic and firm-specific fundamental shocks can matter; specialized, incompletely diversified traders do not automatically eliminate the latter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Shleifer–Vishny（1997），PDF 第 15–17 页／印刷第 49–51 页，§IV](https://web.stanford.edu/~piazzesi/Reading/ShleiferVishny1997.pdf#page=15)：已重开，支持长期基本面风险、仅平均有利的交易，以及专业化交易者面对系统性和特有风险。本卡不沿用原文关于某类市场几乎没有基本面风险的概括；90／100／70 为自拟例子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Shleifer–Vishny, PDF pp. 15–17 / printed pp. 49–51, §IV, reopened, supports long-run fundamental risk, trades favorable only on average, and systematic and idiosyncratic exposure for specialized traders. The paper's broad remarks about certain markets having almost no fundamental risk are not adopted. The 90/100/70 example is independently constructed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [De Long 等（1990），PDF 第 4 页／印刷第 705 页](https://ms.mcmaster.ca/~grasselli/DeLongShleiferSummersWaldmann90.pdf#page=4)：已重开并目视，支持基本面风险与噪声交易者风险的独立区分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- De Long et al., PDF p. 4 / printed p. 705, reopened and visually checked, supports the separate treatment of fundamental and noise-trader risk.
