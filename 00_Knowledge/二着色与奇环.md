# Two-colorability and odd cycles
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*二着色与奇环*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trying to color a square with two colors works: alternate $0,1,0,1$ around its four vertices, and the closing edge joins different colors. On a five-cycle, alternation gives $0,1,0,1,0$; the closing edge now joins two zeros. This obstruction characterizes the entire problem: a finite simple undirected graph can be properly colored with at most two colors exactly when it contains no odd cycle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给正方形的四个顶点交替染上 $0,1,0,1$，闭合边的两端仍然异色，因此二着色可行。五边形环依次得到 $0,1,0,1,0$，闭合边却连接两个零。这一障碍完整刻画了问题：有限简单无向图能用至多两色合法着色，当且仅当它不含奇环。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Three equivalent descriptions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*三个等价描述*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite [[图类型的建模边界|simple undirected graph]]. A [[Proper 着色与独立集划分|proper coloring]] gives different labels to adjacent vertices. A [[游走路径与环|cycle]] returns to its starting vertex without repeating any other vertex or any edge; in this graph type it has at least three edges. An odd cycle has an odd number of edges. A [[二分图|bipartite graph]] admits a split $V=L\mathbin{\dot\cup}R$ with every edge crossing the two disjoint sides; either side may be empty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $G=(V,E)$ 是有限[[图类型的建模边界|简单无向图]]。[[Proper 着色与独立集划分|合法着色]]要求相邻顶点标签不同。[[游走路径与环|环]]回到起点，除此之外不重复顶点，也不重复边；在这种图中它至少有三条边。边数为奇数的环称为奇环。[[二分图]]允许把 $V$ 写成 $L\mathbin{\dot\cup}R$：两侧不交且覆盖全部顶点，每条边跨越两侧，任意一侧可以为空。

> [!theorem] The odd-cycle criterion · 奇环判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The following statements are equivalent: $G$ is bipartite; there is a map $c:V\to\{0,1\}$ with $c(u)\ne c(v)$ for every edge $\{u,v\}$; and $G$ has no odd cycle. The map need not use both labels. For $V=\varnothing$, the empty map satisfies the coloring condition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 以下三项等价：$G$ 是二分图；存在映射 $c:V\to\{0,1\}$，使每条边 $\{u,v\}$ 都满足 $c(u)\ne c(v)$；$G$ 没有奇环。映射不必同时使用两个标签。若 $V=\varnothing$，空映射满足着色条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bipartition and coloring descriptions translate directly: color $L$ by $0$ and $R$ by $1$, or take the sets of vertices labeled $0$ and $1$ as the two sides. Every edge condition is preserved in both directions. It remains to connect coloring with cycles.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

划分与着色可以直接互译：给 $L$ 染 $0$、$R$ 染 $1$；反过来，把标签为 $0$、$1$ 的顶点各收集成一侧。两个方向都保留每条边的限制。剩下要证明的是着色与环之间的联系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why two colors exclude odd cycles
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么二着色排除奇环*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Along a cycle, each edge forces a switch to the other color. After an even number of switches the label is the starting label; after an odd number it is the other one. Since the final vertex is the starting vertex, a proper coloring requires an even number of switches. Thus every cycle must have even length. This argument applies to any cycle inside the graph, even if many other vertices and edges lie outside it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿环每走一条边，就必须切换到另一种颜色。切换偶数次回到原标签，切换奇数次则变成另一标签。但终点就是起点，所以合法着色要求切换次数为偶数，即环长必须为偶数。论证适用于图中任意一个环，即使环外还有许多顶点和边。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why no odd cycle guarantees a coloring
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么没有奇环就一定能够二着色*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Treat each [[无向图连通分量|connected component]] separately. Choose a root and run a complete search to obtain its [[搜索标记与父森林|parent tree]]. Every new vertex is attached to an earlier one, so each vertex has one tree path from the root. Let $d(v)$ be the number of edges in that path, and color $v$ by the parity of $d(v)$: even gives $0$, odd gives $1$. Every tree edge joins a parent and child whose depths differ by one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分别处理每个[[无向图连通分量|连通分量]]。选一个根，完成搜索，得到该分量的[[搜索标记与父森林|父树]]。每次把新顶点接到已有顶点上，因此每个顶点都有一条从根出发的唯一树路径。令 $d(v)$ 为这条路径的边数，按其奇偶性给 $v$ 着色：偶数染 $0$，奇数染 $1$。每条树边连接父子，深度相差一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose some graph edge $uv$ has equal-colored endpoints. They are in the same component and have depths of the same parity. This cannot be a tree edge. Let $z$ be the last common vertex on their two root paths. After $z$ those paths are disjoint. Go from $z$ to $u$ along one branch, cross $uv$, and follow the other branch backward to $z$. This is a simple cycle, and its length is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设某条图边 $uv$ 的两端同色。它们属于同一分量，深度同奇偶，因此 $uv$ 不可能是树边。令 $z$ 是两条根路径上的最后公共顶点；从 $z$ 之后，两条路径不再相交。沿一支从 $z$ 到 $u$，经过边 $uv$，再沿另一支反向回到 $z$，便得到简单环，其长度为

$$\bigl(d(u)-d(z)\bigr)+\bigl(d(v)-d(z)\bigr)+1
=d(u)+d(v)-2d(z)+1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two depths have even sum, so this length is odd, contradicting the assumption. If $z$ equals $u$ or $v$, one branch has length zero; the other branch and the non-tree edge still form the same simple odd cycle. Therefore no equal-colored edge exists. Components have no edges between them, so their colorings combine into a coloring of the whole graph. With no vertices there are no components to process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个深度之和为偶数，因此环长为奇数，与假设矛盾。若 $z$ 就是 $u$ 或 $v$，其中一支长零；另一支与非树边仍形成同样的简单奇环。所以不存在同色边。不同分量之间没有边，合并各分量的着色即可覆盖全图。没有顶点时，也没有需要处理的分量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For [[BFS 分层与无权最短路|BFS]], this tree depth also equals unweighted shortest-path distance from the root. For [[DFS 可达性与非最短路径|DFS]], use tree depth only; shortest distance is not required by the parity argument. The constructive version, including how to return the actual cycle, is [[二着色与奇环证书|two-coloring and odd-cycle certificates]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用 [[BFS 分层与无权最短路|BFS]] 时，树深度还等于到根的无权最短距离；采用 [[DFS 可达性与非最短路径|DFS]] 时，只使用树深度，奇偶证明不需要最短距离。具体怎样执行搜索并输出实际环，见[[二着色与奇环证书]]。

## At most two is not always exactly two
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*至多两色不总是恰需两色*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the [[色数|chromatic number]] convention $\chi(G)=0$ for the empty vertex set, a bipartite graph has $\chi(G)=0$ when $V=\varnothing$, $\chi(G)=1$ when $V\ne\varnothing$ but $E=\varnothing$, and $\chi(G)=2$ when it has an edge. In the last case the edge requires two different colors, and the theorem supplies them. A bipartition answers whether two sides exist; a [[二分图配对关系|matching model]] subsequently asks which allowed cross-edges to select. The coloring itself makes no pairing assignments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用空顶点集的[[色数]]为 $\chi(G)=0$ 的约定，二分图在 $V=\varnothing$ 时色数为零，在 $V\ne\varnothing$ 但 $E=\varnothing$ 时色数为一，在至少有一条边时色数为二。最后一种情形中，一条边要求两个不同颜色，而定理保证两色足够。二分划分回答“两侧能否存在”；[[二分图配对关系|配对模型]]随后才问要选择哪些允许的跨侧边，着色本身不作配对分配。

> [!question]- Does triangle-free imply two-colorable? · 没有三角形就能二着色吗？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. The five-cycle $C_5$ has no triangle, yet the alternating labels conflict at its closing edge. Seven-cycles give another example. All odd cycles must be excluded, or a full two-coloring search must succeed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不够。五环 $C_5$ 没有三角形，但交替标签在闭合边处冲突；七环也是反例。必须排除所有奇环，或让全图二着色搜索成功。
