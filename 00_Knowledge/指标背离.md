# 指标背离是价格与指定指标在对应转折点上呈现不一致高低变化的关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Price-indicator divergence is a mismatch between price and indicator highs or lows at corresponding turning points.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指标背离比较价格走势与一个已指定的 [[技术指标]]：在常规顶背离中，价格的后一个高点高于前一个，指标的对应高点却更低；常规底背离则是价格低点更低，而指标对应低点更高。比较前要说明指标、参数、价格字段，以及哪些转折点算作对应点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price-indicator divergence compares price movements with a specified [[技术指标|technical indicator]]. A regular bearish divergence has a higher price high but a lower corresponding indicator high; a regular bullish divergence has a lower price low but a higher indicator low. Specify the indicator, parameters, price field, and turning-point correspondence first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡以常规背离为对象。例如两个对应价格高点为 $100,105$，指标高点为 $80,75$，结构上属于顶背离。这里比较的是两段高点的变化方向，不是要求价格线与指标线在同一坐标上相交；指标值也不必与价格拥有相同单位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card concerns regular divergence. Corresponding price highs of $100,105$ and indicator highs of $80,75$ form a bearish divergence. The comparison is between changes in successive highs, not a crossing of price and indicator lines on one scale. Their units need not match.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若把某日高点定义为“高于左右各一日”，就要到右侧一天结束后才确认。这个确认延迟必须进入 [[交易信号时序]]，不能把后来确认的拐点当作当天已经知道。较宽的左右窗口会有不同结果和不同延迟；“对应”也可以允许限定错位，但必须提前定好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a high is defined as exceeding the days immediately on both sides, it becomes confirmed only after the right-hand day closes. That delay belongs in [[交易信号时序|signal timing]]; a later-confirmed pivot was not known on the pivot date. Wider windows change both detection and delay. Any permitted offset between corresponding pivots must also be specified beforehand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

背离是已观察路径的关系名称。它能否预测持续反转，需要额外证据，见 [[背离不能确定反转]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Divergence names a relationship in observed paths. Predicting a sustained reversal requires additional evidence; see [[背离不能确定反转|the limits of reversal claims from divergence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
