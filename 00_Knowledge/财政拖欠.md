# 财政拖欠是政府到期应付款项仍未支付的状态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Government payment arrears are amounts that remain unpaid after their payment due date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拖欠同时要求“未付”和“已到期”。已经形成应付款，但还在合同允许的支付期内，不是拖欠；签订尚未履行的采购承诺也不自动是应付款。先用[[现金与权责发生]]确定义务形成时间，再查到期日和实际支付日。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An arrear must be both unpaid and overdue. A payable within its permitted payment period is not yet an arrear, and an unperformed procurement commitment is not automatically a payable. Establish recognition through [[现金与权责发生|cash and accrual timing]], then check due and payment dates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

12月完成的服务费用12，次年1月底到期：12月底记应付款，但没有拖欠；若到2月1日仍未付，才出现12的拖欠。已有应付款变为逾期，不应把同一12再加一次到总债务；到期未付的利息、工资或货款需按对应工具及备忘信息追踪。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A service cost of 12 recognised in December and due at the end of January becomes overdue only if unpaid on 1 February. The existing payable entering arrears is not another 12 of total debt. Track overdue interest, wages and supplier bills in the relevant instruments and supplementary records.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金报表可能因为暂未付款而少记当期流出，但这不表明服务没发生或义务消失。即便逾期只是行政延误，也应如实识别；原因与规模、持续时间、能否及时清偿需要另作判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash outflows may be delayed while the service and obligation already exist. Administrative delay is still an arrear once overdue; its cause, size, persistence and capacity for settlement are separate questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
