# Radius of convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*幂级数收敛半径*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The radius of convergence measures how far we may move from a power series' centre while retaining convergence throughout the interior. It is a distance, not an endpoint coordinate. For $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(x-2)^n$, the [[几何级数|geometric-series condition]] is $|x-2|<1$: the centre is $2$, the radius is $1$, and the interior runs from $1$ to $3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收敛半径衡量：从幂级数中心向外移动多远，仍能保证内部处处收敛。它是一个距离，不是端点坐标。对 $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(x-2)^n$，[[几何级数]]条件为 $|x-2|<1$：中心是 $2$，半径是 $1$，内部区间从 $1$ 延伸到 $3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the real [[幂级数|power series]] $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(x-a)^n$, with fixed real centre $a$ and finite real coefficients $c_n$. At each fixed input $x$, [[绝对收敛|absolute convergence]] means that the magnitude series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}|c_n(x-a)^n|$ has a finite sum; this also guarantees convergence of the original series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑实数[[幂级数]] $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(x-a)^n$，其中实数中心 $a$ 固定，每个实数系数 $c_n$ 都有限。固定输入 $x$ 后，[[绝对收敛]]指绝对值级数 $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}|c_n(x-a)^n|$ 有有限和；它也保证原级数收敛。

> [!important] The distance threshold / 距离阈值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> There is a unique $R\in[0,\infty]$, called the **radius of convergence**, such that the series converges absolutely for $|x-a|<R$ and diverges for $|x-a|>R$. At the centre $x=a$, the sum is always $c_0$.
>
> If $R=0$, only the centre converges. If $R=\infty$, every real input converges absolutely. If $0<R<\infty$, the two endpoints $a-R$ and $a+R$ require separate tests. The symbol $\infty$ means there is no finite distance cutoff; it is not an input to substitute.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 存在唯一的 $R\in[0,\infty]$，称为**收敛半径**，使级数在 $|x-a|<R$ 时绝对收敛，在 $|x-a|>R$ 时发散。中心 $x=a$ 处的和始终为 $c_0$。
>
> $R=0$ 时只有中心收敛；$R=\infty$ 时每个实数输入都绝对收敛；$0<R<\infty$ 时，两个端点 $a-R$、$a+R$ 需要另行判断。这里 $\infty$ 表示没有有限距离上限，不是一个可代入的输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why a convergent point protects all closer points
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一个收敛点为什么能保证更近的点？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the series converges at an input $y\ne a$, and let $d=|y-a|>0$. Its terms $c_n(y-a)^n$ tend to zero by the [[级数通项判别|necessary condition for series convergence]]. They are therefore bounded: after some index their magnitudes are at most $1$, and a finite initial list also has a finite bound. Choose a single constant $M>0$ bounding them all. At any input with $|x-a|<d$, we then have
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设级数在某个 $y\ne a$ 收敛，记其到中心的距离为 $d=|y-a|>0$。由[[级数通项判别]]，通项 $c_n(y-a)^n$ 趋于零，因此有界：足够晚的项的绝对值至多为 $1$，前面的有限项也有有限上界。选一个常数 $M>0$ 统一界住所有项。对任何满足 $|x-a|<d$ 的输入，便有

$$
|c_n(x-a)^n|=|c_n(y-a)^n|\left(\frac{|x-a|}{d}\right)^n
\le Mq^n,\qquad q=\frac{|x-a|}{d}<1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $q\ge0$ is the new distance divided by the old distance. Since $\sum Mq^n$ is a convergent geometric series, [[级数直接比较判别|comparison]] proves absolute convergence at $x$. The original convergence at $y$ need not have been absolute. The smaller distance supplies the extra geometric decay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $q\ge0$ 是新距离与旧距离之比。因为 $\sum Mq^n$ 是收敛的几何级数，由[[级数直接比较判别]]，新输入 $x$ 处绝对收敛。原来 $y$ 处甚至不必绝对收敛；更小的距离提供了额外的几何衰减。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see that one radius describes the whole picture, collect all distances $|y-a|$ at which the series converges. This set contains $0$. If it is unbounded, every finite distance lies below some convergent distance, so every input converges absolutely. Otherwise take its [[上确界|supremum]], the least upper bound $R$, whose existence follows from the [[实数的确界性质|completeness of the real numbers]]. Every distance smaller than $R$ lies below some convergent distance and is covered by the comparison above; a distance larger than $R$ cannot be in the set. If $R=0$, the set contains only $0$. This also proves uniqueness: two distinct candidate radii would give contradictory conclusions at a distance between them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为说明一个半径能描述全局，把所有收敛输入对应的距离 $|y-a|$ 收集起来。这个集合包含 $0$。若它无界，每个有限距离之上都还能找到一个收敛距离，因此所有输入都绝对收敛。若它有界，就取其[[上确界]]，即最小上界 $R$；[[实数的确界性质]]保证其存在。每个小于 $R$ 的距离之上都有某个收敛距离，从而可用上述比较；大于 $R$ 的距离不可能属于这个集合。若 $R=0$，集合就只有 $0$。这也说明半径唯一：若有两个不同的候选半径，位于两者之间的距离会同时被判为收敛和发散。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## All three radius cases occur
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*三种半径都确实会出现*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\sum(x-a)^n$, the radius is $1$. For $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(x-a)^n/n!$, it is $\infty$: for fixed $x\ne a$, the absolute term ratio is $|x-a|/(n+1)\to0$, so the [[级数比值判别|ratio test]] succeeds. Here $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$ for $n\ge1$ and $0!=1$. For $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}n!(x-a)^n$, the ratio is $(n+1)|x-a|\to\infty$ for every $x\ne a$, so the radius is $0$. At $x=a$, both series are checked directly and sum to $1$; their ratios there would involve $0/0$ and are not used. A polynomial, including the zero polynomial, has radius $\infty$ because its late coefficients vanish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\sum(x-a)^n$ 的半径为 $1$。$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(x-a)^n/n!$ 的半径为 $\infty$：固定 $x\ne a$ 时，通项绝对值之比为 $|x-a|/(n+1)\to0$，可用[[级数比值判别]]。这里 $n\ge1$ 时 $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$，并约定 $0!=1$。而 $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}n!(x-a)^n$ 对每个 $x\ne a$ 的比值都是 $(n+1)|x-a|\to\infty$，所以半径为 $0$。中心 $x=a$ 处直接检查，两者都得到和 $1$；此时比值会涉及 $0/0$，不能使用。多项式（包括零多项式）的后续系数最终为零，因此半径为 $\infty$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A general coefficient formula is available even when neighbouring coefficients are zero. Let $L=\limsup_{n\to\infty}|c_n|^{1/n}$, where [[数列上极限|limit superior]] measures the upper limiting size of these nonnegative roots, with $n\ge1$. The [[级数根值判别|root test]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使相邻系数可能为零，也有通用的系数公式。令 $L=\limsup_{n\to\infty}|c_n|^{1/n}$，其中 [[数列上极限|上极限]]衡量这些非负数最终能反复达到的上侧尺度，取 $n\ge1$。由[[级数根值判别]]得到

$$
R=\begin{cases}
\infty,&L=0,\\
1/L,&0<L<\infty,\\
0,&L=\infty.
\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indeed, for $x\ne a$ the root upper limit of the full term is $|x-a|L$. Values below $1$ give absolute convergence and values above $1$ give divergence. When $L=\infty$, this product is considered only at positive distances; the centre is handled separately. Changing finitely many coefficients changes the sum by a polynomial but does not change the radius, since a finite prefix cannot affect convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理由是：对 $x\ne a$，完整通项的根值上极限等于 $|x-a|L$；小于 $1$ 时绝对收敛，大于 $1$ 时发散。$L=\infty$ 时只在正距离处考虑这个乘积，中心单独处理。修改有限多个系数只使和改变一个多项式，不改变半径，因为有限前缀不影响敛散性。

> [!warning] The radius leaves the boundary undecided / 半径没有决定边界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n$ and $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x^n/n^2$ have radius $1$. The first diverges at both $x=\pm1$; the second converges absolutely at both, because the magnitude series is $\sum1/n^2$, covered by the [[p级数判别|p-series test]]. The radius describes the open interior, while [[幂级数端点判别|endpoint testing]] determines which boundary points belong to the complete convergence interval.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n$ 与 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x^n/n^2$ 的半径都是 $1$。前者在 $x=\pm1$ 都发散；后者两端都绝对收敛，因为其绝对值级数为 $\sum1/n^2$，可由 [[p级数判别]]处理。半径描述开区间内部，[[幂级数端点判别]]决定完整收敛区间是否还包含边界点。
