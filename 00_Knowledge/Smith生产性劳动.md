# Smith 的生产性劳动把劳动形成的价值留在可出售的产品中以补回预付并取得利润

在 Smith 的用语中，生产性劳动（productive labour）是把劳动所增加的价值留在某种可出售产品中的劳动；产品在劳动结束后仍然存在，售得的价值通常可以补回先前的支出并带来利润。这个定义关心劳动形成的价值能否保留下来、再支持下一轮生产，而不只是问一项工作有没有用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Smith's terminology, productive labour adds value that is embodied in a vendible product surviving the work itself. Its sale normally replaces earlier advances and yields a profit. The definition concerns whether the value created remains available to support further production, rather than simply whether the work is useful.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

制造工人把原料加工成商品，是他的典型例子：工人的工资虽然由雇主先付，商品卖出后，增加的价值通常会补回工资并提供利润。家庭仆役的即时服务则不同：主人享用了服务，却没有留下可以售出、再购买同等劳动的产品。Smith 把后者称为非生产性劳动（unproductive labour）。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith's example is a manufacturing worker who turns materials into a commodity. The employer advances wages, but the added value of the product normally restores those wages with a profit. A domestic servant's immediate service is different: the employer consumes it without retaining a vendible product that can purchase an equivalent amount of labour again. Smith calls the latter unproductive labour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“非生产性”在这里不等于无价值、不值得付酬或不重要。Smith 紧接着承认仆役劳动有其价值并应得到报酬；他把国防、司法等也列入这一类别，同时明确它们可能有用、可敬，而且不可缺少。因此，不能根据这个术语直接推断应当取消某项服务。分类的依据也不是雇主姓“公”还是姓“私”：家庭仆役本来就可以由私人雇用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unproductive does not mean worthless, undeserving of payment or unimportant. Smith immediately recognises that servants' work has value and deserves remuneration. He also places defence and justice in this category while acknowledging that they may be useful, honourable and necessary. The label alone therefore gives no reason to abolish a service. Nor is the distinction simply public versus private employment: a domestic servant can be privately employed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理解这个历史口径，才能读懂 [[Smith积累机制]] 中“把节省的收入用于维持生产性劳动”的说法：他想说明资源怎样进入可以持续补回的生产过程。至于一般能力和教育的价值，Smith 还在 [[分工的能力代价|分工的能力后果]] 中另作讨论，不能由这套劳动分类一笔带过。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This historical usage is needed to understand [[Smith积累机制|Smith's account of capital accumulation]], where saved income supports productive labour and a process capable of replenishing its advances. The value of education and broader capabilities is also discussed through [[分工的能力代价|the capability consequences of division of labour]]; this labour classification does not settle those questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Smith，[《国富论》第二篇第三章](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=18)，第1–2段：增值、可出售产品、工资预付的恢复与利润，以及非生产性服务的价值、有用性和必要性；第5–6段：作为资本使用与用于当前消费的区分。本卡定义保留 Smith 限定，不将它泛化为所有经济学传统的“生产性劳动”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Book II, Chapter III, paragraphs 1–2 supply the definition, advances and profit, and the acknowledged value and necessity of services classified as unproductive. Paragraphs 5–6 distinguish employment as capital from immediate consumption. The definition remains explicitly Smith's rather than a universal usage across economic traditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
