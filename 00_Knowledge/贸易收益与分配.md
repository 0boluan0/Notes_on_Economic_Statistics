# 贸易的总收益为正并不意味着每个人都受益或损失仅是暂时的
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Positive aggregate gains from trade do not mean that everyone gains or that losses are only temporary.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在给定技术、竞争市场且没有未计入外部成本等条件下，开放贸易可以扩大消费机会；但一个国家能得到更多东西，不等于国内每个人都分到更多。进口品降价有利于购买者，却可能压低进口竞争企业的收入；同一个人还可能同时是消费者、工人和资产持有者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With given technology, competitive markets and no omitted external costs, trade can expand consumption possibilities. That does not ensure a gain for every resident. Cheaper imports help buyers but may reduce income in import-competing firms. A person may simultaneously be a consumer, worker and asset owner.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必须区分两种损失：转岗期间的求职、搬迁、培训和失业成本；以及新均衡中某类技能或资产的回报长期下降。前者是调整过程，后者是分配结果。不能用“以后会找到工作”回答所有损失，也不能用“有人失业”单独证明总收益为负。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish search, relocation, retraining and unemployment costs during adjustment from a persistent fall in returns to particular skills or assets in the new equilibrium. Finding another job need not restore the previous income. Conversely, some unemployment does not by itself establish negative aggregate gains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自拟例：用同一时期、同一货币福利口径衡量，获益群体增加 100，受损群体减少 60，净额是 40。若转移无成本且能准确到达受损者，转移 70 可以使两组都比原来好；**这个算术只说明补偿可能，不说明补偿已经发生。** 实际还要查税收与执行成本、遗漏人群和期限。货币加总也不是效用的无条件加总，见 [[总剩余]] 与 [[剩余与补偿度量]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed example on a common monetary-welfare and time basis, one group gains 100 and another loses 60. Net gains are 40. A costless, accurately targeted transfer of 70 could benefit both relative to the initial situation, but this proves only the possibility of compensation. Actual policy must account for financing, administration, coverage and duration. [[总剩余|Total surplus]] and [[剩余与补偿度量|Surplus versus compensation measures]] explain the limits of monetary aggregation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，评价一项贸易变化要同时报告总收益、损益归属与调整路径。若新增培训或补偿方案，效果仍需与没有该方案的情形比较，复用 [[公共项目反事实]]，不能将整个贸易收益都归功于补偿政策。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Evaluate aggregate gains, their incidence and the adjustment path together. Any training or compensation programme requires its own [[公共项目反事实|counterfactual]]; the entire gain from trade cannot be attributed to that programme.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
