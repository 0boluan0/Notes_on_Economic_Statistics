# 同一银行内支付只重记客户存款而无需跨行准备金结算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An on-us payment reassigns customer deposits within one bank without interbank reserve settlement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子机制
> 付款人与收款人在同一家银行时，银行把存款负债从付款人子账户借记、向收款人子账户贷记。在忽略费用、透支和其他附带交易时，该行总存款负债不变，也没有另一家银行需要用准备金结算。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When payer and recipient use the same bank, the bank debits the payer's deposit subaccount and credits the recipient's. Ignoring fees, overdrafts, and other accompanying transactions, the bank's total deposit liability is unchanged and no second bank requires reserve settlement.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是一项银行内部账簿转移：公众之间的购买力所有者改变，但银行对所有客户的总负债不变。支付系统仍可能处理消息、认证和记录，却不因为每一笔同行支付都需要在两家银行的央行账户间移动余额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an internal ledger transfer: ownership of purchasing power changes between members of the public, while the bank's total liability to all customers is unchanged. Payment infrastructure may still process messages, authentication, and records, but an on-us payment does not require balances to move between two banks' central-bank accounts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- “没有准备金转移”不等于“没有支付”；最终账簿可以是同一家商业银行的负债表。
- 若付款触发透支贷款、手续费或外币兑换，总存款和权益可能发生额外变化。
- 银行内部流动不改变体系总准备金，也不证明银行无需为未来跨行净流出管理准备金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- “No reserve transfer” does not mean “no payment”; final bookkeeping may occur on one commercial bank's balance sheet.
- An overdraft loan, fee, or foreign-exchange conversion can add further changes to deposits and equity.
- An internal transfer does not change system reserves and does not remove the bank's need to manage reserves for future interbank outflows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 同行转账后，为什么该银行总存款不变？
>
> **答案：** 付款人的存款减少与收款人的存款增加等额抵消；改变的是负债归属，不是负债总额。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why is the bank's total deposit balance unchanged after an on-us transfer?
>
> **Answer:** The fall in the payer's deposit is offset by the equal rise in the recipient's deposit. The owner of the liability changes, not its total amount.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
