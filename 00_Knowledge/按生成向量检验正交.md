# A vector is orthogonal to a span exactly when it is orthogonal to every spanning vector
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*与张成空间正交，等价于与每个生成向量正交*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $S=\operatorname{span}\{v_1,\ldots,v_k\}\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, a vector $z\in\mathbb R^n$ is orthogonal to every vector in $S$ if and only if $z\cdot v_i=0$ for every $i$. The spanning vectors may be redundant; they need not form a basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $S=\operatorname{span}\{v_1,\ldots,v_k\}\subseteq\mathbb R^n$，向量 $z\in\mathbb R^n$ 与 $S$ 中每个向量正交，当且仅当每个 $z\cdot v_i=0$。生成向量可以有冗余，不必是一组基。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Necessity follows because each $v_i\in S$. For sufficiency, every $s\in S$ is a linear combination, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必要性来自每个 $v_i\in S$。充分性则利用任意 $s\in S$ 都是生成向量的线性组合：

$$
z\cdot s=z\cdot\left(\sum_i c_i v_i\right)
=\sum_i c_i(z\cdot v_i)=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the plane spanned by $p=(2,1,0)^T$ and $q=(-3,0,1)^T$, write $z=(a,b,c)^T$. The two tests $2a+b=0$ and $-3a+c=0$ give $z=a(1,-2,3)^T$, describing its entire [[正交补|orthogonal complement]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $p=(2,1,0)^T$、$q=(-3,0,1)^T$ 张成的平面，令 $z=(a,b,c)^T$。检验 $2a+b=0$ 和 $-3a+c=0$，得到 $z=a(1,-2,3)^T$，这描述了整个[[正交补]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tested vectors must span the target space. Checking only $p$ is insufficient here: $z=(0,0,1)^T$ is orthogonal to $p$ but has $z\cdot q=1$. A non-spanning subset can miss a direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

被检验的向量必须张成目标空间。此例只检验 $p$ 不够：$z=(0,0,1)^T$ 虽然与 $p$ 正交，却满足 $z\cdot q=1$。未张成整个空间的子集会漏掉方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The official solution supports testing the two plane basis vectors; the proof above expands the linearity argument. The example and non-spanning counterexample are checked by substitution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
