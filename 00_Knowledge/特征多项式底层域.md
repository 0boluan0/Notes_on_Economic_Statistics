# The field of a characteristic polynomial
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*特征多项式的数域：允许在哪些数里寻找根*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An equation can have no solution among real numbers and still have a solution among complex numbers. The same issue arises when finding [[特征对|eigenvalues]]: before asking how many there are, specify which numbers are allowed. The **scalar field** is this collection of allowed scalars, such as the real numbers $\mathbb R$ or the complex numbers $\mathbb C$. It determines both the possible eigenvalues and the allowed coordinates of eigenvectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
一个方程在实数中无解，却可能在复数中有解。寻找[[特征对|特征值]]时也有同样的问题：在问有多少个特征值之前，先说明允许使用哪些数。**标量域**就是允许使用的标量集合，例如实数 $\mathbb R$ 或复数 $\mathbb C$。它既限制特征值可以取哪些数，也限制特征向量的坐标可以取哪些数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One rotation, two choices of scalars
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同一个旋转，换一个允许使用的数域*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The real matrix below rotates a plane vector through a quarter-turn. Its [[特征多项式|characteristic polynomial]] is obtained by taking the determinant of $tI_2-R$, where $t$ is a scalar variable and $I_2$ is the two-dimensional [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
下面的实矩阵把平面向量旋转四分之一圈。它的[[特征多项式]]由 $tI_2-R$ 的行列式给出，其中 $t$ 为标量变量，$I_2$ 为二阶[[单位矩阵]]。

$$R=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
p_R(t)=\det\begin{pmatrix}t&1\\-1&t\end{pmatrix}=t^2+1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every real $t$, $t^2+1>0$. There is no real root and therefore no real eigenvalue. Geometrically, a nonzero real arrow turned through a quarter-turn cannot become a real multiple of the original arrow. Zero is not an eigenvector, even though it always satisfies $R0=\lambda0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对每个实数 $t$，都有 $t^2+1>0$，所以没有实根，也就没有实特征值。从几何上看，一根非零实箭头旋转四分之一圈后，不可能成为原箭头的某个实数倍。零向量虽然总满足 $R0=\lambda0$，却不属于特征向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $\mathbb C$, the number $i$ satisfies $i^2=-1$, and the same polynomial factors as $(t-i)(t+i)$. Complex coordinates now provide actual eigenvectors:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
在复数域 $\mathbb C$ 中，$i$ 满足 $i^2=-1$，同一个多项式可以分解为 $(t-i)(t+i)$。允许复数坐标后，也确实能找到特征向量：

$$R\begin{pmatrix}1\\-i\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}i\\1\end{pmatrix}
=i\begin{pmatrix}1\\-i\end{pmatrix},\qquad
R\begin{pmatrix}1\\i\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}-i\\1\end{pmatrix}
=-i\begin{pmatrix}1\\i\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No entry of $R$ was changed. The polynomial's coefficients were not changed either. We enlarged the allowed scalars and now regard the same array as acting on complex coordinate vectors. These complex eigenvectors are not real arrows that somehow escaped the quarter-turn argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
矩阵 $R$ 的任何条目都没有改动，多项式的系数也没有改变。我们扩大了允许使用的标量集合，并把同一个数组看成作用于复坐标向量的矩阵。这些复特征向量不是绕过了四分之一圈论证的实箭头。

## What “n roots” requires
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*“有 n 个根”需要什么条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$, with $n\ge1$: $A$ has $n$ rows and columns, and its entries lie in the field $\mathbb F$. Its polynomial $p_A(t)=\det(tI_n-A)$ has degree $n$ and leading coefficient $1$. For a scalar $\lambda$ in that same field, $p_A(\lambda)=0$ exactly when $(A-\lambda I_n)v=0$ has a nonzero solution in $\mathbb F^n$. Thus the roots available in the field are exactly the eigenvalues available there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$，$n\ge1$，即 $A$ 有 $n$ 行、$n$ 列，条目属于数域 $\mathbb F$。它的多项式 $p_A(t)=\det(tI_n-A)$ 为 $n$ 次、首项系数为 $1$。对同一域中的标量 $\lambda$，$p_A(\lambda)=0$ 当且仅当 $(A-\lambda I_n)v=0$ 在 $\mathbb F^n$ 中有非零解。因此，这个域中可用的根，恰好就是这个域中可用的特征值。

> [!important] Splitting is the precise condition · 准确条件是完全分裂
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The polynomial **splits over $\mathbb F$** when it can be written
> $$p_A(t)=\prod_{j=1}^{n}(t-\lambda_j),\qquad \lambda_j\in\mathbb F.$$
> The product multiplies $n$ linear factors; the values $\lambda_j$ may repeat. Exactly under this condition, all $n$ roots can be counted in the chosen field, with [[代数重数与几何重数|algebraic multiplicity]]: a factor $(t-\lambda)^a$ counts that root $a$ times. Merely knowing that the degree is $n$ does not establish splitting.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 多项式**在 $\mathbb F$ 上分裂**，是指它能写成上面的 $n$ 个一次因子的乘积，且每个 $\lambda_j$ 都属于 $\mathbb F$；这些值允许重复。恰好在这一条件下，才能在所选域中按[[代数重数与几何重数|代数重数]]数全 $n$ 个根：因子 $(t-\lambda)^a$ 把这个根计 $a$ 次。只知道次数为 $n$，还没有证明它能完全分裂。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The fundamental theorem of algebra guarantees that every nonconstant complex polynomial splits over $\mathbb C$. Consequently every complex $n\times n$ matrix has $n$ eigenvalues counted with multiplicity. A real matrix can also be treated as a complex matrix for this count. It need not have $n$ real roots. For a particular matrix, the required condition is just that **its own polynomial splits**; the whole field need not have this property for every polynomial. For instance, $(t-1)(t-2)$ already splits over $\mathbb R$, although $t^2+1$ does not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
代数基本定理保证，每个非恒定复多项式都在 $\mathbb C$ 上分裂。因此每个复 $n\times n$ 矩阵按重数计都有 $n$ 个特征值；实矩阵也可以视为复矩阵来这样计数，但它未必有 $n$ 个实根。对某一个矩阵，只需**它自己的多项式完全分裂**，不要求整个数域对所有多项式都有这一性质。例如 $(t-1)(t-2)$ 已经在实数域中分裂，尽管 $t^2+1$ 没有。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Splitting also does not provide a [[基|basis]] of eigenvectors. A repeated root can have fewer independent eigenvector directions than its multiplicity. The additional requirement belongs to the [[可对角化判据|diagonalizability criterion]]; the splitting condition itself is also the field boundary for [[Jordan标准形的域条件|Jordan form]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
完全分裂也没有保证存在特征向量[[基]]。某个重根对应的线性无关特征方向数，可能少于它的重数。额外要求由[[可对角化判据]]说明；分裂本身也是 [[Jordan标准形的域条件|Jordan 形的数域条件]]。

> [!example] Why a real 3 × 3 matrix has a real eigenvalue · 实三阶矩阵为何至少有一个实特征值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Its characteristic polynomial is a real cubic with leading term $t^3$. For sufficiently large positive and negative $t$, its values have opposite signs. A polynomial is continuous, so the [[介值定理|intermediate value theorem]] supplies a real root between them. The two remaining roots, counted over $\mathbb C$ with multiplicity, may both be real or form a nonreal conjugate pair. The same sign argument works for every odd positive dimension.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 它的特征多项式是首项为 $t^3$ 的实三次多项式。当 $t$ 分别取绝对值充分大的正数与负数时，函数值异号。多项式连续，因此[[介值定理]]保证两者之间至少有一个实根。其余两个根在复数域中按重数计，可能都是实数，也可能是一对非实共轭根。同样的异号论证适用于每个正奇数维。
