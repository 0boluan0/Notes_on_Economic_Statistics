# 可分离一阶方程把 $y$ 的因子与 $x$ 的因子分到两侧并分别取原函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A separable first-order ODE moves the $y$ factor and $x$ factor to opposite sides and antidifferentiates each side*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子方法
> 对
> $$
> y'=f(x)g(y),
> $$
> 先在 $g(y)\ne0$ 的解支上写
> $$
> \frac{dy}{g(y)}=f(x)\,dx.
> $$
> 若 $H'(y)=1/g(y)$、$F'(x)=f(x)$，积分得到隐式关系
> $$
> H(y)=F(x)+C.
> $$
> 能方便且不丢分支时再解出 $y$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $y'=f(x)g(y)$ on a branch where $g(y)\ne0$, separate to $dy/g(y)=f(x)dx$. If $H'=1/g$ and $F'=f$, integration gives the implicit relation $H(y)=F(x)+C$. Solve explicitly for $y$ only when doing so is convenient and preserves the relevant branches.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $y'=-xy$，非零解支给
$$
\frac{dy}{y}=-x\,dx
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\ln|y|=-\frac{x^2}{2}+C
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
y=Ae^{-x^2/2}.
$$
求出后必须代回原方程；微分符号帮助识别反向链式结构，但严格验算仍靠求导。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y'=-xy$, separation gives $\ln|y|=-x^2/2+C$ and hence $y=Ae^{-x^2/2}$. The final family must be differentiated and substituted into the original equation. Differential notation organizes the reverse chain rule but does not replace verification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

隐式式本身可以是完整答案。强行取逆可能引入正负分支、缩小定义域或把不是全局函数的曲线误写成一条函数。分离前还须另查 $g(y)=0$，见 [[分离变量漏平衡解]]；由初值可达的区间见 [[微分方程最大区间]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An implicit relation may already be a complete solution. Forced inversion can create branches, shrink the domain, or misrepresent a curve that is not globally a function. Equilibria removed by division and the maximal interval selected by initial data require separate checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么分离后两边积分只写一个常数？
>
> **答案：** 两边各自的常数只通过差值出现，而两个任意常数的差仍是一个任意常数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why is only one constant needed after integrating the two separated sides?
>
> **Answer:** The constants from the two sides appear only through their difference, which is itself one arbitrary constant.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
