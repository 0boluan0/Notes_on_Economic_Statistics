# Separation of variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*分离变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Separation of variables** solves certain first-order [[常微分方程|ODEs]] by undoing a chain rule. In $y'=-xy$, the changing slope is the product of $-x$, depending only on the input, and $y$, depending only on the current state. Away from $y=0$, divide by $y$: the expression $y'/y$ is the derivative of $\ln|y|$, where $\ln$ denotes the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]]. That recognisable derivative is the reason the method works.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**分离变量**通过反向使用链式法则，求解某些一阶[[常微分方程]]。在 $y'=-xy$ 中，变化的斜率是两个因子的乘积：$-x$ 只依赖输入，$y$ 只依赖当前状态。在 $y\ne0$ 处除以 $y$ 后，$y'/y$ 正是 $\ln|y|$ 的导数，其中 $\ln$ 表示[[自然对数]]。这种可识别的导数结构，就是方法成立的原因。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Find the product structure before dividing
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先辨认乘积结构，再做除法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A first-order equation is **separable** when it can be written $y'=f(x)g(y)$. Here $f$ depends only on $x$ and $g$ only on $y$; a constant factor is allowed. We use continuous real $f$ on an open input interval $I$ and continuous real $g$ on an open state interval. First check any states $y_*$ with $g(y_*)=0$: the constant function $y(x)=y_*$ has both sides zero wherever the original equation is defined. These are the [[分离变量漏平衡解|equilibrium solutions that division would remove]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一阶方程能写成 $y'=f(x)g(y)$，就称为**可分离**：$f$ 只依赖 $x$，$g$ 只依赖 $y$，其中一个因子也可以是常数。这里使用输入开区间 $I$ 上连续的实函数 $f$，以及状态开区间上连续的实函数 $g$。首先检查 $g(y_*)=0$ 的状态：只要原方程有定义，常数函数 $y(x)=y_*$ 就使两边都为零。它们正是[[分离变量漏平衡解|会被除法删去的平衡解]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then work on an interval of a solution where its values remain in an open state interval $J$ on which $g$ is never zero. The familiar notation is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随后只在这样的解区间上计算：函数值始终位于某个状态开区间 $J$ 内，而且 $g$ 在 $J$ 上处处非零。通常把分离步骤写成

$$\frac{dy}{g(y)}=f(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This [[微分|differential notation]] organises a valid chain-rule calculation; it is not permission to divide by a quantity that might be zero. Choose [[原函数|antiderivatives]] $H$ and $F$ with $H'(u)=1/g(u)$ on $J$ and $F'(x)=f(x)$ on $I$. Along a differentiable solution, the [[链式法则|chain rule]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种[[微分]]记号整理了合法的链式法则计算，却不意味着可以除以一个可能为零的量。选取[[原函数]] $H,F$，使 $H'(u)=1/g(u)$ 在 $J$ 上成立，$F'(x)=f(x)$ 在 $I$ 上成立。沿可导解使用[[链式法则]]，得到

$$\frac d{dx}H(y(x))=\frac{y'(x)}{g(y(x))}=f(x)=F'(x).$$

> [!abstract] Integrate the rate identity · 对变化率恒等式求原函数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> By [[原函数相差常数|equal derivatives on one interval]], the separated relation is $H(y(x))=F(x)+C$, where $C$ is one real constant. Two independently written integration constants reduce to their difference, still one constant. Conversely, any differentiable branch satisfying this relation with values in $J$ satisfies the original ODE when differentiated.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 由[[原函数相差常数|同一区间上导数相同只差常数]]，得到分离后的关系 $H(y(x))=F(x)+C$，其中 $C$ 是一个实常数。若两边各写一个积分常数，它们也只通过差值出现，仍然合成一个常数。反过来，任何满足这个关系、且取值在 $J$ 中的可导分支，求导后都满足原微分方程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the sign and the zero solution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*保留符号，也保留零解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y'=-xy$, first record $y\equiv0$. On a nonzero branch, the calculation is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $y'=-xy$，先记下 $y\equiv0$。在非零分支上计算：

$$\frac{dy}{y}=-x\,dx\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\ln|y|=-\frac{x^2}{2}+C\quad\Longrightarrow\quad|y|=e^Ce^{-x^2/2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $e^C>0$, this last equation gives a positive or a negative branch, $y=Ae^{-x^2/2}$ with $A\ne0$. The already checked zero solution can then be added by allowing $A=0$; it did not come from the logarithm. Differentiating the resulting formula gives $y'=-xAe^{-x^2/2}=-xy$ for every real $A$. For an [[通解与初值|initial value]] $y(0)=3$, choose $A=3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $e^C>0$，最后的等式给出正、负两种分支，即 $y=Ae^{-x^2/2}$ 且 $A\ne0$。再允许 $A=0$，就把预先核对的零解补回；零解并不是从对数式推出来的。对结果求导，得到 $y'=-xAe^{-x^2/2}=-xy$，对每个实数 $A$ 都成立。如果[[通解与初值|初值]]为 $y(0)=3$，便取 $A=3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An implicit relation must still describe a branch
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*隐式关系仍然需要选择解支*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The relation $H(y)=F(x)+C$ need not be conveniently solvable for $y$. Keeping it implicit can be a complete answer if the solution branch and its interval are clear. On the chosen interval $J$, continuous nonzero $g$ has a fixed sign, so $H'=1/g$ has a fixed nonzero sign. Thus $H$ is one-to-one there. Its inverse may be used only for inputs $F(x)+C$ that lie in the range $H(J)$; one inverse cannot silently combine different state intervals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关系 $H(y)=F(x)+C$ 不一定方便解出 $y$。只要解支与区间明确，保留隐式形式也可以是完整答案。在选定的区间 $J$ 上，连续且非零的 $g$ 保持同号，所以 $H'=1/g$ 也保持非零的同一符号。因此 $H$ 在这里一一对应；只有当 $F(x)+C$ 落在值域 $H(J)$ 中时，才能使用相应反函数，不能把不同状态区间不加说明地合并。

> [!example] One circle, two function branches · 一个圆，两条函数分支
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $y'=-x/y$, the original equation excludes $y=0$. Separation gives $y\,dy=-x\,dx$, hence $x^2+y^2=C$. If $y(0)=1$, then $C=1$ and the selected solution is $y=\sqrt{1-x^2}$ on $(-1,1)$. The lower branch satisfies a different initial value, $y(0)=-1$. At $x=\pm1$, the height is zero and the original slope equation is undefined. The entire circle is not one function $y(x)$, and its geometric continuation does not extend this ODE solution through those points.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $y'=-x/y$，原方程排除了 $y=0$。分离得到 $y\,dy=-x\,dx$，因此 $x^2+y^2=C$。若初值为 $y(0)=1$，则 $C=1$，所选解是 $y=\sqrt{1-x^2}$，定义在 $(-1,1)$。下半支对应另一个初值 $y(0)=-1$。在 $x=\pm1$，高度变成零，原斜率方程无定义。整个圆不是 $y(x)$ 这个单一函数；几何曲线可以继续，并不等于这个微分方程的解能穿过这些点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use [[隐式求导|implicit differentiation]] to check a retained relation, and return to the original equation when deciding the [[微分方程最大区间|maximal interval]]. Division only accounts for branches where the divided factor is nonzero. Excluded states, possible joins when uniqueness fails, and restrictions introduced by inversion need their own checks; a successful separation calculation alone is not a completeness theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保留隐式关系时，用[[隐式求导]]检查；判断[[微分方程最大区间]]时，回到原方程。除法只覆盖被除因子非零的分支；被排除的状态、唯一性失效时可能的拼接，以及反解引入的范围限制，都需要另查。分离计算成功，本身不是“已经得到全部解”的定理。
