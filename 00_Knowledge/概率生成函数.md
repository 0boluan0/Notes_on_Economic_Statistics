# Probability generating function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*概率生成函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A probability generating function (PGF) stores the distribution of a count in a power series. The exponent says **which count occurred**, and its coefficient says **how likely that count is**.

For example, toss two fair coins independently and let $N$ count the heads. The equally likely outcomes TT, HT, TH and HH give $N=0,1,1,2$, so the probabilities are $1/4,1/2,1/4$. Their PGF is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
概率生成函数（PGF）把一个计数变量的分布写进幂级数：指数说明**发生了多少个**，系数说明**这个数量有多大概率**。

例如，独立抛两枚公平硬币，用 $N$ 记录正面数。等可能的结果 TT、HT、TH、HH 对应 $N=0,1,1,2$，所以概率依次为 $1/4,1/2,1/4$。它们的 PGF 为

$$G_N(s)=\tfrac14+\tfrac12s+\tfrac14s^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficient of $s$ is $P(N=1)=1/2$; the coefficient of $s^2$ is $P(N=2)=1/4$. The argument $s$ is an auxiliary input to the function, not the success probability of either coin.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$s$ 的系数是 $P(N=1)=1/2$，$s^2$ 的系数是 $P(N=2)=1/4$。函数的自变量 $s$ 是辅助输入，并不是硬币正面出现的概率。

> [!important] Encoding a nonnegative integer distribution / 编码非负整数分布
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $N$ be a random variable taking values in $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, and write $p_n=P(N=n)$ for its [[概率质量函数|probability mass function]]. Its PGF is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 设随机变量 $N$ 取值于 $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$，用 $p_n=P(N=n)$ 表示其[[概率质量函数]]。它的 PGF 是
>
> $$\boxed{G_N(s)=E[s^N]=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}p_ns^n},\qquad |s|\le1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[期望|expectation]] weights $s^n$ by its probability $p_n$. Thus this is the [[普通生成函数|ordinary generating function]] of the probability sequence. The coefficients are nonnegative and sum to one; an arbitrary OGF need not have either property. Here $N$ is a finite integer on each outcome, though its possible values need not have an upper bound.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 这个[[期望]]用概率 $p_n$ 对 $s^n$ 加权。因此，它就是概率数列的[[普通生成函数]]。其系数必须非负，且总和为 1；一般的 OGF 不必满足这两点。这里每个结果下的 $N$ 都是有限整数，但所有可能取值不必有统一上界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reading zero, one and the coefficients
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*读出零点、归一化与各项概率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $s=0$, only the constant term remains: $G_N(0)=p_0$. The term $s^0$ is the constant polynomial one, including at zero. At $s=1$, all powers are one, so $G_N(1)=\sum_n p_n=1$. At any degree $n$, coefficient extraction gives $[s^n]G_N(s)=p_n$, so the whole PGF determines the whole distribution.

Both nonnegativity and normalisation matter. For example, $1-s+s^2$ equals one at $s=1$, but cannot be a PGF because its proposed probability at count one is $-1$. In the coin example, by contrast, all three coefficients are valid probabilities and add to one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$s=0$ 时，只剩常数项，因此 $G_N(0)=p_0$。其中 $s^0$ 按常数多项式 1 解释，在零点也是 1。$s=1$ 时所有幂都为 1，因此 $G_N(1)=\sum_n p_n=1$。任意次数 $n$ 的系数都满足 $[s^n]G_N(s)=p_n$，所以整个 PGF 决定了整个分布。

非负性与归一化缺一不可。例如，$1-s+s^2$ 在 $s=1$ 时等于 1，却不能是 PGF，因为它把计数为一的概率写成了 $-1$。硬币例子中的三个系数则都非负，且相加等于 1。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Where the series is guaranteed to work
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*哪些输入一定可以代入*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every real or complex $s$ with $|s|\le1$, the series converges absolutely because
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对满足 $|s|\le1$ 的实数或复数 $s$，级数一定绝对收敛，因为

$$\sum_{n\ge0}|p_ns^n|=\sum_{n\ge0}p_n|s|^n\le\sum_{n\ge0}p_n=1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus a PGF exists on this region without assuming a finite mean or variance. For $s>1$, it may diverge; that input requires an additional tail condition. At $|s|<1$, [[幂级数逐项求导|termwise differentiation of power series]] is valid. The endpoint $s=1$ needs separate care when derivatives are used to recover moments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
因此，即使没有有限均值或方差，PGF 在这个范围内也存在。$s>1$ 时则可能发散，需要另查尾部条件。在 $|s|<1$ 内，可以使用[[幂级数逐项求导]]；但若想通过导数读出矩，端点 $s=1$ 必须单独处理。

> [!note]- Moments at the endpoint / 端点的矩条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Define $G_N'(1^-)=\lim_{s\uparrow1}G_N'(s)$ and similarly for the second derivative. Differentiating inside $0<s<1$ and then increasing $s$ to one gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 定义 $G_N'(1^-)=\lim_{s\uparrow1}G_N'(s)$，二阶导数类似。在 $0<s<1$ 内逐项求导，再让 $s$ 增加到 1，得到
>
> $$G_N'(1^-)=E[N],\qquad G_N''(1^-)=E[N(N-1)].$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These are limits of nonnegative sums and may be $+\infty$. The [[矩存在性的使用边界|moment conditions]] must therefore be checked. If $E[N^2]<\infty$, the variance can be computed as
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 这些是非负项之和的极限，可以等于 $+\infty$，因此要检查[[矩存在性的使用边界|矩存在条件]]。若 $E[N^2]<\infty$，则可以计算方差
>
> $$\operatorname{Var}(N)=G_N''(1^-)+G_N'(1^-)-\bigl(G_N'(1^-)\bigr)^2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the coin polynomial, the derivative limits are $1$ and $1/2$, giving mean $1$ and variance $1/2$. An infinite-mean example has $p_0=0$ and $p_n=1/[n(n+1)]$ for $n\ge1$: the probabilities sum to one because $p_n=1/n-1/(n+1)$, but $E[N]=\sum_{n\ge1}1/(n+1)=\infty$. Its PGF still satisfies $G_N(1)=1$; this does not make $G_N'(1^-)$ finite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 硬币多项式的一、二阶导数极限分别为 $1$、$1/2$，因此均值为 $1$，方差为 $1/2$。一个无限均值的例子是 $p_0=0$，且 $n\ge1$ 时 $p_n=1/[n(n+1)]$。由于 $p_n=1/n-1/(n+1)$，概率和为 1；但 $E[N]=\sum_{n\ge1}1/(n+1)=\infty$。它仍满足 $G_N(1)=1$，这却不能保证 $G_N'(1^-)$ 有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[矩母函数|moment-generating function]] uses $E[e^{tN}]$ instead. For this integer-valued $N$ it is related by $M_N(t)=G_N(e^t)$ wherever the expectation is finite. Positive $t$ asks for $s>1$, precisely the region where PGF finiteness is not automatic. The two names should therefore not be treated as interchangeable definitions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
[[矩母函数]]使用的是 $E[e^{tN}]$。对这个整数值 $N$，在期望有限的输入处有 $M_N(t)=G_N(e^t)$。正的 $t$ 对应 $s>1$，恰好进入 PGF 不自动保证有限的区域，因此这两个名称不能当成同一个定义互换。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [University of Cambridge, Probability, Chapter 6, pp. 6.1–6.3](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/teaching/0405/Probability/prob06.pdf): probability coefficients, values at zero and one, and finite-distribution moment calculations. The two-coin example is independently enumerated here.
- [Durham University, Probability II, §5.1, Definition 5.2, Remark 5.2.1 and Theorem 5.9](https://www.maths.dur.ac.uk/users/ostap.hryniv/teaching/Probability2/Probability2-notes.html#sec:gen-fns-def-and-props): nonnegative integer support, the unit-disk bound, uniqueness and factorial moments through left-hand derivative limits. The finite-second-moment condition is stated explicitly above before the variance formula.
- [Durrett, Probability: Theory and Examples, 5th ed., §4.3, printed p. 232](https://math.duke.edu/~rtd/PTE/PTE5_011119.pdf): direct verification that the PGF can be infinite for $s>1$, and that the mean is recovered by the derivative limit as $s\uparrow1$. The telescoping heavy-tail example is calculated directly above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 剑桥大学《Probability》第 6 章，6.1–6.3 页：核验概率系数、0 和 1 处的取值，以及有限分布的矩计算；两枚硬币例子另行枚举。
- 杜伦大学《Probability II》§5.1，定义 5.2、注 5.2.1、定理 5.9：核验非负整数支持、单位圆盘界、唯一性及左侧导数极限给出的阶乘矩；本卡在方差公式前明确有限二阶矩条件。
- Durrett 第五版 §4.3，印刷页 232：核验 $s>1$ 时可能无穷，以及 $s\uparrow1$ 的导数极限读出均值；望远镜求和的重尾例子在正文直接核算。
