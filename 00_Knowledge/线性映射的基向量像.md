# Linear maps from prescribed basis images
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*线性映射的基向量像*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To specify a [[线性映射|linear map]], we need not describe its output separately for every possible input. It is enough to prescribe the outputs of a [[基|basis]]: a list of vectors giving every input one unique coefficient representation. Any chosen basis outputs produce **one and only one** linear map on the whole domain. “Any” concerns the output vectors; the input list really must be a basis for this guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

规定一个[[线性映射]]，不需要逐个写出所有可能输入的输出。只要规定一组[[基]]的像就够了：基使每个输入都有唯一的系数表示。任意选定这些基向量的输出，都能得到整个定义域上**恰好一个**线性映射。“任意”指输出向量可以自由选择；这个保证仍要求输入组确实是一组基。

> [!example] Two images determine every plane input · 两个像确定所有平面输入
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The standard basis of $\mathbb R^2$ is $e_1=(1,0)$, $e_2=(0,1)$. Suppose we want a linear map $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ with $T(e_1)=(1,0)$ and $T(e_2)=(1,1)$. Since $(s,t)=se_1+te_2$, linearity forces
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\mathbb R^2$ 的标准基为 $e_1=(1,0)$、$e_2=(0,1)$。假如想要线性映射 $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ 满足 $T(e_1)=(1,0)$、$T(e_2)=(1,1)$，那么由 $(s,t)=se_1+te_2$，线性性迫使
>
> $$T(s,t)=s(1,0)+t(1,1)=(s+t,t).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In particular, $T(3e_1-2e_2)=(1,-2)$. This formula is not just a necessary candidate: adding or scaling coordinate pairs verifies that it is linear and has the two required images. The theorem below explains why this construction always works for a basis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 特别地，$T(3e_1-2e_2)=(1,-2)$。这条公式不只是一个必要的候选：对坐标对做加法或数乘，就能核对它确实线性，并具有所要求的两个像。下面的定理说明为什么对一组基总能这样构造。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Prescribing outputs on a finite basis
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*在有限基上指定输出*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $V,W$ be [[向量空间|vector spaces]] over the same field $\mathbb F$, such as $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. Let $\mathcal B=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ be an ordered finite basis of $V$, with positive integer $n$, and choose any $w_1,\ldots,w_n\in W$. The index $j$ pairs the input $v_j$ with its prescribed output $w_j$; the $w_j$ are not assumed to be a basis of $W$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $V,W$ 是同一域 $\mathbb F$ 上的[[向量空间]]，例如 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。设 $\mathcal B=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ 是 $V$ 的一组有序有限基，其中 $n$ 为正整数，并任意选择 $w_1,\ldots,w_n\in W$。下标 $j$ 把输入 $v_j$ 与指定输出 $w_j$ 配对；并不假设 $w_j$ 是 $W$ 的一组基。

> [!important] Existence and uniqueness · 存在且唯一
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> There exists exactly one linear map $T:V\to W$ satisfying $T(v_j)=w_j$ for every $j=1,\ldots,n$. For an input with basis coordinates $c_1,\ldots,c_n$, the rule is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 存在唯一线性映射 $T:V\to W$，使每个 $j=1,\ldots,n$ 都满足 $T(v_j)=w_j$。如果输入的基坐标是 $c_1,\ldots,c_n$，规则就是
>
> $$x=\sum_{j=1}^n c_jv_j
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
> T(x)=\sum_{j=1}^n c_jw_j.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These coordinates are the scalar coefficients of the whole basis vectors. They need not be the usual coordinates of $x$. No basis or finite-dimensionality assumption on $W$ is required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这些坐标是乘在各个完整基向量前面的标量系数，未必等于 $x$ 的通常坐标。不要求为 $W$ 选基，也不要求 $W$ 有限维。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the construction works
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么构造确实成立？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**First define a function.** Because the $v_j$ span $V$, every $x\in V$ has a basis expansion. Because they are linearly independent, its coefficient list is unique. Thus the displayed formula assigns exactly one output to each input. That output lies in $W$, since vector spaces are closed under linear combinations. This is the well-definedness step: no input is missed and no input receives conflicting answers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**先定义函数。** 因为 $v_j$ 张成 $V$，每个 $x\in V$ 都有基展开；因为它们线性无关，系数列表唯一。因此，上式给每个输入恰好一个输出。向量空间对线性组合封闭，所以这个输出属于 $W$。这一步保证定义没有歧义：既不漏掉输入，也不会给同一个输入两个互相冲突的答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Then verify linearity and the required images.** Write $x=\sum_j c_jv_j$ and $y=\sum_j d_jv_j$, with scalars $c_j,d_j\in\mathbb F$. For arbitrary $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F$, the coordinates of $\alpha x+\beta y$ are $\alpha c_j+\beta d_j$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**再核对线性性与指定的像。** 写 $x=\sum_j c_jv_j$、$y=\sum_j d_jv_j$，其中 $c_j,d_j\in\mathbb F$。对任意 $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F$，$\alpha x+\beta y$ 的基坐标是 $\alpha c_j+\beta d_j$，因此

$$\begin{aligned}
T(\alpha x+\beta y)
&=\sum_{j=1}^n(\alpha c_j+\beta d_j)w_j\\
&=\alpha\sum_{j=1}^n c_jw_j+\beta\sum_{j=1}^n d_jw_j\\
&=\alpha T(x)+\beta T(y).
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x=v_k$, the $k$th coordinate is $1$ and all other coordinates are $0$. The same formula therefore gives $T(v_k)=w_k$. We have now constructed a linear map with all required values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $x=v_k$，第 $k$ 个坐标为 $1$，其余坐标为 $0$，同一公式就给出 $T(v_k)=w_k$。至此，确实构造出了具有全部指定值的线性映射。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Finally prove uniqueness.** If another linear map $S:V\to W$ has the same basis images, then for every input $x=\sum_j c_jv_j$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**最后证明唯一性。** 若另一个线性映射 $S:V\to W$ 具有相同基像，那么对每个输入 $x=\sum_j c_jv_j$，

$$S(x)=\sum_{j=1}^n c_jS(v_j)
=\sum_{j=1}^n c_jw_j=T(x).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hence $S$ and $T$ agree everywhere. Coordinate uniqueness made the construction well-defined; linearity and spanning force any other candidate to agree with it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以 $S,T$ 在所有输入上相同。坐标唯一性保证构造无歧义；线性性与张成性则迫使其他候选与它完全一致。

## Images may be zero or dependent
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*像可以是零，也可以相关*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the nonstandard basis $v_1=(1,1)$, $v_2=(1,-1)$ of $\mathbb R^2$, and prescribe outputs in $\mathbb R$: $T(v_1)=2$, $T(v_2)=0$. To evaluate $T(s,t)$, first find its basis coordinates:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $\mathbb R^2$ 的非标准基 $v_1=(1,1)$、$v_2=(1,-1)$，并在陪域 $\mathbb R$ 中指定 $T(v_1)=2$、$T(v_2)=0$。要算 $T(s,t)$，先求它的基坐标：

$$(s,t)=\frac{s+t}{2}v_1+\frac{s-t}{2}v_2,
\qquad
T(s,t)=\frac{s+t}{2}\,2+\frac{s-t}{2}\,0=s+t.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One nonzero basis vector is sent to zero, and two independent input directions have outputs in a one-dimensional space. Neither fact obstructs linearity. Choosing every $w_j=0_W$ gives the zero map. For the zero domain $V=\{0_V\}$, the basis is empty and there is still exactly one linear map into $W$: it sends $0_V$ to $0_W$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个非零基向量被送到了零，而且两个独立输入方向的输出落在一维空间里；这两点都不妨碍线性性。把所有 $w_j$ 都选为 $0_W$，得到的就是零映射。对于零定义域 $V=\{0_V\}$，基为空组，进入 $W$ 的线性映射仍恰好一个：把 $0_V$ 送到 $0_W$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Polynomial differentiation follows the same construction. Let $P_2$ consist of real polynomials of degree at most two, including zero, with basis $(1,x,x^2)$; let $P_1$ consist of those of degree at most one. Prescribe
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多项式求导的例子也服从同一构造。令 $P_2$ 包含次数不超过二的实系数多项式及零多项式，基为 $(1,x,x^2)$；$P_1$ 包含次数不超过一的多项式。规定

$$D(1)=0,\qquad D(x)=1,\qquad D(x^2)=2x.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then the unique linear map $D:P_2\to P_1$ is $D(a+bx+cx^2)=b+2cx$. This is exactly [[导数|differentiation]]. Here $x$ is the polynomial variable, not a coefficient vector. We can later write the outputs in a chosen codomain basis to obtain [[表示矩阵的列|the columns of a representing matrix]], but the existence proof does not need that extra choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

唯一线性映射 $D:P_2\to P_1$ 就是 $D(a+bx+cx^2)=b+2cx$，恰好是求[[导数]]。这里 $x$ 是多项式变量，不是系数向量。以后可以再把输出写成选定陪域基下的坐标，形成[[表示矩阵的列]]，但存在性证明不需要这一步额外选择。

## What changes when the inputs are not a basis?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*输入组不是基时，哪里会改变？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For dependent inputs, prescribed outputs must [[相关向量像需保持关系|preserve every linear relation]]. For example, since the second input $2e_1$ is twice the first, any linear map must satisfy $T(2e_1)=2T(e_1)$. Prescribing $T(e_1)=1$ and $T(2e_1)=3$ in $\mathbb R$ is impossible. Dependence does not by itself forbid an extension; it imposes consistency conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果输入相关，指定像必须[[相关向量像需保持关系|保持每条线性关系]]。例如第二个输入 $2e_1$ 是第一个输入的两倍，任何线性映射都必须满足 $T(2e_1)=2T(e_1)$。若在 $\mathbb R$ 中指定 $T(e_1)=1$、$T(2e_1)=3$，就不可能实现。相关本身并不排除延拓，而是带来一致性条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For independent inputs that do not span the domain, the missing directions remain free when the codomain contains a nonzero vector; see [[非张成集像不定全映射|the non-spanning boundary]]. For instance, specifying only $T(e_1)=1$ for $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ permits both $T_0(s,t)=s$ and $T_1(s,t)=s+t$. They agree on $e_1$ and differ on $e_2$. If the codomain is $\{0\}$, however, only the zero map exists, so non-spanning alone does not universally imply nonuniqueness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果输入无关但没有张成定义域，那么在陪域含非零向量时，缺失方向仍可自由选择，见[[非张成集像不定全映射]]。例如对 $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$，只规定 $T(e_1)=1$，则 $T_0(s,t)=s$ 与 $T_1(s,t)=s+t$ 都符合要求：它们在 $e_1$ 上相同，在 $e_2$ 上不同。不过，若陪域只有 $\{0\}$，就只有零映射，因此不能脱离陪域条件断言“不张成就一定不唯一”。
