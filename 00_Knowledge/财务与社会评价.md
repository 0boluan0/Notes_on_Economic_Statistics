# 财务评价核对主体收支而社会评价核对评价范围内的福利与资源变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Financial appraisal tracks an entity's finances; social appraisal tracks welfare and resource changes across the evaluation scope.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财务评价问“谁在何时付款、收款，能否负担”；社会评价问“实施方案改变了哪些人的福利，占用了哪些有其他用途的资源”。同一项目需要回答两个问题：有社会价值并不保证预算可承受，能靠收费盈利也不保证已经计入全部社会损害。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financial appraisal asks who pays or receives funds and whether the proposal is affordable. Social appraisal asks whose welfare changes and what alternatively usable resources it absorbs. Social value does not establish affordability, and profitable charges do not account for every social harm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设政府向工人支付 80，工人为项目放弃的其他生产或闲暇价值为 60。若双方都在同一等权货币账户内：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose government pays workers 80 and their forgone production or leisure is worth 60. Include both sides in a common equally weighted monetary account:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\underbrace{-80}_{\text{政府收支}}
+\underbrace{(80-60)}_{\text{工人净得益}}
=\underbrace{-60}_{\text{资源机会成本}}.
$$

预算仍需准备 80；社会账户中的劳动成本是 60。不能一面扣支付额 80，一面再扣机会成本 60，却漏掉工人收到的 80。完整付款账户与直接资源账户在假设一致时应能对上；方法见 [[转移支付核算]]，资源估值见 [[社会机会成本]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The budget still requires 80, while the labour resource cost is 60. Deducting both without including workers' receipts is inconsistent. A complete payment ledger and a direct resource ledger should reconcile; see [[转移支付核算|transfer accounting]] and [[社会机会成本|social opportunity cost]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，免费公共服务的收入为零，不表示服务效益为零；自有土地没有新增付款，也不表示占地没有成本。评价时可并排列财务收支、真实资源与非现金福利影响，并逐项说明从一张表到另一张表如何调整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero revenue from a free service does not imply zero benefit; no new payment for owned land does not imply costless land use. Parallel financial and social ledgers make each adjustment visible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
