# 回测只提供风险模型验证的一类结果证据，不能替代概念、数据、实施、治理与持续监控

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Backtesting supplies one class of outcome evidence for risk-model validation; it cannot replace work on concepts, data, implementation, governance, and ongoing monitoring*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 回测回答“预测与后来结果是否相容”，不是“整个模型是否正确”
> 回测把模型预测同随后实现的结果比较，属于[[风险模型验证|风险模型验证]]中的结果分析。即使检验没有拒绝模型，它也只支持与该检验对象、样本、版本和用途相对应的一部分证据；它不能把完整验证压缩成一个例外数或一个 $p$ 值。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Backtesting compares model forecasts with subsequently realised outcomes and therefore belongs to outcomes analysis within [[风险模型验证|risk-model validation]]. Even when a test does not reject the model, it supports only the evidence corresponding to that test object, sample, version, and use; it cannot compress full validation into an exception count or a $p$-value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 回测能够提供什么证据

对于 VaR，[[Kupiec无条件覆盖]]检验例外频率是否与名义尾概率相容，[[Christoffersen独立性]]检验例外是否聚集，[[Christoffersen条件覆盖]]把两者联合。对于 ES，[[VaR-ES联合识别|VaR–ES 联合识别]]与[[VaR-ES联合评分|联合评分]]把 VaR 和 ES 作为一对来评价，而[[超越均值不足以验证ES|只检查例外日平均损失]]不足以完成一般动态验证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For VaR, [[Kupiec无条件覆盖|Kupiec unconditional coverage]] tests whether the exception frequency is compatible with the nominal tail probability, [[Christoffersen独立性|Christoffersen independence]] tests whether exceptions cluster, and [[Christoffersen条件覆盖|conditional coverage]] combines the two. For ES, [[VaR-ES联合识别|joint VaR–ES identification]] and [[VaR-ES联合评分|joint scoring]] evaluate VaR and ES as a pair, whereas [[超越均值不足以验证ES|checking only the mean loss on exception days]] does not complete general dynamic validation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些检验可以揭示某种预测—现实不一致，也可以在给定样本内提供有利证据。但“未拒绝”不等于证明模型为真：样本可能功效不足，多个错误可能相互抵消，检验也可能没有覆盖模型真正被使用的组合、期限或损益口径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These tests can reveal a particular forecast–reality mismatch and can provide favourable evidence within a given sample. Non-rejection does not prove that the model is true: the sample may have low power, several errors may offset one another, or the test may not cover the portfolio, horizon, or profit-and-loss definition for which the model is actually used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 回测不能替代什么

无论回测结果是否通过，都仍需另行验证：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether the backtest passes or fails, validation must still address:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 模型目的、理论结构、关键假设、遗漏风险因子与已知限制是否适合既定用途；
- 数据定义、代表性、质量、头寸映射、估值与生产实现是否正确；
- 开发、验证、审批、例外、整改与模型使用是否进入有效治理；
- 市场、产品、数据、组合或模型发生变化后，旧结论是否仍然适用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- whether the model purpose, theoretical structure, key assumptions, omitted risk factors, and known limitations suit the intended use;
- whether data definitions, representativeness, quality, position mapping, valuation, and production implementation are correct;
- whether development, validation, approval, exceptions, remediation, and model use enter effective governance; and
- whether an old conclusion remains applicable after changes in markets, products, data, portfolios, or the model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，回测失败说明预测与观察结果在某一检验维度上不相容，却通常不能单独定位根因究竟是数据、损益口径、动态过程、尾部模型、生产代码还是结构变化。反过来，回测通过也不证明这些环节已经正确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A backtest failure therefore indicates incompatibility between forecasts and observations along the tested dimension, but it usually cannot locate the root cause by itself—whether data, the P&L definition, dynamics, the tail model, production code, or structural change. Conversely, a pass does not establish that those components are correct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与监管资格检验的边界

[[FRTB 交易台资格]]中的 VaR 回测与损益归因检验决定交易台能否使用内部模型法，是特定监管机制。通过这些资格测试不等于概念、数据、实施、治理与持续监控已经完成；一般模型验证也不能替代监管规则明文要求的测试。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The VaR backtest and profit-and-loss attribution test in [[FRTB 交易台资格|FRTB trading-desk eligibility]] determine whether a desk may use the internal-models approach and form a specific regulatory mechanism. Passing those eligibility tests does not complete conceptual, data, implementation, governance, and monitoring work; general model validation likewise cannot replace tests expressly required by the regulatory rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

验证结论始终绑定具体模型版本、数据、用途、样本与日期。换版本、换组合或扩展用途后，不能把旧回测和旧验证结论原封不动沿用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A validation conclusion is always tied to a particular model version, data set, use, sample, and date. After a version change, portfolio change, or extension of use, old backtests and validation conclusions cannot simply be carried forward unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 最小自检
> 某 VaR 模型过去一年例外数与 99% 名义覆盖率一致，是否足以批准它用于一个新期权组合？
>
> **答案：** 不足。该结果只提供覆盖证据；还要验证期权非线性、数据与头寸映射、生产实现、测试功效、使用边界以及用途变更后的治理与持续监控。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** A VaR model's exception count matched 99% nominal coverage over the past year. Is that sufficient to approve it for a new option portfolio?
>
> **Answer:** No. The result supplies coverage evidence only. Validation must also address option nonlinearity, data and position mapping, production implementation, test power, use boundaries, and governance and ongoing monitoring after the change of use.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Federal Reserve、OCC 与 FDIC, [*Revised Guidance on Model Risk Management*（2026）](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/guidance/supervisory-guidance-on-model-risk-management.htm)：核验结果分析包括 backtesting，但验证还包括概念健全性与持续监控，治理与控制属于更广的模型风险管理框架。
- [Basel Framework, MAR30](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-framework/standard/mar/30/inforce/2023-01-01/published/2024-07-05)：核验市场风险内部模型的独立验证除回测与损益归因外，还覆盖概念、风险覆盖、假设、损益方法、头寸数据、估值、风险转换计算与持续重新验证。
- Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, [*Sound practices for backtesting counterparty credit risk models*](https://www.bis.org/publ/bcbs185.htm)：核验 backtesting 是模型验证的重要组成部分，但只是其中一个元素。
