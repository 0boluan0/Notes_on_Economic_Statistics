# 价格指数衡量价格变化而全收益指数还按规则计入分配收入
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A price index measures price changes, whereas a total-return index also includes distributions under its rules.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较投资表现之前，先确认是否把股息、利息等分配收入算进来。股票价格指数通常不把普通现金股息当作再投资收益；全收益指数把符合规则的分配计入回报，何时把现金再投入成分资产则由编制方法规定。全收益也不自动等于扣完费用和个人税后的实际收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before comparing performance, check whether distributions are included. A stock price index normally excludes the reinvestment of ordinary cash dividends; a total-return index includes eligible distributions in its return, while its methodology determines when cash is reinvested in constituents. Total return does not automatically mean an investor's return after all fees and personal taxes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一只股票除息前值 100，分红 5，忽略其他消息而除息后值 95。价格收益为 -5%；包括取得的 5 元股息，[[持有期收益率]]是 $(95+5-100)/100=0$。把前者当成投资者总财富损失，或拿含息基金收益与不含息指数比较，都会错配口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stock worth 100 pays a dividend of 5 and, with no other news, falls to 95. Its price return is -5%, but its [[持有期收益率|holding-period return]] including the dividend is 0. Treating the former as total wealth loss, or comparing a dividend-inclusive fund return with a price-only benchmark, mismatches conventions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $I_t^{P}$ 为已正确维护的价格指数、$d_t$ 为当期符合规则的“指数股息点数”，一个标准的逐期全收益更新式为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a properly maintained price index $I_t^P$ and eligible index-dividend points $d_t$, a standard one-period total-return update is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$I_t^{TR}=I_{t-1}^{TR}\frac{I_t^P+d_t}{I_{t-1}^P}.$$

上式采用股票指数的股息点数口径，不能直接替代所有债券指数的现金流公式。例如，FTSE WGBI 的月度全收益包括月内利息和本金现金流，却不假设这些现金流在该月内再投资。“计入收入”和“收到后立刻再投资”不是同一个条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formula uses an equity-index dividend-point convention, not a universal bond-index cash-flow formula. FTSE WGBI monthly total return, for example, includes intra-month interest and principal cash flows without reinvesting them within that month. Including income does not imply immediate reinvestment on receipt.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
