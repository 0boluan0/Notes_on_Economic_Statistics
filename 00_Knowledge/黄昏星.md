# 黄昏星是上涨后由长阳线高位小实体与回落阴线组成的三线形态

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An evening star is a three-candle formation after an advance: a long up candle, a higher small body and a declining down candle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

黄昏星是上涨后，或整理区域上端出现的三根[[K线]]组合：先有长阳线，再有向上与前实体分离的小实体星线，最后是一根收盘明显进入第一根阳线实体的阴线。星线实体可阴可阳；本课第三根的典型穿入程度超过第一实体的一半。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An evening star consists of three [[K线|candlesticks]] after an advance or near the top of a range: a long up candle, a small star body gapping above it, and a down candle closing well inside the first body. Either star-body direction is allowed. The course's typical third candle penetrates more than halfway into the first body.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第三根与星线之间再向下跳空是较强的课程示例，并非必要结构。星线出现时三线形态尚未完成；必须等第三根收盘才能确定其穿入位置。第二根的小实体本身不能确证顶部，完整黄昏星也不保证随后下跌。镜像的下降后组合见[[启明星]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A further gap down between the star and third candle is a stronger course example, not a required structure. The formation remains incomplete at the star and requires the third close to establish penetration. The small second body does not prove a top, and even the completed pattern does not guarantee a decline. Its counterpart after a decline is the [[启明星|morning star]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
