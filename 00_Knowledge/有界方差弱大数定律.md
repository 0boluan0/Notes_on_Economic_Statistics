# 两两独立、具有共同均值且方差一致有界时，样本均值依概率收敛到共同均值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For pairwise independent random variables with a common mean and uniformly bounded variances, the sample mean converges in probability to that mean*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 一组可直接核验的充分条件
> 设 $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ 两两独立，$E[X_i]=\mu$，且存在有限常数 $C$ 使 $\operatorname{Var}(X_i)\le C$。两两独立和有限二阶矩使不同项协方差为零，因此样本均值的方差至多为 $C/n$；Chebyshev 不等式随即给出依概率收敛。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ be pairwise independent, have common mean $\mu$, and satisfy $\operatorname{Var}(X_i)\le C<\infty$. Pairwise independence removes cross-covariances, so the variance of the sample mean is at most $C/n$; Chebyshev's inequality then gives convergence in probability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 定理与一行证明
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The theorem and its one-line proof*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $S_n=\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$、$\bar X_n=S_n/n$。在上述条件下，

$$
E[\bar X_n]=\mu,
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_n)
=\frac1{n^2}\sum_{i=1}^n\operatorname{Var}(X_i)
\le\frac Cn.
$$

所以对每个 $\varepsilon>0$，

$$
P(|\bar X_n-\mu|\ge\varepsilon)
\le\frac{\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_n)}{\varepsilon^2}
\le\frac{C}{n\varepsilon^2}
\longrightarrow0.
$$

因此 $\bar X_n\xrightarrow p\mu$；事实上同一计算还给出 [[均方收敛|$L^2$ 收敛]]。定理只需两两独立，不要求所有有限维联合分布完全独立，也不要求同分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sample mean has mean $\mu$ and variance at most $C/n$. Chebyshev therefore bounds every fixed-tolerance error probability by $C/(n\varepsilon^2)$. The same variance calculation also gives mean-square convergence. Mutual independence and identical distributions are not required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 适用边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Scope and boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 这是 [[弱大数定律]] 的一个清晰版本，不是所有弱大数定律的唯一假设集合；例如 i.i.d. 且仅绝对可积时还有更一般的结论。
- 它给出依概率收敛，不保证一条样本路径最终永久靠近 $\mu$；后者属于 [[强大数定律]]。
- 上界 $C/(n\varepsilon^2)$ 是通用保证，不必等于真实误差概率。
- 平均只能压低随机波动，不能修复抽样偏差、错误目标或共同系统冲击。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These assumptions provide one useful version of the weak law, not its only version. The conclusion is convergence in probability, the Chebyshev bound need not be sharp, and averaging cannot repair bias or a common systematic shock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $X_i=Y$ 对所有 $i$，其中 $P(Y=1)=P(Y=-1)=1/2$，则每项均值为 0、方差为 1，但它们不两两独立，而且 $\bar X_n=Y$ 始终不依概率收敛到 0。共同均值与有界方差本身不足以让共同冲击被平均掉。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If every $X_i$ equals the same Rademacher variable $Y$, all terms have mean zero and variance one, but their average remains $Y$. Common means and bounded variances do not remove a shared shock without the required dependence control.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 已知 $X_i$ 两两独立，$E[X_i]=3$，且 $\operatorname{Var}(X_i)\le4$。用本定理给出 $P(|\bar X_{400}-3|\ge0.5)$ 的上界，并说明这是不是精确概率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Given pairwise independent $X_i$ with mean $3$ and variance at most $4$, bound $P(|\bar X_{400}-3|\ge0.5)$ and say whether the bound is exact.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** Chebyshev 给 $4/(400\times0.5^2)=0.04$。这是通用上界，不是精确概率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Chebyshev gives $4/(400\times0.5^2)=0.04$. This is a general upper bound, not the exact probability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Rick Durrett, [*Probability: Theory and Examples*, 5th ed. (2019), Theorem 2.2.3, pp. 57–58](https://math.duke.edu/~rtd/PTE/PTE5_011119.pdf)：核对共同均值、方差一致有界时样本均值在 $L^2$ 与概率意义下收敛；两两独立是消去交叉协方差的直接充分条件。
- Patrick Billingsley, [*Probability and Measure*, 3rd ed. (1995), §22](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Probability+and+Measure%2C+3rd+Edition-p-9780471007104)：核对 Chebyshev 证明与更一般弱大数定律之间的边界。
