# Weak law under uniformly bounded variances
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有界方差弱大数定律*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why should averaging repeated measurements reduce noise? It does when the observations do not share too much of the same fluctuation. This theorem gives a concrete guarantee: if each measurement has the same expected value, their variances share one finite upper bound, and different measurements are uncorrelated, the variance of their average shrinks at least as fast as $1/n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么把多次测量取平均能降低噪声？关键在于这些观测没有共同承担过多相同的波动。这个定理给出具体保证：若各次测量具有共同的期望、方差由同一个有限常数控制，而且不同测量两两不相关，那么平均值的方差至少按 $1/n$ 的速度缩小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here [[期望|expectation]] $E[X_i]=\mu$ describes the common target; [[方差|variance]] measures the average squared distance from that target. “Uncorrelated” means [[协方差|covariance]] $\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)=0$ whenever $i\ne j$. [[两两独立|Pairwise independence]] is a sufficient way to obtain this zero covariance when the second moments are finite, but is stronger than the calculation needs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里[[期望]] $E[X_i]=\mu$ 描述共同目标；[[方差]]衡量离开这个目标的平均平方距离。“两两不相关”是指任意 $i\ne j$ 都有[[协方差]] $\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)=0$。有限二阶矩下，[[两两独立]]足以推出零协方差，但比下面计算实际需要的条件更强。

> [!important] One bound for the whole sequence · 一个上界控制整个序列
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ be real random variables on one probability space. Assume $E[X_i]=\mu$, $\operatorname{Var}(X_i)\le C<\infty$ for every $i$, and $\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)=0$ for all distinct pairs. Then, for the sample mean $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ 是同一概率空间上的实随机变量。每个 $i$ 都满足 $E[X_i]=\mu$、$\operatorname{Var}(X_i)\le C<\infty$，并且不同项的协方差为零。对样本均值 $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$，
>
> $$
> E[(\bar X_n-\mu)^2]\le\frac Cn,\qquad P(|\bar X_n-\mu|\ge\varepsilon)\le\frac{C}{n\varepsilon^2}\quad(\varepsilon>0).
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The same finite $C$ must work for every $i$; merely saying that each variance is finite is insufficient. The conclusions are [[均方收敛|mean-square convergence]] and hence a [[弱大数定律|weak law]]: $\bar X_n\to\mu$ in probability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 同一个有限的 $C$ 必须对每个 $i$ 都成立；只说每项方差分别有限还不够。结论是[[均方收敛]]，并进一步得到[[弱大数定律]]：样本均值依概率收敛到 $\mu$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From variance to a probability guarantee
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 定理与一行证明

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]] gives $E[\bar X_n]=\mu$, without any independence assumption. Next, [[和的方差协方差项|expanding the variance of a sum]] includes both individual variances and cross-covariances. The latter vanish by the stated assumption:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先由[[期望线性性]]得到 $E[\bar X_n]=\mu$，这一步不需要独立性。再[[和的方差协方差项|展开和的方差]]，其中既有各项方差，也有交叉协方差；后者由假设消失：

$$
\begin{aligned}\operatorname{Var}(\bar X_n)&=\frac1{n^2}\left[\sum_{i=1}^n\operatorname{Var}(X_i)+2\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)\right]\\&=\frac1{n^2}\sum_{i=1}^n\operatorname{Var}(X_i)\le\frac{nC}{n^2}=\frac Cn.\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because the mean is exactly $\mu$, this variance is also $E[(\bar X_n-\mu)^2]$. Applying [[Chebyshev不等式|Chebyshev’s inequality]] converts it into the displayed error-probability bound. For each fixed $\varepsilon>0$, that bound tends to zero. If $C=0$, every observation equals $\mu$ almost surely and the zero bound is valid too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于均值恰好是 $\mu$，这个方差也就是 $E[(\bar X_n-\mu)^2]$。使用 [[Chebyshev不等式]]即可转成上面的超差概率界。每个固定 $\varepsilon>0$ 下，右边都趋于零。若 $C=0$，各观测几乎必然等于 $\mu$，零上界同样成立。

> [!example] A numerical guarantee · 一个可以计算的保证
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose pairwise independent measurements have mean $3$ and variance at most $4$. With $400$ measurements and tolerance $0.5$, the bound is $4/(400\times0.5^2)=0.04$. Thus the average lies within $0.5$ of $3$ with probability at least $0.96$. A bound above $1$ would be uninformative; probabilities are always at most $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设测量两两独立、均值为 $3$、方差至多 $4$。取 $400$ 次测量，容忍值为 $0.5$，超差上界是 $4/(400\times0.5^2)=0.04$。因此平均值距离 $3$ 小于 $0.5$ 的概率至少为 $0.96$。若算出的上界超过 $1$，就没有提供有效信息；概率本来就至多为 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What this version does and does not require
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 适用边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identical distributions are unnecessary. For instance, independent observations $X_i=3+Z_i$ may use $Z_i=\pm1$ with equal probability at odd indices and $Z_i=\pm2$ with equal probability at even indices. Every mean is $3$, variances alternate between $1$ and $4$, and $C=4$ works. The theorem also applies to uncorrelated dependent observations; [[零协方差不推独立|zero covariance does not mean independence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不要求同分布。例如独立观测 $X_i=3+Z_i$ 可以在奇数位置让 $Z_i$ 等概率取 $\pm1$，偶数位置等概率取 $\pm2$。均值都是 $3$，方差在 $1$ 和 $4$ 间交替，所以统一取 $C=4$ 即可。定理也适用于不相关但有依赖的观测；[[零协方差不推独立|零协方差不等于独立]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof works more generally whenever $n^{-2}\sum_{i=1}^n\operatorname{Var}(X_i)\to0$ and the cross-covariances vanish. A uniform bound is an easy sufficient test for this. With unequal means $\mu_i$, the same bound instead centers the average at $n^{-1}\sum_i\mu_i$; convergence to a fixed $\mu$ additionally requires those mean averages to approach $\mu$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般地，只要交叉协方差为零，且 $n^{-2}\sum_{i=1}^n\operatorname{Var}(X_i)\to0$，同一证明就成立；一致有界只是方便的充分检验。若各项均值为不同的 $\mu_i$，同一上界控制的是围绕 $n^{-1}\sum_i\mu_i$ 的误差；若要收敛到固定 $\mu$，还需这些均值的平均趋于 $\mu$。

> [!warning] Repeated copies do not average away a shared shock · 重复同一次冲击不会消除它
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let one random variable $Y$ take $-1$ and $1$ equally likely, and set every $X_i=Y$. Every mean is zero and variance one, but $\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)=1$ and $\bar X_n=Y$. For tolerance $1/2$, the error probability stays one. The missing covariance assumption is exactly what prevents the $1/n$ variance reduction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令同一个随机变量 $Y$ 等概率取 $-1$ 和 $1$，每个 $X_i$ 都等于它。各项均值为零、方差为一，但 $\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)=1$，且 $\bar X_n=Y$。容忍值取 $1/2$ 时，超差概率始终为一。恰恰是协方差条件不成立，阻止了方差按 $1/n$ 缩小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This Chebyshev argument establishes mean-square and probability convergence, not by itself the pathwise conclusion of a [[强大数定律|strong law]]. It is also only one weak-law version: iid absolute integrability can suffice even when variance is infinite. Finally, reducing variation around a mean cannot correct selection bias or a wrongly chosen target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个 Chebyshev 论证得到均方与依概率收敛，本身没有证明[[强大数定律]]的逐路径结论。它也只是弱大数定律的一种版本：独立同分布且绝对可积时，即便方差无穷仍可成立。最后，压低均值周围的波动不能修复选择偏差或目标选错。
