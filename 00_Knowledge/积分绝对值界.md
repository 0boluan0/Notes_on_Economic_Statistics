# The integral triangle inequality
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*积分绝对值界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Accumulating positive and negative contributions can cause cancellation. If two parts contribute $3$ and $-2$, the net contribution has magnitude $|3-2|=1$, while their separate magnitudes add to $3+2=5$. The **integral triangle inequality** says that the same comparison holds for a [[定积分|definite integral]]: the magnitude of the net total cannot exceed the total accumulated magnitude.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正负贡献在累积时可能抵消。例如两部分分别贡献 $3$ 与 $-2$，净贡献的大小是 $|3-2|=1$，但各自大小相加为 $3+2=5$。**积分三角不等式**说明[[定积分]]也有同样的比较：净总量的大小不会超过把各部分大小累积起来的结果。

> [!important] The absolute-value bound · 绝对值界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $f$ be real-valued and Riemann integrable on a finite interval $[a,b]$ with $a<b$. Then the function $|f|$, whose value at $x$ is $|f(x)|$, is also Riemann integrable, and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设实值函数 $f$ 在有限区间 $[a,b]$ 上黎曼可积，且 $a<b$。把每个函数值取绝对值得到 $|f|$，即在 $x$ 处的值是 $|f(x)|$；它也黎曼可积，并且
>
> $$\left|\int_a^bf(x)\,dx\right|\le\int_a^b|f(x)|\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the left, integrate first and then take one absolute value. On the right, take the absolute value at every input before accumulating. In the [[净变化与总变化|net-change versus total-change]] interpretation, a continuous velocity gives displacement on the left before taking its magnitude, and distance travelled on the right.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左边先积分，最后只取一次绝对值；右边先把每个输入处的函数值变成非负数，再累积。在[[净变化与总变化]]的解释中，连续速度的积分给位移，左边取其大小；右边则给走过的路程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the absolute value is still integrable
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么取绝对值后仍然可积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Integrability of $|f|$ is part of the theorem, so it needs justification before its integral is used. Split $[a,b]$ into finitely many pieces $[x_{i-1},x_i]$ with positive widths $\Delta x_i=x_i-x_{i-1}$. On each piece define its oscillation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$|f|$ 的可积性本身就是定理的一部分，不能先默认其积分存在。把 $[a,b]$ 分成有限个小段 $[x_{i-1},x_i]$，正的段宽为 $\Delta x_i=x_i-x_{i-1}$。定义每段的振幅为

$$\omega_i(f)=\sup\{|f(x)-f(y)|:x,y\in[x_{i-1},x_i]\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[上确界|supremum]] is the least upper bound: $\omega_i(f)$ measures the largest spread of heights on that piece, even if no pair attains it. Because a Riemann-integrable function is bounded, each spread is finite. Upper rectangles use the supremum of $f$ on each piece as their height; lower rectangles use its infimum, the greatest lower bound. The sum $\sum_i\omega_i(f)\Delta x_i$ is the gap between these upper and lower rectangle sums. A bounded function is Riemann integrable exactly when such gaps can be made arbitrarily small by a suitable partition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[上确界]]就是最小的上界：$\omega_i(f)$ 衡量这一段内函数高度最大的差距，即使没有某一对点恰好取到它。黎曼可积函数有界，所以各段的差距有限。上矩形以每段上 $f$ 的上确界为高度；下矩形以下确界（最大的下界）为高度。和式 $\sum_i\omega_i(f)\Delta x_i$ 就是这两个矩形和之差。有界函数黎曼可积，当且仅当可以选择适当分割，让这个差任意小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This criterion expresses that the upper and lower estimates agree on one number. When their limiting values agree, choose upper and lower sums close to that number and combine their partition points: refinement cannot widen the gap. Conversely, an arbitrarily small gap traps the upper and lower limiting values together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个判据表达的是：上下估计最终确定同一个数。若上下极限相同，可分别选靠近该数的上和、下和，再合并它们的分割点；细分不会把差距扩大。反过来，若差距能任意小，上下极限就被夹成相同的值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any two real numbers $r,s$, the elementary triangle inequality implies $\bigl||r|-|s|\bigr|\le|r-s|$. Apply it with $r=f(x)$ and $s=f(y)$. Taking absolute values cannot enlarge a piece's spread, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意实数 $r,s$，普通三角不等式给出 $\bigl||r|-|s|\bigr|\le|r-s|$。代入 $r=f(x),s=f(y)$，可见取绝对值不会增大一段内的差距，因此

$$\omega_i(|f|)\le\omega_i(f),\qquad
\sum_i\omega_i(|f|)\Delta x_i\le\sum_i\omega_i(f)\Delta x_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The function $|f|$ is bounded as well. Every partition making the gap small for $f$ also makes it small for $|f|$, which proves its integrability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$|f|$ 同样有界。使 $f$ 的上下和之差很小的分割，也使 $|f|$ 的差很小，因此 $|f|$ 可积。

## Now integrate a pointwise bound
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*再把逐点不等式积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At every input, $-|f(x)|\le f(x)\le|f(x)|$. All three functions are now known to be integrable. [[积分保序性|Order preservation]] and [[定积分线性性|linearity]] for the factor $-1$ give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个输入处都有 $-|f(x)|\le f(x)\le|f(x)|$，现在三个函数都已知可积。由[[积分保序性]]以及系数为 $-1$ 时的[[定积分线性性]]，

$$-\int_a^b|f(x)|\,dx\le\int_a^bf(x)\,dx\le\int_a^b|f(x)|\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The outer quantity $A=\int_a^b|f(x)|\,dx$ is nonnegative. Saying $-A\le I\le A$, where $I=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$, is exactly saying $|I|\le A$. This proves the inequality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

外侧的量 $A=\int_a^b|f(x)|\,dx$ 非负。令 $I=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$，说 $-A\le I\le A$ 就等于说 $|I|\le A$，定理得证。

> [!example] A full sine wave · 一整个正弦周期
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $[0,\pi]$, $\sin x\ge0$ and its integral is $2$. On $[\pi,2\pi]$, $\sin x\le0$ and its integral is $-2$. Splitting at the sign change gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[0,\pi]$ 上，$\sin x\ge0$，积分为 $2$；在 $[\pi,2\pi]$ 上，$\sin x\le0$，积分为 $-2$。在变号点分段，得到
>
> $$\int_0^{2\pi}\sin x\,dx=2-2=0,\qquad
> \int_0^{2\pi}|\sin x|\,dx=2+2=4.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus moving an absolute value across an integral sign generally changes the answer; it produces a bound, not an identity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 所以把绝对值移过积分号通常会改变答案；这里得到的是界，而不是恒等式。

## Orientation and error estimates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*积分方向与误差估计*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For either order of finite endpoints, assume $f$ is integrable on the interval between them. Write $L=\min\{a,b\}$ and $R=\max\{a,b\}$ for the smaller and larger endpoint. The orientation-independent statement is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不论有限端点谁大谁小，都假设 $f$ 在两端点之间的区间可积。记较小端点为 $L=\min\{a,b\}$、较大端点为 $R=\max\{a,b\}$。不受方向影响的写法是

$$\left|\int_a^bf(x)\,dx\right|\le\int_L^R|f(x)|\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a=b$, both sides are zero. For reversed bounds the right side must still be accumulated from smaller to larger: with $f=1$, $|\int_1^0 1\,dx|=1$, whereas $\int_1^0|1|\,dx=-1$ cannot be an upper bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$a=b$ 时两边都为零。反向端点时，右边仍须从小端点积到大端点：取 $f=1$，有 $|\int_1^0 1\,dx|=1$，而 $\int_1^0|1|\,dx=-1$ 不可能充当上界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful application controls approximation error. Suppose $f,g$ are Riemann integrable on $[a,b]$ with $a<b$, and $|f(x)-g(x)|\le\varepsilon$ everywhere, where $\varepsilon\ge0$ is a fixed error bound. Linearity, this theorem and [[积分上下界估计|constant integral bounds]] yield
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个实用用途是控制近似误差。设 $f,g$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上黎曼可积，$a<b$，并且处处 $|f(x)-g(x)|\le\varepsilon$，其中 $\varepsilon\ge0$ 是固定误差界。由线性性、本定理及[[积分上下界估计|常数积分界]]，

$$\left|\int_a^bf-\int_a^bg\right|
=\left|\int_a^b(f-g)\right|
\le\int_a^b|f-g|\le\varepsilon(b-a).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus a height error of at most $0.01$ throughout an interval of width $5$ causes an integral error of at most $0.05$. The bound must hold throughout the interval, not just at the points inspected numerically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，若整个宽度为 $5$ 的区间内，高度误差至多 $0.01$，积分误差就至多 $0.05$。这个误差界必须在整段成立，不能仅在数值检查的那些点成立。
