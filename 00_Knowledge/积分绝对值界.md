# 积分的绝对值不超过绝对值的积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The absolute value of an integral is at most the integral of the absolute value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 在 $[a,b]$ 黎曼可积且 $a<b$，则 $|f|$ 也可积，且
$$\left|\int_a^b f(x)\,dx\right|\le\int_a^b|f(x)|\,dx.$$
先相加再取绝对值，正负项可能抵消；逐项取绝对值则不会。这也是[[净变化与总变化]]中“路程至少等于位移大小”的数学原因。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a Riemann-integrable function with increasing bounds, its absolute value is integrable and bounds the magnitude of its integral. Taking the absolute value after accumulation allows cancellation; taking it first removes cancellation. This explains why distance is at least the magnitude of displacement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明分两步。由 $||u|-|v||\le|u-v|$，取绝对值不会增大每段的振幅，所以上下和判据保证 $|f|$ 可积。再由 $-|f|\le f\le|f|$ 及[[积分保序性]]得到
$$-\int_a^b|f|\le\int_a^b f\le\int_a^b|f|.$$
例如 $\int_0^{2\pi}\sin x\,dx=0$，而 $\int_0^{2\pi}|\sin x|\,dx=4$；不能把绝对值随意移过积分号当作等号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Absolute value cannot increase local oscillation, so the upper–lower sum criterion proves integrability of the absolute value. Integrating its positive and negative bounds then proves the inequality. The sine integral over a full period is zero, while its absolute-value integral is four, so exchanging the absolute value and integral is generally not an equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
