# 金融互换是双方按约定规则在未来交换一组现金流的合约
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A financial swap is an agreement to exchange a series of future cash flows under specified rules*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

互换（swap）把双方的支付规则分别写成两条“腿”。最小的理解方式是列出每个支付日：我付什么、收什么、基于多大的[[衍生品名义本金|计算本金]]、用哪个利率或其他变量。签下一份互换，并不一定意味着今天借到了名义本金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A swap has two payment legs. At each payment date, identify what each party pays and receives, the [[衍生品名义本金|notional amount]], and the applicable rate or variable. Entering the swap does not necessarily deliver that notional as a loan today.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以同币种、普通固定对浮动利率互换为例：我付固定年利率 $k$、收本期适用浮动年利率 $L_i$，两腿使用相同本金 $N$、年计息比例 $\alpha_i$ 和支付日，并可净额支付，则本期我的净收入为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a plain same-currency fixed-for-floating swap, suppose I pay fixed rate $k$ and receive the applicable floating rate $L_i$. With matching notional $N$, accrual fraction $\alpha_i$, payment dates, and contractual net settlement, my net receipt is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$N\alpha_i(L_i-k).$$

若 $N=1{,}000{,}000$，$\alpha_i=0.5$，$k=4\%$，本期浮动利率为 5%，则收浮动 25,000、付固定 20,000，净收 5,000。这个简单同币种互换通常不交换本金。货币互换可能交换不同币种本金，不能照搬这个安排；隔夜复利等浮动腿也必须先按合同算出该期金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $N=1{,}000{,}000$, $\alpha_i=0.5$, $k=4\%$, and a floating rate of 5%, receipts of 25,000 less payments of 20,000 give 5,000 net. Plain same-currency swaps normally do not exchange principal; currency swaps may. Overnight-compounded floating legs require their own contractual accrual calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金流要跨多个未来日期履行，所以当前价值为零不代表以后没有[[对手方敞口指标|对手方风险]]。信用违约互换则把一条腿与约定信用事件相连，其支付要另看[[CDS信用事件与结算]]，不能理解为把资产池分成优先、夹层和劣后三层。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Future performance creates [[对手方敞口指标|counterparty exposure]] even if initial value is zero. A credit default swap links a leg to specified [[CDS信用事件与结算|credit events and settlement terms]]; this is distinct from dividing an asset pool into tranches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
