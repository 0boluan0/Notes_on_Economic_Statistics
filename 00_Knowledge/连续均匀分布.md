# 连续均匀分布在给定区间内为等长区间分配相等概率

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A continuous uniform distribution assigns equal probability to equal lengths in its interval*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$X\sim U[a,b]$（$a<b$）的密度在 $(a,b)$ 内为 $1/(b-a)$，外面为 0；端点处的密度取值不影响概率。其 CDF 在区间内为 $(x-a)/(b-a)$，左侧为 0，右侧为 1。区间内部任一段的概率是长度除以 $b-a$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For U[a,b] with a<b, density is the reciprocal of the interval length inside the interval and zero outside. The CDF grows linearly from zero to one. Endpoint density values do not affect probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如 $U[2,6]$ 对区间 $(3,5]$ 分配概率 $(5-3)/(6-2)=1/2$。均匀不表示每个实数点有相同的正概率；每个单点概率都是 0。矩的计算见 [[均匀分布的矩]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A uniform law on [2,6] assigns probability one half to (3,5]. Uniformity does not mean positive probability at every individual point. See [[均匀分布的矩|uniform moments]] for its mean and variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
