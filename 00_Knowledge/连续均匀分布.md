# Continuous uniform distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*连续均匀分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **continuous uniform distribution** spreads probability evenly across a finite interval. “Evenly” means that intervals of the same length, contained within that range, have the same probability. A segment twice as long receives twice as much probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**连续均匀分布**把概率均匀地铺在一个有限区间上。“均匀”是指：在这个范围内部，长度相同的区间得到相同概率；一段区间的长度变成两倍，它的概率也变成两倍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, suppose a completion time $X$ is modelled as uniform between $2$ and $6$ minutes. The intervals from $2$ to $3$ and from $5$ to $6$ each occupy one quarter of the total four-minute range, so each has probability $1/4$. The interval from $3$ to $5$ has probability $1/2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，假设用 $2$ 到 $6$ 分钟上的均匀分布来建模完成时间 $X$。从 $2$ 到 $3$、从 $5$ 到 $6$ 都占整个四分钟范围的四分之一，所以概率各为 $1/4$；从 $3$ 到 $5$ 占一半，概率就是 $1/2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing only that a time lies between $2$ and $6$ does not establish uniformity. Equal probability per unit of length is an additional modelling assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只知道时间在 $2$ 到 $6$ 之间，并不能推出均匀分布；“每单位长度分配相同概率”是一条额外的建模假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A flat density with total area one
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*密度是总面积为一的矩形*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a$ and $b$ be the lower and upper endpoints, with $a<b$. We write $X\sim U[a,b]$, read “$X$ is uniform on $[a,b]$”. A [[概率密度函数|probability density]] encodes probability as area under a curve. If the uniform density has constant height $h$, its total area is height times width, $h(b-a)$. Requiring total probability one gives $h=1/(b-a)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a,b$ 分别为区间下端点与上端点，且 $a<b$。记作 $X\sim U[a,b]$，读作“$X$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上均匀分布”。[[概率密度函数]]用曲线下的面积表示概率。如果均匀密度的固定高度是 $h$，总面积便是“高乘宽” $h(b-a)$；总概率必须为一，因此 $h=1/(b-a)$。

> [!abstract] Density and interval probability · 密度与区间概率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a numerical location $x$, choose the density
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对数值位置 $x$，可以选择如下密度：
>
> $$f_X(x)=\begin{cases}
> \dfrac{1}{b-a},&a<x<b,\\
> 0,&\text{otherwise}.
> \end{cases}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For endpoints $c,d$ inside the range, with $a\le c<d\le b$, the probability of the segment is its length divided by the total length:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对范围内的端点 $c,d$，若 $a\le c<d\le b$，则这段的概率为“该段长度除以总长度”：
>
> $$P(c<X\le d)=\frac{d-c}{b-a}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $U[2,6]$, the density is $1/4$ per minute. The event $3<X\le5$ has width two minutes, so its probability is $(1/4)\times2=1/2$. If a requested interval extends beyond the range, count only its overlap with $[a,b]$: for example, $P(1<X\le3)=1/4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于 $U[2,6]$，密度为每分钟 $1/4$。事件 $3<X\le5$ 的宽度为两分钟，所以概率为 $(1/4)\times2=1/2$。如果要计算的区间超出了取值范围，只计算它与 $[a,b]$ 重叠的部分；例如，$P(1<X\le3)=1/4$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The CDF measures how much length lies to the left
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*CDF 记录左边已经累积了多少长度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[累积分布函数|cumulative distribution function]] is $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$, the probability accumulated up to a threshold $x$. Below $a$ there is no probability to accumulate. Inside the range, the accumulated length is $x-a$. At or above $b$, all probability has been included. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[累积分布函数]] $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$ 表示累积到阈值 $x$ 为止的概率。小于 $a$ 时还没有累积到概率；在区间内部，已累积的长度是 $x-a$；达到或超过 $b$ 时，全部概率都已包括。因此

$$F_X(x)=\begin{cases}
0,&x<a,\\
\dfrac{x-a}{b-a},&a\le x\le b,\\
1,&x>b.
\end{cases}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $U[2,6]$, $F_X(3)=(3-2)/4=1/4$ and $F_X(5)=3/4$. Subtracting these cumulative probabilities recovers $P(3<X\le5)=1/2$. The CDF rises along a straight line, even though the density is flat: they record different quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于 $U[2,6]$，$F_X(3)=(3-2)/4=1/4$，而 $F_X(5)=3/4$。把两项累计概率相减，就重新得到 $P(3<X\le5)=1/2$。CDF 沿直线上升，密度却保持水平，因为二者记录的量不同。

> [!warning] A single point has no interval length · 单点没有区间长度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every exact point has probability zero. Uniformity therefore does not mean assigning a positive equal probability to each real number. Including or excluding endpoints leaves interval probabilities unchanged; changing density values only at $a$ and $b$ also leaves this distribution unchanged. The requirement $a<b$ matters: if the interval collapses to a point, the variable is constant and does not have this uniform density.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个精确单点的概率都为零，因此“均匀”并不是给每个实数点分配相同的正概率。区间是否包括端点，不会改变概率；只改变 $a,b$ 两处的密度取值，也不会改变这个分布。$a<b$ 的要求不可省略：区间若缩成一个点，变量就是常数，不再具有这里的均匀密度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The location and width of the interval also determine its mean and variance; their formulas and derivations are in [[均匀分布的矩|moments of a uniform distribution]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区间的位置与宽度还决定了均值和方差，对应公式与推导见[[均匀分布的矩]]。
