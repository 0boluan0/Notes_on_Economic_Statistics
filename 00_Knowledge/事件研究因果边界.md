# 预期泄漏与并发信息限制事件异常收益的因果解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Anticipation, leakage, and concurrent information limit causal interpretation of event abnormal returns*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 显著 [[异常收益|AR]]/[[累计异常收益|CAR]] 说明目标窗口内收益偏离了指定正常收益模型；要把它解释为目标事件的因果价格效果，还需要一个能把目标事件增量信息与其他价格冲击隔离开的可信设计。对常规的单次公告短窗，这通常要求时点足够清晰、公告含有未被预期的新信息，并排除或明确处理任何会影响收益的重大并发消息。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Significant AR or CAR shows that returns in the target window departed from the stated normal-return model. A causal interpretation requires a credible design that isolates the target event's incremental information from other price shocks. In a conventional short-window study of a single announcement, this usually means sufficiently precise timing, genuinely unanticipated information, and the exclusion or explicit handling of any material concurrent news that affects returns.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若收购、监管或政策变化经过数月讨论，市场会随事件概率变化逐步定价；公告日的小 AR 不证明事件没有价值。反过来，公告前 CAR 可能是泄漏，也可能是一般风险变化、谣言、选择性披露或别的公司消息；图形本身不能区分机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an acquisition, regulation, or policy change is debated for months, prices can adjust gradually as its probability evolves; a small announcement-day AR does not prove that the event lacked value. Conversely, pre-event CAR may reflect leakage, but also general risk changes, rumours, selective disclosure, or other firm news. The plot alone cannot distinguish these mechanisms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“清洁窗口”应来自预先规定的并发事件筛查，而不是看到不理想回报后删掉样本。若无法隔离消息，应把结论降为“该窗口的模型相对价格反应”，并明确不能把全部 CAR 归因于目标事件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A “clean window” should follow a prespecified screen for confounding events rather than post hoc deletion of inconvenient observations. When news cannot be isolated, the conclusion should be limited to a model-relative price reaction in that window, without attributing the entire CAR to the target event.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

清洁短窗不是唯一的识别办法。若研究利用可信对照组、事件暴露的横截面差异，或对共同冲击作出预先说明的建模，并能支持相应识别假设，并发冲击不必被机械地视为样本失效；结论必须与实际设计能够隔离的增量效应一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A clean short window is not the only identification strategy. A study may instead use a credible control group, cross-sectional differences in event exposure, or a prespecified model of common shocks. Concurrent shocks need not mechanically invalidate such a design, but the causal claim must match the incremental effect that the design can isolate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 公告日 CAR 显著为正，是否足以写“公告使公司价值提高 5%”？
>
> **答案：** 不足。还要给出能隔离公告增量信息的可信设计，并说明正常收益模型和统计推断；在最简单的单次公告短窗里，这通常还要求事件日清晰、公告含有未预期信息，且重大并发消息已被排除或处理。否则只能报告窗口内模型相对反应。
