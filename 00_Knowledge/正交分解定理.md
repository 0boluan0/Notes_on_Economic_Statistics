# Orthogonal decomposition theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正交分解定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An orthogonal decomposition splits a vector into two parts: one lies in a chosen subspace, and the remainder is perpendicular to every direction in that subspace. The theorem guarantees that this split exists and is unique under the conditions below. It is the geometric reason a projection and its residual can be treated as separate components.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正交分解把一个向量拆成两部分：一部分位于选定的子空间中，剩余部分与该子空间的每个方向垂直。下面的条件保证这种拆分存在，而且只有一种。这正是把投影和残差视为两个可以分别处理的几何分量的依据。

> [!example] Along a line and away from it · 沿着直线与偏离直线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In standard $\mathbb R^3$, take the column vector $b=(3,1,4)^T$ and the line $S=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,0)^T\}$. The superscript $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Here [[张成|span]] means all scalar multiples, so a candidate component in $S$ is $p=(t,t,0)^T$ for real $t$. Its remainder is $e=b-p=(3-t,1-t,4)^T$. To make $e$ [[正交|orthogonal]] to the line, set its dot product with the generating vector to zero:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在标准 $\mathbb R^3$ 中，取 $b=(3,1,4)^T$，直线 $S=\operatorname{span}\{(1,1,0)^T\}$。上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。这里[[张成]]包含该向量的全部标量倍数，所以 $S$ 中的候选分量为 $p=(t,t,0)^T$，$t$ 为实数。剩余部分为 $e=b-p=(3-t,1-t,4)^T$。要让 $e$ 与直线[[正交]]，令它与生成向量的点积为零：
>
> $$(3-t)+(1-t)=0,\qquad t=2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Hence $p=(2,2,0)^T$ and $e=(1,-1,4)^T$. Their sum is $b$, and $e$ is perpendicular to **every** multiple of $(1,1,0)^T$. The equation forces $t=2$, so no other point on the line gives a perpendicular remainder.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因此 $p=(2,2,0)^T$，$e=(1,-1,4)^T$。两者之和是 $b$，并且 $e$ 与 $(1,1,0)^T$ 的**每个**倍数都垂直。方程迫使 $t=2$，直线上别的点都不能留下垂直残差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the theorem guarantees
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定理保证什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional real or complex inner-product space: a [[向量空间|vector space]] with a fixed [[内积|inner product]] $\langle x,y\rangle$ that specifies lengths and [[正交|orthogonality]]. Let $S$ be a [[子空间|subspace]] of $V$, so it contains zero and is closed under linear combinations. Its [[正交补|orthogonal complement]] $S^\perp$ consists of all vectors in $V$ perpendicular to every member of $S$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $V$ 是有限维实或复内积空间，即已经固定[[内积]] $\langle x,y\rangle$ 来规定长度和[[正交]]关系的[[向量空间]]。设 $S$ 为 $V$ 的[[子空间]]，所以它含零，并对线性组合封闭。它的[[正交补]] $S^\perp$ 包含 $V$ 中与 $S$ 的每个成员都垂直的全部向量。

> [!important] One component in each subspace · 每边各有唯一分量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every $b\in V$ has exactly one representation
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个 $b\in V$ 都恰有一种表示
>
> $$b=p+e,\qquad p\in S,\qquad e\in S^\perp.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Equivalently, $V=S\oplus S^\perp$. The [[直和|direct-sum symbol]] $\oplus$ means that adding one vector from each subspace covers $V$ with unique components. It does not mean a union of sets.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 等价地，$V=S\oplus S^\perp$。[[直和]]记号 $\oplus$ 表示，从每边各取一个向量相加，能够覆盖 $V$，且两个分量唯一；它不是集合并集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Build the split, then rule out alternatives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 存在与唯一分别来自哪里

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $r=\dim S$, the number of independent directions in $S$. Choose an [[标准正交组|orthonormal basis]] $q_1,\ldots,q_r$ of $S$: these vectors span $S$, have length one and are mutually perpendicular. In complex spaces, use the convention conjugate-linear in the first inner-product argument and linear in the second. Then define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $r=\dim S$，即 $S$ 中独立方向的个数。选取 $S$ 的一组[[标准正交组|标准正交基]] $q_1,\ldots,q_r$：这些向量张成 $S$，长度为一且两两垂直。复数情形采用内积第一个自变量共轭线性、第二个自变量线性的约定。定义

$$p=\sum_{i=1}^{r}q_i\langle q_i,b\rangle,\qquad e=b-p.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The scalar $\langle q_i,b\rangle$ measures the component along $q_i$; multiplying by $q_i$ makes a vector again. Thus $p$ is a linear combination in $S$. For each basis vector $q_j$, its inner product with this sum keeps only the term $i=j$, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标量 $\langle q_i,b\rangle$ 量出沿 $q_i$ 的分量，再乘 $q_i$ 就变回向量。因此 $p$ 是位于 $S$ 的线性组合。对每个基向量 $q_j$，与上述和取内积只留下 $i=j$ 那一项，所以

$$\langle q_j,e\rangle
=\langle q_j,b\rangle-\sum_{i=1}^{r}\langle q_j,q_i\rangle\langle q_i,b\rangle
=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every vector of $S$ is a combination of these basis vectors, so $e$ is perpendicular to all of $S$. This proves existence. If $r=0$, the sum is empty and gives $p=0$, $e=b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$S$ 的每个向量都是这些基向量的组合，因此 $e$ 垂直于整个 $S$，存在性得到证明。若 $r=0$，上式为空和，给出 $p=0$、$e=b$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For uniqueness, suppose $b=p_1+e_1=p_2+e_2$ are two valid splits. The difference $z=p_1-p_2=e_2-e_1$ belongs to both $S$ and $S^\perp$. It must therefore be perpendicular to itself: $\langle z,z\rangle=0$. Positive squared length forces $z=0$, hence $p_1=p_2$ and $e_1=e_2$. The orthonormal basis may change; the resulting vectors $p,e$ cannot.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再看唯一性。若 $b=p_1+e_1=p_2+e_2$ 是两种合法拆分，则差 $z=p_1-p_2=e_2-e_1$ 同时属于 $S$ 与 $S^\perp$。它因此必须与自身垂直：$\langle z,z\rangle=0$。平方长度的正定性迫使 $z=0$，故 $p_1=p_2$、$e_1=e_2$。标准正交基可以更换，但得到的向量 $p,e$ 不会改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The component $p$ is the [[正交投影|orthogonal projection]] of $b$ onto $S$. The [[正交向量勾股关系|Pythagorean identity]] gives $\|b\|^2=\|p\|^2+\|e\|^2$, where $\|v\|=\sqrt{\langle v,v\rangle}$. In the opening example this is $26=8+18$. Why $p$ is also the nearest vector in $S$ is the separate [[最佳逼近与正交残差|nearest-point characterization]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分量 $p$ 就是 $b$ 在 $S$ 上的[[正交投影]]。[[正交向量勾股关系]]给出 $\|b\|^2=\|p\|^2+\|e\|^2$，这里 $\|v\|=\sqrt{\langle v,v\rangle}$。开头例子就是 $26=8+18$。为什么 $p$ 还是 $S$ 中的最近点，由[[最佳逼近与正交残差]]另行解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the space and inner product fixed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 含义边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $S=V$, the split is $b=b+0$; if $S=\{0\}$, it is $b=0+b$. Orthogonality and the norm must use the same chosen inner product. A general direct sum need not be perpendicular, and a merely perpendicular subspace smaller than $S^\perp$ may fail to cover $V$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $S=V$，拆分为 $b=b+0$；若 $S=\{0\}$，则为 $b=0+b$。正交关系与范数必须使用同一个选定内积。一般直和并不要求垂直；一个仅与 $S$ 垂直、却比 $S^\perp$ 小的子空间，也可能无法与 $S$ 一起覆盖 $V$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof actually needs only $S$ to be finite-dimensional; the ambient $V$ may be infinite-dimensional. It uses a finite orthonormal basis of $S$, not of all of $V$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述证明实际上只需要 $S$ 有限维，外部空间 $V$ 可以无限维。证明使用的是 $S$ 的有限标准正交基，并不是整个 $V$ 的基。

> [!note]- When the subspace is infinite-dimensional · 子空间本身无限维时
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a **Hilbert space** $H$, an inner-product space in which every norm-Cauchy sequence has a limit in $H$, the corresponding theorem holds exactly for **closed subspaces** $S$. A norm-Cauchy sequence has terms eventually arbitrarily close to each other; closed means that limits in $H$ of convergent sequences from $S$ still belong to $S$. If $S$ is not closed, the guaranteed decomposition is $H=\overline S\oplus S^\perp$, where $\overline S$ adds all such limits to $S$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **Hilbert 空间** $H$ 是每个范数 Cauchy 序列都在 $H$ 内有极限的内积空间；范数 Cauchy 序列指各项最终彼此任意接近。此时，相应分解定理恰好对**闭子空间** $S$ 成立；“闭”表示从 $S$ 中取收敛序列，其在 $H$ 中的极限仍属于 $S$。若 $S$ 不闭，保证成立的是 $H=\overline S\oplus S^\perp$，其中 $\overline S$ 把这些极限都补入 $S$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is an additional infinite-dimensional theorem, not permission to drop both finite dimension and closedness in an arbitrary inner-product space.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这是额外的无限维定理，并不允许在任意内积空间中同时去掉有限维和闭性条件。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*, 4th ed., 6.48–6.50, PDF pp.225–227](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=225): finite-dimensional subspace decomposition, basis construction and uniqueness. Axler conjugates the second argument; the coefficient order here is adjusted consistently to the first-argument-conjugate convention.
- [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/MIT_OCW_18.06SC_PDF/05_Session_Summaries/MIT18_06SCF11_Ses2.1sum.pdf#page=1|MIT 18.06SC Session 2.1, pp.1–2]] and [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/MIT_OCW_18.06SC_PDF/05_Session_Summaries/MIT18_06SCF11_Ses2.2sum.pdf#page=1|Session 2.2, pp.1–2]]: real orthogonal-complement pairs and projection/residual geometry. The displayed line example is checked directly.
- [MIT 18.102 Lecture 17, Theorems 180–181, PDF pp.2–3](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-102-introduction-to-functional-analysis-spring-2021/c97549663746d6bc4a379077e067c55b_MIT18_102s21_lec17.pdf#page=2): closed-subspace Hilbert decomposition and the closure boundary. Necessity of closedness follows from these results or by taking a limit of vectors in $S$ in the proposed decomposition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Axler 上述条目核对有限维子空间分解、按基构造与唯一性；原书对第二个自变量取共轭，本卡相应调整系数顺序。
- MIT 18.06SC 两份摘要核对实数正交补配对及投影、残差的几何；直线例子直接计算。
- MIT 18.102 定理 180–181 核对闭子空间的 Hilbert 分解与闭包边界；闭性的必要性可由这些结论或对 $S$ 中的收敛序列取极限得到。
