# 贫困线是按指定福利变量和计量口径识别贫困者的阈值

在货币贫困测量中，贫困线 $z$ 是用于判断一个人的收入或消费是否达到指定生活标准的界限。先确定[[贫困测量的福利口径|福利变量]]，再将它与同一单位、同一期间和可比价格下的 $z$ 比较；本组采用 $y_i<z$ 判为贫困，恰好等于线者不计入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In monetary poverty measurement, a poverty line $z$ is the threshold used to judge whether a person's income or consumption meets a specified living standard. Define the [[贫困测量的福利口径|welfare measure]] first, then compare it with a threshold in matching units, periods, and prices. These notes classify $y_i<z$ as poor; equality does not count as poverty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，若线是每人每月 100 个同价货币单位，收入 90 低于线；不能将这条线直接与一户全年的总收入比较。跨地区价格不同，也不能把相同名义金额视为相同生活条件。国际比较中的[[购买力平价]]负责转换价格口径，并不替我们决定哪种生活标准算足够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, with a line of 100 constant-price currency units per person per month, income of 90 falls below the line. Annual total household income is not directly comparable with that threshold. Identical nominal amounts may also buy different living conditions across locations. [[购买力平价|Purchasing power parity]] helps align price units in international comparisons; it does not determine which living standard should count as sufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选线与汇总是两步：[[绝对贫困与相对贫困]]说明阈值怎样确定；[[贫困人口比例]]和[[贫困缺口指数]]说明识别之后怎样汇总。不同国家的本国贫困线可能服务不同政策目标；即使都叫“贫困率”，也不自动表示按同一实际标准比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identification and aggregation are separate steps. [[绝对贫困与相对贫困|Absolute and relative poverty]] concern how thresholds are set; the [[贫困人口比例|headcount ratio]] and [[贫困缺口指数|poverty gap index]] aggregate the resulting shortfalls. National lines can serve different policy objectives, so two reported poverty rates need not use the same real standard.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [World Bank, PIP Methodology, Converting welfare aggregates](https://github.com/worldbank/PIP-Methodology/blob/master/03-Converting-welfare-aggregates.Rmd)：CPIs、PPPs、国际与本国贫困线部分，支持价格/期间一致性与阈值口径；100 单位是教学设定，不是现行国际标准。
- [Foster et al. (2013), 第26–29页](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/281001468323965733/pdf/A-unified-approach-to-measuring-poverty-and-inequality-theory-and-practice.pdf#page=44)：支持先识别、后汇总的测量结构。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The PIP chapter supports price alignment and the distinction between national and international thresholds. Foster and colleagues separate identification from aggregation. The 100-unit line is a constructed example, not a current international standard.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
