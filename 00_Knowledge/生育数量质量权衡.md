# 生育数量质量权衡是子女数量与每名子女人力资本投入因交互成本而相互制约的选择关系

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The fertility quantity–quality trade-off is the choice relationship in which the number of children and human-capital investment per child constrain one another through interacting costs.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

生育数量质量权衡（quantity–quality trade-off）是指：在给定家庭资源下，多养育子女会提高为每名子女增加投入的总成本，而提高每名子女的投入又会提高增加一名子女的成本。这里的“质量”指教育、人力资本等模型对象，不是孩子的人格价值；教育投入也不等于已经实现的教育成果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With household resources held fixed, having more children raises the total cost of increasing investment in each child, while investing more per child raises the cost of an additional child. “Quality” refers to outcomes such as education or human capital, not a child's worth. Educational inputs and achieved outcomes are distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个简化教学预算表示这项交互关系。设 $n\ge0$ 为子女数量，为表示边际成本暂将它连续化；$e\ge0$ 为每名子女接受的同等教育投入，$p>0$ 为投入单价，$a>0$ 为固定的每名子女基础养育成本，$c$ 为其他消费，$M$ 为给定的家庭资源，成本与资源均按同一货币单位计价。令教育投入通过递增的 $h(e)$ 形成子女人力资本，则

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simplified teaching budget, let $n\ge0$ denote the number of children, treated as continuous to express marginal costs; $e\ge0$ equal educational investment per child; $p>0$ its unit price; $a>0$ the fixed basic cost per child; $c$ other consumption; and $M$ household resources. Costs and resources use the same monetary unit. Investment produces human capital through an increasing function $h(e)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
c+\underbrace{n(a+pe)}_{C(n,e)}\le M,
\qquad
\frac{\partial C}{\partial n}=a+pe,
\qquad
\frac{\partial C}{\partial e}=np.
$$

因此，“多一个孩子”的成本取决于为每个孩子选择的投入水平；“每个孩子多投入一单位”的成本取决于孩子总数。构造例子：若 $a=2$、$p=1$，且用于子女的预算固定为 $12$，则 $(n,e)=(2,4)$ 与 $(3,2)$ 都恰好用完预算。两名子女时，把每人的 $e$ 增加 $1$ 共需 $2$；三名子女时则共需 $3$。这些数值只说明预算互动，不是某个家庭的最优生育数或实证估计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cost of another child depends on investment per child, and the cost of increasing every child's investment depends on family size. In a constructed example with $a=2$, $p=1$, and a child-related budget of $12$, both $(n,e)=(2,4)$ and $(3,2)$ exhaust the budget. Raising $e$ by one costs $2$ for two children but $3$ for three. These are feasible bundles, not estimated outcomes or optimal fertility choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这项权衡并不单独证明“多生一个孩子必然降低每名子女受教育程度”。上述预算固定了资源、价格、基础成本和同等投入规则；公共教育供给、家庭资源或投入分配改变时，观察到的数量与教育关系可以不同。工资冲击还会改变育儿时间价格，须另外用[[生育的工资与收入效应]]区分；技术如何改变选择教育的回报，则见[[教育回报与生育转型]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The budget interaction alone does not establish that another birth must causally reduce each child's schooling. Resources, prices, basic costs, and equal investment were held fixed; public provision or changes in household resources and allocation can alter the observed relationship. A wage shock also changes the price of parental time, as explained in [[生育的工资与收入效应|wage and income effects on fertility]]. For changing educational returns, see [[教育回报与生育转型|returns to education and the fertility transition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
