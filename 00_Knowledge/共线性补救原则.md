# Responding to multicollinearity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*共线性补救原则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When predictors move together, the useful question is what the available data can tell us about the quantity we care about. Lowering a diagnostic number is only helpful if it improves that answer. Start by naming the [[估计对象三分|target quantity]]—a particular coefficient, a combination of coefficients, or a future prediction—and then identify why the [[设计矩阵|design columns]] are dependent or nearly dependent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解释变量一起变化时，首先要问现有数据能把关心的量说明到什么程度。降低诊断数值，只有在改善这个答案时才有意义。先说明[[估计对象三分|目标量]]是某个系数、系数组合还是未来预测，再判断[[设计矩阵|设计列]]为何精确或近似依赖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## First separate a missing distinction from a noisy one

With exact dependence, [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|some coefficients are nonunique]]. Suppose two nonconstant columns satisfy $z=x$ and the fitted expression is $\beta_0+\beta_xx+\beta_zz$. It reduces to $\beta_0+(\beta_x+\beta_z)x$. The data can identify the sum, but cannot tell $(\beta_x,\beta_z)=(2,3)$ from $(4,1)$. Dropping one duplicate column lets its coefficient represent the sum. It does not establish that the omitted variable’s separate effect was zero.

With near dependence and full column rank, a unique coefficient may still be weakly determined. Inspect its interval, the supported predictor combinations and the relevant [[近似共线性精度|error-and-design variance mechanism]]. A high [[方差膨胀因子|VIF]] alone does not show that the precision is inadequate for the actual task.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先分清是分不开，还是分得不精确

精确依赖时，[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|部分系数不唯一]]。例如两列非恒定列满足 $z=x$，拟合式 $\beta_0+\beta_xx+\beta_zz$ 就等于 $\beta_0+(\beta_x+\beta_z)x$。数据可以识别总和，却分不清 $(\beta_x,\beta_z)=(2,3)$ 与 $(4,1)$。删除一列重复列后，保留下来的系数可以代表总和，但不能据此认定删去变量的单独效应原本为零。

近似依赖且满列秩时，系数虽然唯一，数据仍可能把它确定得很不精确。应检查区间、有支持的解释变量组合，以及[[近似共线性精度|误差与设计共同决定方差的机制]]。仅有高[[方差膨胀因子|VIF]]，不能证明精度对当前任务一定不够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Match the response to the cause and the target

| Situation | A defensible response | What must be stated |
|---|---|---|
| Duplicate coding or a complete indicator set plus an intercept | Remove redundant coding, choose a reference group, or state identifying restrictions | Which coefficients or contrasts the new parameterization represents |
| Polynomial or interaction terms constructed from the same predictors | Use a meaningful center and retain the terms needed for an equivalent model | Centering changes coordinates; it does not create observations or identify missing effects |
| Separate coefficients are the target, but observed combinations are narrow | Seek informative new observations, or retain the model and report the uncertainty | Which comparisons are supported; [[新增样本与共线性\|more rows need not lower VIF]] |
| A control is needed for the stated causal design | Check that role before any deletion; improve the design or acknowledge limited precision | A low VIF cannot replace the identification argument |
| Prediction is the target | Consider regularization and evaluate on appropriate held-out data | The loss, validation scheme and prediction population |
| A combination is the target or is all that can be estimated | Report the supported combination and its uncertainty | Do not present it as separate effects of its components |

Two measures of the same construct may justify an index or selection of a better-measured variable, but that requires substantive and measurement reasoning. Choosing whichever has the smaller sample p-value supplies neither.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 让处理方式匹配来源与目标

| 情况 | 有依据的处理 | 必须说明什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 重复编码，或全套类别指示变量加截距 | 去掉冗余编码、选择基准组，或明确识别约束 | 新参数化中的系数或对比代表什么 |
| 由同一变量构造多项式或交互项 | 选择有意义的中心，保留等价模型所需的项 | 中心化改变坐标，不会创造观测或识别缺失效应 |
| 目标是分别估计系数，但观测组合狭窄 | 收集有信息的新观测，或保留模型并报告不确定性 | 哪些比较得到支持；[[新增样本与共线性\|行数增加不一定降低 VIF]] |
| 某控制项是当前因果设计所需的 | 删去前检查其角色；改善设计或承认精度有限 | 低 VIF 不能代替识别论证 |
| 目标是预测 | 考虑正则化，并用适当留出数据评价 | 损失函数、验证方案和预测总体 |
| 目标是某个组合，或只能估计该组合 | 报告得到支持的组合及其不确定性 | 不能把组合当成各分量的独立效应 |

若两项指标测量同一个实质构念，可以有依据地合成指标或保留测量更好的一项，但需要理论和测量论证。只选样本 p 值更小者，不能提供这两种依据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep track of what the intervention changes

Removing a nonredundant column changes the fitted model space and may change the adjusted comparison. If it is a relevant control, [[遗漏变量偏差|omitted-variable bias]] relative to the original target can result. If its conditional contribution is truly zero, the [[无关变量代价|irrelevant-regressor comparison]] has its own exogeneity and covariance conditions; an insignificant sample coefficient does not establish that premise.

An invertible change of coordinates, such as centering a complete quadratic model with an intercept, preserves its fitted function and information for the same translated target in exact arithmetic. It may improve interpretation or numerical computation. Dropping lower-order terms merely to lower VIF can instead impose a different model; see [[中心化共线性边界|the centering boundary]].

[[岭正则化|Ridge regularization]] adds a coefficient penalty to the fitting criterion. It changes the estimator and introduces a bias–variance tradeoff; it does not necessarily change the scientific estimand the analyst hopes to learn. A fixed penalized population objective defines its own target, which can differ from the unpenalized projection. Either way, shrinkage does not supply a missing causal identification argument, and better prediction must be checked rather than assumed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 跟踪处理究竟改变了什么

删除非冗余列会改变拟合模型的列空间，也可能改变调整后的比较。若它是相关控制项，可能相对于原目标产生[[遗漏变量偏差]]。若其条件贡献确实为零，[[无关变量代价|无关变量比较]]也有自己的外生性与协方差条件；样本系数不显著，不能证明该前提成立。

可逆坐标变换，例如把含截距及完整低阶项的二次模型中心化，在精确算术下保留拟合函数，以及对同一转换后目标的信息。它可能改善解释或数值计算。仅为降低 VIF 而删除低阶项，则可能强加另一个模型，见[[中心化共线性边界]]。

[[岭正则化]]在拟合准则中加入系数惩罚，改变估计规则并带来偏差—方差取舍，但不必改变研究者想了解的科学目标量。固定的总体惩罚目标函数另行定义其最优解，它可能不同于未惩罚的投影系数。无论哪种情况，收缩都不能补出缺失的因果识别论证，更好的预测也需要验证。

> [!tip] Finish with the revised claim / 最后写清新的结论范围
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After a change, state the target, the design or coding, and the evidence used to assess it. Report a joint effect or a wide interval when that matches the data. If variable selection used the same outcomes as the final test, account for [[选择后推断|selection in the inferential procedure]]. A lower VIF or a newly significant coefficient is not the acceptance criterion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每次处理后写清目标、设计或编码，以及用什么证据评价它。数据只支持联合效应或宽区间时，就如实报告。若选变量和最终检验使用同一批结果，应在[[选择后推断|推断过程中计入选择]]。VIF 下降或某个系数变显著，并不是验收标准。
