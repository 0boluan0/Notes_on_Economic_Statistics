# 多重共线性的补救必须由目标系数和数据生成机制决定，不能按最大 VIF 机械删变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A remedy for multicollinearity must follow the target coefficient and data-generating mechanism, not a mechanical deletion of the variable with the largest VIF*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 决策顺序
> 先写清要估计的是单个系数、联合效应、预测还是某个可识别组合；再查明弱方向来自编码、构造项还是样本支持；最后选择不会悄悄改写研究问题的处理。降低诊断数值不是最终目标。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> First state whether the target is an individual coefficient, a joint effect, prediction, or an identifiable combination. Then determine whether the weak direction comes from coding, constructed terms, or sample support. Only then choose a response that does not silently rewrite the research question. Lowering a diagnostic statistic is not the final objective.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 原因与目标 | 合理处理 | 不能据此宣称什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 重复列、全套虚拟变量、机械恒等式 | 修正编码、选基准组或明确约束 | 软件自动丢列不等于问题已解释 |
| 构造多项式或交互项 | 选有意义的中心、保留层级、报告联合或边际效应 | 单列 VIF 高不授权删低阶项 |
| 两列代表同一实质构念 | 在理论支持下合成指标或重定义参数 | 不能只留样本 $p$ 值较小者 |
| 目标是必要的因果控制后效应 | 保留控制并承认精度或重新设计 | 降低 VIF 不会补回遗漏变量识别 |
| 目标是预测 | 可用岭等正则化并在验证数据中评估 | 收缩系数不能直接当结构效应 |
| 样本只识别联合方向 | 报告联合效应、区间或缩小 estimand | 不能强行制造每个系数的显著性 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Cause and target | Defensible response | What it does not justify |
|---|---|---|
| Duplicate columns, a complete dummy set, or an identity | Correct coding, choose a reference group, or state a restriction | Automatic software dropping is not an explanation |
| Constructed polynomials or interactions | Choose a meaningful center, preserve hierarchy, and report joint or marginal effects | A high column VIF does not license deleting lower-order terms |
| Columns measure the same substantive construct | Combine or redefine parameters when theory supports it | Do not retain whichever sample p-value is smaller |
| A causally required adjusted effect | Retain the control and acknowledge imprecision, or redesign | Lower VIF does not restore omitted-variable identification |
| Prediction | Use ridge or another regularizer and evaluate on validation data | Shrunken coefficients are not automatically structural effects |
| Only a joint direction is supported | Report that joint effect, its interval, or narrow the estimand | Do not force every coefficient to become significant |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

删变量、合成指标、正则化和收集新数据都改变不同对象。删变量改变条件比较，合成指标改变参数，正则化改变估计目标，新数据改变支持。每次处理后应重新写出 estimand、设计矩阵和验证证据，而不是只比较处理前后的 VIF。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Variable deletion, index construction, regularization, and new data change different objects. Deletion changes the conditional comparison, an index changes the parameterization, regularization changes the estimation target, and new data change support. After any repair, restate the estimand, design matrix, and validation evidence rather than comparing only the before-and-after VIFs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为研究教育回报，能力代理变量是必要控制但与教育高度相关。第一反应为什么不应是删掉能力变量？
>
> **答案：** 删除它会改变条件比较并可能引入遗漏变量偏差；应先评估重叠、区间和研究设计，或承认当前数据无法精确分开两者。
