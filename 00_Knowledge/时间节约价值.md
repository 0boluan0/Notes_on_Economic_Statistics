# 时间节约价值是对时间节约的边际支付意愿

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The value of time savings is marginal willingness to pay for saving time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

时间节约价值是给定活动、个人收入与其他条件时，对少耗用一小单位时间的边际支付意愿。例如缩短同一次出行，必须说明交通方式、拥挤和可靠性是否保持不变；释放的时间可用于劳动、闲暇或其他活动，不能统一按工资计价，见[[工资与时间价值]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The value of time savings is marginal willingness to pay for a small reduction in time spent, holding the activity, income and other relevant conditions fixed. For the same trip, specify whether mode, crowding and reliability are unchanged. Released time may support work, leisure or other uses; it is not universally worth the wage, as explained in [[工资与时间价值|wages and time value]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $W(s)$ 为从给定基准节约 $s$ 小时的总支付意愿，且 $W(0)=0$。可微时，局部时间价值是 $v=W'(0)$，单位为元/小时；小幅节时收益近似为 $v\Delta s$。较大变化的平均价值 $W(s)/s$ 不必等于边际价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $W(s)$ denote total willingness to pay to save $s$ hours from a fixed baseline, with $W(0)=0$. When differentiable, local time value is $v=W'(0)$ in currency per hour, giving the approximation $v\Delta s$ for small savings. The average $W(s)/s$ for a larger change need not equal the marginal value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若某人恰好在“多付3元、节省15分钟”和原路线间无差异，则这段节时的平均价值为 $3/(15/60)=12$ 元/小时。若只观察到他选择付3元的路线，通常只能推知这段节时的支付意愿至少为3元，不能断言边际价值恰为12；[[显示偏好估值]]负责这种选择证据的解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indifference between the original route and paying 3 to save 15 minutes implies an average value of $3/(15/60)=12$ per hour for that saving. Merely observing the paid choice usually gives a lower bound of 3, not an exact marginal value of 12; [[显示偏好估值|revealed-preference valuation]] explains this distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
