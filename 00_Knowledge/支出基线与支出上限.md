# 支出基线估计现行政策的未来成本而支出上限限定允许安排的支出规模
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An expenditure baseline projects the future cost of current policy; an expenditure ceiling limits planned spending.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支出基线回答“如果继续现行政策，未来需要花多少”；支出上限回答“按财政安排允许配置多少”。基线随价格、服务需求和已立法承诺变化，即使没有新政策也会增长；上限是控制界限，二者都不能直接代替法定拨款授权。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A baseline asks what existing policies will cost; a ceiling sets the permitted allocation. Prices, service demand and legislated commitments can raise baseline costs without new policy. A ceiling is a control limit; neither figure substitutes for an appropriation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设某年同一覆盖范围的现行政策基线为 112，支出上限为 110。差额 2 表示现行政策成本超过上限，需要可执行的节约、重新排序或按规则批准调整。它本身不能证明存在 2 的浪费，也不能通过把预测数字改成 110 消除。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a year's current-policy baseline is 112 and its ceiling is 110 on identical coverage. The gap of 2 requires feasible savings, reprioritisation or an authorised adjustment. It neither proves waste of 2 nor disappears when the forecast is relabelled as 110.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[中期预算框架]]中，先用可核验假设更新基线，再显式列出新增政策与节约措施，才能看清怎样达到上限。比较前还应核对上限是否排除了利息等项目；不同范围的 112 和 110 不能直接相减。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within a [[中期预算框架|medium-term budget framework]], update the baseline using verifiable assumptions and show new policies and savings separately. Reconcile exclusions such as interest before comparing a baseline with a ceiling.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
