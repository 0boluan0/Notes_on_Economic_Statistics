# Portmanteau theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*依分布收敛能保证哪些事件概率收敛？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Portmanteau theorem translates [[依分布收敛|convergence in distribution]] into statements about the probability of landing in a set. Its key restriction is the set's **boundary**: if the limiting distribution places probability on that boundary, tiny movements can change whether a value is counted inside. We then have one-sided bounds rather than an automatic equality of limiting probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Portmanteau 定理把[[依分布收敛]]翻译成“落入某个集合的概率”应满足的条件。关键限制来自集合的**边界**：如果极限分布在边界上放有概率，极小的移动就可能改变某个值是否算在集合内。这时通常只能保证单侧界，不能自动断言事件概率趋于相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a concrete example, let $X_n=1/n$ and $X=0$, all deterministic. We have $X_n\Rightarrow X$. Every $X_n$ lies in $(0,\infty)$, but $X$ does not; none of the $X_n$ lies in $\{0\}$, but $X$ does. The numbers approach zero while membership flips at the limiting boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，取确定的 $X_n=1/n$、$X=0$，它们满足 $X_n\Rightarrow X$。每个 $X_n$ 都在 $(0,\infty)$ 内，$X$ 却不在；每个 $X_n$ 都不在 $\{0\}$ 内，$X$ 却在。数值接近零，集合归属却在极限边界处发生变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The sets and the inequalities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先认识集合，再读不等式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card states the real-line version. All $X_n$ and $X$ are real-valued random variables; their probability spaces need not be the same. Sets below use the usual topology of $\mathbb R$. An [[开集|open set]] contains a small interval around each of its points; a [[闭集|closed set]] contains limits of its converging sequences. For example, $(a,b)$ is open and $[a,b]$ is closed. The [[集合边界|boundary]] $\partial A$ contains points approached arbitrarily closely from both $A$ and its complement; both these intervals have boundary $\{a,b\}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡陈述实数轴上的版本。所有 $X_n$ 和 $X$ 都是实值随机变量，不必定义在同一概率空间上。下面的集合使用 $\mathbb R$ 的通常拓扑。[[开集]]的每个点都带有一段仍在集合内的小区间；[[闭集]]包含其内部收敛序列的极限。例如，$(a,b)$ 开，$[a,b]$ 闭。[[集合边界|边界]] $\partial A$ 的点可以同时从 $A$ 与补集任意接近；这两个区间的边界均为 $\{a,b\}$。

> [!important] Equivalent criteria · 等价判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each of the following three requirements, applied to **all** sets of its stated class, is equivalent to $X_n\Rightarrow X$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> &\limsup_{n\to\infty}P(X_n\in K)\le P(X\in K)
> &&\text{for every closed }K,\\
> &\liminf_{n\to\infty}P(X_n\in G)\ge P(X\in G)
> &&\text{for every open }G,\\
> &P(X_n\in A)\longrightarrow P(X\in A)
> &&\text{if }P(X\in\partial A)=0.
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the last line $A$ ranges over Borel sets, the measurable class generated by open intervals; such an $A$ is called a continuity set for the **limiting** distribution. The sets are fixed as $n$ changes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 三行分别是：所有闭集上的上极限界、所有开集上的下极限界、所有极限边界概率为零的 Borel 集上的概率收敛。Borel 集是由开区间生成的可测集合类；最后一类集合称为**极限分布**的连续集。每一条都要求覆盖其所属的全部集合，且集合不随 $n$ 改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a bounded numerical sequence $a_n$, $\limsup a_n$ is the limit of the largest value still possible in a late tail, $\lim_N\sup_{n\ge N}a_n$; $\liminf$ uses the smallest tail values instead. Thus the closed-set line says that the probabilities cannot exceed $P(X\in K)$ by a fixed positive margin infinitely often. It does not say that every individual $P(X_n\in K)$ is already below that number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有界数列 $a_n$，$\limsup a_n$ 是越往后看时，剩余尾部上确界的极限，即 $\lim_N\sup_{n\ge N}a_n$；$\liminf$ 则使用尾部下确界。所以闭集那一行表示概率不能在足够晚时仍反复超出 $P(X\in K)$ 一个固定正余量，并不是说每一个 $P(X_n\in K)$ 都已经小于该数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The opening example remembers the directions: for the closed set $K=\{0\}$ we get $0\le1$; for the open set $G=(0,\infty)$ we get $1\ge0$. Both inequalities can be strict. Passing to complements changes one direction into the other because $P(K)=1-P(K^c)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头的例子帮助记忆方向：闭集 $K=\{0\}$ 给出 $0\le1$，开集 $G=(0,\infty)$ 给出 $1\ge0$，两边都可能严格不等。取补集可以在两种方向间转换，因为 $P(K)=1-P(K^c)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why zero boundary probability gives equality
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么边界没有概率，就能得到相等的极限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A set $A$ lies between its [[集合内部|interior]] $A^\circ$ (points with an inside neighbourhood) and its [[集合闭包|closure]] $\overline A$ (all points arbitrarily close to $A$). Their difference is the boundary. If $P(X\in\partial A)=0$, these three sets have the same limiting probability. Applying the open-set bound to the interior and the closed-set bound to the closure squeezes the probabilities:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集合 $A$ 夹在它的[[集合内部|内部]] $A^\circ$（带有内部邻域的点）与[[集合闭包|闭包]] $\overline A$（能被 $A$ 任意接近的点）之间，闭包与内部的差恰为边界。如果 $P(X\in\partial A)=0$，三者在极限分布下的概率相同。把开集界用于内部、闭集界用于闭包，就得到

$$P(X\in A)\le\liminf_nP(X_n\in A)
\le\limsup_nP(X_n\in A)\le P(X\in A).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All four quantities must therefore agree. For $A=(-\infty,x]$, its boundary is $\{x\}$. The requirement $P(X=x)=0$ is exactly continuity of the limiting [[累积分布函数|CDF]] at $x$. Thus the familiar CDF definition is the half-line instance of the continuity-set rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

四个量因此必须相等。对 $A=(-\infty,x]$，边界是 $\{x\}$；条件 $P(X=x)=0$ 恰好表示极限[[累积分布函数]]在 $x$ 连续。熟悉的 CDF 定义就是连续集规则应用于半直线的情形。

> [!example] A closed region with zero limiting mass · 极限质量为零的闭区域
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $X_n\Rightarrow c$ for a constant $c$, choose $K=(-\infty,c-\varepsilon]\cup[c+\varepsilon,\infty)$ with fixed $\varepsilon>0$. It is closed and does not contain $c$, so the closed-set inequality forces $P(|X_n-c|\ge\varepsilon)\to0$. This proves the [[常数分布极限推出概率收敛|constant-limit probability-convergence result]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $X_n\Rightarrow c$，固定 $\varepsilon>0$ 并取闭集 $K=(-\infty,c-\varepsilon]\cup[c+\varepsilon,\infty)$。它不包含 $c$，所以闭集不等式迫使 $P(|X_n-c|\ge\varepsilon)\to0$，得到[[常数分布极限推出概率收敛]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single set satisfying one inequality does not establish distributional convergence. Nor may the fixed set be silently replaced by arbitrary moving sets $A_n$. The theorem compares distributions and event boundaries; it does not establish closeness of a prescribed [[分布收敛不推概率收敛|jointly paired random target]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只在一个集合上满足某个不等式，并不能证明依分布收敛；也不能擅自把固定集合换成任意变化的 $A_n$。这个定理比较的是分布和事件边界，并不保证与某个[[分布收敛不推概率收敛|指定配对的随机目标]]数值接近。
