# Sequential characterization of closed sets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*闭集的序列判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a metric space, a closed set cannot lose a point by taking the limit of a sequence entirely inside it, provided the limit belongs to the ambient space. For instance, a sequence in $[0,1]$ that converges in $\mathbb R$ has its limit in $[0,1]$. By contrast, $1/(n+1)$ lies in $(0,1)$ for every positive integer $n$ but converges to $0$, which is missing from that interval. This single sequence proves that $(0,1)$ is not closed in $\mathbb R$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在度量空间里，对一条所有项都在闭集内的序列取极限，只要极限属于环境空间，就不会“丢到集合外”。例如，$[0,1]$ 内的序列若在 $\mathbb R$ 中收敛，极限仍在 $[0,1]$ 内。相比之下，$1/(n+1)$ 的每一项都在 $(0,1)$ 内，却收敛到该区间缺少的 $0$。这一条序列就能证明 $(0,1)$ 在 $\mathbb R$ 中不闭。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The underlying definition of a [[闭集|closed set]] $F\subseteq X$ is that its complement $X\setminus F$ is [[开集|open]]. The sequence criterion is an equivalent test when $X$ is a [[度量空间|metric space]]. Here [[序列收敛|convergence]] $x_n\to x$ means that every fixed open ball around $x$ contains **all sufficiently late terms**. Early terms may be outside that ball; infinitely many occasional visits alone would not be enough.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[闭集]] $F\subseteq X$ 的基本定义是补集 $X\setminus F$ 为[[开集]]。当 $X$ 是[[度量空间]]时，序列判别提供一个等价检验。这里的[[序列收敛|收敛]] $x_n\to x$ 要求：$x$ 周围每个固定开球都包含**充分靠后的全部项**。早期项可以在这个球外；只偶尔进入无穷多次还不够。

> [!abstract] Every sequence, every ambient limit · 所有序列与所有环境内极限
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a subset $F$ of a metric space $X$,
> $$F\text{ is closed in }X\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> \left[\begin{array}{l}\text{for every sequence }(x_n)_{n\ge1}\text{ in }F\text{ and every }x\in X,\\x_n\to x\text{ in }X\Longrightarrow x\in F\end{array}\right].$$
> The quantifiers cover **every** such sequence and ambient limit. The property on the right is called **sequential closedness**. Both $F=\varnothing$ and $F=X$ satisfy it: for the empty set there is no sequence of its points to test.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对度量空间 $X$ 的子集 $F$，上式对**每一条**各项都属于 $F$ 且在 $X$ 中收敛的序列，以及它在 $X$ 中的极限作出要求。右侧性质称为**序列闭性**。$F=\varnothing$ 与 $F=X$ 都满足；空集中没有可供检验的序列，因此不可能出现反例。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem separates two different tasks. To disprove closedness, find one sequence in $F$ whose ambient limit is outside $F$. To prove closedness by this criterion, start with an arbitrary sequence in $F$ that converges in $X$ and show its limit stays in $F$. Checking just one successful sequence, such as a constant sequence, does not establish a statement about all of them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一定理把两个任务区分开来：要否定闭性，只需找一条来自 $F$、环境极限却在 $F$ 外的序列；要用本判据证明闭性，则应从任意一条来自 $F$ 且在 $X$ 中收敛的序列出发，证明其极限仍属于 $F$。只检查一条成功的序列，例如常数序列，不能证明对所有序列的结论。

## Why closedness and the limit test agree
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*闭性为什么与极限检验一致*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $F$ is closed and $x_n\in F$ for all $n$, with $x_n\to x$ in $X$. If $x\notin F$, the open complement $X\setminus F$ is a neighbourhood of $x$. Convergence would force all sufficiently late terms into that complement, contradicting their membership in $F$. Therefore $x\in F$. This argument uses only open neighbourhoods, so closed sets are sequentially closed in every [[拓扑|topological space]], even where a sequence can have more than one limit. In that case the argument applies to each limit separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $F$ 闭，全部 $x_n\in F$，且 $x_n$ 在 $X$ 中收敛到 $x$。若 $x\notin F$，开补集 $X\setminus F$ 就是 $x$ 的一个邻域。收敛会迫使充分靠后的全部项进入该补集，与每项都属于 $F$ 矛盾，所以 $x\in F$。这个证明只使用开邻域，因此在每个[[拓扑|拓扑空间]]里，闭集都是序列闭的；即使某条序列可以有多个极限，证明也逐个适用于这些极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, suppose $F$ is not closed in a metric space. Its [[集合闭包|closure]] contains some $x\in X$ missing from $F$. The [[闭包的序列判别|closure sequence construction]] chooses
$$x_n\in F\cap B_{1/n}(x),\qquad d(x_n,x)<1/n.$$
Each intersection is nonempty because $x$ belongs to the closure. For every $\varepsilon>0$, an integer $N>1/\varepsilon$ makes $d(x_n,x)<\varepsilon$ for every $n\ge N$. We have produced a sequence in $F$ converging to $x\notin F$, so sequential closedness fails. Together the two directions prove the equivalence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，假设 $F$ 在度量空间中不闭。它的[[集合闭包|闭包]]便包含某个属于 $X$ 却不属于 $F$ 的点 $x$。利用[[闭包的序列判别|闭包的序列构造]]，从每个 $F\cap B_{1/n}(x)$ 中选出 $x_n$，便有 $d(x_n,x)<1/n$。这些交集非空，是因为 $x$ 属于闭包。给定 $\varepsilon>0$，取整数 $N>1/\varepsilon$ 就使全部 $n\ge N$ 满足 $d(x_n,x)<\varepsilon$。这样得到一条来自 $F$ 却收敛到 $F$ 外的序列，序列闭性不成立。两个方向合起来就证明了等价性。

> [!example] The limit must belong to the ambient space · 极限必须属于环境空间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Give $X=(0,2)$ the usual distance restricted from $\mathbb R$, equivalently its [[子空间拓扑|subspace topology]]. Then $F=(0,1]$ is closed in $X$, because its complement in $X$ is the open set $(1,2)$. The sequence $x_n=1/(n+1)$ is in $F$ and tends to $0$ in $\mathbb R$, but $0\notin X$. It therefore does not supply a limit in $X$ outside $F$ and does not contradict closedness in $X$. If the ambient space is changed to $\mathbb R$, the same set $F$ is no longer closed, and this sequence does prove that fact.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 给 $X=(0,2)$ 配上从 $\mathbb R$ 限制而来的通常距离，等价地采用[[子空间拓扑]]。$F=(0,1]$ 在 $X$ 中闭，因为它在 $X$ 中的补集 $(1,2)$ 是开集。$x_n=1/(n+1)$ 的各项都在 $F$ 内，并在 $\mathbb R$ 中趋向 $0$，但 $0\notin X$；它没有给出一个“属于 $X$ 却不属于 $F$”的极限，所以不与 $F$ 在 $X$ 中闭矛盾。若把环境换成 $\mathbb R$，同一个 $F$ 就不闭，而这条序列确实证明了这一点。

## The boundary beyond metric spaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*离开度量空间后的边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equivalence also holds in a **first-countable** space: each point has a countable list of open neighbourhoods such that every open neighbourhood contains a member of the list. Finite intersections turn the list into nested neighbourhoods, giving the same construction; the details are in [[闭包的序列判别|the closure criterion]]. No uniqueness of sequence limits is needed for this extension, because the test includes every ambient limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一等价性也适用于**第一可数**空间：每个点都有一列可数的开邻域，使任何开邻域都包含列中的一个。取有限交后得到嵌套邻域，仍能完成同样的构造，细节见[[闭包的序列判别]]。这一推广不要求序列极限唯一，因为判据本来就要求检验所有环境内极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an arbitrary topology, only the implication “closed $\Rightarrow$ sequentially closed” is guaranteed. For the co-countable topology on $\mathbb R$, nonempty open sets have countable complements. As proved in [[闭包的序列判别|the closure counterexample]], $F=(0,1)$ has closure $\mathbb R$, so it is not closed. Nevertheless every convergent sequence is eventually equal to its limit. If all its terms lie in $F$, that limit must lie in $F$ too. Thus this $F$ is sequentially closed without being closed. When no suitable hypothesis is available, use the open-complement or closure definition to test closedness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在任意拓扑中，只能无条件保证“闭 $\Rightarrow$ 序列闭”。例如在 $\mathbb R$ 的可数补拓扑中，非空开集的补集都是可数集。[[闭包的序列判别|闭包反例]]已经证明：$F=(0,1)$ 的闭包是整个 $\mathbb R$，所以它不闭；但每条收敛序列最终都等于其极限，若各项始终属于 $F$，极限也必属于 $F$。于是这个 $F$ 序列闭却不闭。缺少合适的空间假设时，应回到开补集或闭包定义检验闭性。
