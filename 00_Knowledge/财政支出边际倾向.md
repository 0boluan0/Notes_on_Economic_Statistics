# 财政支出边际倾向是同期支出增量与GDP增量的比值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The marginal propensity for government expenditure is the ratio of the change in spending to the contemporaneous change in GDP.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财政支出边际倾向用来描述：在两个可比观察期之间，GDP 每变化一个货币单位，财政支出同时变化多少。按本课程的有限差分定义，$\Delta Y\ne0$ 时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The measure describes how much expenditure changes alongside a one-unit change in GDP between comparable periods. Under the course's finite-difference definition, for $\Delta Y\ne0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
MGP=\frac{\Delta G}{\Delta Y}
=\frac{G_1-G_0}{Y_1-Y_0}.
$$

支出从 200 增到 220，GDP 从 1,000 增到 1,080，则 $MGP=20/80=0.25$。它描述同期每增加 1 元 GDP，支出增加 0.25 元；并不是说政府拿走了全部 GDP 的 25%，后一个问题对应 [[财政支出占比]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With spending rising from 200 to 220 and GDP from 1,000 to 1,080, $MGP=0.25$. Spending rises by 0.25 per additional unit of GDP over this interval. This is not a 25% [[财政支出占比|expenditure share]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个样本比值不自动是政府的因果反应：经济增长、通胀、政策调整等可能同时改变 $G$ 和 $Y$。若要写成行为函数的导数 $dG/dY$，必须另给模型及其固定条件；若要估计因果效应，则需要识别设计，而不是把两期增量直接相除。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The observed ratio is not automatically a causal response. Growth, prices and policy can jointly change spending and GDP. A derivative $dG/dY$ requires an explicit behavioural model and conditioning variables; a causal estimate requires identification, not merely two observed changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分子与分母须使用一致的货币单位和价格口径。GDP 增量很小时，这个比值可能很大且不稳定；GDP 增量为零时不能计算。换成“增长百分比之比”，得到的是 [[财政支出弹性]]，不是同一个量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use consistent monetary units and price conventions. A small GDP change can produce a large, unstable ratio; a zero change makes it undefined. The ratio of percentage changes is [[财政支出弹性|expenditure elasticity]], a different measure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
