# 指示变量与随机计数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Indicator variables and random counts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当目标是“随机出现了多少个对象、事件、配对或冲突”时，不必先求这个总数的完整分布；把每个候选贡献写成 0/1 indicator，再把它们相加即可。
> **具体锚点：** $n$ 个人生日独立均匀落在 365 天。对每一对 $i<j$ 定义 $I_{ij}=1$ 表示两人同生日，则碰撞 pair 数是 $C=\sum_{i<j}I_{ij}$，所以 $E[C]=\binom n2/365$。
> **核心难点：** 线性期望不要求 indicators 独立，但 variance、tail probability 和“至少发生一次”的概率会受依赖影响；还必须正确选择 index set，避免把同一对象重复计数。
> **为什么重要：** fixed points、collisions、occupied bins、graph substructures、algorithmic events 与 sample counts 都能用同一套路化简。
> **继续：** 先看 [[#把“数量”写成 indicators 的和]]，再用 [[#依赖不影响均值，却影响波动与尾部]] 判断还需要哪些信息；若目标已经从 expected count 转为偏离均值的概率，进入 [[概率不等式与集中界]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** When the target is how many objects, events, pairs, or conflicts occur at random, there is often no need to derive the count's full distribution. Represent each possible contribution by a zero-one indicator and add them.
> **Concrete anchor:** Suppose $n$ birthdays are independent and uniform over 365 days. For every pair $i<j$, let $I_{ij}=1$ when the two birthdays match. The number of colliding pairs is $C=\sum_{i<j}I_{ij}$, so $E[C]=\binom n2/365$.
> **Central difficulty:** Linearity of expectation does not require independent indicators, but variance, tail probabilities, and the probability of at least one occurrence do depend on their joint structure. The index set must also be chosen correctly so that an object is not counted more than once.
> **Why it matters:** Fixed points, collisions, occupied bins, graph substructures, algorithmic events, and sample counts all reduce to the same method.
> **Continue with:** Begin by [[#把“数量”写成 indicators 的和|expressing a count as a sum of indicators]], then use [[#依赖不影响均值，却影响波动与尾部|the distinction between means and dependence-sensitive quantities]] to decide what else is required. For deviations from an expected count, continue to [[概率不等式与集中界|probability inequalities and concentration bounds]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从事件到 0/1 随机变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From an event to a zero-one random variable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对事件 $A\subseteq\Omega$，它的 indicator 定义为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an event $A\subseteq\Omega$, its indicator is defined by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
I_A(\omega)=
\begin{cases}
1,&\omega\in A,\\
0,&\omega\notin A.
\end{cases}
$$

indicator 把一个逻辑命题变成可以相加、相乘和取期望的数值对象。因为它只取 0 与 1，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An indicator converts a logical statement into a numerical object that can be added, multiplied, and averaged. Because it takes only zero and one,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E[I_A]=1\cdot P(A)+0\cdot P(A^c)=P(A),
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(I_A)=P(A)(1-P(A)).
$$

多个 indicators 的代数也保留事件逻辑：$I_AI_B=I_{A\cap B}$；$1-I_A=I_{A^c}$；而 $I_A+I_B-I_AI_B=I_{A\cup B}$。但 $I_A+I_B$ 一般不是 union indicator，因为在 $A\cap B$ 上它等于 2；这个“2”恰表示两个事件同时被计数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indicator algebra preserves event logic: $I_AI_B=I_{A\cap B}$, $1-I_A=I_{A^c}$, and $I_A+I_B-I_AI_B=I_{A\cup B}$. However, $I_A+I_B$ is generally not the union indicator because it equals two on $A\cap B$. That two records that both events occurred and were both counted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 把“数量”写成 indicators 的和
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Expressing a count as a sum of indicators*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若候选对象由 index set $J$ 标记，事件 $A_j$ 表示“对象 $j$ 被计入”，那么随机数量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an index set $J$ labels the candidate objects and $A_j$ means that object $j$ is counted, then the random count is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
X=\sum_{j\in J}I_{A_j}.
$$

这是逐 outcome 的恒等式，而不只是期望层面的近似。对每个 $\omega$，右侧恰有多少项为 1，$X(\omega)$ 就是多少。于是有限情形下由 linearity of expectation，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a pointwise identity for every outcome, not merely an approximation in expectation. For each $\omega$, the number of terms equal to one on the right is exactly $X(\omega)$. Therefore, in the finite case, linearity of expectation gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E[X]
=E\!\left[\sum_{j\in J}I_{A_j}\right]
=\sum_{j\in J}E[I_{A_j}]
=\sum_{j\in J}P(A_j).
$$

这条公式不需要 $A_j$ 独立。证明只是在共同 sample space 上交换有限求和次序，没有把 joint probability 分解成乘积。独立性会在计算某个单项概率 $P(A_j)$ 时有用，也会在后续方差和尾界中有用，但不是把期望拆开的许可证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This formula does not require the events $A_j$ to be independent. Its proof merely exchanges two finite sums on the common sample space and never factors a joint probability into a product. Independence may help calculate an individual $P(A_j)$ and may matter later for variance or tail bounds, but it is not the licence that makes expectation additive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Index set 决定你到底在数什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The index set determines what is being counted*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设计 indicators 前先说清 counting unit。数“至少出现一次的类别”时，应为每个类别设 indicator；数“发生碰撞的 pairs”时，应为 unordered pairs $\{i,j\}$ 设 indicator；数 triangle 时，应为三元 vertex subsets 设 indicator。若生日碰撞同时使用 $(i,j)$ 与 $(j,i)$，期望会无故翻倍；若一个 triangle 用六种 vertex orders 表示，也会重复六次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before defining indicators, specify the counting unit. To count categories that appear at least once, use one indicator per category. To count colliding pairs, index unordered pairs $\{i,j\}$. To count triangles, index three-vertex subsets. Using both $(i,j)$ and $(j,i)$ for birthday collisions doubles the expectation without justification; representing one triangle by all six vertex orders counts it six times.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个稳定流程是：先用一句自然语言定义 $X$；写出所有潜在贡献的 index set；为每个贡献定义 0/1 event；逐 outcome 检查和式确实等于 $X$；最后才求每个 indicator 的概率。这样能把“概率算错”和“对象数错”分开诊断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A reliable workflow is to define $X$ in one sentence, write the index set of all potential contributions, define one zero-one event per contribution, verify pointwise that the sum equals $X$, and only then calculate each indicator probability. This separates an incorrect probability calculation from an incorrect counting object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 依赖不影响均值，却影响波动与尾部
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Dependence does not affect the mean, but it affects variation and tails*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $X=\sum_j I_j$，均值总可直接相加；方差则是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $X=\sum_j I_j$, the means always add, whereas the variance is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)
=\sum_j p_j(1-p_j)
+2\sum_{j<k}\operatorname{Cov}(I_j,I_k),
$$

其中 $p_j=P(I_j=1)$，且
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $p_j=P(I_j=1)$ and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(I_j,I_k)
=P(A_j\cap A_k)-P(A_j)P(A_k).
$$

pairwise independence 已足以让所有 covariance 为 0，从而方差相加；mutual independence 更强，通常是 Chernoff 等利用联合乘积结构的尾界所需。反过来，即使 covariance 为 0 或 indicators pairwise independent，三件及以上事件仍可能存在 joint constraint。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pairwise independence is enough to make every covariance zero and hence to add variances. Mutual independence is stronger and is typically required by tail bounds such as Chernoff that exploit a joint product structure. Conversely, zero covariance or pairwise independence does not rule out constraints involving three or more events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要区分三种问题：$E[X]$ 问长期平均数量；$P(X\ge1)$ 问至少出现一次；$P(|X-E[X]|\ge t)$ 问偏离均值。它们不是同一个数。对非负整数 $X$，Markov 给 $P(X\ge1)\le E[X]$，也就是 union bound 的 indicator 形式，但除非事件互斥等特殊结构成立，这通常只是上界，不是等号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three questions must also be separated: $E[X]$ asks for the long-run average count, $P(X\ge1)$ asks whether anything occurs, and $P(|X-E[X]|\ge t)$ asks about deviation from the mean. They are not the same quantity. For a nonnegative integer $X$, Markov gives $P(X\ge1)\le E[X]$, the indicator form of the union bound. Except under special structures such as mutually exclusive events, this is only an upper bound, not an equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 完整例子一：随机排列中的 fixed points
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example one: fixed points in a random permutation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $n$ 顶帽子均匀随机发回 $n$ 个人，令 $I_i=1$ 表示第 $i$ 人拿回自己的帽子，$F=\sum_{i=1}^n I_i$ 是 fixed points 数。对每个 $i$，自己的帽子在 $n$ 个位置上均匀，所以 $P(I_i=1)=1/n$，于是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distribute $n$ hats uniformly at random among their $n$ owners. Let $I_i=1$ when person $i$ receives their own hat, and let $F=\sum_{i=1}^n I_i$ be the number of fixed points. Each person's hat is uniform over the $n$ positions, so $P(I_i=1)=1/n$, and therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E[F]=\sum_{i=1}^n\frac1n=1.
$$

这并不表示“恰好一人拿对”的概率为 1，更不表示每个 permutation 都有一个 fixed point。事实上 $F$ 可以为 0，也可以大于 1；当 $n-1$ 个人都拿对时最后一人必然拿对，所以 indicators 甚至不 mutually independent。indicator method 的价值正是：不用先求 derangement distribution，也能得到 exact mean。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This does not mean that exactly one person is correct with probability one, nor that every permutation has one fixed point. In fact, $F$ may be zero or greater than one. If $n-1$ people are correct, the last person must also be correct, so the indicators are not even mutually independent. The value of the indicator method is that it gives the exact mean without first deriving the derangement distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 完整例子二：生日碰撞的 mean、variance 与发生概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example two: the mean, variance, and occurrence probability of birthday collisions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $B_1,\ldots,B_n$ independent uniform on $\{1,\ldots,d\}$。对每个 unordered pair $i<j$ 定义 $I_{ij}=I_{\{B_i=B_j\}}$，碰撞 pairs 数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $B_1,\ldots,B_n$ be independent and uniform on $\{1,\ldots,d\}$. For every unordered pair $i<j$, define $I_{ij}=I_{\{B_i=B_j\}}$. The number of colliding pairs is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C=\sum_{1\le i<j\le n}I_{ij},
\qquad
E[C]=\binom n2\frac1d.
$$

两个不同 pair indicators 即使共享一个人也 pairwise independent。例如 $P(B_i=B_j, B_i=B_k)=1/d^2$，恰等于两个边际概率乘积。因此
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two distinct pair indicators are pairwise independent even when they share one person. For example, $P(B_i=B_j, B_i=B_k)=1/d^2$, exactly the product of their marginal probabilities. Therefore,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}(C)
=\binom n2\frac1d\left(1-\frac1d\right).
$$

但三个 pair events $B_1=B_2$、$B_1=B_3$、$B_2=B_3$ 不 mutually independent：前两个发生会强迫第三个发生。另一方面，“至少一对同生日”的 exact probability 是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
But the three pair events $B_1=B_2$, $B_1=B_3$, and $B_2=B_3$ are not mutually independent: the first two force the third. Moreover, the exact probability of at least one shared birthday is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(C\ge1)
=1-\frac{d(d-1)\cdots(d-n+1)}{d^n}
\quad(n\le d),
$$

它与 $E[C]$ 回答不同问题。期望统计重复 pairs；一个三人同生日的群体贡献 3 个 pairs，却只让“是否发生碰撞”这个 event 发生一次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
which answers a different question from $E[C]$. The expectation counts repeated pairs. A group of three people sharing a birthday contributes three pairs but makes the event “some collision occurred” true only once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断与使用边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnostics and limits of use*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 常见做法 | 错在哪里 | 修复 |
|---|---|---|
| 先猜 $X$ 的分布，再硬算 $E[X]$ | 完整分布往往比均值难得多 | 先寻找逐 outcome 成立的 indicator sum |
| 用 ordered pairs 数本应 unordered 的碰撞 | 每个对象重复计数 | 明确 index set 与 symmetry factor |
| 因 events 相关就不敢相加期望 | 把 expectation linearity 与 product factorization 混淆 | 直接用 $E[\sum I_j]=\sum E[I_j]$ |
| 由 $E[X]=1$ 推出 $P(X\ge1)=1$ | 均值不是发生概率 | 单独求 distribution/complement，或仅用 $P(X\ge1)\le E[X]$ |
| 期望不需独立，所以方差也直接相加 | 漏掉 covariance | 检查 pairwise dependence 或显式计算 joint probabilities |
| 对无限多个带正负贡献的 indicators 任意交换求和 | 有限线性性被无条件外推 | 对 nonnegative sums 用 monotone convergence；一般情形先核验 integrability |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Common approach | What goes wrong | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| Guess the distribution of $X$ and then force a direct expectation calculation | The full distribution is often much harder than the mean | First seek a pointwise indicator-sum identity |
| Use ordered pairs for a collision that should use unordered pairs | Every object is counted more than once | State the index set and any symmetry factor explicitly |
| Refuse to add expectations because the events are dependent | Linearity is confused with factorisation of products | Apply $E[\sum I_j]=\sum E[I_j]$ directly |
| Infer $P(X\ge1)=1$ from $E[X]=1$ | A mean is not an occurrence probability | Derive a distribution or complement, or use only $P(X\ge1)\le E[X]$ |
| Since expectation needs no independence, add variances as well | Covariances are omitted | Check pairwise dependence or calculate joint probabilities explicitly |
| Rearrange infinitely many positive and negative contributions freely | Finite linearity is extended without convergence control | Use monotone convergence for nonnegative sums; otherwise verify integrability first |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么 indicator method 求期望不需要独立？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why does the indicator method not require independence to compute an expectation?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 因为 $X=\sum_jI_j$ 是每个 outcome 上都成立的等式，期望对有限和总是线性。这个步骤没有计算任何 joint probability，也没有把它分解为边际概率乘积。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The identity $X=\sum_jI_j$ holds for every outcome, and expectation is linear over finite sums. No joint probability is calculated or factored into marginal products in this step.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一张随机图中要数 triangles，indicator 应按什么对象索引？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*To count triangles in a random graph, what objects should index the indicators?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 按三个 vertices 组成的 unordered subsets 索引；indicator 表示该三点间的三条 edges 是否全部出现。若按六种排列索引，同一 triangle 会重复六次。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Index unordered three-vertex subsets. The indicator records whether all three edges among those vertices are present. Indexing the six vertex orders would count every triangle six times.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 若 $E[X]=0.2$ 且 $X$ 是非负整数，关于 $P(X\ge1)$ 能保证什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If $E[X]=0.2$ and $X$ is a nonnegative integer, what can be guaranteed about $P(X\ge1)$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> Markov 给 $P(X\ge1)\le0.2$。不能仅从均值得到 exact probability，也不能断言它等于 0.2。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Markov gives $P(X\ge1)\le0.2$. The exact probability cannot be recovered from the mean alone, and it need not equal 0.2.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### birthday pair indicators pairwise independent，为什么仍不能称整组 mutually independent？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why are birthday-pair indicators not mutually independent even though they are pairwise independent?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> $B_1=B_2$ 与 $B_1=B_3$ 同时发生会强迫 $B_2=B_3$；三事件的联合概率不能分解为三个边际概率乘积。pairwise 检查没有看到这个三方约束。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If both $B_1=B_2$ and $B_1=B_3$ occur, then $B_2=B_3$ is forced. The three-way joint probability does not factor into the product of three marginals; pairwise checks do not detect this constraint.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### $X=\sum_jI_j$ 后，什么时候必须继续计算 covariances？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*After writing $X=\sum_jI_j$, when must covariances be calculated?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 只求 $E[X]$ 时不需要；要求 $\operatorname{Var}(X)$、Chebyshev bound 或其他依赖二阶波动的结论时，需要证明 pairwise covariance 为 0 或把非零项保留下来。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> They are unnecessary for $E[X]$ alone. For $\operatorname{Var}(X)$, a Chebyshev bound, or another second-order result, prove that pairwise covariances vanish or retain and calculate the nonzero terms.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
