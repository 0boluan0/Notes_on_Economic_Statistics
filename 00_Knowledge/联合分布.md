# 联合分布为多个随机变量的同时取值事件分配概率

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A joint distribution assigns probabilities to simultaneous values of random variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$(X,Y)$ 的联合分布是这个随机向量在数值平面上的概率测度。其联合 CDF 为 $F_{X,Y}(x,y)=P(X\le x,Y\le y)$。离散时以 $p_{x,y}=P(X=x,Y=y)$ 表示，要求 $p_{x,y}\ge0$ 且 $\sum_x\sum_y p_{x,y}=1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The joint law is the probability measure of a random vector. Its joint CDF accumulates probability over a lower-left rectangle; a discrete joint PMF assigns nonnegative masses summing to one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若联合密度存在，区域 $B$ 的概率为 $\iint_B f_{X,Y}(x,y)dxdy$；联合密度的存在不是定义联合分布的前提。联合分布同时包含每个变量的边际信息及它们怎样配对的依赖信息。两变量各自有密度也不保证存在二维联合密度，例如 $Y=X$ 的概率集中在直线上。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a joint density exists, regional probabilities are its integrals. A joint law need not have a density: even two marginally continuous variables may lie on a line such as Y=X. The joint law contains both marginal and dependence information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
