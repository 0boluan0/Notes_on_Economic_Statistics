# Joint distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*联合分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **joint distribution** describes how likely combinations of outcomes are. When two [[随机变量|random variables]] $X$ and $Y$ record two aspects of the same experiment, it tells us the probabilities of statements about both together—for example, that it rains **and** a person carries an umbrella. Knowing only the probability of rain and the probability of carrying an umbrella does not yet tell us how often they occur together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**联合分布**描述多个结果组合起来有多大概率出现。如果两个[[随机变量]] $X$ 和 $Y$ 记录同一次试验的两个方面，它就告诉我们，同时涉及二者的事情有多大概率发生，例如“下雨**并且**某人带了伞”。只知道下雨的概率和带伞的概率，还不能知道二者有多大概率同时发生。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One cell describes one combination
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一个格子对应一种组合*

> [!example] Rain and an umbrella · 下雨与带伞
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a toy model for a chosen day, let $X=1$ mean rain and $X=0$ mean no rain. Let $Y=1$ mean the person carries an umbrella and $Y=0$ mean they do not. The table assigns probabilities to all four combinations; rows give $X$, columns give $Y$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在一个选取某天的示意模型里，$X=1$ 表示下雨，$X=0$ 表示没下雨；$Y=1$ 表示这个人带了伞，$Y=0$ 表示没带伞。下表为四种组合分配概率：行表示 $X$，列表示 $Y$。
>
> | $X\backslash Y$ | $Y=0$ | $Y=1$ |
> |---|---:|---:|
> | $X=0$ | $0.45$ | $0.15$ |
> | $X=1$ | $0.10$ | $0.30$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bottom-right cell says $P(X=1,Y=1)=0.30$: rain and an umbrella occur together with probability $0.30$. Here $P(\cdot)$ means “the probability that ...”, and the comma means **and**. The cell $0.15$ instead describes no rain together with an umbrella. Each day belongs to exactly one cell, so the four probabilities must add to one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

右下角的格子表示 $P(X=1,Y=1)=0.30$，也就是下雨且带伞的概率为 $0.30$。这里 $P(\cdot)$ 表示括号中事情发生的概率，逗号表示“**并且**”。数值 $0.15$ 的格子则表示没下雨却带了伞。每一天恰好落在其中一个格子里，因此四个概率必须相加为一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To answer a question covering several cells, add their probabilities. “It rains or the person carries an umbrella, or both” covers every cell except $(0,0)$, so its probability is $0.15+0.10+0.30=0.55$. The table works because it keeps the different combinations separate before we add the ones we need.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果一个问题包含多个格子，就把这些格子的概率相加。“下雨或带伞，或两者都有”包括除了 $(0,0)$ 以外的三个格子，所以概率为 $0.15+0.10+0.30=0.55$。这张表先把不同组合区分开来，使我们能按问题选取需要的格子。

> [!abstract] Joint probability mass function · 联合概率质量函数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For discrete values, write $p_{x,y}=P(X=x,Y=y)$. This is the two-variable version of a [[概率质量函数|probability mass function]]. Lowercase $x,y$ stand for particular possible values; uppercase $X,Y$ name the random variables. A valid table satisfies
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对离散取值，记 $p_{x,y}=P(X=x,Y=y)$；这就是[[概率质量函数]]的双变量形式。小写 $x,y$ 表示具体的可能取值，大写 $X,Y$ 表示随机变量。一张有效的表必须满足
>
> $$p_{x,y}\ge0,\qquad \sum_x\sum_y p_{x,y}=1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The two sums mean to include every possible pair, just as we included all four cells above.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个求和符号表示要把所有可能的取值对都包括进来，就像上面把四个格子全部相加一样。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the separate distributions leave out
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*分别看两个变量会遗漏什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding the bottom row gives the probability of rain, $0.10+0.30=0.40$. Adding the right-hand column gives the probability of an umbrella, $0.15+0.30=0.45$. These are probabilities from the two [[边际分布|marginal distributions]], which describe each variable on its own. The joint distribution also retains how the outcomes are paired. In this example the probability of both is $0.30$, whereas multiplying $0.40$ by $0.45$ gives $0.18$; the variables are therefore not [[随机变量独立|independent]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把下面一行相加，得到下雨概率 $0.10+0.30=0.40$；把右边一列相加，得到带伞概率 $0.15+0.30=0.45$。这两个概率分别属于各自的[[边际分布]]，只描述单个变量。联合分布还保留了结果怎样配对的信息：这里两者同时发生的概率是 $0.30$，而 $0.40$ 乘 $0.45$ 得到 $0.18$，所以这两个变量并不[[随机变量独立|独立]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The loss of information when we keep only the marginals is explored in [[边际分布不定联合分布|marginal distributions do not determine the joint distribution]]. Independence is an additional property of a joint distribution, not a requirement for having one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只保留边际分布会丢失哪些信息，见[[边际分布不定联合分布]]。独立性是联合分布可能具有的一种额外性质，定义联合分布本身并不要求独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Beyond a finite table
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*不局限于有限的概率表*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The pair $(X,Y)$ is a [[随机向量|random vector]]: one outcome supplies both coordinates. Its joint distribution assigns probabilities to regions of the coordinate plane. One way to describe it for any real-valued $X,Y$ is the joint [[累积分布函数|cumulative distribution function]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数对 $(X,Y)$ 是一个[[随机向量]]：一次结果同时给出两个坐标。它的联合分布为坐标平面上的区域分配概率。对于任意实值的 $X,Y$，都可以用联合[[累积分布函数]]来描述：

$$F_{X,Y}(x,y)=P(X\le x,Y\le y).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each pair of thresholds $x,y$, this asks for the probability that both coordinates are at or below their thresholds—the region extending left and down from $(x,y)$. It describes the distribution even when no joint density exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定两个门槛 $x,y$，这个函数询问的是：两个坐标分别不超过各自门槛的概率是多少？几何上，就是从 $(x,y)$ 向左、向下延伸的区域。这种描述不要求联合密度存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a joint [[概率密度函数|density]] $f_{X,Y}$ does exist, probabilities come from accumulating density over an area. For a region $B$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当联合[[概率密度函数|密度]] $f_{X,Y}$ 确实存在时，可以通过累积区域内的密度来得到概率。对一个区域 $B$，

$$P((X,Y)\in B)=\iint_B f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dx\,dy.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The double integral adds contributions across both coordinates; the density is nonnegative and its integral over the whole plane is one. As with a one-variable density, the height at a point is not that point's probability. The underlying idea of accumulation is explained in [[定积分|the definite integral]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二重积分沿两个坐标累积各处的贡献；密度非负，在整个平面上的积分为一。和单变量密度一样，一个点上的密度高度并不是该点的概率。积分如何表达累积，见[[定积分]]。

> [!warning] Continuous marginals do not guarantee a joint density · 连续边际不保证有联合密度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X$ be [[连续均匀分布|uniform on $(0,1)$]] and set $Y=X$. Both variables have a one-dimensional density, but the pair lies entirely on the diagonal line $y=x$. That line has area zero while carrying probability one. A two-dimensional density cannot put probability one on a zero-area line, so this joint distribution has no such density.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $X$ 在 $(0,1)$ 上服从[[连续均匀分布]]，并令 $Y=X$。两个变量各自都有一维密度，但数对完全落在对角线 $y=x$ 上：这条线的面积为零，却承载了全部概率。二维密度无法把概率一分配给零面积的线，因此这个联合分布没有这样的密度。
