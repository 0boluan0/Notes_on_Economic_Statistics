# Convex transforms of martingales
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*凸变换产生次鞅*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fair game has no predictable gain in its current score, but a curved measure of that score can behave differently. Squaring emphasizes large departures from zero. Even when the next score has conditional mean equal to today's score, its **mean square** can be larger than today's squared score. This is why a convex transformation of a martingale can produce a submartingale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公平游戏的当前得分没有可预测的收益，但把得分经过弯曲的函数变换后，表现可以不同。平方会强调偏离零较远的值：即使下一步得分的条件均值等于今天的得分，下一步的**平方均值**也可能大于今天得分的平方。这就是鞅经过凸变换后可以成为次鞅的原因。

> [!example] One fair step, viewed through a square · 把公平的一步拿来平方
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose the current score is $m$. The next step adds $1$ or $-1$ with equal conditional probabilities, given everything observed so far. The mean next score is $m$, whereas its mean square is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设当前得分为 $m$。给定目前全部已知信息，下一步以各半条件概率加 $1$ 或减 $1$。下一步得分的平均是 $m$，平方的平均却是
>
> $$\frac{(m+1)^2+(m-1)^2}{2}=m^2+1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $m=1$, the next square is $4$ or $0$, with mean $2$, compared with today's square $1$. The conditional average rises although an actual path can go down. The conclusion concerns prediction from present information, not an increasing sample path.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $m=1$ 时，下一步平方为 $4$ 或 $0$，平均是 $2$，而今天平方为 $1$。条件平均上升了，但实际路径仍可能下降。这里说的是使用当前信息作预测，不是每条路径都递增。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formal starting point is a [[鞅定义|martingale]] $M=(M_t)$: an adapted, integrable process whose future conditional mean is its current value. A [[滤过定义|filtration]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ records the information available at each time. A [[次鞅|submartingale]] replaces the equality of conditional means by “future conditional mean is at least the current value”, while still requiring adaptedness and integrability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正式的起点是[[鞅定义|鞅]] $M=(M_t)$：它适应于已知信息，每个时点可积，未来值的条件均值等于当前值。[[滤过定义|滤过]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ 记录每个时点能够使用的信息。[[次鞅]]把条件均值的等式改成“未来条件均值不小于当前值”，但仍要求适应性和可积性。

> [!important] The transformation theorem · 凸变换定理
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $M$ be a real martingale relative to $(\mathcal F_t)$. Let $\varphi:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ be finite-valued and [[凹凸性|convex]], and assume $E|\varphi(M_t)|<\infty$ for every time $t$. Then $(\varphi(M_t))$ is a submartingale relative to the same filtration. No monotonicity of $\varphi$ is required in this martingale case.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设实过程 $M$ 是相对于 $(\mathcal F_t)$ 的鞅，$\varphi:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$ 是有限值[[凹凸性|凸函数]]，并且每个时点 $t$ 都满足 $E|\varphi(M_t)|<\infty$。那么 $(\varphi(M_t))$ 是相对于同一滤过的次鞅。起点是鞅时，不要求 $\varphi$ 单调。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Conditional Jensen supplies the direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 条件 Jensen 给出方向

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix two times $s\le t$. [[条件Jensen不等式|Conditional Jensen's inequality]] compares transforming the conditional mean with taking the conditional mean of the transform. The martingale property then identifies that mean with $M_s$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定两个时点 $s\le t$。[[条件Jensen不等式]]比较“条件均值的变换”和“变换的条件均值”，再由鞅性质把条件均值识别为 $M_s$：

$$
E\!\left[\varphi(M_t)\mid\mathcal F_s\right]
\ge \varphi\!\left(E[M_t\mid\mathcal F_s]\right)
=\varphi(M_s)\quad\text{almost surely}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each fixed pair of times, the displayed inequality holds outside a probability-zero exceptional set. It is exactly the required submartingale inequality. The other two conditions also hold: integrability was assumed, and applying a continuous finite convex function to an $\mathcal F_t$-measurable variable preserves measurability, so the transformed process remains [[适应过程|adapted]]. The argument applies to discrete time, or to each fixed pair in a continuous time index.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每一对固定时点，上式都在某个零概率异常集之外成立，正是次鞅要求的不等式。另外两个条件也满足：可积性已假设；有限凸函数在实数轴上连续，对 $\mathcal F_t$ 可测的变量作此变换仍可测，所以过程依然[[适应过程|适应于该滤过]]。论证适用于离散时间，也适用于连续时间指标中的每一对固定时点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Absolute value and square
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 两个常用特例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\varphi(x)=|x|$, the integrability condition is automatic: every martingale already has $E|M_t|<\infty$. Thus $|M_t|$ is a submartingale. The absolute-value function is convex despite its corner at zero; no derivative is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $\varphi(x)=|x|$，可积性条件自动满足，因为鞅本来就要求 $E|M_t|<\infty$。因此 $|M_t|$ 是次鞅。绝对值函数虽然在零点有尖角，仍是凸函数，不需要导数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\varphi(x)=x^2$, we additionally need $E[M_t^2]<\infty$ at every time. Under this [[矩存在性的使用边界|second-moment condition]], $M_t^2$ is a submartingale. A martingale's finite first absolute moment does not supply that second moment. More generally, $|M_t|^p$ works for a fixed $p\ge1$ when its expectation is finite at every time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $\varphi(x)=x^2$，则要另查每个时点是否满足 $E[M_t^2]<\infty$。在这个[[矩存在性的使用边界|二阶矩条件]]下，$M_t^2$ 才由定理保证为次鞅。鞅的一阶绝对矩有限，不自动提供二阶矩。更一般地，固定 $p\ge1$，若每个时点的 $E|M_t|^p$ 有限，$|M_t|^p$ 也成为次鞅。

> [!warning] Convexity and integrability do different jobs · 凸性与可积性承担不同任务
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Convexity supplies the inequality's direction. Integrability ensures the transformed process belongs to the class in the submartingale definition. A fast-growing convex function can turn an integrable $M_t$ into a nonintegrable variable, so checking only the curve's shape is insufficient.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 凸性提供不等式方向；可积性保证变换后的过程属于次鞅定义讨论的对象。增长很快的凸函数可能把可积的 $M_t$ 变成不可积变量，因此只检查曲线形状还不够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## If the starting process is already a submartingale
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*起点若只是次鞅*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a submartingale $X$, the corresponding guarantee uses a **nondecreasing convex** $\varphi$, with $E|\varphi(X_t)|<\infty$ at every time. The extra monotonicity lets us pass the existing conditional-mean inequality through the function:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若起点是次鞅 $X$，相应的通用保证使用**单调不减的凸函数** $\varphi$，仍要求每个时点 $E|\varphi(X_t)|<\infty$。额外的单调性允许把已有的条件均值不等式传入函数：

$$
E[\varphi(X_t)\mid\mathcal F_s]
\ge\varphi(E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s])
\ge\varphi(X_s)\quad\text{almost surely}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first inequality uses convexity; the second uses monotonicity and the submartingale property. To see why convexity alone gives no general guarantee here, take the deterministic submartingale $X_n=n$ and the convex decreasing function $\varphi(x)=-x$. Its transform is $-n$, whose next value is smaller, so it is not a submartingale. This does not say that a decreasing convex function fails for every process: when the starting process is a martingale, even $-M_t$ is again a martingale by conditional linearity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一步用凸性，第二步用单调性及次鞅性质。仅有凸性不能提供这一通用保证：确定性过程 $X_n=n$ 是次鞅，但凸且递减的函数 $\varphi(x)=-x$ 把它变成 $-n$，下一步反而更小，因而不是次鞅。这并不表示递减凸函数对每个过程都会失败：如果起点是鞅，条件期望的线性性还会使 $-M_t$ 本身仍为鞅。
