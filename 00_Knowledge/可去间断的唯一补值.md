# Repairing a removable discontinuity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*可去间断的唯一补值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sometimes nearby outputs already agree on where a graph should arrive; only the value at the joining point is missing or misplaced. A **removable discontinuity** is a defect of this kind. Filling that point with the nearby finite [[函数极限|limit]] makes the function [[一点连续|continuous at that point]]. The limit determines the filling uniquely, provided the domain really has other points approaching it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有时，附近输出已经指向同一个位置，只是连接点上的函数值缺失或放错了。**可去间断**就是这种缺口。把这一点补成附近的有限[[函数极限|极限值]]，就能使函数[[一点连续|在这一点连续]]。只要定义域中确实有其他点不断靠近它，这个补值就由极限唯一决定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A hole in an otherwise simple formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $f(x)=(x^2-1)/(x-1)$ for real $x\ne1$. Factoring the numerator gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑实数 $x\ne1$ 上的 $f(x)=(x^2-1)/(x-1)$。分解分子，得到

$$f(x)=\frac{(x-1)(x+1)}{x-1}=x+1\qquad(x\ne1).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cancellation is valid only away from $1$: it does not assign a value to the original fraction at $1$. Inputs $0.99$ and $1.01$ give outputs $1.99$ and $2.01$. More decisively, $|f(x)-2|=|x-1|$ for every $x\ne1$, so every requested output tolerance is met by the same input tolerance. Thus the limit at $1$ is $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

约分只在 $x\ne1$ 时合法，并没有替原来的分式定义 $x=1$ 处的值。输入 $0.99,1.01$ 分别得到 $1.99,2.01$。更关键的是，对每个 $x\ne1$ 都有 $|f(x)-2|=|x-1|$：给定任何输出容差，取同样大小的输入容差即可。因此在 $1$ 处的极限是 $2$。

> [!example] The only successful fill · 唯一成功的补值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Define $g(1)=2$ and keep $g(x)=f(x)$ for $x\ne1$. Then $g(x)=x+1$ everywhere, so it is continuous at $1$. Setting $g(1)=100$ leaves the limit equal to $2$ and fails continuity. Leaving the point undefined leaves a hole in the domain. These are different point-value choices for the same nearby behaviour.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 定义 $g(1)=2$，其余点保持 $g(x)=f(x)$，就得到处处等于 $x+1$ 的函数，因此它在 $1$ 连续。若改为 $g(1)=100$，附近极限仍为 $2$，无法连续；若仍不定义，就保留了定义域中的缺口。这些点值选择不同，附近行为却相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the filling exists and is unique
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么能补，而且只能这样补*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $D\subseteq\mathbb R$ be the allowed inputs other than the target point $a$, so $a\notin D$. Assume $a$ is a **cluster point of $D$**: there are points of $D$ arbitrarily close to $a$. Start with $f:D\to\mathbb R$. For a proposed real value $c$, define $g_c$ on $D\cup\{a\}$ by keeping $f$ on $D$ and setting $g_c(a)=c$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $D\subseteq\mathbb R$ 是目标点 $a$ 以外的合法输入，故 $a\notin D$。假设 $a$ 是 **$D$ 的聚点**：无论把距离缩得多小，附近都有 $D$ 中的点。原函数是 $f:D\to\mathbb R$。给定候选实数补值 $c$，在 $D\cup\{a\}$ 上定义 $g_c$：在 $D$ 上保持 $f$ 不变，在 $a$ 处令 $g_c(a)=c$。

> [!important] The extension criterion · 补值判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under these assumptions,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在上述条件下，
>
> $$g_c\text{ is continuous at }a
> \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> \lim_{\substack{x\to a\\x\in D}}f(x)=c\in\mathbb R.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consequently, a finite limit $L$ gives exactly one successful value, $c=L$. If the finite limit does not exist, no real point value can make the extension continuous at $a$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因此，有限极限 $L$ 给出唯一成功的选择 $c=L$；若不存在有限极限，就没有任何实数点值能使补值后的函数在 $a$ 连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove sufficiency, take any output tolerance $\varepsilon>0$. The limit gives a distance $\delta>0$ such that $x\in D$ and $|x-a|<\delta$ imply $|f(x)-L|<\varepsilon$. At the newly added point itself, $|g_L(a)-L|=0$. Together these check every input in the enlarged domain, exactly the definition of continuity. Conversely, continuity of $g_c$ gives the same bound on the subset $D$, proving that its nearby limit is $c$. The cluster-point assumption makes the finite limit unique: if $c\ne L$, take $\varepsilon=|c-L|/3$. There is a domain input close enough to satisfy both proposed limit bounds, which would force $|c-L|<2\varepsilon<|c-L|$, a contradiction. Thus two different successful fillings are impossible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先证补成 $L$ 就够了。任取输出容差 $\varepsilon>0$，极限给出距离 $\delta>0$，使 $x\in D$ 且 $|x-a|<\delta$ 时有 $|f(x)-L|<\varepsilon$。新添的点本身又满足 $|g_L(a)-L|=0$。两部分合起来，覆盖了扩大后的定义域内所有附近输入，恰好就是连续性定义。反过来，若 $g_c$ 连续，把连续性不等式限制在 $D$ 上，就证明附近极限是 $c$。聚点条件还保证有限极限唯一：若 $c\ne L$，取 $\varepsilon=|c-L|/3$，就能找到足够近的定义域输入，同时满足两个候选极限的容差要求，进而推出 $|c-L|<2\varepsilon<|c-L|$，矛盾。因此不可能有两个不同的成功补值。

## Domain and point-value boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the original function already has a value at $a$, apply the criterion after temporarily restricting it to inputs other than $a$. [[点值不决定极限|Changing only that point]] leaves all the tested nearby values unchanged. If its value was already $L$, it was continuous there and needs no repair. If the point was outside the original domain, “removable discontinuity” is the common calculus name for a hole admitting this extension; continuity at $a$ is a property of the enlarged function, not of the original function at a point where it was undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若原函数已经在 $a$ 有值，先暂时只看 $a$ 以外的输入，再用这个判据。[[点值不决定极限|只改一个点]]不会改变正在检验的附近值。若原值本就等于 $L$，它在那里已经连续，无须修复。若原定义域没有 $a$，微积分中常把可这样补上的洞也称为“可去间断”；在 $a$ 连续，是扩大定义域后的函数的性质，并不是原函数在未定义点上已有的性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both-sided input is unnecessary for the extension theorem: if $D=(0,1)$ and $a=0$, the domain-relative limit uses only the right side. In contrast, the cluster-point assumption is essential for uniqueness. For $D=\{1\}$ and $a=0$, the new point is isolated; any real $g(0)$ gives continuity at $0$ by choosing $\delta<1$. Finally, the theorem repairs continuity only at the chosen point. It does not assert that $f$ was continuous at the other inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

补值定理不要求两侧都有输入：若 $D=(0,1)$、$a=0$，相对于定义域的极限只从右侧检验。相反，聚点条件对唯一性很关键。若 $D=\{1\}$、$a=0$，新点就是孤立点；无论把 $g(0)$ 设成哪个实数，取 $\delta<1$ 都能保证在 $0$ 连续。最后，定理只修复所选点的连续性，并没有断言原函数在其他输入处也连续。
