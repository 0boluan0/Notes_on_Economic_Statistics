# 总量排放交易是在总量约束下允许排放配额转让并要求按排放量缴回配额的制度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cap and trade sets an aggregate emissions limit, permits allowance transfers and requires allowances to be surrendered against emissions.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总量排放交易（cap-and-trade）先为覆盖的排放源和履约期间设定总预算，再把预算表示为可转让配额；排放源须依据核定排放缴回对应数量的有效配额。交易让企业调整谁实际排放、谁承担减排，而不因每一次企业间转让就增加配额总预算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cap and trade sets an emissions budget for covered sources and a compliance period, represents it with transferable allowances, and requires sources to surrender valid allowances against verified emissions. Trading changes who emits and who abates without enlarging the total allowance budget through each transfer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最简单的单期版本中，一份配额覆盖一单位同种污染物，配额总数为 $L$。没有跨期结转或借用、外部信用、额外安全阀供给，也没有政府另行收回配额；全部排放完整计量并履约时，各家缴回义务合起来给出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simple single-period version, one allowance covers one unit of the same pollutant and total allowances equal $L$. Exclude banking, borrowing, external credits, additional safety-valve supply and government withdrawal. Complete measurement and compliance imply
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sum_i e_i\le L.
$$

这只是上限，不保证一定等号：需求不足、持有者主动不用或其他限制都可能使排放更低。正价格且没有多余持有动机的基础竞争模型中，总量约束通常绑定。若加入跨期或混合设计，就必须按实际规则重新定义可用预算，不能保留未经调整的单期结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an upper bound, not a guaranteed equality: weak demand, voluntary non-use or other restrictions can leave emissions below it. The cap binds in the basic competitive model with a positive price and no reason to hold surplus allowances. Banking or hybrid designs require redefining the available budget under their actual rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如配额总数100，初始两家各50；第一家转让25给第二家后，持有量变为25和75，合计仍100。若核定排放也是25和75，分别缴回便完成该期配额义务。谁愿意如何减排由[[共同排放价格与减排分担]]解释；这一定义本身既没有指定最优总量，也没有保证交易没有摩擦。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With one hundred allowances initially split equally, a transfer of twenty-five changes holdings to twenty-five and seventy-five while preserving the total. Verified emissions of those amounts can be covered by surrender. [[共同排放价格与减排分担|The common-price mechanism]] explains abatement choices; the definition does not establish an optimal cap or frictionless trading.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

配额的污染物、适用期间和空间范围必须明确；一份配额不是一项不限地点、不限时期的排污权。实际执行见[[排放监测与履约]]，局地边界见[[排放总量与局地损害]]。项目相对基准产生的减排信用属于另一种对象，见[[排放配额与减排信用]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify the pollutant, eligible period and spatial scope: an allowance is not an unrestricted right to pollute anywhere forever. See [[排放监测与履约|monitoring and compliance]], [[排放总量与局地损害|local-damage boundaries]] and [[排放配额与减排信用|allowances versus project credits]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
