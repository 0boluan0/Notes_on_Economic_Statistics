# Minimum-norm least-squares solution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*最小范数最小二乘解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[线性最小二乘|least-squares problem]] first asks for the closest attainable fit. Several coefficient vectors may produce that same fit. The **minimum-norm least-squares solution** then chooses the shortest coefficient vector among those tied solutions. The order matters: it never accepts a worse fit merely to shorten the coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[线性最小二乘]]先寻找最接近目标的可达拟合；多个系数向量可能产生同一个最佳拟合。**最小范数最小二乘解**再从这些并列最优的系数中选出最短的一个。顺序很重要：它不会为了缩短系数而接受更差的拟合。

> [!example] Equal fits, different coefficient lengths / 拟合相同，系数长度不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix}$ and target $b=(2,3)^T$. For real $t$, every input $x=(1+t,1-t)^T$ produces $(2,0)^T$, so every one has the same minimal residual length 3. Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]] and $\|\cdot\|_2$ is [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]]. Their coefficient lengths differ:
>
> $$\|x\|_2^2=(1+t)^2+(1-t)^2=2+2t^2.$$
>
> Only $t=0$ minimizes this second quantity. The chosen input is $(1,1)^T$, while $(2,0)^T$ gives the same fit with larger coefficient norm. The zero input has even smaller coefficient norm, but worse squared fitting error $13$ instead of $9$, so it is not eligible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取上述 $A,b$，对实数 $t$，所有 $x=(1+t,1-t)^T$ 都给出拟合 $(2,0)^T$，最小残差长度均为3；但系数范数平方为 $2+2t^2$，仅在 $t=0$ 最小。因此选出 $(1,1)^T$，而 $(2,0)^T$ 虽拟合相同，系数却更长。零输入虽然更短，拟合误差平方却从9变成13，因此不在可选集合中。$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，$\|\cdot\|_2$ 是[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be any finite real or complex $m\times n$ matrix, $m,n\ge1$, and let $b$ have $m$ coordinates. For the usual Euclidean norm, the unique choice is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $A$ 为任意有限维实或复 $m\times n$ 矩阵，$m,n\ge1$，目标 $b$ 含 $m$ 个坐标。使用通常欧氏范数时，唯一的这种选择为

$$
x_\star=A^+b,
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $A^+$ is the [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse]]. No full-rank assumption is required. For complex coordinates, squared length means the sum of squared absolute values, and $^*$ below denotes conjugate transpose.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $A^+$ 是 [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆]]。不需要满秩条件。复数坐标的长度平方为各坐标绝对值的平方和，下文 $^*$ 表示共轭转置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## First obtain the nearest output
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么它先是最小二乘解

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[列空间|column space]] $C(A)$ consists of every output $Ax$ that the matrix can produce. The [[AA+列空间投影|column-space projection identity]] says that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[列空间]] $C(A)$ 包含矩阵能够产生的全部输出 $Ax$。[[AA+列空间投影]]告诉我们

$$
Ax_\star=AA^+b=P_{C(A)}b=p,
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $P_{C(A)}b$ is the [[正交投影|orthogonal projection]] of $b$: its unique nearest vector in that subspace. The residual $r=b-p$ is perpendicular to every vector in $C(A)$. For any coefficient vector $x$, the difference $Ax-p$ lies in that subspace, so the [[最佳逼近与正交残差|perpendicular-error decomposition]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$P_{C(A)}b$ 是 $b$ 的[[正交投影]]，即该子空间内唯一的最近向量。残差 $r=b-p$ 垂直于整个 $C(A)$。任意系数 $x$ 所产生的 $Ax-p$ 都在列空间中，因此[[最佳逼近与正交残差|垂直误差分解]]给出

$$
\|Ax-b\|_2^2=\|Ax-p\|_2^2+\|r\|_2^2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second term cannot be removed; the first is zero at $x_\star$. Every least-squares solution must therefore give exactly the same fitted vector $p$, even when its coefficients differ. If $b$ is already attainable, the residual is zero and the problem reduces to finding the shortest exact solution of $Ax=b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二项无法消除，第一项在 $x_\star$ 处为零。所以每个最小二乘解都必须产生同一个拟合向量 $p$，即使系数不同。若 $b$ 本来可达，残差为零，问题便变成寻找 $Ax=b$ 的最短精确解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Then remove invisible input directions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么它的范数唯一最小

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[零空间|nullspace]] $N(A)$ consists of vectors $z$ with $Az=0$: adding one to an input cannot change its output. Since all optimal coefficients give the same fit, [[特解加零空间|one particular solution plus the nullspace]] describes them all:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[零空间]] $N(A)$ 包含满足 $Az=0$ 的向量 $z$，向输入添加它不会改变输出。全部最优系数产生同一拟合，所以用[[特解加零空间]]就能写全它们：

$$
x=A^+b+z,\qquad z\in N(A).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[A+A行空间投影|input-space projection identity]] and $A^+AA^+=A^+$ place $A^+b$ in $C(A^*)=N(A)^\perp$, the space perpendicular to every invisible direction. This is the row space in real coordinates. Consequently, the two parts of $x$ are perpendicular and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[A+A行空间投影]]和 $A^+AA^+=A^+$，可知 $A^+b$ 属于 $C(A^*)=N(A)^\perp$，即垂直于全部不可见方向的空间；实数域中就是行空间。因此 $x$ 的两部分正交，有

$$
\|A^+b+z\|_2^2=\|A^+b\|_2^2+\|z\|_2^2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The extra squared length is nonnegative and vanishes only when $z=0$. This proves both minimality and uniqueness. In the opening example, $(1,1)^T$ is perpendicular to the invisible direction $(1,-1)^T$, and varying $t$ only adds that invisible component. Nothing in the observations identifies its true value; minimum norm is an explicit choice among indistinguishable fits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

额外的长度平方非负，且仅在 $z=0$ 时为零，因此同时证明了最小性与唯一性。开头的 $(1,1)^T$ 垂直于不可见方向 $(1,-1)^T$，改变 $t$ 只会添加该分量。观测无法确定它的真实值；最小范数是在无法由拟合区分的输入之间作出的明确选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the exact theorem guarantees
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 精确结论的边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A$ has full column rank, its nullspace contains only zero, so the ordinary least-squares coefficient vector is already unique. If $A=0$, every $x$ gives the same fit zero, and the minimum-norm choice is $x_\star=0$. Neither case requires $Ax=b$ to be consistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A$ 满列秩，零空间只有零向量，普通最小二乘系数已经唯一。若 $A=0$，每个 $x$ 都给出零拟合，最小范数选择为 $x_\star=0$。这两种结论都不要求 $Ax=b$ 有精确解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The norm is part of the question. Changing coefficient units or applying a non-orthonormal reparameterisation can change which input is shortest. A weighted coefficient norm is another selection rule. Also, [[岭正则化|ridge regularisation]] with a positive penalty generally trades fit against coefficient size; it is not the same two-stage optimization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

范数本身是问题的一部分。改变系数单位，或采用不保持内积的重参数化，都可能改变哪个输入最短；加权系数范数也是另一种选解规则。正惩罚强度的[[岭正则化]]通常在拟合与系数大小之间折中，也不同于这里分先后执行的两阶段最优化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For instance, rewrite $x_1=2w_1$, $x_2=w_2$ in the opening example. The same best-fit condition becomes $2w_1+w_2=2$. Minimizing $w_1^2+w_2^2$ selects $w=(4/5,2/5)^T$, hence $x=(8/5,2/5)^T$, rather than $(1,1)^T$. The fit is unchanged; the length being minimized has changed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如把开头例子重写为 $x_1=2w_1$、$x_2=w_2$，同一最佳拟合条件变成 $2w_1+w_2=2$。最小化 $w_1^2+w_2^2$ 会选出 $w=(4/5,2/5)^T$，对应 $x=(8/5,2/5)^T$，而非 $(1,1)^T$。拟合不变，最小化的长度改变了。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem concerns the original matrix's exact $A^+$. [[伪逆SVD公式|Thresholding a small positive singular value]] instead selects a different matrix $A_\tau$, whose minimum-norm least-squares solution is $A_\tau^+b$. Even an exact unique choice can be [[小奇异值放大噪声|sensitive to noise]]. In computation, use a suitable [[秩亏最小二乘算法|rank-aware solver]] and state any tolerance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定理针对原矩阵的精确 $A^+$。[[伪逆SVD公式|用阈值删去很小的正奇异值]]会改为另一个矩阵 $A_\tau$，它的最小范数最小二乘解是 $A_\tau^+b$。即使精确选择唯一，也可能[[小奇异值放大噪声|对噪声敏感]]。计算时应使用适当的[[秩亏最小二乘算法]]，并声明所用容差。

- [LAPACK, linear least squares](https://netlib.org/lapack/lug/node27.html) and [error bounds](https://netlib.org/lapack/lug/node83.html): full-rank/rank-deficient solver scope and explicit thresholded-rank models. The equal-sum and coordinate-rescaling examples are direct minimizations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Axler 第6.67–6.70条核对伪逆、全部最小化解以及唯一最小范数选择；正文将其两个正交分解证明写成矩阵形式。
- MIT Session 3.8 第1–2页核对行空间与列空间之间的逆作用。
- LAPACK 核对满秩／秩亏求解范围与阈值秩模型；和约束及坐标缩放算例由直接最优化核验。
