# 充足准备金框架主要通过管理利率引导短期市场利率并使其对小幅准备金波动不敏感
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ample-reserves framework mainly steers short-term market rates with administered rates while keeping them insensitive to modest reserve fluctuations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“充足”不是一个固定金额，而是准备金供给相对于需求的位置：再增加或减少少量准备金，隔夜利率都不会发生明显变化。在准备金供需图上，供给与需求的近乎平坦部分相交；额外余额的主要边际价值接近央行付息等可用投资选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Ample” describes supply relative to demand, not a fixed balance-sheet number. A modest addition or withdrawal does not materially change the overnight rate. Supply intersects the near-flat part of reserve demand, where marginal balances are valued mainly against remuneration and other available investment options.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设需求区间不变，准备金从120增到125，成交利率仍接近原管理利率。这不表示央行失去利率控制：它可以改变[[央行管理利率]]，从而改变持有余额与市场投资的相对回报。数量与价格可以在一定范围内分别调整，和[[利率走廊]]中靠稀缺性微调市场利率的方式不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With demand unchanged, reserves might rise from 120 to 125 while the traded rate stays close to the existing administered rate. Rate control remains possible by changing [[央行管理利率|administered returns]]. Quantity and price can vary separately over a range, unlike scarcity-based fine-tuning in a [[利率走廊|corridor]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

准备金供给减少过多，或准备金需求上升，都可能让原来的数量不再充足。故“无需每天精调供给”不等于“永远不用补充准备金”。账户和持有人口径复用[[央行准备金余额]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Large withdrawals or an increase in reserve demand can make a previously sufficient stock inadequate. Avoiding daily fine-tuning does not mean reserves never need replenishing. Reuse [[央行准备金余额|reserve balances]] for the account and holder perimeter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The FEDS model defines ample supply by rate sensitivity. The Fed's operations account supports replenishment when needed; 120 and 125 are illustrative units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
