# Lucas 型线性积累以当期学习时间换取未来技能并维持固定比例回报
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Lucas-style linear accumulation trades current learning time for future skills while preserving a proportional return to the skill stock.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Lucas 型积累机制把非闲暇时间分为当期生产与学习：$u(t)\in[0,1]$ 用于生产，$1-u(t)$ 用于积累 [[人力资本]]。若个人技能存量为 $h(t)>0$、学习效率为 $\phi>0$，线性积累技术为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Lucas-style mechanism divides non-leisure time between production and learning: $u(t)\in[0,1]$ goes to production and $1-u(t)$ to accumulating [[人力资本|human capital]]. For skill stock $h(t)>0$ and learning productivity $\phi>0$, the linear accumulation technology is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\dot h(t)=\phi[1-u(t)]h(t),\qquad
\frac{\dot h(t)}{h(t)}=\phi[1-u(t)].
$$

给定学习时间份额，同样的努力可持续带来同样百分比的技能增长，而不会因为既有 $h$ 更高就自动降低增长率。若 $u=1$，没有学习且 $h$ 不增长；若 $u=0$，技能增长达到 $\phi$，但没有时间从事当期商品生产。本文只分析给定 $u$ 的机制，不把它当成最优时间选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a given learning share, the same effort sustains the same percentage growth in skills without the growth rate automatically falling as $h$ rises. At $u=1$, no learning occurs; at $u=0$, skill growth reaches $\phi$, but no time remains for current goods production. This card analyses a given $u$, not an optimal allocation of time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当期机会成本可用无外部性的简化生产式表示。设所有劳动者具有相同技能，人数 $L$ 固定，$B>0$、$0<\alpha<1$，物质资本为 $K$，当前有效劳动是 $uhL$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An externality-free production specification makes the current opportunity cost explicit. Workers have identical skills, population $L$ is fixed, $B>0$, $0<\alpha<1$, physical capital is $K$, and effective labour in production is $uhL$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y=BK^\alpha(uhL)^{1-\alpha},\qquad
Y=C+\dot K.
$$

这个简化沿用 Lucas 原式的无物质资本折旧设定。降低 $u$ 会在当前 $K,h,L$ 不变时减少产出，却提高未来技能增长；消费 $C$ 与物质资本投资仍须满足商品资源约束。仅给定 $u$ 尚未决定消费路径，要得到完整均衡还需偏好、消费与时间选择及相应终端条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This simplification retains the absence of physical-capital depreciation in Lucas's original equation. Lowering $u$ reduces current output at fixed $K,h,L$ while increasing future skill growth; consumption and investment must still satisfy the goods constraint. A given $u$ does not determine consumption. A complete equilibrium additionally requires preferences, consumption and time choices, and terminal conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造例子：$\phi=0.08$，按年计，$u=0.75$，则技能按每年 $2\%$ 连续增长，$h(t)=h(0)e^{0.02t}$。若将 $u$ 降到 $0.50$，技能增长率变为每年 $4\%$；但在改变发生时，取 $\alpha=1/3$，产出相对原值降为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a constructed annual example, $\phi=0.08$ and $u=0.75$ yield continuously compounded skill growth of $2\%$, so $h(t)=h(0)e^{0.02t}$. Reducing $u$ to $0.50$ raises skill growth to $4\%$. At the moment of the change, however, with $\alpha=1/3$, output relative to its previous value falls to:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{Y_{\mathrm{new}}}{Y_{\mathrm{old}}}
=\left(\frac{0.50}{0.75}\right)^{2/3}
\approx0.7631.
$$

这些数值展示了时间机会成本，没有证明增加学习时间提高福利。这里学习时间占用当期生产时间；[[学习效应|干中学]] 则强调实践中获得改进，两者是不同的积累渠道。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The numbers expose the opportunity cost of time, without proving that more learning improves welfare. Here learning withdraws time from current production; [[学习效应|learning by doing]] instead concerns improvements acquired through practice. They are distinct accumulation channels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

维持百分比回报依赖对**技能存量**的线性假设。若改为 $\dot h=\phi(1-u)h^\zeta$，$\zeta<1$，则即使学习时间不超过全部时间，也有
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sustained percentage return depends on linearity in the skill stock. If instead $\dot h=\phi(1-u)h^\zeta$ with $\zeta<1$, the available learning time implies:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\dot h}{h}\le\phi h^{\zeta-1}\longrightarrow0
\qquad\text{当 / as }h\to\infty.
$$

所以“投资人力资本”这个名称本身不能保证持久正增长。把个人技能积累延伸为无限寿命家族的积累，还需要技能在代际间传承的条件。Lucas 的更完整模型可以加入平均技能外部性，但线性积累即使没有该外部性也能提供持续增长机制；外部性与最优配置问题需另外分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Calling an activity human-capital investment does not guarantee persistent positive growth. Extending individual accumulation to an infinitely lived family also requires intergenerational transmission. Lucas's fuller model can add average-skill externalities, but linear accumulation can sustain growth without that externality. External effects and optimal allocation require separate analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Lucas（1988），[On the Mechanics of Economic Development](https://web.stanford.edu/~klenow/Lucas_Mechanics.pdf#page=15)，*Journal of Monetary Economics* 22，3–42，印刷第17–20页（PDF第15–18页），式(11)–(13)：支持时间分配、有效劳动、商品资源约束、线性积累及递减存量回报的边界；第19页说明跨代传承问题。第23页（PDF第21页）明确无平均技能外部性时也可持续增长。
- 记号映射：原文学习效率 $\delta$ 改记 $\phi$，生产率 $A$ 改记 $B$，资本指数 $\beta$ 改记 $\alpha$，人数 $N$ 改记 $L$，原文总消费 $Nc$ 合写为 $C$；商品生产式取原文外部性指数 $\gamma=0$，本例再取固定人口。$\phi$ 不是物质资本折旧。时间改变与产出比率的数例为独立构造，未解完整 Lucas 最优化均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lucas's equations (11)–(13) supply the allocation, resource and accumulation mechanisms and their boundaries; page 23 establishes that sustained growth need not require the average-skill externality. Notation is renamed to avoid confusing learning productivity with depreciation, and aggregate consumption $Nc$ is written as $C$. Production sets the externality exponent to zero, and the example fixes population. The numerical comparison is constructed and does not solve the full optimising equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
