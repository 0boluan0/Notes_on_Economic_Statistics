# 创新是本期观测减去其基于过去信息的一步线性预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An innovation is the current observation minus its one-step linear prediction from the past*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 令 $\mathcal H_{t-1}$ 是由常数项 $1$ 与 $X_{t-1},X_{t-2},\ldots$ 张成的闭线性空间（等价地，可先中心化序列，再投影到中心化的过去）。$X_t$ 相对于这段过去的一步[[创新]]定义为
> $$\varepsilon_t=X_t-P_{\mathcal H_{t-1}}X_t.$$
> 它是本期新到达、无法由过去观测的线性组合预测的那一部分。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $\mathcal H_{t-1}$ be the closed linear span of the constant and past observations, equivalently the span of the centred past after removing the mean. The one-step innovation is $X_t-P_{\mathcal H_{t-1}}X_t$: the part of the current observation that no affine linear prediction from the observed past could predict.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

投影残差与 $\mathcal H_{t-1}$ 中每个变量正交，因此创新彼此跨期不相关。若二阶矩有限且方差不随时间改变，创新序列是[[白噪声二阶定义|二阶白噪声]]。这仍是线性预测结论：除非再有 joint Gaussian、鞅差或其他足够条件，$P_{\mathcal H_{t-1}}X_t$ 不必等于给定全部过去信息的条件期望。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Projection makes the innovation orthogonal to the entire linear past and hence serially uncorrelated with earlier innovations. With finite time-invariant variance it is second-order white noise. This remains a linear-prediction statement; equality with the full conditional-mean error needs joint Gaussianity, an MDS condition, or another sufficient assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

创新也不等于拟合后的 residual。创新是总体模型中相对于指定信息集的潜在新成分；residual 是用有限样本和估计参数算出的创新估计。改变模型、信息集或参数估计，residual 会变；真正的 innovation 定义也必须随所声明的信息集一起读。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An innovation is not the same object as a fitted residual. The innovation belongs to the population model and a stated information set; a residual is its finite-sample estimate using fitted parameters. Changing the model or information set changes what counts as new information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在可逆 ARMA 中，创新还能由当前与过去观测的稳定滤波恢复；若 MA 部分不可逆，同一观测二阶结构可能对应不唯一的噪声表示，此时任意写入方程的 $\varepsilon_t$ 未必就是由观测历史定义的一步创新。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an invertible ARMA model, current and past observations recover the innovation through a stable filter. Without invertibility, a noise term written in one representation need not be the unique one-step innovation determined by the observed history.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 拟合 ARMA 后得到一列残差，能否直接说“我们观察到了真实创新”？
>
> **答案：** 不能。残差依赖估计参数、初值与模型设定；它只是潜在创新的样本估计。
