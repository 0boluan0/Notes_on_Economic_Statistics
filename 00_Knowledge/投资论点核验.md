# 投资论点核验用事先明确的可观测证据检查经营假设并更新估值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Investment thesis verification tests operating assumptions against predefined observable evidence and updates valuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把论点写成一个以后能检查的判断：哪个经营驱动与基准假设不同，证据从哪里来，何时可观察，什么结果会使原判断减弱或被推翻。目标价只是输出，不能代替这一组判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
State a thesis so it can be checked later: identify the operating driver that differs from the baseline, the evidence source, when it becomes observable, and what would weaken or overturn the claim. A target price is an output, not a substitute for these judgments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如假设“续费率提高可以降低维持收入所需的获客支出”。先固定续费率的客户批次和计算方法，再约定核对两个季度的同口径留存、折扣与获客现金支出。若留存改善只是延长免费期造成，就不能把它当作[[护城河证据|客户黏性增强的证据]]。经营改善但股价不涨，也不自动否定经营机制；价格可能已反映改善，或折现率同时改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose higher renewal rates are expected to reduce acquisition spending needed to sustain revenue. Fix the customer cohort and measurement method, then check comparable retention, discounts, and acquisition cash spending over two quarters. Retention improved by longer free trials does not establish [[护城河证据|stronger customer attachment]]. Nor does a flat stock price automatically refute an operating improvement: it may have been expected, or discount rates may have changed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

核验时更新整套[[情景预测]]，保留原预测与当时可用证据，区分模型算错、事实不符与环境改变。“催化剂”只解释什么事件可能让信息被看见，不保证价格会在某日回到自己的估值。以上是研究记录方法，不是机械买卖信号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Update the coherent [[情景预测|scenario]] while preserving the original forecast and contemporaneous evidence. Separate calculation errors, contradicted assumptions, and environmental changes. A catalyst may reveal information; it does not guarantee convergence to one's valuation on a schedule. This is a research-recording method, not an automatic trading signal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
