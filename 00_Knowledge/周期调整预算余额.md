# 周期调整预算余额是按照明确模型扣除经济周期影响后的预算余额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The cyclically adjusted budget balance removes the estimated effect of the economic cycle using an explicit model.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

周期调整预算余额（CAB）从观测余额中扣除估计的周期成分，试图分离[[财政自动稳定器]]等因素随经济状态发生的收支变化。它不是直接观测值，需要指定[[潜在产出]]、[[产出缺口]]以及收入和支出对周期的反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cyclically adjusted balance (CAB) subtracts an estimated cyclical component from the observed balance, separating responses such as [[财政自动稳定器|automatic stabilisers]]. It requires assumptions about [[潜在产出|potential output]], the [[产出缺口|output gap]] and fiscal elasticities; it is not directly observed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一种简化水平法设观测余额为 $OB=R-G$，收入与支出的产出弹性分别为 $\varepsilon_R$、$\varepsilon_G$。实际产出 $Y$ 与潜在产出 $Y^*$ 使用相同价格口径；以下 $R,G,CAB$ 均为同一期间和财政范围的名义金额，$G$ 使用与该余额一致的支出口径：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simplified level-based method, let the observed balance be $OB=R-G$, with output elasticities $\varepsilon_R$ and $\varepsilon_G$. Actual and potential output use a common price basis. Fiscal variables below are nominal amounts for the same period and coverage, with expenditure defined consistently with the balance:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
R^{ca}=R\left(\frac{Y^*}{Y}\right)^{\varepsilon_R},\qquad
G^{ca}=G\left(\frac{Y^*}{Y}\right)^{\varepsilon_G},\qquad
CAB=R^{ca}-G^{ca},\qquad CB=OB-CAB.
$$

设 $Y=95,\ Y^*=100,\ R=38,\ G=42$，并假设 $\varepsilon_R=1,\varepsilon_G=0$。则观测余额为 −4，调整后收入为 40、支出仍为 42，所以 CAB 为 −2，周期成分为 −2。支出弹性为零是本例假设；包含周期敏感失业支出时应另行估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $Y=95,\ Y^*=100,\ R=38,\ G=42$, assume $\varepsilon_R=1,\varepsilon_G=0$. The observed balance is −4; adjusted revenue is 40 and expenditure remains 42, giving CAB=−2 and a cyclical component of −2. Zero expenditure elasticity is an example assumption, not a rule for unemployment-sensitive spending.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CAB 仍可能包含一次性收入等非周期因素；进一步调整见[[结构性预算余额]]。若换算成 GDP 比率，应明确实际还是潜在 GDP 作分母，不能把不同分母的余额比率直接当作上述名义分解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CAB can retain non-cyclical items such as one-off revenue; see the [[结构性预算余额|structural balance]] for further adjustments. Ratios require an explicit actual- or potential-GDP denominator and must not be mixed in a nominal decomposition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
