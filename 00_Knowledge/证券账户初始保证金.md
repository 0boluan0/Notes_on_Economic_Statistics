# 证券账户初始保证金是开立或增加证券融资头寸时要求的最低自有权益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Initial margin in a securities account is the minimum own equity required to open or increase a financed securities position*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证券账户中的初始保证金（initial margin）是建立或增加融资头寸时，规则与经纪商要求投资者提供的最低自有权益，而不是借款本身。以下用普通融资买入说明：若买入证券市值为 $V_0>0$、借款为 $D_0$，且没有其他账户资产负债，自有权益为 $E_0=V_0-D_0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Initial margin in a securities account is the own equity required by the applicable rules and broker to establish or increase a financed position; it is not the loan. For a simple financed purchase with securities worth $V_0>0$, debt $D_0$, and no other account assets or liabilities, equity is $E_0=V_0-D_0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若该交易要求的初始比例为 $m_0$，在这个简化账户中须满足 $E_0/V_0\ge m_0$，即 $D_0\le(1-m_0)V_0$。假设要求 50%，买入 10,000 元证券至少需 5,000 元自有权益；实际投入 7,000 元意味着借款 3,000 元，并不意味着最低要求改成 70%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a required initial fraction $m_0$, the simplified account must satisfy $E_0/V_0\ge m_0$, or $D_0\le(1-m_0)V_0$. A hypothetical 50% requirement on a 10,000 purchase calls for at least 5,000 of own equity. Contributing 7,000 and borrowing 3,000 does not change the minimum requirement to 70%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

初始比例、绝对最低账户金额、可接受抵押资产及折算率是不同条件，还可能有更严格的券商要求。本文不把 50% 当作所有国家、证券和账户的统一规则。初始符合要求也不代表以后一直合格，持仓期间另看[[证券账户维持保证金]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The initial percentage, absolute minimum account equity, eligible collateral, and valuation haircuts are separate conditions; broker requirements may be stricter. The illustration does not impose a universal 50% rule across jurisdictions, securities, or accounts. Initial compliance does not ensure continuing compliance with [[证券账户维持保证金|maintenance margin]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的证券融资自有权益口径，不等于衍生品 IM 对平仓期潜在敞口的模型化保护，也不等于每日 VM。后两者沿用[[净额与抵押品#VM 与 IM 覆盖不同时间段|衍生品保证金的时间区间]]，不要套用本卡的股票购买价分母。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This own-equity convention for securities financing differs from derivatives IM covering potential exposure over close-out and from daily VM. Those uses follow [[净额与抵押品#VM 与 IM 覆盖不同时间段|the time intervals covered by derivatives margin]], not this stock-purchase denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
