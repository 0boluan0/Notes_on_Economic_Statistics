# 函数在临界点连续时可用两侧一阶导数的严格符号判定局部极值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For a function continuous at a critical point, the strict derivative signs on either side classify local extrema*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子方法
> 设 $c$ 是定义域内点，$f$ 在 $c$ 连续，并在 $(c-\delta,c)$ 和 $(c,c+\delta)$ 可导，其中 $\delta>0$。若左侧处处 $f'>0$、右侧处处 $f'<0$，则 $c$ 是严格局部最大点；左负右正给严格局部最小；两侧均严格为正或均严格为负，则 $c$ 不是局部极值。$f'(c)$ 本身可以不存在。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $c$ be an interior domain point, with $f$ continuous at $c$ and differentiable in the adjacent punctured intervals. A strictly positive derivative throughout the left interval and a strictly negative derivative throughout the right give a strict local maximum; reversed signs give a strict local minimum. The same strict sign on both sides excludes a local extremum. The derivative at $c$ itself need not exist.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $f(x)=|x|$ 在零点不可导，但左侧 $f'=-1$、右侧 $f'=1$，所以零点是局部最小。对 $f(x)=x^3$，两侧导数都为正，尽管 $f'(0)=0$，零点仍不是极值。检验依据的是邻域的单调方向，不是中心点的一次数值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $|x|$, the derivative changes from $-1$ to $1$ across zero, so the nondifferentiable point is a local minimum. For $x^3$, the derivative is positive on both sides, so zero is not an extremum despite $f'(0)=0$. The test uses neighborhood behavior rather than one derivative value at the center.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续性把两侧的增减趋势接到实际函数值 $f(c)$。例如左侧递增时，对任意足够近的 $x<c$，在 $[x,c]$ 应用 [[拉格朗日中值定理]] 得 $f(c)-f(x)=f'(\xi)(c-x)>0$；右侧同理。若只知道 $f'\ge0$ 或 $f'\le0$，平坦区间可能产生非严格极值，不能套用“两侧同号就没有极值”的严格版本。若导数反复变号，一两个采样点也不能代替区间证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity connects each side's monotonic behavior to the actual value at $c$. On the increasing left side, the mean value theorem on $[x,c]$ gives $f(c)-f(x)=f'(\xi)(c-x)>0$; the other side follows similarly. Weak derivative inequalities allow flat intervals and non-strict extrema, so they cannot support the strict same-sign conclusion. A few samples cannot establish a sign condition when the derivative oscillates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分类之后，闭区间的绝对极值仍需用 [[闭区间极值法]] 比较端点和全部候选值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After local classification, absolute extrema on a closed interval still require comparing endpoint and candidate values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $f'$ 在 $c$ 左右都为负说明什么？
>
> **答案：** 在本卡的连续性和邻域条件下，两侧导数均严格为负意味着函数穿过 $c$ 时继续递减，因此 $c$ 不是局部极值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** What follows if $f'$ is negative on both sides of $c$?
>
> **Answer:** Under this card's continuity and neighborhood hypotheses, a strictly negative derivative on both sides means that the function keeps decreasing through $c$. Thus $c$ is not a local extremum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
