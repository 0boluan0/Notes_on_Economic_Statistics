# First derivative test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一阶导数检验*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **first derivative test** classifies a possible turning point by looking at how the function approaches it and leaves it. A hill rises on the left and falls on the right; a valley does the reverse. Derivative signs make this picture precise, provided the function actually connects to its value at the point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**一阶导数检验**通过函数如何接近、离开一个候选转折点来判断它的类型：山顶左边上升、右边下降，谷底则相反。导数符号把这个图像变成精确判断，但前提是两侧变化确实接到该点的函数值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[临界点|critical point]] is a candidate where the derivative is zero or does not exist, using the vault’s interior-point convention. Finding one does not establish an extremum. A [[局部极值与绝对极值|local maximum]] compares $f(c)$ with nearby outputs; a strict local maximum requires every sufficiently near $x\ne c$ to have $f(x)<f(c)$. A strict local minimum reverses the inequality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按本库的内点约定，[[临界点]]是导数为零或不存在的候选点。找到它并不等于找到极值。[[局部极值与绝对极值|局部最大值]]只把 $f(c)$ 与附近输出比较；严格局部最大要求所有足够近且 $x\ne c$ 的点都满足 $f(x)<f(c)$。严格局部最小把不等号反向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read both sides of the point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*读懂候选点的两侧*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $c$ be an interior point of the domain. For some $\delta>0$, assume $f$ is [[一点连续|continuous]] at $c$, so nearby outputs tend to $f(c)$, and differentiable throughout $(c-\delta,c)$ and $(c,c+\delta)$. Here $\delta$ specifies a neighborhood in which the sign claims hold. The value $f'(c)$ itself need not exist. Under these assumptions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $c$ 是定义域内点。存在 $\delta>0$，使 $f$ 在 $c$ 处[[一点连续|连续]]，即附近的输出趋于 $f(c)$，并在 $(c-\delta,c)$、$(c,c+\delta)$ 内处处可导。这里 $\delta$ 指定一个导数符号结论全段成立的邻域，不要求 $f'(c)$ 本身存在。在这些条件下：

> [!important] Strict signs classify the point · 用严格符号分类
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Positive derivatives on the whole left interval and negative derivatives on the whole right interval give a **strict local maximum**. Negative on the left and positive on the right give a **strict local minimum**. If both sides have the same strict sign, the function continues through the point in the same direction, so it has **no local extremum there**.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 整个左侧区间导数为正、整个右侧为负，得到**严格局部最大**；左负右正，得到**严格局部最小**；两侧导数保持同一个严格符号，函数便穿过该点继续沿同一方向变化，所以该点**不是局部极值点**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The link is the [[导数单调性判据|derivative criterion for monotonicity]]. To check the left side of a maximum, choose any nearby $x<c$. Continuity at $c$ and differentiability on $(x,c)$ allow the [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]] to give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里使用[[导数单调性判据]]。例如验证最大点的左侧：任取附近 $x<c$，在 $c$ 连续且在 $(x,c)$ 可导，允许[[拉格朗日中值定理]]给出

$$
f(c)-f(x)=f'(\xi)(c-x)>0,\qquad \xi\in(x,c).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the right, the derivative is negative, so $f(x)-f(c)<0$. Both sides are therefore strictly below the central value. The same argument proves the other sign cases and explains why continuity is needed at $c$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

右侧导数为负，因此 $f(x)-f(c)<0$。两侧函数值都严格低于中心值。其余符号组合也用相同论证，这同时说明了为什么必须在 $c$ 连续。

> [!example] Two turning points of one cubic · 同一个三次函数的两个转折点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=3x-x^3$, [[整数幂求导|the power rule]] gives $f'(x)=3-3x^2=3(1-x)(1+x)$. Its zeros are $-1$ and $1$. Factoring establishes the signs throughout all three intervals:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $f(x)=3x-x^3$，[[整数幂求导]]给出 $f'(x)=3-3x^2=3(1-x)(1+x)$，零点为 $-1$、$1$。因式分解能确定三个区间上的全部符号：
>
> $$
> \begin{array}{c|ccc}x&(-\infty,-1)&(-1,1)&(1,\infty)\\\hline f'(x)&-&+&-\end{array}
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $c=-1$, the signs change from negative to positive: a strict local minimum with $f(-1)=-2$. At $c=1$, they change from positive to negative: a strict local maximum with $f(1)=2$. The function is continuous at both points. These are local classifications; this cubic is unbounded above and below on the real line.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $c=-1$，符号由负变正，得到严格局部最小值 $f(-1)=-2$；在 $c=1$，由正变负，得到严格局部最大值 $f(1)=2$。函数在两点都连续。这些是局部分类：该三次函数在整个实轴上既无上界，也无下界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A flat tangent and a sharp corner
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*平坦切线与尖角*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^3$, $f'(0)=0$, but $f'(x)=3x^2>0$ on both sides of zero. The function rises through zero, so there is no extremum. For $f(x)=|x|$, the derivative is $-1$ on the left and $1$ on the right; continuity and the sign switch give a strict minimum even though $f'(0)$ does not exist. The center derivative alone cannot classify the point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x)=x^3$，虽然 $f'(0)=0$，但零点两侧都有 $f'(x)=3x^2>0$。函数上升着穿过零点，因此没有极值。对 $f(x)=|x|$，左侧导数为 $-1$、右侧为 $1$，连续性与符号转换给出严格最小值，尽管 $f'(0)$ 不存在。单看中心导数无法分类。

> [!warning] Keep the hypotheses when reading a sign chart · 读符号表时保留条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Continuity cannot be dropped. Define $g(x)=|x|$ for $x\ne0$ but $g(0)=1$. The side derivatives still go from negative to positive, yet zero is a strict local maximum: nearby values $|x|<1$ lie below $g(0)$. The jump disconnects the central value from the side behavior.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 连续性不能删去。令 $x\ne0$ 时 $g(x)=|x|$，但 $g(0)=1$。两侧导数仍然由负变正，零点却是严格局部最大点，因为附近的 $|x|<1$ 都低于 $g(0)$。跳跃使中心值与两侧趋势脱节。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Weak signs are also different. A constant function has derivative zero on both sides, and every point is both a non-strict local maximum and minimum. Thus “same sign means no extremum” here requires the same **strict** sign. If signs keep oscillating arbitrarily near the point, this version of the test is inconclusive; failure to meet its conditions does not prove the absence of an extremum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 非严格符号也要另看。常函数两侧导数都为零，而每点同时是非严格局部最大和最小。因此，本卡“两侧同号则无极值”要求相同的**严格**符号。若导数在任意近处持续变号，本版本无法判定；不满足检验条件，并不证明没有极值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the method by locating candidates, proving the signs on their adjacent intervals, and then reporting the point together with its value. The [[闭区间极值法|closed-interval method]] answers the separate question of absolute extrema by comparing all relevant candidate and endpoint values. Endpoints are not covered by this two-sided interior test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用时先找候选点，再证明相邻区间上的符号，最后同时报告点与函数值。若问题是绝对极值，则用[[闭区间极值法]]比较所有相关候选值和端点值。端点不属于这里的双侧内点检验。
