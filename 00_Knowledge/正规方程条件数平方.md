# 满列秩矩阵的 Gram 条件数是原矩阵二范数条件数的平方
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For full-column-rank $A$, the Gram-matrix condition number is the square of the original two-norm condition number*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 数值边界
> 若 $A$ 满列秩，奇异值为 $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_n>0$，则
> $$
> \kappa_2(A^TA)
> =\frac{\sigma_1^2}{\sigma_n^2}
> =\kappa_2(A)^2.
> $$
> 因此正规方程在精确算术中刻画正确解，但显式形成并求解 $A^TA\hat x=A^Tb$ 会把原矩阵的谱病态平方。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $A$ has full column rank with singular values $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_n>0$, then $\kappa_2(A^TA)=\sigma_1^2/\sigma_n^2=\kappa_2(A)^2$. The normal equations characterise the correct solution in exact arithmetic, but explicitly forming and solving them squares the spectral ill-conditioning of the original matrix.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么恰好平方
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the square appears exactly*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由 SVD $A=U\Sigma V^T$，
$$
A^TA=V\Sigma^2V^T.
$$
所以 $A^TA$ 的特征值是 $A$ 的奇异值平方。若 $\kappa_2(A)=10^8$，则 $\kappa_2(A^TA)=10^{16}$，已经接近 double precision 可分辨相对尺度的极限；形成 Gram 矩阵时的舍入也可能先损失小奇异方向的信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From $A=U\Sigma V^T$, one obtains $A^TA=V\Sigma^2V^T$, so the eigenvalues of the Gram matrix are squared singular values. If $\kappa_2(A)=10^8$, then $\kappa_2(A^TA)=10^{16}$, near the relative-resolution limit of double precision, and rounding during Gram-matrix formation can erase information in small singular directions before the solve begins.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 算法边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Algorithmic boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“条件数平方”不表示正规方程在每个问题上都不可用：当 $A$ 良态、精度要求较低或性能模型明确支持时，Cholesky 解正规方程可能更便宜。它表示不能从精确公式直接推出数值可靠性；当精度重要或 $A$ 病态时，Householder QR 直接处理 $A$，SVD 还会显出小奇异方向和数值秩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Squaring the condition number does not make normal equations unusable in every setting. For a well-conditioned $A$, modest accuracy requirements, or a justified performance trade-off, Cholesky on the normal equations can be cheaper. The point is that an exact formula does not certify numerical reliability. When accuracy matters or $A$ is ill-conditioned, Householder QR works directly with $A$, while the SVD additionally reveals small singular directions and numerical rank.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 为什么“正规方程解出的残差很小”仍不足以证明系数准确？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does a small residual from the normal equations not by itself prove that the coefficients are accurate?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 病态问题会把很小的后向误差放大成很大的系数前向误差；还要结合 $A$ 的条件性判断。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** An ill-conditioned problem can amplify a tiny backward error into a large forward error in the coefficients; conditioning of $A$ must also be assessed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
