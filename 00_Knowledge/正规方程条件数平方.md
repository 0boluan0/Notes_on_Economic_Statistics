# Normal equations and squared conditioning
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正规方程条件数平方*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[最小二乘正规方程|normal equations]] turn a nearest-fit problem into a square linear system. That is an exact mathematical description of the solution. Computing the square matrix first can nevertheless make the calculation more sensitive: its largest-to-smallest stretch ratio is the **square** of the original matrix's ratio. This explains why two algebraically equivalent routes can behave differently on a computer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[最小二乘正规方程]]把最近拟合问题写成方形线性系统，这是解的精确数学描述。但先算出这个方阵，可能让计算更加敏感：新矩阵最大与最小伸缩量的比值，是原比值的**平方**。这解释了为何代数等价的两条路线，在计算机上可能表现不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a first example, $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,0.01)$ means a diagonal matrix with entries $1,0.01$ and zeros elsewhere. It leaves one coordinate unchanged and shrinks the other by $100$. The Gram matrix $A^TA=\operatorname{diag}(1,0.0001)$ shrinks that second coordinate by $10{,}000$. The ratio grows from $100$ to $10{,}000$ before any rounding has occurred.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看 $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,0.01)$：这表示对角元为 $1,0.01$、其余为零的矩阵。它保持第一个坐标，把第二个缩小为原来的 $1/100$。Gram 矩阵 $A^TA=\operatorname{diag}(1,0.0001)$ 则把第二个缩小为 $1/10{,}000$。尚未发生舍入，两个方向的比值就已从 $100$ 增到 $10{,}000$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the square appears
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么恰好平方

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ have $m$ rows and $n$ columns, with $m\ge n\ge1$ and [[线性无关|linearly independent columns]]. Entries may be real or complex. Write $A^*$ for conjugate transpose: transpose the matrix and change $a+bi$ to $a-bi$, where $i^2=-1$; for real entries this is the usual [[矩阵转置|transpose]] $A^T$. The $n\times n$ **Gram matrix** $G=A^*A$ records inner products between columns. Full column rank makes it [[Gram矩阵正定判据|positive definite and invertible]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 有 $m$ 行、$n$ 列，$m\ge n\ge1$，各列[[线性无关]]；元素可以为实数或复数。$A^*$ 表示共轭转置：先转置，再把 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$，其中 $i^2=-1$；实矩阵时就是普通[[矩阵转置|转置]] $A^T$。$n\times n$ 的 **Gram 矩阵** $G=A^*A$ 记录各列之间的内积。满列秩使它[[Gram矩阵正定判据|正定并可逆]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[奇异值|singular values]] $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_n>0$ measure stretches along suitable perpendicular directions. Using [[欧氏范数|Euclidean lengths]] and the associated [[谱范数|matrix 2-norm]], define $\kappa_2(A)=\sigma_1/\sigma_n$. A [[紧SVD|compact SVD]] writes $A=U\Sigma V^*$, where $U$ has $n$ [[标准正交组|orthonormal columns]], $V$ is an $n\times n$ [[Unitary 矩阵|unitary matrix]], and $\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_n)$. Thus $U^*U=V^*V=I_n$, where $I_n$ is the identity matrix, and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[奇异值]] $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_n>0$ 描述适当的垂直方向上的伸缩量。采用[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]和相应[[谱范数|矩阵二范数]]，定义 $\kappa_2(A)=\sigma_1/\sigma_n$。[[紧SVD]]写成 $A=U\Sigma V^*$：$U$ 有 $n$ 个[[标准正交组|标准正交列]]，$V$ 为 $n\times n$ [[Unitary 矩阵|酉矩阵]]，$\Sigma=\operatorname{diag}(\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_n)$。因此 $U^*U=V^*V=I_n$，其中 $I_n$ 为单位矩阵，并且

$$
G=A^*A=V\Sigma^2V^*,\qquad
\kappa_2(G)=\frac{\sigma_1^2}{\sigma_n^2}=\kappa_2(A)^2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This uses the [[奇异值与Gram谱|Gram-spectrum identity]]: the eigenvalues of $G$ are $\sigma_j^2$. Because $G$ is positive definite, these are also its singular values. The displayed statement concerns this specific norm and full column rank. With dependent columns, $G$ is singular; dividing its largest eigenvalue by its smallest positive one would describe a different, restricted object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里用到了[[奇异值与Gram谱|Gram 谱恒等式]]：$G$ 的特征值为 $\sigma_j^2$。由于 $G$ 正定，这些也是它的奇异值。上式针对指定的二范数与满列秩情形；列线性相关时 $G$ 奇异，若改除以“最小正特征值”，讨论的已是限制在非零方向上的另一个对象。

> [!example] A direction lost while forming the matrix · 形成矩阵时丢失方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\10^{-8}&0\end{bmatrix}$. Its columns are independent, but in exact arithmetic
>
> $$A^TA=\begin{bmatrix}1+10^{-16}&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> In ordinary binary64 [[浮点舍入|round-to-nearest arithmetic]], storing $1+10^{-16}$ gives $1$. Direct formation can therefore produce the singular matrix of all ones. The small distinction between the columns has disappeared before a linear solver sees it. This is a concrete rounding example, not a claim that every matrix with the same condition number loses the same information.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取上述 $A$，它的列线性无关，但精确 Gram 矩阵左上角只有 $1+10^{-16}$。在通常的 binary64 [[浮点舍入|舍入到最近值的算术]]中，这个和存成 $1$，直接形成的矩阵因而可能变成全为一的奇异矩阵。求解器尚未开始，那一点区分两列的信息就已消失。这是一个具体舍入例子，并不声称条件数相同的每个矩阵都会丢失同样的信息。

## What the identity does and does not prescribe
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 算法边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\kappa_2(A)=10^8$, the exact Gram condition number is $10^{16}$. That is a warning at double precision, not a universal failure threshold or an exact count of lost digits. The input, the rounding pattern, residual size and required accuracy also matter. Even a backward-stable solver for the formed $G$ need not give a backward-stable method for the original data $A,b$; [[条件性与算法稳定性|conditioning and algorithmic stability]] answer different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\kappa_2(A)=10^8$，精确 Gram 条件数就是 $10^{16}$。这在双精度计算中值得警惕，但不是统一失效阈值，也不是准确损失位数。输入、舍入方式、残差大小与所需精度都有关。即使求解器对已形成的 $G$ 后向稳定，整个路线也未必对原数据 $A,b$ 后向稳定；[[条件性与算法稳定性]]回答的是不同问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A carefully implemented Householder [[薄QR最小二乘|QR least-squares solve]] avoids explicitly forming the Gram matrix. Its orthonormal factor preserves lengths, so the triangular factor has the same singular values as $A$. Merely naming a factorization “QR” is insufficient: classical Gram–Schmidt can lose orthogonality. Stable QR does not remove sensitivity already present in the problem, and least-squares error bounds for perturbations in both $A$ and $b$ also involve the residual's size. An SVD-based solver can expose small directions; treating them as zero requires a declared rank or regularization decision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可靠实现的 Householder [[薄QR最小二乘]]避免显式形成 Gram 矩阵。其标准正交因子保持长度，因此三角因子与 $A$ 有相同的奇异值。但仅把分解叫作“QR”还不够：经典 Gram–Schmidt 可能丢失正交性。稳定 QR 不能消除问题本身的敏感性；同时扰动 $A,b$ 的最小二乘误差界，还依赖残差大小。SVD 求解器可以显示小方向，而把它们当作零，需要声明数值秩或正则化决定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a sufficiently well-conditioned problem and a suitable accuracy budget, forming the Gram matrix and solving it by Cholesky factorization can still be an acceptable, sometimes cheaper choice. Cholesky factors a positive definite matrix into triangular factors. The decision needs an error and cost assessment; the exact normal-equation identity alone supplies neither. A small fitting residual likewise does not prove accurate coefficients, because a weak direction may barely change the fitted output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对足够良态、精度预算合适的问题，形成 Gram 矩阵后使用 Cholesky 分解仍可能可以接受，有时计算更便宜。Cholesky 把正定矩阵分解为三角因子。选择它需要评估误差与成本，精确的正规方程恒等式本身并不提供这些保证。拟合残差小同样不能证明系数准确，因为沿弱方向大幅改变系数，也可能几乎不改变拟合输出。
