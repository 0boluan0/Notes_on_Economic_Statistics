# 贝叶斯博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bayesian Games*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当玩家不知道对手的成本、偏好或信息，却知道这些类型的概率结构时，怎样形成策略和均衡。
> **具体锚点：** 投标者知道自己的估值但不知道别人估值，策略必须是“每种可能估值对应什么报价”的函数。
> **核心难点：** 类型是玩家的私人信息，策略要对每个类型规定行动；期望收益对他人类型和行动共同取期望。
> **为什么重要：** 拍卖、逆向选择、信号和信息设计都以此为起点。
> **继续：** 若行动还能传递信息，进入 [[信号博弈、序贯理性与完美贝叶斯均衡#信号博弈与序贯信念|信号博弈]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Determine strategies and equilibrium when players do not know opponents' costs, preferences, or information but know the probability structure of those types.
> **Concrete anchor:** A bidder knows their own valuation but not others' valuations, so a strategy must map every possible valuation into a bid.
> **Central difficulty:** A type is a player's private information, and a strategy must specify an action for every type; expected utility averages jointly over others' types and the actions induced by their strategies.
> **Why it matters:** Auctions, adverse selection, signaling, and information design all begin here.
> **Continue with:** If actions also transmit information, go to [[信号博弈、序贯理性与完美贝叶斯均衡#信号博弈与序贯信念|Signaling Games and Sequential Beliefs]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 类型、先验与信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Types, Priors, and Information*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Harsanyi 转换让自然先抽取类型，再让玩家在知道自己类型但未必知道他人类型时行动。模型需说明类型空间、共同先验和每名玩家观察到什么。共同先验是建模假设，不是说玩家知道现实中的真实分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Harsanyi transformation lets Nature draw types before players act knowing their own type but not necessarily others' types. The model must specify the type spaces, the common prior, and what each player observes. A common prior is a modeling assumption; it does not mean that players know the true distribution in the real world.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

类型应收纳所有影响收益或信息、但未被其他玩家直接观察的特征。若某成本既不改变收益也不改变可用信息，就不应为了“更真实”而无目的扩充类型空间；类型过多会增加策略维度，却不增加可检验含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A type should contain every payoff-relevant or information-relevant characteristic not directly observed by other players. If a cost affects neither payoffs nor available information, adding it merely for realism needlessly enlarges the type space and the strategy dimension without adding testable meaning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 策略是类型到行动的映射
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Strategy Maps Types into Actions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

纯策略 $s_i(t_i)$ 为每个可能类型指定行动。把 Bayesian 博弈写成普通策略式时，策略数常迅速增长，因为必须枚举完整映射。不要把“某个类型的行动”误称为玩家的完整策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pure strategy $s_i(t_i)$ assigns an action to every possible type. When a Bayesian game is written in ordinary strategic form, the number of strategies can grow rapidly because complete mappings must be enumerated. Do not call the action of one particular type the player's complete strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如玩家有高、低两种类型，每种类型均可选 $A$ 或 $B$，其纯策略不是两个而是四个：$(A,A)$、$(A,B)$、$(B,A)$、$(B,B)$，两个位置分别说明高、低类型的行动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if a player has high and low types and each type can choose $A$ or $B$, the player has four pure strategies rather than two: $(A,A)$, $(A,B)$, $(B,A)$, and $(B,B)$, where the two positions specify the actions of the high and low types.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Bayesian Nash equilibrium
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bayesian Nash Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定自己的类型，玩家根据条件信念对他人类型取期望并选择最优行动；均衡要求每个类型都最优，且策略与共同先验共同生成这些预期。求解时通常先猜策略形式，再写逐类型或逐区间的激励条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conditional on their own type, a player forms expectations over others' types and chooses an optimal action. Equilibrium requires optimality for every type, with those expectations jointly generated by the strategies and the common prior. In practice, one often conjectures a strategy form and then writes incentive conditions type by type or interval by interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

形式上，对每个 $t_i$，均衡策略满足 $s_i^*(t_i)\in\arg\max_{a_i}E[u_i(a_i,s_{-i}^*(t_{-i}),t_i,t_{-i})\mid t_i]$。期望使用的是玩家在知道自身类型后的条件分布；若类型相关，不能直接使用无条件边际概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Formally, for every $t_i$, an equilibrium strategy satisfies $s_i^*(t_i)\in\arg\max_{a_i}E[u_i(a_i,s_{-i}^*(t_{-i}),t_i,t_{-i})\mid t_i]$. The expectation uses the player's conditional distribution after learning their own type; correlated types cannot be handled by mechanically substituting unconditional marginal probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 拍卖与逆向选择入口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Entry Points to Auctions and Adverse Selection*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在独立私人价值拍卖中，报价权衡赢得概率和支付；机制与信息结构决定是否如实报告。逆向选择则发生在交易前，私人类型影响交易质量或成本，市场价格又改变哪些类型愿意参与。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an independent-private-values auction, a bid trades off the probability of winning against the payment. The mechanism and information structure determine whether truthful reporting is optimal. Adverse selection occurs before contracting: private types affect quality or cost, while the market price changes which types are willing to participate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：两类型进入决策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Entry with Two Types*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设企业成本类型为低 $c_L$ 或高 $c_H$，各以先验概率出现；进入后收益为市场利润减成本，不进入为 0。对每一类型分别比较 $E[\pi\mid c_t]-c_t$ 与 0，即可得到“低成本进入、高成本退出”等策略。若竞争者能从进入行为更新类型判断，这就不再只是静态 Bayesian 博弈，而会进入信号与 PBE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a firm's cost is either low $c_L$ or high $c_H$, each arising with a prior probability. Entry yields expected market profit minus cost, while staying out yields zero. Comparing $E[\pi\mid c_t]-c_t$ with zero for each type can produce a strategy such as “the low-cost type enters and the high-cost type stays out.” If a rival updates beliefs about type after observing entry, the problem becomes a signaling game requiring PBE rather than a purely static Bayesian game.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界与检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundaries and Diagnostic Checks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若类型相关、先验不共同、玩家对模型本身不确定，标准 Bayesian Nash 的解释会变弱。应用时应区分“估计了类型分布”与“假设玩家相信该分布”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When types are correlated, priors are not common, or players are uncertain about the model itself, the standard interpretation of Bayesian Nash equilibrium becomes weaker. Applications must distinguish between estimating a type distribution and assuming that players believe that distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

失败诊断依次检查：策略是否覆盖每个类型；期望是否条件于自身信息；每个类型而非“平均玩家”是否都无有利偏离；推断出的 posterior 是否来自声明的先验。只验证平均收益最大化可能掩盖某一类型的偏离。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diagnose failures in order: Does the strategy cover every type? Are expectations conditional on the player's information? Is every type—not merely an average player—free of profitable deviations? Do the inferred posteriors follow from the stated prior? Maximizing only average payoff can conceal a deviation by a particular type.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 Bayesian 博弈中的策略不是一个单独行动？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is a strategy in a Bayesian game not a single action?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 玩家行动要随其私人类型变化；完整策略必须规定每种可能类型出现时做什么。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The player's action may vary with private type, so a complete strategy must say what to do for every possible type.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### Bayesian Nash 均衡中的期望对什么取？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Over what uncertainty is the expectation in a Bayesian Nash equilibrium taken?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 在给定自身类型和信息后，对未知的他人类型以及由其策略诱导的行动取条件期望。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Conditional on one's own type and information, the expectation is taken over unknown opponents' types and the actions induced by their strategies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 共同先验假设在实证应用中为什么需要谨慎？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does the common-prior assumption require caution in empirical applications?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 研究者估计的分布不必等于玩家主观相信的分布；若二者不一致，均衡预测的行为解释会改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A distribution estimated by the researcher need not equal the distribution players subjectively believe. If they differ, the behavioral interpretation of the equilibrium prediction changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
