# 非线性产品在指定输入范围内的价值不是该输入的仿射函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A nonlinear product has value that is not affine in the specified input over the range considered*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非线性产品是指：在固定其他条件后，其价值在所考察的输入范围内**不能用一条直线 $aS+b$ 表示**。因此，同样幅度的价格变化，从不同位置出发可能产生不同损益；分类必须说明输入和范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A product is nonlinear in a specified input range if its value, with other conditions fixed, cannot be represented by one line $aS+b$. Equal input changes can then produce different P&L depending on the starting point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看涨期权到期支付 $(S-K)^+$ 是最小例子。取 $K=100$，$S$ 从 98 到 99 时支付不变，从 100 到 101 时支付增加 1：跨过执行价后，斜率变了。各段虽分别线性，跨执行价的整体不是；折点也没有普通二阶导数。到期前，在 [[BSM期权定价|BSM 条件]] 下，价格曲线通常光滑，弯曲可由 [[Gamma]] 描述。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A call payoff $(S-K)^+$ gives a minimal example. With $K=100$, moving from 98 to 99 changes nothing, whereas 100 to 101 adds 1. Each segment is linear, but the whole payoff is not, and the kink has no ordinary second derivative. Before maturity, [[BSM期权定价|BSM conditions]] give a smooth curve whose curvature is described by [[Gamma|Gamma]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

某一点的 Gamma 为零不足以证明整个范围线性；也不能仅凭“非线性”决定所有情景都用全重估。近似够不够，要按冲击大小、光滑性和损失用途做 [[估值近似验证]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero Gamma at one point does not prove linearity over a range. Nor does the nonlinear label alone require full repricing in every scenario: use [[估值近似验证|purpose-specific approximation validation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
