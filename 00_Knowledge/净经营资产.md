# 净经营资产是经营资产扣除经营负债后的净占用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net operating assets are operating assets less operating liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净经营资产（NOA）衡量经营资产由经营负债支持后，剩余需要金融融资和股东资本支持的净占用。先依[[管理用报表分类]]确定经营资产 $OA$ 与经营负债 $OL$，再在同一时点计算 $NOA=OA-OL$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NOA measures operating assets remaining after support from operating liabilities, to be financed by financial funding and equity. Use [[管理用报表分类|operating-financing classification]] to identify $OA$ and $OL$, then calculate $NOA=OA-OL$ at the same date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ABC 期末经营资产 2000、经营负债 $228+50=278$，所以 NOA 为 1722。按期限又可写成[[经营营运资本]]加净经营性长期资产：$(700-228)+(1300-50)=472+1250=1722$。这里的“净”是在扣经营负债，不是仅扣资产减值准备。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ABC has operating assets of 2000 and operating liabilities of 278, giving NOA of 1722. By maturity, this is [[经营营运资本|operating working capital]] plus net long-term operating assets: $472+1250=1722$. “Net” here subtracts operating liabilities, not merely impairment allowances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它与[[净值]]不是同一个净额：后者扣全部负债，NOA 只扣已划入经营侧的负债。剩余融资关系见[[管理用资产负债表恒等式]]。NOA 增长可能占用再投资资金，但账面增长能否直接视为现金投入，还须满足[[自由现金流的净经营资产表示]]的条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NOA differs from [[净值|net worth]], which subtracts all liabilities rather than operating liabilities alone. The remaining financing relationship is the [[管理用资产负债表恒等式|management balance-sheet identity]]. Growing NOA may require reinvestment, but treating its book movement as cash investment requires the conditions in [[自由现金流的净经营资产表示|the NOA representation of free cash flow]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
