# Polar area and single coverage
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*极坐标面积与单次覆盖*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To find a region's area in [[极坐标|polar coordinates]], imagine thin slices opening from the origin like a fan. Their contribution depends on the square of the radial length and the small change in angle. Before adding these slices, identify the region they actually sweep: the integral counts every sweep, whereas geometric area counts each piece of the union once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用[[极坐标]]计算面积时，可以想象一把从原点展开的扇子，把区域切成许多窄扇形。每片的贡献取决于径向长度的平方和很小的角度变化。相加之前，要先认清这些薄片实际扫过的区域：积分会累计每一次扫过，几何面积则只把并集中的每一部分算一次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A precise region for the basic formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*基本公式对应什么区域*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $R$ be continuous and nonnegative on a finite interval $[\alpha,\beta]$, with $0<\beta-\alpha\le2\pi$. Angles are in radians. For each angle include every point from the origin to distance $R(\theta)$ along that ray. In symbols the region is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $R$ 在有限区间 $[\alpha,\beta]$ 上连续且非负，满足 $0<\beta-\alpha\le2\pi$，角度以弧度计。对每个角度，取从原点到该方向上距离 $R(\theta)$ 处的全部点。用符号写，区域为

$$D=\{(\rho\cos\theta,\rho\sin\theta):\alpha\le\theta\le\beta,\ 0\le\rho\le R(\theta)\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\rho$ is the running nonnegative distance **inside** a slice; $R(\theta)$ is its outer boundary. The one-turn restriction ensures that distinct interior angles give different rays. The common origin and, for a full turn, the endpoint ray have zero area and cause no area overcount. Under these conditions,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $\rho$ 是薄片**内部**变化的非负距离，$R(\theta)$ 是外边界。角区间不超过一整圈，保证不同内部角度对应不同射线。公共原点以及转满一圈时重合的端点射线，面积都是零，不会造成面积的重复累积。在这些条件下，

$$\operatorname{Area}(D)=\frac12\int_\alpha^\beta R(\theta)^2\,d\theta.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a quarter-disk of radius $2$, take $R=2$ and $0\le\theta\le\pi/2$. The formula gives $\tfrac12\cdot4\cdot\pi/2=\pi$, one quarter of the disk area $4\pi$. The resulting unit is length squared; radians describe an angle ratio and introduce no extra length unit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半径为 $2$ 的四分之一圆盘，可以取 $R=2$、$0\le\theta\le\pi/2$。公式给出 $\tfrac12\cdot4\cdot\pi/2=\pi$，正好是整个圆盘面积 $4\pi$ 的四分之一。结果的单位是长度平方；弧度描述一个角度比值，不增加长度单位。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the factor is one half
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*二分之一从哪里来*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A circular sector of radius $u\ge0$ and angular width $\Delta\theta$ occupies the fraction $\Delta\theta/(2\pi)$ of a disk. Its exact area is therefore $u^2\Delta\theta/2$. Partition the angle interval into small pieces. On piece $i$, let $m_i$ and $M_i$ be the minimum and maximum of the continuous function $R^2$. The actual slice lies between sectors of areas $m_i\Delta\theta_i/2$ and $M_i\Delta\theta_i/2$.

Summing these inner and outer sectors encloses the target region. As the largest angular width tends to zero, continuity makes the two sums approach the same number: they are lower and upper [[黎曼和|Riemann sums]] for $R^2/2$. This proves the area formula and explains why one sample height is an approximation until the partition is refined. These are the same bounding ideas used in [[积分上下界估计|integral estimates]], now applied to sectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半径为 $u\ge0$、角宽为 $\Delta\theta$ 的圆扇形，占整个圆盘的比例为 $\Delta\theta/(2\pi)$，因此其精确面积是 $u^2\Delta\theta/2$。把角区间分成小段；第 $i$ 段上，记连续函数 $R^2$ 的最小值、最大值分别为 $m_i,M_i$，则实际薄片夹在面积分别为 $m_i\Delta\theta_i/2$、$M_i\Delta\theta_i/2$ 的两个扇形之间。

把内外扇形分别相加，就夹住目标区域。当最大角宽趋于零时，连续性使两个和趋向同一个数：它们正是 $R^2/2$ 的上下[[黎曼和]]。这就证明了面积公式，也解释了为何用一个采样高度代替整片时只是近似，需要继续细分。这里把[[积分上下界估计]]中的夹逼思想用于扇形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two radial boundaries on the same ray
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*同一射线上的内外边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the same one-turn angular interval, suppose continuous radial functions satisfy $0\le r_{\rm in}(\theta)\le r_{\rm out}(\theta)$. Including only distances between them gives an annular slice. Subtract the inner sector from the outer one:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同样不超过一圈的角区间上，设连续径向函数满足 $0\le r_{\rm in}(\theta)\le r_{\rm out}(\theta)$。若只取这两个距离之间的点，就得到环状薄片。外扇形减去内扇形，给出

$$A=\frac12\int_\alpha^\beta\left(r_{\rm out}(\theta)^2-r_{\rm in}(\theta)^2\right)\,d\theta.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $1\le\rho\le2$ and $0\le\theta\le\pi$, the area is $\tfrac12(4-1)\pi=3\pi/2$. Squaring the thickness instead would give $(2-1)^2$, which is not the difference of sector areas. When curves exchange inner and outer roles, split the angle range and check the ordering on every piece. Intersections must be actual common Cartesian points; because [[极坐标表示不唯一|polar representations are non-unique]], equal-radius equations at the same written angle are not an unrestricted way to find every intersection of signed polar curves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $1\le\rho\le2$、$0\le\theta\le\pi$，面积为 $\tfrac12(4-1)\pi=3\pi/2$。如果改成对厚度平方，就会得到 $(2-1)^2$，这不是两个扇形面积之差。两条曲线交换内外角色时，要分割角区间，并在每一段核对顺序。交点必须是真正共有的直角坐标点；由于[[极坐标表示不唯一]]，在同一个书写角度下令两个半径相等，并不是任意带符号极坐标曲线全部交点的通用求法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An off-centre disk, including a signed-radius description
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*偏心圆盘与带负半径的表示*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a>0$, the disk $(x-a)^2+y^2\le a^2$ becomes $0\le\rho\le2a\cos\theta$ on $[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$. Indeed, substituting $x=\rho\cos\theta$, $y=\rho\sin\theta$ into the disk inequality gives $\rho^2\le2a\rho\cos\theta$. The nonnegative radial bounds describe the entire disk once apart from its zero-area boundary overlap. Hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $a>0$，圆盘 $(x-a)^2+y^2\le a^2$ 可写成 $[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$ 上的 $0\le\rho\le2a\cos\theta$。将 $x=\rho\cos\theta,y=\rho\sin\theta$ 代入圆盘不等式，得到 $\rho^2\le2a\rho\cos\theta$，即可核对。这些非负径向界把整个圆盘扫一次，边界处零面积的重合不影响面积。因此

$$A=\frac12\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}(2a\cos\theta)^2\,d\theta
=2a^2\int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2}\frac{1+\cos2\theta}{2}\,d\theta=\pi a^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[极坐标曲线的覆盖与重描|same circle is also traced once]] by $r=2a\cos\theta$ on $[0,\pi]$, where $r$ changes sign. To justify area on this interval, split at $\pi/2$. The first part sweeps physical angles $[0,\pi/2]$. On the second, put $\phi=\theta-\pi\in[-\pi/2,0]$ and use nonnegative radius $-r=2a\cos\phi$. These are the two half-disks, with no positive-area overlap, so $\tfrac12\int_0^\pi r^2\,d\theta=\pi a^2$ is valid. Squaring the radius alone would not have supplied this coverage argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[极坐标曲线的覆盖与重描|同一个圆也能在 $[0,\pi]$ 上描一次]]，此时 $r=2a\cos\theta$ 会变号。要在这个区间算面积，应从 $\pi/2$ 分开：第一段扫过物理角度 $[0,\pi/2]$；第二段令 $\phi=\theta-\pi\in[-\pi/2,0]$，取非负半径 $-r=2a\cos\phi$。两段恰好是两个半圆盘，没有正面积重叠，所以 $\tfrac12\int_0^\pi r^2\,d\theta=\pi a^2$ 确实成立。仅把半径平方，并不会自动提供这段覆盖论证。

> [!warning] Squaring does not remove repeated coverage · 平方不能消除重复覆盖
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A constant $R>0$ on $[0,4\pi]$ gives $\tfrac12\int_0^{4\pi}R^2\,d\theta=2\pi R^2$, twice the disk area. Likewise, using $[0,2\pi]$ for $r=2a\cos\theta$ counts its disk twice. A negative radius shifts the physical ray by $\pi$; its square neither records that shift nor removes overlaps. Split signed descriptions into suitable pieces, identify their actual regions, and add only when positive-area overlaps have been excluded or accounted for.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 常数 $R>0$ 在 $[0,4\pi]$ 上给出 $\tfrac12\int_0^{4\pi}R^2\,d\theta=2\pi R^2$，是圆盘面积的两倍。同样，对 $r=2a\cos\theta$ 使用 $[0,2\pi]$，也会把圆盘算两遍。负半径会使物理射线转过 $\pi$，而平方既不记录这个转向，也不会消除重叠。要把带符号的描述分成合适的小段，识别各自的实际区域，只有排除或正确处理了正面积重叠，才能相加。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is a further distinction from boundary retracing. The open spiral $r=1+\theta/(2\pi)$, $0\le\theta\le4\pi$, never revisits a point because its positive distance strictly increases. Yet on the second turn, each origin-to-spiral segment contains the earlier segment on the same ray. Thus its sector integral counts overlapping portions twice if the chosen target is the **union of those radial segments**. An open spiral alone does not specify an enclosed region. One-pass boundary tracing and one-pass area coverage must be checked separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这还不同于边界重描。开螺线 $r=1+\theta/(2\pi)$、$0\le\theta\le4\pi$ 的正距离严格增加，因此从不再次经过同一点。但第二圈中，从原点连到螺线的每条径向线段都包含同一射线上第一圈的线段。所以若目标是**这些径向线段的并集**，扇形积分会把重叠部分算两次。仅一条开螺线并没有指定一个围成区域。边界只描一次与面积只覆盖一次，必须分别检查。
