# 有效凸性是在既定重估模型下衡量基准曲线平行移动的二阶相对价格敏感度

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Effective convexity measures second-order relative price sensitivity to a parallel benchmark-curve shift under a specified repricing model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有效凸性（effective convexity）描述指定曲线冲击下，价格反应偏离当前切线的弯曲程度。沿用 [[有效久期]] 的全价函数 $P(x)$：$x$ 为小数年率的平行位移，估值日及非冲击参数固定，$P_0=P(0)>0$。在二阶导数存在处，局部定义及常用估计为：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Effective convexity describes the curvature of the price response around its current tangent under a specified curve shock. Use the dirty-price function $P(x)$ from [[有效久期|effective duration]]: $x$ is a parallel decimal annual-rate shift, the valuation date and other parameters are fixed, and $P_0=P(0)>0$. Where the second derivative exists, the local measure and usual estimate are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_{\mathrm{eff}}=\frac{P''(0)}{P_0},\qquad
\widehat C_{\mathrm{eff}}(h)=\frac{P(-h)+P(h)-2P_0}{h^2P_0}.
$$

三次估值必须使用相同模型与现金流响应规则。两边价格之和高于 $2P_0$ 时，该步长的差分凸性为正；低于它则为负。凸性没有久期公式前面的负号，分母也没有额外的 2；$1/2$ 出现在二阶价格近似中：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All three valuations must use the same model and cash-flow response rules. A sum above $2P_0$ gives positive difference convexity; a sum below gives negative difference convexity. Convexity has neither duration's leading minus sign nor an extra 2 in its denominator. The factor $1/2$ belongs in the second-order price approximation:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\Delta P}{P_0}\approx-D_{\mathrm{eff}}\Delta x
+\frac12 C_{\mathrm{eff}}(\Delta x)^2.
$$

例如 $P(x)=100e^{-5x}$ 的精确局部凸性是 25；$h=0.001$ 时，三个价格 100、100.501252、99.501248 给出约 25.000052（计算使用未舍入价格）。若 $\Delta x=0.01$，二阶相对变化估计为 $-5\times0.01+\tfrac12\times25\times0.01^2=-4.875\%$，而完整重估给出 $e^{-0.05}-1\approx-4.877058\%$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $P(x)=100e^{-5x}$, local convexity is exactly 25. A 0.001 bump and the prices 100, 100.501252, and 99.501248 give about 25.000052 when calculated before rounding. For $\Delta x=0.01$, the second-order estimate is $-5\times0.01+\tfrac12\times25\times0.01^2=-4.875\%$, whereas full repricing gives $e^{-0.05}-1\approx-4.877058\%$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有效凸性可以用于固定现金流，也可以包含选择权响应而出现 [[负凸性边界|负值]]。它不是 [[曲线形变|收益率曲线横截面的曲率]]。足够光滑时，有限差分随步长缩小逼近导数；但报价误差会被 $h^2$ 放大，跨越行权拐点时局部二阶导数也未必存在。是否足以近似某个冲击，应检查重估误差或 [[Taylor余项]]，不能仅凭“有效”二字保证大幅变化准确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The measure applies to fixed cash flows and can also include option responses that make it [[负凸性边界|negative]]. It is not [[曲线形变|cross-sectional yield-curve curvature]]. With sufficient smoothness, smaller bumps approach the derivative, but price error is amplified by division by $h^2$; an exercise kink may lack a local second derivative. Check repricing errors or a [[Taylor余项|Taylor remainder]] for the proposed shock. “Effective” is not an accuracy guarantee for large moves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
