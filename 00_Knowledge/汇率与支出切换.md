# 汇率引起的支出切换通过本外国产品相对价格改变需求构成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exchange-rate expenditure switching changes the composition of demand through relative prices of domestic and foreign products.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支出切换指购买者在相对价格变化后，调整对本国产品与外国产品的需求。汇率只有真正改变购买者面对的相对价格，并且产品之间能够替代时，才通过这条链影响贸易数量；不同发票币种、定价调整和合同期限会改变传导。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expenditure switching shifts demand between domestic and foreign goods when their relative prices change. Exchange rates affect trade volumes through this channel only insofar as buyers' relative prices change and substitution is possible. Invoicing, repricing and contracts shape the response.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定[[汇率报价方向]]为 $S=$ 本币／外币。设出口商品本币报价 70、进口商品外币报价 10，生产者报价暂时不变。$S$ 从 7 到 7.7 时，外国买者看到出口价从 10 降到约 9.09，本国买者看到进口价从 70 升到 77。这为出口量增加、进口量减少提供替代方向，但没有给出数量变化的大小和日期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use [[汇率报价方向|domestic currency per foreign unit]]. Hold the domestic-currency export price at 70 and the foreign-currency import price at 10. When $S$ rises from 7 to 7.7, the export price faced abroad falls from 10 to about 9.09, while the domestic import price rises from 70 to 77. This supplies a substitution incentive, not the size or date of the volume response.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要区分贸易数量与货币价值余额。若原来出口和进口各 10 件，本币净出口金额为 $70\times10-70\times10=0$；数量暂时不动时，贬值后反而是 $70\times10-77\times10=-70$。若另一个给定数量情景为出口 11、进口 9，则同样价格下金额为 $70\times11-77\times9=77$。后一个数须有需求与供给反应支持，不能从汇率变化单独推出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish volumes from the monetary trade balance. With initial exports and imports of ten units, net export value is zero. If volumes initially stay fixed, depreciation changes it to $70\times10-77\times10=-70$. In a separate assumed volume scenario of eleven exports and nine imports, the balance is $70\times11-77\times9=77$. The latter requires supported demand and supply responses, not exchange-rate arithmetic alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

货币政策相对于海外政策的变化可以影响汇率，但风险溢价与预期同时变化，不能把加息与升值固定配对；资产回报条件复用[[无抵补利率平价]]，制度约束复用[[不可能三角]]。汇率经进口成本影响物价由[[汇率传递]]处理，企业完整净敞口则见[[汇率变动与企业价值]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Relative domestic and foreign policy can influence exchange rates, but changing premia and expectations prevent a mechanical hike-appreciation rule. Reuse [[无抵补利率平价|uncovered interest parity]] for return conditions and the [[不可能三角|trilemma]] for regime constraints. [[汇率传递|Pass-through]] handles import-price effects; [[汇率变动与企业价值|exchange rates and firm value]] addresses firms' net exposures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
