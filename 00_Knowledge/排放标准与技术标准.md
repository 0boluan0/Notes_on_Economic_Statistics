# 排放标准约束排放表现而技术标准指定必须采用的控制手段
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Emissions performance standards constrain emissions outcomes, whereas technology standards prescribe control methods.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

排放绩效标准规定污染者须达到什么排放水平或排放强度，并在规则允许的范围内让其选择方法；技术标准则指定应采用的设备、工艺或控制方式。这里比较非交易型、针对污染源的两类要求，区别在**约束结果还是约束实现手段**，而不是一个强制、另一个不强制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An emissions performance standard specifies the level or rate a source must meet, allowing it to choose methods within the rules. A technology standard prescribes equipment, processes or controls. For these non-tradable source-level requirements, the distinction is outcomes versus prescribed means, not mandatory versus voluntary compliance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如要求某厂一年排放不超过 10 吨，是排放水平标准；要求每单位产品排放不超过 0.1 千克，是强度标准；要求安装并运行指定处理装置，则是技术要求。强度达标不保证总排放固定：产出翻倍、强度不变，总排放仍翻倍。即使每厂有排放上限，厂商数量改变也会改变行业总排放，不能把单源标准称为[[总量排放交易|全行业固定总量]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A ten-tonne annual limit is a level standard; a 0.1-kilogram-per-product limit is an intensity standard; installing and operating specified treatment equipment is a technology requirement. Constant compliant intensity allows total emissions to double when output doubles. Even per-source limits do not fix industry emissions if the number of sources changes; they are not an [[总量排放交易|industry-wide fixed cap]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

允许自选达标方式，通常能让企业寻找更低成本的投入或技术；指定设备会限制这些选择。但如果直接排放难测，而设备是否安装、运行较易检查，技术标准可能具有实施优势。所谓“基于技术可行性设定的排放限值”仍可能是绩效标准，并不等于强制使用那一种参考技术。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Method choice generally allows firms to find cheaper inputs or technologies; prescribed equipment restricts that flexibility. Yet technology standards can be easier to implement when emissions are hard to measure but equipment installation and operation are observable. An emissions limit based on feasible technology can still be a performance standard without mandating that reference technology.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不同厂商的减排成本差异很大时，每家一模一样的数量要求通常不能实现[[成本有效减排|给定总目标的最低成本分担]]。但不能由此断言任何标准都低效：成本近似一致，或标准已按相关差异合理设置时，差别可能较小。对局地环境约束还应检查[[排放总量与局地损害|排放地点造成的不同损害]]，不能只盯行业吨数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identical source requirements generally miss the [[成本有效减排|least-cost allocation for a given aggregate target]] when costs differ substantially. That does not make every standard inefficient: similar costs or appropriately differentiated requirements can narrow the gap. [[排放总量与局地损害|Location-specific damage]] also matters when the objective includes local environmental constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非交易型标准通常只要求达标，不给额外低于限值的减排一个持续价格奖励；但降低达标成本仍可能有利。[[排污费]]对每单位剩余排放收费，因而有不同的持续激励。执行中的排放计量可参考[[排放监测与履约|质量保证和记录检查]]，而具体达标义务由所选制度决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A non-tradable standard generally supplies no continuing price reward for reductions beyond compliance, although lowering compliance costs can still pay. An [[排污费|emissions charge]] prices every remaining unit and therefore provides a different ongoing incentive. [[排放监测与履约|Quality assurance and emissions-record checks]] support measurement; the chosen instrument determines the actual compliance obligation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
