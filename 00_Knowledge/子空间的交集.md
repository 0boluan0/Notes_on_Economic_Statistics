# Intersection of subspaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*子空间的交集*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **intersection** of two sets keeps only their common members. For [[子空间|subspaces]], this means keeping the vectors that satisfy both sets of requirements at once. The result is still a subspace: the addition and scaling rules that hold in each set continue to hold for vectors shared by both.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个集合的**交集**只保留共同成员。对于[[子空间]]，就是只保留同时满足两边要求的向量。结果仍是子空间：两边各自成立的加法、数乘规则，对于同时属于两边的向量仍然成立。

> [!example] Two planes share a line · 两个平面共用一条直线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $\mathbb R^3$, let $S$ be the $xy$-plane and $T$ the $xz$-plane:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\mathbb R^3$ 中，让 $S$ 为 $xy$ 平面，$T$ 为 $xz$ 平面：
>
> $$S=\{(x,y,0):x,y\in\mathbb R\},\qquad T=\{(x,0,z):x,z\in\mathbb R\}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To belong to $S$, a vector needs third coordinate zero; to belong to $T$, it needs second coordinate zero. Belonging to both therefore requires both coordinates to vanish, while the first can be any real number:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 属于 $S$ 要求第三坐标为零，属于 $T$ 要求第二坐标为零。同时属于两边，就要求这两个坐标都为零，第一坐标仍可取任意实数：
>
> $$S\cap T=\{(x,0,0):x\in\mathbb R\}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus the intersection is the $x$-axis. For instance, $(2,0,0)$ is in both planes; $(2,1,0)$ belongs only to $S$ and is excluded from the intersection.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 所以交集是 $x$ 轴。例如，$(2,0,0)$ 在两个平面内，$(2,1,0)$ 只在 $S$ 内，就被交集排除了。

## The definition and the closure argument
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*定义与封闭性证明*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $S,T$ be subspaces of the **same vector space over the same scalar field** $\mathbb F$. The symbol $\cap$ means intersection, and its definition requires membership in both sets:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $S,T$ 是**同一标量域** $\mathbb F$ 上**同一向量空间**的子空间。符号 $\cap$ 表示取交，定义要求同时属于两边：

$$S\cap T=\{v:v\in S\ \text{and}\ v\in T\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, the intersection contains zero, because both subspaces contain zero. Next take any two vectors $u,v$ in the intersection and any scalars $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F$. Both $u$ and $v$ belong to $S$, so closure in $S$ keeps $\alpha u+\beta v$ in $S$. The same two vectors also belong to $T$, so closure in $T$ keeps exactly the same result in $T$. Consequently
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

首先，交集含零，因为两个子空间都含零。再任取交集中的两个向量 $u,v$ 和任意标量 $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F$。$u,v$ 都在 $S$ 中，$S$ 的封闭性保证 $\alpha u+\beta v$ 仍在 $S$ 中；这两个向量也都在 $T$ 中，$T$ 的封闭性保证同一个结果也在 $T$ 中。因此

$$\alpha u+\beta v\in S\cap T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficients $\alpha,\beta$ specify how much of each whole vector to use. The argument works for every such choice, not just one numerical example. Zero plus closure under all these combinations is the subspace test, so $S\cap T$ is a subspace. No finite-dimensional assumption is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数 $\alpha,\beta$ 分别决定使用多少份完整的 $u,v$。上面的论证对每种选择都成立，不只是检查某一个数值例子。含零并对全部这些组合封闭，正是子空间判据，所以 $S\cap T$ 是子空间。这里不要求空间有限维。

> [!note] The common part can be only zero · 共同部分可以只有零
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The horizontal and vertical axes in $\mathbb R^2$ intersect at $\{(0,0)\}$. This is the zero subspace, **not the empty set**. Any two subspaces of the same ambient space share at least zero. At the other extreme, if $S\subseteq T$, then $S\cap T=S$: every vector in the smaller subspace already passes both membership tests.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\mathbb R^2$ 的横轴与纵轴交于 $\{(0,0)\}$。这是零子空间，**不是空集**。同一环境空间的任何两个子空间至少共享零向量。另一种情况是 $S\subseteq T$，此时 $S\cap T=S$：较小子空间中的每个向量已经同时满足两边的成员条件。

## Intersecting constraints
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同时施加约束*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The plane example can also be read as two homogeneous equations: $z=0$ and $y=0$. Intersecting their solution sets asks for both equations to hold. More generally, a matrix [[零空间|nullspace]] is the set of inputs satisfying its homogeneous system $Ax=0$. To satisfy two such systems on the same input, intersect their nullspaces; [[纵向拼接的零空间|vertically stacking the matrices]] writes those two requirements as one system.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面的两个平面也可以读成两个齐次方程：$z=0$ 和 $y=0$。把解集取交，就是要求两个方程同时成立。更一般地，矩阵的[[零空间]]收集满足齐次方程组 $Ax=0$ 的输入。要让同一个输入同时满足两组这样的方程，就取两个零空间的交集；[[纵向拼接的零空间|把矩阵上下拼接]]，就是把这两项要求写成一个方程组。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This differs from the [[子空间并集判据|union]], which retains a vector if it passes either membership test. For the two planes above, $(0,1,0)$ and $(0,0,1)$ belong to the union, but their sum $(0,1,1)$ belongs to neither plane. The intersection preserves closure because both inputs and their combination must pass both tests.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与[[子空间并集判据|并集]]不同：并集只要求通过两项成员测试中的任意一项。对于前面的两个平面，$(0,1,0)$ 和 $(0,0,1)$ 都在并集中，它们的和 $(0,1,1)$ 却不在任何一个平面内。交集保留封闭性，是因为参与运算的向量和运算结果都能通过两项测试。
