# 规范分析依据明确的价值标准判断经济结果是否值得选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Normative analysis judges whether economic outcomes are desirable under an explicit value criterion*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

规范分析回答“什么结果更好、应该选择什么”。它需要先说明评价标准，再判断可行方案是否符合这一标准。标准可以涉及效率、分配、权利或其他社会目标；它不是从一个观测数字中自动得出的。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Normative analysis asks which outcomes are better and what should be chosen. It states an evaluative criterion and assesses feasible alternatives against it. The criterion may concern efficiency, distribution, rights, or other social objectives; no single observed number determines it automatically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，“提高某项税率会筹到多少收入？”是[[实证分析|实证问题]]；“筹到这些收入是否值得承担相应负担？”还需说明收入用于什么、谁承担负担，以及如何评价这些后果。若采用[[社会福利函数]]，就要把所用的福利规则及效用口径写明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“How much revenue would a higher tax rate raise?” is a [[实证分析|positive question]]. “Is that revenue worth the resulting burdens?” also requires specifying its use, who bears those burdens, and how the consequences are evaluated. When using a [[社会福利函数|social welfare function]], state the chosen welfare rule and utility conventions explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

规范分析依赖价值判断，并不意味着不能写公式、计算后果或检验其事实前提；这一区别见[[规范分析的经验前提]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dependence on value judgments does not prevent formalisation, calculation, or testing factual premises; see [[规范分析的经验前提|the empirical premises of normative analysis]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
