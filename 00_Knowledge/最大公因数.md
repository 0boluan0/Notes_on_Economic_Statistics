# Greatest common divisor
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*最大公因数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose two ribbons have lengths $18$ and $24$ whole units. We want one positive whole-unit length that cuts both ribbons into equal pieces with nothing left over. Length $3$ works, and length $6$ works; length $8$ fails for the first ribbon. The **greatest common divisor** chooses the largest length that works for both. This picture introduces the idea for positive inputs; the definition below also handles negative integers and a single zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设两条带子的长度分别是 $18$ 和 $24$ 个整单位。我们想选一个正整数长度，把两条带子都切成同样长的整段，不留下余量。每段长 $3$ 可以，长 $6$ 也可以，但长 $8$ 无法切尽第一条。**最大公因数**选择同时适合两条带子的最大长度。这个图景先说明正输入的含义，下面的定义还覆盖负整数和只有一个输入为零的情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recall that [[整除|divisibility]] means an exact integer multiple: $d\mid a$ means $a=dk$ for some integer $k$. A **common divisor** satisfies that relation for both inputs. The symbol $\gcd(a,b)$ names a single number, while “$d$ divides both” is a condition that several numbers may satisfy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先回忆[[整除]]：$d\mid a$ 表示存在整数 $k$ 使 $a=dk$，即 $a$ 是 $d$ 的整数倍。**公因数**要同时满足对两个输入的整除关系。符号 $\gcd(a,b)$ 指一个数，而“$d$ 同时整除二者”是一个可能被多个数满足的条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The definition and a finite list
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定义与一个有限清单*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For integers $a,b$ not both zero, $\gcd(a,b)$ is the greatest positive integer that [[整除|divides]] both. The positive common divisors of $18$ and $24$ are $1,2,3,6$, hence $\gcd(18,24)=6$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对不全为零的整数 $a,b$，$\gcd(a,b)$ 是同时[[整除]] $a,b$ 的最大正整数。例如 $18$ 与 $24$ 的正公因子是 $1,2,3,6$，所以 $\gcd(18,24)=6$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see every candidate in the example, the positive divisors of $18$ are $1,2,3,6,9,18$, and those of $24$ are $1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24$. Their shared entries are $1,2,3,6$. Taking the largest gives $6$; merely finding the common divisor $3$ would not yet establish the greatest one. In general, the defining conditions for $g=\gcd(a,b)$ are the following, with the last implication required for every positive integer $d$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把例子中的候选列全：$18$ 的正因子是 $1,2,3,6,9,18$，$24$ 的正因子是 $1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24$，共同出现的是 $1,2,3,6$。取其中最大者得到 $6$；只找到公因数 $3$，还不足以证明它最大。一般地，$g=\gcd(a,b)$ 的定义条件如下，其中最后一条蕴含须对每个正整数 $d$ 成立：

$$g\in\mathbb Z_{>0},\qquad g\mid a,\quad g\mid b,\qquad
(d>0,\ d\mid a,\ d\mid b)\Longrightarrow d\le g.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mathbb Z_{>0}$ denotes the positive integers. Why is a greatest candidate guaranteed when the inputs are not both zero? The candidate set contains $1$. Choose a nonzero input $u$. If a positive integer $d$ divides $u$, write $u=dk$; then $k\ne0$ and $|k|\ge1$, so $d\le|u|$. All positive common divisors therefore lie in the finite list $1,\ldots,|u|$. A nonempty finite set of integers has a unique largest member. This establishes the definition before choosing a computation method.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $\mathbb Z_{>0}$ 表示正整数。为什么两个输入不全为零时一定有最大候选？候选集合至少包含 $1$。选一个非零输入 $u$；若正整数 $d$ 整除 $u$，写成 $u=dk$，则 $k\ne0$、$|k|\ge1$，因此 $d\le|u|$。所以所有正公因数都在有限清单 $1,\ldots,|u|$ 中。非空有限整数集合有唯一最大成员；这样就先确立了定义，再选择计算方法。

## Signs, zero and coprimality
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*符号、零与互素*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two integers are coprime when their gcd is $1$; neither needs to be prime, as $8$ and $15$ show. Signs do not change positive common divisors, so $\gcd(a,b)=\gcd(|a|,|b|)$, and $\gcd(a,0)=|a|$ for $a\ne0$. This vault uses the separate convention $\gcd(0,0)=0$; it does not assert that $0,0$ have a greatest positive common divisor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“互素”就是最大公因数等于 $1$，不要求两个数各自都是素数。例如 $8$ 与 $15$ 互素。符号不改变共同正因子，所以 $\gcd(a,b)=\gcd(|a|,|b|)$；对 $a\ne0$，$\gcd(a,0)=|a|$。本库约定 $\gcd(0,0)=0$，这是单独的延拓约定，不是说 $0,0$ 有最大正公因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $|a|$ means the nonnegative absolute value of $a$. If $a=dk$, then $-a=d(-k)$, and the converse follows by changing the sign again. Thus replacing an input by its absolute value preserves its positive divisors. Swapping the two inputs also changes no common-divisor condition, so $\gcd(a,b)=\gcd(b,a)$. For example, $\gcd(-18,24)=6$ and $\gcd(0,-9)=9$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记号 $|a|$ 是 $a$ 的非负绝对值。若 $a=dk$，则 $-a=d(-k)$；反向再变一次符号也成立。因此取绝对值不改变正因子。交换两个输入也不改变“同时整除”的条件，所以 $\gcd(a,b)=\gcd(b,a)$。例如 $\gcd(-18,24)=6$，$\gcd(0,-9)=9$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every positive integer divides $0$, because $0=d\cdot0$. Consequently, when $a\ne0$, the positive common divisors of $a$ and $0$ are exactly the positive divisors of $a$. The largest is $|a|$. When both inputs are zero, however, every positive integer is a common divisor, and any candidate $d$ is exceeded by another candidate $d+1$. There is no greatest positive one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个正整数都整除 $0$，因为 $0=d\cdot0$。所以当 $a\ne0$ 时，$a$ 与 $0$ 的正公因数恰好就是 $a$ 的正因子，其中最大者为 $|a|$。但若两个输入都为零，每个正整数都是公因数，候选 $d$ 后面总还有更大的候选 $d+1$，因此没有最大的正公因数。

> [!important] The value at two zeros is a convention · 双零取值是约定
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The extension $\gcd(0,0)=0$ lets the symbol and the algorithm cover every integer pair. It does not make zero positive, give an unbounded set a maximum, or assign a value to $0/0$. For every pair other than $(0,0)$, the gcd defined above is positive.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 延拓 $\gcd(0,0)=0$ 让记号和算法能够覆盖每一对整数，但没有把零变成正数，没有让无界集合获得最大元，也没有给 $0/0$ 赋值。除 $(0,0)$ 外，上面定义的 gcd 始终为正。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here a [[素数|prime]] is an integer greater than $1$ with exactly two positive divisors, $1$ and itself. A composite is an integer greater than $1$ that is not prime. These terms describe one integer at a time, whereas coprimality compares the common divisors of a pair.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里，[[素数]]是大于 $1$、恰好有 $1$ 与自身两个正因子的整数；合数是大于 $1$ 而不是素数的整数。这两个称谓分别描述单个整数，而互素比较一对整数的共同因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the coprime example, $8$ has positive divisors $1,2,4,8$ and $15$ has $1,3,5,15$; only $1$ is shared. Both inputs are composite, illustrating that coprimality is a relation between two integers rather than primality of either one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在互素例中，$8$ 的正因子为 $1,2,4,8$，$15$ 的正因子为 $1,3,5,15$，共同因子只有 $1$。两个输入本身都是合数，说明互素描述两个整数之间的关系，并不是判断某个输入是否为素数。

## From a target to a computation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从目标到计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definition specifies the target; the [[欧几里得算法|Euclidean algorithm]] finds it without prime factorization. [[Bézout等式|Bézout's identity]] then writes the gcd as an integer linear combination of the inputs, providing the bridge to constructing modular inverses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义告诉我们要找什么，[[欧几里得算法]]告诉我们怎样不靠素因数分解就找到它。[[Bézout等式]]进一步把这个共同因子写成输入的整数线性组合；这正是后来构造模逆元的桥梁。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Listing divisors explains the definition but can be cumbersome for large inputs. The linked algorithm repeatedly replaces the input pair while preserving its common divisors; the next card proves both why this preserves the answer and why the process ends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

列因子有助于理解定义，但输入很大时可能繁琐。所链算法不断替换输入对而保持公因子；下一张卡分别证明为什么答案不变，以及为什么过程会结束。
