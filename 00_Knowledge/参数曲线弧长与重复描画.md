# Arc length and repeated traversal
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*参数曲线弧长与重复描画*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Arc length measures the distance travelled along a [[参数曲线|parametrized curve]], including each return over an earlier segment. For example, $\gamma(t)=(t^2,0)$ for $-1\le t\le1$ travels from $(1,0)$ to $(0,0)$ and then back to $(1,0)$. Its endpoints coincide, but the journey has length $2$. The set of points visited is just a line segment of length $1$. Keeping the parameter interval and its traversal order distinguishes these quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

弧长测量沿[[参数曲线]]实际走过的路程，再次经过旧路段也要再次计入。例如，$\gamma(t)=(t^2,0)$、$-1\le t\le1$ 从 $(1,0)$ 走到 $(0,0)$，再回到 $(1,0)$。起终点相同，路程却为 $2$；经过的点集只是长度为 $1$ 的线段。保留参数区间及经过顺序，才能分清这几个量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Speed accumulates length
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*速率累积为长度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a<b$ be finite real endpoints and let $\gamma(t)=(x(t),y(t))$ be continuous on $[a,b]$. Assume it is **piecewise $C^1$**: the interval can be split into finitely many pieces on each of which the coordinate functions have continuous derivatives, with one-sided continuous extensions of the derivatives at the piece endpoints. Corners between pieces are allowed; jumps in position are not. These are sufficient conditions for the formula below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<b$ 是有限实端点，$\gamma(t)=(x(t),y(t))$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上连续。假设它**分段 $C^1$**：可以把区间分成有限段，每段上的坐标函数都有连续导数，并且导数能从该段内部单侧连续延伸到端点。段与段之间可以有拐角，但位置不能跳跃。这是一组保证下面公式成立的充分条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivative vector $\gamma'(t)=(x'(t),y'(t))$ gives the coordinate rates of change. Its [[欧氏范数|Euclidean norm]], the square root of the sum of squared coordinates, is the nonnegative **speed** $v(t)$. If $t$ is time, this is physical speed; otherwise it is length per unit of parameter. The total length is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

导数向量 $\gamma'(t)=(x'(t),y'(t))$ 给出两个坐标的变化率。它的[[欧氏范数]]——坐标平方和的非负平方根——就是非负的**速率** $v(t)$。若 $t$ 是时间，它就是物理速率；否则是每单位参数对应的长度变化。总长度为

$$
v(t)=\|\gamma'(t)\|_2=\sqrt{x'(t)^2+y'(t)^2},\qquad
\boxed{L(\gamma)=\int_a^b v(t)\,dt.}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Integrate piece by piece if derivatives differ across a corner. Assigning any finite values to the speed at those finitely many joining points does not change the integral. The notation $ds=v(t)\,dt$, with $t$ increasing, records the same length element. It uses speed, not a signed coordinate velocity: reversing direction cannot cancel distance already travelled.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若拐角两侧导数不同，就分段积分。在有限个接合点给速率任意指定有限值，都不影响积分。记号 $ds=v(t)\,dt$（$t$ 按递增方向取值）表达同一个弧长微元。这里使用速率，而不是带符号的坐标速度，所以反向运动不能抵消已经走过的路程。

> [!example] A turnaround and a pause · 掉头与瞬时停下
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $\gamma(t)=(t^2,0)$ on $[-1,1]$, the speed is $2|t|$, so $L=\int_{-1}^1 2|t|\,dt=2$. At $t=0$ the velocity is zero, but the formula still works. For $\eta(t)=(t^3,0)$ on the same interval, the speed is $3t^2$ and $L=2$ again: this curve moves from $(-1,0)$ to $(1,0)$ without retracing, despite its zero speed at the middle. A constant portion contributes zero length.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[-1,1]$ 上的 $\gamma(t)=(t^2,0)$，速率为 $2|t|$，所以 $L=\int_{-1}^1 2|t|\,dt=2$。$t=0$ 处速度为零，公式仍然成立。同一区间上的 $\eta(t)=(t^3,0)$ 速率为 $3t^2$，长度也为 $2$；它从 $(-1,0)$ 走到 $(1,0)$，中途虽瞬时零速，却没有往返。若曲线在一整段参数区间保持不动，那一段贡献零长度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus the nonzero-velocity condition used to identify a [[参数曲线切线的速度条件|regular tangent]] is not required for this length formula. If both coordinate lengths are in metres and $t$ is in seconds, $v$ is in metres per second and $L$ is in metres.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，识别[[参数曲线切线的速度条件|正则切线]]时使用的非零速度条件，并不是这个弧长公式的要求。若两个坐标以米计、$t$ 以秒计，那么 $v$ 的单位是米每秒，$L$ 的单位是米。

## Why adding short chords gives the integral
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么短弦长之和得到这个积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a finite partition $P:a=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_n=b$, as for a [[黎曼和|Riemann sum]], and join successive curve positions by straight segments. The polygonal length is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

像构造[[黎曼和]]那样，取有限分割 $P:a=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_n=b$，用直线段连接相邻曲线位置。所得折线长度为

$$L_P=\sum_{i=1}^n\|\gamma(t_i)-\gamma(t_{i-1})\|_2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding partition points cannot shorten this polygonal route, by the triangle inequality. Define $L(\gamma)=\sup_P L_P$: the [[上确界|supremum]] is the least bound on all these lengths, with value $+\infty$ if no finite bound exists. We now show that our regularity conditions make it finite and equal to the speed integral. It suffices to treat a $C^1$ piece; inserting the finitely many breakpoints and adding the results handles the full curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由三角不等式，增加分割点不会使折线路程缩短。定义 $L(\gamma)=\sup_P L_P$：[[上确界]]是所有这些长度的最小上界；若不存在有限上界，就取 $+\infty$。下面说明，在本卡条件下，它有限且等于速率积分。只需证明一个 $C^1$ 段；插入有限个分段点，再相加，就得到整条曲线的结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply [[Newton-Leibniz公式|the fundamental theorem of calculus]] to both coordinates. On a subinterval $[u,w]$, the displacement is the coordinatewise integral $\gamma(w)-\gamma(u)=\int_u^w\gamma'(s)\,ds$. The triangle inequality for finite vector sums, followed by their Riemann-sum limits, gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对两个坐标分别使用 [[Newton-Leibniz公式]]。在子区间 $[u,w]$ 上，位移就是逐坐标积分 $\gamma(w)-\gamma(u)=\int_u^w\gamma'(s)\,ds$。先对有限向量和使用三角不等式，再取黎曼和极限，得到

$$\|\gamma(w)-\gamma(u)\|_2\le\int_u^w\|\gamma'(s)\|_2\,ds.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hence every $L_P$ is at most $\int_a^b v$. For the reverse bound, continuity of $\gamma'$ on a closed piece gives [[连续函数在紧致集上一致连续|uniform continuity]]: for any $\varepsilon>0$, sufficiently short subintervals have derivative vectors differing by less than $\varepsilon$ throughout. Thus, with $\Delta t_i=t_i-t_{i-1}$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以每个 $L_P$ 都不超过 $\int_a^b v$。反向估计利用导数在闭区间上的连续性所带来的[[连续函数在紧致集上一致连续|一致连续性]]：给定 $\varepsilon>0$，只要子区间足够短，该段内各点的导数向量相差就都小于 $\varepsilon$。记 $\Delta t_i=t_i-t_{i-1}$，便有

$$
\left\|\gamma(t_i)-\gamma(t_{i-1})-\gamma'(t_{i-1})\Delta t_i\right\|_2
\le\varepsilon\Delta t_i.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking norms changes the corresponding chord length from $v(t_{i-1})\Delta t_i$ by at most the same bound. Adding gives an error at most $\varepsilon(b-a)$ between $L_P$ and a speed Riemann sum. As the largest piece width tends to zero, those sums approach $\int_a^b v$, and $\varepsilon$ can be arbitrarily small. This proves the other inequality and therefore the formula. No division by speed occurs, so zero-speed points cause no gap in the proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再取范数，相应弦长与 $v(t_{i-1})\Delta t_i$ 的差也不超过同一个界。相加后，$L_P$ 与速率的一个黎曼和之间误差至多为 $\varepsilon(b-a)$。当最大段宽趋于零，这些和趋于 $\int_a^b v$；而 $\varepsilon$ 可以任意小。这就证明另一方向的不等式，因而得到公式。证明没有除以速率，所以零速度点不会留下漏洞。

## Changing the clock versus repeating the route
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*改变计时方式与真正重复经过*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $\phi:[c,d]\to[a,b]$ is a monotone, one-to-one, onto, piecewise $C^1$ reparameterization, with $c<d$. The new curve is $\eta(u)=\gamma(\phi(u))$. The [[链式法则|chain rule]] and [[定积分换元|definite-integral substitution]], applied on the pieces, give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $\phi:[c,d]\to[a,b]$ 是单调、一一、满射、分段 $C^1$ 的重参数化，其中 $c<d$。新曲线为 $\eta(u)=\gamma(\phi(u))$。逐段使用[[链式法则]]与[[定积分换元]]，得到

$$
L(\eta)=\int_c^d\|\gamma'(\phi(u))\|_2\,|\phi'(u)|\,du
=\int_a^b\|\gamma'(t)\|_2\,dt=L(\gamma).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Increasing $\phi$ preserves direction; decreasing $\phi$ reverses it, with the absolute derivative compensating for the reversed bounds. Its derivative may vanish: for instance, $u\mapsto u^3$ on $[-1,1]$ still gives a valid change of clock. By contrast, tracing $(\cos t,\sin t)$ for $0\le t\le4\pi$ makes two full turns, so its speed $1$ gives length $4\pi$, twice the once-traced circumference. An isolated repeated endpoint adds no extra segment; a second traversal of a whole arc does. This is why [[同轨迹不等于同参数化|the same trace need not give the same parametrized journey]]. The same length element is used in [[旋转曲面面积与重复覆盖|surface area of revolution]], where surface coverage needs a further check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

递增的 $\phi$ 保持方向，递减的 $\phi$ 反转方向；导数的绝对值会补偿反向的积分限。$\phi'$ 可以为零，例如 $[-1,1]$ 上的 $u\mapsto u^3$ 仍是合法的计时改变。相反，在 $0\le t\le4\pi$ 上描画 $(\cos t,\sin t)$ 会完整走两圈，速率 $1$ 给出长度 $4\pi$，是单次圆周长的两倍。孤立的重复端点不增加额外路段；把整段弧再走一遍才会重复计长。这正是[[同轨迹不等于同参数化]]所强调的区别。同一个弧长微元也用于[[旋转曲面面积与重复覆盖]]，但那里还要另查表面覆盖次数。
