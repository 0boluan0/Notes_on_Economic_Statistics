# 收益成本比是在明确核算口径下效益现值与成本现值的比值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The benefit-cost ratio divides present benefits by present costs under an explicit accounting convention.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收益成本比（BCR）表示每单位已计入成本对应多少已计入效益。用同一评价时点和货币口径的效益现值 $PVB$、成本现值 $PVC>0$ 定义：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BCR reports included benefits per unit of included cost. With a common valuation date and monetary basis, define present benefits $PVB$ and strictly positive present costs $PVC$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
BCR=\frac{PVB}{PVC}.
$$

在同一套账里，$BCR>1$ 与 $PVB-PVC>0$ 等价。若效益现值 120、成本现值 100，则比值为 1.2，净收益为 20。比值描述相对大小，差额描述绝对净增量；一个成本 10、效益 15 的小方案比值更高，却只有 5 的净收益。互斥选择的逻辑见 [[互斥项目的增量 NPV]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within the same ledger, $BCR>1$ is equivalent to positive net benefit. Benefits of 120 and costs of 100 give a ratio of 1.2 and a net gain of 20. A smaller option costing ten and yielding fifteen has a higher ratio but a net gain of only five; see [[互斥项目的增量 NPV|incremental comparison of mutually exclusive projects]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解释比值时必须说明分子分母装了什么。例如一笔边界内的纯转移 100 若同时列作效益和成本，净额仍是 $120-100=20$，比值却从 $120/100=1.2$ 变为 $220/200=1.1$。这不是项目突然更差，而是比值受核算表示影响。先用 [[转移支付核算]] 对齐，再用一致约定比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
State what enters each side. Adding an internal transfer of 100 to both benefits and costs leaves the net gain at 20 but changes the ratio from 1.2 to 1.1. This is an accounting effect, not a change in the project. Reconcile [[转移支付核算|transfers]] and use consistent conventions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若把不利影响列为“负效益”而另一套账列为“成本”，也可能改变比值而不改变净现值。未货币化影响、分配及预算约束并不会因比值大于 1 而消失；政策使用边界见 [[公共项目净现值边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recording a harm as a negative benefit rather than a cost can likewise change BCR without changing NPV. Unmonetised impacts, distribution and budget constraints remain relevant; see [[公共项目净现值边界|the limits of social NPV]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
