# 拓扑基础：开集、闭集与连续
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Topology foundations: open sets, closed sets, and continuity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 不依赖具体坐标公式地表达“靠近”“边界”“极限”和“连续”，为优化与分析提供语言。
> **具体锚点：** 开区间 $(0,1)$ 每个点都能容纳一个仍留在集合内的小邻域，但端点 0、1 是边界点却不属于集合。
> **核心难点：** 开与闭不是互斥日常形容词；集合可以既开又闭，也可二者都不是，判断总是相对于给定空间。
> **为什么重要：** 极值存在、约束集合、收敛和函数连续性的严谨条件都依赖这些概念。
> **继续：** 先从度量空间的球理解开集，再连接闭包、紧致与连续映射。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Topology expresses nearness, boundaries, limits, and continuity without depending on a particular coordinate formula.
> **Concrete anchor:** Every point of $(0,1)$ has a small neighbourhood contained in the interval, while $0$ and $1$ are boundary points not contained in it.
> **Central difficulty:** Open and closed are not mutually exclusive everyday labels. A set may be both, neither, or one of them, always relative to an ambient space.
> **Why it matters:** Rigorous conditions for convergence, feasible sets, existence of extrema, and continuity use this language.
> **Continue with:** Begin with metric balls, then connect interior, closure, boundary, compactness, and continuous maps.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 度量、邻域与开球
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Metrics, neighbourhoods, and open balls*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

度量 d 满足非负、区分点、对称和三角不等式。开球 $B_r(x)=\{y:d(x,y)<r\}$ 表达 x 的 r 邻域。不同度量可产生不同几何，但若生成同一开集族则给同一拓扑。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A metric $d$ is nonnegative, separates distinct points, is symmetric, and satisfies the triangle inequality. The open ball $B_r(x)=\{y:d(x,y)<r\}$ expresses an $r$-neighbourhood of $x$. Different metrics may give different geometry, but metrics that generate the same family of open sets induce the same topology.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 开集与闭集
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Open and closed sets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集合 U 开，若每个点都有某个开球完全包含在 U 中；F 闭，若包含其所有极限点，等价于补集开。空集和整个空间既开又闭。子空间拓扑下，集合的开闭性可能与更大空间不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A set $U$ is open if every point has an open ball wholly contained in $U$. A set $F$ is closed if it contains all its limit points, equivalently if its complement is open. The empty set and the whole space are both open and closed. In a subspace topology, a set's status may differ from its status in the larger space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 内部、边界与闭包
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Interior, boundary, and closure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内部是所有内点，闭包是集合加上所有极限点，边界是每个邻域同时碰到集合与补集的点。集合开当且仅当等于内部，闭当且仅当等于闭包。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The interior consists of all interior points; the closure adds all limit points to the set; and a boundary point has every neighbourhood meeting both the set and its complement. A set is open exactly when it equals its interior and closed exactly when it equals its closure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 序列与连续
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sequences and continuity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在度量空间中，$x_n\to x$ 表示距离趋零。函数连续可用 epsilon–delta、开集原像为开集或保持序列极限刻画。连续是局部性质，不要求可导。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a metric space, $x_n\to x$ means that the distance tends to zero. Continuity can be characterised by epsilon–delta conditions, by preimages of open sets being open, or by preservation of sequential limits. Continuity is local and does not require differentiability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 紧致性与极值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Compactness and extrema*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\mathbb R^n$ 中，紧致等价于闭且有界；一般空间中不能直接照搬。连续函数在非空紧致集上取得最大和最小值，这解释了优化中为何“闭且有界的可行域”如此常见。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $\mathbb R^n$, compactness is equivalent to being closed and bounded; this equivalence does not transfer automatically to arbitrary spaces. A continuous function on a nonempty compact set attains its maximum and minimum, explaining the recurring role of closed and bounded feasible sets in finite-dimensional optimisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 连通性入口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Entry point to connectedness*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连通集不能分成两个不交非空相对开集；区间是实线上的连通集。连续像保持紧致和连通，为介值和极值结论提供统一视角。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A connected set cannot be separated into two disjoint, nonempty relatively open sets; intervals are precisely the connected subsets of the real line. Continuous images preserve compactness and connectedness, unifying the extreme-value and intermediate-value results.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：同一集合为何可开可闭
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: why the same set can be open or closed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在整个实线 $\mathbb R$ 中，$[0,1]$ 是闭集但不是开集：端点的任意实线邻域都会超出该集合。若把环境空间改成 $X=[0,1]$，则 $[0,1]=X$ 在子空间中既开又闭。因此“开”不是图形没有端点的绝对属性，而是集合与环境空间的关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the ambient real line $\mathbb R$, $[0,1]$ is closed but not open because every real-line neighbourhood of an endpoint leaves the set. If the ambient space is changed to $X=[0,1]$, then the same set equals the entire space and is both open and closed in the subspace topology. “Open” is therefore a relation to an ambient space, not an absolute visual property of missing endpoints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 诊断顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnostic sequence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断一个结论前，依次问：环境空间是什么？度量或拓扑是什么？对象是点、序列、集合还是函数？要证局部性质还是全局存在性？“闭且有界即紧致”只能在有限维 Euclidean 空间等适用语境中直接使用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before applying a result, ask: What is the ambient space? Which metric or topology is used? Is the object a point, sequence, set, or map? Is the desired property local or a global existence statement? In particular, “closed and bounded implies compact” may be used directly only in settings such as finite-dimensional Euclidean space where the Heine–Borel theorem applies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Minimum self-check*

### 为什么一个集合可以既开又闭？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a set be both open and closed?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 开闭是关于邻域和补集的性质，不是互斥标签；空集和整个空间的补集彼此，二者都既开又闭。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Openness and closedness concern neighbourhoods and complements rather than mutually exclusive labels. The empty set and the whole space are complements of each other, and both are open and closed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 在 $\mathbb R^n$ 中闭且有界为何重要？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is being closed and bounded important in $\mathbb R^n$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> Heine–Borel 定理使其紧致，连续函数因此能在其上取得极值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The Heine–Borel theorem makes such a set compact, so every continuous real-valued function on it attains its extrema.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 开闭性为什么要说明所在空间？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must the ambient space be stated when discussing openness or closedness?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 子空间的邻域和补集改变；例如 [0,1] 在自身作为空间时是开集，在实线中不是开集。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Neighbourhoods and complements change in a subspace. For example, $[0,1]$ is open when treated as the whole ambient space but is not open in the real line.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 如何用开集原像表述函数连续？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How is continuity stated using preimages of open sets?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 对值域中每个开集 $V$，原像 $f^{-1}(V)$ 都必须是定义域中的开集；这个定义不需要坐标或距离公式。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every open set $V$ in the codomain, the preimage $f^{-1}(V)$ must be open in the domain. This formulation requires neither coordinates nor a distance formula.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
