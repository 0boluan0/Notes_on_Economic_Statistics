# Sample mean vector
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*样本均值向量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **sample mean vector** averages several observations while keeping their variables separate. If each student has a study-time measurement and a test score, it reports the average study time and the average score as two coordinates. It does not average hours together with points, and it does not turn all the entries into one number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**样本均值向量**对多个观测取平均，但保留不同变量的区分。若每名学生都有学习时长与测验分数，它把平均学习时长和平均分数作为两个坐标分别报告，不会把小时和分数混在一起，也不会把所有元素压成一个数。

> [!example] Three students, two variables · 三名学生，两个变量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose the recorded values are as follows. There are $n=3$ observations and $p=2$ variables. Average down each column:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设记录如下。观测数为 $n=3$，变量数为 $p=2$；沿每一列取平均：
>
> | Observation · 观测 | Study time (hours) · 学习时长（小时） | Test score (points) · 测验分数（分） |
> |---|---:|---:|
> | 1 | 2 | 61 |
> | 2 | 5 | 74 |
> | 3 | 7 | 83 |
>
> $$\bar x=\begin{bmatrix}(2+5+7)/3\\(61+74+83)/3\end{bmatrix}
> =\begin{bmatrix}14/3\\218/3\end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first coordinate is about $4.67$ hours and the second about $72.67$ points. The result still has $p=2$ coordinates; $n=3$ determines how many observations were averaged, not the length of the answer.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一坐标约为 $4.67$ 小时，第二坐标约为 $72.67$ 分。结果仍有 $p=2$ 个坐标；$n=3$ 决定平均了多少份观测，并不决定答案的长度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the $i$th observation as a column $x_i=(x_{i1},\ldots,x_{ip})^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. The first index $i$ names an observation; the second index $j$ names a variable. For positive integers $n,p$, the sample mean is the $p$-dimensional vector
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把第 $i$ 份观测写成列向量 $x_i=(x_{i1},\ldots,x_{ip})^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。第一个下标 $i$ 指哪一份观测，第二个下标 $j$ 指哪一个变量。对正整数 $n,p$，样本均值是以下 $p$ 维向量：

$$
\boxed{\bar x=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n x_i},
\qquad \bar x_j=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n x_{ij}\quad(j=1,\ldots,p).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sum is taken coordinate by coordinate. For a given coordinate, every row must record the same variable in compatible units. Different coordinates can have different units, as hours and points do here. With $n=1$, the mean is the single observation; with no observations, this formula is undefined because it divides by zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求和逐坐标进行。同一坐标在各行必须记录同一个变量，并使用一致单位；不同坐标则可以有不同单位，就像这里的小时和分数。$n=1$ 时，均值就是唯一一份观测；没有观测时不能用这个公式，因为分母为零。

## Averaging the columns of a data matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 从数据矩阵逐列求平均

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the [[多元数据矩阵|data-matrix convention]] that observations are rows and variables are columns, let $X_{\mathrm{data}}$ be the $n\times p$ matrix whose $i$th row is $x_i^T$. Let $\mathbf1_n$ be a column of $n$ ones. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按[[多元数据矩阵]]的约定，观察在行、变量在列。令 $X_{\mathrm{data}}$ 为 $n\times p$ 数据矩阵，第 $i$ 行是 $x_i^T$，再令 $\mathbf1_n$ 为 $n$ 个一组成的列向量，则

$$\bar x=\frac1nX_{\mathrm{data}}^T\mathbf1_n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The transpose has size $p\times n$; multiplying by the ones adds the $n$ entries for each of the $p$ variables. Dividing by $n$ turns those sums into averages. Although its name contains a capital $X$, $X_{\mathrm{data}}$ here denotes the already recorded numerical matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

转置后的尺寸为 $p\times n$；乘以全一向量，就为每个变量加总其 $n$ 个观测，再除以 $n$ 变成平均。虽然记号里有大写 $X$，这里的 $X_{\mathrm{data}}$ 指已经记录下来的数值矩阵。

## The random statistic and its observed value
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*随机统计量与已经算出的数值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before observing the data, a probability model may describe the observations as [[随机向量|random vectors]] $X_1,\ldots,X_n\in\mathbb R^p$ on one common probability space. Their [[统计量|statistic]]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在观察数据之前，概率模型可以把这些观测描述为同一概率空间上的[[随机向量]] $X_1,\ldots,X_n\in\mathbb R^p$。由它们构成的[[统计量]]为

$$\bar X=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^nX_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This rule varies across repeated possible samples. Once the actual vectors $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ are recorded, substituting them gives its observed value $\bar x$. Thus $E[\bar X]$ describes an average over the sampling model; it does not mean averaging the two coordinates of the recorded $\bar x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个规则会随可能抽到的样本而变化。实际记录到 $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ 后，代入得到实现值 $\bar x$。所以 $E[\bar X]$ 描述的是对抽样模型取平均，不是在已经算出的 $\bar x$ 内部再把两个坐标平均一次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Defining this finite average does not require independence, identical distributions, or finite moments. Those assumptions matter when making probability claims about it. A [[均值向量|population mean vector]] is a property of a specified probability law; the sample mean is calculated from data. [[样本均值无偏性|Unbiasedness of the sample mean]] explains when its sampling expectation equals a chosen mean, while [[样本均值协方差|its covariance]] explains its variation across samples. The data matrix alone [[数据矩阵不推出抽样模型|does not supply the sampling assumptions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义这个有限平均，不要求独立、同分布或有限矩；对它作概率推断时，才需要相应假设。[[均值向量|总体均值向量]]是指定概率规律的性质，样本均值则从数据计算。[[样本均值无偏性]]说明其抽样期望何时等于所选均值，[[样本均值协方差]]说明它在样本之间怎样波动。数据矩阵本身[[数据矩阵不推出抽样模型|不会自动给出抽样假设]]。
