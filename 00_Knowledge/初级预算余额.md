# 初级预算余额是在财政余额中剔除利息支出或明示净利息支出后的余额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The primary fiscal balance removes interest expense, or explicitly defined net interest expense, from the fiscal balance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

初级余额把既有债务利息单独拿出来，看本期收入能否覆盖非利息支出。本卡在总债务分析中采用总利息支出$I$调整：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The primary balance separates inherited debt-service interest from revenue and non-interest expenditure. For gross-debt analysis, this card adds back gross interest expense $I$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
PB=OB+I,
$$

其中$OB$是[[财政赤字|净借出／净借入余额]]，正数为盈余。如果$OB=-10$、利息支出24，则$PB=14$：政府有初级盈余，整体上却仍有赤字。两句话不矛盾，24的利息超过14的非利息盈余。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$OB$ is the [[财政赤字|net lending/borrowing balance]], positive for a surplus. With $OB=-10$ and interest expense 24, $PB=14$: a primary surplus coexists with an overall deficit because interest exceeds the non-interest surplus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若分析净债务并剔除净利息，利息收入也要扣回。若上述$OB=-10$已经包含利息收入4，则这一口径的初级余额为$-10+24-4=10$，不是14。报告时明确两种定义，别在[[债务率动态]]中混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net-debt analysis may remove net interest, also subtracting interest revenue. If the stated $OB=-10$ already includes interest revenue of 4, that primary balance is $-10+24-4=10$, not 14. State the convention before using [[债务率动态|debt-ratio dynamics]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“初级”也不等于“完全由政府主动控制”。既有税制和给付规则会随周期变动，收入与非利息支出仍受[[财政自动稳定器]]影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Primary” does not mean entirely discretionary. Revenue and non-interest expenditure still respond to the cycle through [[财政自动稳定器|automatic stabilisers]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
