# Invertibility of positive-definite and semidefinite matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正定与半正定矩阵的可逆性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[正定矩阵|positive-definite matrix]] never erases a nonzero input, so its action can be reversed. A [[半正定矩阵|positive-semidefinite matrix]] may erase some directions, but need not do so. The difference is whether a nonzero zero direction is present, not the word “semi” by itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[正定矩阵]]不会把非零输入抹成零，因此它的作用可以撤销。[[半正定矩阵]]可能抹去某些方向，但不一定会这样。“半”字本身不决定可逆性，关键在于是否存在非零零方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ sends $(u,v)^T$ to $(u,0)^T$. Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Its quadratic form is $u^2\ge0$, yet the second coordinate is lost. Infinitely many inputs produce the same output, so no [[逆矩阵|inverse matrix]] can recover every original input. By contrast, $B=\operatorname{diag}(1,2)$ is positive definite, and $B^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(1,1/2)$ reverses its two scalings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，[[对角矩阵]] $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ 把 $(u,v)^T$ 送到 $(u,0)^T$。其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。它的二次型 $u^2\ge0$，但第二个坐标丢失了。无穷多个输入给出同一个输出，所以不存在能恢复全部原输入的[[逆矩阵]]。相比之下，$B=\operatorname{diag}(1,2)$ 正定，$B^{-1}=\operatorname{diag}(1,1/2)$ 能撤销它的坐标缩放。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The exact boundary
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*精确的边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work with finite $n\ge1$ and a real symmetric or complex [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]] $n\times n$ matrix $A$. Write $A\succeq0$ for positive semidefiniteness and $A\succ0$ for positive definiteness. An invertible matrix has a two-sided inverse; a singular matrix has none.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n\ge1$ 为有限整数，$A$ 是实对称或复 [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]] 的 $n\times n$ 矩阵。用 $A\succeq0$ 表示半正定，$A\succ0$ 表示正定。可逆矩阵有双侧逆，奇异矩阵则没有。

> [!important] Within the semidefinite class · 在半正定范围内
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Positive definiteness always implies invertibility. Conversely, positive semidefiniteness together with invertibility implies positive definiteness:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 正定必可逆；反过来，半正定加上可逆，就能推出正定：
>
> $$A\succ0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\bigl(A\succeq0\ \text{and }A\text{ is invertible}\bigr).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus a positive-semidefinite matrix that is not positive definite must be singular. Both outcomes are possible within the semidefinite class: the opening $A$ is singular, while $B$ is invertible. The zero matrix is also semidefinite and singular for every $n\ge1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，半正定但非正定的矩阵必然奇异。半正定范围内两种情形都可能出现：开头的 $A$ 奇异，$B$ 可逆。对每个 $n\ge1$，零矩阵也半正定且奇异。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why a zero direction controls reversibility
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么零方向决定能否撤销*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[零空间|nullspace]] $N(A)=\{x:Ax=0\}$ contains the inputs erased by $A$. For a finite square matrix, a trivial nullspace $N(A)=\{0\}$ is equivalent to invertibility: no nonzero relation among its $n$ columns means those columns form a basis of the $n$-dimensional output space. Every output then has exactly one input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[零空间]] $N(A)=\{x:Ax=0\}$ 收集被 $A$ 抹去的输入。对有限阶方阵，平凡零空间 $N(A)=\{0\}$ 等价于可逆：$n$ 列之间没有非零线性关系，就构成 $n$ 维输出空间的一组基，每个输出恰好对应一个输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A\succ0$, a nonzero vector with $Ax=0$ would give $x^*Ax=0$, contradicting strict positivity. Thus the nullspace is trivial. Conversely, assume $A\succeq0$ and $A$ is invertible. The [[半正定零方向|semidefinite zero-direction theorem]] says $x^*Ax=0$ exactly when $Ax=0$. Invertibility excludes this for nonzero $x$, and semidefiniteness excludes negative values. Therefore $x^*Ax>0$ for every nonzero $x$. For real vectors, replace $x^*$ by $x^T$; $*$ denotes conjugate transpose.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A\succ0$，非零向量满足 $Ax=0$ 就会给出 $x^*Ax=0$，与严格正性矛盾，所以零空间平凡。反过来，假设 $A\succeq0$ 且可逆。[[半正定零方向]]定理说明，$x^*Ax=0$ 恰好等价于 $Ax=0$；可逆性排除了非零 $x$ 出现这种情况，半正定又排除了负值，于是所有非零 $x$ 都有 $x^*Ax>0$。实数情形用 $x^T$；星号表示共轭转置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[正定与半正定的谱判据|spectral explanation]] says the same thing: semidefiniteness gives eigenvalues $\lambda_j\ge0$, invertibility excludes zero eigenvalues, and the remaining eigenvalues are all positive. Equivalently, within this class, a nonzero [[可逆性与非零行列式|determinant]] is enough. Why the inverse of a positive-definite matrix is itself positive definite is proved separately in [[正定逆仍正定|the positive-definite inverse theorem]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[正定与半正定的谱判据|谱解释]]表达同一件事：半正定保证特征值 $\lambda_j\ge0$，可逆排除零特征值，剩下的便全部严格为正。等价地，在这一范围内，[[可逆性与非零行列式|行列式]]非零就已经足够。正定矩阵的逆为什么仍正定，由[[正定逆仍正定]]单独证明。

> [!warning] Invertibility alone says nothing about positivity · 仅可逆不保证正性
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The matrix $D=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ is invertible, but its quadratic values have both signs. Even a positive determinant alone is insufficient: $-I_2$ has determinant $1$ and is negative definite. The semidefinite assumption in the reverse implication is essential.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $D=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ 可逆，二次型却有正有负。甚至只要求行列式为正也不够：$-I_2$ 的行列式为 $1$，但它负定。反向推理中的半正定前提不可省略。

## Before inverting a Gram or covariance matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*给 Gram 或协方差矩阵求逆之前*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real $m\times n$ matrix $C$, its Gram matrix $G=C^TC$ is always semidefinite because $x^TGx=\|Cx\|_2^2\ge0$. Here $\|Cx\|_2^2$ is the sum of the squares of the real output coordinates. It is positive definite and invertible exactly when $Cx=0$ has only the zero solution, that is, when the columns of $C$ are linearly independent. The [[满列秩投影公式|full-column-rank projection formula]] uses precisely this condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实 $m\times n$ 矩阵 $C$，Gram 矩阵 $G=C^TC$ 总半正定，因为 $x^TGx=\|Cx\|_2^2\ge0$。这里 $\|Cx\|_2^2$ 是实输出向量各坐标的平方和。它正定且可逆，恰好是 $Cx=0$ 只有零解，也就是 $C$ 的列线性无关时。[[满列秩投影公式]]使用的正是这个条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For instance, $C=(1\ \ 1)$ has $G=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$ and $C(1,-1)^T=0$. Forming a Gram matrix has not restored the information lost by $C$. Covariance matrices likewise require a separate check: [[协方差奇异性|covariance singularity]] describes zero-variance linear combinations, and [[样本协方差秩边界|the sample covariance rank bound]] describes the restriction caused by the number of observations. Being a covariance matrix alone does not justify an ordinary inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $C=(1\ \ 1)$ 给出 $G=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}$，且 $C(1,-1)^T=0$；形成 Gram 矩阵并没有恢复 $C$ 已丢掉的信息。协方差矩阵也需单独检查：[[协方差奇异性]]解释零方差线性组合，[[样本协方差秩边界]]说明观测数量造成的秩限制。不能只因“它是协方差矩阵”就直接求普通逆。
