# 正定矩阵必可逆而半正定矩阵可以奇异
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A positive-definite matrix is invertible, whereas a positive-semidefinite matrix may be singular*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心结论
> 对实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵，
> $$A\succ0\Longrightarrow A\text{ 可逆}.$$
> 而 $A\succeq0$ 允许零特征值，所以可能奇异；反过来，若 $A\succeq0$ 且可逆，则 $A\succ0$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real symmetric or complex Hermitian positive-definite matrix is invertible. A semidefinite matrix may be singular; if it is semidefinite and invertible, it is in fact positive definite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

谱分解给出最短证明。正定时所有 $\lambda_i>0$，所以没有零特征值；半正定时 $\lambda_i\ge0$，允许某些 $\lambda_i=0$，例如 $\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$。正定矩阵求逆后为什么仍正定，见 [[正定逆仍正定]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The spectral proof is immediate: positive eigenvalues exclude zero, whereas semidefiniteness allows zero eigenvalues, as in $\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$. The definiteness of the inverse is treated in [[正定逆仍正定|the positive-definite inverse theorem]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个区别在 Gram 矩阵和协方差矩阵中经常出现。$C^TC$ 何时正定可逆，见 [[满列秩投影公式]]；协方差的零方向与样本量造成的秩限制，分别见 [[协方差奇异性]] 和 [[样本协方差秩边界]]。不能因“它是协方差矩阵”就自动求逆。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This distinction matters for Gram and covariance matrices. [[满列秩投影公式|The full-column-rank projection result]] gives the invertibility boundary for $C^TC$; [[协方差奇异性|the covariance singularity criterion]] and [[样本协方差秩边界|the sample covariance rank bound]] cover the corresponding covariance questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么“正半定且 determinant 非零”已经能推出正定？
>
> **答案：** 半正定使全部特征值非负，determinant 非零排除零特征值，因此全部严格为正。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why do positive semidefiniteness and a nonzero determinant together imply positive definiteness?
>
> **Answer:** Positive semidefiniteness makes every eigenvalue nonnegative, while a nonzero determinant rules out zero eigenvalues. Hence every eigenvalue is strictly positive.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
