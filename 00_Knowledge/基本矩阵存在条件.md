# When does the fundamental matrix exist?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*基本矩阵存在条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To count all visits before absorption, we want to add the probabilities of visiting at time zero, time one, time two, and so on. The result is finite only when the transient part loses probability fast enough. This card explains when that infinite sum is legitimate, and why calculating an inverse alone is not enough for an arbitrary matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了统计吸收前的全部访问，我们想把时刻零、时刻一、时刻二等访问概率相加。暂态区的概率必须消失得足够快，总量才有限。本卡说明何时可以作这个无穷求和，也解释为什么对任意矩阵，单独算出一个逆还不够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[吸收链标准形|canonical form]] $P=\begin{pmatrix}Q&R\\0&I\end{pmatrix}$, suppose there are $t\ge1$ transient states. The $t\times t$ block $Q$ describes moves within that group. Its entries are nonnegative and each row sum is at most one. Starting at transient $i$, the row sum of $Q^n$ is the probability of remaining unabsorbed after $n$ steps. Thus $Q^n\to0$ says that this survival probability vanishes from every transient start.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[吸收链标准形]] $P=\begin{pmatrix}Q&R\\0&I\end{pmatrix}$ 中，设暂态状态数为 $t\ge1$。$t\times t$ 矩阵 $Q$ 描述暂态区内部的转移，条目非负，每行总和不超过一。从暂态 $i$ 出发，$Q^n$ 的行和是 $n$ 步后仍未吸收的概率。因此 $Q^n\to0$ 表示每个暂态初值的存活概率都消失。

> [!important] Three equivalent convergence checks · 三种等价的收敛检查
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a fixed finite $t\times t$ real or complex matrix $Q$, with $t\ge1$,
>
> $$Q^n\longrightarrow0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\rho(Q)<1
> \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}Q^n\text{ converges}.$$
>
> Here [[谱半径|spectral radius]] $\rho(Q)$ is the largest absolute value of the complex eigenvalues. Convergence means convergence of every matrix entry, equivalently convergence in any fixed matrix norm in this finite dimension. The sum is called a **Neumann series**, and $I_t$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. Whenever these conditions hold,
>
> $$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}Q^n=(I_t-Q)^{-1},\qquad Q^0=I_t.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对固定的有限 $t\times t$ 实矩阵或复矩阵 $Q$（$t\ge1$），上述三条件等价。[[谱半径]] $\rho(Q)$ 是全部复特征值模的最大值；矩阵收敛表示每个元素收敛，等价于该有限维空间中任一固定矩阵范数下的收敛。这个和叫 **Neumann 级数**。条件成立时，其和等于 $(I_t-Q)^{-1}$；$Q^0=I_t$ 是[[单位矩阵]]，并非零矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the sum is an inverse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么级数之和是矩阵逆*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equivalence between power decay and $\rho(Q)<1$ is the [[矩阵幂趋零判据|vanishing-power theorem]], which does not require diagonalization. To see the remaining steps, let $S_m=I_t+Q+\cdots+Q^m$, where $m\ge0$ is an integer. Multiplying and cancelling consecutive powers gives

$$(I_t-Q)S_m=S_m(I_t-Q)=I_t-Q^{m+1}.$$

If $Q^n\to0$ and $(I_t-Q)x=0$, then $x=Q^nx\to0$, so $x=0$. A square matrix with no nonzero null vector is [[逆矩阵|invertible]]. Consequently $S_m=(I_t-Q)^{-1}(I_t-Q^{m+1})$ tends to $(I_t-Q)^{-1}$. Conversely, if the partial sums converge, their differences $S_m-S_{m-1}=Q^m$ tend to zero. These arguments justify the series rather than formally manipulating an unverified infinite sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

幂趋零与 $\rho(Q)<1$ 的等价性来自[[矩阵幂趋零判据]]，不要求可对角化。其余步骤可以直接看：令 $S_m=I_t+Q+\cdots+Q^m$，整数 $m\ge0$，相乘后相邻幂抵消，得到上式。若 $Q^n\to0$ 且 $(I_t-Q)x=0$，则 $x=Q^nx\to0$，只能有 $x=0$。方阵没有非零零空间向量，便是[[逆矩阵|可逆矩阵]]。于是 $S_m=(I_t-Q)^{-1}(I_t-Q^{m+1})$ 趋于 $(I_t-Q)^{-1}$。反过来，若部分和收敛，则差 $S_m-S_{m-1}=Q^m$ 趋零。这里是在证明级数合法，而不是形式上操纵尚未核实的无穷和。

## What the probability structure adds
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*概率结构带来什么额外保证*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a genuine finite absorbing chain, [[吸收链必然吸收|almost-sure absorption]] gives $Q^n\to0$. More concretely, finitely many routes to absorption give an integer block length $L\ge1$ and a common lower bound $0<\delta<1$ for the probability of absorption within the next $L$ steps from every transient state. Survival over $k$ blocks is at most $(1-\delta)^k$. That geometric bound also makes total expected pre-absorption visits finite. A one-step row sum of exactly one does not prevent this: a later step can still lead out.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对真正的有限吸收链，[[吸收链必然吸收|几乎必然吸收]]给出 $Q^n\to0$。更具体地，有限条吸收路线提供共同的整数区段长度 $L\ge1$ 及成功概率下界 $0<\delta<1$：从每个暂态出发，随后 $L$ 步内吸收的概率至少为 $\delta$。经过 $k$ 段仍存活的概率至多为 $(1-\delta)^k$；几何界还保证吸收前的期望总访问次数有限。某行一步总和恰为一并不妨碍这一点，因为之后仍可以离开。

> [!example] No immediate exit can still mean quick absorption · 没有立即出口仍可很快吸收
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $Q=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$. From transient state $1$ one must first visit transient state $2$; from state $2$ one leaves the transient set on the next step. Although the first row sum is one, $Q^2=0$, so
>
> $$\sum_{n\ge0}Q^n=I_2+Q=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> The process is absorbed within two steps. Requiring every row sum to be strictly below one would unnecessarily exclude this chain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $Q=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$。从暂态1必须先到暂态2；从暂态2下一步就离开暂态区。尽管第一行和为一，仍有 $Q^2=0$，级数只剩 $I_2+Q$。两步内必定吸收，所以若强制要求每行和都严格小于一，就会不必要地排除这个例子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is an extra equivalence for a **finite nonnegative substochastic** $Q$: $I_t-Q$ is invertible if and only if the three convergence conditions hold. Here is a direct reason. Let $\mathbf1$ be the column of $t$ ones and $h_n=Q^n\mathbf1$. Since $Q\mathbf1\le\mathbf1$ and $Q$ is nonnegative, $h_n$ decreases coordinatewise to a vector $h\ge0$. Finite matrix multiplication passes through this limit, giving $Qh=h$. If $I_t-Q$ is invertible, then $h=0$. Each nonnegative entry of $Q^n$ is at most its row sum, so $Q^n\to0$. The reverse implication was proved above. The monotone argument is special to this probability structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对**有限、非负、次随机**的 $Q$，还有一条额外等价性：$I_t-Q$ 可逆当且仅当前述收敛条件成立。可直接证明：令 $\mathbf1$ 为 $t$ 个一组成的列，$h_n=Q^n\mathbf1$。由于 $Q\mathbf1\le\mathbf1$ 且 $Q$ 非负，$h_n$ 逐坐标递减到某个 $h\ge0$。有限矩阵乘法可以通过极限，所以 $Qh=h$。若 $I_t-Q$ 可逆，就只有 $h=0$；而 $Q^n$ 的每个非负条目不超过对应行和，因此 $Q^n\to0$。反向已经证明。这一步单调论证依赖专门的概率结构。

> [!warning] An arbitrary inverse is not a convergent visit sum · 任意矩阵的逆不保证访问级数收敛
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the one-by-one matrix $Q=(2)$, $I-Q=(-1)$ is invertible, but $1+2+4+\cdots$ diverges. The inverse is $-1$, which cannot be an expected visit count. This $Q$ is nonnegative but is not substochastic. Conversely, a closed two-state swap has $Q=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, $\rho(Q)=1$ and no escape. Its powers do not tend to zero and $I-Q$ is singular. Calling that block “transient” cannot repair the model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对一阶矩阵 $Q=(2)$，$I-Q=(-1)$ 可逆，但 $1+2+4+\cdots$ 发散；逆为 $-1$，也不可能是期望访问次数。这个 $Q$ 虽非负，却不是次随机矩阵。另一方面，闭合的两状态交换块 $Q=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ 有 $\rho(Q)=1$，没有出口，矩阵幂不趋零，$I-Q$ 也奇异。把它叫作“暂态块”不能修复模型。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are fixed finite-matrix results. In a countably infinite chain, almost-sure absorption alone does not guarantee a finite expected total number of steps, as shown by [[必达不推有限期望|almost-sure hitting with infinite mean]]. Infinite-dimensional operators and entrywise visit sums need their own conditions; the finite inverse test should not be carried over without them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些结论针对尺寸固定的有限矩阵。对可数无限链，几乎必然吸收本身不能保证有限的期望总步数，见[[必达不推有限期望]]。无限维算子与逐项访问和各有自己的条件，不能直接照搬有限矩阵的可逆性检查。
