# 重农主义净产品是农业补足再生产预付后的剩余
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Physiocratic net product is the agricultural surplus after restoring the advances needed for reproduction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 在 Quesnay 的农业王国模型中，生产阶级先用土地耕作再生一年的财富，再从这项年度再生产中收回两部分 **reprises（耕作者收回额）**：下一轮耕作所需的年度预付，以及对农具、牲畜等原始预付的年度补偿。Quesnay 把后一项称为原始预付的“利息”，这里主要承担修理、更新与风险准备，而不是现代借款利息。超过全部收回额的余额才是 **produit net（净产品，亦译“纯产品”）**，并作为土地收入交给所有者阶级。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In Quesnay's model of an agricultural kingdom, the productive class regenerates the year's wealth through cultivation and first recovers two components of the cultivators' **reprises**: the annual advances needed for the next cycle and an annual allowance on original advances such as implements and livestock. Quesnay called the latter “interest,” but here it chiefly covers repair, replacement, and risk rather than interest on a modern loan. Only the balance beyond the full reprises is the **produit net** (net product), paid to the proprietor class as land revenue.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“净”不是简单把几种实物投入从收成重量中相减。Quesnay 的表用货币价值表示出售、支出与收入，并预设一种足以维持大规模耕作、补足年度预付和更新原始预付的价格与生产秩序。净产品因此同时是生产剩余和分配对象：它解释为什么土地所有者能取得年度收入，也为其税收主张提供税基。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Net” does not mean merely subtracting a few physical inputs from the weight of the harvest. Quesnay's table represents sales, expenditure, and revenue in money values and assumes a price and production order that sustains large-scale cultivation, restores annual advances, and renews original advances. Net product is therefore both a production surplus and an object of distribution: it explains the proprietor's annual revenue and supplies the proposed tax base.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个概念不是现代企业利润、GDP 或国民账户净增加值的旧名称。它属于一套把农业视为唯一能在补足预付后留下净剩余的特定理论。要判断制造业或服务业在现代经济中是否“创造价值”，必须换用现代生产边界和增加值口径，不能直接从这一定义推出答案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The concept is not an old name for modern business profit, GDP, or net value added in national accounts. It belongs to a theory in which agriculture alone leaves a net surplus after restoring advances. Whether manufacturing or services “create value” in a modern economy requires modern production boundaries and value-added accounting; it cannot be inferred from this definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一项农业销售收入为什么不能全部算作重农主义的净产品？
>
> **答案：** 必须先补足下一轮耕作所需的年度预付，并留下修理、更新原始预付所需的年度补偿；只有超过全部耕作者收回额的部分才进入所有者阶级的净收入。

## 来源与核验

- [Quesnay, “Analyse de la formule arithmétique du Tableau économique”](https://historyofeconomicthought.mcmaster.ca/quesnay/tabeco.htm)：核对耕作者收回额由年度预付与原始预付的年度“利息”构成，扣除全部收回额后的剩余才称为 *produit net*；原文也说明这项“利息”持续用于修理预付。
- [Quesnay, *Tableau œconomique*（BnF Gallica, 1759 影印）](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k106140h.pdf)：交叉核对原表及其以年度农业再生产为前提的历史形式。
- [BnF Gallica, “Théorie économique — Physiocratie”](https://gallica.bnf.fr/selections/fr/html/droit-economie/theorie-economique-physiocratie)：核对 Quesnay 各版 *Tableau* 与重农学派把农业置于财富生产中心的文献位置。
