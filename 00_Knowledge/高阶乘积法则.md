# Higher-order product rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*高阶乘积法则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ordinary [[乘积法则|product rule]] differentiates one factor at a time. If we differentiate a product again, each of those terms splits again, and some terms repeat. The **higher-order product rule**, also called Leibniz's rule, combines those repetitions so that we can take many derivatives without expanding a growing tree of terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通[[乘积法则]]每一项只对一个因子求导。如果再求一次导数，每一项又会分成两项，其中一些项会重复。**高阶乘积法则**也叫 Leibniz 法则，它把这些重复项合并起来，让我们不用一路展开越来越多的项，也能计算高阶导数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## See where the first coefficient 2 comes from
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先看系数 2 是怎样出现的*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $u''$ means differentiate $u$ twice, as explained in [[高阶导数|higher derivatives]]. For twice-differentiable $u$ and $v$, use the product rule on each term of $(uv)'=u'v+uv'$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记号 $u''$ 表示对 $u$ 求两次导数，见[[高阶导数]]。若 $u$、$v$ 二次可导，对 $(uv)'=u'v+uv'$ 的每一项使用乘积法则，得到

$$\begin{aligned}
(uv)''&=(u'v)'+(uv')'\\
&=u''v+u'v'+u'v'+uv''\\
&=u''v+2u'v'+uv''.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The middle term occurs twice: first differentiate $u$ then $v$, or first differentiate $v$ then $u$. A third differentiation gives $(uv)^{(3)}=u^{(3)}v+3u''v'+3u'v''+uv^{(3)}$. The coefficients count repeated routes to the same final term.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中间项出现两次：可以先求导 $u$ 再求导 $v$，也可以先求导 $v$ 再求导 $u$。第三次求导得到 $(uv)^{(3)}=u^{(3)}v+3u''v'+3u'v''+uv^{(3)}$。这些系数数的是：有多少条求导路径会抵达同一个最终项。

> [!abstract] Leibniz's rule · Leibniz 高阶乘积法则
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $n$ be a nonnegative integer. Suppose $u$ and $v$ have derivatives through order $n$ on an open interval. At each point in that interval,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $n$ 是非负整数，$u$、$v$ 在一个开区间上具有直到第 $n$ 阶的各阶导数。则在该区间内每一点，
>
> $$(uv)^{(n)}(x)
> =\sum_{k=0}^n\binom nk u^{(k)}(x)v^{(n-k)}(x).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The index $k$ counts how many of the $n$ differentiations land on $u$; the other $n-k$ land on $v$. Use $u^{(0)}=u$ and $v^{(0)}=v$: zero derivatives means leave the function alone. In particular, these terms are not the number $1$. For $n=0$ the formula just says $uv=uv$; for $n=1$ it is the ordinary product rule.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 下标 $k$ 表示 $n$ 次求导中有几次落在 $u$ 上，其余 $n-k$ 次落在 $v$ 上。约定 $u^{(0)}=u$、$v^{(0)}=v$：零次求导就是保留原函数，不是把它换成数字 $1$。$n=0$ 时公式只是 $uv=uv$；$n=1$ 时就是普通乘积法则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[组合数|binomial coefficient]] $\binom nk=n!/[k!(n-k)!]$ counts which $k$ of the $n$ differentiation steps are assigned to $u$. Once those steps are chosen, every other step goes to $v$. Each choice produces $u^{(k)}v^{(n-k)}$, which explains its coefficient. This counting resembles the [[二项式定理|binomial theorem]], but the superscripts here are derivative orders, not powers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[组合数]] $\binom nk=n!/[k!(n-k)!]$ 计算从 $n$ 个求导步骤中选哪 $k$ 步分给 $u$。选好这些步骤后，其余步骤全部分给 $v$；每种选择都产生 $u^{(k)}v^{(n-k)}$，因此它前面有这样的系数。这个计数结构与[[二项式定理]]相似，但这里的上标表示导数阶数，不是乘方。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the pattern continues at every order
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么这个规律能一直延续*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first-order case is already proved. Suppose the order-$n$ formula holds and the functions also have order-$n+1$ derivatives. Differentiating each term gives two contributions. For an interior index $1\le k\le n$, the term $u^{(k)}v^{(n+1-k)}$ receives coefficient $\binom n{k-1}$ from differentiating the left factor of the previous $k-1$ term, and coefficient $\binom nk$ from differentiating the right factor of the previous $k$ term. By [[Pascal恒等式|Pascal's identity]],
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一阶情形已经证明。假设 $n$ 阶公式成立，并且两个函数还有第 $n+1$ 阶导数。每一项再求导，会产生两部分。对中间下标 $1\le k\le n$，项 $u^{(k)}v^{(n+1-k)}$ 的系数有两个来源：上一阶 $k-1$ 项的左因子求导，贡献 $\binom n{k-1}$；上一阶 $k$ 项的右因子求导，贡献 $\binom nk$。由 [[Pascal恒等式]]，

$$\binom n{k-1}+\binom nk=\binom{n+1}k.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At either endpoint, all derivatives land on one factor and the coefficient remains $1$. Thus one more differentiation gives exactly the next-order formula. This induction also shows the required assumption: the derivatives used at each step must exist. Continuity of the highest derivative is not an extra requirement for this identity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两端的项把全部求导次数分给同一个因子，系数仍为 $1$。因此再求导一次，恰好得到下一阶公式。这个归纳过程也说明了所需条件：每一步用到的导数必须存在。这条恒等式本身不额外要求最高阶导数连续。

> [!example] A linear factor leaves only two terms · 一次因子只留下两项
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $u(x)=x$ and $v(x)=e^x$. The derivatives of $u$ are $u'=1$ and $u^{(k)}=0$ for $k\ge2$. By [[指数函数求导|the natural exponential derivative]], every derivative of $v$ is again $e^x$. In the fifth derivative, therefore, only $k=0$ and $k=1$ survive:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $u(x)=x$、$v(x)=e^x$。$u$ 的导数为 $u'=1$，而 $k\ge2$ 时 $u^{(k)}=0$。由[[指数函数求导|自然指数函数求导]]，$v$ 的每一阶导数都是 $e^x$。所以求第五阶导数时，只有 $k=0$、$k=1$ 两项留下：
>
> $$D^5(xe^x)=\binom50xe^x+\binom51\cdot1\cdot e^x
> =xe^x+5e^x=(x+5)e^x.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a quick check, the first two derivatives are $(x+1)e^x$ and $(x+2)e^x$; each further differentiation increases that constant by one. The rule saves work because it recognizes vanished terms early, not because it makes nonexistent higher derivatives available.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 可以用前两阶检查：它们分别是 $(x+1)e^x$、$(x+2)e^x$，此后每求导一次，括号内的常数就增加一。公式省去的是重复展开：它能提前识别哪些项为零，却不能让本来不存在的高阶导数变得存在。
