# Canonical form of an absorbing chain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*吸收链标准形*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An absorbing-chain model distinguishes states where the process is still moving from states where it has finished. Its **canonical form** puts those two groups next to each other in the transition table. This exposes which probabilities describe continued movement and which describe finishing; it does not change the process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

吸收链模型把“过程仍在进行”的状态与“过程已经结束”的状态分开。**标准形**把两组状态分别排在转移表的一起，让我们看清哪些概率表示继续活动，哪些表示结束；它并没有改变过程本身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a fixed, [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|time-homogeneous]] chain on a finite nonempty state set. An [[吸收状态|absorbing state]] has probability one of staying at itself. In a [[吸收链|finite absorbing chain]], there is at least one such state and every other state can reach one along a positive-probability route. The [[吸收链必然吸收|eventual-absorption theorem]] makes the other states [[常返与暂态|transient]]: they cannot retain the process forever. We use the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]], so row $i$ lists the next-state probabilities from $i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑非空有限状态集上固定的[[DTMC时间齐次转移核|时间齐次]]链。[[吸收状态]]以概率一留在自身。[[吸收链|有限吸收链]]至少有一个吸收状态，其他每个状态都能沿正概率路线到达某个吸收状态。由[[吸收链必然吸收]]，其他状态都是[[常返与暂态|暂态]]，不能永久留住过程。这里采用[[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]]：第 $i$ 行列出从 $i$ 出发的下一步概率。

> [!example] One active state, two possible endings · 一个活动状态，两种结局
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Order the states as $(s,a,b)$, with $a,b$ absorbing, and take
>
> $$P=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&1/4&1/4\\0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> From $s$, the process remains active with probability $1/2$, ends at $a$ with probability $1/4$, and ends at $b$ with probability $1/4$. Here $Q=(1/2)$ records the active-to-active transition, while $R=(1/4\;1/4)$ records the two ways of being absorbed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 按 $(s,a,b)$ 排列，其中 $a,b$ 吸收。从 $s$ 出发，以 $1/2$ 的概率继续活动，以各 $1/4$ 的概率结束在 $a$ 或 $b$。这里 $Q=(1/2)$ 记录活动状态之间的转移，$R=(1/4\;1/4)$ 记录两种吸收出口。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the four blocks
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*读懂四个分块*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $t$ be the number of transient states and $r\ge1$ the number of absorbing states. The symbol $t$ here counts states, not time. Put the $t$ transient states first and the $r$ absorbing states last. A [[分块矩阵乘法|block matrix]] groups entries into rectangular pieces; the transition matrix becomes

$$P=\begin{pmatrix}Q&R\\0&I_r\end{pmatrix},\qquad
Q\in\mathbb R^{t\times t},\quad R\in\mathbb R^{t\times r}.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $t$ 为暂态状态数，$r\ge1$ 为吸收状态数；这里的 $t$ 数状态，不表示时间。把 $t$ 个暂态排在前面，$r$ 个吸收态排在后面。[[分块矩阵乘法|分块矩阵]]是把元素划成若干矩形区域，于是得到上式；$Q$ 为 $t\times t$，$R$ 为 $t\times r$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Entry $Q_{ij}$ is the one-step probability from transient state $i$ to transient state $j$. Entry $R_{ia}$ is the one-step probability from transient state $i$ to absorbing state $a$. The lower-left zero block is $r\times t$: absorption prevents a return to the transient group. The lower-right [[单位矩阵|identity]] $I_r$ has ones on the diagonal and zeros elsewhere: each absorbing state stays at itself, not at some other absorbing state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$Q_{ij}$ 是从暂态 $i$ 到暂态 $j$ 的一步概率；$R_{ia}$ 是从暂态 $i$ 到吸收态 $a$ 的一步概率。左下角的零块大小为 $r\times t$，表示吸收之后不能返回暂态区。右下角[[单位矩阵]] $I_r$ 的主对角为一、其余为零，表示每个吸收态留在自身，并不是在多个吸收态之间移动。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each full row of $P$ sums to one. A row of $Q$ may sum to less than one because the omitted mass appears in $R$. A nonnegative matrix with row sums at most one is called **substochastic**. Do not renormalize $Q$ to make every row sum to one. Dividing a row with positive sum by that sum would condition on no absorption at the next step, changing the calculation. A zero row has no such conditioning event.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$P$ 的完整每行总和为一；$Q$ 的行和可以小于一，因为缺少的质量进入了 $R$。非负且每行总和不超过一的矩阵称为**次随机矩阵**。不要把 $Q$ 的每行重新归一化成一。若行和为正，除以这个行和会变成以“下一步没有吸收”为条件的概率，改变原来的计算；行和为零时，这个条件事件的概率为零，不能这样条件化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The blocks keep track of probability mass
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*分块怎样追踪概率质量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At time $n$, split the probability row as $(u_n,v_n)$: $u_n$ contains the transient-state probabilities and $v_n$ the absorbing-state probabilities. Either part can have total mass below one, while together they sum to one. Block multiplication gives

$$u_{n+1}=u_nQ,\qquad v_{n+1}=u_nR+v_n.$$

The first equation keeps only mass that remains transient. The second adds newly absorbed mass to mass already absorbed. Consequently the upper-left block of $P^n$ is $Q^n$. Starting at transient $i$, $(Q^n)_{ij}$ is the probability of occupying transient $j$ at time $n$; its row sum is the probability of still being unabsorbed at that time. Absorption cannot be followed by a return, so no path that has already ended contributes. This is an [[n步转移非首达|occupancy probability, not a first-visit probability]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在时刻 $n$，把概率行分成 $(u_n,v_n)$：$u_n$ 装暂态概率，$v_n$ 装吸收态概率。任一部分的总质量都可能小于一，两部分合起来才为一。分块相乘得到上面两式：第一式只保留仍在暂态区的质量；第二式把新吸收的质量加入已经吸收的质量。因此 $P^n$ 的左上角为 $Q^n$。从暂态 $i$ 出发，$(Q^n)_{ij}$ 是时刻 $n$ 位于暂态 $j$ 的概率；行和就是该时刻仍未吸收的概率。吸收之后不能返回，所以已经结束的路径不会贡献这一概率。这是[[n步转移非首达|占据概率，不是首次访问概率]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reordering changes the table, not the chain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*重排改变表格，不改变链*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the first example, order the same states as $(a,s,b)$ instead. Reorder both rows and columns using that same list. The matrix becomes

$$\widetilde P=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\1/4&1/2&1/4\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.$$

It describes exactly the same transitions. The clean upper-left $Q$ block disappeared only because the transient states were no longer placed first. If all absorbing states are placed first, the block form is $\begin{pmatrix}I_r&0\\R&Q\end{pmatrix}$. If all states are absorbing, there is no transient block to analyze and $P=I_r$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在第一个例子中，改按 $(a,s,b)$ 排列，同步重排行与列，便得到 $\widetilde P$。它描述完全相同的转移；左上角的整齐 $Q$ 块消失，只因为没有把暂态排在最前。若把全部吸收态排在前面，形式就是 $\begin{pmatrix}I_r&0\\R&Q\end{pmatrix}$。若所有状态都吸收，则没有暂态块需要研究，$P=I_r$。

> [!warning] A block label does not prove transience · 标成一个块不等于已经暂态
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A chain can have an absorbing state $a$ and a separate closed cycle $b\leftrightarrow c$. Merely placing $b,c$ before $a$ also produces a block table, but its upper-left block keeps probability forever. It is not the transient block of a finite absorbing chain. Check reachability or the [[基本矩阵存在条件|fundamental-matrix condition]] before treating all nonabsorbing states as transient; a general [[有限链类分解|finite-chain decomposition]] may contain larger closed classes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一条链可以有吸收态 $a$，同时还有独立的闭循环 $b\leftrightarrow c$。仅把 $b,c$ 放在 $a$ 前面，也能写出分块表，但左上角会永久保存概率质量，并不是有限吸收链的暂态块。把全部非吸收态叫作暂态之前，应核对可达性或[[基本矩阵存在条件]]；一般[[有限链类分解]]可以包含更大的闭类。
