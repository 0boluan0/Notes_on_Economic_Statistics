# 债券凸性是价格对指定收益率的二阶导数除以当前全价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bond convexity is the second derivative of price with respect to the specified yield divided by current full price*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券凸性 $\mathcal C_y=P''(y)/P(y)$ 衡量价格—收益率关系的二阶敏感度：收益率变化时，价格下降或上升的斜率本身也会改变。本页定义不含 $1/2$；$1/2$ 属于二阶价格近似的系数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bond convexity $\mathcal C_y=P''(y)/P(y)$ measures second-order price-yield sensitivity: the slope itself changes as yield changes. This definition excludes the factor $1/2$, which belongs in the second-order price approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $CF_k\ge0$、至少一笔为正，支付在 $t_k>0$ 日历年后。以小数年名义收益率 $y$、每年复利 $m$ 次定价，$a=1+y/m>0$。全价及二阶导数为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix nonnegative cash flows, at least one positive, at $t_k>0$ calendar years. Use decimal nominal annual yield $y$, compounded $m$ times a year, with $a=1+y/m>0$. Full price and its second derivative are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P=\sum_k CF_k a^{-mt_k},\qquad P''(y)=\sum_k t_k(t_k+1/m)CF_k a^{-mt_k-2}.$$
$$\boxed{\mathcal C_y=\frac{\sum_k w_k t_k(t_k+1/m)}{(1+y/m)^2}},\qquad w_k=\frac{CF_ka^{-mt_k}}P.$$

年付息、年复利时成为 $\sum_k w_kt_k(t_k+1)/(1+y)^2$，不能漏掉末尾分母。若用连续复利收益率 $c$，则 $P_{cc}/P=\sum_k w_kt_k^2$；离散与连续的数值不能跨坐标混用。对这组固定非负未来支付，$P''>0$，所以凸性为正；这不把现金流会改变的含期权债券一并纳入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Annual compounding gives $\sum_k w_kt_k(t_k+1)/(1+y)^2$; the denominator must not be omitted. With continuously compounded yield $c$, convexity becomes $P_{cc}/P=\sum_k w_kt_k^2$. Values in different yield coordinates are not interchangeable. Fixed nonnegative future payments give $P''>0$; this conclusion does not automatically cover option-dependent payments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如现金流 $8,8,108$ 在第 $1,2,3$ 年支付，$y=5\%$、$m=1$，得到 $P=108.169744$、$\mathcal C_y=9.880406$。它不是几何学曲线曲率，也不直接等于久期的导数；由 $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=-P'/P$ 可核对 $D_{\mathrm{mod}}'=D_{\mathrm{mod}}^2-\mathcal C_y$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Payments $8,8,108$ in years $1,2,3$, with $y=5\%$ and $m=1$, give $P=108.169744$ and $\mathcal C_y=9.880406$. This is not geometric curvature or simply the derivative of duration: differentiating $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=-P'/P$ gives $D_{\mathrm{mod}}'=D_{\mathrm{mod}}^2-\mathcal C_y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- MIT Slides 40 and 43 support $P''/P$, the discrete-compounding denominator and annualized semiannual convexity. The general formula follows from two derivatives; continuous compounding and the numerical example were checked separately.
- CFA Institute supports second-order risk and positive convexity for option-free fixed-rate bonds. Nonnegative future payments are explicit here.
