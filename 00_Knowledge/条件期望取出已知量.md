# 对条件信息可测的因子可以在可积条件下提出条件期望

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A measurable known factor can be taken outside a conditional expectation under integrability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $E|Y|<\infty$，$h$ 可测且 $h(X)$ 有限，并且 $E|h(X)Y|<\infty$，则
$$E[h(X)Y\mid X]=h(X)E[Y\mid X]\quad\text{almost surely}.$$
给定 $X$ 后 $h(X)$ 已知，因此只对剩余的 $Y$ 不确定性平均。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An integrable product with a finite measurable function of the conditioning variable permits taking that function outside the conditional expectation. Once X is given, the factor is known.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
离散时逐组固定 $x$，$\sum_yh(x)y p(y\mid x)=h(x)\sum_y y p(y\mid x)$。例如 $E[XY\mid X]=X E[Y\mid X]$；再用全期望才得到 $E[XY]=E[X E[Y\mid X]]$，一般仍不能拆成 $E[X]E[Y]$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a discrete group the result is just factoring a constant out of a weighted sum. Total expectation then yields the unconditional mean of X times its conditional mean prediction, not automatically a product of two unconditional means.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
