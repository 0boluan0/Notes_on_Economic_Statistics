# Taking out a known factor
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*条件期望取出已知量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A factor can be taken outside a [[条件期望|conditional expectation]] when the conditioning information already determines that factor. It may still be random before the information is observed. Within a fixed information state, however, it is a constant multiplier, so it need not be averaged again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果条件信息已经确定了某个乘数，就可以把它提出[[条件期望]]。在看到信息之前，这个乘数仍可能是随机的；但在固定信息状态内，它是一个确定的倍数，不需要再次平均。

> [!example] A known rate times an uncertain wait · 已知费率乘不确定等待时间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A service charges one pound per minute on route $X=0$ and two pounds per minute on route $X=1$. Write its rate as $W=1+X$. Suppose waits $Y$ on route $0$ are $0$ or $4$ minutes, equally likely, and on route $1$ are $4$ or $8$, equally likely. The group means are $2$ and $6$. Once the route is known, the expected charge is the known rate times that route’s mean wait: $2$ pounds on route $0$, and $12$ on route $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 某项服务在线路 $X=0$ 上每分钟收一英镑，在线路 $X=1$ 上每分钟收两英镑，费率写成 $W=1+X$。假设线路 $0$ 的等待时间 $Y$ 等概率为 $0$ 或 $4$ 分钟，线路 $1$ 等概率为 $4$ 或 $8$ 分钟，组内均值分别为 $2$、$6$。知道线路后，预期费用就是已知费率乘该线路的平均等待：线路 $0$ 为两英镑，线路 $1$ 为十二英镑。

$$
E[(1+X)Y\mid X]=(1+X)E[Y\mid X].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For route $1$, this is just the ordinary calculation $\tfrac12(2\cdot4)+\tfrac12(2\cdot8)=2[\tfrac12\cdot4+\tfrac12\cdot8]=12$. The rate and waiting time need not be independent. The reason for factoring is that the rate is fixed **within the group being averaged**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在线路 $1$ 中，这就是普通计算 $\tfrac12(2\cdot4)+\tfrac12(2\cdot8)=2[\tfrac12\cdot4+\tfrac12\cdot8]=12$。费率与等待时间不需要独立；能够提出费率，是因为它在**正在平均的这一组内部**固定。

## What “known” and “integrable” require
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*已知与可积分别要求什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\mathcal G$ be the [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]] recording the information. Assume $Y$ is integrable, meaning $E[|Y|]<\infty$. Let $W$ be finite almost surely and $\mathcal G$-measurable: its value can be determined from that information. If the product is also integrable, $E[|WY|]<\infty$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 [[σ-代数]] $\mathcal G$ 记录已知信息。假设 $Y$ 可积，即 $E[|Y|]<\infty$。令 $W$ 几乎处处有限且对 $\mathcal G$ 可测，也就是其值可以由这些信息确定。如果乘积也可积，$E[|WY|]<\infty$，则

$$
E[WY\mid\mathcal G]=W E[Y\mid\mathcal G]\quad\text{almost surely}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product condition ensures that the conditional average on the left is a finite integrable random variable; the theorem also guarantees this for the right. A bounded $W$ automatically satisfies it when $Y$ is integrable. Merely assuming that $W$ and $Y$ separately have finite means does not guarantee an integrable product. Check the [[矩存在性的使用边界|moment conditions]] when factors can be large.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘积可积保证左侧条件平均是有限的可积随机变量，定理同时保证右侧也如此。若 $W$ 有界、$Y$ 可积，乘积条件自动满足；仅仅分别具有有限均值，并不能保证乘积可积。乘数可能很大时，要检查[[矩存在性的使用边界|矩条件]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the information is a real-valued variable $X$, any finite measurable function $h(X)$ is known after observing $X$. The rule becomes $E[h(X)Y\mid X]=h(X)E[Y\mid X]$. For discrete conditioning with $P(X=x)>0$, its elementary reason is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当信息来自实值变量 $X$ 时，看到 $X$ 就知道了任何有限可测函数 $h(X)$ 的值。规则于是写成 $E[h(X)Y\mid X]=h(X)E[Y\mid X]$。在 $P(X=x)>0$ 的离散分组中，原因就是

$$
\sum_y h(x)y\,P(Y=y\mid X=x)=h(x)\sum_y y\,P(Y=y\mid X=x).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For general information, the same rule follows by first checking known event indicators, then simple known factors, and extending with integrability. This general argument is why the rule also works for continuous conditioning, where dividing by the probability of a single observed point may be invalid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一般信息，可以先检查已知事件的指示变量，再检查简单的已知因子，最后在可积条件下推广。因此这条规则也适用于连续条件化，不必除以某个观测单点的概率。

> [!warning] Outside a conditional average is not outside every average · 提出条件平均不等于提出所有平均
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Taking [[全期望公式|total expectation]] leaves the known factor inside the outer average: $E[XY]=E[X E[Y\mid X]]$, whenever $Y$ and $XY$ are integrable. It does not usually give $E[X]E[Y]$. In the two-route model, let route probabilities be $1/4$ and $3/4$. Then $E[XY]=(3/4)\cdot6=4.5$, whereas $E[X]E[Y]=(3/4)\cdot5=3.75$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 再用[[全期望公式]]时，已知因子仍留在外层平均里面：在 $Y$、$XY$ 可积时，$E[XY]=E[X E[Y\mid X]]$，一般不能改成 $E[X]E[Y]$。在上述两线路模型中，让线路概率为 $1/4$、$3/4$，则 $E[XY]=(3/4)\cdot6=4.5$，但 $E[X]E[Y]=(3/4)\cdot5=3.75$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Also check which information is given. An unknown factor cannot simply be removed: with no information and $U=\pm1$ equally likely, $E[U^2]=1$, while $U E[U]=0$. With $U$ itself known, $E[U^2\mid U]=U E[U\mid U]=U^2$ is valid. The scope of the conditioning changes the operation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要检查究竟给了什么信息。未知乘数不能直接提出：若没有额外信息，且 $U$ 等概率取 $\pm1$，则 $E[U^2]=1$，而 $U E[U]=0$。若已知的就是 $U$，则 $E[U^2\mid U]=U E[U\mid U]=U^2$ 成立。条件信息的范围改变了可以进行的运算。
