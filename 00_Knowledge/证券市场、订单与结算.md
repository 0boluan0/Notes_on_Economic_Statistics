# 证券市场、订单与结算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Securities Markets, Orders, and Settlement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释证券从发行融资到二级交易、撮合、清算和最终交割的完整路径，以及交易成本和流动性从哪里来。
> **具体锚点：** 市价买单通常立即吃掉卖盘，但实际成交均价可能高于屏幕上的最佳卖价；限价买单控制最高价格，却可能一直排队不成交。
> **核心难点：** 报价、成交、清算和结算不是同一时点；订单选择在成交确定性、价格确定性、信息暴露和队列优先之间权衡。
> **为什么重要：** 即使估值判断正确，点差、冲击、借券、保证金和结算失败也能使可实现收益与模型收益不同。
> **继续：** 若不清楚交易对象的权利，先读 [[证券工具与现金流权利]]；把规则写成可回测策略见 [[证券技术分析]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It explains the full path from issuance and financing through secondary trading, matching, clearing, and final settlement, including the origins of transaction cost and liquidity.
> **Concrete anchor:** A market buy order normally consumes available sell orders immediately, but its average execution price can exceed the best displayed ask. A limit buy controls the maximum price but may remain queued and never execute.
> **Central difficulty:** Quote, trade, clearing, and settlement are not the same event. Order choice trades off execution certainty, price certainty, information exposure, and queue priority.
> **Why it matters:** Even when valuation is correct, spreads, market impact, securities borrowing, margin, and settlement failure can make realizable return differ from model return.
> **Continue:** If the rights in the traded object are unclear, first read [[证券工具与现金流权利|Securities and Cash-Flow Rights]]. To turn rules into a backtestable strategy, see [[证券技术分析|Technical Analysis of Securities]].

## 一级与二级市场
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Primary and secondary markets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

IPO、增发和债券发行在一级市场把资金给发行人；交易所/场外二级市场让投资者转让。活跃二级市场通过流动性和价格发现降低融资成本，但二级交易本身通常不直接给企业新增资金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
IPOs, seasoned equity offerings, and bond issues transfer funds to issuers in primary markets; exchange and over-the-counter secondary markets let investors transfer existing claims. An active secondary market can lower financing cost through liquidity and price discovery, but secondary trading itself normally provides no new cash directly to the company.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一级和二级并非彼此无关：投资者预期未来容易出售，才更愿意在发行时出资并接受较低流动性补偿；二级价格又为后续发行、员工激励和并购提供估值信号。但把每天股价上涨称为公司“收到资金”仍是资金流错误。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Primary and secondary markets are connected. Investors are more willing to fund an issue and demand less liquidity compensation when they expect easy resale; secondary prices also inform later issuance, employee incentives, and acquisitions. But saying that a company receives cash whenever its stock price rises remains a mistake about fund flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 交易机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Trading mechanisms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

订单驱动市场由限价单簿撮合，报价驱动市场由做市商报双边价。市价单换确定成交、承担价格不确定；限价单控制价格、承担不成交和逆向选择。bid–ask spread 是显性流动性成本之一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An order-driven market matches a limit-order book, while a quote-driven market uses dealers posting two-sided prices. A market order gains execution certainty but bears price uncertainty; a limit order controls price but bears non-execution and adverse-selection risk. The bid–ask spread is one visible component of liquidity cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 限价单簿的最小算例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A minimal limit-order-book example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

卖盘依次为 100 元挂 50 股、100.20 元挂 100 股。买入 120 股的市价单会先成交 50 股于 100，再成交 70 股于 100.20，成交均价为 $(50\times100+70\times100.2)/120\approx100.117$。屏幕最佳卖价 100 不是整笔订单都能成交的保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the sell book offers 50 shares at 100 and 100 shares at 100.20. A market order to buy 120 shares executes 50 at 100 and 70 at 100.20, for an average price of $(50\times100+70\times100.2)/120\approx100.117$. The displayed best ask of 100 does not guarantee that the entire order can trade there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

点差补偿做市、库存风险、处理成本和被信息更充分者交易的风险。订单越大、市场越薄或信息事件越强，成交穿越更多档位并引起后续价格调整的冲击成本越重要。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The spread compensates market making, inventory risk, processing cost, and the risk of trading against better-informed counterparties. As orders grow, markets thin, or information events intensify, the impact cost from walking through more price levels and inducing subsequent price adjustment becomes more important.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 市场参与者与监管
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Market participants and regulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

发行人、个人/机构投资者、中介、交易所、清算和监管者承担不同功能。披露、适当性、市场操纵规则和清算降低信息与对手风险，也有合规成本和监管套利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Issuers, retail and institutional investors, intermediaries, exchanges, clearing entities, and regulators perform different functions. Disclosure, suitability, market-manipulation rules, and clearing reduce information and counterparty risk, while also creating compliance costs and opportunities for regulatory arbitrage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

监管目标之间也可能权衡：更严格披露提高可比性却增加发行成本；更高保证金降低违约概率却可能在压力期放大现金需求；价格限制暂缓失序交易却可能推迟价格发现。评估规则要同时写目标、机制和副作用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regulatory objectives can trade off. Stricter disclosure improves comparability but raises issuance cost; higher margin lowers default probability but can amplify cash demand under stress; price limits may pause disorderly trading but delay price discovery. A rule should be evaluated by stating its objective, mechanism, and side effects together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 卖空、保证金与结算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Short selling, margin, and settlement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

卖空需借券并承担召回、借券费和潜在无限损失；保证金放大收益与亏损。交易日价格和结算日现金/证券交割不同，失败和抵押品管理是基础设施风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Short selling requires borrowing securities and exposes the seller to recall, borrowing fees, and potentially unlimited loss; margin amplifies gains and losses. Trade-date price and settlement-date delivery of cash and securities are different events, and failures plus collateral management create infrastructure risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

清算先确认双方净义务，结算才完成证券与资金的最终交割。中央对手方可以把双边敞口替换为对清算机构的敞口并提高净额效率，但风险不会消失，而是集中到保证金、违约基金、成员管理和处置安排。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Clearing determines and often nets the parties' obligations; settlement completes final delivery of securities and funds. A central counterparty can replace bilateral exposures with exposures to the clearing house and improve netting efficiency, but risk does not disappear—it becomes concentrated in margin, default funds, member management, and resolution arrangements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 完整交易路径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The complete trade path*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一笔交易可按“开户与权限 → 下单 → 风控检查 → 撮合或做市成交 → 成交确认 → 清算净额 → 保证金与交收准备 → 证券和资金结算 → 持仓与公司行动记录”检查。任何回测若只生成理论成交价而忽略其中与策略相关的环节，就不是可实施回测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A trade can be checked through the sequence: account and permissions → order submission → risk checks → auction or dealer execution → trade confirmation → clearing and netting → margin and settlement preparation → securities and cash settlement → position and corporate-action records. A backtest that generates only a theoretical execution price while omitting strategy-relevant stages is not an implementable backtest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把最佳报价乘以全部数量，忽略市场深度和冲击。
- 把限价单未成交的样本删掉，只评价成交样本，会产生选择偏差。
- 卖空回测没有借券可得性、借券费、召回和保证金，策略收益不可实现。
- 把成交视为立即、无条件完成交割，遗漏结算失败和对手方暴露。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Multiplying the best quote by the entire quantity ignores market depth and impact.
- Dropping unfilled limit orders and evaluating only filled observations creates selection bias.
- A short-selling backtest without borrow availability, borrow fees, recall, and margin produces unrealizable returns.
- Treating execution as immediate, unconditional final delivery omits settlement failure and counterparty exposure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一级市场与二级市场的资金流有何不同？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How do fund flows differ between primary and secondary markets?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 一级发行把新资金给发行人；二级交易主要在投资者间转移既有证券。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A primary issue delivers new funds to the issuer; secondary trading mainly transfers existing securities among investors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 市价单和限价单分别承担什么风险？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What risks do market and limit orders bear respectively?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 市价单优先成交但有滑点/价格风险；限价单控制价格但可能不成交并暴露于逆向选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A market order prioritizes execution but bears slippage and price risk; a limit order controls price but may not execute and is exposed to adverse selection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么二级市场不直接融资，却仍会影响一级融资成本？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why can a secondary market affect primary financing cost even though it does not directly fund the issuer?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 二级市场提供退出、流动性和价格发现；若投资者预计证券难以出售或价格不透明，会在发行时要求更高回报或更低发行价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Secondary markets provide exit, liquidity, and price discovery. If investors expect difficult resale or opaque pricing, they demand a higher return or a lower issue price in the primary market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一个回测使用收盘信号并假设同一收盘价满额成交，错在哪里？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is wrong with a backtest that uses a closing-price signal and assumes full execution at that same close?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 信号可能只有收盘后才完整可知，造成未来信息泄露；同时还忽略收盘深度、队列、冲击和费用，必须改用可获得信号后的可执行价格与数量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The complete signal may become known only after the close, causing look-ahead bias. The assumption also ignores closing depth, queue position, impact, and fees; use an executable price and quantity after the signal becomes available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
