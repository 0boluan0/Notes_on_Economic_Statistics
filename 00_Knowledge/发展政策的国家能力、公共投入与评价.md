# 发展政策的国家能力、公共投入与评价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*State Capacity, Public Inputs, and Evaluation in Development Policy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把“某项政策在理论上有用”接到“政府能否收集信息、筹资、执行、维护并从结果中学习”。
> **具体锚点：** 建成一所学校不等于学习改善；还需要教师出勤、教材、营养、学生可达、监测与预算维护这一组互补条件。
> **核心难点：** 资金、人员、组织、信息、权力与信任中任一环可成为紧约束；小规模有效的项目也可在扩大时改变价格、执行质量和政治行为。
> **为什么重要：** 这决定政策应选理论上最精细的工具，还是选在当前能力下可执行、可监测且能逐步学习的工具。
> **继续：** 将这套“诊断—机制—实施—评价”链条用于产业支持时，进入 [[贸易、产业政策与全球价值链]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It connects “this policy can work in theory” to whether government can gather information, finance, implement, maintain, and learn from it.
> **Concrete anchor:** Building a school does not guarantee learning. Teacher attendance, materials, nutrition, student access, monitoring, and maintenance budgets are complementary conditions.
> **Central difficulty:** Finance, staff, organization, information, authority, or trust can each become the binding constraint. A successful small pilot can also change prices, implementation quality, and politics when scaled.
> **Why it matters:** It determines whether policy should choose the theoretically most refined instrument or one that is executable, monitorable, and capable of supporting gradual learning under current capacity.
> **Continue:** To apply this diagnosis–mechanism–implementation–evaluation chain to industrial support, see [[贸易、产业政策与全球价值链|Trade, Industrial Policy, and Global Value Chains]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 公共投入是互补系统
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Public inputs form a complementary system*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

人力资本与道路、电力、数字网络互补。学校入学率不等于学习，基础设施建成不等于维护和利用。供给扩张应同时测质量、可达和实际使用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Human capital complements roads, electricity, and digital networks. School enrollment is not learning, and completed infrastructure is not maintenance or use. Supply expansion should measure quality, access, and actual utilization together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如一条乡村道路的价值取决于农民是否有可销售剩余、市场信息、运输服务、安全与维护。若其中一项为紧约束，道路建设的交通量和收入效果可远低于工程成本—收益预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The value of a rural road depends on whether farmers have a marketable surplus, market information, transport services, security, and maintenance. If one is binding, traffic and income effects can fall far below the engineering cost–benefit forecast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 国家能力与工具复杂度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*State capacity and instrument complexity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

税收、信息、专业官僚、地方执行和可信承诺决定政策能否落地。复杂最优工具在低能力环境可能不如简单可执行工具；能力本身也可通过政策学习积累。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taxation, information, professional bureaucracy, local implementation, and credible commitment determine whether policy can be delivered. A complex optimal instrument may perform worse than a simple executable one in a low-capacity setting; capacity can itself accumulate through policy learning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将能力只当作一个国家排名会失去操作性。应对具体政策问：是否能识别对象？资金是否及时到达？前线人员是否有技能与裁量权？中央和地方能否协调？失败信息能否传回且修改规则？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Treating capacity as one country ranking removes operational meaning. For a specific policy, ask whether recipients can be identified, funds arrive on time, frontline staff have skills and discretion, central and local actors coordinate, and failure information travels back to revise rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 评价回答不同层次的问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Evaluation answers questions at different levels*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用随机实验、准实验、结构模型和过程证据回答不同问题；短期局部效应要与一般均衡、规模化和政治反应区分。外部效度需说明目标地区和制度差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Randomized experiments, quasi-experiments, structural models, and process evidence answer different questions. Short-run local effects must be distinguished from general equilibrium, scale-up, and political response. External validity requires explicit comparison of target regions and institutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评价链应分为：投入是否到位；活动是否按计划执行；中间机制是否改变；最终结果是否改善；成本与副作用是什么。只看最终结果无法区分“理论失败”和“实施失败”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The evaluation chain should separate whether inputs arrived, activities were delivered, intermediate mechanisms changed, final outcomes improved, and costs or side effects arose. Final outcomes alone cannot distinguish theory failure from implementation failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从试点到规模化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From pilot to scale*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个小规模项目评估有效，为何全国推广仍可能失败？规模化会改变价格、资源、执行质量和政治行为，局部均衡处理效应不包含这些反馈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why can a successful small pilot fail at national scale? Scaling changes prices, resources, implementation quality, and political behavior; a local-equilibrium treatment effect does not include these feedbacks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如试点为 20 所学校招聘额外辅导员，可从当地最好的候选人中选择并获得项目团队密集监督。全国扩大后，辅导员工资上升、候选质量下降、监督密度减少，还可挤出现有教师。扩展评估必须重新估计这些均衡变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pilot may hire tutors for 20 schools from the strongest local candidates under intensive supervision. At national scale, tutor wages rise, candidate quality falls, supervision thins, and existing teachers may be displaced. Scale-up evaluation must re-estimate these equilibrium changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 用建设支出、设施数或入学率代替质量、使用和最终结果。
- 把政策失败一律归于“能力低”，却不说明是哪个人员、信息、权限或协调环节失败。
- 将试点平均处理效应直接乘上全国人口，不计算价格、人员和政治反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Substituting construction spending, facility counts, or enrollment for quality, use, and final outcomes.
- Calling every policy failure “low capacity” without identifying the failed staff, information, authority, or coordination link.
- Multiplying a pilot treatment effect by the national population without accounting for price, personnel, and political responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一个小规模项目评估有效，为何全国推广仍可能失败？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a successful small pilot still fail at national scale?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 规模化会改变价格、资源、执行质量和政治行为，局部均衡处理效应不包含这些反馈。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Scale changes prices, resources, implementation quality, and political behavior; a local-equilibrium treatment effect omits these feedbacks.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话区分理论失败与实施失败。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words the difference between theory failure and implementation failure.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 理论失败是按计划交付后，中间机制或结果仍不变；实施失败是对象、资金、人员或活动未按设计到位，因而尚未真正测试机制。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Theory failure means delivery occurred as designed but the mechanism or outcome did not change. Implementation failure means targeting, funds, staff, or activities were not delivered as designed, so the mechanism was not truly tested.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么复杂的理论最优工具在低能力环境可能更差？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a theoretically optimal complex instrument perform worse in a low-capacity setting?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它需要更精准的信息、对象识别、持续监测和裁量能力；实施误差、俘获和行政成本可超过理论优势。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It requires more precise information, targeting, monitoring, and discretion. Implementation error, capture, and administrative cost can exceed its theoretical advantage.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
