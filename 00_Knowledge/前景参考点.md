# 前景参考点是把结果划分为收益与损失的比较基准
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A prospect-theory reference point is the benchmark separating gains from losses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前景参考点是[[前景理论]]中评价变化的零点。若用同一货币尺度表示结果 $z$ 与参考点 $r$，则得失为 $x=z-r$：$x>0$ 是收益，$x<0$ 是损失。参考点不是结果本身，也不必等于当前财富或历史买入价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A reference point is the zero of change in [[前景理论|prospect theory]]. For a monetary outcome $z$ and reference point $r$ on the same scale, $x=z-r$ is a gain when positive and a loss when negative. The reference point is not the outcome itself and need not equal current wealth or the historical purchase price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个结果 $z=105$，相对 $r=100$ 编码为收益 $5$，相对 $r=110$ 则编码为损失 $5$。这只改变评价基准，没有改变 $105$ 的实际结果；只有再指定价值、权重及备选方案，才能推出选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same outcome $z=105$ is a gain of $5$ relative to $100$ but a loss of $5$ relative to $110$. The actual outcome is unchanged. Predicting a choice additionally requires values, weights, and the available alternatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现状、预期或愿望都可能参与形成参考点，具体应用须说明采用哪一种及如何识别。研究[[处置效应]]时用买入价分类，是一个可检验的测量选择，不是所有人的心理基准都固定在买入价。参考点划分得失；[[锚定效应]]描述先前数值对估计的牵引，两者不能仅因都涉及一个数值而视为同一概念。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The status quo, expectations, or aspirations may inform a reference point; an application must specify and identify its choice. Using purchase price to classify gains and losses in a [[处置效应|disposition-effect]] study is a testable measurement choice, not a universal psychological rule. A reference point separates gains from losses; [[锚定效应|anchoring]] concerns the influence of an initial number on an estimate. The concepts are distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Kahneman & Tversky (1979), pp. 274–277、286，PDF 12–15、24](https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Behavioral_Decision_Theory/Kahneman_Tversky_1979_Prospect_theory.pdf#page=12)：支持编码基准、当前资产并非唯一参考及预期、愿望造成的参考点移动。$105$ 的对比为自拟算例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paper supports reference-dependent coding, alternatives to current assets, and shifts associated with expectations or aspirations. The $105$ comparison is independently constructed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Odean (1998), pp. 1777、1782，PDF 3、8](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/Papers%20current%20versions/AreInvestorsReluctant.pdf#page=3)：支持买入价作为参考点代理，以及处置效应检验同时依赖参考点设定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Odean supports purchase price as a proxy and the dependence of disposition-effect tests on the reference-point specification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
