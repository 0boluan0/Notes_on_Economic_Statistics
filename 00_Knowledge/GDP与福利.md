# GDP衡量生产流量但不能独自代表生活福利

[[国内生产总值|GDP]]把一定时期内、核算生产边界内的增加值汇总起来；生活福利还涉及消费的分布、健康、闲暇、环境等结果。GDP 提供了资源规模的信息，却没有把这些结果逐项测量出来。它也不是期末财富存量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[国内生产总值|GDP]] aggregates value added within the accounting production boundary during a period. Welfare also concerns distribution, health, leisure, and environmental outcomes. GDP is informative about resources, but does not measure each of these outcomes; nor is it an end-of-period wealth stock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不能把它的边界概括成“只记录市场交易”。政府提供的非市场服务在核算范围内，缺少市场售价时有相应估值方法；自有住房服务也计入估算租金。另一方面，家庭成员为自家提供的无酬做饭、清洁等服务通常不计入核心 GDP。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Its boundary is not simply “market transactions only.” Government nonmarket services are included using appropriate valuation methods, and owner-occupied housing includes imputed rental services. Unpaid cooking and cleaning supplied within one's own household, however, are generally outside core GDP.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，相同的家务服务从自家无酬完成变为购买有酬服务后，会进入 GDP 的生产边界；但仅凭这种记账变化，不能断言家庭生活改善了相同金额。是否真的更好，还取决于腾出的时间如何使用、服务质量以及费用负担。这个例子比较的是核算边界，不是对整个经济体 GDP 变化作无条件预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the same household service changes from unpaid own-household work to a purchased service, it enters the GDP production boundary. That accounting change does not establish an equal monetary improvement in well-being: time use, service quality, and affordability also matter. The example isolates an accounting boundary, not an unconditional prediction for economy-wide GDP.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

讨论[[经济发展]]时，可以保留 GDP 作为生产指标，再用[[贫困人口比例]]看线下人口、用[[基尼系数]]看相对分配、用[[人类发展指数]]补充健康和教育。后几项也各有边界，不能把某一个“更全面”的数字当作全部生活的替身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When assessing [[经济发展|economic development]], retain GDP as a production measure, use the [[贫困人口比例|poverty headcount ratio]] for people below a threshold, the [[基尼系数|Gini coefficient]] for relative distribution, and the [[人类发展指数|HDI]] to add health and education. Each has limits; no seemingly broader number represents all of life.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [国家统计局，国内生产总值核算说明，§1.1–1.2](https://www.stats.gov.cn/zwfwck/sjfb/202501/t20250118_1958363.html)：GDP、常住范围及包含非市场服务的生产边界。
- [BEA，自有住房服务](https://www.bea.gov/help/faq/488)及[无酬家庭劳动](https://www.bea.gov/help/faq/1297)：估算租金与家庭无酬服务的核算区别。
- [Fixler & Craig (2025), Welfare Indicators and GDP，Introduction](https://www.bea.gov/sites/default/files/papers/BEA-WP2025-6.pdf#page=2)：GDP 能提供福利相关信息，却非福利的完整测度；这是作者工作论文的论述，不作为 BEA 对所有福利问题的官方结论。家务例子为边界的独立应用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NBS and BEA document the production boundary and housing/household-service treatment. Fixler and Craig discuss GDP's informational value and limits for welfare; their working paper represents its authors' analysis. The household example applies the accounting distinction.
