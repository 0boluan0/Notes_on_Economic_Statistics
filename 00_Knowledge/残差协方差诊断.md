# Diagnosing residual scale and dependence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*残差尺度与依赖的诊断*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After fitting a regression, a [[回归残差|residual]] is the observed value minus its fitted value. Looking at where these differences are large, systematically positive, or close to one another in time helps decide what to investigate next. **Residual covariance diagnosis uses these patterns to question the mean, noise scale and dependence structure.** The fitted residuals have already been changed by estimation, so they cannot be treated as a fresh sample of the unobserved errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归拟合后，[[回归残差|残差]]就是观察值减去拟合值。看这些差距在哪些位置更大、持续偏正，或在时间上相近，有助于决定下一步检查什么。**残差协方差诊断利用这些图案，检查均值、噪声尺度与依赖结构。** 估计过程已经改变了残差，因此不能把它们当成一份新抽取的、未观测误差的样本。

> [!example] A curve before any random noise · 没有随机噪声，也能留下图案
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take five points with $x=(-2,-1,0,1,2)$ and $y=x^2=(4,1,0,1,4)$. An OLS line with an intercept has slope $0$ and fitted level $2$. Its residuals are $(2,-1,-2,-1,2)$: the ends are positive and the middle negative. The data have no random noise in this example. The pattern is created by fitting a line to a curve, so it cannot establish heteroskedastic noise.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取五个点：$x=(-2,-1,0,1,2)$、$y=x^2=(4,1,0,1,4)$。含截距的 OLS 直线斜率为 $0$，拟合水平为 $2$；残差是 $(2,-1,-2,-1,2)$，两端为正、中间为负。本例没有任何随机噪声，图案来自用直线拟合曲线，因此不能据此证明噪声异方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same ambiguity exists with noise. Assume $Y$ has a [[矩存在性的使用边界|finite second moment]], so the variances below exist. Let $m(x)=E[Y\mid X=x]$ be the [[条件期望|conditional mean]], and let $g(x)$ be a fixed, finite candidate prediction. Adding and subtracting $m(X)$ separates the discrepancy into a mean-zero fluctuation and a mean error; their conditional cross-term is zero:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有噪声时也有同样歧义。假设 $Y$ 的[[矩存在性的使用边界|二阶矩有限]]，让下述方差有定义。令 $m(x)=E[Y\mid X=x]$ 为[[条件期望|条件均值]]，$g(x)$ 是固定且取有限值的候选预测。加上再减去 $m(X)$，差距便分成条件均值为零的波动和均值偏差；两者的条件交叉项为零：

$$E[(Y-g(X))^2\mid X]=\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)+(m(X)-g(X))^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The squared discrepancy contains both [[条件方差|conditional variance]] and squared mean error. Here $g$ is fixed; a function estimated from the same observations additionally brings fitting effects. This explains why predictable squared residuals need not be pure evidence about [[异方差|heteroskedasticity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平方差同时包含[[条件方差]]与均值偏差的平方。这里 $g$ 固定；若它由同一批观测估计，还会叠加拟合影响。这解释了为什么平方残差可预测，并不一定只是[[异方差]]的证据。

## What fitting does to the residuals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拟合怎样改变残差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed design matrix $X$ with $n$ observations and $k<n$ [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|linearly independent columns]], define the fitted-value matrix $H=X(X^TX)^{-1}X^T$ and the residual matrix $M=I-H$, where $I$ is the identity matrix and $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Applying $H$ to $Y$ produces the OLS fitted values; applying $M$ leaves the residuals. If the true mean vector is $m=E[Y\mid X]$ and the error covariance is $\Omega=\operatorname{Cov}(Y\mid X)$, with finite second moments, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定设计矩阵 $X$ 有 $n$ 个观测、$k<n$ 个[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|线性无关]]的列。定义拟合值矩阵 $H=X(X^TX)^{-1}X^T$、残差矩阵 $M=I-H$，其中 $I$ 是单位矩阵，上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置]]。$H$ 作用于 $Y$ 得到 OLS 拟合值，$M$ 则留下残差。若真实均值向量为 $m=E[Y\mid X]$，误差协方差为 $\Omega=\operatorname{Cov}(Y\mid X)$，且二阶矩有限，则

$$\hat u=MY,\qquad E[\hat u\mid X]=Mm,\qquad\operatorname{Cov}(\hat u\mid X)=M\Omega M^T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the mean is correctly linear, $m=X\beta$, then $Mm=0$ and $\hat u=Mu$ for the true error $u=Y-X\beta$. If also $\Omega=\sigma^2I$, symmetry and $M^2=M$ (removing the fitted part twice makes no further change) give $\operatorname{Cov}(\hat u\mid X)=\sigma^2M$. Write $h_{ij}$ for entry $(i,j)$ of $H$. Thus $\operatorname{Var}(\hat u_i\mid X)=\sigma^2(1-h_{ii})$, while for $i\ne j$ the covariance is $-\sigma^2h_{ij}$. The [[Hat杠杆值|leverage]] $h_{ii}$ measures how strongly observation $i$ enters its own fitted value. Raw residuals can have unequal variances and cross-correlations even with spherical errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若均值确实线性，$m=X\beta$，则 $Mm=0$，且对真实误差 $u=Y-X\beta$ 有 $\hat u=Mu$。若还满足 $\Omega=\sigma^2I$，利用对称性和 $M^2=M$（再去除一次拟合部分，不会继续改变残差），得到 $\operatorname{Cov}(\hat u\mid X)=\sigma^2M$。记 $h_{ij}$ 为 $H$ 的第 $(i,j)$ 个元素。于是 $\operatorname{Var}(\hat u_i\mid X)=\sigma^2(1-h_{ii})$，而 $i\ne j$ 时协方差为 $-\sigma^2h_{ij}$。[[Hat杠杆值|杠杆值]] $h_{ii}$ 衡量第 $i$ 个观测对自身拟合值的影响程度。即使误差球形，原始残差也可能方差不等且相互相关。

> [!note] A small design exposes the constraint · 一个小设计看清拟合约束
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fit an intercept and slope at $x=(0,1,2)$ with independent mean-zero errors of variance one and a correct linear mean. The leverages are $(5/6,1/3,5/6)$, so the three residual variances are $(1/6,2/3,1/6)$. The middle residual is more variable even though the three original errors have identical variances. This is an algebra illustration with only one residual degree of freedom, not a reliable diagnostic dataset.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $x=(0,1,2)$ 处拟合截距与斜率，设线性均值正确，误差独立、均值零、方差一。杠杆值为 $(5/6,1/3,5/6)$，所以三个残差方差为 $(1/6,2/3,1/6)$。原始误差方差完全相同，中间残差却更易波动。这只是残差自由度仅为一的代数示例，不是一份能可靠诊断的数据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[学生化残差|Studentized residuals]] account for fitted scale and leverage under their own assumptions. They are useful for comparing observations, but rescaling cannot make a misspecified mean correct or make dependent errors independent. A small residual at a high-leverage observation is not proof that the observation is harmless.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[学生化残差]]在各自假设下考虑拟合尺度与杠杆，便于比较观测；但重新缩放不能让错误均值变正确，也不能让依赖误差独立。高杠杆点的残差小，并不证明它没有影响。

## Choose plots that answer different questions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*让不同图形回答不同问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the recorded time/order and sampling or assignment structure, then inspect the fitted model. Sorting unrelated rows by their residual size creates an artificial sequence and cannot reveal temporal dependence. If predictor values move systematically with time, a trend in a run-order plot may be hard to separate from a wrong predictor-response function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看真实的时间／观测顺序与抽样或处理分配结构，再检查拟合模型。把互不相关的行按残差大小排序，会造出人为序列，不能揭示时间依赖。若解释变量本来就随时间系统变化，顺序图中的趋势可能难以与错误的变量响应函数区分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| View | Useful question | A competing explanation to check |
|---|---|---|
| Residual versus fitted value and important predictors | Does the centre curve or the spread vary? | Mean misspecification, leverage, and sparse support can distort scale patterns. |
| Residual versus actual time/order | Are there runs, seasonality or breaks? | Omitted mean dynamics can imitate dependence. |
| Residual lag plots and [[自协方差与ACF\|ACF]] | Which time separations show linear association? | Fitting constraints, mean structure, and nonlinear dependence complicate interpretation. |
| [[异常杠杆影响区分\|Leverage and influence]] together with group labels | Do a few observations or shared sampling units drive the pattern? | A label alone does not establish a correct clustering design. |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 图形 | 有用的问题 | 同时检查的其他解释 |
|---|---|---|
| 残差对拟合值与重要解释变量 | 中心是否弯曲？分散程度是否变化？ | 均值设错、杠杆与稀疏支持会扭曲尺度图案。 |
| 残差对实际时间／顺序 | 是否有同号段、季节性或断点？ | 遗漏均值动态也会模仿依赖。 |
| 残差滞后图与 [[自协方差与ACF\|ACF]] | 哪些时距出现线性关联？ | 拟合约束、均值结构与非线性依赖影响解释。 |
| [[异常杠杆影响区分\|杠杆与影响]]结合分组标签 | 是否由少数观测或共享抽样单位主导？ | 有分组标签本身不能建立正确聚类设计。 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A partial autocorrelation at lag $h$ removes the linear contribution of intervening lags. [[ACF-PACF阶数识别|ACF/PACF patterns]] can suggest dynamic candidates, but isolated spikes outside approximate bands do not prove a unique AR order; inspecting many lags also changes the chance of seeing a striking spike. Absence of linear correlation is not proof of independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第 $h$ 阶偏自相关去掉中间滞后的线性贡献。[[ACF-PACF阶数识别|ACF/PACF 图案]]可以提示动态候选，但某些尖峰超出近似带，不能证明唯一 AR 阶数；查看很多滞后，也会改变看到醒目尖峰的概率。没有线性相关，不证明独立。

## Turn a clue into a defensible check
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把线索变成有依据的检查*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A formal test needs a stated null, variance covariates or lags, significance level and valid reference distribution. [[Breusch-Pagan检验|Breusch–Pagan]] and [[White异方差检验|White]] focus on specified variance restrictions; [[Durbin-Watson检验|Durbin–Watson]] and [[Breusch-Godfrey检验|Breusch–Godfrey]] address serial-correlation questions under different conditions. A rejection is evidence against the tested restrictions together with their maintained assumptions, not proof of a unique generating mechanism. A nonrejection does not verify their truth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正式检验需要写明零假设、方差变量或滞后、显著性水平及有效参考分布。[[Breusch-Pagan检验|Breusch–Pagan]] 与 [[White异方差检验|White]] 关注指定方差限制；[[Durbin-Watson检验|Durbin–Watson]] 与 [[Breusch-Godfrey检验|Breusch–Godfrey]] 在不同条件下检查序列相关。拒绝是反对所检验限制及其维持假设的证据，不是唯一生成机制的证明；未拒绝也不验证其为真。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If plots help select a test or model, record that exploration; nominal calibration for a prespecified rule cannot simply be claimed after searching many alternatives. Return to [[设定问题分层|the estimand and specification layer]] before changing anything. A conditional-mean claim may need a better mean; a deliberately descriptive projection needs inference for that projection. [[误差协方差决策|HC, HAC, clustering and re-estimation]] then have distinct roles. Preserve unusual observations while investigating them; visual inconvenience alone is not a reason to delete data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若看图帮助选择了检验或模型，应记录这段探索；搜索多个方案后，不能直接声称仍有预先指定规则的名义校准。改动前回到[[设定问题分层|估计对象与设定层次]]：条件均值主张可能需要更好的均值模型；明确的描述性投影则需针对该投影论证推断。[[误差协方差决策|HC、HAC、聚类与重估]]随后各有职责。调查异常观测时保留数据，仅仅看起来碍眼不是删除理由。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Hansen, *Econometrics*, §3.4 PDF p.39 and §4.6 PDF p.59](https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~xshi28/Econometrics_Hansen.pdf#page=59): residual versus error, the residual covariance transformation and leverage-dependent variance. The three-row matrix is calculated directly.
- NIST/SEMATECH, [functional form](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmd/section4/pmd441.htm), [nonconstant variation](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmd/section4/pmd442.htm), [run order](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmd/section4/pmd443.htm), and [lag plots](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmd/section4/pmd444.htm): different diagnostic questions and competing explanations. The five-point example and squared-discrepancy identity are direct calculations.
- [Penn State STAT 501, Topic 2, §§T.2.2–T.2.3](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat501/Topic2TSandAutocorr.html) and [Lesson 12, §12.9](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat501/Lesson12.html): autoregressive-error diagnostics, scale and omitted-trend pitfalls. Calibration remains conditional on the particular model and test; graphical heuristics do not certify independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Hansen 残差部分核对误差与残差区别、协方差变换及杠杆相关的残差方差；三行设计矩阵直接计算。
- NIST 四页分别核对均值形式、非恒定变化、观测顺序与滞后图的诊断职责和竞争解释；五点例子及平方差分解直接计算。
- Penn State 时间序列 T.2.2–T.2.3 与 Lesson12 的 12.9 节核对自回归误差诊断、尺度及遗漏趋势陷阱。校准始终取决于具体模型与检验，图形经验规则不认证独立。
