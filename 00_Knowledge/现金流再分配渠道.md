# 现金流再分配渠道是利率变化在不同支出倾向的收付方之间转移收入而改变总支出的机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cash-flow redistribution channel changes aggregate spending by shifting interest income between payers and recipients with different spending responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金流再分配渠道指利率变化改变借款人的付款和储蓄者的收入，而收付双方未必同样调整消费。即使在完整、封闭的账户中新增利息收付相抵，总消费的一阶反应仍可能不为零；关键是哪些合同实际重置，以及每类主体收到或失去一单位现金后改变多少支出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rate changes alter borrowers' payments and savers' income, without requiring equal consumption responses. Even if additional interest payments cancel in a complete closed account, the first-round aggregate spending response need not be zero. Identify repricing contracts and each group's spending response to cash gained or lost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一笔两人之间的浮息债务隔离机制：本金固定 100，本年合同利率上升 1 个百分点，借款人多付 1、债权人多收 1。假定这一范围内没有银行、政府或海外遗漏项，没有违约和费用；借款人每失去 1 的当期可用现金少消费 0.8，债权人每多得 1 多消费 0.2。仅这项现金流的一阶总消费变化是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Isolate one floating-rate debt between two people. On unchanged principal of 100, a one-percentage-point contract-rate rise transfers 1 from borrower to lender. Assume no omitted bank, government or foreign positions, default or fees. The borrower cuts current consumption by 0.8 per unit lost, while the lender spends 0.2 per unit gained. The first-round change from this cash flow alone is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta C=-0.8\times1+0.2\times1=-0.6,
\qquad \Delta\text{净利息收入总额}=-1+1=0.
$$

这里的 0.8 与 0.2 是局部行为假设，不是通用参数；交换二者，总消费方向也会改变。0.6 是本例首轮消费减少，不是支付利息本身造成的资源毁损，更不是已经求出的 GDP 或福利损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficients are assumed local responses, not universal parameters; reversing them reverses the spending effect. The 0.6 is a first-round consumption reduction, not resources destroyed by the interest transfer or an identified GDP or welfare loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现实中还需纳入存贷款传导不对称、中介利润、跨境持有、税收和劳动收入反馈。[[合同重定价与政策传导]]决定直接现金流何时变；[[利率与储蓄反应]]讨论跨期价格变化的替代与收入效应。本卡只隔离收付方异质反应，不把各渠道的独立算例简单加成总政策乘数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Actual aggregation includes asymmetric deposit-loan pass-through, intermediaries' profits, cross-border holdings, taxes and labour-income feedback. [[合同重定价与政策传导|Contract repricing]] dates direct cash-flow changes; [[利率与储蓄反应|intertemporal saving analysis]] handles substitution and income effects. Separate channel examples cannot simply be added into an aggregate policy multiplier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
