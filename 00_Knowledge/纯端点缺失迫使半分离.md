# 在有限标准信号博弈的 weak-PBE 口径下，若不存在纯合并与纯分离 PBE，则至少存在一个半分离 PBE
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a finite standard signaling game under weak PBE, if no pure pooling or pure separating PBE exists, then at least one semi-separating PBE exists.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 存在性、纯化与三分法共同给出结论
> 有限标准信号博弈至少存在一份 weak PBE。若这份 PBE 是 pooling 或 separating，两个纯化引理会分别推出一个 pure pooling 或 pure separating PBE；当这两个纯端点都已被排除，现存 PBE 在三分法中只能是 semi-separating。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Existence, purification, and the trichotomy jointly yield the result.*
>
> A finite standard signaling game has at least one weak PBE. If such an equilibrium is pooling or separating, the two purification lemmas produce a pure pooling or pure separating PBE, respectively. Once both pure endpoints have been ruled out, an existing PBE can only be semi-separating under the trichotomy.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三步论证
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three-step argument*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 有限、完美记忆的扩展式博弈存在[[序贯均衡]]；序贯均衡满足 weak-PBE 条件，所以 weak-PBE 集合非空。
2. 任取一份 weak PBE。若它是 mixed pooling，[[混合合并可纯化]]会产生 pure pooling PBE；若它是 mixed separating，[[混合分离可纯化]]会产生 pure separating PBE。两种结果都与题设矛盾。
3. [[混合信号均衡分类]]把发送者的类型条件信号分布互斥且穷尽地分为 pooling、separating 与 semi-separating。因此至少有一份现存 PBE 属于 semi-separating。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
&nbsp;

**1.** A finite extensive-form game with perfect recall has a [[序贯均衡|sequential equilibrium]]. Every sequential equilibrium satisfies the weak-PBE conditions, so the weak-PBE set is nonempty.<br>
**2.** Take any weak PBE. If it is mixed pooling, [[混合合并可纯化|pooling purification]] produces a pure pooling PBE; if it is mixed separating, [[混合分离可纯化|separating purification]] produces a pure separating PBE. Either conclusion contradicts the premise.<br>
**3.** [[混合信号均衡分类|The mixed-strategy trichotomy]] partitions type-contingent signal distributions exhaustively into pooling, separating, and semi-separating. At least one existing PBE must therefore be semi-separating.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 结论的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary of the result*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个结论保证“至少存在一份”，不提供具体混合概率。实际求解仍须联立 Bayes 更新、双方最佳反应、[[混合支持内无差异|支持内无差异]]与所有偏离约束。结论还依赖有限标准信号博弈、weak-PBE 口径以及本库的混合三分法；连续信号、不同均衡概念或不同分类约定都需要另行证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result guarantees existence of at least one equilibrium but does not supply its mixing probabilities. An actual solution must still jointly satisfy Bayesian updating, both players' best responses, [[混合支持内无差异|support indifference]], and every deviation constraint. The argument also relies on a finite standard signaling game, the weak-PBE convention, and this vault's mixed-strategy trichotomy. Continuous signals, other equilibrium concepts, or other classification conventions require separate proofs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 已经在整个有限标准信号博弈中排除了 pure pooling 与 pure separating PBE。要推出半分离 PBE 存在，还缺哪三块逻辑？
>
> **答案：** weak-PBE 非空；任何 mixed pooling 或 mixed separating PBE 都能分别纯化到已被排除的纯端点；pooling、separating、semi-separating 构成穷尽三分法。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Self-check*
>
> Pure pooling and pure separating PBE have been ruled out throughout a finite standard signaling game. Which three ingredients are still needed to infer existence of a semi-separating PBE?
>
> **Answer:** The weak-PBE set is nonempty; every mixed pooling or mixed separating PBE can be purified into the corresponding excluded pure endpoint; and pooling, separating, and semi-separating form an exhaustive trichotomy.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
