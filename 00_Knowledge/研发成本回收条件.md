# 对竞争投入的边际支付耗尽收入时，付费研发需要额外的成本回收安排
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When marginal-product payments to rival inputs exhaust revenue, privately financed R&D requires an additional cost-recovery arrangement.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若生产者先付钱创造一种有用的非竞争设计，再按边际产品支付全部竞争投入，仅有复制生产所得的收入可能不足以同时回收设计成本。这里必须同时说明三个条件：**竞争投入可以复制、它们按边际产品取得报酬、研发成本需要由私人收入回收**。[[技术知识的非竞争性]]本身并不直接决定现实中的融资或市场制度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a producer pays to create a useful nonrival design and then pays every rival input its marginal product, revenue from replicated production may leave nothing to recover the design cost. The argument requires replicable rival inputs, marginal-product remuneration and private recovery of R&D costs. [[技术知识的非竞争性|Nonrivalry of designs]] alone does not determine the financing or market institutions used in practice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设设计知识为 $A$，全部竞争投入为向量 $X$，产出为 $F(A,X)$。既有设计不用重新发明，复制工厂、工人等竞争投入，使下面的复制关系成立；若设计本身也有生产价值，同时增加它会进一步增加产出：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ denote design knowledge and $X$ the complete vector of rival inputs. An existing design need not be invented again when factories and workers are replicated. This gives the replication relation below; increasing productive design knowledge as well raises output further.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
F(A,\lambda X)=\lambda F(A,X),\qquad
F(\lambda A,\lambda X)>\lambda F(A,X),\quad \lambda>1.
$$

在可微条件下，第一条关系给出 $X\cdot F_X=F$。竞争投入按边际产品支付已耗尽产出；若还要支付正的设计边际价值，就有
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under differentiability, the first relation gives $X\cdot F_X=F$: payments to rival inputs already exhaust output. Paying the design its positive marginal value in addition produces the following inequality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
X\cdot F_X+A F_A>F.
$$

因此，这种结构不能同时靠“所有投入都按边际产品拿报酬”和“私人企业自负研发成本”来维持。Romer 选择专利下的 [[Romer中间品加价|中间品加价]]，使后续经营收益具有正现值，再由 [[Romer专利价值]] 把这份回报与前期设计支出连接起来。其他融资安排要另外写明，不能从上述不等式推出现实知识必然由垄断提供。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This structure cannot simultaneously sustain marginal-product payments to every input and private recovery of the design cost. Romer uses [[Romer中间品加价|patented intermediate-input markups]] to generate operating returns, while [[Romer专利价值|patent value]] connects their present value to the initial expenditure. Other financing arrangements require their own specification; the inequality does not establish that knowledge must always be supplied by monopolists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
