# 潜在产出是给定资源技术和制度下经济可持续实现的产出水平
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Potential output is the sustainable level of production given resources, technology, and institutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

潜在产出 $Y^*$ 是资源处于正常、可持续利用状态时的产出基准；它不是机器和工人不休息所能达到的物理极限。实际产出可短暂超过潜能，例如加班和设备超负荷运转，但不能据此把该状态视为可长期维持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Potential output $Y^*$ is a benchmark for normal, sustainable resource use, not a physical maximum with no rest or maintenance. Actual output may temporarily exceed it through overtime or unusually intensive capacity use.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“充分就业”也不表示[[失业率]]为零：岗位匹配和结构调整仍可存在。技术、资本、劳动力和制度变化会改变潜能；它不是永远不动的一根线。$Y^*$ 不能直接观察，需要模型或统计估计，因此会随信息和方法修订。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full employment does not require a zero [[失业率|unemployment rate]]: matching and structural adjustment remain. Technology, capital, labour, and institutions can shift potential. It is estimated rather than directly observed and can be revised with new information or methods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如实际产出从 96 增至 98，而估计潜能从 100 增至 102，经济在增长但仍低于潜能。实际与潜能的相对距离由[[产出缺口]]衡量，在 AD-AS 模型中由[[长期总供给]]标示潜能位置。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If actual output rises from 96 to 98 while estimated potential rises from 100 to 102, output is growing but remains below potential. The [[产出缺口|output gap]] measures the relative distance; [[长期总供给|long-run aggregate supply]] marks potential in an AD-AS model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
