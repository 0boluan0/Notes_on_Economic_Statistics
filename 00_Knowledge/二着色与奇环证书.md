# Two-coloring and odd-cycle certificates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*二着色与奇环证书*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful recognition algorithm should explain either answer. To show that a graph is [[二分图|bipartite]], return labels $0,1$ for every vertex and check that each edge joins different labels. To show that it is not, return a concrete odd cycle whose edges can be checked in the input. The [[二着色与奇环|odd-cycle theorem]] guarantees that one of these two kinds of evidence exists for every finite simple undirected graph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有用的识别算法应能解释两种答案：若图是[[二分图]]，就给出全部顶点的 $0,1$ 标签，让人检查每条边两端异色；若不是，就给出一条具体奇环，让人逐边核对它确实存在于输入中。[[二着色与奇环|奇环判据]]保证，每张有限简单无向图都具有这两类证据中的一种。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Discover, color and record the parent together
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*发现、着色与记录父顶点同时完成*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Represent $G=(V,E)$ by [[邻接表与邻接矩阵|adjacency lists]], which list each vertex's neighbors. Keep a discovered mark, color, parent and tree depth for each vertex. A [[搜索标记与父森林|parent]] records the edge that first discovered the vertex. Depth counts parent edges from the current root, not necessarily shortest distance. Initially all vertices are undiscovered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用[[邻接表与邻接矩阵|邻接表]]存储 $G=(V,E)$，列出每个顶点的邻点。为每个顶点保存发现标记、颜色、父顶点和树深度。[[搜索标记与父森林|父顶点]]记录第一次发现该点所用的边；深度计算从当前根沿父边走过的步数，不一定是最短距离。起初所有顶点均未发现。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose an undiscovered root $r$, mark it, set its color to $0$ and depth to $0$, and give it no parent. In BFS, place it in a first-in, first-out queue. Repeatedly remove a queued vertex $u$ and inspect every neighbor $v$. If $v$ is undiscovered, immediately mark it, set
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选一个未发现根 $r$，标记它，令颜色为 $0$、深度为 $0$，并设为无父顶点。采用 BFS 时，把它放入先进先出队列。反复取出排队顶点 $u$，检查它的每个邻点 $v$。若 $v$ 未发现，立即标记它并设置

$$c(v)=1-c(u),\qquad \operatorname{parent}(v)=u,\qquad d(v)=d(u)+1,$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
then enqueue it. Marking occurs before insertion, so another edge cannot enqueue the same vertex again or overwrite its parent. If $v$ is already discovered, keep its records and test whether $c(v)=c(u)$. Equal colors signal failure; retain the edge $uv$ and reconstruct the cycle below. Otherwise continue. When the queue empties, restart at another undiscovered root, retaining all old marks. Only after every component has finished without a conflict may the algorithm return the complete coloring.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

然后把它入队。标记先于入队，因此另一条边不会再次把同一顶点入队或改写父记录。若 $v$ 已发现，就保留其记录并检查 $c(v)=c(u)$ 是否成立；若同色，保留冲突边 $uv$，按下面的方法还原奇环；否则继续。队列为空后，从另一个未发现点重启，保留旧标记。只有每个分量都完成且没有冲突，才能输出完整着色。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Standard recursive [[DFS 可达性与非最短路径|DFS]] gives the same alternative: after assigning a new child's mark, color and parent, explore that child immediately, then resume the parent's scan. All edges to discovered vertices still need the color test. A successful traversal colors by tree-depth parity in either method. Only [[BFS 分层与无权最短路|BFS]] also makes depth equal unweighted shortest distance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准递归 [[DFS 可达性与非最短路径|DFS]] 也得到同样的两种结果：新子节点的标记、颜色、父记录确定后，立即探索子节点，再恢复父节点的扫描。指向已发现点的边仍要检查颜色。两种方法在成功时都按树深度奇偶性着色，但只有 [[BFS 分层与无权最短路|BFS]] 还保证深度等于无权最短距离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Turn a conflict into a simple odd cycle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把冲突还原为简单奇环*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the first equal-color edge $uv$, the endpoints lie in the same parent tree. A completed earlier component has no edge to a later one, and the current search attaches its discoveries to its own root. Each parent edge joins opposite colors, so $uv$ is not a tree edge. Both root paths are simple. Let $z$ be their last common vertex—the common ancestor farthest from the root. The cycle is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一次遇到同色边 $uv$ 时，两端属于同一棵父树：已经完成的较早分量不会有边通向之后的新分量，当前搜索发现的点都接到自己的根上。每条父边两端异色，所以 $uv$ 不是树边。两条根路径都不重复顶点，令 $z$ 为它们的最后公共顶点，即离根最远的共同祖先。所需环为

$$z\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow u
\;\xrightarrow{\,uv\,}\;v
\longrightarrow\cdots\longrightarrow z.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The arrows here indicate traversal order along undirected edges. Removing the shared root-to-$z$ prefix leaves two branches with no common internal vertex. Adding a non-tree edge therefore gives a simple cycle, not just a closed walk with a repeated detour. Since $c(w)$ is the parity of $d(w)$ for every discovered vertex $w$, equal colors imply that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的箭头只表示沿无向边的行进顺序。删去共同的根到 $z$ 前缀后，两支不再有公共内部顶点；加入非树边便得到简单环，而不只是含重复绕行的闭游走。每个已发现点 $w$ 的颜色 $c(w)$ 都等于树深度 $d(w)$ 的奇偶性，因此同色说明

$$d(u)+d(v)-2d(z)+1$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
is odd. To find $z$ explicitly, save the ancestors of $u$ while following its parents, then follow the parents of $v$ until first reaching that saved set. This first intersection is $z$. Retain only the two branches below it and the conflict edge; include $z$ again at the end of the returned list to record closure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为奇数。具体寻找 $z$ 时，可以沿 $u$ 的父链保存全部祖先，再沿 $v$ 的父链上行，直到首次进入保存的集合；首次相交处就是 $z$。只保留它以下的两支和冲突边，输出列表末尾再写一次 $z$，表示闭合。

> [!example] The common ancestor may be an endpoint · 共同祖先可以就是端点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the triangle $a-b-c-a$, DFS may first discover the path $a,b,c$, giving depths $0,1,2$ and colors $0,1,0$. The edge $ca$ conflicts, and $z=a$. The branch from $z$ to $a$ is empty; the other branch and $ca$ give the three-cycle $a,b,c,a$ (or its reverse). The length formula is $2+0-2\cdot0+1=3$. No second nonempty branch is required. BFS on the same graph instead gives both $b,c$ parent $a$; its conflict is $bc$, with two one-edge branches and the same triangle.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对三角形 $a-b-c-a$，DFS 可能先发现路径 $a,b,c$，深度为 $0,1,2$、颜色为 $0,1,0$。边 $ca$ 发生冲突，且 $z=a$。从 $z$ 到 $a$ 的分支为空，另一支与 $ca$ 仍给出三环 $a,b,c,a$，或其反向，长度为 $2+0-2\cdot0+1=3$。不要求两支都非空。同一图上的 BFS 则让 $b,c$ 的父顶点均为 $a$；冲突边是 $bc$，两支各长一，仍还原同一个三角形。

## What must be checked before returning success?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*什么时候才能返回成功？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the square with only edges $ab,bc,cd,da$, a successful result is $c(a)=c(c)=0$ and $c(b)=c(d)=1$. Checking all four edges verifies the [[Proper 着色与独立集划分|proper coloring]]. Different root or neighbor orders may produce another valid labeling or another odd cycle on failure; neither output is required to be unique.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有边 $ab,bc,cd,da$ 的正方形可以输出 $c(a)=c(c)=0$、$c(b)=c(d)=1$。检查全部四条边即可验证这份[[Proper 着色与独立集划分|合法着色]]。改变根或邻点次序可能得到另一份有效标签，或在失败时得到另一条奇环；输出不要求唯一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every [[无向图连通分量|component]] matters. An isolated vertex together with a separate triangle fails globally, although a search starting only at the isolated vertex finds no conflict. An edgeless graph returns color $0$ for all its vertices; the empty vertex set returns the empty coloring immediately. These are at-most-two-color results, not claims that exactly two colors are needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个[[无向图连通分量|分量]]都必须处理。一个孤立点与另一个独立的三角形放在同一图中，全图不可二着色，尽管只从孤立点开始不会发现冲突。无边图可以给全部顶点返回颜色 $0$；空顶点集直接返回空着色。这些都是“至多两色”的结果，并未断言恰好需要两色。

## Cost of the search and the certificate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*搜索与证书的成本*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n=|V|$ and $m=|E|$. With adjacency lists, constant-time mark/color/parent access, constant-time queue operations or recursive frames, and one scan of each processed list, every vertex is discovered at most once and each undirected edge is examined at most twice. Early failure may leave vertices unvisited. Finding the ancestor intersection and writing one cycle costs at most $O(n)$; stop after this first failure. Thus total time is $O(n+m)$ for nonempty input, or $O(1+n+m)$ including the empty case, and auxiliary storage is $O(n)$ apart from the graph and constant setup. This includes the search records, queue or call stack, and ancestor marks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $n=|V|$、$m=|E|$。采用邻接表，并假设标记、颜色、父记录访问及队列操作或递归帧操作耗常数时间，每个已处理的邻接表只扫描一遍，则每个顶点至多发现一次，每条无向边至多检查两次。提前失败时，有些顶点可能尚未访问。寻找共同祖先并写出一条环至多再耗 $O(n)$，随后立即停止。因此非空输入的总时间为 $O(n+m)$，把空输入的常数操作也计入则写成 $O(1+n+m)$；除输入图和常数初始化外，额外存储为 $O(n)$，包含搜索记录、队列或调用栈及祖先标记。

> [!question]- Why retain the parents? · 为什么要保留父记录？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A single edge $uv$ is not an odd-cycle certificate: by itself it can be colored $0,1$. Equal labels in a failed attempt are also not enough to rule out all other assignments. The parent paths show a cycle that forces alternating labels to contradict themselves. A verifier can check each returned edge, distinct intermediate vertices and odd length without trusting the search history.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 单独一条边 $uv$ 不是奇环证书，因为它本身可以染成 $0,1$。某次尝试把两端染成相同标签，也不足以排除其他赋值。父路径还原的环说明，任何交替标签都会自相矛盾。核验者只需检查输出的每条边、中间顶点不重复以及长度为奇数，不必相信搜索过程。

The general common-ancestor reconstruction is justified above; a BFS conflict's equal depths need not hold for DFS.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- MIT《Mathematics for Computer Science》Theorem12.8.3，PDF530–531，以及 MIT Session20 读本 §11.9.2，PDF8–10：逐分量奇偶着色与奇环障碍。正文通过父路径直接给出简单环，不把共同前缀保留成闭游走。
