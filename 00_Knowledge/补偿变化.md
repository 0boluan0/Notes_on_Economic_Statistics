# 补偿变化以变化后的价格衡量恢复原有效用所需调整的收入
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Compensating variation uses new prices to measure the income adjustment that restores initial utility.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

补偿变化（CV）是以变化后的价格为基准，把新预算下的消费者恢复到原有效用所对应的货币福利变化。本卡采用“福利改善为正”的符号：正 CV 是新状态下最多可扣除的收入；负 CV 表示必须额外给予 $-CV$ 才能补偿损失。它是一个收入调整反事实，不意味着现实中已经支付补偿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CV measures a welfare change at new prices by considering the income adjustment that restores initial utility. We use a gain-positive convention: positive CV is income that can be removed from the new budget; negative CV requires an additional payment of $-CV$. No actual payment is implied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从预算 $(p_0,m_0)$ 变到 $(p_1,m_1)$，记 $u_0=v(p_0,m_0)$。使用 [[普通与补偿需求|支出函数]]，在正价格、连续且局部非饱和偏好等保证标准对偶关系的条件下：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a budget change from $(p_0,m_0)$ to $(p_1,m_1)$, let $u_0=v(p_0,m_0)$. Using the [[普通与补偿需求|expenditure function]], assume positive prices and the usual duality conditions, including continuity and local nonsatiation:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
CV=m_1-e(p_1,u_0),
\qquad v(p_1,m_1-CV)=u_0.
$$

若只有价格变动且收入固定为 $m$，就写 $CV=m-e(p_1,u_0)$。因此 [[Hicks与Slutsky补偿|Hicks 补偿所需增加的收入]] $e(p_1,u_0)-m$ 恰为本卡的 $-CV$；有些教材把“需支付的补偿额”本身记作 CV，使用时须先核对正负号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With income fixed at $m$, CV becomes $m-e(p_1,u_0)$. The required [[Hicks与Slutsky补偿|Hicksian income compensation]] is therefore $-CV$. Some texts call that payment itself CV, so check the sign convention before comparing numbers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $u(x,y)=\sqrt{xy}$、$m=100$、$p_y=1$，$p_x$ 从 $10$ 升至 $20$。原选择为 $(5,50)$，原效用 $u_0=\sqrt{250}$；支出函数为 $e(p_x,1,u)=2u\sqrt{p_x}$，故：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $u(x,y)=\sqrt{xy}$, income $100$, $p_y=1$, and $p_x$ rising from $10$ to $20$, the original bundle is $(5,50)$ and utility is $\sqrt{250}$. The expenditure function gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
CV=100-100\sqrt2\approx-41.421.
$$

涨价后须给 $41.421$ 才能恢复原效用，而不是扣掉这笔钱。若问“原价格下损失多少收入可等价于这次涨价”，那是 [[等价变化]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Restoring initial utility after the price increase requires paying $41.421$, not deducting it. The income loss at original prices equivalent to the increase is instead [[等价变化|equivalent variation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
