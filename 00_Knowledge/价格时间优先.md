# 价格时间优先先比较价格竞争力再对同价可比订单按时间排队

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price–time priority ranks orders by price competitiveness and then queues comparable orders at the same price by time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价格时间优先是一种订单配量规则：在适用规则允许相互比较的订单中，买价较高者、卖价较低者先获得成交机会；同一方向、同一价格的订单再按规定的时间次序分配数量。这里的“先”首先是更优价格，而不是全市场所有订单按到达时间排成一条队。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price–time priority is an allocation rule. Among orders eligible for comparison, higher bids and lower asks receive priority; orders on the same side at the same price are then allocated quantity in the specified time order. Better price comes first: all orders are not placed in one market-wide chronological queue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，只考虑同类普通限价买单：A 较早报 100 元买 30 股，B 随后报 100 元买 70 股，C 更晚报 100.10 元买 20 股。若一个可成交的 60 股卖单到达，并严格采用价格时间优先，配量先给 C 20 股，再给 A 30 股，最后给 B 10 股。B 数量较大不使它越过 A。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider comparable ordinary limit bids: A first bids for 30 shares at 100, B later bids for 70 at 100, and C arrives last bidding for 20 at 100.10. Under strict price–time priority, an eligible 60-share sell order allocates 20 to C, 30 to A and 10 to B. B's larger size does not let it jump ahead of A.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不是所有市场和时段的统一规则。同价数量按比例分配、订单类型优先等安排可以不同；即使某规则另设“客户订单优先”，其分类依据也是客户身份而不是订单数量。修改价格或数量是否重置时间优先，同样要查具体规则。配量顺序本身也未完整规定成交价的选取。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is not a universal rule across venues and sessions. Pro-rata allocation at a price and order-type priority are alternatives. A separate customer-order priority concerns customer status, not order size. Whether amendments reset time priority is also rule-specific. Allocation priority alone does not fully specify the execution-price rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
