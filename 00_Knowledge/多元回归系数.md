# 多元回归系数描述控制已含变量后的线性关联
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A multiple-regression coefficient describes a partial linear association after controlling for included regressors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子解释
> 在 $E(Y\mid X)=\beta_0+\beta_1X_1+\cdots+\beta_kX_k$ 的线性条件均值模型中，$\beta_j$ 表示保持其余已含回归变量不变时，$X_j$ 增加 1 单位对应的条件均值变化。若模型只被解释为最佳线性投影，$\beta_j$ 则是分别从 $Y$ 和 $X_j$ 中剔除其他已含变量的线性投影后，两个剩余部分之间的偏线性斜率；这不声称完整条件均值本身线性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the linear conditional-mean model $E(Y\mid X)=\beta_0+\beta_1X_1+\cdots+\beta_kX_k$, $\beta_j$ is the change in the conditional mean associated with a one-unit increase in $X_j$ while the other included regressors are held fixed. If the model is interpreted only as a best linear projection, $\beta_j$ is instead the partial linear slope between the parts of $Y$ and $X_j$ left after projecting each on the other included regressors; this does not assert that the full conditional mean is linear.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用条件与含义边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Use condition and meaning boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“控制其他变量”只指模型中实际包含、且按指定函数形式进入的变量；没有放入模型的因素并未被控制。若 $X_j$ 同时出现在平方项或交互项中，单独一个 $\beta_j$ 不再是所有取值上的完整边际效应。因果读法还要求外生性或其他识别条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Controlling for other variables” refers only to variables actually included in the model and in the specified functional form; omitted factors are not controlled. If $X_j$ also appears in a square or interaction, $\beta_j$ alone is not the complete marginal effect at every value. A causal reading additionally requires exogeneity or another identification argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“保持不变”的比较还必须落在数据实际支持或有可信外推依据的取值组合上；模型能在一个空白区域算出拟合值，不等于总体中真的存在相应的可比对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The held-fixed comparison must also lie in a covariate combination supported by the data or by a credible extrapolation argument. A model can compute a fitted value in an unsupported region without there being comparable units in the population.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在线性条件均值模型下，工资对教育和经验回归中的教育系数比较经验等已含变量相同、但教育相差一年时的条件均值工资差。若只采用线性投影视角，它读作控制这些已含变量后的偏线性关联。两种读法都不是教育与工资的原始双变量斜率，也都没有自动控制未观测能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under a linear conditional-mean model, the education coefficient in a wage regression on education and experience compares conditional mean wages at the same included controls but one additional year of education. Under a projection-only reading, it is the partial linear association after controlling for those included regressors. Neither reading is the raw bivariate education–wage slope, and neither automatically controls unobserved ability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> “保持其他变量不变”中的“其他变量”到底包括哪些？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Which variables are included in the phrase “holding other variables fixed”?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 只包括该模型实际纳入并按指定形式编码的其他回归变量。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Only the other regressors actually included and encoded in the specified model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
