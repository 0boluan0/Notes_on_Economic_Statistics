# Interpreting multiple-regression coefficients
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多元回归系数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose wages are fitted using both education and experience. An education coefficient answers a different question from a regression using education alone: how does the fitted wage change when education changes **while the included experience value stays fixed**? A [[普通最小二乘|multiple OLS regression]] fits these predictors together. Its coefficients divide the fitted association among the included columns; they do not automatically divide the causes of wages among real-world factors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设用教育和经验一起拟合工资。教育系数回答的问题不同于只用教育的一元回归：**保持模型中经验的取值不变**，改变教育时，拟合工资怎样变化？[[普通最小二乘|多元 OLS 回归]]同时拟合这些预测变量。系数把拟合关联分配给已纳入的各列，并不会自动把工资的真实成因分配给各种因素。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What is being held fixed?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 使用条件与含义边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the fitted equation as $\hat y=\hat\beta_0+\hat\beta_1x_1+\cdots+\hat\beta_kx_k$. The $x_j$ are the actual predictor columns, $\hat\beta_0$ is the intercept, and $\hat\beta_j$ multiplies column $j$. If only that column increases by one and all the others remain fixed, the fitted value changes by $\hat\beta_j$. This is first an algebraic statement about the fitted equation. A [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full-column-rank design]] ensures all fitted coefficients are uniquely determined: no included column is an exact linear combination of the others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把拟合方程写成 $\hat y=\hat\beta_0+\hat\beta_1x_1+\cdots+\hat\beta_kx_k$。$x_j$ 是实际纳入的预测列，$\hat\beta_0$ 是截距，$\hat\beta_j$ 乘在第 $j$ 列前。若只有这一列增加一单位，其他列保持不变，拟合值就改变 $\hat\beta_j$。这首先是拟合方程的代数性质。[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|设计矩阵满列秩]]能保证所有拟合系数唯一，即任何一列都不是其他列的精确线性组合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are two distinct population interpretations. Under a correctly specified **linear conditional-mean model**,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总体层面有两种不同的解释。若正确设定了**线性条件均值模型**，

$$E[Y\mid X_1,\ldots,X_k]=\beta_0+\beta_1X_1+\cdots+\beta_kX_k,$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
then $\beta_j$ describes the conditional-mean difference between supported covariate combinations differing by one in column $j$ only. The [[条件期望|conditional mean]] is the population's average outcome at the given information, not each individual's outcome. A fitted $\hat\beta_j$ is an estimate, rather than the population coefficient itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

那么 $\beta_j$ 描述受到支持、且仅第 $j$ 列相差一单位的取值组合之间的条件均值差。[[条件期望|条件均值]]是总体在给定信息下的平均结果，不是每个人的结果。拟合得到的 $\hat\beta_j$ 是估计值，并不等于总体系数本身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Alternatively, the [[条件均值与线性投影|best linear projection]] chooses the linear predictor with smallest expected squared error, even when the actual conditional mean is curved. With finite second moments and an invertible predictor second-moment matrix, its coefficients are unique. A projection coefficient is a partial linear association; it need not equal the actual conditional-mean change at every covariate value. This distinction prevents a convenient fitted plane from being mistaken for the population's exact response surface.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一种读法是[[条件均值与线性投影|最佳线性投影]]：即使真实条件均值弯曲，也选取使期望平方误差最小的线性预测。在有限二阶矩、预测变量二阶矩矩阵可逆时，系数唯一。投影系数表示偏线性关联，不必等于每个取值处真实的条件均值变化。区分这两者，就不会把方便使用的拟合平面当成总体精确的响应曲面。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Education and experience in four observations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a small illustration, let $x$ be additional years of education above a reference level, $z$ years of experience in this example, and $y$ wage in chosen units. Consider four observations:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个小例子说明。令 $x$ 为相对某参考水平增加的受教育年数，$z$ 为本例中的经验年数，$y$ 为选定单位下的工资。考虑四个观测：

| Observation / 观测 | $x$ | $z$ | $y$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 0 | 0 | 2 |
| 2 | 1 | 0 | 2 |
| 3 | 1 | 1 | 6 |
| 4 | 2 | 1 | 10 |

> [!example] Two slopes from the same data / 同一数据的两个斜率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fitting an intercept, $x$, and $z$ together gives $\hat y=1+2x+4z$. Its predictions are $(1,3,7,9)$ and residuals are $(1,-1,-1,1)$. The education coefficient is $2$: for example, the fitted wages at $(x,z)=(1,1)$ and $(2,1)$ are $7$ and $9$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 同时拟合截距、$x$ 和 $z$，得到 $\hat y=1+2x+4z$。预测值为 $(1,3,7,9)$，残差为 $(1,-1,-1,1)$。教育系数为 $2$：例如 $(x,z)=(1,1)$ 和 $(2,1)$ 处的拟合工资为 $7$ 和 $9$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If experience is omitted, $\bar x=1$, $\bar y=5$, and the [[一元 OLS 斜率|simple OLS slope]] is $8/2=4$. Experience varies with education in this table, so the raw slope mixes their fitted associations. The [[FWL残差化定理|FWL theorem]] shows exactly why adjusting both variables for experience changes the slope to $2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 如果不纳入经验，$\bar x=1$、$\bar y=5$，[[一元 OLS 斜率]]为 $8/2=4$。这张表中经验随教育一起变化，所以原始斜率混合了两者的拟合关联。[[FWL残差化定理]]会准确说明，为什么同时调整两个变量中的经验关联后，斜率变成 $2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The common slope $2$ is a fitted compromise: the two observed wages at $z=0$ differ by $0$, whereas those at $z=1$ differ by $4$. The model imposes a common education slope across the two experience levels. Thus “holding experience fixed” does not say every pair of observed wages differs by exactly the coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共同斜率 $2$ 是拟合后的折中：$z=0$ 的两个观测工资相差 $0$，$z=1$ 的两个则相差 $4$。模型要求两个经验水平共用一个教育斜率。因此，“保持经验不变”并不是说每对实际工资都恰好相差该系数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Columns, support, and causality

A predictor column may be a transformation rather than a separate raw variable; this is the distinction in [[参数线性|linearity in parameters]]. For $\hat y=a+bx+cz+dxz$, changing the raw $x$ by one at fixed $z$ changes the prediction by $b+dz$, because the interaction column $xz$ also changes. For $\hat y=a+bx+cx^2$, a one-unit increase in $x$ changes the prediction by $b+c(2x+1)$, while the [[导数|local derivative]] is $b+2cx$. A single coefficient is therefore not always the complete [[取值依赖边际效应|value-dependent marginal effect]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 预测列、支持范围与因果解释

预测列可以是变量的变换，不一定是独立的原始变量；这正是[[参数线性]]所区分的内容。在 $\hat y=a+bx+cz+dxz$ 中，固定 $z$，将原始 $x$ 增加一单位，预测变化为 $b+dz$，因为交互列 $xz$ 也随之改变。在 $\hat y=a+bx+cx^2$ 中，$x$ 增加一单位的预测变化是 $b+c(2x+1)$，[[导数|局部导数]]却是 $b+2cx$。因此单个系数并不总等于完整的[[取值依赖边际效应|边际效应]]。

> [!warning] Adjusting included columns has a limited meaning / 调整已含列有明确范围
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The model adjusts only for the controls and functional forms actually included. Adding experience does not automatically adjust for unobserved ability; adding a linear age term does not allow every possible age pattern. Nor does a computable prediction in an empty region establish that comparable people exist there. A causal education effect requires a defensible [[条件均值因果边界|identification argument]] beyond the fitted coefficient.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 模型只调整实际纳入的控制变量及函数形式。加入经验不会自动控制未观测能力；加入年龄的一次项，也不等于允许年龄产生任意模式。能在空白区域算出预测值，不代表那里存在可比较的人。教育的因果效应还需要拟合系数以外、可辩护的[[条件均值因果边界|识别论证]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an included predictor is an exact linear combination of other columns, its separate coefficient is not uniquely identified by this regression. If it is only nearly explained by them, uniqueness can remain, but precision needs a separate analysis of the remaining variation and the error covariance. This is why an interpretation of a coefficient should be accompanied by the fitted model and the comparison it represents.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若某个预测变量是其他列的精确线性组合，它的单独系数就不能由该回归唯一确定。若只是几乎能被解释，唯一性可以仍然成立，但精度还要结合剩余变动与误差协方差另行分析。因此，解释一个系数时应同时说明所拟合的模型，以及该系数代表哪一种比较。

## Seeing the adjustment directly
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 继续

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[FWL残差化定理|FWL theorem]] turns “adjusting for included controls” into an explicit residual calculation. Read [[条件均值与线性投影|conditional means and linear projections]] when deciding which population interpretation is justified; read [[取值依赖边际效应|value-dependent marginal effects]] when transformations make a raw-variable change move several columns at once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[FWL残差化定理]]把“调整已含控制变量”变成可直接计算的残差操作。要判断总体解释属于哪一种，可读[[条件均值与线性投影]]；原始变量的变化会同时改变多列时，可读[[取值依赖边际效应]]。
