# Marginal distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*边际分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **marginal distribution** describes one variable on its own when a model contains several variables together. For example, a [[联合分布|joint distribution]] may describe both rain and whether a person carries an umbrella. If we ask only how likely rain is, we need to include every way rain can occur, with or without an umbrella. Adding those possibilities gives the marginal distribution of the rain variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**边际分布**是在一个同时包含多个变量的模型中，单独描述某个变量的分布。例如，[[联合分布]]同时描述下雨与某人是否带伞；如果现在只问下雨的概率，就需要把“下雨且带伞”和“下雨且没带伞”都包括进来。把这些可能性相加，就得到了下雨变量的边际分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Add the possibilities we are no longer distinguishing
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把不再区分的可能性加起来*

> [!example] Totals at the margins · 写在表格边上的合计
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use the rain-and-umbrella model: $X=1$ means rain, $X=0$ means no rain, $Y=1$ means an umbrella, and $Y=0$ means no umbrella. The four inner cells are joint probabilities. The right-hand and bottom totals describe each variable separately.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 沿用雨天与带伞的模型：$X=1$ 表示下雨，$X=0$ 表示没下雨；$Y=1$ 表示带伞，$Y=0$ 表示没带伞。中间四个格子是联合概率，右侧与底部的合计则分别描述单个变量。
>
> | $X\backslash Y$ | $Y=0$ | $Y=1$ | $P(X=x)$ |
> |---|---:|---:|---:|
> | $X=0$ | $0.45$ | $0.15$ | **$0.60$** |
> | $X=1$ | $0.10$ | $0.30$ | **$0.40$** |
> | $P(Y=y)$ | **$0.55$** | **$0.45$** | $1.00$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To find $P(X=1)$, hold the row $X=1$ fixed and add across both values of $Y$: $0.10+0.30=0.40$. These cells are distinct ways of having rain, so their probabilities add. Repeat for $X=0$ to obtain the whole marginal distribution: $X$ is $0$ with probability $0.60$ and $1$ with probability $0.40$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求 $P(X=1)$ 时，固定 $X=1$ 这一行，把 $Y$ 的两种取值都加进来：$0.10+0.30=0.40$。两个格子是下雨发生的不同方式，因此概率可以相加。再对 $X=0$ 做同样的事，就得到完整的边际分布：$X$ 以 $0.60$ 的概率取 $0$，以 $0.40$ 的概率取 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $Y$, work down each column instead. The probability of an umbrella is $P(Y=1)=0.15+0.30=0.45$. The name “marginal” refers to these totals often being written in the margins of a joint table. The operation depends on which variable we keep, not on whether a particular drawing puts it in rows or columns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换成 $Y$，就沿每一列往下相加。例如带伞概率是 $P(Y=1)=0.15+0.30=0.45$。“边际”这个名字来自这些合计常写在联合表的边上。实际该怎么加，取决于我们要保留哪个变量；不同图表可以交换行列的安排。

> [!abstract] Keep $x$, sum over $y$ · 保留 $x$，对 $y$ 求和
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the joint [[概率质量函数|probability mass function]] is $p_{x,y}=P(X=x,Y=y)$, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若联合[[概率质量函数]]为 $p_{x,y}=P(X=x,Y=y)$，则
>
> $$p_X(x)=\sum_y p_{x,y},\qquad p_Y(y)=\sum_x p_{x,y}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the first formula, $x$ stays fixed while $y$ runs through all its possible values. The result is one probability for that $x$. Varying $x$ gives the whole marginal PMF. No independence assumption is needed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一条式子里，$x$ 固定，$y$ 遍历它的所有可能取值，结果就是这个 $x$ 对应的概率。让 $x$ 遍历其取值，才得到整个边际概率质量函数。这个操作不需要独立性假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Ignoring a variable and learning its value are different
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*不区分一个变量，与已知它的值，有什么不同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The marginal probability $P(Y=1)=0.45$ includes both rainy and dry days. If instead we are told that it rains, the question becomes $P(Y=1\mid X=1)$. We keep only the rainy row, whose total probability is $0.40$, and express the umbrella cell as a fraction of that row: $0.30/0.40=0.75$. This is a [[条件分布|conditional distribution]]. The symbol $\mid$ means “given”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

边际概率 $P(Y=1)=0.45$ 同时包括下雨与没下雨的日子。如果已经知道当天下雨，问题就变成 $P(Y=1\mid X=1)$：只保留下雨这一行，它的总概率是 $0.40$，然后看带伞的格子占这一行多少，得到 $0.30/0.40=0.75$。这属于[[条件分布]]，其中 $\mid$ 表示“在已知……的条件下”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Marginalising therefore adds over the other variable; conditioning selects known information and renormalises what remains. Once we have reduced the joint table to separate marginal distributions, we generally cannot reconstruct the original four cells: [[边际分布不定联合分布|the marginals do not determine how the variables are paired]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，边际化把另一个变量的可能性加起来；条件化则选出已知信息允许的部分，再重新归一化。把联合表压缩成各自的边际分布后，通常无法重新找回原来四个格子的数值，因为[[边际分布不定联合分布|边际分布没有保留变量怎样配对的信息]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## With a density, replace the sum by an integral
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*存在密度时，把求和换成积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a joint [[概率密度函数|density]] $f_{X,Y}(x,y)$ exists, the same idea becomes continuous accumulation. To obtain the marginal density of $X$, hold $x$ fixed and integrate over every possible $y$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果联合[[概率密度函数|密度]] $f_{X,Y}(x,y)$ 存在，同一个思路就变成连续的累积。要得到 $X$ 的边际密度，仍然固定 $x$，把所有可能的 $y$ 都积分进去：

$$f_X(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dy,\qquad
f_Y(y)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f_{X,Y}(x,y)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The integration variable disappears; the retained variable may still affect the result. A [[定积分|definite integral]] adds the area under each one-variable slice. Each resulting marginal density must integrate to one. Marginal distributions themselves exist even without a joint density; the formulas above apply when that density representation is available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

被积分的变量会消失，保留的变量仍可能影响结果。这里的[[定积分]]累积的是每条单变量切片下的面积，最后得到的每个边际密度都应积分为一。边际分布本身并不依赖联合密度的存在；上面的公式适用于能用联合密度表示的情形。

> [!example] A triangular region · 三角形区域上的密度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $f_{X,Y}(x,y)=2$ when $0<y<x<1$, and is zero elsewhere. The allowed pairs form the triangle below $y=x$ in the unit square. Its area is $1/2$, so density $2$ gives total probability $2\times(1/2)=1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $0<y<x<1$ 时 $f_{X,Y}(x,y)=2$，其他地方为零。允许的取值对构成单位正方形中 $y=x$ 下方的三角形。它的面积是 $1/2$，所以密度为 $2$ 时总概率是 $2\times(1/2)=1$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fix $0<x<1$. The allowed $y$ values run from $0$ to $x$, so the slice has constant height $2$ and length $x$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定 $0<x<1$ 时，允许的 $y$ 从 $0$ 到 $x$。这条切片的高度恒为 $2$，长度为 $x$：
>
> $$f_X(x)=\int_0^x 2\,dy=2x,\qquad 0<x<1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fix $0<y<1$ instead. Now $x$ runs from $y$ to $1$, so its slice has length $1-y$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果改为固定 $0<y<1$，则 $x$ 从 $y$ 到 $1$，切片长度为 $1-y$：
>
> $$f_Y(y)=\int_y^1 2\,dx=2(1-y),\qquad 0<y<1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both densities are zero outside their stated intervals. The different integration limits come from the triangle: always determine the permitted values before integrating. A density value such as $f_X(0.75)=1.5$ is a height, not a probability of $1.5$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个密度在所写区间以外都为零。不同的积分上下限来自三角形的范围，所以应先确定允许的取值，再积分。例如 $f_X(0.75)=1.5$ 是密度高度，并不是说概率为 $1.5$。
