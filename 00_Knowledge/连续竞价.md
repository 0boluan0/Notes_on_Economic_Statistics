# 连续竞价在交易时段按到达订单与存量订单持续撮合

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A continuous auction repeatedly matches incoming orders against resting orders during its trading session.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续竞价是在交易时段中，随着订单到达，按适用规则检查它与存量订单能否成交并持续进行撮合的机制。它不需要等到该轮所有参与者报完单后才统一撮合定价；但“连续”描述撮合方式，不保证每一笔订单立即成交。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A continuous auction checks and matches incoming orders against resting interest under the applicable rules throughout its session. It need not wait for all participants to submit orders before a common price-setting event. “Continuous” describes the matching mechanism, not guaranteed immediate execution of each order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以采用对手方存量订单价格、允许逐档取量的简化系统为例，卖盘为 100.00 元 50 股、100.20 元 100 股。限价 100.20 元的 120 股买单到达，可依次成交 50 股和 70 股，形成两个成交价。其[[逐档成交均价|加权均价]]不能反过来当作每笔成交价。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a simplified system that executes at resting opposing prices and permits fills across levels. With asks of 50 shares at 100.00 and 100 at 100.20, an incoming 120-share buy limited to 100.20 can fill 50 and then 70 shares at two prices. Its [[逐档成交均价|weighted average price]] is not the price of every individual fill.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与[[集合竞价]]的区别是逐次匹配与一轮统一定价，而不是“限价单／市价单”的区别。是否采用[[价格时间优先]]、如何处理剩余订单、是否暂停交易和具体成交价选取，均由规则决定；不能把某一市场的连续撮合条件直接用来解另一市场的集合竞价题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The contrast with a [[集合竞价|call auction]] is sequential matching versus one price for a round, not limit orders versus market orders. [[价格时间优先|Price–time priority]], residual handling, pauses and execution-price selection are rule-specific. One venue's continuous-matching conditions do not automatically solve another venue's call auction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
