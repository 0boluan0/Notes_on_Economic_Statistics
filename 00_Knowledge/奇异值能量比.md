# Singular-value energy ratio
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*奇异值能量比*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix can be approximated by keeping only its largest singular components. The **retained energy ratio** measures how much of its total squared magnitude those components keep. Here “energy” has a precise mathematical meaning: add the squared magnitudes of all matrix entries. It does not automatically mean physical energy or useful information for a prediction task.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保留矩阵中最大的几个奇异分量，就能得到一个近似矩阵。**保留能量比**衡量这些分量留下了原矩阵平方总量的多少。这里“能量”的含义很具体：把所有矩阵元素的模平方相加；它不自动等于物理能量，也不自动等于预测任务需要的信息。

> [!example] Keeping 90% of a squared total · 保留平方总量的 90%
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider $A=\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}$. Its two [[奇异值|singular values]], the stretch amounts along perpendicular directions, are $3$ and $1$. Keeping the first component gives $A_1=\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}$. The original squared total is $3^2+1^2=10$, and the retained total is $9$, so the energy ratio is $9/10=90\%$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取上述 $A$，它的两个[[奇异值]]，即沿相互垂直方向的伸缩量，为 $3$ 和 $1$。只保留第一分量得到 $A_1$。原平方总量是 $3^2+1^2=10$，保留部分为 $9$，所以能量比是 $9/10=90\%$。

## From matrix entries to singular components
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从矩阵元素转到奇异分量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a nonzero $m\times n$ real or complex matrix, with positive integers $m,n$. Set $p=\min(m,n)$ and list its singular values as $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_p\ge0$, including repeated values and zeros. Its [[Frobenius范数|Frobenius norm]] is the square root of the entrywise squared total. The [[Frobenius范数与奇异值|singular-value identity]] gives the same total in two ways:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 是非零的 $m\times n$ 实或复矩阵，$m,n$ 为正整数。令 $p=\min(m,n)$，把包括重复值和零在内的奇异值排列为 $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_p\ge0$。[[Frobenius范数]]是元素模平方总量的平方根；[[Frobenius范数与奇异值|奇异值恒等式]]给出同一总量的两种算法：

$$\|A\|_F^2=\sum_{j=1}^{m}\sum_{\ell=1}^{n}|a_{j\ell}|^2
=\sum_{i=1}^{p}\sigma_i^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $a_{j\ell}$ is the entry in row $j$, column $\ell$; $|a_{j\ell}|$ is its absolute value for real entries and modulus for complex entries. A [[SVD秩一展开|rank-one SVD expansion]] writes each component as $\sigma_i u_i v_i^*$, where $u_i$ and $v_i$ are unit left and right singular directions. The star means conjugate transpose, or ordinary transpose for real entries. A [[截断SVD|truncated SVD]] keeps the first $k$ components:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$a_{j\ell}$ 是第 $j$ 行、第 $\ell$ 列的元素；$|a_{j\ell}|$ 对实数取绝对值，对复数取模。[[SVD秩一展开]]把每一分量写成 $\sigma_i u_i v_i^*$，其中 $u_i,v_i$ 是单位左、右奇异方向；星号表示共轭转置，实数时就是普通转置。[[截断SVD]]保留前 $k$ 项：

$$A_k=\sum_{i=1}^{k}\sigma_i u_i v_i^*,\qquad 0\le k\le p.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We allow integer $k$ beyond the rank by including zero singular values. The [[矩阵秩|rank]] $r=\operatorname{rank}(A)$ counts independent output directions. Only the first $r$ terms are nonzero; hence $A_k=A$ whenever $r\le k\le p$. For $k=0$, the empty sum means $A_0=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里允许整数 $k$ 超过秩，把零奇异值也列入。[[矩阵秩]] $r=\operatorname{rank}(A)$ 计算独立输出方向的个数，只有前 $r$ 项非零，因此 $r\le k\le p$ 时均有 $A_k=A$。$k=0$ 的空和表示 $A_0=0$。

> [!important] Retained energy and reconstruction error · 保留能量与重构误差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For nonzero $A$, define
>
> $$E_k=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{k}\sigma_i^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{p}\sigma_i^2}
> =\frac{\|A_k\|_F^2}{\|A\|_F^2}
> =1-\frac{\|A-A_k\|_F^2}{\|A\|_F^2}.$$
>
> The denominator is positive. The ratio increases from $E_0=0$ to $E_r=1$ and stays $1$ for $r\le k\le p$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对非零 $A$，按上式定义 $E_k$；分母严格为正。比例从 $E_0=0$ 逐步增加到 $E_r=1$，此后 $r\le k\le p$ 均保持为 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equality follows because the discarded components have squared total $\sum_{i=k+1}^{p}\sigma_i^2$. The kept and discarded SVD pieces are perpendicular under the entrywise matrix inner product, so their squared Frobenius norms add. This identity describes the error of this particular truncation; its [[截断SVD最佳低秩近似|best-approximation property]] is a separate theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述等式来自被丢弃分量的平方总量 $\sum_{i=k+1}^{p}\sigma_i^2$。在逐元素的矩阵内积下，SVD 中保留与舍弃的部分相互垂直，因此它们的 Frobenius 范数平方可以直接相加。该恒等式描述这种截断的误差；它的[[截断SVD最佳低秩近似|最佳近似性质]]是另一个定理。

## What the percentage does and does not measure
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*这个百分比究竟衡量什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the $3,1$ example, the discarded squared proportion is $10\%$, while the relative Frobenius **norm** error is $\sqrt{0.1}\approx31.6\%$. The square matters. The [[谱范数|spectral norm]] measures the largest stretch instead of adding squares: for $k<r$, $\|A-A_k\|_2=\sigma_{k+1}$, so its relative error is $\sigma_{k+1}/\sigma_1$. In this example that is $1/3$. All reconstruction errors are zero once $k\ge r$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $3,1$ 的例子中，舍弃的平方比例为 $10\%$，但相对 Frobenius **范数**误差为 $\sqrt{0.1}\approx31.6\%$，不能漏掉平方。[[谱范数]]衡量最大伸缩量而不把平方相加：$k<r$ 时，$\|A-A_k\|_2=\sigma_{k+1}$，其相对误差为 $\sigma_{k+1}/\sigma_1$，本例为 $1/3$。$k\ge r$ 后，各种重构误差都为零。

> [!warning] A zero matrix has no energy percentage · 零矩阵没有能量百分比
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $A=0$, reconstruction error is zero, but the ratio is $0/0$ and is undefined. Also, the entrywise metric uses the chosen units: multiplying one column by $100$ multiplies that column's squared contribution by $10{,}000$ and can change the singular directions and energy ratios.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $A=0$ 时重构误差为零，但比例是 $0/0$，没有定义。逐元素度量还取决于所选单位：把一列乘以 $100$，该列的平方贡献就乘以 $10{,}000$，奇异方向与能量比例都可能改变。

## When energy is also explained sample variance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*什么时候能量比也等于样本解释方差比*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real data with $N\ge2$ observations in rows and $d$ variables in columns, subtract each column's sample mean to form $X_c$. Its [[样本协方差矩阵|sample covariance matrix]] is $S=X_c^TX_c/(N-1)$. In [[主成分分析|PCA]], the variance of component $i$ is $\sigma_i(X_c)^2/(N-1)$. The sum of all variable variances is $\|X_c\|_F^2/(N-1)$, so the common factor cancels: if $X_c\ne0$, its energy ratio is the cumulative [[解释方差比|explained sample-variance ratio]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实数据，设每行是一个观测，共 $N\ge2$ 行，每列是一个变量，共 $d$ 列。每列减去各自的样本均值后得到 $X_c$，其[[样本协方差矩阵]]是 $S=X_c^TX_c/(N-1)$。[[主成分分析|PCA]] 第 $i$ 个成分的方差为 $\sigma_i(X_c)^2/(N-1)$，全部变量方差之和为 $\|X_c\|_F^2/(N-1)$；共同因子抵消，所以 $X_c\ne0$ 时，能量比就是累计[[解释方差比|样本解释方差比]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Applying SVD directly to uncentred observations generally includes the squared column means as well as variation around them. Standardizing columns changes the matrix and the variance weighting again. Even with correct centring, a large variance percentage alone does not establish [[奇异值能量不等于任务信息|adequacy for a downstream task]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直接对未中心化的观测做 SVD，通常还会把列均值的平方贡献计入，而不只是均值附近的变动。逐列标准化又会改变矩阵与方差权重。即使中心化正确，高方差占比本身也不能保证[[奇异值能量不等于任务信息|满足下游任务需求]]。
