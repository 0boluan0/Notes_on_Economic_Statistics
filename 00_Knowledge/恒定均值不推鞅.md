# Constant means and the martingale property
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*恒定均值与鞅性质*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A process can keep the same overall average while moving systematically within each observed situation. A **martingale** rules out those conditional mean movements; a constant overall mean does not. To see the difference, first reveal a fair gain or loss, then force the total back to zero. The average stays zero throughout, but after the reveal the next movement is already known.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个过程可以在总体平均不变的同时，在每种已经观察到的情形下发生确定方向的平均变化。**鞅**排除了这样的条件均值变化，仅有总体均值不变则没有。最简单的办法是先揭示一次公平的输赢，再把累计值强行归零；各期平均一直为零，但揭示之后，下一步的变化已经知道。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a [[鞅定义|martingale]] $M$ with information flow $(\mathcal F_n)$, the requirement is $E[M_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=M_n$ almost surely, along with adaptedness and integrability at each time. Applying the [[全期望公式|law of total expectation]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对信息流为 $(\mathcal F_n)$ 的[[鞅定义|鞅]] $M$，要求 $E[M_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=M_n$ 几乎必然成立，同时每期适应且可积。由[[全期望公式]]，

$$E[M_{n+1}]=E\!\left[E[M_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]\right]=E[M_n].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus constant finite means are necessary. But the outer expectation averages together all the information states. Reversing that averaging is invalid: one number cannot tell us what the conditional average was in every state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以有限均值恒定是必要条件。但外层期望把所有信息状态再混在一起平均；不能倒过来恢复这一步，因为一个数无法说明每种状态下的条件平均分别是什么。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reveal a fair sign, then erase the value
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个最小反例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Z$ be $-1$ or $1$ with equal probability. Define $M_0=0$, $M_1=Z$, and $M_n=0$ for every $n\ge2$. Use the [[自然滤过|natural filtration]] $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(M_0,\ldots,M_n)$, meaning all observations so far. The whole example has just two equally likely paths:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $Z$ 等概率取 $-1$ 或 $1$。定义 $M_0=0$、$M_1=Z$，此后每个 $n\ge2$ 都令 $M_n=0$。使用[[自然滤过]] $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(M_0,\ldots,M_n)$，即截至当时的全部观察。整个例子只有两条等概率路径：

| $Z$ | $M_0$ | $M_1$ | $M_2,M_3,\ldots$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $-1$ | $0$ | $-1$ | $0,0,\ldots$ |
| $1$ | $0$ | $1$ | $0,0,\ldots$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The process is [[适应过程|adapted]] and bounded by $1$, so it is integrable at every time. Its overall means are all zero. At time $0$, the first value has conditional mean zero, as required. At time $1$, however, $\mathcal F_1=\sigma(Z)$ reveals the sign, and the next value is certainly zero:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

过程[[适应过程|适应]]，绝对值不超过 $1$，所以每期可积；各期总体均值全为零。在时刻 $0$，第一期值的条件均值是零，符合要求。但到时刻 $1$，$\mathcal F_1=\sigma(Z)$ 已揭示符号，下一期又必然归零，因此：

$$E[M_2\mid\mathcal F_1]=0\ne Z=M_1.$$

> [!example] Opposite conditional drifts cancel · 两种条件漂移互相抵消
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After observing $Z=1$, the next change is $-1$; after observing $Z=-1$, it is $1$. Each conditional change is nonzero, but averaging the two gives zero. This is why the unconditional mean misses the failure. Returning the displayed value to zero also does not erase the history: $\mathcal F_2=\sigma(Z)$ still remembers the first observation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 观察到 $Z=1$ 后，下次变化为 $-1$；观察到 $Z=-1$ 后，下次变化为 $1$。各自的条件变化都非零，合起来平均却为零，因此总体均值看不见这种失败。显示值回到零也不会抹去历史：$\mathcal F_2=\sigma(Z)$ 仍记得第一次观察。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same counterexample works for any integrable $Z$ with $E[Z]=0$ and $P(Z\ne0)>0$, not only a fair sign. The conditional equality fails on $\{Z\ne0\}$, a positive-probability event. Boundedness and two equally likely paths merely make the first example easier to inspect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公平符号只是便于检查的特例。任意可积 $Z$，只要 $E[Z]=0$ 且 $P(Z\ne0)>0$，同样构成反例。条件等式在正概率事件 $\{Z\ne0\}$ 上失败；有界性与两条等概率路径只是让最初的例子更直观。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What to check instead
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*应当检查什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the information flow first. Then check observability, integrability and the [[条件期望|conditional equality]] itself. The conditionally fair quantity is the next **change** given the past, as captured by a [[鞅差序列|martingale difference sequence]]. Constant overall means only show that positive and negative conditional changes balance after one more average.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定信息流，再分别检查可观测、可积，以及[[条件期望|条件等式]]本身。条件公平针对的是给定过去后的下一次**变化**，这正由[[鞅差序列]]刻画。总体均值恒定，只说明正负条件变化在再次平均后恰好抵消。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the additional [[次鞅|submartingale]] condition, the conditional expected changes must be nonnegative; the result then changes, as explained in [[恒均值次鞅即鞅|a submartingale with constant means is a martingale]]. That extra one-sided restriction is absent in the example above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若额外已知[[次鞅]]条件，条件期望增量就必须非负，结论会改变，详见[[恒均值次鞅即鞅]]。上面的反例恰好缺少这条额外的单向约束。
