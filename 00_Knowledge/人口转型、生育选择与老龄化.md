# 人口转型、生育选择与老龄化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Demographic Transition, Fertility Choice, and Aging*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释一个社会如何从高生高死走向低生低死，以及这个过程如何先创造潜在人口红利，又在后期带来老龄化压力。
> **具体锚点：** 死亡率先下降而生育率尚未调整时，儿童人口先快速增加；这一代进入工作年龄后，若有教育和就业，抚养比才可能下降并转成红利。
> **核心难点：** 年龄结构只提供潜力；家庭生育、教育、女性时间、就业与养老制度会共同决定结果。
> **为什么重要：** 同一个“人口增长”数字可对学校、就业、住房、养老金和医疗产生完全不同的时序压力。
> **继续：** 如果需要理解为什么前现代收入增长常被人口吸收，回到 [[马尔萨斯陷阱与现代增长转型]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It explains how a society moves from high birth and death rates to low ones, and how that process first creates a potential demographic dividend and later produces aging pressure.
> **Concrete anchor:** When mortality falls before fertility adjusts, the child population rises first. When that cohort reaches working age, a dividend appears only if education and jobs allow the dependency ratio to fall productively.
> **Central difficulty:** Age structure provides potential, not an automatic outcome. Household fertility, education, women's time, employment, and pension institutions jointly determine the result.
> **Why it matters:** The same population-growth number can create entirely different timing pressures on schools, jobs, housing, pensions, and health care.
> **Continue:** To understand why population often absorbed income gains in premodern economies, return to [[马尔萨斯陷阱与现代增长转型|The Malthusian Trap and the Transition to Modern Growth]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 人口转型的时序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The timing of demographic transition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

死亡率通常先因营养、卫生和医疗下降，生育率后降，中间产生人口快速增长；最终进入低生低死。转型路径和时滞因国家、性别教育、避孕可得和社会保障而异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mortality often falls first as nutrition, sanitation, and medicine improve; fertility falls later, producing an interval of rapid population growth before low birth and death rates emerge. Paths and lags differ with country context, women's education, access to contraception, and social protection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个顺序不是简单的四阶段标签，而是队列过程。出生潮先增加学龄人口，约十几年后才增加劳动年龄人口，再数十年后增加老年人口。政策窗口也随队列移动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This sequence is not merely a four-stage label; it is a cohort process. A birth wave first raises the school-age population, then the working-age population roughly one or two decades later, and the elderly population several decades after that. Policy windows move with the cohort.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 生育、教育与家庭选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fertility, education, and household choice*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

家庭在儿童数量、每个儿童教育、母亲时间和养老保障间权衡。儿童死亡下降不必立即降低生育，规范、信贷和公共服务会改变选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Households trade off the number of children, education per child, mothers' time, and old-age security. Lower child mortality need not immediately reduce fertility because norms, credit, and public services alter choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将“收入高了所以少生”当作唯一解释会遗漏机制。收入增加既可降低养育负担，也可提高女性时间的机会成本；教育回报提高使每个孩子的质量投资更有价值；养老金可降低依靠子女养老的需求。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Higher income causes fewer births” is not a complete mechanism. Income can make childrearing more affordable but also raise the opportunity cost of women's time. Higher returns to education increase the value of quality investment per child, and pensions can reduce reliance on children for old-age support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 人口红利的条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditions for a demographic dividend*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

劳动年龄人口占比上升可降低抚养比并提高储蓄/劳动供给，但需要就业、教育、健康和金融制度把潜力转成产出；人口结构本身不是自动红利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rising working-age share can lower the dependency ratio and increase saving or labor supply, but employment, education, health, and financial institutions must turn that potential into output. Age structure itself is not an automatic dividend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个简化抚养比是

$$D=\frac{N_{0-14}+N_{65+}}{N_{15-64}}.$$

若儿童和老年人共 40，劳动年龄人口 60，则 $D=2/3$。若一代人进入劳动年龄后变成 30 名被抚养者和 70 名劳动年龄人口，$D=3/7$。但若 70 人中大量失业，统计抚养比下降也不会自动转成人均产出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simplified dependency ratio is

$$D=\frac{N_{0-14}+N_{65+}}{N_{15-64}}.$$

If there are 40 children and elderly people and 60 working-age people, then $D=2/3$. If a cohort's aging changes this to 30 dependants and 70 working-age people, $D=3/7$. But if many of the 70 are unemployed, a lower statistical dependency ratio does not automatically become higher output per person.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 老龄化的约束与调整
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Constraints and adjustment under aging*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

低生育和长寿提高老年抚养压力，影响养老金、医疗、劳动供给和储蓄。政策可从退休年龄、生产率、移民和制度设计多渠道应对。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Low fertility and longer lives raise old-age dependency pressure and affect pensions, health care, labor supply, and saving. Policy can adjust through retirement ages, productivity, migration, and institutional design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

诊断必须区分财政抚养比和人口抚养比。一个 66 岁人可继续就业纳税，一个 50 岁人也可因退出劳动市场而依赖转移。健康寿命、实际退休年龄、劳动参与和生产率比固定年龄线更接近财政问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diagnosis must distinguish fiscal from demographic dependency. A 66-year-old may still work and pay taxes, while a 50-year-old may rely on transfers after leaving the labor force. Healthy life expectancy, effective retirement age, participation, and productivity are closer to the fiscal question than a fixed age cutoff alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把劳动年龄占比上升直接叫作已实现的人口红利。
- 解释生育下降时只用收入，忽略儿童死亡、教育回报、女性时间与养老保障。
- 只用 65 岁以上占比计算财政压力，不看实际就业、纳税、健康和给付规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Calling a rise in the working-age share an already realized demographic dividend.
- Explaining fertility decline with income alone while ignoring child mortality, returns to education, women's time, and old-age security.
- Measuring fiscal pressure only by the share aged 65 and over without actual employment, taxation, health, and benefit rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 人口转型中死亡率和生育率通常谁先下降？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Which usually falls first in demographic transition, mortality or fertility?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 死亡率常先下降，生育率随后调整，中间阶段人口增长加快。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Mortality often falls first and fertility adjusts later, creating an intermediate period of faster population growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 人口红利为什么不是年龄结构自动兑现？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is a demographic dividend not automatically delivered by age structure?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 新增劳动年龄人口需有技能、健康、工作和资本，否则可能变成失业与社会压力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> New working-age people need skills, health, jobs, and capital; otherwise the cohort can become unemployment and social pressure rather than higher output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明人口老龄化为什么不能只看 65 岁分界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why population aging cannot be diagnosed from an age-65 cutoff alone.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 财政负担取决于实际就业、纳税、健康、退休和给付制度；生理年龄不会自动决定一个人是生产者还是被抚养者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Fiscal burden depends on actual work, taxes, health, retirement, and benefit institutions. Chronological age does not automatically determine whether someone produces or depends on support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
