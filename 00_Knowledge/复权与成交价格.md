# 复权序列用于一致比较，交易账户仍按实际价格、股数和公司行动核算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Adjusted series support consistent comparisons; trading accounts still use actual prices, share quantities and corporate actions.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复权价格是按指定公司行动规则重表达的历史价格序列，用于使跨拆股、分配等事件的数据可比较；成交价格是订单执行时每股实际支付或收到的金额。两种数值承担不同职责。[[交易策略回测]]可以用声明口径的复权序列计算信号，但订单价款、持股数量与现金必须能还原为同一个投资账户。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adjusted prices restate a historical series under specified corporate-action conventions to support comparison across splits or distributions. An execution price is the amount actually paid or received per share at a fill. A [[交易策略回测|strategy backtest]] may calculate signals from a stated adjusted series, but trade consideration, share quantities and cash must reconcile to one investment account.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先问复权因子使用了哪些事件、截至哪个信息时点。某些服务用全部历史公司行动回调过去价格，另一些提供截至模拟时点的调整历史。后来的每股价格尺度不能直接用于过去的绝对价格筛选、下单数量或金额。对所有输入都乘同一正数的纯均线比较，交叉方向在数学上可保持不变；这不代表含价格门槛、取整和成本的整条交易流程也不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check which events and information cutoff determine adjustment factors. Some services restate history using all corporate actions, while others offer adjustments known by the simulated time. A later price scale cannot directly determine historical absolute-price screens, order quantities or cash amounts. Multiplying every input by one positive constant preserves a pure moving-average comparison, but need not preserve a full procedure with price thresholds, rounding and costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用实际未复权成交价时，公司行动在账户内处理。若一股拆成 $k$ 股，忽略其他价格变化，则 $q'=kq$、$P'=P/k$，持仓价值不变。现金分红则要区分取得分配权益与现金到账：除息时可记录应收股利，支付时转为可用现金；不得把尚未到账且不可融资使用的金额提前用于购买。分配是否再投资，另按 [[持有期收益率]]和 [[价格指数与全收益指数]]的口径说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With raw execution prices, process corporate actions inside the account. A $k$-for-one split gives $q'=kq$ and, absent other price changes, $P'=P/k$, preserving position value. For cash dividends, distinguish entitlement from payment: recognise a receivable when appropriate and transfer it to available cash on payment. Amounts not yet available or financeable cannot fund earlier purchases. State reinvestment consistently with [[持有期收益率|holding-period return]] and [[价格指数与全收益指数|price versus total return]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自拟恒等式例：10 股、每股 100 元、现金 200 元，账户价值为 1200 元。发生 1 拆 2 后，20 股 × 50 元，加现金仍为 1200 元。随后每股分红 1 元，假设没有其他消息、折现或税费，除息价为 49 元，20 元股利暂未支付；此时 $20(49)+200+20=1200$。到账后应收归零、现金变为 220 元，价值仍为 1200 元。这些是条件化核算，不是对真实除息价格的预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A constructed identity starts with ten shares at 100 and cash of 200, worth 1200. A two-for-one split produces twenty shares at 50 with unchanged wealth. A subsequent dividend of 1 per share, assuming no other news, discounting or taxes, leaves a price of 49 and a receivable of 20: $20(49)+200+20=1200$. Payment replaces the receivable with cash of 220. This is conditional accounting, not a prediction of a real ex-dividend price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若使用已经计入分配或再投资的收益序列核算财富，就不能再把同一股利另加一次。配股、分拆、并购和零碎股需按各自权利及结算规则处理；“已复权”三个字不足以证明这些处理一致，也不能代替 [[历史股票池|退市持仓的结局记录]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a return series already includes distributions or reinvestment, do not add the same dividend again. Rights issues, spin-offs, mergers and fractional shares require their own entitlement and settlement treatment. An “adjusted” label alone establishes neither consistency nor [[历史股票池|the outcome of a delisted holding]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
