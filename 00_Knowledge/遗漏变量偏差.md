# Omitted-variable bias
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*遗漏变量偏差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A regression coefficient can change when a variable is left out because the retained variable also carries part of the omitted variable’s association with the outcome. “Bias” here needs a named reference: which coefficient, from which model, are we trying to recover? A shorter regression can estimate its own population relationship correctly while differing from the coefficient in a larger model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

省略一个变量后，保留变量可能同时承载了被省略变量与结果的部分关联，因而回归系数发生改变。这里说“偏差”，必须先说明参照：想恢复哪个模型里的哪个系数？较短的回归可以正确估计它自己的总体关系，却与较大模型的系数不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the linear mean model below. $X$ is the variable whose coefficient we want, $Z$ a second variable and $u$ the unexplained disturbance. $\beta_1$ is the [[多元回归系数|coefficient of $X$ with $Z$ included]]. The [[条件期望|zero conditional mean]] says that $u$ averages to zero at given $X,Z$; it is a population assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑下面的线性均值模型。$X$ 是关注其系数的变量，$Z$ 是另一个变量，$u$ 是未解释的扰动。$\beta_1$ 是[[多元回归系数|同时纳入 $Z$ 时 $X$ 的系数]]。[[条件期望|零条件均值]]表示在给定 $X,Z$ 时，$u$ 的平均值为零；这是总体假设。

$$
Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2Z+u,\qquad E(u\mid X,Z)=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume finite second moments and that $(1,X,Z)$ has a nonsingular population second-moment matrix, so the larger model’s coefficients are uniquely defined. In particular, $\operatorname{Var}(X)>0$. If only an intercept and $X$ are retained, the [[条件均值与线性投影|population best-linear slope]] $\alpha_1$ is $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)/\operatorname{Var}(X)$. Here [[协方差|covariance]] measures joint variation and [[方差|variance]] measures the variation of $X$ itself. Substitute the larger model into that covariance:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假定二阶矩有限，并且 $(1,X,Z)$ 的总体二阶矩矩阵可逆，使较大模型的系数能唯一确定；特别地，$\operatorname{Var}(X)>0$。若只保留截距和 $X$，[[条件均值与线性投影|总体最佳线性斜率]] $\alpha_1$ 等于 $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)/\operatorname{Var}(X)$。其中[[协方差]]衡量共同变动，[[方差]]衡量 $X$ 自身的变动。把较大模型代入协方差可得：

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=\beta_1\operatorname{Var}(X)
+\beta_2\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z),
\qquad
\alpha_1-\beta_1=\beta_2\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)}{\operatorname{Var}(X)}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The disturbance contributes no extra term because $E(u\mid X,Z)=0$ implies $\operatorname{Cov}(X,u)=0$. The ratio multiplying $\beta_2$ is the slope from projecting $Z$ on an intercept and $X$: it tells us how much of the omitted variable’s linear movement accompanies $X$. Multiplying by $\beta_2$ translates that movement into the outcome’s scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

扰动没有额外贡献，是因为 $E(u\mid X,Z)=0$ 推出 $\operatorname{Cov}(X,u)=0$。乘在 $\beta_2$ 上的比值，是把 $Z$ 对截距和 $X$ 作总体线性投影的斜率：它说明 $X$ 的变动伴随了多少遗漏变量的线性变动。再乘 $\beta_2$，就转换成结果的尺度。

> [!example] A slope of five versus a slope of two / 斜率五与斜率二
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X$ and $V$ independently take $-1$ or $1$ with equal probability. Set $Z=X+V$ and $Y=10+2X+3Z$. The four equally likely $(X,Z,Y)$ outcomes are $(-1,-2,2)$, $(-1,0,8)$, $(1,0,12)$ and $(1,2,18)$.
>
> With $Z$ included, the coefficient of $X$ is $2$. With only $X$, average $Y$ is $5$ at $X=-1$ and $15$ at $X=1$, giving slope $(15-5)/2=5$. Indeed, $\operatorname{Var}(X)=\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)=1$, so the difference is $3\times1=3$.
>
> Both statements are meaningful: $E(Y\mid X)=10+5X$ and $E(Y\mid X,Z)=10+2X+3Z$. The short regression has the correct mean for its information set, but its slope does not recover the coefficient holding $Z$ fixed. These constructed equations do not by themselves specify a causal intervention.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 令 $X,V$ 各以相同概率取 $-1$ 或 $1$，且相互独立。设 $Z=X+V$、$Y=10+2X+3Z$。四个等概率的 $(X,Z,Y)$ 结果是 $(-1,-2,2)$、$(-1,0,8)$、$(1,0,12)$、$(1,2,18)$。
>
> 纳入 $Z$ 时，$X$ 的系数为 $2$。只用 $X$ 时，$X=-1$ 对应平均 $Y=5$，$X=1$ 对应平均 $Y=15$，斜率为 $(15-5)/2=5$。实际上，$\operatorname{Var}(X)=\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)=1$，所以差异是 $3\times1=3$。
>
> 两个式子都有意义：$E(Y\mid X)=10+5X$，而 $E(Y\mid X,Z)=10+2X+3Z$。短回归正确表示自身信息集下的均值，但其斜率并未恢复保持 $Z$ 不变的系数。这些构造方程本身还没有规定因果干预。

## Direction, target and limits of the formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 方向、目标与边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For this single omitted term, the difference is nonzero exactly when $\beta_2\ne0$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)\ne0$. Matching signs give an upward difference; opposite signs give a downward difference. If either factor is zero, the slope difference vanishes, though the intercept, unexplained variation or prediction quality may still change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对这个单遗漏项，差异非零恰好要求 $\beta_2\ne0$ 且 $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)\ne0$。两个因子同号则向上，异号则向下；任一因子为零，斜率差异就消失，但截距、未解释变动或预测质量仍可能改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an exact difference between population targets. Under [[独立同分布|i.i.d. sampling]] and the stated finite moments, sample OLS in the short regression converges in probability to $\alpha_1$. A finite sample can have no variation in $X$; define the slope arbitrarily as a fixed finite value on that event, whose probability tends to zero under these assumptions. It is then inconsistent for $\beta_1$ when the difference is nonzero. The formula is not automatically the finite-sample expectation $E(\hat\alpha_1)-\beta_1$: a random ratio needs separate integrability and conditional-mean arguments. Sample OLS residual orthogonality does not remove the target difference; see [[样本正交与总体外生性|sample orthogonality versus exogeneity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是两个总体目标之间的精确差异。在[[独立同分布|i.i.d. 抽样]]及上述有限矩条件下，短回归的样本 OLS 斜率依概率收敛到 $\alpha_1$；有限样本中 $X$ 可能恰好完全相同；可在该事件上把斜率约定为任一固定有限值，上述假设保证该事件的概率趋于零。差异非零时，它对 $\beta_1$ 不一致。该式并不自动等于有限样本期望 $E(\hat\alpha_1)-\beta_1$：随机比值的期望还需要另外的可积性和条件均值论证。样本残差正交不会消除目标差异，见[[样本正交与总体外生性]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When other controls remain, the relevant association is after accounting for those controls, using the projection logic of [[FWL残差化定理|FWL residualization]], rather than the raw correlation of two variables. With several omitted terms, their contributions can cancel even when individual coefficients and associations are nonzero. Squared or interaction terms count as omitted columns too. The single-term sign rule must not be applied blindly to their combined effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仍保留其他控制变量时，需要看剔除这些控制项之后的关联，使用 [[FWL残差化定理|FWL 残差化]]的投影逻辑，而不是两个变量的原始相关。遗漏多个项时，即使各项系数及关联都非零，贡献也可能相互抵消。平方项、交互项也属于模型列；不能把单项的符号规则直接套给它们的合计影响。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether to add a variable still depends on [[回归设定起点|the intended comparison]]. A nonzero projection difference alone does not prove confounding or identify a causal effect. For prediction, evaluate the available information and performance on new cases; a population coefficient comparison does not settle finite-sample predictive performance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

是否应该加入一个变量，仍取决于[[回归设定起点|预定比较]]。投影系数存在非零差异，本身不能证明混杂或识别因果效应。预测则应评价可用信息与新观测表现；总体系数比较不能单独决定有限样本预测表现。
