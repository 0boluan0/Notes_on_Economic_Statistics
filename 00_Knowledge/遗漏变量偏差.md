# 遗漏变量同时影响结果并与已含解释变量相关时会污染目标系数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An omitted variable contaminates a target coefficient when it affects the outcome and is related to an included regressor*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子命题
> 若真实模型是
> $$
> Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2Z+u,\qquad E(u\mid X,Z)=0,
> $$
> 却只用截距和 $X$ 逼近 $Y$，且二阶矩存在、$\operatorname{Var}(X)>0$，则遗漏 $Z$ 后的一元总体投影斜率是
> $$
> \tilde\beta_1^*
> =\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)}{\operatorname{Var}(X)}
> =\beta_1+\beta_2\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)}{\operatorname{Var}(X)}.
> $$
> 在随机抽样与大数定律所需条件下，样本 OLS 斜率 $\hat{\tilde\beta}_1$ 的概率极限等于这个总体目标。因而在该简化情形中，只有 $\beta_2\neq0$ 且 $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)\neq0$ 同时成立时，这一偏差项才非零。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the true model contains $X$ and $Z$ but $Y$ is projected only on an intercept and $X$, then, with finite second moments and $\operatorname{Var}(X)>0$, the population simple-regression slope is $\tilde\beta_1^*=\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)/\operatorname{Var}(X)=\beta_1+\beta_2\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)/\operatorname{Var}(X)$. Under random sampling and the conditions needed for a law of large numbers, the sample OLS slope converges in probability to this population target. The bias term is nonzero only when both $\beta_2\neq0$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)\neq0$ in this simplified setting.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 方向、目标与边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Direction, target, and boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

偏差方向由 $\beta_2$ 与 $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)$ 的符号共同决定。两个符号相同，$\tilde\beta_1^*$ 相对 $\beta_1$ 向上；符号相反则向下。只说“遗漏重要变量会向上偏”是错误的。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sign is determined jointly by $\beta_2$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Z)$. Matching signs imply upward bias relative to $\beta_1$; opposite signs imply downward bias. Omission does not generically imply upward bias.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条公式针对一个线性、单遗漏项的比较。多变量情形要看遗漏项在所有已含变量上的线性投影；遗漏非线性项或交互项也可用同一投影逻辑理解。因果问题中的控制变量应依据混杂路径和时间顺序选择，而不是因为某变量能提高样本内拟合就自动加入。预测问题则主要用样本外损失衡量遗漏后果，不能把所有预测误差都称为因果偏差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed formula concerns a linear comparison with one omitted term. With multiple regressors, the relevant object is the omitted term's projection on all included regressors; omitted nonlinear or interaction terms follow the same projection logic. Causal controls should follow confounding structure and temporal order rather than in-sample fit. For prediction, omission is evaluated primarily through out-of-sample loss and should not automatically be called causal bias.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 若遗漏变量影响 $Y$，但在总体中与已含 $X$ 不相关，它是否通过上式污染 $X$ 的斜率？
>
> **答案：** 不会产生上式中的斜率偏差项；但误差方差、预测精度或其他系数仍可能受到影响。
