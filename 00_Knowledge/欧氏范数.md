# Euclidean norm
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*欧氏范数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **Euclidean norm** is the ordinary straight-line length of a real coordinate vector. It turns a list of coordinates into a nonnegative measure of size. In the plane, $x=(3,4)$ means moving three units horizontally and four units vertically. The right triangle has hypotenuse length $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$, so $\|x\|_2=5$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**欧氏范数**就是实坐标向量的普通直线长度：把一组坐标转成一个非负的大小。在平面中，$x=(3,4)$ 表示横向移动三单位、纵向移动四单位；直角三角形的斜边长为 $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5$，所以 $\|x\|_2=5$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Square, add, then take a square root
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先平方、相加，再开平方*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ in the real [[向量空间|coordinate vector space]] $\mathbb R^n$, with $n$ a positive integer, define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实[[向量空间|坐标向量空间]] $\mathbb R^n$ 中的 $x=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$，其中 $n$ 为正整数，定义

$$
\|x\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n x_i^2}=\sqrt{x_1^2+\cdots+x_n^2}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The index $i$ runs through every coordinate. Squaring prevents positive and negative coordinates from cancelling; the nonnegative square root returns the result to the original length scale. The subscript $2$ names this sum-of-squares rule, rather than saying that the vector has two coordinates. For instance, $\|(1,2,2)\|_2=3$ in three dimensions. In one dimension, the rule becomes $\|(a)\|_2=|a|$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下标 $i$ 遍历所有坐标。平方避免正负坐标抵消，非负平方根把结果还原到原来的长度尺度。下标 $2$ 表示这条平方求和规则，并不是说向量只有两个坐标。例如三维向量满足 $\|(1,2,2)\|_2=3$；一维时则变成 $\|(a)\|_2=|a|$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[向量点积|dot product]] adds products of matching coordinates. Taking both vectors to be $x$ gives $x^Tx=\sum_i x_i^2$, so $\|x\|_2=\sqrt{x^Tx}$. The squared norm $\|x\|_2^2$ is $25$ for $(3,4)$, while the norm is $5$. Squared length and length are different quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[向量点积|点积]]把对应坐标的乘积相加。两个向量都取 $x$ 时，$x^Tx=\sum_i x_i^2$，所以 $\|x\|_2=\sqrt{x^Tx}$。对 $(3,4)$，范数平方 $\|x\|_2^2$ 为 $25$，范数则为 $5$；长度平方与长度是不同的量。

> [!important] Zero and scaling · 零向量与缩放
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The norm is always nonnegative, and it is zero exactly when every coordinate is zero. For any real scalar $c$, $\|cx\|_2=|c|\|x\|_2$, because each squared coordinate gains a factor $c^2$. A negative multiplier reverses the arrow, but length does not become negative. Thus $\|(-6,-8)\|_2=10=|-2|\|(3,4)\|_2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 范数总是非负，且仅在每个坐标都为零时等于零。对任意实标量 $c$，有 $\|cx\|_2=|c|\|x\|_2$，因为每个坐标平方都会多出因子 $c^2$。负倍数会翻转箭头方向，但长度不会变成负数。因此 $\|(-6,-8)\|_2=10=|-2|\|(3,4)\|_2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep a direction, or measure a displacement
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*保留方向，或测量位移*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $x\ne0$, dividing each coordinate by the vector’s length gives a **unit vector** $q=x/\|x\|_2$. A unit vector means one with norm $1$. For $x=(3,4)$, $q=(3/5,4/5)$ and $\|q\|_2=1$. Division by a positive length leaves the direction unchanged. The zero vector cannot be normalized this way, because division by its zero length is undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $x\ne0$，把每个坐标都除以长度，得到**单位向量** $q=x/\|x\|_2$；单位向量就是范数为 $1$ 的向量。对 $x=(3,4)$，有 $q=(3/5,4/5)$，且 $\|q\|_2=1$。除以正的长度保持方向不变。零向量不能这样归一化，因为不能除以它的零长度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A norm measures one vector from the origin. To measure the distance between points $a$ and $b$, first subtract their coordinates to obtain the displacement $b-a$, then take its norm: $d(a,b)=\|b-a\|_2$. For $a=(1,2)$ and $b=(4,6)$, the displacement is $(3,4)$ and the distance is $5$. The [[线性最小二乘|least-squares problem]] similarly measures a prediction error through the norm of the residual $Ax-b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

范数测量的是单个向量相对于原点的长度。若要测量点 $a$ 与 $b$ 的距离，先逐坐标相减得到位移 $b-a$，再取范数：$d(a,b)=\|b-a\|_2$。例如 $a=(1,2)$、$b=(4,6)$ 的位移为 $(3,4)$，距离为 $5$。[[线性最小二乘]]也用残差 $Ax-b$ 的范数来测量预测误差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Adding vectors does not usually add their lengths
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*向量相加通常不是长度相加*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The norm satisfies the triangle inequality $\|x+y\|_2\le\|x\|_2+\|y\|_2$: travelling directly along the combined displacement is no longer than travelling along the two displacements in turn. For $x=(3,0)$ and $y=(0,4)$, the direct length is $5$, while the two-leg length is $3+4=7$. The inequality can also be strict because opposite displacements cancel.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

范数满足三角不等式 $\|x+y\|_2\le\|x\|_2+\|y\|_2$：直接走合成位移，不会比依次走两个位移更长。例如 $x=(3,0)$、$y=(0,4)$ 时，直线路程为 $5$，分两段走为 $3+4=7$。相反方向的位移会抵消，也可能导致严格不等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Algebraically, expand the squared norm and use [[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式|Cauchy–Schwarz]] to bound the dot product:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

代数上，展开范数平方，再用[[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式]]控制点积：

$$
\|x+y\|_2^2=\|x\|_2^2+2x^Ty+\|y\|_2^2
\le\|x\|_2^2+2\|x\|_2\|y\|_2+\|y\|_2^2
=(\|x\|_2+\|y\|_2)^2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking nonnegative square roots proves the rule. Together with positivity and absolute scaling, this is what makes the formula a norm. If the vectors are [[正交|orthogonal]], meaning their dot product is zero, their **squared** lengths add instead, as in the [[正交向量勾股关系|Pythagorean identity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对两边取非负平方根，就得到该规则。它与非负性、绝对缩放性质一起，使这条公式成为一种范数。若向量[[正交]]，即点积为零，则相加的是长度的**平方**，见[[正交向量勾股关系]]。

> [!warning] Check what the symbol measures · 先看符号在测量什么
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is the standard Euclidean norm of a real vector in perpendicular unit coordinates. Changing the weights or the coordinate scales changes the geometry being measured. A matrix symbol $\|A\|_2$ usually means the [[谱范数|spectral norm]], the largest vector-length stretch; taking the square root of the sum of squared magnitudes of matrix entries gives the [[Frobenius范数|Frobenius norm]] instead. For complex vectors, lengths use $\sqrt{\sum_i|z_i|^2}$; plain squares $z_i^2$ need not be nonnegative.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 本卡定义的是实向量在垂直单位坐标下的标准欧氏范数。改变权重或坐标尺度，会改变所测量的几何。矩阵记号 $\|A\|_2$ 通常指[[谱范数]]，即最大的向量长度伸缩；对矩阵元素的模平方求和再开平方，则对应[[Frobenius范数]]。复向量的长度使用 $\sqrt{\sum_i|z_i|^2}$，普通平方 $z_i^2$ 未必非负。
