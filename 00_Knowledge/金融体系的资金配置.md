# 金融体系把盈余资金者与赤字资金者连接并决定资金配置方式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The financial system links saver-lenders with borrower-spenders and shapes how funds are allocated*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子概念
> 有些经济主体当期收入超过支出，因而能够提供资金；另一些主体希望在收入到来前消费或投资，因而需要融资。金融体系通过市场、金融中介和支付清算设施把两边连接起来，并同时决定资金的价格、期限、风险由谁承担，以及谁能够获得融资。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Some economic units currently receive more income than they spend and can supply funds; others want to consume or invest before their own income arrives and therefore seek financing. Markets, intermediaries, and payment and settlement arrangements connect the two sides. In doing so, the financial system also shapes the price and maturity of funding, who bears its risks, and who can obtain it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“储蓄者”和“借款者”不是永久身份。家庭通常是净储蓄者，却也会借款买房；企业通常为投资融资，却也会持有现金或购买证券；政府和外国部门也可能在不同时期站在任一边。真正稳定的区分是某一时期内谁提供可用资金、谁使用这些资金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Saver” and “borrower” are not permanent identities. Households often save in net terms but also borrow for housing; firms often finance investment but can also hold cash or buy securities; governments and foreign sectors can appear on either side. The stable distinction is who supplies and who uses funds during the period being analysed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条连接有经济价值，是因为它让有正净现值但缺乏自有现金的项目不必等到资金自然积累后才启动，也让储蓄者能够持有与自己需要更匹配的金融索取权。不过，“资金流向了投资”并不自动等于配置有效：交易成本、信息不对称、市场势力和系统性风险仍可能造成错配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The connection is valuable because positive-net-present-value projects need not wait until their owners accumulate enough cash, while savers can hold claims better suited to their own needs. But channelling funds toward investment does not by itself guarantee efficient allocation: transaction costs, asymmetric information, market power, and systemic risk can still produce misallocation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 具体锚点

一名家庭储户可以购买企业新发行的债券，也可以把资金放入银行并持有银行存款。两种安排都把家庭的购买力连接到融资需求，却产生不同的合同、信息成本、流动性和风险承担方式；这正是区分直接与间接融资的起点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A household may buy a newly issued corporate bond or hold a bank deposit while the bank extends credit. Both arrangements connect household purchasing power to a financing need, but they create different contracts, information costs, liquidity, and risk allocation. That is the starting point for distinguishing direct from indirect finance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么不能把“家庭”固定等同于资金提供者、把“企业”固定等同于资金使用者？
>
> **答案：** 这两个角色按观察期和具体交易定义。家庭会为住房借款，企业也会积累资金并购买资产；应看当期资金净流向，而不是主体名称。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can households not be permanently equated with fund suppliers and firms with fund users?
>
> **Answer:** The roles are defined by the period and transaction. Households borrow for housing, while firms can accumulate funds and acquire financial assets. Follow the net flow of funds rather than the sector label.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
