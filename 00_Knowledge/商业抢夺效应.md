# 商业抢夺效应是企业创新或进入时未计入竞争者既有利润损失的激励外部影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The business-stealing effect is an incentive externality arising when an innovator or entrant ignores the loss of competitors' existing profits.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

企业创新或进入一个市场时，会考虑自己能够取得的利润，却不一定把竞争者失去的既有利润计入决策。在 Aghion–Howitt 的 [[创造性破坏|质量替代]] 模型中，外部创新者成功后取得新垄断利润，旧垄断者的利润归零；这一被私人研究决策遗漏的损失，就是本模型中的商业抢夺效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A firm considers the profits it can obtain from innovation or entry without necessarily accounting for competitors' lost profits. In Aghion–Howitt's [[创造性破坏|quality-replacement model]], an outside innovator gains the new monopoly and the incumbent loses its profit stream. The omitted incumbent loss is the model's business-stealing effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，新企业拿到利润机会 $12$，同时旧企业失去原来价值 $8$ 的利润机会。若这些金额的时期与估值口径一致，生产者利润机会合计增加的是 $12-8=4$，不能把新企业的 $12$ 全部称为新增社会价值。这仍不是完整社会净收益：消费者的变化、研发资源占用及跨期效应也要纳入，见 [[财务与社会评价]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the new firm gains a profit opportunity worth $12$ while the old firm loses one worth $8$, measured on the same timing and valuation basis. Aggregate producer value rises by $4$, not $12$. This is still not total social net benefit: consumer effects, research resources and intertemporal consequences also matter; see [[财务与社会评价|financial versus social appraisal]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

旧企业的租金损失是所有者的实际损失，但不等于同额机器、劳动或知识从社会中消失。另一方面，新旧租金变化也未必是一对一的纯转移，因为质量、价格和数量可同时改变。核算要先定同一 [[公共项目反事实|有无方案的比较基准]]，再比较完整变化；不能把已进入产出或剩余变化的旧租金损失额外扣第二遍。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The incumbent's lost rent is a real loss to its owner, but not an equal destruction of machines, labour or knowledge. Nor must old and new rents be a one-for-one pure transfer: quality, prices and quantities may change. Establish a common [[公共项目反事实|counterfactual]] and compare complete changes, without subtracting an incumbent loss again when already included in output or surplus accounting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

商业抢夺能使争取新利润的私人激励超过增量社会收益，进而诱使过多真实劳动从制造转入研发。不过 [[创新的跨期知识外溢]] 等渠道可能抵消它，因此存在该效应不等于已经证明研发过多。[[金钱外部性]] 中的完整竞争效率基准也不能直接套用这里的垄断创新经济。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Business stealing can make private incentives exceed incremental social returns and draw excessive real labour from manufacturing into research. [[创新的跨期知识外溢|Intertemporal spillovers]] can offset this, so identifying the effect does not establish excessive total research. The complete-competition benchmark for [[金钱外部性|pecuniary externalities]] does not directly apply to this monopoly-based innovation economy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原文这里的受损者是旧垄断者。其他研发企业在自由进入下的预期净价值为零，因此这里不是争夺研发参赛者既有正剩余的机制；模型也没有研究技术带记忆时的策略性抢跑外部性。具体福利方向由 [[私人与社会研发]] 的共同模型决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The harmed party here is the incumbent monopolist. Other researchers have zero expected net value under free entry, so the effect does not concern their losing an existing positive research surplus. The model also omits strategic pre-emption under research technology with memory. [[私人与社会研发|The common welfare model]] determines the overall direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
