# Why sample covariance uses n minus one
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*样本协方差的无偏分母*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When we estimate the center from the same sample, the observations look less spread out around that fitted center than around the true population center. Dividing their squared deviations by the sample size therefore underestimates population variance on average. The divisor **$n-1$** corrects this specific effect; it is not a universal cure for dependent or differently sampled data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用同一份样本估计中心后，观测围绕这个拟合中心，看起来会比围绕真实总体中心更集中。因此把偏差平方和除以样本量，会在平均意义上低估总体方差。分母 **$n-1$** 修正的正是这个效应；它不能普遍解决相关观测或其他抽样方式带来的问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X_1,\ldots,X_n\in\mathbb R^p$, $n\ge2$, be [[独立同分布|independent observations from one distribution]], with finite second moments, mean $\mu$, and [[协方差矩阵|covariance]] $\Sigma$. Their [[样本均值向量|sample mean]] is $\bar X$. Write the centered outer-product sum as $C=\sum_i(X_i-\bar X)(X_i-\bar X)^T$. The [[样本协方差矩阵|sample covariance]] in this vault is $S=C/(n-1)$. An estimator is unbiased when its expectation under repeated sampling equals the population quantity; it need not equal that quantity in one realized sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X_1,\ldots,X_n\in\mathbb R^p$，$n\ge2$，是[[独立同分布|来自同一分布的独立观测]]，各坐标二阶矩有限，均值为 $\mu$、[[协方差矩阵]]为 $\Sigma$。[[样本均值向量]]为 $\bar X$，记中心化外积和 $C=\sum_i(X_i-\bar X)(X_i-\bar X)^T$，本库的[[样本协方差矩阵]]为 $S=C/(n-1)$。无偏是指重复抽样下的期望等于总体目标，不是说某次计算恰好等于总体值。

> [!important] The expectation of the centered sum · 中心化总和的期望
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under these assumptions,
> $$E[C]=(n-1)\Sigma,\qquad E[S]=\Sigma,\qquad
> E[C/n]=\frac{n-1}{n}\Sigma.$$
> No Gaussian assumption is needed. “Downward” means smaller variance in every fixed linear direction: the expected matrix difference is $\Sigma/n$, which is PSD. A negative off-diagonal covariance becomes less negative under this shrinkage; it is not numerically smaller.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在上述条件下，中心化外积和的期望为 $(n-1)\Sigma$，因此除以 $n-1$ 无偏，除以 $n$ 则得到上式的缩小因子。无需 Gaussian 假设。“向下”是指每个固定线性方向的方差被缩小，期望矩阵之差 $\Sigma/n$ 半正定；负的非对角协方差会变得更接近零，并不是数值更小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Where the missing term goes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## $n-1$ 从哪里来

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Put $\delta=\bar X-\mu$. Then $X_i-\mu=(X_i-\bar X)+\delta$. Expanding the outer products and summing makes both cross terms vanish because $\sum_i(X_i-\bar X)=0$. Rearranging gives the exact identity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $\delta=\bar X-\mu$，则 $X_i-\mu=(X_i-\bar X)+\delta$。展开外积并求和时，因为 $\sum_i(X_i-\bar X)=0$，两个交叉项都消失；移项得到精确恒等式

$$C=\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\mu)(X_i-\mu)^T-n(\bar X-\mu)(\bar X-\mu)^T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first sum measures spread around the true center and has expectation $n\Sigma$. The [[样本均值无偏性|mean of $\bar X$ is $\mu$]], and its [[样本均值协方差|covariance is $\Sigma/n$]] under the stated sampling assumptions. Consequently
$$E[n(\bar X-\mu)(\bar X-\mu)^T]=n\,\operatorname{Cov}(\bar X)=\Sigma.$$
Subtracting removes one copy of $\Sigma$ from $n$ copies. This is the full calculation behind the phrase “one degree of freedom is used to estimate the mean.” All matrix expectations are taken entry by entry; finite second moments ensure the products are integrable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一项围绕真实中心计算，期望为 $n\Sigma$。[[样本均值无偏性]]给出 $E[\bar X]=\mu$，上述抽样条件下的[[样本均值协方差]]为 $\Sigma/n$，因此第二项期望恰好为 $\Sigma$。从 $n$ 份中扣除一份，便得到 $(n-1)\Sigma$。这才是“估计均值用去一个自由度”背后的计算。矩阵期望逐元素取，有限二阶矩保证这些乘积可积。

> [!example] Two fair signs · 两个公平随机符号
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $n=2$, $p=1$, with independent observations taking $\pm1$ equally likely. The population variance is $1$. The four equally likely samples are $(1,1),(1,-1),(-1,1),(-1,-1)$. Their centered sums $C$ are $0,2,2,0$. Dividing by $n-1=1$ gives average $1$; dividing by $n=2$ gives average $1/2$. Individual estimates can be zero or two even though the first procedure is unbiased.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取两个独立公平的 $\pm1$ 观测，总体方差为 $1$。四种等概率样本的中心化平方和依次为 $0,2,2,0$。除以 $n-1=1$ 后平均为 $1$；除以 $n=2$ 后平均只有 $1/2$。可见无偏程序在某一次仍可能估出零或二。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What changes when the assumptions change
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*条件改变时，分母的理由也要重查*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the true $\mu$ is known and used for centering, $n^{-1}\sum_i(X_i-\mu)(X_i-\mu)^T$ is already unbiased. If observations share a common mean and covariance but are dependent, the same identity gives $E[C]=n\Sigma-n\operatorname{Cov}(\bar X)$. The result $E[C]=(n-1)\Sigma$ therefore needs the appropriate covariance of the sample mean. Pairwise zero cross-covariance suffices here; full independence is a convenient stronger condition, not logically necessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若真实 $\mu$ 已知并用于中心化，$n^{-1}\sum_i(X_i-\mu)(X_i-\mu)^T$ 本身就无偏。若观测具有共同均值、共同协方差但彼此依赖，同一恒等式给出 $E[C]=n\Sigma-n\operatorname{Cov}(\bar X)$。因此 $(n-1)\Sigma$ 的结论需要相应的样本均值协方差；观测之间两两交叉协方差为零已经足够，完全独立是方便但更强的充分条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an extreme counterexample, copy one random vector with finite, nonzero covariance into every row: $X_1=\cdots=X_n=Z$. Every centered deviation is zero, so $S=0$ despite nonzero population covariance. Changing the divisor cannot restore the missing independent information. In a Gaussian likelihood the $n$ divisor has a different optimization role, with its own existence conditions; see [[Gaussian协方差MLE边界|the Gaussian covariance MLE boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极端反例是把同一个具有有限且非零协方差的随机向量复制到所有行：$X_1=\cdots=X_n=Z$。全部中心化偏差都为零，因此总体协方差非零而 $S=0$；改变分母无法补回缺失的独立信息。Gaussian 似然中的 $n$ 分母承担另一种优化角色，并有存在条件，见[[Gaussian协方差MLE边界]]。
