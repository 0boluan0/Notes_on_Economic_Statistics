# 立体体积等于垂直于同一坐标轴的截面积沿该轴的积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A solid's volume is the integral of its cross-sectional area along the slicing axis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对沿 $x$ 轴从 $a$ 延伸到 $b$ 的有界立体，假设边界分段光滑，令 $A(x)$ 表示平面 $x=\text{常数}$ 截得的完整截面积。若 $a<b$ 且 $A$ 可积，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a bounded solid with a piecewise smooth boundary extending from $x=a$ to $x=b$, let $A(x)$ be its complete cross-sectional area in the plane of constant $x$. If $a<b$ and $A$ is integrable, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
V=\int_a^b A(x)\,dx.
$$

厚度为 $\Delta x_i$ 的薄片体积约为 $A(x_i^*)\Delta x_i$。薄片互不重叠并覆盖整个立体，分割变细后的和给出上式。$dx$ 是垂直于截面的厚度，所以 $A$ 的单位是面积，$A\,dx$ 的单位是体积。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A slab of thickness $\Delta x_i$ has approximate volume $A(x_i^*)\Delta x_i$. Nonoverlapping slabs cover the solid, and their sums converge as the partition is refined. The thickness is perpendicular to the cross section, so area times thickness has units of volume.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键是先由几何形状求 $A(x)$：截面可以是圆、三角形或几个不相连部分的面积之和，立体本身不必由旋转产生。截面公式随位置改变时应分段；若用水平截面，则所有边界和面积函数都改写为 $y$ 的函数并使用 $dy$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Determine $A(x)$ from the geometry first. Cross sections may be circles, triangles, or unions of disjoint pieces; the solid need not arise from rotation. Split the integral where the area formula changes. With horizontal sections, express the bounds and area in terms of $y$ and use $dy$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半径为 $R>0$、中心在原点的球在位置 $x$ 的截面半径为 $\sqrt{R^2-x^2}$，因此
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sphere of radius $R>0$ centred at the origin has cross-sectional radius $\sqrt{R^2-x^2}$ at position $x$. Hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
V=\int_{-R}^{R}\pi(R^2-x^2)\,dx=\frac43\pi R^3.
$$
