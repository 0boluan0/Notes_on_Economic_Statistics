# Volume by slicing
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*切片体积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine cutting a loaf of bread into thin slices. The broad face of a slice has an area; multiplying that area by the slice's thickness estimates its volume. **Volume by slicing** adds these small volumes across the whole solid. Unlike [[曲线间面积|area between curves]], where a strip contributes length times width, a solid requires **area times thickness**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

想象把一条面包切成薄片。每片宽大的切面有一个面积；把面积乘以薄片厚度，就能估计这片的体积。**切片体积**沿整个立体累加这些小体积。[[曲线间面积]]累加的是长度乘宽度，立体则需要**面积乘厚度**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Name the cross section before integrating
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先说明截面，再写积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose the $x$-axis as the slicing direction. The plane $x=\text{constant}$ is perpendicular to that axis. Its intersection with the solid is a **cross section**, which may be a disk, a triangle, a square, or several separated pieces. Let $A(x)$ be the area of the complete cross section, including every piece but excluding holes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选 $x$ 轴为切片方向。平面 $x=\text{常数}$ 与该轴垂直，它与立体相交得到一个**截面**，可能是圆盘、三角形、正方形，也可能包含几个分开的部分。令 $A(x)$ 表示完整截面的面积：包括所有实有部分，但不包括孔洞。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work here with a bounded geometric solid whose boundary is piecewise smooth, over a finite interval $a\le x\le b$ with $a<b$. These are ordinary measurable solids, rather than arbitrary sets of points. Assume their nonnegative cross-sectional area $A$ is Riemann integrable on $[a,b]$; continuous or bounded piecewise continuous area functions are standard cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里处理有限区间 $a\le x\le b$（$a<b$）内边界分段光滑的有界几何立体；这是通常能够测量体积的规则立体，而不是任意点集。假设它的非负截面积 $A$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上黎曼可积；连续或有界分段连续的面积函数是常见情形。

> [!important] The cross section supplies the integrand · 截面给出被积函数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The volume $V$ is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 体积 $V$ 为
>
> $$V=\int_a^b A(x)\,dx.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The bounds locate the first and last slices along the chosen axis. The symbol $dx$ represents thickness in that direction; it is perpendicular to the cross-sectional plane.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上下限沿选定的轴定位起止切片。$dx$ 表示沿该方向的厚度，它与截面平面垂直。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the accumulation, split the interval into pieces of positive widths $\Delta x_i$. Choose $x_i^*$ inside each piece and replace its thin slab by a cylinder with face area $A(x_i^*)$ and thickness $\Delta x_i$. Here a cylinder means a fixed planar shape carried straight through a thickness; its face need not be circular. This gives the approximation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了看清累加过程，把区间分成宽为正数 $\Delta x_i$ 的小段，每段选一个位置 $x_i^*$，用底面积 $A(x_i^*)$、厚度 $\Delta x_i$ 的柱体近似对应薄片。这里的柱体表示把一个固定平面形状沿垂直方向延伸一段厚度，底面不一定是圆。于是有近似

$$V\approx\sum_i A(x_i^*)\Delta x_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Parallel slabs cover the solid without overlapping interiors. For the regular solids above, their geometric volume is the limit of the slice estimates; the [[黎曼和|Riemann sums]] converge to the [[定积分|definite integral]] as the largest thickness tends to zero. A finite thickness gives an approximation, not an exact equality unless the geometry makes it exact. If $A$ is in square metres and thickness is in metres, every term is in cubic metres.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平行薄片覆盖整个立体，内部彼此不重叠。对上述规则立体，几何体积由切片估计的极限给出；当最大厚度趋于零时，这些[[黎曼和]]趋于[[定积分]]。有限厚度给的是近似，除非特定形状使它恰好精确，否则不能直接当作等式。截面积若以平方米计，厚度以米计，每一项就是立方米。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A sphere: find the radius inside each slice
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*球体：先找每个截面的半径*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the solid ball of radius $R>0$ centred at the origin. Its points satisfy $x^2+y^2+z^2\le R^2$. Holding $x$ fixed leaves a disk $y^2+z^2\le R^2-x^2$, which exists for $-R\le x\le R$. Its radius is $r(x)=\sqrt{R^2-x^2}$, so its area is $\pi r(x)^2=\pi(R^2-x^2)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取以原点为中心、半径 $R>0$ 的实心球，内部点满足 $x^2+y^2+z^2\le R^2$。固定 $x$ 后，截面是圆盘 $y^2+z^2\le R^2-x^2$，只在 $-R\le x\le R$ 时存在。它的半径为 $r(x)=\sqrt{R^2-x^2}$，所以面积为 $\pi r(x)^2=\pi(R^2-x^2)$。

> [!example] Integrate the disk area, not its radius · 积分圆盘面积，不是半径
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Applying the [[Newton-Leibniz公式|antiderivative formula]] gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用[[Newton-Leibniz公式]]计算：
>
> $$V=\int_{-R}^{R}\pi(R^2-x^2)\,dx
> =\pi\left[R^2x-\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_{-R}^{R}
> =\frac43\pi R^3.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The central slice has area $\pi R^2$; the end slices have area zero. Using the largest area throughout would give a surrounding cylinder of volume $2\pi R^3$, not the ball's volume. Integrating the radius alone would have units of area, not volume.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 中间截面积为 $\pi R^2$，两端截面积为零。全程使用最大截面积会得到包围球体的圆柱体积 $2\pi R^3$，不是球体积。只积分半径，单位会是面积，也不是体积。

## Slicing does not require rotation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*切片不要求由旋转生成立体*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a noncircular example, use metre-based coordinates and consider the solid with $0\le x\le2$, $0\le y\le x$ and $0\le z\le x$. At position $x$, the cross section is a square of side $x$, so $A(x)=x^2$. Thus $V=\int_0^2x^2\,dx=8/3$ cubic metres. The side length is not yet the integrand: it must first be converted into the square's area.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非圆截面的例子：采用米制坐标，考虑 $0\le x\le2$、$0\le y\le x$、$0\le z\le x$ 的立体。在位置 $x$，截面是边长为 $x$ 的正方形，所以 $A(x)=x^2$，体积为 $V=\int_0^2x^2\,dx=8/3$ 立方米。边长本身还不是被积函数，要先把它换算成正方形面积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the cross section has several disjoint components, add their areas. If it has a hole, subtract the missing area. The [[圆盘垫圈法|disk and washer methods]] are special cases where rotation supplies the cross-sectional geometry. When a formula changes at a particular position, split there using [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若截面包含几个不相交部分，就把它们的面积相加；若存在孔洞，就扣除缺失面积。[[圆盘垫圈法]]是旋转关系给出截面形状的特殊情形。当截面积公式在某个位置改变时，用[[定积分区间可加性]]在那里分段。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choosing horizontal slices is equally valid, but then define the complete area at height $y$, use $dy$ for thickness, and set bounds in $y$. Merely changing $dx$ to $dy$ while retaining the old area formula generally describes a different calculation. Before integrating, identify one actual cross section, its area, the perpendicular thickness, and the interval covering the whole solid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择水平切片同样可行，但要重新定义高度 $y$ 处的完整截面积，用 $dy$ 作厚度，并采用 $y$ 的上下限。只把 $dx$ 换成 $dy$、却保留旧面积公式，通常已经改变了计算对象。积分之前，先确定一个实际截面、它的面积、与它垂直的厚度，以及覆盖整个立体的区间。
