# 有向图的强连通要求互相可达而弱连通会忽略边方向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strong connectivity requires mutual reachability; weak connectivity ignores direction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 在 digraph 中：
> - 顶点 $u,v$ **强连通（strongly connected）**，当且仅当 $u$ 可达 $v$ 且 $v$ 可达 $u$；互相可达的等价类是 strongly connected components（SCCs）。
> - 图 **弱连通（weakly connected）**，是指把每条有向边改成无向边后，所得 underlying undirected graph connected。
> 弱连通只说明忽略方向后没有分裂，不能提供任何往返路径保证。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a digraph:
> - Vertices $u$ and $v$ are **strongly connected** exactly when each is reachable from the other. Mutual-reachability equivalence classes are strongly connected components (SCCs).
> - A digraph is **weakly connected** when replacing every directed edge by an undirected edge produces a connected underlying graph.
> Weak connectivity says only that the graph does not split after direction is forgotten; it guarantees no directed round trip.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如链 $a\to b\to c$ weakly connected，因为忽略箭头后是 $a-b-c$；但没有任何两个不同顶点互相可达，所以三个 SCC 都是单点。相反，若再加 $c\to a$，三个顶点两两互相可达，整张图成为一个 SCC。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The chain $a\to b\to c$ is weakly connected because its underlying graph is $a-b-c$, but no two distinct vertices are mutually reachable, so it has three singleton SCCs. Adding $c\to a$ makes all three vertices mutually reachable and turns the whole graph into one SCC.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 算法边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Algorithmic boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从根 $s$ 做一次 BFS 或 DFS，只得到 $s$ 能到达的集合。即使它覆盖了全部顶点，也只证明 $s$ 能到所有点；还需反向可达信息才能证明所有点能回到 $s$。SCC decomposition 因而需要专门利用反图、finishing order 或 low-link 结构的算法，不能把一棵 directed search tree 当作 SCC partition。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One BFS or DFS from a root $s$ returns the vertices reachable from $s$. Even if it visits every vertex, it proves only that $s$ reaches them; it does not prove that they return to $s$. SCC decomposition therefore needs an algorithm using the transpose graph, finishing order, or low-link structure. A directed search tree is not an SCC partition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 一次 DFS 从 $s$ 访问了 digraph 的全部顶点。能否宣布图 strongly connected？最少还缺哪一方向的证据？
>
> **答案：**不能。已知的是 $s\leadsto v$ 对所有 $v$ 成立；还缺 $v\leadsto s$ 对所有 $v$ 成立。可在反图上从 $s$ 再做一次 reachability search，或运行完整 SCC 算法。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A DFS from $s$ visits every vertex of a digraph. May we declare the graph strongly connected? What direction of evidence is still missing?
>
> **Answer:** No. We know $s\leadsto v$ for every $v$, but still need $v\leadsto s$ for every $v$. One may search from $s$ in the transpose graph or run a full SCC algorithm.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
