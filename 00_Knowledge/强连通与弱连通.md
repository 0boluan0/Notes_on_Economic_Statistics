# Strong and weak connectivity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*强连通与弱连通*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One-way roads give two different meanings to “connected.” Can we travel between places while obeying every arrow, including a route back? Or do the roads form one piece when their arrows are ignored? **Strong connectivity** requires travel in both directions. **Weak connectivity** asks only whether forgetting directions leaves an undirected connection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单行道让“连通”有了两种不同含义：能否遵守箭头在地点之间往返？还是说，忽略箭头后，这些道路在图上连成一块？**强连通**要求两个方向都能到达；**弱连通**只问丢掉方向后是否还有无向连接。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take a finite [[图类型的建模边界|directed graph]] $G=(V,E)$, with vertices $V$ and directed edges $E$. Write $u\leadsto v$ when $v$ is [[图距离与可达性|reachable]] from $u$: there is a directed [[游走路径与环|path]] from $u$ to $v$. A path may have length zero, so $u\leadsto u$ even without a self-loop. The paths in the two directions may use different edges and intermediate vertices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑有限[[图类型的建模边界|有向图]] $G=(V,E)$，其中 $V$ 为顶点集，$E$ 为有向边集。用 $u\leadsto v$ 表示从 $u$ [[图距离与可达性|可达]] $v$，即存在从 $u$ 到 $v$ 的有向[[游走路径与环|路径]]。路径可以长为零，因此即使没有自环，也有 $u\leadsto u$。两个方向的路径不必使用同一组边或中间顶点。

> [!example] A chain and a return edge · 一条链与一条返回边
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $a\to b\to c$, $a$ reaches both other vertices, but neither can return to $a$. Ignoring arrows gives the connected chain $a-b-c$, so the graph is weakly connected but not strongly connected. Add $c\to a$: now a route around the three edges connects every ordered pair, and the whole graph is strongly connected.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $a\to b\to c$ 中，$a$ 可以到达另外两点，但它们都无法返回 $a$。忽略箭头后得到连通的链 $a-b-c$，所以图弱连通，却不强连通。加入 $c\to a$ 后，沿三条边绕行就能连接每一对有先后次序的顶点，整张图于是强连通。

## Mutual reachability forms strong components
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*互相可达形成强连通分量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Vertices $u,v$ are **strongly connected** when both $u\leadsto v$ and $v\leadsto u$. This is an [[等价关系|equivalence relation]]. It is reflexive by zero-length paths, and symmetric because the definition already requires both directions. For transitivity, suppose $u$ and $v$ are mutually reachable, as are $v$ and $w$. Join routes $u\leadsto v\leadsto w$ and $w\leadsto v\leadsto u$; [[游走去环得到路径|delete repeated detours]] if needed to obtain paths. Thus $u$ and $w$ are mutually reachable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $u\leadsto v$ 与 $v\leadsto u$ 同时成立时，称 $u,v$ **强连通**。这是一个[[等价关系]]：零长度路径保证自反性；定义同时要求两个方向，因此对称。再看传递性：若 $u,v$ 互达，$v,w$ 也互达，就把路线拼成 $u\leadsto v\leadsto w$ 与 $w\leadsto v\leadsto u$，必要时[[游走去环得到路径|删去重复绕行段]]得到路径，所以 $u,w$ 也互达。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a vertex $s$, its class is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对顶点 $s$，它所属的类为

$$S(s)=\{v\in V:s\leadsto v\text{ and }v\leadsto s\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **strongly connected component**, abbreviated **SCC**, consists of this whole vertex class and all original directed edges between its vertices. Distinct classes are disjoint and cover $V$, so the components are determined by the graph, not by a search order. They are maximal by inclusion: adding an outside vertex cannot preserve mutual reachability with every vertex already inside. “Maximal” does not mean “largest among all components.” In the one-way chain above the three SCCs are the three singleton vertices; after adding the return edge there is one SCC.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个**强连通分量**（strongly connected component，简称 **SCC**）由这个完整的顶点类及其内部的全部原有有向边组成。不同类互不相交，合起来覆盖 $V$；分量由图本身决定，不由搜索顺序决定。它们按包含关系是极大的：不能加入类外顶点，还保持与内部所有点互达。“极大”不表示它在所有分量里最大。在前面的单向链中，三个 SCC 都是单点；加入返回边后，三个点成为一个 SCC。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The routes between members can stay inside the component. Indeed, if $u,v\in S(s)$ and $x$ lies on a route from $u$ to $v$, then $s\leadsto u\leadsto x$ and $x\leadsto v\leadsto s$. Hence $x\in S(s)$. Thus the component subgraph itself is strongly connected; this is not merely a claim about routes allowed to leave it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分量内部的互达路线可以留在该分量内。事实上，若 $u,v\in S(s)$，而 $x$ 位于一条从 $u$ 到 $v$ 的路线上，则有 $s\leadsto u\leadsto x$ 和 $x\leadsto v\leadsto s$，因此 $x\in S(s)$。所以分量子图本身就强连通，并不是只能借道类外顶点才能往返。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Forget directions to obtain weak components
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*忽略方向得到弱连通分量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **underlying undirected graph** keeps the same vertices and replaces each arrow by an undirected edge between its endpoints. Its [[无向图连通分量|connected components]] give the vertex sets of the **weakly connected components** of $G$; retain the original arrows inside each set when referring to that part of the original digraph. A nonempty digraph is **weakly connected** when its underlying graph has one component. It is **strongly connected** when it has one SCC, equivalently when every vertex reaches every other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**底层无向图**保留全部顶点，把每条箭头改为连接相同端点的无向边。它的[[无向图连通分量|连通分量]]给出 $G$ 的**弱连通分量**的顶点集；若把这些块看作原有向图的一部分，块内仍保留原来的箭头。非空有向图的底层无向图只有一个分量时，称它**弱连通**；只有一个 SCC 时，称它**强连通**，也就是任意顶点都能到达其他每个顶点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every SCC lies inside one weak component, since a directed path remains a route after its arrows are forgotten. Consequently, a nonempty strongly connected graph is weakly connected. The converse fails even more sharply than in a chain: in $a\to b\leftarrow c$, $a$ and $c$ lie in one weak component, but neither reaches the other. Weak connectivity does not even promise a one-way route for each chosen pair.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个 SCC 都包含在某个弱连通分量内，因为有向路径丢掉箭头后仍是一条路线。因此，非空强连通图一定弱连通。反过来却不成立，而且情况可以比单向链更明显：在 $a\to b\leftarrow c$ 中，$a,c$ 属于同一个弱连通分量，却彼此都不可达。弱连通甚至不保证任取一对顶点后，至少有一个方向可达。

## An outward search supplies only one direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 算法边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fresh BFS or DFS from $s$, with every vertex initially undiscovered, finds its outward reachable set $R^+(s)=\{v:s\leadsto v\}$. This need not be $S(s)$: in the chain, $R^+(a)=\{a,b,c\}$ but $S(a)=\{a\}$. Let $R^-(s)=\{v:v\leadsto s\}$ collect the vertices that can return to $s$. The definition gives $S(s)=R^+(s)\cap R^-(s)$, where $\cap$ means membership in both sets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从全部顶点均未发现的初始状态开始，以 $s$ 为源点做 BFS 或 DFS，得到的是向外可达集 $R^+(s)=\{v:s\leadsto v\}$，不一定是 $S(s)$。例如单向链中 $R^+(a)=\{a,b,c\}$，但 $S(a)=\{a\}$。另令 $R^-(s)=\{v:v\leadsto s\}$ 收集可以返回 $s$ 的顶点，则定义直接给出 $S(s)=R^+(s)\cap R^-(s)$；$\cap$ 表示同时属于两个集合。

> [!question]- Does one complete outward search prove strong connectivity? · 向外搜到所有点就足够了吗？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No: it proves $R^+(s)=V$ but says nothing by itself about $R^-(s)=V$. Reverse every arrow to form the **reverse graph**, keeping the same vertices. A fresh search from $s$ there finds exactly $R^-(s)$, because reversing a route reverses its endpoints. For a nonempty graph, if both fresh searches reach every vertex, then for any $u,v$ the routes $u\leadsto s\leadsto v$ prove strong connectivity. A search on the underlying undirected graph instead tests weak connectivity; it answers a different question.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不够：它只证明 $R^+(s)=V$，单凭这一点不知道是否也有 $R^-(s)=V$。把每条箭头反向、保留原顶点，得到**反图**。在反图上从 $s$ 另做一次全新的搜索，恰好找到 $R^-(s)$，因为反转路线也反转了起终点。对非空图，若两次搜索都到达全部顶点，那么任意 $u,v$ 都可沿 $u\leadsto s\leadsto v$ 相连，便证明了强连通。若改在底层无向图上搜索，检验的则是弱连通，问题不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One isolated vertex is an SCC and a weak component: the zero-length path is enough. An edgeless graph on $n\ge1$ vertices has $n$ of each. If empty vertex sets are allowed, both component families are empty; whether to call the empty graph strongly or weakly connected is a separate naming convention. Adding self-loops or parallel copies of existing arrows does not change these classes. Edge weights do not change reachability while the same edges remain allowed. Finally, [[全图搜索与强连通边界|restarting ordinary searches]] covers all vertices, but does not by itself turn the resulting parent trees into an SCC decomposition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个孤立顶点本身就是 SCC 和弱连通分量：零长度路径已经足够。含 $n\ge1$ 个顶点的无边图各有 $n$ 个这两类分量。若允许空顶点集，则两种分量族都为空；是否把空图称为强连通或弱连通，另需命名约定。增加自环或已有箭头的平行副本不会改变这些类；只要同样的边仍允许经过，边权本身也不改变可达性。最后，[[全图搜索与强连通边界|普通搜索反复重启]]可以覆盖全部顶点，却不会自动把所得父树变成 SCC 分解。
