# 一对一交易保持排放总量不变并不保证各受体的污染暴露或损害不变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*One-for-one trades can preserve total emissions without preserving pollution exposure or damage at every receptor.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同吨数污染物在不同地点排放，对受体的影响未必相同。[[总量排放交易]]中的一对一转让保持配额总量，却可能把排放转向影响更大的位置。因此，总量没有增加不是“所有地方的环境质量都没变”的证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal tonnes emitted at different locations need not have equal effects on receptors. A one-for-one transfer under [[总量排放交易|cap and trade]] preserves the allowance total but may relocate emissions to a more damaging source. An unchanged total does not prove unchanged environmental quality everywhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在一个线性传输近似中，$e_i$是源 $i$的排放，$h_{ij}\ge0$是其每单位排放对受体 $j$污染负荷的贡献，受体负荷为 $z_j$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a linear transfer approximation, $e_i$ is source emissions, $h_{ij}\ge0$ is their per-unit contribution to receptor $j$, and receptor load is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
z_j=\sum_i h_{ij}e_i.
$$

从源1向源2转移一单位排放，会使该受体负荷改变 $h_{2j}-h_{1j}$，即使总排放改变为零。这里的传输系数须在相关范围内有效；若真实传播具有重要非线性，应使用相应的传播模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Moving one emissions unit from source one to source two changes receptor load by $h_{2j}-h_{1j}$ even though total emissions do not change. The coefficients must be valid over the relevant range; material nonlinear transport requires a suitable alternative model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小反例：两源排放$(5,5)$，对同一受体的系数$(1,2)$，所以总排放10、负荷15。一份许可从源1转给源2并用于排放后，变为$(4,6)$，总排放仍10，而负荷变成 $4+2\times6=16$。若约束是该受体负荷不得超过15，一对一交易已经违反这个环境目标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With emissions $(5,5)$ and transfer coefficients $(1,2)$, total emissions are ten and receptor load fifteen. Moving and using one allowance at source two gives emissions $(4,6)$: total emissions remain ten, but load rises to sixteen. A receptor limit of fifteen is therefore violated by the one-for-one trade.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也限制了[[成本有效减排]]的用法：如果给定目标是各受体负荷不超限，就应把这些约束写进问题，而非只固定总吨数。多个受体的约束可以对应不同的影子价格；简单“所有源每吨 MAC相等”不再是适用于这些加权约束的完整判别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This limits the use of [[成本有效减排|cost-effective abatement]]. Receptor-specific limits must be included in the optimisation rather than replaced by total tonnage. Multiple receptor constraints can have different shadow prices; equal unweighted MAC per emissions tonne is no longer the complete criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可行设计可能包括交易区位限制、按影响调整的缴回比例或并行局地质量约束；它们改变可交易单位和可行集，也可能增加交易复杂性。是否需要及如何校准这些规则，要依污染传播与受体证据，不是把任何统一配额市场一概判为无效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Possible designs include spatial trade restrictions, impact-adjusted surrender ratios or parallel local-quality constraints. They change tradable units and feasible choices and may increase complexity. Their need and calibration depend on transport and receptor evidence, rather than a blanket rejection of uniform allowance markets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The cited pages define receptor transfer coefficients and their linearity condition, and give the two-source counterexample. The quantities and loads are independently recalculated.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Printed p.3-22 discusses spatial, source-category, surrender-ratio and parallel local-control options and their effects on fungibility. No claim about hotspots in a current market is inferred.
