# 处理组与对照组未处理潜在结果的均值差构成均值比较中的选择偏差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Selection bias in a treated-control mean contrast is their difference in untreated potential-outcome means*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 在二元处理的均值差分解中，选择偏差为 $\mathrm{SB}=E[Y(0)\mid D=1]-E[Y(0)\mid D=0]$。它衡量两组在都不接受处理的情形下，原本会有多大平均差距。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the binary-treatment mean decomposition, selection bias compares the two groups’ untreated potential-outcome means: how different they would be on average without treatment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

若项目专门招收起点较低者，SB 可能为负；若报名与较高的未处理结果有关，SB 可能为正。这里是 selection bias 的一个特定因果推断口径，不涵盖所有失访、筛样等选择问题。单次随机分组的基线不平衡也不能自动判为机制有偏。完整恒等式见[[均值差的因果分解]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Targeting units with low untreated outcomes may produce a negative term; selection of units with high untreated outcomes may produce a positive one. This is a specific causal-inference definition, not every form of selection bias. Chance imbalance in one random allocation does not establish a biased mechanism. See [[均值差的因果分解|the complete decomposition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
