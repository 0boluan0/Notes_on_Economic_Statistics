# Uniqueness of a matrix inverse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*逆矩阵唯一性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Different methods may be used to compute an [[逆矩阵|inverse matrix]], but they cannot give different correct answers. If two matrices both undo the same square matrix in both orders, they must be the same matrix. This **uniqueness** is why the notation $A^{-1}$ can refer to one definite object, whenever an inverse exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算[[逆矩阵]]可以采用不同方法，但不可能得到两个不同的正确答案。如果两个矩阵都能在两个次序下撤销同一个方阵，它们就必须是同一个矩阵。正是这种**唯一性**，使得逆存在时，记号 $A^{-1}$ 可以指向一个确定的对象。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a concrete picture, $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ sends $(u,v)^T$ to $(u+2v,v)^T$. An undoing operation must recover $v$ from the second coordinate and recover $u$ by subtracting twice that coordinate from the first. This produces $\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$. The general theorem proves the same absence of choice without inspecting each coordinate separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看一个具体图像：$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ 把 $(u,v)^T$ 变成 $(u+2v,v)^T$。撤销操作必须从第二个坐标恢复 $v$，再从第一个坐标减去第二个坐标的两倍来恢复 $u$，所以得到 $\begin{pmatrix}1&-2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$。一般定理无需逐个坐标分析，也能证明这种“没有第二种选择”。

> [!abstract] Two candidates must agree · 两个候选必相同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A,B,C$ be real or complex $n\times n$ matrices, with $n$ a positive integer. Suppose both $B$ and $C$ are two-sided inverses of $A$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $A,B,C$ 是实数或复数的 $n\times n$ 矩阵，$n$ 为正整数。假设 $B,C$ 都是 $A$ 的双侧逆：
>
> $$BA=AB=I_n,\qquad CA=AC=I_n.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Then $B=C$. This establishes “at most one”; it does not assert that an inverse exists for every square matrix.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 则 $B=C$。这证明的是“至多一个”，并没有说每个方阵都存在逆矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Connect the candidates in one product
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*在同一个乘积里连接两个候选*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity matrix $I_n$ leaves a matrix unchanged when multiplied on either side. Since $AC=I_n$, we can insert the product $AC$ to the right of $B$. Then regroup the three factors:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位矩阵 $I_n$ 无论从哪一侧相乘，都不会改变原矩阵。由 $AC=I_n$，可以把乘积 $AC$ 放到 $B$ 右侧，再对三个因子重新加括号：

$$B=BI_n=B(AC)=(BA)C=I_nC=C.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The middle step uses associativity of [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]]: $B(AC)$ and $(BA)C$ group the same ordered factors differently. Both represent the same sequence of actions, $C$ first, then $A$, then $B$. No factors exchange places. Finally $BA=I_n$ removes the first two factors. The chain starts with $B$ and ends with $C$, proving they agree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中间一步使用[[矩阵乘法]]的结合律：$B(AC)$ 与 $(BA)C$ 只是对同一串有序因子重新分组。二者都表示先做 $C$、再做 $A$、最后做 $B$，没有任何因子交换位置。最后由 $BA=I_n$ 消去前两个因子。等式链从 $B$ 开始，以 $C$ 结束，因而证明二者相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Although the theorem assumes both candidates are two-sided inverses, this short calculation uses only the two identities $BA=I_n$ and $AC=I_n$. It does not use a commutativity assumption such as $BC=CB$, nor does it need to calculate the individual entries of an inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

虽然定理假设两个候选都是双侧逆，这个简短计算实际只用到了 $BA=I_n$ 与 $AC=I_n$ 两个等式。它没有假设 $BC=CB$ 这样的交换性，也不需要先把逆矩阵的各个元素算出来。

## 边界

> [!warning] A rectangular one-sided inverse can have choices · 矩形矩阵的单侧逆可以有多种选择
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the $2\times1$ column $A=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}$, every row $L_t=\begin{pmatrix}1&t\end{pmatrix}$ satisfies $L_tA=[1]=I_1$. Thus different values of $t$ give different [[左逆|left inverses]]. But
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $2\times1$ 列矩阵 $A=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}$，每个行矩阵 $L_t=\begin{pmatrix}1&t\end{pmatrix}$ 都满足 $L_tA=[1]=I_1$。所以不同的 $t$ 给出不同的[[左逆]]。然而
>
> $$AL_t=\begin{pmatrix}1&t\\0&0\end{pmatrix}\ne I_2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These are not two-sided inverses, so they do not contradict uniqueness. The multiplication sizes also explain why the two identity matrices here have different sizes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 它们不是双侧逆，因此不反驳唯一性。乘法尺寸也说明，这里两侧出现的单位矩阵为什么一个是一阶、另一个是二阶。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For finite square matrices, the stronger [[方阵单侧逆|one-sided inverse theorem]] says that one side already guarantees the other, and the inverse is unique. The result on this card has a narrower job: once two candidates really are inverses, the identity chain forces them to agree. Existence and uniqueness remain separate questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限阶方阵，更强的[[方阵单侧逆]]定理说明，只验证一侧就能保证另一侧成立，而且逆唯一。本卡承担的任务更具体：两个候选确实都是逆以后，用等式链迫使它们相同。存在性与唯一性仍是两个问题。
