# 剩余劳动力是在明确调整条件下可移出而不显著减少来源部门产出的劳动

本卡采用课堂的**产出损失口径**：一部分劳动移出后，在明确的土地、资本、技术、季节和剩余劳动安排下，来源部门总产出几乎不降，就称这部分劳动为剩余劳动力。零边际产出是它的强例；不是用“农业收入低”或“人很多”直接判定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This note uses the classroom's output-loss criterion: labour is surplus if its withdrawal causes little reduction in source-sector output under specified land, capital, technology, season and remaining-work arrangements. Zero marginal product is a strong case, not a conclusion drawn merely from low agricultural income or many workers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在其他投入与每人工作安排固定的简化农业生产函数 $Q_A=F_A(L_A)$ 中，移出 $0<h\le L_A$ 单位同质劳动，损失的是整个区间的产出。若函数在该区间连续可微且边际产出非负，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a simplified agricultural production function $Q_A=F_A(L_A)$ holding other inputs and work arrangements per person fixed, withdrawing $0<h\le L_A$ units of homogeneous labour loses output over an entire interval. If the function is continuously differentiable with nonnegative marginal product on that interval,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta Q_A=F_A(L_A)-F_A(L_A-h)
=\int_{L_A-h}^{L_A}MP_{L,A}(\ell)\,d\ell.
$$

因此，有限数量的劳动可以无损移出，需要相应区间内都没有边际产出损失；一个点上 $MP_{L,A}=0$ 不足以推出任意多人都能离开。构造例 $F_A(L)=\min\{2L,20\}$ 中，原有 12 单位劳动时产出 20；移出 2 单位仍为 20，移出 3 单位则降到 18。折点不可微不妨碍直接计算产出差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lossless withdrawal of a finite amount requires no marginal output loss throughout the relevant interval. Zero marginal product at one point does not permit arbitrarily many departures. In the constructed example $F_A(L)=\min\{2L,20\}$, 12 units of labour produce 20. Removing two leaves output at 20; removing three reduces it to 18. The nondifferentiable kink does not prevent direct calculation of the output difference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若留在农场的人延长工时才维持产出，结论依赖这种劳动调整，不能说社会完全没有成本。Lewis 原文还讨论低而非恰为零的边际产出、非农业活动的人手冗余，以及更广的[[无限劳动供给|给定工资下劳动充足]]情形；本卡不把这些都压成同一个零产出损失阈值。[[农业短缺点与商业化点]]进一步区分农业边际产出转正与达到制度收入基准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If remaining farmers must work longer to maintain output, the conclusion depends on that adjustment and does not imply zero social cost. Lewis also discusses small rather than exactly zero marginal products, redundancy outside agriculture, and the broader condition of [[无限劳动供给|abundant labour at a given wage]]. These are not all one zero-output-loss threshold. [[农业短缺点与商业化点|The agricultural shortage and commercialization points]] distinguish positive marginal product from reaching the institutional income benchmark.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“隐性失业”是此处的传统理论用语，不自动满足统计[[失业率|失业]]的未就业、求职和可工作条件。产出损失小也不等于原有私人收入小，区别见[[边际产出与退出收入]]；平均产出与边际判断的区别见[[平均生产率与迁移收益]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Disguised unemployment” is a traditional theoretical label here, not automatic satisfaction of the joblessness, search and availability conditions for statistical [[失业率|unemployment]]. A small output loss also need not imply little private income; see [[边际产出与退出收入|marginal product and income forgone on exit]]. For average versus marginal comparisons, see [[平均生产率与迁移收益|average productivity and migration gains]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
