# 总供给冲击是使同一价格水平下可供给产出发生变化的扰动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An aggregate supply shock changes output supplied at a given price level.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总供给冲击改变投入成本、技术、可用资源或生产组织等供给条件。成本上升可使[[短期总供给|SRAS]] 左移；生产率改善可使供给右移，并可能提高[[潜在产出]]。必须分清冲击只影响短期成本，还是也影响长期生产能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A supply shock changes input costs, technology, resources, or production organization. Higher costs can shift [[短期总供给|SRAS]] left; productivity improvements can shift supply right and may raise [[潜在产出|potential output]]. Distinguish temporary cost effects from changes in long-run capacity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在向下倾斜的 AD 不变时，负向 SRAS 冲击使短期均衡产出下降、价格水平上升；正向冲击方向相反。一次价格水平上升不等于此后持续高通胀，见[[通胀率与价格水平]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With downward-sloping AD unchanged, an adverse SRAS shock lowers short-run output and raises the price level; a favorable shock reverses these directions. A one-time price-level rise does not imply continuing high inflation; see [[通胀率与价格水平|the rate–level distinction]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

冲击消退、工资调整或政策反应决定后续路径。若 AD 与原 LRAS 都不变且产出最终回到原潜能，长期价格必须回到原 AD–LRAS 交点；不能同时断言价格永久更高。完整两图对照见[[AD-AS长期调整]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Shock reversal, wage adjustment, and policy responses determine the subsequent path. If AD and the original LRAS remain unchanged and output returns to original potential, the long-run price must return to their original intersection. The paired diagrams are explained in [[AD-AS长期调整|long-run AD-AS adjustment]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
