# 具体劳动是以特定目的和方式生产某种使用价值的有用劳动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Concrete labour is useful activity performed in a specific way for the purpose of producing a use-value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**具体劳动（concrete useful labour）**是具有特定目的、操作方式、对象、手段和结果的有用劳动。它回答“做什么、怎样做”，通过改变材料、运用工具等活动生产某种[[Marx使用价值|使用价值]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Concrete useful labour** is useful activity with a definite purpose, mode of operation, object, means and result. It asks what is done and how, producing a particular [[Marx使用价值|use-value]] through activities such as transforming materials and using tools.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

裁衣使布料成为能穿的衣服，织布使纱线成为布。这两种劳动有不同的操作和有用结果；即使同一个人上午织布、下午裁衣，它们的具体形式仍然不同。若把这两种活动作为形成商品价值的一般人类劳动来考察，就转入[[抽象劳动]]的规定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tailoring turns cloth into wearable clothing, while weaving turns yarn into cloth. Their operations and useful results differ even if one person weaves in the morning and tailors in the afternoon. Considering both as human labour forming commodity value introduces their determination as [[抽象劳动|abstract labour]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体劳动生产使用价值，并不意味着物质财富全部由劳动单独创造。外套的布料、棉花等材料及生产中的自然作用，同样是产品形成的条件。Marx 在这里明确区分：劳动改变物质的形式，并与自然共同形成物质财富；价值分析所考察的则是劳动的另一规定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
That concrete labour produces use-values does not make labour the sole source of material wealth. Materials such as cloth and cotton, together with natural forces, also contribute to the product. Marx distinguishes labour's transformation of matter and its combination with nature in producing material wealth from the other determination of labour examined in value analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
