# 分离均衡是满足 PBE 且各发送者类型的信号策略支持互不相交的信号博弈均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A separating equilibrium is a signaling-game equilibrium that satisfies PBE and in which the signaling strategies of different sender types have disjoint supports.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 定义
> “分离”意味着路径上观察到信号后可以唯一识别发送者类型。类型被识别只是 Bayes 推断结果，不自动证明均衡：每个类型都必须愿意保留自己的信号，而不模仿其他类型以获得更有利的接收者反应。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Definition*
>
> “Separation” means that the sender's type can be identified uniquely after any on-path signal. Identification is a Bayesian inference result, not proof of equilibrium: every type must prefer its prescribed signal to imitating another type in order to obtain a more favorable receiver response.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 纯策略与混合策略下的定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Definition under pure and mixed strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

MIT Lecture 3 的纯策略定义要求存在一一映射 $s:\Theta\to S$，使类型 $\theta$ 以概率 1 选择 $s(\theta)$，并且
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The pure-strategy definition in MIT Lecture 3 requires an injective mapping $s:\Theta\to S$ such that type $\theta$ chooses $s(\theta)$ with probability one and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\theta\ne\theta'
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
s(\theta)\ne s(\theta').
$$

在混合策略下，可把完全分离扩展为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under mixed strategies, full separation can be extended as follows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{supp}\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta)
\cap
\operatorname{supp}\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta')
=\varnothing
\qquad(\theta\ne\theta').
$$

因此任一路径上信号最多由一种类型使用。若两种类型共享某个正概率信号，即使它们在其他信号上不同，也不属于完全 separating；统一分类口径见[[混合信号均衡分类]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus every on-path signal is used by at most one type. If two types share any signal with positive probability, the strategy is not fully separating even if they differ on other signals. See [[混合信号均衡分类|classification of mixed signaling equilibria]] for the unified classification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 路径上 posterior 退化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The on-path posterior is degenerate.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若先验对各类型有正概率，且 $s$ 只在类型 $\theta$ 的策略支持中，则[[路径上一致性]]给出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the prior assigns positive probability to every type and $s$ belongs only to type $\theta$'s strategy support, [[路径上一致性|on-path consistency]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p(\theta\mid s)=1,
\qquad
p(\theta'\mid s)=0\quad(\theta'\ne\theta).
$$

接收者随后对“已识别的类型 $\theta$ 与信号 $s$”作最佳反应。这个结论只适用于路径上、属于某类型支持的信号；从未被任何类型采用的信号仍需要[[路径外信念]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The receiver then best responds to the identified type $\theta$ together with signal $s$. This conclusion applies only to on-path signals in some type's support. A signal used by no type still requires an [[路径外信念|off-path belief]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 识别类型后仍必须检查激励相容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Incentive compatibility must still be checked after type identification.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支持互不相交只保证观察能够识别类型，并不能证明各类型愿意执行这套策略。逐类型比较模仿与其他偏离见[[分离模仿约束]]；纯分离何时在组合上已经不可能，见[[分离所需信号数]]。在标准 full-support 模型中，完全分离还会让路径上 posterior 不再依赖先验的具体数值，限定条件见[[完全分离的先验不变性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Disjoint supports guarantee only that an observation identifies type; they do not prove that each type is willing to follow the strategy. See [[分离模仿约束|separating imitation constraints]] for type-by-type comparisons of imitation and other deviations, and [[分离所需信号数|the number of signals required for separation]] for when pure separation is combinatorially impossible. In a standard full-support model, full separation also makes the on-path posterior independent of the prior's numerical values; see [[完全分离的先验不变性|prior invariance under full separation]] for the qualifying conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] Beer–Quiche 中为何分离可能失败
> 假设 weak 选 Quiche、strong 选 Beer，接收者由信号准确识别类型。若 weak 改选 Beer 后能被当作 strong、从而避免 Fight，而且这项收益超过食物偏好损失，weak 就会模仿；posterior 虽然在候选路径上完全分离，候选仍不是均衡。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Why separation can fail in Beer–Quiche*
>
> Suppose the weak type chooses Quiche and the strong type chooses Beer, so the receiver identifies type exactly from the signal. If the weak type can switch to Beer, be treated as strong, and avoid Fight, and if that gain exceeds the loss from eating the less-preferred food, the weak type will imitate. The candidate is not an equilibrium even though its on-path posterior is fully separating.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 路径上每个信号都让接收者正确识别类型。是否已经足以证明 separating PBE？
>
> **答案：** 不足。还要验证接收者对识别出的类型作最佳反应，并逐类型检查所有模仿与其他信号偏离；否则分离策略不会被自愿执行。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Self-check*
>
> Every on-path signal allows the receiver to identify the type correctly. Is that sufficient to establish a separating PBE?
>
> **Answer:** No. One must also verify that the receiver best responds to the identified type and check every type's imitation and other signal deviations. Otherwise the separating strategy will not be followed voluntarily.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
