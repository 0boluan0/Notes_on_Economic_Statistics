# 抽象劳动是撇开具体形式而作为同质人类劳动力耗费来计量的劳动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Abstract labour is labour considered apart from its concrete form as expenditure of homogeneous human labour-power*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Marx 的价值分析中，**抽象劳动（abstract human labour）**是把裁衣、织布等[[具体劳动|具体有用形式]]撇开后，作为一般人类劳动力耗费来考察的劳动。它构成[[Marx商品价值|商品价值]]的共同实体；“抽象”指这一社会计量规定，不是另一次发生在实际劳动之外的活动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Marx's value analysis, **abstract human labour** is labour considered as expenditure of human labour-power in general, apart from [[具体劳动|concrete useful forms]] such as tailoring or weaving. It forms the common substance of [[Marx商品价值|commodity value]]. Abstraction refers to this social determination, rather than to an additional activity occurring outside actual work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一小时裁衣，在使用价值方面是生产衣服的具体劳动；在价值方面，则作为一般人类劳动计量。两者是同一劳动的两个方面，不能把八小时工作日切成“四小时具体劳动、四小时抽象劳动”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An hour of tailoring is concrete labour producing clothing when considered in relation to use-value; in relation to value, it counts as human labour in general. These are two aspects of the same labour. An eight-hour day cannot be divided into four hours of concrete labour and four hours of abstract labour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种共同计量还要交代劳动的社会条件。原文把复杂劳动看作倍加的简单劳动，折算比例由社会过程形成；简单平均劳动本身也随社会和时期而变。因此，数例把裁衣和织布的各一小时记为相同单位时，须先假定同质简单劳动、相同强度，并按[[社会必要劳动时间]]计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common measurement also requires social conditions. The text treats skilled labour as multiplied simple labour, with conversion proportions established through a social process; simple average labour itself varies across societies and periods. A numerical example equating an hour of tailoring with an hour of weaving must therefore assume homogeneous simple labour, equal intensity and measurement in [[社会必要劳动时间|socially necessary labour time]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
