# 信号博弈、序贯理性与完美贝叶斯均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Signaling Games, Sequential Rationality, and Perfect Bayesian Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当行动既改变结果又传递私人类型信息时，怎样同时求出发送者策略、接收者行动和观察后的信念。
> **具体锚点：** 教育若对高生产率劳动者成本更低，较高教育可把类型分开；企业观察教育后更新 belief 并决定工资。
> **核心难点：** 子博弈要求从单节点信息集开始；不完全信息下没有足够真子博弈时，还要用信念和 Bayes 更新约束路径内外行为。
> **为什么重要：** 教育、质量保证、融资、广告和进入威胁的可信性都取决于类型激励与观察后的信念。
> **继续：** 先掌握 [[贝叶斯博弈]] 的类型与策略映射，再按候选 pooling、separating、semi-separating 逐类检查 PBE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** When actions both affect outcomes and reveal information about private types, determine sender strategies, receiver actions, and posterior beliefs jointly.
> **Concrete anchor:** If education is less costly for high-productivity workers, higher education can separate types; firms update beliefs after observing education and then set wages.
> **Central difficulty:** A subgame must begin at a singleton information set. When incomplete information leaves too few proper subgames, beliefs and Bayesian updating are needed to discipline behavior both on and off the equilibrium path.
> **Why it matters:** The credibility of education, warranties, financing, advertising, and entry threats depends on type-specific incentives and posterior beliefs.
> **Continue with:** First master types and strategy mappings in [[贝叶斯博弈|Bayesian Games]], then test candidate pooling, separating, and semi-separating PBEs in turn.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信号博弈研究拥有私人类型的发送者怎样通过可观察行动影响接收者的信念与反应。PBE 同时要求策略在每个信息集上序贯最优，并要求路径上信念与策略通过 Bayes 法则一致；它比只写 Bayesian Nash 均衡多检查了行动发生后的理性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A signaling game studies how a sender with a private type uses an observable action to influence a receiver's beliefs and response. A PBE requires strategies to be sequentially optimal at every information set and on-path beliefs to be consistent with strategies through Bayes' rule. It therefore checks rationality after observed actions, beyond merely writing a Bayesian Nash equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 信号博弈与序贯信念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Signaling Games and Sequential Beliefs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

发送者的类型影响成本或偏好，先选信号；接收者观察信号后更新对类型的 belief 并行动。pooling、separating 和 semi-separating 描述不同类型是否选择相同信号。信号是否可信来自不同类型的激励相容，而非信号本身“看起来昂贵”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sender's type affects costs or preferences and the sender moves first. The receiver observes the signal, updates a belief about the type, and then acts. Pooling, separating, and semi-separating equilibria describe whether different types choose the same signal. A signal is credible because incentive compatibility differs across types, not merely because the signal appears costly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## PBE 的两部分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Two Components of PBE*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完美贝叶斯均衡由策略与信念组成：每个信息集上策略在给定信念下序贯最优；到达概率为正的信息集上信念由 Bayes 法则更新。离开均衡路径的信息集 Bayes 法则可能不约束信念，这正是进一步精炼要处理的自由度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A perfect Bayesian equilibrium consists of strategies and beliefs. At every information set, strategies must be sequentially optimal given beliefs; at information sets reached with positive probability, beliefs must be updated by Bayes' rule. Bayes' rule may not restrict beliefs at off-path information sets, and that freedom is precisely what stronger refinements seek to discipline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

序贯最优不是只在均衡路径上最大化。接收者必须在观察到每一种可能信号后选择最佳行动，发送者每一种类型也必须不愿改发其他信号。信念一致性则说明哪些 posterior 能由先验和策略生成；二者缺一都不是 PBE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sequential optimality is not confined to the equilibrium path. The receiver must choose an optimal action after every possible signal, and every sender type must prefer not to send another signal. Belief consistency determines which posteriors can be generated from the prior and the strategies. Without both requirements, the assessment is not a PBE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 求解顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Solution Procedure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先写完整策略和信息集，再列候选均衡类型；由接收者最佳反应得到信念阈值，再检查发送者各类型的偏离激励，最后验证路径上 Bayes 一致性和路径外信念。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First write complete strategies and information sets, then list candidate equilibrium forms. Derive the receiver's belief thresholds from best responses, check each sender type's deviation incentives, and finally verify on-path Bayesian consistency and specify off-path beliefs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体地，检查 pooling 时先假设两类发送同一信号，用 Bayes 法则得到该路径上的 posterior；确定接收者行动后，再分别检查两类是否愿意偏离。检查 separating 时，路径上观察到的信号通常揭示类型，但仍要验证模仿约束。若无纯 pooling 或 separating 解，再考虑半分离混合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a pooling candidate, assume both types send the same signal and use Bayes' rule to obtain the posterior on that path. After determining the receiver's action, check separately whether each type wants to deviate. For a separating candidate, the observed signal usually reveals the type on path, but imitation constraints must still be checked. If neither pure pooling nor separating works, consider semi-separating mixtures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：教育信号
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Education as a Signal*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设高生产率与低生产率劳动者都可选择教育水平，企业只观察教育并据此支付预期生产率。若教育对高类型成本更低，可能存在 separating：高类型选择较高教育、低类型不模仿；工资差弥补高类型教育成本，却不足以弥补低类型的更高成本。教育在该模型中可以不直接提高生产率，作用来自筛选；若现实教育同时提高技能，必须把人力资本机制另行加入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose high- and low-productivity workers choose education levels while firms observe only education and pay expected productivity. If education is less costly for the high type, a separating equilibrium may exist: the high type chooses more education and the low type does not imitate. The wage gain covers the high type's education cost but not the low type's larger cost. In this signaling model, education need not raise productivity directly; it sorts types. If education also builds skills in reality, that human-capital mechanism must be modeled separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 路径外信念与失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Off-path Beliefs and Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

路径外信念不能由 Bayes 法则唯一确定，但不能因此随便挑一个只为支撑结论。应报告它怎样影响接收者行动，并检查 intuitive criterion 等更强精炼是否会排除候选。常见错误是只验证发送者不偏离、没有验证接收者最优；或把“分离后类型被识别”当作无需检查成本约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bayes' rule does not uniquely determine off-path beliefs, but that does not justify selecting any belief solely to support a preferred conclusion. State how the belief changes the receiver's action and check whether stronger refinements, such as the intuitive criterion, eliminate the candidate. Common errors are checking only that senders do not deviate without verifying the receiver's optimality, or assuming that type revelation under separation eliminates the need to check cost-based incentive constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### PBE 中路径外信念为什么是难点？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why are off-path beliefs difficult in a PBE?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 该信息集在均衡下概率为零，Bayes 法则无法由观察概率唯一更新，某些信念可支撑不合理行为，需要额外精炼筛选。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The information set has zero probability under the equilibrium, so Bayes' rule cannot uniquely update beliefs from observed probabilities. Some beliefs can support implausible behavior and may require additional refinements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### separating 信号为什么必须满足两类人的激励约束？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must a separating signal satisfy the incentive constraints of both types?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 高类型必须愿意承担信号成本，低类型又必须不愿模仿；只满足其中一方，候选策略会被另一方的偏离破坏。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The high type must be willing to bear the signaling cost, while the low type must prefer not to imitate. If only one condition holds, the other type has a profitable deviation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### PBE 相比 Bayesian Nash 多检查了什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does PBE check beyond Bayesian Nash equilibrium?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它显式加入每个信息集的信念，并要求行动后的策略序贯最优以及路径上信念满足 Bayes 一致性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It explicitly adds beliefs at every information set and requires post-action strategies to be sequentially optimal and on-path beliefs to satisfy Bayesian consistency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
