# 高市场集中度本身不能证明竞争弱或企业具有高加价能力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*High market concentration alone does not prove weak competition or strong markup power.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集中度描述谁占有多少业务量；竞争强度还取决于定价方式、产品差异、产能、买方选择、进入条件等。高集中度可能提示需要深入分析，但不是从份额到利润的充分推理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Concentration describes who holds how much activity. Competitive intensity also depends on pricing behavior, differentiation, capacity, buyer options, and entry conditions. High concentration may warrant investigation but is insufficient to infer profits from shares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个严格反例是[[Bertrand边际成本均衡]]：在该卡列明的同质品、相同常数边际成本、无产能约束等条件下，取正边际成本 $c=10$，两家企业都把价格定为 10。平分销量时各占 50%，HHI 为 5000，但价格加价率为零。该结论只在此模型条件下成立，足以反驳“集中必然产生高加价”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Bertrand边际成本均衡|Bertrand marginal-cost equilibrium]] provides a counterexample. Under its homogeneous-product, equal constant-cost, unconstrained-capacity assumptions, take positive marginal cost $c=10$. Both firms price at 10. Equal sales shares give an HHI of 5000, yet the price markup is zero. This model-specific result disproves a necessary link from concentration to high markups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际分析应把份额与[[潜在进入者威胁]]、[[行业内竞争]]和交易双方的选择放在一起；不能据这个反例反向宣称集中度完全没有信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Empirical analysis should combine shares with [[潜在进入者威胁|entry threats]], [[行业内竞争|rivalry]], and trading parties' alternatives. The counterexample does not imply that concentration is uninformative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [HBS Institute for Strategy and Competitiveness, The Five Forces, Rivalry Among Existing Competitors](https://www.isc.hbs.edu/strategy/business-strategy/Pages/the-five-forces.aspx)：竞争强度有多个结构驱动因素，不能只看数量。
- [MIT 15.010/15.011, The Basics of Game Theory, PDF 第 3 页 §4](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-010-economic-analysis-for-business-decisions-fall-2004/807ba86e100d349ef73c294b9e720931_the_bsc_game_thy.pdf#page=3)：重新打开同质品降价偏离与 $p=MC$ 论证，复用[[Bertrand边际成本均衡|现有完整条件]]；50%/50% 的 HHI 独立核算为 5000。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The HBS framework supports multiple structural drivers of rivalry. MIT's teaching argument for undercutting and $p=MC$ was reopened, with the exact conditions supplied by the existing theorem. The equal-share HHI was independently calculated as 5000.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
