# 在固定土地和给定人口反馈模型中一次永久技术提升提高长期人口而不改变稳态人均产出

这个结论说的是：技术水平提高后保持在新水平，人口仍按原来的收入反馈调整。短期人口来不及跳变，人均产出先提高；长期增加的人口使人均产出回到原有[[马尔萨斯生存收入|人口平衡阈值]]。结论必须和模型假设一起使用，不能从“土地有限”一句话直接推出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result concerns a technology level that rises once and then stays at its new level, while the population response to income remains unchanged. Population cannot jump immediately, so output per person first rises. Subsequent population growth returns it to the original [[马尔萨斯生存收入|population-balance threshold]]. The result requires the model's assumptions, not just finite land.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个连续时间教学构造把条件写全：土地 $T>0$ 固定，人口 $N(t)>0$，每人提供的有效劳动不变，因此劳动投入与人口成比例，比例并入技术参数 $A$。取 $0<\alpha<1$、$\beta>0$、$\bar y>0$，令

$$
Y=A T^\alpha N^{1-\alpha},\qquad
y=\frac{Y}{N}=A\left(\frac{T}{N}\right)^\alpha,
\qquad
\frac{\dot N}{N}=\beta\left(\frac{y}{\bar y}-1\right).
$$

这里 $Y$ 是同口径实际产出，$y$ 是平均到总人口的产出，不是工资；$\beta$ 控制调整速度。人口方程直接规定净增长，不单独识别出生和死亡；没有年龄结构、净迁移或资本积累。它是为演示机制选定的函数，不是原课堂图或历史数据拟合出的方程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This continuous-time teaching example fixes land and effective labour per person, with their proportionality absorbed into $A$. $Y$ is consistently measured real output, and $y$ is output per person, not a wage. $\beta$ controls adjustment speed. The population equation specifies net growth without separately identifying births and deaths; it omits age structure, migration and capital accumulation. The functions illustrate the mechanism rather than reproduce the classroom diagram or fit historical data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

技术固定时，对 $y$ 取对数求导：

$$
\frac{\dot y}{y}
=-\alpha\frac{\dot N}{N}
=\alpha\beta\left(1-\frac{y}{\bar y}\right).
$$

$y>\bar y$ 时向下调整，$0<y<\bar y$ 时向上调整。在这个光滑的一维模型中，所有正初值都收敛到 $y^*=\bar y$，相应人口为

$$
N^*=T\left(\frac{A}{\bar y}\right)^{1/\alpha}.
$$

从原稳态出发，令技术从 $A_0$ 一次提高到 $A_1=kA_0$，其中 $k>1$，随后固定。冲击瞬间 $N$ 不变，故 $y$ 跳到 $k\bar y$；最终 $y$ 回到 $\bar y$，而 $N_1^*/N_0^*=k^{1/\alpha}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Log differentiation gives the adjustment equation. Above $\bar y$, income per person falls; below it, positive income rises. This smooth one-dimensional model converges to $\bar y$ from every positive initial value. Starting at the old steady state, a permanent level increase by factor $k$ immediately multiplies output per person by $k$, since population cannot jump. The eventual population increases by $k^{1/\alpha}$ while output per person returns to $\bar y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $T=1$、$\alpha=1/2$、$\bar y=1$，技术从 $10$ 提高到 $12$：人口初始为 $100$，人均产出先从 $1$ 到 $1.2$；新稳态人口为 $144$，人均产出回到 $1$。技术提高 20% 并不在这里永久提高人均产出 20%，但支持的人口提高了 44%。数值只是模型构造。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $T=1$, $\alpha=1/2$ and $\bar y=1$, raising technology from 10 to 12 moves output per person initially from 1 to 1.2 at population 100. The new steady-state population is 144, with output per person back at 1. The 20% technology increase supports 44% more people in this constructed example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A$ 持续变化，上式还会多出 $\dot A/A$；若生育或死亡条件改变，人口反馈和 $\bar y$ 也可能改变。因此这张卡的结论不能直接用于持续技术增长或人口转型，更不能给现实国家推出“资助技术没有用”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continually changing technology adds $\dot A/A$ to the income-growth equation; changed fertility or mortality conditions can change the population response and its threshold. The result therefore does not directly cover sustained technical progress or demographic transition, and does not imply that supporting technology is useless in a real country.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
