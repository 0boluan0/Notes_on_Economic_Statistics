# Multinomial coefficient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*多项式系数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A multinomial coefficient counts ways to put **distinct objects into named groups with specified sizes**. The order inside a group is ignored, but the group names matter.

Suppose Alice, Ben, and Chen must fill two places on the red team and one on the blue team. There are three assignments: the blue member is Alice, Ben, or Chen, and the other two form the red team. Writing the red members in the opposite order does not create a fourth assignment. The multinomial coefficient for these group sizes is $\binom{3}{2,1}=3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
多项式系数数的是：把**不同对象放进有名称的组，并规定各组大小**，有多少种分法。组内顺序不重要，但组的名称有区别。

例如，把 Alice、Ben、Chen 分成两人的红队和一人的蓝队。共有三种分法：蓝队成员可以是 Alice、Ben 或 Chen，剩下两人组成红队。把红队两人的书写顺序交换，并不会产生第四种分法。这组人数对应的多项式系数是 $\binom{3}{2,1}=3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the indices specify
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*下标规定了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n\ge0$ be the number of distinct objects, and let $m\ge1$ be the number of named groups. For each group $i$, the integer $k_i\ge0$ is its required size. Every object goes into exactly one group, so the sizes must satisfy $k_1+\cdots+k_m=n$.

The symbol $\binom{n}{k_1,\ldots,k_m}$ denotes the number of these assignments. As in the [[组合数|binomial coefficient]], we choose membership rather than an ordering inside each group. Here several group sizes are recorded together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $n\ge0$ 是不同对象的总数，$m\ge1$ 是有名称的组数，第 $i$ 组要求有 $k_i\ge0$ 个对象，其中 $n,m,k_i$ 都是整数。每个对象恰好进入一组，因此必须有 $k_1+\cdots+k_m=n$。

符号 $\binom{n}{k_1,\ldots,k_m}$ 表示这样的分配数量。它和[[组合数]]一样，关注的是成员归属，不数组内顺序；这里把多个组的大小一起写在下标中。

> [!important] Counting fixed group sizes / 数固定大小的组
> $$
> \boxed{\binom{n}{k_1,\ldots,k_m}
> =\frac{n!}{k_1!\cdots k_m!}},
> \qquad k_i\ge0,\quad \sum_{i=1}^{m}k_i=n.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The factorial $r!$ counts orderings of $r$ distinct objects, and $0!=1$. A group of size zero has one possible membership: the empty set. When $n=0$, all group sizes are zero and there is one empty assignment. A negative size, or sizes whose sum differs from $n$, describes no assignment of the stated kind; the factorial formula above is used only on its stated domain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 阶乘 $r!$ 数 $r$ 个不同对象的排列，约定 $0!=1$。大小为零的组只有一种成员集合，就是空集。$n=0$ 时所有组大小都为零，只有一种空分配。若某个大小为负，或者大小之和不等于 $n$，就不存在所描述的分配；上面的阶乘公式只在所写范围内使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To obtain the formula, first [[排列|order all $n$ distinct objects]] in a line. Give the first $k_1$ objects to group 1, the next $k_2$ to group 2, and so on. A fixed grouping comes from exactly $k_1!\cdots k_m!$ lines: within each group its members can be rearranged, without changing their membership. The [[除法法则|division rule]] therefore divides the $n!$ lines by this common number of representations.

Alternatively, choose group 1, then group 2 from those left, and continue. Multiplying the binomial coefficients gives the same result:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
推导时，先把全部 $n$ 个不同对象[[排列]]成一行。前 $k_1$ 个给第 1 组，接下来的 $k_2$ 个给第 2 组，依此类推。每一种固定分组都对应恰好 $k_1!\cdots k_m!$ 行排列，因为每组内部的成员可以任意换序，而不改变分组。根据[[除法法则]]，把 $n!$ 行排列除以这个共同的表示次数，就得到公式。

也可以先选第 1 组，再从剩余对象中选第 2 组，继续选下去。把各步组合数相乘，同样得到

$$
\binom n{k_1}\binom{n-k_1}{k_2}\cdots
\binom{k_m}{k_m}
=\frac{n!}{k_1!\cdots k_m!}.
$$

## The same count for repeated letters
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*重复字母也对应同一个计数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the words `AAB`, `ABA`, and `BAA`. There are three positions, and we choose which two belong to the symbol A and which one to B. The positions are distinct; the copies of A are indistinguishable. This is precisely a grouping of positions with sizes $(2,1)$, so the count is again $\binom{3}{2,1}=3$.

In general, a length-$n$ word containing $k_i$ copies of symbol $i$ has $\binom{n}{k_1,\ldots,k_m}$ distinct arrangements. The symbols name the groups of positions. Temporarily labelling each repeated copy and then erasing those labels gives the same fixed-multiplicity proof as above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
看 `AAB`、`ABA`、`BAA` 这三个字符串。它们有三个位置，要指定其中哪两个位置放 A，哪个位置放 B。位置彼此不同，而两个 A 不作区分。这正是把位置分成大小为 $(2,1)$ 的两组，所以数量仍是 $\binom{3}{2,1}=3$。

一般地，一个长为 $n$、第 $i$ 种符号出现 $k_i$ 次的字符串，有 $\binom{n}{k_1,\ldots,k_m}$ 种不同排列。符号的种类就是位置组的名称。给相同符号临时加标签，再擦去标签，也能得到上面相同的固定重数证明。

> [!example] MISSISSIPPI
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> There are 11 positions: M occurs once, I four times, S four times, and P twice. The number of distinct words formed by rearranging these letters is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 总共 11 个位置：M 出现 1 次，I 和 S 各 4 次，P 出现 2 次。重新排列这些字母形成的不同字符串共有
>
> $$
> \binom{11}{1,4,4,2}=\frac{11!}{1!4!4!2!}=34650.
> $$

## What changes when group names disappear?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*取消组名后，要重新检查重复次数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If four distinct people are split into a named red pair and blue pair, the count is $\binom{4}{2,2}=6$. If we want only a partition into two unnamed pairs, each partition has two such colourings, so the count is $6/2!=3$.

For unnamed groups of **specified positive sizes**, only groups of the same size can exchange their original names while respecting that size list. If $r_s$ is the number of groups of size $s$, divide the labelled count by $\prod_s r_s!$. For sizes $(2,1,1)$, the two singleton groups can exchange names, but the pair cannot exchange its name with a singleton: divide by $2!$, not $3!$. If empty groups are allowed, first decide whether they remain actual boxes or are omitted from the partition; do not apply this positive-size rule without that decision.

When these counts are used as coefficients in an algebraic expansion, the connection is explained by the [[多项式定理|multinomial theorem]]. The coefficient card counts assignments; the theorem explains how those assignments arise from multiplying sums.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
把四个不同的人分成有名称的红队两人、蓝队两人，有 $\binom{4}{2,2}=6$ 种。如果只想分成两个无名称的二人组，每种分组都对应两种红蓝命名，所以数量是 $6/2!=3$。

若无名称各组的**指定大小都为正**，只有大小相同的组才能交换原来的组名，同时仍符合给定的大小列表。设 $r_s$ 是大小为 $s$ 的组数，就把有名称的计数除以 $\prod_s r_s!$。例如大小为 $(2,1,1)$ 时，两个单人组可以交换名称，二人组却不能与单人组交换名称，因此要除以 $2!$，不是 $3!$。若允许空组，必须先说明空组仍算实际盒子，还是从分组中省去；不能未经判断就套用这个正大小规则。

当这些计数用作代数展开的系数时，具体联系见[[多项式定理]]。本卡数分配方式；那个定理说明这些分配为什么会出现在多个和式相乘的过程中。
