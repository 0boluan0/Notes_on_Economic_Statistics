# Overlapping coverage in a solid of revolution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*旋转体重复覆盖*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a plane region rotates, two different pieces of the original picture can sweep out the **same part of space**. Geometric volume counts that space once. This is why adding the volumes generated by separate planar pieces can overcount, even when those pieces did not overlap before rotation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平面区域旋转后，原图中不同的两块可能扫出**同一块空间**。几何体积只把这块空间算一次。所以，即使两块平面区域原本不重叠，把它们各自生成的体积直接相加，也可能重复计数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine rotating around the $y$-axis. A vertical segment at $x=1$ and its mirror at $x=-1$, both running from $y=0$ to $y=2$, trace the same cylindrical surface. Giving these segments matching small widths produces overlapping shells. Moving a piece to the other side of the axis has changed its starting angle, not necessarily the space it occupies after a full turn.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

想象绕 $y$ 轴旋转。在 $x=1$ 与 $x=-1$ 处各取一条从 $y=0$ 到 $y=2$ 的竖直线段，二者扫出同一个圆柱侧面；给线段配上对应的小宽度，就会生成重叠的薄壳。把区域移到轴的另一侧，只是改变起始角度，未必改变转一整圈后占据的空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Compare radius and axial position together
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*同时比较半径与沿轴位置*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[圆柱壳法|cylindrical shell]] is organised by a nonnegative distance $r$ from the axis and the positions it occupies along that axis. About the $y$-axis, positions $x=r$ and $x=-r$ have the same radius. At that radius, collect **all occupied height intervals**, then take their union: include each height covered by either side, counting shared heights once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[圆柱壳法]]按到轴的非负距离 $r$，以及沿轴占据的位置组织薄壳。绕 $y$ 轴时，$x=r$ 与 $x=-r$ 具有相同半径。在这个半径处，先收集**所有被占据的高度区间**，再取并集：任一侧覆盖的高度都保留，两侧共有的高度只算一次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, at one radius suppose the right-hand piece covers $[0,2]$ and the left-hand piece covers $[1,3]$. Their union is $[0,3]$, of length $3$, although the two lengths separately sum to $4$. If instead the intervals were $[0,1]$ and $[2,3]$, the occupied total length would be $2$; the empty gap must stay empty. Thus being on both sides of the axis is a reason to check coverage, not proof that overlap occurs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如在某个半径处，右侧覆盖 $[0,2]$，左侧覆盖 $[1,3]$。并集为 $[0,3]$，长度是 $3$，但分别相加会得到 $4$。如果两个区间改成 $[0,1]$ 与 $[2,3]$，实际占据的总长度就是 $2$，中间空隙必须保留。因此，区域出现在轴的两侧，只提示需要检查覆盖，并不证明一定重叠。

> [!important] Merge first, integrate second · 先合并覆盖，再积分
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $H(r)$ be the total length of this union at radius $r$. For bounded regions with the usual piecewise-continuous slice lengths, the actual volume over $0\le r\le R$ is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $H(r)$ 为半径 $r$ 处这些高度区间并集的总长度。对通常切片长度分段连续的有界区域，在 $0\le r\le R$ 内，实际体积为
>
> $$V=2\pi\int_0^R rH(r)\,dr.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $R$ reaches the farthest occupied radius, and $H=0$ at empty radii. The [[定积分|definite integral]] sums circumference times actual occupied length times radial thickness. It does not sum the number of times a planar construction visits the same space.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $R$ 达到最远的占据半径，空半径处取 $H=0$。这个[[定积分]]累加“周长乘实际占据长度乘径向厚度”，不是累加平面构造重复经过同一空间的次数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a complete solid example, take rectangles $1\le x\le2$, $0\le y\le2$ and $-2\le x\le-1$, $1\le y\le3$. Every radius $1\le r\le2$ has the first pair of intervals above. Their rotation therefore has volume $2\pi\int_1^2 3r\,dr=9\pi$. Adding the separately generated volumes gives $12\pi$, overcounting the common layer $1\le y\le2$, whose volume is $3\pi$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看一个完整立体例子：取矩形 $1\le x\le2$、$0\le y\le2$，以及矩形 $-2\le x\le-1$、$1\le y\le3$。每个半径 $1\le r\le2$ 都对应前面的第一组区间，所以旋转后的体积为 $2\pi\int_1^2 3r\,dr=9\pi$。分别求体积再相加会得到 $12\pi$，多算的正是高度 $1\le y\le2$ 的共同部分，其体积为 $3\pi$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One half can already generate the whole solid
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有时半个区域已经生成整个立体*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With coordinate units fixed, rotate $x^2\le y\le1$ about the $y$-axis. At radius $0\le r\le1$, the strips at $x=r$ and $x=-r$ both cover $r^2\le y\le1$. Their common height is $1-r^2$. The right half alone already generates the whole solid, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定坐标单位，把 $x^2\le y\le1$ 绕 $y$ 轴旋转。在半径 $0\le r\le1$ 处，$x=r$ 与 $x=-r$ 的细条都覆盖 $r^2\le y\le1$，共同高度为 $1-r^2$。只用右半区域已经生成整个立体，所以

$$
V=2\pi\int_0^1 r(1-r^2)\,dr
=2\pi\left[\frac{r^2}{2}-\frac{r^4}{4}\right]_0^1
=\frac\pi2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keeping both halves with nonnegative radii gives $2\pi\int_{-1}^1|x|(1-x^2)\,dx=\pi$, twice the volume. Using the signed coordinate $x$ instead gives zero through cancellation. Neither approach fixes the counting problem. Choosing one half is valid in this symmetric example; for a general region, discarding one side may lose material, so use the actual union.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保留两侧并使用非负半径，会得到 $2\pi\int_{-1}^1|x|(1-x^2)\,dx=\pi$，恰好多算一倍；改用带符号的坐标 $x$，又会因正负抵消得到零。两者都没有解决计数问题。这个对称例子可以只取一半；一般区域若直接丢掉一侧，却可能漏掉材料，因此要检查实际并集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Disks and washers also need unions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*圆盘与垫圈也要先取并集*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[圆盘垫圈法|disk and washer method]] fixes an axial height $y$ instead of a radius. First convert the original horizontal slice into the set of distances $|x|$ from the axis. Overlapping radial intervals must be merged before calculating the cross-sectional area.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[圆盘垫圈法]]固定的是沿轴高度 $y$，而不是半径。先把原来的水平切片变成到轴的距离 $|x|$ 所组成的集合；径向区间重叠时，要先合并，再算截面积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A slice $-1\le x\le2$ sweeps out every radius from $0$ to $2$, hence a disk of area $4\pi$: neither $4\pi+\pi$ nor $4\pi-\pi$ is correct. Two pieces $[-3,-2]$ and $[1,5/2]$ produce radial intervals $[2,3]$ and $[1,5/2]$. Their union is $[1,3]$, giving a single washer of area $\pi(3^2-1^2)=8\pi$. By contrast, radial intervals $[1,2]$ and $[3,4]$ remain separated and have total area $\pi(2^2-1^2)+\pi(4^2-3^2)=10\pi$; filling the gap would invent material.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

切片 $-1\le x\le2$ 扫出从 $0$ 到 $2$ 的全部半径，因此是面积 $4\pi$ 的圆盘；既不是 $4\pi+\pi$，也不是 $4\pi-\pi$。两段 $[-3,-2]$ 与 $[1,5/2]$ 则产生径向区间 $[2,3]$ 与 $[1,5/2]$，并集为 $[1,3]$，得到面积 $\pi(3^2-1^2)=8\pi$ 的一个垫圈。相反，径向区间 $[1,2]$ 与 $[3,4]$ 仍然分离，总面积为 $\pi(2^2-1^2)+\pi(4^2-3^2)=10\pi$；填上间隙会添加本来不存在的材料。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the parabola example, a fixed $0\le y\le1$ gives $-\sqrt y\le x\le\sqrt y$, whose rotation is one disk of radius $\sqrt y$. Thus the same volume is $\pi\int_0^1 y\,dy=\pi/2$. The shell and disk calculations agree because both describe the same union of occupied space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在抛物线例子中，固定 $0\le y\le1$ 后有 $-\sqrt y\le x\le\sqrt y$，旋转后是半径 $\sqrt y$ 的一个圆盘。因此同一体积也等于 $\pi\int_0^1 y\,dy=\pi/2$。壳法与圆盘法结果一致，是因为二者描述了同一片实际占据空间的并集。
