# 不同平面细条旋转后若覆盖同一体积就必须去重后再积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Different planar strips that sweep out the same volume must be deduplicated before integration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求旋转体的几何体积时，每一块空间只能计一次。绕同一轴旋转后，原先在轴两侧的细条可能具有相同半径，并占据重叠的轴向区间；把它们各自的壳体积相加，会把交叠部分重复计入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Geometric volume counts each part of space once. Strips on opposite sides of an axis can have the same radius and overlapping axial intervals after rotation. Adding their shell volumes separately then counts the overlap again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判定时比较“轴距”和“沿轴占据区间”，而不是只看原图中的两块区域是否分开。跨轴本身不一定造成重叠：相同半径处的轴向区间若互不相交，仍可相加。出现重叠时，可选一个足以生成立体的子区域，或对每个半径先求轴向区间的并集；也可改用实际截面的 [[圆盘垫圈法]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare distance to the axis and occupied axial intervals, not just whether the original planar pieces are separate. Crossing the axis does not itself imply overlap: disjoint axial intervals at the same radius can be added. When overlap occurs, choose a generating subregion, take the union of axial intervals at each radius, or use the [[圆盘垫圈法|disk and washer method]] on the actual cross sections.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $x^2\le y\le1$ 绕 $y$ 轴旋转。$x$ 与 $-x$ 的细条会生成同一壳层，故只取 $0\le x\le1$ 即得 $V=\pi/2$；如果对整个 $[-1,1]$ 使用非负半径，得到的是重复两次的体积：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x^2\le y\le1$ revolved about the $y$-axis, strips at $x$ and $-x$ generate the same shell. Taking $0\le x\le1$ gives $V=\pi/2$. Integrating a nonnegative radius over all of $[-1,1]$ instead counts the volume twice:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\int_{-1}^{1}2\pi|x|(1-x^2)\,dx=\pi\ne V.
$$

把 $|x|$ 改成 $x$ 会得到零，也不能修复问题：那只是正负抵消，负半径没有几何体积意义。去重必须在建立实际壳层时完成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replacing $|x|$ by $x$ gives zero and does not fix the problem. That creates cancellation, and a negative radius has no geometric volume meaning. Deduplication belongs in the construction of the physical shells.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
