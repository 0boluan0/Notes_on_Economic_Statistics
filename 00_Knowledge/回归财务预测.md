# 回归财务预测用历史项目与业务驱动的估计关系生成条件预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regression-based financial forecasting uses estimated historical relationships between financial items and business drivers to produce conditional forecasts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当一个项目随收入变化，却不适合假设始终占收入固定比例时，可以先估计两者的关系。例如用同一频率、可比会计口径的历史销售 $S_t$ 和存货 $I_t$ 拟合：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an item changes with sales but is not well described by a constant share, estimate their relationship. For example, use historical sales $S_t$ and inventory $I_t$ at matching frequency and on a comparable accounting basis:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$I_t=\alpha+\beta S_t+u_t,\qquad \widehat I_1=\widehat\alpha+\widehat\beta\,\widehat S_1.$$

[[一元 OLS 斜率]]解释怎样从样本求出斜率；这里的应用步骤是先估计参数，再代入预测销售。例如估计式为 $\widehat I=20+0.1S$，销售预测为 1,200 时，存货预测为 140。它允许一个截距项，而[[销售百分比法]]的固定存货收入比相当于把截距约束为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[一元 OLS 斜率|simple OLS slope]] explains estimation. This application estimates coefficients and then substitutes forecast sales. If the fitted equation is $\widehat I=20+0.1S$, sales of 1,200 imply inventory of 140. Unlike a constant inventory share under the [[销售百分比法|percentage-of-sales method]], it allows an intercept.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

截距不自动代表永不变化的最低库存，斜率也不自动代表销售的因果效应。产品组合、提前备货、供应链中断或共同趋势都可能影响估计；即使历史拟合很好，超出历史产能范围的外推仍需经营证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The intercept is not automatically a permanent minimum stock, nor is the slope automatically a causal sales effect. Product mix, advance stocking, supply disruptions, or common trends may affect estimates. Good historical fit does not validate extrapolation beyond the observed capacity range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

做事前预测时，未来销售通常也未知，需要[[收入驱动预测|预测销售]]或声明销售情景，不能在回测中偷用后来实现的销售而称为当时可执行；区别见[[事前与事后回归预测]]。若给出预测区间，还应检查它是否只是给定销售路径后的条件区间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Future sales are normally unknown in an ex-ante forecast, so [[收入驱动预测|forecast them]] or state a scenario. A backtest that substitutes subsequently realised sales is not a contemporaneously feasible forecast; see [[事前与事后回归预测|ex-ante versus ex-post prediction]]. Any interval should also disclose whether it is conditional on the sales path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
