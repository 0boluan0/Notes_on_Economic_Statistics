# 部门产出份额等于就业份额乘以相对总体的劳动生产率

产出份额问一个部门贡献了多少比例的增加值；就业份额问它容纳了多少比例的就业者。二者不相同：在一致口径下，每就业者增加值较高的部门，其产出份额可以明显高于就业份额，不能由前者推断多少劳动者已经进入该部门。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An output share measures a sector's share of value added; an employment share measures its share of employed people. They differ. On a consistent basis, a high-value-added-per-worker sector can have a much larger output share than employment share. The former does not reveal how many workers have moved into it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用[[劳动生产率加总]]，令 $v_i=Y_i/Y$、$s_i=L_i/L$、$p_i=Y_i/L_i$、$P=Y/L$。这里取各部门 $Y_i\ge0$、总体 $Y>0$、各 $L_i>0$，且价格与覆盖范围匹配，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using [[劳动生产率加总|labour-productivity aggregation]], define $v_i=Y_i/Y$, $s_i=L_i/L$, $p_i=Y_i/L_i$ and $P=Y/L$. With nonnegative sectoral value added, positive aggregate value added, positive sectoral employment, and matching prices and coverage,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
v_i=\frac{Y_i}{Y}
=\frac{L_i}{L}\frac{Y_i/L_i}{Y/L}
=s_i\frac{p_i}{P}.
$$

在就业份额 $(0.6,0.4)$、部门生产率 $(2,8)$ 的两部门例中，$P=4.4$。部门 B 只占 40% 就业，但产出份额为 $0.4\times8/4.4=8/11\approx72.7\%$。说它贡献了近四分之三产出，不等于说近四分之三就业者在该部门。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With employment shares $(0.6,0.4)$ and sectoral productivity $(2,8)$, aggregate productivity is 4.4. Sector B employs 40% of workers but accounts for $0.4\times8/4.4=8/11\approx72.7\%$ of output. Producing nearly three quarters of output is not employing nearly three quarters of workers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因而研究[[结构转型]]时，应把两类份额分别列出。现价产出份额还受相对价格变化影响；比较其时间变化，要标明是现价还是可比价，不能将份额变化直接解释为就业流动。生产率和就业份额同时变化时，总体结果由[[劳动生产率分解]]核算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Studies of [[结构转型|structural transformation]] should therefore report both shares separately. Current-price output shares also respond to relative prices. Specify current or comparable prices when comparing changes, and do not equate a changing output share with worker movement. When productivity and employment shares both change, use the [[劳动生产率分解|productivity decomposition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [McMillan、Rodrik、Verduzco-Gallo（2014）](https://drodrik.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum7106/files/dani-rodrik/files/globalization_structural_change_productivity_growth_with_africa_update.pdf#page=2)，印刷第12页：增加值与就业的部门口径及高生产率资源活动吸纳就业有限的讨论。份额恒等式和72.7%数例由定义独立推出，不作为特定国家统计。
- [OECD，行业劳动生产率分解方法说明](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/support-materials/2023/02/oecd-compendium-of-productivity-indicators-2023_bdbeba7d/oecd-compendium-of-productivity-indicators-2023-methodology.pdf#page=2)，第2–3页：增加值、劳动份额、现价与可比价格的对应定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
McMillan and colleagues distinguish value added from employment and discuss limited job absorption in productive resource activities. The identity and numerical shares are independent deductions, not country estimates. OECD supports the corresponding price and labour-share conventions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
