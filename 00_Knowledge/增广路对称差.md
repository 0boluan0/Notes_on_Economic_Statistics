# Augmenting-path flipping
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*沿增广路取对称差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[增广路定义|augmenting path]] tells us how to enlarge a current [[匹配定义|matching]] by replacing some selected edges, instead of only adding edges. On the path, remove the selected edges and insert the unselected ones; off the path, keep every decision unchanged. The result is a matching with **exactly one more edge**. The endpoint and simple-path conditions explain why this replacement is safe.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
[[增广路定义|增广路]]提供一种扩大当前[[匹配定义|匹配]]的方法：允许撤换部分已选边，而不只是不断加边。路径上原来选中的边撤下，原来未选中的边加入；路径外保持不变。结果仍是匹配，而且**恰好多一条边**。端点条件和简单路径条件，正是这种撤换不会产生冲突的原因。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One old pair becomes two
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把一对换成两对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the four distinct vertices $a,b,1,2$ again; the numbers are vertex labels. Let $P=b-1-a-2$, and write $E(P)$ for its set of three edges. The reassignment is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
仍取四个不同顶点 $a,b,1,2$，数字是顶点标签。令 $P=b-1-a-2$，用 $E(P)$ 表示它的三条边组成的集合。重配过程为：

$$
\{a-1\}\quad\longmapsto\quad\{b-1,a-2\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the path $b-1-a-2$, if $a-1$ is selected, the flip removes $a-1$ and inserts $b-1$ and $a-2$. One old assignment is replaced by two, increasing the matching size from one to two.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
在路径 $b-1-a-2$ 中，若 $a-1$ 已选，翻转会删除 $a-1$，加入 $b-1$ 与 $a-2$。一个原有配对被替换成两个配对，matching 大小从 $1$ 变成 $2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The old edge must be removed as part of the operation. Simply taking the union with both new edges would use vertices $a$ and $1$ twice. For a larger matching containing other edges disjoint from these four vertices, those other edges stay selected.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
操作必须包含撤下旧边这一步。如果只是把两条新边并入旧匹配，顶点 $a$ 与 $1$ 都会被重复使用。如果原匹配还含有与这四个顶点无关的其他边，那些边仍然保留。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Symmetric difference means toggling membership
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*对称差就是切换选中状态*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **symmetric difference** of two sets keeps the elements in exactly one of them and discards those in both. Applied to the current matching and the path edge set, it gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
两个集合的**对称差**只保留恰属于其中一个集合的元素，舍去同时属于两者的元素。把它用于当前匹配与路径边集，便得到

$$
M\mathbin{\triangle}E(P)
=\bigl(M\setminus E(P)\bigr)\cup\bigl(E(P)\setminus M\bigr).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\setminus$ means set difference: $M\setminus E(P)$ keeps the old selected edges outside the path; $E(P)\setminus M$ supplies the newly selected path edges. The symbol $\triangle$ is a set operation, not an arithmetic subtraction and not a triangle in the graph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里 $\setminus$ 表示集合差：$M\setminus E(P)$ 保留路径外的旧匹配边，$E(P)\setminus M$ 提供路径上新加入的边。符号 $\triangle$ 表示集合运算，不是算术减法，也不是图中的三角形。

> [!important] The flip theorem / 翻转命题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a finite simple undirected graph, fix a matching $M$ and an $M$-augmenting simple path $P$ of positive length, with distinct unmatched endpoints.
> If $P$ is an augmenting path relative to matching $M$, let
> $$
> M'=M\mathbin{\triangle}E(P).
> $$
> This removes the path edges formerly in $M$ and inserts those formerly outside $M$. The result $M'$ is still a matching, and $|M'|=|M|+1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 在有限简单无向图中，固定匹配 $M$，并取相对于 $M$ 的正长度简单增广路 $P$，其两个不同端点均未匹配。
> 若 $P$ 是相对于 matching $M$ 的 augmenting path，令
> $$
> M'=M\mathbin{\triangle}E(P).
> $$
> 这等于删去路径上原属 $M$ 的边，并加入路径上原不属 $M$ 的边。所得 $M'$ 仍是 matching，而且 $|M'|=|M|+1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check every vertex, including edges outside the path
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*逐个顶点检查，也检查路径外的边*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove that $M'$ is a matching, we must show that no vertex has two selected edges. Every vertex belongs to one of three cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
要证明 $M'$ 仍是匹配，必须说明每个顶点都不会接触两条已选边。全图的顶点分为三种情况。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Internal vertices.** Every internal vertex of the path is incident to one matched and one unmatched path edge. Flipping merely swaps which one is selected, so each internal vertex remains incident to exactly one selected edge. The two endpoints were unmatched and acquire only the first or last path edge, so no endpoint conflict is created.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**内部顶点。** 路径的每个内部顶点原本接触一条路径内 matching edge 和一条 non-matching edge；翻转后只是把“选中哪一条”交换，因此内部顶点仍只接一条选中边。两个端点原本未匹配，翻转后各接上第一条或最后一条边，也不会制造端点冲突。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There cannot be an additional old matching edge leading from an internal vertex to somewhere outside $P$: that vertex already has its one matching edge on $P$, and $M$ is a matching. Nor does the flip add any off-path edge. The simple-path condition means this vertex occupies only one position along $P$, so the check really accounts for all its path edges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
内部顶点也不可能另接一条通向路径外的旧匹配边：它已在路径内接触了一条 $M$ 中的边，而 $M$ 本来就是匹配。翻转也不会新增路径外的边。简单路径条件保证这个顶点只在路径中出现一次，因此上述检查确实涵盖了它的全部路径边。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**The two endpoints.** Each was unmatched in the whole graph, not merely along $P$. Each has one incident path edge, originally outside $M$, which becomes selected. There is no old selected edge anywhere at that endpoint to conflict with it. The endpoints are different, so they are two separate vertices receiving one edge each.

**Vertices outside the path.** No edge incident to such a vertex lies in $E(P)$, so none of its selected-edge decisions changes. It remains incident to at most one edge of $M'$.

These cases cover every vertex and prove that the new edge set is a matching. They do not require a bipartition of the graph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**两个端点。** 它们原先在全图中都未匹配，而不只是路径上未匹配。每个端点只接触一条路径边；这条边原来不在 $M$ 中，翻转后被选中。该端点在任何地方都没有旧匹配边与之冲突。两个端点不同，因此是两个各得到一条选中边的顶点。

**路径外的顶点。** 接触这类顶点的边不属于 $E(P)$，所以是否选中的状态都不改变；它们仍然至多接触一条 $M'$ 中的边。

这三种情况覆盖了全体顶点，证明新边集仍是匹配。论证不需要把图划分成左右两侧。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Count removed and inserted edges separately
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*分别数撤下和加入的边*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An augmenting path begins and ends with unmatched edges. Therefore, if it contains $k$ matched edges, it contains $k+1$ unmatched edges. The flip gains exactly one edge; this is a checkable counting fact, not merely an intuition that the new path looks better.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
augmenting path 以 non-matching edge 开始和结束，因此若其中有 $k$ 条 matching edges，就有 $k+1$ 条 non-matching edges。翻转净增加一条边，这不是“路径通常更好”的直觉，而是可逐边核对的计数结论。

$$
|M'|=|M|-k+(k+1)=|M|+1,\qquad k\ge0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The path is simple, so these are distinct edges rather than repeated traversals. For $k=0$, the path is one edge between two unmatched vertices: there are no internal vertices, and we just add that edge. In general, every previously matched vertex remains matched and exactly the two previously unmatched endpoints become matched. This also agrees with the fact that one extra matching edge covers two extra vertices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
由于路径简单，这里数的是互不相同的边，而不是重复走过同一条边的次数。$k=0$ 时，路径就是两个未匹配顶点之间的一条边，没有内部顶点，只需把该边加入。一般情况下，所有原先已匹配的顶点仍然匹配，恰好新增两个原先未匹配的端点；这也符合匹配每增加一条边就多覆盖两个顶点的计数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conclusion is one local improvement, not a claim that $M'$ is already [[极大与最大匹配|maximum]]. There may be another augmenting path for the new matching. Conversely, the inverse flip on the same edge set returns $M$; the endpoints are now matched, so the path is no longer augmenting relative to $M'$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
结论是完成一次局部改进，不是断言 $M'$ 已经是[[极大与最大匹配|最大匹配]]。相对于新匹配，可能还存在另一条增广路。反过来，再对同一边集翻转就会回到 $M$；此时两个端点都已匹配，所以同一路径相对于 $M'$ 已不再是增广路。

> [!question]- Where could a hidden conflict come from? / 回忆与辨别
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can no internal vertex acquire two selected edges? It exchanges its two path edges, one previously selected and one previously unselected, and it has no selected off-path edge because the original set was already a matching. Both parts of this check matter.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 为什么增广路翻转不会让内部顶点同时接触两条 matching edges？
>
> **答案：** 内部顶点在路径上恰接一条原 matching edge 与一条原 non-matching edge；翻转只交换两者的选中状态。
> 还要注意：原集合已是匹配，因此内部顶点不可能另接一条路径外的匹配边。
