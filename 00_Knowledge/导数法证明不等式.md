# Proving inequalities with derivatives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*导数法证明不等式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To compare two functions at every input in an interval, compare their **difference** with zero. A derivative tells you how that difference changes. If you know its value at one reference input and can prove it never falls below that value in the required direction, you have an inequality for the whole interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要比较两个函数在整个区间上的大小，可以先比较它们的**差**与零。导数告诉你这个差怎样变化。若知道某个基准输入处的差值，又能证明它沿所需方向不会降到这个值以下，就得到了覆盖整个区间的不等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a target $A(x)\ge B(x)$, define $F(x)=A(x)-B(x)$. Often the expected equality input $a$ is useful because $F(a)=0$. The [[导数单调性判据|derivative sign criterion]] then supplies the comparison. It follows from the [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]], which relates a change in function value to a derivative at an intermediate point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若目标是 $A(x)\ge B(x)$，定义 $F(x)=A(x)-B(x)$。预期的等号位置 $a$ 往往很好用，因为 $F(a)=0$。接着用[[导数单调性判据]]完成比较；它来自[[拉格朗日中值定理]]，把函数值的变化与中间某点的导数联系起来。

> [!important] Keep the direction of comparison · 保留比较的方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $F$ is continuous on the segment joining $a$ and $x$, and differentiable inside it. If $x>a$ and $F'\ge0$ throughout the interior, then $F(x)\ge F(a)$. If $x<a$ with the same derivative sign, the conclusion reverses: $F(x)\le F(a)$. To make $a$ a minimum, a useful sufficient pattern is $F'\le0$ to its left and $F'\ge0$ to its right. Each segment must lie in the domain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $F$ 在连接 $a$ 与 $x$ 的闭线段上连续、内部可导。若 $x>a$ 且内部处处 $F'\ge0$，则 $F(x)\ge F(a)$。若 $x<a$ 而导数仍取这个符号，结论反向为 $F(x)\le F(a)$。要让 $a$ 成为最小点，一个有用的充分模式是左侧 $F'\le0$、右侧 $F'\ge0$；每条比较线段都必须在定义域内。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the exponential lies above its tangent
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么指数函数位于切线上方*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We want $e^x\ge1+x$ for every real $x$. The [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]] is positive and strictly increasing, and its [[指数函数求导|derivative]] is itself. Set
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要证明对所有实数 $x$ 有 $e^x\ge1+x$。[[自然指数函数]]为正且严格递增，它的[[指数函数求导|导数]]就是自身。令

$$F(x)=e^x-1-x,\qquad F(0)=0,\qquad F'(x)=e^x-1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x>0$, the derivative is positive, so $F$ increases from zero and $F(x)>0$. For $x<0$, the derivative is negative: as the input moves rightwards towards zero, $F$ decreases to zero. Hence values on the left are also larger than $F(0)$. Equivalently, the mean value theorem gives $F(x)-F(0)=xF'(c)$ for some $c$ between $x$ and zero; when $x<0$, both factors are negative and their product is positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $x>0$ 时，导数为正，$F$ 从零向上增加，所以 $F(x)>0$。当 $x<0$ 时，导数为负：输入向右移向零时，$F$ 下降到零，因此左侧的函数值也大于 $F(0)$。也可以直接用中值定理：$F(x)-F(0)=xF'(c)$，其中 $c$ 在 $x$ 与零之间；当 $x<0$ 时，两因子都负，乘积反而为正。

$$e^x\ge1+x\quad(x\in\mathbb R),\qquad
e^x=1+x\ \Longleftrightarrow\ x=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The line $1+x$ is the tangent at zero, since the exponential has value one and slope one there. The proof establishes the comparison on the entire real line; a tangent-line approximation by itself is only local.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直线 $1+x$ 是零点处的切线，因为指数函数在那里取值为一、斜率为一。等号仅在 $x=0$ 成立。这个证明把大小比较建立在整条实轴上；单靠切线近似本身，只能提供局部信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reuse one inequality to prove a sharper one
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用已证明的不等式再推进一步*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x>0$, compare with the quadratic polynomial $1+x+x^2/2$. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $x>0$，进一步比较二次多项式 $1+x+x^2/2$。定义

$$H(x)=e^x-1-x-\frac{x^2}{2},\qquad
H(0)=0,\qquad H'(x)=e^x-1-x=F(x).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The previous proof gives $H'(x)>0$ for $x>0$. Therefore $H(x)>H(0)=0$, and $e^x>1+x+x^2/2$ on that half-line. This is an iterated derivative argument: the derivative of the harder difference is exactly a difference already controlled. Both steps retain a known value at zero, so no integration constant is lost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上一步已证明 $x>0$ 时 $H'(x)>0$，所以 $H(x)>H(0)=0$，得到该半轴上的 $e^x>1+x+x^2/2$。这就是重复求导论证：较难的差函数，其导数正好是已经控制住的差函数。每一步都保留零点处的已知值，不会漏掉常数信息。

> [!warning] A stronger bound may have a smaller domain · 更强的界可能只在更小范围成立
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The quadratic lower bound does not extend to negative $x$. There, $H'=F>0$, but comparison with the point zero gives $H(x)<H(0)=0$, because $x$ is to its left. Thus $e^x<1+x+x^2/2$ for $x<0$. Repeated differentiation does not remove the need to track the side of the reference point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 二次下界不能直接延伸到负 $x$。那里虽然仍有 $H'=F>0$，但 $x$ 位于零的左侧，因此与零点比较得到 $H(x)<H(0)=0$。也就是说，$x<0$ 时反而有 $e^x<1+x+x^2/2$。重复求导并不会免除跟踪基点左右方向的要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A logarithmic comparison uses a different reference input
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*对数比较要换一个基准输入*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove $\ln x\le x-1$, first impose $x>0$, the domain of the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]]. The equality input is one. With $G(x)=x-1-\ln x$, we have $G(1)=0$ and, by the [[对数函数求导|derivative of the logarithm]],
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明 $\ln x\le x-1$ 时，先要求 $x>0$，这是[[自然对数]]的定义域。等号位置为一。令 $G(x)=x-1-\ln x$，则 $G(1)=0$，并由[[对数函数求导]]得到

$$G'(x)=1-\frac1x=\frac{x-1}{x}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is negative on $(0,1)$ and positive on $(1,\infty)$. Hence $G$ decreases to its minimum zero at one, then increases. It follows that $\ln x\le x-1$ for every positive $x$, with equality only at $x=1$. Zero is a boundary of the domain and cannot be substituted as a reference input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

导数在 $(0,1)$ 为负，在 $(1,\infty)$ 为正。因此 $G$ 先递减到输入一处的最小值零，再递增。于是对每个正 $x$ 有 $\ln x\le x-1$，且仅在 $x=1$ 取等。零是定义域边界，不能拿来代入作为基点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the proof must still establish
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*证明还必须交代什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A derivative sign gives change, not an absolute height: $F(x)=x-100$ increases but remains negative for $x<100$. That is why a known reference value matters. The single equation $F'(a)=0$ likewise does not establish a minimum. For a weak sign such as $F'\ge0$, equality can persist on a flat interval; strictness or a unique equality input requires an additional argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

导数符号给出变化方向，不给出绝对高度：$F(x)=x-100$ 虽递增，却在 $x<100$ 时仍为负，所以需要一个已知的基准值。单独的 $F'(a)=0$ 同样不能证明最小值。若只有 $F'\ge0$ 这样的非严格符号，等号可能在一段平坦区间上持续成立；严格性或唯一等号点需要额外论证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When absolute values or denominators split the domain, use a valid comparison segment on each part and check included boundary points separately. If signs alone are too weak, [[导数界控制函数差|bounds on the derivative]] can instead bound the size of $F(x)-F(a)$. After the argument, translate the conclusion back from the difference to the original two functions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若绝对值或分母使定义域需要分段，就在每一部分使用合法的比较线段，并另查实际包含的边界点。若仅靠符号不够，可以用[[导数界控制函数差|导数的上下界]]进一步控制 $F(x)-F(a)$ 的大小。论证完毕后，还要把差函数的结论翻译回原来两个函数的不等式。
