# 导数法证明不等式把两边之差锚定在基准点并用导数符号控制其余区间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The derivative method proves an inequality by anchoring the difference at a base point and controlling it over the interval through derivative signs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子方法
> 要证明 $A(x)\ge B(x)$，定义差函数 $F(x)=A(x)-B(x)$；找一个容易确认 $F(a)$ 的锚点；在目标区间上用 $F'$ 的符号、导数界或重复求导证明 $F(x)\ge F(a)$；最后检查等号、严格性与定义域。目标不是机械求导，而是把比较转成单调性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To prove $A(x)\ge B(x)$, form $F=A-B$, choose a base point where $F$ is known, and use the sign of $F'$, derivative bounds, or an iterated derivative argument to show that $F(x)\ge F(a)$ on the target interval. Then identify equality, strictness, and domain conditions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明 $e^x\ge1+x$ 可取
$$
F(x)=e^x-1-x,\qquad F(0)=0.
$$
因为 $F'(x)=e^x-1$ 在 $x>0$ 为正、在 $x<0$ 为负，$F$ 从左侧下降到零点、再向右上升，所以 $0$ 是全局最小点，结论对所有实数成立且仅在 $x=0$ 取等号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $e^x\ge1+x$, use $F(x)=e^x-1-x$. Its derivative is negative to the left of zero and positive to the right, so zero is the global minimum and equality occurs only there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只在一个点看到 $F'(a)=0$ 不够；只证明 $F'>0$ 在 $x>0$ 也不能自动覆盖负半轴。严格不等式还要排除其他零点。遇到绝对值、分母或对数时，应先拆定义域和符号分支，再在每个连通区间使用方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The single fact $F'(a)=0$ is insufficient, and a sign argument on the positive half-line says nothing about the negative half-line. Strict inequalities require ruling out other zeros. Absolute values, denominators, and logarithms should be split into their valid connected regions before differentiation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么选差函数时常把希望的等号点作为锚点？
>
> **答案：** 此时差函数值通常为零；只需证明它从该点向目标方向不下降，便同时得到不等式和等号条件。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why is the intended equality point often a useful anchor for the difference function?
>
> **Answer:** The difference is usually zero there. Showing that it does not decrease as we move from that point in the relevant direction establishes the inequality and locates the equality case.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
