# Independent branches across an undefined input
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方程未定义处分支独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An equation cannot connect two parts of a trajectory through an input at which the equation is not defined. For $y'=2y/x$, the input zero is excluded for every state $y$. We can solve on the negative half-line and on the positive half-line separately. Those two problems can carry different constants, even when their graphs fit together smoothly at zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个方程不能在自身没有定义的输入处，把轨迹的两部分连接起来。对 $y'=2y/x$，无论状态 $y$ 是多少，输入零都被排除。因此可以分别在负半轴与正半轴求解，两侧问题可以带有不同常数，即使图像在零点能够光滑地拼接。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[常微分方程|classical ODE solution]] must be differentiable and satisfy the original equation at every input in its stated interval. If that interval contains zero, the expression $2y(0)/0$ has no value. Choosing $y(0)=0$ does not help: $0/0$ is still undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[常微分方程|通常意义的微分方程解]]必须可导，而且原方程在所报区间内的每个输入处都有定义并成立。若区间包含零，表达式 $2y(0)/0$ 没有值；即使选择 $y(0)=0$，$0/0$ 仍然未定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Find all solutions on each side
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*分别求出每一侧的全部解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On an interval with $x\ne0$, [[分离变量|separation]] suggests $y=Ax^2$. To check completeness without losing $y=0$, use the [[商法则|quotient rule]] on any solution:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在满足 $x\ne0$ 的区间上，[[分离变量]]提示 $y=Ax^2$。为了不漏掉 $y=0$，可以对任意解用[[商法则]]直接检查完整性：

$$
\left(\frac{y}{x^2}\right)'=
\frac{x^2y'-2xy}{x^4}=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By [[原函数相差常数|zero derivative on an interval]], $y/x^2$ is constant on each interval. Thus the maximal half-line solutions are $y=A_-x^2$ for $x<0$ and $y=A_+x^2$ for $x>0$, where $A_-$ and $A_+$ are arbitrary real constants. An [[通解与初值|initial value]] on one side fixes its constant, not the other one: $y(1)=2$ fixes $A_+=2$ but says nothing about $A_-$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[原函数相差常数|区间上零导数意味着常数]]，$y/x^2$ 在每个区间上是常数。因此两条最大半轴解分别为：$x<0$ 时 $y=A_-x^2$，$x>0$ 时 $y=A_+x^2$；$A_-,A_+$ 是任意实数。一侧的[[通解与初值|初值]]只确定该侧常数：例如 $y(1)=2$ 确定 $A_+=2$，却没有说明 $A_-$ 是多少。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A differentiable patch still does not solve the missing equation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拼接函数可导，仍不能补上方程的缺失*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define a single function by filling in zero:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在补上零点，定义一个单一函数：

$$
\widetilde y(x)=\begin{cases}A_-x^2,&x<0,\\0,&x=0,\\A_+x^2,&x>0.\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both one-sided values tend to zero. The derivative at zero is also zero, since $(\widetilde y(h)-\widetilde y(0))/h=A_\pm h\to0$ from either side. The derivatives $2A_\pm x$ tend to zero too, so the patch is continuously differentiable for every pair of constants. Taking $A_-=1$ and $A_+=3$ gives a concrete pair with different curvatures and the same value and slope at zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两侧函数值都趋于零。零点导数也为零，因为差商 $(\widetilde y(h)-\widetilde y(0))/h=A_\pm h$ 从两侧都趋零；两侧导数 $2A_\pm x$ 也趋于零。因此任意一对常数都给出连续可导的拼接函数。具体取 $A_-=1$、$A_+=3$，就得到两侧弯曲不同、零点处值与斜率却相同的例子。

> [!warning] Smoothness does not define the right-hand side · 光滑性不会替右端补定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The patch solves $y'=2y/x$ for every $x\ne0$, but not on an interval containing zero: the original right-hand side is undefined there. It is a pair of solutions on disconnected intervals, with an extra chosen fill-in. The [[微分方程最大区间|maximal intervals]] of the original ODE remain $(-\infty,0)$ and $(0,\infty)$, even if $A_-=A_+$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 拼接函数在每个 $x\ne0$ 处都满足 $y'=2y/x$，却不是包含零的区间上的解，因为原右端在那里无定义。它是两个断开区间上的解，再加一个另行选择的补值。原微分方程的[[微分方程最大区间]]仍分别是 $(-\infty,0)$ 与 $(0,\infty)$，即使 $A_-=A_+$ 也一样。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Multiplying by x changes what is required at zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*乘以 x 会改变零点处要求满足的方程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x\ne0$, the equation is equivalent to $xy'=2y$. If the latter is instead declared on all real inputs, it becomes a different problem at zero: there it requires $0=2y(0)$. Every patch above satisfies this extended equation, including the joining point. That is a valid statement about the new equation, not an extension of the undefined quotient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $x\ne0$，原方程等价于 $xy'=2y$。如果另行把后一个方程定义在全部实数上，零点处便是一个不同的问题：它要求 $0=2y(0)$。上面的每个拼接函数都满足这个扩展后的方程，包括拼接点。这是关于新方程的合法结论，不是把未定义的商自动延拓了。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Additional matching conditions can relate the two sides. For example, requiring a second derivative at zero forces $2A_-=2A_+$, so the constants agree. That restriction comes from the extra smoothness requirement. Neither the original ODE on $x\ne0$ nor merely requiring a continuous first derivative imposes it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

额外的匹配条件可以联系两侧。例如，若要求零点处二阶导数存在，就必须有 $2A_-=2A_+$，于是常数相同。这个限制来自额外的光滑性要求，而不是原来只在 $x\ne0$ 成立的方程；只要求一阶导数连续也不会施加该限制。

## Separate a missing equation from failure of uniqueness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*区分方程缺失与唯一性失效*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The defined equation $y'=3|y|^{2/3}$ has distinct solutions through $(0,0)$; that is a genuine failure of uniqueness for allowed initial data. By contrast, $y'=2y/x$ has no ordinary initial-value problem at $x=0$ under its original domain. It fails before uniqueness can be asked there. See [[通解与初值|initial data and uniqueness]] for that distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有定义的方程 $y'=3|y|^{2/3}$ 在 $(0,0)$ 有不同解，这才是合法初值处真正的唯一性失效。相比之下，按原定义域，$y'=2y/x$ 根本不能在 $x=0$ 提出通常的初值问题；问题在询问唯一性之前就已经出现。这个区别见[[通解与初值]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, an excluded curve in the input–state plane blocks only trajectories that try to pass through it. It does not automatically split every solution at the same input. The independent half-line conclusion above uses the specific fact that $x=0$ is excluded for every $y$. Always state the equation's domain and any separate matching conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般地，输入—状态平面中被排除的一条曲线，只阻碍试图穿过它的轨迹，不会自动在同一个输入处切开所有解。上面两半轴独立的结论，用到了“对每个 $y$，$x=0$ 都被排除”这一具体事实。始终写明原方程定义域，以及是否另有匹配条件。
