# 嵌入期权改变现金流分支所以普通到期收益率差不能单独解释信用或持有回报

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Embedded options change the cash-flow branches, so a plain YTM difference cannot by itself identify credit compensation or holding return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通[[到期收益率]]用一组承诺到期现金流把价格压成一个内部收益率。赎回、回售或转股可能使这组现金流被另一条分支替代；同信用、同到期日的两只债券因此可以因期权条款不同而有不同价格和普通 YTM。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Plain [[到期收益率|yield to maturity]] compresses price into an internal rate for promised maturity cash flows. A call, put, or conversion can replace that schedule with another branch. Two bonds with the same credit and maturity can consequently have different prices and plain YTMs because of optionality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如面值 100、两年期年息票 5 的债券，未赎回时未来支付为一年后 5、两年后 105；若允许一年后付息并按 100 赎回，则被赎回分支只有一年后的 105。使用前一组现金流求得的 YTM，不是后一分支必然实现的回报。可转债的股票交付分支也不在普通息票加本金的 YTM 方程里。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a two-year bond with face value 100 and annual coupon 5, the uncalled schedule pays 5 in one year and 105 in two years. If it can be called at 100 after the first coupon, the called branch instead pays only 105 in one year. The first schedule's YTM is not a guaranteed return on the second branch. Likewise, stock delivery upon conversion is absent from a plain coupon-and-principal YTM equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分析时先写允许的行权日期和各分支支付。赎回收益率（YTC）针对指定赎回日及赎回价格计算内部收益率；按报价规则计算的最差收益率（YTW）再比较相应到期／赎回分支。它们仍是条件报价，不是包含违约、再投资和所有市场路径的无条件收益下限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First identify permitted exercise dates and branch payments. Yield to call (YTC) is an internal rate for a specified call date and price; yield to worst (YTW) compares the relevant maturity and call scenarios under its quotation rules. These remain conditional measures, not unconditional return floors covering default, reinvestment, and every market path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要比较信用，需要用相容模型处理可选性，而不只是减去同期限基准 YTM。期权调整利差（OAS）也依赖所用利率路径、波动率和行权模型，不是自动观察到的纯信用补偿。ASW、Z-spread 与基准收益率差的不同口径见[[债券CDS基差]]，不可混报。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Credit comparison requires a consistent treatment of optionality, not merely subtraction of a matched benchmark YTM. An option-adjusted spread (OAS) depends on rate paths, volatility, and exercise modeling; it is not directly observed pure credit compensation. For distinct ASW, Z-spread, and benchmark-yield conventions, see [[债券CDS基差|bond–CDS basis conventions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
