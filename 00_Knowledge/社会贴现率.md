# 社会贴现率是换算不同时点社会效益与成本所采用的贴现率

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A social discount rate is the rate used to translate social benefits and costs across dates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

社会贴现率是在公共项目评价中，把不同时点的社会效益与成本换算到共同评价时点所用的率。它回答跨期社会价值如何比较，不只是政府借款要付多少利息。采用何种率，需要说明评价视角、消费或资本机会成本依据、时间跨度及风险处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A social discount rate translates public-project social benefits and costs to a common appraisal date. It governs intertemporal social comparisons, not merely the government's borrowing bill. Its choice requires an explicit appraisal perspective, consumption or capital-opportunity rationale, horizon and treatment of risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选率的两类经济依据见[[社会时间偏好率]]与[[社会资本机会成本]]，代际含义见[[社会贴现与代际评价]]。确定口径后，现值运算直接复用[[现金流同点折现加总]]与[[期限匹配贴现因子]]；实际社会价值配实际率，名义金额配名义率，遵守[[名义与实际贴现]]，不能把通胀与时间价值当成同一调整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two economic rationales are [[社会时间偏好率|social time preference]] and the [[社会资本机会成本|social opportunity cost of capital]]; see [[社会贴现与代际评价|intergenerational appraisal]] for the ethical implications. Once the basis is chosen, reuse [[现金流同点折现加总|same-date present-value aggregation]] and [[期限匹配贴现因子|maturity-matched discount factors]]. Match real values with real rates and nominal values with nominal rates under [[名义与实际贴现|nominal–real consistency]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如仅为演示，给定一年实际社会净收益103元与匹配实际率3%，现值为100元。这个计算没有证明3%适用于该项目，也没有证明现值为正便足以决定实施；选率依据与分配、风险和不可货币化影响仍须分别交代。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As an illustration, a real social net benefit of 103 in one year has present value 100 at a matching real rate of 3%. The arithmetic neither establishes that 3% is appropriate nor makes positive present value a complete implementation decision. The rate's basis, distribution, risk and unmonetised effects remain separate considerations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

The supplement supports intertemporal social valuation, real-value consistency and scope limitations. Its UK prescribed rates are not treated as universal constants; the example is independently constructed.
