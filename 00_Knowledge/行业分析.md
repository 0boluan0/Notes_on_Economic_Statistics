# 行业分析研究行业需求、供给与竞争结构如何影响经营和盈利
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Industry analysis studies how industry demand, supply, and competitive structure affect operations and profitability.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行业分析以界定后的[[行业]]为对象，研究需求规模与变化、供给能力及成本、竞争者与交易对手的关系，从而解释经营状况和盈利条件。它连接宏观环境与公司分析：同一利率或技术变化，可能通过不同的客户需求、融资和成本渠道影响不同行业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industry analysis examines demand, capacity, costs, competitors, and trading relationships within a defined [[行业|industry]] to explain operating conditions and profitability. It connects the macroeconomy to company analysis: the same interest-rate or technological change can affect industries through different demand, financing, and cost channels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，订单增加而产能过剩，可能带来销量增长却没有提价空间；只报告“需求旺盛”还没有解释利润。[[波特五力]]分析价值如何被竞争各方分配，[[行业景气指标]]观察阶段性变化；两者不能直接推出某只股票值得买，仍需公司现金流和价格判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, orders may rise while excess capacity prevents price increases: stronger demand alone does not explain profit. [[波特五力|Porter's five forces]] examines how competing parties capture value, while [[行业景气指标|industry activity indicators]] track cyclical conditions. Neither alone establishes that a stock is worth buying; company cash flows and price still matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
