# 实施失败与理论失败分别质疑交付是否实现和因果链是否成立

实施失败是指关键交付没有达到设计要求，例如目标对象没有接触到服务、剂量不足或关键操作未执行。理论失败则指：在明确的干预版本、交付和情境条件下，所主张的因果环节或预期结果并不成立。两者质疑的对象不同，需要不同证据；不能从“最终结果没有显著改善”直接二选一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Implementation failure concerns a failure to deliver essential features as intended, such as reach, dose or required activities. Theory failure concerns a proposed causal link or outcome that does not hold for the specified intervention, delivery and context. These diagnoses concern different objects and require different evidence; a statistically insignificant final outcome does not choose between them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先按[[变革理论]]找出被检验的具体主张，再用[[过程评价]]核实际交付、参与者反应和情境。若辅导课程根本未开足，证据首先指向交付偏离；它不能据此否定“足量、合适辅导会改善学习”的主张。不过，实际实施版本的低效果仍可能是政策选择的重要证据，不能以“实施不到位”为由把它全部丢掉。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First identify the claim in the [[变革理论|theory of change]], then use [[过程评价|process evaluation]] to examine delivery, responses and context. Undelivered tutoring sessions first indicate a delivery gap, not a refutation of an adequate tutoring intervention. Nevertheless, weak effects of the version actually implemented can be highly relevant to policy; implementation problems are not grounds for discarding those findings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使交付充分，也要判断是否观察了恰当的结果和时间、比较是否可信、估计是否足够精确，以及不同人群或正负通道是否在平均值中抵消。只有相关因果主张受到有辨别力的证据挑战，才应收窄或修订它；不能从一个地区的一次结果宣布整类政策理论普遍失效。研究设计检查复用[[因果研究设计检查]]，统计判断复用[[显著性解释边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even with adequate delivery, check the outcome and horizon, credibility of the comparison, precision, and possible averaging of heterogeneous or offsetting effects. Revise a causal claim when discriminating evidence challenges it; one result does not refute an entire class of policies everywhere. Reuse [[因果研究设计检查|the causal-design audit]] and [[显著性解释边界|the significance interpretation boundary]] for those judgments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造例中，假设交付已经确认，研究设计支持所报告的因果比较，采用有效的正态近似推断。学习效果估计为0.02个标准差，标准误0.10；95%区间约为 $0.02\pm1.96\times0.10=[-0.176,0.216]$。若事先认为0.10个标准差有实际意义，这个区间仍容许重要正效果。此时不能把未拒绝零效应解释为理论失败；也不能仅凭区间给实施贴标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed example, delivery is confirmed, the design supports the causal contrast, and valid normal-approximation inference is assumed. An effect of 0.02 standard deviations with standard error 0.10 gives a 95% interval of approximately $[-0.176,0.216]$. If 0.10 was prespecified as meaningful, important positive effects remain compatible with the data. Non-rejection therefore establishes neither theory failure nor an implementation diagnosis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不是穷尽所有原因的分类树。实施偏离、情境不匹配和理论缺陷可能并存；测量缺失、观察过早或评价设计不可行也可能使结论暂时不明。交付后的中间变量与结果相关，同样不自动证明该变量是因果机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The distinction is not an exhaustive classification tree. Delivery gaps, contextual mismatch and weaknesses in theory can coexist; missing measurements, premature follow-up or an infeasible evaluation design can leave the answer unresolved. A post-delivery association between an intermediate variable and the outcome also does not by itself establish a causal mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [HM Treasury，Magenta Book（2026）](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/magenta-book-central-government-guidance-on-evaluation-html)，§2.2.3（PDF第51–53页）、理论型影响评价（第59页）、§3.5.1（第83–84页）：支持以过程证据解释设计或交付问题、检验替代解释，以及按可识别的有意义效应考虑精度。
- [MRC，Process evaluation of complex interventions（2015，2026无障碍版）](https://www.ukri.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/MRC-210726-ProcessEvaluationComplexInterventionsFullGuidance.pdf)，印刷第22–26、64–66页，PDF第23–27、67–69页：支持交付是否真正检验了干预理论、实施与评价失败的区别，以及机制和情境的联合检查。本卡区间数例独立构造；未显著不等于无效的统计结论直接复用已有原子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Magenta Book supports process evidence, alternative explanations and precision. MRC guidance distinguishes delivery from evaluation problems and relates implementation to theory and context. The interval example is constructed; the statistical non-rejection boundary is reused rather than redefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
