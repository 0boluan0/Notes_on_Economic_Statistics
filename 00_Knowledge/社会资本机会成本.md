# 社会资本机会成本衡量公共项目挤出的替代资本用途所放弃的社会回报

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The social opportunity cost of capital measures the social return forgone when a public project displaces an alternative capital use.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

社会资本机会成本是公共项目占用资金和资源、挤出其他资本用途时，被放弃的替代用途的社会回报。把它用于[[社会贴现率]]选择，必须先说明公共融资究竟挤出了什么：私人投资、当前消费或其他用途，不能只因政府借款便假定全部来自某一类资本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The social opportunity cost of capital is the social return forgone when public investment displaces alternative capital uses. Applying it to [[社会贴现率|social discount-rate]] choice requires identifying what financing displaces—private investment, current consumption or other uses—rather than assuming every government loan draws entirely on one capital source.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个明确的单期例：公共项目占用100元，恰好使一个原本可行的私人项目不能实施。假设该私人项目一年后产生110元实际社会资源，且无外部效应或其他扭曲，其放弃的社会净回报为10%。若其中4元原本以税收转给政府，投资者税后只得106元，但社会资源仍为110元；把6%的税后投资者回报当成全部社会回报，会漏掉政府账户，见[[转移支付核算]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an explicit one-period example, a public project uses 100 and exactly displaces a feasible private project producing 110 in real social resources next year, with no externalities or other distortions. The forgone net social return is 10%. If 4 would have been transferred to government as tax, the investor receives 106 but social resources remain 110. Treating the 6% investor return as the whole social return omits the government account; see [[转移支付核算|transfer accounting]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不是“任何项目都用税前市场率”的定理：资本风险、外部效应、融资构成及被挤出消费的[[社会时间偏好率]]都会影响口径。政府借款利率是融资价格；只有在进一步条件下，它才等于这项社会机会成本，不能仅凭利息账单作替代。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is not a theorem prescribing a pretax market rate for every project. Capital risk, externalities, financing composition and the [[社会时间偏好率|social time preference]] relevant to displaced consumption affect the basis. Government borrowing rates are financing prices and equal this social opportunity cost only under additional conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
