# 贝叶斯纳什均衡要求每名玩家的每个类型都在自身事中信念下作最佳回应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Bayesian Nash equilibrium requires every type of every player to best respond under that type's own interim belief*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个[[贝叶斯策略|贝叶斯策略]]组合构成**贝叶斯纳什均衡**，当且仅当每名玩家的每个类型都以自己的[[事中信念]]计算期望收益，并且相对于其他玩家的类型条件策略没有有利偏离。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A profile of [[贝叶斯策略|Bayesian strategies]] is a **Bayesian Nash equilibrium** if and only if every type of every player evaluates expected payoff under its own [[事中信念|interim belief]] and has no profitable deviation against the other players' type-contingent strategies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Interim 定义

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Interim Definition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对纯策略组合 $s^*$，BNE 要求对每名玩家 $i$、每个 $t_i\in T_i$ 和每个偏离行动 $a_i\in A_i$，

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a pure-strategy profile $s^*$, BNE requires that, for every player $i$, every $t_i\in T_i$, and every deviating action $a_i\in A_i$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E_{p_i(\cdot\mid t_i)}
\left[
u_i\!\left(\theta,s_i^*(t_i),s_{-i}^*(t_{-i})\right)
\right]\\
&\qquad\ge
E_{p_i(\cdot\mid t_i)}
\left[
u_i\!\left(\theta,a_i,s_{-i}^*(t_{-i})\right)
\right].
\end{aligned}
$$

有限模型中，左侧可展开为

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a finite model, the left-hand side can be expanded as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sum_{(\theta,t_{-i})}
u_i\!\left(\theta,s_i^*(t_i),s_{-i}^*(t_{-i})\right)
p_i(\theta,t_{-i}\mid t_i).
$$

若类型[[相关类型|相关]]，权重必须随 $t_i$ 改变；若使用混合行动策略，则再对各方行动随机化取期望。[[共同先验]]能统一生成这些条件 beliefs，但不是 BNE 定义所必需。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inequality compares the candidate action with every deviation while holding the other players' complete type-contingent strategies fixed. Under [[相关类型|correlated types]], the weights vary with $t_i$; [[相关类型下的事中期望收益|interim expected payoff]] gives the corresponding sum or integral. With mixed-action strategies, the expectation also covers every player's action randomization. A [[共同先验|common prior]] can generate these conditional beliefs jointly, but it is not required by the definition of BNE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么必须逐类型检查

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the Condition Must Be Checked Type by Type*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个 $t_i$，依次做三步：

1. 固定候选策略中其他玩家的映射 $s_{-i}^*(t_{-i})$；
2. 用 $p_i(\theta,t_{-i}\mid t_i)$ 计算当前规定行动与每个偏离的期望收益；
3. 验证 $s_i^*(t_i)$ 属于该类型的[[最佳反应]]集合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each $t_i$, proceed in three steps:

&nbsp;

**1.** Fix the other players' mappings $s_{-i}^*(t_{-i})$ in the candidate profile.<br>
**2.** Use $p_i(\theta,t_{-i}\mid t_i)$ to calculate the expected payoff from the prescribed action and from every deviation.<br>
**3.** Verify that $s_i^*(t_i)$ belongs to that type's [[最佳反应|best-response]] set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

某一类型的损失不能由另一类型的高收益“平均抵消”。只要存在一个类型愿意偏离，候选就不是 interim BNE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Payoff gains for one type cannot average away a profitable deviation by another type. If any type can profitably deviate, the strategy profile fails the interim BNE test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与事前 Nash 的关系

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Relation to Ex Ante Nash Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限模型中，逐类型事中条件与事前期望最优在什么条件下等价、零概率类型为何会使两者分开，见[[零概率类型的BNE边界]]。连续类型还要明确最佳反应是对几乎所有类型成立，还是逐类型成立，见[[连续类型BNE口径]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
See [[零概率类型的BNE边界|the zero-probability boundary of BNE]] for the finite-type conditions under which interim optimality agrees with ex-ante Nash and why null types can separate the two notions. A continuous model must additionally choose between almost-everywhere and pointwise best-response requirements; see [[连续类型BNE口径|continuous-type BNE conventions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 检查一个候选 BNE 时，为什么不能先把所有自身类型的收益加权平均，再只检查这一项平均收益？
>
> **答案：** 因为 BNE 的事中定义逐类型施加最佳回应条件。平均值可能掩盖某个类型的有利偏离；只要一个类型愿意偏离，候选就不是 BNE。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check.** When checking a candidate BNE, why can we not first take a weighted average of payoffs across all own types and then test only that average payoff?
>
> **Answer:** The interim definition of BNE imposes the best-response condition type by type. An average can conceal a profitable deviation by one type; if even one type wants to deviate, the candidate is not a BNE.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
