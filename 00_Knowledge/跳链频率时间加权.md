# Weighting jump visits by holding times
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用停留时间把跳链访问比例换成日历时间占比*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A machine that visits two states equally often need not spend equal time in them. The [[嵌入跳链|embedded jump chain]] counts each arrival once; a clock keeps counting throughout the following stay. To convert visit proportions into time proportions, weight each visit by its [[CTMC指数停留时间|mean holding time]], then normalize the resulting amounts of time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一台机器对两个状态的访问次数相同，不代表在两者停留的时间相同。[[嵌入跳链]]把每次到达记一次；时钟则在接下来的整个停留期间继续计数。把访问比例换成时间占比，需要先乘以每次的[[CTMC指数停留时间|平均停留时间]]，再把这些时长归一化。

> [!example] Equal visits, unequal time · 访问次数相同，停留时间不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose genuine jumps alternate $0\to1\to0\to\cdots$, with exit rates $q_0=2$, $q_1=1$ per hour. Half the visits are to each state, but the mean stays are $1/2$ and $1$ hour. The time weights are therefore $(\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac12,\tfrac12\cdot1)=(1/4,1/2)$. Dividing by their sum $3/4$ gives calendar-time proportions $(1/3,2/3)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若真实跳跃按 $0\to1\to0\to\cdots$ 交替，出口率为每小时 $q_0=2$、$q_1=1$，两个状态各占一半访问次数，但平均停留分别为 $1/2$、$1$ 小时。时间权重因此是 $(1/4,1/2)$，除以总和 $3/4$ 后，日历时间占比为 $(1/3,2/3)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The formula in a finite irreducible class

Consider a finite [[跳链决定CTMC沟通类|irreducible class]] with at least two states, with no exits from that class. Use a time-homogeneous CTMC with generator $Q=(q_{ij})$ and positive finite [[出口率|exit rates]] $q_i=-q_{ii}$. The genuine-jump matrix is $R=(r_{ij})$, where $r_{ij}=q_{ij}/q_i$ for $j\ne i$ and $r_{ii}=0$.

Let the probability row $\alpha=(\alpha_i)$ be the jump chain's [[Markov稳态分布|stationary distribution]]: $\alpha R=\alpha$, $\alpha_i\ge0$ and $\sum_i\alpha_i=1$. Its long-run fraction of visits to $i$ is $\alpha_i$, even if the jump chain is periodic. Each such visit lasts mean $1/q_i$, so set
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有限不可约类上的公式*

考虑一个至少含两个状态、没有通往类外出口的有限[[跳链决定CTMC沟通类|不可约类]]。CTMC 时间齐次，生成矩阵为 $Q=(q_{ij})$，[[出口率]] $q_i=-q_{ii}$ 正且有限。真实跳链矩阵为 $R=(r_{ij})$，其中 $r_{ij}=q_{ij}/q_i$（$j\ne i$）、$r_{ii}=0$。

令概率行 $\alpha=(\alpha_i)$ 是跳链的[[Markov稳态分布|平稳分布]]：$\alpha R=\alpha$、$\alpha_i\ge0$、$\sum_i\alpha_i=1$。即使跳链有周期，长期访问 $i$ 的次数比例仍是 $\alpha_i$。每次这样的访问平均停留 $1/q_i$，所以令

$$Z=\sum_j\frac{\alpha_j}{q_j},\qquad
\pi_i=\frac{\alpha_i/q_i}{Z}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $Z$ is the mean duration per jump when visits have stationary proportions. The index $j$ runs over the class. It is positive and finite in this finite setting. The normalized row $\pi$ is the CTMC's [[CTMC平稳分布|stationary distribution]], and also its long-run time allocation. Write $X_s$ for the state at calendar time $s$. More precisely, for any start in the class,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $Z$ 是访问按平稳比例出现时，每跳平均对应的时长，$j$ 遍历这个类。在有限情形中，$Z$ 正且有限。归一化后的 $\pi$ 是 CTMC 的[[CTMC平稳分布|平稳分布]]，也给出长期时间占比。记 $X_s$ 为日历时刻 $s$ 的状态。准确地说，从类内任意状态出发，

$$\frac1t\int_0^t\mathbf1_{\{X_s=i\}}\,ds\ \longrightarrow\ \pi_i
\qquad\text{almost surely as }t\to\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[事件指示变量|indicator]] is one while the path is in $i$ and zero otherwise; its integral adds the time spent there. This path average differs from the distribution of the jump chain at one specific step, which can keep oscillating in a periodic chain. For general rates $q_0=\lambda>0$ and $q_1=\mu>0$ in the alternating example, the same calculation gives $\pi=(\mu,\lambda)/(\lambda+\mu)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[事件指示变量|指示函数]]在路径处于 $i$ 时等于一，否则等于零；积分把处于该状态的时长加起来。这种路径平均不同于跳链在某一个指定步数的分布，后者在周期链中可能一直振荡。交替例若采用一般正速率 $q_0=\lambda$、$q_1=\mu$，同样得到 $\pi=(\mu,\lambda)/(\lambda+\mu)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Verify the rate balance

The weighting also has an exact algebraic explanation. For a destination $j$, incoming probability per unit time under $\pi$ is $\sum_{i\ne j}\pi_iq_{ij}$; outgoing probability per unit time is $\pi_jq_j$. Since $r_{jj}=0$ and $\alpha R=\alpha$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*直接核对速率平衡*

这个加权还有准确的代数解释。对目标状态 $j$，分配 $\pi$ 下单位时间流入的概率为 $\sum_{i\ne j}\pi_iq_{ij}$，流出为 $\pi_jq_j$。利用 $r_{jj}=0$ 与 $\alpha R=\alpha$，

$$\sum_{i\ne j}\pi_iq_{ij}
=\frac1Z\sum_{i\ne j}\alpha_i r_{ij}
=\frac{\alpha_j}{Z}
=\pi_jq_j.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Including the diagonal $q_{jj}=-q_j$ gives $\pi Q=0$. In a finite state space, the [[有限CTMC平稳生成矩阵判据|stationary generator criterion]] converts this balance into $\pi P(t)=\pi$ for every duration $t$. For the example, $Q=\begin{pmatrix}-2&2\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$ and $(1/3,2/3)Q=(0,0)$, whereas $(1/2,1/2)Q=(-1/2,1/2)$.

This explains the direction of the weighting: compared with time observation, counting arrivals gives more weight to states with shorter stays. It does not assert that a high exit rate alone determines how often a state is reached; the whole transition graph matters too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把对角元 $q_{jj}=-q_j$ 纳入，便得到 $\pi Q=0$。有限状态下，[[有限CTMC平稳生成矩阵判据]]把这一平衡转换成所有时长上的 $\pi P(t)=\pi$。例中 $Q=\begin{pmatrix}-2&2\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}$，且 $(1/3,2/3)Q=(0,0)$；错误的时间比例 $(1/2,1/2)$ 则给出 $(-1/2,1/2)$。

这解释了加权的方向：相对按时间观察，数到达次数会更偏重停留短的状态。它并不意味着只凭出口率高，就能断言这个状态被访问得多；整张转移图也起作用。

## What carries over to countably many states

The algebra works more broadly than the finite probability formula. Suppose every $q_i>0$ and let $\alpha_i\ge0$ be finite weights satisfying $\sum_i\alpha_i r_{ij}=\alpha_j$ for every $j$. Such an **invariant measure** need not have total weight one, or even finite total weight. Put $m_i=\alpha_i/q_i$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*可数无限状态时，哪些部分仍成立*

代数关系比有限概率公式的范围更广。假设每个 $q_i>0$，各个 $\alpha_i\ge0$ 有限，并满足每个 $j$ 的 $\sum_i\alpha_i r_{ij}=\alpha_j$。这种**不变测度**是一组不变的权重，总和不必为一，甚至不必有限。令 $m_i=\alpha_i/q_i$，则

$$\sum_{i\ne j}m_iq_{ij}=m_jq_j,$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
which is the componentwise meaning of $mQ=0$. The incoming sum is finite because it equals the finite number $\alpha_j$. Conversely, a finite-at-each-state nonnegative $m$ satisfying these balance equations gives the invariant jump-chain weights $\alpha_i=q_im_i$. Thus the conversion is two-way. Probability normalization is a further step requiring
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就是逐分量的 $mQ=0$。流入和等于有限数 $\alpha_j$，所以没有把两个无穷数相减。反过来，每个分量有限的非负 $m$ 若满足这些平衡式，则 $\alpha_i=q_im_i$ 是跳链的不变权重。因此换算是双向的。要再归一化成概率，还必须有

$$0<Z=\sum_i\frac{\alpha_i}{q_i}<\infty.$$

> [!warning] A normalized balance solution needs a specified process · 归一化平衡解仍需对应具体过程
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Without further hypotheses, $\pi_i=m_i/Z$ is first a **$Q$-balance candidate**. [[CTMC平稳分布|Process stationarity]] requires $\pi P(t)=\pi$. An explosive minimal process loses original-space mass; a normalized formal solution of $\pi Q=0$ alone does not repair that loss. Absorbing states with $q_i=0$ must also be handled separately, not by division by zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 没有进一步条件时，$\pi_i=m_i/Z$ 首先只是 **$Q$-平衡候选**。[[CTMC平稳分布|过程平稳性]]要求 $\pi P(t)=\pi$。爆炸的最小过程会丢失原状态空间的质量，单凭归一化的形式解 $\pi Q=0$ 不能消除这种流失。$q_i=0$ 的吸收态也须另行处理，不能除以零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One sufficient setting is an irreducible recurrent jump chain with finite-at-each-state invariant weights $\alpha$. [[常返与停留时间|Recurrence prevents explosion]] in its minimal CTMC. If the displayed $Z$ is finite and positive, the normalized weights are genuinely stationary; the CTMC is positive recurrent and they give its long-run time proportions. This does not require $\sum_i\alpha_i<\infty$. If that latter sum is infinite, however, $\alpha$ is not a probability vector of visit proportions: use it as relative invariant weights, not as normalized visit frequencies.

Thus a computation should say which object it has obtained: jump-visit weights, a normalized generator-balance candidate, or a stationary distribution of the specified process. The last existence question is treated in [[可数CTMC稳态存在|countable-state stationary existence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个明确的充分情形是：跳链不可约且常返，$\alpha$ 是每个分量有限的不变权重。其最小 CTMC 由[[常返与停留时间|常返性排除爆炸]]；若上述 $Z$ 正且有限，归一化权重就确实平稳，CTMC 正常返，并具有相应长期时间占比。这不要求 $\sum_i\alpha_i<\infty$。不过，该总和若无穷，$\alpha$ 就不是访问比例的概率向量，只能把它当相对不变权重，不能称为归一化的访问频率。

因此，计算完成后要说明得到的究竟是跳链访问权重、归一化的生成矩阵平衡候选，还是所指定过程的平稳分布；最后一种存在问题见[[可数CTMC稳态存在]]。
