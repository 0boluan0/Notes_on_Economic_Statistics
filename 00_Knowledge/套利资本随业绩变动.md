# 外部投资者以近期业绩推断能力时可用套利资本可能随业绩变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Available arbitrage capital can depend on recent performance when outside investors use returns to infer manager ability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

专业交易知识和资金可以属于不同的人。在这种[[委托代理关系]]中，外部投资者若难以直接评估策略及经理能力，便可能以近期回报更新判断、调整委托资金。于是价格偏离扩大、经理账面亏损时，即使经理认为预期机会更好，手中可用资金仍可能减少；Shleifer–Vishny 将这一设定称为 performance-based arbitrage。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specialized trading knowledge and capital can belong to different people. In this [[委托代理关系|principal–agent relationship]], investors who cannot directly assess a strategy or manager ability may update beliefs from recent returns and adjust delegated funds. Worsening mispricing and marked losses can therefore reduce available capital even when the manager sees a better expected opportunity. Shleifer–Vishny call this setting performance-based arbitrage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为分清损失与赎回，取不计利息、费用和借款的简化情形：初始资源 $F_1>0$，其中 $0\le D_1\le F_1$ 以价格 $p_1>0$ 投入资产，其余持现金。价格变为 $p_2>0$ 后、资金流发生前的净值为 $W_2$。沿用原论文的线性资金规则，且只在 $F_2>0$ 的适用区间内计算：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To separate losses from redemptions, consider a simplified case without interest, fees, or borrowing: initial resources are $F_1>0$, of which $0\le D_1\le F_1$ is invested at $p_1>0$, with the rest in cash. After price changes to $p_2>0$, pre-flow wealth is $W_2$. Use the paper's linear funding rule only in the applicable region with $F_2>0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
W_2=F_1+D_1\left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}-1\right),\qquad
F_2=F_1+aD_1\left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}-1\right),\quad a\ge1.
$$

$$
\text{净外部资金流}=F_2-W_2
=(a-1)D_1\left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}-1\right).
$$

当 $F_1=100,D_1=80,p_2/p_1=0.9$ 时，资产损失为 8，故 $W_2=92$。若 $a=1$，$F_2=92$，没有额外净赎回；若 $a=1.5$，$F_2=88$，其中另外 4 是净赎回。不能把管理规模从 100 降到 88 的全部 12 都叫作投资者撤资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $F_1=100,D_1=80,p_2/p_1=0.9$, the position loses 8 and $W_2=92$. For $a=1$, $F_2=92$ with no additional net redemption. For $a=1.5$, $F_2=88$, of which another 4 is net redemption. The entire decline from 100 to 88 is not investor withdrawal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一规则是带信息条件的机制，不是所有基金的资金流定律。若出资者能够辨认能力与暂时价格噪声，亏损可能反而提高其对未来机会的评价；长期承诺资金也未必允许即时赎回。即便发生赎回，若经理仍有足够现金，也不必立刻卖出原头寸。何时进一步形成价格反馈，见[[委托套利资本反馈]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an information-dependent mechanism, not a universal fund-flow law. Investors able to distinguish ability from temporary price noise may instead improve their assessment of future opportunities after a loss; committed long-term funding may not permit immediate redemption. Even a redemption need not force a position sale when sufficient cash remains. [[委托套利资本反馈|Delegated-capital feedback]] states the additional price-feedback conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
