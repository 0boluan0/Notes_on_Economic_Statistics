# 国民总收入是一个经济体常住单位在一定时期内取得的初次收入总额

国民总收入（GNI）按收入归属的常住单位计量，而[[国内生产总值|GDP]]按本经济体的生产计量。“常住”依据主要经济利益中心，不依据国籍；因此不能把 GNI 读成“所有本国公民在全世界赚到的钱”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gross national income (GNI) measures primary income accruing to resident units, whereas [[国内生产总值|GDP]] measures production in the economy. Residence follows the center of predominant economic interest, not citizenship; GNI is not the worldwide earnings of all citizens.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mathrm{GNI}=\mathrm{GDP}
+\text{从国外应收初次收入}
-\text{向国外应付初次收入}.
$$

跨境初次收入包括符合居民与非居民交易口径的雇员报酬、利息、股息等。假设 GDP 为 1,000，从国外应收初次收入 50，向国外应付初次收入 80，则 GNI 为 970：本地生产规模与归本地常住单位的初次收入可以不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cross-border primary income includes compensation of employees, interest, and dividends that meet the resident–nonresident transaction boundary. With GDP of 1,000, primary income receivable from abroad of 50, and payable abroad of 80, GNI is 970. Domestic production and primary income accruing to residents can differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收到钱也不一定就是初次收入。例如海外常住亲属向本地家庭作一笔 20 的个人转移，这笔二次收入不会把上述 GNI 从 970 改成 990。日常说的“侨汇”可能跨越不同账户，必须先识别交易性质，而不是全部加进 GNI。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Receiving money does not automatically mean receiving primary income. A personal transfer of 20 from a relative resident abroad is secondary income and would not raise this GNI from 970 to 990. Everyday uses of “remittances” can span different accounts, so identify the transaction rather than adding all such receipts to GNI.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[收入分组与发展分类|世界银行收入分组]]使用 Atlas 法换算的人均 GNI；[[人类发展指数]]的收入维度使用 PPP 人均 GNI。同一收入概念采用不同换算和聚合方式，并不意味着两种分类可以互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[收入分组与发展分类|World Bank income groups]] use Atlas-method GNI per capita; the income dimension of the [[人类发展指数|HDI]] uses PPP GNI per capita. Different conversions and aggregation rules mean the classifications are not interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [SNA 2008，§2.19、§2.143](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/nationalaccount/docs/SNA2008.pdf)：常住原则与 GDP 到 GNI 的收入调整。
- [World Bank，净初次收入元数据](https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/world-development-indicators/series/NY.GSR.NFCY.CN)：雇员报酬、财产及企业收入的跨境口径。
- [IMF, BPM6，附录5，表A5.1及§A5.21–22](https://www.imf.org/external/pubs/ft/bop/2007/pdf/appx5.pdf)：侨汇相关项目的初次／二次收入归属及常住身份。数例为独立构造。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
SNA supports residence and the GDP–GNI adjustment; WDI specifies cross-border primary income; BPM6 distinguishes remittance-related accounts and residence. The numerical example is constructed.
