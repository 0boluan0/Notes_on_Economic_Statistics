# 多重共线性与设计矩阵诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Multicollinearity and design-matrix diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 判断 regressors 是否提供了足够独立 variation，让模型能把它们的 partial effects 分开估计。
> **具体锚点：** 若 `age = education + experience + 6` 在数据中精确成立，同时放入 intercept、age、education 与 experience 会产生 exact collinearity；若只近似成立，OLS 可算但单个 coefficients 会很不稳定。
> **核心难点：** collinearity 是 $X$ columns 之间的信息重叠，不是 $X$ 与 error 相关。它主要伤害 identification of separate coefficients 与 precision；高 VIF 既不自动制造 bias，也不自动授权删除理论上必需的 control。
> **为什么重要：** 它解释“整体 model 很稳、joint test 显著，但单个 coefficients 乱跳”的现象，并把 coding error、数据支持不足和真正的 endogeneity 分开。
> **继续：** 先读 [[#从 rank 看 exact 与 near collinearity]]，再用 [[#VIF 为什么能量化某个 coefficient 的方差膨胀]] 和 [[#Condition diagnostics 看整个 design matrix]]；若问题来自少数 rows 而不是 columns，转到 [[异常值、杠杆与影响诊断]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** These diagnostics ask whether the regressors provide enough independent variation to estimate their partial effects separately.
> **Concrete anchor:** If `age = education + experience + 6` holds exactly in the data, including an intercept, age, education, and experience creates exact collinearity. If it holds only approximately, OLS is computable but the individual coefficients may be unstable.
> **Central difficulty:** Collinearity is information overlap among columns of $X$, not correlation between $X$ and the error. It mainly damages identification of separate coefficients and precision. A high VIF neither creates bias automatically nor authorises deletion of a theoretically required control.
> **Why it matters:** It explains how the overall model and a joint test may be stable while individual coefficients move wildly, and it separates coding errors, limited data support, and genuine endogeneity.
> **Continue with:** Begin with [[#从 rank 看 exact 与 near collinearity|exact and near collinearity through matrix rank]], then study [[#VIF 为什么能量化某个 coefficient 的方差膨胀|variance inflation for an individual coefficient]] and [[#Condition diagnostics 看整个 design matrix|whole-matrix condition diagnostics]]. If a few rows rather than columns drive the problem, continue to [[异常值、杠杆与影响诊断|outliers, leverage, and influence]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先区分它不是什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First distinguish what it is not*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多重共线性指 regressors 之间存在 exact 或 near-linear dependence。它与 endogeneity 的对象不同：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multicollinearity is exact or near-linear dependence among regressors. It concerns a different relation from endogeneity:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 问题 | 关系发生在哪里 | 主要后果 |
|---|---|---|
| Multicollinearity | $X$ 的 columns 彼此重叠 | separate coefficients 不可识别或不精确 |
| Endogeneity | $X$ 与 structural error $u$ 相关 | coefficient 可能 biased/inconsistent，causal interpretation 失败 |
| Heteroskedasticity/dependence | $u$ 的 conditional covariance 设错 | classical standard errors/efficiency 失败 |
| Influence | 某些 rows 对 fit 改变过大 | estimate 对 observations 敏感 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Problem | Where the relation occurs | Main consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Multicollinearity | Overlap among columns of $X$ | Separate coefficients are unidentified or imprecise |
| Endogeneity | Correlation between $X$ and the structural error $u$ | Coefficients may be biased or inconsistent and causal interpretation fails |
| Heteroskedasticity or dependence | Misspecified conditional covariance of $u$ | Classical standard errors or efficiency fail |
| Influence | Particular rows change the fit excessively | Estimates become observation-sensitive |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多重共线性主要增大 variance，不在外生性成立时制造 bias；但“unbiased yet uselessly imprecise”仍是实质问题。若 exact collinearity 让 $X'X$ singular，某些 parameter combinations 根本没有 unique estimate，不能再讨论其 standard error 大小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multicollinearity mainly increases variance and does not create bias when exogeneity holds, but an unbiased estimate may still be too imprecise to answer the question. Under exact collinearity, $X'X$ is singular and some parameter combinations have no unique estimate, so discussing a merely large standard error is already too late.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从 rank 看 exact 与 near collinearity
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Exact and near collinearity through matrix rank*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OLS normal equations 要求 design matrix $X$ 有 full column rank：若存在非零 vector $a$ 使 $Xa=0$，则 $\beta$ 与 $\beta+ca$ 对任意 $c$ 给出同一 fitted values，data 无法区分它们。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The OLS normal equations require full column rank of the design matrix $X$. If a nonzero vector $a$ satisfies $Xa=0$, then $\beta$ and $\beta+ca$ yield the same fitted values for every $c$, so the data cannot distinguish them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

exact collinearity 的常见来源包括：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common sources of exact collinearity include:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- intercept 加上一个 categorical variable 的全部 mutually exclusive dummies；
- total 与其 exhaustive components 同时进入，例如 total spending、food spending 与 non-food spending；
- deterministic identity，例如 age、education 与 potential experience；
- duplicate columns、错误 merge 或一个 constant-within-sample variable。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- an intercept together with every mutually exclusive dummy for a categorical variable;
- a total together with all exhaustive components, such as total, food, and non-food spending;
- a deterministic identity such as age, education, and potential experience;
- duplicate columns, an erroneous merge, or a variable that is constant within the sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

software 自动 drop 一列只是选择一个 parameterisation，不是“数据已经解决问题”。应找出 identity，明确 reference category 或重新定义 estimand。若同一 fitted surface 可以用多组 coefficients 表达，只有 identified linear combinations 才有含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When software drops a column automatically, it merely chooses a parameterisation; the data problem has not disappeared. Identify the linear identity, state the reference category, or redefine the estimand. If the same fitted surface can be represented by several coefficient vectors, only identified linear combinations are meaningful.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

near collinearity 则没有 exact $Xa=0$，但存在某个 $a$ 让 $Xa$ 的 variation 很小。$X'X$ 可逆却接近 singular；small changes in observations、coding 或 included controls 会沿这个 weak direction 引起 large coefficient changes。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under near collinearity, no exact $Xa=0$ exists, but some direction $a$ makes the variation in $Xa$ very small. The matrix $X'X$ is invertible yet close to singular, so small changes in observations, coding, or included controls can produce large coefficient changes along that weak direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## FWL 直觉：coefficient 只靠独立剩余 variation
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The FWL intuition: a coefficient relies on residual variation unique to its regressor*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根据 Frisch–Waugh–Lovell logic，$\hat\beta_j$ 等于把 $Y$ 对其他 regressors residualise 后，再对 $X_j$ 被其他 regressors residualise 后的部分做 simple regression。若 residualised $X_j$ 几乎为 0，模型几乎没有能区分 $X_j$ effect 的 independent variation。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By the Frisch–Waugh–Lovell logic, $\hat\beta_j$ is obtained by regressing the part of $Y$ left after removing the other regressors on the part of $X_j$ left after removing those same regressors. If residualised $X_j$ is nearly zero, the model contains almost no independent variation with which to separate the effect of $X_j$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这解释了看似矛盾的结果：$Y$ 可被整个 regressor set 预测得很好，joint F test 也显著，但 individual t tests 弱。数据支持“这些 variables 的某个 combination 有关”，却没有足够独立 variation 分配给每个 coefficient。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This resolves an apparent contradiction: the whole regressor set may predict $Y$ well and be jointly significant while individual t tests are weak. The data support a relation involving some combination of the variables but do not contain enough independent variation to allocate it among the individual coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## VIF 为什么能量化某个 coefficient 的方差膨胀
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why VIF measures variance inflation for one coefficient*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $X_j$ 对其余 regressors 做 auxiliary regression，并记其 $R_j^2$。在 classical homoskedastic model 下，若 $X_j$ 已中心化，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regress $X_j$ on all other regressors and denote the auxiliary coefficient of determination by $R_j^2$. In the classical homoskedastic model, for centred $X_j$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_j\mid X)
=\frac{\sigma^2}
{\sum_i(X_{ij}-\bar X_j)^2(1-R_j^2)}.
$$

因此
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
VIF_j=\frac{1}{1-R_j^2},
\qquad
tolerance_j=1-R_j^2=\frac1{VIF_j}.
$$

若 $R_j^2=0.90$，$VIF_j=10$：相对于保持 $X_j$ total variation 不变但令它与其余 regressors orthogonal 的 benchmark，variance 被乘 10，standard error 被乘 $\sqrt{10}\approx3.16$。VIF 不看 outcome $Y$，所以它诊断 design information，不判断 coefficient bias、substantive importance 或 model correctness。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $R_j^2=0.90$, then $VIF_j=10$. Relative to a benchmark with the same total variation in $X_j$ but orthogonality to the remaining regressors, the variance is multiplied by ten and the standard error by $\sqrt{10}\approx3.16$. VIF does not use the outcome $Y$, so it diagnoses design information rather than coefficient bias, substantive importance, or model correctness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“VIF > 5” 或 “VIF > 10” 只能作 screening convention，不是 universal law。容忍度取决于 estimand、sample size、measurement precision 与所需 accuracy。一个 treatment coefficient 的 VIF=3 也可能让研究失去 power；一个 prediction-only model 的 VIF=20 可能不妨碍 aggregate prediction，但 coefficient interpretation 仍不稳定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rules such as VIF above five or ten are screening conventions, not universal laws. Tolerance depends on the estimand, sample size, measurement precision, and required accuracy. A VIF of three may destroy power for a treatment coefficient, whereas a VIF of twenty may leave aggregate prediction usable while individual coefficient interpretation remains unstable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

pairwise correlation matrix 只能发现 two-column relationships。若 $X_3\approx X_1+X_2$，没有任何单个 pair 必须接近 perfectly correlated，整个 set 却可能 near-dependent。VIF 的 auxiliary regression 能看到 higher-order dependence，但仍是 one-column-at-a-time summary。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pairwise correlation matrix detects only two-column relations. If $X_3\approx X_1+X_2$, no single pair need be nearly perfectly correlated even though the set is nearly dependent. The auxiliary regression behind VIF detects higher-order dependence, but remains a one-column-at-a-time summary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Condition diagnostics 看整个 design matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Condition diagnostics for the whole design matrix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

singular value decomposition 写成 $X=U\Sigma V'$。smallest singular value 接近 0 表示某个 linear combination of columns 几乎没有 variation。常见 condition number 是 $\kappa=\sigma_{max}/\sigma_{min}$；越大，沿 weak direction 的 coefficient 对 perturbation 越敏感。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the singular value decomposition as $X=U\Sigma V'$. A smallest singular value near zero indicates that some linear combination of columns has almost no variation. A common condition number is $\kappa=\sigma_{max}/\sigma_{min}$; larger values imply greater coefficient sensitivity to perturbations along a weak direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

condition number 对 units 与 scaling 敏感。把 income 从 pounds 改成 thousands of pounds 会改变 raw $X$ 的 condition number，却不改变 substantive information。因此应先根据目的 center/standardise non-intercept columns，说明 scaling convention，再看 singular directions 或 condition indices；不能把 raw software number 脱离 scaling 当成诊断结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The condition number is sensitive to units and scaling. Measuring income in pounds rather than thousands of pounds changes the raw condition number without changing substantive information. Centre or standardise non-intercept columns as appropriate, state the scaling convention, and then inspect singular directions or condition indices. A raw software number without its scaling convention is not a diagnosis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

diagnostic 不应止于 scalar：同时检查 rank/aliases、VIF、scaled singular values、coefficient correlation matrix、joint tests 与 leave-one-variable/specification stability。最有用的问题是“哪个 linear combination 缺少 support，以及它是否正是我想估计的 estimand？”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diagnostics should not stop at one scalar. Inspect rank and aliases, VIFs, scaled singular values, the coefficient-correlation matrix, joint tests, and stability across meaningful specifications. The most useful question is which linear combination lacks support and whether it is the estimand of substantive interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Structural collinearity 与 data-based collinearity
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Structural and data-based collinearity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

structural collinearity 由建模构造产生，例如 $X$ 与 $X^2$、main effects 与 interactions、全套 dummies；data-based collinearity 来自 observed support，例如 sample 里 age 与 experience 几乎同步、政策 treatment 几乎只发生在一个 region。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Structural collinearity is induced by model construction, such as $X$ with $X^2$, main effects with interactions, or a complete dummy set. Data-based collinearity comes from observed support, such as age and experience moving almost together in the sample or treatment occurring almost exclusively in one region.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

centering $X$ before building $X^2$ 或 $XZ$ 可降低 lower- and higher-order columns 的 nonessential correlation，并让 main effects 在 meaningful reference point 解释。它不会改变 fitted values of a correctly reparameterised polynomial，也不会解决两种不同 variables 在 data 中共同移动的 essential collinearity。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Centering $X$ before forming $X^2$ or $XZ$ can reduce nonessential correlation between lower- and higher-order columns and make main effects refer to a meaningful point. It does not change fitted values under a correctly reparameterised polynomial, nor does it solve essential collinearity between distinct variables that move together in the observed data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 完整例子：age、education 与 experience
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: age, education, and experience*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

许多 wage datasets 用 $experience=age-education-6$ 构造 potential experience。若同时估计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Many wage datasets construct potential experience as $experience=age-education-6$. If one estimates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\log(wage)
=\beta_0+\beta_1education+\beta_2experience+\beta_3age+u,
$$

则 intercept、education、experience 与 age 精确满足 linear identity，$X$ 不 full rank。software 可能 drop 任意一列；不同 dropped column 会改变其余 coefficient labels，但 fitted values 可以相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
the intercept, education, experience, and age obey an exact linear identity, so $X$ lacks full rank. Software may drop any one column. Different dropped columns change the labels and values of the remaining coefficients even though the fitted values may be identical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 experience 是实际记录而非机械构造，identity 可能只有 near equality。此时 coefficients 能算，但“holding experience fixed, increase age by one while holding education fixed”在 observed sample 中几乎没有 support。高 VIF 正是在提醒这个 partial comparison 主要靠极少 variation 或 extrapolation。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If experience is observed rather than mechanically constructed, the identity may hold only approximately. Coefficients can then be computed, but the comparison of increasing age by one while holding education and experience fixed has almost no support in the observed sample. A high VIF warns that this partial comparison relies on very little variation or on extrapolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

修复取决于 question：研究 returns to schooling 时可依据 theory 选择 age 或 experience 并明确 comparison；研究 life-cycle 与 cohort effects 时则可能需要 richer data、different timing structure 或 external variation，而不是随便删掉 VIF 最大的一列。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The repair depends on the question. A study of returns to schooling may choose age or experience on theoretical grounds and state the comparison explicitly. A study of life-cycle and cohort effects may require richer data, a different temporal structure, or external variation rather than arbitrary deletion of the column with the largest VIF.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 补救必须对应 estimand
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Remedies must match the estimand*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 情况 | 合理行动 | 不应做的事 |
|---|---|---|
| dummy trap / duplicate column | 修正 coding，设 reference category | 假装 software drop 后问题无需说明 |
| constructed polynomial/interactions | center、保留 hierarchy、报告 joint/marginal effects | 因 VIF 高而删除 lower-order terms |
| sample support 太窄 | 收集含独立 variation 的 observations；缩小 estimand | 通过 rescaling 声称增加了信息 |
| 两 variables 代表同一 construct | 在 theory 支持下合成 index 或重定义 parameter | 只按哪个 p 值小就留哪个 |
| causal confounder 与 treatment 高相关 | 保留并承认 limited overlap/precision；重设 design | 为降低 VIF 删除必要 confounder |
| 纯 prediction | 可用 regularisation 或 dimension reduction，并验证 | 把 shrunk coefficients 直接当 structural effects |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Situation | Reasonable action | What not to do |
|---|---|---|
| Dummy trap or duplicate column | Correct the coding and set a reference category | Pretend the issue needs no explanation after software drops a column |
| Constructed polynomials or interactions | Centre, preserve hierarchy, and report joint or marginal effects | Delete lower-order terms merely because VIF is high |
| Narrow sample support | Collect observations with independent variation or narrow the estimand | Claim that rescaling has added information |
| Two variables represent one construct | Combine them or redefine the parameter when theory supports it | Keep whichever variable has the smaller p-value |
| A causal confounder is highly related to treatment | Retain it, acknowledge limited overlap and precision, or redesign the study | Delete a necessary confounder to lower VIF |
| Pure prediction | Use regularisation or dimension reduction and validate it | Interpret shrunken coefficients directly as structural effects |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增加 sample size 只有在新 observations 带来独立 regressor variation 时才有效；复制相同 design pattern 会降低 noise-related uncertainty，却不改变 population support 的 narrow direction。对现有 data，诚实报告 large intervals、joint effects 或 identified combinations 往往比强行制造 single-coefficient significance 更正确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Increasing sample size helps only if the new observations add independent regressor variation. Replicating the same design pattern can reduce noise-related uncertainty without repairing a narrow direction in population support. With the available data, honestly reporting wide intervals, joint effects, or identified combinations is often better than forcing single-coefficient significance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Minimum self-check*

### 用自己的话解释：为什么 multicollinearity 不是 endogeneity？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why is multicollinearity not endogeneity?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> multicollinearity 是 regressors 彼此缺少独立 variation，主要影响 separate coefficients 的 identification/precision；endogeneity 是 regressor 与 structural error 相关，会污染 coefficient 的中心与 causal meaning。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Multicollinearity means regressors lack independent variation from one another and mainly affects identification or precision of separate coefficients. Endogeneity means a regressor is related to the structural error and contaminates the coefficient's centre and causal meaning.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 多重共线性会不会在外生性成立时让 OLS 有偏？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does multicollinearity bias OLS when exogeneity holds?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不会；它主要使相关系数难以分别估计、方差增大，完全共线性才使系数不可识别。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. It mainly makes correlated coefficients difficult to estimate separately and increases their variance. Only perfect collinearity makes the separate coefficients unidentified.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 若 auxiliary regression 得 $R_j^2=0.96$，VIF 与 standard-error inflation 是多少？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If an auxiliary regression gives $R_j^2=0.96$, what are the VIF and standard-error inflation?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> $VIF=1/(1-0.96)=25$；相对于 orthogonal benchmark，variance 乘 25，standard error 乘 $\sqrt{25}=5$。这仍不表示 coefficient 有偏。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $VIF=1/(1-0.96)=25$. Relative to the orthogonal benchmark, the variance is multiplied by 25 and the standard error by five. This still does not imply coefficient bias.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么只看 pairwise correlations 可能漏掉 collinearity？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can pairwise correlations miss collinearity?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> near dependence 可能涉及三列以上的 linear combination，例如 $X_3\approx X_1+X_2$；任何单个 pair 都不必接近 perfectly correlated。VIF 或 singular-value diagnostics 才检查多列共同方向。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Near dependence may involve a combination of three or more columns, such as $X_3\approx X_1+X_2$, without any single pair being nearly perfectly correlated. VIF or singular-value diagnostics inspect the joint column direction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一个 causal control 的 VIF 很高，能否为降低 VIF 直接删除？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Can a causal control be deleted directly because its VIF is high?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不能自动删除。若它阻断 confounding path，删除会用 bias 换 precision。应重新评估 estimand、overlap、数据设计和可识别 linear combinations，并如实报告 limited precision。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Not automatically. If it blocks a confounding path, deletion trades precision for bias. Reassess the estimand, overlap, data design, and identifiable linear combinations, and report limited precision honestly.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
