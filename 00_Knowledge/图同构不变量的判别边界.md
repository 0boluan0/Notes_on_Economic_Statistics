# Isomorphism invariant tests
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*图同构不变量的判别边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before trying every possible matching between two graphs, compare something that relabeling cannot change. A four-vertex path has degrees $1,2,2,1$, while a four-vertex star has degrees $3,1,1,1$. The degree-$3$ center of the star has no possible partner in the path. This alone proves that the graphs are not isomorphic, even though both have four vertices and three edges.

An **isomorphism invariant** is a value or property that is the same for isomorphic graphs. This card concerns finite [[图类型的建模边界|simple undirected graphs]] and [[图同构的定义与不变量|graph isomorphisms]], which are vertex bijections preserving adjacency in both directions. An invariant tests a consequence of that complete matching; it is not usually a replacement for the matching.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

尝试两图之间的全部顶点配对之前，可以先比较重新命名不会改变的量。四顶点路径的度数是 $1,2,2,1$，四顶点星形的度数则是 $3,1,1,1$。星形中心的度数为 $3$，在路径中找不到可能与它对应的顶点。仅这一点就证明两图不同构，尽管它们都有四个顶点、三条边。

**同构不变量**是在同构图之间保持相同的数值或性质。本卡讨论有限[[图类型的建模边界|简单无向图]]及[[图同构的定义与不变量|图同构]]，后者是双向保持邻接的顶点双射。不变量检查的是完整配对的一项必然后果，通常不能代替配对本身。

> [!important] A mismatch rules out isomorphism · 不一致可以排除同构
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $I$ is an isomorphism invariant, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $I$ 是同构不变量，则
>
> $$
> G\cong H\ \Longrightarrow\ I(G)=I(H),
> \qquad
> I(G)\ne I(H)\ \Longrightarrow\ G\not\cong H.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Equality of a selected invariant, or of several selected invariants, does not in general imply isomorphism. A special test could characterize a restricted graph class, but that would need its own sufficient-condition proof.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 某个所选不变量相同，或几个所选不变量都相同，一般不能反推同构。某项特殊检验也许能刻画受限图类，但需要另行证明其充分性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why degree is preserved

For a vertex $v$, its **neighbour set** $N_G(v)$ contains precisely the vertices joined to $v$ by an edge. In a simple graph its size is the [[度数、入度与出度|degree]] $\deg_G(v)$. If $f$ is an isomorphism, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么度数一定保持*

顶点 $v$ 的**邻居集合** $N_G(v)$，包含且仅包含与 $v$ 有边相连的顶点。在简单图中，它的大小就是[[度数、入度与出度|度数]] $\deg_G(v)$。若 $f$ 是同构，则

$$
f:N_G(v)\longrightarrow N_H(f(v))
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
is a bijection. It maps neighbours to neighbours because it preserves edges, and it cannot merge them because $f$ is injective. Every neighbour $y$ of $f(v)$ is reached: write $y=f(x)$ using surjectivity; the reverse adjacency implication forces $x$ to be a neighbour of $v$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也是双射。保持边保证邻居的像仍为邻居，$f$ 的单射性保证不同邻居不会被合并。对 $f(v)$ 的任意邻居 $y$，满射性给出 $y=f(x)$；反向邻接条件又迫使 $x$ 是 $v$ 的邻居。因此

$$
\deg_G(v)=\deg_H(f(v)).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently, the **degree multiset** is preserved. A multiset keeps repetitions but ignores order: four degree-$2$ vertices count as four copies of $2$. Sorting these values gives a degree sequence. Compare the sorted sequences, not values at identically spelled vertex labels; relabeling may move those labels.

An isomorphism also bijects the vertex sets and edge sets, so their sizes are preserved. Mapping a [[游走路径与环|path or cycle]] vertex by vertex preserves consecutive edges, distinct vertices and length; applying $f^{-1}$ gives the reverse correspondence. Thus reachability, the number of [[无向图连通分量|connected components]] (whole reachable pieces), and path and cycle structure are preserved. Each connected piece must map onto a whole connected piece.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，**度数多重集**也保持不变。多重集忽略排列顺序，但保留重复次数，例如四个度数为 $2$ 的顶点对应四份 $2$。把这些数排序，就得到度数序列。应比较排序后的序列，而不是把同名标签处的度数硬作比较，因为重新命名可以改变标签对应。

同构也分别给出顶点集与边集之间的双射，所以顶点数和边数保持不变。把[[游走路径与环|路径或环]]的顶点逐个映过去，会保留相邻边、顶点不重复的要求以及长度；再用逆映射 $f^{-1}$，得到反方向对应。因此，可达性、[[无向图连通分量|连通分量]]（整块可达区域）的数量以及路径和环的结构都被保留；每个完整连通块必须映到另一个完整连通块。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **chromatic number**, the smallest number of colors needed for a [[Proper 着色与独立集划分|proper vertex coloring]], is another invariant for nonempty graphs. Transfer a coloring along $f$: adjacent image vertices came from adjacent original vertices and therefore still have different colors. Transfer back along $f^{-1}$ as well; each graph can use no more colors than the other, so their minima agree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非空图的**色数**——[[Proper 着色与独立集划分|合法顶点着色]]所需的最少颜色数——也是不变量。沿 $f$ 搬运颜色：相邻的像顶点来自相邻的原顶点，所以仍然异色。再沿 $f^{-1}$ 搬回来，得到每图所需颜色都不多于另一图，因此两个最小值相同。

> [!example] Equal degrees, different components · 度数相同，分量不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Two graphs have six vertices, six edges, and degree sequence $(2,2,2,2,2,2)$. Must they be isomorphic?
>
> **Answer:** No. One may be the cycle $C_6$, while the other is the disjoint union of two triangles. Their degrees agree, but one is connected and the other has two components.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两图都有六个顶点、六条边，degree sequence 都是 $(2,2,2,2,2,2)$。能否推出同构？
>
> **答案：**不能。一个图可以是六边形 $C_6$，另一个可以是两个不相交的 triangles；degree sequence 相同，但前者 connected、后者有两个 components，所以不同构。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To make both graphs explicit, use vertices $1,\ldots,6$. In $C_6$ take edges $12,23,34,45,56,61$. In the other graph take $12,23,31,45,56,64$, where $ij$ denotes the unordered edge $\{i,j\}$. Every vertex has exactly two neighbours in each graph. The first graph has a route between any two vertices. The second has no edge between $\{1,2,3\}$ and $\{4,5,6\}$ and therefore no path between those sets. A hypothetical isomorphism would preserve that reachability, giving a contradiction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把两图写具体：都取顶点 $1,\ldots,6$。$C_6$ 的边为 $12,23,34,45,56,61$；另一图的边为 $12,23,31,45,56,64$，其中 $ij$ 表示无序边 $\{i,j\}$。每张图中的每个顶点都恰好有两个邻居。第一图中任意两点有路线相连；第二图在 $\{1,2,3\}$ 与 $\{4,5,6\}$ 之间没有边，因此也没有跨越两组的路径。假想的同构必须保持这种可达性，因而矛盾。

## A failed candidate is not a proof of non-isomorphism

Equal invariants leave possible matchings to investigate. Degree can narrow the search: a degree-$3$ vertex must map to a degree-$3$ vertex, and its neighbours must be matched consistently too. If one proposed bijection fails an adjacency check, only that proposal has been rejected; a different matching may still work.

To prove isomorphism, provide a complete valid bijection or another sufficient argument. To prove non-isomorphism, one differing genuine invariant is enough. If all the measurements you tried agree and no mapping has been established, the conclusion remains undecided by those checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一份候选失败，不等于两图不同构*

不变量相同，只是留下了仍需检查的配对。度数可以缩小搜索范围：度数为 $3$ 的顶点只能映到度数为 $3$ 的顶点，其邻居也必须一致地对应。如果某份候选双射未通过邻接检查，排除的只是这一份候选，另一种配对仍可能成功。

要证明同构，应给出完整合法的双射，或另一项充分论证。要证明不同构，发现一个真正的不变量不同就足够。如果已尝试的测量全部相同，又尚未建立同构映射，那么这些检查还没有作出结论。
