# 生活资料生产率提高缩短必要劳动时间可在工作日不变时增加相对剩余价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Higher subsistence-goods productivity can shorten necessary labour and increase relative surplus value within an unchanged working day*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相对剩余价值生产通过降低 [[劳动力价值]]、缩短 [[必要劳动|必要劳动时间]]，在工作日不变时增加 [[剩余劳动]]。这里考察生产率提高的机制：在劳动强度和价值计量不变、再生产劳动力所需的实际生活资料组合给定、劳动力继续按价值支付的条件下，相关商品的社会必要生产劳动减少，使劳动力价值下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Production of relative surplus value lowers the [[劳动力价值|value of labour-power]] and shortens [[必要劳动|necessary labour time]], increasing [[剩余劳动|surplus labour]] within an unchanged working day. The mechanism considered here is increased productivity. With unchanged labour intensity and value measurement, a given bundle of means of subsistence required to reproduce labour-power, and continued payment at value, less socially necessary labour to produce the relevant commodities lowers the value of labour-power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

生产率提高必须影响生活资料本身，或影响生产这些生活资料所需的原料、机器等投入。一家只生产与劳动力再生产无关商品的企业提高效率，并不因此降低社会的劳动力价值。这里的连接是“相关商品社会价值下降 → 劳动力价值下降 → 必要劳动缩短”，可联系 [[社会必要劳动时间]] 阅读。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Productivity must improve in the production of subsistence goods themselves or of inputs, such as materials and machinery, used to produce them. Greater efficiency at a firm producing goods unrelated to the reproduction of labour-power does not thereby reduce labour-power's social value. The connection is “lower social value of relevant commodities → lower value of labour-power → shorter necessary labour.” See [[社会必要劳动时间|socially necessary labour time]] for the value relation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

教学例中，8小时工作日、正常且不变的劳动强度和固定货币价值下，每小时平均社会劳动表现为100价值单位。原来同一组日生活资料价值400，对应必要劳动4小时、剩余4小时。社会生产率提高使该组合价值降至300，劳动力仍按其价值支付，必要劳动就降至3小时、剩余升至5小时。新价值仍为800，其中 $v$ 从400降为300，$s$ 从400升为500；[[剩余价值率]] 从100%升为约166.7%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the constructed example, the day lasts eight hours and labour intensity is normal and unchanged. With the value of money fixed, each hour of average social labour is expressed in 100 value units. The same daily subsistence bundle initially has a value of 400, corresponding to four necessary and four surplus hours. Higher social productivity lowers that bundle's value to 300. With labour-power still paid at its value, necessary labour falls to three hours and surplus labour rises to five. New value remains 800, while $v$ falls from 400 to 300 and $s$ rises from 400 to 500. The [[剩余价值率|rate of surplus value]] rises from 100% to approximately 166.7%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

个别企业率先采用高效率方法，还可能在技术普及前取得暂时的额外剩余：其个别生产条件优于社会平均条件，可以在个别价值以上出售。Marx 指出，技术普及消除这一差额；一般剩余价值率的上述提高，仍须通过降低劳动力再生产所需商品的社会价值来完成。工作日同时延长时，还会叠加 [[绝对剩余价值生产]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An individual firm adopting a more efficient method can also obtain temporary extra surplus before the method spreads: its conditions are better than the social average, allowing sale above its individual value. Marx explains that diffusion eliminates this gap. The increase in the general rate described above still operates through lower social values of commodities required to reproduce labour-power. If the working day also lengthens, [[绝对剩余价值生产|production of absolute surplus value]] operates alongside it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Marx，《资本论》卷I，[第12章](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch12.htm)，从给定工作日下必要劳动缩短，到劳动力按价值支付、生活资料及其生产资料部门的生产率条件；个别价值与社会价值、额外剩余随技术普及消失的例子；章末固定货币价值下新价值总量及其分割的讨论：支持定义、完整传导与个别企业边界。[第16章](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch16.htm)关于两种剩余生产方法并存、正常劳动强度与生产率条件的段落，支持比较范围。MIA 所载 Moore / Aveling 英译、Engels 编，1887英文初版底本，标示处纳入德文第四版改动。8/4→8/3例为教学构造。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volume I, Chapter 12 supports the mechanism through its treatment of a fixed working day, shorter necessary labour, payment at value, and productivity in subsistence and input industries. It also explains individual versus social value, the disappearance of extra surplus as a method spreads, and the fixed total of new value and its division with a constant value of money. Chapter 16 supports the comparison's scope through the coexistence of the two methods and the assumptions about normal intensity and productivity. The MIA text uses the Moore and Aveling translation edited by Engels, based on the 1887 first English edition with indicated fourth German edition changes. The 8/4-to-8/3 illustration is constructed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
