# 有限类型的 interim-agent 表示把每个玩家—类型对设为辅助博弈玩家，但原贝叶斯博弈的玩家仍是原来的决策者
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The finite-type interim-agent representation makes each player–type pair a player in an auxiliary game while leaving the original decision makers unchanged in the Bayesian game*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限类型贝叶斯博弈 $B=(N,A,\Theta,T,u,p)$，定义辅助的完整信息博弈 $AG(B)$。为避免不同玩家使用同名类型，辅助玩家集合写成不交并

$$
\widehat N=\bigsqcup_{i\in N}\bigl(\{i\}\times T_i\bigr).
$$

代理 $(i,t_i)$ 的策略集是 $A_i$；给定行动表 $\widehat s$，其收益是原玩家 $i$ 在类型 $t_i$ 的条件信念下得到的期望收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite-type Bayesian game, the auxiliary complete-information game $AG(B)$ has one player for every labeled pair $(i,t_i)$. Agent $(i,t_i)$ chooses from $A_i$ and receives the original player $i$'s expected payoff under type $t_i$'s conditional belief.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与贝叶斯策略的对应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Correspondence with Bayesian strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原玩家的纯策略 $s_i:T_i\to A_i$ 对应辅助博弈中的行动坐标

$$
\bigl(s_i(t_i)\bigr)_{(i,t_i)\in\widehat N}.
$$

每个类型代理分别作最佳回应，正好等于逐类型检查 BNE；因此有限类型时

$$
BNE(B)=NE\bigl(AG(B)\bigr).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pure Bayesian strategy corresponds to the list of actions chosen by the player's type agents. Requiring every type agent to best respond is exactly the type-by-type BNE condition, so the Bayesian Nash equilibria of the finite game coincide with the Nash equilibria of its interim-agent game.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 玩家身份的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary on player identity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$AG(B)$ 的形式玩家集合确实是 $\widehat N$，而不是原来的 $N$；但这是求解用的辅助博弈。现实中的企业或个人仍是原玩家 $i$，高成本与低成本只是同一玩家的不同可能类型。无限类型空间不能按有限代理清单直接展开，还需处理可测策略与函数空间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The auxiliary game formally has player set $\widehat N$ rather than $N$, but this is a solution representation. The firm or individual remains the original player $i$; its high- and low-cost cases are possible types of that same player. An infinite type space cannot be replaced by a finite list of agents and requires measurable strategies and function-space analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
