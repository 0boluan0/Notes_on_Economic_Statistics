# 重农主义从自然秩序与农业净产品推出直接地税主张
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Physiocracy derived direct taxation of land revenue from natural order and agricultural net product*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 推导链
> 这条推导同时使用三个前提：[[重农主义自然秩序]]要求主权者保障财产与稳定法律，而不是任意干扰耕作和交易；[[重农主义净产品]]被视为唯一能在不损害下一轮预付的情况下被抽取的剩余；工资税、消费税或流通税的负担则被认为会经工资、价格和支出链转嫁，最终压低土地收入或耕作者收回额。在这些前提下，税收才应直接落在已形成的土地净收入上。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The derivation combines three premises. [[重农主义自然秩序|Natural order]] requires the sovereign to secure property and stable law rather than arbitrarily disrupt cultivation and exchange. [[重农主义净产品|Agricultural net product]] is treated as the only surplus extractable without impairing the next cycle's advances. Taxes on wages, consumption, or circulation are assumed to shift through wages, prices, and expenditure until they reduce land revenue or the cultivators' reprises. Under those premises, taxation should fall directly on already-formed net land revenue.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么不对预付和流通征税
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why not tax advances and circulation?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Quesnay 的一般准则反对把税加在劳动者工资、商品流通或耕作者预付上。在他的模型中，这些税会提高征收与交易成本、侵蚀农业资本，并缩小下一年的再生产。只有接受了间接税最终向土地收入或耕作者收回额转嫁的判断，直接对土地净收入征税才显得能避免中间损耗。推导的政策结论是 [[重农主义单一地税]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quesnay's general maxims reject taxes on workers' wages, commodity circulation, or cultivators' advances. Within his model, such taxes raise collection and transaction costs, erode agricultural capital, and contract the next year's reproduction. Direct taxation of net land revenue appears to avoid intermediate losses only if the assumed shifting of indirect taxes toward land revenue or the cultivators' reprises is accepted. The policy conclusion is the [[重农主义单一地税|physiocratic single land tax]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 推导在哪里会失效
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Where the derivation can fail*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若否定“只有农业产生净产品”的剩余来源前提，或不接受上述税负转嫁路径，单一地税就不再由这条推导必然支持。它也不是现代“土地不易流动，所以地税效率高”的现成论证。现代税制仍需另行比较税基、归宿、行为反应、分配与公共服务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If agriculture is not accepted as the sole source of net product, or if the stated incidence path is rejected, the single land tax no longer follows from this argument. Nor is it the modern claim that taxing immobile land is efficient. Modern tax design must separately compare tax bases, incidence, behavioural responses, distribution, and public services.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么在这条推导中，对耕作者的年度预付征税会破坏单一地税所要保护的东西？
>
> **答案：** 预付是恢复下一轮农业生产的条件；侵蚀它会缩小再生产和未来净产品。按这套理论，较不破坏再生产的税基是已经形成的土地净收入。

## 来源与核验

- [Quesnay, *Physiocratie* 第一部（BnF Gallica, 1767–1768）](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k73143p.pdf)：核对《一般准则》与税收算例把税直接置于土地财产的净产品、保护耕作预付，并主张工资税和消费税会经转嫁损害收入或再生产的原始论证。
- [Quesnay, “Le Droit naturel”](https://historyofeconomicthought.mcmaster.ca/quesnay/drtnat.htm)：核对“自然秩序”包含权利、秩序和社会制度，而不能缩成“政府不作为”。
- [Quesnay, “Analyse de la formule arithmétique du Tableau économique”](https://historyofeconomicthought.mcmaster.ca/quesnay/tabeco.htm)：核对净产品、所有者收入、税收与再生产预付之间的模型联系。
