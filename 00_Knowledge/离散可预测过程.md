# Discrete-time predictable processes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*离散可预测过程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **predictable process** records quantities already determined before the next step occurs. For a sequence of coin tosses, the stake for toss $n$ can depend on earlier results, but must be settled before seeing result $n$. The stake can still be random when viewed from the beginning of the experiment. “Predictable” means known from the specified earlier information, not that the next coin can be forecast correctly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**可预测过程**记录的是下一步发生之前就已经能够确定的量。例如连续掷硬币时，第 $n$ 次的下注额可以参考以前的结果，但必须在看到第 $n$ 次结果之前确定。从整个实验开始时看，下注额仍可能是随机的。“可预测”指的是指定的较早信息已经足以确定它，并不是能够猜中下一枚硬币。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $(\mathcal F_n)_{n\ge0}$ be a [[滤过定义|filtration]]: $\mathcal F_n$ describes the information available after step $n$, and $\mathcal F_{n-1}\subseteq\mathcal F_n$ says that previously known information is retained. Let $H_n$ be the real quantity to be used at step $n$. The formal condition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $(\mathcal F_n)_{n\ge0}$ 是[[滤过定义|滤过]]：$\mathcal F_n$ 表示第 $n$ 步之后可用的信息，而 $\mathcal F_{n-1}\subseteq\mathcal F_n$ 表示已经知道的信息不会丢失。用 $H_n$ 表示第 $n$ 步要使用的实数量。正式条件是

$$
\boxed{H_n\text{ is }\mathcal F_{n-1}\text{-measurable for every }n\ge1.}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Measurability means that the earlier information can determine the value: for every real threshold $a$, the event $\{H_n\le a\}$ belongs to $\mathcal F_{n-1}$. This is an information requirement. By itself it imposes no finite-mean, boundedness or sign requirement on $H_n$; the theorem in which $H_n$ is used may impose additional conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可测性表示较早的信息已经能够确定这个量：对于每个实数阈值 $a$，事件 $\{H_n\le a\}$ 都属于 $\mathcal F_{n-1}$。这是信息要求，本身不要求 $H_n$ 的均值有限、有界或取特定符号；使用它的具体定理可能另有这些条件。

> [!note] Where the indexing starts · 从哪一期开始
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $H=(H_n)_{n\ge1}$ starts at step $1$, so $H_1$ uses $\mathcal F_0$. There is no $\mathcal F_{-1}$ in the setup. If a process also includes $H_0$, its initial-value convention must be stated separately, for example that $H_0$ is $\mathcal F_0$-measurable, or that $H_0=0$ in a decomposition. Do not silently apply the displayed condition at $n=0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $H=(H_n)_{n\ge1}$ 从第 $1$ 步开始，所以 $H_1$ 使用 $\mathcal F_0$。设定中没有 $\mathcal F_{-1}$。若过程还包含 $H_0$，须另行说明初值约定，例如要求 $H_0$ 对 $\mathcal F_0$ 可测，或在某个分解中规定 $H_0=0$，不能直接把上式未经说明地套到 $n=0$。

## One step earlier than adaptedness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 与适应过程的差别

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[适应过程|adapted process]] only requires $H_n$ to be measurable with respect to $\mathcal F_n$, the information available at the current time. Predictability requires the smaller, earlier information $\mathcal F_{n-1}$. Therefore every predictable process is adapted at its indexed times: anything known before step $n$ is still known after it. The reverse implication fails because the current information may reveal the result of step $n$ itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[适应过程|适应过程]]只要求 $H_n$ 对当前信息 $\mathcal F_n$ 可测。可预测则要求使用更早、更小的 $\mathcal F_{n-1}$。因此，每个可预测过程在其编号时刻都适应：第 $n$ 步之前已经知道的量，之后仍然知道。反方向不成立，因为当前信息可能已经揭示第 $n$ 步自己的结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two rules for a coin game
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小下注例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\xi_1,\xi_2,\ldots$ be independent fair coin results, coded as $-1$ for a loss and $1$ for a win, each with probability $1/2$. Use the [[自然滤过|natural filtration]] $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$, meaning that exactly the first $n$ results have been revealed; $\mathcal F_0$ reveals none. The [[事件指示变量|indicator]] $\mathbf1_A$ equals $1$ if event $A$ occurs and $0$ otherwise. Consider the rule “stake one unit initially, then stake two units only after a loss”:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $\xi_1,\xi_2,\ldots$ 为相互独立的公平硬币结果，把输记作 $-1$、赢记作 $1$，概率各为 $1/2$。使用[[自然滤过]] $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$，表示恰好已揭示前 $n$ 次结果；$\mathcal F_0$ 尚未揭示任何结果。[[事件指示变量]] $\mathbf1_A$ 在事件 $A$ 发生时为 $1$，否则为 $0$。考虑“第一轮下注一个单位，以后仅在上一轮输了时下注两个单位”的规则：

$$
H_1=1,\qquad H_n=2\mathbf1_{\{\xi_{n-1}=-1\}}\quad(n\ge2).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This rule is predictable: the first stake is fixed, and every later stake uses only the previous result. Compare it with $K_n=2\mathbf1_{\{\xi_n=1\}}$, which stakes two units only on rounds that turn out to win. The second round exposes the difference:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个规则可预测：第一笔下注是固定值，以后的下注只使用上一轮结果。对比 $K_n=2\mathbf1_{\{\xi_n=1\}}$，它仅在本轮结果为赢时下注两个单位。只看第二轮，就能看出区别：

| $\xi_1$ | $\xi_2$ | $H_2$ | $K_2$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $-1$ | $-1$ | $2$ | $0$ |
| $-1$ | $1$ | $2$ | $2$ |
| $1$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $0$ |
| $1$ | $1$ | $0$ | $2$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before toss $2$, we know which pair of rows applies from $\xi_1$. Within that pair, $H_2$ is constant, but $K_2$ still changes with the unseen $\xi_2$. Each row has probability $1/4$, so this is not an ambiguity confined to a probability-zero event. Thus $K$ is adapted but not predictable for this filtration. If the information were enlarged to reveal the next coin early, predictability would have to be reassessed relative to that new information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二次投掷之前，已知的 $\xi_1$ 告诉我们落在哪一对行中。在这对行内，$H_2$ 不变，$K_2$ 却仍随尚未看见的 $\xi_2$ 改变。每行的概率都是 $1/4$，所以这种不确定性并非只出现在零概率事件上。因此，$K$ 相对于这个滤过适应，却不可预测。若信息扩大到提前揭示下一枚硬币，就必须相对于新的信息重新判断可预测性。

> [!tip]- Why stopped bets use $\{T\ge n\}$ · 停止下注为何出现 $\{T\ge n\}$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A discrete [[停时定义|stopping time]] $T\in\{0,1,\ldots,\infty\}$ lets us decide by time $n-1$ whether we have already stopped. Hence $\{T\ge n\}=\{T\le n-1\}^{c}\in\mathcal F_{n-1}$ for $n\ge1$. The choice $H_n=\mathbf1_{\{T\ge n\}}$ is predictable: it includes step $n$ exactly when stopping has not occurred before it. This is the information condition used in the [[停止过程保持鞅|stopped-martingale argument]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 离散[[停时定义|停时]] $T\in\{0,1,\ldots,\infty\}$ 使我们在时刻 $n-1$ 就能判断是否已经停止。因此，对 $n\ge1$，有 $\{T\ge n\}=\{T\le n-1\}^{c}\in\mathcal F_{n-1}$。选择 $H_n=\mathbf1_{\{T\ge n\}}$ 就是可预测的：只要第 $n$ 步之前尚未停止，就把这一步包括进来。[[停止过程保持鞅]]的论证正使用这一信息条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This definition is specific to discrete time. In continuous time, predictability is defined through a sigma-algebra on time and outcomes; simply replacing $n-1$ by $t-$ is not an unconditional equivalent definition for arbitrary processes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的定义专用于离散时间。连续时间的可预测性通过时间与随机结果上的一个 σ-代数定义；对任意过程，不能不加条件地把 $n-1$ 换成 $t-$ 当作等价定义。
