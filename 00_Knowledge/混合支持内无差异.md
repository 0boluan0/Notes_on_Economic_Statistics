# 信号或回应以正概率混合时，支持内各选择必须产生相同且最大的条件期望收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Whenever signals or responses are mixed with positive probability, every choice in the support must yield the same maximal conditional expected payoff*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

混合不是给多个选择随意分配正概率。在线性期望收益下，只有同为最佳反应的选择才能同时进入策略支持；若其中一个选择严格更差，玩家把它的概率移向更优选择就能提高收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mixing does not permit arbitrary positive probabilities on several choices. With expected utility linear in the mixing probabilities, only choices that are all best responses can simultaneously enter the support. If one supported choice were strictly worse, shifting its probability to a better choice would raise payoff.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 发送者类型在信号之间混合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Sender Type Mixing Across Signals*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定接收者的完整反应 $\sigma_2$，若类型 $\theta$ 对两个信号 $s$ 与 $s'$ 都赋严格正概率，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given the receiver's complete response strategy $\sigma_2$, if type $\theta$ assigns strictly positive probability to both $s$ and $s'$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
U_1(\theta,s;\sigma_2)
=U_1(\theta,s';\sigma_2)
=\max_{\hat s\in S}U_1(\theta,\hat s;\sigma_2).
$$

因此每个进入类型支持的信号都必须实现同一个最大收益；支持外信号可以更差或同样好，但不能严格更好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus every signal in the type's support must attain the same maximal payoff. A signal outside the support may be worse or equally good, but it cannot be strictly better.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 接收者在行动之间混合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Receiver Mixing Across Actions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看到路径上信号 $s$ 后，接收者按 posterior $p(\cdot\mid s)$ 评价行动。若行动 $a$ 与 $a'$ 都被赋予严格正概率，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After observing an on-path signal $s$, the receiver evaluates actions under posterior $p(\cdot\mid s)$. If both $a$ and $a'$ receive strictly positive probability, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mathbb E_{p(\cdot\mid s)}[u_2(\theta,s,a)]
=
\mathbb E_{p(\cdot\mid s)}[u_2(\theta,s,a')]
=
\max_{\hat a\in A}
\mathbb E_{p(\cdot\mid s)}[u_2(\theta,s,\hat a)].
$$

若 $s$ 位于路径外，同一支持内无差异条件仍适用，只是期望收益改由候选 assessment 指定的路径外 belief 计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $s$ is off path, the same support-indifference condition applies, but expected payoffs are computed using the off-path belief specified by the candidate assessment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在半分离均衡中，发送者的混合概率决定 posterior，posterior 又可能把接收者放在无差异点；接收者的混合概率反过来使某个发送者类型在多个信号之间无差异。这些条件通常需要联立求解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a semi-separating equilibrium, sender mixing determines the posterior, which may place the receiver at an indifference point. The receiver's mixing probability can in turn make a sender type indifferent across signals. These conditions generally have to be solved jointly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 纯策略不要求人为制造无差异
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pure Strategies Do Not Require Artificial Indifference*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若玩家以概率 1 选择一项行动，均衡只要求该行动是最佳反应；它可以严格优于其他行动，也可以与其他行动并列最优。支持内无差异约束只在至少两个选择都以正概率出现时产生。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a player chooses one action with probability one, equilibrium requires only that action to be a best response. It may be strictly better than all alternatives or tied with some of them. Support indifference becomes necessary only when at least two choices are played with positive probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
