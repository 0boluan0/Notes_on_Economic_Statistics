# 大数定律与中心极限定理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The law of large numbers and the central limit theorem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释大量重复观测的平均为什么趋于稳定，以及标准化后的随机和为什么常近似正态。
> **具体锚点：** 投掷公平硬币越多，正面比例越接近 $1/2$；但比例围绕 $1/2$ 的剩余波动约按 $1/\sqrt n$ 缩小。
> **核心难点：** LLN 给收敛，CLT 给缩放后的近似分布；二者都依赖条件，并不消除偏差、依赖或厚尾问题。
> **为什么重要：** 样本均值、标准误、置信区间、Monte Carlo 精度和许多渐近推断都依赖它们。
> **继续：** 使用前先在 [[随机变量、分布与矩]] 检查矩，并在 [[条件期望与独立性]] 检查依赖结构。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It explains why averages of many repeated observations stabilise and why standardised random sums are often approximately normal.
> **Concrete anchor:** As a fair coin is tossed more often, the proportion of heads approaches $1/2$, while the remaining fluctuation around $1/2$ shrinks at roughly $1/\sqrt n$.
> **Central difficulty:** The LLN gives convergence, whereas the CLT gives an approximate distribution after scaling. Both require conditions and neither removes bias, dependence, or heavy-tail problems.
> **Why it matters:** Sample means, standard errors, confidence intervals, Monte Carlo precision, and much asymptotic inference depend on these results.
> **Continue with:** Before applying them, check moments in [[随机变量、分布与矩|random variables, distributions, and moments]] and dependence in [[条件期望与独立性|conditional expectation and independence]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 极限定理入口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Entry point to limit theorems*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

大数定律解释样本平均为何稳定到期望；中心极限定理描述适当标准化后的和为何近似正态。它们需要条件，不能把“样本大”当万能通行证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The law of large numbers explains why a sample average stabilises around its expectation. The central limit theorem explains why a suitably standardised sum is approximately normal. Both require assumptions; “a large sample” is not a universal licence to ignore them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 大数定律：稳定到什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The law of large numbers: what stabilises, and to what*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 i.i.d. 且 $E|X_i|<\infty$ 的变量，样本均值 $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_iX_i$ 在适当大数定律下收敛到 $\mu=E[X_i]$。弱大数定律通常给依概率收敛，强大数定律给几乎处处收敛；后者更强，但两者都不直接给有限 n 的误差概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For iid variables with $E|X_i|<\infty$, the sample mean $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_iX_i$ converges to $\mu=E[X_i]$ under an appropriate law of large numbers. A weak LLN usually gives convergence in probability, while a strong LLN gives almost-sure convergence. The latter is stronger, but neither by itself quantifies the finite-$n$ error probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

LLN 只把随机误差平均掉。若测量装置有系统偏差、样本选择不代表目标总体、或观测之间存在强烈共同冲击，平均可以非常稳定地收敛到错误目标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The LLN averages out random error only. With systematic measurement bias, non-representative sampling, or strong common shocks across observations, the average can converge very precisely to the wrong target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 中心极限定理：为什么要标准化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The central limit theorem: why standardisation is necessary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $X_i$ i.i.d.，均值为 $\mu$、方差为 $\sigma^2<\infty$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the $X_i$ are iid with mean $\mu$ and finite variance $\sigma^2$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\sqrt n(\bar X_n-\mu)}{\sigma}\Rightarrow N(0,1).
$$

不乘 $\sqrt n$ 时，$\bar X_n-\mu$ 会退化到 0；标准化把不断缩小的波动放回固定尺度，才得到非退化极限分布。实践中用样本标准差替代未知 $\sigma$ 需要额外的 Slutsky 型论证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without multiplying by $\sqrt n$, $\bar X_n-\mu$ collapses to zero. Standardisation rescales the shrinking fluctuation to a fixed scale and yields a non-degenerate limiting distribution. Replacing unknown $\sigma$ by a sample standard deviation in practice requires an additional Slutsky-type argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：400 次投币
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: 400 coin tosses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $S\sim Binomial(400,0.5)$ 表示正面数，则 $E[S]=200$、$\operatorname{sd}(S)=10$。要近似 $P(S>220)$，连续性修正给 $P(S\ge221)\approx P(N(200,100)>220.5)$，标准化阈值为 2.05，所以概率约为 $1-\Phi(2.05)\approx0.020$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $S\sim Binomial(400,0.5)$ be the number of heads. Then $E[S]=200$ and $\operatorname{sd}(S)=10$. To approximate $P(S>220)$, a continuity correction gives $P(S\ge221)\approx P(N(200,100)>220.5)$. The standardised threshold is 2.05, so the probability is about $1-\Phi(2.05)\approx0.020$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 n 很小、p 极靠近 0 或 1，正态近似可能差；此时优先用精确 Binomial 概率或检查 $np$ 与 $n(1-p)$ 是否足够大。连续性修正不是 CLT 的核心，却常改善离散计数的有限样本近似。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $n$ is small or $p$ is close to zero or one, the normal approximation may be poor. Use the exact Binomial probability or check whether both $np$ and $n(1-p)$ are sufficiently large. Continuity correction is not the essence of the CLT, but it often improves finite-sample approximations for discrete counts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应用前依次检查：目标是否真是和或均值；观测是否独立或满足可用的弱依赖条件；方差是否有限；是否存在一个观测主导总和；以及所需尾部精度是否远超中心近似。厚尾、强依赖、结构变化和稀有事件都可能需要不同极限定理或有限样本方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before applying a limit theorem, check in order whether the target is actually a sum or average, whether observations are independent or satisfy a suitable weak-dependence condition, whether variance is finite, whether one observation dominates the sum, and whether the required tail accuracy exceeds what a central approximation can provide. Heavy tails, strong dependence, structural change, and rare events may require different limit theorems or finite-sample methods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话区分 LLN 与 CLT。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distinguish the LLN from the CLT in your own words.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> LLN 说明样本均值靠近总体期望；CLT 说明把这个误差乘 $\sqrt n$ 并按标准差缩放后，其分布常近似正态。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The LLN says that the sample mean approaches the population expectation. The CLT says that, after multiplying the error by $\sqrt n$ and scaling by the standard deviation, its distribution is often approximately normal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么增加样本不能消除系统偏差？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can increasing the sample size not remove systematic bias?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 大样本缩小围绕当前目标的随机波动，却不改变错误的测量机制或抽样总体，因此可能更精确地估计错误对象。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A larger sample reduces random fluctuation around the current target but does not repair a biased measurement mechanism or a mismatched sampling population, so it may estimate the wrong object more precisely.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 方差不存在时能否直接套用经典 i.i.d. CLT？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Can the classical iid CLT be applied directly when variance does not exist?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不能。有限方差是经典形式的关键条件；厚尾情形可能有不同缩放和稳定分布极限。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. Finite variance is a key condition of the classical form. Heavy-tailed settings may require different scaling and may converge to a stable distribution instead.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
