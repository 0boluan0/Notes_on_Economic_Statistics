# Marginal rate of substitution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*边际替代率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **marginal rate of substitution** describes a local trade-off: if the consumer gets a little more of one good, at what rate can the other good be given up while keeping the same preference level? A direction and units are essential. Here good 1 increases and good 2 is surrendered; the rate is measured in units of good 2 per unit of good 1.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**边际替代率**描述局部交换：消费者多获得一点某种商品时，为了保持同样的偏好水平，另一种商品可以按多大的速率减少？必须先说明方向和单位。这里增加商品1、放弃商品2，速率的单位是“每单位商品1对应多少单位商品2”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the same indifference curve
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*保持在同一条无差异曲线上*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $u(x_1,x_2)$ for a [[效用函数|utility representation]] of two-good bundles. Larger utility means a better ranking; equal utility means indifference. Fix a real number $k$. The bundles satisfying $u(x_1,x_2)=k$ form a [[水平集|level set]], called an [[无差异曲线|indifference curve]] in this two-good setting. The set need not be a smooth curve without further assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $u(x_1,x_2)$ 表示二商品组合的[[效用函数|效用]]：数值较大表示排序较好，相等表示无差异。固定实数 $k$，满足 $u(x_1,x_2)=k$ 的组合构成[[水平集]]，在二商品语境中称为[[无差异曲线]]。没有额外条件时，这个集合未必是一条光滑曲线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $u_i=\partial u/\partial x_i$ denote the [[偏导数|partial derivative]] with respect to good $i$, holding the other quantity fixed; economically it is that good's marginal utility. Suppose $u$ has continuous first partial derivatives ($C^1$) on an open neighbourhood of a point and $u_2\ne0$ at that point. The [[隐函数定理|implicit function theorem]] supplies a local $C^1$ graph $x_2=\phi(x_1)$ of the same level set. This is a sufficient regularity condition for the ratio calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $u_i=\partial u/\partial x_i$ 为关于商品 $i$ 的[[偏导数]]，求导时固定另一种商品数量；经济上称它为该商品的边际效用。设 $u$ 在某点的开邻域上有连续一阶偏导数（$C^1$），且该点 $u_2\ne0$。[[隐函数定理]]保证同一水平集在局部有 $C^1$ 图像 $x_2=\phi(x_1)$。这是使用导数比率的一组充分正则性条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiating the identity $u(x_1,\phi(x_1))=k$ by the [[多元链式法则|chain rule]] gives the balance below. The two derivatives of $u$ are evaluated at the same bundle on the curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对恒等式 $u(x_1,\phi(x_1))=k$ 使用[[多元链式法则]]，得到下面的平衡式。$u$ 的两个导数都在曲线上的同一组合处计算。

$$
u_1(x_1,\phi(x_1))+u_2(x_1,\phi(x_1))\phi'(x_1)=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Implicit differentiation of a regular indifference curve gives the ratio and its sign convention. With positive marginal utilities it measures units of good two surrendered per extra unit of good one. Reversing the goods takes the reciprocal. Corners and a zero denominator require separate treatment. [[生产配置的MRS与MRT|The productive-efficiency condition]] compares consistently oriented ratios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对可微二商品效用 $u(x_1,x_2)$，在 $u_2\ne0$ 的正规无差异曲线段上，$u_1dx_1+u_2dx_2=0$。采用“为一单位商品 $1$ 放弃多少商品 $2$”的方向，定义
$$
MRS_{12}=\frac{u_1}{u_2}=-\frac{dx_2}{dx_1}.
$$
边际效用均正时此比率正；它是曲线斜率的相反数。对 $u=x_1x_2$，$MRS_{12}=x_2/x_1$。交换商品编号会取倒数，所以使用时必须说明方向。折点或分母为零时不能直接用该比值；[[生产配置的MRS与MRT]]将同方向比率用于效率比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivative equation is exact; the finite-change approximation is $\Delta x_2\approx-MRS_{12}\,\Delta x_1$ for a sufficiently small movement along the curve. With $u_1,u_2>0$, its slope is negative and our MRS is positive. If marginal utilities have other signs, the signed ratio still has its algebraic meaning when defined, but need not measure a positive amount of a desirable good surrendered. Some sources use the signed slope itself as “MRS”; always check their convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

导数等式是精确的；有限变化的近似是沿曲线充分小移动时 $\Delta x_2\approx-MRS_{12}\,\Delta x_1$。当 $u_1,u_2>0$，曲线斜率为负，而这里的 MRS 为正。若边际效用符号不同，比率在有定义时仍有代数意义，却未必表示愿意放弃某种好商品的正数量。有些来源直接把带符号的斜率称为“MRS”，使用前应核对约定。

> [!example] A rate of three is not an exact trade of three · 速率为三不等于整单位精确换三
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On the positive quadrant let $u=x_1x_2$. At $(2,6)$, utility is $12$ and $MRS_{12}=6/2=3$. If good 1 rises by $0.1$, keeping utility exactly $12$ requires $x_2=12/2.1=40/7$; the loss of good 2 is $6-40/7=2/7\approx0.286$, close to the linear estimate $3(0.1)=0.3$. For a whole-unit increase to $x_1=3$, however, $x_2=4$ and the exact loss is $2$, not $3$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在正象限上令 $u=x_1x_2$。$(2,6)$ 的效用为 $12$，且 $MRS_{12}=6/2=3$。若商品1增加 $0.1$，要使效用精确保持 $12$，必须令 $x_2=12/2.1=40/7$，商品2减少 $6-40/7=2/7\approx0.286$，接近线性估计 $3(0.1)=0.3$。但若商品1整整增加一单位至 $x_1=3$，则 $x_2=4$，实际减少的是 $2$，不是 $3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reversing the direction gives $MRS_{21}=u_2/u_1=1/MRS_{12}$ **when both denominators are nonzero**. In the example the reverse rate is $1/3$ unit of good 1 per unit of good 2. It is not a market price: it describes the consumer's local willingness to trade at this bundle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来考察商品2增加、商品1减少，得到 $MRS_{21}=u_2/u_1=1/MRS_{12}$，前提是**两个分母都非零**。本例反向速率是每单位商品2对应 $1/3$ 单位商品1。它不是市场价格，而是消费者在这个组合处的局部交换意愿。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A numerical utility scale does not determine the trade-off
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*数值效用尺度不决定交换意愿*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $v=g\circ u$ represents the same ordering through a strictly increasing differentiable $g$, and $g'(u)>0$ at the point, the chain rule gives $v_i=g'(u)u_i$. The common positive factor cancels, so $v_1/v_2=u_1/u_2$. This makes the regular ratio an ordinal trade-off rather than a comparison of absolute utility units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若通过严格递增且可导的 $g$，$v=g\circ u$ 表示同样的排序，并且该点 $g'(u)>0$，则链式法则给出 $v_i=g'(u)u_i$。共同的正因子消去，得到 $v_1/v_2=u_1/u_2$。因此，正则条件下的比率描述序数交换关系，而非绝对效用单位的比较。

> [!warning] The curve can survive when the ratio fails · 曲线仍在，比率却可能失效
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $u=x_1+x_2$ on the positive quadrant, the level $u=2$ is a straight indifference curve with slope $-1$. The strictly increasing transformation $v=(u-2)^3$ represents exactly the same preferences, but both partial derivatives of $v$ vanish on that curve: its ratio is $0/0$. Likewise, $u=x_1$ has vertical indifference lines and $u_2=0$; $u=\min\{x_1,x_2\}$ has a corner at $x_1=x_2>0$. A missing derivative ratio is not proof that preference comparisons cease to exist.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在正象限上取 $u=x_1+x_2$，水平 $u=2$ 是斜率为 $-1$ 的直线无差异曲线。严格递增变换 $v=(u-2)^3$ 表示完全相同的偏好，但 $v$ 的两个偏导数在这条曲线上都为零，比率成为 $0/0$。同样，$u=x_1$ 的无差异线竖直，$u_2=0$；$u=\min\{x_1,x_2\}$ 在 $x_1=x_2>0$ 处有折点。导数比率失效不意味着偏好比较本身消失。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether the rate declines as one moves along a curve is the separate result [[拟凹效用的递减MRS|diminishing MRS under quasiconcavity and regularity]]. Positive marginal utilities alone do not prove it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿一条曲线移动时，该比率是否递减，是[[拟凹效用的递减MRS]]中的独立结论；仅有正边际效用并不能证明它。
