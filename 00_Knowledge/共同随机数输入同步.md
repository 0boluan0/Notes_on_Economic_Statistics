# Synchronising common random numbers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*共同随机数的输入同步*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Input synchronisation** keeps track of what a shared random input means in each simulator. In [[共同随机数|common random numbers]], we want a particular customer, day or demand shock to be paired with its intended counterpart in the other alternative. A seed is an initialisation input for a [[伪随机数生成器|pseudorandom generator]], which produces a deterministic sequence from its state. With the same generator and initialisation protocol, giving two programs the same seed starts their streams at the same place; it does not keep their input meanings aligned after their execution paths diverge.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**输入同步**追踪共享随机输入在两个模拟器里分别代表什么。使用[[共同随机数]]时，通常希望把某个顾客、某一天或某次需求冲击，与另一方案中预定的对应对象配起来。seed 是[[伪随机数生成器]]的初始化输入；生成器根据内部状态产生确定性的数列。在生成器与初始化协议相同时，给两个程序相同 seed 可以让随机流从同一位置开始，但不会在执行路径分叉后自动维持输入含义上的对齐。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An extra draw shifts the later inputs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*额外抽一次数，后续用途就可能错位*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a generator, under a fixed algorithm and calling protocol, supplies the illustrative sequence $0.2,0.8,0.4,0.6,0.1,\ldots$. These are primitive inputs that the simulator transforms into demand or delivery times. Policy A draws demand, then delivery time, each month. Policy B does the same but, in this run, draws an extra emergency-shipping input after month 1 because its inventory has run out.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设某个生成器在固定算法与调用协议下给出示例序列 $0.2,0.8,0.4,0.6,0.1,\ldots$。模拟器把这些基础输入转换为需求量或交付时间。政策 A 每月先抽需求，再抽交付时间；政策 B 通常也这样做，但本次第 1 月缺货，于是在该月之后额外抽一次加急运输输入。

| Stream position / 流位置 | Value / 数值 | Policy A use / A 的用途 | Policy B use / B 的用途 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $0.2$ | Month 1 demand / 第 1 月需求 | Month 1 demand / 第 1 月需求 |
| 2 | $0.8$ | Month 1 delivery / 第 1 月交付 | Month 1 delivery / 第 1 月交付 |
| 3 | $0.4$ | Month 2 demand / 第 2 月需求 | Emergency shipping / 加急运输 |
| 4 | $0.6$ | Month 2 delivery / 第 2 月交付 | Month 2 demand / 第 2 月需求 |
| 5 | $0.1$ | Later input / 后续输入 | Month 2 delivery / 第 2 月交付 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both programs started from the same seed, yet their month 2 demands use different inputs: $0.4$ and $0.6$. Comparing the third generated number in each program misses the problem because its numeric value matches while its meaning differs. A [[伪随机复现|replay protocol]] must record the generator and calls; a synchronisation protocol must additionally record the mapping from calls to simulated objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个程序虽然 seed 相同，第 2 月需求却用了不同输入：$0.4$ 与 $0.6$。只比较“第 3 个生成的数”会漏掉问题，因为数值相同，用途却不同。[[伪随机复现|重放协议]]需要记录生成器与调用；同步协议还要记录这些调用映射到哪个模拟对象。

> [!warning] A different coupling need not change the target · 换了耦合，不一定换了目标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Losing the intended alignment can destroy the planned positive covariance and its precision gain. It does **not by itself** prove a biased estimate. If each alternative still has its correct output distribution and finite absolute mean, the mean of $A-B$ is still $E[A]-E[B]$. The damage can be a worse coupling rather than a wrong marginal model. Conversely, reusing one draw for two shocks that should be independent within a single alternative can change that alternative's model and its target.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 预定对齐丢失，可能破坏计划中的正协方差及精度收益，但**本身并不证明估计有偏**。如果各方案仍具有正确的输出分布与有限绝对均值，$A-B$ 的均值仍是 $E[A]-E[B]$；问题可能是耦合变差，而非边际模型错误。反过来，若在单一方案内部，把一个数重复用于本应独立的两种冲击，就可能改变该方案的模型与目标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Give the inputs stable identities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*给输入稳定的身份*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simple design generates one complete **exogenous scenario** before running either alternative: inputs coming from outside the policies, such as a sequence of customer arrivals. Store inputs by identity, for example `(replication, month, shock type)`. Both policies read the same month 2 demand entry. Policy B reads a separate emergency entry when it needs one; policy A may leave that entry unused. Reserving it prevents the extra branch from consuming next month's demand input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一种简单设计是在运行任何方案前，先生成完整的**外生场景**，即政策之外给定的输入，例如顾客到达序列。按身份存储输入，比如 `(复制编号, 月份, 冲击类型)`。两政策都读取同一条第 2 月需求；B 需要加急时读取另一个加急条目，A 可以不用它。预留条目，使额外分支不会消耗下个月的需求输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a complete scenario is too large, documented [[并行伪随机子流|random substreams]] can separate demand, delivery and emergency inputs. A substream is a managed portion or derived stream of generator outputs with a recorded identity. Separating shock types prevents emergency calls from advancing the demand stream. It does not solve every alignment problem: if one policy skips a demand draw or processes customers in a different order, the demand stream itself can still become misaligned. Then include a stable customer or event identifier, and an occurrence index when one object needs several draws, in the allocation design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完整场景太大时，可以用有文档依据的[[并行伪随机子流|随机子流]]分离需求、交付与加急输入。子流是有记录身份、受管理的一段或派生随机序列。按冲击类型分开，可以防止加急调用推进需求流，但不解决所有同步问题：若某政策跳过一次需求抽样，或按不同顺序处理顾客，需求流内部仍会错位。这时分配设计还需要稳定的顾客或事件标识；同一对象需要多次抽样时，再加发生次序。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, suppose customer 7 is served before customer 6 under one policy but after customer 6 under the other. If service randomness is meant to describe each customer, assign its primitive input by **customer identity**, not “the next service completion”. Different policies may transform that input differently when their service-time distributions differ. The waiting time or completion time is an outcome of the policy and need not match. Synchronisation concerns the specified underlying inputs, not forcing all event times or outcomes to be equal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比如政策 A 先服务顾客 7、再服务顾客 6，而政策 B 顺序相反。如果服务随机性原本描述每个顾客，就应按**顾客身份**分配基础输入，不能按“下一次服务完成”分配。两政策服务时间分布不同时，也可以用不同转换处理同一基础输入。等待时间、完成时间是政策造成的输出，不必相同。同步针对预先说明的基础输入，并不是强迫所有事件时间或结果一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These constructions must preserve the full input law required by each policy, including dependence within its simulated path. They are design patterns, not a rule that every input must be shared. A comparison can share customer arrivals while leaving policy-specific service randomness separate. Document exactly which components are common and which are not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些构造必须保留每个政策需要的完整输入分布，包括模拟路径内部的依赖。它们是设计方式，不是“所有输入都必须共享”的要求。比较可以共享顾客到达，而让各政策特有的服务随机性分开；应明确记录哪些部分共用、哪些不共用。

## Restart within a pair, advance between pairs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一对内部重放，不同对之间前进*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each replication $i$, reserve a fresh scenario or substream, run A, and make the intended same inputs available to B. Then move to a new replication. Restarting at the recorded beginning lets the two alternatives start together, but their internal input mapping still needs the checks above. Resetting every replication to the same initial state would instead copy one scenario repeatedly. The usual paired [[Monte Carlo均值标准误|Monte Carlo standard error]] assumes independent complete paired runs with a common law and finite output second moments; repeated copies do not supply that sampling design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每次复制 $i$ 使用新的场景或子流，先运行 A，再把预定共享的输入提供给 B，随后进入下一次复制。从记录的起点重放，只能让两个方案从同一处开始；内部输入映射仍需上述检查。如果每次复制都重置到同一个初始状态，就只是把一个场景反复抄写。通常的配对 [[Monte Carlo均值标准误]]假设完整配对运行之间独立、同分布，且输出二阶矩有限；重复副本不符合这种抽样设计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A library's stream guarantees matter here. NumPy's compatibility policy requires the same generator, seed, sequence of calls and arguments, build, environment and machine for its stated replay guarantee; even replacing repeated scalar draws by one array draw is not a universal stream-equivalence promise. Its parallel-generation documentation provides spawning, keyed streams and jumping schemes with their stated guarantees. Different-looking seeds alone prove neither independence nor semantic alignment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里还要遵守库的随机流保证。NumPy 的兼容政策要求生成器、seed、调用及参数序列、build、环境与机器等一致，才适用其明示的重放保证；把多次标量抽样改成一次数组抽样，也不具有普遍的流等价承诺。并行生成文档提供 spawning、keyed streams、jumping 等机制及其保证；seed 看起来不同，本身既不证明独立，也不证明语义对齐。

## Check the mapping with a controlled trace
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用可控制的调用记录核对映射*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a small deterministic input table such as the one above and force both the ordinary branch and the emergency branch. Log the replication, object identity, shock type, occurrence and consumed value. Verify that intended shared keys receive the same primitive values and that a branch-specific call does not steal another key's value. For a queue, also change the processing order and check that customer-specific inputs remain attached to the correct customers. This is a concrete [[Monte Carlo实现验证|implementation check]], not a statistical proof from matching seeds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用上面那样可控制的小输入表，强制走普通分支与加急分支，记录复制编号、对象身份、冲击类型、发生次序和使用值。检查预定共享的键是否得到相同基础数值，分支专用调用是否抢用了别的键。对于队列，还应改变处理顺序，检查顾客专属输入是否仍绑定正确顾客。这是具体的[[Monte Carlo实现验证]]，不是从 seed 相同推出统计证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, check each simulator against its own model and examine the paired output covariance. Correct alignment can preserve a chosen coupling without making its covariance positive. Whether the difference becomes more precise is the mathematical question in [[配对方差缩减的协方差方向|the covariance-direction criterion]], not something a successful trace test settles.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后仍需分别核对每个模拟器自己的模型，并检查配对输出的协方差。正确同步可以保住选定的耦合，却不会自动使协方差为正。差值是否更精确，要由[[配对方差缩减的协方差方向]]判断，不能由一次调用记录测试通过来决定。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Art B. Owen, [Chapter 8, §8.6, PDF pp. 17–18 and 19–22](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/Ch-var-basic.pdf#page=17): preserved-marginal coupling, reserving second-stage inputs, complete-input algorithms and partial common randomness. The inventory and customer-identity traces here are direct illustrations of those input-allocation principles.
- Art B. Owen, [Chapter 3, §3.2, PDF pp. 6–7, especially Algorithm 3.1](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/Ch-unifrng.pdf#page=6): separate streams by purpose, a new substream for each replication, restarting within a pair, and the explicit warning that internal details determine continued synchronisation.
- NumPy, [Compatibility policy](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/reference/random/compatibility.html) and [Parallel random number generation](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/reference/random/parallel.html): actual stream-compatibility conditions, scalar/array-call limitation, and documented stream derivation. These implementation guarantees do not establish the correctness of a simulation model or positive output covariance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Owen 第 8 章 §8.6（PDF pp. 17–18、19–22）核对保留边际的耦合、预留第二阶段输入、完整输入算法及部分共享。库存与顾客身份记录是这些输入分配原则的直接示例。
- Owen 第 3 章 §3.2（PDF pp. 6–7，尤其算法 3.1）核对按用途分流、每次复制使用新子流、一对内部重放，以及内部实现决定能否继续同步的明确边界。
- NumPy 的兼容性与并行生成文档核对实际的重放条件、标量与数组调用边界和文档化子流派生。这些实现保证不认证模拟模型正确，也不保证输出正协方差。
