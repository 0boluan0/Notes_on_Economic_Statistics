# 票面利率决定约定息票而贴现率评价未来支付的现值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The coupon rate determines contractual coupons; a discount rate values future payments today*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[债券票面利率]]是合约输入，用来算承诺付多少利息；贴现率是估值输入，用来把未来支付换成今天的价值。即使票面利率在发行后固定不变，市场要求的报酬率仍可改变。对按面值偿还、剩余完整付息期的规则息票债券，在付息后以单一收益率定价时，同周期票息率与收益率相等对应[[债券折溢价判别|平价]]；不能把这一特殊对应推广到任意期限贴现率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[债券票面利率|coupon rate]] is a contractual input specifying promised interest. A discount rate is a valuation input converting future payments into present value. The coupon can stay fixed after issuance while the market-required return changes. For a regular bond redeeming at par with complete periods remaining, valued just after a coupon at one yield, equality of period coupon rate and yield corresponds to [[债券折溢价判别|par pricing]]. This special correspondence does not extend to an arbitrary maturity-specific discount rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，面值 $100$、每年付息 $5$、两年后按面值偿还，以单一年收益率 $6\%$ 贴现：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, a bond with face value 100, annual coupons of 5, and redemption at par in two years has the following price at a single 6% annual yield:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P=\frac{5}{1.06}+\frac{105}{1.06^2}\approx98.1666.$$

分子中的 $5$ 来自票面利率 $5\%$；分母中的 $6\%$ 来自估值收益率。若收益率改为 $4\%$，同一串支付的现值约为 $101.8861$，息票仍是 $5$。反过来，已知价格而求使这条等式成立的单一收益率，才是在求[[到期收益率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The numerator 5 comes from the 5% coupon; the denominator uses the 6% valuation yield. At a 4% yield, the same payments are worth about 101.8861, but the coupon remains 5. Conversely, solving for the single yield that fits an observed price determines [[到期收益率|yield to maturity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
