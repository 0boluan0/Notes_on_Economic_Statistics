# 受偿顺序规定同一偿付资源不足时不同请求权的分配先后

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Claim priority specifies the order of allocation among claims when a shared repayment resource is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

受偿顺序回答“谁先从这笔资源中取得分配”，而不是“谁的证券名称听起来更安全”。需要先识别请求权针对的法律主体与资产池，再按适用法律、有效担保和合同中的优先／次级条款确定顺序。同一公司的不同[[债券]]可以有不同地位；不同主体或不同担保资产上的请求权，也不能未经分析就排在同一条队伍里。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Priority asks who receives a distribution from a particular resource first, not which security sounds safer. Identify the obligor and asset pool, then determine rank under applicable law, valid security interests and seniority or subordination terms. Different [[债券|bonds]] from one issuer can rank differently. Claims against different entities or collateral pools cannot simply be placed in a single queue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，在一个排除其他请求权和执行成本的简化合同模型中，可分配资源为 90，优先债权额为 60，次级债权额为 50，约定优先债权足额受偿后才向次级分配。于是两者分别收到 60 和 30，普通股为 0。优先债权收足只是此例资源够用的结果；若同一资源只有 40，它也只能收到 40。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simplified contractual model with no other claims or enforcement costs, suppose distributable resources are 90, the senior claim is 60 and the subordinated claim is 50, with full senior payment required before a junior distribution. They receive 60 and 30, respectively, and common equity receives zero. Full senior recovery follows from the resources in this example, not from rank alone: with only 40 available, the senior claim receives only 40.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“债权—[[优先股]]—[[普通股]]”是理解一般公司资本请求权的基本顺序，不是完整的破产法清偿表。税费、员工或其他法定请求权、担保物范围和程序费用等可能改变具体分配；无担保债也不等于没有任何请求权。只有一层债务的限定情况可直接复用[[股债到期分配]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Debt—[[优先股|preferred stock]]—[[普通股|common stock]]” is a basic ordering of corporate capital claims, not a complete insolvency-law waterfall. Tax, employee and other statutory claims, collateral scope and proceeding costs can affect actual allocations. An unsecured debt is not a claimless debt. For the restricted case with one debt layer, use [[股债到期分配|terminal debt–equity allocation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
