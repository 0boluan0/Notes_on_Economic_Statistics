# 财政自动稳定器是既有税收和转移制度随经济状态变化而自动缓冲收入与需求波动的机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Automatic fiscal stabilisers operate through existing tax and transfer systems to cushion fluctuations in income and demand as economic conditions change.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在既有税率、资格与给付规则下，收入下降会减少部分税收，符合资格的失业者可能增加福利领取。这些变化不需要为本轮经济波动重新立法，能缓冲私人可支配收入的下降。它是[[财政政策]]的一种自动反应机制，不是另行决定减税或增加项目支出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under existing tax rates and benefit rules, lower incomes reduce some tax payments and eligible unemployment can increase benefit claims. These responses require no new legislation for the current downturn and cushion disposable income. They are an automatic mechanism within [[财政政策|fiscal policy]], distinct from newly chosen tax cuts or spending programmes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设教学模型中税收为收入的 20%，暂不考虑转移。收入从 100 降到 90，税收从 20 降到 18，税后收入从 80 降到 72：税收随收入下降 2，吸收了原始收入损失 10 的一部分。这里没有改变税率，也没有据此计算总产出的乘数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an illustrative model with a 20% income tax and no transfers, income falls from 100 to 90. Tax falls from 20 to 18 and after-tax income from 80 to 72. The tax response absorbs 2 of the initial income loss of 10 without a rate change; this is not an output-multiplier calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

稳定作用取决于税基、给付资格、覆盖程度和家庭反应；“自动”不保证每项收入或支出都与周期反向变化，也不意味着完全消除波动。在其他条件相同时，这类反应可使衰退中的预算余额恶化，因此分析主动政策需进一步看[[周期调整预算余额]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The strength depends on tax bases, benefit eligibility, coverage and household responses. “Automatic” does not mean every fiscal item moves countercyclically or that fluctuations disappear. Other things equal, these responses can worsen the balance in a downturn, motivating the [[周期调整预算余额|cyclically adjusted balance]] when analysing discretionary changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
