# Sample size and simulation repetitions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*样本量与模拟重复次数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simulation of an estimator has two different counts. **Sample size $n$** says how many observations go into one estimate. **Repetitions $R$** says how many times we generate a fresh sample and run that same rule. Turning up $n$ changes the experiment performed in each run; turning up $R$ repeats the chosen experiment more often.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模拟估计量时有两个不同的计数。**样本量 $n$**表示算一个估计值用了多少个观察；**重复次数 $R$**表示重新生成样本、再运行同一规则多少次。增大 $n$ 改变每次实验的内容，增大 $R$ 则把选定的实验重复更多遍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recall that an [[估计对象三分|estimator]] is a rule applied to random data; one observed sample gives one numerical estimate. Its [[抽样分布|sampling distribution]] describes the rule's possible answers under a specified sampling model and sample size. The distribution exists before any simulation is run. Simulation approximates features of it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[估计对象三分|估计量]]是处理随机数据的规则，一份实际样本给出一个数值估计；[[抽样分布]]描述在指定抽样模型和样本量下，该规则会给出哪些答案、各有多大概率。这个分布在模拟之前就已经确定，模拟是在近似了解它。

> [!example] One sample contains two draws · 一份样本包含两次抽取
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Independently draw twice, uniformly and with replacement, from $0,2,4,6$, then average the two values. Here $n=2$. A run might draw $(0,6)$ and return $3$; the next might draw $(2,2)$ and return $2$. With $R=100$ independent runs, we generate $100$ pairs and record $100$ means, using $200$ simulated draws altogether.
>
> There are $16$ equally likely ordered pairs. Their means $0,1,2,3,4,5,6$ have respective probabilities $(1,2,3,4,3,2,1)/16$. Increasing $R$ from $100$ to $10{,}000$ does not change those seven probabilities. It gives more simulated means with which to approximate them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $0,2,4,6$ 中独立、均匀、有放回地抽两次，再取平均，此时 $n=2$。一次抽到 $(0,6)$，输出 $3$；下一次可能抽到 $(2,2)$，输出 $2$。独立重复 $R=100$ 次，会得到 $100$ 对样本、$100$ 个均值，总计使用 $200$ 次模拟抽取。共 $16$ 个等可能有序对，均值 $0$ 至 $6$ 的概率依次为 $(1,2,3,4,3,2,1)/16$。把重复次数改成 $10{,}000$ 不改变这七个概率，只增加用于近似它们的模拟答案。

## Follow one run, then the collection of runs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先看一次实验，再看重复实验的集合*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the output of run $r$ as $T_n^{(r)}$. The lower index $n$ records the observations used by the rule; the upper label $(r)$ identifies the run, not a power. For independent repetitions of the same experiment, $T_n^{(1)},\ldots,T_n^{(R)}$ are independent draws from the same distribution as $T_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把第 $r$ 次输出写为 $T_n^{(r)}$：下标 $n$ 记录规则使用的样本量，上标 $(r)$ 是实验编号，不是幂。独立重复同一实验时，这 $R$ 个输出都是从 $T_n$ 的同一个分布独立抽来的。

> [!important] The two controls change different objects · 两个计数改变不同对象
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At fixed $n$, the sampling law of a **single** $T_n$ does not depend on $R$. Increasing $R$ reduces Monte Carlo uncertainty in estimated summaries of that law under independent repetition. Changing $n$ can change the law of $T_n$ itself; whether it improves the estimator depends on the rule and sampling model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定 $n$ 时，**单个** $T_n$ 的抽样分布不取决于 $R$。独立重复下，增加 $R$ 能降低对该分布摘要进行模拟估计时的 Monte Carlo 不确定性。改变 $n$ 则可能改变 $T_n$ 本身的分布；是否改善估计，还要看规则和抽样模型。

^core

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the sample mean $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n X_i$ of [[独立同分布|iid]] observations with finite variance $\sigma^2$, [[样本均值方差判据|variance of the mean]] is $\sigma^2/n$. In the example, the population variance is $5$: a two-draw mean has variance $5/2$, while an eight-draw mean has variance $5/8$. This change comes from using more observations in each mean. Normality is not needed for this variance formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对具有有限方差 $\sigma^2$ 的[[独立同分布|iid]]观察，样本均值 $\bar X_n=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n X_i$ 的[[样本均值方差判据|方差]]为 $\sigma^2/n$。小例子总体方差为 $5$，两次抽取的均值方差为 $5/2$，八次抽取的均值方差为 $5/8$；改变来自每个均值使用更多观察。这个方差公式不要求正态性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In particular, for an iid normal sample with $n=10$, raising $R$ from $100$ to $10{,}000$ leaves the variance of each sample mean at $\sigma^2/10$. It does not turn it into $\sigma^2/10{,}000$. Conversely, a rule that always reports $X_1$ discards all later observations, so merely increasing the available $n$ does not change its law; see [[无偏不推出一致|the first-observation counterexample]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特别地，对 $n=10$ 的 iid 正态样本，把 $R$ 从 $100$ 改成 $10{,}000$，每个样本均值的方差仍是 $\sigma^2/10$，不会变成 $\sigma^2/10{,}000$。反过来，若规则永远只输出 $X_1$，后来观察全被丢弃，光增加可用的 $n$ 也不改变其分布，见[[无偏不推出一致|只用第一次观察的反例]]。

## What more repetitions can estimate more precisely
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*更多重复可以更准确地估计什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the two-draw example, the probability that a sample mean is at most $1$ is $p=3/16$: the qualifying pairs are $(0,0),(0,2),(2,0)$. To estimate this probability, mark each run with an [[事件指示变量|indicator]] $I_r$, equal to $1$ when its mean is at most $1$, and $0$ otherwise. The simulated proportion is
$$\hat p_R=\frac1R\sum_{r=1}^R I_r,\qquad
E[\hat p_R]=p,\qquad \operatorname{Var}(\hat p_R)=\frac{p(1-p)}R.$$
These identities follow from independent repetitions of a zero-or-one variable. The [[标准误含义|standard error]], the standard deviation of this simulated proportion, is $\sqrt{p(1-p)/R}$. Multiplying $R$ by $100$ divides that standard error by $10$, while the underlying probability remains $3/16$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两次抽取的例子中，均值不超过 $1$ 的概率是 $p=3/16$，对应 $(0,0),(0,2),(2,0)$。用[[事件指示变量]] $I_r$ 标记每次结果：均值不超过 $1$ 时记一，否则记零。$R$ 次标记的平均就是模拟比例 $\hat p_R$。独立重复使其期望等于 $p$、方差等于 $p(1-p)/R$，因此[[标准误含义|标准误]]为 $\sqrt{p(1-p)/R}$。重复次数乘以 $100$，该标准误除以 $10$，但原来的概率仍为 $3/16$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A larger $R$ does not guarantee that every newly realized histogram or proportion is closer than the preceding one; sampling noise remains. Nor does a discrete sampling law become a continuous density merely because its histogram uses more simulations. The seven-point law above remains discrete.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更大的 $R$ 不保证每次实际得到的直方图或比例都比上一次更接近真实值，随机波动仍存在。离散抽样分布也不会因为模拟更多次就变成连续密度；上面的分布始终只取七个值。

> [!warning] Averaging the simulated answers creates a new statistic · 再平均模拟答案，就定义了新统计量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the outputs have finite variance $v_n=\operatorname{Var}(T_n)$ and the $R$ runs are independent, their average $\bar T_{n,R}=R^{-1}\sum_r T_n^{(r)}$ has variance $v_n/R$. This is the variance of the **average of simulated outputs**, not of one $T_n$. It is a [[Monte Carlo估计|Monte Carlo estimate]] of $E[T_n]$; when $T_n$ is biased for the original target, averaging preserves that bias.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若输出方差 $v_n=\operatorname{Var}(T_n)$ 有限、$R$ 次实验独立，则输出的再平均 $\bar T_{n,R}$ 的方差为 $v_n/R$。这是**模拟答案的平均**的方差，不是单个 $T_n$ 的方差。它是对 $E[T_n]$ 的[[Monte Carlo估计]]；若原规则对目标有偏，再平均仍保留该偏差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Generating more data from a chosen model improves a numerical approximation under that model; it does not supply new real-world observations or correct a wrong model. If runs share random inputs or are otherwise dependent, the simple $1/R$ variance formula needs covariance terms. Always identify what one run contains, what is recorded, and which quantity is being averaged before interpreting a reported simulation precision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按选定模型生成更多模拟数据，改善的是该模型内的数值近似；它不会补出新的真实世界观察，也不会纠正错误模型。若不同实验共享随机输入或存在其他依赖，简单的 $1/R$ 方差公式需要加入协方差项。解释模拟精度之前，先确认一次实验包含什么、记录什么，以及最终在对哪个量求平均。
