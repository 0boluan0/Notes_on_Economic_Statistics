# Related rates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*相关变化率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Related rates connect the speeds at which linked quantities change. As a circular patch expands, its radius and its area both change, but in different units and at different rates. A geometric relation between the quantities becomes an equation between their derivatives when we differentiate with respect to the same time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相关变化率把彼此关联的量的变化速度联系起来。圆形区域扩张时，半径和面积都在变化，但单位与变化率不同。对同一个时间求导，就能把数量之间的几何关系变成导数之间的方程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the radius as $r(t)$ and area as $A(t)$, where $t$ is time. A prime here means the [[导数|time derivative]], so $r'=dr/dt$. The [[链式法则|chain rule]] says that an outer function's rate is multiplied by the changing inner input's rate:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把半径与面积写成 $r(t),A(t)$，其中 $t$ 是时间。这里撇号表示[[导数|对时间的导数]]，即 $r'=dr/dt$。[[链式法则]]要求把外层函数的变化率乘以内层输入的变化率：

$$A(t)=\pi r(t)^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad A'(t)=2\pi r(t)r'(t).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $r'=3\,\mathrm{cm/s}$, then $A'=6\pi r\,\mathrm{cm}^2/\mathrm{s}$ when the numerical value of $r$ is measured in centimetres. A current radius is still needed for a numerical area rate. At $r=2\,\mathrm{cm}$, the rate is $12\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2/\mathrm{s}$. The same radius rate at a larger radius produces a larger area rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $r'=3\,\mathrm{cm/s}$，把 $r$ 的数值用厘米计，就有 $A'=6\pi r\,\mathrm{cm}^2/\mathrm{s}$。要给出面积变化率的具体数值，还需要当前半径。在 $r=2\,\mathrm{cm}$ 时，面积变化率为 $12\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2/\mathrm{s}$。同样的半径增长速度，在更大的圆上会产生更大的面积增长速度。

## A radar measures one distance, but the car moves along another
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*雷达测一种距离，汽车沿另一方向移动*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Place a stationary radar $30\,\mathrm{ft}$ perpendicular to a straight road. Let $x(t)$ be the car's signed road coordinate measured from the closest point to the radar, and $D(t)>0$ its straight-line distance from the radar. Choose the positive road direction toward the side occupied by the car at the instant of interest. The right triangle gives an identity valid in a time interval around that instant:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把静止雷达放在距离笔直道路垂直 $30\,\mathrm{ft}$ 的位置。以道路上离雷达最近的点为原点，令 $x(t)$ 为汽车的有向道路坐标，$D(t)>0$ 为车到雷达的直线距离。选择所问时刻汽车所在的一侧为道路正方向。直角三角形给出在该时刻附近一段时间内都成立的恒等式：

$$x(t)^2+30^2=D(t)^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume the motion is differentiable at the instant being studied. Apply [[隐式求导|implicit differentiation]] with respect to $t$; both $x$ and $D$ depend on $t$, while $30$ is constant throughout:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设运动在所问时刻可导。对 $t$ 使用[[隐式求导]]：$x,D$ 都依赖时间，$30$ 则在整个过程中不变：

$$2xx'=2DD'.$$

> [!example] Find the road velocity / 求道路方向速度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When $D=50\,\mathrm{ft}$ and $D'=-80\,\mathrm{ft/s}$, the negative sign means the radar distance is shrinking. Our chosen side gives $x=\sqrt{50^2-30^2}=40\,\mathrm{ft}$. Hence $x'=(D/x)D'=(50/40)(-80)=-100\,\mathrm{ft/s}$. The car moves toward the origin at speed $|x'|=100\,\mathrm{ft/s}$. With the opposite coordinate orientation, $x=-40$ and $x'=100$ describe the very same motion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $D=50\,\mathrm{ft}$、$D'=-80\,\mathrm{ft/s}$，负号表示雷达距离正在缩小。按所选方向，$x=\sqrt{50^2-30^2}=40\,\mathrm{ft}$，所以 $x'=(D/x)D'=(50/40)(-80)=-100\,\mathrm{ft/s}$。汽车正以速率 $|x'|=100\,\mathrm{ft/s}$ 朝原点运动。若反转坐标方向，$x=-40$、$x'=100$ 描述的仍是同一运动。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The factor $D/x$ is dimensionless. The rate $D'$ is the component of the velocity along the line of sight; it need not equal the car's road speed. In this geometry $D>|x|$ when $x\ne0$, so $|D'|=|x|\,|x'|/D<|x'|$ whenever the car has nonzero speed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比例 $D/x$ 没有单位。$D'$ 是速度沿视线方向的分量，并不一定等于汽车沿道路的速率。此处当 $x\ne0$ 时，$D>|x|$；因此汽车速率非零时，$|D'|=|x|\,|x'|/D<|x'|$。

> [!warning] A value at one instant is not a constant function / 瞬时取值不是常函数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Replacing $D(t)$ by $50$ before differentiating would erase its known rate and falsely produce $2xx'=0$. The perpendicular distance $30$ may be substituted earlier because it is fixed throughout. The order is: write the relation valid over time, differentiate, then insert instantaneous values. A single equation true only at one instant cannot by itself be differentiated as an identity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若先把 $D(t)$ 替换成 $50$ 再求导，就会抹掉它已知的变化率，错误得到 $2xx'=0$。垂直距离 $30$ 可以提前代入，因为它全过程固定。顺序应是：写出随时间成立的关系，求导，再代入瞬时数值。只在一个时刻碰巧成立的等式，不能直接当恒等式求导。

## Use the dimensions of the moving shape
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*使用正在变化的那个图形的尺寸*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inverted conical tank has fixed height $10\,\mathrm{ft}$ and top radius $4\,\mathrm{ft}$. Let $h(t)$ be the water depth from its tip and $r(t)$ the water-surface radius. A vertical section gives similar triangles, meaning corresponding side lengths have equal ratios: $r/h=4/10$ for $h>0$. Thus $r=2h/5$, not the fixed top radius $4$. Using the volume of the water cone,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个尖端朝下的锥形水箱，高固定为 $10\,\mathrm{ft}$，顶部半径固定为 $4\,\mathrm{ft}$。令 $h(t)$ 为从尖端量起的水深，$r(t)$ 为水面半径。竖直截面的相似三角形意味着对应边成比例：$h>0$ 时 $r/h=4/10$，所以 $r=2h/5$，而不是固定的箱顶半径 $4$。使用水体所成圆锥的体积，

$$V(t)=\frac{\pi}{3}r(t)^2h(t)=\frac{4\pi}{75}h(t)^3,\qquad V'(t)=\frac{4\pi}{25}h(t)^2h'(t).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If water enters at $V'=2\,\mathrm{ft}^3/\mathrm{min}$, then at depth $h=5\,\mathrm{ft}$ we obtain $2=4\pi h'$, hence $h'=1/(2\pi)\,\mathrm{ft/min}$. This is half the tank's height, not half its volume: the volume fraction is $(5/10)^3=1/8$. Units also distinguish the given volume rate from the desired height rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若注水速率 $V'=2\,\mathrm{ft}^3/\mathrm{min}$，在水深 $h=5\,\mathrm{ft}$ 时便有 $2=4\pi h'$，所以 $h'=1/(2\pi)\,\mathrm{ft/min}$。这是水箱的一半高度，不是一半体积：体积分数为 $(5/10)^3=1/8$。单位也能区分已知的体积变化率与待求的水位变化率。

## Check the coefficient before solving for a rate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*解变化率之前检查系数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivative relation may be correct without determining the desired rate. At the closest road point, $x=0$ and $D=30$, the relation $xx'=DD'$ forces $D'=0$ but leaves $x'$ undetermined. Dividing by $x$ there is invalid. A car may pass that point with nonzero road velocity even though its radar distance is momentarily stationary. More information about the motion is needed at this configuration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求导关系即使正确，也未必能确定所求速率。在道路最近点，$x=0$、$D=30$，关系 $xx'=DD'$ 只要求 $D'=0$，没有确定 $x'$。这里不能除以 $x$。汽车可能以非零道路速度经过该点，而雷达距离恰好瞬时不变。这个位置需要更多运动信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In each problem, fix the coordinate directions and time unit, identify what changes and what stays fixed, and make sure the relation holds locally along the actual motion. After differentiation, retain every chain factor and solve only when the needed coefficient is nonzero. Interpret the final sign as direction or increase/decrease, and report speed as a nonnegative magnitude when that is what the question asks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个问题都先固定坐标方向与时间单位，分清变化量和固定量，并保证关系沿实际运动在局部成立。求导后保留每个链式因子，只有所需系数非零时才作除法。最后把符号解释成方向或增减；若问题问速率大小，就报告非负的绝对值。
