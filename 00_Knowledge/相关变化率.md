# 相关变化率先写全过程恒等关系再对共同参数求导以连接同一时刻的各变量速率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Related rates differentiate an identity valid throughout the motion with respect to a common parameter to connect rates at the same instant*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子方法
> 当 $x(t),y(t),\ldots$ 由同一时间驱动时，先写一个在全过程成立的几何或物理关系；对 $t$ 求导，并给每个变化量补上链式因子；之后才代入指定时刻的数值，解未知速率；最后检查符号、单位与量级。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When several quantities depend on the same time, first write a geometric or physical identity valid throughout the motion. Differentiate it with respect to time, including a chain factor for every changing quantity. Only then substitute values from the specified instant, solve for the unknown rate, and check sign, units, and scale.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

警车距道路固定 $30$ ft，车到警车的距离为 $D(t)$，沿路有向距离为 $x(t)$，则
$$
x^2+30^2=D^2.
$$
取道路上车辆所在的一侧为 $x$ 的正方向。该时刻若 $D=50$ ft、$D'=-80$ ft/s，则由 $x>0$ 得 $x=40$ ft。先求导得 $2xx'=2DD'$，再代值得 $x'=-100$ ft/s。若车辆在坐标负侧，则 $x=-40$、$x'=100$；两者都表示车辆正向最近点驶来，速率为 $100$ ft/s。若求导前把 $D=50$ 代成常数，就会错误地抹掉已知的 $D'$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose the positive road direction toward the side occupied by the car. Then $D=50$ ft gives $x=40$ ft, and $D'=-80$ ft/s yields $x'=-100$ ft/s after differentiating. With the car on the negative side instead, $x=-40$ and $x'=100$; both cases describe motion toward the closest point at speed $100$ ft/s. Replacing $D(t)$ by $50$ before differentiation would erase the known rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定量可以在求导前代入，瞬时取值不可以；“正在减小”应写成负速率；相似三角形消元时必须让每个长度来自同一个随时间变化的图形。速率单位比原量多一个“每时间”，这常能发现漏掉链式因子或使用了错误变量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A quantity known to be constant throughout may be inserted before differentiation; a value known only at one instant may not. Decreasing quantities carry negative rates, and similar-triangle relations must use dimensions from the same moving configuration. Rate units add “per time,” often revealing a missing chain factor or a mismatched variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 面积 $A=\pi r^2$ 且 $r'=3$ 时，为什么还不能给 $A'$ 一个纯数？
>
> **答案：** $A'=2\pi rr'=6\pi r$ 仍取决于所问时刻的半径；已知速率没有给出当前状态。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Given $A=\pi r^2$ and $r'=3$, why can we not yet give a numerical value for $A'$?
>
> **Answer:** The rate $A'=2\pi rr'=6\pi r$ still depends on the radius at the specified instant. Knowing a rate does not determine the current state.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
