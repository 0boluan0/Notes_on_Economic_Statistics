# 在金属货币条件下顺差引起的价格反馈会削弱自身
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Under metallic-money conditions, a surplus can trigger a price feedback that weakens itself*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 调整机制
> Hume 的价格—贵金属流动机制是一个条件性的长期反馈：若贸易顺差带来金银净流入，流通货币增加并推高本国货物与劳动的货币价格，本国出口会相对变贵、进口会相对变便宜；贸易差额因而趋于缩小，金属继续流入的动力也减弱。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Hume's price–specie–flow mechanism is a conditional long-run feedback. If a trade surplus brings a net inflow of gold and silver, the larger money stock raises domestic money prices of goods and labour. Exports become relatively dearer and imports relatively cheaper, so the balance tends to narrow and the force sustaining further specie inflows weakens.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反方向同样重要：金属外流使国内货币与价格相对下降时，出口竞争力可恢复、进口受抑，金属有机会回流。因此，这个机制反驳的是“一国可以只靠金属不断流入而永久维持同样顺差”的静态想象；它没有说任何时点的贸易差额必定为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The reverse direction matters too. When an outflow of specie lowers domestic money and prices relative to elsewhere, export competitiveness can recover, imports can be restrained, and specie may return. The mechanism rejects a static picture in which one country can sustain the same surplus and accumulate specie indefinitely; it does not say that the trade balance must equal zero at every date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该结论依赖金属货币与国际结算、货币数量对价格的传导、相对价格对贸易量的反应，以及没有足以长期抵消反馈的信用、资本流动、政策干预或供给变化。短期价格黏性、储藏金银、生产率变化和金融流动都可能改变速度与幅度，所以不能把 Hume 的思想实验直接当成所有汇率制度下的机械恒等式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result relies on metallic money and settlement, transmission from the money stock to prices, a trade response to relative prices, and no credit, capital flow, policy intervention, or supply change that persistently offsets the feedback. Sticky prices, hoarding, productivity changes, and financial flows can alter its speed and magnitude. Hume's thought experiment is therefore not a mechanical identity for every exchange-rate regime.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若一国顺差带来金属流入，为什么“拥有更多金属”本身不能保证原顺差永久不变？
>
> **答案：** 金属流入可能抬高国内货币价格，使出口相对变贵、进口相对变便宜；产生顺差的相对价格条件会被自己的结果改变。

- [David Hume, “Of the Balance of Trade”](https://www.econlib.org/library/LFBooks/Hume/hmMPL.html?chapter_num=33)：核对金属数量骤减或骤增时，国内劳动与商品价格以及金属流向的双向思想实验。
- [Jacob Viner, *Studies in the Theory of International Trade*, “The Development of the Theory of the Mechanism of International Adjustment”](https://www.econlib.org/library/NPDBooks/Viner/vnSTT.html?chapter_num=9)：核对 Hume 命题在国际调整思想史中的位置，并避免把后来的完整模型反投射给原文。
