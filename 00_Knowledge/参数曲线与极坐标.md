# 参数曲线与极坐标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Parametric curves and polar coordinates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当曲线不适合写成单值函数 $y=f(x)$ 时，用“随参数运动”或“方向加距离”来表示。
> **具体锚点：** 圆可写成 $x=R\cos t,y=R\sin t$，也可写成极坐标 $r=R$；两者都避免把圆拆成上下两个函数。
> **核心难点：** 参数区间和极角区间决定方向、覆盖范围与重复次数；公式正确但区间错仍会多算或漏算。
> **为什么重要：** 运动轨迹、闭合曲线、螺线、弧长和旋转曲面在这些表示中更直接。
> **继续：** 先确认曲线如何被扫过，再计算斜率、面积或弧长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Parametric and polar representations describe curves that are awkward or impossible to treat globally as a single-valued function $y=f(x)$.
> **Concrete anchor:** A circle is $x=R\cos t,y=R\sin t$ parametrically and $r=R$ in polar coordinates, without splitting it into upper and lower graphs.
> **Central difficulty:** Parameter and angle intervals determine orientation, coverage, and repetition. Correct formulas with incorrect intervals still overcount or omit geometry.
> **Why it matters:** Motion paths, closed curves, spirals, arc length, area, and surfaces of revolution become more natural in these representations.
> **Continue with:** Determine exactly how the interval traces the curve before calculating slopes, areas, or lengths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 参数曲线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Parametric curves*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$x=x(t),y=y(t)$ 把曲线看成运动轨迹；同一几何曲线可有不同速度和方向。若 $dx/dt\ne0$，斜率为 $(dy/dt)/(dx/dt)$。弧长是 $\int\sqrt{(x')^2+(y')^2},dt$，来自小位移的勾股近似；参数区间决定走了曲线的哪一段以及是否重复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$x=x(t),y=y(t)$ treats a curve as a trajectory. The same geometric curve can be traversed at different speeds and in different directions. If $dx/dt\ne0$, its slope is $(dy/dt)/(dx/dt)$. Arc length is $\int\sqrt{(x')^2+(y')^2}\,dt$, derived from a Pythagorean approximation to small displacements. The parameter interval determines the traced segment and any repetition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 极坐标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Polar coordinates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$x=r\cos\theta,y=r\sin\theta$ 用方向和距离描述点，但表示不唯一。作图先分析对称性、$r$ 的符号、零点与重复区间。面积微元来自小扇形，故 $A=\frac12\int r^2d\theta$；上下限必须对应“恰好扫过目标区域一次”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$x=r\cos\theta,y=r\sin\theta$ describes a point by direction and signed radial distance, but the representation is not unique. Before graphing, examine symmetry, the sign of $r$, zeros, and repeated intervals. The area element comes from a small sector, giving $A=\frac12\int r^2\,d\theta$; the bounds must sweep the target region exactly once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 弧长、曲面与检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Arc length, surfaces, and checks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有弧长和旋转曲面公式都在累加局部几何片段。结果应做三类检查：单位是否正确、 integrand 是否非负、极端或对称情形是否回到熟悉图形。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every arc-length and surface-of-revolution formula accumulates local geometric pieces. Check units, verify that the integrand is nonnegative, and confirm that limiting or symmetric cases reduce to familiar shapes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：极坐标圆的面积
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: the area of a circle in polar coordinates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

圆 $r=R$ 在 $0\le\theta\le2\pi$ 中恰好扫过一次，因而
$$
A=\frac12\int_0^{2\pi}R^2\,d\theta=\pi R^2.
$$
若把区间误写为 $0\le\theta\le4\pi$，公式本身不会报错，但会因为曲线被扫两次而得到两倍面积。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The circle $r=R$ is swept exactly once over $0\le\theta\le2\pi$, so
$$
A=\frac12\int_0^{2\pi}R^2\,d\theta=\pi R^2.
$$
If the interval is mistakenly extended to $0\le\theta\le4\pi$, the formula itself does not signal an error, but the circle is traced twice and the computed area doubles.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 参数曲线的参数区间为什么是题目的一部分？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is the parameter interval part of a parametric-curve problem?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它决定方向、速度、覆盖范围和重复次数；只给方程不检查区间可能多算或漏算弧长与面积。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It determines orientation, speed, coverage, and repetition. Ignoring it can overcount or omit arc length and area.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 极坐标面积公式里的 $1/2$ 从哪里来？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Where does the factor $1/2$ in the polar-area formula come from?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 每个小角度对应近似扇形，面积约为 $\frac12r^2\Delta\theta$，求和取极限得到公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Each small angular interval forms an approximate sector of area $\frac12r^2\Delta\theta$; summing and taking a limit yields the formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 若 $dx/dt=0$，能否直接断定参数曲线没有切线？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If $dx/dt=0$, can one conclude that a parametric curve has no tangent?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不能。若 $dy/dt\ne0$，往往对应竖直切线；若两者都为零，则需用更高阶展开或重参数化判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> No. If $dy/dt\ne0$, the tangent is typically vertical. If both derivatives vanish, higher-order behaviour or a reparametrisation is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释为什么极坐标表示不唯一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why polar representation is not unique.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 角度加 $2\pi$ 不改变方向，而负半径可以通过将角度加 $\pi$ 改写为正半径，所以同一点有多组 $(r,\theta)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Adding $2\pi$ leaves a direction unchanged, and a negative radius can be replaced by a positive radius after adding $\pi$ to the angle, so one point has many coordinate pairs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
