# 回归标准误只有在协方差估计口径与数据的异方差、时间或组内依赖结构相匹配时，才能支持相应推断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Regression standard errors support inference only when the covariance estimator matches heteroskedastic, temporal, or within-group dependence in the data*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> “稳健标准误”不是一个无条件更强的按钮。同方差公式、异方差稳健公式、HAC/Newey–West 和聚类稳健公式分别允许不同的误差依赖结构；选错口径时，系数数值可能不变，但 $t$ 值、区间和联合检验会失去所声称的校准。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A robust standard error is not a universally stronger switch. Homoskedastic, heteroskedasticity-robust, HAC/Newey–West, and cluster-robust covariance estimators permit different dependence structures. A mismatch can leave coefficient estimates unchanged while invalidating the reported tests and intervals.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OLS 的一般协方差结构是
$$
\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta\mid X)
=(X'X)^{-1}X'\Omega X(X'X)^{-1}.
$$
不同方法的差别在于怎样估计中间的 $\Omega$，以及哪些观测之间被允许共同变化：

| 数据中需要允许的结构 | 典型协方差口径 | 仍不能自动处理 |
|---|---|---|
| 条件方差随观测改变、观测间独立 | White/HC | 时间或组内相关 |
| 时间序列中的弱依赖与异方差；用带宽窗口估计长期协方差 | HAC/Newey–West | 长记忆、结构突变或不合适的带宽 |
| 同组内允许相关、组间近似独立 | cluster-robust | 组数很少或聚类层级选错 |
| 球形误差 $\sigma^2I$ | 经典 OLS | 异方差和相关性 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The estimators differ in how they estimate the middle covariance matrix $\Omega$ and which observations may co-move. White permits heteroskedasticity with cross-observation independence; HAC estimates a long-run covariance under weak temporal dependence using a bandwidth window; clustering permits dependence within groups while relying on appropriate between-group asymptotics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

口径应从数据生成、抽样和处理分配方式出发，而不是从哪个结果更显著出发。时间序列残差有相关性时，只做 White 修正仍遗漏跨期协方差；同一学校、公司或人的观测共享冲击时，把每行当成独立信息会错估抽样不确定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The covariance estimator should follow the data-generating process, sampling scheme, and treatment-assignment mechanism rather than whichever choice produces the preferred significance result. White standard errors still omit cross-period covariance when time-series errors are dependent, and treating clustered observations as independent misstates the amount of sampling information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 聚类层级不是软件选项，而是推断假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A clustering level is an inferential assumption, not a software option*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从误差模型角度看，单向 cluster-robust 口径允许同一 cluster 内的误差任意相关，却仍假定不同 clusters 之间没有需要保留的相关性；因此 cluster 必须宽到足以覆盖解释变量与误差可能共同变化的范围。把州内跨年相关的数据按“州×年份”聚类，反而会错误地强迫同一州不同年份相互独立。若年份冲击和州冲击都可能留下相关性，需要考虑相应的多维结构，而不是随便选一个交叉标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From a model-based perspective, one-way cluster-robust inference permits arbitrary error dependence within a cluster but still excludes relevant dependence across clusters. Clusters must therefore be broad enough to contain the joint movement of regressors and errors. Clustering a state panel by state-year would wrongly force different years of the same state to be independent; dependence along both state and year dimensions may instead require a corresponding multiway structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从设计角度看，是否聚类还取决于不确定性从哪里来：两阶段抽样中先抽 clusters、再抽 cluster 内单位，或处理在 cluster 内相关分配，都会让 cluster 成为独立信息的自然单位。这个判断不能简化成“哪里存在任意相关就在哪里聚类”，也不能在看完结果后挑标准误最大的层级。应先写清总体、抽样设计、处理分配和允许的依赖，再选择协方差估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From a design-based perspective, clustering is warranted when uncertainty arises from two-stage cluster sampling or when treatment assignment is correlated within clusters. This cannot be reduced to clustering wherever any outcome correlation can be found, nor to choosing the largest standard error after seeing the results. State the population, sampling design, assignment mechanism, and permitted dependence before selecting the covariance estimator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常规 cluster-robust 近似依赖独立 clusters 的数量增长，而不是只依赖总行数增长。cluster 很少或高度不平衡时，普通大样本临界值可能过度拒绝、区间过窄；此时要采用与设计相容的小样本自由度修正、wild cluster bootstrap 等专门方法，并报告 cluster 数与校准方式。不存在一个脱离设计、适用于所有问题的“至少多少组”硬阈值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conventional cluster-robust approximations rely on the number of independent clusters growing, not merely the row count. With few or highly unbalanced clusters, default large-sample calibration can over-reject and produce intervals that are too narrow. Use a design-appropriate small-sample correction or method such as the wild cluster bootstrap, and report the cluster count and calibration. There is no universal cluster-count cutoff independent of the design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准误修正只改变不确定性估计，不会修复 $E(u\mid X)\neq0$、错误 estimand、遗漏变量、反向因果或错误函数形式。前者属于协方差层，后者属于识别或设定层；把二者混在一起会得到“标准误很稳健，所以系数可信”的错误结论，见 [[设定问题分层]] 与 [[内生性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A covariance correction does not repair endogeneity, a wrong estimand, omitted-variable bias, reverse causality, or misspecification. Those are identification or specification failures rather than uncertainty-estimation failures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 日收益回归同时存在异方差和残差自相关，只把经典标准误换成 White 标准误是否足够？
>
> **答案：** 不足。White 处理异方差，但仍把不同时点的误差视为不相关；需要与时间依赖相容的口径，例如在条件适用时使用 HAC，并说明滞后选择与大样本依据。
