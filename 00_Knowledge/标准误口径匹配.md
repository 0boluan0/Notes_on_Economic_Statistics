# Choosing regression standard errors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*标准误口径匹配*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A regression coefficient tells us the fitted change in an outcome; its [[标准误含义|standard error]] describes how much that estimate varies under repeated sampling. To estimate that variation, we must say which observations can move together. Two measurements from the same person may share a shock. Calling them two independent pieces of information changes the uncertainty calculation even if the fitted coefficient stays exactly the same.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归系数告诉我们结果的拟合变化；它的[[标准误含义|标准误]]描述该估计在重复抽样中怎样波动。估计这种波动时，必须说明哪些观测会共同变化。同一个人的两次测量可能共享冲击。把它们算成两份独立信息，会改变不确定性计算，即使拟合系数完全不变。

> [!example] The same mean, different uncertainty · 同一个均值，不同的不确定性
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose two measurements $Y_1,Y_2$ each have mean $\mu$, variance $1$, and covariance $\rho$. Their average estimates $\mu$, with variance $(1+\rho)/2$. For $\rho=1/2$, that variance is $3/4$; for $\rho=-1/2$, it is $1/4$. Ignoring covariance gives $1/2$ in both cases. Dependence can therefore increase or decrease uncertainty. This is a calculation with a specified population model, not a way to estimate a correlation reliably from just two observations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两次测量均值都是 $\mu$、方差都是 $1$，协方差为 $\rho$，则平均值的方差是 $(1+\rho)/2$。当 $\rho=1/2$ 时为 $3/4$；当 $\rho=-1/2$ 时为 $1/4$。忽略协方差却总得到 $1/2$。依赖既能增大也能减小不确定性。这是指定总体模型后的计算，不是说仅凭两个观测就能可靠估计相关性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the covariance estimate must retain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*协方差估计要保留哪些共同波动*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the basic conditional model, write $y=X\beta+u$. Here $y$ contains $n$ responses, $\beta$ contains $k$ coefficients, $X$ is the $n\times k$ design, and $u$ contains the model errors. Fix $X$, assume its columns are [[线性无关|linearly independent]], and assume $E(u\mid X)=0$ with finite conditional second moments. The [[协方差矩阵|error covariance matrix]] $\Omega=\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X)$ records each error variance on the diagonal and each pair's covariance off the diagonal. The [[协方差失效下的OLS|general OLS covariance]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基础条件模型写作 $y=X\beta+u$：$y$ 保存 $n$ 个响应，$\beta$ 有 $k$ 个系数，$X$ 是 $n\times k$ 设计矩阵，$u$ 保存模型误差。固定 $X$，要求其列[[线性无关]]，并假定 $E(u\mid X)=0$、误差有有限条件二阶矩。误差[[协方差矩阵]] $\Omega=\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X)$ 的对角元是各误差方差，非对角元是成对协方差。[[协方差失效下的OLS|一般 OLS 协方差]]为

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(\hat\beta\mid X)
=(X^TX)^{-1}X^T\Omega X(X^TX)^{-1}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The superscript $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]] and $^{-1}$ means inverse. The outer matrices describe the design; the middle $X^T\Omega X$ combines its errors' contributions. A covariance estimator need not estimate every entry of a growing $n\times n$ matrix accurately. HC, HAC and cluster methods instead estimate the relevant combined contribution. Choosing one determines which cross-products are kept and how enough information is accumulated to estimate them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置]]，$^{-1}$ 表示逆矩阵。两侧矩阵描述设计，中间 $X^T\Omega X$ 汇总误差的贡献。协方差估计不必逐项准确恢复不断变大的 $n\times n$ 矩阵；HC、HAC 和聚类方法直接估计相关的汇总量。选择方法，就是决定保留哪些交叉乘积，以及靠怎样的信息积累来估计它们。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Model or sampling structure | Usual route | What still needs justification |
|---|---|---|
| Equal conditional error variances and zero cross-error covariances | [[OLS经典协方差估计|Classical OLS covariance]] | Positive residual degrees of freedom; the reference law for a test |
| Independent observations with possibly unequal conditional variances | [[异方差稳健协方差|HC covariance]] | Adequate moments and design as sample size grows; the HC version |
| One suitably ordered series with weak temporal dependence | [[HAC协方差|HAC / Newey–West covariance]] | Moment/dependence conditions, time indexing, kernel and bandwidth |
| Dependence within specified groups, with sufficiently independent groups | [[聚类标准误边界|Cluster-robust covariance]] | The grouping argument, enough suitable clusters and an appropriate calibration |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 模型或抽样结构 | 常用途径 | 仍要核实 |
|---|---|---|
| 条件误差方差相同，跨误差协方差为零 | [[OLS经典协方差估计]] | 正的残差自由度，检验的参考分布 |
| 观测独立，条件方差可能不同 | [[异方差稳健协方差|HC 协方差]] | 样本增长时的矩与设计条件，具体 HC 版本 |
| 一条正确排序、满足弱时间依赖的序列 | [[HAC协方差|HAC / Newey–West]] | 矩与依赖条件、时间索引、核函数和带宽 |
| 指定组内允许依赖，组间足够独立 | [[聚类标准误边界|聚类稳健协方差]] | 分组依据、足够的合适簇及相应校准 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are common sufficient frameworks, not a ranking from weak to strong. HC omits cross-observation terms and does not generally handle serial correlation. Ordinary HAC is not permission for arbitrary dependence: long memory, unstable long-run behavior or an unsuitable bandwidth may defeat its usual approximation. A panel with shared person and year shocks needs a method justified for those dimensions, rather than treating its concatenated rows as one ordinary time series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些是常用的充分框架，不是由弱到强的等级。HC 省去跨观测项，一般不能处理序列相关。普通 HAC 也不允许任意依赖：长记忆、长期行为不稳定或不合适的带宽可能破坏常规近似。面板同时存在个人与年份共同冲击时，需要有对应维度依据的方法，不能只把所有行拼成一条普通时间序列。

## Choosing the clustering level
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 聚类层级不是软件选项，而是推断假设

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the target population, how units entered the sample, how treatment was assigned, and the dependence the analysis allows. Sampling some schools and then pupils within schools creates a different uncertainty source from independently sampling pupils. Assigning a policy to an entire state differs from assigning treatment independently to individuals. Visible residual correlation alone, or whichever method gives the largest standard error or smallest p-value, does not settle the grouping question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先写清目标总体、单位如何进入样本、处理如何分配，以及分析允许哪些依赖。先抽部分学校再抽校内学生，与独立抽取学生的不确定性来源不同；整州分配政策，与独立对个人分配处理也不同。只看残差相关，或挑标准误最大、p 值最小的方法，都不能决定分组层级。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If states are the independent groups and a state's errors can remain related across years, clustering by state-year splits a relevant dependence. Conversely, making clusters ever broader is not automatically safer: it changes the inferential design and leaves fewer groups to average. In the usual many-cluster approximation, information grows through the number of suitable independent clusters, not just more rows inside a few groups. Few or very unequal clusters can make ordinary tests over-reject. Design-appropriate corrections or a justified wild cluster bootstrap may help; none is a universal cure or supplies a universal minimum cluster count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若州是独立组、同州误差允许跨年相关，按“州×年份”聚类就拆开了需要保留的依赖。反过来，分组越来越粗也不自动更安全：它改变推断设计，还减少可平均的组数。常规“许多簇”近似依赖合适的独立簇数增长，不只是少数簇里行数增长。簇少或极不均衡时，普通检验可能过度拒绝；与设计相容的修正或有依据的 wild cluster bootstrap 可能改善，但都不是万能修复，也不存在普适的最低组数。

> [!warning] Match the test as well as the covariance · 协方差与检验一起匹配
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a [[线性组合推断|fixed coefficient combination]], propagate the entire chosen covariance matrix; do not combine robust diagonal standard errors with classical off-diagonal entries. Then justify the reference distribution and degrees of freedom. Merely selecting a Student-t option does not make a robust test exactly t-distributed. Report the covariance version, relevant cluster count or HAC bandwidth, and the calibration used.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对[[线性组合推断|固定系数组合]]，应传播同一张完整协方差矩阵，不能把稳健对角标准误与经典非对角元拼接。接着核实参考分布和自由度。勾选 Student-t 选项，并不会自动让稳健统计量精确服从 t 分布。应报告协方差版本、相关的簇数或 HAC 带宽，以及校准方式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing standard errors leaves the fitted coefficients and their target unchanged. It does not remove [[内生性|endogeneity]], turn an association into a causal effect, or repair an incorrectly stated target. If the intended target is explicitly a best linear approximation rather than the exact conditional mean, suitable robust inference can still be valid for that projection coefficient under its own assumptions; see [[条件均值与线性投影|conditional means and linear projections]]. Establish what the coefficient means before choosing its uncertainty formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更换标准误保留已拟合系数及其目标，不会消除[[内生性]]、把相关变成因果或修复说错的目标。若目标明确是最佳线性近似，而不是精确条件均值，在自身条件成立时仍可对该投影系数作有效的稳健推断，见[[条件均值与线性投影]]。应先说明系数代表什么，再选择其不确定性公式。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Hansen, *Econometrics* (2010), §§4.9, 5.4 and 5.6](https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~xshi28/Econometrics_Hansen.pdf#page=62): HC construction and correctly scaled asymptotic inference for regression/projection coefficients.
- [Cameron and Miller, *A Practitioner's Guide to Cluster-Robust Inference*, §§II.C, IV and VI](https://www.econ.ucdavis.edu/faculty/cameron/research/Cameron_Miller_JHR_2014_July_09.pdf#page=7): retained within-cluster terms, state-year splitting, and the many-cluster/few-cluster distinction.
- [Abadie et al., NBER Working Paper24003 (2017), introduction](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24003/w24003.pdf#page=3), later [published in QJE](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjac038): the directly checked working-paper introduction supports defining the population, sampling and assignment design before choosing clusters.
- [Newey and West, NBER Technical Working Paper55 (1986), equations4–5 and Theorem2](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/t0055/t0055.pdf#page=4): lag-weighted covariance construction and explicit moment, dependence and bandwidth conditions.
- [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/07_异方差.md#4.3. 保留 OLS，只修正推断|Local heteroskedasticity notes, §4.3]] and [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/08_自相关.md#5.3. Newey–West/HAC 修正推断|local serial-correlation notes, §5.3]]: course notation and the unchanged-coefficient distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Hansen 对照 HC 构造与正确尺度下的大样本校准；两次测量的方差直接按协方差展开验证。
- Cameron–Miller 核对组内交叉项、州与州年分组区别及少簇限制；分组决策结合 Abadie 等工作论文引言的抽样与分配论证，不把“取最大簇”当通用准则。
- Newey–West 实际阅读扫描原稿 PDF第4–8页，核对带权滞后项与一致性定理的限制。永久课程对应两节保留课程衔接；未改动源笔记。
