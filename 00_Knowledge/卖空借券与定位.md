# 卖空前的证券定位不等于已经借入证券且不免除到期交付义务
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A short-sale locate is not completed securities borrowing and does not discharge the delivery obligation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在美国 Regulation SHO 的通常 locate 要求下，经纪商须在执行相关股票卖空前，有合理依据认为证券可借得并能按时交付，且完成记录。定位（locate）核验的是可借来源；实际借入涉及证券借贷安排和交付；卖空交易的交收则须向买方交券。这是三个不同状态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the ordinary US Regulation SHO locate requirement, the executing broker must have and document reasonable grounds before the relevant equity short sale to believe securities can be borrowed for timely delivery. A locate identifies a credible source; actual borrowing involves a securities-loan arrangement and delivery; settlement of the sale requires delivery to the buyer. These are distinct states.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，“已 locate 100 股”不等于“账户已收到 100 股”，也不保证原来源在交收时仍能提供股票。具体规则还存在例外、特定情形的预借要求与失败交付平仓要求；不能把 locate 表述成任何市场、任何卖空都适用的同一程序，也不能把“未在下单前实收借券”直接等同于违法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus, locating 100 shares is neither receipt of 100 shares nor a guarantee that the source will remain available at settlement. Exceptions, particular pre-borrow requirements, and fail close-out rules also apply. Locate is not a universal procedure across markets, and not having received borrowed shares before order submission is not by itself a universal test of illegality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

借券成功也不保证可以无限期持有空头。按合同，出借方可能召回，借券费与抵押要求可能变化；若不能获得替代券源，返还义务可能迫使买回。SEC 对美国基金出借证券的说明提供了可终止、须返还的实例，但不能据此断言所有期限借贷都可随时无条件召回。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Successful borrowing does not guarantee an indefinite holding period. Depending on the contract, the lender may recall the loan and borrowing fees or collateral requirements may change. Without replacement borrowing, the return obligation may force repurchase. SEC guidance on US fund lending supplies an example of terminable loans, not a rule that every term loan is unconditionally recallable at any time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验卖空策略能否实施时，应分别核对：允许交易的证券与账户、所需的预借或定位、实际交付、费用、返还期限与替代借券渠道。这些限制可使预期正确的头寸提前退出，复用[[套利限制]]，不把期末价差当作已可获得的收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Implementation checks distinguish eligible securities and accounts, required pre-borrow or locate, actual delivery, costs, return deadlines, and replacement sources. Such constraints can force early exit despite a correct forecast; see [[套利限制|limits of arbitrage]]. A terminal spread is not automatically an attainable return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
