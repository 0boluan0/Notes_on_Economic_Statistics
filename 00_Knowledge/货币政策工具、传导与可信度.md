# 货币政策工具、传导与可信度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Monetary-policy Tools, Transmission, and Credibility*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原主题名称：货币政策与外汇市场
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Original topic name: Monetary Policy and the Foreign-exchange Market
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释中央银行怎样通过短期利率、预期、信贷和资产价格影响通胀与经济，以及开放经济中汇率和资本流动怎样反馈。
> **具体锚点：** 加息提高短期融资成本并可能推动本币升值，但实际效果取决于预期、银行传导和海外政策。
> **核心难点：** 政策工具、操作目标、中间变量和最终目标要区分；汇率变化同时反映利差、风险溢价和预期。
> **为什么重要：** 不能把“央行加息”直接等同于某个固定幅度的通胀或汇率结果。
> **继续：** 先读传导渠道，再用利率平价理解外汇；资产定价基础见 [[利率、即期远期与期限结构|现值、利率与利率期限结构]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Explain how a central bank influences inflation and economic activity through short-term rates, expectations, credit, and asset prices, with feedback from exchange rates and capital flows in an open economy.
> **Concrete anchor:** A rate increase raises short-term financing costs and may appreciate the currency, but its actual effect depends on expectations, bank pass-through, and foreign policy.
> **Central difficulty:** Policy instruments, operating targets, intermediate variables, and final objectives must be separated; exchange-rate changes also combine interest differentials, risk premia, and expectations.
> **Why it matters:** “The central bank raised rates” cannot be translated mechanically into a fixed change in inflation or the exchange rate.
> **Continue with:** Study transmission channels first, then use interest parity in [[外汇市场、利率平价与不可能三角|the foreign-exchange market, interest parity, and the impossible trinity]]; for asset-pricing foundations see [[利率、即期远期与期限结构|present value and the term structure]].

## 政策工具与操作框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Policy Instruments and Operating Frameworks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

工具包括政策利率、公开市场/回购、准备金机制、资产购买和沟通。现代充足准备金框架常通过央行管理利率形成走廊/地板，而非机械控制货币乘数。操作目标与最终通胀/就业目标之间有时滞和不确定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Instruments include policy rates, open-market or repo operations, reserve arrangements, asset purchases, and communication. Modern ample-reserves systems often implement policy through administered rates in a corridor or floor rather than by mechanically controlling a money multiplier. Operating targets affect final inflation and employment objectives only with lags and uncertainty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实施层先让隔夜市场利率接近政策目标，再通过预期和套利影响期限更长、风险更高的融资价格。资产购买主要改变期限和风险资产供给、市场功能与信号，而不是给每笔实体贷款直接配额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Implementation first keeps overnight market rates near the policy target; expectations and arbitrage then influence longer-term and riskier financing prices. Asset purchases mainly alter the supply of duration and risky assets, market functioning, and policy signals rather than assigning quotas to individual real-economy loans.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 传导渠道
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Transmission Channels*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利率渠道改变消费投资的跨期价格，信贷渠道影响外部融资溢价，资产价格和汇率改变财富与净出口，预期渠道影响长期利率和工资定价。渠道强度随资产负债表、固定利率合同和金融结构变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The interest-rate channel changes the intertemporal price of consumption and investment; the credit channel changes external-finance premia; asset prices and exchange rates affect wealth and net exports; the expectations channel influences long rates and wage setting. Channel strength varies with balance sheets, fixed-rate contracts, and financial structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

传导不是一条固定长度的链。加息会很快影响浮动利率和新借款，却只在合同重定价时影响既有固定利率债务；负债高、现金少、银行依赖强的主体反应更大。分析应明确现金流、抵押品、资产价格和预期分别经过谁的资产负债表。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Transmission is not a chain of fixed length. A rate increase quickly affects floating-rate and new borrowing but reaches existing fixed-rate debt only at repricing. Highly indebted, cash-poor, bank-dependent borrowers react more strongly. Trace cash flow, collateral, asset prices, and expectations through the relevant balance sheets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 规则、承诺与时间不一致
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Rules, Commitment, and Time Inconsistency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Taylor-type 规则描述政策对通胀/产出变化的系统反应，但不是必须机械遵守的法律。可信承诺能影响预期，过度承诺又可能在新信息下失去灵活性。前瞻指引依赖公众理解和政策可信度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taylor-type rules describe a systematic response to inflation and output, not a law that must be followed mechanically. Credible commitment can shape expectations, while excessive commitment can sacrifice flexibility when new information arrives. Forward guidance depends on public understanding and policy credibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

时间不一致来自事前承诺低通胀或稳健政策，事后却有刺激产出、降低实际债务等诱因。独立性、清晰目标、声誉和透明反应函数可缓解问题，但面对供给冲击时仍需解释目标权衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Time inconsistency arises when policymakers promise low inflation or restraint ex ante but later have incentives to stimulate output or reduce the real burden of debt. Independence, clear objectives, reputation, and a transparent reaction function can mitigate the problem, but supply shocks still require an explicit explanation of objective trade-offs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：加息的异质传导
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Heterogeneous Transmission of a Rate Increase*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

央行加息 100bp 后，浮动利率企业贷款可能很快重定价，固定利率按揭家庭短期现金流不变，新购房者融资成本立即上升，银行存款利率则可能缓慢跟随。由此消费、投资、住房和银行利润的时点不同，不能用一个“利率乘数”概括。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After a 100-basis-point policy increase, floating-rate corporate loans may reprice quickly, households with fixed-rate mortgages initially see no cash-flow change, new homebuyers face higher financing costs immediately, and deposit rates may adjust slowly. Consumption, investment, housing, and bank profits therefore respond at different times; one “interest-rate multiplier” cannot summarize them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把工具和目标混在一起会把准备金增加直接叫宽松；只看公告方向会忽略市场已定价预期；政策后通胀下降也可能来自供给和海外冲击，需要识别反事实；用一个平均借款利率会掩盖合同与主体异质性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Confusing instruments with objectives makes any reserve increase look like easing. Looking only at an announcement's direction ignores what markets had already priced. Inflation falling after policy may reflect supply or foreign shocks and requires a counterfactual. An average borrowing rate conceals contract and borrower heterogeneity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 政策利率变化通过哪些主要渠道影响经济？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Through which main channels does a change in the policy rate affect the economy?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 短长利率、信贷条件、资产价格、汇率和预期；各渠道有时滞且强度随制度变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Short- and long-term rates, credit conditions, asset prices, the exchange rate, and expectations; every channel has lags and institution-dependent strength.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么准备金增加不能单独判断政策宽松程度？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can an increase in reserves not by itself determine how accommodative policy is?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 在充足准备金框架中，政策立场主要由管理利率、预期和资产购买条件决定；准备金数量也可能因支付或资产负债表技术变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> In an ample-reserves framework, the stance is mainly determined by administered rates, expectations, and asset-purchase conditions; reserve quantity can also change for payment or balance-sheet reasons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么同一次加息对不同借款人影响不同？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does the same rate increase affect borrowers differently?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 合同重定价时点、杠杆、现金缓冲、抵押品和融资渠道不同，使现金流与信贷供给反应异质。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Differences in repricing dates, leverage, cash buffers, collateral, and financing channels create heterogeneous cash-flow and credit-supply responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
