# 差分方程用离散时点之间的关系约束一条序列
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A difference equation constrains a sequence through relations across discrete indices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 差分方程是在离散索引上，把未知序列的不同项、外部输入和时间联系起来的方程。时间序列里常把它写成递推式
> $$
> y_t=F(y_{t-1},\ldots,y_{t-n},x_t,t),
> $$
> 但“递推”只是便于从已知过去计算现在的一种写法；差分方程本身首先规定哪些整条序列是允许的解。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A difference equation relates values of an unknown sequence at different discrete indices, possibly together with inputs and time. A recursive form is convenient for computing the present from the past, but the equation first defines a family of admissible sequences.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方程的**阶数**由真正出现的最远未知量滞后决定。若 $y_{t-2}$ 的系数非零而更早的 $y$ 不出现，方程是二阶；输入 $x_{t-8}$ 的长滞后不会把输出递推本身变成八阶。阶数通常也决定需要多少个独立初始条件才能选出唯一的前向解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The order is determined by the furthest nonzero lag of the unknown output. A long lag of an external input does not by itself raise the order of the output recurrence. Under a regular explicit recurrence, the order also indicates how many initial values are needed for a unique forward solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不要把差分方程与[[差分算子]]混为一谈。$\Delta y_t=2$ 是一个差分方程，其中 $\Delta$ 是用来比较相邻项的算子；$y_t=0.7y_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t$ 也是差分方程，即使式中没有写 $\Delta$。解题前要分别说清方程、输入序列和初始或边界条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A difference equation is not the same object as the difference operator. The equation $\Delta y_t=2$ uses that operator, while $y_t=0.7y_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t$ is also a difference equation without displaying $\Delta$. The equation, forcing sequence, and initial or boundary conditions must be specified separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $y_t=0.4y_{t-1}+3x_{t-12}$ 是几阶差分方程？
>
> **答案：** 对未知输出 $y$ 而言是一阶。$x$ 的十二期滞后属于外部输入结构，不把输出递推升为十二阶。
