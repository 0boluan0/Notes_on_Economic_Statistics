# 若信息集 G1 包含于 G2，则先对 G2 条件化再对 G1 条件化，几乎处处等于直接对 G1 条件化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If information set G1 is contained in G2, conditioning first on G2 and then on G1 is almost surely the same as conditioning directly on G1*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 定理
> 设 $X$ 可积，且 $\mathcal G_1\subseteq\mathcal G_2\subseteq\mathcal F$，则
> $$
> E\!\left[E[X\mid\mathcal G_2]\mid\mathcal G_1\right]
> =E[X\mid\mathcal G_1]\qquad\text{a.s.}
> $$
> 较粗的信息集决定最后保留下来的条件期望；先使用较细信息再把新增信息忘掉，不会改变最终结果。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $X$ is integrable and $\mathcal G_1\subseteq\mathcal G_2\subseteq\mathcal F$, then $E[E[X\mid\mathcal G_2]\mid\mathcal G_1]=E[X\mid\mathcal G_1]$ almost surely. The coarser information set determines the final conditional expectation: conditioning on finer information and then forgetting the extra information gives the same result as conditioning directly on the coarser set.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么等式成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the equality holds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两边都关于 $\mathcal G_1$ 可测。对任意 $A\in\mathcal G_1$，因为 $A$ 也属于 $\mathcal G_2$，条件期望的积分保持性质连续使用两次便给出
$$
\begin{aligned}
E\!\left[E\!\left[E[X\mid\mathcal G_2]\mid\mathcal G_1\right]\mathbf 1_A\right]
&=E\!\left[E[X\mid\mathcal G_2]\mathbf 1_A\right]\\
&=E[X\mathbf 1_A].
\end{aligned}
$$
因此左边满足 $E[X\mid\mathcal G_1]$ 的定义条件；由几乎处处唯一性，两边相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both sides are $\mathcal G_1$-measurable. For any $A\in\mathcal G_1$, we also have $A\in\mathcal G_2$, so two uses of integral preservation give $E[E[E[X\mid\mathcal G_2]\mid\mathcal G_1]\mathbf 1_A]=E[E[X\mid\mathcal G_2]\mathbf 1_A]=E[X\mathbf 1_A]$. The left-hand side therefore satisfies the defining conditions of $E[X\mid\mathcal G_1]$; almost-sure uniqueness gives the equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与全期望的关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Relation to total expectation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $\mathcal G_1$ 取成最粗的平凡信息集，就得到 [[全期望公式]]。在有限分区下，这条特例可以直接写成各组条件均值的概率加权和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking $\mathcal G_1$ to be the trivial information set yields the [[全期望公式|law of total expectation]]. For a finite partition, this special case becomes a probability-weighted sum of the groupwise conditional means.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不能把两层条件期望直接视为相等
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The two conditional expectations are not themselves equal*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

塔式法则没有声称 $E[X\mid\mathcal G_1]=E[X\mid\mathcal G_2]$。较细信息通常会改变预测；定理只说明把较细预测再次投回较粗信息后，会恢复较粗预测。例如 $\mathcal F_t\subseteq\mathcal F_{t+1}$ 时，正确关系是
$$
E_t[E_{t+1}(X)]=E_t(X),
$$
而不是 $E_t(X)=E_{t+1}(X)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tower property does not claim that $E[X\mid\mathcal G_1]=E[X\mid\mathcal G_2]$. Finer information generally changes a forecast; the theorem says only that projecting the finer forecast back onto the coarser information recovers the coarser forecast. Thus $\mathcal F_t\subseteq\mathcal F_{t+1}$ gives $E_t[E_{t+1}(X)]=E_t(X)$, not $E_t(X)=E_{t+1}(X)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么塔式法则要求两个信息集嵌套？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does the tower property require nested information sets?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 证明需要每个 $\mathcal G_1$ 事件同时也是 $\mathcal G_2$ 事件；对两个不可比较的信息集，这一步不成立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The proof needs every $\mathcal G_1$-event to be a $\mathcal G_2$-event as well. That step fails for two incomparable information sets.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
