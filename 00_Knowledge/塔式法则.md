# Tower property of conditional expectation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*条件期望的塔式法则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **tower property** explains how averages at different levels of information fit together. First predict using detailed information, then average those predictions using only coarser information. If the detailed information includes everything in the coarse information, the result is the same as predicting directly from the coarse information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**塔式法则**说明不同信息层次下的平均值怎样衔接：先用详细信息作预测，再只保留较粗信息，对这些预测重新平均。只要详细信息包含较粗信息的全部内容，结果就等于直接使用较粗信息作预测。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here a prediction is a [[条件期望|conditional expectation]], a probability-weighted average using the information available. “More information” means more distinctions can be made between possible outcomes. A [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]] records which distinctions are available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的预测指[[条件期望]]，即利用已有信息得到的概率加权平均。“信息更多”表示能够区分更多可能结果；[[σ-代数]]记录能作哪些区分。

> [!example] A route, then a traffic report · 先知道线路，再知道路况
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose four situations are equally likely. On route $0$, light and heavy traffic give waits of $1$ and $3$ minutes; on route $1$, they give $4$ and $8$ minutes. Knowing just the route gives forecasts $2$ and $6$. Knowing both route and traffic gives the exact waits in this model. Now forget the traffic report and average within each route: $(1+3)/2=2$ and $(4+8)/2=6$. The route-only forecasts return.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设四种情况等概率出现：线路 $0$ 在路况通畅和拥堵时分别等 $1$、$3$ 分钟；线路 $1$ 分别等 $4$、$8$ 分钟。只知道线路时，预测为 $2$ 或 $6$；同时知道线路和路况时，这个模型里的等待时间完全确定。现在忘掉路况，在各线路内部重新平均，得到 $(1+3)/2=2$、$(4+8)/2=6$，又恢复了只知道线路时的预测。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Y$ denote the quantity to predict and assume $E[|Y|]<\infty$. Write $\mathcal G_1$ for coarse information and $\mathcal G_2$ for fine information, with $\mathcal G_1\subseteq\mathcal G_2$. This inclusion says that every event recognisable with the coarse information is also recognisable with the fine information. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $Y$ 表示要预测的量，并假设 $E[|Y|]<\infty$。较粗信息记作 $\mathcal G_1$，较细信息记作 $\mathcal G_2$，要求 $\mathcal G_1\subseteq\mathcal G_2$：较粗信息能辨认的每个事件，较细信息也能辨认。此时

$$
E\!\left[E[Y\mid\mathcal G_2]\mid\mathcal G_1\right]=E[Y\mid\mathcal G_1]\quad\text{almost surely}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read the brackets from inside out: compute the fine-information mean, then average it using coarse information. “Almost surely” means the equality holds with probability one. In grouped data, the second average must use the conditional probabilities within the coarse group; equally weighting unequal subgroups is not justified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从内向外读括号：先求细信息下的均值，再按粗信息平均它。“几乎处处”表示等式以概率一成立。分组情况下，第二次平均必须使用粗组内部各细组的条件概率，不能把大小不同的细组一律等权处理。

## 为什么等式成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the equality holds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->



<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Call the nested average $Z=E[E[Y\mid\mathcal G_2]\mid\mathcal G_1]$. It is integrable and depends only on $\mathcal G_1$. For an event $A$ recognisable with $\mathcal G_1$, let $\mathbf1_A$ be its [[事件指示变量|indicator]], which selects that event’s contribution. Because $A$ also belongs to $\mathcal G_2$, the defining average-preservation property can be used twice:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将两层平均记为 $Z=E[E[Y\mid\mathcal G_2]\mid\mathcal G_1]$。它可积，而且只依赖 $\mathcal G_1$。对于 $\mathcal G_1$ 能辨认的事件 $A$，用[[事件指示变量]] $\mathbf1_A$ 选出该事件的贡献。因为 $A$ 也属于 $\mathcal G_2$，可以连续使用两次条件期望的平均贡献保持性质：

$$
E[Z\mathbf1_A]=E\!\left[E[Y\mid\mathcal G_2]\mathbf1_A\right]=E[Y\mathbf1_A].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $Z$ uses only the coarse information and preserves every coarse event’s contribution, exactly the defining requirements of $E[Y\mid\mathcal G_1]$. Almost-sure uniqueness gives the result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，$Z$ 只使用粗信息，并且保留每个粗信息事件的平均贡献，恰好满足 $E[Y\mid\mathcal G_1]$ 的定义。由几乎处处唯一性，得到等式。

## 与全期望的关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Relation to total expectation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->



<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\mathcal G_1$ contains no information beyond the whole sample space, the formula becomes $E[E[Y\mid\mathcal G_2]]=E[Y]$, the [[全期望公式|law of total expectation]]. In the route example, forgetting the route too gives $(2+6)/2=4$. There is also a reverse-order identity: $E[E[Y\mid\mathcal G_1]\mid\mathcal G_2]=E[Y\mid\mathcal G_1]$. The inner coarse prediction is already known from the fine information, so conditioning leaves it unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\mathcal G_1$ 除整个样本空间外不提供任何信息，公式变成 $E[E[Y\mid\mathcal G_2]]=E[Y]$，即[[全期望公式]]。公交例子中，连线路也忘掉后，得到 $(2+6)/2=4$。反向次序也有 $E[E[Y\mid\mathcal G_1]\mid\mathcal G_2]=E[Y\mid\mathcal G_1]$：内层的粗预测已经能由细信息确定，所以再条件化不会改变它。

## 不能把两层条件期望直接视为相等
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Different information can still give different forecasts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->



<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On route $0$, the coarse forecast is $2$, while the detailed forecast can be $1$ or $3$. The theorem does not equate these forecasts; it equates the coarse forecast with the average of the detailed forecasts. If information grows over time, $\mathcal F_t\subseteq\mathcal F_{t+1}$, and $E_t[\cdot]$ abbreviates conditioning on $\mathcal F_t$, the relation is $E_t[E_{t+1}[Y]]=E_t[Y]$. It does not say $E_t[Y]=E_{t+1}[Y]$. This consistency is useful when studying [[鞅定义|martingales]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在线路 $0$ 中，粗预测是 $2$，细预测却可以为 $1$ 或 $3$。定理没有说两个预测相同，而是说细预测的适当平均等于粗预测。若信息随时间增长，$\mathcal F_t\subseteq\mathcal F_{t+1}$，并用 $E_t[\cdot]$ 简记给定 $\mathcal F_t$ 的条件期望，则关系为 $E_t[E_{t+1}[Y]]=E_t[Y]$，并不是 $E_t[Y]=E_{t+1}[Y]$。这一一致性在[[鞅定义|鞅]]中会用到。

> [!warning]- Without nesting, check the calculation · 信息不嵌套时不能直接套用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Roll a fair die. Let $Y$ indicate an even result. One information set reveals parity; another reveals whether the result is at most three. Neither contains the other. The second information gives means $1/3$ and $2/3$. Averaging those means conditional on an even result gives $(1/3+2/3+2/3)/3=5/9$, while the direct parity-based mean is $1$. Without the inclusion condition, the tower identity can fail; it may still hold accidentally in particular models.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 掷公平骰子，让 $Y$ 记录点数是否为偶数。一套信息只告知奇偶，另一套只告知是否不大于三，两者互不包含。第二套信息下的均值分别为 $1/3$、$2/3$；再给定“偶数”平均这些均值，得到 $(1/3+2/3+2/3)/3=5/9$，而直接按奇偶信息得到的均值是 $1$。缺少包含关系时，塔式等式可能失败；某些模型中仍可能碰巧成立。
