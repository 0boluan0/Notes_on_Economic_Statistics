# Positive-semidefinite matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*半正定矩阵（正半定矩阵）*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **positive-semidefinite matrix** assigns a nonnegative quadratic value to every vector. It permits a nonzero vector to give zero, but does not require such a vector to exist. This inclusive meaning matters: every [[正定矩阵|positive-definite matrix]], whose value is strictly positive away from zero, is also positive semidefinite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**半正定矩阵**使每个向量对应的二次型值都非负。它允许某个非零向量给出零值，但不要求一定存在这样的向量。这个宽义定义很重要：每个[[正定矩阵]]在非零向量处都严格为正，所以也属于半正定矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$, a [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] whose listed values occupy the main diagonal and whose other entries are zero. For a real column $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$, with $T$ denoting [[矩阵转置|transpose]], its [[二次型|quadratic form]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$。这是一个[[对角矩阵]]：列出的值放在主对角线上，其余元素为零。对实列向量 $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$，$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，对应的[[二次型]]为

$$x^TAx=x_1^2\ge0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first coordinate contributes a square, while the second contributes nothing. In particular, $x=(0,1)^T\ne0$ gives zero. Thus $A$ is positive semidefinite but not positive definite. The distinction is about allowing a zero direction, not about one matrix having “smaller positive numbers” than another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一坐标贡献一个平方，第二坐标没有贡献。特别地，$x=(0,1)^T\ne0$ 时结果为零。因此 $A$ 半正定但非正定。两者的差别在于是否允许非零零方向，而不是某个矩阵的“正数稍小”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Nonnegativity in every direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每个方向都非负*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite integer $n\ge1$, a real $n\times n$ matrix is positive semidefinite when it is [[实对称矩阵|symmetric]] and its quadratic form never becomes negative:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n\ge1$ 为有限整数。实 $n\times n$ 矩阵半正定，是指它[[实对称矩阵|对称]]，并且二次型从不取负值：

$$\boxed{A\succeq0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
A=A^T\ \text{and}\ x^TAx\ge0\ \text{for every }x\in\mathbb R^n.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $A\succeq0$ abbreviates the whole condition. At $x=0$ the value is always zero; the restriction is what happens for every other vector. For a $1\times1$ matrix $[a]$, the definition is simply $a\ge0$. The zero matrix is positive semidefinite, and so is $\operatorname{diag}(2,5)$, which is also positive definite. Hence “semidefinite” alone does not mean [[逆矩阵|singular (noninvertible)]] or “not definite.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记号 $A\succeq0$ 是整个条件的简写。$x=0$ 时结果本来就为零，真正的要求在于其他所有向量。对一阶矩阵 $[a]$，定义就是 $a\ge0$。零矩阵半正定；$\operatorname{diag}(2,5)$ 同样半正定，而且还正定。所以“半正定”本身不等于[[逆矩阵|奇异（不可逆）]]或“非正定”。

> [!example] A zero direction can combine coordinates · 零方向可以混合坐标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}$，
>
> $$x^TBx=x_1^2-2x_1x_2+x_2^2=(x_1-x_2)^2\ge0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The nonzero vector $(1,1)^T$ gives zero even though both diagonal entries are positive. Some matrix entries are negative, yet the quadratic value is never negative. Conversely, the entrywise positive matrix $\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix}$ gives $-2$ at $(1,-1)^T$, so it is not positive semidefinite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 非零向量 $(1,1)^T$ 给出零值，尽管两个对角元都为正。矩阵中有负元素，却没有任何负二次型方向。反过来，元素全正的矩阵 $\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix}$ 在 $(1,-1)^T$ 上取值 $-2$，因此并不半正定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a three-coordinate example, $\operatorname{diag}(2,0,3)$ gives $2x_1^2+3x_3^2$. It is nonnegative for all inputs and zero at $e_2=(0,1,0)^T$, so it too is positive semidefinite but not positive definite. Checking a few vectors can disprove nonnegativity if one is negative; proving it requires an argument covering all vectors, such as these square formulas.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再看三坐标例子 $\operatorname{diag}(2,0,3)$，二次型为 $2x_1^2+3x_3^2$，对所有输入非负，并在 $e_2=(0,1,0)^T$ 上为零，所以它也半正定但非正定。试几个向量时，只要找到一个负值就能否定半正定；要证明半正定，则需要像上面的平方表达式那样覆盖所有向量。

## The complex version uses Hermitian symmetry
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*复数版本使用 Hermitian 对称性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$, require [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian symmetry]] $A=A^*$ and replace $x^TAx$ by $z^*Az$. The star is [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]: transpose and conjugate every entry, replacing $a+bi$ by $a-bi$, where $i^2=-1$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$，要求 [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian 对称性]] $A=A^*$，并把 $x^TAx$ 换为 $z^*Az$。星号表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]：转置后把每个 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$，其中 $i^2=-1$。于是

$$\boxed{A\succeq0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
A=A^*\ \text{and}\ z^*Az\ge0\ \text{for every }z\in\mathbb C^n.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hermitian symmetry ensures the value is real, since $\overline{z^*Az}=z^*A^*z=z^*Az$; the bar denotes complex conjugation. For example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Hermitian 对称性保证这个数为实数，因为 $\overline{z^*Az}=z^*A^*z=z^*Az$；横线表示复共轭。例如，

$$H=\begin{pmatrix}1&i\\-i&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
z^*Hz=|z_1+iz_2|^2\ge0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $|w|^2=\bar w w$ is a complex number's squared magnitude. The nonzero vector $z=(-i,1)^T$ makes it zero, so $H$ is positive semidefinite but not positive definite. Using ordinary squares instead of squared magnitudes would destroy this reasoning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $|w|^2=\bar w w$ 表示复数模的平方。非零向量 $z=(-i,1)^T$ 使它为零，所以 $H$ 半正定但非正定。若把模平方换成普通平方，这套非负性论证就不成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symmetry requirement remains part of the definition. For a nonsymmetric real array, [[二次型对称化|symmetrize the quadratic form]] before using symmetric-matrix criteria. A nonnegative quadratic form for a nonsymmetric array does not remove that convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对称性仍是定义的一部分。对非对称实矩阵，使用对称矩阵判据之前，要先做[[二次型对称化]]；非对称矩阵的二次表达式非负，并不会取消这个定义约定。

## What zero directions do and do not tell us
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零方向能说明什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a positive-semidefinite matrix, [[半正定零方向|zero quadratic value is equivalent to a nullspace direction]]: $x^*Ax=0$ exactly when $Ax=0$. The [[零空间|nullspace]] consists of vectors mapped to zero. Thus [[正定与半正定可逆性|a positive-semidefinite matrix is positive definite exactly when it is invertible]], or equivalently has [[矩阵秩|rank]] $n$. The nonnegativity assumption is essential: an invertible matrix such as $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ has a negative direction. These are statements about a fixed matrix, not global conclusions about an arbitrary function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对半正定矩阵，[[半正定零方向|零二次型值恰好对应零空间方向]]：$x^*Ax=0$ 当且仅当 $Ax=0$；[[零空间]]由被映成零的向量组成。因此，[[正定与半正定可逆性|半正定矩阵正定当且仅当它可逆]]，等价地说，[[矩阵秩|秩]]为 $n$。这里不能省略非负性前提：$\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ 可逆，却有负方向。这些结论描述一张固定矩阵，不是对任意函数的全局判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A singular positive-semidefinite quadratic cost need not have a unique minimum or be [[凹凸性|strictly convex]]: $q(x_1,x_2)=x_1^2$ is zero along the entire line $x_1=0$, so it has many minimizers and its midpoint value equals its endpoint values along that line. When $A$ is instead the [[Hessian矩阵|Hessian]], the matrix of second partial derivatives, of a more general function at one [[驻点|stationary point]] (zero gradient), a singular positive-semidefinite value leaves the second-order test inconclusive. Even the smooth one-variable functions $x^4$ and $-x^4$ have the same zero second derivative at the origin, but one has a strict minimum and the other a strict maximum. The full assumptions and further distinctions are in [[半定Hessian无结论|the singular semidefinite Hessian boundary]]. A condition on the Hessian at one point alone also does not establish convexity throughout a domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

奇异半正定的二次代价未必有唯一极小点，也未必[[凹凸性|严格凸]]：$q(x_1,x_2)=x_1^2$ 在整条 $x_1=0$ 直线上都为零，所以极小点不唯一，沿这条线的中点值也等于两端点值。如果 $A$ 是更一般函数在某个[[驻点]]（梯度为零的点）处的 [[Hessian矩阵]]，即二阶偏导组成的矩阵，那么奇异半正定的取值会使二阶检验无定论。即使是光滑的一元函数 $x^4$ 与 $-x^4$，在原点的二阶导都为零，却分别有严格极小和严格极大。完整条件与更细的区别见[[半定Hessian无结论|奇异半定 Hessian 的边界]]。只知道一个点的 Hessian 条件，也不能证明函数在整个定义域上凸。
