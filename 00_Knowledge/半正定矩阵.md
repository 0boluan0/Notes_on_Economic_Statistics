# 实对称或复 Hermitian 矩阵的二次型在每个方向非负时称为半正定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A real symmetric or complex Hermitian matrix is positive semidefinite when its quadratic form is nonnegative in every direction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 定义
> 实矩阵 $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ 称为正半定，记作 $A\succeq0$，当且仅当
> $$A=A^T,\qquad x^TAx\ge0\quad\text{对每个 }x.$$
> 复数版本要求 $A=A^*$ 且 $x^*Ax\ge0$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real matrix is positive semidefinite, written $A\succeq0$, when it is symmetric and $x^TAx\ge0$ for every vector. Over $\mathbb C$, require Hermitian symmetry and $x^*Ax\ge0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与正定的差别不是“数值稍小”，而是是否允许非零零方向。$A=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ 满足 $x^TAx=x_1^2\ge0$，但 $x=(0,1)^T\ne0$ 时等于零，所以它半正定而不正定。半正定矩阵的零二次型方向与零空间恰好如何对应，见 [[半正定零方向]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The distinction from positive definiteness is whether a nonzero zero direction is allowed. For $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$, the quadratic form is $x_1^2$, so the matrix is semidefinite but not definite. [[半正定零方向|The zero-direction result]] identifies precisely when such a direction lies in the nullspace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半正定仍然排除负曲率，但不自动给出可逆性、严格凸性或唯一极小值。若驻点处的 Hessian 只是半正定，平坦方向会让二阶检验失去决定力，见 [[半定Hessian无结论]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Semidefiniteness excludes negative curvature but does not imply invertibility, strict convexity, or uniqueness of a minimizer. When the matrix is a Hessian at a stationary point, flat directions can make the second-order test inconclusive; that boundary is treated in [[半定Hessian无结论|the semidefinite-Hessian result]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $\operatorname{diag}(2,0,3)$ 为什么不是正定？
>
> **答案：** 非零向量 $e_2$ 使二次型为零；它只能是正半定。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Question:** Why is $\operatorname{diag}(2,0,3)$ not positive definite?
>
> **Answer:** The nonzero vector $e_2$ makes its quadratic form zero, so the matrix is positive semidefinite but not positive definite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
