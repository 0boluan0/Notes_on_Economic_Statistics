# Probability density function (PDF)
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*概率密度函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **probability density function**, or **PDF**, describes how probability is spread along a numerical scale. To find the probability of a range of values, take the area under the density curve over that range. The curve's height tells us how concentrated probability is nearby; the height alone is not a probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**概率密度函数**，简称 **PDF**，描述概率怎样分布在一条数轴上。要知道一个取值区间有多大概率，就看这个区间上密度曲线下的面积。曲线的高度表示附近的概率有多密集；单独的高度还不是概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, let the [[随机变量|random variable]] $X$ record a waiting time in seconds. A density lets us answer “How likely is a wait between $0.02$ and $0.04$ seconds?” by combining the density height with the width of that interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，让[[随机变量]] $X$ 记录以秒计的等待时间。密度帮助我们回答“等待时间在 $0.02$ 秒与 $0.04$ 秒之间的概率是多少”：需要把密度的高度和这段区间的宽度结合起来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Height and width work together
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*高度要与宽度一起读*

> [!example] A short uniform wait · 一段均匀的等待时间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose a toy model assigns equal probability to equal-length intervals within $[0,0.1]$ seconds. This is a [[连续均匀分布|uniform distribution]]. Its density is $f_X(x)=10$ on that interval and zero outside it. The whole rectangle has width $0.1$ and height $10$, so its area is $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设一个示意模型在 $[0,0.1]$ 秒内给等长区间分配相等的概率，这就是[[连续均匀分布]]。它在该区间上的密度为 $f_X(x)=10$，区间以外为零。整个矩形宽 $0.1$、高 $10$，面积恰好为 $1$。
>
> $$P(0.02<X<0.04)=10\times(0.04-0.02)=0.2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The probability is the area $0.2$, not the height $10$. A narrow interval can therefore have a density above one while still having a probability below one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 概率是面积 $0.2$，不是高度 $10$。所以，窄区间上的密度可以大于一，同时该区间的概率仍小于一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The density has units **per second**, written $\mathrm{s}^{-1}$; interval width has units of seconds. Their product is a probability with no physical unit. If we record the same wait as $T=1000X$ milliseconds, the density becomes $0.01$ per millisecond on $[0,100]$. The same event now has width $20$ milliseconds, giving $0.01\times20=0.2$. Changing units changes density height but preserves the probability of the event.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

密度的单位是**每秒**，记作 $\mathrm{s}^{-1}$；区间宽度的单位是秒。两者相乘，得到没有物理单位的概率。如果把同一段等待时间改记为 $T=1000X$ 毫秒，密度就变成 $[0,100]$ 上的每毫秒 $0.01$。同一个事件现在占据 $20$ 毫秒宽的区间，所以概率仍是 $0.01\times20=0.2$。改变计量单位会改变密度高度，却不会改变同一事件的概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the curve is not flat, split the interval into narrow pieces and add their contributions. A [[定积分|definite integral]] performs this accumulation. Where $f_X$ is continuous, a sufficiently short interval of width $\Delta x$ starting at $x$ has probability approximately $f_X(x)\Delta x$; this approximation becomes accurate as the width shrinks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果曲线不是平的，就把区间分成很多窄片，累积每片的贡献；[[定积分]]完成的正是这个操作。在 $f_X$ 连续的位置，从 $x$ 开始、宽为 $\Delta x$ 的短区间，其概率近似为 $f_X(x)\Delta x$，区间越窄，这个近似越准确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The formula records area
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*用公式记录面积*

> [!abstract] Density representation · 用密度表示分布
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A nonnegative function $f_X$ is a density for $X$ when interval probabilities satisfy
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果非负函数 $f_X$ 能按下式给出 $X$ 的所有区间概率，它就是 $X$ 的一个密度：
>
> $$P(a<X\le b)=\int_a^b f_X(x)\,dx,\qquad a<b.$$
>
> $$f_X(x)\ge0,\qquad \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f_X(x)\,dx=1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The subscript $X$ identifies the variable, and $x$ is the position on its numerical scale. The bounds $a,b$ select the interval. Nonnegativity prevents negative probabilities, and total area one accounts for all possible values.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 下标 $X$ 指明变量，$x$ 表示数轴上的位置，边界 $a,b$ 指定所求区间。非负性保证概率不会为负；总面积为一表示所有可能取值的概率已全部计入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single specified point has zero width, so $P(X=x)=0$ whenever $X$ has a density. Consequently, including or excluding either endpoint does not change an interval's probability. This does not make every possible result logically impossible: [[零概率不等于不可能|zero probability and an impossible event are different]]. It also explains why a density value must not be read as a [[概率质量函数|point probability]]; see [[PMF不等于密度|PMF versus PDF]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单独指定的一个点没有宽度，所以只要 $X$ 有密度，就有 $P(X=x)=0$。因此，区间是否包含任一端点，都不影响概率。这并不是说每个可能结果在逻辑上都不可能发生，具体区别见[[零概率不等于不可能]]。它也说明密度值不能按[[概率质量函数|单点概率]]来读；两种表示的区别见 [[PMF不等于密度|PMF 与 PDF]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## How a density relates to a CDF
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*密度怎样连接累积分布函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[累积分布函数|cumulative distribution function]], $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$, records all probability accumulated up to $x$. If $X$ has a density, this cumulative probability is the area to the left of $x$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[累积分布函数]] $F_X(x)=P(X\le x)$ 记录到 $x$ 为止已经累积的概率。如果 $X$ 有密度，这就是 $x$ 左边的全部面积：

$$F_X(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{x}f_X(t)\,dt.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $t$ is only the variable being integrated; $x$ sets the upper boundary. A distribution that admits this representation is called **absolutely continuous**. Every real-valued random variable has a CDF, but some have no density. In particular, a CDF can be continuous without admitting this integral representation; [[连续CDF不保证密度|a continuous CDF need not have a density]] explains that boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $t$ 只是被积分的变量，$x$ 决定累积到哪里。能够用这种形式表示的分布叫作**绝对连续分布**。每个实值随机变量都有 CDF，但有些没有密度。尤其要注意：CDF 连续也未必能写成上面的积分，具体边界见[[连续CDF不保证密度]]。

> [!note]- Different formulas can describe the same density law · 不同函数可以表示同一个有密度的分布
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Changing the height at a single point does not change an integral. More generally, two densities for the same distribution agree except possibly on a set of total length zero, measured by Lebesgue measure. This is called equality **almost everywhere**. Conversely, changing a nonnegative density only on such a set leaves all probabilities unchanged. For the uniform example, choosing density $0$ or $10$ at the endpoints gives the same distribution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 只改变某一个点的高度，不会改变积分。更一般地，同一分布的两个密度除了在一个总长度为零的集合上可能不同以外，其余位置都相同；这里“长度”严格说是 Lebesgue 测度。这叫作**几乎处处相等**。反过来，只在这样的零长度集合上修改非负密度，也不会改变任何概率。例如，在均匀分布的两个端点把密度写成 $0$ 或 $10$，描述的都是同一个分布。
