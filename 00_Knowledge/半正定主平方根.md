# The positive-semidefinite principal square root
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*半正定主平方根*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix square root is a matrix that, applied twice, has the same effect as the original matrix. The square means [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]], not squaring each entry. For a [[半正定矩阵|positive-semidefinite matrix]], the principal square root chooses the nonnegative square root of the stretch in each perpendicular eigendirection. This choice gives one particular square root with a useful uniqueness guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵平方根是一个矩阵：连续作用两次，效果等于原矩阵作用一次。这里的平方是[[矩阵乘法]]，不是把每个元素分别平方。对[[半正定矩阵]]，主平方根在彼此垂直的特征方向上，选择伸缩量的非负平方根。这种选择给出一个具有唯一性保证的特定平方根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}5&4\\4&5\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B^2=\begin{pmatrix}4+1&2+2\\2+2&1+4\end{pmatrix}=A.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both matrices preserve the directions $(1,1)^T$ and $(1,-1)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Along those directions $A$ multiplies by $9$ and $1$, while $B$ multiplies by $3$ and $1$. Applying $B$ twice therefore reproduces $A$. Taking square roots entry by entry would instead give $\begin{pmatrix}\sqrt5&2\\2&\sqrt5\end{pmatrix}$, whose square has top-right entry $4\sqrt5$, not $4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个矩阵都保持 $(1,1)^T$、$(1,-1)^T$ 这两个方向，$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。沿它们，$A$ 分别乘以 $9$、$1$，$B$ 则分别乘以 $3$、$1$，所以 $B$ 连续作用两次就重现 $A$。逐元素开方却会得到 $\begin{pmatrix}\sqrt5&2\\2&\sqrt5\end{pmatrix}$；它平方后的右上元素为 $4\sqrt5$，并非 $4$。

## The spectral construction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*沿特征方向构造*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n\ge1$ be a finite integer. Let $A$ be a real symmetric or complex [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]] $n\times n$ matrix, and assume $A\succeq0$: every quadratic value $z^*Az$ is nonnegative. Here $*$ means conjugate transpose; for real matrices it is ordinary transpose. The [[实对称矩阵谱定理|real spectral theorem]] and [[Hermitian 谱定理|Hermitian spectral theorem]] give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n\ge1$ 为有限整数，$A$ 是 $n\times n$ 实对称或复 [[Hermitian 矩阵]]，且 $A\succeq0$，即每个二次值 $z^*Az$ 都非负。$*$ 表示共轭转置，实数情形就是普通转置。由[[实对称矩阵谱定理]]与 [[Hermitian 谱定理]]，

$$A=Q\Lambda Q^*,\qquad Q^*Q=QQ^*=I_n,\qquad
\Lambda=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n),\quad\lambda_i\ge0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The columns $q_i$ of $Q$ are a complete [[标准正交组|orthonormal family]] of [[特征对|eigenvectors]]: each has length one, distinct columns are perpendicular, and $Aq_i=\lambda_iq_i$. The matrix $I_n$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity]]. The notation $\operatorname{diag}$ places the listed entries on a [[对角矩阵|diagonal]], with zeros elsewhere. In the real case $Q$ can be chosen real. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$Q$ 的列 $q_i$ 是一组完整的[[标准正交组]]，其中各向量都是[[特征对|特征向量]]：每列长度为一，不同列互相垂直，且 $Aq_i=\lambda_iq_i$。$I_n$ 是[[单位矩阵]]；$\operatorname{diag}$ 把所列元素放在[[对角矩阵]]的主对角线上，其余位置全零。实数情形可以选择实矩阵 $Q$。定义

$$A^{1/2}:=Q\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt{\lambda_1},\ldots,\sqrt{\lambda_n})Q^*.$$

> [!important] Which square root is unique? · 哪一种平方根唯一？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $A^{1/2}$ is the unique real symmetric or complex Hermitian **positive-semidefinite** matrix $B$ satisfying $B^2=A$. Zero eigenvalues are allowed. If $A$ is positive definite, this root is positive definite too.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $A^{1/2}$ 是满足 $B^2=A$ 的唯一实对称或复 Hermitian **半正定**矩阵 $B$。允许零特征值；若 $A$ 正定，主平方根也正定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To check existence, write $D=\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt{\lambda_i})$. Since $Q^*Q=I_n$, $(QDQ^*)^2=QD^2Q^*=A$. Also $QDQ^*$ is Hermitian, and for every $z$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为核对存在性，记 $D=\operatorname{diag}(\sqrt{\lambda_i})$。由 $Q^*Q=I_n$，有 $(QDQ^*)^2=QD^2Q^*=A$。而且 $QDQ^*$ 为 Hermitian，对任意 $z$，

$$z^*A^{1/2}z=\sum_{i=1}^n\sqrt{\lambda_i}\,|q_i^*z|^2\ge0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $|w|^2=\overline w w$ is the squared magnitude of a complex scalar. If all $\lambda_i>0$, a nonzero $z$ has a nonzero coordinate $q_i^*z$, so the sum is strictly positive. Thus the construction has the stated positivity, including the real case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$|w|^2=\overline w w$ 表示复数的模平方。如果全部 $\lambda_i>0$，非零 $z$ 至少有一个非零坐标 $q_i^*z$，于是总和严格正。这就验证了所需的正性，也包含实数情形。

## Why repeated eigenvalues do not create another principal root
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*重特征值为什么不破坏主平方根的唯一性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose another Hermitian positive-semidefinite matrix $C$ satisfies $C^2=A$. Then $AC=C^3=CA$. If $v$ belongs to the [[特征空间|eigenspace]] $E_\lambda=\{v:Av=\lambda v\}$, then $A(Cv)=C(Av)=\lambda Cv$, so $C$ maps this entire space into itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设另一个 Hermitian 半正定矩阵 $C$ 满足 $C^2=A$。则 $AC=C^3=CA$。若 $v$ 属于[[特征空间]] $E_\lambda=\{v:Av=\lambda v\}$，便有 $A(Cv)=C(Av)=\lambda Cv$，所以 $C$ 把整个特征空间映回自身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Restricted to $E_\lambda$, the map $C$ is still Hermitian and positive semidefinite. Apply the spectral theorem within this space. Each of its eigenvalues $\mu$ is nonnegative and must satisfy $\mu^2=\lambda$, because $C^2v=Av=\lambda v$. Hence every such $\mu$ equals $\sqrt\lambda$, and $C$ acts as multiplication by $\sqrt\lambda$ on the whole space. This also covers $\lambda=0$. The eigenspaces of $A$ together span all inputs, so this determines $C$ everywhere and proves $C=A^{1/2}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $C$ 限制在 $E_\lambda$ 上，它仍为 Hermitian 且半正定。在这个空间内使用谱定理，其每个特征值 $\mu$ 都非负；又因为 $C^2v=Av=\lambda v$，必须有 $\mu^2=\lambda$。所以这些 $\mu$ 全部等于 $\sqrt\lambda$，$C$ 在整个空间上只能是乘以 $\sqrt\lambda$；这也覆盖 $\lambda=0$。$A$ 的各特征空间合起来张成全部输入空间，因此 $C$ 处处被确定，必为 $A^{1/2}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In particular, choosing different orthonormal bases inside a repeated eigenspace does not change the result. The definition selects an operator, not one preferred list of eigenvectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，即使在重特征值对应的空间内换一组标准正交基，结果也不变。定义选定的是一个算子，不是某一份特征向量清单。

## Zero directions and other factorizations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*零方向与其他分解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A=\operatorname{diag}(4,0)$, the principal root is $\operatorname{diag}(2,0)$: $\sqrt0=0$ causes no difficulty. The zero matrix similarly has principal root zero. What fails on a zero eigendirection is taking the [[正定逆平方根|ordinary inverse square root]], not taking the principal square root itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$A=\operatorname{diag}(4,0)$ 的主平方根为 $\operatorname{diag}(2,0)$，因为 $\sqrt0=0$ 没有困难；零矩阵的主平方根同样是零。零特征方向阻止的是取[[正定逆平方根|普通逆平方根]]，不是主平方根本身的存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The complex convention matters. For example, $B=\begin{pmatrix}2&i\\-i&2\end{pmatrix}$ is Hermitian with eigenvalues $3,1$ and $B^2=\begin{pmatrix}5&4i\\-4i&5\end{pmatrix}$. Here $i^2=-1$, and the conjugate entries are what make the quadratic values real.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复数约定也很重要。例如 $B=\begin{pmatrix}2&i\\-i&2\end{pmatrix}$ 为 Hermitian，特征值为 $3,1$，且 $B^2=\begin{pmatrix}5&4i\\-4i&5\end{pmatrix}$。这里 $i^2=-1$，彼此共轭的元素使二次值为实数。

> [!warning] Keep the positivity requirement · 保留半正定要求
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Square roots without the positivity requirement need not be unique: both $I_n$ and $-I_n$ square to $I_n$, but only $I_n$ is positive semidefinite. A [[Cholesky 正定判据|Cholesky factor]] generally solves $LL^*=A$, rather than $L^2=A$, and need not be Hermitian. It is therefore a different object from the principal square root.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不要求半正定时，平方根未必唯一：$I_n$、$-I_n$ 的平方均为 $I_n$，但只有 $I_n$ 半正定。[[Cholesky 正定判据|Cholesky 因子]]通常满足 $LL^*=A$，不是 $L^2=A$，而且不必为 Hermitian。因此它与主平方根是不同对象。
