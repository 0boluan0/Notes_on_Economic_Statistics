# Uniform continuity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一致连续*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a function turns an input into an output, and we want nearby inputs to produce outputs less than $0.01$ apart. **Uniform continuity** means that one positive input-distance threshold can handle every pair of inputs in the chosen domain. We may choose a different threshold for a different output accuracy, but we do not have to choose it again when we move to another part of the domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设函数把输入变成输出，我们希望相近输入的输出相差不到 $0.01$。**一致连续**表示：可以选出一个正的输入距离门槛，让它适用于所选定义域中的每一对输入。改变输出精度时，可以重新选择门槛；但移动到定义域的另一部分时，不必重新选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One tolerance works throughout an interval
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*同一个门槛适用于整个区间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$. For any two inputs $x,y$ in this interval,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取定义在 $[0,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x^2$。对于区间内任意两个输入 $x,y$，

$$|f(x)-f(y)|=|x-y|\,|x+y|\le 2|x-y|.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $|x-y|<0.005$ guarantees $|f(x)-f(y)|<0.01$, whether the inputs lie near $0$, near $1$, or elsewhere in the interval. More generally, for any desired output tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the input tolerance $\delta=\varepsilon/2$ works throughout $[0,1]$. Here $\varepsilon$ names how close the outputs must be, and $\delta$ names how close the inputs must be.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，只要 $|x-y|<0.005$，就能保证 $|f(x)-f(y)|<0.01$，无论输入靠近 $0$、靠近 $1$，还是位于区间其他地方。更一般地，对任意输出精度 $\varepsilon>0$，输入门槛 $\delta=\varepsilon/2$ 都适用于整个 $[0,1]$。其中 $\varepsilon$ 表示希望输出有多接近，$\delta$ 表示要求输入有多接近。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real-valued function $f:S\to\mathbb R$ on a subset $S$ of the real line, the definition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于实数子集 $S$ 上的实值函数 $f:S\to\mathbb R$，定义写成

$$
\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists\delta>0\ \forall x,y\in S:
\quad |x-y|<\delta\ \Longrightarrow\ |f(x)-f(y)|<\varepsilon.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The order matters: choose $\delta$ after learning $\varepsilon$, but before the pair $x,y$ is chosen. Only inputs in $S$ are tested. At endpoints of $[0,1]$, there is no requirement to evaluate the function outside that interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

量词顺序很重要：知道 $\varepsilon$ 后选 $\delta$，但选择必须在具体的 $x,y$ 确定之前完成。只检查属于 $S$ 的输入；对于 $[0,1]$ 的端点，不需要在区间外计算函数值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## How this strengthens ordinary continuity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*它比通常连续多要求了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a fixed input $c$, [[一点连续|continuity at that point]] allows the input tolerance to depend on both $\varepsilon$ and $c$. Requiring [[连续性|continuity everywhere]] still permits a different tolerance at every point. Uniform continuity requires a common tolerance for all points at once. Fixing $y=c$ in the uniform definition immediately gives continuity at $c$, so a uniformly continuous function is continuous everywhere on its domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定输入点 $c$ 时，[[一点连续|该点的连续性]]允许输入门槛同时依赖 $\varepsilon$ 和 $c$。即使要求[[连续性|每一点都连续]]，仍然允许各点使用不同门槛。一致连续则要求所有点同时共用一个门槛。在一致连续定义中固定 $y=c$，立即得到 $c$ 处连续，所以一致连续函数在其定义域内处处连续。

> [!warning] The domain changes the answer · 定义域会改变答案
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The same formula $f(x)=x^2$ is continuous on $\mathbb R$ but is not uniformly continuous there. Let $x_n=n$ and $y_n=n+1/n$ for positive integers $n$. Then
> $$|x_n-y_n|=\frac1n\longrightarrow0,\qquad |f(y_n)-f(x_n)|=2+\frac1{n^2}>2.$$
> Choose the fixed output tolerance $\varepsilon=1$. Whatever $\delta>0$ is proposed, a sufficiently large $n$ gives input distance below $\delta$ but output distance above $1$. No single tolerance works on all of $\mathbb R$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 同一个公式 $f(x)=x^2$ 在 $\mathbb R$ 上连续，却不一致连续。对正整数 $n$，取 $x_n=n$、$y_n=n+1/n$；输入距离 $1/n$ 趋于零，输出距离却为 $2+1/n^2>2$。固定输出精度 $\varepsilon=1$ 后，无论提出多小的 $\delta>0$，充分大的 $n$ 都使输入距离小于 $\delta$，输出距离却大于 $1$。因此全体实数上不存在共同门槛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Restricting a uniformly continuous function to a smaller domain preserves uniform continuity: there are fewer pairs to check. Enlarging the domain need not preserve it, as the change from $[0,1]$ to $\mathbb R$ shows. Uniform continuity also does not mean bounded output: the identity $f(x)=x$ on $\mathbb R$ is uniformly continuous with $\delta=\varepsilon$, even though its values are unbounded.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把一致连续函数限制到较小定义域，仍然一致连续，因为需要检查的输入对减少了；扩大定义域则不一定保留，如 $[0,1]$ 扩成 $\mathbb R$ 的例子所示。一致连续也不意味着输出有界：恒等函数 $f(x)=x$ 在 $\mathbb R$ 上取 $\delta=\varepsilon$ 即可，但它的函数值无界。

## The same definition for general distances
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把绝对值距离换成一般距离*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[度量空间|metric space]] is a set equipped with a distance rule. Let $(X,d_X)$ be the input space and $(Y,d_Y)$ the output space. For a map $f:X\to Y$, $d_X(x,y)$ measures input separation and $d_Y(f(x),f(y))$ measures output separation. Uniform continuity means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[度量空间]]就是配备了距离规则的集合。设 $(X,d_X)$ 是输入空间，$(Y,d_Y)$ 是输出空间。对于映射 $f:X\to Y$，$d_X(x,y)$ 测量输入距离，$d_Y(f(x),f(y))$ 测量输出距离。一致连续指

$$
\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists\delta>0\ \forall x,y\in X:
\quad d_X(x,y)<\delta\ \Longrightarrow\
d_Y\bigl(f(x),f(y)\bigr)<\varepsilon.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This includes the real-line definition by taking absolute-value distance on both sides. It requires specified metrics on both spaces; ordinary [[连续性的等价判别|metric continuity]] has the same distance test with a tolerance chosen separately at each centre. For an empty domain or a one-point domain, every map is uniformly continuous: there are no distinct input pairs that could violate the condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两端都使用绝对值距离，就得到前面的实数定义。这一定义要求明确两个空间各自的度量；通常的[[连续性的等价判别|度量连续性]]使用相同的距离检验，但允许每个中心分别选择门槛。空定义域或单点定义域上的每个映射都一致连续，因为没有不同的输入对能够违反条件。

> [!tip] A useful way to establish the property · 一个常用的保证
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[连续函数在紧致集上一致连续|A continuous map on a compact metric domain is uniformly continuous]]. In particular, a continuous real-valued function on a finite closed interval has a common input tolerance for every requested output accuracy. This theorem supplies existence of a tolerance; an explicit estimate such as $\delta=\varepsilon/2$ may require additional calculation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[连续函数在紧致集上一致连续|紧致度量定义域上的连续映射一定一致连续]]。特别地，有限闭区间上的连续实值函数，对每个输出精度都存在共同输入门槛。定理保证门槛存在；若要写出 $\delta=\varepsilon/2$ 这样的具体估计，可能还需要计算。
