# Linewise minima and local minima
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*逐条直线极小与局部极小*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine checking a landscape by walking away from one point along a straight line. Every chosen line may initially climb, while a curved route immediately descends. This can happen even for a polynomial. The issue is how far “initially” extends: each line may allow its own distance, whereas a local minimum requires one neighborhood that works for all nearby points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设想检查一片地形：从同一点出发，逐条沿直线走，每条选定的直线在最初一小段都上升，某条弯曲路线却可以立刻下降。这种现象即使对多项式也会发生。问题在于“最初一小段”能走多远：每条直线可以各有范围，而局部极小要求同一个邻域覆盖所有附近点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **strict local minimum** at the origin means that some radius $\delta>0$ makes $f(h)>f(0)$ for every vector $h$ with $0<\|h\|_2<\delta$; $\|\cdot\|_2$ is [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]]. See [[局部极值与绝对极值|local extrema]] for the neighborhood comparison. Along one fixed nonzero vector $w$, a strict minimum of the one-variable function $t\mapsto f(tw)$ only requires a positive interval size $\varepsilon_w$ for that particular line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原点是**严格局部极小点**，指存在半径 $\delta>0$，使每个满足 $0<\|h\|_2<\delta$ 的向量 $h$ 都有 $f(h)>f(0)$；$\|\cdot\|_2$ 是[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]。邻域比较的含义见[[局部极值与绝对极值]]。沿固定非零向量 $w$ 考察一元函数 $t\mapsto f(tw)$，则只要求该条直线有自己的正区间大小 $\varepsilon_w$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A polynomial that passes every straight-line test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每条直线都通过检验的多项式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider, on all of $\mathbb R^2$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在整个 $\mathbb R^2$ 上考虑

$$f(x,y)=(y-x^2)(y-2x^2)=y^2-3x^2y+2x^4,\qquad f(0,0)=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a nonzero direction $w=(u,v)^T$ and substitute $(x,y)=(tu,tv)$. The superscript $T$ writes the pair as a column. Factoring out $t$ from each factor gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定一个非零方向 $w=(u,v)^T$，代入 $(x,y)=(tu,tv)$；上标 $T$ 把这一对数写成列向量。从每个因子提出一个 $t$，得到

$$f(tu,tv)=t^2(v-tu^2)(v-2tu^2).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $v\ne0$ and $u\ne0$, choose $|t|<|v|/(4u^2)$. Then $|tu^2|<|v|/4$ and $|2tu^2|<|v|/2$. Both brackets therefore have the same sign as $v$, so their product is positive. With $t\ne0$, the factor $t^2$ is positive as well. This bound works for both positive and negative $t$.

If $u=0$, nonzero $w$ means $v\ne0$, and the expression is simply $t^2v^2>0$. If $v=0$, then $u\ne0$ and the expression becomes $2t^4u^4>0$. Thus **every fixed line through the origin** has a strict local minimum there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $v\ne0$ 且 $u\ne0$，取 $|t|<|v|/(4u^2)$，则 $|tu^2|<|v|/4$、$|2tu^2|<|v|/2$。因此，两个括号都与 $v$ 同号，乘积为正；再由 $t\ne0$ 得 $t^2>0$。这个界同时适用于正、负两侧的 $t$。

若 $u=0$，由于 $w\ne0$，必有 $v\ne0$，表达式直接变为 $t^2v^2>0$。若 $v=0$，则 $u\ne0$，表达式变为 $2t^4u^4>0$。所以，**经过原点的每条固定直线**都在原点严格局部极小。

> [!example] A descending parabola · 下降的抛物线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Now approach along $y=3x^2/2$. Substitution gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 现在沿 $y=3x^2/2$ 接近原点，代入得到
>
> $$f\!\left(x,\frac32x^2\right)
> =\left(\frac12x^2\right)\left(-\frac12x^2\right)
> =-\frac14x^4<0\qquad(x\ne0).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These points approach $(0,0)$ as $x\to0$, so every ball around the origin contains a point with smaller value. The origin is not even a non-strict local minimum. Along the $y$ axis, the values $f(0,y)=y^2>0$ also occur arbitrarily nearby; the stationary origin is a saddle.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $x\to0$ 时，这些点趋于 $(0,0)$，所以原点周围每个球都包含更低的函数值，原点连非严格局部极小都不是。沿 $y$ 轴又有 $f(0,y)=y^2>0$，任意近处也能找到更高的值；原点这个驻点因此是鞍点。

## Where the inference fails
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么不能交换“每个”与“存在一个”*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The straight-line statement has the order “for each fixed direction, there exists a small interval.” A strict local minimum needs “there exists a small ball, for every displacement in that ball.” The first permits the interval to shrink as the direction changes; it does not provide the common ball required by the second.

Each point on the descending parabola lies on some line through the origin. There is no contradiction: as the point approaches the origin, that line changes. The point can be outside the positive interval for its particular line while still being arbitrarily close to the origin in the plane. This is why examining all lines separately does not exhaust the possible approaches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直线结论的顺序是：“对每个固定方向，存在一段小区间。”严格局部极小需要的是：“存在一个小球，对球内每个位移都成立。”前者允许区间随着方向改变而缩小，并未提供后者所需的共同小球。

下降抛物线上的每个点，当然也在某条经过原点的直线上。这并不矛盾：当点趋近原点时，对应的直线也在改变。这个点可以落在它所属直线的正值区间之外，同时在平面中任意接近原点。因此，逐条固定直线检查，并没有穷尽全部趋近方式。

## Why a positive-definite Hessian does give a neighborhood result
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正定 Hessian 怎样补上统一控制*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For this polynomial the [[全微分#实值函数、梯度与全微分|gradient]], the column of first partials, and the [[Hessian矩阵|Hessian]] are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本例的[[全微分#实值函数、梯度与全微分|梯度]]（一阶偏导组成的列）和 [[Hessian矩阵|Hessian]] 为

$$\nabla f(x,y)=\begin{pmatrix}-6xy+8x^3\\2y-3x^2\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
H_f(x,y)=\begin{pmatrix}-6y+24x^2&-6x\\-6x&2\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the origin the gradient is zero and $H_f(0,0)=\operatorname{diag}(0,2)$, a [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] with entries $0$ and $2$, hence singular and [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefinite]]. Its quadratic form is $2h_2^2$: it vanishes along the horizontal direction. The [[半定Hessian无结论|singular semidefinite boundary]] therefore applies. Smoothness is not the problem—the function is a polynomial—but strict quadratic control is absent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原点处梯度为零，$H_f(0,0)=\operatorname{diag}(0,2)$，即对角元为 $0$、$2$ 的[[对角矩阵]]，因而奇异且[[半正定矩阵|半正定]]。其二次型为 $2h_2^2$，在水平方向为零，因此属于[[半定Hessian无结论|奇异半定边界]]。问题不在光滑性——函数是多项式——而在于缺少严格的二次控制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In contrast, a [[正定谱下界|positive-definite Hessian has a uniform lower bound]] $h^THh\ge m\|h\|_2^2$ with one $m>0$ for every $h$. At a $C^2$ stationary point, the [[多元Taylor近似|Taylor remainder]] is also controlled over all small displacements. These two uniform statements prove the [[Hessian 局部极小判据|strict local minimum criterion]]. Merely obtaining a positive value on each fixed line does not supply them. This example also shows that an indefinite Hessian is sufficient, but not necessary, for a saddle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相比之下，[[正定谱下界|正定 Hessian 存在统一下界]] $h^THh\ge m\|h\|_2^2$，同一个 $m>0$ 对每个 $h$ 都成立。在 $C^2$ 驻点处，[[多元Taylor近似|Taylor 余项]]也同时受控于全部小位移。两种统一控制结合，才证明[[Hessian 局部极小判据]]。仅仅在每条固定直线上分别获得正值，并没有提供这些保证。本例也说明：Hessian 不定是鞍点的充分条件，却不是必要条件。
