# 财务报表比较分析先选择与问题匹配的基准再解释同口径差异
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Comparative financial statement analysis selects a benchmark suited to the question before interpreting like-for-like differences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先明确要解释趋势、相对表现还是预算执行，再分别选本企业历史、可比企业或预算作为基准；完成[[财务比率口径匹配]]后，比较金额、[[结构百分比报表|结构占比]]或比率，追查差异所在的具体项目。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Decide whether the question concerns a trend, relative performance, or budget execution. Select the firm's history, comparable firms, or a budget accordingly. After [[财务比率口径匹配|aligning measurement bases]], compare amounts, [[结构百分比报表|common-size proportions]], or ratios and investigate the items responsible for the differences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

历史值能显示变化，却不保证代表合理水平；行业平均可能混合不同业务模式；预算偏差也可能来自预算假设错误。比较基准提供问题的参照，不自动提供“好/坏”的答案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Historical values reveal change without establishing an appropriate level. Industry averages may combine different business models, and budget variances may reflect flawed assumptions. A benchmark frames the question; it does not automatically answer whether performance is good or bad.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如本年利润120、上年100、预算150：同比增长20%，但低于预算20%。两句可同时成立；进一步要区分销售环境、执行情况和预算设定，不能挑一个百分比代替完整判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Profit of 120 versus 100 last year and a budget of 150 is both 20% higher year on year and 20% below budget. Both statements are true. Sales conditions, execution, and budget assumptions must then be distinguished instead of selecting one percentage as the whole conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
