# 市场份额是经营者在界定市场中占有的同口径业务量比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Market share is an operator's proportion of consistently measured business activity in a defined market.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场份额把一家企业或经营者的销售额、销量等指定指标，除以同一市场、同一时期和同一计量口径的总量。若 $q_i\geq0$ 且 $Q=\sum_{j=1}^Nq_j>0$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market share divides an operator's sales value, volume, or other specified measure by the corresponding total for the same market and period. With $q_i\geq0$ and $Q=\sum_{j=1}^Nq_j>0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$s_i=\frac{q_i}{Q},\qquad \sum_{i=1}^Ns_i=1.$$

例如，一家公司卖出 20 万件，而该市场共售出 100 万件，销量份额为 20%。若该公司产品单价更高，其销售额份额可能不同。只拿上市公司销售额作分母，得到的是该样本内份额，不是包含未上市企业及进口供给的完整市场份额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Selling 200,000 units in a market with one million units gives a 20% volume share. A higher product price may imply a different revenue share. Using only listed companies in the denominator gives a sample share, not the share of a complete market that includes unlisted firms and imports.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

份额必须先有边界，才能用于[[行业集中率]]或[[HHI指数]]；它本身不说明利润率或竞争行为。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The boundary must be fixed before shares are used in a [[行业集中率|concentration ratio]] or [[HHI指数|HHI]]. A share alone does not describe margins or competitive conduct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
