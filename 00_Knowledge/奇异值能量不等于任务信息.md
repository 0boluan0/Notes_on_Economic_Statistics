# Singular-value energy and task information
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*奇异值能量与任务信息的区别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keeping most of a matrix's singular-value energy can reproduce its entries closely while losing the feature needed to predict a target. The two questions are different: **How much does the matrix change?** and **Can the retained representation still do the intended job?** A percentage answering the first question cannot by itself answer the second.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保留矩阵的大部分奇异值能量，可以让重构后的元素很接近原矩阵，却仍然丢掉预测目标所需的特征。这里有两个问题：**矩阵改动了多少？**以及**保留的表示还能否完成预定任务？**回答第一个问题的百分比，不能单独回答第二个问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonzero matrix $A$, a [[截断SVD|truncated SVD]] $A_k$ keeps $k$ leading singular components. Its [[奇异值能量比|energy ratio]] satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非零矩阵 $A$，[[截断SVD]] $A_k$ 保留前 $k$ 个奇异分量，其[[奇异值能量比]]满足

$$E_k=1-\frac{\|A-A_k\|_F^2}{\|A\|_F^2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The squared [[Frobenius范数|Frobenius norm]] adds the squared magnitudes of all entries. Thus a high $E_k$ guarantees a small **relative squared reconstruction error** for this matrix in this metric. The calculation does not use a response $y$, a classification cost or a causal assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Frobenius范数]]的平方把所有元素的模平方相加。因此，高 $E_k$ 保证的是：在这个度量下，当前矩阵的**相对平方重构误差**较小。计算中没有使用预测目标 $y$、分类错误代价或因果假设。

## A small direction can contain the whole target
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*小方向可以承载整个预测目标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider four observations with two measured features $x_1,x_2$, and a target $y$ to predict. Treat the four rows equally when measuring prediction error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑四个观测，每个观测有两个特征 $x_1,x_2$，以及待预测目标 $y$。计算预测误差时，四行取相同权重。

| Observation / 观测 | $x_1$ | $x_2$ | $y$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $5$ | $1/2$ | $1$ |
| 2 | $5$ | $-1/2$ | $-1$ |
| 3 | $-5$ | $1/2$ | $1$ |
| 4 | $-5$ | $-1/2$ | $-1$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Put the two feature columns in $A$. They both have mean zero. Their [[向量点积|dot product]] is zero, while their squared lengths are $100$ and $1$. The [[矩阵转置|transpose]] $A^T$ exchanges rows and columns, so $A^TA$ records these column dot products:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把两个特征列排成矩阵 $A$。它们的均值均为零，[[向量点积]]为零，长度平方分别为 $100$ 与 $1$。[[矩阵转置]] $A^T$ 交换行列，所以 $A^TA$ 记录这些列的点积：

$$A=\begin{bmatrix}5&1/2\\5&-1/2\\-5&1/2\\-5&-1/2\end{bmatrix},
\qquad A^TA=\begin{bmatrix}100&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[奇异值与Gram谱|Gram spectral identity]] therefore gives singular values $10$ and $1$. The leading right singular direction is the first coordinate, so the rank-one truncation keeps $x_1$ and sets the second column to zero. It retains
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[奇异值与Gram谱|Gram 谱恒等式]]，奇异值为 $10$ 和 $1$。第一右奇异方向是第一个坐标，因此秩一截断保留 $x_1$，把第二列置零，能量保留率为

$$E_1=\frac{10^2}{10^2+1^2}=\frac{100}{101}\approx99.01\%.$$

> [!example] Almost all energy, no distinction between opposite targets · 几乎保留全部能量，却分不开相反目标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Before truncation, the rule $y=2x_2$ predicts all four targets exactly. Afterwards, observations 1 and 2 have identical retained inputs, as do observations 3 and 4, yet each pair has opposite targets. Any prediction rule using only the retained representation must give the same prediction within each pair.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 截断前，规则 $y=2x_2$ 精确预测四个目标。截断后，观测 1、2 的保留输入完全相同，观测 3、4 也相同，但每对目标互为相反数。只使用保留表示的任何预测规则，都只能在每对中给出相同预测。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If that common prediction is $c$, the pair's average squared error is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若同一对中的共同预测为 $c$，这一对的平均平方误差是

$$\frac{(1-c)^2+(-1-c)^2}{2}=1+c^2\ge1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The best prediction for each pair is therefore zero, with overall mean squared error $1$, whereas the original two-feature representation allows error $0$. This is a statement about this explicit four-point example, not a claim about every future population. It disproves any universal guarantee based only on the energy percentage. Since the columns are already centred, merely adding centring does not repair the failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以每对的最佳预测都是零，整体平均平方误差为 $1$；原来的双特征表示则可以做到误差 $0$。这是对这个明确四点例子的结论，并非对所有未来总体的断言；它已经足以否定仅靠能量百分比给出的普遍保证。由于两列已经中心化，再加一步中心化也不能修复此例的问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example does not say that small components are always valuable. They may contain noise, useful signal or both. It shows that ordering directions by their size alone does not determine their usefulness for an external target. Changing a feature's units can also change that ordering without changing which underlying quantity determines $y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子不表示小分量总有价值。它们可能含噪声、有效信号，或二者兼有。它说明，仅按方向大小排序，不能决定方向对外部目标的用途。改变特征单位还可能改变这种排序，但决定 $y$ 的基础量并没有因此改变。

## Evaluate the job that the representation must do
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*按表示实际要完成的任务评价*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For compression under entrywise squared error, $E_k$ is directly relevant: select a storage/error tradeoff in stated units. For prediction or classification, compare candidate values of $k$ using the actual target and loss. A large matrix component can be background variation, and a rare but important outcome can contribute little to an unweighted squared total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若压缩任务采用逐元素平方误差，$E_k$ 就直接相关：在明确单位后选择存储量与误差的折中。若任务是预测或分类，应使用实际目标与损失比较候选 $k$。矩阵中的大分量可能只是背景变化，少见但重要的结果也可能只占未加权平方总量的一小部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use [[训练验证测试划分|training data to fit, validation to select, and untouched test data to assess the selected procedure]]. Within each validation split, learn means, scales and singular directions on the training portion; apply that same fitted transform to the evaluation portion. This is the [[PCA验证与泄漏|PCA leakage boundary]], and choosing $k$ is part of selection. The [[验证结构匹配|split must also match the deployment question]], such as future-time prediction or prediction for new groups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按[[训练验证测试划分]]，训练数据用于拟合，验证用于选择，未参与选择的测试数据用于评价选定流程。每次验证切分内，都应在训练部分学习均值、尺度与奇异方向，再把同一已拟合变换用于评价部分。这是[[PCA验证与泄漏|PCA 的泄漏边界]]，选择 $k$ 也是选择过程的一部分。[[验证结构匹配|切分还应匹配部署问题]]，例如预测未来时间或新群组。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For causal analysis, reconstruction alone supplies no assumptions linking observed variation to an intervention. For inverse problems, the separate issue is [[小奇异值放大噪声|noise amplification when a small singular value is inverted]]. These require their own evidence; neither follows from an energy threshold. The PCA-specific variance interpretation is discussed in [[解释方差不等于任务信息|explained variance and task information]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对因果分析，重构本身不提供把观测变动连接到干预效果的假设。对反问题，另一个问题是[[小奇异值放大噪声|小奇异值取倒数时放大噪声]]。这些问题需要各自的证据，都不能由能量阈值推出。PCA 中方差占比的具体解释由[[解释方差不等于任务信息]]承接。

> [!question]- What is missing from a 95% claim? · “保留 95%”还缺什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Two components retain 95% of the energy, so they are sufficient to predict $y$.” The missing evidence is target-based evaluation under a suitable validation design. The percentage certifies a reconstruction ratio, while the predictive claim requires evidence about prediction loss.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> “两个分量保留 95% 能量，所以足以预测 $y$。”缺少的是针对目标、采用合适验证设计的评价。百分比确认的是重构比例，预测结论则需要预测损失方面的证据。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [*Randomized Numerical Linear Algebra: A Perspective on the Field With an Eye to Software*](https://www.netlib.org/utk/people/JackDongarra/PAPERS/rand-templates.pdf), §4.1.1, printed pp. 65–67 (PDF pp. 72–74): the reconstruction norm and centred PCA objectives. The four-point example, its singular values, energy ratio and best retained-input squared error are calculated directly above.
- [MathWorks, *Partial Least Squares Regression and Principal Components Regression*](https://www.mathworks.com/help/stats/partial-least-squares-regression-and-principal-components-regression.html), introductory comparison and “Choosing the Number of Components with Cross-Validation”: PCR chooses directions from predictor variation without the response; predictive performance requires a separate criterion.
- [scikit-learn, *Common pitfalls*, §12.2](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html#data-leakage) and [*Cross-validation*, introduction, §3.1.1 and §§3.1.2.4–3.1.2.6](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html): fit/selection/evaluation separation, PCA preprocessing leakage and group/time split conditions. These support the evaluation procedure, not a universal performance guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Netlib 所存综述第 4.1.1 节核对重构范数与中心化 PCA 目标；正文四点反例的奇异值、能量比及仅用保留输入时的最佳平方误差均直接计算。
- MathWorks 的 PCR/PLS 比较与交叉验证选成分部分核对：PCR 依据预测变量变动选方向，不使用响应，预测表现需要另外的评价准则。
- scikit-learn 核对拟合、选择、评价的分工，PCA 预处理泄漏及群组、时间切分条件；这些支持评价流程，不构成普遍性能保证。
