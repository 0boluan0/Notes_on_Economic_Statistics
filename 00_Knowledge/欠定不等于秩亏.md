# Underdetermined systems and rank deficiency
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*欠定不等于秩亏*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One equation cannot usually determine two unknown numbers uniquely. For example, $x_1+x_2=1$ allows $(1,0)$, $(0,1)$ and infinitely many other pairs. Yet its matrix $A=[1\ 1]$ has rank 1, the largest rank a one-row matrix can have. It is **underdetermined** because there are more unknowns than equations; it is **full rank for its shape** because the one available equation direction is present. These describe different features.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条方程通常不能唯一确定两个未知数。例如 $x_1+x_2=1$ 允许 $(1,0)$、$(0,1)$ 等无穷多组解，但矩阵 $A=[1\ 1]$ 的秩为1，已经是一行矩阵所能达到的最大秩。它因未知数多于方程而**欠定**，又因一个可用的方程方向已经具备而**达到其形状允许的满秩**。这是两个不同特征。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a real $m\times n$ matrix, with positive integers $m,n$. In $Ax=b$, there are $m$ equations and $n$ unknown coefficients. The [[矩阵秩|rank]] $r$ counts independent column directions; it also equals the number of independent rows. It cannot exceed either $m$ or $n$. We use the rectangular-matrix convention

$$\text{full rank: }r=\min(m,n),\qquad
\text{rank deficient: }r<\min(m,n).$$

An underdetermined system has $m<n$. It may have full row rank $r=m$, or it may additionally be rank deficient with $r<m$. The phrase “not full column rank” means $r<n$ and applies to every underdetermined matrix; it is not the same test as rank deficiency under this convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为实 $m\times n$ 矩阵，$m,n$ 是正整数。在 $Ax=b$ 中有 $m$ 条方程、$n$ 个未知系数。[[矩阵秩|秩]] $r$ 计数独立列方向，也等于独立行数，不可能超过 $m$ 或 $n$。这里采用上述矩形矩阵口径：满秩为 $r=\min(m,n)$，秩亏为 $r<\min(m,n)$。

欠定指 $m<n$；它可以满行秩 $r=m$，也可以另外秩亏 $r<m$。“不满列秩”指 $r<n$，每个欠定矩阵都满足它，但在上述口径下，这不等于秩亏判据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Every target is reachable, but the input is not unique
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The especially simple matrix $A=[1\ 0]$ sends $(x_1,x_2)^T$ to $x_1$. For any scalar target $b$, every solution has the form

$$x=(b,t)^T,\qquad t\in\mathbb R.$$

Its rank is $1=\min(1,2)$, so it is not rank deficient. The second input is invisible to the output. Its [[欧氏范数|squared Euclidean length]] is $b^2+t^2$, minimized uniquely by $t=0$. Thus the shortest exact solution is $(b,0)^T$, despite the infinitely many exact solutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最简单的 $A=[1\ 0]$ 把 $(x_1,x_2)^T$ 送到 $x_1$。对任何标量目标 $b$，全部解都是上面的 $(b,t)^T$。秩为 $1=\min(1,2)$，因此并不秩亏；第二输入对输出不可见。系数的[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度平方]]为 $b^2+t^2$，仅在 $t=0$ 最小，所以虽有无穷多精确解，最短精确解仍唯一，为 $(b,0)^T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any full-row-rank underdetermined matrix, the [[列空间|column space]] $C(A)$ is all of $\mathbb R^m$: every target $b$ is attainable. Equivalently, $A$ has a [[右逆与满行秩|right inverse]] that supplies a solution to each target. Meanwhile the [[零空间|null space]] $N(A)=\{z:Az=0\}$ contains invisible input directions. The [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity theorem]] gives

$$\dim N(A)=n-r=n-m>0.$$

If $x_0$ solves $Ax=b$, then so does $x_0+z$ for every $z\in N(A)$. Over the reals or complex numbers, varying any nonzero such $z$ produces infinitely many solutions. A nontrivial null space is therefore compatible with maximum possible rank in a wide matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意欠定满行秩矩阵，[[列空间]] $C(A)$ 等于整个 $\mathbb R^m$，所以任何目标 $b$ 都可达；等价地，它有[[右逆与满行秩|右逆]]，可为每个目标给出一个解。同时[[零空间]] $N(A)=\{z:Az=0\}$ 包含不可见输入方向；[[秩零度定理]]给出上式 $\dim N(A)=n-m>0$。若 $x_0$ 解 $Ax=b$，则每个 $x_0+z$ 也解它。在实数或复数域，沿任一非零的此类方向变化，便有无穷多个解。因此，宽矩阵达到最大可能秩，仍可有非平凡零空间。

> [!warning] Too few equations does not promise consistency / 方程少不保证有解
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\2&0&0\end{pmatrix}$. It is underdetermined because $2<3$, and rank deficient because its rank is $1<2$. For $b=(1,2)^T$ there are infinitely many exact solutions $(1,s,t)^T$. For $b=(1,3)^T$ there is no exact solution: the second output must be twice the first. Underdetermined shape alone neither guarantees nor excludes a fit; full row rank is what guarantees a fit for **every** target.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\2&0&0\end{pmatrix}$。它因 $2<3$ 而欠定，也因秩 $1<2$ 而秩亏。$b=(1,2)^T$ 时有无穷多个精确解 $(1,s,t)^T$；$b=(1,3)^T$ 时无精确解，因为第二输出必须是第一输出的两倍。仅由欠定形状，既不能保证也不能排除可拟合；满行秩保证的才是**每个**目标都可拟合。

## Choose a method after separating shape and rank
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 算法边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a full-row-rank underdetermined $A$, a QR factorization of its transpose gives a convenient minimum-norm route. Its [[薄QR分解|thin QR]] is $A^T=Q_1R$, with $Q_1$ of size $n\times m$ and orthonormal columns, and invertible upper-triangular $R$ of size $m\times m$. Solve $R^Ty=b$, then set $x=Q_1y$. Indeed $Ax=R^TQ_1^TQ_1y=b$, and $x$ lies perpendicular to $N(A)$, so it is the shortest solution. Factoring $A$ as a lower-triangular factor times a matrix with orthonormal rows is the equivalent LQ route.

In contrast, when the matrix is genuinely rank deficient, first check whether the target is attainable and use a [[秩亏最小二乘算法|rank-aware minimum-norm least-squares method]] if that is the desired objective. SVD works for both branches. A small positive singular value treated as zero under a tolerance is a separate [[数值秩|numerical-rank]] choice, not something implied by the row/column counts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

欠定满行秩的 $A$ 可对其转置作 QR 来求最小范数解。[[薄QR分解]]为 $A^T=Q_1R$，其中 $Q_1$ 为 $n\times m$，各列标准正交；$R$ 是可逆的 $m\times m$ 上三角矩阵。先解 $R^Ty=b$，再令 $x=Q_1y$，有 $Ax=R^TQ_1^TQ_1y=b$。而 $x$ 垂直于 $N(A)$，所以是最短解。等价的 LQ 路线将 $A$ 分为下三角因子与标准正交行矩阵的乘积。

真正秩亏时，先分清目标是否可达；若需要最小范数最小二乘，就用[[秩亏最小二乘算法|显式处理秩的算法]]。SVD 对两个分支都适用。按容差将小正奇异值视为零，则是另一个[[数值秩]]选择，并非由行数与列数自动决定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, rank 3 in a $3\times5$ matrix means underdetermined and full row rank, with nullity $5-3=2$; rank 2 would mean underdetermined and rank deficient, with nullity 3. The same shape/rank distinction holds over the complex numbers. There, use conjugate transpose in the QR construction and squared absolute values for lengths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$3\times5$ 矩阵若秩为3，就是欠定满行秩，零度为 $5-3=2$；若秩为2，才同时欠定与秩亏，零度为3。复数域中形状与秩的区别不变，但上述 QR 构造须使用共轭转置，长度平方须用绝对值平方和。
