# 欠定且满行秩的矩阵不秩亏但仍有非平凡零空间和无穷多个精确解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An underdetermined full-row-rank matrix is not rank deficient, yet it has a nontrivial null space and infinitely many exact solutions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 概念区分
> 对 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，秩亏是
> $$
> \operatorname{rank}(A)<\min(m,n).
> $$
> 若 $m<n$ 且 $\operatorname{rank}(A)=m$，则 $A$ 已达到其形状允许的最大秩，是欠定且满行秩，并不秩亏。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Rank deficiency means rank below the maximum allowed by the matrix shape. An $m\times n$ matrix with $m<n$ and rank $m$ is underdetermined and full row rank, not rank deficient.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满行秩使 $C(A)=\mathbb R^m$，所以每个 $b\in\mathbb R^m$ 都有精确解；同时秩—零度定理给出
$$
\dim N(A)=n-m>0,
$$
因此每个精确解都可加上任意零空间向量，解不唯一。欠定描述方程数少于未知数；秩亏描述没有达到形状允许的最大秩。二者不能互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full row rank makes the map onto $\mathbb R^m$, so every right-hand side has an exact solution. Rank-nullity also gives a nontrivial null space of dimension $n-m$, making those solutions nonunique. Underdetermined describes shape; rank deficient describes failure to reach the maximum rank permitted by that shape.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $A=[1\ 0]\in\mathbb R^{1\times2}$。它的秩为 $1=\min(1,2)$，所以不秩亏；但方程 $Ax=b$ 的解是
$$
x=(b,t)^T,
\qquad t\in\mathbb R,
$$
有无穷多个。唯一的 Euclidean 最小范数解是 $(b,0)^T$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A=[1\ 0]$, the rank is $1=\min(1,2)$, so the matrix is not rank deficient. Nevertheless, $Ax=b$ has the infinitely many solutions $(b,t)^T$; the unique minimum-norm solution is $(b,0)^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 算法边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Algorithm boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

欠定满行秩系统可用 LQ、对 $A^T$ 做 QR，或用 SVD 求最小范数解。不能仅因为它“不满列秩”就把它送入秩亏分支；应先按 $\min(m,n)$ 判断是否达到最大可能秩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A full-row-rank underdetermined system may be solved by LQ, by QR of $A^T$, or by the SVD when the minimum-norm solution is wanted. Lack of full column rank alone does not make it rank deficient; compare its rank with $\min(m,n)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个 $3\times5$ 矩阵的秩为 $3$。它是欠定、秩亏，还是两者都是？
>
> **答案：** 它欠定且满行秩，但不秩亏，因为 $3=\min(3,5)$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Question:** A $3\times5$ matrix has rank $3$. Is it underdetermined, rank deficient, or both?
>
> **Answer:** It is underdetermined and full row rank, but not rank deficient, because $3=\min(3,5)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
