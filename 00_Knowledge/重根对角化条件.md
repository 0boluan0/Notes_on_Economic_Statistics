# Diagonalization with repeated eigenvalues
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*重根的对角化判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Seeing a repeated eigenvalue is a reason to count eigenvectors, not a verdict that diagonalization fails. [[对角化|Diagonalization]] needs a full [[基|basis]] of independent eigenvectors. A repeated root succeeds when its eigenspace supplies as many independent directions as the polynomial asks for.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

发现重复特征值，接下来应当数独立特征向量，而不能立刻断定对角化失败。[[对角化]]需要一整组独立特征向量构成的[[基]]。一个重根能否通过检查，要看其特征空间是否提供了多项式要求的那么多个独立方向。

> [!example] The same two roots, different outcomes · 相同的两个根，不同的结果
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Over $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, compare the [[单位矩阵|identity]] $I_2$ with
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在实数或复数域上，比较[[单位矩阵]] $I_2$ 与
>
> $$J=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both have characteristic polynomial $(t-1)^2$. For $I_2$, every vector satisfies $I_2v=v$, so $(1,0)^T,(0,1)^T$ give two independent eigenvectors. For $J$, the equation $J(x,y)^T=(x,y)^T$ forces $y=0$, leaving only the direction $(1,0)^T$. The superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Thus $I_2$ is already diagonal, whereas $J$ cannot be diagonalized.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两者的特征多项式都是 $(t-1)^2$。对 $I_2$，每个向量都满足 $I_2v=v$，所以 $(1,0)^T,(0,1)^T$ 给出两个独立特征向量。对 $J$，$J(x,y)^T=(x,y)^T$ 迫使 $y=0$，只留下 $(1,0)^T$ 这一个方向；上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。所以 $I_2$ 已经是对角矩阵，而 $J$ 不能对角化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## First fix the scalar field, then compare the counts
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先固定标量域，再比较计数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ matrix over a fixed field $\mathbb F$, with $n\ge1$. Its [[特征多项式|characteristic polynomial]] $p_A(t)=\det(tI_n-A)$ **splits over $\mathbb F$** when it can be written entirely as linear factors $t-\lambda_i$ with $\lambda_i\in\mathbb F$. Here $t$ is a polynomial variable and $\det$ is the [[行列式|determinant]]. The [[特征多项式底层域|choice of field]] determines which roots and eigenvectors are available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为固定域 $\mathbb F$ 上的 $n\times n$ 方阵，$n\ge1$。它的[[特征多项式]] $p_A(t)=\det(tI_n-A)$ **在 $\mathbb F$ 上分裂**，意思是它能够完全写成线性因子 $t-\lambda_i$ 的乘积，且每个 $\lambda_i\in\mathbb F$。其中 $t$ 是多项式变量，$\det$ 为[[行列式]]。[[特征多项式底层域|标量域的选择]]决定哪些根和特征向量可用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each distinct root, [[代数重数与几何重数|algebraic multiplicity]] $m_a(\lambda)$ counts its factor repetitions, while geometric multiplicity $m_g(\lambda)=\dim E_\lambda(A)$ counts independent directions in the [[特征空间|eigenspace]]. One always has $1\le m_g\le m_a$ for an eigenvalue in that field.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个不同的根，[[代数重数与几何重数|代数重数]] $m_a(\lambda)$ 数因子重复次数，几何重数 $m_g(\lambda)=\dim E_\lambda(A)$ 数[[特征空间]]中的独立方向。对该域中的特征值，总有 $1\le m_g\le m_a$。

> [!important] The complete multiplicity test · 完整的重数判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The matrix $A$ is diagonalizable over $\mathbb F$ exactly when $p_A$ splits over $\mathbb F$ and every eigenvalue satisfies
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $A$ 在 $\mathbb F$ 上可对角化，当且仅当 $p_A$ 在 $\mathbb F$ 上分裂，并且每个特征值都满足
>
> $$m_g(\lambda)=m_a(\lambda).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Once splitting has been established, only repeated roots need further dimension calculations: for a simple root, $1\le m_g\le m_a=1$ already forces $m_g=1$. One strict mismatch is enough to rule out diagonalization.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 确认分裂以后，只需对重根进一步计算维数：单根有 $1\le m_g\le m_a=1$，自动得到 $m_g=1$。发现一处严格不相等，就足以否定对角化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why these counts give the answer
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*这些计数为什么足以判断*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A$ is diagonalizable, some invertible matrix $S$ gives $S^{-1}AS=D$, where $D$ is a [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]]. Its diagonal entries belong to $\mathbb F$, so its polynomial is a product of the corresponding linear factors. For each $\lambda$, the number of diagonal entries equal to $\lambda$ is both its root multiplicity and the dimension of its eigenspace. [[相似保持特征多项式|Similarity preserves the polynomial]], and [[相似保持特征空间维数|it preserves eigenspace dimensions]], so both properties also hold for $A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A$ 可对角化，就有可逆矩阵 $S$ 使 $S^{-1}AS=D$，其中 $D$ 是[[对角矩阵]]。对角元都属于 $\mathbb F$，所以其特征多项式是相应线性因子的乘积。对每个 $\lambda$，等于 $\lambda$ 的对角元个数，既是根的重数，也是特征空间维数。[[相似保持特征多项式|相似保留多项式]]，也[[相似保持特征空间维数|保留特征空间维数]]，所以 $A$ 也满足这两项条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, suppose the polynomial splits, and list its distinct roots as $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_s$. Its degree is $n$, so the algebraic multiplicities sum to $n$. If every geometric multiplicity matches, choose a basis of each eigenspace: the combined list contains $\sum_{i=1}^s m_g(\lambda_i)=n$ vectors. The [[互异特征值向量独立|independence argument for distinct eigenvalues]] shows that bases from different eigenspaces fit together independently: group any zero combination by eigenvalue, force every group sum to zero, and then use independence within each basis. We have an eigenvector basis, which is exactly the [[可对角化判据|diagonalizability criterion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，若多项式已经分裂，把不同的根列为 $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_s$。多项式次数是 $n$，故代数重数之和为 $n$。若每个几何重数都相等，各特征空间分别选基，合起来便有 $\sum_{i=1}^s m_g(\lambda_i)=n$ 个向量。[[互异特征值向量独立|互异特征值的独立性论证]]保证不同特征空间的基合起来仍独立：把零组合按特征值分组，先迫使每组和为零，再用每组基内部的独立性。于是获得一整组特征向量基，这正是[[可对角化判据]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Work through a repeated root
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*实际检查一个重根*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real example, take
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取一个实矩阵例子：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0&1\\0&2&0\\0&0&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad p_A(t)=(t-2)^2(t-3).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The polynomial splits, with $m_a(2)=2$ and $m_a(3)=1$. Solve $(A-2I_3)(x,y,z)^T=0$: it requires $z=0$, while $x,y$ are free. Hence $E_2$ has basis $(1,0,0)^T,(0,1,0)^T$ and dimension two. The simple root $3$ already passes the count test; solving for a vector gives $(1,0,1)^T$. Put these three vectors into columns:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多项式已分裂，$m_a(2)=2$、$m_a(3)=1$。解 $(A-2I_3)(x,y,z)^T=0$，得到 $z=0$，而 $x,y$ 自由。因此 $E_2$ 的基可取 $(1,0,0)^T,(0,1,0)^T$，维数为2。单根3已经自动通过计数检查；若再求出一个向量，可取 $(1,0,1)^T$。把这三个向量作为列：

$$S=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&1\\0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad AS=S\begin{pmatrix}2&0&0\\0&2&0\\0&0&3\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The columns are independent, so $S$ is invertible and the last equality produces the diagonalization. The repeated value $2$ caused no failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三列独立，所以 $S$ 可逆，最后一个等式便给出对角化。重复值2没有造成失败。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In practice, read $m_a$ from the polynomial and solve the homogeneous equations $(A-\lambda I_n)v=0$ for each repeated root. Equivalently, the [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity theorem]] gives $m_g=n-\operatorname{rank}(A-\lambda I_n)$. If one root has $m_a=3$ and $m_g=2$, no further root checks can rescue diagonalization: one required independent direction is already missing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际操作时，先从多项式读取 $m_a$，再对每个重根解齐次方程 $(A-\lambda I_n)v=0$。也可用[[秩零度定理]]得到 $m_g=n-\operatorname{rank}(A-\lambda I_n)$。若某根已出现 $m_a=3$、$m_g=2$，不必再检查其他根来挽救对角化，因为已经少了一个必需的独立方向。

> [!warning] Do not skip the field condition · 不要漏掉域条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The real rotation matrix $R=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ has $p_R(t)=t^2+1$. It has no real root and no real eigenvector basis. Checking all its real eigenvalues would check an empty list and miss the obstruction. Over $\mathbb C$, the roots $i,-i$, with $i^2=-1$, are distinct, so it is diagonalizable there. Conversely, a zero eigenvalue does not obstruct diagonalization: the zero matrix is already diagonal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 实旋转矩阵 $R=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ 的 $p_R(t)=t^2+1$，没有实根，也没有实特征向量基。若只检查“全部实特征值”，检查的是空列表，就会漏掉障碍。在 $\mathbb C$ 上，根为互异的 $i,-i$，其中 $i^2=-1$，所以它在复数域上可对角化。另外，零特征值并不妨碍对角化：零矩阵本来就是对角矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the field condition holds but an eigenspace is too small, [[广义特征向量|generalized eigenvectors]] and [[Jordan链|Jordan chains]] describe the missing layers. They give a different form of simplification; they do not make the failed eigenvector-basis test pass.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当域条件成立、某个特征空间却太小时，[[广义特征向量]]和[[Jordan链]]描述缺少的层级。它们提供另一种简化形式，并不会使已经失败的特征向量基检查重新通过。
