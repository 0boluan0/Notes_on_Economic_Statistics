# 乌云盖顶是上涨后高开阴线收盘深入前阳线实体的两线组合

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dark cloud cover is a two-candle formation after an advance whose second candle opens higher and closes downward deep inside the first upward body.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乌云盖顶是上涨后，或水平整理区上端出现的两根[[K线]]组合：第一根为较长阳线，第二根高开后转为阴线，收盘明显深入前阳线实体。本课采用第二期开盘高于第一期最高价、收盘低于前实体中点的识别口径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dark cloud cover occurs after an advance or near the top of a trading range. A long up [[K线|candle]] is followed by a higher opening and a down candle closing well inside the first body. The course convention places the second open above the first high and its close below the first body midpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若第一根 $O_1=100,C_1=110,H_1=111$，第二根 $O_2=112,C_2=104$，其收盘已低于中点105，但仍高于前开盘100；这与把前实体完全覆盖的看跌[[吞没形态]]有别。若仅进入实体、尚未到中点，本课要求另等看跌确认，不能直接套用较严口径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $O_1=100,C_1=110,H_1=111$ and $O_2=112,C_2=104$, the second close is below the midpoint 105 but above the first open 100. This differs from bearish [[吞没形态|engulfing]], which contains the whole first body. If penetration stops above the midpoint, the course requires further bearish confirmation rather than treating the stricter criterion as satisfied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二根收盘前不能确定最终穿入程度。该形态的看跌解释属于K线学派的信号约定，不保证原上涨已经结束；对应的下降后形态见[[刺穿形态]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The final penetration is unknown before the second close. Its bearish reading is a candlestick convention, not a guarantee that the advance has ended. The counterpart after a decline is the [[刺穿形态|piercing pattern]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
