# 债券是发行人按约定条件承担偿付义务的债务证券

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bond is a debt security under which the issuer undertakes payment obligations on specified terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券将[[债务合约]]表现为发行的证券，持有人取得约定的债权，而不是因此取得发行人的普通股所有权。发行人可以是政府、公司或其他主体；应付什么、何时应付、由谁承担偿付义务，都应从发行条款读取。偿付承诺不是已经实现的现金流，发行人仍可能违约。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bond expresses a [[债务合约|debt contract]] as an issued security. Its holder acquires the specified creditor claim, not common-equity ownership merely by holding the debt. The issuer may be a government, company or another entity. The terms determine what is due, when it is due and who owes it. A payment promise is not an already-realised cash flow; default remains possible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一张条款约定一年后偿还 100、期间不付息的[[零息债券]]，给持有人的是到期请求 100 的权利，不是“买入成本必为 100”或“最终必收足 100”的保证。是否收到及收到多少，还取决于履约和违约后的可执行权利。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[零息债券|zero-coupon bond]] promising 100 in one year with no interim coupon gives the holder a claim for 100 at maturity. It does not imply a purchase price of 100 or guaranteed full recovery. Actual payment depends on performance and enforceable rights following default.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券不必同时具有固定息票和固定到期日。[[固定息票债券]]、[[浮动利率债券]]、[[通胀挂钩债券]]描述不同现金流安排；永续债没有预设到期日。[[可赎回债券]]和[[可转换债券]]还含改变偿付时间或形式的权利。分类维度可以同时出现，不能仅用“债券”推断期限、担保、流动性或回报高低。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bonds need not have both fixed coupons and a fixed maturity date. [[固定息票债券|Fixed-coupon]], [[浮动利率债券|floating-rate]] and [[通胀挂钩债券|inflation-linked bonds]] describe different cash-flow arrangements; perpetual bonds have no preset maturity. [[可赎回债券|Callable]] and [[可转换债券|convertible bonds]] include rights that can change payment timing or form. Classification dimensions can coexist: the bond label alone does not establish maturity, security, liquidity or relative returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
