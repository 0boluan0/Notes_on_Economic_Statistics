# 通货紧缩是一般价格水平持续下降的现象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deflation is a sustained decline in the general price level.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通货紧缩不同于某个商品降价，也不同于[[通胀率与价格水平|正通胀率下降]]。对给定广泛价格指数，负通胀率意味着该比较期间价格水平下降；判断持续、广泛的通缩过程，还要看多期和覆盖范围，不能只靠一次受基数或季节影响的读数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deflation differs from one cheaper product or [[通胀率与价格水平|a falling positive inflation rate]]. Negative inflation measures a decline over the specified comparison period. Establishing a sustained, broad process requires evidence across periods and coverage, not one reading affected by base or seasonal effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通缩与产出下降不是同义词：需求收缩可同时压低价格与产出，生产率改善也可压低价格而增加产出。对固定名义债务，价格下降会提高其以商品衡量的实际负担；这是一个传导渠道，不是“任何通缩必导致衰退”的定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deflation is not synonymous with falling output. Weak demand can reduce both prices and output, whereas improved productivity can lower prices while raising output. Falling prices increase the goods-denominated burden of fixed nominal debt; this is a channel, not a definition implying that every deflation causes recession.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如名义到期债务为 100，价格指数从 100 降至 95，其按初期价格衡量的负担由 100 变为 $100/0.95\approx105.26$。是否引发违约还取决于收入、流动性和债务条款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With nominal debt of 100, a price index falling from 100 to 95 raises its burden in initial-period purchasing power to $100/0.95\approx105.26$. Default still depends on income, liquidity, and contract terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
