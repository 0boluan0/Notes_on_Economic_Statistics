# A known initial value makes first differences uniquely invertible by cumulative sums
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*固定初值后，可由累加唯一还原一阶差分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite real sequence with **known $x_0$**, let $d_k=x_k-x_{k-1}$ for $k=1,\ldots,n$. The original sequence is uniquely determined by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限实数序列，若 **$x_0$ 已知**，定义 $d_k=x_k-x_{k-1}$（$k=1,\ldots,n$），则原序列由下式唯一确定：

$$
x_k=x_0+\sum_{j=1}^k d_j.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof is telescoping: summing $x_j-x_{j-1}$ cancels the intermediate terms and leaves $x_k-x_0$. Conversely, adjacent cumulative sums differ by $d_k$, so the reconstruction satisfies every difference equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明就是相邻项相消：把 $x_j-x_{j-1}$ 加起来，中间项抵消，只剩 $x_k-x_0$。反过来，相邻累加和之差正好为 $d_k$，因此重建值满足每条差分方程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $x_0=0$ and $n=3$, the difference and summation maps are inverse matrices:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $x_0=0$、$n=3$ 时，差分与累加对应互逆矩阵：

$$
D=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\-1&1&0\\0&-1&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
S=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\1&1&0\\1&1&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad DS=SD=I.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $D(1,4,9)^T=(1,3,5)^T$ and $S(1,3,5)^T=(1,4,9)^T$. If $x_0$ is unknown, the differences determine the sequence only up to a common additive constant: replacing every $x_k$, including $x_0$, by $x_k+c$ preserves every $d_k$. Thus [[差分算子|differencing]] needs a boundary value to recover absolute levels. For known nonzero $x_0$, reconstruction is the affine map $x=x_0\mathbf1+Sd$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $D(1,4,9)^T=(1,3,5)^T$，而 $S(1,3,5)^T=(1,4,9)^T$。若 $x_0$ 未知，差值只能确定相差一个共同加法常数的一族序列：将包括 $x_0$ 在内的每个 $x_k$ 都改成 $x_k+c$，所有 $d_k$ 不变。因此，[[差分算子|差分]]需要边界值才能恢复绝对水平。已知非零 $x_0$ 时，重建是仿射映射 $x=x_0\mathbf1+Sd$。
