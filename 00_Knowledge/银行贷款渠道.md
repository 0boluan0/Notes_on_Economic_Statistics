# 银行贷款渠道是货币政策通过银行自身融资和资产负债表条件改变贷款供给的机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bank lending channel changes loan supply through monetary policy's effects on banks' own funding and balance-sheet conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行贷款渠道指政策改变银行提供贷款的能力或意愿，例如资金成本、盈利和资本承载变化使其调整报价、额度或审批标准。它是供给端机制，不等于借款人因项目回报下降而少申请贷款，也不是准备金按固定倍数变化的故事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bank lending channel concerns banks' ability or willingness to supply credit: funding costs, profits and capital capacity can change quotes, quantities or approval standards. It is not the same as fewer applications because projects become less attractive, nor a fixed reserve-multiplier mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，政策紧缩若使某银行融资成本上升而资产收入暂时固定，或使其承担更多损失，可能侵蚀资本并限制新贷款。但方向取决于资产负债重定价、对冲和损失情况；加息不保证每家银行利润下降，降息也不保证每家银行立即扩贷。完整约束清单复用[[银行信贷创造约束]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tightening may erode capital and constrain new lending when funding costs rise before asset income or losses increase. The direction depends on repricing, hedging and losses: hikes need not reduce every bank's profits, and cuts need not immediately expand every bank's lending. Reuse [[银行信贷创造约束|the constraints on bank credit creation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个条件化容量例：将既有风险加权资产固定为 100，新增贷款风险权重为 100%，资本要求假定为 8%，且不能立即补充资本。合格资本为 10 时，允许的新增贷款上界为 $10/0.08-100=25$；若资本状态改为 8，上界为 0。这里只比较给定资本状态下的约束空间，不把 8% 当作所有银行当前适用要求，也不保证原先的 25 一定会贷出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a conditional capacity example, hold existing risk-weighted assets at 100, give new loans a 100% risk weight, assume an 8% capital requirement and no immediate equity issuance. Capital of 10 allows at most $10/0.08-100=25$ in additional loans; capital of 8 leaves no room. This compares constraint space, not a universal current regulatory threshold or a prediction that all available room is used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若借款人能无成本转向其他银行或债券市场，总融资未必减少；要形成较强实体效应，还需融资替代不完全。识别时应比较相似需求下不同银行的供给反应，不能仅凭贷款总量下降下结论。借款人净值和[[外部融资溢价]]形成的反馈则复用[[金融加速器]]，不要与银行自身资本约束合为一个对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Costless substitution to other banks or bond markets could leave total finance unchanged; stronger real effects require imperfect alternatives. Identification compares supply responses with comparable demand rather than inferring supply contraction from falling loan totals. Borrower net-worth feedback through the [[外部融资溢价|external finance premium]] belongs to the [[金融加速器|financial accelerator]], distinct from banks' own capital constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Bank of England, About a rate of (general) interest](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/-/media/boe/files/quarterly-bulletin/2024/about-a-rate-of-general-interest-how-monetary-policy-transmits.pdf)，PDF 第 17–19 页 §3.2.5：支持银行供给、资本、盈利、固定资产收入与融资重定价的条件化机制，及其与借款人资产负债表渠道的区别。容量例为单约束计算。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Section 3.2.5 supports bank supply, capital, profits and repricing conditions, distinguished from borrower balance sheets. The capacity example isolates one constraint.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [ECB, Transmission mechanism](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/mopo/intro/transmission/html/index.en.html)，Affects the supply of bank loans：支持银行自身资本与外部资金边际成本进入贷款供给。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The bank-loan subsection supports the role of bank capital and marginal funding costs in credit supply.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Ben S. Bernanke, The Financial Accelerator and the Credit Channel](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/bernanke20070615a.htm)，2007，Monetary Policy and the Credit Channel 中讨论借款人能否转向其他贷方、银行资本筹集成本的段落：支持融资替代不完全这一实体效应条件，以及银行自身资本与借款人净值机制的区分。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The discussion of alternative lenders and costly bank capital supports imperfect financing substitution as a condition for real effects, and separates bank capital from borrower net-worth mechanisms.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
