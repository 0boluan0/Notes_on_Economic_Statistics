# 边际效用只能解释价值悖论的一部分而不能单独决定市场价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Marginal utility explains only part of the paradox of value and does not determine market price by itself*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 边际主义对价值悖论的解释比较的是稀缺条件下的边际评价，而不是一种物品全部用途的总和。水的总用途可以远高于钻石，但在水充足、钻石稀缺时，再多一单位水的评价仍可能低于再多一单位钻石的评价。这澄清了悖论，却没有把边际评价直接等同于实际市场价格。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The marginalist explanation of the paradox compares marginal valuation under scarcity with the total usefulness of an entire stock. Water may have far greater total usefulness than diamonds, while one additional unit of abundant water is valued less than one additional scarce diamond. This clarifies the paradox without identifying marginal valuation directly with the observed market price.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个人对新增单位的边际评价会随现有数量、偏好与预期用途而变；收入和替代品还会通过可行选择与机会成本影响其支付意愿。市场价格同时取决于生产与供给条件、其他买家的需求、市场结构以及交易制度。把“边际效用”直接写成“价格由效用决定”，会把需求侧机制误写成整个均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An individual's marginal valuation of an additional unit varies with current holdings, preferences, and expected uses; income and substitutes also shape willingness to pay through the feasible set and opportunity cost. Market price simultaneously depends on production and supply conditions, other buyers' demand, market structure, and trading institutions. Writing “utility determines price” mistakes one demand-side mechanism for the entire equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，观察到高价格也不能反推出一种物品具有更高总社会价值。价格是在特定分配、预算与供给约束下形成的边际交换比率；它不是跨商品、跨人的总福利排行榜。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A high observed price likewise does not prove that a good has greater total social value. Price is a marginal exchange ratio formed under a particular distribution of endowments, budgets, and supply constraints, not a ranking of total welfare across goods or people.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 干旱使饮用水供给骤减时，水的边际评价为什么会上升？这是否说明水此前没有总效用？
>
> **答案：** 供给减少使最后可用单位用于更迫切的需要，边际评价上升；水此前一直有很高总用途，只是额外一单位在充足状态下不稀缺。

- [Jevons, *The Theory of Political Economy*, Chapter IV](https://www.econlib.org/library/YPDBooks/Jevons/jvnPE.html?chapter_num=8)：核对 final degree、交换比率与总效用是不同对象；本卡没有把 Jevons 的需求侧交换条件扩写成完整价格理论。
- [Marshall, *Principles of Economics*, Book V](https://oll-resources.s3.amazonaws.com/titles/1676/Marshall_0197_EBk_v6.0.pdf)：核对市场价格必须放在供求共同作用和具体时间期中解释。
