# 信用与风险转换通过筛选分散和资本重新分配损失而不消灭风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Credit and risk transformation reallocates losses through screening, diversification, and capital without eliminating risk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子机制
> 中介可以持有许多有信用风险的贷款，却向客户发行风险形态不同、优先级更高或更易理解的索取权。筛选与监督降低预期损失，分散化减少单一借款人的特定风险，资本和次级索取权先吸收损失，担保或保险再把部分损失转给其他承担者。风险被重新组合并分层承担，而不是凭空消失。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An intermediary can hold many credit-risky loans while issuing claims with different risk characteristics, higher priority, or simpler payoffs. Screening and monitoring reduce expected loss; diversification reduces borrower-specific risk; capital and subordinated claims absorb losses first; guarantees or insurance transfer some losses to other bearers. Risk is repackaged and allocated across layers, not made to vanish.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设银行有 100 笔彼此关联较弱的小额贷款。若其中一笔违约，其损失可先由其余资产收益和银行资本吸收，单个存款人不必直接承担这笔贷款的全部波动。但若贷款都依赖同一房地产价格、行业或宏观收入，压力来临时相关性会上升，“贷款数量很多”也不能提供足够分散。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a bank holds 100 small loans whose outcomes are only weakly related. One default can be absorbed first by returns on other assets and by bank capital, so no depositor bears the full volatility of that loan directly. But if every loan depends on the same property price, industry, or aggregate income, correlations rise under stress and a large loan count does not provide adequate diversification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

风险转换也会改变激励。若客户相信自己的索取权完全安全，而中介股东在成功时获得上行、失败时把部分成本转给保险人或社会，中介可能承担过多风险。因此资本要求、风险定价、监督和处置制度不仅是事后救援，也是让风险承担者面对适当损失的事前约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Risk transformation also changes incentives. If customers view their claims as perfectly safe while intermediary shareholders receive the upside and shift part of failure costs to insurers or society, the intermediary may take excessive risk. Capital requirements, risk pricing, supervision, and resolution regimes are therefore not merely ex-post rescue tools; they are ex-ante constraints intended to align risk taking with loss bearing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 分散化主要削弱个体特有风险，不能消除共同宏观冲击、模型错误或同时挤兑。
- “更安全的负债”来自资产组合、优先级、资本、保险与制度安排的共同作用，不能只归功于其中一项。
- 证券化可以把风险分层出售，却可能使最终承担者和共同敞口更难识别；复杂包装不是风险减少的证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Diversification primarily reduces idiosyncratic risk; it cannot eliminate common macro shocks, model error, or simultaneous runs.
- A safer liability reflects the joint effect of the asset pool, seniority, capital, insurance, and institutional arrangements, not any one feature alone.
- Securitisation can tranche and distribute risk while making its ultimate bearers and common exposures harder to identify. Complexity is not evidence of risk reduction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么“把 1,000 笔房贷放进一个池子”不能单独证明风险已经充分分散？
>
> **答案：** 笔数只说明名义上的分散。若借款人收入和抵押品价值都受同一利率或房地产冲击影响，违约会一起上升；还要看相关性、集中度、资本和损失优先级。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does placing 1,000 mortgages in one pool not by itself prove adequate diversification?
>
> **Answer:** Loan count shows only nominal dispersion. If borrower income and collateral values respond to the same interest-rate or property shock, defaults rise together. Correlation, concentration, capital, and loss priority still matter.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
