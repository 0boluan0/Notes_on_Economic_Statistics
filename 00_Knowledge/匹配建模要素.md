# 匹配建模先固定两侧、边、覆盖、容量与目标

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A matching model first fixes its sides, edge meaning, coverage, capacities, and objective*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子程序
> 在求解前依次写清五件事：
>
> 1. 左右两侧分别代表什么；
> 2. 一条边精确表示什么资格；
> 3. 必须覆盖哪一侧，还是只求尽量多；
> 4. 每个顶点的容量是否为一；
> 5. 成功标准只问可行性，还是要求 maximum cardinality、成本或权重最优，或者偏好稳定。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Before solving, state five things in order:
>
> &nbsp;
> **1.** What the left and right sides represent.<br>
> **2.** Exactly what eligibility an edge represents.<br>
> **3.** Which side must be covered, or whether the goal is only to cover as many vertices as possible.<br>
> **4.** Whether every vertex has unit capacity.<br>
> **5.** Whether success means mere feasibility, maximum cardinality, optimal cost or weight, or preference stability.<br>
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这五项决定使用哪个结果。单位容量、只问覆盖 $L$ 时，进入 [[Hall 定理|Hall condition]]；要实际增大 matching 时，进入 [[增广路定义|augmenting path]]；右侧容量大于一时，考虑 [[整数容量的槽化与网络流|slots 或 flow]]。边有权重或双方有偏好时，Hall 单独不够，还要分别加入 [[Hall 条件与带权最优|weighted optimisation]] 或 [[稳定匹配与普通匹配|stable matching]] 的成功标准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These choices determine which result applies. For unit capacities and required coverage of $L$, use [[Hall 定理|Hall's condition]]. To enlarge a current matching, use [[增广路定义|augmenting paths]]. Right-side capacities above one suggest [[整数容量的槽化与网络流|slots or flow]]. With edge weights or two-sided preferences, Hall alone is insufficient: add the success criterion of [[Hall 条件与带权最优|weighted optimisation]] or [[稳定匹配与普通匹配|stable matching]], respectively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如“给学生分项目”仍不够具体。必须继续问：边是否只表示符合最低资格，是否还另有偏好排序？每个项目只收一人还是有多个名额？必须让每名学生都有项目，还是允许少数人落空？若多个可行方案存在，是追求最大覆盖、最低通勤成本，还是没有 blocking pair？每个答案都可能改变模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, “assign students to projects” remains underspecified. Does an edge encode only minimum eligibility, and is there a separate preference ordering? Does each project accept one student or several? Must every student receive a project? If several feasible assignments exist, should the model maximise coverage, minimise travel cost, or eliminate blocking pairs? Every answer can change the model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 看到一句“把工人分给任务”，为什么不应立刻套 Hall 定理？
>
> **答案：** 还不知道允许边、覆盖目标、容量与成功标准；Hall 只处理指定一侧在单位容量 allowed-edge graph 中的可行覆盖。
