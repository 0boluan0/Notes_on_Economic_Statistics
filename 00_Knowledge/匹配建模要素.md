# Specifying a matching model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*匹配建模要素*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Assign students to projects” does not yet define a mathematical problem. It leaves open who is eligible, who must receive an assignment, how many students each project can accept, and what makes one valid assignment better than another. A useful specification connects five decisions: the two roles, allowed edges, required coverage, capacities, and the success criterion. Follow one small assignment through these decisions before choosing a theorem or algorithm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“给学生分项目”还不是一个完整数学问题：哪些人符合资格、谁必须得到分配、每个项目能接收几人，以及两份合法分配怎样比较，都尚未说明。一份可用说明需要把五项决定连起来：两类角色、允许边、覆盖要求、容量和成功标准。先用一个小分配例逐项落实，再选择定理或算法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Start with roles and allowed pairs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先确定角色与允许配对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the students be $L=\{a,b,c\}$ and the projects $R=\{P,Q,R_0\}$. The symbol $R$ names the entire right side, while $R_0$ names one project. Treat these as distinct role-tagged objects even if a real person could play both roles in another application. Use a finite simple undirected [[二分图配对关系|eligibility model]]: an edge joins a student to a project exactly when that assignment is permitted. The [[二分图|bipartition]] is supplied by the roles, not inferred from where the points are drawn.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设学生为 $L=\{a,b,c\}$，项目为 $R=\{P,Q,R_0\}$。$R$ 表示整个右侧，而 $R_0$ 是一个项目的名字。各对象按角色区分，即使在别的应用中同一真人可能扮演两种角色，也应分别建模。采用有限简单无向的[[二分图配对关系|资格模型]]：学生与项目之间有边，当且仅当允许这项分配。[[二分图|二分划分]]由角色给定，不由图画位置推断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the permitted pairs and travel costs are as follows. Each cost measures the same unit, so adding them has a stated meaning. A missing pair is forbidden, not a free assignment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设允许配对及通勤成本如下。各成本使用同一种单位，因此相加有明确含义。没有列出的配对不允许，而不是免费分配。

| Allowed pair · 允许配对 | Cost · 成本 |
|---|---:|
| $aP$ | $4$ |
| $aQ$ | $1$ |
| $bP$ | $3$ |
| $bQ$ | $2$ |
| $cR_0$ | $1$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $aQ$ means the undirected edge $\{a,Q\}$. The eligibility rule could be “the student meets the project's prerequisites.” It must be the same rule for all five edges. The number $1$ on $aQ$ says that this allowed assignment costs one unit; it does not by itself specify either participant's preference ranking.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$aQ$ 表示无向边 $\{a,Q\}$。资格规则可以是“学生满足项目的先修要求”，五条边必须使用同一种规则。$aQ$ 上的数值 $1$ 表示这项允许分配成本为一个单位，并没有自动给出任何一方的偏好排名。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Decide who must be covered and how often
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*决定谁必须被覆盖、每人能参与几次*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Require every student to receive exactly one project, and initially let each project accept at most one student. A [[匹配定义|matching]] already enforces at most one selected edge at every vertex. Requiring [[左侧覆盖与完美匹配|coverage of the left side]] $L$ changes the left-side “at most once” to “exactly once.” Projects may be unused as a modeling rule; in this particular example, equal finite side sizes mean that any left-covering matching also uses all three projects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要求每名学生恰好得到一个项目，先假设每个项目至多接收一名学生。[[匹配定义|匹配]]本身保证每个顶点至多参与一条选中边；再要求[[左侧覆盖与完美匹配|覆盖左侧]] $L$，就把学生侧的“至多一次”变成“恰好一次”。建模规则允许闲置项目；但本例两侧有限且等大，所以任何覆盖左侧的匹配恰好也会用完三个项目。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two complete feasible assignments are $M_1=\{aP,bQ,cR_0\}$ and $M_2=\{aQ,bP,cR_0\}$. Student $c$ must use $R_0$; then $a,b$ must split $P,Q$ in one of the two possible ways. Selecting $aQ,bQ,cR_0$ would cover every student but violate the one-place capacity at $Q$. Thus coverage alone does not establish feasibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两份完整可行分配是 $M_1=\{aP,bQ,cR_0\}$ 与 $M_2=\{aQ,bP,cR_0\}$。学生 $c$ 必须使用 $R_0$，于是 $a,b$ 只能以两种方式分用 $P,Q$。若选 $aQ,bQ,cR_0$，虽然覆盖每名学生，却违反 $Q$ 只有一个名额的容量限制。因此，覆盖全部学生还不足以说明方案可行。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now change one assumption: project $Q$ has two interchangeable places, while each student still needs one place in exactly one project. Then $aQ,bQ,cR_0$ becomes a valid capacitated assignment. It is not an ordinary matching on the original graph, because two selected edges share $Q$. The [[整数容量的槽化与网络流|unit-slot or flow representation]] expresses that changed capacity explicitly. It requires that each assignment consumes one interchangeable unit; differing workloads, special slot requirements or interactions between selected students need additional constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在只改一个假设：项目 $Q$ 有两个可互换名额，每名学生仍需在一个项目中占一个名额。此时 $aQ,bQ,cR_0$ 成为合法的容量分配，但它不是原图上的普通匹配，因为两条选中边共享 $Q$。[[整数容量的槽化与网络流|单位槽或流的表示]]可以显式表达这个新容量；其前提是每次分配消耗一个可互换单位。若工作量不同、名额有独立要求，或被选学生之间存在联动，就需另外加入约束。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Specify what counts as success
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*明确什么才算成功*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to unit capacity. If success only means that a full student assignment exists, either $M_1$ or $M_2$ is enough. If success means minimum total travel cost among full student assignments, add the edge costs:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回到每个项目一个名额的情形。如果成功只指存在一份完整学生分配，$M_1,M_2$ 任一份都够。如果成功要求在完整分配中使通勤总成本最低，就需累加边成本：

$$
C(M_1)=4+2+1=7,\qquad C(M_2)=1+3+1=5.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are exactly these two full assignments, so $M_2$ is the unique cheapest one. [[Hall 条件与带权最优|Hall feasibility does not make this comparison]]. If the requirement to cover all students is silently dropped while these positive costs are minimized, the empty assignment costs zero and wins instead. It solves a different problem. Under the earlier two-place capacity at $Q$, the new full assignment $aQ,bQ,cR_0$ costs $4$; changing capacity can change the feasible choices as well as the optimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完整分配恰好只有这两种，所以 $M_2$ 是唯一最低成本解。[[Hall 条件与带权最优|Hall 可行性并没有完成这一比较]]。若悄悄删掉覆盖全部学生的要求，再最小化这些正成本，空分配以零成本胜出，但那已经是另一个问题。在前面 $Q$ 有两个名额的变体中，新完整分配 $aQ,bQ,cR_0$ 的成本为 $4$；改变容量既可能改变可行选择，也可能改变最优解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If full coverage cannot be required, one possible rule is to maximize the number of assigned students first, then minimize cost among all assignments reaching that number. State this priority explicitly. For instance, deleting $cR_0$ from the original instance makes full coverage impossible but still allows two students to be assigned. A [[极大与最大匹配|maximum-cardinality]] solution to that relaxed task is not a solution to the original all-students requirement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果不能硬性要求全覆盖，可以明确规定：先使已分配人数最大，再在达到该人数的全部方案中最小化成本。例如从原例删掉 $cR_0$ 后，全覆盖不可能，但仍可分配两名学生。这个放宽任务的[[极大与最大匹配|最大基数]]解，并不是原先“所有学生都有项目”的解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If participants can reject an assignment in favor of one another, separate preference lists and a [[稳定匹配与普通匹配|stability requirement]] are needed. In the course's equal-side, complete strict-preference model, a blocking pair consists of two people not matched together who both prefer each other to their current partners. Eliminating such a pair is not the same as minimizing a sum of travel costs. Do not assume stability from the cost table or use Hall alone to certify it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若参与者可能为了彼此而拒绝当前分配，就需要另外的偏好列表和[[稳定匹配与普通匹配|稳定性要求]]。在课程的两侧等大、偏好完整且严格的模型中，阻挡对是当前没有配在一起、但双方都更喜欢彼此而非现任伙伴的一对人。排除这种情形与最小化通勤成本之和不同；不能从成本表推断稳定，也不能只用 Hall 来认证它。

> [!note] A complete problem statement · 一份完整问题说明
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the original instance: choose only listed student–project edges; assign every student exactly once; use every project at most once; minimize the sum of the listed costs. Roles identify the objects, the edge rule identifies permitted pairs, coverage and capacities define feasible choices, and the objective ranks those choices.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对原例，可以完整写成：只选表中允许的学生—项目边；每名学生恰好一次；每个项目至多一次；使所列成本总和最小。角色确定对象，边规则确定允许配对，覆盖与容量确定可行选择，目标再比较这些选择。

## Choose a tool only after the model is fixed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*模型确定后再选工具*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite bipartite graph with unit capacities and required left coverage, [[Hall 定理|Hall's theorem]] gives an existence test through every left subset's neighbors. To increase a current ordinary matching, [[增广路定义|augmenting paths]] explain a valid reassignment. Nonunit interchangeable capacities lead to the separate slot/flow model; weights require an optimization objective, and preferences require the stated stability convention. These tools answer different parts of the specification rather than repairing missing assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限二分图、单位容量且要求覆盖左侧的任务，[[Hall 定理]]通过每个左侧子集的邻点提供存在判据。要增大当前普通匹配，[[增广路定义|增广路]]解释怎样合法重配。非单位且可互换的容量进入独立的槽／流模型；权重需要优化目标，偏好需要明确的稳定性约定。不同工具回答说明中的不同问题，不能替代尚未写清的假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before solving “assign workers to tasks,” be able to identify the objects, explain one present and one absent edge, name the required coverage and capacity limits, and say why one feasible output would count as better than another—or state that any feasible output is enough. Empty sides and isolated objects are legitimate model data: their consequences depend on which objects must be served.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看到“把工人分给任务”时，应先能指出各对象、解释一条存在与一条不存在的边、写出覆盖和容量限制，并说明两份可行输出怎样比较，或者明确任意可行输出都够。空侧与孤立对象也可以是合法输入，它们造成什么后果，取决于哪些对象必须得到服务。
