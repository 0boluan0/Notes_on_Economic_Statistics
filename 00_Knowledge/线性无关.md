# Linear independence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*线性无关*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A list of vectors is **linearly independent** when none of its vectors can be made from the others by scaling and adding. Each contributes something the remaining list cannot supply. The important consequence is uniqueness: whenever a target can be made from an independent list, there is only one choice of coefficients that makes it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一组向量**线性无关**，表示其中没有一个能靠其余向量数乘、相加拼出来；每个向量都提供了其余向量无法替代的部分。一个重要后果是表示唯一：只要某个目标能由这组无关向量拼出，就只有一组系数能拼出它。

> [!example] Can the contributions cancel? · 是否存在非平凡的抵消？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the real [[向量空间|vector space]] $\mathbb R^2$, take $v_1=(1,0)$ and $v_2=(1,1)$. A linear combination multiplies each whole vector by its own scalar coefficient. Asking whether they can cancel gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在实[[向量空间]] $\mathbb R^2$ 中，取 $v_1=(1,0)$、$v_2=(1,1)$。线性组合给每个完整向量乘上自己的标量系数，再相加。检查它们能否抵消，就是求
>
> $$c_1v_1+c_2v_2=(c_1+c_2,c_2)=(0,0).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The second coordinate forces $c_2=0$, then the first forces $c_1=0$. There is no cancellation except switching off both contributions, so the list is independent. For the standard pair $(1,0),(0,1)$, the same test is simply $(c_1,c_2)=(0,0)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第二坐标迫使 $c_2=0$，第一坐标再迫使 $c_1=0$。除了把两份贡献都关掉，没有别的抵消方式，因此这组向量无关。标准向量 $(1,0),(0,1)$ 的同一检查则直接是 $(c_1,c_2)=(0,0)$。

> [!abstract] The zero-combination test · 零组合判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For vectors $v_1,\ldots,v_k$ in a vector space over the scalar field $\mathbb F$, the list is linearly independent when
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对标量域 $\mathbb F$ 上向量空间中的 $v_1,\ldots,v_k$，线性无关指
>
> $$c_1v_1+\cdots+c_kv_k=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad c_1=\cdots=c_k=0,$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> for coefficients in $\mathbb F$. If even one solution has coefficients **not all zero**, the list is linearly dependent. “Not all zero” means at least one coefficient is nonzero; it does not require every coefficient to be nonzero. The $0$ on the left is the zero vector, while each coefficient zero is a scalar.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 其中系数取自 $\mathbb F$。只要存在一组**不全为零**的系数满足等式，这组向量就线性相关。“不全为零”是至少一个系数非零，不是要求每一个都非零。左边的 $0$ 是零向量，每个系数中的零则是标量。

## 直觉与矩阵语言
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why this test controls unique coefficients*

Suppose the same target has two representations, $b=\sum_j a_jv_j=\sum_j c_jv_j$. Subtract them to get $\sum_j(a_j-c_j)v_j=0$. If the list is independent, the zero-combination test forces every $a_j-c_j=0$, so the representations coincide. Conversely, a nonzero coefficient relation for zero gives two representations of zero: that relation and the all-zero one. This is why the test concerns zero even when the target of interest is not zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假如同一目标有两套表示 $b=\sum_j a_jv_j=\sum_j c_jv_j$，相减得到 $\sum_j(a_j-c_j)v_j=0$。如果这组向量无关，零组合判据迫使每个 $a_j-c_j=0$，所以两套表示完全相同。反过来，如果存在一组非零系数关系生成零，零就已有两套表示：这一组和全零系数。这说明了为什么即使目标不是零，也可以靠检查零来判断唯一性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For coordinate vectors, put $v_1,\ldots,v_k$ into the columns of a matrix $A$. The [[Ax的列组合解释|column-combination rule]] turns the test into $Ac=0$, where $c=(c_1,\ldots,c_k)^T$ contains the coefficients. The [[零空间|nullspace]] $N(A)$ collects all such coefficient solutions. The columns are independent exactly when $N(A)=\{0\}$: the only solution is the zero coefficient vector. This does not mean that the nullspace is empty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于坐标向量，把 $v_1,\ldots,v_k$ 放入矩阵 $A$ 的列。[[Ax的列组合解释|列组合规则]]把判据写成 $Ac=0$，其中 $c=(c_1,\ldots,c_k)^T$ 存放系数。[[零空间]] $N(A)$ 收集所有这样的系数解。列向量无关，当且仅当 $N(A)=\{0\}$，即只有零系数向量这个解；这不是说零空间为空。

## 最小例子与边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Dependence, zero vectors and coverage*

The list $(1,0),(2,0)$ is dependent: $2(1,0)-(2,0)=0$, using coefficients $2,-1$. For a longer list, dependence can involve several vectors at once. The list $(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)$ has no two parallel members, yet the first two add to the third. Checking only pairs is therefore insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$(1,0),(2,0)$ 相关，因为 $2(1,0)-(2,0)=0$，系数是 $2,-1$。较长的向量组还可能由多个向量共同产生关系：$(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)$ 中没有两个互为倍数，但前两个相加就是第三个，因此仅检查每一对不够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Any nonempty list containing the zero vector is dependent: put coefficient $1$ on zero and $0$ on the others. By convention, the empty list is independent because it has no nontrivial coefficient relation. Independence also depends on the scalar field: $1,i$ in $\mathbb C$ are independent over $\mathbb R$, but dependent over $\mathbb C$ because $i\cdot1-1\cdot i=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任何包含零向量的非空向量组都相关：给零向量系数 $1$，其余系数全取 $0$ 即可。按约定，空向量组无关，因为它不存在非平凡系数关系。无关性也依赖标量域：$\mathbb C$ 中的 $1,i$ 在 $\mathbb R$ 上无关，在 $\mathbb C$ 上却有 $i\cdot1-1\cdot i=0$ 这条非平凡关系。

> [!warning] Uniqueness applies only to reachable targets · 唯一性只针对能表示的目标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The vectors $(1,0,0),(0,1,0)$ are independent, but they cannot produce $(0,0,1)$. Independence gives at most one representation; [[张成|spanning]] asks whether all vectors of a chosen target space have any representation. A [[基|basis]] requires both.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $(1,0,0),(0,1,0)$ 无关，却无法拼出 $(0,0,1)$。无关性只保证至多一种表示；[[张成]]检查指定目标空间的每个向量是否都存在表示。[[基]]需要同时满足这两点。
