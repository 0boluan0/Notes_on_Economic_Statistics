# Zero directions of a positive-semidefinite form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*半正定二次型的零方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero value of a [[二次型|quadratic form]] is only one scalar equation. A zero output from a matrix is a whole vector equation. For a [[半正定矩阵|positive-semidefinite matrix]], these two tests identify exactly the same inputs. This tells us when a direction with no quadratic cost is actually erased by the matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[二次型]]的值为零，只是一个标量等式；矩阵的输出为零，却是一整个向量等式。对[[半正定矩阵]]，这两项检查恰好找出同一批输入。它说明：一个不产生二次代价的方向，什么时候确实会被矩阵抹去。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the real symmetric matrix below. For the column $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], multiplication gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看下面的实对称矩阵。对列向量 $x=(x_1,x_2)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，相乘得到

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad x^TAx=(x_1-x_2)^2,\qquad Ax=\begin{pmatrix}x_1-x_2\\-x_1+x_2\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The quadratic value is nonnegative and is zero exactly when $x_1=x_2$. The output vector is zero under that same condition. Thus $(1,1)^T$, and every multiple of it, belongs to the [[零空间|nullspace]] $N(A)=\{x:Ax=0\}$. The zero vector belongs too; a “zero direction” usually refers to a nonzero member.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二次型非负，且恰在 $x_1=x_2$ 时为零。输出向量也恰在同一个条件下为零。因此 $(1,1)^T$ 及其所有倍数都属于[[零空间]] $N(A)=\{x:Ax=0\}$。零向量也在其中；说“零方向”时通常指其中的非零向量。

> [!important] The equivalence · 核心等价
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $n\ge1$ be finite. If $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ is symmetric and positive semidefinite, then for every real column $x$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $n\ge1$ 为有限整数。若 $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ 实对称且半正定，那么对每个实列向量 $x$，
>
> $$x^TAx=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad Ax=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a complex [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]] positive-semidefinite matrix, the equivalent statement is $z^*Az=0\iff Az=0$ for all $z\in\mathbb C^n$. The star means conjugate transpose: transpose and conjugate each entry. In either field, semidefiniteness is inclusive; a positive-definite matrix simply has no nonzero zero direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对复 [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]] 半正定矩阵，相应结论是：所有 $z\in\mathbb C^n$ 都满足 $z^*Az=0\iff Az=0$。星号表示共轭转置，即转置并对元素取共轭。两种数域中都采用宽义半定；正定矩阵只是没有非零零方向。

## A zero sum cannot hide a positive spectral term
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*非负谱项之和为零意味着什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[正定与半正定的谱判据|spectral criterion]] writes a real symmetric positive-semidefinite matrix as $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$. The columns of $Q$ are orthonormal eigenvectors, so $Q^TQ=I$, where $I$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]; $\Lambda$ is a [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] with eigenvalues $\lambda_j\ge0$. Changing coordinates to $y=Q^Tx$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[正定与半正定的谱判据]]把实对称半正定矩阵写成 $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$。$Q$ 的列是标准正交特征向量，故 $Q^TQ=I$，其中 $I$ 为[[单位矩阵]]；$\Lambda$ 是[[对角矩阵]]，其对角元是非负特征值 $\lambda_j$。改用坐标 $y=Q^Tx$，得到

$$x^TAx=y^T\Lambda y=\sum_{j=1}^n\lambda_j y_j^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every summand is nonnegative. If their sum is zero, each summand must be zero: whenever $\lambda_j>0$, we must have $y_j=0$. If $\lambda_j=0$, the product $\lambda_jy_j$ already vanishes. Hence $\Lambda y=0$ and $Ax=Q\Lambda y=0$. Conversely, $Ax=0$ immediately gives $x^TAx=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每一项都非负。总和为零时，每项都必须为零：只要 $\lambda_j>0$，就必有 $y_j=0$；若 $\lambda_j=0$，乘积 $\lambda_jy_j$ 本来就为零。因此 $\Lambda y=0$，从而 $Ax=Q\Lambda y=0$。反过来，$Ax=0$ 立即推出 $x^TAx=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For Hermitian $A$, use $A=Q\Lambda Q^*$ and $y=Q^*z$ instead. The summands become $\lambda_j|y_j|^2$; their nonnegativity gives the same argument. This is a statement about nonnegative spectral terms. In the original coordinates, expanded cross terms may still cancel, as they do in the opening square.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 Hermitian 矩阵，改用 $A=Q\Lambda Q^*$、$y=Q^*z$，各项变成 $\lambda_j|y_j|^2$，同样依靠非负性完成证明。这里说的是非负的谱坐标项；在原坐标中展开交叉项，仍然可能发生抵消，开头的平方就是这样。

> [!note] The square-root viewpoint · 平方根视角
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[半正定主平方根|positive-semidefinite square root]] $B=A^{1/2}$ satisfies $B^*=B$ and $B^2=A$. Therefore $z^*Az=(Bz)^*(Bz)=\|Bz\|_2^2$. Here $\|Bz\|_2^2=\sum_j|(Bz)_j|^2$ is the sum of squared coordinate magnitudes. This squared length is zero exactly when $Bz=0$, which implies $Az=B(Bz)=0$. This gives another proof; it does not require $A$ to be invertible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[半正定主平方根]] $B=A^{1/2}$ 满足 $B^*=B$、$B^2=A$，因此 $z^*Az=(Bz)^*(Bz)=\|Bz\|_2^2$。其中 $\|Bz\|_2^2=\sum_j|(Bz)_j|^2$ 是各坐标模长的平方和。这个长度平方为零恰好意味着 $Bz=0$，继而 $Az=B(Bz)=0$。这是另一种证明，不要求 $A$ 可逆。

## Where the assumption matters
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*半定前提在哪里起作用*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $D=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ and $x=(1,1)^T$, we have $x^TDx=1-1=0$, but $Dx=(1,-1)^T\ne0$. Here positive and negative spectral terms cancel. The implication $Ax=0\Rightarrow x^TAx=0$ holds for every real square matrix, but the reverse implication needs additional structure. The theorem above supplies it through symmetry and semidefiniteness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $D=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ 与 $x=(1,1)^T$，则 $x^TDx=1-1=0$，但 $Dx=(1,-1)^T\ne0$。这里确实是正负谱项抵消。$Ax=0\Rightarrow x^TAx=0$ 对任意实方阵都成立，反向却需要额外结构；上面的定理用对称性和半定性提供了这种结构。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An immediate application is the [[正定与半正定可逆性|invertibility boundary]]: if $A\succeq0$ and $N(A)=\{0\}$, no nonzero vector can have zero quadratic value. Negative values are already excluded, so every nonzero value is strictly positive and $A$ is positive definite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个直接应用是[[正定与半正定可逆性|可逆性的边界]]：若 $A\succeq0$ 且 $N(A)=\{0\}$，非零向量就不可能给出零二次型值。负值已被半正定排除，因此每个非零输入都严格取正值，$A$ 就正定。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, Appendix A.5.2, printed pp.646–647 / PDF pp.660–661](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=660): symmetric spectral coordinates, nonnegative eigenvalues and the positive-semidefinite square root. The zero-direction implication is derived above from these facts.
- [Axler, Linear Algebra Done Right, fourth edition, §7C, results 7.34–7.38, PDF pp.265–266](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=265): real and complex positive operators, orthonormal diagonal coordinates and positive square roots. Axler uses “positive” for the nonstrict convention. Both examples are verified directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Boyd 与 Vandenberghe，附录A.5.2，印刷646–647／PDF660–661页：核对谱坐标、非负特征值与半正定平方根；零方向结论由这些事实按正文推导。
- Axler第4版§7C，7.34–7.38，PDF265–266页：核对实/复正算子、标准正交对角表示和正平方根；其“positive”采用非严格约定。两个例子另行直接计算。
