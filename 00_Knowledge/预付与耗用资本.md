# 预付资本衡量投入生产的资本总额而耗用资本衡量本期被消耗的部分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Advanced capital measures the capital committed to production; consumed capital measures the portion used up in the period.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

生产可能需要一次预付整台机器的价值，本期产品却只承担它本期耗损所转移的价值。预付总资本用于说明投入生产的资本规模，本期耗用资本用于说明产品中需要补偿的成本部分；有[[固定与流动资本|跨多轮使用的固定资本]]时，两者尤其不能混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Production may require advancing a machine's full value while the current product carries only the value transferred through its current wear. Total advanced capital measures the capital committed to production; consumed capital identifies the cost portion replaced in the current product. The distinction is especially important with [[固定与流动资本|fixed capital used across several rounds]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造一个只有一轮可变资本周转的期间：期初机器价值100，本期耗损10；原料预付20并全部耗用；劳动力预付10并在该期使用，产生剩余价值10。所有数值采用同一价值单位，无价格重估。于是，预付不变资本 $c_a=120$、预付可变资本 $v_a=10$，总预付为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Construct a period containing one turnover of variable capital. A machine starts at 100 and loses ten through wear; materials advanced at 20 are fully consumed; labour power advanced at ten is used during the period and generates surplus value of ten. Values share one unit and there is no revaluation. Advanced constant capital is $c_a=120$, advanced variable capital $v_a=10$, and the total advance is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C=c_a+v_a=120+10=130.
$$

本期耗用不变资本为 $c_u=10+20=30$。在本例单轮条件下，[[产品价值分解|产品价值]]为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Constant capital consumed in the period is $c_u=10+20=30$. With the example's single turnover, [[产品价值分解|product value]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
W=c_u+v+s=30+10+10=50,
\qquad k=c_u+v=40.
$$

其中 $v=10$ 是活劳动创造的新价值中补回劳动力预付的部分，不能解释为工资把旧价值转移给产品。生产后机器尚存价值90，加上产品价值50，总计140，比原预付130多出剩余10。因此，在利润等于该剩余的当前分析条件下，[[Marx利润率|利润率]]为 $10/130$；$10/40$ 则以本期成本为分母，回答另一问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $v=10$ is the portion of newly created value replacing the labour-power advance, not old value transferred by wages. The machine's remaining 90 plus product value of 50 totals 140, exceeding the original advance of 130 by the surplus of ten. Under the present assumption that profit equals that surplus, the [[Marx利润率|profit rate]] is $10/130$. The ratio $10/40$ instead uses current cost as its denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
