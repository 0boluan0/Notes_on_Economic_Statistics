# Mean-square convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*均方收敛 · $L^2$ 收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To judge a random approximation, we can square its error and average that squared error over all outcomes, using their probabilities. **Mean-square convergence** says that this average tends to zero as the approximation index increases. Squaring makes a large error expensive, even if it happens rarely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评价一个随机近似时，可以先把误差平方，再按各个结果的概率计算平均。**均方收敛**说的是：随着近似的索引增加，这个平均平方误差趋于零。平方会放大大误差的影响，即使这种误差很少发生。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X_n$ be the approximation and $X$ the target. Both are [[随机变量|random variables]] on the same [[概率空间|probability space]], so their difference compares the same outcome. The [[期望|expectation]] $E[(X_n-X)^2]$ averages squared error, rather than squaring the average error. Positive and negative errors therefore cannot cancel. If the target is a fixed parameter, this is the usual [[均方误差|mean squared error (MSE)]] of an estimator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $X_n$ 为近似、$X$ 为目标，两者是同一[[概率空间]]上的[[随机变量]]，以便比较同一个结果。[[期望]] $E[(X_n-X)^2]$ 是先平方误差再平均，而不是先平均误差再平方，所以正负误差不会互相抵消。目标若是固定参数，这就是估计量通常的[[均方误差|均方误差（MSE）]]。

> [!important] Convergence in $L^2$ · 在平方可积空间中收敛
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In this card, $X_n$ and $X$ are all **square-integrable**: $E[X_n^2]<\infty$ for every $n$ and $E[X^2]<\infty$. We write $X_n\xrightarrow{L^2}X$ when
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$E[(X_n-X)^2]\longrightarrow0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The symbol $L^2$ denotes the space of square-integrable random variables, identifying variables that differ only on a probability-zero set. The associated distance is $\|X_n-X\|_2=\sqrt{E[(X_n-X)^2]}$, the root mean squared error; it tends to zero exactly when its square does.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 本卡要求每个 $X_n$ 和 $X$ 都**平方可积**：$E[X_n^2]$ 与 $E[X^2]$ 有限；上式成立时记为 $X_n\xrightarrow{L^2}X$。$L^2$ 表示平方可积随机变量组成的空间，把仅在零概率集合上不同的变量视为同一个元素。相应距离为 $\|X_n-X\|_2=\sqrt{E[(X_n-X)^2]}$，也就是均方根误差；它和它的平方同时趋于零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These [[矩存在性的使用边界|finite second-moment conditions]] matter. They ensure the squared difference is integrable, since $(X_n-X)^2\le2X_n^2+2X^2$. No independence is needed, and $X$ may be random. Some uses of “mean-square error tends to zero” assume only the difference has a finite second moment; this card uses the stricter, standard **convergence inside $L^2$** convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些[[矩存在性的使用边界|有限二阶矩条件]]有实际作用：由 $(X_n-X)^2\le2X_n^2+2X^2$，可知平方误差可积。定义不要求独立，目标 $X$ 也可以随机。有些表述只要求差值具有有限二阶矩，就说“均方误差趋于零”；本卡使用更明确的**在 $L^2$ 空间内收敛**约定。

## An error whose scale shrinks
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Z$ take $-2$ and $2$ with equal probability. Its mean is zero, but its mean square is four. Put $X_n=Z/\sqrt n$ and take target zero. Direct calculation gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $Z$ 等概率取 $-2$ 和 $2$。它的均值为零，但平方的均值为四。取 $X_n=Z/\sqrt n$，目标为零，直接计算得到

$$E[(X_n-0)^2]=\tfrac12\left(\frac{-2}{\sqrt n}\right)^2+\tfrac12\left(\frac2{\sqrt n}\right)^2=\frac4n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $X_n\xrightarrow{L^2}0$, with distance $2/\sqrt n$. At $n=100$, the MSE is $0.04$ and the distance is $0.2$; these are different quantities. More generally, for any $Z$ with $E[Z^2]<\infty$, the same scaling gives $E[(Z/\sqrt n)^2]=E[Z^2]/n\to0$. By contrast, the unscaled sequence $Y_n=Z$ has zero mean at every step but MSE four, so it does not converge in mean square to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 $X_n\xrightarrow{L^2}0$，距离为 $2/\sqrt n$。在 $n=100$ 时，MSE 为 $0.04$，距离为 $0.2$，二者并非同一个量。更一般地，对任意满足 $E[Z^2]<\infty$ 的 $Z$，同样有 $E[(Z/\sqrt n)^2]=E[Z^2]/n\to0$。反过来，不缩放的序列 $Y_n=Z$ 虽然每一步均值为零，MSE 却始终为四，因此不均方收敛到零。

> [!warning] Rare errors can still carry a large square · 罕见误差仍可贡献很大的平方
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Draw one $U$ [[连续均匀分布|uniformly on $[0,1]$]] and set $Y_n=n\mathbf1\{U\le1/n^2\}$, using an [[事件指示变量|indicator]]. The nonzero value occurs with probability $1/n^2$, but its square is $n^2$, so $E[Y_n^2]=1$ for every $n$. This sequence approaches zero [[依概率收敛|in probability]] and even [[几乎必然收敛|almost surely]], yet not in mean square. A rare event's probability and its error size must be considered together.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 只抽一次[[连续均匀分布|均匀分布在 $[0,1]$]] 上的 $U$，用[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]定义 $Y_n=n\mathbf1\{U\le1/n^2\}$。非零值的概率为 $1/n^2$，但其平方是 $n^2$，所以每个 $n$ 都有 $E[Y_n^2]=1$。这个序列依概率、甚至几乎必然收敛到零，却不均方收敛到零。必须同时看罕见事件的概率和误差大小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The forward guarantee is useful: [[均方收敛推出概率收敛|mean-square convergence implies convergence in probability]] by applying Markov's inequality to squared error. That separate card gives the proof and the boundary of the converse. Mean-square convergence itself is an average-error statement, rather than a promise about the eventual behaviour of every sample path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正向保证很有用：把 Markov 不等式用于平方误差，可得[[均方收敛推出概率收敛]]；证明和反向的边界在对应卡片中展开。均方收敛本身是平均误差的陈述，而不是对每条样本路径最终行为的承诺。
