# 结构性预算余额是在周期调整基础上进一步剔除明确界定的一次性及其他暂时性因素的余额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The structural balance extends cyclical adjustment by removing explicitly identified one-off and other temporary influences.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结构性预算余额（SB）在[[周期调整预算余额]]的基础上，对一次性项目及选定的非标准暂时因素继续调整，以便观察较持续的财政状况。不同机构可能对商品价格、资产价格和一次性项目采用不同方法，因此应同时给出调整项与计算规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The structural balance (SB) extends the [[周期调整预算余额|cyclically adjusted balance]] to examine more persistent fiscal conditions. Methods differ in their treatment of commodity prices, asset prices and one-off items; adjustments and conventions must be disclosed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一金额口径下，设 $CB$ 为周期成分，$O$ 为一次性项目对余额的净贡献，$X$ 为其他明确列示且未重复计入的暂时性贡献，则一种透明的分解写法是：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a common monetary basis, let $CB$ denote the cyclical component, $O$ the net contribution of one-offs, and $X$ other explicitly identified temporary contributions without double-counting. A transparent decomposition is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
SB=OB-CB-O-X=CAB-O-X.
$$

例如，观测余额 −4、已估计周期成分 −2、净一次性收入贡献 +1，且无其他调整，则 CAB=−2、SB=−3。这是独立的加总例；实际估计时，通常先识别一次性项目，再计算相应税基的周期反应，不能把一次性收入也无差别乘上普通收入弹性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the observed balance is −4, its estimated cyclical component is −2 and net one-off revenue contributes +1, with no other adjustments, CAB=−2 and SB=−3. This is an additive illustration. In estimation, one-offs should be identified before applying the relevant cyclical adjustment rather than applying ordinary revenue elasticities indiscriminately to them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“只发生一次”不足以自动排除一项政策：临时刺激措施或大型资本支出可能正是需要分析的政策选择。应解释排除标准，并在不确定时展示调整前后结果；指标的解释边界见[[结构性余额的识别边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An item is not automatically excluded merely because it occurs once: temporary stimulus or a large capital project may be the policy choice under analysis. Explain exclusion criteria and show alternative treatments when uncertain; see [[结构性余额的识别边界|interpretation limits]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
