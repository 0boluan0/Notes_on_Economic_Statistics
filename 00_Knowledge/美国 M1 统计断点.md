# 美国 2020 年 M1 重定义造成统计断点而不是等额经济增量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The 2020 U.S. M1 redefinition created a statistical break, not an equal economic increase*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子诊断
> Federal Reserve 在 2020 年取消储蓄存款每月六次转账限制后，把储蓄存款与其他支票类存款合并为 other liquid deposits，并从 2020 年 5 月观察值起纳入 M1。这个重分类使 M1 出现结构性断点，而 M2 总量不因此改变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After the Federal Reserve removed the six-per-month transfer limit on savings deposits in 2020, it combined savings deposits with other checkable deposits as other liquid deposits and included them in M1 beginning with the May 2020 observation. This reclassification created a structural break in M1 while leaving M2 unchanged for that reason.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Federal Reserve Technical Q&A 记录：纳入时全美存款机构业的储蓄存款约为 11.2 万亿美元，地域为美国，口径为行业储蓄存款，观察起点为 2020 年 5 月。这个数字说明断点规模，不表示 5 月发生了同额贷款、支付或净财富创造。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Federal Reserve Technical Q&A records approximately USD 11.2 trillion of savings deposits across the U.S. depository-institution industry at inclusion, with the series break beginning in May 2020. That figure describes the scale of the reclassification; it does not mean that an equal amount of lending, payment, or net wealth was created in May.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 2020 年 4 月与 5 月的 M1 水平不能当作同一不变口径直接计算经济增量。
- M1 跳升而 M2 不变，正是“同一资产从 M2 的非 M1 部分转入 M1”的诊断信号。
- FRED M1SL 明确标注 2020 年 5 月前用 OCD、之后用 other liquid deposits；画图和回归必须处理断点。
- Regulation D 删除的是联邦六次转账限制；它不强迫每家银行取消自设的产品限制，也不表示每个账户在经济上立即变成无条件的活期账户。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- April and May 2020 M1 levels should not be treated as observations under an unchanged definition.
- A jump in M1 with M2 unchanged is the diagnostic signature of the same assets moving from non-M1 M2 into M1.
- FRED M1SL explicitly uses OCDs before May 2020 and other liquid deposits from May 2020; charts and regressions must handle the break.
- Regulation D removed the federal six-transfer limit; it did not compel every bank to remove its own product restrictions or make every savings account economically identical to an unrestricted checking account overnight.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若 2020 年 5 月 M1 巨幅上升而 M2 没有因这项变更上升，最先应检查什么？
>
> **答案：** 检查统计定义和系列断点，而不是先把 M1 跳升解释为等额新货币或新支出。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If M1 jumps in May 2020 while M2 does not rise because of that change, what should be checked first?
>
> **Answer:** Check the statistical definition and series break before interpreting the M1 jump as equal new money or spending.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Federal Reserve Board, H.6 Technical Q&As](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h6/h6_technical_qa.htm)：核对 2020 年监管变化、2020 年 5 月断点、约 11.2 万亿美元储蓄存款与 M2 不变。
- [Federal Reserve Board, Regulation D amendment, 24 April 2020](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/bcreg20200424a.htm)：核对删除的是联邦层面的六次便利转账限制，银行仍可保留自设限制。
- [FRED, M1SL series notes](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/M1SL) 与 [FRED, M2SL series notes](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/M2SL)：核对两条序列在 2020 年 5 月前后的构造。

- The FRED M1SL and M2SL notes were checked for the construction on each side of the break.
