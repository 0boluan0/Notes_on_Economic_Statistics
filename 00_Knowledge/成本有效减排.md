# 给定总减排目标时，成本有效分担使可调整企业的边际减排成本相等
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For a given total abatement target, a cost-effective allocation equalises marginal abatement costs across firms that can adjust at the margin.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

成本有效减排回答“既定减排目标由谁完成才最省资源”。如果两家都还能调整减排量，而一家下一单位减排更便宜，就把少量任务从高成本者转给低成本者；总减排不变，成本下降。直到没有这种可行的节省方向，才达到给定目标下的最低成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cost-effective abatement asks who should meet a fixed target to minimise resource use. If two firms can adjust and one can abate the next unit more cheaply, shift a small amount of abatement to it. Total abatement stays unchanged while cost falls. A least-cost allocation leaves no feasible saving of this kind.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定企业集合、时期和同一种污染物的可比减排单位。令[[产量、排放与减排|减排量]]为 $a_i\in[0,\bar a_i]$，真实资源成本 $C_i(a_i)$ 连续可微、凸且非递减，成本可分别加总、没有遗漏的治理外部效应。给定可行总目标 $A$，问题是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the firms, period and comparable abatement units for one pollutant. Let [[产量、排放与减排|abatement]] satisfy $a_i\in[0,\bar a_i]$. Real resource costs are differentiable, convex, nondecreasing and additive, without omitted external effects of abatement. For a feasible total target $A$, solve
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\min_{\{a_i\}}\sum_i C_i(a_i)
\quad\text{s.t.}\quad
\sum_i a_i=A,\qquad 0\le a_i\le\bar a_i.
$$

内点企业满足 $C_i'(a_i^*)=\lambda$；达到零减排或容量上限的企业不能被强行代入同一个等式。完整的边际条件是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interior firms satisfy $C_i'(a_i^*)=\lambda$. Firms at zero or at capacity need not satisfy that equality. The complete marginal conditions are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
C_i'(a_i^*)&=\lambda &&(0<a_i^*<\bar a_i),\\
C_i'(0)&\ge\lambda &&(a_i^*=0),\\
C_i'(\bar a_i)&\le\lambda &&(a_i^*=\bar a_i).
\end{aligned}
$$

这些条件连同可行性，是该凸问题的最优判别；[[KKT条件]]与[[KKT充分性]]负责一般数学原理。严格凸时分担唯一；离散技术或非凸成本不能仅凭“令导数相等”宣告全局最低成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Together with feasibility, these conditions characterise an optimum of this convex problem. [[KKT条件|KKT conditions]] and [[KKT充分性|convex sufficiency]] provide the general mathematics. Strict convexity makes the allocation unique. Discrete technologies or nonconvex costs require more than equating derivatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自造例：两家容量均为100，$C_1=a_1^2/2$、$C_2=3a_2^2/2$，目标 $A=100$。由 $a_1=3a_2$ 与 $a_1+a_2=100$ 得 $(75,25)$，总成本为 $2812.5+937.5=3750$。各减50时成本为 $1250+3750=5000$；重新分担节省1250。统一分担并非必然差，差别取决于它是否满足正确的边际条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For capacities of one hundred each, take $C_1=a_1^2/2$, $C_2=3a_2^2/2$ and total abatement one hundred. Equal marginal costs give $(75,25)$ and total cost $3,750$. Equal abatement $(50,50)$ costs $5,000$, so reassignment saves $1,250$. Uniform assignments are not inherently inferior; the test is whether the marginal conditions hold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里没有选择 $A$ 本身：见[[成本有效与社会最优]]。同吨减排若对不同地点的损害不同，还要先检查[[排放总量与局地损害|环境效果是否真的可比]]，不能用不变总吨数替代给定的局地质量目标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This does not choose $A$ itself; see [[成本有效与社会最优|cost-effectiveness versus social optimality]]. If equal-ton reductions affect different places differently, check [[排放总量与局地损害|whether environmental effects are comparable]]. A constant tonnage is not a substitute for a specified local-quality target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
