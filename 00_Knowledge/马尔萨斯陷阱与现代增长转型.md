# 马尔萨斯陷阱与现代增长转型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Malthusian Trap and the Transition to Modern Growth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释为什么前现代经济的技术进步常先提高人口而不是持久提高人均收入，以及这条反馈为何在现代增长中被打破。
> **具体锚点：** 若土地固定，新农业技术先让每人收入上升，较低死亡率或较高生育率随后增加人口，人均土地下降，收入又被拉回。
> **核心难点：** “技术进步”和“人均持续增长”在这个机制中不是同一件事；必须同时追踪人口对收入的反应。
> **为什么重要：** 它给出了理解工业革命前长期生活水平、现代人口转型与人力资本投资的共同起点。
> **继续：** 看生育选择、人口红利和老龄化，进入 [[人口转型、生育选择与老龄化]]；看资本与技术的现代基准，进入 [[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It explains why technological progress in premodern economies often raised population rather than permanently raising income per person, and why modern growth broke that feedback.
> **Concrete anchor:** With fixed land, a new agricultural technique first raises income per person. Lower mortality or higher fertility then raises population, land per person falls, and income is pulled back down.
> **Central difficulty:** Technological progress and sustained per-capita growth are not the same outcome in this mechanism. Population's response to income must be tracked explicitly.
> **Why it matters:** It gives a common starting point for understanding preindustrial living standards, the modern demographic transition, and human-capital investment.
> **Continue:** For fertility choice, the demographic dividend, and aging, see [[人口转型、生育选择与老龄化|Demographic Transition, Fertility Choice, and Aging]]. For the modern capital-and-technology benchmark, see [[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛|The Solow Growth Model, Steady State, and Convergence]].

## 马尔萨斯反馈环
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Malthusian feedback loop*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

技术提高总产出，在土地/资源固定和递减收益下先提高人均收入；更高收入降低死亡或提高生育，人口增长再压低人均资源，形成接近生存水平的长期陷阱。该机制描述特定前现代条件，不是所有时代普遍定律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Technology raises total output and, with fixed land or resources and diminishing returns, initially raises income per person. Higher income lowers mortality or raises fertility; population growth then reduces resources per person and returns income toward subsistence. This mechanism describes particular premodern conditions rather than a universal law for every era.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个最小表达是 $Y=A F(T,N)$，其中土地 $T$ 固定、人口 $N$ 对收入 $y=Y/N$ 做净增长反应 $\dot N/N=n(y)$。若 $n(y^*)=0$，技术 $A$ 一次性上升会先使 $y>y^*$，但只要 $n'(y)>0$，人口便继续增长，直到 $y$ 靠近 $y^*$。长期结果更可能是更多人口，而非同比例更高的人均收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A minimal representation is $Y=A F(T,N)$, where land $T$ is fixed and population $N$ responds to income $y=Y/N$ through net growth $\dot N/N=n(y)$. If $n(y^*)=0$, a one-time rise in technology $A$ first makes $y>y^*$. But whenever $n'(y)>0$, population keeps growing until $y$ approaches $y^*$. The long-run result is more likely to be a larger population than a proportionally higher income per person.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 如何逃离陷阱
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How the trap is escaped*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

持续技术增长、人口反应变化、人力资本和制度可使生产率增长超过人口稀释。工业化还改变儿童的机会成本、城市生活和教育回报，生育数量—质量权衡发生变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sustained technological growth, a changed population response, human capital, and institutions can make productivity growth exceed population dilution. Industrialization also changes the opportunity cost of children, urban living, and returns to education, altering the quantity–quality trade-off in fertility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键不是某一年技术突然跳升，而是反馈环改变：知识可以持续累积，人力资本回报上升，养育和教育每个儿童的成本增加，人均收入上升不再必然转化为更高净生育。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The key is not a single year's technological jump but a change in the feedback loop: knowledge can accumulate continuously, returns to human capital rise, the cost of raising and educating each child increases, and higher income no longer necessarily becomes higher net fertility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 模型边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Model boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

马尔萨斯框架是机制模型，不是“所有社会都精确停在同一生存收入”的历史断言。制度、土地分配、城市化、战争和疾病会使生活水平在长期均值附近有很大差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Malthusian framework is a mechanism model, not a historical claim that every society sat at exactly the same subsistence income. Institutions, land distribution, urbanization, war, and disease can produce large differences around long-run averages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从原主题保留的全局定位
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Global orientation retained from the original topic*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **它解决什么：** 解释人口、资源、技术和家庭生育选择怎样共同塑造长期生活水平，并理解从高生高死到低生低死的转型。
> **具体锚点：** 在前现代经济中，技术提高收入后人口增长可能把人均收入拉回生存水平；现代人力资本和生育成本改变了这条反馈。
> **核心难点：** 人口既是劳动力也是被抚养者；短期抚养负担、人口红利和长期老龄化方向不同。
> **为什么重要：** 发展、迁移、教育、财政和增长政策都受人口年龄结构约束。
> **继续：** 先读 Malthus 反馈，再看 demographic transition 与家庭选择；劳动力流动见 [[Lewis—Fei–Ranis 二元经济模型|二元经济与劳动力迁移]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It explains how population, resources, technology, and household fertility choices jointly shape long-run living standards and the transition from high birth and death rates to low ones.
> **Concrete anchor:** In a premodern economy, population growth may pull income per person back toward subsistence after technology raises income. Modern human capital and the cost of fertility changed this feedback.
> **Central difficulty:** Population consists of both workers and dependants; short-run dependency burdens, a demographic dividend, and long-run aging move in different directions.
> **Why it matters:** Development, migration, education, fiscal policy, and growth policy are constrained by population age structure.
> **Continue:** Begin with the Malthusian feedback, then examine demographic transition and household choice. For labor movement, see [[Lewis—Fei–Ranis 二元经济模型|Dual Economy and Labor Migration]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把一次技术提升直接当成人均收入永久提升，未追踪人口反应。
- 把前现代机制当成现代所有国家的精确预测。
- 只说“技术打破陷阱”，不说为什么技术能持续增长且生育反应会变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Treating a one-time technological gain as a permanent rise in income per person without tracing population response.
- Applying a premodern mechanism as an exact forecast for every modern country.
- Saying only that “technology broke the trap” without explaining why technology became sustained and fertility responses changed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 马尔萨斯陷阱中技术进步为何未必长期提高人均收入？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why might technological progress fail to raise income per person permanently in a Malthusian trap?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 短期收入提高诱发人口增长，在固定资源和递减收益下稀释人均产出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The short-run income gain induces population growth, which dilutes output per person when resources are fixed and returns diminish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明“逃离陷阱”改变了哪个反馈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words which feedback changes when an economy escapes the trap.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 生产率开始持续增长，而收入上升不再持续转成更高净生育；教育和儿童质量投资吸收更多家庭资源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Productivity begins to grow persistently, while rising income no longer translates continuously into higher net fertility; education and child-quality investment absorb more household resources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 什么证据会使简单马尔萨斯模型不适合某个时期？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What evidence would make the simple Malthusian model inappropriate for a period?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 例如人均收入持续增长而净生育下降，土地不再是主要稀缺要素，或技术与人力资本的持续积累主导生产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Examples include sustained growth in income per person alongside falling net fertility, land no longer being the dominant scarce input, or persistent accumulation of technology and human capital driving production.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
