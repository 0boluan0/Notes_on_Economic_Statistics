# 一个点沿每个方向的方向导数都存在，仍不足以推出函数在该点多元可微，因为这些方向导数未必组成同一个线性映射
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The existence of directional derivatives in every direction is insufficient for multivariable differentiability because those rates need not form one linear map*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 逐条直线都有极限，不等于存在统一线性近似
> [[方向导数]]每次固定一条直线，再取得一个一元极限。多元可微要求更强：所有这些方向上的一阶变化必须同时来自同一个线性映射 $Df(a)$，而且对一般扰动还要有统一的小余项。即使每个 $D_vf(a)$ 都存在，只要 $v\mapsto D_vf(a)$ 不是线性的，就不可能是 [[全微分]]。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A directional derivative fixes one line at a time. Differentiability requires all first-order directional behaviour to come from one linear map, together with a uniform remainder estimate for general perturbations. If $v\mapsto D_vf(a)$ is not linear, no total differential can generate those rates.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反例：每个方向都有导数，但方向映射不是线性的
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Counterexample: every direction has a derivative but the direction map is nonlinear*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义 $g(0,0)=0$，并在 $(x,y)\ne(0,0)$ 时令
$$
g(x,y)=\frac{x^3}{x^2+y^2}.
$$
对任意非零 $v=(v_1,v_2)$ 和 $t\ne0$，
$$
\frac{g(tv)-g(0)}{t}
=\frac{v_1^3}{v_1^2+v_2^2}.
$$
右边与 $t$ 无关，所以每个方向导数都存在。然而
$$
D_{(1,0)}g(0)=1,
\qquad
D_{(0,1)}g(0)=0,
\qquad
D_{(1,1)}g(0)=\frac12.
$$
若这些数来自线性映射 $L$，就应有 $L(1,1)=L(1,0)+L(0,1)=1$，与 $1/2$ 矛盾。因此 $g$ 在原点不可微。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The difference quotient is constant in $t$, so every directional derivative exists. But the resulting values violate additivity: a linear derivative would give $L(1,1)=L(1,0)+L(0,1)=1$, whereas the actual directional derivative is $1/2$. Hence no derivative linear map exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它比“偏导不推出可微”更强在哪里
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why this boundary is stronger than the partial-derivative boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[偏导不可推可微]] 只说明有限条坐标线不够。更强的结论是：即使把**每一条穿过该点的直线**都检查完，仍可能缺少跨方向的线性兼容性和对非直线路径的统一余项控制。方向数量更多，并不会自动把逐条极限变成一个多元导数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The partial-derivative boundary shows that finitely many coordinate lines are insufficient. This stronger example checks every line through the point and still fails, because the rates are not linearly compatible and do not provide a uniform remainder for general approaches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若每个 $D_vf(a)$ 都存在，要想让它们可能来自全微分，映射 $v\mapsto D_vf(a)$ 至少必须满足哪两条线性性质？满足它们是否已经自动证明统一余项条件？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If every $D_vf(a)$ exists, what two linearity properties must the map $v\mapsto D_vf(a)$ satisfy before it can possibly come from a total differential? Do those properties alone prove the uniform remainder condition?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 至少要满足可加性和齐次性。即使满足线性，也还需验证对一般 $h\to0$ 的余项是 $o(\|h\|)$；逐方向极限本身不提供这种统一控制。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** At minimum, it must satisfy additivity and homogeneity. Even if it is linear, one must still verify that the remainder is $o(\|h\|)$ for general $h\to0$; directional limits alone do not provide that uniform control.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
