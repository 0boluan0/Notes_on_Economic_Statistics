# Directional derivatives without differentiability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方向导数存在不保证可微*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[方向导数|directional derivative]] follows one fixed straight line through a point and measures the output's first-order rate along that line. Checking every line gives more information than checking the coordinate axes, but a [[全微分|total differential]] must approximate all nearby inputs with one linear rule. There are two possible gaps: the directional rates may fail to form a linear map; even when they do, the approximation may fail along curved approaches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[方向导数]]沿一条固定的直线穿过某点，测量输出沿这条线的一阶变化率。检查每一条直线，比只检查坐标轴得到的信息更多；但[[全微分]]必须用同一个线性规则近似附近所有输入。这里有两层缺口：各方向变化率可能无法组成线性映射；即使组成了线性映射，近似也可能沿曲线趋近时失败。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work with a real-valued $f$ on an [[开集|open set]] $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, at $a\in U$, where $n\ge2$ is a finite integer. For each fixed vector $v$, use the **two-sided** convention
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实值函数 $f$ 定义在[[开集]] $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 上，$a\in U$，维数 $n\ge2$ 为有限整数。对每个固定向量 $v$，使用**双侧**约定

$$D_vf(a)=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{f(a+tv)-f(a)}t.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The vector need not have length $1$: this is change per unit of the line parameter $t$. The zero vector gives the constant path and derivative $0$. All directional derivatives claimed below are finite and exist from both signs of $t$. Differentiability would require a [[线性映射|linear map]] $L$, satisfying $L(u+v)=L(u)+L(v)$ and $L(cv)=cL(v)$ for every real $c$, with
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

向量不必长为 $1$：这里测量的是直线参数 $t$ 每单位变化带来的输出变化。零向量给出常值路径，导数为 $0$。以下声称存在的方向导数，都有限且从 $t$ 的正负两侧取得同一极限。可微则要求有[[线性映射]] $L$，对任意实数 $c$ 满足 $L(u+v)=L(u)+L(v)$、$L(cv)=cL(v)$，并且

$$f(a+h)-f(a)=L(h)+r(h),\qquad
\frac{|r(h)|}{\|h\|_2}\longrightarrow0\quad(h\to0,\ h\ne0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $h$ is an arbitrary displacement and $\|h\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_i h_i^2}$ is its [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]]. Substituting $h=tv$ into this estimate forces $D_vf(a)=L(v)$. The following examples show why that necessary equality is not a sufficient test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $h$ 是任意位移，$\|h\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_i h_i^2}$ 是其[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]。在余项估计中代入 $h=tv$，就必有 $D_vf(a)=L(v)$。下面的例子说明，这条必要等式为什么还不是充分判据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Every line can have a rate that fails additivity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反例：每个方向都有导数，但方向映射不是线性的

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set $g(0,0)=0$ and, elsewhere in $\mathbb R^2$, let
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $g(0,0)=0$，并在 $\mathbb R^2$ 的其余点定义

$$g(x,y)=\frac{x^3}{x^2+y^2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This function is even [[连续性|continuous]] at the origin, because $|g(x,y)|\le|x|\le\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\to0$. For a nonzero vector $v=(v_1,v_2)$, substituting $(x,y)=(tv_1,tv_2)$ cancels the powers of $t$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个函数在原点甚至[[连续性|连续]]，因为 $|g(x,y)|\le|x|\le\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\to0$。对非零向量 $v=(v_1,v_2)$，代入 $(x,y)=(tv_1,tv_2)$ 后约去 $t$ 的幂，得到

$$\frac{g(tv)-g(0,0)}t
=\frac{v_1^3}{v_1^2+v_2^2}\qquad(t\ne0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The quotient is independent of $t$, so its two-sided limit exists for every such vector. But the following three values cannot come from one linear map:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

差商与 $t$ 无关，所以每个这样的向量都给出存在的双侧极限。但以下三个值无法来自同一个线性映射：

$$D_{(1,0)}g(0)=1,\qquad D_{(0,1)}g(0)=0,\qquad
D_{(1,1)}g(0)=\frac12.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $(1,1)=(1,0)+(0,1)$, additivity would require the third value to be $1+0=1$. It is $1/2$ instead. Thus $g$ is not differentiable at the origin, despite continuity and directional derivatives in all directions. Using nonunit vectors here is intentional and follows the stated parameter convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $(1,1)=(1,0)+(0,1)$，可加性应当使第三个值等于 $1+0=1$，实际却为 $1/2$。因此，即使连续且所有方向导数都存在，$g$ 在原点仍不可微。这里有意使用非单位向量，遵循开头给出的参数变化率约定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Even a linear collection of rates can miss a curved approach
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方向变化率已经线性，仍可能漏掉曲线趋近*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now strengthen the test: suppose every directional derivative is zero. These rates certainly form the linear map $L=0$. Nevertheless, define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把检验加强：设所有方向导数都为零。这些变化率当然组成线性映射 $L=0$。但仍可定义如下反例：

$$q(0,0)=0,\qquad
q(x,y)=\frac{x^4y}{x^6+y^2}\quad((x,y)\ne(0,0)).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The denominator is positive away from the origin, so the function is defined on all of $\mathbb R^2$. The inequality $2|x^3y|\le x^6+y^2$, obtained from $(|x^3|-|y|)^2\ge0$, gives $|q(x,y)|\le|x|/2$. Hence $q$ is continuous at the origin as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分母在原点之外严格为正，所以函数定义在整个 $\mathbb R^2$ 上。由 $(|x^3|-|y|)^2\ge0$ 得 $2|x^3y|\le x^6+y^2$，进而有 $|q(x,y)|\le|x|/2$。因此 $q$ 在原点也连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix $v=(v_1,v_2)$. If $v_2=0$, the whole line has output zero. If $v_2\ne0$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $v=(v_1,v_2)$。若 $v_2=0$，整条直线上输出都为零；若 $v_2\ne0$，则

$$\frac{q(tv)-q(0,0)}t
=\frac{t^2v_1^4v_2}{t^4v_1^6+v_2^2}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So $D_vq(0)=0$ for every vector $v$, with the same candidate $L=0$. But choose the curved displacement $h(s)=(s,s^3)$, where the nonzero real parameter $s$ tends to zero. Direct substitution yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此每个向量 $v$ 都满足 $D_vq(0)=0$，候选映射同为 $L=0$。然而，取沿曲线变化的位移 $h(s)=(s,s^3)$，令非零实参数 $s\to0$。直接代入可得

$$q(s,s^3)=\frac{s}{2},\qquad
\frac{|q(s,s^3)-q(0,0)-L(s,s^3)|}{\|(s,s^3)\|_2}
=\frac1{2\sqrt{1+s^4}}\longrightarrow\frac12.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The relative error does not tend to zero, so no total differential exists. The coordinate rates already force any possible derivative to be $L=0$; choosing another linear map cannot repair the failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相对误差没有趋于零，所以全微分不存在。坐标方向的变化率已经强迫任何可能的导数都必须是 $L=0$；换一个线性映射无法补救这个失败。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## More directions do not change the order of the quantifiers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它比“偏导不推出可微”更强在哪里

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[偏导不可推可微|The partial-derivative boundary]] concerns finitely many coordinate lines. Here even every fixed line has been checked. In the first example the rates disagree with additivity. In the second they are perfectly linear, but the scale at which each line's error becomes small can depend on that line. Along $h(s)=(s,s^3)$ the direction changes with $s$, so it is not one of those fixed-line limits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[偏导不可推可微]]讨论的是有限条坐标线。本卡则连每条固定直线都检查过：第一个例子败在变化率不满足可加性；第二个例子的变化率完全线性，但每条线的误差要缩到多小才够小，可以依赖于那条线。沿 $h(s)=(s,s^3)$ 时，方向也随 $s$ 改变，因此不是其中某一条固定直线的极限。

> [!important] One threshold must control every small displacement · 一个阈值要控制全部小位移
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For differentiability, each error tolerance $\varepsilon>0$ must admit one $\delta>0$ such that $|r(h)|<\varepsilon\|h\|_2$ for **all** $0<\|h\|_2<\delta$. A separate limit for each fixed direction does not supply this one threshold. The second example fails it even though every line rate equals the same linear map.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 可微要求：对每个误差容许量 $\varepsilon>0$，都有同一个 $\delta>0$，使**所有** $0<\|h\|_2<\delta$ 都满足 $|r(h)|<\varepsilon\|h\|_2$。每个固定方向分别存在一个极限，并不提供这个统一阈值。第二个例子中，虽然每条线的变化率都等于同一线性映射，这个要求仍然失败。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Real homogeneity of existing two-sided directional derivatives follows by replacing $t$ with $ct$ for $c\ne0$, and by the constant path for $c=0$. Additivity does not follow this way. Checking both properties is necessary before calling the rates a linear derivative, but the small-remainder condition still has to be proved separately. Both counterexamples already occur in two real dimensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于存在的双侧方向导数，实数齐次性可以由 $c\ne0$ 时换参数 $t\mapsto ct$、以及 $c=0$ 时的常值路径得到；可加性却不能这样自动推出。要把这些变化率称为线性导数，必须满足这两条性质，但还要另外证明小余项条件。上述两种反例都已经发生在二维实空间中。
