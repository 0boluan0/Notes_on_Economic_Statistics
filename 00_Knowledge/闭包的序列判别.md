# Sequential characterization of closure
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*闭包的序列判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A point can be approachable from a set without belonging to it. On the usual real line, the numbers $a_n=1/(n+1)$, for $n=1,2,3,\ldots$, all lie in $A=(0,1)$ and approach $0$. The missing endpoint $0$ belongs to the [[集合闭包|closure]] of $A$: every open neighbourhood of $0$ meets $A$. In a metric space, these two ways of describing approach agree. A closure point can always be reached as the limit of a sequence of points from the set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个点可以被集合内的点逼近，却不属于原集合。在通常实线上，$a_n=1/(n+1)$（$n=1,2,3,\ldots$）的每一项都在 $A=(0,1)$ 内，并趋向 $0$。缺少的端点 $0$ 属于 $A$ 的[[集合闭包|闭包]]：它的每个开邻域都碰到 $A$。在度量空间中，这两种“逼近”描述一致：每个闭包点都能成为集合内某条序列的极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $(X,d)$ be a [[度量空间|metric space]], where $X$ is the ambient set and $d(a,x)$ measures distance. The open ball $B_r(x)=\{y\in X:d(y,x)<r\}$ consists of points less than distance $r>0$ from $x$. [[序列收敛|Convergence]] $a_n\to x$ means that for every chosen radius $\varepsilon>0$, there is an index $N$ after which **all** terms satisfy $d(a_n,x)<\varepsilon$. An open neighbourhood is an [[开集|open set]] containing the point; in a metric space every such neighbourhood contains an open ball around it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $(X,d)$ 为[[度量空间]]，其中 $X$ 是环境集合，$d(a,x)$ 衡量两点距离。开球 $B_r(x)=\{y\in X:d(y,x)<r\}$ 包含距 $x$ 小于 $r>0$ 的点。[[序列收敛|收敛]] $a_n\to x$ 的意思是：对每个选定的半径 $\varepsilon>0$，都存在一个下标 $N$，使此后**所有**项满足 $d(a_n,x)<\varepsilon$。开邻域就是包含该点的[[开集]]；度量空间中，每个开邻域都包含以该点为中心的某个开球。

> [!abstract] The sequence criterion · 序列判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A\subseteq X$ and $x\in X$ in a metric space,
> $$x\in\overline A\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\text{there is a sequence }(a_n)_{n\ge1}\text{ with every }a_n\in A\text{ and }a_n\to x.$$
> Here $\overline A$ is the closure in $X$. Terms may repeat. If $x\in A$, the constant sequence $a_n=x$ works; distinct terms are not required. If $A=\varnothing$, no such sequence exists and its closure is empty.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对度量空间中的 $A\subseteq X$ 与 $x\in X$，上式把“属于闭包”与“存在一条各项都来自 $A$ 的收敛序列”对应起来。$\overline A$ 是在 $X$ 中的闭包。序列允许重复取值；若 $x\in A$，常数序列 $a_n=x$ 就可以，不必找互不相同的点。若 $A=\varnothing$，不存在这样的序列，闭包也为空。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the two directions work
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两个方向为什么成立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First suppose all $a_n$ lie in $A$ and $a_n\to x$. Every open neighbourhood $U$ of $x$ eventually contains every term, so in particular it contains at least one point of $A$. Thus $U\cap A\ne\varnothing$, exactly the closure test. This direction uses only open neighbourhoods and remains valid in any [[拓扑|topological space]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先假设所有 $a_n$ 都属于 $A$，且 $a_n\to x$。$x$ 的每个开邻域 $U$ 最终都包含序列的全部后续项，因此至少包含一个 $A$ 中的点。于是 $U\cap A\ne\varnothing$，这正是闭包判据。这个方向只用了开邻域，在任意[[拓扑|拓扑空间]]中都成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the reverse direction, suppose $x\in\overline A$. Every ball $B_{1/n}(x)$ meets $A$, so choose one point from each nonempty intersection:
$$a_n\in A\cap B_{1/n}(x),\qquad d(a_n,x)<\frac1n.$$
Given any $\varepsilon>0$, choose an integer $N>1/\varepsilon$. Then for every $n\ge N$,
$$d(a_n,x)<\frac1n\le\frac1N<\varepsilon.$$
This proves convergence of the whole sequence. The choices need not follow a straight line or have steadily decreasing distances; the shrinking upper bound is enough.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，假设 $x\in\overline A$。每个球 $B_{1/n}(x)$ 都与 $A$ 相交，因此可以从每个非空交集中选一个点，得到上式。给定任意 $\varepsilon>0$，取整数 $N>1/\varepsilon$，则对全部 $n\ge N$ 都有 $d(a_n,x)<1/n\le1/N<\varepsilon$。这证明整条序列收敛。选出的点不必沿直线排列，距离也不必逐项减小；有这个不断缩小的上界就足够。

> [!example] “Eventually” depends on the neighbourhood · “最终”取决于所选邻域
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $a_n=1/(n+1)$, every term with $n\ge10$ lies in $B_{0.1}(0)$, and every term with $n\ge100$ lies in $B_{0.01}(0)$. Earlier terms may lie outside either ball. Convergence requires a whole tail inside each fixed neighbourhood, not just occasional visits, and it does not require one index that works for every radius at once.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $a_n=1/(n+1)$，$n\ge10$ 的全部项都落在 $B_{0.1}(0)$ 内，$n\ge100$ 的全部项都落在 $B_{0.01}(0)$ 内。早期项可以在这些球外。收敛要求对每个固定邻域都有一整段尾部留在其中，不只是偶尔进入；也不要求存在一个同时适用于所有半径的下标。

## How far the criterion extends
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*这一判据可以推广到哪里*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The crucial ingredient is a countable list of increasingly restrictive neighbourhoods. A space is called **first-countable** if, at each point $x$, one can list open neighbourhoods $U_1,U_2,\ldots$ such that every open neighbourhood of $x$ contains at least one $U_j$. Replace that list by
$$V_n=U_1\cap\cdots\cap U_n.$$
These are still open neighbourhoods, and they are nested: $V_{n+1}\subseteq V_n$. If $x\in\overline A$, choose $a_n\in A\cap V_n$. Once $U_j$ is contained in a given neighbourhood $U$, all $n\ge j$ give $a_n\in V_n\subseteq U_j\subseteq U$. Hence the same criterion holds in every first-countable space. Metric spaces supply such a list through the $1/n$ balls. First-countability is a sufficient setting here; it is not claimed to be necessary for every use of sequences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键是有一列可数的、约束越来越强的邻域。若每个点 $x$ 都有一列开邻域 $U_1,U_2,\ldots$，使它的每个开邻域都包含某个 $U_j$，就称这个空间满足**第一可数性**。把这列集合换成 $V_n=U_1\cap\cdots\cap U_n$，它们仍是开邻域，并满足 $V_{n+1}\subseteq V_n$。若 $x\in\overline A$，选 $a_n\in A\cap V_n$。对给定邻域 $U$，找到 $U_j\subseteq U$ 后，全部 $n\ge j$ 都满足 $a_n\in V_n\subseteq U_j\subseteq U$，所以仍得到收敛。度量空间的 $1/n$ 球恰好提供这样的邻域列。这里给出的是一个充分适用范围，并未声称每种序列判别都必须要求第一可数性。

## A topology where sequences miss closure points
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*序列探测不到所有闭包点的拓扑*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see why a scope restriction matters, keep the underlying set $X=\mathbb R$ but change its open sets. Declare $\varnothing$ open, and declare $U\ne\varnothing$ open when $\mathbb R\setminus U$ is **countable**: finite or listable as a sequence. This is the **co-countable topology**. It satisfies the topology axioms: a nonempty union has a complement contained in the countable complement of any one nonempty member. For a finite intersection of nonempty open sets, the complement is a finite union of countable sets. If a member is empty, the intersection is empty and therefore open. The empty set and the whole space are included as required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了看清适用范围的必要性，仍取底层集合 $X=\mathbb R$，但改变“开集”的规定：空集是开集；非空 $U$ 是开集，当且仅当 $\mathbb R\setminus U$ **可数**，即有限，或可以用一条序列列出。这称为**可数补拓扑**。它满足拓扑公理：非空并集的补集包含于其中任意一个非空成员的可数补集。对有限个非空开集取交，其补集是有限个可数集的并；若其中有空集，交集为空，仍是开集。空集和整个空间也都包括在内。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the same underlying subset $A=(0,1)$. A nonempty open interval contains uncountably many real numbers, so deleting a countable set cannot remove all of $A$. Every nonempty open set therefore meets $A$, and its closure is now all of $\mathbb R$, including $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再取同一个底层子集 $A=(0,1)$。非空开实区间含有不可数多个点，删除一个可数集不能把它全部删空。因此每个非空开集都与 $A$ 相交，这次闭包是整个 $\mathbb R$，其中也包括 $2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Yet no sequence from $A$ converges to $2$ in this topology. More generally, if a sequence $(a_n)$ converges to $x$, form the countable set $C=\{a_n:a_n\ne x\}$ of all its values different from $x$. The set $U=\mathbb R\setminus C$ is an open neighbourhood of $x$ whose only possible sequence value is $x$ itself. Convergence forces $a_n=x$ for all sufficiently large $n$. A sequence with all terms in $A$ therefore cannot converge outside $A$. Closure can contain more than sequential limits; the corresponding consequence for testing closed sets is explained in [[闭集的序列判别|the sequence criterion for closed sets]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

但在这个拓扑中，没有来自 $A$ 的序列能收敛到 $2$。更一般地，若 $(a_n)$ 收敛到 $x$，把它所有不等于 $x$ 的取值收集为可数集 $C=\{a_n:a_n\ne x\}$。$U=\mathbb R\setminus C$ 是 $x$ 的开邻域，序列能在其中取到的值只可能是 $x$ 本身。收敛便迫使充分靠后的所有项都等于 $x$。因此各项都在 $A$ 中的序列不能收敛到 $A$ 外。闭包可能比所有序列极限组成的集合更大；这对检验闭集的影响见[[闭集的序列判别]]。
