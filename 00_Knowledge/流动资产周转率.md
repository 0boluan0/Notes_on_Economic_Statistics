# 流动资产周转率是营业收入与相应流动资产存量之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Current asset turnover is revenue divided by the corresponding current-asset stock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

流动资产周转次数为 $S/CA$，衡量一元流动资产占用支持的营业收入；通常用期间平均流动资产，CPA本章示例约定使用期末数。与[[流动比率]]不同，它的分子是销售流量而非资产，分母也不是负债。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Current asset turnover $S/CA$ measures revenue supported per unit of current assets. Average assets are generally used, while CPA's examples use ending balances by convention. Unlike the [[流动比率|current ratio]], it uses sales rather than assets in the numerator and does not use liabilities in the denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ABC营业收入3000、期末流动资产700，次数4.2857。若期间365天，折算天数 $365\times700/3000\approx85.1667$，流动资产/收入比为23.3333%。这是同一比率的倒数换算，不是保证85天把全部流动资产变现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ABC's revenue of 3,000 and ending current assets of 700 give turnover of 4.2857, implied days of 85.1667 for a 365-day year, and current assets/revenue of 23.3333%. These are reciprocal expressions, not a guarantee of converting every current asset into cash in 85 days.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

周转上升可来自收入增长或资产占用下降，需再看现金、应收和库存；不能在资产不足、季节性或会计重分类下只凭高低评分。零分母及口径统一见[[财务比率口径匹配]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Higher turnover can reflect sales growth or lower asset use; inspect cash, receivables, and inventory next. Underinvestment, seasonality, or reclassification can undermine a simple ranking. See [[财务比率口径匹配|basis matching]] for denominator and comparability checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
