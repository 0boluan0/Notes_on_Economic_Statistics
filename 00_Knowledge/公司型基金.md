# 公司型基金是以具有法人资格的投资公司组织资产并向投资者发行基金股份的基金形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A corporate fund organizes investments in a separate legal company and issues fund shares to investors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公司型基金把基金本身设为投资公司：公司作为独立法人组织并持有基金资产，投资者是该基金公司的股东，按股份类别、章程和法律享有相应权利。这里的基金公司是投资载体，不是替许多基金提供服务的基金管理公司；持有某基金股份不等于持有其管理人的股份。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A corporate fund incorporates the investment vehicle itself. The company is a separate legal person organizing and holding fund assets, while investors are its shareholders with rights determined by share class, constitutional documents, and law. This fund company is not the management company serving multiple funds; owning fund shares does not mean owning the manager.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产由托管人保管、投资由外聘管理人执行，并不会改变这一区别。以香港开放式基金型公司（OFC）为例，它有董事会和独立法人身份，须有符合当地资格要求的投资管理人，并须按制度安排基金资产托管。公司层面的治理职责、经理的投资执行和保管资产是不同角色。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Custody by a custodian and investment execution by an external manager do not alter that distinction. A Hong Kong open-ended fund company (OFC), for example, has a board and separate legal personality, must have a locally qualified investment manager, and has a prescribed custody arrangement. Corporate governance, investment execution, and asset safekeeping are different roles.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公司型描述法律组织形式，并不推出基金是[[封闭式基金|封闭式]]。香港 OFC 就提供“公司型且[[开放式基金|开放式]]”的例子。类似地，持有基金股份也不保证每类股东拥有相同表决权、可以直接指挥每笔交易或得到固定分红；权利应按具体文件核对。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Corporate form does not imply a [[封闭式基金|closed-end]] structure. Hong Kong OFCs provide an example that is both corporate and [[开放式基金|open-ended]]. Nor does fund share ownership guarantee identical voting rights across classes, direct control of every trade, or fixed dividends; the relevant documents determine those rights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与[[契约型基金]]对照时，先找“有没有作为基金载体的独立公司、投资者持有其股份吗”，再看申赎和交易安排。不要用“名称里有基金公司”作判断，因为两种基金都可能聘用依法设立的管理公司。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When comparing with a [[契约型基金|contractual fund]], first ask whether a separate fund company exists and whether investors own its shares, then examine dealing and trading arrangements. A fund manager's corporate name is not decisive: either form can appoint an incorporated management company.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
