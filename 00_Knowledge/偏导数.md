# Partial derivatives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*偏导数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **partial derivative** asks how an output changes when you vary one input and hold all the other inputs fixed. If a temperature map $T(x,y)$ gives the temperature at a location, $T_x$ measures the local change when moving in the $x$ direction while keeping $y$ unchanged. Moving in another direction is a different question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**偏导数**回答：只改变一个输入、把其余输入固定时，输出怎样变化？若温度图 $T(x,y)$ 给出位置处的温度，$T_x$ 测量保持 $y$ 不变、沿 $x$ 方向移动时的局部变化率；换一个移动方向，就是另一个问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The calculation uses the ordinary [[导数|derivative]], a limit of output change divided by input change. Fixing the other coordinates leaves a function of one variable, so the familiar derivative rules apply to that restricted function. The symbol $\partial$ in $\partial f/\partial x$ reminds us which input is being varied; $f_x$ is another notation for the same quantity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里仍然使用普通[[导数]]：输出增量除以输入增量，再取极限。固定其余坐标后，只剩一个变量，所以可以对这个一元函数使用熟悉的求导规则。$\partial f/\partial x$ 中的 $\partial$ 提醒我们指定了变化的输入；$f_x$ 是同一个量的另一种记号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Intuition and a minimal example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 直觉与最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $f(x,y)=x^2y$. At the point $(2,3)$, first hold $y=3$ fixed. The resulting function is $g(x)=3x^2$, so $g'(2)=12$. If instead we hold $x=2$ fixed, we get $h(y)=4y$, whose derivative at $y=3$ is $4$. In symbols,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $f(x,y)=x^2y$，考察点 $(2,3)$。先固定 $y=3$，得到 $g(x)=3x^2$，在 $x=2$ 处的导数是 $12$；若改为固定 $x=2$，得到 $h(y)=4y$，在 $y=3$ 处导数为 $4$。写成记号就是

$$f_x(x,y)=2xy,\qquad f_y(x,y)=x^2,\qquad f_x(2,3)=12,\quad f_y(2,3)=4.$$

> [!example] A small step is not a whole unit · 小步变化不是整单位变化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Keeping $y=3$, a step $h$ in $x$ changes the output by $f(2+h,3)-f(2,3)=12h+3h^2$. The derivative predicts the leading term $12h$. For $h=0.01$, this predicts $0.12$, while the exact change is $0.1203$. It does not claim that increasing $x$ by a full unit always adds exactly $12$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 保持 $y=3$，让 $x$ 增加 $h$，输出真实变化为 $f(2+h,3)-f(2,3)=12h+3h^2$。偏导数给出主导项 $12h$。取 $h=0.01$ 时，预测为 $0.12$，真实变化为 $0.1203$；这并不声称 $x$ 增加一整单位就总是恰好增加 $12$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Likewise, “hold $y$ fixed” means that $y$ is a constant during differentiation, not that it is set to zero. To differentiate $x^2y$ with respect to $x$, keep the factor $y$ and differentiate $x^2$. If $y$ changes together with $x$, you are differentiating a composite function; the [[多元链式法则|multivariable chain rule]] accounts for both changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“固定 $y$”是把它当作求导过程中的常数，不是把它设成零。对 $x^2y$ 关于 $x$ 求导，要保留系数 $y$，再对 $x^2$ 求导。若 $y$ 也随 $x$ 改变，就在求复合函数的导数；[[多元链式法则]]会把两种变化都计入。

## The difference quotient at a point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一点处的差商定义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume $f$ is defined on an [[开集|open set]] $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, so every point of $U$ has a small ball lying inside the domain. Choose $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_n)\in U$. The vector $e_i$ has a $1$ in coordinate $i$ and zeros elsewhere; adding $te_i$ changes only that coordinate by $t$. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f$ 定义在[[开集]] $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 上，即 $U$ 中每点周围都有一个小球完全落在定义域内。取 $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_n)\in U$。向量 $e_i$ 的第 $i$ 个坐标为 $1$、其余为零；加上 $te_i$，就只把第 $i$ 个坐标改变 $t$。定义

$$\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_i}(a)=\partial_i f(a)
=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{f(a+te_i)-f(a)}{t},$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
provided this two-sided limit exists as a finite real number. Both positive and negative small $t$ are allowed. For two inputs, the two quotients are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件是这个双侧极限存在且为有限实数；足够小的正、负 $t$ 都参与极限。对两个输入，两个差商分别是

$$f_x(a,b)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(a+h,b)-f(a,b)}h,\qquad
f_y(a,b)=\lim_{k\to0}\frac{f(a,b+k)-f(a,b)}k.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $(a,b)$ denotes the two coordinates of the chosen point; $h,k$ are input increments. The open-domain assumption keeps these nearby points available on both sides. A boundary point may instead require a one-sided derivative, which must be stated separately. A subscript does not guarantee existence: for $f(x,y)=|x|+y$ at the origin, $f_y=1$, but $f_x$ fails because $|h|/h$ has opposite one-sided limits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $(a,b)$ 表示选定点的两个坐标，$h,k$ 是输入增量。开定义域保证两侧的这些邻近点都可取；边界点可能只能用单侧导数，需要另行说明。写出下标并不保证导数存在：例如原点处的 $f(x,y)=|x|+y$ 有 $f_y=1$，但 $f_x$ 不存在，因为 $|h|/h$ 左右极限相反。

## Relation to directional derivatives and the total differential
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与方向导数和全微分的关系

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[方向导数|directional derivative]] follows a line whose vector may change several coordinates at once. Taking that vector to be $e_i$ recovers a partial derivative: $\partial_i f(a)=D_{e_i}f(a)$. A [[全微分|total differential]] asks for more: one linear rule that approximates the output change for every sufficiently small input perturbation, not just a selected coordinate line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[方向导数]]沿一条直线变化，其方向向量可以让几个坐标同时改变。把向量取成 $e_i$，就回到偏导数：$\partial_i f(a)=D_{e_i}f(a)$。[[全微分]]要求更多：用同一个线性规则近似每一种足够小的输入扰动所造成的输出变化，而不只检查一条坐标线。

> [!warning] Coordinate checks do not settle all nearby behavior · 坐标方向不能包办附近所有变化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $f$ is differentiable at $a$, its partial derivatives supply the coefficients of the total differential. The converse is false: [[偏导不可推可微|all partial derivatives may exist without continuity or differentiability]]. That card gives a counterexample and the useful stronger condition of nearby partial derivatives continuous at the point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $f$ 在 $a$ 可微，偏导数确实给出全微分的系数；反方向却不成立：[[偏导不可推可微|所有偏导都存在，仍可能既不连续也不可微]]。链接卡给出反例，并说明“偏导在邻域存在且在该点连续”这一常用的更强充分条件。
