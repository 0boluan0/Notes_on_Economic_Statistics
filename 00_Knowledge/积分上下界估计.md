# Bounding a definite integral
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*积分上下界估计*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An integral may be difficult to calculate exactly even when the function's size is easy to control. If a flow rate stays between $2$ and $3$ litres per minute for five minutes, the total lies between $10$ and $15$ litres. **Bounding an integral** uses this idea: accumulate certified lower and upper estimates to enclose the unknown total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

积分的精确值可能不好求，但函数的大小却容易控制。例如，流量在五分钟内始终介于每分钟 $2$ 与 $3$ 升之间，总水量就介于 $10$ 与 $15$ 升之间。**积分上下界估计**就是把有依据的下界与上界分别累积起来，夹住未知总量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f$ be a real-valued function with a [[定积分|Riemann integral]] on a finite interval $[a,b]$, where $a<b$. This integral is the limit of sums of height times width. Choose two functions $\ell$ and $u$ that are also integrable on that interval and satisfy $\ell(x)\le f(x)\le u(x)$ at **every** $x\in[a,b]$. The letter $\ell$ denotes the lower comparison function, and $u$ the upper one. [[积分保序性|Order preservation]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实值函数 $f$ 在有限区间 $[a,b]$ 上有[[定积分|黎曼积分]]，其中 $a<b$。这个积分是“高度乘宽度再相加”的极限。选取同样在整段可积的函数 $\ell,u$，使**每个** $x\in[a,b]$ 都满足 $\ell(x)\le f(x)\le u(x)$；字母 $\ell$ 表示下比较函数，$u$ 表示上比较函数。由[[积分保序性]]，

$$\int_a^b\ell(x)\,dx\le\int_a^b f(x)\,dx\le\int_a^b u(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The functions need not be positive. The argument compares signed contributions at the same input. In particular, constants $m\le f(x)\le M$ throughout the interval give the rectangle bounds
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些函数不必为正；比较的是同一输入处带符号的贡献。特别地，若常数 $m,M$ 在整段满足 $m\le f(x)\le M$，就得到矩形界

$$m(b-a)\le\int_a^b f(x)\,dx\le M(b-a).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The numbers $m,M$ may be any valid bounds; they need not be attained minimum and maximum values. If $f$ is continuous on the closed interval, the [[极值定理|extreme value theorem]] guarantees attained extrema that can serve as these bounds. Checking only a few sampled heights does not establish a bound between the samples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$m,M$ 可以是任何有效的界，不必恰好是取到的最小值、最大值。若 $f$ 在闭区间连续，[[极值定理]]保证能取到极值，可用来选界。只检查有限几个采样高度，并不能证明采样点之间也满足同样的界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Improve the bound by using local information
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*利用局部信息收紧估计*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $f(x)=1/(1+x)$ on $[0,4]$. As $x$ increases, its positive denominator increases, so its value decreases from $1$ to $1/5$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $[0,4]$ 上的 $f(x)=1/(1+x)$。随着 $x$ 增加，正的分母变大，函数值从 $1$ 减少到 $1/5$，所以

$$\frac45\le I:=\int_0^4\frac{dx}{1+x}\le4.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $I$ names the integral being estimated. Its exact value is $\ln5$, using the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]], but that value is not needed to obtain the bounds. We can do better by splitting at $x=1$: on $[0,1]$ the height lies between $1/2$ and $1$, and on $[1,4]$ it lies between $1/5$ and $1/2$. [[定积分区间可加性|Interval additivity]] lets us add the two estimates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $I$ 是待估积分的名字。用[[自然对数]]表示，其精确值是 $\ln5$，但求界不需要先知道这个值。在 $x=1$ 处分段可以进一步收紧：$[0,1]$ 上高度介于 $1/2$ 与 $1$，$[1,4]$ 上高度介于 $1/5$ 与 $1/2$。[[定积分区间可加性]]允许把两段估计相加。

> [!example] Two smaller rectangles per bound · 每个界分成两个矩形
> $$\underbrace{\frac12\cdot1}_{[0,1]}+\underbrace{\frac15\cdot3}_{[1,4]}
> \le I\le
> \underbrace{1\cdot1}_{[0,1]}+\underbrace{\frac12\cdot3}_{[1,4]},\qquad
> \frac{11}{10}\le\ln5\le\frac52.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The second piece has width $3$, not width $1$. These bounds improve both ends of the original interval $[4/5,4]$ without finding a new antiderivative.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第二段宽度是 $3$，不是 $1$。这两个界同时收紧了原来的 $[4/5,4]$，不必另求原函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, split $a=x_0<x_1<\cdots<x_N=b$ into $N$ pieces and certify constants $m_i\le f(x)\le M_i$ on each entire piece $[x_{i-1},x_i]$. With its width $\Delta x_i=x_i-x_{i-1}$, additivity gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，把区间分成 $a=x_0<x_1<\cdots<x_N=b$ 的 $N$ 段，在每个**整段** $[x_{i-1},x_i]$ 上确认常数界 $m_i\le f(x)\le M_i$。记段宽为 $\Delta x_i=x_i-x_{i-1}$，由可加性得到

$$\sum_{i=1}^N m_i\Delta x_i\le\int_a^bf(x)\,dx\le\sum_{i=1}^N M_i\Delta x_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When subdividing an existing piece, reusing its bounds keeps the estimate unchanged; replacing them with tighter valid bounds improves it or leaves it equal. Subdivision alone does not guarantee improvement if the new bounds are chosen more loosely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

细分原来的一段时，沿用原界会得到相同估计；改用更紧且有效的界，就会改善或保持原估计。若新界反而选得更松，单纯“多切几段”并不保证改善。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A bound can become the next comparison function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把已有下界变成下一步的比较函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]], $e^t\ge1$ when $t\ge0$. Fix $x\ge0$. Since $(e^t)'=e^t$, [[Newton-Leibniz公式|antiderivative evaluation]] and comparison give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[自然指数函数]]，$t\ge0$ 时有 $e^t\ge1$。固定 $x\ge0$，由于 $(e^t)'=e^t$，由[[Newton-Leibniz公式|原函数端点评价]]与比较可得

$$e^x-1=\int_0^xe^t\,dt\ge\int_0^x1\,dt=x.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We have now established $e^t\ge1+t$ for every $t\ge0$, so it is valid throughout the next integration interval $[0,x]$. Integrating this stronger bound gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就对每个 $t\ge0$ 建立了 $e^t\ge1+t$，因此它在下一次积分的整个区间 $[0,x]$ 都有效。积分这个更强的下界，得到

$$e^x-1\ge\int_0^x(1+t)\,dt=x+\frac{x^2}{2},\qquad e^x\ge1+x+\frac{x^2}{2}\quad(x\ge0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same idea estimates a net change: if $F$ is continuously differentiable on $[a,b]$ and $m\le F'(x)\le M$, then integrating its derivative yields $m(b-a)\le F(b)-F(a)\le M(b-a)$. Here the continuity assumption makes the derivative integrable and justifies antiderivative evaluation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样的方法可以估计净变化：若 $F$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上连续可微，且 $m\le F'(x)\le M$，积分导数就得到 $m(b-a)\le F(b)-F(a)\le M(b-a)$。这里的连续可微条件保证导数可积，并允许原函数端点评价。

> [!warning] Keep the direction of integration · 保留积分方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The estimates above use increasing bounds. Reversing them gives $-M(b-a)\le\int_b^a f\le-m(b-a)$; equal endpoints give zero. The exponential quadratic lower bound is false for some negative inputs: at $x=-1$, $e^{-1}\approx0.3679<1/2$. One must recheck the comparison and orientation on the actual interval. These are ordinary finite-interval Riemann integrals; unbounded functions or infinite endpoints require the separate limits in [[反常积分|improper integration]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上面的估计使用正向端点。反向后为 $-M(b-a)\le\int_b^a f\le-m(b-a)$；同端点积分为零。指数函数的二次下界对某些负输入不成立：$x=-1$ 时，$e^{-1}\approx0.3679<1/2$。必须在实际区间重新核对比较关系和积分方向。这里讨论普通有限区间黎曼积分；无界函数或无穷端点需要[[反常积分]]中的另一步极限处理。
