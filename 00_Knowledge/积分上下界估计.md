# 用容易积分的上下比较函数夹住目标积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bound a target integral between integrals of tractable comparison functions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<b$ 且目标函数 $f$ 在 $[a,b]$ 黎曼可积。先在**整个积分区间**找出可积函数 $\ell(x)\le f(x)\le u(x)$，再用[[积分保序性]]得到
$$\int_a^b\ell\le\int_a^bf\le\int_a^bu.$$
最简单的选择是常数界 $m\le f\le M$，这给出 $m(b-a)\le\int_a^bf\le M(b-a)$。上下界越贴近函数，结论通常越有用；只在采样点成立的界不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume the target function is Riemann integrable on the closed interval with increasing bounds. Find integrable lower and upper comparators valid throughout that interval, then integrate their pointwise bounds. Constants give height-times-width bounds. Tighter comparators improve the result, while bounds checked only at sampled points do not certify the full integral.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $1/5\le1/(1+x)\le1$ 在 $[0,4]$ 成立，所以
$$\frac45\le\int_0^4\frac{dx}{1+x}=\ln5\le4.$$
分成 $[0,1]$ 与 $[1,4]$ 后分别估计，可收紧为 $11/10\le\ln5\le5/2$。这说明细分区间本身也能改善误差，而不必寻找新的原函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Constant bounds first place the logarithmic integral between $4/5$ and four. Estimating separately on the two indicated subintervals tightens this to $11/10$ and $5/2$. Refining the interval can improve a bound without any new antidifferentiation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较函数也可以不是常数。由 $e^t\ge1$ 对 $t\ge0$ 成立，积分得 $e^x\ge1+x$；再把这一新下界积分，得到 $e^x\ge1+x+x^2/2$，这里 $x\ge0$。最后一个方向不能直接搬到负 $x$，因为反向积分会反转不等式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Comparators need not be constant. Integrating the exponential lower bound once and then integrating the improved bound produces its linear and quadratic lower estimates for nonnegative inputs. The quadratic direction cannot simply be carried to negative inputs because integration orientation reverses the inequality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
