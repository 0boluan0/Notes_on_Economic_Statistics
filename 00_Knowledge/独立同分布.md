# Independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.)
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*独立同分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Independent and identically distributed**, abbreviated **i.i.d.**, describes a collection of uncertain numerical outcomes. Each has the same probability rule, and knowing some of the outcomes gives no additional information about the others. To understand these two requirements, imagine recording a sequence of coin tosses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**独立同分布**，英文缩写为 **i.i.d.**，描述一组尚不确定的数值结果：每个结果遵循相同的概率规则，而且知道其中一些结果，不会为其他结果提供额外的信息。可以从记录一连串抛硬币的结果来理解这两个要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Recording a coin toss as a number
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把抛硬币的结果记成数字*

> [!example] Recording the tosses · 记录投掷结果
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Record heads as $1$ and tails as $0$. Write $X_1$ for the first toss, $X_2$ for the second, and so on; the subscript tells us which toss we mean. Each $X_i$ is a [[随机变量|random variable]]: a rule for turning an uncertain outcome into a number. The actual record might be $1,0,1,1,0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把正面记作 $1$，反面记作 $0$。用 $X_1$ 表示第一次投掷，$X_2$ 表示第二次，依此类推；右下角的数字告诉我们说的是哪一次。每个 $X_i$ 都是一个[[随机变量]]：它规定如何把尚不确定的结果记成数字。实际记录可能是 $1,0,1,1,0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In our model, every toss has a $1/2$ chance of heads and a $1/2$ chance of tails. These probabilities describe the distribution of each recorded number; for outcomes such as $0$ and $1$, the full list of value-probability pairs is a [[概率质量函数|probability mass function]]. Since the same list applies to every toss, the variables are **identically distributed**. Their realised values can still differ, as the record above shows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在这个模型里，每次投掷出现正面和反面的概率都各为 $1/2$。这些概率描述了每个数字的分布；对于 $0$、$1$ 这样的离散取值，把各个值及其概率列出来，就是[[概率质量函数]]。每次投掷都适用同一份概率表，所以它们**同分布**。上面的记录里既有 $0$ 又有 $1$，同分布完全允许实际结果不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now make a second assumption: knowing the results of any of the other tosses leaves each remaining toss's probabilities unchanged. For example, after seeing heads on the first toss, the chance of heads on the second is still $1/2$; this also holds after any longer observed history. This is the idea of [[相互独立|mutual independence]]. In particular, the probability of two heads in a row is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再加上第二个假设：知道其他投掷的结果以后，每次尚未知道结果的投掷，其概率仍保持原样。例如，第一次已经出现正面，第二次出现正面的概率仍然是 $1/2$；即使知道了更多次投掷的结果，也一样。这就是[[相互独立]]的意思。于是，连续两次都是正面的概率为

$$
P(X_1=1,\ X_2=1)=\frac12\times\frac12=\frac14.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $P(\cdot)$ means “the probability that ...”; the comma means both stated outcomes occur. Independence lets us multiply the probabilities, and identical distributions make both factors $1/2$. This is one calculation that the i.i.d. assumptions make possible. Whether those assumptions suit a real experiment must be checked from how the observations are obtained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $P(\cdot)$ 表示括号内事情发生的概率，逗号表示两件事同时发生。独立让我们可以把概率相乘，同分布让这里的两个因子都是 $1/2$。这就是 i.i.d. 假设能帮助我们做的一件事。实际实验是否适合这些假设，需要根据观测是怎样取得的来判断。

## 两项缺一不可
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Both conditions are necessary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] Copying the first result · 把第一次的结果抄一遍
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose we toss the fair coin just once and copy its result into the second entry: $X_2=X_1$. Each entry still has a $1/2$ chance of being $1$, so their distributions match. But as soon as we know the first entry, we know the second exactly. In this experiment, two recorded heads have probability $1/2$, because only one actual toss has to land heads. The product calculation $1/2\times1/2$ would be wrong.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假如只抛一次前面的公平硬币，然后把结果抄到第二个位置，令 $X_2=X_1$。两个位置出现 $1$ 的概率仍然各为 $1/2$，所以它们同分布。可是，一旦知道第一个位置，第二个位置也就完全确定了。这时两个位置都是正面的概率是 $1/2$，因为实际上只需要那唯一的一次投掷出现正面；再用 $1/2\times1/2$ 就算错了。

> [!example] Two different coins · 两枚不同的硬币
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the other direction, imagine tossing two biased coins independently. The first has a $0.2$ chance of heads and the second a $0.8$ chance. The tosses are independent by assumption, but their probability rules differ. The name [[Bernoulli分布|Bernoulli distribution]] is shorthand for this kind of $0/1$ result: its parameter is the probability of $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 反过来，设想独立地抛两枚有偏硬币：第一枚出现正面的概率为 $0.2$，第二枚为 $0.8$。两次投掷按假设是独立的，但概率规则不同，所以不同分布。[[Bernoulli分布]]就是这种 $0/1$ 结果的分布名称，括号里的参数表示取值为 $1$ 的概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identical distributions do not guarantee independence—for example, let $X_2=X_1$. Independence does not guarantee identical distributions either: two independent Bernoulli variables can have different success probabilities. The “independent” in i.i.d. is not merely adjacent or pairwise independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同分布不保证独立，例如令 $X_2=X_1$；独立也不保证同分布，例如两个独立 Bernoulli 变量可以有不同成功概率。i.i.d. 中的“独立”不是只检查相邻变量或两两独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With three or more variables, information can also be hidden in a combination of outcomes. Checking each pair separately is insufficient; the distinction is explained in [[两两独立不推出相互独立|pairwise independence does not imply mutual independence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个及以上的变量还可能通过结果的组合产生依赖，因此逐对检查不够。这个区别在[[两两独立不推出相互独立]]中展开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reading the formal definition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把刚才的意思读回正式定义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The distribution of one variable considered on its own is its [[边际分布|marginal distribution]]. The [[联合分布|joint distribution]] describes probabilities for combinations of values. With those names in place, the definition says:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把一组变量中的某个变量单独拿出来看，它自己的分布叫[[边际分布]]；把多个变量的取值组合起来考虑，相应的概率由[[联合分布]]描述。有了这两个名字，正式定义就是：

> [!abstract] What i.i.d. requires · i.i.d. 的两个条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> A sequence of random variables $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ is i.i.d. exactly when:<br>
> **1.** every $X_i$ has the same marginal distribution; and<br>
> **2.** every finite subcollection is mutually independent, so its joint distribution factorises into the product of the marginals.<br>
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 随机变量序列 $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ 是 i.i.d.，当且仅当：
> 1. 每个 $X_i$ 有相同的边际分布；
> 2. 任意有限子列都相互独立，其联合分布分解为边际分布的乘积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Every finite subcollection” means any finite selection of variables, including non-adjacent ones. The same definition applies to other numerical observations, such as waiting times. For general real-valued variables, a distribution can be specified by its [[累积分布函数|cumulative distribution function]], which gives the probability of a value at or below each threshold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“任意有限子列”就是从这列变量里任取有限多个，也包括不相邻的那些。这一定义同样适用于等待时间等其他数值观测。对于一般的实值随机变量，可以用[[累积分布函数]]描述分布：它告诉我们，取值不超过每个给定门槛的概率是多少。

> [!question]- Reading the two-coin example · 用符号读两枚硬币的例子
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The symbol $\sim$ below means “has the distribution”. Read the earlier two-coin example in this notation:
>
> If $X_1,X_2$ are independent but $X_1\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(0.2)$ and $X_2\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(0.8)$, are they i.i.d.?
>
> **Answer:** No. They are independent but not identically distributed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 下面的 $\sim$ 表示“服从这个分布”。用这种记号重新读刚才两枚硬币的例子：
>
> 若 $X_1,X_2$ 独立，但 $X_1\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(0.2)$、$X_2\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(0.8)$，二者是否 i.i.d.？
>
> **答案：** 不是；它们独立但分布不同。
