# 经营口径与现金再投资匹配时企业自由现金流等于税后经营净利润减净经营资产增加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When operating scope and cash reinvestment are matched, FCFF equals NOPAT less the increase in net operating assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在简化经营模型中，若净经营资产的增加恰好代表当期净现金再投资，[[企业自由现金流]]可写成 $FCFF=NOPAT-\Delta NOA$。这不是独立于现金流定义的另一套算法，而是把折旧和长期资产净变动整理后的同一个结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simplified operating model, if the increase in NOA represents net cash reinvestment, [[企业自由现金流|FCFF]] can be written as $NOPAT-\Delta NOA$. It is a rearrangement of the cash-flow construction, not an unrelated alternative algorithm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记经营营运资本为 $ONWC$，净经营性长期资产为 $NOLA$，折旧摊销为 $D$。若已匹配的净资本支出满足 $CapEx=\Delta NOLA+D$，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let operating working capital be $ONWC$, net long-term operating assets be $NOLA$, and depreciation and amortisation be $D$. If consistently measured net capital expenditure satisfies $CapEx=\Delta NOLA+D$, then:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\begin{aligned}FCFF&=NOPAT+D-\Delta ONWC-CapEx\\&=NOPAT-\Delta ONWC-\Delta NOLA\\&=NOPAT-\Delta NOA.\end{aligned}$$

ABC 的 NOA 从 1446 增至 1722，NOPAT 为 206.72，因此 $FCFF=206.72-276=-69.28$。教材另假设折旧 45，得到 $206.72+45-21-300=-69.28$；45 的作用是展示两种写法如何相消，不是补出一笔已核实的原始交易。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ABC's NOA rises from 1446 to 1722 while NOPAT is 206.72, giving −69.28. With the textbook's assumed depreciation of 45, the longer calculation also gives −69.28. The assumed 45 demonstrates cancellation rather than establishing an underlying observed transaction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里有一个会实际改变答案的现金口径：CPA 把期末 44、期初 25 的现金纳入经营资产。剔除现金后，ONWC 分别是 $472-44=428$ 和 $451-25=426$，增加只有 2。若保持 NOPAT、折旧和资本支出的其余假设不变，[[企业自由现金流]]卡的非现金营运资本版本得到 $206.72+45-2-300=-50.28$；再留出必要经营现金增加的 19，才得到 −69.28。两个数回答的可分配现金口径不同，不能在估值和现金需求中混用或重复扣这 19。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash scope changes the answer here. CPA includes ending cash of 44 and opening cash of 25 in operating assets. Excluding cash gives ONWC of 428 and 426, an increase of only 2. Holding all other NOPAT, depreciation, and capex assumptions fixed, the non-cash working-capital version in [[企业自由现金流|the FCFF atom]] yields $206.72+45-2-300=-50.28$. Reserving the additional required operating cash of 19 then gives −69.28. These are different cash-availability conventions; do not mix them in valuation or deduct the 19 twice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用前要核对现金税、经营现金范围与资产变动。减值、重估、汇率折算、并购范围变化或非现金租赁等，可能让账面 $\Delta NOA$ 偏离现金再投资；须与利润及现金流一起调节。[[经营营运资本]]是否含必要经营现金也要一致。缺少这些条件时，不能只拿两张资产负债表相减就声称已经算出 FCFF。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check cash taxes, operating cash scope, and asset movements first. Impairment, revaluation, currency translation, acquisition scope changes, or non-cash leases can make book changes diverge from cash reinvestment and require joint profit/cash-flow adjustments. Whether [[经营营运资本|operating working capital]] includes required cash must also be consistent. Two balance sheets alone do not establish FCFF without these conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
