# 同质劳动者的城市就业概率只有在明确随机分配规则后才能由岗位与竞争人数之比换算

把“城市有多少岗位”换成“我有多大概率得到岗位”，必须先定义竞争者集合、分配规则和时间事件。[[Harris–Todaro模型]]的 $M/S$ 是**同质城市劳动者参加周期随机岗位分配**时的概率；“新增空缺数除以尚未就业的求职者数”则对应另一种条件获聘事件，不能直接互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Turning job counts into an individual's chance of employment requires a defined applicant pool, allocation rule and time event. In the [[Harris–Todaro模型|Harris–Todaro model]], $M/S$ applies to homogeneous urban workers in a periodic random allocation. New vacancies divided by remaining job seekers describe a different conditional hiring event.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 周期随机分配：现有岗位除以整个竞争池

设有 $S>0$ 名同等资格的城市劳动者和 $M\le S$ 个岗位；每期从这 $S$ 人中等概率选出 $M$ 人，每个岗位一人、每人最多一个岗位。对任意一名竞争者：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $S$ equally eligible urban workers compete for $M\le S$ jobs, with a uniformly random selection of $M$ people each period, one worker per job and at most one job per worker. For each competitor:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$p=\frac{M}{S},\qquad E[w_U]=p\bar w_M+(1-p)w_0.$$

例如20个岗位在40人之间随机分配，个人概率为 $1/2$。$S$ 包括参加本期分配的原有城市劳动者和迁入者；不是全部城市人口，也不是仅当期迁入者。若已在岗者不会被重新分配、技能决定排序或部分人有优先权，就不能把同一个总体比例当作所有人的个人概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Twenty jobs randomly allocated among 40 people give each a one-half probability. The pool includes existing urban workers and migrants participating in that allocation, not all residents or just recent arrivals. Protected incumbents, skill-based ranking or priority rules invalidate a common individual probability inferred from the aggregate share.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $w_0$ 必须是未获该岗位状态下的真实收益假设。零收益是基础模型的设定，不是[[非正规就业]]的定义。若一部分人在其他岗位就业，$1-M/S$ 也不是统计[[失业率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$w_0$ must reflect the assumed payoff without that job. Zero earnings are a benchmark assumption, not the definition of [[非正规就业|informal employment]]. If some workers hold other jobs, $1-M/S$ is not the statistical [[失业率|unemployment rate]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 新增空缺分配：先算条件概率，再累计

另一种制度是原有岗位保留，只让尚未获聘的 $Q_t$ 人竞争 $J_t$ 个新空缺。若每人等概率、$Q_t>0$ 且 $0\le J_t\le Q_t$，条件获聘概率为 $\pi_t=J_t/Q_t$。这是“此前未获聘，今期被选中”的概率，不是总体在岗率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a different system, incumbents keep their jobs and $Q_t$ remaining seekers compete equally for $J_t$ new vacancies. Then $\pi_t=J_t/Q_t$ is a conditional hiring probability, given no previous hire, provided $Q_t>0$ and $0\le J_t\le Q_t$. It is not the overall employment share.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若获得岗位后在考察期限内不离职、不失业，也不退出这个过程，令 $p_t$ 为“截至 $t$ 已获聘且仍在岗”的概率。由未获聘者今期可能进入岗位可得：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If hiring is absorbing over the horizon—no separation, job loss or exit—let $p_t$ be the probability of having been hired and remaining employed by $t$. Previously unsuccessful seekers can enter this period, so:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p_0=0,\qquad p_t=p_{t-1}+(1-p_{t-1})\pi_t
=1-\prod_{s=1}^{t}(1-\pi_s).
$$

例如每期条件获聘概率均为5%，两期累计概率是 $1-0.95^2=9.75\%$，不是10%。这里的 $\pi_t$ 已经按此前未获聘作条件化，不额外需要把所有日期的无条件事件宣称为独立；有离职或状态变化时则应重写转移规则。将 $p_t$ 放进[[迁移收益现值]]时，还须确认本期获得多少工作时长和工资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A 5% conditional chance each period gives a two-period cumulative probability of 9.75%, not 10%. These probabilities are already conditional on no prior hire; unconditional independence across dates is not additionally asserted. Separations or other state changes require a different transition rule. Using $p_t$ in [[迁移收益现值|migration present values]] also requires checking paid time within the period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Harris & Todaro（1970），[Migration, Unemployment and Development](https://www.aeaweb.org/aer/top20/60.1.126-142.pdf)，印刷第127–129页，PDF第2–4页，式(6)及脚注5、7：支持周期随机选择和城市就业比例的基准。原文允许更一般的预期形成；比例式不是不带条件的经验估计。
- Todaro & Smith（2012），[Economic Development, 11th ed.](https://business.tiu.edu.iq/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/Economic-Development-11th-Edition-Michael-P.-Todaro-Stephen-C.-Smith-1.pdf)，印刷第341、356–357页，PDF第367、382–383页，式(A7.1.2)–(A7.1.4)：支持岗位异质性边界、$p$与$\pi$的区分以及累计概率。本文显式写出递推所需的无离职条件，乘积式和5%数例独立复算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original paper supports the periodic-allocation benchmark; the textbook distinguishes hiring probabilities, accumulated employment probabilities and worker heterogeneity. The absorbing-employment condition is made explicit, and the product expression and example are independently derived.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
