# Elementary matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*初等矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **elementary matrix** packages one reversible row operation as matrix multiplication. Instead of rewriting every entry of a row operation each time, we can store its action in a small square matrix and apply it to any matrix with the matching number of rows. The identity matrix provides the starting record because it initially leaves every row unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**初等矩阵**把一次可逆行操作写成矩阵乘法。我们可以把操作记录在一个方阵中，再用于行数匹配的任何矩阵，不必每次重新描述怎样改每个元素。记录从单位矩阵开始，因为它最初保持所有行不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Make the record on the identity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*在单位矩阵上建立记录*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I_m$ has $m$ rows and columns, diagonal entries $1$ and other entries $0$. Apply **one** of the three elementary row operations to $I_m$; the resulting $m\times m$ matrix $E$ is an elementary matrix. For a matrix $A$ with $m$ rows and any positive number $n$ of columns, [[矩阵乘法|left multiplication]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[单位矩阵]] $I_m$ 有 $m$ 行、$m$ 列，对角元为 $1$，其余为 $0$。对 $I_m$ 作**一次**三类初等行操作之一，所得 $m\times m$ 矩阵 $E$ 就是初等矩阵。对有 $m$ 行、任意正整数 $n$ 列的矩阵 $A$，按[[矩阵乘法]]从左侧作用，得到

$$EA=\text{the result of the same row operation on }A.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $m$ is a positive integer, and $R_i$ denotes row $i$. All entries and operation coefficients belong to the same real or complex field. For example, subtract three times row 1 from row 2:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $m$ 是正整数，$R_i$ 表示第 $i$ 行。矩阵元素和操作系数取同一实数域或复数域。例如，从第二行减去第一行的三倍：

$$I_2=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
\xrightarrow{\ R_2\leftarrow R_2-3R_1\ }
E=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-3&1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now apply that record to the coefficient matrix from [[高斯消元|the elimination example]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把记录用于[[高斯消元|消元例子]]中的系数矩阵：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&7\end{pmatrix},\qquad
EA=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-3&1\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&7\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Row 1 is copied; row 2 becomes $-3$ times the old first row plus the old second row. For the right-hand side $b=(5,16)^T$, where $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]], the same matrix gives $Eb=(5,1)^T$. Thus $E[A\mid b]=[EA\mid Eb]$: the record transforms both sides together, as required to [[行操作的解集不变性|preserve the solutions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一行照抄；第二行变成原第一行的 $-3$ 倍加原第二行。右端 $b=(5,16)^T$ 也得到 $Eb=(5,1)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。因此 $E[A\mid b]=[EA\mid Eb]$：记录同步变换两边，正是[[行操作的解集不变性|保持解集]]所要求的操作。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is not a coincidence of the example. In a product $EA$, output row $i$ is $\sum_{j=1}^m e_{ij}A_{j:}$, where $e_{ij}$ is the entry of $E$ in row $i$, column $j$, and $A_{j:}$ is row $j$ of $A$. A changed row of the identity stores exactly the coefficients of the desired row combination. Unchanged identity rows continue to copy their corresponding rows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不是算例中的巧合。在乘积 $EA$ 中，第 $i$ 条结果行是 $\sum_{j=1}^m e_{ij}A_{j:}$；$e_{ij}$ 是 $E$ 第 $i$ 行第 $j$ 列的元素，$A_{j:}$ 是 $A$ 的第 $j$ 行。单位矩阵中改变的那一行，恰好记录所需行组合的系数；没有改变的单位行继续照抄对应原行。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Three operations, three ways to undo them
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 三类与逆矩阵

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Swap two distinct rows.** Swap the same two rows of $I_m$. Repeating that swap restores every row, so the resulting matrix is its own inverse. The $2\times2$ example is $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**交换两条不同行。** 对 $I_m$ 交换同样两行即可。再交换一次就恢复，所以所得矩阵的逆是它自身。二维例子为 $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Scale one row by $c\ne0$.** Replace its diagonal $1$ in $I_m$ by $c$. Undo the operation by scaling the same row by $1/c$. For instance, $\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ has inverse $\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**一行乘 $c\ne0$。** 把 $I_m$ 中该行的对角元 $1$ 换成 $c$。撤销时把同一行乘以 $1/c$。例如 $\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ 的逆为 $\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Add $c$ times row $j$ to a different row $i$.** Put $c$ in entry $(i,j)$ of $I_m$, keeping all its diagonal ones. Undo by adding $-c$ times row $j$ to row $i$; row $j$ was unchanged, so it is still available. In the opening example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**给第 $i$ 行加上另一行 $j$ 的 $c$ 倍。** 要求 $i\ne j$，把 $I_m$ 的 $(i,j)$ 元改为 $c$，保留所有对角线的 $1$。撤销时给第 $i$ 行加上第 $j$ 行的 $-c$ 倍；第 $j$ 行一直未变，所以仍可用它恢复。开头例子中，

$$E^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\3&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
E^{-1}E=EE^{-1}=I_2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These explicit reversals prove that every elementary matrix has an [[逆矩阵|inverse]], itself elementary. Scaling by zero is excluded because it erases a row; adding a row to itself is not the distinct-row replacement rule and must instead be checked as a scaling.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些具体逆操作证明：每个初等矩阵都有[[逆矩阵]]，而且逆仍是初等矩阵。一行乘零会抹掉信息，因此被排除；把某行加到它自身不属于“不同行替换”，应改按缩放检查。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A sequence has a definite multiplication order
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*操作序列有确定的乘法顺序*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $E_1$ acts first and $E_2$ second, the result is $E_2(E_1A)=(E_2E_1)A$. The most recent operation appears on the left. In general, $s$ successive operations give $E_s\cdots E_2E_1A$. Their product is invertible, but need not itself represent just one elementary operation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若先作 $E_1$、再作 $E_2$，结果为 $E_2(E_1A)=(E_2E_1)A$，最近一次操作写在左边。一般 $s$ 次连续操作给出 $E_s\cdots E_2E_1A$。乘积仍可逆，但不一定只代表一次初等操作。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, do the opening subtraction $E$ first, then double row 2 using $D=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$. The combined record is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，先作开头的减法 $E$，再用 $D=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ 把第二行加倍。合成记录为

$$DE=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-6&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
ED=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-3&2\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
They differ because doubling before or after the subtraction changes the weight on row 1. The matrix $DE$ combines mixing and scaling and cannot arise from one of the three operations on $I_2$. It is an invertible **product** of elementary matrices. Reversing a longer elimination requires undoing the operations in reverse chronological order; this is the recording used in [[消元与LU分解|elimination factorizations]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两者不同，因为先加倍或后加倍，会改变第一行的权重。$DE$ 同时混合并缩放，不能由 $I_2$ 作上述三类操作中的一次得到；它是初等矩阵的可逆**乘积**。撤销更长的消元过程时，要按时间顺序倒过来逐步撤销，[[消元与LU分解|消元分解]]使用的正是这种记录。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Left and right multiplication act differently
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Left multiplication acts on rows. Right multiplication acts on columns and needs a matrix sized by the number of columns. Even when the same $E$ fits on either side of square $A$, the actions differ. For the opening matrices,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左乘操作行，右乘操作列，而且右侧矩阵的尺寸须匹配列数。即使 $A$ 为方阵、同一个 $E$ 能放两侧，两种作用也不同。对开头矩阵，

$$AE=\begin{pmatrix}-5&2\\-18&7\end{pmatrix}\ne
EA=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $EA$ replaces row 2 by row 2 minus three times row 1, while $AE$ replaces **column 1** by column 1 minus three times column 2. The changed index switches sides. For a rectangular $m\times n$ matrix, row operations require $m\times m$ records and column operations require $n\times n$ records. A general invertible record and a single elementary matrix should also remain distinct concepts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $EA$ 把第二行换成“第二行减三倍第一行”，而 $AE$ 把**第一列**换成“第一列减三倍第二列”，被改变的编号随作用侧不同而转换。对 $m\times n$ 矩形矩阵，行操作记录必须为 $m\times m$，列操作记录则为 $n\times n$。一般可逆记录与单个初等矩阵也应区分。

> [!question]- Undo one replacement · 撤销一次替换
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $E$ adds $c$ times row $j$ to row $i\ne j$, what does $E^{-1}$ do? It adds $-c$ times the unchanged row $j$ to row $i$. Both operations must include every column of an augmented system.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $E$ 给第 $i\ne j$ 行加上第 $j$ 行的 $c$ 倍，$E^{-1}$ 做什么？给第 $i$ 行加上未改变的第 $j$ 行的 $-c$ 倍；作用于增广系统时，两次操作都必须包括全部列。
