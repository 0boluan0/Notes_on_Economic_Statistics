# 更高的宏观增长不单独保证现有股东获得更高股票回报
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Higher economic growth alone does not guarantee higher equity returns for existing shareholders.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[实际GDP增长]]衡量总体产出变化，股票回报则取决于投资者以什么价格获得哪些现金流权利。新增企业、增发融资、非上市部门和劳动收入都可能贡献增长，而收益不必按比例归属原有上市公司每一股；跨国公司的收入来源也不等于上市地 GDP。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[实际GDP增长|Real GDP growth]] measures aggregate output, whereas equity returns depend on the price paid for particular cash-flow rights. New firms, share issuance, unlisted activity, and labour income can contribute to growth without proportionately benefiting each existing listed share. Multinationals' revenues also extend beyond their listing country's GDP.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使每股未来现金流确实增加，若起点价格已充分反映增长，投资回报仍未必更高。教学对照：两项投资一年后实现的全部支付分别为 110 与 120，若买价分别为 100 与 120，实现回报分别为 10% 与 0%；更高支付不自动意味着更高收益率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even higher future per-share cash flows need not mean higher returns if the starting price already reflects them. In a constructed one-period comparison, realized total payments of 110 and 120 bought for 100 and 120 produce realized returns of 10% and 0%. A larger realized payment alone does not imply a higher realized return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不意味着增长消息对股票无关。相对预期的增长变化可改变经营现金流与风险要求，见[[意外信息定价]]；公司新增投资是否创造价值还要看[[增长不等于增值]]。历史跨国相关性也不能推出“增长越快必跌”的反向因果律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Growth news still matters: surprises may change cash flows and required returns, as explained in [[意外信息定价|unexpected-information pricing]]. Whether reinvestment creates value is addressed by [[增长不等于增值|growth versus value creation]]. Historical cross-country correlations do not establish a reverse law that faster growth causes losses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
