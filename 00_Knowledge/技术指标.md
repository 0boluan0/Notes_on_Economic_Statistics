# 技术指标是按指定公式将行情数据转换为数值特征的工具
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A technical indicator transforms market data into numerical features using a specified formula.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

技术指标（technical indicator）按预先指定的计算规则，把价格、成交量或市场涨跌家数等行情数据转为可比较的数值。输入字段、频率、窗口、初始化和缺失值处理共同决定输出；只知道指标名称通常还不足以复算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A technical indicator applies a specified calculation to market records such as prices, volume, or counts of advancing and declining securities. Input fields, frequency, window length, initialization, and missing-data handling determine the output. A name alone is often insufficient to reproduce it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[简单移动平均]] 输出近期价格的均值，保留价格单位；[[RSI]] 输出平滑上涨幅度在总涨跌幅度中的百分比；[[MACD]] 比较快慢均线及差值的平滑线。它们回答不同的描述问题，不能把数值同为“高”理解成同一个经济状态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[简单移动平均|simple moving average]] reports recent average prices in price units; [[RSI]] reports smoothed gains as a percentage of total smoothed gains and losses; [[MACD]] compares fast and slow averages and smooths their difference. These describe different features, so “high” does not have the same economic meaning across them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如四个收盘价为 $10,12,11,15$，最近三期 SMA 是 $38/3$。这个数把三个价格压缩为一个平均值；是否据此下单，仍需另外写定操作规则。多个变换怎样合用，见 [[指标同源边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For closes of $10,12,11,15$, the latest three-period SMA is $38/3$. It compresses three prices into one mean. A separate decision rule is needed to turn it into an order. Combining several transformations requires the distinction explained in [[指标同源边界|the information limits of same-input indicators]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
