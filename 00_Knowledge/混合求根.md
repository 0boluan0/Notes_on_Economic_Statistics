<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Hybrid root finding
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*混合求根*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A hybrid root solver keeps a trustworthy interval containing a solution while trying faster guesses inside it. A guess may come from a tangent line, as in [[Newton迭代|Newton iteration]], or from interpolation through known function values. The algorithm accepts such a guess only under explicit safeguards; otherwise it uses the midpoint step of [[二分求根|bisection]]. There are two separate jobs: **retain a root** and **make sufficient progress toward it**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

混合求根器一边保留一个可靠含根的区间，一边尝试更快的猜测。候选可以来自[[Newton迭代]]使用的切线，也可以来自通过已知函数值作出的插值。算法只在明确的保护条件通过时采用候选，否则使用[[二分求根]]的中点步骤。这里有两项不同的责任：**保住根**，以及**向根取得足够进展**。

> [!note] Separate containment and acceleration · 保根与加速分工
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Bisection retains a certified region and contracts it at a fixed rate. Newton or interpolation steps are often faster but can leave the trusted region. A hybrid separates the responsibilities: the sign-changing bracket prevents loss of the root, while a fast candidate accelerates progress only when it is safe; otherwise the method falls back to bisection. The acceptance test is algorithm-specific and cannot be replaced by the phrase “combine both.”
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 二分法知道根仍在什么范围内，并按固定比例收缩；Newton 或插值步通常更快，却可能跳出可信区域。混合法把两种责任分开：异号区间负责不丢根，快速候选步负责在安全时加速；候选不合格就退回二分。具体接受条件属于具体算法，不能用一句“二者结合”代替。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Preserve the bracket before trying to accelerate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不变量先于加速步骤

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f$ be continuous and evaluable on $[a_k,b_k]$. First handle an endpoint root: return it if $f(a_k)=0$ or $f(b_k)=0$. Otherwise maintain the strict sign invariant below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f$ 在 $[a_k,b_k]$ 上连续并可求值。每轮先检查端点：若 $f(a_k)=0$ 或 $f(b_k)=0$，就直接返回该端点根；其余路径维护严格不变量

$$
f(a_k)f(b_k)<0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity together with this strict sign condition guarantees at least one root in the open interval. Propose a candidate $c_k$ using Newton, a secant, inverse quadratic interpolation or another local model. Accept it only if it is inside the bracket, numerically usable and satisfies the method's sufficient-progress test; otherwise use the midpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个严格符号条件和连续性保证开区间内至少有一个根。混合法每轮提出一个候选 $c_k$：它可能来自 Newton 步、割线、逆二次插值或其他局部模型。只有当候选位于括区间内部、数值可用，并满足该方法规定的充分进展条件时才接受；否则令 $c_k$ 为中点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After evaluating $f(c_k)$, return $c_k$ if its value is exactly zero. Otherwise exactly one of $[a_k,c_k]$ and $[c_k,b_k]$ has opposite endpoint signs; keep that interval as $[a_{k+1},b_{k+1}]$. A non-midpoint candidate need not halve the width. Whether or not the fast step is used, preserve the strict invariant. The bracket certifies the update before the fast guess is trusted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求得 $f(c_k)$ 后，若 $f(c_k)=0$ 就直接返回；否则，严格异号不变量保证 $[a_k,c_k]$ 与 $[c_k,b_k]$ 中恰有一个子区间的端点严格异号，保留它作为 $[a_{k+1},b_{k+1}]$。候选不必是中点，所以这个子区间不一定恰好是一半。无论快速步是否被接受，新区间都必须继续满足严格不变量。这个顺序很重要：不是先相信快速步，再在失败后猜测根去了哪里。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A checkable safeguard and its convergence proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个可检验的保护规则及其收敛证明

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here is one deliberately simple hybrid, in exact arithmetic. Fix a fraction $0<\eta\le1/2$ once and for all; for example choose $\eta=1/4$. At each step let $L_k=b_k-a_k>0$. Accept a finite, usable proposed point $q_k$ only when it lies in the central part of the bracket:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面给出一种刻意简洁的混合法，先在精确算术中讨论。预先固定比例 $0<\eta\le1/2$，例如 $\eta=1/4$。每轮令 $L_k=b_k-a_k>0$；只有有限、可用的候选 $q_k$ 落在区间中央部分时才接受：

$$
a_k+\eta L_k\le q_k\le b_k-\eta L_k,
\qquad
c_k=\begin{cases}
q_k,&\text{if the safeguard passes},\\
a_k+L_k/2,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\eta=1/4$, this means rejecting candidates in the outer quarters. If a quantity needed by the chosen proposal is unavailable (such as a derivative for Newton), or its denominator is zero, use the midpoint fallback; do the same for an out-of-bracket or non-finite proposal. Then evaluate $f(c_k)$, handle an exact zero, and perform the sign-based interval update. If the function evaluation itself is invalid or its sign cannot be trusted, report an evaluation failure; it cannot certify which half to keep.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $\eta=1/4$ 时，这相当于拒绝两端各四分之一区域内的候选。所选候选公式需要的量不可用（例如 Newton 所需的导数）、分母为零、候选越界或不是有限数，也都触发退回中点。随后计算 $f(c_k)$，处理精确零值，再按符号更新区间。若函数求值本身失效，或符号无法信任，应报告求值失败；这样的结果无法证明该保留哪一半。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both the accepted proposal and the fallback midpoint are at least $\eta L_k$ from either endpoint. Consequently either retained subinterval has width at most $(1-\eta)L_k$, so induction gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无论接受快速候选还是退回中点，实际采用的点距两个端点都至少有 $\eta L_k$。因此保留的任一子区间，宽度最多为 $(1-\eta)L_k$；逐步应用这个关系可得

$$
L_{k+1}\le(1-\eta)L_k,
\qquad L_k\le(1-\eta)^kL_0\longrightarrow0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The brackets are nested and retain opposite signs. As in the [[二分求根#为什么嵌套区间会逼近某个根|nested-interval proof for bisection]], take $r=\sup_k a_k$: it lies in every bracket, and both endpoints approach it because their separation tends to zero. Continuity and the fixed opposite sign orientation imply $f(r)=0$. Every accepted test point $c_k\in[a_k,b_k]$ also approaches this root. If we return the **midpoint of the current bracket**, its positional error is at most $L_k/2$; an arbitrary proposed point in that bracket has only the general bound $L_k$. Thus the guard proves convergence from any valid initial bracket, with no assumption of a unique root or a nonzero derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些区间层层嵌套，并始终保留端点异号。沿用[[二分求根#为什么嵌套区间会逼近某个根|二分法的嵌套区间证明]]，取 $r=\sup_k a_k$：它属于每个括区间；由于两端间距趋零，两个端点都趋向它。连续性与固定的异号朝向给出 $f(r)=0$。每个实际测试点 $c_k\in[a_k,b_k]$ 也逼近此根。若返回**当前括区间的中点**，位置误差不超过 $L_k/2$；若返回区间内任意候选，一般只能用 $L_k$。因此该保护规则从任意有效初始括区间证明收敛，不要求根唯一或导数非零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a sufficient safeguard, not the definition of every hybrid. It guarantees a width factor at most $3/4$ when $\eta=1/4$, whereas pure bisection guarantees $1/2$. It can reject an excellent Newton step close to an endpoint, so it does not promise faster convergence than bisection or eventual quadratic convergence. More sophisticated hybrids use different history-dependent safeguards.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是一个充分的保护规则，并非所有混合法的定义。$\eta=1/4$ 时它保证宽度比例最多为 $3/4$，而纯二分保证 $1/2$。它可能拒绝靠近端点、实际非常好的 Newton 步，所以并不承诺一定比二分快，也不承诺最终进入二次收敛。更精细的混合法会采用依赖历史步长的其他保护条件。

> [!example] One accepted Newton step, then one fallback · 一次接受与一次回退
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use $f(x)=x^2-2$ on $[1,2]$ and $\eta=1/4$. A Newton proposal from the right endpoint is $q_0=2-(4-2)/4=3/2$, inside the permitted $[5/4,7/4]$. Since $f(3/2)=1/4>0$, keep $[1,3/2]$. The next Newton proposal from $3/2$ is $q_1=3/2-(1/4)/3=17/12$. It lies inside the bracket but outside its central part $[9/8,11/8]$, since $17/12>11/8$. Reject it and evaluate the midpoint $5/4$ instead. Its value is $-7/16$, so the new bracket is $[5/4,3/2]$, of width $1/4$. The rejected proposal was actually closer to $\sqrt2$ than the fallback; this conservative guard prioritises a provable reduction of uncertainty.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[1,2]$ 上的 $f(x)=x^2-2$，取 $\eta=1/4$。从右端点提出 Newton 候选 $q_0=2-(4-2)/4=3/2$，位于允许范围 $[5/4,7/4]$ 内。因为 $f(3/2)=1/4>0$，保留 $[1,3/2]$。下一次从 $3/2$ 提出的候选是 $q_1=3/2-(1/4)/3=17/12$。它虽在括区间内，却不在中央部分 $[9/8,11/8]$ 内，因为 $17/12>11/8$。因此拒绝它，改为检查中点 $5/4$；函数值为 $-7/16$，新区间为 $[5/4,3/2]$，宽度 $1/4$。被拒绝的候选实际上比回退点更靠近 $\sqrt2$；这个保守规则优先保证的是不确定区间必然缩小。

> [!warning] Keeping a bracket alone need not make progress · 只保括区间仍可能没有足够进展
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $f(x)=x-1$ and start from $[a_0,b_0]=[0,2]$. A bad algorithm proposes $c_k=1/2-2^{-(k+2)}$ at step $k\ge0$. Each proposal is strictly inside the current bracket, has negative output, and produces $[a_{k+1},b_{k+1}]=[c_k,2]$ with opposite signs. Yet $c_k\to1/2$, not the root $1$, and the widths tend to $3/2$, not zero. Even the returned bracket midpoints tend to $5/4$, not $1$. Every interval still contains the root; the total movement is simply insufficient. A uniform positive $\eta$ excludes this behaviour.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $f(x)=x-1$，初始区间为 $[a_0,b_0]=[0,2]$。一个坏算法在第 $k\ge0$ 步提出 $c_k=1/2-2^{-(k+2)}$。每个候选都严格位于当前括区间内，函数值为负，于是更新成仍然端点异号的 $[a_{k+1},b_{k+1}]=[c_k,2]$。然而 $c_k\to1/2$，不是根 $1$；宽度趋向 $3/2$，而不是零。即使返回括区间中点，也只趋向 $5/4$，不是 $1$。每个区间都保住了根，只是总共推进得不够。固定的正比例 $\eta$ 能排除这种行为。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Different hybrids have different safeguards
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## “混合”不是一个唯一算法

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Safeguarded Newton** tries tangent-based candidates and falls back when its specific safety or progress test fails.
- **Brent-type methods** combine a bracket, bisection, secant steps and inverse quadratic interpolation; no derivative is required. Inverse quadratic interpolation fits input $x$ as a quadratic function of three known output values and predicts its value at output zero, when those data permit it.
- Actual solvers differ in the permitted distance from endpoints, checks involving previous progress, and treatment of rounding stagnation.

The containment-and-conditional-acceleration pattern is reusable, but a library's convergence proof, stopping convention and tolerances belong to its particular algorithm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **Safeguarded Newton** 在 Newton 候选留在括区间且不过分冒险时使用它，否则二分。
- **Brent 类方法** 结合括区间、二分、割线和逆二次插值；它不需要导数，也不等同于“Newton 加二分”。
- 其他实现对候选离端点多近、上次收缩是否充分以及舍入停滞怎样处理，会采用不同接受规则。

逆二次插值把输入 $x$ 视为三个已知输出值的二次函数，并在这些数据允许插值时，预测输出为零所对应的输入。

因此可以复用“稳健保障 + 条件加速”这一模式，但不能把某个库的收敛证明、停止口径或默认容差自动移植到另一种混合法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The central-fraction guard above is **not Brent's algorithm**. For example, SciPy's version 1.16.0 `brentq` implementation tests an interpolated step using a previous step length and the current bisection displacement, with a minimum displacement related to tolerance. Its documented algorithm and implementation must be consulted for its own guarantees; our $(1-\eta)^k$ proof belongs only to the explicit central-fraction scheme.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的中央比例规则**并不等同于 Brent 算法**。例如 SciPy 1.16.0 的 `brentq` 会结合前一步长度与当前二分位移来检验插值步，还包含与容差有关的最小位移。要判断它自己的保证，应查阅对应算法与实现；这里的 $(1-\eta)^k$ 证明只属于明确写出的中央比例方案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A bracket still has prerequisites
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 括区间本身也有前提

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Opposite endpoint signs do not certify a root without continuity throughout the interval. For example, $f(x)=1/x$ has opposite signs at $-1$ and $1$, but it is undefined at zero and has no root. The initial interval must lie within a continuous real-valued domain; NaNs, undefined evaluations and discontinuities cannot be treated as ordinary sign information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

端点异号若没有连续性，并不保证有根。例如 $f(x)=1/x$ 在 $[-1,1]$ 两端异号，却在 0 不连续且无零点。NaN、不可求值点和断点都必须在建括区间时处理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, an even-multiplicity root may touch the axis without changing sign, so a sign scan can miss it. With several roots, the invariant retains at least one but does not choose a prescribed one automatically. A hybrid improves reliability under the given problem assumptions and initial bracket; it is not an algorithm for finding all roots.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，偶重根可能接触横轴而不变号，因此符号扫描可能完全找不到它。一个区间内若有多个根，符号不变量只保证至少保留一个，不能自动控制最后得到哪一个。混合法提高的是在既定问题和括区间下的可靠性，不是“找到所有根”的算法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Stopping needs matching evidence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 停止仍需对应证据

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bracket width provides a positional bound. A [[求根残差|residual]] requires [[残差控制根误差|additional conversion assumptions]] before it bounds positional error; successive step sizes and interpolation models have their own limits. An implementation should retain iteration counts, function evaluations, the final bracket and convergence status, including [[数值迭代停止条件|iteration limits and non-finite-evaluation failures]]. Interpret the answer in the problem's units and account for [[条件性与算法稳定性|conditioning near the root]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

括区间宽度给出位置界；[[求根残差|残差]]若要控制位置误差，需要 [[残差控制根误差|额外转换条件]]，相邻步长和插值模型也各有自己的解释前提。实际实现还应记录迭代次数、函数调用、最终括区间和收敛状态，并保留 [[数值迭代停止条件|最大迭代与非有限值失败出口]]。即使库返回一个浮点数，也应根据问题单位和 [[条件性与算法稳定性|根附近条件性]] 判断它是否足以使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In floating-point arithmetic, recheck whether the computed trial point lies strictly inside the bracket. If even a midpoint rounds to an endpoint, the exact-arithmetic contraction proof no longer describes that step. Report stagnation or a separately verified tolerance result. For a returned floating-point point, use the distance-to-endpoints certificate explained in [[二分误差界#给实际返回点计算证书|bisection error bounds]], rather than assuming it is the exact midpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在浮点运算中，还要重新检查实际算出的测试点是否严格位于括区间内部。若连中点都舍入成端点，该步就不再满足精确算术的收缩证明，应报告停滞，或另有证据证明已达到容差。对实际返回的浮点位置，使用[[二分误差界#给实际返回点计算证书|到两端点的距离证书]]，不要直接假定它是精确中点。

> [!question] Check the safeguard · 检查保护规则
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Does doing one bisection step and then accepting every Newton step unconditionally confer a bracketing guarantee?
>
> **Answer:** No. If subsequent candidates leave the bracket or the algorithm stops preserving opposite endpoint signs, the guarantee is lost. Fast steps must remain subject to a bracket and an explicit acceptance rule. Retaining signs also needs sufficient progress, as the preceding counterexample demonstrates.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个算法先做一次二分，随后无条件接受所有 Newton 步。它是否已经因为“用过二分”而拥有括区间法的保障？
>
> **答案：** 没有。只要后续候选可以跳出括区间，且算法不再维护端点异号不变量，保障就已经丢失。快速步必须受括区间和明确接受规则约束。
>
> 仅保留符号还需要足够进展；前面的反例说明了这个区别。
