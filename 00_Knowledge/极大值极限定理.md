# 独立同分布样本最大值的非退化仿射归一化极限必为广义极值分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Every nondegenerate affine-normalised limit of i.i.d. sample maxima is a generalized extreme-value distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X_i$ [[独立同分布]]，共同 CDF 为 $F$，$M_n=\max_{1\le i\le n}X_i$。如果存在 $a_n>0$、$b_n\in\mathbb R$ 使 $(M_n-b_n)/a_n$ 依分布收敛到非退化 CDF $H$，那么 $H$ 必属于[[广义极值分布|GEV]] 族。这是 Fisher–Tippett–Gnedenko 极大值极限定理，也称极值类型定理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X_i$ be [[独立同分布|i.i.d.]] with CDF $F$ and $M_n=\max_{1\le i\le n}X_i$. If constants $a_n>0$ and $b_n\in\mathbb R$ make $(M_n-b_n)/a_n$ converge in distribution to a nondegenerate CDF $H$, then $H$ belongs to the [[广义极值分布|GEV]] family. This is the Fisher–Tippett–Gnedenko, or extremal types, theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定理分类的是“如果极限存在，它能是什么”，不保证任意 $F$ 都存在这样的归一化极限；存在性由[[极大值吸引域]]条件表达。它也不直接声称高阈值超额服从 GPD：那是另一个相连但不同的[[超阈值极限定理]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem classifies possible limits; it does not guarantee a suitable nondegenerate limit for every $F$. Existence is expressed by the [[极大值吸引域|domain-of-attraction condition]]. Nor does this theorem directly identify high-threshold excesses as GPD: that is the separate, connected [[超阈值极限定理|threshold-excess limit theorem]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分类背后的结构是“多个独立块的最大值仍是合并样本的最大值”。极限因而必须在再取最大值和重新选取位置、尺度后保持同一类型；满足这种极大稳定结构的非退化实数分布正是 GEV 三类。这里是定理的结构解释，不以它代替完整的类型分类证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The structural reason is that the maximum of several independent block maxima is the maximum of the pooled sample. A limit must therefore preserve its type after taking maxima and rescaling location and scale. The nondegenerate real-valued max-stable types are precisely the three GEV types. This explains the theorem's structure rather than replacing its full classification proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个可直接核验的例子是 $X_i\sim U(0,1)$。令 $Z_n=n(M_n-1)$，对固定 $z<0$ 及足够大的 $n$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A directly checkable example is $X_i\sim U(0,1)$. Set $Z_n=n(M_n-1)$. For fixed $z<0$ and sufficiently large $n$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(Z_n\le z)=\left(1+\frac zn\right)^n\longrightarrow e^z.
$$

对 $z\ge0$，CDF 为 1。极限是 $\xi=-1,\mu=-1,\sigma=1$ 的 GEV；未归一化的 $M_n$ 则趋向端点 1，只有退化极限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $z\ge0$ the CDF is one. The limit is a GEV with $\xi=-1,\mu=-1,\sigma=1$. The unnormalised maximum instead approaches the endpoint 1 and has only a degenerate limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Martin Haugh，[Extreme Value Theory，PDF 第 6–8 页](https://www.columbia.edu/~mh2078/QRM/EVT_MasterSlides.pdf#page=8)：核对定理的条件、非退化性和 GEV 类型结论。均匀分布的归一化例由 $F^n$ 独立推导，并与第 4 页 GEV 公式逐项比对。
- Anthony Davison，[Statistics of Extremes: Overview（2022），PDF 第 5 页，Max-stability and the GEV](https://www.birs.ca/ubco-workshops/2022/22w5079/files/Anthony%20Davison/Extremes_Overview.pdf#page=5)：核对独立块合并、极大稳定性与类型分类之间的结构解释。
- McNeil–Frey，[1999-06-19 作者稿，PDF／文内第 7 页及脚注 4](https://statmath.wu.ac.at/~frey/publications/evt-garch.pdf#page=7)：核对超阈值 GPD 结果应归于 Balkema–de Haan 与 Pickands，而非将两条定理混为一条。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Haugh's notes, PDF pp. 6–8, support the theorem's assumptions, nondegeneracy, and GEV conclusion. The uniform example is independently derived from $F^n$ and matched to the GEV formula on p. 4.
- Anthony Davison's *Statistics of Extremes: Overview* (2022), PDF p. 5, “Max-stability and the GEV”, supports the structural explanation connecting pooled independent blocks, max-stability, and type classification.
- The McNeil–Frey author draft, 19 June 1999, PDF and internal p. 7 with footnote 4, supports distinguishing the Balkema–de Haan/Pickands excess result from the maxima theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
