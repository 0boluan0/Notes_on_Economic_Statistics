# 工资只有在劳动闲暇可自由调整等条件下才等于边际时间价值

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Wages equal marginal time value only under conditions such as freely adjustable work and leisure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

工资可以为[[时间节约价值]]提供一个有条件的基准：劳动时间可自由微调、选择为内点、无税与其他工资楔子、消费价格为1，且少一小时工作恰好多一小时闲暇。此时最优选择把闲暇的边际货币价值与工资配平。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Wages provide a conditional benchmark for [[时间节约价值|time savings]] when work hours are freely adjustable at an interior optimum, consumption costs one per unit, taxes and other wedges are absent, and one less hour of work yields one more hour of leisure. The optimum then equates marginal leisure value with the wage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设可支配时间为 $T$、闲暇为 $l$、非劳动收入为 $m$、工资为 $w$，消费 $c=m+w(T-l)$。对可微效用 $u(c,l)$ 且 $u_c>0$，内点一阶条件给出：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With available time $T$, leisure $l$, non-labour income $m$ and wage $w$, consumption is $c=m+w(T-l)$. For differentiable utility with $u_c>0$, an interior optimum satisfies:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$-wu_c+u_l=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \frac{u_l}{u_c}=w.$$

例如 $u=\ln c+\ln l$、$T=16$、$m=0$、$w=10$，最优 $l=8,c=80$，故 $u_l/u_c=c/l=10$。若雇主规定只能工作固定时数，这个等式就不再由可行的边际换时保证；所得税下还应区分税前工资与实际可支配的边际工资。旅行中的拥挤、疲劳与可靠性，也不是上述闲暇模型自动涵盖的属性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $u=\ln c+\ln l$, $T=16$, $m=0$ and $w=10$, the optimum is $l=8,c=80$, yielding $u_l/u_c=10$. Fixed hours remove the feasible marginal adjustment behind this equality. Income taxation also separates gross wages from marginal take-home wages. Crowding, fatigue and reliability during travel are additional attributes outside this simple leisure model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
