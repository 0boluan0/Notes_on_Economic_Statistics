# When is an upper triangular matrix symmetric?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*上三角矩阵何时也对称？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An upper triangular matrix has zeros below its main diagonal. If it is also [[实对称矩阵|real symmetric]], reflection across the diagonal forces the upper entries to be zero too. Only the diagonal entries can remain. For a $2\times2$ matrix, the entire argument is visible at once:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上三角矩阵在主对角线下方全为零。如果它还[[实对称矩阵|实对称]]，镜像位置必须相等，上方的元素也就只能为零，于是只剩对角元可以非零。对 $2\times2$ 矩阵，可以直接看到这个过程：

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\0&c\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A^T=\begin{pmatrix}a&0\\b&c\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[矩阵转置|transpose]] swaps row and column positions. Equality $A=A^T$ therefore requires $b=0$, while $a$ and $c$ stay arbitrary. The matrices satisfying both conditions are exactly $\operatorname{diag}(a,c)=\begin{pmatrix}a&0\\0&c\end{pmatrix}$. This notation lists the diagonal entries; all off-diagonal entries are zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[矩阵转置]]交换行列位置。要求 $A=A^T$，就必须令 $b=0$；$a,c$ 仍可任取。因此同时满足两个条件的矩阵，恰好是 $\operatorname{diag}(a,c)=\begin{pmatrix}a&0\\0&c\end{pmatrix}$。这个记号列出对角元，其余位置全为零。

> [!important] The intersection is the diagonal space · 交集是对角矩阵空间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fix a positive integer $n$ and work with real $n\times n$ matrices. Let $U_n$ be the [[三角矩阵|upper triangular matrices]], $S_n$ the symmetric matrices and $D_n$ the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrices]]. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定正整数 $n$，讨论实 $n\times n$ 矩阵。令 $U_n$ 为[[三角矩阵|上三角矩阵]]集合，$S_n$ 为对称矩阵集合，$D_n$ 为[[对角矩阵]]集合，则
>
> $$U_n\cap S_n=D_n.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[子空间的交集|intersection]] symbol $\cap$ means that a matrix must satisfy both conditions at the same time.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[子空间的交集|交集]]符号 $\cap$ 表示同一个矩阵要同时满足两个条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove this for every $n$, take $A\in U_n\cap S_n$. When $i>j$, position $(i,j)$ is below the diagonal, so $a_{ij}=0$. When $i<j$, its reflected entry $a_{ji}$ is below the diagonal; symmetry then gives $a_{ij}=a_{ji}=0$. Hence all off-diagonal entries vanish and $A\in D_n$. Conversely, every real diagonal matrix has zero lower entries and equal reflected pairs of zeros, so it is both upper triangular and symmetric. The two inclusions establish the equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意 $n$ 的证明如下。取 $A\in U_n\cap S_n$。若 $i>j$，位置 $(i,j)$ 在对角线下方，因此 $a_{ij}=0$；若 $i<j$，镜像位置 $a_{ji}$ 在下方，再由对称性得到 $a_{ij}=a_{ji}=0$。所以全部非对角元都为零，$A\in D_n$。反过来，每个实对角矩阵下方全零，镜像位置也都是相等的零，因而同时上三角且对称。两个方向的包含关系共同证明等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result is a whole [[子空间|subspace]] of the [[矩阵空间|matrix space]], not a single matrix or point. Its $n$ diagonal values can vary independently, giving a [[基|basis]] $E_{11},\ldots,E_{nn}$, where $E_{ii}$ has a single $1$ in diagonal position $i$ and zeros elsewhere. Thus $D_n$ has [[维数|dimension]] $n$ over $\mathbb R$; for $n=3$ it is three-dimensional. When $n=1$, all real one-by-one matrices already satisfy both conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结果是[[矩阵空间]]中的一整个[[子空间]]，不是一个矩阵或一个点。它的 $n$ 个对角值可以自由变化，一组[[基]]为 $E_{11},\ldots,E_{nn}$，其中 $E_{ii}$ 只在第 $i$ 个对角位置为 $1$、其余为零。因此 $D_n$ 在实数域上的[[维数]]为 $n$；$n=3$ 时是三维。$n=1$ 时，全部实一阶矩阵本来就同时满足两个条件。
