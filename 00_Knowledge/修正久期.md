# 修正久期是债券全价对指定到期收益率的负相对导数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Modified duration is minus the relative derivative of full price with respect to the specified YTM*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

修正久期定义为 $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=-P'(y)/P(y)$：在现金流、估值日及其他定价条件不变时，衡量全价对单一到期收益率的局部敏感度。收益率用小数表示；导数定义本身是精确关系，拿它估算有限幅度的价格变动才是近似。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Modified duration is $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=-P'(y)/P(y)$, the local sensitivity of full price to a single YTM with cash flows, valuation date and other pricing inputs held fixed. Yield is expressed as a decimal. The derivative definition is exact; using it for a finite price change is an approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $y$ 为每年复利 $m$ 次的年名义收益率，$a=1+y/m>0$，$t_k$ 为剩余日历年数。对固定非负现金流定价式逐项求导：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $y$ be nominal annual yield compounded $m$ times a year, $a=1+y/m>0$, and $t_k$ remaining calendar years. Differentiate the fixed nonnegative cash-flow price term by term:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P(y)=\sum_k CF_k a^{-mt_k},\qquad P'(y)=-\sum_k t_k CF_k a^{-mt_k-1}.$$
$$\boxed{D_{\mathrm{mod}}=\frac{D_M}{1+y/m}}.$$

例如 $D_M=2.792046$ 年、$y=5\%$、$m=1$，则 $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=2.659091$。若先用付息期数算久期，应先按[[债券付息周期换算|周期口径]]换成年，不能把半年期数直接代入年化公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $D_M=2.792046$ years, $y=5\%$ and $m=1$, modified duration is $2.659091$. A duration first calculated in coupon periods must be converted to years using the [[债券付息周期换算|period convention]] before applying the annualized formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若改用连续复利年收益率 $c$，$P(c)=\sum_k CF_ke^{-ct_k}$，则 $-P_c/P=\sum_k t_kw_k=D_M$。这与离散复利下除以 $1+y/m$ 不矛盾：两者对不同的收益率坐标求导。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With continuously compounded annual yield $c$, $P(c)=\sum_k CF_ke^{-ct_k}$ gives $-P_c/P=\sum_k t_kw_k=D_M$. This does not contradict the discrete-compounding denominator: the derivatives use different yield coordinates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单只债券的 YTM 变化并不自动代表所有期限的即期利率同幅平移，见 [[收益率久期与曲线久期]]。要衡量曲线冲击，必须另指定曲线及冲击方式；要让期权或浮息现金流随情景变化，也不能只沿本页固定支付式求导。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bond's own YTM change does not automatically mean an equal shift in every spot rate; see [[收益率久期与曲线久期|yield versus curve duration]]. Curve risk requires a specified curve and shock; option-dependent or floating payments also require more than differentiation of the fixed-payment formula here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
