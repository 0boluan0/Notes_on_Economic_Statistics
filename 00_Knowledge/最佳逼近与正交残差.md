# Best approximation and orthogonal residuals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*最佳逼近与正交残差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose we want to approximate a vector $b$, but our replacement must lie in a chosen [[子空间|subspace]] $S$. The nearest replacement is characterized by its error: the residual $b-p$ must be perpendicular to **every allowed direction** in $S$. This turns a search over all candidates into an orthogonality check, and it proves that the nearest vector is unique.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设要逼近向量 $b$，但替代向量只能位于指定[[子空间]] $S$ 中。最近的替代向量可以由误差来刻画：残差 $b-p$ 必须垂直于 $S$ 内的**每一个允许方向**。这样就把遍历全部候选的搜索，变成了正交条件检验，并能证明最近向量唯一。

> [!example] Moving along a line / 沿直线移动
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $b=(3,2)^T$ and $S=\{(t,t)^T:t\in\mathbb R\}$ in the usual plane; $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]] so the vectors are columns. A candidate $s=(t,t)^T$ has squared distance
> $$\|b-s\|^2=(3-t)^2+(2-t)^2=\tfrac12+2(t-\tfrac52)^2.$$
> The unique minimum is at $t=5/2$, giving $p=(5/2,5/2)^T$. Its residual is $(1/2,-1/2)^T$, perpendicular to the line because its dot product with $(1,1)^T$ is zero. For example, moving to $s=(3,3)^T$ increases squared error from $1/2$ to $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在通常平面中取 $b=(3,2)^T$，$S=\{(t,t)^T:t\in\mathbb R\}$；$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，将坐标写成列向量。候选 $s=(t,t)^T$ 的距离平方如上，唯一最小值在 $t=5/2$ 取得，故 $p=(5/2,5/2)^T$。残差是 $(1/2,-1/2)^T$，它与直线方向 $(1,1)^T$ 的点积为零。例如，移到 $s=(3,3)^T$ 后，误差平方由 $1/2$ 增至 $1$。

## The exact nearest-point criterion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*精确的最近点判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a real or complex [[内积|inner-product]] space $V$, with length $\|x\|=\sqrt{\langle x,x\rangle}$. Let $S$ be a finite-dimensional subspace and $b\in V$. Alternatively, allow $S$ to be a closed subspace of a Hilbert space; closedness and completeness are explained below. The [[正交分解定理|orthogonal decomposition theorem]] supplies the [[正交投影|projection]] $p=P_Sb$ and residual $e=b-p\in S^\perp$, where the [[正交补|complement]] $S^\perp$ consists of vectors perpendicular to all of $S$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定实或复[[内积|内积空间]] $V$，长度为 $\|x\|=\sqrt{\langle x,x\rangle}$。设 $S$ 是有限维子空间，$b\in V$；也可以让 $S$ 是 Hilbert 空间中的闭子空间，下文会说明闭性与完备性。[[正交分解定理]]给出[[正交投影]] $p=P_Sb$ 及残差 $e=b-p\in S^\perp$；这里[[正交补]] $S^\perp$ 由与整个 $S$ 垂直的向量组成。

> [!important] The nearest vector / 最近向量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a candidate $q\in S$, the following are equivalent: $q$ minimizes $\|b-s\|$ over all $s\in S$; $b-q\in S^\perp$; and $q=P_Sb$. The minimizer is unique. The requirement $q\in S$ is part of the criterion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对候选 $q\in S$，以下三件事等价：$q$ 在全部 $s\in S$ 中使 $\|b-s\|$ 最小；$b-q\in S^\perp$；$q=P_Sb$。最小化向量唯一，而且 $q\in S$ 本身就是判据的一部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Any other candidate adds a squared error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么正交就给最近点

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any candidate $s\in S$, write $b-s=(b-p)+(p-s)$. The first term is perpendicular to $S$, while the second lies in $S$. The [[正交向量勾股关系|Pythagorean identity for orthogonal vectors]], valid over both real and complex inner-product spaces, therefore gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任取候选 $s\in S$，写成 $b-s=(b-p)+(p-s)$。第一项垂直于 $S$，第二项位于 $S$ 中。因此，在实、复内积空间都成立的[[正交向量勾股关系]]给出

$$\|b-s\|^2=\underbrace{\|b-p\|^2}_{\text{unavoidable error}}
+\underbrace{\|p-s\|^2}_{\text{additional error}}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term does not depend on the new candidate. The second is nonnegative and is zero exactly when $s=p$. Thus $p$ is a global minimizer, and every different candidate is strictly worse. Squaring distances does not change their order because distances are nonnegative. If $b$ already belongs to $S$, the unavoidable error is zero and $p=b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一项不随新候选改变，是无法消除的误差；第二项非负，且只有 $s=p$ 时等于零。因此 $p$ 是全局最小点，每个不同候选都严格更差。距离本身非负，所以比较平方不会改变大小顺序。如果 $b$ 已在 $S$ 中，无法消除的误差为零，$p=b$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the converse, start with a candidate $q\in S$ whose residual is not perpendicular. Decompose that residual as $b-q=u+e$, where $u\in S$, $e\in S^\perp$. Nonperpendicularity means $u\ne0$. Moving the candidate to $q+u\in S$ removes $u$ and changes squared error from $\|u\|^2+\|e\|^2$ to $\|e\|^2$. It is strictly smaller, so $q$ was not a nearest point. This proof checks the full complex orthogonality condition too, rather than only the real part of an inner product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，若候选 $q\in S$ 的残差不垂直，就把它分解为 $b-q=u+e$，其中 $u\in S$、$e\in S^\perp$。不垂直意味着 $u\ne0$。把候选移到 $q+u\in S$，就去掉了 $u$，误差平方由 $\|u\|^2+\|e\|^2$ 降到 $\|e\|^2$，严格变小，因此 $q$ 不可能最近。这个证明也检验了复空间中完整的正交条件，而不只是内积的实部。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Distance, existence, and uniqueness concern different choices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 含义边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The norm must come from the same inner product used for orthogonality. For example, weighting one coordinate twice as heavily changes the distance we minimize and can move the nearest point; the [[正交投影|projection example with a weighted inner product]] computes that change. A nearest point for one metric need not be nearest for another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

范数必须来自检验正交时使用的同一个内积。例如，把一个坐标的权重加倍，会改变要最小化的距离，并可能移动最近点；[[正交投影|加权内积投影例子]]具体算出了这种变化。对一种距离最近的点，对另一种距离未必最近。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A Hilbert space is an inner-product space complete in its induced distance: every Cauchy sequence, whose later terms become arbitrarily close to one another, has a limit in the space. A subspace is closed if it contains limits of its convergent sequences. Closed subspaces of a Hilbert space have nearest points for every $b$. If $S$ is not closed, choose $b\in\overline S\setminus S$, where $\overline S$ denotes all limits approachable by sequences from $S$. Candidates can get arbitrarily close to $b$, so the infimum distance is zero, but no candidate attains it. The [[双重正交补|finite-support sequence example]] makes this failure explicit. All finite-dimensional subspaces are closed, and their finite-basis decomposition already establishes the theorem without needing completeness of the ambient space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Hilbert 空间是在内积诱导距离下完备的空间，即每条 Cauchy 序列（后面的项彼此可以任意接近）都在空间内有极限。子空间闭，是指它包含其中收敛序列的极限。Hilbert 空间的闭子空间对每个 $b$ 都有最近点。若 $S$ 不闭，取 $b\in\overline S\setminus S$，其中 $\overline S$ 表示能用 $S$ 内序列逼近的全部极限。候选可以任意接近 $b$，所以下确界距离为零，却没有候选真正取得它。[[双重正交补|有限支撑序列的例子]]会具体展示这一失败。所有有限维子空间都闭，而且其有限基分解已能证明本定理，不必要求外部空间完备。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a general nonlinear set, perpendicularity to a subspace is no longer the same problem. Nonempty closed convex sets in Hilbert space still have a unique nearest point (convex means containing the whole segment between any two of their points), but their optimality condition is an inequality along feasible directions; arbitrary closed nonconvex sets need not have unique nearest points. Also distinguish the unique fitted vector from coefficients representing it: in [[线性最小二乘|linear least squares]], $p=A\hat x$ is unique, while different $\hat x$ may produce the same $p$ when the columns of $A$ are dependent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一般非线性集合，“垂直于子空间”不再是同一个问题。Hilbert 空间里的非空闭凸集仍有唯一最近点（凸表示包含任意两点之间的整条线段），但最优条件是沿可行方向的不等式；任意闭的非凸集则未必有唯一最近点。此外，应区分唯一拟合向量与表示它的系数：[[线性最小二乘]]中 $p=A\hat x$ 唯一，但若 $A$ 的列线性相关，不同 $\hat x$ 可能产生同一个 $p$。

- [Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*, §6C, 6.49 and 6.61–6.62, PDF pp. 226–227 and 231–232](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=231): decomposition for a finite-dimensional subspace, nearest-point inequality and its equality condition. The converse is obtained by applying that decomposition to the candidate's residual.
- [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/MIT_OCW_18.06SC_PDF/05_Session_Summaries/MIT18_06SCF11_Ses2.2sum.pdf#page=1|MIT 18.06SC Session 2.2 summary, pp. 1–2]] and [[02_Least Squares, Determinants and Eigenvalues#Session 2.2 Projections onto subspaces|permanent Session 2.2 notes]]: real projection, perpendicular residuals and the least-squares interpretation. The line example is checked by completing the square.
- [MIT 18.102 Lecture 17, Theorems 178 and 180, PDF pp. 1–3](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-102-introduction-to-functional-analysis-spring-2021/c97549663746d6bc4a379077e067c55b_MIT18_102s21_lec17.pdf#page=1): existence and uniqueness for closed convex sets and decomposition for closed linear subspaces in Hilbert space. The nonclosed-subspace failure follows directly from the definition of closure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Axler 上述分解与最小距离结果：核对有限维子空间、最近点不等式及等号条件；必要性由候选残差的正交分解推出。
- MIT Session 2.2 摘要与永久笔记：核对实投影、垂直残差和最小二乘解释；直线数例通过配方验算。
- MIT 18.102 Lecture 17 定理 178、180：核对 Hilbert 空间闭凸集的最近点存在唯一性及闭线性子空间的分解；不闭时的失败直接按闭包定义核验。
