# Divisibility
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*整除*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Twelve objects can be arranged in three complete groups of four. Fourteen objects cannot be arranged in complete groups of four without a remainder. **Divisibility** records whether an exact integer multiple is possible. It is a statement that is true or false, rather than the numerical result of a division.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

十二个物品可以恰好分成三组，每组四个；十四个物品却不能在没有余数的情况下分成每组四个的整组。**整除**记录能否得到一个精确的整数倍，是一个有真假之分的关系，而不是除法算出的数值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For integers $a,b$, the statement $a\mid b$ means that $b=ak$ for some integer $k$. It asks whether $b$ is a whole-number multiple of $a$, not for the quotient $a/b$. Thus $4\mid12$ because $12=4\cdot3$, whereas $4\nmid14$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对整数 $a,b$，$a\mid b$（读作“$a$ 整除 $b$”）表示存在整数 $k$ 使 $b=ak$。它问的是“能否用完整的 $a$ 凑出 $b$”，不是要求计算分数 $a/b$。例如 $4\mid12$，因为 $12=4\cdot3$；但 $4\nmid14$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Integers include $\ldots,-2,-1,0,1,2,\ldots$, written as $\mathbb Z$. The multiplier $k$ must belong to this set; a fractional multiplier does not count. The symbol $\mid$ is read “divides,” and $\nmid$ means “does not divide.” In symbols,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

整数包括 $\ldots,-2,-1,0,1,2,\ldots$，记作 $\mathbb Z$。倍数系数 $k$ 必须属于这个集合，分数系数不算。$\mid$ 读作“整除”，$\nmid$ 表示“不整除”。用符号写成

$$a\mid b\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\exists k\in\mathbb Z\text{ such that }b=ak.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here “there exists” asks for at least one suitable integer. To establish divisibility, display a multiplier and check the equation. To establish nondivisibility, show that no integer multiplier can satisfy it. For example, $14=4k$ would force $k=7/2$, which is not an integer, so $4\nmid14$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“存在”只要求至少一个合适的整数。证明整除时，可以给出一个倍数系数并核对等式；证明不整除时，则要排除全部整数倍数系数。例如 $14=4k$ 会迫使 $k=7/2$，它不是整数，所以 $4\nmid14$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the direction before calculating
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先辨认方向，再计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $a\mid b$, $a$ is a **divisor** or **factor** of $b$, and $b$ is a **multiple** of $a$. These words describe the same relation from opposite ends. Divisibility is not symmetric: $4\mid12$, but $12\nmid4$ because $4/12=1/3$ is not an integer. When $a\ne0$, the relevant quotient for testing $a\mid b$ is $b/a$, not $a/b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $a\mid b$ 中，$a$ 是 $b$ 的**因子**或**约数**，$b$ 是 $a$ 的**倍数**；它们是从两端描述同一个关系。整除没有对称性：$4\mid12$，但 $12\nmid4$，因为 $4/12=1/3$ 不是整数。当 $a\ne0$ 时，要检验 $a\mid b$，应看 $b/a$ 是否为整数，而不是 $a/b$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Negative integer multiples are allowed: $3\mid-12$. Divisibility is also distinct from division by zero: every integer divides $0$, while $0\mid b$ holds only for $b=0$. These statements follow from $b=ak$ without dividing by zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里允许负整数倍：$3\mid-12$。还要区别“整除关系”与“除以零”：每个整数都整除 $0$，而 $0\mid b$ 仅在 $b=0$ 时成立；这些判断都来自 $b=ak$，没有做除以零的运算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation $-12=3(-4)$ supplies the first multiplier. Likewise $12=(-3)(-4)$ shows $-3\mid12$. A sign does not prevent divisibility; the multiplier may be positive, negative or zero. For a nonzero $a$, its multiplier is unique: $ak=a\ell$ implies $k=\ell$. This uniqueness is not part of the definition, which only requires existence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等式 $-12=3(-4)$ 给出了第一个倍数系数；同样，$12=(-3)(-4)$ 说明 $-3\mid12$。负号不妨碍整除，系数可以为正、为负或为零。当 $a\ne0$ 时系数是唯一的，因为 $ak=a\ell$ 推出 $k=\ell$；不过定义本身只要求存在，不要求先证明唯一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Zero is handled by multiplication
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零的情形直接用乘法判断*

> [!important] The relation does not divide by zero · 关系定义没有除以零
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For any integer $a$, $0=a\cdot0$, so $a\mid0$. In particular $0\mid0$ is true. Conversely, if $0\mid b$, then $b=0\cdot k=0$, so zero divides no nonzero integer. At $a=b=0$, every integer $k$ works; there is no uniquely determined quotient and no value assigned to $0/0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每个整数 $a$，都有 $0=a\cdot0$，因此 $a\mid0$；特别地，$0\mid0$ 为真。反过来，若 $0\mid b$，则 $b=0\cdot k=0$，所以零不整除任何非零整数。当 $a=b=0$ 时，每个整数 $k$ 都满足等式；这没有给出唯一的商，也没有给 $0/0$ 赋值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same definition gives $a\mid a$ using $k=1$, and $1\mid b$ and $-1\mid b$ using $k=b$ and $k=-b$. None of these facts says that a divisor must be smaller than the number it divides: zero and negative integers make that informal picture unreliable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一定义还给出 $a\mid a$（取 $k=1$），以及 $1\mid b$、$-1\mid b$（分别取 $k=b$、$k=-b$）。这些事实都没有要求约数必须比被整除的数小；涉及零和负整数时，不能依赖这种直观大小比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Where the relation is used
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*这个关系用在哪里*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When discussing a greatest positive common divisor below, take two integer inputs that are not both zero. For the congruence notation below, use the linked card's integer modulus $n>1$. These are conditions of the later concepts, not restrictions on $a\mid b$ itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面说“最大正公因数”时，两个整数输入应不全为零；下面的同余记号采用所链卡片的整数模数 $n>1$。这些是后续概念的条件，不是 $a\mid b$ 定义本身的限制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[最大公因数|greatest common divisor]] is the greatest positive integer satisfying this relation for both inputs. [[同余|Congruence]] applies the relation to a difference: $a\equiv b\pmod n$ means $n\mid(a-b)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读 [[最大公因数]] 时，要寻找的是共同满足这个关系的最大正整数；读 [[同余]] 时，则把它用于两个整数的差：$a\equiv b\pmod n$ 意味着 $n\mid(a-b)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two zero inputs, all positive integers are common divisors, so there is no greatest positive one; the linked gcd card states its separate value convention at $(0,0)$. If a positive divisor does not divide the input, [[带余除法|division with remainder]] still describes the input by a multiple plus a smaller nonnegative leftover.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个输入都为零时，每个正整数都是公因数，因此没有最大的正公因数；所链 gcd 卡另外说明 $(0,0)$ 处的取值约定。当一个正除数不能整除输入时，[[带余除法]]仍可以把输入表示为一个整数倍加上较小的非负余量。
