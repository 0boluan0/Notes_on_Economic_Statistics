# 贫困人口比例是在同一贫困线下被识别为贫困的人口占比

贫困人口比例 $H$，也记为 $P_0$，回答“多少比例的人低于[[贫困线]]”。它数的是人口，不是贫困家庭占全部家庭的比例，也不是这些人占全部收入的份额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The poverty headcount ratio $H$, also written $P_0$, is the share of people below the [[贫困线|poverty line]]. It is a population share, not the share of households that are poor or the income share received by poor people.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $n>0$ 个等权个人，福利变量为 $y_i$，阈值为 $z$，其中 $q$ 人满足 $y_i<z$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n>0$ equally weighted people with welfare $y_i$, let $q$ have welfare below threshold $z$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H=\frac{q}{n}=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbf1(y_i<z).
$$

四人收入为 40、90、120、150，且 $z=100$ 时，$H=2/4=0.5$。若前两人的收入都变为 90，$H$ 仍为 0.5：它不会记录尚未越线的改善。需要观察短缺的变化时，打开[[贫困缺口指数]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With incomes of 40, 90, 120, and 150 and $z=100$, $H=2/4=0.5$. If the first two incomes both become 90, $H$ remains 0.5: improvements that do not cross the line are invisible to this measure. Use the [[贫困缺口指数|poverty gap index]] to track their shortfalls.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

调查中若记录 $i$ 代表 $w_i>0$ 个人，应使用人口权重：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If survey record $i$ represents $w_i>0$ people, use population weights:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H_w=\frac{\sum_iw_i\mathbf1(y_i<z)}{\sum_iw_i}.
$$

例如两户抽样权重相同，一户 4 人且人均福利低于线，另一户 1 人且高于线。贫困人口比例为 $4/5$，贫困家庭比例才是 $1/2$。如何由家庭总量构造人均福利与人数权重，见[[贫困测量的福利口径]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider two households with equal sampling weights: four members in a poor household and one member in a nonpoor household. The population poverty rate is $4/5$; only the household poverty rate is $1/2$. See [[贫困测量的福利口径|welfare construction for poverty measurement]] for the link between household aggregates, per-person welfare, and population weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [World Bank, PIP Methodology, Monetary poverty measures](https://github.com/worldbank/PIP-Methodology/blob/master/04-Calculating-survey-estimates.Rmd)：$P_0$ 的定义与严格小于阈值的指示函数。
- [PIP, Equivalence scale](https://github.com/worldbank/PIP-Methodology/blob/master/02-Constructing-welfare-aggregates.Rmd)：家庭抽样权重乘家庭人数，确保估计人口比例。
- [Foster et al. (2013), 第114–115页](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/281001468323965733/pdf/A-unified-approach-to-measuring-poverty-and-inequality-theory-and-practice.pdf#page=132)：不越线的收入变化不改变 headcount；两组数例是教学构造并已独立复算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
PIP supports the indicator formula and population weighting. Foster and colleagues explain insensitivity to changes that do not cross the line. Both examples are constructed applications checked independently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
