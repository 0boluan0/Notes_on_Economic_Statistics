# 库存现金是银行持有的实物通货而不是央行账户准备金余额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Vault cash is physical currency held by a bank, not a reserve balance in its central-bank account*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 库存现金是银行金库、柜台或现金设备中持有的纸币硬币；准备金余额是银行或其他合格机构在央行账户中的电子余额。两者都可支持流动性，但物理位置、账户形式和统计处理不同。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Vault cash is notes and coins held in a bank's vaults, tills, or cash devices. A reserve balance is an electronic balance in the institution's central-bank account. Both can support liquidity, but they differ in physical form, account location, and statistical treatment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

美国 H.6 的 M1 currency 明确排除存款机构金库中的通货。历史上美国 total reserves 在 2020 年 4 月以前可包括用于满足准备金要求的 vault cash；现行 H.6 把 reserve balances 单列。定义变动说明不能把教材里 reserves 的旧合并用法直接当成当前账户概念。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
U.S. H.6 money-stock currency explicitly excludes currency in depository-institution vaults. Historically, U.S. total reserves could include vault cash used to satisfy reserve requirements before April 2020; current H.6 reports reserve balances separately. This definitional history is why an older textbook's combined use of reserves must not be treated as the current account concept.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- physical currency 是按形态识别的较大集合；vault cash 是其中由银行持有的部分；reserve balances 则是另一种央行账户资产，不能把三者当成三个并列且互斥的集合。
- 银行从央行提取纸币时，准备金余额下降、库存现金上升；资产形态转换不等于银行资产总额自动增加。
- M1 排除 vault cash 不表示实物纸币失去央行负债身份；它只是被排除在“公众持有通货”口径之外。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Physical currency is the broader form-based category; vault cash is the portion held by banks, whereas reserve balances are a separate central-bank account asset. The three are not parallel, mutually exclusive sets.
- When a bank obtains banknotes from the central bank, reserve balances fall and vault cash rises; the asset changes form without automatically increasing total bank assets.
- Exclusion of vault cash from M1 does not remove the liability identity of the notes; it excludes them from the public-currency measure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 银行把央行账户中的余额换成纸币运到金库后，哪个项目下降、哪个项目上升？
>
> **答案：** 央行账户中的准备金余额下降，银行持有的库存现金上升；不能把二者继续记成同一项电子准备金。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A bank converts a central-bank account balance into banknotes delivered to its vault. Which item falls and which rises?
>
> **Answer:** Its reserve balance falls and its vault cash rises. The physical notes must not remain recorded as the same electronic reserve balance.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
