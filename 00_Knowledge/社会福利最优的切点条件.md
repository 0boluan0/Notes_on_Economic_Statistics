# 光滑内点的社会福利最优要求社会无差异曲线与效用前沿斜率相等
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A smooth interior welfare optimum requires equal slopes of the isowelfare curve and the utility frontier*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

切点条件是两人[[社会福利函数|社会福利最大化]]在光滑前沿内点上的一阶必要条件。设可行效用满足 $u_1\in[a,b]$、$u_2\le F(u_1)$，上边界 $F$ 可行；社会评价为 $W(u_1,u_2)$。在候选点附近，若 $F,W$ 可微、$W_2=\partial W/\partial u_2>0$，且最优点的 $u_1^*\in(a,b)$ 允许沿前沿向左右小幅移动，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tangency is a first-order necessary condition for two-person [[社会福利函数|social welfare maximisation]] at an interior point of a smooth frontier. Suppose feasible utilities satisfy $u_1\in[a,b]$ and $u_2\le F(u_1)$, with the upper boundary itself feasible. Around the candidate, let $F$ and $W$ be differentiable and $W_2=\partial W/\partial u_2>0$. If an optimum has $u_1^*\in(a,b)$ with small feasible frontier movements in both directions, then:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{d}{ds}W(s,F(s))\bigg|_{s=u_1^*}
=W_1+W_2F'(u_1^*)=0,
\qquad
-\frac{W_1}{W_2}=F'(u_1^*).
$$

各偏导均在 $(u_1^*,F(u_1^*))$ 取值。左右两边分别是同一效用坐标下社会无差异曲线和效用前沿的斜率。它平衡的是社会评价与可行效用权衡，不是商品空间中两人的 MRS 相等。前沿对应的效率含义先按[[效用可能性边界|支配标准]]检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All partial derivatives are evaluated at $(u_1^*,F(u_1^*))$. The two sides are the slopes of the isowelfare curve and the utility frontier in the same utility coordinates. This balances social valuation against feasible utility trade-offs; it is not equality of two consumers' MRS in commodity space. Check the frontier's efficiency interpretation using the [[效用可能性边界|dominance criterion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

切点本身不保证最大值。一个清楚的充分条件是：$W$ 对 $u_2$ 在整个可行域上非递减，且沿完整前沿的函数 $g(s)=W(s,F(s))$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上凹。在这种情况下，内点 $g'(s)=0$ 给出全局最大值；若 $g$ 严格凹，前沿上的最大点唯一。不能只凭前沿看起来向右下倾斜就假定这些条件成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tangency alone does not establish a maximum. A sufficient condition is that $W$ be nondecreasing in $u_2$ throughout the feasible set and that $g(s)=W(s,F(s))$ be concave over the full interval $[a,b]$. An interior stationary point then gives a global maximum; strict concavity makes the maximiser on the frontier unique. A downward-sloping frontier alone does not establish these conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个自设例均取 $0\le u_2\le F(s)$，用直接代入说明必要条件的范围：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The following constructed examples all use $0\le u_2\le F(s)$. Direct substitution shows the scope of the necessary condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **凹目标的内点最大值：** $F(s)=4-s^2$、$s\in[0,2]$，$W=s+u_2$。于是 $g(s)=4+s-s^2$，$g'(s)=1-2s$，得到 $(u_1,u_2)=(0.5,3.75)$，$W=4.25$；$g''=-2$ 使此点为全局最大值。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  **Interior maximum with a concave objective:** with $F(s)=4-s^2$, $s\in[0,2]$, and $W=s+u_2$, we obtain $g(s)=4+s-s^2$. Its derivative vanishes at $(u_1,u_2)=(0.5,3.75)$, giving $W=4.25$. Since $g''=-2$, this is a global maximum.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- **角点最优而不相切：** $F(s)=1-s$、$s\in[0,1]$，$W=2s+u_2$。$g(s)=1+s$ 一直上升，最大值在 $(1,0)$；前沿斜率为 $-1$，社会无差异曲线斜率为 $-2$。端点只能单侧移动，不适用内点等式。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  **Corner optimum without tangency:** with $F(s)=1-s$, $s\in[0,1]$, and $W=2s+u_2$, the function $g(s)=1+s$ increases to its maximum at $(1,0)$. The frontier slope is $-1$, whereas the isowelfare slope is $-2$. Only one-sided movements are feasible at the endpoint, so the interior equality does not apply.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- **非凸例中的切点是沿前沿的最小值：** $F(s)=(1-s)^2$、$s\in[0,1]$，$W=s+u_2$。$g(s)=1-s+s^2$ 在 $s=0.5$ 满足切点等式，却只有 $W=0.75$，低于两个端点的 $W=1$。可行域非凸：可行端点 $(0,1)$、$(1,0)$ 的中点 $(0.5,0.5)$ 高于前沿 $F(0.5)=0.25$。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  **A tangency that minimises welfare along a nonconvex frontier:** with $F(s)=(1-s)^2$, $s\in[0,1]$, and $W=s+u_2$, the function $g(s)=1-s+s^2$ is stationary at $s=0.5$. Welfare is only $0.75$, below the value 1 at either endpoint. The feasible set is nonconvex: the midpoint $(0.5,0.5)$ of feasible endpoints $(0,1)$ and $(1,0)$ lies above $F(0.5)=0.25$.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前沿或社会无差异曲线有折点时，普通导数等式也不适用。实际求解应比较可行端点、折点和各光滑段的候选值；若可行域还有其他限制，也必须一并纳入，而不是只找图上相切的位置。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a kink of the frontier or an isowelfare curve, the ordinary derivative equality is also unavailable. Compare feasible endpoints, kinks, and candidates on each smooth segment, incorporating any additional feasibility constraints rather than searching only for visible tangencies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Avinash Dixit, Princeton ECO 300, Lecture 20, PDF 第 8 页](https://www.princeton.edu/~dixitak/Teaching/MicroLowCalculus/Slides/Lec20.pdf#page=8)：效用空间中的社会福利等值线、前沿及切点图示；前沿并不必然凹。图示的内点解在本卡明确补齐条件。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Supports the utility-space diagram of isowelfare curves and the frontier, including the warning that the frontier need not be concave. This card states the conditions for its interior tangency interpretation.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, §4.2.1–4.2.3，印刷第 137–140 页／PDF 第 151–154 页](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=151)：凹最大化、局部与全局最优及可微一阶条件。本卡先用链式法则将问题化成 $g(s)$，三个数例均为自设并直接验算，不是原书案例。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Supports concave maximisation, local versus global optimality, and differentiable first-order conditions. The chain rule reduces this problem to $g(s)$. All three numerical examples are constructed and checked directly, not copied from the book.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Ted Bergstrom, UCSB, *A Primitive Public Economy*, 印刷第 3–5、8–10 页／PDF 第 4–6、9–11 页](https://faculty.econ.ucsb.edu/~tedb/Courses/UCSBpf/pflectures/chap1.pdf#page=4)：边界与内点的区别、相切但无效率的反例、效用集可能非凸，以及带约束最优条件不能简化为任意切线。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Supports the interior/boundary distinction, inefficient tangencies, possible nonconvexity, and the need for constrained optimality conditions rather than an unrestricted tangency rule.
