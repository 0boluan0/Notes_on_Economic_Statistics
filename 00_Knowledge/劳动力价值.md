# 劳动力价值由再生产劳动力所需生活资料及训练的社会必要劳动决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The value of labour-power is determined by the socially necessary labour required for its reproduction through subsistence and training*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**劳动力价值（value of labour-power）**是再生产[[Marx劳动力|劳动能力]]所需生活资料和相应训练的价值，其量由生产这些条件所需的[[社会必要劳动时间]]决定。日常维持劳动者、补充下一代劳动者，以及形成特定技能所需训练，都是原文讨论的再生产内容。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **value of labour-power** is the value of the means of subsistence and training needed to reproduce [[Marx劳动力|the capacity to work]], measured by the [[社会必要劳动时间|socially necessary labour time]] required to produce these conditions. The text includes daily maintenance, replacement through the next generation and training for particular skills.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“必要生活资料”带有历史和社会条件：气候、社会发展、劳动者阶级形成的生活习惯等，影响正常维持劳动能力需要什么。因此，劳动力价值不能简单缩为勉强存活的食物价值。给定社会和时期的必要生活标准后，相关商品的[[生产率与单位价值|价值变化]]仍会改变劳动力价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Necessary subsistence has historical and social conditions. Climate, social development and the living habits in which the working class developed influence what is needed to maintain normal working capacity. The value of labour-power therefore cannot simply be reduced to the food required for bare survival. Given a society's standard for a period, [[生产率与单位价值|changes in the values]] of the relevant goods can still alter it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假定把生活资料、代际补充和训练等折算为一天平均，合计需要四小时同质社会劳动；一天劳动力价值就相当于四小时。若每小时社会劳动以100个货币单位表达，并且假定工资按劳动力价值支付，则日工资为 $4\times100=400$。四小时是在计算这一天劳动能力的再生产价值；它还没有规定购买者实际使用该能力的工作日长度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose subsistence, generational replacement and training are averaged over a day and together require four hours of homogeneous social labour. A day's labour-power then has a value equivalent to four hours. If each hour of social labour is expressed as 100 monetary units, and wages are assumed equal to the value of labour-power, the daily wage is $4\times100=400$. The four hours measure reproduction of the day's capacity; they do not yet determine how long the buyer actually uses it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
