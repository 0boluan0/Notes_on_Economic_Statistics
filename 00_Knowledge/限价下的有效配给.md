# 限价下按需求曲线计算消费者剩余需要商品分给支付意愿最高的买方

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Calculating consumer surplus from the demand curve under a price ceiling requires allocation to the highest-willingness-to-pay buyers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定质量相同的商品和成交总量，**有效配给**把有限商品配置给支付意愿最高的购买用途，从而最大化这些成交单位的未加权总支付意愿。[[短边成交条件]]只说明能成交多少；即使卖方愿售的商品全部售出，也没有保证获得商品的是这些买方。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a homogeneous good and a given traded quantity, **efficient rationing** allocates the limited units to the uses with the highest willingness to pay, maximizing their unweighted total willingness to pay. [[短边成交条件|The conditions for short-side trading]] determine how much can be traded. Selling every unit offered does not ensure that these buyers receive it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $P_D(q)$ 为按边际支付意愿由高到低排列的反需求曲线，实际成交量为 $Q^T$，统一成交价为 $\bar p$。在有效配给且没有另计的排队、搜索等资源耗费时，普通需求图中的[[消费者剩余]]面积为：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $P_D(q)$ be the inverse demand curve ordering marginal willingness to pay from highest to lowest, with traded quantity $Q^T$ and a uniform transaction price $\bar p$. Under efficient rationing and without additional resource costs from queuing, search, or similar activities, [[消费者剩余|consumer surplus]] is the familiar demand-diagram area:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
CS=\int_0^{Q^T}\bigl[P_D(q)-\bar p\bigr]\,dq.
$$

该式沿用普通需求的 Marshallian 剩余面积；将它解释为精确福利变化，还需另查收入效应等条件，不能仅凭有效配给确认。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the Marshallian surplus area based on ordinary demand. An exact welfare interpretation additionally requires checking conditions such as income effects; efficient rationing alone does not establish it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

抽签、先到先得或关系分配可能把商品给支付意愿较低的人，此时应按**实际获配者**的支付意愿计算；相同租金与成交量可以对应不同消费者剩余。若获得商品还耗费了排队时间或其他资源，应另外计入这些成本，不能仅用低价节省的钱代表净收益。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lotteries, first-come-first-served rules, or personal connections may assign goods to lower-willingness-to-pay buyers. Surplus must then use the willingness to pay of the **actual recipients**; the same price and quantity can produce different consumer surplus. Any time or other resources spent obtaining access must also be counted, so money saved through the lower price alone does not measure the net benefit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“有效”只针对给定数量的未加权支付意愿，不把支付意愿当成分配正当性，也不证明低收入者应当让位。公平、权利和分配权重属于[[政府干预的效率与分配理由|政策评价的独立标准]]；选择何种配给规则必须说明采用的标准。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Efficient” here concerns unweighted willingness to pay at a fixed quantity. It does not establish distributive legitimacy or imply that lower-income people should give way. Equity, rights, and distributional weights are [[政府干预的效率与分配理由|separate policy-evaluation criteria]]; choosing a rationing rule requires stating the criterion used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
