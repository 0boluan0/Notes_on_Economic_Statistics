# ETF是基金份额在交易所交易并以大额创设赎回连接资产池的基金结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An ETF combines exchange trading of fund shares with large-block creation and redemption against the asset pool*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ETF（exchange-traded fund）是一种[[证券投资基金]]：投资者持有基金份额，份额在交易所按市价交易，同时基金通过规定的创设、赎回渠道增减份额。这里的 ETF 指基金型产品；不能仅凭名称，把所有交易所交易票据或商品交易产品都当作同一种基金法律结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An exchange-traded fund is a [[证券投资基金|pooled investment fund]] whose shares trade on an exchange while specified creation and redemption channels expand or contract the outstanding shares. ETF here refers to a fund vehicle; the label alone does not make every exchange-traded note or commodity product the same legal structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两层交易的对象不同。二级市场买卖把已有份额从卖方交给买方；一级市场创设、赎回则把约定的一篮子证券、现金或其组合与大额份额单位交换。在美国 SEC 所述的基金型 ETF 中，通常由获授权参与者（AP）直接与基金进行这类大额交易，零售投资者通常经经纪商在交易所买卖。参与资格、最小单位、现金替代和交收规则须按法域及具体产品核对。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two markets do different things. Secondary trading transfers existing shares between investors; primary creation and redemption exchange specified securities, cash, or both for large blocks of shares. For the U.S. fund ETFs described by the SEC, authorized participants normally transact directly with the fund in these blocks, while retail investors normally trade through brokers on an exchange. Eligibility, block size, cash substitution, and settlement depend on the jurisdiction and product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ETF 不必只持股票，也不必是[[指数基金]]：它可以持有债券等资产，并可采取指数追踪或主动管理策略。“是否开放式”“是否上市交易”“是否追踪指数”是不同维度；美国许多 ETF 的法律形式本身就是开放式投资公司。以资产换取基金份额是资产池的申赎，不是将股票作为担保品后再分割一张债权凭证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ETF need not hold only stocks or be an [[指数基金|index fund]]: it may hold bonds or other assets and use index-tracking or active management. Open-endedness, exchange listing, and index tracking are separate dimensions; many U.S. ETFs are legally open-end investment companies. Exchanging assets for fund shares is a subscription into the pool, not the splitting of a debt claim secured by those stocks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如某 ETF 规定一个创设单位为 5 万份，买入 100 份的二级市场投资者并不因此拥有直接提交一个创设单位的资格。其退出通常是按可成交市价卖出；[[ETF申赎价格联结]]有助于约束[[基金折溢价]]，但不承诺零偏离、即时流动性或本金安全。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a particular ETF sets a creation unit at 50,000 shares, buying 100 shares on the exchange does not itself confer eligibility to submit a creation unit. Exit normally occurs at an executable market price. The [[ETF申赎价格联结|creation–redemption link]] can constrain [[基金折溢价|premiums and discounts]], without promising zero deviations, immediate liquidity, or principal protection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
