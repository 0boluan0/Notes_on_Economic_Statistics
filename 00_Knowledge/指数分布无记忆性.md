# Memorylessness of the exponential distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*指数分布的无记忆性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Memorylessness** means that, after waiting for an event that has still not happened, the remaining waiting time has the same distribution as the original wait. It is a statement about a conditional prediction. It does not mean that the time already spent disappears or that an actual clock resets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**无记忆性**指：已经等待了一段时间，而事件仍未发生时，剩余等待时间与最初等待时间具有相同的分布。这说的是一个条件预测，不是已经花掉的时间消失了，也不是实际时钟重新归零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $T$ has an [[指数分布|exponential distribution]] with rate $\lambda>0$. The event $T>s$ says that at elapsed time $s$ we are still waiting. A further wait longer than $t$ then means $T>s+t$. Using [[条件概率|conditional probability]], the statement is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $T$ 服从速率 $\lambda>0$ 的[[指数分布]]。事件 $T>s$ 表示到了时长 $s$ 时仍在等待；之后还要等超过 $t$，就意味着总等待 $T>s+t$。用[[条件概率]]写出来就是

> [!important] The residual law · 剩余等待的分布
> $$P(T>s+t\mid T>s)=P(T>t)=e^{-\lambda t},
> \qquad s,t\ge0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Equivalently, the conditional distribution of $T-s$ given $T>s$ is $\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda)$. Every conditioning event has positive probability $e^{-\lambda s}$, so the conditional probability is defined for every finite $s$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 等价地，给定 $T>s$ 后，$T-s$ 的条件分布仍为 $\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda)$。每个条件事件的概率 $e^{-\lambda s}$ 都大于零，所以任意有限 $s$ 下的条件概率都有定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The conditional probability is a ratio of tails
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*条件概率就是两个尾概率之比*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nonnegative $s,t$, the event $T>s+t$ is contained in $T>s$. Therefore the numerator in the conditional-probability formula is just $P(T>s+t)$. Substituting the exponential survival function gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $s,t\ge0$，事件 $T>s+t$ 包含在 $T>s$ 中。因此，条件概率公式的分子直接就是 $P(T>s+t)$。代入指数分布的生存函数：

$$
P(T>s+t\mid T>s)
=\frac{P(T>s+t)}{P(T>s)}
=\frac{e^{-\lambda(s+t)}}{e^{-\lambda s}}
=e^{-\lambda t}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The factor for the elapsed time $s$ cancels. Nothing in this calculation asserts that $T$ is independent of the event $T>s$; that event is determined by $T$. The equality concerns the shifted residual duration under that condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

已等待时长 $s$ 对应的因子被约掉了。这个计算没有声称 $T$ 与事件 $T>s$ 独立，后者本来就是由 $T$ 决定的。相等的是：在这个条件下，减去已等待时长之后的剩余时间分布。

> [!example] Waited three hours, then look two hours ahead · 已等三小时，再往后看两小时
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $\lambda=1/2$ per hour, so the original mean wait is two hours. Given that three hours have passed without completion, the probability of needing more than two additional hours is $P(T>5\mid T>3)=e^{-1}\approx0.368$. The conditional mean remaining wait is still two hours, while the conditional mean **total** wait is $E[T\mid T>3]=3+2=5$ hours.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取每小时 $\lambda=1/2$，最初的平均等待为两小时。已过三小时仍未结束时，还需超过两小时的概率为 $P(T>5\mid T>3)=e^{-1}\approx0.368$。条件下的平均剩余等待仍是两小时，但平均**总**等待是 $E[T\mid T>3]=3+2=5$ 小时。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why this property identifies the exponential law
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么这条性质能确定指数分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is also a converse. Consider a waiting time that is strictly positive and finite with probability one, and has $P(T>s)>0$ at every finite $s\ge0$. Within this class, having the displayed memoryless property for **all** $s,t\ge0$ is equivalent to having an exponential distribution with some finite rate $\lambda>0$. We do not need to assume a density in advance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来也成立。考虑一种等待时间：它以概率一严格为正且有限，并且对每个有限的 $s\ge0$ 都有 $P(T>s)>0$。在这个范围内，对**所有** $s,t\ge0$ 满足上述无记忆性，等价于服从某个有限正速率 $\lambda>0$ 的指数分布。不需要预先假定它有密度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $S(t)=P(T>t)$ for its survival function. The conditional equality is equivalent to $S(s+t)=S(s)S(t)$. A survival function is nonincreasing: lasting beyond a longer duration cannot be more likely. Also $S(0)=1$, and finiteness with probability one gives $S(t)\to0$ as $t\to\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $S(t)=P(T>t)$ 记生存函数。条件概率等式等价于 $S(s+t)=S(s)S(t)$。生存函数不会随时间增加，因为等得更久的概率不可能反而更大。还有 $S(0)=1$，而以概率一有限意味着 $t\to\infty$ 时 $S(t)\to0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose one unit of time and put $c=S(1)$. The assumptions give $c>0$. If $c=1$, repeated multiplication would give $S(n)=1$ for every positive integer $n$, contradicting a finite waiting time. Thus $0<c<1$, and we can write $c=e^{-\lambda}$ with $\lambda=-\ln c>0$, using the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选定一个时间单位，令 $c=S(1)$。由假设可知 $c>0$。若 $c=1$，反复使用乘法关系就有每个正整数 $n$ 都满足 $S(n)=1$，与等待时间有限矛盾。所以 $0<c<1$，可以用[[自然对数]]写成 $c=e^{-\lambda}$，其中 $\lambda=-\ln c>0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For positive integers $m,n$, divide one time unit into $n$ pieces and then join $m$ pieces:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正整数 $m,n$，把一个时间单位分成 $n$ 份，再拼起其中 $m$ 份：

$$
S(1)=S(1/n)^n,\qquad
S(m/n)=S(1/n)^m=c^{m/n}=e^{-\lambda m/n}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So the formula holds at every nonnegative rational time. For any real $t\ge0$, place rational numbers $a,b$ arbitrarily close on either side, with $a\le t\le b$. Monotonicity gives $e^{-\lambda b}\le S(t)\le e^{-\lambda a}$. The two bounds approach $e^{-\lambda t}$, so $S(t)=e^{-\lambda t}$ at every real time too. This proves the converse without assuming differentiability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，公式在每个非负有理数时刻都成立。对任意实数 $t\ge0$，取从两侧任意逼近它的有理数 $a,b$，满足 $a\le t\le b$。由单调性，$e^{-\lambda b}\le S(t)\le e^{-\lambda a}$。两侧都趋于 $e^{-\lambda t}$，所以每个实数时刻也都有 $S(t)=e^{-\lambda t}$。这样就证明了逆命题，并未假定可微性。

> [!warning] Most waiting laws retain information about elapsed time · 许多等待分布会保留已等待时间的信息
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $T$ is [[连续均匀分布|uniform on $[0,10]$]], then $P(T>2)=0.8$, but $P(T>8\mid T>6)=(10-8)/(10-6)=0.5$. Knowing that six units have passed changes the chance of waiting two more. This bounded waiting law is not memoryless. A state with zero exit rate instead gives $T=\infty$ almost surely; its survival function is constantly one, so it is excluded by the converse's finite-wait assumption. It is not an ordinary $\operatorname{Exp}(0)$ distribution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $T$ 在 $[0,10]$ 上[[连续均匀分布|均匀分布]]，则 $P(T>2)=0.8$，但 $P(T>8\mid T>6)=(10-8)/(10-6)=0.5$。知道已经过了六个单位，会改变还要等两个单位的概率，所以这种有界等待没有无记忆性。零出口率的状态则给出 $T=\infty$ 几乎必然，生存函数恒为一；逆命题中的“等待有限”假设排除了这种情况，它不是普通的 $\operatorname{Exp}(0)$ 分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a [[CTMC指数停留时间|homogeneous CTMC holding time]], the Markov property and time homogeneity provide the residual-wait equality under the standard jump-process assumptions. The present theorem then identifies the exponential law. A model whose completion rate changes with elapsed age cannot keep that age irrelevant while preserving this same waiting law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[CTMC指数停留时间|时间齐次 CTMC 的停留时间]]，在标准纯跳过程假设下，Markov 性与时间齐次性给出剩余等待等式，本定理再把相应分布识别为指数分布。如果模型中的完成速率会随已等待时长变化，就不能一面忽略这个时长，一面保留同样的等待规律。
