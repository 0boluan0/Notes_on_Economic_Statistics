# The minimal-counterexample method
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*最小反例法与递降*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove that every case works, try to locate the first case that could fail. If failure is indexed by a nonnegative integer, a nonempty set of failures has a least member. That least failure is unusually constrained: all smaller allowed cases already work. A proof uses this information either to show that the chosen case works too, or to force an even smaller failure. Both routes contradict the choice of a least failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
要证明每种情形都成功，可以先问：第一次失败可能在哪里？如果失败情形由非负整数编号，那么非空的失败集合必有最小成员。这个最小失败受到很强的限制：所有更小的合法情形已经成功。证明可以据此推出选中的情形其实也成功，或者逼出一个更小的失败；两条路线都会与“最小失败”的选择矛盾。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Define exactly what would fail
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先把失败条件说清楚*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $P(n)$ be the [[命题与谓词|predicate]] to prove for every $n\in\mathbb N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$. A counterexample is an allowed index at which that statement is false. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
令 $P(n)$ 是希望对每个 $n\in\mathbb N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ 证明的[[命题与谓词|谓词]]。反例是在所述论域中使陈述为假的下标。定义

$$C=\{n\in\mathbb N_0:P(n)\text{ is false}\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This notation means the set of all nonnegative integers satisfying the stated failure condition. In a [[反证法|proof by contradiction]], suppose $C$ is nonempty. The [[良序原理适用域|well-ordering principle]] then gives its least member $c$. Consequently $P(c)$ is false, whereas $P(k)$ is true for every integer $0\le k<c$. The latter fact comes from leastness: any failing smaller $k$ would itself belong to $C$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这个记号表示所有满足所写失败条件的非负整数构成的集合。使用[[反证法]]，先反设 $C$ 非空，再由[[良序原理适用域|良序原理]]取其最小成员 $c$。于是 $P(c)$ 为假，而每个整数 $0\le k<c$ 都满足 $P(k)$。后一事实来自最小性：更小的 $k$ 若也失败，就会同样属于 $C$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The substantive work begins here. One route is to derive $P(c)$ using those smaller successful cases. Another route is to construct a permitted $d<c$ and prove $P(d)$ false. The first contradicts $c\in C$ directly; the second contradicts the minimality of $c$ within $C$. Neither route may assume the conclusion it is trying to establish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
真正需要解决的证明从这里开始。一条路线是利用较小情形成功，推导出 $P(c)$；另一条路线是构造合法的 $d<c$，并证明 $P(d)$ 为假。前者直接与 $c\in C$ 矛盾，后者与 $c$ 在 $C$ 中最小矛盾。两种路线都不能预先假定自己要证明的结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A complete sum proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把求和例子完整证明*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a proof of the summation formula, let $c$ be the least failing index. First verify $c\ne0$. Since $c-1$ is not a counterexample, the formula holds through $c-1$; adding the $c$th term proves it at $c$, contradicting $c\in C$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
证明求和公式时，设 $c$ 是最小失败下标。先验证 $c\ne0$，再利用 $c-1$ 不是反例，得到截至 $c-1$ 的公式成立；加上第 $c$ 项便推出公式在 $c$ 也成立，与 $c\in C$ 矛盾。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To make each step explicit, let $S_n=1+2+\cdots+n$ for $n\ge1$, and define $S_0=0$, the sum with no terms. Our target is $P(n):S_n=n(n+1)/2$. If failures exist, choose the least $c$. Since $S_0=0=0(0+1)/2$, zero is not a failure, so $c\ge1$. Therefore $c-1$ is a permitted, strictly smaller index, and its formula is true. Now
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
逐步写出论证：对 $n\ge1$，令 $S_n=1+2+\cdots+n$；另定义 $S_0=0$，即没有项的和。目标为 $P(n):S_n=n(n+1)/2$。若存在失败下标，选取最小的 $c$。由于 $S_0=0=0(0+1)/2$，零不是反例，所以 $c\ge1$。因此 $c-1$ 是合法且严格更小的下标，其公式成立。于是

$$
\begin{aligned}
S_c&=S_{c-1}+c\\
&=\frac{(c-1)c}{2}+c\\
&=\frac{c(c+1)}2.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This proves $P(c)$, although $c$ was selected because $P(c)$ was false. The assumption of a nonempty failure set is therefore impossible: every allowed index satisfies the formula. Notice that we did not produce a smaller failing index in this presentation; we used the truth of the smaller case to eliminate the proposed first failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这就证明了 $P(c)$，但选取 $c$ 的理由恰是 $P(c)$ 为假。因此，“失败集合非空”的反设不可能成立，公式对全部合法下标成立。注意，这种写法并没有制造更小的失败下标，而是利用较小情形为真，排除了所假设的第一次失败。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The descent route
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*改用更小反例的路线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same idea can also be organized by explicitly descending inside the failure set:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
同一思路也可以组织成在反例集合内部明确递降：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The minimal-counterexample method supposes that the counterexample set $C\subseteq\mathbb N$ is nonempty, uses well ordering to choose its least member $c$, and derives from the failure at $c$ that some $d<c$ also satisfies the original counterexample definition. Then $d\in C$ contradicts the minimality of $c$, so $C$ must be empty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
最小反例法先假设反例集合 $C\subseteq\mathbb N$ 非空，用良序原理取最小反例 $c$，再由 $c$ 的失败推出某个 $d<c$ 也满足原反例定义。于是 $d\in C$ 与 $c$ 的最小性矛盾，故 $C$ 只能为空。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mathbb N$ again means the nonnegative integers, and this paragraph describes the descent route. For the sum example, the calculation already shows that a correct formula at $c-1$ would force a correct formula at $c$. If $c$ really failed, $c-1$ would have to fail too. Once $c\ge1$ is established, $d=c-1$ is thus a smaller member of the same failure set. This is an alternative presentation of the contradiction, not an additional compulsory stage after the previous proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里的 $\mathbb N$ 仍指非负整数，上段描述的是递降路线。在求和例子中，前述计算已经说明：$c-1$ 处的公式若正确，就会迫使 $c$ 处也正确。因此，$c$ 若确实失败，$c-1$ 就必须同样失败。在先证明 $c\ge1$ 后，$d=c-1$ 便是同一反例集中的更小成员。这是矛盾的另一种呈现，并不是前一证明完成后还必须追加的步骤。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When using the descent route, keep the failure condition fixed:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
采用递降路线时，必须始终保持同一个失败条件：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Producing merely a smaller number is insufficient; it must satisfy the same counterexample predicate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
只构造一个更小的数不够；必须证明它仍满足同一个“反例”谓词。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $c$ might be the first point of the domain, then $c-1$ may lie outside it. The proof must handle the base case and establish that the least counterexample lies above the starting point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若 $c$ 可能是论域起点，$c-1$ 可能越界，所以必须先处理基例并证明最小反例大于起点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a domain beginning at an integer $n_0$, use the shifted well-ordering argument and check $c-1\ge n_0$ before using that index. If failures are arbitrary objects, first specify a nonnegative integer size for each object; well ordering selects a least failing size, not a unique failing object. A smaller size is useful only when an actual counterexample of that size has been justified. One cannot simply select a least positive-real failure, since such a set need not have a least member.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
论域若从整数 $n_0$ 开始，可使用平移后的良序论证，并在调用前检查 $c-1\ge n_0$。若失败情形是一般对象，应先为每个对象规定非负整数大小；良序原理选出的是最小失败大小，并不保证这个大小只对应一个失败对象。只有确实论证该大小下存在反例，更小的大小才有用。也不能直接选取最小的正实数反例，因为这样的集合未必有最小成员。

> [!question]- What contradicts leastness? · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Someone chooses a least counterexample $c$, observes that $c-1<c$, and declares a contradiction. What is missing?
>
> **Answer:** They must also prove that $c-1$ is a counterexample to the same proposition, namely $c-1\in C$. A smaller number outside $C$ does not violate the minimality of $c$ within $C$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 某人取最小反例 $c$，随后发现 $c-1<c$，就宣布矛盾。缺少哪一步？
>
> **答案：** 他还必须证明 $c-1$ 也是原命题的反例，即 $c-1\in C$。单有大小关系不会违反 $c$ 在 $C$ 中最小。
