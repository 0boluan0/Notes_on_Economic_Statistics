# 拉氏价格指数比较基期数量篮子在报告期和基期价格下的价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Laspeyres price index compares the value of the base-period quantity basket at current and base-period prices.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拉氏指数把基期数量 $q_{i0}$ 固定在分子、分母两边，仅改变价格。价格和数量口径须一致，分母为正。基值为 $B$ 时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Laspeyres holds the base quantities $q_{i0}$ fixed in both numerator and denominator and changes only prices. Use consistent price and quantity definitions and a positive denominator. With base value $B$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$I_L=B\frac{\sum_i p_{it}q_{i0}}{\sum_i p_{i0}q_{i0}}.$$

两种股票基期价格为 10、20，报告期为 12、18；基期数量为 2、1。旧篮子原来值 40，现在值 42，所以指数为 105。$q$ 可以表示课程题目给出的成交数量，也可以表示另一个应用中的消费数量；“拉氏”指固定数量的时点，不是某种商品专用标签。以发行股数为数量时，同样要区分基期和报告期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With base prices 10 and 20, current prices 12 and 18, and base quantities 2 and 1, the old basket changes in value from 40 to 42: the index is 105. Quantities may be traded shares in the course exercise or consumption quantities elsewhere. “Laspeyres” identifies the quantity reference period, not the kind of item measured.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

改用报告期数量固定两边，就成为[[派氏价格指数]]。二者同一时点的比较不是必然谁大谁小；差异来自价格变化与数量变化的组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fixing current quantities on both sides instead gives the [[派氏价格指数|Paasche index]]. Their ordering depends on how prices and quantities change; neither is unconditionally larger.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
