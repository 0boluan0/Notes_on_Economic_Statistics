# 单看注意力权重不能判定 token 对最终预测的贡献
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Attention weights alone do not determine a token's contribution to the final prediction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[注意力读取|注意力权重]] 描述一次读取中各 value 的加权系数。仅看这些标量，不能判定某个 token 向这一头实际写入多少信息，更不能单独确定它对最终预测的因果作用；还要看被加权的向量、输出变换及后续计算路径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[注意力读取|Attention weights]] are the coefficients used to mix values in one read operation. These scalars alone do not determine a token's vector contribution to that head, much less its causal effect on the final prediction. The weighted vectors, output transformation, and subsequent computation also matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 [[多头注意力]] 中的一头，把它在输出投影 $W_O$ 中对应的分块记作 $W_O^{(h)}$。其输出可写为

$$
o_i^{(h)}=\sum_j a_{ij}^{(h)}\bigl(v_j^{(h)}W_O^{(h)}\bigr).
$$

权重 $a_{ij}^{(h)}$ 相同，向量的大小和方向仍可不同；多个向量还可能抵消。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one head in [[多头注意力|multi-head attention]], let $W_O^{(h)}$ be its block of the output projection. Its contribution has the displayed weighted-vector form. Equal coefficients can multiply vectors with different magnitudes and directions, and vector contributions may cancel.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如固定一头的权重为 $(0.9,0.1)$，投影后的两个一维 value 为 $(0,10)$。输出是 $0.9\times0+0.1\times10=1$：第一项权重更大，却写入零向量。这个例子只比较本次固定计算中的加权项；若真的修改输入 token，Q/K、其他 value 和后续隐状态都可能一起变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a head has fixed weights $(0.9,0.1)$ and projected scalar values $(0,10)$. Its output is $1$: the larger weight multiplies a zero vector. This example compares terms within a fixed computation. Actually changing an input token may also change Q/K, other values, and later hidden states.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使把向量大小也纳入分析，完整模型仍有其他头、[[残差连接]] 和后续层。因此热图适合提出“这个位置可能在读取什么”的假设。若要回答“该 token 是否造成某个预测”，需要明确预测指标和干预方式，并检查干预后的输出；不能把任意重排权重后的结果直接当作原训练模型的解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even an analysis that includes vector magnitudes does not cover other heads, [[残差连接|residual connections]], or later layers. Attention maps can support hypotheses about what a position reads. Claims that a token causes a prediction require a specified output measure and intervention, followed by checking the resulting output. Arbitrarily rearranged weights do not automatically explain the original trained model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的结论是一个解释边界：权重本身不是完整归因。它没有否定经过适当检验的注意力分析可能揭示语法、对齐或模型行为。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a limit on interpretation: weights alone are not a complete attribution. Properly tested attention analyses may still reveal syntax, alignment, or model behavior.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Kobayashi et al. (2020), *Attention is Not Only a Weight: Analyzing Transformers with Vector Norms*](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.574.pdf)，§2.1–2.3、§3：直接区分权重与变换后 value 向量，并在 BERT/Transformer NMT 上研究其影响；支持本卡的加权向量边界。数值例子为独立构造。
- [Wiegreffe and Pinter (2019), *Attention is not not Explanation*](https://aclanthology.org/D19-1002.pdf)，§2.1、§3–4：支持解释定义和模型一致的干预条件。其主要实验是带注意力的 RNN 分类模型，不能据此把所有 Transformer 热图判为有效或无效。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Kobayashi et al., §2.1–2.3 and §3, separates attention weights from transformed value vectors and studies BERT and Transformer NMT. It supports the weighted-vector limitation; the numerical example is independently constructed.
- Wiegreffe and Pinter, §2.1 and §3–4, examines definitions of explanation and model-consistent interventions. Its main experiments use attention-based RNN classifiers and do not settle the validity of every Transformer attention map.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
