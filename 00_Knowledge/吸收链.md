# Absorbing Markov chains
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*吸收链*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **absorbing chain** models a system with permanent endpoint states and a possible route to such an endpoint from every starting state. Think of an order that can end as completed or cancelled. Those endpoints stay fixed; other states must have some route to completion or cancellation. The route may take several steps and need not lead to the same endpoint from every start.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**吸收链**描述这样一种系统：存在进入后不再离开的终点状态，而且从每个起点都有可能走到某个终点。例如，订单最终可以进入“已完成”或“已取消”；终点本身固定，其他状态必须有通往其中某个终点的路线。路线可以不止一步，不同起点也不必通向同一个终点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here we use the standard **finite-state, time-homogeneous** definition. Let $S$ be the nonempty finite state set and $P=(p_{ij})$ the [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|fixed transition rule]] in the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]]. Thus $p_{ij}$ is the next-step probability from $i$ to $j$, every entry is nonnegative, and every row sums to one. Let
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡采用标准的**有限状态、时间齐次**口径。令 $S$ 为非空有限状态集，$P=(p_{ij})$ 是[[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]]下的[[DTMC时间齐次转移核|固定转移规则]]：$p_{ij}$ 是从 $i$ 下一步到 $j$ 的概率，条目非负，每行和为一。令

$$A=\{a\in S:p_{aa}=1\}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
be the set of all [[吸收状态|absorbing states]], each of which retains the chain once entered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即全部[[吸收状态]]构成的集合，每个成员都会在进入后留住这条链。

> [!important] Endpoints and routes from every state · 终点与覆盖每个状态的路线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The chain is absorbing if $A$ is nonempty and every state outside $A$ can reach some state in $A$ along a finite positive-probability route. Equivalently, for every $i\notin A$, there are an $a\in A$ and a positive integer $n$ with
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $A$ 非空，而且每个 $A$ 外的状态都能沿有限正概率路线到达 $A$ 中的某个状态，就称这条链为吸收链。等价地，对每个 $i\notin A$，存在某个 $a\in A$ 和正整数 $n$，使
>
> $$p_{ia}^{(n)}=(P^n)_{ia}>0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The superscript $(n)$ means exactly $n$ transitions. This is [[马氏链沟通类|accessibility]], not a claim of immediate arrival. A route has positive probability when every transition along it has positive probability; multiply those probabilities to obtain the probability of that route.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上标 $(n)$ 表示恰好走 $n$ 步。这是[[马氏链沟通类|可达性]]，没有要求下一步就到。路线上的每次转移概率都为正，整条路线才有正概率；它等于沿途这些概率的乘积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two possible absorbing destinations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*可以有不止一个吸收终点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Order the states as completed, processing, cancelled, labeled $1,2,3$, and take
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按已完成、处理中、已取消排列状态，分别标为 $1,2,3$，取

$$P=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\1/2&0&1/2\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
States 1 and 3 are absorbing because their diagonal entries are one. From state 2, one step reaches either 1 or 3, each with probability $1/2$. Thus $A=\{1,3\}$ and the route condition is satisfied. There is no requirement that all paths end at 1, or that states 1 and 3 can reach each other. Each is its own [[闭沟通类|closed singleton class]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

状态 1、3 的对角元都为一，所以都吸收。从 2 出发，一步就以各 $1/2$ 的概率到达 1 或 3。因此 $A=\{1,3\}$，路线条件成立。定义没有要求全部路径都到 1，也没有要求 1 与 3 互达；它们各自构成一个[[闭沟通类|单点闭类]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A delay does not invalidate the definition. With states $s,c,a$ and transitions $s\to c\to a$, where $a$ stays at itself, $s$ needs two steps to reach the absorbing state. Random self-loops before taking these forward routes can make the wait longer while preserving the positive route. The definition checks whether the route exists, not a prescribed deadline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存在延迟并不会破坏定义。例如，状态按 $s\to c\to a$ 转移，且 $a$ 留在自身，那么 $s$ 需要两步才能到吸收态。在前进之前再加上随机自环，会让等待更久，但只要前进路线仍有正概率，就不破坏可达性。定义检查路线存在，不是在规定期限。

> [!warning] One absorbing state is not enough · 只有一个吸收点还不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In state order $a,b,c$, consider
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 按 $a,b,c$ 排列状态，考虑
>
> $$P=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0\\0&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> State $a$ is absorbing, but $b$ and $c$ alternate forever and never reach it. Their pair is a closed class with internal motion. The whole chain is therefore not an absorbing chain under the stated definition. It is not enough to find a successful route from only some initial states.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $a$ 是吸收状态，但 $b,c$ 永远交替，无法到达 $a$。这两个状态组成内部仍在运动的闭类。因此，按本卡定义，整条链不是吸收链。只从部分初态找到成功路线是不够的。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the other extreme, if every state is absorbing, the chain does satisfy the definition: the second condition has no states left to check. Starting anywhere already means absorption at time zero. Multiple absorbing states also allow different eventual destinations, so absorption must not be confused with convergence to one common state from every start.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一端，若每个状态都吸收，链仍满足定义：第二项条件已经没有待检查的状态。从任意状态开始，都在时刻零吸收。多个吸收态还允许不同的最终去向，因此不能把吸收与所有初态都趋向同一个状态混为一谈。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite homogeneous chain, these route conditions imply [[吸收链必然吸收|eventual absorption with probability one]], and in fact a finite mean waiting time. That conclusion requires a proof; it is not built into the words “positive-probability route.” On a countably infinite state space, the same graph description alone need not imply almost-sure absorption. Keep the finite-state qualifier when using this definition and the later [[吸收链标准形|absorbing-chain block form]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限齐次链，这些路线条件可以推出[[吸收链必然吸收|最终以概率一吸收]]，甚至推出平均等待有限。但这是需要证明的结论，不是“正概率路线”几个字自带的含义。在可数无限状态空间上，相同的图结构条件不足以保证几乎必然吸收。使用本定义与后续[[吸收链标准形]]时，要保留有限状态限定。

## Sources and verification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Grinstead and Snell, Introduction to Probability, Definition 11.1 and Example 11.13, PDF p.424 / printed p.416](https://math.dartmouth.edu/~prob/prob/prob.pdf#page=424): absorbing state versus absorbing chain, nonempty absorbing set, and reachability that may take several steps. The small order and closed-cycle examples are checked directly.
- [Weber, Cambridge Markov Chains, Theorem 2.4 and §2.5, PDF pp.11–12 / printed pp.7–8](https://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/~rrw1/markov/M.pdf#page=11): positive finite routes, accessibility and closed classes. The probability-one consequence is separated into the linked theorem rather than assumed from accessibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Grinstead–Snell 定义11.1及例11.13（PDF424／印刷416页）：核对状态与全链的不同定义、非空吸收集合及允许多步的可达条件；订单与闭循环小例直接核算。
- Weber 定理2.4和§2.5（PDF11–12／印刷7–8页）：核对有限正概率路线、可达性与闭类；概率一的结论单独交由所链定理证明，不把可达性直接当作必然性。
