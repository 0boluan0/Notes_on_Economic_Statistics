# 有效市场假说与事件研究
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Efficient-Market Hypothesis and Event Studies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 判断某个信息集能否持续预测风险调整后的异常回报，并用事件研究检验价格在信息到达前后怎样反应。
> **具体锚点：** 公司公布“很好”的利润却股价下跌，可能因为利润低于市场已定价的更高预期；回报来自结果相对预期的意外部分。
> **核心难点：** 市场有效不是“价格永远等于真值”，而且任何异常回报都必须相对于一个资产定价基准计算，因此有效性与模型被联合检验。
> **为什么重要：** 它决定主动策略、基本面研究、技术规则和监管事件证据应怎样设立可证伪的基准。
> **继续：** 风险调整基准见 [[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本]]；若要解释偏差为何可能持续，读 [[行为金融与套利限制]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It asks whether a specified information set can persistently predict risk-adjusted abnormal returns and uses event studies to test how prices respond before and after information arrives.
> **Concrete anchor:** A company can report strong earnings and still see its share price fall because earnings were below even higher expectations already embedded in price; return depends on the surprise relative to expectations.
> **Central difficulty:** Market efficiency does not mean price always equals a timeless true value, and every abnormal return is calculated relative to an asset-pricing benchmark, so efficiency and the model are tested jointly.
> **Why it matters:** It determines how active strategies, fundamental research, technical rules, and regulatory event evidence should establish falsifiable benchmarks.
> **Continue:** For a risk-adjustment benchmark, see [[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|CAPM, Systematic Risk, and the Cost of Capital]]. To explain why deviations may persist, read [[行为金融与套利限制|Behavioral Finance and Limits to Arbitrage]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 价格、回报与预期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Prices, returns, and expectations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产价格等于对未来现金流和贴现因子的条件期望。价格上涨可能来自现金流预期上调、风险溢价下降或无风险利率下降。观察到高平均回报不自动证明低估，可能是承担了系统风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Asset price is a conditional expectation of future cash flows and discount factors. A price increase can result from higher expected cash flow, a lower risk premium, or a lower risk-free rate. High average return does not automatically prove undervaluation; it may compensate for systematic risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“好消息”必须相对事件前预期定义。若市场预期每股收益 2.00，实际为 1.80，即使同比增长也是负面意外；若市场预期亏损 1.00，实际只亏 0.20，即使利润为负也可能是正面意外。事件研究关注的是意外信息而非新闻形容词。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Good news must be defined relative to pre-event expectations. If the market expected earnings per share of 2.00 and realized EPS is 1.80, year-on-year growth can still be a negative surprise. If the market expected a loss of 1.00 and the loss is only 0.20, negative earnings can still be a positive surprise. Event studies examine unexpected information, not journalistic adjectives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 有效市场的层级
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Forms of market efficiency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

弱式讨论历史价格信息，半强式讨论公开信息，强式连私人信息也包括。可检验命题总是“某信息是否在选定资产定价模型下预测异常回报”，因此有效市场检验常与定价模型联合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weak-form efficiency concerns historical price information, semi-strong efficiency concerns public information, and strong-form efficiency includes private information. The testable proposition is always whether an information set predicts abnormal return under a chosen asset-pricing model, so market-efficiency tests are joint with the pricing model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这三个层级是关于信息集的嵌套命题，不是市场从“差”到“完美”的质量评分。某市场可能对高频公开价格快速反应，却对复杂会计信息反应较慢；必须说明资产、时期、信息和可实施成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three forms are nested propositions about information sets, not a quality score from poor to perfect. A market may react quickly to high-frequency public prices but more slowly to complex accounting information; specify the asset, period, information, and implementable cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 理性预期与信息反应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Rational expectations and information response*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理性预期不要求每个人预测正确，而要求系统误差不被可用信息持续预测。事件研究观察公告前后异常回报；信息泄露、预期已形成和同时事件会影响窗口解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rational expectations do not require every individual forecast to be correct; they require systematic errors not to remain predictably related to available information. Event studies examine abnormal returns around announcements; information leakage, prior anticipation, and concurrent events affect window interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 事件研究的最小步骤
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum steps in an event study*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 定义事件、事件日和样本纳入规则，避免事后挑选。
2. 选定不受事件污染的估计窗口，估计正常回报模型。
3. 计算 $AR_{it}=R_{it}-\widehat{E}(R_{it}\mid X_t)$，并在事件窗累加为 $CAR$。
4. 处理横截面相关、事件聚集、交易停牌、薄交易和多重检验。
5. 用不同窗口、基准模型和样本规则做稳健性检验，并列出同时发生的重大事件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Define the event, event date, and sample-inclusion rules without ex post selection.
2. Choose an estimation window uncontaminated by the event and estimate the normal-return model.
3. Calculate $AR_{it}=R_{it}-\widehat{E}(R_{it}\mid X_t)$ and cumulate it over the event window as $CAR$.
4. Address cross-sectional dependence, clustered events, trading halts, thin trading, and multiple testing.
5. Test robustness across windows, benchmark models, and sample rules, and list major concurrent events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设某股票公告日回报 4%，市场模型基准预测回报 1%，则当日异常回报为 3%。这不是事件“创造了 3% 价值”的充分因果结论：还需确保事件未被提前预期、窗口没有其他公司信息，并检验统计与经济显著性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a stock returns 4% on the announcement day and its market-model benchmark predicts 1%, the day's abnormal return is 3%. That is not sufficient causal proof that the event created 3% of value: the event must not have been fully anticipated, the window must be free of other company news, and both statistical and economic significance must be assessed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 市场异象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Market anomalies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

动量、反转、规模、价值等模式可能来自风险、行为、交易成本、数据挖掘或样本特定时期。发现统计模式后要做外样本、成本、实施和多重检验检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Patterns such as momentum, reversal, size, and value may arise from risk, behavior, transaction costs, data mining, or a sample-specific period. After finding a statistical pattern, test it out of sample and examine costs, implementability, and multiple testing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 因价格后来改变就断言早先价格错误；新信息本来就应改变有效价格。
- 用 CAPM 算异常回报后把显著 alpha 同时称为 CAPM 失败和市场无效，却未区分联合假设。
- 事件窗过长并包含其他消息，却把全部累计回报归给目标事件。
- 在数百个信号中挑出最优历史策略，未校正数据挖掘和成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Inferring that an earlier price was wrong merely because price later changed; new information should change an efficient price.
- Using CAPM to calculate abnormal returns and then calling significant alpha both CAPM failure and market inefficiency without separating the joint hypotheses.
- Attributing all cumulative return in a long event window containing other news to the target event.
- Selecting the best historical strategy from hundreds of signals without correcting for data mining and costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 有效市场是否意味着市场价格等于永恒不变的真实价值？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does an efficient market imply that price equals a timeless, unchanging true value?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不是。新信息会改变合理价格，且有效性是信息不可产生风险调整后可持续超额利润的命题。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. New information changes a reasonable price, and efficiency is a proposition that an information set cannot generate persistent risk-adjusted excess profit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 发现一个历史超额回报策略后应检查什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What should be checked after discovering a historical excess-return strategy?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 风险暴露、交易成本、样本外稳定性、数据挖掘/多重检验和实施容量。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Risk exposures, transaction costs, out-of-sample stability, data mining and multiple testing, and implementation capacity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 好公司为什么不必是好投资？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why need a good company not be a good investment?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 当前价格可能已反映其优点；投资回报取决于未来结果相对市场预期的意外部分和买入价格。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The current price may already reflect its strengths; investment return depends on future outcomes relative to market expectations and on the purchase price.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：什么是市场有效性检验的联合假设问题？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: what is the joint-hypothesis problem in a market-efficiency test?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 异常回报必须先用某个模型定义正常回报；检验拒绝时，可能是市场没有充分反映信息，也可能是正常回报模型错误，单一检验无法无条件区分。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Abnormal return requires a model of normal return. When the test rejects, either the market failed to incorporate information or the normal-return model is wrong; one test cannot distinguish them unconditionally.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
