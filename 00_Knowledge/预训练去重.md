# 预训练去重是按指定比较单位、匹配判据与保留规则识别并移除或降权重复训练样本的方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pretraining deduplication identifies and removes or downweights repeated training examples using a specified comparison unit, matching rule, and retention rule*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> “做过去重”不是一个完整方法说明。预训练去重必须同时给出：比较什么单位、怎样判定匹配、命中后保留或降权哪一项。三者共同决定哪些重复暴露会从训练分布中消失。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Deduplicated” is not a complete method description. Pretraining deduplication must specify the comparison unit, the matching rule, and which matched item is retained or downweighted. Together they determine which repeated exposures disappear from the training distribution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 比较单位
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Comparison units*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 单位 | 比较对象 | 典型盲点 |
|---|---|---|
| URL-level | 同一 URL 的多个抓取版本 | 不同 URL 下的镜像与转载 |
| document-level | 完整文档 | 只复用段落或模板的文档 |
| line-level | 单行或短块 | 跨行、改写后的重复 |
| substring-level | 文档中的连续片段 | 表面不同但意义接近的表达 |

比较单位决定算法能看见哪种重复。document-level MinHash 不会自动发现所有跨文档复用段落，除非管线也在段落或 substring 单位运行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The comparison unit determines which repetition is visible. URL-level rules miss mirrors at different URLs; document-level rules miss reused passages; line-level rules miss cross-line or edited reuse; substring-level rules miss semantically similar rewrites. Document-level MinHash therefore does not automatically remove every repeated paragraph unless the pipeline also operates at that unit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 匹配与保留规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Matching and retention rules*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **exact**：要求字节、字符、token 或哈希完全相同，精确但对微小改动敏感。
- **near**：用 n-gram、MinHash、edit similarity 等表面相似度；判定强度取决于具体阈值方向。
- **semantic**：用表示向量等寻找意义接近的样本；能发现更深冗余，也更容易误合并独立表达。

命中后还要说明保留最新版、随机保留一个、按质量分保留，还是仅降低抽样权重。删除范围扩大并不自动意味着模型更好，这个效果边界单列在 [[去重强度与净收益]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exact matching requires identical bytes, characters, tokens, or hashes. Near matching uses surface similarity such as n-grams, MinHash, or edit distance; the direction of a “stronger threshold” depends on the implementation. Semantic matching uses representations to find meaning-level similarity, reaching deeper redundancy at greater risk of merging independent expression. A pipeline must also state whether it keeps the newest or highest-quality item, chooses one at random, or merely downweights matches. Wider removal is not automatically better; see [[去重强度与净收益]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 最小例子
> 同一文章被 30 个站点转载，每个 URL 都不同。URL-level 去重一个也删不掉；document-level exact 可以删逐字镜像；改过页眉的版本需要 near matching。若只说“用了 MinHash”，仍不知道它比较的是文档还是段落，也不知道命中后保留谁。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> One article is syndicated across 30 distinct URLs. URL-level deduplication removes none; document-level exact matching removes verbatim mirrors; versions with edited headers require near matching. Saying only “we used MinHash” still omits the comparison unit and retention rule.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> “训练集做了全局 document-level near dedup”能否推出指定 benchmark 已经无污染？
>
> **答案：** 不能。语料内部去重比较训练样本彼此；benchmark decontamination 以评测集为参照。两者的参照集合为何不能互换，见 [[语料去重不等于基准去污染]]。

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Lee et al. (2022), *Deduplicating Training Data Makes Language Models Better*](https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.577/)：分别实现 exact substring 与 approximate full-document 去重，直接展示比较单位和匹配规则是两个维度。
- [Grattafiori et al. (2024), *The Llama 3 Herd of Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21783)：第 3.1.1 节分别记录 URL、document-MinHash 与 line-level 去重。
- [Abbas et al. (2023), *SemDeDup*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.09540)：给出基于表示向量与相似阈值的 semantic dedup 原始方法。
