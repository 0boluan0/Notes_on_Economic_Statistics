# 移出劳动不损失产出并不意味着退出者放弃的私人收入为零

[[剩余劳动力|移出劳动造成多少产出损失]]是生产问题；劳动者留在原处能消费多少、离开后放弃多少，是收入分配与选择问题。在家庭分享产出的安排下，即使某人的边际产出为零，他仍可能拥有正的消费份额，因此不能把零边际产出直接当作雇到他的零工资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[剩余劳动力|Output lost when labour leaves]] is a production question. Consumption available to someone who stays, and what they forgo by leaving, depend on distribution and choice. A family member may have zero marginal product but a positive share of consumption, so zero marginal product does not imply that the person can be hired at a zero wage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造一个无地租、按人数平分净产出的家庭：5 名成员分享每期 20 单位消费品，其中一人离开后，农场仍能生产 20。另明确外出者不再分享原产出，不计汇款、迁移成本或其他转移。于是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a constructed household paying no rent and sharing net output equally. Five members share 20 units of consumption goods per period, and output remains 20 when one leaves. Assume explicitly that the departing member no longer receives a share, with no remittances, migration costs or other transfers. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\text{移出造成的产出损失}=20-20=0,
\qquad
\text{离开前可消费份额}=\frac{20}{5}=4.
$$

这个人的私人比较基准是原可消费的 4，不是零；而留守 4 人现在各分到 $20/4=5$。这说明产出不变、私人收益变化和分配变化可以同时发生。若外出者仍获家庭供养，或整个家庭共同选择与分享外出工资，就须重写预算，不能沿用“放弃 4”的结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The person's private benchmark is the previous consumption share of 4, not zero, while each remaining member now receives $20/4=5$. Unchanged output can coexist with changing private returns and distribution. Continued family support or joint household choice and sharing of outside earnings require a different budget; the conclusion that 4 is forgone cannot simply be retained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Lewis 用生计部门可得收入解释资本主义工资的下限；在自有农场且分享产出的情形，平均可得产出可以充当这个基准，有地租等扣除时则不是毛平均产出。本例按剩余人数重新平分，所以基准可以上升；[[农业短缺点与商业化点]]的教学版本另固定制度收入 $b$，两者是不同收入规则。判断[[无限劳动供给|工资能否保持水平]]时必须说明采用哪一种。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lewis uses earnings outside capitalist employment to explain a wage floor. On an owner-operated farm sharing output, average available product can provide that benchmark; with rent or other deductions, gross average product need not do so. Equal sharing among fewer people raises the benchmark in this example. The teaching version in [[农业短缺点与商业化点|agricultural shortage and commercialization points]] instead fixes institutional income $b$. These are different income rules, which must be specified when assessing [[无限劳动供给|a horizontal wage supply schedule]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Lewis（1954），[UT Austin 托管全文重排版](https://la.utexas.edu/users/hcleaver/368/368lewistable.pdf#page=6)，PDF 第 6–7 页 §3：原部门收入下限、自有农场平均产出、地租与工资差；第 19 页 §8：产出不变但分享人数减少可提高人均收入。5 人分享 20 的例子及退出后的分享规则为独立教学构造。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lewis supports outside earnings, the owner-farm benchmark, rent qualifications and rising average income when fewer people share unchanged output. The five-person example and its post-exit sharing rule are constructed for teaching.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
