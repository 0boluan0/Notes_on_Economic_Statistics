# 趋势线是连接选定同向转折点并向未来延伸的价格参考直线

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A trend line is a price-reference line joining selected directional turning points and extended forward in time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

趋势线是在价格—时间图上连接选定转折点、并向未来延伸的直线。上升趋势线连接相继抬高的低点，作为候选支撑；下降趋势线连接相继降低的高点，作为候选阻力。两个不同时间的点即可确定直线，后续接近或触及是对这条既定线的进一步观察。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A trend line joins selected turning points on a price–time chart and extends forward. A rising line joins higher lows as potential support; a falling line joins lower highs as potential resistance. Two points at different times determine the line; subsequent approaches or touches provide additional observations of that fixed line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在固定线性价格坐标下，锚点为 $(t_1,P_1)$、$(t_2,P_2)$ 且 $t_2>t_1$ 时，参考价为：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a fixed linear price scale, anchors $(t_1,P_1)$ and $(t_2,P_2)$ with $t_2>t_1$ define:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
L(t)=P_1+\frac{P_2-P_1}{t_2-t_1}(t-t_1).
$$

应声明采样周期、价格轴、锚点选择与触及容差。若锚点需后续行情确认，直线只有在该信息可得后才能用于判断；事后重画线不能替代历史当时版本，见[[预测时点与信息集]]。突破可以触发重新评估或[[支阻转换]]，但不保证主要趋势反转。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify sampling, axis scale, anchor selection and touch tolerance. When a turning point needs later prices for confirmation, its line becomes usable only after that information arrives; a revised line cannot replace the historical version. See the [[预测时点与信息集|information-set boundary]]. A breach may prompt reassessment or [[支阻转换|role reversal]], but does not guarantee a primary-trend reversal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
