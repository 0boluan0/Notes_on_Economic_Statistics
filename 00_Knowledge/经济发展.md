# 经济发展是经济能力与社会结构改善并扩大人们生活机会的长期过程

经济发展关心一个经济体能生产什么、怎样组织生产，以及这些变化能否让人们过上更好的生活。产出增长是其中的重要部分；健康、教育、获得基本服务的机会和摆脱贫困的能力，也是判断发展结果时要看的内容。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Economic development concerns what an economy can produce, how production is organized, and whether these changes improve people's lives. Output growth is an important part of this process. Health, education, access to basic services, and the ability to escape poverty also matter when assessing its outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里要区分两种提问。[[实际GDP增长]]问的是“扣除价格变化后，生产增加了多少”；发展问题还会追问“增加的资源变成了谁的收入、哪些服务和什么生活机会”。后一个问题不能由一个增长率直接回答，具体的分配边界见[[增长与减贫]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[实际GDP增长|Real GDP growth]] asks how much production increased after removing price changes. Development also asks whose income, which services, and what opportunities the additional resources support. A growth rate alone cannot answer this second question; see [[增长与减贫|growth and poverty reduction]] for the distributional boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一个地区新建了诊所，这是供给能力的增加；诊所是否有医生、穷人能否负担交通和诊费、可避免的疾病是否减少，才把这项投入接到生活结果上。投入、可使用的服务和最终结果应当分开观察，不能把建成一栋楼直接当成健康改善。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Building a clinic adds capacity. Staffing, affordable travel and treatment, and fewer avoidable illnesses connect that investment to people's lives. Inputs, usable services, and final outcomes therefore need separate observation: completing a building does not itself establish improved health.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[人类发展指数]]用健康、教育和收入提供一个简明的结果摘要，但没有穷尽“发展”：它不能替代对机会分布、制度或环境的具体考察。使用指标时，应先说明自己要判断发展的哪一面。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[人类发展指数|Human Development Index]] summarizes health, education, and income, but does not exhaust the meaning of development. It cannot replace direct examination of opportunities, institutions, or the environment. Specify which aspect of development an indicator is meant to assess.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
