# 两个函数可以渐近同阶而它们的比值没有极限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Two functions can have the same asymptotic order without a convergent ratio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对最终正函数，[[大Θ记号|同阶]] $f=\Theta(g)$ 只要求 $f/g$ 最终有正下界和有限上界。**比值趋向一个正的有限常数是同阶的充分条件，但不是必要条件。**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For eventually positive functions, [[大Θ记号|equal order]] $f=\Theta(g)$ only requires an eventual positive lower bound and finite upper bound on $f/g$. **A finite positive ratio limit is sufficient for equal order, but not necessary.**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $n$ 为正整数，取
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For positive integers $n$, take
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
g(n)=n,\qquad f(n)=\bigl(2+(-1)^n\bigr)n.
$$

因为 $n\le f(n)\le3n$，两者同阶。但偶数子序列上 $f/g=3$，奇数子序列上 $f/g=1$，所以比值没有极限，更不满足[[渐近等价]] $f\sim g$。这个例子始终为正，失败来自振荡，不是除以零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $n\le f(n)\le3n$, the functions have the same order. Yet the ratio is three on even inputs and one on odd inputs, so it has no limit and certainly does not satisfy [[渐近等价|asymptotic equivalence]] $f\sim g$. Both functions remain positive: oscillation, not division by zero, causes the failure of convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

遇到振荡函数时，先寻找双侧常数界；普通极限失败只是“这个快捷判别不可用”，不是“同阶失败”。若比值能任意接近零而仍有上界，则上界可能保留而同阶失败，见 [[严格大O不等于小o]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For oscillating functions, first seek two-sided constant bounds. Failure of the ordinary limit only disables that shortcut; it does not disprove equal order. A ratio that approaches zero along a subsequence while staying bounded above may retain big O but lose Theta; see [[严格大O不等于小o|strict big O versus little o]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
