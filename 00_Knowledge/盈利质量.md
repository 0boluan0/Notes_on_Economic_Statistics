# 盈利质量是利润反映真实经营表现并支持未来持续盈利判断的程度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Earnings quality is the extent to which earnings reflect economic performance and inform judgments about sustainable future profitability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

盈利质量评价的是利润“如何产生、能说明什么”，而不只是利润数额大小。本页采用财务分析语境：先确认报告是否忠实，再判断利润来自持续活动还是偶发项目、相应回报是否足以支撑企业的长期经营。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Earnings quality concerns how profit is generated and what it reveals, not merely its size. In this financial-analysis usage, first assess faithful reporting, then examine persistence and whether the underlying returns support the business over time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如甲、乙本期净利润都为 100。甲来自有证据支持的正常业务；乙的正常业务亏损 20，另有一次性诉讼收益 120。若两者都准确披露，乙不一定“做假账”，但把其 100 原样外推到下一期的依据更弱。报告质量良好与经营结果可持续，是相连但不同的判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose both firms report profit of 100. One earns it through evidenced recurring operations; the other has an operating loss of 20 offset by a one-off legal gain of 120. Accurate disclosure does not make the latter fraudulent, but provides less support for repeating the total profit. Faithful reporting and sustainable results are connected but distinct judgments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金转换、应计项目和一次性损益是线索，不存在普适的“CFO/净利润超过某数就高质量”定律。[[利润增长不等于现金增长]]；增长阶段的合理营运资本投入也会造成短期差额。实际应用见[[盈利质量核查]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash conversion, accruals, and one-offs are evidence, not a universal CFO-to-profit cutoff. [[利润增长不等于现金增长|Profit and cash growth differ]], and legitimate working-capital investment can create temporary gaps. See [[盈利质量核查|earnings-quality checks]] for application.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
