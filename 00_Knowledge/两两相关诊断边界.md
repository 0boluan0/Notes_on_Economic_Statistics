# Limits of pairwise correlation diagnostics
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两两相关诊断的边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pairwise correlation screen asks whether one predictor is almost a straight-line copy of another. [[多重共线性|Multicollinearity]] asks a broader question: can a column be reproduced, or nearly reproduced, by a combination of several columns? Looking only for a large individual correlation can miss that joint relationship.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两两相关筛查问的是：一个解释变量是否几乎是另一个的直线式复制。[[多重共线性]]问得更广：某列能否被其他几列的组合复现，或近似复现？如果只寻找很大的单个相关系数，就可能漏掉这种联合关系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For finite observed nonconstant columns $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$ and $b=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$, let $\bar a,\bar b$ be their sample means. The sample version of [[相关系数|Pearson correlation]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限观察中的非恒定列 $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$、$b=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$，用 $\bar a,\bar b$ 表示样本均值。[[相关系数|Pearson相关系数]]的样本形式为

$$
r(a,b)=
\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(a_i-\bar a)(b_i-\bar b)}
{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(a_i-\bar a)^2\sum_{i=1}^{n}(b_i-\bar b)^2}}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sums of squared deviations in the denominator must be positive. A constant column, including an intercept, has no ordinary Pearson correlation. A correlation with absolute value near 1 indicates a near-affine relationship between two columns; checking only positive correlations also misses near-negative copies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分母中的两项偏差平方和必须为正。常数列，包括截距，没有普通Pearson相关系数。绝对值接近 1 的相关提示两列近似满足仿射关系；如果只看正相关，还会漏掉近似互为相反数的列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A direct counterexample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个直接反例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider four observations with three predictor columns. Each column has mean zero:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑四个观察的三条解释变量列，每列均值都为零：

| Observation / 观察 | $x_1$ | $x_2$ | $x_3$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | $-1$ | $-1$ | $-2$ |
| 2 | $-1$ | $1$ | $0$ |
| 3 | $1$ | $-1$ | $0$ |
| 4 | $1$ | $1$ | $2$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $x_3=x_1+x_2$ at every row. The third column contributes no new linear direction, so the [[设计矩阵|design]] with columns $1,x_1,x_2,x_3$ does not have [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full column rank]]. Yet $\sum x_1x_2=0$, $\sum x_1^2=\sum x_2^2=4$, $\sum x_3^2=8$ and $\sum x_1x_3=\sum x_2x_3=4$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每行都有 $x_3=x_1+x_2$，第三列没有提供新的线性方向，所以含 $1,x_1,x_2,x_3$ 的[[设计矩阵|设计]]不[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]。但有 $\sum x_1x_2=0$、$\sum x_1^2=\sum x_2^2=4$、$\sum x_3^2=8$，以及 $\sum x_1x_3=\sum x_2x_3=4$，因此

$$
r(x_1,x_2)=0,\qquad
r(x_1,x_3)=r(x_2,x_3)=\frac{4}{\sqrt{4\cdot8}}
=\frac1{\sqrt2}\approx0.707.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rule that flags only absolute correlations above 0.8 would miss this exact dependence. The numerical cutoff is just a counterexample to that screen, not a proposed diagnostic threshold.

This is the finite-data counterpart of the population example: if centered random variables $X_1,X_2$ have equal **positive finite variance**, zero covariance, and $X_3=X_1+X_2$, the two correlations with $X_3$ are again $1/\sqrt2$. Zero or infinite variances would not justify that calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若规则只标记绝对相关大于0.8的列对，就会漏掉这条精确依赖。这里的0.8只用来反驳该筛查规则，不是建议采用的诊断阈值。

它也对应一个总体例子：若中心化随机变量 $X_1,X_2$ 的方差相同且**有限、严格为正**，协方差为零，并令 $X_3=X_1+X_2$，则两者与 $X_3$ 的相关也都是 $1/\sqrt2$。零方差或无穷方差不能支持这项计算。

> [!example] Unique coefficients can still have a weak direction / 系数唯一时仍可能存在弱方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the four-row table, let $w=(1,-1,-1,1)$ and replace $x_3$ by $x_3^{*}=x_1+x_2+\delta w$, where $\delta$ is a small nonzero number. The vector $w$ has mean zero and is orthogonal to $x_1,x_2$, so $1,x_1,x_2,x_3^{*}$ are linearly independent. However, the combination $x_3^{*}-x_1-x_2=\delta w$ is small.
>
> The pairwise correlations with $x_3^{*}$ are $1/\sqrt{2+\delta^2}$, still near 0.707. Regressing $x_3^{*}$ on $1,x_1,x_2$ instead leaves residual $\delta w$: its residual sum of squares is $4\delta^2$ versus total sum $8+4\delta^2$. Thus its [[方差膨胀因子|VIF]] is $(2+\delta^2)/\delta^2$, which is 201 at $\delta=0.1$. This four-row example illustrates design algebra only: four coefficients leave zero residual degrees of freedom, so it cannot supply the usual residual estimate of error variance.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在四行表中令 $w=(1,-1,-1,1)$，把第三列改为 $x_3^{*}=x_1+x_2+\delta w$，其中 $\delta$ 是很小的非零数。$w$ 均值为零，且与 $x_1,x_2$ 正交，因此 $1,x_1,x_2,x_3^{*}$ 线性无关；但组合 $x_3^{*}-x_1-x_2=\delta w$ 很小。
>
> 它与前两列的两两相关都为 $1/\sqrt{2+\delta^2}$，仍在0.707附近。改为把 $x_3^{*}$ 对 $1,x_1,x_2$ 回归，残差就是 $\delta w$，残差平方和为 $4\delta^2$，总平方和为 $8+4\delta^2$。所以其[[方差膨胀因子|VIF]] 为 $(2+\delta^2)/\delta^2$，$\delta=0.1$ 时等于201。这个四行例子只说明设计代数：四个系数使残差自由度为零，不能用它计算通常的残差误差方差估计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use the whole relationship, not just the largest entry

Pairwise plots and correlations remain useful first checks. To inspect joint dependence, examine the full design's rank, each relevant auxiliary regression, and [[设计矩阵条件数|singular directions under a specified scaling]]. Explain which column combination is weak and what the associated coefficients mean. In the exact example, auxiliary $R^2=1$ signals redundancy; a finite VIF formula is no longer available.

The full [[相关矩阵|correlation matrix]] contains all centered inner products after scaling. Its eigenvalues or rank can reveal joint dependence; the limitation is inspecting its entries one at a time. If all the nonconstant columns on the same complete rows are pairwise exactly uncorrelated, their centered columns are orthogonal and hence linearly independent. The claim here is that merely “not very high” pairwise correlations are insufficient, not that the full correlation matrix carries no joint information.

Finally, these calculations concern observed predictor columns. They do not test the relationship between predictors and an unobserved target-equation error, so they do not diagnose [[共线性与内生性|endogeneity]]. Nor does [[零协方差不推独立|zero Pearson correlation establish stochastic independence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 看完整关系，而不只看最大一个数

两两散点与相关仍是有用的初步检查。要检查联合依赖，还应看整个设计的秩、相关的辅助回归，以及[[设计矩阵条件数|在指定缩放下的奇异方向]]，并解释哪一个列组合较弱、对应系数代表什么。精确例子中，辅助回归 $R^2=1$ 表示冗余，有限VIF公式已不能使用。

完整[[相关矩阵]]包含缩放后的全部中心化内积；看其特征值或秩，可以发现联合依赖。局限在于把各个元素单独筛一遍。若所有非恒定列都使用同一组完整观察，而且彼此样本相关严格为零，则中心化后的列正交，因而线性无关。本卡说的是“每一对都不太高”不充分，不是说完整相关矩阵没有联合信息。

最后，这些计算只涉及已观察的解释变量列，没有检验它们与未观察目标方程误差的关系，因此不能诊断[[共线性与内生性|内生性]]。[[零协方差不推独立|Pearson相关为零，也不证明随机独立]]。
