# 没有任何接近一的两两相关也不能排除三个或更多设计列共同形成的线性依赖
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The absence of any pairwise correlation near one does not rule out a linear dependence involving three or more design columns*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 诊断边界
> 相关矩阵一次只看两列。多重共线性却允许一列接近**若干列的组合**，因此“每一对都不算特别高”不能证明整个设计矩阵有充分的独立方向。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A correlation matrix examines two columns at a time. Multicollinearity allows one column to be close to a combination of several others, so modest pairwise correlations do not prove that the full design contains enough independent directions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个直接反例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A direct counterexample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设已中心化的 $X_1$ 与 $X_2$ 相互不相关、方差相同，并令
$$
X_3=X_1+X_2.
$$
三列精确线性相关，但
$$
\operatorname{Corr}(X_1,X_3)
=\operatorname{Corr}(X_2,X_3)
=\frac1{\sqrt2}\approx0.707,
$$
没有一对相关达到接近 1。给等式加入很小噪声，就得到同样会漏检的近似依赖。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let centered $X_1$ and $X_2$ be uncorrelated with equal variances and define $X_3=X_1+X_2$. The three columns are exactly dependent, yet each correlation between $X_3$ and a component is only $1/\sqrt2\approx0.707$. Adding a small noise term gives a near dependence with the same diagnostic problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两两相关仍有价值：它能快速定位明显的二列关系，并帮助理解变量含义。但完整诊断还要看辅助回归 VIF、秩、缩放后的奇异值或条件指标，并检查具体的弱线性组合。任何一个标量都不能替代这一步语义解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pairwise correlations remain useful for quickly locating obvious two-column relations and understanding variable meanings. A full diagnosis also needs auxiliary-regression VIFs, rank, scaled singular values or condition indices, and inspection of the weak linear combination itself. No scalar replaces that semantic interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 所有两两相关都低于 0.8，能否据此宣布没有多重共线性？
>
> **答案：** 不能。三列以上的线性组合仍可能近似为零；还要检查多变量辅助回归或矩阵方向。

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Penn State STAT 501, Lesson 12.4](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat501/Lesson12)：明确说明两两相关可能漏掉三个或更多变量的线性依赖，并给出 $X_3=2X_1+5X_2+error$ 型例子。
- [NIST Dataplot, “Regression Diagnostics”, Multi-collinearity](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman1/auxillar/regrdiag.htm)：核对 pairwise collinearity 不能发现 higher-order multicollinearity。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Penn State explicitly notes that pairwise correlations can miss a dependence involving three or more predictors. NIST independently distinguishes pairwise from higher-order multicollinearity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
