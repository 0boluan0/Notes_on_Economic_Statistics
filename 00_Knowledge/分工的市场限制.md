# 专业化能否维持取决于能否把超出自用的产出交换成所需生活资料

一个人把工作集中到某种产品上，通常会生产出远超过自己需要的数量。他必须把多出来的产出换成食物、衣物等其他物品，才能继续专业生产。因此，Smith 所说的“[[分工]] 受市场范围限制”，首先是说专业化需要足够的交换机会，而不是只看一个地方有多大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A person specialising in one product will usually make far more of it than they personally need. To continue specialising, they must exchange that surplus for food, clothing and other goods. Smith's claim that [[分工|division of labour]] is limited by the extent of the market therefore concerns opportunities for exchange, not geographical area alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Smith 用偏远乡村的制钉匠说明这一点：如果每天能做1,000枚钉子，一年工作300天，就能做300,000枚；但当地连1,000枚也难以卖完，这个工人就不能靠全年只制钉生活。问题不在于他不会制钉，而在于足够多的产出不能换回他需要的其他东西。他可能只在有人需要时制钉，其余时间兼做别的工作。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith illustrates the constraint with a nail maker in a remote village. At 1,000 nails a day over 300 working days, annual capacity would be 300,000 nails. If the locality cannot absorb even 1,000, making nails throughout the year cannot support the worker. The limitation is not a lack of skill but an inability to exchange enough output for other needs. The worker may make nails when required and perform other jobs for the rest of the time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交通改善为什么有帮助？因为运费较低时，原本不值得运到外地的产品也可能卖出去。Smith 以水运相对陆运的成本优势说明：同一处生产活动可以面对更多买者，才有机会容纳更专门的工种。附近地区的收入、人口，以及实际能否运抵买者，都会影响这个交换范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Transport improvements help because lower freight costs can make sales to distant buyers worthwhile. Smith uses water transport's cost advantage over land transport to explain how a producer can reach a wider market and sustain more specialised occupations. Incomes, population and practical access to buyers all affect that scope for exchange.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以，市场扩大可以为 [[分工增产机制|更细分工带来的生产率改善]] 创造条件，但不等于需求一增加，所有细分都值得采用。还要有能衔接的生产安排，以及 [[分工的预付条件|售出以前维持生产所需的资源]]。市场范围回答“产出能换到什么”，预付条件回答“在交换完成前靠什么生产”，两者不是同一个约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A larger market can enable [[分工增产机制|productivity gains from finer division of labour]], without making every subdivision worthwhile. Production must remain coordinated, and [[分工的预付条件|resources must support it before sales are completed]]. Market extent concerns what output can be exchanged for; advance provision concerns how production continues until that exchange. They are different constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Smith，[《国富论》第一篇第三章](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=6)，第1–4段：分别支持交换范围约束、乡村制钉匠、水运与运输成本，以及邻近地区财富和人口对市场范围的影响。300,000枚/年为1,000枚/日×300日/年的核算；兼做其他工作是结合原文乡村多工种劳动的解释。
- [第二篇导论](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=15)，第2–4段：支持售出前资源条件；完整解释复用 [[分工的预付条件]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Book I, Chapter III, paragraphs 1–4 supply the exchange constraint, village nail-maker example, transport mechanism and local-market determinants. Annual output is calculated as 1,000 times 300. The discussion of other work draws on Smith's account of workers combining occupations. Paragraphs 2–4 of Book II's introduction supply the separate advance-provision condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
