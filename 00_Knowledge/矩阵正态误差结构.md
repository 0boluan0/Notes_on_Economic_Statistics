# Matrix-normal errors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*矩阵正态误差结构*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose each student has a mathematics score and a reading score. A prediction model gives a mean for each score; the remaining error is the difference between the random score and that mean. There are two possible directions of association: two scores from the same student can move together, and errors from different students can move together. A **matrix-normal error model** describes both directions while placing a joint Gaussian distribution on the entire error table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设每名学生都有数学与阅读成绩，预测模型给出各项成绩的均值；随机成绩减去这个均值，就是误差。这里有两个可能的联系方向：同一学生的两项成绩可能共同变化，不同学生的误差也可能共同变化。**矩阵正态误差模型**同时描述这两个方向，并给整张误差表规定联合 Gaussian 分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In [[多响应线性回归|multivariate linear regression]], write $Y=XB+U$. There are $n\ge1$ observations, $q\ge1$ responses, and $k\ge1$ encoded regressors, including an intercept if used: $Y,U$ are $n\times q$, the design $X$ is $n\times k$, and the fixed coefficient matrix $B$ is $k\times q$. The mean table is $M=XB$. The [[多元数据矩阵|data-matrix convention]] places observations in rows and responses in columns. Here $U$ means deviation from the model mean, not the fitted [[多响应残差与误差SSP|residual matrix]] $Y-X\hat B$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[多响应线性回归]]中，写成 $Y=XB+U$。设有 $n\ge1$ 个观测、$q\ge1$ 个响应，以及 $k\ge1$ 个已编码回归项（如有截距，也计入其中），则 $Y,U$ 为 $n\times q$，设计矩阵 $X$ 为 $n\times k$，固定系数矩阵 $B$ 为 $k\times q$，均值表为 $M=XB$。[[多元数据矩阵]]采用“观测为行、响应为列”的约定。这里的 $U$ 是相对于模型均值的误差，不是拟合后的[[多响应残差与误差SSP|残差矩阵]] $Y-X\hat B$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Turn the table into one Gaussian vector
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把整张表看成一个 Gaussian 向量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Treat $X$ as fixed. For a random design, the following is instead an explicitly assumed [[条件分布|conditional model]] given its realised value; it does not by itself specify the unconditional distribution after averaging over $X$. All distributions and covariances below use this same conditioning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把 $X$ 看作固定。若设计本身随机，下面就是给定其实现值后明确假设的[[条件分布|条件模型]]；它本身没有规定再对 $X$ 平均后的无条件分布。下文所有分布与协方差都使用这一共同条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The operator $\operatorname{vec}$ **stacks columns**, without changing any values. For a two-by-two table,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\operatorname{vec}$ 的操作是**按列堆叠**，只重新排列，不改变数值。对一个二行二列表，

$$
U=\begin{bmatrix}U_{11}&U_{12}\\U_{21}&U_{22}\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\operatorname{vec}(U)=(U_{11},U_{21},U_{12},U_{22})^T.
$$

> [!important] Definition and convention · 定义与约定
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the nonsingular model, choose real symmetric [[正定矩阵|positive-definite]] matrices $\Omega\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ and $\Sigma\in\mathbb R^{q\times q}$. They are covariance factors across observations and responses, respectively. The notation
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在非退化模型中，取实对称[[正定矩阵]] $\Omega\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ 与 $\Sigma\in\mathbb R^{q\times q}$，分别作为观测方向、响应方向的协方差因子。记号
>
> $$
> U\mid X\sim MN_{n,q}(0,\Omega,\Sigma)
> \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> \operatorname{vec}(U)\mid X\sim N_{nq}(0,\Sigma\otimes\Omega).
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Equivalently, $Y\mid X\sim MN_{n,q}(XB,\Omega,\Sigma)$. The right side is one [[联合Gaussian|joint Gaussian distribution]] on all $nq$ entries, not merely a statement that each entry is individually normal. The symbol $\otimes$ is a **Kronecker product**: replace each entry $\Sigma_{ab}$ by the block $\Sigma_{ab}\Omega$. Its size is $nq\times nq$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 等价地，$Y\mid X\sim MN_{n,q}(XB,\Omega,\Sigma)$。右边要求全部 $nq$ 个元素一起服从[[联合Gaussian|联合 Gaussian 分布]]，不是只要求每个元素各自正态。符号 $\otimes$ 表示 **Kronecker 积**：把 $\Sigma$ 中每个元素 $\Sigma_{ab}$ 替换成整块 $\Sigma_{ab}\Omega$，结果尺寸为 $nq\times nq$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The factor order follows the stacking order. Column stacking puts all observations of response 1 first, then all observations of response 2, so the covariance blocks are indexed by response pairs. Stacking rows instead gives $\Omega\otimes\Sigma$ with a correspondingly permuted vector. These are two coordinate conventions for the same model; they cannot be interchanged while keeping the vector unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因子顺序由堆叠顺序决定。逐列堆叠先放响应 1 的全部观测，再放响应 2 的全部观测，因此协方差的整块由响应对来编号。若改为逐行堆叠，就得到 $\Omega\otimes\Sigma$，同时向量也必须按相应顺序重排。这是同一模型的两种坐标约定，不能保持向量不变却交换因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read one covariance at a time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一次读懂一个协方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For row indices $i,j$ and response indices $a,b$, the block definition gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $i,j$ 为观测行编号，$a,b$ 为响应编号；根据分块定义，

$$\operatorname{Cov}(U_{ia},U_{jb}\mid X)=\Omega_{ij}\Sigma_{ab}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus the two factors multiply to give an actual [[协方差|covariance]]. The covariance of row $i$, viewed as a $q$-dimensional column vector, is $\Omega_{ii}\Sigma$; that of response column $a$ is $\Sigma_{aa}\Omega$. In particular, $\Sigma$ itself is the within-row covariance when $\Omega_{ii}=1$. The factors are not separately fixed by their product: their interpretation and scale convention are developed in [[观测行协方差|the row-covariance factor]]. Neither factor is the covariance of the coefficient estimate $\hat B$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个因子相乘，才得到实际的[[协方差]]。把第 $i$ 行看成 $q$ 维列向量，其协方差为 $\Omega_{ii}\Sigma$；第 $a$ 个响应列的协方差为 $\Sigma_{aa}\Omega$。特别地，只有 $\Omega_{ii}=1$ 时，$\Sigma$ 本身才是该行的协方差。乘积不能分别固定两个因子的尺度，具体含义与规范见[[观测行协方差]]。它们都不是系数估计量 $\hat B$ 的协方差。

> [!example] Two students, two scores · 两名学生，两项成绩
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $\Omega=\begin{bmatrix}1&1/2\\1/2&1\end{bmatrix}$ and $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}4&2\\2&9\end{bmatrix}$. Each student's mathematics error has variance $4$, reading error variance $9$, and their within-student covariance is $2$. Across students, the mathematics errors have covariance $(1/2)4=2$, while student 1's mathematics error and student 2's reading error have covariance $(1/2)2=1$. The model distinguishes the two directions even when two numerical covariances happen to agree.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $\Omega=\begin{bmatrix}1&1/2\\1/2&1\end{bmatrix}$、$\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}4&2\\2&9\end{bmatrix}$。每名学生的数学误差方差为 $4$、阅读误差方差为 $9$，同一学生两者的协方差为 $2$。跨学生的数学误差协方差为 $(1/2)4=2$；学生 1 的数学误差与学生 2 的阅读误差协方差为 $(1/2)2=1$。即使两个协方差数值碰巧相等，它们连接的方向也不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Independent errors need not mean identically distributed responses
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*误差独立同分布，不等于响应行同分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Setting $\Omega=I_n$, the identity matrix, makes distinct error rows have zero cross-covariance. Because the entire error table is jointly Gaussian, the [[联合高斯独立判据|Gaussian independence criterion]] makes the rows mutually independent. Every error row also has mean zero and covariance $\Sigma$, so these error vectors are [[独立同分布|i.i.d.]] $N_q(0,\Sigma)$ in the stated conditional model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取单位矩阵 $\Omega=I_n$，不同行误差的交叉协方差为零。因为整张误差表联合 Gaussian，[[联合高斯独立判据]]使各误差行相互独立；每行又都有零均值与协方差 $\Sigma$，所以在所述条件模型中，这些误差向量是[[独立同分布]]的 $N_q(0,\Sigma)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The response rows add their model means. If $m_i$ is row $i$ of $XB$, written as a column vector, then $Y_i\mid X\sim N_q(m_i,\Sigma)$. They remain independent conditional on $X$, but different $m_i$ give different distributions. For example, means $(60,70)^T$ and $(65,80)^T$ give independent rows with the same noise covariance and different centres. Calling these response rows i.i.d. would discard their different means. Conditioning also matters: conditional independence need not survive averaging over a random shared design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

响应行还要加上各自的模型均值。将 $XB$ 第 $i$ 行写成列向量 $m_i$，则 $Y_i\mid X\sim N_q(m_i,\Sigma)$。给定 $X$ 后它们仍独立，但不同的 $m_i$ 给出不同分布。例如，均值分别为 $(60,70)^T$ 与 $(65,80)^T$ 时，两行可以独立、具有相同噪声协方差，却有不同中心，不能称为独立同分布。这里的条件也重要：再对随机的共同设计平均，条件独立未必保留为无条件独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the Gaussian model adds
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Gaussian 模型额外规定了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful construction starts with a table $Z$ of mutually independent standard normals. Choose fixed matrices $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ and $C\in\mathbb R^{q\times q}$ with $AA^T=\Omega$ and $CC^T=\Sigma$, for example their [[半正定主平方根|symmetric square roots]], and set $U=AZC^T$. Left multiplication mixes observations; right multiplication mixes responses. By [[Gaussian仿射闭包|Gaussian closure under fixed linear transformations]], the result is jointly Gaussian, and its entry covariances are exactly $\Omega_{ij}\Sigma_{ab}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一种生成方法从相互独立的标准正态元素组成的表 $Z$ 开始。选择固定矩阵 $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$、$C\in\mathbb R^{q\times q}$ 满足 $AA^T=\Omega$、$CC^T=\Sigma$，例如取[[半正定主平方根|对称平方根]]，再令 $U=AZC^T$。左乘混合观测方向，右乘混合响应方向。由[[Gaussian仿射闭包]]，结果仍联合 Gaussian，逐元素协方差恰为 $\Omega_{ij}\Sigma_{ab}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same construction with merely uncorrelated, mean-zero, variance-one entries in $Z$ still gives the same covariance, but need not give a Gaussian law. Also, an arbitrary joint Gaussian table need not have a covariance expressible as one Kronecker product. These are two separate restrictions: Gaussian shape and separable covariance. The [[观测行协方差|row-covariance card]] explains the second restriction without assuming the first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $Z$ 的元素只满足互不相关、零均值、单位方差，同一构造仍给出相同协方差，却未必给出 Gaussian 分布。反过来，任意联合 Gaussian 表的协方差，也未必能写成单一 Kronecker 积。因此，Gaussian 分布形状与可分离协方差是两项不同限制；[[观测行协方差]]在不要求前者的情况下解释后者。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Allowing positive-semidefinite singular factors extends the vector definition to a degenerate Gaussian law, with no full-dimensional density; see [[Gaussian密度与奇异支撑|singular Gaussian support]]. The usual inverse-based matrix-normal density and SciPy's `matrix_normal` implementation require positive-definite factors. Finally, least-squares coefficients can be computed without a Gaussian assumption, whereas the classical [[误差SSP抽样律|residual Wishart law]] needs its own independent-row Gaussian and design conditions. Correlated-row matrix-normal errors do not automatically retain that unmodified law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若允许半正定但奇异的因子，向量定义可以延伸为退化 Gaussian 分布，但没有全维密度，见[[Gaussian密度与奇异支撑]]。通常含逆矩阵的矩阵正态密度，以及 SciPy 的 `matrix_normal` 实现，要求因子正定。最后，最小二乘系数可以在没有 Gaussian 假设时计算；经典[[误差SSP抽样律|残差 Wishart 律]]则需要它自己的独立 Gaussian 行与设计条件。相关行的矩阵正态模型不会自动保留未作调整的经典抽样律。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Hoff, *Separable covariance arrays via the Tucker product*, §2.2, PDF p.4; §3.1, pp.5–6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1008.2169#page=4): separates covariance-only bilinear constructions from Gaussian ones, gives the Kronecker representation and shifted normal model. [SciPy `matrix_normal`, Notes](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.matrix_normal.html) fixes column stacking, factor dimensions, and its nonsingular implementation.
- [Boehm et al. (2019), Methods, printed p.2318 / PDF p.2](https://pages.stat.wisc.edu/~yandell/doc/2019/g3.2317.full.pdf#page=2): explicit column-wise `vec`, mean-zero matrix-normal errors and row/response covariance factors in a primary model.
- [Sawyer, *Multivariate Linear Models*, §1, pp.1–2](https://www.math.wustl.edu/~sawyer/handouts/manovas.pdf#page=1): independent Gaussian error rows, shared response covariance and potentially varying model means. Sawyer uses row ordering in his Kronecker formula; this card consistently uses column ordering.
- [Fan et al., *Statistical Foundations of Data Science*, §2.2.2, printed p.28 / PDF p.44](https://fan.princeton.edu/sites/g/files/toruqf5476/files/documents/chapters1-3.pdf#page=44): fixed design versus conditioning on a realised random design. The conditional matrix model here is stated explicitly rather than inferred from a marginal error law.
- [[01_Math/04_多元统计分析/07_多元线性回归multivariate linear regression.md#1.1. 模型形式|Course Chapter 7 §§1.1–1.2]] provides the regression and error-model starting point; its main presentation is single-response. The multiple-response extension is supported by the sources above. Numerical covariances are direct calculations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Hoff 第4–6页区分可分离二阶矩与 Gaussian 分布，SciPy 核对逐列堆叠、因子尺寸与正定实现要求；Boehm 等原文第2318页给出明确的矩阵正态误差实例。
- Sawyer 第1–2页核对误差行独立、共同响应协方差及均值结构；其逐行顺序与本卡逐列顺序明确区分。Fan 等第28印刷页核对固定设计与条件模型的关系。
- 永久课程第7章§1.1–1.2提供单响应回归范围；多响应扩展由所列原源支撑，数值协方差直接计算。
