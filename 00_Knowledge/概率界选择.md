# Choosing a probability bound
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*概率界的选择*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choosing a probability bound means matching an event we care about to information we actually have. For a waiting time $X$, “at least 70 seconds”, “20 seconds above the mean”, and “20 seconds away from the mean in either direction” are different events. Write the target first, then choose an inequality whose assumptions are satisfied. The answer will be an [[尾界含义|upper guarantee]] for that event.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择概率界，就是把关心的事件与实际掌握的信息对上。对等待时间 $X$ 而言，“至少 70 秒”“比均值高 20 秒”“向任一方向偏离均值至少 20 秒”是不同的事件。先写出目标，再选条件确实成立的不等式；结果是该事件概率的一个[[尾界含义|上界保证]]。

> [!example] Use the same target throughout · 始终对准同一个事件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $X\ge0$, its [[期望|mean]] is $E[X]=50$ seconds, and its [[方差|variance]] is $25$ square seconds. We want $P(X\ge70)$. Markov gives $50/70=5/7$. For Chebyshev, the threshold is the **distance from the mean**, $70-50=20$ seconds. Every outcome with $X\ge70$ also has $|X-50|\ge20$, so
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $X\ge0$，[[期望|均值]] $E[X]=50$ 秒，[[方差]]为 $25$ 平方秒。目标是 $P(X\ge70)$。Markov 给出 $50/70=5/7$。换用 Chebyshev 时，阈值是**离均值的距离** $70-50=20$ 秒。凡是 $X\ge70$ 的结果，都满足 $|X-50|\ge20$，因此
>
> $$P(X\ge70)\le P(|X-50|\ge20)\le\frac{25}{20^2}=\frac1{16}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both bounds are valid; $1/16$ gives the stronger guarantee here. If an almost-sure lower bound $X\ge40$ is also known, shifted Markov gives $(50-40)/(70-40)=1/3$, still larger than $1/16$. Each comparison concerns exactly the same event.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个界都有效；本例的 $1/16$ 给出更强保证。若还知道 $X\ge40$ 以概率一成立，平移 Markov 给出 $(50-40)/(70-40)=1/3$，仍大于 $1/16$。这里每次比较都针对同一个事件。

## Match the available information
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把已知信息与适用条件对齐*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Available information and target | A useful candidate | Check before using it |
|---|---|---|
| A nonnegative $X$ with finite mean; $P(X\ge a)$ | [[Markov不等式|Markov]]: $E[X]/a$ | $a>0$ and $X\ge0$ almost surely |
| A known lower bound $X\ge b$ and finite mean; $P(X\ge a)$ | [[平移Markov界|Shifted Markov]]: $(E[X]-b)/(a-b)$ | $a>b$; $b$ is a bound on possible values, not just an observed minimum |
| Finite variance; deviation by at least $t$ from the mean | [[Chebyshev不等式|Chebyshev]]: $\operatorname{Var}(X)/t^2$ | $t>0$; a one-sided target may be included in this two-sided event |
| A finite, computable or bounded [[矩母函数|exponential moment]] | [[指数Markov法|Exponential Markov]] | A positive finite parameter for an upper tail; a negative one for a lower tail |
| $S=\sum_iX_i$ with mutually independent $X_i\in[0,1]$; $S\ge(1+\delta)E[S]$ | [[乘法Chernoff上界|Multiplicative Chernoff]] | $\delta>0$ and $E[S]>0$ for a relative deviation |
| $S=\sum_iX_i$ with independent $X_i\in[a_i,b_i]$; $S-E[S]\ge t$ | [[Hoeffding不等式|Hoeffding]] | $t>0$; the bound depends on the lengths $b_i-a_i$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 已知信息与目标 | 可考虑的界 | 使用前核对 |
|---|---|---|
| 非负 $X$，均值有限；目标 $P(X\ge a)$ | [[Markov不等式|Markov]]：$E[X]/a$ | $a>0$；$X\ge0$ 以概率一成立 |
| 已知下界 $X\ge b$ 且均值有限；目标 $P(X\ge a)$ | [[平移Markov界]]：$(E[X]-b)/(a-b)$ | $a>b$；$b$ 是可能取值的下界，不能仅是观察到的最小值 |
| 方差有限；偏离均值至少 $t$ | [[Chebyshev不等式|Chebyshev]]：$\operatorname{Var}(X)/t^2$ | $t>0$；单侧目标可以包含于这个双侧事件 |
| 能计算或控制有限的[[矩母函数|指数矩]] | [[指数Markov法]] | 右尾使用正的有限参数；左尾使用负参数方向 |
| $S=\sum_iX_i$，各项相互独立且在 $[0,1]$；目标 $S\ge(1+\delta)E[S]$ | [[乘法Chernoff上界]] | 相对偏差要求 $\delta>0$、$E[S]>0$ |
| $S=\sum_iX_i$，各项独立且 $X_i\in[a_i,b_i]$；目标 $S-E[S]\ge t$ | [[Hoeffding不等式|Hoeffding]] | $t>0$；常数由区间长度 $b_i-a_i$ 决定 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The general exponential method needs a finite exponential moment, even when $X$ is a single variable. Independence becomes relevant when we calculate an exponential moment of a **sum** by multiplying the moments of its terms. The familiar bounded-sum Chernoff and Hoeffding formulas use this additional structure. Knowing just a mean and variance does not establish it. Here “independent” means [[相互独立|mutually independent]], rather than merely [[两两独立|pairwise independent]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般的指数方法需要有限指数矩，$X$ 即使只有一个变量也能使用。把**和**的指数矩拆成各项指数矩的乘积时，才涉及独立性。常见的有界和 Chernoff、Hoeffding 公式使用了这层额外结构；只知道均值和方差，不能直接推断这些条件成立。这里的“独立”是[[相互独立]]，不能仅用[[两两独立]]替代。

## Compare valid answers, then interpret the result
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*比较有效的界，再解释所得保证*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a selection guide, not a universal numerical ranking. An exponential formula can give a weaker bound at some thresholds. If the full distribution is known and easy to sum or integrate, an exact probability may be simpler. Otherwise compare all applicable bounds for the same event and take their minimum together with one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些条目是选择依据，不是固定的数值排名。指数公式在某些阈值下也可能给出较弱的界。如果完整分布已知且容易求和或积分，直接算精确概率可能更简单；否则，就比较同一事件上所有条件满足的界，并与 1 一起取最小值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before reporting, check the event inclusion direction and the units: in the example, variance has units of seconds squared, so it must be divided by the squared distance $20^2$, not by $70$ or $20$. Finally, a bound equal to one is a [[尾界含义|trivial guarantee]], not an estimate that the event will almost certainly happen.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

报告前核对事件包含的方向和单位：例子里的方差单位是平方秒，必须除以距离的平方 $20^2$，不能除以 $70$ 或 $20$。最后，上界等于 1 只是[[尾界含义|平凡保证]]，不能据此估计事件几乎一定发生。
