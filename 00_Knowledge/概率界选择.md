# 概率界应按已知信息与目标事件选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Choose a probability bound from the target event and available information*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子规则
> 先把 target event、threshold 和已知条件写出来，再选择条件完全满足的界。公式“看起来更高级”不是使用它的理由；指数界在条件满足时常能给出更快衰减的保证，是因为它使用了 independence、boundedness 或 moment-generating function 等额外信息，但这不保证它在每组参数下数值都更小。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Write the target event, threshold, and available assumptions before selecting a bound whose conditions are all satisfied. A formula is not justified because it looks more advanced. When their assumptions hold, exponential bounds often decay faster because they use extra information such as independence, boundedness, or a moment-generating function, but they are not guaranteed to be numerically smaller for every parameter choice.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 目标与已知信息 | 首先考虑 | 关键边界 |
|---|---|---|
| $P(X\ge a)$；$X\ge0$ 且均值有限 | [[Markov不等式|Markov]] | 只控制右尾；不能删掉非负性 |
| 另知确定下界 $X\ge b$ | [[平移Markov界|shifted Markov]] | $b$ 必须是 almost-sure lower bound |
| $P(|X-EX|\ge t)$；方差有限 | [[Chebyshev不等式|Chebyshev]] | 双侧、按 $t^{-2}$ 衰减 |
| 和的相对上尾；各项 mutually independent 且在 $[0,1]$ | [[乘法Chernoff上界|multiplicative Chernoff]] | 需要联合独立与支持集条件 |
| 和的加性偏差；各项 independent 且 $X_i\in[a_i,b_i]$ | [[Hoeffding不等式|Hoeffding]] | 常数由区间长度 $b_i-a_i$ 决定 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Target and available information | First candidate | Essential boundary |
|---|---|---|
| $P(X\ge a)$; $X\ge0$ with finite mean | [[Markov不等式|Markov]] | Upper tail only; non-negativity cannot be removed |
| A deterministic lower bound $X\ge b$ is also known | [[平移Markov界|shifted Markov]] | $b$ must be an almost-sure lower bound |
| $P(|X-EX|\ge t)$; finite variance | [[Chebyshev不等式|Chebyshev]] | Two-sided and polynomial in $t^{-2}$ |
| Relative upper tail of a sum; mutually independent terms in $[0,1]$ | [[乘法Chernoff上界|multiplicative Chernoff]] | Joint independence and support conditions are required |
| Additive deviation of a sum; independent $X_i\in[a_i,b_i]$ | [[Hoeffding不等式|Hoeffding]] | Constants depend on the interval lengths $b_i-a_i$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个表不是“永远从上到下越来越好”的排名。同一个事件上，哪一界更紧取决于阈值和参数；若完整分布易得，直接计算 exact probability 往往更合适。选定公式后还要检查 event inclusion 的方向、单位、独立性层级，以及数值是否只给出平凡界 1。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The table is not a universal ranking from weak to strong. Which valid bound is tighter depends on the threshold and parameters. If the full distribution is easy to use, direct calculation of the exact probability may be preferable. After choosing a formula, check the direction of event inclusion, units, the required level of independence, and whether the numerical result is merely the trivial bound one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 只知道 $EX$ 与 $\operatorname{Var}(X)$，但不知道 $X$ 是否为独立有界和。能否因为 Chernoff 通常更紧就直接使用它？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose only $EX$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X)$ are known, with no representation as an independent bounded sum. May Chernoff be used merely because it is often tighter?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。现有条件只支持 Chebyshev 一类二阶矩界；Chernoff 所需的联合独立、支持集或相应 MGF 控制尚未建立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. The available information supports a second-moment bound such as Chebyshev. The joint independence, support, or MGF control required by Chernoff has not been established.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [[01_Math/07-Mathematics for Computer Science/MIT-6-042j-pdf.pdf|MIT Mathematics for Computer Science]] 第 20.1–20.5 节，尤其是 20.5.7：比较 Markov、Chebyshev 与 Chernoff 分别使用哪些信息，以及由此得到的尾部保证。
- [Hoeffding（1963），定理 1–2](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830)：核对独立有界加数的条件，并支持区分相对偏差参数化与加性偏差参数化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Sections 20.1–20.5 of [[01_Math/07-Mathematics for Computer Science/MIT-6-042j-pdf.pdf|MIT Mathematics for Computer Science]], especially Section 20.5.7, compare exactly which information Markov, Chebyshev, and Chernoff use and the resulting tail guarantees.
- [Hoeffding (1963), Theorems 1–2](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830) support the independent bounded-summand conditions and the distinction between relative and additive parameterisations.
