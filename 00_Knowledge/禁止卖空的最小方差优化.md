# 禁止卖空时最小方差组合须在非负权重约束下重新求解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A short-sale ban requires minimizing variance with nonnegative weights included in the optimization.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在资产收益具有有限二阶矩、$\Sigma\succeq0$ 的单期模型中，禁止卖空意味着完全投资权重同时满足 $w_i\ge0$。目标收益 $r$ 下应解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a single-period model with finite second moments and $\Sigma\succeq0$, a short-sale ban requires nonnegative fully invested weights. At target mean $r$, solve
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\min_w\frac12w^T\Sigma w
\quad\text{s.t.}\quad
\mathbf1^Tw=1,\qquad\mu^Tw=r,\qquad w\ge0.
$$

若只求无目标的[[全局最小方差组合]]，删除收益等式。不能先用允许卖空的闭式，再把负权重剪成零并归一化，便宣称得到约束最优解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the [[全局最小方差组合|GMV portfolio]] without a target, omit the return equality. Clipping negative unrestricted weights to zero and renormalizing does not establish a constrained optimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先查可行，再查最优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Check feasibility before optimality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仅有上述约束时，可达目标恰为 $\min_i\mu_i\le r\le\max_i\mu_i$，因为组合均值是资产均值的凸组合，区间内任一点又可由端点资产混合实现。区间外无需继续找最优解。区间内的可行域非空紧致，连续方差必取到最小值；若 $\Sigma\succ0$，该解唯一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With exactly these constraints, attainable means are precisely $\min_i\mu_i\le r\le\max_i\mu_i$: portfolio means are convex combinations, and any point in the interval can be attained by mixing endpoint assets. Outside the interval the problem is infeasible. Inside it, the feasible set is nonempty and compact, so continuous variance attains a minimum; positive-definite $\Sigma$ makes it unique.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以用凸二次规划求解器，或在小问题中检验候选活动约束。为与[[KKT条件]]的最大化约定一致，将目标改写为最大化 $-\tfrac12w^T\Sigma w$，不等式写成 $-w_i\le0$；对应系统可写为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a convex quadratic-programming solver, or inspect candidate active constraints in a small problem. To match the maximization convention in [[KKT条件|the KKT definition]], maximize $-\tfrac12w^T\Sigma w$ with inequalities $-w_i\le0$. The resulting system can be written as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Sigma w+\nu\mathbf1+\gamma\mu-\eta=0,
\quad \mathbf1^Tw=1,\quad\mu^Tw=r,
\quad w\ge0,\quad\eta\ge0,\quad\eta_iw_i=0.
$$

其中 $\nu,\gamma$ 无符号限制。这些条件共同满足时，[[KKT充分性]]证明全局最优；只求出驻点而忽略非负性或互补条件不够。不同目标可能改变哪些资产权重为零，因而不能继续无条件使用整条边界的[[双基金分离]]公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equality multipliers $\nu,\gamma$ are unrestricted. Joint satisfaction certifies global optimality by [[KKT充分性|KKT sufficiency]]; stationarity alone is not enough. The zero-weight assets can change with the target, so the unrestricted [[双基金分离|two-fund formula]] does not automatically span the constrained frontier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\mu=(0.08,0.12)^T$、$\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}0.04&0.015\\0.015&0.09\end{bmatrix}$。允许卖空时，目标 $14\%$ 得 $w=(-0.5,1.5)^T$。剪裁归一化得到 $(0,1)^T$，其收益仅为 $12\%$：这不是目标问题的解；原禁止卖空问题本就不可行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the stated inputs, unrestricted target $14\%$ gives $w=(-0.5,1.5)^T$. Clipping and renormalizing gives $(0,1)^T$, whose mean is only $12\%$. It does not solve the target problem, which is infeasible under the short-sale ban.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定同一 $\mu,\Sigma$ 与目标，增加约束只能使最小方差不变或上升，因为搜索集合缩小。若输入来自估计，约束组合的**真实样本外**风险反而可能更低，但那属于[[组合优化的输入误差|估计误差与约束的权衡]]，不是样本内最小值下降，也不是普遍保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding $\mu,\Sigma$ and the target fixed, additional constraints can only leave the minimum variance unchanged or raise it because the search set shrinks. With estimated inputs, true out-of-sample risk may instead improve; that is an [[组合优化的输入误差|estimation-versus-restriction trade-off]], not a decrease in the in-sample minimum or a universal guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Zivot, *Portfolio Theory with Short Sales Constraints*, §13.3.2, equations (13.2)–(13.3)](https://bookdown.org/compfinezbook/introcompfinr/Portfolio-Theory-with-1.html)：支持非负权重 QP、目标可行范围、相同输入下风险不下降以及全局双基金性质的限制。原页称必须数值求解，本卡只把数值 QP 作为方法，不排除小问题的解析活动集求解；原页部分使用 frontier 泛称，本卡另行区分最小方差与有效前沿。
- [[KKT条件]]、[[KKT充分性]]：按库内最大化符号约定独立推导并核对乘子系统；例子的目标不可行性与剪裁失效直接代入验证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Zivot, §13.3.2, equations (13.2)–(13.3), supports nonnegative-weight QPs, target feasibility, the fixed-input risk comparison, and limits to global two-fund spanning. This note does not adopt the page's overbroad requirement of numerical solution: small active-set problems may be solved analytically. Minimum-variance and efficient frontiers remain explicitly distinguished.
- [[KKT条件|KKT conditions]] and [[KKT充分性|sufficiency]] support the independently derived multiplier system under the vault's maximization convention. Infeasibility and the failure of clipping are verified by substitution.
