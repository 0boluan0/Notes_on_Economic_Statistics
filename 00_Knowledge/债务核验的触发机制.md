# 确定性核验模型中的固定非核验支付可将有成本核验集中在违约申报分支

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a deterministic-verification model, a fixed payment without verification can concentrate costly verification in the declared-default branch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Townsend 的[[有成本状态核验]]模型中，借款人知道结果并选择是否请求核验；核验后结果被准确揭示，约定支付可以执行。若非核验分支允许根据无法检查的报告支付不同数额，借款人会选择最低支付的报告。因此激励相容的非核验分支支付同一个常数 $C$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Townsend's [[有成本状态核验|costly-state-verification]] model, the borrower knows the outcome and chooses whether to request verification; verification reveals the truth and agreed payments are enforceable. If unverifiable reports allowed different payments without verification, the borrower would choose the cheapest report. Incentive compatibility therefore requires a constant payment $C$ throughout the nonverification branch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $S$ 为核验区，$g(y)$ 为核验后的支付，$k(y)$ 为借款人承担的核验资源成本。原模型规定无差异时不请求核验，因此要使借款人在 $S$ 内自愿进入核验分支，需满足：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $S$ be the verification region, $g(y)$ the verified transfer, and $k(y)$ the verification cost borne by the borrower. With the original tie-break rule favoring no verification, voluntary verification within $S$ requires:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$g(y)=C\quad(y\notin S),\qquad g(y)+k(y)<C\quad(y\in S).$$

固定支付像债务正常履约，申报不能照付时进入核验及不同支付。这节约的是不必在每次正常支付时查清全部收入的成本，不是“只观察有没有违约就永远无需审计”。若要进一步证明核验区一定是低收益区、或该契约最优，还必须加入论文相应命题中的偏好、成本和可行性条件，不能仅从这两条式子推出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The fixed branch resembles ordinary debt service; a declaration of inability to pay leads to verification and a different transfer. The saving is that complete income need not be established for every normal payment, not that observing default removes all auditing. Proving that verification covers low outcomes or that a contract is optimal additionally requires the preference, cost, and feasibility assumptions of the relevant propositions; the two conditions alone do not establish those results.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

确定性核验也不是全部可行合同：原论文给出随机核验可优于最优确定性安排的例子。故此机制说明债务的一种信息优势，不证明债务普遍优于股权，也不取代现实中的持续监督。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deterministic verification is not the entire feasible contract space: the paper gives an example where stochastic verification dominates the best deterministic arrangement. This mechanism explains one informational advantage of debt, not universal superiority over equity or the dispensability of ongoing monitoring.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
