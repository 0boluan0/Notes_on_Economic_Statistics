# 现金预算按实际收付时点预测现金余额并识别维持最低余额所需的融资
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A cash budget forecasts balances using receipt and payment timing and identifies financing needed to maintain a minimum balance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金预算把未来每期真正收到和付出的钱排在时间线上，从期初可用现金推到期末余额。它不仅问全年是否有盈余，还问付款当天能否满足最低现金要求。销售收入按确认期间进入利润预测，赊销回款则按客户实际付款假设进入现金预算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A cash budget places expected receipts and payments on a timeline, moving from opening available cash to closing balances. It asks whether minimum liquidity can be met when payments fall due, not merely whether the whole year has a surplus. Revenue follows recognition in the profit forecast; credit collections follow expected payment dates in the cash budget.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先算融资前余额，再加入新增借款、还款或其他明确的融资收付：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First calculate the pre-financing balance, then incorporate borrowing, repayments, or other specified financing flows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$C_t^{\mathrm{pre}}=C_{t-1}+Receipts_t-Payments_t,$$
$$C_t=C_t^{\mathrm{pre}}+Borrow_t-Repay_t.$$

下例单位一致，暂不计利息；期初现金 100，最低现金 20，其他收付已全部包括在表中：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use one common monetary unit, ignore interest temporarily, and include all other receipts and payments. Opening cash is 100 and the minimum is 20:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 月份 | 期初现金 | 收款 | 付款 | 融资前余额 | 新借款 | 还款 | 期末现金 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 100 | 20 | 70 | 50 | 0 | 0 | 50 |
| 2 | 50 | 15 | 80 | -15 | 35 | 0 | 20 |
| 3 | 20 | 100 | 40 | 80 | 0 | 35 | 45 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Month 1 closes at 50. Month 2 would close at −15 without financing, so borrowing 35 restores the minimum of 20. Month 3 generates a pre-financing balance of 80; repaying 35 leaves 45.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个月合计收款 135、付款 190，期初现金加净流量仍有 45，最终也没有未还借款。但第二个月中间需要融资 35。由此可见，年度或季度[[外部融资需求|资金总量]]平衡不能代替逐期流动性检查；即使月末余额够用，月内集中付款还可能要求更细的日历。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Total receipts are 135 and payments 190, leaving 45 from opening cash and no debt outstanding at the end. Yet 35 of temporary financing is needed in month 2. Aggregate [[外部融资需求|funding balance]] does not replace period-by-period liquidity checks; concentrated intra-month payments may require a finer calendar.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最低现金额来自经营政策，不能任意设零；受限资金也不等于可用现金。借款要检查额度、可提用条件及到账日期，利息和费用应回到预算，必要时解[[融资利息循环]]。如果借不到钱，预算显示的是待处理缺口，而不是已经自动完成融资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The minimum balance comes from operating policy, and restricted funds are not automatically available. Verify credit limits, drawdown conditions, and arrival dates; incorporate interest and fees, resolving [[融资利息循环|interest feedback]] where necessary. Unavailable borrowing leaves an unresolved gap rather than an automatically funded plan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
