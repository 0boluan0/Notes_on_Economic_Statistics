# Mean value theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*拉格朗日中值定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **mean value theorem** connects change over a whole interval with change at one instant. Suppose a graph rises by $8$ units while the input increases by $2$. Its average slope is $4$. Under suitable continuity and differentiability conditions, some interior tangent has exactly that same slope, even if the slope varies along the way.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**拉格朗日中值定理**把整个区间上的变化，与某一瞬间的变化联系起来。假设图像在输入增加 $2$ 的过程中上升了 $8$，平均斜率就是 $4$。在适当的连续与可导条件下，内部至少有一条切线的斜率恰好也是 $4$，即使沿途斜率一直在变。

> [!example] A changing slope matches the average · 变化中的斜率也会等于平均值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^2$ on $[1,3]$, the output changes from $1$ to $9$. The secant line through the endpoints has slope $(9-1)/(3-1)=4$. The [[导数|derivative]] is $f'(x)=2x$, so at $c=2$ the instantaneous slope is $4$. It need not stay equal to $4$ at other inputs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[1,3]$ 上的 $f(x)=x^2$，输出从 $1$ 变为 $9$。穿过两个端点的割线斜率是 $(9-1)/(3-1)=4$。[[导数]]为 $f'(x)=2x$，所以在 $c=2$ 处，瞬时斜率恰好是 $4$；其他位置并不需要保持这个斜率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The exact statement
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*精确的结论与适用条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a<b$ be finite real numbers and let $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$. Require [[一点连续|continuity]] at every point of the closed interval, including approach from inside the interval at the endpoints. Require a finite [[导数|derivative]] at every interior point. Define the number $m$ to be the average output change per input unit:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<b$ 是有限实数，$f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$。要求闭区间上每一点都[[一点连续|连续]]，包括端点处从区间内部趋近时的连续性；还要求每个内点都存在有限的[[导数]]。把每单位输入的平均输出变化记为 $m$：

$$m=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}.$$

> [!important] Some interior derivative equals the secant slope · 某个内部导数等于割线斜率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under these conditions, at least one $c\in(a,b)$ satisfies
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在这些条件下，至少存在一个 $c\in(a,b)$ 满足
>
> $$f'(c)=m,\qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad f(b)-f(a)=f'(c)(b-a).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is an exact equality. No derivative is required at $a$ or $b$, and the derivative function $f'$ is not required to be continuous. The interval must have positive length: $a<b$ makes the denominator nonzero and leaves interior points available.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这是精确等式，不要求端点 $a,b$ 可导，也不要求导数函数 $f'$ 连续。区间必须具有正长度：$a<b$ 既保证分母非零，也保证存在内点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For instance, $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ on $[0,1]$ is continuous everywhere on that interval and differentiable on $(0,1)$. Its average slope is $1$, and $f'(x)=1/(2\sqrt{x})$ equals $1$ at $c=1/4$. The absence of a finite right derivative at the endpoint $0$ does not obstruct this application.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$[0,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ 在整个区间连续、在 $(0,1)$ 可导。平均斜率是 $1$，而 $f'(x)=1/(2\sqrt{x})$ 在 $c=1/4$ 时等于 $1$。端点 $0$ 没有有限右导数，并不妨碍这次应用。

## Subtract the secant's tilt
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*减去割线的倾斜部分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[罗尔定理|Rolle's theorem]] handles equal endpoint heights: a continuous function, differentiable inside, must then have a zero derivative somewhere inside. To use it when $f(a)$ and $f(b)$ differ, subtract the linear change $m(x-a)$ and define an auxiliary function $g$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[罗尔定理]]处理端点等高的情形：连续且内部可导的函数，内部至少有一个零导数点。为了在 $f(a)$ 与 $f(b)$ 不等时也使用它，减去线性变化 $m(x-a)$，构造辅助函数 $g$：

$$g(x)=f(x)-m(x-a).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtracting this continuous linear function preserves continuity and differentiability. At the endpoints, its correction is zero at $a$ and exactly the total change of $f$ at $b$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

减去这个连续的线性函数，会保留连续性与可导性。在端点处，修正项在 $a$ 为零，在 $b$ 刚好等于 $f$ 的总变化：

$$g(a)=f(a),\qquad g(b)=f(b)-m(b-a)=f(b)-[f(b)-f(a)]=f(a).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $g(a)=g(b)$. Rolle's theorem supplies $c\in(a,b)$ with $g'(c)=0$. By [[导数线性法则|linearity of differentiation]], $g'(c)=f'(c)-m$, so $f'(c)=m$. Geometrically, removing the overall tilt turns a parallel-tangent question into a horizontal-tangent question. The extreme-value argument inside Rolle's theorem, not the appearance of a drawing, guarantees the point exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

于是 $g(a)=g(b)$。罗尔定理给出某个 $c\in(a,b)$，使 $g'(c)=0$。由[[导数线性法则]]，$g'(c)=f'(c)-m$，所以 $f'(c)=m$。从几何上看，减去整体倾斜，把“寻找平行切线”变成“寻找水平切线”。真正保证该点存在的是罗尔定理内部的极值论证，而不只是图像看起来如此。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Existence does not locate a unique instant
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*存在不等于确定唯一时刻*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an affine function $f(x)=ux+v$, where $u,v$ are constants, every interior derivative equals the average slope $u$, so every interior point works. Even when the matching point is unique, endpoint data need not identify it. On $[0,1]$, both $x^2$ and $x^3$ have endpoint values $0,1$ and average slope $1$, but their matching points are respectively $1/2$ and $1/\sqrt{3}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于仿射函数 $f(x)=ux+v$，其中 $u,v$ 是常数，每个内部导数都等于平均斜率 $u$，所以所有内点都符合要求。即使符合要求的点恰好唯一，两个端点的数据也未必能确定它。在 $[0,1]$ 上，$x^2$ 与 $x^3$ 的端点值都是 $0,1$，平均斜率都是 $1$，但对应点分别为 $1/2$ 与 $1/\sqrt{3}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the travel interpretation, let $s(t)$ be position along a fixed straight-line coordinate, in kilometres, with $t$ in hours. If $s$ is continuous on $[0,6]$, differentiable inside, and $s(6)-s(0)=480$, the average **signed velocity** is $80$ km/h. The theorem gives some $c\in(0,6)$ with $s'(c)=80$ km/h, without identifying the time from those totals alone. Displacement is not total distance travelled if the car reverses direction. A claim based on total distance instead applies the theorem to a cumulative-distance function, after checking its continuity and interior differentiability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在行车解释中，令 $s(t)$ 是沿固定直线坐标的位置，单位为千米，$t$ 的单位为小时。若 $s$ 在 $[0,6]$ 连续、内部可导，且 $s(6)-s(0)=480$，平均的**带符号速度**就是 $80$ km/h。定理保证某个 $c\in(0,6)$ 满足 $s'(c)=80$ km/h，却不能仅凭这些总量指出时刻。如果车辆曾经掉头，位移不等于总路程。若要从总路程讨论速率，应改对累计路程函数应用定理，并检查它的连续性与内部可导性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equality also differs from [[线性近似|linear approximation]]. Linear approximation uses the derivative at a chosen base point to estimate a nearby value. The mean value theorem uses a generally unspecified interior point to express the finite change exactly. Consequences such as [[导数单调性判据|monotonicity from derivative signs]] and [[导数界控制函数差|bounds on function changes]] use this exact identity without needing to solve for $c$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个等式也不同于[[线性近似]]。线性近似使用选定基点处的导数，估计附近的函数值；中值定理则使用通常尚未确定的内部点，把有限变化准确表示出来。[[导数单调性判据]]、[[导数界控制函数差]]等推论，正是利用这个精确等式，而不需要先求出 $c$。

> [!warning] One missing condition can break the guarantee · 一个缺失条件就可能破坏保证
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $[0,1]$, let $f(x)=x$ for $x<1$ and $f(1)=0$. The secant slope is zero but every interior derivative is $1$; the endpoint discontinuity invalidates the theorem. For $f(x)=|x|$ on $[-1,1]$, the secant slope is again zero, yet the derivative is $-1$ or $1$ where it exists and is missing at $0$. Continuity alone is not enough. These counterexamples remove the general guarantee; a particular function may still have a matching derivative even if it fails a hypothesis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[0,1]$ 上，对 $x<1$ 令 $f(x)=x$，但令 $f(1)=0$。割线斜率为零，而内部导数都为 $1$；端点间断使定理不再适用。对 $[-1,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=|x|$，割线斜率同样为零，但导数存在处只有 $-1$ 或 $1$，在 $0$ 则不可导；只有连续性还不够。这些反例说明一般保证消失，不排除某个不满足假设的函数仍碰巧有符合等式的导数。
