# 闭区间连续且内部可导的函数在某个内部点的瞬时变化率等于整个区间的平均变化率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For a function continuous on a closed interval and differentiable inside, an interior instantaneous rate equals the interval's average rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 设 $a<b$，$f$ 在 $[a,b]$ 连续且在 $(a,b)$ 可导。则至少有一个 $c\in(a,b)$ 满足
> $$
> f'(c)=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}.
> $$
> 它把一个精确的整体平均变化率，连接到某个通常未知的内部瞬时变化率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $a<b$, $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$, and differentiable on $(a,b)$, then some $c\in(a,b)$ satisfies $f'(c)=[f(b)-f(a)]/(b-a)$. The theorem exactly links an interval-wide average change to an instantaneous rate at a usually unknown interior point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令割线斜率 $m=[f(b)-f(a)]/(b-a)$，构造
$$
g(x)=f(x)-m(x-a).
$$
可验证 $g(a)=g(b)$，所以 [[罗尔定理]] 给 $g'(c)=0$；于是 $f'(c)=m$。证明的关键不是图像“看起来总有一条平行切线”，而是减去割线后把一般问题转成端点等高的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtract the secant's linear part: $g(x)=f(x)-m(x-a)$. Then $g(a)=g(b)$, so Rolle's theorem supplies $g'(c)=0$, which is exactly $f'(c)=m$. This auxiliary-function reduction, rather than the appearance of a drawing, carries the proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定理只断言某个 $c$ 存在；它可能不唯一，也通常不能由端点数据单独算出。闭区间连续与开区间可导必须逐项核对：函数在一个内部点不可导，就不能跨过该点直接应用。它也不同于 [[线性近似]]：中值定理是未知点处导数的精确等式，线性近似是已知基点处导数的局部近似。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The point may be nonunique and cannot usually be calculated from endpoint data alone. Continuity on the closed interval and differentiability at every interior point must be checked. The theorem is also distinct from linearization: it gives an exact equality using a derivative at an unknown point, whereas linearization uses the derivative at a known base point approximately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一辆车六小时平均速度为 80 km/h，定理能否告诉你何时瞬时速度为 80？
>
> **答案：** 在位置函数连续、内部可导的条件下能保证至少有这样一个时刻，但不能仅凭总路程与总时间指出具体时刻。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** A car averages 80 km/h over six hours. Can the theorem tell you when its instantaneous speed was 80 km/h?
>
> **Answer:** With continuity of the position function and differentiability in the interior, it guarantees at least one such instant. Total distance and elapsed time alone do not identify which instant it is.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
