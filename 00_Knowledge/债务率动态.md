# 债务率变化由有效利率与增长的差额、初级余额和存量流量调节共同决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Debt-to-GDP dynamics combine the effective interest-growth differential, the primary balance and stock-flow adjustments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用同一范围和本币计量，令$B_{t-1}>0$为上年末债务，$i_t$为本期利息支出除以上年末债务的有效名义利率，$PB_t$为[[初级预算余额|初级盈余]]，$A_t$为[[债务存量流量调节]]。先从金额恒等式开始：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use consistent coverage and domestic-currency amounts. Let opening debt be $B_{t-1}>0$, $i_t$ equal current interest expense divided by opening debt, $PB_t$ be the [[初级预算余额|primary surplus]], and $A_t$ the [[债务存量流量调节|stock-flow adjustment]]. Begin with amounts:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
B_t=(1+i_t)B_{t-1}-PB_t+A_t.
$$

本期名义GDP为$Y_t=(1+n_t)Y_{t-1}>0$，令$b_t=B_t/Y_t$、$pb_t=PB_t/Y_t$、$a_t=A_t/Y_t$。两边除以本期GDP，再减去$b_{t-1}$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With nominal GDP $Y_t=(1+n_t)Y_{t-1}>0$ and contemporaneous ratios $b_t$, $pb_t$ and $a_t$, divide by current GDP and subtract the opening ratio:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
b_t=\frac{1+i_t}{1+n_t}b_{t-1}-pb_t+a_t,
\qquad
\Delta b_t=\frac{i_t-n_t}{1+n_t}b_{t-1}-pb_t+a_t.
$$

例：债务600、GDP1000，下一年利息24、初级盈余14、调节17、名义增长4%。债务变为$600+24-14+17=627$，GDP为1040，债务率由60%变为约60.29%。$i=n=4\%$只使利差项为零，并没有抵销另外两项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Debt 600 and GDP 1000, followed by interest 24, primary surplus 14, adjustment 17 and nominal growth 4%, give debt 627 and GDP 1040. The ratio rises from 60% to about 60.29%. Equal interest and growth only eliminate the differential term.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若改用实际量，应以同一GDP平减指数定义$1+r=(1+i)/(1+\pi)$、$1+n=(1+g)(1+\pi)$，这才有$(1+i)/(1+n)=(1+r)/(1+g)$。近似式$(r-g)b-pb+a$省掉了分母，增长较大时不可冒充精确式。外币重估已放入$A$，不能再加一遍；有效存量利率也不等于今天新发债的市场利率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using one GDP deflator, define $1+r=(1+i)/(1+\pi)$ and $1+n=(1+g)(1+\pi)$, so the nominal ratio equals $(1+r)/(1+g)$. The approximation $(r-g)b-pb+a$ drops the denominator. Do not double-count currency revaluation already in $A$, or substitute a marginal issuance yield for the effective stock rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
