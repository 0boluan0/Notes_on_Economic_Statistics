# 断点回归利用处理规则在阈值处的跳变构造局部因果比较

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Regression discontinuity uses a treatment-rule cutoff to construct a local causal comparison*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 断点回归（RD）比较运行变量在处理阈值两侧的单位。基本 sharp RD 中，处理由阈值确定；若两种潜在结果的条件均值在阈值连续且两侧有支持，观测结果均值的跳变识别阈值处的处理效应。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> RD compares units on opposite sides of a treatment cutoff. In a basic sharp design, treatment is determined by the threshold. Continuity of both potential-outcome conditional means and support on both sides let the outcome jump identify the effect at the cutoff.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

奖学金按分数阈值发放时，阈值附近的比较目标是局部效应，不能未经额外假设推广为全体学生的 ATE。阈值附近的操纵、其他同步规则和 fuzzy treatment 都需要另行处理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A scholarship cutoff yields a local comparison near the threshold, not automatically an ATE for all students. Manipulation, other discontinuous rules, and fuzzy treatment assignment require further analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
