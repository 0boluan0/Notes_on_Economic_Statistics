# Mathematical proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*数学证明*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **mathematical proof** explains why a claim follows from the facts we are allowed to use. It is a finite chain of justified reasoning: the reader can check where the argument starts, why each step is permitted, and how it reaches the claim. Seeing that a formula works for a few numbers may suggest a claim; a proof explains why it holds throughout its stated scope.

The target is a [[命题与谓词|proposition]], a statement with a truth value once its meaning and any variables have been fixed or quantified. The starting facts may include [[公理、定理、引理与推论|axioms and previously proved results]], together with the particular problem's assumptions. Definitions tell us what the words and symbols mean. For example, saying that an integer is even means that it is twice an integer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**数学证明**解释一个结论为什么能从允许使用的事实推出。它是一条有限的、有依据的推理链：读者能检查从哪里开始、每一步为什么可以这样走，以及如何到达结论。代几个数字可能帮助猜出规律；证明则说明规律为什么在所声明的整个范围内成立。

证明的目标是[[命题与谓词|命题]]：意义明确，变量已固定或被量词约束后，能够谈论真假的陈述。起点可以包括[[公理、定理、引理与推论|公理与已经证明的结果]]，再加上题目给定的假设；定义则说明术语和符号的含义。例如，一个整数是偶数，就是说它等于某个整数的两倍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A mathematical proof begins with definitions, axioms, stated assumptions, and previously established results, and reaches the target proposition in finitely many sound inferential steps. Every step must follow from what precedes it and from results that are allowed in the context.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
数学证明从定义、公理、题设假设和已经证明的结论出发，用可靠的推理规则在有限步内得到目标命题。每一步都必须能由前文与允许使用的结果推出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What makes a step justified?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一步推理怎样才有依据？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sound inference rule preserves truth: **if all its premises are true, its conclusion must be true**. The word “if” matters. A valid inference does not establish its own starting assumptions; a proof using a problem's hypotheses establishes what follows when those hypotheses hold.

For a simple rule, let $P$ and $Q$ denote propositions. The [[蕴含真值条件|implication]] $P\Rightarrow Q$ says that $P$ cannot hold while $Q$ fails. If we have established both $P$ and $P\Rightarrow Q$, we may conclude $Q$. This rule is called **modus ponens**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
可靠的推理规则保持真实性：**如果全部前提都真，结论就一定真**。这里的“如果”很关键。推理有效并不会自动证明起点假设；使用题设条件的证明，说明的是这些条件成立时会得到什么。

例如，用 $P,Q$ 表示两个命题。[[蕴含真值条件|蕴含]] $P\Rightarrow Q$ 表示不能出现 $P$ 成立而 $Q$ 失败的情况。若已经知道 $P$ 和 $P\Rightarrow Q$ 都成立，就可以推出 $Q$；这条规则叫作**肯定前件式（modus ponens）**。

$$P,\quad P\Rightarrow Q\quad\Longrightarrow\quad Q.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose an integer $n$ is divisible by $4$. Then $n=4k=2(2k)$ for some integer $k$, so $n$ is even. The facts “$n$ is divisible by $4$” and “divisibility by $4$ implies evenness” therefore justify the conclusion “$n$ is even.” Merely knowing that $n$ is even does **not** justify the reverse conclusion: $n=2$ is even but not divisible by $4$. A reliable chain must use a rule in a direction it actually permits; [[逆否与逆命题|reversing an implication]] is a different claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
假设整数 $n$ 能被 $4$ 整除，则存在整数 $k$ 使 $n=4k=2(2k)$，所以 $n$ 是偶数。于是，“$n$ 能被 $4$ 整除”与“能被 $4$ 整除会推出偶数”这两个前提，足以推出“$n$ 是偶数”。反过来，只知道 $n$ 是偶数，**不能**推出它能被 $4$ 整除：$n=2$ 就不行。推理必须沿规则允许的方向走；[[逆否与逆命题|把蕴含反过来]]已经换成了另一个命题。

> [!example] Two even integers / 两个偶数相加
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Claim:** the sum of any two even integers is even. Take arbitrary even integers $m,n$. By the definition of evenness, there are integers $a,b$ with $m=2a$ and $n=2b$. Substitution and the distributive law give
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> **目标：** 任意两个偶数之和仍是偶数。取任意偶整数 $m,n$。根据偶数定义，存在整数 $a,b$，使 $m=2a,n=2b$。代入并使用分配律，得到
>
> $$m+n=2a+2b=2(a+b).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Integers are closed under addition, so $a+b$ is an integer. The last expression is therefore twice an integer, which is exactly what “even” means. We did not choose particular values for $m,n$, so the argument applies to every pair allowed by the claim. The proof used a definition, substitution, an algebraic law and closure of the integers; each has a specific job.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 整数相加仍是整数，所以 $a+b$ 是整数。最后的式子是某个整数的两倍，正好满足偶数定义。我们没有给 $m,n$ 指定某一对数值，因此推理对目标所允许的每一对偶数都适用。这里依次使用了定义、代入、代数运算法则和整数的封闭性，每一步都有明确依据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a [[直接证明|direct proof]]: the reasoning starts from the assumptions and reaches the conclusion. It also illustrates what [[全称证明与存在见证|taking arbitrary objects]] means. Checking $2+4=6$ would establish one example; writing $m=2a,n=2b$ represents any two even integers without losing their defining property.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这是一份[[直接证明]]：从假设出发，一步步到达结论。它也展示了[[全称证明与存在见证|取任意对象]]的含义。检查 $2+4=6$ 只验证了一个例子；写成 $m=2a,n=2b$，则用定义表示任意两个偶数。

## A true ending does not validate the route
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*终点正确不代表过程有效*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently, a proof cannot be judged solely by whether its final sentence is true. An argument may happen to reach a true conclusion after dividing by zero, reversing an implication, or assuming the desired conclusion. Audit the **first** step that no longer follows; elegant calculations after that point cannot repair the broken chain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
因此，判断证明不能只看最后一句是否正确。一个论证可能碰巧得到真结论，却在中途除以零、倒置蕴含或预设了待证结论。审查时应定位**第一处**不再由前文推出的步骤；后面的计算即使漂亮，也不能修复断掉的推理链。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here is a deliberately faulty calculation. Set $a=b=1$. The equality $a^2=ab$ is true. Subtracting $b^2$ and factoring are also valid:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
看一段故意写错的计算。令 $a=b=1$，则 $a^2=ab$ 成立。两边减去 $b^2$，再因式分解，也都合法：

$$a^2-b^2=ab-b^2,\qquad (a-b)(a+b)=b(a-b).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cancelling $a-b$ would produce $a+b=b$, hence $2=1$. **That cancellation is the first invalid step:** $a-b=0$, and cancellation by division requires a nonzero divisor. If we then multiply the false equality $2=1$ by zero, we arrive at the true equality $0=0$. The true ending does not retroactively license the division. The claim “$0=0$” has easy correct proofs, but this particular chain is not one of them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若约掉 $a-b$，就会得到 $a+b=b$，进而得到 $2=1$。**第一次错误发生在约掉 $a-b$ 时**：它等于 $0$，而通过除法约去因子必须保证因子非零。如果接着把错误等式 $2=1$ 两边乘以零，反而会到达正确等式 $0=0$。终点正确并不能倒过来允许前面的除零操作。“$0=0$”当然能被正确证明，但这条推理链不是正确证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A second failure is circular reasoning: using the target itself as an unproved reason for that target. In a proof of $P\Rightarrow Q$, it is legitimate to assume $P$ temporarily and derive $Q$; assuming $Q$ just to obtain $Q$ does not establish that implication. The question is whether each required fact was licensed before it was used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
另一种错误是循环论证：把待证结论本身当作尚未证明的理由。证明 $P\Rightarrow Q$ 时，可以暂时假设 $P$，再推出 $Q$；但只是先假设 $Q$ 再得到 $Q$，并不能建立这个蕴含。关键在于，每个所需事实是否在被使用之前已有依据。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Soundness means truth preservation: whenever all premises are true, the conclusion cannot be false. It is unrelated to the writer's confidence or the number of examples supplied.

A course-level proof may omit details genuinely established for its audience, but it should state crucial assumptions and dependencies. Concision does not license an inferential gap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
“可靠”是指推理规则保真：只要前提都真，结论就不能为假；它不等于作者的语气自信或例子很多。

课堂证明可以省略对受众确实已知的细节，但应明示关键假设与所用结果。省略书写不等于允许逻辑跳跃。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite reasoning can prove a statement about infinitely many objects: the even-sum argument uses finitely many steps with arbitrary integers. Conversely, checking many objects does not prove a claim about all integers. Exhaustive checking can establish a claim about a **finite** domain only when every allowed case has actually been covered and each check is justified. Proof methods such as [[普通数学归纳法|mathematical induction]] supply other finite arguments for infinite families.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
有限步推理可以证明关于无穷多个对象的结论：前面的偶数和证明，用有限步骤处理任意整数。反过来，检查很多对象并不能证明所有整数都满足某性质。只有论域**有限**、允许的情形已全部覆盖且各项检查可靠时，穷尽核查才足够；[[普通数学归纳法|数学归纳法]]等方法则提供了证明无穷族命题的其他有限论证。

> [!question]- Check the chain / 检查推理链
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An argument ends at a proposition already known to be true. Why does this not establish that the argument is valid?
>
> **Answer:** Validity requires every step to follow soundly from the premises. An invalid step can accidentally lead to a true conclusion, so the chain, not just its endpoint, must be checked.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 某论证最后得到一个已知真命题。为什么这仍不足以证明论证有效？
>
> **答案：** 有效性要求每一步都由前提可靠推出；错误步骤也可能偶然通向真结论，所以必须检查推理链而非只核对终点。
