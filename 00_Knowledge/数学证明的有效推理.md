# 证明是从明示前提经可靠推理到结论的有限论证
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A proof is a finite argument that reaches its conclusion from explicit premises through sound inference*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 数学证明从定义、公理、题设假设和已经证明的结论出发，用可靠的推理规则在有限步内得到目标命题。每一步都必须能由前文与允许使用的结果推出。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A mathematical proof begins with definitions, axioms, stated assumptions, and previously established results, and reaches the target proposition in finitely many sound inferential steps. Every step must follow from what precedes it and from results that are allowed in the context.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，判断证明不能只看最后一句是否正确。一个论证可能碰巧得到真结论，却在中途除以零、倒置蕴含或预设了待证结论。审查时应定位**第一处**不再由前文推出的步骤；后面的计算即使漂亮，也不能修复断掉的推理链。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently, a proof cannot be judged solely by whether its final sentence is true. An argument may happen to reach a true conclusion after dividing by zero, reversing an implication, or assuming the desired conclusion. Audit the **first** step that no longer follows; elegant calculations after that point cannot repair the broken chain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- “可靠”是指推理规则保真：只要前提都真，结论就不能为假；它不等于作者的语气自信或例子很多。
- 课堂证明可以省略对受众确实已知的细节，但应明示关键假设与所用结果。省略书写不等于允许逻辑跳跃。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

**1.** Soundness means truth preservation: whenever all premises are true, the conclusion cannot be false. It is unrelated to the writer's confidence or the number of examples supplied.<br>
**2.** A course-level proof may omit details genuinely established for its audience, but it should state crucial assumptions and dependencies. Concision does not license an inferential gap.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 某论证最后得到一个已知真命题。为什么这仍不足以证明论证有效？
>
> **答案：** 有效性要求每一步都由前提可靠推出；错误步骤也可能偶然通向真结论，所以必须检查推理链而非只核对终点。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An argument ends at a proposition already known to be true. Why does this not establish that the argument is valid?
>
> **Answer:** Validity requires every step to follow soundly from the premises. An invalid step can accidentally lead to a true conclusion, so the chain, not just its endpoint, must be checked.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
