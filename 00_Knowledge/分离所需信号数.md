# 有限纯策略完全分离需要类型到信号的单射，混合完全分离需要每个类型拥有非空且互不相交的信号支持
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Full separation in finite pure strategies requires an injection from types to signals; full mixed separation requires every type to have a nonempty signal support disjoint from every other type's support*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完全分离要求观察到路径上信号后至多剩下一种可能类型。这个信息要求先对类型数与可用信号数施加组合条件，之后才轮到收益和激励约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full separation requires every on-path signal to leave at most one possible sender type. This informational requirement first imposes a combinatorial condition on the numbers of types and available signals; payoff and incentive constraints come only afterward.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 纯策略的单射条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Injection Condition for Pure Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

纯策略候选为每个类型指定一个信号 $s:\Theta\to S$。完全分离要求
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pure-strategy candidate assigns one signal to each type through a map $s:\Theta\to S$. Full separation requires
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\theta\ne\theta'
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
s(\theta)\ne s(\theta').
$$

因此 $s$ 必须是单射。若 $\Theta$ 与 $S$ 都有限，鸽巢原理立即给出必要条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $s$ must be injective. When both $\Theta$ and $S$ are finite, the pigeonhole principle immediately gives the necessary condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
|S|\ge |\Theta|.
$$

若 $|S|<|\Theta|$，至少两个类型必然共享一个信号，所以纯策略完全分离不可能。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $|S|<|\Theta|$, at least two types must share a signal, making full pure separation impossible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 混合策略的支持条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Support Condition for Mixed Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在混合策略下，每个类型的支持必须非空，并且不同类型的支持两两不相交：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under mixed strategies, every type's support must be nonempty, and the supports of distinct types must be pairwise disjoint:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{supp}\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta)\ne\varnothing,
$$

$$
\operatorname{supp}\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta)
\cap
\operatorname{supp}\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta')
=\varnothing
\qquad(\theta\ne\theta').
$$

因此在有限信号集合中，允许混合并不会绕过 $|S|\ge|\Theta|$：每个类型的非空支持仍至少占用一个不与其他类型共享的信号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore, with a finite signal set, allowing mixing does not circumvent $|S|\ge|\Theta|$: every type's nonempty support still uses at least one signal unavailable to all other types.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 只是必要条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Necessary Condition Only*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信号数量足够只说明分离在组合上可行，不说明分离是均衡。接收者必须对识别出的类型作最佳反应，每个发送者类型还必须满足[[分离模仿约束|模仿与其他信号的偏离约束]]。即使 $|S|\ge|\Theta|$，这些激励条件仍可能失败。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Having enough signals establishes only combinatorial feasibility, not equilibrium. The receiver must best respond to each identified type, and every sender type must satisfy [[分离模仿约束|imitation and other signal-deviation constraints]]. These incentive conditions can fail even when $|S|\ge|\Theta|$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
