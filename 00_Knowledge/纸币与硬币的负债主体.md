# 中央银行发行的纸币是其负债而硬币负债主体须按法域核对
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Central-bank-issued banknotes are its liabilities, while the issuer and accounting treatment of coins must be checked by jurisdiction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 公众持有的中央银行纸币是中央银行负债、持有人的资产。日常分析常把纸币硬币合称现金或公共货币，但精确资产负债表必须按法域分别核对硬币的发行者、持有机构的记账方式，以及它是否、由谁列为负债。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Central-bank banknotes held by the public are liabilities of the central bank and assets of the holder. Everyday analysis often groups notes and coins as cash or public money, but a precise balance sheet must separately check the coin issuer, the holder's accounting, and whether and by whom coins are recorded as a liability in that jurisdiction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在美国，Federal Reserve notes 列在 Federal Reserve 负债端；美国硬币由 Treasury 旗下的 U.S. Mint 发行。Federal Reserve Banks 持有的硬币列为资产，不是 Federal Reserve 负债。H.6 可把纸币与硬币一起纳入 currency 统计，但统计集合不因此把两者的发行与负债归属变成同一件事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the United States, Federal Reserve notes appear on the Federal Reserve's liability side, while U.S. coins are issued by the U.S. Mint within the Treasury. Coins held by Federal Reserve Banks are recorded as assets, not Federal Reserve liabilities. H.6 can combine notes and coins in a currency measure without making their issuance and liability treatment identical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- “现金是央行负债”是对中央银行纸币的可靠简化，不是跨法域对每一枚硬币的普遍法律结论。
- “由财政部门发行”不足以单独推出一项与可兑付存款完全相同的负债处理；必须继续查该法域的会计与法律规则。
- 尚未进入流通、仍在发行机构持有的纸币硬币与公众持有通货的统计处理可能不同。
- 银行金库里的实物现金仍是纸币硬币，不会因为持有人是银行就变成央行账户中的 reserve balance。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- “Cash is a central-bank liability” is a sound shorthand for central-bank banknotes, not a universal legal statement about every coin in every jurisdiction.
- Issuance by a fiscal authority does not by itself establish liability accounting identical to a redeemable deposit; the jurisdiction's legal and accounting rules must still be checked.
- Notes and coins still held by issuing authorities may be treated differently from currency held in circulation.
- Physical cash in a bank vault remains notes and coins; it does not become a reserve balance merely because a bank holds it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么 H.6 把美国纸币和硬币合在 currency 中，仍不能说二者法律发行主体完全相同？
>
> **答案：** 统计口径可以聚合不同发行与负债归属的工具；Federal Reserve notes 是美联储负债，美国硬币由财政部系统发行，联储持有的硬币反而是其资产。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does H.6's combined U.S. currency measure not prove that notes and coins have the same legal issuer?
>
> **Answer:** A statistical measure may aggregate instruments with different issuance and liability treatment. Federal Reserve notes are Fed liabilities, U.S. coins are issued within the Treasury system, and coins held by the Fed are Fed assets.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
