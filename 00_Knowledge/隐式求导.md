# 隐式求导沿已存在的解支对关系式两边求导并解出未知导数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Implicit differentiation differentiates a relation along an existing solution branch and solves for the unknown derivative*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 当 $x$ 与 $y$ 由关系 $F(x,y)=0$ 联系、而 $y$ 已知在所考察点附近沿某条解支随 $x$ 变化时，隐式求导把它写成恒等式 $F(x,y(x))=0$，对 $x$ 求导，再解出 $y'(x)$。过程中每个含 $y$ 的项都要通过 [[链式法则]] 带出 $y'$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When $x$ and $y$ satisfy $F(x,y)=0$ and $y$ follows an existing local solution branch, implicit differentiation treats the relation as the identity $F(x,y(x))=0$, differentiates with respect to $x$, and solves for $y'(x)$. Every term involving $y$ must contribute a factor of $y'$ through the [[链式法则|chain rule]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对圆 $x^2+y^2=25$，
$$
2x+2yy'=0,
\qquad y'=-\frac{x}{y}.
$$
在 $(3,4)$ 处斜率为 $-3/4$。这里不是把 $y$ 当常数；$d(y^2)/dx=2yy'$ 正是在追踪沿圆周移动时 $y$ 对 $x$ 的反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the circle $x^2+y^2=25$, differentiation gives $2x+2yy'=0$ and hence $y'=-x/y$; at $(3,4)$ the slope is $-3/4$. The term $2yy'$ records that $y$ changes with $x$ along the circle rather than being treated as a constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算式只在相应局部解支存在且求导条件满足时解释为函数导数。标量情形若 $F_y\ne0$，[[隐函数导数公式]] 给出 $y'=-F_x/F_y$；若 $F_y=0$，这个分式不可用，但不能仅凭分母为零断言解支不存在，见 [[隐函数奇异块不推无解支]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The calculation represents a function derivative only on a valid local branch under the required smoothness. When $F_y\ne0$, the [[隐函数导数公式|implicit-function derivative formula]] gives $y'=-F_x/F_y$. If $F_y=0$, that formula is unavailable, but a zero denominator alone does not prove that no branch exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 对 $x^2+y^2=25$ 求导时，为什么 $d(y^2)/dx$ 不是 $2y$？
>
> **答案：** 因为沿解支 $y=y(x)$；链式法则给出 $d(y(x)^2)/dx=2y(x)y'(x)$。
