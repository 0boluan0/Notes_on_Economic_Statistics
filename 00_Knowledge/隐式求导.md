# Implicit differentiation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*隐式求导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sometimes an equation tells us how two quantities must stay related without giving one explicitly as a function of the other. A point moving on the circle $x^2+y^2=25$ must adjust its height $y$ when its horizontal position $x$ changes. **Implicit differentiation** finds that adjustment rate directly from the equation, without first solving it for $y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有时方程只规定两个量必须维持怎样的关系，并没有显式地把一个量写成另一个量的函数。点沿圆 $x^2+y^2=25$ 移动时，横坐标 $x$ 变化，纵坐标 $y$ 就必须随之调整。**隐式求导**直接从这个方程求出调整的变化率，不必先把 $y$ 解出来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The whole circle is not a single function of $x$: at $x=3$, it has both $y=4$ and $y=-4$. Near $(3,4)$, however, we can follow the upper branch $y(x)=\sqrt{25-x^2}$; near $(3,-4)$, we can follow the lower branch. A branch is a consistent local choice of solutions. To differentiate its equation, we need a **differentiable** branch, not just some solution at one point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

整圆不是 $x$ 的单个函数：在 $x=3$，既有 $y=4$，也有 $y=-4$。但在 $(3,4)$ 附近，可以沿上半圆分支 $y(x)=\sqrt{25-x^2}$；在 $(3,-4)$ 附近，可以沿下半圆分支。分支就是对局部解做一致的选择。要对它满足的方程求导，需要的是**可导分支**，而不只是某一点存在一个解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Remember that y moves with x
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*记住 y 也随 x 变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[导数|derivative]] $y'(x)$ is the local change of $y$ per unit change of $x$. The [[链式法则|chain rule]] handles a function nested inside another function. In $y(x)^2$, the outer function squares its input and the inner function is $y(x)$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[导数]] $y'(x)$ 是 $x$ 每改变一个单位时 $y$ 的局部变化率。[[链式法则]]处理函数里面套函数的情况。在 $y(x)^2$ 中，外层是平方，内层是 $y(x)$，因此

$$
\frac d{dx}\bigl(y(x)^2\bigr)=2y(x)y'(x).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiate the circle identity along the branch. [[导数线性法则|Linearity]] differentiates its sum term by term, and [[整数幂求导|the integer power rule]] differentiates the squares:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿着分支对圆的恒等式求导。[[导数线性法则]]允许对和逐项求导，[[整数幂求导]]负责平方项：

$$
x^2+y(x)^2=25
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
2x+2y(x)y'(x)=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 y'(x)=-\frac{x}{y(x)}\quad(y(x)\ne0).
$$

> [!example] The point selects the branch · 具体点决定分支
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $(3,4)$, the slope is $-3/4$: moving right makes the upper arc go down. At $(3,-4)$, it is $3/4$: moving right makes the lower arc go up. The same differentiated relation covers both branches, but the chosen point supplies a different value of $y$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $(3,4)$，斜率为 $-3/4$：向右移动时，上半圆向下走。在 $(3,-4)$，斜率为 $3/4$：向右移动时，下半圆向上走。同一个求导后的关系式适用于两个分支，但选定的点提供不同的 $y$ 值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Products need both sources of change
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*乘积中的两个变化来源都要保留*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $x^2+xy+y^2=7$ near $(1,2)$. The mixed term $xy(x)$ contains two changing factors. By the [[乘积法则|product rule]], its derivative is $y+xy'$, not just $xy'$ and not just $y$. Differentiate first, collect all the terms containing $y'$, and only then divide by their common coefficient:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $(1,2)$ 附近的关系 $x^2+xy+y^2=7$。混合项 $xy(x)$ 的两个因子都在变化，按[[乘积法则]]，其导数是 $y+xy'$，既不能只写 $xy'$，也不能只写 $y$。先求导，再把所有含 $y'$ 的项合并，最后才能除以它们的共同系数：

$$
2x+y+xy'+2yy'=0,
\qquad (x+2y)y'=-(2x+y),
$$
$$
y'=-\frac{2x+y}{x+2y}\quad(x+2y\ne0),
\qquad y'(1)=-\frac45\quad\text{on the branch through }(1,2).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This procedure explains why the inner $y'$ is needed, why mixed terms require an additional rule, and why a zero coefficient must be examined before division. It is not a rule to append $y'$ once to every term that happens to contain the letter $y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个过程说明了为什么需要内层的 $y'$，为什么混合项还需要乘积法则，以及为什么除法前必须检查系数是否为零。它并不是“每个含字母 $y$ 的项末尾一律补一个 $y'$”的机械规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Calculation and local existence answer different questions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*求导计算与局部存在性回答不同的问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a relation $F(x,y)=0$, the [[隐函数定理|implicit function theorem]] supplies a sufficient existence test. If $F$ has continuous first partial derivatives near a point on the curve and $F_y\ne0$ there, a unique differentiable local branch $y(x)$ exists near that point. Here the [[偏导数|partial derivative]] $F_y$ means changing $y$ while holding $x$ fixed. The [[隐函数导数公式|implicit derivative formula]] then summarizes the calculation as $y'=-F_x/F_y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对关系 $F(x,y)=0$，[[隐函数定理]]提供一个充分的存在性判据：若 $F$ 在曲线上某点附近有连续的一阶偏导数，且该点 $F_y\ne0$，就存在经过该点的唯一局部可导分支 $y(x)$。这里的[[偏导数]] $F_y$ 指保持 $x$ 不变，只改变 $y$ 时的变化率。随后，[[隐函数导数公式]]把计算概括为 $y'=-F_x/F_y$。

> [!warning] A failed division needs a new argument · 不能除时需要另作判断
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $(5,0)$ on the circle, $2y=0$ and $-x/y$ cannot be used. The circle has a vertical tangent there; it cannot be a differentiable $y(x)$ on an open interval around $x=5$. Switching the roles of the variables does work: $x(y)=\sqrt{25-y^2}$ has $dx/dy=0$ at $y=0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在圆上的 $(5,0)$，$2y=0$，不能使用 $-x/y$。这里是竖直切线，无法在 $x=5$ 周围的开区间上形成可导的 $y(x)$。交换变量角色却可行：$x(y)=\sqrt{25-y^2}$ 在 $y=0$ 的 $dx/dy=0$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A zero coefficient does not always mean that a differentiable branch is absent. For $y^3-x^3=0$, the real branch is $y=x$, with slope $1$ even at the origin; differentiating the equation at that point only gives $0=0$. See [[隐函数奇异块不推无解支|why a singular derivative block does not rule out a branch]] for this logical boundary.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 系数为零也不总意味着可导分支不存在。对 $y^3-x^3=0$，实数解支是 $y=x$，即使在原点斜率也是 $1$；在原点对方程求导却只得到 $0=0$。这个逻辑边界见[[隐函数奇异块不推无解支]]。
