# Real power rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*实数幂求导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A power function can have a fixed exponent that is not an integer: $\sqrt x=x^{1/2}$ and $x^{\sqrt2}$ are examples. The **real power rule** extends [[整数幂求导|the integer power rule]] to every fixed real exponent. To treat all such exponents with one definition and one proof, we begin on the positive half-line $x>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

幂函数的固定指数不一定是整数，例如 $\sqrt x=x^{1/2}$、$x^{\sqrt2}$。**实数幂求导法则**把[[整数幂求导]]推广到任意固定实数指数。为了用同一个定义和证明覆盖这些指数，我们先在正半轴 $x>0$ 上讨论。

> [!example] The square root near 4 · 4 附近的平方根
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With the fixed exponent $r=1/2$, the rule gives $d\sqrt x/dx=\tfrac12x^{-1/2}=1/(2\sqrt x)$ for $x>0$. At $x=4$ this derivative is $1/4$: increasing $x$ by a small $h$ increases $\sqrt x$ by approximately $h/4$. The same rule gives $d(x^{\sqrt2})/dx=\sqrt2\,x^{\sqrt2-1}$, even though the exponent is irrational.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定指数为 $r=1/2$ 时，在 $x>0$ 上得到 $d\sqrt x/dx=\tfrac12x^{-1/2}=1/(2\sqrt x)$。在 $x=4$ 处导数为 $1/4$：$x$ 增加很小的 $h$，平方根约增加 $h/4$。同一条规则还给出 $d(x^{\sqrt2})/dx=\sqrt2\,x^{\sqrt2-1}$，即使这个指数是无理数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Define the power before differentiating it
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先定义实数幂，再求导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] reverses the natural exponential. For $x>0$, define $x^r=\exp(r\ln x)$. This agrees with ordinary integer powers and positive-input rational powers, while giving meaning to irrational exponents. The number $r$ is a parameter held constant as $x$ changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[自然对数]]是自然指数的反函数。在 $x>0$ 上，定义 $x^r=\exp(r\ln x)$。它与普通整数幂、正输入的有理幂一致，同时也定义了无理指数的幂。这里 $r$ 是一个参数：$x$ 变化时，它保持不变。

> [!abstract] A fixed real exponent · 固定实数指数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every fixed $r\in\mathbb R$, the function $x^r=\exp(r\ln x)$ is differentiable on $x>0$, and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每个固定的 $r\in\mathbb R$，函数 $x^r=\exp(r\ln x)$ 在 $x>0$ 上可导，且
>
> $$\frac{d}{dx}x^r=rx^{r-1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof uses [[指数函数求导|the derivative of the natural exponential]], [[对数函数求导|the derivative of the logarithm]], and the [[链式法则|chain rule]]. The inner function $r\ln x$ has derivative $r/x$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明用到[[指数函数求导|自然指数函数的导数]]、[[对数函数求导|自然对数的导数]]和[[链式法则]]。内层 $r\ln x$ 的导数为 $r/x$，所以

$$\frac{d}{dx}\exp(r\ln x)
=\exp(r\ln x)\frac r x
=rx^{r-1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalently, [[对数求导|logarithmic differentiation]] starts with $y=x^r>0$, writes $\ln y=r\ln x$, and obtains $y'/y=r/x$. Multiplying by $y$ gives the same result. Both derivations use the same positive domain; the exponential definition already guarantees the differentiability needed by the logarithmic calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也可以用[[对数求导]]：从 $y=x^r>0$ 出发，写出 $\ln y=r\ln x$，再得到 $y'/y=r/x$；乘回 $y$ 后是同一结果。两种推导都使用相同的正定义域；指数形式的定义已经保证了对数求导时需要的可导性。

## What changes at negative inputs or at zero?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*负输入和零点需要另看什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The positive half-line is a common domain for **all** real exponents; it is not necessarily the largest domain for a particular exponent. Integer powers also allow negative inputs. If $r=p/q$ is a rational number in lowest terms with $q>0$ odd, the real $q$th root gives a branch at negative inputs as well; at nonzero negative inputs the power derivative has the same form on that branch. For example, $x^{1/3}=\sqrt[3]x$ is real for every $x$, and its derivative at $x=-8$ is $1/(3(-8)^{2/3})=1/12$. If the reduced denominator is even, such as $r=1/2$, there is no negative-input real branch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正半轴是**所有**实指数共同适用的定义域，不一定是某个具体指数的最大定义域。整数幂还能取负输入。若约分后的有理指数为 $r=p/q$，其中 $q>0$ 为奇数，实数 $q$ 次根也给出负输入上的分支；在非零负输入处，该分支的导数仍有同样形式。例如 $x^{1/3}=\sqrt[3]x$ 对所有实数都有定义，在 $x=-8$ 处导数为 $1/(3(-8)^{2/3})=1/12$。如果约分后的分母是偶数，如 $r=1/2$，就没有负输入的实值分支。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero must be checked separately from the original function and its difference quotient. The cube root is defined at zero but has no finite derivative there; $x^{4/3}=|x|^{4/3}$ is differentiable there with derivative zero. The square root has zero as an endpoint of its real domain and no finite right derivative there. These facts cannot be obtained by ignoring the $x>0$ condition of the uniform theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零点必须回到原函数及其差商单独检查。立方根在零点有定义，却没有有限导数；$x^{4/3}=|x|^{4/3}$ 在零点可导且导数为零。平方根的实定义域以零为端点，在那里也没有有限的右导数。这些判断不能靠忽略统一定理的 $x>0$ 条件来完成。

> [!warning] A moving exponent is a different calculation · 指数也变化时要重新求导
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $x^x$, the exponent is not a fixed parameter. Replacing $r$ by $x$ in the power-rule answer would lose a term. On $x>0$, [[对数求导|logarithmic differentiation]] gives $(x^x)'=x^x(1+\ln x)$. By contrast, for a differentiable positive base $u(x)$ and a **fixed** exponent $r$, the chain rule gives $(u(x)^r)'=r\,u(x)^{r-1}u'(x)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $x^x$ 的指数不是固定参数，不能把幂法则结果中的 $r$ 直接替换成 $x$，否则会漏项。在 $x>0$ 上，[[对数求导]]给出 $(x^x)'=x^x(1+\ln x)$。如果是可导的正底数 $u(x)$ 和**固定**指数 $r$，则由链式法则得到 $(u(x)^r)'=r\,u(x)^{r-1}u'(x)$。
