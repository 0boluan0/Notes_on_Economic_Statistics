# 水平外资溢出发生于同行企业之间而前后向溢出沿供应关系传递

外资溢出的水平、后向和前向，区分的是跨国企业与本地企业之间的潜在传递关系：水平通道连接同一行业中的企业；后向通道从外资客户连接本地上游供应商；前向通道从外资供应商连接本地下游客户。这是通道分类，不保证实际存在[[外资正向生产率溢出|正向生产率溢出]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Horizontal, backward and forward FDI spillovers distinguish potential links between multinational and domestic firms. Horizontal channels connect firms in the same industry; backward channels connect a foreign customer to upstream domestic suppliers; forward channels connect a foreign supplier to downstream domestic customers. This classifies channels without guaranteeing an actual [[外资正向生产率溢出|positive productivity spillover]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造例：外资自行车组装厂的生产方法被本地组装厂学习，是潜在水平通道；它帮助本地刹车供应商改进工艺，是潜在后向通道；外资材料厂向本地自行车厂提供更适用的材料或技术服务，是潜在前向通道。要判定溢出，还须检查生产率变化及收益是否被交易安排完全内部化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Learning an assembly method from a foreign bicycle assembler is a potential horizontal channel for a domestic assembler. Assistance from that foreign assembler to a domestic brake supplier is a potential backward channel. Better-suited materials or technical services from a foreign materials producer to a domestic bicycle producer form a potential forward channel. Establishing a spillover still requires examining productivity and whether contracts fully internalize the benefit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“后向”不是[[全球价值链参与方向|后向GVC参与]]的同义词：后者统计本国出口中的外国增加值；本卡按外资企业与供应商的联系方向分类，即使企业产品不出口也适用。既有客户关系可能帮助知识传递，但[[供应商联系与溢出|建立联系不等于已经证明溢出]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Backward” here is not synonymous with [[全球价值链参与方向|backward GVC participation]], which measures foreign value added in a country's exports. This card classifies supplier relationships around foreign firms and can apply even without exports. Customer relationships can facilitate knowledge transmission, but [[供应商联系与溢出|a linkage is not proof of a spillover]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
