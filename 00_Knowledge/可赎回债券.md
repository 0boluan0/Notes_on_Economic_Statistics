# 可赎回债券赋予发行人按约定时点与价格提前偿还债务的权利

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A callable bond gives its issuer the right to redeem the debt before maturity at the specified dates and prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可赎回债券（callable bond）在通常债务承诺之外，允许发行人决定是否按赎回条款提前结束部分或全部债务。选择权属于发行人，不属于持有人；必须先读允许赎回的日期、价格或价格计算规则、通知期和其他触发条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A callable bond adds an issuer-controlled choice to retire some or all of the debt early. The option belongs to the issuer, not the holder. Its exercise dates, price or pricing rule, notice period, and other conditions must be specified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如五年到期的债券允许发行人在第二年付息后按 102 赎回每 100 面值。发行人若依约行权，持有人收到约定赎回款及应付利息，之后不再收到被赎回部分的未来息票；若不行权，债务继续按原安排存续。这不等于投资者可在任何一天按 102 卖回。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, a five-year bond may permit redemption at 102 per 100 face value after the second-year coupon. Valid exercise pays the agreed redemption amount and interest due, ending future coupons on the redeemed portion. Without exercise, the debt continues. This does not give investors a right to sell back at 102 on any date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本文定义自愿赎回权。强制偿债基金摊还或特定事件下必须偿还，是合同义务而非同一种选择权；make-whole 条款的赎回价也可能随约定贴现规则变化，不能一律视为固定面值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This entry concerns optional redemption. Mandatory sinking-fund retirement or compulsory event-driven repayment is an obligation, not the same option. A make-whole redemption price may vary with a contractual discounting rule rather than equal a fixed par amount.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
