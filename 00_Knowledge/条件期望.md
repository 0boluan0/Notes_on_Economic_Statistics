# Conditional expectation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*条件期望*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **conditional expectation** is an average that uses specified information. If you know which bus route someone takes, you can average waiting times within that route instead of pooling every passenger together. The result depends on the information: different routes may have different average waits. The [[期望|expectation]] used here is a probability-weighted average in a model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**条件期望**是利用指定信息得到的平均值。知道一个人乘哪条公交线路后，就可以只在这条线路内平均等待时间，而不把所有乘客混在一起。不同线路可能对应不同的平均等待时间，所以结果随已知信息变化。这里的[[期望]]是模型中按概率加权的平均值。

> [!example] Knowing the route · 已知线路
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let the [[随机变量|random variable]] $X$ record the route as $0$ or $1$, and let $Y$ record the wait in minutes. Assume these are the only possibilities; the last column gives the probability of each pair.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用[[随机变量]] $X$ 把线路记为 $0$ 或 $1$，用 $Y$ 记录等待分钟数。假设只有下列可能，最后一列给出每个组合的概率。
>
> | $X$ | $Y$ | $P(X=x,Y=y)$ |
> |---|---|---|
> | $0$ | $0$ | $1/8$ |
> | $0$ | $4$ | $1/8$ |
> | $1$ | $4$ | $3/8$ |
> | $1$ | $8$ | $3/8$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Route $0$ has total probability $1/4$. Within that group, the [[条件概率|conditional probabilities]] of waits $0$ and $4$ are each $(1/8)/(1/4)=1/2$. Its mean wait is therefore $2$. Route $1$ has probability $3/4$ and equally likely waits $4$ and $8$, giving mean $6$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 线路 $0$ 的总概率是 $1/4$。在这一组内，等待 $0$ 和 $4$ 分钟的[[条件概率]]分别为 $(1/8)/(1/4)=1/2$，所以组内平均等待时间是 $2$。线路 $1$ 的概率是 $3/4$，组内等概率等待 $4$ 或 $8$ 分钟，平均值是 $6$。

## 为什么它仍是随机变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why it is still a random variable*

Fixing a route gives a **number**: $E[Y\mid X=0]=2$ and $E[Y\mid X=1]=6$. Collect these answers into a rule $g$, with $g(0)=2$ and $g(1)=6$. Applying that rule to the random route $X$ gives a **random variable**:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定一条线路后，得到的是一个**数值**：$E[Y\mid X=0]=2$，$E[Y\mid X=1]=6$。把这些答案组成规则 $g$，即 $g(0)=2$、$g(1)=6$。再把随机的线路 $X$ 代入，就得到一个**随机变量**：

$$
E[Y\mid X]=g(X)=\begin{cases}2,&X=0,\\6,&X=1.\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before seeing the route, this conditional mean equals $2$ with probability $1/4$ and $6$ with probability $3/4$. Once the route is observed, its value is known, although the actual wait can still vary. In general, for discrete $Y$ and $P(X=x)>0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看到线路之前，这个条件均值以 $1/4$ 的概率等于 $2$，以 $3/4$ 的概率等于 $6$。看到线路之后，条件均值确定了，实际等待时间却仍可能不同。一般地，当 $Y$ 离散且 $P(X=x)>0$ 时，

$$E[Y\mid X=x]=\sum_y y\,P(Y=y\mid X=x).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sum runs over possible waits $y$, using their probabilities **within the selected group**. A finite conditional mean is guaranteed here if $Y$ is integrable, meaning $E[|Y|]<\infty$; the [[矩存在性的使用边界|moment-existence conditions]] explain why this assumption matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求和遍历可能的等待时间 $y$，权重是它们**在选定组内**的概率。如果 $Y$ 可积，即 $E[|Y|]<\infty$，这里的条件均值保证有限；[[矩存在性的使用边界]]解释了为何要检查这一条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Expressing “use only this information” precisely
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把“只使用这些信息”写准确*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]] $\mathcal G$ records the events distinguishable with the available information. For the bus example, knowing $X$ lets us distinguish the two route groups, but not the waits within each group. This information is written $\sigma(X)$, and $E[Y\mid X]$ means $E[Y\mid\sigma(X)]$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[σ-代数]] $\mathcal G$ 记录利用现有信息能够辨认的事件。在公交例子中，知道 $X$ 可以区分两条线路，却不能区分同一线路内的等待时间。这些信息记作 $\sigma(X)$，而 $E[Y\mid X]$ 就是 $E[Y\mid\sigma(X)]$。

> [!abstract] The general definition · 一般定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On a [[概率空间|probability space]] $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$, assume $E[|Y|]<\infty$ and $\mathcal G\subseteq\mathcal F$. A conditional expectation $M=E[Y\mid\mathcal G]$ is integrable and $\mathcal G$-measurable: it uses only the specified information. It must also preserve the original average contribution on every event $A\in\mathcal G$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在[[概率空间]] $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ 上，假设 $E[|Y|]<\infty$ 且 $\mathcal G\subseteq\mathcal F$。条件期望 $M=E[Y\mid\mathcal G]$ 要可积、对 $\mathcal G$ 可测，即只使用指定的信息；同时，对每个 $A\in\mathcal G$，它要保留原变量在该事件上的平均贡献：
>
> $$E[M\mathbf1_A]=E[Y\mathbf1_A].$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[事件指示变量|indicator]] $\mathbf1_A$ is $1$ on $A$ and $0$ outside it, so it selects that event's contribution. Such an $M$ exists and is unique up to equality with probability one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[事件指示变量]] $\mathbf1_A$ 在 $A$ 发生时等于 $1$，其余时候为 $0$，因此选出该事件的贡献。这样的 $M$ 存在，并且在“以概率 $1$ 相等”的意义下唯一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A=\{X=0\}$, the definition reads $2\times(1/4)=0\times(1/8)+4\times(1/8)$. Replacing each wait by its group mean preserves the group's contribution to the overall average. Measurability is essential too: $Y$ itself preserves these contributions, but usually uses more information than the route reveals. The general definition also works when information cannot be represented by a finite list of groups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $A=\{X=0\}$，定义要求的就是 $2\times(1/4)=0\times(1/8)+4\times(1/8)$：把每次等待换成所属组的平均值，保留了该组对总体平均的贡献。可测性也必不可少：$Y$ 自己当然保留这些贡献，但它通常使用了线路信息之外的具体等待结果。一般定义也适用于无法用有限个组表示的信息。

## 两个边界例与唯一性边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Two boundary cases and the uniqueness boundary*

With no information beyond “something in $\Omega$ occurred”, $E[Y\mid\mathcal G]=E[Y]$ almost surely. If the information already determines $Y$, then $E[Y\mid\mathcal G]=Y$ almost surely. Between these extremes, the conditional mean uses what is known and averages what remains unknown.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果除了“$\Omega$ 中某个结果发生了”之外没有任何信息，那么 $E[Y\mid\mathcal G]=E[Y]$ 几乎处处成立。如果信息已经确定了 $Y$，那么 $E[Y\mid\mathcal G]=Y$ 几乎处处成立。两者之间，条件均值使用已知部分，对仍未知的部分取平均。

> [!warning] Conditioning on a zero-probability value · 条件取值概率为零时
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The discrete ratio formula cannot be used when $P(X=x)=0$. Nevertheless, for real-valued $X$ and integrable $Y$, the general definition still gives $E[Y\mid X]=g(X)$ for a measurable function $g$. This includes continuous $X$. Two valid choices of $g$ may differ on a measurable set $N$ with $P(X\in N)=0$; they give the same random variable almost surely. The definition alone therefore does not fix the value at a particular null point. It does **not** allow arbitrary changes at all points just because each individual point has probability zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $P(X=x)=0$ 时，不能使用离散的概率比值公式。但对于实值 $X$ 和可积 $Y$，一般定义仍给出 $E[Y\mid X]=g(X)$，其中 $g$ 是可测函数；连续 $X$ 也包括在内。两个合法的 $g$ 可以在满足 $P(X\in N)=0$ 的可测集合 $N$ 上不同，它们代入 $X$ 后仍几乎处处相等。因此，仅凭定义无法确定某个零概率点的取值；不能因为每一个单点概率都是零，就把所有点的值都任意改掉。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Averaging the conditional means across information states recovers the overall mean; this is the [[全期望公式|law of total expectation]]. Measuring the spread remaining around a conditional mean leads to [[条件方差|conditional variance]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再对不同信息状态下的条件均值取平均，就恢复总体均值，这就是[[全期望公式]]。考察结果围绕条件均值还剩多少波动，则得到[[条件方差]]。
