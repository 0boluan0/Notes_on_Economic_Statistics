# 完全相关的两资产只有满足相应带符号波动抵消条件才能组成零方差组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Perfectly correlated assets form a zero-variance portfolio only at weights that cancel their signed volatility exposures*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设两项资产标准差均严格为正、权重为 $(w,1-w)$。当相关系数为 $\pm1$，[[组合均值与方差|组合方差]]变成一个线性式的平方；必须让这个线性式为零，才能精确抵消波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With positive asset standard deviations and weights $(w,1-w)$, correlation $\pm1$ turns portfolio variance into the square of a linear expression. Exact cancellation requires that expression to vanish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 相关性 | 标准差，必须保留绝对值 | 零方差权重 $w$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $\rho=-1$ | $\lvert w\sigma_A-(1-w)\sigma_B\rvert$ | $\sigma_B/(\sigma_A+\sigma_B)$ |
| $\rho=1$ | $\lvert w\sigma_A+(1-w)\sigma_B\rvert$ | $\sigma_B/(\sigma_B-\sigma_A)$，须 $\sigma_A\ne\sigma_B$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For correlation $-1$, cancellation uses $w=\sigma_B/(\sigma_A+\sigma_B)$ and two long positions. For correlation $+1$, it requires unequal volatilities and $w=\sigma_B/(\sigma_B-\sigma_A)$, involving a short position. Standard deviation is the absolute value of the signed exposure, never a potentially negative expression.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\sigma_A=20\%,\sigma_B=30\%$：完全负相关时权重 $(0.6,0.4)$；完全正相关时权重 $(3,-2)$。若完全正相关且两者波动相同，任何预算和为 1 的权重都留下同样的共同波动，不能造出零方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At volatilities $20\%$ and $30\%$, the cancelling weights are $(0.6,0.4)$ for perfect negative correlation and $(3,-2)$ for perfect positive correlation. Equal volatilities with perfect positive correlation leave the same common shock under every fully invested combination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是理想单期模型的恒等式，不是“历史相关性接近 $-1$ 就无风险”。零方差只说明给定分布下回报几乎处处为常数，并不自动保证正回报；若该组合可双向交易且可按 $r_f$ 自由借贷，无套利要求其确定回报等于 $r_f$。此时协方差矩阵奇异，不能直接套用要求逆矩阵的[[全局最小方差解]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an exact one-period statement, not a promise based on an estimated correlation near $-1$. Zero variance means an almost-surely constant return, not necessarily a positive return. If the portfolio can be traded in either direction and borrowing/lending at $r_f$ is unrestricted, no arbitrage requires its constant return to equal $r_f$. Perfect correlation makes the covariance matrix singular, so inverse-covariance formulas do not apply directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
