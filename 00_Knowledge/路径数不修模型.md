# More paths and model error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*路径数与模型误差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a program is meant to estimate the probability of heads for a fair coin, but its input generator produces heads with probability $0.6$. Running it a million times can give an extremely stable answer near $0.6$. That stability answers the question encoded in the program; it does not recover the intended answer $0.5$. **Increasing the path count repeats the chosen experiment. It does not, by itself, change which experiment is being simulated.**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设程序本想估计公平硬币的正面概率，实际输入却以 $0.6$ 的概率产生正面。运行一百万次，可以得到极稳定、接近 $0.6$ 的答案。稳定性回答的是程序实际编码的问题，不会自动找回原本的 $0.5$。**增加路径数，是重复选定的试验；它本身不会改变究竟模拟了哪个试验。**

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In [[Monte Carlo估计|Monte Carlo estimation]], a path means one simulated input or trajectory, from which an output is computed. The [[Monte Carlo误差分层|error-layer distinction]] separates random sampling fluctuation from the model, numerical approximation and implementation. This card explains why averaging more outputs addresses only the sampling part when the other choices are held fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 [[Monte Carlo估计|Monte Carlo 估计]]中，一条路径指一次模拟输入或轨迹，再据此计算输出。[[Monte Carlo误差分层]]把随机抽样波动与模型、数值近似、程序实现区分开。本卡说明：其他选择固定时，为什么平均更多输出只能处理其中的抽样部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Name both the intended and implemented targets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先写出想算的量和实际在算的量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X$ describe the input, and let $P$ be the intended [[概率空间|probability model]], assigning probabilities to possible input events. Let $g(X)$ be the real-valued output of interest. The intended [[期望|expectation]] is $\mu=E_P[g(X)]$: the subscript $P$ tells us which probabilities are used in averaging. Assume $E_P|g(X)|<\infty$, so this target is finite. The program instead uses a distribution $\widetilde P$ and a rule $\widetilde g$. A tilde marks the implemented choice, which may or may not match the intended one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X$ 描述输入，$P$ 是原本想用的[[概率空间|概率模型]]，给可能的输入事件分配概率；$g(X)$ 是关心的实数输出。目标[[期望]]为 $\mu=E_P[g(X)]$，下标 $P$ 说明按哪些概率进行加权平均。假设 $E_P|g(X)|<\infty$，因此目标有限。程序实际使用的分布和计算规则记为 $\widetilde P$、$\widetilde g$；波浪号标记实际实现的选择，它们可能与原计划相同，也可能不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the basic averaging argument, assume the implemented inputs $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ are [[独立同分布|iid]] under $\widetilde P$ and $E_{\widetilde P}|\widetilde g(X_1)|<\infty$. With $N$ a positive integer, define the average and its implemented target:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本平均论证假设：程序输入 $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ 按 $\widetilde P$ [[独立同分布|iid]]，且 $E_{\widetilde P}|\widetilde g(X_1)|<\infty$。用正整数 $N$ 表示路径数，定义平均值与实际实现的目标：

$$\widehat\mu_N=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N\widetilde g(X_i),\qquad \widetilde\mu=E_{\widetilde P}[\widetilde g(X_1)].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[强大数定律|iid integrable strong law]] gives $\widehat\mu_N\to\widetilde\mu$ [[几乎必然收敛|almost surely]], meaning that the running average tends to this value along a probability-one set of entire runs. It is a statement about $\widetilde\mu$, not a theorem that $\widetilde\mu=\mu$. Finite variance is not needed for this convergence statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[强大数定律|可积 iid 强大数定律]]，$\widehat\mu_N\to\widetilde\mu$ [[几乎必然收敛|几乎必然成立]]，也就是在概率为一的一组完整运行中，逐步平均会趋向这个值。定理谈的是 $\widetilde\mu$，并没有证明它等于 $\mu$；这项收敛结论也不需要有限方差。

> [!important] Two terms with different remedies · 两种误差需要不同处理
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Write $b=\widetilde\mu-\mu$, a fixed target discrepancy when the model and program are fixed. Then
> $$\widehat\mu_N-\mu=(\widehat\mu_N-\widetilde\mu)+b.$$
> The first term is sampling fluctuation around the implemented mean. The second is unchanged by repeating the same sampling rule. If $b\ne0$, the estimation error tends to $b$, not zero. A different distribution or rule does not necessarily change this particular expectation: two errors can cancel, or the target may be insensitive to their difference. Agreement on one number therefore does not validate the whole model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 模型与程序固定时，记目标差 $b=\widetilde\mu-\mu$。如上分解中，第一项是围绕实际均值的抽样波动，第二项不会因重复同一抽样规则而改变。若 $b\ne0$，估计误差趋向 $b$，而不是零。反过来，分布或规则不同，也不一定改变这一个期望：错误可能抵消，或者所关心的量对这项差异不敏感。因此，一个数字吻合也不能验证整个模型。

## A shrinking standard error can coexist with a bias floor
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*标准误在缩小，偏差仍留下误差下限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now add the [[矩存在性的使用边界|finite-second-moment condition]] $E_{\widetilde P}[\widetilde g(X_1)^2]<\infty$. Let $\widetilde\sigma^2$ be the [[方差|variance]] of one implemented output, and let $\widetilde\sigma=\sqrt{\widetilde\sigma^2}\ge0$. At a fixed, prespecified $N$, the [[Monte Carlo均值标准误|Monte Carlo mean standard error]] and [[均方误差|mean squared error]] relative to the intended target satisfy
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再增加[[矩存在性的使用边界|有限二阶矩条件]] $E_{\widetilde P}[\widetilde g(X_1)^2]<\infty$。用 $\widetilde\sigma^2$ 表示一次实际输出的[[方差]]，并令 $\widetilde\sigma=\sqrt{\widetilde\sigma^2}\ge0$。对于事先选定、固定的 $N$，[[Monte Carlo均值标准误]]与相对于原目标的[[均方误差]]满足

$$SE(\widehat\mu_N)=\frac{\widetilde\sigma}{\sqrt N},\qquad E[(\widehat\mu_N-\mu)^2]=\frac{\widetilde\sigma^2}{N}+b^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the second identity, square the two-term error decomposition. Its cross term has expectation zero because $E[\widehat\mu_N-\widetilde\mu]=0$; the remaining random term has variance $\widetilde\sigma^2/N$. Thus four times as many paths exactly halve the true standard error in this setting, while $b^2$ remains in the mean squared error. This does not say that every realised error decreases when another path is added.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二个恒等式可以直接看：把两项误差分解平方，由于 $E[\widehat\mu_N-\widetilde\mu]=0$，交叉项的期望为零；剩余随机项的方差是 $\widetilde\sigma^2/N$。因此在这些条件下，路径数变成四倍会精确地把真实标准误减半，但均方误差里仍留下 $b^2$。这不是说每加一条路径，实际误差都会减小。

> [!example] Fair-coin target, biased-coin program · 公平硬币目标，有偏硬币程序
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let the output be the [[事件指示变量|indicator]] of heads: $1$ for heads and $0$ otherwise. The intended probability is $0.5$, while independent program outputs have heads probability $0.6$. Thus $\mu=0.5$, $\widetilde\mu=0.6$, $b=0.1$, and $\widetilde\sigma^2=0.6(1-0.6)=0.24$.
>
> At $N=100$, the true standard error is about $0.0490$ and the mean squared error is $0.24/100+0.01=0.0124$. At $N=400$, they are about $0.0245$ and $0.0106$. The simulation becomes more precise around $0.6$; its mean squared error against $0.5$ tends to $0.01$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 输出取正面的[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]：正面为 $1$，否则为 $0$。原目标概率为 $0.5$，程序独立输出正面的概率却为 $0.6$，所以 $\mu=0.5$、$\widetilde\mu=0.6$、$b=0.1$，且 $\widetilde\sigma^2=0.6(1-0.6)=0.24$。
>
> $N=100$ 时，真实标准误约 $0.0490$，均方误差为 $0.0124$；$N=400$ 时，分别约为 $0.0245$ 和 $0.0106$。模拟围绕 $0.6$ 越来越精确，但相对于 $0.5$ 的均方误差趋向 $0.01$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use extra paths after identifying the error layer
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先辨明误差层，再决定是否增加路径*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an exact benchmark is available, compare like with like: the same target, parameter values and units. [[Monte Carlo实现验证|Implementation verification]] checks the declared calculation with controlled inputs; numerical refinement changes a grid or time step; model assessment compares the chosen assumptions with evidence for the intended use. Adding paths changes none of these choices automatically. When fitted parameters are held fixed during simulation, the resulting Monte Carlo standard error also does not automatically include uncertainty from fitting them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若有解析基准，先确认比较的是同一目标、参数值和单位。[[Monte Carlo实现验证]]用受控输入检查程序是否执行了声明的计算；数值加密改变网格或时间步长；模型评估则针对预定用途，把假设与相关证据比较。增加路径不会自动完成这些修改。把估计出来的参数固定后再模拟，所得 Monte Carlo 标准误也不会自动包括参数估计的不确定性。

> [!question] A smaller reported error bar · 更小的报告标准误
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The path count rises from 25,000 to 100,000. The estimated standard error falls from roughly $0.8$ to $0.4$, but the answer remains about $3$ above an analytic benchmark. Has the program been validated? No. Halving the reported standard error is compatible with the iid square-root rule, but is not evidence that the target, code or numerical approximation is correct. Investigate the discrepancy and whether the standard-error assumptions are reliable; rare missed events or dependence can also make an apparently stable estimate misleading.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 路径数从 25,000 增至 100,000，估计标准误约从 $0.8$ 降至 $0.4$，答案却一直比解析基准高约 $3$。程序是否已经通过验证？没有。标准误减半与 iid 平方根规律相容，却不能证明目标、代码或数值近似正确。应调查偏离，也要检查标准误假设是否可靠；尚未遇到的稀有事件或依赖，同样可能让看似稳定的估计产生误导。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formulas above concern a mean of iid outputs, not every simulation design. [[VaR采样误差|Quantile sampling error]] needs its own distributional conditions; correlated sampling, MCMC, QMC and nested simulation require their own convergence and error arguments. If a numerical approximation or fitted model is changed as $N$ grows, the implemented target can change too: then analyse both changes rather than attributing improvement to path count alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述公式针对 iid 输出均值，不覆盖所有模拟设计。[[VaR采样误差|分位数抽样误差]]需要自己的分布条件；相关抽样、MCMC、QMC 和嵌套模拟也需要各自的收敛与误差论证。如果数值近似或拟合模型随着 $N$ 增长也在改变，实际目标也可能变化；此时要分析两种变化，不能把改进全部归给路径数。

Owen, [*Monte Carlo Theory, Methods and Examples*, Chapter 2, §§2.1–2.2, pp. 15–18](https://artowen.su.domains/mc/Ch-intro.pdf#page=15): the iid law-of-large-numbers target, variance/standard error, and separation of sampling error from model and parameter errors (p. 17). The two-target decomposition and coin arithmetic are direct calculations.
- MIT 18.05, [Reading 6b, §2.1, pp. 1–2, and §5.2, p. 7](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-05-introduction-to-probability-and-statistics-spring-2022/mit18_05_s22_class06-prep-b.pdf): averaging cannot repair systematic measurement error; iid mean and variance identities. The finite-variance normal approximation is not used to justify the more general integrable strong law.
- [[VaR采样误差|VaR sampling error]] carries the separate fixed-quantile asymptotic formula and its assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Owen 第 2 章第 2.1–2.2 节，第 15–18 页：核对 iid 大数定律的目标、方差和标准误，第 17 页区分模型/参数错误与抽样误差。两个目标的误差分解与硬币例子直接计算。
- MIT 18.05 Reading 6b 第 2.1 节第 1–2 页、5.2 节第 7 页：核对平均不能修复系统测量误差，以及 iid 均值方差恒等式；不拿有限方差的正态近似代替可积强大数定律。
- [[VaR采样误差]]保留固定分位水平的专门渐近公式和条件。
