# 增加 Monte Carlo 路径只降低给定模型下的抽样误差，不能修复模型误设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*More Monte Carlo paths reduce sampling error under a fixed model but do not repair model misspecification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 更精确地逼近错误对象，仍然是错误
> 路径数控制有限样本造成的随机波动。若抽样分布、参数、依赖、状态变量或目标函数写错，增加路径只会让程序更稳定地逼近这个错误对象。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Path count controls finite-sample fluctuation. If the sampling law, parameters, dependence, state variables, or target function are wrong, more paths only estimate the wrong object more precisely.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设真正关心的量为

$$
\mu=E_P[g(X)],
$$

但程序实际从 $\widetilde P$ 抽样并计算 $\widetilde g$。即使大数定律使模拟均值收敛，也只是

$$
\widehat\mu_N
\longrightarrow
E_{\widetilde P}[\widetilde g(X)],
$$

而这个极限不必等于 $\mu$。增加 $N$ 能收缩围绕错误极限的抽样波动，却不会让 $\widetilde P$ 自动变成 $P$，也不会让 $\widetilde g$ 自动改对。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a program samples from $\widetilde P$ and evaluates $\widetilde g$, its large-sample limit is $E_{\widetilde P}[\widetilde g(X)]$, not automatically the intended $E_P[g(X)]$. Increasing $N$ contracts fluctuation around that implemented target without correcting the target itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 i.i.d. 有限方差均值基准下，四倍路径通常只把标准误减半；它没有改变模型偏差、代码错误或离散化偏差。风险分位数的专门抽样公式与条件由 [[VaR采样误差]] 承载，不在本卡重复。MCMC、QMC、相关抽样和嵌套模拟也不必服从同一 $N^{-1/2}$ 速率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an iid finite-variance mean, four times as many draws typically halve the standard error. They do not change model bias, implementation mistakes, or discretisation bias. The specialised sampling law for risk quantiles remains in the VaR sampling-error atom, and other simulation designs need not share the same rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检
> 路径数从 25,000 增至 100,000 后，标准误约从 0.8 降至 0.4，但估计均值始终比解析答案高 3。能否称程序已经正确？
>
> **答案：** 不能。标准误减半符合平方根律；固定约 3 的偏离仍指向目标、模型、实现或数值近似问题。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. The halved standard error matches the square-root law, but the persistent gap of about three still indicates an estimand, model, implementation, or numerical-approximation problem.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
