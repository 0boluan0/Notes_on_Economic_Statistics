# Random variable
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*随机变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **random variable** is a rule for recording the outcome of an uncertain experiment as a number. For a coin toss, we might record heads as $1$ and tails as $0$. For a die roll, we could record the face value or just whether it is at least $5$. The rule is fixed in advance; the number we observe depends on which outcome happens.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**随机变量**是一条记录规则：它把一次不确定试验的结果记成一个数。抛硬币时，可以把正面记作 $1$、反面记作 $0$；掷骰子时，可以记录点数，也可以只记录“点数是否至少为 $5$”。规则事先确定，最后观察到的数字取决于实际发生了哪个结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One experiment, different numbers to record
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同一次试验，可以记录不同的数字*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one six-sided die, the possible outcomes form the **sample space** $\Omega=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$. A set of outcomes is an **event**; these ideas and their probabilities are introduced in [[离散概率空间|discrete probability spaces]]. Write $\omega$ for one outcome. A random variable called $X$ assigns it the number $X(\omega)$, read as “the value of $X$ at outcome $\omega$”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于一枚六面骰子，可能的结果组成**样本空间** $\Omega=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$，其中的一组结果叫**事件**；[[离散概率空间]]介绍了这些概念和概率如何分配。用 $\omega$ 表示某个基本结果，名为 $X$ 的随机变量会给它指定数字 $X(\omega)$，读作“结果为 $\omega$ 时，$X$ 的值”。

> [!example] Recording whether the result is at least 5 · 记录点数是否至少为 5
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Define $X$ to record $1$ for a result of $5$ or $6$, and $0$ otherwise. Define $Y$ to record the face value itself. The two rules give:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 规定：掷出 $5$ 或 $6$ 时，$X$ 记作 $1$，其余结果都记作 $0$。再用 $Y$ 直接记录点数。两条规则如下：
>
> | $\omega$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ | $5$ | $6$ |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | $X(\omega)$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $0$ | $1$ | $1$ |
> | $Y(\omega)$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ | $5$ | $6$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the die lands on $6$, the same experiment produces $X(6)=1$ and $Y(6)=6$. Different outcomes may produce the same value of $X$: both $5$ and $6$ are recorded as $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假如掷出了 $6$，同一次试验就得到 $X(6)=1$ 和 $Y(6)=6$。不同结果也可以对应同一个 $X$ 值：$5$ 和 $6$ 都被记成 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The letter $X$ names the whole recording rule. A lowercase $x$ usually names a particular numerical value that we ask about, such as $x=1$. Keeping the rule, the original outcome, and the recorded value separate helps us read probability statements correctly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

大写字母 $X$ 表示整条记录规则，小写 $x$ 通常表示我们正在讨论的某个数值，例如 $x=1$。把“规则”“原始结果”和“记录下来的值”分清楚，就更容易读懂概率表达式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## How does a random variable get its probabilities?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*随机变量的概率从哪里来？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the example, the statement $X=1$ is true exactly when the die lands on $5$ or $6$. Thus $P(X=1)$ asks for the probability of the event $\{5,6\}$. For a fair die,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在刚才的例子里，$X=1$ 恰好对应骰子掷出 $5$ 或 $6$。所以 $P(X=1)$ 问的就是事件 $\{5,6\}$ 的概率。若骰子公平，

$$
P(X=1)=P(\{5,6\})=\frac16+\frac16=\frac13.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We did not assign a new probability independently of the experiment: we grouped the original outcomes that $X$ records as the same number. For a variable with finitely or countably many values, the resulting table of values and probabilities is its [[概率质量函数|probability mass function]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

我们没有脱离原试验另行指定概率，而是把被 $X$ 记成同一个数的原始结果归到一起。当变量的取值只有有限个或可数多个时，把各个值和对应概率列成表，就得到它的[[概率质量函数]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same idea works for more complicated experiments. If two dice produce an ordered pair $(i,j)$, one variable can record the sum $i+j$, another the larger value $\max(i,j)$, and another a payoff determined by the pair. They share the experiment but answer different numerical questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更复杂的试验也是一样。掷两枚骰子得到有序数对 $(i,j)$，可以用一个变量记录和 $i+j$，另一个记录较大的点数 $\max(i,j)$，再一个记录由这个数对决定的收益。它们来自同一次试验，却回答不同的数值问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the formal definition says “measurable”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*正式定义为什么还要说“可测”？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a general [[概率空间|probability space]] $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$, $\Omega$ contains the outcomes, $\mathcal F$ lists the sets that count as events, and $P$ assigns probabilities to those events. The event collection $\mathcal F$ is a [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]]: it is closed under complements and countable unions. This matters because a probability is defined only for an event in $\mathcal F$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在一般的[[概率空间]] $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ 中，$\Omega$ 装着基本结果，$\mathcal F$ 指定哪些结果集合可以作为事件，$P$ 给这些事件分配概率。事件族 $\mathcal F$ 是一个 [[σ-代数]]：取补集或对可数个事件取并集，仍然得到其中的事件。这里关键的是：只有属于 $\mathcal F$ 的事件才有定义好的概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To ask about the numerical output of $X$, we choose a set $A$ of numbers, for example the interval $[0,1]$. We then collect all original outcomes whose recorded values lie in $A$. **Measurability** requires this collection to be an event. In the usual real-valued definition, this must work for every **Borel set** $A$; Borel sets form the smallest sigma-algebra containing all open intervals, and include the intervals and individual points used here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要问 $X$ 的数值落在哪里，我们先选一个数值集合 $A$，例如区间 $[0,1]$，再找出哪些原始结果会被记录成 $A$ 里面的数。**可测性**要求这些原始结果组成一个合法事件。通常的实值随机变量定义要求：对每个 **Borel 集** $A$ 都如此。Borel 集组成包含所有开区间的最小 σ-代数，也包括这里用到的区间和单个点。

> [!abstract] The real-valued definition · 实值随机变量的定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real random variable is a measurable function
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 实随机变量是可测函数
>
> $$X:(\Omega,\mathcal F)\longrightarrow(\mathbb R,\mathcal B(\mathbb R)).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $\mathbb R$ is the real number line and $\mathcal B(\mathbb R)$ is its collection of Borel sets. “Measurable” means that, for every $A\in\mathcal B(\mathbb R)$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 其中 $\mathbb R$ 是实数轴，$\mathcal B(\mathbb R)$ 是它的 Borel 集族。“可测”就是：对每个 $A\in\mathcal B(\mathbb R)$，都有
>
> $$\{X\in A\}=\{\omega\in\Omega:X(\omega)\in A\}\in\mathcal F.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The middle expression means “all outcomes $\omega$ for which $X(\omega)$ belongs to $A$”. Because this set is in $\mathcal F$, we may take its probability, written $P(X\in A)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 中间的表达式读作：“所有使得 $X(\omega)$ 属于 $A$ 的基本结果 $\omega$”。这个集合属于 $\mathcal F$，因此可以求它的概率，记作 $P(X\in A)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite or countable sample space where every subset is an event, every real-valued recording rule automatically meets this condition. The notation for that event collection is $\mathcal F=2^\Omega$, the set of all subsets of $\Omega$. In general spaces, measurability remains part of the definition and must not be omitted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于有限或可数的样本空间，如果每个子集都是事件，那么任何实数值记录规则都会自动满足这个条件。“所有子集组成的集合”记作 $2^\Omega$，所以这种情形写作 $\mathcal F=2^\Omega$。但在一般空间中，可测性仍是定义的一部分，不能省略。
