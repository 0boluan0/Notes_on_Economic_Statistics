# 外国直接投资是一个经济体的投资者对另一经济体企业形成持久利益和重大影响的跨境投资

外国直接投资（foreign direct investment，FDI）是一个经济体的常住投资者，对另一经济体的企业形成持久利益、并能对其经营管理施加重大影响的跨境投资关系。这里的跨境按经济体的居民身份区分，不按投资者国籍区分；直接投资也不要求投资者完全控制企业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Foreign direct investment is a cross-border investment relationship in which a resident investor in one economy has a lasting interest and significant influence over the management of an enterprise resident in another economy. The distinction is based on economic residence, not nationality; direct investment does not require full control.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OECD第五版统计标准以直接或间接持有至少10%的投票权作为直接投资关系的识别门槛，并按直接投资关系框架识别间接关系。10%指投票权，不是任意金融资产占比，也不是“超过一半”的控制门槛。简单直接持股例中，一名境外投资者取得12%的投票权即可满足这一统计门槛，即使其并不控制企业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The OECD fifth-edition statistical standard identifies a direct-investment relationship through ownership of at least 10% of voting power, directly or indirectly, using its Framework for Direct Investment Relationships for indirect links. The threshold concerns voting power, not an arbitrary share of financial assets, and is distinct from majority control. In a simple direct holding, a nonresident investor with 12% of voting power meets the statistical threshold without necessarily controlling the enterprise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

FDI统计可以包括股权、再投资收益和符合规定的关联企业债务；不能把所有外国贷款都归为FDI。FDI流入也不等于新增厂房设备：收购既有企业是所有权交易，再投资收益可以在没有新增跨境现金汇款时被记录。新产能、就业和[[外资正向生产率溢出|本地生产率溢出]]须分别识别，而不能由一个FDI流量数字直接推出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
FDI statistics can include equity, reinvested earnings and qualifying debt between related enterprises; not every foreign loan is FDI. Inflows also do not equal new plant and equipment: acquiring an existing firm is an ownership transaction, and reinvested earnings can be recorded without a new cross-border cash remittance. New capacity, employment and [[外资正向生产率溢出|domestic productivity spillovers]] must be identified separately rather than inferred from an FDI-flow figure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OECD supplies the residence-based definition, voting-power threshold, reinvested-earnings treatment and the distinction between FDI transactions and new productive capacity. The 12% holding is a simple constructed example, not a rule for calculating indirect ownership chains.
