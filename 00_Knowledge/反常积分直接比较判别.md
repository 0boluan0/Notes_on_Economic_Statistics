# Direct comparison for improper integrals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*反常积分直接比较判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A difficult total can sometimes be controlled by an easier, larger one. **Direct comparison** uses a point-by-point inequality to decide whether an [[反常积分|improper integral]] of a nonnegative function has a finite value. To prove convergence, find a larger function with a finite integral; to prove divergence, find a smaller function whose integral is already infinite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个难求的总量，有时可以用更大而容易处理的总量来控制。**直接比较判别**通过函数逐点的大小关系，判断非负函数的[[反常积分]]是否有限。证明收敛，寻找积分有限的上界函数；证明发散，寻找积分已经无穷的下界函数。

> [!example] A Gaussian tail under an exponential tail · 用指数尾部盖住高斯尾部
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $x\ge1$, we have $x^2\ge x$, hence $-x^2\le-x$. The [[自然指数函数|exponential function]] preserves this order, so $0<e^{-x^2}\le e^{-x}$. Every finite cutoff $R>1$ therefore satisfies
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $x\ge1$，有 $x^2\ge x$，因而 $-x^2\le-x$。[[自然指数函数]]保持这个大小顺序，所以 $0<e^{-x^2}\le e^{-x}$。于是每个有限截断 $R>1$ 都满足
>
> $$0\le\int_1^R e^{-x^2}\,dx\le\int_1^R e^{-x}\,dx=e^{-1}-e^{-R}<e^{-1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Gaussian accumulation on the left increases with $R$ but can never exceed $e^{-1}$. It therefore approaches a finite limit. We have proved convergence without finding an elementary [[原函数|antiderivative]] of $e^{-x^2}$. This estimate does not give the exact Gaussian integral. The reason increasing, bounded accumulations have limits is made explicit below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左边的高斯累积随 $R$ 增加，却永远不超过 $e^{-1}$，因此趋于有限极限。即使没有找到 $e^{-x^2}$ 的初等[[原函数]]，也已证明收敛。这个估计并不给出高斯积分的精确值；递增而有上界的累积为什么有极限，下面会说明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What has to hold on the part being compared
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先核对被比较的那一段*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a finite starting point $A$. Let $f,g:[A,\infty)\to\mathbb R$ be Riemann integrable on every finite interval $[A,R]$, $R>A$. This means every truncated integral is an ordinary [[定积分|definite integral]] before we take a limit. Suppose also that $0\le f(x)\le g(x)$ for all $x\ge A$. Neither function needs to decrease with $x$, and [[连续函数可积|continuity on each finite interval]] is sufficient for local integrability but is not required by this version.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定有限起点 $A$。设 $f,g:[A,\infty)\to\mathbb R$ 在每个有限区间 $[A,R]$（$R>A$）上黎曼可积，也就是取极限之前，每个截断都有普通[[定积分]]的意义。再要求所有 $x\ge A$ 都满足 $0\le f(x)\le g(x)$。两个函数本身不必随 $x$ 递减；[[连续函数可积|每个有限区间上连续]]可以保证局部可积，但这个版本不要求一定连续。

> [!important] The two valid directions · 两个有效方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $\int_A^\infty g$ converges, then $\int_A^\infty f$ converges. If $\int_A^\infty f$ diverges, then $\int_A^\infty g$ diverges. Because these tails are nonnegative, divergence here means that their truncated integrals tend to $+\infty$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $\int_A^\infty g$ 收敛，则 $\int_A^\infty f$ 收敛。若 $\int_A^\infty f$ 发散，则 $\int_A^\infty g$ 发散。由于这里的尾部非负，发散表现为截断积分趋于 $+\infty$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only behaviour near the endpoint is needed. If the original lower bound is $a<A$ and both functions are properly integrable on $[a,A]$, that prefix adds a fixed finite number and does not change convergence. This is [[反常积分敛散的局部性|locality of improper convergence]]. A hidden singularity in the omitted interval must instead be checked separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只需要问题端附近满足比较。若原起点是 $a<A$，且两个函数在 $[a,A]$ 上都正常可积，这段只加上固定有限数，不改变敛散性；这是[[反常积分敛散的局部性]]。但若省略的区间藏着奇点，就必须另外检查。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the comparison proves existence of a limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么比较能够证明极限存在*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $F(R)=\int_A^R f$ and $G(R)=\int_A^R g$. These capital letters name accumulated values, not assumed antiderivatives. [[积分保序性|Order preservation of the integral]] gives $0\le F(R)\le G(R)$. Nonnegativity also makes each accumulation nondecreasing as the cutoff moves right.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $F(R)=\int_A^R f$、$G(R)=\int_A^R g$。这里的大写字母表示累积值，并不是预先假设的原函数。由[[积分保序性]]，$0\le F(R)\le G(R)$；非负性还使每个累积随截断点向右移动而不减。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $G(R)\to L<\infty$, its nondecreasing values are at most $L$, so the set of values $F(R)$ is nonempty and bounded above. By the [[实数的确界性质|least-upper-bound property]], it has a finite supremum $S$, its smallest upper bound. For every $\varepsilon>0$, some cutoff $R_0$ satisfies $F(R_0)>S-\varepsilon$. For all $R\ge R_0$, monotonicity then gives $S-\varepsilon<F(R)\le S$. This proves $F(R)\to S$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $G(R)\to L<\infty$，它的不减性保证所有值都不超过 $L$，因此 $F(R)$ 的值构成非空、有上界的集合。由[[实数的确界性质]]，它有有限上确界 $S$，即最小上界。任给 $\varepsilon>0$，都能找到截断点 $R_0$ 使 $F(R_0)>S-\varepsilon$；对所有 $R\ge R_0$，不减性保证 $S-\varepsilon<F(R)\le S$。这就证明了 $F(R)\to S$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If instead $F(R)\to+\infty$, the inequality $G(R)\ge F(R)$ forces $G(R)\to+\infty$. These arguments explain both directions without assuming the unknown integral already exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，若 $F(R)\to+\infty$，由 $G(R)\ge F(R)$ 可知 $G(R)\to+\infty$。两种论证都没有预先假设那个未知的反常积分已经存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same idea at a finite singular endpoint
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*有限奇异端点也用同一个原理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a right endpoint $b<\infty$, choose $c<b$ and assume proper integrability on every $[c,t]$ with $c<t<b$. If $0\le f\le g$ there, compare $\int_c^t f$ and $\int_c^t g$, then let $t\uparrow b$. For a finite left endpoint $a<c$, require proper integrability and $0\le f\le g$ on every $[a+\varepsilon,c]$, $0<\varepsilon<c-a$. Compare $\int_{a+\varepsilon}^{c} f$ and $\int_{a+\varepsilon}^{c}g$ as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$. The intervals grow toward the endpoint, so the same nonnegative-accumulation argument applies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于有限右端点 $b$，选 $c<b$，并要求每个 $[c,t]$（$c<t<b$）上正常可积。若这里 $0\le f\le g$，就比较 $\int_c^t f$ 与 $\int_c^t g$，再令 $t\uparrow b$。对有限左端点 $a<c$，要求每个 $[a+\varepsilon,c]$（$0<\varepsilon<c-a$）上正常可积，且 $0\le f\le g$；再比较 $\int_{a+\varepsilon}^{c} f$ 与 $\int_{a+\varepsilon}^{c}g$，令 $\varepsilon\downarrow0$。区间逐渐向端点扩大，因此非负累积的论证完全相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, on $0<x\le1$, $0<1/(\sqrt{x}(1+x))\le1/\sqrt{x}$. The upper comparator has integral $2$ from zero to one by the [[零附近 p 积分判别|p-integral test near zero]], so the smaller integral converges too. If an integral has several bad points, apply the test to each required approach under [[反常积分逐坏点拆分|the separate-end rule]]; one successful comparison does not settle the others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，在 $0<x\le1$ 上，$0<1/(\sqrt{x}(1+x))\le1/\sqrt{x}$。由[[零附近 p 积分判别]]，上界函数从零到一的积分为 $2$，所以较小函数的积分也收敛。若积分有多个问题点，须按[[反常积分逐坏点拆分|逐端规则]]分别比较；一个端点成功，不代表其他端点也通过。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Do not reverse the arrows
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 方向不能倒用

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On $[1,\infty)$, $1/x^2\le1/x$, but the [[无穷远 p 积分判别|power-tail test]] gives a convergent smaller integral and a divergent larger one. Thus a convergent lower bound does not prove convergence, and a divergent upper bound does not prove divergence. The inequality has the wrong direction for either conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $[1,\infty)$ 上，$1/x^2\le1/x$，但由[[无穷远 p 积分判别]]，较小者积分收敛，较大者积分发散。因此，收敛下界不能证明收敛，发散上界不能证明发散；两种推断的不等式方向都不对。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lower bound $0$ also matters. Merely having $f\le g$ is insufficient: $f=-1\le g=0$ on $[1,\infty)$, yet the upper integral is zero while the lower integral diverges to $-\infty$. For signed functions, cancellation needs its own argument; a nonnegative comparison can instead be applied to the magnitude $|f|$ when that is the quantity being controlled.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下界 $0$ 也重要。仅有 $f\le g$ 不够：在 $[1,\infty)$ 上，$f=-1\le g=0$，但上方积分为零，下方积分却趋于 $-\infty$。对有正有负的函数，抵消需要自己的论证；若要控制大小，可以把非负比较用于绝对值 $|f|$ 这一对象。

> [!question]- What does a divergent upper bound tell you? · 发散上界能说明什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $0\le f(x)\le1/x$ for $x\ge1$, can we decide convergence? No: $f(x)=1/x^2$ converges, whereas $f(x)=1/(2x)$ diverges. Both satisfy the same upper bound.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $x\ge1$ 时 $0\le f(x)\le1/x$，能否判断敛散？不能：$f(x)=1/x^2$ 收敛，$f(x)=1/(2x)$ 发散，却都满足这个上界。
