# Natural filtration
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*自然滤过*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A process's **natural filtration** is the information supplied by observing that process up to the current time. It remembers the whole observed history. For a random walk, seeing only the current position can lose information about the route; seeing its natural history retains every earlier position.

A [[滤过定义|filtration]] is an increasing family of event collections. A [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]] describes which events can be distinguished. Natural filtration specifies these collections from the observations themselves, without choosing extra signals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

过程的**自然滤过**是观察该过程截至当前时刻所得到的信息，保留整段已经看到的历史。例如随机游走只看当前位置，可能丢失到达这里的路线；自然历史则保留此前每个位置。

[[滤过定义|滤过]]是逐时递增的事件族，[[σ-代数]]描述哪些事件能够区分。自然滤过直接从过程的观察值确定这些事件族，不额外加入其他信号。

> [!abstract] The raw history · 原始历史
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X=(X_t)_{t\in I}$ be a real-valued [[随机过程|stochastic process]] on $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$. Its raw natural filtration is
> $$\mathcal F_t^X=\sigma(X_u:u\in I,\ u\le t).$$
> The notation $\sigma(\cdots)$ means the **smallest sigma-algebra making every listed variable measurable**. Concretely, it contains all events $\{X_u\le c\}$ for $u\le t$ and $c\in\mathbb R$, and all events forced by complements and countable unions. For other measurable state spaces, use the inverse images of their measurable sets instead of real thresholds.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $X=(X_t)_{t\in I}$ 是概率空间 $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ 上的实值[[随机过程]]。其原始自然滤过由上式定义。$\sigma(\cdots)$ 表示**使列出的每个变量都可测的最小 σ-代数**：它包含全部 $u\le t$、$c\in\mathbb R$ 对应的事件 $\{X_u\le c\}$，也包含由补集和可数并强制得到的事件。如果过程取值于其他可测状态空间，就改用该空间中可测集合的原像。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An event such as “the position at the first observation was positive” is in this information once that observation has occurred. The notation describes a collection of events on $\Omega$, not a collection of numerical values. “Generated by” also matters: it is a sigma-algebra closure, not merely the unclosed list of threshold events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，“第一次观测时位置为正”这个事件，在第一次观测以后就属于自然信息。记号描述的是 $\Omega$ 上的一族事件，不是一组数值。“生成”还意味着取 σ-代数封闭：不能只保留未经补集、可数并封闭的阈值事件列表。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the history is a filtration
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么它确实是滤过

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $s\le t$, every variable observed up to $s$ is also among those observed up to $t$. Their generated event collections therefore satisfy
$$\mathcal F_s^X\subseteq\mathcal F_t^X.$$
The current variable $X_t$ is itself in the generating family, so it is measurable with respect to $\mathcal F_t^X$. Thus $X$ is [[适应过程|adapted]] to its natural filtration: its current value is available in the current information.

To see minimality, let $(\mathcal G_t)$ be any other filtration to which $X$ is adapted. For $u\le t$, $X_u$ is measurable with respect to $\mathcal G_u$, and $\mathcal G_u\subseteq\mathcal G_t$. Therefore $\mathcal G_t$ makes every past $X_u$ measurable, and
$$\mathcal F_t^X=\sigma(X_u:u\le t)\subseteq\mathcal G_t.$$
The natural filtration is consequently the smallest filtration making $X$ adapted. Another information flow may be strictly larger, but cannot omit any event generated by the observed history.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $s\le t$，截至 $s$ 观察到的变量，都也出现在截至 $t$ 的变量列表中，所以生成的事件族满足 $\mathcal F_s^X\subseteq\mathcal F_t^X$。当前变量 $X_t$ 自身也在生成族里，因而对 $\mathcal F_t^X$ 可测。于是，过程总是对自己的自然滤过[[适应过程|适应]]，即当前信息足以确定当前值。

再看最小性。假设另一个滤过 $(\mathcal G_t)$ 也使 $X$ 适应。对 $u\le t$，$X_u$ 对 $\mathcal G_u$ 可测，而 $\mathcal G_u\subseteq\mathcal G_t$，因此 $\mathcal G_t$ 使所有过去的 $X_u$ 都可测。由生成 σ-代数的最小性得到上式。自然滤过因而是使过程适应的最小滤过；别的信息流可以更大，却不能遗漏观察历史生成的任何事件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The current value may forget the route
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 当前值不等于完整历史

> [!example] A walk can return by two routes · 回到原点的两条路线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let two tosses give increments $\xi_1,\xi_2$, each $+1$ for heads and $-1$ for tails. Put $S_0=0$, $S_1=\xi_1$ and $S_2=\xi_1+\xi_2$.
>
> | Outcome | $S_1$ | $S_2$ |
> |---|---:|---:|
> | $HH$ | $1$ | $2$ |
> | $HT$ | $1$ | $0$ |
> | $TH$ | $-1$ | $0$ |
> | $TT$ | $-1$ | $-2$ |
>
> Knowing only $S_2=0$ cannot distinguish $HT$ from $TH$. Their full histories do distinguish them. Indeed $\sigma(S_2)$ groups the outcomes into $\{HH\},\{HT,TH\},\{TT\}$, whereas $\mathcal F_2^S=\sigma(S_0,S_1,S_2)$ distinguishes all four outcomes. The event $\{S_1=1\}=\{HH,HT\}$ is not in $\sigma(S_2)$. Thus the collections $\sigma(S_t)$ need not increase with time, and the list of current-value sigma-algebras need not be a filtration.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两次抛掷产生增量 $\xi_1,\xi_2$，正面记 $+1$、反面记 $-1$。令 $S_0=0$、$S_1=\xi_1$、$S_2=\xi_1+\xi_2$，得到表中四条路径。
>
> 只知道 $S_2=0$，无法区分 $HT$ 与 $TH$，完整历史却能区分。$\sigma(S_2)$ 把结果分成 $\{HH\}$、$\{HT,TH\}$、$\{TT\}$ 三组；而 $\mathcal F_2^S=\sigma(S_0,S_1,S_2)$ 可以区分全部四个结果。事件 $\{S_1=1\}=\{HH,HT\}$ 不属于 $\sigma(S_2)$。所以 $\sigma(S_t)$ 未必随时间扩大，仅由“各时刻当前值”生成的一串 σ-代数未必是滤过。

## Raw and augmented natural filtrations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*原始自然滤过与增广*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here “natural” means the raw generated family above. Continuous-time results sometimes use its **usual augmentation**: include subsets of probability-zero events, and make the filtration right-continuous. Right-continuity means
$$\mathcal F_t=\bigcap_{u>t}\mathcal F_u.$$
These operations must be specified; the raw natural filtration is not automatically right-continuous, even if the process has continuous paths.

For example, take a nonconstant coin variable $Z\in\{-1,1\}$ and set $X_t=tZ$ for $t\ge0$. At zero, $X_0=0$ reveals nothing, so $\mathcal F_0^X$ is trivial. At every $t>0$, observing $X_t$ reveals $Z=X_t/t$, so $\mathcal F_t^X=\sigma(Z)$. Consequently $\bigcap_{u>0}\mathcal F_u^X=\sigma(Z)$ is larger than $\mathcal F_0^X$, although each path is a straight continuous line.

The minimality proved above concerns the raw family. If an author uses “natural filtration” to mean a completed or usual augmentation, interpret minimality within the explicitly stated convention. A [[鞅定义|martingale]] property likewise refers to a specified filtration; expanding information need not preserve its conditional-average property.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“自然滤过”指上面直接生成的原始事件族。连续时间结果有时使用它的**通常增广**：加入零概率事件的子集，并使滤过右连续。右连续性就是上式。这些操作需要明确说明；即使过程路径连续，原始自然滤过也未必右连续。

例如，取一个非常数硬币变量 $Z\in\{-1,1\}$，令 $X_t=tZ$。零时刻 $X_0=0$ 没有揭示信息，所以 $\mathcal F_0^X$ 是平凡 σ-代数。任意 $t>0$ 时，观察 $X_t$ 就知道 $Z=X_t/t$，因此 $\mathcal F_t^X=\sigma(Z)$。于是 $\bigcap_{u>0}\mathcal F_u^X=\sigma(Z)$ 严格大于 $\mathcal F_0^X$，尽管每条路径都是连续直线。

前面证明的最小性针对原始事件族。如果某个作者用“自然滤过”表示完备化或通常增广后的版本，就应在其明确约定下理解最小性。[[鞅定义|鞅]]性质也针对指定滤过；扩大信息未必保留条件平均性质。
