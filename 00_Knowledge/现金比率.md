# 现金比率是所声明现金资产口径与流动负债之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cash ratio divides the stated cash-asset measure by current liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金比率以现金资产存量而非现金流量作分子，在流动负债为正时衡量其账面覆盖。CPA本章用“货币资金/流动负债”，ABC为 $44/300\approx0.1467$；其他口径可能包括现金等价物或特定短期投资，不能不加说明混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cash ratio uses a stock of cash assets, not a cash flow, to assess book coverage of positive current liabilities. CPA uses monetary funds divided by current liabilities: $44/300\approx0.1467$ for ABC. Other definitions may include cash equivalents or selected short-term investments and must not be mixed silently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若问题是实际支付能力，还需区分受限资金与可动用资金。现金增加可能来自经营、出售资产或借款；单看现金比率不能判断现金来源是否可持续，也不能替代[[现金流量比率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For actual payment capacity, distinguish restricted from available cash. Cash may increase through operations, asset sales, or borrowing. The cash ratio alone does not establish sustainability and does not replace the [[现金流量比率|operating-cash-flow-to-current-liabilities ratio]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
