# Principal minors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*主子式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **principal minor** is a number obtained by selecting matching rows and columns of a square matrix, then taking the determinant of the smaller square matrix. “Matching” means the same index set on both sides. The selected indices need not be consecutive. First select a matrix; only then compute its [[行列式|determinant]], a scalar associated with a square matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**主子式**是一个数：先从方阵中选出下标相同的一组行与列，再计算所得到的小方阵的行列式。“相同”指两边使用同一个下标集合，所选下标不必连续。先选出一张矩阵，再取它的[[行列式]]，才得到标量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the matrix below. To keep indices $1$ and $3$, keep rows $1,3$ and columns $1,3$. Crossing those rows with those columns gives the displayed $2\times2$ matrix:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑下面的矩阵。若保留下标 $1$ 和 $3$，就同时保留第 $1,3$ 行与第 $1,3$ 列；这些行列交叉处的元素组成所显示的二阶方阵：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}4&1&2\\1&5&3\\2&3&6\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A_{\{1,3\},\{1,3\}}=\begin{pmatrix}4&2\\2&6\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This smaller matrix is a **principal submatrix**. Its determinant is the principal minor $4\cdot6-2\cdot2=20$. The submatrix and the number $20$ are different objects; keeping them separate prevents mistakes when a later condition asks for a determinant's sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这张小矩阵叫作**主子矩阵**。它的行列式为 $4\cdot6-2\cdot2=20$，这才是主子式。主子矩阵与数 $20$ 是不同对象；把它们分清，后面要求检查行列式符号时才不会用错对象。

> [!important] The same retained indices · 保留同一组下标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A=(a_{ij})$ be an $n\times n$ matrix, where $n\ge1$ is an integer and $a_{ij}$ is the entry in row $i$, column $j$. Choose a nonempty set $I=\{i_1,\ldots,i_k\}$ with $1\le i_1<\cdots<i_k\le n$. The **principal submatrix** and its **principal minor of order $k$** are
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $A=(a_{ij})$ 为 $n\times n$ 矩阵，$n\ge1$ 为整数，$a_{ij}$ 表示第 $i$ 行、第 $j$ 列的元素。选择非空下标集 $I=\{i_1,\ldots,i_k\}$，其中 $1\le i_1<\cdots<i_k\le n$。相应的**主子矩阵**与 **$k$ 阶主子式**分别是
>
> $$A_{I,I}=\bigl[a_{i_p i_q}\bigr]_{p,q=1}^k,
> \qquad \det A_{I,I}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The order $k$ is the number of retained rows, also the number of retained columns. We keep their original increasing order. Equivalently, delete the same complementary indices from the rows and columns. Real or complex entries are allowed; neither symmetry nor positive definiteness is part of this definition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 阶数 $k$ 是保留的行数，也是保留的列数；行列均沿用原来的递增次序。等价地，也可以说从行与列中删去同一个补下标集。矩阵元素可以是实数或复数；定义本身不要求对称或正定。

## List the selections before calculating
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先列下标，再算数值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening $3\times3$ matrix, the three first-order principal minors are the diagonal entries $4,5,6$. The three second-order choices are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对开头的三阶矩阵，一阶主子式就是三个对角元 $4,5,6$；二阶共有三个选择：

$$\begin{aligned}
\det A_{\{1,2\},\{1,2\}}&=4\cdot5-1\cdot1=19,\\
\det A_{\{1,3\},\{1,3\}}&=4\cdot6-2\cdot2=20,\\
\det A_{\{2,3\},\{2,3\}}&=5\cdot6-3\cdot3=21.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At order three, only the whole matrix remains. Expanding its determinant along the first row gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三阶时只有整个矩阵。沿第一行展开行列式，得到

$$\det A=4(30-9)-1(6-6)+2(3-10)=70.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus the seven nonempty principal minors, labelled by their selections, have values $4,5,6,19,20,21,70$. In general, a first-order principal minor is $a_{ii}$; the only order-$n$ principal minor is $\det A$. For $n=1$, these are the same single selection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，按下标选择列出的七个非空主子式取值为 $4,5,6,19,20,21,70$。一般而言，一阶主子式是 $a_{ii}$，唯一的 $n$ 阶主子式是 $\det A$；当 $n=1$ 时，两者就是同一个选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choosing $k$ indices from $n$ gives the [[组合数|binomial coefficient]] $\binom nk$, so there are $\binom nk$ principal minors of order $k$. Across all nonempty orders there are $2^n-1$: each index can be retained or omitted, giving $2^n$ subsets, and we exclude the empty one. This counts **index selections**, not distinct numerical answers. For the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]], every principal minor is $1$, but the different selections still count separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $n$ 个下标中选 $k$ 个，有[[组合数]] $\binom nk$ 种选择，所以 $k$ 阶主子式有 $\binom nk$ 个。所有非空阶合计 $2^n-1$ 个：每个下标可选或不选，共 $2^n$ 个子集，再排除空集。这里计算的是**下标选择的数量**，不是互不相同的数值数量。例如，[[单位矩阵]]的每个主子式都等于 $1$，但不同下标选择仍分别计数。

## What makes a minor nonprincipal?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*什么情况下只是一般子式？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep rows $1,3$ but columns $1,2$ in the same example. The selected matrix is $\begin{pmatrix}4&1\\2&3\end{pmatrix}$ and its determinant is $10$. It is a valid square submatrix and a valid minor, but this selection is not principal: the row and column index sets differ. Even if its determinant happened to equal a principal minor, equality of numbers would not change the selection's type.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一例子中，保留第 $1,3$ 行，却保留第 $1,2$ 列，得到 $\begin{pmatrix}4&1\\2&3\end{pmatrix}$，行列式为 $10$。它是合法的方形子矩阵，其行列式也是合法的子式，但这次选择不是主子式，因为行、列下标集不同。即使数值碰巧等于某个主子式，也不会改变这次选择的类型。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[顺序主子式|Leading principal minors]] impose one further restriction: $I$ must be $\{1,\ldots,k\}$. They form one nested sequence within the full collection. Which collection of signs is sufficient for a matrix test is a separate theorem; the definition of a principal minor does not itself imply positivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[顺序主子式]]再加一条限制：$I$ 必须是 $\{1,\ldots,k\}$，因而在全部主子式中形成一串嵌套的选择。究竟检查哪一组符号才能判断矩阵性质，属于另外的定理；主子式的定义本身不带正值要求。
