# Integration by substitution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*换元积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substitution gives a complicated expression a simpler name while keeping track of how that expression changes. For example, in $\int x^3(x^4+2)^5\,dx$, expanding the fifth power is unnecessary: the inner expression $x^4+2$ has [[导数|derivative]] $4x^3$, and almost that factor is already present. The goal is to find an [[原函数|antiderivative]], a function whose derivative recovers the given integrand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换元积分给复杂表达式换一个简单名字，同时记录它怎样变化。例如 $\int x^3(x^4+2)^5\,dx$ 不必展开五次幂：内层 $x^4+2$ 的[[导数]]是 $4x^3$，而原式中已经有了几乎相同的因子。我们要找的是[[原函数]]，也就是求导后恢复被积函数的函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Recognize the inner expression and its change
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 识别与执行

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set $u=x^4+2$, where $u$ is the new variable. The [[微分|differential]] notation $du=4x^3\,dx$ records its derivative, so the entire factor $x^3\,dx$ becomes $du/4$. The outer fifth power becomes $u^5$. Both replacements are needed:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令新变量 $u=x^4+2$。[[微分]]记号 $du=4x^3\,dx$ 记录它的导数，所以整个 $x^3\,dx$ 应换成 $du/4$，外层五次幂则变成 $u^5$。两部分一起替换：

$$
\int x^3(x^4+2)^5\,dx
=\frac14\int u^5\,du
=\frac{u^6}{24}+C
=\frac{(x^4+2)^6}{24}+C.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $C$ is an arbitrary real constant. The original question used $x$, so the final function is written in $x$. Check it by differentiating: the outer power contributes $6(x^4+2)^5$, the inner derivative contributes $4x^3$, and $6\cdot4/24=1$. This check catches a missing chain factor or a wrong constant multiplier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $C$ 是任意实常数。原题的变量是 $x$，最后也要写回 $x$。求导检查时，外层贡献 $6(x^4+2)^5$，内层贡献 $4x^3$，而 $6\cdot4/24=1$。这个检查能发现漏掉的链式因子或错误系数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the method works
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*依据是链式法则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the inner function as $g(x)$ and the outer integrand as $f(u)$. Suppose $g$ is differentiable on an open interval $I$, its values lie in an open interval $J$, and $F$ is differentiable on $J$ with $F'=f$. The [[链式法则|chain rule]] says that differentiating the composition $F(g(x))$ gives two factors:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把内层函数记作 $g(x)$，外层被积函数记作 $f(u)$。假设 $g$ 在开区间 $I$ 上可导，取值都在开区间 $J$ 内，并且 $F$ 在 $J$ 上可导、满足 $F'=f$。[[链式法则]]说明，对复合函数 $F(g(x))$ 求导会得到两部分：

$$
\frac{d}{dx}F(g(x))=f(g(x))g'(x).
$$

> [!abstract] Read differentiation backwards · 反向阅读求导公式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under these assumptions, an antiderivative is known explicitly:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在上述条件下，我们已经知道一个原函数：
>
> $$\int f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx=F(g(x))+C.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Writing $u=g(x)$ and $du=g'(x)\,dx$ is shorthand for this chain-rule identity. It does not require dividing by $g'(x)$, so zeros of $g'$ and a nonmonotone $g$ do not invalidate it. By [[原函数相差常数|the constant-difference theorem]], these are all antiderivatives on the chosen interval; separate domain intervals may have separate constants.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $u=g(x)$、$du=g'(x)\,dx$ 是这条链式恒等式的简写。它不需要除以 $g'(x)$，所以 $g'$ 为零或 $g$ 不单调，并不使它失效。由[[原函数相差常数]]，这给出所选区间上的全部原函数；定义域分成多个区间时，各段的常数可以不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus a useful first attempt is to identify an inner expression, compute its differential, and check whether the rest supplies that differential up to a constant. After rewriting, use only the new variable inside the integral, then substitute back and differentiate the answer. This is a recognition strategy, not a claim that every useful change of variable must already display that exact factor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，首先可以寻找内层表达式，计算微分，再检查其余部分是否只差一个常数就能配对。改写后，积分内部应只保留新变量；算完换回，并求导检查。这是优先尝试的识别策略，并不是说所有有效换元都必须预先显露这一因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A legal change of variable need not simplify the problem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*换元合法，不代表更容易算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $\int\cos(x^2)\,dx$, setting $u=x^2$ does **not** give $\int\cos u\,du$: differentiating $\sin(x^2)$ would produce the unwanted factor $2x$. Yet on the interval $x>0$, we can solve for $x=\sqrt u$. The [[反函数导数|inverse derivative]] gives $dx=du/(2\sqrt u)$ for $u>0$, so the valid transformed integral is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $\int\cos(x^2)\,dx$，令 $u=x^2$，**不能**直接得到 $\int\cos u\,du$，因为 $\sin(x^2)$ 求导会多出 $2x$。但在区间 $x>0$ 上，可以反解 $x=\sqrt u$；[[反函数导数]]给出 $u>0$ 时 $dx=du/(2\sqrt u)$，合法的改写其实是

$$\int\cos(x^2)\,dx=\int\frac{\cos u}{2\sqrt u}\,du\qquad(x>0),$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
with the resulting antiderivative finally composed with $u=x^2$. On $x<0$, the inverse is $x=-\sqrt u$ and its derivative has a minus sign. Neither branch formula covers $x=0$. The original integrand is defined there; the difficulty is with this inverse-coordinate formula. This substitution has removed $x$, but has not reduced the task to the elementary integral of cosine.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后仍需把所得原函数中的 $u$ 换回 $x^2$。在 $x<0$ 上，应选 $x=-\sqrt u$，反函数导数也多一个负号。两条分支公式都不覆盖 $x=0$；原被积函数在那里有定义，出问题的是这种反解坐标公式。此换元确实消去了 $x$，却没有把题目化成简单的余弦积分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, if a chosen interval admits a differentiable inverse $x=\phi(u)$ of $u=g(x)$, a continuous integrand $H(x)$ transforms to $H(\phi(u))\phi'(u)$. This follows from the chain rule again. A continuously differentiable $g$ with nonzero derivative on that interval supplies such an inverse on its image. If rewriting really requires an inverse and no single inverse covers the interval, use suitable [[非单调换元需分支|inverse branches]]. Merely seeing a residual symbol $x$ is not decisive: for instance a residual $x^2$ is already $u$ when $u=x^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般地，若所选区间上 $u=g(x)$ 有可导反函数 $x=\phi(u)$，连续被积函数 $H(x)$ 就变成 $H(\phi(u))\phi'(u)$，依据仍是链式法则。若 $g$ 在该区间连续可微且导数处处非零，就能在它的像上得到这样的反函数。若改写确实需要反解，而整个区间没有一个单一反函数，才需要选择合适的[[非单调换元需分支|反函数分支]]。不能仅凭还出现符号 $x$ 就下结论：例如 $u=x^2$ 时，残留的 $x^2$ 已经可以直接写成 $u$。

> [!question]- Compare two similar integrals · 比较两道相似题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With $u=3x^2+1$, $\int6x(3x^2+1)^4\,dx$ becomes $\int u^4\,du$, giving $(3x^2+1)^5/5+C$. Without $6x$, the same direct recognition fails, but on $x>0$ a valid inverse substitution gives $\int u^4/[6\sqrt{(u-1)/3}]\,du$ with $u>1$. One route is immediately useful; the other is still mathematically valid on its stated branch.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $u=3x^2+1$，$\int6x(3x^2+1)^4\,dx$ 直接变成 $\int u^4\,du$，结果为 $(3x^2+1)^5/5+C$。去掉 $6x$ 后，不能照抄这一步；但在 $x>0$ 上仍可合法反解，得到 $u>1$ 时的 $\int u^4/[6\sqrt{(u-1)/3}]\,du$。前一种立即简化了计算，后一种在明确分支后仍然合法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For definite integrals, the limits also belong to the variable being used; [[定积分换元|definite-integral substitution]] explains their transformation and orientation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对定积分，上下限也属于当前使用的变量；[[定积分换元]]进一步解释如何换限及保留方向。
