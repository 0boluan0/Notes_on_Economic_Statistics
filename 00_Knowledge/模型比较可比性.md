# 只有响应尺度、评价样本、权重以及似然或损失口径相容时，模型分数才可直接比较
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Model scores are directly comparable only when outcome scale, evaluation sample, weights, and likelihood or loss conventions are compatible*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 比较契约
> 一个分数只有相对于同一比较问题才有意义。比较前应确认：模型解释同一个观测结果或同一可转换概率模型，使用同一批评价观测与权重，按同一损失或完整似然口径计分，并来自事先说明的候选集合。否则“分数更小”可能只是数据、单位或计算常数变了。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A score is meaningful only relative to one comparison problem. Before ranking models, verify that they concern the same observed outcome or a properly transformed common probability model, use the same evaluation observations and weights, are scored under the same loss or full-likelihood convention, and belong to an explicit candidate set. Otherwise a smaller number may merely reflect changed data, units, or conventions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 四项逐一核对
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Check four items explicitly*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **响应与尺度：** level wage 与 log wage 的普通 RSS、$R^2$ 或省略 Jacobian 的 likelihood 不是同一个量。若变换模型都写成原观测尺度上的完整概率模型，才可能重新建立合法比较。
- **评价观测：** 某模型因缺失值只使用 800 行，另一个使用 1,000 行时，AIC、RSS 或 validation loss 的差异混入了样本变化。
- **权重与计分规则：** RMSE、MAE、log score 和加权损失强调不同错误；不同 survey weights 也对应不同目标总体。
- **候选集合与拟合协议：** 分数只能在实际比较过的候选中排序。事后加入模型、改变预处理或更换超参数搜索预算，都会改变选择过程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Outcome and scale:** Ordinary RSS, $R^2$, or a likelihood that omits the transformation Jacobian does not put wages and log wages on one scale. A valid comparison may be recovered only when both are expressed as complete probability models for the same observed outcome.
- **Evaluation observations:** If missingness makes one model use 800 rows and another 1,000, differences in AIC, RSS, or validation loss also contain a sample difference.
- **Weights and score:** RMSE, MAE, log score, and weighted losses value errors differently, and different survey weights target different populations.
- **Candidate set and fitting protocol:** A score ranks only the candidates actually compared. Adding models after inspection, changing preprocessing, or changing the tuning budget changes the selection procedure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“同一样本”不表示所有准则都可互换。[[嵌套模型F检验]]回答预先给定的线性限制是否与数据相容；[[AIC]]和[[BIC]]比较可比 likelihood 下的 fit–complexity 权衡；[[K折交叉验证]]估计整个训练流程在模拟新数据上的损失。先满足各自的比较条件，再根据[[模型选择目标]]决定用哪类证据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the same sample does not make all criteria interchangeable. A [[嵌套模型F检验|nested F test]] assesses prespecified linear restrictions; [[AIC]] and [[BIC]] balance fit and complexity for comparable likelihoods; [[K折交叉验证|K-fold cross-validation]] estimates the loss of an entire fitting procedure on simulated new data. First satisfy the criterion's comparison conditions, then choose evidence according to the [[模型选择目标|selection target]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> Model A 用全部 1,000 行，Model B 因新增变量缺失只剩 720 行。能否直接说 B 的 AIC 更低，所以更好？
>
> **答案：** 不能。两个最大似然值来自不同数据，差异同时反映模型和样本。应先构造共同评价样本，或明确改用能够针对同一部署任务计分的外部评价设计。

## 来源与核验

- Akaike（1974），[A New Look at the Statistical Model Identification](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.1974.1100705)：核验 AIC 是在候选模型间进行相对识别的 likelihood–complexity 准则。
- Schwarz（1978），[Estimating the Dimension of a Model](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176344136)：核验 BIC 的比较对象是同一数据问题下不同维数的候选模型。
- Penn State STAT 501, [Lesson 10: Model Building](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat501/Lesson10)：核对回归信息准则和交叉验证都只能在一致的模型构建问题中解释。
