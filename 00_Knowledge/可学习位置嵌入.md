# 可学习位置嵌入为每个已定义位置训练一个可查表的向量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Learned positional embeddings train a lookup vector for each defined position*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**可学习位置嵌入**在这里指常见的**绝对位置查表**：为每个已定义的序列位置分配一个向量，把它作为模型参数随训练更新。若最大已定义位置数为 $L_{\max}$，则位置表为 $P\in\mathbb R^{L_{\max}\times d_{\mathrm{model}}}$，第 $p$ 个位置读取第 $p$ 行。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here, **learned positional embeddings** means the common **absolute-position lookup table**: each defined sequence position has a vector that is updated as a model parameter during training. A table for $L_{\max}$ positions has shape $P\in\mathbb R^{L_{\max}\times d_{\mathrm{model}}}$, and position $p$ retrieves row $p$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小的输入组合形式是

$$
z_p=E[\mathrm{token}_p]+P[p],
\qquad 0\le p<L_{\max}.
$$

[[Token嵌入|词表 $E$]] 按 token ID 查，位置表 $P$ 按位置查。它们是两个独立的参数表：相同 token 可以重复使用同一词向量，却在不同位置加上不同的位置向量。模型也可以另加 segment 等其他输入项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed expression is the minimal additive construction. The [[Token嵌入|token table $E$]] is indexed by token ID, while $P$ is indexed by sequence position. They are separate parameter tables: repeated tokens can share their token vector while receiving different positional vectors. A model may add further terms, such as segment embeddings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $L_{\max}=4$ 时，表内有 $P[0],P[1],P[2],P[3]$；第五个位置需要 $P[4]$，原表没有这一行。增加表长、初始化新行、插值或重新训练，都属于额外的扩展方法。即使某一行已经存在，也要区分它是否在训练中得到过足够更新。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $L_{\max}=4$, the table contains rows $P[0]$ through $P[3]$. A fifth position requires $P[4]$, which is absent. Extending the table, initializing new rows, interpolating, or retraining are additional methods. Even when a row exists, its existence does not establish that it received sufficient training.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种 [[位置编码]] 让位置向量适应训练数据；[[正弦位置编码]] 则由固定函数直接生成。二者如何处理新位置不同，不能单凭“可学习”或“固定”判断哪个在所有任务上更好。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This [[位置编码|positional mechanism]] adapts its vectors to training data, whereas [[正弦位置编码|sinusoidal positional encoding]] generates them from a fixed function. Their handling of new positions differs; “learned” versus “fixed” alone does not determine which works better for every task.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Gehring et al. (2017), *Convolutional Sequence to Sequence Learning*](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/gehring17a/gehring17a.pdf)，§3.1、§5.4：支持绝对位置嵌入与 token 嵌入相加及最大长度限制；四行表例子是对查表定义的直接应用。
- [Devlin et al. (2019), *BERT*](https://aclanthology.org/N19-1423.pdf)，§3 “Input/Output Representations”、Appendix A.2：支持 token、segment、position 嵌入求和；其训练安排明确为学习较长位置嵌入使用长度 512 的序列。
- [Su et al., *RoFormer*, v5](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.09864v5#S2.SS2)，§2.2 Eq. 3：明确描述可训练的绝对位置向量集合及最大序列长度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Gehring et al., §3.1 and §5.4, describes additive absolute-position embeddings and their maximum-length constraint. The four-row example applies that lookup definition directly.
- Devlin et al., §3 and Appendix A.2, describes summed token, segment, and position embeddings, and training on longer sequences to learn the corresponding positional embeddings.
- Su et al., §2.2 Equation 3, explicitly describes trainable absolute-position vectors and a maximum sequence length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
