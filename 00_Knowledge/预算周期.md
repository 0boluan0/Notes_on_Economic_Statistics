# 预算周期是预算从编制与批准到执行、报告和审计反馈的连续制度过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The budget cycle connects preparation and approval with execution, reporting, audit and feedback.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预算周期说明[[政府预算]]如何由计划变成受监督的执行：编制阶段估计资源与政策成本，批准阶段给予相应授权，执行阶段安排承诺与支付，报告和审计阶段核对结果并向下一轮决策反馈。不同年度的这些阶段通常交错进行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The budget cycle turns a [[政府预算|budget]] into supervised implementation. Preparation estimates resources and policy costs; approval grants authority; execution manages commitments and payments; reporting and audit assess results and inform subsequent decisions. Stages for different years often overlap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，本年度年中既可能发布本年执行报告，也在编制下年度预算，同时审查上年度决算。发现偏差后，管理上的调剂、正式补充预算与事后报告承担不同职责：哪些调剂可在现有授权内完成、哪些变化需要重新批准，应依适用制度判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At mid-year, government may report current execution, prepare next year's budget and review last year's accounts. Administrative reallocations, supplementary budgets and retrospective reporting serve different purposes; the applicable framework determines when fresh approval is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

执行反馈首先比较[[预算支出与决算支出]]，再解释差异与服务结果。报告和审计不是把已经发生的每项超支自动合法化；同样，支出恰好等于预算也不能证明公共服务有效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Feedback first compares [[预算支出与决算支出|appropriations with outturns]], then explains deviations and service results. Reporting and audit do not automatically authorise past overruns, while exact budget execution does not establish service effectiveness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [OECD, Recommendation on Budgetary Governance](https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/public/doc/319/body-text.en.html)，原则 7、8、10：支持执行、调整权限、报告、评估和内外部审计的衔接。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Principles 7, 8 and 10 support execution, adjustment authority, reporting, evaluation and audit.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [OECD, Quality Budget Institutions, Chapter 9](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/quality-budget-institutions_8e811202-en/full-report/budget-transparency_ed612352.html)，§§9.2.1–9.2.3：支持预算周期各阶段的文件与公开；跨年度时间例为流程说明。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Sections 9.2.1–9.2.3 describe documents and disclosure across the cycle. The overlapping-year example illustrates this process.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
