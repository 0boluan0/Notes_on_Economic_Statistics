# Weighted geometric sum
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*加权几何和*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A weighted geometric sum multiplies each power by its index before adding. Here we study the specific linear weights $1,2,\ldots,n$. For example, with $r=1/2$ and three terms, the contributions are $1\cdot(1/2)$, $2\cdot(1/2)^2$, and $3\cdot(1/2)^3$. Their total is $1/2+1/2+3/8=11/8$. The weight counts how many copies of that power contribute.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

加权几何和先给各次幂乘一个权重，再相加。本卡讨论特定的线性权重 $1,2,\ldots,n$。例如，$r=1/2$、取三项时，贡献分别是 $1\cdot(1/2)$、$2\cdot(1/2)^2$、$3\cdot(1/2)^3$，合计 $1/2+1/2+3/8=11/8$。权重表示这个幂项贡献多少份。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonnegative integer $n$ and a real number $r$, define $W_n(r)=\sum_{i=1}^n ir^i$. Unlike an unweighted [[几何和|geometric sum]], its successive nonzero terms do not generally have one fixed ratio: the factor $i$ also changes. We need a different identity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非负整数 $n$ 和实数 $r$，定义 $W_n(r)=\sum_{i=1}^n ir^i$。与普通[[几何和]]不同，它相邻非零项之间通常没有固定公比，因为权重 $i$ 也在变，所以需要另一条恒等式。

> [!important] Finite weighted identity / 有限加权恒等式
> $$
> \boxed{W_n(r)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}ir^i=\frac{r-(n+1)r^{n+1}+nr^{n+2}}{(1-r)^2}},\qquad r\ne1.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This holds for every $n\ge0$; $n=0$ means the empty sum zero. No condition $|r|<1$ is needed for a finite sum. At $r=0$, the result is zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这对每个 $n\ge0$ 都成立；$n=0$ 表示空和，等于零。有限和不要求 $|r|<1$；$r=0$ 时结果也为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Differentiation creates the weight
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*求导怎样产生权重*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regard $r$ as a variable and keep the integer $n$ fixed. Start with $S_n(r)=1+r+\cdots+r^n$. The [[整数幂求导|power rule]] gives $(r^i)'=ir^{i-1}$ for $i\ge1$, and [[导数线性法则|linearity of differentiation]] lets us differentiate this finite sum term by term. The constant term has derivative zero. Multiplying the derivative by $r$ restores each exponent:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $r$ 看作变量，而把整数 $n$ 固定。先从 $S_n(r)=1+r+\cdots+r^n$ 出发。[[整数幂求导]]给出 $i\ge1$ 时 $(r^i)'=ir^{i-1}$，[[导数线性法则]]允许对有限和逐项求导；常数项的导数是零。最后乘回 $r$，恢复各项原来的次数：

$$
S_n'(r)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}ir^{i-1},\qquad rS_n'(r)=W_n(r).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $r\ne1$, the finite-sum formula and the [[商法则|quotient rule]] give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $r\ne1$，使用有限和公式和[[商法则]]可得

$$
\begin{aligned}S_n'(r)&=\frac{-(n+1)r^n(1-r)+(1-r^{n+1})}{(1-r)^2}\\&=\frac{1-(n+1)r^n+nr^{n+1}}{(1-r)^2}.\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying by $r$ gives the stated numerator. At $n=0$, the derivative of $S_0=1$ is zero; the same identity reduces to zero. All differentiation here concerns a finite polynomial, so there is no exchange of differentiation with an infinite sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再乘 $r$，就得到恒等式中的分子。$n=0$ 时，$S_0=1$ 的导数为零，所得恒等式也退化为零。这里求导的始终是有限多项式，不涉及把求导与无穷求和交换次序。

> [!example] The ratio can exceed one / 公比可以大于一
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $n=3,r=2$, the sum is $2+8+24=34$. The quotient gives $[2-4\cdot16+3\cdot32]/(1-2)^2=34$. For the opening $r=1/2$ example, it gives $11/8$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $n=3,r=2$ 时，原和为 $2+8+24=34$；闭式给出 $[2-4\cdot16+3\cdot32]/(1-2)^2=34$。开头 $r=1/2$ 的例子则得到 $11/8$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The missing point in the quotient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*分式漏掉的点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $r=1$, return to the original sum: $W_n(1)=1+2+\cdots+n=n(n+1)/2$, also zero at $n=0$. Pairing the sum with its reverse gives $n$ pairs each equal to $n+1$, so twice the sum is $n(n+1)$. The quotient is undefined at one because of its denominator; the polynomial sum is perfectly defined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$r=1$ 时回到原和：$W_n(1)=1+2+\cdots+n=n(n+1)/2$，$n=0$ 时也为零。把正序与倒序的和逐项相加，得到 $n$ 对，每对为 $n+1$，所以原和的两倍是 $n(n+1)$。分式在 1 处因分母而未定义，多项式求和本身则有定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If one later lets $n\to\infty$, convergence becomes a separate problem. The finite identity alone does not justify discarding its terms containing $r^{n+1}$. The [[几何级数|geometric-series convergence condition]] helps identify when decay can overcome the extra linear weight.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若之后再让 $n\to\infty$，收敛性就成为需要另查的问题。不能仅凭有限恒等式就扔掉含 $r^{n+1}$ 的项；[[几何级数|几何级数的收敛条件]]有助于判断衰减何时能够压过额外的线性权重。
