# 弱式半强式与强式有效市场是假设嵌套信息集而非质量评分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Weak, semi-strong, and strong-form efficiency concern nested information sets rather than market-quality scores*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子分类
> 弱式考察历史价格与交易信息，半强式扩展到全部公开信息，强式再扩展到私人信息。信息集依次扩大，所以强式命题最强；它们不是把一个市场从“差”到“完美”打三档分数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Weak-form efficiency concerns historical price and trading information, semi-strong efficiency expands to all public information, and strong-form efficiency further includes private information. The sets are nested, so the strong-form claim is strongest; the labels are not three quality grades from poor to perfect.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一市场可能对标准化公开公告反应很快，却对复杂、模糊或取得成本高的公开披露反应较慢。检验结果还随资产、时期、频率与执行约束而变；不能把某一类信息的一次检验外推成“整个市场达到半强式”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same market may react quickly to standardised announcements but more slowly to complex, ambiguous, or costly public disclosures. Results can also vary with asset, period, frequency, and execution constraints. One test of one information type cannot certify an entire market as “semi-strong efficient.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

强式不是规范目标。内幕信息未进入价格可能同时反映信息确实私有、法律禁止交易或无法可靠推断；这不把弱式或半强式结论自动推翻。每项证据应回到它实际覆盖的信息集。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Strong-form efficiency is not a normative target. Private information may remain outside prices because it is genuinely private, legally non-tradable, or hard to infer; that does not automatically overturn weak- or semi-strong-form evidence. Each result must be mapped back to the information set it actually covers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个市场对盈利公告在数分钟内反应，能否据此宣布它“强式有效”？
>
> **答案：** 不能。该证据至多涉及一种公开信息的吸收速度；它没有检验私人信息，也没有覆盖其他资产、时期和成本条件。
