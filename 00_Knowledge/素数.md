# Prime numbers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*素数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Seven counters can be arranged as one group of seven or seven groups of one. They cannot be divided into equal whole groups of size two, three, four, five or six. Six counters have another arrangement: two groups of three. A **prime number** has only the first two kinds of positive-factor arrangement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

七个物品可以排成一组七个，或七组一个，却不能恰好分成每组二、三、四、五或六个的整组。六个物品还有另一种排法：两组三个。**素数**只有前面那两种正因子分组方式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here a positive **divisor** means a positive integer that [[整除|divides]] the number exactly: $d\mid p$ means $p=dk$ for an integer $k$. The vertical stroke is a relation, not a quotient. We use “prime” and “composite” below for positive integers; negative factors do not create extra positive divisors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的正**因子**是能恰好[[整除]]这个数的正整数：$d\mid p$ 表示存在整数 $k$ 使 $p=dk$。竖线表示关系，不表示商。下面的“素数”和“合数”都用于正整数；负因子不算额外的正因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Exactly two positive divisors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*恰好两个正因子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An integer $p>1$ is prime if its only positive divisors are $1$ and $p$, as with $2,3,5,7$. A positive integer greater than $1$ that is not prime is composite, as with $6=2\cdot3$. The integer $1$ is neither prime nor composite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

整数 $p>1$ 若只有 $1$ 和 $p$ 两个正因子，就称为素数；例如 $2,3,5,7$。大于 $1$ 而不是素数的正整数称为合数，例如 $6=2\cdot3$。$1$ 既不是素数，也不是合数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both conditions matter: $p$ must be an integer greater than one, and **every** positive divisor must be either $1$ or $p$. The symbols $\mathbb Z$ and $\mathbb Z_{>0}$ denote the integers and the positive integers. In symbols,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个条件都重要：$p$ 必须是大于一的整数，而且**每个**正因子都只能是 $1$ 或 $p$。符号 $\mathbb Z$、$\mathbb Z_{>0}$ 分别表示整数与正整数。用符号写成

$$p\in\mathbb Z,\quad p>1,\qquad
\forall d\in\mathbb Z_{>0},\quad d\mid p\Longrightarrow(d=1\text{ or }d=p).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $p>1$, the divisors $1$ and $p$ are distinct and always work. Thus the formula says there are exactly two positive divisors. For $6$, the extra divisor $2$ gives $6=2\cdot3$, so the condition fails. In general, if $n>1$ has a divisor $d$ with $1<d<n$, then $n=dk$ with a positive integer $1<k<n$. Conversely, a factorization into two integers strictly between $1$ and $n$ supplies an extra divisor. This follows from the definition, without assuming unique factorization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $p>1$ 时，$1$ 与 $p$ 不同，且必定都能整除 $p$，因此公式确实要求恰好两个正因子。对 $6$，额外因子 $2$ 给出 $6=2\cdot3$，所以它不满足条件。一般地，若 $n>1$ 有因子 $d$ 满足 $1<d<n$，则 $n=dk$，其中 $k$ 是满足 $1<k<n$ 的正整数；反过来，这样的两个因子相乘也给出了额外因子。这个结论直接来自定义，不需要先假设唯一分解。

> [!example] One, two and nine · 一、二与九
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The only positive divisor of $1$ is $1$, so it does not have two distinct positive divisors and fails $p>1$. The number $2$ has divisors $1,2$ and is prime. Every even integer $n>2$ has the extra divisor $2$, so $2$ is the only even prime. Being odd is insufficient: $9=3\cdot3$ has positive divisors $1,3,9$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $1$ 的正因子只有 $1$，没有两个不同的正因子，也不满足 $p>1$。$2$ 的正因子为 $1,2$，所以是素数。每个大于 $2$ 的偶整数都有额外因子 $2$，因此 $2$ 是唯一偶素数。奇数却未必是素数：$9=3\cdot3$ 的正因子为 $1,3,9$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero and negative integers are outside this prime/composite classification. In particular, every positive integer divides zero because $0=d\cdot0$; zero does not acquire just two positive divisors. A noninteger such as $5/2$ is also outside the definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零和负整数不属于这里的素数／合数分类。特别地，每个正整数都整除零，因为 $0=d\cdot0$；零并不是只有两个正因子。$5/2$ 这样的非整数也不在定义范围内。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Prime and coprime answer different questions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*素数与互素回答不同问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Primality concerns the divisors of one integer; coprimality concerns whether two integers have [[最大公因数|gcd]] equal to $1$. Thus $8$ and $15$ are coprime without being prime. For a prime $p$, if $p\nmid a$, then $\gcd(p,a)=1$, since the gcd must be one of $p$'s two positive divisors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

素数说的是一个整数自身的因子；“互素”说的是两个整数的[[最大公因数]]是否为 $1$。因此 $8$ 与 $15$ 可以互素，却都不是素数。对素数 $p$，若 $p\nmid a$，则 $\gcd(p,a)=1$，因为 gcd 只能是 $p$ 的两个正因子之一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To unpack the last statement, fix a prime $p$ and any integer $a$. The [[最大公因数|greatest common divisor]] $g=\gcd(p,a)$ exists and is positive because $p\ne0$. Since $g\mid p$, primality leaves only $g=1$ or $g=p$. The second choice would imply $p\mid a$. Therefore $p\nmid a$ forces $g=1$. For instance, $\gcd(5,6)=1$, whereas $\gcd(5,10)=5$. At $a=0$, we have $p\mid0$ and $\gcd(p,0)=p$, so the nondivisibility condition does not hold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把最后一句展开：固定素数 $p$ 与任意整数 $a$。由于 $p\ne0$，[[最大公因数]] $g=\gcd(p,a)$ 存在且为正；又因 $g\mid p$，素数定义只允许 $g=1$ 或 $g=p$。第二种选择会推出 $p\mid a$，所以 $p\nmid a$ 时只能有 $g=1$。例如 $\gcd(5,6)=1$，而 $\gcd(5,10)=5$。当 $a=0$ 时，$p\mid0$ 且 $\gcd(p,0)=p$，不满足“不整除”的条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This definition supplies the hypothesis for [[素数整除乘积|Euclid's lemma]] and [[费马小定理|Fermat's little theorem]]. “Prime” cannot be replaced by “odd” or “composite”: for example, odd $9$ has the additional positive divisor $3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个定义是[[素数整除乘积]]与[[费马小定理]]的条件，不能把这些定理里的“素数”随意换成“奇数”或“合数”。例如 $9$ 是奇数，但有额外正因子 $3$。
