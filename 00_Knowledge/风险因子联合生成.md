# 风险因子联合生成必须同时规定边际分布与横截面依赖
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Joint risk-factor generation must specify both marginal distributions and cross-sectional dependence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 单因子拟合正确，不等于组合联合分布正确
> 边际决定每个风险因子在目标时点怎样变化，横截面依赖决定这些因子在同一时点怎样共同变化；只有两者同时给定，才定义了可用于组合损失的终点联合分布。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Margins determine how each risk factor moves at the target horizon, while cross-sectional dependence determines how those factors move together at that horizon. Both are required to define a terminal joint distribution for portfolio loss.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个可审计的终点联合生成器至少应写清：

1. 每个价格、利率、汇率、利差或波动率因子的创新分布、尺度、偏态、厚尾与支持集；
2. 相关矩阵、因子模型或 copula 如何连接边际，是否允许尾部依赖、共同跳跃或状态变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An auditable terminal-state generator states each factor's innovation distribution and support and explains how a correlation matrix, factor model, or copula joins the margins and represents tail dependence, common jumps, or state dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如两个因子各自的正态边际都拟合良好，但在危机中存在未建模的共同跳跃，则单因子直方图可以正确，组合 99% 损失尾部仍会严重错误。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, both factors can have well-fitted normal margins while an omitted common crisis jump makes the portfolio's 99% loss tail badly wrong.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 每个风险因子的边际分布都通过检验，是否足以验证组合尾部？
>
> **答案：** 不足。还要验证横截面依赖和尾部共同运动；正确的单变量直方图不能证明联合尾部正确。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** If every risk factor's marginal distribution passes validation, is that sufficient to validate the portfolio tail?
>
> **Answer:** No. Cross-sectional dependence and joint tail movement must also be validated; correct univariate histograms do not prove that the joint tail is correct.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- 常数相关矩阵不能自动表示危机依赖。
- 正半定协方差只保证数学可用，不保证经济代表性。
- 终点联合分布不等于完整路径模型；需要多期状态时还要进入 [[风险因子时间动态]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- A constant correlation matrix does not automatically represent crisis dependence.
- Positive semidefiniteness ensures mathematical validity, not economic representativeness.
- A terminal joint distribution is not a complete path model; multi-period states additionally require a [[风险因子时间动态|risk-factor time-dynamics model]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
