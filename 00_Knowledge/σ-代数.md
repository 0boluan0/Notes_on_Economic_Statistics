# σ-代数是包含全集并对补集和可数并封闭的事件集合族

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A sigma-algebra contains the whole space and is closed under complements and countable unions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 给定 $\Omega$，集合族 $\mathcal F$ 是 σ-代数，当它包含 $\Omega$，且 $A\in\mathcal F$ 推出 $A^c\in\mathcal F$，以及可数个 $A_j\in\mathcal F$ 的并仍在 $\mathcal F$ 中。它规定[[概率空间]]里哪些事件可以被赋予概率。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A sigma-algebra contains the whole space and is closed under complements and countable unions. It specifies the events to which probability can be assigned in a [[概率空间|probability space]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

硬币若允许问“正面吗”，就也要允许问其补事件“不是正面吗”。$\{\varnothing,\{H\},\Omega\}$ 缺少 $\{T\}$，不是 σ-代数。有限空间可取全部子集；也可取更粗的合法集合族。骰子只报告奇偶时，$\{\varnothing,\{1,3,5\},\{2,4,6\},\Omega\}$ 描述这一级信息，不能区分单点。信息随时间增长时接到[[滤过定义]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If “heads?” is an allowed question, its complement must be allowed too. Omitting tails makes the proposed collection invalid. A finite space may use its full power set or a coarser sigma-algebra, such as one recording only a die’s parity. Growing information over time leads to [[滤过定义|a filtration]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
