# Sigma-algebra
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*σ-代数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **sigma-algebra** is a collection of events that stays consistent when we combine questions about an experiment. If “the result is odd” is an event in the collection, then “the result is not odd” must be included too. If we can ask each of a countable list of questions, we must also be able to ask whether at least one of them is true. These requirements explain what “closed under complements and countable unions” means.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**σ-代数**是一族事件，要求我们把关于试验的问题组合起来以后，仍能得到这族中的事件。如果它包含“结果是奇数”，就也必须包含“结果不是奇数”；如果可以分别提出可数多个问题，就也必须能问“这些问题中是否至少有一个为真”。这就是“对补集与可数并封闭”的含义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a [[概率空间|probability space]], the sample space $\Omega$ is the set of possible outcomes. Each event is a subset of $\Omega$, and $\mathcal F$ is a collection of such subsets. Thus $A\subseteq\Omega$ means that every outcome in $A$ belongs to $\Omega$, whereas $A\in\mathcal F$ means that the set $A$ is one of the events in the collection. The sigma-algebra specifies the events; it does not yet specify their probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[概率空间]]中，样本空间 $\Omega$ 是可能结果的集合，每个事件是 $\Omega$ 的一个子集，而 $\mathcal F$ 是由这些子集组成的集合族。因此，$A\subseteq\Omega$ 表示 $A$ 中的每个结果都属于 $\Omega$；$A\in\mathcal F$ 则表示集合 $A$ 是这族事件中的一员。σ-代数规定有哪些事件，还没有规定这些事件各有多大概率。

> [!example] A display that reveals only parity · 只显示奇偶的屏幕
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Roll a die, but reveal only whether its face is odd or even. Write $O=\{1,3,5\}$ and $E=\{2,4,6\}$. The events distinguishable from this display are
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 掷一枚骰子，但只显示点数的奇偶。记奇数点集合为 $O=\{1,3,5\}$，偶数点集合为 $E=\{2,4,6\}$。根据这个显示结果能够区分的事件是
>
> $$\mathcal F=\{\varnothing,O,E,\Omega\},\qquad\Omega=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These mean “never”, “odd”, “even”, and “always”. The exact result $1$ is still in $\Omega$, but the event $\{1\}$ is not in this $\mathcal F$: an odd display does not distinguish $1$ from $3$ or $5$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 四个事件分别对应“永不发生”“奇数”“偶数”“必定发生”。具体结果 $1$ 仍在 $\Omega$ 中，但事件 $\{1\}$ 不属于这个 $\mathcal F$：看到奇数，不能区分点数究竟是 $1$、$3$ 还是 $5$。

## The operations that must stay inside the collection
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*组合之后仍然属于这族事件*

> [!abstract] The closure requirements · 封闭条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A collection $\mathcal F$ of subsets of $\Omega$ is a sigma-algebra if it satisfies all three requirements below.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\Omega$ 的子集族 $\mathcal F$ 是 σ-代数，当它满足下面三个要求。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **The whole space is included:** $\Omega\in\mathcal F$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **包含全集：** $\Omega\in\mathcal F$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Complements stay inside:** if $A\in\mathcal F$, then $A^c=\Omega\setminus A\in\mathcal F$. Here $A^c$ contains the outcomes outside $A$, so it represents “$A$ does not occur”.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **补集仍在其中：** 若 $A\in\mathcal F$，则 $A^c=\Omega\setminus A\in\mathcal F$。$A^c$ 收集 $A$ 之外的结果，表示“$A$ 没有发生”。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Countable unions stay inside:** if $A_1,A_2,\ldots$ all belong to $\mathcal F$, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **可数并仍在其中：** 若 $A_1,A_2,\ldots$ 都属于 $\mathcal F$，则
>
> $$\bigcup_{j=1}^{\infty}A_j\in\mathcal F.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This union contains every outcome belonging to at least one $A_j$. “Countable” allows finitely many events or an infinite list indexed by $1,2,3,\ldots$; the events need not be disjoint.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这个并集收集至少属于某个 $A_j$ 的结果。“可数”可以是有限多个，也可以是能用 $1,2,3,\ldots$ 编号的无穷列表；这里不要求事件互斥。

^core

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The parity collection satisfies these requirements. The complement of $O$ is $E$, and the complement of $\Omega$ is $\varnothing$. Any union drawn from its four events is again one of those four events. By contrast, on a coin space $\{H,T\}$, the collection $\{\varnothing,\{H\},\Omega\}$ fails: it includes $\{H\}$ but omits its complement $\{T\}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

奇偶例子满足这些要求。$O$ 的补集是 $E$，$\Omega$ 的补集是 $\varnothing$；无论从这四个事件中选哪些来取并集，结果仍然是这四者之一。相比之下，在硬币空间 $\{H,T\}$ 上，集合族 $\{\varnothing,\{H\},\Omega\}$ 不合格：它包含 $\{H\}$，却没有包含其补集 $\{T\}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rules also give us the empty set and intersections. The empty set is $\Omega^c$. An intersection $A\cap B$ asks for both events to occur; it can be written as $(A^c\cup B^c)^c$, so it stays in $\mathcal F$ too. The same reasoning works for countably many intersections. There is no need to add these as separate requirements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些规则也保证空集和交集在其中：空集就是 $\Omega^c$；交集 $A\cap B$ 要求两个事件都发生，可以写成 $(A^c\cup B^c)^c$，因此仍属于 $\mathcal F$。同样的推理也适用于可数多个事件的交集，所以不用再把它们列为额外条件。

## Why “countable” is stronger than “finite”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么只检查有限次合并还不够*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a finite sample space, a collection containing $\Omega$ and closed under complements and finite unions is already closed under countable unions: there are only finitely many distinct subsets to combine. On an infinite sample space this implication can fail. For example, take $\Omega=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$ and allow sets that are finite or have a finite complement. Complements and finite unions remain allowed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在有限样本空间上，若一个包含 $\Omega$ 的集合族对补集和有限并封闭，它也会对可数并封闭，因为可供合并的不同子集总共只有有限个。但在无限样本空间中，这个推论可能失败。例如，取 $\Omega=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$，只允许有限集合，以及补集为有限集合的集合。取补集或有限次并集，都不会超出这一范围。

> [!warning] Every finite step can pass while an infinite union fails · 有限步都通过，无穷并仍可能失败
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each singleton $\{2\},\{4\},\{6\},\ldots$ is finite and therefore allowed in that example. Their countable union is the set of all even positive integers. It is infinite, and its complement—the odd positive integers—is also infinite. The union is therefore excluded. This collection is an **algebra of sets**, but not a sigma-algebra.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上例中的每个单点集 $\{2\},\{4\},\{6\},\ldots$ 都有限，因此都被允许。但它们的可数并是全部正偶数，既不是有限集合，补集——全部正奇数——也不是有限集合，所以这个并集被排除了。这族集合是一个**集合代数**，却不是 σ-代数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The word “countable” limits the number of events combined in one operation; it does not say that $\mathcal F$ itself must contain only countably many events. Taking every subset of $\Omega$ is always a sigma-algebra as a set collection, while smaller sigma-algebras can encode less detailed information. When that information grows over time, a nested sequence of sigma-algebras is called a [[滤过定义|filtration]]. Assigning probabilities consistently on a chosen sigma-algebra is the separate job of a probability measure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“可数”限制的是一次合并操作中选取的事件数量，不是要求 $\mathcal F$ 本身只能含可数个事件。作为集合族，把 $\Omega$ 的全部子集都取来，总是一个 σ-代数；较小的 σ-代数则可以表达较粗的信息。信息随时间增加时，逐层扩大的 σ-代数族叫作[[滤过定义|滤过]]。在选定的 σ-代数上一致地分配概率，是概率测度负责的另一件事。
