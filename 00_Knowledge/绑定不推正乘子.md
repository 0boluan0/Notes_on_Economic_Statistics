# Binding constraints with zero multipliers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*绑定约束与零乘子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A limit may be met exactly even when relaxing it has no first-order benefit. Think of a cap that happens to pass through the best unconstrained choice: the cap touches the chosen point, but a small relaxation need not improve the objective. **Binding** describes the constraint value. A **multiplier** comes from the stationarity equations. They are different pieces of information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条上限恰好被用尽，也可能没有一阶放宽收益。设上限刚好穿过无约束下最好的选择：它碰到了所选点，但稍微放宽未必改善目标。**绑定**描述约束值；**乘子**由驻点方程确定。两者提供的信息不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[KKT条件|maximization convention]], write an inequality as $g_i(x)\le0$ and its multiplier as $\lambda_i\ge0$, with Lagrangian $\mathcal L=f-\sum_i\lambda_i g_i$ when there are no equalities. A feasible $x^*$ is binding for index $i$ if $g_i(x^*)=0$, and strictly slack if $g_i(x^*)<0$. [[互补松弛|Complementary slackness]] imposes $\lambda_i g_i(x^*)=0$. It permits both factors to vanish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按[[KKT条件|最大化的号约定]]，不等式写作 $g_i(x)\le0$，乘子为 $\lambda_i\ge0$；没有等式时取 $\mathcal L=f-\sum_i\lambda_i g_i$。在可行点 $x^*$，$g_i(x^*)=0$ 表示第 $i$ 条绑定，$g_i(x^*)<0$ 表示严格松弛。[[互补松弛]]要求 $\lambda_i g_i(x^*)=0$，允许两个因子同时为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Complementary slackness implies that a slack constraint has zero multiplier and that a positive multiplier forces binding. The converse from binding to a positive multiplier fails because both factors may be zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在最大化、$g_i(x)\le0$、$\lambda_i\ge0$ 的约定下，[[互补松弛]] 给出
$$
g_i(x^*)<0\Rightarrow\lambda_i^*=0,
\qquad
\lambda_i^*>0\Rightarrow g_i(x^*)=0.
$$
反向的“$g_i(x^*)=0\Rightarrow\lambda_i^*>0$”不成立，因为乘积 $\lambda_i^*g_i(x^*)$ 允许两项同时为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One active constraint, one zero multiplier
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小反例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maximising $-x^2$ subject to $x\le0$ gives the binding optimum $x^*=0$. Stationarity for $\mathcal L=-x^2-\lambda x$ yields $\lambda^*=0$. The constraint touches the optimum but has no first-order marginal value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑
$$
\max_x -x^2\quad\text{s.t.}\quad x\le0.
$$
最优点 $x^*=0$ 使约束绑定。取 $g(x)=x$ 与 $\mathcal L=-x^2-\lambda x$，驻点条件给
$$
-2x^*-\lambda^*=0,
$$
所以 $\lambda^*=0$。约束碰到最优点，却没有一阶边际价值；移除它也不会改变无约束最优点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every feasible $x\le0$, $-x^2\le0=f(0)$, with equality only at zero. The chosen point is therefore the unique global maximizer, rather than merely a solution of a derivative equation. Here $g(0)=0$ verifies activity, $\lambda=0$ satisfies its nonnegative sign, the displayed stationarity equation holds, and $\lambda g(0)=0$ verifies complementarity. Moreover, $g^{\prime}(0)=1\ne0$, so the one-member gradient family satisfies [[LICQ条件|LICQ]]. A zero multiplier is compatible even with this regularity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意可行 $x\le0$，有 $-x^2\le0=f(0)$，仅在零点相等。因此它是唯一全局最大点，而不只是导数方程的一个解。这里 $g(0)=0$ 验证活动性，$\lambda=0$ 符合非负号要求，上述驻点式成立，$\lambda g(0)=0$ 验证互补性。此外，$g^{\prime}(0)=1\ne0$，单个梯度组成的族满足[[LICQ条件|LICQ]]；零乘子甚至与这种正则性相容。

## What a zero marginal value does and does not say
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零边际价值的范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To check the sensitivity interpretation rather than assume it, replace the cap by $x\le b$, where $b$ is a real parameter, and let $v(b)$ be the largest attainable objective value. If $b\ge0$, the unconstrained maximizer zero is feasible. If $b<0$, every feasible point is at most $b$, and the least possible squared distance from zero is $b^2$, achieved at $x=b$. Hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为直接检验敏感度解释，把上限改成 $x\le b$，其中 $b$ 为实参数，$v(b)$ 表示可达到的最大目标值。若 $b\ge0$，无约束最大点零可行；若 $b<0$，所有可行点都不超过 $b$，离零的最小平方距离是 $b^2$，在 $x=b$ 取得。因此

$$
v(b)=\begin{cases}-b^2,&b<0,\\0,&b\ge0.\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The left difference quotient at zero is $(-b^2-0)/b=-b\to0$; the right quotient is zero. Thus $v^{\prime}(0)=0$, exactly the multiplier found above. Nevertheless, tightening the cap to $b=-0.1$ lowers the value to $-0.01$. Zero first-order value does not mean every finite tightening is harmless. In general, interpreting a multiplier as a derivative of a value function requires the hypotheses described by [[影子价格|shadow prices]]; the product equation alone does not supply them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零点左侧差商是 $(-b^2-0)/b=-b\to0$，右侧差商为零，所以 $v^{\prime}(0)=0$，恰与前面算出的乘子一致。但把上限收紧到 $b=-0.1$ 会使最优值降到 $-0.01$。一阶价值为零，不表示任意有限收紧都无影响。一般情况下，把乘子解释成价值函数导数还需要[[影子价格]]所述前提；仅凭乘积为零不能得到这些条件。

## Activity is decided before multiplier signs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 活动集仍按约束值定义

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The active set is defined by $g_i(x^*)=0$, not by positive multipliers. Using multipliers would omit binding zero-multiplier normals and corrupt the LICQ test. Positivity on every binding inequality is an additional strict-complementarity property.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

活动集是 $\mathcal A(x^*)=\{i:g_i(x^*)=0\}$，不能改成 $\{i:\lambda_i^*>0\}$。否则会漏掉绑定零乘子的法向量，并错误改变 [[LICQ条件]] 的检查对象。“每条绑定约束都有正乘子”称为严格互补的一部分，是额外性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here “normals” refers to the indexed constraint gradients used in the stationarity equation. Every active index stays in the LICQ family even if its multiplier, or its gradient, is zero. At a feasible complementary pair, strict complementarity for inequalities asks that each index have either strictly positive slack $-g_i(x^*)$ or a strictly positive multiplier; it excludes the simultaneous-zero case but is not part of the ordinary KKT definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里“法向量”指驻点式使用的、带约束索引的梯度。每个活动索引都须留在 LICQ 梯度族中，即使乘子为零，甚至梯度本身为零。在可行且互补的候选对上，不等式的严格互补要求每个索引要么松弛量 $-g_i(x^*)$ 严格为正，要么乘子严格为正；它排除了同时为零的情形，但不是普通 KKT 定义的一部分。

> [!question]- Check the implication · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a resource constraint binds at the optimum, does that fact alone make its [[影子价格|shadow price]] strictly positive?
>
> **Answer:** No. Binding determines only the constraint value. Its multiplier may still be zero and must be determined from stationarity and the local sensitivity of the value function.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 已知资源约束在最优点取等号，能否仅凭这一点宣布其 [[影子价格]] 严格为正？
>
>
>
> **答案：** 不能。绑定只确定约束值；乘子还可能为零，必须由驻点系统和价值函数局部敏感度确定。
