# 约束在最优点绑定并不能推出其乘子严格为正；互补松弛允许约束值与乘子同时为零
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A binding constraint does not imply a strictly positive multiplier because complementary slackness permits both the constraint value and multiplier to be zero*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 互补松弛只有两个安全方向
> 在最大化、$g_i(x)\le0$、$\lambda_i\ge0$ 的约定下，[[互补松弛]] 给出
> $$
> g_i(x^*)<0\Rightarrow\lambda_i^*=0,
> \qquad
> \lambda_i^*>0\Rightarrow g_i(x^*)=0.
> $$
> 反向的“$g_i(x^*)=0\Rightarrow\lambda_i^*>0$”不成立，因为乘积 $\lambda_i^*g_i(x^*)$ 允许两项同时为零。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Complementary slackness implies that a slack constraint has zero multiplier and that a positive multiplier forces binding. The converse from binding to a positive multiplier fails because both factors may be zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小反例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal counterexample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑
$$
\max_x -x^2\quad\text{s.t.}\quad x\le0.
$$
最优点 $x^*=0$ 使约束绑定。取 $g(x)=x$ 与 $\mathcal L=-x^2-\lambda x$，驻点条件给
$$
-2x^*-\lambda^*=0,
$$
所以 $\lambda^*=0$。约束碰到最优点，却没有一阶边际价值；移除它也不会改变无约束最优点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maximising $-x^2$ subject to $x\le0$ gives the binding optimum $x^*=0$. Stationarity for $\mathcal L=-x^2-\lambda x$ yields $\lambda^*=0$. The constraint touches the optimum but has no first-order marginal value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 活动集仍按约束值定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The active set is still defined by constraint values*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

活动集是 $\mathcal A(x^*)=\{i:g_i(x^*)=0\}$，不能改成 $\{i:\lambda_i^*>0\}$。否则会漏掉绑定零乘子的法向量，并错误改变 [[LICQ条件]] 的检查对象。“每条绑定约束都有正乘子”称为严格互补的一部分，是额外性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The active set is defined by $g_i(x^*)=0$, not by positive multipliers. Using multipliers would omit binding zero-multiplier normals and corrupt the LICQ test. Positivity on every binding inequality is an additional strict-complementarity property.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 已知资源约束在最优点取等号，能否仅凭这一点宣布其 [[影子价格]] 严格为正？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a resource constraint binds at the optimum, does that fact alone make its [[影子价格|shadow price]] strictly positive?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。绑定只确定约束值；乘子还可能为零，必须由驻点系统和价值函数局部敏感度确定。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. Binding determines only the constraint value. Its multiplier may still be zero and must be determined from stationarity and the local sensitivity of the value function.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
