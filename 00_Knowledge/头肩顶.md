# 头肩顶是中峰高于两肩并在右肩后跌破颈线的三峰形态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A head-and-shoulders top has a central peak above two shoulders and completes with a neckline break after the right shoulder.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

头肩顶是上涨后依次形成左肩、较高头部和较低右肩，并在右肩之后向下突破颈线的反转形态。左肩与头部之间、头部与右肩之间的两个低点连成颈线，作为 [[支撑位]]参考。三峰轮廓只是候选；两肩应处于相近高位，但不必完全等高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A head-and-shoulders top follows an advance: a left shoulder, a higher head and a lower right shoulder precede a downward neckline break. The two intervening lows define the neckline as a [[支撑位|support]] reference. The three-peak outline is only a candidate; the shoulders occupy similar, not necessarily identical, levels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

颈线可以水平或倾斜；取两低点的时间和价格坐标延长，突破时应与该时点的线值比较。它使用 [[趋势线]]式连线构造，但不是连接三个高点的线。峰谷确认、肩高容差和突破缓冲须先固定；右肩完成或画出颈线本身不等于已经成交，见 [[交易信号时序]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The neckline may be horizontal or sloped. Extend the line through the two lows in time-price coordinates and compare a possible break with its value at that time. It uses a [[趋势线|trendline-style]] construction, not a line through the three highs. Fix turning-point confirmation, shoulder tolerance and breakout buffers; identifying a right shoulder or drawing a neckline does not itself execute a trade. See [[交易信号时序|signal and execution timing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对照：若三个选定峰依次为 110、120、110，中间两谷在 $(t,P)=(2,100),(4,104)$，则线性坐标下颈线为 $N(t)=100+2(t-2)$。在右肩之后的 $t=6$，颈线是 108；采用收盘严格下破且无缓冲的规则，收盘 107 才满足下破，而不是拿它与旧谷 100 比较。这个数例只检验几何条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For selected peaks of 110, 120 and 110 with intervening lows at $(2,100)$ and $(4,104)$, the linear-axis neckline is $N(t)=100+2(t-2)$. At $t=6$ after the right shoulder, it is 108. A close at 107 satisfies a strict, unbuffered closing break; the comparison is not with the old trough at 100. This example tests geometry only.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
