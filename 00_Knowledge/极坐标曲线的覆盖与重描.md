# Tracing a polar curve
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*极坐标曲线的覆盖与重描*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A polar equation tells us where a point is for each allowed angle. To know the whole drawing, we must also know which angles are allowed and how the point moves as they increase. The same equation on two different intervals may draw one arc, a complete curve, or the same curve several times.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极坐标方程说明每个允许角度对应的点在哪里。要知道整幅图，还必须知道允许哪些角度，以及角度增加时点怎样移动。同一个方程配上不同区间，可能只画出一段弧，也可能画出完整曲线，或把同一条曲线描很多遍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f$ be a continuous real-valued function on an interval $I$. Using the [[极坐标|polar coordinate map]], the equation $r=f(\theta)$ defines the [[参数曲线|parametric curve]]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f$ 是区间 $I$ 上的连续实值函数。利用[[极坐标]]映射，方程 $r=f(\theta)$ 定义[[参数曲线]]

$$\gamma(\theta)=\bigl(f(\theta)\cos\theta,\,f(\theta)\sin\theta\bigr),\qquad \theta\in I.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\gamma$ names the point-valued map, and its image $\gamma(I)$ is the set of points visited. If a formula is undefined at an angle, that angle is not an allowed parameter value without a separately justified extension. When $f(\theta)<0$, the physical ray is $\theta+\pi$, not $\theta$. The [[极坐标表示不唯一|equivalent polar representations]] explain how different parameter values can reach the same point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $\gamma$ 是取值为平面点的映射，像集 $\gamma(I)$ 是实际经过的点的集合。若某个角度处公式无定义，就不能未经另行论证延拓而把该角度当作合法参数。当 $f(\theta)<0$ 时，物理射线的方向是 $\theta+\pi$，不是 $\theta$。[[极坐标表示不唯一|等价极坐标表示]]解释了不同参数值为何可以到达同一个点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One circle, two possible covering counts
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*同一个圆，不同的覆盖次数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $r=2a\cos\theta$ with $a>0$. The double-angle identities give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $r=2a\cos\theta$，其中 $a>0$。由倍角恒等式，

$$\gamma(\theta)=\bigl(a(1+\cos2\theta),\,a\sin2\theta\bigr),\qquad (x-a)^2+y^2=a^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a circle of radius $a$ centred at $(a,0)$. Relative to its centre, the point turns through angle $2\theta$. Consequently, a closed interval of length $\pi$ traces the circle once, with the start and end point identified; a closed interval of length $2\pi$ traces it twice. Eliminating the parameter finds the circle but loses this count, just as in [[同轨迹不等于同参数化|different parametrisations of one trace]].

The interval $[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$ is convenient because $r\ge0$. But $[0,\pi]$ also traces it once: $\theta=0,\pi/4,\pi/2,3\pi/4,\pi$ give $(2a,0),(a,a),(0,0),(a,-a),(2a,0)$. In the second half the radius is negative, placing the point below the horizontal axis. Requiring a nonnegative radius is a useful choice of representation, not a necessary condition for this one-pass parametrisation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是以 $(a,0)$ 为圆心、半径为 $a$ 的圆。相对于圆心，点转过的角度为 $2\theta$。因此，长度为 $\pi$ 的闭参数区间恰好描圆一次，起点与终点重合；长度为 $2\pi$ 的闭区间描两次。消去参数能找到圆，却丢失了这个次数，正如[[同轨迹不等于同参数化|同一轨迹的不同参数化]]。

区间 $[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$ 很方便，因为其上 $r\ge0$。但 $[0,\pi]$ 也描圆一次：$\theta=0,\pi/4,\pi/2,3\pi/4,\pi$ 依次给出 $(2a,0),(a,a),(0,0),(a,-a),(2a,0)$。后半段半径为负，使点落在水平轴下方。非负半径是一种方便的表示选择，并不是这个单次描画的必要条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The period of the radius is not the period of the point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*半径函数的周期不等于平面点的周期*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A repeated radius value does not by itself repeat the direction. A constant radius $R>0$ has every positive number as a period as a scalar function, but $(R\cos\theta,R\sin\theta)$ has least positive period $2\pi$. Conversely, the circle above has $f(\theta+\pi)=-f(\theta)$, while the direction also reverses; the two reversals make $\gamma(\theta+\pi)=\gamma(\theta)$.

For the rose $r=\sin2\theta$, the scalar function has period $\pi$, but $\gamma(\theta+\pi)=-\gamma(\theta)$. Thus a shift of $\pi$ usually reaches the opposite point rather than repeating the point. On $[0,\pi/2]$, the radius starts at zero, reaches $1$ at $\pi/4$, and returns to zero, drawing the first-quadrant petal. The next interval $[\pi/2,\pi]$ has negative radius and draws the fourth-quadrant petal. Continuing to $2\pi$ draws the third- and then second-quadrant petals. There are four petals, not two. Revisiting the origin between petals is not retracing a nonzero arc.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半径值重复，本身并不意味着方向重复。常数半径 $R>0$ 作为标量函数，以任意正数为周期，但 $(R\cos\theta,R\sin\theta)$ 的最小正周期为 $2\pi$。反过来，上面的偏心圆满足 $f(\theta+\pi)=-f(\theta)$，而方向也反转，两次反转使 $\gamma(\theta+\pi)=\gamma(\theta)$。

对玫瑰线 $r=\sin2\theta$，标量函数的周期为 $\pi$，但 $\gamma(\theta+\pi)=-\gamma(\theta)$。因此，参数增加 $\pi$ 通常到达相反的点，而不是重复原来的点。$[0,\pi/2]$ 上半径从零开始，在 $\pi/4$ 达到 $1$，再回到零，画出第一象限的一瓣。下一段 $[\pi/2,\pi]$ 的半径为负，画出第四象限的一瓣。继续到 $2\pi$，依次画出第三、第二象限的花瓣，总共四瓣而非两瓣。花瓣之间再次经过原点，不等于重描一段非零弧。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use a few points to support a coverage argument
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用关键点辅助覆盖论证*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the allowed interval and split it at zeros, undefined angles, and useful sign changes. On each piece, determine the physical direction and use key angles to follow the motion. Symmetries and identities for the full map $\gamma$ can then establish when a complete piece repeats. A few plotted points help locate a curve, but do not prove that nothing was missed between them.

Finally distinguish two questions: which **boundary points** are traced, and which **radial segments** sweep the proposed region? A curve can avoid retracing its boundary while its origin-to-curve segments overlap. The [[极坐标面积与单次覆盖|polar area formula]] needs the second question answered before its integral can be called the area of the geometric union.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确定允许的区间，再在零点、无定义角度及有用的符号分界处分段。每一段都要确定物理方向，并用关键角度跟随点的移动。然后用完整映射 $\gamma$ 的对称性与恒等式，证明完整的一段何时重复。有限几个描点有助于定位，但不能证明点与点之间没有遗漏。

最后分清两个问题：描过了哪些**边界点**，以及哪些**径向线段**扫过了目标区域？曲线即使不重描自身，从原点连到曲线的线段仍可能重叠。使用[[极坐标面积与单次覆盖|极坐标面积公式]]时，必须先回答第二个问题，才能把积分称为几何并集的面积。
