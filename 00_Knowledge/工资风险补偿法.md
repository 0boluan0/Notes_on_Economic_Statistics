# 工资风险补偿法从可比工作的工资与死亡风险权衡估计边际风险价值

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compensating wage differentials estimate marginal mortality-risk value from wage–risk trade-offs across comparable jobs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

工资风险补偿法是[[享乐价格估值]]在工作风险上的应用：在技能、工时和其他工作属性可比时，估计工人为接受更高死亡风险得到的工资补偿。对知情且可自由选择的边际工人，光滑工资—风险表在其内点选择处的斜率，可以衡量其边际风险补偿要求，并为[[统计生命价值]]提供局部证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This method applies [[享乐价格估值|hedonic valuation]] to jobs, estimating wage compensation for higher fatality risk while comparing skills, hours and other job attributes. For an informed marginal worker choosing freely at an interior point, the slope of a smooth wage–risk schedule measures marginal required compensation and provides local evidence for [[统计生命价值|VSL]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

工资和风险必须覆盖同一期间。若在一段足够小且近似线性的风险区间中，其他属性相同的工作年薪相差1000元，年死亡概率相差0.001，则局部比值为 $1000/0.001=1000000$ 元。若风险数据写为“每10万人每年多100例”，须先转成 $100/100000=0.001$；只看到某人选了高风险工作，则仍可能只有选择界，而非精确无差异值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Wages and risks must cover the same period. Over a sufficiently small, approximately linear interval, an annual wage difference of 1,000 for an annual fatality-probability difference of 0.001 implies a local ratio of one million. An extra 100 deaths per 100,000 workers per year must first be converted to 0.001. Observing one worker's risky-job choice may still provide only a bound, not exact indifference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际估计要处理风险误测、工人的风险认知、未观察技能、伤病或其他不适，以及工人与岗位的选择匹配。均衡工资差对应边际换岗者，不自动代表全体居民或平均工人的偏好；也不能把工资差全都归因于死亡风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Empirical work must address risk measurement, perceived risks, unobserved skills, injuries or other disamenities, and worker–job sorting. Equilibrium wage differences reflect marginal switchers, not automatically all residents or the average worker; the entire wage gap cannot simply be attributed to fatality risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
