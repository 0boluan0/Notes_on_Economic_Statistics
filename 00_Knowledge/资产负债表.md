# 资产负债表是列示报告主体在特定时点所确认资产、负债和所有者权益的报表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A balance sheet reports an entity's recognised assets, liabilities, and equity at a specified date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产负债表（balance sheet，也称财务状况表）回答“在这个时点，账上有哪些资源、义务和所有者剩余权益”。它按适用会计准则确认和计量项目，并满足[[会计恒等式]]；不同日期的比较列仍各自是一个存量快照，不是全年累计交易额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A balance sheet, or statement of financial position, reports the resources, obligations, and owners' residual interest recorded at a date. Recognition and measurement follow the applicable accounting framework, and the totals obey the [[会计恒等式|accounting equation]]. Comparative columns are separate snapshots, not cumulative transaction totals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，某公司期末确认资产150、负债90、权益60，资产中现金只有5。这张表没有说公司当年收入是150，也没有说股东可立即取走60：前者要看[[利润表]]，后者还涉及变现、偿债、分配条件。[[净值]]帮助区分账面、市场和可变现口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose closing assets are 150, liabilities 90, and equity 60, with only 5 of cash. This does not mean annual revenue was 150 or that shareholders can immediately withdraw 60. Revenue belongs in the [[利润表|income statement]]; distributions also depend on realisation, obligations, and distribution constraints. [[净值|Net worth]] distinguishes book, market, and realisable measures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产账面额也不是企业所有经济优势的标价总和。确认条件、减值和计量基础会影响列示金额，部分信息需去[[财务报表附注]]查明；因此不能直接把账面权益当作股权市值。现金余额的期间来源则由[[现金流量表]]解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recorded assets are not a price list of every economic advantage. Recognition criteria, impairment, and measurement bases affect reported amounts, with supporting details in the [[财务报表附注|notes]]. Book equity therefore need not equal the market value of equity. The [[现金流量表|cash-flow statement]] explains cash movements over the period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
