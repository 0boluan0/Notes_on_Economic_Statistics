# 连续非负函数的尾积分收敛仍不足以推出函数值趋于零
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A convergent tail integral need not force a continuous nonnegative integrand to tend to zero*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子反例
> 即使 $f$ 连续、非负并且
> $$\int_1^\infty f(x)\,dx<\infty,$$
> 也未必有 $f(x)\to0$。有限总面积只限制全部尖峰面积之和；它仍允许越来越窄、但高度不下降的尖峰不断出现在远处。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A finite tail integral controls the sum of the areas under all spikes, not their individual heights. Continuous nonnegative spikes may become narrower while retaining height one, so integrability alone need not imply pointwise convergence to zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令
$$
f(x)=\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\max\!\left\{1-2^{n+1}|x-n|,0\right\}.
$$
第 $n$ 个三角尖峰以 $n$ 为中心，高为 $1$，半底宽为 $2^{-(n+1)}$。这些支撑互不相交，所以求和在每一点附近至多留下一个尖峰，$f$ 因而连续且非负。

每个尖峰的面积是
$$
\frac12\cdot 2^{-n}\cdot1=2^{-n-1},
$$
故
$$
\int_1^\infty f(x)\,dx
=\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}2^{-n-1}
=\frac14<\infty.
$$
可是对每个整数 $n\ge2$ 都有 $f(n)=1$，所以 $f(x)$ 不可能趋于零。这里失败的是由“总面积有限”倒推“每个远端函数值都小”；只增加普通连续性仍排除不了窄尖峰。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $n$th triangular bump is centred at $n$, has height one, and has half-width $2^{-(n+1)}$. Their supports are disjoint, so the sum is continuous and nonnegative. The $n$th area is $2^{-n-1}$ and the total integral is $1/4$, while $f(n)=1$ for every $n\ge2$. Thus the function does not tend to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么普通连续性没有阻止这个反例？
>
> **答案：** 每个尖峰本身都连续，但尖峰宽度可以随位置变窄；连续性没有给出一个对所有远端位置都统一有效的变化尺度。

## 来源与核验

- 本卡的三角尖峰是自包含的直接构造：逐项核对了支撑、高度、连续性和面积，并由 $f(n)=1$ 直接否定 $f(x)\to0$。当前课程材料只核验 [[趋零不推尾积分收敛|相反方向的 $1/x$ 反例]]，不把它误写成本卡的外部来源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- This card uses a self-contained triangular-bump construction. Its supports, heights, continuity, and areas are checked directly, and $f(n)=1$ disproves convergence to zero. The current course source covers only the opposite $1/x$ counterexample and is not presented as an external source for this construction.
