# A narrow-spike counterexample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*窄尖峰反例：有限积分与函数趋零*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A curve can have finite total area over an endless interval while repeatedly reaching height one, however far right we look. The trick is to make the high parts narrower and narrower. This gives a [[全称命题与反例|counterexample]] to the claim that convergence of $\int_1^\infty f(x)\,dx$ forces $f(x)\to0$, even when $f$ is continuous and nonnegative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

曲线在无限区间上的总面积可以有限，却在任意远处仍反复达到高度一。构造方法是让这些高处越来越窄。这给出一个[[全称命题与反例|反例]]：即使 $f$ 连续、非负，$\int_1^\infty f(x)\,dx$ 收敛也不能推出 $f(x)\to0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two statements measure different things. The [[反常积分|improper integral]] concerns accumulated area up to a moving cutoff. The [[无穷极限与无穷远极限|function limit at infinity]] concerns the height at **every sufficiently distant input**. Small total area does not itself make each distant height small.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这两种说法衡量的是不同对象。[[反常积分]]关心随截断点移动而累积的面积；[[无穷极限与无穷远极限|函数在无穷远的极限]]关心**每一个充分远的输入位置**上的高度。总面积很小，本身并不保证每处远端高度都小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Build one triangle at a time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先看一个三角尖峰*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each integer $n\ge2$, put a triangle of height $1$ at $x=n$, with half-width $h_n=2^{-(n+1)}$. The distance from its centre is $|x-n|$. Its height function is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个整数 $n\ge2$，在 $x=n$ 放一个高度为 $1$ 的三角形，半底宽取 $h_n=2^{-(n+1)}$。$|x-n|$ 表示到中心的距离。这个三角形的高度函数写成

$$b_n(x)=\max\{1-2^{n+1}|x-n|,\,0\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $\max$ chooses the larger of its two entries. At the centre, $b_n(n)=1$. Moving a distance $h_n$ left or right reduces the height to zero; farther away, the maximum keeps the value at zero. Each triangle therefore has base width $2h_n=2^{-n}$ and area $\tfrac12(2h_n)\cdot1=h_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\max$ 选择两个数中较大的一个。中心处 $b_n(n)=1$；向左或向右走过距离 $h_n$，高度就降到零；再远的地方，由取最大值保持为零。所以每个三角形的底宽为 $2h_n=2^{-n}$，面积为 $\tfrac12(2h_n)\cdot1=h_n$。

| Centre / 中心 | Height / 高度 | Full width / 全底宽 | Area / 面积 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $2$ | $1$ | $1/4$ | $1/8$ |
| $3$ | $1$ | $1/8$ | $1/16$ |
| $4$ | $1$ | $1/16$ | $1/32$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now define a single function on $[1,\infty)$ by adding these triangles:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把这些三角形合起来，在 $[1,\infty)$ 上定义一个函数：

$$f(x)=\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}b_n(x)
=\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}\max\{1-2^{n+1}|x-n|,\,0\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is no convergence difficulty in this pointwise sum. The centres are one unit apart, while every half-width is at most $1/8$, so their nonzero regions do not overlap. Only finitely many triangles meet any bounded interval. Each triangle joins zero continuously at its ends, so locally $f$ is a finite sum of continuous functions. Hence $f$ is continuous, with $0\le f(x)\le1$, and is [[连续函数可积|properly integrable on every finite closed interval]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个逐点求和没有无穷叠加的困难。相邻中心相隔一，而所有半底宽都不超过 $1/8$，因此非零部分互不重叠。任意有界区间只能遇到有限多个三角形；每个三角形又在两端连续接到零，所以局部只是有限个连续函数相加。于是 $f$ 连续，满足 $0\le f(x)\le1$，并且[[连续函数可积|在每个有限闭区间上正常可积]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The accumulated area converges
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*总面积为什么有限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The areas form a [[几何级数|geometric series]]: each is half the preceding area. Through triangle $N\ge2$, their finite sum is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

各三角形的面积构成[[几何级数]]：后一项总是前一项的一半。累积到第 $N$ 个尖峰（$N\ge2$），有限和为

$$S_N=\sum_{n=2}^{N}2^{-n-1}
=\frac14-2^{-N-1}\longrightarrow\frac14.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We must also control cutoffs that stop partway through a triangle. Write $A(R)=\int_1^R f(x)\,dx$ for a finite $R>1$. If $R\ge N+1/2$, all triangles through $N$ are complete. Any additional full or partial triangles contribute nonnegative area, no more than the sum of their full areas. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还需要处理截断点落在三角形中间的情况。对有限 $R>1$，写 $A(R)=\int_1^R f(x)\,dx$。只要 $R\ge N+1/2$，到第 $N$ 个为止的三角形就全部包含在内；后面那些完整或不完整三角形的面积非负，且不超过它们完整面积之和。因此

$$0\le\frac14-A(R)\le2^{-N-1}
\qquad(R\ge N+1/2).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The right side can be made arbitrarily small. This proves convergence for all sufficiently large real cutoffs, rather than only one sequence of convenient cutoffs. Consequently,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

右侧可以任意小，因此证明的是所有充分大的实数截断点都逼近同一个值，而不只是某一列方便的截断点。由此得到

$$\int_1^\infty f(x)\,dx=\frac14.$$

> [!example] Height one keeps returning · 高度一不断再现
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At every integer centre $n\ge2$, $f(n)=1$. Between the triangles, $f(n+1/2)=0$. Both input sequences go to infinity, but their heights stay at different values. Thus $f(x)$ has no limit at infinity and, in particular, does not tend to zero. Even the tolerance $1/2$ fails: however far out we choose a threshold, a later integer has height $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在每个整数中心 $n\ge2$，都有 $f(n)=1$；在三角形之间，$f(n+1/2)=0$。两列输入都趋于无穷，高度却一直不同，所以 $f(x)$ 在无穷远根本没有极限，当然不趋于零。即使只要求高度小于 $1/2$，也做不到：无论阈值选得多远，后面的整数位置仍有高度 $1$。

## Why continuity does not prevent this
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*连续为什么挡不住尖峰*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary continuity gives a suitable distance scale around each fixed point; it does not demand one scale that works everywhere on an unbounded domain. Here the peak and its zero-height endpoint are only $h_n$ apart, and $h_n\to0$, while their heights differ by $1$. In particular this function is not [[一致连续|uniformly continuous]], which would require one input tolerance for a chosen output tolerance throughout the domain. The construction therefore satisfies ordinary continuity without satisfying that stronger requirement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通连续性允许在每个固定点附近选择合适的距离尺度，并没有要求一个尺度适用于整个无界定义域。这里，峰顶与零高度端点只相距 $h_n$，而 $h_n\to0$，两处高度却相差 $1$。因此这个函数并不[[一致连续]]：一致连续要求对选定的输出误差，在整个定义域共用一个输入距离限制。本构造满足普通连续性，却不满足这个更强的要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[级数通项判别|vanishing-term condition for a convergent series]] does not transfer directly to arbitrary sampled heights of a curve. The quantities being added here are triangle **areas**, and those do tend to zero; their heights need not. The converse mistake is separate: [[趋零不推尾积分收敛|heights tending to zero need not give a finite integral]] either.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不能把[[级数通项判别|收敛级数的通项必须趋零]]直接套到曲线上任意采样的高度。本例相加的是三角形的**面积**，这些面积确实趋零，但高度不必趋零。反方向的错误则是另一个问题：[[趋零不推尾积分收敛|高度趋零也不保证积分有限]]。

## 来源与核验

- Klymchuk《Counterexamples in Calculus》§5.13（印本93页、PDF104页）：支持连续非负三角尖峰的反例构造思路。该书使用不同的高度与宽度；本卡保留原有高度一的几何构造，面积及显式余量界直接核算。
- Lebl《Basic Analysis I》第5.5节定义5.5.1（PDF214页）：正常截断积分的极限定义。正文按定义核对任意实数截断点；普通连续与一致连续的区别由峰顶及端点这对位置直接核对。
