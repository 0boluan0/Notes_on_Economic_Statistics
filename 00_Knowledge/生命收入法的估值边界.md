# 生命收入法衡量的产出损失不等于个人降低死亡风险的支付意愿

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Output losses measured by an earnings-based approach are not willingness to pay for lower mortality risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

生命收入法从死亡导致的未来劳动收入或产出损失衡量经济影响；支付意愿法则询问个人愿放弃多少货币来降低事前死亡风险。前者讨论生产或收入后果，后者讨论风险变化的福利价值，不能因为二者都以货币表示就互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An earnings-based approach measures future earnings or output lost through death. A willingness-to-pay approach asks what someone would surrender to reduce ex ante fatality risk. One concerns production or income consequences and the other welfare from risk changes; monetary units do not make them interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小反例：一位已退休者没有未来市场工资，但愿付10元使本年死亡风险降低 $1/100000$。把未来工资记为零，并不能推出风险降低价值为零；按[[统计生命价值]]的局部换算，这组支付意愿对应100万元的风险单位价值。未领取工资的家务或照护也不等于没有产出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A retired person may have no future market wages yet pay 10 to lower this year's fatality risk by $1/100000$. Zero future wages do not imply zero risk-reduction value; the local [[统计生命价值|VSL]] ratio is one million. Unpaid household work or care likewise need not produce zero output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用时先说明是在估计资源产出损失，还是完整风险支付意愿。后者可能已经体现个人关心的收入与消费后果；再把同一部分未来工资机械叠加，会重复计价。若另有未包含的第三方产出或财政影响，应明确对象和账户后再分析，而不是把所有工资损失都无条件加到VSL上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify whether the target is lost production or comprehensive willingness to pay for risk reduction. The latter may already reflect income and consumption consequences of concern to the person; mechanically adding the same future earnings would double count. Any excluded third-party production or fiscal effects require a separately defined scope and account, not an unconditional addition to VSL.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
