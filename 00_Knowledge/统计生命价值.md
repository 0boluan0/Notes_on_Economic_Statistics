# 统计生命价值是微小死亡风险降低的边际货币价值

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The value of a statistical life is the marginal monetary value of a small reduction in fatality risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

统计生命价值（VSL）把人们对微小死亡概率降低的支付意愿换算成每单位风险变化的货币价值。它评价的是事前的风险变化，不是为某个身份已知的人的生命标价，也不代表事后确定救活一人的市场交易价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The value of a statistical life (VSL) expresses willingness to pay for a small reduction in fatality probability per unit of risk change. It values an ex ante risk change, not an identified person's life or a market transaction for a certain rescue after the event.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定个人收入、基准死亡概率和风险所属时期，令 $W(s)$ 为降低死亡概率 $s$ 的总支付意愿，$W(0)=0$。可微时，$VSL=W'(0)$；小变化用 $W(\Delta p)/\Delta p$ 作局部近似。概率用0到1的比例，不用未经换算的百分数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix income, baseline fatality probability and the risk horizon. Let $W(s)$ be total willingness to pay to reduce fatality probability by $s$, with $W(0)=0$. When differentiable, $VSL=W'(0)$; for a small change, $W(\Delta p)/\Delta p$ is a local approximation. Probabilities use the zero-to-one scale, not unconverted percentage figures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设一个人愿为本年死亡概率降低 $1/100000$ 支付10元，则局部比值为 $10/(1/100000)=1000000$ 元。这个数是风险单位换算，不意味着该人愿意或能够支付100万元消除全部死亡风险。项目中多人风险变化的加总由[[死亡风险降低收益]]处理；工资证据见[[工资风险补偿法]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If someone would pay 10 for a reduction of $1/100000$ in this year's fatality probability, the local ratio is 1,000,000. This rescales risk units; it does not imply willingness or ability to pay that amount to eliminate all fatality risk. [[死亡风险降低收益|Mortality-risk benefit aggregation]] handles populations, while [[工资风险补偿法|wage–risk trade-offs]] provide one evidence source.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
