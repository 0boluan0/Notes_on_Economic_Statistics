# 网络效应是其他参与者的使用规模影响用户所获价值的效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Network effects arise when other participants' usage affects the value a user receives.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

网络效应把用户价值与其他参与者的使用联系起来，而不只是说企业卖得更多。正向网络效应下，更多相关用户加入会提高现有用户的价值：通信服务中可联络的人增多，是直接效应；更多使用者吸引互补产品和服务，再提高系统价值，是间接效应。实际分析要说明哪一组用户影响哪一组，效应也可能为负。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Network effects link user value to others' usage, not simply to more sales by the firm. Under positive effects, relevant additional users increase existing users' value: more reachable contacts provide a direct effect; adoption attracting complementary products and services provides an indirect effect. Identify which group affects which; effects can also be negative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，两位原本无法在某通信服务上联系的朋友都加入后，彼此得到新的联系机会。即使他们同时使用另一个通信服务，这个价值变化仍可存在。因此，不能把“用户无法同时使用竞争平台”（不能多归属）当作网络效应的必要条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, two friends who previously could not communicate through a service gain that opportunity when both join. The benefit can exist even if both also use a rival service. Inability to use competing platforms simultaneously—an absence of multi-homing—is therefore not necessary for a network effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

网络效应与[[规模经济]]要分开：前者直接讨论用户价值如何依赖他人采用，后者讨论产出规模如何影响平均成本。网络效应也不自动意味着一家企业长期垄断或赚取高利润；兼容性、多归属、收费方式及竞争仍影响价值由谁获取，进一步判断见[[护城河证据]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separate network effects from [[规模经济|economies of scale]]: the former concern user value as others adopt, while the latter concern average costs as output changes. Network effects do not automatically imply durable monopoly or high profits. Compatibility, multi-homing, pricing, and competition affect who captures value; see [[护城河证据|assessing durable-advantage evidence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Katz–Shapiro（1994），Systems Competition and Network Effects，PDF 第 2–4 页／印刷第 94–96 页](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/shapiro/systems.pdf#page=2)：已重新读取并目视第 94、96 页，核对用户采用、通信直接效应与互补品间接效应；不把其特定模型中的锁定假设提升成定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Katz–Shapiro (1994), PDF pp. 2–4 / printed pp. 94–96, reread with pp. 94 and 96 visually checked: supports adoption, direct communication effects, and indirect complementary-product effects. Model-specific lock-in is not made definitional.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [OECD Digital Economy Outlook 2020，Evolving business models，Online platforms share certain economic characteristics](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-digital-economy-outlook-2020_bb167041-en/full-report/component-13.html)：已重开直接效应的正负及不同用户侧的说明；只引用这些范围，不采用“所有平台都有正间接效应”的过宽概括。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- OECD (2020), the specified section, reopened: supports positive/negative direct effects and distinctions between user groups. Its broad claim about every platform having positive indirect effects is not adopted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [OECD（2021），Data Portability, Interoperability and Competition，About](https://www.oecd.org/en/events/2021/06/data-portability-interoperability-and-competition.html)：已重开互操作性可便利多归属并保留网络效应的段落；朋友例为自拟反例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- OECD (2021), “About,” reopened: explains how interoperability can facilitate multi-homing while preserving network effects. The friends example is a constructed counterexample.
