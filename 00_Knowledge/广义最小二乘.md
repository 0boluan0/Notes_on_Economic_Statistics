# Generalized least squares
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*广义最小二乘（GLS）*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Generalized least squares fits a linear mean using the **whole pattern of error variation**. [[加权最小二乘|Inverse-variance WLS]] accounts for some observations being noisier than others. GLS also accounts for errors moving together: two readings can share the same disturbance, so treating them as two separate pieces of information can waste precision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

广义最小二乘利用**误差波动的完整结构**来拟合线性均值。[[加权最小二乘|逆方差 WLS]]考虑不同观测的噪声大小不同；GLS 还考虑误差一起变化的情况：两次读数可能共享同一个扰动，将它们当成两份彼此无关的信息，可能损失估计精度。

> [!example] A second reading that adds only noise · 第二次读数只增加噪声
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $Y_1=\mu+v$ and $Y_2=\mu+v+e$, where $\mu$ is the unknown common mean. The errors $v,e$ have mean zero, variances $1,3$, and zero covariance. Then $Y_1$ has variance $1$, $Y_2$ has variance $4$, and their covariance is $1$. Their difference $Y_2-Y_1=e$ contains no $\mu$ at all.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $Y_1=\mu+v$、$Y_2=\mu+v+e$，其中 $\mu$ 是未知共同均值。误差 $v,e$ 均值为零，方差分别为 $1,3$，协方差为零。那么 $Y_1$ 的方差是 $1$，$Y_2$ 的方差是 $4$，二者协方差为 $1$。两者之差 $Y_2-Y_1=e$ 根本不含 $\mu$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> GLS uses $\hat\mu=Y_1$, with variance $1$. The ordinary average is $\mu+v+e/2$, with variance $1+3/4=7/4$. Weights based only on the two marginal variances would instead give $(4Y_1+Y_2)/5=\mu+v+e/5$, with variance $1+3/25$. All three rules are unbiased, but the last two add unnecessary noise. Readings $10,14$ give GLS $10$, ordinary averaging $12$, and diagonal-only weighting $10.8$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> GLS 使用 $\hat\mu=Y_1$，方差为 $1$。普通平均数是 $\mu+v+e/2$，方差为 $1+3/4=7/4$。如果只按两个边际方差加权，就会得到 $(4Y_1+Y_2)/5=\mu+v+e/5$，方差为 $1+3/25$。三种规则都无偏，但后两种加入了不必要的噪声。读数为 $10,14$ 时，GLS 给出 $10$，普通平均数为 $12$，仅按对角方差加权则为 $10.8$。

## Specify the mean and the covariance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先说明均值与协方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $y=X\beta+u$ over the real numbers. Here $y$ contains $n$ responses, $X$ is the $n\times k$ design matrix, $\beta\in\mathbb R^k$ is the unknown coefficient vector, and $u$ is the error vector. Fix $X$, or condition on its realised value, and assume its columns are [[线性无关|linearly independent]]. The [[条件期望|conditional-mean]] condition $E(u\mid X)=0$ says that the errors average to zero at this design. With finite conditional second moments, suppose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在实数范围写 $y=X\beta+u$。这里 $y$ 包含 $n$ 个响应，$X$ 为 $n\times k$ 设计矩阵，$\beta\in\mathbb R^k$ 为未知系数向量，$u$ 为误差向量。固定 $X$，或给定其实现值，并假设各列[[线性无关]]。[[条件期望|条件均值]]条件 $E(u\mid X)=0$ 表示在这个设计下误差平均为零。再假设条件二阶矩有限，且

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2\Omega,\qquad 0<\sigma^2<\infty.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] records error variances on its diagonal and cross-covariances elsewhere. Here $\Omega$ is a known symmetric [[正定矩阵|positive definite]] $n\times n$ shape matrix: every nonzero error direction has positive variance. The scale $\sigma^2$ may be unknown. In the opening example, $X=(1,1)^T$, $\sigma^2=1$, and $\Omega=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&4\end{pmatrix}$. Its inverse is $\frac13\begin{pmatrix}4&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[协方差矩阵]]的对角线记录误差方差，其他位置记录交叉协方差。这里 $\Omega$ 是已知、对称、[[正定矩阵|正定]]的 $n\times n$ 形状矩阵：任何非零误差方向都有正方差。尺度 $\sigma^2$ 可以未知。开头例子中，$X=(1,1)^T$、$\sigma^2=1$，且 $\Omega=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&4\end{pmatrix}$，其逆为 $\frac13\begin{pmatrix}4&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a candidate coefficient vector $b$, GLS measures the residual vector $y-Xb$ using $\Omega^{-1}$, rather than summing its coordinates' squares without adjustment:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于候选系数向量 $b$，GLS 用 $\Omega^{-1}$ 衡量残差向量 $y-Xb$，而不是直接把各坐标平方相加：

$$
\hat\beta_G
=\arg\min_b (y-Xb)^T\Omega^{-1}(y-Xb)
=(X^T\Omega^{-1}X)^{-1}X^T\Omega^{-1}y.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inverse exists because $X$ has full column rank and $\Omega$ is positive definite. A common positive multiplier of $\Omega$ cancels from the estimator. Substituting the example's matrices gives $\hat\mu=Y_1$, as claimed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $X$ 满列秩且 $\Omega$ 正定，公式中的逆存在。将 $\Omega$ 乘以同一个正常数，不改变估计量。代入例子中的矩阵，就得到前面的 $\hat\mu=Y_1$。

## Whitening explains the formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用白化理解公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose an invertible matrix $C$ satisfying $C\Omega C^T=I_n$, where $I_n$ is the identity matrix. This is called **whitening**: rotate or combine the observations and rescale their noise directions so that the transformed covariance is proportional to the identity. Applying the same transformation to the entire model gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择可逆矩阵 $C$，使 $C\Omega C^T=I_n$，其中 $I_n$ 是单位矩阵。这称为**白化**：旋转或组合观测，再缩放噪声方向，让变换后的协方差与单位矩阵成比例。对整个模型施加同一个变换，得到

$$
Cy=CX\beta+Cu,\qquad
E(Cu\mid X)=0,\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(Cu\mid X)=\sigma^2I_n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By the [[协方差仿射变换|covariance transformation rule]], the last equality follows from $C\Omega C^T=I_n$. OLS on $Cy$ and $CX$ is exactly GLS. In the example, choose $C=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-1/\sqrt3&1/\sqrt3\end{pmatrix}$. The transformed observations are $Y_1$ and $(Y_2-Y_1)/\sqrt3$, while the transformed design is $(1,0)^T$. The second transformed response therefore does not enter the least-squares normal equation for the mean coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按照[[协方差仿射变换|协方差变换规则]]，最后一个等式直接来自 $C\Omega C^T=I_n$。对 $Cy$ 和 $CX$ 做 OLS，恰好就是 GLS。例子中可取 $C=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-1/\sqrt3&1/\sqrt3\end{pmatrix}$；变换后的观测为 $Y_1$、$(Y_2-Y_1)/\sqrt3$，设计向量则为 $(1,0)^T$，所以第二个变换后响应不进入均值系数的最小二乘正规方程。

> [!note] What whitening guarantees · 白化保证什么
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Equal variances and zero cross-covariances do not generally imply independence. If the original errors are [[联合Gaussian|jointly Gaussian]] conditional on $X$, their whitened coordinates are independent Gaussian errors. Without that extra distributional assumption, the second-moment statement remains valid, but an exact Gaussian $t$ or $F$ calibration does not follow from whitening alone.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 方差相等、交叉协方差为零，一般不意味着独立。如果原误差在给定 $X$ 后[[联合Gaussian|联合高斯]]，白化后的坐标才是独立高斯误差。没有这一额外分布假设时，二阶矩结论仍成立，但不能只凭白化就得到高斯模型下精确的 $t$ 或 $F$ 校准。

## The efficiency claim and its limits
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*效率结论与边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the stated fixed-design conditions, the [[经典 Gauss–Markov 定理|Gauss–Markov comparison]] applied after whitening gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在上述固定设计条件下，对白化后的模型使用[[经典 Gauss–Markov 定理|Gauss–Markov 比较]]，得到

$$
E(\hat\beta_G\mid X)=\beta,\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(\hat\beta_G\mid X)=\sigma^2(X^T\Omega^{-1}X)^{-1}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
GLS is BLUE: among rules $Ay$ that are linear in the outcomes, fixed given $X$, and unbiased for every $\beta$, no rule has smaller variance for any fixed coefficient combination. This allows equality with OLS; when $\Omega$ is proportional to $I_n$, they are identical, and this is a sufficient rather than necessary condition for equality. The theorem needs finite second moments, not normality, and it does not promise finite-sample superiority over every biased or nonlinear estimator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

GLS 是 BLUE：在形如 $Ay$、对结果线性、给定 $X$ 后固定且对所有 $\beta$ 无偏的规则中，任何固定系数组合的方差都不可能更小。这个比较允许与 OLS 相等；当 $\Omega$ 与 $I_n$ 成比例时二者相同，但这只是相同的充分条件，并非必要条件。定理需要有限二阶矩，不需要正态性，也不保证有限样本下胜过所有有偏或非线性估计量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diagonal $\Omega$ recovers inverse-variance WLS. Estimated $\Omega$ leads to [[可行GLS|feasible GLS]], whose guarantees need an additional argument. A singular covariance does not fit the inverse-based formula above. Finally, whitening changes the fitting rule but need not change its numerical answer, and it cannot by itself repair a wrong [[条件均值与线性投影|conditional-mean specification]] or establish a causal interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\Omega$ 为对角矩阵时，就回到逆方差 WLS。若需要估计 $\Omega$，就进入[[可行GLS]]，其保证需要额外论证。奇异协方差不适用上述基于普通逆的公式。最后，白化改变拟合规则，但不一定改变最终数值；它也不能自行修复错误的[[条件均值与线性投影|条件均值设定]]或确立因果解释。
