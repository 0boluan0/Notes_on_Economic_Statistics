# 误差协方差矩阵已知到比例时，广义最小二乘通过白化模型获得相应的有效线性估计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When the error covariance matrix is known up to scale, generalized least squares whitens the model to obtain the corresponding efficient linear estimator*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 从球形距离改成协方差距离
> 若线性模型满足 $E(u\mid X)=0$ 且
> $$\operatorname{Var}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2\Omega,$$
> 其中正定 $\Omega$ 已知，则
> $$
> \hat\beta_{GLS}=(X'\Omega^{-1}X)^{-1}X'\Omega^{-1}Y.
> $$
> GLS 最小化 $(Y-X\beta)'\Omega^{-1}(Y-X\beta)$，让高噪声方向和重复信息较少支配拟合。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> GLS replaces ordinary Euclidean residual distance with the distance induced by $\Omega^{-1}$. Under a correct known covariance shape, it discounts noisy directions and redundant correlated information.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若取矩阵 $C$ 使 $C\Omega C'=I$，则变换后的模型
$$CY=CX\beta+Cu$$
具有球形误差，对它做 OLS 就得到 GLS。这种“白化”解释比死记公式更重要：GLS 同时利用对角线上的异方差和非对角线上的相关。[[加权最小二乘]]只是 $\Omega$ 为对角矩阵时的特例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose $C$ so that $C\Omega C'=I$; OLS on the whitened model yields GLS. This shows that GLS handles both unequal diagonal variances and off-diagonal correlation, while WLS is the diagonal-covariance special case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

已知正确 $\Omega$ 时，GLS 在相应条件下是 BLUE；但它没有放松 $E(u\mid X)=0$。若均值方程内生、变量遗漏或目标错误，白化不能恢复因果资格。并且 GLS 会改变系数估计；它不是像 HC/HAC 那样只替换标准误。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With correct known $\Omega$, GLS is BLUE under the corresponding conditions, but it does not relax exogeneity. Whitening cannot repair omitted variables or endogeneity, and unlike HC/HAC it changes coefficient estimates as well as their covariance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现实困难是无结构的 $n\times n$ 协方差矩阵含太多参数，不能只凭一份样本自由估计。必须施加如“对角方差函数”“AR(1) 误差”或“簇内共同结构”等限制；一旦 $\Omega$ 需要估计，方法就变成[[可行GLS]]，其质量取决于这些限制是否可信。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An unrestricted $n\times n$ covariance matrix has too many parameters to estimate from one sample. GLS therefore needs substantive restrictions such as a diagonal variance function or AR(1) errors. Once $\Omega$ is estimated, the method becomes feasible GLS and inherits covariance-model risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> WLS 为什么是 GLS 的特例？
>
> **答案：** WLS 假定不同观测不相关，所以 $\Omega$ 只有不同的对角元；GLS 允许非对角元也非零，并用完整 $\Omega^{-1}$ 加权。
