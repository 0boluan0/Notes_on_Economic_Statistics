# 扇形线是由同一起点依次连向后续转折点形成的一组趋势线

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fan lines are successive trend lines drawn from a common origin through later turning points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

扇形线是保留一个重要行情起点、依次连接后续转折点的一组[[趋势线]]。本课上升示意中，较陡的第一条低点连线被跌破后，从原起点连到新低点，再出现更缓的线；下降示意则从原高点依次连接后来的高点。斜率逐次变化，使多条线展开成扇形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fan lines retain a major starting point while drawing successive [[趋势线|trend lines]] through later turns. In the course's rising example, a steep line through a low is breached and a flatter line joins the same origin to a later low. The falling example joins a common high to later highs. The changing slopes spread into a fan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图中的L1、L2、L3展示的是随新价格出现而更新的构造次序。L2、L3所需的转折点未确认之前，这些线不属于当时已知信息；不能把完成后的整把扇形当成起点时已经可用的工具。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
L1, L2 and L3 in the figure show an updating sequence as new prices arrive. L2 and L3 are not available before their turning points can be identified. The completed fan cannot be treated as if all its lines existed at the origin.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共同起点与实际转折点连线是本卡的构造职责；不把“第三条线破位必然反转”作为保证。它也不同于按固定比例画线的[[速度线]]或[[甘氏线]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The defining construction is a common origin joined to actual turning points; a third-line breach does not guarantee reversal. This differs from the fixed-ratio constructions of [[速度线|speed lines]] and [[甘氏线|Gann lines]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
