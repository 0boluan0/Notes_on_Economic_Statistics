# 股利低于股权自由现金流时两种估值能否一致取决于留存现金的完整处理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When dividends fall below FCFE, reconciliation depends on fully accounting for retained cash.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[股利贴现模型]]追踪预计支付给持股人的现金；[[股权自由现金流|FCFE]]衡量既定经营和债务安排后可供股权分配的现金。看到 FCFE 大于股利，不能直接宣布前一个模型漏估了价值：先看留下的钱会怎样使用、何时最终归股东，以及相关风险和融资政策是否已纳入两边。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[股利贴现模型|DDM]] follows expected shareholder payments; [[股权自由现金流|FCFE]] measures available cash after operating and debt needs. A gap between them is not itself evidence of undervaluation. Trace retained cash, eventual distributions, risk, and financing assumptions in both models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个只有两期、没有其他现金流的算例：第一年末可分配 100。若全数发出，在 10% 必要回报下今天值 $100/1.10$；若只发 60，余下 40 以匹配风险下的 10% 回报投资，第二年末将 44 全数发出，则今天仍值 $60/1.10+44/(1.10)^2=100/1.10$。少发的 40 没有消失，只是支付被推迟且得到恰当补偿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a two-period example, distributing 100 at year 1 is worth $100/1.10$. Alternatively, distribute 60 then and invest 40 at a matching 10% return, paying 44 at year 2. The present value is unchanged: $60/1.10+44/(1.10)^2=100/1.10$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若留存款投入负净现值项目，或最终分配被遗漏，两套估值就不会这样相等。反之，如果已从同一份完整预测得到所有股东现金流，又把这些留存资产单独加价，也会重复计算。比较模型前应先对齐留存、回购、未来股权注资和终值，而不是按哪个结果更高来选模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Value differs if retained cash funds negative-NPV uses or eventual distributions are omitted. Adding retained assets separately after already including all their shareholder cash flows also double counts them. Align retention, repurchases, future equity funding, and terminal value before comparing outputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
