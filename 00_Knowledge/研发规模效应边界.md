# 研发资源水平提高长期增长率的强规模效应依赖知识生产函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The strong scale effect whereby more research resources raise long-run growth depends on the knowledge-production function.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

研发增长模型中的**强规模效应**，指研究相关资源的水平增加，可以永久提高长期比例增长率。它不是“更多资源使产出总量更大”这一水平比较，也不是所有内生增长模型共有的结论；必须找出模型中的规模变量和知识积累方程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strong scale effect in an R&D growth model means that a higher level of research-related resources permanently raises the long-run proportional growth rate. This differs from higher total output and is not a universal implication of endogenous growth. Identify the model's scale variable and knowledge-accumulation equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 [[Romer平衡增长率|Romer（1990）的正增长内部解]] 中，规模变量是固定的**总人力资本 $H$**。$\delta>0$ 是研发效率；令 $\gamma=1-\alpha-\beta\in(0,1)$、$\Lambda=\alpha/[\gamma(1-\gamma)]$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[Romer平衡增长率|positive interior solution of Romer (1990)]], the scale variable is fixed total human capital $H$. Let $\delta$ be research productivity, $\gamma=1-\alpha-\beta\in(0,1)$ and $\Lambda=\alpha/[\gamma(1-\gamma)]$. Then:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
g=\frac{\delta H-\Lambda\rho}{1+\Lambda\sigma},\qquad
\frac{\partial g}{\partial H}=\frac\delta{1+\Lambda\sigma}>0.
$$

这里 $\rho>0$ 为时间贴现率，$\sigma>0$ 为效用曲率。普通劳动力或人口 $L$ 并未出现在这个增长式中：在原文特定函数形式下，$L$ 提高研究专利收益，也提高研究者在生产部门的机会成本，两项恰好抵消。Romer 同时指出，这个抵消依赖函数形式，不能据此宣布人口在其他模型或现实中完全无关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\rho$ is time preference and $\sigma$ utility curvature. Ordinary labor or population $L$ does not appear in this growth equation: under the original functional forms, its effect on patent returns exactly offsets its effect on researchers' production opportunities. Romer notes that the cancellation is specification-dependent, so it does not establish that population is irrelevant elsewhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Jones（1995）检验的疑问是：历史上研究资源显著增加，为什么增长率没有相应地不断提高？这是对强规模预测的挑战，不能翻译成“研发没有效果”。他另建一套模型，把知识对后续研发的作用从线性改为较弱的幂次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Jones (1995) asks why substantial historical increases in research resources were not accompanied by correspondingly rising growth rates. This challenges a strong scale prediction; it does not establish that research has no effect. His alternative model weakens the proportional effect of existing knowledge on research productivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**以下切换到 Jones 的版本**：$L_A$ 是研究劳动，$\delta_J>0$ 是研发效率，$\phi<1$ 控制知识外溢，$0<\lambda\le1$ 允许重复研究降低聚合效率。它们不是前面 Romer 的人力配置参数。聚合研发式为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The following equations switch to Jones's specification. Research labor is $L_A$, research productivity is $\delta_J>0$, knowledge spillovers are governed by $\phi<1$, and $0<\lambda\le1$ allows duplication to reduce aggregate research efficiency. These are not parameters of the preceding Romer human-capital allocation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\dot A=\delta_J A^\phi L_A^\lambda,\qquad
g_A=\frac{\delta_J L_A^\lambda}{A^{1-\phi}}.
$$

若在该模型的平衡增长中，研究劳动随劳动力以常数 $n>0$ 增长，而 $g_A$ 恒定，对后一式取对数再微分：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a balanced path in this model, suppose research labor grows with the labor force at constant rate $n>0$ and $g_A$ is constant. Log differentiation of the second relation gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$0=\lambda n-(1-\phi)g_A,\qquad
g_A=\frac{\lambda n}{1-\phi}.$$

研发仍由经济内部的投入产生，但长期增长率由人口增长与技术参数决定；提高研究资源的**水平**不再自动永久提高增长率，仍可能改变水平或转轨。固定人口时，这个设定不足以维持正的长期比例知识增长率。这个“半内生”例子只界定原线性设定的边界，不能把它的增长式代回 Romer 固定 $H$ 的工资与专利方程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Research still arises from resources allocated within the economy, but long-run growth now depends on population growth and technology parameters. A higher level of research resources no longer automatically raises the permanent growth rate, though it may change levels or transitions. With fixed population, this specification cannot sustain positive long-run proportional knowledge growth. Its formula must not be inserted into Romer's fixed-$H$ wage and patent equations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Romer（1990），[Endogenous Technological Change](https://web.stanford.edu/~klenow/Romer_1990.pdf#page=24)，印刷S93–S96（PDF第24–27页）：$H$ 的规模作用、$L$ 与单位机器成本的抵消及其函数形式边界；式(13)见印刷S92。
- Jones（1995），[R&D-Based Models of Economic Growth，作者托管原文](https://web.stanford.edu/~chadj/JonesJPE95.pdf#page=3)，印刷pp.760–767（PDF第3–10页）：强规模预测与历史比较、式(6)–(8)、半内生版本。p.762脚注1明确 Romer（1990）的研发投入是人力资本；本卡用 $\delta_J$ 区分作者原文的 $\delta$。
- 两个版本分别检查，Jones增长式从其聚合式独立求导；历史证据仅用于识别该论文的模型批评，不作为当前增长数据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Romer supplies the original scale variable and cancellation boundary. Jones supplies the historical challenge and alternative research law; his footnote explicitly distinguishes Romer's human-capital input. Each model's equations were checked separately, and the historical evidence is not presented as current data.
