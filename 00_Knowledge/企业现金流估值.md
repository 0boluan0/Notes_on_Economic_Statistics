# 企业现金流估值按匹配的资本成本折现经营自由现金流以估计经营资产价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
FCFF valuation discounts operating free cash flows at matching costs of capital to estimate operating asset value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把未来经营翻译为[[企业自由现金流|FCFF]]，再用[[加权平均资本成本|WACC]]折现，得到这组经营资产对全部资本提供者的价值。此处现金流不含非经营资产的收入，因此其现值也不含那些资产；普通股价值还需经过[[经营价值转股权价值|权利与资产调整]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Forecast [[企业自由现金流|FCFF]] and discount it at a matching [[加权平均资本成本|WACC]] to value the operating assets for all capital providers. Non-operating assets are outside this cash-flow stream. Reaching common equity requires [[经营价值转股权价值|asset and claim adjustments]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设显性期为 $1,\ldots,n$，模型为各年选定一致的确定性资本成本路径 $w_1,\ldots,w_n$；$TV_n$ 是依据今天信息和同一预测形成的第 $n$ 年末[[估值终值]]估计。则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For explicit years $1,\ldots,n$, let the model specify a consistent deterministic required-return path $w_1,\ldots,w_n$. Let $TV_n$ estimate year-$n$ [[估值终值|terminal value]] using today's information and the same forecast:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$V_{op,0}=\sum_{t=1}^{n}\frac{\mathbb E_0[FCFF_t]}{\prod_{s=1}^{t}(1+w_s)}+\frac{TV_n}{\prod_{s=1}^{n}(1+w_s)}.$$

这是一套明确假设下的现金流估值表示，不是说任何风险都能由任意挑选的一串平均利率处理。现金流、税盾、杠杆政策与回报要求要来自同一模型；只有各期 $w_s=w$ 时，分母才简化为 $(1+w)^t$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a conditional valuation representation, not permission to handle arbitrary risk with arbitrary average rates. Cash flows, tax shields, leverage policy, and required returns must be consistent. The denominator reduces to $(1+w)^t$ only when all period rates equal $w$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如前两年 FCFF 为 40、50，从第三年起第一笔稳定期现金为 60，此后按 3% 增长。若稳定期 WACC 为 8%、前两年为 10%，先算 $TV_2=60/(0.08-0.03)=1200$，再算 $V_{op,0}=40/1.10+(50+1200)/(1.10)^2\approx1069.42$。还不能直接把 1069.42 除以股数：它属于经营业务的全部资本，而不只是普通股。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose years 1 and 2 yield FCFF of 40 and 50, followed by 60 in year 3 growing at 3%. With stable WACC of 8% and a 10% rate for the first two years, $TV_2=1200$ and operating value is approximately 1069.42. That amount is not yet common equity and cannot simply be divided by ordinary shares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从报表构造现金流时，[[自由现金流的净经营资产表示|NOPAT 减净经营资产增加]]只有在现金再投资及经营现金范围已对齐时才能代用。再扣利息会改变分子的索取权；再加一遍同一经营资产的账面价值则重复计价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[自由现金流的净经营资产表示|NOPAT-minus-NOA-change representation]] is usable only after matching cash reinvestment and operating cash scope. Deducting interest changes the claim being valued, while adding the same operating assets' book value would count them again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
