# Harsanyi 转换在共同先验下让 Nature 事前抽取收益状态与类型，并只向每名玩家透露其自身类型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Under a common prior, the Harsanyi transformation lets Nature draw the payoff state and type profile ex ante and reveal each player only their own type*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**Harsanyi 转换**把具有共同先验的贝叶斯博弈表示为一场由 Nature 开场的扩展式博弈：Nature 先按 $\pi\in\Delta(\Theta\times T)$ 抽取 $(\theta,t)$，随后玩家 $i$ 只观察 $t_i$，再同时选择行动。玩家在行动时使用的事中信念就是

$$
p_i(\theta,t_{-i}\mid t_i)=\pi(\theta,t_{-i}\mid t_i).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **Harsanyi transformation** represents a common-prior Bayesian game as an extensive-form game opened by Nature: Nature draws $(\theta,t)$ according to $\pi\in\Delta(\Theta\times T)$, each player $i$ observes only $t_i$, and the players then choose actions. The interim belief used by a player is the conditional distribution generated by that draw.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 表示条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Condition for the representation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一份共享的 Nature 概率必须同时生成所有玩家的类型条件信念。若玩家的 interim beliefs 彼此不能由同一 $\pi$ 条件化得到，就不能选取某一人的主观 prior 充当公共 Nature lottery；此时应直接保留各玩家自己的信念。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single Nature lottery must generate every player's type-contingent beliefs. If those interim beliefs cannot be obtained by conditioning one common $\pi$, one player's subjective prior cannot be relabeled as the shared lottery; the model must retain the players' separate beliefs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这幅树是[[Harsanyi类型空间|类型空间]]在共同先验下的事前表示，不要求现实中真的存在一个先于信息获得的抽签时刻。在发牌等场景中它可以有字面时间含义；在既有成本或既有认知分歧中，它只是一种建模表示。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tree is an ex-ante representation of a [[Harsanyi类型空间|type space]] under a common prior and need not describe a literal historical lottery. It may have a direct temporal interpretation in settings such as card dealing, while in other settings it is only a modeling representation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
