# 正外部性补贴是为活动者未能获得的外部收益而提供的矫正支付
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A corrective subsidy for a positive externality rewards benefits the actor creates for others but does not capture.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正外部性补贴把一部分外部收益转成活动者自己可获得的回报，使其愿意增加原本激励不足的活动。依据是**新增活动给他人带来的、尚未进入私人决策的边际收益**，不是活动名称听起来有益就应获得补贴。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A corrective subsidy makes an otherwise uncaptured external benefit part of the actor's private return, encouraging an activity with insufficient private incentives. Its basis is the additional benefit to others omitted from the private decision, not simply a socially appealing label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设活动量为 $q$、私人边际收益为 $MPB(q)$、私人边际资源成本为 $MC(q)$、边际外部收益为 $MEB(q)$。在[[外部性数量偏离|没有其他扭曲、货币口径一致且最优在内点]]的基准中，社会条件与恒定单位补贴条件分别为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let activity be $q$, private marginal benefit $MPB(q)$, private marginal resource cost $MC(q)$ and marginal external benefit $MEB(q)$. Under the [[外部性数量偏离|undistorted, consistently valued interior benchmark]], the social condition and the private condition with a constant unit subsidy are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$MPB(q^*)+MEB(q^*)=MC(q^*),$$
$$MPB(q)+s=MC(q),\qquad s^*=MEB(q^*).$$

还要使补贴后的私人目标有相应的最优解，例如净私人收益严格凹。若 $MEB$ 随 $q$ 变，恒定 $s^*$ 是在最优点匹配，而不是把整条私人收益曲线变成社会收益曲线。补贴支持的实际行为、可核实的活动量与支付单位也必须对应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The subsidised private objective must have the intended optimum, for example through strict concavity of net private benefit. With variable $MEB$, constant $s^*$ matches at the optimum rather than converting the entire private-benefit curve into the social curve. The supported behaviour, measurable activity and payment unit must also correspond.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小例：$MPB=10-q,MC=4,MEB=2$。私人选择 $q=6$；社会条件给 $q^*=8$；每单位补贴 2 使私人也选 8。也可以把同一式子写成 $MPB=MC-MEB$，把他人得到的收益记在净社会成本一侧。**同一个外部收益只能计一次**，不能既在左边加 2 又在右边减 2；后一写法也不表示生产实际少用了两单位资源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $MPB=10-q$, $MC=4$ and $MEB=2$, private activity is six, efficient activity eight, and a subsidy of two induces eight. Equivalently, write $MPB=MC-MEB$, crediting the external benefit on the net-social-cost side. Count that benefit once: adding two on the left and subtracting two on the right duplicates it. The latter convention does not imply that production physically uses fewer resources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

研发知识被其他企业利用，可以形成这种收益，但不是所有研发投入都产生相同外溢。[[基础研究]]说明研究类型的目的标准，并不替我们估计 $MEB$。把补贴交给谁、是否产生额外活动、如何筹款仍要另查；它不是一项不计成本的普遍研发补助承诺。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowledge used by other firms can create such spillovers, but not all R&D generates the same external benefit. [[基础研究|Basic research]] defines a research category by its purpose rather than estimating $MEB$. Recipient choice, additional activity and financing still require assessment; this is not an unconditional or costless commitment to subsidise research.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
