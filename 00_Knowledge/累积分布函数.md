# Cumulative distribution function (CDF)
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*累积分布函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **cumulative distribution function** answers a question at every numerical threshold: **what is the probability that the result is at most this large?** For example, it can tell us the chance that a die roll is at most $3$, or that a waiting time is at most $10$ minutes. Moving the threshold lets us describe where the probability lies across the whole range of possible values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**累积分布函数**在每一个数值门槛上回答同一个问题：**结果不超过这个门槛的概率是多少？** 例如，骰子点数不超过 $3$ 的概率是多少，或者等待时间不超过 $10$ 分钟的概率是多少。把门槛从小到大移动，就能描述概率在整个取值范围内怎样分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X$ be a [[随机变量|random variable]], a rule that records an uncertain outcome as a number. A lowercase $x$ is a fixed threshold that we choose; it need not be a value that $X$ can actually take. The notation $P(X\le x)$ means the probability of the event “the recorded number is at most $x$”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $X$ 表示一个[[随机变量]]，也就是把尚不确定的结果记成数字的规则。小写的 $x$ 是我们选定的门槛，不一定是 $X$ 实际能取到的值。$P(X\le x)$ 表示“记录下来的数字不超过 $x$”这件事发生的概率。

> [!abstract] Accumulating probability up to a threshold · 累积到门槛为止
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The CDF of $X$ is the function $F_X$ defined by
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $X$ 的累积分布函数记为 $F_X$，定义为
>
> $$F_X(x)=P(X\le x).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The subscript $X$ identifies the variable. The equality sign in $\le$ matters: probability at the threshold itself is included.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 下标 $X$ 说明这是哪个变量的分布函数。$\le$ 中的等号很重要：恰好等于门槛的概率也算在内。

> [!example] Reading a fair die's CDF · 读一枚公平骰子的 CDF
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X$ be the result of a fair six-sided die. Then $F_X(3)=3/6$, since $1,2,3$ all qualify. Also $F_X(3.5)=3/6$: raising the threshold from $3$ to $3.5$ adds no possible result. At $4$, the value becomes $F_X(4)=4/6$. Below $1$ the CDF is $0$, and from $6$ onward it is $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $X$ 为一枚公平六面骰子的点数。$F_X(3)=3/6$，因为 $1,2,3$ 都符合要求。$F_X(3.5)$ 仍然是 $3/6$：把门槛从 $3$ 提到 $3.5$，没有多包含任何可能的点数。到 $4$ 时，$F_X(4)=4/6$。门槛低于 $1$ 时 CDF 为 $0$，达到 $6$ 及以上时为 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Recovering probabilities from the CDF
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从累积概率读回区间和单点概率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $a<b$ are two thresholds. $F_X(b)$ counts everything at or below $b$; subtracting $F_X(a)$ removes everything at or below $a$. What remains is exactly the event $a<X\le b$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<b$ 是两个门槛。$F_X(b)$ 包含所有不超过 $b$ 的结果，再减去 $F_X(a)$，就去掉了所有不超过 $a$ 的结果。剩下的恰好是 $a<X\le b$：

$$P(a<X\le b)=F_X(b)-F_X(a).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the die, $P(2<X\le5)=5/6-2/6=3/6$, corresponding to $3,4,5$. Notice that the lower endpoint is excluded. These interval probabilities, over all endpoints, uniquely determine the probability distribution of a real-valued variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

骰子的例子中，$P(2<X\le5)=5/6-2/6=3/6$，对应 $3,4,5$ 三个点数。注意下端点 $2$ 被排除了。知道所有端点下的这些区间概率，就唯一确定了这个实值变量的概率分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **jump** in the CDF tells us that one particular value has positive probability. Write $F_X(x-)$ for the value approached by thresholds just below $x$; it equals $P(X<x)$. Subtracting it from $F_X(x)$ leaves only the probability at $x$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CDF 的一次**跳跃**说明某个具体取值有正概率。用 $F_X(x-)$ 表示门槛从 $x$ 的左侧不断靠近时，CDF 接近的值；它等于 $P(X<x)$。从 $F_X(x)$ 中减去它，剩下的就是恰好取到 $x$ 的概率：

$$P(X=x)=F_X(x)-F_X(x-).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the die, the jump at $3$ is $3/6-2/6=1/6$. This is the probability recorded by its [[概率质量函数|probability mass function]] at $3$. More generally, if a CDF jumps from $0.30$ to $0.45$ at $2$, then $P(X=2)=0.15$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

骰子的 CDF 在 $3$ 处跳了 $3/6-2/6=1/6$，这正是[[概率质量函数]]在 $3$ 处记录的概率。换成一般情形，如果一个 CDF 在 $2$ 处从 $0.30$ 跳到 $0.45$，那么 $P(X=2)=0.15$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What every CDF must look like
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么它只能这样变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As we raise the threshold, we include more results without losing any already included. Therefore a CDF is **nondecreasing** and stays between $0$ and $1$. Moving the threshold toward $-\infty$ makes its value approach $0$; moving toward $+\infty$ makes it approach $1$. A distribution need not reach these limits at any finite threshold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

提高门槛，只会纳入更多结果，不会丢掉已经纳入的结果。因此 CDF **单调不减**，并且始终在 $0$ 与 $1$ 之间。门槛趋向 $-\infty$ 时，函数值趋向 $0$；门槛趋向 $+\infty$ 时，函数值趋向 $1$。它不一定在某个有限门槛处就达到这两个极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A CDF is also **right-continuous**: if a threshold $t$ approaches $x$ from above, then $F_X(t)$ approaches $F_X(x)$. This is a [[函数极限|limit]], written $\lim_{t\downarrow x}F_X(t)=F_X(x)$; the downward arrow means approaching from larger thresholds. For the die, thresholds $3.1,3.01,3.001$ all include $3$, so their CDF values agree with $F_X(3)$. Approaching from below instead excludes $3$ and gives $F_X(3-)=2/6$. Right-continuity permits jumps; it specifies which height the function takes at a jump.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CDF 还必须**右连续**：门槛 $t$ 从比 $x$ 大的一侧靠近 $x$ 时，$F_X(t)$ 趋向 $F_X(x)$，这是一个[[函数极限]]，记作 $\lim_{t\downarrow x}F_X(t)=F_X(x)$；向下的箭头表示从较大的门槛靠近。骰子的门槛取 $3.1,3.01,3.001$ 时都已经包含 $3$，所以函数值与 $F_X(3)$ 相同。若从左侧靠近，则还没有包含 $3$，得到 $F_X(3-)=2/6$。右连续允许跳跃，只是规定了跳跃点本身取哪一个高度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, a nondecreasing, right-continuous function with these two end limits is the CDF of a unique probability distribution on the real line. Thus these properties also let us check whether a proposed function can represent a distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，任何单调不减、右连续、且具有上述两端极限的函数，都对应实数轴上唯一的概率分布。因此，这些性质也能用来检查一个给定函数能不能作为 CDF。

> [!warning] A continuous CDF need not have a density · 连续的 CDF 未必有密度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A [[概率密度函数|probability density function]] $f_X$ describes probability through area. When a distribution has such a density—called an **absolutely continuous distribution**—its CDF satisfies
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[概率密度函数]] $f_X$ 通过面积描述概率。有这种密度的分布称为**绝对连续分布**，其 CDF 满足
>
> $$F_X(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{x}f_X(t)\,dt.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The integral accumulates area up to $x$; $t$ is the variable being integrated. The [[导数|derivative]] $F'_X(x)$ measures the CDF's local rate of increase. Then $F'_X(x)=f_X(x)$ almost everywhere: the equality may fail on a set of zero length. A CDF merely having no jumps is a weaker property. The Cantor distribution has a continuous CDF but no density; see [[连续CDF不保证密度|why continuity alone does not guarantee a density]]. The CDF itself works in all these cases.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 积分表示把截至 $x$ 的面积累加起来，$t$ 是积分中的变量。[[导数]] $F'_X(x)$ 描述 CDF 在这一点的局部增长率。这时 $F'_X(x)=f_X(x)$ 几乎处处成立，意思是等式只可能在一个长度为零的集合上失效。但 CDF 没有跳跃是更弱的条件：Cantor 分布的 CDF 连续，却没有密度，详见[[连续CDF不保证密度]]。CDF 本身在这些情形中都适用。
