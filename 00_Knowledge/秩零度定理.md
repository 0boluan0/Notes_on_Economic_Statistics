# Rank–nullity theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*秩零度定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A linear map with a finite-dimensional domain can send some input directions to zero while retaining other independent directions in its outputs. The **rank–nullity theorem** accounts for every dimension of the input: the dimension lost to zero, called [[零度|nullity]], plus the dimension of the reachable output space, called [[矩阵秩|rank]] for a matrix, equals the dimension of the domain. The count concerns independent directions, not the number of nonzero coordinates or the number of written equations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义域有限维的线性映射，可以把一些输入方向送到零，同时在输出中保留其他独立方向。**秩零度定理**核算输入的全部维数：被送到零的方向所成空间的维数，称为[[零度]]；可达输出空间的维数，在矩阵情形称为[[矩阵秩|秩]]。两者相加，等于定义域的维数。这里数的是独立方向，不是非零坐标数，也不是写了几条方程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Three input choices, one surviving output choice
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*三个输入选择，只留下一个输出选择*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the real [[线性映射|linear map]] below. Its second output is always twice its first, so all outputs lie on one line:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑下面的实[[线性映射]]。第二个输出恒为第一个的两倍，所以全部输出位于一条直线上：

$$T(x,y,z)=(x+y,\,2x+2y),\qquad T:\mathbb R^3\longrightarrow\mathbb R^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every $(s,2s)$ occurs by choosing $(x,y,z)=(s,0,0)$, so the image has basis $(1,2)$ and dimension one. To find the [[零空间|kernel]], solve $T(x,y,z)=0$: this requires $x+y=0$ and leaves $z$ free. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个 $(s,2s)$ 都能通过 $(x,y,z)=(s,0,0)$ 得到，所以像空间以 $(1,2)$ 为基，维数为一。求[[零空间|核]]就是解 $T(x,y,z)=0$：这要求 $x+y=0$，而 $z$ 可自由选择。因此

$$\ker T=\{(t,-t,s):t,s\in\mathbb R\}
=\operatorname{span}\{u_1,u_2\},\qquad
u_1=(1,-1,0),\quad u_2=(0,0,1).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\operatorname{span}$ means all [[张成|linear combinations]]. The two displayed vectors are independent and form a basis of the kernel. Add $v=(1,0,0)$ to obtain a basis of the whole input space, as the unique representation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $\operatorname{span}$ 表示全部线性组合，即[[张成]]。列出的两个向量无关，构成核的一组基。再加入 $v=(1,0,0)$，就得到整个输入空间的一组基，因为有唯一表示

$$(x,y,z)=-y\,u_1+z\,u_2+(x+y)v.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Applying $T$ removes the first two terms and keeps $(x+y)T(v)$, where $T(v)=(1,2)$. Two basis directions disappear and one gives a basis of the image: $3=2+1$. The output has two coordinates, but the sum in the theorem is the input dimension three.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

施加 $T$ 后，前两项消失，只剩 $(x+y)T(v)$，其中 $T(v)=(1,2)$。两根基方向被消去，一根产生像空间的基：$3=2+1$。虽然输出有两个坐标，定理中相加得到的却是输入维数三。

> [!abstract] Finite-dimensional domain · 有限维定义域
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $T:V\to W$ be linear between vector spaces over the same field $\mathbb F$, with $n=\dim V<\infty$. Define the kernel $\ker T=\{v\in V:T(v)=0\}$ and the image $\operatorname{im}T=\{T(v):v\in V\}$. Then the image is finite-dimensional and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $T:V\to W$ 是同一标量域 $\mathbb F$ 上向量空间之间的线性映射，且 $n=\dim V<\infty$。定义核 $\ker T=\{v\in V:T(v)=0\}$、像 $\operatorname{im}T=\{T(v):v\in V\}$。则像空间有限维，并且
>
> $$\boxed{\dim V=\dim\ker T+\dim\operatorname{im}T.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These two subspace dimensions are the nullity and rank of the map. Only the **domain** is assumed finite-dimensional; $W$ need not be. For a matrix map $x\mapsto Ax$ with an $m$-row, $n$-column matrix $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$, the kernel is $N(A)$ and the image is the [[列空间|column space]] $C(A)$, giving
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这两个子空间的维数，就是映射的零度与秩。只要求**定义域**有限维，$W$ 无须有限维。对矩阵映射 $x\mapsto Ax$，其中 $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$ 有 $m$ 行、$n$ 列，核为 $N(A)$，像为[[列空间]] $C(A)$，于是
>
> $$\boxed{n=\operatorname{nullity}(A)+\operatorname{rank}(A).}$$

## Why completing a kernel basis proves the formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么补全核的基就能证明公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $k=\dim\ker T$, and choose a [[基|basis]] $u_1,\ldots,u_k$ of the kernel. A subspace of finite-dimensional $V$ has a finite basis. Extend this independent list to a basis of $V$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $k=\dim\ker T$，并选取核的一组[[基]] $u_1,\ldots,u_k$。有限维空间 $V$ 的子空间也有有限基。把这个无关组扩充成 $V$ 的一组基：

$$u_1,\ldots,u_k,\ v_1,\ldots,v_{n-k}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see why extension is possible, start with any finite spanning list of $V$ and append its vectors whenever they are not already spanned. Independence is preserved at each addition, and at the end every vector of the spanning list is generated. The result is a basis and therefore has $n$ members. We will show that the images of the $n-k$ added vectors form a basis of $\operatorname{im}T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

扩充之所以可行，是因为可以取 $V$ 的任意有限张成组，依次加入尚未被当前向量组张成的成员。每次加入都保持无关，结束时又能生成原张成组的全部成员，于是得到一组基，必然含 $n$ 个成员。接下来证明：新增的 $n-k$ 根向量的像，构成 $\operatorname{im}T$ 的一组基。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Spanning the image.** Every $x\in V$ has coefficients $a_i,b_j\in\mathbb F$ such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**张成像空间。** 每个 $x\in V$ 都有系数 $a_i,b_j\in\mathbb F$，使得

$$x=\sum_{i=1}^{k}a_i u_i+\sum_{j=1}^{n-k}b_jv_j,
\qquad
T(x)=\sum_{j=1}^{n-k}b_jT(v_j).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $u_i$ terms vanish because $T(u_i)=0$. Thus every output is a combination of $T(v_1),\ldots,T(v_{n-k})$. This also proves that the image is finite-dimensional, even when $W$ is not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $T(u_i)=0$，所有 $u_i$ 项都消失。因此每个输出都是 $T(v_1),\ldots,T(v_{n-k})$ 的组合。这同时证明了像空间有限维，即使 $W$ 不是有限维。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Independence of those images.** Suppose scalars $c_j$ satisfy $\sum_j c_jT(v_j)=0$. Linearity implies $\sum_jc_jv_j\in\ker T$, so it has kernel-basis coefficients $d_i$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**这些像线性无关。** 假设标量 $c_j$ 满足 $\sum_j c_jT(v_j)=0$。由线性性，$\sum_jc_jv_j\in\ker T$，所以它能用核的基表示，具有系数 $d_i$：

$$\sum_{j=1}^{n-k}c_jv_j=\sum_{i=1}^{k}d_i u_i,
\qquad
\sum_{j=1}^{n-k}c_jv_j-\sum_{i=1}^{k}d_i u_i=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The combined list is a basis of $V$, hence independent. Every coefficient in the last equation must be zero, particularly every $c_j$. The images are therefore independent as well as spanning. Their number is $n-k$, so $\dim\operatorname{im}T=n-k$ and the theorem follows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

合起来的向量组是 $V$ 的基，因此无关。最后一个等式中的系数必须全部为零，特别是每个 $c_j=0$。这些像既张成又无关，数量为 $n-k$，故 $\dim\operatorname{im}T=n-k$，定理得证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reading the dimensions without identifying the spaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*数维数，不把不同空间当成同一个空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The added input vectors are not uniquely determined. In the example, $v=(1,0,0)$ is one choice; it is not even perpendicular to $u_1=(1,-1,0)$. The theorem does not assert a preferred orthogonal split of the domain. The image is a subspace of the **output** space, whereas the kernel is a subspace of the **input** space. It is their dimensions that add to $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

补入的输入向量不是唯一确定的。在例子里，$v=(1,0,0)$ 只是一个选择，它甚至不与 $u_1=(1,-1,0)$ 垂直。定理没有声称定义域存在一套优先的正交划分。像空间属于**输出**空间，核属于**输入**空间；相加得到 $n$ 的，是它们的维数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The endpoint cases also fit. If $T$ is zero, the kernel is all of $V$ and the image is $\{0\}$: $n=n+0$. If the kernel is $\{0\}$, its empty basis contributes zero dimensions and the image has dimension $n$. If $V=\{0\}$, then $n=0$ and both terms are zero; empty sums in the proof mean zero. This finite-dimensional formula does not license subtracting “infinity minus infinity” for an infinite-dimensional domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

端点情形也适用。若 $T$ 是零映射，核为整个 $V$，像为 $\{0\}$，于是 $n=n+0$。若核为 $\{0\}$，其空基贡献零维，像的维数就是 $n$。若 $V=\{0\}$，则 $n=0$，右边两项都为零；证明中的空和按零处理。这个有限维公式不允许对无限维定义域直接做“无穷减无穷”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In elimination, [[主元与自由变量|$r$ pivots and $n-r$ free variables]] express the same count in coordinates: the homogeneous solutions have $n-r$ independent parameters. The relevant $n$ is the number of columns, because inputs lie in $\mathbb F^n$. For a **real** matrix, applying the theorem separately to its [[矩阵转置|transpose]] $A^T$ gives left-nullspace dimension $m-r$, using [[行秩等于列秩|equality of row and column rank]]. This is a different domain and a different nullspace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

消元中，[[主元与自由变量|$r$ 个主元和 $n-r$ 个自由变量]]是同一计数的坐标表现：齐次解具有 $n-r$ 个独立参数。这里 $n$ 是列数，因为输入位于 $\mathbb F^n$。对**实矩阵**，向[[矩阵转置|转置]] $A^T$ 单独应用定理，并使用[[行秩等于列秩]]，得到左零空间维数 $m-r$；这是另一个定义域、另一个零空间。

> [!question]- Which ambient dimension? · 用哪个环境维数？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real $6\times9$ matrix has rank $4$. Its nullity is $9-4=5$, because its inputs have nine coordinates. Its left nullspace has dimension $6-4=2$. The two answers concern different spaces, not two ways of calculating the same nullity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个 $6\times9$ 实矩阵的秩为 $4$。它的零度为 $9-4=5$，因为输入有九个坐标；左零空间维数则为 $6-4=2$。两者对应不同空间，不是同一个零度的两种算法。
