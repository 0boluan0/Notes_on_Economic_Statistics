# 同期限同风险且可执行的本外币融资与远期交易在无套利下满足抵补利率平价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Covered interest parity follows from no arbitrage only for maturity-matched, risk-comparable, executable domestic and foreign funding and forward trades*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子条件
> 在 $S_t$、$F_t$ 均报每单位外币的本币价格时，若本币总回报为 $R_d$、外币总回报为 $R_f$，抵补利率平价为
> $$R_d=R_f\frac{F_t}{S_t},\qquad \frac{F_t}{S_t}=\frac{R_d}{R_f}.$$
> 等式不是报价定义；它来自两条锁定终值路径在无套利且可比、可执行时不能持续给出不同净回报。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With both $S_t$ and $F_t$ quoted as domestic currency per unit of foreign currency, covered interest parity is $R_d=R_fF_t/S_t$. This equality is not a quotation identity. It follows because two locked terminal-payoff paths cannot deliver persistently different net returns when the trades are comparable and executable under no arbitrage.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

教材套利方向是：借入净回报较低的一条融资路径，投资净回报较高的路径，并同时锁定远期换汇。但这一步要求借贷而不只是存款报价都可得、到期日与复利口径相同、名义本金匹配，并处理信用、抵押、税务、资本占用、结算和对手方风险。若借款利率高于存款利率，不能拿不可获得的最优惠利率拼出套利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The textbook arbitrage borrows along the lower-return funding path, invests along the higher-return path, and locks the currency conversion forward. That construction requires executable borrowing as well as deposit quotes, matched maturities and compounding, matched notionals, and treatment of credit, collateral, tax, balance-sheet, settlement, and counterparty risks. A deposit rate unavailable to the borrower cannot be inserted into an executable arbitrage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理论上，满足上述理想条件的 CIP 是精确的无套利限制，而不是“近似恒等式”。实务中相对于选定现金利率和衍生品报价观察到的 cross-currency basis，可能包含买卖价差、融资与信用差异、监管资本和资产负债表容量、流动性及市场分割。只有在所有交易成本和约束后的可执行净利润仍为正时，偏离才构成当事人的套利机会。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the ideal conditions above, CIP is an exact no-arbitrage restriction, not an approximate identity. An observed cross-currency basis relative to selected cash and derivative benchmarks may contain bid–ask spreads, funding and credit differences, regulatory and balance-sheet costs, liquidity, and market segmentation. A deviation is an arbitrage opportunity for a particular trader only if an executable positive net profit remains after all such costs and constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CIP 不使用对未来即期汇率的预测：远期率已经把汇率腿锁定。它也不推出未套保高利率货币必然有更高实现回报；后者属于 [[无抵补利率平价]] 的预期与风险问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CIP does not use a forecast of the future spot rate because the currency leg is already locked. It also does not imply that an unhedged high-interest currency must realize a higher return; that is an expectations-and-risk question for uncovered interest parity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 看到屏幕上的 $R_d\ne R_fF_t/S_t$，为什么不能立即宣布存在免费套利？
>
> **答案：** 先要核对双向可执行报价、借贷利差、相同到期日、信用与抵押、税费、结算和资产负债表成本；基准间的测量差异不等于任何交易者都能获得无风险净利润。
