# Strict concavity, uniqueness and attainment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*严格凹性、唯一性与取得性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“There cannot be two best choices” and “there is a best choice” are different statements. [[凹凸性|Strict concavity]] provides the first on a [[凸集|convex feasible set]]: any genuine blend of two distinct inputs lies strictly above the corresponding blend of their outputs. It does not provide the second. An input that actually achieves the largest value is a **maximizer**; a [[上确界|supremum]] is the least upper bound on values, and need not be achieved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“不可能有两个最好的选择”和“确实有一个最好的选择”是不同的陈述。[[凹凸性|严格凹性]]在[[凸集|凸可行集]]上提供前者：两个不同输入作真正的混合时，函数值严格高于对应输出的混合。它不提供后者。实际取得最大值的输入叫**最大点**；[[上确界]]是所有输出的最小上界，却不一定能取得。

> [!summary] Two separate claims · 唯一性与存在性是两条证明
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Strict concavity rules out two distinct maximizers on a convex feasible set. It therefore gives uniqueness conditional on attainment. It says neither that the feasible set is nonempty nor that the supremum is attained by a feasible point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在凸可行集上，严格凹性排除了两个不同的最大点。因此它给出的是
> $$
> \text{最大点若存在，则至多一个}.
> $$
> 它没有说明可行集非空，也没有说明上确界会在可行集中被某个点达到。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why two maximizers are impossible
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么不能有两个最大点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose distinct points $x,y\in C$ both maximize a real-valued strictly concave function, with common value $M$. Convexity puts their midpoint $z=(x+y)/2$ in $C$. Strict concavity then gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设不同的 $x,y\in C$ 都是实值严格凹函数的最大点，共同取得值 $M$。凸性保证中点 $z=(x+y)/2$ 仍在 $C$，严格凹性给出

$$f(z)>\tfrac12 f(x)+\tfrac12 f(y)=M.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
That contradicts the meaning of a largest value. Hence there is at most one maximizer. Ordinary concavity would only give a weak inequality, so it allows ties: a constant function on $[0,1]$ attains its maximum at every point. The argument above does not manufacture an input attaining the supremum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与“最大值”的含义矛盾，所以最大点至多一个。普通凹性只给弱不等式，因此允许并列：$[0,1]$ 上的常函数在每个点都取得最大值。上面的论证并没有构造一个取得上确界的输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A missing endpoint prevents attainment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反例：上确界在缺失的边界点

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The function $f(x)=-(1-x)^2$ is strictly concave on the open convex set $(0,1)$. Its values approach the supremum zero as $x\to1$, but the endpoint is infeasible, so no maximizer exists. Strict concavity has not failed; there are simply no attained maximizers to compare.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

函数
$$
f(x)=-(1-x)^2
$$
在开凸集 $C=(0,1)$ 上严格凹。对每个 $x<1$，向右移动一点都能提高 $f$；当 $x\to1$ 时，$f(x)\to0$，但 $1\notin C$。所以
$$
\sup_{x\in C}f(x)=0
$$
却没有最大点。严格凹没有失败：这里根本没有两个最大点可供它排除。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For every $x\in(0,1)$ choose $y=(x+1)/2$, which remains in $(0,1)$ and is larger than $x$. Direct subtraction gives $f(y)-f(x)=(y-x)(2-x-y)>0$. Thus no candidate can be maximal. By the [[导数判凹凸|derivative criterion]], the function is strictly concave since $f^{\prime\prime}(x)=-2$ throughout the interval. Values are all negative and approach zero, so zero really is the least upper bound, not a value in the range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个 $x\in(0,1)$，取 $y=(x+1)/2$，仍在 $(0,1)$ 且大于 $x$。直接相减得到 $f(y)-f(x)=(y-x)(2-x-y)>0$，因此没有任何候选点能是最大点。按[[导数判凹凸]]，处处 $f^{\prime\prime}(x)=-2$ 保证严格凹；所有输出为负并逼近零，所以零确实是最小上界，但不是函数实际取到的值。

> [!example] Change the domain, keep the function · 函数不变，改变定义域
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the same $f(x)=-(1-x)^2$, the domain $[0,1]$ includes $1$, so its unique maximizer is $1$ with value $0$. The domain $\mathbb R$ also has that maximizer, even though it is open and unbounded. Thus openness or lack of compactness does not force failure; it simply means compactness alone cannot supply the existence proof.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 同一个 $f(x)=-(1-x)^2$ 若定义在 $[0,1]$，输入 $1$ 就可行，因此唯一最大点是 $1$，值为 $0$。定义在开且无界的 $\mathbb R$ 上也仍有这个最大点。可见开性或不紧致并不必然导致失败，只是不能直接依靠紧致性提供存在证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Prove attainment before uniqueness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样补上存在性

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A standard route first uses continuity on a nonempty compact feasible set to establish attainment, then uses strict concavity to establish uniqueness. Closed unbounded sets require a different attainment argument, such as upper semicontinuity together with compact upper level sets or an appropriate coercivity condition. Writing a value function with a supremum keeps it meaningful before attainment is proved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常用路线是先用非空紧可行集与连续目标调用 [[极值定理]]，证明最大值确实被取得，再用严格凹证明取得它的点唯一。对闭但无界的可行集，则需另给上半连续性、紧的上水平集或适当强制性等条件。[[值函数]] 写成 supremum，正是为了在最优值尚未取得时仍保持定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The compact route uses a nonempty compact $C$ and a continuous real-valued $f$: the [[极值定理|extreme-value theorem]] supplies an attained maximum. To add uniqueness by strict concavity, also retain convexity of $C$. In finite-dimensional Euclidean space, compact means closed and bounded. Closedness alone is insufficient: on $[0,\infty)$ the function $g(x)=-e^{-x}$ is continuous and strictly concave, with $g^{\prime\prime}(x)=-e^{-x}<0$, yet its supremum $0$ is approached only as $x\to\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

紧集路线采用非空紧集 $C$ 和连续实值函数 $f$，由[[极值定理]]得到实际取得的最大值；再用严格凹性加上唯一性时，还要保留 $C$ 的凸性。在有限维欧氏空间中，紧等价于闭且有界。仅闭还不够：$[0,\infty)$ 上的 $g(x)=-e^{-x}$ 连续且严格凹，$g^{\prime\prime}(x)=-e^{-x}<0$，但上确界 $0$ 仅在 $x\to\infty$ 时逼近。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One useful alternative keeps only the promising choices in a compact set. Suppose $f$ is continuous on $C$, choose a feasible $x_0$, and suppose the [[上轮廓集|upper level set]] $K=\{x\in C:f(x)\ge f(x_0)\}$ is compact. It is nonempty because it contains $x_0$. The extreme-value theorem gives $x^*\in K$ maximizing $f$ on $K$; every point outside $K$ has value below $f(x_0)\le f(x^*)$. Thus $x^*$ maximizes over all of $C$. This is a sufficient existence argument, independent of strict concavity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一条常用路线只把“有希望的选择”留在紧集里。设 $f$ 在 $C$ 上连续，选一个可行点 $x_0$，并证明[[上轮廓集|上水平集]] $K=\{x\in C:f(x)\ge f(x_0)\}$ 紧。它包含 $x_0$，因此非空。极值定理给出 $K$ 上的最大点 $x^*$；$K$ 外的每一点都满足 $f(x)<f(x_0)\le f(x^*)$，故 $x^*$ 也是整个 $C$ 上的最大点。这是一条独立于严格凹性的充分存在证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The more general terminology in the preceding course summary refers to related ways of closing this same gap. **Upper semicontinuity** uses the [[数列上极限|limit superior]], the limiting upper level of sequence tails, and requires $\limsup f(x_k)\le f(x)$ when feasible $x_k\to x$; it permits upward values at the limiting point but prevents a maximizing sequence from losing value there. For maximization, an appropriate **coercivity** condition makes $f(x)\to-\infty$ as feasible $\|x\|_2\to\infty$; with a nonempty closed domain and continuity, it keeps a nonempty upper level set bounded and closed. These conditions need their stated domain and regularity assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前面课程概括中的较一般用语，也是在补这个存在性缺口。**上半连续**使用[[数列上极限]]，即不断去掉有限开头后尾部上界的极限，并要求可行序列 $x_k\to x$ 时 $\limsup f(x_k)\le f(x)$：允许极限点的值向上跳，但防止逼近最优的序列在极限点丢失函数值。最大化所需的适当**强制性**让可行的 $\|x\|_2\to\infty$ 时 $f(x)\to-\infty$；配合非空闭定义域与连续性，它能使非空上水平集既闭又有界。这些条件都需要相应的定义域与正则性前提。

> [!question]- Check your understanding · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Which two logical steps are missing from the claim, “The objective is strictly concave, so the problem has a unique solution”?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> “目标严格凹，所以问题有唯一解”缺少哪两个逻辑环节？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** One must show that the feasible set is nonempty and that a feasible point attains the supremum. Strict concavity gives uniqueness only after attainment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 还要证明可行集非空，并证明上确界被某个可行点取得。严格凹只在取得以后给唯一性。
