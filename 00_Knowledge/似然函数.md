# 似然函数把观测数据固定并比较不同参数下该数据的概率或密度

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A likelihood fixes the observed data and varies the parameter in its probability or density*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 给定模型 $f_\theta(x)$ 和已观察数据 $x$，似然为 $L(\theta;x)=f_\theta(x)$，此时自变量是 $\theta$。它不是参数的概率分布；通常不对 $\theta$ 求和或积分为 1。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For observed data and a statistical model, likelihood evaluates the data probability or density as a function of the parameter. It is not a probability distribution over parameter values and need not normalise over them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

独立同分布样本的似然为 $\prod_{i=1}^nf_\theta(x_i)$。某袋红球比例为 $\theta$，有放回独立抽三次均为红球，则 $L(\theta)=\theta^3$；$L(0.9)=0.729$、$L(0.1)=0.001$。这不表示 $P(\theta=0.9\mid x)=0.729$，后者还需[[先验分布]]与归一化。连续数据用[[概率密度函数|密度]]，单点概率通常为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An iid likelihood multiplies the observation densities. Three independent red draws with replacement have likelihood $\theta^3$. The evaluations at 0.9 and 0.1 compare explanations of the same evidence; neither is a posterior probability. The [[先验分布|prior]] and normalisation are still needed. Continuous observations use [[概率密度函数|densities]], not point probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
