# 财政支出占比是同一时期财政支出与GDP的比值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The fiscal expenditure-to-GDP ratio compares expenditure with GDP for the same period.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财政支出占比把某一明确范围的 [[财政支出]] 与同期 GDP 相比，用来观察支出相对于经济规模有多大。记支出为 $G_t$、GDP 为 $Y_t>0$，则比例为 $s_t=G_t/Y_t$；以百分数报告时再乘 $100\%$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ratio compares a defined [[财政支出|fiscal expenditure]] aggregate with contemporaneous GDP. With expenditure $G_t$ and GDP $Y_t>0$, $s_t=G_t/Y_t$; multiply by $100\%$ to report a percentage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，同一年度按当年价格计量的支出为 200、GDP 为 1,000，占比就是 20%。比较国家或年份时，应同时固定政府覆盖范围、预算/决算口径、会计基础和合并方法；通常用当年价格的支出与当年价格 GDP，不把名义支出除以不变价 GDP。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Current-price expenditure of 200 and GDP of 1,000 imply a 20% ratio. Comparisons must align government coverage, budget/outturn status, accounting basis and consolidation. Normally both numerator and denominator are at current prices, not nominal expenditure divided by constant-price GDP.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

合并中央与地方时尤其要避免重复。若中央向地方拨款 30，地方用这 30 向政府体系之外采购，中央、地方账上都出现支出，但全国合并数中的这部分是 30，不是 60。先消除政府内部拨款，再观察整个政府体系的支出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If central government grants 30 to local government, which spends it outside government, summing the two expenditure records gives a misleading 60. Consolidation removes the internal grant, leaving 30 of expenditure for the combined government sector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

占比上升也不等于政府主动加大支出：支出仍为 200、GDP 从 1,000 降至 900，占比便升到约 22.22%。要说明变化来自哪里，再看 [[财政支出增长率]] 与 [[财政支出弹性]]。由于财政支出包含转移等项目，这个比值也不能直接解释成“政府生产了 GDP 的多少”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keeping expenditure at 200 while GDP falls from 1,000 to 900 raises the ratio to about 22.22%, without an expenditure increase. Examine [[财政支出增长率|growth]] and [[财政支出弹性|elasticity]] to separate numerator and denominator changes. Because spending includes transfers, the ratio is not government's share of GDP production.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
