# 幂级数在收敛半径内部可逐项积分且积分级数保持原收敛半径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Inside its radius, a power series can be integrated termwise without changing that radius*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(x-a)^n$ 的收敛半径为 $R>0$。对 $|x-a|<R$，
$$
\int_a^x f(t)\,dt
=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{c_n}{n+1}(x-a)^{n+1}.
$$
右侧级数的收敛半径仍为 $R$；不定积分再加一个任意常数 $C$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a power series with positive radius $R$, integration from the centre to any interior point is given by the displayed series. The integrated series has the same radius; an indefinite integral adds a constant $C$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选中心作下限能直接固定常数项。例如将 $1/(1-t)=\sum t^n$ 从 $0$ 积分到 $x$，得到 $-\ln(1-x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x^n/n$（$|x|<1$）。这也说明积分后端点可改变：原几何级数在 $x=-1$ 发散，积分后的级数在那里条件收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the centre as lower limit fixes the constant automatically. Integrating $1/(1-t)=\sum t^n$ from zero gives $-\ln(1-x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x^n/n$ in the interior. At $x=-1$, the original geometric series diverges but the integrated series converges conditionally.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本定理允许在严格位于收敛区间内部的有限积分区间上交换求和与积分；积分到边界或无穷远需要额外论证。无限收敛半径并不自动允许将积分上限换成 $\infty$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem permits interchange on finite integration intervals strictly inside the convergence interval. Integration to a boundary or to infinity needs a further argument; even an infinite radius alone does not justify an infinite integration limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
