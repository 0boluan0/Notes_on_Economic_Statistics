# Termwise integration of power series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*幂级数逐项积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Termwise integration replaces the integral of an infinite power-series sum by the sum of the integrals of its powers. This is useful when the powers are easy to integrate even though the original function is less convenient. The key condition is geometric: the whole finite interval of integration must stay strictly inside the convergence radius.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逐项积分把“幂级数的和再积分”改成“分别积分各个幂，再把结果相加”。当各个幂容易积分、原函数不那么方便时，这个方法很有用。关键范围条件是：积分的整个有限区间都必须严格位于收敛半径内部。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Integrating a geometric series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从几何级数得到一个新展开*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $|t|<1$, the [[几何级数|geometric series]] gives $1/(1-t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}t^n$. Integrate from $0$ to a fixed $x$ with $|x|<1$. The theorem below allows each $t^n$ to contribute $x^{n+1}/(n+1)$, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $|t|<1$ 时，[[几何级数]]给出 $1/(1-t)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}t^n$。从 $0$ 积分到固定的 $x$，并要求 $|x|<1$。下面的定理允许每一项 $t^n$ 分别贡献 $x^{n+1}/(n+1)$，于是

$$
\int_0^x\frac{dt}{1-t}
=-\ln(1-x)
=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}
=\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{x^k}{k},\qquad |x|<1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] is defined here because $1-x>0$. The new index is $k=n+1$, so its first value is $1$. The lower limit $0$ fixes the constant: both sides are zero when $x=0$. At $x=1/2$, the first three terms give $1/2+1/8+1/24=2/3$ as an approximation to $\ln2$. The positive remainder is bounded by
$\sum_{k=4}^{\infty}(1/2)^k/k\le(1/4)\sum_{k=4}^{\infty}(1/2)^k=1/32$.
Thus the exact integral lies between $2/3$ and $2/3+1/32$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $1-x>0$，所以[[自然对数]]有定义。新下标是 $k=n+1$，起点相应改为 $1$。下限 $0$ 自动固定了常数：$x=0$ 时两边都为零。在 $x=1/2$ 处，前三项给出 $1/2+1/8+1/24=2/3$，用于近似 $\ln2$。正的余项满足 $\sum_{k=4}^{\infty}(1/2)^k/k\le(1/4)\sum_{k=4}^{\infty}(1/2)^k=1/32$，所以精确积分介于 $2/3$ 与 $2/3+1/32$ 之间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The formula and its constant
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*公式与积分常数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a,c_n$ be real, and let $f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(x-a)^n$ be the sum of a [[幂级数|power series]] with [[幂级数收敛半径|radius]] $R>0$, possibly infinite. The symbol $t$ below is the running integration variable; $x$ is the chosen upper endpoint. For every $|x-a|<R$, define the accumulated [[定积分|definite integral]] $F(x)$ by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a,c_n$ 为实数，$f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(x-a)^n$ 是[[幂级数]]的和，其[[幂级数收敛半径|半径]] $R>0$，允许无穷。下面的 $t$ 是积分过程中变化的变量，$x$ 是选定的上端点。对每个 $|x-a|<R$，用累积的[[定积分]]定义 $F(x)$：

$$
F(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt
=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{c_n}{n+1}(x-a)^{n+1}.
$$

> [!important]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The integrated series has exactly the same radius $R$ and satisfies $F(a)=0$. It is an [[原函数|antiderivative]] of $f$ on the interior interval. The full family of antiderivatives there is $F(x)+C$, where $C$ is one arbitrary real constant, because [[原函数相差常数|antiderivatives on an interval differ by a constant]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 积分级数的半径仍恰好是 $R$，并满足 $F(a)=0$。它是内部区间上 $f$ 的一个[[原函数]]。该区间内全部原函数构成 $F(x)+C$，其中 $C$ 是任意实常数，因为[[原函数相差常数|同一区间上的原函数只相差常数]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lower endpoint need not be the centre. For any finite $u,v$ strictly inside the convergence interval, subtract the two centre-based integrals to obtain
$\int_u^v f(t)\,dt=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n[(v-a)^{n+1}-(u-a)^{n+1}]/(n+1)$.
This includes $u>v$ with the usual reversed sign, and $u=v$ with value zero. The entire segment between $u$ and $v$ is still inside the interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下限不必选中心。对收敛区间内任意两个有限的严格内点 $u,v$，把两个以中心为下限的积分相减，就得到 $\int_u^v f(t)\,dt=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n[(v-a)^{n+1}-(u-a)^{n+1}]/(n+1)$。当 $u>v$ 时保留反向积分的负号；$u=v$ 时结果为零。连接 $u,v$ 的整段仍然位于内部。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One tail bound controls the whole interval
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用同一个尾界控制整段*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix $x$ in the interior and choose a finite $r$ with $|x-a|<r<R$. The nonnegative series $\sum |c_n|r^n$ converges by interior absolute convergence. Its tail $E_N=\sum_{n>N}|c_n|r^n$ tends to zero. For the polynomial $S_N(t)=\sum_{n=0}^N c_n(t-a)^n$, every $t\in[a-r,a+r]$ satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定内部的 $x$，选一个有限 $r$，使 $|x-a|<r<R$。由内部绝对收敛性，非负级数 $\sum|c_n|r^n$ 收敛，其尾和 $E_N=\sum_{n>N}|c_n|r^n$ 趋于零。对多项式 $S_N(t)=\sum_{n=0}^N c_n(t-a)^n$，每个 $t\in[a-r,a+r]$ 都满足

$$
|f(t)-S_N(t)|\le \sum_{n>N}|c_n|\,|t-a|^n\le E_N.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is called **uniform convergence** on the smaller interval: the same $N$ makes the error small at every input there. It also proves continuity of $f$. Near any fixed input, bound the two tails by $E_N$ and use continuity of the one fixed polynomial $S_N$ to control the middle difference. Choosing $N$ first makes both tails arbitrarily small. Thus $f$ is continuous on that closed interval, and [[连续函数可积|continuous functions on finite closed intervals are integrable]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这叫作在较小区间上**一致收敛**：同一个 $N$ 就能让整段内所有输入处的误差都很小。它也能证明 $f$ 连续：比较附近两点的函数值时，两端的尾部分别不超过 $E_N$，中间用固定多项式 $S_N$ 的连续性控制；先选 $N$，就能让两端尾部都任意小。因此 $f$ 在该闭区间上连续，再由[[连续函数可积|有限闭区间上连续函数可积]]，可以讨论其积分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[积分绝对值界|integral error bound]] now turns a height error into an accumulated error:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在用[[积分绝对值界]]，把高度误差变成累积误差：

$$
\left|\int_a^x f(t)\,dt-\int_a^x S_N(t)\,dt\right|
\le |x-a|E_N\longrightarrow0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The absolute length $|x-a|$ covers either orientation. Each $S_N$ is a finite polynomial, so its integral is exactly the finite sum of $c_n(x-a)^{n+1}/(n+1)$. Taking $N\to\infty$ proves the formula. The same argument gives a bound $|v-u|E_N$ on any finite interior segment. Nothing here depends on exchanging two infinite operations without control.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

绝对长度 $|x-a|$ 同时处理两个积分方向。每个 $S_N$ 都是有限多项式，其积分恰好等于有限多项 $c_n(x-a)^{n+1}/(n+1)$ 的和。令 $N\to\infty$ 就证明了公式；任意内部有限段也有相应的界 $|v-u|E_N$。关键是先给出误差控制，再交换积分与无穷求和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The integrated series converges absolutely at every interior distance, since its term magnitudes are at most $|x-a|\,|c_n||x-a|^n$. It cannot have a larger radius than the original series: [[幂级数逐项求导|differentiating that new power series]] would recover the original coefficients while preserving its radius. This proves equality of the radii and also $F'=f$. For complex coefficients and a real input, define the integral separately on real and imaginary parts; the same absolute tail bounds, using complex magnitudes, give the same result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

积分级数在每个内部距离处都绝对收敛，因为各项大小不超过 $|x-a|\,|c_n||x-a|^n$。它也不能有比原级数更大的半径：对这个新幂级数使用[[幂级数逐项求导]]，会恢复原系数，并保持新半径。由此得到半径恰好相同，同时得到 $F'=f$。若系数为复数、输入仍为实数，分别对实部和虚部定义积分；把绝对值换成复数的模，相同尾界仍证明同一结论。

## Endpoints and an infinite upper limit need another argument
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*端点与无穷上限需要另外论证*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening geometric example, the original series at $x=-1$ is $1-1+1-\cdots$ and diverges. The integrated series becomes $\sum_{k\ge1}(-1)^k/k$: it converges by the [[交错级数判别|alternating test]] but its absolute-value series is harmonic and diverges, so it is [[条件收敛|conditionally convergent]]. At $x=1$ the integrated series itself is harmonic and diverges. Radius one is preserved even though the endpoint set changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在开头的几何级数例子中，原级数在 $x=-1$ 变成 $1-1+1-\cdots$，发散；积分后的级数则变为 $\sum_{k\ge1}(-1)^k/k$，由[[交错级数判别]]收敛，但绝对值级数是发散的调和级数，因此属于[[条件收敛]]。在 $x=1$，积分后的级数本身就是调和级数而发散。虽然端点集合变化，半径仍为一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[幂级数端点判别|Endpoint convergence]] alone does not identify the endpoint sum with the continuation of the interior formula. For this particular negative endpoint, the extra step can be proved using a finite identity. For $N\ge1$ and $0\le u\le1$,
$1/(1+u)=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}(-u)^j+(-u)^N/(1+u)$.
Integrating this finite identity from $0$ to $1$ bounds the remaining integral in magnitude by $\int_0^1u^Ndu=1/(N+1)\to0$. Hence $\sum_{k\ge1}(-1)^{k-1}/k=\ln2$, and the integrated series at $x=-1$ equals $-\ln2$. This is an additional endpoint proof, not an automatic substitution into the interior theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[幂级数端点判别|端点收敛]]本身，并不证明端点和等于内部公式的延伸值。本例的负端点可以另用有限恒等式证明：当 $N\ge1$、$0\le u\le1$ 时，$1/(1+u)=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}(-u)^j+(-u)^N/(1+u)$。把这个有限恒等式从 $0$ 积到 $1$，剩余积分的绝对值不超过 $\int_0^1u^Ndu=1/(N+1)\to0$。因此 $\sum_{k\ge1}(-1)^{k-1}/k=\ln2$，积分级数在 $x=-1$ 的和为 $-\ln2$。这是另外补上的端点证明，不是直接把端点代入内部定理。

> [!warning]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Even $R=\infty$ permits the preceding argument only on each fixed finite interval. An integral to infinity is an [[反常积分|improper integral]] and needs separate convergence and interchange arguments. For example, $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n/n!$, where $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$ for $n\ge1$ and $0!=1$, has infinite radius by the ratio test, but for $x\ge0$ its sum is at least $1$, so its integral over $[0,\infty)$ is not finite. A radius statement alone does not control accumulation over an unbounded interval.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 即使 $R=\infty$，前面的论证也只是对每个固定的有限区间成立。积分到无穷远属于[[反常积分]]，需要另外证明收敛以及交换运算合法。例如 $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n/n!$ 中，$n\ge1$ 时 $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$，并规定 $0!=1$；比值判别给出其半径无穷，但在 $x\ge0$ 上，其和至少为 $1$，所以从 $0$ 到无穷的积分不是有限值。半径条件本身不能控制无界区间上的累积。
