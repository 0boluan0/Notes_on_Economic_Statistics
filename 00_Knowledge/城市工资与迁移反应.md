# 城市工资提高是否增加均衡失业人数取决于岗位需求和农村收入的调整

在[[Harris–Todaro模型]]中，提高城市制度工资会增加**获得岗位后的报酬**，但不能只据此断言均衡城市劳动力或失业人数一定增加。必须同时说明企业提供多少岗位、迁移怎样改变竞争池、农村收入如何变化。工资提高与[[岗位创造与诱致迁移|新增城市岗位]]是不同冲击，不能共用一个未经区分的“悖论”结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[Harris–Todaro模型|Harris–Todaro model]], a higher institutional wage raises the payoff conditional on employment. It does not alone determine equilibrium migration or the number unemployed. Jobs, the competing labour pool and rural earnings must adjust consistently. A wage increase and [[岗位创造与诱致迁移|job creation]] are distinct shocks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先区分直接激励与均衡结果

城市预期收入为 $\bar w_M M/S$。如果短期内岗位 $M$ 与城市劳动力 $S$ 都固定，工资提高确实提高预期收入；允许迁移后，$S$ 便不能继续当作常量。如果企业也调整劳动需求 $M=M(\bar w_M)$，连“提高工资必提高当前竞争池的预期收入”都需要条件：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expected urban earnings are $\bar w_M M/S$. Holding both jobs and the urban pool fixed, a wage increase raises this expectation. Once migration occurs, the pool is no longer fixed. If employers also adjust labour demand, even the direct expected-income effect for the initial pool is conditional:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\left.\frac{d(\bar w_MM/S)}{d\bar w_M}\right|_{S}
=\frac{M+\bar w_M M'}{S}
=\frac{M}{S}(1-\varepsilon),\qquad
\varepsilon=-\frac{\bar w_M M'}{M}.
$$

若向下倾斜的劳动需求弹性 $\varepsilon<1$，工资账单上升；若 $\varepsilon>1$，岗位收缩可使工资账单下降。这里是**给定竞争池时**的直接效应，不是已经求完迁移均衡；农业边际产出和两部门产品价格的调整还会改变农村收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With downward-sloping labour demand, elasticity below one raises the wage bill, while elasticity above one can reduce it. This is the direct effect for a given pool, not the completed migration equilibrium. Agricultural marginal product and relative product prices also affect rural earnings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 用同一个起点比较两种岗位设定

以下是固定产品价格的教学模型，用于隔离机制，不是原文含内生相对价格的完整模型。总劳动力 $L=100$，农业收入为 $w_A=120-L_A=20+S$，未获城市岗位者收入为零。初始工资120、岗位20，均衡城市劳动力 $S=40$。现在把工资提高到140，分别考察：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The following constructed model fixes product prices to isolate the mechanism; it is not the original paper's full endogenous-price system. Total labour is 100, rural earnings are $120-L_A=20+S$, and urban joblessness pays zero. Initially the wage is 120 and jobs number 20, giving an equilibrium urban pool of 40. Raise the wage to 140 under two different job assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 情景 | 现代岗位 $M$ | 由 $\bar w_MM/S=20+S$ 解出的城市劳动力 $S$ | 失业人数 $U=S-M$ | 失业率 $u=U/S$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 起点：工资120 | 20 | 40 | 20 | 50% |
| 工资140，岗位仍固定20 | 20 | 43.852 | 23.852 | 54.392% |
| 工资140，岗位按 $M=(160-\bar w_M)/2$ 调整 | 10 | 28.730 | 18.730 | 65.193% |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the common starting point, unemployment is 20 people or 50%. Holding 20 jobs fixed at the higher wage gives about 23.852 unemployed or 54.392%. With the specified wage-sensitive demand, employment falls to 10 and the urban pool to 28.730: unemployment falls to 18.730 people even though its rate rises to 65.193%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一行的劳动需求可由固定资本下的制造业产出 $F(M)=160M-M^2$、竞争企业条件 $F'(M)=\bar w_M$ 得到；在所用区间 $F'>0,F''<0$。工资提高使岗位从20减至10，而不是不改变岗位。它是“工资提高必使失业人数增加”的明确反例，也显示[[失业率|人数与比例]]必须分开；不能把它读成加工资改善了所有人的处境。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last row follows from $F(M)=160M-M^2$ and competitive hiring $F'(M)=\bar w_M$, with positive diminishing marginal product over the relevant range. The wage increase reduces jobs from 20 to 10. This counters an unconditional claim about unemployment headcounts and separates [[失业率|counts from rates]]; it does not show that all workers become better off.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Harris 与 Todaro 原文第130页脚注10说明均衡轨迹取决于劳动需求和相对价格弹性；第131页脚注11进一步明确：在另一种轨迹斜率下，提高最低工资减少制造业就业、增加农业就业，不能再普遍断言失业人数随最低工资一起上升。现实工资政策还需其实际覆盖、生产率、需求与执行等证据，以上模型不直接提供政策效果估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Footnote 10 on page 130 of the original paper makes the equilibrium locus depend on labour-demand and price elasticities. Footnote 11 on page 131 explicitly allows a configuration where a higher minimum wage reduces manufacturing employment and increases agricultural employment, removing an unconditional claim that unemployment headcounts rise with the wage. Actual policy evaluation additionally requires evidence about coverage, productivity, demand and enforcement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
