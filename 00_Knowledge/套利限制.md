# 套利限制是阻碍知情交易者充分实施价格纠偏交易的风险与约束
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Limits of arbitrage are risks and constraints that prevent informed traders from fully implementing price-correcting trades*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

套利限制（limits of arbitrage）指：交易者即使识别出自己认为可信的估值偏离，也未必能以足够规模、持续足够久地交易，使价格立即回到基本价值。这里的“套利”沿用行为金融文献中的广义价格纠偏交易，并不把[[套利与价差交易|严格套利与风险价差交易]]混成同一种现金流承诺。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Limits of arbitrage mean that recognizing a credible valuation discrepancy need not enable a trader to trade at sufficient scale for sufficiently long to restore fundamental value immediately. “Arbitrage” here follows the behavioral-finance literature's broad use for price-correcting trading, without conflating [[套利与价差交易|strict arbitrage and risky relative-value trades]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

限制可能来自未能完全对冲的[[基本面风险]]、错误定价继续扩大的[[噪声交易者风险]]，也可能来自融资、借券、交易成本、期限和[[套利资本随业绩变动|委托资金的业绩约束]]。前两项解释为什么纠偏头寸可能亏损；实施与资金约束解释为什么看对方向仍不能无限扩仓或一直等待。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Limits can arise from unhedged [[基本面风险|fundamental risk]], [[噪声交易者风险|noise-trader risk]] that mispricing worsens, or funding, security borrowing, trading costs, horizons, and [[套利资本随业绩变动|performance-sensitive delegated capital]]. The first two explain potential losses; implementation and funding constraints explain why a correct directional judgment does not permit unlimited positions or indefinite waiting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它不是“任何异象都能用摩擦解释”的结论。应具体指出谁实施交易、需要什么头寸、哪项约束在什么状态下绑定，以及新增资金或对冲工具为何不能消除该约束。若专业交易者拥有充足且稳定的资金，相关风险又可低成本对冲，同一机制的解释力就会减弱。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is not a claim that friction explains every anomaly. Identify the trader, required positions, constraint that binds in a particular state, and why additional capital or hedges cannot remove it. The same mechanism weakens when specialists have ample stable funding and the relevant risk can be cheaply hedged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，“有人错误地看空”只给出了潜在卖压。要解释价格持续偏低，还需说明接盘交易者面对哪些剩余风险或资金限制。投资者偏差与套利限制是价格解释链中不同的环节，不能只凭前者存在就宣布后者已被证实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, mistaken pessimism supplies potential selling pressure. Explaining persistent underpricing additionally requires identifying residual risks or capital constraints facing potential buyers. Investor bias and limits of arbitrage play different roles in the pricing explanation; evidence of the former does not establish the latter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Shleifer–Vishny（1997），PDF 第 1–4、15–17 页／印刷第 35–38、49–51 页](https://web.stanford.edu/~piazzesi/Reading/ShleiferVishny1997.pdf#page=1)：已重开，支持资本需求、专业化和委托资金限制，以及风险与期限如何影响纠偏交易；印刷第 49 页也讨论不依赖短期业绩的新资本可能抵消强制减仓。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Shleifer–Vishny, PDF pp. 1–4 and 15–17 / printed pp. 35–38 and 49–51, reopened, supports capital requirements, specialization, delegated funding, and the effects of risk and horizons. Printed p. 49 also explains how capital independent of short-term performance can counter forced liquidation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [De Long–Shleifer–Summers–Waldmann（1990），Noise Trader Risk in Financial Markets，PDF 第 4、6 页／印刷第 705、707 页](https://ms.mcmaster.ca/~grasselli/DeLongShleiferSummersWaldmann90.pdf#page=4)：已重开且目视第 705 页，支持风险厌恶、短期限与不可分散的噪声风险限制纠偏规模；不把其模型结论外推成所有错误信念必然影响价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- De Long–Shleifer–Summers–Waldmann, PDF pp. 4 and 6 / printed pp. 705 and 707, reopened with p. 705 visually checked, supports limits from risk aversion, short horizons, and undiversifiable noise risk. Its model is not treated as proving that every mistaken belief affects prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
