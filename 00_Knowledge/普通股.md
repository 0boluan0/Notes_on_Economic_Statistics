# 普通股是以非优先的利润分配权和清算剩余权为特征的股份类别

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common stock is a share class characterised by non-preferential distribution rights and a residual claim in liquidation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通股是公司的[[股票]]类别：在持续经营时，持有人可以参与依法决定的股利分配，但没有仅凭本期盈利就要求按固定比例付现的权利；在清算时，通常是在债权和适用的优先股请求权之后取得剩余。前一种股利决策不等于必须先偿清公司所有尚未到期的债务，后一种[[受偿顺序]]也不保证最终还有剩余。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common stock is a class of company [[股票|stock]]. While the company operates, holders may participate in lawfully determined dividends, but current profit alone does not entitle them to a fixed cash payout. In liquidation, they normally receive the residual after creditor and applicable preferred-share claims. A dividend decision does not require every unmatured company debt to have been repaid; liquidation [[受偿顺序|priority]] does not ensure that any residual remains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若公司本期利润为 1,000，但依法决定分给某同权普通股类别的现金总额只有 300，持有该类 10% 股份者按比例收到 30，而不是自动收到利润的 10%，即 100。未分配利润与股东本期实际收到的现金不能混同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If current company profit is 1,000 but the lawful cash distribution to an equal-rights common-share class is 300, an investor owning 10% of that class receives 30 proportionally, not automatically 100 as 10% of profit. Retained profit and cash actually received by shareholders are different quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通股常附表决权，但不同类别可以有不同票数或受限权利；“普通股”不是“一股必有一票”的充分信息。[[有限责任]]说明股东承担公司债务的边界；只含一层债务和普通股的到期现金分配，直接使用[[股债到期分配]]中的限定模型。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common shares often carry voting rights, but classes can have different voting powers or restrictions. The label alone does not establish one vote per share. [[有限责任|Limited liability]] explains the boundary of shareholder liability, while [[股债到期分配|terminal debt–equity allocation]] supplies the explicitly restricted model with one debt layer and common equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
