# The complement rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*补事件法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sometimes it is easier to calculate how a target **fails to occur** than to list all the ways it can occur. The **complement rule** uses this idea: subtract the probability of failure from one. For example, “at least one head in three tosses” includes many records, while its failure is just “three tails”. The method works only when the failure event includes every outcome outside the target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有时，与其逐一列出目标发生的各种方式，不如计算目标**没有发生**的概率。**补事件法**就利用这一点：用一减去失败的概率。例如，“三次投掷中至少一次正面”包含很多记录，而它没有发生只对应“三次全是反面”。使用这个方法时，失败事件必须包含目标之外的全部结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[概率空间|sample space]] $\Omega$ is the set of possible outcomes, and an event $A$ contains the outcomes meeting our target. Its **complement**, written $A^c$ or $\Omega\setminus A$, consists of all outcomes in the same sample space that are not in $A$. The superscript $c$ means complement; it is not a numerical exponent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[概率空间|样本空间]] $\Omega$ 是全部可能结果的集合，事件 $A$ 包含符合目标的结果。它的**补事件**记为 $A^c$ 或 $\Omega\setminus A$，收集同一个样本空间里所有不属于 $A$ 的结果。右上角的 $c$ 表示补集，不是数字指数。

> [!abstract] Two exhaustive alternatives · 两种覆盖全部结果的情况
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every outcome is either in $A$ or in $A^c$, and none is in both. The [[互斥事件加法|addition rule for disjoint events]] and $P(\Omega)=1$ therefore give
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个结果要么属于 $A$，要么属于 $A^c$，不可能同时属于两者。因此，根据[[互斥事件加法]]以及 $P(\Omega)=1$，
>
> $$P(A)+P(A^c)=1,$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> so we may calculate either side from the other:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 所以可以通过其中一方计算另一方：
>
> $$P(A)=1-P(A^c),\qquad P(A^c)=1-P(A).$$

## Replacing “at least one” with “none”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把“至少一次”换成“一次也没有”*

> [!example] Three tosses of a fair coin · 三次公平硬币投掷
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Toss a fair coin three times, assuming [[相互独立|mutual independence]]. Write $H$ for heads and $T$ for tails. Let $A$ mean “at least one head”. Its complement is $A^c=\{TTT\}$: no heads at all. Independence gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把公平硬币抛三次，并假设三次[[相互独立]]。用 $H$ 表示正面，$T$ 表示反面，令 $A$ 表示“至少一次正面”。补事件为 $A^c=\{TTT\}$，也就是一次正面都没有。根据独立性，
>
> $$P(A^c)=P(TTT)=\frac12\times\frac12\times\frac12=\frac18.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Therefore $P(A)=1-1/8=7/8$. Counting every record with at least one head also gives seven of the eight equally likely records, but the complement requires only one record.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因此 $P(A)=1-1/8=7/8$。逐个数所有至少有一次正面的记录，也会得到八个等可能结果中的七个；用补事件则只需处理一个记录。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The complement rule itself needs no independence assumption. Independence was used only to calculate $P(TTT)$ as a product. If one fair toss is copied into all three positions, the only records are $HHH$ and $TTT$, each with probability $1/2$. The complement rule still gives the correct answer, $1-1/2=1/2$; using $(1/2)^3$ would apply the wrong model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

补事件公式本身不要求独立。上例的独立性只用于把 $P(TTT)$ 写成乘积。如果实际只抛一次公平硬币，再把结果抄到三个位置，就只会出现 $HHH$ 或 $TTT$，各占 $1/2$。补事件公式仍然给出正确答案 $1-1/2=1/2$；使用 $(1/2)^3$ 则是套用了错误模型。

> [!warning] A different event may cover only part of the complement · 另一个事件未必就是完整补事件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The complement of “exactly one head in three tosses” is **zero, two, or three heads**. “No heads” alone misses the cases with two or three. Likewise, the complement of “all trials succeed” is “at least one fails”, not “all fail”. To check a proposed complement, ask whether every possible outcome falls into exactly one of the two events.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> “三次中恰好一次正面”的补事件是**零次、两次或三次正面**；只取“没有正面”，就漏掉了两次和三次的情况。同样，“全部试验成功”的补事件是“至少一次失败”，不是“全部失败”。检查补事件时，要确认每个可能结果都恰好落在这两个事件中的一个。

## Keep the reference space fixed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*补集必须相对于同一个范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a fair die result is already known to be even. Within that known event $B=\{2,4,6\}$, the complement of “the result is six” consists of $\{2,4\}$. Odd outcomes are no longer under consideration. [[条件概率|Conditional probabilities]] also obey the complement rule: if $P(B)>0$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果已经知道公平骰子的点数是偶数，在这个已知事件 $B=\{2,4,6\}$ 内，“点数是六”的补事件就是 $\{2,4\}$，不再考虑奇数结果。[[条件概率]]同样满足补事件法：当 $P(B)>0$ 时，

$$P(A\mid B)=1-P(A^c\mid B).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here conditioning restricts the original complement $A^c$ to $B$, leaving $B\setminus A$. For $A=\{6\}$, this gives $P(A\mid B)=1-2/3=1/3$. The number one refers to the total conditional probability inside $B$. The elementary conditional formula is unavailable if $P(B)=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里，条件 $B$ 把原来的补集 $A^c$ 限制在 $B$ 内，只留下 $B\setminus A$。取 $A=\{6\}$，便得到 $P(A\mid B)=1-2/3=1/3$。式中的一，是 $B$ 内的条件总概率；若 $P(B)=0$，初等条件公式就不可用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The useful decision is therefore not “always take a complement”, but whether the full complement is easier to describe and calculate in the current model. Once it is known, subtracting from one finishes the calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，选择补事件法时要判断的不是“是否总该取补集”，而是当前模型下的**完整补事件是否更容易描述和计算**。算出它的概率以后，再用一减去即可。
