# 补事件把目标概率转成一减去其补集概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The complement rule turns a target probability into one minus its complement probability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子规则
> 因为 $A$ 与 $A^c$ 互斥且合起来是整个样本空间，
> $$P(A^c)=1-P(A).$$
> 当目标事件有许多重叠情形，而“目标没有发生”容易描述时，算补事件通常更省力。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> Because $A$ and $A^c$ are disjoint and together exhaust the sample space,
> $$P(A^c)=1-P(A).$$
> When the target event has many overlapping cases but its failure is easy to describe, the complement is often the simpler calculation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[相互独立|相互独立地]]抛一枚公平硬币三次。“至少一次正面”的补事件是“三次都反面”，所以概率为 $1-(1/2)^3=7/8$。补事件公式本身不要求独立；这里的独立性只用于把“三次都反面”的概率写成 $(1/2)^3$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Toss a fair coin mutually independently three times. The complement of “at least one head” is “all three tosses are tails”, so the probability is $1-(1/2)^3=7/8$. Independence is not needed for the complement rule itself; it is used only to factor the probability of three tails as $(1/2)^3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> “至少一次成功”最自然的补事件是什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> What is the natural complement of “at least one success”?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** “一次也没有成功”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** “No successes at all.”
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
