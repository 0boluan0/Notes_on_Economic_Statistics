# 模型选择必须先固定用途、目标量与将遇到的新数据，脱离这些条件不存在统一的最佳模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Model selection must first fix the use, target, and future data; there is no universally best model without them*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 决策起点
> “哪个模型最好”必须补全为“对谁、为了什么、按什么损失或证据、面对什么新情形最好”。描述当前样本、预测新观测、估计因果效应和表达理论机制不是同一个任务；它们可能合理地保留不同变量，也可能给同一组候选模型排出不同顺序。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Which model is best?” is incomplete until it states for whom, for what purpose, under which loss or evidential standard, and in which future setting. Describing the observed sample, predicting new observations, estimating a causal effect, and expressing a mechanism are different tasks. They may legitimately retain different variables and rank the same candidate models differently.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先写出选择问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Write the selection problem first*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在计算任何分数前，至少固定四件事：

1. **用途：** description、prediction、causal estimation，还是 mechanism communication；
2. **目标：** 要解释或预测的结果、因果 estimand，或需要保留的理论参数；
3. **部署情形：** 新时间、新个体、新群组，还是当前总体中的同类观测；
4. **评价证据：** 样本外损失、预先设定的限制检验、似然准则、识别论证，或可解释性约束。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before computing a score, fix the intended use, the target outcome or estimand, the future setting—new times, people, groups, or observations from the current population—and the evidence that will judge success. That evidence may be out-of-sample loss, a prespecified restriction test, a likelihood criterion, an identification argument, or an interpretability constraint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预测任务可以保留一个稳定、部署时可得的强 proxy，只要它降低目标分布上的损失。因果任务却要先判断该变量是处理前混杂因素、中介还是碰撞点；它提高预测精度，并不能证明它属于合法调整集。反过来，一个理论上必须保留的交互项，即使让一次 validation score 略差，也未必应从机制模型中删除。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A prediction task may retain a stable proxy available at deployment if it reduces loss on the target distribution. A causal task must instead determine whether that variable is a pretreatment confounder, mediator, or collider; predictive gain does not make it an admissible adjustment variable. Conversely, a theoretically required interaction need not be deleted from a mechanism model merely because one validation score deteriorates slightly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 同一数据，两种正确选择
> 用工资数据预测下一位求职者的工资时，可以按未见样本 RMSE 选择模型；估计教育回报时，则要先说明教育系数对应什么反事实比较，并保留识别该比较所需的控制变量。两个模型不同，不表示其中一个计算错了，而是因为选择问题不同。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For forecasting a new applicant's wage, a model may be selected by held-out RMSE. For estimating returns to education, the analysis must first define the counterfactual comparison and retain controls required for identification. Different models need not mean that one calculation is wrong; the selection questions differ.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么不能把“调整 $R^2$、AIC、BIC、交叉验证中数值最优的那个”直接定义为最佳模型？
>
> **答案：** 因为这些准则使用不同信息并服务不同目标，而且都不能自行决定候选变量在实质或因果上是否合法。必须先固定任务，准则才有解释对象。
