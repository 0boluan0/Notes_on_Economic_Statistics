# Row rank equals column rank
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行秩等于列秩*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix can be read as a list of output columns or as a list of equations given by its rows. **Row rank equals column rank** says that these two lists contain the same number of independent directions. The rows and columns need not have the same length or span the same set; the equality concerns their [[维数|dimensions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个矩阵既可以按列理解为输出方向，也可以按行理解为方程的系数。**行秩等于列秩**说明：这两组向量包含相同数量的独立方向。行与列不必一样长，也不必张成同一个集合；相等的是它们的[[维数]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A rectangular example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先看一个矩形矩阵*

$$A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first and third columns, $(1,0)^T$ and $(0,1)^T$, are a [[基|basis]] of the [[列空间|column space]]: the second column is twice the first, and these two chosen columns generate every vector in $\mathbb R^2$. Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], so the entries are written as a column. The two rows $(1,2,0)$ and $(0,0,1)$ are [[线性无关|independent]] and span the [[行空间|row space]] in $\mathbb R^3$. Thus both dimensions are two, even though one space has two-coordinate vectors and the other has three-coordinate vectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一列 $(1,0)^T$ 和第三列 $(0,1)^T$ 构成[[列空间]]的一组[[基]]：第二列是第一列的两倍，而这两根选出的列能生成 $\mathbb R^2$ 的每个向量。这里 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把这些数写成一列。两行 $(1,2,0)$、$(0,0,1)$ [[线性无关]]，并张成位于 $\mathbb R^3$ 的[[行空间]]。所以两个维数都为二，尽管一边的向量有两个坐标，另一边有三个坐标。

> [!abstract] The equality · 相等的是什么
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$, with positive integers $m,n$ and scalar field $\mathbb F$ (in particular $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$). The column rank is $\dim C(A)$; the row rank is $\dim C(A^T)$ after writing the rows as columns. All spans and dimensions use the same field. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$，其中 $m,n$ 是正整数，$\mathbb F$ 是标量域，特别可以是 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。列秩是 $\dim C(A)$；把行写成列向量后，行秩是 $\dim C(A^T)$。所有张成与维数均使用同一个域。则
>
> $$\boxed{\dim C(A^T)=\dim C(A)=\operatorname{rank}(A).}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here [[矩阵秩|matrix rank]] is defined as the dimension of the column space. This theorem justifies computing that same number from the row space.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里[[矩阵秩]]按列空间维数定义。这条定理说明，改从行空间数维数，也得到同一个数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Build the matrix from a column basis
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*用列空间的一组基搭出整个矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $r=\dim C(A)$. If $r=0$, every column is zero, so $A=0$ and its row space also has dimension zero. Suppose $r>0$. Choose $r$ columns of $A$ that form a basis of its column space, and place them in an $m\times r$ matrix $C$. Every column of $A$ is a linear combination of these chosen columns. Put its $r$ coefficients into the corresponding column of an $r\times n$ matrix $R$. By [[Ax的列组合解释|the column-combination meaning of matrix multiplication]],
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $r=\dim C(A)$。若 $r=0$，每一列都为零，所以 $A=0$，行空间维数也为零。下面设 $r>0$。从 $A$ 中选取构成列空间基的 $r$ 列，把它们放进一个 $m\times r$ 矩阵 $C$。$A$ 的每一列都是这些基列的线性组合；把它的 $r$ 个组合系数放到一个 $r\times n$ 矩阵 $R$ 的相应列中。由[[Ax的列组合解释|矩阵乘法的列组合含义]]，

$$A=CR.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the opening example, choose $C=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}$ and $R=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}$. The columns of $R$ record the coefficients $(1,0)^T$, $(2,0)^T$ and $(0,1)^T$ of the three original columns. In the general proof, $R$ is a coefficient matrix; it is not assumed to be a row-reduced matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在开头的例子中，可以取 $C=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}$、$R=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix}$。$R$ 的三列分别记录原三列的系数 $(1,0)^T$、$(2,0)^T$、$(0,1)^T$。一般证明中的 $R$ 只是系数矩阵，并没有假设它是行化简后的矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now read the product by rows. For each $i=1,\ldots,m$, the $i$th row of $A$ is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再按行读这个乘积。对每个 $i=1,\ldots,m$，$A$ 的第 $i$ 行满足

$$\operatorname{row}_i(A)=\sum_{k=1}^{r} C_{ik}\operatorname{row}_k(R).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $C_{ik}$ is the entry of $C$ in row $i$, column $k$. Every row of $A$ therefore lies in the [[张成|span]] of the $r$ rows of $R$. A space generated by $r$ vectors has dimension at most $r$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $C_{ik}$ 是 $C$ 的第 $i$ 行、第 $k$ 列元素。因此 $A$ 的每一行都在 $R$ 的 $r$ 行所[[张成]]的空间中。由 $r$ 个向量生成的空间，维数至多为 $r$，于是

$$\text{row rank}(A)\le \text{column rank}(A).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This inequality has just been proved for every finite matrix. Apply it to $A^T$. Transposing exchanges the row list and the column list, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

刚才的不等式对每个有限矩阵都已证明。把它用到 $A^T$ 上：转置交换行列表与列列表，因此

$$\text{column rank}(A)=\text{row rank}(A^T)
\le \text{column rank}(A^T)=\text{row rank}(A).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two inequalities give equality. This argument uses only a column basis and the multiplication formula; it does not assume the row-rank theorem or the four-subspace dimension formulas along the way.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个方向的不等式合起来就得到相等。论证只使用列空间基和乘法公式，没有在途中预先使用行秩定理或四空间维数公式。

## Reading the same count after elimination
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*消元后怎样读出同一个数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reversible row operations preserve the row space. In [[行最简形|reduced row echelon form]], the nonzero rows have distinct leading [[主元|pivot]] positions and are independent, so they form a row-space basis. The corresponding pivot positions select a column-space basis from the **original** matrix. These are the [[RREF读取子空间基|different basis-reading rules]] behind the familiar computation “rank equals the number of pivots.” Equal ranks do not mean that row operations preserve the actual column space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可逆行操作保持行空间。在[[行最简形]]中，非零行具有不同的首个[[主元]]位置，因而无关，构成行空间基。相应主元位置则要返回**原矩阵**选出列空间基。这就是“秩等于主元数”背后的[[RREF读取子空间基|不同取基规则]]。秩相等，并不表示行操作保持列空间的具体成员。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even for a square matrix the two spaces can differ. For $B=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\0&0\end{bmatrix}$, the column space is the line through $(1,0)^T$, whereas the row space, written as columns, is the line through $(1,2)^T$. Each has dimension one. For complex matrices the theorem still uses ordinary transpose. Conjugation also preserves independence, so $\operatorname{rank}(A^*)=\operatorname{rank}(A)$, where $A^*$ is the conjugate transpose; this dimension equality does not identify $C(A^T)$ with $C(A^*)$. Their different roles in complex orthogonality are explained in [[行空间|the row-space definition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使是方阵，两个空间也可能不同。对 $B=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\0&0\end{bmatrix}$，列空间是 $(1,0)^T$ 所在直线，行空间写成列后却是 $(1,2)^T$ 所在直线；两者都是一维。复矩阵的这条定理仍使用普通转置。共轭也保持线性无关性，所以 $\operatorname{rank}(A^*)=\operatorname{rank}(A)$，其中 $A^*$ 是共轭转置；但维数相同不等于 $C(A^T)=C(A^*)$。两者在复数正交几何中的不同角色见[[行空间]]。

> [!question]- Equal counts, different spaces · 数量相同，空间不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A $2\times5$ matrix has column rank $2$. What is its row rank? **Answer:** $2$. Its column space lies in $\mathbb F^2$, while its row space lies in $\mathbb F^5$; the theorem does not make them the same set.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个 $2\times5$ 矩阵的列秩为 $2$，行秩是多少？**答案：** 也是 $2$。列空间位于 $\mathbb F^2$，行空间位于 $\mathbb F^5$；定理没有把它们变成同一个集合。
