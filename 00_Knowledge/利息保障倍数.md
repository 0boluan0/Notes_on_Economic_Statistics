# 利息保障倍数是息税前利润对明确利息支出口径的覆盖倍数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interest coverage is earnings before interest and tax divided by a clearly specified interest-charge measure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本课用 $EBIT/I$ 衡量一元利息支出对应多少息税前利润。CPA取 $EBIT=净利润+费用化利息+所得税费用$，而分母 $I$ 包括本期费用化和资本化利息；两处“利息”并非必然同一个数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course uses $EBIT/I$ to measure earnings before interest and tax per unit of interest charges. CPA constructs EBIT as net income plus expensed interest plus income-tax expense, while $I$ includes both expensed and capitalised interest for the period. The two interest amounts need not coincide.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ABC假定无资本化利息，故 $(136+110+64)/110\approx2.8182$。若另有资本化利息10而其他给定量不变，分母应为120，覆盖为 $310/120\approx2.5833$；不应把未减少当期利润的10再加回净利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ABC assumes no capitalised interest, giving $(136+110+64)/110\approx2.8182$. If capitalised interest of 10 is added while the other stated figures stay fixed, the denominator becomes 120 and coverage is about 2.5833. The extra 10 did not reduce current profit and should not be added back again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

覆盖低于1提示本期该利润不足覆盖利息，但实际支付还依赖现金与融资；高倍数不保证本金可偿。$I=0$时不计算通常倍数，负EBIT需明确亏损含义。与[[现金流量利息保障倍数]]比较时保持利息范围和期间一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Coverage below one indicates insufficient earnings on this basis, while actual payments also depend on cash and financing. High coverage does not guarantee principal repayment. No usual multiple is defined at zero interest, and negative EBIT indicates losses. Match interest scope and periods when comparing with [[现金流量利息保障倍数|cash-flow interest coverage]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
