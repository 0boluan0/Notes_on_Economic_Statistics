# 通胀率下降不等于价格水平下降
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A falling inflation rate does not imply a falling price level.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价格水平 $P_t$ 是指数的水平，[[通货膨胀|通胀率]] $\pi_t$ 是其期间变化率。只要 $\pi_t>0$，就有 $P_t>P_{t-1}$；$\pi_t$ 下降但仍为正称通胀放缓，不能据此说发生[[通货紧缩]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The price level $P_t$ is an index level; [[通货膨胀|inflation]] $\pi_t$ is its rate of change. If $\pi_t>0$, then $P_t>P_{t-1}$. Declining but positive inflation is disinflation, not [[通货紧缩|deflation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $100\to104\to106.08$ 对应 4% 再到 2%：第二期涨得较慢，却仍比第一期贵。另一个常见错误是把 AD-AS 图的一次价格水平上移当成此后每期持续通胀；若随后价格保持新水平，后续期间通胀率为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The path $100\to104\to106.08$ gives 4% and then 2% inflation: prices rise more slowly but remain higher. Likewise, a one-time upward shift in the price level in an AD-AS diagram does not imply continuing inflation. If prices then stay at the new level, subsequent inflation is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较前先固定指数、频率和基期。某月同比下降而环比上涨并不矛盾，它们比较的不是同一对价格水平。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the index, frequency, and reference period before comparing rates. Negative year-on-year inflation can coexist with a positive monthly rate because the comparison dates differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
