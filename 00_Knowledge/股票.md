# 股票是表明持有人公司股东身份及相应股权的证券

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stock is a security representing shareholder status and the associated equity rights in a company.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股票把公司的所有权份额表现为可识别的证券份额，持有人据此享有相应的[[股权]]。这里的对象是证券；它承载哪些分配与治理权利，要看股份类别及适用条款，不能仅凭“买了股票”就断定每股都有相同投票权、固定股利或随时向公司退还本金的权利。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Stock represents company ownership in identifiable security units carrying the corresponding [[股权|equity rights]]. The security's distribution and governance rights depend on its class and terms. Owning stock does not by itself imply identical voting rights, a fixed dividend, or a right to demand repayment from the company at any time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，甲向乙购买 10 股，支付 100，价款流向卖方乙，并不因此形成公司欠甲 100 的债务。如果甲有资格领取公司另行宣告并实际支付的每股 2 元现金股利，忽略税费时甲收到 20。卖出收入与公司分配是两条不同的现金流。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose A buys 10 shares from B for 100. The purchase money goes to B; it does not create a company debt of 100 to A. If A is eligible for a cash dividend of 2 per share that the company separately declares and pays, A receives 20 before taxes and fees. Sale proceeds and company distributions are different cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[普通股]]与[[优先股]]是按权利区分的股份类别。股票也不必是一张纸质凭证；例如美国 Delaware 公司法允许无纸质证书股份。该法同时允许符合条件的股份回购与约定赎回，因此“股东没有一般的随时退股权”不等于“公司永远不能回购或赎回”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[普通股|Common stock]] and [[优先股|preferred stock]] distinguish classes by their rights. Stock need not be a paper certificate: Delaware corporate law, for example, permits uncertificated shares. It also permits qualifying repurchases and contractual redemptions. The absence of a general right to withdraw at will is therefore different from a prohibition on all issuer repurchases or redemptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Delaware General Corporation Law, §§151(a)–(b), 151(f), 158 and 160](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc05/index.html) supports class-specific rights, uncertificated shares and the possibility of repurchase or redemption within that jurisdiction; its detailed legal requirements are not universalised.
