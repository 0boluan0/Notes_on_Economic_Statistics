# 计价单位为价格合同和账簿提供共同名义尺度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A unit of account supplies a common nominal scale for prices, contracts, and records*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 计价单位是标示和比较经济价值所使用的共同名义尺度。商品价格、工资、债务合同和会计记录用同一单位表达后，不必为每一对商品分别维护交换比率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A unit of account is the common nominal scale used to quote and compare economic values. Once goods, wages, debts, and accounts are expressed in one unit, a separate exchange ratio is not needed for every pair of goods.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计价单位与实际交付的结算资产可以在概念上分开：合同可能用英镑计价，却通过某种银行存款转账完成；历史上也可能用一种单位记账、用另一种资产支付。但在稳定的现代货币体系中，共同计价与按面值结算通常相互强化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The unit in which an obligation is denominated can conceptually differ from the asset delivered in settlement: a contract may be quoted in pounds and paid by transferring a bank deposit. Historically, one item could be used for accounting and another for payment. In a stable modern monetary system, however, common denomination and settlement at par usually reinforce each other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- “价格用某单位写”不自动说明每笔支付都交付该单位的实物现金。
- 计价单位解决可比性；交换媒介解决实际转移，两项功能相关但不相同。
- 高通胀会削弱共同尺度跨期的可解释性，即使标价仍写同一货币单位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Quoting a price in a currency does not imply that physical cash of that currency is delivered in every payment.
- A unit of account solves comparability; a medium of exchange enables transfer. The functions are related but distinct.
- High inflation weakens the intertemporal meaning of the common scale even if prices remain quoted in the same currency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 商店以英镑标价、顾客刷卡支付时，计价单位和实际支付载体分别是什么？
>
> **答案：** 英镑是计价单位；实际支付通常是银行存款负债在账户之间的转移。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A shop quotes prices in pounds and the customer pays by card. What are the unit of account and the payment asset?
>
> **Answer:** Sterling is the unit of account; the payment normally transfers a commercial-bank deposit between accounts.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
