# 冲销决定外汇干预是否改变本币货币条件但不保证持久汇率效果或绕开不可能三角
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sterilization determines whether foreign-exchange intervention changes domestic monetary conditions, but it does not guarantee a lasting exchange-rate effect or evade the trilemma*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子机制边界
> 央行买入外汇并以本币准备金结算时，通常同时增加外币资产和准备金负债；若不做抵消操作，这是未冲销干预，会改变基础货币或短期货币条件。若央行用国内资产交易或其他流动性工具抵消该影响，使基础货币或操作利率回到原目标，则是冲销干预。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When a central bank purchases foreign exchange and settles with domestic reserves, it normally increases both foreign assets and reserve liabilities. Without an offset, this unsterilized intervention changes the monetary base or short-term monetary conditions. If domestic-asset operations or other liquidity tools offset that effect and return the monetary base or operating rate to its prior target, the intervention is sterilized.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最简 T-account 中，未冲销买汇写为“外汇资产 $+X$；准备金负债 $+X$”。传统稀缺准备金框架可通过出售国内证券冲销；在充足准备金、走廊或其他操作框架中，央行可能使用不同工具并瞄准短期利率而非固定准备金数量。冲销的定义应按实际操作目标判断，不能只看某一日准备金是否变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a minimal T-account, an unsterilized FX purchase records foreign assets $+X$ and reserve liabilities $+X$. A traditional scarce-reserves framework may sterilize by selling domestic securities. Ample-reserves, corridor, and other frameworks may use different tools and target a short rate rather than a fixed reserve quantity. Sterilization must therefore be judged against the operating target, not from one day's reserve movement alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 冲销后的可能渠道不等于保证有效

冲销后，干预仍会改变公众持有的本外币资产构成，并可能通过 portfolio-balance 渠道影响所需风险补偿，或通过 signaling 渠道改变对未来政策的预期。但资产若接近完全替代、操作规模较小或信号不可信，独立效果可能很弱或短暂；有效性还依赖市场深度、执行方式、信誉和宏观环境。储备变化本身还混有估值、利息和其他官方流量，不能机械等同于当期干预量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After sterilization, intervention still changes the currency composition of assets held by the public and may affect required risk compensation through a portfolio-balance channel or expectations through a signalling channel. But if assets are close substitutes, the operation is small, or the signal lacks credibility, its independent effect may be weak or temporary. Effectiveness depends on market depth, execution, credibility, and the macroeconomic setting. Reserve changes also include valuation, income, and other official flows, so they cannot be equated mechanically with current intervention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

冲销不会创造 [[不可能三角]] 的“第四个角”。在高资本流动和硬汇率承诺下，持续维持偏离锚货币的独立利率仍会带来反复资本流动、carry cost、资产负债表和储备压力；央行最终仍需调整利率路径、汇率承诺或资本开放程度。干预是一种执行工具，不是对制度约束的逻辑反例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sterilization does not create a fourth corner of the trilemma. With high capital mobility and a hard exchange-rate commitment, persistently maintaining an interest-rate path away from the anchor still generates repeated capital flows, carry costs, balance-sheet exposure, and reserve pressure. The authority eventually has to adjust the rate path, the exchange-rate commitment, or capital openness. Intervention is an implementation tool, not a logical exception to the institutional constraint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 央行买入外汇后又卖出等额国内证券，能否据此断言汇率一定改变且货币政策完全独立？
>
> **答案：** 不能。第二步说明货币影响被冲销；汇率效果仍取决于资产替代、规模、信号和环境，且冲销不解除资本流动、汇率承诺与独立政策之间的长期约束。
