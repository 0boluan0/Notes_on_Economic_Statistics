# 到期收益率不自动等于按实际现金流计算的实现复合收益率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Yield to maturity does not automatically equal the compound return realised from actual cash flows*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[到期收益率]]是价格与承诺支付之间的单一内部收益率。这个定义本身不需要先假定息票再投资；若承诺都按时兑现，把每笔收入放在实际收到的时点计算 IRR，仍会得到购入时的 YTM。但把全部收入累积到某个持有期末，再计算财富的复合增长率，是另一个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[到期收益率|YTM]] is the single internal rate linking price to promised payments. Its definition does not first require coupon reinvestment. If every promise is fulfilled on schedule, the IRR of payments recorded at their receipt dates remains the purchase YTM. Accumulating all proceeds to a holding horizon and measuring compound wealth growth is a different question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设以全价 $P_0>0$ 买入、持有 $T$ 年，没有额外外部注资或提款，把息票及出售／到期所得连同再投资结果都计入终值 $W_T>0$，则实现年复合收益率为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose purchase dirty price is $P_0>0$, the holding horizon is $T$ years, there are no additional external contributions or withdrawals, and terminal wealth $W_T>0$ includes coupons, sale or redemption proceeds, and reinvestment results. The realised annual compound return is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$R_{\rm realised}=\left(\frac{W_T}{P_0}\right)^{1/T}-1.$$

若无违约、无提前赎回、持有至到期且忽略费用税收，把每笔息票都按与原 YTM 相同的有效率再投资至到期，足以使这个复合收益率等于同口径 YTM。更一般地，真正需要的是合计终值恰好等于 $P_0(1+y)^T$；并非每一段再投资利率相同才可能偶然得到这个终值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With no default or early redemption, holding to maturity and ignoring fees and taxes, reinvesting every coupon to maturity at the original YTM's effective rate is sufficient for equality. More generally, the required condition is terminal wealth equal to $P_0(1+y)^T$; different reinvestment paths could also happen to produce that total.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $P_0=5/1.06+105/1.06^2\approx98.1666$，一年后收 $5$、两年后收 $105$，购入 YTM 为年有效 $6\%$。首笔 $5$ 若按 $6\%$ 再投资一年，终值为 $110.30$，实现收益率为 $6\%$；若只保留现金、收益率为 $0$，终值为 $110$，实现年复合收益率约为 $5.8557\%$，但原两笔按期支付的 IRR 仍是 $6\%$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $P_0=5/1.06+105/1.06^2\approx98.1666$, payments of 5 in one year and 105 in two years give a 6% effective annual YTM. Reinvesting the first 5 for one year at 6% gives terminal wealth 110.30 and a 6% compound return. Holding it as zero-yield cash gives terminal wealth 110 and a compound return of about 5.8557%, although the IRR of the original timely payments remains 6%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

提前出售引入未知售价；违约改变实际支付；费用税收改变净收入。[[零息债券]]若按约支付、持有至到期且忽略费用税收，没有中间息票再投资问题，同口径的终值复合收益率便等于购入 YTM。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Early sale introduces an unknown sale price; default changes payments; fees and taxes change net proceeds. A [[零息债券|zero-coupon bond]] held to maturity and paid as promised, ignoring fees and taxes, has no interim coupon-reinvestment issue, so its terminal-wealth compound return equals its purchase YTM on the same basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [FINRA，Understanding Bond Yield and Return，Key Terms 与 Figuring Return](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/bond-yield-return)：支持 YTM 的现值定义、到期／出售所得、息票、再投资和费用影响实现回报。其一般性再投资叙述在本页用“现金流 IRR／终值复合增长”两个量明确区分。
- [Bank of England，Yield curve terminology and concepts，脚注 2–3](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/statistics/yield-curves/terminology-and-concepts)：支持零息单笔支付与附息债单一 YTM 定义。两年算例直接用终值恒等式复算，未沿用简单除以年数的单利年化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- FINRA's *Understanding Bond Yield and Return* supports the PV definition and the effects of sale or redemption proceeds, coupons, reinvestment, and costs. This page distinguishes cash-flow IRR from terminal-wealth compound growth to make the reinvestment issue precise.
- Bank of England's terminology page, footnotes 2–3, supports the zero-coupon payment structure and coupon-bond YTM. The two-year example was recalculated from terminal wealth rather than simple annualisation by division.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
