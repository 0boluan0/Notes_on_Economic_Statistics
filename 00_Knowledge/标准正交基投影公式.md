# Projection from an orthonormal basis
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*标准正交基投影公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a plane or another subspace has been described by perpendicular unit directions. To find the closest vector in that subspace to a given vector, measure its component along each direction and add those components. The matrix formula $QQ^T$ packages these two steps: first read the coordinates, then rebuild the projected vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一个平面或其他子空间已经用彼此垂直的单位方向描述，要找该子空间中离给定向量最近的向量，就量出它沿每个方向的分量，再把这些分量加起来。矩阵公式 $QQ^T$ 把这两步合在一起：先读出坐标，再合成投影向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $q_1,\ldots,q_r\in\mathbb R^m$ be [[标准正交组|orthonormal vectors]]: each has length 1, and distinct vectors have zero [[向量点积|dot product]]. Put them into the columns of $Q=[q_1\ \cdots\ q_r]$, so $Q^TQ=I_r$, with $T$ denoting [[矩阵转置|transpose]] and $I_r$ the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. Their [[列空间|column space]] $S=C(Q)$ consists of all their linear combinations. For any $b\in\mathbb R^m$, the [[正交投影|orthogonal projection]] $p$ is the unique nearest vector to $b$ in $S$. It is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $q_1,\ldots,q_r\in\mathbb R^m$ 是[[标准正交组]]：每个长度为1，不同向量的[[向量点积|点积]]为零。把它们排成矩阵 $Q=[q_1\ \cdots\ q_r]$ 的列，就有 $Q^TQ=I_r$，其中 $T$ 为[[矩阵转置|转置]]、$I_r$ 为[[单位矩阵]]。其[[列空间]] $S=C(Q)$ 包含这些列的全部线性组合。对任意 $b\in\mathbb R^m$，[[正交投影]] $p$ 是 $S$ 中距 $b$ 最近的唯一向量，公式为

$$
p=QQ^Tb=\sum_{i=1}^{r}q_i(q_i^Tb),\qquad P=QQ^T=\sum_{i=1}^{r}q_iq_i^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The number $q_i^Tb$ is the signed coordinate along the unit direction $q_i$. Multiplying it by $q_i$ makes a vector in the original $m$-dimensional space. The product $q_iq_i^T$ is an $m\times m$ matrix, whereas $q_i^Tq_i$ is the scalar 1; their order matters. $P$ is the matrix of the projection operation, and $p=Pb$ is its result on this particular input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数 $q_i^Tb$ 是沿单位方向 $q_i$ 的带符号坐标，再乘 $q_i$ 就变回原来 $m$ 维空间中的向量。$q_iq_i^T$ 是 $m\times m$ 矩阵，而 $q_i^Tq_i$ 是标量1，乘法顺序不能交换。$P$ 是投影操作的矩阵，$p=Pb$ 是这次输入得到的投影向量。

> [!example] Project onto a slanted plane / 投影到倾斜平面
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $q_1=(1,1,0)^T/\sqrt2$, $q_2=(0,0,1)^T$ and $b=(3,1,4)^T$. The two coordinates are $q_1^Tb=2\sqrt2$ and $q_2^Tb=4$. Rebuilding gives $p=2\sqrt2q_1+4q_2=(2,2,4)^T$. The residual $b-p=(1,-1,0)^T$ has zero dot product with both basis vectors. The plane consists of vectors whose first two coordinates are equal; projection replaces 3 and 1 by their mean 2 while retaining the third coordinate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $q_1=(1,1,0)^T/\sqrt2$、$q_2=(0,0,1)^T$、$b=(3,1,4)^T$。两个坐标为 $q_1^Tb=2\sqrt2$、$q_2^Tb=4$，合成得 $p=2\sqrt2q_1+4q_2=(2,2,4)^T$。残差 $b-p=(1,-1,0)^T$ 与两条基向量的点积都为零。这个平面中的向量前两个坐标相等；投影把3和1换成平均值2，同时保留第三坐标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the formula works
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么公式成立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The vector $p=QQ^Tb$ is a combination of the columns, so it belongs to $S$. Moreover, $Q^T(b-p)=Q^Tb-Q^TQQ^Tb=0$. Thus the residual is perpendicular to every column, and hence to every vector in $S$. The [[最佳逼近与正交残差|nearest-point characterization]] says exactly that these two properties make $p$ the unique nearest vector. They also show that $P$ keeps every vector already in $S$ and sends the [[正交补|orthogonal complement]] $S^\perp$ to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$p=QQ^Tb$ 是各列的组合，所以位于 $S$。又有 $Q^T(b-p)=Q^Tb-Q^TQQ^Tb=0$，故残差垂直于每一列，也就垂直于 $S$ 中每个向量。[[最佳逼近与正交残差|最近点判据]]正好说明，这两个条件使 $p$ 成为唯一最近向量。它们也表明，$P$ 保留已经位于 $S$ 的向量，把[[正交补]] $S^\perp$ 中的向量送到零。

$$
P^T=P,\qquad P^2=Q(Q^TQ)Q^T=P.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are symmetry and idempotence: projecting again does nothing further. They are the [[正交投影矩阵判别|matrix criterion for orthogonal projection]]. Its range is $S$ and its null space is $S^\perp$. Choosing a different orthonormal basis of the same subspace changes $Q$ and the coordinate vector $Q^Tb$, but not the projection matrix $P$: the nearest vector is determined by the subspace, not its chosen basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这分别是对称性与幂等性：投影一次之后，再投影不会继续改变。这也是[[正交投影矩阵判别|正交投影的矩阵判据]]。其值域为 $S$，零空间为 $S^\perp$。为同一子空间换一组标准正交基，会改变 $Q$ 和坐标向量 $Q^Tb$，但不改变投影矩阵 $P$；最近向量由子空间决定，而非由所选基决定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Dimension and inner-product boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 尺寸边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$Q$ has size $m\times r$, so $Q^Tb$ has $r$ coordinates while $Pb$ has $m$ coordinates. When $r<m$, $Q^TQ=I_r$ but $QQ^T\ne I_m$: $P$ has rank $r$ and removes the directions perpendicular to $S$. When $r=m$, $Q$ is a [[正交矩阵|square orthogonal matrix]], $S=\mathbb R^m$, and $P=I_m$. Projection then leaves every vector unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$Q$ 的尺寸为 $m\times r$，所以 $Q^Tb$ 有 $r$ 个坐标，$Pb$ 却有 $m$ 个坐标。若 $r<m$，虽有 $Q^TQ=I_r$，却有 $QQ^T\ne I_m$：$P$ 的秩为 $r$，会消去垂直于 $S$ 的方向。若 $r=m$，$Q$ 成为[[正交矩阵|方阵正交矩阵]]，$S=\mathbb R^m$，故 $P=I_m$，每个向量都保持不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The columns must be both perpendicular and unit length for the formula $QQ^T$ to apply directly. For example, using the single column $q=(2,0)^T$ gives $qq^T=\operatorname{diag}(4,0)$, which sends $(1,1)^T$ to $(4,0)^T$ rather than its nearest point $(1,0)^T$. Orthonormalize the basis, or use the [[满列秩投影公式|formula for a general independent basis]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$QQ^T$ 能直接使用的前提，是列既垂直又有单位长度。例如单列 $q=(2,0)^T$ 给出 $qq^T=\operatorname{diag}(4,0)$，会把 $(1,1)^T$ 送到 $(4,0)^T$，而最近点应为 $(1,0)^T$。应先把基标准正交化，或使用[[满列秩投影公式|一般独立基的公式]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For complex vectors with their usual Euclidean inner product, replace transpose by conjugate transpose throughout: $Q^*Q=I_r$ and $P=QQ^*$. For example, $q=(1,i)^T/\sqrt2$, where $i^2=-1$, has $q^*q=1$ but $q^Tq=0$. Ordinary transpose therefore cannot supply the required coordinates. The range and orthogonal-complement conclusions remain the same with this inner product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对使用通常欧氏内积的复向量，必须把转置全部换成共轭转置：$Q^*Q=I_r$，$P=QQ^*$。例如 $q=(1,i)^T/\sqrt2$，其中 $i^2=-1$，满足 $q^*q=1$，却有 $q^Tq=0$，所以普通转置不能给出所需坐标。值域与正交补的结论在该内积下保持不变。
