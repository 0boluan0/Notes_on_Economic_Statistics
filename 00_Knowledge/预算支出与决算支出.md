# 预算支出是获批的支出安排而决算支出是相应年度按既定口径报告的实际执行额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Budgeted expenditure is an approved spending allocation; the outturn reports actual execution for that year on the stated accounting basis.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预算支出回答“批准安排花多少”，决算支出回答“这一年度实际执行多少”。这里说的是已获批准的预算；尚未批准的草案、部门申请和预测数，需要另加标签。决算中的“实际”也依赖现金或权责发生等记账口径，并不自动等于已经交付了多少公共服务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The approved budget states how much spending is authorised; the outturn records execution. Drafts, requests and forecasts require separate labels. Actual expenditure depends on the accounting basis and does not by itself measure services delivered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断执行偏差时，要明确比较初始预算还是年内调整后的预算。设某项目同一年度、同一范围的初始预算为 100，调整预算为 120，决算为 114：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose between the original and revised budget as the comparator. Suppose one project has an original budget of 100, a revised budget of 120 and an outturn of 114, with identical period and scope:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{114}{100}=114\%,\qquad
\frac{114}{120}=95\%.
$$

这表示实际支出高于初始计划 14%，但比调整后安排少 6，即低 5%。两个百分比可以同时正确；若只说“超预算 14%”，读者无法知道它是否超过最终授权。项目是否按时完工、质量是否达标，还需要产出和效果证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Actual spending is 14% above the original plan but 6, or 5%, below the revised allocation. Both percentages are correct. “14% over budget” alone does not establish an overrun of final authorisation. Completion and quality require output and outcome evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

跨年计算 [[财政支出增长率]] 时，也要用可比序列，例如连续两年的同口径决算；不要把上年初始预算与本年实际支出直接当作同比变化。若报表范围或记账基础改变，先做衔接表再比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When calculating [[财政支出增长率|expenditure growth]], use comparable observations, such as two years' outturns. Changes in coverage or accounting basis require reconciliation before comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
