# Dangling-node transitions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

悬空节点转移

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A dangling vertex has no outgoing link to follow. A [[图上随机游走|graph random walk]] therefore needs an extra rule for what happens there. If the walker is to remain in the model at the next time step, the rule must distribute all its probability somewhere: it may stay, return to another vertex, or restart according to a specified distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
悬空节点没有可供下一步沿着走的出链。因此，[[图上随机游走]] 必须另外规定到了这里怎么办。若下一时刻游走者仍留在模型里，就必须把全部概率分配到某些状态：可以原地停留、返回其他顶点，也可以按指定分布重新出发。

> [!example] One missing row / 缺了一整行的规则
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose the only link is $A\to B$. The first row is $(0,1)$: starting at $A$, the walker reaches $B$ with certainty. Vertex $B$ has no outgoing link. Filling its row with zeros gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 假设图中只有一条边 $A\to B$。第一行是 $(0,1)$：从 $A$ 出发，必定到达 $B$。但 $B$ 没有出链。若把它的行填成零，就得到
> $$
> H=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> (1,0)H=(0,1),\qquad (1,0)H^2=(0,0).
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After two steps the displayed mass sums to zero. It is no longer a probability distribution over $\{A,B\}$. The zero row has not said “stay at $B$”; staying would require row $(0,1)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 两步后所显示的质量总和成了零，已经不是 $\{A,B\}$ 上的概率分布。零行不表示“留在 $B$”；留在 $B$ 应写成 $(0,1)$。

## Completing a probability row
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
补全概率行

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]]: $P_{uv}$ is the probability of moving from $u$ to $v$. On a finite vertex set $V$, a vertex $u$ with [[度数、入度与出度|out-degree]] $d^+(u)=0$ makes the uniform-outgoing formula $1/d^+(u)$ undefined. Choose a probability row $r_u$ and set
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
采用 [[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]]：$P_{uv}$ 表示从 $u$ 走到 $v$ 的概率。在有限顶点集合 $V$ 上，顶点 $u$ 若 [[度数、入度与出度|出度]] $d^+(u)=0$，均匀出边公式 $1/d^+(u)$ 就没有定义。此时选择一个概率行 $r_u$，令

$$
P_{uv}=r_u(v),\qquad r_u(v)\ge0,\qquad \sum_{v\in V}r_u(v)=1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $r_u(v)$ is the probability assigned to destination $v$ when the current vertex is $u$. The replacement may allow moves not present in the original link graph: that is part of the new model. To define a [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|time-homogeneous kernel]], choose this row independently of time and earlier history. A weighted walk needs the same decision when its total outgoing weight is zero, even if some drawn edges carry zero weight. Having no incoming links is a different property and does not itself leave the outgoing row undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里的 $r_u(v)$ 是当前位于 $u$ 时分给目的地 $v$ 的概率。替代规则可能允许原始链接图中没有的移动，这正是新模型的一部分。若要定义 [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|时间齐次转移核]]，这一行的选择就不能随时刻或更早历史改变。加权游走若总出权重为零，也需要相同的处理，即使图上还画着权重为零的边。没有入链是另一个性质，本身不会使出发概率行缺失。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Different repairs describe different walks
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
不同修补对应不同游走

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the first row $(0,1)$ in the example. Three possible choices for the missing row give three different distributions after two steps from $A$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
保持例子中的第一行 $(0,1)$ 不变。对缺失行的三种选择，会使从 $A$ 出发两步后的分布不同：

| Rule / 规则 | Row at $B$ / $B$ 的概率行 | Distribution after two steps / 两步后分布 |
|---|---|---|
| Stay at $B$ / 留在 $B$ | $(0,1)$ | $(0,1)$ |
| Return to $A$ / 返回 $A$ | $(1,0)$ | $(1,0)$ |
| Restart uniformly / 均匀重新出发 | $(1/2,1/2)$ | $(1/2,1/2)$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first choice makes $B$ an [[吸收状态|absorbing state]]: after reaching it, the walker stays forever. The second makes the path alternate $A,B,A,B,\ldots$. The third permits both return and holding. These differences remain after the matrix is computed; they are not interchangeable numerical tricks.

For the third choice, the probability row $(1/3,2/3)$ is a [[Markov稳态分布|stationary distribution]], because multiplication by $P$ leaves it unchanged:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
第一种选择使 $B$ 成为 [[吸收状态]]，一旦到达就永远留在那里。第二种使路径按 $A,B,A,B,\ldots$ 交替。第三种既允许返回，也允许停留。这些是模型本身的差别，不是可互换的计算技巧。

第三种选择下，概率行 $(1/3,2/3)$ 是一个 [[Markov稳态分布]]，因为乘以 $P$ 后保持不变：

$$
(1/3,2/3)\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1/2&1/2\end{pmatrix}=(1/3,2/3).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first and second choices instead have stationary rows $(0,1)$ and $(1/2,1/2)$. In the second case, starting from $A$ still produces alternating distributions; a stationary row does not by itself make every initial distribution converge. The distinction is explained in [[稳态唯一不推收敛|uniqueness of stationarity versus convergence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
前两种选择的稳态行分别为 $(0,1)$ 与 $(1/2,1/2)$。第二种从 $A$ 开始时，分布仍然来回交替；存在稳态行本身不保证任意初始分布都收敛。更完整的区别见 [[稳态唯一不推收敛|唯一稳态与收敛的区别]]。

> [!important] Repairing missing rows and teleporting from every row / 补缺行与全局传送
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Dangling-node completion supplies a rule where following outgoing links was undefined. [[PageRank随机传送|PageRank teleportation]] adds a further restart component at every vertex, including vertices that already had outgoing links. These are separate choices. A personalized replacement distribution can assign zero probability to some vertices; merely making every row sum to one does not guarantee that all vertices communicate or that the distributions converge.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 悬空修补是在“沿出链移动”原本没有定义的地方补规则。[[PageRank随机传送]] 则进一步在每个顶点加入重新出发的成分，也包括原本已有出链的顶点。两者是不同的选择。个性化替代分布可以给某些顶点零概率；仅仅把每行补到总和为 $1$，并不保证所有顶点彼此沟通或分布收敛。

## If stopping is part of the model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若模型本来就允许终止

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero row can deliberately represent disappearance from the recorded vertices. The distribution of the surviving mass then has total mass at most one on the original vertices; it need not be a probability distribution of total mass one. To keep an ordinary Markov chain, add a separate “stopped” state, send a dangling vertex there with probability one, and make that new state absorbing. State explicitly whether reaching a dead end means stopping or restarting.

Uniform restart over all vertices is available on a finite graph and includes the current vertex unless explicitly excluded. On a countably infinite graph, equal probability at every vertex cannot sum to one; a restart rule must instead specify some normalized probability distribution. The defining requirement remains nonnegativity and total mass one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
零行也可以被有意用来表示从所记录的顶点中消失；此时剩余质量描述的是尚未终止的部分，而不是原状态集上总和为 $1$ 的概率分布。若要保留普通 Markov 链形式，可以另加一个“已终止”状态，让悬空顶点以概率 $1$ 进入它，再使这个新状态吸收。到达死端究竟意味着终止还是重启，需要由模型写清。

有限图上可以向所有顶点均匀重启，除非明确排除，否则“所有顶点”包含当前点。可数无穷图上，各顶点取相等概率不能使总和为 $1$，因此必须另外给出一个归一化概率分布。核心要求仍是非负且总质量为 $1$。

## 来源与核验

- [Weber, Markov Chains, §8.3, Example 8.6, PDF p. 36 (printed 32)](https://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/~rrw1/markov/M.pdf#page=36): explicitly replaces a zero-outdegree row by a uniform probability row, then separately adds a global restart mixture. The alternative two-state repairs above are verified by direct multiplication.
- [[01_Math/07-Mathematics for Computer Science/MIT-6-042j-pdf.pdf#page=987|MIT Mathematics for Computer Science, §21.2.2, PDF p. 987 (printed 979)]]: missing outgoing links require an explicit additional rule; its supervertex construction adds an actual state, so it should not be silently identified with a direct restart on the original state set.
- [Levin, Peres and Wilmer, Markov Chains and Mixing Times, §9.1, PDF p. 132 (printed 116)](https://pages.uoregon.edu/dlevin/MARKOV/mcmt2e.pdf#page=132): outgoing-weight normalization explains the zero-denominator boundary. Probability-row completion and the added stopping state follow directly from the transition-kernel definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Weber, Markov Chains, §8.3, Example 8.6，PDF 36／印刷 32](https://www.statslab.cam.ac.uk/~rrw1/markov/M.pdf#page=36)：明确先用均匀概率行补零出度，再另外作全局重启混合；本卡两状态的不同修补由矩阵乘法直接核算。
- [[01_Math/07-Mathematics for Computer Science/MIT-6-042j-pdf.pdf#page=987|MIT Mathematics for Computer Science, §21.2.2，PDF 987／印刷 979]]：核验没有出链时须另定规则；其 supervertex 构造实际新增了一个状态，不能默认为原状态集上的直接重启。
- [Levin、Peres 与 Wilmer, Markov Chains and Mixing Times, §9.1，PDF 132／印刷 116](https://pages.uoregon.edu/dlevin/MARKOV/mcmt2e.pdf#page=132)：总出权重归一化说明零分母边界；补概率行与增加终止状态的合法性则直接按转移核定义核验。
