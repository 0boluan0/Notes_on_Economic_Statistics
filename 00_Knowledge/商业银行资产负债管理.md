# 商业银行资产负债管理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Commercial-bank Balance-sheet Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原主题名称：商业银行经营、监管与货币供给
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Original topic name: Bank Management, Regulation, and Money Supply
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 从银行资产负债表理解盈利、流动性、信用和资本如何相互约束，并解释贷款与存款怎样共同创造。
> **具体锚点：** 长期固定利率贷款由短期可撤回存款融资时，银行赚期限利差，却同时暴露于资金成本上升和集中提款。
> **核心难点：** 资本吸收损失，准备金和流动资产完成结算；两者都限制风险，却不能互相替代。
> **为什么重要：** 高 ROE、快速贷款增长或资本充足率单独看都可能掩盖杠杆、信用质量和流动性脆弱性。

> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Use a bank's balance sheet to understand how profitability, liquidity, credit risk, and capital constrain one another and how loans and deposits are created together.
> **Concrete anchor:** A bank funding long-term fixed-rate loans with short-term withdrawable deposits earns a maturity spread while becoming exposed to rising funding costs and concentrated withdrawals.
> **Central difficulty:** Capital absorbs losses, while reserves and liquid assets settle payments; both constrain risk but cannot substitute for each other.
> **Why it matters:** High ROE, rapid loan growth, or a strong capital ratio can each conceal leverage, weak credit quality, or liquidity fragility when viewed alone.

## 银行资产负债表与利润
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Bank Balance Sheet and Profit*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产含贷款、证券和准备金，负债含存款与批发融资，股本吸收损失。净利息收入取决于资产收益与资金成本及重定价，非息收入和运营/信用损失共同决定利润。高 ROE 可能来自高杠杆而非高质量经营。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assets include loans, securities, and reserves; liabilities include deposits and wholesale funding; equity absorbs losses. Net interest income depends on asset yields, funding costs, and repricing, while non-interest income and operating or credit losses jointly determine profit. High ROE may reflect high leverage rather than high-quality operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本恒等式是资产 = 负债 + 权益。贷款减值先降低资产和利润，再侵蚀权益；提款通常同时降低准备金资产和存款负债。用复式记账追踪冲击，能避免把资本、现金和利润混成一个“资金池”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The basic identity is assets = liabilities + equity. Loan impairment first reduces assets and profit and then erodes equity; a withdrawal usually reduces both reserve assets and deposit liabilities. Tracking shocks through double-entry accounting prevents capital, cash, and profit from being collapsed into one undifferentiated “funding pool.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 流动性管理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Liquidity Management*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

银行持有低收益流动资产以应付提款和结算，也可从市场或央行融资。期限转换带来利差也带来 rollover 和挤兑风险。流动性覆盖率等要求不能替代应急融资和抵押品管理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Banks hold lower-yielding liquid assets to meet withdrawals and settlement and may borrow from markets or the central bank. Maturity transformation creates interest margin as well as rollover and run risk. Liquidity ratios cannot replace contingency funding and collateral management.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

流动性计划应按日内、短期压力和长期稳定融资分别观察现金流，并考虑抵押品是否已占用、市场融资是否在系统压力中同时关闭。账面可出售资产不等于危机时能按无损价格出售。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Liquidity planning should examine intraday flows, short-term stress, and long-term stable funding separately, while checking whether collateral is already encumbered and whether market funding may close simultaneously during system stress. An asset classified as saleable is not guaranteed to sell without loss in a crisis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 信用与资本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Credit Risk and Capital*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预期损失应由定价和拨备覆盖，意外损失由资本吸收。资本充足率以风险加权资产为分母，杠杆率提供不依赖风险权重的后备约束。资本不是锁在金库的现金，而是损失吸收融资来源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expected loss should be covered through pricing and provisions, while capital absorbs unexpected loss. Risk-weighted capital ratios use RWA as the denominator, and the leverage ratio supplies a backstop independent of risk weights. Capital is not cash locked in a vault; it is loss-absorbing finance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增长管理必须同时问新贷款的风险调整收益、所需拨备和资本、流动性消耗及集中度。只追求余额或息差可能在景气期积累同一行业和抵押品风险，并在周期反转时同步恶化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Growth management must jointly assess the risk-adjusted return on new lending, required provisions and capital, liquidity use, and concentration. Targeting balances or spreads alone can accumulate common industry and collateral exposure during booms and deteriorate simultaneously when the cycle turns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 监管逻辑
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Logic of Bank Regulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

存款保险和最后贷款人削弱挤兑，却增加道德风险；资本、流动性、治理、检查和处置要求相互补充。微观审慎保护单家机构，宏观审慎关注共同敞口和顺周期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deposit insurance and lender-of-last-resort facilities reduce runs but increase moral hazard. Capital, liquidity, governance, supervision, and resolution requirements complement one another. Microprudential policy protects individual institutions, while macroprudential policy addresses common exposures and procyclicality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

监管不是用一个比率替代管理。风险权重可能错估，杠杆率不区分资产风险，流动性规则依赖情景，治理和处置决定损失能否及时承认并由正确主体承担。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Regulation does not replace management with a single ratio. Risk weights may be wrong, leverage ratios do not distinguish asset risk, liquidity rules depend on scenarios, and governance and resolution determine whether losses are recognized promptly and assigned to the proper parties.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：贷款损失与提款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Loan Losses and Withdrawals*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一笔 10 的贷款确认全部损失时，贷款资产和权益各减 10，但准备金不必立即变化；客户提款 10 时，存款负债和准备金资产各减 10，但若没有额外损失，权益不变。前者首先是偿付能力冲击，后者首先是流动性冲击，二者可能在恐慌和火售中互相转化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a loan of 10 is written off completely, loan assets and equity each fall by 10, while reserves need not change immediately. If a customer withdraws 10, deposit liabilities and reserve assets each fall by 10, while equity is unchanged absent additional loss. The first is initially a solvency shock and the second a liquidity shock, although panic and fire sales can turn one into the other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 银行资本和准备金有什么区别？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is the difference between bank capital and reserves?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 资本是资产减负债、吸收损失的权益融资；准备金是银行持有的中央银行资产，用于结算和流动性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Capital is equity finance equal to assets minus liabilities and absorbs losses; reserves are central-bank assets held by banks for settlement and liquidity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 银行能创造存款，为什么仍会缺流动性？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a bank that creates deposits still run short of liquidity?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 存款转往别行或被提现时需要结算资产/融资；账面扩张不自动提供外部结算资源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Deposits transferred to another bank or withdrawn require settlement assets or funding; balance-sheet expansion does not automatically provide external settlement resources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 风险加权资本率为何还需要杠杆率补充？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must a risk-weighted capital ratio be supplemented by a leverage ratio?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 风险权重可能低估或被优化，杠杆率对总敞口设简单后备约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Risk weights may understate risk or be optimized around, so the leverage ratio imposes a simple backstop on total exposure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
