# Marx 所说的商品价值是凝结在商品中的抽象人类劳动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For Marx, commodity value is abstract human labour embodied in the commodity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Marx 的理论中，**商品价值（value）**是凝结在商品中的[[抽象劳动|抽象人类劳动]]。不同产品的用途各异，但作为价值，它们都表现社会人类劳动的耗费；价值的这种共同性质，使其能够在商品之间的交换关系中表现出来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Marx's theory, **commodity value** is [[抽象劳动|abstract human labour]] embodied in the commodity. Products differ in their uses, but as values they express expenditure of social human labour. This common character allows value to be expressed in exchange relations between commodities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价值的实体与价值量要分清：前者问“什么形成价值”，后者问“形成了多少”。价值量由生产商品所需的[[社会必要劳动时间]]衡量。同类商品在正常条件下需要两小时生产，某个生产者花了四小时，不能由个人耗时直接把该商品价值翻倍。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish the substance of value from its magnitude: the first asks what forms value, the second how much is formed. Its magnitude is measured by [[社会必要劳动时间|socially necessary labour time]]. If a commodity normally requires two hours, an individual producer's four-hour expenditure does not by itself double its value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价值也不是物品的一种可摸到的自然属性。它在[[Marx交换价值|交换价值]]中取得表达，例如一定量麻布的价值表现为一定数量外套。沿着思想史继续比较时，[[劳动支配与劳动投入|劳动支配量]]问商品能买到多少别人的劳动，本卡则在说明商品价值包含的社会劳动；二者不能只因都使用工时就视为同一尺度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Value is not a tangible natural property of the object. It is expressed in [[Marx交换价值|exchange-value]], as when a quantity of linen expresses its value in a number of coats. For comparison in the history of thought, [[劳动支配与劳动投入|labour commanded]] asks how much of other people's labour a commodity can purchase, whereas this account concerns the social labour embodied in its value. Using hours does not make the measures identical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
