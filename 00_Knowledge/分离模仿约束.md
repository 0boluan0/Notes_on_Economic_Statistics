# 信号能识别类型不等于形成分离均衡，每个类型仍须通过模仿与其他信号的偏离约束
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A signal's identifying the sender's type does not by itself establish a separating equilibrium; every type must still satisfy imitation and other signal-deviation constraints*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分离策略使路径上的信号揭示类型，从而钉住接收者的 posterior；它只解决“接收者能否识别”，没有解决“发送者是否愿意被这样识别”。分离均衡还要求每个类型都宁愿使用自己的规定信号，而不是模仿其他类型或改发任何其他信号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A separating strategy makes on-path signals reveal the sender's type and therefore pins down the receiver's posterior. This solves identification, not the sender's willingness to be identified in that way. A separating equilibrium also requires every type to prefer its prescribed signal to imitating another type or sending any other signal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 逐类型偏离约束
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Type-by-Type Deviation Constraints*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定接收者在信号 $s$ 后的混合反应 $\sigma_2(\cdot\mid s)$，定义类型 $\theta$ 发送 $s$ 的期望收益为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given the receiver's mixed response $\sigma_2(\cdot\mid s)$ after signal $s$, define type $\theta$'s expected payoff from sending $s$ by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
U_1(\theta,s;\sigma_2)
=\sum_{a\in A}\sigma_2(a\mid s)u_1(\theta,s,a).
$$

若纯分离候选把类型 $\theta$ 配给信号 $s^*(\theta)$，发送者的必要最优性条件是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a pure separating candidate assigns signal $s^*(\theta)$ to type $\theta$, sender optimality requires
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
U_1\bigl(\theta,s^*(\theta);\sigma_2\bigr)
\ge
U_1(\theta,s;\sigma_2)
\qquad
\text{for every }s\in S.
$$

这个不等式必须对每个类型分别成立。只检查低类型是否模仿高类型不够；高类型降级模仿低类型、改发未被使用的信号，以及其他所有可行偏离都在约束范围内。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inequality must hold separately for every type. Checking only whether a low type imitates a high type is insufficient: downward imitation by a high type, deviations to unused signals, and every other feasible signal deviation must also be covered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 接收者反应也是约束的一部分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Receiver Responses Are Part of the Constraint*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

路径上的识别使接收者对相应类型形成退化 posterior，但接收者仍须在该 posterior 下作最佳反应。未使用信号后的反应则须由合法的[[路径外信念|路径外 belief]]支持。发送者比较偏离收益时，要使用接收者对每个可能信号规定的完整反应，而不能假设接收者在偏离后继续采取原路径行动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On-path identification gives the receiver a degenerate posterior over the corresponding type, but the receiver must still best respond to that posterior. Responses after unused signals must be supported by admissible [[路径外信念|off-path beliefs]]. When evaluating a deviation, the sender uses the receiver's complete prescribed response to every possible signal, not the response that would have followed on the original path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
