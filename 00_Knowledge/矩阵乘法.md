# Matrix multiplication
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵乘法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Matrix multiplication** combines two successive matrix actions into one. A matrix is a rectangular array of numbers; when it acts on a column of input coordinates, each row gives the weights used to form one output coordinate. Start with these two matrices:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**矩阵乘法**把前后两次矩阵作用合成一次。矩阵是按行列排列的数表；它作用于一列输入坐标时，每一行给出计算某个输出坐标所用的权重。先看两个具体矩阵：

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}3&0\\4&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rule $B$ sends an input $(u,v)^T$ to $(3u,4u+v)^T$. The rule $A$ sends any input $(s,t)^T$ to $(s+2t,t)^T$. Here the superscript $T$ turns a written row into a column; see [[矩阵转置|matrix transpose]]. Apply $B$ first, then $A$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

规则 $B$ 把输入 $(u,v)^T$ 变为 $(3u,4u+v)^T$；规则 $A$ 则把任意输入 $(s,t)^T$ 变为 $(s+2t,t)^T$。上标 $T$ 把横写的行变成列，见[[矩阵转置]]。先做 $B$，再做 $A$，得到

$$
\begin{pmatrix}u\\v\end{pmatrix}
\xrightarrow{\ B\ }
\begin{pmatrix}3u\\4u+v\end{pmatrix}
\xrightarrow{\ A\ }
\begin{pmatrix}3u+2(4u+v)\\4u+v\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}11u+2v\\4u+v\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The combined rule therefore has matrix $AB=\begin{pmatrix}11&2\\4&1\end{pmatrix}$. The rightmost matrix acts first: $(AB)x=A(Bx)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此合成规则的矩阵是 $AB=\begin{pmatrix}11&2\\4&1\end{pmatrix}$。最右边的矩阵先作用，即 $(AB)x=A(Bx)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Computing each entry
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*逐个计算结果中的元素*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The top-left coefficient $11$ came from $1\cdot3+2\cdot4$: pair row $1$ of $A$ with column $1$ of $B$, multiply matching positions, then add. Each output position uses its own row–column pair. For the same example, all four calculations are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左上角系数 $11$ 来自 $1\cdot3+2\cdot4$：把 $A$ 的第一行与 $B$ 的第一列配对，对应位置相乘后求和。结果中每个位置都使用自己的行列配对。上例的四个计算为

$$
AB=
\begin{pmatrix}
1\cdot3+2\cdot4&1\cdot0+2\cdot1\\
0\cdot3+1\cdot4&0\cdot0+1\cdot1
\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}11&2\\4&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

> [!important] Match the inner sizes · 中间尺寸必须匹配
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $A$ has $m$ rows and $n$ columns, and $B$ has $n$ rows and $p$ columns, then $C=AB$ has $m$ rows and $p$ columns. Its entry in row $i$, column $j$ is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $A$ 有 $m$ 行、$n$ 列，$B$ 有 $n$ 行、$p$ 列，则 $C=AB$ 有 $m$ 行、$p$ 列。第 $i$ 行、第 $j$ 列的元素为
>
> $$c_{ij}=\sum_{k=1}^{n}a_{ik}b_{kj},\qquad 1\le i\le m,\quad1\le j\le p.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The indices $i,j$ fix the output position. The index $k$ runs across the chosen row of $A$ and down the chosen column of $B$, adding exactly $n$ products. All sizes here are positive integers; entries may be real or complex numbers, using their ordinary addition and multiplication.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $i,j$ 固定结果位置；$k$ 沿 $A$ 的所选行与 $B$ 的所选列依次走过，正好累加 $n$ 个乘积。这里各尺寸为正整数；元素可以是实数或复数，使用它们通常的加法与乘法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same size rule also follows from the successive actions: $B$ takes $p$ coordinates to $n$, then $A$ takes those $n$ coordinates to $m$. For example, a $2\times3$ matrix can multiply a $3\times4$ matrix to give a $2\times4$ matrix. In the reverse order, a $3\times4$ matrix cannot multiply a $2\times3$ matrix: four entries in a left row cannot pair with two entries in a right column.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从前后作用也能看出尺寸规则：$B$ 把 $p$ 个坐标变成 $n$ 个，$A$ 再把这 $n$ 个变成 $m$ 个。例如，$2\times3$ 可以乘 $3\times4$，结果为 $2\times4$；反序的 $3\times4$ 乘 $2\times3$ 则没有定义，因为左行的四个元素无法与右列的两个元素配对。

> [!warning] Keep the order and use a sum · 保留次序，而且需要求和
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Ordinary matrix multiplication is not multiplication of entries in the same position. In the example, the top-left entry is $11$, not $1\cdot3=3$. Nor can we interchange the factors: direct calculation gives $BA=\begin{pmatrix}3&6\\4&9\end{pmatrix}\ne AB$. Even when both orders exist, they may have different sizes: a $1\times2$ row times a $2\times1$ column gives a $1\times1$ result, while the reverse gives a $2\times2$ result.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 通常的矩阵乘法不是同位置元素相乘。上例左上角为 $11$，不是 $1\cdot3=3$。也不能交换因子：直接计算得到 $BA=\begin{pmatrix}3&6\\4&9\end{pmatrix}\ne AB$。即使两个顺序都有定义，尺寸也可能不同：$1\times2$ 行乘 $2\times1$ 列得到 $1\times1$，反过来则得到 $2\times2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[矩阵乘积逐列作用|columnwise]], [[矩阵乘积逐行作用|rowwise]], and [[矩阵乘积外积展开|outer-product]] views regroup this same calculation. They provide useful ways to read a product without changing its definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[矩阵乘积逐列作用|逐列]]、[[矩阵乘积逐行作用|逐行]]与[[矩阵乘积外积展开|外积和]]读法重新组织的是同一个计算。它们让我们能从不同角度理解乘积，而没有改变定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Such rules preserve addition and scalar multiplication, which makes them [[线性映射|linear maps]]. When matrices represent abstract maps in chosen bases, the intermediate coordinate system must agree; [[复合映射的表示矩阵|the matrix of a composition]] explains that qualification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些规则保持向量加法与数乘，因此属于[[线性映射]]。如果矩阵是在选定基下表示抽象映射，中间坐标系还必须一致，详见[[复合映射的表示矩阵]]。
