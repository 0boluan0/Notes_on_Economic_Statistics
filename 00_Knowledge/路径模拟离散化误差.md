# 路径模拟的时间网格可能漏过障碍或日内极端，必须用网格加密、桥修正或可解析特例核验离散化误差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A path-simulation time grid can miss barriers or intraday extremes; discretisation error must be checked by grid refinement, bridge corrections, or tractable benchmarks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 路径有顺序，不代表路径足够细
> 连续时间模型通常只能在有限网格上计算。若事件可能发生在两个网格点之间，只检查离散端点会系统性漏记触碰、极值或提前触发状态。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An ordered path is not necessarily a sufficiently fine path. Continuous-time models are usually computed on a finite grid, and events between grid points can make endpoint-only simulation systematically miss crossings, extrema, or early triggers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设障碍为 $B$，模拟只观察 $S_{t_j}$。即使相邻端点都低于 $B$，区间 $(t_j,t_{j+1})$ 内的连续路径仍可能先上穿再返回。把“两个端点都没触碰”当作“整段路径没触碰”，会低估敲出概率，并把偏差传入组合价值、VaR 或 ES。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With barrier $B$, two adjacent simulated endpoints may both lie below $B$ even though the continuous path crossed and returned between them. Treating “neither endpoint crossed” as “the interval never crossed” understates knock-out probability and propagates bias into value, VaR, or ES.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

至少应使用一种与模型和产品相匹配的核验：

1. **网格加密。** 逐步缩短时间步长，检查目标风险量是否稳定，而不只检查普通情景的平均价值。
2. **桥或条件穿越修正。** 在端点之间按模型的条件路径分布补计穿越概率；这种修正本身也依赖动态模型正确。
3. **可解析或高精度基准。** 在存在闭式结果、已知 hitting probability 或可信高精度实现的特例上比较偏差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a check appropriate to the model and instrument: refine the grid and monitor the target risk statistic; apply a bridge or conditional-crossing correction when its assumptions hold; or compare with a closed-form, known hitting-probability, or trusted high-precision benchmark.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

时间步长控制离散化偏差，路径数控制给定离散方案下的抽样波动。把路径数从一万增至一百万可以让粗网格结果更稳定，却不会补回网格点之间被遗漏的穿越事件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Time step controls discretisation bias, while path count controls sampling fluctuation under the chosen discretisation. Raising the path count from ten thousand to one million can stabilise a coarse-grid answer without restoring crossings missed between grid points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 障碍期权的风险模拟把路径数增加一百倍后标准误很小，但日度网格改成小时网格时 VaR 明显变化。哪个误差仍未解决？
>
> **答案：** 时间离散化误差。更多路径只减少原日度网格下的抽样误差；网格加密导致结果变化，说明跨步穿越或日内极端仍重要。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** A barrier-option risk simulation has a tiny standard error after increasing path count one hundredfold, but VaR changes materially when a daily grid is replaced by an hourly grid. Which error remains?
>
> **Answer:** Time-discretisation error. More paths only reduce sampling error on the daily grid; sensitivity to grid refinement shows that between-step crossings or intraday extremes remain material.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- 网格收敛不能证明基础动态模型正确；它只检查该模型的数值离散。
- 桥修正依赖相应过程的条件路径分布，不能机械套用到任意跳跃、局部波动率或状态依赖模型。
- 动态对冲还要让模拟网格与实际再平衡规则、交易成本和信息时点一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Grid convergence does not validate the underlying dynamics; it checks only their numerical discretisation.
- Bridge corrections rely on a conditional path law and cannot be transferred mechanically to arbitrary jump, local-volatility, or state-dependent models.
- Dynamic hedging must also align the simulation grid with actual rebalancing rules, transaction costs, and information times.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
