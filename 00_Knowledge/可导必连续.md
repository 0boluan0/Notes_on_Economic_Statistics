# Differentiability implies continuity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*可导必连续*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A finite derivative means that the change in output, divided by a small change in input, settles near one finite number. The output change itself must then become small: it is that controlled ratio multiplied by an input change tending to zero. This is why a function with a derivative at a point cannot have a break in its value there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限导数表示：把输出变化除以一个很小的输入变化，所得比率趋近同一个有限数。于是输出变化本身也必须变小，因为它等于这个受控的比率乘上趋于零的输入变化。这就是为什么函数在一点有导数，就不能在该点发生函数值的断裂。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, take $f(x)=x^2$ at input $a=2$. A nonzero input change $h$ moves us from $2$ to $2+h$. The output change and its ratio to $h$ are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，取 $f(x)=x^2$，考察输入 $a=2$。非零输入增量 $h$ 把输入从 $2$ 移到 $2+h$。输出变化及其与 $h$ 的比率为

$$f(2+h)-f(2)=4h+h^2=(4+h)h,
\qquad \frac{f(2+h)-f(2)}h=4+h.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ratio tends to $4$, so $f'(2)=4$. Multiplying by $h\to0$ makes the output change tend to zero, so $f(2+h)\to f(2)=4$. The general proof uses exactly this factorisation, without assuming a polynomial formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比率趋于 $4$，所以 $f'(2)=4$。再乘上趋于零的 $h$，输出变化便趋于零，因此 $f(2+h)\to f(2)=4$。一般证明用的正是这一分解，并不要求函数是多项式。

> [!important] A local implication · 同一点上的蕴含
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $f$ be a real-valued function defined on an open interval containing $a$. If its [[导数|derivative]] exists as a finite real number,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设实值函数 $f$ 定义在包含 $a$ 的某个开区间上。若它的[[导数]]存在且是有限实数，
>
> $$f'(a)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(a+h)-f(a)}h=L\in\mathbb R,$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> then $f$ is [[一点连续|continuous at $a$]]: nearby outputs approach its actual point value, $f(a+h)\to f(a)$. The nonzero increments $h$ may be positive or negative. The symbol $L$ names the limiting slope, whereas $f(a)$ names the output value; these are different quantities even when their numbers happen to agree.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 那么 $f$ 在[[一点连续|该点连续]]：附近输出趋于它的实际点值，即 $f(a+h)\to f(a)$。非零增量 $h$ 可以为正，也可以为负。$L$ 表示极限斜率，$f(a)$ 表示输出点值；两者是不同的量，即使数值恰好相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Recover the output change from the slope
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么成立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $h\ne0$, call the difference quotient $q(h)=[f(a+h)-f(a)]/h$. Multiplying back by $h$ gives the exact identity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $h\ne0$，把差商记作 $q(h)=[f(a+h)-f(a)]/h$。再乘回 $h$，得到准确的恒等式

$$f(a+h)-f(a)=q(h)h.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiability says that $q(h)\to L$, with $L$ finite. Since $h\to0$, the [[极限定律适用条件|product law for finite limits]] yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可导性保证 $q(h)\to L$，且 $L$ 有限。又因为 $h\to0$，由[[极限定律适用条件|有限极限的乘法法则]]得到

$$\lim_{h\to0}\bigl[f(a+h)-f(a)\bigr]=L\cdot0=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalently, write $x=a+h$. For $x\ne a$, this is the familiar identity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等价地，令 $x=a+h$。对 $x\ne a$，这就是常见的写法

$$f(x)-f(a)=\frac{f(x)-f(a)}{x-a}(x-a).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No division by zero occurs: division is performed at nonzero $h$, before taking the limit. The value at $h=0$ contributes the output difference $f(a)-f(a)=0$, which already meets every positive error tolerance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里没有除以零：先在 $h\ne0$ 时作除法，再取极限。至于 $h=0$，输出差直接等于 $f(a)-f(a)=0$，已经满足任意正的误差要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We can also see explicitly how the finite slope controls all nearby inputs. Because $q(h)\to L$, choose a radius $\eta>0$ such that $0<|h|<\eta$ implies $|q(h)-L|<1$. Thus $|q(h)|<|L|+1$. For a desired output tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, take
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还可以直接看出有限斜率怎样控制附近的所有输入。由 $q(h)\to L$，可以选半径 $\eta>0$，使 $0<|h|<\eta$ 时有 $|q(h)-L|<1$，因此 $|q(h)|<|L|+1$。给定输出容差 $\varepsilon>0$，取

$$\delta=\min\!\left\{\eta,\frac{\varepsilon}{|L|+1}\right\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The minimum chooses the smaller radius, so both requirements hold. Every nonzero $|h|<\delta$ then gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小值选取两个半径中较小的一个，因此两项要求同时成立。对每个满足 $0<|h|<\delta$ 的增量，都有

$$|f(a+h)-f(a)|=|q(h)|\,|h|
<(|L|+1)\delta\le\varepsilon.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Together with the zero error at $h=0$, this is the [[一点连续|definition of continuity]]. It also shows why the word **finite** matters: a ratio growing without bound cannot be controlled by this fixed number $|L|+1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再加上 $h=0$ 时误差为零，这就完整满足[[一点连续|连续性的定义]]。它也解释了为什么必须强调**有限**：若比率无界增大，就不能用固定数 $|L|+1$ 控制它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Endpoints use the allowed side
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*端点按允许的一侧解释*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the left endpoint $a$ of a nondegenerate interval such as $[a,b)$ with $a<b$, a finite **right derivative** uses $h\to0^+$, so only positive increments are tested. The same proof gives continuity relative to that interval, using only inputs from the right. At a right endpoint, use the left derivative and left-side continuity instead. State this convention explicitly; the usual interior derivative tests both sides.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在非退化区间的左端点 $a$，例如 $a<b$ 时的 $[a,b)$，有限的**右导数**使用 $h\to0^+$，只检查正增量。同样的证明给出相对于这个区间的连续性，只从右侧趋近。右端点则对应左导数与左侧连续。使用单侧约定时要明确说出；通常的内点导数检查两侧。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the function is defined on both sides, a right derivative alone does not establish two-sided continuity. For example, let $g(x)=x$ for $x\ge0$ and $g(x)=1$ for $x<0$. At zero the right difference quotient is $1$, but values approaching from the left stay at $1$ instead of approaching $g(0)=0$. The two-sided theorem's hypothesis has not been met.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果函数在两侧都有定义，只有右导数并不能保证双侧连续。例如，令 $x\ge0$ 时 $g(x)=x$，$x<0$ 时 $g(x)=1$。零点的右差商恒为 $1$，但左侧函数值一直是 $1$，并不趋于 $g(0)=0$。这时没有满足双侧定理的前提。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the logical direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 结论的方向不能倒过来

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a statement about the **same point**, not a promise about other points. Its contrapositive is also valid: discontinuity at an interior point rules out a finite derivative there. The converse is false: continuity alone does not guarantee a derivative, as the absolute-value example in [[连续不必可导|continuity without differentiability]] shows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个结论针对**同一个点**，并不保证其他点的情形。逆否命题也成立：如果在某个内点不连续，就不可能在该点有有限导数。逆命题则不成立：仅有连续性不能保证可导，[[连续不必可导]]中的绝对值反例说明了这一点。

> [!question]- Apply the implication · 用一次逻辑方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A function has a jump at an interior point. Is it necessary to compute its difference quotient to rule out differentiability there? No: the jump violates continuity, so the contrapositive already rules out a finite derivative. This does not say that every failure of differentiability is a jump.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 函数在某个内点有跳跃，还必须计算差商才能排除可导吗？不必：跳跃破坏连续性，逆否命题已经排除了有限导数。但这不表示所有不可导情形都是跳跃。
