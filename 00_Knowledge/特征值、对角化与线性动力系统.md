# 特征值、对角化与线性动力系统
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Eigenvalues, diagonalisation, and linear dynamical systems*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 找出线性变换下方向不变、只被缩放的模式，并用它们解释矩阵幂和长期动态。
> **具体锚点：** 若 $Ax=\lambda x$，则 $A^kx=\lambda^kx$；对该模式，长期行为直接由 $|\lambda|$ 或 $\operatorname{Re}\lambda$ 控制。
> **核心难点：** 特征值重复不代表一定不可对角化；需比较代数重数和特征空间维数。边界特征值也需检查 Jordan 结构。
> **为什么重要：** 差分/微分方程、Markov 链、稳定性、PCA 和迭代算法都在分解这些模式。
> **继续：** 先找特征值与特征空间，再判断能否对角化，最后把初始状态分解到各模式上。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Eigenvectors identify directions preserved by a linear map up to scaling, making matrix powers and long-run dynamics intelligible.
> **Concrete anchor:** If $Ax=\lambda x$, then $A^kx=\lambda^kx$; the mode's long-run behaviour follows directly from $|\lambda|$ in discrete time or $\operatorname{Re}\lambda$ in continuous time.
> **Central difficulty:** A repeated eigenvalue need not prevent diagonalisation; compare algebraic multiplicity with eigenspace dimension. Boundary eigenvalues also require attention to Jordan structure.
> **Why it matters:** Difference and differential equations, Markov chains, stability, PCA, and iterative algorithms decompose into these modes.
> **Continue with:** Find eigenvalues and eigenspaces, test diagonalisation, and decompose the initial state into the resulting modes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 特征值与特征向量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Eigenvalues and eigenvectors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$Ax=\lambda x$ 寻找方向不变的向量。特征值由 $\det(A-\lambda I)=0$ 得到，但特征向量必须回代求零空间。迹等于特征值之和、行列式等于特征值之积（计代数重数），可用于检查计算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$Ax=\lambda x$ seeks vectors whose direction is unchanged. Eigenvalues solve $\det(A-\lambda I)=0$, but eigenvectors require computing the null space for each value. Trace equals the sum and determinant the product of eigenvalues, counting algebraic multiplicity, which provides useful checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 对角化与矩阵幂
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagonalisation and matrix powers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若有 n 个线性无关特征向量，$A=S\Lambda S^{-1}$，于是 $A^k=S\Lambda^kS^{-1}$。重复特征值不自动导致不可对角化，关键是几何重数是否足够。不可对角化时 Jordan 块会给动态增加多项式因子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $n$ independent eigenvectors, $A=S\Lambda S^{-1}$ and $A^k=S\Lambda^kS^{-1}$. Repeated eigenvalues do not automatically prevent diagonalisation; what matters is whether the eigenspaces supply enough independent vectors. A Jordan block introduces polynomial factors into the dynamics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 线性动力系统
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Linear dynamical systems*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

离散系统 $x_{t+1}=Ax_t$ 的每个特征模式按 $\lambda^t$ 演化；连续系统 $x'=Ax$ 按 $e^{\lambda t}$ 演化。离散稳定通常要求相关特征值模小于 1，连续稳定要求实部小于 0；还应区分渐近稳定、边界稳定和爆炸。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each eigenmode of the discrete system $x_{t+1}=Ax_t$ evolves as $\lambda^t$, while a continuous system $x'=Ax$ evolves as $e^{\lambda t}$. Discrete asymptotic stability generally requires relevant eigenvalues inside the unit circle, and continuous asymptotic stability requires negative real parts. Distinguish asymptotic stability, marginal behaviour, and explosion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Markov 矩阵与稳态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Markov matrices and stationary states*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在固定约定下，转移矩阵保留概率总和，因此 1 是特征值。稳态分布是相应特征向量并需非负、归一化。是否收敛到唯一稳态还取决于不可约性和非周期性，不能只看到特征值 1 就下结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under a fixed row- or column-stochastic convention, a transition matrix preserves total probability, so one is an eigenvalue. A stationary distribution is a corresponding eigenvector that is nonnegative and normalised. Convergence to a unique stationary distribution also depends on properties such as irreducibility and aperiodicity; eigenvalue one alone is insufficient. See [[离散时间马尔可夫链|discrete-time Markov chains]] for the probabilistic conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Jordan 结构的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The role and limitations of Jordan structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当矩阵不可对角化，广义特征向量形成 Jordan 链。Jordan 形适合理论分类，但对数值扰动极敏感，计算中通常用 Schur 分解或 SVD。知道这个边界比强求 Jordan 计算更重要。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a matrix is not diagonalisable, generalised eigenvectors form Jordan chains. Jordan form is useful for theoretical classification but highly sensitive to numerical perturbations; Schur decomposition or SVD is normally preferred computationally. Understanding this limitation matters more than forcing a Jordan calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：两个模式的长期分离
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: separating two long-run modes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A=\begin{pmatrix}0.8&0\\0&1.1\end{pmatrix}$ 且 $x_0=(2,1)^T$，则 $x_t=A^tx_0=(2\cdot0.8^t,1.1^t)^T$。第一个模式衰减到 0，第二个模式爆炸，所以整体不稳定。若初值恰好为 $(2,0)^T$，观察到的轨迹会衰减，但这不能证明系统对任意小扰动稳定；隐藏的 $1.1$ 模式仍存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A=\begin{pmatrix}0.8&0\\0&1.1\end{pmatrix}$ and $x_0=(2,1)^T$. Then $x_t=A^tx_0=(2\cdot0.8^t,1.1^t)^T$. The first mode decays and the second explodes, so the system is unstable. If $x_0=(2,0)^T$, the observed trajectory decays, but that does not prove stability to arbitrary perturbations; the hidden $1.1$ mode remains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 重复特征值何时仍可对角化？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When can a repeated eigenvalue still permit diagonalisation?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 该特征值的特征空间维数等于其代数重数，并且所有特征空间合计能提供 n 个独立特征向量时。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When its eigenspace dimension equals its algebraic multiplicity and all eigenspaces together provide $n$ independent eigenvectors.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 Markov 链有稳态方程仍不保证从任意初态收敛？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does a stationary equation not guarantee convergence from every initial state?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 周期性可能造成循环，不可约性失败可能产生多个闭类；稳态存在、唯一和收敛是不同问题。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Periodicity can cause cycling, and reducibility can create several closed classes. Existence, uniqueness, and convergence are distinct questions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 离散时间和连续时间稳定性的特征值判准有何不同？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How do eigenvalue stability criteria differ in discrete and continuous time?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 离散模式是 $\lambda^t$，需 $|\lambda|<1$ 才衰减；连续模式是 $e^{\lambda t}$，需 $\operatorname{Re}\lambda<0$ 才衰减。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A discrete mode is $\lambda^t$ and decays when $|\lambda|<1$; a continuous mode is $e^{\lambda t}$ and decays when $\operatorname{Re}\lambda<0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明为什么 Jordan 形理论上有用却不是首选数值工具。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain why Jordan form is useful theoretically but not usually preferred numerically.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它精确分类特征空间不足时的代数结构，但任意小扰动都可能剧烈改变 Jordan 块；Schur 或 SVD 对浮点误差更稳定。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It classifies deficient eigenspaces exactly, but arbitrarily small perturbations can change Jordan blocks dramatically. Schur decomposition and SVD are more stable under floating-point error.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
