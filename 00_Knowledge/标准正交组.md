# 标准正交组由两两正交且范数均为 1 的向量组成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An orthonormal family consists of pairwise orthogonal unit vectors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 内积空间中的有限向量组 $q_1,\ldots,q_r$ 称为**标准正交组**，当且仅当
> $$
> \langle q_i,q_j\rangle=\delta_{ij}
> =\begin{cases}
> 1,&i=j,\\
> 0,&i\ne j.
> \end{cases}
> $$
> 也就是说，每个向量的范数都是 $1$，不同向量两两正交。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A finite family $q_1,\ldots,q_r$ in an inner-product space is orthonormal exactly when $\langle q_i,q_j\rangle=\delta_{ij}$. Each vector has norm one, and distinct vectors are pairwise orthogonal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 向量组与基的区别
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The distinction between a family and a basis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准正交组自动线性无关：若 $\sum_i c_iq_i=0$，分别与 $q_j$ 取内积便得到 $c_j=0$。当它还张成子空间 $S$ 时，才称为 $S$ 的**标准正交基**。因此，一个标准正交组可以只是大空间中的部分方向，同时又是其自身张成空间的标准正交基。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An orthonormal family is automatically linearly independent: taking the inner product of $\sum_i c_iq_i=0$ with each $q_j$ gives $c_j=0$. It becomes an orthonormal basis of a subspace $S$ only when it also spans $S$. Thus an orthonormal family may cover only part of the ambient space while still being a basis of its own span.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 矩阵写法与尺寸边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Matrix form and the dimension boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把这些向量排成列矩阵 $Q=[q_1\ \cdots\ q_r]$。在实空间中，列标准正交等价于
$$
Q^TQ=I_r;
$$
在复空间中则是 $Q^*Q=I_r$。若 $Q$ 是高矩阵，这并不推出 $QQ^T=I$ 或 $QQ^*=I$；只有列向量张成整个空间时，另一侧乘积才是单位矩阵。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Place the vectors into the columns of $Q$. Over the reals, orthonormal columns are equivalent to $Q^TQ=I_r$; over the complex numbers the condition is $Q^*Q=I_r$. For a tall matrix this does not imply $QQ^T=I$ or $QQ^*=I$. The reverse product is the identity only when the columns span the whole ambient space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如
$$
q_1=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
q_2=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix}
$$
构成 $\mathbb R^2$ 的标准正交基；单独的 $q_1$ 只是 $\mathbb R^2$ 中的标准正交组，但它是直线 $\operatorname{span}\{q_1\}$ 的标准正交基。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed vectors $q_1$ and $q_2$ form an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb R^2$. The single vector $q_1$ is only an orthonormal family in $\mathbb R^2$, although it is an orthonormal basis of the line it spans.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> $Q\in\mathbb R^{5\times2}$ 满足 $Q^TQ=I_2$。它的两列是什么关系？它们是否已经构成 $\mathbb R^5$ 的基？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $Q\in\mathbb R^{5\times2}$ satisfies $Q^TQ=I_2$, how are its columns related, and do they form a basis of $\mathbb R^5$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 两列彼此正交且范数均为 $1$；它们只张成一个二维子空间，不是整个 $\mathbb R^5$ 的基。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The columns are orthogonal unit vectors. They span only a two-dimensional subspace and therefore are not a basis of all of $\mathbb R^5$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
