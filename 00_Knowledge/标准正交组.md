# Orthonormal families
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*标准正交组*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **orthonormal family** is a collection of mutually perpendicular unit vectors. The two parts of the name do separate jobs: “orthogonal” keeps different directions from mixing in an [[内积|inner product]], while “normal” fixes every length at one. Together they make coefficients easy to read from a vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**标准正交组**是一组相互垂直的单位向量。名称中的两个要求各有作用：“正交”使不同方向在[[内积]]中互不混入，“标准”把每个长度固定为一。两者结合后，从一个向量中读出各方向的系数就很方便。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $(1,1)^T$ and $(1,-1)^T$ are [[正交|orthogonal]] because their dot product is zero. Each has [[欧氏范数|length]] $\sqrt2$. Dividing each by its own length produces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$(1,1)^T$ 与 $(1,-1)^T$ 的点积为零，因此[[正交]]；它们的[[欧氏范数|长度]]都是 $\sqrt2$。各自除以长度后，得到

$$q_1=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
q_2=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now $q_1^Tq_1=q_2^Tq_2=1$ and $q_1^Tq_2=0$. Dividing by a nonzero length preserves orthogonality. Dividing arbitrary vectors by their lengths, however, does not create it: $(1,0)^T$ and $(1,1)^T/\sqrt2$ are unit vectors whose dot product is $1/\sqrt2$, not zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

此时 $q_1^Tq_1=q_2^Tq_2=1$，且 $q_1^Tq_2=0$。除以非零长度保留已有的正交关系；但把任意向量分别归一化，并不会制造正交。例如 $(1,0)^T$ 与 $(1,1)^T/\sqrt2$ 都是单位向量，点积却是 $1/\sqrt2$，并非零。

> [!important] Check self-products and cross-products · 同时检查自身与交叉内积
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fix an inner product. A finite family $q_1,\ldots,q_r$ is orthonormal when every self-product is one and every product between different positions is zero:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定一种内积。有限向量组 $q_1,\ldots,q_r$ 是标准正交组，要求每个自身内积为一、不同位置之间的内积为零：
>
> $$\langle q_i,q_j\rangle=\delta_{ij}
> =\begin{cases}1,&i=j,\\0,&i\ne j.\end{cases}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $r$ is the number of vectors, $i,j$ select two positions, and $\delta_{ij}$ is shorthand for the displayed two cases. No vector can be zero, and the same vector cannot appear twice in the list. The norm is the one induced by the chosen inner product.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $r$ 是向量个数，$i,j$ 选择两个位置，$\delta_{ij}$ 只是所列两种情况的缩写。组中不能有零向量，也不能把同一个向量放入两个位置。长度必须使用所选内积诱导的范数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A family covers only the directions it spans
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 向量组与基的区别

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[基|basis]] must both be [[线性无关|linearly independent]] and [[张成|span]] the space under discussion: every vector in that space must be a linear combination of its members. An orthonormal family already supplies independence. To see why, assume $\sum_{i=1}^r c_iq_i=0$, where the scalars $c_i$ are possible coefficients. With the convention that the inner product is linear in its second argument, take its product with $q_j$ on the left:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一组[[基]]既要[[线性无关]]，也要[[张成]]正在讨论的空间：那个空间中的每个向量都必须能写成这些成员的线性组合。标准正交组已经保证线性无关。设 $\sum_{i=1}^r c_iq_i=0$，其中标量 $c_i$ 是待检验的系数。采用内积对第二个自变量线性的约定，把 $q_j$ 放在左侧取内积：

$$0=\left\langle q_j,\sum_{i=1}^r c_iq_i\right\rangle
=\sum_{i=1}^r c_i\langle q_j,q_i\rangle=c_j.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This holds for every $j$, so all coefficients vanish. The same calculation gives $c_j=\langle q_j,v\rangle$ whenever $v=\sum_i c_iq_i$. This coefficient-reading rule explains why orthonormal coordinates are useful; [[标准正交基投影公式|orthonormal projection]] handles a vector that may also have a component outside their span.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个 $j$ 都成立，所以所有系数只能为零。同样的计算还说明：只要 $v=\sum_i c_iq_i$，就有 $c_j=\langle q_j,v\rangle$。这条直接读取系数的规则说明了标准正交坐标的用途；当向量还含有张成空间以外的分量时，由[[标准正交基投影公式]]处理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two displayed vectors form an orthonormal basis of $\mathbb R^2$. Keeping only $q_1$ leaves an orthonormal family in $\mathbb R^2$, and an orthonormal basis of the **line** it spans, but not a basis of the whole plane. Always name the space when saying “basis”. In an $n$-dimensional space, an orthonormal family has at most $n$ members; it is a basis of that space when it has $n$ members. By the usual empty-family convention, zero members form a basis only of the zero subspace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头的两个向量构成 $\mathbb R^2$ 的标准正交基。如果只保留 $q_1$，它仍是 $\mathbb R^2$ 中的标准正交组，也是自身张成的那条**直线**的标准正交基，却不是整个平面的基。因此说“基”时要指出对应空间。在 $n$ 维空间中，标准正交组最多有 $n$ 个成员；恰有 $n$ 个成员时，它才是该空间的基。按通常的空向量组约定，零个成员只构成零子空间的基。

## Put the vectors into columns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 矩阵写法与尺寸边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $r\ge1$ vectors in $\mathbb F^n$, where $\mathbb F$ is $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$ and $n\ge1$, arrange them as the $n\times r$ column matrix $Q=[q_1\ \cdots\ q_r]$. In the standard inner product, entry $(i,j)$ of $Q^*Q$ is $q_i^*q_j$. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $\mathbb F^n$ 中的 $r\ge1$ 个向量，其中 $\mathbb F$ 是 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$、$n\ge1$，把它们排成 $n\times r$ 列矩阵 $Q=[q_1\ \cdots\ q_r]$。在标准内积中，$Q^*Q$ 的第 $(i,j)$ 项就是 $q_i^*q_j$。所以

$$\text{the columns are orthonormal}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad Q^*Q=I_r.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $*$ is conjugate transpose and $I_r$ is the $r\times r$ [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. For real entries, replace $*$ by $T$. This formula uses the standard coordinate inner product; under a weighted product $x^*My$ the corresponding test is $Q^*MQ=I_r$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $*$ 是共轭转置，$I_r$ 是 $r\times r$ [[单位矩阵]]。实矩阵把 $*$ 换成 $T$ 即可。该式使用标准坐标内积；若使用加权内积 $x^*My$，对应判据就是 $Q^*MQ=I_r$。

> [!example] The reverse product has another size · 反向乘积的尺寸不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $Q=q_1$, the single column above. Then $Q^TQ=[1]$, but
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 只取开头的一列 $Q=q_1$，则 $Q^TQ=[1]$，然而
>
> $$QQ^T=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&1\end{pmatrix}\ne I_2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This matrix keeps the component along the diagonal line and removes its perpendicular component. It is the identity on that line, not on all of $\mathbb R^2$. In general, under $Q^*Q=I_r$, the $n\times n$ product $QQ^*$ equals $I_n$ exactly when the columns span all of $\mathbb F^n$, equivalently $r=n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这个矩阵保留沿斜线的分量，去掉垂直于斜线的分量。它只在那条直线上等同于单位作用，并非在整个 $\mathbb R^2$ 中都如此。一般地，在 $Q^*Q=I_r$ 的前提下，$n\times n$ 乘积 $QQ^*$ 等于 $I_n$，当且仅当各列张成整个 $\mathbb F^n$，等价于 $r=n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For instance, a $5\times2$ matrix with $Q^TQ=I_2$ has two perpendicular unit columns spanning a two-dimensional subspace, not a basis of $\mathbb R^5$. A square real matrix with orthonormal columns is an [[正交矩阵|orthogonal matrix]]; its complex counterpart is a [[Unitary 矩阵|unitary matrix]]. The vector-family definition itself does not require a square matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $5\times2$ 矩阵满足 $Q^TQ=I_2$ 时，两列相互垂直且长度为一，只张成二维子空间，并非 $\mathbb R^5$ 的基。列标准正交的实方阵称为[[正交矩阵]]，复数对应物称为 [[Unitary 矩阵|酉矩阵]]。标准正交组本身的定义并不要求组成方阵。
