# 封闭式基金是在设立的资本安排中不向持有人提供持续赎回权的基金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A closed-end fund is established with a capital structure that does not give holders a continuous redemption right*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

封闭式基金的结构不依靠持续接受持有人申赎来调整份额。在中国《证券投资基金法》的封闭式运作定义中，基金合同期限内份额总额固定，持有人不得申请赎回。判断依据是基金设立的运作安排，不能把普通[[开放式基金]]暂时暂停赎回就叫作封闭式基金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A closed-end structure does not adjust its units through ongoing investor subscriptions and redemptions. Under the Chinese Fund Law's closed-end definition, units remain fixed during the contractual term and holders cannot request redemption. Classification concerns the fund's established operating structure: temporarily suspending redemptions does not turn an ordinary [[开放式基金|open-end fund]] into a closed-end fund.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上市封闭式基金的持有人通常通过把份额卖给另一位投资者退出，成交价可以高于或低于[[基金份额净值]]。若基金有 100 万份，甲向乙卖出 1 万份，仍有 100 万份；这不是基金支付赎回款并注销份额。份额总数固定，也不意味着资产价值或每份净值固定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holders of listed closed-end funds generally exit by selling units to another investor, at a price above or below [[基金份额净值|per-unit NAV]]. Transferring 10,000 of 1 million outstanding units leaves 1 million outstanding; the fund has not paid a redemption or cancelled units. A fixed unit count does not fix asset value or NAV.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

法域之间不要机械套同一细则：美国 closed-end funds 一般没有持有人随时要求基金回购的权利，但可有专门回购安排或定期回购型产品，而且并非全部上市。因此“永不增减份额”“必有 10 或 15 年期限”“必然折价”都不是通用定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Jurisdictions do not share every detail. U.S. closed-end funds generally lack investor-demand redemption, but may use specific repurchase arrangements or interval-fund structures; not all are exchange-listed. Never-changing units, a mandatory ten- or fifteen-year life, and inevitable discounts are not universal definitions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

少了日常赎回压力，不等于没有流动性风险。基金仍需支付费用、分配或到期债务；持有人转售也需要买方，并可能承受[[基金折溢价|折价扩大]]。封闭式结构改变资金退出渠道，没有消除底层资产风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Less routine redemption pressure does not eliminate liquidity risk. The fund still faces expenses, distributions, or maturing obligations, while resale needs a buyer and may expose the holder to a [[基金折溢价|widening discount]]. The structure changes the exit channel rather than removing underlying risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
