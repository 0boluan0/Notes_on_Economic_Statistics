# Multiplication rule of probability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*概率乘法法则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **multiplication rule** finds the probability that two events both happen. Start with the probability of one event, then multiply by the probability of the other **within that first event**. For example, to find the chance that a randomly chosen part comes from factory B and is defective, combine B's share of the shipment with the defect rate among B's parts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**概率乘法法则**用来求两件事同时发生的概率：先取一件事的概率，再乘上**在这件事已经发生的条件下**另一件事的概率。例如，要算“随机抽到的零件来自 B 厂，而且有缺陷”的概率，就要结合 B 厂在整批货物中所占的比例，以及 B 厂零件内部的缺陷比例。

> [!example] A proportion within a proportion · 一部分中的一部分
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose B supplies 40% of a shipment, and 5% of B's parts are defective. In a hypothetical shipment of 10,000 parts with exactly these proportions, 4,000 come from B and 200 of those are defective. A uniformly chosen part therefore has probability $200/10{,}000=0.02$ of being both from B and defective. The same calculation is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 B 厂供应整批货物的 40%，其中 5% 有缺陷。想象一批恰好符合这些比例的 10,000 个零件：4,000 个来自 B 厂，其中 200 个有缺陷。因此，等可能随机抽一个零件，它既来自 B 厂又有缺陷的概率是 $200/10{,}000=0.02$。同一个计算可以写成
>
> $$0.40\times0.05=0.02.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The 5% applies to B's parts, not to the whole shipment. Multiplying by 40% puts the answer back on the scale of the whole shipment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里的 5% 以 B 厂零件为范围；乘上 40%，才换回整批货物中的比例。

## Turning the example into a rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把例子写成通用法则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An event describes a collection of possible outcomes. Let $D$ mean “the part is defective” and $B$ mean “it comes from factory B”. Their intersection $D\cap B$ means both descriptions hold. The [[条件概率|conditional probability]] $P(D\mid B)$ is the defect probability after restricting attention to B's parts; the event after the bar sets that restriction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

事件描述一组可能结果。令 $D$ 表示“零件有缺陷”，$B$ 表示“零件来自 B 厂”。交集 $D\cap B$ 表示两个描述同时成立。[[条件概率]] $P(D\mid B)$ 则是在只考虑 B 厂零件之后，有缺陷的概率；竖线后面的事件指定了这个范围。

> [!abstract] From conditional to joint probability · 从条件概率得到联合概率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For any events $A,B$ with $P(B)>0$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对任意事件 $A,B$，若 $P(B)>0$，则
>
> $$P(A\cap B)=P(A\mid B)P(B).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This follows by multiplying both sides of $P(A\mid B)=P(A\cap B)/P(B)$ by $P(B)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这是把定义 $P(A\mid B)=P(A\cap B)/P(B)$ 两边同乘 $P(B)$ 得到的。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $P(A)>0$, we can also begin with $A$ and write $P(A\cap B)=P(B\mid A)P(A)$. These are two ways to calculate the same intersection. The order is an order of calculation; the events need not occur at different times. Reversing the condition also changes the probability multiplying it. [[Bayes法则|Bayes' rule]] uses this equality to reverse a conditioning direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $P(A)>0$，也可以从 $A$ 出发，写成 $P(A\cap B)=P(B\mid A)P(A)$。两式计算的是同一个交集。这里的先后是计算顺序，事件不必在时间上先后发生。反转条件方向时，旁边相乘的概率也要换；[[Bayes法则]]正是利用这两个表达式相等来反转条件方向。

> [!warning] Multiplication does not require independence · 乘法法则本身不要求独立
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The rule uses the conditional probability $P(A\mid B)$, which may differ from $P(A)$. Only if the events are [[事件独立|independent]] may we replace it with $P(A)$ and use $P(A)P(B)$. Also, when $P(B)=0$, the elementary conditional probability is undefined: the intersection probability is zero, but “undefined times zero” is not a valid derivation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 乘法法则使用的 $P(A\mid B)$ 可能与 $P(A)$ 不同。只有事件[[事件独立|独立]]时，才能把它换成 $P(A)$，直接算 $P(A)P(B)$。此外，若 $P(B)=0$，初等条件概率没有定义；交集的概率确实为零，但不能用“未定义的量乘零”来计算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a quick check of the scale, if $P(B)=0.4$ and $P(A\mid B)=0.25$, then $P(A\cap B)=0.1$: one quarter of the 40% group is 10% of the whole. If several non-overlapping groups can produce the event of interest, calculate such a contribution for each group and add them using the [[全概率公式|law of total probability]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再检查一次比例：若 $P(B)=0.4$、$P(A\mid B)=0.25$，则 $P(A\cap B)=0.1$；占整体 40% 的那一组里，四分之一就是整体的 10%。若目标事件可能来自几个互不重叠的组，就逐组计算这样的贡献，再用[[全概率公式]]相加。
