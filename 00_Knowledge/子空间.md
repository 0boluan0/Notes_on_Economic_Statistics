# Subspace
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*子空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **subspace** is a collection inside a [[向量空间|vector space]] that is itself a vector space using the same addition and scalar multiplication. It may be smaller, such as a line inside a plane, but being a subset alone is not enough: every permitted addition and scaling must keep us inside that collection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**子空间**是某个[[向量空间]]内部的一部分，它沿用原来的加法和数乘，本身仍然构成向量空间。它可以更小，例如平面中的一条直线；但仅仅是子集还不够，所有允许的加法、数乘都必须让结果留在其中。

> [!example] A line through zero · 经过零的直线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider $S=\{(t,-t):t\in\mathbb R\}$ inside $\mathbb R^2$. This means “choose any real number $t$, put it in the first coordinate, and put its negative in the second”. Every vector satisfies $x+y=0$. Adding two members gives $(s,-s)+(t,-t)=(s+t,-s-t)$, another member; multiplying by a real number $a$ gives $(at,-at)$, another member. Taking $t=0$ includes $(0,0)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\mathbb R^2$ 中考虑 $S=\{(t,-t):t\in\mathbb R\}$：任取一个实数 $t$ 放在第一坐标，第二坐标放它的相反数。因此每个向量都满足 $x+y=0$。两个成员相加得到 $(s,-s)+(t,-t)=(s+t,-s-t)$，仍在其中；乘以实数 $a$ 得到 $(at,-at)$，也仍在其中。令 $t=0$，便包含了 $(0,0)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The letters $s,t,a$ are arbitrary, so the calculation checks every allowed choice. Testing a few numerical examples would only confirm those examples. The checks are simpler than verifying every vector-space axiom again: rules such as commutativity are inherited from the surrounding space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 $s,t,a$ 可以任意取值，因此计算覆盖了全部允许情况。只试几个数值例子，只能确认那几个例子。无需重新验证全部向量空间公理，是因为加法交换律等规则直接继承自外部空间。

> [!abstract] The subspace test · 子空间判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $S\subseteq V$, where $V$ is a vector space over a scalar field $\mathbb F$. Then $S$ is a subspace exactly when it contains the zero vector and is closed under addition and multiplication by **every** scalar in $\mathbb F$. Equivalently, require $S$ to be nonempty and require
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $S\subseteq V$，其中 $V$ 是标量域 $\mathbb F$ 上的向量空间。$S$ 是子空间，当且仅当它包含零向量，且对加法、对来自 $\mathbb F$ 的**任意**标量的数乘封闭。等价地，可以要求 $S$ 非空，并满足
>
> $$\alpha u+\beta v\in S\qquad\text{for all }u,v\in S,\quad\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $\alpha$ tells us how much of $u$ to use and $\beta$ how much of $v$. Their sum is a linear combination. Nonemptiness matters: the empty set has no zero vector. For a nonempty set, choosing a vector and multiplying by zero forces zero into the set.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 其中 $\alpha$ 决定使用多少份 $u$，$\beta$ 决定使用多少份 $v$；相加得到线性组合。非空条件不可丢掉：空集没有零向量；对于非空集合，选一个成员再乘零，就能迫使零属于集合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A shifted line $x+y=1$ fails immediately because $(0,0)$ is absent. Containing zero alone also does not suffice: the first quadrant contains zero but fails multiplication by $-1$. Both the one-point set $\{0\}$ and the entire space $V$ are valid subspaces. A subspace need not be strictly smaller than its surrounding space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平移后的直线 $x+y=1$ 立即失败，因为没有 $(0,0)$。仅仅包含零向量还不够：第一象限包含零，却无法对乘 $-1$ 封闭。单点集合 $\{0\}$ 和整个空间 $V$ 都是合法子空间；子空间不要求一定比外部空间严格更小。

## 与线性方程组的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Homogeneous and shifted solution sets*

A **homogeneous** linear system has a zero right-hand side. For example, $x+y=0$ produced the line above. More generally, $Ax=0$ has the [[零空间|nullspace]] $N(A)=\{x:Ax=0\}$ as its solution set. Matrix multiplication preserves linear combinations: if $Au=Av=0$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**齐次**线性方程组的右端是零，例如刚才 $x+y=0$ 的解是一条过零直线。一般地，$Ax=0$ 的全部解组成[[零空间]] $N(A)=\{x:Ax=0\}$。矩阵乘法保留线性组合：如果 $Au=Av=0$，那么

$$A(\alpha u+\beta v)=\alpha Au+\beta Av=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The zero input is also a solution, so this is a subspace. If $Ax=b$ has a solution $x_p$ and $b\ne0$, all solutions instead have the form $x_p+z$ with $z\in N(A)$: subtracting $Ax_p=b$ from $Ax=b$ gives $A(x-x_p)=0$. This is a shifted, or affine, copy of the nullspace. It excludes zero because $A0=0\ne b$, so it is not a subspace. The details of the solution representation belong to [[特解加零空间|particular solution plus nullspace]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零输入也满足方程，所以解集是子空间。如果 $Ax=b$ 有一个解 $x_p$，且 $b\ne0$，全部解则写成 $x_p+z$，其中 $z\in N(A)$：把 $Ax_p=b$ 从 $Ax=b$ 中减去，就得到 $A(x-x_p)=0$。这是一份平移后的、也叫仿射的零空间副本。由于 $A0=0\ne b$，它不含零，因此不是子空间。解的完整表示由[[特解加零空间]]展开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subspaces can be combined, but the operation matters. An intersection stays a subspace because its members satisfy both sets of closure conditions. A union need not: choosing one vector from each subspace can produce a sum in neither. The exact union criterion is in [[子空间并集判据|when a union of subspaces is a subspace]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对子空间做集合运算时，还要分清操作。交集仍是子空间，因为其成员同时满足两边的封闭条件；并集却未必，从两个子空间各取一个向量，相加可能两边都不属于。精确的并集条件见[[子空间并集判据]]。
