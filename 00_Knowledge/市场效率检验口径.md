# 市场效率检验必须相对于信息集资产时期风险模型与实施成本陈述
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Market efficiency must be stated relative to an information set, asset, period, risk model, and implementable costs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子检验口径
> “市场有效”不是市场永久正确的总评。可检验命题应写成：在指定资产与样本期内，给定信息集是否还能在指定正常收益模型下、扣除取得信息和实际交易成本后，系统预测可实施的风险调整异常收益。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “The market is efficient” is not an all-time quality verdict. A testable claim asks whether, for specified assets and a specified period, an information set still predicts implementable risk-adjusted abnormal returns under a stated normal-return model after information and trading costs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Fama 的零成本严格表述把“价格充分反映全部可得信息”作为清晰基准；但现实信息取得与交易都有成本。经济上较弱的版本允许价格反映信息到边际收益不再超过边际成本。因而，同一信号在高流动性大盘股上不可获利，并不自动证明它在另一资产、另一时期或另一成本结构下也无效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fama uses the zero-cost statement that prices fully reflect available information as a clean benchmark, while recognising that information acquisition and trading are costly in practice. A weaker economic version allows information to be incorporated until its marginal benefit no longer exceeds its marginal cost. Failure of a signal in liquid large-cap equities therefore does not establish the same result for every asset, period, or cost structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“有人赚到钱”也不等于否定有效性：收益可能是风险补偿、偶然实现、事先无法复制的信息优势，或毛收益尚未覆盖研究、融资、冲击与税费。结论必须把资产范围、时期、信息可得时点、风险基准和成本口径写全。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The existence of a profitable investor does not by itself reject efficiency. The return may compensate risk, be a lucky realisation, rely on information that was not reproducibly available ex ante, or disappear after research, funding, impact, and tax costs. Conclusions must state the asset universe, period, information timestamp, risk benchmark, and cost convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> “技术指标在某十年预测了小盘股毛收益”是否已经构成市场无效证据？
>
> **答案：** 还没有。需先冻结资产与时期、确认信号当时可得、选择风险调整模型，并扣除点差、冲击、借券与容量成本，再做样本外检验。
