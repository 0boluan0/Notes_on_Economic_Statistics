<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Root-finding residual
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*求根残差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **residual** is what remains when you substitute a proposed answer into the equation. If a calculator proposes 1.4 as the square root of 2, squaring it gives 1.96: the equation is short by 0.04. That leftover is directly computable even when the exact root is unknown.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**残差**就是把猜测的答案代回方程后，等式还剩下的缺口。如果计算器把 1.4 当作 2 的平方根，平方得到 1.96，比 2 少 0.04。即使还不知道精确根，也能直接算出这个缺口。

> [!important] Signed and absolute residuals · 带符号与绝对残差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a scalar equation $f(x)=0$, substituting a candidate $\hat x$ gives the signed residual $r_f=f(\hat x)$. Its magnitude $\rho_f=|f(\hat x)|$ is the absolute residual. A residual answers how far the candidate is from satisfying the equation in function-value units.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对标量方程 $f(x)=0$，把候选点 $\hat x$ 代入方程后得到的
> $$
> r_f=f(\hat x)
> $$
> 是**带符号残差**；通常用于判断缺口大小的是**绝对残差**
> $$
> \rho_f=|f(\hat x)|.
> $$
> 残差回答“这个候选还差多少才满足方程”，其单位与 $f$ 相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $f$ is the function in the equation and the hat on $\hat x$ marks a candidate, not a certified answer. The subscript in $r_f$ identifies the equation whose defect is being measured; it does not name a root. For an equation $g(x)=h(x)$, first set $f(x)=g(x)-h(x)$. Reversing that subtraction reverses the signed residual but leaves its magnitude unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $f$ 是方程中的函数，$\hat x$ 上的帽子表示它是候选值，并不表示已经验收。$r_f$ 的下标说明测量的是哪条方程的缺口；这个符号不是根的名称。若原方程为 $g(x)=h(x)$，可先令 $f(x)=g(x)-h(x)$；反过来相减会改变残差的符号，但不会改变它的绝对值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## How to calculate a residual
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样计算残差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compute a residual by evaluating the original equation at the candidate and, when only defect size matters, taking the absolute value. A zero residual means that the candidate satisfies the equation under the current arithmetic and function evaluation. The sign shows which side of zero the function value lies on; the magnitude gives the remaining function-value defect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算只需两步：

1. 把候选 $\hat x$ 代入原方程的左侧，得到 $f(\hat x)$；
2. 若只关心缺口大小，再取绝对值得到 $|f(\hat x)|$。

若 $f(\hat x)=0$，候选在当前算术与函数求值下精确满足方程。若不为 0，符号说明函数值位于零的哪一侧，绝对值说明方程还剩多大的函数值缺口。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The statement about a zero result refers to the arithmetic actually used. For exact real evaluation, $f(\hat x)=0$ is exactly the definition of a root. A program instead evaluates an approximation, which we may denote $\widetilde f(\hat x)$; the program returning zero does not by itself prove that the real number $f(\hat x)$ is zero. The [[浮点精确等号|meaning of floating-point equality]] and [[浮点表示误差与打印|representation and rounding]] explain that distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的零结果陈述指实际采用的算术。在精确实数求值下，$f(\hat x)=0$ 就是根的定义；程序实际得到的却是近似值，可以记为 $\widetilde f(\hat x)$。程序返回零本身不能证明实数 $f(\hat x)$ 为零。[[浮点精确等号]]以及[[浮点表示误差与打印]]解释了这个区别。

> [!example] A computed zero can hide a defect · 求值为零也可能藏着缺口
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In exact arithmetic, $f(x)=(10^{16}+x)-10^{16}=x$, so $f(1)=1$ and the root is $0$. On the usual IEEE 754 binary64 arithmetic with rounding to nearest and ties to even, `(1e16 + 1.0) - 1e16` evaluates to `0.0`: the addition has rounded away the 1 before the subtraction. The computed residual is zero, although the candidate is one unit from the mathematical root.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在精确算术下，$f(x)=(10^{16}+x)-10^{16}=x$，因此 $f(1)=1$，根是 $0$。在通常按最近值舍入、恰在中点时取偶数的 IEEE 754 binary64 算术中，`(1e16 + 1.0) - 1e16` 得到 `0.0`：相减之前，加法已经把 1 舍掉。计算出的残差为零，但候选与数学根仍相差 1。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Intuition: substitute the candidate back
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 直觉：先把候选代回去

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A root finder produces a candidate rather than a labelled true root. The residual is a directly computable substitution check: if the goal is to make $f(x)$ zero, evaluate what remains at the candidate. It is not itself a distance from the candidate to a true root; that conversion needs the additional conditions in [[残差控制根误差|Residual Control of Root Error]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求根算法产生的是候选，不是真根标签。残差提供一个可直接计算的回代检查：如果目标是让 $f(x)$ 变成 0，就查看候选代入后还剩多少。它本身不是候选到某个真根的距离；若要把函数值缺口换成位置误差，需要额外条件，见 [[残差控制根误差]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sign is a sign of the **output defect**, not a universal direction toward the root. For $f(x)=x-1$, a positive residual lies to the right of the root; for $g(x)=1-x$, a positive residual lies to its left. Both equations have the same root. An algorithm needs additional shape or bracketing information to decide which way to move.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

符号表示的是**输出缺口**的正负，并不普遍指明应该朝哪个方向找根。对 $f(x)=x-1$，残差为正意味着候选在根的右侧；对 $g(x)=1-x$，残差为正却意味着候选在根的左侧。两条方程的根相同，算法仍需函数形状或括区间信息才能判断移动方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A square-root example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2-2$ and candidate $\hat x=1.4$, the signed residual is $-0.04$ and the absolute residual is $0.04$. The candidate leaves a function-value defect of size $0.04$; this calculation does not directly give the distance from $1.4$ to $\sqrt2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对

$$
f(x)=x^2-2
$$

取候选 $\hat x=1.4$，则

$$
r_f=f(1.4)=1.4^2-2=-0.04,
\qquad
\rho_f=|r_f|=0.04.
$$

所以这个候选代回方程后还留下大小为 $0.04$ 的缺口。这里计算的是残差；它没有直接给出 $1.4$ 到 $\sqrt2$ 的距离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $x$ represents length, then $x^2-A=0$ compares areas: its residual has area units while the error in $x$ has length units. This is why [[绝对误差与相对误差|input error]] and residual cannot be interchanged just because both are small numbers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $x$ 表示长度，方程 $x^2-A=0$ 比较的就是面积：残差的单位是面积，而 $x$ 的误差单位是长度。因此，即使两个数都很小，也不能把[[绝对误差与相对误差|输入误差]]与残差互换。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The equation scale sets the residual scale
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方程的尺度决定残差尺度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replace $f$ by $g=c f$ for a nonzero constant $c$. The root set is unchanged, but the absolute residual becomes $|g(\hat x)|=|c|\,|f(\hat x)|$. For the same candidate 1.4 above, multiplying the equation by 100 changes the absolute residual from 0.04 to 4. A residual tolerance therefore belongs to a specified equation, scale and unit. A normalised residual may make comparisons more meaningful, but normalisation itself does not prove a root exists or that the candidate is near it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $f$ 换成 $g=c f$，其中常数 $c\ne0$，根的集合不变，但绝对残差变成 $|g(\hat x)|=|c|\,|f(\hat x)|$。对上面的同一个候选 1.4，方程乘以 100 后，绝对残差就从 0.04 变成 4。因此，残差容差必须对应一条指定的方程、尺度和单位。归一化残差可以使比较更有意义，但归一化本身不证明根存在，也不证明候选靠近根。
