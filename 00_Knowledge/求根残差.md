# 求根残差是把候选点代入方程后仍未消除的函数值缺口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A root-finding residual is the function-value defect remaining after substituting a candidate into the equation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 对标量方程 $f(x)=0$，把候选点 $\hat x$ 代入方程后得到的
> $$
> r_f=f(\hat x)
> $$
> 是**带符号残差**；通常用于判断缺口大小的是**绝对残差**
> $$
> \rho_f=|f(\hat x)|.
> $$
> 残差回答“这个候选还差多少才满足方程”，其单位与 $f$ 相同。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a scalar equation $f(x)=0$, substituting a candidate $\hat x$ gives the signed residual $r_f=f(\hat x)$. Its magnitude $\rho_f=|f(\hat x)|$ is the absolute residual. A residual answers how far the candidate is from satisfying the equation in function-value units.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样计算残差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How to Calculate a Residual*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算只需两步：

1. 把候选 $\hat x$ 代入原方程的左侧，得到 $f(\hat x)$；
2. 若只关心缺口大小，再取绝对值得到 $|f(\hat x)|$。

若 $f(\hat x)=0$，候选在当前算术与函数求值下精确满足方程。若不为 0，符号说明函数值位于零的哪一侧，绝对值说明方程还剩多大的函数值缺口。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compute a residual by evaluating the original equation at the candidate and, when only defect size matters, taking the absolute value. A zero residual means that the candidate satisfies the equation under the current arithmetic and function evaluation. The sign shows which side of zero the function value lies on; the magnitude gives the remaining function-value defect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 直觉：先把候选代回去
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Intuition: Substitute the Candidate Back into the Equation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求根算法产生的是候选，不是真根标签。残差提供一个可直接计算的回代检查：如果目标是让 $f(x)$ 变成 0，就查看候选代入后还剩多少。它本身不是候选到某个真根的距离；若要把函数值缺口换成位置误差，需要额外条件，见 [[残差控制根误差]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A root finder produces a candidate rather than a labelled true root. The residual is a directly computable substitution check: if the goal is to make $f(x)$ zero, evaluate what remains at the candidate. It is not itself a distance from the candidate to a true root; that conversion needs the additional conditions in [[残差控制根误差|Residual Control of Root Error]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对

$$
f(x)=x^2-2
$$

取候选 $\hat x=1.4$，则

$$
r_f=f(1.4)=1.4^2-2=-0.04,
\qquad
\rho_f=|r_f|=0.04.
$$

所以这个候选代回方程后还留下大小为 $0.04$ 的缺口。这里计算的是残差；它没有直接给出 $1.4$ 到 $\sqrt2$ 的距离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2-2$ and candidate $\hat x=1.4$, the signed residual is $-0.04$ and the absolute residual is $0.04$. The candidate leaves a function-value defect of size $0.04$; this calculation does not directly give the distance from $1.4$ to $\sqrt2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
