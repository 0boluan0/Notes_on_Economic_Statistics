# The stationary law of a finite irreducible chain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有限不可约链的唯一平稳分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When every state can eventually reach every other state, a finite chain cannot sustain two different stationary probability allocations. There is exactly one, and every state receives positive probability. This does not require the chain to stop oscillating when started elsewhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当每个状态最终都能到达其他每个状态时，有限链不能维持两种不同的不变概率分配。平稳分布恰好只有一个，而且每个状态都有正概率。这不要求链从其他初态出发后不再振荡。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A two-state balance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两个状态的平衡*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]], take

$$P=\begin{pmatrix}0.9&0.1\\0.2&0.8\end{pmatrix},\qquad \pi=(a,1-a).$$

A [[Markov稳态分布|stationary distribution]] satisfies $\pi P=\pi$ with nonnegative entries summing to one. The first coordinate equation is $0.9a+0.2(1-a)=a$, giving $a=2/3$ and $\pi=(2/3,1/3)$. Each state can reach the other in one step. A chain with this mutual reachability for every pair is [[不可约链|irreducible]]; in general the paths may require several steps, rather than one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用[[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]]，取上面的两状态矩阵及概率行 $\pi=(a,1-a)$。[[Markov稳态分布]]满足 $\pi P=\pi$，元素非负、总和为一。第一个坐标的等式为 $0.9a+0.2(1-a)=a$，解得 $a=2/3$，即 $\pi=(2/3,1/3)$。这里两状态一步就能互达；若一般链的每一对状态都能互达，就称[[不可约链|不可约]]，但路径可以需要多步，不必一步直达。

> [!important] Finite and irreducible is enough · 有限加不可约已经足够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $P$ be a fixed row-stochastic transition matrix on a finite nonempty state set $S$, and suppose the chain is irreducible. Then there is exactly one probability row $\pi$ with $\pi P=\pi$, and
>
> $$\pi_j>0\qquad\text{for every }j\in S.$$
>
> Having positive mass on every state is called **full support**. Irreducibility concerns positive-probability paths; it does not require every matrix entry to be positive. No aperiodicity assumption appears in this theorem.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $P$ 是有限非空状态集 $S$ 上固定的行随机转移矩阵，并且链不可约。那么恰有一个概率行 $\pi$ 满足 $\pi P=\pi$，且每个 $j\in S$ 都有 $\pi_j>0$。每个状态都有正质量，称为**满支持**。不可约要求正概率路径，不要求矩阵每个元素都为正；本定理没有非周期假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Existence first, then positivity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先有存在性，再证明严格正性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[有限Markov链稳态存在|finite-state existence theorem]] supplies at least one stationary probability row $\pi$. Because its entries sum to one, some state $i$ has $\pi_i>0$. For any state $j$, irreducibility provides an integer $m\ge0$ with $(P^m)_{ij}>0$. The matrix entry is the probability of going from $i$ to $j$ in $m$ steps. Stationarity also gives $\pi P^m=\pi$, so

$$\pi_j=\sum_{x\in S}\pi_x(P^m)_{xj}\ \ge\ \pi_i(P^m)_{ij}>0.$$

Thus positive stationary mass propagates along every reachable path. This argument applies to any stationary probability distribution, not just the first one we found.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[有限Markov链稳态存在]]，至少存在一个平稳概率行 $\pi$。因为其元素和为一，至少有一个状态 $i$ 满足 $\pi_i>0$。对任意状态 $j$，不可约性给出某个整数 $m\ge0$，使 $(P^m)_{ij}>0$；该元素表示从 $i$ 出发，$m$ 步后位于 $j$ 的概率。又由平稳性 $\pi P^m=\pi$，得到上式的正下界。所以正的平稳质量会沿所有可达路径传播。这个论证适用于任意平稳概率分布，不只适用于最先找到的一个。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why a second stationary law must agree
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么第二个平稳分布必须相同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $\mu$ is another stationary probability row. Since every $\pi_j>0$, compare their coordinate ratios and set

$$c=\min_{j\in S}\frac{\mu_j}{\pi_j},\qquad w=\mu-c\pi.$$

The minimum exists because there are finitely many states. Every entry of $w$ is nonnegative, at least one is zero, and linearity gives $wP=w$. If $w$ were nonzero, its entries would have a positive sum; dividing by that sum would produce a stationary probability distribution with a zero entry. The positivity argument has just ruled that out. Therefore $w=0$, so $\mu=c\pi$. Both total masses are one, forcing $c=1$ and $\mu=\pi$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $\mu$ 是另一个平稳概率行。所有 $\pi_j$ 都为正，因此可以逐坐标取比值，并令 $c=\min_j\mu_j/\pi_j$、$w=\mu-c\pi$。状态数有限，最小值能够取到。$w$ 的每项非负，至少一项为零；由线性性又有 $wP=w$。如果 $w$ 非零，其分量和就为正，除以这个和，会得到一个含零分量的平稳概率分布。但刚才的正性论证已经排除了这种分布。因此 $w=0$，即 $\mu=c\pi$；二者总质量都为一，迫使 $c=1$，所以 $\mu=\pi$。

> [!warning] A unique stationary law can coexist with a cycle · 唯一稳态仍可伴随循环
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $F=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, the chain is irreducible and its unique stationary row is $(1/2,1/2)$. Yet $(1,0)F^k$ alternates between $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$. Returns occur only at even positive times, so the [[状态周期|period]] is two. A deterministic three-state cycle similarly has period three and unique uniform stationary probabilities. [[有限链逐步收敛|Convergence from every initial distribution]] needs the additional aperiodicity condition in the finite irreducible setting.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对交换矩阵 $F$，链不可约，唯一平稳行是 $(1/2,1/2)$；但 $(1,0)F^k$ 在 $(1,0)$ 和 $(0,1)$ 之间交替。正时刻的返回只能发生在偶数步，所以[[状态周期|周期]]为二。确定性的三状态循环也一样：周期为三，仍有唯一的均匀平稳分布。在有限不可约的前提下，要保证[[有限链逐步收敛|从每个初态逐步收敛]]，还需要额外的非周期性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same finite-path bound used in [[有限链类分解|class decomposition]] also shows that the first positive return time to any state has finite mean: its tail is bounded by a geometric sequence in blocks of a fixed length. This is [[正常返与零常返|positive recurrence]], a consequence here rather than an extra hypothesis. For an infinite state space, irreducibility alone does not provide this finite mean or a stationary probability distribution. Finally, irreducibility is sufficient, not necessary for uniqueness of a full finite chain: a reducible chain with just one closed class also has a unique stationary law, as explained by [[有限链平稳分解|stationary decomposition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[有限链类分解]]中的有限路径界还说明：返回任意状态的首次正时间具有有限均值，因为其尾概率在固定长度区段上被几何序列控制。这就是[[正常返与零常返|正常返]]，在这里是结论，不是额外假设。对无限状态空间，仅不可约不能保证这种有限均值，也不能保证平稳概率分布存在。最后，不可约是整条有限链稳态唯一的充分条件，不是必要条件：只有一个闭类的可约链也有唯一平稳分布，见[[有限链平稳分解]]。
