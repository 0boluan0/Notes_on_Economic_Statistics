# 成对偏好模型根据同一提示下两个候选的相对选择，估计一个候选胜过另一个的条件概率或排序规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A pairwise preference model uses relative choices between two candidates for the same prompt to estimate the conditional probability or ranking rule by which one candidate beats the other*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 一条成对偏好记录可写成 $(x,y_w,y_l)$：面对同一提示 $x$ 和两个候选回答，记录者选择了 $y_w$ 而不是 $y_l$。成对偏好模型把许多这类相对选择变成一条可泛化的规则，用来预测“在给定提示和标注条件下，候选 A 胜过候选 B 的概率或次序”。它建模的是比较结果，不要求数据先给每个回答一个绝对分数。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A pairwise preference record can be written $(x,y_w,y_l)$: for the same prompt and two candidate responses, the observer chose $y_w$ over $y_l$. A pairwise preference model turns many such relative choices into a rule that predicts the probability or ordering by which candidate A beats candidate B under the stated prompt and annotation conditions. The data need not supply an absolute score for either response.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 数据、选择模型与训练管线是三层
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The data, choice model, and training pipeline are three layers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **比较数据**只记录某一对候选中谁被选中，并连同提示、标注者和说明形成观察条件。
2. **选择模型**规定怎样从回答特征或潜在分数得到选择概率。[[Bradley–Terry模型]] 是常见的一维分数差模型，但不是“成对偏好模型”的全部定义。
3. **训练管线**规定模型怎样被使用：可以先拟合显式奖励模型再优化策略，也可以像 [[DPO目标|DPO]] 那样让偏好对直接进入策略损失。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pairwise data record which candidate won under an observation protocol. A choice model specifies how response features or latent scores imply a selection probability. A training pipeline then decides how that model is used. Bradley–Terry is one common choice model; explicit-reward-model RLHF and DPO are two different ways in which pairwise information can enter policy training.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一条标签只给出局部相对信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*One label supplies local relative information*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若记录显示 A 胜过 B，可以得出“在这次比较条件下 A 被选中”；不能仅凭这一条记录推出 A 胜过所有回答、A 在绝对意义上正确，或偏好来自事实性而非长度与语气。许多彼此相连的比较能约束更广的排序，但模型能推广多远仍取决于候选覆盖、提示分布和所选模型形式。

因此，读取结果时要保留协议限定。分数是否能被解释为事实性、安全性或广泛用户效用，见 [[偏好分数解释边界]]；具体的长度与迎合线索，见 [[长度代理偏差]] 和 [[迎合偏好偏差]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A label saying A beats B supports that relative choice under the observed conditions. By itself it does not establish that A beats every response, is absolutely correct, or was selected for factuality rather than length or tone. Connected comparisons can constrain a broader ranking, but generalization still depends on candidate coverage, prompt distribution, and model form. The [[偏好分数解释边界|interpretation boundary]] treats the stronger claims separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] “成对”描述观察单位，不指定唯一公式
> 选择可以由 logistic 分数差、带平局的模型、多维效用或其他规则表示。看到 chosen/rejected 数据，不能自动断言系统采用 Bradley–Terry、训练了独立奖励模型或运行了 PPO；必须继续核对实际损失与更新过程。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Pairwise” describes the observation unit, not one mandatory formula. Logistic score differences, tie-aware models, multidimensional utilities, and other rules are possible. Chosen/rejected data alone do not establish that a system used Bradley–Terry, trained a separate reward model, or ran PPO; inspect the actual loss and update procedure.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个数据集对每个提示给出 chosen/rejected 两个回答。只看这个格式，已经知道什么？还不能确定什么？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A dataset gives chosen and rejected responses for each prompt. What is known from this format alone, and what remains undetermined?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 已知每条记录提供同一提示下的相对选择。尚不能确定选择准则、选择概率模型、是否存在显式标量奖励，以及数据最终进入奖励建模、DPO 还是其他训练过程。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Each record supplies a relative choice between two responses to one prompt. The annotation criterion, probabilistic choice model, existence of a scalar reward, and downstream use in reward modeling, DPO, or another procedure remain unknown.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Ouyang et al. (2022), *Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback*](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.02155#S3.SS2)：第 3.2–3.3 节分别描述比较数据与奖励模型训练，展示“记录选择”和“拟合选择模型”是两层。
- [Rafailov et al. (2023), *Direct Preference Optimization*](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.18290#S3)：第 3–4 节展示同类成对偏好也可直接进入策略目标，不必先运行显式奖励模型管线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Ouyang et al. (2022), §§3.2–3.3, separates collection of comparison records from fitting the reward model that consumes them.
- Rafailov et al. (2023), §§3–4, shows that pairwise preferences can instead enter a direct policy objective without first running an explicit-reward-model pipeline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
