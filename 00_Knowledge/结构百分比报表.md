# 结构百分比报表将同一报表的各项目除以明确的共同基数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common-size financial statement expresses each item relative to a stated common base.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结构百分比报表把项目金额 $X_i$ 改写为 $X_i/B$，其中共同基数 $B\ne0$。利润表常以营业收入为基数，资产负债表常以总资产为基数；基数的名称、期间和主体必须明确。它方便比较结构，但没有消除业务模式与会计政策差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common-size statement replaces an amount $X_i$ with $X_i/B$, for a stated nonzero base. Revenue is commonly used for an income statement and total assets for a balance sheet. The base, period, and reporting entity must be specified. Scaling facilitates structural comparisons without eliminating differences in business models or accounting policies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收入1000、成本700变为收入1200、成本900时，成本增加200；成本率则从70%升至75%，上升5个百分点。金额变化与结构变化回答不同问题，不能把5个百分点写成成本只增长5%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If revenue and cost change from 1,000 and 700 to 1,200 and 900, cost rises by 200 while the cost ratio rises from 70% to 75%, a five-percentage-point increase. Amount changes and structural changes answer different questions; this does not mean cost grew by only 5%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零收入无法按收入归一化；收入负值或项目净额抵销时，百分比仍可能算得出，但不能照普通正收入结构解释。各层小计与明细也不应重复相加。用于跨期比较时再查[[财务报表比较分析]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero revenue cannot serve as a denominator. Negative revenue or netted items can produce computable percentages that do not have the usual positive-revenue interpretation. Subtotals and their components must not be double-counted. See [[财务报表比较分析|comparative analysis]] for cross-period use.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
