# Singular values
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*奇异值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix may stretch some input directions strongly and barely preserve others. Its **singular values** measure these stretch amounts along the perpendicular directions selected by the [[奇异值分解|singular value decomposition (SVD)]]. Each amount compares the length of an output with a unit-length input. It records size, while the corresponding [[奇异向量|singular vectors]] describe the directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵可能把某些输入方向拉得很长，却几乎抹去另一些方向。**奇异值**衡量[[奇异值分解|SVD]]所选垂直方向上的伸缩大小：以长度为1的输入为基准，观察输出有多长。数值负责说明大小，对应的[[奇异向量]]负责说明方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Seeing two stretch amounts
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

> [!example] Three output coordinates do not mean three singular values / 三个输出坐标不代表三个奇异值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take
> $$A=\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix}.$$
> The unit input $(1,0)^T$ becomes $(3,0,0)^T$, of length 3. The perpendicular unit input $(0,1)^T$ becomes $(0,1,0)^T$, of length 1. The singular values are therefore $3,1$. The third output coordinate is always zero, but there are only two diagonal positions in this $3\times2$ matrix. Here $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]] and length means the [[欧氏范数|Euclidean norm]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 单位输入 $(1,0)^T$ 变成长度为3的 $(3,0,0)^T$，与之垂直的单位输入 $(0,1)^T$ 变成长度为1的 $(0,1,0)^T$，所以奇异值为 $3,1$。第三输出坐标始终为零，但 $3\times2$ 矩阵只有两个对角位置，不能因此再添一个奇异值。$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，长度由[[欧氏范数]]衡量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The nonnegative, ordered list
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*非负、按大小排列的一列数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ have $m$ rows and $n$ columns, with positive integers $m,n$, and real or complex entries. In a full SVD,
$$A=U\Sigma V^*,\qquad p=\min(m,n),\qquad
\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_p\ge0.$$
The **singular values** are the $p$ displayed diagonal entries of the rectangular diagonal matrix $\Sigma$. This card uses the matrix convention that lists exactly $\min(m,n)$ values, including zeros. The star is [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]: transpose and change each $a+bi$ to $a-bi$, where $i^2=-1$. The square factors $U,V$ are [[Unitary 矩阵|unitary]] (orthogonal over the reals), so their changes of coordinates preserve length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 有 $m$ 行、$n$ 列，$m,n$ 为正整数，元素可以为实数或复数。在完整 SVD 中，**奇异值**就是长方对角矩阵 $\Sigma$ 上显示的 $p$ 个对角元。本卡采用矩阵惯例，固定列出 $\min(m,n)$ 个值，包含其中的零。星号是[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]：转置后把每个 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$，其中 $i^2=-1$。方形因子 $U,V$ 为[[Unitary 矩阵|酉矩阵]]，实数时为正交矩阵，所以坐标变换本身不改变长度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a matched unit input/output pair $v_i,u_i$, the SVD gives $Av_i=\sigma_i u_i$, hence $\|Av_i\|_2=\sigma_i$. This is why the values are stretch **magnitudes**. The magnitude can exceed 1, lie between 0 and 1, or be zero. A zero means that the associated input direction disappears. Nonnegative does not mean strictly positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对配对的单位输入、输出向量 $v_i,u_i$，SVD 给出 $Av_i=\sigma_i u_i$，因而 $\|Av_i\|_2=\sigma_i$。这说明奇异值记录的是伸缩的**大小**：可以大于1、处于0与1之间，也可以为零。零表示对应输入方向被消去；非负并不是严格为正。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Signs and complex phases belong to the vectors, not to the singular values. The one-entry matrices $[-3]$ and $[3i]$ each have singular value $3$: the output reverses or changes phase, but its length is still three times the input length. Nor are singular values generally the absolute values of a matrix's [[特征对|eigenvalues]]; eigenvalues compare an input with a multiple of itself, whereas SVD can use different input and output directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正负号与复相位由向量承担，不由奇异值承担。单元素矩阵 $[-3]$ 与 $[3i]$ 的奇异值都是 $3$：输出可能反向或改变相位，但长度仍是输入的三倍。一般也不能把奇异值当成矩阵[[特征对|特征值]]的绝对值；特征值比较输入与其自身的倍数，而 SVD 可以使用不同的输入和输出方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What is fixed, and where the zeros go
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*哪些量唯一，以及零应该列在哪里*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ordered singular-value list is fixed by $A$, even when singular vectors are not unique. One way to see this is the [[奇异值与Gram谱|Gram spectral identity]]: if the eigenvalues of $A^*A$ are $\lambda_1\ge\cdots\ge\lambda_n\ge0$, then $\sigma_i=\sqrt{\lambda_i}$ for $1\le i\le p$. For example, positive Gram eigenvalues $25,4$ give positive singular values $5,2$, not $25,4$. If $n>p$, the additional $n-p$ Gram eigenvalues are zeros omitted from our length-$p$ list.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使奇异向量不唯一，矩阵 $A$ 的降序奇异值列表也已确定。[[奇异值与Gram谱|Gram 谱恒等式]]说明：若 $A^*A$ 的特征值为 $\lambda_1\ge\cdots\ge\lambda_n\ge0$，则 $1\le i\le p$ 时，$\sigma_i=\sqrt{\lambda_i}$。例如，正 Gram 特征值 $25,4$ 对应正奇异值 $5,2$，不能直接照抄 $25,4$。若 $n>p$，额外 $n-p$ 个 Gram 特征值为零，不列入本卡长度为 $p$ 的列表。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the [[矩阵秩|rank]] is $r$, exactly the first $r$ values are positive and the remaining $p-r$ are zero; see [[奇异值与秩|the rank identity]]. Repetition is allowed: the diagonal matrix with entries $2,2,0$ has values $2,2,0$. The zero matrix has $p$ zero singular values. Keeping precisely the positive directions gives an exact [[紧SVD|compact SVD]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若[[矩阵秩]]为 $r$，则恰有前 $r$ 个值为正，其余 $p-r$ 个为零，详见[[奇异值与秩]]。允许重复，例如对角元为 $2,2,0$ 的对角矩阵，其奇异值就是 $2,2,0$。零矩阵则有 $p$ 个零奇异值。仅保留全部正方向得到仍然精确的[[紧SVD]]。

> [!warning] A short list can omit invisible input directions / 短列表可能没有列出不可见输入方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A=[3\ 0]$, $m=1,n=2$, so the list is just $(3)$. Yet $A(0,1)^T=0$: an entire input direction is lost. The [[零空间|nullspace]] has dimension $n-r=1$, although the length-$p$ list has no zero. “All listed singular values are positive” means rank $\min(m,n)$; for a wide matrix it does not mean that every input can be recovered.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $A=[3\ 0]$，$m=1,n=2$，列表只有 $(3)$；但 $A(0,1)^T=0$，仍丢失一条输入方向。[[零空间]]维数为 $n-r=1$，尽管长度为 $p$ 的列表中没有零。“列出的奇异值全为正”表示秩为 $\min(m,n)$；对宽矩阵，它并不表示每个输入都能恢复。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The largest value measures the greatest possible length amplification, as formalized by [[谱范数与最大奇异值|the spectral-norm identity]]. That interpretation concerns the Euclidean geometry of the chosen coordinates. Changing measurement units can change the matrix and its singular values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最大奇异值衡量可能出现的最大长度放大，精确结论见[[谱范数与最大奇异值]]。它描述的是所选坐标中的欧氏几何；改变计量单位会改变矩阵，也可能改变奇异值。
