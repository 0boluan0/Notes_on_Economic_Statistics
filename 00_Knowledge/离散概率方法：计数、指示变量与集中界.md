# 离散概率方法：计数、指示变量与集中界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Discrete probability methods: counting, indicators, and concentration bounds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 在有限或可数样本空间中计算随机事件、期望与集中程度，并用随机变量压缩复杂结果。
> **具体锚点：** 洗牌后某张牌位置均匀，但两张牌位置不独立；对称性给边际分布，约束决定依赖。
> **核心难点：** 等可能必须由随机机制证明；线性期望不需要独立，而方差相加通常需要控制协方差。
> **为什么重要：** 随机算法、哈希、图、排队和统计抽样都从离散模型开始。
> **继续：** 先明确样本空间和随机机制，再用条件化、指示变量和尾界；连续/一般框架见 [[概率论基础]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It calculates events, expectations, and concentration in finite or countable sample spaces, using random variables to compress complex outcomes.
> **Concrete anchor:** After shuffling, the position of each individual card is uniform, but the positions of two cards are not independent. Symmetry determines the marginals, while the sampling constraint determines dependence.
> **Central difficulty:** Equiprobability must be justified by the random mechanism. Linearity of expectation does not require independence, whereas adding variances generally requires control of covariance.
> **Why it matters:** Randomised algorithms, hashing, graphs, queues, and statistical sampling all begin with discrete models.
> **Continue with:** First specify the sample space and random mechanism, then use conditioning, indicators, and tail bounds. For the general framework, see [[概率空间、条件概率与 Bayes 法则|probability foundations]].

## 等可能空间与计数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equiprobable spaces and counting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有基本结果等可能时，$P(A)=|A|/|\Omega|$。随机排列、抽样有放回/无放回产生不同样本空间。先定义每个基本结果是什么，再计数，避免分子分母使用不同粒度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formula $P(A)=|A|/|\Omega|$ applies only when the elementary outcomes are equiprobable. Random permutations and sampling with or without replacement generate different sample spaces. Define an elementary outcome before counting so that numerator and denominator use the same level of description.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计数前依次问：对象能否区分、顺序是否重要、能否重复、约束是否使不同序列具有不同概率。组合公式只负责大小，不负责证明随机机制确实在这些对象上均匀。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before counting, ask whether objects are distinguishable, whether order matters, whether repetition is allowed, and whether constraints make different sequences unequally likely. Combinatorial formulas count objects; they do not prove that the random mechanism is uniform over those objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 随机变量与指示变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Random variables and indicator variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指示变量 $I_i$ 在事件发生时为 1，$E[I_i]=P(A_i)$。计数 X 可写为指标和，故 $E[X]=\sum P(A_i)$，即使事件相关也成立；这是随机图和碰撞问题的高效工具。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An indicator $I_i$ equals one when an event occurs, and $E[I_i]=P(A_i)$. A count $X$ can be written as a sum of indicators, giving $E[X]=\sum P(A_i)$ even when the events are dependent. This is an efficient tool for random graphs and collision problems.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：生日碰撞的期望
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: expected birthday collisions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 n 个人的生日独立且均匀落在 365 天。对每一对 $i<j$ 定义 $I_{ij}=1$ 表示生日相同，则碰撞对数 $C=\sum_{i<j}I_{ij}$。因为 $E[I_{ij}]=1/365$，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume that the birthdays of $n$ people are independent and uniform over 365 days. For each pair $i<j$, define $I_{ij}=1$ when the two birthdays match. The number of colliding pairs is $C=\sum_{i<j}I_{ij}$. Since $E[I_{ij}]=1/365$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E[C]=\binom n2\frac1{365}.
$$

这些指标彼此不全独立，但求期望不受影响。若问题改成“至少有一对碰撞的概率”，仅知道 $E[C]$ 不足以给精确答案，需要补事件乘积、容斥或界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The indicators are not all mutually independent, but that does not affect the expectation. If the question instead asks for the probability of at least one collision, knowing $E[C]$ alone is not enough for an exact answer; one needs the complement product, inclusion–exclusion, or a bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 方差、协方差与集中
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Variance, covariance, and concentration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$Var(\sum X_i)=\sum Var(X_i)+2\sum Cov(X_i,X_j)$。Markov 不等式只需非负，Chebyshev 只需有限方差；更强 Chernoff/Hoeffding 界需要独立或有界等条件。界的假设与指数速度同样重要。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$Var(\sum X_i)=\sum Var(X_i)+2\sum Cov(X_i,X_j)$. Markov's inequality requires only non-negativity, and Chebyshev's inequality only finite variance. Stronger Chernoff or Hoeffding bounds require conditions such as independence or boundedness. The assumptions behind a bound matter as much as its exponential rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Markov 给 $P(X\ge a)\le E[X]/a$；对均值为 $\mu$ 的变量，Chebyshev 给 $P(|X-\mu|\ge t)\le Var(X)/t^2$。两者是“信息有多少，就保证多少”的基线：若已知分布或 mgf，往往能给更紧的界，但不能把更强结论的条件省略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Markov gives $P(X\ge a)\le E[X]/a$. For a variable with mean $\mu$, Chebyshev gives $P(|X-\mu|\ge t)\le Var(X)/t^2$. These are baseline guarantees that use only the information stated. A known distribution or mgf may yield tighter bounds, but the assumptions required for those stronger conclusions cannot be omitted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 条件化与对称性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditioning and symmetry*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在离散问题中，先条件于一个会解除依赖或固定规模的变量，常能把复杂空间分成简单层。对称性只有在存在保持随机机制的双射时才成立；“标签看起来一样”不是证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a discrete problem, conditioning first on a variable that removes dependence or fixes the size often decomposes a complicated space into simple layers. Symmetry is valid only when a bijection preserves the random mechanism; labels that merely look similar are not a proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 随机过程入口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Entry point to stochastic processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重复独立试验生成 Bernoulli/Binomial 结构，随机游走把增量累加，Markov 链只保留当前状态。过程选择取决于历史怎样影响下一步。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated independent trials generate Bernoulli and Binomial structures, a random walk accumulates increments, and a Markov chain retains only the current state. The appropriate process depends on how history affects the next step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 线性期望为什么不需要独立？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does linearity of expectation not require independence?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 期望对有限和总是线性；独立主要在乘积期望、分布卷积和方差简化中发挥作用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Expectation is linear for every finite sum. Independence is mainly used for expectations of products, convolution of distributions, and simplification of variances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 随机抽两张不放回的牌，它们为何相关？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why are two cards drawn without replacement dependent?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 第一张结果改变第二张的剩余牌组成和条件概率，尽管每个位置边际上仍可能均匀。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The first draw changes the composition of the remaining deck and therefore the conditional probability of the second draw, even though each position may still be marginally uniform.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 什么时候用指示变量特别有效？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When are indicator variables especially effective?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 目标是事件个数、配对数、碰撞数或满足性质的对象数时，可拆成许多 0/1 事件并直接求期望。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> When the target is a number of events, pairs, collisions, or objects with a property, it can often be decomposed into many zero-one events whose expectations are immediate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用 Chebyshev 界前必须确认什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What must be checked before using Chebyshev's inequality?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 目标变量有有限均值和方差，并且阈值确实是相对均值的偏离；若变量是和，还要正确保留协方差项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The variable must have finite mean and variance, and the threshold must be a deviation from the mean. For a sum, the covariance terms must also be handled correctly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
