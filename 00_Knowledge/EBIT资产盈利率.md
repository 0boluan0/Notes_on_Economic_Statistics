# EBIT资产盈利率是期间息税前利润相对于所声明总资产占用的比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
EBIT return on assets measures period earnings before interest and taxes relative to a stated total-asset base.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

EBIT 资产盈利率使用息税前利润，而非净利润作为分子。设该期间 EBIT 为 $E$、所采用总资产口径为 $A_*>0$，则 $r_E=E/A_*$；分析期间资金占用时通常采用平均资产，使用期末代理必须明说。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
EBIT return on assets uses earnings before interest and taxes rather than net income. With period EBIT $E$ and specified positive asset base $A_*$, $r_E=E/A_*$. Average assets commonly represent period capital usage; an end-period proxy must be identified explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如全年 EBIT 为 20、净利润为 12、平均资产为 200，则本比率为 10%，[[总资产净利率]]则为 6%。两者回答的盈利层次不同，不应互为别名。若净收入 $S>0$ 且资产口径相同，代数上有：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For annual EBIT of 20, net income of 12, and average assets of 200, this ratio is 10%, while [[总资产净利率|net-income ROA]] is 6%. They concern different earnings levels and are not aliases. With positive net revenue $S$ and the same asset basis:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\frac{EBIT}{A_*}=\frac{EBIT}{S}\times\frac{S}{A_*}.$$

右侧是 EBIT 利润率乘[[总资产周转率]]；只有本题营业利润恰好等于 EBIT 时，才可把第一项直接称为[[营业利润率]]。它仍是会计利润／账面资产指标，不是税后经营资本回报、现金收益率或投资者实现回报；“融资前”也不保证不同资产计量的公司可直接比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The right side is EBIT margin times [[总资产周转率|asset turnover]]. The first factor equals [[营业利润率|reported operating margin]] only when operating profit equals EBIT in the stated setup. This remains an accounting-profit-to-book-assets measure, not after-tax operating-capital return, cash yield, or investor realized return. A pre-financing numerator does not remove asset-measurement differences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
