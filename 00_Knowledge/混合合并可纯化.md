# 在有限标准信号博弈的 weak-PBE 口径下，每个 mixed pooling PBE 都可从共同支持中选一个信号纯化为 pure pooling PBE
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a finite standard signaling game under weak PBE, every mixed pooling PBE can be purified into a pure pooling PBE by selecting one signal from the common support.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设所有发送者类型在一份 mixed pooling PBE 中使用同一分布 $q\in\Delta(S)$。任选 $s^*\in\operatorname{supp}q$，让所有类型改为以概率 1 发送 $s^*$；其余参与者策略先保持不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose every sender type uses the same distribution $q\in\Delta(S)$ in a mixed pooling PBE. Choose any $s^*\in\operatorname{supp}q$ and let every type send $s^*$ with probability one, initially leaving the other players' strategies unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么接收者仍然最优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the receiver remains optimal*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共同分布意味着原均衡中每个 $q(s)>0$ 的信号后 posterior 都等于先验。纯化后，$s^*$ 后的 posterior 与接收者反应不变。对原共同支持中其余现已离轨的信号，指定先验并沿用原反应；这些反应原本就是同一先验下的最佳反应。原本就在路径外的信号则沿用原 belief 与反应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because all types use the same distribution, the posterior after every signal with $q(s)>0$ equals the prior in the original equilibrium. After purification, the posterior and receiver response after $s^*$ are unchanged. At every other signal formerly in the common support and now off path, assign the prior and retain the original response, which was already optimal under that prior. Signals that were already off path retain their original beliefs and responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么不会出现新的发送者偏离
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why no new sender deviation appears*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个类型原本对 $q$ 的支持内信号都赋正概率，所以这些信号在给定接收者完整反应时必须给该类型相同的最大收益；支持外信号也不能更优。改为共同选择 $s^*$ 只是在原最优支持中选定一项，不改变任何偏离所诱导的接收者反应。因此每个类型仍无有利偏离，所得 assessment 是 pure pooling weak PBE。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every type originally assigned positive probability to every signal in the support of $q$, so those signals had to give that type the same maximal payoff under the receiver's complete response strategy; no signal outside the support could be better. Selecting the common signal $s^*$ merely chooses one action from each type's original optimal support and leaves the receiver response induced by every deviation unchanged. No type therefore gains a profitable deviation, and the resulting assessment is a pure pooling weak PBE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个纯化依赖有限信号集合、所有类型使用完全相同的分布，以及 weak PBE 允许为新离轨信号直接指定合法 belief。它不是一般的 purification theorem，也不自动适用于连续信号或更强 consistency 概念。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The construction relies on a finite signal set, an identical distribution for every type, and the weak-PBE permission to specify a legal belief directly at a newly off-path signal. It is not a general purification theorem and does not automatically extend to continuous signals or stronger consistency concepts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- MIT Lecture 2, slide 11, verifies that a newly off-path information set under weak PBE requires a legal belief and sequential rationality, while Bayes consistency is imposed on path.
