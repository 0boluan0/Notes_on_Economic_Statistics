# 普通需求剩余变化一般不等于补偿变化或等价变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Marshallian surplus change generally differs from compensating and equivalent variation.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[消费者剩余]] 的价格变化积分固定名义收入；[[补偿变化]] 固定原效用并使用新价格作货币基准；[[等价变化]] 固定新效用并使用原价格作货币基准。收入效应使这些需求路径不同，因此三种金额一般不能互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[消费者剩余|Marshallian surplus]] holds nominal income fixed. [[补偿变化|CV]] uses initial utility and new reference prices, whereas [[等价变化|EV]] uses final utility and original reference prices. Income effects generally separate the amounts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仅一项价格由 $p_0$ 变为 $p_1$、其他价格及名义收入固定，且支出函数可微、补偿需求可积分时，在“福利改善为正”的约定下：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one price changing from $p_0$ to $p_1$, hold other prices and nominal income fixed. With differentiable expenditure and integrable compensated demand, the gain-positive measures are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta CS^M=-\int_{p_0}^{p_1}x(p,m)\,dp,
\qquad CV=-\int_{p_0}^{p_1}h(p,u_0)\,dp,
\qquad EV=-\int_{p_0}^{p_1}h(p,u_1)\,dp.
$$

精确重合的一组充分条件是：$u(x,y)=b(x)+y$、计价物 $y$ 的价格固定为 $1$，且实际与补偿问题在整个相关范围都保留正数量的 $y$。此时相关商品没有收入效应，三条需求相同，故 $\Delta CS^M=CV=EV$。仅在一个点收入效应为零，不足以推出有限价格变化的全程相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sufficient case is $u(x,y)=b(x)+y$, with the numeraire price fixed at one and positive numeraire consumption throughout both actual and compensated choices. Demand then has no income effect over the relevant range, making all three measures equal. A zero income effect at one point is insufficient for a finite change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $b(x)=12x-x^2/2$、收入 $100$，价格 $7\to8$ 时需求 $5\to4$，计价物消费在各反事实中均为正，三种变化同为 $-4.5$。相反，$u=\sqrt{xy}$、收入 $100$、$p_y=1$、$p_x:10\to20$ 时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $b(x)=12x-x^2/2$ and income $100$, a price increase from $7$ to $8$ changes demand from $5$ to $4$; all relevant numeraire choices remain positive and each measure is $-4.5$. In contrast, take $u=\sqrt{xy}$, income $100$, $p_y=1$, and $p_x$ rising from $10$ to $20$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
CV=100-100\sqrt2\approx-41.421,
\qquad
\Delta CS^M=-50\ln2\approx-34.657,
\qquad
EV=100/\sqrt2-100\approx-29.289.
$$

这里 $x(p,100)=50/p$，所以 CS 的有限变化可直接积分；不能以三者都为负为由把数值混用。收入效应小可支持近似，但不保证 [[税收超额负担|超额负担]] 的相对误差也小；多个价格同时改变时，还须说明普通需求线积分的路径，不能悄悄套用单价公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary demand is $50/p$, so its finite change integrates directly. Agreement on the negative sign does not make the magnitudes interchangeable. Small income effects can support approximation without guaranteeing a small relative error in [[税收超额负担|excess burden]]. For multiple price changes, specify the ordinary-demand integration path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
