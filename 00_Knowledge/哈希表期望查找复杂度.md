# 哈希表的期望常数查找不等于最坏常数查找

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Expected constant-time lookup in a hash table is not worst-case constant-time lookup*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区别
> “哈希表查找是 $O(1)$”省略了条件。更准确地说，在负载受控且哈希或探测具有合适随机性时，查找可有期望常数成本；某次操作的最坏成本仍可能是 $\Theta(n)$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Hash-table lookup is $O(1)$” suppresses its conditions. More precisely, lookup can have expected constant cost when load is controlled and hashing or probing has suitable randomness; the worst cost of an individual operation may still be $\Theta(n)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拉链法中，许多键可能全部进入同一桶，查找就要检查整条长度为 $n$ 的链。开放寻址中，键可能经历很长的探测序列；在线性探测里，连续占用区域还会形成聚集。即便日常工作负载下这些情况很少，最坏界也没有因此消失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under chaining, many keys may all land in one bucket, forcing lookup through a chain of length $n$. Under open addressing, a key may traverse a long probe sequence, and linear probing can form clusters of occupied slots. These cases may be rare in an ordinary workload, but rarity does not remove the worst-case bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三种“平均”不要混用

- **期望成本**：平均可能来自随机选择的哈希函数、探测序列或明确的输入分布。
- **摊还成本**：把一串操作中的偶发扩容费用分摊，并不需要输入随机。
- **平均实测延迟**：是某个真实工作负载上的统计量，可能掩盖长尾。

这三者可以同时为常数阶，也可以表现不同。写复杂度时应说明究竟对什么取平均，以及是否包含重建成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Expected cost** averages over a random hash choice, probe sequence, or an explicitly modelled input distribution.
- **Amortized cost** spreads occasional rebuilds across a sequence and does not require random input.
- **Measured average latency** is a statistic of one real workload and may hide a long tail.

All three may be constant in a well-run table, but they answer different questions. A complexity claim should say what is being averaged and whether rebuild work is included.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与辨别
> “扩容是摊还 $O(1)$”能否证明每次键查找最坏也是 $O(1)$？
>
> **答案：** 不能。摊还结论只分配一串更新中的重建费用；碰撞形成的长链或长探测仍可让某次查找达到 $\Theta(n)$。
