# Minimum spanning trees
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*最小生成树的定义与唯一性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **minimum spanning tree (MST)** connects every vertex without a cycle and has the smallest possible sum of edge weights among all such trees. For example, if edge weights are cable costs, it chooses a whole connecting network with minimum total cost. The optimization compares complete trees, not individual routes from a chosen starting vertex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**最小生成树（MST）**把全部顶点连起来、不形成环，并在所有这样的树中使边权总和最小。例如，边权表示铺设电缆的费用时，它选择总费用最低的完整连接网络。比较的对象是整棵树，而不是从某个起点出发的各条路线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The feasible trees and their total weights
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*允许选择的树及其总权重*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $G=(V,E,w)$ be a **finite, nonempty, connected undirected graph**, with a finite real [[图类型的建模边界|edge weight]] $w(e)$ for every edge $e$. A [[生成树|spanning tree]] $T=(V,F)$ keeps all vertices and selects an edge set $F\subseteq E$ that is connected and acyclic. Its total weight is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $G=(V,E,w)$ 是**有限、非空、连通的无向图**，每条边 $e$ 的[[图类型的建模边界|权重]] $w(e)$ 都是有限实数。[[生成树]] $T=(V,F)$ 保留全部顶点，所选边集 $F\subseteq E$ 连通且无环。它的总权重是

$$w(T)=\sum_{e\in F}w(e).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An MST is a spanning tree $T_*$ such that $w(T_*)\le w(T)$ for **every** spanning tree $T$ of the same weighted graph. There is no selected root in this definition. By the [[Forest 的边数公式|tree edge-count formula]], all spanning trees already have $|V|-1$ edges; what changes is which edges are chosen and the sum of their weights. The [[搜索树与 MST 的目标差异|search-tree and shortest-path objectives]] are different.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若生成树 $T_*$ 对同一带权图的**每一棵**生成树 $T$ 都满足 $w(T_*)\le w(T)$，它就是 MST。这个定义没有指定根。由[[Forest 的边数公式|树的边数公式]]，所有生成树本来就有 $|V|-1$ 条边；变化的是选中了哪些边，以及这些边的权重和。[[搜索树与 MST 的目标差异|搜索树与最短路树的目标]]与此不同。

> [!example] Three possible trees · 三种候选树
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the triangle on $a,b,c$, let $w(ab)=1$, $w(ac)=1$ and $w(bc)=2$, with $ab$ denoting the undirected edge joining $a,b$. Every spanning tree uses two edges. The choices $\{ab,ac\}$, $\{ab,bc\}$ and $\{ac,bc\}$ have total weights $2,3,3$. Thus $\{ab,ac\}$ is the unique MST, even though two edge weights are equal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在顶点 $a,b,c$ 的三角形上，令 $w(ab)=1,w(ac)=1,w(bc)=2$，其中 $ab$ 表示连接 $a,b$ 的无向边。每棵生成树选两条边；$\{ab,ac\}$、$\{ab,bc\}$、$\{ac,bc\}$ 的总权重分别为 $2,3,3$。因此 $\{ab,ac\}$ 是唯一 MST，尽管有两条边的权重相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An MST exists under the stated assumptions. Connectedness supplies at least one spanning tree by the [[生成树的存在条件|existence theorem]]. A finite edge set has only finitely many subsets, so there are only finitely many candidate spanning trees. Their finite real total weights form a nonempty finite list, which has a minimum. This establishes existence without requiring distinct, positive or nonzero weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
在上述条件下，MST 一定存在。连通性通过[[生成树的存在条件|存在定理]]保证至少一棵生成树。有限边集只有有限多个子集，因此候选生成树也只有有限多棵；它们的有限实数总权重构成非空有限列表，必有最小值。这个存在证明不要求权重互异、为正或非零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What guarantees uniqueness?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*什么条件保证唯一？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Unique” means that exactly one **edge set** attains the minimum. Several edge sets may have the same minimum total: if all three triangle weights are $1$, all three two-edge trees have weight $2$, so there are three MSTs. Equal weights do not force this outcome; the $1,1,2$ example above has only one minimum tree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
“唯一”是指恰好一个**边集**达到最小值。不同边集可能达到同一个最小总权重：若三角形的三条边权全为 $1$，三棵两边树的总权重都为 $2$，所以有三棵 MST。等权并不强迫这种结果；前面的 $1,1,2$ 例只有一棵最优树。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The separate [[最小生成树的唯一性|MST uniqueness theorem]] gives a useful sufficient condition: pairwise distinct edge weights guarantee a unique MST. The linked card gives a complete exchange proof and shows why distinctness is not necessary. This leaves the definition here applicable both to a unique optimum and to several optimal trees.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
独立的[[最小生成树的唯一性|MST 唯一性定理]]给出一条有用的充分条件：不同边的权重两两互异，就保证 MST 唯一。该卡给出完整交换证明，也说明互异并非必要条件。这里的定义同时适用于唯一最优树和多棵最优树。

## Zero weights, negative weights and disconnected inputs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*零权、负权与不连通输入*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weights need not be geometric distances. A triangle with weights $-3,0,2$ has candidate tree totals $-3,-1,2$, so its MST has total $-3$. A zero-weight edge still connects its endpoints. The tree constraint remains essential: adding every negative edge could create a cycle, and such a graph would not be an MST even if its total weight were lower.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
权重不必是几何距离。边权为 $-3,0,2$ 的三角形，其候选树总权重为 $-3,-1,2$，所以 MST 总权重为 $-3$。零权边仍然连接两端。树的约束依然不可省略：把所有负权边都加进来可能形成环，即使总权重更低，也不再是 MST。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A one-vertex graph has the empty edge set as its unique spanning tree, with weight $0$ by the empty-sum convention. The empty vertex set is outside the stated scope. If parallel edges or loops are permitted, edge identity is retained: exchanging equal-weight parallel edges can yield different trees, while a self-loop never belongs to a tree, even when its weight is negative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
单顶点图的唯一生成树使用空边集，按空和约定总权重为 $0$。空顶点集不在本卡范围内。若允许平行边或自环，仍须保留边的身份：互换等权平行边可以产生不同的树；自环则无论权重是否为负，都不能属于树。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A disconnected graph has no spanning tree on all vertices, so it has no MST in this sense. Choosing an MST inside each [[无向图连通分量|connected component]] instead gives a **minimum spanning forest**, with one tree per original component. This componentwise requirement matters, especially with negative weights. Algorithms for constructing these optima use the [[MST 割性质|cut property]] to preserve the possibility of completing their selected edges to an MST.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
不连通图没有覆盖全部顶点的一棵生成树，因此没有这里所定义的 MST。改为在每个[[无向图连通分量]]内选一棵 MST，得到**最小生成森林**，即每个原分量各有一棵树。这个逐分量生成要求不能省略，尤其是在允许负权时。构造这些最优结果的算法利用[[MST 割性质]]，保证当前已选边仍能补成某棵 MST。
