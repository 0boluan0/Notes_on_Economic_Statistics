# 一阶差分算子用相邻时点之差度量离散变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The first-difference operator measures discrete change between adjacent periods*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 一阶差分算子定义为
> $$
> \Delta y_t=y_t-y_{t-1}=(1-L)y_t.
> $$
> 它把水平序列映射成相邻两期的变化量。这里的 $\Delta$ 是作用在整条序列上的规则，不是一个可以脱离对象单独解释的普通数值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first-difference operator maps a level sequence into adjacent-period changes: $\Delta y_t=y_t-y_{t-1}=(1-L)y_t$. It is an operation on a sequence rather than an ordinary scalar.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重复施加算子得到
$$
\Delta^2y_t=(1-L)^2y_t=y_t-2y_{t-1}+y_{t-2}.
$$
二阶差分衡量“一阶变化本身怎样改变”，不是简单的 $y_t-y_{t-2}$。后者是 $(1-L^2)y_t$，属于周期为 2 的季节差分。一般地，[[普通与季节差分|$\Delta_s=1-L^s$ 与 $\Delta^s=(1-L)^s$]] 是不同滤波器。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated differencing gives $\Delta^2y_t=y_t-2y_{t-1}+y_{t-2}$, the change in the first change. It is not $y_t-y_{t-2}$, which is the seasonal operator $(1-L^2)y_t$. More generally, $1-L^s$ and $(1-L)^s$ are different filters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

差分会改变学习对象和样本边界。第一项通常因缺少 $y_{t-1}$ 而不可得；做 $d$ 次普通差分至少损失 $d$ 个起始位置。把差分后的增长或变化模型还原成水平预测时，还必须带回最后已知水平与累积预测误差，不能只把点预测机械相加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differencing changes both the target and the usable boundary of the sample. Reconstructing level forecasts requires the last observed level and accumulation of forecast uncertainty, not merely adding point forecasts back without a distributional calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $\Delta^2y_t$ 是否等于 $y_t-y_{t-2}$？
>
> **答案：** 不等于。前者是 $y_t-2y_{t-1}+y_{t-2}$；后者是 $(1-L^2)y_t$。
