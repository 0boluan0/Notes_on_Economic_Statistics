# 财政支出增长率是支出增量与可比基期支出的比值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fiscal expenditure growth is the change in expenditure divided by comparable base-period expenditure.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财政支出增长率回答“支出比基期增加或减少了百分之几”。设两个可比期间的支出为 $G_0>0$、$G_1$，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expenditure growth asks how much spending has changed relative to its base. For comparable periods with $G_0>0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
g_G=\frac{G_1-G_0}{G_0}.
$$

支出从 200 增到 220，增量是 20，增长率是 $20/200=10\%$。分母是基期支出，不是本期支出；基期为零时，这个增长率没有定义。同比、环比和多年累计增长使用不同基期，必须写清。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An increase from 200 to 220 is a change of 20 and growth of 10%. The denominator is base-period, not current-period spending. A zero base leaves the rate undefined. Year-on-year, period-on-period and cumulative growth use different comparators.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金额按当年价格记录时，算出的是名义增长。若适用支出价格指数上涨 5%，名义增长 10% 对应的实际增长为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Current-price amounts give nominal growth. If the appropriate expenditure price index rises 5%, nominal growth of 10% implies real growth of:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
g_G^{\mathrm{real}}=\frac{1.10}{1.05}-1\approx4.76\%.
$$

不要不加说明地用消费价格指数替代所有政府投入的价格变化；这里的 5% 是构造例中明确给定的适用指数。要看财政在经济中的相对规模，还得另算 [[财政支出占比]]：支出增长，并不保证占比上升。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The 5% is a stipulated appropriate price index, not a claim that consumer-price inflation measures every government input price. Relative size requires the [[财政支出占比|expenditure-to-GDP ratio]]; expenditure growth alone does not establish a rising share.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算前还要检查两期是否采用相同政府范围、[[预算支出与决算支出|执行口径]] 和分类。某项支出新纳入统计，也可能使账面增长率上升，却没有相应的新增活动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Align coverage, [[预算支出与决算支出|budget/outturn status]] and classification. Bringing an existing activity into the reporting perimeter can raise recorded growth without a corresponding new activity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
