# Coordinatewise increase and quasiconcavity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*逐坐标递增与拟凹性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Increasing one coordinate while holding the others fixed compares choices that move in the same coordinate direction. [[拟凹函数|Quasiconcavity]] asks a different question: when two acceptable choices are mixed, does every mixture stay at or above their common threshold? In several dimensions a mixture can raise one coordinate and lower another. Coordinatewise increase alone does not answer that question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定其他坐标，只增加一个坐标，比较的是沿同一坐标方向变化的选择。[[拟凹函数|拟凹性]]问的则是：把两个达标选择混合时，每种混合是否仍达到共同门槛？在多维中，混合可能使一个坐标增加、另一个减少，逐坐标递增本身没有回答这个问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A function that really increases in both coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*确实沿两个坐标都递增的函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the strictly positive quadrant $U=(0,\infty)^2$, meaning $x>0$ and $y>0$, and let $f(x,y)=x^2+y^2$. The domain is [[凸集|convex]]: each coordinate of a mixture of two positive points is a nonnegative weighted average of two positive numbers, with weights adding to one, and is therefore positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取严格正象限 $U=(0,\infty)^2$，即 $x>0$、$y>0$，令 $f(x,y)=x^2+y^2$。这个定义域是[[凸集]]：两个正坐标点混合后，每个坐标都是两个正数的非负加权平均，权重和为一，因此仍为正。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Strictly increasing in each coordinate” means that raising that coordinate, with the other fixed, strictly raises the output. If $0<x_1<x_2$ and $y>0$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“对每个坐标严格递增”，是指保持另一坐标不变、提高该坐标时，输出严格增加。若 $0<x_1<x_2$ 且 $y>0$，则

$$f(x_2,y)-f(x_1,y)=x_2^2-x_1^2=(x_2-x_1)(x_2+x_1)>0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same calculation with $y_1<y_2$ proves strict increase in $y$. This verifies monotonicity directly; a derivative test is unnecessary. If neither coordinate decreases and at least one increases, changing coordinates one at a time also proves that the output strictly increases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将同样的计算用于 $y_1<y_2$，就得到对 $y$ 的严格递增。这里直接核实了单调性，不需要导数判据。如果两个坐标都不下降、至少一个严格增加，逐个改变坐标也能证明输出严格增加。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two qualifying inputs and one failing midpoint
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两个达标输入与一个失败中点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $a=(2,1)$ and $b=(1,2)$. The midpoint $z=(a+b)/2=(3/2,3/2)$ is an allowed input because both coordinates are positive. Direct substitution gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $a=(2,1)$、$b=(1,2)$。中点 $z=(a+b)/2=(3/2,3/2)$ 的两个坐标都为正，因此仍是合法输入。逐项代入得到

$$f(a)=2^2+1^2=5,\qquad f(b)=1^2+2^2=5,\qquad f(z)=\frac94+\frac94=\frac92<5.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sum of squares is strictly increasing in each coordinate on the positive quadrant, yet the stated midpoint violates the [[拟凹性线段判据|quasiconcavity criterion]]. A segment can exchange one coordinate for the other, which separate monotonicity does not control.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $(0,\infty)^2$ 上，$f(x,y)=x^2+y^2$ 对每个坐标严格递增。但取 $a=(2,1),b=(1,2)$，两端值均为 $5$，中点值 $f(1.5,1.5)=4.5$，违反[[拟凹性线段判据]]。

> [!example] The failed threshold is 5 · 失败门槛为 5
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[上轮廓集|upper contour set]] $C_5^+=\{(x,y)\in U:x^2+y^2\ge5\}$ contains $a$ and $b$, including their equality cases, but not $z$. A convex set must contain every midpoint of its members. This one missing midpoint proves that $C_5^+$ is not convex and that $f$ is not quasiconcave on $U$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[上轮廓集]] $C_5^+=\{(x,y)\in U:x^2+y^2\ge5\}$ 包含 $a,b$，两端恰好等于门槛也算达标，却不包含 $z$。凸集必须包含其任意两点的中点；这一处遗漏就证明 $C_5^+$ 不凸，从而 $f$ 在 $U$ 上不拟凹。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The missing comparison
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*单调性没有覆盖的比较*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From $a$ toward $b$, the first coordinate decreases while the second increases. Neither endpoint has both coordinates at least as large as the other's. Coordinatewise monotonicity therefore supplies no comparison between these endpoints or between an endpoint and the midpoint. The calculation shows exactly how the common threshold can be lost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $a$ 走向 $b$，第一坐标下降，第二坐标上升。没有哪个端点的两个坐标都不小于另一个端点；逐坐标单调性因而没有给出这些端点之间、或端点与中点之间的比较。具体计算展示了共同门槛如何丢失。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A segment can increase one coordinate while decreasing another. Monotonicity controls coordinate comparisons in the same direction; quasiconcavity controls these mixed paths as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连接两点时一个坐标可能升、另一个降。单调性控制逐坐标同向比较，拟凹性控制这种混合路径，不能由前者替代后者。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For [[一维单调函数的拟凹拟凸性|a monotone function on a real interval]], every intermediate input is ordered between its endpoints, so its value stays between theirs. That ordering argument has no counterpart for all pairs in this quadrant. The counterexample disproves a general implication; it does not say that every coordinatewise increasing function fails quasiconcavity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[一维单调函数的拟凹拟凸性|实数区间上的单调函数]]，中间输入总按实数次序夹在两端之间，函数值因此也介于两端值之间。这个排序论证不能套到正象限中的任意点对。反例否定的是一般推论，并不是说每个逐坐标递增函数都不拟凹。
