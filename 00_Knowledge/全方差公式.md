# Law of total variance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*全方差公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **law of total variance** separates overall spread into two sources: outcomes vary within each group, and the groups themselves have different means. A [[方差|variance]] measures the average squared distance from a mean. This identity tells us which mean to use at each stage, and why averaging group variances alone can miss part of the overall spread.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**全方差公式**把总体波动分成两个来源：每组内部的结果会变化，各组的均值也可能不同。[[方差]]衡量结果离均值的平方距离的平均值。这条公式告诉我们每一步该围绕哪个均值计算，也解释了为什么只平均各组方差可能漏掉一部分总体波动。

> [!example] Waiting times within and between routes · 线路内部与线路之间的等待波动
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X$ label a bus route and $Y$ be the waiting time in minutes. Within either route, the two listed waits are equally likely. The [[条件期望|conditional mean]] is the route's average wait; the [[条件方差|conditional variance]] averages squared distances from that route's own mean.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用 $X$ 标记公交线路，用 $Y$ 表示等待分钟数。每条线路内列出的两个时间等概率出现。[[条件期望|条件均值]]是该线路的平均等待时间；[[条件方差]]则围绕这条线路自己的均值，平均平方距离。
>
> | $x$ | $P(X=x)$ | Waits / 等待时间 | Mean / 均值 | Variance / 方差 |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | $0$ | $1/4$ | $0,4$ | $2$ | $4$ |
> | $1$ | $3/4$ | $4,8$ | $6$ | $4$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For route $0$, the variance is $\tfrac12(0-2)^2+\tfrac12(4-2)^2=4$. The same calculation around $6$ gives $4$ for route $1$. Their probability-weighted average is the **within-group** contribution:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 线路 $0$ 的方差为 $\tfrac12(0-2)^2+\tfrac12(4-2)^2=4$；线路 $1$ 围绕均值 $6$ 同样算得 $4$。按各组概率加权，得到**组内**贡献：
>
> $$E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)]=\tfrac14\cdot4+\tfrac34\cdot4=4.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The overall mean is $\mu=\tfrac14\cdot2+\tfrac34\cdot6=5$. The conditional mean itself takes values $2$ and $6$. Its variance is the **between-group** contribution:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 总体均值为 $\mu=\tfrac14\cdot2+\tfrac34\cdot6=5$。条件均值本身取值为 $2$ 或 $6$，它的方差就是**组间**贡献：
>
> $$\operatorname{Var}(E[Y\mid X])=\tfrac14(2-5)^2+\tfrac34(6-5)^2=3.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus the total variance is $4+3=7$ square minutes. A direct check gives $E[Y^2]=0^2/8+4^2/8+3\cdot4^2/8+3\cdot8^2/8=32$, so $\operatorname{Var}(Y)=32-5^2=7$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因此总方差为 $4+3=7$ 平方分钟。直接计算也得到 $E[Y^2]=0^2/8+4^2/8+3\cdot4^2/8+3\cdot8^2/8=32$，所以 $\operatorname{Var}(Y)=32-5^2=7$。

> [!abstract] The variance decomposition · 方差分解
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $E[Y^2]<\infty$, and a [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]] $\mathcal G$ describes the information used for conditioning, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $E[Y^2]<\infty$，且 [[σ-代数]] $\mathcal G$ 表示条件化所用的信息，则
>
> $$\operatorname{Var}(Y)=E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid\mathcal G)]+\operatorname{Var}(E[Y\mid\mathcal G]).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first term averages the uncertainty remaining after the information is known. The second measures how the conditional mean changes across information states. Both terms are finite under the stated second-moment condition, often written $Y\in L^2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一项平均“知道信息以后仍剩下的波动”；第二项衡量不同信息状态下条件均值的变化。上述有限二阶矩条件保证两项都有限，这一条件也常写成 $Y\in L^2$。

## 为什么分解成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the decomposition holds*

Write $m=E[Y\mid\mathcal G]$ for the conditional mean and $\mu=E[Y]$ for the overall mean. Each deviation splits into a within-group part and a group-mean part:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记条件均值为 $m=E[Y\mid\mathcal G]$，总体均值为 $\mu=E[Y]$。每个偏差都可以拆成“离组均值的偏差”与“组均值离总体均值的偏差”：

$$Y-\mu=(Y-m)+(m-\mu).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Squaring gives two squared terms and the cross term $2(Y-m)(m-\mu)$. That cross term has mean zero: after conditioning, $m-\mu$ is known, while $Y-m$ has conditional mean zero. Using [[全期望公式|total expectation]],
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平方后得到两个平方项以及交叉项 $2(Y-m)(m-\mu)$。交叉项的均值为零：给定信息后，$m-\mu$ 已知，而 $Y-m$ 的条件均值是零。用[[全期望公式]]写成

$$E[(Y-m)(m-\mu)]=E\!\left[(m-\mu)E[Y-m\mid\mathcal G]\right]=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This zero-product-average property is the “orthogonality” used in the [[条件期望投影|projection interpretation of conditional expectation]]. Also $E[m]=\mu$, and total expectation gives $E[(Y-m)^2]=E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid\mathcal G)]$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个“乘积平均为零”的性质，就是[[条件期望投影]]中所说的“正交”。此外，$E[m]=\mu$，全期望公式又给出 $E[(Y-m)^2]=E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid\mathcal G)]$。因此

$$\operatorname{Var}(Y)=E[(Y-m)^2]+E[(m-\mu)^2]=E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid\mathcal G)]+\operatorname{Var}(m).$$

> [!warning] When can the between-group term be omitted? · 何时能省略组间项？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both terms are nonnegative. Using only the expected conditional variance never exceeds the total variance, and strictly underestimates it whenever conditional means vary with positive probability. Equality holds exactly when $E[Y\mid\mathcal G]$ is a constant almost surely. In the bus example, omitting the second term would report $4$ instead of $7$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两项都非负。只用条件方差的期望不会高估总方差；条件均值只要不是几乎处处恒定，就会严格低估。等号成立当且仅当 $E[Y\mid\mathcal G]$ 几乎处处是常数。公交例子中，省略第二项就会把 $7$ 错算成 $4$。

## 应用入口：Poisson 随机和
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Application entry: a Poisson random sum*

When a total adds up a random number of contributions, conditioning on the number separates variation in individual contributions from variation in the count. The [[复合泊松过程|compound Poisson process]] card supplies the model, independence assumptions, and calculation for a Poisson count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总量由随机多个贡献相加时，可以先给定项数，把每项贡献的波动与项数的波动分开。[[复合泊松过程]]给出项数服从 Poisson 分布时的模型、独立性假设和具体计算。
