# 平方可积变量的方差等于条件方差的期望加条件均值的方差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The variance of a square-integrable variable equals expected conditional variance plus the variance of the conditional mean*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 恒等式
> 设 $X\in L^2$，$\mathcal G\subseteq\mathcal F$，则
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}(X)
> =E[\operatorname{Var}(X\mid\mathcal G)]
> +\operatorname{Var}(E[X\mid\mathcal G]).
> $$
> 第一项是获得信息后仍剩下的平均不确定性；第二项是不同信息状态下条件均值的变化。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $X\in L^2$ and $\mathcal G\subseteq\mathcal F$, then $\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[\operatorname{Var}(X\mid\mathcal G)]+\operatorname{Var}(E[X\mid\mathcal G])$. The first term is average uncertainty that remains after observing the information; the second is variation in conditional means across information states.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么分解成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the decomposition holds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $m=E[X\mid\mathcal G]$。由 [[条件方差]] 与 [[全期望公式]]，
$$
E[\operatorname{Var}(X\mid\mathcal G)]=E[(X-m)^2].
$$
又因为 $E[X-m\mid\mathcal G]=0$，残差 $X-m$ 与 $\mathcal G$-可测的 $m-E[X]$ 正交。于是
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}(X)
&=E[(X-E[X])^2]\\
&=E[(X-m)^2]+E[(m-E[X])^2]\\
&=E[\operatorname{Var}(X\mid\mathcal G)]
+\operatorname{Var}(m).
\end{aligned}
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Setting $m=E[X\mid\mathcal G]$, total expectation turns conditional variance into $E[(X-m)^2]$. The residual has zero conditional mean and is therefore orthogonal to the $\mathcal G$-measurable variable $m-E[X]$. Expanding the squared deviation removes the cross term and yields the decomposition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为两项都非负，忽略第二项不会高估总体方差：当 $\operatorname{Var}(E[X\mid\mathcal G])>0$ 时会严格低估；若条件均值几乎处处为常数，则恰好相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both terms are nonnegative, so omitting the second term cannot overstate total variance. It strictly understates total variance when $\operatorname{Var}(E[X\mid\mathcal G])>0$ and is exact when the conditional mean is almost surely constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 应用入口：Poisson 随机和
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Application entry: a Poisson random sum*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定 Poisson 项数后再分解随机和的方差，是这条恒等式的标准应用。具体模型、假设和推导见 [[复合泊松过程]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conditioning on a Poisson count before decomposing a random sum is a standard application. The complete model, assumptions, and derivation appear in [[复合泊松过程|compound Poisson process]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 什么时候可以只用 $E[\operatorname{Var}(X\mid\mathcal G)]$ 表示总体方差？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When can expected conditional variance alone equal the total variance?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 当且仅当条件均值 $E[X\mid\mathcal G]$ 几乎处处为常数，使条件均值的方差为零。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Exactly when $E[X\mid\mathcal G]$ is almost surely constant, so the variance of the conditional mean is zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
