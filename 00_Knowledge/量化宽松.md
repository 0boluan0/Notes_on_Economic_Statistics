# 量化宽松是中央银行以新创造的央行货币进行大规模资产购买以放松金融条件的政策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quantitative easing uses newly created central-bank money to finance large-scale asset purchases intended to ease financial conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

量化宽松通常购买较长期证券，通过影响收益率、投资组合和预期来支持支出与通胀目标。它常在短期政策利率接近[[有效利率下限]]时使用，但其含义由购买计划、融资方式和政策目的决定，不是“利率等于零”的另一个名字。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
QE usually purchases longer-term securities to influence yields, portfolios, and expectations in support of spending and inflation objectives. It is often used near the [[有效利率下限|effective lower bound]], but is defined by the purchases, financing, and purpose—not by a policy rate exactly equal to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如央行从非银行投资者买入100的长期债券，按标准银行结算链，投资者从持有债券变为持有存款，银行获得准备金并新增对应存款负债，央行持有债券。具体分录复用[[央行购债结算链]]。卖方是出售资产获得款项，不是无偿收到同额净财富；随后价格、风险承担和支出怎样变，才是传导问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a central bank buys 100 of long bonds from a non-bank investor through standard bank settlement, the investor replaces bonds with a deposit, the bank gains reserves and a deposit liability, and the central bank holds the bonds. Reuse [[央行购债结算链|the purchase settlement chain]]. The seller exchanges an asset rather than receiving a free net-wealth transfer; subsequent prices, risks, and spending are transmission questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常规[[公开市场操作]]也可以买证券，但维持结算需要的短期操作不因此都成为QE。QE对资产组合的作用见[[资产购买的组合再平衡]]；准备金增加为何不能按固定倍数推出贷款增加，见[[QE 与贷款创造]]。两者分别说明经济机制和记账边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary [[公开市场操作|open-market operations]] also trade securities; routine settlement support is not automatically QE. [[资产购买的组合再平衡|Portfolio rebalancing]] explains a transmission mechanism, while [[QE 与贷款创造|QE and loan creation]] fixes the separate accounting boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Joyce and co-authors define QE and its transmission. The Fed distinguishes purchase and reserve-management purposes; the transaction amount is illustrative.
