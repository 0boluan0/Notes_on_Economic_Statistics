# 污染检测器标记的重叠比例不能直接换算为训练—评测重叠造成的基准得分增益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The overlap rate flagged by a contamination detector cannot be converted directly into the benchmark score gain caused by training–evaluation overlap*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 两个量
> 污染标记率回答“在某个匹配规则和阈值下，多少评测样本被标记”；得分增益回答“如果相关训练暴露不存在，同一训练流程会少得多少分”。前者是 detector 输出，后者是反事实影响。两者既不共用单位，也没有固定换算比例。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The flagged rate asks how many evaluation examples a detector marks under a particular rule and threshold. Score gain asks how much lower the same training procedure would score without the relevant exposure. The first is detector output; the second is counterfactual impact. They use different units and have no fixed conversion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 标记率依赖 detector
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The flagged rate depends on the detector*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $Z(x)\in\{0,1\}$ 表示 detector 是否标记评测样本 $x$。对 $n$ 个样本，标记率为

$$
\hat c=\frac1n\sum_{j=1}^{n}Z(x_j).
$$

$\hat c$ 会随 n-gram 长度、阈值、是否匹配答案、是否允许近重复，以及可检索训练语料是否完整而变化。通用短语会产生 false positive；改写、翻译、答案单独暴露和训练语料缺失会产生 false negative。因此它不是无误差的“真实污染比例”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Z(x)\in\{0,1\}$ mark an evaluation example. The flagged rate is $\hat c=n^{-1}\sum_jZ(x_j)$. It changes with n-gram length, threshold, answer matching, near-match policy, and coverage of searchable training data. Generic phrases create false positives; paraphrases, translations, answer-only exposure, and missing corpora create false negatives. It is not an error-free true contamination rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 得分增益需要另一个设计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Score gain needs another design*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

真正想问的因果量是：相同训练流程若没有相关暴露，分数会是多少。这个 clean 反事实通常不可直接观察。比较 flagged 与 unflagged 子集的分数，或比较 clean subset 与完整集，只是受题目难度、阈值选择和样本量影响的估计；受控注入、可比 clean 训练、时间切分或其他设计才能更接近因果影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The causal question is what the same training procedure would score without relevant exposure. That clean counterfactual is usually unobserved. Comparing flagged with unflagged items, or a clean subset with the full set, remains an estimate affected by item difficulty, threshold selection, and sample size. Controlled injection, comparable clean training, temporal splits, or another design is needed to approach causal impact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Llama 3 的同一分析直接展示两者不成比例：NaturalQuestions 约 52% 被标记，但 8B、70B、405B 的 estimated gain 约为 1.6、0.9、0.8 个百分点；HellaSwag 约 85% 被标记，相应估计约为 14.8、14.8、14.3 个百分点。这些是该论文方法下的估计，不是 benchmark 永久不变的真值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One Llama 3 analysis shows the lack of proportionality. NaturalQuestions is about 52% flagged, with estimated gains of about 1.6, 0.9, and 0.8 points for 8B, 70B, and 405B; HellaSwag is about 85% flagged, with estimates near 14.8, 14.8, and 14.3 points. These are method-specific estimates, not permanent benchmark truths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> detector 报告 60% overlap。能否写成“模型分数被高估 60 个百分点”？
>
> **答案：** 不能。还要估计 detector 误差、clean/flagged 表现差异及没有相关暴露时的反事实分数。

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Grattafiori et al. (2024), *The Llama 3 Herd of Models*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21783)：第 5.1.4 节和表 15 分列 contaminated percentage 与 estimated performance gain，并明确说明方法存在误报和漏报。
- [Singh et al. (2024), *Evaluation data contamination in LLMs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.03923)：系统改变 n-gram 指标与阈值，并用被标记样本的表现差异评价 detector，支持检测与影响联合但分开估计。
