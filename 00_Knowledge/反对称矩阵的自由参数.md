# Free parameters of a skew-symmetric matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*反对称矩阵的自由参数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[实反对称矩阵|real skew-symmetric matrix]] is determined by the entries strictly above its diagonal. Reflecting each chosen entry across the diagonal changes its sign, and every diagonal entry is zero. For example, an arbitrary $3\times3$ matrix of this type is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个[[实反对称矩阵]]只由严格上三角部分的元素决定。把选好的元素反射到对角线另一侧时，需要变号；所有对角元都为零。例如，任意这类 $3\times3$ 矩阵都形如

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}0&a&b\\-a&0&c\\-b&-c&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad a,b,c\in\mathbb R.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three real numbers $a,b,c$ can be chosen separately. Once $a$ is chosen at $(1,2)$, the entry at $(2,1)$ must be $-a$; it is not a second free choice. The same applies to the other pairs. The diagonal contributes no parameters, because $a_{ii}=-a_{ii}$ forces a real $a_{ii}$ to be zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个实数 $a,b,c$ 可以分别选择。$(1,2)$ 处选定 $a$ 后，$(2,1)$ 处就必须填 $-a$，不能再自由选一次；其他位置对同理。对角线不贡献参数，因为 $a_{ii}=-a_{ii}$ 迫使实数 $a_{ii}$ 为零。

> [!important] Count only one strict triangular half · 只数一个严格三角部分
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a positive integer $n$, the number of free real parameters is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对正整数 $n$，自由实参数的数目为
>
> $$\#\{(i,j):1\le i<j\le n\}=\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}(n-i)=\frac{n(n-1)}2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The symbol $\#$ counts positions; the sum is zero when $n=1$ because there are no such positions. Row $1$ contributes $n-1$ positions above the diagonal, row $2$ contributes $n-2$, and so on. For $n=4$, there are six choices, rather than the ten in a [[对称矩阵的自由参数|symmetric matrix]]: the four diagonal choices are absent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\#$ 表示位置的数量；$n=1$ 时没有这样的位置，所以求和为零。第一行在对角线上方有 $n-1$ 个位置，第二行有 $n-2$ 个，依此类推。$n=4$ 时有六个选择，比[[对称矩阵的自由参数|对称矩阵]]的十个少了四个对角元的选择。

## Turning parameters into basis coefficients
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把参数看成基的系数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These matrices form a [[转置等式定义的矩阵子空间|real vector subspace]] of the [[矩阵空间|space of matrices]]. For size $2\times2$, every member is a multiple of one matrix:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些矩阵在[[矩阵空间]]中构成[[转置等式定义的矩阵子空间|实向量子空间]]。对 $2\times2$，其中每个成员都是同一个矩阵的倍数：

$$\begin{pmatrix}0&t\\-t&0\end{pmatrix}=t\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficient $t$ is recovered from the top-right entry, so it is unique. Thus the matrix on the right forms a one-element [[基|basis]]. More generally, let $E_{ij}$ have one $1$ at $(i,j)$ and zeros elsewhere. For each $i<j$, the matrix $E_{ij}-E_{ji}$ contributes $+1$ above the diagonal and $-1$ at its reflected position. Every skew-symmetric matrix has the unique expansion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从右上角就能读回系数 $t$，所以表示唯一。因此右边的矩阵单独构成一组[[基]]。一般地，令 $E_{ij}$ 只在 $(i,j)$ 处为 $1$、其余为零；对每个 $i<j$，矩阵 $E_{ij}-E_{ji}$ 在对角线上方贡献 $+1$，镜像位置贡献 $-1$。任意反对称矩阵都有唯一展开

$$A=\sum_{1\le i<j\le n}a_{ij}(E_{ij}-E_{ji}).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficient $a_{ij}$ is precisely the chosen upper entry. These matrices therefore span the space, and reading the upper entries back proves their linear independence. They form a basis, so the [[维数|dimension]] of this real matrix space is $n(n-1)/2$. For $n=1$, there are no pairs: the only matrix is $(0)$, its basis is empty and its dimension is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数 $a_{ij}$ 正是选定的上方元素。因此这些矩阵能张成全空间；从上方元素读回系数，又证明它们线性无关。它们构成一组基，所以这个实矩阵空间的[[维数]]为 $n(n-1)/2$。当 $n=1$ 时没有位置对，唯一矩阵是 $(0)$，基为空，维数为零。

> [!warning] One free parameter does not mean rank one · 一个自由参数不代表秩一
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The matrix $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$ belongs to the one-dimensional skew-symmetric $2\times2$ space, but its two columns are independent, so its [[矩阵秩|rank]] is $2$. Space dimension counts independently variable whole-matrix directions; rank counts independent columns of one chosen matrix.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}$ 属于一维的 $2\times2$ 反对称矩阵空间，但它的两个列向量相互独立，因此[[矩阵秩|秩]]为 $2$。空间维数数可自由变化的整个矩阵方向；秩数某个选定矩阵的独立列。
