# 制度与收入的相关不能排除反向因果共同原因与测量误差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Correlation between institutions and income cannot rule out reverse causality, common causes, or measurement error*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 识别问题
> 将制度记为 $I$、收入记为 $Y$。目标机制是 $I\rightarrow Y$，但观察数据还可能包含 $Y\rightarrow I$，以及历史、地理或政治权力 $H\rightarrow I$ 与 $H\rightarrow Y$。治理指标对制度的测量误差还可能弱化或扭曲回归关系。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let institutions be $I$ and income be $Y$. The target mechanism is $I\rightarrow Y$, but observed data may also contain $Y\rightarrow I$ and common historical, geographic, or political causes $H\rightarrow I$ and $H\rightarrow Y$. Measurement error in governance proxies can further attenuate or distort the regression relationship.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反向因果不是抽象藉口。收入增长可以增加税收、法院资源、官僚专业化和对规则质量的需求，因此今日治理质量可能部分是过去收入的结果。同样，一个“征收风险”评分只表示制度群中的一个维度，不能代表司法、市场进入、政治约束与国家能力的全部。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reverse causality is substantive: higher income can expand tax revenue, court resources, bureaucratic professionalization, and demand for better rules, so current governance can partly result from past income. Likewise, a protection-against-expropriation score captures one institutional dimension rather than the full set of courts, market entry, political constraints, and state capacity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此经验研究需要把三件事分开：先说明所测的制度维度；再用随机、准实验、历史工具或其他设计建立反事实；最后检查投资、信贷、进入、纠纷解决或公共品等机制是否真的响应。历史设计也不会消灭假设，只是用一组更明确的假设替代简单相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Empirical work should separate three tasks: define the institutional dimension measured; construct a counterfactual through random, quasi-experimental, historical-instrument, or other design; and test whether investment, credit, entry, dispute resolution, or public-goods mechanisms actually respond. A historical design does not remove assumptions; it replaces a raw correlation with a more explicit set of assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，土地登记改革后投资上升，不足以单独证明产权改善造成了发展。还要检查改革是否优先到达本来更富的地区，新增投资是长期生产性资本还是短期土地投机，信贷获得和纠纷解决是否真的变化，以及改革成本与收益落在谁身上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, an increase in investment after land registration does not by itself show that stronger property rights caused development. The evaluation should check whether reform first reached places that were already richer, whether new investment was long-lived productive capital or short-run land speculation, whether credit access and dispute resolution actually changed, and who bore the costs and received the benefits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 最小自检
> 一项跨国回归发现治理评分高的国家收入也高，还缺哪类证据？
>
> **答案：** 缺少能排除收入反向改善治理和历史/地理共同塑造二者的识别设计，也缺少评分是否准确测量目标制度维度的证据。
