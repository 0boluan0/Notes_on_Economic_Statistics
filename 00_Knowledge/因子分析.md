# 因子分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Factor analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 用少数不可直接观测的公共因子解释多个观测变量的共同协方差，同时为每个变量保留特殊误差。
> **具体锚点：** 数学、物理和统计成绩相关，可能因为共同的数量能力；每门课仍有自己的知识与测量误差。
> **核心难点：** 因子方向不可唯一识别，旋转改善解释而不自动改善拟合；“潜在因子”需要理论与外部验证。
> **为什么重要：** 量表、心理测量、经济指标和高维特征解释都需要区分共同变异与特殊变异。
> **继续：** 仅需压缩数据时转到 [[主成分分析 PCA]]；有潜变量主张时依次检查模型、因子数、旋转和新样本稳定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It explains shared covariance among observed variables with a small number of unobserved common factors while retaining variable-specific error.
> **Concrete anchor:** Mathematics, physics, and statistics scores may correlate because of a common quantitative ability, while each subject also has its own knowledge and measurement error.
> **Central difficulty:** Factor directions are not uniquely identified, and rotation can improve interpretation without improving fit. A claimed latent factor requires theory and external validation.
> **Why it matters:** Scales, psychometrics, economic indicators, and interpretation of high-dimensional features all require a separation of common and specific variation.
> **Continue with:** If only compression is needed, go to [[主成分分析 PCA|principal component analysis]]. For a latent-variable claim, check the model, number of factors, rotation, and stability in new samples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 因子模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The factor model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$X=\mu+LF+\epsilon$，其中 L 是载荷，F 为公共因子，特殊误差 $\epsilon$ 解释变量独有变异。协方差分解为 $LL^T+Psi$；对角线中的共同部分是公共度，$\Psi$ 是特殊方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$X=\mu+LF+\epsilon$, where $L$ is the loading matrix, $F$ contains common factors, and the specific errors $\epsilon$ explain variation unique to each observed variable. The covariance decomposes as $LL^T+Psi$; the shared diagonal component is the communality, while $\Psi$ contains unique variances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在常用标准化下，假定 $E[F]=0$、$Cov(F)=I$、$E[\epsilon]=0$、$Cov(F,\epsilon)=0$ 且 $Cov(\epsilon)=\Psi$ 为对角矩阵。若特殊误差彼此相关，简单因子模型的协方差结构错设，额外增加因子不一定是正确修复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under a common standardisation, assume $E[F]=0$, $Cov(F)=I$, $E[\epsilon]=0$, $Cov(F,\epsilon)=0$, and diagonal $Cov(\epsilon)=\Psi$. If specific errors are correlated, the simple factor covariance structure is misspecified; adding more factors is not necessarily the correct remedy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 因子数与估计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Choosing and estimating the number of factors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主轴因子法与最大似然因子分析的目标和假设不同；最大似然版本通常依赖多元正态以进行似然比检验和区间推断。因子数可结合 scree plot、平行分析、残差相关、理论和新样本复现，而不应只使用 eigenvalue > 1。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Principal-axis factoring and maximum-likelihood factor analysis have different objectives and assumptions. The maximum-likelihood version usually relies on multivariate normality for likelihood-ratio tests and interval inference. The number of factors should combine scree plots, parallel analysis, residual correlations, theory, and replication rather than relying solely on the eigenvalue-greater-than-one rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 旋转与可识别性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Rotation and identification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 Q 正交，$LF=(LQ)(Q^TF)$，说明因子方向不唯一。旋转追求更简单可解释的载荷结构，不会提高模型对协方差的拟合。正交旋转假设因子不相关，斜交旋转允许相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an orthogonal $Q$, $LF=(LQ)(Q^TF)$, showing that factor directions are not unique. Rotation seeks a simpler, more interpretable loading structure and does not improve the model's fit to the covariance matrix. Orthogonal rotation keeps factors uncorrelated, whereas oblique rotation permits correlation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

斜交旋转后应区分 pattern matrix 与 structure matrix：前者类似控制其他因子的回归载荷，后者是变量与因子的相关。因子相关时二者不同，混用会造成错误命名。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After oblique rotation, distinguish the pattern matrix from the structure matrix. The former resembles regression loadings controlling for other factors; the latter contains variable–factor correlations. They differ when factors correlate, and confusing them can lead to incorrect labels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：两因子测量结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: a two-factor measurement structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

六个题项中，前三个主要测数量能力，后三个主要测语言能力。若旋转后前三项在因子 1 上载荷约 0.8、后三项在因子 2 上约 0.8，而交叉载荷很小，可暂将两因子解释为数量与语言。但还需查看特殊方差、样本量、因子相关和新样本中结构是否复现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the first three of six items mainly measure quantitative ability and the last three verbal ability. If, after rotation, the first three load around 0.8 on factor 1 and the last three around 0.8 on factor 2 with small cross-loadings, the factors may provisionally be labelled quantitative and verbal. Unique variances, sample size, factor correlation, and replication in new data must still be examined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

高载荷不证明题项只受该因子因果影响；共同措辞、同一测量方式或样本选择也能制造相关。探索性因子分析提出结构，确认性因子分析与外部效标才进一步检验结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A high loading does not prove that the factor causally determines the item. Shared wording, a common measurement method, or sample selection can also generate correlation. Exploratory factor analysis proposes a structure; confirmatory factor analysis and external criteria test it further.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检查相关矩阵是否有足够共同结构、样本协方差是否稳定、Heywood case（负特殊方差或公共度大于 1）、强交叉载荷和旋转敏感性。若不同合理方法给完全不同结构，应报告不确定性而不是挑最好讲故事的一组。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check whether the correlation matrix contains enough shared structure, whether sample covariance is stable, whether Heywood cases occur, whether cross-loadings are strong, and whether results are sensitive to rotation. If reasonable methods yield entirely different structures, report the uncertainty rather than choosing the easiest story.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 旋转后的因子更好解释，是否说明模型拟合更好？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does a more interpretable rotated solution imply better model fit?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 正交旋转不改变模型重构的共同协方差和总体拟合，只改变载荷的表示方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Orthogonal rotation does not change the reproduced common covariance or overall fit; it changes only the orientation in which loadings are represented.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 公共度与特殊方差分别表示什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What do communality and unique variance represent?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 公共度是公共因子解释的变量方差，特殊方差是该变量独有及测量误差部分；标准化变量下二者通常加总为 1。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Communality is variance explained by common factors; unique variance is variable-specific variation and measurement error. For a standardised variable, they typically sum to one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么不能只按载荷给因子命名？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why should factors not be named from loadings alone?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 载荷只给样本中的统计关系，命名还需题项内容、理论、旋转稳定性和外部/新样本验证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Loadings give only sample-level statistical relationships. Naming also requires item content, theory, rotational stability, and external or new-sample validation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
