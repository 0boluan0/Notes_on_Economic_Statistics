# 优先股是依条款在股利或清算分配等方面优先于普通股的股份类别

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Preferred stock is a share class with contractual preferences over common stock in dividends, liquidation distributions or other specified rights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

优先股仍是[[股票]]，其“优先”描述与[[普通股]]相比的特定权利顺序，而不是比一切债权人优先或无条件保证收益。需要逐项读取条款：优先的是股利还是清算金额，分配率如何确定，未付股利是否累积，以及是否附带参与剩余分配、表决、赎回或转换权。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Preferred stock remains [[股票|stock]]. Its preference concerns specified rights relative to [[普通股|common stock]], not priority over every creditor or an unconditional return guarantee. The terms identify dividend or liquidation preferences, the distribution-rate rule, whether unpaid dividends accumulate, and any participation, voting, redemption or conversion rights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，假设公司依法决定分配现金 6，某优先股类别本期应先分 4，且没有历史欠付或额外参与权；依该设定，优先股类别先得 4，普通股类别分余下 2。这里的 4 来自具体条款，不是“优先股”这个名称自动给出的数值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a company lawfully decides to distribute 6 in cash, and the terms give a preferred class the first 4 for the period, with no arrears or additional participation rights. The preferred class receives 4 and the common class the remaining 2. The amount 4 comes from the stated terms, not from the preferred-stock label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定股利、无表决权、股利累积都是可能的设计，不是所有优先股的共同定义。美国 Delaware 公司法便明确允许累积或非累积股利，以及不同表决、赎回和转换安排。累积通常关系到以后向普通股分配前须处理的欠付优先额，不能直接当作普通债券到期本息的同一义务；实际清算仍须识别[[受偿顺序]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fixed dividends, no voting rights and cumulative dividends are possible designs, not universal defining features. Delaware law explicitly permits cumulative or noncumulative dividends and different voting, redemption and conversion arrangements. Cumulation typically affects arrears that must be addressed before later common distributions; it should not simply be equated with scheduled bond principal and interest. Liquidation still requires identifying [[受偿顺序|claim priority]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sources and verification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [SEC Investor.gov, Stocks — “What kinds of stocks are there?”及风险段](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/stocks)：支持优先股相对普通股的分配优先地位，以及其位于债权之后的基本公司清算语境。
- [Delaware General Corporation Law, §151(a)–(e)](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc05/index.html)：核对表决权、分配率及条件、累积选择、清算与转换等权利由条款规定；这里只用该辖区证明不能把一种优先股设计视为普遍定义。
- [OpenStax, Principles of Finance 2e, §11.4 “Features of Preferred Stock”](https://openstax.org/books/principles-finance-2e/pages/11-4-preferred-stock)：支持优先权和累积／非累积区别；不将其固定股利与无投票权简表外推至所有品种。最小例为自拟条款下的分配。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [SEC Investor.gov, Stocks, classes and risks](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/investment-products/stocks) supports preferred-over-common priority and the basic corporate-liquidation ordering after creditors.
- [Delaware General Corporation Law, §151(a)–(e)](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc05/index.html) supports term-specific voting, dividend, cumulation, liquidation and conversion rights as a jurisdiction-specific counterexample to universal design claims.
- [OpenStax, Principles of Finance 2e, §11.4, preferred-stock features](https://openstax.org/books/principles-finance-2e/pages/11-4-preferred-stock) supports preferences and the cumulative/noncumulative distinction. Its simplified fixed-dividend and voting table is not universalised. The allocation example uses original stated terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
