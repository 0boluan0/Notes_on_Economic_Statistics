# Common random numbers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*共同随机数（CRN）*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Common random numbers** compare two simulated alternatives by exposing them to shared random inputs. Imagine comparing two delivery policies: if one is tested on easy days and the other on difficult days, their observed difference mixes the policy difference with variation in the days. Running both policies on each simulated day can make their difference easier to estimate. The benefit depends on how their outputs move together; sharing inputs alone does not guarantee an improvement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**共同随机数（CRN）**让两个模拟方案使用共享随机输入，再比较输出。比如比较两种配送政策：如果一种只碰上容易的日子，另一种碰上困难的日子，观察差异就混入了日子本身的波动。在每一个模拟日分别运行两种政策，可能更容易估计政策差异。但收益取决于两个输出怎样一起变化；共享输入本身不保证改善。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two policies on the same day
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*让两种政策面对同一天*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $U$ describe the day: $U=0$ for an easy day and $U=1$ for a difficult day, each with probability $1/2$. Suppose the delivery times, in minutes, are $A=10+4U$ under policy A and $B=8+3U$ under policy B. These are illustrative rules, not fitted data. Subtract within each day to obtain $D=A-B$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $U$ 描述日子：容易时为 $0$，困难时为 $1$，概率各 $1/2$。假设政策 A 的配送分钟数为 $A=10+4U$，政策 B 为 $B=8+3U$。这是用于讲解的规则，不是拟合数据。在同一天内相减，得到 $D=A-B$。

| Day / 日子 | Probability / 概率 | $A$ | $B$ | $D=A-B$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy / 容易，$U=0$ | $1/2$ | $10$ | $8$ | $2$ |
| Difficult / 困难，$U=1$ | $1/2$ | $14$ | $11$ | $3$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[期望|expected values]], or probability-weighted averages, are $E[A]=12$ and $E[B]=9.5$. Our target is their difference $\Delta=E[A]-E[B]=2.5$ minutes. The paired difference is only $2$ or $3$, even though each individual delivery time varies more. If A and B instead use independent days, their difference can be $-1,2,3,6$, each with probability $1/4$. Both designs have mean $2.5$, but the independent comparison fluctuates more.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按概率加权的[[期望]]为 $E[A]=12$、$E[B]=9.5$。目标是两者的差 $\Delta=E[A]-E[B]=2.5$ 分钟。配对差值只有 $2$ 或 $3$，比两个单独配送时间的变化更小。如果 A、B 各用独立的日子，差值可以是 $-1,2,3,6$，概率各 $1/4$。两种设计的均值都是 $2.5$，但独立比较波动更大。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Preserve each alternative's distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*保留各方案本来应该有的分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, write $A_i=f_A(U_i)$ and $B_i=f_B(U_i)$ for the two outputs in replication $i$. Here $U_i$ contains all the random inputs needed for that paired run; it may be a vector or a simulated path rather than one number. The fixed rules $f_A,f_B$ turn those inputs into outputs. Using one input source constructs a **coupling**: a [[联合分布|joint distribution]] for the pair that preserves each alternative's intended [[边际分布|marginal distribution]]. The two marginals need not be the same; in the table, A takes values $10,14$ while B takes $8,11$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般写成 $A_i=f_A(U_i)$、$B_i=f_B(U_i)$，表示第 $i$ 次复制中两种方案的输出。$U_i$ 包含该次配对运行所需的全部随机输入，可以是向量或整条路径，不必只是一个数；固定规则 $f_A,f_B$ 把输入变成输出。共享输入构造了一种**耦合（coupling）**：为两个输出指定[[联合分布]]，同时保留各方案本来应该有的[[边际分布]]。两个边际不必相同：表中 A 取 $10,14$，B 取 $8,11$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a fixed positive integer $N$ before looking at the outputs. Suppose each pair has the intended marginal laws and finite absolute means, $E|A_i|<\infty$ and $E|B_i|<\infty$. Define the average paired difference by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

查看输出之前选定正整数 $N$。假设每次配对都保留目标边际分布，且绝对值的期望有限：$E|A_i|<\infty$、$E|B_i|<\infty$。平均配对差值定义为

$$
D_i=A_i-B_i,\qquad
\widehat\Delta_N=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^{N}D_i.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[期望线性性|Linearity of expectation]] gives $E[\widehat\Delta_N]=E[A]-E[B]=\Delta$. This unbiasedness calculation does not require independence within a pair or between replications. Independence between replications enters the usual variance and standard-error calculation, which is a separate step. To justify those marginal output laws, verify each simulator against its own input model, including any dependence among its inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[期望线性性]]给出 $E[\widehat\Delta_N]=E[A]-E[B]=\Delta$。这一步无偏性计算不要求同一对内部独立，也不要求不同复制之间独立。复制之间的独立进入通常的方差与标准误计算，那是另一步。为了论证输出边际正确，应核对每个模拟器自己的输入模型，包括输入之间原本应有的依赖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why positive covariance helps a difference
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正协方差为什么有利于求差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now assume the complete pairs $(A_i,B_i)$ are [[独立同分布|iid across replications]], with $E[A_i^2]$ and $E[B_i^2]$ finite. Write $\sigma_A^2,\sigma_B^2$ for their marginal [[方差|variances]] and $c=\operatorname{Cov}(A_i,B_i)$ for their within-pair [[协方差|covariance]], which measures their average co-movement about their means. Expanding the centred square gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在进一步假设完整的对 $(A_i,B_i)$ 在不同复制间[[独立同分布|iid]]，且 $E[A_i^2]$、$E[B_i^2]$ 有限。用 $\sigma_A^2,\sigma_B^2$ 表示两个边际的[[方差]]，$c=\operatorname{Cov}(A_i,B_i)$ 表示同一对内部的[[协方差]]，衡量它们相对各自均值的平均共同变化。展开中心化后的平方可得

$$
\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\Delta_N)
=\frac{\sigma_A^2+\sigma_B^2-2c}{N}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare this with $N$ independent runs of each alternative, with both sets also independent of each other and preserving those same marginals: the variance of the difference of their averages is $(\sigma_A^2+\sigma_B^2)/N$. Thus $c>0$ makes the paired estimator's variance strictly smaller, $c=0$ leaves it equal, and $c<0$ makes it larger. This compares equal numbers of evaluations. Actual time and setup costs belong in [[方差缩减比较口径|an efficiency comparison]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较基准是各方案各做 $N$ 次独立运行，两组也相互独立，且保持相同边际；两个平均数之差的方差为 $(\sigma_A^2+\sigma_B^2)/N$。因此 $c>0$ 时配对方差严格更小，$c=0$ 时相同，$c<0$ 时更大。这里比较的是相同评估次数；实际运行时间与启动成本还要按[[方差缩减比较口径]]另算。

> [!example] Compute the gain, then reverse the sign · 算出收益，再改变方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the delivery table, $\operatorname{Var}(U)=1/4$, so $\sigma_A^2=4$, $\sigma_B^2=9/4$ and $c=4\cdot3/4=3$. The difference has variance $1/4$, compared with $25/4$ under independent days. For $N=100$ independent pairs, the corresponding standard errors are $0.05$ and $0.25$ minutes. Neither policy's individual mean becomes more precise just because the other is paired with it.
>
> Keep the same marginal delivery times for B, but use $B=11-3U$. Now difficult days help B while hurting A: $c=-3$, and $A-B$ is $-1$ or $6$, with variance $49/4$. Shared inputs now make the difference worse than the independent benchmark $25/4$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 配送表中 $\operatorname{Var}(U)=1/4$，所以 $\sigma_A^2=4$、$\sigma_B^2=9/4$、$c=4\cdot3/4=3$。差值方差为 $1/4$，独立日子下则为 $25/4$。若有 $N=100$ 个独立配对，对应标准误为 $0.05$ 和 $0.25$ 分钟。把另一政策配进来，并不会自动让某一政策自身的均值更精确。
>
> 保留 B 原来的边际配送时间，却改用 $B=11-3U$。此时困难日对 B 有利、对 A 不利，$c=-3$，差值为 $-1$ 或 $6$，方差为 $49/4$。共享输入反而比独立基准的 $25/4$ 更差。

## One pair is one Monte Carlo observation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一整对才是一次 Monte Carlo 观测*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The data for uncertainty estimation are $D_1,\ldots,D_N$, not the $2N$ separate outputs treated as independent. For $N\ge2$, let $s_D^2=(N-1)^{-1}\sum_i(D_i-\widehat\Delta_N)^2$ and let $s_D$ be its nonnegative square root. Applying the [[Monte Carlo均值标准误|standard error of a Monte Carlo mean]] to the differences gives the estimated standard error $s_D/\sqrt N$. Copying one paired scenario repeatedly does not create independent replications; an observed zero sample variance also does not prove zero true uncertainty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估计不确定性所用的数据是 $D_1,\ldots,D_N$，不是把 $2N$ 个单独输出当成独立观测。对 $N\ge2$，定义 $s_D^2=(N-1)^{-1}\sum_i(D_i-\widehat\Delta_N)^2$，$s_D$ 是它的非负平方根。把[[Monte Carlo均值标准误]]应用于差值，得到估计标准误 $s_D/\sqrt N$。把一个配对场景反复复制并不产生独立复制；看到样本方差为零，也不证明真实不确定性为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The implementation must specify which shocks are shared, including what happens when the programs take different branches; that is the responsibility of [[共同随机数输入同步|input synchronisation]]. Only some inputs may be shared, provided each alternative's model is preserved. The wider distinction between reducing a difference and reducing a paired average is developed in [[配对方差缩减的协方差方向|covariance directions for paired variance reduction]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实现中还要规定究竟共享哪些冲击，以及两个程序进入不同分支时怎样处理，这由[[共同随机数输入同步]]展开。也可以只共享部分输入，前提是保留各方案的模型。求差与对一对输出取平均时需要不同的协方差方向，见[[配对方差缩减的协方差方向]]。
