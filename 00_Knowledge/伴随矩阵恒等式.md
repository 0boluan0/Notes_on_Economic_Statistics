# Adjugate identity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*伴随矩阵恒等式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[伴随矩阵|adjugate]] places a square matrix's cofactors in transposed positions. Those positions make a useful cancellation happen: multiplying the matrix by its adjugate, in either order, gives its determinant times the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. Every diagonal entry of the product is the determinant; every off-diagonal entry is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[伴随矩阵]]把方阵的代数余子式放在转置位置。这种安排使乘法中出现有用的抵消：原矩阵与伴随矩阵无论按哪个顺序相乘，结果都是行列式乘[[单位矩阵]]。乘积的每个对角元等于行列式，每个非对角元等于零。

> [!important] Both orders give the same scalar matrix · 两个次序得到同一标量矩阵
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$, where $n\ge1$ is an integer and $\mathbb F$ is a field,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对域 $\mathbb F$ 上的方阵 $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$，其中整数 $n\ge1$，有
>
> $$A\operatorname{adj}(A)=\operatorname{adj}(A)A=(\det A)I_n.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $I_n$ has diagonal entries $1$ and all other entries $0$. No assumption that $A$ is invertible is needed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $I_n$ 的对角元为 $1$，其余元素为 $0$。这个恒等式不要求 $A$ 可逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, the [[行列式|determinant]] of $A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{pmatrix}$ is $-2$, and its adjugate is $\begin{pmatrix}4&-2\\-3&1\end{pmatrix}$. Computing both products gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\3&4\end{pmatrix}$ 的[[行列式]]是 $-2$，伴随矩阵是 $\begin{pmatrix}4&-2\\-3&1\end{pmatrix}$。两个顺序分别相乘，得到

$$
A\operatorname{adj}(A)
=\begin{pmatrix}4-6&-2+2\\12-12&-6+4\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}-2&0\\0&-2\end{pmatrix},
$$
$$
\operatorname{adj}(A)A
=\begin{pmatrix}4-6&8-8\\-3+3&-6+4\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}-2&0\\0&-2\end{pmatrix}
=-2I_2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] does not generally commute. This identity establishes commutation for the particular pair $A$ and $\operatorname{adj}(A)$, rather than permitting arbitrary factors to be exchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般的[[矩阵乘法]]并不可交换；这个恒等式证明的是 $A$ 与 $\operatorname{adj}(A)$ 这对特定矩阵可以交换，不能据此任意调换其他因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the left product works
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先看原矩阵在左的乘积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $C_{ij}$ for the cofactor of entry $a_{ij}$. By the definition of the adjugate, its entry at $(k,j)$ is $C_{jk}$. Thus the row–column multiplication rule gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $C_{ij}$ 表示元素 $a_{ij}$ 的代数余子式。按照伴随矩阵定义，它在 $(k,j)$ 处的元素是 $C_{jk}$。由行列相乘的规则，

$$
\bigl(A\operatorname{adj}(A)\bigr)_{ij}
=\sum_{k=1}^{n}a_{ik}C_{jk}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The indices $i,j$ specify the output entry; $k$ runs through the $n$ matching positions. If $i=j$, this sum is exactly the [[余子式展开|cofactor expansion]] along row $i$, so it equals $\det A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$i,j$ 固定结果中的位置，$k$ 依次走过 $n$ 个配对位置。若 $i=j$，这就是沿第 $i$ 行的[[余子式展开]]，因此等于 $\det A$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $i\ne j$, form $B$ by replacing row $j$ of $A$ with a copy of row $i$. Expanding $\det B$ along row $j$ gives the same sum. Why are the cofactors still $C_{jk}$ from $A$? Each is computed after deleting row $j$, so the replacement disappears entirely from its deleted submatrix. But $B$ has two equal rows, and hence determinant zero. Therefore every off-diagonal entry of $A\operatorname{adj}(A)$ is zero. Together with the diagonal entries, this proves $A\operatorname{adj}(A)=(\det A)I_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $i\ne j$，把 $A$ 的第 $j$ 行换成第 $i$ 行的副本，得到 $B$。沿 $B$ 的第 $j$ 行展开，所得正是上面的和。为什么这里仍能使用 $A$ 的 $C_{jk}$？因为计算每个代数余子式时都会删去第 $j$ 行，刚才替换的内容随之全部消失，删剩的子矩阵没有改变。而 $B$ 有两行相同，行列式为零，所以 $A\operatorname{adj}(A)$ 的每个非对角元都为零。合并对角元结果，即得 $A\operatorname{adj}(A)=(\det A)I_n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the right product also works
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*再看原矩阵在右的乘积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now use the other order. The entry at $(i,k)$ of the adjugate is $C_{ki}$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在改变相乘次序。伴随矩阵的 $(i,k)$ 元素是 $C_{ki}$，因此

$$
\bigl(\operatorname{adj}(A)A\bigr)_{ij}
=\sum_{k=1}^{n}C_{ki}a_{kj}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $i=j$, scalar multiplication commutes in the field, and this is the cofactor expansion down column $i$. If $i\ne j$, replace column $i$ of $A$ by column $j$ and expand along column $i$. The cofactors in that column are unchanged because their computation deletes column $i$. The new matrix has two equal columns, so its determinant is zero. This proves $\operatorname{adj}(A)A=(\det A)I_n$ separately, without assuming that matrix multiplication commutes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $i=j$，域内标量乘法可交换，这就是沿第 $i$ 列向下展开。若 $i\ne j$，把 $A$ 的第 $i$ 列换成第 $j$ 列，再沿第 $i$ 列展开。这一列的代数余子式不变，因为计算时会删去第 $i$ 列。新矩阵有两列相同，所以行列式为零。这就单独证明了 $\operatorname{adj}(A)A=(\det A)I_n$，没有假设矩阵乘法可交换。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof uses expansion and the zero determinant of a matrix with repeated rows or columns. It never divides by $\det A$ or uses $A^{-1}$, so singular matrices are included from the start. For $n=1$, the convention $\operatorname{adj}([a])=[1]$ gives $[a][1]=[1][a]=aI_1$ directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明只用了展开公式和重复行列的行列式为零，既没有除以 $\det A$，也没有使用 $A^{-1}$，因此一开始就涵盖奇异矩阵。$n=1$ 时，约定 $\operatorname{adj}([a])=[1]$ 直接给出 $[a][1]=[1][a]=aI_1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Singular matrices still satisfy the identity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 奇异矩阵仍满足恒等式

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\det A=0$, the right-hand side is the zero matrix. This does not force the adjugate itself to be zero. For instance,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\det A=0$，右侧就是零矩阵；这并不迫使伴随矩阵本身为零。例如，

$$
B=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\operatorname{adj}(B)=\begin{pmatrix}4&-2\\-2&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B\operatorname{adj}(B)=\operatorname{adj}(B)B
=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both factors are nonzero, yet their product is zero; one cannot cancel $B$ as if it were an invertible scalar. In dimension one, even $A=[0]$ has adjugate $[1]$, and its product is still $[0]$. When $\det A\ne0$, rescaling the identity instead yields the [[伴随矩阵求逆|adjugate inverse formula]]. That nonzero condition belongs to division and inversion, not to the identity proved here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里两个因子都不是零矩阵，乘积却为零，不能把 $B$ 当作可逆标量直接约去。一阶时，甚至 $A=[0]$ 的伴随矩阵仍是 $[1]$，乘积照样为 $[0]$。当 $\det A\ne0$ 时，才可对恒等式缩放，得到[[伴随矩阵求逆|伴随求逆公式]]。非零条件属于除法与求逆，并非本恒等式的前提。
