# Maximal solution intervals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*微分方程最大区间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finding a formula for an [[常微分方程|ODE solution]] does not yet say how far that solution can continue. The function $y=1/(1-x)$ satisfies $y'=y^2$ near $x=0$. It also works on a larger interval, but it cannot pass through $x=1$ as a finite differentiable function. A **maximal solution interval** records how far the same trajectory can be extended while still solving the same equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求出一个[[常微分方程|微分方程解]]的公式，还没有说明它能继续多远。函数 $y=1/(1-x)$ 在 $x=0$ 附近满足 $y'=y^2$，在更大的区间也成立，但不能作为有限可导函数穿过 $x=1$。**最大解区间**记录的是：保持同一条轨迹、继续满足同一个方程时，解究竟能延伸到哪里。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Extension preserves all values already determined
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*延拓必须保留已经确定的全部值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a real scalar equation $y'=F(x,y)$ with a specified domain $D$ of allowed input–state pairs $(x,y)$. A solution on a nonempty open interval $I$ is differentiable, has $(x,y(x))\in D$, and satisfies the equation at every $x\in I$. An **extension** is a solution $\widetilde y$ on an open interval $J$ containing $I$ such that $\widetilde y(x)=y(x)$ for every $x\in I$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑实数标量方程 $y'=F(x,y)$，并指定允许的输入—状态对 $(x,y)$ 所组成的定义域 $D$。非空开区间 $I$ 上的解必须可导，满足 $(x,y(x))\in D$，且方程在每个 $x\in I$ 成立。**延拓**是在包含 $I$ 的开区间 $J$ 上找到一个解 $\widetilde y$，使每个 $x\in I$ 都有 $\widetilde y(x)=y(x)$。

> [!important] Maximal means no proper extension · 最大指不能再真延拓
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The solution is maximal if no such extension exists with $J\supsetneq I$. Its interval $I$ is then a maximal solution interval. This compares extensions of the given solution, not the lengths of unrelated trajectories. A solution on all of $\mathbb R$ is global and therefore maximal. A restriction to a smaller interval remains a valid solution but may not be maximal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若不存在 $J\supsetneq I$ 的这种延拓，就称该解为最大解，$I$ 为其最大解区间。这里比较的是给定解的延拓，不是在不同轨迹之间比较谁的区间更长。定义在整个 $\mathbb R$ 上的解是全局解，因此必为最大解；限制到较小区间后仍是合法解，却未必最大。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A smooth equation can have a finite-time endpoint
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方程光滑，解仍可能在有限输入处终止*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the [[通解与初值|initial-value problem]] $y'=y^2$, $y(0)=1$, differentiation gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[通解与初值|初值问题]] $y'=y^2$、$y(0)=1$，求导验证

$$
y(x)=\frac1{1-x},\qquad y'(x)=\frac1{(1-x)^2}=y(x)^2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The interval containing zero is $(-\infty,1)$. As $x\to1^-$, $y(x)\to+\infty$. Any extension across one would have to assign a finite value there and be [[一点连续|continuous]], because differentiability implies continuity. The infinite limit makes this impossible. There is no finite left endpoint to extend, so $(-\infty,1)$ is maximal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

包含零的区间是 $(-\infty,1)$。当 $x\to1^-$，$y(x)\to+\infty$。任何穿过一的延拓都必须在那里给出有限值并[[一点连续|连续]]，因为可导必连续；无穷极限使这种延拓不可能。左边已没有可继续跨过的有限端点，所以 $(-\infty,1)$ 最大。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The restriction to $(-1/2,1/2)$ solves the same initial-value problem but is not maximal. The same written formula on $(1,\infty)$ solves a different initial-value problem, for example $y(2)=-1$. The disconnected set $\mathbb R\setminus\{1\}$ is not one solution interval through zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

限制在 $(-1/2,1/2)$ 上，也能解同一个初值问题，只是区间还不最大。同一公式放在 $(1,\infty)$ 上，对应另一个初值问题，例如 $y(2)=-1$。断开的集合 $\mathbb R\setminus\{1\}$ 不能充当一条经过零的解区间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A broken expression is not always a broken solution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*表达式的缺口不一定是解的终点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y'=1$, $y(1)=1$, suppose a calculation gives $y=x^2/x$ for $x>0$. This expression is undefined at zero, but it equals $x$ on its stated interval. The function $\widetilde y=x$ on all real inputs preserves those values and still has derivative one. Thus the original solution extends across zero; the formula's removable gap was not an obstruction in the equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $y'=1$、$y(1)=1$，假设计算得到 $x>0$ 时 $y=x^2/x$。这个表达式在零点无定义，却在原区间上等于 $x$。定义在全部实数上的 $\widetilde y=x$ 保留了原值，导数也仍为一，所以解可以穿过零；公式的可去缺口并不是原方程的障碍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A denominator, logarithm or square root therefore identifies a boundary to investigate, not automatically a maximal endpoint. Check the original domain and the actual limiting trajectory. By contrast, if the right-hand side itself excludes a point reached by the trajectory, [[方程未定义处分支独立|filling in the function alone does not fill in the equation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，分母、对数或根号给出的是待调查的边界，并不自动证明最大区间到此为止。要检查原方程定义域和轨迹的真实极限。反过来，若轨迹碰到原方程右端本身排除的位置，[[方程未定义处分支独立|只给函数补值，并不能给方程补定义]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A geometric curve may continue after a function branch ends
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*几何曲线能继续，不等于函数解能继续*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course equation $y'=-x/(2y)$ excludes $y=0$. [[分离变量|Separation]] gives $y^2+x^2/2=C$. For $C>0$, the upper and lower branches are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程方程 $y'=-x/(2y)$ 排除了 $y=0$。[[分离变量]]给出 $y^2+x^2/2=C$。当 $C>0$，上下两支分别是

$$
y_\pm(x)=\pm\sqrt{C-\frac{x^2}{2}},\qquad
-\sqrt{2C}<x<\sqrt{2C}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each branch differentiates to $-x/(2y)$ throughout that interval. At either endpoint its value tends to zero, outside the equation's domain; its slope also becomes unbounded. The ellipse can continue geometrically around a vertical tangent, but this does not extend the same differentiable function $y(x)$ solving the original equation. The point case $C=0$ is not a solution on an open interval, and $C<0$ gives no real points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每支在这个区间内求导都等于 $-x/(2y)$。在两端，函数值趋于零，落到原方程定义域之外，斜率也变得无界。椭圆作为几何曲线可以绕过竖直切线继续，但这不等于原来的可导函数 $y(x)$ 还能继续满足原方程。$C=0$ 只给一个点，不是开区间上的解；$C<0$ 则没有实数点。

## What local uniqueness adds
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*局部唯一性能额外保证什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful sufficient setting is an open domain $D$ on which $F$ is continuously differentiable in both variables, written $C^1$. The [[通解与初值|local existence-and-uniqueness theorem]] then applies at every allowed pair. Solutions with the same initial data agree wherever their intervals overlap. Their intervals can be united, and the agreeing pieces define one unique maximal solution. This explains why the maximal interval is well determined under these hypotheses; it need not be all of $\mathbb R$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一种有用的充分情形是：定义域 $D$ 为开集，且 $F$ 对两个变量连续可微，记为 $C^1$。此时[[通解与初值|局部存在唯一性定理]]能在每个合法输入—状态对处应用。同一初值的解在区间交叠处必须一致，所以可以把这些区间取并，用彼此一致的片段组成唯一的最大解。这说明了这些假设下最大区间为何确定，却不保证它就是整个 $\mathbb R$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In this setting, a finite endpoint $b$ is not terminal if $y(x)\to L$ as $x\to b$ and $(b,L)$ lies inside $D$: the local solution through $(b,L)$ supplies an extension. Continuity of $F$ gives the matching limiting slope $F(b,L)$. Blow-up and reaching the boundary of $D$ are genuine obstructions to examine. Without uniqueness, different maximal solutions may share initial data; for $y'=3|y|^{2/3}$ both $y=0$ and $y=x^3$ are global solutions through $(0,0)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在上述情形下，若有限端点 $b$ 满足 $x\to b$ 时 $y(x)\to L$，而 $(b,L)$ 位于 $D$ 内部，那么 $b$ 并非终点：经过 $(b,L)$ 的局部解可提供延拓，$F$ 的连续性使两边极限斜率都为 $F(b,L)$。因此要检查真正的爆破或轨迹是否到达 $D$ 的边界。没有唯一性时，不同最大解可能共有初值；例如 $y'=3|y|^{2/3}$ 的 $y=0$ 与 $y=x^3$ 都是经过 $(0,0)$ 的全局解。
