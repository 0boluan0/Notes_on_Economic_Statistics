# 超买超卖是技术指标落入预先指定偏高或偏低区域的状态标签
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Overbought and oversold label indicator states in predefined upper or lower regions.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

超买与超卖是在指定指标及参数下，对行情处于某个极端区域的分类。它们需要明确使用哪条指标、哪种尺度、阈值和比较方向；不是证券内在价值被高估或低估的定义，也不是直接的买卖指令。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Overbought and oversold classify extreme regions under a specified indicator and parameter set. The indicator, scale, thresholds, and comparison directions must be stated. These labels do not define fundamental overvaluation or undervaluation and are not orders to trade.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如采用 [[RSI]]，并事先约定严格大于 $70$ 为超买、严格小于 $30$ 为超卖：$RSI=75$ 属于前者，$RSI=25$ 属于后者，恰好 $70$ 或 $30$ 均不满足本例的严格不等式。采用 $80/20$ 或含等号的另一约定，分类会变化；公式本身没有变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For [[RSI]], suppose values strictly above $70$ are classified as overbought and strictly below $30$ as oversold. Values of $75$ and $25$ fall into these respective regions, while exact boundary values do not satisfy this example's strict inequalities. Choosing $80/20$ or inclusive boundaries changes classification, not the RSI formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断时还需检查指标是否有效：历史不足或分母为零产生的 NA 不能被归为超卖。不同指标的正负号约定可能使“数值更大”对应相反方向，因此不能直接挪用另一指标的阈值解释。状态能否支持反转判断，见 [[超买超卖不保证反转]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First check that the indicator is valid: NA from insufficient history or a zero denominator is not oversold. Sign conventions can reverse the meaning of larger values across indicators, so another indicator's thresholds cannot be transferred mechanically. Whether a state implies reversal is addressed in [[超买超卖不保证反转|the limits of overbought and oversold reversal claims]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
