# 并行模拟应从可记录根状态派生独立性有构造依据的子流；同算法和调用协议下复制 seed 会复制随机流，相邻 seed 也不构成独立性证明
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Parallel simulation should derive substreams with a documented independence construction from a recorded root state; copying a seed under the same algorithm and call protocol duplicates the random stream, while adjacent seeds do not prove independence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 名义路径数只有在随机流没有被复制时才代表新增信息
> 每个 worker 用同一 seed、同一算法，并按同一调用协议消费随机流，会重复同一路径集合。正确做法是使用生成器明确支持的 spawning、jump-ahead 或独立 key 机制，并记录根状态到 worker 子流的映射。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Giving every worker the same seed and algorithm, and consuming the stream with the same call protocol, repeats the same paths. Use documented spawning, jump-ahead, or independent-key mechanisms and record the mapping from the root state to worker streams.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 20 个 worker 都执行 `random.Random(42)` 并按相同顺序生成 5,000 条路径，程序名义上产生 100,000 条路径，实际却只是把同一组 5,000 条路径复制 20 次。把这些副本当独立观测，会严重低估标准误。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Twenty workers that initialise the same generator with the same seed and consume it identically repeat one set of 5,000 paths twenty times. Treating the copies as independent makes the nominal sample size misleading and understates uncertainty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随手给 worker 使用相邻整数 seed 也不是通用的无重叠证明。优先顺序是：

1. 使用库提供的 `spawn` 或 `SeedSequence` 树；
2. 使用文档化的 jump-ahead，把每个 worker 放到不重叠区段；
3. 对 counter-based generator 使用唯一、可记录的 key；
4. 保存根 seed、worker ID、spawn path 和任务身份，以便重放。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adjacent integer seeds are not a general proof of non-overlap. Prefer a documented spawn tree, generator-specific jump-ahead, or unique keys for a counter-based generator, and retain the root seed, worker identity, and derivation path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“独立子流”是由生成器构造和碰撞分析支持的统计主张，不是看到不同 seed 就获得的数学独立。仍应检查跨 worker 重复、异常相关和对分流方案的敏感性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Independent substreams” is a statistical claim supported by the generator construction and collision analysis, not a theorem obtained merely from visibly different seeds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检
> 并行程序把 `root_seed + worker_id` 当每个 worker 的 seed，为什么仍不应把“数字不同”当成已经证明独立？
>
> **答案：** 独立性取决于具体 seeding 算法与流构造；应使用文档化的派生机制，并检查跨运行或跨 worker 的重复与相关。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Independence depends on the concrete seeding and stream construction. Use a documented derivation mechanism and inspect duplication or dependence across workers and runs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
