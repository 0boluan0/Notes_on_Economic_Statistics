> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释经济为何需要货币和金融中介，以及中央银行怎样维护支付、流动性、价格与金融稳定。

> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Explain why an economy needs money and financial intermediaries and how a central bank supports payments, liquidity, price stability, and financial stability.
> **Concrete anchor:** A bank deposit is both spendable money for the public and a liability of the bank; central-bank reserves are the settlement asset between banks.
> **Central difficulty:** Money is not only cash. Different aggregates capture different degrees of liquidity, and deposit creation by commercial banks remains constrained by capital, risk, funding, and credit demand.
> **Why it matters:** Interest rates, monetary policy, bank crises, and asset prices must be understood through a network of balance sheets.

## 货币的功能与口径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Functions and Measures of Money*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

货币充当交换媒介、计价单位和价值储藏。M0/M1/M2 等口径按现金、活期存款和较广流动资产划分，定义因国家制度而异。流动性是连续谱，资产被计入某口径不等于风险和收益相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Money serves as a medium of exchange, a unit of account, and a store of value. Aggregates such as M0, M1, and M2 group cash, transaction deposits, and broader liquid assets, with definitions varying across institutional systems. Liquidity is a continuum; inclusion in the same aggregate does not make assets identical in risk or return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断某资产是否“像货币”，应问它能否按面值迅速结算、价格是否稳定、接受范围多大以及转换成本多高。货币口径是政策与统计选择，不是自然界固定分类；比较国家或时期前必须核对定义变更。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To judge how money-like an asset is, ask whether it settles quickly at par, how stable its price is, how widely it is accepted, and how costly conversion is. Monetary aggregates are policy and statistical conventions, not immutable natural categories; compare countries or periods only after checking definition changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三类货币与对应负债
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three Forms of Money and Their Corresponding Liabilities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金是中央银行负债，银行存款是商业银行负债，准备金是商业银行持有的中央银行资产。公众通常不能直接持有准备金；银行间支付最终通过准备金账户结算。区分发行主体能避免把“公众存款增加”和“银行准备金增加”当成同一件事。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash is a central-bank liability, bank deposits are commercial-bank liabilities, and reserves are central-bank assets held by commercial banks. The public generally cannot hold reserves directly; interbank payments settle through reserve accounts. Distinguishing issuers prevents treating an increase in public deposits as the same event as an increase in bank reserves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：工资支付跨行结算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Interbank Settlement of a Salary Payment*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

甲银行客户向乙银行客户支付工资时，甲客户存款减少、乙客户存款增加，同时甲银行准备金转给乙银行。公众货币在账户间移动，银行体系总存款未必改变；准备金只在银行间重新分配。若中央银行购买证券，则可能改变体系准备金总量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a customer of Bank A pays a salary to a customer of Bank B, the payer's deposit falls, the recipient's deposit rises, and reserves move from Bank A to Bank B. Public money moves between accounts while total system deposits need not change; reserves are redistributed between banks. A central-bank securities purchase, by contrast, can change aggregate reserves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把现金占比当全部货币会严重低估支付余额；把货币基础和广义货币混用会误读 QE；把“流动资产”都当作面值稳定货币会漏掉市场风险和赎回风险。任何货币数据都应附口径、发行主体和观察日期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Treating cash as all money severely understates transaction balances. Mixing the monetary base with broad money misreads quantitative easing. Calling every liquid asset stable-at-par money ignores market and redemption risk. Every monetary statistic should state its aggregate, issuer, and observation date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Minimal Self-check*

### 银行存款为什么既是货币又是银行负债？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is a bank deposit both money and a bank liability?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 对公众它可支付和储值；对银行它是必须按约兑付/转账的负债，资产端则是贷款和证券。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> For the public it provides payment and storage of value; for the bank it is an obligation to redeem or transfer, backed by loans and securities on the asset side.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 准备金和银行存款的持有人为何不同？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why are reserves and bank deposits held by different sectors?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 准备金是合格机构在中央银行的账户资产，用于银行间结算；公众通常持有商业银行存款并通过银行接入支付体系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Reserves are account assets of eligible institutions at the central bank and settle interbank payments; the public normally holds commercial-bank deposits and accesses payments through banks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### M1 与 M2 差异为什么必须按国家和时期核对？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must the distinction between M1 and M2 be checked by country and period?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 金融产品和统计定义会改变，各国把交易存款、储蓄工具和货币市场资产纳入的规则并不完全相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Financial products and statistical definitions change, and countries differ in how they classify transaction deposits, savings instruments, and money-market assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
