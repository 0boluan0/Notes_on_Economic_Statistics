# 隐函数定理给出的局部解支满足 $Dg=-(D_vF)^{-1}D_uF$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The local branch supplied by the implicit function theorem satisfies $Dg=-(D_vF)^{-1}D_uF$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 公式
> 设 $F:\mathbb R^p\times\mathbb R^q\to\mathbb R^q$，而 [[隐函数定理]] 在 $(u_0,v_0)$ 附近给出解支 $v=g(u)$。在这条解支上，
> $$
> Dg(u)=-[D_vF(u,g(u))]^{-1}D_uF(u,g(u)).
> $$
> 左侧是 $q\times p$ 矩阵；右侧先用 $D_uF$ 读取参数扰动，再由 $D_vF$ 的逆换算成维持方程成立所需的 $v$ 变化。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $F:\mathbb R^p\times\mathbb R^q\to\mathbb R^q$, and suppose the [[隐函数定理|implicit function theorem]] supplies a local branch $v=g(u)$ near $(u_0,v_0)$. Along this branch, $Dg(u)=-[D_vF(u,g(u))]^{-1}D_uF(u,g(u))$. The left side is a $q\times p$ matrix. On the right, $D_uF$ first measures the effect of a parameter perturbation, and the inverse of $D_vF$ converts it into the compensating change in $v$ required to keep the equations satisfied.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 公式从哪里来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Where the formula comes from*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解支恒等满足
$$
F(u,g(u))=0.
$$
由 [[多元链式法则]]，
$$
D_uF+D_vF\,Dg=0.
$$
因为定理已经保证 $D_vF$ 可逆，左乘它的逆便得到公式。矩阵乘法次序由维度和复合顺序决定，不能把标量分式中的分子、分母随意换位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The branch satisfies the identity $F(u,g(u))=0$. The multivariable chain rule gives $D_uF+D_vF\,Dg=0$. Because the theorem guarantees that $D_vF$ is invertible, left multiplication by its inverse gives the formula. Matrix order is fixed by dimensions and composition; the numerator and denominator of the scalar mnemonic cannot be rearranged freely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 标量特例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Scalar special case*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $F(x,y)=0$ 且 $F_y\ne0$，局部解支 $y=g(x)$ 满足
$$
g'(x)=-\frac{F_x(x,g(x))}{F_y(x,g(x))}.
$$
例如 $F(x,y)=x^2+y^2-25$ 在 $(3,4)$ 附近给出上半圆分支，因而
$$
g'(3)=-\frac{2\cdot3}{2\cdot4}=-\frac34.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one equation with $F_y\ne0$, the local branch $y=g(x)$ satisfies $g'(x)=-F_x/F_y$. For the circle $x^2+y^2=25$ near $(3,4)$, the upper branch therefore has slope $g'(3)=-3/4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When the formula may be used*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条公式是在已经由定理确认的局部解支上求导，不单独证明解支存在。必须使用与“待解变量”对应的可逆方块 $D_vF$；若换一组变量作为输出，就要重新选择并检查相应的 Jacobian 块。块奇异时公式不可用，但这不等于解支必不存在，见 [[隐函数奇异块不推无解支]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formula differentiates a local branch whose existence has already been established by the theorem; it does not prove that a branch exists by itself. The invertible block must correspond to the variables being solved for. Choosing a different output block requires a new Jacobian check. If the block is singular, the formula is unavailable, but a branch need not be absent; see [[隐函数奇异块不推无解支|the singular-block boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 在矩阵公式中，为什么是 $-(D_vF)^{-1}D_uF$，而不是把两个因子的次序对调？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the matrix formula, why is the order $-(D_vF)^{-1}D_uF$ rather than the reverse?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 链式法则先给出 $D_vF\,Dg=-D_uF$；要解出 $Dg$，必须在左侧乘 $(D_vF)^{-1}$，而且反向乘积通常连尺寸都不相容。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The chain rule first gives $D_vF\,Dg=-D_uF$. Solving for $Dg$ requires left multiplication by $(D_vF)^{-1}$, and the reversed product is generally dimensionally incompatible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
