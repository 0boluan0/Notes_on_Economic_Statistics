# The derivative of an implicit function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*隐函数导数公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An equation can constrain several quantities to move together. The **implicit-function derivative formula** tells us how the quantities being solved for must change when the input parameters change, while all the equations remain satisfied. It computes the rate of a local solution already justified by the [[隐函数定理|implicit function theorem]]; it can often do so even when we cannot write the solution explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个方程或一组方程，可以约束几个量一起变化。**隐函数导数公式**告诉我们：输入参数改变时，为了继续满足所有方程，被求解的量必须怎样改变。它计算的是已经由[[隐函数定理]]保证存在的局部解的变化率；即使写不出显式解，也经常能求出这个变化率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one equation $F(x,y)=0$, think of moving $x$ a little. The direct change in $F$ caused by $x$ must be cancelled by the change caused by the accompanying motion of $y$. The scalar formula $y'=-F_x/F_y$ expresses that balance. [[隐式求导|Implicit differentiation]] gives the hands-on method; this card states the conditions and extends the balance to several inputs and equations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一个方程 $F(x,y)=0$，可以想象让 $x$ 稍微改变。$x$ 引起的 $F$ 的直接变化，必须被 $y$ 随之调整所引起的变化抵消。标量公式 $y'=-F_x/F_y$ 表达的就是这种平衡。[[隐式求导]]介绍实际计算方法；本卡说明公式的条件，并把这种平衡扩展到多个输入和多个方程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Name the inputs, unknowns and derivative blocks
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*分清输入、未知量和导数块*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $u\in\mathbb R^p$ be a vector of $p$ input parameters and $v\in\mathbb R^q$ a vector of $q$ quantities to solve for. The function $F(u,v)$ returns $q$ numbers, one for each constraint; the equation $F(u,v)=0$ means every component is zero. A solution branch $v=g(u)$ assigns a nearby solution vector to each nearby input vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $u\in\mathbb R^p$ 是包含 $p$ 个输入参数的向量，$v\in\mathbb R^q$ 是包含 $q$ 个待求量的向量。函数 $F(u,v)$ 输出 $q$ 个数，每个数对应一条约束；$F(u,v)=0$ 表示所有分量都等于零。解支 $v=g(u)$ 为附近的每个输入向量指定一个附近的解向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[偏导数|partial derivative]] changes one variable while holding the others fixed. A [[Jacobian矩阵|Jacobian matrix]] collects these partial derivatives, with output components in its rows and input variables in its columns. Thus $D_uF$ is $q\times p$, $D_vF$ is $q\times q$, and the desired derivative $Dg$ is $q\times p$. The subscript specifies which variables are allowed to change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[偏导数]]让一个变量改变，同时固定其余变量。[[Jacobian矩阵]]把这些偏导数收在一起，行对应输出分量，列对应输入变量。因此 $D_uF$ 是 $q\times p$ 矩阵，$D_vF$ 是 $q\times q$ 矩阵，所求的导数 $Dg$ 是 $q\times p$ 矩阵。下标说明允许改变的是哪一组变量。

> [!abstract] A local formula under the implicit function theorem · 隐函数定理条件下的局部公式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $F$ is defined on an open neighborhood of $(u_0,v_0)$, all its first partial derivatives are continuous there ($F$ is $C^1$), $F(u_0,v_0)=0$, and $D_vF(u_0,v_0)$ is invertible. The theorem gives a unique local $C^1$ branch $v=g(u)$ through that point. On a sufficiently small neighborhood of $u_0$, its derivative is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $F$ 定义在 $(u_0,v_0)$ 的某个开邻域上，所有一阶偏导数在该邻域连续（即 $F$ 为 $C^1$），并且 $F(u_0,v_0)=0$、$D_vF(u_0,v_0)$ 可逆。定理给出经过该点的唯一局部 $C^1$ 解支 $v=g(u)$。在 $u_0$ 充分小的邻域内，它的导数为
>
> $$
> Dg(u)=-\bigl[D_vF(u,g(u))\bigr]^{-1}D_uF(u,g(u)).
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Unique local” refers to solutions near $v_0$, not every possible solution elsewhere. Both derivative blocks are evaluated at the same point **on the branch**, $(u,g(u))$. Continuity allows the neighborhood to be small enough that the solved-variable block stays invertible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> “局部唯一”指的是 $v_0$ 附近的解，并不排除远处存在别的解。两个导数块都必须在**解支上的同一点** $(u,g(u))$ 计算。借助连续性，可以把邻域缩小到求解变量块始终可逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Where the formula comes from

The branch satisfies the identity $F(u,g(u))=0$. As $u$ changes, $F$ changes both directly through its first argument and indirectly through $g(u)$. The [[多元链式法则|multivariable chain rule]] adds these two contributions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 公式从哪里来

解支满足恒等式 $F(u,g(u))=0$。当 $u$ 变化时，$F$ 一方面通过第一个输入直接变化，另一方面通过 $g(u)$ 间接变化。[[多元链式法则]]把这两个贡献相加：

$$
D_uF(u,g(u))+D_vF(u,g(u))\,Dg(u)=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every term is $q\times p$: the second product has sizes $(q\times q)(q\times p)$. The [[逆矩阵|inverse matrix]] of $D_vF$ undoes its action, so multiply on the **left** to isolate $Dg$. This gives the boxed formula. [[矩阵乘法|Matrix multiplication]] is ordered; swapping the factors can be undefined, and even for square matrices usually changes the result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每一项都是 $q\times p$：第二项的乘积尺寸为 $(q\times q)(q\times p)$。$D_vF$ 的[[逆矩阵]]能消去它的作用，因此要从**左侧**乘以逆矩阵，才能把 $Dg$ 单独留下，得到上面的公式。[[矩阵乘法]]有次序；交换因子可能连尺寸都不匹配，即使都是方阵，一般也会改变结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One equation and one unknown

When $p=q=1$, write the input as $x$ and the unknown as $y=g(x)$. The two blocks are the numbers $F_x$ and $F_y$, and the matrix equation becomes $F_x+F_y y'=0$. Thus:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 标量特例

当 $p=q=1$，把输入记作 $x$，未知量记作 $y=g(x)$。两个导数块就是数值 $F_x$ 和 $F_y$，矩阵等式变成 $F_x+F_y y'=0$。因此：

$$
y'(x)=-\frac{F_x(x,y(x))}{F_y(x,y(x))},\qquad F_y(x,y(x))\ne0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $F(x,y)=x^2+y^2-25$, $F_x=2x$ and $F_y=2y$. Near $(3,4)$ the assumptions hold because $F_y=8\ne0$. The upper-circle branch therefore has $y'=-x/y$, giving $y'(3)=-3/4$. This conclusion includes both the existence of the local differentiable branch and the computation of its slope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $F(x,y)=x^2+y^2-25$，有 $F_x=2x$、$F_y=2y$。在 $(3,4)$，$F_y=8\ne0$，满足定理条件。因此上半圆分支有 $y'=-x/y$，给出 $y'(3)=-3/4$。这里既有局部可导分支的存在保证，也有它的斜率计算。

> [!example] Two unknowns respond to one parameter · 两个未知量响应一个参数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $u$ be one real parameter and require $v_1+v_2=u$ and $v_1-v_2=2u$. Set $F(u,v)=(v_1+v_2-u,\ v_1-v_2-2u)^T$. The derivative blocks are constant:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $u$ 为一个实数参数，要求 $v_1+v_2=u$、$v_1-v_2=2u$。设 $F(u,v)=(v_1+v_2-u,\ v_1-v_2-2u)^T$，则两个导数块是常矩阵：
>
> $$
> D_vF=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},
> \qquad D_uF=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\-2\end{pmatrix},
> \qquad (D_vF)^{-1}=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
> $$
> Dg=-\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}
> \begin{pmatrix}-1\\-2\end{pmatrix}
> =\begin{pmatrix}3/2\\-1/2\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An increase of one unit in $u$ therefore requires $v_1$ to increase by $3/2$ and $v_2$ to decrease by $1/2$. Here explicit solution is easy: $g(u)=(3u/2,-u/2)^T$, which verifies the formula. The derivative has two rows and one column because one input drives two outputs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因此 $u$ 增加一个单位，要求 $v_1$ 增加 $3/2$、$v_2$ 减少 $1/2$。本例可以直接解出 $g(u)=(3u/2,-u/2)^T$，核对得到同一个导数。导数有两行一列，因为一个输入驱动两个输出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When the chosen block is singular

Invertibility concerns the variables chosen as unknowns. On the circle at $(5,0)$, solving for $y$ fails this test because $F_y=0$. Solving for $x$ instead uses $F_x=10\ne0$ and gives a valid local branch $x(y)$ with derivative zero at $y=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用边界

可逆性针对的是选作未知量的那组变量。在圆上的 $(5,0)$，求解 $y$ 时因 $F_y=0$ 而不满足判据；改为求解 $x$，则 $F_x=10\ne0$，可以得到局部解支 $x(y)$，且在 $y=0$ 的导数为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $D_vF$ is singular, this inverse-matrix formula cannot be used. That alone does not establish that a branch is absent or nonsmooth; [[隐函数奇异块不推无解支|the singular-block boundary]] gives examples where smooth branches still exist. The formula also does not assert global uniqueness. When these hypotheses hold for equations defining an economic equilibrium or an optimum, the same local calculation becomes [[隐函数比较静态|implicit comparative statics]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $D_vF$ 奇异，这条含逆矩阵的公式就不能使用。但仅凭这一点，不能断言分支不存在或不光滑；[[隐函数奇异块不推无解支]]给出了光滑分支仍然存在的例子。公式也不声称全局唯一。当经济均衡或最优解满足的方程具备这些条件时，同样的局部计算就成为[[隐函数比较静态]]。
