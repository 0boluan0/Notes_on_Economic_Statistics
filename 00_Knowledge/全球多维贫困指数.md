# 全球多维贫困指数是多维贫困人口比例与贫困者平均加权剥夺程度的乘积

全球多维贫困指数（global MPI）是 UNDP 与 OPHI 使用健康、教育和生活水平的家庭剥夺信息，识别[[多维贫困]]人口后，将其人口比例 $H$ 与贫困者平均加权剥夺程度 $A$ 相乘得到的指标。它在家庭层面识别剥夺、把家庭判定赋予每个成员，再按人口加总；不是贫困家庭户数占比，也不是 $1-HDI$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The global Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI), produced by UNDP and OPHI, identifies [[多维贫困|multidimensionally poor]] people using household deprivations in health, education and living standards, then multiplies their population share $H$ by their average weighted deprivation intensity $A$. Household classifications are assigned to each member and aggregated over people. It is neither the share of households that are poor nor $1-HDI$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

2025 技术说明沿用十项指标。三个维度各占 $1/3$，维度内等权，因此不是十项各占 $1/10$：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The 2025 technical specification uses ten indicators. Each dimension receives weight $1/3$, with equal weights within dimensions, rather than weight $1/10$ on every indicator:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 维度 | 指标 | 每项权重 |
|---|---|---:|
| 健康 | 营养、儿童死亡 | $1/6$ |
| 教育 | 已完成受教育年限、在校就读 | $1/6$ |
| 生活水平 | 电力、卫生设施、饮用水、住房、烹饪燃料、资产 | $1/18$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Dimension | Indicators | Weight per indicator |
|---|---|---:|
| Health | Nutrition; child mortality | $1/6$ |
| Education | Years of schooling; school attendance | $1/6$ |
| Living standards | Electricity; sanitation; drinking water; housing; cooking fuel; assets | $1/18$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先按技术说明的具体年龄、年限、设施和调查缺失规则判定每项剥夺，再记 $d_{ij}\in\{0,1\}$。例如“没有电力”是一项剥夺；有死亡日期资料时，儿童死亡指标指调查前五年内发生的未满 18 岁儿童死亡，缺日期资料时则按技术说明的例外规则编码；教育年限与在校就读也不是同一个指标。十项信息须来自同一份家庭调查，不能把不同年份的全国均值拼成家庭剥夺记录。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First apply the technical note's age, duration, facility and missing-data rules to classify each deprivation, then code $d_{ij}\in\{0,1\}$. For example, no electricity is a deprivation. When death dates are available, child mortality concerns a death below age 18 during the five years before the survey; missing dates follow the technical note's exceptional coding rules. School attainment and attendance are separate indicators. All ten indicators must come from the same household survey; national averages from different years cannot form a household deprivation record.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $s_i=\sum_{j=1}^{10}w_jd_{ij}$，$\sum_jw_j=1$。本方案以 $s_i\ge1/3$ 识别贫困，包括恰好等于门槛者。等权人口共 $n$ 人，其中贫困者集合为 $P$、人数为 $q>0$，则：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $s_i=\sum_{j=1}^{10}w_jd_{ij}$, with $\sum_jw_j=1$. This system identifies poverty at $s_i\ge1/3$, including equality at the cutoff. For $n$ equally weighted people, with poor set $P$ containing $q>0$ people:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H=\frac{q}{n},\qquad
A=\frac{1}{q}\sum_{i\in P}s_i,\qquad
MPI=HA=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}s_i\mathbf1(s_i\ge1/3).
$$

最后一式说明：未达到总体贫困门槛者的剥夺不计入 MPI，尽管这些不足真实存在。若 $q=0$，MPI 为 0，条件均值 $A$ 的比式则没有定义；不能真的计算 $0/0$。抽样调查使用调查权重，并按家庭人口数展开或等价加权。这里的 $H$ 与货币[[贫困人口比例]]有相同的“贫困人数／总人口”形式，但识别贫困的规则不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last expression excludes the deprivations of people below the overall poverty cutoff from the MPI, even though those shortfalls exist. When $q=0$, the MPI is zero, while the conditional-mean expression for $A$ is undefined; do not calculate $0/0$. Surveys require sampling weights and household-size expansion or equivalent weighting. Here $H$ shares the poor-persons-over-population form of the monetary [[贫困人口比例|poverty headcount]], but uses a different identification rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自造两户例：甲户 2 人，只有两项教育剥夺，故每人 $s=1/3$；乙户 4 人，只有电力和烹饪燃料剥夺，故每人 $s=1/9$。甲户成员被识别为贫困，乙户成员未达到门槛。因此 $H=2/6=1/3$、$A=1/3$、$MPI=1/9$。若错按户数算，便会得到 $H=1/2$。MPI 的 $1/9$ 也不表示九分之一人口贫困——人口比例是 $H$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an original two-household example, household A has two members and only the two education deprivations, so each has $s=1/3$. Household B has four members and only electricity and cooking-fuel deprivations, so each has $s=1/9$. Only A's members meet the poverty cutoff. Thus $H=2/6=1/3$, $A=1/3$ and $MPI=1/9$. Counting households instead would incorrectly give $H=1/2$. An MPI of $1/9$ does not mean one ninth of people are poor: that proportion is $H$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全球 MPI 的权重和门槛是明确的测量设计；国家自定的多维贫困指标可能不同。比较还须核对调查年份、可用指标与一致化处理，不能因为报告在同一年出版就认定各国观察完全同期。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The global MPI's weights and cutoff are explicit measurement choices; national multidimensional poverty measures may differ. Comparisons must check survey years, available indicators and harmonization. A common publication year does not make every country's observations contemporaneous.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
