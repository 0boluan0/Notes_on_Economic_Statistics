# 控制变量利用一个已知期望的相关变量构造零均值修正，以降低目标估计量的方差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A control variate uses a correlated variable with known expectation to construct a zero-mean correction that reduces estimator variance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 估计 $E[Y]$ 时，若能同时计算一个与 $Y$ 相关、期望 $m=E[C]$ 已知的变量 $C$，就可从 $Y$ 中减去 $\beta(C-m)$；固定 $\beta$ 时，这个修正期望为零。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To estimate $E[Y]$, use a correlated variable $C$ whose mean $m$ is known and subtract $\beta(C-m)$. For fixed $\beta$, the correction has zero expectation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义

$$
Y_\beta=Y-\beta(C-m).
$$

只要 $m=E[C]$ 正确且 $\beta$ 视为固定常数，

$$
E[Y_\beta]=E[Y].
$$

同一批样本的估计量为

$$
\widehat\mu_\beta
=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N
\bigl[Y_i-\beta(C_i-m)\bigr].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The fixed-coefficient correction preserves the target because $E[C-m]=0$. Its usefulness comes from making the corrected output less variable than $Y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

好的 control 通常应当便宜、与目标输出强相关、且其期望能从解析公式或可信的外部计算得到。最佳固定系数见 [[控制变量最优系数]]；把 $m$ 写错会直接引入偏差，见 [[控制变量均值误设]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful control is inexpensive, strongly associated with the target output, and has a genuinely known expectation. The optimal fixed coefficient and the bias caused by a wrong mean are separate results.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
