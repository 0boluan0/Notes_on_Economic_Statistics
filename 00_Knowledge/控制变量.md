# Control variates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Monte Carlo 控制变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **control variate** is an auxiliary simulation output whose [[期望|expectation]] is already known. It tells us whether a run has sampled unusually high or low values of something related to our target. We use that discrepancy to adjust the target estimate. The aim is to remove some simulation noise while preserving the same expectation. Here “control” means a Monte Carlo variance-reduction device; it does not mean a regression covariate used to address confounding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**控制变量**是一个期望已经知道的辅助模拟输出。它帮助我们判断：这一轮是否在某个与目标相关的量上，恰好抽到了偏高或偏低的结果？我们据此修正目标估计，希望保留同一期望，同时去掉一部分模拟噪声。这里的“控制”指 Monte Carlo 方差缩减工具，不是回归里为处理混杂而加入的控制协变量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A small simulation with a known reference
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从一个已知参照的模拟开始*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $C$ record a simulated delay: $C=0$ or $1$, each with probability $1/2$. Let an independent noise $\varepsilon$ be $-1$ or $1$, also equally likely. Suppose the cost output is $Y=10+2C+\varepsilon$. The known delay mean is $m=E[C]=1/2$. The four equally likely outcomes are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $C$ 表示模拟中是否延误，取 $0$、$1$ 的概率各为 $1/2$；独立噪声 $\varepsilon$ 等概率取 $-1$、$1$。成本输出为 $Y=10+2C+\varepsilon$，已知延误均值为 $m=E[C]=1/2$。四个等概率结果如下：

| $C$ | $\varepsilon$ | $Y$ | $Y-2(C-1/2)$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | −1 | 9 | 10 |
| 0 | 1 | 11 | 12 |
| 1 | −1 | 11 | 10 |
| 1 | 1 | 13 | 12 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The correction removes the random delay contribution and adds back its known average: $Y-2(C-1/2)=11+\varepsilon$. Both outputs have mean $11$, but the [[方差|variance]] falls from $2$ to $1$. In a run with too many delays, subtracting $2(\bar C-1/2)$ corrects the corresponding upward movement of the mean cost; with too few delays it adds instead. This small model has a calculable target so we can check the method. In a useful application, $E[Y]$ is the difficult quantity and $E[C]$ is the easy one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

修正去掉随机延误贡献，再加回其已知平均贡献：$Y-2(C-1/2)=11+\varepsilon$。两个输出均值都是 $11$，但[[方差]]从 $2$ 降为 $1$。如果某轮延误次数偏多，减去 $2(\bar C-1/2)$ 就修正成本均值相应的上移；延误偏少时则加回。这是目标可计算的小模型，用来检查方法；实际应用中，通常难算的是 $E[Y]$，易知的是 $E[C]$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The zero-mean adjustment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零均值修正为什么保留目标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $\mu=E[Y]$ for the target, $m=E[C]$ for the correctly known control mean, and $\beta$ for a fixed real adjustment coefficient. Assume $E|Y|<\infty$ and $E|C|<\infty$, so both means are finite. Define one corrected output $Z_\beta$ and, for a fixed integer $N\ge1$, its sample average:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记目标为 $\mu=E[Y]$，正确已知的控制均值为 $m=E[C]$，固定实数调整系数为 $\beta$。假设 $E|Y|<\infty$、$E|C|<\infty$，保证两个均值有限。定义单次修正输出 $Z_\beta$，以及固定整数 $N\ge1$ 次输出的平均：

$$
Z_\beta=Y-\beta(C-m),\qquad \widehat\mu_\beta=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N[Y_i-\beta(C_i-m)]=\bar Y-\beta(\bar C-m).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bars denote sample averages, and $i$ indexes a complete simulation producing the pair $(Y_i,C_i)$. By [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]], $E[Z_\beta]=\mu-\beta(m-m)=\mu$. If every pair has these same finite means, $E[\widehat\mu_\beta]=\mu$ too. This [[估计量无偏性|unbiasedness]] calculation does not require independence between $Y$ and $C$, or between replications. In fact, association within each pair is what can make the control useful.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上横线表示样本平均，$i$ 标记一次完整模拟产生的配对 $(Y_i,C_i)$。由[[期望线性性]]，$E[Z_\beta]=\mu-\beta(m-m)=\mu$。若每对都有这些共同的有限均值，平均也满足 $E[\widehat\mu_\beta]=\mu$。这一[[估计量无偏性|无偏性]]计算不要求 $Y$ 与 $C$ 独立，也不要求各次复制独立；配对内部的关联恰恰可能使控制变量有用。

> [!important] Preserving the mean does not guarantee a gain · 保留均值不保证降低方差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With finite second moments, the adjustment can increase or decrease variance depending on $\beta$ and the [[协方差|covariance]] of $Y$ and $C$. The best fixed scalar coefficient is derived in [[控制变量最优系数|the optimal-coefficient result]]. Setting $\beta=0$ always recovers the ordinary [[Monte Carlo估计|Monte Carlo mean]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 二阶矩有限时，修正后方差可能增加，也可能减少，取决于 $\beta$ 及 $Y,C$ 的[[协方差]]。最好的固定单控制系数在[[控制变量最优系数]]中推导。取 $\beta=0$ 总会回到普通 [[Monte Carlo估计|Monte Carlo 均值]]。

## Replications, uncertainty and coefficient selection
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样重复、报告误差与选择系数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For [[独立同分布|iid]] complete pairs and finite second moments, the corrected outputs are iid and $\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\mu_\beta)=\operatorname{Var}(Z_\beta)/N$. For $N\ge2$, compute the usual sample variance of the $N$ corrected outputs, then divide its nonnegative square root by $\sqrt N$ to estimate the [[Monte Carlo均值标准误|Monte Carlo standard error]]. Do not count the two entries in a pair as separate replications. Extra cost for computing the control belongs in [[方差缩减比较口径|the efficiency comparison]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若完整配对[[独立同分布]]且二阶矩有限，修正输出也独立同分布，均值方差为 $\operatorname{Var}(Z_\beta)/N$。当 $N\ge2$，对这 $N$ 个修正输出计算通常的样本方差，再用其非负平方根除以 $\sqrt N$，估计 [[Monte Carlo均值标准误]]。不能把一对内的两个数算作两次独立复制。计算控制量的额外成本应放进[[方差缩减比较口径|效率比较]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof above fixes $\beta$ before using the production sample. A coefficient estimated from those same observations can move with $\bar C$, so the correction need not average to zero; see [[同样本估计控制系数偏差|same-sample coefficient estimation]]. One way to preserve the argument is to choose a coefficient from an independent pilot sample, then freeze it for fresh production pairs. Conditional on the pilot, it is fixed. For unconditional unbiasedness the resulting estimator must also be integrable; a bounded pilot coefficient is a simple sufficient safeguard when $Y,C$ are integrable. Splitting iid simulations into disjoint pilot and production sets supplies this independence, with a cost in production sample size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的证明在使用正式样本之前固定 $\beta$。若系数由同一批观察估计，就可能随 $\bar C$ 一起变动，修正项未必平均为零，见[[同样本估计控制系数偏差]]。一种保留论证的方法是用独立预试样本选择系数，然后固定下来，应用于新的正式配对；给定预试结果后，系数就是常数。要得到无条件无偏性，最终估计量还必须可积；在 $Y,C$ 可积时，把预试系数限制在有界范围是一个简单的充分保障。把 iid 模拟分成不重叠的预试集和正式集可提供这种独立性，但会减少正式估计可用的样本数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A control mean must refer to the actual simulated law. An external approximation or an estimate is not automatically an exact known mean. The resulting bias or extra uncertainty is explained in [[控制变量均值误设|misspecified control means]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

控制均值必须对应实际模拟的分布。外部近似或估计不能自动当作精确已知均值；相应偏差或额外不确定性见[[控制变量均值误设]]。
