# Subspaces of $\mathbb R^2$ and $\mathbb R^3$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*二维、三维实空间的子空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[子空间|subspace]] is a collection of vectors that contains zero and remains closed when we add vectors or multiply them by allowed scalars. In the usual real plane $\mathbb R^2$ and real three-dimensional space $\mathbb R^3$, this requirement leaves only a few geometric possibilities: zero alone, whole lines through zero, whole planes through zero, and the whole ambient space when its dimension permits it. A segment, a curved path, or a shifted line does not become a subspace just because it lies inside the space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[子空间]]是一群包含零向量、并且在向量相加和乘允许的标量后仍留在其中的向量。在通常的实平面 $\mathbb R^2$ 和实三维空间 $\mathbb R^3$ 中，这项要求只留下少数几何形状：零向量本身、过零的整条直线、过零的整片平面，以及维数允许时的整个环境空间。线段、曲线路径或平移直线，不会仅因位于空间内部就成为子空间。

> [!example] One vector forces a whole line · 一个向量带来整条直线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose a subspace of $\mathbb R^2$ contains $(1,2)$. Multiplying by every real number forces it to contain $(2,4)$, $(-1,-2)$, $(0,0)$ and every other $(t,2t)$. Here $t$ is the scalar multiplying the whole vector:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设 $\mathbb R^2$ 的一个子空间含有 $(1,2)$。允许乘任意实数，就迫使它包含 $(2,4)$、$(-1,-2)$、$(0,0)$，以及每个 $(t,2t)$。这里 $t$ 是乘整个向量的标量：
>
> $$L=\{t(1,2):t\in\mathbb R\}=\{(t,2t):t\in\mathbb R\}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is an entire line through the origin, extending in both directions. It is itself a subspace: adding two multiples gives another multiple, and scaling a multiple does too. A short segment of that line fails because, for example, doubling an endpoint can leave the segment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这是双向无限延伸、经过原点的整条直线。它自身就是子空间：两个倍数相加仍是一个倍数，再数乘也一样。只取其中一小段则不行，例如把端点加倍就可能离开线段。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the same subspace also contains $(0,1)$, it must contain every combination $a(1,2)+b(0,1)=(a,2a+b)$. For any desired $(x,y)$, choosing $a=x$ and $b=y-2x$ produces it. Thus these two directions force the entire plane. This is [[张成|spanning]] in action: include the directions, then include all their combinations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果同一个子空间还包含 $(0,1)$，它就必须包含每个组合 $a(1,2)+b(0,1)=(a,2a+b)$。对于任意目标 $(x,y)$，取 $a=x$、$b=y-2x$ 就能得到它。因此这两个方向迫使整个平面都在其中。这就是[[张成]]的具体含义：包含这些方向，也包含它们的所有组合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The complete list
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*全部可能的类型*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[维数|dimension]] counts the number of vectors in a [[基|basis]]: enough independent directions to generate every vector in the subspace. The following lists are exhaustive under ordinary coordinate addition and multiplication by **all real scalars**.

| Ambient space | Subspace dimension | All subspaces of that dimension |
|---|---:|---|
| $\mathbb R^2$ | $0$ | $\{(0,0)\}$ |
| $\mathbb R^2$ | $1$ | Any whole line through the origin |
| $\mathbb R^2$ | $2$ | All of $\mathbb R^2$ |
| $\mathbb R^3$ | $0$ | $\{(0,0,0)\}$ |
| $\mathbb R^3$ | $1$ | Any whole line through the origin |
| $\mathbb R^3$ | $2$ | Any whole plane through the origin |
| $\mathbb R^3$ | $3$ | All of $\mathbb R^3$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[维数]]是[[基]]所含向量的个数，也就是生成子空间中全部向量所需的独立方向数。在通常坐标加法和**所有实标量**的数乘下，以上已经列全：$\mathbb R^2$ 中只有零子空间、过原点直线、整个平面；$\mathbb R^3$ 中只有零子空间、过原点直线、过原点平面、整个三维空间，维数分别为 $0,1,2,3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $\{(t,2t,3t):t\in\mathbb R\}$ is a line in $\mathbb R^3$ because one coefficient $t$ chooses every point. The set $\{(a,b,0):a,b\in\mathbb R\}$ is the $xy$-plane because two coefficients vary independently. Planes need not be coordinate planes: $x+y+z=0$ gives $(x,y,z)=a(1,0,-1)+b(0,1,-1)$, another plane through zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$\{(t,2t,3t):t\in\mathbb R\}$ 是 $\mathbb R^3$ 中的直线，因为一个系数 $t$ 就能选出每个点。$\{(a,b,0):a,b\in\mathbb R\}$ 是 $xy$ 平面，因为两个系数可以独立变化。平面不必是坐标平面：$x+y+z=0$ 可写为 $(x,y,z)=a(1,0,-1)+b(0,1,-1)$，同样是过零的平面。

## Why there are no further shapes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么没有其他形状*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The dimension of a subspace cannot exceed the ambient dimension. A zero-dimensional space contains only zero. A one-dimensional space has a single nonzero basis vector $u$, so every member is $tu$: exactly its whole line. A two-dimensional subspace of $\mathbb R^3$ has two [[线性无关|independent]] basis vectors $u,v$, so its members are exactly $au+bv$: their plane. If a subspace has the same dimension as its finite-dimensional ambient space, it equals that space. These facts rule out every further case in the table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

子空间的维数不能超过环境空间的维数。零维空间只含零。一维空间的基只有一个非零向量 $u$，每个成员都是 $tu$，恰好整条直线。$\mathbb R^3$ 的二维子空间有两个[[线性无关]]的基向量 $u,v$，其成员恰好是所有 $au+bv$，即它们所在的平面。若子空间与有限维环境空间的维数相同，子空间就是整个环境空间。这些事实排除了表格之外的其他情况。

> [!warning] Through zero is necessary, not sufficient · 经过零还不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The shifted line $y=1$ misses zero and fails immediately. But the parabola $y=x^2$ contains zero and still fails: $(1,1)$ lies on it, while $2(1,1)=(2,2)$ does not, since $2\ne2^2$. The full closure test matters, not just the presence of the origin.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 平移直线 $y=1$ 不含零，立刻失败。但抛物线 $y=x^2$ 虽经过零，仍然失败：$(1,1)$ 在曲线上，$2(1,1)=(2,2)$ 却不在，因为 $2\ne2^2$。必须检查完整的封闭性，不能只看是否经过原点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A line inside $\mathbb R^2$ has dimension one even though its vectors have two coordinates. For $L$ above, the point $(3,6)$ is selected by the single coefficient $t=3$; it is still an ordered pair, not the one-coordinate vector $3\in\mathbb R^1$. Dimension measures the number of independent coefficients needed within the space, while the ambient coordinates record where its vectors live.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\mathbb R^2$ 内的直线是一维的，其中向量却有两个坐标。对于前面的 $L$，一个系数 $t=3$ 就选出 $(3,6)$；它仍是有序数对，不是 $\mathbb R^1$ 中的单坐标向量 $3$。维数计算在这个空间内部需要多少个独立系数，环境坐标则记录向量位于哪里。
