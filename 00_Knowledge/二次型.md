# 形如 $q(x)=x^TAx$ 的实值齐次二次函数称为实二次型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A homogeneous quadratic polynomial in a real vector can be written as $q(x)=x^TAx$ and is called a quadratic form*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 定义
> 对 $x\in\mathbb R^n$，形如
> $$
> q(x)=x^TAx
> $$
> 的标量函数称为二次型。它是各分量的二次齐次多项式：每一项的总次数都是 $2$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A scalar function $q(x)=x^TAx$ on $\mathbb R^n$ is a quadratic form. It is a homogeneous polynomial of degree two in the components of $x$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二维时，若
$$
S=\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{bmatrix},
$$
则
$$
q(x_1,x_2)=ax_1^2+2bx_1x_2+cx_2^2.
$$
矩阵把平方项与交叉项集中到一个对象里；向量 $x$ 选择方向和尺度，$q(x)$ 则给出该方向上的二次“能量”或曲率值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the displayed symmetric matrix, the form is $ax_1^2+2bx_1x_2+cx_2^2$. The matrix stores the squared and cross terms, while $q(x)$ measures the quadratic value in the direction and at the scale selected by $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二次型不是任意二次函数。它满足
$$
q(tx)=t^2q(x),
$$
所以不含线性项或常数项。例如 $x_1^2+x_2$ 是二次函数，却不是二次型。矩阵表示若允许非对称 $A$ 会有冗余；[[二次型对称化]] 说明为什么每个实二次型都应选唯一的对称代表。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A quadratic form is not an arbitrary polynomial of degree at most two: it obeys $q(tx)=t^2q(x)$ and therefore has no linear or constant term. If nonsymmetric representing matrices are allowed, the representation is redundant; [[二次型对称化|the symmetric-part identity]] selects the unique symmetric representative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 矩阵 $S=\begin{bmatrix}3&-2\\-2&5\end{bmatrix}$ 对应的二次型是什么？
>
> **答案：** $3x_1^2-4x_1x_2+5x_2^2$；交叉项系数是两个对称位置之和。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> What quadratic form corresponds to $S=\begin{bmatrix}3&-2\\-2&5\end{bmatrix}$?
>
> **Answer:** $3x_1^2-4x_1x_2+5x_2^2$. The cross-term coefficient is the sum of the two symmetric off-diagonal entries.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
