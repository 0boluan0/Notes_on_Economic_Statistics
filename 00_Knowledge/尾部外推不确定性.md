# 尾部外推的不确定性同时来自有限尾部样本和阈值依赖结构及尾模型假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Tail extrapolation is uncertain because of finite tail data and assumptions about thresholds, dependence, and the tail model.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

POT 能给出超过历史最大损失的分位估计，因为它把拟合尾函数延伸到未观测区间；这不是获得了新的极端观测。报告一个外推数值时，应区分给定模型下的抽样误差与模型本身的近似、选择和结构风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
POT can estimate quantiles beyond the historical maximum by extending a fitted tail function into an unobserved region. This does not create new extreme observations. Distinguish sampling error conditional on a model from approximation, selection, and structural risks in that model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在固定数值阈值下，$p_u=P(L>u)$ 是总体参数，$N_u/n$ 是它的随机估计；形状和尺度也由有限超额样本估计。只固定 $\hat p_u$、阈值和过滤参数后为 GPD 两个参数计算区间，会遗漏这些被固定步骤的不确定性。若阈值由样本选择，需明确区间是否覆盖该选择过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a fixed numerical threshold, $p_u=P(L>u)$ is a population parameter and $N_u/n$ its random estimate. Shape and scale are also estimated from finite excess data. Intervals for the two GPD parameters that hold the exceedance fraction, threshold, and filtering parameters fixed omit uncertainty in those steps. If the threshold is selected from the sample, state whether the interval accounts for that selection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个敏感性例子：固定 $u=100,\beta=10,p_u=0.05$，只把 $\xi$ 从 $0.1$ 改到 $0.3$。按[[POT分位数]]，99% 分位约从 $117.46$ 变到 $120.69$；99.99% 分位约从 $186.16$ 变到 $281.73$。这不是置信区间，而是同样形状扰动在更远外推中放大的模型对照。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a sensitivity example, fix $u=100$, $\beta=10$, and $p_u=0.05$, changing only $\xi$ from $0.1$ to $0.3$. The [[POT分位数|POT quantile]] moves from about $117.46$ to $120.69$ at 99%, but from $186.16$ to $281.73$ at 99.99%. These are not confidence limits; they show how the same shape perturbation can be amplified by more distant extrapolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区间方法要与估计条件匹配：在适用的正规区域，可用参数重表达后的剖面似然或合适的模拟/Bootstrap 方法，并在每次重抽时重做要纳入不确定性的拟合步骤。时间相关的数据还需要相容的依赖处理。方法名称本身不保证覆盖率；给定模型的区间也不能自动涵盖未来断点或错误尾族。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Match interval construction to the estimation conditions. In a suitable regular regime, use reparameterized profile likelihood or a justified simulation/bootstrap procedure, refitting the steps whose uncertainty is to be included. Dependent data also require compatible dependence handling. A method's name does not guarantee coverage, and model-conditional intervals do not automatically cover future breaks or an incorrect tail family.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 [[POT尾部ES|ES]] 更要看 $\xi=1$ 的有限均值边界：即使点估计小于 1，若合理的不确定性范围包含不小于 1 的形状，有限 ES 就不是该范围内所有模型的共同结论。强制截断参数空间可得到有限数字，但那是新增模型限制，不是尾均值有限的观测证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For [[POT尾部ES|ES]], inspect the finite-mean boundary at $\xi=1$. Even with a point estimate below one, a plausible uncertainty range containing shapes at or above one does not support finite ES for every model in that range. Truncating the parameter space can force finite outputs, but adds a modelling restriction rather than observational evidence of a finite tail mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Sources and verification*

- [Haugh，Columbia《Extreme Value Theory》，PDF 第 28–29 页](https://martin-haugh.github.io/files/QRM/EVT_MasterSlides.pdf#page=29)：支持风险值外推、置信区间与阈值敏感性检查。形状扰动例按同一分位公式独立复算，并明确不是区间估计。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  PDF pp. 28–29 support risk extrapolation, interval reporting, and threshold sensitivity checks. The shape-perturbation example is independently computed from one quantile formula and is explicitly not an interval estimate.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Belzile，UNIL 2025，“Risk measures and extrapolation”“Common challenges”“Metric-based adjustment”“Bayesian predictive distribution”](https://lbelzile.github.io/UNIL-2025-choosing-threshold/UNIL-choosing_threshold.html)：支持尾比例作为额外参数、选择过程与重拟合的不确定性。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The listed sections support the tail fraction as an additional parameter and uncertainty from selection and refitting.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Belzile，EVA 2023，“Statistical inference”及“Generalized Pareto distribution”](https://lbelzile.github.io/EVA2023-Rtutorial/content/likelihood.html#statistical-inference)：支持剖面似然区间及常规似然推断的适用边界；ES 有限性的范围判断由[[广义Pareto矩存在条件]]独立核验。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  These sections support profile-likelihood intervals and the limits of conventional likelihood inference. The range-wise ES finiteness conclusion follows from [[广义Pareto矩存在条件|the GPD moment conditions]].
