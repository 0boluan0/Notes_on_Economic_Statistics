# Quasiconvex functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拟凸函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **quasiconvex function** keeps a shared upper limit intact when inputs are blended. Think of its value as a cost: if two choices each cost at most a chosen budget, every point between them also respects that budget. This must work for every budget, not merely for one convenient value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**拟凸函数**在混合输入时保住共同的上限。把函数值想成成本：如果两个选择的成本都不超过某个预算，它们之间的每个选择也都不超过该预算。这个要求针对每个预算，而不是某个方便的值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The allowed inputs form a [[凸集|convex domain]], so their connecting segments are allowed too. For a fixed budget, the inputs costing at most that amount form a [[下轮廓集|lower contour set]]. Quasiconvexity asks whether each such input set is convex; it does not directly compare the cost of a mixture with the average of the two costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

合法输入组成[[凸集|凸定义域]]，因此连接它们的线段也合法。固定预算下，成本不超过预算的输入组成[[下轮廓集]]。拟凸性检查每个这样的输入集合是否凸，而不直接把混合成本与两端成本的平均值比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Every sublevel set must be convex
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每个下轮廓集都必须凸*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A quasiconvex function has a convex domain and convex lower contour sets at every threshold
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在凸域 $U$ 上，$f$ 拟凸，是指每个门槛 $k$ 的[[下轮廓集]] $\{x:f(x)\le k\}$ 都为[[凸集]]。它等价于 $-f$ [[拟凹函数|拟凹]]。

> [!important] Inputs, outputs and the threshold · 输入、输出与门槛
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be convex and $f:U\to\mathbb R$ be real-valued. For every $k\in\mathbb R$, define $C_k^-=\{x\in U:f(x)\le k\}$. Quasiconvexity means that every $C_k^-$ is convex. Thus any $x,y\in C_k^-$ and $t\in[0,1]$ have $tx+(1-t)y\in C_k^-$. The set consists of inputs; $k$ bounds their output values.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 为凸集，$f:U\to\mathbb R$ 为实值函数。对每个 $k\in\mathbb R$，定义 $C_k^-=\{x\in U:f(x)\le k\}$。拟凸性要求每个 $C_k^-$ 都凸：任取 $x,y\in C_k^-$ 及 $t\in[0,1]$，仍有 $tx+(1-t)y\in C_k^-$。集合中的元素是输入，门槛 $k$ 限制的是这些输入产生的输出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A cubic that is quasiconvex but not convex
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拟凸但不凸的立方函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quasiconvexity means every [[下轮廓集|lower contour set]] is [[凸集|convex]], equivalently that the negative function is [[拟凹函数|quasiconcave]]. The monotone cubic has the property on the real line without being convex there. The [[拟凸性线段判据|segment criterion]] is an equivalent test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一元 $f(x)=x^3$ 单调，故下轮廓集是半直线，函数拟凸；但它在整个实线上不凸。拟凸不要误读为函数图像一定处处向上弯。等价不等式见[[拟凸性线段判据]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any real threshold $k$, $x^3\le k$ exactly when $x\le\sqrt[3]{k}$, where $\sqrt[3]{k}$ is the real cube root. Therefore $C_k^-=(-\infty,\sqrt[3]{k}]$, a convex interval. For example, $k=8$ gives $(-\infty,2]$ and $k=-8$ gives $(-\infty,-2]$. The argument covers all real thresholds, including negative ones.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意实数门槛 $k$，$x^3\le k$ 等价于 $x\le\sqrt[3]{k}$，其中 $\sqrt[3]{k}$ 表示实立方根。因此 $C_k^-=(-\infty,\sqrt[3]{k}]$ 是凸区间。例如 $k=8$ 给出 $(-\infty,2]$，$k=-8$ 给出 $(-\infty,-2]$。同一个论证覆盖包括负数在内的全部门槛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see why ordinary [[凹凸性|convexity]] fails, use inputs $-2$ and $0$. Their midpoint is $-1$, but $f(-1)=-1>\tfrac12f(-2)+\tfrac12f(0)=-4$, opposite to the required convex inequality. Quasiconvexity only keeps the midpoint below the shared upper budget $0$; indeed $-1\le0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要看出普通[[凹凸性|凸性]]失败，取输入 $-2$ 和 $0$。其中点为 $-1$，但 $f(-1)=-1>\tfrac12f(-2)+\tfrac12f(0)=-4$，与凸函数要求的方向相反。拟凸性只要求中点不超过两端共同满足的上限 $0$；这里确有 $-1\le0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Negating the function reverses the threshold
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*函数取负，门槛随之反向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sign-reversal statement follows directly from the sets, without differentiability. Put $g=-f$. At any upper threshold $a$ for $g$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取负对应直接来自集合，不需要可导。令 $g=-f$，则 $g$ 在任意上门槛 $a$ 下满足

$$\{x\in U:g(x)\ge a\}=\{x\in U:f(x)\le-a\}=C_{-a}^-.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As $a$ ranges over every real number, so does $-a$. Hence all upper sets of $g$ are convex exactly when all lower sets of $f$ are convex. This proves $f$ is quasiconvex if and only if $-f$ is [[拟凹函数|quasiconcave]], with the same convex domain. The separate [[拟凸性线段判据|segment criterion]] gives the corresponding endpoint inequality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $a$ 取遍实数时，$-a$ 也取遍实数。因此 $g$ 的全部上轮廓集凸，当且仅当 $f$ 的全部下轮廓集凸。这就证明了在同一个凸定义域上，$f$ 拟凸当且仅当 $-f$ [[拟凹函数|拟凹]]。独立的[[拟凸性线段判据]]给出对应的端点不等式。

## Which apparent shortcuts fail?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*哪些看似方便的检查不够？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One convex sublevel set is insufficient. For $q(x)=-x^2$ on $\mathbb R$, the set at $k=0$ is the entire line. At $k=-1$, both $-1$ and $1$ qualify but their midpoint $0$ does not, since $q(0)=0>-1$. Thus $q$ is not quasiconvex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个凸下轮廓集还不够。对 $\mathbb R$ 上的 $q(x)=-x^2$，门槛 $k=0$ 给出整条实线。但 $k=-1$ 时，$-1$ 和 $1$ 都达标，中点 $0$ 却不达标，因为 $q(0)=0>-1$。因此 $q$ 不拟凸。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Neither continuity nor differentiability is assumed. The step function $s(x)=0$ for $x<0$ and $s(x)=1$ for $x\ge0$ has sublevel sets that are empty for $k<0$, $(-\infty,0)$ for $0\le k<1$, and $\mathbb R$ for $k\ge1$. They are convex even though the middle set is open and the function is discontinuous. A weak inequality does not by itself make a sublevel set closed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义既不假设连续，也不假设可导。阶跃函数 $s(x)=0$（$x<0$）、$s(x)=1$（$x\ge0$）的下轮廓集在 $k<0$ 时为空集，在 $0\le k<1$ 时为 $(-\infty,0)$，在 $k\ge1$ 时为 $\mathbb R$。这些集合全凸，尽管中间的集合是开的，函数也不连续。仅仅写出弱不等号，并不能保证下轮廓集闭。

> [!warning] Weak does not mean strict · 弱条件不是严格条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The definition includes $f(x)=k$. Replacing $\le$ by $<$ can change a given threshold set: the sets differ exactly when $f$ attains $k$. A mixture need not strictly reduce costs. For the constant function $f=3$, each weak sublevel set is empty for $k<3$ and the whole convex domain for $k\ge3$, so the function is quasiconvex without any strict reduction. Empty sets and single-point domains cause no exception; an empty domain satisfies the condition vacuously.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 定义包含 $f(x)=k$。把 $\le$ 换成 $<$ 可能改变固定门槛的集合：当且仅当 $f$ 实际取到值 $k$ 时，两者才不同。混合也不一定严格降低成本。常函数 $f=3$ 在 $k<3$ 时的弱下轮廓集为空，在 $k\ge3$ 时为整个凸定义域，因此它拟凸，却没有任何严格降低。空集和单点定义域都不构成例外；空定义域上的条件真空成立。
