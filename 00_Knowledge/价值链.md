# 价值链是企业为交付客户价值而开展并相互联结的活动体系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A value chain is the interconnected system of activities through which a firm delivers customer value.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价值链把企业拆成具有战略意义的活动，考察成本和差异化从哪里产生。波特的基本框架包含进货物流、生产作业、出货物流、营销销售与服务，以及采购、技术、人力资源和企业基础设施等支持活动。“拆开分析”不表示活动彼此独立；活动之间的协调也会影响成本与客户体验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A value chain disaggregates the firm into strategically relevant activities to locate costs and sources of differentiation. Porter's framework includes inbound logistics, operations, outbound logistics, marketing and sales, and service, supported by procurement, technology, human resources, and firm infrastructure. Disaggregation does not imply independence: coordination affects both costs and customer experience.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自拟例：家具设计成可平板包装，可能同时减少仓储和运输占用，却把部分组装工作交给客户。分析重点不是单独宣称“物流便宜”，而是设计、交付和客户接受度能否共同支撑[[价值主张]]；若售后返工抵消节约，局部省钱未必是全链优势。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed example, flat-pack furniture design can reduce storage and transport requirements while shifting assembly work to customers. The question is whether design, delivery, and customer acceptance jointly support the [[价值主张|value proposition]]. If service rework offsets the savings, one cheaper activity need not create an overall advantage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

企业价值链通常处于包含上游供应商和下游渠道的更大价值系统中，但它不是仅列供应商到客户的货物流向，也不是把各部门会计利润简单相加的公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A firm's chain usually sits within a wider value system containing suppliers and downstream channels. It is not merely a goods-flow diagram or a formula that adds up departmental accounting profits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
