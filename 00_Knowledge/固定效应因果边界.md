# 个体固定效应只消除加性时间不变的单位成分而不自动识别因果效应

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Unit fixed effects remove only additive time-invariant unit components and do not automatically identify a causal effect*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子命题
> 个体固定效应消去的是 $c_i$ 这类加性且时间不变的单位成分。随时间变化的遗漏冲击仍在 $u_{it}$ 中；只要它们与回归量的单位内变化相关，FE 斜率就不能仅凭“加了固定效应”获得因果解释。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Unit fixed effects remove additive, time-invariant unit components such as $c_i$. Time-varying omitted shocks remain in $u_{it}$; when they correlate with within-unit regressor changes, adding fixed effects alone does not give the slope a causal interpretation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

培训前后比较可以消去一个人稳定能力的加性水平，但若培训同年发生晋升，晋升仍随时间变化并可能与培训相关。FE 改变了比较对象，却没有自己创造可信反事实；因果解释还需要研究设计、外生变化或明确的结构条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A before-after comparison may remove a person's stable additive ability level. If promotion occurs in the training year, however, promotion remains time-varying and may correlate with training. FE changes the comparison but does not create a credible counterfactual; causal interpretation still requires a research design, exogenous variation, or explicit structural conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若问题本质上是政策处理比较，应先定义目标效应与对照组，再判断 fixed effects 是否执行了那个比较；具体设计边界复用 [[DID 估计目标与对照组]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a policy-treatment comparison, first define the target effect and comparison group, then ask whether the fixed-effects specification implements that comparison. The DID design boundary remains in its dedicated atom.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 为什么 FE 能处理不变能力，却不能单凭自身处理“低工资冲击促使下期参加培训”？
>
> **答案：** 不变能力被单位效应吸收；冲击到未来培训的反馈是一条时变路径，仍会破坏变换后的矩条件。
