# The implicit function theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*隐函数定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An equation can describe a relationship without explicitly giving one variable as a function of the others. For example, $x^2+y^2=25$ describes a circle. Near $(3,4)$, a small change in $x$ determines one nearby positive value of $y$. The **implicit function theorem** gives a sufficient condition for this kind of local solving, even for several equations whose solutions cannot be written down explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个方程可以描述变量间的关系，而没有直接把其中某个变量写成其他变量的函数。例如，$x^2+y^2=25$ 描述一个圆。在 $(3,4)$ 附近，稍微改变 $x$，就能确定附近唯一的正数 $y$。**隐函数定理**给出这种局部求解的充分条件；即使有多个方程、写不出显式解，也可以使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Inputs, unknowns, and the derivative block
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先选输入、待解变量和对应导数块*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $u\in\mathbb R^p$ be the inputs that may vary, and $v\in\mathbb R^q$ the variables to be solved for; $p,q$ are positive integers. A map $F:O\to\mathbb R^q$ collects $q$ equations $F_1(u,v)=0,\ldots,F_q(u,v)=0$. Its domain $O\subseteq\mathbb R^{p+q}$ is an [[开集|open set]], so small perturbations around each of its points remain available. Assume $F$ is $C^1$ on $O$: every component has continuous first [[偏导数|partial derivatives]] in all input coordinates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $u\in\mathbb R^p$ 是允许变化的输入，$v\in\mathbb R^q$ 是待解变量，$p,q$ 均为正整数。映射 $F:O\to\mathbb R^q$ 汇集了 $q$ 个方程 $F_1(u,v)=0,\ldots,F_q(u,v)=0$。定义域 $O\subseteq\mathbb R^{p+q}$ 是[[开集]]，保证各点附近的小扰动仍在可讨论范围内。假设 $F$ 在 $O$ 上为 $C^1$，即每个分量关于全部输入坐标的一阶[[偏导数]]都存在且连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Jacobian矩阵|Jacobian matrix]] $DF$ has one row for each equation and one column for each input coordinate. Split its columns according to $u$ and $v$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Jacobian矩阵]] $DF$ 每一行对应一个方程，每一列对应一个输入坐标。按 $u$ 与 $v$ 拆开各列，得到

$$DF(u,v)=\begin{pmatrix}D_uF(u,v)&D_vF(u,v)\end{pmatrix},\qquad
D_uF\in\mathbb R^{q\times p},\quad D_vF\in\mathbb R^{q\times q}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The entry in row $i$, column $j$ of $D_vF$ is $\partial F_i/\partial v_j$: vary the $j$th unknown while holding all other coordinates fixed. This is the square block to test. Its [[逆矩阵|invertibility]] means that its linear action can be reversed; the whole $q\times(p+q)$ Jacobian is rectangular and has no ordinary square-matrix inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$D_vF$ 的第 $i$ 行、第 $j$ 列是 $\partial F_i/\partial v_j$：固定其余坐标，只改变第 $j$ 个待解变量。这才是需要检验的方块。它[[逆矩阵|可逆]]，表示其线性作用能够被撤销；整个 $q\times(p+q)$ Jacobian 是长方矩阵，没有普通方阵意义的逆。

> [!important] A local graph of all nearby solutions · 全部附近解构成一张局部图像
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $(u_0,v_0)\in O$, $F(u_0,v_0)=0$, and $D_vF(u_0,v_0)$ is invertible. There are open neighbourhoods $U$ of $u_0$ and $V$ of $v_0$, with $U\times V\subseteq O$, and a unique $C^1$ map $g:U\to V$ such that $g(u_0)=v_0$ and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $(u_0,v_0)\in O$、$F(u_0,v_0)=0$，且 $D_vF(u_0,v_0)$ 可逆，则存在 $u_0$ 的开邻域 $U$、$v_0$ 的开邻域 $V$，满足 $U\times V\subseteq O$，以及唯一的 $C^1$ 映射 $g:U\to V$，使 $g(u_0)=v_0$，并且
>
> $$\text{for every }(u,v)\in U\times V,\qquad
> F(u,v)=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad v=g(u).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus each nearby input has a solution in $V$, that solution is unique in $V$, and it varies with continuous first derivatives. An open neighbourhood means an open set containing the specified point. The conclusion is stronger than merely selecting one solution from several.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因此，每个附近输入在 $V$ 内都有解，该解在 $V$ 内唯一，而且随输入变化时有连续的一阶导数。开邻域就是包含指定点的开集。这个结论比“从几个解中选出一条”更强。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Local uniqueness is not global uniqueness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## “局部唯一”不是“全局只有一个解”

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $F(x,y)=x^2+y^2-25$, the derivative with respect to the unknown $y$ is $F_y=2y$. At $(3,4)$ it is $8\ne0$. Here $p=q=1$, so the block is a single nonzero number. We can explicitly choose $U=(2,4)$ and $V=(2,5)$. For every $x\in U$, the only root in $V$ is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $F(x,y)=x^2+y^2-25$，关于待解变量 $y$ 的导数为 $F_y=2y$，在 $(3,4)$ 处为 $8\ne0$。这里 $p=q=1$，方块退化成一个非零数。可以具体取 $U=(2,4)$、$V=(2,5)$。对每个 $x\in U$，唯一落在 $V$ 中的根为

$$y=g(x)=\sqrt{25-x^2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indeed, $4<x^2<16$ gives $3<g(x)<\sqrt{21}<5$. The negative root lies outside $V$. The lower semicircle therefore does not contradict uniqueness: the theorem describes the zero set inside a chosen product neighbourhood, not all solutions everywhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $4<x^2<16$，所以 $3<g(x)<\sqrt{21}<5$，而负根不在 $V$ 内。下半圆并不违反唯一性：定理描述的是选定乘积邻域内的零集，不是各处的全部解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choose the block for the variables you want to solve for
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 必须选择与待解变量对应的可逆方块

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $(5,0)$ on the same circle, $F_y=0$ but $F_x=10\ne0$. Solving for $x$ in terms of $y$ works: near $y=0$ the branch is $x=h(y)=\sqrt{25-y^2}$, with $h(0)=5$. Solving for $y$ over an open interval of $x$ around $5$ does not work for this particular circle: $x>5$ has no real solution, and $x<5$ sufficiently close to $5$ has two solutions close to $0$. This failure follows from the circle equation; it is not a general consequence of $F_y=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一圆在 $(5,0)$ 处有 $F_y=0$，但 $F_x=10\ne0$。把 $x$ 作为 $y$ 的函数求解是可行的：在 $y=0$ 附近，解支为 $x=h(y)=\sqrt{25-y^2}$，且 $h(0)=5$。对这个圆，不能在包含 $5$ 的开区间上把 $y$ 写成唯一的 $x$ 函数：$x>5$ 时没有实数解，$x<5$ 且足够接近 $5$ 时，则有两个接近 $0$ 的解。这个失败由圆方程直接判断，并不是 $F_y=0$ 的一般推论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For several equations, the same choice matters: solving for $q$ selected coordinates requires testing the corresponding $q$ columns. Counting equations and unknowns alone does not establish invertibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多个方程时也一样：要解出选定的 $q$ 个坐标，就检验它们对应的 $q$ 列。仅仅数出方程与未知量的个数，不能证明可逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the theorem follows from local inversion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么可以由局部求逆得到解支*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[逆函数定理|inverse function theorem]] recovers all inputs from a square map whose derivative is invertible. To use it here, retain $u$ and replace $v$ by the equation values:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[逆函数定理]]能从导数可逆的方形映射中局部恢复全部输入。为了在这里使用它，保留 $u$，把 $v$ 换成方程的值：

$$H(u,v)=(u,F(u,v)),\qquad
DH(u_0,v_0)=\begin{pmatrix}I_p&0\\ A&B\end{pmatrix},
\quad A=D_uF(u_0,v_0),\quad B=D_vF(u_0,v_0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $H$ has $p+q$ inputs and outputs, $I_p$ is the $p\times p$ [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]], and the upper-right zero block is $p\times q$. Given an output increment $(s,t)\in\mathbb R^p\times\mathbb R^q$, solving $DH(u_0,v_0)(h,k)=(s,t)$ gives the unique increments
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $H$ 的输入与输出都有 $p+q$ 个坐标，$I_p$ 是 $p\times p$ [[单位矩阵]]，右上方零块的尺寸为 $p\times q$。给定输出增量 $(s,t)\in\mathbb R^p\times\mathbb R^q$，方程 $DH(u_0,v_0)(h,k)=(s,t)$ 有唯一的输入增量

$$h=s,\qquad k=B^{-1}(t-As).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So $DH(u_0,v_0)$ is invertible. The inverse function theorem supplies a $C^1$ inverse $K$ between open neighbourhoods of $(u_0,v_0)$ and $(u_0,0)$. Because $H$ leaves its first coordinates unchanged, this inverse has the form $K(s,w)=(s,k(s,w))$. Setting $w=0$ gives $g(s)=k(s,0)$ and $F(s,g(s))=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 $DH(u_0,v_0)$ 可逆。逆函数定理在 $(u_0,v_0)$ 与 $(u_0,0)$ 的开邻域之间给出 $C^1$ 逆映射 $K$。由于 $H$ 保持前一组坐标不变，其逆必为 $K(s,w)=(s,k(s,w))$。令 $w=0$，就得到 $g(s)=k(s,0)$，并有 $F(s,g(s))=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For completeness, choose small open sets $U_0,V$ around $u_0,v_0$ with $U_0\times V$ inside the neighbourhood where $H$ is one-to-one. Shrink $U_0$ so that $(u,0)$ lies in the domain of $K$; then shrink it again to an open $U$ so that $g(U)\subseteq V$, using the continuity of $g$ and $g(u_0)=v_0$. For any zero $(u,v)\in U\times V$, both $(u,v)$ and $(u,g(u))$ have the same image $(u,0)$ under the one-to-one map $H$. They must coincide. This proves existence, local uniqueness and $C^1$ regularity in the stated product neighbourhood.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

补齐邻域细节：先在 $u_0,v_0$ 周围选小开集 $U_0,V$，使 $U_0\times V$ 落在 $H$ 单射的邻域内。缩小 $U_0$，保证 $(u,0)$ 属于 $K$ 的定义域；再由 $g$ 的连续性及 $g(u_0)=v_0$，缩成开集 $U$，使 $g(U)\subseteq V$。若 $(u,v)\in U\times V$ 是零点，则它与 $(u,g(u))$ 经单射 $H$ 都映到 $(u,0)$，两点必相同。这样便证明了所述乘积邻域中的存在性、局部唯一性和 $C^1$ 正则性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## After the theorem supplies the branch
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 定理给出解支之后

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem guarantees a branch; it need not give a closed expression for it or guarantee global continuation. Once the branch exists, the [[多元链式法则|multivariable chain rule]] applied to $F(u,g(u))=0$ gives the [[隐函数导数公式|implicit derivative formula]]. If the selected block is singular, the theorem's hypothesis fails, but a smooth branch may still exist; that distinction is the subject of [[隐函数奇异块不推无解支|the singular-block boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定理保证解支存在，不保证能写出闭式表达式，也不保证能全局延拓。解支建立以后，对 $F(u,g(u))=0$ 使用[[多元链式法则]]，即可得到[[隐函数导数公式]]。若所选块奇异，定理的假设不成立，但光滑解支仍可能存在；这个区别见[[隐函数奇异块不推无解支]]。

> [!question]- Which variable can be solved for? · 应选择哪个待解变量？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the circle at $(5,0)$, which choice lets you apply the theorem directly? **Answer:** solve for $x$, since $F_x=10\ne0$. The conclusion is the unique local $C^1$ branch $x=h(y)$ through $(5,0)$, not a unique $y=g(x)$ there.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在圆的 $(5,0)$ 处，选哪个变量能直接使用定理？**答案：** 选 $x$，因为 $F_x=10\ne0$。结论是经过 $(5,0)$ 的唯一局部 $C^1$ 解支 $x=h(y)$，不是该处唯一的 $y=g(x)$。
