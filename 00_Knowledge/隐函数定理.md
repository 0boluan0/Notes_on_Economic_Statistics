# 若对待解变量的 Jacobian 块可逆，隐函数定理在零点附近给出唯一的 $C^1$ 解支
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An invertible Jacobian block for the solved-for variables gives a locally unique $C^1$ implicit branch near a zero*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心定理
> 设 $F:\mathbb R^p\times\mathbb R^q\to\mathbb R^q$ 在 $(u_0,v_0)$ 附近为 $C^1$，并满足 $F(u_0,v_0)=0$。若对待解变量 $v$ 的方块
> $$
> D_vF(u_0,v_0)\in\mathbb R^{q\times q}
> $$
> 可逆，则存在 $u_0$ 的邻域 $U$、$v_0$ 的邻域 $V$，以及唯一的 $C^1$ 函数 $g:U\to V$，使 $g(u_0)=v_0$，并且对每个 $(u,v)\in U\times V$，
> $$
> F(u,v)=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad v=g(u).
> $$
> 存在性、唯一性和光滑性都只针对指定零点附近的这一条解支。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $F:\mathbb R^p\times\mathbb R^q\to\mathbb R^q$ be $C^1$ near a zero $(u_0,v_0)$. If the displayed square Jacobian block with respect to the variables $v$ being solved for is invertible, then there are neighbourhoods $U$ of $u_0$ and $V$ of $v_0$ and a unique $C^1$ map $g:U\to V$ with $g(u_0)=v_0$ such that $F(u,v)=0$ exactly when $v=g(u)$ within $U\times V$. Existence, uniqueness, and smoothness are local to this branch near the specified zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## “局部唯一”不是“全局只有一个解”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Local uniqueness is not global uniqueness*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对圆
$$
F(x,y)=x^2+y^2-25=0,
$$
在点 $(3,4)$ 有 $F_y=8\ne0$。因此可以选择 $y$ 为待解变量：在 $(3,4)$ 附近，圆的零集恰好是一条唯一的 $C^1$ 图像 $y=g(x)$。圆在别处仍有下半圆分支，但它不位于所选的局部邻域中，所以不与定理的局部唯一性冲突。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the circle $F(x,y)=x^2+y^2-25=0$, one has $F_y=8\ne0$ at $(3,4)$. We may therefore choose $y$ as the solved-for variable, and near $(3,4)$ the zero set is exactly one $C^1$ graph $y=g(x)$. The lower semicircle still exists elsewhere, but it lies outside the chosen local neighbourhood and does not conflict with local uniqueness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 必须选择与待解变量对应的可逆方块
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The invertible block must correspond to the variables being solved for*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个关系能否写成函数，取决于把哪些变量指定为输出。在圆的点 $(5,0)$，$F_y=0$，所以标准定理不能在该点把 $y$ 唯一写成 $x$ 的函数；但 $F_x=10\ne0$，于是可以把 $x$ 局部写成 $y$ 的唯一 $C^1$ 函数。对一般方程组，要解出 $q$ 个变量，就必须从 Jacobian 中选取与这 $q$ 个变量对应的 $q\times q$ 可逆块。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether the same relation can be represented as a function depends on which variables are designated as outputs. At $(5,0)$ on the circle, $F_y=0$, so the standard theorem does not solve locally for a unique $y$ as a function of $x$. Since $F_x=10\ne0$, it does solve for $x$ as a unique $C^1$ function of $y$. For a general system, solving for $q$ variables requires the corresponding invertible $q\times q$ block of the Jacobian.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 定理给出解支之后
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*After the theorem supplies the branch*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一旦局部解支已经建立，其导数可由链式法则计算，见 [[隐函数导数公式]]。若选定的 Jacobian 块奇异，只能说这条标准定理不再提供保证，不能据此宣布解支不存在；这一逻辑边界见 [[隐函数奇异块不推无解支]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once the local branch has been established, its derivative follows from the chain rule; see [[隐函数导数公式|the derivative formula for an implicit function]]. If the selected Jacobian block is singular, this standard theorem no longer supplies a guarantee, but that fact alone does not prove that no branch exists; see [[隐函数奇异块不推无解支|the singular-block boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 对 $F(x,y)=x^2+y^2-25$，在 $(5,0)$ 应选择哪个变量作为待解变量，才能直接使用隐函数定理？这给出什么局部结论？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $F(x,y)=x^2+y^2-25$, which variable should be selected as the solved-for variable at $(5,0)$ in order to apply the implicit function theorem directly, and what local conclusion follows?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 应选择 $x$，因为 $F_x(5,0)=10\ne0$。定理给出 $(5,0)$ 附近唯一的 $C^1$ 解支 $x=h(y)$；它不在该点保证唯一的 $y=g(x)$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Choose $x$, because $F_x(5,0)=10\ne0$. The theorem gives a unique local $C^1$ branch $x=h(y)$ near $(5,0)$; it does not supply a unique branch $y=g(x)$ there.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
