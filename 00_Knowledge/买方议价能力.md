# 买方议价能力是购买方争取更有利交易条件的能力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Buyer bargaining power is purchasers' ability to obtain more favorable transaction terms.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

买方议价能力表现为压低价格、要求更高质量或更多服务、改善付款等条款，从而影响卖方可保留的利润。能力来自双方的选择和依赖关系，而不是买方“很想便宜买”这一愿望。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Buyer power can produce lower prices, higher quality, additional service, or better payment terms, affecting the seller's retained profit. It depends on the parties' alternatives and dependence, not merely the buyer's desire for a lower price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

大额采购、可替换供应商和低转换成本可能增强能力。产品占买方成本比例高，首先说明它可能更在意价格，并不单独证明它能迫使卖方让步。还须区分直接客户与最终用户：一个集中采购的经销商可能比许多分散消费者更有议价能力。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Large purchases, alternative suppliers, and low switching costs may strengthen power. A high share of the buyer's costs primarily suggests price sensitivity; it does not alone establish the ability to force concessions. Distinguish direct customers from end users: a bulk-purchasing distributor may have more bargaining power than dispersed consumers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
