# 剩余价值率是剩余价值与可变资本的比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The rate of surplus value is surplus value divided by variable capital*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

剩余价值率（rate of surplus value）是 [[剩余价值]] $s$ 与 [[可变资本]] $v$ 的比率，表示剩余相对于购买劳动力所用价值的大小。在同一劳动力使用期内，劳动力按其价值购买、两部分劳动采用一致的社会劳动折算时，它等于剩余劳动时间与必要劳动时间之比。Marx 用这个比率表示劳动力受资本剥削的程度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rate of surplus value is the ratio of [[剩余价值|surplus value]] $s$ to [[可变资本|variable capital]] $v$. It expresses the surplus relative to the value used to purchase labour-power. For the same period of labour-power's use, with labour-power purchased at its value and both portions of labour converted consistently into social labour, it equals surplus labour time divided by necessary labour time. Marx uses this ratio to express the degree of exploitation of labour-power by capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{s}{v}=\frac{t_s}{t_n}.
$$

在8小时、必要劳动4小时的教学例中，剩余劳动也为4小时，故剩余价值率为 $4/4=100\%$。若工作日仍为8小时、必要劳动变为3小时，则它为 $5/3\approx166.7\%$。分母为必要劳动时间；$5/8$ 则表示剩余劳动占整个工作日的份额，回答的是另一个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the constructed example of an eight-hour day with four hours of necessary labour, surplus labour also lasts four hours, so the rate is $4/4=100\%$. If the day stays at eight hours and necessary labour falls to three, the rate becomes $5/3\approx166.7\%$. The denominator is necessary labour time. The ratio $5/8$ instead gives surplus labour's share of the whole working day and answers a different question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个比率也不确定剩余价值总量或工作日长度：4小时必要加4小时剩余，与5小时必要加5小时剩余，都是100%，但在同样的劳动折算下，后者创造的剩余价值更多。计算 [[Marx利润率|利润率]] 又需要全部预付资本作为分母；跨多个周转期计算时，应先统一 [[预付与耗用资本|预付量、期间耗用量与周转]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ratio alone does not determine the mass of surplus value or the working day's length. Four necessary plus four surplus hours and five necessary plus five surplus hours both yield 100%, while the latter produces more surplus value with the same labour conversion. A [[Marx利润率|profit rate]] uses total capital advanced as its denominator. Across multiple turnovers, first align [[预付与耗用资本|advances, period consumption and turnover]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Marx，《资本论》卷I，[第9章第1节及注7](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch09.htm)：支持 $s/v$ 的定义、劳动时间比的等价条件、剥削程度含义及比率与绝对量的区分；[第10章第1节](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch10.htm#S1)说明同一100%不确定工作日长度。MIA 所载 Moore / Aveling 英译、Engels 编，1887英文初版底本，标示处纳入德文第四版改动。4/4与5/3为统一口径下的教学计算；利润率与周转口径由所链专卡展开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volume I, Chapter 9, Section 1 and note 7 support $s/v$, its conditional equivalence to the labour-time ratio, its meaning as a degree of exploitation, and the distinction between rate and absolute amount. Chapter 10, Section 1 explains why 100% does not determine the working day's length. The MIA text uses the Moore and Aveling translation edited by Engels, based on the 1887 first English edition with indicated fourth German edition changes. The ratios 4/4 and 5/3 are constructed with consistent assumptions; the linked notes develop profit-rate and turnover measurement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
