# Variance of a linear combination
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*线性组合的方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose you combine several measurements into one number: add two readings, subtract one from another, or take a weighted average. Their individual variances are not enough to determine how much the result varies. You also need to know how the readings move together. **The variance of a linear combination** uses the covariance matrix to account for both contributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把多个测量值合成一个数时，可以相加、相减，也可以加权平均。仅知道各自的方差，还不足以确定结果怎样波动；还要知道这些量怎样共同变化。**线性组合的方差**通过协方差矩阵同时计入这两部分。

> [!example] A difference can be less variable than a sum · 相减与相加的波动不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X_1,X_2$ be two readings with variances $4,9$ and covariance $3$. Their [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] collects those numbers as $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}4&3\\3&9\end{bmatrix}$. The difference uses weights $a=(1,-1)^T$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个读数 $X_1,X_2$ 的方差为 $4,9$，协方差为 $3$。[[协方差矩阵]]将它们整理为 $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}4&3\\3&9\end{bmatrix}$。差值使用权重 $a=(1,-1)^T$：
>
> $$\operatorname{Var}(X_1-X_2)=4+9-2(3)=7.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The sum instead has variance $4+9+2(3)=19$. Positive covariance means some of the movements reinforce each other in the sum and cancel in the difference. The weights do not have to be probabilities or add to one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 和的方差则为 $4+9+2(3)=19$。正协方差表示一部分共同波动在相加时增强、相减时抵消。权重不必是概率，也不必加起来等于一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[随机向量|random vector]] $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$ lists the measurements from the same outcome. The superscript $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. For a fixed real coefficient vector $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_p)^T$, the scalar $a^TX=\sum_j a_jX_j$ is one linear combination. Assume $p\ge1$ and every coordinate has a finite second moment, $E[X_j^2]<\infty$. Write $\mu=E[X]$ and $\Sigma=\operatorname{Cov}(X)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[随机向量]] $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$ 把同一结果中的多个测量值列在一起，上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。固定的实系数向量 $a=(a_1,\ldots,a_p)^T$ 给出标量 $a^TX=\sum_j a_jX_j$，也就是一个线性组合。设 $p\ge1$，各坐标二阶矩有限，即 $E[X_j^2]<\infty$，并记 $\mu=E[X]$、$\Sigma=\operatorname{Cov}(X)$。

> [!important] One quadratic form gives the variance · 一个二次型读出方差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every fixed $a\in\mathbb R^p$, including $a=0$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每个固定 $a\in\mathbb R^p$，包括 $a=0$，都有
>
> $$
> \boxed{\operatorname{Var}(a^TX)=a^T\Sigma a
> =\sum_{j=1}^p a_j^2\Sigma_{jj}
> +2\sum_{j<k}a_ja_k\Sigma_{jk}.}
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first sum counts the individual variances. The second counts each distinct pair once with its factor of two. No independence or normality assumption is needed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一项汇总各自方差，第二项把每个不同坐标对计入一次，再乘二。不需要独立或正态分布假设。

## Expanding the centered combination
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 直接从中心化投影展开

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[期望线性性|Linearity of expectation]] gives $E[a^TX]=a^T\mu$. Hence its deviation from its own mean is $a^T(X-\mu)$. The definition of [[方差|variance]] averages the square of this scalar:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[期望线性性]]，$E[a^TX]=a^T\mu$。因此，组合偏离自身均值的量是 $a^T(X-\mu)$。[[方差]]定义要求平均这个标量的平方：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}(a^TX)
&=E\!\left[(a^T(X-\mu))^2\right]\\
&=E\!\left[a^T(X-\mu)(X-\mu)^Ta\right]\\
&=a^TE[(X-\mu)(X-\mu)^T]a\\
&=a^T\Sigma a.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second line rewrites a scalar square as a row–matrix–column product. The fixed coefficients can be taken outside the expectation. Finite second moments make all these products integrable, as the [[期望的Cauchy–Schwarz不等式|Cauchy–Schwarz inequality for expectations]] shows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二行把标量平方改写成“行—矩阵—列”的乘积。系数固定，因此可移到期望外；有限二阶矩通过[[期望的Cauchy–Schwarz不等式]]保证这些乘积可积。

## Reading a direction without confusing its scale
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方向与尺度要分开*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The expression $a^T\Sigma a$ is a [[二次型|quadratic form]]: it gives one number for a chosen coefficient vector. If $a=e_j$, with a one in position $j$ and zeros elsewhere, it selects $\Sigma_{jj}=\operatorname{Var}(X_j)$. Replacing $a$ by $2a$ multiplies the variance by four. Thus comparing directions requires fixing the length of $a$; [[主成分目标|principal component analysis]] uses unit-length directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$a^T\Sigma a$ 是[[二次型]]：选定系数向量后得到一个数。若 $a=e_j$，即第 $j$ 位为一、其余为零，就读出 $\Sigma_{jj}=\operatorname{Var}(X_j)$。把 $a$ 换成 $2a$，方差会乘四。因此比较方向时应固定 $a$ 的长度；[[主成分目标]]使用单位长度方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a unit vector $a$, $a^TX$ is the signed scalar coordinate along that direction. The vector-valued [[正交投影|orthogonal projection]] onto its line is $aa^TX$, a different object. The variance identity itself works for arbitrary fixed weights, whether or not $a$ is a unit vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$a$ 为单位向量时，$a^TX$ 是沿该方向的带符号标量坐标；投到这条直线上的向量[[正交投影]]则为 $aa^TX$，是另一个对象。方差恒等式本身适用于任意固定权重，不要求 $a$ 为单位向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since variance is nonnegative for every fixed $a$, this identity explains [[协方差矩阵半正定性|covariance semidefiniteness]]. A zero value for some nonzero $a$ means that combination is constant almost surely, the condition in [[协方差奇异性|the covariance singularity criterion]]. Random weights require a separate analysis: they cannot be taken outside the expectation by this argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个固定 $a$ 对应的方差都非负，这解释了[[协方差矩阵半正定性]]。某个非零 $a$ 得到零值，则表示该组合几乎必然为常数，正是[[协方差奇异性]]的条件。随机权重需要另行分析，不能照这里的推导移到期望之外。
