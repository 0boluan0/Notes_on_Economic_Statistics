# Standard deviation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*标准差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Standard deviation** describes the spread of a distribution around its mean, in the same units as the variable. It is the square root of [[方差|variance]]. Variance averages squared distances from the mean; taking the square root brings that measure back from squared units to the units we originally measured.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**标准差**描述一个分布在均值周围散开到什么程度，单位与原变量相同。它是[[方差]]的平方根：方差先平均到均值的平方距离，标准差再开平方，把平方后的单位还原成原来的计量单位。

> [!example] Reading a spread in seconds · 用秒理解散布程度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A waiting time $X$ is $2$ seconds or $6$ seconds with equal probability. Its [[期望|mean]] is $4$ seconds. Both possible values are $2$ seconds from the mean, so the average squared distance is $4\,\mathrm{s}^2$. The standard deviation is $\sqrt{4\,\mathrm{s}^2}=2\,\mathrm{s}$. If the values were $3$ and $5$ seconds instead, the same centre would have standard deviation $1$ second.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 等待时间 $X$ 以相同概率取 $2$ 秒或 $6$ 秒，[[期望|均值]]为 $4$ 秒。两个可能值都离均值 $2$ 秒，因此平均平方距离是 $4\,\mathrm{s}^2$，标准差为 $\sqrt{4\,\mathrm{s}^2}=2\,\mathrm{s}$。如果取值改成 $3$ 秒和 $5$ 秒，中心仍相同，但标准差只有 $1$ 秒。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Square, average, then take the square root
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*平方、平均、再开平方*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\mu=E[X]$ be the mean and assume $E[X^2]<\infty$, so the second moment and variance are finite. Write $\sigma_X$ (the Greek letter sigma, with an $X$ subscript) for the standard deviation:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $\mu=E[X]$ 为均值，并假设 $E[X^2]<\infty$，从而二阶矩与方差有限。用 $\sigma_X$（希腊字母 sigma，带下标 $X$）表示标准差：

$$\sigma_X=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)}=\sqrt{E[(X-\mu)^2]}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The order matters. Subtract the mean from each value, square that difference, take its probability-weighted average, and finally take the square root. For a discrete variable the average is a weighted sum; for a variable with a density it is an integral. The [[方差|variance card]] explains both computations. Standard deviation is nonnegative, and it is zero exactly when $X$ equals its mean with probability one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

运算顺序很重要：每个取值先减去均值，再把差平方，按概率加权平均，最后开平方。离散变量用加权求和，有密度的变量用积分；[[方差]]卡片解释了这两种计算。标准差非负，而且恰好在 $X$ 以概率一等于均值时为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a root-mean-square distance, not generally the mean absolute distance $E[|X-\mu|]$. For $X=0$ with probability $3/4$ and $X=2$ with probability $1/4$, the mean is $1/2$. The average absolute distance is $3/4$, while the variance is $3/4$ and the standard deviation is $\sqrt{3}/2\approx0.866$. Squaring gives more influence to larger distances before the final square root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是距离的均方根，一般不同于平均绝对距离 $E[|X-\mu|]$。例如，$X$ 以 $3/4$ 的概率取 $0$，以 $1/4$ 的概率取 $2$，均值为 $1/2$。平均绝对距离是 $3/4$，方差也是 $3/4$，但标准差为 $\sqrt{3}/2\approx0.866$。平方会在最后开平方之前，给较大的偏离更大影响。

## How to use the scale
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样使用这个尺度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding a fixed amount shifts the values and their mean together, leaving standard deviation unchanged. Multiplying by a fixed number $a$ multiplies distances by $|a|$, so $\sigma_{aX+b}=|a|\sigma_X$ for fixed $a,b$. Thus a standard deviation of $2$ seconds becomes $2000$ milliseconds when the unit changes. The [[方差的仿射变换|affine transformation rule]] explains why variance instead gets the squared multiplier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

加上一个固定数，会同时移动取值与均值，因此不改变标准差。乘以固定数 $a$，会把距离变成 $|a|$ 倍，所以对固定的 $a,b$，有 $\sigma_{aX+b}=|a|\sigma_X$。例如，标准差 $2$ 秒换成毫秒就是 $2000$ 毫秒。[[方差的仿射变换]]解释了为什么方差使用的却是乘数的平方。

> [!warning] A scale does not determine a whole distribution · 尺度不能决定整个分布
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The mean and standard deviation do not tell us every interval probability. The familiar approximately $68\%$ within one standard deviation is a [[正态分布|normal-distribution]] property, not a universal rule. In the equally likely $2$-or-$6$ example, both values lie within the inclusive interval $\mu\pm\sigma_X=[2,6]$, so that interval has probability $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 均值与标准差不能告诉我们每一个区间的概率。“约 $68\%$ 落在一个标准差以内”是[[正态分布]]的性质，不是通用规则。在等概率取 $2$ 或 $6$ 的例子中，两个值都在包含端点的 $\mu\pm\sigma_X=[2,6]$ 内，因此这个区间的概率为 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card defines the spread of a probability distribution. A standard deviation calculated from observed data estimates or summarises spread using those data. A [[标准误含义|standard error]] refers instead to the sampling spread of a statistic, such as an estimated mean; it is not interchangeable with the spread of individual observations. If the required second moment is not finite, check [[矩存在性的使用边界|moment existence]] before reporting a finite standard deviation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡定义的是概率分布的散布。用观测数据算出的标准差，则是在用这些数据估计或概括散布。[[标准误含义|标准误]]指的是统计量（例如估计均值）在重复抽样中的散布，不能与个体观测值的散布互换。若所需二阶矩不有限，应先检查[[矩存在性的使用边界]]，再判断是否能报告有限标准差。
