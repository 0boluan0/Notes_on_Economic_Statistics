# 交换价值是商品价值表现为另一种商品数量的交换比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Exchange-value expresses a commodity's value as a quantitative relation to another commodity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**交换价值（exchange-value）**首先表现为不同种类商品相互交换的数量比例。在 Marx 的分析中，它是[[Marx商品价值|价值]]的表现形式：一种商品通过另一种商品的数量表达自身价值。价值回答被表现的内容是什么，交换价值回答它怎样在商品关系中表现出来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Exchange-value** first appears as the quantitative proportion in which commodities of different kinds exchange. In Marx's analysis it is the form in which [[Marx商品价值|value]] appears: one commodity expresses its value through a quantity of another. Value concerns the content expressed; exchange-value concerns its expression in a relation between commodities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原文的简单表达是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The text's elementary expression is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
20\text{ yards of linen}=1\text{ coat}.
$$

在这个方向上，20 码麻布的价值用一件外套表达：麻布处于相对价值形式，外套充当等价物。把等式倒转，才是在用麻布表达外套的价值。两个商品在这里具有同等价值，并不表示它们有相同的[[Marx使用价值|用途]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In this direction, twenty yards of linen express their value in one coat: linen occupies the relative form and the coat serves as equivalent. Reversing the expression instead expresses the coat's value in linen. Equal value in this relation does not imply identical [[Marx使用价值|uses]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一商品可以用多种其他商品表达价值，所以它并非只有一个孤立的交换比率。这也说明为何商品的两个因素在进一步分析中称为“使用价值与价值”：交换价值是后者取得表达的形式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same commodity can express its value in various other commodities, so it does not have just one isolated exchange ratio. This explains why the two factors are subsequently named use-value and value: exchange-value is the form in which the latter is expressed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
