# 货银对付是把证券最终交付与对应付款的最终完成互为条件的结算机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Delivery versus payment makes final delivery of securities conditional on final payment, and vice versa*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

货银对付（delivery versus payment，DvP）把证券转移和对应资金转移联结起来：证券交付最终完成，当且仅当对应付款也最终完成。其核心是两项义务最终性的条件联结，避免一方最终交出本金价值却未收到对价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Delivery versus payment links the securities and cash transfers: delivery becomes final if and only if the corresponding payment becomes final. The defining feature is conditional finality, preventing one party from finally surrendering principal value without receiving the consideration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，买方应付 1,000 元、卖方应交 50 股。只有收到可撤销的付款指令便不可撤销地交券，不构成充分的 DvP 保护；系统必须确保最终收款和最终交券的联结。具体处理可以先锁定证券、确认最终付款，再释放证券，但锁定资产的法律与操作保护必须有效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a buyer owes 1,000 and a seller owes 50 shares. Irrevocably delivering the shares merely upon receipt of a revocable payment instruction is not sufficient DvP protection. The system must link final payment and final delivery. An arrangement may block securities, confirm final payment, then release them, provided the legal and operational protection of the blocked assets is effective.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

DvP 不要求两条指令在同一个时钟瞬间处理，也不限定必须逐笔全额结算。总额、净额及不同时间安排都可能实现这种联结。它消除的特定风险及仍须管理的风险，见[[货银对付的风险边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DvP does not require both instructions to be processed at an identical clock instant, nor does it require gross settlement. Gross, net, and different timing arrangements can implement the linkage. Its protection and remaining risks are separated in [[货银对付的风险边界|the risk boundary of DvP]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
