# 刺穿形态是下降后阳线收盘越过前阴线实体中点的两线组合

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A piercing pattern is a two-candle formation after a decline whose second, upward candle closes above the first downward body's midpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

刺穿形态是下降走势后的两根[[K线]]组合：先有较长阴线，随后向下跳空开盘，再以阳线收盘明显进入前阴线实体并越过其中点。本课经典口径要求第二期开盘低于第一期最低价；若第二根只回到前收盘附近或尚未越过实体中点，就不满足这个刺穿条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A piercing pattern follows a decline: a long down [[K线|candle]] is followed by a gap-down opening and an up candle closing well inside the first body, above its midpoint. The course's classical version opens below the first period's low. A recovery merely to the previous close or below the body midpoint does not meet this criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令第一根 $O_1>C_1$，实体中点为 $(O_1+C_1)/2$。例如 $O_1=110,C_1=100,L_1=99$，第二根 $O_2=98,C_2=106$，有 $C_2>105$，而 $C_2<110$，是穿入而未吞没整个前实体。若第二实体进一步完整覆盖前实体，应按[[吞没形态]]区别识别；镜像的上涨后组合是[[乌云盖顶]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $O_1>C_1$, the first body midpoint is $(O_1+C_1)/2$. With $O_1=110,C_1=100,L_1=99$ and $O_2=98,C_2=106$, the second close exceeds 105 but remains below 110: it penetrates without engulfing the whole body. Full containment calls for comparison with [[吞没形态|engulfing]]. The counterpart after an advance is [[乌云盖顶|dark cloud cover]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二根收盘才确定是否刺穿。K线学派把它当作潜在止跌线索，但中点越过是结构条件，不是随后上涨的充分条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Penetration is determined at the second close. The charting school treats it as a possible bottoming clue, but crossing the midpoint is a structural condition, not sufficient evidence of a subsequent advance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
