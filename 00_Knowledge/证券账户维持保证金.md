# 证券账户维持保证金是持仓期间必须持续满足的最低账户权益要求
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Maintenance margin is the minimum account equity required while securities positions remain open*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

维持保证金（maintenance margin）是证券融资账户在持仓期间，按适用规则及券商要求持续保有的最低权益。它按当前头寸和估值检验，不是只在开仓时检查的[[证券账户初始保证金]]，也不是某次市值损失的另一个名称。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Maintenance margin is the minimum equity that a securities margin account must retain while positions are open under applicable rules and broker requirements. It is tested against current positions and valuations, unlike [[证券账户初始保证金|initial margin]], and is not another name for a mark-to-market loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在仅持有一类融资买入证券、无其他资产负债的简化账户中，证券市值为 $V>0$、融资债务为 $D$，权益 $E=V-D$。若维持比例按该证券市值计为 $m$，合格条件是 $E\ge mV$。例如 $V=12,000,D=8,000,E=4,000$：25% 要求为 3,000 元，能够满足；40% 要求为 4,800 元，尚差 800 元。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a simplified account holding only one class of financed long securities, let market value be $V>0$, debt $D$, and equity $E=V-D$. For a maintenance fraction $m$ of that securities value, compliance requires $E\ge mV$. If $V=12,000,D=8,000,E=4,000$, a 25% requirement is 3,000 and is met; a 40% requirement is 4,800, leaving a shortfall of 800.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价格变化、利息累积和要求上调都可能使账户不再合格。处置可以涉及追加现金、提交合格证券或平仓，但实际计量须核对现金处理、折算率与账户规则，不能把某一个多头股票比率套给短仓、期权或组合保证金账户。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price changes, accrued interest, or higher requirements can remove compliance. Responses may involve cash, eligible securities, or liquidation, but valuation depends on cash treatment, haircuts, and account rules. A long-stock ratio cannot be imposed on short positions, options, or portfolio-margin accounts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

低于维持要求不等于总权益已经为零，也不保证券商一定先通知并等待补款。美国 SEC 所述保证金协议可能允许不事先咨询便卖出资产；具体通知、时限和选择权须按适用合同与规则判断。股价跳跃亦可能使平仓后的权益为负，维持要求不是损失上限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A maintenance breach does not require zero equity, nor does it guarantee notice and time to deposit funds. US margin agreements described by the SEC may permit sales without prior consultation; actual notice, deadlines, and choices depend on the rules and agreement. Price jumps may leave negative equity after liquidation, so maintenance margin is not a loss cap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
