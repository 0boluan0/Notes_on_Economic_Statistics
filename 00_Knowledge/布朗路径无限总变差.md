# Infinite total variation of Brownian paths
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*布朗路径无限总变差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A path may stay within a finite range while moving up and down so often that its accumulated absolute movement has no finite bound. This happens to [[标准布朗运动|standard Brownian motion]] on every positive-length finite time interval, with probability one.

The [[函数总变差|total variation]] of a path measures absolute movement. Observe it at ordered times and add the magnitudes of the successive changes; then allow every possible finite choice of observation times. It differs from the net change between the first and last values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条路径可以始终处于有限的高度范围，却因为上下往返太多，使累积的绝对变化量没有有限上界。[[标准布朗运动]]以概率一在每个正长度的有限时间区间上都具有这种性质。

路径的[[函数总变差|总变差]]衡量绝对变化量：按时间顺序观测，把相邻读数变化的绝对值相加，再允许所有可能的有限观测时刻选择。它与首尾读数的净变化不同。

> [!example] Net change and accumulated movement · 净变化与累积变化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Readings $0,1,0,2$ give net change $2-0=2$, but the absolute changes add to $1+1+2=4$. More observations may reveal additional backtracking. For a fixed continuous path, every finite set of readings gives a finite sum. Infinite total variation means that **no single finite number bounds all these sums**.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 读数 $0,1,0,2$ 的净变化为 $2-0=2$，绝对变化之和却是 $1+1+2=4$。更多观测可能揭示额外的往返。对于一条固定连续路径，每组有限读数的和都有限；总变差无穷表示**不存在一个有限数能统一约束所有这些和**。

> [!important] Every nontrivial interval · 每个非退化区间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For standard Brownian motion $W$,
> $$P\!\left(\forall\,0\le a<b<\infty:\ V_a^b(W)=\infty\right)=1,$$
> where
> $$V_a^b(W)=\sup_{\pi}\sum_{i=1}^n|W_{t_i}-W_{t_{i-1}}|,\qquad
> \pi:\ a=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_n=b.$$
> The supremum ranges over all finite partitions $\pi$, with any positive integer $n$. The endpoints must satisfy $a<b$: on a one-point interval the variation is zero. This is one probability-one event for all intervals, not a new exceptional set chosen separately for each interval.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上式的上确界取遍所有有限分割 $\pi$，分段数 $n$ 可以是任意正整数。必须有 $a<b$：单点区间的变差为零。结论要求同一个概率一事件同时覆盖全部区间，而不是对每个区间各选一个例外集。

## A direct argument using quadratic variation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Brownian 专属的二次变差论证

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix $0\le a<b<\infty$. Divide this interval into $2^n$ equal pieces, with endpoints $t_{n,i}=a+i(b-a)/2^n$, and write $\Delta W_{n,i}=W_{t_{n,i}}-W_{t_{n,i-1}}$. These are deterministic dyadic partitions: each step refines the previous one.

The [[布朗二次变差|quadratic-variation theorem]] says, on this sequence,
$$Q_n=\sum_{i=1}^{2^n}(\Delta W_{n,i})^2\longrightarrow b-a
\quad\text{almost surely}.$$
Here we add **squares** along a specified sequence of partitions; we do not take the supremum over every partition. The same result applies after time $a$ because $W_{a+t}-W_a$ has the standard Brownian increment law. The dyadic mesh is summable, so the theorem supplies the almost-sure conclusion required here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $0\le a<b<\infty$，把它等分为 $2^n$ 段，端点为 $t_{n,i}=a+i(b-a)/2^n$，记 $\Delta W_{n,i}=W_{t_{n,i}}-W_{t_{n,i-1}}$。这是确定的二进分割，每次都细化上一层。

[[布朗二次变差]]定理说明，沿这列分割，增量**平方**之和 $Q_n$ 几乎必然趋于 $b-a$。这里沿指定分割序列求极限，没有对所有分割取上确界。由于 $W_{a+t}-W_a$ 具有标准布朗增量律，该结论也适用于从 $a$ 开始的区间。二进网格的大小可求和，因此满足此处所需的几乎必然收敛条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a path on which continuity and this limit both hold. [[连续函数在紧致集上一致连续|Continuity on a closed bounded interval gives uniform continuity]]: a sufficiently short time gap makes every corresponding change in value small, wherever that gap lies. Thus
$$m_n=\max_i|\Delta W_{n,i}|\longrightarrow0.$$
If this path had finite total variation $V=V_a^b(W)$, each partition would obey
$$0\le Q_n
\le m_n\sum_i|\Delta W_{n,i}|
\le m_nV\longrightarrow0.$$
But we already know $Q_n\to b-a>0$. This contradiction proves infinite total variation on the fixed interval. In fact, on these paths the dyadic absolute-increment sums themselves tend to infinity, because they are at least $Q_n/m_n$ once $Q_n>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选定一条同时满足连续性和上述极限的路径。[[连续函数在紧致集上一致连续|闭有界区间上的连续函数一致连续]]：只要时间间隔足够短，无论它位于哪里，对应的值变化都会小。因此最大增量 $m_n=\max_i|\Delta W_{n,i}|$ 趋于零。

假如这条路径的总变差 $V$ 有限，每个分割的平方和就满足上面的不等式，被 $m_nV$ 压到零。但已知其极限是 $b-a>0$，产生矛盾，所以固定区间上的总变差必须无穷。事实上，在这些路径上，二进分割的绝对增量和本身也趋于无穷：当 $Q_n>0$ 时，它至少为 $Q_n/m_n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To obtain the simultaneous statement, first apply this argument to every interval with rational endpoints. There are only countably many, so intersecting their probability-one events still gives probability one. Every interval of positive length contains such a rational subinterval. Its total variation cannot be smaller than the variation of that subinterval, hence it is infinite too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了得到同时覆盖全部区间的结论，先对所有有理端点区间使用上述论证。这些区间只有可数个，所以对应概率一事件的交仍为概率一。每个正长度区间都包含一个有理端点子区间，而整个区间的总变差不会小于子区间的总变差，因而也为无穷。

## Relation to differentiability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与处处不可导的逻辑关系

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[布朗路径处处不可导|Nowhere differentiability]] and infinite total variation describe different features. The first concerns the local difference quotient at each point; the second concerns absolute changes accumulated over an interval. Infinite variation alone does not rule out differentiability, even at every point; the example attached to [[有界变差函数几乎处处可导|the bounded-variation differentiability theorem]] shows why the converse fails.

In the other direction, a nowhere differentiable function on a positive-length interval cannot have bounded variation, because that real-analysis theorem would make it differentiable outside a set of time points of length zero. That is another route to the Brownian conclusion. The quadratic-variation argument above establishes it directly from Brownian increments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[布朗路径处处不可导]]与总变差无穷描述不同特征：前者考察每一点附近的差商，后者考察一个区间上累积的绝对变化。无穷总变差本身不排除可导，甚至不排除每一点都可导；[[有界变差函数几乎处处可导]]中的反例说明为什么不能逆推。

反过来，在正长度区间上处处不可导的函数不可能有界变差，否则那条实分析定理会使它除了一个长度为零的时间点集合以外都可导。这是得到布朗结论的另一条路线；上面的二次变差证明则直接使用布朗增量。

> [!tip] What continuity still guarantees · 连续性仍保证什么
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On each compact interval, a continuous Brownian path is bounded and its largest increment on a fine grid tends to zero. Neither fact bounds the **sum** of all absolute increments: the number of terms grows as the grid is refined. Many small changes can accumulate without a finite upper bound.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在每个紧区间上，连续布朗路径有界，细网格中的最大增量也趋于零。但这两点都没有限制全部绝对增量的**和**，因为细化网格时项数也在增加。许多小变化仍可能累积到没有有限上界。
