# Union bound
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*并事件上界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **union bound** limits the probability that at least one of several events occurs. It adds the individual event probabilities, even when we do not know how the events depend on one another. The sum can count an outcome more than once, so it is an upper bound rather than generally an exact answer. The same result is also called **Boole's inequality**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**并事件上界**限制“若干事件中至少一个发生”的概率：即使不知道事件之间如何依赖，也可以先把各自概率相加。同一个结果可能被重复计算，因此通常得到的是上界，而不是准确答案。这条结果也叫 **Boole 不等式**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An event is a set of outcomes in a [[概率空间|probability space]]. Write $A_i$ for event number $i$, and $\bigcup_{i=1}^n A_i$ for their union: the event that at least one of the $n$ events occurs. Multiple events may occur together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

事件是[[概率空间]]中的一组结果。用 $A_i$ 表示第 $i$ 个事件，$\bigcup_{i=1}^n A_i$ 表示它们的并集，也就是 $n$ 个事件中至少一个发生；多个事件可以同时发生。

> [!example] At least one failed check · 至少一项检查失败
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose a system has three checks. Let $A_1,A_2,A_3$ be their failure events, with probabilities $0.02,0.03,0.01$. We do not need to know whether one fault can make several checks fail. The probability of any failure is at most
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设系统有三项检查，用 $A_1,A_2,A_3$ 表示各项失败事件，概率分别为 $0.02,0.03,0.01$。无需知道同一个故障会不会使多项检查一起失败，就能得到至少一项失败的概率至多为
>
> $$P(A_1\cup A_2\cup A_3)\le0.02+0.03+0.01=0.06.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus the model guarantees at least $94\%$ probability that none fails. It does not say the failure probability is exactly $6\%$. Overlap may make it smaller.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因此，这个模型保证三项全不失败的概率至少为 $94\%$；并不表示失败概率恰好是 $6\%$。事件重叠时，实际值可以更小。

> [!abstract] The finite union bound · 有限个事件的并界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For any events $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ in the same probability space,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对同一概率空间中的任意事件 $A_1,\ldots,A_n$，
>
> $$P\!\left(\bigcup_{i=1}^n A_i\right)\le\sum_{i=1}^nP(A_i).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> There is no independence or disjointness requirement. Since a probability is at most one, $\min\{1,\sum_iP(A_i)\}$ is the usable bound. The sum need not itself be a probability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 既不要求独立，也不要求互斥。由于概率不超过一，实际可用的界是 $\min\{1,\sum_iP(A_i)\}$；右侧的和本身不一定是某个事件的概率。

## Overcounting explains the inequality
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*重复计算解释了不等号*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two events, the [[互斥事件加法|general addition formula]] subtracts the overlap once:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个事件时，[[互斥事件加法|一般加法公式]]要减去一次重叠：

$$P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)\le P(A)+P(B).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The intersection $A\cap B$ is where both occur, and its probability cannot be negative. If $A=B$ and $P(A)=0.1$, adding the two probabilities gives $0.2$ while the union probability remains $0.1$. If the two events are disjoint, there is no overlap and equality holds; more generally, zero probability of overlap suffices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交集 $A\cap B$ 表示两者同时发生，它的概率不可能为负。如果 $A=B$ 且 $P(A)=0.1$，相加得到 $0.2$，并集概率却仍是 $0.1$。若两事件互斥，没有重叠，就取等号；更一般地，只要重叠的概率为零也足够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any finite number of events, [[事件指示变量|indicators]] make the same reasoning explicit. The union indicator records only whether any event occurred, while the sum counts how many occurred. Therefore, for every outcome,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意有限个事件，[[事件指示变量]]把同一个道理写得更清楚：并集的指示变量只记录有没有事件发生，右侧的和则记录发生了几个。因此，对每个结果都有

$$\mathbf1_{\bigcup_{i=1}^n A_i}\le\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbf1_{A_i}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take expectations. [[期望线性性|Linearity]] separates the finite sum without any independence assumption, and [[指示变量期望|each indicator mean is its event probability]]. This proves the finite bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两边取期望：[[期望线性性]]无需独立性就能拆开有限和，再由[[指示变量期望|指示变量期望等于事件概率]]，得到有限并界。

## Countably many events and the limit of the claim
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*可数多个事件及结论范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bound also holds for a sequence $A_1,A_2,\ldots$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对可以编号成序列的事件 $A_1,A_2,\ldots$，同样成立：

$$P\!\left(\bigcup_{i=1}^{\infty}A_i\right)\le\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}P(A_i).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To justify the step, let $B_n=A_1\cup\cdots\cup A_n$. These events grow as $n$ increases. Their probabilities tend to the probability of their full union, a consequence of the countable-additivity [[概率三公理|probability axiom]] called continuity from below. Apply the finite bound to $B_n$, then take the limit. The nonnegative series on the right may be infinite; the inequality still holds but then gives no useful improvement over one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为说明这个推广，令 $B_n=A_1\cup\cdots\cup A_n$。$n$ 增大时，事件只会扩张；它们的概率趋于完整并集的概率。这是[[概率三公理|概率公理]]中可数可加性的推论，叫作从下连续性。先对 $B_n$ 用有限并界，再取极限即可。右侧非负级数可以发散到无穷，此时不等式仍成立，但没有比概率不超过一更有用的信息。

> [!warning] Countable is a real restriction · 可数是实质条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Do not extend the sum rule to arbitrary uncountable families. Under a [[连续均匀分布|uniform distribution]] on $[0,1]$, every singleton event has probability zero, but the union of all those singletons is the whole interval and has probability one. There are uncountably many singletons, so this does not contradict the countable union bound.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不能把这个求和规则直接扩展到任意不可数事件族。在 $[0,1]$ 上的[[连续均匀分布]]中，每个单点事件的概率都是零，但所有单点的并集是整个区间，概率为一。这些单点有不可数多个，因此不违背可数并界。
