# 任意有限或可数个事件的并概率不超过各事件概率之和

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A union probability is bounded by the sum of its event probabilities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意有限或可数事件族，不要求独立，
$$P\!\left(\bigcup_i A_i\right)\le\sum_iP(A_i).$$
右边可能超过 1，此时只是一个较松的上界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The union bound applies to any finite or countable family of events without independence. A sum above one is simply a loose upper bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
两个事件时，$P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)\le P(A)+P(B)$。有限项由归纳得到；可数项对前 $n$ 项并取极限，使用概率的从下连续性。它可以给“至少一个坏事件发生”的风险设上界，即使事件之间依赖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtracting the nonnegative overlap proves the two-event case. Induction gives finite unions, and continuity from below extends the result to countable unions. It bounds the chance of at least one adverse event even under dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
