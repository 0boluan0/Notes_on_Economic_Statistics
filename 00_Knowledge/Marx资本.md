# Marx 所说的资本是在运动中保存并增殖自身的价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For Marx, capital is value that preserves and expands itself through its movement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Marx 的分析中，**资本（capital）**是处于不断运动、以自身增殖为目的的[[Marx商品价值|价值]]。货币和商品是它在运动中采取的形式；把货币预付出去、经过商品形式后收回更多货币，是资本在流通领域呈现的一般形式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Marx's analysis, **capital** is [[Marx商品价值|value]] in continuing movement directed towards its own expansion. Money and commodities are forms it assumes along the way. Advancing money, passing through the commodity form and recovering more money gives the general form in which capital appears in circulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本总公式写作
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The general formula for capital is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
M-C-M',\qquad M'=M+\Delta M.
$$

其中 $M$ 是预付货币，$C$ 是商品，$M'$ 是回流货币，$\Delta M$ 是超过原预付额的增量。例如预付100、回流110，则增量为10。公式把要解释的增殖呈现出来；仅写出100变成110，还没有说明[[等价交换与剩余|这个增量怎样产生]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $M$ is money advanced, $C$ the commodity form, $M'$ returning money and $\Delta M$ the increment over the advance. If 100 returns as 110, the increment is 10. The formula displays the expansion to be explained; writing the change from 100 to 110 does not yet explain [[等价交换与剩余|its source]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通商品流通 $C-M-C$ 的目的，是卖出一种商品以取得另一种[[Marx使用价值|使用价值]]，例如卖粮后买衣。资本运动的起点和终点都是货币，区别在数量；回流的货币又可成为下一次运动的起点。个别经营可能失败或亏损，一般公式表达的是增殖的目的及其完成形式，并不保证每次预付成功。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Simple commodity circulation, $C-M-C$, sells one commodity to obtain another [[Marx使用价值|use-value]], as when grain is sold to buy clothes. Capital begins and ends in money, with a difference in quantity; the returning money can begin another movement. Individual operations may fail or lose money. The general formula expresses the aim and completed form of expansion, not a guarantee that every advance succeeds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
