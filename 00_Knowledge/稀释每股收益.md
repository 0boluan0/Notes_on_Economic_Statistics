# 稀释每股收益是在基本每股收益口径上计入稀释性潜在普通股影响后的每股损益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diluted EPS adjusts basic EPS for the effects of dilutive potential ordinary shares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

稀释每股收益是在[[基本每股收益]]的归属口径上，假设适用的潜在普通股转为普通股，同时调整利润和加权股数后的会计指标。它不是把所有可能发行的股份不加判断地塞进分母，也不是预测实际转换概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diluted EPS preserves the attribution basis of [[基本每股收益|basic EPS]] while adjusting both profit and weighted shares for assumed conversion of applicable potential ordinary shares. It neither indiscriminately includes all possible shares nor forecasts the probability of actual conversion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$EPS_d=\frac{NI_c+\Delta NI_c}{\bar N+\Delta\bar N}.$$

例如全年在外的[[可转换债券]]若假设转股，可新增加权普通股 20 万股，并节省已计入损益的税后利息 6 万元，且无其他连带损益。原普通股利润 120 万元、加权股数 100 万股，基本 EPS 为 1.20；假设转换后为 $126/120=1.05$ 元／股。此简例全部盈利来自持续经营，转换确有稀释作用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a [[可转换债券|convertible bond]] outstanding all year would add 200,000 weighted shares and eliminate 60,000 of recognized after-tax interest, with no other consequential earnings changes. Original ordinary profit of 1.2 million and 1 million weighted shares give basic EPS of 1.20; assumed conversion gives 1.05. With all earnings from continuing operations in this example, the conversion is dilutive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

IAS 33 以归属于母公司普通股股东的持续经营损益判断稀释性：减少每股盈利或增加每股亏损才纳入；反稀释工具排除。不同工具要逐项并按稀释程度检验；转股债的利息调整、期权的净增股份等规则不同，不能只套“原利润／更多股数”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
IAS 33 tests dilution using continuing-operations profit or loss attributable to ordinary equity holders of the parent: an instrument is included when it lowers EPS or increases loss per share; antidilutive instruments are excluded. Issues are tested separately in dilution order. Convertible-debt interest adjustments and incremental option shares follow different rules, so unchanged profit divided by more shares is not a general method.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
