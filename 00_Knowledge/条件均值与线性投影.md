# 线性条件均值与最佳线性投影不是同一对象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A linear conditional mean and a best linear projection are not the same object*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心区别
> 条件均值 $m(X)=E(Y\mid X)$ 是给定 $X$ 后 $Y$ 的真实总体平均；最佳线性投影则选择
> $$
> \beta^*=\arg\min_b E[(Y-X'b)^2].
> $$
> 只有当 $m(X)$ 本身落在 $X$ 张成的线性函数空间内时，$X'\beta^*$ 才等于整个条件均值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The conditional mean $m(X)=E(Y\mid X)$ is the true population average of $Y$ given $X$. The best linear projection instead chooses $\beta^*=\arg\min_b E[(Y-X'b)^2]$. Only when $m(X)$ lies in the linear function space spanned by $X$ does $X'\beta^*$ equal the full conditional mean.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用条件与含义边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Use condition and meaning boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使 $m(X)$ 非线性，只要 $E(Y^2)<\infty$、$E(XX')$ 存在且正定，最佳线性投影的系数仍然存在且唯一。此时系数描述的是“在当前 $X$ 分布下最接近条件均值的线性近似”；更换 $X$ 的分布，投影系数也可能改变。把这个投影进一步解释成结构参数或因果效应，还需要独立的识别假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even when $m(X)$ is nonlinear, the best-linear-projection coefficients exist and are unique when $E(Y^2)<\infty$ and $E(XX')$ exists and is positive definite. They describe the linear approximation closest to the conditional mean under the current distribution of $X$; changing that distribution can change the projection coefficients. Interpreting the projection as a structural parameter or causal effect requires separate identification assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小对照
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal contrast*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $E(Y\mid X)=X^2$，且模型只放截距与 $X$，对称分布下 $X$ 的线性投影斜率可以为 $0$；若样本主要落在 $X>0$，最佳直线的斜率会变成正数。真实条件均值没有改变，改变的是用一条直线逼近它时所依赖的 $X$ 分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $E(Y\mid X)=X^2$ and the fitted model contains only an intercept and $X$, the projection slope can be zero under a symmetric distribution of $X$. If observations lie mainly at $X>0$, the best-fitting line has a positive slope. The conditional mean has not changed; the distribution used to approximate it by a line has.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 条件均值是非线性的，是否意味着 OLS 没有总体目标？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the conditional mean is nonlinear, does OLS lack a population target?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不意味着。它仍可估计最佳线性投影，但该投影不等于完整条件均值，而且系数依赖 $X$ 的分布。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. OLS can still estimate the best linear projection, but that projection is not the full conditional mean and its coefficients depend on the distribution of $X$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
