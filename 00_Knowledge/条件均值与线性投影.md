# Conditional mean and best linear prediction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*条件均值与最佳线性投影*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[条件期望|conditional mean]] asks for the average outcome at each observed value of $X$. A **best linear predictor** asks for the single straight line that gives the smallest average squared error across the population. The first may trace a curve; the second must stay within the chosen class of lines. Distinguishing the two tells us what a regression is trying to approximate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[条件期望|条件均值]]问的是：在每个给定的 $X$ 值下，结果平均是多少。**最佳线性预测**问的是：哪一条直线能让整个总体上的平方误差平均最小。前者可以是一条曲线，后者必须留在选定的直线范围内。区分两者，才能知道回归究竟在逼近什么。

> [!example] A curve and its best straight line · 曲线与最佳直线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X=-1,0,1$ with equal probabilities and let $Y=X^2$. The conditional mean is $m(X)=E[Y\mid X]=X^2$, giving $1,0,1$ and no prediction error. A line $a+bX$ cannot match all three values. Its best choice is $a=2/3$, $b=0$, with mean squared error $2/9$. A zero slope here does not mean that knowing $X$ is useless: it determines $Y$ exactly.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $X$ 等概率取 $-1,0,1$，且 $Y=X^2$。条件均值为 $m(X)=E[Y\mid X]=X^2$，对应 $1,0,1$，预测没有误差。直线 $a+bX$ 无法同时通过这三点；最佳选择为 $a=2/3$、$b=0$，均方误差 $2/9$。这里斜率为零不表示 $X$ 没有信息：知道 $X$ 就完全知道 $Y$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two prediction classes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两种允许的预测范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume $E[Y^2]<\infty$. The [[条件期望投影|conditional-mean projection theorem]] says that $m(X)$ minimises $E[(Y-g(X))^2]$ over square-integrable measurable functions $g(X)$: predictions that use only $X$, with any permitted shape. Restricting the choices to $g(X)=a+bX$ gives the best affine predictor, conventionally called linear regression with an intercept. Here $a$ is the intercept and $b$ is the slope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $E[Y^2]<\infty$。[[条件期望投影|条件均值投影定理]]说明，$m(X)$ 在所有平方可积的可测函数 $g(X)$ 中，使 $E[(Y-g(X))^2]$ 最小：这些预测只使用 $X$，但不限定形状。若把候选限制为 $g(X)=a+bX$，就得到最佳仿射预测，通常称作带截距的线性回归；$a$ 是截距，$b$ 是斜率。

$$
(a^*,b^*)=\arg\min_{a,b}E[(Y-a-bX)^2].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $E[X^2]<\infty$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X)>0$, the unique coefficients are $b^*=\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)/\operatorname{Var}(X)$ and $a^*=E[Y]-b^*E[X]$. [[协方差|Covariance]] measures the average centred product, while [[方差|variance]] measures the spread of $X$. In the example, these are $0$ and $2/3$, so the slope is zero and the intercept is $2/3$. The errors are $1/3,-2/3,1/3$; averaging their squares gives $2/9$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $E[X^2]<\infty$ 且 $\operatorname{Var}(X)>0$ 时，唯一系数为 $b^*=\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)/\operatorname{Var}(X)$、$a^*=E[Y]-b^*E[X]$。[[协方差]]是中心化乘积的平均，[[方差]]衡量 $X$ 的波动。本例两者分别为 $0$、$2/3$，所以斜率为零、截距为 $2/3$。三处误差为 $1/3,-2/3,1/3$，平方平均即为 $2/9$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why does fitting $Y$ by a line also approximate its conditional mean? The error identity is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么用直线预测 $Y$，也等价于用直线逼近条件均值？因为误差可以分解为

$$
E[(Y-a-bX)^2]=E[(Y-m(X))^2]+E[(m(X)-a-bX)^2].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term does not depend on the line. Minimising total prediction error therefore minimises the second term, the average squared gap between the line and the conditional-mean curve. They coincide exactly when $m(X)=a^*+b^*X$ almost surely on the population’s support; matching outside that support is not required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一项与所选直线无关。因此，让总预测误差最小，等价于让第二项——直线与条件均值曲线之间的平方差平均——最小。二者恰好在 $m(X)=a^*+b^*X$ 在总体支持上几乎处处成立时相同；不要求在总体不会出现的 $X$ 值处也一致。

## 使用条件与含义边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditions and interpretation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For several chosen regressors, collect them into a finite vector $Z=Z(X)$. For example, $Z=(1,X,X^2)'$ includes an intercept and a square term. The prime (also written $\mathsf T$) denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], and $Z'\beta=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2X^2$. “Linear” now means linear in the coefficients $\beta$, not necessarily a straight line in the original observation $X$. Adding $X^2$ lets the example’s conditional mean enter the allowed class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有多个选定的回归量时，将它们放入有限维向量 $Z=Z(X)$。例如 $Z=(1,X,X^2)'$ 包含截距与平方项；撇号（也写作 $\mathsf T$）表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，$Z'\beta=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2X^2$。这里“线性”指对系数 $\beta$ 线性，不一定是原始观测 $X$ 的直线。加入 $X^2$ 后，上例条件均值就进入了允许的函数范围。

$$
\beta^*\in\arg\min_{b\in\mathbb R^k}E[(Y-Z^{\mathsf T}b)^2],\qquad E[Z(Y-Z^{\mathsf T}\beta^*)]=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $k$ is the number of entries in $Z$. If $Y$ and every entry of $Z$ have finite second moments and $Q=E[ZZ']$ is [[正定矩阵|positive definite]], the coefficients exist uniquely. In this setting positive definiteness means that no nonzero coefficient combination of the regressors is zero almost surely: there is no exact redundancy. If $Q$ is singular, the best predicted random variable still exists and is unique almost surely, but different coefficient vectors can represent it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $k$ 是 $Z$ 的分量个数。若 $Y$ 和每个回归量的二阶矩有限，且 $Q=E[ZZ']$ [[正定矩阵|正定]]，投影系数存在且唯一。这里正定意味着：不存在非零系数组合使回归量的组合几乎处处为零，也就是没有精确冗余。若 $Q$ 奇异，最佳预测随机变量仍然存在且几乎处处唯一，但不同系数向量可能表示同一个预测。

> [!warning] Orthogonality has a limited scope · 正交条件有范围
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A projection residual $e=Y-Z'\beta^*$ satisfies $E[Ze]=0$ for the included regressors. An included intercept gives $E[e]=0$. This does not generally give $E[e\mid X]=0$, which would make the fitted function the full conditional mean. In the three-point example, the constant-line residual is $X^2-2/3$, a nonzero function of $X$. Population orthogonality also does not, by itself, identify a causal effect.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 投影残差 $e=Y-Z'\beta^*$ 与纳入的回归量满足 $E[Ze]=0$；包含截距时还有 $E[e]=0$。这一般不推出 $E[e\mid X]=0$，后者才让拟合函数等于完整条件均值。在三点例子中，常数直线的残差是 $X^2-2/3$，它随 $X$ 改变。总体正交条件本身也不识别因果效应。

## 最小对照
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Changing the population weights*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the same rule $Y=X^2$, but now let $X=0,1,2$ with equal probabilities. Then $E[X]=1$, $E[Y]=5/3$, $\operatorname{Var}(X)=2/3$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=4/3$. The best straight line becomes $-1/3+2X$. The conditional-mean rule is still a square; the best line changes because squared approximation errors are averaged over a different distribution of $X$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保持 $Y=X^2$ 不变，改让 $X$ 等概率取 $0,1,2$。此时 $E[X]=1$、$E[Y]=5/3$、$\operatorname{Var}(X)=2/3$、$\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=4/3$，最佳直线变成 $-1/3+2X$。条件均值规则仍是平方；最佳直线改变，是因为平方逼近误差使用了不同的 $X$ 分布来平均。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are population projections, defined using the true distribution. [[普通最小二乘|Ordinary least squares]] replaces the expectation by a sample average and estimates the coefficients. A nonlinear conditional mean does not remove the population linear-projection target; it changes what that target means. Claims about consistency, structural parameters or causal effects need their own sampling and identification conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些都是由真实分布定义的总体投影。[[普通最小二乘]]用样本平均替代期望，估计相应系数。条件均值非线性不意味着总体线性投影目标消失，而是改变了这一目标的含义。一致性、结构参数或因果效应的解释，仍需要各自的抽样和识别条件。
