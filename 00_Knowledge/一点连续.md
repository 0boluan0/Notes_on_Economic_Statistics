# 函数在一点连续，是指靠近该点的定义域内输入会使函数值任意接近该点值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A function is continuous at a point when nearby inputs in its domain force its values arbitrarily close to the value at that point*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 设 $f:D\to\mathbb R$ 且 $a\in D$。$f$ 在 $a$ 连续，是指对任意 $\varepsilon>0$，都存在 $\delta>0$，使每个 $x\in D$ 只要满足
> $$
> |x-a|<\delta,
> $$
> 就一定满足 $|f(x)-f(a)|<\varepsilon$。若 $a$ 还是 $D$ 的聚点，这一定义等价于
> $$
> \lim_{\substack{x\to a\\x\in D}}f(x)=f(a).
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $f:D\to\mathbb R$ and $a\in D$. Continuity at $a$ means that for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is a $\delta>0$ such that $x\in D$ and $|x-a|<\delta$ imply $|f(x)-f(a)|<\varepsilon$. When $a$ is a cluster point of $D$, this is equivalent to $\lim_{x\to a,\,x\in D}f(x)=f(a)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 内点处的三项检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The three checks at an interior point*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $a$ 是定义域内部点时，上面的定义等价于同时满足：

1. $f(a)$ 有定义；
2. 有限双侧极限 $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)$ 存在；
3. $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=f(a)$。

三项缺一不可。点值存在不等于附近有共同趋势；极限存在也不等于它已经接上点值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an interior point, continuity is equivalent to three simultaneous facts: $f(a)$ is defined, the finite two-sided limit exists, and that limit equals $f(a)$. A point value alone does not create a nearby trend, and an existing limit need not already match the point value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 端点要沿定义域趋近
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Approach endpoints through the domain*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $a$ 是定义域端点，就只比较仍在定义域中的输入。例如 $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ 的定义域是 $[0,\infty)$；它在 $0$ 连续，因为右侧趋近时 $\sqrt{x}\to0=f(0)$，不需要虚构左侧输入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a domain endpoint, approach only through points that remain in the domain. For $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ on $[0,\infty)$, continuity at $0$ uses the right-hand approach; no left-hand input is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 孤立点自动连续
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Continuity is automatic at an isolated domain point*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $a$ 是 $D$ 的孤立点，可以取足够小的 $\delta$，使这个邻域里的定义域点只有 $a$ 自己。此时定义中的条件自动成立。因此，极限等于点值只是在聚点处的等价刻画，不能拿来排除孤立点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $a$ is isolated in $D$, choose $\delta$ small enough that $a$ is the only domain point in the neighbourhood. The continuity condition then holds automatically. Equality of a limit and the point value is therefore an equivalent characterisation only at cluster points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x)=(x^2-1)/(x-1)$，原式在 $x=1$ 没有定义，所以“原函数是否在 $1$ 连续”并不是一个成立的点上性质：连续性只在定义域内讨论。若另行补上 $f(1)=2$，补值后的函数才在 $1$ 连续。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=(x^2-1)/(x-1)$, the original function is undefined at $x=1$, so continuity there is not a property it can have: continuity is defined only at domain points. After defining $f(1)=2$, the extended function is continuous at $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 判断函数在定义域内部点 $a$ 连续，需要同时检查哪三件事？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Which three facts must be checked for continuity at an interior point $a$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 点值有定义、有限双侧极限存在、极限等于点值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The point value is defined, the finite two-sided limit exists, and the limit equals the point value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
