# 预算余额规则是对指定口径的预算盈余或赤字施加持续性数值约束的财政规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A budget balance rule imposes a lasting numerical constraint on a specified measure of fiscal surplus or deficit.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预算余额规则为某种余额设定下限，或等价地为赤字设定上限。它可以约束观测的总体余额、[[初级预算余额]]、[[周期调整预算余额]]或[[结构性预算余额]]；判断是否达标前，必须先读清指标定义、期间与机构范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A budget balance rule sets a floor for a balance or, equivalently, a ceiling for a deficit. It can use the overall, [[初级预算余额|primary]], [[周期调整预算余额|cyclically adjusted]] or [[结构性预算余额|structural]] balance. Compliance depends on the definition, period and institutional coverage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，盈余取正时，“余额不低于 −2”与“赤字不超过 2”表达同一界限；不能再把 −2 当作应达到的最大盈余。采用 GDP 比率时还要说明分母与数据版本，采用跨年平均时也不能用单年观测替代检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With surpluses positive, a balance floor of −2 equals a deficit ceiling of 2. It is not a maximum permissible surplus. Ratios require a specified denominator and data vintage; a multi-year average cannot be tested using a single annual observation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总体余额与融资需求联系较直接，但利息和周期性税收变化不完全受当年政策控制。初级余额去除利息支出的影响；周期或结构性口径进一步调整经济状态，却增加估计和监督难度。[[余额规则与顺周期]]解释为什么这些选择影响衰退中的政策空间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The overall balance connects relatively directly to financing needs, but interest and cyclical revenue are not fully controlled by current policy. Primary balances remove interest expenditure; cyclical or structural measures adjust further but are harder to estimate and monitor. See [[余额规则与顺周期|balance rules and procyclicality]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
