# 历史股票池是在每个过去决策时点按当时可得资料确定的候选证券集合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A point-in-time stock universe is the candidate security set determined from information available at each historical decision time.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

历史股票池为每个决策时点 $t$ 指定一组候选证券 $U_t$：成员资格来自当时可得的上市、指数成分或预定筛选资料。今天仍上市或今天属于某指数，不等于它在过去一直符合条件；后来退出的证券也可能曾是合法候选。研究对象可以固定或动态，但集合的形成方式必须与所声称的历史决策相容。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A point-in-time universe specifies candidate securities $U_t$ at each decision time using then-available listing, index-membership or prespecified screening information. Current listings or constituents need not have qualified throughout the past, and later departures may once have been eligible. A research universe may be fixed or dynamic, but its construction must match the historical decision being claimed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若只保留样本结束时仍存续的证券，就按后来的生存结果筛选过去，产生幸存偏差。正确处理要保留历史入池与出池时间，用持续身份追踪改名、换代码和公司重组，并分别记录“可供选择”“已经持有”“当前可交易”。证券出池不自动让已有持仓消失，停牌也不是按最后报价卖出的机会。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keeping only securities surviving to the end selects past observations by later survival. Preserve entry and exit dates, track securities across ticker changes and reorganisations, and distinguish eligibility, existing holdings and current tradability. Removal from the candidate set does not erase a position, and a halt does not create an opportunity to sell at the last quote.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

退市后的账户结局取决于实际可得的交易、并购对价、清算分配或其他剩余权利。不能统一按零处理，也不能删除记录后重新分配其权重。若终值缺失，应标记缺口并对预先声明的处理作敏感性分析。使用收益数据库时，还要查明退市收益是否已经并入期间收益，避免再次相乘而重复计入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A delisted holding's outcome depends on available trading, merger consideration, liquidation distributions or other remaining rights. Neither blanket zero valuation nor deleting the observation and reallocating its weight is justified. Missing terminal values require explicit treatment and sensitivity analysis. Check whether a return database already incorporates the delisting return before combining it again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小例子：期初两只合格股票 A、B 均为 10 元，分别投入 500 元买入 50 股；期末 A 为 12 元，B 已退市且实际收到每股 2 元清算款，其他收益与费用为零。终值为 $50(12)+50(2)=700$，组合收益为 −30%。事后仅选仍上市的 A，会报告 +20%，回答的是另一个经过未来筛选的问题。这个例子说明偏差机制，不保证所有策略的误差都同向或同幅。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed example, eligible stocks A and B both start at 10; invest 500 in each for 50 shares apiece. A ends at 12, while delisted B actually pays liquidation proceeds of 2 per share, with no other returns or costs. Terminal wealth is 700 and return is −30%. Selecting only surviving A afterward gives +20% and answers a future-selected question. This illustrates the mechanism without asserting an identical direction or size for every strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [QuantConnect, Research Guide](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/key-concepts/research-guide)，Look-ahead Bias、Survivorship Bias：支持当时可得宇宙与事后存续筛选的区分；不采用其特定策略收益数字。
- [CRSP US Stock Databases](https://indexes.morningstar.com/research-data-products/crsp-us-stock-databases)，Key Features、Permanent Identifiers：支持保留活跃及不活跃证券并以持续标识跟踪历史。
- [CRSP US Stock & Indexes Databases: SAS, ASCII & R Files](https://www.crsp.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/crsp_us_stock.pdf#page=15)，Delisting Flag 与 Examples §3：支持终值来源、缺失及已并入退市收益的区别；这些字段属于该版文件格式，使用其他版本须重查其字典。两股例为独立账户复算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- QuantConnect supports point-in-time selection and survivorship bias. CRSP documents inactive securities, permanent identifiers and version-specific flags for delisting outcomes already included in returns. The two-stock account is independently constructed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
