# 美国现行 M2 在 M1 上增加小额定期存款和零售货币市场基金并扣除退休账户余额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Current U.S. M2 adds small time deposits and retail money-market funds to M1 and subtracts specified retirement balances*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 按 Federal Reserve H.6 于 2026-08-25 发布的美国口径，M2 以 M1 为起点，加上金额低于 100,000 美元的小额定期存款和零售货币市场基金份额，再扣除存款机构及货币市场基金中的 IRA 与 Keogh 退休账户余额。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under the U.S. definition in the Federal Reserve H.6 release dated 25 August 2026, M2 starts with M1, adds small-denomination time deposits issued in amounts below USD 100,000 and retail money-market-fund balances, and subtracts IRA and Keogh retirement balances held at depository institutions and money-market funds.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

M2 是对 M1 的扩展，不是与 M1 并列后再相加的第二堆货币。其非 M1 成分通常比可直接支付的存款多一道期限、赎回或转换步骤，所以“被纳入 M2”不能替代对具体工具风险与使用条件的判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
M2 extends M1; it is not a second disjoint pile to be added to M1 again. Its non-M1 components normally involve an additional maturity, redemption, or conversion step relative to directly spendable deposits, so M2 membership does not replace analysis of the instrument's risks and use conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 现行 M2 不再在 M1 之外另加储蓄存款，因为储蓄存款自 2020 年 5 月起已经进入 M1。
- institutional money funds 不等同于 H.6 的 retail money market funds，不能仅凭名称纳入。
- 100,000 美元是美国 H.6 对小额定期存款的产品分类阈值，不是货币总量的观察值。
- H.6 自 2026 年 7 月 28 日发布起改用总量层面的 IRA／Keogh 扣除并把新方法应用于整条序列；这里写的是 2026-08-25 发布版本的构造，不能倒推每个历史 vintage 当时都用相同算法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Current M2 does not add savings deposits again outside M1, because savings deposits have been inside M1 since May 2020.
- Institutional money funds are not the retail money-market funds included by H.6.
- USD 100,000 is the U.S. H.6 product-classification threshold for small time deposits, not an observation of the money stock.
- Beginning with the 28 July 2026 release, H.6 changed to a total-level IRA/Keogh subtraction and applied the method to the full series. The construction here describes the 25 August 2026 release; it does not imply that every historical data vintage used the same algorithm at the time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么把“储蓄存款 + 小额定期存款 + 零售货币市场基金”全部加在现行 M1 之上会重复？
>
> **答案：** 因为储蓄存款已经属于现行 M1 的 other liquid deposits；M2 只在 M1 上再加指定的小额定期存款和零售基金并做退休账户扣除。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why would adding savings deposits, small time deposits, and retail money-market funds on top of current M1 double count?
>
> **Answer:** Savings deposits are already in current M1 as other liquid deposits. M2 adds the specified time deposits and retail funds and applies the retirement-account subtraction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
