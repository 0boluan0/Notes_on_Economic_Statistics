# Logit 与 Probit 的预测必须把所有变换、类别项和交互项代入完整线性指数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Logit and probit predictions must substitute every transformation, categorical term, and interaction into the complete linear index*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 不能把单个系数送进 CDF
> 对 logit 或 probit，预测概率是
> $$
> \hat p(x)=G[\hat\eta(x)],\qquad \hat\eta(x)=x'\hat\beta.
> $$
> $\hat\eta(x)$ 必须重建拟合模型中的完整指数。截距、主效应、非线性变换、类别项和交互项缺一不可；$G(\hat\beta_j)$ 通常不是任何明确协变量配置的预测概率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For logit or probit, $\hat p(x)=G[\hat\eta(x)]$ with $\hat\eta(x)=x'\hat\beta$. The complete fitted index must be reconstructed: intercept, main effects, nonlinear transformations, categorical terms, and interactions all matter. In general, $G(\hat\beta_j)$ is not the predicted probability for any stated covariate configuration.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 logit 指数为 $\eta=-2+0.8D+0.05A-0.02DA$。在 $A=10$ 时，$D=0$ 的指数是 $-1.5$，$D=1$ 的指数是 $-0.9$，所以应比较 $\Lambda(-1.5)$ 与 $\Lambda(-0.9)$。只算 $\Lambda(0.8)$ 同时遗漏了基准位置、$A$ 的主效应和交互。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, suppose the logit index is $\eta=-2+0.8D+0.05A-0.02DA$. At $A=10$, the index is $-1.5$ for $D=0$ and $-0.9$ for $D=1$, so the relevant comparison is between $\Lambda(-1.5)$ and $\Lambda(-0.9)$. Computing only $\Lambda(0.8)$ omits the baseline, the main effect of $A$, and the interaction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

报告预测时还要说明 $x$ 是真实个体、政策相关配置、样本中的每个观测，还是人为构造的“均值人”。即使完整指数算对，不同配置回答的仍是不同问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A report should also state whether $x$ represents an actual individual, a policy-relevant configuration, each observation in the sample, or an artificial “person at the means.” Even with a correctly reconstructed index, different covariate configurations answer different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
