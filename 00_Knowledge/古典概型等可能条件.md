# 只有等可能基本结果才能用有利个数除以总数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Favourable outcomes divided by total outcomes is valid only for equiprobable elementary outcomes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子判断
> 对有限样本空间，只有当每个基本结果都等可能时，才有
> $$P(A)=\frac{|A|}{|\Omega|}.$$
> 若基本结果的概率不同，必须把 $A$ 中各结果的真实概率相加，不能只数个数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> For a finite sample space,
> $$P(A)=\frac{|A|}{|\Omega|}$$
> holds only when all elementary outcomes are equiprobable. If their probabilities differ, the actual probabilities of outcomes in $A$ must be added; counting alone is invalid.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小反例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal counterexample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设硬币正面概率为 $0.8$。样本空间仍是 $\{H,T\}$，但 $P(H)=0.8$，不是 $1/2$。两个可能结果并不自动意味着两个结果等可能。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a coin lands heads with probability $0.8$. The sample space is still $\{H,T\}$, but $P(H)=0.8$, not $1/2$. Having two possible outcomes does not make them equally likely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个样本空间有四个基本结果，能否仅凭“事件 $A$ 含两个结果”断定 $P(A)=1/2$？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A sample space has four elementary outcomes and event $A$ contains two of them. Is that alone enough to conclude that $P(A)=1/2$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能；还必须知道四个基本结果等可能。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. The four elementary outcomes must also be equiprobable.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
