# When probability can be found by counting
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*古典概型的等可能条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Favourable outcomes divided by total outcomes” works when each outcome contributes the same amount of probability. This is the **equiprobable counting rule**. A fair six-sided die is a simple example: each face has probability $1/6$, so an event containing three faces has probability $3/6$. The key assumption is equal probability, not merely a finite list of possibilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“有利结果数除以总结果数”之所以能算概率，是因为每个结果贡献的概率相同，这就是**等可能结果计数法则**。公平六面骰子是一个简单例子：每个点数的概率都是 $1/6$，所以包含三个点数的事件，概率就是 $3/6$。关键假设是等可能，而不只是“可能结果能列成有限项”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\Omega$ be the sample space and $A$ an event, a set of outcomes we want to count. In a [[离散概率空间|discrete probability space]], the general calculation is to add the probabilities of the outcomes in $A$. If the space is finite and all its outcomes are equally likely, each must have probability $1/|\Omega|$ because their probabilities sum to one. The notation $|\Omega|$ means the number of elements of $\Omega$; $|A|$ is the number in $A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $\Omega$ 为样本空间，$A$ 为事件，也就是我们关心的一组结果。在[[离散概率空间]]中，一般做法是把 $A$ 中各个结果的概率相加。如果空间有限且所有结果等可能，因为总概率为一，每个结果的概率就必须是 $1/|\Omega|$。这里 $|\Omega|$ 表示 $\Omega$ 的元素个数，$|A|$ 表示 $A$ 的元素个数。

> [!abstract] Equal weights turn a sum into a count · 等权重把求和变成计数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On a finite nonempty sample space with equally likely outcomes,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在非空、有限且各结果等可能的样本空间上，
>
> $$P(A)=\sum_{\omega\in A}\frac{1}{|\Omega|}=\frac{|A|}{|\Omega|}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $\omega$ denotes one outcome and $\sum_{\omega\in A}$ adds over the outcomes in $A$. Each counted outcome has the same weight. This formula works for every event exactly when the finite model is uniform.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\omega$ 表示一个结果，$\sum_{\omega\in A}$ 表示对 $A$ 中的结果求和。每个被数到的结果权重相同。这个公式能对所有事件通用，当且仅当有限模型中的各结果等可能。

> [!warning] Two outcomes do not imply a half chance · 两种结果不意味着各占一半
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A biased coin may have $P(\{H\})=0.8$ and $P(\{T\})=0.2$. Its sample space is still $\{H,T\}$, but heads has probability $0.8$, not $1/2$. Counting the two labels ignores their unequal weights.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一枚有偏硬币可以满足 $P(\{H\})=0.8$、$P(\{T\})=0.2$。样本空间仍是 $\{H,T\}$，但正面的概率是 $0.8$，不是 $1/2$。只数两个标签，会丢掉它们不同的概率权重。

## A fair experiment can produce unequal categories
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*公平试验也可能产生不等可能的分类*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Roll two fair dice independently and record the ordered pair of faces. There are $6\times6=36$ outcomes, each with probability $1/36$ by [[事件独立|independence]]. The sum is $7$ for six pairs:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

独立地掷两枚公平骰子，按顺序记录两个点数。共有 $6\times6=36$ 种结果，根据[[事件独立|独立性]]，每种的概率都是 $1/36$。其中六种的和为 $7$：

$$(1,6),(2,5),(3,4),(4,3),(5,2),(6,1).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus the chance of a sum of $7$ is $6/36=1/6$. If we record only the sum, there are eleven possible labels, $2,3,\ldots,12$, but they are not equally likely. A sum of $2$ comes only from $(1,1)$ and has probability $1/36$. A sum of $7$ is six times as likely, so assigning $1/11$ to each sum would be wrong.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，点数和为 $7$ 的概率是 $6/36=1/6$。如果只记录点数和，则有 $2,3,\ldots,12$ 十一个可能标签，但它们并不等可能。点数和为 $2$ 只有 $(1,1)$ 这一种来源，概率为 $1/36$；点数和为 $7$ 的概率是它的六倍，因此不能给每个和都分配 $1/11$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sum is a [[随机变量|random variable]] that maps several underlying outcomes to one number. Combining outcomes into a category adds their probabilities; it does not make the categories equally likely. We may use either representation, provided we carry the correct probabilities with it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

点数和是一个[[随机变量]]，它会把几个底层结果映射到同一个数字。把结果合并成一类时，应把这些结果的概率相加，而不会自动让各类等可能。两种表示方式都可以使用，只要带上相应的正确概率。

## What to check before using the shortcut
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*使用计数公式前，先核对什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check that the outcomes are finite, mutually exclusive and exhaustive, then justify why they have equal probabilities from the sampling rule or model assumptions. Count the numerator and denominator at the same level: for example, pairs of die faces in both, rather than pairs in one and sums in the other. If weights differ, return to adding the actual probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先检查结果是否有限、互斥且穷尽，再依据抽样规则或模型假设说明它们为什么等可能。分子与分母还要在同一层次计数，例如都数骰子的有序点数对，不能一边数点数对、一边数点数和。如果权重不同，就回到按实际概率求和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing that a space has four outcomes and $A$ contains two of them is not enough to conclude $P(A)=1/2$. If the four probabilities are $0.1,0.2,0.3,0.4$, the first two have total probability $0.3$. A particular event's count fraction may happen to equal its probability even in a nonuniform model; such a coincidence does not justify using the formula for other events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只知道样本空间有四个结果、$A$ 包含其中两个，还不足以断定 $P(A)=1/2$。如果四个概率分别为 $0.1,0.2,0.3,0.4$，前两个的总概率就是 $0.3$。在非均匀模型中，某个事件的个数比例可能恰好等于它的概率，但这种巧合不能证明其他事件也可以直接按个数计算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This counting formula is for a finite space. An infinite space does not supply a finite denominator to count. Continuous uniform models instead compare lengths, areas or volumes under their own assumptions; for instance, see [[连续均匀分布|the uniform distribution on an interval]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个个数公式适用于有限空间。无限空间没有可直接数出的有限分母。连续均匀模型则在各自的假设下比较长度、面积或体积，例如[[连续均匀分布|区间上的均匀分布]]。
