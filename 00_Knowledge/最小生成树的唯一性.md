# Uniqueness of a minimum spanning tree
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*最小生成树的唯一性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[最小生成树的定义与唯一性|minimum spanning tree]] has minimum total edge weight, but that minimum value need not identify just one tree. **Uniqueness** means there is exactly one selected edge set attaining it. A useful guarantee is that all edge weights are different: then two different trees cannot both be optimal. Understanding the guarantee also matters when weights tie, because a tie alone does not decide uniqueness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
[[最小生成树的定义与唯一性|最小生成树]]使边权总和最小，但这个最小数值未必只对应一棵树。**唯一性**是指恰好一个所选边集达到它。一条有用的保证是所有边权都互不相同：此时两棵不同的树不可能同时最优。理解这条保证也有助于处理等权，因为仅凭出现并列，无法决定是否唯一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, give the triangle on $a,b,c$ weights $w(ab)=1,w(ac)=2,w(bc)=4$, where $ab$ is the undirected edge joining $a,b$. Its three spanning trees have totals $3,5,6$. The tree selecting $ab,ac$ is the sole minimum. The theorem extends this conclusion to every finite connected graph satisfying the distinct-weight condition, without listing all its trees.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，在顶点 $a,b,c$ 的三角形上令 $w(ab)=1,w(ac)=2,w(bc)=4$，其中 $ab$ 表示连接 $a,b$ 的无向边。三棵生成树的总权重是 $3,5,6$，只有选择 $ab,ac$ 的树达到最小值。定理把这个结论推广到满足互异权重条件的任意有限连通图，无需把所有树逐一列出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A sufficient condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一条充分条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $G=(V,E,w)$ be a finite, nonempty, connected undirected graph with a finite real weight $w(e)$ assigned to each edge $e$. A [[生成树|spanning tree]] keeps all vertices and is connected and acyclic. Its total weight is the sum over its selected edges. Connectedness and finiteness guarantee that at least one MST exists, as explained in the [[最小生成树的定义与唯一性|definition and existence discussion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $G=(V,E,w)$ 是有限、非空、连通无向图，每条边 $e$ 的权重 $w(e)$ 是有限实数。[[生成树]]保留全部顶点、连通且无环，总权重是所选边的权重和。连通性与有限性保证至少存在一棵 MST，具体见[[最小生成树的定义与唯一性|定义及存在性说明]]。

> [!theorem] Distinct weights guarantee uniqueness · 互异权重保证唯一
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $w(e)\ne w(f)$ whenever $e,f$ are different edges, then $G$ has exactly one MST. The hypothesis compares all edges in the graph, not merely the edges of one candidate tree.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若任意两条不同边 $e,f$ 都满足 $w(e)\ne w(f)$，则 $G$ 恰有一棵 MST。前提比较全图的边，而不只是某一棵候选树中的边。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Exchange the lightest edge on which two trees differ
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*交换两棵树最轻的差异边*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For membership statements below, write $T_1,T_2$ for the selected edge sets of the two trees. “Different trees” therefore means different edge sets on the same vertex set. The notation $T_2\setminus T_1$ means edges in $T_2$ that are absent from $T_1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
下面讨论边的归属时，用 $T_1,T_2$ 表示两棵树各自选中的边集。“不同的树”因而指同一顶点集上的不同边集；$T_2\setminus T_1$ 表示属于 $T_2$ 却不属于 $T_1$ 的边。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose two different MSTs $T_1,T_2$ existed. Among the edges belonging to exactly one of them, choose the lightest edge $e$; interchange the tree names if necessary so that $e\in T_1$ and $e\notin T_2$. Adding $e$ to $T_2$ creates a unique cycle, consisting of $e$ and the [[有限树的等价刻画|unique tree path]] between its endpoints. That path contains an edge $f$ not in $T_1$. Otherwise the entire cycle would lie in $T_1$, contradicting that $T_1$ is a tree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
假设有两棵不同的 MST $T_1,T_2$。在“只属于其中一棵”的边中，取最轻的 $e$；必要时交换树的名字，使 $e\in T_1$ 而 $e\notin T_2$。把 $e$ 加入 $T_2$，会形成唯一一个环：它由 $e$ 与两端之间的[[有限树的等价刻画|唯一树路径]]组成。这条路径中一定有一条边 $f$ 不属于 $T_1$；否则整个环都在 $T_1$ 中，与它是树矛盾。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $f\in T_2\setminus T_1$ is another edge considered when choosing $e$. Distinct weights give $w(e)<w(f)$. Removing $f$ from the new cycle produces a spanning tree $T'_2$ with
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
因此 $f\in T_2\setminus T_1$，也是选择 $e$ 时比较过的边。权重互异给出 $w(e)<w(f)$。从新环删去 $f$，得到生成树 $T'_2$，满足

$$w(T'_2)=w(T_2)+w(e)-w(f)<w(T_2),$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This contradicts minimality of $T_2$. Hence there cannot be two different MSTs. The argument uses strict comparison of two different edges, not positivity of the weights. It therefore works with negative and zero weights too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这与 $T_2$ 的最小性矛盾，因此不可能有两棵不同的 MST。论证用到的是两条不同边之间的严格大小比较，不是权重为正；所以负权和零权也不妨碍结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Equal weights leave both possibilities open
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*有相等权重时，两种结果都可能*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The converse fails. In a triangle with weights $1,1,2$, the three tree totals are $2,3,3$, so the two weight-$1$ edges form the unique MST. Distinct edge weights are therefore sufficient but not necessary. If instead all three weights are $1$, every two-edge tree has total $2$ and there are three different MSTs. Equal weights remove the strict inequality in the proof; they do not by themselves prove that a second optimum exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
逆命题不成立。边权为 $1,1,2$ 的三角形，其三棵树总权重为 $2,3,3$，两条权重为 $1$ 的边组成唯一 MST。因此边权互异是充分条件，而非必要条件。若三条边权全为 $1$，每棵两边树的总权重都为 $2$，就有三棵不同的 MST。出现相等权重会使证明中的严格不等式失效，但这本身没有证明第二个最优解存在。

> [!warning] An algorithm's tie-break is a choice · 算法打破并列是在作选择
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Choosing one tied edge by a fixed rule may make an implementation return one reproducible MST. That does not make the original weighted graph's optimum unique. In the all-weight-$1$ triangle, a rule can always return the same two edges while the other two minimum trees still exist.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用固定规则选择一条并列边，可以让程序每次返回同一棵 MST，却不会让原带权图的最优解变成唯一。三边全为 $1$ 的三角形中，规则可以总返回同样两条边，另外两棵最优树仍然存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A one-vertex graph has one spanning tree, the empty edge set, regardless of loops; its weight is $0$. If loops are allowed, they never belong to a tree. If parallel edges are allowed, keep their identities distinct: the exchange proof still works, including a two-edge cycle formed by parallel edges. A disconnected graph has no whole-graph MST under this definition; uniqueness of a componentwise minimum spanning forest asks for uniqueness separately in every connected component.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
单顶点图只有一棵生成树，即空边集，不受自环影响，其权重为 $0$。若允许自环，它们永不属于树；若允许平行边，则保留不同边的身份，交换证明仍适用，包括平行边形成的两边环。不连通图没有此处定义的全图 MST；若考虑逐分量的最小生成森林，其唯一性要在每个连通分量内分别判断。
