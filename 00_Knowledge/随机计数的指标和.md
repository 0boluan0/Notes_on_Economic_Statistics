# Counting with indicators
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*随机计数的指标和*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A random count can often be built from a list of yes-or-no questions. To count how many people are late, ask whether each person is late, record one for yes and zero for no, then add the records. The useful consequence is that the expected count can be found from the individual probabilities, even when the people affect one another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个随机计数，往往可以拆成一串“是或否”的问题。要数有几个人迟到，就逐个问“这个人迟到了吗”，是记一，否记零，再把记录相加。这样做的用途在于：即使这些人彼此影响，也可以从各人的概率计算迟到人数的期望。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For three people, call their lateness events $A_1,A_2,A_3$. An [[事件指示变量|indicator]] $I_{A_i}$ is one when person $i$ is late and zero otherwise. If one outcome has records $(1,0,1)$, the first and third people are late, and the count is $1+0+1=2$. The addition describes what happened on that outcome; it does not yet average over possible days.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以三个人为例，分别用 $A_1,A_2,A_3$ 表示他们迟到的事件。[[事件指示变量|指示变量]] $I_{A_i}$ 在第 $i$ 人迟到时为一，否则为零。若某次结果的记录为 $(1,0,1)$，就是第一、第三人迟到，人数为 $1+0+1=2$。这次相加描述的是当前结果，还没有对所有可能日子求平均。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## First establish what is being counted
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先建立逐结果成立的计数关系*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n$ be a fixed finite number of candidate contributions, indexed by $i=1,\ldots,n$. Let $A_i$ mean “candidate $i$ is counted”, and let $N$ be the number of candidates actually counted. If $\omega$ denotes a complete outcome, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设一共有固定的有限个候选贡献，数量为 $n$，用 $i=1,\ldots,n$ 编号。让 $A_i$ 表示“候选 $i$ 被计入”，$N$ 表示实际计入的候选数量。若 $\omega$ 是一个完整结果，则

$$N(\omega)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}I_{A_i}(\omega).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The summation adds one for each counted candidate and zero for each remaining candidate. This equality must hold for **every outcome**, not just on average. If $n=0$, the empty sum and the count are both zero. Choosing useful candidates while avoiding omissions or duplicate contributions is the separate task of [[计数指标的索引策略|choosing an indicator index]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求和时，每个被计入的候选贡献一，其余贡献零。这个等式必须对**每个结果**成立，而不仅是在平均意义上成立。$n=0$ 时，空和与计数都为零。怎样选择便于计算的候选，同时避免遗漏或重复贡献，是[[计数指标的索引策略]]负责的问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The events may overlap. If two people are late on the same day, both should contribute, so their sum is two. In contrast, the indicator of “at least one person is late” is only one on that day. Adding indicators counts occurrences; taking the indicator of the [[指示变量的并事件恒等式|union]] records whether there is any occurrence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些事件可以重叠。如果同一天有两个人迟到，两个人都应该贡献一次，所以和为二；但“至少一人迟到”的指示变量在这一天只为一。指示变量相加数的是发生了几次，[[指示变量的并事件恒等式|并事件]]的指示变量记录的是有没有发生。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Then average the count
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*再对计数取期望*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $p_i=P(A_i)$ for the probability that candidate $i$ is counted. Each indicator is bounded and therefore has a finite [[期望|expectation]]. We may use [[期望线性性|finite linearity of expectation]], followed by the rule [[指示变量期望|$E[I_{A_i}]=P(A_i)$]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $p_i=P(A_i)$ 表示候选 $i$ 被计入的概率。每个指示变量都有界，因此具有有限[[期望]]。先用[[期望线性性|有限期望线性性]]，再用[[指示变量期望|$E[I_{A_i}]=P(A_i)$]]，得到

> [!abstract] Expected count · 计数的期望
> $$E[N]=E\!\left[\sum_{i=1}^{n}I_{A_i}\right]=\sum_{i=1}^{n}E[I_{A_i}]=\sum_{i=1}^{n}p_i.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No independence assumption is needed. Equal probabilities are not required either. When all $p_i$ happen to equal a common $p$, the sum simplifies to $np$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不需要独立性，也不要求各人的概率相同。只有所有 $p_i$ 恰好都等于同一个 $p$ 时，和式才简化为 $np$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the three people have lateness probabilities $0.2,0.4,0.7$, then $E[N]=0.2+0.4+0.7=1.3$. An actual count is an integer from zero to three; its average can be $1.3$. We have obtained that average without specifying how the three lateness events depend on each other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若三个人的迟到概率分别为 $0.2,0.4,0.7$，则 $E[N]=0.2+0.4+0.7=1.3$。实际人数是零到三之间的整数，平均数却可以是 $1.3$。我们还没有指定三个人怎样相互依赖，就已经得到了这个平均数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A mean does not give the chance of any occurrence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*期望人数不等于至少一人发生的概率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a different model, suppose all three people ride the same bus and are late exactly when that bus is delayed, with delay probability $0.2$. Their indicators are identical. The count is three with probability $0.2$ and zero with probability $0.8$, so $E[N]=3(0.2)=0.6$, while $P(N\ge1)=0.2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换一个模型：假设三个人都乘同一辆公交车，而且恰好在公交晚点时才迟到，晚点概率为 $0.2$。三人的指示变量完全相同：人数以 $0.2$ 的概率为三，以 $0.8$ 的概率为零。所以 $E[N]=3(0.2)=0.6$，但 $P(N\ge1)=0.2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If instead their lateness events were independent with the same individual probability $0.2$, the mean would still be $0.6$, but $P(N\ge1)=1-0.8^3=0.488$. The individual probabilities determine the mean count; in general they do not determine its variance, full distribution, or probability of being positive. A [[Bernoulli和的二项分布|binomial model for the count]] is one further conclusion available under suitable assumptions, not a requirement for the mean formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果改为三个迟到事件独立、各自概率仍为 $0.2$，均值依然是 $0.6$，但 $P(N\ge1)=1-0.8^3=0.488$。各人的概率足以决定人数期望，一般却不足以决定方差、完整分布或人数为正的概率。在适当假设下，可以进一步得到[[Bernoulli和的二项分布|计数的二项分布]]，但均值公式不以这个分布为前提。

> [!example]- Randomly returned hats · 随机归还的帽子
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $n\ge1$ people each receive one of their $n$ hats, with every permutation equally likely. For each person, the probability of receiving their own hat is $1/n$. If $F$ counts people receiving their own hats and $A_i$ is that event for person $i$, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $n\ge1$ 个人各领回这 $n$ 顶帽子中的一顶，所有排列等可能。每个人拿回自己帽子的概率都是 $1/n$。若 $F$ 数拿回自己帽子的人，$A_i$ 表示第 $i$ 人拿对帽子，则
>
> $$F=\sum_{i=1}^{n}I_{A_i},\qquad E[F]=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac1n=1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $n\ge2$, these indicators are not mutually independent: if the first $n-1$ people have their own hats, the last person must also have theirs. That dependence does not change the calculation. The expectation is one, even though a particular return may give zero, one, or several correct hats, depending on $n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $n\ge2$ 时，这些指示变量不相互独立：如果前 $n-1$ 人都拿对，最后一人也必然拿对。这种依赖不影响计算。期望为一，但某次归还可能拿对零顶、一顶或多顶，具体哪些数量可能出现取决于 $n$。

> [!note]- Infinitely many candidates need a limiting argument · 无穷多个候选需要极限论证
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For countably many indicators, let $N_m=\sum_{i=1}^{m}I_{A_i}$ count only the first $m$ candidates. These partial counts increase to $N=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}I_{A_i}$. Because every term is nonnegative, the [[单调收敛定理|monotone convergence theorem]] allows $E[N]=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}P(A_i)$, with $+\infty$ permitted. This extension follows from increasing nonnegative partial sums; signed infinite sums require their own conditions, as discussed under [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对可数多个指示变量，令 $N_m=\sum_{i=1}^{m}I_{A_i}$ 只数前 $m$ 个候选。这些部分计数递增到 $N=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}I_{A_i}$。因为每项都非负，[[单调收敛定理]]允许写 $E[N]=\sum_{i=1}^{\infty}P(A_i)$，两边可以为 $+\infty$。这个推广依靠非负部分和递增的结构；含正负项的无穷和需要另查条件，见[[期望线性性]]。
