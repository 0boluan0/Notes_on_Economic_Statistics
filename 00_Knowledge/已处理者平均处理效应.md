# ATET 是处理组成员的个体处理效应的条件期望

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The ATET is the expected individual treatment effect among treated units*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 当 $P(D=1)>0$ 时，$\mathrm{ATET}=E[Y(1)-Y(0)\mid D=1]$，也常写作 ATT。它先按 $D=1$ 确定人群，再在同一批人的两种潜在结果之间作比较；它不是处理组的观测均值。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When treatment has positive probability, the ATET (also ATT) is the conditional mean effect among treated units. Conditioning selects the group; the contrast compares that same group under two treatment states. It is not their observed outcome mean.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

若处理比例为 $p$，则 $\mathrm{ATE}=p\,\mathrm{ATET}+(1-p)E[Y(1)-Y(0)\mid D=0]$。效应异质且选择与效应有关时，ATE 与 ATET 可以不同；谁更大没有普遍规律。[[随机分配识别]]给出使二者相等的一组条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ATE weights the treated and untreated groups’ mean effects by their population shares. With heterogeneous effects and selection on effects, ATE and ATET can differ in either direction. [[随机分配识别|Random assignment]] supplies conditions under which they coincide.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
