# Singularity of a covariance matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*协方差矩阵的奇异性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Several measurements can vary individually while carrying an exact linear redundancy. If one weighted combination is always the same number, that combination has no random variation. **A covariance matrix is singular exactly when such a nontrivial combination exists**, under finite second moments. The redundancy may involve several coordinates; no individual coordinate has to be constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多个测量值可以各自变化，却含有精确的线性冗余。如果某个加权组合始终等于同一个数，这个组合就没有随机波动。在有限二阶矩条件下，**协方差矩阵奇异，当且仅当存在这样的非平凡组合**。冗余可以涉及多个坐标，不要求某个单独坐标恒定。

> [!example] Two changing coordinates, one fixed combination · 两个坐标都变化，一个组合却固定
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $Z$ equal $-1$ or $1$ with equal probability, and set $X=(Z,2Z+3)^T$. The possible vectors are $(-1,1)^T$ and $(1,5)^T$, with mean $\mu=(0,3)^T$. Both coordinates change. Nevertheless, subtracting the second coordinate from twice the first always gives $-3$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $Z$ 等概率取 $-1$ 或 $1$，设 $X=(Z,2Z+3)^T$。可能向量为 $(-1,1)^T$ 和 $(1,5)^T$，均值是 $\mu=(0,3)^T$。两个坐标都会变，但第一坐标的两倍减去第二坐标始终等于 $-3$：
>
> $$a=\begin{bmatrix}2\\-1\end{bmatrix},\qquad a^TX=2Z-(2Z+3)=-3=a^T\mu.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Since $\operatorname{Var}(Z)=1$, the [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] is $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}$, and direct multiplication gives $\Sigma a=0$. Thus a nonzero vector lies in its [[零空间|nullspace]], so the matrix is [[非零齐次解与不可逆|singular, meaning not invertible]]. The two positive diagonal entries $1$ and $4$ do not prevent singularity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因为 $\operatorname{Var}(Z)=1$，[[协方差矩阵]]为 $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}$，直接相乘得到 $\Sigma a=0$。这说明[[零空间]]中有非零向量，因此矩阵[[非零齐次解与不可逆|奇异，也就是不可逆]]。对角上两个正数 $1$ 和 $4$ 并不能排除奇异性。

## The precise equivalence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把“固定组合”写准确*

> [!important] One fixed nonzero weight vector · 一个固定的非零权重向量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T\in\mathbb R^p$, with $p\ge1$ and $E[X_j^2]<\infty$ for every coordinate. Write $\mu=E[X]$ and $\Sigma=\operatorname{Cov}(X)$. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$
> \boxed{\Sigma\text{ is singular}
> \iff \exists a\in\mathbb R^p\setminus\{0\}:\ a^TX=a^T\mu\quad\text{almost surely}.}
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The vector $a$ is deterministic: its weights are chosen once and do not change with the outcome. “Almost surely” means the relation holds on an event of probability one. The constant is allowed to be nonzero; subtracting the mean gives the equivalent relation $a^T(X-\mu)=0$ almost surely.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T\in\mathbb R^p$，$p\ge1$，每个坐标都满足 $E[X_j^2]<\infty$，并记 $\mu=E[X]$、$\Sigma=\operatorname{Cov}(X)$，则有上式。$a$ 是确定的：权重只选一次，不随结果改变。“几乎必然”表示关系在概率为一的事件上成立。常数可以不为零；减去均值后，等价地写成 $a^T(X-\mu)=0$ 几乎必然。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a fixed combination is almost surely equal to some constant $c$, taking expectations shows that $c=a^T\mu$, so the displayed value of the constant is forced. The restriction $a\ne0$ matters: the zero weights would give a constant combination for every random vector and tell us nothing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果固定组合几乎必然等于某个常数 $c$，两边取期望就得到 $c=a^T\mu$，所以上式的常数值是必然确定的。$a\ne0$ 很关键：全零权重对任何随机向量都会给出常数组合，不能说明任何奇异性。

## Why zero variance is the connecting fact
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零方差怎样连接两边*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A covariance matrix is [[协方差矩阵半正定性|symmetric positive semidefinite]]. For such matrices, the [[半正定零方向|zero-direction criterion]] says $a^T\Sigma a=0$ exactly when $\Sigma a=0$. Combining this fact with the [[线性投影方差|linear-combination variance identity]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

协方差矩阵[[协方差矩阵半正定性|对称且半正定]]。对这种矩阵，[[半正定零方向]]判据说明 $a^T\Sigma a=0$ 当且仅当 $\Sigma a=0$。再接上[[线性投影方差|线性组合的方差恒等式]]，得到

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Sigma\text{ singular}
&\iff \exists a\ne0:\ \Sigma a=0\\
&\iff \exists a\ne0:\ a^T\Sigma a=0\\
&\iff \exists a\ne0:\
E\!\left[(a^T(X-\mu))^2\right]=0.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last integrand is nonnegative. A nonnegative random variable has expectation zero exactly when it is zero almost surely, so the last line says $a^T(X-\mu)=0$ almost surely. This is the familiar [[方差|zero-variance characterization]] applied to the scalar $a^TX$. Finite second moments justify all the means and variances; nothing requires a normal distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后被平均的量非负。非负随机变量的期望为零，当且仅当它几乎必然为零，所以最后一行正好表示 $a^T(X-\mu)=0$ 几乎必然。这是在标量 $a^TX$ 上使用熟悉的[[方差|零方差刻画]]。有限二阶矩保证所有均值和方差有意义，并不要求正态分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Geometrically, $a^Tx=a^T\mu$ describes a line in two dimensions or a hyperplane in higher dimensions. The distribution lies entirely on that set up to probability-zero exceptions. In the example, this is the line $x_2=2x_1+3$. Without the offset, $X=(Z,2Z)^T$ satisfies $2X_1-X_2=0$; for any $Z$ with finite positive variance it has the same singularity mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

几何上，$a^Tx=a^T\mu$ 在二维描述一条直线，在更高维描述一个超平面。除去零概率例外，整个分布都位于这个集合中。开头例子就是直线 $x_2=2x_1+3$。去掉偏移后，$X=(Z,2Z)^T$ 满足 $2X_1-X_2=0$；只要 $Z$ 的方差有限且为正，就有同样的奇异机制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Exact linear redundancy is the boundary
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*边界是精确的线性冗余*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A constant coordinate is one way to obtain a zero-variance direction, but the example shows it is not the only way. Conversely, a deterministic **nonlinear** relation need not make the covariance singular. If $Z$ takes $-1,0,1$ with equal probabilities and $X=(Z,Z^2)^T$, the second coordinate is fully determined by the first, yet direct calculation gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

某个坐标恒定，是产生零方差方向的一种方式，但不是唯一方式。反过来，确定性的**非线性**关系也不必使协方差奇异。若 $Z$ 等概率取 $-1,0,1$，$X=(Z,Z^2)^T$，第二坐标完全由第一坐标决定，但直接计算可得

$$
\mu=\begin{bmatrix}0\\2/3\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}2/3&0\\0&2/9\end{bmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both diagonal entries are positive, and this diagonal matrix is invertible. The three possible points do not lie on one line. Thus covariance singularity detects an almost-sure affine linear constraint, not every kind of dependence. A sample covariance can also be singular because there are too few observations; the [[样本协方差秩边界|sample rank bound]] treats that distinct issue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个对角元素都为正，这张对角矩阵可逆，三个可能点也不在同一条直线上。因此，协方差奇异性检测的是几乎必然成立的仿射线性约束，不是任意类型的依赖。样本协方差还可能因为观测数太少而奇异，那是[[样本协方差秩边界]]处理的另一个问题。

> [!question]- A third coordinate adds no new linear variation · 第三个坐标没有增加线性波动
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $X_3=X_1+X_2$ almost surely and all coordinates have finite second moments, which weights prove that the covariance is singular?
>
> Use $a=(1,1,-1)^T$. It is nonzero and $a^TX=0$ almost surely. Therefore its variance is zero and $a$ lies in the covariance nullspace.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $X_3=X_1+X_2$ 几乎必然成立，且各坐标二阶矩有限，哪些权重能证明协方差奇异？
>
> 取 $a=(1,1,-1)^T$。它非零，且 $a^TX=0$ 几乎必然，因此该组合方差为零，$a$ 属于协方差矩阵的零空间。

> [!note]- Complex coordinates · 复数坐标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $Z\in\mathbb C^p$ with finite $E|Z_j|^2$, use $\mu=E[Z]$ and the Hermitian covariance $\Sigma=E[(Z-\mu)(Z-\mu)^*]$, where $*$ is conjugate transpose. The same criterion holds with a fixed nonzero $a\in\mathbb C^p$ and $a^*(Z-\mu)=0$ almost surely: the connecting nonnegative expectation is $E|a^*(Z-\mu)|^2=a^*\Sigma a$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每个 $E|Z_j|^2$ 都有限的 $Z\in\mathbb C^p$，使用 $\mu=E[Z]$ 及 Hermitian 协方差 $\Sigma=E[(Z-\mu)(Z-\mu)^*]$，其中 $*$ 是共轭转置。同一判据改为存在固定非零 $a\in\mathbb C^p$，使 $a^*(Z-\mu)=0$ 几乎必然；连接两边的非负期望是 $E|a^*(Z-\mu)|^2=a^*\Sigma a$。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Geyer, *Stat 5101 Lecture Notes*, Corollaries 5.5–5.6, printed p. 136 / PDF p. 144](https://www.stat.umn.edu/geyer/old/5102/n.pdf#page=144): covariance semidefiniteness and the equivalence between a zero-variance linear combination and concentration on a hyperplane. Corollary 2.34, printed p. 69 / PDF p. 77, gives zero variance if and only if almost-sure constancy.
- [Stanford EE278, *Random Vectors*, PDF p. 8](https://isl.stanford.edu/~abbas/ee278/lect03.pdf#page=8): the outer-product identity and the nonnegative quadratic form. The singularity chain uses the linked semidefinite nullspace criterion; the explicit real examples and complex absolute-square extension are derived above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Geyer 推论 5.5–5.6（印刷第 136 页／PDF 第 144 页）核对半正定性、零方差线性组合与分布集中于超平面的等价；推论 2.34（印刷第 69 页／PDF 第 77 页）核对零方差当且仅当几乎必然为常数。
- Stanford EE278 PDF 第 8 页核对外积恒等式与非负二次型；奇异性链条结合所链接的半正定零空间判据。实数例子和复数绝对平方推广在正文直接推导。
