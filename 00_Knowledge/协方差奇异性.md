# 实随机向量的协方差矩阵奇异当且仅当存在非零向量使对应线性组合几乎处处为常数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A real random vector has a singular covariance matrix exactly when some nonzero linear combination is almost surely constant*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心等价
> 设 $X\in\mathbb R^p$ 有有限二阶矩，均值为 $\mu$，协方差矩阵为 $\Sigma$。则
> $$
> \Sigma\text{ 奇异}
> \iff
> \exists a\ne0:\ a^T(X-\mu)=0\quad\text{几乎处处}.
> $$
> 等价地，存在非零 $a$ 使 $a^TX$ 几乎处处等于常数 $a^T\mu$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The covariance matrix of a real random vector with finite second moments is singular exactly when some nonzero linear combination has no random variation and is almost surely constant.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

协方差矩阵半正定，因此
$$
\Sigma\text{ 奇异}
\iff
\exists a\ne0:\ \Sigma a=0
\iff
\exists a\ne0:\ a^T\Sigma a=0.
$$
又由线性投影方差恒等式，
$$
a^T\Sigma a=\operatorname{Var}(a^TX)
=E\!\left[(a^T(X-\mu))^2\right].
$$
非负随机变量的期望为零，当且仅当它几乎处处为零，于是得到结论。对复随机向量 $Z$，用 $\Sigma=E[(Z-\mu)(Z-\mu)^*]$；相应条件是存在 $a\ne0$ 使 $a^*(Z-\mu)=0$ 几乎处处。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive semidefiniteness turns singularity into the existence of a nonzero zero-quadratic-form direction. Projected variance then gives $a^*\Sigma a=E|a^*(Z-\mu)|^2$, which vanishes exactly when the centred projection is zero almost surely. The real case uses transpose and an ordinary square.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如令 $X=(Z,2Z)^T$，其中 $Z$ 有有限且正的方差。取 $a=(2,-1)^T$，则 $a^TX=0$ 几乎处处，所以 $\Sigma$ 奇异。这里每个分量都可以有正方差；奇异性来自分量之间的确定线性关系，而不是某个分量必须恒定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X=(Z,2Z)^T$, where $Z$ has finite positive variance. With $a=(2,-1)^T$, we have $a^TX=0$ almost surely, so $\Sigma$ is singular. Each component may still have positive variance; singularity comes from the deterministic linear relation between the components, not from requiring one component itself to be constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若 $X_3=X_1+X_2$ 几乎处处成立，为什么三维协方差矩阵必奇异？
>
> **答案：** 取 $a=(1,1,-1)^T\ne0$，则 $a^TX=0$ 几乎处处，所以这个方向的方差为零，$a$ 属于协方差矩阵的零空间。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $X_3=X_1+X_2$ almost surely, why must the three-dimensional covariance matrix be singular?
>
> **Answer:** Take $a=(1,1,-1)^T\ne0$. Then $a^TX=0$ almost surely, so the variance in this direction is zero and $a$ lies in the nullspace of the covariance matrix.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
