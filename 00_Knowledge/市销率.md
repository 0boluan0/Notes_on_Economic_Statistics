# 市销率是普通股市场价值与明确期间营业收入的比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price-to-sales is ordinary equity market value divided by revenue for a specified period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市销率用收入而非利润作规模参照。设所选日期普通股市值为 $M$、所选期间收入为 $S>0$，则 $PS=M/S$。若定义每股收入为 $S/N$、股价满足 $M=PN$，则也可写成 $P/(S/N)$；此处股数是同一市值换算口径，不能未经说明换成 EPS 的加权股数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
P/S uses revenue rather than profit as the scale: $PS=M/S$ for market capitalization $M$ and positive period revenue $S$. If revenue per share is $S/N$ and $M=PN$, then $PS=P/(S/N)$. The share count must match the market-cap conversion, not silently change to weighted EPS shares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如市值 200、年收入 100，市销率为 2 倍。即使当年亏损，这个比率仍能为正，但它没有说明收入扣除成本后能留下多少利润或现金。比较时须核对总额／净额收入确认、业务范围、利润率与资本需求。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market capitalization of 200 and annual revenue of 100 give P/S of 2. It can remain positive during a loss, but says nothing by itself about profit or cash remaining after costs. Check gross-versus-net revenue recognition, business scope, margins, and capital needs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它还有一个固有的范围差异：分子仅为股东价值，收入却来自整个企业的业务。两家经营相似、债务不同的公司，不能仅因市销率较低便断言较便宜；企业价值／收入是另一个指标，不能混称市销率。在股数与利润范围匹配且利润非零时，代数上 $PS=PE\times NI/S$，所以同一个市销率可以对应很不同的[[市盈率]]和[[营业净利率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Its numerator belongs only to equity while revenue comes from the whole business. Different leverage can therefore undermine cheapness comparisons; enterprise-value-to-sales is a different measure. With matched shares and profit scope and non-zero profit, $PS=PE\times NI/S$, so identical P/S ratios can coexist with very different [[市盈率|P/E ratios]] and [[营业净利率|net margins]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
