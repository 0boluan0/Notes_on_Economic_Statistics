# 正弦位置编码用多组固定频率的 sin 与 cos 为绝对位置生成向量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sinusoidal positional encoding generates absolute-position vectors using fixed sine and cosine frequencies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**正弦位置编码**是一种固定函数生成的 [[位置编码]]：对每个绝对位置 $p$，用多组不同频率的 sin/cos 构造位置向量。原始 Transformer 把该向量加到相同维度的 token 输入表示上；这些位置向量本身不是逐位置训练出来的参数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Sinusoidal positional encoding** is a [[位置编码|positional encoding]] generated by a fixed function. It constructs a vector for absolute position $p$ from sine/cosine pairs at different frequencies. The original Transformer adds this vector to the token input representation of the same width; the position vectors themselves are not separately trained parameters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取偶数维度 $d_{\mathrm{model}}$，按从零开始的维度索引写成

$$
\omega_r=10000^{-2r/d_{\mathrm{model}}},
\qquad r=0,\ldots,d_{\mathrm{model}}/2-1,
$$

$$
\mathrm{PE}_{p,2r}=\sin(p\omega_r),
\qquad
\mathrm{PE}_{p,2r+1}=\cos(p\omega_r).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For even width $d_{\mathrm{model}}$, the displayed equations use zero-based dimension indices. Each adjacent pair shares a frequency, and the frequencies follow a geometric progression.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $d_{\mathrm{model}}=4$ 时，两组频率是 $1$ 和 $0.01$，所以

$$
\mathrm{PE}_{0}=(0,1,0,1),
\qquad
\mathrm{PE}_{1}=(\sin1,\cos1,\sin0.01,\cos0.01).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At width four, the two frequencies are $1$ and $0.01$, giving the displayed encodings for positions zero and one. This makes the frequency and dimension conventions explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

sin/cos 成对还有一个便于理解的性质：固定偏移 $k$ 对每一对编码都是同一个线性变换。令 $\theta=k\omega_r$，则

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
\sin((p+k)\omega_r)\\
\cos((p+k)\omega_r)
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
\cos\theta&\sin\theta\\
-\sin\theta&\cos\theta
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
\sin(p\omega_r)\\
\cos(p\omega_r)
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

这个性质由和角公式直接得到，说明固定频率也能表达相对位移关系。它没有保证模型一定学会使用该关系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By the angle-addition identities, a fixed offset $k$ applies the same linear transformation to each sine/cosine pair, as shown above. Fixed frequencies can therefore express relative-offset relationships, but this does not guarantee that a trained model learns to use them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与 [[可学习位置嵌入]] 的有限查表不同，此公式能为训练范围外的位置继续生成向量；“向量可计算”不等于“长序列预测可靠”。原论文把长度外推列为选择该形式的动机，没有把它证明成泛化保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unlike a finite table of [[可学习位置嵌入|learned positional embeddings]], the formula can generate vectors beyond the positions seen in training. Being able to compute a vector does not guarantee reliable predictions on longer sequences. The original paper presents extrapolation as a motivation, not a proven generalization guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
