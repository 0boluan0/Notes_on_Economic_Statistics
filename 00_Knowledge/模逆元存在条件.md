# When does a modular inverse exist?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*模逆元存在条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trying every remainder can find an inverse for a small modulus, but it does not explain which inputs permit one. The decisive question is whether the input and modulus share a positive divisor greater than one. If they do, every product of the input keeps that divisor and cannot become one modulo the modulus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模数小时可以逐个余数试出逆元，但这种办法没有解释哪些输入能够有逆元。关键是输入与模数是否共有大于一的正因数；若有，输入的每个乘积都保留这个因数，就不能在该模数下变成一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[模逆元|modular inverse]] is represented by an integer $u$ satisfying $au\equiv1\pmod n$. By [[同余|congruence]], this means $au-1=qn$ for an integer $q$. The [[最大公因数|greatest common divisor]] $\gcd(a,n)$ is the greatest positive common divisor; the inputs are **coprime** when it equals one. Since $n\ge2$, this gcd is positive even if $a=0$ or $a<0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[模逆元]]由满足 $au\equiv1\pmod n$ 的整数 $u$ 代表。按[[同余]]定义，这表示 $au-1=qn$，其中 $q$ 是整数。[[最大公因数]] $\gcd(a,n)$ 是最大的正公因数；它等于一时称二者**互素**。因为 $n\ge2$，即使 $a=0$ 或 $a<0$，这里的 gcd 也为正。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The criterion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*存在判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a\in\mathbb Z$ and let $n>1$ be an integer. A [[模逆元|modular inverse]] of $a$ modulo $n$ exists exactly when $\gcd(a,n)=1$. The requirement is **coprimality with the modulus**, not merely $a\not\equiv0\pmod n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a\in\mathbb Z$、$n>1$ 为整数。$a$ 模 $n$ 的 [[模逆元]] 存在，当且仅当 $\gcd(a,n)=1$。条件是与**模数互素**，而不只是 $a\not\equiv0\pmod n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why a common divisor obstructs an inverse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么公因数构成障碍*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Necessity: if $au\equiv1\pmod n$, then $au-qn=1$ for some integer $q$. Every common divisor of $a$ and $n$ divides the left-hand side and therefore divides one, so their greatest common divisor must be one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必要性：若 $au\equiv1\pmod n$，则存在整数 $q$ 使 $au-qn=1$。$a,n$ 的每个公因子都整除左边，因而整除 1，所以最大公因数只能为 1。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the obstruction explicitly, suppose a positive integer $d$ divides both $a$ and $n$. Then it divides $au-qn$ for every pair of integers $u,q$. If an inverse existed, this expression would equal one, so $d\mid1$, forcing $d=1$. In particular, no shared divisor $d>1$ is compatible with an inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体地说，设正整数 $d$ 同时整除 $a,n$。无论整数 $u,q$ 怎样取，$d$ 都整除 $au-qn$。如果逆元存在，这个表达式就等于一，于是 $d\mid1$，只能有 $d=1$。因此任何公共因数 $d>1$ 都与逆元的存在冲突。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why coprimality supplies an inverse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么互素足以构造逆元*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sufficiency: if $\gcd(a,n)=1$, [[Bézout等式|Bézout's identity]] provides integers $s,t$ with $sa+tn=1$. Reducing modulo $n$ gives $sa\equiv1\pmod n$, so $s$ represents an inverse; reducing $s$ gives its standard representative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

充分性：若 $\gcd(a,n)=1$，[[Bézout等式]] 给出整数 $s,t$ 满足 $sa+tn=1$。模 $n$ 取余就得到 $sa\equiv1\pmod n$，所以 $s$ 是逆元的一个代表；再对 $s$ 取标准余数即可。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The integer $s$ is the coefficient of the number $a$ being inverted, not the coefficient $t$ of the modulus. Bézout applies because $a,n$ are not both zero. Reducing $s$ to its standard remainder changes it by a multiple of $n$, so multiplying by $a$ still gives one modulo $n$. Existence and uniqueness are different steps: the argument here constructs an inverse; the definition card proves that any two such representatives are congruent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里应取待求逆数 $a$ 的系数 $s$，不是模数的系数 $t$。由于 $a,n$ 不全为零，可以应用 Bézout 等式。把 $s$ 换成标准余数，只改变了一个 $n$ 的整数倍，所以再乘 $a$ 仍然模 $n$ 余一。存在与唯一是不同的步骤：这里构造出逆元；定义卡另证任意两个逆元代表必同余。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Computing the inverse of seventeen modulo forty-three
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*求十七模四十三的逆元*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $43=2\cdot17+9$, $17=9+8$ and $9=8+1$ give $1=2\cdot43-5\cdot17$ by back-substitution. Thus $17^{-1}\equiv-5\equiv38\pmod{43}$, checked by $17\cdot38=646=15\cdot43+1$. The [[扩展欧几里得算法|extended Euclidean algorithm]] systematically produces this Bézout coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $43=2\cdot17+9$、$17=9+8$、$9=8+1$，回代得到 $1=2\cdot43-5\cdot17$。因此 $17^{-1}\equiv-5\equiv38\pmod{43}$，核对 $17\cdot38=646=15\cdot43+1$。[[扩展欧几里得算法]] 正是系统地产生这个 Bézout 系数的方法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last nonzero remainder is one. Recover its coefficient of seventeen by substituting each previous remainder, without exchanging the roles of the two inputs:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一个非零余数是一。依次代入前面的余数，把十七的系数找出来；过程中不交换两个输入所扮演的角色：

$$\begin{aligned}
1&=9-8\\
 &=9-(17-9)=2\cdot9-17\\
 &=2(43-2\cdot17)-17=2\cdot43-5\cdot17.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus the coefficient to keep is $-5$, and $-5+43=38$ is its standard representative. The coefficient $2$ belongs to the modulus and is not the inverse of seventeen. If the input changes to $-17$, the identity becomes $1=2\cdot43+5(-17)$, so five is an inverse of $-17$ modulo forty-three. The sign change belongs in the coefficient of that input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此应取系数 $-5$，其标准代表是 $-5+43=38$；系数二属于模数，并不是十七的逆元。若输入换成 $-17$，等式变成 $1=2\cdot43+5(-17)$，所以五是 $-17$ 模四十三的逆元。输入的符号改变，应由对应系数吸收。

## Prime and composite moduli
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*素数与合数模数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a prime modulus $p$, every residue $1,\ldots,p-1$ is coprime to $p$ and hence invertible. Composite moduli lack this property: $\gcd(6,15)=3$, so six has no inverse modulo fifteen despite being a nonzero residue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当模数 $p$ 为素数时，$1,\ldots,p-1$ 每个余数都与 $p$ 互素，因而都有逆元；合数模数不具备这个性质。例如 $\gcd(6,15)=3$，所以 6 虽然不是零余数，也没有模 15 的逆元。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For prime $p$, a standard nonzero remainder $a$ satisfies $1\le a<p$. A common positive divisor of $a,p$ can only be one or $p$, by the [[素数|definition of a prime]]; $p$ cannot divide this $a$, so the gcd is one. The zero class is still excluded. A composite modulus can have invertible nonzero classes too: modulo six, both one and five are their own inverses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $p$ 为素数，标准非零余数 $a$ 满足 $1\le a<p$。按[[素数]]定义，$a,p$ 的正公因数只能是一或 $p$；这个 $a$ 不可能被 $p$ 整除，所以 gcd 为一。零类仍然排除。合数模数也可以有可逆的非零类：例如模六时，一和五都以自身为逆元。

> [!example] All six classes · 六个类逐一检查
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Modulo six, the standard representatives $0,1,2,3,4,5$ have gcds $6,1,2,3,2,1$ with the modulus. Exactly the representatives one and five are invertible: $1\cdot1\equiv1$ and $5\cdot5=25\equiv1\pmod6$. This tests the criterion on every class without confusing “nonzero” with “coprime.”
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 模六的标准代表 $0,1,2,3,4,5$ 与模数的 gcd 分别是 $6,1,2,3,2,1$。恰好一和五可逆：$1\cdot1\equiv1$，$5\cdot5=25\equiv1\pmod6$。这把判据用于每个类，同时区分“非零”与“互素”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a=0$ and $n\ge2$, the gcd is $n$, so there is no inverse. Negative inputs are allowed and use the same criterion, because $\gcd(a,n)=\gcd(|a|,n)$. Modulus one is outside the adopted scope. Failure of the inverse criterion means $ax\equiv1\pmod n$ has no solution; it does not say that every congruence with coefficient $a$ is unsolvable. For instance, $2x\equiv2\pmod6$ has $x=1$ as a solution; the general problem belongs to [[线性同余求解|solving linear congruences]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $a=0,n\ge2$ 时，gcd 等于 $n$，所以没有逆元。允许负输入，并使用同一个判据，因为 $\gcd(a,n)=\gcd(|a|,n)$；模一不在本卡约定范围内。判据失败说明 $ax\equiv1\pmod n$ 无解，却没有说所有以 $a$ 为系数的同余式都无解。例如 $2x\equiv2\pmod6$ 有解 $x=1$；一般问题由[[线性同余求解]]处理。
