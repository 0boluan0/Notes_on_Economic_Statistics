# 每个有限策略式博弈的混合扩展都至少存在一个纳什均衡，而纯策略存在性与连续策略博弈都需要额外条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Every finite strategic-form game has at least one Nash equilibrium in its mixed extension, whereas pure-strategy existence and continuous-strategy games require additional conditions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Nash 存在性定理保证的是：只要玩家数与每人的纯策略数有限，允许玩家使用[[混合策略]]后，至少存在一个 Nash 均衡。这个结论可能由纯策略均衡实现，也可能只能由真正的随机化实现；它不保证唯一、易算或会被实际学习过程选中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Nash existence theorem guarantees that a game with finitely many players and finitely many pure strategies has at least one equilibrium once [[混合策略|mixed strategies]] are allowed. The equilibrium may be degenerate and pure or may require genuine randomization. Existence does not imply uniqueness, computational ease, or convergence of actual learning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么有限混合扩展满足存在性结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the Finite Mixed Extension Has the Needed Structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个 $\Delta(S_i)$ 都是非空、紧致、凸的单纯形；期望收益对混合概率连续，并对玩家自己的混合策略线性。于是联合最佳反应对应满足固定点定理所需的非空、凸值与连续性条件；其固定点正是所有玩家同时最佳反应的策略组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each $\Delta(S_i)$ is a nonempty compact convex simplex. Expected payoffs are continuous in all probabilities and linear in a player's own mixture. The joint best-response correspondence therefore has the structure needed by a fixed-point theorem, and any fixed point is precisely a profile of mutual best responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它不保证纯策略均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*It Does Not Guarantee a Pure Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

配对硬币和石头剪刀布都没有纯策略 Nash 均衡，却有混合均衡。因此“有限博弈必有均衡”不能简写成“收益矩阵里必有一格双方都画线”。矩阵没有纯最佳反应交点时，应进入[[混合均衡求解|支持集与无差异检验]]，而不是宣布不存在均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Matching Pennies and Rock–Paper–Scissors have no pure Nash equilibrium but do have mixed equilibria. “Every finite game has an equilibrium” therefore does not mean that every payoff matrix contains a cell marked as a best response for both players. When no such cell exists, proceed to [[混合均衡求解|support and indifference analysis]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 连续策略博弈的一个常用充分条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Common Sufficient Condition for Continuous-Strategy Games*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若每个策略集合 $S_i$ 是欧氏空间中的非空、紧致、凸集，且每个 $u_i(s)$ 对整个 $s$ 连续、对自身策略 $s_i$ 准凹，则存在纯策略 Nash 均衡。这里每项条件都有工作：

- 紧致性与连续性帮助保证最佳反应能取到；
- 凸性与自身准凹性帮助保证最佳反应集合为凸；
- 固定点把各方最佳反应拼成相互一致的策略组合。

这是充分条件，不是必要条件；某个模型不满足其中一项，并不能单凭这一点断言均衡不存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A standard sufficient condition for a pure equilibrium is that each strategy set be nonempty, compact, and convex in Euclidean space, while every payoff is continuous in the full profile and quasiconcave in the player's own strategy. Compactness and continuity help best responses attain maxima; convexity and quasiconcavity make best-response sets convex; a fixed-point theorem makes those responses mutually consistent. These conditions are sufficient, not necessary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个有限矩阵没有纯策略 Nash 均衡。能否由此反驳 Nash 存在性定理？
>
> **答案：** 不能。定理针对有限博弈的混合扩展。没有纯均衡只说明任何均衡都必须让至少一名玩家真正随机化。
