<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Strong law of large numbers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*强大数定律*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine watching one endless sequence of coin tosses and recalculating the heads proportion after every toss. A **strong law of large numbers** says that, with probability one, this running average settles towards the target along that very sequence. Eventually it stays within any tolerance you choose, though a different realised sequence may take longer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

想象观察同一串无限继续的硬币投掷，并在每次投掷后重新计算正面比例。**强大数定律**说，以概率一，沿这同一条序列计算的平均值会趋近目标。对于你选的任何容忍值，它最终都会持续保持在范围内，不过不同的实际序列可能需要等不同长的时间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card states the classical **iid integrable version**. The observations $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ are [[独立同分布|independent and identically distributed]], and $E|X_1|<\infty$: their average absolute size is finite. Consequently their common [[期望|expectation]] $\mu=E[X_1]$ is a finite number. Define $S_n=\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$ and $\bar X_n=S_n/n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡陈述经典的**独立同分布且可积版本**。观测 $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ [[独立同分布]]，并满足 $E|X_1|<\infty$，即平均绝对大小有限。因此共同[[期望]] $\mu=E[X_1]$ 是有限数。记总和 $S_n=\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$，样本均值 $\bar X_n=S_n/n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One event containing the whole limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 定理的精确事件

> [!important] The iid integrable strong law · 可积 iid 强大数定律
> $$
> P\!\left(\left\{\omega:\lim_{n\to\infty}\bar X_n(\omega)=\mu\right\}\right)=1,\qquad \bar X_n\xrightarrow{a.s.}\mu.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The outcome $\omega$ specifies an entire run of the experiment. [[几乎必然收敛|Almost sure convergence]] means that on one event of probability one, **for every** $\varepsilon>0$ there is an $N(\omega,\varepsilon)$ such that **all** $n\ge N$ satisfy $|\bar X_n(\omega)-\mu|<\varepsilon$. The cutoff depends on the realised path as well as the tolerance.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 结果 $\omega$ 指定试验的一次完整运行。[[几乎必然收敛]]表示：在同一个概率为一的事件上，**每个** $\varepsilon>0$ 都对应某个 $N(\omega,\varepsilon)$，使**所有** $n\ge N$ 都满足 $|\bar X_n(\omega)-\mu|<\varepsilon$。这个起点既依赖容忍值，也依赖具体路径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[弱大数定律|weak law]] instead makes the error probability at each index tend to zero. The strong conclusion [[几乎必然收敛推出概率收敛|implies the weak conclusion]], but the converse does not follow from the meaning of probability convergence alone. “Strong” names the stronger conclusion; it does not mean a universal requirement of stronger moments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[弱大数定律]]让每个索引处的超差概率趋于零；强结论[[几乎必然收敛推出概率收敛|能够推出弱结论]]，但仅靠依概率收敛的含义不能倒推。“强”指结论的收敛方式更强，并不意味着所有版本都必须要求更高阶的矩。

> [!example] One path can remain exceptional · 可以有例外路径
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For independent fair-coin records, the theorem gives a limiting heads proportion of $1/2$ almost surely. On the path consisting entirely of heads, however, every average is $1$. That path has probability zero: its probability is at most $2^{-n}$ for every $n$. Thus probability one is compatible with an exceptional possible path. The theorem also does not say that the proportion moves monotonically towards $1/2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 独立公平硬币的正面记录几乎必然以 $1/2$ 为长期平均。然而在“永远都是正面”的路径上，每个平均值都是 $1$。这条路径概率为零，因为对每个 $n$，其概率都不超过 $2^{-n}$。所以概率一允许存在可能但概率为零的例外路径。定理也没有说正面比例会单调趋向 $1/2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Finite mean, possibly infinite variance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 条件边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite variance is sufficient but unnecessary. To see a concrete example, consider the nonnegative [[概率密度函数|density]] $f(x)=\tfrac32x^{-5/2}$ for $x\ge1$, and zero otherwise. Its integral is one. The mean and second moment are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限方差足够，却不是必要条件。具体看一个非负分布：[[概率密度函数|密度]]在 $x\ge1$ 时为 $f(x)=\tfrac32x^{-5/2}$，其他位置为零，其总积分为一。均值与二阶矩为

$$
E[X]=\frac32\int_1^\infty x^{-3/2}\,dx=3,\qquad E[X^2]=\frac32\int_1^\infty x^{-1/2}\,dx=\infty.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These [[反常积分|improper integrals]] are limits of integrals with a finite upper endpoint. Because $X\ge0$, $E|X|=3$ too. Iid draws from this distribution therefore have $\bar X_n\to3$ almost surely, despite infinite variance. A Chebyshev calculation using the variance would not establish this result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些[[反常积分]]是上端点有限的积分再取极限。由于 $X\ge0$，也有 $E|X|=3$。因此从这个分布独立同分布地抽样，尽管方差无穷，仍有 $\bar X_n\to3$ 几乎必然。直接把方差代进 Chebyshev 不等式无法证明这个结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The general proof handles the tail by truncation, controls a suitable subsequence of averages, and then fills the gaps between its indices; it does not assume the original second moments exist. Durrett’s version actually allows [[两两独立|pairwise independence]] with identical distributions and absolute integrability. The iid statement here is a direct special case. For other dependence structures or changing distributions, use a theorem with its own explicit assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般证明通过截断处理尾部，先控制一个合适子序列的平均，再补上索引间隙；它不假设原变量的二阶矩存在。Durrett 的版本实际上允许[[两两独立]]，再加同分布和绝对可积。本卡的 iid 陈述是直接特例。对于其他依赖结构或变化的分布，必须使用明确给出对应条件的定理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Neither the statement nor its probability-one event supplies a numerical error bound at a chosen finite $n$, a rate of convergence, or one deterministic cutoff that works for every path. For example, knowing $E[X_i]=2$, $E|X_i|<\infty$ and $E[X_i^2]=\infty$ is enough for the iid strong law, but not enough for a numerical bound on $P(|\bar X_{100}-2|>0.1)$. The target is the sampling distribution’s expectation; a systematic bias remains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定理陈述及其概率为一的事件本身，都没有提供指定有限 $n$ 下的数值误差界、收敛速度，或对所有路径有效的确定起点。例如 iid 情形中，$E[X_i]=2$、$E|X_i|<\infty$、$E[X_i^2]=\infty$ 已足以使用强大数定律，却不足以给出 $P(|\bar X_{100}-2|>0.1)$ 的数值上界。目标仍是抽样分布的期望；系统偏差不会因此消失。

## A heavy tail that defeats averaging
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不可积反例：Cauchy 平均不会稳定

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The standard Cauchy distribution has density $f(x)=1/[\pi(1+x^2)]$ on the real line. Its positive and negative parts both have infinite expectation, so its symmetric shape does **not** give an ordinary expectation of zero. In particular $E|X|=\infty$. For iid standard Cauchy observations, a special addition property gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准 Cauchy 分布在实数轴上的密度为 $f(x)=1/[\pi(1+x^2)]$。它的正部和负部期望都无穷，所以对称形状**不能**赋予它通常意义下的零期望，且 $E|X|=\infty$。对独立同分布的标准 Cauchy 观测，其特殊的加法性质给出

$$
\frac{X_1+\cdots+X_n}{n}\overset d=X_1,\qquad P(|\bar X_n|>1)=\frac12\quad\text{for every }n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $\overset d=$ means equal distributions, not equal realised values. The second equality follows by integrating the density over $[-1,1]$, which gives probability $1/2$. The distribution never narrows around a constant; for any finite proposed target there remains a positive probability outside a fixed neighbourhood. Thus there is no probability convergence, and hence no almost-sure convergence, to a finite constant. The addition property is verified in the cited Cauchy exercise; it is not a property of arbitrary heavy-tailed distributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\overset d=$ 表示分布相同，不是每次实际取值相同。第二个等式来自密度在 $[-1,1]$ 上积分为 $1/2$。这个分布始终没有围绕常数收窄；对任意有限目标，其固定邻域外仍有正概率。因此它不依概率、更不几乎必然收敛到有限常数。这里的加法性质由所引 Cauchy 习题核验，并非任意厚尾分布都有。
