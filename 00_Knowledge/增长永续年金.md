# 增长永续年金使用下一期现金流且要求贴现率大于增长率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A growing perpetuity uses next-period cash flow and requires the discount rate to exceed growth*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子公式
> 若下一期现金流为 $C_1$，以后每期按固定率 $g$ 永久增长，且匹配的每期贴现率为 $r$，则
> $$PV_0=\frac{C_1}{r-g},\qquad r>g.$$
> 分子是下一期现金流，不是最近一期已实现的 $C_0$；若给的是 $C_0$，先算 $C_1=C_0(1+g)$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> If next period's cash flow is $C_1$ and it grows forever at constant rate $g$, with matching per-period discount rate $r$, then
> $$PV_0=\frac{C_1}{r-g},\qquad r>g.$$
> The numerator is next period's cash flow, not the most recently realised $C_0$. If $C_0$ is given, first compute $C_1=C_0(1+g)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件 $r>g$ 保证折现后的无限几何级数收敛。数学收敛还不等于经济合理：永久增长率必须与长期可持续规模一致；一个主体不可能永远以高于整个经济的速度增长而不最终超过经济本身。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The condition $r>g$ makes the discounted infinite geometric series converge. Mathematical convergence is not enough for economic plausibility: perpetual growth must be sustainable at long-run scale. An entity cannot outgrow the entire economy forever without eventually becoming larger than it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 公司刚支付 $C_0=10$，预计以后永久增长 3%，贴现率 8%。为什么现值不是 $10/(0.08-0.03)$？
>
> **答案：** 公式需要下一期现金流，所以分子应为 $C_1=10(1.03)=10.3$，现值为 $10.3/0.05=206$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A firm has just paid $C_0=10$, expected to grow forever at 3%, with an 8% discount rate. Why is value not $10/(0.08-0.03)$?
>
> **Answer:** The formula requires next period's cash flow: $C_1=10(1.03)=10.3$, so value is $10.3/0.05=206$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
