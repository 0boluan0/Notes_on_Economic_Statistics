# Modular inverse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*模逆元*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying by three modulo seven can be undone by multiplying by five: $3\cdot5=15$ leaves remainder one. A **modular inverse** is a multiplier that restores this multiplicative identity. It depends on the chosen modulus, and some multipliers have no inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模七时，乘三可以通过再乘五撤销：$3\cdot5=15$ 除以七余一。**模逆元**就是把乘法恢复到单位元的乘数。它依赖指定的模数，并不是每个乘数都有逆元。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The equation to check
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*要检查的等式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix an integer modulus $n\ge2$. The [[同余|congruence]] $x\equiv y\pmod n$ means that $x-y$ is an integer multiple of $n$. Its residue class $[x]_n$ collects all integers with that remainder. In [[模运算|modular arithmetic]], multiply representatives and then take the class of the product; $[1]_n$ is the multiplicative identity because $[1]_n[x]_n=[x]_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定整数模数 $n\ge2$。[[同余]] $x\equiv y\pmod n$ 表示 $x-y$ 是 $n$ 的整数倍；同余类 $[x]_n$ 收集余数相同的所有整数。[[模运算]]先把代表元相乘，再取乘积的类；$[1]_n$ 是乘法单位元，因为 $[1]_n[x]_n=[x]_n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an integer $a$ and an integer modulus $n>1$, an integer $u$ satisfying $au\equiv1\pmod n$ is called a **multiplicative inverse of $a$ modulo $n$**, more precisely an integer representing the inverse residue class. It is denoted $a^{-1}\pmod n$, usually with $0\le u<n$. This notation does not mean the real reciprocal $1/a$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对整数 $a$ 和整数模数 $n>1$，若整数 $u$ 满足 $au\equiv1\pmod n$，就称 $u$（更准确地说，它的同余类）为 **$a$ 模 $n$ 的乘法逆元**，记作 $a^{-1}\pmod n$。通常选取 $0\le u<n$ 的代表元；这个记号不是实数倒数 $1/a$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The defining equation can therefore be written $[a]_n[u]_n=[1]_n$. To check a proposed integer $u$, compute $au-1$ and verify that it is a multiple of $n$. Ordinary integer equality $au=1$ is not required. For $a=3,n=7,u=5$, the difference is $15-1=14=2\cdot7$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义式也就是 $[a]_n[u]_n=[1]_n$。检查候选整数 $u$ 时，计算 $au-1$，确认它是 $n$ 的整数倍；不要求普通整数等式 $au=1$。例如 $a=3,n=7,u=5$ 时，$15-1=14=2\cdot7$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One inverse class, many representatives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个逆元类，多个代表*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an inverse exists, its residue class is unique. If $au\equiv av\equiv1\pmod n$, then $u\equiv u(av)\equiv(ua)v\equiv v\pmod n$. Integer representatives may differ: because $3\cdot5=15\equiv1\pmod7$, the integers five, twelve and negative two represent the same inverse of three modulo seven.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逆元若存在，其同余类唯一。因为若 $au\equiv av\equiv1\pmod n$，则 $u\equiv u(av)\equiv(ua)v\equiv v\pmod n$。整数代表元可以不同，例如 $3\cdot5=15\equiv1\pmod7$，所以 5、12、$-2$ 都代表 $3$ 模 7 的同一个逆元。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here is each step of the uniqueness argument. Assume $u$ and $v$ both satisfy the definition. Replace one by $av$ because $av\equiv1$, regroup the product, and replace $ua$ by one because $ua\equiv1$. All congruences use the same modulus:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把唯一性论证逐步展开：假设 $u,v$ 都满足定义。由 $av\equiv1$，可把一换成 $av$；重新结合乘积，再由 $ua\equiv1$ 把它换成一。下面始终使用同一个模数：

$$u\equiv u\cdot1\equiv u(av)=(ua)v\equiv1\cdot v=v\pmod n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This proof multiplies known congruences and uses associativity; it does not cancel $a$ by an unproved rule. Also, if $u$ works, then $u+kn$ works for every integer $k$, since $a(u+kn)-1=(au-1)+akn$. Conversely, uniqueness shows that every inverse representative differs from $u$ by a multiple of $n$. Thus there is one inverse class and one standard representative in $0,\ldots,n-1$, but infinitely many integer representatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个证明只把已知同余式相乘并使用结合律，没有凭未经证明的规则约去 $a$。此外，若 $u$ 可用，那么每个整数 $k$ 对应的 $u+kn$ 也可用，因为 $a(u+kn)-1=(au-1)+akn$。反过来，唯一性说明所有逆元代表都与 $u$ 相差 $n$ 的整数倍。因此逆元类只有一个，$0,\ldots,n-1$ 中的标准代表只有一个，但整数代表有无穷多个。

> [!example] Seven candidates · 七个候选
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $a=3$ modulo $7$, checking all seven standard candidates gives the following remainders. Only $u=5$ produces one. The integers $12=5+7$ and $-2=5-7$ name that same answer.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对模 $7$ 的 $a=3$，逐个检查七个标准候选，得到下面的余数。只有 $u=5$ 产生一；$12=5+7$ 与 $-2=5-7$ 表示同一个答案。

$$\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrr}u&0&1&2&3&4&5&6\\\operatorname{rem}(3u,7)&0&3&6&2&5&1&4\end{array}$$

## Undo multiplication, with the modulus fixed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*在固定模数下撤销乘法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Dividing by $a$” modulo $n$ means multiplying by this inverse, not ordinary integer division. A nonzero residue need not have an inverse: $2u$ is always even and cannot be congruent to one modulo six. The [[模逆元存在条件|inverse-existence criterion]] determines when an inverse exists, and the [[扩展欧几里得算法|extended Euclidean algorithm]] computes one when it does.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模意义的“除以 $a$”是乘这个逆元，并非普通整数除法。并不是每个非零余数都有逆元：例如 $2u$ 总是偶数，不可能与 1 模 6 同余。是否存在逆元由 [[模逆元存在条件]] 判断；存在时可用 [[扩展欧几里得算法]] 求出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $3x\equiv4\pmod7$ becomes $x\equiv5\cdot4\equiv6\pmod7$ after multiplication by the inverse five. Substitution checks $3\cdot6=18\equiv4\pmod7$. The same integer three has no inverse modulo six: every $3u$ is divisible by three, whereas an integer congruent to one modulo six has the form $1+6k$ and is not. The modulus is part of the question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，把 $3x\equiv4\pmod7$ 两边乘逆元五，得到 $x\equiv5\cdot4\equiv6\pmod7$。代回可核对 $3\cdot6=18\equiv4\pmod7$。同一个整数三却没有模六逆元：每个 $3u$ 都被三整除，而模六余一的整数形如 $1+6k$，不能被三整除。模数是问题的一部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero has no inverse for any allowed modulus: $0u-1=-1$ cannot be divisible by $n\ge2$. This card adopts the course convention $n>1$; it makes no assertion about the separate one-class convention at modulus one. Negative integers are allowed as representatives, as the example $u=-2$ already shows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任何允许的模数，零都没有逆元，因为 $0u-1=-1$ 不能被 $n\ge2$ 整除。本卡采用课程的 $n>1$ 约定，不把模一的单类约定混入此处。负整数可以作代表，前面的 $u=-2$ 已经给出例子。
