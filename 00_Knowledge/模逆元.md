# a 模 n 的逆元是乘以 a 后同余于 1 的整数所代表的同余类
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An inverse of a modulo n is the residue class of an integer whose product with a is congruent to one*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对整数 $a$ 和整数模数 $n>1$，若整数 $u$ 满足 $au\equiv1\pmod n$，就称 $u$（更准确地说，它的同余类）为 **$a$ 模 $n$ 的乘法逆元**，记作 $a^{-1}\pmod n$。通常选取 $0\le u<n$ 的代表元；这个记号不是实数倒数 $1/a$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an integer $a$ and an integer modulus $n>1$, an integer $u$ satisfying $au\equiv1\pmod n$ is called a **multiplicative inverse of $a$ modulo $n$**, more precisely an integer representing the inverse residue class. It is denoted $a^{-1}\pmod n$, usually with $0\le u<n$. This notation does not mean the real reciprocal $1/a$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逆元若存在，其同余类唯一。因为若 $au\equiv av\equiv1\pmod n$，则 $u\equiv u(av)\equiv(ua)v\equiv v\pmod n$。整数代表元可以不同，例如 $3\cdot5=15\equiv1\pmod7$，所以 5、12、$-2$ 都代表 $3$ 模 7 的同一个逆元。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an inverse exists, its residue class is unique. If $au\equiv av\equiv1\pmod n$, then $u\equiv u(av)\equiv(ua)v\equiv v\pmod n$. Integer representatives may differ: because $3\cdot5=15\equiv1\pmod7$, the integers five, twelve and negative two represent the same inverse of three modulo seven.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模意义的“除以 $a$”是乘这个逆元，并非普通整数除法。并不是每个非零余数都有逆元：例如 $2u$ 总是偶数，不可能与 1 模 6 同余。是否存在逆元由 [[模逆元存在条件]] 判断；存在时可用 [[扩展欧几里得算法]] 求出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Dividing by $a$” modulo $n$ means multiplying by this inverse, not ordinary integer division. A nonzero residue need not have an inverse: $2u$ is always even and cannot be congruent to one modulo six. The [[模逆元存在条件|inverse-existence criterion]] determines when an inverse exists, and the [[扩展欧几里得算法|extended Euclidean algorithm]] computes one when it does.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
