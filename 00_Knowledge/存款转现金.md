# 公众把存款换成现金会改变银行流动资产结构而货币基础不必改变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Converting a deposit into cash changes bank liquid-asset composition without necessarily changing the monetary base*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子机制
> 客户从银行提现时，客户存款资产与银行存款负债下降，公众手中现金上升。若银行直接交付已有库存现金，银行库存现金下降而央行账户准备金当下不变；银行以后从央行补充纸币时，准备金余额再转成库存现金。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When a customer withdraws cash, the customer's deposit asset and the bank's deposit liability fall while cash held by the public rises. If the bank hands over existing vault cash, vault cash falls and its central-bank reserve balance is initially unchanged; when the bank later replenishes notes from the central bank, reserve balances are converted into vault cash.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在美国官方简化记账中，银行从 Federal Reserve 取得纸币会使其 reserve balance 下降、Federal Reserve notes outside Reserve Banks 上升；Federal Reserve 总负债在其他条件不变时不变。由于美国货币基础是流通中通货加准备金余额，这种一增一减不必改变货币基础总量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the official U.S. simplified accounting, obtaining notes from the Federal Reserve reduces the bank's reserve balance and increases Federal Reserve notes outside Reserve Banks, leaving total Federal Reserve liabilities unchanged, all else equal. Because the U.S. monetary base is currency in circulation plus reserve balances, the offsetting changes need not alter the total base.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 客户在柜台拿到库存现金与银行向央行补充库存是两个可以分开的步骤。
- 在现行美国 H.6 下，若从一个计入 M1 的存款等额提现为计入 M1 的公众通货，M1 只改变构成、总量不变；若原存款不在 M1、通货被排除、发生手续费或伴随其他交易，结论须重算。
- 货币基础不变不等于商业银行流动性位置不变：可用于电子跨行结算的 reserve balance 可能下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Delivery of existing vault cash to the customer and later replenishment from the central bank are separable steps.
- Under current U.S. H.6 definitions, an equal withdrawal from an M1-included deposit into M1-included public currency changes only M1's composition, not its total. Recalculate if the original deposit is outside M1, the currency is excluded, a fee is charged, or other transactions occur.
- An unchanged monetary base does not mean the bank's liquidity position is unchanged: reserve balances available for electronic interbank settlement may fall.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 银行从央行取出纸币补充金库时，为什么货币基础可以不变？
>
> **答案：** 准备金余额下降与流通中通货上升可以等额抵消；改变的是货币基础内部构成。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can the monetary base remain unchanged when a bank obtains notes from the central bank to replenish its vault?
>
> **Answer:** The fall in reserve balances can be offset by the rise in currency in circulation; the composition of the base changes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
