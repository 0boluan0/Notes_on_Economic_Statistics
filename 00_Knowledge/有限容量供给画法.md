# 常数单位成本与有限产能给出价格阈值上的水平段和阈值之间的竖直段

画有限容量的供给曲线，先固定坐标：**纵轴是价格 $P$，横轴是总产量 $Q$**。然后逐个价格问经营者愿意生产多少。不要先凭“阶梯”二字决定哪一段水平；价格不变而数量变化才是水平段，数量固定而价格变化则是竖直段。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To draw supply with finite capacities, first put **price $P$ on the vertical axis and total output $Q$ on the horizontal axis**. Then determine the output supplied at each price. A constant-price range of quantities is horizontal; a fixed quantity over a price range is vertical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个粮食市场的独立教学模型。三类土地各有每期 10 吨的容量，同质粮食的无地租单位成本分别为 2、4、6 货币／吨。土地可在容量内分割使用；没有另待支付的固定成本，单位成本包括其他投入和正常资本报酬。生产者接受价格，土地没有其他用途收入。本例先固定工资等成本，不同时求解人口和工资反馈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a constructed grain market. Three land grades each have capacity of 10 tonnes per period and non-rent unit costs of 2, 4 and 6 currency units per tonne. Output is divisible within capacity. Unit cost includes other inputs and the normal return to capital, with no separate fixed cost. Producers take price as given and land has no alternative-use income. Wages and other costs are held fixed rather than jointly determined through population feedback.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设第 $i$ 类产量为 $q_i$，容量为 $\bar q_i$。在这些条件下，多生产一吨的剩余是 $P-c_i$，于是供给选择为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For grade $i$, write output as $q_i$ and capacity as $\bar q_i$. An extra tonne yields a surplus of $P-c_i$, so its supply choices are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
q_i(P)=
\begin{cases}
0,&P<c_i,\\
[0,\bar q_i],&P=c_i,\\
\bar q_i,&P>c_i.
\end{cases}
$$

等于成本时，经营者对容量内各产量无差异，具体产量由市场需求决定。将三类供给加总，就得到完整的图形依据：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a cost threshold, the producer is indifferent among outputs within capacity; market demand selects the actual quantity. Adding the three grades gives the complete supply correspondence:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 价格 $P$ | 总供给 $Q$ | 在 $P$ 纵、$Q$ 横坐标上的形状 |
|---|---|---|
| $P<2$ | 0 | 数量为零 |
| $P=2$ | $[0,10]$ | 水平段 |
| $2<P<4$ | 10 | 竖直段 |
| $P=4$ | $[10,20]$ | 水平段 |
| $4<P<6$ | 20 | 竖直段 |
| $P=6$ | $[20,30]$ | 水平段 |
| $P>6$ | 30 | 总容量上的竖直段 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Below price 2, output is zero. At prices 2, 4 and 6, the respective horizontal quantity ranges are 0–10, 10–20 and 20–30. Between thresholds, output is fixed at 10 or 20; above 6 it is fixed at total capacity 30. These fixed-output ranges are vertical in the stated axes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如反需求为 $P=5.5-0.1Q$，交点在 $P=4$ 的水平段：$Q=15$，第一类生产 10、第二类生产 5、第三类不生产。若交点在 $Q=10$ 的竖直段，需求右移却可能只提高价格，不增加产量。它也说明为何不能从最高活跃土地直接推出零租，见 [[边际土地零租条件]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For inverse demand $P=5.5-0.1Q$, equilibrium lies on the horizontal segment at $P=4$: output is 15, with grades producing 10, 5 and zero. On the vertical segment at $Q=10$, a demand increase may raise price without raising output. This also explains the qualification in [[边际土地零租条件|the zero-rent margin condition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ricardo（1821），[《原理》II.4–15](https://www.econlib.org/library/Ricardo/ricP.html?chapter_num=3#2.4)：提供生产条件不同、竞争和新增粮食生产边际的理论背景；原书不包含本卡这条有限容量供给曲线。
- 分段对应关系直接由 $0\le q_i\le10$ 下最大化 $(P-c_i)q_i$ 推导。容量、单位成本和需求函数均为独立教学构造；$P=4,Q=15$ 已回代需求并检查每类产量可行性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ricardo supplies the background of heterogeneous production conditions and a competitive margin, not this finite-capacity curve. The correspondence follows directly from the stated constrained surplus problem. All parameters are constructed, and the equilibrium has been checked against demand and capacities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
