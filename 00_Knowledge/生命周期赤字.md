# 生命周期赤字是某年龄人口的消费超过其劳动收入的差额

国民转移账户中的生命周期赤字衡量消费与劳动收入的差额。消费超过劳动收入时为正；劳动收入超过消费时为负，也称生命周期盈余。它针对指定时期的年龄组，而不是自动表示一个人一生累计欠下的债。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In National Transfer Accounts, a life cycle deficit is consumption minus labour income. It is positive when consumption exceeds labour income and negative—a life cycle surplus—when labour income exceeds consumption. It refers to an age group in a specified period, not automatically to a person’s cumulative lifetime debt.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
LCD_a=C_a-Y^L_a.
$$

同一式可用年龄组总量或年龄组人均值，但两项必须使用同一种口径。消费包括按 NTA 口径分配给该年龄组的私人和公共消费；劳动收入包括雇员及自雇劳动所得，不包括养老金转移或资产收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation works for group totals or per-capita values, provided both terms use the same basis. Consumption includes private and public consumption allocated to the age group under NTA conventions. Labour income includes employee and self-employment labour income, not pension transfers or asset income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如某年龄组人均消费 100、劳动收入 20，则生命周期赤字为 80。即使这 80 全由自己的资产收入提供，指标仍为 80，因此正赤字不等于贫困或依赖政府。如何筹得这部分资源由[[老年消费的筹资]]的完整账户回答，不能把它直接当作财政赤字。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an age group consumes 100 per person and receives labour income of 20, its life cycle deficit is 80. It remains 80 even if assets fully fund the gap, so a positive deficit does not establish poverty or government dependence. The complete account in [[老年消费的筹资|financing old-age consumption]] identifies the resources; the gap is not a fiscal deficit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
