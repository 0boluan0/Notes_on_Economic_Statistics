# Ridge regularization
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*岭正则化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ridge regularization fits data while charging a cost for large coefficients. Ordinary [[线性最小二乘|least squares]] chooses coefficients only to make predicted and observed outputs close. Ridge adds a second preference: among possible fits, it is willing to accept some mismatch to keep the coefficient vector shorter. This can be useful when very large, mutually cancelling coefficients would make a fit sensitive to small changes in the data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

岭正则化在拟合数据的同时，对过大的系数收取代价。普通[[线性最小二乘]]只根据预测输出与观测输出的差距来选系数；岭增加一个偏好：愿意接受部分拟合误差，以换取更短的系数向量。当很大且相互抵消的系数使拟合对数据微小变化敏感时，这种取舍可能有用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A smaller coefficient can be worth some fitting error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a single measurement is $b=2$, and the model predicts $x$. Least squares minimizes $(x-2)^2$, choosing $x=2$. Give coefficient size the same weight as fitting error and minimize
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设只有一个测量值 $b=2$，模型预测为 $x$。最小二乘最小化 $(x-2)^2$，选出 $x=2$。若让系数大小与拟合误差具有相同权重，就改为最小化

$$(x-2)^2+x^2=2(x-1)^2+2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The minimum is at $x=1$. Choosing $x=2$ gives zero fitting error but a penalty of 4; choosing $x=1$ gives fitting error 1 and penalty 1, for a smaller total of 2. Choosing zero is too severe here: its fitting error alone is 4. Ridge balances the two costs rather than simply forcing the coefficient to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小值在 $x=1$ 取得。选 $x=2$ 时拟合误差为零，惩罚却为4；选 $x=1$ 时拟合误差与惩罚各为1，总代价只有2。直接选零又过于激进，因为仅拟合误差就为4。岭是在两项代价之间取舍，并非直接把系数强制归零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## State the penalty before solving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先说明惩罚什么，再求解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a fixed real or complex $m\times n$ matrix, with $m,n\ge1$. Its $n$ input coefficients form the vector $x$, its $m$ observed outputs form $b$, and $Ax$ is the model's output. The [[欧氏范数|squared Euclidean length]] $\|x\|_2^2$ adds the squared absolute values of its coordinates. In the standard, all-coordinate version, choose a fixed number $\lambda>0$ and define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 是固定的实或复 $m\times n$ 矩阵，$m,n\ge1$。$n$ 个输入系数组成向量 $x$，$m$ 个观测输出组成 $b$，而 $Ax$ 是模型输出。[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度平方]] $\|x\|_2^2$ 把各坐标绝对值的平方相加。在标准的全部坐标受罚版本中，选定常数 $\lambda>0$，定义

$$\hat x_\lambda=\arg\min_x J_\lambda(x),\qquad
J_\lambda(x)=\|Ax-b\|_2^2+\lambda\|x\|_2^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\arg\min$ means the coefficient vector giving the smallest objective, and $\hat x_\lambda$ denotes that chosen vector. The first term measures mismatch; the second charges for **every coordinate** of $x$. Larger $\lambda$ puts more weight on small coefficients. This is also called zeroth-order Tikhonov regularization. A penalty on differences or derivatives of coefficients is a different Tikhonov specification. The definition itself needs no random sampling or Gaussian assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\arg\min$ 表示让目标最小的系数向量，$\hat x_\lambda$ 表示选出的向量。第一项度量拟合差距，第二项惩罚 $x$ 的**每个坐标**；$\lambda$ 越大，越重视小系数。这也称为零阶 Tikhonov 正则化。若改为惩罚系数之间的差分或导数，则是另一种 Tikhonov 设定。定义本身无需随机抽样或 Gaussian 假设。

> [!important] A unique answer even with dependent columns / 列相关时也有唯一答案
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The minimizer satisfies
> $$(A^*A+\lambda I_n)\hat x_\lambda=A^*b.$$
> The star is [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]] (ordinary transpose for real entries), and $I_n$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. The matrix on the left is invertible for every $\lambda>0$, even when $A$ is rank deficient.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 极小点满足上述方程。星号表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]，实矩阵时就是普通转置；$I_n$ 是[[单位矩阵]]。只要 $\lambda>0$，左侧矩阵就可逆，即使 $A$ 秩亏。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the guarantee, set $H=A^*A+\lambda I_n$. For every nonzero vector $z$,
$$z^*Hz=\|Az\|_2^2+\lambda\|z\|_2^2>0.$$
Thus $H$ is [[正定矩阵|positive definite]]. If $\hat x_\lambda=H^{-1}A^*b$, expanding the objective around that vector gives
$$J_\lambda(x)=J_\lambda(\hat x_\lambda)+(x-\hat x_\lambda)^*H(x-\hat x_\lambda).$$
The last term is positive unless $x=\hat x_\lambda$, proving existence and uniqueness without needing independent columns. If $A=0$, the answer is zero. At $\lambda=0$, the penalty disappears and ordinary least squares may have multiple minimizing coefficient vectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $H=A^*A+\lambda I_n$。任何非零向量 $z$ 都满足上面的正值关系，所以 $H$ 是[[正定矩阵]]。取 $\hat x_\lambda=H^{-1}A^*b$，在该向量处展开目标，便得到显示的平方差恒等式。除非 $x=\hat x_\lambda$，额外项都为正，因此无需列线性无关也能证明极小点存在且唯一。若 $A=0$，答案为零；若直接取 $\lambda=0$，惩罚消失，普通最小二乘可能有多个最小化系数向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation characterizes the answer; it is not an instruction to form an inverse numerically. An equivalent least-squares problem stacks $A$ above $\sqrt\lambda I_n$ and stacks $b$ above a zero vector. It can be solved using an appropriate [[QR分解|QR factorization]]. The separate [[岭的SVD滤波公式|ridge SVD formula]] explains how the same rule acts in each singular direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该方程刻画答案，并不要求数值实现时显式求逆。等价的最小二乘问题把 $A$ 与 $\sqrt\lambda I_n$ 上下堆叠，右端把 $b$ 与零向量堆叠，可用适当的 [[QR分解]]求解。独立的[[岭的SVD滤波公式]]说明同一规则如何作用于各个奇异方向。

## Intercepts, units and parameter conventions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*截距、单位与参数约定*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In ordinary unweighted real regression, an intercept is often exempt from the penalty. Write the fitted response as $a\mathbf1+Z\beta$: $a$ is the intercept, $\mathbf1$ is a column of ones, the columns of $Z$ are predictors, and $\beta$ contains their slopes. Let $y$ denote the observed response vector. Then the objective is
$$\|a\mathbf1+Z\beta-y\|_2^2+\lambda\|\beta\|_2^2.$$
Only the slopes are penalized. For any fixed $\beta$, the best intercept is $a=\bar y-\bar z^T\beta$, where $\bar y$ is the response mean and $\bar z$ lists predictor-column means. [[中心化矩阵|Subtracting these column means]] reduces the problem to ridge on the centered response and predictors; recover $a$ afterwards. Applying the all-coordinate formula directly to $[\mathbf1\ Z]$ would penalize the intercept too and solve a different problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通未加权实数回归中，截距通常免罚。把拟合响应写为 $a\mathbf1+Z\beta$：$a$ 是截距，$\mathbf1$ 是全1列，$Z$ 的各列是解释变量，$\beta$ 包含相应斜率。令 $y$ 表示观测响应向量；此时目标如上，只有斜率受罚。固定 $\beta$ 后，最优截距为 $a=\bar y-\bar z^T\beta$，其中 $\bar y$ 是响应均值，$\bar z$ 收集解释变量各列均值。[[中心化矩阵|分别减去这些列均值]]，即可对中心化后的响应和解释变量做岭，再恢复 $a$。直接对完整设计 $[\mathbf1\ Z]$ 套用全部坐标受罚公式，会连截距一起惩罚，求解另一个问题。

> [!example] Keep the response level / 保留响应水平
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For predictor values $t=(-1,0,1)^T$ and responses $y=(9,10,11)^T$, take $\lambda=2$. With an unpenalized intercept, $a=10$ and the slope is $2/(2+2)=1/2$. If the intercept is penalized by the same weight, it instead becomes $30/(3+2)=6$. The convention changes even the overall fitted level.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 解释变量取 $t=(-1,0,1)^T$，响应为 $y=(9,10,11)^T$，取 $\lambda=2$。截距免罚时，$a=10$，斜率为 $2/(2+2)=1/2$；若截距也按同一权重受罚，它就变成 $30/(3+2)=6$。两种约定甚至会改变整体拟合水平。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The units matter too. Replacing a predictor $t$ by $100t$ represents the same fitted contribution with a coefficient one hundredth as large, whose squared penalty is one ten-thousandth as large. Keeping the same $\lambda$ therefore changes the tradeoff. Centering changes the origin; it does not standardize the units. Choose the scale and penalty together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位同样重要。把解释变量 $t$ 改为 $100t$ 后，相同拟合贡献只需原来百分之一的系数，其平方惩罚为原来的万分之一。因此沿用同一 $\lambda$ 会改变取舍。中心化只是改变原点，不会把单位标准化；尺度与惩罚应一起明确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some sources write $\alpha^2\|x\|_2^2$ instead: then this card's $\lambda$ equals $\alpha^2$. If the fitting term is averaged over $m$ observations, $\|Ax-b\|_2^2/m+\lambda\|x\|_2^2$, its sum-loss penalty weight is $m\lambda$. The symbol alone does not specify the strength.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有些来源把惩罚写为 $\alpha^2\|x\|_2^2$，此时本卡的 $\lambda=\alpha^2$。若拟合项取 $m$ 个观测的平均，即 $\|Ax-b\|_2^2/m+\lambda\|x\|_2^2$，换成求和损失后惩罚权重为 $m\lambda$。只看参数名称无法确定强度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A changed estimation rule is not an accuracy guarantee
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 含义边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ridge changes the optimization rule. It may reduce [[小奇异值放大噪声|amplification of data noise]], but it can also shrink genuine signal: if the opening measurement $b=2$ is already exact and the true input is 2, choosing 1 introduces error. Claims about [[MSE偏差方差分解|bias, variance and mean squared error]] need a specified noise model and a recovery or prediction target. A scientific quantity of interest need not change when its estimator changes; what changes here is how the estimate is chosen. Neither lower prediction error nor the original least-squares inference guarantees follow just from adding a penalty. Compare the alternative of deleting directions in [[截断SVD与岭正则化|TSVD versus ridge]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

岭改变的是优化规则。它可能减弱[[小奇异值放大噪声|数据噪声的放大]]，也可能收缩真实信号：若开头的 $b=2$ 已经完全准确、真实输入就是2，改选1反而引入误差。关于[[MSE偏差方差分解|偏差、方差与均方误差]]的结论，需要指定噪声模型及恢复或预测目标。估计方法改变时，科学上关心的目标量未必改变；这里改变的是如何选出估计值。加入惩罚本身既不保证更低预测误差，也不让新系数自动继承原最小二乘推断保证。另一种删除方向的办法见[[截断SVD与岭正则化]]。
