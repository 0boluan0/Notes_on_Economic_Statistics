# 岭正则化在最小二乘目标中加入二范数惩罚
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Ridge regularization adds a squared-norm penalty to least squares*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 给定 $\lambda>0$，岭正则化（也称零阶 Tikhonov 正则化）把线性最小二乘改为
> $$
> \hat x_\lambda
> =\arg\min_x\bigl\{\|Ax-b\|_2^2+\lambda\|x\|_2^2\bigr\}.
> $$
> 第一项要求拟合数据，第二项惩罚过大的系数范数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $\lambda>0$, ridge regularization, or zeroth-order Tikhonov regularization, replaces linear least squares by $\hat x_\lambda=\arg\min_x\{\|Ax-b\|_2^2+\lambda\|x\|_2^2\}$. The first term fits the data; the second penalizes a large coefficient norm.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $A$ 为实矩阵时，一阶条件给出
$$
(A^TA+\lambda I)\hat x_\lambda=A^Tb.
$$
$\lambda>0$ 使 $A^TA+\lambda I$ 正定，因此目标严格凸并有唯一极小点。它如何逐个改变奇异方向的系数，是独立的 [[岭的SVD滤波公式]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real matrix, the first-order condition is $(A^TA+\lambda I)\hat x_\lambda=A^Tb$. The positive penalty makes $A^TA+\lambda I$ positive definite, so the objective is strictly convex and has a unique minimiser. Its direction-by-direction spectral action is given separately in [[岭的SVD滤波公式|the SVD filter formula for ridge]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对标量问题 $A=[1]$、$b=2$、$\lambda=1$，目标为
$$
(x-2)^2+x^2,
$$
其唯一极小点是 $x=1$。无惩罚最小二乘解为 $2$；加入惩罚后，较小系数范数与拟合误差之间发生取舍。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the scalar problem $A=[1]$, $b=2$, and $\lambda=1$, the objective is $(x-2)^2+x^2$ and its unique minimiser is $x=1$. The unpenalised least-squares solution is $2$, so the penalty trades fit against a smaller coefficient norm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 含义边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Meaning boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非零矩阵使用 $\lambda>0$ 时，岭解不是“更稳定地计算同一个精确伪逆”，而是改变了优化目标。它用系数偏差换取较小的方差和更稳定的反演。$\lambda$ 依赖噪声、变量尺度和下游目标；它是建模参数，不是无害的数值开关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonzero matrix and $\lambda>0$, ridge is not a more stable computation of the same exact pseudoinverse; it changes the optimization problem. It trades coefficient bias for lower variance and a more stable inverse. The choice of $\lambda$ depends on noise, variable scaling, and the downstream objective. It is a modelling parameter, not a harmless numerical switch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 为什么 $\lambda>0$ 的岭不能称为原矩阵伪逆的“纯数值修复”？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why is ridge with $\lambda>0$ not merely a numerical repair of the original pseudoinverse?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 它在目标函数中加入了新惩罚，并改变每个正奇异方向的滤波因子，因而改变了估计目标。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** It adds a new penalty to the objective and changes the filter factor in every positive singular direction, so it changes the estimation target.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
