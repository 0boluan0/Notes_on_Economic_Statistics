# 福利经济学与政府干预
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Welfare Economics and Government Intervention*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 建立评价资源配置和公共政策的语言：先描述行为与后果，再说明用什么价值标准判断“更好”。
> **具体锚点：** 限价可能让部分租户房租降低，却造成短缺和非价格配给；实证效果与是否值得实施是两层问题。
> **核心难点：** Pareto 标准回避人与人之间福利权衡，社会福利函数则必须显式引入价值判断；市场失灵也不自动证明政府能更好。
> **为什么重要：** 公共品、外部性、税收和社会保险的政策分析都需要这套基准。
> **继续：** 先分清实证/规范与效率/公平，再进入 [[公共品、搭便车与 Samuelson 条件|公共品与外部性]] 和 [[税收归宿与超额负担]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Establish a language for evaluating allocations and public policy: first describe behavior and consequences, then state the value criterion used to judge what is better.
> **Concrete anchor:** Rent control may reduce rent for some tenants while creating shortages and non-price rationing; its positive effects and whether it is desirable are separate questions.
> **Central difficulty:** The Pareto criterion avoids interpersonal welfare comparisons, while a social welfare function explicitly introduces value judgments. Market failure alone does not prove that government will perform better.
> **Why it matters:** Policy analysis of public goods, externalities, taxation, and social insurance requires this benchmark.
> **Continue with:** Separate positive from normative analysis and efficiency from equity, then proceed to [[公共品、搭便车与 Samuelson 条件|Public Goods, Free Riding, and the Samuelson Condition]] and [[税收归宿与超额负担|Tax Incidence and Excess Burden]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要在进入市场失灵之前看清竞争市场的效率基准，可先读 [[交换经济、Edgeworth box 与福利定理]]；它把 Pareto 效率、竞争均衡和初始禀赋之间的关系展开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before moving to market failure, read [[交换经济、Edgeworth box 与福利定理|Exchange Economies, the Edgeworth Box, and the Welfare Theorems]] to establish the competitive-market efficiency benchmark and unpack the relationship among Pareto efficiency, competitive equilibrium, and initial endowments.

## 实证与规范分析
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Positive and Normative Analysis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实证分析问政策会造成什么，可被数据和模型证伪；规范分析问结果是否值得，依赖福利标准、权利和分配权重。二者需要连接，但不能把“模型预测总剩余增加”直接写成“政策正确”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive analysis asks what a policy will cause and can be challenged by data and models. Normative analysis asks whether the result is desirable and depends on welfare criteria, rights, and distributional weights. The two must be connected, but “the model predicts higher total surplus” cannot be turned directly into “the policy is right.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个完整政策论证应分别写出因果预测与价值判断：谁的行为怎样变化、资源和风险怎样变化，以及这些后果按什么福利或权利标准加权。把两层混在一句话中，会让事实争议和价值分歧都无法定位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A complete policy argument states causal predictions and value judgments separately: whose behavior changes, how resources and risks change, and under which welfare or rights criterion those consequences are weighted. Combining both layers in one sentence makes it impossible to locate either factual disputes or value disagreements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 剩余与总福利
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Surplus and Total Welfare*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

消费者剩余是支付意愿与实际支付之差，生产者剩余是收入与可变机会成本之差。竞争均衡在标准无外部性、完全信息等条件下最大化总剩余。剩余是货币化近似，对收入边际效用、非市场价值和分配需另作处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consumer surplus is willingness to pay minus actual payment; producer surplus is revenue minus variable opportunity cost. Under standard conditions such as no externalities and complete information, competitive equilibrium maximizes total surplus. Surplus is a monetized approximation, so marginal utility of income, non-market values, and distribution require separate treatment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

转移支付和税收收入通常在社会总量中是不同主体之间的转移，而真实资源成本来自行为扭曲、征管和机会成本。做福利表时应把每个群体收益、政府收入和净外部影响分别列出，避免把同一元钱既算损失又算收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Transfers and tax revenue are usually movements between parties in the social total, while real resource costs arise from behavioral distortions, administration, and opportunity cost. A welfare table should list each group's gains, government revenue, and net external effects separately to avoid counting the same unit of money both as a loss and as revenue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Pareto、补偿与社会福利
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pareto, Compensation, and Social Welfare*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Pareto 改进让至少一人更好且无人更差，标准很强，现实改革很少满足。Kaldor–Hicks 只要求赢家理论上能补偿输家，未实际补偿时不能忽略分配。社会福利函数用权重汇总个人效用，把价值判断显式化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A Pareto improvement makes at least one person better off and no one worse off, a demanding standard rarely met by real reforms. Kaldor–Hicks requires only that winners could in principle compensate losers; distribution cannot be ignored when compensation is not actually paid. A social welfare function aggregates individual utility with weights and makes the value judgment explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

效率与公平不是两套互不相干的打分。收入不同会改变一元钱的边际福利，风险和机会也会改变效用。可以先报告未加权资源效果，再清楚说明分配权重如何改变结论，而不是把权重隐藏在“社会收益”中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Efficiency and equity are not two unrelated scorecards. Income differences change the marginal welfare value of a unit of money, and risk and opportunity also affect utility. Report unweighted resource effects first and then state clearly how distributional weights alter the conclusion, rather than hiding those weights inside “social benefit.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 市场失灵
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Market Failure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

外部性、公共品、市场力量、信息不对称和不完整市场会破坏竞争基准。但政策需针对具体楔子：价格工具、数量管制、产权、信息披露或公共供给的效果与行政成本不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Externalities, public goods, market power, asymmetric information, and incomplete markets break the competitive benchmark. Policy must target the specific wedge: price instruments, quantity regulation, property rights, disclosure, and public provision have different effects and administrative costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 政府失灵与制度比较
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Government Failure and Institutional Comparison*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信息限制、执行成本、寻租、政治激励和政策俘获会使政府方案偏离社会目标。正确问题是比较可行制度在同一信息与执行环境下的表现，而非拿理想政府对比现实市场。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Information limits, enforcement costs, rent seeking, political incentives, and regulatory capture can push government policy away from social objectives. The right comparison is between feasible institutions under the same informational and enforcement environment, not between ideal government and an actual market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 政策评价框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Policy-evaluation Framework*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

依次说明基准配置、失灵机制、政策改变的边际激励、效率与分配、执行和一般均衡反馈、以及可检验预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
State in sequence the baseline allocation, the failure mechanism, how policy changes marginal incentives, efficiency and distributional effects, implementation and general-equilibrium feedback, and testable predictions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：租金管制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Rent Control*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价格上限低于均衡租金会提高需求、降低供给或维护激励，并产生排队、关系分配等非价格配给；现有租户可能受益，未进入者和房东可能受损。判断政策不能只看受保护租户支付的租金，还要比较住房数量、质量、流动、长期建设和分配目标，并与住房补贴或供给改革等可行替代制度比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rent ceiling below equilibrium raises demand, weakens supply or maintenance incentives, and creates non-price rationing through queues or connections. Incumbent tenants may benefit while excluded applicants and landlords lose. Evaluation cannot stop at the rent paid by protected tenants; it must compare housing quantity, quality, mobility, long-run construction, and distributional goals against feasible alternatives such as housing subsidies or supply reform.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看到偏离完全竞争不等于已经识别可修正失灵；政策改善某个局部指标也不等于总福利提高。若分析没有明确反事实、受益和受损群体、行政成本与一般均衡反馈，它仍只是政策直觉而不是完整福利分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A departure from perfect competition does not by itself identify a correctable failure, and improving a local metric does not prove that total welfare rises. Without an explicit counterfactual, winners and losers, administrative cost, and general-equilibrium feedback, the analysis remains a policy intuition rather than a complete welfare evaluation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么实证结论不能自动推出规范结论？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a positive conclusion not automatically imply a normative conclusion?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 事实说明后果，是否值得还需福利标准、分配权重、权利和风险偏好等价值判断。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Facts describe consequences; deciding whether they are desirable also requires value judgments about welfare criteria, distributional weights, rights, and risk preferences.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### 市场失灵是否足以证明任何政府干预都会改善福利？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Is market failure sufficient to prove that any government intervention improves welfare?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不足；还需比较具体工具的信息要求、执行成本、政治激励和副作用。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. The information requirements, enforcement costs, political incentives, and side effects of specific instruments must still be compared.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
### Kaldor–Hicks 改善与 Pareto 改善差在哪里？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How does a Kaldor–Hicks improvement differ from a Pareto improvement?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 前者只要求赢家收益足以潜在补偿输家，后者要求现实中无人变差。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The former requires only that winners could potentially compensate losers; the latter requires that no one is actually made worse off.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
