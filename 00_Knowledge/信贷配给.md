# 信贷配给是借款需求受到不能靠提高愿付利率解除的数量限制

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Credit rationing is a quantity restriction on borrowing demand that a willingness to pay a higher interest rate cannot remove*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡采用 Stiglitz–Weiss（1981）的严格口径：在银行看来可观察特征相同的申请人中，有人获贷、有人未获贷；被拒者即使愿意提高贷款利率，也不能因此取得贷款。比较的是银行当时可用的信息，不要求申请人的真实风险相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the strict Stiglitz–Weiss (1981) usage, some applicants who appear identical to the bank receive loans while others do not; rejected applicants cannot obtain credit simply by offering a higher rate. Comparability concerns information available to the bank, not necessarily identical true risks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原文还包括一种群体排除：给定信贷供给时，可识别的一类人按任何利率都借不到钱，但若信贷供给更大，他们就能获得贷款。“供给增加后会获贷”的反事实条件很重要，不能把任何永远不符合还款要求的人都放进这一类。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paper also includes group exclusion: at a given credit supply, an identifiable group cannot borrow at any rate, although it would receive loans if credit supply were larger. That supply counterfactual matters; exclusion of borrowers who would never meet lending requirements is not automatically this case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，100 名可观察特征相同且愿意接受契约的申请人中，银行只给 60 人贷款，另 40 人提价也无法获贷，是需要解释的配给现象。仅看到拒贷还不够：不同公开信用等级的差别定价、没有人愿意借款，以及一般的[[银行信贷创造约束|资本或流动性约束]]，都不单独证成这种配给，更不证明它已经是[[均衡信贷配给条件|均衡结果]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose 100 observably identical applicants accept the terms, but only 60 receive loans and the other 40 cannot gain access by offering more. That is a rationing phenomenon requiring explanation. Rejection alone is insufficient: pricing different publicly known credit classes, absent borrowing demand, or general [[银行信贷创造约束|capital or liquidity constraints]] does not by itself establish this rationing, much less an [[均衡信贷配给条件|equilibrium outcome]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
