# Filtration
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*滤过*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **filtration** describes which questions can be answered as information arrives over time. Before two coin tosses are revealed, we cannot tell whether the first toss is heads. After seeing the first toss, we can answer that question but not yet determine the second toss. After both are revealed, we can distinguish all four possible histories. A filtration records these successive levels of information.

The building block is a [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]]: a collection of events closed under taking complements and countable unions. Think of its events as yes-or-no questions whose answers the available observation determines. “Information increases” means that every question answerable earlier remains answerable later.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**滤过**描述信息随时间到来时，我们能回答哪些问题。两次抛硬币的结果都没揭晓前，还不知道第一次是不是正面；看到第一次后，可以回答这个问题，但仍不能确定第二次的结果；两次都看到后，才可以区分四种完整历史。滤过记录的就是这些逐步变化的信息层次。

它的基本组成是[[σ-代数]]：对补集和可数并封闭的一族事件。可以把其中的事件理解为当前观察足以判断真假的问题。“信息增加”表示先前能回答的问题，以后仍然能回答。

> [!example] Reveal two tosses one at a time · 逐次揭晓两次抛掷
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $\Omega=\{HH,HT,TH,TT\}$, where $H$ means heads and $T$ means tails; the first letter is the first toss. Put $A=\{HH,HT\}$, the event “the first toss is heads.” One information flow is
> $$\mathcal F_0=\{\varnothing,\Omega\},\qquad
> \mathcal F_1=\{\varnothing,A,A^c,\Omega\},\qquad
> \mathcal F_2=2^\Omega.$$
> The notation $2^\Omega$ means all subsets of $\Omega$, here sixteen events. At time one, the observation separates the possibilities into the two groups $\{HH,HT\}$ and $\{TH,TT\}$. The event “second toss is heads,” $\{HH,TH\}$, splits both groups and is not in $\mathcal F_1$. It is in $\mathcal F_2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $\Omega=\{HH,HT,TH,TT\}$，其中 $H$ 表示正面、$T$ 表示反面，第一个字母记录第一次。记 $A=\{HH,HT\}$，即“第一次是正面”。上式给出一种信息流；$2^\Omega$ 表示 $\Omega$ 的全部子集，这里有十六个事件。
>
> 时刻一的观察把可能性分成 $\{HH,HT\}$ 与 $\{TH,TT\}$ 两组。事件“第二次是正面”对应 $\{HH,TH\}$，它把两组都拆开了，所以不属于 $\mathcal F_1$；到了时刻二，它属于 $\mathcal F_2$。

## An increasing family of event collections
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*逐时扩大的事件族*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work on a fixed [[概率空间|probability space]] $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$: $\Omega$ contains outcomes, $\mathcal F$ the model's measurable events, and $P$ assigns their probabilities. For an ordered time set $I$, a family $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\in I}$ is a filtration when each $\mathcal F_t$ is a sigma-algebra on this same $\Omega$ and
$$\mathcal F_s\subseteq\mathcal F_t\subseteq\mathcal F\qquad(s\le t).$$
A sub-sigma-algebra can have fewer allowed events, while keeping the same underlying outcomes. The inclusion can be equality: a time step need not bring new information. Common choices of $I$ are $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ and $[0,\infty)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在固定[[概率空间]] $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ 上，$\Omega$ 收集可能结果，$\mathcal F$ 收集模型中的可测事件，$P$ 为事件分配概率。对有序时间集 $I$，若每个 $\mathcal F_t$ 都是同一个 $\Omega$ 上的 σ-代数，而且对 $s\le t$ 满足上述包含关系，就称 $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\in I}$ 为滤过。

子 σ-代数允许区分的事件可能更少，底层可能结果仍是同一个集合。包含关系允许取等号：时间前进一步，不一定会有新信息。常见时间集为 $\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ 或 $[0,\infty)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An information structure is not a list of observed values
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么滤过不是一串观测值

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the coin example, $\mathcal F_1$ contains both $A$ and $A^c$. After observing heads, $A$ is true and $A^c$ is false, but both are **decidable**. The collection describes the observation rule for every possible outcome; it is not just the events that happened on this realization.

A model chooses what is revealed. Observing the history of a [[随机过程|stochastic process]] gives its [[自然滤过|natural filtration]]. Observing extra signals can give a larger filtration. A process is [[适应过程|adapted]] when its current value is determined by the information available then. None of these terms by itself asserts that future increments are independent or that the process is Markov.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在硬币例子里，$\mathcal F_1$ 同时包含 $A$ 和 $A^c$。观察到正面后，$A$ 为真、$A^c$ 为假，但两者都已经**可以判定**。这个集合族描述对所有可能结果都适用的观察规则，不是只收集本次实际发生的事件。

模型需要明确哪些信息会被揭示。观察某个[[随机过程]]的历史，得到它的[[自然滤过]]；同时观察额外信号，可能得到更大的滤过。如果过程当前值能由当前信息确定，就称它对该滤过[[适应过程|适应]]。这些术语本身都没有断言未来增量独立或过程具有 Markov 性。

## The information changes the conditional average
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*信息如何改变条件平均*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now make the two tosses independent and fair, and let $\xi_2$ be $1$ for a second head and $-1$ for a second tail. With only the first toss revealed,
$$E[\xi_2\mid\mathcal F_1]=0.$$
Both second-toss outcomes remain equally likely inside either first-toss group. If an alternative observation reveals both tosses already at time one, its information is $\mathcal G_1=2^\Omega$ and
$$E[\xi_2\mid\mathcal G_1]=\xi_2.$$
This [[条件期望|conditional expectation]] now equals the already known value. The random variable and its original distribution have not changed; the information used to condition has. Consequently, a [[鞅定义|martingale]] assertion must specify its filtration as well as its process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在令两次抛掷独立且公平，并令 $\xi_2$ 在第二次为正面时取 $1$、反面时取 $-1$。只看到第一次时，每个第一轮分组内部的两种第二轮结果仍各半，因此 $E[\xi_2\mid\mathcal F_1]=0$。

如果另一种观察方式在时刻一就揭晓两次结果，此时信息为 $\mathcal G_1=2^\Omega$，[[条件期望]]便等于已经知道的值 $\xi_2$。随机变量及其原有分布都没变，变的是用于条件化的信息。因此，声称某过程是[[鞅定义|鞅]]时，必须同时指定过程和滤过。

> [!note] Additional conditions must be stated · 附加条件须明确
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The filtration definition requires increasing information, not continuity in time. In continuous-time theory, right-continuity and inclusion of probability-null subsets are often added as the “usual conditions.” They are extra requirements. The [[自然滤过|natural-filtration card]] distinguishes the raw history from such an augmentation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 滤过定义要求信息递增，没有要求它对时间连续。连续时间理论常另外加入右连续性和对零概率子集的包含，称为“通常条件”。这些是附加要求；[[自然滤过]]卡区分了原始历史与这种增广。
