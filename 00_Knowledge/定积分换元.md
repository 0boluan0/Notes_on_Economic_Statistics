# Substitution in definite integrals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定积分换元*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[定积分|definite integral]] describes accumulation between two specified inputs. When the input variable changes, the endpoint labels must change with it. For example, let $u=x^2$ in $\int_0^1 2x\cos(x^2)\,dx$. As $x$ goes from $0$ to $1$, $u$ goes from $0$ to $1$, and $du=2x\,dx$. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[定积分]]描述两个指定输入之间的累积。改换输入变量时，端点也要随它改名。例如对 $\int_0^1 2x\cos(x^2)\,dx$，令 $u=x^2$：$x$ 从 $0$ 走到 $1$，$u$ 也从 $0$ 走到 $1$，且 $du=2x\,dx$。于是

$$\int_0^1 2x\cos(x^2)\,dx=\int_0^1\cos u\,du=\sin1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The new expression evaluates the same number. The [[换元积分|indefinite substitution method]] explains the chain-rule factor; here we also need a theorem covering the whole integration interval, not just a pointwise symbolic replacement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

新表达式计算的是同一个数。[[换元积分]]解释链式因子；这里还需要覆盖整个积分区间的定理，不能只做逐点符号替换。

> [!abstract] A sufficient substitution theorem · 一组足够的换元条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $a<b$ be real endpoints. Suppose $g$ is continuously differentiable on $[a,b]$: it is [[一点连续|continuous at every point]] there, differentiable inside, and its derivative extends continuously to the endpoints. Let the real-valued function $f$ be continuous on an interval containing **every value** $g(x)$ for $a\le x\le b$. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设实数端点满足 $a<b$。假设 $g$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上连续可微：函数在闭区间每一点都[[一点连续|连续]]，内部可导，导数能连续延伸到两端。实值函数 $f$ 在一个包含 $a\le x\le b$ 时 **全部** $g(x)$ 值的区间上连续。则
>
> $$\boxed{\int_a^b f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx=\int_{g(a)}^{g(b)}f(u)\,du.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The set of all these values is the image $g([a,b])$; checking only $g(a)$ and $g(b)$ is insufficient. Here $u$ is the new integration variable. The formula does not require $g$ to be one-to-one or monotone, and $g'$ may vanish. These are sufficient regularity conditions, not a claim that every substitution requires them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 全部这些取值组成像集 $g([a,b])$；只检查 $g(a)$、$g(b)$ 两个值不够。$u$ 是新的积分变量。公式不要求 $g$ 一一对应或单调，也允许 $g'$ 为零。这是一组足够的正则条件，并不是所有换元的必要条件。

## Why the full image and endpoint order matter
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*完整像域与端点方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[定积分区间可加性|definite integral has an orientation]]: $\int_c^d f=-\int_d^c f$, and $\int_c^c f=0$. So the transformed lower endpoint is $g(a)$ and the upper endpoint is $g(b)$ **in that order**, even when the first is numerically larger. For $u=1-x$, $du=-dx$ and the bounds $x=0,1$ become $u=1,0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[定积分区间可加性|定积分保留方向]]：$\int_c^d f=-\int_d^c f$，且 $\int_c^c f=0$。所以换元后的下限为 $g(a)$、上限为 $g(b)$，**顺序不能擅改**，即使前一个数更大。令 $u=1-x$，则 $du=-dx$，原来的 $x=0,1$ 对应 $u=1,0$：

$$\int_0^1(1-x)^2\,dx=-\int_1^0u^2\,du=\int_0^1u^2\,du=\frac13.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove the theorem, take an [[原函数|antiderivative]] $F$ of $f$ over the whole image interval, supplied by the [[积分求导定理|fundamental theorem for accumulation]]. If this interval has included endpoints, continuity of $f$ allows a continuous extension just beyond them by holding the endpoint values constant; this makes $F$ differentiable wherever the composition needs it. The [[链式法则|chain rule]] gives $(F\circ g)'=f(g)g'$. Both integrands in the boxed formula are continuous on their finite integration ranges, so [[Newton-Leibniz公式|endpoint evaluation]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明时，由[[积分求导定理]]在整个像所在区间上构造 $f$ 的一个[[原函数]] $F$。若这个区间包含端点，可以把 $f$ 在端点外短距离延伸为对应端点的常数值，得到连续延拓，使复合运算涉及的各处都可对 $F$ 求导。[[链式法则]]给出 $(F\circ g)'=f(g)g'$。框中两边的被积函数都在各自有限积分范围上连续，于是[[Newton-Leibniz公式]]给出

$$
\int_a^b f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx
=F(g(b))-F(g(a))
=\int_{g(a)}^{g(b)}f(u)\,du.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No inverse function appears in this proof. Even if $g$ reverses direction, the derivative factor carries its signed movement. If $g$ is constant, the left integrand is zero and the right bounds coincide; the same formula still works.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明没有使用反函数。即使 $g$ 改变运动方向，导数因子也会保留带符号的变化。若 $g$ 是常函数，左边被积函数为零，右边上下限相同，公式同样成立。

> [!example] A turning substitution can work directly · 有转折也能直接换元
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $g(x)=x^2$ on $[-1,1]$ and $f(u)=e^u$. The inner value travels from $1$ down to $0$ and back to $1$, and $f$ is continuous throughout that image. Because the complete derivative factor $2x$ is present,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[-1,1]$ 上取 $g(x)=x^2$、$f(u)=e^u$。内层取值从 $1$ 降到 $0$，再回到 $1$；$f$ 在整个像域上连续。因为含有完整的导数因子 $2x$，
>
> $$\int_{-1}^{1}2xe^{x^2}\,dx=\int_1^1e^u\,du=0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is signed accumulation: the two traversals cancel. In contrast, $\int_{-1}^1x^2\,dx=2/3$ does not contain that factor. It cannot be turned into a single $1$-to-$1$ integral by pretending $x=\sqrt u$ everywhere. An approach that uses an inverse must distinguish $x=-\sqrt u$ and $x=\sqrt u$; see [[非单调换元需分支|the inverse-branch distinction]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这是带符号的累积，两次经过同一范围的贡献相消。相反，$\int_{-1}^1x^2\,dx=2/3$ 没有该因子，不能假装处处都有 $x=\sqrt u$，就改成从 $1$ 到 $1$ 的单个积分。如果采用反解路线，必须分清 $x=-\sqrt u$ 与 $x=\sqrt u$ 两支，见[[非单调换元需分支|反函数分支的区别]]。

## Keep one variable system throughout the evaluation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*端点评价保持同一套变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the course example, $u=x^3+2$ gives $du=3x^2\,dx$. The original bounds $1,2$ become $3,10$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程例题中，$u=x^3+2$ 给出 $du=3x^2\,dx$；原来的上下限 $1,2$ 变成 $3,10$：

$$
\int_1^2(x^3+2)^5x^2\,dx
=\frac13\int_3^{10}u^5\,du
=\left[\frac{u^6}{18}\right]_3^{10}
=\frac{10^6-3^6}{18}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Alternatively, substitute back first and evaluate $[(x^3+2)^6/18]_1^2$. Both routes agree. The expression $[u^6/18]_1^2$ would mix the new variable with the old endpoints and evaluate a different quantity. The bracket notation $[A(v)]_c^d$ means $A(d)-A(c)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也可以先换回原变量，再算 $[(x^3+2)^6/18]_1^2$，结果相同。若写成 $[u^6/18]_1^2$，就把新变量和旧端点混在了一起，计算了另一个量。方括号 $[A(v)]_c^d$ 表示 $A(d)-A(c)$。

> [!warning] Equal image endpoints do not repair a singularity · 相同像端点不能消除奇点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Formally choosing $g(x)=x^2$ and $f(u)=1/u$ in $\int_{-1}^1 2x/x^2\,dx$ suggests bounds $1,1$, but the full image contains $0$, where $f$ is undefined. The original integrand is also undefined at $x=0$. This is not a proper integral to which the theorem applies. For [[反常积分|improper integration]], each side of the singularity must be treated by its own limit; the two sides here diverge, so cancelling their endpoint expressions does not give an ordinary convergent integral.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $\int_{-1}^1 2x/x^2\,dx$ 形式上取 $g(x)=x^2$、$f(u)=1/u$，看似得到上下限 $1,1$，但完整像域包含 $0$，$f$ 在那里没有定义，原被积函数在 $x=0$ 也没有定义。这不是可套用该定理的正常定积分。按[[反常积分]]处理时，要分别取奇点两侧的极限；本例两边都发散，不能把端点表达式相消就当作普通收敛积分。
