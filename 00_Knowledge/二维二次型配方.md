# 二维对称二次型可通过配方把定号问题化为两个平方系数的符号

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Completing the square reduces a two-dimensional symmetric quadratic form to two signed square coefficients*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $Q=ax_1^2+2bx_1x_2+cx_2^2$，若 $a\ne0$，
$$
Q=a\left(x_1+\frac ba x_2\right)^2+\frac{ac-b^2}{a}x_2^2.
$$
新坐标 $u=x_1+(b/a)x_2,v=x_2$ 可逆，所以两个系数同正给正定，同负给负定，异号给不定；系数为零时保留平坦方向。由此得到 $a>0,ac-b^2>0$ 的二维[[Sylvester 正定判据]]。

若 $a=0$，不能使用含 $1/a$ 的公式。此时 $Q(1,0)=0$，不可能定；$b\ne0$ 时不定，$b=0$ 时只需看 $cx_2^2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed identity uses an invertible change of coordinates, so the signs of its two weights determine the form. It explains the two-dimensional [[Sylvester 正定判据|Sylvester criterion]]. Division by zero is excluded: when the first squared coefficient vanishes, inspect the original cross term and the remaining square separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
