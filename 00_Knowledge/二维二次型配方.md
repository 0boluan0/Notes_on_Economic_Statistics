# Completing the square in two variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*二维二次型配方*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Completing the square rewrites a quadratic expression so that its positive and negative contributions can be seen separately. The cross term mixes two coordinates; replacing one coordinate by a suitable combination removes that mixture without losing any possible input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

配方把二次表达式改写成可以分别看出正负贡献的形式。交叉项把两个坐标混在一起；用合适的坐标组合替换其中一个，就能消去这种混合，同时不遗漏任何输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the [[二次型|real quadratic form]]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看这个实[[二次型]]：

$$q(x_1,x_2)=2x_1^2+6x_1x_2+7x_2^2
=2\left(x_1+\frac32x_2\right)^2+\frac52x_2^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expanding the first square gives $2x_1^2+6x_1x_2+(9/2)x_2^2$; the remaining $5/2$ restores the coefficient $7$. Both weights are positive. If $x_2\ne0$, the second term is positive; if $x_2=0$ but $x_1\ne0$, the first is positive. Thus every nonzero input gives a positive value, the defining requirement for [[正定矩阵|positive definiteness]] of the associated symmetric matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

展开第一项平方得到 $2x_1^2+6x_1x_2+(9/2)x_2^2$，再加 $5/2$ 恢复原系数 $7$。两个权重都正：若 $x_2\ne0$，第二项正；若 $x_2=0$ 但 $x_1\ne0$，第一项正。因此每个非零输入都给出正值，满足相应实对称矩阵[[正定矩阵|正定]]的定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Add and subtract the same square
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*补上同一项，再减回去*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a,b,c$ and $x_1,x_2$ be real numbers. The symmetric coefficient matrix and its quadratic expression are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a,b,c,x_1,x_2$ 都为实数。对称系数矩阵及其二次表达式为

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix},\qquad
q(x_1,x_2)=x^TAx=ax_1^2+2bx_1x_2+cx_2^2,\quad x=(x_1,x_2)^T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. The cross coefficient is $2b$, because both off-diagonal entries contribute $bx_1x_2$. If $a\ne0$, factor out $a$ from the first two terms and add and subtract exactly $b^2x_2^2/a$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。交叉项系数是 $2b$，因为两个非对角元各贡献一次 $bx_1x_2$。当 $a\ne0$，先从前两项提出 $a$，再补上并减去同一个 $b^2x_2^2/a$：

$$\begin{aligned}
q&=a\left(x_1^2+2\frac ba x_1x_2+\frac{b^2}{a^2}x_2^2\right)
 +\left(c-\frac{b^2}{a}\right)x_2^2\\
 &=a\left(x_1+\frac ba x_2\right)^2+\frac{\Delta}{a}x_2^2,
 \qquad \Delta=ac-b^2=\det A.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\det A$ is the [[行列式|determinant]]. Set $u=x_1+(b/a)x_2$ and $v=x_2$. This is an invertible change: given any $u,v$, recover $x_2=v$ and $x_1=u-(b/a)v$. In particular, $(u,v)=(0,0)$ exactly when $x=(0,0)$, and the new coordinates vary independently. The expression becomes simply
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\det A$ 表示[[行列式]]。令 $u=x_1+(b/a)x_2$、$v=x_2$。这个变换可逆：给定任意 $u,v$，反解为 $x_2=v$、$x_1=u-(b/a)v$。特别地，新坐标同时为零当且仅当原输入为零；而且 $u,v$ 可以独立取值。表达式于是变成

$$q=a u^2+\frac{\Delta}{a}v^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the signs through independent coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*通过独立坐标读取符号*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $a\ne0$, the two coefficients $a$ and $\Delta/a$ give a complete classification:

- If both are positive, every nonzero input gives a positive value. Equivalently, $a>0$ and $\Delta>0$: this is the two-dimensional [[Sylvester 正定判据|Sylvester test]]. Necessity follows by testing $(u,v)=(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$.
- If both are negative, every nonzero input gives a negative value: $a<0$ and $\Delta>0$. This is [[负定矩阵|negative definiteness]].
- If they have opposite signs, equivalently $\Delta<0$, the two coordinate choices give opposite signs. The form is [[不定二次型|indefinite]].
- If $\Delta=0$, one square remains. Its sign is selected by $a$; the form is semidefinite but not definite. The nonzero vector $(-b/a,1)^T$ makes it zero. Positive $a$ gives [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefiniteness]], and negative $a$ gives [[半负定矩阵|negative semidefiniteness]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $a\ne0$ 时，两个系数 $a$、$\Delta/a$ 给出完整分类：

- 两者都正，则每个非零输入的值都正；等价于 $a>0$、$\Delta>0$，即二维 [[Sylvester 正定判据]]。必要性由分别取 $(u,v)=(1,0)$、$(0,1)$ 得到。
- 两者都负，则每个非零输入的值都负；等价于 $a<0$、$\Delta>0$，即[[负定矩阵|负定]]。
- 两者异号，等价于 $\Delta<0$，分别沿两个新坐标方向便得到正负两种值，故为[[不定二次型]]。
- $\Delta=0$ 时只剩一个平方，符号由 $a$ 决定，半定但非定。非零向量 $(-b/a,1)^T$ 使其为零：$a>0$ 时[[半正定矩阵|半正定]]，$a<0$ 时[[半负定矩阵|半负定]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A small change in the opening example shows why the cross term matters:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把开头例子的交叉系数稍作改变，就能看出它的作用：

$$2x_1^2+8x_1x_2+7x_2^2=2(x_1+2x_2)^2-x_2^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The diagonal coefficients are still $2,7$, both positive, but the determinant is now $14-16=-2$. The input $(1,0)$ gives $2$, whereas $(-2,1)$ gives $-1$. This is an explicit pair of witnesses for indefiniteness, and illustrates [[正对角元的正定边界|why positive diagonal entries alone do not suffice]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对角系数仍是正数 $2,7$，但行列式变为 $14-16=-2$。输入 $(1,0)$ 给出 $2$，而 $(-2,1)$ 给出 $-1$。这两个具体方向直接证明不定，也说明[[正对角元的正定边界|仅有正对角元为什么不够]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Start again when the first coefficient is zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*首项系数为零时，回到原式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $a=0$, division by $a$ is unavailable. Also $q(1,0)=0$, so strict positive or negative definiteness is impossible. Two cases remain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$a=0$ 时不能除以 $a$。而且 $q(1,0)=0$，所以不可能正定或负定。接下来分两种情况。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $b\ne0$, set $x_2=1$. Then $q(x_1,1)=2bx_1+c$ takes every real value as $x_1$ varies. More explicitly, $x_1=(1-c)/(2b)$ gives $1$, and $x_1=(-1-c)/(2b)$ gives $-1$. Hence the form is indefinite for every real $c$. For example, $2x_1x_2$ has values $2$ and $-2$ at $(1,1)$ and $(1,-1)$; the zero first coefficient has not removed the interaction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $b\ne0$，取 $x_2=1$，则 $q(x_1,1)=2bx_1+c$ 随 $x_1$ 变化可以取遍实数。具体地，$x_1=(1-c)/(2b)$ 时值为 $1$，$x_1=(-1-c)/(2b)$ 时值为 $-1$。所以无论实数 $c$ 是多少，都不定。例如 $2x_1x_2$ 在 $(1,1)$、$(1,-1)$ 处分别为 $2$、$-2$；首项系数为零并未消除交叉作用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $b=0$ as well, then $q=cx_2^2$. It is positive semidefinite for $c>0$, negative semidefinite for $c<0$, and identically zero for $c=0$. The zero form is both positive and negative semidefinite, while neither strictly definite. This separate branch covers all cases excluded by the division.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若同时 $b=0$，就只有 $q=cx_2^2$：$c>0$ 时半正定，$c<0$ 时半负定，$c=0$ 时恒为零。零型同时半正定、半负定，却既不正定也不负定。这个单独分支覆盖了除法排除的全部情况。

> [!note] The weights need not be eigenvalues · 平方权重不必是特征值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This coordinate change is generally a shear, not a length-preserving rotation. Its inverse preserves the available directions, which is enough to read signs, but its two weights need not be the matrix's eigenvalues. In the opening example they are $2,5/2$, whose sum is $9/2$; the eigenvalues instead sum to the [[迹与特征值总和|original trace]], the sum of diagonal entries, $2+7=9$. The general [[LDL正定判别|$LDL^*$ viewpoint]] uses this same distinction between a change in quadratic coordinates and a spectral decomposition. This card treats real variables and ordinary squares; complex Hermitian forms require conjugation and absolute-value squares.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里的坐标变换通常是剪切，并非保持长度的旋转。可逆性保证所有方向仍可取得，足以判断符号，但两个平方权重不必等于特征值。开头例子的权重是 $2,5/2$，和为 $9/2$；特征值之和却等于[[迹与特征值总和|原矩阵的迹]]，即对角元之和 $2+7=9$。一般 [[LDL正定判别|$LDL^*$ 观点]]也区分二次型坐标变换与谱分解。本卡讨论实变量及普通平方；复 Hermitian 型则须使用共轭与模平方。
