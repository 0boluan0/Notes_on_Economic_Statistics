# 价格缺口是相邻期间高低价格区间之间出现的不重叠空档
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A price gap is a non-overlapping interval between adjacent periods' high-low price ranges.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价格缺口比较相邻两个期间的完整价格区间。采用相同交易时段和价格口径，若本期最低价高于前期最高价，就是向上缺口；若本期最高价低于前期最低价，就是向下缺口。两区间只是相接而没有正宽度空档时，不满足本卡定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A price gap compares adjacent periods' complete ranges on the same session and price basis. An upward gap has the current low above the previous high; a downward gap has the current high below the previous low. Merely touching ranges do not leave a positive-width gap under this definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $[L_{t-1},H_{t-1}]$ 和 $[L_t,H_t]$ 为两根 [[K线]] 的高低区间：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For adjacent [[K线|candlesticks]] with ranges $[L_{t-1},H_{t-1}]$ and $[L_t,H_t]$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\text{向上缺口： }L_t>H_{t-1},\qquad
\text{向下缺口： }H_t<L_{t-1}.$$

例如前日区间为 $[98,102]$，今日为 $[105,108]$，空档宽度为 $105-102=3$。只知道今日开盘高于前收盘还不够：若今天开在105却最低到101，两个全日区间已经重叠。全日缺口须等全日结束才能完整确认；开盘跳空是较早可观察的另一种条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ranges of $[98,102]$ and $[105,108]$ leave a gap of 3. An opening above the previous close is insufficient: an opening at 105 followed by a low of 101 makes the full-day ranges overlap. A full-day gap is confirmed only at the day's end; an opening gap is a different condition observable earlier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先排除拆股等公司行动造成的尺度变化，见 [[复权与成交价格]]；再说明是否只观察常规时段，不能把未收集的盘后交易当作全市场没有成交。缺口名称及后续解读的时点约束见 [[缺口分类时点]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check for corporate-action scale changes using [[复权与成交价格|adjusted-price and account conventions]], and specify whether observations cover regular sessions only. Missing after-hours observations do not establish an absence of trading everywhere. See [[缺口分类时点|gap-classification timing]] for the information requirements of subsequent interpretations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
