# 自愿成交量不超过供需意愿数量的较小者且充分匹配时才等于短边

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Voluntary trade cannot exceed the smaller desired quantity and equals the short side only with sufficient matching*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定同一价格、质量、时期和市场范围，令 $Q^D$ 为买方愿意购买的数量、$Q^S$ 为卖方愿意出售的数量。每笔自愿成交都必须同时被双方接受，所以实际成交量 $Q^T$ 满足：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the same price, quality, time period, and market scope, let $Q^D$ be the quantity buyers wish to purchase and $Q^S$ the quantity sellers wish to sell. Each voluntary transaction requires acceptance by both sides, so actual trade $Q^T$ satisfies:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Q^T\leq\min\{Q^D,Q^S\}.
$$

供需两方中意愿数量较小的一方称为**短边**。只有所有短边意愿都成功找到兼容的交易对象并完成交易，才可把不等式写为等式。搜索和匹配摩擦、交易资格限制或额外配额，都可能使实际成交量低于这一上界；仅知道供需数量不相等，还没有证明所有短边意愿都被满足。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The side with the smaller desired quantity is the **short side**. Equality requires every desired unit on that side to find a compatible counterpart and complete a transaction. Search and matching frictions, eligibility restrictions, or additional quotas can leave trade below this upper bound. Unequal desired quantities alone do not establish that every desired transaction on the short side occurs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，[[约束性价格上限]]下若 $Q^D(\bar p)>Q^S(\bar p)$，卖方是短边。充分匹配时成交量等于 $Q^S(\bar p)$，超额需求为 $Q^D(\bar p)-Q^S(\bar p)$。这一步确定数量；住房或商品最终分给哪些买方，以及能否按需求曲线计算剩余，仍取决于[[限价下的有效配给]]条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, under a [[约束性价格上限|binding price ceiling]], if $Q^D(\bar p)>Q^S(\bar p)$, sellers are the short side. With sufficient matching, trade equals $Q^S(\bar p)$ and excess demand is $Q^D(\bar p)-Q^S(\bar p)$. This determines quantity. Which buyers receive the goods, and whether surplus can be calculated from the demand curve, still depends on [[限价下的有效配给|efficient rationing under the ceiling]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
