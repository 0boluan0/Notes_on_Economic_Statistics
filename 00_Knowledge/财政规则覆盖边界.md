# 判断财政规则约束了什么必须同时识别机构范围、交易范围、记账基础和例外条款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*To identify what a fiscal rule constrains, specify its institutional scope, transaction coverage, accounting basis and exceptions.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验财政规则前，先把其指标边界写清：覆盖中央政府还是一般政府，哪些收支或债务进入计算，采用什么确认基础，哪些项目有明确排除。只有这些条件一致，规则界限才能与报告数值直接比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before testing a rule, establish whether it covers central or general government, which transactions or liabilities count, the recognition basis and explicit exclusions. The reported measure must match these conditions before comparison with the limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

机构边界复用[[一般政府与公共部门]]，内部往来处理复用[[财政合并口径]]，确认时点复用[[现金与权责发生]]。例如，推迟已经到期的付款可能暂时改善现金支出数，但形成[[财政拖欠]]；若只看现金余额而不披露拖欠，就无法据此判断财政负担是否改善。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reuse [[一般政府与公共部门|institutional-sector coverage]], [[财政合并口径|consolidation]] and [[现金与权责发生|cash versus accrual accounting]]. Delaying an overdue payment may temporarily improve a cash measure while creating [[财政拖欠|arrears]]. The cash balance alone then cannot establish an improvement in fiscal obligations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

某项活动位于规则之外，不自动证明它违法或是为了规避规则；需要核查真实的机构归属、授权、交易和风险转移。反过来，只要狭窄指标合规，也不能推出整个公共部门风险已受控制。用[[预算完整性]]与[[财政或有负债]]补充观察范围，再作实质判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An activity outside a rule is not automatically illegal or evasive; assess its actual classification, authorisation, transactions and risk allocation. Conversely, compliance with a narrow measure does not establish control of wider public-sector risks. [[预算完整性|Budget comprehensiveness]] and [[财政或有负债|contingent liabilities]] broaden the assessment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
