# 利率渠道是货币政策经实际储蓄回报与融资机会成本改变消费和投资的传导机制
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The interest-rate channel transmits monetary policy through real saving returns and financing opportunity costs to consumption and investment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利率渠道指政策改变家庭与企业面对的利率条件，进而改变何时消费、借款和投资。央行的隔夜利率不是所有主体直接面对的价格：先看与支出期限相符的市场和借贷利率，再用[[事前实际利率]]核对预期购买力回报。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The interest-rate channel changes the timing of consumption, borrowing and investment through the rates households and firms face. An overnight policy rate is not everybody's transaction rate. Identify maturity-relevant market and lending rates, then assess the [[事前实际利率|ex ante real return]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际利率上升提高推迟消费的相对回报；但总储蓄反应还取决于净借贷头寸、收入和约束，完整两期辨析复用[[利率与储蓄反应]]。在宏观传导中，名义价格与工资不能立即完全调整，是政策能在短期改变实际需求的重要机制条件，不是声称所有主体都按同一比例减少支出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A higher real rate raises the relative reward to postponing consumption, but total saving also depends on net borrowing, income and constraints; reuse [[利率与储蓄反应|the two-period saving analysis]]. In macroeconomic transmission, incomplete immediate adjustment of nominal prices and wages is an important mechanism allowing short-run real-demand effects, not a common spending coefficient for everyone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

投资端可用一项独立项目说明：今天实际成本 100，明年确定的全部实际净流入 104。若其他条件固定，匹配的实际机会成本从 2% 升至 6%，按[[净现值决策]]，净现值从约 $104/1.02-100=1.96$ 变成 $104/1.06-100=-1.89$。这解释边际项目为何可能被放弃，不把政策利率直接当作每个风险项目的贴现率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider an independent project costing 100 today and paying a certain total real net cash flow of 104 next year. Holding other conditions fixed, a rise in its matched real opportunity cost from 2% to 6% changes [[净现值决策|NPV]] from about 1.96 to −1.89. This illustrates a marginal investment decision, without equating the policy rate with every risky project's discount rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当前政策未变，[[未来短率预期]]变化也可能先改变长期融资条件；同时还须分开[[期限溢价]]。反过来，利率报价下降但信贷不可得、销售前景恶化或支出已经提前完成，都可能削弱观察到的消费投资反应。渠道说明条件化机制，不给固定时滞或固定乘数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changes in [[未来短率预期|expected future short rates]] can alter longer-term financing before the current policy rate moves, with the [[期限溢价|term premium]] treated separately. Unavailable credit, weaker sales prospects or earlier purchases can weaken observed spending responses despite lower quoted rates. The channel does not prescribe a fixed lag or multiplier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
