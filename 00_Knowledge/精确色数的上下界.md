# Proving an exact chromatic number
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用上、下界证明精确色数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Showing a valid coloring answers “Do these colors suffice?” Proving an exact minimum also answers “Why can no smaller palette work?” For a finite nonempty simple undirected graph, its [[色数|chromatic number]] $\chi(G)$ is trapped between these two kinds of evidence. They serve different purposes, even when they appear in the same picture.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给出一种合法着色，回答的是“这些颜色够不够”；证明精确最小值，还要回答“为什么更少的颜色一定不够”。有限非空简单无向图的[[色数]] $\chi(G)$，由这两类证据从两边夹住。它们的作用不同，即使证据出现在同一张图中。

> [!important] Two inequalities give equality / 两条不等式给出等号
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A verified [[Proper 着色与独立集划分|proper coloring]] using $U$ colors gives $\chi(G)\le U$. A proof excluding every coloring with fewer than $L$ colors gives $\chi(G)\ge L$. If $L=U=k$, these facts force $\chi(G)=k$. The lower-bound claim is about **all** proposed smaller colorings, not just one failed attempt.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个使用 $U$ 色、逐边核验过的[[Proper 着色与独立集划分|合法着色]]给出 $\chi(G)\le U$；证明所有少于 $L$ 色的着色都不可能，给出 $\chi(G)\ge L$。两边若在 $L=U=k$ 相遇，就迫使 $\chi(G)=k$。下界针对的是**全部**更少颜色的方案，不是某次尝试失败。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, finding a proper 5-coloring and three pairwise adjacent vertices establishes only $3\le\chi(G)\le5$. It leaves the values 3, 4 and 5 open. Trying a greedy procedure and obtaining five colors does not rule out a different order or a different method using fewer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，找到合法五着色，又找到三个两两相邻的顶点，目前只能得到 $3\le\chi(G)\le5$；3、4、5仍都有可能。一次贪心过程用了五色，不能排除另一种顺序或另一种方法用得更少。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Useful lower-bound certificates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 常用下界证书

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $r$ vertices are pairwise adjacent, they form a **clique** $K_r$. Every pair must have different labels, so those vertices alone require $r$ colors and $\chi(G)\ge r$. This is a certificate inside the graph, not a claim that the clique is the whole graph. A triangle therefore gives a lower bound of three, but other vertices may force more colors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $r$ 个顶点两两相邻，它们形成一个**团** $K_r$。每一对都必须异色，仅这组顶点就需要 $r$ 色，故 $\chi(G)\ge r$。这是图内部的证书，不是说整张图只有这个团。三角形因此给出三的下界，但其他顶点仍可能迫使更多颜色。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An odd [[游走路径与环|cycle]] of length $2t+1\ge3$ rules out two colors. Starting at one vertex, the edge constraints force the two labels to alternate. After the even number $2t$ of edges to the last vertex, that vertex has the starting label, and the closing edge violates properness. Thus an odd cycle gives $\chi(G)\ge3$; the full [[二着色与奇环|two-colorability theorem]] treats the converse separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长度为 $2t+1\ge3$ 的奇[[游走路径与环|环]]排除二色方案。从一个顶点出发，每条边都会强迫两种标签交替；走过偶数条 $2t$ 条边到达最后一个顶点时，它与起点同色，闭合边便违反合法性。因此奇环给出 $\chi(G)\ge3$；完整的[[二着色与奇环|二着色判据]]另处理反方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A third certificate limits the size of each color class. Write $n=|V|$ and let $\alpha(G)$ be the largest size of an [[图的独立集|independent vertex set]]. Every color class has at most $\alpha(G)$ vertices. If a coloring uses $r$ nonempty classes, adding their sizes gives $n\le r\alpha(G)$. Since a nonempty simple graph has $\alpha(G)\ge1$, division and rounding up give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第三类证书限制每个颜色类的大小。记 $n=|V|$，$\alpha(G)$ 是[[图的独立集]]的最大大小。每个颜色类至多容纳 $\alpha(G)$ 个顶点。若一种着色用了 $r$ 个非空类，把它们的大小相加得到 $n\le r\alpha(G)$。非空简单图满足 $\alpha(G)\ge1$，所以可以相除并向上取整，得到

$$\chi(G)\ge \left\lceil\frac{|V|}{\alpha(G)}\right\rceil.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\lceil x\rceil$ means the smallest integer at least $x$. One must know the maximum size or prove a valid upper bound on it. Finding one independent set of size $s$ proves only $\alpha(G)\ge s$; substituting $s$ for $\alpha(G)$ can create a false lower bound. In an edgeless graph on four vertices, a singleton is independent, but using $s=1$ would wrongly predict four colors rather than one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $\lceil x\rceil$ 表示不小于 $x$ 的最小整数。使用此法需要知道最大大小，或者证明它的有效上界。只找到大小为 $s$ 的一个独立集，得到的是 $\alpha(G)\ge s$；擅自用 $s$ 替代 $\alpha(G)$，可能制造错误下界。例如四个顶点的无边图中，单点集确实独立，但若取 $s=1$ 就会误推需要四色，实际一色足够。

## A complete short proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个完整而短的证明

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose exams $A,B,C$ form a triangle and every other exam is a leaf adjacent to only one of them. The triangle forces three colors, so $\chi(G)\ge3$. Color the triangle with three colors and give each leaf any existing color different from its sole neighbor; then $\chi(G)\le3$. Hence $\chi(G)=3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设考试 $A,B,C$ 两两冲突，形成 triangle；其他考试都是只连接其中一个顶点的 leaves。triangle 迫使 $A,B,C$ 使用三种颜色，故 $\chi(G)\ge3$。再给 triangle 三色，并让每个 leaf 选择不同于其唯一邻居的任一已有颜色，得到 proper 3-coloring，故 $\chi(G)\le3$。因此 $\chi(G)=3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The construction checks every edge: triangle edges join different colors, and each remaining edge joins a leaf to its sole neighbor. There are no leaf–leaf edges under the stated hypothesis. This is why the proposed coloring is a certificate, rather than an unverified picture.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个构造检查了全部边：三角形内部的边两端异色，其余每条边都把叶子连到唯一邻点。按题设不存在叶子之间的边，因此这是一份可核验的着色证书，不只是未经检查的图画。

> [!warning] A missing certificate does not prove a smaller answer / 找不到某类证书不代表答案更小
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The five-cycle $C_5$ has no triangle but still needs three colors. Two colors fail by the odd-cycle argument; the cyclic label sequence $1,2,1,2,3$ gives a proper 3-coloring. Thus a clique lower bound need not reach the true chromatic number. A four-coloring alone is also only an upper bound; it does not rule out three or two.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 五环 $C_5$ 没有三角形，却仍需要三色。奇环论证排除二色；沿环依次标 $1,2,1,2,3$ 给出合法三着色。因此团下界不一定达到真实色数。仅画出四着色也只是上界，不能排除三色或二色。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an empty vertex set, use the separately declared convention for the chromatic number. The independent-set quotient is then $0/0$ and is undefined. The finite loop-free scope also matters: a loop prevents every proper coloring, so the finite-minimum argument cannot simply be reused.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

顶点集合为空时，采用色数定义中单独声明的约定；独立集比值此时成为没有定义的 $0/0$。有限、无自环的范围同样重要：自环会排除全部合法着色，不能继续套用有限最小值的论证。
