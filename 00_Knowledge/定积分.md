# 定积分是分割充分细时所有黎曼和共同趋近的累积值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A definite integral is the common accumulation limit of all sufficiently fine Riemann sums*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f$ 在有限闭区间 $[a,b]$ 上有界且 $a<b$。若存在同一个数 $I$，使任意分割、任意合法采样的[[黎曼和]]都在最大段宽 $\lVert P\rVert=\max_i\Delta x_i\to0$ 时趋于 $I$，就说 $f$ **黎曼可积**，并记
$$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx=I.$$
这定义的是一个数；积分变量 $x$ 可以改名为 $t$，而 $a,b$ 和被积函数决定它的值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a bounded function on a finite closed interval, Riemann integrability means that every tagged sum approaches the same number as the maximum subinterval width tends to zero. That number is the definite integral. The integration variable is a dummy name; the function and bounds determine the value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“所有”很重要：精确地说，对每个 $\varepsilon>0$，存在 $\delta>0$，使所有满足 $\lVert P\rVert<\delta$ 的分割及其采样都满足 $|S-I|<\varepsilon$。只算出某一种固定采样的极限，还没有证明可积。课程常用的连续函数由[[连续函数可积]]保证这一点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The quantifier is essential: one mesh threshold must make every tagged sum accurate to any prescribed tolerance. Convergence of one selected sampling sequence alone does not establish integrability. The [[连续函数可积|continuous-function theorem]] supplies this guarantee in the standard course setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直观上，$f\ge0$ 时它是曲线下的面积；$f$ 有正有负时它是带符号的累积，见[[净变化与总变化]]。例如 $f(x)=c$ 的每个黎曼和都恰为 $c(b-a)$，因此 $\int_a^b c\,dx=c(b-a)$。约定 $\int_a^a f=0$、$\int_b^a f=-\int_a^b f$，使积分可以记录方向。无限区间或无界被积函数需要另用[[反常积分]]的极限定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonnegative function the integral measures area; in general it is signed accumulation, distinguished from [[净变化与总变化|total variation]]. Every sum for a constant equals height times interval length. Equal bounds give zero and reversing bounds negates the integral. Unbounded domains or integrands require the separate limiting definition of an [[反常积分|improper integral]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
