# Definite integral
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*定积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **definite integral** adds up contributions across an interval. Each small piece contributes a height or rate multiplied by its width. If a tap supplies water at three litres per minute for four minutes, the total is $3\times4=12$ litres. Integration extends this calculation to a rate that changes from place to place or moment to moment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**定积分**把一个区间内的贡献累积起来：每个小段的贡献等于高度或速率乘以该段宽度。例如水龙头每分钟供水三升，持续四分钟，总量就是 $3\times4=12$ 升。积分把这个计算推广到高度或速率随位置、时间变化的情况。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$ specifies the function being accumulated, $f$, and the starting and ending inputs, $a$ and $b$. The symbol $dx$ identifies the input variable and corresponds to the widths used in the limiting sum. Here the definite integral is one number; renaming the input variable from $x$ to $t$ does not change it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记号 $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$ 指定要累积的函数 $f$，以及起点和终点输入 $a,b$。$dx$ 指明积分变量，并对应极限求和中的小段宽度。这里的定积分是一个数；把输入变量从 $x$ 改名为 $t$，不会改变结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Approximate with rectangles, then refine them
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先用矩形近似，再把分割细化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[黎曼和|Riemann sum]] implements the small-piece idea. Split the interval into pieces, choose a sample point in each piece, multiply its function value by the piece width, and add. The sample height is used throughout that piece, so the sum is generally an approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[黎曼和]]把“小段贡献”变成具体计算：把区间分成小段，每段选一个采样点，用该点的函数值乘以段宽，再全部相加。因为整段都使用同一个采样高度，所以通常只得到近似。

> [!example] A rising straight line · 一条上升的直线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider $f(x)=x$ on $[0,2]$. Two equal pieces with right-endpoint heights $1,2$ give $1\times1+2\times1=3$. Four pieces of width $1/2$ give $(1/2+1+3/2+2)(1/2)=2.5$. With $n$ equal pieces, the right-endpoint sum is $2+2/n$, tending to $2$. Left endpoints instead give $2-2/n$, tending to the same number.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑 $[0,2]$ 上的 $f(x)=x$。等分两段、取右端点高度 $1,2$，得到 $1\times1+2\times1=3$；等分四段、每段宽 $1/2$，得到 $(1/2+1+3/2+2)(1/2)=2.5$。等分为 $n$ 段时，右端点和为 $2+2/n$，趋于 $2$；左端点和则为 $2-2/n$，同样趋于 $2$。
>
> $$\int_0^2 x\,dx=2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The graph encloses a triangle with base two and height two, so its area is also $2$. The function is continuous; [[连续函数可积|continuous functions on a closed interval are integrable]], which ensures that other sufficiently fine partitions and sample choices agree as well.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 图像围成底为二、高为二的三角形，面积也为 $2$。函数在这里连续，[[连续函数可积|闭区间上的连续函数可积]]保证：换成其他足够细的分割和采样，也会得到同一个极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What must be independent of the approximation choices?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*哪些近似选择不能影响最终结果？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a bounded function on a finite interval $[a,b]$ with $a<b$, write a partition as $a=x_0<x_1<\cdots<x_n=b$. The $i$th width is $\Delta x_i=x_i-x_{i-1}$, and $\xi_i$ is any sample point in that piece. The corresponding sum is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限区间 $[a,b]$（$a<b$）上的有界函数，把分割写成 $a=x_0<x_1<\cdots<x_n=b$。第 $i$ 段宽度为 $\Delta x_i=x_i-x_{i-1}$，$\xi_i$ 是这一段内任意选取的采样点。对应的和为

$$S=\sum_{i=1}^{n}f(\xi_i)\Delta x_i,\qquad \xi_i\in[x_{i-1},x_i].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The largest width, $\lVert P\rVert=\max_i\Delta x_i$, measures how fine the partition $P$ is. Requiring it to tend to zero means no wide piece may remain. The [[函数极限|limit]] concerns the approximation error as this mesh becomes small.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用最大段宽 $\lVert P\rVert=\max_i\Delta x_i$ 衡量分割 $P$ 的精细程度。要求它趋于零，意味着不能留下任何一段仍然很宽。这里的[[函数极限|极限]]考察分割变细时，近似误差怎样变化。

> [!abstract] A common limit for every sufficiently fine sum · 所有充分细的和共有一个极限
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The function is **Riemann integrable** if there is a number $I$ such that, for every error tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, some mesh threshold $\delta>0$ guarantees
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果存在一个数 $I$，使得对每个误差容限 $\varepsilon>0$，都能找到网格阈值 $\delta>0$，保证
>
> $$\lVert P\rVert<\delta\quad\Longrightarrow\quad |S-I|<\varepsilon$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> for every allowed partition and every choice of sample points, then $I$ is the definite integral $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$. One common threshold must work for all those choices.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对所有合法分割、所有采样点选择都成立，就称函数**黎曼可积**，并把 $I$ 记为定积分 $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$。同一个阈值必须同时控制这些选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finding a limit for just one chosen sequence of sums does not establish integrability. For a constant function $f(x)=c$, by contrast, every sum is already $c$ times the total width, so $\int_a^b c\,dx=c(b-a)$ immediately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只算出某一种固定采样序列的极限，还不能证明可积。相反，常数函数 $f(x)=c$ 的每个和都已经等于 $c$ 乘以总宽度，因此立即得到 $\int_a^b c\,dx=c(b-a)$。

## Signed accumulation and direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*带符号的累积与方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a<b$ and a nonnegative function, the integral is geometric area under the graph. Negative function values contribute negatively. For $f(x)=x$ on $[-1,1]$, the triangle below the axis contributes $-1/2$ and the one above contributes $1/2$, so the integral is zero although the total geometric area is one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $a<b$ 且函数非负时，积分就是图像下方的几何面积。函数值为负时，贡献也带负号。例如 $[-1,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x$，轴下三角形贡献 $-1/2$，轴上三角形贡献 $1/2$，所以积分为零，但总几何面积为一。

> [!warning] Cancellation is meaningful · 正负抵消有实际含义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the integrand is velocity, its integral gives displacement; travelling forward and backward can cancel. Total distance instead accumulates the magnitude of velocity. See [[净变化与总变化|net change and total change]] for this distinction. In general, total geometric area uses $\int_a^b|f(x)|\,dx$ when $a<b$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果被积函数是速度，积分给出位移，向前与向后运动可以抵消；路程则累积速度的大小。这个区别见[[净变化与总变化]]。一般地，$a<b$ 时的总几何面积应使用 $\int_a^b|f(x)|\,dx$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Orientation is recorded by the conventions $\int_a^a f=0$ and $\int_b^a f=-\int_a^b f$. Reversing the water-flow bounds from $0\to4$ to $4\to0$ changes $12$ to $-12$. If the upper bound is allowed to vary, the result becomes an [[累积函数|accumulation function]]. Infinite intervals or unbounded integrands require the separate limit definition of an [[反常积分|improper integral]], whose convergence must be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

积分用 $\int_a^a f=0$、$\int_b^a f=-\int_a^b f$ 记录方向。把供水例子的积分方向从 $0\to4$ 改成 $4\to0$，结果就从 $12$ 变为 $-12$。若允许上端点变化，得到的则是[[累积函数]]。无穷区间或无界被积函数需要另用[[反常积分]]的极限定义，并检查是否收敛。
