# 当最优性方程关于内生变量的 Jacobian 非奇异时，隐函数定理给出最优选择的局部比较静态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When the Jacobian of the optimality system with respect to endogenous variables is nonsingular, the implicit function theorem gives local comparative statics of the optimal choice*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 把最优性条件当作一组隐式方程
> 隐函数定理（implicit function theorem, IFT）可以把一组局部方程解变成参数的函数。设 $z$ 收集最优选择以及必要的活动乘子，参数为 $\theta$，最优性系统写成
> $$
> F(z,\theta)=0.
> $$
> 若 $F$ 在 $(z_0,\theta_0)$ 附近为 $C^1$、$F(z_0,\theta_0)=0$，且 $D_zF(z_0,\theta_0)$ 非奇异，则该方程在局部确定唯一的 $C^1$ 分支 $z(\theta)$，其[[隐函数导数公式|导数]]为
> $$
> D_\theta z(\theta_0)
> =-[D_zF(z_0,\theta_0)]^{-1}D_\theta F(z_0,\theta_0).
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The implicit function theorem (IFT) turns a local system of equations into functions of parameters. Collect the optimal choice and any required active multipliers in $z$, and write the optimality system as $F(z,\theta)=0$. If $F$ is $C^1$ near $(z_0,\theta_0)$, $F(z_0,\theta_0)=0$, and $D_zF(z_0,\theta_0)$ is nonsingular, then the system defines a unique local $C^1$ branch $z(\theta)$ with [[隐函数导数公式|derivative]] $D_\theta z=-[D_zF]^{-1}D_\theta F$ at the reference point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 无约束问题中的用法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Use in an unconstrained problem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对内部最优 $x^*(\theta)$，可取
$$
F(x,\theta)=\nabla_x f(x,\theta).
$$
此时 $D_xF=\nabla_{xx}^2f$。若 Hessian 在参考点可逆，就能不用显式解出 $x^*(\theta)$ 而求其局部导数。这个公式追踪的是某条局部驻点分支；在非凹问题中，还要另行确认这条分支仍是所关心的局部或全局最优。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an interior optimum $x^*(\theta)$, take $F(x,\theta)=\nabla_x f(x,\theta)$. Then $D_xF=\nabla_{xx}^2f$. If the Hessian is nonsingular at the reference point, the local derivative of $x^*(\theta)$ can be found without explicitly solving for the optimizer. The formula follows a local stationary branch; in a nonconcave problem one must separately verify that the branch remains the relevant local or global optimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 约束问题中的用法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Use in a constrained problem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定参考点附近不变的活动集，把驻点、活动约束等式与原有等式约束一起放进 $F$，并把 $z=(x,\lambda_{\mathcal A},\nu)$。此时 $D_zF$ 是相应的 KKT 或边框矩阵。只有在该矩阵非奇异、约束正则且活动制度没有切换时，普通隐函数公式才给出局部比较静态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix an active set that remains unchanged near the reference point, place stationarity, active inequalities as equalities, and the original equalities into $F$, and set $z=(x,\lambda_{\mathcal A},\nu)$. Then $D_zF$ is the corresponding KKT or bordered matrix. The ordinary implicit formula applies only when this matrix is nonsingular, the constraints are regular, and the active regime does not switch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

活动约束进入或退出、乘子碰到零、多个局部解相交或全局最优在分支间跳转时，单一光滑分支可能不存在。此时公式失效表示“这套局部表示不够”，不是经济对象一定不存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When constraints enter or leave the active set, a multiplier reaches zero, local branches meet, or the global optimum jumps between branches, a single smooth optimizer branch may not exist. Failure of the formula means that this local representation is inadequate, not that the economic object itself ceases to exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 非奇异性必须独立验证
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Nonsingularity must be verified independently*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应用这一方法前要直接检查 $D_zF$，不能把“参考解严格最优”当作可逆性的替代证明。严格最优但系统 Jacobian 奇异、最优分支不可微的反例见 [[严格最优不推系统非奇异]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before applying the formula, check $D_zF$ directly rather than treating strict optimality as a substitute for invertibility. [[严格最优不推系统非奇异|The strict-optimality boundary]] gives a counterexample with a strict optimum, a singular system Jacobian, and a nondifferentiable optimizer branch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

隐函数比较静态回答“最优选择如何随参数变化”；[[包络定理]] 回答“最优价值如何随参数变化”。前者通常要解一个线性系统，后者在适用时可以绕过 $D_\theta x^*$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Implicit-function comparative statics asks how the optimal choice changes with a parameter. [[包络定理|The envelope theorem]] asks how the optimal value changes. The former generally solves a linear system; the latter can, when applicable, avoid computing $D_\theta x^*$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个驻点分支的隐函数导数已经算出，为什么还不能直接说全局最优选择也按这个导数变化？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After computing the implicit derivative of a stationary branch, why can one not immediately claim that the global optimizer changes at that rate?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 隐函数定理只追踪参考点附近的一条局部方程解；还要确认它保持可行、保持最优，并且没有另一条分支取代它成为全局最优。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The implicit function theorem follows one local solution of the equations. Feasibility and optimality must persist, and no competing branch may overtake it as the global optimum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
