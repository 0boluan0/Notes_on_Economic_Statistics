<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Implicit-function comparative statics
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*隐函数比较静态*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Comparative statics** asks how a choice changes when an external parameter changes: how production responds to a price, or how an allocation responds to a resource limit. The implicit-function method starts from equations already satisfied by a candidate and asks how a nearby solution must move to keep those equations true. A **branch** is this local rule assigning a nearby solution to each nearby parameter. Whether the branch consists of actual optimizers is a separate check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**比较静态**研究外部参数变化时选择怎样变化，例如产量怎样响应价格，或者配置怎样响应资源上限。隐函数法从候选解已经满足的方程出发，计算附近的解必须怎样移动，才能继续满足这些方程。**分支**就是为附近每个参数指定一个附近解的局部规则；这条分支是否真的由最优解组成，还要另行判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $z\in\mathbb R^k$ collect the $k$ unknowns and $\theta\in\mathbb R^r$ the $r$ parameters. The system $F$ has $k$ equations and is defined on an open neighborhood of $(z_0,\theta_0)$. Its [[Jacobian矩阵|Jacobian]] records the effect of changing each input on every equation: $D_zF$ is $k\times k$, $D_\theta F$ is $k\times r$, and the response $D_\theta z$ is $k\times r$. The [[隐函数定理|implicit function theorem]] requires $C^1$ regularity—continuous first partial derivatives—of $F$ in all these variables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $z\in\mathbb R^k$ 汇集 $k$ 个未知量，$\theta\in\mathbb R^r$ 汇集 $r$ 个参数。系统 $F$ 有 $k$ 条方程，定义在 $(z_0,\theta_0)$ 的开邻域上。它的 [[Jacobian矩阵]]记录各输入改变时每条方程的响应：$D_zF$ 为 $k\times k$，$D_\theta F$ 为 $k\times r$，所求 $D_\theta z$ 为 $k\times r$。[[隐函数定理]]要求 $F$ 关于这些变量共同为 $C^1$，即一阶偏导数连续。

> [!important] A nearby equation branch · 附近的方程解支
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The implicit function theorem (IFT) turns a local system of equations into functions of parameters. Collect the optimal choice and any required active multipliers in $z$, and write the optimality system as $F(z,\theta)=0$. If $F$ is $C^1$ near $(z_0,\theta_0)$, $F(z_0,\theta_0)=0$, and $D_zF(z_0,\theta_0)$ is nonsingular, then the system defines a unique local $C^1$ branch $z(\theta)$ with [[隐函数导数公式|derivative]] $D_\theta z=-[D_zF]^{-1}D_\theta F$ at the reference point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 隐函数定理（implicit function theorem, IFT）可以把一组局部方程解变成参数的函数。设 $z$ 收集最优选择以及必要的活动乘子，参数为 $\theta$，最优性系统写成
> $$
> F(z,\theta)=0.
> $$
> 若 $F$ 在 $(z_0,\theta_0)$ 附近为 $C^1$、$F(z_0,\theta_0)=0$，且 $D_zF(z_0,\theta_0)$ 非奇异，则该方程在局部确定唯一的 $C^1$ 分支 $z(\theta)$，其[[隐函数导数公式|导数]]为
> $$
> D_\theta z(\theta_0)
> =-[D_zF(z_0,\theta_0)]^{-1}D_\theta F(z_0,\theta_0).
> $$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Unique” means unique among solutions near $z_0$, for parameters near $\theta_0$; it does not exclude another distant solution. By the [[多元链式法则|chain rule]], the identity $F(z(\theta),\theta)=0$ gives $D_zF\,D_\theta z+D_\theta F=0$. The direct effect of the parameter must be cancelled by the movement of the unknowns. In practice, solve $D_zF\,Y=-D_\theta F$ for $Y=D_\theta z$; there is no need to form a matrix inverse explicitly. Both derivative blocks are evaluated at the same point on the branch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“唯一”只指参数在 $\theta_0$ 附近时，$z_0$ 附近的方程解唯一，不排除远处另有解。由[[多元链式法则]]，恒等式 $F(z(\theta),\theta)=0$ 给出 $D_zF\,D_\theta z+D_\theta F=0$：参数带来的直接变化，必须被未知量的移动抵消。计算时可直接解线性系统 $D_zF\,Y=-D_\theta F$，其中 $Y=D_\theta z$，无需实际构造逆矩阵。两个导数块都在分支上的同一点计算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use in an unconstrained problem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 无约束问题中的用法

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an interior optimum $x^*(\theta)$, take $F(x,\theta)=\nabla_x f(x,\theta)$. Then $D_xF=\nabla_{xx}^2f$. If the Hessian is nonsingular at the reference point, the local derivative of $x^*(\theta)$ can be found without explicitly solving for the optimizer. The formula follows a local stationary branch; in a nonconcave problem one must separately verify that the branch remains the relevant local or global optimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对内部最优 $x^*(\theta)$，可取
$$
F(x,\theta)=\nabla_x f(x,\theta).
$$
此时 $D_xF=\nabla_{xx}^2f$。若 Hessian 在参考点可逆，就能不用显式解出 $x^*(\theta)$ 而求其局部导数。这个公式追踪的是某条局部驻点分支；在非凹问题中，还要另行确认这条分支仍是所关心的局部或全局最优。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For this gradient system, assuming $f$ is $C^2$ jointly in $(x,\theta)$ is a convenient sufficient condition for $F$ to be $C^1$. A [[Hessian矩阵|Hessian]] contains second derivatives with respect to choices. For example, maximize $f(x,\theta)=\theta x-x^2$ over $\mathbb R$. Stationarity is $F=\theta-2x=0$, so $F_x=-2$, $F_\theta=1$, and $dx^*/d\theta=1/2$. Here the completed square $f=-(x-\theta/2)^2+\theta^2/4$ separately proves that $x^*=\theta/2$ is the unique global maximizer. The derivative formula alone did not prove that last fact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对这个梯度系统，要求 $f$ 关于 $(x,\theta)$ 共同为 $C^2$，就是保证 $F$ 为 $C^1$ 的一项方便的充分条件。[[Hessian矩阵]]由关于选择变量的二阶偏导组成。例如，在 $\mathbb R$ 上最大化 $f(x,\theta)=\theta x-x^2$，驻点方程为 $F=\theta-2x=0$，故 $F_x=-2$、$F_\theta=1$，得到 $dx^*/d\theta=1/2$。配方 $f=-(x-\theta/2)^2+\theta^2/4$ 另外证明 $x^*=\theta/2$ 是唯一全局最大点；最后这个结论并不是导数公式本身证明的。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use in a constrained problem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 约束问题中的用法

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix an active set that remains unchanged near the reference point, place stationarity, active inequalities as equalities, and the original equalities into $F$, and set $z=(x,\lambda_{\mathcal A},\nu)$. Then $D_zF$ is the corresponding KKT or bordered matrix. The ordinary implicit formula applies only when this matrix is nonsingular, the constraints are regular, and the active regime does not switch.

When constraints enter or leave the active set, a multiplier reaches zero, local branches meet, or the global optimum jumps between branches, a single smooth optimizer branch may not exist. Failure of the formula means that this local representation is inadequate, not that the economic object itself ceases to exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定参考点附近不变的活动集，把驻点、活动约束等式与原有等式约束一起放进 $F$，并把 $z=(x,\lambda_{\mathcal A},\nu)$。此时 $D_zF$ 是相应的 KKT 或边框矩阵。只有在该矩阵非奇异、约束正则且活动制度没有切换时，普通隐函数公式才给出局部比较静态。

活动约束进入或退出、乘子碰到零、多个局部解相交或全局最优在分支间跳转时，单一光滑分支可能不存在。此时公式失效表示“这套局部表示不够”，不是经济对象一定不存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the [[KKT条件|KKT system]] of a maximization problem, use $\mathcal L=f-\lambda^Tg-\nu^Th$, with $g\le0$, $h=0$ and $\lambda\ge0$. At the reference point, the active set is $\mathcal A=\{i:g_i=0\}$, determined by constraint values rather than positive multipliers. Form $F=(\nabla_x\mathcal L,g_{\mathcal A},h)$, set inactive multipliers to zero, and let $z=(x,\lambda_{\mathcal A},\nu)$. With jointly $C^2$ data this is a $C^1$ square system. Its Jacobian includes multiplier columns and constraint rows; it is not merely the Hessian of $f$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最大化问题的 [[KKT条件|KKT 系统]]采用 $\mathcal L=f-\lambda^Tg-\nu^Th$，其中 $g\le0$、$h=0$、$\lambda\ge0$。参考点的活动集为 $\mathcal A=\{i:g_i=0\}$，由约束值决定，而非由乘子是否为正决定。取 $F=(\nabla_x\mathcal L,g_{\mathcal A},h)$，把不活动乘子设为零，并令 $z=(x,\lambda_{\mathcal A},\nu)$。函数关于变量和参数共同为 $C^2$ 时，这构成 $C^1$ 方程组；其 Jacobian 包含乘子对应的列和约束对应的行，不只是目标 $f$ 的 Hessian。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The IFT first supplies a branch of this **equation system**. Check that it stays within the domain, that inactive inequalities remain negative and active multipliers remain nonnegative. If inactive constraints are strictly slack and all active multipliers are strictly positive at the reference point, continuity preserves those signs on a small neighborhood: this is a useful sufficient way to keep the active set valid, not a necessary condition for every smooth branch. To call the resulting KKT branch optimal, verify a suitable local sufficient condition, such as [[等式约束二阶条件|the tangent-space second-order test]] for equality-only constraints, or, in the convex setting, use [[KKT充分性|KKT sufficiency]]. Global membership in the [[最优解对应|optimizer correspondence]] is not established by the IFT itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

IFT 首先给出这套**方程系统**的解支。还要检查它是否留在定义域内、不活动不等式是否仍严格小于零、活动乘子是否仍非负。若参考点的不活动约束严格松弛，且所有活动乘子严格为正，连续性就会在小邻域内保留这些符号。这是维持活动集有效的一项充分办法，不是每条光滑分支都必须满足的条件。要进一步把 KKT 分支称为最优分支，还须验证适合的局部充分条件，例如仅有等式约束时的[[等式约束二阶条件|切空间二阶判据]]，或在凸问题中使用 [[KKT充分性]]；IFT 本身不会证明分支属于全局[[最优解对应]]。

> [!example] An equality-constrained response · 等式约束下的响应
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Maximize $f(x,\theta)=-(x_1-\theta)^2-x_2^2$ subject to $x_1+x_2=1$. With $z=(x_1,x_2,\nu)$, the equations are $-2(x_1-\theta)-\nu=0$, $-2x_2-\nu=0$, and $x_1+x_2-1=0$. The system derivative and parameter column are
>
> $$D_zF=\begin{pmatrix}-2&0&-1\\0&-2&-1\\1&1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad D_\theta F=\begin{pmatrix}2\\0\\0\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> The determinant is $-4\ne0$. Solving $D_zF\,z'=-D_\theta F$ gives $z'=(1/2,-1/2,1)^T$. Indeed $x_1^*=(1+\theta)/2$, $x_2^*=(1-\theta)/2$, and $\nu^*=\theta-1$. Increasing $\theta$ shifts half a unit toward the first choice and removes half a unit from the second, preserving the equality. Strict concavity and the affine constraint certify global optimality separately.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 最大化 $f(x,\theta)=-(x_1-\theta)^2-x_2^2$，满足 $x_1+x_2=1$。取 $z=(x_1,x_2,\nu)$，方程为 $-2(x_1-\theta)-\nu=0$、$-2x_2-\nu=0$、$x_1+x_2-1=0$。上面的 Jacobian 行列式为 $-4\ne0$，解 $D_zF\,z'=-D_\theta F$ 得到 $z'=(1/2,-1/2,1)^T$。显式解确为 $x_1^*=(1+\theta)/2$、$x_2^*=(1-\theta)/2$、$\nu^*=\theta-1$。参数增加一个微小单位时，第一项增加一半，第二项减少一半，保持等式成立。严格凹性与仿射约束另外保证全局最优。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nonsingular system can continue beyond the range in which its branch is optimal. For $\max_{0\le x\le b}(4x-x^2)$ with $b>0$, the upper-bound-active system is $4-2x-\lambda=0$, $x-b=0$. Its Jacobian in $(x,\lambda)$ is $\begin{pmatrix}-2&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, with determinant $1$. It yields $x=b$ and $\lambda=4-2b$ smoothly for all $b$. But for $b>2$ this multiplier is negative, so the branch is no longer KKT-valid; the actual maximizer is then $x=2$, $\lambda=0$. Invertibility has not prevented an active-set change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非奇异方程组的解支，也可能延伸到它不再最优的范围。对 $\max_{0\le x\le b}(4x-x^2)$，取 $b>0$。假定上限活动，方程是 $4-2x-\lambda=0$、$x-b=0$，关于 $(x,\lambda)$ 的 Jacobian 为 $\begin{pmatrix}-2&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$，行列式恒为 $1$。它给出始终光滑的 $x=b$、$\lambda=4-2b$；但 $b>2$ 时乘子变负，这条分支不再满足 KKT，实际最优是 $x=2$、$\lambda=0$。可逆性本身没有阻止活动集切换。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Nonsingularity must be verified independently
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 非奇异性必须独立验证

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before applying the formula, check $D_zF$ directly rather than treating strict optimality as a substitute for invertibility. [[严格最优不推系统非奇异|The strict-optimality boundary]] gives a counterexample with a strict optimum, a singular system Jacobian, and a nondifferentiable optimizer branch.

Implicit-function comparative statics asks how the optimal choice changes with a parameter. [[包络定理|The envelope theorem]] asks how the optimal value changes. The former generally solves a linear system; the latter can, when applicable, avoid computing $D_\theta x^*$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应用这一方法前要直接检查 $D_zF$，不能把“参考解严格最优”当作可逆性的替代证明。严格最优但系统 Jacobian 奇异、最优分支不可微的反例见 [[严格最优不推系统非奇异]]。

隐函数比较静态回答“最优选择如何随参数变化”；[[包络定理]] 回答“最优价值如何随参数变化”。前者通常要解一个线性系统，后者在适用时可以绕过 $D_\theta x^*$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the branch only maximizes within a local neighborhood, differentiating $f(x(\theta),\theta)$ describes that branch's payoff. To identify it with the global [[值函数|value function]], establish global optimality at the nearby parameter values as well. A singular system also does not prove that no smooth branch exists; the [[隐函数奇异块不推无解支|singular-block boundary]] gives the exact distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若分支只在局部邻域内最大化，求 $f(x(\theta),\theta)$ 的导数，描述的是该分支的收益。要把它认作全局[[值函数]]，还须证明附近参数下仍为全局最优。系统奇异也不等于没有光滑解支；[[隐函数奇异块不推无解支]]解释了这一区分。

> [!question] Check the interpretation · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After computing the implicit derivative of a stationary branch, why can one not immediately claim that the global optimizer changes at that rate?
>
> **Answer:** The implicit function theorem follows one local solution of the equations. Feasibility and optimality must persist, and no competing branch may overtake it as the global optimum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个驻点分支的隐函数导数已经算出，为什么还不能直接说全局最优选择也按这个导数变化？
>
> **答案：** 隐函数定理只追踪参考点附近的一条局部方程解；还要确认它保持可行、保持最优，并且没有另一条分支取代它成为全局最优。
