# 债券面值是合约标示的名义本金金额
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bond face value is the nominal principal amount stated in the contract*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券面值（face value、par value）是合约标示的名义本金金额。对按面值到期偿还、未摊还本金的普通债券，面值 $F$ 是承诺到期偿还的本金，也是计算固定息票的基数；它不是今天的交易价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Face value, or par value, is the nominal principal stated in the contract. For an ordinary bond with no principal amortisation and redemption at par, face value $F$ is the promised principal repayment at maturity and the base for calculating fixed coupons. It is not today's trading price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面值 $100$、年票面利率 $5\%$ 的债券，可以按 $98$ 成交；若每年付息且按面值到期偿还，最后一次承诺支付是 $5+100=105$。面值、市场价格和含最后息票的到期款是三个不同金额。发生违约时，实际回收还可能少于承诺本金；赎回溢价、指数化或本金摊还则须另读合约。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bond with face value 100 and a 5% annual coupon can trade at 98. With annual coupons and redemption at par, its final promised payment is $5+100=105$. Face value, market price, and the final payment including the last coupon are distinct amounts. Default may reduce actual recovery below promised principal; redemption premiums, indexation, or amortisation require checking the contract separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
