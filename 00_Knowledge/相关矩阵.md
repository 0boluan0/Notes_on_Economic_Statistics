# Correlation matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*相关矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Covariance measures how two variables vary together, but its number depends on their measurement units. A **correlation matrix** puts all the pairwise Pearson correlations into one table after expressing each variable's deviations in units of its own standard deviation. It lets us read the strength and sign of linear association on a common scale, while setting aside the original variances and units.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

协方差衡量两个变量怎样共同变化，但数值受测量单位影响。**相关矩阵**先把每个变量的偏移改用“自身标准差的倍数”表示，再将两两Pearson相关系数放进一张表。这样就能用共同尺度读取线性关联的强弱和方向，同时舍去原来的方差大小和单位。

> [!example] Divide by both standard deviations · 除以双方的标准差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}4&3\\3&9\end{bmatrix}$ is the [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] of two real measurements. Their [[标准差|standard deviations]] are $2$ and $3$. The covariance $3$ therefore becomes correlation $3/(2\cdot3)=1/2$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设两个实测量值的[[协方差矩阵]]为 $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}4&3\\3&9\end{bmatrix}$。它们的[[标准差]]为 $2$ 和 $3$，因此协方差 $3$ 变成相关系数 $3/(2\cdot3)=1/2$：
>
> $$R=\begin{bmatrix}1&1/2\\1/2&1\end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each diagonal entry is one because a nonconstant variable is perfectly correlated with itself. The off-diagonal entries compare the two different variables. A value $1/2$ measures positive linear association; it is not a probability of agreement or a causal effect.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对角为一，因为非恒定变量与自身完全相关；非对角比较不同变量。$1/2$ 表示正的线性关联，不是两个变量“保持一致的概率”，也不是因果效应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$ be a real random vector with finite second moments and **strictly positive variance in every coordinate**. Write $\mu=E[X]$, $\Sigma=\operatorname{Cov}(X)$, and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实随机向量 $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$ 的各坐标二阶矩有限，而且**每个坐标的方差都严格为正**。记 $\mu=E[X]$、$\Sigma=\operatorname{Cov}(X)$，再令

$$
D=\operatorname{diag}(\Sigma_{11},\ldots,\Sigma_{pp}),\qquad
D^{-1/2}=\operatorname{diag}\!\left(
\frac{1}{\sqrt{\Sigma_{11}}},\ldots,\frac{1}{\sqrt{\Sigma_{pp}}}\right).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\operatorname{diag}$ means the matrix with the listed diagonal entries and zeros elsewhere. The second matrix divides each coordinate by its standard deviation. The $p\times p$ population **Pearson correlation matrix** is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\operatorname{diag}$ 表示以列出的数为对角、其余位置为零的矩阵；第二张矩阵负责逐坐标除以标准差。$p\times p$ 的总体 **Pearson相关矩阵**定义为

$$
\boxed{R=D^{-1/2}\Sigma D^{-1/2}},\qquad
R_{jk}=\frac{\Sigma_{jk}}{\sqrt{\Sigma_{jj}\Sigma_{kk}}}
=\operatorname{Corr}(X_j,X_k).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This formula is the [[相关系数|Pearson correlation coefficient]] applied to every pair. Multiplication on the left divides row $j$ by the standard deviation of $X_j$; multiplication on the right divides column $k$ by that of $X_k$. Both divisions are needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个公式对每一对变量使用[[相关系数|Pearson相关系数]]定义。左乘把第 $j$ 行除以 $X_j$ 的标准差，右乘把第 $k$ 列除以 $X_k$ 的标准差，两次除法缺一不可。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Covariance of standardized variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 它是标准化变量的协方差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define the standardized vector $Z=D^{-1/2}(X-\mu)$. Its coordinate $Z_j$ says how many standard deviations $X_j$ lies above or below its mean. The [[协方差仿射变换|affine covariance rule]] gives $E[Z]=0$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(Z)=R$. Consequently $R$ is symmetric and [[协方差矩阵半正定性|positive semidefinite]], and its diagonal is all ones. The [[期望的Cauchy–Schwarz不等式|Cauchy–Schwarz bound]] on centered products gives $|R_{jk}|\le1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义标准化向量 $Z=D^{-1/2}(X-\mu)$。其坐标 $Z_j$ 表示 $X_j$ 高于或低于均值多少个自身标准差。由[[协方差仿射变换]]，$E[Z]=0$、$\operatorname{Cov}(Z)=R$。因此，$R$ 对称且[[协方差矩阵半正定性|半正定]]，对角全为一；将[[期望的Cauchy–Schwarz不等式]]用于中心化乘积，得到 $|R_{jk}|\le1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Semidefinite need not mean invertible. For $X=(U,2U)^T$ with finite positive $\operatorname{Var}(U)$, all entries of $R$ are one: standardization leaves the exact linear redundancy intact. Also, merely filling a symmetric table with numbers in $[-1,1]$ and ones on the diagonal does not guarantee a valid correlation matrix. A $3\times3$ table with every off-diagonal entry $-0.9$ gives quadratic value $3+6(-0.9)=-2.4$ at $(1,1,1)^T$, violating semidefiniteness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半正定不意味着可逆。若 $X=(U,2U)^T$ 且 $\operatorname{Var}(U)$ 有限为正，$R$ 的全部元素都为一：标准化保留了精确的线性冗余。反过来，只把 $[-1,1]$ 内的数字填入对称表，再把对角填成一，也不保证合法。一个非对角全为 $-0.9$ 的 $3\times3$ 矩阵，在 $(1,1,1)^T$ 方向给出二次型 $3+6(-0.9)=-2.4$，违反半正定性。

> [!warning] A constant coordinate has no Pearson correlation · 常数坐标没有普通Pearson相关
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $\Sigma_{jj}=0$, then $X_j$ is constant almost surely and its standard deviation is zero. The denominator in the correlation formula vanishes. Its ordinary Pearson correlations, including correlation with itself, are undefined; filling them with zero or one would be a separate convention, not this definition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $\Sigma_{jj}=0$，则 $X_j$ 几乎必然为常数，标准差为零，相关系数的分母也为零。它与其他坐标乃至自身的普通Pearson相关都未定义；填零或填一需要另定约定，不能由这里的定义得到。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Standardization does not enlarge the kinds of dependence Pearson correlation detects: [[零协方差不推独立|zero correlation need not imply independence]]. A sample correlation matrix estimates this population matrix from data. Changing coordinate scales is handled by [[相关矩阵尺度不变性|the scale-change rule]]; choosing covariance or correlation for an analysis is a separate [[PCA尺度选择|PCA scaling decision]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准化没有扩大Pearson相关能够识别的依赖类型：[[零协方差不推独立|零相关一般不推出独立]]。样本相关矩阵由数据计算，用于估计这里的总体对象。坐标尺度改变由[[相关矩阵尺度不变性]]承接；分析时选协方差还是相关矩阵，则是另外的[[PCA尺度选择]]问题。
