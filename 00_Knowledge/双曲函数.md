# Hyperbolic functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*双曲函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two basic **hyperbolic functions** combine exponential growth with its reflected decay. Take the [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]] $e^x$ and its reflection $e^{-x}$. Half their difference is called **hyperbolic sine**, written $\sinh x$; half their sum is **hyperbolic cosine**, written $\cosh x$. Here $x$ is any real input, and each of these symbols names one function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两种基本的**双曲函数**把指数增长与反向的指数衰减组合起来。取[[自然指数函数]] $e^x$ 及其反射 $e^{-x}$：两者差的一半称为**双曲正弦**，记作 $\sinh x$；两者和的一半称为**双曲余弦**，记作 $\cosh x$。这里 $x$ 可以是任意实数，每个记号都是一个函数的名称。

$$\sinh x=\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}2,\qquad
\cosh x=\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}2,\qquad x\in\mathbb R.$$

> [!example] Evaluate the exponentials first · 先计算两个指数值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $x=0$, both exponentials are one, giving $\sinh0=0$ and $\cosh0=1$. For an exact nonzero example, take $x=\ln2$, where [[自然对数|the natural logarithm]] means $e^x=2$. Then $e^{-x}=1/2$, so
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $x=0$，两个指数值都为一，因此 $\sinh0=0$、$\cosh0=1$。再取一个非零的精确例子：$x=\ln2$。按[[自然对数]]的含义，$e^x=2$，所以 $e^{-x}=1/2$，从而
>
> $$\sinh(\ln2)=\frac{2-1/2}{2}=\frac34,\qquad
> \cosh(\ln2)=\frac{2+1/2}{2}=\frac54.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In particular, neither function is confined to the interval $[-1,1]$ as ordinary sine and cosine are. The shared names suggest related identities, not identical values or periodic motion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 尤其不能像普通正弦、余弦那样，把这两个函数的值限制在 $[-1,1]$。相似的名字提示恒等式之间的联系，并不表示它们数值相同或作周期运动。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Changing the sign of the input
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把输入换成相反数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replacing $x$ by $-x$ swaps the two exponentials. Their difference changes sign, while their sum stays the same:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $x$ 换成 $-x$，两个指数项交换位置，因此它们的差变号，和不变：

$$\sinh(-x)=-\sinh x,\qquad\cosh(-x)=\cosh x.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first identity says $\sinh$ is **odd**, with graph symmetric about the origin; the second says $\cosh$ is **even**, with graph symmetric about the vertical axis. Also, $\cosh x\ge1$, with equality only at zero. For a direct proof, let $a=e^x>0$ and expand the nonnegative square $(a-1)^2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一个等式说明 $\sinh$ 是**奇函数**，图像关于原点对称；第二个说明 $\cosh$ 是**偶函数**，图像关于纵轴对称。另外，$\cosh x\ge1$，且只有 $x=0$ 时取等。直接证明可令 $a=e^x>0$，展开非负的平方 $(a-1)^2$：

$$\cosh x-1=\frac{a+a^{-1}-2}{2}=\frac{(a-1)^2}{2a}\ge0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The difference $e^x-e^{-x}$ is zero only at $x=0$ and has the same sign as $x$. As $x\to+\infty$, both $\sinh x$ and $\cosh x$ grow without bound, since $e^x$ grows and $e^{-x}$ tends to zero. Together with the symmetry above, this explains the unbounded shapes instead of oscillations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

差 $e^x-e^{-x}$ 只有在 $x=0$ 时为零，其符号与 $x$ 相同。当 $x\to+\infty$，$e^x$ 增大而 $e^{-x}$ 趋零，因此 $\sinh x$ 和 $\cosh x$ 都无界增大。结合上面的对称性，就能理解它们为什么是无界的形状，而不是振荡曲线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The hyperbola behind the name
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*名称背后的双曲线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding and subtracting the definitions recovers the original exponentials:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把两个定义相加、相减，会还原出原来的指数函数：

$$\cosh x+\sinh x=e^x,\qquad
\cosh x-\sinh x=e^{-x}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiply these identities. Since $e^xe^{-x}=1$, the difference of two squares gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把两个等式相乘，利用 $e^xe^{-x}=1$ 与平方差公式，得到

$$\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x
=(\cosh x+\sinh x)(\cosh x-\sinh x)=1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\cosh^2x$ means $(\cosh x)^2$. The point $(u,v)=(\cosh x,\sinh x)$ therefore lies on $u^2-v^2=1$, specifically its right branch because $u\ge1$. For $x=\ln2$, the point $(5/4,3/4)$ checks the identity: $25/16-9/16=1$. Ordinary circular functions instead trace $u^2+v^2=1$; the minus sign changes the geometry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $\cosh^2x$ 表示 $(\cosh x)^2$。所以点 $(u,v)=(\cosh x,\sinh x)$ 位于 $u^2-v^2=1$ 上，而且由于 $u\ge1$，位于它的右支。取 $x=\ln2$，点 $(5/4,3/4)$ 满足 $25/16-9/16=1$。普通圆三角函数对应的则是 $u^2+v^2=1$；一个负号改变了几何对象。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two common functions made from this pair are the hyperbolic tangent and hyperbolic secant:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由这对函数构成的两个常用函数是双曲正切与双曲正割：

$$\tanh x=\frac{\sinh x}{\cosh x},\qquad
\operatorname{sech}x=\frac1{\cosh x}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both are defined for every real $x$, since $\cosh x\ge1$. The identity gives $|\sinh x|<\cosh x$, so $-1<\tanh x<1$; also $0<\operatorname{sech}x\le1$. These ratios and reciprocals do not undo the original functions. In particular, $\operatorname{sech}$ is a reciprocal, not an inverse hyperbolic cosine; and $\sinh$ itself is not an inverse sine.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $\cosh x\ge1$，两者对全部实数 $x$ 都有定义。恒等式给出 $|\sinh x|<\cosh x$，所以 $-1<\tanh x<1$；同时 $0<\operatorname{sech}x\le1$。这些比值与倒数并没有把原函数“反解回来”。特别地，$\operatorname{sech}$ 是倒数，不是双曲余弦的反函数；$\sinh$ 本身也不是反正弦。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[双曲函数求导|derivative rules]] explain why this pair appears in differential equations: differentiating twice returns the original function. Hyperbolic cosine also describes an ideal uniform flexible cable suspended under its own weight, a **catenary**, after suitable scaling and translation. In Lorentz geometry, the same functions describe transformations preserving a difference of squares. These are applications of the exponential definitions and hyperbolic identity, not extra assumptions in the definitions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[双曲函数求导]]说明了这对函数为什么会出现在微分方程中：连续求导两次会回到原函数。双曲余弦经适当缩放、平移后，还能描述只受自身重量作用的理想均匀柔索形状，即**悬链线**。在 Lorentz 几何中，同一组函数用于描述保持平方差的变换。这些都是指数定义与双曲恒等式的应用，而不是定义另加的前提。
