# 非流动资产周转率是营业收入与相应非流动资产存量之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Noncurrent asset turnover is revenue divided by the corresponding noncurrent-asset stock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比率为 $S/NCA$，描述一元非流动资产账面占用所支持的营业收入。分母通常用期间平均值，CPA示例用期末；它包括所声明的非流动资产范围，不等于仅用固定资产的固定资产周转率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ratio $S/NCA$ describes revenue supported per unit of book noncurrent assets. Period averages are generally used, while CPA uses ending stocks. The stated noncurrent-asset scope is broader than fixed assets alone and must not be confused with fixed-asset turnover.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ABC收入3000、期末非流动资产1300，次数2.3077；365天下折算 $365\times1300/3000\approx158.1667$ 天。此天数只是资本占用/收入的尺度，不表示厂房每158天出售一次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ABC's revenue of 3,000 and ending noncurrent assets of 1,300 give turnover of 2.3077 and 158.1667 implied days. This is a capital-to-revenue scale, not a claim that a factory is sold every 158 days.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

老资产累计折旧较多、外包或资产处置都可能推高比率，不自动说明产能或技术效率更好。比较应结合投资阶段、租赁和分类，零分母不能计算通常次数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Accumulated depreciation, outsourcing, or asset disposals may raise the ratio without improving capacity or technical efficiency. Consider investment stage, leases, and classification. A zero denominator makes ordinary turnover undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
