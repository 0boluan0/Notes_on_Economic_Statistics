# 借贷利率不等使固定风险组合的配置线在全额投资处折弯
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Different borrowing and lending rates kink the allocation line at full investment in a fixed risky portfolio*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设出借／现金投资收益为 $r_L$，借款利率为 $r_B>r_L$，两者在同一持有期内确定；固定风险组合 $P$ 的 $\mu_P$ 有限且 $\sigma_P>0$。忽略其他费用，对 $P$ 的权重取 $y\ge0$。当 $y\le1$ 时剩余资金出借，当 $y>1$ 时缺口按 $r_B$ 融资，因此不能将原 [[资本配置线]] 的低利率直线原样延伸至杠杆区。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the lending/cash return be $r_L$ and borrowing cost be $r_B>r_L$, both certain over the same holding period. Fix a risky portfolio $P$ with finite mean $\mu_P$ and $\sigma_P>0$, omit other fees, and choose $y\ge0$. For $y\le1$, remaining cash is lent; for $y>1$, the funding gap costs $r_B$. The original low-rate [[资本配置线|capital allocation line]] therefore cannot be extended unchanged into the leveraged region.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mu_C(y)=
\begin{cases}
r_L+y(\mu_P-r_L),&0\le y\le1,\\
r_B+y(\mu_P-r_B),&y>1,
\end{cases}
\qquad \sigma_C=y\sigma_P.
$$

两段在 $y=1$ 都连接到 $(\sigma_P,\mu_P)$，所以收益连续；但对标准差的斜率从 $(\mu_P-r_L)/\sigma_P$ 降为 $(\mu_P-r_B)/\sigma_P$。借款利率截距只是杠杆段的代数延长线，不表示投资者可把全部财富按 $r_B$ 出借。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both segments meet at $(\sigma_P,\mu_P)$ when $y=1$, so return is continuous. Their slopes with respect to standard deviation differ, falling from $(\mu_P-r_L)/\sigma_P$ to $(\mu_P-r_B)/\sigma_P$. The borrowing-rate intercept is only an extrapolation of the leveraged segment; it does not mean the investor can lend all wealth at $r_B$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\mu_P=9\%$、$\sigma_P=20\%$、$r_L=3\%$、$r_B=5\%$：$y=0.5$ 时均值／波动为 $6\%/10\%$；$y=1.5$ 时为 $11\%/30\%$。若误把 3% 同时当借款成本，会把后者期望收益写成 12%，高估 1 个百分点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\mu_P=9\%$, $\sigma_P=20\%$, $r_L=3\%$, and $r_B=5\%$, $y=0.5$ gives mean/volatility $6\%/10\%$, while $y=1.5$ gives $11\%/30\%$. Incorrectly using 3% for borrowing would produce a 12% mean, overstating it by one percentage point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是“固定 $P$”的机会轨迹，不是已经对所有风险组合重新优化的有效前沿。若同时选择 $P$，借款者与出借者面对不同的净超额收益向量，最优风险构成可能不同；因此 [[风险资产与无风险分离|共同风险基金分离]] 的同率前提不能略去。但利率不同也不强迫每个特例的最优基金都不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the opportunity locus for a fixed $P$, not the efficient frontier after reoptimizing over all risky portfolios. If $P$ is also chosen, borrowers and lenders face different excess-mean vectors and their optimal risky compositions can differ. The common-rate premise of [[风险资产与无风险分离|common risky-fund separation]] therefore matters, although unequal rates do not force different optimal funds in every special case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
