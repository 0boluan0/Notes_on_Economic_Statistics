# 配额净买卖由最终排放与初始配额之差决定，而边际减排由成本与价格比较决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Net allowance purchases depend on final emissions minus the initial endowment; marginal abatement depends on the comparison of cost with price.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“再减一单位是否划算”与“本期最终是净买家还是净卖家”是两个问题。价格接受企业在下一单位减排成本低于配额价格时有增加减排的激励；但它减排之后若仍缺配额，依旧是净买家。低成本企业不能仅凭“成本低”就被认定为净卖家。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether another unit of abatement pays and whether the firm ends the period as a net buyer are different questions. A price taker has an incentive to abate more when the next unit costs less than an allowance, yet it remains a net buyer if its resulting emissions exceed its endowment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在无结转、借用、信用及其他持有目的的单期模型中，初始有效配额为 $l_i$，最终排放为 $e_i=e_{0i}-a_i$，完成该期履约所需的净购买量为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a single-period model without banking, borrowing, credits or other reasons to hold allowances, let initial valid allowances be $l_i$ and final emissions $e_i=e_{0i}-a_i$. Net purchases needed for compliance are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
n_i=e_i-l_i.
$$

$n_i>0$表示净买，$n_i<0$表示净卖；而边际选择比较 $MAC_i(a_i)$ 与给定价格 $p$。可调整的内点最优满足 $MAC_i=p$，所以最终净买家与净卖家完全可能具有相同的边际减排成本。若还有期末留存目标 $s_i$，且 $l_i$已包含期初全部有效配额，则应改为 $n_i=e_i+s_i-l_i$；其他配额流入流出也须另行核对。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive $n_i$ means net buying and negative $n_i$ net selling. Marginal choice instead compares $MAC_i(a_i)$ with $p$. Interior buyers and sellers may both have $MAC_i=p$ at their optimum. With a desired closing inventory $s_i$ and $l_i$ already including all valid opening allowances, use $n_i=e_i+s_i-l_i$ and reconcile any other allowance flows separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自造例：两家无治理排放各100，$C_1=a_1^2/2$、$C_2=3a_2^2/2$，总配额100。竞争价格75下，减排分别75、25，最终排放25、75。只改初始配额便得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take baseline emissions of one hundred each, costs $C_1=a_1^2/2$ and $C_2=3a_2^2/2$, and one hundred total allowances. A competitive price of seventy-five gives abatement $(75,25)$ and emissions $(25,75)$. Changing only initial endowments gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 初始配额 $(l_1,l_2)$ | 最终排放 $(e_1,e_2)$ | 净购买 $(n_1,n_2)$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $(50,50)$ | $(25,75)$ | $(-25,+25)$ |
| $(10,90)$ | $(25,75)$ | $(+15,-15)$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Initial allowances | Final emissions | Net purchases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $(50,50)$ | $(25,75)$ | $(-25,+25)$ |
| $(10,90)$ | $(25,75)$ | $(+15,-15)$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二行中第一家虽然更善于减排，仍净买15份配额；第二家反而净卖15份。两家最终 MAC都是75。若从零减排开始，第一家 $MAC_1(0)=0<75$只说明它应增加减排，不能推出最终净卖。配置保持不变所需的条件，见[[初始配额中性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the second row, the lower-cost abater still buys fifteen allowances while the other firm sells fifteen. Both end with MAC seventy-five. Starting from zero abatement, $MAC_1(0)<75$ signals profitable additional abatement, not eventual net selling. [[初始配额中性|Allocation neutrality]] states the conditions under which the final allocation remains unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
