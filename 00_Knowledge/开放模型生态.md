# 开放模型生态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Open Model Ecosystem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 在大量“开放”模型中判断哪些权重、代码、数据和许可真正可用、可复现并适合目标任务。
> **具体锚点：** 一个可下载权重的模型可能允许研究却限制商业用途，也可能完全不公开训练数据；这和开源软件不是同一件事。
> **核心难点：** 模型名、参数量和聊天榜单口径不统一，家族不同版本又有 base/instruct、dense/MoE、上下文和许可证差异。
> **为什么重要：** 选型错误会在部署后表现为许可风险、硬件不匹配、不可验证的数据来源或无法复现实验。
> **继续：** 先确认开放层级与许可证，再比较任务质量、资源与生态；具体性能必须回到 [[LLM 评测]] 和 [[LLM 推理效率]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Problem addressed:** Determine which among many “open” models provide weights, code, data, and permissions that are actually usable, reproducible, and suitable for the target task.
> **Concrete anchor:** A downloadable-weight model may permit research but restrict commercial use and may disclose no training data. That is not the same as open-source software.
> **Central difficulty:** Model names, parameter counts, and chat leaderboards use inconsistent definitions, while versions within one family differ by base versus instruct, dense versus MoE, context, and license.
> **Why it matters:** A poor selection can later surface as licensing risk, hardware mismatch, unverifiable data provenance, or irreproducible experiments.
> **Continue with:** Confirm the openness layer and exact license first, then compare task quality, resources, and ecosystem. Performance claims must return to [[LLM 评测|LLM Evaluation]] and [[LLM 推理效率|LLM Inference Efficiency]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## “开放”有哪些层级
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Layers of Openness*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

open-weight 只保证某种权重可取得；更强的开放还可能包括架构/代码、训练配方、数据组成或数据本身、日志与中间 checkpoints。缺一项仍可有使用价值，但不能笼统称为完全可复现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Open weight guarantees only that some weights are obtainable. Stronger openness may also include architecture and code, training recipes, data composition or data itself, logs, and intermediate checkpoints. A model can remain useful while missing some layers, but should not be called fully reproducible without them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评估时做一张 artifact matrix：权重、推理代码、训练代码、配置、tokenizer、数据清单、数据获取方式、评测代码、日志、checkpoints 与许可证逐项写“可得/不可得/受限”。“模型开放”这一总标签的信息量远低于这张表。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Build an artifact matrix covering weights, inference code, training code, configuration, tokenizer, data manifest, data access, evaluation code, logs, checkpoints, and licenses, with each marked available, unavailable, or restricted. The blanket label “open model” conveys far less than this matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OSI 的 Open Source AI Definition 从使用、研究、修改与共享的自由出发，并要求获得理解和修改系统所需的 preferred form。它提供一种正式口径，但具体 artifact 是否满足仍需按版本检查，不能仅看发布者宣传。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The OSI Open Source AI Definition begins from freedoms to use, study, modify, and share and requires access to the preferred form needed to understand and modify the system. It provides a formal vocabulary, but whether a particular artifact version qualifies still requires inspection rather than reliance on marketing language.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 许可证与使用边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Licenses and Use Boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阅读具体版本许可证：商业使用、再分发、衍生模型、可接受用途、归属和专利条款可能不同。README 的“open source”宣传不能替代许可证文本；模型输出和训练数据权利也可能是另一问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read the exact version's license. Commercial use, redistribution, derivative models, acceptable-use restrictions, attribution, and patent terms may differ. A README's “open source” claim cannot substitute for the license text, and rights concerning outputs or training data may be separate questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

许可证检查要绑定到 artifact 与版本：代码可能使用一种许可证，权重使用另一种，数据再各有条款。发现用途限制、用户规模门槛或强制下游条款时，应交由合格法律人员按实际部署判断；知识卡只负责暴露问题，不给法律结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
License review must bind terms to each artifact and version: code may use one license, weights another, and data separate terms. When use restrictions, user-scale thresholds, or downstream obligations appear, qualified legal review should assess the real deployment; this knowledge card identifies questions rather than giving legal conclusions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 模型家族和版本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Model Families and Versions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记录精确 artifact：家族、版本、base/instruct、参数/激活参数、量化、tokenizer、上下文、发布日期和 hash。不同 tokenizer 或 chat template 会显著改变结果，不能只写“Llama/Qwen/DeepSeek”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Record the exact artifact: family, version, base or instruct, total and active parameters, quantization, tokenizer, context, release date, and hash. Different tokenizers or chat templates can materially change results, so writing only a family name is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

model card 应把预期用途、限制、训练/评测数据说明、指标、风险和使用示例与 artifact 绑定。下载时同时保存配置、tokenizer、generation defaults 和 revision；上游仓库的浮动 main branch 不能作为可复现实验标识。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A model card should bind intended use, limitations, training and evaluation data descriptions, metrics, risks, and examples to the artifact. Save configuration, tokenizer, generation defaults, and revision with the download; a floating upstream main branch is not a reproducible experiment identifier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 技术报告比较
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Comparing Technical Reports*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将报告中的训练 token、数据类别、架构、精度、对齐方法和评测预算放到同一口径。厂商自报结果可用于了解设计，不应在不同提示、工具或采样预算下直接排行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Normalize training tokens, data categories, architecture, precision, alignment method, and evaluation budget across reports. Vendor-reported results can explain a design but should not be ranked directly when prompts, tools, or sampling budgets differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 MoE 同时记录 total 与 active parameters；对量化版本记录方法、group size、校准集和目标 kernel；对 instruct 版本记录 base、SFT/偏好阶段和 chat template。看似同规模的两个下载文件可能有完全不同的计算和行为来源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For MoE, record total and active parameters. For quantized versions, record method, group size, calibration set, and target kernel. For instruct versions, record the base model, SFT and preference stages, and chat template. Two downloads that look equally large can have very different computational and behavioral origins.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 复现与数据透明度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Reproducibility and Data Transparency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可复现至少需要获得 artifact、执行环境、提示/评测代码和数据版本。训练级复现还需数据与配方，成本可能使“公开”仍不可实际重训。数据透明度影响污染、版权、偏见和领域覆盖判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reproduction minimally requires the artifact, execution environment, prompts and evaluation code, and data version. Training-level reproduction additionally requires data and recipe, and cost can make an open release impractical to retrain. Data transparency affects judgments about contamination, copyright, bias, and domain coverage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区分 artifact reproducibility、evaluation reproducibility 与 training reproducibility。能复跑权重推理不等于能复现报告分数；能复现分数也不等于能从原始数据重训出同一模型。结论必须写清是哪一层。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish artifact reproducibility, evaluation reproducibility, and training reproducibility. Re-running inference does not imply reproducing reported scores; reproducing scores does not imply retraining the same model from raw data. Claims must name the layer they establish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：两个“开放”模型怎样比较
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Compare Two “Open” Models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模型 A 发布权重、推理代码和宽松权重许可证，但只给数据类别；模型 B 发布权重、训练代码、完整配方与可重建数据清单，却限制商业部署。研究训练机制时 B 可能更可复现；商用本地部署时 A 可能更合适。不存在脱离用途的单一“更开放”排行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Model A releases weights, inference code, and a permissive weight license but only high-level data categories. Model B releases weights, training code, a full recipe, and a reconstructable data manifest but restricts commercial deployment. B may be more reproducible for studying training, while A may better suit commercial local deployment. There is no single context-free ranking of openness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两者性能比较还要锁定精确 instruct revision、chat template、quantization、context 和推理预算，在自己的任务上重测。若 A 的 leaderboard 分高但许可证不允许目标用途，这一分数不进入可行方案集合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Performance comparison must also fix the exact instruct revision, chat template, quantization, context, and inference budget and rerun on the target task. If A leads a leaderboard but its license does not permit the intended use, that score does not place it in the feasible set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 选型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Selection Workflow*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先列硬约束：许可、部署位置、语言/领域、上下文、延迟、显存、安全和维护。再在真实任务上比较 base/instruct 与量化版本，保留失败案例；生态规模只有在它减少集成和运维成本时才有意义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
List hard constraints first: license, deployment location, language and domain, context, latency, memory, safety, and maintenance. Then compare base, instruct, and quantized variants on real tasks and retain failure cases. Ecosystem size matters only when it reduces integration and operational cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 无法确认可否使用：停止在 README 推测，保存精确许可证版本并让有资格的人按用途审查。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Use permission is unclear: stop inferring from the README, preserve the exact license revision, and obtain qualified review for the intended use.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 同名模型结果无法复现：补 revision/hash、tokenizer、template、precision、generation config 与依赖。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Results for a named model cannot be reproduced: add revision or hash, tokenizer, template, precision, generation configuration, and dependencies.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 本地吞吐远低于报告：核对硬件、kernel、batch、长度分布、量化和 active parameters，转到 [[LLM 推理效率]]。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Local throughput is far below the report: align hardware, kernels, batch, length distribution, quantization, and active parameters, then use [[LLM 推理效率|LLM Inference Efficiency]].
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 排行好但目标任务差：检查 benchmark 匹配、chat template、语言/领域数据和评测预算，回到 [[LLM 评测]]。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Leaderboard performance is strong but target-task performance poor: inspect benchmark match, chat template, language and domain data, and evaluation budget, then return to [[LLM 评测|LLM Evaluation]].
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum Self-Check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 可下载权重为什么不自动等于开源软件？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why do downloadable weights not automatically equal open-source software?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 许可证、训练代码、数据和配方可能不开放；开放权重只是开放层级之一。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Licenses, training code, data, and recipes may remain closed; open weights are only one layer of openness.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 记录模型实验时为什么必须写精确版本和 chat template？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must a model experiment record an exact version and chat template?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 同一家族不同版本、tokenizer/模板会改变输入分布和输出，模糊名称无法复现。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Versions, tokenizers, and templates within one family change input distribution and output; a vague family name cannot reproduce the experiment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 两个技术报告的榜单能否直接比较？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Can leaderboards in two technical reports be compared directly?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 只有任务版本、提示、采样/工具预算和评分口径一致时才近似可比；最好在自己的任务上重测。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> They are approximately comparable only when task versions, prompts, sampling and tool budgets, and scoring definitions match; rerunning on the target tasks is preferable.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一份开放模型审计最少应列哪些 artifact？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Which artifacts should a minimal open-model audit list?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 权重、配置与 tokenizer、推理/训练代码、训练配方、数据清单或访问方式、评测代码、版本标识，以及各自许可证与限制。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> List weights, configuration and tokenizer, inference and training code, training recipe, data manifest or access method, evaluation code, version identifiers, and the license and restrictions for each.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
