# 实施能力诊断应从具体交付缺口追查组织条件及其治理激励

诊断[[公共组织实施能力]]时，先指出哪项任务在哪一步没有兑现，再查人员、资金、信息和管理，以及支配这些环节的治理激励。一个交付缺口可以有多个相互作用的来源；“能力低”只是待拆解的判断，不是已经找到原因。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diagnosing [[公共组织实施能力|implementation capacity]] starts with the specific task and delivery step that fell short, then examines personnel, finance, information, management and their governance incentives. Several interacting weaknesses can produce one gap. “Low capacity” names a question rather than identifying its cause.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**先固定要解释的缺口。** 写明机构职责、服务对象、标准和期限，用[[项目投入产出与结果|投入与实际交付记录]]区分“钱未到”“活动未完成”和“完成后结果未改善”。最后一种还可能涉及政策机制、使用条件或外部变化，不能直接倒推某个实施环节失灵。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First specify the mandate, beneficiaries, standard and deadline. Use [[项目投入产出与结果|input and delivery records]] to distinguish missing funds, undelivered activities and outcomes that failed to improve after delivery. The last case can involve policy mechanisms, use or external change rather than a particular implementation failure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**沿组织环节提出可核假说。** 下表是检查方向，不是每个项目都必须有同一种制度形式：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Generate testable explanations along the organizational chain. These are diagnostic questions, not a requirement that every project adopt one institutional form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 环节 | 针对具体任务核什么 |
|---|---|
| 人员 | 是否有相应技能、岗位配置与努力激励？ |
| 财务资源 | 是否有可用且及时到达的资金，支出程序能否执行？ |
| 信息系统 | 是否实际收集、使用并共享所需的可靠信息，而非仅安装系统？ |
| 管理 | 责任、协调、授权与监督是否使任务可完成？ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check task-relevant skills and incentives; usable, timely finance; reliable information actually collected and shared; and responsibility, coordination, delegation and oversight that permit delivery.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**把治理问题加到每个环节。** 谁能看到真实行动，谁能反馈并追究责任，谁可能为私人利益扭曲资源或决定？例如缺技术人员，既可能因为招聘标准错误，也可能因为机构没有专业岗位，或招聘受到关系影响。访谈、招聘与支出记录、交付核验等证据应能区分这些解释；不能只列七个维度就宣称诊断完成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply governance questions to each component: who can observe actions, influence decisions and enforce consequences, and who may distort them for private gain? Missing expertise might reflect recruitment criteria, absent specialist posts or patronage. Interviews and recruitment, expenditure and delivery records should distinguish these explanations; listing dimensions is not a diagnosis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**再比较可执行的改动。** 复杂工具的要求要落到具体数据、技能和监督任务。例如按每段道路的实时交通与损坏程度安排养护，理论上可以比固定巡检规则更有针对性；但若实时数据无法持续核验，精细公式可能只是精细地使用错误信息。此时应比较补足信息能力、修改分配规则或采用较易核验的方案，同时计入遗漏需求与执行成本。这是条件化构造，不证明简单工具总是更好，也不证明能力提升应一概先于政策。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare implementable changes by spelling out each instrument's data, skills and oversight requirements. A constructed example is road maintenance allocated by real-time traffic and damage: finer targeting may help, but unreliable inputs can undermine it. Compare improving information, revising the rule or using a more verifiable alternative, including unmet needs and operating costs. Simplicity is not universally superior, nor must all capacity building precede policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

诊断的可用产物是“待验证的瓶颈—支持和反驳它的证据—可行改动—后续检查”，而非给出一个总排名。完成这一步仍不等于证明政策有效或值得推广；净效果要另按[[公共项目反事实]]比较，公众利益与可行替代还涉及[[政府失灵|政府失灵的判定边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful diagnosis specifies a candidate constraint, supporting and contrary evidence, a feasible change and follow-up checks, not merely a ranking. It does not establish effectiveness or justify expansion: net effects need a [[公共项目反事实|counterfactual]], while public interests and feasible alternatives also concern the [[政府失灵|boundary for identifying government failure]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Kim 等（2026），[Institutional Capacity for Policy Implementation: An Analytical Framework](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099635001072670597/pdf/IDU-3f31d845-5873-45f2-8870-25d1793e57cd.pdf#page=7)，Working Paper11279，2026年1月版：§3.1印刷5–12页（PDF7–14）支持组织环节、实际功能与形式的区别、信息使用、监督与裁量的情境性；§3.3印刷20页（PDF22）及表1–2（PDF23–26）支持交叉诊断；§4印刷25页（PDF27）明确表中只是潜在解释、多个维度可以相互作用。该框架不提供自动判因算法或所有工具的优劣排序。
- 道路养护规则及“瓶颈—证据—改动—检查”的写法是依框架组织的教学应用；结果层次、反事实与评价标准复用所链接共享对象，不把该论文当作已经识别道路效果的研究。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paper supports component-wise and cross-cutting diagnosis, functional rather than merely formal assessment, and context-dependent management trade-offs. Its tables suggest possible explanations rather than an automatic causal classifier. The road-maintenance example and diagnostic workflow are teaching applications; existing shared objects supply outcome, counterfactual and evaluation distinctions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
