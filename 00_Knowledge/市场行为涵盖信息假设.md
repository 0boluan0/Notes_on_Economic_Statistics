# 市场行为涵盖信息假设是把影响价格的因素视为已反映在行情中的技术分析前提
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The market-action assumption treats factors affecting price as already reflected in observable market behaviour.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“市场行为涵盖信息”是技术分析的一项工作假设：影响证券价格的因素已经通过交易行为反映在价格、成交量等行情中，因此可以从行情入手研究市场。原课使用更强的“一切信息”表述；这是该分析框架的前提，不是本卡证明所有市场都满足的事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The market-action assumption treats influences on a security's price as already expressed through trading behaviour, such as prices and volume, making market records a starting point for analysis. The course states the stronger version, “all information.” It is a premise of the framework, not a fact established here for every market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它回答“为什么研究行情”，却没有回答“行情中的哪些可见特征还能预测未来”。尤其不能仅凭“价格包含信息”，就推出利用历史价格能够取得超额收益。[[有效市场假说]] 讨论的是相对指定信息集，价格是否已经充分反映信息；[[有效市场三种形式]] 进一步区分历史交易信息、公开信息和更广信息集。两种表述相近，不代表经验含义相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This motivates studying market records but does not identify which observable features can still predict the future. The claim that prices contain information alone does not imply that past prices yield excess returns. The [[有效市场假说|efficient market hypothesis]] asks whether prices fully reflect a specified information set; its [[有效市场三种形式|three forms]] distinguish past trading information, public information, and broader sets. Similar wording need not have the same empirical meaning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如观察到公告后价格立刻上涨，可能是价格吸收新增信息的结果；它本身既不能证明公告前所有信息都已进入价格，也不能证明公告后的上涨仍可被无风险捕捉。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An immediate price rise after an announcement may reflect the incorporation of new information. It does not establish that all information was reflected beforehand or that the rise remained available as a riskless trading opportunity afterward.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
