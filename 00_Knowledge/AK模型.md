# AK 模型是采用线性生产技术 Y=AK 的资本积累模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The AK model is a capital-accumulation model with the linear production technology Y=AK.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

AK 模型用 $Y(t)=AK(t)$ 表示总产出：$K(t)>0$ 是可再生产的资本存量，$Y(t)$ 是单位时间的产出，$A>0$ 是固定的单位资本生产率。因此资本增加一倍，产出也增加一倍；资本的边际产出始终是 $A$，不会随着资本积累而下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The AK model writes aggregate output as $Y(t)=AK(t)$. Here $K(t)>0$ is reproducible capital, $Y(t)$ is output per unit of time, and $A>0$ is a fixed productivity coefficient. Doubling capital doubles output, and its marginal product remains $A$ as capital accumulates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在最小的封闭单商品经济中，同一种商品可用于消费 $C$ 或总投资 $I$。若资本按速率 $\delta\ge0$ 折旧，资源约束与积累方程为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the minimal closed economy, one good can be consumed, $C$, or invested, $I$. With capital depreciation at rate $\delta\ge0$, the resource constraint and accumulation equation are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y=C+I,\qquad \dot K=I-\delta K=AK-C-\delta K.
$$

这些关系还没有确定消费和投资如何分配。可以给定储蓄率，也可以让家庭选择消费路径；这是 [[AK闭合方式]] 要补上的行为关系。生产技术本身不等于完整均衡，也不单独保证人均正增长，具体条件见 [[AK人均增长条件]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These relationships do not yet determine the allocation between consumption and investment. A saving rule or household optimisation supplies the missing behavioural relation; see [[AK闭合方式|AK model closure]]. The technology alone is neither a complete equilibrium nor a guarantee of positive per-capita growth; see [[AK人均增长条件|the per-capita growth condition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 $A$ 保持不变，增长动力来自资本积累，因此 AK 可以说明一种 [[内生增长]]，但没有解释新技术如何发明。最纯粹的 $Y=AK$ 也没有把原始劳动列为独立生产投入。若把 $K$ 解释为包含设备、技能等的广义资本，仍需说明这些要素怎样合成以及为何维持线性回报；“广义资本”这个名称本身不提供证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $A$ stays fixed and accumulation drives growth, providing one route to [[内生增长|endogenous growth]] without explaining invention. Pure $Y=AK$ also omits raw labour as a separate productive input. Interpreting $K$ as a broad aggregate of equipment, skills and other assets still requires explaining aggregation and the linear return; the label alone does not establish either.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线性技术是一项强假设，而非所有资本的普遍性质。与之对照，标准 Solow 模型的资本回报与端点条件产生有限的正稳态，见 [[Solow 稳态条件]]。更一般的持续增长模型也可能在部分区间有递减回报；这张卡只定义纯 AK 技术。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Linearity is a strong modelling assumption, not a universal property of capital. Standard Solow return and endpoint conditions instead produce a finite positive steady state; see [[Solow 稳态条件|Solow steady-state conditions]]. More general sustained-growth models may have diminishing returns over some ranges; this card defines pure AK technology.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
