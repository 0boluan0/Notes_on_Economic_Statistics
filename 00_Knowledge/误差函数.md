# The error function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*误差函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The error function gives a standard name to the accumulated area under the Gaussian curve $e^{-t^2}$, measured from zero and multiplied by a fixed scale factor. Its name does not mean the error left by a numerical approximation. It is a function in its own right, useful for expressing probabilities and for calculating an integral whose antiderivative is not elementary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

误差函数给高斯曲线 $e^{-t^2}$ 从零开始的累积面积取了一个标准名称，并乘上固定的比例因子。这里“误差”不是某次数值近似留下的误差；它本身就是一个函数，可以表达概率，也能用来计算原函数并非初等函数的积分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]] makes $e^{-t^2}$ positive and continuous for every real $t$. It has height one at zero, is unchanged when $t$ is replaced by $-t$, and decreases as $|t|$ grows. For any finite endpoint $x$, the [[连续函数可积|continuous-integrability theorem]] guarantees that the integral over the finite segment between $0$ and $x$ exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[自然指数函数]]的性质，$e^{-t^2}$ 在每个实数 $t$ 处都为正且连续。它在零点高度为一，把 $t$ 换成 $-t$ 后不变，并随 $|t|$ 增大而下降。对任意有限终点 $x$，[[连续函数可积]]保证 $0$ 与 $x$ 之间的有限线段上积分存在。

> [!important]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every real $x$, define
>
> $$\operatorname{erf}(x)=\frac{2}{\sqrt\pi}\int_0^x e^{-t^2}\,dt.$$
>
> The symbol $\pi$ is the circle constant, $t$ is the integration variable, and $x$ selects the endpoint. The factor $2/\sqrt\pi$ is part of the definition. For $x<0$, the [[定积分|oriented integral]] equals $-\int_x^0e^{-t^2}\,dt$, so the value is negative.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $\pi$ 是圆周率，$t$ 是积分变量，$x$ 决定积分终点。系数 $2/\sqrt\pi$ 是定义的一部分。若 $x<0$，按[[定积分|有向积分]]的约定，积分等于 $-\int_x^0e^{-t^2}\,dt$，因此函数值为负。

## Why this particular scale factor
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么选这个比例因子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The separate [[高斯积分|Gaussian integral theorem]] gives $\int_0^\infty e^{-t^2}\,dt=\sqrt\pi/2$. Multiplying by its reciprocal normalises the positive-half-line total to one. Symmetry and the reversed-limit convention then give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

独立的[[高斯积分]]定理给出 $\int_0^\infty e^{-t^2}\,dt=\sqrt\pi/2$。乘以它的倒数，就把正半轴上的总量归一化为一。再由对称性和反向积分约定，得到

$$\operatorname{erf}(0)=0,\qquad
\operatorname{erf}(-x)=-\operatorname{erf}(x),\qquad
\lim_{x\to\infty}\operatorname{erf}(x)=1,\quad
\lim_{x\to-\infty}\operatorname{erf}(x)=-1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For finite $x$, a strictly positive tail of the Gaussian integral remains, so $-1<\operatorname{erf}(x)<1$: the limiting values are never attained. The [[积分求导定理|accumulation differentiation theorem]] gives

$$\operatorname{erf}'(x)=\frac{2}{\sqrt\pi}e^{-x^2}>0.$$

Thus the function increases continuously from a limit of $-1$ to a limit of $1$. For example, $\operatorname{erf}(1)\approx0.842701$ and $\operatorname{erf}(-1)\approx-0.842701$. The [[误差函数幂级数|error-function series]] supplies these values with controllable truncation error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限的 $x$ 后面仍留下严格为正的高斯积分尾部，因此 $-1<\operatorname{erf}(x)<1$，不会在有限位置取到两端极限。由[[积分求导定理]]得到上面的正导数，所以函数连续递增，从极限 $-1$ 走向极限 $1$。例如，$\operatorname{erf}(1)\approx0.842701$，$\operatorname{erf}(-1)\approx-0.842701$。[[误差函数幂级数]]能够在控制截断误差的同时计算这些值。

## Translate it into a normal probability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*换成正态概率的语言*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Z$ have the [[正态分布|standard normal distribution]], with mean $0$ and variance $1$. Its [[累积分布函数|cumulative distribution function]] $\Phi(z)=P(Z\le z)$ accumulates density from $-\infty$, whereas erf starts at zero. Their exponent scales also differ: the normal density is $e^{-u^2/2}/\sqrt{2\pi}$. Splitting its integral at zero and substituting $u=\sqrt2\,t$ gives, for every real $z$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $Z$ 服从均值为 $0$、方差为 $1$ 的[[正态分布|标准正态分布]]。其[[累积分布函数]] $\Phi(z)=P(Z\le z)$ 从 $-\infty$ 开始累积密度，而 erf 从零开始。指数中的尺度也不同：标准正态密度是 $e^{-u^2/2}/\sqrt{2\pi}$。把积分在零处分开，再代入 $u=\sqrt2\,t$，对每个实数 $z$ 得到

$$\begin{aligned}
\Phi(z)&=\frac12+\frac1{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_0^z e^{-u^2/2}\,du\\
&=\frac12+\frac1{\sqrt\pi}\int_0^{z/\sqrt2}e^{-t^2}\,dt
=\frac12\left[1+\operatorname{erf}\!\left(\frac z{\sqrt2}\right)\right].
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalently, $\operatorname{erf}(x)=2\Phi(\sqrt2\,x)-1$. Both the shift by $1/2$ and the rescaling by $\sqrt2$ matter. In particular, erf is not itself a CDF on the real line: it takes negative values when $x<0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等价地，$\operatorname{erf}(x)=2\Phi(\sqrt2\,x)-1$。其中 $1/2$ 的平移与 $\sqrt2$ 的缩放都不能省略。特别地，erf 本身不是实数轴上的 CDF，因为 $x<0$ 时它取负值。

> [!example]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $x\ge0$, symmetry gives
>
> $$P(-\sqrt2\,x\le Z\le\sqrt2\,x)=\operatorname{erf}(x).$$
>
> A probability within one standard deviation therefore uses $x=1/\sqrt2$, not $x=1$:
> $P(-1\le Z\le1)=\operatorname{erf}(1/\sqrt2)\approx0.682689$. At zero, the contrasting values $\Phi(0)=1/2$ and $\operatorname{erf}(0)=0$ immediately check which accumulation is being used.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $x\ge0$，由对称性得到上面的中心区间概率。因此，“均值左右一个标准差”的概率要取 $x=1/\sqrt2$，得到 $P(-1\le Z\le1)=\operatorname{erf}(1/\sqrt2)\approx0.682689$，而不是取 $x=1$。零点的 $\Phi(0)=1/2$ 与 $\operatorname{erf}(0)=0$ 也能快速检查自己正在使用哪一种累积。

## An integral definition is already a definition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*积分定义本身已经确定了函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Gaussian kernel has [[无初等原函数的积分|no elementary antiderivative]]: that is a result about the allowed class of finite formulas, not a failure of this integral to exist. The MIT calculus source states this limitation without proving the advanced non-elementarity theorem. The definition, derivative, normalisation and probability relation above do not require such a proof. Naming erf lets us state the answer precisely; the separate series card explains how to approximate it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

高斯核[[无初等原函数的积分|没有初等原函数]]，这是对有限公式表达类别的限制，不是说这里的积分不存在。MIT 微积分来源陈述这一限制，但不证明背后进阶的非初等性定理。上面的定义、导数、归一化与概率关系都不需要依赖该证明。命名 erf 使我们能准确表达答案，另外的级数卡则解释如何近似计算它。
