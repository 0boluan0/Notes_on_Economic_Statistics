# Derivative tests for monotonicity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*导数单调性判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sign of a slope describes the direction of change near a point. A **derivative test for monotonicity** turns that local information into a statement about an entire interval: if all slopes point upwards, later inputs give larger outputs. The word “all” matters; one upward-pointing tangent cannot describe a whole curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

斜率的符号描述一点附近的变化方向。**导数单调性判据**把这种局部信息转成整个区间的结论：若所有斜率都向上，后面的输入就产生更大的输出。“所有”很关键，一条向上的切线不能说明整条曲线的走向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To say $f$ is **nondecreasing** on an interval $I$ means that any two points $x_1<x_2$ in $I$ satisfy $f(x_1)\le f(x_2)$. **Strictly increasing** replaces $\le$ by $<$, so no two ordered inputs have equal outputs. Nonincreasing and strictly decreasing reverse the inequalities. These definitions compare pairs of function values, while a [[导数|derivative]] measures the local rate at one point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

称 $f$ 在区间 $I$ 上**非减**，是说 $I$ 内任意 $x_1<x_2$ 都满足 $f(x_1)\le f(x_2)$。**严格递增**则把 $\le$ 换成 $<$，不允许两处按大小排列的输入产生相同输出。非增与严格递减把不等号反向。这些定义比较两个函数值，而[[导数]]衡量单点的局部变化率。

> [!example] A square changes direction at zero · 平方函数在零点两侧改变方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^2$, the derivative $2x$ is negative when $x<0$ and positive when $x>0$. Thus the function is strictly decreasing on $(-\infty,0]$ and strictly increasing on $[0,\infty)$. The value of the derivative at zero is zero; this does not prevent either one-sided interval statement.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $f(x)=x^2$，导数 $2x$ 在 $x<0$ 时为负，在 $x>0$ 时为正。因此函数在 $(-\infty,0]$ 上严格递减，在 $[0,\infty)$ 上严格递增。零点的导数为零，并不妨碍这两个单侧区间结论。

## Conditions that connect slopes to values
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把斜率与函数值连接起来的条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $I$ be an interval containing more than one point. Assume $f$ is differentiable throughout its interior and [[一点连续|continuous]] at any endpoints that belong to $I$: the value there agrees with the limit from inside the interval. The interval can be open, closed, half-open or unbounded. Under these conditions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $I$ 是包含不止一个点的区间，$f$ 在内部处处可导，并在属于 $I$ 的端点处[[一点连续|连续]]，即该端点函数值等于从区间内趋近时的极限。区间可以开、闭、半开，也可以无界。在这些条件下：

> [!important] Keep strict and weak signs distinct · 区分严格与非严格符号
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $f'\ge0$ throughout the interior, $f$ is nondecreasing. If $f'>0$ throughout, it is strictly increasing. Similarly, $f'\le0$ gives nonincreasing behavior and $f'<0$ gives strict decrease. If $f'=0$ throughout, the function is constant on $I$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若内部处处 $f'\ge0$，则 $f$ 非减；若处处 $f'>0$，则严格递增。同样，$f'\le0$ 给出非增，$f'<0$ 给出严格递减。若内部处处 $f'=0$，函数就在整个 $I$ 上为常数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove this, choose any $x_1<x_2$ in $I$. The whole segment $[x_1,x_2]$ is still inside $I$, so the [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]] supplies a point $c\in(x_1,x_2)$ with
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明时，任取 $I$ 内 $x_1<x_2$。整段 $[x_1,x_2]$ 仍位于 $I$ 内，因此[[拉格朗日中值定理]]给出某个 $c\in(x_1,x_2)$，使

$$
f(x_2)-f(x_1)=f'(c)(x_2-x_1).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The length $x_2-x_1$ is positive, so the sign of the function difference follows from the derivative sign. Because the pair was arbitrary, the conclusion holds throughout $I$. No continuity of the derivative is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长度 $x_2-x_1$ 为正，所以函数差的符号由导数决定。由于两点是任取的，结论就在整个 $I$ 上成立。这里不需要导数本身连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A zero derivative does not automatically make a plateau
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一点导数为零，不等于出现平坦区间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A differentiable nondecreasing function must have $f'(x)\ge0$ at every interior point: for a positive input step the difference quotient is nonnegative, and for a negative step both numerator and denominator are nonpositive. Taking their limit preserves the weak inequality. Strict increase does not strengthen this conclusion to $f'>0$ everywhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可导的非减函数在每个内部点必有 $f'(x)\ge0$：输入步长为正时，差商非负；步长为负时，分子、分母都非正，差商仍非负。取极限保留这个非严格不等号。但严格递增不能把结论加强为导数处处严格为正。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $f(x)=x^3$ is strictly increasing, although $f'(0)=0$. To see strictness directly, for $a<b$ write $b^3-a^3=(b-a)(a^2+ab+b^2)>0$; the second factor is positive unless $a=b=0$, which is excluded. Thus a zero at an isolated point need not destroy strict increase. It is the behavior across intervals, not a single zero, that matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $f(x)=x^3$ 严格递增，却有 $f'(0)=0$。直接核对：对 $a<b$，$b^3-a^3=(b-a)(a^2+ab+b^2)>0$，第二个因子只有在 $a=b=0$ 时才可能为零，而此情形已被排除。因此孤立的一点导数为零，不一定破坏严格递增；要看的是区间上的行为。

> [!warning] Do not cross a gap or an endpoint jump · 不要跨过定义域缺口或端点跳跃
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The function $1/x$ has derivative $-1/x^2<0$ on its domain, but its domain has two separate intervals. It decreases on each of them, not on their union: $-1<1$ while $f(-1)=-1<f(1)=1$. The theorem cannot bridge the missing point zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 函数 $1/x$ 在定义域内都有导数 $-1/x^2<0$，但定义域分为两个区间。它在各区间内递减，却不在两者的并集上递减：$-1<1$，但 $f(-1)=-1<f(1)=1$。定理不能跨过缺失的零点。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Endpoint continuity also matters. On $[0,1]$, define $g(0)=2$ and $g(x)=x$ for $x>0$. Its interior derivative is $1$, yet $g(0)>g(1)$. The jump at the included endpoint violates the hypotheses.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 端点连续也很重要。在 $[0,1]$ 上定义 $g(0)=2$，$x>0$ 时 $g(x)=x$。内部导数处处为 $1$，但 $g(0)>g(1)$。包含的端点出现跳跃，违反了定理条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a sign chart, first find intervals on which the derivative sign is justified throughout; then apply this criterion to each interval. Testing a single point is safe only after a reason has established that the sign cannot change within that interval. The [[一阶导数检验|first derivative test]] next compares the directions on the two sides of a candidate extremum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

制作符号表时，先找出能证明导数全段同号的区间，再分别应用本判据。只有已经有理由确定该段不会变号时，检验一个代表点才足够。下一步的[[一阶导数检验]]会比较候选极值点两侧的单调方向。
