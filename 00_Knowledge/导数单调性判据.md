# 区间上导数的符号通过平均值定理决定函数的单调方向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The sign of the derivative on an interval determines monotonic direction through the mean value theorem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 设 $f$ 在区间 $I$ 的内部可导，并在所含端点处连续。若内部处处 $f'\ge0$，则 $f$ 非减；若处处 $f'>0$，则 $f$ 严格递增。把不等号反向得到非增与严格递减；若处处 $f'=0$，则 $f$ 为常函数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $f$ be differentiable in the interior of an interval and continuous at any included endpoints. If $f'\ge0$ throughout the interior, $f$ is nondecreasing; if $f'>0$, it is strictly increasing. Reversing the signs gives the decreasing cases, and $f'=0$ throughout forces $f$ to be constant.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明只需任取 $x_1<x_2$。[[拉格朗日中值定理]] 给某个 $c\in(x_1,x_2)$ 使
$$
f(x_2)-f(x_1)=f'(c)(x_2-x_1).
$$
因为第二个因子为正，函数差的符号由导数决定。关键是导数符号要控制整个区间，而不是只看几个采样点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any $x_1<x_2$, the mean value theorem gives $f(x_2)-f(x_1)=f'(c)(x_2-x_1)$ for some intermediate $c$. The second factor is positive, so the derivative controls the sign of the function change. The sign condition must cover the interval, not a few sampled points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逆命题要分清强弱。若可导函数非减，则每个存在的导数都 $\ge0$；但严格递增不要求导数处处严格为正，例如 $x^3$ 严格递增却有 $f'(0)=0$。因此在符号表里，孤立的零点可以分隔检验区间，却不自动破坏严格单调。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The converse requires care. A differentiable nondecreasing function has nonnegative derivative wherever it exists, but strict increase does not require a strictly positive derivative at every point: $x^3$ is strictly increasing although $f'(0)=0$. An isolated zero can divide a sign chart without destroying strict monotonicity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么只知道 $f'(1)>0$ 不能证明 $f$ 在 $[0,2]$ 递增？
>
> **答案：** 一点的导数只给局部信息；MVT 的区间结论需要控制每个可能中间点的导数符号。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why does $f'(1)>0$ not prove that $f$ increases on $[0,2]$?
>
> **Answer:** A derivative at one point supplies only local information. The mean value theorem's interval-wide conclusion requires controlling the sign at every possible intermediate point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
