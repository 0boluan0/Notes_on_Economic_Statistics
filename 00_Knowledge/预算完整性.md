# 预算完整性要求政府资金活动在适当的预算授权与总体财政呈现中得到全面覆盖
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Budget comprehensiveness requires government financial activities to be fully covered by appropriate authorisation and the overall fiscal presentation.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预算完整性要求看全政府如何筹资、使用资源并承担义务，避免仅因资金被放在不同账户或机构就遗漏。它同时涉及各项活动是否有适当授权，以及预算文件能否呈现整体资金安排和风险；不要求所有活动都适用同一年度拨款程序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Budget comprehensiveness requires a full view of how government finances activities, uses resources and incurs obligations. Moving funds between accounts or institutions should not make them disappear. Appropriate authorisation and a comprehensive fiscal presentation need not mean one annual appropriation procedure for every activity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，某社会保险基金由常设法律安排收支，可以有自己的预算和账户；全面呈现时仍应说明它与中央预算的转移、余额和风险。是否属于政府统计范围由[[一般政府与公共部门]]判断，内部往来如何抵销由[[财政合并口径]]处理，不能简单把各张报表相加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A social insurance fund may operate under standing legislation with separate accounts, while its transfers, balance and risks remain part of the overall presentation. Use [[一般政府与公共部门|institutional-sector boundaries]] to determine coverage and [[财政合并口径|fiscal consolidation]] to avoid double-counting internal transactions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完整性还要求让决策者看到当期拨款之外的负担，例如[[财政或有负债]]。披露一项担保不意味着把其全部面额立即记为本期支出；授权、风险披露与会计确认是不同问题。信息是否及时清楚、可供公众比较，则由[[预算透明度]]进一步判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Comprehensiveness also brings obligations beyond current appropriations, such as [[财政或有负债|contingent liabilities]], into view. Disclosing a guarantee does not mean recognising its full face value as current expenditure. Authorisation, risk disclosure and accounting recognition are distinct; [[预算透明度|budget transparency]] concerns how usable the information is.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [OECD, Recommendation on Budgetary Governance](https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/public/doc/319/body-text.en.html)，原则 6、9：支持全面呈现收入、支出、资产负债及财政风险。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Principles 6 and 9 support comprehensive presentation of revenue, expenditure, assets, liabilities and fiscal risks.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [OECD, Quality Budget Institutions, Chapter 9](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/quality-budget-institutions_8e811202-en/full-report/budget-transparency_ed612352.html)，§9.3.1：支持社会保险、地方和预算拨款范围以外活动的整体呈现。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Section 9.3.1 supports coverage beyond annual appropriations, including social security and subnational activities.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
