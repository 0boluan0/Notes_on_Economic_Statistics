# Derivatives of exponential functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*指数函数求导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an [[指数函数|exponential]] $a^x$, the amount of change is proportional to the current function value. Its [[导数|derivative]] measures the instantaneous version of that change. The proportionality factor depends on the fixed base: it is $\ln a$, the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] of that base. For instance, the slope of $2^x$ at zero is $\ln2\approx0.693147$, while its increase over the whole interval from zero to one is $2^1-2^0=1$. A local rate and a one-unit increase are different quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[指数函数]] $a^x$，变化量与当前函数值成比例。[[导数]]衡量这种变化在瞬间的速度，比例因子由固定底数决定，即底数的[[自然对数]] $\ln a$。例如 $2^x$ 在零点的斜率是 $\ln2\approx0.693147$，但从零到一整个区间的增加量为 $2^1-2^0=1$。局部变化率和整整一步的增加量不是同一个量。

> [!important] Fixed positive base · 固定的正底数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a fixed real $a>0$ and every real $x$,
>
> $$\frac{d}{dx}a^x=(\ln a)a^x.$$
>
> The base $a$ stays constant while $x$ changes. The formula includes $a=1$: $\ln1=0$ gives the derivative of the constant function $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定实数 $a>0$ 后，上式对所有实数 $x$ 成立。变化的是 $x$，底数 $a$ 不动。它也包含 $a=1$：由于 $\ln1=0$，正好得到常数函数 $1$ 的导数为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the factor is a logarithm
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么因子是对数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]] is normalised so that the derivative of $e^z$ is $e^z$. Since $a=e^{\ln a}$, we can express every positive base in this scale. Then use the [[链式法则|chain rule]]: differentiate the outer exponential at the inner value, and multiply by the derivative of that inner value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[自然指数函数]]已经标准化：$e^z$ 对 $z$ 求导仍是 $e^z$。由于 $a=e^{\ln a}$，每个正底数都可以换成这个尺度。再使用[[链式法则]]：先在内层取值处求外层指数函数的导数，再乘以内层的导数。

$$a^x=e^{x\ln a},\qquad\frac{d}{dx}e^{x\ln a}=e^{x\ln a}\ln a.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\ln a$ is a constant, so the inner linear function $x\ln a$ has derivative $\ln a$. For $a=e$ this factor is one. If $a>1$ it is positive; if $0<a<1$ it is negative, matching exponential growth and decay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $\ln a$ 是常数，所以内层一次函数 $x\ln a$ 的导数为 $\ln a$。$a=e$ 时这个因子等于一；$a>1$ 时因子为正；$0<a<1$ 时因子为负，与指数增长和衰减一致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same structure is visible directly in the difference quotient:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在差商中也能直接看到同一结构：

$$\frac{a^{x+h}-a^x}{h}=a^x\frac{a^h-1}{h}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After taking $h\to0$, the second factor is the slope at zero, traditionally denoted $M(a)$. The formula just established proves that this limit exists and equals $\ln a$. Factoring the difference quotient alone would not prove that a limit exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $h\to0$，第二个因子就是零点处的斜率，课程中记作 $M(a)$。刚才建立的求导公式证明了这个极限存在，并且等于 $\ln a$。仅仅把差商因式分解，还不能单独证明极限存在。

## When the exponent is another function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*指数本身也是函数时*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $g$ is differentiable at the point being considered and $a>0$ remains fixed, the changing inner exponent contributes another factor:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $g$ 在所考虑的点可导，且 $a>0$ 仍然固定，内层指数的变化会再贡献一个因子：

$$\frac{d}{dx}a^{g(x)}=(\ln a)a^{g(x)}g'(x).$$

> [!example] Track the inner change · 跟上内层的变化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=2^{x^2}$, the inner function is $g(x)=x^2$, with derivative $2x$. Hence $f'(x)=2x(\ln2)2^{x^2}$, and $f'(1)=4\ln2\approx2.77259$. At $x=0$ the derivative is zero, even though $2^z$ increases with $z$: the inner function has zero slope there. For $q(x)=e^{-3x}$, the inner derivative is $-3$, so $q'(x)=-3e^{-3x}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $f(x)=2^{x^2}$，内层函数是 $g(x)=x^2$，导数为 $2x$。因此 $f'(x)=2x(\ln2)2^{x^2}$，在 $x=1$ 得 $f'(1)=4\ln2\approx2.77259$。在 $x=0$ 导数为零，尽管 $2^z$ 随 $z$ 递增，因为那里的内层函数斜率为零。对 $q(x)=e^{-3x}$，内层导数为 $-3$，所以 $q'(x)=-3e^{-3x}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the base also varies, as in $x^x$, do not hold it constant by mistake. On a positive-base domain one can instead rewrite the expression as $e^{g(x)\ln a(x)}$ and differentiate the entire inner product; [[对数求导|logarithmic differentiation]] organises this calculation. A negative base is not covered by the all-real exponential formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若底数也变化，例如 $x^x$，就不能误把底数当常数。在底数为正的定义域上，可以改写为 $e^{g(x)\ln a(x)}$，并对整个内层乘积求导；[[对数求导]]整理了这种计算。负底数不属于这里的全实数指数函数公式。
