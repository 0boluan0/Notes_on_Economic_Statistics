# 信号博弈策略要求发送者为每个类型给出信号分布，并要求接收者为每个可能信号给出行动分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A strategy in a signaling game assigns the sender a distribution over signals for every type and the receiver a distribution over actions after every possible signal.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在发送者类型集合为 $\Theta$、信号集合为 $S$、接收者行动集合为 $A$ 的信号博弈中，行为策略由两组条件分布组成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a signaling game with sender types $\Theta$, signals $S$, and receiver actions $A$, a behavioral-strategy profile consists of two families of conditional distributions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sigma_1(\cdot\mid\theta)\in\Delta(S)\quad(\theta\in\Theta),
\qquad
\sigma_2(\cdot\mid s)\in\Delta(A)\quad(s\in S).
$$

前者说明每一种发送者类型怎样随机选择信号，后者说明接收者观察到每一种可能信号后怎样随机选择行动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first family specifies how each sender type randomizes over signals; the second specifies how the receiver randomizes over actions after each possible observed signal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 发送者策略覆盖每个类型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The sender's strategy covers every type.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

发送者在行动前知道自己的类型，因此完整策略必须逐类型规定选择。即使某个类型的先验概率很小，或候选均衡中某个类型只使用一个纯信号，该类型的策略仍是策略剖面的一部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because the sender knows their own type before acting, a complete strategy must specify a choice for every type. A type remains part of the strategy profile even if it has a very small prior probability or uses a pure signal in the candidate equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 接收者策略覆盖每个可能信号
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The receiver's strategy covers every possible signal.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接收者的策略是对观察到的信号作出的完整应对计划。即使某个信号在候选策略下以零概率出现，接收者仍必须规定看到它以后采取什么行动；“不会到达”只会改变该处信念的一致性要求，不会删去该处的策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The receiver's strategy is a complete contingent plan for responding to observed signals. Even when a signal occurs with zero probability under the candidate strategy, the receiver must still specify an action after observing it. Non-reachability changes the consistency requirement on beliefs there; it does not remove that component of the strategy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal completeness check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Beer–Quiche 一类两类型、两信号模型中，完整策略至少要回答四个条件选择：弱类型发什么、强类型发什么、看到 Beer 后做什么、看到 Quiche 后做什么。若只写路径上实际出现的信号与行动，提交的不是完整策略剖面。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a two-type, two-signal model such as Beer–Quiche, a complete strategy must answer at least four contingent questions: what the weak type sends, what the strong type sends, what the receiver does after Beer, and what the receiver does after Quiche. Listing only the signals and actions that actually occur on the path does not specify a complete strategy profile.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
