# 开区间上可导函数为凸当且仅当导数非减而二阶可导时等价于二阶导数处处非负
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A differentiable function on an open interval is convex exactly when its derivative is nondecreasing, equivalently when its second derivative is everywhere nonnegative if it exists*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 导数怎样判断凹凸
> 设 $f$ 在开区间 $I$ 上可导。则 $f$ 为凸函数当且仅当 $f'$ 在 $I$ 非减。若 $f''$ 也在 $I$ 每一点存在，这又等价于 $f''(x)\ge0$ 对所有 $x\in I$ 成立。把非减改为非增、非负改为非正，即得凹函数判据。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On an open interval, a differentiable function is convex exactly when its derivative is nondecreasing. If a second derivative exists everywhere, this is equivalent to its being nonnegative everywhere. Reverse the monotonicity and sign for concavity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为何导数非减就能控制弦？任取 $x<z<y$，分别在 $[x,z]$ 与 $[z,y]$ 用 [[拉格朗日中值定理]]。两个割线斜率等于 $f'(\xi)$ 和 $f'(\eta)$，其中 $\xi<z<\eta$，因此
$$
\frac{f(z)-f(x)}{z-x}\le\frac{f(y)-f(z)}{y-z}.
$$
交叉相乘并整理，就得到 [[凹凸性]] 的弦不等式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply the mean value theorem on two adjacent subintervals. A nondecreasing derivative orders the two secant slopes, and rearranging that inequality puts the middle function value below the chord.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，凸函数的弦不等式使割线斜率有序；让割线两端分别靠近 $x$ 与 $y$，便得
$$
f'(x)\le\frac{f(y)-f(x)}{y-x}\le f'(y).
$$
所以 $f'$ 非减。若存在二阶导数，再对 $f'$ 使用 [[导数单调性判据]]，即得到二阶形式。这说明判据要求区间内处处成立，而非只检查一个点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, ordered secant slopes approach the derivatives at their endpoints, giving $f'(x)\le[f(y)-f(x)]/(y-x)\le f'(y)$. Applying the derivative monotonicity criterion to $f'$ gives the second-order version. The condition covers an interval, not a single sampled point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\ln x$ 在正半轴上有 $f''(x)=-1/x^2<0$，所以在那里凹。对含端点的区间，还要核对 $f$ 在所含端点连续，才可用极限把内部的弦不等式延伸至边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $\ln x$ has negative second derivative throughout the positive half-line and is therefore concave there. To include interval endpoints, check continuity there so the interior chord inequalities extend by limits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么 $f''(c)>0$ 只能给局部信息？
>
> **答案：** 凸性比较区间内任意两点，判据需要在整段控制导数；一个点的数值没有约束远处的弦。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why does $f''(c)>0$ supply only local information?
>
> **Answer:** Convexity compares arbitrary pairs of points in an interval. The criterion requires derivative control throughout that interval; one value does not constrain chords farther away.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
