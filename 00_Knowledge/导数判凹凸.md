# Derivative criteria for convexity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*导数判凹凸*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A curve can be increasing while bending either upwards or downwards. To detect the bending, compare its **slopes**: if later slopes are never smaller than earlier ones, the curve lies below its chords and is [[凹凸性|convex]]. This turns a geometric comparison across an interval into a test using derivatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条递增曲线既可能向上弯，也可能向下弯。判断弯曲时，应比较它的**斜率**：若后面的斜率从不小于前面的斜率，图像就不高于连接图像点的弦，也就是[[凹凸性|凸函数]]。这样，整个区间上的几何比较便转成了导数检验。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2$, the [[导数|slope]] $f'(x)=2x$ increases even on the negative half-line, where the function itself decreases. Thus convexity does not mean “increasing”. In calculus terminology, convex is **concave up**; concave is **concave down**, with all the inequalities below reversed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x)=x^2$，[[导数|斜率]] $f'(x)=2x$ 一直递增，即使在函数本身递减的负半轴上也如此。因此凸性不等于“函数递增”。微积分术语 **concave up** 对应凸，**concave down** 对应凹；凹的版本把下列不等号全部反向。

> [!important] The interval is part of the hypothesis · 区间也是条件的一部分
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $f:I\to\mathbb R$ be differentiable at every point of a nonempty open interval $I$. Then $f$ is convex on $I$ **if and only if** $f'$ is nondecreasing there: $x<y$ implies $f'(x)\le f'(y)$. If the [[高阶导数|second derivative]] also exists at every point of $I$, these are equivalent to $f''(x)\ge0$ for every $x\in I$. Neither equivalence requires the second derivative to be continuous. Concavity corresponds to a nonincreasing first derivative and, when it exists everywhere, a nonpositive second derivative.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $f:I\to\mathbb R$ 在非空开区间 $I$ 的每一点可导。则 $f$ 在 $I$ 上凸，**当且仅当** $f'$ 在这里非减，即 $x<y$ 推出 $f'(x)\le f'(y)$。若[[高阶导数|二阶导数]]也在 $I$ 每一点存在，这又等价于每个 $x\in I$ 都满足 $f''(x)\ge0$。这些等价关系不要求二阶导数连续。凹函数对应一阶导数非增；二阶导数处处存在时，对应它处处非正。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why ordered slopes put the curve below its chords
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么斜率有序会使曲线不高于弦*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose any $x<z<y$ in $I$. The entire two segments $[x,z]$ and $[z,y]$ lie in $I$. Differentiability gives continuity, so the [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]] applies on each segment. It gives points $\xi\in(x,z)$ and $\eta\in(z,y)$ such that the two average slopes equal $f'(\xi)$ and $f'(\eta)$. Since $\xi<\eta$ and $f'$ is nondecreasing,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任取 $I$ 中的 $x<z<y$，则 $[x,z]$、$[z,y]$ 整段都在 $I$ 中。可导保证连续，所以[[拉格朗日中值定理]]可分别用于两段，给出 $\xi\in(x,z)$、$\eta\in(z,y)$，使两段平均斜率等于 $f'(\xi)$、$f'(\eta)$。由于 $\xi<\eta$ 且 $f'$ 非减，

$$\frac{f(z)-f(x)}{z-x}\le\frac{f(y)-f(z)}{y-z}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both denominators are positive. Multiplying them out and collecting the terms containing $f(z)$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个分母都为正。交叉相乘，并把含 $f(z)$ 的项移到一起，得到

$$f(z)\le\frac{y-z}{y-x}f(x)+\frac{z-x}{y-x}f(y).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two coefficients add to one and describe the position of $z$ between $x$ and $y$. The right side is exactly the height of the chord at input $z$. The cases $z=x$ or $z=y$ give equality, so this establishes the full convexity definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个系数之和为一，表示 $z$ 在 $x$、$y$ 之间的位置。右边恰好是输入 $z$ 处弦的高度。$z=x$ 或 $z=y$ 时为等号，因此这就证明了完整的凸性定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why convexity forces the derivative order
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么凸性反过来迫使导数有序*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now assume the chord inequality. For $x<z<y$, rearranging it in two ways yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在假设弦不等式成立。对 $x<z<y$，把它分别整理为

$$\frac{f(z)-f(x)}{z-x}\le\frac{f(y)-f(x)}{y-x}\le\frac{f(y)-f(z)}{y-z}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $z\downarrow x$ in the first inequality and $z\uparrow y$ in the second. The outer quotients approach the respective derivatives, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在第一个不等式中令 $z\downarrow x$，在第二个中令 $z\uparrow y$。两边外侧的差商趋于对应导数，于是

$$f'(x)\le\frac{f(y)-f(x)}{y-x}\le f'(y).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hence $f'$ is nondecreasing. If $f''$ exists everywhere, apply the [[导数单调性判据|derivative monotonicity criterion]] to the function $f'$ itself. The condition $f''\ge0$ makes $f'$ nondecreasing; conversely, the difference quotients of a differentiable nondecreasing function are nonnegative, so their limit $f''$ is nonnegative. This proves the second-order equivalence without assuming continuity of $f''$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以 $f'$ 非减。若 $f''$ 处处存在，就对函数 $f'$ 本身应用[[导数单调性判据]]。$f''\ge0$ 使 $f'$ 非减；反过来，可导非减函数的差商非负，取极限便得到 $f''\ge0$。这就证明了二阶等价关系，并未假设 $f''$ 连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## How to use the test without strengthening it
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样使用判据而不误加强条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The function $\ln x$ has $f''(x)=-1/x^2<0$ throughout $(0,\infty)$, so it is concave there. Strict positivity of $f''$ throughout an interval is sufficient for **strict** convexity: the mean value theorem gives a strictly increasing $f'$, and the two secant slopes in the proof are strictly ordered. It is not necessary. The function $x^4$ is strictly convex, since $4x^3$ is strictly increasing, despite $f''(0)=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\ln x$ 在整个 $(0,\infty)$ 上满足 $f''(x)=-1/x^2<0$，所以在那里为凹。区间上 $f''$ 处处严格为正，足以推出**严格**凸性：中值定理使 $f'$ 严格递增，证明中的两个割线斜率便严格有序。但这个严格正号不是必要条件。$x^4$ 的导数 $4x^3$ 严格递增，所以它严格凸，尽管 $f''(0)=0$。

> [!warning] One value does not certify an interval · 一个数值不能证明整个区间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^2-x^4$, $f''(0)=2>0$, but $f''(1)=-10<0$. Thus the value at zero cannot establish convexity on the whole real line. If $f''$ is continuous at a point where it is positive, continuity does guarantee positivity in some sufficiently small neighbourhood; that additional argument concerns a neighbourhood, not every input.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $f(x)=x^2-x^4$，$f''(0)=2>0$，但 $f''(1)=-10<0$。因此零点的数值不能证明整条实轴上的凸性。若 $f''$ 在某个取正值的点连续，连续性确实能保证足够小邻域内仍为正；这个额外论证只控制一个邻域，并不控制所有输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an interval includes endpoints, a sufficient extension is [[一点连续|continuity at those endpoints]]: approximate the endpoint inputs by interior inputs and pass to the limit in the chord inequality. Without an endpoint check, the interior test alone does not decide the enlarged interval. For example, set $g(0)=-1$ and $g(x)=0$ for $0<x\le1$. Its interior second derivative is zero, but $g(1/2)=0>\tfrac12g(0)+\tfrac12g(1)=-1/2$, so $g$ is not convex on $[0,1]$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若区间包含端点，一个足够的延伸条件是在这些端点[[一点连续|连续]]：用内部输入逼近端点，再对弦不等式取极限即可。未检查端点时，内部判据本身不能决定扩大后的区间。例如令 $g(0)=-1$，$0<x\le1$ 时 $g(x)=0$。内部二阶导数为零，但 $g(1/2)=0>\tfrac12g(0)+\tfrac12g(1)=-1/2$，所以它在 $[0,1]$ 上不凸。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Likewise, a domain with a missing interior point must be treated as separate intervals; the chord between two allowed inputs may pass through a forbidden input. Convexity can also exist without differentiability—$|x|$ is a familiar example—so failing the derivative hypotheses does not itself disprove convexity. These interval tests feed into [[拐点|inflection analysis]], while the [[二阶导数检验|second derivative test for a local extremum]] answers a different question at a stationary point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，若定义域中间缺点，就要分区间处理；连接两个合法输入的弦可能穿过禁止的输入。凸性也可以在不可导时成立，$|x|$ 就是熟悉的例子，所以不满足导数假设并不自动否定凸性。这些区间判据可用于[[拐点|拐点分析]]；[[二阶导数检验|局部极值的二阶导数检验]]则回答驻点处的另一类问题。
