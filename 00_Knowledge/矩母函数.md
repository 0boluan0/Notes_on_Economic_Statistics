# Moment-generating function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*矩母函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **moment-generating function**, or **MGF**, describes a distribution by averaging exponentially transformed values of a [[随机变量|random variable]]. Rather than returning a single summary such as the mean, it lets us change a parameter and obtain a whole function. This function can reveal ordinary [[原点矩与中心矩|moments]] through derivatives, and it supplies the exponential averages used in [[指数Markov法|exponential probability bounds]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**矩母函数**（MGF）把[[随机变量]]的取值先作指数变换，再取平均，用这个办法描述分布。它不只返回均值这样的一个概括数字，而是允许我们改变一个参数，得到一整个函数。对这个函数求导，可以读出通常的[[原点矩与中心矩|矩]]；它也提供了[[指数Markov法|指数概率界]]所需的指数平均数。

> [!example] Transform first, then average · 先变换，再平均
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X$ be one with probability $1/4$ and zero with probability $3/4$. Transform each value by $2^X$: zero becomes one, and one becomes two. Its [[期望|expectation]], the probability-weighted average, is $\frac34\cdot1+\frac14\cdot2=\frac54$. An MGF records averages of this kind for different exponential transformations. The value $5/4$ is an average of transformed values, so it need not be a probability between zero and one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $X$ 以 $1/4$ 的概率取一，以 $3/4$ 的概率取零。把各取值变成 $2^X$：零变成一，一变成二。它的[[期望]]，即按概率加权的平均数，是 $\frac34\cdot1+\frac14\cdot2=\frac54$。矩母函数记录的就是不同指数变换下的这类平均值。$5/4$ 是变换后数值的平均，不是概率，因此不必落在零和一之间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The variable and the parameter play different roles
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*随机变量与参数各做什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $e\approx2.718$ for the base of the [[自然指数函数|natural exponential]] and let $s$ be a real parameter we choose. The definition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $e\approx2.718$ 为[[自然指数函数|自然指数]]的底数，$s$ 为我们选择的实参数。定义是

$$M_X(s)=E[e^{sX}].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The randomness is in $X$. For a fixed $s$, take the average over all possible values of $X$; the result $M_X(s)$ is a fixed number or $+\infty$. Then vary $s$ to see how this average changes. At $s=0$, every transformed value is one, so $M_X(0)=1$ for every distribution. The [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] $\ln2$ is the number whose exponential equals two. At $s=\ln2$, $e^{sX}=2^X$, recovering the example above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机性来自 $X$。固定 $s$ 后，对 $X$ 的所有可能取值平均，结果 $M_X(s)$ 是一个确定数或 $+\infty$；再改变 $s$，观察这个平均怎样变化。$s=0$ 时，每个变换后的值都是一，因此所有分布都有 $M_X(0)=1$。[[自然对数]] $\ln2$ 就是“指数等于二”所对应的数。$s=\ln2$ 时，$e^{sX}=2^X$，就回到上面的例子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[LOTUS|rule for averaging a function of a variable]] computes the MGF directly from the original distribution. With discrete probabilities $p_X(x)=P(X=x)$, or with a [[概率密度函数|density]] $f_X$, respectively,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利用[[LOTUS|随机变量函数的期望公式]]，可以直接按原分布计算矩母函数。若离散概率为 $p_X(x)=P(X=x)$，或者存在[[概率密度函数|密度]] $f_X$，则分别有

$$M_X(s)=\sum_x e^{sx}p_X(x),\qquad
M_X(s)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{sx}f_X(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the zero-or-one example this gives $M_X(s)=3/4+e^s/4$, which is finite for every real $s$. A positive $s$ magnifies larger values more strongly; a negative $s$ magnifies values farther in the negative direction. Finiteness must be checked in the direction we need.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的零一变量给出 $M_X(s)=3/4+e^s/4$，每个实数 $s$ 都对应有限值。正的 $s$ 更强地放大较大取值，负的 $s$ 则放大更靠负方向的取值；所需方向上的有限性必须检查。

> [!warning] The finite domain can be limited · 有限值不一定覆盖所有参数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A valid distribution need not have a finite MGF away from zero. For a concrete example, let $f_X(x)=e^{-x}$ for $x\ge0$ and zero otherwise. The density integrates to one. Its MGF is $\int_0^\infty e^{(s-1)x}\,dx=1/(1-s)$ for $s<1$, but the integral is infinite for $s\ge1$. Thus the **finite domain**, $D_X=\{s:M_X(s)<\infty\}$, is $(-\infty,1)$ in this example. Some other distributions have only $D_X=\{0\}$. In contrast, a variable bounded between two finite constants has a finite MGF at every real parameter.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个合法分布未必在零以外有有限矩母函数。例如，令 $f_X(x)=e^{-x}$（$x\ge0$），其余位置为零；密度积分为一。其矩母函数在 $s<1$ 时为 $\int_0^\infty e^{(s-1)x}\,dx=1/(1-s)$，但在 $s\ge1$ 时积分无穷。因此，本例的**有限定义域** $D_X=\{s:M_X(s)<\infty\}$ 是 $(-\infty,1)$。另一些分布甚至只有 $D_X=\{0\}$。相对地，若变量被两个有限常数夹住，每个实参数都给出有限的矩母函数。

## Why it generates moments
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么叫“矩母函数”*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume $M_X$ is finite throughout some open interval around zero: there is an $\varepsilon>0$ such that it is finite for every $|s|<\varepsilon$. Under this condition, taking the [[导数|derivative]] $k$ times and evaluating at zero gives the $k$th **raw moment**, the average of $X^k$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $M_X$ 在零周围某个开区间内处处有限：存在 $\varepsilon>0$，使每个 $|s|<\varepsilon$ 都有有限值。在这个条件下，求 $k$ 次[[导数]]再代入零，就得到 $k$ 阶**原点矩**，即 $X^k$ 的平均：

$$M_X^{(k)}(0)=E[X^k],\qquad k=1,2,\ldots.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The reason for the formula is that each derivative of $e^{sX}$ brings down another factor of $X$, and $e^{0X}=1$. The neighbourhood-finiteness condition justifies moving derivatives through the expectation; without it, formal differentiation under an infinite sum or integral is not automatically valid. In particular,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个式子的来由是：对 $e^{sX}$ 每求一次导数，就多出一个 $X$ 因子，而 $e^{0X}=1$。零附近的有限性保证了导数可以移入期望；缺少条件时，不能自动对无穷求和或积分这样操作。特别地，

$$E[X]=M_X'(0),\qquad E[X^2]=M_X''(0),\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(X)=M_X''(0)-\bigl(M_X'(0)\bigr)^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $M_X(s)=3/4+e^s/4$, both derivatives at zero equal $1/4$. Hence $E[X]=1/4$, $E[X^2]=1/4$, and the variance is $1/4-1/16=3/16$. The second derivative produces the second raw moment; subtracting the squared mean converts it to [[方差|variance]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $M_X(s)=3/4+e^s/4$，前两阶导数在零处都等于 $1/4$，因此 $E[X]=1/4$、$E[X^2]=1/4$，方差为 $1/4-1/16=3/16$。二阶导数产生二阶原点矩，再减去均值的平方，才得到[[方差]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A second use is to control extreme values: if $M_X(s)$ is finite for some $s>0$, then $P(X\ge a)\le e^{-sa}M_X(s)$. The [[指数Markov法|exponential Markov method]] explains this inequality and why a sum of independent variables is particularly convenient. This use at a positive parameter does not require the stronger two-sided neighbourhood condition used above for generating all moments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一个用途是控制极端值：若某个 $s>0$ 对应有限 $M_X(s)$，就有 $P(X\ge a)\le e^{-sa}M_X(s)$。[[指数Markov法]]解释这个不等式，也说明为什么独立变量之和特别方便。仅在某个正参数使用这个界，不要求前面为生成所有矩而采用的双侧邻域有限条件。
