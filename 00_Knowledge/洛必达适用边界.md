# 洛必达法则只适用于满足条件的 0 除以 0 或无穷比无穷
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*L'Hôpital's rule applies only to qualifying zero-over-zero or infinity-over-infinity limits*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 只有当原比值确实呈 $0/0$ 或 $\infty/\infty$ 型，并且 [[洛必达法则]] 所列的可导性、$g'(x)\ne0$ 与导数比极限条件都满足时，才能把原比值极限转成导数比极限。看见“分式”本身并不是使用理由。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A quotient limit may be converted to a derivative-quotient limit only when the original quotient genuinely has form $0/0$ or $\infty/\infty$ and all differentiability, nonzero-denominator-derivative, and derivative-limit hypotheses of [[洛必达法则|L'Hôpital's rule]] hold. Merely seeing a fraction is not enough.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 每次使用都要重新检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Recheck before every application*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 法则处理的是比值的**极限**，不能写成函数恒等式 $f/g=f'/g'$。
- 再次使用前，要重新确认新的导数比仍呈 $0/0$ 或 $\infty/\infty$ 型并满足定理条件。
- $0\cdot\infty$、$\infty-\infty$ 和幂型未定式不是法则的直接输入，必须先改写成合适的比值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

**1.** The rule concerns a quotient **limit**, not the identity $f/g=f'/g'$.  
**2.** Before applying it again, verify that the new derivative quotient still has an allowed indeterminate form and satisfies the theorem's hypotheses.  
**3.** Forms such as $0\cdot\infty$, $\infty-\infty$, and indeterminate powers must first be rewritten as suitable quotients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Counterexample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $x\to0$ 时，$(1+x)/x$ 的分子趋于 $1$，分母趋于 $0$；原式不是 $0/0$ 或 $\infty/\infty$。若无视前提而对分子分母求导，会得到 $1$，但原比值右侧趋于 $+\infty$、左侧趋于 $-\infty$，有限双侧极限并不存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As $x\to0$, the numerator of $(1+x)/x$ tends to $1$ while the denominator tends to $0$, so the quotient has neither allowed indeterminate form. Blind differentiation would give $1$, even though the original quotient tends to $+\infty$ from the right and $-\infty$ from the left.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 对一个比值连续使用两次洛必达法则时，第一次通过条件检查是否已经足够？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If L'Hôpital's rule is used twice, is the first applicability check enough for both uses?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不够。第二次使用面对的是新的导数比，必须重新检查其形式与全部假设。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. The second application concerns a new derivative quotient, whose form and hypotheses must be checked again.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
