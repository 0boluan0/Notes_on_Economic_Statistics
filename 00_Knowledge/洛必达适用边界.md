# When L'Hôpital's rule applies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*洛必达适用边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiating the top and bottom of a fraction can produce a very tidy answer and still solve the wrong problem. For $(1+x)/x$ near zero, the derivative ratio is $1/1=1$, while the original expression is $1+1/x$. Its right-hand limit is $+\infty$ and its left-hand limit is $-\infty$. The numerator approaches $1$, so this is not the [[零比零未定式|zero-over-zero form]] required by that version of [[洛必达法则|L'Hôpital's rule]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把分式的分子、分母分别求导，可能得到很整齐的答案，却算错了问题。零附近的 $(1+x)/x$，导数比为 $1/1=1$，原式却是 $1+1/x$：右极限为 $+\infty$，左极限为 $-\infty$。分子趋于 $1$，因此它不属于[[洛必达法则]]相应版本要求的[[零比零未定式|零比零形式]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The issue is whether a theorem justifies transferring the derivative ratio's limit to the original ratio. A failed assumption removes that justification; it does **not** decide whether the original limit exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键在于：是否有定理保证，导数比的极限可以传递给原比值。某个假设不成立，就失去了这项保证；它**不能**直接决定原极限是否存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Follow the assumptions through each application
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 每次使用都要重新检查

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First identify the allowed direction and the separate limits of the numerator $f(x)$ and denominator $g(x)$. The familiar version covers $f,g\to0$ or $|f|,|g|\to\infty$. More generally, the denominator-divergent version proved in [[洛必达法则|the theorem card]] only needs $|g|\to\infty$, together with the derivative assumptions. These are sufficient versions, not a claim that every quotient limit belongs to one of two forms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确定合法趋近方向，再分别求分子 $f(x)$、分母 $g(x)$ 的极限。常用版本覆盖 $f,g\to0$，或 $|f|,|g|\to\infty$。更一般地，[[洛必达法则|定理卡]]证明的分母发散版本只要求 $|g|\to\infty$，再加上相应的导数条件。这些是充分版本，并不是说所有分式极限只能属于两种形式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next check that both functions are differentiable at **every** point of a one-sided interval or tail, and that $g'$ is nonzero throughout it. The derivative ratio must have a limit, finite or signed infinite, in the same direction. Values at a finite excluded target need not be defined. By contrast, $g'$ must not vanish at the nearby inputs on which this version is applied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接着检查：两函数是否在某个单侧区间或尾部的**每一点**可导，$g'$ 是否在其中处处非零；导数比是否沿相同方向存在有限或带符号无穷极限。有限目标点本身可以没有定义；但使用这个版本时，不能忽略附近输入处 $g'$ 非零的要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the derivative ratio is another indeterminate quotient, a second application is a **new** theorem application. For example, using the independently established [[指数函数求导|exponential derivative]], work from the final easy limit backwards:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若导数比仍是未定式，第二次使用就是一次**新的**定理应用。例如，使用已经独立建立的[[指数函数求导]]，应从最后容易确定的极限反向核对：

$$\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x}{2}=\frac12,
\qquad
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x-1}{2x}=\frac12,
\qquad
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x-1-x}{x^2}=\frac12.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The middle quotient is $0/0$ with denominator derivative $2$. This establishes the derivative-ratio limit needed for the last displayed quotient, whose denominator derivative is $2x\ne0$ for nearby $x\ne0$. Both applications are justified separately. Continuing to differentiate after reaching $e^x/2$ would create a zero denominator derivative and abandon these assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中间的比值是 $0/0$，分母导数为 $2$。它的极限建立以后，才得到了最后一个比值所需的导数比极限；最后一个比值的分母导数是 $2x$，在附近的 $x\ne0$ 处不为零。两次应用各自得到论证。已经到达 $e^x/2$ 后再机械求导，会把分母导数变成零，破坏这些假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A derivative ratio may oscillate while the original ratio converges
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 反例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f(x)=x+\sin x$ and $g(x)=x$, with angles in radians and $x\to+\infty$. Both functions tend to $+\infty$, and $g'=1$. Yet the derivative ratio oscillates. The [[正弦余弦求导|sine derivative]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $f(x)=x+\sin x$、$g(x)=x$，角度按弧度计，令 $x\to+\infty$。两函数都趋于 $+\infty$，且 $g'=1$，但导数比仍然振荡。由[[正弦余弦求导]]，

$$\frac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}=1+\cos x.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $x=2\pi n$ this equals $2$, and at $x=(2n+1)\pi$ it equals $0$, for positive integers $n$. These two sequences both tend to infinity, so there is no single derivative-ratio limit. Nevertheless,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正整数 $n$，在 $x=2\pi n$ 时它等于 $2$，在 $x=(2n+1)\pi$ 时等于 $0$。两列输入都趋于无穷，因此导数比没有统一极限。然而

$$\left|\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}-1\right|
=\left|\frac{\sin x}{x}\right|\le\frac1x\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original ratio therefore tends to $1$, by a direct bound or the [[夹逼定理|squeeze theorem]]. L'Hôpital's implication cannot be reversed: knowing the original limit does not establish a derivative-ratio limit. Failure of this derivative test is not evidence that the original limit fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用直接误差界或[[夹逼定理]]，便得到原比值趋于 $1$。洛必达法则的推论不能倒过来使用：知道原极限，并不能推出导数比有极限。导数检验失败，也不是原极限不存在的证据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Change the expression without changing the question
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*改写要保留定义域和趋近方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Products such as $0\cdot\infty$, differences such as $\infty-\infty$, and indeterminate powers are not directly derivative-ratio problems. They may be rewritten as quotients, but the rewrite must be valid for all sufficiently close allowed inputs. For example, $x\ln x=\ln x/(1/x)$ for $x>0$; as $x\to0^+$ both numerator and denominator diverge in magnitude. Using the [[对数函数求导|logarithm derivative]], the derivative ratio is $(1/x)/(-1/x^2)=-x\to0$, so the original product tends to zero. The positive-input restriction cannot be dropped.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$0\cdot\infty$ 型乘积、$\infty-\infty$ 型差式与幂型未定式，并不是可以直接分别求导的比值问题。可以先改写成分式，但等式必须对充分接近目标的全部合法输入成立。例如在 $x>0$ 时，$x\ln x=\ln x/(1/x)$；当 $x\to0^+$，分子、分母的绝对值都发散。结合[[对数函数求导|自然对数求导]]，导数比是 $(1/x)/(-1/x^2)=-x\to0$，因此原乘积趋于零。不能丢掉正输入的限制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rule establishes equality of limits, not $f/g=f'/g'$ point by point. Also check the proof order: if the [[正弦余弦求导|sine derivative]] was proved using the [[正弦基本极限|fundamental sine limit]], applying that derivative to prove the same limit for the first time would be circular. Algebra, known limits and bounds remain legitimate alternatives; the [[极限计算流程|limit evaluation workflow]] selects a tool from the expression's structure, rather than requiring a fixed sequence of tools.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

法则建立的是极限相等，不是逐点恒等式 $f/g=f'/g'$。还要检查证明顺序：如果[[正弦余弦求导|正弦导数]]本来就是用[[正弦基本极限]]证明的，再用该导数首次证明同一个极限，就会循环论证。代数化简、已知极限与上下界仍然可以直接使用；[[极限计算流程]]根据表达式结构选择工具，而不是强制按某个固定顺序走完所有工具。
