# 无股息且利率非负时无摩擦市场中的美式看涨期权不因提前行权增加价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a frictionless market with no dividends and nonnegative interest rates, early exercise adds no value to an American call*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于固定执行价 $K>0$ 的普通美式看涨，若股票在剩余期限内不付股息，市场无摩擦且无套利，可自由交易、卖空并以相同的确定性非负利率借贷，则**保留到期行权的策略在价值上至少不差于提前行权**。在通常可复制的定价模型中，美式与同条件欧式看涨价值相等；这不等于每一种退化情形下提前行权都严格更差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a vanilla American call with fixed strike $K>0$, assume no dividends over its remaining life, a frictionless arbitrage-free market, unrestricted trading and shorting, and borrowing and lending at the same deterministic nonnegative rate. Retaining the right to exercise at expiry is at least as valuable as exercising early. In the usual replicable pricing model, its value equals that of the corresponding European call; early exercise need not be strictly worse in every degenerate case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为写清公式，取常数连续复利利率 $r\ge0$、剩余时间 $\tau=T-t$，并排除持股特有的额外收益或成本。欧式到期支付同时不低于零和 $S_T-K$，按无套利价格的保序性，欧式看涨价值满足
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the formulas, use constant continuously compounded $r\ge0$, remaining time $\tau=T-t$, and no additional benefits or costs specific to holding the stock. The European terminal payoff dominates both zero and $S_T-K$. No-arbitrage price ordering therefore gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_E(t)\ge\max\{S_t-Ke^{-r\tau},0\}
\ge(S_t-K)^+.
$$

第二个不等式正是 $r\ge0$ 的作用：延后支付执行价不会增加其现值，而且行权会失去股票下跌时不买入的选择。这个比较对**每个可能的提前行权时点及状态**都成立。因而一个复制欧式看涨的组合，在任何提前行权时都足以支付立即行权金额；其成本 $C_E$ 给出美式价值的上界。反过来，美式持有人可选择一直等到到期，所以 $C_A\ge C_E$。合起来有
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second inequality uses $r\ge0$: delaying the strike payment does not increase its present cost, while exercise gives up the choice not to buy after a stock-price decline. This comparison holds at every possible early-exercise time and state. A portfolio replicating the European call can therefore cover any early-exercise payoff, so its cost $C_E$ bounds the American value from above. Conversely, the American holder may wait until expiry, implying $C_A\ge C_E$. Together:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$C_A=C_E.$$

这不是对未来实际股价路径收益的保证，而是同一市场模型中的价值比较；不需要另加股票服从 BSM 对数正态过程的假设。若恰好满足 [[BSM期权定价]] 的其他条件，欧式公式才可同时给出这类美式看涨的价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a value comparison within one market model, not a guarantee about realized returns along every future stock-price path. It does not require BSM lognormal stock dynamics. If the remaining assumptions of [[BSM期权定价|BSM valuation]] also hold, its European formula prices this American call as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 严格优势与平手的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strict advantage versus indifference*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $r>0,\tau>0$ 且当前严格实值，上面的下界已经严格大于 $S_t-K$。但取 $r=0$、无股息且股票确定性地一直为 $120$、$K=100$，则现在或到期取得的支付价值都是 $20$；这是零波动率下的平手例。$r=0$ 或零波动率本身也不保证平手，仍须检查具体支付。因此“等待至少不差”不能改写成“任何条件下都不可能理性提前行权”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $r>0$, $\tau>0$ and the call is strictly in the money, the lower bound is already strictly greater than $S_t-K$. But with $r=0$, no dividends, a stock that remains deterministically at 120, and strike 100, exercise now or at expiry has value 20. This is a zero-volatility tie. Neither zero rates nor zero volatility alone guarantees indifference; the payoffs still matter. “Waiting is at least as good” must not become “rational early exercise is impossible under every condition.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股息、负利率、借贷价差、交易费用或约束会改变上述比较，须回到 [[美式行权决策]]；这些条件不满足也不表示一定应立即行权。看跌不适用本定理，因为其行权提前取得执行价，价值比较不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dividends, negative rates, borrowing/lending spreads, transaction costs or constraints alter the comparison and require an [[美式行权决策|exercise-versus-continuation decision]]. Failure of these assumptions does not itself mandate immediate exercise. The theorem does not apply to puts, whose exercise receives the strike earlier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Kohn–Allen，[*Derivative Securities*, Section 1，PDF 第 4–6 页](https://math.nyu.edu/~kohn/derivative.securities/2007/section1.pdf#page=4)：已重开无股息远期复制、价格保序性及无摩擦假设；[第 6 页](https://math.nyu.edu/~kohn/derivative.securities/2007/section1.pdf#page=6) 已渲染核对欧式看涨下界。$r\ge0$ 的第二个不等式、逐时点复制覆盖论证及零利率平手例为本页据这些条件展开的推导，不把原文的欧式下界误称为原文已证明完整美式结论。
- Haugh–Kogan，[*Pricing American Options: A Duality Approach*，PDF 第 5–6 页，文内第 4–5 页](https://www.columbia.edu/~mh2078/HaughKogan2001_rev2.pdf#page=5)：已重开可复制的完整市场及美式最优停止定价设定，用于区分“每个时点的行权比较”与单独一次的当前价格比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Kohn–Allen, Section 1, PDF pp. 4–6, was reopened for non-dividend forward replication, price ordering and frictionless assumptions; p. 6 was rendered to check the European call lower bound. The nonnegative-rate inequality, replication cover at each exercise time and zero-rate tie are developed here from those conditions, rather than attributed to the source as a complete American-option proof.
- Haugh–Kogan, PDF pp. 5–6 (printed pp. 4–5), supports the complete replicable market and American optimal-stopping framework, distinguishing comparison at every exercise time from a single current-price comparison.
