# 均值—方差投资组合理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mean–Variance Portfolio Theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释多个资产放在一起后，组合风险为什么取决于共同变化而不只是单项波动，并在给定期望收益下寻找最低方差组合。
> **具体锚点：** 两只波动率都为 20% 的资产各占一半；若相关系数为 1，组合波动仍为 20%，若相关系数为 0，组合波动约降到 14.14%。
> **核心难点：** 协方差矩阵决定分散化，期望收益估计误差却会被优化器放大；数学上的最优组合可能极不稳健。
> **为什么重要：** 它是资产配置、风险预算和因子分散的共同起点，也说明“多持有几只证券”不自动等于真正分散。
> **继续：** 若要从最优组合进入均衡要求回报，读 [[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本]]；尾部和流动性风险需再进入相应风险卡片。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It explains why portfolio risk depends on co-movement rather than only individual volatility and finds the minimum-variance portfolio for a specified expected return.
> **Concrete anchor:** Two assets each have 20% volatility and equal weights. With correlation 1, portfolio volatility remains 20%; with correlation 0, it falls to about 14.14%.
> **Central difficulty:** The covariance matrix determines diversification, while expected-return estimation error is amplified by optimization; a mathematically optimal portfolio can be highly unstable.
> **Why it matters:** It is a common starting point for asset allocation, risk budgeting, and factor diversification, and shows why merely owning more securities does not guarantee genuine diversification.
> **Continue:** To move from optimal portfolios to equilibrium required returns, read [[CAPM、系统风险与资本成本|CAPM, Systematic Risk, and the Cost of Capital]]. Tail and liquidity risks require their dedicated risk cards.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 组合收益与方差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Portfolio return and variance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$E(R_p)=w^T\mu$，$Var(R_p)=w^T\Sigma w$。两资产方差含 $2w_1w_2Cov(R_1,R_2)$；相关低于 1 就有分散空间。权重、再平衡和估计窗口决定实际结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$E(R_p)=w^T\mu$ and $Var(R_p)=w^T\Sigma w$. For two assets, variance includes $2w_1w_2Cov(R_1,R_2)$; correlation below 1 creates diversification potential. Weights, rebalancing, and the estimation window determine realized results.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两资产、各权重 0.5、各波动率 20% 时，组合方差为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With two assets, weights 0.5 each, and volatility 20% each, portfolio variance is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sigma_p^2=0.5^2(0.2)^2+0.5^2(0.2)^2+2(0.5)(0.5)\rho(0.2)(0.2).
$$

当 $\rho=1$，$\sigma_p=20\%$；当 $\rho=0$，$\sigma_p=\sqrt{0.02}\approx14.14\%$；当 $\rho=-1$ 且波动、权重匹配，风险可在这个两状态线性模型中完全抵消。关键不是资产数量，而是暴露是否共同变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $\rho=1$, $\sigma_p=20\%$; when $\rho=0$, $\sigma_p=\sqrt{0.02}\approx14.14\%$; when $\rho=-1$ with matching volatilities and weights, risk can cancel entirely in this linear two-asset model. The key is not the number of assets but whether their exposures co-move.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 有效前沿
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The efficient frontier*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在给定期望收益下最小方差，或给定方差下最大期望收益的组合构成有效前沿。输入均值估计噪声很大，优化会产生极端权重；约束、收缩和稳健优化往往比精确样本解更可靠。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Portfolios with minimum variance for a given expected return, or maximum expected return for a given variance, form the efficient frontier. Estimated means are noisy, and optimization can produce extreme weights; constraints, shrinkage, and robust optimization are often more reliable than the exact sample solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全局最小方差组合只需要协方差矩阵，不需要期望收益；有效前沿上其他点还需要均值。因为均值相对波动很难估准，一点点输入变化就可能让优化器在近似资产间大幅换仓。报告权重时应同时报告输入敏感性、换手和约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The global minimum-variance portfolio requires only the covariance matrix; other points on the frontier also require expected returns. Because means are difficult to estimate precisely relative to volatility, tiny input changes can make the optimizer shift dramatically among similar assets. Portfolio weights should therefore be reported with input sensitivity, turnover, and constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 无风险资产与资本市场线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The risk-free asset and the capital market line*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

允许以无风险利率借贷时，最优风险组合是 Sharpe ratio 最高的切点组合；不同风险偏好沿无风险资产与切点组合连线选择。现实借贷利率、卖空和资产可交易限制会破坏简单分离。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When investors can borrow and lend at a risk-free rate, the optimal risky portfolio is the tangency portfolio with the highest Sharpe ratio; investors with different risk preferences choose along the line joining the risk-free asset and that portfolio. Different real-world borrowing rates, short-sale limits, and incomplete tradability break the simple separation result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资本市场线描述有效组合的总波动与期望收益，不是单只证券的定价线。单只证券可能带有大量可分散波动，不能因其位于线下便简单称为“错误定价”；这一步需要进入 CAPM 的系统风险关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The capital market line relates total volatility to expected return for efficient portfolios; it is not a pricing line for individual securities. An individual security may carry substantial diversifiable volatility and cannot be called mispriced merely because it lies below the line; that step requires CAPM's systematic-risk relation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从原主题保留的全局定位
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Global orientation retained from the original topic*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **它解决什么：** 说明多个资产组合后风险为何不只是单项风险相加，并给出系统风险与要求回报的基准关系。
> **具体锚点：** 两只各自波动大的股票若收益不完全同步，组合波动可下降；可分散掉的是特有风险，不是共同市场冲击。
> **核心难点：** 协方差决定组合风险；CAPM 的 beta 衡量与市场共同变化，不是总波动，也不是所有风险的完整解释。
> **为什么重要：** 资产配置、资本成本、业绩归因和风险管理都以分散化和系统风险为起点。
> **继续：** 先掌握均值—方差与有效前沿，再把 CAPM 当基准而非自然定律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It explains why portfolio risk is not the sum of individual risks and gives a benchmark relation between systematic risk and required return.
> **Concrete anchor:** Two individually volatile stocks can reduce portfolio volatility when their returns do not move perfectly together; idiosyncratic risk can be diversified, but common market shocks cannot.
> **Central difficulty:** Covariance determines portfolio risk; CAPM beta measures co-movement with the market, not total volatility and not every possible dimension of risk.
> **Why it matters:** Asset allocation, cost of capital, performance attribution, and risk management begin with diversification and systematic risk.
> **Continue:** Learn mean–variance analysis and the efficient frontier first, then treat CAPM as a benchmark rather than a law of nature.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把相关系数固定在正常期样本值，却用组合应对危机；压力期相关可能同时上升。
- 用大量高度相似证券制造“持仓很多”的表象，底层因子暴露仍高度集中。
- 无约束优化得到巨大多空头寸，却忽略估计误差、融资成本与成交容量。
- 把方差当作全部风险，遗漏偏度、尾部、流动性和路径依赖。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Fixing correlation at its normal-period sample value while using the portfolio for crises ignores the possibility that correlations rise under stress.
- Holding many highly similar securities creates the appearance of diversification while underlying factor exposure remains concentrated.
- Unconstrained optimization can generate huge long and short positions while ignoring estimation error, financing cost, and execution capacity.
- Treating variance as all risk omits skewness, tails, liquidity, and path dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么组合风险不能把各资产波动率按权重相加？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can portfolio risk not be calculated by weighting and adding individual volatilities?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 还要包含资产间协方差；不同步的涨跌会相互抵消。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Covariances between assets must also be included; asynchronous gains and losses partly offset each other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么样本均值—方差优化容易给极端权重？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does sample mean–variance optimization tend to produce extreme weights?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 期望收益和协方差有估计误差，逆协方差优化会放大小差异，尤其在高维/共线资产中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Expected returns and covariances are estimated with error, and inverse-covariance optimization magnifies small differences, especially with many collinear assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么持有十只银行股仍可能没有充分分散？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why may holding ten bank stocks still be poorly diversified?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它们可能共同暴露于利率、信用周期、融资流动性和监管冲击；证券数量增加了，但协方差结构和共同因子没有被分散。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> They may share exposure to rates, the credit cycle, funding liquidity, and regulation. The number of securities rises, but covariance structure and common factors remain concentrated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
