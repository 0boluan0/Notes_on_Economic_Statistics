# 圆弧形态是价格总体斜率逐渐改变而形成的弧状转向结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A rounding pattern is an arcing change of direction formed by a gradual change in the price path's overall slope.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

圆弧形态描述价格在一段观察区间内逐渐转向的总体轮廓。圆弧顶由上升逐渐趋缓，再转为下降；圆弧底由下降逐渐趋缓，再转为上升。顶与底是同一种弧状构造的方向对照，并不要求每根价格柱光滑排列或严格落在一个圆上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rounding pattern describes a gradual directional change over an observation interval. A rounding top moves from a slowing advance into a decline; a rounding bottom moves from a slowing decline into an advance. They are directional versions of the same arcing construction, not requirements for every bar to lie smoothly on a mathematical circle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断“总体斜率”须说明时间尺度以及是否使用平滑；局部锯齿波动可以与圆弧轮廓并存。课程图没有给出唯一数值完成阈值，因此本卡定义几何对象，不把肉眼看见弧形等同于已经触发交易。若要形成规则，须再固定识别窗口、容差及突破或其他确认条件，并遵守 [[交易信号时序]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An overall-slope assessment needs a time scale and a stated smoothing choice; local jagged moves can coexist with the arc. The course figure supplies no unique numerical completion threshold. This card therefore defines the geometry rather than treating visual recognition as a trade trigger. An operational rule needs a fixed window, tolerance and breakout or other confirmation condition, followed by [[交易信号时序|signal and execution timing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如等距观测的总体参考路径 90、96、100、102、102、100、96、90，逐期价差为 6、4、2、0、−2、−4、−6，示意由上行放缓到下行的圆弧顶。它不是一项收益预测检验；若根据未来半段才选出这段窗口，识别结果也只能在那时可用，不能回填到最高点交易。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An equally spaced reference path of 90, 96, 100, 102, 102, 100, 96 and 90 has increments of 6, 4, 2, 0, −2, −4 and −6, illustrating a rounding top. It is not a return-prediction test. If the window is selected using its later half, the recognition becomes available only then, not at an earlier peak for a backfilled trade.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
