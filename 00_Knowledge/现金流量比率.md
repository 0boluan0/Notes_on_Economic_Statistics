# 现金流量比率是期间经营活动现金流量净额与所选流动负债存量之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The operating-cash-flow ratio divides a period's net operating cash flow by the chosen current-liability stock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本课现金流量比率为 $CFO/CL_{end}$：分子来自[[现金流量表]]的经营活动现金流量净额，分母采用期末流动负债，问本期经营现金生成相对于期末尚欠短期义务的规模。ABC为 $246/300=0.82$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course defines the ratio as $CFO/CL_{end}$: net operating cash flow from the [[现金流量表|cash-flow statement]] divided by end-period current liabilities. It compares current-period cash generation with short-term obligations outstanding at period-end. ABC gives $246/300=0.82$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里采用期末而非平均负债有明确解释目的，见[[财务比率口径匹配]]。它也不是未来一年偿债82%的承诺：历史现金可能已经使用，下一期经营现金与债务到期分布可能不同，且还可能需要投资支出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using ending rather than average debt has a stated purpose; see [[财务比率口径匹配|measurement-basis matching]]. The result is not a promise to repay 82% next year: historical cash may already be spent, future cash generation and maturities can differ, and investment needs may remain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

负CFO得到负比率，表示本期经营净耗现，不是负数债务保障；$CL=0$时比率未定义。比较时保持CFO分类和期间一致，不把它与现金存量的[[现金比率]]混同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Negative CFO indicates net operating cash consumption, not negative debt protection. The ratio is undefined when $CL=0$. Keep cash-flow classification and periods consistent and distinguish it from the stock-based [[现金比率|cash ratio]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
