# 艾略特交替原则提示相邻同类修正可能在外形和复杂度上不同
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Elliott alternation suggests that successive comparable corrections may differ in shape and complexity.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交替原则是 [[艾略特波浪]] 的经验指引：分析相似位置的下一段浪形时，不要默认它会照搬上一段的外形。在 [[标准推动浪]] 中，典型比较是第二与第四浪——第二浪若是陡峭回撤，第四浪常被预期为横向整理，反之亦然。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Alternation is an [[艾略特波浪|Elliott]] guideline against assuming that the next comparable wave repeats the last one's shape. Within a [[标准推动浪|standard impulse]], the usual comparison is between waves 2 and 4: a sharp wave-2 correction motivates expecting a more sideways wave 4, and conversely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它不是“第四浪必须更复杂”，也不是带有已给概率的统计模型。两个修正呈相似外形，不因违反这条经验指引就自动使计数失效；是否违反 [[推动浪计数约束]] 要单独检查。对角线结构也不能直接套用推动浪第二、第四浪的交替期待。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is neither a requirement that wave 4 be more complex nor a statistical model with a specified probability. Similar corrections do not automatically invalidate a count merely by failing this guideline; check [[推动浪计数约束|mandatory impulse constraints]] separately. The impulse expectation for waves 2 and 4 also does not transfer directly to diagonals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复习时可把它当作候选比较问题：“这一段可能与上一段在哪方面不同？”不要把它改写为已经知道下一段必然出现的形状。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use it to compare candidates by asking how this correction might differ from the previous one, rather than claiming foreknowledge of a necessary shape.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
