# 风险的确定性等价是在指定效用和背景下与风险结果无差异的确定结果

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A certainty equivalent is a certain outcome indifferent to a risky outcome under specified utility and background conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

偏好意义的确定性等价（CE）是在给定偏好、背景与概率分布下，使决策者与原风险结果无差异的确定金额。本卡先把 $X$ 定义为同一未来时点的终期财富，而不是增量项目收益。若偏好有期望效用表示，$u$ 连续且严格递增，$E[u(X)]$ 有限并落在 $u$ 的值域内，则唯一CE满足：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A preference-based certainty equivalent (CE) is the certain amount indifferent to a risky outcome given preferences, background and probabilities. Here $X$ initially denotes terminal wealth at one future date, not incremental project benefits. With expected-utility preferences, continuous strictly increasing $u$, and finite $E[u(X)]$ in its range, the unique CE satisfies:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$u(CE_X)=E[u(X)],\qquad CE_X=u^{-1}\!\left(E[u(X)]\right).$$

例如 $X$ 以各半概率取100和400，$u(x)=\sqrt{x}$，则期望效用为15，$CE_X=225$，而期望财富为250。若同一效用还为凹函数，并且相关期望存在，由Jensen不等式可得 $CE_X\le E[X]$；这是风险厌恶下愿意用一定均值换取确定性的表现，见[[损失厌恶与风险厌恶]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $X$ equals 100 or 400 with equal probability and utility is square-root, expected utility is 15, so $CE_X=225$, while expected wealth is 250. If utility is also concave and the relevant expectations exist, Jensen's inequality gives $CE_X\le E[X]$. This captures willingness to surrender some mean wealth for certainty under [[损失厌恶与风险厌恶|risk aversion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要表达增量收益 $Y$ 的CE，必须写明固定背景财富 $w$，并计算 $CE_Y=u^{-1}(E[u(w+Y)])-w$。上例若 $w=100$，$Y$ 为0或300，则增量CE为125，不是225；背景变化时不能假定CE不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To express a CE for incremental benefits $Y$, specify fixed background wealth $w$ and calculate $CE_Y=u^{-1}(E[u(w+Y)])-w$. In the example, with $w=100$ and $Y$ equal to 0 or 300, incremental CE is 125, not 225. It need not remain unchanged when background wealth changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个CE表达指定决策者的偏好，不自动等于市场定价所隐含的确定现金。[[风险调整不重复计价]]负责“有定价依据的CE如何与无风险贴现匹配”；不能仅因算出某个人的效用CE，就无条件把它当公共项目或资产的市场价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This CE expresses a specified decision-maker's preferences, not automatically the certain cash implied by market pricing. [[风险调整不重复计价|Avoiding duplicate risk adjustment]] handles matching a pricing-based CE with risk-free discounting. An individual's utility CE cannot unconditionally be treated as a public project's or asset's market price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
