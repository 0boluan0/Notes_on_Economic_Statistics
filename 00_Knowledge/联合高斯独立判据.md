# 不相关推出独立要求变量联合 Gaussian 等额外结构
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Zero correlation implies independence only with extra structure such as joint Gaussianity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 若随机向量 $(X,Y)$ **联合 Gaussian**，零互协方差使协方差矩阵分块对角。Gaussian 特征函数因而分解为 $X$ 块和 $Y$ 块的乘积，所以两块独立。这个论证也覆盖协方差矩阵奇异、不存在 Lebesgue 联合密度的退化 Gaussian 向量。一般变量中，不相关只排除线性协变，不能排除非线性依赖。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a jointly Gaussian vector, zero cross-covariance block-diagonalises the covariance matrix and factorises the Gaussian characteristic function, so the blocks are independent. This argument also covers singular Gaussian laws that have no Lebesgue joint density. In general, zero correlation rules out only linear comovement.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“各自边际 Gaussian”仍不够。令 $Z\sim N(0,1)$，$S$ 为独立等概率 $\pm1$，取 $X=Z,Y=SZ$。则 $X,Y$ 各自标准正态且协方差为 0，但 $|X|=|Y|$ 几乎处处成立，显然不独立；关键缺失正是 joint Gaussian。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gaussian marginals alone are insufficient. If $X=Z$ and $Y=SZ$ with $Z\sim N(0,1)$ and an independent random sign $S$, both marginals are standard normal and uncorrelated, yet $|X|=|Y|$ almost surely. The pair is not jointly Gaussian and not independent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $X$ 和 $Y$ 各自都是标准正态，且 $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0$，是否已经足以推出它们独立？
>
> **答案：** 不足。必须再知道 $(X,Y)$ 联合 Gaussian；单独的 Gaussian 边际加零协方差仍允许 $|X|=|Y|$ 这类非线性依赖。
