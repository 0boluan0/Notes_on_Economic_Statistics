# Independence of jointly Gaussian variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*联合 Gaussian 的独立判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[协方差|Covariance]] measures the average product of two centered deviations. Zero covariance removes that particular linear association, whereas [[随机变量独立|independence]] requires all joint probabilities to factor. Usually the second requirement is much stronger. A **jointly Gaussian** model is a special case in which zero covariance is enough to establish independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[协方差]]平均两个中心化偏移的乘积。零协方差排除了这种线性联系，而[[随机变量独立|独立性]]要求所有联合概率都能分解成乘积，通常强得多。**联合 Gaussian** 模型是一种特殊情形：在这个模型内，零协方差已经足以确定独立。

> [!example] The same sources can produce independent outputs · 同一组来源可以产生独立输出
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take independent [[正态分布|standard normal variables]] $Z_1,Z_2$, and let $U=Z_1+Z_2$ and $V=Z_1-Z_2$. Both outputs use both inputs. Still,
>
> $$E[U]=E[V]=0,\quad \operatorname{Var}(U)=\operatorname{Var}(V)=2,\quad
> \operatorname{Cov}(U,V)=E[Z_1^2-Z_2^2]=1-1=0.$$
>
> The pair is [[联合Gaussian|jointly Gaussian]] because it is a fixed linear transformation of independent standard normals. The criterion below therefore makes $U$ and $V$ independent, each with law $N(0,2)$. Merely sharing an input is not a proof of dependence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取独立[[正态分布|标准正态变量]] $Z_1,Z_2$，令 $U=Z_1+Z_2$、$V=Z_1-Z_2$。两个输出都用到了两个输入，但上式给出它们均值为零、方差为 $2$、协方差为零。由于二者是独立标准正态的固定线性变换，这一对[[联合Gaussian|联合 Gaussian]]；下面的判据因此说明 $U,V$ 独立，各自服从 $N(0,2)$。仅仅共用输入，并不能证明依赖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The condition belongs to the whole joint law
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*条件加在整个联合分布上*

> [!important] Gaussian independence criterion · Gaussian 独立判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For two real random variables $U,V$, if the pair $(U,V)^T$ is jointly Gaussian, then
>
> $$\boxed{U\text{ and }V\text{ are independent}\iff\operatorname{Cov}(U,V)=0.}$$
>
> “Jointly Gaussian” means every fixed linear combination $aU+bV$ is normal, allowing constants as variance-zero normals. It is stronger than saying that $U$ and $V$ are each normal. Finite moments are automatic in this model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对实随机变量 $U,V$，若向量 $(U,V)^T$ 联合 Gaussian，就有上面的等价关系。“联合 Gaussian”要求每个固定线性组合 $aU+bV$ 都正态，并把常数纳入方差为零的退化正态；它强于“$U,V$ 各自都正态”。这个模型自动具有有限矩。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is also a block version. Let $U\in\mathbb R^p$ and $V\in\mathbb R^q$, where $p,q\ge1$, and assume the stacked vector $(U^T,V^T)^T$ is jointly Gaussian. Its [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] can be partitioned as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还有向量块版本。设 $U\in\mathbb R^p$、$V\in\mathbb R^q$，其中 $p,q\ge1$，并假设把两块堆起来得到的 $(U^T,V^T)^T$ 联合 Gaussian。其[[协方差矩阵]]可以分块写成

$$
\begin{bmatrix}\Sigma_U&C\\C^T&\Sigma_V\end{bmatrix},
\qquad C_{jk}=\operatorname{Cov}(U_j,V_k).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two **entire vectors** are independent exactly when every entry of the $p\times q$ cross-covariance block $C$ is zero. Coordinates inside either block may remain dependent. For a whole jointly Gaussian vector, a diagonal covariance matrix likewise gives mutual independence of all coordinates, not just pairwise independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这两个**完整向量**独立，当且仅当 $p\times q$ 的交叉协方差块 $C$ 每个元素都为零；每块内部的坐标仍可以依赖。同样，对整个联合 Gaussian 向量，协方差矩阵为对角阵会给出所有坐标相互独立，而不只是两两独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why covariance is sufficient here
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么在这里协方差已经足够*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The key fact is that a Gaussian law is determined completely by its mean vector and covariance matrix. When $C=0$, construct independent Gaussian blocks with the same individual means and covariances as $U$ and $V$. Their stacked vector is Gaussian and has exactly the same parameters as the original stacked vector. The two joint laws must therefore coincide. Since the constructed law has independent blocks, the original law does too. The other direction—independence gives zero covariance—follows from factoring expectations and does not require Gaussianity when second moments are finite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键事实是：Gaussian 分布由均值向量与协方差矩阵完全决定。当 $C=0$ 时，构造两块相互独立的 Gaussian 向量，分别保持 $U,V$ 原来的均值和协方差。新向量堆起来后仍是 Gaussian，并且与原堆叠向量具有完全相同的参数，因此联合分布必定相同。构造出来的分布具有块独立性，原分布也就如此。反方向——独立推出零协方差——来自期望乘积的分解；在有限二阶矩下，这个方向不需要 Gaussian 假设。

> [!note]- The same proof in transform form · 用变换写出同一个证明
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[特征函数|characteristic function]] encodes the entire law; its Gaussian form is derived in [[联合Gaussian|the Gaussian definition card]]. With mean vectors $\mu_U,\mu_V$, fixed real test vectors $s,t$, and $C=0$, its exponent separates:
>
> $$\varphi_{U,V}(s,t)
> =e^{is^T\mu_U-\frac12s^T\Sigma_U s}\,
> e^{it^T\mu_V-\frac12t^T\Sigma_V t}
> =\varphi_U(s)\varphi_V(t).$$
>
> This is the transform of the independent product law. Uniqueness of characteristic functions gives independence without dividing by a determinant or assuming that a joint density exists.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[特征函数]]编码完整分布，Gaussian 形式见[[联合Gaussian|Gaussian 定义卡]]。记均值为 $\mu_U,\mu_V$，$s,t$ 为固定实测试向量；$C=0$ 时，指数中没有交叉项，因此如上式分解。这正是独立乘积分布的特征函数；由特征函数唯一性得到独立，不必除以行列式，也不必假设联合密度存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently the criterion includes [[Gaussian密度与奇异支撑|singular Gaussian laws]]. A constant coordinate is independent of every other coordinate and has zero covariance with it. Its Pearson [[相关系数|correlation coefficient]] is undefined because its standard deviation is zero, so **zero covariance** is the reliable wording at this boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，该判据也包括[[Gaussian密度与奇异支撑|奇异 Gaussian 分布]]。常数坐标与其他任意坐标独立，协方差为零；但它的标准差为零，所以 Pearson [[相关系数]]未定义。在这个边界上，用**零协方差**表述才准确。

## Normal marginals do not supply the missing joint assumption
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*各自正态不能补上联合假设*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Z\sim N(0,1)$ and let $S$ be an independent random sign, taking $1$ and $-1$ each with probability $1/2$. Put $U=Z$ and $V=SZ$. The symmetry of the standard normal means that both $Z$ and $-Z$ have the same law, so $U$ and $V$ are each standard normal. Nevertheless,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $Z\sim N(0,1)$，$S$ 是与 $Z$ 独立的随机符号，以各半概率取 $1$、$-1$。设 $U=Z$、$V=SZ$。标准正态的对称性使 $Z$ 与 $-Z$ 同分布，所以 $U,V$ 各自都标准正态。然而，

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(U,V)=E[SZ^2]=E[S]E[Z^2]=0,
\qquad |U|=|V|\quad\text{almost surely}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here is an event-level check of dependence. Let $q=P(|Z|\le1)$, so $0<q<1$. The events $A=\{|U|\le1\}$ and $B=\{|V|\le1\}$ are identical. Thus $P(A\cap B)=q$, whereas $P(A)P(B)=q^2\ne q$. This violates the definition of independence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以直接用事件检验依赖性。令 $q=P(|Z|\le1)$，有 $0<q<1$。事件 $A=\{|U|\le1\}$ 与 $B=\{|V|\le1\}$ 完全相同，所以 $P(A\cap B)=q$，但 $P(A)P(B)=q^2\ne q$，违反独立定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The pair fails joint Gaussianity visibly: $U+V=(1+S)Z$ equals zero with probability $1/2$ but is not constant. A nondegenerate normal has no point masses, while a degenerate normal puts all its mass at one point. This sum is neither. The example demonstrates [[零协方差不推独立|why zero covariance alone is insufficient]], even when both marginals happen to be normal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这对变量也明显不联合 Gaussian：$U+V=(1+S)Z$ 以 $1/2$ 的概率等于零，却不是常数。非退化正态没有单点质量，退化正态则把全部质量放在一个点；这个和两者都不是。因此，即使两个边际都正态，仍然可能出现[[零协方差不推独立]]。
