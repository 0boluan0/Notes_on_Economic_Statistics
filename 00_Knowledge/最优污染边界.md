# 净收益最大化是否保留污染取决于边际权衡和可行边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Whether net-benefit maximisation retains pollution depends on marginal trade-offs and feasible boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把污染纳入决策，不等于把排放机械降到零；但“最优一定有污染”也不是一般结论。经济效率问题比较**少排一点所避免的损害**与**为此付出的资源或净收益**，还要检查零排放是否就是最佳可行角点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Accounting for pollution does not mechanically require zero emissions, but positive efficient emissions are not guaranteed either. Compare the damage avoided by emitting less with the resource cost or benefit forgone, including the possibility that zero emissions is the best feasible corner.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $e\in[0,\bar e]$，允许排放带来的净私人收益为 $B(e)$，社会损害为 $D(e)$，且 $W=B-D$ 严格凹。内点最优满足 $B'(e^*)=D'(e^*)$；若右导数 $W'(0)\le0$，则零排放最优。若 $W'(\bar e)\ge0$，最优可能在上界，而不能硬解一个区间外的交点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For emissions $e\in[0,\bar e]$, let net private benefit be $B(e)$ and damage $D(e)$, with strictly concave $W=B-D$. An interior optimum solves $B'(e^*)=D'(e^*)$. If the right derivative $W'(0)\le0$, zero emissions is optimal; if $W'(\bar e)\ge0$, the upper boundary can be optimal. An intersection outside the feasible range is not a solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如固定上界 $\bar e=100$，取 $B(e)=100e-e^2/2$。若 $D(e)=e^2/2$，最优 $e^*=50$；若改为 $D(e)=120e$，则 $W'(e)=-20-e<0$，最优是0。区别来自损害函数，不来自预先偏好哪种政策。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the upper bound at $\bar e=100$ and let $B(e)=100e-e^2/2$. Damage $D(e)=e^2/2$ yields $e^*=50$. Replacing it with $D(e)=120e$ gives $W'(e)=-20-e<0$, making zero optimal. The damage function determines the difference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个简化问题没有决定法律许可、不可逆生态阈值或分配权重。若这些属于实际决策要求，应写入可行集或评价目标，再比较方案；不能拿一个无约束货币模型替代它们。具体项目的遗漏影响与替代方案复用[[公共项目净现值边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This simplified objective does not settle legal permissions, irreversible ecological thresholds or distributional weights. Include relevant requirements in the feasible set or objective. Use [[公共项目净现值边界|the public-project appraisal boundary]] for omitted effects and alternatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
