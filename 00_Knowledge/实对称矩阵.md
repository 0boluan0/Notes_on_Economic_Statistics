# Real symmetric matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*实对称矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **real symmetric matrix** has matching entries on opposite sides of its main diagonal. The main diagonal runs from the top-left corner to the bottom-right corner; a position above it is paired with the reflected position below it. For example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**实对称矩阵**在主对角线两侧的镜像位置上填着相同的数。主对角线从左上角走到右下角；上方的一个位置，与下方反射过去的位置配成一对。例如，

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&5\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The top-right and bottom-left entries are both $-1$, so they match. The diagonal entries $2$ and $5$ need not match each other: each is reflected back to its own position. Symmetry does not mean that all rows are equal or that all entries have the same sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

右上角与左下角都是 $-1$，两者相同。对角元 $2$ 与 $5$ 不必彼此相同，因为每个对角位置反射后仍是自己。对称不等于每行一样，也不要求所有元素同号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[矩阵转置|transpose]] exchanges rows and columns, so it swaps each reflected pair. In the example, this operation leaves $A$ unchanged. This gives the compact definition:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[矩阵转置]]把行列交换，因此会交换每一对镜像位置。在上例中，转置后 $A$ 完全不变。于是得到简洁的定义：

> [!important] Equal to its transpose · 等于自身的转置
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a real $n\times n$ matrix $A$, symmetry means
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对实 $n\times n$ 矩阵 $A$，对称是指
>
> $$A=A^T\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a_{ij}=a_{ji}\text{ for every }i,j.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $a_{ij}$ is the entry in row $i$, column $j$. The condition checks every reflected pair; diagonal entries are unrestricted real numbers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $a_{ij}$ 表示第 $i$ 行、第 $j$ 列的元素。条件检查每一对镜像位置；对角元可以是任意实数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing the lower-left entry to $1$ produces $\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\1&5\end{pmatrix}$, which is not symmetric: its reflected off-diagonal entries differ. The size restriction matters too. The transpose of a $2\times3$ matrix has size $3\times2$, so the two arrays cannot be equal. In contrast, every real $1\times1$ matrix is symmetric.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若把左下角改成 $1$，得到 $\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\1&5\end{pmatrix}$，就不对称了，因为镜像的两个非对角元不同。尺寸条件也不可省：$2\times3$ 矩阵转置后是 $3\times2$，两个数组无法相等。相反，每个实 $1\times1$ 矩阵都对称。

> [!warning] Symmetric does not mean positive definite · 对称不等于正定
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The matrix $D=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$ is symmetric. Yet $x^TDx=x_1^2-x_2^2$ is negative at $x=(0,1)^T$ and positive at $x=(1,0)^T$. It therefore fails the requirement in [[正定矩阵|positive definiteness]] that this value be positive for every nonzero real vector. Symmetry specifies an entry pattern; positivity is an additional condition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $D=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$ 是对称矩阵，但 $x^TDx=x_1^2-x_2^2$ 在 $x=(0,1)^T$ 处为负，在 $x=(1,0)^T$ 处为正。因此它不满足[[正定矩阵]]要求的“对每个非零实向量，这个值都为正”。对称只规定元素的排列关系；正定是额外条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The real-entry assumption also keeps this definition distinct from [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian symmetry]], which uses conjugate transpose for complex entries. The important consequences for eigenvalues belong to the [[实对称矩阵谱定理|spectral theorem]]; they are not extra tests required to recognise a symmetric matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实数元素这一前提也把这里的定义与[[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian 对称性]]区分开：后者对复数元素使用共轭转置。有关特征值的重要结论属于[[实对称矩阵谱定理]]；判断一个矩阵是否对称，不需要先检验那些结论。
