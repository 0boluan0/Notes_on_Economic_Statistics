# 当前创新提高后代创新的生产率基础却未获得全部后续回报
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An innovation raises the productivity foundation inherited by later innovations without capturing all subsequent returns.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Aghion–Howitt 模型中的跨期知识外溢，是本次创新把以后各代创新所继承的生产率起点永久抬高，而当前创新者只取得被下一代替代之前的部分回报。**原创新者的利润可以消失，创新对后代的贡献仍然存在。** 这是 [[创新租金的替代风险|私人收租期限]] 与知识贡献期限的区别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The intertemporal spillover in Aghion–Howitt is that an innovation permanently raises the productivity foundation inherited by later generations, while its owner captures only part of the return before replacement. Private profits can disappear while the contribution survives. This separates the [[创新租金的替代风险|duration of private rents]] from the duration of the knowledge contribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如每次创新使生产率乘以 $\gamma=1.5$。今天把起点从 $1$ 提到 $1.5$ 后，再有一次同样的质量改进，生产率达到 $2.25$；若没有今天这一贡献、但后来仍发生一次相同的改进，则只有 $1.5$。此比较保持后续改进次数与步长相同，用来隔离本次创新抬高后续起点的作用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose every discovery multiplies productivity by $1.5$. Raising today's base from one to $1.5$ lets one further improvement reach $2.25$. Without today's contribution, one otherwise identical later improvement reaches only $1.5$. Holding the number and size of subsequent improvements fixed isolates the inherited productivity gain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

后来创新者在更高起点上取得自己的租金，消费者也可享有更高生产能力；他们不为这一后续基础向早期创新者支付全部补偿。于是私人研发奖励漏掉部分跨代收益，这一渠道本身使研发倾向不足。但总方向仍要与 [[商业抢夺效应|旧租金损失]] 等因素一同比较，见 [[私人与社会研发]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Later innovators earn rents on the higher foundation, and consumers can benefit from greater productive capacity, without fully compensating the original innovator for that foundation. This omission tends to depress private research. The overall direction still depends on [[商业抢夺效应|displaced incumbent rents]] and other effects; see [[私人与社会研发|private versus socially optimal research]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个机制不表示“别的实验室本期做得越多，我本期越容易成功”。原文基本模型明确排除同时期研究投入之间的这种外溢；给定本企业投入，其到达技术不取决于其他企业的当期研究。跨代质量起点更高，也不等于未来每单位研发劳动的到达效率 $\lambda$ 随之提高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is not a contemporaneous learning effect in which other laboratories' current effort raises a firm's current success rate. The basic paper explicitly excludes that effect: conditional on its own inputs, a firm's arrival technology does not depend on other firms' current research. A higher inherited productivity level also does not mean that research efficiency $\lambda$ rises.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
