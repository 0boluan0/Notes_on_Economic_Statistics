# 处置效应是投资者相对更倾向实现盈利而保留亏损头寸的行为模式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The disposition effect is a greater propensity to realize gains than losses on investment positions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

处置效应是以指定基准划分账面盈亏后，投资者相对于可实现机会，更倾向卖出盈利头寸而保留亏损头寸的行为模式。它不是简单地“卖出的赢家数量多于输家”：上涨市场本来就可能提供更多盈利头寸。买入价是常用的[[前景参考点|分类基准]]，但其设定需要明确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The disposition effect is a greater tendency to sell winning positions and retain losing ones, relative to opportunities to realize each, after gains and losses are classified against a specified benchmark. Merely selling more winners is insufficient: a rising market may provide more winning positions. Purchase price is a common [[前景参考点|classification benchmark]], whose specification must be explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Odean 的实现比例按账户发生卖出的日期统计已实现与未实现盈亏机会。记 $R_G,P_G$ 为已实现盈利与账面盈利次数，$R_L,P_L$ 为对应亏损次数；在分母为正时，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Odean's realization proportions count realized and unrealized gain/loss opportunities on dates when an account makes a sale. Let $R_G,P_G$ count realized and paper gains, and $R_L,P_L$ the corresponding losses. With positive denominators,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\mathrm{PGR}=\frac{R_G}{R_G+P_G},\qquad
\mathrm{PLR}=\frac{R_L}{R_L+P_L}.$$

例如自拟汇总为 $(R_G,P_G)=(3,7)$、$(R_L,P_L)=(1,9)$，则 PGR 为 $0.3$，PLR 为 $0.1$，表现为实现盈利的比例较高。这些是机会计数而非收益金额；实际推断还需足够样本、清晰的盈亏分类及恰当的统计比较，不能由一笔卖赢交易判定某个人具有稳定偏差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Illustrative counts $(R_G,P_G)=(3,7)$ and $(R_L,P_L)=(1,9)$ give PGR $0.3$ and PLR $0.1$: gains are realized at a higher rate. These are opportunity counts, not monetary returns. Inference still requires adequate data, explicit classification, and suitable statistical comparison; one winning sale does not establish a persistent individual bias.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“卖得过早、持有过久”的规范评价还需要税收、交易成本、再平衡或预期收益等基准；行为模式本身也没有唯一识别其心理原因，见[[处置效应解释边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Calling sales “too early” or holding periods “too long” additionally requires benchmarks involving taxes, costs, rebalancing, or expected returns. The pattern does not uniquely identify its psychological cause; see [[处置效应解释边界|the explanation boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
