# 吞没形态是趋势后第二根K线实体覆盖第一根实体的两线组合

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An engulfing pattern is a two-candle formation after a trend in which the second real body contains the first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

吞没形态是明确上涨或下降走势后出现的两根[[K线]]组合：第二根较大的实体覆盖第一根实体，两根通常方向相反。下降后由阳线实体覆盖阴线实体称为看涨吞没；上涨后由阴线实体覆盖阳线实体称为看跌吞没。判断覆盖对象是开盘与收盘围成的实体，不要求第二根同时包住第一根的最高价和最低价。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An engulfing pattern consists of two [[K线|candlesticks]] after an identifiable advance or decline: the larger second real body contains the first, usually with opposite body directions. An up body engulfing a down body after a decline is bullish engulfing; the opposite after an advance is bearish engulfing. Containment concerns open-to-close bodies, not necessarily the full high-low ranges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如第一根 $O_1=103,C_1=100,H_1=105,L_1=99$，第二根 $O_2=99.5,C_2=104,H_2=104.5,L_2=99.2$，第二实体覆盖 $[100,103]$，但第二根的整个价格区间没有覆盖第一根。先前还须有下降走势，才能按看涨吞没解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $O_1=103,C_1=100,H_1=105,L_1=99$ and $O_2=99.5,C_2=104,H_2=104.5,L_2=99.2$, the second body contains $[100,103]$ without containing the first candle's full range. A preceding decline is still required for the bullish interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本课允许第一根近似十字线时出现同方向实体的例外；把规则用于程序识别时，应先冻结“近似”“较大”和端点相等的容差。第二根收盘前不能确定其最终实体。看涨、看跌表示学派赋予的候选方向，不是后续收益保证；它与后小实体被前长实体包住的[[孕育形态]]方向相反。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course allows a same-direction exception when the first body is nearly a doji. A programmed detector must first specify size, near-doji and endpoint-equality tolerances. The second body is final only at its close. Bullish and bearish name candidate interpretations rather than guaranteed returns. The containment direction is opposite to that in a [[孕育形态|harami]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
