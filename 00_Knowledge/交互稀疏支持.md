# 空交互格使相应饱和对比不可识别，稀疏或缺乏共同支持的格子使交互估计依赖外推和少数观测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An empty interaction cell makes the corresponding saturated contrast unidentified, while sparse cells or poor common support make interaction estimates depend on extrapolation and a few observations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 软件能返回数字不等于数据有比较
> 交互效应比较一个变量的效应如何随另一个变量变化，因此必须在相关组合中都有可用信息。对两个类别变量，先列每个单元格的观测数；对类别×连续变量，先画各组 $X$ 的支持范围。没有共同支持时，模型只能靠函数形式把一组的回归线延伸到另一组从未出现的位置。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An interaction compares how one variable's effect changes with another, so the relevant combinations must contain usable information. For two factors, tabulate observations in every cell. For an indicator-by-continuous interaction, plot the support of $X$ in each group. Without common support, the model can answer only by extrapolating one group's regression line into values that group never exhibits.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在饱和 2×2 模型中，若 $(D_1,D_2)=(1,1)$ 从未出现，四格差异之差缺少 $\mu_{11}$，相应设计列无法提供独立方向；除非额外施加限制，否则交互参数没有数据内答案。某格只有极少观测时，代数上可能仍满秩，但该格均值和交互对比会有很大不确定性，并可能被一两个异常观测主导。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a saturated 2×2 model, if $(D_1,D_2)=(1,1)$ never occurs, the four-cell contrast lacks $\mu_{11}$ and the corresponding design column supplies no independent direction. Without an additional restriction, the interaction has no data-internal answer. With only a few observations in a cell, the design may remain full rank, but the cell mean and interaction contrast are imprecise and can be driven by one or two unusual observations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续变量还要区分“各组都出现”与“各组在同一取值附近都出现”。若基准组经验只在 0–5 年、比较组只在 15–20 年，软件可以拟合两条线，却不能从数据直接比较 10 年经验处的组差。中心化到 10 年只改变系数参照点，不会补出缺失观测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a continuous variable, it is not enough that both groups exist; they must also appear near the same values. If the reference group has 0–5 years of experience and the comparison group 15–20, software can fit two lines but cannot directly learn the group difference at ten years. Centering at ten changes the coefficient reference point without creating missing observations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

验收顺序是：检查单元格计数和各组支持图，标出目标对比所在位置，再查看杠杆、区间和设定敏感性。若目标落在共同支持外，应缩小 estimand、收集缺失组合或明确标注模型外推，而不是只报告交互项 $p$ 值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check cell counts and group-specific support plots first, mark where the target contrast lies, and only then inspect leverage, intervals, and specification sensitivity. If the target lies outside common support, narrow the estimand, collect the missing combinations, or label the result as model extrapolation rather than reporting only an interaction p-value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两组都有 100 个观测，但一组的年龄全在 18–25，另一组全在 60–70。样本量大是否足以支持比较 40 岁处的组差？
>
> **答案：** 不足。40 岁处两组都没有共同支持，比较依赖所选函数形式的外推，而不是直接的同龄数据比较。
