# Data support for interaction comparisons
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*交互比较的数据支持*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before interpreting an interaction estimate, ask which observations support the comparison. A total sample can be large while one crucial category combination is empty, or while two groups occupy completely different ranges of a continuous predictor. These situations affect what can be learned from the data in different ways: an empty cell can prevent a particular comparison from being estimated, a sparse cell can make it sensitive to a few observations, and poor overlap can make a fitted comparison depend on extrapolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解释交互估计之前，先问：比较所需的信息来自哪些观测？即使总样本很大，一个关键类别组合也可能没有人，或两组在连续变量上占据完全不同的范围。这些情况的后果不同：空格可能使特定对比无法估计，稀疏格可能让结果依赖少数观测，重叠不足则可能让拟合比较依靠外推。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An empty cell leaves a direction undetermined
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*空格留下一个无法由样本确定的方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the [[类别类别交互|two-indicator interaction]], each observation has a design row $(1,D_1,D_2,D_1D_2)$, where the [[事件指示变量|indicators]] are zero or one. With exactly these four columns, seeing every combination makes the columns [[线性无关|linearly independent]] and the [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|OLS coefficients unique]]. Suppose instead the $(1,1)$ cell is absent from the usable sample. Every observed product $D_1D_2$ is then zero: changing its coefficient cannot change any fitted observation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[类别类别交互|两指示变量交互]]，每个观测的设计行是 $(1,D_1,D_2,D_1D_2)$，其中[[事件指示变量]]取零或一。只使用这四列时，四种组合都出现就使各列[[线性无关]]，从而 [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|OLS 系数唯一]]。假如可用样本中没有 $(1,1)$ 格，所有观察到的 $D_1D_2$ 都为零；无论怎样改变其系数，都不会改变任何已观察结果的拟合值。

> [!example] The same observed cells permit different interactions · 相同已观察格允许不同交互
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose the three nonempty cells have sample means $\bar Y_{00}=2$, $\bar Y_{10}=5$, $\bar Y_{01}=4$. The models $2+3D_1+2D_2$ and $2+3D_1+2D_2+3D_1D_2$ fit those cells identically. They predict $7$ and $10$ in the missing cell and give interaction contrasts $0$ and $3$. The sample alone cannot choose between them in the unrestricted four-cell model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设三个非空格的样本均值为 $2,5,4$。模型 $2+3D_1+2D_2$ 与 $2+3D_1+2D_2+3D_1D_2$ 对这三个格给出相同拟合，却对缺失格分别预测 $7$、$10$，对应交互 $0$、$3$。在不加限制的四格模型中，样本本身无法选择其中一个。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is **non-estimability from the realised design**, not proof that every population parameter is unidentified. A cell may have positive population probability but happen to be empty in this sample. If its population probability is actually zero, the ordinary conditional mean $E[Y\mid D_1=1,D_2=1]$ is not determined by the observed distribution at all; assigning it a value needs an additional model or target definition. The [[条件期望|definition of conditional expectation]] explains the zero-probability boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是**给定本次实现设计时的不可估性**，不等于证明所有总体参数都不可识别。某格在总体中可能有正概率，只是本次样本恰好没有。若它在总体中的概率确实为零，通常的条件均值 $E[Y\mid D_1=1,D_2=1]$ 就根本不由观察分布确定；给它指定数值需要额外模型或目标定义。[[条件期望]]的定义解释了零概率边界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Not every contrast disappears with the empty cell. In the example, the $D_1$ gap within $D_2=0$ is still $5-2=3$. What fails is a comparison requiring the unobserved cell, such as the unrestricted four-cell difference in differences. Dropping a software column, choosing a reference group or imposing zero interaction can select a number, but does not manufacture data for the missing comparison. A justified restriction can make a different, restricted model estimable; it must be named as such.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

空一格并不会使所有对比都消失。本例在 $D_2=0$ 内的 $D_1$ 组差仍为 $5-2=3$；无法从样本确定的是需要缺失格的比较，例如不受限的四格差异之差。软件删除一列、选择基准或强制交互为零，都可能选出一个数字，却不会创造缺失比较的数据。有依据的限制可以使另一个受限模型可估，但需要明确说明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A sparse cell is a precision question, not automatically a rank failure
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*稀疏格关乎精度，不自动等于秩亏*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n_{ab}>0$ count the observations in each of the four cells. One observation per cell is already enough for full column rank in the four-column model. It is not enough to estimate a separate within-cell variance, and four observations in total leave no residual degrees of freedom: the four fitted cell means already fit all four observations exactly. Algebraic uniqueness and statistical precision are separate properties.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $n_{ab}>0$ 为每格观测数。在四列模型中，每格一个观测就足以保证满列秩；但这不足以估计各格自己的组内方差，而且总共只有四个观测时，四个拟合格均值已经把四个观测完全拟合，没有剩余残差自由度。代数唯一性和统计精度是不同性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see how counts can affect precision, condition on the fixed cell assignments and assume independent observations with a common finite error variance $\sigma^2$. The fitted interaction is $\hat\beta_{12}=\bar Y_{11}-\bar Y_{01}-\bar Y_{10}+\bar Y_{00}$. Independence removes the cross-covariances, and each cell average has variance $\sigma^2/n_{ab}$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了看清人数如何影响精度，固定各观测的类别，假设观测相互独立、误差具有相同有限方差 $\sigma^2$。拟合交互为 $\hat\beta_{12}=\bar Y_{11}-\bar Y_{01}-\bar Y_{10}+\bar Y_{00}$。独立性消除了交叉协方差，每格均值的方差为 $\sigma^2/n_{ab}$，因此

$$
\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_{12}\mid\text{cell assignments})
=\sigma^2\left(\frac1{n_{00}}+\frac1{n_{10}}+\frac1{n_{01}}+\frac1{n_{11}}\right).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If three cells each have $25$ observations and the fourth has $1$, this is $(28/25)\sigma^2$. If that last cell grows to $25$ while the others stay the same, it becomes $(4/25)\sigma^2$. The sparse design has seven times the variance in this comparison, and $\sqrt7$ times the [[标准误含义|standard error]]. Under these assumptions, adding many observations only to already large cells cannot eliminate the $\sigma^2/n_{11}$ contribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若三个格各有 $25$ 个观测，第四格只有 $1$ 个，方差为 $(28/25)\sigma^2$；保持前三格不变，把最后一格增至 $25$ 个后，变为 $(4/25)\sigma^2$。在这个比较中，稀疏设计的方差是七倍，[[标准误含义|标准误]]是 $\sqrt7$ 倍。在这些假设下，只往已经很大的格子里增加观测，无法消除 $\sigma^2/n_{11}$ 这一项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Counts alone do not prove that the absolute uncertainty is large: the noise level matters too. With unequal variances, replace each term by its cell variance divided by its count; with dependent observations, include the relevant [[协方差|covariances]]. A few observations also make a cell mean sensitive to individual values, so inspect those observations and report uncertainty using the appropriate [[线性组合推断|contrast variance]], rather than treating one universal sample-size cutoff as decisive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仅凭人数少，不能证明绝对不确定性一定很大，噪声水平也很重要。若各格方差不同，应分别使用格内方差除以人数；若观察之间依赖，还需计入相应[[协方差]]。少数观测也会让格内均值对个别数值敏感，因此要查看这些观察，并用合适的[[线性组合推断|对比方差]]报告不确定性，而不是用一个通用人数阈值作结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two fitted lines can exist without an observed same-value comparison
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*能够拟合两条线，不代表有同一取值下的观察比较*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a [[类别连续交互|group-by-continuous interaction]], distinguish variation within each group from overlap between groups. Suppose observed experience ranges from $0$ to $5$ years in group $D=0$, and from $15$ to $20$ in group $D=1$. Each group can contain several distinct experience values, so the full two-line design can have full column rank. Nevertheless, comparing the groups at ten years extends both fitted lines beyond their observed ranges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[类别连续交互|类别与连续变量交互]]，要区分各组内部有变化与两组之间有重叠。设观察到的经验范围在 $D=0$ 组为 $0$–$5$ 年，在 $D=1$ 组为 $15$–$20$ 年。每组都可以有多个不同经验值，所以完整两直线设计仍可满列秩；但在十年经验处比较，需要把两条拟合线都延伸到各自观察范围之外。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A linear model can produce that extrapolated gap. Its interpretation depends on extending the chosen line shapes into the unobserved region, not on a direct ten-year comparison in the data. Moving the reference point to ten years by centring $X$ leaves this situation unchanged; [[交互主效应基准|centring changes the coefficient interpretation]], not the available observations. Even $100$ observations aged $18$–$25$ in one group and $100$ aged $60$–$70$ in another do not directly supply a comparison at age $40$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线性模型能计算出这种外推组差，但其解释依赖把选定的直线形状延伸到未观察区域，而不是数据中直接存在十年经验的组间比较。把 $X$ 中心化到十年不改变这一点：[[交互主效应基准|中心化改变系数的解释]]，不增加可用观察。同样，即使一组有 $100$ 人、年龄都为 $18$–$25$，另一组有 $100$ 人、年龄都为 $60$–$70$，也没有直接提供四十岁处的比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sample range is evidence about **observed coverage**, not proof that the population assigns zero probability outside it. For continuous predictors, useful overlap concerns observations in neighbourhoods of the target value, not exact repeats of one real number. There may also be large gaps inside a group's minimum-to-maximum range. Look at the distribution of observations, not just its endpoints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

样本范围反映的是**观察覆盖**，不能证明总体在范围外的概率为零。连续变量的有用重叠，是目标值附近有观察，而不是某一个实数必须恰好重复。即使落在一组最小值与最大值之间，中间也可能存在大段缺口；应查看观察的分布，不能只看两端。

## Match the response to the missing information
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*根据缺少哪类信息决定下一步*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with the intended contrast, then inspect cell counts or group-specific predictor plots on the actual estimation sample. Distinguish an absent combination, a noisy small cell and an extrapolated target. Collect the missing combinations where feasible, or explicitly redefine the comparison to a supported population or region. A restriction or smooth model can borrow information, but its assumptions become part of the answer. Show sensitivity to consequential observations and plausible alternative specifications; an interaction p-value alone cannot repair absent support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先明确目标对比，再按真正用于估计的样本查看单元格计数或各组预测变量分布，区分缺失组合、噪声较大的小格与需要外推的目标。可行时补充缺失组合的数据，或明确把比较对象改为有支持的总体或区域。限制或平滑模型可以借用信息，但相应假设就成为答案的一部分。应展示结果对关键观测与合理替代设定的敏感性；单独一个交互项 $p$ 值不能补足缺失支持。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Data support is also separate from [[条件均值因果边界|causal identification]]. Observing every cell does not prove that training was as-if randomly assigned. Conversely, a model-based prediction outside observed overlap should be described with the identifying or extrapolation assumptions it uses, rather than being presented as a directly observed causal comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数据支持还不同于[[条件均值因果边界|因果识别]]：四格都有人，并不证明培训如同随机分配。反过来，超出观察重叠的模型预测，应交代所依赖的识别或外推假设，不能被说成直接观察到的因果比较。

- [Stata, *margins* manual, PDF p. 9, pp. 40–41 and p. 46](https://www.stata.com/manuals/rmargins.pdf#page=46): empty-cell estimability, the risks of bypassing estimability checks, and how reweighting changes a marginal target. The two indistinguishable fits above demonstrate the boundary directly.
- [Penn State STAT 462, §10.10, Extrapolation](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat462/node/185/): extrapolation beyond observed predictor ranges and the role of model shape outside those ranges. The two-group experience and age examples apply this principle.
- [NIST/SEMATECH, Two-Way Crossed ANOVA](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/ppc/section2/ppc232.htm): crossed cells and within-cell replication. The rank and count-dependent variance results above are derived directly; normality is not needed for those algebra/moment statements.
- [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/10_虚拟变量.md#1.5 先检查支持，再解释、推断和命名|The permanent course section]] gives the workflow's context. The current card distinguishes realised-sample estimability from population identification and observed ranges from population support.

- Stata *margins* 手册 PDF p. 9、pp. 40–41、p. 46 核对空格可估性、绕过检查的风险和重新加权改变目标；两个同样拟合的模型直接展示边界。
- Penn State STAT 462 第 10.10 节 Extrapolation 核对观察范围之外的外推及函数形式作用；经验与年龄例子是其两组情形应用。
