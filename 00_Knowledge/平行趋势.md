# 平行趋势约束未处理潜在结果的变化而非结果水平

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Parallel trends restricts changes in untreated potential outcomes, not outcome levels*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心区别
> 两组在政策前可以相差很远；DID 要求的是：若没有政策，处理组与对照组的平均结果会以相同幅度变化。固定的水平差不会破坏平行趋势，变化速度的系统差异才会。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The groups may be far apart before treatment. DID instead requires their average untreated outcomes to change by the same amount in the absence of treatment. A fixed level gap is compatible with parallel trends; a systematic difference in their rates of change is not.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最简单的无条件平行趋势写成

$$
E[Y_1(0)-Y_0(0)\mid G=1]
=E[Y_1(0)-Y_0(0)\mid G=0].
$$

这里比较的是 $Y(0)$ 的变化。处理后，处理组的 $Y_1(0)$ 恰好看不见，所以平行趋势本质上是一个反事实假设。它既不是“处理前均值相同”，也不是“观测到的处理后结果继续平行”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation compares changes in $Y(0)$. After treatment, the treated group's $Y_1(0)$ is precisely what cannot be observed, so parallel trends is fundamentally a counterfactual assumption. It is neither equality of pretreatment means nor a requirement that observed post-treatment outcomes remain parallel.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

核心是 $E[Y_t(0)-Y_{t-1}(0)\mid G=1]=E[\cdot\mid G=0]$，另需无预期、稳定样本构成、处理定义稳定和组间无干扰等条件。处理前趋势图和伪处理检验（placebo）能发现明显不相容，却无法证明处理后未处理趋势本会平行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The core condition is $E[Y_t(0)-Y_{t-1}(0)\mid G=1]=E[\cdot\mid G=0]$. Additional assumptions include no anticipation, stable sample composition, a stable treatment definition, and no interference across groups. Pretreatment trend plots and placebo tests can reveal clear incompatibilities, but they cannot prove that untreated trends would have remained parallel after treatment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 水平不同仍可平行

若处理组结果从 $50$ 本会升到 $55$，对照组从 $30$ 升到 $35$，两组始终相差 $20$，但未处理变化同为 $5$，平行趋势成立。相反，若处理组本会从 $50$ 升到 $60$、对照组从 $30$ 升到 $35$，即使某个处理前时点两组均值偶然相同，也不能用对照组的 $5$ 代表处理组本来的 $10$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the treated group would rise from $50$ to $55$ and the comparison group from $30$ to $35$, their levels remain 20 apart but both untreated changes equal 5, so parallel trends holds. If instead the treated group would rise from $50$ to $60$ while the comparison group rises from $30$ to $35$, the comparison change of 5 cannot represent the treated group's counterfactual change of 10—even if their means happened to coincide at some earlier date.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 条件平行趋势不是“多放控制变量”

有时无条件趋势不同，但在相同年龄、行业或处理前结果历史 $X$ 下，未处理变化更可比。此时假设变成对每个有共同支持的 $X$，两组满足条件平行趋势。它是一个新的识别主张：研究者必须说明为何这些处理前变量足以解释趋势差异，并检查处理组与对照组在这些变量上是否真有可比区域。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unconditional trends may differ while untreated changes are comparable within the same age, industry, or pretreatment outcome history $X$. The assumption then becomes conditional parallel trends over values of $X$ with common support. This is a new identifying claim, not a mechanical instruction to add controls: the researcher must explain why the pretreatment covariates account for trend differences and verify that treated and comparison units overlap on them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 处理前一年，处理组均值为 100、对照组为 60。这个 40 的差距本身是否违反平行趋势？还缺什么信息？
>
> **答案：** 不违反。还要判断在没有处理时两组的平均变化是否会相同；水平差本身不回答趋势差的问题。
