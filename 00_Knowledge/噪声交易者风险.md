# 噪声交易者风险是未来情绪变化使错误定价在退出前进一步扩大的风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Noise-trader risk is the risk that future sentiment changes make mispricing worse before a position is closed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

噪声交易者风险来自未来[[噪声交易者|错误信念所形成的需求]]难以预测。买入被悲观情绪压低的资产后，悲观可能继续加深；卖空被乐观情绪推高的资产后，乐观也可能更强。交易者退出时面对的不只是基本价值，还包括那时别人愿意付的价格。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Noise-trader risk comes from uncertainty about future [[噪声交易者|demand generated by mistaken beliefs]]. Pessimism can intensify after buying an underpriced asset; optimism can intensify after shorting an overpriced one. At exit, the trader faces not only fundamental value but also the price others will then pay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它可以在没有[[基本面风险]]时出现：设基本价值固定为 100，当前情绪把价格压到 90，下一期价格可能为 80 或 100。忽略利息与费用，买一份后必须在下一期卖出，则损益分别为 −10 和 +10。这个例子没有指定两种状态的概率，因此不声称预期利润为多少；它只展示基本价值不变仍不能排除退出损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It can arise without [[基本面风险|fundamental risk]]. Let fundamental value remain 100, current sentiment push price to 90, and next-period price be either 80 or 100. Ignoring interest and costs, buying one unit and having to sell next period yields −10 or +10. No probabilities are specified, so no expected profit is claimed. The example only shows that unchanged fundamental value does not rule out exit losses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 De Long 等（DSSW）模型中，风险厌恶与有限持有期限使理性交易者不愿无限承接这种风险。模型还要求相关噪声不能被轻易分散：其基准设定是市场总体的情绪风险，而不是把无数互不相关且微小的个股误差直接当成同一个总风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the De Long et al. (DSSW) model, risk aversion and finite holding horizons prevent rational traders from absorbing this risk without limit. Relevant noise must also resist easy diversification: the benchmark is marketwide sentiment risk, not an automatic aggregation of countless tiny, uncorrelated stock-specific errors into the same total risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这解释了“看得更准”与“能立刻消除偏离”之间的缺口，却不证明所有逆向交易都赚钱，也不证明噪声交易者在任何参数下都会取得更高收益或永久存活。模型可能允许噪声交易者获得较高预期回报，但预期回报、风险、实现财富和存活不是一个命题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mechanism explains the gap between better valuation and immediate elimination of a discrepancy. It does not show that every contrarian trade profits or that noise traders earn more or survive forever under all parameters. A model can allow higher expected returns for noise traders, but expected return, risk, realized wealth, and survival are different claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [De Long–Shleifer–Summers–Waldmann（1990），PDF 第 4–8 页／印刷第 705–709 页](https://ms.mcmaster.ca/~grasselli/DeLongShleiferSummersWaldmann90.pdf#page=4)：已重开且目视第 705 页，支持未来情绪继续偏离、短期限与风险厌恶、固定基本现金流以及市场总体噪声条件。−10／+10 是本卡自拟的无概率退出算例，不是原论文的均衡参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- De Long–Shleifer–Summers–Waldmann, PDF pp. 4–8 / printed pp. 705–709, reopened with p. 705 visually checked, supports worsening future sentiment, short horizons, risk aversion, fixed fundamental cash flows, and marketwide noise. The −10/+10 example is an independent exit-payoff illustration without assigned probabilities, not the paper's equilibrium calibration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Shleifer–Vishny（1997），PDF 第 4 页／印刷第 38 页](https://web.stanford.edu/~piazzesi/Reading/ShleiferVishny1997.pdf#page=4)：已重开，核对最终价值已知但中途悲观冲击仍可加深的设定，作为区别基本面风险与中间价格风险的对照。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Shleifer–Vishny, PDF p. 4 / printed p. 38, reopened, provides a contrasting setup with known final value but potentially worsening interim pessimism, separating fundamental from intermediate price risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
