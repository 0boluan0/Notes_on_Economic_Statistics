# Smith 的有效需求是在自然价格上愿意且有能力购买商品、足以支持其上市供给的需求
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Smith's effectual demand is demand willing and able to purchase at natural price, sufficient to bring the commodity to market*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Smith 所说的**有效需求（effectual demand）**，是买者愿意并能够按[[Smith自然价格|自然价格]]购买的需求。这个价格足以支付供应商品所需的通常报酬，因此这类需求能够支持生产者把商品送到市场上；只有愿望，还达不到这一步。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith's **effectual demand** is demand from buyers willing and able to pay the [[Smith自然价格|natural price]]. Because that price covers the ordinary returns required to supply the commodity, such demand can induce it to be brought to market. A wish alone need not do so.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

他的例子是，一个穷人也许很想拥有豪华马车，却不能为它支付足以维持供应的价格。这个人确实有愿望，但不是该商品的有效需求者。区分的是购买条件，不是说穷人的需要不重要，更不是给需要的社会价值排序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
His example is a poor person who might like a coach and six but cannot pay a price that sustains its supply. The wish is real, yet the person is not an effectual demander of that commodity. The distinction concerns purchasing conditions, not the importance or social worth of the person's needs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这样就能理解 Smith 如何判断供给过多或过少：比较的是送到市场的数量与**自然价格上能被购买的数量**。若前者不足，部分买者会竞相加价；若前者过多，一部分货物要降价才能卖出。随后报酬和供给怎样调整，由[[自然价格的趋向机制]]继续说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This explains Smith's comparison of excessive or insufficient supply: the quantity brought to market is compared with **the quantity buyers will purchase at natural price**. A shortage leads some buyers to bid more; an excess requires some goods to be sold for less. The subsequent response of returns and supply is explained by the [[自然价格的趋向机制|gravitation mechanism]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里是在解释单项商品的价格与供应。读到中文“有效需求”时，要保留 Smith 的这个语境；它不是 Keynes 用来讨论总产出与就业的总量命题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The argument concerns the price and supply of a particular commodity. Retain this Smithian context when reading the term: it is not Keynes's aggregate proposition about output and employment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Smith，《国富论》I.VII.8–11](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=10)：第 8 段定义 effectual demand，并以贫穷者想要马车区别愿望与可支持供给的需求；第 9–11 段据此比较上市量及价格。后来的总量理论不作为此历史术语的定义依据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Smith, *The Wealth of Nations*, I.VII.8–11](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=10): paragraph 8 defines effectual demand and contrasts it with a poor person's wish for a coach; paragraphs 9–11 use it to relate market supply and price. Later aggregate theory does not define this historical term.
