# Consistency of prescribed images
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*相关向量的指定像必须保持关系*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose we specify what a [[线性映射|linear map]] should do to several input vectors. If one input can already be built from the others, its output cannot be chosen independently: the same combination must build its output. Otherwise one input would receive two conflicting answers. The condition is to preserve **every linear relation** among the specified inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假如我们指定一个[[线性映射]]对若干输入向量的作用。如果某个输入已经能由其他输入组合出来，它的输出就不能独立选择：相同组合必须得到它的输出。否则，同一个输入会得到两个冲突的答案。需要保持的是指定输入之间的**每一条线性关系**。

> [!example] Three values with only two independent inputs · 三个值，只有两个独立输入
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $\mathbb R^2$, take $u_1=(1,0)$, $u_2=(0,1)$ and $u_3=(1,1)=u_1+u_2$. Seek a linear map into $\mathbb R$ with $T(u_1)=2$ and $T(u_2)=-1$. Linearity forces
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\mathbb R^2$ 中取 $u_1=(1,0)$、$u_2=(0,1)$ 和 $u_3=(1,1)=u_1+u_2$。希望构造进入 $\mathbb R$ 的线性映射，使 $T(u_1)=2$、$T(u_2)=-1$。线性性迫使
>
> $$T(u_3)=T(u_1+u_2)=T(u_1)+T(u_2)=1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Prescribing $T(u_3)=4$ is impossible. Prescribing $T(u_3)=1$ works: $T(s,t)=2s-t$ satisfies all three requirements. The redundant input supplies a consistency check, not an extra free value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 再规定 $T(u_3)=4$ 就不可能实现；规定 $T(u_3)=1$ 则可行，因为 $T(s,t)=2s-t$ 满足全部三个要求。冗余的输入提供一致性检查，而非额外可自由选择的值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A condition for all coefficient relations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*检查所有系数关系*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $V,W$ be [[向量空间|vector spaces]] over the same scalar field $\mathbb F$. Choose a finite list $u_1,\ldots,u_k\in V$, where $k\ge1$, and prescribed outputs $w_1,\ldots,w_k\in W$. Write
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $V,W$ 是同一标量域 $\mathbb F$ 上的[[向量空间]]。给定有限组 $u_1,\ldots,u_k\in V$，其中 $k\ge1$，并为它们指定输出 $w_1,\ldots,w_k\in W$。记

$$U=\operatorname{span}(u_1,\ldots,u_k).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This [[张成|span]] is the set of all combinations $\sum_j c_ju_j$ with coefficients $c_j\in\mathbb F$. It is the part of the input space reachable from the given list. A relation is an equality $\sum_j c_ju_j=0_V$; the subscript distinguishes the zero vector of $V$ from that of $W$. A nontrivial relation has coefficients not all zero and makes the list [[线性无关|linearly dependent]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个[[张成]]由所有系数 $c_j\in\mathbb F$ 所给的组合 $\sum_j c_ju_j$ 构成，是这组输入能够覆盖的部分。关系指等式 $\sum_j c_ju_j=0_V$；下标区分 $V$ 与 $W$ 的零向量。非平凡关系的系数不全为零，意味着这组向量不是[[线性无关]]的，而是线性相关的。

> [!important] Exactly the consistency condition · 恰好刻画一致性
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> There exists a linear map $S:U\to W$ with $S(u_j)=w_j$ for all $j$ if and only if
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 存在线性映射 $S:U\to W$ 满足全部 $S(u_j)=w_j$，当且仅当
>
> $$\boxed{\text{for every }(c_1,\ldots,c_k)\in\mathbb F^k,\quad
> \sum_{j=1}^k c_ju_j=0_V\ \Longrightarrow\ \sum_{j=1}^k c_jw_j=0_W.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When it exists, this map is **unique on $U$**, and its rule is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一旦存在，它在 **$U$ 上唯一**，规则为
>
> $$S\!\left(\sum_{j=1}^k c_ju_j\right)=\sum_{j=1}^k c_jw_j.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inputs need not be independent. In that case their coefficient representations may differ, but the displayed condition ensures that the resulting output does not. No finite-dimensionality assumption on the ambient $V$ or on $W$ is needed for this statement about the finite span $U$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入不必无关；相关时，同一输入可能有不同的系数表示，但上述条件保证算出的输出相同。这个关于有限张成空间 $U$ 的结论，不要求整个 $V$ 或 $W$ 有限维。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the condition is necessary and sufficient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么既必要又充分？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Necessity.** A linear map carries a combination to the same combination of its outputs and sends zero to zero. Thus any relation among the inputs gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**必要性。** 线性映射把输入的组合变成相同系数的输出组合，并把零送到零。因此，输入的任一关系都给出

$$\sum_j c_jw_j=\sum_j c_jS(u_j)
=S\!\left(\sum_j c_ju_j\right)=S(0_V)=0_W.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Sufficiency begins with a well-defined output.** For $x\in U$, choose an expression $x=\sum_j a_ju_j$ and propose $S(x)=\sum_j a_jw_j$. Suppose another expression is $x=\sum_j b_ju_j$. Subtracting the two input expressions produces a zero relation. The condition then gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**充分性先保证输出无歧义。** 对 $x\in U$，任选表示 $x=\sum_j a_ju_j$，拟定义 $S(x)=\sum_j a_jw_j$。若另有表示 $x=\sum_j b_ju_j$，两种输入表示相减便得到零关系，条件随即给出

$$\sum_j(a_j-b_j)u_j=0_V
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum_j(a_j-b_j)w_j=0_W
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum_j a_jw_j=\sum_j b_jw_j.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So the answer depends only on $x$, not on the chosen coefficients. Every $x\in U$ has at least one expression, and the output belongs to $W$, which is closed under linear combinations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以答案只取决于 $x$，不取决于所选系数。每个 $x\in U$ 至少有一种表示；而 $W$ 对线性组合封闭，因此输出确实属于 $W$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Check linearity and the values.** If $x=\sum_j a_ju_j$, $y=\sum_j b_ju_j$ and $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F$, we may use the coefficients $\alpha a_j+\beta b_j$ to compute
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**检查线性性与指定值。** 若 $x=\sum_j a_ju_j$、$y=\sum_j b_ju_j$，且 $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F$，可使用系数 $\alpha a_j+\beta b_j$ 计算

$$S(\alpha x+\beta y)=\sum_j(\alpha a_j+\beta b_j)w_j
=\alpha S(x)+\beta S(y).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Putting coefficient $1$ on $u_j$ and $0$ on the other inputs gives $S(u_j)=w_j$. Finally, any other linear map with these values must give the same output on every combination of the $u_j$. That proves uniqueness on $U$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给 $u_j$ 系数 $1$、其余输入系数 $0$，便得到 $S(u_j)=w_j$。最后，另一个具有这些指定值的线性映射，对任意 $u_j$ 的组合都必须给出相同输出，这就证明了在 $U$ 上的唯一性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Redundancy and incomplete coverage are different issues
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*冗余与覆盖不足是不同问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Every relation” is stronger than checking a convenient one. In $\mathbb R^2$ add $u_4=2u_1$ to the opening example. The outputs $2,-1,1,5$ preserve $u_3=u_1+u_2$, but fail $u_4=2u_1$, which requires output $4$. No linear map realizes all four values. Likewise a zero input must be assigned zero, and repeated inputs must be assigned the same output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“每条关系”比检查一条顺手的关系更强。在开头的 $\mathbb R^2$ 例子中再加 $u_4=2u_1$。输出 $2,-1,1,5$ 保持了 $u_3=u_1+u_2$，却违反 $u_4=2u_1$ 所要求的输出 $4$，因此没有线性映射能实现这四个值。同样，零输入必须配零输出，重复输入必须配相同输出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the inputs are independent, the only zero relation uses all-zero coefficients, so every choice of outputs is consistent. If they also span $V$, they are a [[基|basis]], and this becomes [[线性映射的基向量像|the basis-image extension theorem]]. More generally, even a dependent spanning list determines exactly one map on $V$ once all its relations are preserved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若输入无关，零关系只有全零系数这一种，所以任意选择输出都一致。若它们还张成 $V$，就构成[[基]]，此时得到[[线性映射的基向量像|基像延拓定理]]。更一般地，即使张成组相关，只要保持它的全部关系，也恰好确定 $V$ 上一个映射。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $U$ is smaller than $V$, uniqueness has so far been proved only on $U$. In finite-dimensional $V$, a consistent map on $U$ can be extended using a completed basis; when $W$ contains a nonzero vector, there is freedom outside $U$. See [[非张成集像不定全映射|the boundary created by a non-spanning input set]]. Consistency answers whether the given values can agree; spanning answers how much of the domain they determine.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $U$ 小于 $V$，目前只证明了 $U$ 上的唯一性。在有限维 $V$ 中，可以通过补基将 $U$ 上的一致映射延拓；当 $W$ 含非零向量时，$U$ 以外仍有自由。详见[[非张成集像不定全映射]]。一致性回答给定的值能否兼容，张成性回答它们确定了多少定义域。

> [!question]- Check a prescribed third image · 检查第三个指定像
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $u_1,u_2$ are independent and $u_3=2u_1-u_2$. If their prescribed images are $a,b,c\in W$, when is there a linear map on their span?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $u_1,u_2$ 无关，$u_3=2u_1-u_2$，指定像为 $a,b,c\in W$。何时存在它们张成空间上的线性映射？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Exactly when $c=2a-b$. The first two inputs form a basis of that span, so arbitrary $a,b$ determine a unique map there, and the third value is forced.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当且仅当 $c=2a-b$。前两个输入构成该张成空间的基，任意 $a,b$ 都唯一确定其上的映射，第三个值则被迫确定。

## Sources and verification

- [Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*, fourth edition, §2A, PDF pp.43,45–48, and §3A, pp.66–68](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=66): span, coefficient relations, linearity and the basis-image construction. The necessary-and-sufficient condition on a generating list is derived above by comparing two expressions for one input; it does not rely on coefficient uniqueness.

The conflicting-output examples are checked by their displayed relations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Axler，第 2A 节 PDF43、45–48 与第 3A 节 PDF66–68：核对张成、系数关系、线性性与基像构造。生成组上的充要条件已通过比较同一输入的两种表示完整推导，不依赖系数唯一。
- Axler 2.30–2.32，PDF54–55：核对从张成组抽取基、无关组补基，支持有限维延拓边界。
