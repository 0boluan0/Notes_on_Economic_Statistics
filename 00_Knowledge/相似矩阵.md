# Similar matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*相似矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two matrices can have different entries while describing the same linear action in different coordinates. Such matrices are called **similar**. A [[基|basis]] is an ordered set of vectors that gives each vector one list of coefficients; changing that basis changes the coefficient list. Similarity changes the coordinates of both the input and the output in the same way, while keeping the underlying action fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个矩阵的元素可以不同，却在不同坐标下描述同一个线性作用，这样的矩阵称为**相似矩阵**。[[基]]是一组有顺序的向量，使每个向量都有唯一的一份系数列表；换基改变的就是这份坐标列表。相似变换让输入与输出同步换坐标，而底层的线性作用保持不变。

> [!example] The same stretch in a tilted basis · 同一次伸缩，换一组斜着的基
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In ordinary coordinates, let $A$ double the first coordinate and triple the second. Use the new basis $m_1=(1,0)^T$, $m_2=(1,1)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Put those new basis vectors into the columns of $M$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在通常的坐标中，令 $A$ 把第一坐标乘以2，第二坐标乘以3。新基取 $m_1=(1,0)^T$、$m_2=(1,1)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。把新基向量按列放进 $M$：
>
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix},\qquad M=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad M^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A new-coordinate column $c=(1,2)^T$ represents $1m_1+2m_2=(3,2)^T$ in ordinary coordinates. Applying $A$ gives $(6,6)^T$. This output is $0m_1+6m_2$, so its new-coordinate column is $(0,6)^T$. The matrix taking $(1,2)^T$ to $(0,6)^T$ in this coordinate system is $B=M^{-1}AM$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 新坐标列 $c=(1,2)^T$ 表示 $1m_1+2m_2$，通常坐标为 $(3,2)^T$。施加 $A$ 得到 $(6,6)^T$；这个输出等于 $0m_1+6m_2$，所以新坐标列为 $(0,6)^T$。在这套坐标中，把 $(1,2)^T$ 送到 $(0,6)^T$ 的矩阵就是 $B=M^{-1}AM$。

## Read the three operations from right to left
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从右向左读三个操作*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[换基矩阵的方向|direction of a change-of-basis matrix]] comes from its columns. If $M$ contains the new basis vectors expressed in the old basis, then $x=Mc$ turns new coordinates $c$ into old coordinates $x$. Multiplication by $A$ gives the output in old coordinates. Finally, the [[逆矩阵|inverse matrix]] $M^{-1}$ recovers its new coordinates:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[换基矩阵的方向]]由各列的含义决定。如果 $M$ 的列是新基向量在旧基下的坐标，$x=Mc$ 就把新坐标 $c$ 变成旧坐标 $x$。再乘 $A$，得到旧坐标中的输出；最后由[[逆矩阵]] $M^{-1}$ 求回输出的新坐标：

$$c\ \xrightarrow{\ M\ }\ x\ \xrightarrow{\ A\ }\ Ax\ \xrightarrow{\ M^{-1}\ }\ M^{-1}AMc=Bc.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the example, direct [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] gives $B=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$. Its off-diagonal entry does not represent a new physical effect: it comes from using a tilted basis. The identity $AM=MB$ provides another check. The $j$th column says that applying $A$ to the new basis vector $m_j$ gives the combination of new basis vectors recorded in column $j$ of $B$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例子中直接做[[矩阵乘法]]，得到 $B=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$。非对角元不表示多出了一种实际作用，而是使用斜基产生的坐标效果。还可以用 $AM=MB$ 核对：第 $j$ 列说明，对新基向量 $m_j$ 施加 $A$ 后，所得向量在新基中的组合系数，正是 $B$ 的第 $j$ 列。

> [!important] Similarity over one field · 在同一标量域上相似
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A,B$ be $n\times n$ matrices over a fixed scalar field $\mathbb F$, with integer $n\ge1$. They are similar over $\mathbb F$ if there is an invertible $n\times n$ matrix $M$ with entries in that same field such that
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $A,B$ 是固定标量域 $\mathbb F$ 上的 $n\times n$ 方阵，整数 $n\ge1$。如果存在同一域上的可逆 $n\times n$ 矩阵 $M$，满足
>
> $$B=M^{-1}AM,$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> then $A$ and $B$ are similar. In the usual applications $\mathbb F$ is $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. Neither $A$ nor $B$ needs to be invertible; it is the coordinate conversion $M$ that must be invertible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 就称 $A,B$ 相似。通常应用中 $\mathbb F$ 为 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。$A$、$B$ 本身不必可逆；必须可逆的是坐标转换矩阵 $M$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even reordering the basis is a similarity. With $P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, we have $P^{-1}=P$ and $P^{-1}AP=\begin{pmatrix}3&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$. This is the original stretch with the two coordinate directions listed in reverse order. More generally, the columns of $M$ need not be perpendicular or have unit length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只把基的顺序调换，也是一种相似变换。取 $P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$，则 $P^{-1}=P$，且 $P^{-1}AP=\begin{pmatrix}3&0\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$。这仍是原来的伸缩，只把两条坐标方向倒序列出。一般的 $M$ 也不要求列向量互相垂直或长度为一。

## Distinguish the objects being transformed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*分清正在变换的对象*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[行等价|Row operations]] usually multiply a matrix only from the left. They serve a different purpose and need not preserve the underlying operator. For a [[一般线性映射的换基|general linear map]] whose input and output use different new bases, the formula is $Q^{-1}AP$; it becomes a similarity when both sides use the same conversion, $P=Q=M$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[行等价|行操作]]通常只从左侧乘矩阵，服务于另一种目的，不保证保留原来的线性算子。对[[一般线性映射的换基]]，如果输入与输出分别采用不同新基，公式是 $Q^{-1}AP$；两侧采用同一个转换 $P=Q=M$ 时，就得到这里的相似换基公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Congruence concerns a scalar-valued form instead. Substituting $x=Mc$ into $x^TAx$ gives $c^T(M^TAM)c$, while the complex expression $x^*Ax$ gives $c^*(M^*AM)c$. Here $*$ denotes [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]. These use a transpose or conjugate transpose on the left, rather than an inverse. The formulas can coincide for special $M$, but congruence and similarity are different relations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

合同变换处理的则是标量值的型。把 $x=Mc$ 代入 $x^TAx$，得到 $c^T(M^TAM)c$；复数表达式 $x^*Ax$ 则变成 $c^*(M^*AM)c$，星号表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]。左边使用转置或共轭转置，而非逆矩阵；特殊 $M$ 下公式可以重合，但合同与相似是不同的关系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Similarity preserves the [[相似保持特征多项式|characteristic polynomial]] and [[相似保持特征空间维数|eigenspace dimensions]]. Those are consequences with their own proofs. Conversely, matching a few spectral quantities does not establish similarity; see [[同谱不必相似|the same spectrum need not imply similarity]]. To check the coordinate direction yourself, ask why $AM$ alone is incomplete: it still reports the output in the old coordinates, so the final $M^{-1}$ is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相似保留[[相似保持特征多项式|特征多项式]]与[[相似保持特征空间维数|特征空间维数]]，两者各有独立证明。反过来，若干谱量相同并不能确立相似，见[[同谱不必相似]]。可以用一个问题核对方向：为什么只算 $AM$ 还不完整？因为这时输出仍在旧坐标中，尚需最后的 $M^{-1}$。
