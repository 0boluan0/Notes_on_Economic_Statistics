# 有限一次性贝叶斯博弈中，混合策略与行为策略实现相同结果分布，但二者不一一对应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a finite one-shot Bayesian game, mixed and behavioral strategies induce the same outcome distributions, but the correspondence is not one-to-one*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在有限一次性贝叶斯博弈中，每名玩家最终只实现一个自身类型。于是，完整计划中各个反事实类型的行动怎样相关，不会在同一局中被共同观察；结果只取决于各类型下的行动边际分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a finite one-shot Bayesian game, only one type of each player is realized. Correlation among prescriptions for counterfactual types is therefore never jointly observed in a single play; outcomes depend only on the action marginal attached to each type.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从混合策略得到行为策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From a Mixed Strategy to a Behavioral Strategy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\mu_i\in\Delta(S_i)$ 且 $S_i=A_i^{T_i}$，它诱导的逐类型行动分布为

$$
\beta_i(a_i\mid t_i)
=
\sum_{s_i:s_i(t_i)=a_i}\mu_i(s_i).
$$

这一步只保留每个类型坐标的边际分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A mixed strategy $\mu_i\in\Delta(S_i)$ induces the displayed type-by-type action marginals. This operation retains only the marginal distribution at each type coordinate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从行为策略构造一种混合策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Constructing One Mixed Strategy from a Behavioral Strategy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，任意行为策略 $\beta_i:T_i\to\Delta(A_i)$ 都可由下列产品分布实现：

$$
\mu_i^{\beta}(s_i)
=
\prod_{t_i\in T_i}\beta_i\bigl(s_i(t_i)\mid t_i\bigr).
$$

因此两种口径诱导相同的 $(\theta,t,a)$ 分布与期望收益。但这个产品分布只是一个实现：不同 $\mu_i$ 可以具有同一组逐类型边际，所以对应关系并不一一。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, the displayed product distribution implements any behavioral strategy. The two formulations can therefore induce the same distribution over $(\theta,t,a)$ and the same expected payoffs. The product distribution is only one implementation, however: different $\mu_i$ can share the same type-by-type marginals, so the correspondence is not one-to-one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 适用边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary of the Result*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的论证依赖“一次只实现一个自身类型”。若同一玩家可能在多个相关决策点行动，或外部观察者能看到跨决策点的共同随机化，就不能只靠逐类型边际断言结果等价；扩展式博弈还要检查 perfect recall 等条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The argument relies on only one own type being realized. If the same player acts at several related decision points, or if common randomization across decisions is observable, type-by-type marginals alone do not establish outcome equivalence; an extensive-form analysis must also check conditions such as perfect recall.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
