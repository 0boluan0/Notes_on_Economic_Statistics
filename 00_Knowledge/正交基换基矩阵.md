# Changing between orthonormal bases
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*标准正交基之间的换基*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **orthonormal basis** describes vectors using perpendicular unit directions. If both the old and new bases have this property under the same inner product, changing coordinates preserves the usual lengths and inner products of the coordinate columns. The conversion matrix is therefore orthogonal over the reals and unitary over the complexes, so its inverse is especially easy to obtain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**标准正交基**用彼此垂直的单位方向描述向量。如果旧基、新基在同一个内积下都满足这项条件，换坐标就会保持坐标列的通常长度和内积。因此转换矩阵在实数域中正交，在复数域中酉，求逆尤其简单。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The word “unit” matters. The perpendicular plane vectors $(1,1)$ and $(1,-1)$ have length $\sqrt2$. Dividing both by $\sqrt2$ gives a new orthonormal basis. Relative to the standard old basis, its coordinate-conversion matrix is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“单位”不可省略。平面向量 $(1,1)$ 和 $(1,-1)$ 虽然垂直，长度却是 $\sqrt2$；各除以 $\sqrt2$，才成为一组新的标准正交基。相对于标准旧基，坐标转换矩阵为

$$Q=\frac1{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad Q^TQ=I_2,\qquad Q^{-1}=Q^T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## State the two bases and the inner product
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*说明两组基与内积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional real or complex [[内积|inner-product space]], with dimension $n\ge1$. An inner product measures lengths and perpendicularity. Suppose $\mathcal B=(e_1,\ldots,e_n)$ and $\mathcal B'=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ are both [[标准正交组|orthonormal]] [[基|bases]] of $V$ for that same inner product. Each vector has length one, and distinct vectors within each basis have inner product zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $V$ 是维数 $n\ge1$ 的有限维实或复[[内积|内积空间]]。内积测量长度和垂直关系。假设 $\mathcal B=(e_1,\ldots,e_n)$、$\mathcal B'=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ 在这个同一内积下都是[[标准正交组|标准正交]][[基]]：每个向量长度为一，同一组中不同向量内积为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Form $P$ by putting the old-basis coordinate column of $v_j$ in column $j$. The [[换基矩阵的方向|direction of conversion]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $v_j$ 在旧基下的坐标列放入 $P$ 的第 $j$ 列，[[换基矩阵的方向|转换方向]]为

$$P=\bigl(\,[v_1]_{\mathcal B}\ \cdots\ [v_n]_{\mathcal B}\,\bigr),
\qquad [x]_{\mathcal B}=P[x]_{\mathcal B'}.$$

> [!important] Invert by transposing, with conjugation if needed · 用转置求逆，复数时加共轭
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Over $\mathbb R$, $P$ is an [[正交矩阵|orthogonal matrix]], so $P^{-1}=P^T$. Over $\mathbb C$, it is a [[Unitary 矩阵|unitary matrix]], so $P^{-1}=P^*$. The star means [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]: transpose and conjugate every entry. In both cases the matrix is square because both bases describe the whole same space.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\mathbb R$ 上，$P$ 是[[正交矩阵]]，所以 $P^{-1}=P^T$；在 $\mathbb C$ 上，$P$ 是[[Unitary 矩阵|酉矩阵]]，所以 $P^{-1}=P^*$。星号表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]，即转置并逐项取共轭。两种情形中矩阵都是方阵，因为两组基描述同一个完整空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why both bases give perpendicular unit columns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么两组基保证坐标列也标准正交*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the convention that the inner product is conjugate-linear in its first input. Thus the standard complex coordinate product is $a^*b=\sum_k\overline{a_k}b_k$. When $u=\sum_k a_ke_k$ and $v=\sum_k b_ke_k$, the old basis being orthonormal gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里采用内积对第一个自变量共轭线性的约定，标准复坐标内积为 $a^*b=\sum_k\overline{a_k}b_k$。若 $u=\sum_k a_ke_k$、$v=\sum_k b_ke_k$，旧基的标准正交性给出

$$\langle u,v\rangle
=\sum_{k,\ell}\overline{a_k}b_\ell\langle e_k,e_\ell\rangle
=\sum_k\overline{a_k}b_k=a^*b.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All cross terms vanish, and each diagonal term has factor one. Consequently taking old coordinates preserves inner products. Applying this to columns $i,j$ of $P$, and now using the new basis being orthonormal, yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交叉项全为零，对角项的内积因子均为一，所以取旧坐标保持内积。把这一结果用于 $P$ 的第 $i,j$ 列，再用新基的标准正交性，就得到

$$(P^*P)_{ij}
=[v_i]_{\mathcal B}^*[v_j]_{\mathcal B}
=\langle v_i,v_j\rangle=\delta_{ij}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\delta_{ij}$ is one if $i=j$ and zero otherwise. Thus $P^*P=I_n$, where $I_n$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. Since $P$ is already an invertible change-of-coordinate matrix, multiplication by $P^{-1}$ gives $P^*=P^{-1}$ and hence $PP^*=I_n$. For real entries conjugation does nothing, leaving the transpose formulas. This proof shows exactly where each basis assumption is used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\delta_{ij}$ 表示 $i=j$ 时为一，否则为零。因此 $P^*P=I_n$，其中 $I_n$ 是[[单位矩阵]]。$P$ 已是可逆的坐标转换矩阵，右乘 $P^{-1}$ 就得到 $P^*=P^{-1}$，进而 $PP^*=I_n$。实数元素不受共轭影响，就得到普通转置公式。这个证明明确指出两组基的条件分别用在哪里。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A complex coordinate needs conjugation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*复坐标为什么需要共轭*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $\mathbb C^2$ with the standard inner product, take the standard old basis $e_1=(1,0)^T,e_2=(0,1)^T$ and new basis $ie_1,e_2$, where $i^2=-1$. Multiplication by $i$ does not change length, so both bases are orthonormal. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在具有标准内积的 $\mathbb C^2$ 中，取标准旧基 $e_1=(1,0)^T,e_2=(0,1)^T$，新基 $ie_1,e_2$，其中 $i^2=-1$。乘 $i$ 不改变长度，所以两组基都标准正交。此时

$$P=\begin{pmatrix}i&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
P^{-1}=P^*=\begin{pmatrix}-i&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
P^T=P\ne P^{-1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The vector $e_1$ has new coordinates $(-i,0)^T$, because $(-i)(ie_1)=e_1$. Using the plain transpose would return $(i,0)^T$, which reconstructs $-e_1$ instead. The conjugation changes the actual answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

向量 $e_1$ 的新坐标为 $(-i,0)^T$，因为 $(-i)(ie_1)=e_1$。若误用普通转置，会得到 $(i,0)^T$，还原的是 $-e_1$。这里共轭直接影响计算答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the conclusion does not establish
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*结论的边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Having only the new basis orthonormal is insufficient. In ordinary $\mathbb R^2$, take old basis $\mathcal B=(2e_1,e_2)$ and new basis $\mathcal B'=(e_1,e_2)$. The coordinate columns give the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $P=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1)$, where $\operatorname{diag}$ puts the listed numbers on the diagonal and zeros elsewhere. Thus $P^TP=\operatorname{diag}(1/4,1)\ne I$, even though the new vectors are orthonormal. Old coordinates do not measure lengths by the ordinary dot product in this example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有新基标准正交还不够。在通常的 $\mathbb R^2$ 中，取旧基 $\mathcal B=(2e_1,e_2)$、新基 $\mathcal B'=(e_1,e_2)$，坐标列给出[[对角矩阵]] $P=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1)$；$\operatorname{diag}$ 把列出的数放在主对角线上，其余为零。于是 $P^TP=\operatorname{diag}(1/4,1)\ne I$，尽管新基确实标准正交。本例的旧坐标不能直接通过普通点积测量原向量长度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, an orthogonal or unitary conversion matrix does not by itself prove that either basis is orthonormal. Compare the nonorthogonal basis $((1,0),(1,1))$ with itself: the conversion is $I_2$, yet the two basis vectors have inner product one. The theorem assumes the bases' geometry and concludes a property of their coordinate matrix; reversing that inference without further assumptions is invalid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，转换矩阵正交或酉，也不能单独证明任一组基标准正交。把非正交基 $((1,0),(1,1))$ 与它自身比较，转换矩阵就是 $I_2$，但两个基向量内积为一。本定理从两组基的几何条件推出坐标矩阵的性质，不能无条件把推理倒过来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inner product must also stay fixed: being orthonormal under two different length rules is not the stated hypothesis. For a general basis change, keep $P^{-1}$ until orthogonality or unitarity has actually been established. The [[标准正交基投影公式|orthonormal coordinate formula]] explains the related shortcut of obtaining coefficients directly from inner products.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内积也必须保持同一个：分别在两套不同长度规则下标准正交，不满足本定理的前提。一般换基要保留 $P^{-1}$，直到确实证明正交性或酉性。[[标准正交基投影公式]]解释了相关的快捷计算：直接由内积取得坐标系数。

## Sources and verification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*, fourth edition, 6.27–6.30, PDF pp.213–214](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=213): orthonormal bases and coordinate preservation of inner products. Axler uses the opposite slot convention; the formulas here consistently use $a^*b$.
- [Axler, 7.56–7.57, PDF p.277](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=277): the matrix of an isometry between orthonormal bases and the equivalent unitary identities. Applied to the identity map, this gives the change-of-coordinate theorem.
- [[01_Math/02_linear algebra/MIT_OCW_18.06SC_PDF/06_Lecture_Transcripts/U3_S07_Lecture_Lecture_31_Change_of_Basis_Image_Compression.pdf#page=13|MIT Lecture 31, pp.13–14]]: orthogonality versus normalization and the real transpose-inverse shortcut. The real, complex and nonorthonormal boundary examples are checked directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Axler 6.27–6.30，PDF213–214：核对标准正交基与坐标内积保持。Axler 对内积的线性自变量采用相反约定；本卡始终使用 $a^*b$。
- Axler 7.56–7.57，PDF277：核对等距映射在标准正交基下的矩阵，以及酉矩阵的等价恒等式；用于恒等映射就得到换坐标定理。
- MIT Lecture 31 第13–14页：核对正交与归一化的区别，以及实数域中逆等于转置的条件。实、复与非标准正交边界例子均直接核算。
