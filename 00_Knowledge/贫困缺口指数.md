# 贫困缺口指数是全体人口的标准化贫困短缺的平均值

贫困缺口指数 $PG$，也记为 $P_1$，先计算每个人低于[[贫困线]]的距离占该线的比例，再对**全体人口**取平均。非贫困者的缺口为零，但仍进入分母，因此它不是只对贫困者平均。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The poverty gap index $PG$, also written $P_1$, measures each person's shortfall below the [[贫困线|poverty line]] as a fraction of that line, then averages over the **whole population**. Nonpoor people have zero shortfalls but remain in the denominator; this is not an average restricted to poor people.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同价、同期间、等权个人的设定下，取 $z>0$，令 $g_i=\max\{z-y_i,0\}/z$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For equally weighted people measured at matching prices and periods, take $z>0$ and define $g_i=\max\{z-y_i,0\}/z$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
PG=P_1=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n g_i.
$$

$z=100$，收入为 40、90、120、150 时，标准化缺口是 $0.6,0.1,0,0$，所以 $PG=0.7/4=0.175$。若前两人都收入 90，则 $PG=0.2/4=0.05$，尽管[[贫困人口比例]]一直是 0.5。$0.175$ 表示全体人口平均短缺为贫困线的 17.5%，并非贫困者平均只差 17.5%。后者见[[贫困者平均缺口]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $z=100$ and incomes of 40, 90, 120, and 150, normalized gaps are $0.6,0.1,0,0$, so $PG=0.7/4=0.175$. If the first two incomes both become 90, $PG=0.2/4=0.05$, although the [[贫困人口比例|headcount ratio]] stays at 0.5. A value of 0.175 means the population-average shortfall is 17.5% of the line, not that poor people alone fall short by 17.5%. See the [[贫困者平均缺口|average gap among poor people]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若有个人权重 $w_i$，改为 $PG_w=\sum_iw_i g_i/\sum_iw_i$。在福利非负时，$0\le PG\le1$；若原始收入含负数，必须交代数据处理，不能不核条件就套这个范围。该指标加总缺口，但不会区分相同总缺口怎样分布在仍然贫困的人之间；这正是[[平方贫困缺口]]要补充的信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With population weights $w_i$, use $PG_w=\sum_iw_i g_i/\sum_iw_i$. Nonnegative welfare gives $0\le PG\le1$; negative raw incomes require an explicit treatment before that bound is invoked. The index totals shortfalls but does not distinguish how a fixed total is distributed among people who remain poor. The [[平方贫困缺口|squared poverty gap]] captures that additional information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

PIP supports the population denominator and zero shortfalls above the line. Equations 2.37–2.39 distinguish the overall index from the conditional gap. The examples were recalculated from those definitions.
