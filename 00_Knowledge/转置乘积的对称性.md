# Symmetry of transpose products
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵与自身转置相乘的对称性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A real matrix need not be square or symmetric to produce a [[实对称矩阵|symmetric matrix]]: multiply it by its own [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Either order produces equal entries in reflected positions, although the two results may have different sizes. Start with the row $R=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\end{pmatrix}$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个实矩阵即使不是方阵、也不对称，仍能产生[[实对称矩阵]]：把它与自身的[[矩阵转置|转置]]相乘即可。两个次序都会使镜像位置的元素相等，但所得矩阵可能尺寸不同。先看行矩阵 $R=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\end{pmatrix}$：

$$
R^T=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
R^TR=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&2\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
RR^T=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix}=(5).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first result is $2\times2$, with matching off-diagonal entries $2$. The second is $1\times1$, which is automatically symmetric. Both products are defined by [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]], but they are different matrices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前一个结果为 $2\times2$，两个非对角元都等于 $2$；后一个为 $1\times1$，自然满足对称。两个[[矩阵乘法|乘积]]都有定义，却是不同的矩阵。

> [!important] Both orders are symmetric · 两个次序都对称
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a real matrix $R$ with $m$ rows and $n$ columns,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对具有 $m$ 行、$n$ 列的实矩阵 $R$，
>
> $$R^TR\in\mathbb R^{n\times n},\qquad RR^T\in\mathbb R^{m\times m},$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> and each equals its own transpose. Thus both are real symmetric matrices. The positive integers $m,n$ need not be equal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 而且二者分别等于各自的转置，因此都是实对称矩阵。正整数 $m,n$ 不必相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The product rule returns the same matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*乘积转置规则会把它送回自身*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[乘积的转置|transpose-of-a-product rule]] reverses the two factors. Transposing $R$ twice returns $R$, so for the first product,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[乘积的转置|乘积转置规则]]把两个因子反序，而 $R$ 转置两次后回到 $R$。因此对第一个乘积，

$$
(R^TR)^T=R^T(R^T)^T=R^TR.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the other order, the same steps give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对另一个次序，同样得到

$$
(RR^T)^T=(R^T)^TR^T=RR^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each equality establishes symmetry of one product. Neither equation says that $R^TR=RR^T$. The opening example already shows why that would be a different and generally false claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个等式分别证明一个乘积对称；没有任何一步声称 $R^TR=RR^T$。开头的例子已经说明，后者是另一条通常不成立的断言。

> [!warning] Symmetry does not guarantee an inverse · 对称不保证可逆
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the example, $R^TR=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}$ sends the nonzero input $(-2,1)^T$ to zero. It cannot have an [[逆矩阵|inverse]] that recovers every input: zero and $(-2,1)^T$ already have the same output. Thus a transpose product can be singular, even though it is symmetric.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上例的 $R^TR=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}$ 会把非零输入 $(-2,1)^T$ 送到零。因此它不可能有能恢复每个输入的[[逆矩阵]]，因为零和 $(-2,1)^T$ 已经产生同一输出。所以转置乘积虽然对称，仍可能是奇异矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The statement here concerns real entries and ordinary transpose. In complex inner-product calculations, conjugate transpose is a different operation; the distinction is explained in [[矩阵转置|the transpose definition]] and used by [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian matrices]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡的结论讨论实数元素与普通转置。复数内积计算所用的共轭转置是另一个操作，其区别见[[矩阵转置]]，[[Hermitian 矩阵]]会使用它。
