# Products of generating functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*生成函数乘积与卷积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying two ordinary generating functions combines two components whose sizes add. The coefficient for a total size must collect **all ways to split that size between the two components**. The resulting sum is called the convolution of their coefficient sequences.

For a small example, put identical tokens into two labelled boxes. Box A holds at most two, and box B at most one. As [[普通生成函数|ordinary generating functions]], their allowed counts are $A(x)=1+x+x^2$ and $B(x)=1+x$. To place two tokens in total, the possible count pairs are $(1,1)$ and $(2,0)$. Multiplication records these two possibilities:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
两个普通生成函数相乘，可以组合规模相加的两个部件。总规模对应的系数，要累计**把总规模分给两个部件的所有可能方式**。得到的和称为两个系数数列的卷积。

先看一个小例子：把相同筹码放入两个有标号的盒子，A 最多装两个，B 最多装一个。用[[普通生成函数]]表示允许的数量，得到 $A(x)=1+x+x^2$、$B(x)=1+x$。若总共放两个，数量对只有 $(1,1)$ 和 $(2,0)$。乘法记录了这两种可能：

$$
(1+x+x^2)(1+x)=1+2x+2x^2+x^3.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $2x^2$ comes from $x\cdot x$ and $x^2\cdot1$. A hypothetical $(0,2)$ contributes nothing, because box B cannot hold two tokens: the coefficient of $x^2$ in $B$ is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
其中 $2x^2$ 来自 $x\cdot x$ 与 $x^2\cdot1$。假设的 $(0,2)$ 不贡献任何数量，因为 B 盒装不下两个；$B$ 中 $x^2$ 的系数为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Collect terms with the same total degree
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*收集总次数相同的项*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $A(x)=\sum_{k\ge0}a_kx^k$ and $B(x)=\sum_{j\ge0}b_jx^j$, with real or complex coefficients. The index $k$ records the first component's size, and $j$ the second's. Multiplying one term from each series gives $a_kb_jx^{k+j}$. To contribute to degree $n$, the indices must satisfy $k+j=n$, or $j=n-k$.

There are exactly $n+1$ possible index pairs, from $(0,n)$ through $(n,0)$. This yields the coefficient identity, where $[x^n]$ means “coefficient of $x^n$”:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
写成 $A(x)=\sum_{k\ge0}a_kx^k$、$B(x)=\sum_{j\ge0}b_jx^j$，系数为实数或复数。索引 $k$ 记录第一部件的规模，$j$ 记录第二部件的规模。从两个级数各取一项相乘，得到 $a_kb_jx^{k+j}$。要贡献到次数 $n$，必须有 $k+j=n$，即 $j=n-k$。

这样的索引对恰好有 $n+1$ 个，从 $(0,n)$ 一直到 $(n,0)$。于是得到下面的系数恒等式，其中 $[x^n]$ 表示“$x^n$ 的系数”：

> [!important] Coefficient convolution / 系数卷积
> $$
> \boxed{c_n=[x^n](A(x)B(x))=\sum_{k=0}^{n}a_kb_{n-k}},\qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Equivalently, $A(x)B(x)=\sum_{n\ge0}c_nx^n$. At degree zero, $c_0=a_0b_0$. At degree one, $c_1=a_0b_1+a_1b_0$. Multiplication therefore does not mean multiplying only the matching-index coefficients $a_nb_n$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 等价地，$A(x)B(x)=\sum_{n\ge0}c_nx^n$。零次项为 $c_0=a_0b_0$，一次项为 $c_1=a_0b_1+a_1b_0$。所以相乘不是只把相同索引的两个系数 $a_nb_n$ 相乘。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even for infinite formal series, each fixed $c_n$ uses only those finitely many pairs, so this identity needs no numerical convergence assumption. If the series are instead evaluated as numerical functions, [[幂级数乘法|multiplying convergent power series]] requires the corresponding convergence conditions.

For example, if $a_0=1,a_1=2,b_0=3,b_1=4$, then $c_1=1\cdot4+2\cdot3=10$. Later coefficients of either series cannot affect this degree-one coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
即使两个形式级数都是无限的，每个固定 $c_n$ 也只涉及这些有限的配对，因此恒等式不要求数值收敛。若把级数改作数值函数求值，就要满足[[幂级数乘法]]相应的收敛条件。

例如，若 $a_0=1,a_1=2,b_0=3,b_1=4$，就有 $c_1=1\cdot4+2\cdot3=10$。两个级数后面的系数，都不可能影响这个一次项系数。

## When the coefficient counts the final objects
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*什么时候系数就是最终对象数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $a_k$ and $b_j$ are finite counts of the two types of component. If every first component may be paired with every second component of the required sizes, the [[乘法法则|product rule]] gives $a_kb_{n-k}$ pairs for a fixed split. Different splits are disjoint, so the [[加法法则|sum rule]] adds them. Thus $c_n$ counts **ordered component pairs** of total size $n$.

To interpret it as a count of completed objects, each completed object must correspond to exactly one such pair, and each pair must be a valid construction. Labels, an observable dividing point, or another reversible decomposition can supply this [[双射计数|one-to-one correspondence]]. Compatibility restrictions must also be respected; a product cannot automatically count only the allowed pairs if some combinations are forbidden.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $a_k$、$b_j$ 分别是两类部件的有限计数。若符合规模的每个第一部件都能与每个第二部件配对，固定拆分时，[[乘法法则]]给出 $a_kb_{n-k}$ 对；不同规模拆分互不重叠，再用[[加法法则]]相加。因此 $c_n$ 数的是总规模为 $n$ 的**有序部件对**。

要把它解释为完成品数量，还必须保证：每个完成品恰好对应一个这样的部件对，而且每对部件都能合法构造一个完成品。标签、可见的分界点，或者其他可逆分解，都可以提供这种[[双射计数|一一对应]]。部件之间的兼容限制也不能忽略；若某些组合被禁止，直接相乘不会自动排除它们。

> [!warning] Forgetting a split can merge representations / 忘掉分界会合并不同表示
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If both components are words and we concatenate them while discarding the dividing point, the completed word `AB` can come from `(empty, AB)`, `(A, B)`, or `(AB, empty)`. The product counts all three component pairs. It is not a count of distinct completed words unless that dividing point is part of the recorded object, or another condition makes the split unique.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 若两个部件都是字符串，拼接后却不保留分界，完成品 `AB` 可以来自“空串与 AB”、“A 与 B”或“AB 与空串”。乘积把这三个部件对都数进去了。除非分界本身也是对象记录的一部分，或另有条件保证分解唯一，否则这不是不同完成字符串的数量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two labelled boxes with no capacity limits, each has generating function $1/(1-x)$. A total of $n$ identical tokens has the $n+1$ distinct allocations $(0,n),(1,n-1),\ldots,(n,0)$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对于两个没有容量上限的有标号盒子，每盒的生成函数都是 $1/(1-x)$。总共 $n$ 个相同筹码有 $(0,n),(1,n-1),\ldots,(n,0)$ 这 $n+1$ 种不同分配，因此

$$
\frac1{1-x}\frac1{1-x}
=\frac1{(1-x)^2}
=\sum_{n\ge0}(n+1)x^n.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $n=0$ this includes the one pair of empty boxes. This is also the two-box case of [[隔板法|stars and bars]]. These are counts, not probabilities; no random experiment or independence assumption has been introduced.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$n=0$ 时也包括两个盒子都空着的唯一分配。这同时是[[隔板法]]的两个盒子情形。这里得到的是计数，而不是概率；尚未引入随机试验，也没有概率独立性的假设。
