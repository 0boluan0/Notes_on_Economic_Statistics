# 借款人净值和可抵押资产通过外部融资溢价使金融摩擦放大并延长实体冲击
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Borrower net worth and pledgeable assets amplify and prolong real shocks through the external finance premium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子机制
> 金融加速器不是危机的初始冲击，而是冲击经过借款人资产负债表后被放大和延长的反馈。收入、现金流或资产价格下降会削弱借款人净值与可抵押资产，提高 [[外部融资溢价]] 或收紧非价格融资条款；投资和支出随之下降，又进一步压低现金流、资产价值和下一期净值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The financial accelerator is not the initiating shock. It is the feedback through which a shock is amplified and prolonged by borrower balance sheets. Lower income, cash flow, or asset prices weaken borrower net worth and pledgeable assets, raising the [[外部融资溢价|external finance premium]] or tightening non-price financing terms. Lower investment and spending then depress cash flow, asset values, and next-period net worth further.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

核心链条是：

$$
\text{冲击}
\rightarrow \text{借款人净值或可抵押资产下降}
\rightarrow \text{代理与回收风险上升}
\rightarrow \text{外部融资溢价上升或信贷条款收紧}
\rightarrow \text{投资与支出下降}
\rightarrow \text{现金流、资产价值与净值进一步下降}.
$$

净值在这里不仅是会计结果，也是契约中的激励与损失缓冲。借款人自己的投入越少，贷方越担心隐藏行动、核验成本和违约后的回收；抵押品越少，承诺可执行的融资也越少。贷方可能提高有效融资成本、要求更多抵押或限制额度，因而使本来短暂的冲击产生更大、更持久的实体影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net worth is not merely an accounting outcome here; it is also an incentive stake and loss buffer in the contract. With less of the borrower's own wealth at risk, lenders worry more about hidden action, verification costs, and recovery after default. With less pledgeable collateral, less finance can be credibly promised. Lenders may raise effective financing costs, demand more collateral, or restrict quantities, allowing a short-lived shock to produce larger and more persistent real effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 数值锚点：抵押约束怎样放大价格下跌
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Numerical anchor: how a collateral constraint amplifies a price decline*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一家企业持有市值 150 的抵押资产、债务 100，净值为 50。贷款人规定债务不得超过抵押品价值的 80%。资产价格下跌 20% 后，抵押品价值降到 120，可支持债务只有 96。若企业能用**不减少这批抵押品**的内部现金或新增权益还债，偿还 4 即可恢复约束；若它只能出售这批抵押品并把所得全部还债，出售会同时减少债务与抵押品，所需出售额 $s$ 必须满足 $100-s\leq0.8(120-s)$，即至少为 20。削减投资以保留现金会直接压低实体支出；集中出售还可能继续压低市场价格，使其他受同一约束的借款人也被迫调整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A firm holds collateral worth 150 and debt of 100, so net worth is 50. The lender limits debt to 80% of collateral value. After a 20% price fall, collateral is worth 120 and supports only 96 of debt. If the firm can repay with internal cash or new equity that does **not reduce this collateral pool**, repayment of 4 restores compliance. If it can only sell this collateral and apply all proceeds to debt, both debt and collateral shrink: the sale amount $s$ must satisfy $100-s\leq0.8(120-s)$, so at least 20 must be sold. Cutting investment to preserve cash directly reduces real spending; concentrated sales can also depress the market price and force adjustment by other constrained borrowers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子说明一种抵押约束，不是说所有金融加速器都必须出现火售，也不是说 80% 是普遍阈值。分析时必须指出实际绑定的契约条件，例如贷款成数、净值要求、现金流约束、契约条款或融资额度；资产价格、信贷和投资同向变化本身还不是机制证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example illustrates one collateral constraint; it does not say that every financial accelerator must contain a fire sale or that 80% is a universal threshold. An analysis must identify the binding contractual condition—such as a loan-to-value limit, net-worth requirement, cash-flow constraint, covenant, or credit quantity. Co-movement among asset prices, credit, and investment alone is not evidence of the mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与银行资本—火售渠道的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary with the bank-capital and fire-sale channel*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本原子隔离的是**最终借款人**（企业或家庭）的净值、抵押品与外部融资条件。[[火售流动性偿付反馈]] 和 [[挤兑火售反馈]] 隔离的是银行或其他中介遭撤资、资本损失、保证金与被迫出售时的资产负债表反馈。两条链可能相互作用：银行火售可压低借款人抵押品，借款人违约又可侵蚀银行资本；但不能把“银行资本下降—火售”和“借款人净值下降—外部融资溢价上升”写成同一条未经区分的机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This atom isolates the net worth, collateral, and financing conditions of the **ultimate borrower**—a firm or household. [[火售流动性偿付反馈|Fire-sale liquidity-solvency feedback]] and [[挤兑火售反馈|run and fire-sale feedback]] isolate intermediary balance-sheet mechanisms involving withdrawals, capital losses, margin constraints, and forced sales. The channels can interact: bank fire sales can lower borrower collateral values, and borrower defaults can erode bank capital. They must not, however, be collapsed into one undifferentiated mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若叙述转向中介链，还要另行指出资本要求、保证金、到期的短期融资、可赎回负债或投资者风险限额中哪一项真正绑定，不能把这些约束统称为外部融资溢价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the account turns to an intermediary channel, it must separately identify which capital requirement, margin constraint, maturing short-term funding claim, redeemable liability, or investor risk limit actually binds. These constraints cannot all be renamed the external finance premium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么“资产价格下降后银行减少放贷”还不足以证明借款人金融加速器？
>
> **答案：** 还要确认借款人的净值或抵押品下降怎样提高外部融资溢价或收紧契约，并进一步压低投资或支出。银行也可能仅因自身资本或融资受损而缩贷，那是另一条中介资产负债表渠道。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why is “banks lend less after asset prices fall” insufficient to establish the borrower financial accelerator?
>
> **Answer:** One must show how weaker borrower net worth or collateral raises the external finance premium or tightens the contract and then reduces investment or spending. Banks may instead cut lending because their own capital or funding is impaired, which is a distinct intermediary balance-sheet channel.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **原始机制模型：** Bernanke, B. S., Gertler, M., and Gilchrist, S.（1999），《[The Financial Accelerator in a Quantitative Business Cycle Framework](https://www.nber.org/papers/w6455)》：核对金融市场内生变化放大并传播宏观冲击，以及借款人融资条件在模型中的位置。
- **原始经验延伸：** Bernanke, B. S., Gertler, M., and Gilchrist, S.（1994），《[The Financial Accelerator and the Flight to Quality](https://www.nber.org/papers/w4789)》：核对金融加速器来自贷款代理成本的周期性变化，并非“所有信贷收缩”的同义词。
- **后续权威解释：** Bernanke, B. S.（2007），《[The Financial Accelerator and the Credit Channel](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/bernanke20070615a.htm)》：核对借款人净值、流动性和现金流与外部融资溢价的反向关系，以及该关系如何提高冲击的持续性和幅度。
- **课程化应用：** [[02_Economy/03_货币银行学/3_金融机构/12_金融危机.md#资产价格繁荣和破灭(和前一个差不多)|课程：资产价格繁荣和破灭]]：核对课程中的资产价格—抵押品—净值—去杠杆链；本原子用原始模型固定机制边界，再保留课程的危机应用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Original mechanism model:** Bernanke, Gertler, and Gilchrist (1999) was checked for endogenous credit-market developments that amplify and propagate macroeconomic shocks and for the role of borrower financing conditions.
- **Original empirical extension:** Bernanke, Gertler, and Gilchrist (1994) was checked for the interpretation of the accelerator through cyclical agency costs of lending rather than as a synonym for every credit contraction.
- **Later authoritative exposition:** Bernanke (2007) was checked for the inverse relationship between borrower financial condition and the external finance premium and for amplification of shock persistence and magnitude.
- **Course-level application:** the local asset-price boom-and-bust section was checked for the course's asset-price, collateral, net-worth, and deleveraging chain. The original model fixes the mechanism boundary; the course note supplies the crisis application.
