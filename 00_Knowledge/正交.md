# Orthogonality
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正交*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Orthogonal** means perpendicular in ordinary real-coordinate geometry. Its algebraic test is that the [[向量点积|dot product]], the sum of products of matching coordinates, is zero. This test also works in higher dimensions where drawing a right angle is difficult. More generally, we use a chosen **[[内积|inner product]]** to decide what orthogonality means.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**正交**在普通实坐标几何中就是垂直。代数检验是[[向量点积|点积]]为零：对应坐标相乘后相加，结果为零。这在难以画出直角的高维空间中仍然可用。更一般地，我们用事先选定的**[[内积]]**来规定正交的含义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A first calculation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the column vectors $x=(1,1)^T$ and $y=(1,-1)^T$. The superscript $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]], so $x^T$ is the row used to multiply the column $y$. Their coordinate contributions cancel:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取列向量 $x=(1,1)^T$ 与 $y=(1,-1)^T$。上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，所以 $x^T$ 是用来乘列向量 $y$ 的行。对应坐标的贡献恰好抵消：

$$x^Ty=1\cdot1+1\cdot(-1)=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two diagonal directions are perpendicular, even though neither follows a coordinate axis. Multiplying either vector by any real number preserves the zero product. Their [[欧氏范数|lengths]] are both $\sqrt2$, so orthogonality does **not** require unit length; adding that requirement gives an [[标准正交组|orthonormal family]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个斜方向彼此垂直，虽然都不沿坐标轴。把任一向量乘以任意实数，零点积仍然保留。两者的[[欧氏范数|长度]]都是 $\sqrt2$，可见正交**不要求**长度为一；再加上单位长度要求，才得到[[标准正交组]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The inner product specifies the test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*由内积规定检验方式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inner product assigns a scalar $\langle x,y\rangle$ to two vectors in the same [[向量空间|vector space]]. It extends the dot product while retaining addition, scaling, conjugate symmetry, and a positive self-product for every nonzero vector. The self-product determines length through $\|x\|=\sqrt{\langle x,x\rangle}$. An inner-product space is a vector space together with this choice of measurement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内积把同一[[向量空间]]中的两个向量对应到一个标量 $\langle x,y\rangle$。它推广点积，同时保留对加法、数乘的规则、共轭对称性，以及每个非零向量与自身的内积严格为正。自身内积通过 $\|x\|=\sqrt{\langle x,x\rangle}$ 确定长度。内积空间就是一个向量空间连同所选的这种测量方式。

> [!important] Zero inner product · 内积为零
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For vectors $x,y$ in one fixed inner-product space, write $x\perp y$ exactly when
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对同一个已固定内积的空间中的向量 $x,y$，记号 $x\perp y$ 的定义是
>
> $$\langle x,y\rangle=0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The relation is symmetric: $\langle y,x\rangle=\overline{\langle x,y\rangle}$, and the conjugate of zero is zero. With standard real coordinates, the test is $x^Ty=0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这个关系是对称的，因为 $\langle y,x\rangle=\overline{\langle x,y\rangle}$，零的共轭仍是零。在标准实坐标中，检验就是 $x^Ty=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For complex coordinates, this card uses the convention **conjugate-linear in the first argument and linear in the second**. If $x,y\in\mathbb C^n$, the standard inner product is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对复坐标，本卡采用**第一个自变量共轭线性、第二个自变量线性**的约定。若 $x,y\in\mathbb C^n$，标准内积为

$$\langle x,y\rangle=x^*y=\sum_{j=1}^n\overline{x_j}y_j.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $n$ is the number of coordinates, the bar conjugates each complex number, and $*$ means conjugate transpose. For complex scalars $a,b$ and another vector $z$, $\langle ax+by,z\rangle=\bar a\langle x,z\rangle+\bar b\langle y,z\rangle$, whereas $\langle z,ax+by\rangle=a\langle z,x\rangle+b\langle z,y\rangle$. Other books conjugate the second argument instead; this changes coefficient formulas but not which pairs have zero inner product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $n$ 是坐标个数，横线对复数取共轭，$*$ 表示共轭转置。对复标量 $a,b$ 和另一个向量 $z$，$\langle ax+by,z\rangle=\bar a\langle x,z\rangle+\bar b\langle y,z\rangle$，而 $\langle z,ax+by\rangle=a\langle z,x\rangle+b\langle z,y\rangle$。有些教材改为对第二个自变量取共轭；这会改变提取系数的公式，但不会改变哪些向量对的内积为零。

> [!example] Why conjugation matters · 为什么需要共轭
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $i^2=-1$ and $x=(1,i)^T$. The ordinary transpose gives $x^Tx=1+i^2=0$, although $x\ne0$. The correct self-product is $x^*x=1+(-i)i=2$, so $x$ is not orthogonal to itself. Its partner $y=(1,-i)^T$ does satisfy $x^*y=1+(-i)(-i)=0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $i^2=-1$，取 $x=(1,i)^T$。普通转置给出 $x^Tx=1+i^2=0$，但 $x\ne0$。正确的自身内积是 $x^*x=1+(-i)i=2$，所以 $x$ 并不与自身正交。另一个向量 $y=(1,-i)^T$ 才满足 $x^*y=1+(-i)(-i)=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Zero vectors and the choice of geometry
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 使用条件与含义边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The zero vector is orthogonal to every vector, since its inner products all vanish. It has no direction, so it has no angle with another vector. For **nonzero real vectors** in the standard Euclidean geometry, the angle $\theta$ satisfies $x^Ty=\|x\|_2\|y\|_2\cos\theta$; only then does zero inner product mean a $90^\circ$ angle. Positive self-products also show that zero is the only vector orthogonal to itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零向量与每个向量都正交，因为所有相关内积都为零。它没有方向，因此也没有与另一个向量的夹角。对标准欧氏几何中的**非零实向量**，夹角 $\theta$ 满足 $x^Ty=\|x\|_2\|y\|_2\cos\theta$；在这个前提下，内积为零才对应 $90^\circ$。自身内积的正定性还说明：只有零向量能够与自身正交。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inner product matters. A real symmetric [[正定矩阵|positive-definite matrix]] $M$ of size $n\times n$ defines $\langle x,y\rangle_M=x^TMy$: symmetry supplies the symmetry rule, and $x^TMx>0$ for $x\ne0$ supplies positive squared length. Over complex coordinates require $M=M^*$ and $x^*Mx>0$, and use $x^*My$. These are valid alternative geometries, not the standard inner product unless $M$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

内积的选择会影响结果。实对称 $n\times n$ [[正定矩阵]] $M$ 定义 $\langle x,y\rangle_M=x^TMy$：对称性提供内积的对称规则，非零 $x$ 满足 $x^TMx>0$ 则提供正的长度平方。复坐标下要求 $M=M^*$ 且非零 $x$ 满足 $x^*Mx>0$，并使用 $x^*My$。这都是合法的其他几何；只有 $M$ 是[[单位矩阵]]时，它才等于标准内积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $M=\operatorname{diag}(2,1)$, the original pair $(1,1)^T,(1,-1)^T$ has weighted product $2-1=1$ and is no longer orthogonal. Conversely, $(1,1)^T,(1,-2)^T$ has ordinary product $-1$ but weighted product $2-2=0$. Coordinates alone therefore do not determine orthogonality until the inner product is fixed. The same choice must be used when forming an [[正交补|orthogonal complement]] or an [[正交投影|orthogonal projection]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $M=\operatorname{diag}(2,1)$，原来的一对 $(1,1)^T,(1,-1)^T$ 的加权内积为 $2-1=1$，不再正交。反过来，$(1,1)^T,(1,-2)^T$ 的普通点积为 $-1$，加权内积却为 $2-2=0$。因此，仅有坐标还不能确定正交关系，必须先固定内积。求[[正交补]]或[[正交投影]]时，也要沿用同一种选择。
