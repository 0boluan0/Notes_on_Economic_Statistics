# 金额久期是头寸价值对指定收益率的负导数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Money duration is minus the derivative of position value with respect to the specified yield*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

金额久期把相对敏感度转成与头寸规模相符的货币敏感度。本页采用 $M_y=-\partial V/\partial y$ 的约定，$y$ 是以小数表示的指定年收益率；普通固定非负现金流多头的 $M_y$ 为正，空头反号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Money duration converts relative sensitivity into currency sensitivity at the position's scale. This page uses $M_y=-\partial V/\partial y$, with the specified annual yield $y$ expressed as a decimal. An ordinary long position in fixed nonnegative cash flows has positive $M_y$; a short position reverses the sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

持有数量 $q$ 不随冲击改变，单位全价为 $P$，则 $V=qP$。对同一收益率坐标，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed holding quantity $q$ and unit full price $P$, position value is $V=qP$. In the same yield coordinate:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$M_y=-qP'(y)=qP D_{\mathrm{mod}},\qquad \Delta V\approx-M_y\Delta y.$$

若多头市值为 $10{,}000{,}000$ 元、修正久期为 $5$，则 $M_y=50{,}000{,}000$ 元／单位收益率。加息 $1\%$ 即 $\Delta y=0.01$，一阶损失约 $500{,}000$ 元；金额久期本身不是加息一个基点的损失，后者须乘 $10^{-4}$，见 [[DV01]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a long market value of 10,000,000 and modified duration of 5, $M_y=50,000,000$ currency units per unit yield. A one-percentage-point increase, $\Delta y=0.01$, gives an approximate loss of 500,000. Money duration itself is not the loss for one basis point; multiply by $10^{-4}$ for [[DV01|DV01]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有些课堂或系统把 $\partial V/\partial y$ 本身称为金额久期，或把“dollar duration”缩放到每 $1\%$、每 $1$ bp。使用数值前必须核对符号、缩放和金额范围；仅凭名称不能互换。每 $100$ 面值的价格要先按持有面值换成头寸全价，不能拿面值直接替代市值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some courses or systems call $\partial V/\partial y$ money duration, or scale “dollar duration” per 1% or per basis point. Check the sign, scaling and position scope before using a number. A price per 100 of par must first be scaled into full position value; par value cannot simply replace market value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
