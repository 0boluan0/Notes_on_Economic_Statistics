# 劳动年龄占比上升只有通过实际就业或生产率等渠道才会体现为更高的人均产出

判断[[人口红利]]是否兑现，应区分“有多少人处于劳动年龄”“实际有多少人就业”和“每位就业者生产多少”。年龄结构变得有利，可以扩大机会，但不能代替后两项的实际变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To assess realization of a [[人口红利|demographic dividend]], distinguish people of working age, people actually employed and output per employed person. A favourable age structure can expand opportunities but does not substitute for changes in employment or productivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $Y$ 为同期可比价产出、$N>0$ 为总人口、$E>0$ 为与产出对应的就业人数。复用[[人均GDP]]与[[劳动生产率]]的分母定义，得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Y$ be output at comparable prices, $N>0$ total population and $E>0$ corresponding employment for the same period. Applying the denominator definitions in [[人均GDP|GDP per capita]] and [[劳动生产率|labour productivity]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{Y}{N}=\frac{Y}{E}\frac{E}{N}.
$$

若再用 $W$ 表示 15–64 岁人口，并明确假定这个例子里所有就业者都属于该年龄组，则 $E/N=(E/W)(W/N)$。此时 $E/W$ 才是该年龄组的就业人口比，不能写成 $1-u$：[[失业率]] $u$ 的分母是劳动力，而不是整个年龄组。若有年龄组外的就业者，须分组计入，不能悄悄省略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $W$ denotes ages 15–64 and all employment in the example is explicitly assumed to lie within that range, then $E/N=(E/W)(W/N)$. Here $E/W$ is the age group’s employment-to-population ratio, not one minus the [[失业率|unemployment rate]], whose denominator is the labour force. Employment outside the chosen age range must otherwise be included separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造例中总人口始终为 100，劳动年龄人口由 60 升到 70，实际就业却始终为 48，每就业者产出始终为 2。[[年龄抚养比]]从 66.7 降至 42.9，但人均产出仍是 $2\times48/100=0.96$。如果就业增加到 56 且生产率不变，人均产出才增至 1.12；这是假设下的核算，不是教育或就业政策的因果估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed example, population stays at 100, the working-age population rises from 60 to 70, employment stays at 48 and output per employed person stays at 2. The [[年龄抚养比|age dependency ratio]] falls from 66.7 to 42.9, but output per capita remains $2\times48/100=0.96$. With employment instead rising to 56 and productivity unchanged, output per capita becomes 1.12. This is accounting under stated assumptions, not a causal estimate of education or employment policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [World Bank WDI，年龄抚养比方法](https://databank.worldbank.org/metadataglossary/world-development-indicators/series/SP.POP.DPND)：年龄比不等于实际经济依赖。
- [UNFPA，Demographic dividend](https://www.unfpa.org/demographic-dividend)，Necessary investments、What it takes：教育、健康与就业是机会转化的条件。
- [UN，NTA Manual（2013）](https://ntaccounts.org/doc/repository/NTA%20manual%202013.pdf#page=27)，印刷第10页式(1.1)–(1.2)、第109页：将经济表现分为生产与供养因素的思路。本卡使用未加权实际人数重新推导 GDP 恒等式，不把 NTA 有效消费者分母误写为总人口；算例为独立构造。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
WDI separates age composition from economic dependency; UNFPA identifies enabling conditions. The NTA Manual motivates separating productive and support factors. The identity here is independently derived using actual employment and population, not NTA-equivalent consumers. The example is constructed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
