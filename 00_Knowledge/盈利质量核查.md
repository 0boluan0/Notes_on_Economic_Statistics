# 盈利质量核查是沿利润来源与现金调节追索可验证证据的分析程序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An earnings-quality check traces profit sources and cash reconciliations to verifiable evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

核查[[盈利质量]]时，把“这笔利润能否相信、能否延续”变成可以查证的问题。先做[[财务比率口径匹配]]，再沿同一条证据链检查，不以一项异常比率直接宣布舞弊。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To assess [[盈利质量|earnings quality]], turn credibility and persistence into testable questions. Start with [[财务比率口径匹配|basis matching]], then follow an evidence chain rather than declaring fraud from one unusual ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 找增量：利润增加来自主营业务、合并范围变化、资产出售还是估计变动？将金额对回报表和附注。
2. 查确认：抽查重要收入的合同、交付、退回条款及费用资本化依据；关注[[关联方交易]]的交易实质与结算条件。
3. 对现金：从净利润到 CFO 的调节中，区分非现金损益、非经营项目和营运资本；查期后回款或冲回，勿预设二者固定比例。
4. 下有边界的结论：写清已核证据、尚缺材料和可能推翻解释的事实；用后续数据复核，而不是把假设直接当结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Locate the change: operations, consolidation scope, disposals, or estimates; reconcile it to statements and notes.<br>
**2.** Inspect recognition: material contracts, delivery and return terms, capitalization evidence, and the substance of [[关联方交易|related-party transactions]].<br>
**3.** Reconcile cash: separate non-cash, non-operating, and working-capital effects; inspect later collections or reversals without assuming a fixed profit-to-CFO ratio.<br>
**4.** State a bounded conclusion: distinguish verified evidence, missing records, and falsifying observations, then revisit it with later data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如收入增长 30% 而应收增长 80%，可以提出“回款放慢”或“年末销售集中”等竞争解释。应查看赊销额、账龄、合同和期后回款；没有这些证据，既不能断言虚增收入，也不能因最终收到一笔钱就排除全部确认问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revenue growth of 30% alongside receivables growth of 80% suggests competing explanations such as slower collection or concentrated year-end sales. Examine credit sales, aging, contracts, and later receipts. Without them, neither fabricated revenue nor complete accounting validity is established.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
