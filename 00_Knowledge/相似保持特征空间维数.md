# Eigenspaces under similarity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*相似变换下的特征空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing coordinates can change the written eigenvectors, but it cannot change how many independent eigenvector directions belong to a fixed eigenvalue. The reason is stronger than a coincidence of counts: the coordinate conversion pairs every vector in one eigenspace with exactly one vector in the other, and the conversion can be undone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换坐标可以改变写出来的特征向量，却不能改变某个固定特征值有多少个线性无关的特征方向。原因不只是数量碰巧相等：坐标转换把一个特征空间里的每个向量，与另一个空间里的恰好一个向量配对，而且这种转换可以反向还原。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $B=M^{-1}AM$, the definition of [[相似矩阵|similarity]], for $n\times n$ matrices over the same field $\mathbb F$, with $n\ge1$ and $M$ invertible. For a scalar $\lambda\in\mathbb F$, the solution space
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设同一域 $\mathbb F$ 上的 $n\times n$ 矩阵满足 $B=M^{-1}AM$，其中 $n\ge1$、$M$ 可逆；这正是[[相似矩阵|相似]]的定义。对标量 $\lambda\in\mathbb F$，考虑解空间

$$E_\lambda(A)=\{v\in\mathbb F^n:Av=\lambda v\}=\operatorname{null}(A-\lambda I_n).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $\lambda$ is an eigenvalue, this is its [[特征空间|eigenspace]]: all associated eigenvectors together with zero. The notation $\operatorname{null}$ means the [[零空间|nullspace]], the inputs sent to zero, and $I_n$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity]]. The space's [[维数|dimension]] counts the vectors in a [[基|basis]], not the number of nonzero vectors in the space. At an eigenvalue this count is called the [[代数重数与几何重数|geometric multiplicity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $\lambda$ 是特征值时，这就是其[[特征空间]]：所有相应特征向量再加上零向量。$\operatorname{null}$ 表示[[零空间]]，即被矩阵送到零的输入；$I_n$ 是[[单位矩阵]]。空间的[[维数]]数的是一组[[基]]里有几个向量，不是空间里有几个非零向量。对特征值而言，这个数量称为[[代数重数与几何重数|几何重数]]。

> [!example] The direction changes in coordinates · 方向的坐标写法变了
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$ and $M=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$, giving $B=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$. For $A$, the eigenvectors at $3$ are the nonzero multiples of $v=(0,1)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Converting this vector gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$、$M=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$，得到 $B=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$。$A$ 在特征值3处的特征向量，是 $v=(0,1)^T$ 的所有非零倍数，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。转换这个向量，得到
>
> $$w=M^{-1}v=\begin{pmatrix}-1\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad Bw=\begin{pmatrix}-3\\3\end{pmatrix}=3w.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus $E_3(A)$ is the line of multiples of $(0,1)^T$, while $E_3(B)$ is the line of multiples of $(-1,1)^T$. Both lines require one scalar coefficient, so both have dimension one. They are not equal as sets of coordinate columns.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因此 $E_3(A)$ 是 $(0,1)^T$ 的倍数构成的直线，而 $E_3(B)$ 是 $(-1,1)^T$ 的倍数构成的直线。两条线都只需要一个标量系数，维数都为一；但作为坐标列的集合，它们并不相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The forward map and its inverse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正向转换与反向还原*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start from $Av=\lambda v$ and set $w=M^{-1}v$. Associativity of [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $Av=\lambda v$ 出发，令 $w=M^{-1}v$。利用[[矩阵乘法]]的结合律，得到

$$Bw=M^{-1}AMM^{-1}v=M^{-1}Av=\lambda M^{-1}v=\lambda w.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So $w$ belongs to $E_\lambda(B)$. If $v\ne0$, then $w\ne0$, because otherwise $v=Mw=0$. Conversely, $Bw=\lambda w$ implies $A(Mw)=M(Bw)=\lambda Mw$, so $Mw\in E_\lambda(A)$. These two conversions undo each other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以 $w\in E_\lambda(B)$。若 $v\ne0$，则 $w\ne0$，否则会有 $v=Mw=0$。反过来，$Bw=\lambda w$ 推出 $A(Mw)=M(Bw)=\lambda Mw$，所以 $Mw\in E_\lambda(A)$。这两个转换互相还原。

> [!important] An invertible linear correspondence · 可逆的线性对应
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The map below is linear and one-to-one onto, with inverse $w\mapsto Mw$. Such a correspondence is a **linear isomorphism**:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 下面的映射是线性的，而且一一对应到整个目标空间；逆映射为 $w\mapsto Mw$。这样的对应称为**线性同构**：
>
> $$\Phi_\lambda:E_\lambda(A)\longrightarrow E_\lambda(B),\qquad v\longmapsto M^{-1}v.$$
> $$E_\lambda(B)=M^{-1}E_\lambda(A),\qquad \dim_{\mathbb F}E_\lambda(B)=\dim_{\mathbb F}E_\lambda(A).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The set notation $M^{-1}E_\lambda(A)$ means applying $M^{-1}$ to every vector of that space. Linearity means that sums and scalar multiples are preserved. To see why dimension follows, take a basis $v_1,\ldots,v_d$ of $E_\lambda(A)$. Every vector of $E_\lambda(B)$ is a combination of $M^{-1}v_1,\ldots,M^{-1}v_d$. If a combination of these images is zero, multiplication by $M$ gives the same combination of the original basis equal to zero; [[线性无关|independence]] forces all coefficients to vanish. The image list is therefore a basis with the same number of vectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集合写法 $M^{-1}E_\lambda(A)$ 表示对空间中的每个向量都施加 $M^{-1}$。线性意味着保持加法与标量乘法。要说明维数为何相同，取 $E_\lambda(A)$ 的基 $v_1,\ldots,v_d$：$E_\lambda(B)$ 中每个向量都能写成 $M^{-1}v_1,\ldots,M^{-1}v_d$ 的组合。若这些像的某个组合为零，左乘 $M$ 就得到原基的同一个组合为零；[[线性无关]]迫使所有系数都为零。因此，这些像构成一组长度仍为 $d$ 的基。

## What the dimension test can and cannot decide
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*维数能判断什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\lambda$ is not an eigenvalue, the same equations give $E_\lambda(A)=E_\lambda(B)=\{0\}$, both of dimension zero. The argument is always made over the same field. For a real matrix and a nonreal $\lambda$, first regard both matrices and their vector spaces as complex; do not compare a real-space dimension with a complex-space dimension.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\lambda$ 不是特征值，同样的方程给出 $E_\lambda(A)=E_\lambda(B)=\{0\}$，维数都为零。整个论证始终在同一个域上进行。对实矩阵和非实数 $\lambda$，应先把两个矩阵及其向量空间都放到复数域；不能把实空间维数与复空间维数混着比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[相似保持特征多项式|Characteristic-polynomial invariance]] preserves roots and algebraic multiplicities. The present argument preserves eigenspace dimensions, which are different information. For example, $2I_2$ has a two-dimensional eigenspace at $2$, whereas $C=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ has only the line $\{(a,0)^T:a\in\mathbb F\}$, since $(C-2I_2)(a,b)^T=(b,0)^T$. The dimensions differ, so the matrices cannot be similar, despite their identical polynomial $(t-2)^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[相似保持特征多项式]]保留根与代数重数；本卡保留特征空间维数，这是另一类信息。例如，$2I_2$ 在特征值2处的特征空间为二维；而 $C=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ 只有直线 $\{(a,0)^T:a\in\mathbb F\}$，因为 $(C-2I_2)(a,b)^T=(b,0)^T$。维数不同，所以它们不可能相似，尽管特征多项式都是 $(t-2)^2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A dimension mismatch is a decisive way to rule out similarity. Matching all ordinary eigenspace dimensions is not, by itself, a complete similarity test; further structure can matter, as the [[Jordan形唯一性|Jordan-block classification]] explains when its field conditions hold. Nor does equal dimension say that the same numerical vectors are eigenvectors of both matrices: the required conversion is $M^{-1}v$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

维数不一致，足以排除相似；但所有普通特征空间维数都一致，本身还不是完整的相似判据。在相应域条件成立时，[[Jordan形唯一性|Jordan块分类]]说明还可能需要哪些结构。另外，维数相同不表示两个矩阵具有相同坐标的特征向量；正确的转换仍是 $M^{-1}v$。
