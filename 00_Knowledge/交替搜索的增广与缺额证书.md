# 交替搜索要么找到增广路要么给出 Hall 瓶颈

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An alternating search finds either an augmenting path or a Hall bottleneck*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子证明策略
> 在二分图中，若当前 matching 尚未覆盖 $L$，就从所有未匹配左点出发，只沿 non-matching edges 从左走到右，再沿 matching edges 从右走回左。若到达未匹配右点，搜索树给出 augmenting path；若没有，则已到达的左右集合构成一个 Hall-deficient certificate。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a bipartite graph, if the current matching does not cover $L$, start from every unmatched left vertex, traverse non-matching edges from left to right, and traverse matching edges from right to left. Reaching an unmatched right vertex yields an augmenting path. If none is reached, the reached left and right sets form a Hall-deficient certificate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取一个 maximum matching $M$，并假设它仍未覆盖 $L$。令非空集合 $S_0$ 是未匹配左点；交替搜索到达的左点集合记为 $S$，右点集合记为 $T$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take a maximum matching $M$ and suppose it still fails to cover $L$. Let the nonempty set $S_0$ contain the unmatched left vertices. Write $S$ and $T$ for the left and right vertices reached by the alternating search.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $T$ 含未匹配右点，从 $S_0$ 到该点的搜索路径就是 augmenting path，与 $M$ maximum 矛盾。因此 $T$ 中每个点都已匹配。它们的 matching partners 恰好是 $S\setminus S_0$，所以
$$
|T|=|S|-|S_0|<|S|.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $T$ contained an unmatched right vertex, the search path from $S_0$ to that vertex would be augmenting, contradicting the maximum cardinality of $M$. Thus every vertex in $T$ is matched. Their matching partners are exactly $S\setminus S_0$, and hence
$$
|T|=|S|-|S_0|<|S|.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先证明 $N(S)=T$ 的两个方向。每个 $t\in T$ 都是从某个已到达左点沿边到达，所以 $T\subseteq N(S)$。反过来，若 $s\in S$ 与右点 $t$ 相邻：当 $st\notin M$ 时，搜索会沿这条边到达 $t$；当 $st\in M$ 时，$s$ 不属于未匹配根集 $S_0$，并且 $s$ 正是经这条唯一 matching edge 从 $t$ 到达，所以 $t\in T$。因此 $N(S)\subseteq T$，合起来得到 $N(S)=T$。结合上式，
$$
|N(S)|=|T|<|S|,
$$
于是搜索失败本身产生了一个 Hall bottleneck。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove $N(S)=T$, check both inclusions. Every $t\in T$ was reached along an edge from a reached left vertex, so $T\subseteq N(S)$. Conversely, let $s\in S$ be adjacent to a right vertex $t$. If $st\notin M$, the search traverses that edge and reaches $t$. If $st\in M$, then $s$ is not an unmatched root in $S_0$ and was reached from $t$ along this unique matching edge. Thus $t\in T$ in either case, proving $N(S)\subseteq T$. Hence $N(S)=T$. Combining this with the previous inequality gives
$$
|N(S)|=|T|<|S|,
$$
meaning that search failure itself produces a Hall bottleneck.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个二选一结论同时解释算法与定理：不断增广可寻找更大的 matching；若最终仍未覆盖 $L$，搜索前沿给出不可行证书。若 Hall condition 预先保证不存在缺额集合，第二种结果不可能发生，因此 maximum matching 必覆盖 $L$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This either-or result explains both the algorithm and the theorem. Repeated augmentation searches for larger matchings; if full coverage still fails, the final frontier certifies infeasibility. Under Hall's condition, the certificate outcome is impossible, so a maximum matching must cover $L$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与辨别
> 为什么在没有找到增广路时，已到达右点数会严格少于已到达左点数？
>
> **答案：** 每个已到达右点都匹配到一个已到达的非根左点，而至少有一个未匹配根留在 $S_0$；所以 $|T|=|S|-|S_0|<|S|$。
