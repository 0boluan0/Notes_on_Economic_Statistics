# Alternating search and Hall certificates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*交替搜索的增广与缺额证书*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When an assignment leaves a student without a project, there are two different explanations: the current choices need rearranging, or the allowed choices cannot serve everyone. **Alternating search** distinguishes these possibilities in a [[二分图|bipartite graph]]. It either finds an [[增广路定义|augmenting path]] that allows one more pair, or, after exhausting the relevant search, gives a [[Hall 缺额证书|Hall deficiency certificate]] proving that full left coverage is impossible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
一份分配留下学生未得到项目，可能有两种原因：当前选择需要重排，或者允许的选择本来就无法服务所有人。**交替搜索**在[[二分图]]中区分这两种情况：它要么找出可多配一对的[[增广路定义|增广路]]，要么在穷尽所需搜索后，给出[[Hall 缺额证书]]，证明覆盖全部左侧根本不可能。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Fix the matching and choose every unmatched left root
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*固定匹配，取全部未匹配左点为根*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $G=(L\cup R,E)$ be a finite simple undirected bipartite graph with specified disjoint sides. Fix **any** [[匹配定义|matching]] $M\subseteq E$; it need not be maximum. A vertex is unmatched when no edge of $M$ touches it anywhere in the graph. Let $S_0$ be all unmatched left vertices. If $S_0=\varnothing$, $M$ already [[左侧覆盖与完美匹配|covers]] $L$, so return that result without seeking a deficient set. This includes $L=\varnothing$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $G=(L\cup R,E)$ 是有限简单无向二分图，两侧已指定且不相交。固定**任意**[[匹配定义|匹配]] $M\subseteq E$，不必预先知道它最大。顶点未匹配，是指全图没有任何 $M$ 中的边接触它。令 $S_0$ 为全部未匹配左点。若 $S_0=\varnothing$，$M$ 已经[[左侧覆盖与完美匹配|覆盖]] $L$，直接返回此结果，不寻找缺额集合；这也包括 $L=\varnothing$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Otherwise initialize the search with every vertex of $S_0$. Temporarily give the edges the following search directions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
否则，以 $S_0$ 的全部顶点初始化搜索，临时给边规定以下搜索方向：

$$\ell\longrightarrow r\quad\text{if }\ell r\notin M,
\qquad r\longrightarrow\ell\quad\text{if }\ell r\in M.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\ell r$ denotes the original undirected edge $\{\ell,r\}$. The arrows describe possible reassignment, not a change to the allowed-pair model: try an unselected pair going right, then undo the right vertex's current pair going left. Keep $M$ fixed throughout this one search. If a path is later flipped, start a new search for the new matching.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
其中 $\ell r$ 表示原来的无向边 $\{\ell,r\}$。箭头描述重配路线，并非改变允许配对模型：向右尝试一条未选边，再向左撤换该右点现有的配对。在这一轮搜索中，$M$ 保持不变；以后若翻转路径，应针对新匹配重新搜索。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Record how each vertex is first reached
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*记录每个顶点第一次怎样到达*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A queue gives a concrete implementation. Mark every root discovered before putting it in the queue; roots have no parent. Remove a queued vertex and inspect its allowed outgoing arrows. At a left vertex, these are all incident edges outside $M$; at a matched right vertex, the only outgoing arrow goes to its unique partner. When an unseen vertex is reached, mark it immediately and store the current vertex as its parent. Enqueue it unless it is an unmatched right vertex, in which case return the parent path. Ignore already discovered vertices, including ones still waiting in the queue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
可以用队列具体实现。先把每个根标为已发现，再放入队列，根没有父节点。每次取出一个排队顶点，检查其允许的出箭头：左点检查全部不属于 $M$ 的关联边；已匹配右点只有一条出箭头，通向唯一伙伴。首次到达尚未发现的顶点时，立即标记并记下当前点为其父节点；通常把它入队，若它是未匹配右点，就直接返回父链。已发现的顶点一律不重新设置父节点，包括仍在队列中等待处理的顶点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every parent was discovered before its child. Following parents backwards therefore cannot cycle and ends at an unmatched left root. Reversing the list yields a simple path respecting the arrows. A newly reached free right vertex gives positive odd length, with unselected and selected edges alternating and both endpoints unmatched. It is an augmenting path; [[增广路对称差|flipping its edge membership]] gives a matching of size $|M|+1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
每个父节点都比子节点更早被发现，因此反向沿父节点回溯不会成环，并最终结束于未匹配左根。把得到的列表倒序，就得到遵守箭头的简单路径。若终点是新到达的未匹配右点，路径长度为正奇数，未选边与已选边交替，两端都未匹配，所以它是增广路；[[增广路对称差|翻转路径上的选边状态]]后，匹配大小变为 $|M|+1$。

> [!example] Rearrange one pair to make two · 重排一对得到两对
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $L=\{a,b\}$, $R=\{1,2\}$, $E=\{a1,a2,b1\}$ and $M=\{a1\}$. The only root is $b$. Search follows $b\to1\to a\to2$: the first and last edges are unselected, the middle edge is selected, and $2$ is free. Flipping gives $\{b1,a2\}$. Stopping after noticing that $b$'s immediate neighbor $1$ is occupied would incorrectly declare failure.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 设 $L=\{a,b\}$、$R=\{1,2\}$、$E=\{a1,a2,b1\}$、$M=\{a1\}$。唯一的根是 $b$，搜索沿 $b\to1\to a\to2$ 前进：首尾边未选，中间边已选，且 $2$ 未匹配。翻转后得到 $\{b1,a2\}$。若只看到 $b$ 的直接邻居 $1$ 已被占用就停止，会错误地宣布失败。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Exhaustion turns reached sets into a certificate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*穷尽搜索才把可达集合变成证书*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $S_0\ne\varnothing$, the queue becomes empty, and no unmatched right vertex was reached. Let $S$ and $T$ be the discovered left and right sets. Exhaustion means every outgoing arrow of every discovered vertex has been processed, so these are the full sets reachable from $S_0$. There are two facts to prove, not just a count of the vertices currently on screen.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $S_0\ne\varnothing$，队列已经耗尽，且没有到达任何未匹配右点。令 $S,T$ 分别为已发现的左点集与右点集。穷尽意味着每个已发现点的所有出箭头都已处理，因此它们正是从 $S_0$ 出发的完整可达集合。下面要证明两件事，而不是只数暂时显示出来的顶点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**First, matching partners pair $T$ with $S\setminus S_0$.** Every $r\in T$ is matched, because a free right vertex would have caused a successful return. Its partner is reached along its outgoing matching arrow and lies in $S\setminus S_0$. Conversely, every discovered nonroot left vertex was first reached from a right vertex along its unique matching edge. Distinct right vertices have distinct partners, since $M$ is a matching. This proves the bijection and the count
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**第一，匹配伙伴把 $T$ 与 $S\setminus S_0$ 一一配对。** 每个 $r\in T$ 都已匹配，否则搜索早已成功返回；它的伙伴由出发的匹配箭头到达，属于 $S\setminus S_0$。反过来，每个被发现的非根左点，都是从某个右点沿它唯一的匹配边首次到达的。不同右点的伙伴不同，因为 $M$ 是匹配。因此得到双射及计数

$$|T|=|S|-|S_0|.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Second, $N(S)=T$.** Every vertex in $T$ was reached from a left vertex in $S$, so $T\subseteq N(S)$. For the opposite inclusion, take any neighbor $r$ of $\ell\in S$. If $\ell r\notin M$, exhaustion ensures that the arrow $\ell\to r$ was processed, so $r\in T$. If $\ell r\in M$, then $\ell$ is not a root; it was reached from this very $r$, its unique matching partner, so again $r\in T$. These two edge cases cover every neighbor, proving $N(S)\subseteq T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**第二，$N(S)=T$。** $T$ 中每个点都从 $S$ 中某个左点到达，所以 $T\subseteq N(S)$。反向取 $\ell\in S$ 的任意邻居 $r$：若 $\ell r\notin M$，搜索穷尽保证箭头 $\ell\to r$ 已被处理，因此 $r\in T$；若 $\ell r\in M$，则 $\ell$ 不是根，它恰好经由唯一匹配伙伴 $r$ 到达，因此仍有 $r\in T$。两类边覆盖了全部邻居，所以 $N(S)\subseteq T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Combining the two facts yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
合并两点便得到

$$|N(S)|=|T|=|S|-|S_0|<|S|.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $S$ is a Hall-deficient set, with deficit exactly $|S_0|$. By the certificate's partner-counting proof, every matching has size at most $|L|-|S_0|$. The current matching already has that many edges, since $S_0$ contains all its unmatched left vertices. It is therefore maximum as well as unable to cover $L$. The certificate proof supplies this conclusion directly; the general [[Berge最大匹配定理|Berge criterion]] gives another interpretation through the absence of augmenting paths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
因此 $S$ 是 Hall 缺额集合，缺额恰为 $|S_0|$。由缺额证书的伙伴计数，任意匹配的大小都至多为 $|L|-|S_0|$。当前匹配已经有这么多条边，因为 $S_0$ 包含它的全部未匹配左点，所以它也是最大匹配，但仍无法覆盖 $L$。这个结论直接来自证书证明；一般的 [[Berge最大匹配定理|Berge 判据]]还从不存在增广路的角度解释同一结果。

> [!example] A complete failed search · 一轮完整的失败搜索
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Remove edge $a2$ from the preceding example, keeping $M=\{a1\}$. The root $b$ reaches $1$, then $a$, and there are no further permitted arrows. Thus $S=\{a,b\}$, $T=\{1\}$ and $S_0=\{b\}$. The actual neighborhood is $N(S)=\{1\}$: two students share only one project. An unused right vertex $2$ does not help when neither student is adjacent to it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 从前例删除边 $a2$，仍取 $M=\{a1\}$。根 $b$ 到达 $1$，再到达 $a$，之后没有其他允许箭头。因此 $S=\{a,b\}$、$T=\{1\}$、$S_0=\{b\}$，完整邻居集确实是 $N(S)=\{1\}$：两名学生共享唯一项目。右侧虽然还有未使用的 $2$，但两名学生都不与它相邻，因此无济于事。

## One search, then a new matching
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一轮搜索之后，再处理新匹配*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A premature stop with no path found is not the certificate outcome. For example, before scanning any edge, the root set is known but the discovered right set is empty even when an immediate augmenting edge exists. Only complete exploration justifies $N(S)=T$. Starting from all unmatched left roots also checks every relevant component and gives the displayed exact deficit. If there are no roots, return coverage instead of mistaking $S=T=\varnothing$ for a strict shortage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
中途停止且没有发现路径，不等于得到缺额证书。例如尚未检查任何边时，根已知而发现的右点集为空，即使马上就有一条可增广边。只有完整探索才能保证 $N(S)=T$。从全部未匹配左根出发，还会检查每个相关分量，并得到上式中的准确缺额。若没有根，应返回已覆盖，不把 $S=T=\varnothing$ 误当成严格缺额。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each successful flip adds exactly one edge. From an initial matching $M_0$, at most $|L|-|M_0|$ such improvements are possible before left coverage or a deficient-set return. If [[Hall 定理|Hall's condition]] holds, a deficient set cannot exist, so repetition must reach left coverage. In the original maximum-matching proof, one starts with a maximum $M$ that allegedly leaves a left vertex unmatched: a successful path would contradict maximal cardinality, and the exhaustive alternative gives the same Hall bottleneck. The search itself does not require maximum cardinality in advance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
每次成功翻转恰好增加一条边。从初始匹配 $M_0$ 出发，在覆盖左侧或返回缺额集合之前，至多能改进 $|L|-|M_0|$ 次。若 [[Hall 定理|Hall 条件]]成立，缺额集合不可能存在，因此反复搜索必到达左侧覆盖。原先的最大匹配证明，则从一个据称仍遗漏左点的最大匹配 $M$ 出发：若找到路径就与边数最大矛盾，穷尽搜索的另一分支给出同一 Hall 瓶颈。执行搜索本身不要求预先已是最大匹配。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For indexed vertices, adjacency lists, constant-time partner lookup, marking and queue operations, one search takes $O(1+|L|+|R|+|E|)$ time, including initialization, and $O(1+|L|+|R|)$ additional space. Each vertex is discovered at most once; a left adjacency list is scanned at most once, and a matched right vertex has only one partner to follow. Parent recovery takes at most $|L|+|R|-1$ steps. These are per-search bounds, not the cost of the entire repeated-augmentation algorithm; a dense adjacency-matrix implementation has a different scanning cost. Edge weights, preferences and capacities are not optimized by this unit-pair cardinality procedure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
在顶点可按编号访问、使用邻接表，且伙伴查询、标记、队列操作均为常数时间的模型下，一轮搜索含初始化的时间为 $O(1+|L|+|R|+|E|)$，额外空间为 $O(1+|L|+|R|)$。每点至多发现一次，每张左侧邻接表至多扫描一次，已匹配右点只有一个伙伴要跟进。恢复父链至多走 $|L|+|R|-1$ 步。这是单轮界，不是整个反复增广算法的总成本；若用稠密邻接矩阵，扫描成本会不同。这个单位配对、按边数改进的过程不负责优化边权、偏好或容量。

> [!question]- Where does the strict inequality come from? · 严格缺额从哪里来？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> After an exhaustive search with no free right endpoint, the matching pairs every reached right vertex with exactly one reached nonroot left vertex. At least one unmatched left root remains, so $|N(S)|=|T|=|S|-|S_0|<|S|$. Both the nonempty-root premise and exhaustion are needed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 穷尽搜索且未到达未匹配右端后，每个可达右点恰对应一个可达非根左点；此外还留下至少一个未匹配左根。因此 $|N(S)|=|T|=|S|-|S_0|<|S|$。非空根集与搜索穷尽两个前提都需要。
