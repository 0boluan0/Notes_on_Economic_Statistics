# 连续函数在区间端点异号时，二分法保持至少含一个根的括区间并使区间宽度每轮减半
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When a continuous function has opposite signs at the endpoints, bisection preserves a bracket containing at least one root and halves its width on every iteration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子括区间算法
> 设实函数 $f$ 在 $[a_0,b_0]$ 上连续。先单独返回端点根；若其余情形满足 $f(a_0)$ 与 $f(b_0)$ 异号，介值定理只保证区间内**至少有一个根**。二分法检查中点并保留仍然异号的一半，因此不会丢掉全部根，而且每轮把区间宽度精确减半。这个结论不需要根唯一，也不保证算法会选择多个根中的哪一个。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let a real-valued function $f$ be continuous on $[a_0,b_0]$. Endpoint roots are returned first. Otherwise, opposite endpoint signs imply only that the interval contains **at least one root**. Bisection evaluates the midpoint and retains the half whose endpoints still have opposite signs, so it never discards every root and it halves the bracket width exactly. The conclusion neither requires uniqueness nor controls which root is selected when several are present.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 介值定理给的是存在性，不是唯一性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Intermediate Value Theorem Gives Existence, Not Uniqueness*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经典二分求根从三个前提开始：$a_0<b_0$，$f$ 在闭区间 $[a_0,b_0]$ 上连续，而且在排除端点根后

$$
f(a_0)f(b_0)<0.
$$

乘积为负就是“端点异号”的数学简写。由介值定理（Intermediate Value Theorem, IVT），存在至少一个 $x^*\in(a_0,b_0)$ 使 $f(x^*)=0$。这里没有单调性或唯一性结论：连续函数可以在同一括区间内穿过横轴多次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Classical bisection starts with $a_0<b_0$, continuity of $f$ on the closed interval, and—after endpoint roots have been handled—opposite endpoint signs, written mathematically as $f(a_0)f(b_0)<0$. The Intermediate Value Theorem then guarantees at least one $x^*\in(a_0,b_0)$ with $f(x^*)=0$. It supplies neither monotonicity nor uniqueness: a continuous function may cross the axis several times inside one bracket.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $f(a_0)=0$ 或 $f(b_0)=0$，该端点已经是根，应立即返回。把端点根与严格异号测试分开，既不会漏掉合法答案，也能让后续不变量保持严格形式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f(a_0)=0$ or $f(b_0)=0$, that endpoint is already a root and should be returned immediately. Separating endpoint roots from the strict opposite-sign test avoids rejecting a valid answer and keeps the later invariant in strict form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 中点更新保留括区间不变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Midpoint Update Preserves the Bracketing Invariant*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设第 $n$ 轮开始时区间 $[a_n,b_n]$ 满足

$$
f(a_n)f(b_n)<0.
$$

取中点

$$
m_n=a_n+\frac{b_n-a_n}{2}.
$$

若 $f(m_n)=0$，中点就是根，可以提前停止。否则 $f(m_n)$ 与两个端点中的一个同号：若它与 $f(a_n)$ 同号，就保留 $[m_n,b_n]$；若它与 $f(b_n)$ 同号，就保留 $[a_n,m_n]$。新区间上的函数仍连续，端点仍异号，所以介值定理再次保证其中至少有一个根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $[a_n,b_n]$ begins an iteration with opposite endpoint signs, and form $m_n=a_n+(b_n-a_n)/2$. If $f(m_n)=0$, the midpoint is a root. Otherwise the midpoint shares the sign of exactly one endpoint: retain $[m_n,b_n]$ when it shares the sign of $f(a_n)$, and retain $[a_n,m_n]$ when it shares the sign of $f(b_n)$. The function remains continuous on the retained interval and its endpoints still have opposite signs, so the Intermediate Value Theorem again certifies at least one root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就是算法真正拥有的不变量：“当前闭区间连续、端点异号、因而至少含一个根。”取中点使

$$
b_{n+1}-a_{n+1}
=\frac{b_n-a_n}{2},
$$

但“总是取中点”本身不是正确性证明；正确性来自每轮都按符号保留有证书的一半。累计区间宽度、中点位置误差与达到容差所需轮数见 [[二分误差界]]；[[求根残差|函数残差]]能否控制根位置见 [[残差控制根误差]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The actual invariant is: the function is continuous on the current closed interval, its endpoint signs differ, and the interval therefore contains at least one root. Taking the midpoint makes the next width exactly half the current width, but midpoint selection alone is not the correctness proof; correctness comes from retaining the certified half by sign. [[二分误差界|Bisection error bounds]] owns cumulative width, midpoint positional error, and required iteration counts; [[残差控制根误差|Residual Control of Root Error]] owns the conditions under which a function residual controls root position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 多根、端点根与偶重根
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Multiple Roots, Endpoint Roots, and Even-Multiplicity Roots*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **端点已经是根：** 先返回端点；不要让严格异号测试把合法答案误报为“没有括区间”。
- **区间内有多个根：** 二分法只承诺保留至少一个根，通常不能指定最后得到哪一个；若任务要求特定根或全部根，必须先隔离根或改用相应方法。
- **偶重根可能不变号：** $f(x)=(x-1)^2$ 在 $[0,2]$ 内有根 $1$，但两个端点值都为正。经典异号二分法无法从这个区间取得存在性证书；这不等于区间里没有根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **An endpoint is already a root:** return it before applying the strict opposite-sign test.
- **Several roots lie in the interval:** bisection promises to retain at least one of them but generally cannot specify which one will be obtained. Finding a particular root or all roots requires prior root isolation or another method.
- **An even-multiplicity root may not change sign:** $f(x)=(x-1)^2$ has a root at $1$ on $[0,2]$, while both endpoint values are positive. Classical sign-changing bisection has no existence certificate on this interval; that does not prove that no root is present.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续性也不能省略。令

$$
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
-1,&x<0,\\
1,&x\ge0.
\end{cases}
$$

它在 $[-1,1]$ 两端异号却没有零点。若仍机械二分，区间会缩向跳点 $0$，但这不是求根收敛。异号加连续性才是介值定理的完整前提。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity is equally indispensable. The jump function $f(x)=-1$ for $x<0$ and $f(x)=1$ for $x\ge0$ has opposite signs at $-1$ and $1$ but no zero. Mechanical interval halving shrinks toward the discontinuity at zero rather than toward a root. Opposite signs and continuity together form the complete Intermediate Value Theorem premise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与离散二分查找的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary with Discrete Binary Search*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[二分查找]]与二分求根都反复排除一半，但证明对象不同。离散二分查找处理有序有限候选或数组索引；中点与目标的大小比较证明某一侧不可能含有目标，并能在候选耗尽时证明目标不在该有限集合中。二分求根处理实区间上的连续函数；端点符号和介值定理只证明新区间仍至少含一个根。初始区间没有异号，只表示这个证书不能启动，不能据此断言区间中没有根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[二分查找|Binary search]] and bisection root-finding both discard half repeatedly, but they certify different objects. Discrete binary search works on ordered finite candidates or array indices; comparison with the target proves that one side is impossible, and exhausting the candidates proves absence from that finite set. Bisection root-finding works on a continuous real function; endpoint signs and the Intermediate Value Theorem prove only that the retained interval still contains at least one root. Failure to obtain an initial sign-changing bracket means that this certificate cannot start, not that the interval has no root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检与反例
> 1. 为什么“每轮取中点”还不足以证明二分求根正确？
> 2. $f(x)=(x-1)^2$ 在 $[0,2]$ 上没有端点异号。能否因此断言区间内没有根？
> 3. 一个函数在区间两端异号，但区间内有跳跃不连续。异号是否仍足以保证零点？
>
> **答案：** 1. 还必须根据符号保留端点异号的一半，才能继续使用介值定理；任意丢掉一半可能丢掉所有根。2. 不能；$x=1$ 是偶重根，只是异号括区间证书失效。3. 不能；跳跃函数可以从负值直接跳到正值而从不取零，连续性不可省略。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> 1. Why is taking a midpoint on every iteration insufficient to prove bisection correct? 2. Does the lack of opposite endpoint signs for $f(x)=(x-1)^2$ on $[0,2]$ prove that no root exists? 3. If endpoint signs differ but the function has a jump inside the interval, is a zero still guaranteed?
>
> **Answers:** 1. The method must retain the half whose endpoint signs still differ, so the Intermediate Value Theorem remains applicable; discarding an arbitrary half can lose every root. 2. No. The even-multiplicity root at $1$ exists even though the sign-change certificate fails. 3. No. A discontinuous function can jump from negative to positive without taking the value zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
