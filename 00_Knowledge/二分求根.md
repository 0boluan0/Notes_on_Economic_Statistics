# Bisection root finding
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*二分求根*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bisection finds an input where a continuous real function becomes zero by repeatedly cutting a known containing interval in half. To solve $x^2=2$, write $f(x)=x^2-2$: among nonnegative inputs, a point below the positive square root gives a negative output, and one above it gives a positive output. We keep two endpoints with opposite signs and use a midpoint test to decide which half still contains a root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二分求根通过反复把一个已知含根的区间对半缩小，寻找让连续实函数输出为零的输入。求 $x^2=2$ 时，令 $f(x)=x^2-2$：限定非负输入时，在正平方根下方输入会得到负值，上方输入会得到正值。保留两个函数值异号的端点，再检查中点，就能决定哪一半仍然含根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **root** is an input $r$ satisfying $f(r)=0$. A **bracket** here is a closed interval $[a,b]$ on which $f$ is [[一点连续|continuous]]—nearby inputs have nearby outputs—and whose endpoint outputs have opposite signs. The [[介值定理|intermediate value theorem]] explains why these assumptions certify at least one root. The algorithm and exact halving statements below use exact arithmetic and reliable function signs; the final section explains what changes on a computer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**根**就是满足 $f(r)=0$ 的输入 $r$。这里的**括区间**是闭区间 $[a,b]$：$f$ 在其上[[一点连续|处处连续]]，即相近输入给出相近输出，而且端点输出异号。[[介值定理]]说明这些前提为何保证至少有一个根。下文算法与精确减半结论先采用精确算术和可靠符号；最后再说明计算机实现的边界。

> [!important] What the bracket guarantees · 括区间保证什么
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let a real-valued function $f$ be continuous on $[a_0,b_0]$. Endpoint roots are returned first. Otherwise, opposite endpoint signs imply only that the interval contains **at least one root**. Bisection evaluates the midpoint and retains the half whose endpoints still have opposite signs, so it never discards every root and it halves the bracket width exactly. The conclusion neither requires uniqueness nor controls which root is selected when several are present.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设实函数 $f$ 在 $[a_0,b_0]$ 上连续。先单独返回端点根；若其余情形满足 $f(a_0)$ 与 $f(b_0)$ 异号，介值定理只保证区间内**至少有一个根**。二分法检查中点并保留仍然异号的一半，因此不会丢掉全部根，而且每轮把区间宽度精确减半。这个结论不需要根唯一，也不保证算法会选择多个根中的哪一个。

> [!example] Two updates for a square root · 平方根的两次更新
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^2-2$ on $[1,2]$, the endpoint values are $-1$ and $2$. The first midpoint is $m_0=3/2$, with $f(m_0)=1/4>0$, so keep $[1,3/2]$. The next midpoint is $m_1=5/4$, with $f(m_1)=-7/16<0$, so keep $[5/4,3/2]$. After two updates the root is in an interval of width $1/4$. Its new midpoint $m_2=11/8$ is within $1/8$ of a root, even without evaluating $f(m_2)$. It is not an exact answer: $f(11/8)=-7/64$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[1,2]$ 上的 $f(x)=x^2-2$，端点函数值是 $-1$ 和 $2$。首次中点 $m_0=3/2$ 满足 $f(m_0)=1/4>0$，故保留 $[1,3/2]$。下次中点 $m_1=5/4$ 满足 $f(m_1)=-7/16<0$，故保留 $[5/4,3/2]$。两次更新后，根位于宽度 $1/4$ 的区间中；新中点 $m_2=11/8$ 到某个根的距离不超过 $1/8$，即使尚未计算 $f(m_2)$ 也成立。它并非精确答案，因为 $f(11/8)=-7/64$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The Intermediate Value Theorem Gives Existence, Not Uniqueness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 介值定理给的是存在性，不是唯一性

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Classical bisection starts with $a_0<b_0$, continuity of $f$ on the closed interval, and—after endpoint roots have been handled—opposite endpoint signs, written mathematically as $f(a_0)f(b_0)<0$. The Intermediate Value Theorem then guarantees at least one $x^*\in(a_0,b_0)$ with $f(x^*)=0$. It supplies neither monotonicity nor uniqueness: a continuous function may cross the axis several times inside one bracket.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经典二分求根从三个前提开始：$a_0<b_0$，$f$ 在闭区间 $[a_0,b_0]$ 上连续，而且在排除端点根后

$$
f(a_0)f(b_0)<0.
$$

乘积为负就是“端点异号”的数学简写。由介值定理（Intermediate Value Theorem, IVT），存在至少一个 $x^*\in(a_0,b_0)$ 使 $f(x^*)=0$。这里没有单调性或唯一性结论：连续函数可以在同一括区间内穿过横轴多次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f(a_0)=0$ or $f(b_0)=0$, that endpoint is already a root and should be returned immediately. Separating endpoint roots from the strict opposite-sign test avoids rejecting a valid answer and keeps the later invariant in strict form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $f(a_0)=0$ 或 $f(b_0)=0$，该端点已经是根，应立即返回。把端点根与严格异号测试分开，既不会漏掉合法答案，也能让后续不变量保持严格形式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The Midpoint Update Preserves the Bracketing Invariant
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 中点更新保留括区间不变量

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $[a_n,b_n]$ begins an iteration with opposite endpoint signs, and form $m_n=a_n+(b_n-a_n)/2$. If $f(m_n)=0$, the midpoint is a root. Otherwise the midpoint shares the sign of exactly one endpoint: retain $[m_n,b_n]$ when it shares the sign of $f(a_n)$, and retain $[a_n,m_n]$ when it shares the sign of $f(b_n)$. The function remains continuous on the retained interval and its endpoints still have opposite signs, so the Intermediate Value Theorem again certifies at least one root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设第 $n$ 轮开始时区间 $[a_n,b_n]$ 满足

$$
f(a_n)f(b_n)<0.
$$

取中点

$$
m_n=a_n+\frac{b_n-a_n}{2}.
$$

若 $f(m_n)=0$，中点就是根，可以提前停止。否则 $f(m_n)$ 与两个端点中的一个同号：若它与 $f(a_n)$ 同号，就保留 $[m_n,b_n]$；若它与 $f(b_n)$ 同号，就保留 $[a_n,m_n]$。新区间上的函数仍连续，端点仍异号，所以介值定理再次保证其中至少有一个根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The actual invariant is: the function is continuous on the current closed interval, its endpoint signs differ, and the interval therefore contains at least one root. Taking the midpoint makes the next width exactly half the current width, but midpoint selection alone is not the correctness proof; correctness comes from retaining the certified half by sign. [[二分误差界|Bisection error bounds]] owns cumulative width, midpoint positional error, and required iteration counts; [[残差控制根误差|Residual Control of Root Error]] owns the conditions under which a function residual controls root position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就是算法真正拥有的不变量：“当前闭区间连续、端点异号、因而至少含一个根。”取中点使

$$
b_{n+1}-a_{n+1}
=\frac{b_n-a_n}{2},
$$

但“总是取中点”本身不是正确性证明；正确性来自每轮都按符号保留有证书的一半。累计区间宽度、中点位置误差与达到容差所需轮数见 [[二分误差界]]；[[求根残差|函数残差]]能否控制根位置见 [[残差控制根误差]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[循环不变量与终止|invariant]] is a statement established before the loop and preserved by every update. Here it certifies that a root survives; halving separately guarantees progress toward any positive width tolerance. An exact root need not be reached after finitely many steps. The word “discard” means that the retained half still has a certificate, not that the other half has been proved root-free.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[循环不变量与终止|循环不变量]]是开始前成立、每次更新后仍成立的命题。在这里，它证明根仍被保留；减半则另外保证能够达到任意正的区间宽度容差。有限步不一定恰好遇到精确根。“舍弃”只表示留下的一半仍有存在证书，不表示另一半已被证明没有根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the nested brackets approach one root
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么嵌套区间会逼近某个根

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If no exact zero is returned, the retained brackets are nested: each lies inside the previous one. Writing $L_0=b_0-a_0$ for the initial width, their widths are $L_0/2^n\to0$. To justify the limiting point without assuming a unique root initially, let $r=\sup\{a_n:n\ge0\}$, the [[上确界|least upper bound]] of the left endpoints. It exists by the [[实数的确界性质|completeness of the real numbers]] because this set is nonempty and bounded above by $b_0$. For every $n$, all left endpoints lie below $b_n$, so $a_n\le r\le b_n$. Therefore both endpoints and their midpoints [[序列收敛|converge to]] $r$. The endpoint signs keep the same orientation throughout: for example $f(a_n)<0<f(b_n)$. Continuity then gives $f(r)\le0$ from the left sequence and $f(r)\ge0$ from the right, hence $f(r)=0$. The reversed sign orientation gives the same conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若没有提前返回精确零点，保留下来的区间就层层嵌套，每个都在前一个之内，记初始宽度为 $L_0=b_0-a_0$，则宽度 $L_0/2^n\to0$。不必预先假设根唯一，也能论证极限点：令 $r=\sup\{a_n:n\ge0\}$，即左端点集合的[[上确界|最小上界]]。该集合非空，且有上界 $b_0$，所以[[实数的确界性质]]保证 $r$ 存在。对每个 $n$，所有左端点都不超过 $b_n$，故 $a_n\le r\le b_n$。因此两端点及中点都[[序列收敛|收敛到]] $r$。端点的符号朝向始终不变，例如 $f(a_n)<0<f(b_n)$；连续性使左端点序列推出 $f(r)\le0$，右端点序列推出 $f(r)\ge0$，于是 $f(r)=0$。符号朝向相反时结论相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There may have been many roots initially; shrinking brackets eventually select one. No other point can belong to every bracket because two distinct points have a positive distance while the widths tend to zero. This explains both convergence and the absence of a promise about which initial root will be chosen.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

初始区间内可以有多个根；不断收缩的区间最终选中其中一个。不会有另一个不同的点同时属于所有区间，因为两个不同点的距离为正，而区间宽度趋于零。这样既证明了收敛，也说明为什么算法没有承诺选中初始诸根中的哪一个。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Multiple Roots, Endpoint Roots, and Even-Multiplicity Roots
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 多根、端点根与偶重根

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **An endpoint is already a root:** return it before applying the strict opposite-sign test.
- **Several roots lie in the interval:** bisection promises to retain at least one of them but generally cannot specify which one will be obtained. Finding a particular root or all roots requires prior root isolation or another method.
- **An even-multiplicity root may not change sign:** $f(x)=(x-1)^2$ has a root at $1$ on $[0,2]$, while both endpoint values are positive. Classical sign-changing bisection has no existence certificate on this interval; that does not prove that no root is present.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **端点已经是根：** 先返回端点；不要让严格异号测试把合法答案误报为“没有括区间”。
- **区间内有多个根：** 二分法只承诺保留至少一个根，通常不能指定最后得到哪一个；若任务要求特定根或全部根，必须先隔离根或改用相应方法。
- **偶重根可能不变号：** $f(x)=(x-1)^2$ 在 $[0,2]$ 内有根 $1$，但两个端点值都为正。经典异号二分法无法从这个区间取得存在性证书；这不等于区间里没有根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity is equally indispensable. The jump function $f(x)=-1$ for $x<0$ and $f(x)=1$ for $x\ge0$ has opposite signs at $-1$ and $1$ but no zero. Mechanical interval halving shrinks toward the discontinuity at zero rather than toward a root. Opposite signs and continuity together form the complete Intermediate Value Theorem premise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续性也不能省略。令

$$
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
-1,&x<0,\\
1,&x\ge0.
\end{cases}
$$

它在 $[-1,1]$ 两端异号却没有零点。若仍机械二分，区间会缩向跳点 $0$，但这不是求根收敛。异号加连续性才是介值定理的完整前提。

## Boundary with Discrete Binary Search
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与离散二分查找的边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[二分查找|Binary search]] and bisection root-finding both discard half repeatedly, but they certify different objects. Discrete binary search works on ordered finite candidates or array indices; comparison with the target proves that one side is impossible, and exhausting the candidates proves absence from that finite set. Bisection root-finding works on a continuous real function; endpoint signs and the Intermediate Value Theorem prove only that the retained interval still contains at least one root. Failure to obtain an initial sign-changing bracket means that this certificate cannot start, not that the interval has no root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[二分查找]]与二分求根都反复排除一半，但证明对象不同。离散二分查找处理有序有限候选或数组索引；中点与目标的大小比较证明某一侧不可能含有目标，并能在候选耗尽时证明目标不在该有限集合中。二分求根处理实区间上的连续函数；端点符号和介值定理只证明新区间仍至少含一个根。初始区间没有异号，只表示这个证书不能启动，不能据此断言区间中没有根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the sign test meaningful in finite precision
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 有限精度下也要保住符号证据

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product $f(a)f(b)<0$ is mathematical shorthand. In code, first check endpoint zeros and finite values, then compare signs directly: multiplying two tiny nonzero outputs can [[浮点溢出与下溢|underflow]] to zero. A computed zero is an exact root of the mathematical function only when the evaluation itself is exact or separately certified; cancellation or underflow can create a floating-point zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘积 $f(a)f(b)<0$ 是数学简写。代码中应先处理端点零值与非有限值，再直接比较符号：两个很小的非零输出相乘可能[[浮点溢出与下溢|下溢]]成零。计算出的零值只有在求值精确或另有证明时，才是数学函数的精确根；消去或下溢也能产生浮点零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Neither $(a+b)/2$ nor $a+(b-a)/2$ is universally overflow-proof for arbitrary finite endpoints: the sum or the difference can exceed the available range. Use a suitable midpoint routine or rescale the problem, and check that the rounded result lies strictly inside the current bracket. [[浮点舍入|Rounding]] can make a midpoint equal an endpoint, especially when the endpoints are adjacent representable numbers. Stop with an honest tolerance or stagnation status; do not count a nonmoving step as another halving. The positional certificate and stopping details are developed in [[二分误差界|bisection error bounds]] and [[数值迭代停止条件|numerical stopping criteria]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意有限端点，$(a+b)/2$ 和 $a+(b-a)/2$ 都不是无条件防溢出的写法：和或差本身可能超出数值范围。应使用适合数值范围的中点算法或缩放问题，并检查舍入后的结果确实位于当前区间内部。[[浮点舍入]]可能把中点变成端点，尤其在两端已是相邻可表示数时。此时应如实报告达到容差或停滞，不能把没有移动的一步再计作一次减半。位置证书与停止细节见[[二分误差界]]及[[数值迭代停止条件]]。

> [!question]- Check the certificate · 检查存在证书
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> &nbsp;
>
> **1.** Why is taking a midpoint on every iteration insufficient to prove bisection correct?<br>
> **2.** Does the lack of opposite endpoint signs for $f(x)=(x-1)^2$ on $[0,2]$ prove that no root exists?<br>
> **3.** If endpoint signs differ but the function has a jump inside the interval, is a zero still guaranteed?
>
> **Answers:** 1. The method must retain the half whose endpoint signs still differ, so the Intermediate Value Theorem remains applicable; discarding an arbitrary half can lose every root. 2. No. The even-multiplicity root at $1$ exists even though the sign-change certificate fails. 3. No. A discontinuous function can jump from negative to positive without taking the value zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 1. 为什么“每轮取中点”还不足以证明二分求根正确？
> 2. $f(x)=(x-1)^2$ 在 $[0,2]$ 上没有端点异号。能否因此断言区间内没有根？
> 3. 一个函数在区间两端异号，但区间内有跳跃不连续。异号是否仍足以保证零点？
>
> **答案：** 1. 还必须根据符号保留端点异号的一半，才能继续使用介值定理；任意丢掉一半可能丢掉所有根。2. 不能；$x=1$ 是偶重根，只是异号括区间证书失效。3. 不能；跳跃函数可以从负值直接跳到正值而从不取零，连续性不可省略。
