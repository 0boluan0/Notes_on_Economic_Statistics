<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# From probability to distributional convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*依概率靠近为何足以保证分布靠近*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an approximation is usually close to its target on the **same outcome**, their probabilities of falling below a cutoff should also become close, except where the target has a probability mass exactly at that cutoff. This is the bridge from [[依概率收敛|convergence in probability]] to [[依分布收敛|convergence in distribution]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果一个近似在**同一个结果下**通常都很接近目标，那么两者落在某个阈值以下的概率也应越来越接近；需要避开的是目标恰好在该阈值放有正概率质量的情况。这就是从[[依概率收敛]]到[[依分布收敛]]的桥梁。

> [!important] The implication · 正向蕴含
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For real-valued random variables $X_n$ and $X$ on one probability space,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$X_n\xrightarrow pX\quad\Longrightarrow\quad X_n\Rightarrow X.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The limit may be random. No independence, density, or finite-moment assumption is needed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 同一概率空间上的实值随机变量满足这一蕴含。极限可以随机；不要求独立、有密度或矩有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Allow a small margin around the cutoff
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么正向一定成立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the cutoff is $10$ and the approximation error is at most $0.1$. If $X\le9.9$, then necessarily $X_n\le10$. Conversely, if $X_n\le10$, then necessarily $X\le10.1$. Only an error larger than $0.1$ can break these implications. The general argument uses a cutoff $x$ and a margin $\delta>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把阈值想成 $10$，允许的近似误差为 $0.1$。如果 $X\le9.9$，就一定有 $X_n\le10$；反过来，如果 $X_n\le10$，就一定有 $X\le10.1$。只有误差超过 $0.1$ 时，这些推断才可能失效。一般证明把阈值写成 $x$，余量写成 $\delta>0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $q_n(\delta)=P(|X_n-X|>\delta)$, the probability of excessive error. Write $F_n(x)=P(X_n\le x)$ and $F(x)=P(X\le x)$ for the two [[累积分布函数|cumulative distribution functions]]. The observations above give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $q_n(\delta)=P(|X_n-X|>\delta)$ 为超差概率，记 $F_n(x)=P(X_n\le x)$、$F(x)=P(X\le x)$ 为两者的[[累积分布函数]]。前面的观察给出

$$F(x-\delta)-q_n(\delta)\ \le\ F_n(x)\ \le\ F(x+\delta)+q_n(\delta).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the left inequality, remove the bad-error outcomes from $\{X\le x-\delta\}$; everything left belongs to $\{X_n\le x\}$. For the right inequality, $\{X_n\le x\}$ is contained in $\{X\le x+\delta\}$ together with the bad-error event. The [[并事件上界|union bound]] adds at most $q_n(\delta)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左边的不等式是：从 $\{X\le x-\delta\}$ 中排除超差结果，剩下的必在 $\{X_n\le x\}$ 中。右边是不等式：$\{X_n\le x\}$ 包含在 $\{X\le x+\delta\}$ 与超差事件的并中，[[并事件上界]]说明最多再加上 $q_n(\delta)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now choose a point $x$ where the **limit** CDF $F$ is continuous. Given any accuracy $\eta>0$, continuity lets us choose a fixed $\delta>0$ so that both $F(x-\delta)$ and $F(x+\delta)$ differ from $F(x)$ by less than $\eta$. Keep that margin fixed. Probability convergence then makes $q_n(\delta)<\eta$ for all sufficiently large $n$. The displayed inequalities give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在选取**极限**分布函数 $F$ 的一个连续点 $x$。给定任意精度 $\eta>0$，连续性让我们选出一个固定的 $\delta>0$，使 $F(x-\delta)$ 与 $F(x+\delta)$ 距离 $F(x)$ 都小于 $\eta$。保持这个余量不变，依概率收敛保证足够大的 $n$ 都有 $q_n(\delta)<\eta$。代入上式便得

$$F(x)-2\eta<F_n(x)<F(x)+2\eta.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $\eta$ was arbitrary, $F_n(x)\to F(x)$ at every continuity point of $F$. That is exactly distributional convergence. The order matters: first choose a small fixed margin, then let $n$ grow; probability convergence alone does not control a margin shrinking arbitrarily fast with $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $\eta$ 任意，$F_n(x)$ 在 $F$ 的每个连续点都趋于 $F(x)$，这恰好是依分布收敛。这里的顺序很重要：先选一个足够小但固定的余量，再增大 $n$；依概率收敛本身不能控制随 $n$ 任意快速缩小的余量。

> [!example] A jump explains the continuity restriction · 跳点解释了连续点限制
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take deterministic $X_n=1/n$ and $X=0$. They converge in probability because $1/n$ eventually falls below each fixed positive tolerance. Yet $F_n(0)=0$ for every $n$, whereas $F(0)=1$. The theorem remains valid: zero is a jump point of the limiting CDF and is excluded from the required comparisons.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取确定的 $X_n=1/n$、$X=0$。由于 $1/n$ 最终小于每个固定正容忍值，它们依概率收敛。但始终 $F_n(0)=0$，而 $F(0)=1$。这不违反定理：零是极限 CDF 的跳点，不在必须比较的点之列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[分布收敛不推概率收敛|reverse implication fails in general]] because a distribution does not specify which outcomes are paired. When the limiting law is concentrated at a [[常数分布极限推出概率收敛|constant]], the reverse implication does hold. Another standard proof uses almost-surely convergent further subsequences; the margin proof above avoids needing that additional subsequence theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般情况下，[[分布收敛不推概率收敛|反向蕴含失败]]，因为分布没有规定同一次结果如何配对。当极限分布集中在[[常数分布极限推出概率收敛|常数]]上时，在这个特例中，反向也成立。另一种标准证明使用几乎必然收敛的进一步子列；上面的余量证明使读者不必先掌握那条子列定理。
