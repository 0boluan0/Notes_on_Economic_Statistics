# Explosion criterion for a pure-birth chain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*纯生链的爆炸判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[纯生链|pure-birth chain]] must pass through every next level. To decide whether infinitely many births can fit into finite time, add the successive waiting times. The **reciprocal-rate criterion** replaces this random infinite sum by a deterministic series of mean waits. It is an exact test for this sequential structure, not a rule for summing rates across an arbitrary Markov chain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[纯生链]]必须依次经过每个后续层。要判断无限次出生能否挤进有限时间，就要把逐层等待相加。**倒数率判据**把这个随机无穷和的判断，转化为各层平均等待组成的确定性级数。它是针对逐级结构的精确判据，不是把任意 Markov 链所有状态的速率随意求和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a nonnegative integer starting level $X_0=n_0$. First assume every rate on the reachable tail is finite and strictly positive: $0<\lambda_n<\infty$ for all $n\ge n_0$. Let the independent holding times be $H_n\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda_n)$, so their [[期望|means]] are $1/\lambda_n$. The explosion time is the extended nonnegative sum $\zeta=\sum_{n=n_0}^{\infty}H_n$. [[CTMC爆炸|Explosion]] means $\zeta<\infty$; nonexplosion means that only finitely many births occur before each finite time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定非负整数起点 $X_0=n_0$。先假设整个可达尾部的速率均有限且严格为正：对所有 $n\ge n_0$，有 $0<\lambda_n<\infty$。令独立停留时间 $H_n\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda_n)$，所以其[[期望|均值]]为 $1/\lambda_n$。爆炸时刻是允许取无穷的非负和 $\zeta=\sum_{n=n_0}^{\infty}H_n$。[[CTMC爆炸|爆炸]]表示 $\zeta<\infty$；非爆炸则表示每个有限时刻以前都只出生有限次。

> [!important] Add the mean waits · 累加各层平均等待
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the minimal pure-birth chain under these assumptions, the following two equivalences determine its lifetime:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在这些假设下，对最小纯生链，下面两条等价确定它的寿命：
>
> $$\sum_{n=n_0}^{\infty}\frac1{\lambda_n}=\infty\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \Pr_{n_0}(\zeta=\infty)=1,$$
>
> $$\sum_{n=n_0}^{\infty}\frac1{\lambda_n}<\infty\ \Longleftrightarrow\ \Pr_{n_0}(\zeta<\infty)=1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $\Pr_{n_0}$ means probability when starting at $n_0$. A [[无穷级数|series]] here is the limit of its nonnegative partial sums. There is no intermediate explosion probability in this fixed-start, all-positive sequential model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\Pr_{n_0}$ 表示从 $n_0$ 出发的概率；这里的[[无穷级数|级数]]是非负部分和的极限。在固定起点、沿途全为正速率的逐级模型里，不会出现介于零与一之间的爆炸概率。

## Why an infinite mean needs a separate argument
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*无穷均值为什么还需要另一步证明*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Z_m=\sum_{n=n_0}^{m}H_n$. These nonnegative partial sums increase to $\zeta$. The [[单调收敛定理|monotone convergence theorem]] permits the expectation of their limit to equal the limit of their expectations, even if it is infinite:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $Z_m=\sum_{n=n_0}^{m}H_n$。非负部分和递增到 $\zeta$；[[单调收敛定理]]允许把极限的期望写成期望的极限，即使结果为无穷：

$$E[\zeta]=\sum_{n=n_0}^{\infty}E[H_n]=\sum_{n=n_0}^{\infty}\frac1{\lambda_n}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If this sum is finite, then $\zeta$ is finite almost surely: a nonnegative quantity that equals infinity with positive probability cannot have finite expectation. The reverse direction cannot follow merely from $E[\zeta]=\infty$; an almost-surely finite variable can have an infinite mean. Independence and the exponential law supply the missing step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果这个和有限，$\zeta$ 就几乎必然有限：一个以正概率等于无穷的非负量，不可能有有限期望。反方向却不能只凭 $E[\zeta]=\infty$ 推出，因为一个几乎必然有限的变量也可能有无穷均值。缺少的那一步要使用独立性和指数分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose any fixed $\theta>0$ with units of inverse time. The bounded transform $e^{-\theta Z_m}$ decreases to $e^{-\theta\zeta}$, with $e^{-\theta\infty}=0$. Integrating the [[指数分布|exponential density]] gives $E[e^{-\theta H_n}]=\lambda_n/(\lambda_n+\theta)$. Independence factors the finite product; then [[控制收敛定理|dominated convergence]], using the upper bound $1$, gives the formula below, where $\log$ is the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任取单位为时间倒数的固定数 $\theta>0$。有界变换 $e^{-\theta Z_m}$ 递减到 $e^{-\theta\zeta}$，约定 $e^{-\theta\infty}=0$。对[[指数分布|指数密度]]积分可得 $E[e^{-\theta H_n}]=\lambda_n/(\lambda_n+\theta)$。独立性让有限乘积的期望分解，再由上界为 $1$ 的[[控制收敛定理]]得到下式，其中 $\log$ 表示[[自然对数]]

$$E[e^{-\theta\zeta}]
=\lim_{m\to\infty}\prod_{n=n_0}^{m}\frac{\lambda_n}{\lambda_n+\theta}
=\lim_{m\to\infty}\exp\!\left(-\sum_{n=n_0}^{m}\log\left(1+\frac{\theta}{\lambda_n}\right)\right).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\sum_n1/\lambda_n$ diverges, the logarithmic sum also diverges. Indeed, either infinitely many $\theta/\lambda_n$ exceed $1$, each contributing at least $\log2$, or eventually $0\le u=\theta/\lambda_n\le1$, where $\log(1+u)\ge u/2$. Thus the product tends to zero. Since $e^{-\theta\zeta}$ is strictly positive whenever $\zeta$ is finite, its zero expectation forces $\zeta=\infty$ almost surely. This proves the other direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\sum_n1/\lambda_n$ 发散，上面的对数和也发散。因为要么有无穷多个 $\theta/\lambda_n>1$，每项至少贡献 $\log2$；要么最终 $0\le u=\theta/\lambda_n\le1$，此时 $\log(1+u)\ge u/2$。于是乘积趋于零。而只要 $\zeta$ 有限，$e^{-\theta\zeta}$ 就严格为正；它的期望为零，便强迫 $\zeta=\infty$ 几乎必然成立，完成另一个方向的证明。

## Fast growth is not enough by itself
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*仅仅速率无界还不够*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use one fixed time unit and start at $n_0=1$ in these examples. If $\lambda_n=n$, the reciprocal partial sums are [[调和数|harmonic numbers]] and diverge, so the chain is nonexplosive despite unbounded rates. If $\lambda_n=n^2$, [[p级数判别|the convergent square-reciprocal series]] gives explosion almost surely. If $\lambda_n=2^n$, the [[几何级数|geometric series]] has sum $1$, so $E[\zeta]=1$ time unit and explosion occurs almost surely. This is a mean lifetime, not a claim that every path explodes exactly at time $1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些例子都固定一个时间单位，并从 $n_0=1$ 开始。若 $\lambda_n=n$，倒数部分和是发散的[[调和数]]，所以即使速率无界，链仍不爆炸。若 $\lambda_n=n^2$，由[[p级数判别|平方倒数级数收敛]]，链以概率一爆炸。若 $\lambda_n=2^n$，[[几何级数]]之和为 $1$，所以 $E[\zeta]=1$ 个时间单位，并以概率一爆炸。这是平均寿命，不表示每条路径都恰好在时刻 $1$ 爆炸。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The slower boundary example $\lambda_n=n\log(n+1)$ is also nonexplosive. Here $\log$ is the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]]. For $n\ge2$, $\log(n+1)\le2\log n$, so the reciprocal series dominates $\tfrac12\sum_{n\ge2}1/(n\log n)$. The latter diverges by the [[级数积分判别|integral test]], since $\int_2^M dx/(x\log x)=\log\log M-\log\log2\to\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

边界例 $\lambda_n=n\log(n+1)$ 同样不爆炸，这里的 $\log$ 是[[自然对数]]。对 $n\ge2$，有 $\log(n+1)\le2\log n$，因此倒数级数逐项不小于 $\tfrac12\sum_{n\ge2}1/(n\log n)$。后者由[[级数积分判别]]发散，因为 $\int_2^M dx/(x\log x)=\log\log M-\log\log2\to\infty$。

> [!warning] Check the reachable route first · 先检查实际可达路线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a finite reachable level $m\ge n_0$ has $\lambda_m=0$, the chain reaches the first such level after finitely many finite waits and stays there: no explosion occurs. Do not apply the all-positive theorem to rates above that barrier. Starting above the barrier is a different problem. In particular, $\lambda_n=n$ started at $0$ is absorbed immediately; the harmonic example above starts at $1$. For a general CTMC, a random jump chain may revisit or avoid states, so an arbitrary enumeration of all reciprocal exit rates is not this criterion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若某个有限可达层 $m\ge n_0$ 满足 $\lambda_m=0$，链经过有限个有限等待后到达第一个这样的层，并永久停留，因此不爆炸。不能对屏障以上的速率套用全正率定理；从屏障以上出发则是另一问题。特别是 $\lambda_n=n$ 若从 $0$ 出发会立即吸收，上面的调和例从 $1$ 出发。一般 CTMC 的随机跳链可以重复访问或绕过状态，随意列出所有出口率的倒数，并不构成本判据。
