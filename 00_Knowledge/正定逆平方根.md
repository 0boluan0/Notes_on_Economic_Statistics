# 正定矩阵的逆平方根是其半正定主平方根的逆
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The inverse square root of a positive-definite matrix is the inverse of its positive-semidefinite principal square root*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 若实对称 $A\succ0$ 且 $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$，定义
> $$
> A^{-1/2}:=(A^{1/2})^{-1}
> =Q\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_i^{-1/2})Q^T.
> $$
> 复 Hermitian 情形把 $T$ 换成 $*$。它是正定矩阵，并满足 $A^{-1/2}AA^{-1/2}=I$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a positive-definite matrix, the inverse principal square root is obtained by replacing each positive eigenvalue $\lambda_i$ by $\lambda_i^{-1/2}$. It is positive definite and whitens $A$ to the identity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正定性保证每个 $\lambda_i>0$，所以开方以后仍可取倒数。谱坐标中
$$
A^{-1/2}AA^{-1/2}
=Q\Lambda^{-1/2}\Lambda\Lambda^{-1/2}Q^T=I.
$$
这正是白化、Mahalanobis 几何和加权内积中反复出现的标准化操作。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive definiteness makes every eigenvalue strictly positive, so the principal square root is invertible. In spectral coordinates the two inverse-half powers cancel $\Lambda$, producing the identity; this is the standardisation used in whitening and Mahalanobis geometry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A=\operatorname{diag}(4,1)$，则
$$
A^{1/2}=\operatorname{diag}(2,1),
\qquad
A^{-1/2}=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1),
$$
并且 $A^{-1/2}AA^{-1/2}=I$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A=\operatorname{diag}(4,1)$, the principal square root is $\operatorname{diag}(2,1)$ and the inverse square root is $\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1)$, which whitens $A$ to the identity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 奇异边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Singular boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A\succeq0$ 但有零特征值，主平方根仍存在，普通逆平方根却不存在。例如 $\operatorname{diag}(4,0)$ 的主平方根是 $\operatorname{diag}(2,0)$，但第二个方向无法取有限倒数。使用 Moore–Penrose 逆平方根属于另一种明确的广义逆口径，不能冒充普通逆平方根。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A singular positive-semidefinite matrix still has a principal square root but no ordinary inverse square root. A Moore-Penrose inverse square root is a separately declared generalised-inverse convention, not an ordinary inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么 $A\succeq0$ 还不足以定义普通 $A^{-1/2}$？
>
> **答案：** 半正定允许零特征值；对零特征值无法取有限的 $\lambda^{-1/2}$，所以必须进一步要求 $A\succ0$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Question:** Why is $A\succeq0$ insufficient for defining an ordinary $A^{-1/2}$?
>
> **Answer:** Semidefiniteness permits zero eigenvalues, for which $\lambda^{-1/2}$ is not finite. Positive definiteness is required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
