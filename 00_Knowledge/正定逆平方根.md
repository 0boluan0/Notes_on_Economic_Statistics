# The positive-definite inverse square root
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*正定逆平方根*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inverse square root rescales each eigendirection by the reciprocal of the square root of its positive stretch. For example, a direction stretched by $4$ is rescaled by $1/2$. Applying this rescaling on both sides of the matrix cancels the stretch: $(1/2)\cdot4\cdot(1/2)=1$. This is the algebra behind a symmetric whitening transformation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逆平方根沿每个特征方向，乘以该正伸缩量的平方根的倒数。例如原伸缩量为 $4$，就乘以 $1/2$。在矩阵两侧各施加一次这种缩放，会消去原伸缩：$(1/2)\cdot4\cdot(1/2)=1$。这就是对称白化变换背后的代数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A diagonal example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $\operatorname{diag}$ makes a [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] with the listed entries and zeros elsewhere. For
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\operatorname{diag}$ 表示把所列元素放在[[对角矩阵]]的对角线上，其余全零。取

$$A=\operatorname{diag}(4,1),\qquad
A^{1/2}=\operatorname{diag}(2,1),\qquad
W=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,1).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $A^{1/2}$ is the [[半正定主平方根|principal square root]], whose square is $A$. The matrix $W$ is its [[逆矩阵|inverse]]: $WA^{1/2}=A^{1/2}W=I_2$, where $I_2$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. Thus $W=A^{-1/2}$, and multiplication gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$A^{1/2}$ 是平方等于 $A$ 的[[半正定主平方根|主平方根]]。$W$ 是它的[[逆矩阵]]，满足 $WA^{1/2}=A^{1/2}W=I_2$，其中 $I_2$ 为[[单位矩阵]]。所以 $W=A^{-1/2}$，相乘得到

$$WAW=\operatorname{diag}(1,1)=I_2,\qquad
W^2=\operatorname{diag}(1/4,1)=A^{-1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The inverse square root and the inverse are different operations and need not give the same matrix: for an eigenvalue $\lambda>0$, their directional multipliers are $\lambda^{-1/2}$ and $\lambda^{-1}$ respectively.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逆平方根与求逆是不同运算，所得矩阵一般不同：对特征值 $\lambda>0$，沿该方向的乘数分别是 $\lambda^{-1/2}$ 和 $\lambda^{-1}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Definition and spectral formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*定义与谱公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n\ge1$ be a finite integer, and let $A$ be a real symmetric or complex [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]] [[正定矩阵|positive-definite matrix]] of size $n\times n$. Positive definite means $z^*Az>0$ for every nonzero $z$. The star denotes conjugate transpose and reduces to [[矩阵转置|transpose]] in the real case. Its [[正定与半正定的谱判据|positive spectrum]] permits the decomposition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n\ge1$ 为有限整数，$A$ 是 $n\times n$ 实对称或复 [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]] [[正定矩阵]]；正定指每个非零 $z$ 都满足 $z^*Az>0$。星号表示共轭转置，实数情形就是[[矩阵转置|转置]]。由[[正定与半正定的谱判据]]，可以分解为

$$A=Q\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n)Q^*,\qquad
Q^*Q=QQ^*=I_n,\quad \lambda_i>0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The columns $q_i$ of $Q$ are a complete [[标准正交组|orthonormal family]] of [[特征对|eigenvectors]], and $\lambda_i$ is the multiplier in direction $q_i$. The ordinary inverse square root is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$Q$ 的列 $q_i$ 构成一组完整的[[标准正交组]]，且都是[[特征对|特征向量]]，$\lambda_i$ 是沿 $q_i$ 方向的伸缩量。普通逆平方根定义为

> [!important] Invert the principal root · 对主平方根取逆
> $$A^{-1/2}:=(A^{1/2})^{-1}
> =Q\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1^{-1/2},\ldots,\lambda_n^{-1/2})Q^*.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every $\lambda_i^{-1/2}=1/\sqrt{\lambda_i}$ is finite and positive. Thus $A^{-1/2}$ is itself Hermitian positive definite; it is also the principal square root of $A^{-1}$, so $(A^{-1})^{1/2}=A^{-1/2}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个 $\lambda_i^{-1/2}=1/\sqrt{\lambda_i}$ 都有限且严格正，所以 $A^{-1/2}$ 本身为 Hermitian 正定矩阵。它也是 $A^{-1}$ 的主平方根，因此 $(A^{-1})^{1/2}=A^{-1/2}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These identities follow by multiplying the diagonal factors in the same eigenbasis. In particular, with $W=A^{-1/2}$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些恒等式都可以在同一个特征基中逐个相乘对角因子得到。特别地，令 $W=A^{-1/2}$，则

$$WAW=Q\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_i^{-1/2}\lambda_i\lambda_i^{-1/2})Q^*=I_n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The principal square root is unique, and so is its inverse. Choosing a different eigenbasis within a repeated eigenspace cannot change $W$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主平方根唯一，它的逆也唯一。因此在重特征空间内更换特征基，不会改变 $W$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Rescaling mixed coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*坐标相互混合时怎样缩放*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the nondiagonal matrix from the principal-root example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对主平方根例子中的非对角矩阵，

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}5&4\\4&5\end{pmatrix},\qquad
A^{1/2}=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
W=\frac13\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\-1&2\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Direct multiplication gives $WA^{1/2}=I_2$ and $WAW=I_2$. The eigenvalues of $A$ are $9,1$, so those of $W$ are $1/3,1$. The negative off-diagonal entries of $W$ do not violate positive definiteness: that condition concerns every quadratic value, not the signs of individual entries. Simply dividing each original coordinate by $\sqrt5$ would give $A/5$, which still has off-diagonal entries $4/5$ and is not the identity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直接相乘得到 $WA^{1/2}=I_2$、$WAW=I_2$。$A$ 的特征值为 $9,1$，所以 $W$ 的特征值为 $1/3,1$。$W$ 的非对角元为负并不违背正定：正定要求每个二次值为正，不要求每个元素为正。若只把原坐标分别除以 $\sqrt5$，得到的是 $A/5$，非对角元仍为 $4/5$，不是单位矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For real vectors this also explains the [[马氏距离|Mahalanobis rescaling]]: since $W^TW=W^2=A^{-1}$, one has $x^TA^{-1}x=\|Wx\|_2^2$, where $\|\cdot\|_2$ is the [[欧氏范数|Euclidean norm]]. The complex identity uses $x^*$ and $W^*$. These are algebraic identities and require no distributional assumption. A whitening matrix need not be this symmetric one: if $A=LL^*$ is a [[Cholesky 正定判据|Cholesky factorization]], then $L^{-1}A(L^{-1})^*=I_n$ too, although $L^{-1}$ generally differs from $W$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实向量，这也解释了[[马氏距离]]中的缩放：因为 $W^TW=W^2=A^{-1}$，所以 $x^TA^{-1}x=\|Wx\|_2^2$，其中 $\|\cdot\|_2$ 为[[欧氏范数]]。复数恒等式改用 $x^*$、$W^*$。这些是代数恒等式，不要求概率分布假设。白化矩阵也不必采用这个对称版本：若 $A=LL^*$ 为 [[Cholesky 正定判据|Cholesky 分解]]，则 $L^{-1}A(L^{-1})^*=I_n$，尽管 $L^{-1}$ 一般不同于 $W$。

## What a zero eigenvalue prevents
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 奇异边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A$ is positive semidefinite but [[正定与半正定可逆性|singular]], some eigenvalue is zero. Its principal square root still exists, but has the same zero direction and no ordinary inverse. For $A=\operatorname{diag}(4,0)$, the root is $\operatorname{diag}(2,0)$; a finite reciprocal of its second diagonal entry is impossible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A$ 半正定却[[正定与半正定可逆性|不可逆]]，至少一个特征值为零。主平方根仍存在，却有相同的零方向，不能取普通逆。$A=\operatorname{diag}(4,0)$ 的主平方根是 $\operatorname{diag}(2,0)$，第二个对角元不存在有限倒数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A separately declared [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse]] keeps that zero direction at zero. For this example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若另行明确采用 [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆]]口径，就把零方向仍设为零。对这个例子，

$$C=(A^{1/2})^+=\operatorname{diag}(1/2,0),\qquad
CAC^*=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)\ne I_2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The superscript $+$ denotes the pseudoinverse. This result is the [[AA+列空间投影|orthogonal projector onto the range]] of $A$, namely the first coordinate line. The same spectral calculation gives the range projector for any singular positive-semidefinite $A$. It does not recreate the missing directions and is not an ordinary inverse square root. Zero eigenvalues cannot be replaced by tiny positive values without changing the matrix being studied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上标 $+$ 表示伪逆。所得矩阵是 $A$ 的[[AA+列空间投影|列空间上的正交投影]]，这里就是第一条坐标轴。对任意奇异半正定 $A$，同样的谱计算也给出其列空间投影；它不能重建缺失方向，不能冒充普通逆平方根。把零特征值换成微小正数，会改变正在研究的矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even a strictly positive but small eigenvalue $\varepsilon$ produces the large multiplier $1/\sqrt\varepsilon$. The exact definition remains valid, while numerical use must account for input accuracy and the chosen computational method.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使特征值 $\varepsilon$ 严格正，只要很小，也会产生很大的乘数 $1/\sqrt\varepsilon$。精确定义仍成立，数值使用时则须考虑输入精度与计算方法。

## 来源与核验
