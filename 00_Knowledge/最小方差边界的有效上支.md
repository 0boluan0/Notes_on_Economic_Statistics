# 正定且允许卖空的均值方差模型中最小方差边界不低于全局最小方差收益的部分才有效
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In the positive-definite mean-variance model with unrestricted short selling, precisely the minimum-variance branch at or above the GMV mean is efficient.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $\Sigma\succ0$，$\mu$ 不是 $\mathbf1$ 的倍数，权重可行域只有 $\mathbf1^Tw=1$，不附加卖空或杠杆限制。沿用[[目标收益最小方差解]]的 $A,B,C,D$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume $\Sigma\succ0$, $\mu$ is not proportional to $\mathbf1$, and the allowed weights satisfy only $\mathbf1^Tw=1$, without short-sale or leverage limits. Using $A,B,C,D$ from the [[目标收益最小方差解|target-return solution]],
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mu_G=\frac BA,\qquad
v(r)=\frac1A+\frac A D(r-\mu_G)^2.
$$

[[最小方差边界]]中的 $w(r)$ [[均值方差有效前沿|有效]]，当且仅当 $r\ge\mu_G$。这包含 GMV 点本身，而不是只取严格高于它的部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[最小方差边界|minimum-variance portfolio]] $w(r)$ is [[均值方差有效前沿|efficient]] exactly when $r\ge\mu_G$. This includes the GMV point itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $r<\mu_G$，GMV 组合收益更高且方差严格更低，直接支配 $w(r)$。若 $r\ge\mu_G$，任何收益更高的目标 $q>r$ 都满足 $v(q)>v(r)$；相同收益 $q=r$ 又不能取得比 $v(r)$ 更小的方差。因此不存在支配它的可行组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $r<\mu_G$, the GMV portfolio has both higher mean and strictly lower variance, so it dominates $w(r)$. If $r\ge\mu_G$, every higher target $q>r$ requires $v(q)>v(r)$, while the same target cannot attain variance below $v(r)$. No feasible portfolio can therefore dominate it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\mu=(0.08,0.12)^T$、$\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}0.04&0.015\\0.015&0.09\end{bmatrix}$ 时，$\mu_G=9\%$。收益 $8\%$ 与 $10\%$ 的边界点方差都为 $0.04$，但前者位于非有效下支，后者位于有效上支。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the stated two-asset inputs, $\mu_G=9\%$. Frontier points at $8\%$ and $10\%$ both have variance $0.04$, but the former lies on the inefficient lower branch and the latter on the efficient upper branch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在横轴为方差、纵轴为收益的图中，上式给出横向抛物线；横轴改为标准差时满足 $\sigma^2-\tfrac AD(r-\mu_G)^2=1/A$，是双曲线的右支。不能脱离坐标和条件，把任意受约束边界统称同一条双曲线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With variance on the horizontal axis and mean vertically, the equation is a sideways parabola. With standard deviation horizontally, $\sigma^2-\tfrac AD(r-\mu_G)^2=1/A$ is the right-hand branch of a hyperbola. This geometry depends on the axes and model; arbitrary constrained frontiers need not share it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Sources and verification*

- [[目标收益最小方差解]]：配方得到几何式，保证 $A,D>0$；两资产例子独立代入核算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Zivot, p.17, Proposition 9(b), explicitly uses $\mu\ge\mu_G$ to distinguish efficient from inefficient frontier portfolios. The page was visually checked; this note proves the claim from the variance quadratic and includes equality at GMV.
- [[目标收益最小方差解|The target-return solution]] supplies $A,D>0$ and the quadratic, which is completed to a square. The two-asset example is independently recalculated.
