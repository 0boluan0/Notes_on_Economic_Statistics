# The direction of a change-of-basis matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*换基矩阵的方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A change of coordinates describes the **same vector** using a different basis. The main practical question is which coordinate column a given matrix expects as input. Read its columns: if they contain the new basis vectors written in old coordinates, the matrix takes **new coordinates to old coordinates**. Its inverse goes the other way.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换坐标是用另一组基描述**同一个向量**。实际计算时，首先要辨清给定矩阵接受哪一套坐标。读它的列：如果各列是新基向量在旧基下的坐标，这个矩阵就把**新坐标变成旧坐标**，逆矩阵才负责反方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, in the plane let the old basis be the usual horizontal and vertical unit vectors. Take new basis vectors $v'_1=(1,1)$ and $v'_2=(1,-1)$. New coordinates $(2,1)^T$ mean “two copies of $v'_1$ plus one copy of $v'_2$,” which gives the vector $(3,1)$ in old coordinates. The superscript $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]], turning the displayed row into a column.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，在平面中把通常的横纵单位向量作为旧基，取新基向量 $v'_1=(1,1)$、$v'_2=(1,-1)$。新坐标 $(2,1)^T$ 表示“两份 $v'_1$ 加一份 $v'_2$”，组合出的向量在旧坐标中为 $(3,1)$。上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把所列的一行变成一列。

$$P=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad P\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\end{pmatrix}
=2\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}+\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}3\\1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read what each column represents
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先读每一列的含义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional [[向量空间|vector space]] over $\mathbb F$, with $n\ge1$ finite. Choose two ordered [[基|bases]], old $\mathcal B=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ and new $\mathcal B'=(v'_1,\ldots,v'_n)$. The notation $[x]_{\mathcal B}$ means the unique coefficient column in $x=\sum_j c_jv_j$; changing the basis changes what each coefficient multiplies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $V$ 是标量域 $\mathbb F$ 上的 $n$ 维[[向量空间]]，其中 $n\ge1$ 有限。选两组有序[[基]]：旧基 $\mathcal B=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ 和新基 $\mathcal B'=(v'_1,\ldots,v'_n)$。记号 $[x]_{\mathcal B}$ 表示展开 $x=\sum_j c_jv_j$ 中唯一的系数列；改变基，就是改变每个系数所乘的向量。

> [!important] Columns determine the direction · 由列决定方向
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Define the square matrix
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 定义方阵
>
> $$P=\bigl(\,[v'_1]_{\mathcal B}\ \cdots\ [v'_n]_{\mathcal B}\,\bigr).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It converts new coordinates into old coordinates, and its [[逆矩阵|inverse]] converts back:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 它把新坐标变成旧坐标，其[[逆矩阵]]反向转换：
>
> $$\boxed{[x]_{\mathcal B}=P[x]_{\mathcal B'},\qquad
> [x]_{\mathcal B'}=P^{-1}[x]_{\mathcal B}.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To derive the first formula, write $x=\sum_j c_jv'_j$, so $[x]_{\mathcal B'}=(c_1,\ldots,c_n)^T$. Expressing each $v'_j$ in the old basis and collecting coefficients gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要推出第一个公式，先写 $x=\sum_j c_jv'_j$，于是 $[x]_{\mathcal B'}=(c_1,\ldots,c_n)^T$。把每个 $v'_j$ 展开到旧基中，再收集系数，得到

$$[x]_{\mathcal B}=\sum_j c_j[v'_j]_{\mathcal B}=P[x]_{\mathcal B'}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last equality is [[Ax的列组合解释|matrix multiplication as a combination of columns]]. This also proves why the inverse exists: every old coordinate column describes exactly one vector, and that vector has exactly one new coordinate column. Thus $c\mapsto Pc$ is a bijection. Equivalently, its columns are independent because a zero combination of their coordinate columns would give a zero combination of the new basis vectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一个等号就是[[Ax的列组合解释|矩阵乘法的列组合含义]]。这也说明逆为什么存在：每个旧坐标列描述唯一向量，该向量又有唯一的新坐标列，所以 $c\mapsto Pc$ 是一一对应。等价地，$P$ 的列线性无关，因为若坐标列的某个组合为零，相同系数就会把新基向量组合成零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the [[表示矩阵的列|column rule]] applied to the identity map: each vector is left unchanged, while the input and output descriptions use different bases. The label “change-of-basis matrix” alone is insufficient, because another author may give that name to $P^{-1}$ instead.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就是把[[表示矩阵的列|列规则]]用于恒等映射：每个向量保持不变，但输入、输出的描述采用不同基。只看“换基矩阵”这个名称还不够，因为另一位作者可能把反方向的 $P^{-1}$ 叫作换基矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Solve for the desired coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*求所需坐标就是解方程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the opening example, new coefficients $(a,b)^T$ give old coordinates $(a+b,a-b)^T$. If the old coordinates are $(s,t)^T$, solve $a+b=s$, $a-b=t$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头的例子中，新系数 $(a,b)^T$ 给出旧坐标 $(a+b,a-b)^T$。若已知旧坐标 $(s,t)^T$，就解 $a+b=s$、$a-b=t$：

$$a=\frac{s+t}{2},\qquad b=\frac{s-t}{2},\qquad
P^{-1}=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $(s,t)=(3,1)$ we recover $(a,b)=(2,1)$. Multiplying the old column by $P$ instead would give $(4,2)^T$, the wrong coefficients. In general, if $b$ is the given old coordinate column, find the new column $c$ by solving $Pc=b$. Writing $c=P^{-1}b$ states the exact solution; it does not require forming an explicit numerical inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $(s,t)=(3,1)$，就恢复 $(a,b)=(2,1)$。若误用 $P$ 乘旧坐标，会得到 $(4,2)^T$，并非所求系数。一般地，已知旧坐标列 $b$，求新坐标列 $c$ 就是解 $Pc=b$。写成 $c=P^{-1}b$ 是在表达精确解，不要求数值计算时显式构造逆矩阵。

> [!warning] Perpendicular is not yet unit length · 垂直还不等于单位长度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here the new basis vectors are perpendicular, but each has length $\sqrt2$. Accordingly $P^TP=2I$, and $P^{-1}=\tfrac12P^T$, not $P^T$. Two orthonormal bases under the same inner product give the special simplification: transpose over the reals, conjugate transpose over the complexes. See [[正交基换基矩阵|orthonormal changes of coordinates]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 本例的新基向量虽然垂直，长度却均为 $\sqrt2$，所以 $P^TP=2I$，$P^{-1}=\tfrac12P^T$，而非 $P^T$。同一内积下的两组标准正交基保证相应简化：实数域用转置，复数域用共轭转置。详见[[正交基换基矩阵]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an actual map $T:V\to W$, changing both input and output descriptions uses two coordinate conversions around its old matrix. The [[一般线性映射的换基|general change-of-basis formula]] keeps those two directions explicit. First identify what each column means; then the multiplication order follows from the coordinate path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于真正的映射 $T:V\to W$，同时改变输入、输出描述，需要在原矩阵两侧各作一次坐标转换。[[一般线性映射的换基]]明确区分这两个方向。先辨认每列的含义，再沿坐标转换路线确定乘法次序。
