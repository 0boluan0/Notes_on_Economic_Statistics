# 市场效率检验与正常收益模型构成联合假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A market-efficiency test is jointly a test of efficiency and the normal-return model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 异常收益只有相对于某个正常收益模型才存在。若检验拒绝零异常收益，数据可能说明信息没有及时进入价格，也可能说明 CAPM、市场模型、因子集合、市场代理或参数稳定性错误；单一检验不能无条件分开两者。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Abnormal return exists only relative to a normal-return model. A rejection of zero abnormal return may indicate that information was not promptly incorporated, or that the CAPM, market model, factor set, market proxy, or parameter-stability assumption is wrong. One test cannot separate these explanations unconditionally.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

联合假设不是停止实证研究的理由，而是结论格式的约束。研究应说明基准模型与代理，比较替代正常收益模型，并报告异常结果对模型选择是否稳健。未拒绝也只表示“该效率—模型组合与当前样本相容”，不是证明市场永远有效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The joint-hypothesis problem is not a reason to stop empirical work; it constrains the form of the conclusion. State the benchmark and proxy, compare alternative normal-return models, and report whether the anomaly is robust to model choice. Failure to reject means only that the efficiency–model pair is compatible with the current sample, not that the market has been proved efficient forever.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与 [[Alpha基准依赖性]] 的边界相连但不重复：Alpha 卡解释回归截距怎样随绩效基准改变；本卡解释为何基准依赖使市场效率本身不能被单独识别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This links to but does not duplicate [[Alpha基准依赖性|Alpha's benchmark dependence]]. That atom explains how a performance intercept changes with its benchmark; this atom explains why such dependence prevents market efficiency from being identified on its own.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 用 CAPM 得到显著正异常收益，能否同时断言“CAPM 被拒绝”且“市场无效”？
>
> **答案：** 不能把同一偏离无条件归给两个原因。它拒绝的是当前效率—CAPM—市场代理组合；要靠替代模型、设计证据与可实施性检查缩小解释范围。
