# 可抵押资产不足可能阻止优质项目融资因为项目质量不等于既有财富

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Insufficient pledgeable assets can block financing of a good project because project quality is not the same as pre-existing wealth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

项目即使在适当努力下有正的经济剩余，借款人也可能没有足够自有资金或担保物，同时满足出资人的回报要求和自己的[[借款人自有资金激励|激励约束]]。项目值得做与可签出双方愿意履行的融资合同，是两个不同问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even a project with positive economic surplus under diligence may lack sufficient own funds or collateral to satisfy both investor returns and the borrower's [[借款人自有资金激励|incentive constraint]]. Economic desirability and the existence of a mutually implementable financing contract are different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小对照是两个技术和努力能力相同的项目：贷款方案要求可执行抵押价值至少 20，一方只有 5，另一方有 30。前者不能通过这项资产门槛，不能据此认定其项目更差。这个对照只说明门槛的排除作用，不证明银行在所有可行替代合同下仍应拒绝前者。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider two projects with identical technology and effort capacity. A loan offer requires enforceable collateral worth at least 20; one applicant has 5 and the other 30. The former fails the asset threshold, but that does not establish inferior project quality. This contrast illustrates exclusion by a threshold, not rejection under every feasible alternative contract.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也不能直接推出取消抵押要求就会形成可持续贷款：须说明谁承担新增风险，能否以监督、担保或不同收益分配替代，以及这些安排的成本。资产不足导致的拒贷，亦不能未经条件核对就等同于[[信贷配给]]模型中的均衡现象。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Removing collateral requirements does not automatically produce sustainable lending. One must specify who bears added risk, whether monitoring, guarantees, or different payoff allocations can substitute, and their costs. Rejection due to insufficient assets is not automatically the equilibrium phenomenon in a [[信贷配给|credit-rationing]] model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
