# 汇率对企业价值的方向取决于报价、净币种敞口和经营融资反应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The effect of exchange rates on firm value depends on quotation, net currency exposure, and operating and financing responses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定[[汇率报价方向]]。若 $S$ 表示一单位外币的本币价格，$S$ 上升是本币贬值。再分别列外币销售收款、进口支出、债务支付和套保合约；“出口企业”或“进口企业”的标签不能代替净敞口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First fix the [[汇率报价方向|quotation convention]]. If $S$ is domestic currency per foreign unit, a rise in $S$ is domestic depreciation. Then list foreign-currency receipts, import payments, debt service, and hedges; an exporter or importer label is not a net exposure calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如一期、数量价格固定且未套保的公司收取外币 100、支付外币进口成本 70，净经营收款为 30。$S$ 从 7 到 7.7，使该项本币净收款从 210 到 231，增加 21；若外币收款只有 30、成本为 50，则同样贬值使净收款从 −140 变为 −154，恶化 14。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With fixed quantities and foreign prices and no hedge, receipts of 100 and import costs of 70 leave net foreign operating cash flow of 30. Moving $S$ from 7 to 7.7 raises its domestic value from 210 to 231. With receipts of 30 and costs of 50 instead, net cash flow deteriorates from −140 to −154.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述只隔离直接换算效应。完整价值还受提价能力、需求弹性、竞争者成本、合同重定价、外币债务和所需回报影响。现金流币种、通胀与贴现率须相容；不能把本币贬值一概解释为出口股利好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These examples isolate translation effects. Full value also depends on pass-through, demand elasticity, competitors' costs, repricing, foreign debt, and required returns. Cash-flow currency, inflation, and discount rates must be consistent; depreciation is not universally good for export stocks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
