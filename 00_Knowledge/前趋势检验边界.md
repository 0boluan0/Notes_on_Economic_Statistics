# 处理前趋势检验不能证明平行趋势

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pretreatment trend tests cannot prove parallel trends*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心区别
> 处理前图和 placebo 能暴露明显不相容，却不能观察处理后缺失的 $Y(0)$。所以“没有拒绝处理前系数为零”最多说明数据没有发现某类预先差异，不等于平行趋势已经成立。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Pretreatment plots and placebos can reveal clear incompatibility, but they cannot observe the missing post-treatment $Y(0)$. Failing to reject zero pretreatment coefficients therefore means only that the data did not detect a specified prior difference; it does not establish parallel trends.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个常规联合检验的原假设通常是若干处理前 event-time 系数都为零。$p$ 值大可能有两种原因：真实差异很小，也可能样本噪声大、处理组少或处理前时期短，检验几乎看不见有实质意义的偏离。“不显著”不是“足够小”，更不是“正好为零”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A conventional joint test usually takes several pretreatment event-time coefficients to be zero under the null. A large $p$-value may mean that the true difference is small, or it may mean that noise is high, treated groups are few, or the pretreatment window is too short to detect a meaningful departure. “Not significant” does not mean “small enough,” let alone “exactly zero.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 通过检验后再报告也会改变推断

若研究者只在 pretrend 检验通过时保留规格，最终样本不是随机得到的：它被“看起来平行”这一条件筛选过。Roth 证明，这种先检验再条件化的做法可能扭曲点估计，并使置信区间覆盖不足。因此，pretrend 检验不能当作一张自动放行证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a specification is retained only after it passes a pretrend test, the reported sample of estimates is selected for looking parallel. Roth shows that conditioning on such a pretest can distort point estimates and reduce confidence-interval coverage. A pretrend test is therefore not an automatic licence to proceed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 更有信息的做法

先从制度和机制解释为什么对照组应共享未处理趋势；展示原始水平、样本构成和处理前变化；报告检验对多大偏离有能力；用替代对照、伪处理时点和结果变量检查具体机制；最后说明结论对允许的小幅非平行趋势有多敏感。各项证据可以互相约束，却没有一项能单独“证明”反事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with an institutional account of why the comparison group should share the untreated trend. Show raw levels, composition, and pretreatment changes; report what size of deviation the test could detect; use alternative controls, placebo dates, and outcomes to probe specific mechanisms; and assess sensitivity to modest violations of parallel trends. These pieces can constrain the counterfactual together, but none proves it alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与辨别
> 三个处理前系数都不显著，能否写“平行趋势检验通过，因此假设成立”？应怎样更准确地表达？
>
> **答案：** 不能。应说样本未检测到所检验的处理前差异，同时报告置信区间或检验功效，并用设计逻辑和敏感性分析继续评估假设。

## 来源与核验

- Roth (2022), [*Pretest with Caution: Event-Study Estimates after Testing for Parallel Trends*](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aeri.20210236)：核验常规 pretrend 检验功效不足，以及条件化于预检结果会扭曲估计与推断。
- Sun & Abraham (2021), [*Estimating Dynamic Treatment Effects in Event Studies with Heterogeneous Treatment Effects*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.05785)：核验错位处理和效应异质时，传统事件研究的处理前系数本身也可能受其他时期效应污染。
