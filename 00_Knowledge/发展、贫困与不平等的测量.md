# 发展、贫困与不平等的测量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Measuring Development, Poverty, and Inequality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把“一个国家是否发展得更好”拆成可比较的收入、健康、教育、贫困和分配指标。
> **具体锚点：** 两个地区的贫困人口比例都是 20%，但一地的穷人只略低于贫困线，另一地远低于贫困线；headcount 相同，poverty gap 却不同。
> **核心难点：** 指标不是现实本身；不同价格口径、调查误差、非正规活动和分布形状会改变结论。
> **为什么重要：** 如果没有先说清“发展”和“贫困”怎样测，增长或政策是否成功就无法被准确判断。
> **继续：** 看长期差距为何存续，进入 [[制度、地理与长期发展差距]]；看产业和劳动力如何变动，进入 [[结构转型、生产率重配与非正规部门]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It turns the question “is a country developing?” into comparable measures of income, health, education, poverty, and distribution.
> **Concrete anchor:** Two regions can both have a 20% poverty headcount, yet poor households in one sit just below the poverty line while those in the other are far below it. Their headcounts match, but their poverty gaps do not.
> **Central difficulty:** An indicator is not reality itself. Price conventions, survey error, informal activity, and distributional shape can all change the conclusion.
> **Why it matters:** Unless development and poverty are defined and measured first, claims about growth or policy success cannot be judged precisely.
> **Continue:** For the persistence of long-run gaps, see [[制度、地理与长期发展差距|Institutions, Geography, and Long-Run Development Gaps]]. For sectoral and labor reallocation, see [[结构转型、生产率重配与非正规部门|Structural Transformation, Productivity Reallocation, and Informality]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 增长与发展不是同一问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Growth and development are not the same question*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经济增长通常指实际产出/人均产出增加，发展还包括健康、教育、贫困、机会、结构和制度。人均 GDP 是重要资源指标但不含分配、非市场活动和多维能力；HDI 等也只是特定加权摘要。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Economic growth usually means an increase in real output or real output per person. Development also includes health, education, poverty, opportunity, structure, and institutions. GDP per capita is an important resource measure, but it omits distribution, nonmarket activity, and multidimensional capabilities; composite measures such as the HDI are also particular weighted summaries rather than complete welfare measures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此测量要先回答决策问题：是比较可支配资源、实际生活结果、分布底部，还是机会的稳定性？对不同问题使用同一排名，会制造假精确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Measurement must therefore begin with the decision question: are we comparing command over resources, realized living outcomes, the bottom of the distribution, or the security of opportunities? Using one ranking for different questions creates false precision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 跨国比较的口径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conventions for cross-country comparison*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较需区分名义汇率和 PPP、总量和人均、水平和增长率。数据质量、非正规部门和价格篮子差异会影响低收入国家测量。国别平均掩盖国内地区与群体差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Comparison requires separating market exchange rates from purchasing-power parity, aggregates from per-capita values, and levels from growth rates. Data quality, informal activity, and differences in price baskets affect measurement especially in low-income economies. Country averages also hide regional and group differences within countries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，用市场汇率换算适合判断一国在国际市场上能购买多少；用 PPP 更适合比较本地消费品所支撑的生活水平。两种口径回答不同问题，不应为了得到想要的排名才临时选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, conversion at market exchange rates is useful for judging how much a country can buy on world markets, while purchasing-power parity is more suitable for comparing living standards supported by locally priced consumption. The two conventions answer different questions and should not be selected after seeing which ranking is preferred.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 贫困深度与不平等
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Poverty depth and inequality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

绝对/相对贫困、headcount、poverty gap 和多维贫困回答不同问题。Gini 压缩整个分布但对差异位置不敏感；增长是否减贫取决于增长率和分配变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Absolute and relative poverty, the headcount, the poverty gap, and multidimensional poverty answer different questions. The Gini coefficient compresses an entire distribution and is insensitive to where a particular transfer occurs. Whether growth reduces poverty depends on both the growth rate and distributional change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设贫困线为 $z$，样本中有 $q$ 人的收入 $y_i<z$，样本数为 $n$，则贫困人口比例是

$$H=\frac{q}{n},$$

平均标准化贫困缺口是

$$PG=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{z-y_i}{z}\mathbf 1(y_i<z).$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the poverty line be $z$. If $q$ of $n$ sampled people have income $y_i<z$, the poverty headcount is

$$H=\frac{q}{n},$$

and the average normalized poverty gap is

$$PG=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{z-y_i}{z}\mathbf 1(y_i<z).$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

算例：$z=100$，四人收入为 $40,90,120,150$。此时 $H=2/4=0.5$，而 $PG=[(100-40)/100+(100-90)/100]/4=0.175$。如果前两人收入都变为 90，headcount 仍是 0.5，poverty gap 却降到 0.05。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Worked example: let $z=100$ and four incomes be $40,90,120,150$. Then $H=2/4=0.5$, while $PG=[(100-40)/100+(100-90)/100]/4=0.175$. If the first two incomes both become 90, the headcount remains 0.5 but the poverty gap falls to 0.05.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 只报 GDP 增长，便宣称生活水平已普遍改善。
- 看到贫困 headcount 不变，便忽略贫困缺口已大幅缩小。
- 用名义美元和 PPP 口径的数字直接比较。
- 将一个综合指数的权重当成不存在价值判断的自然事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Reporting GDP growth alone and declaring that living standards improved broadly.
- Treating an unchanged poverty headcount as proof that poverty did not improve even when the poverty gap shrank sharply.
- Directly comparing numbers expressed at market-dollar and purchasing-power-parity conventions.
- Treating the weights in a composite index as value-free facts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么人均 GDP 增长不能完整代表发展？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can growth in GDP per person not fully represent development?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它不直接描述分配、健康、教育、权利、环境和非市场能力，且平均值可掩盖群体差异。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It does not directly describe distribution, health, education, rights, the environment, or nonmarket capabilities, and an average can hide group differences.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释 headcount 和 poverty gap 的差别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words the difference between the poverty headcount and the poverty gap.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> Headcount 只问多少人低于贫困线；poverty gap 还问他们平均离贫困线有多远。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The headcount asks only how many people fall below the line. The poverty gap also asks how far below the line they are on average.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 什么时候应优先用 PPP，什么时候应优先用市场汇率？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When should purchasing-power parity be preferred, and when should market exchange rates be preferred?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 比较本地生活水平时 PPP 通常更合适；判断国际购买力、外债或进口能力时市场汇率更相关。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Purchasing-power parity is generally better for comparing locally supported living standards. Market exchange rates are more relevant to international purchasing power, external debt, or import capacity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
