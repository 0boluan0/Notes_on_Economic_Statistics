# 风险中性定价是用与交易资产价格相容的定价概率计算贴现现金流价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Risk-neutral pricing values discounted cash flows using probabilities consistent with traded asset prices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

风险中性定价（risk-neutral pricing）使用一组与当前交易资产价格相容的概率权重 $Q$，计算未来支付的贴现期望。**它改变的是定价权重，不是断言现实投资者不厌恶风险。** 以下先考虑无中间支付、可由 [[自融资策略]] 复制的到期现金流 $H_T$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Risk-neutral pricing uses probabilities $Q$ consistent with traded asset prices to value discounted future payments. These are pricing weights, not a claim about investors' actual risk preferences. First consider a terminal payment $H_T$ replicated by a [[自融资策略|self-financing strategy]], with no intermediate payments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以无风险现金账户 $B_t$ 为计价基准，在合适的无套利模型下，复制组合的贴现价值是 $Q$ 鞅，即条件期望保持当前贴现值。因此

$$V_t=B_t\,E^Q\!\left[\frac{H_T}{B_T}\mid\mathcal F_t\right].$$

若利率 $r$ 为常数，则化为 $V_t=e^{-r(T-t)}E^Q[H_T\mid\mathcal F_t]$。随机利率下，贴现因子一般须留在期望内；有中间支付时逐笔加入现金流。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the cash account as numeraire makes the discounted replicating value a $Q$ martingale under the model's conditions, giving the formula above. Constant $r$ allows discounting outside the expectation; stochastic discounting generally stays inside, and intermediate payments must be included separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看一个可亲手验证的一期模型：股票现在 100，到期为 120 或 80，现金增长倍数为 1.05。定价权重须满足 $100=(120p_Q+80(1-p_Q))/1.05$，解得 $p_Q=0.625$。执行价 100 的看涨支付为 20 或 0，所以价值为 $0.625\times20/1.05=11.904762$。这也等于持有 0.5 股、借入 $40/1.05$ 的复制成本：两种状态都恰好复制支付。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a one-period model, a stock moves from 100 to 120 or 80 and cash grows by 1.05. Price consistency gives $p_Q=0.625$. A strike-100 call is worth $0.625\times20/1.05=11.904762$, equal to the cost of half a share financed by borrowing $40/1.05$. Both terminal payments are exactly replicated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

真实上涨概率可以不同，不能把 $0.625$ 当成预测。不可复制的支付在不完备市场中可能没有唯一无套利价格，须另作模型与校准选择；公式不保证任何模型都与真实市场完全吻合。预测与条件估值如何一起使用，见 [[风险模拟P-Q分工]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The actual up probability can differ. Unreplicable claims in incomplete markets may lack a unique arbitrage-free value and need additional modeling and calibration. This formula does not guarantee an exact market fit. See [[风险模拟P-Q分工|the P/Q division in risk simulation]] for combining forecasting with valuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*

- Haugh, [*A Brief Review of Derivatives Pricing & Hedging*](https://www.columbia.edu/~mh2078/QRM/DerivativesReview.pdf), pp. 2–6, supports self-financing, numeraires, martingale pricing, replication and incomplete markets. The finite-model fundamental theorem is not extended unconditionally to every continuous-time model.
- Kohn–Allen, [*Derivative Securities*, Section 4](https://math.nyu.edu/~kohn/derivative.securities/2007/section4.pdf), pp. 5–7, supports binomial pricing weights and their continuous limit. The one-period example was constructed separately and checked in each state.
