# Choosing a convergence test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*如何选择级数判别法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a series has no obvious sum formula, the useful first question is often whether its running totals settle at all. **Choosing a convergence test** means looking for a feature that a theorem can use: a known comparison, a simple ratio, a recognizable cancellation, or controlled alternating signs. The expression suggests a route; checking that route's hypotheses makes it a proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

级数没有显然的求和公式时，先问累计值能否稳定，通常更有用。**选择级数判别法**就是寻找定理可以利用的特征：已知的比较对象、容易化简的比值、可识别的消项结构，或受控制的交错符号。表达式提示路线，核对路线的条件才形成证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an [[无穷级数|infinite series]] $\sum a_n$, convergence concerns the finite partial sums $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N a_n$. Decide whether you need only convergence, a classification as [[绝对收敛|absolute]] or [[条件收敛|conditional]], an exact sum, or a numerical error bound. These are different questions. A successful convergence test often gives no exact value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[无穷级数]] $\sum a_n$，收敛说的是有限部分和 $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N a_n$ 的极限。先分清需要的是收敛与否、[[绝对收敛]]或[[条件收敛]]的分类、精确和，还是数值误差界。这些问题不同，成功的收敛判别往往并不给精确值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Take the inexpensive information first
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先利用容易看出的信息*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check that every included term is defined and finite. For example, $1/(n^2-1)$ needs a valid starting index such as $n=2$; a theorem about tails cannot repair an included $1/0$. If it is easy to see that $a_n$ fails to tend to zero, the [[级数通项判别|nth-term test]] proves divergence immediately. Terms tending to zero leave the question open. An uncomputed limit is not evidence that the terms fail to vanish; another test may settle the series directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确认纳入的每项都有定义且有限。例如 $1/(n^2-1)$ 需要从 $n=2$ 之类的合法索引开始；尾部定理不能修复已纳入的 $1/0$。若容易看出 $a_n$ 不趋零，[[级数通项判别]]立即给出发散；通项趋零则仍未决定。尚未算出极限，不等于证明了通项不趋零；也可以直接用另一个判别解决。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recognize familiar structures before doing a long calculation. In a [[几何级数|geometric series]], each term is the preceding term times a fixed factor; a [[p级数判别|p-series]] has terms $1/n^p$ with fixed $p$, and a [[望远镜级数|telescoping series]] simplifies through cancellation in finite partial sums. Keep each structure's boundary conditions: a zero geometric coefficient gives the zero series; a telescoping expression still needs the limit of its remaining boundary terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长算之前，先识别熟悉结构：[[几何级数]]有固定公比；[[p级数判别|p级数]]是固定指数的 $1/n^p$；[[望远镜级数]]在有限部分和中消项。每种结构仍有自己的边界：几何项系数为零时就是零级数；望远镜消项后，仍要检查留下的首尾表达式的极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Match the structure to a usable hypothesis
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把结构与真正适用的条件配起来*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The following routes concern nonnegative terms, or the magnitudes $|a_n|$ when testing absolute convergence. More than one may work; choose the one whose hypotheses and algebra are easiest to verify.

| Visible feature | A useful route and the condition to check |
|---|---|
| Each term has a convenient bound | [[级数直接比较判别\|Direct comparison]]: eventually nonnegative; a convergent upper bound or divergent lower bound. |
| Terms resemble a known power or geometric tail | [[级数极限比较判别\|Limit comparison]]: a positive comparison denominator and a finite positive limiting ratio give equivalence. |
| A corresponding curve has a simple integral | [[级数积分判别\|Integral test]]: a continuous, positive, nonincreasing function on an entire tail interval, matching the sampled terms. |
| Adjacent division cancels factorials or products | [[级数比值判别\|Ratio test]]: nonzero tail denominators and an existing absolute-ratio limit; below one proves absolute convergence, above one proves divergence. |
| The whole term is an nth power | [[级数根值判别\|Root test]]: nth-root magnitudes; the limsup version also permits zeros and roots without an ordinary limit. Below one proves absolute convergence, above one divergence. |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面的路线用于非负项；若要检验绝对收敛，就用于幅度 $|a_n|$。可能有多条路线都有效，选条件与代数最容易核对的一条即可。

| 可见结构 | 可尝试的路线及条件 |
|---|---|
| 每项容易被已知量控制 | [[级数直接比较判别]]：最终非负；收敛找上界，发散找下界。 |
| 通项像已知幂函数或几何尾部 | [[级数极限比较判别]]：比较分母为正，比值趋于有限正数时同敛散。 |
| 对应曲线容易积分 | [[级数积分判别]]：整个尾区间上连续、正、单调不增，且整数采样等于通项。 |
| 相邻相除可消去阶乘或乘积 | [[级数比值判别]]：尾部除数非零，绝对值比存在极限；小于一得绝对收敛，大于一得发散。 |
| 整项是某表达式的n次幂 | [[级数根值判别]]：看幅度的n次根；上极限版本还允许零项及根值没有普通极限。小于一得绝对收敛，大于一得发散。 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The root test's “limsup” measures the eventual upper level of these root magnitudes, using the suprema of successive tails; the [[数列上极限|limit-superior definition]] explains it. A limit or limsup equal to one is inconclusive. For $1/n^2$, both the adjacent ratio and nth-root magnitude tend to one, but the p-series criterion proves convergence. The same test values occur for the divergent harmonic series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

根值判别中的“上极限”通过依次截去有限前缀、考察剩余尾部上确界，描述根值最终的上方水平；[[数列上极限]]中会解释。比值极限或根值上极限等于一时，不能判定。$1/n^2$ 的相邻比值与n次根都趋一，但p级数判别立即证明收敛；发散的调和级数也具有相同的这两个判别值。

> [!example] Let the algebra explain the choice · 让代数解释为什么选它
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $a_n=n/(n^3+1)$, compare with $b_n=1/n^2$: both are positive and $a_n/b_n=n^3/(n^3+1)\to1$. Since the comparison p-series converges, so does the target. For $a_n=2^n/n!$, where $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$, adjacent division gives $a_{n+1}/a_n=2/(n+1)\to0$. The ratio test is convenient because the factorials cancel. Neither argument has computed the exact sum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $a_n=n/(n^3+1)$，取 $b_n=1/n^2$：两者为正，且 $a_n/b_n=n^3/(n^3+1)\to1$。比较的p级数收敛，所以目标也收敛。对 $a_n=2^n/n!$，其中 $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$，相邻相除得到 $a_{n+1}/a_n=2/(n+1)\to0$。比值法方便，是因为阶乘恰好消去；这两个论证都没有求精确和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a_n=[3/(n+1)]^n$, taking the nth root directly gives $3/(n+1)\to0$, so the root test proves convergence. For $1/[n(\ln n)^2]$ starting at $n=2$, the [[级数积分判别|integral-test example]] checks the full curve's decreasing behaviour and evaluates its integral. The presence of a logarithm alone would not justify choosing that test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $a_n=[3/(n+1)]^n$，直接开n次根就得到 $3/(n+1)\to0$，根值判别因而证明收敛。对从 $n=2$ 开始的 $1/[n(\ln n)^2]$，[[级数积分判别|积分判别例子]]会核对整个曲线的递减性并计算积分；仅仅看到对数，并不足以证明积分法适用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep signs and the requested conclusion in view
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*始终区分符号与所需结论*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\sum|a_n|$ converges, [[绝对收敛推出收敛|absolute convergence implies convergence]] finishes the original convergence question. If it diverges, the original series may still converge through cancellation. For alternating real signs, the [[交错级数判别|alternating test]] requires nonnegative magnitudes that eventually never increase and tend to zero. If only convergence is requested and those conditions are obvious, it is reasonable to use that test immediately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\sum|a_n|$ 收敛，由[[绝对收敛推出收敛]]即可完成原级数的收敛判断。若幅度级数发散，原级数仍可能靠抵消收敛。对实数项的交错符号，[[交错级数判别]]要求非负幅度最终不增且趋零；如果只问收敛，而这些条件显然成立，就可以直接使用它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To classify $\sum(-1)^{n-1}/n$, the alternating test first proves convergence; the divergent harmonic magnitude sum then proves that it is conditional. In contrast, $\sum(-1)^{n-1}/n^2$ is absolute by the p-series test applied to magnitudes. Alternating signs alone do not distinguish the two. If the magnitudes fail eventual monotonicity, failure of this sufficient test does not itself prove divergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要分类 $\sum(-1)^{n-1}/n$，先用交错判别证明收敛，再用调和幅度级数发散证明它条件收敛。相对地，$\sum(-1)^{n-1}/n^2$ 的幅度由p级数判别收敛，因此绝对收敛；只有交错符号不能区分两者。幅度不满足最终单调性时，也只是这个充分判别失效，并非已经证明发散。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once convergence is established, a numerical task needs an additional estimate: [[交错级数误差界|alternating remainder bounds]] control a suitable alternating tail, while [[积分判别余项界|integral remainder bounds]] control a suitable positive decreasing tail. State the actual conclusion and its assumptions. “The test gave one” or “I could not find a comparison” means that this route has not settled the question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明收敛后，数值题还需要误差估计：[[交错级数误差界]]控制符合条件的交错尾部，[[积分判别余项界]]控制符合条件的正递减尾部。写清实际得到了什么结论、依赖什么条件。“判别值是一”或“暂时没找到比较对象”，都只表示这条路线尚未解决问题。
