# 选择级数判别法应先看通项与结构再核对符号和所需结论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Choose a series test by inspecting the terms and structure, then checking signs and the conclusion needed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择判别法是一条决策程序：先找成本低的决定性信息，再根据通项结构选有适用条件的定理，最后区分得到的是收敛、绝对收敛、条件收敛，还是尚无结论。没有一种判别法覆盖所有级数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choosing a test is a decision procedure: seek inexpensive decisive information, match the term structure to a theorem whose hypotheses hold, and identify whether it proves convergence, absolute convergence, conditional convergence, or nothing yet. No test covers every series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确认每项有定义。若能直接看出 $a_n\not\to0$，用 [[级数通项判别]] 停止；$a_n\to0$ 则继续。若已是 [[几何级数]]、[[p级数判别|p级数]]，或有限部分和能发生 [[望远镜级数|望远镜抵消]]，直接使用该结构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First check that every term is defined. If $a_n\not\to0$ is immediate, finish with the [[级数通项判别|term test]]; if the terms approach zero, continue. Recognise [[几何级数|geometric series]], [[p级数判别|p-series]], and [[望远镜级数|telescoping partial sums]] before trying more general tools.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于最终非负的项，按下面的可识别结构选工具；对变号项，可先把这一选择用于 $|a_n|$ 来检验绝对收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For eventually nonnegative terms, match the recognisable structure below to a tool. For signed terms, this selection can first be applied to $|a_n|$ to test absolute convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 通项结构 | 优先尝试与需要核对的条件 |
|---|---|
| 能被已知级数逐项夹住 | [[级数直接比较判别]]；收敛找上界，发散找下界 |
| 主导项像 $n^{-p}$ 或 $r^n$ | [[级数极限比较判别]]；分母最终正，比例极限为有限正数 |
| 对应函数容易积分 | [[级数积分判别]]；尾区间上连续、正、单调不增 |
| 阶乘或相邻项相除会大量消去 | [[级数比值判别]]；尾项非零，绝对值比极限存在 |
| 整体有 $n$ 次幂或指数尺度明显 | [[级数根值判别]]；计算绝对值的 $n$ 次根上极限 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Term structure | First choice and checks |
|---|---|
| A known series bounds the terms | [[级数直接比较判别\|Direct comparison]]; use an upper bound for convergence and a lower bound for divergence |
| Leading behaviour resembles $n^{-p}$ or $r^n$ | [[级数极限比较判别\|Limit comparison]]; require an eventually positive denominator and a finite positive ratio limit |
| The associated function integrates easily | [[级数积分判别\|Integral test]]; check continuity, positivity, and nonincreasing behaviour on a tail interval |
| Adjacent division cancels factorials or products | [[级数比值判别\|Ratio test]]; require nonzero tail terms and an existing absolute-ratio limit |
| An nth power or exponential scale is visible | [[级数根值判别\|Root test]]; evaluate the limsup of nth-root magnitudes |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若绝对值级数已证明收敛，由 [[绝对收敛推出收敛]] 完成。若绝对值级数发散，原级数仍可能收敛；若符号交替，检查 [[交错级数判别]] 的幅度最终单调趋零条件。只有同时得到“原级数收敛”和“绝对值级数发散”，才能认定 [[条件收敛]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the absolute-value series converges, [[绝对收敛推出收敛|absolute convergence implies convergence]] completes the argument. If it diverges, the original series may still converge. For alternating signs, check the eventually decreasing-to-zero magnitudes required by the [[交错级数判别|alternating test]]. [[条件收敛|Conditional convergence]] requires both convergence of the original series and divergence of its absolute-value series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比值或根值等于 1 表示换方法，不能写成发散。例如 $\sum1/n^2$ 的这两个判别都无结论，但 $p=2$ 立即给出收敛。若题目还要求数值误差，证明收敛之后再用 [[交错级数误差界]] 或 [[积分判别余项界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A ratio or root value of one calls for another method, not a divergence conclusion. Both tests are inconclusive for $\sum1/n^2$, while $p=2$ settles convergence immediately. If a numerical error is required, follow the convergence proof with [[交错级数误差界|alternating remainder bounds]] or [[积分判别余项界|integral remainder bounds]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
