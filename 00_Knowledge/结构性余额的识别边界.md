# 结构性余额依赖潜在产出和调整方法的估计因而不能直接识别政策的因果效果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Structural balances depend on estimated potential output and adjustment conventions and therefore do not directly identify the causal effects of policy.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[结构性预算余额]]是模型辅助指标：潜在产出修订、税收弹性变化、收入构成和一次性项目处理都可能改变估计值。结构性余额变动因此不能直接等同于政府新采取了多少政策，更不能直接等同于政策造成的产出变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[结构性预算余额|structural balance]] is model-based. Revisions to potential output, elasticities, revenue composition or one-off treatment can alter it. Its change is neither a direct measure of new policy measures nor the resulting change in output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用[[周期调整预算余额]]的教学参数 $Y=95,R=38,G=42$、收入弹性 1、支出弹性 0：潜在产出估计为 100 时 CAB=−2；若仅把潜在产出估计修订为 98，调整收入变成 39.2，CAB=−2.8。观测收支没有变化，指标却变化了 −0.8。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using the [[周期调整预算余额|CAB example]] with $Y=95,R=38,G=42$, unit revenue elasticity and zero expenditure elasticity, estimated potential output of 100 gives CAB=−2. Revising potential output alone to 98 gives adjusted revenue of 39.2 and CAB=−2.8. Observed fiscal amounts are unchanged, yet the estimate changes by −0.8.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际判断应保留[[经济数据的实时口径]]，报告估计方法和敏感性，并用具体税支措施核对主动政策变化。要判断需求效果，还需说明政策构成、融资与货币条件等传导机制；这属于[[财政政策]]的效果分析，而不是把结构性余额变化直接乘上固定乘数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Preserve [[经济数据的实时口径|real-time data vintages]], disclose methods and sensitivities, and check specific tax and spending measures. Demand effects additionally require composition, financing and monetary conditions, as in [[财政政策|fiscal-policy analysis]], rather than a fixed multiplier applied mechanically to a structural-balance change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
