# 采用奇异值阈值口径时，给定容差 τ 后矩阵的数值秩是满足 σ_i>τ 的奇异值个数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Under a singular-value-threshold convention, the numerical rank at tolerance τ is the number of singular values satisfying σ_i>τ*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 若 $A$ 的奇异值为 $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_p\ge0$，并预先给定绝对阈值 $\tau\ge0$，则奇异值阈值口径定义
> $$
> \operatorname{rank}_\tau(A)
> =\#\{i:\sigma_i>\tau\}.
> $$
> 它记录在已声明的尺度、精度与判零规则下被视为可分辨的方向数；它不是一个脱离容差的新代数不变量。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the singular values of $A$ are $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_p\ge0$ and an absolute threshold $\tau\ge0$ has been fixed in advance, the singular-value-threshold convention defines $\operatorname{rank}_\tau(A)=\#\{i:\sigma_i>\tau\}$. It records how many directions count as resolvable under the stated scale, precision, and zero-classification rule; it is not a new tolerance-free algebraic invariant.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[奇异值与秩|代数秩]]严格计数 $\sigma_i>0$ 的方向，是精确矩阵自身的性质。数值秩则回答一个带口径的问题：在当前误差尺度下，哪些正奇异值仍应与零区分。两者可以不同，但只有在容差和判定规则均已声明时，这个差异才可复核。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[奇异值与秩|Algebraic rank]] counts directions with $\sigma_i>0$ and is a property of the exact matrix. Numerical rank answers a convention-dependent question: under the current error scale, which positive singular values should still be distinguished from zero? The two ranks may differ, but the difference is reproducible only when both the tolerance and the classification rule are stated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 绝对阈值与相对阈值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Absolute and relative thresholds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

容差可以直接给成绝对阈值 $\tau$，也可以按最大奇异值缩放，例如
$$
\tau=\mathrm{rcond}\,\sigma_1.
$$
相对口径会随矩阵整体缩放而调整，但 `rcond` 仍是需要选择和报告的参数。它应与数据误差、变量单位、机器精度和下游损失相匹配；软件默认值只是一个可执行口径，不是数据自动宣布的唯一“真实秩”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tolerance may be an absolute threshold $\tau$ or a relative threshold such as $\tau=\mathrm{rcond}\,\sigma_1$. A relative convention adapts to an overall rescaling of the matrix, but `rcond` remains a parameter that must be chosen and reported. It should reflect data error, variable units, machine precision, and downstream loss. A software default is an executable convention, not a unique “true rank” revealed automatically by the data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## SVD 阈值与 CPQR 估计不是同一判定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An SVD threshold and a CPQR estimate are not the same rank test*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

LAPACK `DGELSD` 直接计算奇异值，把满足
$$
\sigma_i\le \mathrm{RCOND}\,\sigma_1
$$
的方向视为零；若 `RCOND<0`，它使用机器精度作为默认相对阈值。因此其有效秩正是上述相对奇异值阈值的计数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
LAPACK `DGELSD` computes the singular values directly and treats directions satisfying $\sigma_i\le\mathrm{RCOND}\,\sigma_1$ as zero. If `RCOND<0`, it uses machine precision as the default relative threshold. Its effective rank is therefore exactly a count under the relative singular-value-threshold convention above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

列主元 QR 路线并不直接应用同一奇异值计数。LAPACK `DGELSY` 通过领先三角块的条件估计和 `RCOND` 选择有效秩；这是实用的秩估计，但不保证逐个矩阵都与 SVD 阈值计数相同。普通 CPQR 能提供什么、不能保证什么，见 [[CPQR秩揭示边界]]。因此报告“数值秩”时还应说明使用了哪种分解与判定规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A column-pivoted QR route does not apply the same singular-value count directly. LAPACK `DGELSY` selects an effective rank from `RCOND` and a condition estimate of the leading triangular block. This is a practical rank estimate, but it is not guaranteed to agree matrix by matrix with the SVD-threshold count. See [[CPQR秩揭示边界|the CPQR rank-revealing boundary]] for what ordinary CPQR does and does not guarantee. A reported numerical rank should therefore identify the factorization and decision rule as well as the tolerance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,10^{-8})$ 的代数秩始终为 $2$。在 SVD 阈值口径下，若 $\tau=10^{-6}$，数值秩为 $1$；若 $\tau=10^{-10}$，数值秩为 $2$。改变阈值会改变 [[截断SVD反演]] 所保留的方向及其解范数；[[岭的SVD滤波公式|岭正则化]]则连续收缩方向，并不通过离散数值秩决定哪些方向保留。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A=\operatorname{diag}(1,10^{-8})$, the algebraic rank is always $2$. Under the SVD-threshold convention, the numerical rank is $1$ when $\tau=10^{-6}$ and $2$ when $\tau=10^{-10}$. Changing the threshold changes which directions [[截断SVD反演|truncated SVD inversion]] retains and therefore changes the solution norm. [[岭的SVD滤波公式|Ridge regularization]] instead shrinks directions continuously and does not use a discrete numerical rank to decide which directions survive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> “这个矩阵的数值秩是 7”为什么还不是一句完整的可复核陈述？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why is “the numerical rank of this matrix is 7” not yet a complete, reproducible statement?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 还需报告使用的分解或判定规则、容差及其绝对或相对缩放方式。SVD 阈值计数与 CPQR 条件估计即使偶尔给出同一数字，也不是同一个判定。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The statement must also identify the factorization or rank test, the tolerance, and whether its scaling is absolute or relative. An SVD-threshold count and a CPQR condition estimate are different tests even when they happen to return the same number.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [LAPACK `DGELSD`](https://www.netlib.org/lapack/explore-html/d9/d67/group__gelsd_ga0bee7e1b9e7e43f59ecf2419b2759c42.html)：核验 $\sigma_i\le\mathrm{RCOND}\,\sigma_1$ 时判零、`RCOND<0` 时使用机器精度，以及输出有效秩的精确定义。
- [LAPACK `DGELSY`](https://www.netlib.org/lapack/explore-html/d6/d4b/dgelsy_8f_source.html)：核验 CPQR 路线通过 `RCOND` 与领先三角块条件估计选择有效秩，而不是直接执行奇异值阈值计数。
- Gene H. Golub and Charles F. Van Loan, *Matrix Computations*, 4th ed., §2.4：核验数值秩、扰动尺度与奇异值阈值之间的关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- LAPACK `DGELSD` verifies the zero classification $\sigma_i\le\mathrm{RCOND}\,\sigma_1$, the use of machine precision when `RCOND<0`, and the exact definition of its reported effective rank.
- LAPACK `DGELSY` verifies that the CPQR route selects an effective rank using `RCOND` and a condition estimate of the leading triangular block rather than a direct singular-value-threshold count.
- Golub and Van Loan, *Matrix Computations*, 4th ed., Section 2.4, support the relation among numerical rank, perturbation scale, and singular-value thresholds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
