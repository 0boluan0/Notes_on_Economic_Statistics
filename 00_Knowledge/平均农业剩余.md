# 平均农业剩余是可释放的农业产品剩余除以已经转入工业的劳动者人数

平均农业剩余把[[农业剩余]]分摊到已经配置到工业的劳动者，衡量每位工业劳动者对应多少可供其使用的农业产品。它的分母是工业劳动者，不是留农者；因而既不是农业平均产出，也不是农业劳动者的平均收入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Average agricultural surplus divides [[农业剩余|total agricultural surplus]] among workers already allocated to industry, measuring agricultural goods available per industrial worker. Its denominator is industrial rather than remaining agricultural labour. It is therefore neither average agricultural product nor agricultural workers' average income.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设工业劳动 $L_M>0$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For industrial labour $L_M>0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
AAS=\frac{TAS}{L_M}.
$$

在起点全部劳动都留在农业、全部农业产出都由农业劳动者消费的基准中，$F_A(N)=bN$。随后固定人口与初始制度食品收入 $b$，移出零边际产出的劳动不降低农业产量，却每人释放原先消费的 $b$ 单位食品。因此 $TAS=bL_M$、$AAS=b$。一旦继续移出劳动开始减少农业产出，TAS即使仍增加，也可能赶不上工业劳动者人数增长，AAS于是下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the baseline, all labour initially works in agriculture and agricultural workers consume all its output, so $F_A(N)=bN$. Subsequently holding population and initial institutional food income $b$ fixed, withdrawing zero-marginal-product labour preserves output while releasing each worker's former consumption of $b$. Thus $TAS=bL_M$ and $AAS=b$. Once additional withdrawal reduces agricultural output, TAS can still increase but fail to keep pace with industrial labour, causing AAS to fall.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如20位工业劳动者对应农业剩余160，$AAS=8$；后来40位对应剩余270，$AAS=6.75$。剩余总量增加110，但每人可得食品减少1.25。这个分母变化正是[[农业短缺点与商业化点|短缺点]]所关心的供养问题，不是“农业已经没有产品可卖”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If 20 industrial workers correspond to surplus of 160, AAS is 8. If 40 workers later correspond to surplus of 270, AAS is 6.75. Total surplus increases by 110 while food available per worker falls by 1.25. This denominator-sensitive support problem matters at the [[农业短缺点与商业化点|shortage point]]; it does not mean that agriculture has no goods left to market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $L_M=0$ 的起点，不能计算 $0/0$；初期的 $AAS=b$ 是进入工业的劳动量为正时的关系。AAS也只是可供量，是否转化为价格上涨、工资调整或消费替代，还要看[[食品价格与工业工资]]所需的商品市场与工资条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $L_M=0$, the ratio $0/0$ is undefined; the initial relation $AAS=b$ applies once a positive amount of labour enters industry. AAS measures available goods. Whether it produces higher prices, wage adjustment or consumption substitution depends on the market and wage conditions in [[食品价格与工业工资|food prices and industrial wages]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
