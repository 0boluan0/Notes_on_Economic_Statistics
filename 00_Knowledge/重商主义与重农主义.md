# 重商主义与重农主义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mercantilism and Physiocracy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 展示经济分析在古典政治经济学之前如何围绕国家力量、贸易、货币与年度社会剩余组织问题。
> **具体锚点：** 重商主义政策者可用航运限制和出口支持增加贸易顺差与国家财政能力；Quesnay 的《经济表》则追踪农业净产品如何在生产阶级、土地所有者和“不生产”阶级之间循环。
> **核心难点：** 两者都不是一条现代公式；必须在金属货币、战争财政、殖民贸易和农业剩余的历史语境中理解。
> **为什么重要：** 重商主义把“国家能掌握多少资源”置于中心，重农主义则把“社会剩余在部门与阶级间如何再生产”推到前台。
> **继续：** Smith 如何把国家财富改写为分工、市场范围和资本积累，见 [[Smith、Ricardo 与古典政治经济学]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It shows how economic analysis before classical political economy organized questions around state power, trade, money, and the annual social surplus.
> **Concrete anchor:** Mercantilist policy could use navigation restrictions and export support to enlarge the trade surplus and fiscal capacity. Quesnay's *Tableau* instead traced how agriculture's net product circulated among the productive class, landowners, and the “sterile” class.
> **Central difficulty:** Neither body of thought is one modern formula. Both must be read in the historical setting of metallic money, war finance, colonial trade, and agricultural surplus.
> **Why it matters:** Mercantilism centers how many resources the state can command; physiocracy foregrounds how social surplus is reproduced across sectors and classes.
> **Continue:** For Smith's reframing of national wealth through division of labor, market extent, and capital accumulation, see [[Smith、Ricardo 与古典政治经济学|Smith, Ricardo, and Classical Political Economy]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!source] 本节依据
> - Schumpeter, *History of Economic Analysis* 与 Blaug, *Economic Theory in Retrospect*：核验流派的历史位置、内部多样性与后世概括的边界。
> - Quesnay, *Tableau économique* 及其算术解释：核验净产品、阶级支付和年度再生产的循环。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> - Schumpeter's *History of Economic Analysis* and Blaug's *Economic Theory in Retrospect* verify historical placement, internal diversity, and the limits of later summaries.
> - Quesnay's *Tableau économique* and its arithmetic explanation verify net product, class payments, and annual reproduction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 重商主义的问题意识
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The mercantilist problem setting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重商主义关注国家力量、贸易顺差和金银流入，政策倾向保护、航运和殖民体系；并非一套统一理论。重农主义把农业净产品和经济循环置于中心，Quesnay 的 Tableau 是早期整体再生产图景，并主张自然秩序与单一地租税。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mercantilism focused on state power, trade surpluses, and inflows of gold and silver; policy often favored protection, navigation controls, and colonial systems. It was not one unified theory. Physiocracy placed agriculture's net product and economic circulation at the center. Quesnay's *Tableau* was an early picture of economy-wide reproduction and supported a natural order and a single tax on land rent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不应把重商主义压成“把金子当财富”。在强国需要支付军队和进口战略物资的环境中，可国际支付的金属、海运能力、关税收入和制造业都与国家安全相连。问题在于，个别国家的顺差并不能被所有国家同时复制，金属流入和国内价格反应也会削弱顺差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mercantilism should not be compressed into “gold is wealth.” In a setting where powerful states had to pay armies and import strategic goods, internationally acceptable specie, shipping capacity, customs revenue, and manufacturing were tied to security. The problem is that every country cannot simultaneously copy a surplus strategy, and specie inflows with domestic price responses can erode the surplus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 重农主义的净产品与循环
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Physiocratic net product and circulation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重农学派把土地产出超过农业生产必要投入的部分视为净产品。这一剩余以地租等形式到达土地所有者，再通过对农产品和手工品的支出在阶级间循环，使下一年生产得以恢复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Physiocrats treated the part of land output above the inputs required for agricultural production as the net product. This surplus reached landowners through rent and then circulated through spending on agricultural and manufactured goods, restoring the conditions for the next year's production.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“只有农业生产”是特定的剩余定义，不等于现代国民账户说制造和服务没有增值。它的历史贡献之一是把经济看成需要再生产条件的相互依赖支付流，而不是一串孤立交易。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Only agriculture is productive” is a particular definition of surplus; it is not a modern national-accounts claim that manufacturing and services create no value added. One historical contribution was to view the economy as interdependent payment flows that must reproduce production conditions rather than as isolated trades.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 对比时要看“剩余由何而来”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Compare them by asking where the surplus comes from*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重商主义更常从对外交易、货币和国家能掌握的资源追踪富强；重农主义从土地生产和年度净产品追踪剩余。后来 Smith 会把生产力的来源扩展到分工与市场范围，Ricardo 则把重心移向剩余在工资、利润和地租间的分配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mercantilist reasoning more often traced power through foreign trade, money, and resources commanded by the state. Physiocracy traced surplus through land production and the annual net product. Smith later broadened productivity to division of labor and market extent, while Ricardo shifted attention toward distribution among wages, profits, and rent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把所有重商主义作者写成共享一个严整模型。
- 用现代 GDP 增值定义直接嘲笑重农学派，未读出其年度剩余和再生产问题。
- 把历史解释变成对当代关税或农业政策的直接赞成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Writing every mercantilist as if all shared one rigorous model.
- Mocking physiocracy through modern GDP value-added definitions without recognizing its questions about annual surplus and reproduction.
- Turning historical interpretation into direct endorsement of contemporary tariffs or agricultural policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么不能把重商主义简单等同于“相信贸易永远零和”？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can mercantilism not simply be equated with believing trade is always zero-sum?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它是多种国家建设和贸易政策主张的集合，处于金属货币、战争财政和殖民竞争语境，并非单一严整命题。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It was a collection of state-building and trade-policy arguments in the setting of metallic money, war finance, and colonial rivalry, not one rigorous proposition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明《经济表》想追踪什么。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words what the Tableau was trying to trace.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它追踪农业净剩余如何通过地租和各阶级支出在经济中循环，并恢复下一轮生产的投入。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It traced how agriculture's net surplus circulated through rent and class spending and restored inputs for the next production cycle.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 如果一国贸易顺差带来金属流入，什么调整可以削弱顺差？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If a trade surplus brings specie inflows, what adjustment can weaken the surplus?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 金属流入可推高国内货币与价格，使出口变贵、进口相对变便宜，从而迫使顺差缩小。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Specie inflows can raise domestic money and prices, making exports more expensive and imports relatively cheaper, thereby narrowing the surplus.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
