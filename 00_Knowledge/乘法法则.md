# Product rule of counting
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*乘法法则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a captain and then a different deputy from Ana, Ben and Cara. A result records both roles, so $(\text{Ana},\text{Ben})$ differs from $(\text{Ben},\text{Ana})$. The six results are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 Ana、Ben、Cara 中先选队长，再选一位不同的助理。结果记录两个角色，因此 $(\text{Ana},\text{Ben})$ 与 $(\text{Ben},\text{Ana})$ 不同。全部六个结果是

$$
(A,B),\ (A,C),\ (B,A),\ (B,C),\ (C,A),\ (C,B),
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $A,B,C$ abbreviate the three names. There are three choices for the captain and, after **every** captain choice, two choices for the deputy. Hence $3\times2=6$. The available names change, but the number of available names stays two. This is the idea behind the **generalised product rule**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $A,B,C$ 是三个人名的缩写。队长有三种选择；**无论**选谁当队长，助理都恰好剩两种选择，所以得到 $3\times2=6$。剩余人名会变，但剩余人数始终为二，这就是**广义乘法法则**的核心。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Counting complete choice sequences

A **prefix** is a partially completed sequence of choices: after selecting Ana as captain, $(A)$ is a prefix. An **extension** adds the next legal choice. Suppose a construction has $k$ stages, with $n_1$ first-stage choices; at stage $j$, every legal prefix of length $j-1$ has exactly $n_j$ extensions. The integers $n_j$ count choices, not probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*数完整的选择序列*

**前缀**是尚未完成的选择序列：选 Ana 当队长后，$(A)$ 就是前缀；**延伸**是在它后面再添加一个合法选择。设构造共有 $k$ 步，第一步有 $n_1$ 个选择；第 $j$ 步中，每个长度为 $j-1$ 的合法前缀都恰好有 $n_j$ 个延伸。这里的整数 $n_j$ 计算选项数，不是概率。

> [!important] Equal branching counts at each stage · 同一阶段的延伸数固定
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The number of complete choice sequences is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 完整选择序列的数量为
>
> $$
> n_1n_2\cdots n_k.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each stage multiplies the number of existing prefixes by its common number of extensions. To count a different kind of final object, also check that each object has exactly one complete description; see [[计数对象|counting objects and descriptions]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每一步都把已有前缀数乘上共同的延伸数。如果最终想数的不是选择序列本身，还须确认每个对象恰好有一种完整描述，见[[计数对象|计数对象与描述的区别]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rule is about how many legal continuations exist. It does **not** require the later choices to ignore earlier ones, or require probabilistic independence. Selecting distinct people already changes the later options. If several sequences describe one final object—for example, the two orders of an unordered pair—use a justified [[除法法则|division rule]] rather than treating those sequences as different objects.

For length-$n$ binary strings, each position allows $0$ or $1$, giving $2^n$ strings. Here $n$ is a nonnegative integer; at $n=0$ there is one empty string, corresponding to an empty product of value $1$. For three-digit decimal numbers, the first digit has only nine choices, since it cannot be zero; the count is $9\cdot10\cdot10=900$, not $10^3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个法则关心合法延伸**有几个**，不要求后面的选择与前面的选择毫无关系，也不要求概率意义上的独立。选不同的人，本来就会改变后面的候选名单。如果多条序列描述同一个最终对象，例如无序二人组的两种书写顺序，就要在验证重数后使用[[除法法则]]，不能把描述当成不同对象。

长度为 $n$ 的二进制串每位可选 $0$ 或 $1$，所以有 $2^n$ 个。这里 $n$ 是非负整数；$n=0$ 时有一个空串，对应空乘积的值 $1$。若构造三位十进制数，首位不能为零，只有九种选择，后两位各有十种，因此是 $9\cdot10\cdot10=900$，不是 $10^3$。

> [!example] Unequal branches must be added · 分支数不同时要相加
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Form a two-symbol string from $0,1,2$ with the second symbol larger than the first. The complete list is $01,02,12$. Prefix $0$ has two completions; prefix $1$ has one; prefix $2$ has none. The answer is $2+1+0=3$, not “three first choices times two second choices.”
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用 $0,1,2$ 构造两位符号串，要求第二位大于第一位。全部结果是 $01,02,12$。前缀 $0$ 有两个延伸，$1$ 有一个，$2$ 没有，所以共有 $2+1+0=3$ 个，不能算成“三种首位乘两种末位”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, immediately before the final step, let $P$ be the set of possible prefixes and let $d(p)$ be the number of legal final choices after prefix $p$. Complete sequences are separated by their prefixes, so the [[加法法则|sum rule]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般地，在最后一步之前，令 $P$ 为可能的前缀集合，$d(p)$ 为前缀 $p$ 后面合法末项的数量。不同前缀对应的完整序列互不重叠，所以由[[加法法则]]得到

$$
\text{number of complete sequences}=\sum_{p\in P}d(p).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When every prefix has exactly $d$ continuations, this simplifies to $|P|d$. A zero extension count contributes no completed sequence; if a stage has zero extensions for every surviving prefix, there are no full results.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个前缀都恰有 $d$ 个延伸时，上式就可简化为 $|P|d$。零延伸数不产生完整序列；若某一步所有已有前缀都无法继续，最终结果数就是零。
