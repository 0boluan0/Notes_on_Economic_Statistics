# 财政逃逸条款是在预先规定的重大事件下授权暂时偏离规则并规定回归程序的制度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A fiscal escape clause authorises temporary departures from a rule for specified major events and establishes a return procedure.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财政逃逸条款把严重衰退、重大灾害等适用制度明确列出的异常情况，与暂时偏离常规数值约束的权限连接起来。它应说明谁判断触发、谁批准偏离、偏离如何受监督，以及如何回到正常框架。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An escape clause links exceptional events specified by the relevant framework, such as a severe recession or major disaster, to authority for temporary deviation. It identifies who assesses the trigger, who authorises departure, how it is monitored and how the normal framework resumes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断条款是否可执行，至少要看到触发条件、授权程序、偏离范围与理由、回归路径，以及公开报告安排。只写“特殊时期可以例外”，既难以检验是否适用，也难以约束持续偏离。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An operational clause specifies triggers, authorisation, the scope and justification of deviation, a return path and public reporting. A generic exception for “special circumstances” is difficult to test or discipline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归不等于冲击尚未消退就立即一次性抵销全部偏离；过快调整可能触发[[余额规则与顺周期]]中的问题。回归可以依清楚说明的时间表或经济条件安排，若需要延长，应按规定程序更新理由与计划，并接受[[独立财政机构]]等适当机构的审查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Returning does not require immediately offsetting every deviation before a shock subsides; abrupt correction may create [[余额规则与顺周期|procyclical adjustment]]. A transparent time- or state-contingent path can guide return. Extensions require the specified procedure, updated justification and appropriate scrutiny, potentially by [[独立财政机构|independent fiscal institutions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
