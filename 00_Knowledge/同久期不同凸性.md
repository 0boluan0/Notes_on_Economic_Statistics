# 同一收益率口径下久期相同的现金流组合仍可具有不同凸性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cash-flow portfolios with equal duration in the same yield convention can still have different convexities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

久期只约束当前一阶相对敏感度，不决定二阶敏感度。即使两组现金流在同一收益率下价格、久期都相等，它们对有限幅度变动的价格反应也可能不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Duration constrains current first-order relative sensitivity, not second-order sensitivity. Even equal prices and durations at the same yield need not imply equal responses to finite yield changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用连续复利年收益率 $c$ 说明最清楚：固定非负现金流的 $D=\sum_kw_kt_k$，$\mathcal C=\sum_kw_kt_k^2$。在共同基准 $c_0$ 下，组合 A 在第 $1$、$5$ 年各有一笔现值 $50$ 的支付，组合 B 在第 $3$ 年有一笔现值 $100$ 的支付。对应固定金额分别为 $50e^{c_0}$、$50e^{5c_0}$，以及 $100e^{3c_0}$；冲击时不再改变这些金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuous annual yield $c$ gives a clear example: for fixed nonnegative payments, $D=\sum_kw_kt_k$ and $\mathcal C=\sum_kw_kt_k^2$. At a common baseline $c_0$, portfolio A has payments with present values of 50 in years 1 and 5, while B has present value 100 in year 3. Their fixed payment amounts are $50e^{c_0}$, $50e^{5c_0}$ and $100e^{3c_0}$, respectively; these amounts do not change in the shock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_A=P_B=100,\qquad D_A=\frac{1+5}{2}=3=D_B,$$
$$\mathcal C_A=\frac{1^2+5^2}{2}=13,\qquad \mathcal C_B=3^2=9.$$

当 $c$ 同幅改变 $x$ 时，精确价格为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the same change $x$ in $c$, exact prices are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_A(x)=50e^{-x}+50e^{-5x},\qquad P_B(x)=100e^{-3x}.$$

二阶价格差为 $\tfrac12\times100(13-9)x^2=200x^2$。例如 $x=0.01$，二阶差约 $0.0200$，精确差约 $0.0194$。而且本例精确满足 $P_A/P_B=(e^{2x}+e^{-2x})/2\ge1$，非零冲击时严格大于 $1$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second-order price difference is $\tfrac12\times100(13-9)x^2=200x^2$. At $x=0.01$, it is about 0.0200 versus an exact difference of about 0.0194. This example also has the exact ratio $P_A/P_B=(e^{2x}+e^{-2x})/2\ge1$, strictly above one for a nonzero shock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的精确优势来自特定固定现金流构造与共同连续利率冲击，不是所有“高凸性资产必然更好”的结论；价格、收益率、信用、期权、资金成本或曲线变动模式改变，比较条件也随之改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exact advantage comes from this particular fixed-cash-flow construction and common continuous-yield shock. It does not establish that every higher-convexity asset is preferable; different prices, yields, credit, options, funding costs or curve movements change the comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
