# 固定训练 FLOPs 下，模型参数与训练 token 必须联合分配而不能单独最大化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Under fixed training FLOPs, model parameters and training tokens must be jointly allocated rather than maximized separately*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 对标准 dense Transformer 训练，常用近似是
> $$C\approx 6ND,$$
> 其中 $C$ 是训练 FLOPs，$N$ 是参数量，$D$ 是训练 token 数。固定 $C$ 时，$N$ 与 $D$ 的乘积近似固定：增加参数会挤压可训练 token，增加 token 也要求缩小模型或增加预算。计算最优不是“模型尽量大”或“数据尽量多”，而是在明确的 loss、架构、数据和预算下联合选择 $(N,D)$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For standard dense Transformer training, a common approximation is $C\approx6ND$, where $C$ is training FLOPs, $N$ parameter count, and $D$ training tokens. With $C$ fixed, increasing model size reduces the affordable token count, and increasing tokens requires a smaller model or more compute. Compute optimality therefore jointly chooses $(N,D)$ for a specified loss, architecture, data distribution, and budget.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么最优点必须平衡两个边际收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the optimum balances two marginal gains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Hoffmann et al. 用经验损失面
$$\hat L(N,D)=E+\frac{A}{N^\alpha}+\frac{B}{D^\beta}$$
表示不可约损失、有限参数造成的损失和有限数据造成的损失。把预算约束 $D=C/(6N)$ 代入：
$$\hat L(N)=E+\frac{A}{N^\alpha}
+B\left(\frac{6N}{C}\right)^\beta.$$
第一项随 $N$ 增大而下降，第二项却因可用 $D$ 变少而上升。内部最优点的一阶条件是
$$\alpha A N^{-\alpha}=\beta B D^{-\beta},$$
即最后一单位计算投给参数容量与投给更多训练 token 的边际 loss 收益相平衡。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hoffmann et al. fit an empirical loss surface with irreducible, finite-parameter, and finite-data terms. Substituting the compute constraint makes the parameter term fall as $N$ grows while the data term rises because affordable $D$ shrinks. At an interior optimum, the marginal loss benefit per unit of compute is balanced between model capacity and additional training tokens.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个推导只说明“必须联合优化”，不凭空给出常数。$A,B,\alpha,\beta$ 来自特定架构、数据分布、tokenizer、训练方案和 loss 的拟合；换设置后，应重新测量或至少做小规模 isoFLOP 实验，而不是把旧系数当自然定律。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This derivation establishes the need for joint optimization but does not supply universal constants. The fitted coefficients depend on architecture, data distribution, tokenizer, training recipe, and loss. A changed setting calls for refitting or at least small isoFLOP experiments rather than treating old coefficients as natural law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Chinchilla 结果的经验范围
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Empirical scope of the Chinchilla result*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Hoffmann et al. 训练了 400 多个 **dense autoregressive Transformer**，摘要给出的探索范围约为 70M 到超过 16B 参数、5B 到 500B 训练 token。在这个经验区间和训练目标下，三种估计方法都支持参数量与 token 数随预算近似等比例增长；其参数化拟合给出的预算指数约为 $0.46$ 与 $0.54$，接近各占一半。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hoffmann et al. trained more than 400 **dense autoregressive Transformers** over an abstract-reported range of roughly 70M to more than 16B parameters and 5B to 500B training tokens. Within that empirical regime and objective, three approaches supported scaling parameters and tokens in roughly equal proportions; their parametric fit gave compute exponents near $0.46$ and $0.54$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

论文随后以接近相同训练计算比较 70B Chinchilla（1.4T token）与 280B Gopher（300B token），验证“更小但训练更久”的配置。$1.4\text{T}/70\text{B}=20$，所以 Chinchilla 这一实例常被概括为约 20 token/parameter；这个比值是该经验前沿上的配置，不是跨架构、跨数据质量、跨 tokenizer、跨预算都必须保持的通则。用来拟合缩放曲线的 analysis runs 都少于一轮数据遍历；大规模的直接对照主要就是 Gopher 与 Chinchilla 这一对，且两次训练并非只改了 $N,D$。因此它是对前沿预测的有力验证，不是严格单因素因果实验。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The paper then compared 70B Chinchilla trained on 1.4T tokens with 280B Gopher trained on 300B tokens at approximately equal training compute, validating a smaller-but-longer configuration. Chinchilla's ratio is $1.4\text{T}/70\text{B}=20$ tokens per parameter. That ratio describes a configuration on this empirical frontier, not a universal constant across architectures, data quality, tokenizers, and budgets. The analysis runs used for the scaling fits each covered less than one data epoch; the main large-scale direct comparison was the Gopher–Chinchilla pair, and those runs did not differ only in $(N,D)$. This is strong evidence for the frontier prediction, not a strict single-factor causal experiment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：参数翻四倍会挤掉多少 token
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: how quadrupling parameters displaces tokens*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设某 dense Transformer 预算满足
$$C/6=1.4\times10^{22}\ \text{parameter-token units}.$$
选择 $N=70$B 时，可训练约 $D=200$B token；若把参数增至 $N=280$B 而预算不变，只剩约 $D=50$B token。两种配置的近似训练计算相同，不能仅凭 280B 大于 70B 就判定后者更优；必须在目标数据与训练方案上比较其最终 loss。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a dense-Transformer budget has $C/6=1.4\times10^{22}$ parameter-token units. A 70B-parameter model can train on about 200B tokens, while a 280B model under the same budget can train on only about 50B. Their approximate training compute is equal. The larger parameter count alone does not rank them; final loss under the target data and recipe must be compared.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个算例也说明 token 总数的口径不能省略。不同 tokenizer 会把同一文本切成不同的 $D$，不同 packing、重复 epoch 和过滤方案也会改变“看过多少独立证据”。因此计算表中的 token 数必须与 [[Token口径]] 一起报告。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example also shows why token accounting cannot be omitted. Different tokenizers produce different $D$ for the same text, while packing, repeated epochs, and filtering alter how much independent evidence was seen. Compute tables must therefore report tokens together with the [[Token口径|token accounting convention]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 外推边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Extrapolation boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- **架构边界：** $6ND$ 是 dense Transformer 的近似。MoE 的总参数、激活参数、路由与通信成本不再由一个 $N$ 完整表示。
- **经验区间：** 70M–16B、5–500B 是拟合证据的范围，不等于任意数量级上的严格定律。
- **数据边界：** token 质量、重复程度和可获得的独立数据会改变边际收益；“再训练同一批低价值 token”不能自动替代新证据。
- **目标边界：** Chinchilla 优化的是给定训练 FLOPs 下的预训练 loss，不是下游准确率、延迟、显存或商业成本的单一总目标。
- **对照边界：** Gopher 与 Chinchilla 的优化器、优化器状态中是否保留高精度权重副本、tokenizer 等细节并非完全一致；大规模结果支持预测，但不应被改写为严格只操纵参数—token 比的实验。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- **Architecture:** $6ND$ is a dense-Transformer approximation. Total and active parameters, routing, and communication make one $N$ insufficient for MoE.
- **Empirical range:** 70M–16B parameters and 5–500B tokens bound the cited fit rather than proving an exact law at every scale.
- **Data:** quality, repetition, and available independent data change marginal returns; repeated low-value tokens are not automatically equivalent to new evidence.
- **Objective:** Chinchilla optimizes pretraining loss under training FLOPs, not a single objective combining downstream accuracy, latency, memory, and business cost.
- **Comparison:** Gopher and Chinchilla did not fully match the optimizer, the use of a high-precision weight copy in optimizer state, the tokenizer, and other details. The large-scale result supports the prediction but should not be rewritten as a strict experiment that manipulates only the parameter–token ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 训练最优不等于生命周期最优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Training optimality is not lifecycle optimality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡只回答“固定训练 FLOPs 时，怎样在参数与训练 token 之间分配”。一旦模型会被大量调用，参数量还会在每个推理 token 上重复产生计算、显存和货币代价；这时应转到 [[生命周期最优]]，重新声明预计需求和优化目标，不能把 Chinchilla 的训练期前沿直接当作部署期答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card answers only how to allocate parameters and training tokens under fixed training FLOPs. When a model will be served heavily, parameter count is charged again through every inference token; use [[生命周期最优|lifecycle optimality]] rather than treating a training-only frontier as a deployment answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 在架构和 $6ND$ 近似不变时，团队把参数量扩大四倍、训练 FLOPs 保持不变，却仍声称会使用原来的 token 数。这个计划缺少什么？能否从“参数更大”推出 loss 更低？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With architecture and the $6ND$ approximation unchanged, a team quadruples parameters, holds training FLOPs fixed, and still claims it will use the original token count. What is missing, and does the larger model imply lower loss?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 三个条件不能同时成立：固定 $C$ 与参数四倍要求 token 数约降为四分之一，除非实际预算增加或 FLOP 模型改变。参数更大不能单独推出 loss 更低，因为数据不足项可能恶化；需要联合配置和目标设置上的 isoFLOP 证据。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The three claims are incompatible: with fixed $C$, quadrupling parameters requires roughly one quarter as many tokens unless compute rises or the FLOP model changes. Larger $N$ alone does not imply lower loss because the finite-data term can worsen; a joint configuration and isoFLOP evidence are required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
