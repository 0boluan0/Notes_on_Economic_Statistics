# 均线交叉规则用短长均线的相对位置变化触发开多与平仓
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A moving-average crossover rule uses changes in the relative positions of short and long averages to trigger long entries and exits.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

均线交叉规则把两条不同窗口均线的位置变化转成订单条件。这里固定为 long/flat：上穿后可以开多，下穿后退出实际多头，仓位不变成负数。以下是可复算的教学规格；它定义如何行动，不提供这组参数能够盈利的经验证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A crossover rule converts changes in the relative positions of two averages into order conditions. This version is long/flat: an upward crossing permits a long entry, and a downward crossing exits the actual long holding without creating a negative position. The following reproducible teaching specification defines actions, not evidence that its parameters are profitable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用截至 $t$ 日收盘、已可得且尺度相容的数据计算 [[简单移动平均]]，短窗为 $m$、长窗为 $n$，其中 $m,n$ 为正整数且 $1\le m<n$；令 $d_t=MA_{m,t}-MA_{n,t}$。最初空仓，凑齐首个长窗时只初始化比较，下一天才开始检查：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Calculate [[简单移动平均|simple moving averages]] from available, comparably scaled closing data through day $t$, with positive-integer windows $1\le m<n$, and let $d_t=MA_{m,t}-MA_{n,t}$. Start flat. The first complete long window only initialises the comparison; signal checks begin the next day:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$u_t=\mathbf1\{d_{t-1}\le0,\ d_t>0\},\qquad
v_t=\mathbf1\{d_{t-1}\ge0,\ d_t<0\}.$$

当前相等时不发新信号；从相等转为严格大于或小于则按上述条件处理。这是本规则的等号约定。输入缺失时不生成新交叉，也不把不相邻的有效日偷偷当成前后日。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equality produces no new signal; a move from equality to a strict inequality is handled by the displayed conditions. This is the rule's stated equality convention. Missing input generates no new crossing, and nonadjacent valid days are not silently treated as consecutive days.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为把信号变成可实施教学例，再固定以下执行政策。只在下一合格常规交易时段提交一次限价 IOC 委托，并假设所用场所允许这种委托；买价上限为信号日未复权收盘价的 1.01 倍，卖价下限为前一有效交易日未复权收盘价的 0.98 倍。提交前核对可用现金至少覆盖请求股数 $Q$ 乘买入限价加显性费用上界；不足时放弃该次开仓，不能等成交后才检查资金。小数价位按例中允许的精度处理，真实场所须遵守最小变动价位和交易单位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the executable teaching example, submit one limit IOC order in the next eligible regular session, assuming the venue permits it. The buy limit is 1.01 times the signal day's raw close; the sell limit is 0.98 times the preceding valid trading day's raw close. Before submission, require available cash to cover requested shares $Q$ times the buy limit plus an upper bound on explicit charges; otherwise skip the entry. Funding cannot be checked only after execution. The example permits its displayed price precision; an actual venue's ticks and trading units still apply.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

空仓且出现 $u_t=1$ 时，请求买入固定 $Q$ 股。IOC 剩余量取消；部分成交后只持有已买数量，不补单，完全未成交则等待下一次上穿。持多且出现 $v_t=1$ 时进入退出状态，下一合格时段卖出实际持股；若未全部成交，余股仍承担风险，并逐个合格时段按最新已知收盘价更新限价重试，直至平仓。退出过程中不新开仓，恢复空仓后再等待新的上穿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When flat and $u_t=1$, request a fixed $Q$ shares. Cancel the IOC remainder; retain a partial entry without topping up, or wait for a new upward crossing if nothing fills. When long and $v_t=1$, enter exit mode and offer the actual holding in the next eligible session. Any residual remains exposed and is offered again in successive eligible sessions with a limit based on the latest known close until flat. Do not open during exit mode; after becoming flat, wait for a new upward crossing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小例子取 $m=2,n=3,Q=8$，初始现金 1000 元，无公司行动、利息和外部资金流；每笔有成交的委托只收 1 元显性费用，成交价已包含执行价差，不再重复另扣。为隔离信号与成交，本例简化为即时结算。收盘序列和完整长窗后的差值为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Set $m=2,n=3,Q=8$ and initial cash to 1000, with no corporate actions, interest or external flows. Charge 1 per order with any fill; execution prices already include execution-price differences, which are not deducted again. Settlement is immediate in this example to isolate signal and fill timing. Closing prices and differences after the long window is complete are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 日 / Day | 收盘 / Close | $MA_2$ | $MA_3$ | $d_t$ | 收盘后信号 / Post-close signal |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 100 | — | — | — | — |
| 2 | 98 | 99 | — | — | — |
| 3 | 96 | 97 | 98 | −1 | 初始化 / Initialise |
| 4 | 102 | 99 | 98.6667 | 0.3333 | 上穿 / Up |
| 5 | 104 | 103 | 100.6667 | 2.3333 | — |
| 6 | 100 | 102 | 102 | 0 | — |
| 7 | 96 | 98 | 100 | −2 | 下穿 / Down |

第 4 日信号得到买入限价 $102(1.01)=103.02$。第 5 日订单到达时，假设静态卖盘只有 4 股报 103.00，下一档报 103.10；买到 4 股，另 4 股取消。现金为 $1000-4(103)-1=587$，第 5 日收盘价值为 $587+4(104)=1003$。第 6、7 日收盘价值分别为 987、971，不能用请求的 8 股计收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The day-4 signal gives a buy limit of $102(1.01)=103.02$. When the day-5 order arrives, assume a static ask book offers four shares at 103.00 and the next level at 103.10. Four shares fill and four are cancelled. Cash becomes 587 and day-5 closing wealth is 1003. Day-6 and day-7 closing wealth are 987 and 971; returns cannot use the requested eight shares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第 7 日下穿后，第 8 日卖价下限为 $96(0.98)=94.08$。假设订单到达时 94.90 的买盘足够接下全部 4 股，卖出后现金为 $587+4(94.90)-1=965.60$，总收益为 −3.44%。本例按初值、各日收盘及最后平仓值观测的 [[最大回撤]]为 $(1003-965.60)/1003\approx3.73\%$；未观测的盘中路径不在这个数字内。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Following the day-7 downward crossing, the day-8 sell limit is $96(0.98)=94.08$. Assume the bid at 94.90 can absorb all four shares when the order arrives. Final cash is 965.60 and total return is −3.44%. Using initial wealth, daily closes and the final liquidation value, [[最大回撤|maximum drawdown]] is approximately 3.73%. Unobserved intraday paths are outside this statistic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
