# 切点组合是在给定机会集和无风险利率下实现最高正Sharpe的全额风险组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A tangency portfolio is a fully invested risky portfolio attaining the highest positive Sharpe ratio for a given opportunity set and risk-free rate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本主题采用通常的正 Sharpe 切点含义：在指定风险资产机会集与 $r_f$ 下，切点组合 $T$ 是使 [[资本配置线]] 斜率达到可实现最大正值的全额风险组合，风险资产权重满足 $\mathbf1^\top w_T=1$。“全额”指净权重和为 1，不自动表示每项权重非负。定义只在这个最大值确实达到时适用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here tangency refers to the usual positive-Sharpe tangency portfolio. Given the risky opportunity set and $r_f$, $T$ is a fully invested risky portfolio attaining the largest achievable positive slope of a [[资本配置线|capital allocation line]], with $\mathbf1^\top w_T=1$. Fully invested means net weights sum to one, not that every weight is nonnegative. This definition applies only when the maximum is attained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在通常的光滑风险资产前沿上，从 $(0,r_f)$ 引出的最陡可行直线与前沿相切，由此得名。任意指定的风险组合都有自己的配置轨迹，但并非每条 CAL 都达到有效前沿；也不能只凭图中“像是相切”就跳过 [[切点组合存在边界|存在性和可行约束]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the usual smooth risky frontier, the steepest feasible line from $(0,r_f)$ is tangent to the frontier, giving the portfolio its name. Every chosen risky portfolio has an allocation locus, but not every CAL is efficient. A visually apparent tangency does not replace checks of [[切点组合存在边界|existence and feasibility]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，比较同一 $r_f=3\%$ 下两个候选组合：A 的 $(\mu,\sigma)=(8\%,20\%)$，B 的 $(9\%,20\%)$，其 Sharpe 分别为 0.25 和 0.30。B 的配置线更陡，但这只能说明 B 优于 A 的这项比较；除非已覆盖整个允许的机会集，否则还不能证明 B 就是切点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two candidates under $r_f=3\%$, let A have $(\mu,\sigma)=(8\%,20\%)$ and B have $(9\%,20\%)$. Their Sharpe ratios are 0.25 and 0.30. B has the steeper allocation line, but this comparison alone does not establish B as the tangency portfolio unless the whole permitted opportunity set has been considered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

切点是组合选择的对象，不是预先指定的股票指数。只有加入 [[CAPM假设|共同机会集与市场出清等均衡条件]]，共同切点才可识别为理论市场组合；现实指数的代理问题复用 [[市场代理联合检验]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tangency is a portfolio-choice object, not a stock index chosen in advance. Only with [[CAPM假设|equilibrium conditions including a common opportunity set and market clearing]] can the common tangency portfolio be identified with the theoretical market portfolio. Practical index proxies are addressed by [[市场代理联合检验|the market-proxy joint-test limitation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
