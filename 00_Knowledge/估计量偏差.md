# Bias of an estimator
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*估计量的偏差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An estimator can fluctuate from sample to sample and still be systematically centred above or below its target. **Bias** measures that difference between the estimator's sampling mean and the quantity it is meant to estimate. It describes the rule's average error under a model, not how far one particular answer missed the target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估计量会随样本波动，同时仍可能在平均意义上高估或低估目标。**偏差**衡量估计量的抽样均值与目标量相差多少。它描述某模型下这条规则的平均误差，不是某一次答案偏离目标多少。

^core

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Average over possible samples, not observations within one sample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*平均的是可能样本产生的结果*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First separate the [[估计对象三分|target, estimation rule and realized estimate]]. Let $T_n$ be an estimator using $n$ observations, and suppose the data model is indexed by a parameter $\theta$. The subscript in $E_\theta$ means that the [[期望|expectation]] is calculated using the sampling model with that fixed parameter value. For a finite real target $g(\theta)$ and $E_\theta[|T_n|]<\infty$, define
$$\operatorname{Bias}_\theta(T_n;g)=E_\theta[T_n]-g(\theta).$$
If the target is $\theta$ itself, this becomes $E_\theta[T_n]-\theta$. The parameter, sample size, target and rule must all be specified: changing any of them may change the bias.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先分清[[估计对象三分|目标、规则与估计值]]。$T_n$ 是使用 $n$ 个观察的估计量，$\theta$ 标记数据模型的参数。$E_\theta$ 的下标表示：按固定为该参数值的抽样模型计算[[期望]]。有限实数目标为 $g(\theta)$，且 $E_\theta[|T_n|]<\infty$ 时，偏差定义为上式。目标就是 $\theta$ 时，简化成 $E_\theta[T_n]-\theta$。参数、样本量、目标和规则都须明确，改变任何一项都可能改变偏差。

> [!example] Zero average error, but a nonzero error today · 平均误差为零，今天仍可能估错
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At one fixed model value, let the target be $20$ and let $T_n$ take $14,18,22,26$ with probability $1/4$ each. Its [[抽样分布|sampling distribution]] gives
> $$E[T_n]=\frac{14+18+22+26}{4}=20,\qquad \operatorname{Bias}(T_n)=0.$$
> If today's result is $14$, its realized error is $14-20=-6$. These statements agree: averaging the four possible errors $-6,-2,2,6$ gives zero. It does not make any one of those errors zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定一个模型值，目标为 $20$，$T_n$ 在[[抽样分布]]下等概率取 $14,18,22,26$。期望为 $20$，偏差为零；这次若算出 $14$，实现误差仍为 $-6$。四种可能误差 $-6,-2,2,6$ 平均为零，不等于每一种误差都为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now use the rule $U_n=T_n+2$ for the same target. Its possible values are $16,20,24,28$, its expectation is $22$, and its bias is $+2$. Yet one possible estimate is exactly $20$. Thus a biased rule can hit the target on a particular sample. Positive bias means average overestimation; negative bias means average underestimation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同一个目标，改用 $U_n=T_n+2$，可能值变成 $16,20,24,28$，期望为 $22$、偏差为 $+2$，但其中一个估计值恰好就是 $20$。所以有偏规则也能某次命中目标。正偏差表示平均高估，负偏差表示平均低估。

## What the bias does and does not tell us
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*偏差描述中心，还不能描述全部误差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bias is a fixed number at a specified model value and sample size. The estimation error $T_n-g(\theta)$ is random before data are observed; its expectation equals the bias when the integrability condition above holds. A zero bias at one parameter value is weaker than [[估计量无偏性|unbiasedness throughout the model]], which requires zero bias at every parameter value under discussion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在给定模型值和样本量下，偏差是固定数；观察前的估计误差 $T_n-g(\theta)$ 是随机量，在上述可积条件下，它的期望等于偏差。只在某个参数值偏差为零，弱于[[估计量无偏性|对整个模型无偏]]，后者要求所讨论的每个参数值都满足。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bias does not describe the spread around the estimator's own mean; that is measured by its [[标准误含义|standard error]] when its variance is finite. To combine spread and systematic error, [[均方误差|mean squared error]] averages squared distances from the target. With finite second moments, the [[MSE偏差方差分解|bias–variance decomposition]] gives MSE = variance + squared bias. Zero bias alone therefore does not imply a small total error or [[估计量一致性|consistency as sample size grows]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

偏差不描述估计量围绕自身均值的分散程度；方差有限时，这由[[标准误含义|标准误]]衡量。[[均方误差]]把与目标的平方距离取平均，综合系统误差与波动。二阶矩有限时，[[MSE偏差方差分解|MSE 等于方差加偏差平方]]。因此仅凭零偏差，不能推出总体误差很小或[[估计量一致性|随样本量增长的一致性]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ordinary finite bias is not defined when the estimator lacks a finite expectation. A finite observed answer cannot repair that missing model property. Nor can one calculate an unknown bias simply by subtracting an unknown true parameter from a single observed estimate; theoretical assumptions or an explicitly justified estimation procedure are needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估计量没有有限期望时，通常的有限偏差就没有定义。某次结果是有限数，不能补上模型缺失的这个性质。也不能仅凭一次观察，把估计值与未知真值相减就声称算出了未知偏差；需要理论假设或有依据的偏差估计方法。
