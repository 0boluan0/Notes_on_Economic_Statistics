# 估计量偏差是重复抽样期望与目标参数之差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Estimator bias is its sampling expectation minus the target parameter*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 若期望存在，估计量 $T_n$ 对目标 $\theta$ 的偏差为 $\operatorname{Bias}_\theta(T_n)=E_\theta[T_n]-\theta$。它是给定参数和样本量下的一个数，区别于单次随机误差 $T_n-\theta$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Bias is the sampling expectation of an estimator minus its target, when that expectation exists. It is a fixed quantity at a given parameter and sample size, unlike the realised estimation error.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

若目标为 20，$T_n$ 等概率取 14、18、22、26，则其均值为 20、偏差为 0；一次取到 14 的误差仍是 $14-20=-6$。偏差为零就是[[估计量无偏性]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An estimator equally likely to take 14, 18, 22, or 26 has expectation 20 and zero bias for target 20, although the realised error at 14 is minus six. Zero bias defines [[估计量无偏性|unbiasedness]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
