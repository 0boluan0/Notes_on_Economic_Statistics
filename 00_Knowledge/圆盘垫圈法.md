# Disk and washer method
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*圆盘垫圈法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine cutting a solid into thin coins perpendicular to its axis. Some coins are solid disks; others have a round hole and look like washers. The **disk and washer method** finds the area of each such cross section and adds area times thickness. It is a special case of [[切片体积|volume by slicing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

想象沿着一个立体的轴，把它切成一片片薄硬币。有些截面是实心圆盘，有些中间有圆孔，像垫圈。**圆盘垫圈法**先求每片截面的面积，再把“面积乘厚度”累加；它是[[切片体积]]的一种具体情形。

> [!example] Subtract areas, not radii · 相减的是面积
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A washer with outer radius $3$ and inner radius $2$ has area $9\pi-4\pi=5\pi$. Its radial width is $1$, but it is not a disk of radius $1$. A thin piece of thickness $\Delta s$ therefore has volume approximately $5\pi\Delta s$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 外半径为 $3$、内半径为 $2$ 的垫圈，面积是 $9\pi-4\pi=5\pi$。虽然环的径向宽度为 $1$，它并不是半径为 $1$ 的圆盘。厚为 $\Delta s$ 的薄片体积因此近似为 $5\pi\Delta s$。

## Move along the axis
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*沿着旋转轴累加*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A solid of revolution is the region swept out when a planar region turns one full revolution around a line in its plane. Let $s$ measure position **along that axis**, from finite $a$ to $b$, with $a<b$. At position $s$, suppose the complete perpendicular cross section is an annulus with outer radius $R(s)$ and inner radius $r(s)$. Both radii are distances from the axis, so $R(s)\ge r(s)\ge0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平面区域绕同一平面内的一条直线转一整圈，扫出的空间区域称为旋转体。用 $s$ 表示**沿旋转轴**的位置，从有限的 $a$ 到 $b$，其中 $a<b$。设位置 $s$ 处完整的垂直截面是一个圆环，外半径为 $R(s)$，内半径为 $r(s)$。二者都从旋转轴量起，是距离，因此 $R(s)\ge r(s)\ge0$。

> [!important] Area times axial thickness · 截面积乘轴向厚度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When these radius functions are [[一点连续|continuous]] at every point of $[a,b]$, the cross-sectional area is $A(s)=\pi[R(s)^2-r(s)^2]$, and the volume is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若这两个半径函数在 $[a,b]$ 上每一点都[[一点连续|连续]]，截面积为 $A(s)=\pi[R(s)^2-r(s)^2]$，体积为
>
> $$V=\int_a^b A(s)\,ds=\pi\int_a^b\bigl[R(s)^2-r(s)^2\bigr]\,ds.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Partition the axis into short intervals. A sample cross section on interval $i$, multiplied by its width $\Delta s_i$, approximates that slab’s volume. These slabs fill the solid without overlapping interiors. The [[黎曼和|Riemann sums]] converge to the displayed [[定积分|definite integral]]. Continuity is a convenient sufficient condition; finitely many piecewise-continuous radius pieces can also be handled separately. More generally, the slicing rule applies when the actual cross-sectional area is integrable under the geometric hypotheses stated in the slicing card.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把轴分成短区间。在第 $i$ 段取一个截面，把它的面积乘以段宽 $\Delta s_i$，就近似得到这一薄片的体积。薄片填满整个立体，内部互不重叠；相应[[黎曼和]]趋于上面的[[定积分]]。连续是方便的充分条件；有限段分段连续的半径函数也可以分别处理。更一般地，在切片卡所述几何条件下，只要实际截面积可积，就可以使用切片公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a horizontal axis such as $y=c$, the axial coordinate is $x$, so use vertical sections and $dx$. For a vertical axis such as $x=c$, it is $y$, so use horizontal sections and $dy$. Every radius and every bound must be expressed using that same chosen coordinate. The thickness is along the axis; it is not the difference $R-r$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

绕 $y=c$ 这样的水平轴旋转时，沿轴坐标是 $x$，所以取竖直截面并用 $dx$。绕 $x=c$ 这样的竖直轴时，沿轴坐标是 $y$，所以取水平截面并用 $dy$。所有半径和积分上下限都要用同一个所选坐标表达。薄片的厚度沿着轴，不是 $R-r$。

## A disk, then a shifted-axis washer
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先看圆盘，再看偏移轴的垫圈*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $0\le x\le1$, rotate the region $0\le y\le x$ about the $x$-axis. Each section is a disk with $R(x)=x$ and $r(x)=0$. Hence $V=\pi\int_0^1x^2\,dx=\pi/3$. The zero inner radius simply means there is no hole.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $0\le x\le1$，把 $0\le y\le x$ 的区域绕 $x$ 轴旋转。每个截面都是外半径 $R(x)=x$、内半径 $r(x)=0$ 的圆盘，所以 $V=\pi\int_0^1x^2\,dx=\pi/3$。内半径为零，只表示没有孔。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now rotate the region bounded by $y=e^x$, $y=2$ and $x=0$ about $y=-1$, where $e^x$ is the [[自然指数函数|natural exponential function]]. The curves meet at $x=\ln2$, where $\ln$ denotes the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]]. For $0\le x\le\ln2$, a vertical segment runs from $e^x$ to $2$, entirely above the axis. Its outer distance is $2-(-1)=3$, and its inner distance is $e^x-(-1)=e^x+1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在把 $y=e^x$、$y=2$ 和 $x=0$ 围成的区域绕 $y=-1$ 旋转，其中 $e^x$ 是[[自然指数函数]]。两条曲线在 $x=\ln2$ 相交，这里的 $\ln$ 是[[自然对数]]。在 $0\le x\le\ln2$ 上，竖直线段从 $e^x$ 延伸到 $2$，全部位于轴上方。外侧距离为 $2-(-1)=3$，内侧距离为 $e^x-(-1)=e^x+1$。

$$
V=\pi\int_0^{\ln2}\left[9-(e^x+1)^2\right]\,dx
=\pi\left(8\ln2-\frac72\right).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $x=0$, the radii are $3$ and $2$, recovering the opening area $5\pi$. At $x=\ln2$, both radii are $3$, so the cross section has zero area. The intermediate areas are nonnegative, as a volume calculation requires.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $x=0$ 处，半径为 $3$ 和 $2$，正好恢复开头的面积 $5\pi$；在 $x=\ln2$ 处，两半径都为 $3$，截面积为零。中间各截面积均非负，符合体积的含义。

> [!warning] Use the section actually swept out · 看实际扫出的截面
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The higher curve is not automatically the outer radius. Distances must be measured from the chosen axis. If one segment crosses the axis, its rotation fills a disk: for example, $-1\le y\le2$ rotated about $y=0$ has radius $2$ and no hole. Subtracting the disk of radius $1$ would remove material that is actually present.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 较高的曲线不一定给出外半径；要从所选轴量距离。若一条线段跨过轴，它旋转后会填成圆盘。例如把 $-1\le y\le2$ 绕 $y=0$ 旋转，得到半径 $2$ 的实心圆盘，没有内孔；减去半径 $1$ 的圆盘反而会删掉实际存在的材料。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a cross section has several disjoint annuli, add their areas; if candidate annuli overlap, first form their actual union. Split the axial interval where the section formula changes, using [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]]. [[旋转体重复覆盖|Overlapping rotational coverage]] explains this counting issue. The [[圆柱壳法|shell method]] instead organises the same volume by radial layers, which can make the geometry easier in a different direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若截面有几个互不相交的圆环，就把面积相加；若候选圆环重叠，应先取实际的并集。截面公式改变处要用[[定积分区间可加性]]对轴向区间分段。[[旋转体重复覆盖]]专门说明这种计数问题；[[圆柱壳法]]则改按径向壳层组织同一体积，有时能使另一个方向的几何表达更简单。
