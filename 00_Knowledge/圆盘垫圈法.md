# 圆盘垫圈法沿旋转轴积分外圆面积与内孔面积之差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The disk and washer method integrates outer-circle area minus hole area along the axis of rotation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

圆盘垫圈法取垂直于旋转轴的截面。若沿轴坐标 $s\in[a,b]$ 的每个截面都是外半径 $R(s)$、内半径 $r(s)$ 的圆环，$R\ge r\ge0$，且截面积可积，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take cross sections perpendicular to the axis of rotation. If each section at axial coordinate $s\in[a,b]$ is an annulus with outer radius $R(s)$ and inner radius $r(s)$, where $R\ge r\ge0$ and the area is integrable, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
V=\pi\int_a^b\bigl[R(s)^2-r(s)^2\bigr]\,ds.
$$

内半径为零时就是圆盘。半径都从旋转轴量起，是非负距离；被积函数为 $\pi(R^2-r^2)$，而不是 $\pi(R-r)^2$。若截面含多个分离圆环，应分别算面积后相加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero inner radius gives a disk. Radii are nonnegative distances measured from the axis. The area is $\pi(R^2-r^2)$, not $\pi(R-r)^2$. If a cross section contains several separate annuli, add their areas.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如由 $y=e^x$、$y=2$、$x=0$ 围成的区域绕 $y=-1$ 旋转。竖切片垂直于水平轴；$x$ 从 $0$ 到 $\ln2$，外半径为 $2-(-1)=3$，内半径为 $e^x-(-1)=e^x+1$，所以
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revolve the region bounded by $y=e^x$, $y=2$, and $x=0$ about $y=-1$. Vertical slices are perpendicular to this horizontal axis. For $0\le x\le\ln2$, the outer radius is $3$ and the inner radius is $e^x+1$, giving
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
V=\pi\int_0^{\ln2}\left[9-(e^x+1)^2\right]\,dx.
$$

> [!question]- 自检
> 外半径为 $3$、内半径为 $2$ 的垫圈，截面积是 $\pi$ 还是 $5\pi$？
>
> **答案：** $5\pi$。厚度为 $1$ 的环并不是半径为 $1$ 的圆盘。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Is the area of a washer with radii $3$ and $2$ equal to $\pi$ or $5\pi$?
>
> **Answer:** $5\pi$. A ring of radial width $1$ is not a disk of radius $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
