# 周期型行业是经营表现对整体经济周期较为敏感的行业
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A cyclical industry has operating performance that is relatively sensitive to the overall business cycle.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

周期型行业的需求、收入或利润通常随整体经济活动明显起伏。例如，可推迟购买的耐用品和依赖资本开支的设备，在需求转弱时可能同时受到订单减少与固定成本压力。具体敏感程度仍取决于业务、地域和时期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A cyclical industry's demand, revenue, or profit typically fluctuates substantially with aggregate activity. Purchases of durable goods or capital equipment can be postponed, so weaker demand may combine falling orders with fixed-cost pressure. Sensitivity still depends on the business, geography, and period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“较敏感”不表示每一期都同向，也不规定各行业复苏的固定先后顺序。经营周期敏感性不是股票[[Beta与总波动|beta 或总波动率]]的定义；价格还会提前反映预期与估值变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Relative sensitivity does not require the same direction in every period or a fixed order of industry recovery. Operating cyclicality does not define a stock's [[Beta与总波动|beta or total volatility]]; prices also reflect expectations and valuation changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
