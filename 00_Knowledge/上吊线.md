# 上吊线是上涨走势后实体靠近区间上端且下影线较长的K线

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A hanging man is a candle after an advance with a small body near the top of its range and a long lower shadow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上吊线是出现在上涨走势之后的一根[[K线]]：小实体位于本期价格区间上端，下影线较长，上影线没有或很短。按本课口径，下影线至少为实体长度的2倍，实体方向不限。相同形状出现在下降后则称为[[锤子线]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A hanging man is a [[K线|candlestick]] after an advance, with a small body near the top of the period's range, a long lower shadow and little or no upper shadow. The course requires the lower shadow to be at least twice the body length; either body direction is allowed. The same shape after a decline is a [[锤子线|hammer]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上吊线自身收盘仍可能靠近本期高位，因此本课要求等待后续看跌确认，例如下一期向下低开，或下一期形成阴线且收盘低于上吊线收盘。若规则要求“下一期收盘确认”，信号须等到那时才成立，不能倒填到上吊线当天。该确认是学派的信号规则，仍不保证反转发生。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because the candle may still close near its period high, the course requires subsequent bearish confirmation, such as a lower opening or a following down candle closing below the hanging man's close. A rule requiring the next period's close is available only then and cannot be backdated to the hanging-man period. Confirmation is a charting rule, not a guarantee of reversal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
