# 全局博弈是在随机收益结构上为各玩家加入私人噪声观察的一类贝叶斯博弈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A global game is a Bayesian game in which players receive private noisy observations of a randomly selected payoff structure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**全局博弈**不是与贝叶斯博弈并列的另一类模型，而是它的一个特殊族：收益取决于玩家不能完全观察的共同基本面 $\theta$，每名玩家只看到带私人噪声的信号，例如

$$
x_i=\theta+\sigma\varepsilon_i.
$$

策略因而是从自身信号到行动的[[贝叶斯策略]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **global game** is not an alternative to a Bayesian game but a special family of Bayesian games. Payoffs depend on a common fundamental $\theta$ that no player observes perfectly, and each player instead receives a privately noisy signal such as $x_i=\theta+\sigma\varepsilon_i$. A strategy is therefore a [[贝叶斯策略|Bayesian strategy]] from the player's signal to an action.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个信号同时回答两个问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*One Signal Answers Two Questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

观察 $x_i$ 后，玩家既要判断基本面 $\theta$，也要判断其他玩家大概观察到了什么。即使噪声项 $\varepsilon_i$ 彼此独立，信号 $x_i$ 仍通过共同的 $\theta$ 相关。因此，自己的信号通常会改变对 $x_{-i}$ 的条件分布，行动比较必须使用[[相关类型下的事中期望收益|给定自身信号的事中分布]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After observing $x_i$, a player learns both about the fundamental and about what the other players are likely to have observed. Even if the noise terms are independent, the signals remain correlated through the common $\theta$. The player's action comparison must therefore use the [[相关类型下的事中期望收益|interim distribution conditional on their own signal]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么常见阈值策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why Cutoff Strategies Commonly Appear*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在协调或战略互补模型中，较高信号往往同时改变基本面判断与对他人行动的判断。若收益差满足合适的单调性，最佳反应可写成[[贝叶斯阈值策略|阈值策略]]。这给出从私人信号到可计算均衡的路径，但阈值形式与均衡唯一性都不是“加入噪声”自动带来的，后者见[[全局博弈唯一性边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In coordination or strategic-complementarity models, a higher signal often changes both the assessment of the fundamental and the forecast of others' actions. Under suitable monotonicity, best responses take a [[贝叶斯阈值策略|cutoff form]]. This provides a route from private signals to a tractable equilibrium, but neither the cutoff form nor uniqueness follows automatically from adding noise; see [[全局博弈唯一性边界|the uniqueness boundary of global games]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与普通独立私人成本例子的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary with an Ordinary Independent Private-Cost Example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只给每名玩家一个彼此独立的私人收益扰动，并不自动构成全局博弈。关键结构是：同一个随机收益环境被多人带噪观察。课程中[[01_Math/03_game theory/05_贝叶斯博弈#4. 含噪声的性别博弈|独立私人扰动的性别博弈]]展示阈值求解；[[01_Math/03_game theory/05_贝叶斯博弈#7. 相关类型变体|相关信号的协同进攻变体]]才更接近全局博弈的共同基本面逻辑。但后一例现存课堂材料缺少成本先验、联合信号结构与边界规则，因而其“临界信号为 5”的数值记录仍明确标为待核。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Giving each player an independent private payoff shock does not by itself create a global game. The characteristic structure is that several players noisily observe the same random payoff environment. The course's independent-shock Battle of the Sexes illustrates cutoff calculation, whereas its correlated-signal coordinated-attack variant is closer to the common-fundamental logic of a global game. The surviving material for the latter omits the cost prior, joint signal structure, and boundary rule, so its recorded cutoff at 5 remains explicitly unverified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
