# 上升三角形是近水平高点区与逐渐抬高低点围成的收敛价格形态

上升三角形是在选定观察尺度下，价格多次接近近水平高点区，同时相继低点抬高，由近水平上界和上升下界围成的收敛形态。“上升”描述下边界的方向，并不表示价格已经向上突破。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An ascending triangle is a contracting price formation with repeated tests of an approximately level high zone and successively higher lows, bounded by a near-horizontal upper line and a rising lower line. “Ascending” describes the lower boundary, not an upward breakout that has already occurred.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上界可作为候选[[阻力位]]，下界由抬高低点连成。识别前须说明采样周期、局部高低点规则，以及“近水平”和触及的容差；若两个边界都明显倾斜，不能只因下界上升就归为上升三角形。[[对称三角形]]的上界下降、下界上升，是另一种几何结构。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The upper boundary is candidate [[阻力位|resistance]], while higher lows define the lower boundary. Specify sampling, pivot identification, and tolerances for levelness and touches. Two clearly sloping boundaries do not constitute an ascending triangle merely because the lower one rises. A [[对称三角形|symmetrical triangle]] instead combines a falling upper line with a rising lower line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如多个高点接近120，而低点依次为100、106、111，体现上界近水平、下界抬高的候选结构。传统分析把它列为偏看涨形态，但价格仍可能下破；只有按事先条件确认离开边界，才记录本次突破的实际方向，不能从名称预填结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated highs near 120 and lows at 100, 106 and 111 illustrate the candidate structure. Traditional analysis treats it as bullish-leaning, but a downside break remains possible. Record the actual direction only after price exits under the predefined confirmation rule, rather than assigning an outcome from the name.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
