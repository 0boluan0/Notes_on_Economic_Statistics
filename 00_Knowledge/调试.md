# 调试是根据已观察到的程序失败定位并修复其原因的过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Debugging locates and repairs the causes of observed program failures.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

失败可以是抛出异常，也可以是正常结束却给出错误值、错误副作用或不终止。调试从“实际行为与要求有什么差别”开始，追查产生该行为的执行状态，而不只是修改报错显示的那一行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A failure may be an exception, an incorrect value or side effect despite normal completion, or nontermination. Debugging begins with the difference between actual and required behavior and investigates the states that produced it, rather than merely editing the line displayed in an error message.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，回文函数对非回文返回 `True`，没有异常也已经失败。“缺少一次方法调用”是可能的原因，“两个名称共享了同一列表”是另一种原因；同一个错误输出可以由多层问题造成，需要证据区分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A palindrome checker returning `True` for a non-palindrome has failed without raising an exception. A missing method invocation and two names sharing one list are different possible causes. One wrong output may involve multiple faults, which evidence must distinguish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[软件测试]]产生失败证据，[[最小失败复现]]缩小观察范围，[[调试假设检验]]比较可能原因；修复后再测试，并保留[[回归测试]]。一次改动使一个用例通过，只确认了这次检查，尚未说明所有症状都得到解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[软件测试|Testing]] supplies failure evidence, a [[最小失败复现|minimized reproduction]] narrows the setting, and [[调试假设检验|hypothesis-driven debugging]] distinguishes causes. Retest after a repair and retain [[回归测试|regression checks]]. Passing one case confirms that check, not that every symptom has been explained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
