# 条件收敛是指原级数收敛而其绝对值级数发散
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditional convergence means that a series converges while its absolute-value series diverges*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\sum a_n$ 条件收敛，必须同时满足两件事：按原顺序求和有有限极限，而 $\sum|a_n|$ 发散。它说的是一种收敛类型，不是“还没有检查条件”的暂定结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conditional convergence requires both convergence in the given order and divergence of $\sum|a_n|$. It is a definite type of convergence, not a provisional conclusion awaiting further conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交错调和级数 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n-1}/n$ 是典型例子：由 [[交错级数判别]] 得到原级数收敛；由 [[调和数对数增长]] 得到绝对值级数发散。两步缺一不可。符号交错本身不等于条件收敛，例如公比 $-1/2$ 的几何级数仍然 [[绝对收敛]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the alternating harmonic series, the [[交错级数判别|alternating-series test]] proves convergence, while the [[调和数对数增长|harmonic-number bound]] proves divergence after taking absolute values. Both steps matter. Alternating signs alone do not imply conditional convergence: ratio $-1/2$ still gives [[绝对收敛|absolute convergence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这类实数级数的有限和可以照常交换有限多项；改变整个无穷序列的排列则可能改变和，见 [[条件收敛重排]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite rearrangements remain harmless, but changing the order of infinitely many terms can alter the sum; see [[条件收敛重排|rearrangement of conditionally convergent series]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
