# Conditional convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*条件收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some infinite sums settle to a finite value because the terms cancel in the given order, even though their total magnitude grows without bound. This is **conditional convergence**. The word “conditional” names a definite type of convergence; it does not mean that the calculation is unfinished or that some unspecified condition remains to be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有些无穷和按给定顺序相加时，会因各项抵消而趋于有限值；但如果只累计各项大小，总量却没有上限。这就是**条件收敛**。“条件”描述一种确定的收敛类型，并不表示计算还没有完成，或者还缺某个未说明的条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[无穷级数|infinite series]] $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ is defined by its partial sums $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}a_n$. The terms may be finite real or complex numbers. Its [[绝对收敛|magnitude series]] has partial sums $A_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}|a_n|$, where $|a_n|$ is the real absolute value or complex magnitude.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[无穷级数]] $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ 通过部分和 $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}a_n$ 定义，项可以是有限的实数或复数。其[[绝对收敛|绝对值级数]]的部分和为 $A_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}|a_n|$；这里 $|a_n|$ 表示实数绝对值或复数的模。

> [!important] Two conclusions are required / 必须同时证明两个结论
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The series is **conditionally convergent** exactly when $S_N\to S$ for a finite real or complex value $S$, while $A_N\to+\infty$. Equivalently, the original series converges and its magnitude series diverges. Since $A_N$ is nondecreasing, divergence of this nonnegative series means growth to $+\infty$, not oscillation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **条件收敛**要求 $S_N\to S$，其中 $S$ 是有限实数或复数，同时 $A_N\to+\infty$。等价地说，原级数收敛，而绝对值级数发散。由于 $A_N$ 单调不减，这条非负项级数的发散意味着趋于 $+\infty$，而非振荡。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The alternating harmonic series: both steps matter
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*交错调和级数：两步缺一不可*

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n}
=1-\frac12+\frac13-\frac14+\cdots.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first four partial sums are $1,\ 1/2,\ 5/6,\ 7/12$. They move back and forth, but a few values cannot establish convergence. The [[交错级数判别|alternating-series test]] supplies the reason: the magnitudes $b_n=1/n$ are positive, decrease, and tend to zero. Even partial sums increase, odd partial sums decrease, and the gap $S_{2k+1}-S_{2k}=1/(2k+1)$ tends to zero. The test proves they approach one common finite limit, hence so does the full sequence of partial sums.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前四个部分和是 $1,\ 1/2,\ 5/6,\ 7/12$。它们来回移动，但几个数值不能证明收敛。[[交错级数判别]]提供了理由：幅度 $b_n=1/n$ 为正、递减并趋于零。偶数部分和递增，奇数部分和递减，两者之间的差 $S_{2k+1}-S_{2k}=1/(2k+1)$ 趋于零。该判别证明它们趋于同一个有限极限，因此全部部分和也趋于这个极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
That establishes only the first requirement. Taking magnitudes gives $A_N=1+1/2+\cdots+1/N$, the harmonic numbers. The [[调和数对数增长|harmonic-number bound]] $A_N\ge\ln(N+1)$, where $\ln$ is the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]], shows $A_N\to+\infty$. Together, these two conclusions prove conditional convergence. No exact value of the original infinite sum is needed for this classification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这还只完成第一个要求。取绝对值后，$A_N=1+1/2+\cdots+1/N$ 成为调和数。由[[调和数对数增长]]中的界 $A_N\ge\ln(N+1)$（$\ln$ 是[[自然对数]]），可知 $A_N\to+\infty$。把两个结论合起来，才证明条件收敛。作这种分类并不需要知道原无穷和的精确值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What would not be enough?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*哪些事实还不够？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Failure of absolute convergence alone is insufficient: $\sum1/n$ has a divergent magnitude series, but the original series itself diverges too. Alternating signs alone are also insufficient: $1-1+1-1+\cdots$ has partial sums $1,0,1,0,\ldots$ and does not converge. Conversely, the [[几何级数|geometric series]] $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1/2)^n$ alternates yet converges absolutely, because $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(1/2)^n=2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只知道不绝对收敛还不够：$\sum1/n$ 的绝对值级数发散，但原级数也发散。只有符号交错同样不够：$1-1+1-1+\cdots$ 的部分和为 $1,0,1,0,\ldots$，并不收敛。反过来，[[几何级数]] $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1/2)^n$ 虽然交错，却绝对收敛，因为 $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(1/2)^n=2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nonnegative real terms, conditional convergence is impossible: the original and magnitude series are identical. The definition also applies to complex terms, but “positive versus negative” is then no longer a general description. For example, multiplying every term of the alternating harmonic series by $i$, where $i^2=-1$, multiplies its convergent partial sums by $i$ while leaving every magnitude $1/n$ unchanged. This gives a conditionally convergent complex series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非负实数项级数不可能条件收敛，因为原级数和绝对值级数完全相同。定义也适用于复数项，但此时不能一般地用“正项与负项”来描述。例如，把交错调和级数的每一项都乘以满足 $i^2=-1$ 的 $i$，部分和就变成原收敛部分和的 $i$ 倍，而每项的模仍为 $1/n$。这样便得到一个条件收敛的复数级数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the order remains part of the problem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么还必须在意顺序*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Permuting only finitely many terms preserves a convergent sum: after all moved positions have been passed, the partial sums are exactly the same as before. Altering finitely many finite term values also preserves the convergence type, although the sum may shift. Neither operation describes a general permutation of infinitely many terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只交换有限多项不会改变一个收敛级数的和：当相加的位置已经超过所有被移动的位置时，部分和与原来完全一致。修改有限多个有限值项也不改变收敛类型，只是和可能平移。这两种操作都不等于任意重新排列无穷多项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a conditionally convergent **real** series, [[条件收敛重排|the rearrangement theorem]] shows that an ordering using every term exactly once can change the sum, even making it any prescribed real value. This is a separate theorem, not the definition, and does not say every rearrangement changes the sum. The real-valued target statement should not be silently replaced by an arbitrary complex target statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对条件收敛的**实数**级数，[[条件收敛重排]]说明：在每项恰好使用一次的前提下，改变排列可以改变和，甚至使其等于任意指定的实数。这是另一个定理，并非定义本身，也不是说每一种重排都会改变和。其中“任意实数目标”的结论不能直接改成“任意复数目标”。
