# 对同一冲击参数求导且组合价值非零时组合久期等于市值加权久期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For a common shock parameter and nonzero portfolio value, portfolio duration is market-value-weighted component duration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限个头寸以同一币种估值，持有数量 $q_j$ 固定，单位全价 $P_j(x)>0$ 对同一个利率冲击参数 $x$ 可导。若 $V=\sum_jq_jP_j\ne0$，定义各资产对该参数的久期 $D_j=-P_j'/P_j$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider finitely many positions in one valuation currency, with fixed quantities $q_j$ and positive unit full prices $P_j(x)$ differentiable in one common rate-shock parameter $x$. If $V=\sum_jq_jP_j\ne0$, define $D_j=-P_j'/P_j$ for that parameter. Then:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\boxed{D_V=-\frac{V'}V=\sum_j w_jD_j},\qquad w_j=\frac{q_jP_j}V.$$

证明只需将 $V'=\sum_jq_jP_j'$ 代入。使用的是当前经济市值权重，不是面值权重；多空组合允许 $q_j<0$，所以权重未必非负，组合久期也未必落在各资产久期之间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substitute $V'=\sum_jq_jP_j'$ to prove the result. Weights use current economic market value, not face value. Short quantities may be negative, so weights need not be nonnegative and portfolio duration need not lie between component durations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如两个多头市值分别为 $60$ 和 $40$，同一冲击下久期分别为 $2$ 和 $5$，则组合久期为 $3.2$。若改成市值 $100$ 的多头与市值 $80$ 的空头，久期仍分别为 $2$ 和 $5$，净值为 $20$，组合久期为 $(100\times2-80\times5)/20=-10$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Long positions worth 60 and 40, with durations 2 and 5 to the same shock, give portfolio duration 3.2. A long worth 100 and a short worth 80, still with durations 2 and 5, have net value 20 and duration $(100\times2-80\times5)/20=-10$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共同参数不要求各债券起始 YTM 相等。例如可以指定 $y_j(x)=y_{j,0}+x$，这样各债券自身 YTM 同幅变动；但这未必等于某条即期曲线平移。若输入的是不同曲线、不同冲击形状或不同收益率单位的久期，不能直接平均。$V=0$ 时相对久期无定义，但[[组合DV01聚合|金额敏感度仍可相加]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A common parameter does not require equal starting YTMs: setting $y_j(x)=y_{j,0}+x$ shifts each own YTM equally, but need not shift a spot curve in parallel. Durations based on different curves, shock shapes or yield units cannot be averaged directly. At zero net value, relative duration is undefined, but [[组合DV01聚合|currency sensitivities still add]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本页聚合的是相对导数。把各债券的麦考利久期加权成一个支付时间平均，需要另核对现金流现值使用同一套贴现权重；不能从上述导数恒等式直接得到任意“组合 YTM”的麦考利久期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This result aggregates relative derivatives. Aggregating Macaulay durations into a payment-time average also requires consistent cash-flow discount weights; the derivative identity alone does not produce Macaulay duration at an arbitrary portfolio YTM.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
