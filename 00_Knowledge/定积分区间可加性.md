# 有向定积分可以沿中间端点拆分并相加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Oriented integrals add when a path through the interval is split at an intermediate bound*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 在包含 $a,b,c$ 的有限闭区间上黎曼可积，则
$$\int_a^c f=\int_a^b f+\int_b^c f.$$
当 $a<b<c$，这是把分割的两段拼起来；其他端点顺序由[[定积分]]的反向取负约定得到。这里的 $b$ 不必在 $a,c$ 之间，但经过的整段都必须允许积分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the function is integrable on a closed interval containing all three bounds, the two oriented integrals through the middle bound add to the direct integral. Ordered bounds follow by joining partitions; other orders follow by reversing signs. The middle bound need not lie between the other two, but the full traversed interval must be admissible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

尤其，$G(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$ 满足
$$G(x+h)-G(x)=\int_x^{x+h}f(t)\,dt.$$
原来从 $a$ 到 $x$ 的部分相消，只留下末端新增加的一小段。这一步是[[积分求导定理]]差商证明的起点，也适用于 $h<0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an accumulation function, subtracting two nearby values cancels their shared history and leaves only the short oriented interval between the endpoints. This identity, valid for either sign of the increment, starts the [[积分求导定理|differentiation proof]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
