# Additivity over integration intervals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定积分的区间可加性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A total accumulated over a journey can be split into totals for successive parts. For a [[定积分|definite integral]], this is **interval additivity**: retain the same function and split where the accumulation takes place. Unlike [[定积分线性性|linearity]], which separates added functions on one interval, this rule separates the interval itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一段行程的累积总量，可以拆成各段的累积量再相加。对[[定积分]]，这称为**区间可加性**：函数不变，拆开的是累积经过的区间。[[定积分线性性|线性性]]拆分同一区间内相加的函数；这里拆分的是区间本身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a real-valued function $f$ is Riemann integrable on a finite closed interval containing the three inputs $a,b,c$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实值函数 $f$ 在包含 $a,b,c$ 三个输入的某个有限闭区间上黎曼可积，则

$$\int_a^c f(x)\,dx=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx+\int_b^c f(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $a<b<c$, $b$ is an interior cut. Each [[黎曼和|Riemann sum]] with $b$ in its partition splits exactly into a left sum and a right sum. Refining these pieces gives the formula. More precisely, $f$ is integrable on $[a,c]$ if and only if its restrictions to both $[a,b]$ and $[b,c]$ are integrable. Thus the split does not require continuity, and each resulting integral exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $a<b<c$ 时，$b$ 是内部切分点。分割中含有 $b$ 的每个[[黎曼和]]，都恰好分成左段的和与右段的和；把两段都细分，就得到上述公式。准确地说，$f$ 在 $[a,c]$ 上可积，当且仅当它在 $[a,b]$ 与 $[b,c]$ 上分别可积。因此，切分不要求连续，切分出的两个积分也都有定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The middle bound may be outside the direct interval
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*中间经过的端点也可以在原区间之外*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Integrals record direction: $\int_v^u f=-\int_u^v f$ and $\int_u^u f=0$. These conventions extend additivity to every order of $a,b,c$, including equal bounds. An excursion beyond the destination contributes once going out and the opposite amount coming back.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

积分保留方向：$\int_v^u f=-\int_u^v f$，而 $\int_u^u f=0$。这些约定使可加性适用于 $a,b,c$ 的所有顺序，也包括端点相同的情况。先越过终点再折回时，多走的部分在去程与回程分别贡献相反的量。

> [!example] Go past the destination and return · 越过终点再折回
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=2$ on $[0,3]$, travel from $0$ to $1$ through $3$: $\int_0^1 2\,dx=\int_0^3 2\,dx+\int_3^1 2\,dx=6-4=2$. The number $3$ need not lie between $0$ and $1$. What matters is that $f$ is integrable over the entire interval traversed, here $[0,3]$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $f(x)=2$ 定义在 $[0,3]$。从 $0$ 走到 $1$，先经过 $3$：$\int_0^1 2\,dx=\int_0^3 2\,dx+\int_3^1 2\,dx=6-4=2$。$3$ 不必位于 $0,1$ 之间，但经过的整个区间（这里是 $[0,3]$）必须允许积分。

## Subtracting two accumulated totals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两个累积总量相减时发生了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[累积函数|accumulation function]] $G(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$ keeps the start $a$ fixed and lets the endpoint $x$ move. For $a$, $x$ and $x+h$ inside the same interval on which $f$ is integrable, additivity gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[累积函数]] $G(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$ 固定起点 $a$，让终点 $x$ 变化。当 $a,x,x+h$ 都位于 $f$ 可积的同一区间中时，可加性给出

$$G(x+h)-G(x)=\int_x^{x+h}f(t)\,dt.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The shared accumulation from $a$ cancels, leaving just the short oriented segment between the endpoints. This works for $h<0$ as well as $h>0$. Dividing by $h$ and then taking a limit, under the needed continuity condition, is the starting point of the [[积分求导定理|theorem on differentiating an accumulation integral]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $a$ 开始的共有累积部分抵消，只留下两终点之间那一小段有向积分。$h<0$ 与 $h>0$ 都适用。再除以 $h$，在所需的连续条件下取极限，就是[[积分求导定理]]证明的起点。

> [!warning] Splitting does not remove a singularity · 切开并不会消除奇点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The ordinary formula assumes integrability across every piece used. For $f(x)=1/x$ on a route crossing $0$, the two sides cannot be treated as finite ordinary integrals and cancelled. The separate limits required by an [[反常积分|improper integral]] must be checked first.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 普通公式以所用各段都可积为前提。若 $f(x)=1/x$ 的路径经过 $0$，不能把两侧当成有限的普通积分再抵消。必须先检查[[反常积分]]要求的各个单独极限。
