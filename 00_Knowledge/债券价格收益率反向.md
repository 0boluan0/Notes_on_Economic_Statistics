# 固定非负未来现金流的债券价格随单一贴现收益率严格下降
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A bond's fixed nonnegative future cash flows have a strictly decreasing price as the single discount yield rises*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定支付时点 $t_k>0$ 与支付额 $CF_k\ge0$，并至少有一笔 $CF_k>0$。若所有支付用同一每期有效收益率 $i>-1$ 表示价格，则 $P(i)=\sum_k CF_k(1+i)^{-t_k}$ 随 $i$ 严格下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix payment dates $t_k>0$ and amounts $CF_k\ge0$, with at least one positive payment. If price is represented using one effective period yield $i>-1$, then $P(i)=\sum_k CF_k(1+i)^{-t_k}$ is strictly decreasing in $i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每笔正支付在收益率上升时现值更低；也可直接求导：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each positive payment has a lower present value at a higher yield. Equivalently, differentiate:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P'(i)=-\sum_k t_kCF_k(1+i)^{-t_k-1}<0.$$

例如，两年后到期、每年息票 $5$、面值 $100$ 的价格，在收益率 $4\%$ 时约为 $101.8861$，在 $6\%$ 时约为 $98.1666$。即使收益率为负，只要 $i>-1$，同一单调性仍成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A two-year bond with annual coupons of 5 and face value 100 is worth about 101.8861 at 4% and 98.1666 at 6%. The same monotonicity holds for negative yields as long as $i>-1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是一条“其他现金流条件固定”的价格—收益率关系，不等于任意一次市场新闻必使价格如何变化。收益率曲线各期限可朝不同方向移动；浮息、违约和嵌入期权也可能改变支付，不能把它们全部压成这条固定现金流命题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a price-yield relation with cash flows held fixed, not a prediction of every price response to market news. Different curve maturities can move in opposite directions; floating coupons, default, and embedded options may alter payments. Those cases cannot all be reduced to this fixed-cash-flow statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
