# 道氏趋势层级把市场变动分为主要运动次级折返和微小波动

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dow's trend hierarchy separates market movement into primary trends, secondary reactions and minor fluctuations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[道氏理论]]把市场变动分为三个相互嵌套的观察层级：主要运动描述较长期的总体多头或空头方向；次级折返是在主要方向内部、与之相反的重要回调；微小波动是构成前两者的更短期变化。因此，多头市场中的一次次级下跌并不单凭方向就成为主要空头市场。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[道氏理论|Dow theory]] distinguishes three nested scales: the primary movement is the broad bull or bear direction; a secondary reaction is a material counter-move within it; minor fluctuations form the shorter-term components of both. A secondary decline within a bull market does not, by direction alone, establish a primary bear market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本课用“主要趋势通常一年以上”“次级折返通常三周至数月、回吐前一主要方向行情约三分之一至三分之二”“微小趋势通常更短”帮助识别。这些是课程的经验尺度，不是所有市场必须满足的自然常数；实际693页也列出微小运动可能延续更久的例外，不能把时长界线当成互不重叠的机械分箱。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course's typical scales are a year or more for primary trends, three weeks to several months and roughly one-third to two-thirds retracement for secondary reactions, and shorter intervals for minor movements. These are conventional guides, not universal constants. Page 693 itself allows longer minor movements, so duration alone is not a set of disjoint mechanical bins.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如一段上涨从100到130，随后回落到120，回吐的是前段30点涨幅中的10点，即三分之一，而不是价格130的三分之一。还须判断它在所选主要趋势中的位置，不能仅凭这个比例自动确认层级。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An advance from 100 to 130 followed by a decline to 120 retraces 10 of the prior 30 points, or one-third—not one-third of 130. Its position within the chosen primary trend still matters; the ratio alone does not identify the hierarchy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
