# 可积函数的线性组合可以逐项积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A linear combination of integrable functions can be integrated term by term*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f,g$ 在 $[a,b]$ 黎曼可积，$\alpha,\beta$ 是常数，则
$$\int_a^b[\alpha f(x)+\beta g(x)]\,dx=\alpha\int_a^b f(x)\,dx+\beta\int_a^b g(x)\,dx.$$
这是[[黎曼和]]中乘法分配律在极限下的保留：必须使用同一个区间，系数也不能随积分变量变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Integration commutes with linear combinations of integrable functions with constant coefficients on the same interval. This follows by distributing each Riemann sum and taking limits; variable coefficients cannot simply be pulled outside.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\int_0^1(3x^2-2)\,dx=3(1/3)-2(1)=-1$。这条规则不扩展为乘积规则：$\int_0^1 x\cdot x\,dx=1/3$，而 $(\int_0^1x\,dx)^2=1/4$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The integral of $3x^2-2$ over the unit interval is $-1$. Linearity does not make integration multiplicative: the integral of $x^2$ is $1/3$, whereas the square of the integral of $x$ is $1/4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
