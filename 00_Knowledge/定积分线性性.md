# Linearity of the definite integral
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定积分的线性性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If two taps contribute water over the same time interval, the combined amount is the sum of their individual amounts. Doubling one tap’s flow doubles its contribution. **Linearity** is the rule that lets a [[定积分|definite integral]] combine and rescale contributions in this way. It applies to signed quantities too, such as inflow minus outflow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两只水龙头在同一段时间供水，合计水量等于各自水量之和；把其中一只的流量加倍，它的贡献也加倍。**线性性**就是允许[[定积分]]这样相加和缩放贡献的规则。流入减去流出等带符号的量也适用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $f$ and $g$ are real-valued Riemann-integrable functions on a finite interval $[a,b]$, where $a<b$. Let $\alpha,\beta$ be fixed real numbers. The function $\alpha f+\beta g$ is also integrable, and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实值函数 $f,g$ 在有限区间 $[a,b]$ 上黎曼可积，且 $a<b$；$\alpha,\beta$ 为固定实数。函数 $\alpha f+\beta g$ 也可积，并且

$$\int_a^b[\alpha f(x)+\beta g(x)]\,dx=\alpha\int_a^b f(x)\,dx+\beta\int_a^b g(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficients can be negative or zero. “Fixed” means independent of the variable being integrated: if $t$ is a separate parameter, then $t\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx=\int_0^1 t f(x)\,dx$ for each fixed $t$. The bounds must describe the same interval on both sides. Equal or reversed bounds also satisfy the identity under the usual zero and sign conventions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数可以为负数或零。“固定”指不随积分变量变化：若 $t$ 是另一个参数，则对每个固定 $t$，都有 $t\int_0^1 f(x)\,dx=\int_0^1t f(x)\,dx$。等式两边必须使用同一组积分端点。按照同端点取零、反向取负的约定，相同或反向端点也满足该公式。

## Why summing and scaling survive the limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么相加、缩放可以带进极限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[黎曼和|Riemann sum]] approximates an integral by adding sampled height times piece width. Use the same partition and sample points for both functions. Write $S_f=\sum_i f(\xi_i)\Delta x_i$ and $S_g=\sum_i g(\xi_i)\Delta x_i$, where $\xi_i$ is a sample point and $\Delta x_i$ the width of its piece. Ordinary distribution gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[黎曼和]]用“采样高度乘以小段宽度”再相加来近似积分。给两函数使用同一分割与采样点，记 $S_f=\sum_i f(\xi_i)\Delta x_i$、$S_g=\sum_i g(\xi_i)\Delta x_i$；其中 $\xi_i$ 是采样点，$\Delta x_i$ 是对应段宽。普通乘法分配律给出

$$\sum_i[\alpha f(\xi_i)+\beta g(\xi_i)]\Delta x_i=\alpha S_f+\beta S_g.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because both functions are integrable, sufficiently fine partitions make $S_f$ close to its integral and $S_g$ close to its integral, regardless of sample choices. The error in their combination is at most $|\alpha|$ times the first error plus $|\beta|$ times the second. It therefore also tends to zero. This explains both the new function’s integrability and its integral.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为两函数可积，分割足够细时，无论怎样采样，$S_f,S_g$ 都分别接近各自的积分。组合后的误差不超过“$|\alpha|$ 乘第一个误差，加上 $|\beta|$ 乘第二个误差”，所以也趋于零。这同时说明了组合函数为什么可积，以及积分为什么具有上述值。

> [!example] Separate the terms · 分开计算各项
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $[0,1]$, $\int_0^1x^2\,dx=1/3$ and $\int_0^1 1\,dx=1$. Thus $\int_0^1(3x^2-2)\,dx=3(1/3)-2(1)=-1$. The $-2$ is a constant function over an interval of width one; it does not disappear.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[0,1]$ 上，$\int_0^1x^2\,dx=1/3$、$\int_0^1 1\,dx=1$，所以 $\int_0^1(3x^2-2)\,dx=3(1/3)-2(1)=-1$。这里的 $-2$ 是贯穿整个宽度为一的区间的常数函数，不能丢掉。

> [!warning] Products are different · 乘积不能照搬
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Linearity permits addition and constant scaling, not multiplication of integrals. For instance, $\int_0^1x\cdot x\,dx=1/3$, whereas $(\int_0^1x\,dx)^2=1/4$. A factor depending on $x$ must remain inside unless another justified method handles it. A finite sum follows by repeating linearity; an infinite series requires separate justification before exchanging sum and integral.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 线性性允许相加、提出常数，不允许把乘积的积分改成积分的乘积。例如 $\int_0^1x\cdot x\,dx=1/3$，而 $(\int_0^1x\,dx)^2=1/4$。随 $x$ 变化的因子要留在积分号内，除非另外有合法方法处理它。有限多项可以反复使用线性性；无穷级数与积分交换则还需要额外论证。
