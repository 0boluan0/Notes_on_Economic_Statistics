# 实际GDP增长衡量剔除价格变动后的产出量变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Real GDP growth measures changes in output volume after removing price changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际 GDP 增长比较同一可比价格或官方链式数量口径下的[[国内生产总值]]。若 $Y_t^R$ 是可比实际产出指数，增长率为 $g_t=Y_t^R/Y_{t-1}^R-1$；现价 GDP 的增加同时包含数量和价格变化，不能直接当作实际增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Real GDP growth compares [[国内生产总值|GDP]] using comparable prices or an official chain-volume measure. For a comparable real-output index $Y_t^R$, growth is $g_t=Y_t^R/Y_{t-1}^R-1$. Nominal GDP growth includes both volume and price changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如实际指数从 100 到 103，实际增长为 3%；即使同期现价金额从 100 到 108，也不能说产出量增长 8%。读取数字还要分清同比、环比、季调与折年率：季调季度环比 1% 若按该速度复合折年，得到 $(1.01)^4-1\approx4.06\%$，不是已经观察到的全年同比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A real index rising from 100 to 103 indicates 3% volume growth, even if nominal output rises by 8%. Also distinguish year-on-year, quarter-on-quarter, seasonal adjustment, and annualization. Annualizing a 1% quarterly increase gives $(1.01)^4-1\approx4.06\%$; it is not an observed annual growth rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增长为正不代表处于[[潜在产出]]之上，增长变慢也不等于产出下降。识别周期时，应结合[[产出缺口]]和[[经济数据的实时口径]]；链式数量分项也未必能像现价项目一样直接相加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive growth does not imply output above [[潜在产出|potential]], and slower growth need not mean falling output. Cycle analysis also needs the [[产出缺口|output gap]] and [[经济数据的实时口径|real-time data vintage]]. Chain-volume components need not be additive like current-price components.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
