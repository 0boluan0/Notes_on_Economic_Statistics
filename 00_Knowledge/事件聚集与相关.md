# 事件聚集与横截面相关要求在推断中保留共同协方差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Event clustering and cross-sectional dependence require inference to retain common covariance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 多家公司的事件窗在同一日历时期重叠时，异常收益会受共同市场、行业或政策冲击影响。把 $N$ 个事件当作独立观测会遗漏跨证券协方差，通常使平均 [[异常收益|AR]]/[[累计异常收益|CAR]] 的标准误过小。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When event windows for multiple firms overlap in calendar time, abnormal returns can share market, industry, or policy shocks. Treating $N$ events as independent omits cross-security covariance and will often understate the standard error of average AR or CAR.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“样本公司很多”不等于有效独立信息很多。完全聚集的政策公告可能只有少数共同时间冲击；应采用能保留横截面相关的事件时间组合、合适的聚类/随机化设计或多元回归，并让标准误层级对应冲击实际发生的层级。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A large number of firms does not imply an equally large number of independent pieces of information. A fully clustered policy announcement may contain only a few common time shocks. Use an event-time portfolio, a suitable clustering or randomisation design, or a multivariate regression that preserves cross-sectional dependence, with uncertainty clustered at the level where shocks actually occur.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无事件窗重叠可支持横截面不相关，但并非普遍保证；供应链、共同所有权或行业消息也能让不同日期事件相关。应画出事件的日历分布，并检查结论是否由少数日期或公司主导。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Non-overlapping event windows can support cross-sectional independence but do not guarantee it universally. Supply chains, common ownership, or industry news can correlate events on different dates. Show the calendar distribution of events and check whether a few dates or firms dominate the result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 同一天政策影响 200 家银行，能否按 200 个独立事件计算标准误？
>
> **答案：** 不能直接这样做。它们共享同一政策与市场冲击，必须保留横截面协方差；有效信息量可能远小于公司数。
