# 异常值、杠杆与影响诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Outliers, leverage, and influence diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 区分 observation 在 outcome direction 不寻常、在 predictor space 不寻常，以及它是否真的改变 regression conclusion。
> **具体锚点：** 一个极高 income household 可能有 high leverage；若消费也恰好沿原趋势，它未必 influential。另一个 residual 很大的普通 household 可能是 outlier，却不一定明显改变 slope。
> **核心难点：** outlier、leverage 与 influence 是三个不同判断。raw residual 会因 leverage 而失真，单个 threshold 也不能替代 leave-one-out sensitivity 与 data provenance 检查。
> **为什么重要：** 少数 rows 可以改变 coefficients、standard errors、p-values 和 fitted values；自动删点既可能掩盖 data error，也可能把真实但稀有的 population information 删除。
> **继续：** 先用 [[#三个概念必须分开]] 建立对象，再读 [[#Hat matrix 与 leverage]]、[[#Studentized residuals 识别 outcome-direction 异常]] 与 [[#Influence 是删除 observation 后结论改变多少]]；若问题是 regressors 整体缺少独立 variation，转到 [[多重共线性与设计矩阵诊断]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** These diagnostics distinguish an observation that is unusual in the outcome direction, unusual in predictor space, and genuinely capable of changing a regression conclusion.
> **Concrete anchor:** A very high-income household may have high leverage. If its consumption lies on the existing trend, it need not be influential. Another household with an ordinary income but a large residual may be an outlier without materially changing the slope.
> **Central difficulty:** Outlier status, leverage, and influence are three separate judgments. Raw residuals are distorted by leverage, and a single threshold cannot replace leave-one-out sensitivity and data-provenance checks.
> **Why it matters:** A few rows can change coefficients, standard errors, p-values, and fitted values. Automatic deletion may hide a data error or remove rare but genuine population information.
> **Continue with:** Begin by [[#三个概念必须分开|separating the three concepts]], then study [[#Hat matrix 与 leverage|leverage through the hat matrix]], [[#Studentized residuals 识别 outcome-direction 异常|studentized residuals]], and [[#Influence 是删除 observation 后结论改变多少|case-deletion influence]]. If the regressors as a whole lack independent variation, continue to [[多重共线性与设计矩阵诊断|multicollinearity and design-matrix diagnostics]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三个概念必须分开
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The three concepts must be separated*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

异常值指 y 方向 residual 大；高杠杆点在 X 空间远离主体；影响点是删除后显著改变估计。Cook's distance、DFBETAs 和 leverage 只提示检查，应核实数据、报告敏感性，而非按阈值自动删除。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An outlier has a large residual in the $y$ direction. A high-leverage point lies far from the bulk of the data in $X$ space. An influential point substantially changes the estimate when removed. Cook's distance, DFBETAs, and leverage are prompts for investigation: verify the data and report sensitivity rather than deleting observations automatically at a threshold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 概念 | 依赖什么 | 问的问题 | 可能但不必然导致 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outlier | $y_i$ 相对 fitted value | outcome 是否不符合 fitted trend？ | large residual variance、poor fit |
| Leverage | predictor row $x_i$ | 该 predictor combination 是否远离其余 sample？ | 改变 slope 的潜力 |
| Influence | $x_i$ 与 $y_i$ 共同作用 | 删除 row 后目标结论改变多少？ | coefficient/test/prediction sensitivity |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Concept | What it depends on | Question | Possible but not necessary consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outlier | $y_i$ relative to its fitted value | Does the outcome depart from the fitted trend? | Larger residual variance or poor fit |
| Leverage | The predictor row $x_i$ | Is this predictor combination far from the rest of the sample? | Potential to change the slope |
| Influence | The joint action of $x_i$ and $y_i$ | How much does the target conclusion change when the row is removed? | Coefficient, test, or prediction sensitivity |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个 high-leverage point 若恰好落在 regression surface 上，residual 可很小而 influence 也有限；一个 vertical outlier 若在 $X$ center 附近，可能抬高 residual variance 却不明显旋转 fitted plane；既 high leverage 又有 large residual 的 row 最容易成为 influential point。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A high-leverage point that lies on the regression surface may have a small residual and limited influence. A vertical outlier near the centre of $X$ may raise residual variance without rotating the fitted plane much. A row with both high leverage and a large residual is most likely to be influential.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Hat matrix 与 leverage
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The hat matrix and leverage*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OLS fitted values 可写成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
OLS fitted values can be written as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\hat y=X(X'X)^{-1}X'y=Hy,
$$

其中 $H=X(X'X)^{-1}X'$ 是 hat matrix。diagonal element
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $H=X(X'X)^{-1}X'$ is the hat matrix. Its diagonal element
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
h_{ii}=x_i'(X'X)^{-1}x_i
$$

衡量 row $i$ 的 predictor combination 对自身 fitted value 的 leverage。它只依赖 $X$，满足 $0\le h_{ii}\le1$，且
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
measures the leverage of row $i$'s predictor combination on its own fitted value. It depends only on $X$, satisfies $0\le h_{ii}\le1$, and obeys
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sum_{i=1}^n h_{ii}=\operatorname{tr}(H)=k,
$$

所以 average leverage 是 $k/n$，$k$ 包括 intercept。$2k/n$ 或 $3k/n$ 常被用作 screening line，但不是 population law；balanced designed experiment 可能有规律的高 leverage，rare but valid group 也可能自然远离 center。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
so average leverage is $k/n$, where $k$ includes the intercept. Values such as $2k/n$ or $3k/n$ are common screening lines rather than population laws. A balanced designed experiment may contain systematically high leverage, and a rare but valid group may naturally lie far from the centre.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

leverage 是 Mahalanobis-like distance in design space，不等于某个单独 predictor 的 univariate extreme。一个 row 的每个 $X_j$ 都在普通 range 内，但 combination 极少见，仍可有 high leverage；因此 multiple regression 不能只逐列画 boxplots。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Leverage is a Mahalanobis-like distance in design space, not an extreme value in one predictor alone. Every $X_j$ in a row may lie within an ordinary marginal range while their combination is rare and highly leveraged. Multiple regression therefore cannot rely only on separate predictor boxplots.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Studentized residuals 识别 outcome-direction 异常
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Studentized residuals for outcome-direction anomalies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

raw residual 是 $e_i=y_i-\hat y_i$。在 classical homoskedastic model 下，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The raw residual is $e_i=y_i-\hat y_i$. Under the classical homoskedastic model,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}(e_i\mid X)=\sigma^2(1-h_{ii}).
$$

因此不同 rows 的 raw residuals 天生不具有相同 variance；high-leverage row 的 residual 被 fitted surface 拉向自己，甚至可能显得异常地小。internally studentized residual 用 full-sample $s$ 标准化：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Raw residuals therefore do not have equal variances across rows. A high-leverage row pulls the fitted surface toward itself and may consequently display an unusually small residual. The internally studentized residual uses the full-sample estimate $s$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
r_i=\frac{e_i}{s\sqrt{1-h_{ii}}}.
$$

externally studentized 或 deleted residual 则用删除 row $i$ 后的 $s_{(i)}$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The externally studentized, or deleted, residual uses $s_{(i)}$ estimated after removing row $i$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
t_i=\frac{e_i}{s_{(i)}\sqrt{1-h_{ii}}}.
$$

因为可疑 observation 不再参与估计 denominator，external studentization 更适合发现它自己抬高 $s$ 后被 masking 的情况。像 $|t_i|>2$ 或 3 的规则只是 flags；同时看 sample size、multiple comparisons、distributional assumptions 与 scientific plausibility。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because the suspicious observation does not contribute to the denominator, external studentization is better able to reveal an observation that masks itself by inflating $s$. Rules such as $|t_i|>2$ or three are flags only; sample size, multiple comparisons, distributional assumptions, and scientific plausibility must also be considered.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Influence 是删除 observation 后结论改变多少
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Influence is how much the conclusion changes after deleting an observation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

记 $\hat\beta_{(i)}$ 为删除 row $i$ 后的 estimate。influence diagnostics 都是在近似或汇总 $\hat\beta-\hat\beta_{(i)}$ 对不同 targets 的影响：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\hat\beta_{(i)}$ denote the estimate after row $i$ is deleted. Influence diagnostics approximate or summarise the difference $\hat\beta-\hat\beta_{(i)}$ for different targets:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| Diagnostic | 关注 target | 读法 |
|---|---|---|
| Cook's $D_i$ | 整组 fitted values / coefficient vector 的 aggregate change | residual 与 leverage 的 joint signal |
| DFFITS$_i$ | observation $i$ 的 fitted value 改变 | prediction at that design point 的 sensitivity |
| DFBETA$_{ij}$ | coefficient $j$ 的改变 | 哪个 parameter 被该 row 推动 |
| COVRATIO | coefficient covariance volume | uncertainty estimate 是否被 row 改变 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Diagnostic | Target | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Cook's $D_i$ | Aggregate change in fitted values or the coefficient vector | A joint residual-and-leverage signal |
| DFFITS$_i$ | Change in the fitted value for observation $i$ | Sensitivity of prediction at that design point |
| DFBETA$_{ij}$ | Change in coefficient $j$ | Which parameter is being driven by the row |
| COVRATIO | Volume of the coefficient covariance matrix | Whether the row changes estimated uncertainty |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在线性 homoskedastic OLS 中，一个常见 Cook's distance form 是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In homoskedastic linear OLS, a common form of Cook's distance is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
D_i=\frac{e_i^2}{k s^2}\frac{h_{ii}}{(1-h_{ii})^2}.
$$

formula 清楚显示：large residual 或 large leverage 单独不一定产生极端 $D_i$；两者结合才最危险。DFFITS 可写成 externally studentized residual 乘 leverage factor：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The formula makes clear that a large residual or large leverage alone need not produce an extreme $D_i$; their combination is most dangerous. DFFITS can be written as an externally studentized residual multiplied by a leverage factor:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
DFFITS_i=t_i\sqrt{\frac{h_{ii}}{1-h_{ii}}}.
$$

Cook's $D>1$、$4/n$、DFFITS $>2\sqrt{k/n}$ 或 DFBETA $>2/\sqrt n$ 都只是不同教材的软件 screening conventions。最可靠的做法是看 relative pattern、明确 target coefficient，并实际比较 full-sample 与 case-deleted estimates/intervals。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rules such as Cook's $D>1$, $4/n$, DFFITS above $2\sqrt{k/n}$, or DFBETA above $2/\sqrt n$ are screening conventions that vary across texts and software. A more reliable practice is to inspect the relative pattern, specify the target coefficient, and compare full-sample and case-deleted estimates and intervals directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 四种几何情形
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Four geometric cases*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 情形 | Residual | Leverage | 通常现象 |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 普通点 | 小 | 低 | 对 fit 贡献普通 |
| vertical outlier near center | 大 | 低 | 可能抬高 $s$，但 slope change 有限 |
| extreme $X$ on trend | 小 | 高 | 有改变 slope 的潜力，但当前 influence 可小 |
| extreme $X$ off trend | 大 | 高 | 最可能显著改变 coefficients 与 predictions |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Case | Residual | Leverage | Typical behaviour |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Ordinary point | Small | Low | Ordinary contribution to the fit |
| Vertical outlier near the centre | Large | Low | May inflate $s$ while changing the slope little |
| Extreme $X$ lying on the trend | Small | High | Has potential to change the slope but may have little current influence |
| Extreme $X$ away from the trend | Large | High | Most likely to change coefficients and predictions materially |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也是为什么 residual plot、leverage plot 与 Cook/DFBETAs 要一起看。任何单独 measure 都只回答一部分问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is why residual plots, leverage plots, and Cook or DFBETA measures should be read together. Each individual measure answers only part of the question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 发现 flag 后的处理顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What to do after an observation is flagged*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **核实 provenance：** 回到 source record 检查 transcription、units、decimal point、merge、duplicate 与 impossible values。
2. **确认 population：** observation 是否属于 target population，还是违反 sampling frame？稀有不等于错误。
3. **检查 specification：** 它是否暴露 missing nonlinear term、group interaction、wrong link/scale 或 unmodelled cluster？
4. **定位 target sensitivity：** 比较 full data 与 leave-one-out / leave-cluster-out 的 key coefficients、intervals、predictions，而不只看 global $R^2$。
5. **选择有理由的 action：** correction、exclusion rule、robust model、transformation 或保留并报告 sensitivity；理由应在看到 desired result 之前就成立。
6. **透明报告：** 给出 flagged cases 的判据、是否修改 data、主结果与 sensitivity result。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. **Verify provenance:** Return to the source record and check transcription, units, decimal points, merges, duplicates, and impossible values.
2. **Confirm the population:** Decide whether the observation belongs to the target population or violates the sampling frame. Rarity is not an error.
3. **Inspect the specification:** Ask whether it reveals a missing nonlinear term, group interaction, wrong link or scale, or unmodelled cluster.
4. **Locate target sensitivity:** Compare key coefficients, intervals, and predictions under full-data and leave-one-out or leave-cluster-out fits rather than looking only at global $R^2$.
5. **Choose a justified action:** Correct, apply a prespecified exclusion rule, use a robust model or transformation, or retain and report sensitivity. The reason should be valid before seeing the preferred result.
6. **Report transparently:** State the flagging criteria, any data changes, the main result, and the sensitivity result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自动删除所有 Cook's $D$ 或 studentized residual 超阈值的 rows 会改变 sampling process，并可能造成 selective reporting。若 observation 是 data error，应纠正或依据 documented rule 排除；若是真实 population member，保留它并承认结论依赖它，往往比“清洗到显著”更科学。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Automatically deleting every row above a Cook or studentized-residual threshold changes the sampling process and may create selective reporting. Correct or exclude an observation under a documented rule when it is a data error. When it is a genuine population member, retaining it and acknowledging dependence of the conclusion is often more scientific than cleaning until significance appears.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Clustered、panel 与 time-series data 的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary for clustered, panel, and time-series data*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

ordinary case-deletion formulas 把 rows 当作 basic observational units。若 treatment 在 school level 分配、同一 firm 有多年 observations，删除一个 row 可能低估一个 whole cluster 对 estimate 的 influence。应把 sampling/assignment unit 与 dependence structure 纳入 leave-cluster-out 或 specialised influence analysis，并使用匹配的 covariance estimator。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary case-deletion formulas treat rows as the basic observational units. If treatment is assigned at school level or a firm contributes many annual observations, deleting one row may understate the influence of an entire cluster. The sampling or assignment unit and dependence structure should enter a leave-cluster-out or specialised influence analysis together with a matching covariance estimator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

time-series 中 isolated spike 还可能是 intervention、structural break 或 innovation，而不是可以删除的 iid outlier。先建模 chronology；关于 error dependence 见 [[异方差与自相关]]，关于 trend/break-related specification 见 [[趋势、单位根与差分]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a time series, an isolated spike may represent an intervention, structural break, or innovation rather than a disposable independent outlier. Model chronology first. See [[异方差与自相关|heteroskedasticity and autocorrelation]] for error dependence and [[趋势、单位根与差分|trends, unit roots, and differencing]] for trend-related specification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 完整例子：同一 high-leverage point 的两种结局
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: two outcomes for the same high-leverage design point*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设大多数 households 的 annual income 在 20–80 thousand 之间，另有一户为 300 thousand。仅看 $X$，它有 high leverage。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose most households have annual incomes between twenty and eighty thousand, while one household has income of three hundred thousand. Based on $X$ alone, that household has high leverage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 若其 consumption 接近由中等收入 households 外推的 trend，residual 小；删除它可能让 slope standard error 变大，因为它提供了远端 $X$ variation，但 point estimate 变化不大。它 high leverage，却未必 influential。
- 若其 consumption 远低于 trend，residual 与 leverage 都大；fitted line 会向它旋转，删除后 slope、Cook's $D$ 与 relevant predictions 都明显改变。它很可能 influential。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- If its consumption lies near the extrapolated trend from middle-income households, the residual is small. Deleting it may increase the slope standard error by removing distant $X$ variation while changing the point estimate little. It has high leverage but need not be influential.
- If its consumption lies far below the trend, both the residual and leverage are large. The fitted line rotates toward it, and deletion materially changes the slope, Cook's distance, and relevant predictions. It is likely influential.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两种情况都不能只凭 income 极端就删除。应先核实 units、确认 high-income household 属于 target population，并问 model 是否需要 nonlinear income effect。若研究对象只包括 ordinary households，population rule 可以排除；若目标覆盖 full income distribution，这一 observation 可能是最有信息、也最需要谨慎建模的一点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Neither case justifies deletion merely because income is extreme. Verify units, confirm that the high-income household belongs to the target population, and ask whether the model needs a nonlinear income effect. A population rule may exclude it if the study concerns ordinary households only. If the target spans the full income distribution, this observation may be both the most informative and the most demanding to model carefully.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*

### 用自己的话解释：outlier、high leverage 与 influential point 分别是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: what are an outlier, a high-leverage point, and an influential point?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> outlier 在 outcome direction 对 fitted model 有大 residual；high-leverage point 的 predictor combination 远离其余 sample；influential point 是删除后让目标 coefficient、prediction 或 test 明显改变的 observation。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An outlier has a large residual in the outcome direction. A high-leverage point has a predictor combination far from the rest of the sample. An influential point materially changes a target coefficient, prediction, or test when removed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 高杠杆点是否一定是异常点？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Is a high-leverage point necessarily an outlier?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不一定。它在 X 空间偏远，但可能非常符合回归关系；影响取决于杠杆与残差共同作用。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. It is unusual in $X$ space but may fit the regression relationship very well. Influence depends on the combination of leverage and residual size.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 high-leverage observation 的 raw residual 可能具有欺骗性？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can the raw residual of a high-leverage observation be misleading?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它会把 fitted surface 拉向自己，而且 $\operatorname{Var}(e_i\mid X)=\sigma^2(1-h_{ii})$ 随 leverage 变化。应使用 studentized/deleted residual，并结合 leverage 与 case-deletion influence。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It pulls the fitted surface toward itself, and $\operatorname{Var}(e_i\mid X)=\sigma^2(1-h_{ii})$ varies with leverage. Use a studentized or deleted residual together with leverage and case-deletion influence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Cook's distance 很大时，第一步为什么不是删除？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is deletion not the first step when Cook's distance is large?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> Cook's $D$ 只说明该 row 对 fitted model 敏感；它可能是 data error、wrong units、missing nonlinear structure，也可能是真实而有信息的 rare case。先核实 provenance、population 与 specification，再做有理由的 sensitivity analysis。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Cook's $D$ indicates sensitivity of the fitted model to the row. The row may be a data error, have incorrect units, expose missing nonlinear structure, or be a genuine informative rare case. Verify provenance, population, and specification before a justified sensitivity analysis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### panel data 中为什么应考虑 leave-one-cluster-out，而不只 leave-one-row-out？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why may panel data require leave-one-cluster-out rather than only leave-one-row-out analysis?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 同一单位的 rows 共享 shocks 和 assignment，真正独立的信息单位可能是 person、firm 或 region。逐 row 删除会低估整个 cluster 对 coefficient 与 covariance 的影响。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Rows from the same unit share shocks and assignment, so the independent information unit may be a person, firm, or region. Deleting one row at a time can understate the influence of the whole cluster on coefficients and covariance.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
