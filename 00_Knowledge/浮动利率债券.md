# 浮动利率债券是按约定利率基准和重置规则调整息票率的债券

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A floating-rate bond resets its coupon rate using a specified interest-rate benchmark and reset rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

浮动利率债券是息票率随约定利率基准重置的[[债券]]。常见简单结构为“基准利率加固定利差”：固定的是加点，不是两者相加后的[[债券票面利率|息票率]]。需要另看基准取值时点、重置频率、计息和支付规则；重置一次不一定就支付一次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A floating-rate bond is a [[债券|bond]] whose coupon rate resets with an agreed interest-rate benchmark. A common simple structure adds a fixed spread to the benchmark. The spread is fixed, not the resulting [[债券票面利率|coupon rate]]. Benchmark observation, resetting, accrual and payment rules must be identified separately: a reset need not coincide with a payment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在本金 $F$ 不变、没有利率上限或下限、每期采用一个基准值的简化条款下，令 $L_k$ 为第 $k$ 期采用的年化基准利率，$s$ 为同口径固定利差，$\tau_k$ 为该期计息年数，则约定息票为：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For simplified terms with constant principal $F$, no cap or floor and one benchmark observation per accrual period, let $L_k$ be the annualised benchmark for period $k$, $s$ the spread on the same basis and $\tau_k$ the accrual year fraction. The promised coupon is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
c_k=L_k+s,\qquad C_k=F c_k\tau_k.
$$

例如，$F=100$、$s=1\%$、每期一年，若连续两期的基准分别为 $3\%$ 和 $5\%$，两期息票率为 $4\%$、$6\%$，对应息票 4、6。固定加点 1% 并没有把它变成[[固定息票债券]]；这个一年一期例子也不是美国国债 FRN 的实际支付日程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $F=100$, $s=1\%$ and one-year periods, benchmark observations of $3\%$ and $5\%$ give coupon rates of $4\%$ and $6\%$, hence coupons of 4 and 6. A fixed 1% spread does not make this a [[固定息票债券|fixed-coupon bond]]. This annual-period illustration is not the actual US Treasury FRN payment schedule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里变化的对象是利率基准，不是消费物价指数；后者对应[[通胀挂钩债券]]的指数化安排。合同若含上限、下限、复合计息或其他调整，必须按真实公式算息票，不能机械套用上述简单式。利率重置也不消除发行人的履约风险。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The changing reference is an interest rate, not a consumer-price index; the latter belongs to [[通胀挂钩债券|inflation-linked arrangements]]. Caps, floors, compounded accrual or other adjustments require the actual contractual formula rather than the simple expression above. Rate resetting does not eliminate issuer default risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
