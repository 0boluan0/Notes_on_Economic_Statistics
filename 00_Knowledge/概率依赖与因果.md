# 概率依赖不能单独证明因果关系
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Probabilistic dependence alone cannot establish a causal relationship*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 若存在可测取值集合 $A,B$ 使
> $$
> P(X\in A,Y\in B)
> \neq P(X\in A)P(Y\in B),
> $$
> 则观察分布中的 $X$ 与 $Y$ 有概率依赖。等价地，在条件概率有定义时，知道一方落入某个集合会改变另一方落入某个集合的概率。这仍没有回答“若干预改变 $X$，$Y$ 会怎样”；因果效应比较干预或反事实结果，还需要研究设计和可识别性假设。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> If some measurable value sets $A,B$ satisfy $P(X\in A,Y\in B)\neq P(X\in A)P(Y\in B)$, then $X$ and $Y$ are probabilistically dependent in the observed distribution. This does not by itself answer what would happen to $Y$ under an intervention on $X$. A causal effect compares intervention or counterfactual outcomes and additionally requires a study design and identification assumptions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Bayes 后验也不例外
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Bayes posterior is no exception*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极高的 $P(H\mid B)$ 可以说明证据 $B$ 对假设 $H$ 很有诊断价值，却不能自动说明 $B$ 导致了 $H$，也不能说明干预 $B$ 会改变 $H$。共同原因、反向因果和选择机制都可能产生同样的概率关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A high $P(H\mid B)$ can show that evidence $B$ is diagnostically informative about hypothesis $H$, but it does not automatically show that $B$ caused $H$ or that intervening on $B$ would change $H$. A common cause, reverse causation, or selection can generate the same probabilistic relationship.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个后验概率很高，为什么仍不能直接声称证据具有因果作用？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does a high posterior probability still not justify claiming that the evidence has a causal effect?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 后验更新的是观察到的概率关系；因果问题比较干预后的反事实，需要额外设计和假设。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** A posterior updates an observed probabilistic relationship. A causal question compares counterfactual outcomes under interventions and therefore needs additional design and assumptions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
