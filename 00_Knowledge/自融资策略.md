# 自融资策略在初始投入后用组合内部资产与现金账户支付交易而不追加或提取外部资金
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A self-financing strategy funds trades from its own assets and cash account after the initial investment, without external additions or withdrawals*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自融资是一条交易资金约束：初始建仓之后，新增资产必须由出售其他资产或调整已计入组合的现金／借款账户来支付。组合仍可有非零初始成本、正负现金余额和利息收入或支出；“自融资”不等于免费交易、不借钱或不亏损。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Self-financing is a trading-budget constraint. After initial formation, asset purchases must be paid for by selling other assets or adjusting cash or borrowing already included in the portfolio. Initial cost can be nonzero, cash can be positive or negative, and interest can be earned or paid. Self-financing does not mean free trading, no borrowing, or no losses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先取无分红、无交易费用的离散市场。在同一交易时点，股票价格为 $S$，持股从 $h_{old}$ 改为 $h_{new}$，现金金额从 $b_{old}$ 改为 $b_{new}$。自融资要求交易前后价值不变：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First consider a discrete market without dividends or transaction costs. At one trading time with stock price $S$, changing shares from $h_{old}$ to $h_{new}$ and cash from $b_{old}$ to $b_{new}$ is self-financing when pre- and post-trade value agree:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
h_{new}S+b_{new}=h_{old}S+b_{old},\qquad
b_{new}=b_{old}-(h_{new}-h_{old})S.
$$

例如股价为 102，原来持股 $0.5$、现金 $-40$，价值为 11。增加到 $0.6$ 股需要支出 $10.2$，现金必须变为 $-50.2$，新价值仍为 11。若保留现金 $-40$ 却增加股票，就有 $10.2$ 的外部资金没有入账。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At price 102, holding 0.5 shares and cash −40 gives value 11. Increasing to 0.6 shares costs 10.2, so cash becomes −50.2 and value remains 11. Keeping cash at −40 while adding the shares leaves an unrecorded external contribution of 10.2.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个交易时点之间，原持仓的价格变化与现金利息会改变组合价值；“再平衡本身不凭空创造价值”不表示整个期间的价值不变。若有分红或交易成本，应把收到的现金流与支付费用显式计入现金账户。买卖价格不同时，成本会使交易后价值降低，不能继续套用上面的无摩擦等式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Between trading times, price changes on existing holdings and interest on cash change portfolio value. Rebalancing creates no value by itself, but holding-period value need not be constant. Dividends and trading costs must enter the cash ledger explicitly. With different bid and ask prices, costs reduce post-trade value and the frictionless equality no longer applies unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

策略还必须遵守信息时点：用于承担下一段价格变化的持仓只能依赖当时已知信息。事后用未来价格选择一开始的对冲数量，即使现金账平衡，也不是只使用当时信息的可实施策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The strategy must also respect information timing: holdings exposed to the next price interval can use only information already available. Choosing the starting hedge with hindsight is not an implementable strategy using only contemporaneously available information, even if its cash account balances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
