# Jevons、Menger 与 Walras 共享边际转向但建立了不同理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Jevons, Menger, and Walras shared a marginal turn but built distinct theories*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 历史区分
> Jevons、Menger 与 Walras 都在 1870 年代把稀缺条件下的边际评价放到价值分析中心，但三人没有提出同一套模型：Jevons 强调可微效用和交换的边际条件；Menger 用需要的重要性、可用数量与高阶财货之间的因果链解释价值；Walras 用联立方程研究多个市场同时相容的价格与数量。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Jevons, Menger, and Walras all placed marginal valuation under scarcity near the centre of value theory in the 1870s, but they did not offer one identical model. Jevons emphasised differentiable utility and marginal exchange conditions; Menger developed a causal account linking the importance of wants, available quantities, and higher-order goods; Walras studied mutually consistent prices and quantities through simultaneous equations across markets.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

共同点是分析从“整类商品包含多少劳动或总用途”转向：在当前数量与约束下，失去或获得下一部分会改变什么。差异则涉及方法、数学形式、因果叙事与研究对象。把三人压缩成一句“主观效用决定价格”，既抹掉这些差异，也会越过供给与市场协调问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Their shared move was away from asking only how much labour or total usefulness an entire class of goods contains and toward asking what changes when the next portion is gained or lost under current constraints. Their methods, mathematical forms, causal narratives, and objects of study differed. Reducing all three to “subjective utility determines price” erases those differences and skips supply and market coordination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“边际革命”还是后来的历史归类，不意味着三个作者在同一时点、彼此协调地创立一个完成态的新古典体系。Marshall 的局部均衡、Arrow–Debreu 的一般均衡和现代序数效用理论都是后续整理与扩展，不能完整反投射给三人在 1870 年代发表的原著。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The “marginal revolution” is also a later historical classification; it does not imply that the three authors coordinated one finished neoclassical system at the same moment. Marshallian partial equilibrium, Arrow–Debreu general equilibrium, and modern ordinal utility theory are later developments and should not be projected wholesale back onto the works the three published in the 1870s.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么“Walras 也讲边际效用”不足以说明他的理论和 Jevons 完全相同？
>
> **答案：** 共享概念不等于共享研究对象与方法；Walras 的核心推进还包括多市场联立与全体系相容，而 Jevons 主要用效用微积分组织交换条件。

## 来源与核验

- Jevons, *The Theory of Political Economy*, [Chapter III](https://www.econlib.org/library/YPDBooks/Jevons/jvnPE.html?chapter_num=7) 与 [Chapter IV](https://www.econlib.org/library/YPDBooks/Jevons/jvnPE.html?chapter_num=8)：分别核对 final degree of utility 与交换的数学入口。
- [Menger, *Principles of Economics*](https://cdn.mises.org/Principles%20of%20Economics_5.pdf)：核对需要、可用数量、具体财货价值与高阶财货的因果分析。
- [Walras, *Elements of Pure Economics*](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL32990431M/Elements_of_pure_economics)：核对稀少性、交换方程与多市场一般均衡对象。
- [William Jaffé, “Menger, Jevons and Walras De-Homogenized” (1976)](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1465-7295.1976.tb00439.x)：核对“边际革命”作为后来的史学归类，以及三位作者不能被同质化处理。
