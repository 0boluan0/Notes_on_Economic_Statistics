# 检测灵敏度不等于阳性后的患病概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Test sensitivity is not the probability of disease after a positive result*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 灵敏度是 $P(+\mid D)$；阳性后患病概率是 $P(D\mid +)$。后者还取决于患病率 $P(D)$ 和未患病者的假阳性率 $P(+\mid D^c)$：
> $$P(D\mid +)=\frac{P(+\mid D)P(D)}{P(+\mid D)P(D)+P(+\mid D^c)P(D^c)}.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> Sensitivity is $P(+\mid D)$; the probability of disease after a positive result is $P(D\mid +)$. The latter also depends on prevalence $P(D)$ and the false-positive rate among those without the disease, $P(+\mid D^c)$:
> $$P(D\mid +)=\frac{P(+\mid D)P(D)}{P(+\mid D)P(D)+P(+\mid D^c)P(D^c)}.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 10,000 人频数表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A natural-frequency table for 10,000 people*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

患病率为 $1\%$、灵敏度为 $90\%$、特异度为 $95\%$ 时：约 $100$ 人患病，其中 $90$ 人阳性；约 $9{,}900$ 人未患病，其中 $5\%\times9{,}900=495$ 人假阳性。因此
$$P(D\mid +)\approx\frac{90}{90+495}=15.4\%,$$
远低于 $90\%$ 的灵敏度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With prevalence $1\%$, sensitivity $90\%$, and specificity $95\%$: about $100$ people have the disease and $90$ of them test positive; about $9{,}900$ do not have the disease and $5\%\times9{,}900=495$ of them test positive falsely. Therefore
$$P(D\mid +)\approx\frac{90}{90+495}=15.4\%,$$
far below the $90\%$ sensitivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 上例中，阳性者分母为什么是 $90+495$ 而不是 $100$？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the example, why is the denominator for positive results $90+495$ rather than $100$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 条件是“检测阳性”，所以分母必须包括所有真阳性和假阳性；$100$ 是所有患病者，只适用于计算灵敏度。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The condition is “tested positive”, so the denominator must include all true and false positives. The $100$ people with disease are the denominator for sensitivity instead.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
