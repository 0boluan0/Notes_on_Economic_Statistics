# 分期融资将资金分阶段投入并使后续融资取决于阶段信息和约定条件

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Staged financing supplies capital in successive rounds, with continuation depending on interim information and agreed conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分期融资（staged financing）不是一次把全部所需资本交给项目，而是先提供当前阶段所需资金，再根据进度、信息和合同安排决定下一阶段融资。它划分的是出资进程，不是借款人的分期还款表。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Staged financing provides the capital needed for the current phase before later financing decisions are made using progress, information, and contractual arrangements. It stages funding, not the borrower's repayment schedule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如先提供 20 完成原型，再审查测试结果后决定是否另投 80。若后续资金尚未无条件承诺，出资人保留不继续投入的选择；已投入的 20 不会因此自动收回。若合同已经承诺未来拨款，就必须依该承诺及退出条件处理，不能把分期解释成随时单方停贷。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, an investor supplies 20 for a prototype and reviews test results before deciding whether to provide another 80. If the later capital has not been unconditionally committed, the investor retains an option not to continue; the initial 20 is not automatically recovered. An existing funding commitment remains subject to its terms and exit conditions, not an unrestricted right to stop lending.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

阶段安排要配合有效的[[委托筛选与监督|信息获取与监督]]。仅把付款日期切开而不获得有用信息、也不改变后续融资选择，不会自动产生上述控制作用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Staging requires useful [[委托筛选与监督|information gathering and monitoring]]. Merely splitting payment dates, without obtaining information or changing continuation choices, does not automatically create this control mechanism.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
