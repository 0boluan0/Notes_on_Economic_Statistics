# Gradients and level sets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*梯度与水平集*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **level set** collects input points at which a function has the same value. On a map of altitude, a contour joins places with the same height. Walking along that contour changes position without changing height. At a regular point, this lack of change has a precise first-order description: the gradient is perpendicular to every tangent direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**水平集**收集函数值相同的输入点。地形图上的等高线把高度相同的位置连起来；沿等高线行走，位置改变而高度不变。在正则点，这种“不变”有精确的一阶描述：梯度与每个切方向垂直。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, the level $3$ of $f(x,y)=x^2+2y^2$ is the ellipse $x^2+2y^2=3$. The point $a=(1,1)$ lies on it. A tangent direction describes an instantaneous velocity along the ellipse, not a straight path that must stay on the ellipse for a finite distance. We will find that direction below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$f(x,y)=x^2+2y^2$ 的水平 $3$ 是椭圆 $x^2+2y^2=3$，点 $a=(1,1)$ 位于其上。切方向描述沿椭圆运动的瞬时速度，并不是说沿该直线走一段后仍必须留在椭圆上。下面会具体求出这个方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From a constant value to a tangent space
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从恒定函数值到切空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work in real coordinates with the standard [[向量点积|dot product]]. Let $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be an [[开集|open set]], let $n\ge2$ be a finite integer, and let $f:U\to\mathbb R$. For $a\in U$, put $c=f(a)$ and define $L_c=\{x\in U:f(x)=c\}$. Assume $f$ is $C^1$ near $a$: all its first [[偏导数|partial derivatives]] exist and are continuous in a neighborhood of $a$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用实坐标和标准[[向量点积|点积]]。设 $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 是[[开集]]，$n\ge2$ 是有限整数，$f:U\to\mathbb R$。对 $a\in U$，记 $c=f(a)$，定义 $L_c=\{x\in U:f(x)=c\}$。假设 $f$ 在 $a$ 附近为 $C^1$，即所有一阶[[偏导数]]在某个邻域内存在且连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The gradient is the column of partial derivatives, $\nabla f(a)=(\partial_1 f(a),\ldots,\partial_n f(a))^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. The [[全微分|derivative map]] $Df(a)$ predicts the scalar first-order change caused by an input velocity $v$. In standard coordinates, the [[方向导数梯度公式|gradient formula]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

梯度是偏导数组成的列向量 $\nabla f(a)=(\partial_1 f(a),\ldots,\partial_n f(a))^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。[[全微分|导数映射]] $Df(a)$ 预测输入速度 $v$ 造成的标量一阶变化；在标准坐标中，[[方向导数梯度公式]]给出

$$Df(a)v=\nabla f(a)^Tv=\sum_{i=1}^n\partial_i f(a)v_i.$$

> [!important] A regular level set · 正则水平集
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $\nabla f(a)\ne0$, the [[隐函数定理|implicit function theorem]] makes $L_c$ near $a$ a $C^1$ graph with $n-1$ free coordinates. This is a regular hypersurface: a curve when $n=2$, a surface when $n=3$. Its tangent space consists exactly of the velocities of $C^1$ curves in $L_c$ passing through $a$, and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $\nabla f(a)\ne0$，[[隐函数定理]]保证 $L_c$ 在 $a$ 附近是一张具有 $n-1$ 个自由坐标的 $C^1$ 图形。这称为正则超曲面：$n=2$ 时是曲线，$n=3$ 时是曲面。其切空间恰好由经过 $a$、留在 $L_c$ 内的 $C^1$ 曲线的速度组成，并且
>
> $$T_aL_c=\ker Df(a)=\{v\in\mathbb R^n:\nabla f(a)^Tv=0\}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The symbol $\ker$ means the [[零空间|nullspace]] of the derivative row: all inputs sent to zero. Thus $T_aL_c$ is an $(n-1)$-dimensional vector space. Its normal space, the vectors perpendicular to every tangent vector, is $\{\lambda\nabla f(a):\lambda\in\mathbb R\}$. In particular the nonzero gradient is a normal vector; it need not have length one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\ker$ 表示导数行向量的[[零空间]]，即所有被送到零的输入。因此 $T_aL_c$ 是一个 $(n-1)$ 维向量空间。法向空间由与每个切向量都垂直的向量组成，等于 $\{\lambda\nabla f(a):\lambda\in\mathbb R\}$。特别地，非零梯度是一个法向量，但不必是单位长度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The tangent space contains velocity vectors, including zero. The tangent line or plane **through the point** is the translated set $a+T_aL_c=\{a+v:v\in T_aL_c\}$. Keeping points and velocities separate prevents us from accidentally writing the tangent plane through the origin.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

切空间装的是速度向量，也包含零向量。**经过该点**的切线或切平面则是平移后的集合 $a+T_aL_c=\{a+v:v\in T_aL_c\}$。区分点和速度，就不会误把切平面写成经过原点的平面。

## Why orthogonality is necessary, and why the converse holds here
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么切向量与梯度正交

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\gamma:(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)\to L_c$ be a curve with $\gamma(0)=a$, where $\varepsilon>0$. If $\gamma$ is differentiable at $0$ and $f$ is differentiable at $a$, the [[多元链式法则|multivariable chain rule]] applied to the constant function $f(\gamma(t))=c$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取曲线 $\gamma:(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)\to L_c$，满足 $\gamma(0)=a$，其中 $\varepsilon>0$。只要 $\gamma$ 在 $0$ 可微、$f$ 在 $a$ 可微，就可以对恒等式 $f(\gamma(t))=c$ 使用[[多元链式法则]]，得到

$$0=\left.\frac{d}{dt}f(\gamma(t))\right|_{t=0}
=Df(a)\gamma'(0)=\nabla f(a)^T\gamma'(0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So every actual tangent velocity satisfies the linear equation. This necessary condition uses differentiability at the relevant points; it does not require a nonzero gradient or continuous partial derivatives throughout a neighborhood. Those stronger assumptions above supply the **converse**, which says that every vector solving the equation really is a tangent velocity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，每个真实切向速度都满足这个线性方程。这一必要条件只用了相应点处的可微性，不要求梯度非零，也不要求整个邻域中的偏导连续。前面更强的假设提供的是**反向结论**：每个满足该方程的向量，确实都能成为切向速度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here is how. Since one partial derivative is nonzero, relabel coordinates so that $\partial_n f(a)\ne0$. Write $a=(u_0,z_0)$ with $u_0\in\mathbb R^{n-1}$. The implicit function theorem writes the nearby level set as $(u,\varphi(u))$, where $\varphi$ is $C^1$, $\varphi(u_0)=z_0$, and $f(u,\varphi(u))=c$. Differentiating this identity in any velocity $w\in\mathbb R^{n-1}$ yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体来看，某个偏导非零，所以可以重新排列坐标，使 $\partial_n f(a)\ne0$。写 $a=(u_0,z_0)$，其中 $u_0\in\mathbb R^{n-1}$。隐函数定理把附近水平集写成 $(u,\varphi(u))$：$\varphi$ 为 $C^1$，$\varphi(u_0)=z_0$，并且 $f(u,\varphi(u))=c$。沿任意速度 $w\in\mathbb R^{n-1}$ 对这个恒等式求导，得到

$$D\varphi(u_0)w
=-\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}\partial_i f(a)w_i}{\partial_n f(a)}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now take any $v=(w,v_n)$ with $Df(a)v=0$. The equation forces $v_n=D\varphi(u_0)w$. For sufficiently small $|t|$, the curve $\gamma(t)=(u_0+tw,\varphi(u_0+tw))$ stays in the level set and has velocity $\gamma'(0)=v$. This proves equality with the kernel, not just containment. It also exhibits the $n-1$ free coordinates of the tangent space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在取任意满足 $Df(a)v=0$ 的 $v=(w,v_n)$，方程迫使 $v_n=D\varphi(u_0)w$。当 $|t|$ 足够小时，曲线 $\gamma(t)=(u_0+tw,\varphi(u_0+tw))$ 留在水平集内，而且 $\gamma'(0)=v$。这就证明了切空间与核相等，而不只是包含于核；同时也看到了切空间的 $n-1$ 个自由坐标。

## An ellipse: directions and the line through the point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening example $f(x,y)=x^2+2y^2$, calculate $f(1,1)=3$ and $\nabla f(1,1)=(2,4)^T\ne0$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回到开头的 $f(x,y)=x^2+2y^2$，计算得 $f(1,1)=3$、$\nabla f(1,1)=(2,4)^T\ne0$。因此

$$T_{(1,1)}L_3=\{(v_1,v_2)^T:2v_1+4v_2=0\}
=\{s(2,-1)^T:s\in\mathbb R\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A tangent direction is $(2,-1)^T$ and a normal direction is $(2,4)^T$: their dot product is $4-4=0$. The tangent line through the point, expressed with position coordinates $(x,y)$, is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

切方向可取 $(2,-1)^T$，法方向可取 $(2,4)^T$，两者点积为 $4-4=0$。经过该点的切线用位置坐标 $(x,y)$ 表示为

$$2(x-1)+4(y-1)=0,\qquad x+2y=3.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The line touches the ellipse locally but is not the ellipse itself. For example, $(3,0)$ lies on this line, while $f(3,0)=9\ne3$. Tangency describes first-order behavior, not equality of the two sets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条直线在局部与椭圆相切，但不等于椭圆。例如 $(3,0)$ 在直线上，却满足 $f(3,0)=9\ne3$。相切描述一阶行为，并不表示两个集合相同。

## What the hypotheses give us
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 条件边界与后续

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $C^1$ and nonzero-gradient hypotheses are a sufficient route to the local graph and tangent-space formula. Merely knowing partial derivatives at one point does not supply that argument. If the gradient is zero, the equation accepts all velocities and needs separate geometric analysis; a level set may still be smooth. The examples in [[零梯度不定切空间|zero gradient and tangent-space ambiguity]] distinguish those possibilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$C^1$ 与非零梯度是假设中保证局部图形和切空间公式的一条充分途径。仅知道一点处有偏导，不能提供这套论证。若梯度为零，方程会接受所有速度，需要另行分析几何；水平集仍然可能是光滑的。[[零梯度不定切空间]]中的例子区分这些情况。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a regular equality constraint, this normal-space description also supplies the geometry behind the [[Lagrange必要条件|Lagrange necessary condition]]: at a constrained local extremum, the objective has no first-order change in feasible tangent directions. That optimization conclusion uses its own objective and constraint assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正则等式约束，这个法向空间描述也提供[[Lagrange必要条件]]的几何出发点：约束下局部极值处，目标函数沿可行切方向没有一阶变化。该优化结论还需要自身关于目标函数和约束的假设。

> [!question]- Check the distinction · 检查两种方程
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $(1,1)$ on $x^2+2y^2=3$, why is $2v_1+4v_2=0$ the equation for velocities, while $2(x-1)+4(y-1)=0$ describes the tangent line? The first is a vector space at zero; the second translates it to the point of contact. A tangent velocity is $(2,-1)^T$, and the normal is $(2,4)^T$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $x^2+2y^2=3$ 上的 $(1,1)$ 处，为什么速度满足 $2v_1+4v_2=0$，切线却写成 $2(x-1)+4(y-1)=0$？前者是以零向量为起点的向量空间，后者把它平移到接触点。切向速度可取 $(2,-1)^T$，法向量可取 $(2,4)^T$。
