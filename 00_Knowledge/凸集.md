# Convex sets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*凸集*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **convex set** has a simple geometric property: choose any two points in it and travel straight from one to the other; the entire journey stays in the set. In an optimization problem, the set might describe allowed choices. Convexity then says that blending two allowed choices remains allowed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**凸集**有一个直观的几何性质：任选其中两点，沿直线从一点走到另一点，整段路都留在集合里。在优化问题中，集合可以表示允许的选择；凸性意味着把两个允许的选择混合，仍然是允许的选择。

> [!example] A filled disk and its rim · 圆盘与圆周
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A filled disk includes its interior and boundary. A straight segment joining two of its points stays inside it, so it is convex. The circle consisting only of the rim is not convex: the midpoint of opposite points is the centre, which is absent from the rim. One such pair is enough to disprove convexity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 圆盘包含内部与边界，两点之间的直线段仍在圆盘中，所以它是凸集。仅由边缘组成的圆周不是凸集：相对两点的中点是圆心，但圆心不在圆周上。只要找到这样一对点，就能否定凸性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Writing every point on the segment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把线段上的每一点写出来*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be a set of points with $n$ real coordinates. For two points $x,y$, a point between them can be written $z=tx+(1-t)y$, where $0\le t\le1$. The two weights are nonnegative and add to one; this is called a **convex combination**. When $t=0$ we get $y$, when $t=1$ we get $x$, and $t=1/2$ gives their midpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 表示由 $n$ 个实坐标组成的点集。两点 $x,y$ 之间的点可以写成 $z=tx+(1-t)y$，其中 $0\le t\le1$。两个权重非负且和为一，称为**凸组合**。$t=0$ 时得到 $y$，$t=1$ 时得到 $x$，$t=1/2$ 时得到中点。

> [!important] Every pair, every weight · 任意两点与全部权重
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The set $U$ is convex exactly when
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$tx+(1-t)y\in U\qquad\text{for every }x,y\in U\text{ and every }t\in[0,1].$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Checking one pair or only its midpoint does not establish this whole statement. Convexity concerns the segment, not the line extending beyond its endpoints.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对任意 $x,y\in U$、任意 $t\in[0,1]$ 都成立时，$U$ 才是凸集。只检查一对点或只检查其中点，不能证明完整命题；定义要求线段留在集合中，不要求端点之外的整条直线都留在其中。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, the interval $[0,1]$ is convex. If $0\le x,y\le1$, multiplying by the nonnegative weights and adding gives $0\le tx+(1-t)y\le1$. The interval excludes many points on the extended line, such as $2$, which causes no problem. Open and half-open intervals are convex too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如区间 $[0,1]$ 是凸集：如果 $0\le x,y\le1$，乘以非负权重后相加，便得 $0\le tx+(1-t)y\le1$。它不包含延长线上的许多点，例如 $2$，但这不妨碍凸性。开区间与半开区间也都是凸集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The half-space $U=\{x:c^Tx\le b\}$ is another example. Here the fixed nonzero coefficient vector $c$ forms the [[向量点积|dot product]] $c^Tx$, and $b$ is a fixed bound. For $x,y\in U$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半空间 $U=\{x:c^Tx\le b\}$ 是另一个例子。其中固定非零系数向量 $c$ 与 $x$ 构成[[向量点积]] $c^Tx$，$b$ 是固定上界。对 $x,y\in U$，

$$c^T(tx+(1-t)y)=t\,c^Tx+(1-t)c^Ty\le tb+(1-t)b=b.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So a linear inequality remains satisfied after blending. The disk argument can also be made precise: if $\|x\|_2,\|y\|_2\le1$, the triangle inequality for the [[欧氏范数|Euclidean norm]] gives $\|tx+(1-t)y\|_2\le t\|x\|_2+(1-t)\|y\|_2\le1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，混合之后仍满足同一个线性不等式。圆盘的论证也可以精确写出：若 $\|x\|_2,\|y\|_2\le1$，[[欧氏范数]]的三角不等式给出 $\|tx+(1-t)y\|_2\le t\|x\|_2+(1-t)\|y\|_2\le1$。

## A property of allowed inputs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*凸性先描述允许哪些输入*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The empty set is convex because there is no pair of its points that could violate the rule. A one-point set is convex because every mixture returns that same point. Convexity does not require the set to have an interior or to contain all its boundary points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

空集也是凸集，因为其中不存在能够违反规则的一对点。单点集也是凸集，因为每个混合点都仍是同一点。凸性的定义既不要求集合有内部，也不要求包含全部边界点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a property of a set of inputs. [[凹凸性|Convexity of a function]] additionally compares output values: the function at a mixed input is compared with the corresponding mixture of output values. Requiring a convex domain ensures that the mixed input is available before making that comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里讨论的是输入集合的性质。[[凹凸性|函数的凸性]]还要比较输出：混合输入处的函数值，与输出值的同权重混合相比如何。先要求定义域是凸集，才能保证用来比较的混合输入确实有定义。
