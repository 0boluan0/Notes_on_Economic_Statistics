# Harrod—Domar 增长模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Harrod–Domar Growth Model*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释投资同时创造当期需求和未来产能时，经济要以多快的速度增长，才能让企业预期的产能利用率实现。
> **具体锚点：** 若储蓄率 $s=20\%$，增加 4 单位资本才能多生产 1 单位产出，即资本—产出比 $v=4$，则保证需求与新产能一致的增长率约为 $g_w=s/v=5\%$。
> **核心难点：** $s/v$ 不是无条件的长期增长预测；它来自固定资本系数、投资转化为产能和计划利用率等特定假设。
> **为什么重要：** 它说明“多投资”不只是供给问题；如果需求、产能和劳动力的增长率不匹配，失业或过度利用可以累积。
> **继续：** 若需要用可替代要素和资本边际收益递减解决不稳定，进入 [[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It asks how quickly an economy must grow for investment's simultaneous creation of current demand and future capacity to deliver the utilization rate firms expected.
> **Concrete anchor:** If the saving rate is $s=20\%$ and four units of capital are needed for one extra unit of output, so the capital–output ratio is $v=4$, the warranted growth rate is approximately $g_w=s/v=5\%$.
> **Central difficulty:** $s/v$ is not an unconditional long-run growth forecast. It follows from particular assumptions about fixed capital coefficients, conversion of investment into capacity, and planned utilization.
> **Why it matters:** “More investment” is not only a supply-side issue. When demand, capacity, and labor-force growth are mismatched, unemployment or overutilization can accumulate.
> **Continue:** To address instability through factor substitution and diminishing returns to capital, see [[Solow 增长模型、稳态与收敛|The Solow Growth Model, Steady State, and Convergence]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!source] 本节依据
> - Harrod（1939）《[An Essay in Dynamic Theory](https://doi.org/10.2307/2225181)》：核验实际、保证与自然增长率的区分及动态不稳定。
> - Domar（1946）《[Capital Expansion, Rate of Growth, and Employment](https://doi.org/10.2307/1905364)》：核验投资的收入创造与产能创造两面。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> - Harrod (1939), “[An Essay in Dynamic Theory](https://doi.org/10.2307/2225181),” verifies the distinction among actual, warranted, and natural growth and the dynamic instability.
> - Domar (1946), “[Capital Expansion, Rate of Growth, and Employment](https://doi.org/10.2307/1905364),” verifies the income-creating and capacity-creating sides of investment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从投资的两面到 $g=s/v$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From the two sides of investment to $g=s/v$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在固定资本—产出比 v 和储蓄率 s 下，需求/产能一致的 warranted growth 近似 $g=s/v$。实际、warranted 和人口决定的 natural growth 不一致时可能累积偏离，形成 knife-edge。模型强调投资的需求与产能双重作用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a fixed capital–output ratio $v$ and saving rate $s$, warranted growth consistent with demand and capacity is approximately $g=s/v$. When actual, warranted, and population-constrained natural growth differ, deviations can accumulate and produce a knife-edge dynamic. The model emphasizes investment's dual effects on demand and capacity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

推导是一个会计与技术结合：储蓄转成投资 $I=sY$，而固定资本系数意味着要支持产出增量 $\Delta Y$，需要 $\Delta K=v\Delta Y$。若 $I=\Delta K$，则

$$sY=v\Delta Y\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{\Delta Y}{Y}=\frac{s}{v}.$$

这个等式不证明实际经济会自动达到该增长率，只给出计划一致所要求的增长率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivation combines accounting and technology. Saving becomes investment, $I=sY$, while a fixed capital coefficient means that supporting an output increment $\Delta Y$ requires $\Delta K=v\Delta Y$. If $I=\Delta K$, then

$$sY=v\Delta Y\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{\Delta Y}{Y}=\frac{s}{v}.$$

This equality does not prove that the economy automatically reaches that rate. It states the growth rate required for plan consistency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三个增长率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three growth rates*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 实际增长率 $g_a$：统计上实际发生的产出增长。
- 保证增长率 $g_w$：让企业对产能利用率的预期被实现、不想修改投资计划的增长率。
- 自然增长率 $g_n$：劳动力与技术增长所允许的长期供给上限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Actual growth $g_a$: the statistically realized growth of output.
- Warranted growth $g_w$: the rate that validates firms' expected capacity utilization so that they do not wish to revise investment plans.
- Natural growth $g_n$: the long-run supply ceiling permitted by labor-force and technological growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $g_a>g_w$，企业可观察到产能比预期更紧，进一步增加投资，从而放大过热；若 $g_a<g_w$，闲置产能使投资缩减，又可加深不足。这就是“刀锋”的经济直觉：调整方向可以远离保证路径，而不是回归它。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $g_a>g_w$, firms may observe tighter-than-planned capacity and invest more, amplifying overheating. If $g_a<g_w$, idle capacity causes investment cuts that can deepen weakness. This is the economic intuition of the knife edge: adjustment may move away from the warranted path rather than back toward it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把 $s/v$ 当成不含任何假设的经验定律。
- 把保证增长率误解为政策必然能实现的目标率。
- 只调高储蓄，不检查投资质量、产能利用、进口设备和劳动力约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Treating $s/v$ as an empirical law without assumptions.
- Misreading warranted growth as a target rate that policy can necessarily deliver.
- Raising saving without checking investment quality, capacity utilization, imported equipment, or labor constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Harrod–Domar 的刀锋不稳定来自什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What creates knife-edge instability in Harrod–Domar?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 固定比例和不同增长率条件下，偏离均衡会改变投资/产能，使偏离可能自我放大而缺少平滑回归机制。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under fixed proportions, deviations change investment and capacity in ways that can amplify the deviation rather than smoothly return the economy to the warranted path.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释投资的“需求面”和“产能面”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words the demand and capacity sides of investment.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 投资支出当期直接增加总需求；新资本建成后又增加未来可生产的产能。两者需要一致的增长路径。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Investment spending immediately adds to aggregate demand, while completed capital raises future productive capacity. A consistent growth path must reconcile both.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 已知 $s=0.18$ 且 $v=3$，保证增长率的简化值是多少，为什么它不是预测？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If $s=0.18$ and $v=3$, what is the simplified warranted growth rate, and why is it not a forecast?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> $g_w=0.18/3=0.06$，即 6%。它是特定固定系数和储蓄—投资转化假设下的一致条件，不保证实际需求、利用率与劳动供给会配合。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $g_w=0.18/3=0.06$, or 6%. It is a consistency condition under fixed coefficients and saving-to-investment conversion, not a guarantee that demand, utilization, and labor supply will align.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
