# 虚拟变量与交互项
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Indicator variables and interaction terms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 回归只能直接处理数字，但研究问题经常涉及性别、地区、制度状态等类别，以及“一个变量的作用是否因组别或另一个变量而改变”。虚拟变量把类别变成可比较的组别差异，交互项把“作用会变”写进模型。
> **具体锚点：** 在工资回归中，`female` 的系数可以表示某个基准经验水平下的性别差异；`female × experience` 则允许经验回报率因性别而不同。
> **核心难点：** 有交互项时，单个系数通常不再是完整效应。必须先写出各组方程，再计算给定取值处的差异或斜率。
> **为什么重要：** 错选基准组只会改变表达方式；漏掉主效应、忽略稀疏组别或把交互系数直接当因果效应，却会改变模型含义甚至制造错误结论。
> **继续：** 先看 [[#从类别到设计矩阵]]，再看 [[#交互项如何改变斜率与组差]]；若因变量本身只有 0/1，转到 [[二元因变量模型：LPM、Logit 与 Probit]]；政策前后两组的交互见 [[双重差分法（DID）]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Regression works directly with numbers, but research questions often involve categories—such as gender, region, or institutional status—and ask whether the effect of one variable changes across groups or with another variable. Indicators encode group contrasts, while interactions put “the effect varies” into the model.
> **Concrete anchor:** In a wage regression, the coefficient on `female` can represent the gender gap at a reference level of experience. `female × experience` allows the return to experience to differ by gender.
> **Central difficulty:** Once an interaction is present, a single coefficient is usually not the complete effect. Write the equation for each group first, then calculate the difference or slope at the value of interest.
> **Why it matters:** Changing the reference group only changes the representation. Omitting lower-order terms, ignoring sparse cells, or treating an interaction coefficient as a causal effect can change the model's meaning and produce false conclusions.
> **Continue with:** Start with [[#从类别到设计矩阵|categories and the design matrix]], then read [[#交互项如何改变斜率与组差|how interactions change slopes and group differences]]. If the outcome itself is binary, continue to [[二元因变量模型：LPM、Logit 与 Probit|binary-outcome models]]. For a group-by-period interaction in policy evaluation, see [[双重差分法（DID）|difference-in-differences]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从类别到设计矩阵
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From categories to a design matrix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $D_i=1$ 表示个体属于某一组，$D_i=0$ 表示属于基准组。在没有交互项的线性模型中，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $D_i=1$ indicate membership in one group and $D_i=0$ denote the reference group. In a linear model without interactions,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1D_i+\gamma'Z_i+u_i,
$$

$D=0$ 组在相同 $Z$ 下的条件均值是 $\beta_0+\gamma'Z$，$D=1$ 组是 $\beta_0+\beta_1+\gamma'Z$。因此，$\beta_1$ 是控制 $Z$ 后两组的条件均值差，而不是 $D=1$ 组的绝对水平。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conditional mean for the $D=0$ group at the same $Z$ is $\beta_0+\gamma'Z$, while that for the $D=1$ group is $\beta_0+\beta_1+\gamma'Z$. Thus, $\beta_1$ is the conditional mean difference between the two groups after controlling for $Z$, not the absolute level of the $D=1$ group.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个差异只有在额外的识别条件成立时才有因果含义。把“是否参加培训”编码成 0/1，并不会自动让培训成为随机分配；若能力同时影响培训选择和工资，$D$ 的系数仍可能混入能力差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This contrast has a causal interpretation only under additional identification conditions. Coding “received training” as 0/1 does not make training randomly assigned. If ability affects both training selection and wages, the coefficient on $D$ may still absorb ability differences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 多类别变量与基准组
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Multicategory variables and reference groups*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若学历有“高中、本科、研究生”三类，模型含截距时可以把“高中”作为基准，只放入 `本科` 和 `研究生` 两个 indicator。两个系数分别回答“本科相对高中”和“研究生相对高中”的条件均值差。若改以“本科”为基准，拟合值、残差和所有可识别的组间对比都不变，只是系数换了一套坐标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If education has three categories—high school, bachelor's, and postgraduate—and the model contains an intercept, high school can be the reference category while indicators for bachelor's and postgraduate are included. Their coefficients answer “bachelor's versus high school” and “postgraduate versus high school.” Changing the reference category to bachelor's leaves fitted values, residuals, and every identified group contrast unchanged; it merely uses a different coordinate system for the coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

含截距时把 $m$ 个类别的全部 $m$ 个 indicator 都放入模型，会使它们之和恒等于截距列，产生完全多重共线性，即 dummy-variable trap。解决办法是省去一个基准组，或者去掉公共截距；前者通常更易解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Including all $m$ indicators for an $m$-category variable together with an intercept makes their sum identical to the intercept column, creating perfect multicollinearity—the dummy-variable trap. The usual solution is to omit one reference category; removing the common intercept is another parameterization, but is often less convenient for comparisons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 交互项如何改变斜率与组差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How interactions change slopes and group differences*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑一个连续变量 $X$ 与组别 indicator $D$ 的交互：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider an interaction between a continuous variable $X$ and a group indicator $D$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2D+\beta_3(XD)+u.
$$

把 $D=0$ 和 $D=1$ 分别代入，比盯着四个系数更可靠：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substituting $D=0$ and $D=1$ separately is more reliable than staring at four coefficients:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 对象 | 条件均值 | $X$ 的斜率 |
|---|---|---|
| 基准组 $D=0$ | $\beta_0+\beta_1X$ | $\beta_1$ |
| 比较组 $D=1$ | $(\beta_0+\beta_2)+(\beta_1+\beta_3)X$ | $\beta_1+\beta_3$ |
| 在给定 $X=x$ 处的组差 | $\beta_2+\beta_3x$ | — |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Object | Conditional mean | Slope with respect to $X$ |
|---|---|---|
| Reference group, $D=0$ | $\beta_0+\beta_1X$ | $\beta_1$ |
| Comparison group, $D=1$ | $(\beta_0+\beta_2)+(\beta_1+\beta_3)X$ | $\beta_1+\beta_3$ |
| Group difference at $X=x$ | $\beta_2+\beta_3x$ | — |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以 $\beta_2$ 只是在 $X=0$ 时的组差，$\beta_3$ 只是两组斜率之差；给定 $x$ 的完整组差是 $\beta_2+\beta_3x$。若 $X=0$ 没有实际意义，可以令 $X_c=X-c$，把 $c$ 设为样本均值或有意义的政策点。中心化不改变拟合值，却使 indicator 的主效应变成“在 $X=c$ 时的组差”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore, $\beta_2$ is only the group difference at $X=0$, and $\beta_3$ is only the difference between the two slopes. The complete group difference at $x$ is $\beta_2+\beta_3x$. If $X=0$ is not meaningful, define $X_c=X-c$, where $c$ is the sample mean or a meaningful policy value. Centering leaves fitted values unchanged but makes the indicator's main effect the group difference at $X=c$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

通常应保留构成交互项的低阶项：若放入 $XD$ 却删掉 $X$，就强制基准组的 $X$ 斜率为 0；若删掉 $D$，就强制两组在 $X=0$ 时截距相同。这些限制并非永远错误，但必须来自明确理论，而不能只是因为某个主效应不显著。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lower-order terms that compose an interaction should usually remain in the model. Including $XD$ while omitting $X$ forces the $X$ slope in the reference group to zero; omitting $D$ forces equal intercepts at $X=0$. Such restrictions are not inherently wrong, but they require a substantive justification and should not be imposed merely because a main effect is statistically insignificant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!source] 关系核验
> MIT 14.310x Lecture 19 直接把包含 $D$、$M$ 与 $D\times M$ 的模型拆成组别方程；Wooldridge Chapter 7.4 给出含虚拟变量的交互解释。两者都支持“主效应依赖基准值、交互项是差异之差”的读法。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> MIT 14.310x Lecture 19 explicitly rewrites a model containing $D$, $M$, and $D\times M$ as group-specific equations. Wooldridge Chapter 7.4 gives the corresponding interpretation for interactions involving indicators. Both support reading main effects at their reference values and the interaction as a difference in differences.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两个类别变量：四个格子而不是一个系数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Two categorical variables: four cells, not one coefficient*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $D$ 表示处理组、$T$ 表示处理后时期，模型 $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1D+\beta_2T+\beta_3DT+u$ 对应四个条件均值。$\beta_3$ 是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $D$ denotes the treated group and $T$ the post-treatment period, the model $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1D+\beta_2T+\beta_3DT+u$ represents four conditional means. Here $\beta_3$ is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\big[E(Y\mid D=1,T=1)-E(Y\mid D=1,T=0)\big]
-\big[E(Y\mid D=0,T=1)-E(Y\mid D=0,T=0)\big].
$$

代数上它是 difference-in-differences；但把它解释成政策因果效应，还需要平行趋势、无提前反应、构成稳定等识别条件。交互项提供参数化，不能替代研究设计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Algebraically, this is a difference in differences. Interpreting it as a causal policy effect additionally requires identifying conditions such as parallel trends, no anticipation, and stable composition. The interaction supplies a parameterization; it does not replace the research design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个连续变量的交互同样遵循“效应依赖取值”：若 $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2Z+\beta_3XZ+u$，则 $X$ 的边际效应是 $\partial E(Y\mid X,Z)/\partial X=\beta_1+\beta_3Z$。因此报告 $\beta_3$ 之外，还应在有意义的 $Z$ 取值处报告斜率或画出带置信区间的预测线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An interaction between two continuous variables follows the same “effect depends on values” logic. If $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2Z+\beta_3XZ+u$, the marginal effect of $X$ is $\partial E(Y\mid X,Z)/\partial X=\beta_1+\beta_3Z$. Report slopes at meaningful values of $Z$, or plot fitted lines with confidence intervals, rather than reporting $\beta_3$ alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 完整例子：经验回报是否因组别而变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: does the return to experience differ across groups?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设小时工资（英镑）的估计方程为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the estimated equation for hourly wages in pounds is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\widehat{wage}=12+0.80\,experience-1.50\,female-0.25(experience\times female).
$$

以 `female=0` 为基准组时，其起点和经验斜率分别为 12 与 0.80；`female=1` 组的起点为 $12-1.50=10.50$，经验斜率为 $0.80-0.25=0.55$。在 10 年经验处，预测组差不是 $-1.50$，而是 $-1.50-0.25\times10=-4.00$ 英镑。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With `female=0` as the reference group, its intercept and experience slope are 12 and 0.80. For the `female=1` group, the intercept is $12-1.50=10.50$ and the experience slope is $0.80-0.25=0.55$. At ten years of experience, the fitted group difference is not $-1.50$ but $-1.50-0.25\times10=-4.00$ pounds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不同问题对应不同检验：两组经验斜率相同检验 $H_0:\beta_3=0$；在 10 年经验处无组差检验 $H_0:\beta_2+10\beta_3=0$；整条回归线相同则联合检验 $H_0:\beta_2=\beta_3=0$。第二个问题需要线性组合的标准误，不能把两个单独的 p-value 拼起来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Different questions require different tests. Equal experience slopes test $H_0:\beta_3=0$; no group difference at ten years tests $H_0:\beta_2+10\beta_3=0$; identical regression lines require the joint test $H_0:\beta_2=\beta_3=0$. The second question needs the standard error of a linear combination; it cannot be answered by combining two separate p-values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子只说明如何读取模型，并不证明性别导致工资差异。教育、职业选择、工时、选择进入就业样本等都可能影响因果解释；估计对象和识别策略必须另行说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This example demonstrates how to read the model; it does not prove that gender causes the wage difference. Education, occupational selection, hours, and selection into employment may all affect a causal interpretation. The estimand and identification strategy must be stated separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 诊断、失败边界与报告
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnostics, failure boundaries, and reporting*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

建模前先画出或列出各类别与交互组合的样本量。某个格子几乎没有观测时，交互项虽然能被软件估计，却主要依靠外推；标准误会很大，结果也可能被少数点支配。连续变量的比较范围应限制在各组共同支持的区间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before fitting the model, tabulate or plot sample sizes for every category and interaction cell. If a cell has almost no observations, software may still estimate the interaction, but the estimate relies heavily on extrapolation, has a large standard error, and may be driven by a few points. Comparisons involving a continuous variable should be restricted to the range with common support across groups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在非线性模型（如 logit/probit）中，线性指数里的交互系数不等于概率尺度上的交互效应。应直接计算四格预测概率的差异之差，或在目标取值处计算边际效应；具体解释见 [[二元因变量模型：LPM、Logit 与 Probit#怎样解释 Logit 与 Probit]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In nonlinear models such as logit or probit, the interaction coefficient in the linear index is not the interaction effect on the probability scale. Compute the difference in differences of predicted probabilities or marginal effects at target values instead; see [[二元因变量模型：LPM、Logit 与 Probit#怎样解释 Logit 与 Probit|interpreting logit and probit]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准误应匹配数据生成方式：独立横截面可使用异方差稳健标准误；同一单位重复观测时通常按单位聚类；若处理在学校、地区等更高层分配，推断层级也应与分配和相关结构相匹配。稳健标准误修复的是推断，不会修复错误函数形式、遗漏变量或稀疏支持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Standard errors should match the data-generating structure. Heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors may suit independent cross-sections; repeated observations of a unit usually call for clustering by unit; if treatment is assigned at a higher level such as school or region, the inferential level should reflect that assignment and dependence. Robust standard errors repair inference, not a misspecified functional form, omitted variables, or sparse support.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一份可复现的报告至少应说明：indicator 何时取 1、基准组是谁、连续变量是否中心化、完整模型式、目标对比的估计值与置信区间，以及该对比是描述性还是因果性的。最清楚的展示通常是组别方程或预测图，而不是孤立的一列回归系数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A reproducible report should state when each indicator equals one, identify the reference group, note whether continuous variables were centered, show the complete model, report the target contrast with a confidence interval, and distinguish descriptive from causal interpretation. Group-specific equations or prediction plots are usually clearer than an isolated column of regression coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么改变基准组不会改变模型的拟合值？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why does changing the reference group leave fitted values unchanged?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 基准组只决定系数从哪一组开始表达。改变基准组相当于重新编码同一组条件均值；每个观测对应的预测仍是同一个数，所以残差与可识别的组间对比不变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The reference group only determines the origin from which coefficients express group differences. Changing it reparameterizes the same set of group means. Each observation receives the same fitted value, so residuals and identified group contrasts remain unchanged.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 在 $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2D+\beta_3XD+u$ 中，$D=1$ 组的 $X$ 斜率和 $X=5$ 时的组差分别是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In $Y=\beta_0+\beta_1X+\beta_2D+\beta_3XD+u$, what are the $X$ slope for the $D=1$ group and the group difference at $X=5$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 斜率是 $\beta_1+\beta_3$；组差是 $\beta_2+5\beta_3$。$\beta_3$ 本身只是斜率差。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The slope is $\beta_1+\beta_3$, and the group difference is $\beta_2+5\beta_3$. The coefficient $\beta_3$ alone is only the difference in slopes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 某模型含 `treated × post`，交互系数显著。为什么还不能直接声称政策有效？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A model contains `treated × post`, and the interaction coefficient is significant. Why is that insufficient to claim that the policy worked?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 交互系数只给出四格均值的差异之差。因果解释还需要平行趋势、无提前反应、无同时发生且差异化的冲击等识别条件，并需要与处理分配相匹配的推断。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The interaction coefficient only gives a difference in differences across four cell means. A causal interpretation additionally requires conditions such as parallel trends, no anticipation, and no simultaneous differential shocks, together with inference appropriate to treatment assignment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么“删掉不显著的主效应，只保留交互项”可能改变研究问题？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can deleting an insignificant main effect while retaining the interaction change the research question?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 删除主效应不是简化记号，而是施加约束：删 $X$ 会强制基准组斜率为 0，删 $D$ 会强制两组在 $X=0$ 时相同。只有理论明确要求该约束时才合理。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Removing a main effect imposes a restriction rather than merely simplifying notation. Omitting $X$ forces the reference group's slope to zero; omitting $D$ forces the groups to coincide at $X=0$. Such a restriction is appropriate only when explicitly justified by theory.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
