# 序列 $(1+1/n)^n$ 收敛到自然常数 $e$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The sequence $(1+1/n)^n$ converges to Euler's number $e$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> $$
> \lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\frac1n\right)^n=e.
> $$
> 更一般地，在 $1+h>0$ 的方向上，
> $$
> \lim_{h\to0}(1+h)^{1/h}=e.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The sequence $(1+1/n)^n$ converges to $e$. More generally, $(1+h)^{1/h}\to e$ as $h\to0$ through values satisfying $1+h>0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先取自然对数。令 $h=1/n$，则
$$
\ln\left(1+\frac1n\right)^n
=\frac{\ln(1+h)-\ln1}{h}
\longrightarrow (\ln x)'\big|_{x=1}=1.
$$
再用指数函数的连续性，原序列趋于 $e^1=e$。证明把移动指数转成了 [[对数函数求导|对数在 $1$ 处的差商]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take logarithms and set $h=1/n$. The logarithm becomes $[\ln(1+h)-\ln1]/h$, the difference quotient of $\ln$ at one, which tends to one. Continuity of the exponential then returns the original limit as $e^1=e$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个极限是 $e$ 的等价刻画，不应在同一论证中既用它定义 $e$，又用已经依赖该定义的 $d(\ln x)/dx=1/x$ 来证明它。当前知识链先用 [[自然指数函数|斜率标准化]]定义 $e$，再推导对数导数，最后得到本极限，因此没有循环。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This limit is an equivalent characterisation of $e$. One must not define $e$ by the limit and then prove the same limit with a logarithmic derivative already derived from that definition. The present chain first defines $e$ through exponential slope normalisation, derives the logarithmic derivative, and only then proves this limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
