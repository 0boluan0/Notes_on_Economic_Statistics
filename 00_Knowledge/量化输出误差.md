# 权重量化造成的线性层输出误差取决于量化误差与输入的乘积

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The output error from weight quantization in a linear layer depends on the product of weight error and input*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对固定输入、只量化权重的线性层，输出变化等于权重误差乘以输入。因此，位宽或权重误差的平均大小不能单独保证模型行为；同一组权重误差遇到不同输入方向和幅值，会产生不同的输出误差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a linear layer with fixed input and only its weights quantized, the output change equals the weight error multiplied by the input. Bit width or average weight error alone therefore cannot guarantee model behavior: input direction and magnitude determine how weight errors affect the output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 固定输入下的恒等式

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The identity at a fixed input*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本条局部采用**列向量输入** $x\in\mathbb R^{d_{\mathrm{in}}}$，权重为 $W\in\mathbb R^{d_{\mathrm{out}}\times d_{\mathrm{in}}}$，输出 $y=Wx$；若有 bias，假设它保持不变。令反量化后的近似权重为 $\widehat W=W+\Delta W$，于是

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This note locally uses a **column input vector** $x\in\mathbb R^{d_{\mathrm{in}}}$, weights $W\in\mathbb R^{d_{\mathrm{out}}\times d_{\mathrm{in}}}$, and output $y=Wx$. Any bias is assumed unchanged. Let the dequantized approximate weights be $\widehat W=W+\Delta W$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta y=\widehat Wx-Wx
=(W+\Delta W)x-Wx
=\Delta W x.
$$

前文 Transformer 常以 token 为行写 $Y=XW$。两种记号可以通过转置转换：本条变为 $y^\top=x^\top W^\top$，误差是 $\Delta y^\top=x^\top\Delta W^\top$；必须同时转换输入和权重方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Transformer notation elsewhere often places tokens in rows and writes $Y=XW$. Transposing both the input and weights converts the convention: here $y^\top=x^\top W^\top$ and $\Delta y^\top=x^\top\Delta W^\top$. Both orientations must change together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同样的权重误差大小，不同的输出误差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equal weight-error magnitude, unequal output error*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取单行权重 $w=(1,1)$，比较两组构造的量化误差：$\Delta w_A=(0.1,0)$、$\Delta w_B=(0,0.1)$。二者的平方误差和都是 $0.01$，对两个元素的 MSE 都是 $0.005$。但取输入 $x=(100,1)^\top$ 时，

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take row weights $w=(1,1)$ and two constructed quantization errors, $\Delta w_A=(0.1,0)$ and $\Delta w_B=(0,0.1)$. Both have squared error sum $0.01$, or MSE $0.005$ over two elements. Yet for input $x=(100,1)^\top$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta y_A=(0.1,0)\begin{pmatrix}100\\1\end{pmatrix}=10,
\qquad
\Delta y_B=(0,0.1)\begin{pmatrix}100\\1\end{pmatrix}=0.1.
$$

若换成 $x=(1,100)^\top$，两个输出误差就交换。这个反例说明权重误差的方向和输入分布不可忽略；它仅构造两种误差，不声称任何指定量化算法必然产生它们。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing the input to $x=(1,100)^\top$ swaps the output errors. The counterexample shows why the error direction and input distribution matter. It stipulates two errors without claiming that a particular quantization algorithm must produce them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从局部误差到任务质量还缺什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What remains between local error and task quality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个恒等式只覆盖固定输入、单层、仅权重量化。深层模型还有误差传播、非线性，以及可能同时发生的[[量化对象|激活或 KV 量化]]。因此需要在目标输入和任务上检查准确率、生成行为及所需的长上下文能力；局部权重 MSE 不能代替这些结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity covers a fixed input, one layer, and weight-only quantization. Deep models also involve error propagation, nonlinearities, and possibly [[量化对象|activation or KV quantization]]. Target inputs and tasks must therefore be used to evaluate accuracy, generation behavior, and any required long-context capabilities. Local weight MSE cannot replace those results.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若使用困惑度，需要遵守[[困惑度可比性|匹配评测口径]]，且不能将一个语料上的 PPL 近似不变解释为所有任务都无损。质量与[[量化加速条件|速度收益]]是两个需要同时验收的结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Perplexity requires [[困惑度可比性|a matched evaluation protocol]]. Nearly unchanged PPL on one corpus does not establish lossless behavior on every task. Quality and [[量化加速条件|speed gains]] are separate outcomes that both require validation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
