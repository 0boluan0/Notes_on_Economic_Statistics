# 按量减排补贴是对相对核定基准减少的污染排放给予支付的奖励
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A per-unit abatement subsidy rewards pollution reductions measured against an established baseline.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按量减排补贴按实际核实的减排数量支付：相对核定基准少排一单位，领取 $s$ 单位货币。它奖励的是减排结果，不是仅因购买指定设备或报销一笔支出就发钱。设备补助也可能促进减排，但其支付依据和激励不与本工具自动相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A per-unit abatement subsidy pays $s$ for each verified emission unit reduced against an established baseline. Payment rewards the measured outcome, not merely equipment purchases or expenditure reimbursement. Equipment support may also reduce pollution, but its payment basis and incentives are not automatically the same.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定参与者、产出等其他选择和基准排放 $e_0$，令 $a=e_0-e\in[0,e_0]$、净私人减排成本为可微凸函数 $C(a)$。企业在可兑现的单位补贴下选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hold participants, output, other choices and baseline emissions $e_0$ fixed. For abatement $a=e_0-e\in[0,e_0]$ and differentiable convex net private cost $C(a)$, a credible unit subsidy leads the firm to solve
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\min_{0\le a\le e_0}\{C(a)-sa\},\qquad C'(a)=s\quad\text{（内点 / interior）}.$$

下一单位减排成本低于补贴时值得多减排，高于补贴时停止增加。若 $C'(0)\ge s$，零减排可最优；若 $C'(e_0)\le s$，完全减排可最优。例如固定产出的技术例 $e_0=100,C(a)=a^2/2,s=50$ 给出减排 50、剩余排放 50；企业花费 1250、获得补贴 2500。这是既有主体的选择，不是全行业进入退出后的排放预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Abate further when the next unit costs less than the subsidy. Zero abatement can be optimal if $C'(0)\ge s$; full abatement if $C'(e_0)\le s$. In a fixed-output technology example with $e_0=100$, $C(a)=a^2/2$ and $s=50$, the firm abates fifty, spends 1,250 and receives 2,500. This is an existing participant's choice, not an industry-wide prediction after entry and exit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“比核定基准少排”先决定可以申领多少；“比没有补贴时少排”才回答政策有没有带来额外改善。两者不保证相同。评价增量时要建立[[公共项目反事实|无政策的动态比较基准]]，实施时则要防止抬高支付基准或把本来就会发生的减排领作补贴，见[[排放配额与减排信用|基准减排与额外性的区别]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reductions against the payment baseline determine eligibility; reductions against what would happen without the subsidy determine additional impact. These need not coincide. Appraisal needs a [[公共项目反事实|dynamic no-policy counterfactual]], while implementation must address inflated baselines and payments for reductions that would occur anyway; see [[排放配额与减排信用|baseline reductions and additionality]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位补贴若要达到社会最优，还需要匹配最优点上少排一单位的社会收益。它与同额[[排污费]]的个体边际激励虽可相同，财政收支、利润与市场参与后果却不同，见[[税费与补贴的减排激励]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An efficient rate must also match the social benefit of the next unit abated at the optimum. An equal [[排污费|emissions charge]] can provide the same individual marginal incentive while producing different fiscal, profit and participation effects; see [[税费与补贴的减排激励|the tax–subsidy incentive boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
