# 持有期收益率是指定持有期间的总投资增益与期初投入之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Holding-period return is the total investment gain over a specified holding period divided by the initial investment*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

持有期收益率（HPR）既包括价格变化，也包括持有资产带来的现金分配。先固定币种和起止时点：期初以 $P_0>0$ 买入并保持同一数量的资产，期末资产价值为 $P_T$；股利或息票单独留在现金账户，其期末累计价值为 $D_T$；期间没有额外注入或取出资金，暂忽略交易和融资费用，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
HPR includes price changes and distributions. Fix the currency and dates. An unchanged asset quantity costs $P_0>0$ initially and is worth $P_T$ at the end. Distributions are kept in a separate cash account worth $D_T$ at that date. With no additional external flows and before trading or financing costs,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$R_{0,T}=\frac{P_T+D_T-P_0}{P_0}.$$

100 元买入，期末卖得 98 元，期间收到并保留 5 元分配且现金不计息，则总回报为 3%，不是价格跌幅 −2%。对债券，购入和卖出都用实际全价，再单列期间收到的息票；不能只看净价差，也不能把已包含在全价中的同一笔应计利息重复加一次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Buying for 100, selling for 98, and retaining a distribution of 5 without cash interest gives a 3% return, not the −2% price change. For bonds, use actual dirty purchase and sale prices and separately include coupons received; do not add accrued interest twice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若分配被用来增购资产，改从整个投资账户的期末财富核算，不再把已计入持仓价值的再投资额重复计入 $D_T$。若有外部入金或提款，或要比较不同币种的结果，也要分别处理流量时点与[[跨币种套保回报|货币换算]]。零初始价值的衍生合约不能直接以零作分母，应明确保证金或投入资本等另一个回报基数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If distributions buy more assets, calculate terminal account wealth and avoid counting them again as cash. External deposits or withdrawals require cash-flow timing, and currency comparisons require [[跨币种套保回报|currency conversion]]. A zero-value derivative cannot use zero as the return denominator; specify a separate capital or margin base.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
