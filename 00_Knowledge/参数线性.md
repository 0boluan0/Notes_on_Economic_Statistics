# Linearity in parameters
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*参数线性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A regression can draw a curve and still be a **linear regression**. “Linear” describes how the unknown coefficients enter the fitted expression, not whether the raw predictor traces a straight line. For example, $\beta_0+\beta_1x^2$ multiplies the known feature $x^2$ by one unknown coefficient and adds an intercept. Once $x$ is observed, its square is just another known number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归画出一条曲线，也仍然可以是**线性回归**。“线性”描述未知系数怎样进入拟合式，不是在问原始解释变量上的图像是不是直线。例如，$\beta_0+\beta_1x^2$ 用未知系数乘已知特征 $x^2$，再加截距；$x$ 观察到后，平方只是另一个已知数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $z$ denote the raw predictors and let $g_1,\ldots,g_k$ be specified feature functions whose values do not depend on the unknown coefficient vector $\beta$. With an intercept $\beta_0$, the expression is linear in parameters when it has the form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $z$ 表示原始解释变量，$g_1,\ldots,g_k$ 是已指定的特征函数，其取值不依赖未知系数向量 $\beta$。包含截距 $\beta_0$ 时，下式就是对参数线性的表达：

$$Y=\beta_0+\beta_1g_1(z)+\cdots+\beta_kg_k(z)+u.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $Y$ is the outcome, $u$ the model error, and $\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_k$ the unknown weights. Each coefficient multiplies a known feature and the terms are added. The intercept is also a coefficient: its feature is the constant $1$. This specifies a form for the model; it does not yet assert that $E[u\mid z]=0$ or that any coefficient has a causal interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $Y$ 是结果，$u$ 是模型误差，$\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_k$ 是未知权重。每个系数乘一个已知特征，然后将各项相加。截距也是系数，只是对应特征恒为 $1$。这规定了模型的形式，尚未断言 $E[u\mid z]=0$，也没有赋予系数因果解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A square is a known feature
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小对照

> [!example] Fit a curve by weighting two columns · 给两列加权就能拟合曲线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take four observed predictor values $x=(-2,-1,1,2)$. For the model $\beta_0+\beta_1x^2$, form the design matrix with columns $1$ and $x^2$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取四个解释变量观测 $x=(-2,-1,1,2)$。模型为 $\beta_0+\beta_1x^2$ 时，用常数列和平方列构造设计矩阵：
>
> $$X=\begin{pmatrix}1&4\\1&1\\1&1\\1&4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> \beta=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> X\beta=\begin{pmatrix}9\\3\\3\\9\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The curve is $1+2x^2$, yet computing its values only requires a weighted sum of two fixed columns. If the observed outcomes are $(9,3,3,9)$, [[普通最小二乘|OLS]] recovers coefficients $(1,2)$ with zero [[回归残差|residuals]]: all four fitted values equal their observations. The two columns are [[线性无关|linearly independent]]—neither is a multiple of the other—so the coefficients are unique. Repeated values of $x^2$ do not by themselves destroy that independence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 曲线为 $1+2x^2$，计算它的值只需给两列固定数值加权相加。若结果正好是 $(9,3,3,9)$，[[普通最小二乘|OLS]]得到系数 $(1,2)$，[[回归残差]]为零：四个拟合值都等于观察值。两列[[线性无关]]，即其中一列不是另一列的倍数，所以系数唯一；平方特征有重复取值，并不会单独破坏列独立性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Contrast $\beta_0+\sqrt{\beta_1}x$. As a function of the originally named parameter $\beta_1\ge0$, the coefficient is transformed by a square root, so this is not linear in that parameter. Likewise, $\beta_0+e^{\beta_1}x$ and an expression involving a product of two unknown coefficients are not linear in those original coefficients. The relevant question is which symbols are unknown parameters, not how straight the graph looks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较 $\beta_0+\sqrt{\beta_1}x$：以原先命名的 $\beta_1\ge0$ 为参数时，它被开了平方，所以模型对这个参数不线性。同样，$\beta_0+e^{\beta_1}x$ 或含两个未知系数乘积的表达，对原系数也不线性。关键是哪些符号是待估参数，而不是图像看起来多直。

## Choose the features before interpreting the coefficients
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用条件与含义边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Powers such as $x^2$, $\log x$ on the domain $x>0$, and products of observed features can all be design columns. For example, a [[类别连续交互|categorical–continuous interaction]] uses a recorded group indicator multiplied by a continuous predictor. Specified spline basis functions—fixed piecewise-polynomial features joined at chosen knots—also fit this form. If knot locations or other feature-shape parameters are themselves unknown and optimised jointly, that enlarged parameterisation is generally not linear in all its parameters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$x^2$ 这样的幂、定义在 $x>0$ 上的 $\log x$，以及已观察特征之间的乘积，都可以作为设计列。例如，[[类别连续交互]]把记录的组别指示值与连续解释变量相乘。已指定的样条基函数——在选定结点连接起来的固定分段多项式特征——也属于这种形式。若结点位置或其他特征形状参数本身未知，并与系数一起优化，扩大的参数化一般就不再对全部参数线性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n$ observations, build one row of $X$ from each observation’s feature values; there are $p=k+1$ columns when the intercept is included. Then the model is $y=X\beta+u$. [[普通最小二乘|OLS]] can minimise squared residuals in this representation. [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|Full column rank]] is the separate condition that makes the unconstrained coefficient vector unique. For example, if every observed $x$ is either $-1$ or $1$, the columns $1$ and $x^2$ coincide. The expression remains linear in parameters, but this sample identifies only $\beta_0+\beta_1$, not the two coefficients separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $n$ 个观测，每个观测的特征值组成 $X$ 的一行；含截距时共有 $p=k+1$ 列，模型写成 $y=X\beta+u$。这种表示可以用[[普通最小二乘|OLS]]最小化残差平方和。无约束系数向量是否唯一，还要另看[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]。例如，若全部观察到的 $x$ 只取 $-1$ 或 $1$，则常数列和 $x^2$ 列相同；模型仍对参数线性，但该样本只识别 $\beta_0+\beta_1$，不能分别识别两个系数。

> [!warning] Renaming a parameter can preserve a constraint · 换参数后约束仍在
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $\beta_0+\sqrt{\beta_1}x$, let $\gamma=\sqrt{\beta_1}$. The expression becomes linear in $(\beta_0,\gamma)$, but equivalence to the original model requires $\gamma\ge0$, with $\beta_1=\gamma^2$. An unrestricted negative OLS slope is not allowed by that original model. Reparameterisation must track both the new target and its domain; it is not permission to discard restrictions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\beta_0+\sqrt{\beta_1}x$ 中令 $\gamma=\sqrt{\beta_1}$，表达式就对 $(\beta_0,\gamma)$ 线性。但要与原模型等价，必须保留 $\gamma\ge0$，并以 $\beta_1=\gamma^2$ 还原。无约束 OLS 若给出负斜率，它就不在原模型允许范围内。重新参数化要同时追踪新目标和参数域，不能丢掉原有约束。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The meaning of a coefficient also depends on its feature. In $m(x)=\beta_0+\beta_1x^2$, the [[导数|derivative]] with respect to the raw $x$ is $2\beta_1x$, not generally $\beta_1$. Thus linearity in parameters does not make the marginal slope constant. [[取值依赖边际效应|Value-dependent marginal effects]] and [[level-log 系数解释|level–log coefficient interpretation]] develop those readings. Whether the chosen function is the population conditional mean or merely a [[条件均值与线性投影|linear projection using chosen features]] is another, separate modelling question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数的含义还取决于对应特征。在 $m(x)=\beta_0+\beta_1x^2$ 中，对原始 $x$ 的[[导数]]为 $2\beta_1x$，一般不是 $\beta_1$。所以参数线性不意味着边际斜率恒定。[[取值依赖边际效应]]与 [[level-log 系数解释]]进一步解释这些读法。选定函数究竟是总体条件均值，还是[[条件均值与线性投影|使用所选特征的线性投影]]，则是另一层建模问题。
