# Fermat's little theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*费马小定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Modulo five, $3^4=81$ leaves remainder one, while $5^4$ leaves zero. Fermat’s little theorem guarantees a return to one for prime moduli and bases not divisible by the prime. A related form, $a^p\equiv a\pmod p$, also covers the excluded zero class. Distinguishing these two statements is essential when the input might be a multiple of the prime.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模五时，$3^4=81$ 的余数为一，$5^4$ 的余数却为零。费马小定理说明：模数为素数、底数不被该素数整除时，可以得到回到一的幂式。相关的形式 $a^p\equiv a\pmod p$ 还能涵盖被前一式排除的零类。输入可能是素数的倍数时，必须区分这两个陈述。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here a [[素数|prime]] $p$ is an integer greater than one with no positive divisors other than one and itself. The base $a$ is any integer. The notation $p\nmid a$ means that $p$ does not [[整除|divide]] $a$, and [[同余|congruence]] compares integer remainders modulo this same $p$. No assumption that $a$ itself is prime is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的[[素数]] $p$ 是大于一、正因子只有一与自身的整数；底数 $a$ 是任意整数。$p\nmid a$ 表示 $p$ 不[[整除]] $a$，[[同余]]则比较模同一个 $p$ 的整数余数，并不要求底数 $a$ 本身也是素数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two forms, with different hypotheses
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两种形式，条件不同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a prime $p$ and $p\nmid a$, the first congruence follows from [[欧拉定理|Euler's theorem]] and $\varphi(p)=p-1$. Multiplication by $a$, together with the zero-residue case, gives the second form for every integer $a$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $p$ 为素数且 $p\nmid a$，则
$$
a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod p.
$$
因为 $\varphi(p)=p-1$，这正是 [[欧拉定理]] 的素数情形。乘以 $a$，并另加 $p\mid a$ 时两边都为零的情况，得到对任意整数 $a$ 都成立的形式
$$
a^p\equiv a\pmod p.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Derive the prime case and then include zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先推素数情形，再补零类*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose first that $p\nmid a$. The positive common divisors of $a$ and $p$ can only be one or $p$; the latter is ruled out by $p\nmid a$. Hence $\gcd(a,p)=1$. Every representative $1,\ldots,p-1$ is likewise coprime to $p$, and zero is not, so the [[欧拉函数|totient]] is $\varphi(p)=p-1$. All hypotheses of Euler’s theorem now hold and give the first form. Multiplying it by $a$ gives the second form for these bases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先设 $p\nmid a$。$a,p$ 的正公因数只能是一或 $p$，而后者被 $p\nmid a$ 排除，所以 $\gcd(a,p)=1$。同样，代表 $1,\ldots,p-1$ 全与 $p$ 互素，零却不互素，因此[[欧拉函数]]为 $\varphi(p)=p-1$。这时欧拉定理的条件全部满足，得到第一式；再两边乘以 $a$，便得到这些底数的第二式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If instead $p\mid a$, write $a=pt$ for an integer $t$. Because $p\ge2$, both $a$ and $a^p=p^pt^p$ are divisible by $p$, so $a^p\equiv a\equiv0\pmod p$. This proves the second form for every integer, including zero and negative multiples. Conversely, for $p\nmid a$, the second form yields the first by cancelling the invertible class of $a$ using the [[同余消去律|cancellation criterion]]. That cancellation is unavailable for the zero class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $p\mid a$，写成 $a=pt$，其中 $t$ 是整数。由于 $p\ge2$，$a$ 与 $a^p=p^pt^p$ 都被 $p$ 整除，所以 $a^p\equiv a\equiv0\pmod p$。这样就证明了第二式对所有整数成立，包括零和负倍数。反过来，在 $p\nmid a$ 时，可以用[[同余消去律]]消去第二式中可逆的 $a$ 类，得到第一式；零类则不能这样消去。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first form excludes residue zero, while the second includes it. Modulo five, $5^4$ is zero rather than one, but $5^5\equiv5\equiv0$. The proof of [[RSA全消息正确性|RSA correctness for every message]] uses this distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个形式的假设不能混用：$a^{p-1}\equiv1$ 排除了零余数，$a^p\equiv a$ 则包含零余数。例如模 $5$ 时，$5^4\equiv0$ 而不是 $1$，但 $5^5\equiv5\equiv0$ 成立。[[RSA全消息正确性]] 正需要把零余数与非零余数分开处理。

> [!example] Negative bases and the prime two · 负底数与素数二
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $p=5,a=-2$, we have $(-2)^4=16\equiv1$ and $(-2)^5=-32\equiv-2\pmod5$. Negative integers are allowed; divisibility, rather than the sign, controls the first form. When $p=2$, the first form says every odd $a$ satisfies $a^1\equiv1\pmod2$. The second says $a^2\equiv a\pmod2$ for both parities, including $a=0$. There is no exception requiring an odd prime.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $p=5,a=-2$，有 $(-2)^4=16\equiv1$，且 $(-2)^5=-32\equiv-2\pmod5$。允许负整数；第一式取决于整除条件，不取决于正负。当 $p=2$ 时，第一式说明每个奇数 $a$ 都有 $a^1\equiv1\pmod2$；第二式说明奇偶两类都满足 $a^2\equiv a\pmod2$，包括 $a=0$。定理不要求素数为奇数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What can be done with an exponent
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样使用指数结论*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $p\nmid a$ and a nonnegative integer exponent $k$, write $k=q(p-1)+r$ with $0\le r<p-1$. Then $a^k\equiv a^r\pmod p$. For example, $100=25\cdot4$ gives $3^{100}\equiv1\pmod5$; the remainder exponent zero is valid and means $3^0=1$. When $p=2$, every nonnegative exponent of an odd base reduces to zero modulo $p-1=1$, consistently giving one modulo two.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $p\nmid a$ 且指数 $k$ 为非负整数时，把 $k$ 写成 $q(p-1)+r$，其中 $0\le r<p-1$，便有 $a^k\equiv a^r\pmod p$。例如 $100=25\cdot4$ 给出 $3^{100}\equiv1\pmod5$；余下的零指数合法，表示 $3^0=1$。当 $p=2$ 时，奇数底数的每个非负指数模 $p-1=1$ 都余零，也确实都给出模二余一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonzero integer $a$ divisible by $p$, every positive power is zero modulo $p$, whereas $a^0=1$. Reducing a positive exponent to zero would therefore be wrong; $5^4$ is the example above. If $a=0$, the theorem uses only the positive exponents $p-1$ and $p$ and does not assign a value to $0^0$. As in Euler’s theorem, comparing two nonnegative exponents with a negative difference uses symmetry after ordering them; negative powers themselves require a separately defined modular inverse and are not supplied for the zero class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若非零整数 $a$ 被 $p$ 整除，每个正幂都模 $p$ 余零，而 $a^0=1$。把正指数约简成零就会出错，上面的 $5^4$ 正是例子。若 $a=0$，定理只使用正指数 $p-1$ 与 $p$，没有给 $0^0$ 赋值。与欧拉定理相同，比较两个非负指数而差为负时，先按大小排列再用同余的对称性即可；负幂本身需要另外定义模逆元，零类没有这样的逆元。

## A successful test is not a primality proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*通过检验不等于证明素数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The converse fails: the composite number $341=11\cdot31$ satisfies $2^{340}\equiv1\pmod{341}$ because $2^{10}=1024\equiv1\pmod{341}$. Passing the congruence test for one base does not prove primality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它的逆命题不成立：合数 $341=11\cdot31$ 也有 $2^{340}\equiv1\pmod{341}$，因为 $2^{10}=1024\equiv1\pmod{341}$。所以某个底数通过费马同余检验，不能单独证明模数是素数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed counterexample is exact: $1024=3\cdot341+1$, so raising that congruence to the power $34$ gives $2^{340}\equiv1\pmod{341}$. Yet $341=11\cdot31$ has two factors greater than one. A prime modulus guarantees the congruence for every permitted base; observing it for one base does not reverse that implication. Conversely, for an integer candidate $N>1$ and an integer $a$ with $1\le a<N$, failure of $a^{N-1}\equiv1\pmod N$ proves that $N$ is not prime. This is a one-sided exclusion test, not a proof that every composite will fail a chosen base.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反例可以精确核算：$1024=3\cdot341+1$，将这个同余式取 $34$ 次幂，就得到 $2^{340}\equiv1\pmod{341}$。但 $341=11\cdot31$ 确实有两个大于一的因子。素数模数保证每个允许底数都满足同余式；只观察一个底数成立，不能把蕴含反过来。另一方面，对整数候选 $N>1$ 和 $1\le a<N$ 的整数底数，若 $a^{N-1}\equiv1\pmod N$ 失败，就能证明 $N$ 不是素数。这是单向排除检验，不是说每个合数都会在某个指定底数上失败。
