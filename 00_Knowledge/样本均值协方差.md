# Covariance of the sample mean vector
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*样本均值向量的协方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Averaging can reduce uncertainty when observations bring information that is not already contained in the others. Copying the same measurement many times does not have that effect. **The covariance of the sample mean** describes how the vector of averages varies across possible samples, including how different observations move together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果观测带来了其他观测中没有的信息，平均可能降低不确定性；把同一份测量复制多次，并不会产生同样效果。**样本均值的协方差**描述平均向量在不同可能样本间怎样波动，并计入不同观测之间的共同变化。

> [!example] Two identical rows are still one random measurement · 两行相同记录仍只有一份随机测量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In one dimension, let $X_1=X_2=Z$ with finite positive variance $\operatorname{Var}(Z)=\sigma^2$. Then $\bar X=(X_1+X_2)/2=Z$, so $\operatorname{Var}(\bar X)=\sigma^2$, not $\sigma^2/2$. The second row repeats exactly the same uncertainty.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一维时，令 $X_1=X_2=Z$，且 $\operatorname{Var}(Z)=\sigma^2$ 有限为正。那么 $\bar X=(X_1+X_2)/2=Z$，所以 $\operatorname{Var}(\bar X)=\sigma^2$，并非 $\sigma^2/2$。第二行重复了完全相同的不确定性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keeping observation pairs and coordinate pairs distinct
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*区分两份观测与两个坐标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n,p\ge1$ be integers and let $X_1,\ldots,X_n\in\mathbb R^p$ be random observation vectors on one probability space. The index $i$ identifies an observation and $j$ identifies a coordinate $X_{ij}$. Assume **every coordinate has a finite second moment**, $E[X_{ij}^2]<\infty$. Write $\mu_i=E[X_i]$ and $\bar X=n^{-1}\sum_iX_i$ for the [[样本均值向量|sample mean vector]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n,p\ge1$ 为整数，$X_1,\ldots,X_n\in\mathbb R^p$ 是同一概率空间上的随机观测向量。下标 $i$ 指哪份观测，$j$ 指坐标 $X_{ij}$。假设**每个坐标的二阶矩有限**，即 $E[X_{ij}^2]<\infty$。记 $\mu_i=E[X_i]$，[[样本均值向量]]为 $\bar X=n^{-1}\sum_iX_i$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For observations $i$ and $k$, define the $p\times p$ cross-covariance block
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对第 $i$、$k$ 份观测，定义 $p\times p$ 的交叉协方差块

$$
C_{ik}=\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_k)
=E[(X_i-\mu_i)(X_k-\mu_k)^T],
\qquad (C_{ik})_{j\ell}=\operatorname{Cov}(X_{ij},X_{k\ell}).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus a block compares every coordinate of one observation with every coordinate of another. The diagonal block $C_{ii}$ is the usual [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] of one observation. For $i\ne k$, $C_{ik}$ need not be symmetric; exchanging the two observations gives $C_{ki}=C_{ik}^T$. These are matrices, not just one scalar correlation between rows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以，一个块比较的是一份观测的每个坐标与另一份观测的每个坐标。对角块 $C_{ii}$ 是单份观测通常的[[协方差矩阵]]。$i\ne k$ 时，$C_{ik}$ 不必对称，交换两份观测得到的是 $C_{ki}=C_{ik}^T$。它们是矩阵，并不只是“两个行之间的一个相关系数”。

> [!important] Keep every ordered pair · 保留每一对有序观测
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under the finite second-moment assumptions,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在上述有限二阶矩条件下，
>
> $$
> \boxed{\operatorname{Cov}(\bar X)
> =\frac1{n^2}\sum_{i=1}^n\sum_{k=1}^n C_{ik}}
> =\frac1{n^2}\left[\sum_{i=1}^n C_{ii}
> +\sum_{i<k}(C_{ik}+C_{ik}^T)\right].
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The result is $p\times p$: it describes the $p$ coordinates of the average. It allows differing observation means, differing covariances and dependence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 结果是 $p\times p$，因为它描述平均向量的 $p$ 个坐标；各观测可以均值不同、协方差不同，也可以相互依赖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To derive it, [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]] gives $E[\bar X]=n^{-1}\sum_i\mu_i$. Therefore the centered mean is $n^{-1}\sum_i(X_i-\mu_i)$. Its outer product expands as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

推导时，先由[[期望线性性]]得到 $E[\bar X]=n^{-1}\sum_i\mu_i$，所以中心化后的平均是 $n^{-1}\sum_i(X_i-\mu_i)$。它的外积展开为

$$
(\bar X-E[\bar X])(\bar X-E[\bar X])^T
=\frac1{n^2}\sum_i\sum_k (X_i-\mu_i)(X_k-\mu_k)^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take expectations entry by entry. Finite second moments ensure the centered products are integrable, giving exactly the displayed blocks. For scalars the paired cross terms combine into twice a covariance, as in [[和的方差协方差项|the variance-of-a-sum rule]]. For vectors, use $C_{ik}+C_{ik}^T$; replacing this by $2C_{ik}$ is generally incorrect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逐元素取期望，有限二阶矩保证中心化乘积可积，得到上面的交叉块。标量时，成对交叉项可以合为协方差的两倍，见[[和的方差协方差项]]；向量时则应使用 $C_{ik}+C_{ik}^T$，一般不能换成 $2C_{ik}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When division by the sample size is valid
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*什么时候可以直接除以样本量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose all observations have the same covariance $C_{ii}=\Sigma$, and distinct observations are uncorrelated in every coordinate pair: $C_{ik}=0$ for all $i\ne k$. The double sum then contains just $n$ copies of $\Sigma$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若每份观测的协方差相同，即 $C_{ii}=\Sigma$，且不同观测之间的每一对坐标都不相关，即所有 $i\ne k$ 都有 $C_{ik}=0$，双重和只剩 $n$ 份 $\Sigma$，于是

$$\operatorname{Cov}(\bar X)=\frac{n\Sigma}{n^2}=\frac{\Sigma}{n}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[独立同分布|I.i.d. sampling]] with finite coordinate second moments is a common sufficient case. Independence of the **observation vectors** does not require coordinates within one vector to be independent; off-diagonal entries of $\Sigma$ can remain nonzero. The weaker uncorrelated-observation condition above also suffices, without identical distributions. If their individual covariances differ but the cross blocks vanish, the answer is instead $n^{-2}\sum_i C_{ii}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具有有限坐标二阶矩的[[独立同分布|i.i.d.抽样]]是常用充分情形。**观测向量之间**独立，并不要求单个向量内部的坐标独立，所以 $\Sigma$ 的非对角元仍可非零。上面更弱的观测间不相关条件也已足够，不必同分布。如果各自协方差不同，但交叉块为零，则结果是 $n^{-2}\sum_iC_{ii}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, with $n=4$ uncorrelated observations each having covariance $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}$, the mean covariance is $\begin{bmatrix}1/4&1/2\\1/2&1\end{bmatrix}$. The coordinates within each observation are correlated; averaging does not require eliminating that structure. Equality to $\Sigma/n$ alone does not prove zero cross blocks: different cross contributions could cancel.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$n=4$ 份不相关观测各有协方差 $\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{bmatrix}$，平均后的协方差为 $\begin{bmatrix}1/4&1/2\\1/2&1\end{bmatrix}$。每份观测内部的两个坐标相关，并不妨碍这个结果。反过来，仅看到结果等于 $\Sigma/n$ 不能证明交叉块全为零，因为不同交叉贡献可能抵消。

## Why row count alone is insufficient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 相关观察为什么不能只数行

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two scalar observations with the same variance $\sigma^2$ and covariance $c$, the general formula becomes $\operatorname{Var}(\bar X)=(\sigma^2+c)/2$. Uncorrelated observations give $c=0$ and halve the variance. Duplicate observations have $c=\sigma^2$ and do not reduce it. Dependence can also reduce variance: if $X_1=\mu+Z$ and $X_2=\mu-Z$, with $E[Z]=0$ and finite variance, then $c=-\operatorname{Var}(Z)$ and the average is exactly $\mu$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个标量观测若方差都为 $\sigma^2$、协方差为 $c$，通式变成 $\operatorname{Var}(\bar X)=(\sigma^2+c)/2$。不相关时 $c=0$，方差减半；完全复制时 $c=\sigma^2$，方差不减少。依赖也可能降低方差：若 $X_1=\mu+Z$、$X_2=\mu-Z$，$E[Z]=0$ 且方差有限，则 $c=-\operatorname{Var}(Z)$，平均恰好等于 $\mu$。

> [!question]- One hundred rows from twenty people · 二十个人的一百行记录
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose each of twenty people contributes five repeated measurements. Can row count alone justify $\Sigma/100$? No: the within-person cross covariances must be included. In the extreme model where each person's five rows are exact copies, and the twenty person-level vectors are independent with common covariance $\Sigma$, averaging all rows is the same as averaging twenty vectors, so the covariance is $\Sigma/20$. Less extreme dependence needs its own cross blocks.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 二十个人每人提供五次重复测量，能仅凭行数写成 $\Sigma/100$ 吗？不能，必须计入同一人内的交叉协方差。极端模型中，若同一个人的五行是精确复制，而二十个人的向量相互独立、共有协方差 $\Sigma$，平均全部行等于平均二十个向量，协方差为 $\Sigma/20$。其他程度的依赖需要相应交叉块。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This covariance concerns the **random mean statistic**, not the [[样本协方差矩阵|sample covariance matrix]] computed to describe observed rows. A specialized [[观测行协方差|separable row-covariance factor]] is one modeling tool for certain regression data; it is not required for the general formula here. The [[样本均值方差判据|WSS time-series variance criterion]] studies a different specialization in which cross covariances depend only on lag and the sample size tends to infinity. When $n=1$, there are no distinct-observation pairs and the formula simply gives $\operatorname{Cov}(X_1)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的协方差描述的是**随机的均值统计量**，不是用已观测数据计算的[[样本协方差矩阵]]。特定回归模型中的[[观测行协方差|可分离行协方差因子]]是一种建模工具，并不是本卡通式的前提。[[样本均值方差判据]]研究另一个宽平稳时间序列特例，其中交叉协方差只依赖滞后，并考察样本量趋于无穷的行为。$n=1$ 时不存在不同观测对，公式直接退化为 $\operatorname{Cov}(X_1)$。

The finite second-moment condition and the weaker uncorrelated/common-covariance sufficient conditions are stated explicitly here.
