# 交错级数的非负幅度最终单调不增且趋于零就保证收敛
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An alternating series converges if its nonnegative magnitudes eventually decrease monotonically to zero*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于 $\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}b_n$ 或其整体负号版本，若存在 $N_0$，使 $0\le b_{n+1}\le b_n$ 对所有 $n\ge N_0$ 成立，且 $b_n\to0$，则级数收敛。所有前项须有定义；有限前缀不影响结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The series $\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}b_n$, or its negative, converges if $0\le b_{n+1}\le b_n$ eventually and $b_n\to0$. Every initial term must be defined; a finite prefix does not affect convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单调性使尾部的奇、偶部分和分别从两侧逼近，且相邻部分和的距离 $b_n$ 趋于零，因此两侧极限相同。允许相等和零项：如果幅度在单调尾部达到零，之后只能全为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Monotonicity makes odd and even tail partial sums approach from opposite sides, and their gap tends to zero. Their limits therefore coincide. Equal and zero magnitudes are allowed; once a nonincreasing nonnegative tail reaches zero, it stays zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本判别只证明收敛。要证明 [[条件收敛]]，还必须证明 $\sum b_n$ 发散；例如 $b_n=1/n$ 时，第二步由 [[调和数对数增长]] 完成。若 $b_n=1/n^2$，交错判别同样成立，但 [[p级数判别]] 表明这是 [[绝对收敛]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The test proves convergence only. [[条件收敛|Conditional convergence]] additionally requires divergence of $\sum b_n$, as [[调和数对数增长|harmonic-number growth]] shows when $b_n=1/n$. With $b_n=1/n^2$, the same alternating test succeeds, but the [[p级数判别|p-series test]] gives [[绝对收敛|absolute convergence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只看到正负交替不够；若幅度不趋于零，原级数由 [[级数通项判别]] 发散。若幅度趋零但不满足最终单调，本判别无结论，需要另找论证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Alternating signs alone are insufficient. If magnitudes do not approach zero, the [[级数通项判别|term test]] proves divergence. If they approach zero without eventual monotonicity, this test is inconclusive and another argument is needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
