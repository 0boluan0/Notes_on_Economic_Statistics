# The alternating series test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*交错级数判别（Leibniz 判别）*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding, then subtracting, then adding again can make a running total move back and forth. The **alternating series test** gives a reason for those movements to settle: if their sizes eventually never increase and tend to zero, the totals approach a finite limit. Alternating signs alone do not provide that guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先加、再减、再加，累计结果就会来回移动。**交错级数判别**解释这种移动何时能够稳定：如果每次移动的大小最终不再增大，并趋于零，累计值就趋于有限极限。只有正负交替，还不足以保证这一点。

> [!example] Moving between shrinking bounds · 在逐渐靠近的两边来回移动
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $1-1/2+1/3-1/4+\cdots$, the first four partial sums are $1$, $1/2$, $5/6$, $7/12$. The even totals begin $1/2,7/12,\ldots$ and move upward; the odd totals begin $1,5/6,\ldots$ and move downward. Their gap shrinks as the next added or subtracted amount shrinks. We will prove that both approach the same number, without needing its exact value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $1-1/2+1/3-1/4+\cdots$，前四个部分和是 $1$、$1/2$、$5/6$、$7/12$。偶数部分和从 $1/2,7/12,\ldots$ 向上移动，奇数部分和从 $1,5/6,\ldots$ 向下移动。随着下一次加减的量缩小，两边的间隔也缩小。下面会证明它们趋于同一个数，而不需要先知道精确数值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Separate the sign from the magnitude
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把符号与幅度分开*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the real [[无穷级数|series]] as $\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}b_n$, where $b_n\ge0$ is the magnitude of term $n$. The factor $(-1)^{n-1}$ gives a positive sign at odd $n$ and a negative sign at even $n$. The version $\sum(-1)^n b_n$ reverses every sign and has the same convergence behaviour. Every term must be defined and finite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把实数项[[无穷级数|级数]]写成 $\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}b_n$，其中 $b_n\ge0$ 是第 $n$ 项的幅度。因子 $(-1)^{n-1}$ 在奇数 $n$ 时为正，偶数 $n$ 时为负。若写成 $\sum(-1)^n b_n$，只是所有符号反向，敛散性相同。每项都必须有定义且有限。

> [!important] Two conditions on the tail · 尾部的两个条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose there is a positive integer $N_0$ such that $0\le b_{n+1}\le b_n$ for every $n\ge N_0$, and suppose $b_n\to0$. Then the alternating series converges. “Nonincreasing” permits equal consecutive magnitudes. If a magnitude in this nonnegative nonincreasing tail becomes zero, all following ones are zero too.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若存在正整数 $N_0$，使每个 $n\ge N_0$ 都满足 $0\le b_{n+1}\le b_n$，并且 $b_n\to0$，则交错级数收敛。“单调不增”允许相邻幅度相等；如果这个非负、不增尾部中某个幅度降到零，后面就只能全部为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Convergence means a finite limit of the partial sums, not a limit of the magnitudes alone. Only the tail conditions matter: a finite prefix of defined terms adds a fixed finite constant. Thus an early increase in magnitude does not prevent applying the test after it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收敛指部分和具有有限极限，不是只看幅度的极限。条件只需最终成立：有限多个已定义前项只加上固定有限常数。所以，早期出现一次幅度增大，并不妨碍在它之后使用判别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the two sets of partial sums meet
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*奇偶两列部分和为什么会相遇*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First assume the conditions hold from the first term and the first sign is positive. Put $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N(-1)^{n-1}b_n$. For $k\ge1$, comparing two totals of the same parity gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先假设条件从第一项就成立，并且第一项符号为正。记 $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N(-1)^{n-1}b_n$。对 $k\ge1$，比较两个奇偶性相同的部分和，得到

$$S_{2k+2}-S_{2k}=b_{2k+1}-b_{2k+2}\ge0,$$
$$S_{2k+1}-S_{2k-1}=-b_{2k}+b_{2k+1}\le0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The even totals therefore increase or stay fixed, and the odd totals decrease or stay fixed. Also $0\le S_{2k}\le S_{2k+1}\le b_1$: pairing adjacent terms gives the lower bound, and the decreasing odd totals stay below $S_1=b_1$. Both subsequences are bounded and monotone, so the [[实数的确界性质|least-upper-bound property]] gives finite limits. Their gap is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，偶数部分和不减，奇数部分和不增。并且 $0\le S_{2k}\le S_{2k+1}\le b_1$：相邻项成对可得下界，奇数部分和不增则保证不超过 $S_1=b_1$。两列都有界且单调，由[[实数的确界性质]]具有有限极限。它们之间的间隔为

$$S_{2k+1}-S_{2k}=b_{2k+1}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Their limits must consequently be equal. Every sufficiently late partial sum is either an even or an odd one, so the whole sequence tends to this common limit. If the conditions begin only at $N_0$, relabel that tail starting at one, reverse all its signs if necessary, apply the argument there, and finally add back the finite prefix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以两列极限相同。每个足够晚的部分和不是偶数项，就是奇数项，因而整列都趋于这个共同极限。若条件到 $N_0$ 才成立，就把该尾部重新从一编号，必要时把尾部所有符号反向，在尾部使用同一论证，最后加回有限前缀。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the result does not decide
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*这个结果还没有决定什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $b_n=1/n$, the two conditions hold, so the alternating harmonic series converges. The sum of its magnitudes is the ordinary harmonic series, which diverges by [[调和数对数增长|harmonic-number growth]]. This makes the original series [[条件收敛|conditionally convergent]]. For $b_n=1/n^2$, the same alternating test succeeds, but the [[p级数判别|p-series test]] shows the magnitude series converges too: this is [[绝对收敛|absolute convergence]]. Passing the alternating test alone does not choose between these two cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $b_n=1/n$，两个条件都成立，所以交错调和级数收敛；其幅度之和是普通调和级数，由[[调和数对数增长]]可知发散。因此原级数[[条件收敛]]。取 $b_n=1/n^2$，交错判别同样成功，但 [[p级数判别]]说明幅度级数也收敛，所以这是[[绝对收敛]]。只通过交错判别，还没有区分这两类。

> [!warning] Tending to zero without decreasing is not enough · 只趋零而不递减，仍可能失败
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Set $b_{2k-1}=1/k$ and $b_{2k}=1/(2k)$ for $k\ge1$. The magnitudes tend to zero, and the signs alternate, but each positive–negative pair contributes $1/(2k)$. Thus $S_{2K}=\tfrac12\sum_{k=1}^K1/k\to\infty$, and $S_{2K+1}=S_{2K}+1/(K+1)\to\infty$ too. Magnitudes repeatedly jump upward between pairs, so eventual monotonicity fails.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $k\ge1$ 时 $b_{2k-1}=1/k$、$b_{2k}=1/(2k)$。幅度趋于零，符号也交替，但每对正负项仍留下 $1/(2k)$。于是 $S_{2K}=\tfrac12\sum_{k=1}^K1/k\to\infty$，而 $S_{2K+1}=S_{2K}+1/(K+1)\to\infty$ 也成立。两组之间的幅度反复向上跳，违反最终单调性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Failure of monotonicity does not itself prove divergence. For example, $b_{2k-1}=1/k^2$ and $b_{2k}=2/k^2$ increase inside every pair, but their total magnitude is $3\sum_{k=1}^\infty1/k^2<\infty$; [[绝对收敛推出收敛|absolute convergence therefore proves convergence]]. The alternating test is sufficient, not necessary. If instead $b_n$ fails to tend to zero, the [[级数通项判别|nth-term test]] directly rules out convergence. For an accuracy guarantee after truncation, continue to [[交错级数误差界|the alternating remainder bound]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不满足单调性，本身也不能证明发散。例如，$b_{2k-1}=1/k^2$、$b_{2k}=2/k^2$ 在每组内都增大，但总幅度是 $3\sum_{k=1}^\infty1/k^2<\infty$，故由[[绝对收敛推出收敛]]得到收敛。交错判别给的是充分条件，不是必要条件。若幅度本身不趋零，则可直接用[[级数通项判别]]排除收敛。截断后的精度保证见[[交错级数误差界]]。

## 来源与核验

- [Lebl, Basic Analysis I v6.3, Proposition2.6.2, PDF101–102](https://www.jirka.org/ra/realanal.pdf#page=101): monotone magnitudes tending to zero and the bounded-parity proof. The proof here uses a positive first sign; the source uses a negative first sign.

Equal/zero magnitudes and finite prefixes are handled explicitly above. Both nonmonotone examples are checked by their finite paired sums or total magnitudes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Lebl《Basic Analysis I》6.3版，命题2.6.2，PDF101–102页：幅度单调趋零与有界奇偶部分和证明。正文首项取正，原书首项取负，故相应奇偶方向不同。
- OpenStax §5.5 定理5.13支持最终成立的条件，以及调和与平方倒数两类例子的区分。相等或零幅度、有限前缀在正文明确处理；两个非单调例子通过有限配对和或总幅度直接核验。
