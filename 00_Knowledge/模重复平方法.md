# Modular exponentiation by repeated squaring
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*模重复平方法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A large power can be evaluated without ever writing the large integer. Repeated squaring builds powers with exponents one, two, four, eight, and so on; the binary digits of the requested exponent tell us which ones to multiply. Keeping only remainders makes the intermediate numbers manageable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算很大的幂，不必先写出那个巨大整数。重复平方法依次建立指数为一、二、四、八等的幂，再按目标指数的二进制位选择要乘的项；过程中只保留余数，就能控制中间数的大小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here [[模运算|modular arithmetic]] means taking the standard remainder after an integer calculation. For a positive integer modulus $n$, that remainder is one of $0,\ldots,n-1$. Multiplication respects remainders: replacing either factor by its remainder leaves the product in the same [[同余|congruence class]]. This fact, rather than an assumption about invertibility, justifies every reduction below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的[[模运算]]是把整数运算结果换成标准余数。正整数模数 $n$ 的标准余数属于 $0,\ldots,n-1$。乘法与取余相容：把因子换成其余数，乘积仍处于同一个[[同余|同余类]]。下面每次取余都由这个性质保证，不依赖可逆性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Select powers using the exponent’s binary digits
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*按指数的二进制位选幂*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For integer $a$, modulus $n\ge2$, and nonnegative exponent $k$, compute the residues of successive squared powers and multiply those selected by the one-bits of $k$. Reduce after each square and product. The base need not be coprime to the modulus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对整数 $a$、模数 $n\ge2$ 和非负整数指数 $k$，把 $k$ 写成二进制，再依次计算 $a,a^2,a^4,a^8,\ldots$ 的余数，乘起对应于二进制中 $1$ 的那些项，即可求出 $a^k\bmod n$。每次平方和乘法后立即取模；不要求 $\gcd(a,n)=1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exponent is an integer. For example, $13=(1101)_2=8+4+1$ means $a^{13}=a^8a^4a$. Each new power is obtained by squaring the previous one: $(a^{2^j})^2=a^{2^{j+1}}$. The digit for $2^1$ is zero, so $a^2$ is computed on the way but is not selected in the final product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指数必须是整数。例如 $13=(1101)_2=8+4+1$ 表示 $a^{13}=a^8a^4a$。每个新幂由前一个平方得到：$(a^{2^j})^2=a^{2^{j+1}}$。$2^1$ 对应的位为零，因此中途虽算出 $a^2$，最后却不选它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $k=13=8+4+1$, the displayed residues give $7^{13}\equiv31\cdot25\cdot7\equiv13\pmod{33}$. Only small residues need to be retained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $13=8+4+1$，计算 $7^{13}\bmod33$ 时有
$$
7^1\equiv7,\quad7^2\equiv16,\quad
7^4\equiv25,\quad7^8\equiv31\pmod{33}.
$$
于是 $7^{13}\equiv31\cdot25\cdot7\equiv16\cdot7\equiv13\pmod{33}$。计算只保留小余数，无需写出完整的 $7^{13}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep an accumulator, a current power and a remaining exponent
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*保留累积值、当前幂与剩余指数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Initialize $(r,b,t)=(1,a\bmod n,k)$. For positive $t$, multiply $r$ by $b$ modulo $n$ when $t$ is odd, then square $b$ modulo $n$ and halve $t$ using integer division. Combining the [[不变量与部分正确性|invariant and exit condition]] proves the returned $r$ is correct. The nonnegative integer $t$ strictly decreases at each nonterminal step, so it is also a [[秩函数与终止证明|ranking function proving termination]]. Exponent zero returns one immediately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个可核验的循环维护 $(r,b,t)$，初始化为 $(1,a\bmod n,k)$。当 $t>0$，若 $t$ 为奇数就先令 $r\leftarrow rb\bmod n$；随后令 $b\leftarrow b^2\bmod n$、$t\leftarrow\lfloor t/2\rfloor$。不变量为
$$
r b^t\equiv a^k\pmod n.
$$
终止时 $t=0$，返回 $r$，这是将 [[不变量与部分正确性|不变量与退出条件合并]] 的应用。每个未终止步骤都令非负整数 $t$ 严格下降，因此 $t$ 也是保证终止的 [[秩函数与终止证明|秩函数]]。若 $k=0$，直接返回 $1$，无需进入循环。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The accumulator $r$ stores the powers already selected. The value $b$ stores the current squared power as a remainder, and $t$ is the part of the exponent still to process. An odd $t$ has last binary digit one, so include $b$ in $r$; then discard that digit by taking $\lfloor t/2\rfloor$ and advance $b$ by squaring it. The floor symbol means the greatest integer no larger than its argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

累积值 $r$ 存放已经选中的幂的乘积余数；$b$ 存放当前待选幂的余数；$t$ 是尚未处理的指数。$t$ 为奇数，表示末位为一，就把 $b$ 乘入 $r$；随后用 $\lfloor t/2\rfloor$ 去掉末位，并把 $b$ 平方，前进到下一位。下取整符号表示不大于括号内数的最大整数。

> [!example] Follow the state · 跟踪状态
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $(a,k,n)=(7,13,33)$, the table lists the state at each round’s start and at exit. At the third round, $7\cdot25=175$ has remainder $10$; at the fourth, $10\cdot31=310$ has remainder $13$. The last square is harmless even though its value will no longer be used.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $(a,k,n)=(7,13,33)$，表中列出各轮开始及退出时的状态。第三轮 $7\cdot25=175$ 的余数是 $10$；第四轮 $10\cdot31=310$ 的余数是 $13$。最后仍做一次平方不会改变答案，只是所得值已经用不上。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> | Round start / exit | $r$ | $b$ | $t$ |
> | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
> | 1 | 1 | 7 | 13 |
> | 2 | 7 | 16 | 6 |
> | 3 | 7 | 25 | 3 |
> | 4 | 10 | 31 | 1 |
> | Exit | 13 | 4 | 0 |

## Why the loop returns the right remainder
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*循环为什么返回正确余数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[不变量与部分正确性|invariant]] is a statement that is true initially and survives each round. Initially $r=1$, $b\equiv a\pmod n$ and $t=k$, so $rb^t\equiv a^k$. Write the old exponent as $t=2s+\varepsilon$, where $\varepsilon$ is zero or one. Before reductions, the next accumulator is $rb^\varepsilon$, the next base is $b^2$, and the next exponent is $s$. Thus their combined expression is $rb^\varepsilon(b^2)^s=rb^{2s+\varepsilon}=rb^t$. Reducing either factor modulo $n$ preserves this congruence. This proves preservation for both even and odd rounds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[不变量与部分正确性|不变量]]是初始成立且每轮之后仍成立的命题。初始时 $r=1$、$b\equiv a\pmod n$、$t=k$，因此 $rb^t\equiv a^k$。把旧指数写成 $t=2s+\varepsilon$，其中 $\varepsilon$ 为零或一。暂不取余时，新累积值是 $rb^\varepsilon$，新底数是 $b^2$，新指数是 $s$；合起来得到 $rb^\varepsilon(b^2)^s=rb^{2s+\varepsilon}=rb^t$。把两个因子分别取余仍保留同余关系，这同时证明奇数轮与偶数轮的不变量保持。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For positive integer $t$, $0\le\lfloor t/2\rfloor<t$. The [[秩函数与终止证明|decreasing nonnegative integer]] therefore reaches zero after finitely many rounds. At exit, $b^0=1$, so the invariant gives $r\equiv a^k\pmod n$. Since every update keeps $0\le r<n$, the returned value is the unique standard remainder, not just an unspecified congruent integer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正整数 $t$ 满足 $0\le\lfloor t/2\rfloor<t$，所以这个[[秩函数与终止证明|严格下降的非负整数]]会在有限轮后到达零。退出时 $b^0=1$，不变量化为 $r\equiv a^k\pmod n$；每次更新又都保持 $0\le r<n$，故返回的是唯一标准余数，而不只是某个与答案同余的整数。

## Cost and input boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*计算量与输入边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The number of rounds is the bit length of a positive $k$, giving $O(\log(k+1))$ modular multiplications, not that many bit operations. Correctness of intermediate reduction follows from [[模运算|modular multiplication]]. Reducing the exponent itself requires a separate result such as [[欧拉定理|Euler's theorem]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $k\ge1$ 时，循环轮数为二进制位数，所以需要 $O(\log(k+1))$ 次模乘。这里数的是模乘次数，不是位运算总成本。每步取模的合法性来自 [[模运算]] 与乘法兼容；它没有授权把指数直接按 $n$ 取余，指数约简需另用 [[欧拉定理]] 等条件。

> [!warning] The exponent is not a residue · 指数不能随意取余
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For example, $2^5\bmod5=2$, whereas replacing the exponent by $5\bmod5=0$ gives $2^0\bmod5=1$. The algorithm reduces products, not the exponent modulo the same $n$. It also works for noncoprime bases: $6^3\bmod9=0$, although six has no inverse modulo nine.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 例如 $2^5\bmod5=2$；若把指数换成 $5\bmod5=0$，就变成 $2^0\bmod5=1$。算法约简的是乘积，不是把指数也对同一个 $n$ 取余。它也适用于不互素底数：$6^3\bmod9=0$，尽管六在模九下没有逆元。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $k>0$, there are $\lfloor\log_2k\rfloor+1$ rounds, at most two modular multiplications per round in this version. A modular multiplication has its own cost depending on the size of $n$. Exponent zero uses the empty-product convention $a^0=1$, including $a=0$ for this algorithm; this is an explicit discrete convention, not a claim about limits involving $0^0$. Negative bases are first replaced by their standard remainder. Negative exponents are outside this method’s stated input domain; interpreting them requires a [[模逆元|modular inverse]] when one exists. Modulus one is also outside the stated domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $k>0$ 时，轮数是 $\lfloor\log_2k\rfloor+1$，这个版本每轮至多做两次模乘；一次模乘本身的成本还随 $n$ 的大小变化。零指数采用空乘积约定 $a^0=1$，本算法中也包括 $a=0$；这是明确的离散运算约定，不是在讨论 $0^0$ 型极限。负底数先换成标准余数。负指数不在这里的输入域中；解释负幂需要存在相应[[模逆元]]。模数一也不在本卡声明的输入域内。
