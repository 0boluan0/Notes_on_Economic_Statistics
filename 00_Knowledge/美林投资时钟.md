# 美林投资时钟是以增长与通胀状态组织资产轮动假设的配置框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The investment clock organizes asset-rotation hypotheses around growth and inflation states.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

美林投资时钟把增长与通胀的强弱或改善方向组合成阶段，再联系不同资产的相对偏好。它是经验配置框架，不是从[[经济周期]]定义推出的定理；使用版本必须说明“增长”和“通胀”各采用什么指标与分类规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The investment clock combines growth and inflation conditions or directions into phases and associates them with relative asset preferences. It is an empirical allocation framework, not a theorem implied by the [[经济周期|business-cycle definition]]. Each implementation must specify its measures and classification rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本课的四象限叙事如下；“偏好”表示框架中的相对配置假设，不是下一期必然跑赢。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course's four-quadrant narrative is summarized below. A preference is a relative allocation hypothesis, not guaranteed outperformance next period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 增长方向 / Growth | 通胀方向 / Inflation | 课堂阶段 / Phase | 典型偏好 / Preference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 下行 / Weakening | 下行 / Falling | 衰退 / Reflation-downturn | 债券 / Bonds |
| 改善 / Improving | 下行 / Falling | 复苏 / Recovery | 股票 / Equities |
| 改善 / Improving | 上行 / Rising | 过热 / Overheat | 商品 / Commodities |
| 下行 / Weakening | 上行 / Rising | 滞胀 / Stagflation | 现金 / Cash |

增长改善并不要求产出高于潜能；增长下降也不等于实际 GDP 为负。图中时钟方向不是时间日程。怎样得到阶段判断见[[投资时钟阶段识别]]，怎样限制配置结论见[[投资时钟配置边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Improving growth does not require output above potential, and weakening growth need not mean negative GDP growth. The clock is not a calendar. See [[投资时钟阶段识别|phase identification]] and [[投资时钟配置边界|allocation boundaries]] for its use.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
