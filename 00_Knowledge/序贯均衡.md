# 序贯均衡是同时满足序贯理性与 Kreps–Wilson 一致性的策略—信念评估
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A sequential equilibrium is a strategy-belief assessment satisfying both sequential rationality and Kreps–Wilson consistency*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 路径外 beliefs 来自同一 tremble 极限
> 序贯均衡保留逐信息集的最优性，并要求所有 beliefs 可由同一序列完全混合策略经 Bayes 更新后共同收敛得到。每个信息集的 perturbation 因而属于同一套策略序列，而不是分别为各处挑选互不相容的解释。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Sequential equilibrium retains optimality at every information set and requires all beliefs to arise jointly as limits of Bayes updates from one sequence of completely mixed strategies. Perturbations at different information sets therefore belong to a common sequence rather than to separately chosen explanations.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 经典有限行动定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Classical finite-action definition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在具有完美记忆和有限行动集合的经典扩展式环境中，令 $(\sigma,\mu)$ 是一份[[策略信念评估|assessment]]。它满足 **Kreps–Wilson consistency**，当且仅当存在 assessment 序列

$$
(\sigma^m,\mu^m)\longrightarrow(\sigma,\mu),
$$

其中每个 $\sigma^m$ 都 completely mixed，即每个信息集上的每个可行行动都有严格正概率；并且对所有信息集 $I$ 及节点 $x\in I$，

$$
\mu^m(x\mid I)
=
\frac{P_{\sigma^m}(x)}{P_{\sigma^m}(I)}.
$$

由于完全混合使每个信息集以正概率到达，这个 Bayes 分式在每个 $m$、每个信息集上都有定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the classical finite-action extensive-form setting with perfect recall, an assessment $(\sigma,\mu)$ is Kreps–Wilson consistent if there exists a sequence $(\sigma^m,\mu^m)\to(\sigma,\mu)$ in which every $\sigma^m$ is completely mixed and every $\mu^m$ is generated by Bayes' rule from that same $\sigma^m$ at every information set. Complete mixing gives every feasible action positive probability and makes each Bayesian denominator positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一份 assessment 是**序贯均衡**，当且仅当它既满足上述 consistency，又满足[[序贯理性]]：在每个信息集上，给定极限 belief 与其他玩家续局策略，当前玩家的完整续局策略最大化条件期望收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An assessment is a sequential equilibrium exactly when it is both consistent in this sense and sequentially rational: at every information set, the player's complete continuation strategy maximizes conditional expected payoff given the limiting belief and the opponents' continuation strategies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同一序列约束 beliefs 的联合来源
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*One sequence constrains the joint origin of beliefs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Consistency 不是“每个路径外信息集都各自找到某个 tremble”。必须存在一列完全混合策略组合 $\{\sigma^m\}$，它同时生成全部 $\{\mu^m(\cdot\mid I)\}_I$。一个信息集上的相对 tremble 速度会与其他信息集共享同一策略结构，因此某些分别看来可合理化的 beliefs 不能共同组成 consistent assessment。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consistency does not mean finding an unrelated tremble for each off-path information set. One sequence of completely mixed strategy profiles must generate all belief systems simultaneously. Relative tremble rates across information sets belong to the same strategy structure, so beliefs that are separately rationalizable need not be jointly consistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Consistency 限制的是整套 beliefs 的共同来源，却不是唯一性定理；不同 tremble 速度为何仍可能留下多份 consistent 离轨信念，见 [[序贯信念不唯一]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consistency restricts the joint origin of the belief system, but it is not a uniqueness theorem. See [[序贯信念不唯一|non-uniqueness of sequential beliefs]] for how different relative tremble rates can still support multiple consistent off-path beliefs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两个路径外信息集上的 beliefs 各自都能由某个完全混合 perturbation 产生，但找不到同一策略序列同时产生二者。该 assessment 是否 consistent？
>
> **答案：** 不是。Kreps–Wilson consistency 要求同一序列 $\sigma^m$ 在所有信息集共同生成 beliefs。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check.** Beliefs at two off-path information sets can each be generated by some completely mixed perturbation, but no single strategy sequence generates both. Is the assessment consistent?
>
> **Answer:** No. Kreps–Wilson consistency requires one sequence $\sigma^m$ to generate beliefs jointly at every information set.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
