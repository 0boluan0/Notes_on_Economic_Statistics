# Why a fixed grid can miss a root
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*固定网格为什么会漏根*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A root can sit between two sampled numbers, with every acceptable approximation also lying between them. **Grid miss** is this failure of coverage: the search finishes checking its chosen points without ever entering the region it would accept. First read [[固定步长求根|fixed-step root search]] for how the points are chosen, and [[求根残差|residual]] for what acceptance measures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个根可能落在两个采样点之间，而所有满足容差的近似点也恰好挤在这两点之间。**网格漏根**就是这种覆盖失败：搜索确实检查完了所选的点，却从未进入可以接受的区域。候选如何生成见 [[固定步长求根]]；验收衡量什么见 [[求根残差]]。

> [!warning] Existence is not coverage · 存在不等于覆盖
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a fixed-step grid $G_h$ and residual-acceptance region $A_\tau=\{x\in[a,b]:|f(x)|\le\tau\}$, the scan succeeds exactly when $G_h\cap A_\tau\ne\varnothing$. A root in the interval makes $A_\tau$ nonempty but does not force the chosen grid to intersect it. Exhausting the grid without a hit therefore does not imply that the interval contains no root.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对固定步长网格 $G_h$ 和残差接受区域 $A_\tau=\{x\in[a,b]:|f(x)|\le\tau\}$，扫描成功的精确条件是 $G_h\cap A_\tau\ne\varnothing$。区间里存在根只保证 $A_\tau$ 非空，并不保证预先选定的网格与它相交；因此扫描完所有网格点仍未命中，不能推出区间里没有根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $[a,b]$ is a finite closed interval with $a\le b$, $h>0$, and $\tau>0$; grid indices are integers. “Succeeds exactly when” describes a complete scan with exact, terminating residual decisions. It is not a claim that a budget-limited or invalid floating-point run must find an existing acceptable point. The set $A_\tau$ consists of acceptable **inputs**; for a general function it need not be one interval or describe distance to a root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $[a,b]$ 是有限闭区间，$a\le b$、$h>0$、$\tau>0$，网格下标取整数。“成功的精确条件”描述的是残差判定准确且能终止的完整扫描，不保证提前耗尽预算或求值失效的浮点程序也一定找到合格点。集合 $A_\tau$ 收集的是合格的**输入位置**；对一般函数，它未必是一个区间，也不直接表示离根多近。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A nonempty acceptance region can lie between samples
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 非空接受区域不推出网格命中

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $x^*\in[a,b]$ is a root, then $x^*\in A_\tau$ for every $\tau>0$. But [[固定步长求根|fixed-step root search]] inspects only $G_h=\{a+jh:0\le j\le\lfloor(b-a)/h\rfloor\}$, not every real point in the interval. Thus $A_\tau\ne\varnothing$ does not imply $G_h\cap A_\tau\ne\varnothing$. Tightening $\tau$ shrinks the acceptance region without changing the grid, so acceptance precision and sampling coverage are distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $x^*\in[a,b]$ 且 $f(x^*)=0$，那么对任何 $\tau>0$ 都有 $x^*\in A_\tau$。但 [[固定步长求根]] 只检查

$$
G_h
=
\left\{a+jh:0\le j\le\left\lfloor\frac{b-a}{h}\right\rfloor\right\},
$$

而不是检查 $[a,b]$ 的每个实数。逻辑上，

$$
A_\tau\ne\varnothing
\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad
G_h\cap A_\tau\ne\varnothing.
$$

把 $\tau$ 设得更小只会令 $A_\tau$ 缩小，不会改变 $G_h$。所以“提高验收精度”和“提高采样覆盖”是两件不同的事。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More precisely, if $0<\tau_2\le\tau_1$, then $A_{\tau_2}\subseteq A_{\tau_1}$. With the grid fixed, a miss at $\tau_1$ must remain a miss at $\tau_2$. Stricter acceptance cannot add a sampled point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更精确地说，若 $0<\tau_2\le\tau_1$，则 $A_{\tau_2}\subseteq A_{\tau_1}$。保持网格不变时，在 $\tau_1$ 下已经漏掉的区域，换成 $\tau_2$ 后仍会漏掉；收紧验收条件不会新增采样点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A root exists, but every grid point fails
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## $x^2-2$ 给出可核算的漏根反例

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2-2$ on $[0,2]$ with $h=0.1$ and $\tau=0.001$, the positive root is $\sqrt2\approx1.41421$. The nearby grid points $1.4$ and $1.5$ have residuals $0.04$ and $0.25$. Yet $A_{0.001}=[\sqrt{1.999},\sqrt{2.001}]\approx[1.41386,1.41457]$ lies strictly between them. A root exists and the finite scan terminates normally, but no candidate passes. The counterexample refutes a coverage guarantee for the fixed grid, not the meaning of residual itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令

$$
f(x)=x^2-2,
\qquad [a,b]=[0,2],
\qquad h=0.1,
\qquad \tau=0.001.
$$

区间内有正根 $\sqrt2\approx1.41421$。网格在它附近只检查 $1.4$ 和 $1.5$，两点残差分别为

$$
|f(1.4)|=0.04,
\qquad
|f(1.5)|=0.25,
$$

都大于 $0.001$。在非负区间内，接受区域是

$$
A_{0.001}
=
\left[\sqrt{1.999},\sqrt{2.001}\right]
\approx[1.41386,1.41457],
$$

它完全位于相邻网格点 $1.4$ 与 $1.5$ 之间。因此根存在、程序也能正常耗尽有限网格，但没有任何候选通过验收。这个反例否定的是固定网格的覆盖保证，不是否定残差本身的意义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why do the two neighbouring checks rule out all the other grid points? On $[0,2]$, $x^2$ increases with $x$. Every grid point at or below $1.4$ therefore has residual at least $0.04$; every grid point at or above $1.5$ has residual at least $0.25$. There is no grid point between them. Equivalently, solving $|x^2-2|\le0.001$ gives $1.999\le x^2\le2.001$, and taking nonnegative square roots gives exactly the narrow interval above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么只核算相邻两点，就能排除其余全部网格点？在 $[0,2]$ 上，$x^2$ 随 $x$ 增大；所有不超过 $1.4$ 的网格点，残差至少是 $0.04$，所有不小于 $1.5$ 的网格点，残差至少是 $0.25$，中间又没有别的网格点。等价地，解 $|x^2-2|\le0.001$ 得到 $1.999\le x^2\le2.001$，再取非负平方根，就恰好得到上面的窄区间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What a smaller step can and cannot fix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 加密网格需要怎样的覆盖条件

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Halving $h$ while keeping $a$ and $b$ fixed produces a nested exact grid: every old point $a+jh$ becomes the new point with index $2j$. Thus $G_h\subseteq G_{h/2}$, and an existing hit is retained. An arbitrary smaller step need not retain the old marks. For $f(x)=x-0.5$ on $[0,1]$ with $\tau=0.05$, the grid with $h=0.5$ hits the root, but $h=0.3$ gives only $0,0.3,0.6,0.9$; its smallest residual is $0.1$, so it misses. Changing the starting anchor can likewise move every useful mark.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保持 $a,b$ 不变并把 $h$ 减半，会得到嵌套的精确网格：旧点 $a+jh$ 成为新网格下标 $2j$ 的点，所以 $G_h\subseteq G_{h/2}$，已有的命中得以保留。但任意改成一个较小步长，未必保留旧刻度。例如在 $[0,1]$ 上令 $f(x)=x-0.5$、$\tau=0.05$：$h=0.5$ 正好命中根；改用 $h=0.3$ 后，只有 $0,0.3,0.6,0.9$，最小残差是 $0.1$，反而漏根。改变起始锚点同样可能移走原本有用的刻度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fixed grid can fail even for a continuous function, as the counterexample above shows. [[连续函数网格命中|Continuity at a root guarantees that sufficiently fine grids will hit]] for each fixed positive residual tolerance; its proof explains what “sufficiently fine” means. If a derivative bound is available, [[导数界控制网格步长|a derivative-based step condition]] gives a numerical sufficient choice and explains why the truncated right-hand tail matters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的反例说明，连续函数也可能被某个固定网格漏掉。[[连续函数网格命中|根处连续可保证固定正残差容差下足够细网格最终命中]]，完整证明说明了“足够细”的含义。若还掌握导数上界，[[导数界控制网格步长]]给出可计算的充分步长条件，并解释右端截断尾段为何不能忽略。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the failure state and improve the coverage argument
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败状态应保留，修复要改变覆盖论证

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After grid exhaustion, the justified conclusion is only that no point in the current $G_h$ satisfies $|f(x)|\le\tau$. A repair must change the coverage argument: derive a quantitative relation between $h$ and $\tau$ from regularity information, switch to [[二分求根|bisection]] when continuity and a sign-changing bracket are available, or preserve an explicit `no_grid_hit` status instead of presenting the final guess as success.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

网格耗尽后，正确结论是“当前 $G_h$ 内没有点满足 $|f(x)|\le\tau$”。不能把它升级成“$[a,b]$ 内无根”。可选的下一步取决于额外信息：

- 若能从函数的连续性或变化率得到 $h$ 与 $\tau$ 的定量覆盖条件，可以据此重设步长；
- 若函数连续且已有端点异号，应改用 [[二分求根]] 保存根存在的括区间证书；
- 若没有覆盖证书，就保留 `no_grid_hit` 之类的失败状态，而不是返回最后一个猜测冒充成功。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A complete valid scan and a trustworthy residual test remain necessary for interpreting the result. Implementation failure or an early work limit must be distinguished from grid exhaustion; see [[固定步长求根|the fixed-step search contract]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解释扫描结果时，仍须确认扫描完整、判定有效。实现失效或提前达到工作上限，要与网格耗尽分开；具体合同见 [[固定步长求根]]。

> [!question]- Tightening the tolerance · 收紧容差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does reducing $\tau$ from $0.01$ to $0.001$ at fixed $h$ not automatically repair a missed root?
>
> **Answer:** The grid is unchanged while the acceptance region becomes smaller. A stricter tolerance creates no new candidate points; repairing coverage requires changing the grid or using a method with a different certificate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定 $h$ 后把 $\tau$ 从 $0.01$ 缩到 $0.001$，为什么不会自动修复漏根？
>
> **答案：** 网格 $G_h$ 完全没变，接受区域 $A_\tau$ 反而缩小；原本没有网格点命中的窄区域不会因容差更严格而获得新候选。要修复覆盖问题，必须改变网格或换用带有其他证书的方法。
