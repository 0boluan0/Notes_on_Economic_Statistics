# 固定步长扫描即使搜索区间内存在根也可能完全错过残差接受区域
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A fixed-step scan can miss the entire residual-acceptance region even when its search interval contains a root*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 失效边界
> 对固定步长网格 $G_h$ 和残差接受区域 $A_\tau=\{x\in[a,b]:|f(x)|\le\tau\}$，扫描成功的精确条件是 $G_h\cap A_\tau\ne\varnothing$。区间里存在根只保证 $A_\tau$ 非空，并不保证预先选定的网格与它相交；因此扫描完所有网格点仍未命中，不能推出区间里没有根。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a fixed-step grid $G_h$ and residual-acceptance region $A_\tau=\{x\in[a,b]:|f(x)|\le\tau\}$, the scan succeeds exactly when $G_h\cap A_\tau\ne\varnothing$. A root in the interval makes $A_\tau$ nonempty but does not force the chosen grid to intersect it. Exhausting the grid without a hit therefore does not imply that the interval contains no root.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 非空接受区域不推出网格命中

若 $x^*\in[a,b]$ 且 $f(x^*)=0$，那么对任何 $\tau>0$ 都有 $x^*\in A_\tau$。但 [[固定步长求根]] 只检查

$$
G_h
=
\left\{a+jh:0\le j\le\left\lfloor\frac{b-a}{h}\right\rfloor\right\},
$$

而不是检查 $[a,b]$ 的每个实数。逻辑上，

$$
A_\tau\ne\varnothing
\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad
G_h\cap A_\tau\ne\varnothing.
$$

把 $\tau$ 设得更小只会令 $A_\tau$ 缩小，不会改变 $G_h$。所以“提高验收精度”和“提高采样覆盖”是两件不同的事。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $x^*\in[a,b]$ is a root, then $x^*\in A_\tau$ for every $\tau>0$. But [[固定步长求根|fixed-step root search]] inspects only $G_h=\{a+jh:0\le j\le\lfloor(b-a)/h\rfloor\}$, not every real point in the interval. Thus $A_\tau\ne\varnothing$ does not imply $G_h\cap A_\tau\ne\varnothing$. Tightening $\tau$ shrinks the acceptance region without changing the grid, so acceptance precision and sampling coverage are distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## $x^2-2$ 给出可核算的漏根反例

令

$$
f(x)=x^2-2,
\qquad [a,b]=[0,2],
\qquad h=0.1,
\qquad \tau=0.001.
$$

区间内有正根 $\sqrt2\approx1.41421$。网格在它附近只检查 $1.4$ 和 $1.5$，两点残差分别为

$$
|f(1.4)|=0.04,
\qquad
|f(1.5)|=0.25,
$$

都大于 $0.001$。在非负区间内，接受区域是

$$
A_{0.001}
=
\left[\sqrt{1.999},\sqrt{2.001}\right]
\approx[1.41386,1.41457],
$$

它完全位于相邻网格点 $1.4$ 与 $1.5$ 之间。因此根存在、程序也能正常耗尽有限网格，但没有任何候选通过验收。这个反例否定的是固定网格的覆盖保证，不是否定残差本身的意义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2-2$ on $[0,2]$ with $h=0.1$ and $\tau=0.001$, the positive root is $\sqrt2\approx1.41421$. The nearby grid points $1.4$ and $1.5$ have residuals $0.04$ and $0.25$. Yet $A_{0.001}=[\sqrt{1.999},\sqrt{2.001}]\approx[1.41386,1.41457]$ lies strictly between them. A root exists and the finite scan terminates normally, but no candidate passes. The counterexample refutes a coverage guarantee for the fixed grid, not the meaning of residual itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败状态应保留，修复要改变覆盖论证

网格耗尽后，正确结论是“当前 $G_h$ 内没有点满足 $|f(x)|\le\tau$”。不能把它升级成“$[a,b]$ 内无根”。可选的下一步取决于额外信息：

- 若能从函数的连续性或变化率得到 $h$ 与 $\tau$ 的定量覆盖条件，可以据此重设步长；
- 若函数连续且已有端点异号，应改用 [[二分求根]] 保存根存在的括区间证书；
- 若没有覆盖证书，就保留 `no_grid_hit` 之类的失败状态，而不是返回最后一个猜测冒充成功。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After grid exhaustion, the justified conclusion is only that no point in the current $G_h$ satisfies $|f(x)|\le\tau$. A repair must change the coverage argument: derive a quantitative relation between $h$ and $\tau$ from regularity information, switch to [[二分求根|bisection]] when continuity and a sign-changing bracket are available, or preserve an explicit `no_grid_hit` status instead of presenting the final guess as success.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 固定 $h$ 后把 $\tau$ 从 $0.01$ 缩到 $0.001$，为什么不会自动修复漏根？
>
> **答案：** 网格 $G_h$ 完全没变，接受区域 $A_\tau$ 反而缩小；原本没有网格点命中的窄区域不会因容差更严格而获得新候选。要修复覆盖问题，必须改变网格或换用带有其他证书的方法。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why does reducing $\tau$ from $0.01$ to $0.001$ at fixed $h$ not automatically repair a missed root?
>
> **Answer:** The grid is unchanged while the acceptance region becomes smaller. A stricter tolerance creates no new candidate points; repairing coverage requires changing the grid or using a method with a different certificate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
