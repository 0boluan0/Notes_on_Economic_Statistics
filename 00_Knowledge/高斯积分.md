<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# The Gaussian integral
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*高斯积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The graph of $e^{-x^2}$ is a bell: it has height $1$ at zero and becomes small on either side. The Gaussian integral asks for **all the area under this bell over the real line**. Since the interval is infinite, this is an [[反常积分|improper integral]]: we must first show that each tail approaches a finite area. The answer also supplies the constant used to normalise a normal probability density.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$e^{-x^2}$ 的图像像一口钟：零点高度为 $1$，向两侧逐渐变小。高斯积分问的是：**整条实轴上，这口钟下面一共有多少面积？**区间无限，所以这是[[反常积分]]，须先证明每侧尾部的面积都有有限极限。它的答案还给出了正态概率密度所需的归一化常数。

> [!important] Total and half-line area · 全线与半线面积
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both one-sided integrals converge, and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两侧积分分别收敛，并且
>
> $$\boxed{\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-x^2}\,dx=\sqrt\pi},
> \qquad \int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx=\frac{\sqrt\pi}{2}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The half-line value follows from the symmetry $e^{-(-x)^2}=e^{-x^2}$. The total is approximately $1.77245$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 半线值来自对称性 $e^{-(-x)^2}=e^{-x^2}$；全线总量约为 $1.77245$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The integrand has [[无初等原函数的积分|no elementary antiderivative]], but that does not prevent an exact total area. The useful idea is to turn the **square of the desired area into a volume**. A volume with circular symmetry can then be calculated by shells. We work on finite regions first, so the argument establishes convergence as well as the value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

被积函数[[无初等原函数的积分|没有初等原函数]]，却仍可能精确算出总面积。关键想法是把**所求面积的平方变成一个体积**；这个体积具有圆对称性，可以用壳层求出。先在有限区域运算，证明就能同时给出收敛性与积分值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Squeezing between finite squares and disks
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 有限方形与圆盘夹逼

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a finite $R>0$ and write $Q_R=\int_{-R}^R e^{-x^2}\,dx$. This integral exists by [[连续函数可积|continuity on the closed interval]]. Now imagine the surface $z=e^{-(x^2+y^2)}$ above the square $B_R=[-R,R]\times[-R,R]$. Here $x$ and $y$ are independent horizontal coordinates, and $z$ is height; choosing $y$ does not determine $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定有限的 $R>0$，记 $Q_R=\int_{-R}^R e^{-x^2}\,dx$。由[[连续函数可积|闭区间上的连续性]]，这个积分存在。现在想象方形 $B_R=[-R,R]\times[-R,R]$ 上方的曲面 $z=e^{-(x^2+y^2)}$。$x,y$ 是两个独立的水平坐标，$z$ 是高度；选定 $y$ 并不决定 $x$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a fixed $y$, a vertical slice has height $e^{-y^2}e^{-x^2}$. The factor $e^{-y^2}$ is constant while $x$ moves across the slice, so its area is $e^{-y^2}Q_R$. Adding these areas through $y$ by [[切片体积|volume by slices]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $y$ 后，竖直切片的高度是 $e^{-y^2}e^{-x^2}$。当 $x$ 横穿切片时，$e^{-y^2}$ 是常数，因此切片面积为 $e^{-y^2}Q_R$。再沿 $y$ 用[[切片体积|截面积乘厚度累加]]，得到

$$\iint_{B_R}e^{-(x^2+y^2)}\,dx\,dy
=\int_{-R}^R e^{-y^2}\!\left(\int_{-R}^R e^{-x^2}\,dx\right)dy
=Q_R\int_{-R}^R e^{-y^2}\,dy=Q_R^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The double-integral symbol $\iint_{B_R}$ means “add height times small base area over the square”; the next expression calculates that volume one coordinate at a time. Continuity on this finite rectangle justifies the calculation. The same curve is integrated twice, once in each independent coordinate, which is why the result is a square.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重积分符号 $\iint_{B_R}$ 的意思是“在方形内把高度乘小块底面积累加”；后面的式子按两个坐标逐次算出这个体积。在有限矩形上的连续性保证这一步成立。同一条曲线分别沿两个独立坐标积分，所以结果是平方。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $D_R$ be the disk centred at the origin with radius $R$. Above a circle of radius $r$, every point of the surface has the same height $e^{-r^2}$. A thin [[圆柱壳法|cylindrical shell]] therefore has radius $r$, circumference $2\pi r$, height $e^{-r^2}$ and thickness $dr$. The volume above the disk is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $D_R$ 是以原点为中心、半径为 $R$ 的圆盘。距离原点为 $r$ 的圆周上，曲面高度处处等于 $e^{-r^2}$。因此一个薄[[圆柱壳法|圆柱壳]]的半径是 $r$、周长是 $2\pi r$、高度是 $e^{-r^2}$、厚度是 $dr$。圆盘上方的体积为

$$V_R=\iint_{D_R}e^{-(x^2+y^2)}\,dx\,dy
=\int_0^R2\pi r e^{-r^2}\,dr
=\pi(1-e^{-R^2}).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last step uses $(e^{-r^2})'=-2re^{-r^2}$: the shell circumference supplies exactly the factor needed for an elementary integral. The geometry now connects this easy volume to the square volume. The radius-$R$ disk lies inside the square, whose farthest corners are at distance $\sqrt2R$. Hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一步利用 $(e^{-r^2})'=-2re^{-r^2}$：壳的周长恰好提供了完成初等积分所需的因子。接下来用几何关系把这个易算的体积与方形体积联系起来。半径 $R$ 的圆盘位于方形内，而方形最远的角距原点为 $\sqrt2R$，所以

$$D_R\subset B_R\subset D_{\sqrt2R}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All heights are nonnegative. Enlarging the base therefore adds volume, so the same reasoning as [[积分保序性|integral comparison]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有高度非负，扩大底面只会增加体积，因此按[[积分保序性|积分比较]]的同一思路，有

$$\pi(1-e^{-R^2})\le Q_R^2\le\pi(1-e^{-2R^2}).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both outer expressions tend to $\pi$ as $R\to\infty$. The [[夹逼定理|squeeze theorem]] gives $Q_R^2\to\pi$; since $Q_R>0$, we take the positive square root and get $Q_R\to\sqrt\pi$. Evenness also gives $\int_0^R e^{-x^2}\,dx=Q_R/2$. Thus the positive and negative one-sided integrals each have finite limit $\sqrt\pi/2$, and adding them proves the claimed ordinary improper integral.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $R\to\infty$，两端都趋于 $\pi$。由[[夹逼定理]]，$Q_R^2\to\pi$；又因 $Q_R>0$，取正平方根得到 $Q_R\to\sqrt\pi$。偶性还给出 $\int_0^R e^{-x^2}\,dx=Q_R/2$，因此正、负两侧的积分各有有限极限 $\sqrt\pi/2$。相加后，才得到所说的通常意义下的反常积分。

> [!warning] Check each tail · 分别检查两侧尾部
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A symmetric limit alone is not enough for a general signed integrand: $\int_{-R}^R x\,dx=0$ for every $R$, but neither one-sided integral is finite. The Gaussian proof succeeds because it also establishes the two separate half-line limits.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对一般带符号函数，仅有对称极限还不够：每个 $R$ 都有 $\int_{-R}^R x\,dx=0$，但两侧积分都不是有限值。高斯证明成立，是因为它还建立了两个各自存在的半线极限。

## Deriving the normalising constant
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 归一化常数由积分值推出

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a positive real $\alpha$, stretching the horizontal coordinate by $u=\sqrt\alpha x$ makes the exponent $-u^2$. The [[定积分换元|substitution]] gives $dx=du/\sqrt\alpha$. Apply it first on finite intervals, then take the convergent limits:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正实数 $\alpha$，以 $u=\sqrt\alpha x$ 改变横向尺度，指数就变成 $-u^2$。由[[定积分换元]]，$dx=du/\sqrt\alpha$。先对有限区间换元，再取已经收敛的极限：

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-\alpha x^2}\,dx
=\frac{1}{\sqrt\alpha}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}e^{-u^2}\,du
=\sqrt{\frac\pi\alpha},\qquad \alpha>0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In particular, $\alpha=1/2$ gives total $\sqrt{2\pi}$. A [[概率密度函数|probability density]] must be nonnegative and have total integral $1$. Consequently
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特别地，$\alpha=1/2$ 时总积分为 $\sqrt{2\pi}$。[[概率密度函数]]必须非负且总积分为 $1$，因此

$$\phi(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-x^2/2}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
is correctly normalised. We derived the constant from the integral; we did not assume normalisation to prove the integral. Positivity of $\alpha$ matters: at $\alpha=0$ the integrand is $1$, and at $\alpha<0$ it grows, so neither case has a finite full-line integral.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具有正确的归一化常数。这里从积分推出常数，没有先假设概率归一化再证明积分。$\alpha>0$ 很关键：$\alpha=0$ 时被积函数恒为 $1$，$\alpha<0$ 时反而增长，这两种情况在全实轴上的积分都不是有限值。

> [!example]- Normalise a narrower bell · 给更窄的钟形曲线归一化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To make $c e^{-4x^2}$ a density on the real line, its integral must be $c\sqrt\pi/2=1$, so $c=2/\sqrt\pi$. The bell is narrower than $e^{-x^2}$ and therefore needs a larger vertical multiplier to keep total area $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 要让 $c e^{-4x^2}$ 成为实轴上的密度，须有 $c\sqrt\pi/2=1$，所以 $c=2/\sqrt\pi$。它比 $e^{-x^2}$ 更窄，为保持总面积 $1$，需要更大的竖直倍数。

For this nonnegative continuous function on the plane, it allows either order of integration even before finiteness is known. The finite-region proof above does not require this alternative; it uses continuity on bounded rectangles and nonnegative volume comparison.
