# 固定规模融资买入的权益收益率等于资产损益扣除借款成本后除以初始权益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For a fixed financed purchase, equity return divides asset gains net of borrowing costs by initial equity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑一项初始证券资产价值 $V_0>0$、初始自有权益比例 $0<m<1$ 的[[融资买入]]。投资者投入 $E_0=mV_0$，借款 $D_0=(1-m)V_0$。假设持有期间不改变规模、无追保或提款等外部现金流，资产总回报 $r_A$ 与借款利率 $r_L$ 都是同一持有期间的有效回报率，且暂不计其他费用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a [[融资买入|margin-financed purchase]] with initial asset value $V_0>0$ and equity fraction $0<m<1$. Own funds are $E_0=mV_0$ and debt is $D_0=(1-m)V_0$. Assume a fixed position, no external cash flows such as margin deposits or withdrawals, and no other fees. Asset total return $r_A$ and borrowing rate $r_L$ are effective rates over the same holding period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将资产及分配计到期末，剩余权益为 $E_1=V_0(1+r_A)-D_0(1+r_L)$，因此
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Valuing the asset and distributions at the end gives $E_1=V_0(1+r_A)-D_0(1+r_L)$, hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
r_E=\frac{E_1-E_0}{E_0}
=\frac{r_A-(1-m)r_L}{m},
\qquad
r_E-r_A=\frac{1-m}{m}(r_A-r_L).
$$

所以，杠杆提高相对于无借款资产回报的权益回报，当且仅当此模型中 $r_A>r_L$；不是资产价格一上涨，融资就一定更合算。其他费用若按期末值计为 $F$，还须从 $r_E$ 扣 $F/E_0$，并确保没有在 $r_A$ 或 $r_L$ 中重复计入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus leverage raises equity return relative to the unlevered asset return if and only if $r_A>r_L$ in this model. A price increase alone is insufficient. Other costs valued at the end as $F$ reduce $r_E$ by $F/E_0$, without double counting costs already included in $r_A$ or $r_L$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$V_0=10,000,m=0.4,r_L=5\%$。资产总回报为 $10\%$ 时，权益期末为 $11,000-6,300=4,700$，回报 $17.5\%$；资产回报为 $-10\%$ 时，权益为 $9,000-6,300=2,700$，回报 $-32.5\%$。贷款成本让上下两种结果并不以零对称。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $V_0=10,000,m=0.4,r_L=5\%$, an asset return of $10\%$ leaves $11,000-6,300=4,700$ of equity, a $17.5\%$ return. An asset return of $-10\%$ leaves $9,000-6,300=2,700$, a $-32.5\%$ return. Financing costs make these outcomes asymmetric around zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是按给定持仓存续到期的现金流恒等式，不保证账户能承受中途价格路径。若触发[[证券账户维持保证金|维持保证金要求]]后补款或平仓，应按实际现金流和持仓重新计算，不能只把最终价格代入原式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This identity assumes the specified position survives to the end; it does not guarantee sufficient resources along the intervening path. Deposits or liquidation following [[证券账户维持保证金|maintenance requirements]] require actual cash-flow and position accounting rather than substitution of the final price into the unchanged model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
