# D1 的类型删除结论取决于集合包含口径，弱包含与严格包含只在两个回应集合恰好相等时发生分歧
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*D1's type-elimination conclusion depends on the set-inclusion convention; weak and strict inclusion differ only when the two response sets are equal*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 先固定定义，再做集合比较
> 令 $W_\theta=D_\theta(s,\sigma)\cup D_\theta^0(s,\sigma)$ 表示使类型 $\theta$ 弱愿意偏离的理性接收者混合回应，令 $S_{\theta'}=D_{\theta'}(s,\sigma)$ 表示使另一类型 $\theta'$ 严格愿意偏离的回应。不同来源对“何时删除 $\theta$”采用了两种相邻但不相同的包含关系。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $W_\theta=D_\theta(s,\sigma)\cup D_\theta^0(s,\sigma)$ collect rational receiver mixed responses under which type $\theta$ weakly deviates, and let $S_{\theta'}=D_{\theta'}(s,\sigma)$ collect responses under which another type $\theta'$ strictly deviates. Sources use two closely related inclusion conventions for deciding when to eliminate $\theta$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

MIT 2024 Lecture 3 使用弱包含：

$$
W_\theta\subseteq S_{\theta'}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{从离轨 posterior 支持中删除 }\theta.
$$

MIT 2016 notes 使用严格包含：

$$
W_\theta\subsetneq S_{\theta'}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{删除 }\theta.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
MIT's 2024 Lecture 3 uses weak inclusion, whereas the 2016 MIT notes use proper inclusion. Both compare the same weak-deviation set for $\theta$ with the strict-deviation set for $\theta'$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $W_\theta$ 真的是 $S_{\theta'}$ 的真子集，两种口径都删除 $\theta$；若 $W_\theta$ 不是子集，两种口径都不凭这对类型删除它。唯一的分歧是

$$
W_\theta=S_{\theta'}.
$$

此时弱包含成立而严格包含不成立，所以前者删除 $\theta$，后者不删除。应用 [[D1准则]] 时必须沿用同一来源对删除集合、保留集合与正式通过条件的整套定义，不能只把 $\subseteq$ 与 $\subsetneq$ 互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both conventions eliminate $\theta$ under proper inclusion and neither does so when inclusion fails. They differ only under equality: weak inclusion eliminates $\theta$, while proper inclusion does not. An application must keep one source's elimination rule, survivor set, and passing condition together rather than swapping a symbol in isolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检
> 若 $W_\theta=S_{\theta'}=\{\alpha_1,\alpha_2\}$，两种 D1 口径的删除结论是否相同？
>
> **答案：** 不同。弱包含口径删除 $\theta$；严格包含口径因为没有真包含而不删除 $\theta$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. Weak inclusion eliminates $\theta$, whereas proper inclusion does not because equality is not a proper subset relation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
