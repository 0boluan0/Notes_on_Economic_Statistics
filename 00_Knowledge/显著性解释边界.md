# Statistical significance and practical importance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*统计显著性与实际重要性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Statistically significant” describes the outcome of a specified statistical test. “Practically important” asks whether the magnitude matters for a stated decision, in meaningful units. A very precisely measured tiny effect can pass a significance threshold; an imprecise estimate can fail that threshold while leaving important effects unresolved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“统计显著”描述一个指定统计检验的结果；“实际重要”则问：这个量级放到明确的决策和有意义的单位里，是否值得重视。测得很精确的微小效应可以跨过显著性门槛；估得不精确的效应即使没有跨过门槛，重要的量级仍可能没有被排除。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[p值条件解释|p-value]] measures a tail position under a specified null model; it is not a magnitude scale or a probability that the null is true. For a zero-effect normal reference, the statistic $z=\hat\theta/s$ divides the estimate $\hat\theta$ by its [[标准误含义|standard error]] $s>0$. A large ratio can arise from a large numerator, a small denominator, or both. To judge magnitude, retain the measurement units and a [[置信区间覆盖率|confidence interval]], whose interpretation depends on the validity of its coverage procedure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[p值条件解释|p 值]]衡量结果在指定零假设模型下的尾部位置，不是效应大小刻度，也不是零假设为真的概率。零效应的正态参考统计量 $z=\hat\theta/s$，把估计值 $\hat\theta$ 除以正的[[标准误含义|标准误]] $s$。比值大，可以来自分子大、分母小，或两者兼有。判断量级时，要保留测量单位和[[置信区间覆盖率|置信区间]]；区间解释仍依赖覆盖程序的有效性。

> [!example] The same significance label hides different questions · 星号背后的问题不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose a normal approximation is justified, use a two-sided 5% zero-effect test, and form an approximate 95% interval $\hat\theta\pm1.96s$. Two studies give:
>
> | Study | Estimate | SE | $z$ | Approximate 95% interval |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | A | $0.001$ | $0.0001$ | $10$ | $[0.000804,0.001196]$ |
> | B | $0.20$ | $0.15$ | $1.33$ | $[-0.094,0.494]$ |
>
> A rejects zero very clearly. B does not ($p\approx0.182$), and its interval includes both zero and a positive effect of $0.40$. If, **before this analysis**, the relevant decision treats effects between $-0.01$ and $0.01$ units as negligible, A's entire interval lies inside that band; B remains too imprecise to exclude important effects. The band is a stated decision assumption, not a universal statistical cutoff.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设正态近似有依据，使用双侧 5% 零效应检验，并构造近似 95% 区间 $\hat\theta\pm1.96s$。两项研究的结果可完整算出：A 的估计值 $0.001$、标准误 $0.0001$，得到 $z=10$ 与区间 $[0.000804,0.001196]$；B 的估计值 $0.20$、标准误 $0.15$，得到 $z\approx1.33$ 与区间 $[-0.094,0.494]$。
>
> A 明确拒绝零。B 不拒绝（$p\approx0.182$），但区间同时包含零与 $0.40$ 这样的正效应。如果**分析前**已经说明，对当前决策而言 $-0.01$ 到 $0.01$ 单位都可忽略，那么 A 的整个区间位于这个范围内；B 仍不够精确，无法排除重要效应。这个范围是明示的决策假设，不是统计学通用门槛。

## Error control and the chance of detection
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 大小与功效回答不同问题

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A valid level-$\alpha$ test limits false rejection under the null; it does not promise reliable detection at every alternative. [[检验功效|Power]] is the probability of rejecting under a specified true alternative value and design. It changes with the true effect, noise, sample size, dependence and rejection rule. Thus a test can control false positives while often missing an effect large enough to matter. A nonsignificant result alone does not tell us whether this was a near-zero effect, an unfortunate sample, or an insufficiently informative design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有效的水平 $\alpha$ 检验控制零假设下的错误拒绝，却没有承诺对每种备择都能可靠发现。[[检验功效|功效]]是在指定真实备择值和设计下拒绝的概率，随真实效应、噪声、样本量、依赖和拒绝规则变化。因此，一个检验可以控制假阳性，却仍常常漏掉值得关心的效应。仅凭不显著，无法判断究竟是效应接近零、这次样本不走运，还是设计所含信息不足。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Showing an effect is small is a different question
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*要说明效应很小，需要换一个问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To support practical equivalence, prespecify meaningful lower and upper bounds and use a procedure calibrated for that question. In the usual two-one-sided-tests (TOST) construction, let $\theta$ be the true effect and let $a<b$ be the prespecified bounds. Test the two one-sided null hypotheses $\theta\le a$ and $\theta\ge b$, each at level $\alpha$; both must reject to support an effect inside $(a,b)$. Under the corresponding continuous equal-tailed interval construction, this matches a $100(1-2\alpha)\%$ interval lying strictly inside the bounds. At $\alpha=0.05$, that interval is **90%**, not 95%. Inclusion of zero alone establishes none of this. A narrower “negligible” claim is still tied to those particular bounds, population, outcome and model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要支持实际等效，先指定有实质意义的上下界，再使用针对该问题校准的程序。通常的双单侧检验（TOST）令 $\theta$ 表示真实效应，$a<b$ 表示事先指定的界。分别检验单侧零假设 $\theta\le a$ 和 $\theta\ge b$，每个采用水平 $\alpha$；两者都拒绝，才支持效应位于 $(a,b)$ 内。在相应连续、等尾区间的构造下，这对应 $100(1-2\alpha)\%$ 区间严格落在等效界内；$\alpha=0.05$ 时用的是 **90%** 区间，不是 95%。仅仅包含零并不能建立这些结论。“小到可忽略”的判断仍针对这组界、总体、结果变量和模型。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The 95% interval in A is a transparent, stricter illustration that all its reported values fit inside the chosen band; it is not a claim that 95% is the standard TOST interval. Neither a significant zero-null test nor an equivalence finding establishes a causal mechanism. Bias from design or a mismatched target can remain even when sampling uncertainty is tiny.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

A 使用 95% 区间，是更严格而直观地展示“报告区间内的所有值都位于所选范围”，并不是把 95% 说成标准 TOST 区间。拒绝零效应和发现实际等效，都不能单独建立因果机制；即使抽样不确定性很小，设计偏差或目标错配仍可能存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Report what was estimated, its units, the estimate and interval, the prespecified meaningful threshold, and the model/design assumptions. Transformations need care: [[level-log 系数解释|logarithmic regression scales]] change the coefficient's units, while [[取值依赖边际效应|interactions or nonlinear terms]] may require evaluation at a meaningful covariate value. A star beside one coefficient cannot supply those choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

报告估计对象、单位、估计值与区间、事先说明的重要性阈值，以及模型和设计假设。变量变换需要注意：[[level-log 系数解释|对数回归尺度]]改变系数单位，[[取值依赖边际效应|交互项或非线性项]]可能需要在有意义的协变量值处评价。系数旁的星号无法代替这些说明。
