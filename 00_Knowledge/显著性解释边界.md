# 拒绝或未拒绝零假设都不能单独判断效应是否实质重要
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Neither rejecting nor failing to reject a null hypothesis alone determines whether an effect is practically important*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 显著性检验比较数据与一个预先指定的 null hypothesis 是否相容；实际判断则关心效应在有意义单位上有多大。拒绝 $H_0$ 不能把很小的效应变得重要，未拒绝 $H_0$ 也不能把仍与数据相容的重要效应变成零。两种结论都必须回到 estimate、confidence interval、单位、功效和事先给定的实质阈值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A significance test asks whether the data are compatible with a prespecified null hypothesis, whereas a substantive judgment asks how large the effect is on a meaningful scale. Rejecting $H_0$ cannot make a tiny effect important, and failing to reject $H_0$ cannot turn an important effect still compatible with the data into zero. Both conclusions must return to the estimate, confidence interval, units, power, and a substantively meaningful threshold chosen in advance.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 大小与功效回答不同问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Size and power answer different questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

显著性水平 $\alpha$ 控制原假设成立时错误拒绝的概率。对具体备择值 $\theta$，功效函数是

$$
\pi(\theta)
=
P_\theta(\text{reject }H_0).
$$

功效不是检验的一个固定形容词；它随真实效应、噪声、设计、样本量和拒绝规则变化。一个检验可以把 Type I error 控制在 5%，同时因为标准误很大而很难发现仍有实质意义的效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The significance level $\alpha$ controls false rejection when the null is true. At a specified alternative $\theta$, power is $\pi(\theta)=P_\theta(\text{reject }H_0)$. Power is not a fixed adjective attached to a test; it changes with the true effect, noise, design, sample size, and rejection rule. A test can control Type I error at 5% yet have little chance of detecting an effect that would matter in practice because its standard error is large.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，$p>\alpha$ 的准确结论只是“当前检验未拒绝这个 null”。若要主张效应小到可忽略，应先给出有实际含义的范围，例如 $[-\delta,\delta]$，再检查区间是否排除了范围外的效应，或使用与这一目标匹配的 equivalence procedure。仅仅让区间包含 0 不足以证明接近 0。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $p>\alpha$ means only that the present test did not reject this null. To argue that an effect is negligible, first define a substantively meaningful range such as $[-\delta,\delta]$, then examine whether the interval excludes effects outside that range or use an equivalence procedure designed for that goal. Merely having an interval that includes zero does not establish proximity to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，大样本可以让很小的偏离得到很小的 p 值。拒绝零效应后，仍需报告原单位或可解释变换下的 estimate 与 interval。例如 log-level coefficient 的百分比换算见 [[level-log 系数解释]]，二次项与交互项在有意义取值处的组合见 [[取值依赖边际效应]]。统计显著也不会自动提供因果识别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, a large sample can make a tiny departure produce a small p-value. After rejecting a zero effect, report the estimate and interval on the original scale or a meaningful transformed scale. Percentage interpretation for a log-level coefficient is handled in [[level-log 系数解释|the log-level coefficient atom]], and combinations at meaningful covariate values in [[取值依赖边际效应|value-dependent marginal effects]]. Statistical significance also does not automatically provide causal identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 同一个星号可能遮住相反问题
> 研究 A 估计效应为 $0.001$、standard error 为 $0.0001$：零效应很容易被拒绝，但 $0.001$ 是否重要仍取决于单位与阈值。研究 B 估计效应为 $0.20$、standard error 为 $0.15$：零效应可能未被拒绝，但区间仍可能包含决策上重要的正效应。A 面临“显著但可能太小”，B 面临“未显著但仍不够精确”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Study A estimates an effect of $0.001$ with standard error $0.0001$: a zero effect is readily rejected, but whether $0.001$ matters depends on units and a substantive threshold. Study B estimates $0.20$ with standard error $0.15$: zero may not be rejected, yet the interval can still include positive effects large enough to matter. A may be statistically significant but too small; B may be inconclusive rather than evidence of no effect.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若研究 A 得到极小 p 值但区间整体落在决策上可忽略的范围内，研究 B 得到 $p=0.20$ 但区间同时包含零和一个很重要的正效应，分别能否写成“重要效应”和“没有效应”？说明理由。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Study A has a very small p-value but its interval lies entirely inside a range considered negligible for the decision. Study B has $p=0.20$ but its interval contains both zero and an important positive effect. Can A be called an important effect and B no effect? Explain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 都不能。A 的数据可以反对精确零效应，却同时支持“量级很小”；B 的检验没有足够精度区分零与重要正效应，未拒绝不是零效应证据。结论要由区间与预先说明的实际阈值决定，不能由是否跨过 0.05 单独决定。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Neither statement is justified. A can be incompatible with an exactly zero effect while remaining substantively negligible. B lacks enough precision to distinguish zero from an important positive effect; non-rejection is not evidence of zero. The interval and a prespecified substantive threshold, rather than crossing 0.05 alone, determine the interpretation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
