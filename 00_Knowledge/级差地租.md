# 级差地租是土地质量或投入生产率差异形成的土地收益差额

**级差地租**是不同土地，或同一土地上不同次投入，由于生产率不同而形成的土地收益差额。它回答的是：生产同一种粮食、面对同一个市场，为什么有些土地能够留下更多收益？这里比较的是在同等资本和劳动投入下的生产结果，不能只比地块总产量而忽略投入多少。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Differential rent** is the difference in land returns arising from unequal productivity across plots or successive applications of inputs to one plot. It explains why some land yields a larger return when farms produce the same grain for the same market. The comparison holds capital and labour inputs equal; comparing total output alone would not isolate productivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Ricardo 的例子中，同等资本和劳动在三类土地上分别得到 100、90、80 单位粮食的净产出。这里的“净产出”已扣除原例中维持劳动者的部分，尚待在地租和资本收益之间分配。若第二类是可以不付地租而耕作的边际选择，资本在它上面取得 90；第一类多出的 10 就可归地主。否则，经营者会争着租第一类地，直到这项优势反映在租金里。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ricardo considers equal capital and labour yielding net grain outputs of 100, 90 and 80 on three grades of land. His net output is after supporting workers and remains to be divided between rent and the return to capital. If the second grade is available rent-free at the margin, capital earns 90 there. Competition to rent the first grade can then transfer its extra 10 to its owner.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当第三类成为无租边际，同样的比较得到第一类地租 20、第二类 10、第三类 0。注意，优地的生产率没有提高，租金仍可上升：变化的是用来比较的边际生产条件。这个“边际”也可以是旧地上的最后一次追加投入，见 [[土地的集约边际]]，不一定是一块新开垦的土地。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the third grade becomes the rent-free margin, rents are 20, 10 and zero. Better land has not become more productive; the benchmark against which its advantage is measured has changed. That benchmark may also be the last application of inputs to an existing plot, as explained in [[土地的集约边际|the intensive margin of land use]], rather than newly cultivated land.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这套数例依赖资本竞争、相同投入可比较、确有无租边际等条件。若所有在产土地的容量都已用满，连其中最差的一类也可能取得稀缺收益，见 [[边际土地零租条件]]。这种共同的稀缺部分不能全部归为土地间生产率差异产生的级差地租；农场租赁款中包含的建筑、排水等回报还应按 [[土地租金与改良回报]] 区分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example requires competition for capital, comparable inputs and an available rent-free margin. If all active land is at capacity, even its least productive grade can earn scarcity returns; see [[边际土地零租条件|the conditions for a zero-rent margin]]. A common scarcity component is not wholly differential rent arising from productivity differences. Payments for buildings or drainage also require the distinction in [[土地租金与改良回报|land rent and returns to improvements]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ricardo（1821），[《政治经济学及赋税原理》II.4–9](https://www.econlib.org/library/Ricardo/ricP.html?chapter_num=3#2.4)：支持质量差异、同等资本和劳动的比较、100／90／80 净产出例及追加资本边际。差额 10、20、10 已逐项相减核算。
- 共同容量稀缺与级差的区分以 [[边际土地零租条件]] 的明确构造为范围检验，不声称 Ricardo 在这些段落中给出了该现代供给模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chapter II supports the equal-input comparisons, net-output example and intensive margin. The numerical differences have been recomputed. The linked capacity example checks the scope of the differential explanation; it is not attributed to Ricardo as his supply model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
