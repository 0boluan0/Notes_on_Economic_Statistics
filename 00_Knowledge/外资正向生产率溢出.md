# 外资正向生产率溢出是外资存在提高本地企业生产率而收益未被外资完全内部化

外资正向生产率溢出是跨国企业进入或存在，使本地企业生产率提高，而且跨国企业没有完全内部化相应收益的现象。它是[[外部性]]的一种具体形式；外资企业自己的高生产率、外资金额增加或本地销售增加，都不直接构成这一现象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A positive FDI productivity spillover occurs when multinational entry or presence raises domestic firms' productivity and the multinational does not fully internalize the resulting benefits. It is a specific [[外部性|externality]]. High productivity within the foreign firm, a larger investment flow, or greater local sales does not by itself establish such a spillover.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可能通道包括模仿、受训员工流动或客户向供应商提供技术帮助，不要求每次都同时具备全部通道。构造例：一名受训员工转到本地企业，带来一种操作方法，使该企业在相同投入下产出更多合格产品；若原外资企业没有通过合同等方式取得全部新增收益，就可能存在正向溢出。因果作用和收益归属是例子的设定，不是只观察员工流动即可证明的事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Possible channels include imitation, movement of trained workers and technical assistance from customers to suppliers; all channels need not operate simultaneously. In a constructed example, a trained worker joins a domestic firm and introduces a method that produces more quality-adjusted output from the same inputs. A positive spillover may arise if the original multinational does not capture all the additional benefit through contracts or other arrangements. Causation and benefit accrual are assumptions of this example, not facts established merely by observing a worker move.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

测量时须区分生产效率与销售价格：价格上涨造成收入或利润增加，不足以证明技术学习；若使用[[劳动生产率]]，还要说明投入资本、工时和产出质量的可比性。单纯经价格传递的收益另见[[金钱外部性]]；可能的企业间方向见[[外资溢出的传递方向]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Measurement must distinguish productive efficiency from sales prices: higher revenue or profits caused by higher prices do not demonstrate learning. A [[劳动生产率|labour-productivity measure]] also requires attention to capital inputs, hours and output quality. For benefits transmitted solely through prices, see [[金钱外部性|pecuniary externalities]]; for possible interfirm channels, see [[外资溢出的传递方向|directions of FDI spillovers]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Javorcik（2004），Does Foreign Direct Investment Increase the Productivity of Domestic Firms?](https://users.ox.ac.uk/~econ0247/JavorcikAER.pdf)，American Economic Review 94(3)，印刷第605–608页/PDF第1–4页，尤其第607页的定义：生产率提高与未完全内部化、模仿和员工流动；第605–606页支持选择、企业构成变化等替代解释的边界。员工例为独立构造，不是论文估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Javorcik defines positive productivity spillovers and discusses imitation, worker mobility and alternative explanations for observed productivity patterns. The worker example is constructed rather than an empirical estimate from the paper.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
