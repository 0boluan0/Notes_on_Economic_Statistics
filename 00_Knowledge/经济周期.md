# 经济周期是总体经济活动扩张与收缩反复出现但时长不固定的波动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Business cycles are recurring expansions and contractions in aggregate activity with irregular durations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经济周期研究产出、就业、收入和销售等广泛活动的共同波动。经典周期中，峰到谷是收缩，谷到下一峰是扩张；峰与谷是转折点，不是预先分配好长度的时期。“复苏、繁荣、衰退、萧条”是课堂阶段语言，不意味着每次严格重复同样的四段轨迹。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Business-cycle analysis studies broad movements in output, employment, income, and sales. In a classical cycle, contraction runs from peak to trough and expansion from trough to the next peak. Turning points are not fixed-length periods, and classroom phase labels do not imply an identical four-stage path every time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

衰退通常要求广泛且持续的活动下降。“实际 GDP 连续两个季度下降”是常用简则，不是所有国家周期认定的充分必要定义。另有相对趋势或潜能的增长周期，必须与[[产出缺口]]、增长率和经典水平周期分清。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A recession usually involves a broad and persistent decline in activity. Two consecutive quarterly declines in real GDP are a rule of thumb, not a universal necessary-and-sufficient definition. Growth cycles relative to trend or potential must be distinguished from classical level cycles and the [[产出缺口|output gap]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股价会反映未来预期，既不必与当前周期同步，也没有固定领先期。用周期做投资判断还要说明当时已知信息与市场定价，不能把事后峰谷直接当成实时交易信号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equity prices reflect expectations and need neither move simultaneously with current activity nor lead by a fixed interval. Ex-post turning points are not automatically real-time trading signals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
