# 艾略特修正浪是对更大一级趋势作逆向调整的波浪模式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An Elliott corrective wave is a countertrend structure, including its variations, relative to the next larger degree.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

艾略特修正浪是 [[艾略特波浪]] 中相对于更大一级趋势作逆向调整的模式。课程的 $A-B-C$ 是基本图示；“修正”不意味着每个子段都向同一方向走，也不等于任意三段折线都满足某一种修正模式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An Elliott correction is a pattern moving against the next larger degree in the [[艾略特波浪|Elliott framework]]. The course's $A-B-C$ is an introductory illustration. A correction need not move in one direction in every subsegment, and an arbitrary three-segment line need not satisfy a specific corrective pattern.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该学派主要区分锯齿型 zigzag（内部 $5-3-5$）、平台型 flat（$3-3-5$）、三角修正（$3-3-3-3-3$）及组合。括号描述内部细分，不是日数。例如三角修正有五个依次标记的主要分段，各分段按修正模式展开，常简写为“3”；这个记号允许相应变化形式，不是恰好三条终端折线。三角修正并不是五浪驱动，因此不能把“修正通常按三浪模式理解”改成“所有修正都恰好三个主要分段”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The school distinguishes zigzags ($5-3-5$), flats ($3-3-5$), triangles ($3-3-3-3-3$), and combinations. These numbers describe subdivisions, not days. A corrective triangle has five successive major segments, each unfolding in corrective mode, conventionally abbreviated “3.” This permits appropriate variations rather than requiring exactly three terminal segments. A corrective triangle is not a five-wave motive structure, and corrective mode does not require exactly three major segments in every correction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡提供修正类别的入口和基本范围；仅凭分段数并不能完整识别某个具体类型，仍需该类型的价格位置与内部约束。真实数据中的完成时点还可能要等后续走势才能辨认，必须记录在 [[预测时点与信息集]] 中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This entry establishes the corrective category and its scope. Segment counts alone do not fully identify a specific type; price relationships and internal constraints still matter. Completion in real data may be recognisable only later and must respect [[预测时点与信息集|information availability]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
