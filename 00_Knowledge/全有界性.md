# Total boundedness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*全有界性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To describe every point of $[0,1]$ to within an error of $0.1$, a finite grid is enough. For an error of $0.001$, we need a finer grid, but still only finitely many representatives. **Total boundedness** asks for this finite approximation at every positive precision. The number of representatives can increase as the requested error decreases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要把 $[0,1]$ 中每个点描述到误差小于 $0.1$，一个有限网格就够了；误差要求改成 $0.001$ 时，需要更细的网格，但仍只需有限多个代表点。**全有界性**要求每一种正精度下都能做到这样的有限近似。要求越精确，所需代表点的数量可以越多。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $K$ be a subset of a [[度量空间|metric space]] $(X,d)$. Distances on $K$ are inherited from $X$. Write $B_\varepsilon^K(x)=\{y\in K:d(y,x)<\varepsilon\}$ for the relative open ball about a point $x\in K$. It keeps only the points of $K$ within distance $\varepsilon>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $K$ 是[[度量空间]] $(X,d)$ 的子集，使用从 $X$ 继承的距离。记 $B_\varepsilon^K(x)=\{y\in K:d(y,x)<\varepsilon\}$，其中中心 $x\in K$；这个相对开球只保留 $K$ 内与 $x$ 距离小于 $\varepsilon>0$ 的点。

> [!important] A finite net at every resolution · 每种精度都有有限网
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The set $K$ is totally bounded if, for every $\varepsilon>0$, there are finitely many centres $x_1,\ldots,x_m\in K$ such that
> $$K\subseteq\bigcup_{j=1}^{m}B_\varepsilon^K(x_j).$$
> The centres form a finite $\varepsilon$-net: every point of $K$ lies less than $\varepsilon$ from at least one centre. The empty set is totally bounded with $m=0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若对每个 $\varepsilon>0$，都能选出有限多个中心 $x_1,\ldots,x_m\in K$，使这些半径为 $\varepsilon$ 的球盖住 $K$，就称 $K$ 全有界。中心组成有限 $\varepsilon$-网：每个点都距某个中心不到 $\varepsilon$。空集用 $m=0$ 即可，因而也全有界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An interval has finite approximations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*区间具有有限近似*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given $\varepsilon>0$, choose a positive integer $m>1/\varepsilon$. The grid $0,1/m,2/m,\ldots,1$ lies in $[0,1]$. For every $x<1$, choose the left endpoint of its grid interval; the distance is less than $1/m<\varepsilon$. The point $1$ is itself a centre. Thus $[0,1]$ is totally bounded. Every finite set is also totally bounded: use all its points as centres, at any precision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定 $\varepsilon>0$，选正整数 $m>1/\varepsilon$。网格 $0,1/m,2/m,\ldots,1$ 全部位于 $[0,1]$。对每个 $x<1$，选其所在网格区间的左端点，距离小于 $1/m<\varepsilon$；点 $1$ 自己就是中心。因此 $[0,1]$ 全有界。任意有限集合也全有界：无论精度多高，都把其全部点作为中心即可。

## One large ball is a weaker requirement
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个大球是更弱的要求*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nonempty set is **bounded** when one ball of some finite radius contains it. Total boundedness implies this: take a finite cover by radius-$1$ balls with centres $x_1,\ldots,x_m$, and let
$$R=1+\max_{1\le j\le m}d(x_1,x_j).$$
For any $y\in K$, choose a centre with $d(y,x_j)<1$. The triangle inequality gives $d(y,x_1)<R$, so one finite-radius ball contains $K$. The empty set is bounded by convention. The converse fails because a large ball may contain infinitely many mutually separated points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非空集合**有界**，只要求存在某个有限半径的大球容纳整个集合。全有界推出有界：先取有限多个半径 $1$ 的球覆盖，中心为 $x_1,\ldots,x_m$，再令 $R=1+\max_j d(x_1,x_j)$。对任意 $y\in K$，选满足 $d(y,x_j)<1$ 的中心；三角不等式给出 $d(y,x_1)<R$，于是一个有限半径球便容纳了 $K$。空集约定为有界。反向不成立，因为一个大球可能容纳无穷多个彼此分离的点。

> [!example] Bounded, but no finite fine grid · 有界却没有有限细网
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $X=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$ use the discrete metric: $d(i,j)=0$ if $i=j$, and $d(i,j)=1$ otherwise. The whole space lies in $B_2(1)$, so it is bounded. But each ball of radius $1/2$ is a singleton. Finitely many such balls contain only finitely many integers and cannot cover $X$. Thus this bounded metric space is not totally bounded.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $X=\{1,2,3,\ldots\}$ 上采用离散度量：同一点距离为 $0$，不同点距离为 $1$。整个空间都在 $B_2(1)$ 中，所以有界；但半径 $1/2$ 的每个球都只有一个点。有限多个这样的球只能包含有限多个整数，无法覆盖 $X$。因此这个有界度量空间并不全有界。

## How compactness enters
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*它与紧致性怎样联系*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[紧致性|compact]] metric set is totally bounded. Fix any $\varepsilon>0$ and cover it by all the relative $\varepsilon$-balls centred at its points. Compactness selects finitely many of these balls, which is exactly the required finite net. This works at each requested precision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[紧致性|紧致]]的度量集合一定全有界。固定任意 $\varepsilon>0$，用以集合各点为中心的全部相对 $\varepsilon$-球覆盖它。紧致性选出其中有限多个，恰好给出所需的有限网。对每种精度都可以这样做。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Total boundedness alone does not guarantee compactness. The open interval $(0,1)$ is totally bounded: for a positive integer $m>1/\varepsilon$, use the $m$ midpoints $(j+1/2)/m$, where $j=0,\ldots,m-1$. They all belong to $(0,1)$ and every point is at distance at most $1/(2m)<\varepsilon$ from one of them. Yet the sequence $1/(n+1)$ approaches the missing endpoint $0$ and has no subsequence converging within $(0,1)$. The [[紧致性的序列判别|metric sequence criterion]] therefore rules out compactness. Finite approximations do not by themselves ensure that a limiting point is present.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有全有界还不能保证紧致。开区间 $(0,1)$ 全有界：给定正整数 $m>1/\varepsilon$，取 $m$ 个中点 $(j+1/2)/m$，其中 $j=0,\ldots,m-1$。它们都在 $(0,1)$ 内，每个区间内的点距某个中点最多 $1/(2m)<\varepsilon$。但序列 $1/(n+1)$ 接近缺失的端点 $0$，没有在 $(0,1)$ 内收敛的子列，所以[[紧致性的序列判别|度量空间的序列判别]]排除了紧致性。能够有限近似，本身不保证极限点仍然存在。
