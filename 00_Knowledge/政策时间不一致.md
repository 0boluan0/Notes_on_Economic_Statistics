# 政策时间不一致是事前最优计划在私人先行决策既定后不再是续局最优选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Policy time inconsistency occurs when an ex-ante optimal plan is no longer optimal in the continuation after private decisions are fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政策时间不一致可以在社会目标、偏好和已知经济结构不变、没有意外新消息时发生。事前，政府选择未来政策时会考虑它怎样影响今天的投资、定价或预期；事后，那些先行选择已经作出，重新优化便可能更愿意偏离原计划。改变的是决策时点与既定行动，不必是决策者变得更短视。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Time inconsistency can arise without changed social preferences, a changed economic structure or unexpected news. Ex ante, future policy influences current investment, prices or expectations. Once those choices are fixed, re-optimisation may favour a different policy. The timing and predetermined choices change; policymakers need not become more shortsighted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经典例子是洪泛区建设：事前，希望不在危险地区建房；如果不提供保护的政策可信，人们可能另选地点。但房屋一旦建成，政府可能认为付出防洪成本比承受损失更好。居民若预见这种事后选择，事前的“不保护”声明就未必能约束建设。这个例子要求“建房前避免建设较好、建房后保护较好”同时成立，不是所有防灾政策都有这个问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the floodplain example, avoiding construction is preferred before houses are built. Afterwards, protection may be less costly than allowing damage. Residents who anticipate protection need not be deterred by an announcement of no protection. The example requires different rankings before and after construction; it does not apply to every disaster policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别时画出三步：公众先根据什么承诺选择；哪些行动随后无法重选；政府在同一目标下为何想改。若只是按事先说明的状态依存计划应对新冲击，并不自动构成时间不一致。制度怎样改变续局激励复用[[承诺与可信性]]；货币政策中的独立推导见[[相机抉择的通胀偏差]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the promise affecting private choices, the decisions that subsequently become fixed, and the reason for a policy reversal under unchanged objectives. Following an announced state-contingent plan after new shocks is not automatically time inconsistency. Reuse [[承诺与可信性|commitment and credibility]] for institutional incentives and [[相机抉择的通胀偏差|discretionary inflation bias]] for the monetary example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Kydland 与 Prescott（1977），Rules Rather than Discretion，pp. 473–477、481](https://www.sfu.ca/~kkasa/prescott_77.pdf#page=4)：同一社会目标下的续局最优性、先行私人决策、洪泛区例子及并非短视的解释。
- [[承诺与可信性]]：复用可观察且改变后续行动、收益或信息的承诺机制；本卡只定义政策时间不一致。
