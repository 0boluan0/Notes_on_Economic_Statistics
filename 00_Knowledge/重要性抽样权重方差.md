# Variance of importance-sampling outputs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*重要性抽样的加权输出方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An importance sampler can target the correct average and still fluctuate too much to be useful. The quantity whose variance matters is the **weighted output** $h(X)p(X)/q(X)$, not the weight $p(X)/q(X)$ alone. A large weight may be harmless where $h$ is tiny, and an ordinary-looking set of observed weights may have missed the dangerous region.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重要性抽样可以对准正确的平均值，却仍因波动过大而难以使用。需要检查方差的是**加权输出** $h(X)p(X)/q(X)$，不是单独的权重 $p(X)/q(X)$。在 $h$ 很小的地方，大权重可能无妨；一批看起来平稳的权重，也可能只是尚未碰到危险区域。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The second moment belongs to the corrected output
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*二阶矩要对修正后的输出计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use [[重要性抽样|ordinary importance sampling]] with fixed normalised densities $p,q$, a fixed positive sample count $N$, and [[独立同分布|iid]] draws from $q$. Assume $E_p|h(X)|<\infty$ and the [[重要性抽样支持条件|coverage condition]] for this target. Then $Z=h(X)p(X)/q(X)$ has mean $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$. Its [[原点矩与中心矩|second raw moment]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑[[重要性抽样|普通重要性抽样]]：固定已归一化密度 $p,q$，预先固定正整数样本数 $N$，从 $q$ [[独立同分布|iid]]抽样。假定 $E_p|h(X)|<\infty$ 且满足本目标的[[重要性抽样支持条件]]。那么 $Z=h(X)p(X)/q(X)$ 的均值为 $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$，它的[[原点矩与中心矩|二阶原点矩]]为

$$E_q[Z^2]=\int_{q>0}\frac{h(x)^2p(x)^2}{q(x)}\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The denominator has only one factor of $q$: squaring the output supplies $q^2$ below, then integrating under the sampling density cancels one factor. If this integral is finite,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分母最终只有一个 $q$：输出平方先产生 $q^2$，再按抽样密度积分时约去一个 $q$。若此积分有限，则

$$\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\mu_{IS})=\frac{E_q[Z^2]-\mu^2}{N}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the second moment is infinite, the corrected output still has a finite mean under the assumptions above, but its variance is infinite. The ordinary finite-variance standard-error and central-limit calculation no longer applies. This does not contradict a [[强大数定律|strong law]] based on integrability: consistency alone gives no usual $N^{-1/2}$ error scale. Discrete models use sums in the same formulas.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若二阶矩无穷，上述假设仍保证修正输出有有限均值，但方差为无穷，通常的有限方差标准误与中心极限定理计算不再适用。这并不违背基于可积性的[[强大数定律]]：一致性本身不提供通常的 $N^{-1/2}$ 误差尺度。离散模型将同样公式中的积分改为求和。

> [!example] Correct coverage, infinite variance · 覆盖正确，方差仍可无穷
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take the target $p(x)=1$ on $(0,1)$, the proposal $q(x)=2x$, and $h(x)=1$. Both densities integrate to one, and the proposal is positive throughout the interval. Yet $Z=1/(2X)$ has $E_qZ=1$ and $E_qZ^2=\int_0^1(2x)^{-1}\,dx=\infty$. Near zero, the proposal visits rarely and the correction becomes arbitrarily large. The known target $1$ makes this a diagnostic example rather than a problem needing simulation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 目标密度取 $(0,1)$ 上的 $p(x)=1$，提议密度取 $q(x)=2x$，令 $h(x)=1$。两个密度都积分为一，提议在整个区间为正。但 $Z=1/(2X)$ 满足 $E_qZ=1$，而 $E_qZ^2=\int_0^1(2x)^{-1}\,dx=\infty$。靠近零的区域很少被访问，修正幅度却任意大。目标 $1$ 本来已知，这个例子是用来诊断原理，而不是说它需要模拟求解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the same $p,q$ but change the recorded quantity to $h(x)=x$. Now $Z=x/(2x)=1/2$ almost surely, so the estimator has zero variance. The weights have not changed at all. This is why “the weights have infinite variance” and “this weighted estimate has infinite variance” are different claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保持同样的 $p,q$，仅把记录值改成 $h(x)=x$，现在 $Z=x/(2x)=1/2$ 几乎必然成立，估计量方差为零。权重一点没变。所以“权重方差无穷”与“这个加权估计方差无穷”是不同的结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Even finite variance can be worse than direct sampling
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方差有限，也可能比直接抽样更差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to a failure event with target probability $0.01$. If the proposal instead assigns failure probability $0.0001$, the weighted indicator is $100$ with probability $0.0001$ and $0$ otherwise. Its mean is still $0.01$, but its variance is $1-0.0001=0.9999$. The direct indicator variance is $0.0099$, so this proposal makes variance $101$ times larger at the same number of independent draws. Correct weighting repairs the mean, not a poor sampling design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回到目标概率为 $0.01$ 的失败事件。若提议反而只给失败概率 $0.0001$，加权指示变量以概率 $0.0001$ 等于 $100$，其余时候为零。均值仍为 $0.01$，方差却是 $1-0.0001=0.9999$；直接指示变量方差为 $0.0099$，所以相同独立抽样次数下，这个提议使方差增大 $101$ 倍。权重修复的是均值，不是糟糕的抽样设计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful sufficient check is a finite bound $w(x)=p(x)/q(x)\le c$ on the target-relevant region, together with $E_p[h(X)^2]<\infty$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个有用的充分检查是：在目标相关区域内，$w(x)=p(x)/q(x)\le c<\infty$，并且 $E_p[h(X)^2]<\infty$。此时

$$E_q[Z^2]\le cE_p[h(X)^2],$$
$$\operatorname{Var}_q(Z)\le c\operatorname{Var}_p(h(X))+(c-1)\mu^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This proves finite variance, not an improvement over direct sampling. In particular, the extra $(c-1)\mu^2$ term cannot generally be dropped. Weights can also be unbounded while this particular weighted output has finite variance, as the $h(x)=x$ example shows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个界证明方差有限，却不保证优于直接抽样。尤其不能一般性地去掉额外的 $(c-1)\mu^2$ 项。权重也可以无界而特定加权输出方差有限，上面的 $h(x)=x$ 就是例子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Estimate uncertainty from the weighted outputs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从加权输出估计不确定性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With finite second moments and $N\ge2$, apply the [[Monte Carlo均值标准误|sample-mean calculation]] to the actual $Z_i$ values:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当二阶矩有限且 $N\ge2$，对实际的 $Z_i$ 使用[[Monte Carlo均值标准误|样本均值计算]]：

$$s_Z^2=\frac1{N-1}\sum_{i=1}^N(Z_i-\overline Z)^2,\qquad \widehat{\operatorname{SE}}(\widehat\mu_{IS})=\frac{s_Z}{\sqrt N}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $s_Z=\sqrt{s_Z^2}$. The sample variance estimates the single-output variance without bias under these assumptions; the resulting $s_Z/\sqrt N$ is not generally an unbiased standard-error estimate. A [[Monte Carlo近似区间|normal approximation interval]] needs separate justification, especially when outputs are highly skewed. A realised finite sample variance does not prove that the population second moment is finite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $s_Z=\sqrt{s_Z^2}$。在这些假设下，样本方差无偏估计单次输出方差，但得到的 $s_Z/\sqrt N$ 一般不是无偏的标准误估计。[[Monte Carlo近似区间|正态近似区间]]需要另核条件，输出高度偏斜时尤其如此。某次样本方差算得出有限值，并不证明总体二阶矩有限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weight histograms, the share contributed by the largest outputs, repeated runs and effective-sample-size summaries can reveal trouble. They cannot prove its absence: every run might miss the same rare high-weight region. Weight-only diagnostics also ignore $h$. Assess the actual integral $\int h^2p^2/q$, inspect the weighted outputs, and include sampling and density-evaluation costs in [[方差缩减比较口径|an efficiency comparison]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

权重直方图、最大几个输出的贡献占比、重复运行及有效样本量摘要，都可以暴露问题，却不能证明没有问题：每次运行可能都没碰到同一片稀有的大权重区域。只看权重的诊断还忽略了 $h$。应检查实际积分 $\int h^2p^2/q$，查看加权输出，并在[[方差缩减比较口径|效率比较]]中计入抽样和密度计算成本。
