# 支持覆盖只保证重要性抽样目标正确；有限方差还要求 h·p/q 在 q 下平方可积，极端权重可使方差无限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Support coverage only preserves the importance-sampling target; finite variance also requires h times p over q to be square-integrable under q, and extreme weights can make variance infinite*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] proposal 可以无偏却极不稳定
> 即使 $q$ 覆盖了目标支持，若 $q$ 在重要区域相对 $p$ 太薄，似然比 $p/q$ 会产生极端值；此时估计量可能比直接抽样方差更大，甚至没有有限方差。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Even with correct support, a proposal that is too light where the target matters creates extreme likelihood ratios. The estimator can be worse than direct sampling or have no finite variance.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通重要性抽样单次加权输出为

$$
Z=h(X)\frac{p(X)}{q(X)},
\qquad X\sim q.
$$

其二阶矩为

$$
E_q[Z^2]
=\int_{{q>0\}}
\frac{h(x)^2p(x)^2}{q(x)}\,dx.
$$

只有该积分有限时，经典方差

$$
\operatorname{Var}(\widehat\mu_{IS})
=\frac1N\left(E_q[Z^2]-\mu^2\right)
$$

才存在。支持正确是换测度恒等式的条件，平方可积是经典标准误与效率比较的另一个条件；二者逻辑独立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The weighted second moment is the integral of $h^2p^2/q$. Support coverage preserves the target identity, whereas finiteness of this integral is what permits the classical variance and standard-error calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

样本中偶尔出现巨大权重、估计值被极少数点支配、重复运行差异极大，都是 proposal 与目标尾部不匹配的警报。权重直方图或 effective sample size 是诊断，不是有限方差证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rare enormous weights, domination by a few observations, and large run-to-run changes warn of tail mismatch. Weight plots and effective-sample-size summaries are diagnostics, not proofs of finite variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检
> 一个 proposal 在所有目标支持上都为正，是否足以保证重要性抽样标准误有限？
>
> **答案：** 不足。还要检查 $\int h^2p^2/q$ 是否有限。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. The weighted second moment $\int h^2p^2/q$ must also be finite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
