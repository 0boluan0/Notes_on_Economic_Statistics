# 和的方差必须包含所有协方差项
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The variance of a sum includes every covariance term*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子公式
> 对具有有限二阶矩的有限组随机变量 $X_1,\ldots,X_n$，
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^nX_i\right)
> =\sum_{i=1}^n\operatorname{Var}(X_i)
> +2\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j).
> $$
> 和的方差等于各方差之和当且仅当 $\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)=0$。所有交叉协方差逐项为 0 是常用的充分条件，但不是必要条件，因为正负协方差可能相互抵消。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a finite collection $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ with finite second moments,
> $$
> \operatorname{Var}\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^nX_i\right)
> =\sum_{i=1}^n\operatorname{Var}(X_i)
> +2\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j).
> $$
> The variance of the sum equals the sum of the variances exactly when $\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)=0$. Vanishing of every cross-covariance is a common sufficient condition, not a necessary one, because positive and negative covariances may cancel.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

展开 centered sum 的平方即可看到交叉项：

$$
E\!\left[\left(\sum_i(X_i-EX_i)\right)^2\right]
=\sum_iE[(X_i-EX_i)^2]
+2\sum_{i<j}E[(X_i-EX_i)(X_j-EX_j)].
$$

最后一组期望正是 covariances，不能因为“期望可相加”就自动删掉。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expanding the square of the centred sum produces the displayed cross terms. Those expectations are exactly the covariances; they do not disappear merely because expectations themselves always add.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

pairwise uncorrelated 已足以使全部协方差为 0；[[两两独立|pairwise independence]] 在二阶矩有限时又推出 pairwise uncorrelated，因此也足以让方差相加。这里不需要 mutual independence。反过来，协方差为 0 一般不推出独立，而 [[两两独立不推出相互独立]] 说明两两独立也不能升级成联合独立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pairwise lack of correlation is enough to make all covariances zero. With finite second moments, pairwise independence implies pairwise lack of correlation and is therefore also enough for variance additivity; mutual independence is unnecessary for this second-order step. Conversely, zero covariance generally does not imply independence, and [[两两独立不推出相互独立|pairwise independence does not imply mutual independence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小反例是令 $X_1=X_2=Z$，其中 $EZ=0$、$\operatorname{Var}(Z)=1$。则

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X_1+X_2)=\operatorname{Var}(2Z)=4,
$$

而 $\operatorname{Var}(X_1)+\operatorname{Var}(X_2)=2$；少掉的 2 正来自 $2\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A minimal counterexample sets $X_1=X_2=Z$, where $EZ=0$ and $\operatorname{Var}(Z)=1$. Then $\operatorname{Var}(X_1+X_2)=\operatorname{Var}(2Z)=4$, while the sum of the two variances is only two. The missing two is exactly $2\operatorname{Cov}(X_1,X_2)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一组变量只被证明 pairwise independent。计算有限和的方差够不够？分解整个联合 MGF 又够不够？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose a finite collection is known only to be pairwise independent. Is that enough to calculate the variance of its sum by addition? Is it enough to factorise the full joint MGF?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 有限二阶矩下，前者够；后者一般不够，因为多因子期望分解需要相互独立等更强的联合结构。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** With finite second moments, pairwise independence is enough for variance additivity. It is generally not enough for the many-factor MGF decomposition, which requires a stronger joint structure such as mutual independence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
