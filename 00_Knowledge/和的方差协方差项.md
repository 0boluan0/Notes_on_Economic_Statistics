# Variance of a sum
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*和的方差与协方差项*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The variability of a total depends on how its parts move together. Parts that tend to rise together can make the total fluctuate more; parts that offset one another can make it fluctuate less. Adding their individual variances alone misses this joint behaviour. **Covariance terms** supply the correction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总量波动多大，取决于各部分怎样一起变化。倾向于同时上升的部分，可能使总量更不稳定；相互抵消的部分，可能使总量更稳定。只把各自方差相加，会漏掉这种共同变化，**协方差项**正是对它的修正。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A variable's [[方差|variance]] averages its squared distance from its mean. The [[协方差|covariance]] of two variables averages the product of their deviations from their own means. For $X,Y$ with finite second moments, meaning $E[X^2]<\infty$ and $E[Y^2]<\infty$, all these quantities are finite and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个变量的[[方差]]是它偏离均值的距离平方的平均；两个变量的[[协方差]]则平均各自偏差的乘积。若 $X,Y$ 有限二阶矩，即 $E[X^2]<\infty$、$E[Y^2]<\infty$，这些量都有限，并且

> [!abstract] Two parts of a total · 两部分组成的总量
> $$\operatorname{Var}(X+Y)=\operatorname{Var}(X)+\operatorname{Var}(Y)+2\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y).$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The two variance terms describe separate fluctuations; the covariance term describes their interaction. Independence is not required for this identity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个方差项描述各自波动，协方差项描述它们怎样共同变化；这个恒等式不要求独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the cross term appears twice
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么交叉项带有系数二*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $U=X-E[X]$ and $V=Y-E[Y]$ for the centred deviations. By [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]], the deviation of $X+Y$ from its mean is $U+V$. Ordinary algebra gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $U=X-E[X]$、$V=Y-E[Y]$ 表示中心化偏差。由[[期望线性性]]，$X+Y$ 相对自身均值的偏差就是 $U+V$。普通代数给出

$$(U+V)^2=U^2+UV+VU+V^2=U^2+2UV+V^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product $UV$ occurs once from each order of multiplication. Averaging gives $E[U^2]=\operatorname{Var}(X)$, $E[V^2]=\operatorname{Var}(Y)$ and $E[UV]=\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$. Linearity allows us to average these terms separately; it does not make the cross term vanish.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$UV$ 从两种乘法次序中各出现一次。取平均后，$E[U^2]=\operatorname{Var}(X)$、$E[V^2]=\operatorname{Var}(Y)$、$E[UV]=\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$。线性性允许分别平均这些项，却不会让交叉项自动消失。

> [!example] Copying the same fluctuation · 把同一个波动复制两遍
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $Z$ take $-1$ and $1$ with equal probability, so $E[Z]=0$ and $\operatorname{Var}(Z)=1$. Set $X=Y=Z$. Each part has variance one, but the total is $2Z$ and its variance is four by the [[方差的仿射变换|squared scaling rule]]. The covariance is one, so the full formula gives $1+1+2(1)=4$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $Z$ 等概率取 $-1$ 和 $1$，因此 $E[Z]=0$、$\operatorname{Var}(Z)=1$。设 $X=Y=Z$，各部分方差都为一，总量却为 $2Z$，按[[方差的仿射变换|平方缩放规则]]，方差为四。协方差是一，完整公式给出 $1+1+2(1)=4$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If instead $Y=-Z$, the covariance is $-1$. The sum is always zero, and the formula gives $1+1-2=0$. Positive and negative cross terms have concrete effects on the total.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果改成 $Y=-Z$，协方差为 $-1$，和始终为零，公式也给出 $1+1-2=0$。正负交叉项确实会改变总量的波动。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## More than two variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两个以上的变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ be a finite collection with finite second moments. The notation $\sum_{i<j}$ means to include each distinct unordered pair of indices once: for three variables the pairs are $(1,2),(1,3),(2,3)$. The same square expansion gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ 是有限个具有有限二阶矩的变量。记号 $\sum_{i<j}$ 表示每对不同下标只取一次：三个变量时包括 $(1,2),(1,3),(2,3)$。同样展开平方，得到

$$\operatorname{Var}\!\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_i\right)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\operatorname{Var}(X_i)+2\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each unordered pair represents both multiplication orders, which is why the factor two remains. If every pair has zero covariance, the cross terms disappear. With finite second moments, [[两两独立|pairwise independence]] is sufficient for that; [[相互独立|mutual independence]] is stronger than this calculation needs. For [[随机计数的指标和|indicator counts]], write $p_i=P(A_i)$ for the probability of event $A_i$. Each variance is $p_i(1-p_i)$, and each pair's correction can be found from the [[指示变量协方差|joint occurrence probability]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个无序下标对代表两种乘法次序，所以仍有系数二。如果每对协方差都为零，交叉项就全部消失。有限二阶矩下，[[两两独立]]足以保证这一点；[[相互独立]]比本次计算所需条件更强。对[[随机计数的指标和|指标计数]]，各项方差为 $p_i(1-p_i)$，每对修正量由[[指示变量协方差|同时发生的概率]]算出，其中 $p_i$ 是第 $i$ 个事件的概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exact condition for variance additivity is that the **total** cross-covariance sum is zero. Requiring each covariance separately to be zero is sufficient, but is not necessary when several positive and negative terms can cancel:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方差可加的精确条件，是交叉协方差的**总和**为零。要求每项协方差分别为零是充分条件；当多项正负值可以抵消时，它并非必要条件：

$$\operatorname{Var}\!\left(\sum_iX_i\right)=\sum_i\operatorname{Var}(X_i)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j)=0.$$

> [!note]- Cancellation without pairwise zero covariance · 各项不为零也可以相互抵消
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $Z,W$ be independent variables, each taking $-1,1$ with equal probability. Define $X_1=Z$, $X_2=Z+W$, and $X_3=W-Z$. Their pair covariances are $1,-1,0$, so their sum is zero. The individual variances are $1,2,2$, and the total is $Z+2W$, with variance $5=1+2+2$. Variance additivity here does not establish pairwise zero covariance or independence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $Z,W$ 独立，且各自等概率取 $-1,1$。定义 $X_1=Z$、$X_2=Z+W$、$X_3=W-Z$。三对协方差依次为 $1,-1,0$，总和为零；各方差为 $1,2,2$，总量为 $Z+2W$，方差为 $5=1+2+2$。这里方差可加，并不能证明逐对零协方差或独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are second-moment statements. [[零协方差不推独立|Zero covariance generally does not imply independence]], and [[两两独立不推出相互独立|pairwise independence need not describe the full joint law]]. In particular, pairwise independence alone does not generally justify multiplying the factors of a joint moment-generating function, which encodes joint information through an exponential average. That further factorisation needs its own assumptions and existence conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以上结论只涉及二阶矩。[[零协方差不推独立|零协方差一般不推出独立]]，[[两两独立不推出相互独立|两两独立也未必决定完整联合分布]]。例如，仅有两两独立，一般不能把联合矩母函数分解成各个因子的乘积；矩母函数通过指数的平均编码联合信息，这种进一步分解需要另外核对假设与存在条件。
