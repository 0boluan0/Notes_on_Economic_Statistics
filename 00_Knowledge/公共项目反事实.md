# 公共项目反事实是在不实施待评方案时相关结果将如何变化的比较基准
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A public-project counterfactual is the baseline path of relevant outcomes without the proposal being appraised.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评价公共项目时，先写出**不实施这个方案，接下来会怎样**。未实施方案也可能包含维修、人口变化、私人投资和已有政策的延续；它不是把今天的世界永久冻结。用“有项目”减去这个动态基准，才得到待评价的增量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First describe what would happen without this proposal. Maintenance, demographic change, private investment and existing policies can continue in the baseline. Incremental appraisal subtracts that evolving baseline, not a permanently frozen present.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用同一种结果 $Y$ 表示某年的通勤耗时、事故数或服务产出：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Y$ denote a consistently measured outcome, such as travel time, accidents or service output:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta Y_t=Y_t(\text{实施})-Y_t(\text{未实施}).
$$

假设目前每次通勤需 40 分钟；两年后即使不扩路，需求增长也会使它达到 50 分钟，而扩路方案预计为 35 分钟。方案相对基准节省的是 **15 分钟**，不是相对今天的 5 分钟。相反，若另一条已经获批的线路本来就会把通勤降到 35 分钟，则不能把这项既有改善记给新项目。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a trip takes 40 minutes today. In two years it would take 50 without widening but 35 with it. The proposal saves 15 minutes relative to the baseline, not five relative to today. Improvements from a separately approved scheme must also remain in the baseline rather than being credited to the new project.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个差额是要估计的对象，不是自动成立的因果证据。同一对象的两种状态不能同时观察，见 [[反事实不可同时观察]]；实际预测需要明确交通需求、执行程度等假设，事后识别仍需合适的研究设计。最好同时展示两条结果路径及差额，让读者能看出改善来自哪一步。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The contrast defines the estimand, not proof of causation; see [[反事实不可同时观察|why both counterfactual states cannot be observed]]. Forecasts require assumptions, and retrospective identification requires a suitable design. Showing both paths and their difference makes the claimed increment inspectable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

企业投资中的 [[项目增量现金流]] 使用同样的有无比较思想，但对象是企业未来税后现金流；这里包括评价边界内的社会影响，不能直接把两套金额相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[项目增量现金流|Incremental corporate cash flow]] uses the same with–without logic, but measures the firm's future after-tax cash flows rather than all included social impacts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
