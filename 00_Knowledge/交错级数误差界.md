# The alternating-series remainder bound
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*交错级数误差界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once its magnitudes are nonincreasing, an alternating sum satisfying the convergence conditions bounds its final value between successive partial sums. After $N$ terms, the **first omitted term** provides a bound on how much remains unaccounted for. Consecutive partial sums also tell us which side of the answer we are on.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满足收敛条件的交错求和，在幅度已经不增的尾部中，相邻部分和能够从两侧夹住最终值。加完前 $N$ 项以后，**第一项被遗漏的项**就能控制还没算进来的总误差。相邻部分和还会告诉我们，当前近似位于答案的哪一侧。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use $S=\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}b_n$, with finite nonnegative magnitudes $b_n$. Assume that for some positive integer $N_0$ and all $n\ge N_0$, $b_{n+1}\le b_n$, and that $b_n\to0$. These are the [[交错级数判别|alternating-test conditions]], which establish the finite sum $S$. Put $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N(-1)^{n-1}b_n$ and $R_N=S-S_N$. The symbol $R_N$ denotes the signed remainder; $|R_N|$ is the absolute approximation error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

写成 $S=\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}b_n$，幅度 $b_n$ 有限且非负。假设存在某个正整数 $N_0$，使所有 $n\ge N_0$ 都有 $b_{n+1}\le b_n$，并且 $b_n\to0$。这些[[交错级数判别|交错判别条件]]保证有限和 $S$ 存在。再记 $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N(-1)^{n-1}b_n$、$R_N=S-S_N$；$R_N$ 是带符号余项，$|R_N|$ 才是近似的绝对误差。

> [!important] Use the next magnitude · 使用下一项幅度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For any integer $N\ge N_0$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对整数 $N\ge N_0$，
>
> $$|R_N|\le b_{N+1},\qquad
> \min(S_N,S_{N+1})\le S\le\max(S_N,S_{N+1}).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The bound is $b_{N+1}$ because term $N$ has already been included. If $R_N\ne0$, its sign equals the sign of the first omitted term $(-1)^N b_{N+1}$. A remainder can also be exactly zero. Reversing every sign of the series reverses the inequalities' orientation but preserves the absolute-error bound.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上界是 $b_{N+1}$，因为第 $N$ 项已经包含在内。若 $R_N\ne0$，它与第一项遗漏项 $(-1)^N b_{N+1}$ 同号；余项也可能恰好为零。把级数所有符号反向，只改变夹逼的方向，不改变绝对误差界。

## Why two consecutive totals trap all later ones
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*相邻两个累计值为什么能夹住后面的值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start at $S_N$. The next step lands at $S_{N+1}$, a distance $b_{N+1}$ away. Subsequent steps alternate direction and never increase in magnitude. As in the parity proof of the [[交错级数判别|alternating test]], the later totals of one parity move inward from one endpoint, and those of the other parity move inward from the other endpoint. Every later total lies inside the closed interval with endpoints $S_N,S_{N+1}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $S_N$ 出发，下一步落到 $S_{N+1}$，相距 $b_{N+1}$。后续每一步方向交替，幅度不增。按照[[交错级数判别]]中的奇偶证明，一类部分和从区间一端向内移动，另一类从另一端向内移动。所有后续部分和都位于以 $S_N,S_{N+1}$ 为端点的闭区间内。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking their common limit keeps $S$ in that same interval. Its distance from $S_N$ is therefore at most the interval length $b_{N+1}$, and any nonzero displacement from $S_N$ points toward $S_{N+1}$. This proves both the magnitude bound and the sign statement. No exact formula for $S$ is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取共同极限后，$S$ 仍在这个区间内。因此，它到 $S_N$ 的距离至多是区间长度 $b_{N+1}$，而非零位移的方向必然指向 $S_{N+1}$。这同时证明了幅度界与符号结论，不需要知道 $S$ 的精确公式。

> [!example] Thirty-one terms of alternating reciprocal squares · 交错平方倒数的前31项
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider $S=1-1/2^2+1/3^2-\cdots$. Its magnitudes $b_n=1/n^2$ decrease to zero from the start. If we include the first 31 terms, the next magnitude is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑 $S=1-1/2^2+1/3^2-\cdots$。幅度 $b_n=1/n^2$ 从开始就递减趋零。加完前31项，下一项的幅度为
>
> $$b_{32}=\frac1{32^2}=\frac1{1024}<10^{-3}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus $|S-S_{31}|\le1/1024<0.001$. The omitted 32nd term is negative, so $S_{32}\le S\le S_{31}$: $S_{31}$ is an upper estimate and $S_{32}$ is a lower estimate. This establishes an interval for the answer without invoking its exact value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因此 $|S-S_{31}|\le1/1024<0.001$。遗漏的第32项为负，所以 $S_{32}\le S\le S_{31}$：$S_{31}$ 是上估计，$S_{32}$ 是下估计。这不借助精确值，就给出了答案所在区间。

## Turn an error request into a sufficient term count
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把误差要求变成足够的项数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an error at most $\varepsilon>0$, find an integer $N\ge N_0$ with $b_{N+1}\le\varepsilon$. If using this bound to guarantee a strictly smaller error, choose $b_{N+1}<\varepsilon$. In the square example, $N=31$ guarantees error below $0.001$. For $N=30$, the bound $1/31^2$ exceeds $0.001$; that only means this bound has not certified the target, not that the actual error necessarily exceeds it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要求误差至多为 $\varepsilon>0$，找到满足 $N\ge N_0$ 且 $b_{N+1}\le\varepsilon$ 的整数 $N$ 即可。若用此界保证严格更小的误差，就选 $b_{N+1}<\varepsilon$。平方倒数例子中，$N=31$ 保证误差小于 $0.001$；$N=30$ 时上界 $1/31^2$ 超过 $0.001$，只表示这个界尚未保证达标，不代表实际误差必然超标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The weak inequality matters when equal or zero magnitudes are allowed. If the omitted tail is $c-c+0+0+\cdots$, with $c>0$ and the displayed signs matching its first two positions, the true remainder is zero although the bound is $c$. If it is $c-0+0-0+\cdots$, the remainder equals $c$ and the bound is attained. Reversing the first sign reverses the remainder's sign in each case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

允许相等或零幅度时，非严格不等号很重要。若遗漏尾部是 $c-c+0+0+\cdots$，其中 $c>0$，显示的符号对应尾部的前两个位置，则真实余项为零，上界却为 $c$。若尾部是 $c-0+0-0+\cdots$，余项恰为 $c$，上界可以取到。反转首项符号，就相应反转余项符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Do not apply the bound before verifying the tail conditions. Merely having a convergent series with alternating signs is insufficient; magnitudes may rise again after the next term. If monotonicity is known only after $N_0$, use a cutoff in that controlled tail. Positive nonalternating series use a different estimate, such as [[积分判别余项界|integral remainder bounds]]. Both kinds bound mathematical truncation error; rounding error in a numerical partial sum needs separate accounting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先核对尾部条件，再使用误差界。只有“收敛且符号交替”还不够，因为下一项之后，幅度可能再次变大。若只从 $N_0$ 以后知道单调性，就在这个受控尾部中选择截断点。正项而非交错的级数，可使用其他估计，例如[[积分判别余项界]]。两类方法控制的都是数学截断误差，数值部分和的舍入误差需要另计。
