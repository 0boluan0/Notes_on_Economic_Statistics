# 满足交错判别条件的截断误差不超过下一项幅度且真值夹在相邻部分和之间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Under the alternating-test hypotheses, the error is at most the next magnitude and the sum lies between consecutive partial sums*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $S=\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}b_n$，其中 $b_n$ 在 $n\ge N_0$ 后非负、单调不增且趋于零。取 $N\ge N_0$，令 $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}(-1)^{n-1}b_n$、$R_N=S-S_N$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $S=\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}b_n$, where the magnitudes are nonnegative and nonincreasing to zero for $n\ge N_0$. For $N\ge N_0$, define the partial sum $S_N$ and remainder $R_N=S-S_N$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
|R_N|\le b_{N+1},
\qquad
\min(S_N,S_{N+1})\le S\le\max(S_N,S_{N+1}).
$$

因此非零余项与第一项被略去的项 $(-1)^N b_{N+1}$ 同号；余项也可能恰为零。注意上界是 $b_{N+1}$，因为已经加到第 $N$ 项。该界适用于满足 [[交错级数判别]] 条件的尾部，不能仅凭“级数收敛且符号交替”套用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nonzero remainder has the sign of the first omitted term $(-1)^N b_{N+1}$; it may also be zero. The bound uses $b_{N+1}$ because the first $N$ terms were included. It requires the [[交错级数判别|alternating-test hypotheses]] on the tail, not merely convergence and alternating signs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理由是后续奇、偶部分和被夹在 $S_N$ 与 $S_{N+1}$ 之间，并趋于同一个 $S$；两端距离恰好为 $b_{N+1}$。给定容许误差 $\varepsilon>0$，找到 $N$ 使 $b_{N+1}\le\varepsilon$，就得到 $|S-S_N|\le\varepsilon$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subsequent odd and even partial sums stay between $S_N$ and $S_{N+1}$ and converge to the same sum. Their enclosing interval has length $b_{N+1}$. To guarantee error at most $\varepsilon$, choose $N$ with $b_{N+1}\le\varepsilon$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}/n^2$ 取前 31 项，误差不超过 $1/32^2=1/1024<10^{-3}$。第 32 项为负，所以 $S_{32}\le S\le S_{31}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}/n^2$, the first 31 terms give error at most $1/32^2=1/1024<10^{-3}$. The 32nd term is negative, so $S_{32}\le S\le S_{31}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
