# Value function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*值函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **value function** tells you the best objective value available under each setting of a problem. A setting might be a budget, a price, or a resource limit. First hold that setting fixed and compare the allowed choices; then repeat for a different setting. The output records how good the best result is, while [[最优解对应|the solution correspondence]] records the choices that achieve it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个**值函数**告诉你：问题的条件每换一种，目标最好能有多好。条件可以是预算、价格或资源上限。先固定这一条件，比较所有允许的选择；再换一个条件重新比较。输出记录最好的结果有多好，[[最优解对应]]则记录哪些选择真正实现了它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $\theta$ for the **parameter**, the setting held fixed during one optimization, and $x$ for the **choice variable** being varied. The feasible set $X(\theta)$ lists the allowed choices, and $f(x,\theta)$ is their numerical score. Here $f$ is real-valued at each feasible pair. The [[上确界|supremum]] is the least upper bound of the attainable scores; it need not itself be attained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $\theta$ 表示**参数**，即每次优化时固定的问题条件；用 $x$ 表示需要调整的**选择变量**。可行集 $X(\theta)$ 列出允许的选择，$f(x,\theta)$ 给每个选择打出一个数值分数。这里假设每个可行的 $(x,\theta)$ 都产生有限实数分数。[[上确界]]是这些分数的最小上界，不保证某个选择恰好取得它。

> [!definition] The optimal-value rule · 最优值的规则
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the parameterised maximisation problem $\sup_{x\in X(\theta)}f(x,\theta)$, the value function is $v(\theta)=\sup_{x\in X(\theta)}f(x,\theta)$. It records how good the objective can be at each parameter. [[最优解对应|The solution correspondence]] separately records which choices attain that value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对参数 $\theta$ 下的最大化问题
> $$
> \sup_{x\in X(\theta)} f(x,\theta),
> $$
> 值函数定义为
> $$
> v(\theta)=\sup_{x\in X(\theta)} f(x,\theta).
> $$
> 它只记录“在当前参数下，目标最好能有多好”；哪些选择实现这个值，由 [[最优解对应]] 另行记录。

> [!example] A changing resource limit · 改变资源上限
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $\theta\ge0$, let $X(\theta)=[0,\theta]$, and score a choice by $f(x,\theta)=2x$. Every feasible choice satisfies $2x\le2\theta$, and the feasible choice $x=\theta$ reaches that bound. Therefore $v(\theta)=2\theta$. With limits $\theta=1$ and $\theta=3$, the best scores are $2$ and $6$, while the maximizing choices are $1$ and $3$. At $\theta=0$, the only choice is zero and the value is zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $\theta\ge0$，可行集为 $X(\theta)=[0,\theta]$，选择的分数为 $f(x,\theta)=2x$。每个可行选择都满足 $2x\le2\theta$，而可行点 $x=\theta$ 恰好达到这个上界，所以 $v(\theta)=2\theta$。上限分别为 $1$ 和 $3$ 时，最优分数分别为 $2$ 和 $6$，实现它们的选择则分别为 $1$ 和 $3$。当 $\theta=0$，唯一选择与最优值均为零。

## Why the definition uses a supremum
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么定义用 supremum

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The optimal value may be approached without being attained. Maximising $x$ over $(0,1)$ gives the finite supremum one but no maximizer. Only after attainment has been proved may $\sup$ be replaced by $\max$. Continuity on a nonempty compact feasible set is one standard route; see [[极值定理|the extreme-value theorem]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最优值可能被无限逼近，却没有被任何可行选择取到。例如在开区间 $(0,1)$ 上最大化 $f(x)=x$ 时，
$$
v=\sup_{0<x<1}x=1,
$$
但不存在最大点。只有在另行证明最优值被取得后，才能把 $\sup$ 改写为 $\max$。连续目标加非空紧可行集是一条常用的取得性路线，见 [[极值定理]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indeed, every allowed score is below one, and scores can approach one as closely as desired. But any proposed choice $x\in(0,1)$ can be improved to $(x+1)/2$, still inside the interval. Thus one is the optimal **value** in the supremum sense, not a maximum that some allowed point produces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体地说，每个允许的分数都小于一，却可以任意接近一。但任意候选选择 $x\in(0,1)$ 都能改成仍在区间中的 $(x+1)/2$，取得更高分数。因此，一是上确界意义的最优**值**，并不是某个合法点产生的最大值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under extended-real conventions, an infeasible maximisation problem is often assigned value $-\infty$, while an unbounded-above problem has value $+\infty$. Infeasibility, unboundedness, a finite but unattained supremum, and an attained optimum are four different states.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在扩展实数约定下，最大化问题的可行集为空时，其值常记为 $-\infty$；目标在可行集上无上界时，记为 $+\infty$。因此要把“不可行”、“无界”、“有限但不取得”和“取得最优”分开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, maximizing $x$ over all of $\mathbb R$ is unbounded above: for any proposed score there is a larger one, so the value is $+\infty$. This does not mean a feasible input has the score infinity. With our real-valued objective convention, an attained optimal value is finite; the symbols $\pm\infty$ describe the optimization outcome rather than additional feasible scores.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，在整个 $\mathbb R$ 上最大化 $x$，任意给定分数之上都有更高分数，所以最优值记为 $+\infty$；这不表示某个可行输入的分数是无穷大。在本卡的实值目标约定下，真正取得的最优值是有限数；$\pm\infty$ 描述优化问题的结果，不是额外增加的可行分数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The value does not record how it is achieved
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 值不记录“怎么做到”

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One optimal value may be attained by one, several, or infinitely many choices. The value can therefore be smooth even when a selected optimizer path jumps. Implicit comparative statics studies a choice branch; envelope results study the value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个最优值可以由一个、多个甚至无限多个选择实现。因而 $v(\theta)$ 可以很平滑，而某条最优选择路径却发生跳跃。[[隐函数比较静态]] 研究选择分支怎样变；[[包络定理]] 和 [[Danskin定理]] 研究值怎样变。这些问题的研究对象不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, maximizing the zero function on $[-1,1]$ gives one value, zero, but every point achieves it. The links to [[隐函数比较静态|implicit comparative statics]], [[包络定理|the envelope theorem]], and [[Danskin定理|Danskin’s theorem]] address different derivative questions under their own assumptions. A direct comparison is worked out in [[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微|optimizer jumps and value differentiability]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，在 $[-1,1]$ 上最大化零函数，最优值只有一个零，但每个点都能取得它。关于选择变化与价值变化的定理都有各自的前提；两者不能混为一谈，具体对照见 [[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Profit and indirect-utility functions are maximum-value functions; cost and expenditure functions are minimum-value functions. For minimisation, replace the supremum with an infimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利润函数和间接效用函数是最大值函数；成本函数和支出函数是最小值函数。最小化时把 $\sup$ 换成 $\inf$，定义的核心不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An infimum is the greatest lower bound, with the same distinction between approaching and attaining it. For minimization the extended-real signs reverse: an empty feasible set has value $+\infty$, and an objective unbounded below has value $-\infty$. The economic names above describe the direction of optimization; existence of an actual maximizing or minimizing choice still needs verification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下确界是最大的下界，同样要区分“逼近”和“取得”。最小化的扩展实数符号相反：可行集为空时记为 $+\infty$，目标向下无界时记为 $-\infty$。上面的经济学名称说明优化方向；实际最大点或最小点是否存在，仍需核验。

> [!question]- A finite value · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $v(\theta)$ is finite, can one conclude that an optimal choice exists at that parameter?
>
> **Answer:** No. A finite supremum may be unattained; a separate attainment argument is still required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $v(\theta)$ 是有限数，能否据此断言该参数下存在最优选择？
>
> **答案：** 不能。有限上确界仍可能不被取得；还需要独立的取得性证明。
