# 资本技术人力资本是发展差距的近因而制度地理历史解释这些近因为何不同
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Capital, technology, and human capital are proximate causes of development gaps; institutions, geography, and history ask why those proximate causes differ*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 解释层次
> 资本存量、人力资本和生产率可以分解“产出差在计算上来自哪里”，因而是近因。制度、地理与历史解释则追问：为什么不同社会长期积累不同的资本和技术，又为什么面对不同的生产与市场条件。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Capital, human capital, and productivity decompose where output differences come from and are therefore proximate causes. Institutional, geographic, and historical explanations ask why societies persistently accumulate different amounts of those inputs and face different production and market conditions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

近因与根因不是二选一。“制度影响发展”若没有连到投资、创新、教育、市场进入或公共品等近因，就没有完成机制解释；只说“穷国资本少”，也没有解释资本为何长期不能积累。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Proximate and fundamental causes are not competitors. A claim that institutions matter is incomplete unless it connects institutions to investment, innovation, education, market entry, public goods, or another proximate mechanism. Saying only that a poor country has little capital likewise does not explain why accumulation remains low.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“根因”也是相对于当前问题的解释层次，不是已被证明的唯一终极原因。制度可能受政治权力、资源分布和历史冲击影响；地理可能同时直接影响成本，又经由历史制度间接作用。因此需要比较可并存的通道，而不是给国家贴上单一命运标签。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Fundamental” is relative to the question being asked, not proof of one ultimate cause. Institutions can themselves depend on political power, resource distribution, and historical shocks; geography can act directly on costs and indirectly through historical institutions. Competing channels must therefore be compared rather than converted into a single deterministic label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 最小自检
> “A 国穷是因为资本少”为什么只是近因解释？
>
> **答案：** 它描述了产出差的一个计算来源，却没有说明储蓄为何没有转化为长期投资、技术为何没有采用，以及风险、回报和市场条件如何形成。
