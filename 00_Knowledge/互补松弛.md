# Complementary slackness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*互补松弛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Complementary slackness** links how much room an inequality leaves to the coefficient assigned to that inequality. A constraint that has room left must have coefficient zero in a KKT triple. A positive coefficient can occur only when the constraint is binding. Both quantities may be zero: “at least one is zero” does not mean “exactly one is zero.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**互补松弛**把不等式剩余的空间与配给它的系数联系起来。在 KKT 三元组中，一条还有余量的约束必须配零系数；正系数只能出现在绑定约束上。两者也可以同时为零：“至少一个为零”不等于“恰好一个为零”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the [[KKT条件|KKT convention]] of maximizing a real objective $f$ subject to finitely many inequalities $g_i(x)\le0$ and equalities $h_j(x)=0$, with $\mathcal L=f-\lambda^Tg-\nu^Th$. The input $x^*$ is a candidate; $\lambda_i$ is its inequality multiplier. The **slack** $s_i=-g_i(x^*)$ measures the nonnegative gap to equality at a feasible input. For example, the bound $x\le5$ is $g(x)=x-5\le0$; at $x^*=3$, its slack is $5-3=2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用 [[KKT条件]]的约定：最大化实值目标 $f$，有限条不等式为 $g_i(x)\le0$、等式为 $h_j(x)=0$，并取 $\mathcal L=f-\lambda^Tg-\nu^Th$。$x^*$ 是候选输入，$\lambda_i$ 是对应不等式乘子。**松弛量** $s_i=-g_i(x^*)$ 表示可行输入到取等号还差多少，因此非负。例如 $x\le5$ 写成 $g(x)=x-5\le0$；在 $x^*=3$，松弛量为 $5-3=2$。

> [!summary] A zero product for every inequality · 每条不等式的零乘积
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under the convention of maximising subject to $g_i(x)\le0$ with $\lambda_i\ge0$, complementary slackness is $\lambda_i g_i(x^*)=0$. Equivalently, with nonnegative slack $s_i=-g_i(x^*)$, the product $\lambda_i s_i$ is zero. It is one of the four KKT classes.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在最大化、$g_i(x)\le0$、$\lambda_i\ge0$ 的约定下，互补松弛是
> $$
> \lambda_i g_i(x^*)=0.
> $$
> 若把松弛量写成 $s_i=-g_i(x^*)\ge0$，同一条件就是 $\lambda_i s_i=0$：每条不等式的乘子与松弛量至少有一个为零。这是 [[KKT条件]] 的四个组成部分之一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## How to check it constraint by constraint
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样逐条检查

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First check primal feasibility, then multiplier signs, and finally each product. Strict slack forces a zero multiplier, while a positive multiplier forces equality. The failed converse from binding to positivity is explained in [[绑定不推正乘子|the binding-versus-positive-multiplier boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先检查原问题可行性 $g_i(x^*)\le0$，再检查对偶可行性 $\lambda_i\ge0$，最后计算每个乘积。若约束有严格松弛，乘积为零会迫使乘子为零；若乘子为正，则约束必须取等号。绑定时乘子是否一定为正，见 [[绑定不推正乘子]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $i=1,\ldots,m$, if $s_i>0$, the equation $\lambda_i s_i=0$ forces $\lambda_i=0$. Conversely, if $\lambda_i>0$, division by that positive coefficient gives $s_i=0$, equivalently $g_i(x^*)=0$. But when $s_i=0$, multiplying it by either zero or a positive coefficient gives zero. Thus the **active set** $\{i:g_i(x^*)=0\}$ is read from the constraint values, not from the test $\lambda_i>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $i=1,\ldots,m$，若 $s_i>0$，方程 $\lambda_i s_i=0$ 就迫使 $\lambda_i=0$；反过来，若 $\lambda_i>0$，除以这个正系数就得到 $s_i=0$，即 $g_i(x^*)=0$。但 $s_i=0$ 时，无论另一个因子为零还是正数，乘积都为零。因此，**活动集** $\{i:g_i(x^*)=0\}$ 要从约束值读取，不能用 $\lambda_i>0$ 来定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a concrete three-constraint check, suppose a feasible candidate gives $g(x^*)=(-2,0,0)^T$. The following vectors pass the sign and product checks:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

作一个具体的三约束检查：假设某个可行候选给出 $g(x^*)=(-2,0,0)^T$。下面这些向量通过符号与乘积检查：

$$
s=(2,0,0)^T,\qquad \lambda=(0,3,0)^T,\qquad
(\lambda_1s_1,\lambda_2s_2,\lambda_3s_3)=(0,0,0).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first constraint is slack with coefficient zero; the second is binding with a positive coefficient; the third is binding with coefficient zero. Replacing the first coefficient by $1$ would produce $\lambda_1s_1=2$ and fail complementarity. These checks alone do not establish stationarity or optimality. The [[KKT条件#把完整候选代回系统|complete two-variable KKT example]] supplies an objective and checks every condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一条约束松弛且系数为零，第二条绑定且系数为正，第三条绑定但系数仍为零。如果把第一系数改成 $1$，就会得到 $\lambda_1s_1=2$，违反互补松弛。这些检查本身还没有证明驻点或最优性；[[KKT条件#把完整候选代回系统|完整二维 KKT 算例]]给出目标函数并核对全部条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equality multipliers $\nu_j$ are unrestricted in sign. Their constraints already satisfy $h_j(x^*)=0$, so the automatic identity $\nu_jh_j(x^*)=0$ does not impose an additional complementary-slackness restriction. In particular, a zero equality coefficient does not permit deleting that equality from the feasible set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

等式乘子 $\nu_j$ 不受符号限制；其约束已经要求 $h_j(x^*)=0$，因此自动成立的 $\nu_jh_j(x^*)=0$ 并没有增加另一条互补松弛限制。尤其不能因为等式系数为零，就从可行集中删除那条等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Its role in a proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它在证明中的作用

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Complementary slackness separates active and slack regimes and makes the Lagrangian value equal the original objective at the candidate. This identity is a key step in KKT sufficiency, but it must be used together with feasibility, signs, and stationarity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

互补松弛把不等式候选分成活动与松弛制度，并使最优点处的 Lagrangian 值回到原目标值。这个等式是 [[KKT充分性]] 证明中连接 Lagrangian 与原问题价值的关键一步；但它必须和可行性、符号与驻点一起使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity can be checked before claiming any optimum. At a feasible candidate, $h(x^*)=0$, and $g_i(x^*)=-s_i$. With nonnegative inequality multipliers,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个等式在声称最优之前就能核对。在可行候选处，$h(x^*)=0$、$g_i(x^*)=-s_i$；不等式乘子非负时，

$$
\mathcal L(x^*,\lambda,\nu)-f(x^*)
=-\sum_{i=1}^m\lambda_i g_i(x^*)
=\sum_{i=1}^m\lambda_i s_i\ge0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every term in this finite sum is nonnegative. Therefore its sum is zero if and only if each term is zero: under feasibility and the sign restrictions, complementarity is exactly what makes $\mathcal L(x^*,\lambda,\nu)=f(x^*)$. Without the sign restrictions, cancellation in a sum would not establish the individual zero products.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个有限和的每项都非负，因此总和为零当且仅当每项都为零：在可行性和符号限制之下，互补松弛恰好使 $\mathcal L(x^*,\lambda,\nu)=f(x^*)$。如果没有这些符号限制，总和中的抵消不能推出每个乘积分别为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any other feasible input $z$, the same fixed multipliers give $f(z)\le\mathcal L(z,\lambda,\nu)$. To conclude $f(z)\le f(x^*)$, a proof must also control the Lagrangian away from the candidate, for example through the concavity and stationarity hypotheses of [[KKT充分性|KKT sufficiency]]. A zero complementary product by itself supplies no such global comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对另一个任意可行输入 $z$，保持乘子固定可得 $f(z)\le\mathcal L(z,\lambda,\nu)$。要推出 $f(z)\le f(x^*)$，证明还必须控制候选以外的 Lagrangian，例如使用 [[KKT充分性]]中的凹性和驻点假设。单独一个互补乘积为零，并没有提供这种全局比较。

> [!question]- Check your understanding · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $g_i(x^*)=-2$ and $\lambda_i\ge0$, what value does complementary slackness require for $\lambda_i$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $g_i(x^*)=-2$ 且 $\lambda_i\ge0$，互补松弛要求 $\lambda_i$ 等于多少？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** It must be zero; otherwise the complementary product cannot vanish.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 必须有 $\lambda_i=0$，否则乘积不能为零。
