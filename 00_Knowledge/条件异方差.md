# 条件异方差是给定过去信息的条件方差随信息状态变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditional heteroskedasticity means that variance conditional on past information varies with the information state*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 假定所需条件二阶矩有限。令
> $$\mu_t=E(y_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}),\qquad \varepsilon_t=y_t-\mu_t,$$
> 并定义
> $$h_t=\operatorname{Var}(y_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})
> =E(\varepsilon_t^2\mid\mathcal F_{t-1}).$$
> 若 $h_t$ 不是一个几乎处处不变的常数，而会随 $\mathcal F_{t-1}$ 中已经知道的状态改变，就称序列具有**条件异方差**。变化的是在观察 $y_t$ 之前可预测的剩余不确定性，不是事后实现的 $\varepsilon_t^2$ 本身。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] What it is
> Assuming the required conditional second moment is finite, let $\mu_t=E(y_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})$ and $\varepsilon_t=y_t-\mu_t$, and define $h_t=\operatorname{Var}(y_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})=E(\varepsilon_t^2\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})$. Conditional heteroskedasticity means that $h_t$ varies with the information state instead of being almost surely constant. It concerns uncertainty predictable before $y_t$ arrives, not the subsequently realised square $\varepsilon_t^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这与“无条件方差随日历时间改变”不是同一件事。一个严格平稳的 GARCH 过程可以拥有不随 $t$ 改变的无条件分布，却仍在每条路径上根据最新信息更新 $h_t$。反过来，若长期方差发生结构断点，只用固定参数 GARCH 吸收它，也可能把制度变化误读成持续的条件异方差动态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is not the same as an unconditional variance that changes with calendar time. A strictly stationary GARCH process may have the same unconditional distribution at every date while updating $h_t$ along each realised path. Conversely, a structural break in the long-run variance can be mistaken for persistent conditional-variance dynamics when a fixed-parameter GARCH model is forced over the whole sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件异方差也能与原创新的零自相关共存。若 $E(\varepsilon_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})=0$，则创新方向不能由过去线性预测；但若 $h_t$ 随过去变化，创新的平方仍可预测。于是“原残差像白噪声”不能代替对平方残差的检查。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conditional heteroskedasticity can coexist with zero serial correlation in the innovations. The condition $E(\varepsilon_t\mid\mathcal F_{t-1})=0$ rules out predictable direction, while a varying $h_t$ leaves predictable structure in squared innovations. White-looking residuals therefore do not remove the need to inspect their squares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 最小例子
> 在 ARCH(1) 中，$h_t=0.2+0.5\varepsilon_{t-1}^2$。若上一期创新分别为 $0$ 与 $2$，当期条件方差分别为 $0.2$ 与 $2.2$。这两个数是在同一模型、不同已知历史下得到的风险判断；当期真正实现的平方创新仍可能高于或低于它们。

> [!question]- 自检
> 若一个序列的无条件方差恒定，能否据此排除条件异方差？
>
> **答案：** 不能。无条件方差把不同信息状态平均在一起；条件方差仍可能随历史改变。
