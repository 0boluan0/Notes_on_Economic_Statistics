# Equilibria lost during separation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*分离变量漏平衡解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dividing an equation by the unknown function can make a valid solution disappear. For $y'=2y$, the function $y(x)=0$ works at every input: its [[导数|derivative]] and the right-hand side are both zero. The divided equation $y'/y=2$ cannot even be evaluated on that solution. The loss happens at the division, before any integration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

除以未知函数，可能让一个合法解从计算中消失。对 $y'=2y$，函数 $y(x)=0$ 在每个输入处都成立：它的[[导数]]与右端都为零。但除法后的 $y'/y=2$ 根本不能在这个解上求值。解是在除法那一步丢失的，还没有轮到积分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check the zero factor in the original equation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先把因子的零点代回原方程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a [[分离变量|separable equation]] $y'=f(x)g(y)$, $x$ is the input, $y(x)$ the unknown state, and the two factors depend on $x$ and $y$ separately. Take continuous real $f$ on an open input interval $I$ and continuous real $g$ on a state interval $J$. If $y_*\in J$ satisfies $g(y_*)=0$, then the constant function $y(x)=y_*$ is an **equilibrium solution** on $I$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[分离变量|可分离方程]] $y'=f(x)g(y)$ 中，$x$ 是输入，$y(x)$ 是未知状态，两个因子分别只依赖 $x$ 与 $y$。设实函数 $f$ 在输入开区间 $I$ 上连续，$g$ 在状态区间 $J$ 上连续。若 $y_*\in J$ 满足 $g(y_*)=0$，那么常数函数 $y(x)=y_*$ 就是 $I$ 上的一个**平衡解**：

$$
y'(x)=0=f(x)g(y_*)\qquad(x\in I).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original equation must be defined at every $(x,y_*)$ used here; a zero factor cannot repair another undefined factor. This test supplies every constant state coming from a zero of $g$. If $f$ is identically zero, all allowed constant states solve the equation, so zeros of $g$ need not exhaust the constant solutions in that special case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里用到的每个 $(x,y_*)$ 都必须是原方程有定义的位置；一个零因子不能修复另一个未定义的因子。这个检验找出所有由 $g$ 的零点产生的常数状态。若 $f$ 恒为零，则全部允许的常数状态都是解，此时常数解并不限于 $g$ 的零点。

> [!important] Division has a restricted domain · 除法有自己的适用范围
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The step $y'/g(y)=f(x)$ is equivalent to the original equation only along solution intervals where $g(y(x))\ne0$ throughout. First record and verify the excluded constant states. After solving the divided equation, report those states together with the nonzero-factor branches, using an appropriate interval for each.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 步骤 $y'/g(y)=f(x)$ 只在整个解区间都满足 $g(y(x))\ne0$ 时，才与原方程等价。先记录并代回验证被排除的常数状态；解完除法后的方程，再把它们与非零因子的解支一起报告，每个解都带上适当区间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An exponential parameter can restore zero, but a logarithm cannot
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*指数参数可以补回零，对数本身不能*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y'=ry$ with fixed real $r$, the nonzero calculation gives $\ln|y|=rx+C$, where $\ln$ is the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]]. Exponentiation gives $y=Ae^{rx}$ with $A\ne0$: the sign chooses a branch and $|A|=e^C>0$. Allowing $A=0$ afterwards adds the independently checked zero solution; no finite $C$ made $e^C$ zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对固定实数 $r$ 的方程 $y'=ry$，非零分支计算得到 $\ln|y|=rx+C$，其中 $\ln$ 是[[自然对数]]。指数化得到 $y=Ae^{rx}$ 且 $A\ne0$：符号选择分支，$|A|=e^C>0$。计算结束后允许 $A=0$，才把独立核对的零解加回来；并没有某个有限 $C$ 能使 $e^C=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the restored family really is complete: the [[乘积法则|product rule]] gives $(e^{-rx}y)'=e^{-rx}(y'-ry)=0$ for any solution. By [[原函数相差常数|zero derivative on an interval]], $e^{-rx}y$ is constant. This argument covers zero without dividing by $y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里补全后的解族确实完整：对任意解，[[乘积法则]]给出 $(e^{-rx}y)'=e^{-rx}(y'-ry)=0$。由[[原函数相差常数|区间上零导数意味着常数]]，$e^{-rx}y$ 必为常数。这个论证不除以 $y$，因此包含零解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A logistic family can still miss an equilibrium
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Logistic 公式仍可能漏掉一个平衡解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the course's scaling $y'=ry(K-y)$ with $r>0$ and $K>0$. Its equilibria are $0$ and $K$. Dividing by $y(K-y)$ excludes both. On a branch avoiding them, integration and exponentiation give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用课程中的比例写法 $y'=ry(K-y)$，其中 $r>0$、$K>0$。平衡状态是 $0$ 与 $K$，除以 $y(K-y)$ 会把两者都排除。在避开它们的分支上，积分和指数化给出

$$
\frac1K\ln\left|\frac y{K-y}\right|=rx+C,
\qquad y=\frac{KAe^{rKx}}{1+Ae^{rKx}}\quad(A\ne0).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose an interval on which the denominator is nonzero. Allowing $A=0$ restores $y=0$, but no finite $A$ gives the constant $y=K$: that would require $Ae^{rKx}=1+Ae^{rKx}$. Thus $y=K$ must be listed separately. Another parameterisation may hide a different excluded case; directly substituting the equilibria is more reliable than guessing from the final formula. This is the same [[Logistic增长|logistic model]] often written $y'=\rho y(1-y/K)$, with $\rho=rK$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

需选取分母非零的区间。允许 $A=0$ 可以补回 $y=0$，但没有有限 $A$ 能给出恒等的 $y=K$：那将要求 $Ae^{rKx}=1+Ae^{rKx}$。因此必须另列 $y=K$。换一种参数化可能隐藏另一个被排除情形；直接代回平衡状态，比猜最后公式是否包含它更可靠。这与常写作 $y'=\rho y(1-y/K)$ 的[[Logistic增长|Logistic 模型]]相同，其中 $\rho=rK$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Restoring constants alone need not recover every solution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*补回常数解，也未必已经找全全部解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If local uniqueness holds, a solution that reaches an equilibrium must agree with that constant solution on their common connected interval. Without uniqueness, a solution can remain at the equilibrium and then leave. For example, for any real $c$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若局部唯一性成立，某个解一旦到达平衡状态，就必须在共同的连通区间上与该常数解一致。没有唯一性时，解可能先停在平衡状态，随后离开。例如，对任意实数 $c$，

$$
y'=3|y|^{2/3},\qquad
y_c(x)=\begin{cases}0,&x\le c,\\(x-c)^3,&x>c.\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x<c$ both sides are zero; for $x>c$ both are $3(x-c)^2$. At $c$, the difference quotient from the right is $h^2\to0$, matching the zero derivative from the left. Hence this is a differentiable solution on all of $\mathbb R$. A division by $|y|^{2/3}$ misses its whole waiting portion. Merely adjoining the constant zero solution to the separated nonzero branches does not describe this joined solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $x<c$，两边都为零；在 $x>c$，两边都为 $3(x-c)^2$。在 $c$，右侧差商为 $h^2\to0$，与左侧零导数相同，所以它是整条实轴上的可导解。除以 $|y|^{2/3}$ 会漏掉整段等待部分。仅把常数零解添到分离得到的非零分支旁边，还没有描述这种拼接解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[通解与初值|existence-and-uniqueness conditions]] determine whether such joins are possible. Equilibrium also does not mean stability: a constant trajectory can exist even when nearby trajectories move away. Division, uniqueness and stability are separate checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种拼接是否可能，要由[[通解与初值|存在唯一性条件]]判断。平衡也不等于稳定：即使附近轨迹会远离，常数轨迹本身仍然可以存在。除法、唯一性与稳定性是三项不同的检查。
