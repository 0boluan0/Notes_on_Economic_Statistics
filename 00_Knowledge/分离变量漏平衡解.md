# 分离变量时除以状态因子会删去使该因子为零的平衡解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Dividing by the state factor during separation of variables removes equilibrium solutions where that factor vanishes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 对 $y'=f(x)g(y)$，任何满足 $g(y_*)=0$ 的常数函数 $y(x)\equiv y_*$ 都是平衡解。步骤 $dy/g(y)=f(x)dx$ 只在 $g(y)\ne0$ 上合法，因此必须在除法前列出这些解，并在最后与非平衡解支合并。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $y'=f(x)g(y)$, every constant $y\equiv y_*$ with $g(y_*)=0$ is an equilibrium solution. Dividing by $g(y)$ is valid only away from those states, so list them before separation and reunite them with the non-equilibrium branches afterward.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $y'=ry$，除以 $y$ 会暂时删去 $y\equiv0$。推导出的 $y=Ae^{rx}$ 恰好可用 $A=0$ 补回它，但这只是该例参数化的幸运结果。对 Logistic 方程，除以 $y(K-y)$ 会同时删去 $y\equiv0$ 与 $y\equiv K$；不能假设最终分式自动包含二者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y'=ry$, division by $y$ temporarily removes the zero solution, which happens to be restored by setting $A=0$ in $Ae^{rx}$. In a logistic equation, division by $y(K-y)$ removes both equilibria. One must not assume the final parameterization automatically includes every deleted branch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是一般代数纪律：每次除以可能为零的表达式，都要记录被排除的情形并直接代回原方程检查。被删去的状态是否稳定是另一个问题；“是平衡解”只表示从该状态出发变化率为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a general algebraic discipline: whenever a potentially zero expression is divided out, record the excluded cases and test them in the original equation. Whether an equilibrium is stable is a separate question; equilibrium only means that the rate vanishes at that state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么 $g(y_*)=0$ 立刻给出常数解？
>
> **答案：** 取 $y(x)\equiv y_*$ 时左侧 $y'=0$，右侧 $f(x)g(y_*)=0$，原方程逐点成立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why does $g(y_*)=0$ immediately give a constant solution?
>
> **Answer:** With $y(x)\equiv y_*$, the left side is $y'=0$ and the right side is $f(x)g(y_*)=0$. The original equation is satisfied pointwise.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
