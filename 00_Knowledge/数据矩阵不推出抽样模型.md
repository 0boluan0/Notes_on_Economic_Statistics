# Data layout and the sampling model
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*数据排布与抽样模型*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A table tells us what was recorded and where it is stored. A **sampling model** describes how possible datasets are generated and which of them are more likely. Knowing that a [[多元数据矩阵|data matrix]] has $n$ rows and $p$ columns does not, by itself, tell us whether those rows are independent, whether they share one distribution, or what population an average is intended to describe.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

表格告诉我们记录了什么、记录放在哪里；**抽样模型**描述可能的数据集怎样产生，以及哪些数据集更可能出现。仅知道[[多元数据矩阵]]有 $n$ 行、$p$ 列，并不能知道这些行是否独立、是否服从同一个分布，或平均值打算描述哪个总体。

> [!example] The same two rows can arise in two different ways · 相同两行可以有不同来历
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose a measurement vector can be $a=(1,3)^T$ or $b=(3,7)^T$, each with probability $1/2$. One procedure draws two vectors independently. Another draws once and copies the result into the second row. Both procedures can produce the observed table with rows $a^T,a^T$: its probability is $1/4$ under independent drawing and $1/2$ under copying.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设测量向量等概率取 $a=(1,3)^T$ 或 $b=(3,7)^T$。一种程序独立抽取两次，另一种只抽一次，再把结果复制到第二行。两种程序都可能产生两行均为 $a^T$ 的记录，但该表在独立抽取下的概率为 $1/4$，在复制下为 $1/2$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The visible table is identical, while the two joint probability laws differ. The repeated row is therefore neither proof of dependence nor proof of independence. Information about the collection procedure and the statistical model is needed to interpret it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 可见表格完全相同，两种联合概率规律却不同。因此，重复的一行既不能单独证明依赖，也不能单独证明独立；必须结合收集程序与统计模型解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To formulate a model, let $X_i\in\mathbb R^p$ be the [[随机向量|random vector]] for observation $i$, for $i=1,\ldots,n$. The actual row $x_i^T$ records a realization. Writing
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

建立模型时，令 $X_i\in\mathbb R^p$ 为第 $i$ 份观测对应的[[随机向量]]，其中 $i=1,\ldots,n$；实际的行 $x_i^T$ 记录它的一次实现。写出

$$X_1,\ldots,X_n\overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}F$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
adds two claims: all these vectors have the same probability distribution $F$, and they are mutually independent. The [[独立同分布|i.i.d. condition]] is a property of their joint model, not of their arrangement on a screen. It does not follow from consecutive row numbers, a rectangular shape, or the use of one column name for each variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

就增加了两项主张：所有向量拥有同一个概率分布 $F$，而且它们相互独立。[[独立同分布]]是联合模型的性质，不是屏幕排列方式的性质；连续行号、矩形形状以及各列有统一变量名，都不能推出它。

## Different observation structures fit the same table
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 同一张表可以来自不同抽样结构

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adjacent rows in a time series may depend on earlier outcomes. Repeated visits by one person may share person-specific variation. Cases and controls may be sampled from different groups. Survey weights may assign different population representation to different rows. Each setting fits into an $n\times p$ array, but the observation unit, dependence, distributions and target population must be stated separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

时间序列的相邻行可能依赖此前结果；同一个人的重复就诊可能共享个体差异；病例与对照可能来自不同群体；调查权重可能让各行代表不同数量的总体单位。这些情形都能装进 $n\times p$ 数组，但观察单位、依赖、分布与目标总体都要另行说明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reordering whole rows changes their labels, not the fact that some observations came from the same person or shared a random cause. A fixed permutation of an independent family remains independent, and it cannot turn a dependent family into an independent one. Randomly shuffling rows is not a general remedy either: two exact copies remain exact copies after any shuffle. Sorting by a measured value can also change the joint law of the ordered rows; it should not be treated as a new random sample without checking the model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重排整行改变的是标签，不会消除“某些观测来自同一个人”或“共享随机原因”的事实。固定置换保留原有的整组独立性，也不能把依赖的一组变成独立的一组。随机打乱同样不是一般解决办法：两份精确复制的记录无论怎样打乱都仍是复制。按测量值排序还可能改变有序行的联合分布，不能未经模型检查就把它们视为一份新的随机样本。

## Which conclusions need which assumptions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*结论各自需要什么条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Computing a finite [[样本均值向量|sample mean]] needs only the numerical records. Saying that it is [[样本均值无偏性|unbiased]] for a target mean requires a model and suitable finite first moments; common observation means are sufficient, without independence. The general cross-block formula for its [[样本均值协方差|sampling covariance]] assumes finite second moments of the observation coordinates and retains every cross-observation covariance block. Common covariance $\Sigma$ and zero cross blocks are sufficient for the familiar $\Sigma/n$ formula; i.i.d. sampling with finite second moments is one way to ensure them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算有限的[[样本均值向量]]只需要数值记录。声称它对某个目标均值[[样本均值无偏性|无偏]]，则需要模型及适当的有限一阶矩；各观测有共同均值已足够，不要求独立。其[[样本均值协方差|抽样协方差]]的一般交叉块公式，假定各观测坐标的二阶矩有限，并保留全部观测间交叉协方差块。共同协方差 $\Sigma$ 加上交叉块为零，是常见 $\Sigma/n$ 公式的充分条件；有限二阶矩下的i.i.d.抽样是一种保证方式。

> [!question]- One hundred rows, twenty people · 一百行，二十个人
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If each of twenty people supplies five measurements, can the mean covariance be set to $\Sigma/100$ from the row count alone? No. The within-person and between-person cross covariances matter. If each person's five rows are exact copies and the twenty person-level vectors are independent with common finite covariance $\Sigma$, the answer is $\Sigma/20$. Other dependence patterns require their own calculation. Zero cross blocks and common covariance suffice for division by the row count, but that sufficient condition is not a necessary inference from the final numerical equality: cross terms can cancel.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若二十个人每人提供五次测量，能只凭行数就把均值协方差写成 $\Sigma/100$ 吗？不能，同一人内和不同人间的交叉协方差都有关。若同一人的五行是精确复制，且二十个人的向量独立、共有有限协方差 $\Sigma$，答案为 $\Sigma/20$。其他依赖方式要另算。交叉块为零且协方差相同，足以按行数相除，但不能从最终数值恰好相等反推这个充分条件必然成立，因为交叉项可能抵消。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful first step is to identify the unit represented by one row, the collection or assignment procedure, and the target population. Then state the assumptions needed by the intended estimator. [[多元数据诊断|Data diagnostics]] can reveal inconsistencies and challenge a proposed model, but matrix dimensions alone cannot establish it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际分析可以先明确一行代表的单位、数据收集或分配程序，以及目标总体，再写明拟使用估计量需要的假设。[[多元数据诊断]]能发现不一致，也能挑战所提模型，但矩阵尺寸本身不能确立模型。
