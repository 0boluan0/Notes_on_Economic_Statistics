# Reduced row echelon form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行最简形*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Reduced row echelon form**, usually shortened to **RREF**, is a way to arrange a matrix so that each independent equation has one variable isolated from the other leading variables. The remaining variables can still occur in several equations. This makes the equations easy to read without requiring the whole matrix to be an identity matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**行最简形**，简称 **RREF**，把矩阵整理成一种容易读方程的形式：每条独立方程有一个领先变量，而且其他方程中不再出现这个领先变量。剩余变量仍可以同时出现在几条方程里。因此，容易读并不意味着整个矩阵必须变成单位矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read a matrix as equations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先把矩阵读成方程*

$$R=\begin{bmatrix}1&0&2\\0&1&-3\\0&0&0\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the homogeneous system $Rx=0$, where $x=(x_1,x_2,x_3)^T$, the rows say $x_1+2x_3=0$, $x_2-3x_3=0$ and $0=0$. Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. We can choose $x_3=t$ freely and read off $x_1=-2t$, $x_2=3t$. The first nonzero entries of the two nonzero rows are their [[主元|pivots]], both equal to one. Each is alone in its column, so there is no need to solve simultaneously for $x_1$ and $x_2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对齐次方程组 $Rx=0$，其中 $x=(x_1,x_2,x_3)^T$，三行分别表示 $x_1+2x_3=0$、$x_2-3x_3=0$ 和 $0=0$；上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。任意选择 $x_3=t$，就能直接读出 $x_1=-2t$、$x_2=3t$。前两个非零行的首个非零项是它们的[[主元]]，都等于一。每个主元在所在列中独占非零位置，所以不必再联立求解 $x_1,x_2$。

> [!abstract] The defining shape · 定义这种形状的条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A finite matrix over a scalar field, such as the real or complex numbers, is in RREF when all four conditions hold:
>
> - Every zero row comes after every nonzero row.
> - In each nonzero row, its first nonzero entry is $1$.
> - These leading positions move strictly to the right as we go down the nonzero rows.
> - A leading $1$ is the only nonzero entry in its entire column.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对实数、复数等标量域上的有限矩阵，行最简形要求同时满足：
>
> - 零行全部位于非零行下方。
> - 每个非零行的首个非零项等于 $1$。
> - 从上到下，各非零行的领先位置严格向右移动。
> - 每个领先的 $1$ 是其整列中唯一的非零项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The leading positions need not be on the main diagonal or in consecutive columns. Entries in a nonpivot column need not vanish: the $2$ and $-3$ in the example are allowed. The zero matrix also meets the definition; it simply has no pivots.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

领先位置不必在主对角线上，也不必占据连续的列。非主元列不要求清零：上例中的 $2$、$-3$ 完全允许。零矩阵也满足定义，只是没有主元。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What “reduced” adds to echelon form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*“最简”比普通阶梯形多做了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary **row echelon form** puts zero rows at the bottom and makes the first nonzero positions step to the right, with zeros below each pivot. Its pivots need not equal one, and entries above a pivot may remain. For example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通**行阶梯形**把零行放在底部，让每行首个非零位置依次右移，并在主元下方清零。它不要求主元等于一，也允许主元上方还有非零项。例如

$$U=\begin{bmatrix}2&4&-8\\0&3&-9\\0&0&0\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Divide the first row by $2$ and the second by $3$. The resulting rows start with ones, but the second pivot still has a $2$ above it. Subtract twice the second row from the first:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把第一行除以 $2$、第二行除以 $3$，虽然每行都以一开头，但第二个主元上方仍有 $2$。再从第一行减去第二行的两倍：

$$
U\ \longrightarrow\
\begin{bmatrix}1&2&-4\\0&1&-3\\0&0&0\end{bmatrix}
\ \longrightarrow\
\begin{bmatrix}1&0&2\\0&1&-3\\0&0&0\end{bmatrix}=R.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are reversible [[行操作的解集不变性|elementary row operations]]. If solving a nonhomogeneous system $Ax=b$, perform every operation on the right-hand side $b$ as well, using the augmented matrix $[A\mid b]$. Changing the coefficients alone generally changes the problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里使用的是可逆的[[行操作的解集不变性|初等行操作]]。若求解非齐次方程组 $Ax=b$，必须在增广矩阵 $[A\mid b]$ 中对右端 $b$ 同步实施每一步操作；只改系数通常会改变原问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Forward [[高斯消元|elimination]] finds the staircase; normalization and clearing above the pivots produce RREF. The process terminates because each new pivot uses a later column and a new row. The precise existence and uniqueness statement is given in [[RREF存在唯一性|the RREF theorem]]. Different legal elimination routes give the same final RREF in exact arithmetic, though ordinary echelon forms can differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

向前的[[高斯消元|消元]]找到阶梯结构，随后归一化并清除主元上方元素，得到 RREF。每个新主元都使用更靠后的列和新的一行，所以过程会结束。精确的存在性与唯一性见[[RREF存在唯一性]]：精确运算下，不同合法消元路线得到同一个 RREF，而普通阶梯形未必相同。

> [!warning] A solved shape may display a contradiction · 整理完也可能看见矛盾
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In testing the shape of an augmented matrix, include its last column. The matrix below is in RREF but represents $x_1=0$ and $0=1$, so its system has no solution. The last column is a right-hand side, not an extra unknown.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 检查增广矩阵的形状时，最后一列也要计入。下面这个矩阵是 RREF，却表示 $x_1=0$ 和 $0=1$，因此方程组无解。最后一列是右端，不是额外的未知量。
>
> $$\left[\begin{array}{cc|c}1&0&0\\0&0&1\end{array}\right].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once consistency is known, [[主元与自由变量|pivot and free-variable counting]] explains the number of parameters. To extract a basis of a particular space, use [[RREF读取子空间基|the rule for that space]]: different spaces require different information from the reduction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

确认相容之后，[[主元与自由变量]]解释参数的个数。若要提取某个空间的基，则使用[[RREF读取子空间基|对应空间的取基规则]]；不同空间需要读取消元结果中的不同信息。
