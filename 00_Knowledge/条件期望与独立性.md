# 条件期望与独立性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditional expectation and independence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 在一部分信息已经知道时重新计算随机变量的平均，并用“信息是否改变分布”区分独立、条件独立和不相关。
> **具体锚点：** 知道今天是否下雨后，通勤时间的平均值会改变；$E[T\mid R]$ 是随天气状态变化的随机变量，而不是一个固定数字。
> **核心难点：** 条件期望是已知信息的函数；独立是联合分布分解，不相关只消除一个二阶线性量。
> **为什么重要：** 回归函数、Bayes 更新、鞅、方差分解和预测都建立在条件期望上。
> **继续：** 事件层面的条件概率见 [[概率空间、条件概率与 Bayes 法则]]；时间信息流见 [[鞅、停时与可选停止]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It recalculates the mean of a random variable after some information is known, and distinguishes independence, conditional independence, and lack of correlation by asking whether information changes a distribution.
> **Concrete anchor:** Once we know whether it is raining, the expected commuting time changes. $E[T\mid R]$ is a random variable that varies with the weather state, not a single fixed number.
> **Central difficulty:** Conditional expectation is a function of the information being conditioned on. Independence is factorisation of a joint distribution, while lack of correlation removes only one second-order linear quantity.
> **Why it matters:** Regression functions, Bayesian updating, martingales, variance decompositions, and forecasting are all built on conditional expectation.
> **Continue with:** For event-level conditioning, see [[概率空间、条件概率与 Bayes 法则|probability spaces, conditional probability, and Bayes' rule]]. For time-indexed information, see [[鞅、停时与可选停止|martingales, stopping times, and optional stopping]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 独立、不相关与条件期望
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Independence, lack of correlation, and conditional expectation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

独立要求联合分布分解，通常推出不相关；反向一般不成立。条件期望 $E[X\mid Y]$ 是 Y 的函数，满足迭代期望 $E[E(X\mid Y)]=E(X)$，是回归与鞅的核心。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Independence requires the joint distribution to factorise and usually implies zero correlation; the converse generally fails. The conditional expectation $E[X\mid Y]$ is a function of $Y$ and satisfies the law of iterated expectations, $E[E(X\mid Y)]=E(X)$. It is central to regression and martingale theory.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

严格说，$E[X\mid Y]$ 是所有只依赖 Y 的可测函数中，对 X 的均方误差最小预测。它不要求 X 是 Y 的线性函数；线性回归只是把候选预测函数限制在线性类中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More precisely, $E[X\mid Y]$ is the minimum-mean-square predictor of $X$ among all measurable functions of $Y$. It does not require $X$ to be linear in $Y$; linear regression merely restricts the candidate predictor to a linear class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 条件化与全期望
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditioning and total expectation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按一个能简化问题的随机量 Y 分层：$E[X]=E[E(X\mid Y)]$、$P(A)=\sum_yP(A\mid Y=y)P(Y=y)$。好的条件变量能让剩余试验独立或对称。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Condition on a random quantity $Y$ that simplifies the problem: $E[X]=E[E(X\mid Y)]$ and $P(A)=\sum_yP(A\mid Y=y)P(Y=y)$. A well-chosen conditioning variable can make the remaining experiment independent or symmetric.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全方差公式进一步把不确定性拆成组内和组间两部分：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The law of total variance further separates uncertainty into within-group and between-group components:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[\operatorname{Var}(X\mid Y)]+\operatorname{Var}(E[X\mid Y]).
$$

第一项是知道 Y 后仍剩下的平均噪声，第二项是不同 Y 组的条件均值变化。若忘记第二项，就会系统性低估总体方差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term is the average noise that remains after learning $Y$; the second is variation in conditional means across values of $Y$. Omitting the second term systematically understates the overall variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：随机和
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: a random sum*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设顾客数 $N\sim Poisson(\lambda)$，每位顾客消费 $Y_i$ 独立同分布，均值为 $\mu$、方差为 $\sigma^2$，且与 N 独立。总销售额 $S=\sum_{i=1}^N Y_i$。先给定 N：$E[S\mid N]=N\mu$、$\operatorname{Var}(S\mid N)=N\sigma^2$；再去条件得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the number of customers be $N\sim Poisson(\lambda)$. Each customer's expenditure $Y_i$ is iid with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$, and is independent of $N$. Total sales are $S=\sum_{i=1}^N Y_i$. Conditional on $N$, $E[S\mid N]=N\mu$ and $\operatorname{Var}(S\mid N)=N\sigma^2$. Removing the conditioning gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
E[S]=\lambda\mu,\qquad \operatorname{Var}(S)=\lambda\sigma^2+\lambda\mu^2.
$$

方差中的第二项来自顾客数量本身波动。只写 $\lambda\sigma^2$ 等于假装 N 固定，是典型的漏项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second variance term comes from randomness in the number of customers. Writing only $\lambda\sigma^2$ effectively treats $N$ as fixed and is a standard omitted-component error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 条件独立与解释边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditional independence and limits of interpretation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

X 与 Y 在给定 Z 后条件独立，表示联合条件分布可分解为 $p(x,y\mid z)=p(x\mid z)p(y\mid z)$。无条件相关可能在控制共同原因 Z 后消失；无条件独立也可能在条件化碰撞点后被破坏。因而“加控制变量”不是单调增加可信度的机械动作。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conditional independence of $X$ and $Y$ given $Z$ means that the conditional joint distribution factorises as $p(x,y\mid z)=p(x\mid z)p(y\mid z)$. An unconditional association may disappear after conditioning on a common cause, while unconditional independence may be destroyed by conditioning on a collider. Therefore, adding control variables is not a mechanical operation that always improves credibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释迭代期望。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain the law of iterated expectations in your own words.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 先在每个已知信息组内求平均，再按这些组出现的概率加权，得到总体平均。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> First take the average within each information group, then weight those group averages by how often the groups occur to recover the overall average.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### $E[X\mid Y]$ 为什么是随机变量而不是常数？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is $E[X\mid Y]$ a random variable rather than a constant?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它是 Y 的函数；样本点不同会产生不同 Y，从而给出不同条件均值。只有再对 Y 取期望才回到常数 $E[X]$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It is a function of $Y$. Different outcomes produce different values of $Y$ and therefore different conditional means. Taking expectation over $Y$ returns the constant $E[X]$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 条件独立能否由零条件协方差推出？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Can conditional independence be inferred from zero conditional covariance?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 一般不能。零协方差仍只排除条件分布内的线性共同变化；需要更强分布假设或直接证明条件联合分解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Generally no. Zero covariance still removes only linear co-movement within each conditional distribution. Stronger distributional assumptions or direct factorisation of the conditional joint distribution are required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
