# The column picture describes a column combination while the column space collects all attainable outputs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*列图像解释列的线性组合而列空间收集所有可达输出*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $A=[a_1\;\cdots\;a_n]\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$, the **column picture** interprets $Ax=b$ as $x_1a_1+\cdots+x_na_n=b$. The [[列空间|column space]] $C(A)=\{Ax:x\in\mathbb R^n\}$ is the set of all possible outputs as the coefficients vary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $A=[a_1\;\cdots\;a_n]\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，**列图像**把 $Ax=b$ 解释为 $x_1a_1+\cdots+x_na_n=b$。[[列空间]] $C(A)=\{Ax:x\in\mathbb R^n\}$ 则收集系数变化时的全部输出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed target $b$, the question is whether the column combination can reach it, equivalently $b\in C(A)$. The coefficient vector $x$ lives in $\mathbb R^n$; the target and column space live in $\mathbb R^m$. The two views are connected, but one names an interpretation and the other a mathematical set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定目标 $b$ 后，问题是列组合能否到达它，等价于 $b\in C(A)$。系数向量 $x$ 位于 $\mathbb R^n$，目标与列空间位于 $\mathbb R^m$。二者互相联系，但一个是解释视角，一个是数学集合。

$$\nA=\begin{pmatrix}1&3\\2&3\\4&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad A\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}4\\5\\5\end{pmatrix}.\n$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This one combination is a column-picture example. Allowing every pair of coefficients gives the entire plane spanned by the two independent columns, not merely those two arrows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一组系数构成一个列图像例子；允许任意系数对，得到两条独立列向量张成的整个平面，而不只是两根箭头。
