# 证券监管旨在保护投资者维护公平有效透明的市场并降低系统性风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Securities regulation aims to protect investors, maintain fair, efficient and transparent markets, and reduce systemic risk.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评价一项证券规则，先说明它要改善哪个问题：披露要求帮助投资者获得重要信息，行为监管约束欺诈和操纵，交易与金融基础设施安排则关系到市场秩序和风险传播。这些目标是评价标准，不是监管已经保证实现的结果；投资者保护也不等于保证证券不跌价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Evaluate a rule by identifying its intended problem: disclosure supplies material information, conduct regulation constrains fraud and manipulation, and trading and infrastructure arrangements affect order and risk transmission. These are objectives, not guaranteed outcomes; investor protection does not insure against falling security prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一规则还应检查副作用和适用条件。例如，提高抵押品要求可以增加履约缓冲，却也可能加大压力期现金需求，具体机制见[[保证金顺周期]]；[[发行注册与投资价值]]说明为什么审核发行并不替投资者作价值判断。不能只写“更严格所以更安全”，也不能只凭合规成本就断言监管无益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check conditions and side effects as well. More collateral can strengthen performance buffers while increasing stressed funding needs; see [[保证金顺周期|margin procyclicality]]. [[发行注册与投资价值|Registration and investment merit]] explains why issuance review does not replace valuation. Neither stricter rules nor higher compliance costs alone settle the welfare question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
