# 国债期货交割货款按期货交割结算价乘转换因子再加应计利息并换算面值计算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Treasury futures invoice multiplies the delivery settlement quote by the conversion factor, adds accrued interest, and scales to delivered face value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对采用转换因子交割的国债期货，先把三个量放到同一报价口径：期货交割结算价 $F_{d}$ 按每百元名义面值报价，可交割券的 CF 无量纲，交割日应计利息 $AI_{100}$ 也按每百元实际券面值计算。交付面值为 $Q$ 的债券时，货款为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For Treasury futures using conversion-factor delivery, align the quote units: $F_d$ is the delivery settlement quote per 100 nominal face value, CF is dimensionless, and $AI_{100}$ is accrued interest per 100 face value of the delivered bond. For delivered face value $Q$, the invoice is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\mathrm{Invoice}=\frac{Q}{100}\left(F_d\times CF+AI_{100}\right).$$

若 $F_d=102$、$CF=0.98$、$AI_{100}=1.20$，每百元货款是 $102\times0.98+1.20=101.16$；交付面值 1,000,000，则货款为 1,011,600。CF 只乘净价部分，不把应计利息再乘一次；利息的日计数与交割日归属依该合约确定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $F_d=102$, $CF=0.98$, and $AI_{100}=1.20$, the invoice per 100 is 101.16. Delivered face value of 1,000,000 gives 1,011,600. Apply CF to the quoted clean-price component, not again to accrued interest. Day count and delivery-date entitlement follow the contract.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

货款是**以债券换取现金的交割金额**，不是期货头寸从建仓以来的总损益。[[期货合约|此前逐日结算的盈亏]]已经发生；若分析买券交割策略，还要把买券全价、持有利息、融资成本与期货结算现金流分别列入，不能把交割货款直接当成利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The invoice is the cash exchanged for the delivered bond, not total futures profit since inception. Earlier [[期货合约|daily settlements]] have already occurred. A cash-and-delivery strategy also requires the bond purchase price, coupon receipts, financing, and futures settlements; invoice cash is not profit by itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
