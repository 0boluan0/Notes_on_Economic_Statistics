# 汇率传递是汇率变化进入本币进口价格及消费者价格的程度与过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exchange-rate pass-through is the extent and process by which exchange-rate changes enter domestic import and consumer prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

汇率传递首先问汇率怎样改变本币计价的进口价格，再问进口品和进口投入怎样进入最终消费价格。两阶段可以有不同幅度和时滞，不能把进口边境价变动直接当作整体通胀的同幅变动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pass-through first concerns domestic-currency import prices, then how imported goods and inputs enter final consumer prices. The stages can differ in magnitude and timing; a border-price change is not an equal change in aggregate inflation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定[[汇率报价方向]]为 $S=$ 本币／外币。若外币发票价 $P^*$ 不变，无额外税费，则边境价 $P_M=SP^*$。例如 $P^*=100$，$S$ 从 7 到 7.7，进口价从 700 到 770，上涨 10%。这是固定外币价格下的换算，不是所有合同的一般传递系数：本币发票、供应商调价或利润率吸收都可能改变结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix [[汇率报价方向|the quotation]] at domestic currency per foreign unit. With an unchanged foreign-currency invoice price and no additional taxes or fees, $P_M=SP^*$. At $P^*=100$, a rise in $S$ from 7 to 7.7 raises the border price from 700 to 770, or 10%. This is a fixed-invoice conversion, not a universal coefficient; domestic-currency invoicing, supplier repricing and margins can alter transmission.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

继续构造同一商品：零售价原为 1000，其中进口成本 700，其余国内成本及利润金额合计 300。若后者保持不变且进口成本全额转入，零售价为 1070，只涨 7%。再假定该类商品在固定数量消费篮子的期初支出权重为 20%，其余价格不变，则篮子价格指数从 100 到 101.4，上涨 1.4%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Extend the example: an initial retail price of 1000 comprises 700 of imports and 300 of domestic costs and profit. Holding the latter amount fixed and passing on the entire import-cost rise gives 1070, a 7% increase. If these goods have an initial 20% expenditure weight in a fixed-quantity basket and all other prices are unchanged, its price index rises from 100 to 101.4, or 1.4%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是一轮价格水平变化，不表示以后每年都额外通胀 1.4%。实际判断还要检查冲击持续性、进口中间品、成本与加价反应，以及采用同比还是环比通胀。汇率改变商品需求的另一条链见[[汇率与支出切换]]；本卡不由央行加息直接推定汇率方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a one-time price-level change, not an additional 1.4% inflation every subsequent year. Assess persistence, imported intermediates, costs, markups and the inflation horizon. [[汇率与支出切换|Expenditure switching]] covers the demand channel. This card does not infer an exchange-rate direction directly from a policy hike.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
