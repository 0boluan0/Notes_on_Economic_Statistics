# Testing the endpoints of a power series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*幂级数端点判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finding a power series' radius settles every input strictly inside or outside it. Two inputs can still remain undecided: the left and right endpoints. To decide them, substitute each endpoint into the original series and examine the resulting numerical sum. A test that succeeded in the interior may give no conclusion on the boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求出幂级数半径后，严格位于内部和外部的输入就已经判定了，但还可能剩下两个未决定的输入：左端点与右端点。要处理它们，应分别代回原级数，判断得到的数项级数。内部有效的判别，在边界可能没有结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real [[幂级数|power series]] $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(x-a)^n$ with [[幂级数收敛半径|radius]] $0<R<\infty$, the two inputs are $x=a-R$ and $x=a+R$. Substitution produces $\sum c_n(-R)^n$ and $\sum c_nR^n$. Between them, on $(a-R,a+R)$, the series already converges absolutely. Outside the closed interval, it diverges. If $R=0$, check only the centre, where the sum is $c_0$; if $R=\infty$, every real input converges and there are no finite endpoints to test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实数[[幂级数]] $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(x-a)^n$，若[[幂级数收敛半径|半径]]满足 $0<R<\infty$，两个输入就是 $x=a-R$ 与 $x=a+R$，代入后得到 $\sum c_n(-R)^n$ 与 $\sum c_nR^n$。两者之间的 $(a-R,a+R)$ 已经绝对收敛，闭区间之外则发散。若 $R=0$，只需直接看中心，和为 $c_0$；若 $R=\infty$，每个实数输入都收敛，没有有限端点需要判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Follow one shifted example all the way to its interval
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把一个移位例子完整判到区间*

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(x-2)^n}{n3^n}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The centre is $a=2$, and the coefficient of $(x-2)^n$ is $1/(n3^n)$ for $n\ge1$; the constant coefficient is zero. Write the entire term as $u_n(x)=(x-2)^n/(n3^n)$. For a fixed $x\ne2$, the [[级数比值判别|absolute term ratio]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中心为 $a=2$，$n\ge1$ 时 $(x-2)^n$ 的系数为 $1/(n3^n)$，常数项系数为零。把完整通项记为 $u_n(x)=(x-2)^n/(n3^n)$。固定 $x\ne2$ 时，[[级数比值判别|通项绝对值之比]]为

$$
\left|\frac{u_{n+1}(x)}{u_n(x)}\right|
=\frac{|x-2|}{3}\frac{n}{n+1}\longrightarrow\frac{|x-2|}{3}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is less than $1$ in the limit when $|x-2|<3$, and greater than $1$ when $|x-2|>3$. At the excluded centre $x=2$, every term is zero, so convergence is immediate without dividing by zero. Thus $R=3$, with candidate endpoints $-1$ and $5$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$|x-2|<3$ 时比值极限小于 $1$，$|x-2|>3$ 时大于 $1$。刚才排除的中心 $x=2$ 使每项都为零，直接得到收敛，不需要除以零。因此 $R=3$，待查端点为 $-1$ 和 $5$。

> [!example] The two substitutions produce different series / 两次代入得到不同级数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At the left endpoint $x=-1$, $(x-2)^n=(-3)^n$, giving $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^n/n$. Its magnitudes $1/n$ decrease to zero, so the [[交错级数判别|alternating-series test]] proves convergence. Its magnitude sum is the divergent harmonic series, so this convergence is [[条件收敛|conditional]].
>
> At the right endpoint $x=5$, $(x-2)^n=3^n$, giving $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}1/n$. Its partial sums are the [[调和数对数增长|harmonic numbers]], which grow without bound, so it diverges. The full interval of convergence is therefore $[-1,5)$, while absolute convergence holds exactly on $(-1,5)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 左端点 $x=-1$ 使 $(x-2)^n=(-3)^n$，得到 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^n/n$。幅度 $1/n$ 递减趋零，故[[交错级数判别]]保证收敛；绝对值之和却是发散的调和级数，因此属于[[条件收敛]]。
>
> 右端点 $x=5$ 使 $(x-2)^n=3^n$，得到 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}1/n$。其部分和是无界增长的[[调和数对数增长|调和数]]，所以发散。完整收敛区间因此是 $[-1,5)$，绝对收敛则恰在 $(-1,5)$ 成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At both endpoints, the ratio limit is exactly $1$. That result means the ratio test is inconclusive; it does not mean that the series diverges, converges, or is conditionally convergent. Those conclusions came from the substituted series themselves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两端的比值极限都恰为 $1$。这只表示比值判别没有结论，既不表示发散，也不表示收敛或条件收敛。上述结论来自代入后的具体级数。

## Which endpoint patterns are possible?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*端点可能有哪些组合？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For radius $1$ and centre $0$, $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n$ diverges at both endpoints. The series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x^n/n^2$ converges absolutely at both by the [[p级数判别|p-series test]]. The series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x^n/n$ converges only at the left endpoint, while $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^n x^n/n$ converges only at the right endpoint. Thus all four open/closed endpoint patterns can occur; the full interval need not be symmetric about the centre.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取半径 $1$、中心 $0$：$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n$ 两端都发散；$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x^n/n^2$ 由 [[p级数判别]]知两端都绝对收敛；$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}x^n/n$ 只有左端点收敛；$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^n x^n/n$ 则只有右端点收敛。因此左右开闭的四种组合都可能出现，完整区间不必关于中心对称。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is nevertheless a useful constraint on absolute convergence: the two endpoint magnitude series are identical, since $|c_n(-R)^n|=|c_nR^n|$. Consequently, if one endpoint converges absolutely, so does the other. Separate testing is essential for ordinary convergence because the signs can differ; it does not mean that all aspects of the two tests are unrelated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不过，绝对收敛有一个约束：两端的绝对值级数完全相同，因为 $|c_n(-R)^n|=|c_nR^n|$。所以，一端绝对收敛，另一端也一定绝对收敛。普通收敛需要分别判断，是因为符号可能不同，并非两个端点之间毫无联系。

## Retest after changing the series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*级数变化后，再查端点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorems for [[幂级数逐项求导|termwise differentiation]] and [[幂级数逐项积分|termwise integration]] give the same radius and the corresponding function identities in the strict interior when the original radius is positive. They do not automatically preserve endpoint behaviour. For example, integrating $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n$ from the centre gives $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^{n+1}/(n+1)=\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}x^m/m$. At $x=-1$, the original geometric series diverges, but the integrated series converges conditionally. Differentiating the latter recovers the geometric series and loses that endpoint again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原半径为正时，[[幂级数逐项求导]]与[[幂级数逐项积分]]定理保证半径相同，并在严格内部给出对应的函数等式，却不自动保留端点敛散性。例如，把 $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n$ 从中心积分，得到 $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^{n+1}/(n+1)=\sum_{m=1}^{\infty}x^m/m$。在 $x=-1$，原几何级数发散，积分后的级数却条件收敛；对后者求导，回到几何级数，又失去这个端点。

> [!warning] A function value is not an endpoint convergence proof / 函数有值不等于端点级数收敛
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The identity $1/(1+x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-x)^n$ holds for $|x|<1$. Although the fraction equals $1/2$ at $x=1$, the substituted series is $1-1+1-1+\cdots$, whose partial sums alternate between $1$ and $0$. Its ordinary sum does not exist. If an endpoint series does converge, identifying its sum with a proposed function value still needs an endpoint argument, such as the finite-remainder argument in [[对数函数幂级数|the logarithm expansion]]. An interior identity alone states no equality at an excluded endpoint.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $1/(1+x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-x)^n$ 在 $|x|<1$ 成立。虽然分式在 $x=1$ 等于 $1/2$，代入后的级数却为 $1-1+1-1+\cdots$，部分和在 $1$ 与 $0$ 之间交替，通常意义下的和不存在。即使某个端点级数收敛，要把它的和认定为某个目标函数值，仍需端点论证，例如[[对数函数幂级数]]使用的有限余项证明。内部恒等式本身并未陈述被排除端点上的等式。
