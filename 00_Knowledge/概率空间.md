# Probability space
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*概率空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **probability space** is a complete mathematical model of an uncertain experiment. It tells us what outcomes are possible, which collections of outcomes we can ask about, and what probabilities those collections receive. For a coin toss, listing “heads or tails” answers only the first question: we still need to say which events we consider and how likely they are.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**概率空间**是一套完整描述随机试验的数学模型。它告诉我们：哪些结果可能出现，哪些结果集合可以拿来提问，以及这些集合分别有多大概率。对于一次抛硬币，只列出“正面或反面”仅回答了第一个问题；还要说明考虑哪些事件，以及它们的概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From an outcome to a question about outcomes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从一次结果，到对结果提出的问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **sample space**, written $\Omega$ (capital omega), is the set of possible outcomes. For one coin toss, take $\Omega=\{H,T\}$, where $H$ means heads and $T$ means tails. An individual outcome is often written $\omega$ (lowercase omega); in this model it can be $H$ or $T$. Once the experiment takes place, exactly one of these outcomes occurs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**样本空间**记为 $\Omega$，读作大写 omega，是所有可能结果组成的集合。一次抛硬币可以取 $\Omega=\{H,T\}$，其中 $H$ 表示正面，$T$ 表示反面。单个结果常记为小写的 $\omega$；在这个模型中，它可以是 $H$ 或 $T$。试验实际进行后，其中恰好一个结果发生。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **event** collects outcomes satisfying a description. The event “heads occurs” is the one-element set $\{H\}$; “heads or tails occurs” is $\{H,T\}=\Omega$; the empty set $\varnothing$ contains no outcomes. An event occurs when the realised outcome belongs to it. This is why $H$ and $\{H\}$ play different roles: one is an outcome, and the other is a set used as an event.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**事件**把符合某个描述的结果收集起来。“出现正面”这个事件是只含一个结果的集合 $\{H\}$；“出现正面或反面”对应 $\{H,T\}=\Omega$；空集 $\varnothing$ 则不含任何结果。当实际结果落入某个事件中，就说该事件发生了。因此 $H$ 与 $\{H\}$ 的角色不同：前者是结果，后者是作为事件使用的集合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The event family $\mathcal F$ specifies which subsets of $\Omega$ are included as events. It must be a [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]]: a collection that remains valid when we take complements (“not this event”) or combine countably many events (“at least one occurs”). The function $P$ then takes an event as input and returns its probability. Such a function is called a **probability measure** when it satisfies the [[概率三公理|probability axioms]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

事件族 $\mathcal F$ 规定 $\Omega$ 的哪些子集被纳入模型、作为事件。它必须是一个[[σ-代数]]：对其中的事件取补集（“这个事件没有发生”），或合并可数多个事件（“至少有一个发生”），得到的集合仍在其中。然后，函数 $P$ 以事件为输入，给出它的概率。满足[[概率三公理]]的这种函数叫作**概率测度**。

> [!abstract] The three parts of the model · 模型的三个部分
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A probability space is the triple
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 概率空间是下面的三元组：
>
> $$(\Omega,\mathcal F,P),\qquad P:\mathcal F\longrightarrow[0,1].$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $\Omega$ contains outcomes; $\mathcal F$ contains events, which are sets of outcomes; $P$ assigns numbers to those events. The arrow says that $P$ takes an element of $\mathcal F$ and returns a number between $0$ and $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\Omega$ 里面装的是结果，$\mathcal F$ 里面装的是事件——也就是结果的集合，$P$ 则给这些事件分配数字。箭头表示 $P$ 接受一个 $\mathcal F$ 中的事件，返回 $0$ 到 $1$ 之间的数。

^core

> [!example] A complete fair-coin model · 完整的公平硬币模型
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a fair coin, use all four subsets of $\Omega$ as events:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 公平硬币模型把 $\Omega$ 的四个子集全部作为事件：
>
> $$\Omega=\{H,T\},\qquad \mathcal F=\{\varnothing,\{H\},\{T\},\Omega\}.$$
> $$P(\varnothing)=0,\quad P(\{H\})=\frac12,\quad P(\{T\})=\frac12,\quad P(\Omega)=1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The statement $\{H\}\in\mathcal F$ says that heads is an event included in the model. The different statement $P(\{H\})=1/2$ assigns its probability. The symbol $\in$ means “is an element of”.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\{H\}\in\mathcal F$ 说的是“正面”是模型纳入的事件；$P(\{H\})=1/2$ 说的是给这个事件分配多少概率。符号 $\in$ 表示“是其中的一个元素”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same $\Omega$ and $\mathcal F$ can model a biased coin by replacing the two probabilities with $0.8$ and $0.2$. Listing two outcomes does not make them equally likely. For finite or countable spaces with every subset included, probabilities can be specified outcome by outcome and added; that construction is explained in [[离散概率空间|discrete probability spaces]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样的 $\Omega$ 与 $\mathcal F$，把正反面的概率改成 $0.8$ 与 $0.2$，就得到一枚有偏硬币的模型。只有两种结果，并不意味着二者等可能。在有限或可数的样本空间中，若所有子集都被纳入，可以逐个指定结果的概率，再相加得到事件概率；这个构造见[[离散概率空间]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the sample space and event family are separate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么样本空间与事件族要分开*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine a die whose exact face is hidden and whose display reports only odd or even. We can retain $\Omega=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$ while using an event family containing only the empty event, the odd faces, the even faces, and the whole space. It distinguishes parity but does not distinguish $1$ from $3$. This is a smaller valid sigma-algebra; the [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra card]] checks why it works.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设想骰子的具体点数被遮住，显示器只告诉我们奇偶。可以仍取 $\Omega=\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$，但让事件族只包含空事件、奇数点集合、偶数点集合和全集。这个事件族能区分奇偶，却不能区分 $1$ 与 $3$。它是一个较小但合法的 σ-代数，[[σ-代数|相应卡片]]会逐项检查为什么它成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An excluded subset is not thereby impossible: it simply has no assigned probability within that chosen event family. More detailed models can use larger families. On uncountable spaces, such as an interval of possible measurements, suitable sigma-algebras also let us assign probabilities consistently to intervals and other events without requiring a probability for every conceivable subset. The triple keeps the possible outcomes, the chosen events, and their probability assignment explicit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

某个子集没有被事件族纳入，并不说明它不可能发生，只说明在选定的事件族中没有给它分配概率。更精细的模型可以使用更大的事件族。对于一个测量值区间这样的不可数空间，适当的 σ-代数还让我们能一致地给区间及其他事件分配概率，而不必要求每一个可以想象的子集都有概率。三元组把可能结果、选定事件和概率分配分别写清楚。
