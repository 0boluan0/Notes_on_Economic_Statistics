# 概率空间由样本空间、事件的 σ-代数和概率测度组成

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A probability space consists of outcomes, a sigma-algebra of events, and a probability measure*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 概率空间为 $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$：$\Omega$ 包含所有可能结果；[[σ-代数|$\mathcal F$]]收集允许赋概率的事件；$P:\mathcal F\to[0,1]$ 为每个事件赋概率，并满足[[概率三公理]]。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A probability space is a triple: the sample space of outcomes, a [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]] of events, and a probability measure satisfying the [[概率三公理|probability axioms]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

公平硬币取 $\Omega=\{H,T\}$、$\mathcal F=\{\varnothing,\{H\},\{T\},\Omega\}$。$H$ 是结果，$\{H\}$ 是事件，$\mathcal F$ 是事件的集合。$P(\{H\})=1/2$，函数 $P$ 的输入是事件。有限模型的求和表示见[[离散概率空间]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fair coin, $H$ is an outcome, $\{H\}$ is an event, and $\mathcal F$ is a collection of events. The input to $P$ is an event. See [[离散概率空间|discrete probability spaces]] for the finite-model summation rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
