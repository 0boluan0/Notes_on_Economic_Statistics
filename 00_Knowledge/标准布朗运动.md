# Standard Brownian motion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*标准布朗运动*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Standard Brownian motion is a model of continuous random motion: the position starts at zero, and each new time interval contributes a Gaussian displacement independent of the displacements on nonoverlapping intervals. Longer intervals bring more uncertainty. “Standard” fixes the scale so that an interval of length one contributes variance one.

It is a [[随机过程|stochastic process]] $W=(W_t)_{t\ge0}$: at a fixed time $t$, $W_t$ is a random position; after an outcome $\omega$ has occurred, $t\mapsto W_t(\omega)$ is one entire [[样本路径|sample path]]. These are two views of the same object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准布朗运动是连续随机运动的一种模型：位置从零开始，每个新时间段贡献一个 Gaussian 位移，互不重叠时间段的位移彼此独立。时间越长，累积的不确定性越大。“标准”固定了尺度：长度为一的时间段贡献方差一。

它是一个[[随机过程]] $W=(W_t)_{t\ge0}$：固定时刻 $t$ 时，$W_t$ 是随机位置；一次结果 $\omega$ 发生后，$t\mapsto W_t(\omega)$ 是整条[[样本路径]]。这是同一个对象的两种看法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the change over an interval
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先看一段时间内的变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **increment** $W_t-W_s$ is the change between times $s$ and $t$. In the notation [[正态分布|$N(\mu,v)$]], the second parameter $v$ is the [[方差|variance]], not the standard deviation. For Brownian motion,

$$W_t-W_s\sim N(0,t-s)\qquad(0\le s<t).$$

Thus the increment has mean zero and [[标准差|standard deviation]] $\sqrt{t-s}$. Zero mean balances positive and negative displacements over repeated realizations; it does not say the position stays still.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**增量** $W_t-W_s$ 就是从时刻 $s$ 到 $t$ 的变化量。在[[正态分布|$N(\mu,v)$]] 的记号中，第二个参数 $v$ 是[[方差]]，不是标准差。布朗增量满足上式，所以其均值为零、[[标准差]]为 $\sqrt{t-s}$。均值为零表示重复实现时正负位移相互平衡，不表示位置保持不动。

> [!example] Two successive intervals · 两段连续的时间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> From time $0$ to $1$, the displacement is $W_1\sim N(0,1)$. From time $1$ to $4$, the new displacement is $W_4-W_1\sim N(0,3)$ and is independent of the first. Their sum is $W_4\sim N(0,4)$, with standard deviation $2$. The **positions** $W_1$ and $W_4$ are dependent: the later position includes the earlier displacement.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $0$ 到 $1$ 的位移为 $W_1\sim N(0,1)$；从 $1$ 到 $4$ 的新位移为 $W_4-W_1\sim N(0,3)$，并且与第一段独立。两者相加得到 $W_4\sim N(0,4)$，标准差为 $2$。但**位置** $W_1,W_4$ 依赖，因为后一个位置包含前一段的位移。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The complete definition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*完整定义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A real-valued process is standard Brownian motion when the following requirements hold together. “Almost surely” means outside an event of probability zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实值过程同时满足下面的要求时，才是标准布朗运动。“几乎必然”指可以排除一个概率为零的事件。

> [!important] Starting point, increments and paths · 起点、增量与路径
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The process starts at zero: $P(W_0=0)=1$. For every finite ordered choice $0=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_n$, the increments
>
> $$W_{t_1}-W_{t_0},\ldots,W_{t_n}-W_{t_{n-1}}$$
>
> are [[相互独立|mutually independent]], and every increment over $s<t$ has law $N(0,t-s)$. Finally, there is one event $\Omega_0$ with $P(\Omega_0)=1$ such that, for every $\omega\in\Omega_0$, the whole function $t\mapsto W_t(\omega)$ is continuous on $[0,\infty)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 过程从零开始：$P(W_0=0)=1$。对每一组有限的有序时刻 $0=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_n$，上列增量[[相互独立]]，并且每段 $s<t$ 的增量都服从 $N(0,t-s)$。最后，存在**同一个**事件 $\Omega_0$，满足 $P(\Omega_0)=1$，使其中每个 $\omega$ 对应的整条函数 $t\mapsto W_t(\omega)$ 都在 $[0,\infty)$ 上连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mutual independence concerns any finite collection, not just every pair. It also gives independence for separated intervals: insert their endpoints into one ordered time grid and group the appropriate increments. Endpoints may touch; the intervals must not share positive time length.

The law $N(0,t-s)$ depends only on interval length, so **stationary increments** already follow from the definition. The level process itself is not stationary: $W_1$ has variance $1$, whereas $W_4$ has variance $4$. See [[布朗运动不平稳|the distinction between stationary increments and stationary levels]]. At $t=0$, the value is the constant zero; the notation $N(0,0)$, if used, denotes this point mass and has no ordinary normal density.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相互独立要求任意有限组的联合分布都能分解，不是只检查每一对。它也给出分离区间的增量独立性：把所有端点放入同一有序网格，再合并相应增量即可。端点可以相接，但区间不能共享正长度的时间。

$N(0,t-s)$ 只取决于区间长度，所以定义已经包含**平稳增量**。水平过程本身却不平稳：$W_1$ 的方差为 $1$，$W_4$ 的方差为 $4$，具体区别见[[布朗运动不平稳]]。在 $t=0$，过程值是常数零；若写 $N(0,0)$，表示这个单点质量，不是有普通正态密度的分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Marginals do not replace the path requirement
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 路径条件不能由单时点分布替代

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The one-time law $W_t\sim N(0,t)$ for $t>0$ is a consequence, not a sufficient definition. Take a single standard normal variable $Z$ and set $X_t=\sqrt t\,Z$. Each position has the correct marginal law and every path is continuous, but $X_1=Z$ and $X_4-X_1=Z$ are the same nonconstant variable. Those two increments cannot be independent.

Even all [[有限维分布|finite-dimensional distributions]] describe only finitely many times at once. They do not ensure continuity of the particular version supplied to us; the random-spike example in [[有限维分布不定路径正则性|finite-dimensional laws and path regularity]] makes the quantifier issue explicit. The [[协方差核刻画布朗运动|Gaussian covariance characterization]] therefore retains an almost-sure continuous-path condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $t>0$ 的单时点分布 $W_t\sim N(0,t)$ 是结论，不是充分定义。只取一个标准正态变量 $Z$，设 $X_t=\sqrt t\,Z$，每个位置都有正确的边际分布，路径也都连续；然而 $X_1=Z$、$X_4-X_1=Z$ 是同一个非常数随机变量，这两个增量不可能独立。

即便给齐全部[[有限维分布]]，每个联合分布也只同时涉及有限个时刻，不能保证当前交给我们的版本路径连续；[[有限维分布不定路径正则性]]中的随机尖峰例子说明了量词区别。因此，[[协方差核刻画布朗运动]]仍要保留几乎必然连续的路径条件。

## What changes when the scale changes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*改变尺度之后*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a constant $\sigma>0$, $B_t=\sigma W_t$ has increment variance $\sigma^2(t-s)$. Its variance parameter is $\sigma^2$ and its increment standard deviation is $\sigma\sqrt{t-s}$. It is standard only when $\sigma=1$. At $\sigma=0$ the process is identically zero, a degenerate limit rather than standard Brownian motion. A nonzero starting value or a linear drift belongs to the more general [[带漂移布朗运动|Brownian model with drift]]. The [[布朗有限维分布|finite-dimensional law]] card next explains how several Brownian positions fit together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对常数 $\sigma>0$，$B_t=\sigma W_t$ 的增量方差为 $\sigma^2(t-s)$，方差参数是 $\sigma^2$，增量标准差是 $\sigma\sqrt{t-s}$；只有 $\sigma=1$ 时它才是标准布朗运动。$\sigma=0$ 时过程恒为零，是退化极限而非标准布朗运动。非零起点或线性漂移属于更一般的[[带漂移布朗运动|带漂移布朗模型]]。下一步可由[[布朗有限维分布]]理解多个位置如何组成联合分布。
