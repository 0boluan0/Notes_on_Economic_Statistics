# 确定贴现下单期信用债价格等于风险中性到期支付的贴现期望

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With deterministic discounting, a one-period credit bond is priced by discounting its risk-neutral expected terminal payment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定一个无套利定价模型及其风险中性测度 $\mathbb Q$。零息债在 $T>0$ 年后承诺支付 $F>0$；期限内违约概率为 $q^{\mathbb Q}$。无违约时在 $T$ 支付 $F$，违约时也只在 $T$ 支付固定回收额 $RF$，其中 $0\le R\le1$；期中无其他支付，忽略税费、流动性和嵌入期权，贴现因子 $D(0,T)>0$ 确定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix an arbitrage-free pricing model and its risk-neutral measure $\mathbb Q$. A zero-coupon bond promises $F>0$ in $T>0$ years, with horizon default probability $q^{\mathbb Q}$. It pays $F$ at $T$ if there is no default and a fixed recovery $RF$ at that same date otherwise, where $0\le R\le1$. There are no interim payments, taxes or fees, liquidity effects, or embedded options, and the discount factor $D(0,T)>0$ is deterministic.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按两个状态分别加权，得到：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weighting the two terminal outcomes gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P=D(0,T)\big[(1-q^{\mathbb Q})F+q^{\mathbb Q}RF\big]
=D(0,T)F\big[1-q^{\mathbb Q}(1-R)\big].$$

例如 $T=1$、$F=100$、一年无风险有效利率 5%、$q^{\mathbb Q}=2\%$、$R=40\%$，风险中性期望到期支付为 $98.8$，价格为 $98.8/1.05\approx94.0952$。承诺到期收益率为 $100/P-1\approx6.2753\%$，但风险中性期望支付除以价格仍等于 1.05；二者分母相同、分子不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $T=1$, $F=100$, a 5% one-year effective risk-free rate, $q^{\mathbb Q}=2\%$, and $R=40\%$, expected terminal payment under $\mathbb Q$ is $98.8$ and price is $98.8/1.05\approx94.0952$. The promised yield is $100/P-1\approx6.2753\%$, while risk-neutral expected payment divided by price is 1.05. These ratios share a denominator but use different numerators.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $1-R$ 是相对到期承诺 $F$、同在 $T$ 结算的损失率，不自动等于监管或回收处置模型中的[[违约损失口径|经济 LGD]]。若改为违约当时回收、回收额随机或贴现随机，就必须按相应日期和联合分布重新定价。把历史 $\mathbb P$ 概率直接放进本式，一般也不能保留无风险贴现的定价结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $1-R$ is a loss fraction of the promised $F$, settled entirely at $T$, not automatically the [[违约损失口径|economic LGD]] used in regulatory or workout models. Recovery at default, random recovery amounts, or stochastic discounting requires the relevant dates and joint distribution. Substituting historical $\mathbb P$ probabilities generally does not preserve this risk-free-discounted pricing result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
