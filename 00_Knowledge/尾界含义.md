# Interpreting a tail bound
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样理解尾概率上界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **tail bound** limits how likely a random quantity is to reach an extreme region. If $X$ is a waiting time in seconds, the event $X\ge30$ means “wait at least 30 seconds”. A result such as $P(X\ge30)\le0.02$ guarantees that this event has probability at most $2\%$. Equivalently, the chance of waiting **less than** 30 seconds is at least $98\%$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**尾概率上界**限制一个随机量落入某个极端区域的可能性。若 $X$ 是以秒计的等待时间，事件 $X\ge30$ 就是“至少等待 30 秒”。结论 $P(X\ge30)\le0.02$ 保证该事件的概率最多为 $2\%$；等价地，等待**不到** 30 秒的概率至少为 $98\%$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $P(A)$ denotes the probability of an [[概率空间|event]] $A$, a set of possible outcomes. The “tail” may mean unusually large values, unusually small values, or large departures in either direction from the [[期望|mean]]. The event must be specified before the bound can be interpreted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $P(A)$ 表示[[概率空间|事件]] $A$ 的概率，而事件是一组可能结果。“尾部”可以指异常大的值、异常小的值，也可以指向任一方向远离[[期望|均值]]。先写清具体事件，才能解释它的界。

> [!example] One guarantee, several possible realities · 同一个保证可以对应不同真值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If all we know is $P(A)\le0.02$, the true probability could be $0.003$, $0.01$, or $0.02$. An upper bound gives a ceiling. It does not locate the probability near that ceiling or tell us the error of treating the ceiling as an estimate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果只知道 $P(A)\le0.02$，真实概率可以是 $0.003$、$0.01$，也可以恰好是 $0.02$。上界给的是天花板；它没有说概率靠近天花板，也没有告诉我们把天花板当估计值会差多少。

## Why the same inequality can be exact or loose
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么同一个不等式有时精确，有时宽松*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a nonnegative waiting time has mean 10 seconds. [[Markov不等式|Markov's inequality]] guarantees $P(X\ge30)\le10/30=1/3$. It uses only nonnegativity and the mean, so it must cover every distribution with those properties. If the wait is always 10 seconds, the true tail probability is zero. If it is 30 seconds with probability $1/3$ and zero otherwise, the mean is still 10 seconds and the tail probability is exactly $1/3$. The bound is valid for both.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设一个非负等待时间的均值为 10 秒。[[Markov不等式]]保证 $P(X\ge30)\le10/30=1/3$。它只使用非负性和均值，因此必须照顾所有符合这两条信息的分布。如果每次都等 10 秒，真实尾概率是零；如果以 $1/3$ 的概率等 30 秒、其余情况等零秒，均值仍为 10 秒，尾概率却恰好是 $1/3$。同一个界对两者都成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the **same event under the same assumptions**, a smaller valid upper bound gives a stronger guarantee. Bounds of $0.02$ and $0.1$ can be combined by taking the smaller one. But knowing an upper bound of $0.1$ does not establish that the true probability exceeds $0.02$. A loose bound can fail to certify a desired target even when the actual probability meets it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对**相同假设下的同一个事件**，更小的有效上界给出更强保证。若分别得到 $0.02$ 和 $0.1$，可以取较小者。但只知道上界为 $0.1$，不能断言真实概率超过 $0.02$：一个宽松的界可能无法证明目标已经达到，而真实概率其实满足目标。

> [!warning] When the calculation exceeds one · 算出来超过 1 时
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Probabilities always lie between zero and one. If an otherwise valid calculation gives $P(A)\le1.4$, combine it with $P(A)\le1$ and report the **trivial bound** $P(A)\le\min\{1,1.4\}=1$. This supplies no restriction beyond the probability axioms. It is neither a probability of $140\%$ nor evidence that the event is likely. A negative proposed upper bound indicates a mistake in the calculation or assumptions; it cannot be repaired by simply replacing it with zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 概率总在零和一之间。如果一个条件成立的计算给出 $P(A)\le1.4$，应结合 $P(A)\le1$，报告**平凡界** $P(A)\le\min\{1,1.4\}=1$。它没有提供超出概率公理的新限制，既不表示“概率为 $140\%$”，也不表示事件很可能发生。若得到负的概率上界，则要检查计算或假设，不能简单改成零来补救。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once this meaning is clear, [[概率界选择|choosing a probability bound]] explains how the available information determines which inequalities can supply such a guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理解了“保证”的含义以后，[[概率界选择]]会说明如何根据已知信息选择能提供保证的不等式。
