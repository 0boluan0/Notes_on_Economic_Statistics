# 央行管理利率只有在准入和交易条件可比时才能形成严格的市场套利边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An administered rate forms a strict arbitrage bound only when access and transaction conditions are comparable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“央行付4%，所以没人愿意以更低利率出借”的推理，预设出借人能获得这4%，或者中介能无成本、无上限地替它取得收益。若参与者没有资格、银行中介要承担资产负债表成本，市场利率就可能低于央行付息率而不存在可无限复制的无风险利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The claim “the central bank pays 4%, so nobody lends below it” assumes access or unlimited costless intermediation. Ineligible lenders and bank balance-sheet costs can allow market rates below remuneration without an unlimited, riskless arbitrage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设机构甲不能直接获得IORB，银行乙可以获得4%，但为甲提供中介融资需要承担等价年化0.15%的全部边际成本。乙从甲以3.90%借入后再持有准备金，净收益为4%−3.90%−0.15%=−0.05%，不是稳赚。因此，不能拿4%为甲的所有投资画一条绝对下限。这是假设计价例子，不是对实际利差成本的估计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose lender A cannot earn IORB, while bank B can earn 4% but faces total marginal intermediation costs equivalent to 0.15% annually. Borrowing at 3.90% to hold reserves yields 4%−3.90%−0.15%=−0.05%, not a sure profit. The 4% option is not an absolute floor for A. These assumed costs are not an empirical spread estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

美国ON RRP向更广的合格货币市场参与者提供外部投资选择，可补充IORB的覆盖；它也有自身资格和操作条件。上侧同理：抵押品不足、操作时点或[[贴现窗口污名]]可以使市场融资价超过看似可用的央行便利利率。[[利率走廊]]应作为有条件的机制图读，目标区间也不是逐笔交易的法律限价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
U.S. ON RRP broadens the outside option beyond IORB-eligible institutions but has its own conditions. On the upper side, collateral, timing, or [[贴现窗口污名|discount-window stigma]] can make market funding exceed a nominal facility rate. Read an [[利率走廊|interest-rate corridor]] as a conditional mechanism, not a legally binding trade-price band.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Ihrig等（2020），Ample-Reserves Regime，The Fed’s administered rates及脚注3](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/implementing-monetary-policy-in-an-ample-reserves-regime-the-basics-note-1-of-3-20200701.html)：IOR准入、FHLB及货币基金、ON RRP补充作用与贴现污名。
- [Bowman等（2010），Interest on Excess Reserves，§1、结论](https://www.federalreserve.gov/pubs/ifdp/2010/996/ifdp996.htm)：不完全准入使市场下限不再不可穿透；自造数例将中介成本显式列出。
- [Keating与Macchiavelli（2018），Interest on Reserves and Arbitrage in Post-Crisis Money Markets，Figure 1后第一段](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/interest-on-reserves-and-arbitrage-in-post-crisis-money-markets-20180301.html)：资产负债表成本形成市场利率与准备金付息之间的楔子；不采用其历史监管差异或实证系数作为现行通用参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sources establish access restrictions, supplementary facilities and non-impermeable bounds. Keating and Macchiavelli directly support the balance-sheet-cost wedge. The numerical example assumes intermediation costs rather than estimating them; historical regulatory differences are not generalised into current parameters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
