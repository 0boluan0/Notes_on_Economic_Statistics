# Addition rules for events
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*事件概率的加法：互斥与重叠*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **addition rule** finds the probability that at least one of several events occurs. The question is whether adding the event probabilities counts any outcome twice. If the events cannot occur together, there is no overlap and we add directly. If they overlap, we must correct the double counting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**事件概率的加法**用来求几个事件中至少发生一个的概率。关键是：直接把概率相加，会不会重复计算某些结果？如果事件不能同时发生，就没有重叠，可以直接相加；如果有重叠，就要修正重复计数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a [[概率空间|probability space]], an event is a set of possible outcomes. The union $A\cup B$ contains outcomes in $A$, in $B$, or in both; it means **at least one** event occurs. The intersection $A\cap B$ contains the outcomes shared by both events. The empty set $\varnothing$ contains no outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[概率空间]]中，事件是一组可能结果。并集 $A\cup B$ 包含属于 $A$、属于 $B$，或同时属于两者的结果，表示**至少发生一个**事件。交集 $A\cap B$ 则只包含两个事件共有的结果。空集 $\varnothing$ 不含任何结果。

> [!example] Two disjoint ways to qualify · 两种不重叠的合格方式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Roll a fair six-sided die. Let $A=\{1,2\}$ and $B=\{5,6\}$. No roll belongs to both, so the events are **mutually exclusive**, also called **disjoint**. Their union is $\{1,2,5,6\}$, and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 掷一枚公平六面骰子，令 $A=\{1,2\}$、$B=\{5,6\}$。没有哪个点数同时属于两者，所以这两个事件**互斥**，也叫**不相交**。并集为 $\{1,2,5,6\}$，因此
>
> $$P(A\cup B)=\frac26+\frac26=\frac46.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each qualifying outcome contributes once. This is why direct addition works.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个符合要求的结果都只贡献一次，这就是可以直接相加的原因。

> [!abstract] Direct addition for disjoint events · 互斥事件直接相加
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $A\cap B=\varnothing$, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $A\cap B=\varnothing$，则
>
> $$P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Equal probabilities are not required. Disjoint events with probabilities $0.1$ and $0.3$ have union probability $0.4$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里不要求两个事件等可能。两个互斥事件的概率若为 $0.1$ 与 $0.3$，并集概率就是 $0.4$。

## What changes when the events overlap?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*事件重叠时，哪里被多算了一次？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the same fair die, now let $A=\{2,4,6\}$ mean “even” and $B=\{4,5,6\}$ mean “at least four”. Adding their probabilities gives $3/6+3/6=1$. But the union is only $\{2,4,5,6\}$, with probability $4/6$. The shared outcomes $4$ and $6$ were counted once in $A$ and again in $B$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仍用这枚公平骰子，现在令 $A=\{2,4,6\}$ 表示“偶数”，$B=\{4,5,6\}$ 表示“至少四点”。直接相加得到 $3/6+3/6=1$，但并集只有 $\{2,4,5,6\}$，概率为 $4/6$。共有的 $4$ 和 $6$ 在 $A$ 中算过一次，又在 $B$ 中各算了一次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtracting the overlap once leaves every outcome in the union counted exactly once. This gives the general two-event formula, also called inclusion–exclusion:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把重叠部分减掉一次后，并集中的每个结果就都恰好被计入一次。由此得到适用于任意两个事件的一般加法公式，也称两事件的容斥公式：

$$P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the die, this is $3/6+3/6-2/6=4/6$. With $P(A)=0.6$, $P(B)=0.5$ and $P(A\cap B)=0.2$, the same formula gives $0.9$. The union is not “exactly one occurs”: outcomes where both occur remain included, but only once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

骰子例子就是 $3/6+3/6-2/6=4/6$。若 $P(A)=0.6$、$P(B)=0.5$、$P(A\cap B)=0.2$，同一公式给出 $0.9$。并集不是“恰好发生一个”：两个事件都发生的情况仍然包括在内，只是保留一份。

> [!warning] Disjointness is not independence · 互斥与独立不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[事件独立|Independence]] means that the joint probability equals the product, $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)$. Disjointness says the intersection is empty. For two positive-probability events these cannot both hold: learning that one occurred rules out the other. See [[正概率互斥不独立|why positive-probability disjoint events are dependent]]. The addition rule uses overlap; it does not assume independence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[事件独立|独立]]要求联合概率等于乘积，即 $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)$；互斥则表示交集为空。两个事件概率都为正时，这两种关系不能同时成立：知道一个发生，就排除了另一个。详见[[正概率互斥不独立]]。加法公式处理的是重叠关系，本身不假设独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is a finer distinction: direct addition only needs the overlap to have **probability zero**, even if it is not empty. For a [[连续均匀分布|uniformly selected point]] on $[0,1]$, the events $[0,1/2]$ and $[1/2,1]$ share the point $1/2$, but that point has probability zero. Their probabilities still add to $1$. Empty overlap is a sufficient condition; zero-probability overlap is the exact condition for the numerical two-event equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

还要区分一个更细的边界：直接相加只要求重叠部分的**概率为零**，它未必真的为空。例如，在 $[0,1]$ 上[[连续均匀分布|均匀取一点]]时，事件 $[0,1/2]$ 和 $[1/2,1]$ 共享点 $1/2$，但该点概率为零，两个事件的概率仍可相加得到 $1$。交集为空是充分条件；交集概率为零才是数值上两事件直接相加的精确条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite or countably infinite list of pairwise disjoint events $A_1,A_2,\ldots$, the [[概率三公理|countable-additivity axiom]] gives $P(\bigcup_i A_i)=\sum_iP(A_i)$. A countable list means the events can be indexed by $1,2,3,\ldots$; the claim is not an unrestricted rule for every infinite collection. Dividing a target event into such disjoint pieces is the step used in the [[全概率公式|law of total probability]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于有限或可数无穷多个两两互斥的事件 $A_1,A_2,\ldots$，[[概率三公理|可数可加性公理]]给出 $P(\bigcup_i A_i)=\sum_iP(A_i)$。可数列表意味着可以用 $1,2,3,\ldots$ 给事件编号，这不是对任意无穷集合族都可使用的规则。把目标事件分成这样的互斥小块，正是[[全概率公式]]使用的步骤。
