# 互斥事件的并概率等于各事件概率之和
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The probability of a union of mutually exclusive events equals the sum of their probabilities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子规则
> 若 $A\cap B=\varnothing$，则
> $$P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B).$$
> 一般情况下交集会被直接相加两次，因此
> $$P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B).$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> If $A\cap B=\varnothing$, then
> $$P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B).$$
> In general, direct addition counts the intersection twice, so
> $$P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B).$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“互斥”描述两个事件不能同时发生；它保证可以直接相加，不是独立性的同义词。更一般地，若 $P(A\cap B)=0$，即使交集并非空集，数值上仍有 $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)$。对两两互斥的可列事件族，可列可加性给出 $P(\bigcup_i A_i)=\sum_iP(A_i)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Mutually exclusive” means that two events cannot occur together. It guarantees direct addition and is not a synonym for independence. More generally, $P(A\cap B)=0$ is enough for the numerical equality even when the intersection is not empty. For a countable pairwise-disjoint family, countable additivity gives $P(\bigcup_i A_i)=\sum_iP(A_i)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若 $P(A)=0.6$、$P(B)=0.5$、$P(A\cap B)=0.2$，$P(A\cup B)$ 是多少？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $P(A)=0.6$, $P(B)=0.5$, and $P(A\cap B)=0.2$, what is $P(A\cup B)$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** $0.6+0.5-0.2=0.9$；直接相加会重复计算交集。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** $0.6+0.5-0.2=0.9$; direct addition would count the intersection twice.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
