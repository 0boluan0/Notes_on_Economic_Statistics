# The full-column-rank pseudoinverse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*满列秩伪逆公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a matrix has independent columns, different coefficient vectors give different outputs. A target may still lie outside the possible outputs, so an exact solution may fail to exist. The **full-column-rank pseudoinverse formula** gives the unique coefficient vector whose output is closest to any target. It is the special form of the [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|Moore–Penrose inverse]] when none of the input directions is lost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当矩阵各列独立时，不同系数向量产生不同输出。但目标仍可能在可达输出之外，因此未必存在精确解。**满列秩伪逆公式**给出唯一的系数向量，使其输出最接近任意给定目标。这是没有输入方向丢失时，[[Moore–Penrose 伪逆]]的特殊形式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One coefficient, two observations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

> [!example] The closest point on a line / 找直线上最近的点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A=(1,2)^T$. A scalar coefficient $t$ produces $At=(t,2t)^T$. For target $b=(1,0)^T$, no $t$ matches both coordinates. The squared [[欧氏范数|Euclidean]] fitting error is
>
> $$\|At-b\|_2^2=(t-1)^2+(2t)^2=5(t-1/5)^2+4/5.$$
>
> It is minimized uniquely at $t=1/5$. The general rule for any real target $b=(b_1,b_2)^T$ is
>
> $$A^+=\frac15\begin{pmatrix}1&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad A^+b=\frac{b_1+2b_2}{5}.$$
>
> Here $A^+$ maps a two-coordinate target back to one coefficient. For $(1,0)^T$, the fitted point is $(1/5,2/5)^T$ and the residual is $(4/5,-2/5)^T$, perpendicular to the line generated by $(1,2)^T$. The symbol $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $A=(1,2)^T$，标量系数 $t$ 产生输出 $(t,2t)^T$。目标为 $(1,0)^T$ 时无法同时匹配两个坐标；上面的[[欧氏范数|欧氏]]误差平方仅在 $t=1/5$ 最小。对一般实目标 $b=(b_1,b_2)^T$，伪逆返回 $(b_1+2b_2)/5$。它把两坐标目标映回一个系数。本例拟合点为 $(1/5,2/5)^T$，残差 $(4/5,-2/5)^T$ 垂直于 $(1,2)^T$ 张成的直线；$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a real or complex $m\times n$ matrix with $m\ge n\ge1$. **Full column rank** means its $n$ columns are [[线性无关|linearly independent]], so its [[矩阵秩|rank]] is $n$. Write $A^*$ for conjugate transpose: transpose the matrix and conjugate each entry; for a real matrix it is simply $A^T$. Then

$$\boxed{A^+=(A^*A)^{-1}A^*.}$$

The [[Gram矩阵正定判据|Gram matrix]] $A^*A$ has size $n\times n$ and compares the columns by inner products; its inverse exists under the stated assumption. Consequently $A^+$ has size $n\times m$. The superscript $+$ denotes the pseudoinverse, while $^{-1}$ denotes an ordinary matrix inverse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为实或复 $m\times n$ 矩阵，$m\ge n\ge1$。**满列秩**指 $n$ 个列[[线性无关]]，因此[[矩阵秩]]为 $n$。$A^*$ 是共轭转置，即转置后再对各元素取共轭；实矩阵时就是 $A^T$。此时伪逆由上式给出。[[Gram矩阵正定判据|Gram 矩阵]] $A^*A$ 为 $n\times n$，用内积比较各列，在上述假设下可逆；结果 $A^+$ 为 $n\times m$。上标 $+$ 表示伪逆，$^{-1}$ 才表示普通逆矩阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the formula gives the unique closest fit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么公式成立

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Independent columns mean $Ax\ne0$ whenever the coefficient $x\ne0$. Thus

$$x^*A^*Ax=\|Ax\|_2^2>0\quad(x\ne0).$$

The Gram matrix is [[正定矩阵|positive definite]] and [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]], so it is invertible. For complex vectors, squared length is the sum of squared absolute values. A [[线性最小二乘|least-squares]] solution minimizes $\|Ax-b\|_2$ for a target $b$ with $m$ coordinates. Its residual must be perpendicular to every column, which gives the [[最小二乘正规方程|normal equations]]

$$A^*(b-A\widehat x)=0,\qquad A^*A\widehat x=A^*b.$$

Inverting the Gram matrix gives $\widehat x=(A^*A)^{-1}A^*b$. To see both minimality and uniqueness, let $h$ be any change in coefficients. Orthogonality removes the cross term:

$$\|b-A(\widehat x+h)\|_2^2=\|b-A\widehat x\|_2^2+\|Ah\|_2^2.$$

The second term vanishes only for $h=0$. Hence there is exactly one minimizer for every $b$, whether or not an exact fit exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

列独立意味着只要系数 $x\ne0$，就有 $Ax\ne0$，从而得到上面的严格正二次型。因此 Gram 矩阵为 [[Hermitian 矩阵|Hermitian]] [[正定矩阵]]，必定可逆；复向量的长度平方使用坐标绝对值的平方和。对含 $m$ 个坐标的目标 $b$，[[线性最小二乘]]最小化 $\|Ax-b\|_2$。最优残差须垂直于每一列，给出[[最小二乘正规方程]]；乘 Gram 矩阵的逆便得 $\widehat x=(A^*A)^{-1}A^*b$。

为同时说明最优性和唯一性，令 $h$ 为任意系数改变量。正交性使展开后的交叉项消失，得到上面的误差平方分解；第二项仅在 $h=0$ 时为零。因此每个目标都只有一个最小二乘解，无论是否可精确拟合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The map $G=(A^*A)^{-1}A^*$ satisfies $GA=I_n$, so it is a [[左逆|left inverse]]: coefficients are recovered exactly from outputs already of the form $Ax$. The opposite product $AG=A(A^*A)^{-1}A^*$ is the [[满列秩投影公式|orthogonal projector onto the column space]], not generally $I_m$. These identities make $G$ satisfy all four Moore–Penrose conditions, confirming that it is $A^+$. They distinguish the coefficient answer $A^+b$ from the fitted output $AA^+b$; $I_n$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

映射 $G=(A^*A)^{-1}A^*$ 满足 $GA=I_n$，所以是[[左逆]]：对已经形如 $Ax$ 的输出，能精确恢复系数。反向乘积 $AG=A(A^*A)^{-1}A^*$ 是[[满列秩投影公式|到列空间的正交投影]]，一般不是 $I_m$。这些恒等式使 $G$ 满足四个 Moore–Penrose 条件，确认它就是 $A^+$。注意系数答案 $A^+b$ 与拟合输出 $AA^+b$ 是两个不同对象；$I_n$ 是[[单位矩阵]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Where this closed form stops being useful
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 使用边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A square full-rank matrix reduces to $A^+=A^{-1}$. A genuinely wide matrix ($m<n$) cannot have full column rank; its appropriate full-rank formula is the [[满行秩伪逆公式|full-row-rank formula]]. If the columns are dependent, including a zero matrix with positive dimensions, $A^*A$ is singular and the displayed ordinary inverse does not exist. The pseudoinverse still exists through the [[伪逆SVD公式|SVD formula]].

Conjugation matters. For the complex column $A=(1,i)^T$, where $i^2=-1$, the correct Gram matrix is $A^*A=2$ and $A^+=(1,-i)/2$. Using $A^T$ instead would give $A^TA=0$ even though the column is nonzero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满秩方阵时，公式归结为 $A^+=A^{-1}$。真正的宽矩阵 $m<n$ 不可能满列秩，其对应满秩情形使用[[满行秩伪逆公式]]。如果列相关，包括正尺寸的零矩阵，$A^*A$ 就奇异，上式中的普通逆不存在；伪逆本身仍存在，可用[[伪逆SVD公式]]。

共轭不可省略。例如复列 $A=(1,i)^T$、$i^2=-1$ 的正确 Gram 矩阵为 $A^*A=2$，伪逆为 $(1,-i)/2$；若误用 $A^T$，反而得到 $A^TA=0$，尽管该列非零。

> [!warning] An identity is not a stable implementation recipe / 闭式恒等式不等于稳定实现处方
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Forming $A^*A$ [[正规方程条件数平方|squares the two-norm condition number]]: for full column rank, $\kappa_2(A^*A)=\kappa_2(A)^2$, where $\kappa_2(A)$ is the ratio of largest to smallest singular value. Rounding can then obscure a weak direction. To solve a least-squares problem, a [[薄QR最小二乘|QR solve]] or an SVD-based method can work directly with $A$; explicitly forming the inverse is unnecessary. These methods improve the calculation but cannot remove sensitivity already present in the data.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 满列秩时，形成 $A^*A$ 会使[[正规方程条件数平方|二范数条件数平方]]：$\kappa_2(A^*A)=\kappa_2(A)^2$，其中 $\kappa_2(A)$ 是最大与最小奇异值之比，舍入可能掩盖较弱的方向。实际求最小二乘解可用直接处理 $A$ 的[[薄QR最小二乘|QR 求解]]或 SVD 方法，无须显式形成逆矩阵。它们改善计算过程，却不能消除数据问题本来具有的敏感性。
