# 股利收益率是明确期间的每股现金股利与所选股价的比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dividend yield divides cash dividends per share for a stated period by a selected share price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股利收益率把股利与股票价格比较。设所选期间每股现金股利为 $DPS$、参考股价为 $P>0$，则 $y_D=DPS/P$。必须说明股利来自过去十二个月、最近完整年度还是未来预测，以及股价是哪一天或自己的买入价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dividend yield compares cash dividends with share price: $y_D=DPS/P$ for $P>0$. Specify trailing-twelve-month, last-fiscal-year, or forecast dividends, and whether the reference price is a dated market quote or the investor's purchase price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如过去十二个月每股派现 1 元，当前股价 20 元，历史股利收益率为 5%。若未来股利降为 0.5 元而价格仍为 20，前瞻值则为 2.5%；历史 5% 不承诺下一年收到同额。它的分母是价格，与[[股利支付率]]的利润分母不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trailing dividends of 1 per share and a current price of 20 give a 5% historical yield. If future dividends fall to 0.5 while price remains 20, the forward yield is 2.5%; the historical figure promises no future payment. Its price denominator differs from earnings in the [[股利支付率|payout ratio]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

股利收益率不是持有期总收益率。忽略税费且股利不再投资时，一年买价 20、收股利 1、年末卖价 16，总收益率为 $(16-20+1)/20=-15\%$。高股利收益率可能来自较高分红，也可能来自股价大跌，仍需判断派息持续性与经营风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dividend yield is not total holding-period return. Ignoring tax and costs without reinvestment, buying at 20, receiving 1, and selling at 16 gives a −15% return. A high yield can reflect larger dividends or a falling share price; distribution sustainability and business risk still matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
