# 函数在一点的导数是差商在自变量增量趋于零时的有限极限
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The derivative of a function at a point is the finite limit of its difference quotient as the input increment tends to zero*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 对定义在 $x_0$ 附近的函数 $f$，若有限极限
> $$
> f'(x_0)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)}{h}
> $$
> 存在，就称 $f$ 在 $x_0$ 可导，这个极限称为 $f$ 在该点的导数。分子是输出增量，分母是输入增量；导数是它们在观察尺度缩到零时稳定下来的比率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a function $f$ defined near $x_0$, if the finite limit $f'(x_0)=\lim_{h\to0}[f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)]/h$ exists, then $f$ is differentiable at $x_0$ and the limit is its derivative there. The numerator is the output increment and the denominator the input increment; the derivative is the ratio that stabilises as the observation scale shrinks to zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个数有三种相容的读法：它是图像在 $(x_0,f(x_0))$ 处的切线斜率，是随时间变化时的瞬时变化率，也是局部线性式
$$
f(x_0+h)=f(x_0)+f'(x_0)h+o(h)
$$
中的比例系数。前两种是几何和运动解释，最后一种说明导数为何能支持近似；它们不是三种不同的导数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same number is the tangent slope at $(x_0,f(x_0))$, an instantaneous rate when the input is time, and the coefficient in the local-linear relation $f(x_0+h)=f(x_0)+f'(x_0)h+o(h)$. These are compatible interpretations of one object, not three different derivatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $f(x)=x^2$ 在 $x_0=3$ 时，
$$
\frac{(3+h)^2-3^2}{h}=6+h\longrightarrow6.
$$
因此切线斜率和局部变化率都是 $6$。计算中只在 $h\ne0$ 时约去 $h$，最后才取极限；直接令 $h=0$ 会得到没有定义的 $0/0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^2$ at $x_0=3$, the difference quotient is $6+h$, which tends to 6. Cancellation is performed while $h\ne0$ and the limit is taken afterwards; substituting $h=0$ at the start merely produces the undefined form $0/0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两侧差商必须趋于同一个有限数。尖角、尖点和竖直切线都可能使这个条件失败；在区间端点使用单侧导数时，应明确说明采用的是单侧约定。导数存在会推出 [[可导必连续|连续]]，但连续本身不够，见 [[连续不必可导]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two one-sided difference quotients must approach the same finite number. Corners, cusps, and vertical tangents can all violate this requirement. At a domain endpoint, any one-sided derivative convention should be stated explicitly. Differentiability implies [[可导必连续|continuity]], but continuity alone is insufficient; see [[连续不必可导|the converse boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么导数定义中的 $h\to0$ 不能改成 $h=0$？
>
> **答案：** $h=0$ 时差商没有定义；导数问的是在所有足够小但非零的增量上，差商是否趋于同一个有限值。
