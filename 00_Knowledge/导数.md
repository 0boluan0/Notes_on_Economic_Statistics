# Derivative
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*导数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **derivative** measures how fast a function changes near one particular input. If the input is time and the output is position, it gives instantaneous velocity. On a graph it gives the slope of the tangent line. Both interpretations start by comparing two nearby inputs, then making their separation smaller.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**导数**衡量函数在某个输入附近变化得有多快。如果输入是时间、输出是位置，它给出瞬时速度；在图像上，它给出切线的斜率。这两种解释都从比较两个邻近输入开始，再让它们的间隔缩小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Start with a change over a nonzero interval
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先看非零区间上的平均变化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix an input $x_0$, where $f(x_0)$ is the output. Move to $x_0+h$, with a nonzero increment $h$. The input changes by $h$, while the output changes by $f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)$. Their ratio is the **difference quotient**, or average rate of change:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定输入 $x_0$，它对应输出 $f(x_0)$。把输入移到 $x_0+h$，其中增量 $h$ 不为零。输入改变了 $h$，输出改变了 $f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)$；两者之比叫作**差商**，也就是平均变化率：

$$\frac{f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)}{h}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Geometrically, this is the slope of the secant line through the two graph points. A negative $h$ compares the base point with a point to its left. A [[函数极限|function limit]] asks whether these ratios approach one number for all sufficiently small nonzero increments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在几何上，它是穿过这两个图像点的割线斜率。$h<0$ 时，比较的是基点左侧的点。[[函数极限]]让我们进一步问：当非零增量充分小时，这些比率是否都趋近同一个数？

> [!abstract] Derivative at a point · 一点处的导数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a real-valued function defined on an open interval around $x_0$, the derivative is the following **finite** limit, when it exists:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对于定义在 $x_0$ 周围某个开区间上的实值函数，若下面的**有限**极限存在，它就是该点的导数：
>
> $$f'(x_0)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x_0+h)-f(x_0)}{h}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The symbol $f'(x_0)$ is read “f prime at $x_0$”. The function is then called differentiable at that point. The limit must agree from positive and negative $h$. The derivative has units of output per unit of input.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $f'(x_0)$ 读作“f prime 在 $x_0$ 处”，此时称函数在该点可导。$h$ 从正侧或负侧趋于零，得到的极限必须相同。导数的单位是“输出单位除以输入单位”。

> [!example] The slope of a square · 平方函数的斜率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $f(x)=x^2$ and $x_0=3$. For $h\ne0$, expanding the square and cancelling $h$ gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $f(x)=x^2$、$x_0=3$。在 $h\ne0$ 时展开平方，再约去 $h$，得到
>
> $$\frac{(3+h)^2-3^2}{h}=\frac{6h+h^2}{h}=6+h.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $h=0.1$, $0.01$ and $-0.01$, the secant slopes are $6.1$, $6.01$ and $5.99$. As $h$ tends to zero from either side, $6+h$ tends to $6$. Therefore $f'(3)=6$. Cancellation happens while $h$ is nonzero; substituting $h=0$ into the original ratio would give the undefined expression $0/0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $h=0.1$、$0.01$、$-0.01$ 时，割线斜率分别为 $6.1$、$6.01$、$5.99$。从任意一侧让 $h$ 趋于零，$6+h$ 都趋于 $6$，所以 $f'(3)=6$。约分时 $h$ 仍非零；直接在原差商中代入 $h=0$，只会得到没有定义的 $0/0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same number gives a local approximation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同一个数也给出局部近似*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the base input $3$, the derivative is $6$, so a small input change $h$ produces an output change approximately $6h$. The [[线性近似|local linear approximation]] is therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在基点输入 $3$ 处，导数为 $6$，表示输入发生很小的改变 $h$ 时，输出改变约为 $6h$。因此，[[线性近似|局部线性近似]]为

$$f(3+h)\approx9+6h.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $h=0.01$, this predicts $9.06$, while the exact square is $9.0601$. The error is $h^2=0.0001$. More generally, differentiability means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $h=0.01$ 时，近似值为 $9.06$，准确平方值为 $9.0601$，误差为 $h^2=0.0001$。一般地，可导意味着

$$f(x_0+h)=f(x_0)+f'(x_0)h+r(h),\qquad \frac{r(h)}{h}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The remainder $r(h)$ is the approximation error. The condition says that it becomes negligible relative to the input increment; it is often written $r(h)=o(h)$, read “little-o of $h$”. Thus tangent slope, instantaneous rate and local linear coefficient describe the same derivative. A tangent need not meet the graph only once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

余项 $r(h)$ 是近似误差。这个条件表示：相对于输入增量，误差会变得可以忽略；常写成 $r(h)=o(h)$，读作“h 的小 o”。因此，切线斜率、瞬时变化率和局部线性系数说的是同一个导数。切线也不要求只与曲线相交一次。

> [!warning] A corner has incompatible slopes · 尖角的两侧斜率不一致
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=|x|$ at zero, the difference quotient is $|h|/h$. It is $1$ for $h>0$ and $-1$ for $h<0$. The function is continuous there, but no single derivative exists. See [[连续不必可导|why continuity does not guarantee differentiability]]. In the other direction, [[可导必连续|differentiability does imply continuity]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $f(x)=|x|$ 在零处的情况，差商为 $|h|/h$：$h>0$ 时等于 $1$，$h<0$ 时等于 $-1$。函数在这里连续，却没有唯一的导数，见[[连续不必可导]]。反过来，[[可导必连续]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A cusp or a vertical tangent may also prevent a finite derivative; a slope tending to infinity is not a real-valued derivative under this definition. At a domain endpoint, only one side may be available, so a one-sided derivative must be identified explicitly. The question is always which difference quotients are being compared and whether they approach a common finite value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

尖点或竖直切线也可能导致有限导数不存在；在这个定义下，斜率趋于无穷不算实数导数。在定义域端点可能只有一侧可用，此时若采用单侧导数，必须明确说明。始终要检查：比较的是哪些差商，它们是否趋向同一个有限值。
