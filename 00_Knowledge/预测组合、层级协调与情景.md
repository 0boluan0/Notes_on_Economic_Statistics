# 预测组合、层级协调与情景
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Forecast combinations, hierarchical reconciliation, and scenarios*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当多个模型各有错设风险、多层预测必须加总一致、或未来取决于无法点预测的政策/事件时，用组合、reconciliation 与情景管理这些不确定性。
> **具体锚点：** 产品—地区—全公司的预测若分别产生，底层加总往往不等于总量；reconciliation 将它们投影回可加总的空间。
> **核心难点：** 组合权重会过拟合，层级误差相关不能忽略，情景不是概率预测；专家调整必须保留统计基线与理由。
> **为什么重要：** 真实预测系统往往同时有多模型、多层级和不可编码的未来信息，单一点预测无法满足决策。
> **继续：** 先为每个成分模型完成 [[预测问题、基准方法与评估]]，再用外样本决定组合与协调方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** When models have different misspecification risks, forecasts across levels must add up, or the future depends on policy and events that cannot be point-predicted, combinations, reconciliation, and scenarios manage these uncertainties.
> **Concrete anchor:** Product–region–company forecasts produced separately usually fail to sum consistently; reconciliation projects them back into the coherent aggregation space.
> **Central difficulty:** Estimated combination weights can overfit, hierarchical errors are correlated, and a scenario is not a probability forecast. Expert adjustments must preserve the statistical baseline and their rationale.
> **Why it matters:** Real forecasting systems commonly involve several models, several aggregation levels, and future information that cannot be encoded in one model; one point forecast is insufficient for decisions.
> **Continue with:** Complete [[预测问题、基准方法与评估|forecasting workflow and evaluation]] for every component model before choosing combination and reconciliation methods out of sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 组合与层级
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Combinations and hierarchies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 预测组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Forecast combinations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平均多个合理模型常降低单模型错设风险。产品—地区—总量等层级预测应 reconcile，使各层加总一致；独立预测后手工比例分摊可能破坏准确性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Averaging several defensible models often reduces the risk of any one misspecification. Product–region–total hierarchies should be reconciled so that levels add consistently; manually allocating independent forecasts by fixed proportions can degrade accuracy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

组合点预测为 $\hat y^{(c)}=\sum_{m=1}^M w_m\hat y^{(m)}$，常规约束是 $\sum_m w_m=1$。组合误差方差是 $w^T\Sigma_e w$，所以有用的不只是每个模型自己多准，还有它们错误是否不完全同步。两个略差但错误互补的模型，可比两个几乎相同的强模型更有组合价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A combined point forecast is $\hat y^{(c)}=\sum_{m=1}^M w_m\hat y^{(m)}$, often with $\sum_m w_m=1$. Its error variance is $w^T\Sigma_e w$, so value depends not only on each model's accuracy but also on whether their errors move together. Two slightly weaker but complementary models may combine more effectively than two nearly identical strong models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估计最优权重需要估计 $\Sigma_e$，在样本少、模型多时很容过拟合；因此等权平均常是很强的基准。权重不应在测试期上调整，且应分 horizon 估计/验证，因为一步与长期的模型优势可不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Optimal weights require estimation of $\Sigma_e$ and can overfit when histories are short and models numerous; equal weighting is therefore a strong baseline. Weights must not be tuned on the test period and should be estimated or validated by horizon because one-step and long-horizon strengths may differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 层级与分组预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Hierarchical and grouped forecasts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若底层向量为 $b_t$，加总矩阵为 $S$，则所有可观测层级满足 $y_t=Sb_t$。对各层独立建模得到的 base forecasts $\hat y_h$ 通常不在 $S$ 的列空间中；reconciliation 构造 $\tilde y_h=SG\hat y_h$，使结果重新满足加总约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If bottom-level observations are $b_t$ and the summing matrix is $S$, every observed level satisfies $y_t=Sb_t$. Base forecasts $\hat y_h$ estimated separately at each level usually do not lie in the column space of $S$. Reconciliation constructs $\tilde y_h=SG\hat y_h$ so that aggregation constraints hold again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

bottom-up 只预测底层再加总，简单且保留底层信息，但底层很噪时不稳；top-down 先预测总量再分摊，会丢掉底层特有信号且可引入偏差；MinT 使用各层 base forecast 及其误差协方差，在相干性约束下最小化协调预测误差方差之和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bottom-up forecasts the bottom level and aggregates it; it is simple and preserves local information but may be unstable when bottom series are noisy. Top-down forecasts the total and allocates it, potentially discarding local signals and introducing bias. MinT uses base forecasts at all levels and their error covariance to minimise the sum of reconciled forecast-error variances subject to coherence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相干不等于准确。若所有 base forecasts 都差，投影到加总空间只会让数学关系一致，不会创造预测信号。各层应分别报误差，并检查协调是否把某一重要层级的强 base forecast 拉坏。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Coherence is not accuracy. If every base forecast is poor, projection into the aggregation space enforces arithmetic but creates no signal. Report errors by level and verify that reconciliation does not degrade a strong base forecast at a decision-critical level.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 判断调整
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Judgmental adjustments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结构突变、政策和新产品使纯历史规律失效。此时提供条件情景、专家调整和透明假设，并将 judgment adjustment 与统计基线分开记录。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Structural breaks, policy changes, and new products can invalidate purely historical patterns. Provide conditional scenarios, expert adjustments, and transparent assumptions, while recording judgmental adjustments separately from the statistical baseline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

情景回答“如果假设 A/B/C 发生，结果会怎样”，并不自动给出 A/B/C 的概率。应先确定对决策重要的少数不确定轴，保持各变量路径内在一致，然后为每个情景生成条件预测和行动阈值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A scenario answers "what follows if assumptions A, B, or C occur" and does not automatically assign probabilities to those assumptions. Identify a small number of decision-relevant uncertainty axes, keep variable paths internally consistent, and generate conditional forecasts and action thresholds for each scenario.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

专家调整只应用于模型中确实没有的新信息，而不是用直觉“修正”模型已经处理的噪声模式。每次调整记录调整前基线、调整幅度、信息来源、负责人和到期结果，才能事后判断人的信息是否真的增值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expert adjustments should use genuinely new information absent from the model, not intuition to "correct" noise patterns the model already handles. Record the baseline, adjustment magnitude, information source, owner, and eventual outcome for every override so that its incremental value can be assessed later.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：产品—地区销量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: product-by-region sales*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设两个地区 A/B 各有两个产品，独立 base forecasts 给出四个底层数总和 102，但公司总量模型给 110。直接把 8 按完全固定比例分给底层忽略了各序列的误差精度与相关。MinT 会根据估计的 base-error 协方差在所有节点之间分配调整，同时强制底层、地区与总量相加一致。若另有新产品上市情景，它应作为带明确假设的独立情景，不应悄然改动统计 base forecast。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose regions A and B each contain two products. Four independent bottom-level forecasts sum to 102, while the company-total model forecasts 110. Allocating the difference of eight by fixed proportions ignores each series' error precision and error correlation. MinT distributes adjustments across all nodes using estimated base-error covariance while enforcing consistency among products, regions, and the total. A new-product launch should be represented as a separate, explicitly assumed scenario rather than silently changing the statistical base forecast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*

### 为什么组合简单模型常有帮助？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does combining simple models often help?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不同模型的错设和噪声不完全相同，平均可降低对单一模型错误的敏感性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Different models have imperfectly correlated misspecification and noise, so averaging can reduce sensitivity to any single model's errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 相干预测为什么不一定准确？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why need a coherent forecast not be accurate?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 相干只表示加总等式成立；它没有保证 base forecasts 含有真实信号，也不会消除共同偏差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Coherence enforces aggregation equalities only; it neither guarantees signal in the base forecasts nor removes a shared bias.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 情景预测与概率预测有何不同？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How does a scenario forecast differ from a probabilistic forecast?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 情景预测在给定外生假设路径下给条件结果，通常不为各情景分配概率；概率预测描述结果的完整分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A scenario forecast gives conditional outcomes under specified external paths, usually without assigning probabilities to those paths. A probabilistic forecast describes a full distribution over outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明为什么专家调整必须保留统计基线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why judgmental adjustment must preserve the statistical baseline.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 只有保留调整前的预测，才能事后比较专家新信息是增值还是引入偏见，也能避免将决策目标偷换为预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Retaining the pre-adjustment forecast permits later measurement of whether expert information added value or bias and prevents a decision target from being disguised as a forecast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
