# 经济供养比是按年龄劳动收入加权的有效生产者数与按年龄消费加权的有效消费者数之比

在国民转移账户（NTA）中，经济供养比是按年龄劳动收入加权的有效生产者数，除以按年龄消费加权的有效消费者数。它不把所有 15–64 岁的人都算作同样的生产者，也不把所有人的消费需求都算成相同；因此不同于[[年龄抚养比]]的简单倒数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In National Transfer Accounts (NTA), the economic support ratio divides effective producers weighted by age-specific labour income by effective consumers weighted by age-specific consumption. It neither treats everyone aged 15–64 as an identical producer nor assigns identical consumption to every person. It is therefore not simply the reciprocal of the [[年龄抚养比|age dependency ratio]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设年龄 $a$ 的人口为 $N_a$，劳动收入权重为 $\ell_a$、消费权重为 $c_a$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For population $N_a$ at age $a$, labour-income weight $\ell_a$ and consumption weight $c_a$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
SR=\frac{\sum_a\ell_aN_a}{\sum_a c_aN_a}.
$$

NTA 手册的构造以基期年龄别人均劳动收入，相对于 30–49 岁的平均值，定义 $\ell_a$；消费也相对于该年龄段的平均消费定义 $c_a$。两个基准是各自变量的平均值，不能把劳动收入与消费用同一个数随意归一化。跨期固定这些权重时，$SR$ 的变化隔离的是人口结构效应；若权重也改变，变化还包含行为、工资和消费模式等因素。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The NTA Manual defines $\ell_a$ from base-year per-capita labour income at age $a$, relative to its average at ages 30–49; $c_a$ similarly uses consumption relative to average consumption at those ages. Each variable has its own reference mean. Holding these weights fixed isolates population-composition effects; changing weights also introduces behavioural, wage and consumption-pattern changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如三组人口为 $(20,60,20)$，劳动收入权重为 $(0,1,0.2)$、消费权重为 $(0.6,1,1.2)$，则有效生产者为 64、有效消费者为 96，$SR=2/3$。这不表示只有 64 个实际就业者，更不是每人生产量；[[劳动生产率]]使用的是另一种分母。老年消费如何获得资源，见[[老年消费的筹资]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With populations $(20,60,20)$, labour-income weights $(0,1,0.2)$ and consumption weights $(0.6,1,1.2)$, there are 64 effective producers and 96 effective consumers, so $SR=2/3$. This is not a count of 64 actual workers or a measure of output per person; [[劳动生产率|labour productivity]] uses a different denominator. Funding for consumption at older ages is addressed in [[老年消费的筹资|financing old-age consumption]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [UN，NTA Manual（2013）](https://ntaccounts.org/doc/repository/NTA%20manual%202013.pdf#page=126)，印刷第109页 Support ratio 与表5.6：有效生产者／消费者、各自30–49岁基准、固定基期权重。第10–11页说明年龄模式包含参与、就业、工时与收入差异。三组算例为独立构造。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The NTA Manual supports equivalent producers and consumers, separate prime-age reference means and fixed base-year weights. Its discussion connects age profiles to participation, employment, hours and income. The example is constructed.
