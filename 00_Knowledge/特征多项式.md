# Characteristic polynomial
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*特征多项式：把寻找特征值变成一个标量方程*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **characteristic polynomial** turns a square matrix into a polynomial in one scalar variable: a finite sum of nonnegative integer powers of that variable, each multiplied by a fixed coefficient. A root is a value making the polynomial zero. Its roots in the chosen field are exactly the matrix's eigenvalues: the multipliers for which some nonzero vector satisfies $Av=\lambda v$, as defined by an [[特征对|eigenpair]]. It finds possible multipliers; finding the corresponding vectors is a separate null-space calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**特征多项式**把一个方阵变成关于单个标量变量的多项式：把这个变量的非负整数次幂分别乘以固定系数，再作有限项相加。根就是使多项式取值为零的数。它在所选数域中的根，恰好是矩阵的特征值：也就是使某个非零向量满足 $Av=\lambda v$ 的倍数，见[[特征对]]。它负责寻找可能的倍数，而对应向量还需要另外求零空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From a small matrix to a polynomial
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从一个小矩阵开始计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $A=\begin{bmatrix}3&1\\1&3\end{bmatrix}$. Introduce a variable $t$ and form $tI_2-A$, where the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I_2$ has diagonal entries $1$ and off-diagonal entries $0$. Subtraction gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
取 $A=\begin{bmatrix}3&1\\1&3\end{bmatrix}$。引入变量 $t$，构造 $tI_2-A$，其中[[单位矩阵]] $I_2$ 的对角元是 $1$，非对角元是 $0$。相减得到

$$tI_2-A=\begin{bmatrix}t-3&-1\\-1&t-3\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[行列式|determinant]] assigns a scalar to a square matrix; for a $2\times2$ matrix $\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}$ it is $ad-bc$. Applying that rule here produces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
[[行列式]]把方阵对应到一个标量；对 $2\times2$ 矩阵 $\begin{bmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{bmatrix}$，它等于 $ad-bc$。把这个规则用于上式，就得到

$$p_A(t)=\det(tI_2-A)=(t-3)^2-1=(t-4)(t-2).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The roots are $4$ and $2$. Indeed, $A(1,1)^T=4(1,1)^T$ and $A(1,-1)^T=2(1,-1)^T$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]] to write a column vector. Notice that $t$ was introduced only on the diagonal before subtracting $A$; it was not subtracted from every entry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
根是 $4$ 和 $2$。直接检查也有 $A(1,1)^T=4(1,1)^T$、$A(1,-1)^T=2(1,-1)^T$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置]]，把坐标写成列向量。注意，先构造的 $tI_2$ 只有对角线上含 $t$，然后才减去 $A$，并不是在每个元素上都引入 $t$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Definition and the reason its roots work
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*定义，以及根为什么就是特征值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an $n\times n$ matrix $A$ with $n\ge1$ over a scalar field $\mathbb F$, define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对标量域 $\mathbb F$ 上的 $n\times n$ 矩阵 $A$，其中 $n\ge1$，定义

$$\boxed{p_A(t)=\det(tI_n-A).}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $t$ is a scalar variable, and the coefficients of the polynomial lie in the same field as the entries of $A$. The highest power with a nonzero coefficient is its degree; here the degree is $n$. The polynomial is **monic**, meaning its leading coefficient is $1$. In the determinant expansion, the term $t^n$ comes from choosing $t$ in every diagonal position; every other term has smaller degree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里 $t$ 是标量变量，多项式的系数与 $A$ 的元素属于同一个域。系数非零的最高幂指数叫作次数；这里次数为 $n$。这个多项式还是**首一的**，即最高次项系数为 $1$。在行列式展开中，只有从每个对角位置都选取 $t$ 才会得到 $t^n$，其余项的次数都更低。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now substitute a particular scalar $\lambda\in\mathbb F$. The link to eigenvalues follows from the [[可逆性与非零行列式|determinant criterion for invertibility]]: a square matrix has determinant zero exactly when its homogeneous equation has a nonzero solution. Therefore,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
现在代入某个具体的标量 $\lambda\in\mathbb F$。它与特征值的联系来自[[可逆性与非零行列式|行列式的可逆性判据]]：方阵的行列式为零，当且仅当它的齐次方程存在非零解。因此，

$$
\begin{aligned}
p_A(\lambda)=0
&\iff \text{there exists }v\ne0\text{ with }(\lambda I_n-A)v=0\\
&\iff \text{there exists }v\ne0\text{ with }Av=\lambda v.
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After finding a root, solve $(A-\lambda I_n)v=0$. All its solutions form the [[特征空间|eigenspace]], and its nonzero solutions are the eigenvectors. The polynomial alone does not specify those vectors or the dimension of the eigenspace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
找到一个根后，再求解 $(A-\lambda I_n)v=0$。全部解组成[[特征空间]]，其中的非零解才是特征向量。单凭多项式并不能给出这些向量，也不能确定特征空间的维数。

> [!note] Two sign conventions, the same roots / 两种符号约定，根相同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Some sources use $\det(A-tI_n)$. Since $A-tI_n=-(tI_n-A)$, multiplying all $n$ rows by $-1$ gives $\det(A-tI_n)=(-1)^n p_A(t)$. The roots are unchanged. For $A=[3]$, our convention gives $t-3$ and the other gives $3-t$; both vanish at $3$. Keep the convention consistent when comparing coefficients.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 有些资料采用 $\det(A-tI_n)$。因为 $A-tI_n=-(tI_n-A)$，把全部 $n$ 行分别乘以 $-1$ 就有 $\det(A-tI_n)=(-1)^n p_A(t)$，根不会改变。例如 $A=[3]$ 时，本卡约定给出 $t-3$，另一约定给出 $3-t$，二者都在 $3$ 处为零。比较系数时需要保持同一种约定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the degree does and does not guarantee
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*次数、数域与重根的边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Having degree $n$ does not guarantee $n$ distinct roots, or even a root in the current field. For the real quarter-turn matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
次数为 $n$ 并不保证有 $n$ 个不同的根，甚至不保证在当前域中存在根。对实数域上的四分之一圈旋转矩阵，

$$R=\begin{bmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{bmatrix},\qquad
p_R(t)=\det\begin{bmatrix}t&1\\-1&t\end{bmatrix}=t^2+1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is no real root, so there is no real eigenvalue. Over $\mathbb C$, the roots are $i$ and $-i$, where $i^2=-1$. Extending the allowed field does not change this polynomial's coefficients; it changes which roots are available. Over $\mathbb C$, a degree-$n$ characteristic polynomial has $n$ roots counted with multiplicity. The exact field condition is explained in [[特征多项式底层域|the field of the characteristic polynomial]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
它没有实根，所以没有实特征值。在复数域中，根为 $i$ 与 $-i$，其中 $i^2=-1$。扩大允许使用的数域不会改变这个多项式的系数，但会改变有哪些根可用。在复数域上，$n$ 次特征多项式按重数计恰有 $n$ 个根。准确的数域条件见[[特征多项式底层域]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A repeated root counts a repeated polynomial factor, not a promised number of independent eigenvectors. For example, $\begin{bmatrix}3&1\\0&3\end{bmatrix}$ and $3I_2$ both have polynomial $(t-3)^2$, but their eigenspaces for $3$ have dimensions $1$ and $2$. This is the distinction between [[代数重数与几何重数|algebraic and geometric multiplicity]]. Zero is also a legitimate root: $p_A(0)=(-1)^n\det A$, so zero is an eigenvalue exactly when $A$ is singular, meaning noninvertible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
重根数的是多项式因子的重复次数，并不保证同样多个线性无关特征向量。例如 $\begin{bmatrix}3&1\\0&3\end{bmatrix}$ 与 $3I_2$ 都有多项式 $(t-3)^2$，但在特征值 $3$ 下的特征空间维数分别为 $1$ 和 $2$，这正是[[代数重数与几何重数]]的区别。零也可以是根：$p_A(0)=(-1)^n\det A$，所以当且仅当 $A$ 奇异、即不可逆时，零是特征值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ordinary definition requires a square matrix. For an $m\times n$ matrix with $m\ne n$, there is no same-size identity matrix from which to form this determinant, and $Av\in\mathbb F^m$ cannot be equated to $\lambda v\in\mathbb F^n$ as vectors in the same coordinate space. The ordinary eigenvalue problem concerns a map from one space back to itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这个普通定义要求方阵。若矩阵是 $m\times n$ 且 $m\ne n$，就没有尺寸匹配的单位矩阵来构造这个行列式；$Av\in\mathbb F^m$ 与 $\lambda v\in\mathbb F^n$ 也不能作为同一个坐标空间中的向量直接相等。普通特征值问题研究的是把一个空间映回自身的映射。
