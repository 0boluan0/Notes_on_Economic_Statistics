# Vector space
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*向量空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **vector space** is a collection of objects that we can add and multiply by numbers, while staying in the collection and following consistent calculation rules. The objects are called **vectors** and the numbers used to multiply them are called **scalars**. Coordinate pairs are the first example, but a whole matrix or a function can also play the role of one vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**向量空间**是一类可以做加法、乘以数，而且运算后仍留在同一集合、遵循一致计算规则的对象。对象叫**向量**，用来乘它们的数叫**标量**。最先见到的例子是坐标对，但一整个矩阵或一个函数，也可以作为一个向量。

> [!example] Adding and scaling coordinate pairs · 坐标对的加法与数乘
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $\mathbb R^2$, each vector is a pair of real numbers. Take $u=(1,2)$ and $v=(3,-1)$. Add matching coordinates, and multiply every coordinate by the scalar:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\mathbb R^2$ 中，每个向量是一对实数。取 $u=(1,2)$、$v=(3,-1)$。加法把对应坐标相加，数乘把每个坐标乘以同一个标量：
>
> $$u+v=(4,1),\qquad 2u=(2,4),\qquad -u=(-1,-2).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> All three results are still real coordinate pairs. We can also combine the operations: $2u-v=(-1,5)$. This is a **linear combination**: the coefficient $2$ tells us how much of $u$ to use, and the coefficient $-1$ tells us how much of $v$ to use. Coefficients may be negative, zero or fractional; they need not add to one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 三个结果仍然都是实数坐标对。还可以把两种运算结合：$2u-v=(-1,5)$。这叫**线性组合**：系数 $2$ 决定使用多少份 $u$，系数 $-1$ 决定使用多少份 $v$。系数可以为负、零或分数，不要求加起来等于一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What must the operations preserve?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*运算必须遵守什么？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Closed” means that an allowed operation never takes us outside the collection. For a vector space, this must hold for **every** pair of vectors and every allowed scalar. The scalars come from a specified **field** $\mathbb F$, commonly the real numbers $\mathbb R$ or the complex numbers $\mathbb C$. A field is a number system with the usual consistent rules for addition, subtraction, multiplication and division by a nonzero number. Unless stated otherwise, the coordinate examples here use real scalars.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“封闭”表示允许的运算不会把结果带出这个集合。对向量空间，这必须对**任意**一对向量、任意允许的标量成立。标量来自指定的**域** $\mathbb F$，通常是实数 $\mathbb R$ 或复数 $\mathbb C$。域是一种数系，具有一致的加减乘除规则，其中除数不能为零。除非另有说明，这里的坐标例子使用实数标量。

> [!abstract] The rules of a vector space · 向量空间的运算规则
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A set $V$, together with addition and scalar multiplication over $\mathbb F$, is a vector space when the operations stay in $V$ and satisfy the rules below. Here $u,v,w$ are any vectors in $V$, while $a,b$ are any scalars in $\mathbb F$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 集合 $V$ 连同加法及来自 $\mathbb F$ 的数乘构成向量空间，要求运算结果仍在 $V$，且满足下列规则。其中 $u,v,w$ 是 $V$ 中任意向量，$a,b$ 是 $\mathbb F$ 中任意标量。
>
> | Rule / 规则 | Formula / 公式 |
> |---|---|
> | Addition can be reordered / 加法可交换 | $u+v=v+u$ |
> | Addition can be regrouped / 加法可结合 | $(u+v)+w=u+(v+w)$ |
> | A zero vector exists / 存在零向量 | $v+0=v$ |
> | Every vector has an additive inverse / 每个向量有加法逆元 | $v+(-v)=0$ |
> | Successive scalings combine / 连续数乘可结合 | $a(bv)=(ab)v$ |
> | Scaling by one changes nothing / 乘一不改变向量 | $1v=v$ |
> | Scaling distributes over vector addition / 数乘对向量加法分配 | $a(u+v)=au+av$ |
> | Adding scalars distributes / 标量和可分配 | $(a+b)v=av+bv$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The $0$ in $V$ means its additive identity: adding it changes nothing. In $\mathbb R^2$ it is $(0,0)$. These rules also give $0v=0$ and $(-1)v=-v$. Thus allowing every scalar includes the ability to collapse to zero and reverse a vector. The word “space” does not add a requirement to multiply two vectors together; addition and scalar multiplication are the operations being specified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$V$ 中的 $0$ 指加法恒等元：加上它以后什么也不变。在 $\mathbb R^2$ 中，它是 $(0,0)$。这些规则也推出 $0v=0$、$(-1)v=-v$，所以允许任意标量，就包括能缩到零、能把向量反向。“空间”这个词没有额外要求两个向量之间必须能相乘；这里规定的运算是加法与数乘。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Vectors need not be coordinate arrows
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*向量不一定是坐标箭头*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All real $m\times n$ matrices form a vector space when we add corresponding entries and multiply all entries by a real number; its zero is the all-zero matrix. For example, a $2\times2$ matrix is **one vector in this space**, although it contains four entries. Real polynomials of degree at most $d$, including the zero polynomial, also form a vector space: adding or scaling them cannot create a power higher than $d$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有实数 $m\times n$ 矩阵按对应元素相加、每个元素乘以实数，构成向量空间；其中的零是全零矩阵。例如，一个 $2\times2$ 矩阵虽然有四个元素，在这个空间里却是**一个向量**。次数不超过 $d$ 的实系数多项式连同零多项式也构成向量空间：相加或数乘不会产生高于 $d$ 的幂。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The continuous real functions on a fixed interval provide another example. Addition is pointwise: $(f+g)(t)=f(t)+g(t)$, and $(af)(t)=af(t)$. Sums and scalar multiples remain continuous; the zero vector is the function that is zero at every input. These examples explain why the definition describes operations rather than requiring a particular shape of object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定区间上的连续实函数也是一个例子。加法按每个输入点定义：$(f+g)(t)=f(t)+g(t)$，数乘为 $(af)(t)=af(t)$；相加、数乘后仍然连续。零向量是在每个输入点都取零的函数。这些例子说明，定义关心运算怎样进行，而不是要求对象长成某一种样子。

## 最小反例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A line that fails the definition*

The line $S=\{(x,y):x+y=1\}$ is not a vector space under the usual operations. It contains $(1,0)$, but $0(1,0)=(0,0)$ is outside $S$; also $(1,0)+(1,0)=(2,0)$ is outside it. By contrast, the line $x+y=0$ contains zero and retains every linear combination. It is a [[子空间|subspace]] of $\mathbb R^2$, meaning a smaller vector space using the inherited operations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直线 $S=\{(x,y):x+y=1\}$ 在通常运算下不是向量空间。它包含 $(1,0)$，但 $0(1,0)=(0,0)$ 不在 $S$ 中；$(1,0)+(1,0)=(2,0)$ 也不在其中。相比之下，直线 $x+y=0$ 包含零，且保留任意线性组合；它是 $\mathbb R^2$ 的[[子空间]]，即沿用原运算的较小向量空间。

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The operations and scalar field are part of the definition*

Checking addition alone is insufficient. The first quadrant $\{(x,y):x,y\ge0\}$ contains zero and is closed under addition, but multiplying $(1,1)$ by $-1$ leaves it. The same underlying objects with different operations or a different scalar field can therefore give different structures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只检查加法不够。第一象限 $\{(x,y):x,y\ge0\}$ 包含零、对加法封闭，但把 $(1,1)$ 乘以 $-1$ 就离开了它。同一批对象如果更换运算或标量域，得到的结构也可能不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, in $\mathbb C$ write $i^2=-1$. Using real coefficients, $a\cdot1+b\cdot i=0$ forces $a=b=0$. Using complex coefficients, $i\cdot1-1\cdot i=0$ is already a nontrivial relation. This changes [[线性无关|linear independence]], and consequently [[基|bases]] and [[维数|dimension]]. Always identify the allowed scalars before asking which linear combinations are possible; [[张成|span]] describes the complete set of those combinations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如在 $\mathbb C$ 中，$i^2=-1$。使用实数系数时，$a\cdot1+b\cdot i=0$ 迫使 $a=b=0$；允许复数系数时，$i\cdot1-1\cdot i=0$ 已经是一条非平凡关系。标量域改变了[[线性无关]]性，进而影响[[基]]与[[维数]]。判断能形成什么线性组合之前，要先明确允许哪些标量；[[张成]]描述的就是全部这些组合组成的集合。
