# 成本领先是以较广市场为范围追求可持续相对成本优势的竞争战略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cost leadership pursues a sustainable relative cost advantage across a broad market.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

成本领先以较广泛的客户市场为竞争范围，通过[[价值链]]的配置追求低于竞争者的成本，同时提供客户能够接受的质量与功能。若只面向一个狭窄细分市场追求成本优势，则属于[[集聚战略|成本集聚]]。成本是资源耗用，价格是向客户收费，两者不能互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cost leadership configures the [[价值链|value chain]] to pursue lower costs than rivals while supplying acceptable quality and features across a broad market. Seeking a cost advantage only within a narrow segment is [[集聚战略|cost focus]]. Cost measures resources consumed; price is what the customer is charged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自拟例：同一可比产品，甲的单位总成本为 60，乙为 75，均卖 90，单位利润分别为 30 和 15。甲也可降价，但低成本不要求始终报最低价；反过来，乙暂时卖 55，只能证明它低价销售，不能证明成本领先。这里固定产品质量、成本口径和销量条件，不用单一毛利率替代成本比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed example, comparable products cost firms A and B 60 and 75 per unit. Both charge 90, earning unit profits of 30 and 15. A may cut its price, but low cost does not require the lowest selling price. B temporarily charging 55 establishes low pricing, not low cost. Product quality, cost definitions, and volume conditions are held comparable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“只有一家企业可以独享最低成本地位”可描述严格唯一最低值，但不是识别这种战略取向的普遍必要条件：多家企业可以追求它，甚至在某个口径下成本并列。战略是否成功、优势是否持久，仍须核验技术、组织和竞争反应，而不是依据历史案例名称直接判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only one firm can occupy a strictly unique lowest-cost position, but uniqueness is not a universal requirement for identifying this strategic orientation: several firms may pursue it or have tied measured costs. Success and durability require evidence about technology, organization, and rival responses, not merely a historical company label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
