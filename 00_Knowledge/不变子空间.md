# Invariant subspace
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*不变子空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An invariant subspace is a collection of directions that a linear map never sends outside that same subspace. Individual vectors may move, stretch, rotate or become zero. What stays fixed is the boundary of the allowed space: starting inside it guarantees that the output stays inside it. This lets us study part of a transformation as a smaller problem in its own right.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不变子空间是一组方向构成的子空间，线性映射不会把其中的向量送到这个子空间外。单个向量可以移动、伸缩、旋转，甚至变成零；保持的是活动范围：从里面出发，输出仍在里面。于是可以把原变换的一部分当成更小的独立问题来研究。

> [!example] An axis can be preserved without fixing its vectors / 保留坐标轴不等于每个向量不动
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.$$
> The horizontal axis $W=\{(a,0)^T:a\in\mathbb R\}$ is invariant: $A(a,0)^T=(2a,0)^T$ stays horizontal. The vertical axis is invariant too, even though every vector on it is sent to zero. The diagonal line $\{(a,a)^T:a\in\mathbb R\}$ is not invariant: $(1,1)^T$ is sent to $(2,0)^T$, outside that line.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对显示的矩阵，水平轴 $W=\{(a,0)^T:a\in\mathbb R\}$ 不变，因为 $A(a,0)^T=(2a,0)^T$ 仍在水平轴上。竖直轴也不变，虽然其中每个向量都被送到零。对角线 $\{(a,a)^T:a\in\mathbb R\}$ 却不是不变子空间：$(1,1)^T$ 被送到线外的 $(2,0)^T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The condition concerns every vector in the subspace
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*条件要求子空间内的每个向量都留下来*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $V$ be a real or complex vector space, let $T:V\to V$ be a [[线性映射|linear map]], and let $W$ be a [[子空间|subspace]] of $V$. Linearity means $T(au+bv)=aT(u)+bT(v)$, while being a subspace means that linear combinations stay in $W$. We say that $W$ is invariant under $T$ when
$$T(W)\subseteq W,\qquad\text{that is, }T(w)\in W\text{ for every }w\in W.$$
This is inclusion, not necessarily equality. The vertical-axis example has $T(W)=\{0\}$, a proper subset of $W$. The definition also does not require $T(w)=w$. Both $\{0\}$ and $V$ are always invariant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $V$ 是实或复向量空间，$T:V\to V$ 是[[线性映射]]，$W$ 是 $V$ 的[[子空间]]。线性性指 $T(au+bv)=aT(u)+bT(v)$，子空间则要求线性组合仍在 $W$ 中。若每个 $w\in W$ 都满足 $T(w)\in W$，即 $T(W)\subseteq W$，就称 $W$ 在 $T$ 下不变。这里是包含关系，不必相等：竖直轴例子的像为 $\{0\}$，只是 $W$ 的真子集；定义也不要求 $T(w)=w$。只含零向量的零子空间 $\{0\}$ 与全空间 $V$ 总是不变的。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To check a finite-dimensional candidate, choose a [[基|basis]] $w_1,\ldots,w_k$ of $W$. It suffices to check $T(w_j)\in W$ for every basis vector. Indeed, every $w\in W$ is a linear combination of these vectors, and linearity makes $T(w)$ the same combination of their images. A few arbitrary sample vectors do not provide this guarantee unless they span the whole candidate subspace.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检查有限维候选子空间时，取它的一组[[基]] $w_1,\ldots,w_k$。逐个确认 $T(w_j)\in W$ 就足够：任意 $w\in W$ 都是这些基向量的线性组合，线性性又使 $T(w)$ 成为各个像的相同线性组合。随便检查几个样本向量则没有这种保证，除非它们张成整个候选子空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once invariance holds, the restriction $T|_W:W\to W$ means applying the same rule only to inputs in $W$. Repeated applications remain inside $W$ as well: $T(w),T^2(w),\ldots\in W$. Without invariance, restricting the input alone would not produce a transformation from $W$ back to itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不变性成立后，限制映射 $T|_W:W\to W$ 就是只对 $W$ 中的输入使用原规则。反复作用仍留在 $W$ 内，即 $T(w),T^2(w),\ldots\in W$。若没有不变性，仅限制输入范围，并不能得到从 $W$ 回到自身的变换。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An invariant line is special; larger spaces allow mixing
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一维不变直线特殊，更大子空间允许方向混合*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nonzero $v$, the line $W=\operatorname{span}(v)$ is invariant exactly when $T(v)=\lambda v$ for some scalar $\lambda$ in the chosen field. That is precisely a [[特征对|nonzero eigenvector and its eigenvalue]]. A higher-dimensional invariant subspace need not have every vector as an eigenvector. For example, in $\mathbb R^3$ let
$$T(x,y,z)=(-y,x,2z).$$
The plane $z=0$ is invariant, but its nonzero vectors rotate by a quarter turn and none is a real eigenvector within that plane. The field matters: over $\mathbb C$, that rotation has complex eigenvectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非零 $v$，直线 $W=\operatorname{span}(v)$ 不变，当且仅当存在所用数域中的标量 $\lambda$，使 $T(v)=\lambda v$；这正是[[特征对|非零特征向量与特征值]]。高维不变子空间中的向量却不必逐个都是特征向量。例如在 $\mathbb R^3$ 上令 $T(x,y,z)=(-y,x,2z)$，平面 $z=0$ 不变，但其中非零向量都旋转四分之一圈，没有平面内的实特征向量。数域很重要：转到 $\mathbb C$ 后，该旋转有复特征向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is why [[Schur分解|Schur decomposition]] can expose invariant subspaces even when individual eigenvectors do not form a basis. In complex Schur form, its leading basis columns span spaces that stay closed under the matrix action; in real Schur form, take only a whole sequence of diagonal blocks. The [[Jordan、Schur与SVD用途边界|choice between Jordan, Schur and SVD]] uses this distinction between preserving a subspace and preserving each of its directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也解释了为什么即使单个特征向量不能构成基，[[Schur分解]]仍能揭示不变子空间：复 Schur 形前部的基向量张成对矩阵作用封闭的空间；实 Schur 形则须取完整的一串对角块。[[Jordan、Schur与SVD用途边界]]就需要分清“保留整个子空间”与“保留其中每一条方向”。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*, 4th ed., §5A, definition 5.2 and the discussion of invariant lines, printed pp.133–134 / PDF pp.147–148](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=147): invariance as inclusion, restriction to the subspace, and the connection with eigenvectors.
- The axis, diagonal-line and plane-rotation examples, the basis check and the repeated-application argument are verified directly from the displayed linear maps.
- [LAPACK, Nonsymmetric Eigenproblems](https://netlib.org/lapack95/lug95/node34.html): Schur vectors as bases of invariant subspaces; real Schur selections must respect whole diagonal blocks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Axler 第5A节定义5.2及不变直线讨论，印刷133–134页／PDF147–148页，核对包含关系、子空间上的限制映射及与特征向量的联系。
- 坐标轴、对角线与平面旋转例子，以及基检验和反复作用的论证，均由显示的线性映射直接核算。
- LAPACK 非对称特征问题说明核对 Schur 向量作为不变子空间基的用途；实 Schur 选择需尊重完整对角块。
