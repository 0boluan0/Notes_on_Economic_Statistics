# 钳制问题是不可缺少的权利持有人利用否决能力索取更多交易收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Holdout occurs when an indispensable rights holder uses veto power to demand a larger share of the gains from agreement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

钳制问题出现在一项活动必须集齐多方许可或互补权利才能进行，而某个权利持有人知道自己不可缺少，因而拖延或拒绝同意，以争取更大份额。这里要识别的是**协议需要他的同意**，不只是有人要价高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holdout arises when an activity requires several complementary rights or permissions and an indispensable holder delays consent to seek more of the gains. The key is that the agreement needs that holder's consent, not simply that someone asks a high price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设工厂获得两位渔民许可后能多得15，每位渔民实际损失2，且没有其他成本。存在净收益为11的可行协议；但若双方都坚持至少收9，合计18超过工厂收益，协议不能按这些要价达成。这个例子说明索取收益的策略可能阻碍交易，不证明任何谈判程序都会失败。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose permission from two fishers gives a factory fifteen, each fisher loses two and there are no other costs. A feasible agreement creates eleven of gains. If each insists on at least nine, their joint demand of eighteen prevents agreement at those terms. This illustrates a strategic obstacle, not inevitable failure under every bargaining procedure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与[[公共品搭便车|搭便车]]相比，钳制者控制不可缺少的许可并要求别人付钱；搭便车者则希望别人付出成本，自己仍分享改善。产权归属和协议规则会改变遇到哪种问题，因此[[科斯定理]]的“有效协商”需要单独验证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unlike [[公共品搭便车|free riding]], holdout uses indispensable consent to demand payment. A free rider wants others to fund an improvement whose benefits remain available to them. Entitlements and agreement rules shape these problems, so the [[科斯定理|Coase theorem's]] bargaining assumption must be checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
