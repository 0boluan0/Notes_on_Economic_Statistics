# Testing independence from a distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*随机变量独立的分布判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To check whether two random variables are independent, compare their actual probabilities together with what multiplying their separate probabilities would predict. [[随机变量独立|Independence]] requires this agreement for every relevant event. A joint PMF, joint CDF, or joint density packages those events in a form that is easier to check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断两个随机变量是否独立，可以比较两个东西：它们实际共同出现的概率，以及把各自概率相乘会得到的结果。[[随机变量独立|独立性]]要求所有相应事件都满足这种一致性。联合 PMF、联合 CDF 或联合密度，可以把这些事件整理成更方便检查的形式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[联合分布|joint distribution]] describes combinations of values, while each [[边际分布|marginal distribution]] describes one variable on its own. First obtain both from the given model. Multiplying the marginals before checking the joint distribution would simply assume the independence we are trying to test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[联合分布]]描述取值的组合，[[边际分布]]则分别描述单个变量。应先从给定模型中得到它们，再做比较。如果还没检查联合分布，就先把边际概率相乘当作联合概率，等于直接假定了正在检验的独立性。

## 常用计算形式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Start with the representation you have*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For discrete variables, a [[概率质量函数|PMF]] gives the probability of each value. Write $p_{X,Y}(x,y)=P(X=x,Y=y)$ for a cell of the joint table, and $p_X(x)$ and $p_Y(y)$ for its row and column totals. Independence is equivalent to
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于离散变量，[[概率质量函数|PMF]] 给出每个取值的概率。用 $p_{X,Y}(x,y)=P(X=x,Y=y)$ 表示联合表中的一个格子，用 $p_X(x)$、$p_Y(y)$ 表示它对应的行合计和列合计。独立性等价于

$$
p_{X,Y}(x,y)=p_X(x)p_Y(y)
\quad\text{for every pair }(x,y).
$$

> [!example] Every cell equals its row total times its column total · 逐格比较
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose a model gives the following table. The last column and last row are obtained by addition, without assuming independence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设模型给出了下表。最右列和最下行由相加得到，计算它们不需要独立性假设。
>
> | $X\backslash Y$ | $Y=0$ | $Y=1$ | $p_X(x)$ |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | $X=0$ | $0.10$ | $0.30$ | $0.40$ |
> | $X=1$ | $0.15$ | $0.45$ | $0.60$ |
> | $p_Y(y)$ | $0.25$ | $0.75$ | $1$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The four products are $0.40\times0.25=0.10$, $0.40\times0.75=0.30$, $0.60\times0.25=0.15$, and $0.60\times0.75=0.45$. Every cell agrees, so $X$ and $Y$ are independent. Values outside the displayed ranges have zero marginal and joint probabilities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 四个乘积分别为 $0.40\times0.25=0.10$、$0.40\times0.75=0.30$、$0.60\times0.25=0.15$、$0.60\times0.75=0.45$。每格都相等，所以 $X$、$Y$ 独立。所列范围以外的取值，其边际和相应联合概率都为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The test covers every combination of marginally possible values, including joint cells whose probability is zero. If a cell is zero while both its row and column totals are positive, the product is positive and independence fails immediately. A single failing cell disproves independence; a single matching cell does not establish it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检查范围包括两个变量各自可能取值的**所有组合**，也包括联合概率为零的格子。如果某格为零，而对应行、列合计都为正，那么乘积为正，立即说明不独立。一个失败的格子足以否定独立，一个成立的格子则不足以证明独立。

## The general test uses cumulative probabilities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*没有密度时也能用的通用判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[累积分布函数|cumulative distribution function]] (CDF) $F_X(x)=P(X\leq x)$ asks how much probability lies at or below a threshold $x$. For two variables, $F_{X,Y}(x,y)=P(X\leq x,Y\leq y)$ asks for both threshold conditions at once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[累积分布函数|累积分布函数（CDF）]] $F_X(x)=P(X\leq x)$ 回答“取值不超过门槛 $x$ 的概率是多少”。对两个变量，$F_{X,Y}(x,y)=P(X\leq x,Y\leq y)$ 则同时要求两个门槛条件成立。

> [!abstract] CDF criterion · CDF 判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Real-valued random variables $X,Y$ are independent if and only if
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 实值随机变量 $X,Y$ 独立，当且仅当
>
> $$F_{X,Y}(x,y)=F_X(x)F_Y(y)\qquad\text{for all }x,y\in\mathbb R.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This checks every pair of thresholds. Those cumulative probabilities determine the whole joint distribution, so the criterion is sufficient as well as necessary. It does not require a PMF or density.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里要检查每一对门槛。这些累积概率能够确定整个联合分布，所以该条件既必要又充分；它不要求 PMF 或密度存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, let $X$ be $0$ or $1$ with equal probabilities and set $Y=X$. At $x=y=0$, the joint CDF is $P(X=0,Y=0)=1/2$, but the product is $(1/2)(1/2)=1/4$. This one threshold pair proves dependence. To prove independence, an equality must hold for all thresholds, not merely for a few selected ones.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，让 $X$ 等可能地取 $0$、$1$，并令 $Y=X$。在 $x=y=0$ 时，联合 CDF 为 $P(X=0,Y=0)=1/2$，两个边际 CDF 的乘积却为 $(1/2)(1/2)=1/4$。这一对门槛就证明了不独立；若要证明独立，则必须对所有门槛成立，不能只抽查几处。

## When a joint density exists
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*存在联合密度时*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the pair has a joint [[概率密度函数|probability density]] $f_{X,Y}$, with marginal densities $f_X,f_Y$, independence is equivalent to
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果数对存在联合[[概率密度函数|概率密度]] $f_{X,Y}$，边际密度为 $f_X,f_Y$，独立性等价于

$$
f_{X,Y}(x,y)=f_X(x)f_Y(y)\quad\text{almost everywhere on }\mathbb R^2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Almost everywhere” here means except on a set of two-dimensional area zero. Densities produce probabilities through integration, so changing density values only on such a set changes no probability. Include the regions where the density is zero when checking a factorisation; a formula valid only inside part of the allowed region is not the whole density.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里“几乎处处”表示可以排除一个二维面积为零的集合。密度通过积分给出概率，因此只在这样的集合上改动密度值，不会改变任何概率。检查分解时，密度为零的区域也必须包括在内；只在允许范围中的一部分成立的公式，不是完整的密度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The existence of two marginal densities alone does not guarantee a joint density; [[联合分布|joint distributions]] explain this boundary. Use the CDF criterion when a density representation is unavailable. PMF and density formulas are useful versions of the same independence test, with their own applicability conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仅有两个边际密度，不保证联合密度存在；这一边界在[[联合分布]]中解释。没有密度表示时，可以使用 CDF 判据。PMF 与密度公式是同一个独立性判据在各自适用条件下的计算形式。
