# Bias from estimating a control coefficient on the same sample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同样本估计控制系数的偏差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[控制变量|control variate]] corrects a simulation average using another quantity whose mean is known. If we learn the size of that correction from the very observations being corrected, the fitted coefficient and the observed deviation move together. Their product can have a nonzero average over repeated samples, introducing [[估计量偏差|estimator bias]]. Same-sample fitting can cause bias; it does not necessarily cause bias in every model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[控制变量]]利用另一个均值已知的量来修正模拟平均值。如果修正的力度也由正在被修正的这批观测估计，拟合系数和观测偏离就会共同变化。把它们相乘后，在反复抽样中的平均值可能不为零，从而产生[[估计量偏差]]。同样本拟合可能产生偏差，并不是每个模型都必然有偏。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $Y$ for the output whose mean $\mu=E[Y]$ we want, and $C$ for a control with correctly known mean $m=E[C]$. For a fixed positive integer $N$, take $N$ [[独立同分布|independent, identically distributed pairs]] $(Y_i,C_i)$. Let $\bar Y=N^{-1}\sum_iY_i$ and $\bar C=N^{-1}\sum_iC_i$ be the two averages. With a fixed real coefficient $\beta$ and finite absolute means,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $Y$ 表示目标输出，要估计的是 $\mu=E[Y]$；用 $C$ 表示控制量，其均值 $m=E[C]$ 已正确知道。对预先固定的正整数 $N$，从 $N$ 对[[独立同分布]]的观测 $(Y_i,C_i)$ 计算两个平均值 $\bar Y=N^{-1}\sum_iY_i$、$\bar C=N^{-1}\sum_iC_i$。若实数系数 $\beta$ 是固定的，且两个变量的绝对期望有限，则

$$
\widehat\mu_\beta=\bar Y-\beta(\bar C-m),
\qquad E[\widehat\mu_\beta]=\mu-\beta(E[\bar C]-m)=\mu.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last equality uses [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]] and takes a fixed number outside the expectation. An estimated coefficient $\widehat\beta$ is itself random; this step no longer works merely because $E[\bar C-m]=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一步使用[[期望线性性]]，把固定数提出期望。估计出来的系数 $\widehat\beta$ 本身也是随机的，不能仅凭 $E[\bar C-m]=0$ 就沿用这一步。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two observations already give a counterexample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*只用两个观测就能看见偏差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take independent controls $C_1,C_2$ [[连续均匀分布|uniform on $(0,1)$]], so each has density one there, and set $Y_i=C_i^2$. Integrating against that density gives the known control mean $m=\int_0^1c\,dc=1/2$ and the target $\mu=\int_0^1c^2\,dc=1/3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取独立的控制量 $C_1,C_2$，都在 $(0,1)$ 上服从[[连续均匀分布]]，区间内密度为一；令 $Y_i=C_i^2$。对这个密度积分可得已知控制均值 $m=\int_0^1c\,dc=1/2$，而目标为 $\mu=\int_0^1c^2\,dc=1/3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fit a straight line to the two points $(C_1,Y_1)$ and $(C_2,Y_2)$ by [[普通最小二乘|ordinary least squares with an intercept]]. The two controls differ with probability one, so the fitted line passes through both points. Its slope is the change in $Y$ divided by the change in $C$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对两个点 $(C_1,Y_1)$、$(C_2,Y_2)$ 做含截距的[[普通最小二乘]]直线拟合。两个控制量以概率一互不相等，拟合直线便穿过这两点；斜率是 $Y$ 的变化除以 $C$ 的变化：

$$
\widehat\beta=\frac{C_1^2-C_2^2}{C_1-C_2}=C_1+C_2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the probability-zero event $C_1=C_2$, define $\widehat\beta=C_1+C_2$ as well; this makes the formula defined everywhere without changing its expectation. Applying that fitted slope to the same two observations gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在概率为零的事件 $C_1=C_2$ 上也定义 $\widehat\beta=C_1+C_2$，使公式处处有定义而不改变期望。把这个拟合斜率应用于原来两个观测，得到

$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat\mu_{\widehat\beta}
&=\frac{C_1^2+C_2^2}{2}
 -(C_1+C_2)\left(\frac{C_1+C_2}{2}-\frac12\right)\\
&=\frac{C_1+C_2}{2}-C_1C_2.
\end{aligned}
$$

> [!example] The repeated-sample average is $1/4$, not the target $1/3$ · 反复抽样的平均值偏离目标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Independence gives $E[C_1C_2]=E[C_1]E[C_2]=1/4$. Therefore
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 独立性给出 $E[C_1C_2]=E[C_1]E[C_2]=1/4$，所以
>
> $$E[\widehat\mu_{\widehat\beta}]=\frac12-\frac14=\frac14,
> \qquad \operatorname{Bias}(\widehat\mu_{\widehat\beta})=\frac14-\frac13=-\frac1{12}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> All variables here are bounded. The bias comes from estimating and applying the coefficient on the same observations, rather than from a nonexistent expectation or an incorrectly specified control mean.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里所有变量都有界，控制均值也完全正确；偏差来自用同一批观测估计并应用系数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The product responsible for the bias
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*偏差来自哪一个乘积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the general scalar case, suppose $Y,C$ have finite second moments and $v_C=\operatorname{Var}(C)>0$. The [[控制变量最优系数|population variance-minimising coefficient]] is the fixed number $\beta^*=\operatorname{Cov}(Y,C)/v_C$. Adding and subtracting the estimator that uses this fixed coefficient gives the exact identity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般的单控制量情形中，假设 $Y,C$ 的二阶矩有限，且 $v_C=\operatorname{Var}(C)>0$。[[控制变量最优系数|使总体方差最小的系数]]是固定数 $\beta^*=\operatorname{Cov}(Y,C)/v_C$。加上再减去使用这个固定系数的估计量，就得到恒等式

$$
\widehat\mu_{\widehat\beta}-\mu
=\underbrace{(\widehat\mu_{\beta^*}-\mu)}_{\text{fixed-coefficient error}}
 +\underbrace{(\widehat\beta-\beta^*)(m-\bar C)}_{\text{extra product}}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The fixed-coefficient error has mean zero. If $E|\widehat\beta(\bar C-m)|<\infty$, the bias is therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定系数的误差均值为零。若 $E|\widehat\beta(\bar C-m)|<\infty$，偏差便是

$$
\operatorname{Bias}(\widehat\mu_{\widehat\beta})
=E[(\widehat\beta-\beta^*)(m-\bar C)].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite $E[\widehat\beta^2]$ is one sufficient integrability condition, by the [[期望的Cauchy–Schwarz不等式|Cauchy–Schwarz inequality for expectations]]. The fitted coefficient itself need not be unbiased for $\beta^*$. Even when both factors in the displayed product have mean zero, their dependence can give the product a nonzero expectation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[期望的Cauchy–Schwarz不等式]]，$E[\widehat\beta^2]<\infty$ 是保证上述可积性的一条充分条件。拟合系数本身未必是 $\beta^*$ 的无偏估计；即使上式中两个因子的均值都为零，依赖关系也可能使乘积期望不为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the uniform example, $E[C^3]=1/4$ and $\operatorname{Var}(C)=1/12$, so $\beta^*=(1/4-(1/3)(1/2))/(1/12)=1$. Here $E[\widehat\beta]=1$ really does hold. Nevertheless,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在均匀分布例子中，$E[C^3]=1/4$、$\operatorname{Var}(C)=1/12$，所以 $\beta^*=(1/4-(1/3)(1/2))/(1/12)=1$。这一次也确实有 $E[\widehat\beta]=1$，但仍然有

$$
(\widehat\beta-1)(1/2-\bar C)
=-\frac12(C_1+C_2-1)^2,
\qquad E\left[-\frac12(C_1+C_2-1)^2\right]=-\frac1{12}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus even an unbiased fitted slope does not make the corrected mean unbiased. Conversely, some same-sample fits are unbiased; for example, if $Y=a+bC$ exactly and the sample slope is identifiable, the fitted slope equals $b$ and the correction gives the constant $a+bm=\mu$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，拟合斜率无偏也不保证修正后的均值无偏。反过来，有些同样本拟合确实无偏：例如 $Y=a+bC$ 精确成立，且样本能够识别斜率时，拟合斜率就是 $b$，修正结果恒等于 $a+bm=\mu$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A small probability order does not bound expected bias
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*概率意义下的小量不自动给出期望偏差的阶*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $R_N=O_p(a_N)$, for positive numbers $a_N$, means that $R_N/a_N$ is bounded in probability: for any small probability tolerance, one can choose a fixed finite bound that is exceeded with at most that probability for all sufficiently large $N$. It describes how large errors are likely to be; rare extreme values may still dominate their expectations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正数序列 $a_N$，记号 $R_N=O_p(a_N)$ 表示 $R_N/a_N$ 在概率意义下有界：给定任意小的概率容忍度，都能选出一个固定有限界，使足够大的 $N$ 下越过该界的概率不超过这个容忍度。它控制误差通常有多大，但罕见的极端值仍可能支配期望。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the fitted coefficient satisfies $\widehat\beta-\beta^*=O_p(N^{-1/2})$, then finite control variance gives $\bar C-m=O_p(N^{-1/2})$. Their product is $O_p(N^{-1})$. This is a statement about the extra **random error term**, not by itself a proof that its expectation is $O(N^{-1})$. The fixed-coefficient error is $O_p(N^{-1/2})$ under the finite-variance assumptions; its actual variance can also be zero in special models.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果拟合系数满足 $\widehat\beta-\beta^*=O_p(N^{-1/2})$，控制量的有限方差又给出 $\bar C-m=O_p(N^{-1/2})$，那么两者的乘积是 $O_p(N^{-1})$。这是对额外**随机误差项**的描述，本身并不证明其期望为 $O(N^{-1})$。有限方差条件下，固定系数误差为 $O_p(N^{-1/2})$；特殊模型中，它的实际方差也可能为零。

> [!warning] Rare values can dominate an expectation · 罕见大值仍能支配期望
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For each positive integer $N$, let $R_N=N$ with probability $1/N$, and $R_N=0$ otherwise. Then $R_N=O_p(N^{-1})$: for any fixed positive $M$, $P(|R_N|>M/N)\le1/N\to0$. Yet $E[R_N]=1$ for every $N$. A probability-order statement alone cannot justify an expectation-order claim.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每个正整数 $N$，令 $R_N$ 以概率 $1/N$ 取值 $N$，否则取零。它满足 $R_N=O_p(N^{-1})$，因为任取固定 $M>0$ 都有 $P(|R_N|>M/N)\le1/N\to0$；但每个 $N$ 都有 $E[R_N]=1$。只有概率阶，不能据此断言期望的阶。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stronger, explicit condition does give a bias bound. Suppose some fixed finite $K$ satisfies $E[(\widehat\beta-\beta^*)^2]\le K/N$ for all sufficiently large $N$. Because $E[(\bar C-m)^2]=v_C/N$, Cauchy–Schwarz gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更强且明确的条件可以给出偏差界。假设存在固定有限常数 $K$，使足够大的 $N$ 都满足 $E[(\widehat\beta-\beta^*)^2]\le K/N$。由于 $E[(\bar C-m)^2]=v_C/N$，Cauchy–Schwarz 不等式给出

$$
|\operatorname{Bias}(\widehat\mu_{\widehat\beta})|
\le\sqrt{E[(\widehat\beta-\beta^*)^2]\,E[(\bar C-m)^2]}
\le\frac{\sqrt{K v_C}}{N}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a valid $O(N^{-1})$ expected-bias bound under a mean-square condition. It is not a universal property of fitted controls. A positive population control variance does not even ensure a positive sample control variance in every finite sample; a regression rule must specify what it does on a zero-denominator event. Unstable coefficients, small samples or an increasing number of controls require their own checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这才是在均方条件下成立的 $O(N^{-1})$ 期望偏差界，而不是所有拟合控制系数的普遍性质。总体控制方差为正，甚至不能保证每份有限样本的控制方差都为正；回归规则必须说明分母为零时如何处理。系数不稳定、小样本或控制量数量不断增加时，都需要另外核查。

## Why an independent pilot restores unbiasedness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*独立预备样本为什么能恢复无偏性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use a pilot sample to fit a finite coefficient $\widetilde\beta$, freeze it, and apply it to a new production sample of the fixed size $N$, independent of the entire pilot. Conditional on the pilot, the coefficient is fixed and the new control average still has mean $m$. With the corrected estimator integrable, [[条件期望|conditional expectation]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用预备样本（pilot sample）拟合出有限系数 $\widetilde\beta$ 后将它固定，再应用于一份大小为预先固定的 $N$、与整个预备样本独立的新模拟样本。给定预备样本，系数就是固定的，新控制平均值的均值仍为 $m$。在修正估计量可积的条件下，[[条件期望]]给出

$$
E[\bar Y-\widetilde\beta(\bar C-m)\mid\text{pilot}]
=\mu-\widetilde\beta(m-m)=\mu.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[全期望公式|law of total expectation]] then gives unconditional mean $\mu$. A sufficient integrability condition is $E|\widetilde\beta|<\infty$, together with finite absolute means of $Y,C$ and the stated independence. If finite unconditional variance is also needed, finite $E[\widetilde\beta^2]$ and finite second moments of $Y,C$ are sufficient; unbiasedness alone does not guarantee useful precision.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再用[[全期望公式]]，无条件均值也是 $\mu$。一组保证可积性的充分条件是 $E|\widetilde\beta|<\infty$、$Y,C$ 的绝对期望有限，以及上述独立性。若还要求无条件方差有限，则 $E[\widetilde\beta^2]<\infty$ 配合 $Y,C$ 的有限二阶矩是一组充分条件；无偏本身不保证估计足够精确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Splitting iid replications into two fixed disjoint groups implements this argument: one group fits the coefficient, the other estimates the mean. The production group has fewer observations than the original full sample. Reusing pilot observations in the final average, choosing the split from observed values, or splitting dependent observations does not automatically preserve the required independence. These cases need a separate argument. The control mean must still be correct; [[控制变量均值误设|misspecifying it]] creates a different source of bias.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把独立同分布的重复模拟按预先固定的规则分成两个互不重叠的组，就能实现这个论证：一组拟合系数，另一组估计均值，代价是最终估计使用的观测数减少。如果把预备观测重新放回最终平均、根据观测值决定如何分组，或拆分本来就相互依赖的观测，所需独立性都不会自动保留，必须另行论证。控制均值仍须正确；[[控制变量均值误设]]是另一种偏差来源。

The two-uniform-observation counterexample, rare-value example, and bias bound are calculated explicitly in this card.

- Owen 第 8 章第 8.9 节 PDF 第 29–32 页支持固定系数估计量、拟合斜率、式 (8.34) 的分解与独立预备样本论证。本文区分第 32 页的概率阶与期望偏差界，后者在上文明示的均方条件下推导；不把拟合斜率在一般情况下视为无偏。
