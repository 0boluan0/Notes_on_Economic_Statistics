# 在同一样本上估计并应用控制变量系数通常产生有限样本偏差，但在固定维度和常规条件下该偏差通常小于主抽样误差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Estimating and applying a control-variate coefficient on the same sample generally creates finite-sample bias, though under regular fixed-dimensional conditions it is usually smaller than the leading sampling error*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] “任意固定 β 都无偏”不能直接套到随机估计出的 β̂
> 固定系数时，$\beta(C-m)$ 的期望为零；但若 $\widehat\beta$ 与同一样本的 control 平均共同变化，两者乘积的期望一般不再等于期望的乘积。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The zero-mean correction proves unbiasedness for fixed $\beta$. When $\widehat\beta$ is estimated from the same observations, it co-moves with the sample control mean and the product need not have zero expectation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把总体最优系数记为 $\beta^*$、control 样本均值记为 $\bar C$，同样本回归估计量与固定最优估计量之差包含

$$
(\widehat\beta-\beta^*)(m-\bar C).
$$

两个因子各自可以均值为零，但其乘积通常不以零为期望，因此会出现有限样本偏差。在固定 control 维度和常规矩条件下，这个偏差通常为 $O(N^{-1})$，小于主抽样误差 $O_p(N^{-1/2})$；小样本、高维或不稳定回归时不能据此忽略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same-sample correction contains the product $(\widehat\beta-\beta^*)(m-\bar C)$. Even if each factor is centred, their product generally is not. Under standard fixed-dimensional conditions the resulting bias is usually order $N^{-1}$, smaller than the leading $N^{-1/2}$ sampling error, but that reassurance can fail in small or unstable problems.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若用独立 pilot 样本估计 $\widehat\beta$，再条件于 pilot 把它当固定系数应用于新样本，固定系数的无偏论证可以恢复；样本分割的代价是减少最终估计可用的路径数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Estimating the coefficient on an independent pilot sample and applying it to fresh draws restores the fixed-coefficient unbiasedness argument conditional on the pilot, at the cost of fewer draws for the final estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
