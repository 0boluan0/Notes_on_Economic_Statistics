# 可复现构建要求相同源、环境与指令产生逐字节相同的指定产物
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A reproducible build yields bit-for-bit identical specified artifacts from the same source, environment, and instructions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子判据
> 给定同一份源代码、构建环境和构建指令，任何一方都能重新生成全部**指定产物**的逐字节相同副本，才满足 Reproducible Builds 项目的定义。通常用加密哈希比较这些产物。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under the Reproducible Builds project definition, any party given the same source code, build environment, and build instructions must be able to recreate bit-for-bit identical copies of all **specified artifacts**. Cryptographic hashes are commonly used to compare those artifacts.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“指定”很重要：可执行文件、发行包或文件系统镜像可以是主产物，构建日志通常不是。作者或发行方必须先说明哪些源、哪些环境属性、哪些指令和哪些输出属于这次可复现性主张。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Specified” matters. Executables, distribution packages, or filesystem images may be primary artifacts, while build logs normally are not. The author or distributor must define the source, relevant environmental attributes, instructions, and outputs covered by the reproducibility claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

两次都“编译成功”、测试都通过，甚至程序行为看起来相同，都比逐字节相同更弱。反过来，日志中的路径或耗时不同也不必推翻一项只针对 binary 的可复现性主张。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two successful compilations, two passing test suites, or apparently equivalent runtime behaviour are all weaker than byte identity. Conversely, different paths or timings in logs need not defeat a claim that explicitly covers only a binary artifact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两个 binary 的测试结果相同但 SHA-256 不同，能否称其满足逐字节可复现判据？
>
> **答案：** 不能；行为相似不等于指定产物逐字节相同。
