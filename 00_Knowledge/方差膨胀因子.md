# 方差膨胀因子 VIF_j=1/(1-R_j²) 量化其他回归量使第 j 个 OLS 系数方差相对正交基准放大的倍数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The variance inflation factor VIF_j=1/(1-R_j²) measures how much the other regressors inflate the variance of the jth OLS coefficient relative to an orthogonal benchmark*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 把焦点回归量 $X_j$ 对模型中的其余设计列做辅助回归，记决定系数为 $R_j^2$。对一个单自由度回归量，在经典同方差模型下，
> $$
> VIF_j=\frac{1}{1-R_j^2},
> \qquad
> tolerance_j=1-R_j^2=\frac1{VIF_j}.
> $$
> 它比较“当前相关设计”与“保持 $X_j$ 总变异不变、但令它和其余列正交”的系数方差。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Regress the focal predictor $X_j$ on the remaining design columns and call the auxiliary coefficient of determination $R_j^2$. For a one-degree-of-freedom regressor under the classical homoskedastic model, $VIF_j=1/(1-R_j^2)$ and tolerance is its reciprocal. VIF compares the current correlated design with a benchmark that preserves the total variation of $X_j$ but makes it orthogonal to the remaining columns.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若模型含截距，并令 $SST_j=\sum_i(X_{ij}-\bar X_j)^2$，则
$$
\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_j\mid X)
=\frac{\sigma^2}{SST_j(1-R_j^2)}
=\frac{\sigma^2}{SST_j}\,VIF_j.
$$
所以 VIF=10 表示**方差**乘以 10，而标准误乘以 $\sqrt{10}\approx3.16$，不是标准误乘以 10。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With an intercept, $\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_j\mid X)=\sigma^2VIF_j/SST_j$. A VIF of ten therefore multiplies the variance by ten and the standard error by $\sqrt{10}\approx3.16$; it does not multiply the standard error by ten.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它能回答什么，不能回答什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What it can and cannot answer*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

VIF 完全由 $X$ 决定，不使用结果 $Y$。它诊断的是一个系数方向上的设计信息，不判断该变量是否重要、是否外生、函数形式是否正确，也不自动告诉你该删哪一列。$R_j^2=1$ 时已是完全共线，有限 VIF 公式的前提失效；此时应回到[[满列秩与OLS唯一性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
VIF depends only on $X$, not on the outcome $Y$. It diagnoses design information for one coefficient direction; it does not determine substantive importance, exogeneity, functional-form correctness, or which variable to remove. If $R_j^2=1$, the design is exactly collinear and the finite-VIF interpretation gives way to the rank problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通 VIF 对应一个设计列。多自由度分类变量、样条或一整组编码项若要作为同一个科学对象判断，应使用匹配的组检验或广义诊断，不能把若干逐列 VIF 当作唯一结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary VIF concerns one design column. A multi-degree-of-freedom factor, spline, or encoded block may require a matching block or generalized diagnostic; a list of column-wise VIFs need not answer the scientific question about the term as a whole.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 辅助回归得到 $R_j^2=0.96$。VIF 和标准误膨胀倍数分别是多少？
>
> **答案：** $VIF=25$，相对正交基准的标准误膨胀倍数是 $\sqrt{25}=5$。
