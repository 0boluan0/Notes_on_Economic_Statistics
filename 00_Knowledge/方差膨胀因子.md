# Variance inflation factors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*方差膨胀因子（VIF）*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A predictor can vary a lot overall yet contribute little new variation once the other predictors are known. A **variance inflation factor**, or **VIF**, compares those two amounts for one predictor column. If only one fifth of its centered squared variation remains after an auxiliary regression on the other columns, its VIF is $5$. The word “variance” gains a precise statistical meaning under the regression-error assumptions explained below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个预测变量总体上可以变化很大，但已知其他预测变量后，它可能只剩少量新变动。**方差膨胀因子（VIF）**对一个预测列比较这两部分变动。如果把它对其他列作辅助回归后，只剩原中心化变动平方和的五分之一，VIF 就是 $5$。名称中的“方差”，在下述回归误差假设下具有精确统计含义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Regress a predictor on the other predictors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把一个预测变量对其他预测变量回归*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider an unweighted [[普通最小二乘|OLS]] design $X$ with $n$ observations and $p$ [[线性无关|linearly independent columns]], **including an intercept**. Choose one nonconstant column $x_j$ other than that intercept. Let $\bar x_j$ be its sample mean and $T_j=\sum_i(x_{ij}-\bar x_j)^2>0$ its total centered squared variation. Regress $x_j$ on the remaining columns, retaining their intercept. Let $r_j$ be the resulting [[回归残差|residual vector]] and $S_j=r_j^Tr_j$ its squared sum; $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. This is an auxiliary regression: its response is $x_j$, not the original outcome $y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑一个不加权的 [[普通最小二乘|OLS]] 设计 $X$，有 $n$ 个观测、$p$ 个[[线性无关|线性无关的列]]，**其中包含截距**。选定截距以外的一个非常数列 $x_j$。令 $\bar x_j$ 为样本均值，$T_j=\sum_i(x_{ij}-\bar x_j)^2>0$ 为它的总中心化变动平方和。将 $x_j$ 对其余列回归，保留这些列中的截距；所得[[回归残差|残差向量]]为 $r_j$，平方和为 $S_j=r_j^Tr_j$，上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。这是辅助回归，响应是 $x_j$，不是原来的结果变量 $y$。

> [!important] The centered single-column VIF · 中心化的单列 VIF
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Define the auxiliary proportion explained by $R_j^2=1-S_j/T_j$. Since the full design has independent columns, $0\le R_j^2<1$ and
>
> $$\mathrm{VIF}_j=\frac{T_j}{S_j}=\frac{1}{1-R_j^2},\qquad
> \mathrm{tolerance}_j=1-R_j^2=\frac{1}{\mathrm{VIF}_j}.$$
>
> Thus $\mathrm{VIF}_j\ge1$. It equals $1$ when the centered focal column is orthogonal to all the other centered predictor columns; the auxiliary slopes then explain none of its centered variation. Tolerance records the fraction left over.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 定义辅助回归解释比例 $R_j^2=1-S_j/T_j$。完整设计各列线性无关，故 $0\le R_j^2<1$，$\mathrm{VIF}_j=T_j/S_j=1/(1-R_j^2)$，容忍度 $\mathrm{tolerance}_j=1-R_j^2$ 为其倒数。VIF 至少为 $1$；当中心化后的焦点列与其他各中心化预测列正交时，VIF 等于 $1$，辅助回归的斜率没有解释掉任何中心化变动。容忍度就是剩余比例。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Calculate one from four rows
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用四行数据直接计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $z=(-1,-1,1,1)^T$, $w=(-1,1,-1,1)^T$, and $x=z+\tfrac12w=(-1.5,-0.5,0.5,1.5)^T$. Include an intercept, $z$, and $x$ in the design. Both $z$ and $w$ have mean zero, squared length $4$, and zero inner product. The auxiliary fit of $x$ on the intercept and $z$ is exactly $z$; its residual is $\tfrac12w$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $z=(-1,-1,1,1)^T$、$w=(-1,1,-1,1)^T$，以及 $x=z+\tfrac12w=(-1.5,-0.5,0.5,1.5)^T$。设计包含截距、$z$ 和 $x$。$z,w$ 均值都为零，平方长度均为 $4$，内积为零。把 $x$ 对截距和 $z$ 作辅助回归，拟合值恰好为 $z$，残差为 $\tfrac12w$。因此

$$T_x=2.25+0.25+0.25+2.25=5,\quad S_x=1,\quad R_x^2=0.8,\quad \mathrm{VIF}_x=5.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No outcome values were needed. Changing $y$ while keeping this design fixed leaves its VIF unchanged. The example has substantial total spread in $x$, but only one fifth remains to distinguish its coefficient from the coefficient of $z$. [[近似共线性精度|The precision mechanism]] explains why that leftover part estimates the separate slope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算没有用到结果变量。保持设计不变，即使改变 $y$，VIF 也不变。本例的 $x$ 总变动并不小，但只有五分之一留下来区分它与 $z$ 的系数。[[近似共线性精度]]解释为什么正是这部分剩余变动用于估计单独斜率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What variance is being compared?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*究竟比较哪一个方差？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now specify $y=X\beta+u$, where $\beta$ is the target coefficient vector and $u$ the model-error vector. For the fixed design assume $E[u\mid X]=0$: errors average to zero given the design. Also assume $\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2I_n$, where $I_n$ is the identity matrix and $0<\sigma^2<\infty$. Thus errors have a common finite conditional variance and zero cross-covariances. Writing $\hat\beta_j$ for the fitted OLS coefficient of $x_j$, the [[FWL残差化定理|FWL variance calculation]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在指定模型 $y=X\beta+u$，其中 $\beta$ 为目标系数向量，$u$ 为模型误差向量。对固定设计，假定 $E[u\mid X]=0$，即给定设计后误差平均为零；另假定 $\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2I_n$，其中 $I_n$ 为单位矩阵、$0<\sigma^2<\infty$。这表示误差的条件方差相同且有限，交叉协方差为零。用 $\hat\beta_j$ 表示 $x_j$ 的 OLS 系数估计，[[FWL残差化定理|FWL 方差计算]]给出

$$\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_j\mid X)
=\frac{\sigma^2}{S_j}
=\frac{\sigma^2}{T_j}\,\mathrm{VIF}_j.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The benchmark $\sigma^2/T_j$ keeps the **same noise variance and the same total centered predictor spread**, but removes the focal column's linear overlap with the other centered predictors. VIF is the ratio to this benchmark. In the example with $\sigma^2=1$, the actual coefficient variance is $1$ and the benchmark is $1/5$, giving ratio $5$. This is not automatically the ratio between the standard errors printed by a full model and a model that deletes controls: deletion may change the target, error model and estimated noise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基准 $\sigma^2/T_j$ 保持**相同噪声方差和相同的预测变量总中心化变动**，只去掉焦点列与其他中心化预测列的线性重叠。VIF 是相对这个基准的比值。本例若 $\sigma^2=1$，真实系数方差为 $1$，基准为 $1/5$，比值为 $5$。它不自动等于完整模型与删去控制变量后模型所报告标准误的比值：删除控制项可能改变目标、误差模型和估计噪声。

> [!tip] Variance and standard deviation have different multipliers · 方差与标准差的倍数不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> VIF $10$ multiplies the **true variance** by $10$ relative to the matched benchmark, so the sampling standard deviation is multiplied by $\sqrt{10}\approx3.16$. If $R_j^2=0.96$, VIF is $25$ and that standard-deviation multiplier is $5$. A reported [[标准误含义|standard error]] estimates this standard deviation. The same square-root multiplier applies to the corresponding classical estimates only when the same noise estimate is used in both expressions; it is not a universal comparison across separately fitted models.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 相对匹配的基准，VIF 为 $10$ 表示**真实方差**乘以 $10$，抽样标准差则乘以 $\sqrt{10}\approx3.16$。若 $R_j^2=0.96$，VIF 为 $25$，标准差倍数为 $5$。报告的[[标准误含义|标准误]]是这个标准差的估计。只有在两边使用相同噪声估计时，相应经典估计才具有同样的平方根倍数；不能把它当成分别拟合两个模型后的通用比值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read the diagnostic within its scope
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它能回答什么，不能回答什么

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
VIF measures design overlap for one column, not its scientific importance, exogeneity, or the correctness of the conditional-mean model. Even a large VIF need not imply inadequate precision if $T_j$ is large enough or the noise small enough. Conversely, VIF $1$ does not guarantee a small variance when the predictor barely varies or the noise is large. A screening cutoff is not part of this definition; see [[VIF阈值边界|the boundary of VIF thresholds]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

VIF 衡量一个设计列的重叠程度，不判断其科学重要性、外生性或条件均值模型是否正确。即使 VIF 很大，若 $T_j$ 足够大或噪声足够小，精度也不一定不足；反过来，若预测变量几乎不变或噪声很大，VIF 为 $1$ 也不保证方差小。筛查阈值不是定义的一部分，见[[VIF阈值边界]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed centered definition requires an intercept and a nonconstant focal column. Without an intercept, software may use an uncentered diagnostic with a different baseline; do not substitute its number into this formula without checking the convention. If $S_j=0$, exact dependence prevents a separately identified coefficient: writing VIF as infinity is only a diagnostic convention, not an infinite variance for a uniquely defined OLS slope. If $T_j=0$, the constant column cannot have this centered VIF.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的中心化定义要求包含截距，且焦点列不是常数。没有截距时，软件可能使用基准不同的未中心化诊断；未核实口径前，不能把其数值直接代入这里的解释。若 $S_j=0$，精确依赖使单独系数无法识别：把 VIF 记为无穷只是诊断约定，并不是某个唯一 OLS 斜率具有无穷方差。若 $T_j=0$，这个常数列不能定义上述中心化 VIF。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For heteroskedastic or correlated errors, the algebraic design ratio remains computable but generally is not the multiplier for the actual OLS variance; the full [[协方差失效下的OLS|error covariance]] matters. Ordinary VIF also describes one column, not a whole multi-column factor, spline, or interaction term. A block-level scientific question needs an appropriately defined group diagnostic or [[Wald联合检验|joint contrast]], rather than a mechanical reading of several column-wise numbers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

误差存在异方差或相关时，代数上的设计比值仍可计算，却一般不再是实际 OLS 方差的倍数，完整[[协方差失效下的OLS|误差协方差]]必须纳入考虑。普通 VIF 也只描述一列，不代表整个多列分类变量、样条或交互项。关于整组的科学问题，需要适当定义的组诊断或[[Wald联合检验|联合对比]]，不能机械读取几个逐列数值来回答。

- Penn State 第12.4节核对辅助回归的 $R_j^2$、VIF 与方差基准；本文明确固定噪声和总中心化变动，阈值建议另归边界卡。
- Hansen 指定章节核对残差化、协方差、标准差与标准误及设计依赖的区别；四行例子、极限情况和匹配的平方根倍数均直接核算。
- NIST 用于容忍度术语与 catcher-matrix 的另一计算式；本文公式另由 $S_j=T_j(1-R_j^2)$ 和 Penn State 的定义交叉核验。
