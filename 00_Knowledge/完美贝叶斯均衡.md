# 弱完美贝叶斯均衡是同时满足每个信息集序贯理性与正概率信息集 Bayes 一致性的策略—信念评估
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A weak perfect Bayesian equilibrium is a strategy–belief assessment that is sequentially rational at every information set and Bayes-consistent at every information set reached with positive probability.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 定义
> **弱 PBE** 的候选对象是完整的[[策略信念评估|assessment]] $(\sigma,\mu)$：$\sigma$ 在每个信息集给定 $\mu$ 时[[序贯理性|序贯最优]]，$\mu$ 在所有正概率信息集满足[[路径上一致性|Bayes 一致性]]。不同教材还使用更强的 PBE 定义，术语边界见[[PBE定义口径]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Definition*
>
> A **weak PBE** is a complete [[策略信念评估|assessment]] $(\sigma,\mu)$: given $\mu$, $\sigma$ is [[序贯理性|sequentially optimal]] at every information set, and $\mu$ satisfies [[路径上一致性|Bayesian consistency]] at every information set reached with positive probability. Some textbooks use stronger definitions of PBE; see [[PBE定义口径|PBE definition conventions]] for the terminological boundary.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两项条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Two conditions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

assessment $(\sigma,\mu)$ 是 weak PBE，当且仅当：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An assessment $(\sigma,\mu)$ is a weak PBE if and only if:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **序贯理性。** 对每名玩家 $i$ 的每个信息集 $I\in\mathcal H_i$，$\sigma_i$ 从 $I$ 起的续局策略，在给定 $\mu(\cdot\mid I)$ 与 $\sigma_{-i}$ 时最大化条件期望收益；
2. **路径上 Bayes 一致性。** 对每个 $P_\sigma(I)>0$ 的信息集及每个 $x\in I$，

$$
\mu(x\mid I)=\frac{P_\sigma(x)}{P_\sigma(I)}.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;

**1. Sequential rationality.** For every player $i$ and every information set $I\in\mathcal H_i$, $\sigma_i$'s continuation strategy from $I$ maximizes conditional expected payoff given $\mu(\cdot\mid I)$ and $\sigma_{-i}$;<br>
**2. On-path Bayesian consistency.** For every information set with $P_\sigma(I)>0$ and every $x\in I$, beliefs satisfy the equation above.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两项缺一不可。只有 Bayes 更新而没有续局最优，策略可能不理性；只有序贯最优而 belief 与正概率历史矛盾，推断可能不可信。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both conditions are essential. Bayesian updating without optimal continuation can leave an irrational strategy; sequential optimality with beliefs that contradict positive-probability histories can leave an invalid inference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $P_\sigma(I)=0$ 时，第二项不决定 $\mu(\cdot\mid I)$；但[[路径外信念]]仍是 assessment 的一部分，并且第一项仍在该处生效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $P_\sigma(I)=0$, the second condition does not determine $\mu(\cdot\mid I)$. The [[路径外信念|off-path belief]] remains part of the assessment, however, and the first condition still applies there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样使用这一定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How to use the definition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在标准两阶段[[信号博弈]]中，逐项核验完整策略、posterior、接收者最佳反应与发送者各类型偏离，见[[信号博弈弱PBE检验]]。不要把 weak PBE 自动画进 SPE：这一反例边界见[[弱PBE不必精炼SPE]]；它与 BNE 的对象差异见[[BNE与PBE]]，更强的 belief consistency 则见[[序贯均衡]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a standard two-stage [[信号博弈|signaling game]], verify the complete strategies, posteriors, receiver best responses, and deviations by each sender type; see [[信号博弈弱PBE检验|the signaling-game weak-PBE test]]. Weak PBE should not automatically be treated as a refinement of SPE; see [[弱PBE不必精炼SPE|weak PBE need not refine SPE]] for the counterexample boundary. See [[BNE与PBE|BNE versus PBE]] for the difference in candidate objects and [[序贯均衡|sequential equilibrium]] for stronger belief consistency.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 一个策略组合为何还不是 PBE
> 即使发送者与接收者的完整策略构成 Nash equilibrium，若看到某个正概率信号后的 posterior 不是由先验与发送概率经 Bayes 法则得出，或接收者在某个路径外信息集上给定 belief 后选择严格较差行动，该策略—belief 对都不是 weak PBE。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Why a strategy profile alone is not yet a PBE*
>
> Even if the sender's and receiver's complete strategies form a Nash equilibrium, the strategy–belief pair is not a weak PBE if the posterior after a positive-probability signal is not derived from the prior and signaling probabilities by Bayes' rule, or if the receiver chooses a strictly inferior action under the stated belief at an off-path information set.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一份 assessment 在每个信息集都序贯理性，但某个正概率信息集上的 belief 与策略诱导概率不同。它是否是弱 PBE？
>
> **答案：** 不是。序贯理性只满足第一项；正概率信息集还必须满足 Bayes consistency。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Self-check*
>
> An assessment is sequentially rational at every information set, but its belief at one positive-probability information set differs from the distribution induced by the strategies. Is it a weak PBE?
>
> **Answer:** No. Sequential rationality satisfies only the first condition; every positive-probability information set must also satisfy Bayesian consistency.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
