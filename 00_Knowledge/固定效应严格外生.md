# 固定效应通常要求每期冲击对整条回归量路径严格外生

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fixed-effects estimation ordinarily requires each period's shock to be strictly exogenous to the entire regressor path*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子命题
> 静态线性 FE 常用的充分条件是，对每个 $t$，
> $$
> E(u_{it}\mid X_i,c_i)=0,
> \qquad X_i=(x_{i1},\ldots,x_{iT_i}).
> $$
> 它要求本期冲击对过去、当期和未来回归量都条件均值为 0，而不只是 $E(u_{it}\mid x_{it},c_i)=0$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A common sufficient condition for static linear FE is $E(u_{it}\mid X_i,c_i)=0$ for every $t$, where $X_i$ is the entire regressor path. This covers past, current, and future regressors, not merely $x_{it}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若本期意外低工资 $u_{it}$ 促使工人下期培训 $x_{i,t+1}$，严格外生失败。单位去均值会把各期误差混进 $\bar u_i$，所以这种反馈会进入多个 transformed observations；普通 FE 可能不一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an unexpected low wage shock $u_{it}$ causes training in period $t+1$, strict exogeneity fails. Unit demeaning mixes period-specific errors into $\bar u_i$, so the feedback enters multiple transformed observations and ordinary FE may be inconsistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数学上，within 正规方程所需的聚合正交可以弱于完整严格外生；但在要解释结构或因果参数时，很难只靠这个较弱矩条件排除反馈和时序错配。若回归量含滞后因变量，严格外生通常机械失败，应进入 [[动态固定效应偏误]]，而不是继续把静态 FE 当作放行条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The aggregate orthogonality condition in the within normal equations can be weaker than full strict exogeneity. For structural or causal interpretation, however, the weaker moment rarely rules out feedback or timing mismatch. A lagged dependent variable generally violates strict exogeneity mechanically and belongs in the dynamic-panel boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> $E(u_{it}\mid x_{it},c_i)=0$ 为什么仍不足以排除反馈？
>
> **答案：** 它只限制当期 $x_{it}$；当前冲击仍可能改变未来 $x_{i,t+1}$，而严格外生对整条 $X_i$ 路径施加条件。
