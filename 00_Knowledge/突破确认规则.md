# 突破确认规则先固定识别线和缓冲再按确认条件产生可执行的交易信号
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A breakout confirmation rule fixes a reference boundary and buffer before applying a confirmation test to generate an actionable signal.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把“突破某价位”写成规则，需要先确定识别线、缓冲、观察价格和确认时点，再规定订单与失效后的动作。以下固定一个上涨突破后的 long/flat 教学版本：只开多或持有现金，不做空。它说明如何重复执行，不提供这些参数能够盈利的证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A breakout rule needs a reference boundary, buffer, observed price and confirmation time, followed by order and invalidation policies. This teaching version is long/flat after an upward breakout: it holds a long position or cash, without shorting. It specifies repeatable actions, not evidence that its parameters are profitable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先选整数 $N\ge1$ 和比例缓冲 $b\ge0$。在 $t$ 日开始前，使用此前 $N$ 个完整交易日的最高价，排除当前日；令 $H_j^{(t)}$ 为只按截至当时已生效拆并股换算到 $t$ 日每股单位的历史最高价，$C_t$ 为当日未复权收盘价，则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose an integer $N\ge1$ and proportional buffer $b\ge0$. Before day $t$, take highs from the preceding $N$ complete trading days, excluding the current day. Let $H_j^{(t)}$ be a historical high converted to day-$t$ share units using only splits or consolidations already effective, and $C_t$ the raw close:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$R_t=\max_{j=t-N,\ldots,t-1}H_j^{(t)},\qquad
z_t=\mathbf1\{C_t>(1+b)R_t\}.$$

从第 $N+1$ 个交易日才首次可判断；所需前 $N$ 个交易日最高价和当日收盘必须全都有效，任一缺失则不发新信号，不拿更远的有效日冒充缺失日。必须等当日收盘真正可得才判断 $z_t$，等号不确认。初始空仓，每个空仓日只要 $z_t=1$ 就可尝试一次；资金不足、被拒或完全未成交后，下一次按新一天的 $R_t$ 重新判断，不要求另一次上穿事件。参见 [[交易信号时序]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first possible test is on trading day $N+1$. Missing required highs or the current close suppress a new signal rather than being replaced with more distant days. Evaluate only once the close is available; equality does not confirm. Start flat and allow one attempt on any flat day with $z_t=1$. After insufficient funding, rejection or zero fill, evaluate a later day against its newly formed boundary; no separate crossing event is required. See [[交易信号时序|signal and execution timing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定请求数量 $Q$ 为场所允许的正整数股数，在下一合格常规交易时段提交一次 [[限价单|限价 IOC]]，买入上限为信号日未复权收盘价的 1.01 倍。提交前，可用现金必须至少为 $Q$ 乘限价加显性费用上界，否则放弃本次入场。场所须允许这种委托、价位精度和交易单位。成交价与数量由当时可得盘口及执行模型决定；剩余量取消，部分成交只保留实得股数，不追补。入场决定等待处理时不叠加新订单。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix $Q$ as a venue-permitted positive integer number of shares. Submit one [[限价单|limit IOC]] in the next eligible regular session at a buy limit of 1.01 times the signal day's raw close. Before submission, available cash must cover $Q$ times the limit plus an upper bound on explicit charges; otherwise skip the entry. The venue must permit the order, price precision and trading unit. Prices and quantities depend on the available book and execution model. Cancel the remainder, retain only filled shares and do not top up. Do not stack entry orders while the preceding decision awaits processing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只要入场有成交，就保存产生该订单信号时的 $R_t$ 为 $R^*$；持仓期间不拿每天新滚动高点替换它。后续某日收盘严格低于同单位的 $R^*$ 时，进入退出状态：下一合格时段用前一有效收盘价的 0.98 倍作卖价下限，以 IOC 出售实际持股。未卖完的余股持续承担风险，逐个合格时段以最新已知收盘价更新限价重试，直到平仓；退出期间不重新开仓。恢复空仓后，从下一次收盘判断开始按当时新 $R_t$ 重新评价。跳空可以越过失效线，退出状态不是损失上限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an entry fills at all, store the signal day's $R_t$ as $R^*$ and do not replace it with later rolling highs. A later close strictly below the same-unit $R^*$ enters exit mode. In the next eligible session, offer the actual holding by IOC with a sell limit of 0.98 times the preceding valid close. Residual shares remain exposed and are reoffered in successive eligible sessions using the latest known close until flat. Do not enter during exit mode. After becoming flat, resume evaluation from the next closing test using its new $R_t$. Gaps may cross the invalidation boundary; exit mode is not a loss ceiling.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本教学价格口径只对拆并股换单位，现金分红不缩放识别线，而按 [[复权与成交价格]]处理账户权益与到账时点；因此除息下跳仍可能触发本规则退出。若持仓一股拆成 $k$ 股，则持股乘 $k$、$R^*$ 除以 $k$，订单引用的旧收盘价也换成当前每股单位。信号到提交之间发生拆并股时，取消该次待入场决定，下一收盘重新判断。其他公司行动需补足处理规则才可继续声称整段回放可解释，不能让未处理的持仓凭空消失。期末未退出的持股按声明口径估值，费用和分配均只计一次，见 [[交易策略回测]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This teaching price convention rescales only for splits or consolidations. Cash dividends leave the boundary unchanged and enter the account under [[复权与成交价格|corporate-action and payment conventions]], so an ex-dividend drop can trigger this exit rule. A $k$-for-one split multiplies held shares by $k$, divides $R^*$ by $k$, and converts any referenced old close to current share units. A split between signal and submission cancels that pending entry decision; reassess at the next close. Other corporate actions need explicit treatment before claiming an interpretable replay; unprocessed holdings cannot disappear. Mark remaining positions under a stated convention and count charges and distributions once, as in [[交易策略回测|strategy backtesting]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

小例：某日事前 $R_t=100$、$b=1\%$，当天收盘 100.60 与 101.20 是两种可能结果；前者不确认，后者严格高于 101。选后者，取 $Q=8$、可用现金 1000、每笔有成交委托收费 1，且无公司行动；下单限价为 102.212，资金上界需求 $8(102.212)+1=818.696$。若该精度被允许，订单到达时仅 3 股卖盘报 102、下一档 103，则买 3 股、取消 5 股，现金成为 $1000-3(102)-1=693$，保存 $R^*=100$。后来收盘 99 才触发退出，下一时段限价为 97.02；并非在 100 或 99 已经卖出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a pre-session boundary of 100 and a 1% buffer, closes at 100.60 and 101.20 are alternative outcomes for the same day. Only the latter exceeds 101. For that outcome, set $Q=8$, available cash to 1000 and the fee to 1 per filled order, without corporate actions. The buy limit is 102.212 and the funding bound is 818.696. Assuming permitted precision and an ask book with three shares at 102 followed by a level at 103, three shares fill and five cancel. Cash becomes 693 and $R^*=100$ is stored. A later close at 99 triggers exit mode with a next-session sell limit of 97.02; it does not imply a sale at 100 or 99.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若上述 3 股持仓在退出前发生一拆二，且无其他价格变化，持股变 6 股、$R^*$ 变 50。比较对象都换单位，而非拿拆股后的约 50 元价格与旧线 100 比较。现金 693 不因拆股增加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If those three held shares undergo a two-for-one split before exit, absent other price changes, holdings become six and $R^*$ becomes 50. Compare quantities in the new units instead of comparing an approximately 50 price with the old 100 boundary. The split does not increase cash of 693.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Sources and verification*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Backtrader supports the prior-bar highest-high construction, while TradingView supports waiting for confirmed bar values. Execution, accounting and corporate-action boundaries use the shared atoms. Parameters, limit multipliers, invalidation, retry and split conventions are the stated teaching policy, not a source-certified optimal strategy. Funding, partial fills and split arithmetic are independently checked.
