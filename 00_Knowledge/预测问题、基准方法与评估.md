# 预测问题、基准方法与评估
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Forecasting problems, benchmarks, and evaluation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 在未来尚未发生时定义要预测的对象、期限和决策损失，用真实未见时期判断模型是否比简单基准更有用。
> **具体锚点：** 明日销量和未来一年总销量需要不同模型与误差；随机划分时间数据会把未来信息泄漏给训练集。
> **核心难点：** 拟合残差不是预测误差；评估必须保持时间顺序，并让指标与业务损失和尺度匹配。
> **为什么重要：** 没有基准和外样本评估，复杂模型只是在解释过去。
> **继续：** 先写 forecast target 与 decision，再做 naïve 基准和 rolling-origin 验证；方法见 [[指数平滑与 ETS|指数平滑、回归与时间序列预测]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Before the future occurs, define the target, horizon, and decision loss, then use genuinely unseen future periods to determine whether a model is more useful than a simple benchmark.
> **Concrete anchor:** Tomorrow's sales and total sales over the next year require different horizons and error summaries. Randomly splitting time data leaks future information into training.
> **Central difficulty:** In-sample residuals are not forecast errors. Evaluation must respect chronological information and use metrics aligned with scale and decision loss.
> **Why it matters:** Without benchmarks and out-of-sample evaluation, a complex model has shown only that it can explain the past.
> **Continue with:** Define the forecast target and decision, establish naïve benchmarks, and use rolling-origin validation before comparing [[指数平滑与 ETS|ETS]], [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测|ARIMA-family models]], or [[回归预测与动态回归|dynamic regression]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!source] 本节依据
> - Hyndman & Athanasopoulos, [Forecasting: Principles and Practice, 3rd ed.](https://otexts.com/fpp3/)：支持预测流程、基准、评估、ETS、回归与 ARIMA。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> - Hyndman and Athanasopoulos, [Forecasting: Principles and Practice, 3rd ed.](https://otexts.com/fpp3/), was checked for target definition, benchmark methods, accuracy measures, time-series cross-validation, and probabilistic evaluation.

## 定义预测任务
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Define the forecasting task*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

说明预测变量、时间戳可用信息、horizon、更新频率、粒度和点/区间/分布输出。预测“下月总需求”和“每天需求再加总”信息不同；业务动作决定错误成本和最小可用提前量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify the forecast variable, information available at the forecast timestamp, horizon, update frequency, granularity, and whether the output is a point, interval, or distribution. Forecasting next month's aggregate demand differs from forecasting daily demand and then summing it. The downstream action determines error cost and the minimum useful lead time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个可执行的 target 声明应类似：“每周一 08:00，使用当时已发布的数据，为每家门店生成未来 1–4 周需求的 10/50/90% 分位数，用于补货”。如果不能用一句话说清这些元素，就还没有定义可评估的预测问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An operational target statement might be: "Every Monday at 08:00, using only data released by then, produce 10th, 50th, and 90th percentiles of demand for each store at horizons of one to four weeks for replenishment decisions." If these elements cannot be stated in one sentence, the forecasting problem is not yet evaluable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 数据与信息集
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Data and the information set*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只使用预测生成时真实可知的变量。发布时间滞后、数据修订和未来促销计划需按历史可用版本模拟。缺失、异常和结构变化的处理应在每个训练窗口内完成，避免泄漏。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use only variables genuinely known when a forecast would have been generated. Publication delays, data revisions, and future promotion plans must be replayed in their historically available versions. Missing-value, outlier, and structural-change processing belongs inside each training window to avoid leakage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对宏观数据，“今天下载到的历史 GDP”常包含之后的修订，不是当时决策者看到的 vintage。对促销、天气和价格等预测变量，评估时也不能用事后实现值，除非任务明确是条件预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For macroeconomic data, today's downloaded GDP history often contains later revisions and is not the vintage available to decision-makers at the time. Forecast predictors such as promotions, weather, and prices must also not use ex-post realised values during evaluation unless the task is explicitly conditional forecasting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 基准
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Benchmarks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

mean、naïve、seasonal naïve 和 drift 是必要基准。一个复杂模型若不能稳定胜过与数据结构匹配的简单基准，就没有证明新增复杂度有价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mean, naïve, seasonal-naïve, and drift forecasts are essential benchmarks. If a complex model cannot reliably beat a simple benchmark matched to the data structure, it has not demonstrated that its added complexity is valuable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基准应匹配频率与任务：季节数据的首要基准通常是 seasonal naïve，价格水平常用 naïve，无明显结构的平稳序列可用历史均值。“胜过错的基准”不是进步。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Benchmarks must match frequency and task: seasonal data usually require a seasonal-naïve baseline, price levels often use naïve, and a stationary structureless series may use its historical mean. Beating an inappropriate benchmark is not evidence of progress.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 训练/测试与滚动起点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Training/test splits and rolling origins*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

测试期必须晚于训练期。rolling/expanding origin 反复用过去预测下一段，模拟真实部署；多步预测应按每个 horizon 评估，不只看一步。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The test period must come after training. Rolling or expanding origins repeatedly train on the past and forecast the next segment, approximating deployment. Multi-step forecasts must be evaluated separately by horizon rather than only at one step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若使用滚动窗口，旧数据会被丢弃，适合关系可能变化的环境；扩展窗口保留全部历史，适合稳定且数据宝贵的环境。窗口选择是对结构稳定性的假设，不是纯技术参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rolling window discards old data and suits environments where relationships may change; an expanding window retains all history and suits stable settings where data are scarce. Window choice is an assumption about structural stability, not merely a technical parameter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 误差指标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Error measures*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

MAE 易解释、RMSE 更惩罚大错、MASE 可跨序列比较；MAPE 在实际值接近零时失真且不对称。指标应匹配决策损失，不能仅挑让模型最好看的一个。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
MAE is interpretable, RMSE penalises large errors more heavily, and MASE supports comparison across series. MAPE is distorted near zero and asymmetric. Metrics should match decision loss rather than being selected because they make a preferred model look best.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非季节序列，MASE 用训练期一步 naïve 误差平均缩放：
$$
\operatorname{MASE}=\frac{\frac1H\sum_{h=1}^H|e_h|}{\frac1{T-1}\sum_{t=2}^T|y_t-y_{t-1}|}.
$$
分母必须只用训练窗口计算；季节序列则应使用季节 naïve 差值。MASE $<1$ 表示在该缩放口径下平均绝对误差小于基准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a nonseasonal series, MASE scales by the mean one-step naïve error in the training period:
$$
\operatorname{MASE}=\frac{\frac1H\sum_{h=1}^H|e_h|}{\frac1{T-1}\sum_{t=2}^T|y_t-y_{t-1}|}.
$$
The denominator must be computed only from the training window; seasonal series should use seasonal-naïve differences. MASE below one means average absolute error is smaller than the benchmark under that scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 概率预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Probabilistic forecasts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区间要同时评估 coverage 和 sharpness；分位数用 pinball loss，完整分布可用 proper scoring rules。只有覆盖率正确但区间极宽没有决策价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Intervals require both coverage and sharpness evaluation; quantiles can use pinball loss and full distributions can use proper scoring rules. Correct coverage achieved through extremely wide intervals has little decision value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对分位水平 $\tau$ 和预测 $q_\tau$，pinball loss 是
$$
L_\tau(y,q_\tau)=
\begin{cases}
\tau(y-q_\tau),&y\ge q_\tau,\\
(1-\tau)(q_\tau-y),&y<q_\tau.
\end{cases}
$$
不对称权重迫使最优预测对应真实 $\tau$ 分位数。若决策对缺货比库存过剩更敏感，分位预测比对称点误差更直接。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For quantile level $\tau$ and forecast $q_\tau$, pinball loss is
$$
L_\tau(y,q_\tau)=
\begin{cases}
\tau(y-q_\tau),&y\ge q_\tau,\\
(1-\tau)(q_\tau-y),&y<q_\tau.
\end{cases}
$$
Its asymmetric weights make the true $\tau$ quantile optimal. When stock-outs are more costly than excess inventory, quantile forecasts align with the decision more directly than symmetric point errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 监控与更新
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Monitoring and updating*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

部署后监控偏差、误差、覆盖率、数据漂移和基准差异，按预定规则再训练。真实结果到达前无法完整评估最新预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After deployment, monitor bias, errors, coverage, data drift, and performance relative to benchmarks, and retrain under prespecified rules. The most recent forecasts cannot be fully evaluated until outcomes arrive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不同 horizon 的结果到达时间不同，因此监控表应记录 forecast origin、target time、horizon、当时模型/数据版本和最终实现值。否则很容易将不同期限、不同版本的误差混在一起。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Outcomes for different horizons arrive at different times. A monitoring table should therefore record forecast origin, target time, horizon, model and data version, and eventual realisation; otherwise errors from different horizons and vintages are easily mixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么不能随机打乱时间序列做普通交叉验证？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why should an ordinary randomly shuffled cross-validation split not be used for time series?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 会让未来观测进入训练并预测过去，破坏真实信息集并产生泄漏。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It lets future observations enter training and predict the past, violating the real information set and creating leakage.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 模型训练残差很小为什么仍可能预测差？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a model with small training residuals still forecast poorly?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 残差是用已参与估计的数据计算，模型可过拟合；预测需在未见未来时期评估。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Residuals use data that helped estimate the model, so overfitting is possible. Forecasts must be assessed on unseen future periods.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 区间预测只看 coverage 为什么不够？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is coverage alone insufficient for interval forecasts?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 极宽区间可轻易覆盖但没有信息；还要看 sharpness/宽度和适当评分。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Extremely wide intervals can cover easily while conveying little information; sharpness, width, and proper scores are also required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释为什么预测任务必须先写决策。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why the downstream decision must be stated before evaluating forecasts.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 决策决定哪个 horizon、粒度和输出形式有用，也决定高估与低估、大错与小错的成本；没有它就无法选择合适指标。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The decision determines the useful horizon, granularity, and output, and sets the relative costs of over- versus under-forecasting and large versus small errors. Without it, no evaluation metric is substantively justified.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
