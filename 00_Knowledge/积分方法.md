# 积分方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Techniques of integration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当积分不能直接看出原函数，或曲线用普通 $y=f(x)$ 表示很笨重时，选择更合适的代数与坐标表示。
> **具体锚点：** $\int x e^x dx$ 适合分部积分；圆和螺线常用参数或极坐标比直角坐标自然。
> **核心难点：** 方法选择依赖结构，不是记住越多公式越好；变换变量后边界、微分和几何含义必须同步改变。
> **为什么重要：** 正确表示往往把复杂问题降为标准型。
> **继续：** 先用“方法选择树”；收敛端点问题见 [[反常积分#反常积分|反常积分]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Integration techniques rewrite an integrand into a form whose antiderivative or definite integral can be evaluated. Parametric and polar representations are now a separate retrieval unit.
> **Concrete anchor:** $\int xe^x\,dx$ suits integration by parts because differentiating $x$ simplifies it while $e^x$ remains easy to integrate.
> **Central difficulty:** Method choice follows structure. Every substitution must update the differential and, for a definite integral, the bounds.
> **Why it matters:** The right representation often reduces a hard integral to a standard form.
> **Continue with:** Use the selection sequence below; for convergence at singular or infinite endpoints see [[反常积分|improper integrals]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 积分方法选择树
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A decision tree for integration techniques*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先化简并识别是否为链式法则的反向：出现 $g'(x)f(g(x))$ 优先换元。乘积中一项求导会变简单、另一项易积分时用分部积分。真有理函数先长除，再按因式分解做部分分式。根式含 $a^2-x^2$、$a^2+x^2$ 或 $x^2-a^2$ 时，三角代换可能把恒等式显露出来。最后用求导检查原函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Simplify first and look for the chain rule in reverse: an integrand of the form $g'(x)f(g(x))$ suggests substitution. Use integration by parts when differentiating one factor simplifies it and integrating the other remains easy. For a rational function, perform long division if needed and then decompose the proper fraction. Radicals involving $a^2-x^2$, $a^2+x^2$, or $x^2-a^2$ may expose a trigonometric identity after substitution. Finally, differentiate the proposed antiderivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三角积分、分部积分与部分分式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Trigonometric integrals, integration by parts, and partial fractions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三角幂积分的奇偶性决定保留哪一个因子做换元。分部积分 $\int u,dv=uv-\int v,du$ 来自乘积法则，选择 $u$ 的标准是让新积分更简单而非机械遵循口诀。部分分式的分子次数和重复因子项必须完整，否则解出的系数系统不可能表示原函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Parity determines which factor to retain for substitution in trigonometric power integrals. Integration by parts, $\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du$, comes from the product rule; choose $u$ so that the new integral becomes simpler, not by following a mnemonic blindly. A partial-fraction decomposition must include the correct numerator degree and every repeated-factor term, or its coefficient system cannot reconstruct the original rational function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：分部积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: integration by parts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $\int xe^x\,dx$，取 $u=x$ 与 $dv=e^x dx$，则 $du=dx$、$v=e^x$，因而
$$
\int xe^x\,dx=xe^x-\int e^x\,dx=e^x(x-1)+C.
$$
求导检查：$[e^x(x-1)]'=e^x(x-1)+e^x=xe^x$。若反过来选 $u=e^x$，新积分不会变得更简单，这就是方法选择依赖结构的具体体现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\int xe^x\,dx$, choose $u=x$ and $dv=e^x dx$, so $du=dx$ and $v=e^x$. Hence
$$
\int xe^x\,dx=xe^x-\int e^x\,dx=e^x(x-1)+C.
$$
Differentiation verifies the result: $[e^x(x-1)]'=e^x(x-1)+e^x=xe^x$. Choosing $u=e^x$ instead would not simplify the new integral, illustrating why structure governs the choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 看到一个积分时，为什么先化简比立即套方法更重要？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is simplification more important than immediately applying a technique?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 化简可能直接暴露换元结构、消掉项或把假分式变成多项式加真分式，避免不必要的复杂方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Simplification may reveal a substitution, cancel terms, or turn an improper rational function into a polynomial plus a proper fraction, avoiding needless machinery.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 换元处理定积分时，为什么不能只替换 integrand？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a definite-integral substitution not replace only the integrand?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 变量改变后，微分和上下限必须一起改为新变量，或在求得原函数后完整换回旧变量再代原边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A change of variable must also transform the differential and the bounds, or the antiderivative must be converted fully back to the original variable before applying the original bounds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么用求导检查原函数是最可靠的收尾？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is differentiation the most reliable final check of an antiderivative?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它直接检查结果的导数是否回到原 integrand，能发现链式因子、符号和系数错误。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It directly tests whether the result differentiates back to the original integrand and exposes missing chain factors, signs, and constants.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 部分分式前为什么要先做长除？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must polynomial long division precede partial fractions when necessary?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 标准部分分式分解要求分子次数低于分母；长除先分出多项式部分，剩下的真分式才能正确分解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Standard partial fractions require the numerator degree to be lower than the denominator degree. Long division separates the polynomial part and leaves a proper fraction to decompose.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
