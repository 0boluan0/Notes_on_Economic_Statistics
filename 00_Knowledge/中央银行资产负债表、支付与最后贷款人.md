# 中央银行资产负债表、支付与最后贷款人
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Central-bank Balance Sheets, Payments, and the Lender of Last Resort*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 从央行资产负债表理解准备金和现金怎样产生、支付怎样最终结算，以及流动性支持怎样阻止挤兑扩散。
> **具体锚点：** 央行买入证券时增加证券资产并贷记银行准备金；这创造央行负债，但不等于商业银行立即增加等量贷款。
> **核心难点：** 准备金、公众存款和银行资本是不同负债或权益；最后贷款人还必须在危机中区分暂时缺流动性与已经资不抵债。
> **为什么重要：** 货币政策实施、支付安全、银行挤兑与 QE 都通过央行和银行资产负债表连接。
> **继续：** 政策利率怎样传导见 [[货币政策工具、传导与可信度]]；银行流动性约束见 [[商业银行资产负债管理]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Use the central-bank balance sheet to understand how reserves and currency arise, how payments settle finally, and how liquidity support can stop runs from spreading.
> **Concrete anchor:** When a central bank buys a security, it adds the security as an asset and credits bank reserves as a liability; this does not make commercial-bank lending rise by the same amount automatically.
> **Central difficulty:** Reserves, public deposits, and bank capital are distinct liabilities or equity positions. In a crisis, the lender of last resort must also distinguish temporary illiquidity from insolvency.
> **Why it matters:** Monetary-policy implementation, payment safety, bank runs, and quantitative easing are connected through central-bank and bank balance sheets.
> **Continue with:** For policy-rate transmission see [[货币政策工具、传导与可信度|monetary-policy tools, transmission, and credibility]]; for bank liquidity constraints see [[商业银行资产负债管理|commercial-bank balance-sheet management]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 中央银行资产负债表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Central-bank Balance Sheet*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主要负债是现金和银行准备金，资产可含政府证券、外汇和贷款。公开市场操作或贷款改变准备金和资产构成；准备金是银行间结算货币，不直接等同于公众持有的广义货币。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The main liabilities are currency and bank reserves, while assets may include government securities, foreign exchange, and loans. Open-market operations or lending change reserves and the asset composition. Reserves are interbank settlement money, not the same as broad money held by the public.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

央行购买 100 的国债时，资产端证券增加 100，负债端准备金增加 100；卖出则相反。若交易对手是非银行，其商业银行存款也会先增加，但准备金和存款属于不同机构负债，后续组合调整不受简单固定乘数支配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a central bank buys 100 of government securities, securities assets rise by 100 and reserve liabilities rise by 100; a sale reverses the entries. If the seller is a non-bank, that party's commercial-bank deposit initially rises as well, but reserves and deposits are liabilities of different institutions, and subsequent portfolio adjustment is not governed by a fixed mechanical multiplier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 目标、独立性与问责
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Objectives, Independence, and Accountability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中央银行常追求价格稳定，并兼顾就业、金融稳定或汇率。工具独立可缓解短期政治压力，但目标授权、透明沟通和问责仍必要。多目标可能在冲击下冲突。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Central banks commonly pursue price stability and may also consider employment, financial stability, or the exchange rate. Instrument independence can reduce short-term political pressure, but statutory objectives, transparent communication, and accountability remain necessary. Multiple objectives can conflict under shocks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

独立性不是脱离公共授权，而是明确谁设目标、谁选工具、怎样解释偏离和由谁监督。政策造成分配与财政影响时，透明资产负债表和决策记录尤其重要。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Independence is not freedom from public authorization. It requires clarity about who sets objectives, who chooses instruments, how deviations are explained, and who provides oversight. Transparent balance sheets and decision records are especially important when policy has distributional or fiscal effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 支付与最后贷款人
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Payments and the Lender of Last Resort*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支付体系依赖最终结算和日内流动性。最后贷款人向有偿付能力但缺流动性的机构提供支持，可阻止挤兑扩散；抵押、惩罚利率和监管用于缓解道德风险，但现实中偿付能力难即时判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Payment systems rely on final settlement and intraday liquidity. A lender of last resort can stop runs from spreading by supporting solvent but illiquid institutions. Collateral, penalty pricing, and supervision mitigate moral hazard, although solvency is difficult to determine in real time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支持应解决结算时间错配，而不是永久填补资产价值缺口。若抵押品价值在火售中下跌，流动性与偿付能力会相互影响；因此央行贷款、政府资本重组和银行处置是不同工具，不能用一项含糊“救助”概括。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Support should bridge settlement timing rather than permanently fill an asset-value shortfall. When collateral values fall in a fire sale, liquidity and solvency interact. Central-bank lending, government recapitalization, and bank resolution are therefore distinct tools and should not be collapsed into the vague label of a bailout.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 金融稳定边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Boundary of Financial Stability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

个体机构看似稳健不保证系统稳定，共同敞口、期限错配和火售形成外部性。宏观审慎政策因此关注系统相关性而非只看单家银行。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apparently sound individual institutions do not guarantee system stability. Common exposures, maturity mismatch, and fire sales create externalities, so macroprudential policy focuses on systemic connections rather than only individual banks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：央行证券购买
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: A Central-bank Securities Purchase*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

央行从基金购买 100 国债，通过基金的银行结算：央行证券资产 +100、银行准备金负债 +100；银行对基金的存款负债 +100、银行准备金资产 +100；基金国债 -100、存款 +100。私人部门持有的资产组合变得更短、更流动，但没有凭空多出 100 净财富。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the central bank buys 100 of government bonds from a fund through the fund's bank. The central bank records securities assets +100 and reserve liabilities +100; the bank records reserve assets +100 and deposit liabilities to the fund +100; the fund records bonds -100 and deposits +100. The private sector's portfolio becomes shorter and more liquid, but it does not receive 100 of net wealth from nowhere.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把央行资产购买说成“印钱直接借给家庭”会错过交易对手和复式记账；把准备金当银行资本会混淆流动性与损失吸收；把所有困难机构都称为缺流动性会掩盖资不抵债和财政损失分配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Describing central-bank asset purchases as “printing money and lending it directly to households” omits counterparties and double-entry accounting. Treating reserves as bank capital confuses liquidity with loss absorption. Calling every troubled institution illiquid conceals insolvency and the fiscal allocation of losses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 准备金增加是否机械等比例增加广义货币？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does an increase in reserves mechanically produce a proportional increase in broad money?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不一定。贷款和存款创造受资本、风险、融资成本、贷款需求与政策利率影响，简单乘数是特定条件近似。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Not necessarily. Loan and deposit creation depends on capital, risk, funding costs, credit demand, and the policy rate; the simple multiplier is an approximation under particular conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 最后贷款人为何会产生道德风险？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a lender of last resort create moral hazard?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 若机构预期危机时获救，平时可能承担更多流动性或信用风险，因此需抵押、定价、监管和处置框架。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> If institutions expect rescue in a crisis, they may take more liquidity or credit risk beforehand, so collateral, pricing, supervision, and resolution frameworks are needed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 央行证券购买为什么不等于创造同额净财富？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is a central-bank securities purchase not an equal creation of net wealth?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它通常把私人部门持有的证券换成准备金/存款，改变资产构成和价格，而非无条件增加私人部门净资产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> It usually exchanges privately held securities for reserves or deposits, changing portfolio composition and prices rather than unconditionally increasing private-sector net worth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
