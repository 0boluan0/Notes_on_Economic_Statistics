# 两两独立不推出相互独立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Pairwise independence does not imply mutual independence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> [[两两独立]]只控制每一对事件，[[相互独立]]还控制三重及更高阶交集。因此，两两独立只能满足相互独立定义中的二阶部分，不能补齐更高阶条件。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> Events $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ are pairwise independent if every pair satisfies $P(A_i\cap A_j)=P(A_i)P(A_j)$. Mutual independence requires, for every subset $J$ containing at least two events,
> $$P\!\left(\bigcap_{j\in J}A_j\right)=\prod_{j\in J}P(A_j).$$
> Pairwise factorisation alone does not guarantee factorisation of triple or higher-order intersections.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小反例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal counterexample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公平硬币独立抛两次。令 $A=$“第一次正面”、$B=$“第二次正面”、$C=$“两次结果相同”。三对事件都独立；但
$$P(A\cap B\cap C)=P(HH)=\tfrac14\neq\tfrac18=P(A)P(B)P(C),$$
所以三者不相互独立。
令 $X=I_A,Y=I_B,Z=I_C$，同一构造也给出三个两两独立但不相互独立的随机变量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Toss a fair coin independently twice. Let $A$ be “the first toss is heads”, $B$ be “the second toss is heads”, and $C$ be “the two tosses match”. Every pair is independent, but
$$P(A\cap B\cap C)=P(HH)=\tfrac14\neq\tfrac18=P(A)P(B)P(C),$$
so the three events are not mutually independent.
The indicators $I_A,I_B,I_C$ give the same boundary for random variables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 验证三个事件相互独立时，只检查三对交集够不够？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When checking whether three events are mutually independent, is checking the three pairwise intersections enough?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不够；还必须检查三重交集是否分解为三个边际概率的乘积。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. The triple intersection must also factorise into the product of the three marginal probabilities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
