# Why pairwise independence is not enough
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两两独立不推出相互独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three events can pass every two-event independence check and still depend on one another as a group. [[两两独立|Pairwise independence]] checks each pair; [[相互独立|mutual independence]] also checks larger selections. A small coin model shows exactly what the pairwise checks can miss: two pieces of information together may determine a third, even when either piece alone does not help.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个事件可以通过所有逐对独立性检查，却仍然作为一个整体存在依赖。[[两两独立]]检查每一对，[[相互独立]]还要检查更大的组合。一个很小的硬币模型就能看出逐对检查漏掉了什么：单独一条信息没有帮助，但两条信息合起来可能完全决定第三件事。

## 最小反例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Two tosses, three questions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Toss a fair coin independently twice. There are four equally likely outcomes $HH,HT,TH,TT$, each of probability $1/4$, where $H$ means heads and $T$ tails. Ask three questions: $A$ is “the first toss is heads”, $B$ is “the second toss is heads”, and $C$ is “the two results match”. The table marks an event with $1$ if it occurs and $0$ otherwise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

独立地抛两次公平硬币，$HH,HT,TH,TT$ 四种结果各有 $1/4$ 的概率，其中 $H$ 表示正面，$T$ 表示反面。现在问三个问题：$A$ 是“第一次正面”，$B$ 是“第二次正面”，$C$ 是“两次结果相同”。下表用 $1$ 表示事件发生、$0$ 表示没有发生。

> [!example] The complete outcome table · 把所有结果列出来
>
> | $\omega$ | $A$ | $B$ | $C$ | $P(\{\omega\})$ |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | $HH$ | $1$ | $1$ | $1$ | $1/4$ |
> | $HT$ | $1$ | $0$ | $0$ | $1/4$ |
> | $TH$ | $0$ | $1$ | $0$ | $1/4$ |
> | $TT$ | $0$ | $0$ | $1$ | $1/4$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each event occurs in two rows, so $P(A)=P(B)=P(C)=1/2$. Probabilities come from adding the appropriate rows, as in a [[离散概率空间|discrete probability space]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个事件都在两行里发生，因此 $P(A)=P(B)=P(C)=1/2$。像[[离散概率空间]]中一样，选出相应的行，把概率相加，就得到事件概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Every pair passes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先把三对全部检查完*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[事件独立|Two events are independent]] when their actual intersection probability equals the product of their probabilities. In the table, each of the pairs $A,B$, $A,C$ and $B,C$ occurs together only in the $HH$ row. Therefore,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[事件独立|两个事件独立]]，要求实际交集概率等于各自概率的乘积。表中 $A,B$、$A,C$、$B,C$ 三对事件，都只有在 $HH$ 那一行同时发生，所以

$$
\begin{aligned}
P(A\cap B)&=\tfrac14=P(A)P(B),\\
P(A\cap C)&=\tfrac14=P(A)P(C),\\
P(B\cap C)&=\tfrac14=P(B)P(C).
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All three checks hold. This proves pairwise independence, not just independence of the original two tosses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三项检查全部成立。这证明了这三个事件两两独立，不能仅用“原先两次投掷独立”这句话替代上述检查。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The triple fails
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*三个一起时，乘积条件失败*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All three events also occur together exactly in the $HH$ row. Their actual joint probability is therefore $1/4$. Mutual independence would instead require the product of all three individual probabilities:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个事件一起发生时，同样恰好对应 $HH$ 这一行，因此实际联合概率是 $1/4$。相互独立却要求它等于三个单独概率的乘积：

> [!warning] The missing condition · 逐对检查漏掉的条件
>
> $$P(A\cap B\cap C)=\frac14\ne\frac18=\frac12\times\frac12\times\frac12=P(A)P(B)P(C).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The three events are pairwise independent but not mutually independent. The three pair conditions do not imply the triple condition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 所以这三个事件两两独立，却不相互独立。三个逐对条件推不出三重条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mechanism is visible without formulas. Knowing only that the first toss is heads leaves $HH$ and $HT$, so matching still has probability $1/2$. Knowing both that the first and second tosses are heads leaves only $HH$, so matching is certain. The information in a combination can be stronger than the information in either member alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不用公式也能看到原因。只知道第一次正面，还剩 $HH$ 和 $HT$，两次相同的概率仍为 $1/2$；但如果同时知道第一次和第二次都是正面，就只剩 $HH$，两次相同变成了必然。组合起来的信息，可以比其中任一条单独提供的信息更强。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same example as random variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把三个事件记成三个随机变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X=I_A$, $Y=I_B$ and $Z=I_C$, where an [[事件指示变量|event indicator]] equals $1$ when its event occurs and $0$ otherwise. The table already lists these three numerical records. Pick any two columns: all four pairs $(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)$ occur once, each with probability $1/4$. Each variable has probabilities $1/2,1/2$, so every two-variable PMF factorises as required by the [[随机变量独立的分布判据|discrete independence criterion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $X=I_A$、$Y=I_B$、$Z=I_C$；[[事件指示变量]]在相应事件发生时等于 $1$，否则等于 $0$。表格已经列出了这三个数值记录。任取两列，$(0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1)$ 四种组合各出现一次，概率都为 $1/4$。每个变量取 $0$、$1$ 的概率各为 $1/2$，因此任意两个变量的 PMF 都满足[[随机变量独立的分布判据|离散独立性判据]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Yet $P(X=1,Y=1,Z=1)=1/4$, whereas the product of the three individual probabilities is $1/8$. Thus these random variables are also pairwise independent without being mutually independent. The example preserves all pairwise independence requirements while exposing a dependence visible only when the three are considered together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

然而 $P(X=1,Y=1,Z=1)=1/4$，三个单独概率的乘积却为 $1/8$。所以这三个随机变量也两两独立，但不相互独立。这个构造保留了全部逐对独立条件，同时展示了只有把三个成员一起考虑才会显现的依赖。
