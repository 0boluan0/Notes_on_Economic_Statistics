# 光谱风险度量以非负、归一且随损失分位水平不减的权重汇总损失分位点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A spectral risk measure aggregates loss quantiles with non-negative, normalized weights that do not decrease toward worse quantiles*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 光谱风险度量用一个权重函数汇总损失分布的全部分位点。权重非负且总量为一；越靠近更坏尾部，权重不能更低，因此权重函数把“更坏的损失应受到至少同样重视”写进风险度量。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A spectral risk measure aggregates all quantiles of a loss distribution through a weight function. Weights are non-negative and sum to one; they cannot decrease toward worse outcomes, encoding the requirement that worse losses receive at least as much weight.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令损失随机变量为 $L$，左分位函数为

$$
q_u(L)=\inf\{\ell:F_L(\ell)\ge u\},
\qquad 0<u<1.
$$

取 $\phi\in L^1(0,1)$，并按“几乎处处”理解下列条件：

$$
\phi(u)\ge0,
\qquad
\int_0^1\phi(u)\,du=1,
\qquad
u_1<u_2\Rightarrow\phi(u_1)\le\phi(u_2).
$$

在

$$
\int_0^1\!\left|\phi(u)q_u(L)\right|du<\infty
$$

的损失变量上，光谱风险度量定义为

$$
\rho_\phi(L)
=\int_0^1\phi(u)q_u(L)\,du.
$$

因为较大的 $u$ 对应更坏的损失分位点，$\phi$ 不减表示坏结果不会比好结果获得更少权重。若改用收益排序，尾部方向和单调方向都要随之改写，不能照搬损失口径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Larger $u$ corresponds to a worse loss quantile, so a non-decreasing $\phi$ prevents worse outcomes from receiving less weight than better ones. A return convention reverses the tail ordering and therefore requires the monotonic direction to be rewritten.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

光谱规定的是怎样汇总同一个损失分布中的不同尾部位置；它不负责估计损失分布，也不证明数据、依赖或尾部模型正确。[[ES是光谱特例]] 给出一个合法阶梯权重，[[VaR单点权重边界]] 则说明单点分位权重为何不属于这里的普通可积权重类。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The spectrum specifies how positions within one loss distribution are aggregated. It does not estimate that distribution or validate its data, dependence, or tail model. [[ES是光谱特例|ES as a spectral special case]] supplies an admissible step weight, while [[VaR单点权重边界|the VaR point-mass boundary]] explains why a single-quantile weight is not an ordinary integrable spectrum in this class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 在损失口径下，为什么光谱权重不能随着分位水平上升而下降？
>
> **答案：** 更高分位对应更坏损失；权重下降会让更坏结果获得更少重视，违背风险厌恶型光谱的排序要求。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** Under a loss convention, why may spectral weights not decrease as the quantile level rises?
>
> **Answer:** Higher quantiles are worse losses. Decreasing weights would give worse outcomes less importance, violating the ordering required of a risk-averse spectrum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
