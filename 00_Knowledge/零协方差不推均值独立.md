# Zero covariance without mean independence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零协方差不推出均值独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero [[协方差|covariance]] says that the average product of two centred variables is zero. Positive and negative contributions may cancel. It does not require the average of $Y$ to be the same at every value of $X$, which is the requirement for [[均值独立|mean independence of $Y$ from $X$]]. Squaring a symmetric variable makes the distinction visible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[协方差]]为零，表示两个变量各自减去均值后，其乘积的平均值为零；正负贡献可以互相抵消。它没有要求每个 $X$ 值下的 $Y$ 均值都相同，而后者才是[[均值独立|$Y$ 对 $X$ 均值独立]]的要求。对一个对称变量取平方，就能看清区别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A three-point joint distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个三点联合分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X$ take $-1,0,1$ with probability $1/3$ each, and set $Y=X^2$. Knowing $X$ determines $Y$ exactly. The full joint table is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $X$ 等概率取 $-1,0,1$，每个值概率为 $1/3$，并令 $Y=X^2$。知道 $X$ 就完全确定 $Y$。完整联合表如下：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| $X\backslash Y$ | $0$ | $1$ | Row total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $-1$ | $0$ | $1/3$ | $1/3$ |
| $0$ | $1/3$ | $0$ | $1/3$ |
| $1$ | $0$ | $1/3$ | $1/3$ |
| Column total | $1/3$ | $2/3$ | $1$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[期望|expectations]] are $E[X]=0$, $E[Y]=2/3$, and $E[XY]=E[X^3]=(-1+0+1)/3=0$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由表计算[[期望]]：$E[X]=0$，$E[Y]=2/3$，$E[XY]=E[X^3]=(-1+0+1)/3=0$。因此

$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=E[XY]-E[X]E[Y]=0-0\cdot\frac23=0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Everything is bounded, so the covariance is well defined. The centred products at $X=-1,0,1$ are respectively $-1/3,0,1/3$. Their equally weighted average is zero: the two ends cancel. Covariance compresses this information into one number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有变量都有界，协方差确实存在。在 $X=-1,0,1$ 三处，中心化乘积分别为 $-1/3,0,1/3$；等权平均后，两端恰好抵消。协方差把这些信息压缩成了一个数。

> [!warning] The conditional mean still changes · 条件均值仍随信息变化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Because $Y=X^2$ is already known once $X$ is known, $E[Y\mid X]=X^2$. Its values are $1,0,1$, not the constant $E[Y]=2/3$. In particular, the event $X=0$ has probability $1/3$ and gives conditional mean zero. This is a genuine failure of mean independence, not a discrepancy confined to a probability-zero point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因为知道 $X$ 后就知道了 $Y=X^2$，所以 $E[Y\mid X]=X^2$。它取 $1,0,1$，并不是常数 $E[Y]=2/3$。尤其 $X=0$ 以概率 $1/3$ 出现，该组均值为零；这是真正违反均值独立，而不是只在零概率点上版本不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The reverse direction does hold here
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*本例的反向却成立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $Y=0$, then $X=0$. If $Y=1$, the two possible values $X=-1$ and $X=1$ have conditional probabilities $(1/3)/(2/3)=1/2$. Both columns therefore give $E[X\mid Y]=0=E[X]$. So $X$ is mean independent of $Y$, although $Y$ is not mean independent of $X$. Covariance itself is symmetric; the two mean-independence questions are not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $Y=0$，则 $X=0$；若 $Y=1$，则 $X=-1$ 和 $1$ 的条件概率均为 $(1/3)/(2/3)=1/2$。所以两列都给出 $E[X\mid Y]=0=E[X]$。本例中 $X$ 对 $Y$ 均值独立，$Y$ 却不对 $X$ 均值独立。协方差是对称的，这两个均值独立问题则不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This counterexample isolates the missing step between [[独立性强弱关系|zero covariance and mean independence]]. It also disproves independence, but [[零协方差不推独立|that broader conclusion]] is a separate boundary. In prediction language, a straight-line slope can be zero while the full [[条件均值与线性投影|conditional-mean function]] is curved and highly informative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个反例专门否定[[独立性强弱关系|零协方差到均值独立]]的中间箭头。它也否定独立性，但[[零协方差不推独立]]讨论的是更宽的边界。用预测语言说，最佳直线斜率可以为零，而完整[[条件均值与线性投影|条件均值函数]]仍有弯曲关系并提供充分信息。
