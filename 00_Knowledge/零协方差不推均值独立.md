# 零协方差可以与随 X 改变的条件均值同时存在

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Zero covariance can coexist with a conditional mean that varies with X*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $X$ 在 $\{-1,0,1\}$ 上等概率，$Y=X^2$。$E[X]=E[X^3]=0$、$E[Y]=2/3$，所以
$$\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=E[X^3]-E[X]E[X^2]=0.$$
但 $E[Y\mid X=x]=x^2$，在 $x=0$ 时不等于 $E[Y]$，故 $Y$ 不对 $X$ 均值独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A symmetric three-point variable and its square have zero covariance, while the conditional mean of the square changes with the source value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
正负两端的中心化乘积贡献抵消，协方差因此看不见这条确定的非线性关系。这比 [[零协方差不推独立]]更明确地否定“零协方差推出均值独立”这条中间箭头。反向 $E[X\mid Y]=0$ 在本例却成立，再次显示方向性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Symmetric product contributions cancel despite a deterministic nonlinear relation. This isolates the failed intermediate implication more precisely than [[零协方差不推独立|zero covariance versus independence]]. The reverse mean-independence condition does hold in this example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
