# Inference with correlated rows
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*相关观测行下的推断*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Four measurements on one person can share a person-specific disturbance. Averaging them reduces the separate measurement noise, but it does not average away the disturbance that appears in all four. Treating those rows as four independent repetitions can therefore give the wrong [[标准误含义|standard error]]—the sampling standard deviation of an estimate—even when the estimate is centred on the right mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个人的四次测量可能共享一项个人冲击。取平均能减小各次测量独有的噪声，却不能消掉四次都带着的共同冲击。因此，把这些行当成四次独立重复，可能错算[[标准误含义|标准误]]，也就是估计量在重复抽样中的标准差，即使估计量的中心仍然正确。

> [!example] Four rows, partly shared information
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $Y_i=\mu+A+e_i$, for $i=1,\ldots,4$. The fixed target is $\mu$; the common disturbance $A$ and the four individual noises $e_i$ have mean zero, are mutually independent, and have variances $\operatorname{Var}(A)=3$ and $\operatorname{Var}(e_i)=1$. Each reading has variance $4$, but two different readings have covariance $3$. By the [[和的方差协方差项|variance-of-a-sum formula]],
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> $$
> E(\bar Y)=\mu,\qquad
> \operatorname{Var}(\bar Y)=3+\frac14=\frac{13}{4}.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Even if the marginal variance $4$ were known, incorrectly assuming independent rows would give $4/4=1$, rather than $13/4$. The true standard error is $\sqrt{13}/2\approx1.80$, compared with $1$ from that calculation. This example isolates the effect of ignored covariance; estimating the variance from the same four readings introduces a separate issue.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $Y_i=\mu+A+e_i$，其中 $\mu$ 是固定目标，$A$ 与四个 $e_i$ 互相独立、均值为零，方差分别为 $3$ 与 $1$。每次读数方差为 $4$，不同读数的协方差却为 $3$。由[[和的方差协方差项]]，均值仍为 $\mu$，平均数方差是 $13/4$。即使已知单次读数方差为 $4$，错按独立计算仍会得到 $1$；真实标准误约为 $1.80$。这里特意假定边际方差已知，以单独展示忽略协方差的后果；再从这四次读数估方差是另一个问题。

## A correct centre does not fix the uncertainty
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*中心正确，不代表误差条正确*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In [[多响应线性回归|multiple-response regression]], write $Y=XB+U$: $n$ observations form the rows, $q\ge1$ responses form the columns, $X$ is an $n\times k$ shared design with $1\le k\le n$ and full column rank (its columns are [[线性无关|linearly independent]]), and $B$ is a fixed $k\times q$ coefficient matrix. Work with fixed $X$, or explicitly condition the entire model on its realised value. Suppose $E(U\mid X)=0$ and finite conditional second moments exist. The [[共享设计的逐响应OLS|OLS estimate]] is $\hat B=AY$, where $A=(X^TX)^{-1}X^T$. Since $AX=I_k$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
\hat B-B=AU,\qquad E(\hat B\mid X)=B.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is conditional [[估计量无偏性|unbiasedness]]. It requires no independence between error rows. It also does not make the response rows identically distributed: their means, the rows of $XB$, can differ. In the classical Gaussian model, it is the zero-mean **error** rows that are iid with a common covariance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[多响应线性回归]] $Y=XB+U$ 中，$n$ 行是观测，$q\ge1$ 列是响应，$X$ 是 $n\times k$ 的满列秩共同设计，即各列[[线性无关]]，$1\le k\le n$，$B$ 是固定的 $k\times q$ 系数矩阵。把 $X$ 固定，或明确对其实现值作条件分析。若 $E(U\mid X)=0$ 且存在有限条件二阶矩，[[共享设计的逐响应OLS|OLS 估计]]为 $\hat B=AY$，其中 $A=(X^TX)^{-1}X^T$。因为 $AX=I_k$，上式立即给出条件[[估计量无偏性|无偏性]]，不需要误差行独立。响应行的均值是 $XB$ 的各行，可以不同；经典 Gaussian 模型中的 iid 对象是零均值的**误差行**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the [[观测行协方差|separable covariance model]], let $\operatorname{Cov}(U_{ia},U_{jb}\mid X)=\Omega_{ij}\Sigma_{ab}$, where $\Omega$ is the $n\times n$ row factor and $\Sigma$ the $q\times q$ response factor. Both are fixed symmetric [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefinite]] matrices in this conditional model. For coefficient indices $r,s$ and response indices $a,b$, the [[协方差仿射变换|fixed linear covariance rule]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
\operatorname{Cov}(\hat B_{ra},\hat B_{sb}\mid X)
=(A\Omega A^T)_{rs}\Sigma_{ab}.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $\Omega=I_n$, the row part is $AA^T=(X^TX)^{-1}$. For a general $\Omega$, the actual quantity is $A\Omega A^T$. Equality can occur for a particular design even without $\Omega=I_n$; it must be checked, not assumed. Likewise, ignored correlation does not always make standard errors too small: the direction depends on the covariances and the contrast being estimated. A covariance matrix alone also does not establish independence; the [[联合高斯独立判据|Gaussian independence criterion]] needs a joint Gaussian law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[观测行协方差|可分离协方差模型]]中，$\Omega$ 与 $\Sigma$ 分别是固定的、对称[[半正定矩阵|半正定]]的 $n\times n$ 行因子与 $q\times q$ 响应因子，满足 $\operatorname{Cov}(U_{ia},U_{jb}\mid X)=\Omega_{ij}\Sigma_{ab}$。上式来自[[协方差仿射变换|固定线性协方差变换]]，其中 $r,s$ 是系数索引，$a,b$ 是响应索引。$\Omega=I_n$ 时，行方向的协方差因子简化为 $(X^TX)^{-1}$；一般情况却要算 $A\Omega A^T$。特定设计下两者可能碰巧相同，因此单位行因子是充分条件，不能反说它是数值相等的必要条件。忽略相关也不一定低估标准误，方向取决于协方差和所估计的对比。协方差本身更不等于独立性；[[联合高斯独立判据]]还要求联合 Gaussian 分布。

## More rows need not mean convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*增加行数，未必让误差消失*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[估计量一致性|Consistency]] is a different claim: for every fixed tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, the probability that the estimation error exceeds it must tend to zero as the sample size grows. Extend the example to $n$ readings with one shared $A\sim N(0,3)$ and independent $e_i\sim N(0,1)$, independent of $A$. Then $\bar Y_n$ is unbiased for every $n$, but
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
\bar Y_n-\mu\sim N\!\left(0,3+\frac1n\right),\qquad
P(|\bar Y_n-\mu|>\varepsilon)
\longrightarrow P(|A|>\varepsilon)>0.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The shared disturbance survives averaging. This proves inconsistency here; merely observing a nonvanishing variance would not, by itself, prove that for an arbitrary distribution. General [[OLS一致性条件|OLS consistency conditions]] require suitable convergence of sample moments and an informative limiting design, not just a zero conditional mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[估计量一致性|一致性]]说的是：对每个固定容差 $\varepsilon>0$，估计误差超过它的概率随样本量增加趋于零。把例子扩展到 $n$ 次读数，令共同冲击 $A\sim N(0,3)$，各次独有噪声独立服从 $N(0,1)$，并与 $A$ 独立。平均数每次都无偏，但上面的正态误差概率趋于一个正数，因此不一致。这里真正证明的是概率不收缩；对任意分布，仅有方差不趋零不足以证明不一致。一般[[OLS一致性条件]]还需要相应样本矩收敛和能识别参数的极限设计。

## Exact residual laws also have assumptions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*残差的精确分布也依赖模型*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[多响应残差与误差SSP|residual SSCP]] is $R^TR$, where $R=Y-X\hat B$. Under independent zero-mean Gaussian error rows with common covariance $\Sigma$, for $n>k$ its [[误差SSP抽样律|Wishart sampling law]] has $n-k$ degrees of freedom. If $n=k$, the full-rank fit leaves $R=0$ and no positive-degree residual calibration. Correlated rows do not generally preserve that law with the same scale matrix. In the scalar common-shock example, fitting an intercept gives $Y_i-\bar Y=e_i-\bar e$: the common $A$ vanishes from the residuals. For $n\ge2$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
$$
\sum_{i=1}^n(Y_i-\bar Y)^2\sim\chi^2_{n-1},
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
not $4\chi^2_{n-1}$, which independent $N(\mu,4)$ readings would produce. Here [[卡方分布|chi-square]] describes a sum of squares of independent standard normal directions. Residuals can therefore look modest while the fitted mean still carries the shared shock. Exact $t$, $F$ or [[MANOVA检验校准|MANOVA calibrations]] must be derived for the stated design and covariance model. A special coincidence of a point estimate or one variance does not validate all these reference laws.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[多响应残差与误差SSP|残差 SSCP]]为 $R^TR$，其中 $R=Y-X\hat B$。独立、零均值、共同协方差为 $\Sigma$ 的 Gaussian 误差行，在 $n>k$ 时给出自由度 $n-k$ 的[[误差SSP抽样律|Wishart 残差律]]；$n=k$ 时满秩拟合使 $R=0$，不再有正自由度的残差校准。相关行一般不能沿用同一尺度矩阵的结论。在标量共同冲击例子中，拟合截距后 $Y_i-\bar Y=e_i-\bar e$，共同冲击恰好被残差化消掉。因此 $n\ge2$ 时残差平方和服从 $\chi^2_{n-1}$，而不是独立 $N(\mu,4)$ 读数会给出的 $4\chi^2_{n-1}$；这里[[卡方分布]]对应独立标准正态方向的平方和。残差可能很小，但均值估计仍带着共同冲击。精确的 $t$、$F$ 或 [[MANOVA检验校准|MANOVA 校准]]必须对应实际设计和协方差模型，不能凭某个点估计或方差碰巧相同就一并继承。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A correctly specified, known row covariance can be used in [[相关行下的多响应GLS|multivariate GLS]]. [[聚类标准误边界|Cluster-robust inference]] instead targets suitable dependence within groups under its own sampling and approximation conditions; time dependence has its own [[HAC协方差|HAC conditions]]. None of these fixes a wrong conditional mean. The choice follows the sampling design and inferential target, rather than a generic “correlation detected” software option.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正确指定且已知的行协方差可用于[[相关行下的多响应GLS]]；[[聚类标准误边界|聚类稳健推断]]则在自己的抽样与近似条件下处理组内依赖，时间依赖还有相应的 [[HAC协方差|HAC 条件]]。这些方法都不能修复错误的条件均值。应根据抽样设计和目标选方法，而不是把“发现相关”翻译成一个固定软件选项。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Shalizi, CMU 36-401, Lectures 24–25, §6–7, PDF pp.24–27](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~cshalizi/mreg/15/lectures/24/lecture-24--25.pdf#page=24): correlated-error covariance, GLS and the separate issue of mean misspecification. Positive definiteness is an additional assumption for inverse-based GLS; a general covariance need only be semidefinite.
- [Sawyer, *Multivariate Linear Models*, §1–3, pp.1–4, and the Wishart definition in §5, pp.5–6](https://www.math.wustl.edu/~sawyer/handouts/manovas.pdf#page=1): independent Gaussian error rows, coefficient covariance, residual SSCP and the reference-distribution setting. Its row-stacking convention is not mixed with column stacking.
- [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/08_自相关.md#5.1.1. GLS 原理|The permanent autocorrelation course section]] distinguishes model-based transformation from covariance corrections. The shared-shock examples, general covariance expression and probability-limit counterexample are direct calculations from the stated model; no unrestricted consistency claim is imported from a short lecture summary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- CMU 原讲义第24–27页核对相关误差、GLS 与均值错设的分工；一般协方差只需半正定，使用逆矩阵另加正定条件。
- Sawyer 第1–4页及第5–6页的 Wishart 定义核对独立 Gaussian 行、系数协方差、SSCP 与精确分布的模型条件；其逐行堆叠约定没有与逐列约定混用。
- 永久课程的自相关章节提供方法范围。共同冲击、一般协方差式及不一致例子均按明示模型直接推导，不沿用未附渐近条件的概括句。
