# 行为金融用有关信念偏好和认知限制的心理学证据研究金融决策与市场结果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Behavioral finance studies financial decisions and market outcomes using psychological evidence about beliefs, preferences, and cognitive limits*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行为金融研究人在金融决策中怎样形成判断、评价得失和分配注意力，以及这些选择怎样影响交易、融资与价格。它把可观察的心理机制放进经济模型，而不是把事后解释不了的价格都叫作“情绪”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Behavioral finance studies judgment, gain–loss evaluation, and attention in financial decisions, and their consequences for trading, financing, and prices. It puts observable psychological mechanisms into economic models rather than labeling unexplained prices as sentiment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里要区分研究对象。解释一个人为何拒绝小额赌局，可以从其[[前景理论|得失评价]]入手；解释市场价格为何偏离基准，还必须交代这些人的需求如何影响成交价格，以及其他交易者为何没有完全抵消，见[[套利限制]]。个人决策证据与市场定价证据因此不是同一层证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Explaining one person's rejection of a small gamble can begin with [[前景理论|gain–loss evaluation]]. Explaining a market price also requires an account of demand and why counterparties do not fully offset it; see [[套利限制|limits to arbitrage]]. Individual-choice evidence and pricing evidence operate at different levels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，调查显示一组人看涨，不足以说明股票被高估：还不知道他们持有多少钱、会不会下单，以及卖方如何回应。真正的模型要把这些环节补上，并提出可以失败的预测，见[[行为解释证据链]]。这并不要求宣布整个市场无效；效率仍是[[有效市场假说|相对于信息集的命题]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bullish survey alone does not establish overpricing: wealth, orders, and counterparties remain unknown. A useful model supplies those links and rejectable predictions; see [[行为解释证据链|testing a behavioral explanation]]. It need not declare an entire market inefficient; [[有效市场假说|efficiency is information-relative]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Barberis (2018), *Psychology-based Models of Asset Prices and Trading Volume*](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24723)：核对信念、偏好与认知限制的研究范围。
- [Barberis & Thaler (2003), *A Survey of Behavioral Finance*，NBER 工作论文入口](https://www.nber.org/papers/w9222)：核对心理机制与套利限制两个支柱。调查例子为本卡自拟的识别边界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Barberis defines the psychological modeling scope; Barberis and Thaler distinguish psychology from limits to arbitrage. The survey example is an original identification illustration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
