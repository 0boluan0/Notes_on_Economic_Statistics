# 模型对齐需要分别定义有用性、诚实性、安全性及不同人偏好的聚合规则，单一总分不能替代这些取舍
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Model alignment requires separate definitions of helpfulness, honesty, safety, and the aggregation rule for heterogeneous preferences; a single score cannot replace these trade-offs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] “更对齐”必须先说明对谁、在哪些维度、怎样取舍
> 有用性、诚实性与安全性不是一个天然给定的标量。一个回答可以更详细却更容易诱导危险操作，可以更谨慎却过度拒答，也可以让多数人满意却持续损害少数群体。训练最终或许需要一个标量 loss，但把多个目标压成一个数之前，必须公开每个维度的定义、硬约束、权重与不同人的偏好怎样聚合。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Helpfulness, honesty, and safety do not arrive as one naturally given scalar. A response may be more detailed yet enable harm, more cautious yet over-refuse, or popular with a majority while persistently disadvantaging a minority. Training may ultimately require a scalar loss, but the definitions, hard constraints, weights, and rule for aggregating heterogeneous preferences must be explicit before objectives are compressed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## HHH 是有用的工作框架，不是完备安全定理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*HHH is a useful working framework, not a complete security theorem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Askell et al. 用 helpful、honest、harmless（有用、诚实、无害）组织通用助手的初步目标与评测。这个分解的价值在于迫使研究者看到冲突：诚实需要表达不确定性，有用性可能鼓励给出更多细节，无害性可能要求限制某些帮助。

但 HHH 不是证明系统安全的充分条件。它没有自动指定威胁模型、权限边界、隐私、工具副作用、长程交互、错误恢复或谁有权决定可接受风险；三个词内部也仍需具体评分规则（rubric）与分母。把一次 HHH 总分上升写成“模型已经安全”，超出了框架能支持的结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Askell et al. organise initial objectives and evaluations for a general assistant around helpfulness, honesty, and harmlessness. The decomposition exposes conflicts: honesty calls for calibrated uncertainty, helpfulness may favour detail, and harmlessness may restrict assistance. HHH is not a sufficient security theorem. It does not automatically specify a threat model, permissions, privacy, tool side effects, long-horizon interaction, recovery, or decision rights, and every dimension still needs a concrete rubric and denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不同人的偏好还需要一条社会选择规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Heterogeneous preferences require an aggregation rule*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设第 $i$ 个评估者对回答 $y$ 的评价为 $u_i(y)$。最大化平均值 $\frac1n\sum_i u_i(y)$、最大化最低评价 $\min_i u_i(y)$、要求每个群体达到底线，或允许某些安全维度一票否决，会选出不同回答。数据不会自己决定哪条规则正当；抽样谁、如何加权、哪些权利不可交易，都是治理选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let evaluator $i$ assign value $u_i(y)$ to response $y$. Maximising the mean, maximising the minimum, enforcing a floor for every group, or allowing a safety veto can select different responses. Data do not determine which rule is legitimate. Who is sampled, how people are weighted, and which rights are non-tradeable are governance choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Bakker et al. 在道德与政治议题上收集群体成员观点和评分，用奖励模型预测个人偏好，再按不同社会福利函数排序共识陈述。被排除在构造过程外的人更可能反对结果，说明“为谁聚合”会实际改变共识。该研究展示了一种受控任务中的可行机制，不证明某个福利函数解决了普遍价值对齐，也不把多数赞同等同于真理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bakker et al. collected opinions and ratings on moral and political questions, predicted individual preferences with a reward model, and ranked candidate consensus statements under different social-welfare functions. People excluded from constructing a consensus were more likely to dissent, showing that whose preferences enter the aggregation changes the outcome. This demonstrates a mechanism in a controlled task; it does not solve general value alignment or turn majority approval into truth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先保留目标向量，再决定是否汇总
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Preserve the objective vector before deciding whether to aggregate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

评测至少应分别报告：正常任务有用性、事实正确与校准、危险服从、良性请求过度拒答，以及关键群体的分层结果。若业务必须使用总分，应同时给出聚合公式、权重来源、硬约束和各分项变化。一个总分只方便排序；它不能告诉读者进步来自哪里，也不能证明没有被平均数掩盖的严重退化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Evaluation should at least report ordinary-task utility, factual correctness and calibration, harmful compliance, over-refusal on benign requests, and stratified results for relevant groups. If an aggregate score is operationally necessary, publish its formula, source of weights, hard constraints, and component changes. A scalar facilitates ranking; it cannot reveal which objective improved or rule out severe regressions hidden by averaging.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 新模型的对齐总分从 82 升至 85，但平均有用性提高 8 分，诚实性不变，一个高风险群体的危险服从率从 2% 升至 7%。能否只凭总分发布“对齐改善”？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An alignment score rises from 82 to 85: average helpfulness gains eight points, honesty is unchanged, and harmful compliance for one high-risk group rises from 2% to 7%. Is the aggregate sufficient to announce improved alignment?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。总分隐藏了安全退化和受影响群体。必须说明聚合权重与安全底线，并分别报告各维度和群体结果；若 7% 违反预先规定的安全约束，平均有用性收益不能把它抵消掉。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. The aggregate hides a safety regression and the affected group. Report the aggregation weights, safety floor, dimensions, and group results separately. If 7% violates a predeclared safety constraint, an average helpfulness gain cannot compensate for it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Askell et al. (2021), “A General Language Assistant as a Laboratory for Alignment”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.00861)：核对 helpful、honest、harmless 的通用助手工作框架与对应实验目标。本文将 HHH 用作目标分解，不把它扩写为完备的系统安全保证。
- [Bakker et al. (2022), “Fine-tuning language models to find agreement among humans with diverse preferences”](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2022/hash/f978c8f3b5f399cae464e85f72e28503-Abstract-Conference.html)：核对个人偏好模型、社会福利函数、共识陈述排序及排除部分成员后的异议结果。实验限定于其参与者、议题和共识任务，不给出普遍正确的价值聚合规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Askell et al. (2021) was checked for the helpful, honest, and harmless working framework and its experimental objectives. HHH is used here as an objective decomposition, not expanded into a complete system-security guarantee.
- Bakker et al. (2022) was checked for individual preference models, social-welfare functions, ranking of consensus statements, and dissent when some members were excluded. The experiments are bounded by their participants, issues, and consensus task; they do not establish a universally correct value-aggregation rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
