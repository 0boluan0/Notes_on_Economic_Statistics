# 无违约的固定债券现金流应逐笔按对应期限贴现后相加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Value default-free fixed bond cash flows by discounting each payment at its matching maturity and adding*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若债券未来支付的金额和日期已确定，且不存在违约或改变支付的未决选择权，就先列出结算后买方有权获得的每笔 $CF_k$，再使用同币种、同风险口径的期限贴现因子：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When future payment amounts and dates are fixed, with no default or unresolved option that changes them, first list every $CF_k$ to which the buyer is entitled after settlement. Then apply maturity-matched discount factors with consistent currency and risk conventions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P=\sum_{k=1}^{N}CF_k\,DF(0,t_k).$$

对按面值 $F$ 到期偿还、规则付息 $C$ 的债券，最后一期是 $CF_N=C+F$，此前为 $C$。若每年付息，以年有效[[即期利率]] $s_k$ 表示同一组贴现因子，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For regular coupons $C$ and redemption at face value $F$, the last payment is $CF_N=C+F$ and earlier payments are $C$. With annual coupons and effective annual [[即期利率|spot rates]] $s_k$ representing those discount factors,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P=\sum_{k=1}^{N}\frac{C}{(1+s_k)^k}+\frac{F}{(1+s_N)^N}.$$

例如面值 $100$、年息票 $5$、两年到期，若 $DF(0,1)=0.96$、$DF(0,2)=0.90$，价格是 $5\times0.96+105\times0.90=99.30$。到期本金只在第二年出现，不能把 $110$ 全部当作两年后的支付。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For face value 100, annual coupons of 5, and two years remaining, discount factors of 0.96 and 0.90 give $5\times0.96+105\times0.90=99.30$. Principal appears only at the second date; the total 110 cannot all be treated as a payment in two years.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用单一[[到期收益率]]重现这只债券的价格，是另一种价格表达，不证明即期曲线平坦。存在违约风险时，也不能把承诺支付直接套进无风险贴现因子；须另建[[单期信用债定价|违约、回收及风险定价模型]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reproducing this bond's price with one [[到期收益率|yield to maturity]] is another price representation, not evidence of a flat spot curve. With default risk, promised payments cannot simply be discounted at default-free factors; [[单期信用债定价|default, recovery, and risk pricing require a separate model]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
