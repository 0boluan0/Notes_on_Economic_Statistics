# 拟凹函数是在凸定义域上所有上轮廓集都凸的函数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A quasiconcave function has a convex domain and convex upper contour sets at every threshold*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在凸域 $U$ 上，$f:U\to\mathbb R$ 拟凹，是指每个 $k\in\mathbb R$ 的[[上轮廓集]] $C_k^+$ 都为[[凸集]]。只要两点都达到某门槛，所有混合也达到。

$\min\{x_1,x_2\}$ 拟凹：其上轮廓集为 $x_1\ge k,x_2\ge k$。它可以在对角线上不可导，所以拟凹定义不依赖 Hessian。[[凹性推出拟凹性]]，但反向不成立；等价的逐线段写法见[[拟凹性线段判据]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quasiconcavity means that every [[上轮廓集|upper contour set]] is [[凸集|convex]] on a convex domain. The minimum of two coordinates is an example: both coordinates must exceed the threshold. Differentiability is unnecessary. [[凹性推出拟凹性|Concavity implies the property]], while the [[拟凹性线段判据|segment criterion]] gives an equivalent formulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
