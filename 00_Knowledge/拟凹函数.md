# Quasiconcave functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拟凹函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **quasiconcave function** keeps a shared performance threshold intact when inputs are blended. If two choices both achieve at least a chosen value, every point on the segment between those choices also achieves that value. The requirement applies to every threshold, not just the highest value or one attractive-looking contour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**拟凹函数**在混合输入时保住共同的达标门槛：如果两个选择都至少达到某个值，它们之间整条线段上的选择也都达到该值。这个要求针对每一个门槛，而不仅是最高值或某一条看起来合适的轮廓。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The required input geometry is a [[凸集|convex domain]]: every segment between allowed inputs stays allowed. At a fixed threshold, the qualifying inputs form an [[上轮廓集|upper contour set]], which includes equality. Quasiconcavity requires each of these input sets to be convex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入先组成[[凸集|凸定义域]]，即合法输入之间的整条线段仍然合法。固定门槛时，达标输入组成包含等号的[[上轮廓集]]；拟凹性要求这些输入集合逐个都是凸集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One condition for every threshold
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*对每个门槛提出同一个条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A quasiconcave function has a convex domain and convex upper contour sets at every threshold
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在凸域 $U$ 上，$f:U\to\mathbb R$ 拟凹，是指每个 $k\in\mathbb R$ 的[[上轮廓集]] $C_k^+$ 都为[[凸集]]。只要两点都达到某门槛，所有混合也达到。

> [!important] Read the quantifiers · 读清量词
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be convex and $f:U\to\mathbb R$ be real-valued. For **every** real threshold $k$, form $C_k^+=\{x\in U:f(x)\ge k\}$. The function is quasiconcave when each $C_k^+$ is convex: for every $x,y\in C_k^+$ and every $t\in[0,1]$, the point $tx+(1-t)y$ is still in $C_k^+$. Here $x,y$ are inputs, $k$ is an output threshold, and $t$ determines the mixture.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 为凸集，$f:U\to\mathbb R$ 为实值函数。对**每个**实数门槛 $k$，构造 $C_k^+=\{x\in U:f(x)\ge k\}$。每个 $C_k^+$ 都凸时，$f$ 才拟凹：任取 $x,y\in C_k^+$ 及 $t\in[0,1]$，$tx+(1-t)y$ 仍属于 $C_k^+$。其中 $x,y$ 是输入，$k$ 是输出门槛，$t$ 决定混合比例。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quasiconcavity means that every [[上轮廓集|upper contour set]] is [[凸集|convex]] on a convex domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A minimum of two coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两个坐标取较小值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The minimum of two coordinates is an example: both coordinates must meet the threshold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $f(x_1,x_2)=\min\{x_1,x_2\}$ 把两个实坐标中较小的一个作为输出。较小者达标，等价于两个坐标都达标。

$$C_k^+=\{(x_1,x_2)\in\mathbb R^2:x_1\ge k,\ x_2\ge k\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At threshold $k=2$, $(2,5)$ and $(4,2)$ qualify, including their boundary coordinates; their midpoint $(3,7/2)$ qualifies too. The general argument uses any two qualifying points $x,y$. For each coordinate $i=1,2$, we have $tx_i+(1-t)y_i\ge tk+(1-t)k=k$. Thus the entire segment qualifies. This proves convexity for arbitrary $k$, rather than checking only that midpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在门槛 $k=2$ 时，$(2,5)$ 与 $(4,2)$ 都达标，等号处的坐标也包含在内；中点 $(3,7/2)$ 同样达标。一般证明取任意两个达标点 $x,y$：对每个坐标 $i=1,2$，都有 $tx_i+(1-t)y_i\ge tk+(1-t)k=k$，因此整条线段都达标。这对任意 $k$ 成立，而不仅检查了一个中点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[凹性推出拟凹性|Concavity implies the property]], while the [[拟凹性线段判据|segment criterion]] gives an equivalent formulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\min\{x_1,x_2\}$ 拟凹：其上轮廓集为 $x_1\ge k,x_2\ge k$。它可以在对角线上不可导，所以拟凹定义不依赖 Hessian。[[凹性推出拟凹性]]，但反向不成立；等价的逐线段写法见[[拟凹性线段判据]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One good threshold is not enough
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个门槛通过还不够*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $q(x)=x^2$ on $\mathbb R$, the upper set at $k=0$ is all of $\mathbb R$, so that set is convex. At $k=1$, however, both $-1$ and $1$ qualify while their midpoint $0$ does not. This single failed threshold disproves quasiconcavity. A positive conclusion needs a reason covering every real $k$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $\mathbb R$ 上的 $q(x)=x^2$，$k=0$ 的上轮廓集是整个 $\mathbb R$，确实凸。但 $k=1$ 时，$-1$ 和 $1$ 都达标，中点 $0$ 却不达标。这一个失败门槛就足以否定拟凹；要证明拟凹，则须给出覆盖全部实数 $k$ 的理由。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Empty and whole-domain sets are valid cases. For the constant function $f=3$, the upper set is $U$ when $k\le3$ and empty when $k>3$. Both are convex when $U$ is convex. A single-point domain also satisfies the definition; if one allows the empty domain, the condition is vacuous there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

空集和整个定义域都是合法情形。常函数 $f=3$ 在 $k\le3$ 时的上轮廓集为 $U$，在 $k>3$ 时为空集；$U$ 凸时，两种集合都凸。单点定义域也满足定义；若允许空定义域，这里的条件就真空成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Weak thresholds do not require smooth curves
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*弱门槛不要求光滑曲线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiability is unnecessary. Even continuity is unnecessary: let $s(x)=0$ for $x<0$ and $s(x)=1$ for $x\ge0$. Its upper sets are $\mathbb R$ for $k\le0$, $[0,\infty)$ for $0<k\le1$, and empty for $k>1$. Every set is convex, although $s$ jumps at zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不要求可导，甚至不要求连续。令 $s(x)=0$（$x<0$），$s(x)=1$（$x\ge0$）：其上轮廓集在 $k\le0$ 时为 $\mathbb R$，在 $0<k\le1$ 时为 $[0,\infty)$，在 $k>1$ 时为空集。它们全都凸，尽管函数在零点跳跃。

> [!warning] Keep equality and shape separate · 分清等号与形状
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The definition uses $f(x)\ge k$, not $f(x)>k$. At a fixed threshold they can differ: they differ exactly when $f$ attains the value $k$. Convexity is also weak: a mixture may exactly meet the threshold. The constant example therefore qualifies without strictly improving any value. Testing strict upper sets does not turn the word “quasiconcave” into a strict-improvement claim.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 定义使用 $f(x)\ge k$，不是 $f(x)>k$；在固定门槛下，两者可能不同：当且仅当 $f$ 实际取到值 $k$ 时，它们才不同。凸性要求也是弱的：混合结果可以恰好达到门槛。因此常函数符合定义，却没有严格改善任何值。考察严格上轮廓集，并不把“拟凹”变成严格改善的断言。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The domain need not be open, and the definition does not ask for a Hessian. It describes threshold sets, not the stronger weighted-average comparison in [[凹凸性|concavity]]. Nor does it certify that a stationary point is optimal; that separate limitation is explained in [[拟凹驻点不保证最优|the stationary-point boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义域不必开，定义也不要求存在 Hessian。它描述门槛集合，不是[[凹凸性|凹性]]中更强的加权平均比较；它也不能保证驻点最优，具体限制见[[拟凹驻点不保证最优]]。
