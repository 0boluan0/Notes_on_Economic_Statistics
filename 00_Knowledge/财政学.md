# 财政学研究政府在经济中的作用及其筹资支出和政策选择的后果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Public finance studies government's economic role and the consequences of its financing, spending, and policy choices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财政学研究政府怎样参与经济：何时有理由介入、采用什么工具、造成什么后果，以及政府实际上为何这样决策。税收、支出和债务是重要对象，规制、公共服务的提供方式以及政治和行政约束也在这一研究范围内。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Public finance studies government's role in the economy: when intervention is warranted, which instruments are used, what consequences follow, and why governments actually make particular choices. Taxation, spending, and debt are central subjects, alongside regulation, public-service delivery, and political or administrative constraints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看政府与家庭、企业之间的循环流时，应同时追踪资金与真实资源。政府购买商品服务会使用资源；现金转移则先改变谁拥有购买力，随后还可能影响行为，核算区别见[[转移支付核算]]。财政学不只问预算收支是否相抵，也问人的选择和境况怎样改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the flows linking government, households, and firms, track both money and real resources. Government purchases use resources; a cash transfer first changes who holds purchasing power and may subsequently change behaviour. [[转移支付核算|Transfer accounting]] develops that distinction. Public finance asks not only whether budgets balance, but how choices and well-being change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中，“政策会怎样”进入[[实证分析]]，“政策是否值得”进入[[福利经济学]]，“政策为什么实际被选中”进入[[公共选择分析]]。这三个问题互相提供信息，但不能用其中一个问题的答案代替另外两个。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“What will the policy do?” leads to [[实证分析|positive analysis]]; “Is it desirable?” to [[福利经济学|welfare economics]]; and “Why was it actually chosen?” to [[公共选择分析|public choice]]. These questions inform one another, but an answer to one does not substitute for answers to the others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
