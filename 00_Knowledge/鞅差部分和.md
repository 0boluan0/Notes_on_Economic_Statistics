# 鞅差序列的部分和是鞅
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Partial sums of a martingale difference sequence form a martingale*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 部分和定理
> 设 $(D_n)_{n\ge1}$ 是相对滤过 $(\mathcal F_n)_{n\ge0}$ 的[[鞅差序列]]。若 $M_0$ 对 $\mathcal F_0$ 可测且可积，并定义
> $$
> M_n=M_0+\sum_{k=1}^nD_k,
> $$
> 则 $(M_n,\mathcal F_n)_{n\ge0}$ 是鞅。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $(D_n)$ is a martingale difference sequence and $M_0$ is integrable and measurable with respect to $\mathcal F_0$, then $M_n=M_0+\sum_{k=1}^nD_k$ is a martingale relative to the same filtration.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么部分和满足鞅条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why the partial sums satisfy the martingale condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个 $D_k$ 对 $\mathcal F_k$ 可测且可积，所以 $M_n$ 对 $\mathcal F_n$ 可测且可积。再利用 $M_{n-1}$ 对 $\mathcal F_{n-1}$ 可测以及鞅差的条件零均值，

$$
\begin{aligned}
E(M_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1})
&=E(M_{n-1}+D_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1})\\
&=M_{n-1}+E(D_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1})\\
&=M_{n-1}.
\end{aligned}
$$

这正是离散时间鞅的一步条件期望判据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adaptedness and integrability pass from the increments to each finite partial sum. Conditioning one step back leaves $M_{n-1}$ fixed, while the new increment has conditional mean zero, so the conditional expectation equals the current value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 零无条件均值不够
> 证明使用的是 $E(D_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1})=0$，而不只是 $E(D_n)=0$。若旧信息已能预测新增量的方向，部分和就不必是鞅。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The proof needs zero conditional mean given the preceding information, not merely zero unconditional mean. Predictable conditional drift can remain hidden inside an unconditional average of zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 已知 $E(D_n)=0$ 且 $D_n$ 可积。为什么还不能直接断言 $\sum_{k=1}^nD_k$ 是鞅？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why do integrability and $E(D_n)=0$ alone not prove that the partial sums are a martingale?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 还需要相对指定滤过的适应性和 $E(D_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1})=0$。无条件零均值不排除上一期信息已经能预测方向。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The increments must be adapted to the chosen filtration and have zero conditional mean given the preceding information. An unconditional mean of zero does not rule out predictable conditional drift.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
