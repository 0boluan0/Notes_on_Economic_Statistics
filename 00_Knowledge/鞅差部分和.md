# Partial sums of martingale differences
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*鞅差部分和*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A martingale difference describes one conditionally fair change. Adding such changes produces a running total whose next conditional average equals its current level. This is the **partial-sum theorem**: it turns a statement about individual increments into a statement about the accumulated process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

鞅差描述一次条件公平的变化。把这样的变化逐次加起来，就得到一个累计过程：下一期的条件平均等于当前水平。**鞅差部分和定理**正是把逐项增量的条件，转成累计过程的性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a [[滤过定义|filtration]] $(\mathcal F_n)_{n\ge0}$ recording available information. A [[鞅差序列|martingale difference sequence]] $(D_n)_{n\ge1}$ is observed step by step, has $E|D_n|<\infty$, and satisfies $E[D_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]=0$. Choose a real initial value $M_0$ that is $\mathcal F_0$-measurable and integrable. It may be random, provided it is already known at time zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定记录可用信息的[[滤过定义|滤过]] $(\mathcal F_n)_{n\ge0}$。[[鞅差序列]] $(D_n)_{n\ge1}$ 逐期可观察，满足 $E|D_n|<\infty$，且 $E[D_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]=0$。选择实初值 $M_0$，要求它对 $\mathcal F_0$ 可测且可积；它可以随机，只要在零时刻已经知道。

$$M_n=M_0+\sum_{k=1}^{n}D_k,\qquad n\ge0.$$

> [!important] A martingale for the same information · 相对于同一信息流的鞅
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The finite partial sums $(M_n)$ form a [[鞅定义|martingale]] relative to the same $(\mathcal F_n)$. At $n=0$ the sum is empty and equals zero, so the formula retains the chosen $M_0$. This conclusion needs neither independent increments nor finite second moments.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这些有限部分和 $(M_n)$ 相对于同一个 $(\mathcal F_n)$ 构成[[鞅定义|鞅]]。$n=0$ 时和式为空、取零，因此公式保留所选初值 $M_0$。结论不要求增量独立，也不要求有限二阶矩。

## Check all three martingale requirements
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么部分和满足鞅条件

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, every past $D_k$ with $k\le n$ is known by time $n$, because $\mathcal F_k\subseteq\mathcal F_n$. The initial value is known too. Their sum is therefore [[适应过程|adapted]]. Next, the triangle inequality gives a finite first absolute moment:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看信息：对 $k\le n$，有 $\mathcal F_k\subseteq\mathcal F_n$，所以到时刻 $n$ 已经知道每个过去的 $D_k$，也知道初值，和式因此[[适应过程|适应]]。再看可积性，三角不等式给出有限的绝对值期望：

$$E|M_n|\le E|M_0|+\sum_{k=1}^{n}E|D_k|<\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally write $M_n=M_{n-1}+D_n$. In a [[条件期望|conditional expectation]] given $\mathcal F_{n-1}$, the previous total is already known, while the new change averages to zero:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后写成 $M_n=M_{n-1}+D_n$。给定 $\mathcal F_{n-1}$ 取[[条件期望]]时，之前累计值已知，新的变化则平均为零：

$$\begin{aligned}
E[M_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]
&=M_{n-1}+E[D_n\mid\mathcal F_{n-1}]\\
&=M_{n-1}.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These one-step equalities imply $E[M_n\mid\mathcal F_m]=M_m$ for every $m\le n$ by repeated use of the [[塔式法则|tower property]]. Adaptedness, integrability and this conditional equality establish the full martingale claim. Conversely, subtracting two successive values of an integrable martingale gives adapted, integrable increments with zero preceding conditional mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反复使用[[塔式法则]]，一步等式就推出任意 $m\le n$ 时的 $E[M_n\mid\mathcal F_m]=M_m$。适应、可积与该条件等式合起来，才是完整的鞅结论。反过来，把可积鞅的相邻两项相减，就得到适应、可积且给定上一期信息均值为零的增量。

## A running total with dependent changes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*增量存在依赖的累计例子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take independent fair signs $\xi_1,\xi_2,\ldots$ and their natural filtration, with $\mathcal F_0$ trivial. Start from $M_0=5$, let $D_1=\xi_1$, and set $D_n=(1+\mathbf1_{\{\xi_{n-1}=1\}})\xi_n$ for $n\ge2$. Here the [[事件指示变量|indicator]] $\mathbf1_A$ is $1$ on event $A$ and $0$ otherwise. Each multiplier is known before the next fair sign, so the preceding conditional mean is zero. The [[鞅差序列|definition card]] verifies the dependence and the whole increment sequence; the first two steps here give:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取相互独立的公平符号 $\xi_1,\xi_2,\ldots$ 及其自然滤过，$\mathcal F_0$ 平凡。从 $M_0=5$ 出发，令 $D_1=\xi_1$，对 $n\ge2$ 令 $D_n=(1+\mathbf1_{\{\xi_{n-1}=1\}})\xi_n$。这里的[[事件指示变量]] $\mathbf1_A$ 在事件 $A$ 发生时为 $1$，否则为 $0$。每次倍数在新公平符号揭晓前已知，所以给定此前信息，条件均值为零。[[鞅差序列|定义卡]]核验了增量之间的依赖及整个序列；这里前两步的累计值如下：

| $\xi_1$ | $\xi_2$ | $D_1$ | $D_2$ | $M_1$ | $M_2$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $-1$ | $-1$ | $-1$ | $-1$ | $4$ | $3$ |
| $-1$ | $1$ | $-1$ | $1$ | $4$ | $5$ |
| $1$ | $-1$ | $1$ | $-2$ | $6$ | $4$ |
| $1$ | $1$ | $1$ | $2$ | $6$ | $8$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each row has probability $1/4$. After the first loss, the next total averages to $(3+5)/2=4=M_1$; after the first win, it averages to $(4+8)/2=6=M_1$. The process has no conditional mean drift even though the size of its next change depends on the observed past.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每行概率都是 $1/4$。首轮输后，下期累计值平均为 $(3+5)/2=4=M_1$；首轮赢后，平均为 $(4+8)/2=6=M_1$。下一步变化的大小虽依赖已知过去，累计过程仍没有条件均值漂移。

> [!warning] A finite-time conclusion · 结论针对有限时刻
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The theorem does not assert that an infinite series converges, that the partial sums are [[一致可积|uniformly integrable]], or that expectations may be moved through a limit or a random stopping time. Those require further hypotheses. Nor is $E[D_n]=0$ enough: from $M_0=0$, repeating one revealed fair sign gives $D_n=Z$ and $M_n=nZ$, whose next increment is already known after time $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 定理没有断言无穷级数收敛、部分和[[一致可积]]，或期望可以直接穿过极限、随机停时；这些都要额外条件。仅有 $E[D_n]=0$ 也不够：从 $M_0=0$ 开始，反复使用同一个已经揭示的公平符号，得到 $D_n=Z$、$M_n=nZ$，时刻 $1$ 之后下一项增量已经知道。
