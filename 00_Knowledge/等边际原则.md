# 内部消费最优使各商品每元边际效用相等
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An interior consumer optimum equalises marginal utility per unit of expenditure across goods*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子条件
> 对可微效用 $U(x,y)$、正价格 $p_x,p_y$ 与绑定预算，若内部最优满足一阶约束资格，则存在乘子 $\lambda$ 使 $MU_x=\lambda p_x$、$MU_y=\lambda p_y$，因而
> $$\frac{MU_x}{p_x}=\frac{MU_y}{p_y},$$
> 当 $MU_y\ne0$ 时，还可写成 $MRS_{xy}=MU_x/MU_y=p_x/p_y$。若一边更大，把少量支出移向该商品会在一阶近似下提高效用。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For differentiable utility $U(x,y)$, positive prices, a binding budget, and an interior optimum satisfying the first-order constraint qualification, there is a multiplier $\lambda$ such that $MU_x=\lambda p_x$ and $MU_y=\lambda p_y$, hence
> $$\frac{MU_x}{p_x}=\frac{MU_y}{p_y},$$
> and, when $MU_y\ne0$, $MRS_{xy}=MU_x/MU_y=p_x/p_y$. If one side is larger, a small expenditure reallocation toward that good raises utility to first order.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个等式不是任何消费问题都能直接套用。局部非饱和通常使标准预算约束绑定；若存在饱和点，预算也可能松弛。若某商品取零、数量不可分、偏好不光滑或存在配给，就要改用不等式、次梯度或 [[KKT条件|Kuhn–Tucker 条件]]。内部一阶条件还只给候选点；要用 [[凸优化全局性|效用凹性等充分条件]] 或直接的全局比较确认最优。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equality is not universal. Local nonsatiation normally makes the standard budget constraint bind; with a satiation point, it may be slack. A corner solution, indivisible quantities, non-smooth preferences, or rationing calls for inequalities, subgradients, or the [[KKT条件|Kuhn–Tucker conditions]]. An interior first-order condition supplies only a candidate; [[凸优化全局性|concavity-based sufficient conditions]] or a direct global comparison are needed to establish optimality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

效用的单位本身没有跨人可比含义。严格递增变换 $f(U)$ 总会保留偏好排序和最优选择；若 $f$ 在所考察效用水平可微且 $f'(U)>0$，各边际效用乘以同一正因子，MRS 也保持不变。仅仅“严格递增且可微”并不保证每一点都有 $f'(U)>0$，所以不能无条件用导数比证明序数不变性，也不能把“6 个效用单位对 3 个效用单位”当作可观测心理刻度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Utility units are not interpersonally comparable measurements. Any strictly increasing transformation $f(U)$ preserves the preference ordering and optimal choices. Where $f$ is differentiable and $f'(U)>0$, all marginal utilities are multiplied by the same positive factor and the MRS is also unchanged. Strict increase plus differentiability alone does not force $f'(U)>0$ at every point, so ordinal invariance should not be proved unconditionally from a derivative ratio. Numerical “utility units” are not an observed psychological scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若 $MU_x/p_x>MU_y/p_y$ 且两个商品都为正，边际上应怎样调支出？什么时候这个动作会停？
>
> **答案：** 从 $y$ 向 $x$ 移动少量支出；到两边相等或碰到某个商品为零等边界时停止。

- [Stanford Econ 11, Lecture 3](https://web.stanford.edu/~jay/micro_class/lecture3.pdf)：核对保序变换保留偏好排序；导数比不应被当成序数不变性的无条件定义。
- [[Lagrange必要条件]]、[[KKT条件]] 与 [[凸优化全局性]]：分别承接内部一阶条件、边界条件与全局充分性。
