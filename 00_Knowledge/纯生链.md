# Pure-birth chain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*纯生链*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **pure-birth chain** is a count that can only rise by one at each actual jump. Imagine recording how many items have been produced: the next event changes $0$ to $1$, then $1$ to $2$, and so on. The route through the states is fixed, but the time spent at each level is random. The word “birth” describes this upward transition pattern; the counted objects need not be living things.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**纯生链**是一种每次真正跳跃都只增加一的计数模型。想象记录已经生产出的物品数：下一次事件把 $0$ 变为 $1$，再把 $1$ 变为 $2$，依次上升。状态路线固定，在各层停留多久却是随机的。“生”描述的是这种向上转移结构，不要求所数的对象一定是生物。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We use a [[CTMC定义|continuous-time Markov chain]] with a [[CTMC时间齐次转移函数|time-homogeneous rule]]. Fix an initial level $X_0=n_0$, where $n_0$ is a nonnegative integer. At level $n\ge n_0$, the birth rate is a fixed finite number $\lambda_n\ge0$. It has units of inverse time. A larger positive rate means a shorter average wait, not a larger jump.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里使用具有[[CTMC时间齐次转移函数|时间齐次规律]]的[[CTMC定义|连续时间 Markov 链]]。固定初始层数 $X_0=n_0$，其中 $n_0$ 为非负整数。在每层 $n\ge n_0$，出生率是固定的有限数 $\lambda_n\ge0$，单位为时间的倒数。正速率越大，平均等待越短，而不是一次跳得越远。

> [!example] Same route, different clocks · 路线相同，时钟不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Start at $0$ with rates $\lambda_0=2$, $\lambda_1=4$ and $\lambda_2=0$, measured per hour. The mean wait to go from $0$ to $1$ is $1/2$ hour, and from $1$ to $2$ it is $1/4$ hour. At $2$ the process stops changing. One possible path waits $0.3$ hour, jumps to $1$, waits another $0.2$ hour, and then stays at $2$. Those realised waits need not equal their averages.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $0$ 出发，取每小时速率 $\lambda_0=2$、$\lambda_1=4$、$\lambda_2=0$。从 $0$ 到 $1$ 的平均等待为半小时，从 $1$ 到 $2$ 为四分之一小时；到达 $2$ 后就不再改变。一条可能路径先等0.3小时跳到 $1$，再等0.2小时到达 $2$ 并停在那里。实际等待不必等于平均等待。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One permitted destination from each level
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每一层只允许一个去向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[生成矩阵约束|generator]] $Q=(q_{nj})$ records rates from row state $n$ to column state $j$. The pure-birth pattern is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[生成矩阵约束|生成矩阵]] $Q=(q_{nj})$ 记录从行状态 $n$ 到列状态 $j$ 的速率。纯生结构写为

$$q_{n,n+1}=\lambda_n,\qquad q_{nn}=-\lambda_n,\qquad
q_{nj}=0\quad(j\notin\{n,n+1\}),\qquad 0\le\lambda_n<\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The diagonal is the negative total [[出口率|exit rate]], not a negative probability. If $\lambda_n>0$, leaving $n$ always means entering $n+1$. If $\lambda_n=0$, $n$ is absorbing: no next actual jump occurs. The chain cannot jump directly from $0$ to $2$ or fall from $2$ to $1$. It can nevertheless be at $2$ at a later observation time after making two separate jumps. Permitting downward jumps instead leads to the broader [[生灭链|birth–death model]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对角元是总[[出口率]]的负数，不是负概率。若 $\lambda_n>0$，离开 $n$ 就必定进入 $n+1$；若 $\lambda_n=0$，$n$ 是吸收态，不再发生下一次真正跳跃。链不能一次从 $0$ 直接跳到 $2$，也不能从 $2$ 降到 $1$；但经过两次分别发生的跳跃，稍后的观测当然可能看到 $2$。若还允许向下跳，就属于更一般的[[生灭链|生灭模型]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Build the path by adding waiting times
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把等待时间相加，得到路径*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the fixed starting level, let $H_n$ be the holding time on the first and only visit to level $n$. Along a positive-rate route, the [[CTMC指数停留时间|holding-time construction]] uses mutually independent [[指数分布|exponential variables]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于固定起点，令 $H_n$ 表示首次且唯一访问第 $n$ 层时的停留时间。沿着正速率路线，[[CTMC指数停留时间|停留时间构造]]使用相互独立的[[指数分布|指数变量]]：

$$H_n\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda_n),\qquad
\Pr(H_n>h)=e^{-\lambda_n h},\qquad E[H_n]=\frac1{\lambda_n}\quad(\lambda_n>0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $h\ge0$ is a waiting duration and $E[H_n]$ is its [[期望|mean]]. These waits are independent because each arrival starts a fresh clock depending only on the newly reached state. They need not be [[独立同分布|identically distributed]]: their rates can differ. In this special chain the [[嵌入跳链|embedded state sequence]] is deterministic, $n_0,n_0+1,\ldots$, until absorption, so there is no random route to mix together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $h\ge0$ 是等待时长，$E[H_n]$ 是它的[[期望|平均值]]。每次进入新状态后，只按新状态启动一个新时钟，因此这些等待相互独立；它们未必[[独立同分布|同分布]]，因为速率可以不同。这条特殊链的[[嵌入跳链|嵌入状态序列]]在吸收前固定为 $n_0,n_0+1,\ldots$，没有随机路线需要混合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $T_k$ be the time of the $k$th birth, with $T_0=0$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $T_k$ 为第 $k$ 次出生的时刻，并约定 $T_0=0$，则

$$T_k=\sum_{r=0}^{k-1}H_{n_0+r},\qquad
X_t=n_0+k\quad\text{when }T_k\le t<T_{k+1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the first reachable zero-rate level, set its wait to $\infty$ and stop the construction there. This means permanent absorption, not an ordinary finite exponential variable of rate zero. Rates at higher, unreachable levels no longer affect this path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

遇到第一个可达的零速率层时，将其等待记为 $\infty$，构造就在该层停止。这表示永久吸收，不能当作一个普通的、取有限值的零速率指数变量。更高的不可达层具有怎样的速率，已经不会影响这条路径。

> [!warning] Local rules do not guarantee an unlimited lifetime · 局部规则不保证无限寿命
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Even when every rate is finite and positive, the sum $\zeta=\sum_{n=n_0}^{\infty}H_n$ can be finite. Then infinitely many births accumulate by time $\zeta$: this is [[CTMC爆炸|explosion]]. The [[最小CTMC|minimal construction]] follows the levels only before $\zeta$ and uses a cemetery state afterwards. The exact [[纯生链爆炸判据|reciprocal-rate criterion]] decides this boundary. A constant positive rate gives the familiar [[Poisson是常速纯生链|Poisson special case]], but general state-dependent rates need not.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 即使每个速率都有限且为正，总等待 $\zeta=\sum_{n=n_0}^{\infty}H_n$ 仍可能有限，于是在 $\zeta$ 时刻之前完成无限次出生，这就是[[CTMC爆炸|爆炸]]。[[最小CTMC|最小构造]]只在 $\zeta$ 前沿各层运行，以后进入墓地状态；精确判断见[[纯生链爆炸判据|倒数率判据]]。常数正速率给出熟悉的[[Poisson是常速纯生链|Poisson 特例]]，一般状态依赖速率则未必。
