# 一个点的所有偏导数都存在，仍不足以推出函数在该点连续或多元可微
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The existence of every partial derivative at a point is still insufficient to imply continuity or multivariable differentiability there*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 坐标方向的信息不足以控制所有方向
> [[偏导数]]逐一检查有限条坐标线；多元可微却要求**同一个线性映射**控制所有足够小的扰动。前者存在，只说明坐标方向的差商极限存在，不能自动推出函数沿斜线、曲线或一般点列趋近时也有一致行为。因此，“所有偏导存在”既不保证连续，也不保证 [[全微分]] 存在。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[偏导数|Partial derivatives]] inspect the coordinate lines one at a time, whereas multivariable differentiability requires one linear map to control every sufficiently small perturbation. Coordinate difference quotients can therefore exist even when the function behaves inconsistently along other approaches. The existence of all partial derivatives implies neither continuity nor the existence of a [[全微分|total differential]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反例：两条坐标轴都平静，轴外立即跳变
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Counterexample: both axes are flat while points off the axes jump*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义
$$
g(x,y)=
\begin{cases}
0,&xy=0,\\
1,&xy\ne0.
\end{cases}
$$
沿 $x$ 轴有 $g(t,0)=0$，沿 $y$ 轴也有 $g(0,t)=0$。所以
$$
g_x(0,0)=g_y(0,0)=0.
$$
然而取点列 $(1/k,1/k)\to(0,0)$，每一项都满足 $xy\ne0$，因而 $g(1/k,1/k)=1$，并不趋于 $g(0,0)=0$。函数在原点不连续；而可微必然推出连续，所以它更不可能在原点可微。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both coordinate-axis restrictions are identically zero, so both partial derivatives at the origin exist and equal zero. Along the sequence $(1/k,1/k)$, however, the function remains one and does not approach its value at the origin. The function is not continuous there; since differentiability implies continuity, it is not differentiable either.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 错误推理究竟错在哪里
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Where the invalid implication breaks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若函数可微，候选线性映射确实由偏导数组成：对实值函数，$Df(a)h=\nabla f(a)^Th$。但“候选写得出来”不等于余项已经满足
$$
\frac{f(a+h)-f(a)-Df(a)h}{\|h\|}\longrightarrow0.
$$
反例中候选映射是零映射；当 $h=(t,t)$ 时，分子恒为 $1$，分母趋于零，所以这个比值反而发散。失败的正是对所有趋近方向统一控制余项这一步。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a function is differentiable, its partial derivatives determine the candidate derivative matrix. But writing down that candidate does not prove that the remainder is negligible relative to $\|h\|$. In the counterexample the candidate is the zero map, while along $h=(t,t)$ the numerator stays one and the denominator tends to zero. Uniform control over all approaches is exactly what fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 连续偏导是常用充分条件，但不是定义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Continuous partial derivatives are a useful sufficient condition, not the definition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若各一阶偏导在 $a$ 的某个邻域存在，并且在 $a$ 连续，则 $f$ 在 $a$ 可微。这个 $C^1$ 判据把“分别计算偏导”升级成了对邻近点的一致控制，因而在实际计算中很好用。

逻辑方向必须保持清楚：

- 邻域内一阶偏导存在并在 $a$ 连续 $\Rightarrow$ $f$ 在 $a$ 可微；
- $f$ 在 $a$ 可微 $\Rightarrow$ $f$ 在 $a$ 连续，且各偏导存在；
- 各偏导只在 $a$ 存在 $\nRightarrow$ 连续或可微。

连续偏导只是充分条件，不是可微的定义，也不是必要条件；有些函数在一点可微，但偏导在该点附近并不连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the first partial derivatives exist in a neighbourhood of $a$ and are continuous at $a$, then the function is differentiable at $a$. This $C^1$ criterion is convenient and sufficient, but it is neither the definition nor a necessary condition. Differentiability implies continuity and the existence of partial derivatives; the existence of partial derivatives at the point alone implies neither.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检：哪一条是合法推论？
> 已知 $f_x(0,0)$ 和 $f_y(0,0)$ 都存在。下面哪一项可以立即推出：函数连续、函数可微、两个坐标方向的差商极限存在？还缺什么条件才能方便地推出可微？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If both coordinate partial derivatives exist at the origin, which conclusion follows immediately: continuity, differentiability, or the existence of the two coordinate difference-quotient limits? What common extra condition would conveniently imply differentiability?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 只能立即确认两条坐标方向的差商极限存在。若一阶偏导在原点的某个邻域存在并在原点连续，就可用常见充分条件推出可微。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Only the two coordinate difference-quotient limits are established. A standard sufficient condition is that the first partial derivatives exist in a neighbourhood and are continuous at the point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
