# Partial derivatives without differentiability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*偏导存在不保证连续或可微*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[偏导数|partial derivative]] measures what happens when one input changes while the other inputs stay fixed. For a function of two variables, testing the two coordinate directions at the origin examines only the two axes. It says nothing by itself about moving both coordinates together. A function can be completely flat on those axes and still jump immediately away from them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[偏导数]]测量的是只改变一个输入、保持其他输入不动时的变化。对二元函数，在原点检查两个坐标方向，只看了两条坐标轴；这些检查本身并没有说明两个坐标一起变化时会怎样。函数可以在两条轴上完全平坦，却在轴外立刻跳变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here functions are real-valued on an [[开集|open set]] $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, with $n\ge2$ a finite integer and $a\in U$. Openness allows small perturbations of either sign in every coordinate. All derivatives below are finite, two-sided derivatives at an interior point. A [[全微分|total differential]] requires one linear approximation for **every** sufficiently small perturbation, which is stronger than these separate coordinate tests.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡讨论[[开集]] $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 上的实值函数，其中 $n\ge2$ 为有限整数，$a\in U$。开性保证每个坐标都可以作正负两侧的小扰动。以下导数都指内点处有限的双侧导数。[[全微分]]要求同一个线性近似适用于**每一种**足够小的扰动，比逐个坐标检查更强。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Flat axes do not control points off the axes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反例：两条坐标轴都平静，轴外立即跳变

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define $g:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ by the following rule. The condition $xy=0$ means that at least one coordinate is zero, so the point lies on an axis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义 $g:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R$ 如下。条件 $xy=0$ 表示至少一个坐标为零，也就是点在某条坐标轴上。

$$
g(x,y)=\begin{cases}
0,&xy=0,\\
1,&xy\ne0.
\end{cases}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the origin $g(0,0)=0$. For every nonzero real $t$, both $g(t,0)$ and $g(0,t)$ equal zero. Thus the difference quotients themselves are zero, not just their limits:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原点处 $g(0,0)=0$。任取非零实数 $t$，都有 $g(t,0)=g(0,t)=0$。因此差商本身就为零，并不只是极限为零：

$$
g_x(0,0)=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{g(t,0)-g(0,0)}t=0,
\qquad
g_y(0,0)=\lim_{t\to0}\frac{g(0,t)-g(0,0)}t=0.
$$

> [!example] Change both coordinates together · 同时改变两个坐标
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For each positive integer $k$, the point $(1/k,1/k)$ is off both axes, so $g(1/k,1/k)=1$. These points approach $(0,0)$, but their outputs do not approach $g(0,0)=0$. This violates [[连续性|continuity]] at the origin: arbitrarily small input changes can leave an output error of $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每个正整数 $k$，点 $(1/k,1/k)$ 都不在两条轴上，所以 $g(1/k,1/k)=1$。这些点趋于 $(0,0)$，输出却不趋于 $g(0,0)=0$。因此原点处的[[连续性]]失败：任意小的输入变化，仍可能造成大小为 $1$ 的输出误差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A row of coefficients is only a candidate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 错误推理究竟错在哪里

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $h=(h_1,\ldots,h_n)$ for the input displacement from $a$. Its [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]] is $\|h\|_2=\sqrt{h_1^2+\cdots+h_n^2}$. Differentiability at $a$ means that there is a [[线性映射|linear map]] $L:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$, preserving addition and real scalar multiplication, such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把输入相对 $a$ 的位移写作 $h=(h_1,\ldots,h_n)$，其[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]为 $\|h\|_2=\sqrt{h_1^2+\cdots+h_n^2}$。在 $a$ 可微，是指存在保持加法与实数倍乘的[[线性映射]] $L:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$，使得

$$
f(a+h)-f(a)=L(h)+r(h),\qquad
\frac{|r(h)|}{\|h\|_2}\longrightarrow0\quad(h\to0,\ h\ne0).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The remainder $r(h)$ is the error after the linear prediction. The quotient compares that error with the size of the input change. It must become small for all small nonzero $h$, including displacements along curves. Testing $h=te_i$, where $e_i$ has a $1$ in coordinate $i$ and zeros elsewhere, forces $L(e_i)=\partial_i f(a)$. Consequently the **only possible** map is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

余项 $r(h)$ 是线性预测留下的误差；这个比值把误差与输入变化大小相比较。它必须对所有小的非零 $h$ 都变小，包括沿曲线取出的位移。令 $h=te_i$，其中 $e_i$ 的第 $i$ 个坐标为 $1$、其余为零，就必有 $L(e_i)=\partial_i f(a)$。因此，**唯一可能**的映射是

$$L(h)=\sum_{i=1}^n\partial_i f(a)h_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Computing those coefficients does not establish the remainder estimate. For $g$, both coefficients are zero, so the candidate is $L=0$. Along $h=(t,t)$ with $t\ne0$, the error is $1$ and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

算出这些系数，并没有证明余项估计。对 $g$，两个系数都为零，候选只能是 $L=0$。沿 $h=(t,t)$ 且 $t\ne0$ 时，误差为 $1$，于是

$$\frac{|g(t,t)-g(0,0)-L(t,t)|}{\|(t,t)\|_2}
=\frac1{\sqrt2\,|t|}\longrightarrow+\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
So $g$ is not differentiable. Its lack of continuity already rules this out as well: a fixed finite-dimensional linear map sends $h\to0$ to $L(h)\to0$, and the required remainder also tends to zero. Differentiability therefore implies continuity, whereas coordinate partials alone do not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以 $g$ 不可微。它不连续这一点本来也足以排除可微：有限维空间中的固定线性映射使 $h\to0$ 时 $L(h)\to0$，而所要求的余项也趋于零。因此可微推出连续，单有坐标偏导却不能这样推断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What continuity of nearby partial derivatives adds
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 连续偏导是常用充分条件，但不是定义

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful sufficient condition is that all first partial derivatives exist in a neighborhood of $a$ and are continuous **at $a$**. Then $f$ is differentiable at $a$. The added continuity controls how the coordinate rates change at nearby points; knowing their values only at $a$ provides no such control. In brief, change the coordinates one at a time and apply the [[拉格朗日中值定理|one-variable mean value theorem]] to each change. The resulting rates are evaluated at points tending to $a$, so their differences from $\partial_i f(a)$ tend to zero. Multiplying by $h_i$ and using $\sum_i|h_i|\le n\|h\|_2$ gives the required small remainder.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个常用充分条件是：各一阶偏导在 $a$ 的某个邻域存在，并且都**在 $a$ 连续**。这时 $f$ 在 $a$ 可微。新增的连续性控制了附近点的坐标变化率如何变化；只知道它们在 $a$ 的值，并没有这种控制。简要理由是逐个改变坐标，对每一步使用一元[[拉格朗日中值定理]]；得到的变化率都取在趋于 $a$ 的点上，所以它们与 $\partial_i f(a)$ 的差趋于零。再乘上对应的 $h_i$，并用 $\sum_i|h_i|\le n\|h\|_2$，便得到所需的小余项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If these partials are continuous throughout an open neighborhood, one has the stronger usual $C^1$ condition there. Neither that neighborhood condition nor continuity of the partials at the point is necessary for differentiability at just that point. For example, set $F(x,y)=x^2\sin(1/x)$ when $x\ne0$ and $F(0,y)=0$. Since $|\sin u|\le1$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若这些偏导在整个开邻域连续，就得到通常更强的邻域 $C^1$ 条件。但无论这个邻域条件，还是偏导在该点的连续性，都不是只在该点可微的必要条件。例如，令 $x\ne0$ 时 $F(x,y)=x^2\sin(1/x)$，并令 $F(0,y)=0$。由 $|\sin u|\le1$，

$$\frac{|F(x,y)-F(0,0)|}{\sqrt{x^2+y^2}}
\le\sqrt{x^2+y^2}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $F$ is differentiable at the origin with $L=0$. But the [[乘积法则|product rule]] and [[链式法则|chain rule]], together with the [[正弦余弦求导|sine derivative]] give $\partial_xF(x,y)=2x\sin(1/x)-\cos(1/x)$ for $x\ne0$, while $\partial_xF(0,0)=0$. At $x=1/(2\pi k)$, this nearby partial equals $-1$, so it is not continuous at the origin. This separates a helpful sufficient test from the definition itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 $F$ 在原点可微，且 $L=0$。但由[[乘积法则]]、[[链式法则]]和[[正弦余弦求导]]，$x\ne0$ 时有 $\partial_xF(x,y)=2x\sin(1/x)-\cos(1/x)$，而 $\partial_xF(0,0)=0$。在 $x=1/(2\pi k)$ 处，这个附近的偏导为 $-1$，因此它在原点不连续。这就把有用的充分判据与可微定义区分开了。

> [!question]- What have two partial derivatives established? · 两个偏导究竟建立了什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $f_x(0,0)$ and $f_y(0,0)$ exist, can you immediately claim continuity or differentiability? **No:** only the two coordinate difference-quotient limits are known. Nearby partial derivatives continuous at the origin are one sufficient route to differentiability. Even checking every straight direction requires care; see [[方向导数不可推可微|the directional-derivative boundary]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $f_x(0,0)$ 与 $f_y(0,0)$ 存在，能否立即断言连续或可微？**不能：**目前只知道两个坐标差商极限存在。邻域中偏导存在且在原点连续，是推出可微的一条充分路径。即使检查每条直线方向也仍需谨慎，见[[方向导数不可推可微]]。
