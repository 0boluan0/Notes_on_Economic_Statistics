# 项目投入是所用资源、产出是交付产品而结果是这些交付带来的变化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Project inputs are resources, outputs are delivered products, and outcomes are the changes those deliveries bring about.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

项目评价应分清三个层次：**投入**是使用的劳动、设备、土地等资源；**产出**是用资源交付的设施或服务；**结果**是设施或服务带来的变化。花完预算、建成设施与改善生活回答的是不同问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish resources used, facilities or services delivered, and the changes those deliveries produce. Spending a budget, completing a facility and improving lives answer different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如修建一座桥：工时、钢材和占地是投入；建成并可通行的桥是产出；出行时间、事故风险和两岸可达性的变化才是结果。桥建好了却接不上道路，产出可以完成，预期结果却可能没有实现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a bridge, labour, steel and land are inputs; a completed usable bridge is an output; changes in travel time, accident risk and accessibility are outcomes. A completed bridge without connecting roads can fail to deliver the expected outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，从产出走到结果需要一段可检查的因果解释：谁会使用、行为怎样改变、互补条件是否到位？设目标为“建桥”会过早锁定手段；若目标是“在给定安全标准下缩短两岸通勤”，还可以比较渡运改善或既有路网改造。这正是 [[公共项目反事实]] 和备选方案比较开始的地方。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Moving from output to outcome requires a testable causal explanation: who uses the service, what behaviour changes, and which complementary conditions hold? An accessibility objective allows alternatives to a bridge itself, connecting to [[公共项目反事实|counterfactual and option comparison]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结果指标也不自动等于项目造成的净效果。事故数下降可能同时受车辆安全改善影响；评价项目贡献仍需与合理基准比较。持续记录投入、交付和结果有助于发现哪一环出了问题，但单独的完成率不能替代因果识别或 [[成本—收益分析|社会价值评价]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An observed outcome is not automatically attributable to the project: safer vehicles could also reduce accidents. Monitoring each stage helps locate implementation problems, but completion rates do not replace causal identification or [[成本—收益分析|social value appraisal]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
