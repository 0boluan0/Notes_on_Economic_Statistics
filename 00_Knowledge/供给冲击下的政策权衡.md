# 供给冲击下的货币政策响应取决于冲击持续性、传导时滞与通胀产出的福利权衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The policy response to a supply shock depends on persistence, transmission lags and the welfare trade-off between inflation and activity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面对[[总供给冲击]]，不能只凭“通胀上升”或“冲击来自供给”决定加息或不加息。先判断相对价格冲击的持续时间、工资定价的二轮效应、实际收入对需求的冲击和政策生效时点，再说明希望稳定什么产出基准与通胀期限。货币政策也不能代替修复能源供给等更直接的政策。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[总供给冲击|supply shock]] does not determine the rate decision by its label alone. Assess duration, wage-price spillovers, real-income effects on demand and policy timing, then specify the output benchmark and inflation horizon. Monetary policy cannot substitute for direct remedies to energy supply or similar disruptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若冲击只暂时提高年度通胀、后续传导有限，政策作用却在冲击消退后达到高峰，急于抵销当期通胀可能造成随后低于目标；若二轮效应或预期漂移使压力持久，完全忽略冲击又可能不合适。实际收入损失压低需求时，进一步紧缩的必要程度也会变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If inflation effects are temporary and spillovers limited, a delayed policy response can overshoot after the shock fades. Persistent second-round effects or drifting expectations can instead make complete inaction inappropriate. Real-income losses that depress demand also alter the need for tightening.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个隔离权衡的静态教学模型，固定未来预期，令 $p=\pi-\pi^*$ 为通胀偏离、$x$ 为相对所选福利基准的产出缺口；$u>0$ 表示给定该基准后的成本推动楔子，而非把所有供给冲击直接代入同一数字。设：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To isolate the trade-off, hold future expectations fixed in a static teaching model. Let $p$ be inflation's deviation from target and $x$ the gap from the chosen welfare benchmark. The positive cost-push wedge $u$ is defined relative to that benchmark; it is not a generic numerical representation of every supply shock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$p=\kappa x+u,\qquad L=p^2+\lambda x^2,\qquad\kappa,\lambda>0.$$

代入后最小化，得到 $x^*=-\kappa u/(\kappa^2+\lambda)$、$p^*=\lambda u/(\kappa^2+\lambda)$。取 $\kappa=\lambda=1,u=2$，最优折中为 $x=-1,p=1,L=2$；完全稳定通胀需 $x=-2,L=4$，完全稳定产出则 $p=2,L=4$。这里求的是给定约束下的政策目标组合，不是计算应加息几个基点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Minimisation gives $x^*=-\kappa u/(\kappa^2+\lambda)$ and $p^*=\lambda u/(\kappa^2+\lambda)$. With both coefficients equal to 1 and $u=2$, the compromise is $x=-1,p=1$, with loss 2. Stabilising only inflation or only output gives loss 4. This chooses an outcome combination under stated constraints, not a basis-point rate change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

边界：技术或资源冲击也可能改变[[潜在产出]]，实际产出下降并不必然产生相同的福利缺口。在某些简化模型中稳定通胀与稳定适当产出缺口并不冲突；额外扭曲或二轮效应才改变权衡。上面的静态算例不能替代含时滞、预期与多部门的动态分析，也不能证明任何统一政策处方。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Technology or resource shocks can change [[潜在产出|potential output]], so lower actual output need not imply the same welfare gap. Some simplified models align inflation and appropriate output-gap stabilisation; additional distortions or spillovers change that result. The static example does not replace dynamic analysis with lags, expectations and multiple sectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
