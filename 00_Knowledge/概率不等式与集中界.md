# 概率不等式与集中界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Probability inequalities and concentration bounds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 不必知道随机变量的完整分布，只凭 nonnegativity、mean、variance、independence 或 boundedness 等有限信息，就能给“离均值很远”或“超过危险阈值”的概率上界。
> **具体锚点：** 1000 枚公平币的正面数 $S$ 均值 500。Markov 只利用非负与均值；Chebyshev 再利用 variance；Chernoff/Hoeffding 进一步利用独立与有界，因而把 polynomial tail 改进为 exponential tail。
> **核心难点：** 不等式的强弱来自假设，不来自公式看起来更高级。Markov 必须作用于 nonnegative variable；variance of a sum 必须保留 covariance；Chernoff/Hoeffding 不能把 pairwise independence 冒充 mutual independence。
> **为什么重要：** randomized algorithms、sampling error、load balancing、risk thresholds 与 large-deviation reasoning 经常只需要一个可靠上界，而不需要 exact distribution。
> **继续：** 用 [[#先按已知信息选择界]] 选工具，再看 Markov、Chebyshev 与 exponential-moment 路线；若随机量本身还是一个 event count，先到 [[指示变量与随机计数]] 建模。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Without knowing a full distribution, limited information such as non-negativity, a mean, a variance, independence, or boundedness can still upper-bound the probability of a large deviation or dangerous threshold exceedance.
> **Concrete anchor:** Let $S$ be the number of heads in 1,000 fair coin flips, with mean 500. Markov uses only non-negativity and the mean; Chebyshev also uses the variance; Chernoff and Hoeffding exploit independence and boundedness to replace polynomial decay by exponential decay.
> **Central difficulty:** A stronger inequality earns its strength from stronger assumptions, not from more sophisticated notation. Markov must be applied to a nonnegative variable, the variance of a sum must retain covariances, and pairwise independence cannot be substituted for the mutual independence required by Chernoff or Hoeffding.
> **Why it matters:** Randomised algorithms, sampling error, load balancing, risk thresholds, and large-deviation arguments often require a reliable upper bound rather than an exact distribution.
> **Continue with:** Use [[#先按已知信息选择界|the information-to-bound map]] to choose a tool, then study the Markov, Chebyshev, and exponential-moment routes. If the random quantity is itself an event count, first model it in [[指示变量与随机计数|indicator variables and random counts]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先按已知信息选择界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Choose a bound from the information available*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 已知信息 | 可直接使用 | 典型结论 | 没有得到什么 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $X\ge0$ 与 $E[X]$ | Markov | 单侧 upper tail，按 $1/a$ 衰减 | 不利用 variance 或 independence |
| $E[X]=\mu$、finite variance $\sigma^2$ | Chebyshev | 双侧 deviation，按 $1/t^2$ 衰减 | 不区分分布形状，常较松 |
| $S=\sum X_i$，$X_i$ mutually independent 且 $0\le X_i\le1$ | Chernoff | 相对均值的 exponential tail | 依赖结构或 unbounded terms 不自动适用 |
| independent mean-zero $X_i$ 且 $|X_i|\le a$ | symmetric Hoeffding | additive exponential tail | 不利用各变量更精细的 variance 或分布 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Information available | Direct tool | Typical conclusion | What it does not provide |
|---|---|---|---|
| $X\ge0$ and $E[X]$ | Markov | A one-sided upper tail decaying as $1/a$ | It uses neither variance nor independence |
| $E[X]=\mu$ and finite variance $\sigma^2$ | Chebyshev | A two-sided deviation bound decaying as $1/t^2$ | It ignores distributional shape and is often loose |
| $S=\sum X_i$, with mutually independent $X_i$ in $[0,1]$ | Chernoff | An exponential tail relative to the mean | It does not automatically survive dependence or unbounded terms |
| Independent mean-zero $X_i$ with $|X_i|\le a$ | Symmetric Hoeffding | An additive exponential tail | It does not exploit finer variances or full distributions |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把目标 event 写清楚，再选 bound。若结果大于 1，只能截为 1，说明信息太弱或阈值太近；若 exact distribution 易得，直接计算可能更好。不等式给的是保证，不声称真实概率接近上界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the target event explicitly before choosing a bound. If the numerical bound exceeds one, cap it at one; this means that the available information is too weak or the threshold too close. If the exact distribution is easy to obtain, direct calculation may be better. An inequality gives a guarantee, not a claim that the true probability is close to its upper bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Markov：把均值当作尾部质量预算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Markov: treating the mean as a budget for upper-tail mass*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $X\ge0$ almost surely 且 $a>0$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $X\ge0$ almost surely and $a>0$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(X\ge a)\le\frac{E[X]}a.
$$

证明是逐点不等式 $X\ge aI_{\{X\ge a\}}$：在 tail event 上左侧至少为 $a$，事件外右侧为 0；取期望后得到 $E[X]\ge aP(X\ge a)$。这个证明也解释 nonnegativity 为何不能省：若 $X$ 可大幅为负，负值能抵消均值，却不会消除正尾。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proof is the pointwise inequality $X\ge aI_{\{X\ge a\}}$: on the tail event, the left side is at least $a$, while outside it the right side is zero. Taking expectations gives $E[X]\ge aP(X\ge a)$. The proof also explains why non-negativity cannot be dropped. Large negative values can offset the mean without removing the positive tail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若知道 lower bound $X\ge b$，可对 $X-b\ge0$ 使用 Markov：对 $a>b$，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a lower bound $X\ge b$ is known, apply Markov to $X-b\ge0$. For $a>b$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(X\ge a)
=P(X-b\ge a-b)
\le\frac{E[X]-b}{a-b}.
$$

选择变换是 Markov 的核心，不是机械地把原变量代入。对 event count $N\ge0$，$P(N\ge1)\le E[N]$；对 squared deviation 应用 Markov，就得到 Chebyshev。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choosing the transformed variable is the heart of Markov's method; it is not a mechanical substitution of the original quantity. For an event count $N\ge0$, $P(N\ge1)\le E[N]$. Applying Markov to a squared deviation yields Chebyshev.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Chebyshev：用 variance 控制双侧偏离
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Chebyshev: controlling two-sided deviation with variance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $E[X]=\mu$ 且 $\operatorname{Var}(X)=\sigma^2<\infty$，则对 $t>0$，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $E[X]=\mu$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X)=\sigma^2<\infty$, then for $t>0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(|X-\mu|\ge t)\le\frac{\sigma^2}{t^2}.
$$

因为 $(X-\mu)^2\ge0$，且 $|X-\mu|\ge t$ 等价于 $(X-\mu)^2\ge t^2$，对平方变量用 Markov 即得。写成标准差单位就是 $P(|X-\mu|\ge k\sigma)\le1/k^2$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $(X-\mu)^2\ge0$ and $|X-\mu|\ge t$ is equivalent to $(X-\mu)^2\ge t^2$, Markov applied to the squared deviation gives the result. In standard-deviation units, $P(|X-\mu|\ge k\sigma)\le1/k^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若目标只是 $P(X-\mu\ge t)$，它包含在双侧 event 中，所以 Chebyshev 仍给合法上界，但可能浪费另一侧信息。更关键的是先算对 variance：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the target is only $P(X-\mu\ge t)$, it is contained in the two-sided event, so Chebyshev remains valid but may waste information about the other tail. More importantly, the variance must first be calculated correctly:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\operatorname{Var}\!\left(\sum_iX_i\right)
=\sum_i\operatorname{Var}(X_i)
+2\sum_{i<j}\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,X_j).
$$

pairwise independence 或 pairwise zero covariance 足以删掉交叉项；否则必须保留。general moment language 与 covariance 解释见 [[随机变量、分布与矩#期望、方差与协方差]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pairwise independence or pairwise zero covariance is enough to remove the cross terms; otherwise, they must remain. For the general language of moments and covariance, see [[随机变量、分布与矩#期望、方差与协方差|expectation, variance, and covariance]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Chernoff：对 exponential moment 使用 Markov
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Chernoff: applying Markov to an exponential moment*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $T_1,\ldots,T_n$ mutually independent，$0\le T_i\le1$，$T=\sum_iT_i$，$\mu=E[T]$。MIT 6.042J 给出的 multiplicative upper-tail form是：对 $c\ge1$，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $T_1,\ldots,T_n$ be mutually independent, with $0\le T_i\le1$, and write $T=\sum_iT_i$ and $\mu=E[T]$. The multiplicative upper-tail form used in MIT 6.042J states that for $c\ge1$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(T\ge c\mu)
\le \exp\!\left[-\bigl(c\ln c-c+1\bigr)\mu\right].
$$

令 $c=1+\delta$ 可把阈值写成超过均值的相对比例。证明的机制比常数形式更重要：对任意 $\lambda>0$，单调性把 $T\ge a$ 改写为 $e^{\lambda T}\ge e^{\lambda a}$；Markov 给
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Setting $c=1+\delta$ expresses the threshold as a relative excess over the mean. The proof mechanism matters more than the particular constant. For any $\lambda>0$, monotonicity rewrites $T\ge a$ as $e^{\lambda T}\ge e^{\lambda a}$, and Markov gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(T\ge a)
\le e^{-\lambda a}E[e^{\lambda T}]
=e^{-\lambda a}\prod_iE[e^{\lambda T_i}].
$$

最后的 product 正是 mutual independence 发挥作用的地方；再用 boundedness 控制每项 mgf，并优化 $\lambda$，得到 exponential decay。若只有 pairwise independence，这个多项乘积一般不能成立，不能沿用 Chernoff 结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The final product is exactly where mutual independence is used. Boundedness then controls each moment-generating function, and optimising $\lambda$ produces exponential decay. Pairwise independence alone does not generally justify this many-factor product and therefore does not justify the Chernoff conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Hoeffding：有界独立增量的 additive deviation
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Hoeffding: additive deviations of bounded independent increments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本文保留一个容易检查的 symmetric bounded version：若 $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ independent，$E[X_i]=0$ 且 $|X_i|\le a$ almost surely，则对 $t\ge0$，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We retain an easy-to-audit symmetric bounded version. If $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ are independent, $E[X_i]=0$, and $|X_i|\le a$ almost surely, then for $t\ge0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P\!\left(\left|\sum_{i=1}^nX_i\right|\ge t\right)
\le 2\exp\!\left(-\frac{t^2}{2na^2}\right).
$$

它同样用 exponential Markov，再用 boundedness 给 $E[e^{\lambda X_i}]$ 的 quadratic upper bound。与上面的 Chernoff form 相比，Hoeffding 更自然地表达 absolute/additive deviation；两者都要求 independence 与 support control，只是参数化和保留的信息不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It uses the same exponential-Markov idea, with boundedness supplying a quadratic upper bound on $E[e^{\lambda X_i}]$. Compared with the multiplicative Chernoff form above, Hoeffding naturally expresses an absolute or additive deviation. Both require independence and support control, but parameterise and retain information differently.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $Y_i\in[0,1]$ independent 且 $X_i=Y_i-E[Y_i]$，则 $|X_i|\le1$，对 sample mean $\bar Y$ 可直接得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If independent $Y_i$ lie in $[0,1]$ and $X_i=Y_i-E[Y_i]$, then $|X_i|\le1$. For the sample mean $\bar Y$, the displayed version gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(|\bar Y-E[\bar Y]|\ge\varepsilon)
\le2\exp\!\left(-\frac{n\varepsilon^2}{2}\right).
$$

这是从 $|X_i|\le1$ 直接得到的可靠版本，不是最尖锐的 $[0,1]$ interval-length constant。若结论对常数敏感，应使用与具体支持区间匹配的 general Hoeffding theorem，而不是把不同版本的指数常数混在一起。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a reliable consequence of the simple bound $|X_i|\le1$, not the sharpest interval-length constant for variables in $[0,1]$. When constants matter, use the general Hoeffding theorem matched to the actual support intervals rather than mixing constants from different versions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 完整例子：1000 枚公平币的右尾
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: the upper tail of 1,000 fair coins*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $S=\sum_{i=1}^{1000}I_i$，其中 $I_i$ 是 independent Bernoulli$(1/2)$。于是 $E[S]=500$、$\operatorname{Var}(S)=250$。我们要界 $P(S\ge600)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $S=\sum_{i=1}^{1000}I_i$, where the $I_i$ are independent Bernoulli$(1/2)$ variables. Then $E[S]=500$ and $\operatorname{Var}(S)=250$. We want to bound $P(S\ge600)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Markov 只用 $S\ge0$ 与 mean：$P(S\ge600)\le500/600=5/6$。
- Chebyshev 用 variance，并扩大为双侧 event：$P(S\ge600)\le P(|S-500|\ge100)\le250/100^2=0.025$。
- Chernoff 取 $c=1.2$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Markov uses only $S\ge0$ and its mean: $P(S\ge600)\le500/600=5/6$.
- Chebyshev uses the variance and enlarges the target to a two-sided event: $P(S\ge600)\le P(|S-500|\ge100)\le250/100^2=0.025$.
- Chernoff uses $c=1.2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P(S\ge600)
\le \exp[-(1.2\ln1.2-0.2)500]
\approx 8.34\times10^{-5}.
$$

三者都正确，但利用的信息逐步增加，所以保证显著变紧。若再用专门针对 bounded additive deviation 的 Hoeffding form，还可得到另一 exponential bound；这里的重点不是宣布某个公式永远最好，而是把每个数字与它实际使用的条件一一对应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All three bounds are valid, but each uses more information than the preceding one and therefore gives a much tighter guarantee. A Hoeffding form specialised to bounded additive deviations gives another exponential bound. The lesson is not that one formula is always best, but that every numerical guarantee must be paired with the assumptions it actually uses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断与选择流程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnostics and selection workflow*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 失败模式 | 为什么错 | 修复 |
|---|---|---|
| 对可取负值的 $X$ 直接用 Markov | proof 的 pointwise inequality 失效 | 找已知 lower bound 后平移，或换用 squared/exponential transform |
| Chebyshev 中把 threshold 写成 $t$，分母却忘记平方 | 它来自对 $(X-\mu)^2$ 的 Markov | 检查量纲：variance 与 $t^2$ 单位相同 |
| variance of sum 直接写成 variance sum | covariance 可能非零 | 证明 pairwise uncorrelated/independent，或显式保留 cross terms |
| pairwise independent 就套 Chernoff | mgf product 需要 joint factorisation | 核验 mutual independence 或使用适合依赖结构的界 |
| 变量无界却套 bounded Hoeffding | support 假设缺失 | 使用 mgf/sub-Gaussian 条件、截断，或换更合适的 inequality |
| 上界是 1.4 仍报告“概率 140%” | 概率最多为 1 | 报告 trivial bound 1，并承认现有信息不足 |
| 把 tail bound 当 exact probability | inequality 不描述 tightness | 有完整 distribution 时直接算，或说明 bound 的保守性 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Failure mode | Why it is wrong | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| Apply Markov directly to an $X$ that may be negative | The proof's pointwise inequality fails | Shift by a known lower bound, or use a squared or exponential transform |
| Write threshold $t$ in Chebyshev but forget to square the denominator | The inequality comes from Markov on $(X-\mu)^2$ | Check units: variance and $t^2$ have the same units |
| Replace the variance of a sum by the sum of variances immediately | Covariances may be nonzero | Prove pairwise lack of correlation or independence, or retain the cross terms |
| Apply Chernoff under pairwise independence | The mgf product needs joint factorisation | Verify mutual independence or use a bound designed for the dependence structure |
| Apply bounded Hoeffding to unbounded variables | The support assumption is missing | Use an mgf or sub-Gaussian condition, truncate, or choose another inequality |
| Report a bound of 1.4 as a “140% probability” | Probabilities cannot exceed one | Report the trivial bound one and acknowledge that the information is insufficient |
| Treat a tail bound as an exact probability | An inequality does not describe tightness | Calculate from the full distribution when available, or state the conservatism of the bound |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择顺序可以固定为：写 target event 与 threshold；列出已知的 support、mean、variance、independence 与 mgf 条件；选满足条件的最强简单 bound；检查 event inclusion 方向、量纲和结果是否超过 1；最后明确写“upper bound”而不是“probability equals”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stable selection order is: write the target event and threshold; list the known support, mean, variance, independence, and mgf conditions; choose the strongest simple bound whose assumptions hold; check event inclusion, units, and whether the result exceeds one; and finally label the result as an upper bound rather than an equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么更强的 concentration bound 必须附带更强假设？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why must a stronger concentration bound come with stronger assumptions?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 只知道 mean 时，概率质量可以被放在很远的尾部并由其他取值补偿；variance、boundedness 与 independence 逐步排除这些极端 joint distributions，所以才可能把 polynomial guarantee 收紧为 exponential guarantee。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With only a mean, probability mass can be placed far in the tail and compensated elsewhere. Variance, boundedness, and independence progressively rule out such extreme joint distributions, making exponential rather than merely polynomial guarantees possible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### $X$ 的均值为 10，但 $X$ 可取负值。能否直接断言 $P(X\ge100)\le0.1$？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If $E[X]=10$ but $X$ may be negative, can we directly assert $P(X\ge100)\le0.1$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不能。Markov 要求 nonnegative；负尾可以把均值压低却保留很大的正尾。若另知 $X\ge b$，可对 $X-b$ 使用 shifted Markov。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. Markov requires non-negativity; a negative tail can lower the mean while leaving a large positive tail. If a lower bound $X\ge b$ is known, shifted Markov may be applied to $X-b$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ 仅 pairwise independent，哪些步骤仍安全，哪些不自动安全？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ are only pairwise independent, which steps remain safe and which do not automatically follow?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 期望相加始终安全；有有限二阶矩时 pairwise independence 足以让 covariance 为 0，因此 variance 相加和 Chebyshev 可用。Chernoff/Hoeffding 的 mgf product 一般需要 mutual independence，不能自动使用。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Expectations always add. With finite second moments, pairwise independence makes covariances zero, so variances add and Chebyshev is available. The mgf product behind Chernoff and Hoeffding generally requires mutual independence and does not follow automatically.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 已知 $\mu=50,\sigma^2=25$，怎样界 $P(X\ge70)$？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Given $\mu=50$ and $\sigma^2=25$, how can $P(X\ge70)$ be bounded?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> $X\ge70$ 蕴含 $|X-50|\ge20$，所以 Chebyshev 给 $P(X\ge70)\le25/20^2=1/16$。这是把单侧 event 放进双侧 event 得到的合法、但可能偏松的界。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The event $X\ge70$ implies $|X-50|\ge20$, so Chebyshev gives $P(X\ge70)\le25/20^2=1/16$. This is a valid but potentially loose bound obtained by embedding a one-sided event in a two-sided one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一个计算得到 upper bound 为 1.3，应该怎样报告？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If a calculation produces an upper bound of 1.3, how should it be reported?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 概率上界截为 1；应报告“该 inequality 在这些信息下只给 trivial bound 1”，并考虑更远 threshold、更多 moment/support 信息或更适合的 bound。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Cap the probability bound at one. Report that the inequality provides only the trivial bound under the available information, then seek a farther threshold, additional moment or support information, or a more suitable bound.
