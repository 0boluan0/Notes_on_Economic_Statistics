# AD-AS长期调整须先固定冲击改变的曲线再求工资价格调整后的潜能交点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Long-run AD-AS adjustment first identifies which curves shift and then locates the potential-output intersection after wage and price adjustment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先说明[[总需求|AD]] 与[[长期总供给|LRAS]] 是否改变，再找短期 AD–SRAS 交点，最后在工资、成本和预期可调整的模型中求长期交点。不要把“产出回到潜能”直接套成“价格一定更高”；长期价格由**调整后的 AD 与 LRAS**共同决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First specify whether [[总需求|AD]] and [[长期总供给|LRAS]] change, then solve the short-run AD–SRAS intersection and the long-run equilibrium under wage, cost, and expectation adjustment. Returning to potential does not by itself imply a higher price; the final AD and LRAS determine it jointly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用教学线性模型比较两条路径。初始 $AD:P=200-Y$、$SRAS:P=Y$，潜能 $Y^*=100$，故 $A=(100,100)$。暂时成本冲击把 SRAS 改成 $P=Y+20$，得到 $B=(90,110)$；若冲击消退且 AD、潜能不变，回到 $A$，不是更高价格的长期点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a constructed linear model: initially $AD:P=200-Y$, $SRAS:P=Y$, and $Y^*=100$, giving $A=(100,100)$. A temporary cost shock changes SRAS to $P=Y+20$, giving $B=(90,110)$. If the shock reverses with demand and potential unchanged, equilibrium returns to A.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另从相同初始点出发，永久需求增加使 $AD:P=220-Y$，短期到 $B'=(110,110)$。若潜能仍为 100、工资成本随之调整，长期必须满足 $Y=100$ 和新 AD，故 $C=(100,120)$；与之相容的最终 SRAS 是 $P=Y+20$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Starting from the same initial point, a persistent demand increase to $AD:P=220-Y$ gives short-run $B'=(110,110)$. With potential still 100 and wages and costs adjusting, the new AD requires long-run $C=(100,120)$; $SRAS:P=Y+20$ is consistent with that endpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两例都回到原潜能，但只有第二例的 AD 永久改变。若冲击损坏生产能力、政策另作反应或调整不充分，必须重新设定曲线与条件；该方法不提供自动复原的日历期限，也不把一次价格跳升当持续通胀。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both examples return to initial potential, but only the second permanently shifts AD. Capacity damage, further policy responses, or incomplete adjustment require new assumptions. The method specifies neither a calendar deadline nor continuing inflation after a one-time price change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
