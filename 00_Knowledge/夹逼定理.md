# Squeeze theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*夹逼定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A complicated output can become predictable when it is trapped between two simpler outputs that approach the same number. For example, $\sin(1/x)$ keeps oscillating near zero, but multiplying it by $x$ confines the result between $-|x|$ and $|x|$. Both boundaries approach zero, leaving no other limiting value for the trapped function. This is the idea of the **squeeze theorem**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

复杂的输出若被两个趋于同一个数的简单输出夹住，就能确定它的趋势。例如 $\sin(1/x)$ 在零附近一直振荡，但乘上 $x$ 后，结果被限制在 $-|x|$ 与 $|x|$ 之间。两条边界都趋于零，被夹住的函数便没有别的极限选择。这就是**夹逼定理**的想法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The only limit concept needed is a [[函数极限|function limit]]: after any output tolerance is specified, all sufficiently close allowed inputs must meet it. In the theorem, the bounds and the middle function must be evaluated at the same inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

需要的极限概念是[[函数极限]]：指定任意输出容差后，所有足够近的合法输入都须满足它。在夹逼定理中，上下界与中间函数必须使用同一批输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The hypotheses of the theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 使用条件

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $g,f,h:D\to\mathbb R$, where $D$ is their common input domain. Let $a$ be a cluster point of $D$, so there are allowed non-target inputs arbitrarily close to $a$. Suppose that for some $r>0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $g,f,h:D\to\mathbb R$，$D$ 为共同输入定义域。令 $a$ 为 $D$ 的聚点，即有不同于目标点的合法输入能任意接近 $a$。假设存在 $r>0$，使

$$g(x)\le f(x)\le h(x)\qquad(x\in D,\ 0<|x-a|<r),$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
and that both bounding functions have the same finite limit $L$ along $D$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

并且上下界沿 $D$ 都有同一个有限极限 $L$。那么

> [!important] A common limit forces the middle limit · 共同极限确定中间极限
> $$\lim_{\substack{x\to a\\x\in D}}g(x)=L
> =\lim_{\substack{x\to a\\x\in D}}h(x)
> \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
> \lim_{\substack{x\to a\\x\in D}}f(x)=L.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No prior limit or continuity assumption on $f$ is required. The inequalities need only hold sufficiently near $a$, and no value at $a$ itself is needed. The upper and lower bounds need not vary monotonically.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不需要预先知道 $f$ 有极限，也不要求它连续。不等式只须在 $a$ 附近足够小的范围内成立，目标点本身无需有值；上下界也不必单调变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the inequalities imply convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么夹住就能推出收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose any requested error $\varepsilon>0$. The lower bound approaches $L$, so it is greater than $L-\varepsilon$ whenever $0<|x-a|<\delta_g$ for some $\delta_g>0$. The upper bound approaches $L$, so it is less than $L+\varepsilon$ whenever $0<|x-a|<\delta_h$ for some $\delta_h>0$. Take $\delta=\min\{r,\delta_g,\delta_h\}$. For every allowed input with $0<|x-a|<\delta$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任取误差要求 $\varepsilon>0$。下界趋于 $L$，所以存在 $\delta_g>0$，使 $0<|x-a|<\delta_g$ 时下界大于 $L-\varepsilon$；上界趋于 $L$，所以存在 $\delta_h>0$，使 $0<|x-a|<\delta_h$ 时上界小于 $L+\varepsilon$。取 $\delta=\min\{r,\delta_g,\delta_h\}$。对每个满足 $0<|x-a|<\delta$ 的合法输入，

$$L-\varepsilon<g(x)\le f(x)\le h(x)<L+\varepsilon.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $|f(x)-L|<\varepsilon$, which is exactly the limit requirement. Including $r$ in the minimum ensures that the trapping inequalities are valid wherever the argument uses them. The theorem proves both existence and the value of the middle limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

于是 $|f(x)-L|<\varepsilon$，恰好满足极限要求。最小值中包括 $r$，保证论证使用夹逼不等式时，它们确实成立。定理同时证明了中间极限的存在性和值。

## An oscillation with a shrinking amplitude
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x\ne0$, the bound $|\sin(1/x)|\le1$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $x\ne0$ 时，由 $|\sin(1/x)|\le1$ 得

$$|x\sin(1/x)|\le|x|,\qquad -|x|\le x\sin(1/x)\le |x|.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both bounding limits are zero, so $x\sin(1/x)\to0$. More directly, $0<|x|<\varepsilon$ already forces the output magnitude below $\varepsilon$. The absolute values handle negative inputs correctly; multiplying $-1\le\sin(1/x)\le1$ by a negative $x$ without reversing signs would not. This does not use the product limit law: $\sin(1/x)$ itself has no limit, so its limit cannot be multiplied by zero. See the explicit opposite-output sequences in [[零比零未定式|the zero-over-zero examples]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两条界的极限都是零，所以 $x\sin(1/x)\to0$。更直接地，$0<|x|<\varepsilon$ 已经保证输出绝对值小于 $\varepsilon$。使用绝对值才能正确处理负输入；若直接把 $-1\le\sin(1/x)\le1$ 乘以负数 $x$ 却不反向，就会出错。这里没有使用积的极限定律：$\sin(1/x)$ 本身没有极限，不能把它的极限拿来乘零。明确的两列不同输出见[[零比零未定式|零比零例子]]。

## The trigonometric application from the course
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*课程中的三角极限应用*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With angles in radians and $0<\theta<\pi/2$, unit-circle geometry gives $\sin\theta<\theta<\tan\theta$. Since all terms involved are positive, it follows that $\cos\theta<\sin\theta/\theta<1$. In particular, the course's looser upper bound is also valid:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

角度采用弧度，且 $0<\theta<\pi/2$ 时，单位圆几何给出 $\sin\theta<\theta<\tan\theta$。由于涉及的量为正，可以得到 $\cos\theta<\sin\theta/\theta<1$。特别地，课程中较松的上界也成立：

$$\cos\theta<\frac{\sin\theta}{\theta}<\frac1{\cos\theta}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once $\cos\theta\to1$ is established geometrically, both displayed bounds tend to $1$. Squeezing gives the right limit $1$; replacing $\theta$ by $-\theta$ leaves the ratio unchanged and supplies the left limit. The [[正弦基本极限|fundamental sine limit]] card explains the geometry and derives the cosine limit without using trigonometric derivatives. Here this is an application of an already proved squeeze theorem, not a premise of its proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在用几何确立 $\cos\theta\to1$ 后，所列两个界都趋于 $1$，夹逼得到右极限 $1$；把 $\theta$ 换成 $-\theta$ 不改变比值，便得到左极限。[[正弦基本极限]]卡片展开几何步骤，并在不使用三角导数的前提下推出余弦极限。这里是已经证明的夹逼定理的一项应用，不是它自身证明的前提。

## What weaker information cannot guarantee
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*较弱的信息为什么不够*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two bounds approaching different values do not determine a middle limit. For example, $1\le3/2+(1/2)\sin(1/x)\le2$ near zero, yet the middle expression has no limit: two input sequences make it equal to $1$ and $2$. A single upper bound is also insufficient for a finite conclusion: $-1\le0$ does not make the constant function $-1$ approach zero.

Even a narrowing gap is not enough if its location never settles. The functions $g(x)=\sin(1/x)$, $f(x)=g(x)$ and $h(x)=g(x)+|x|$ satisfy $g\le f\le h$ and $h-g\to0$, but $f$ still oscillates. The two bounds must have a common limit, not merely a difference approaching zero. Checking inequalities only at selected sample points is likewise insufficient when the original limit tests all nearby domain inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个界趋于不同值，不能确定中间极限。例如零附近有 $1\le3/2+(1/2)\sin(1/x)\le2$，但两列输入分别使中间表达式等于 $1$ 与 $2$，所以没有极限。只有一个上界也不足以得到有限结论：$-1\le0$ 并不使常值函数 $-1$ 趋于零。

即使夹缝变窄，位置若一直不稳定也不够。取 $g(x)=\sin(1/x)$、$f(x)=g(x)$、$h(x)=g(x)+|x|$，则 $g\le f\le h$ 且 $h-g\to0$，但 $f$ 仍在振荡。两个界必须有共同极限，不能仅仅是差趋于零。若原极限要检查所有附近定义域输入，只在选出的样本点检验不等式同样不足。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same argument works for a one-sided limit by restricting the domain, and at $+\infty$ by taking the largest of three input cutoffs instead of the smallest of three radii. Infinite output conclusions use threshold comparison: if $g(x)\le f(x)$ eventually and $g(x)\to+\infty$, then $f(x)\to+\infty$. This is why one lower bound can suffice for a positive infinite conclusion even though it cannot generally determine a finite limit. The relevant input/output meanings are in [[无穷极限与无穷远极限|infinite limits and limits at infinity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把定义域限制到一侧，证明同样适用于单侧极限；在 $+\infty$，把三个输入门槛取最大值即可，替代三个半径取最小值。输出无穷时使用门槛比较：若最终有 $g(x)\le f(x)$ 且 $g(x)\to+\infty$，那么 $f(x)\to+\infty$。这解释了为什么一条下界就能推出正无穷结论，却通常不能确定有限极限。输入与输出的具体含义见[[无穷极限与无穷远极限]]。
