# Convex and concave functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*函数的凹凸性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **convex function** lies at or below every chord joining two points on its graph. A chord is the straight segment between those graph points. A **concave function** lies at or above every such chord. The comparison asks how the function behaves between inputs, rather than whether its values are positive or whether it is increasing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**凸函数**的图像不高于连接任意两个图像点的弦；这里的弦就是两个图像点之间的直线段。**凹函数**的图像不低于每一条这样的弦。这是在比较函数在输入之间怎样变化，不是在判断函数值是否为正，也不是在判断它是否递增。

> [!example] A curve below its chord · 曲线位于弦下方
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^2$, choose inputs $-1$ and $3$. Their midpoint is $1$, where the curve has height $f(1)=1$. The chord's height halfway across is the average of the endpoint heights: $(f(-1)+f(3))/2=(1+9)/2=5$. The curve is lower: $1\le5$. A convexity proof must establish the analogous comparison for every pair and every position between them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $f(x)=x^2$，选择输入 $-1$、$3$。中点是 $1$，曲线在这里的高度为 $f(1)=1$；弦在一半位置的高度则是端点高度的平均：$(1+9)/2=5$。曲线更低，即 $1\le5$。证明凸性还需要对任意两点、两点之间的任意位置都作出这样的比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Compare the same mixture on both sides
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两边使用同一组权重*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f:U\to\mathbb R$ be a finite-valued function on a [[凸集|convex domain]] $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$. A convex domain contains the segment between any two inputs, so $z=(1-t)x+ty$ stays in the domain for $x,y\in U$ and $0\le t\le1$. The left side below evaluates the function at the mixed input; the right side mixes the two output values with exactly the same weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设有限值函数 $f:U\to\mathbb R$ 定义在[[凸集|凸定义域]] $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 上。凸定义域包含任意两输入之间的线段，所以对 $x,y\in U$、$0\le t\le1$，混合输入 $z=(1-t)x+ty$ 仍在定义域中。下面左边先混合输入再计算函数，右边则按完全相同的权重混合两个输出。

> [!important] Chord inequalities · 弦不等式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Convexity requires, for every such $x,y,t$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$f((1-t)x+ty)\le(1-t)f(x)+tf(y).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Concavity reverses this inequality. Equivalently, $f$ is concave when $-f$ is convex. The inputs may be vectors; the function values being compared are real numbers.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对所有这样的 $x,y,t$ 都满足上式，就是凸函数；反向不等式定义凹函数。等价地，$f$ 为凹函数当且仅当 $-f$ 为凸函数。输入可以是向量，比较的函数值则是实数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the square function, subtract the curve value from the chord value:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对平方函数，用弦的高度减去曲线高度：

$$(1-t)x^2+ty^2-\big((1-t)x+ty\big)^2=t(1-t)(x-y)^2\ge0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every factor on the right is nonnegative. This proves convexity on the whole real line, including the parts where $x^2$ decreases and increases. Negating the same calculation shows that $-x^2$ is concave.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

右边各因子均非负，因此证明了平方函数在整条实数轴上的凸性，包括它递减和递增的两部分。把同一计算取负，就证明 $-x^2$ 为凹函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Equality and corners are allowed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*允许等号，也允许尖角*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an affine function $f(x)=ax+b$ in one dimension, the function value always equals the chord height. Thus affine functions are both convex and concave. The multivariable form $f(x)=a^Tx+b$ has the same property. Convexity is a weak inequality; **strict convexity** additionally requires a strict inequality when $x\ne y$ and $0<t<1$. The square calculation shows why $x^2$ is strictly convex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一维仿射函数 $f(x)=ax+b$，函数值始终等于弦的高度，因此仿射函数既凸又凹。多元形式 $f(x)=a^Tx+b$ 也有同样的性质。凸性允许等号；**严格凸性**则进一步要求：当 $x\ne y$ 且 $0<t<1$ 时，不等式必须严格成立。平方函数的计算也说明了 $x^2$ 为什么严格凸。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No derivative appears in the definition. For example, $f(x)=|x|$ is convex because the triangle inequality gives $|(1-t)x+ty|\le(1-t)|x|+t|y|$, even though its graph has a corner at zero. It is not strictly convex on all of $\mathbb R$: between two positive inputs, its graph is a straight line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义中没有出现导数。例如 $f(x)=|x|$ 虽然在零点有尖角，仍因三角不等式 $|(1-t)x+ty|\le(1-t)|x|+t|y|$ 而为凸函数。它在整个 $\mathbb R$ 上并不严格凸：两个正输入之间的图像是一条直线。

> [!note] Calculus terminology · 微积分中的称呼
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the linked calculus course, **concave up** names the convex shape, and **concave down** names the concave shape. The chord inequalities above keep the direction precise across terminology. When derivatives are available, [[导数判凹凸|derivative criteria]] can test this property; they are not part of its definition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 所引微积分课程中，**concave up** 对应这里的凸形状，**concave down** 对应凹形状。不同术语下都可以用上面的弦不等式确认方向。导数存在时，可以用[[导数判凹凸]]检验这一性质；导数并不是定义的一部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same comparison explains why [[期望的Jensen不等式|Jensen's inequality]] orders transformation and averaging. Optimization uses the geometry for [[凸优化全局性|global optimality results]]; the [[凹性一阶判据|first-order concavity criterion]] and [[Hessian凹凸性判据|Hessian criterion]] provide separate differentiable formulations with their own assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样的比较解释了[[期望的Jensen不等式|Jensen不等式]]为什么能判断变换与平均的大小关系。优化利用这种几何得到[[凸优化全局性|全局最优性结论]]；[[凹性一阶判据]]与[[Hessian凹凸性判据]]则分别给出带有各自假设的可导形式。
