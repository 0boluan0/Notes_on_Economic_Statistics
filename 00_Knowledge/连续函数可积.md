# 有限闭区间上的连续函数必定黎曼可积
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Every continuous real-valued function on a finite closed interval is Riemann integrable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ 连续且 $a<b$ 有限，则[[定积分]] $\int_a^b f$ 存在。这里同时使用了“连续”和“闭区间”：$1/x$ 虽然在 $(0,1]$ 连续，却不满足本定理的条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A continuous real-valued function on a finite closed interval has a Riemann integral. Continuity and the closed domain both matter: $1/x$ is continuous on $(0,1]$, but that is not the hypothesis of this theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么越来越窄的矩形不会一直给出不同答案？闭区间上的连续函数有界，而且一致连续：给定高度容差 $\eta>0$，可以找到适用于整个区间的段宽限制，使同一小段内任意两个函数值之差小于 $\eta$。于是每段最高、最低矩形之差加起来至多是 $\eta(b-a)$。取 $\eta<\varepsilon/(b-a)$，全部采样和便被夹在相差不到 $\varepsilon$ 的上下和之间；这就是可积性的上下和判据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity on a compact interval gives boundedness and uniform continuity. A single mesh restriction makes the oscillation on every piece smaller than any chosen height tolerance. The total gap between upper and lower rectangles is then at most that tolerance times the interval length. It can be made arbitrarily small, establishing integrability by the upper–lower sum criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 $e^{-x^2}$ 在每个有限闭区间上的积分都存在，即使找不到初等公式形式的原函数；那是[[无初等原函数的积分]]处理的另一件事。本定理是充分条件，不声称可积函数处处连续。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently, $e^{-x^2}$ is integrable on every finite closed interval despite lacking an elementary antiderivative. [[无初等原函数的积分|Elementary expressibility]] is a separate issue. Continuity is sufficient, not necessary, for integrability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
