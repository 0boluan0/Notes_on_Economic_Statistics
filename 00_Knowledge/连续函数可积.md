# Riemann integrability of continuous functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*连续函数可积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[黎曼和|Riemann sum]] uses one sampled height on each small interval. Different samples usually give different finite answers. **Continuity on a finite closed interval guarantees that all sufficiently fine sampling choices converge to one common finite answer.** This is an existence theorem for the [[定积分|Riemann integral]]; we can establish that the integral exists before finding a formula for its value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[黎曼和]]在每个小区间选一个高度，不同取点通常会给出不同的有限近似值。**如果函数在有限闭区间上连续，那么所有充分细的分割和取点方式都会趋向同一个有限数。**这是[[定积分|黎曼积分]]的存在性定理：即使还没有算出积分值，也能先确定它确实存在。

> [!example] The gap between two rectangle estimates · 两种矩形估计之间的差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$, split at $1/2$. Using the smallest height on each half gives $(0+1/4)/2=1/8$; using the largest gives $(1/4+1)/2=5/8$. Every choice of one sample point in each half gives a sum between these numbers. With $n$ equal pieces, the gap between the upper and lower estimates is exactly $1/n$. The theorem explains why shrinking this uncertainty works for every continuous function, even one that is not increasing.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[0,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x^2$，在 $1/2$ 处分段。每半段取最低高度，得到 $(0+1/4)/2=1/8$；取最高高度，得到 $(1/4+1)/2=5/8$。每段任取一个采样点，所得和都夹在二者之间。等分为 $n$ 段时，上下估计之差恰好为 $1/n$。定理说明这种缩小不确定性的办法为什么适用于每个连续函数，即使它并不递增。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The interval and the continuity assumption
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*区间与连续性条件*

> [!important] Continuous integrability · 连续可积性
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $a<b$ be finite real numbers. If the real-valued function $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ is continuous at every point of $[a,b]$, then the finite Riemann integral $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$ exists.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $a<b$ 是有限实数。若实值函数 $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ 在 $[a,b]$ 的每一点连续，则有限的黎曼积分 $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$ 存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[一点连续|Continuity at a point]] means that nearby allowed inputs give outputs near the value at that point. At $a$ and $b$, “nearby” is taken **within $[a,b]$**: right-sided continuity at $a$ and left-sided continuity at $b$ suffice. We do not require an extension outside the interval or a derivative anywhere. For the degenerate interval $a=b$, the integral is zero by convention; reversed bounds are handled by changing the sign after integrating on the interval between them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[一点连续]]指附近的合法输入会给出接近该点函数值的输出。在端点 $a,b$，只从 **$[a,b]$ 内部**靠近：$a$ 处右连续、$b$ 处左连续即可。不要求函数延伸到区间外，也不要求任何地方可导。若 $a=b$，按约定积分为零；若上下限反向，则先在两端点之间积分，再取负号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why every fine sampling choice agrees
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么所有充分细的取点方式都会一致*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two consequences of continuity on this closed bounded interval control the rectangles. First, the [[极值定理|extreme value theorem]] gives finite largest and smallest values, so $f$ is bounded. Second, [[连续函数在紧致集上一致连续|there is one input-distance threshold that works throughout the interval]]: for every height tolerance $\eta>0$, some $\delta>0$ satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

闭有界区间上的连续性有两个结果，用来控制矩形。首先，[[极值定理]]保证函数取得有限的最大、最小值，因此 $f$ 有界。其次，[[连续函数在紧致集上一致连续|可以用同一个输入距离阈值控制整个区间]]：对每个高度容限 $\eta>0$，都存在 $\delta>0$，使

$$u,v\in[a,b],\quad |u-v|<\delta
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad |f(u)-f(v)|<\eta.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This property is called **[[一致连续|uniform continuity]]**. Unlike continuity at just one point, the chosen $\delta$ may depend on $\eta$ but not on the locations of $u,v$. Thus it controls every narrow piece simultaneously.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个性质叫作**[[一致连续]]**。与只在某一点连续不同，这里的 $\delta$ 可以依赖 $\eta$，但不能依赖 $u,v$ 位于哪里，因此能同时控制每个窄小区间。

> [!note]- Why one threshold exists on a closed bounded interval · 为什么闭有界区间上有统一阈值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Otherwise some fixed $\eta>0$ would allow pairs $u_n,v_n\in[a,b]$ with $|u_n-v_n|<1/n$ but $|f(u_n)-f(v_n)|\ge\eta$, for every positive integer $n$. By [[紧致性的序列判别|sequential compactness]] of a closed bounded interval, a subsequence $u_{n_k}$ converges to a point $c$ still in $[a,b]$. The corresponding $v_{n_k}$ also converges to $c$, because its distance from $u_{n_k}$ tends to zero. Continuity at $c$ makes both output sequences tend to $f(c)$, contradicting their fixed positive separation. Here a subsequence selects some terms in their original order.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 否则，就会存在一个固定的 $\eta>0$，对每个正整数 $n$ 都能找到 $u_n,v_n\in[a,b]$，满足 $|u_n-v_n|<1/n$，却有 $|f(u_n)-f(v_n)|\ge\eta$。由闭有界区间的[[紧致性的序列判别|序列紧致性]]，可以选出子列 $u_{n_k}$ 收敛到仍在 $[a,b]$ 内的点 $c$。对应的 $v_{n_k}$ 与它的距离趋于零，因此也趋于 $c$。在 $c$ 连续就使两列输出都趋于 $f(c)$，与固定的正间隔矛盾。这里的子列就是按原顺序选取的一部分项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now partition $[a,b]$ at $a=x_0<x_1<\cdots<x_n=b$, with every width $\Delta x_i=x_i-x_{i-1}$ smaller than $\delta$. On piece $i$, let $m_i$ be the smallest height of $f$ and $M_i$ the largest. These heights are attained because that piece is again a closed interval. Uniform continuity gives $M_i-m_i<\eta$. The lower and upper rectangle sums are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在用 $a=x_0<x_1<\cdots<x_n=b$ 分割区间，要求每个段宽 $\Delta x_i=x_i-x_{i-1}$ 都小于 $\delta$。在第 $i$ 段，把最低高度记为 $m_i$，最高高度记为 $M_i$。每段仍是闭区间，因此这两个高度确实取得。一致连续性给出 $M_i-m_i<\eta$。下矩形和与上矩形和分别为

$$L(P,f)=\sum_{i=1}^n m_i\Delta x_i,\qquad
U(P,f)=\sum_{i=1}^n M_i\Delta x_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The letter $P$ denotes the partition. Every sampled sum $S=\sum_i f(\xi_i)\Delta x_i$, with $\xi_i$ chosen anywhere in piece $i$, satisfies $L(P,f)\le S\le U(P,f)$. Moreover,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

字母 $P$ 代表这个分割。在每段内任取 $\xi_i$，所得采样和 $S=\sum_i f(\xi_i)\Delta x_i$ 都满足 $L(P,f)\le S\le U(P,f)$。而且，

$$U(P,f)-L(P,f)
=\sum_{i=1}^n(M_i-m_i)\Delta x_i
<\eta\sum_{i=1}^n\Delta x_i
=\eta(b-a).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given a total error tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, choose $\eta=\varepsilon/[2(b-a)]$. The upper–lower gap is then less than $\varepsilon/2$. The **upper–lower sum criterion** says that a bounded function is Riemann integrable when such gaps can be made arbitrarily small. Why does it identify one number? Every lower sum lies below every upper sum, even from a different partition: combine their partition points to compare both on a common refinement. The [[上确界|supremum]] of all lower sums and the infimum of all upper sums therefore enclose one interval of possible values; arbitrarily small gaps force its endpoints to coincide. That common value is the integral.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定总误差容限 $\varepsilon>0$，取 $\eta=\varepsilon/[2(b-a)]$，上下和之差就小于 $\varepsilon/2$。**上下和判据**说明：有界函数若能让这个差任意小，就黎曼可积。为什么会确定同一个数？任意一个下和都不超过任意一个上和，即使二者来自不同分割；合并分割点，在共同细分上比较即可。于是，所有下和的[[上确界]]与所有上和的下确界夹住可能的积分值；上下和之差能任意小，就迫使这两个界相同。这个共同值就是积分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the fine partitions used above, both the integral and every tagged sum lie between the same lower and upper estimates. Their difference is therefore less than $\varepsilon$. This recovers the all-partitions, all-tags meaning of integrability, not merely convergence of one convenient sequence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对上面这些充分细的分割，积分与每一种采样和都夹在同一对上下估计之间，因而相差小于 $\varepsilon$。这正好给出所有分割、所有采样方式的可积性含义，而不只是某一种方便序列收敛。

## What the guarantee does and does not require
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*这个保证覆盖哪些情况*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem applies to $|x|$ on $[-1,1]$ despite its corner, because continuity is enough; differentiability is unnecessary. It also applies to $e^{-x^2}$ on every finite closed interval. The latter is a standard example with [[无初等原函数的积分|no elementary antiderivative]]: integrability and elementary expressibility are different questions, and the non-elementarity claim requires separate theory.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$|x|$ 虽然在零点有尖角，在 $[-1,1]$ 上仍满足定理，因为连续就够了，不需要可导。$e^{-x^2}$ 在每个有限闭区间上也满足定理。后者是[[无初等原函数的积分|没有初等原函数]]的标准例子：可积性与初等表达式是不同问题，非初等性本身还需要另外的理论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity is sufficient, not necessary. For example, the step function that is $0$ on $[0,1/2)$ and $1$ on $[1/2,1]$ has integral $1/2$ despite its jump. Enclose the jump in an interval of arbitrarily small width; outside that interval the function is constant, so only that short piece contributes to the upper–lower gap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续是充分条件，不是必要条件。例如，在 $[0,1/2)$ 上为 $0$、在 $[1/2,1]$ 上为 $1$ 的阶跃函数虽然有跳跃，积分仍为 $1/2$。用任意窄的小区间包住跳跃点，外面各段都是常值，所以上下和之差只来自那个很短的小段。

> [!warning] Keep the whole finite closed interval · 保留完整的有限闭区间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The function $1/x$ is continuous on $(0,1]$, but it is unbounded near the missing endpoint and cannot become Riemann integrable on $[0,1]$ by assigning a value at zero. An infinite interval is also outside this theorem. Such cases require the separate limit question of [[反常积分|improper integration]]. The theorem itself gives no conclusion about convergence there.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $1/x$ 在 $(0,1]$ 上连续，但在缺失的端点附近无界；给零点随便补一个值，也不能让它在 $[0,1]$ 上黎曼可积。无穷区间同样不在本定理范围内。这些情形要另按[[反常积分]]讨论极限；本定理本身并不保证它们收敛。
