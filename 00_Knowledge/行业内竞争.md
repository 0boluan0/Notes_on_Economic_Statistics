# 行业内竞争是现有经营者争夺客户与价值的竞争行为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Industry rivalry is competition among existing operators for customers and value.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行业内竞争可以采取降价、改善质量、创新、广告、渠道或服务等形式。不同竞争方式对需求与成本的影响不同，不能把竞争只理解成价格战，也不能把所有竞争都等同于行业总利润必然下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rivalry may involve price cuts, quality, innovation, advertising, channels, or service. These forms affect demand and costs differently. Competition is not limited to price wars, nor does every competitive action necessarily reduce total industry profit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

需求增长缓慢、供给过剩、产品难以区分或[[退出壁垒]]较高时，争夺现有销量的压力可能更强；企业数量和规模只是其中的因素。[[集中度与竞争强度]]说明，少数企业仍可能在特定规则下激烈竞争。分析应追踪实际价格、折扣、产能与服务变化，而不是只数竞争者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Slow demand growth, excess capacity, weak differentiation, or high [[退出壁垒|exit barriers]] can intensify competition for existing sales. Firm count and size are only some of the drivers. [[集中度与竞争强度|Concentration versus competitive intensity]] shows how a few firms can compete aggressively under particular rules. Examine actual pricing, discounts, capacity, and service changes rather than merely counting rivals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
