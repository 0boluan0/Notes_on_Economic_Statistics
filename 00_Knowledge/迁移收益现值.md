# 比较迁移收益现值必须统一期限与成本口径并说明如何评价收入风险

判断迁移是否有吸引力，要在相同计划期限内比较“迁移”和“留在原地”的收益与成本，先把各期金额折算到同一时点，再说明如何评价风险。**一次性搬迁费不能未经换算就直接从每年工资中永久扣除**；第一年预期收入较低，也不等于整个迁移计划不合算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assess migration against staying over the same planning horizon, discounting comparable amounts to a common date and specifying the treatment of risk. A one-off moving expense cannot simply be deducted from annual wages forever. A first-year earnings shortfall does not determine the value of the entire plan.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先定义每一个金额和概率

下面是教材连续时间现值式的离散教学改写。一次性迁移费 $C_0$ 在现在支付；第 $t$ 期末城市现代岗位的整期收入为 $w_{M,t}$，未获该岗位时整期收入为 $w_{0,t}$，留在农村的可比收入为 $w_{A,t}$；$\Delta c_t$ 是迁移相对不迁移增加的当期生活等成本。这里将这些金额视为给定，收入以同一实际购买力计价，$r$ 为与一期长度一致的实际贴现率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a discrete teaching version of the textbook's continuous-time expression. $C_0$ is paid now. End-of-period amounts are earnings in a modern job, earnings without that job, comparable rural earnings, and additional recurring costs of moving rather than staying. Amounts are treated as given and measured in consistent real purchasing power; $r$ matches the period length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
V_0=-C_0+\sum_{t=1}^{T}
\frac{p_t w_{M,t}+(1-p_t)w_{0,t}-w_{A,t}-\Delta c_t}{(1+r)^t}.
$$

$p_t$ 必须与收入事件一致：若它表示“第 $t$ 期拥有整期岗位”的概率，可以乘整期收入；若只表示“年内某天找到工作”，就不能默认获得全年工资，应另算工作时长及收入路径。概率是潜在迁移者依据其信息形成的就业机会判断；库存比例或新增岗位比例的换算条件见[[城市就业概率换算]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The probability must match the income event. A probability of holding a job for the whole period can weight full-period earnings; merely finding a job at some point during a year does not guarantee a full year's pay. Probabilities reflect the migrant's information; see [[城市就业概率换算|employment-probability conversion]] before using stock or vacancy ratios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

机会成本不能重复扣除：公式已扣 $w_{A,t}$，就不要在 $\Delta c_t$ 中再次扣同一笔放弃的农村收入。搬迁费与每年较高的房租分别进入一次性项和当期增量项。若使用的收入已扣生活成本，也不再扣同一笔成本。非货币便利、家庭约束或心理成本不能在没有说明的情况下当作零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Avoid double-counting opportunity costs: rural earnings forgone are already subtracted. Moving expenses and additional annual rent belong in different terms. Costs already netted from income must not be deducted again, and unpriced amenities, family constraints or psychic costs cannot silently be set to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 现值为正是什么意义

在本例采用的期望货币收益最大化规则下，$V_0>0$ 表示迁移优于留乡；这需要把未列入的影响固定或明确排除，并有对所比较风险的适当评价条件。[[期望]]只是概率加权均值；将它直接作为偏好排序，一个明确的充分条件是对这些货币风险保持风险中性，参见[[期望净收益准则]]。有风险厌恶、借款限制或最低生存要求时，正的期望现值并不保证迁移是可行或偏好的选择，须考察消费路径、风险和资金约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the expected-monetary-payoff rule used here, positive $V_0$ favours moving, holding other consequences fixed or explicitly excluding them. [[期望|Expectation]] is only a probability-weighted mean. Risk neutrality over the compared monetary risks is one sufficient condition for this ranking, as explained in the [[期望净收益准则|expected-net-benefit criterion]]. Risk aversion, borrowing constraints or subsistence requirements may make a positive expected present value insufficient for feasibility or preference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设每年农村收入60、城市现代岗位收入120、未获该岗位收入0；一次性迁移费5、无额外当期成本，年贴现率10%。假设三年的整期岗位概率分别为 $0.2,0.8,0.8$。一期计划现值为 $-5-36/1.1\approx-37.73$；三期计划现值为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed annual example, rural earnings are 60, modern urban earnings 120, other earnings zero, the moving expense 5 and the discount rate 10%, with no additional recurring costs. Full-period job probabilities are 0.2, 0.8 and 0.8. A one-period plan has value about −37.73, whereas the three-period value is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$V_0=-5-\frac{36}{1.1}+\frac{36}{1.1^2}+\frac{36}{1.1^3}\approx19.07.$$

这只说明**同一收益路径的计划期限会影响判断**，不是已估计出真实迁移者的就业概率或融资能力。[[Harris–Todaro模型]]的同期预期收入均衡是把期限、成本与收益状态大幅简化后的另一层分析，不能无条件替代这个个体计划比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example shows that the planning horizon matters for the same earnings path; it does not estimate actual hiring chances or financing capacity. The contemporaneous equilibrium in the [[Harris–Todaro模型|Harris–Todaro model]] is a different, more simplified layer of analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
