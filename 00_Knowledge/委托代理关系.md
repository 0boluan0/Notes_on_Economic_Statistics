# 委托代理关系是委托人授权代理人为其提供服务并行使部分决策权的关系

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A principal–agent relationship exists when a principal engages an agent to perform a service on the principal's behalf and delegates some decision authority.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

委托人（principal）把一部分事务或决策交给代理人（agent）。例如公司出资者把日常经营交给经理人：经理人作决定，而这些决定会影响出资者的收益。这里是经济学上的授权分析，不直接判定某法域中的法定代理身份或全部受托义务。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The principal delegates some activity or decisions to an agent. For example, investors entrust day-to-day operations to managers whose decisions affect investor payoffs. This is an economic analysis of delegation, not a determination of legal agency status or every fiduciary duty in a particular jurisdiction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

授权本身不等于已经发生损害。代理问题出现于代理人的目标或利益与委托人不完全一致，而且行为或结果无法被无成本地观察、约定和执行时；此时仅要求“按委托人利益办事”未必提供充分激励。分析时先指出谁授权、谁决策、谁承担哪些收益，再检查具体的信息与执行限制。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Delegation does not itself establish harm. Agency problems arise when objectives or interests diverge and behavior or outcomes cannot be costlessly observed, contracted on, and enforced. A request to act in the principal's interest may then provide insufficient incentives. Identify delegation, decision authority, and payoff exposure before examining information and enforcement limits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
