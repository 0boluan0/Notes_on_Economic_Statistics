# 作业状态切换先用jobs确认目标再区分暂停后台继续与前台继续
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Identify a job with jobs before choosing stop, background continuation, or foreground continuation.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

适用于启用作业控制的交互 shell，且只操作自己已确认的作业：先用 `jobs -l` 看编号与状态；需要暂停当前前台程序时，通常按 `Ctrl-Z`；暂停后用 `bg %n` 继续后台执行，或用 `fg %n` 回到前台继续交互。`fg` 也可把运行中的后台作业移到前台。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an interactive shell with job control, act only on an identified job you own. Inspect its number and state with `jobs -l`; normally use `Ctrl-Z` to stop the current foreground program, `bg %n` to resume it in the background, or `fg %n` to resume foreground interaction. `fg` can also bring a running background job to the foreground.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

`Ctrl-Z` 通常产生 `SIGTSTP`，程序可以处理或忽略它，故操作后要再次查状态。`bg` 不会给需要终端输入的程序提供输入；它可能因 `SIGTTIN` 再次暂停，此时通常应回到前台，而不是不断重复 `bg`。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
`Ctrl-Z` normally generates `SIGTSTP`, which a program may handle or ignore, so check the state afterward. `bg` does not supply terminal input. A program may stop again with `SIGTTIN`, in which case foreground interaction is usually appropriate rather than repeatedly issuing `bg`.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

状态验证应区分“提示符回来了”“作业继续运行”“作业完成”三个结果。要等完成并取得状态，使用[[等待子进程]]；要终止则使用[[正常终止与强制终止]]的判断，不把暂停当作关闭。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish return of the prompt, continued execution, and completion. Use [[等待子进程|child waiting]] to collect completion status, and [[正常终止与强制终止|termination judgment]] to stop a task permanently rather than confusing suspension with shutdown.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
