# Cycle space and conserved flows
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*回路空间与守恒流*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Water or current can circulate around a network while no node gains or loses any amount overall. A **conserved flow**, or **circulation**, records that balance on every node. Its edge values are real numbers with signs: a negative value means motion opposite to the chosen reference arrow. All such flows together form the **real cycle space**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

水流或电流可以绕网络循环，同时任何节点都没有净增加或净减少。**守恒流**，也称**环流**，在每个节点记录这种平衡。各边流量是带符号的实数；负值表示逆着参考箭头流动。全部这样的流组成**实数域回路空间**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take a finite [[图类型的建模边界|undirected graph]], initially without self-loops; distinguish parallel edges if present. Let $n\ge1$ be its node count, $m\ge0$ its edge count and $c$ its number of [[无向图连通分量|connected components]], including isolated nodes. Use the real [[关联矩阵势差映射|incidence matrix]] $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$, with one row per edge: $-1$ at its reference tail and $+1$ at its head. The [[矩阵转置|transpose]] $A^T$ maps an edge-flow column $y=(y_1,\ldots,y_m)^T$ to node balances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑有限[[图类型的建模边界|无向图]]，先排除自环；若有平行边，则分别记录。令 $n\ge1$ 为节点数、$m\ge0$ 为边数、$c$ 为[[无向图连通分量|连通分量]]数，包括孤立节点。使用实[[关联矩阵势差映射|关联矩阵]] $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，每行对应一条边，参考起点为 $-1$、终点为 $+1$。其[[矩阵转置|转置]] $A^T$ 把边流量列 $y=(y_1,\ldots,y_m)^T$ 映为节点平衡量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specifically, $(A^Ty)_i$ is the total inflow minus outflow at node $i$. Consequently the cycle space is the [[左零空间|left nullspace]] of $A$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体地，$(A^Ty)_i$ 是节点 $i$ 的总流入减去总流出。因此，回路空间就是 $A$ 的[[左零空间]]：

$$\mathcal C=\{y\in\mathbb R^m:A^Ty=0\}=N(A^T).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $N$ denotes the [[零空间|nullspace]], the solution set of a homogeneous linear system. Adding two balanced flows or multiplying one by a real number preserves every node balance, so $\mathcal C$ is a vector space. This describes conservation without external injection; it does not impose capacities, nonnegative edge values, or a relation between current and voltage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $N$ 表示[[零空间]]，即齐次线性方程组的解集。两个平衡流相加，或把一个平衡流乘以实数，仍在每个节点平衡，所以 $\mathcal C$ 是向量空间。它描述无外部注入时的守恒，不附带容量限制、边流量非负限制，也不规定电流与电压之间的关系。

## One vector for a cycle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把沿环流动的一单位流量写成向量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Label the five edges $1\to2$, $2\to3$, $1\to3$, $1\to4$, $3\to4$ in that order. Then the four node balances are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

依次把五条边标为 $1\to2$、$2\to3$、$1\to3$、$1\to4$、$3\to4$。四个节点的平衡量为

$$A^Ty=
\begin{pmatrix}
-y_1-y_3-y_4\\
y_1-y_2\\
y_2+y_3-y_5\\
y_4+y_5
\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For instance, the second coordinate is $y_1-y_2$: edge $1$ enters node $2$ and edge $2$ leaves it. Traverse the triangle $1\to2\to3\to1$. The last step opposes the reference arrow on edge $3$, so this one-unit circulation is $q_1$ below. The triangle $1\to3\to4\to1$ similarly gives $q_2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，第二个分量为 $y_1-y_2$，因为边 $1$ 进入节点 $2$，边 $2$ 离开它。沿三角形 $1\to2\to3\to1$ 走一周，最后一步逆着边 $3$ 的参考箭头，因此一单位环流写成下面的 $q_1$。三角形 $1\to3\to4\to1$ 同样给出 $q_2$：

$$q_1=(1,1,-1,0,0)^T,\qquad q_2=(0,0,1,-1,1)^T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substitution makes every node balance zero. More generally, traverse any [[游走路径与环|undirected cycle]] once, using $+1$ on edges followed with their arrows, $-1$ against them, and zero elsewhere. Each visited node then has one unit in and one unit out. Reference arrows need not form a directed cycle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

代入后，每个节点的平衡量均为零。一般地，沿任意[[游走路径与环|无向环]]走一周，顺参考箭头的边取 $+1$，逆箭头的取 $-1$，其余取零；每个经过的节点都有一单位流入、一单位流出。参考箭头本身不必构成有向环。

> [!example] Three visible cycles, two independent flows · 三个可见环，两条独立环流
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The outer cycle is $q_1+q_2=(1,1,0,-1,1)^T$. The two contributions on their shared edge cancel. The outer cycle therefore supplies no third independent direction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 外圈向量为 $q_1+q_2=(1,1,0,-1,1)^T$，共用边上的两份流量抵消。因此，外圈并不提供第三个独立方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In fact, solving the displayed balances gives $y_1=y_2$, $y_4=-y_5$ and $y_3=-y_2+y_5$, while the remaining equation follows automatically. Thus every conserved flow is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直接求解所列平衡式，得到 $y_1=y_2$、$y_4=-y_5$、$y_3=-y_2+y_5$，剩下一个方程自动成立。因此全部守恒流为

$$y=\alpha q_1+\beta q_2=(\alpha,\alpha,-\alpha+\beta,-\beta,\beta)^T,
\qquad \alpha,\beta\in\mathbb R.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficients are uniquely read from edges $2$ and $5$, so $q_1,q_2$ are a [[基|basis]]. This verifies both independence and spanning, rather than merely counting the drawn cycles.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数可从边 $2$、$5$ 的流量唯一读出，所以 $q_1,q_2$ 是一组[[基]]。这同时核验了无关性与张成性，而不是仅凭图上画了几个环。

## The number of independent cycles
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从生成森林构造全部自由环流*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a [[关联矩阵与生成森林|spanning forest]] $F$: one tree in each original component, with $n-c$ edges. Every edge $e$ outside $F$ joins endpoints already connected by a unique path in $F$. Adding $e$ closes one cycle. Traverse it so that its coefficient on $e$ is $1$, and call the resulting flow $z_e$. This is a **fundamental cycle** relative to the chosen forest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选一片[[关联矩阵与生成森林|生成森林]] $F$，即在每个原分量里各取一棵树，共 $n-c$ 条边。任一森林外的边 $e$，其两端已有唯一森林路径相连；加入 $e$ 就闭合一个环。让环的行进方向使 $e$ 上的系数为 $1$，所得流量向量记作 $z_e$。这叫相对于所选森林的**基本环**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These vectors are independent: $z_e$ has coefficient $1$ on its own nonforest edge and zero on every other nonforest edge. To prove they span, start with any conserved flow $y$ and subtract
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些向量线性无关：$z_e$ 在自己对应的森林外边上为 $1$，在其他森林外边上均为零。证明张成性时，从任意守恒流 $y$ 中减去

$$\sum_{e\notin F}y_ez_e.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The remainder is still conserved, but now is supported entirely on forest edges. At a leaf, its sole incident flow must be zero; delete that edge and repeat. All remaining flows vanish. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

余量仍守恒，但现在只可能在森林边上非零。叶节点上唯一相接的边流量必须为零；删去该边并重复，所有剩余流量都为零。因此

$$y=\sum_{e\notin F}y_ez_e,\qquad
\dim\mathcal C=m-(n-c)=m-n+c.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This proves the basis claim directly. It also agrees with [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity]] applied to $A^T$: its domain has dimension $m$, and its rank is $n-c$ by [[行秩等于列秩|transpose rank equality]] and the incidence-rank theorem. In the example, $m=5,n=4,c=1$, so the dimension is $2$. Choosing $F=\{e_1,e_3,e_4\}$ makes $q_1$ and $q_2$ precisely the fundamental cycles associated with $e_2$ and $e_5$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这直接证明了基的结论，也与对 $A^T$ 使用[[秩零度定理]]一致：其定义域维数是 $m$，而由[[行秩等于列秩|转置不改变秩]]和关联矩阵秩定理，其秩为 $n-c$。本例 $m=5,n=4,c=1$，维数为 $2$；若选 $F=\{e_1,e_3,e_4\}$，那么 $q_1,q_2$ 恰好分别对应森林外边 $e_2,e_5$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A different spanning forest can give a different basis of the same space. If the graph itself is a forest, $m=n-c$ and the only conserved flow is zero. Adding an isolated node increases both $n$ and $c$ by one and changes no edge-flow freedom. These conclusions include disconnected and edgeless graphs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更换生成森林可能得到同一空间的另一组基。若原图本身就是森林，则 $m=n-c$，唯一守恒流为零。添加一个孤立节点使 $n,c$ 同时增加一，不改变边流量的自由度。这些结论也包含不连通图和无边图。

## What changes with signs, loops or external input
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*方向、自环与外部注入分别改变什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reversing an edge's reference arrow negates its incidence row and the coordinate representing the same physical flow. These two sign changes cancel in the node balances. Thus the coordinate description changes, but conservation and dimension do not. This real vector space uses signed addition; it is different from the binary cycle space over the two-element field, where edge selections are added modulo $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反转一条边的参考箭头，会把其关联矩阵行和描述同一实际流动的流量坐标同时变号。节点平衡式中两次变号相消。因此，坐标描述变了，守恒性质和维数不变。这里的实向量空间使用带符号的加法，不同于在二元域上把边选择按模 $2$ 相加的二进制回路空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two parallel edges with the same arrow have equal incidence rows, so $(1,-1)^T$ on those two edges is a circulation around their two-edge cycle. If self-loops are admitted, use the signed-incidence convention: their departure and arrival cancel at the same node, giving zero rows. Each such loop may carry an arbitrary circulation by itself, adds one independent flow coordinate, and is omitted from every spanning forest. With loops counted in $m$, the dimension remains $m-n+c$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同向的两条平行边具有相同关联行，因此在它们上取 $(1,-1)^T$ 就是沿二边环的环流。若允许自环，采用带符号关联约定：同一节点上的离开与到达相消，对应零行。每条自环可独自携带任意环流，增加一个独立流量坐标，并被排除在所有生成森林之外。只要 $m$ 包含自环数，维数仍为 $m-n+c$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, let $f_i$ denote external injection into node $i$, positive for input. No accumulation now means $A^Ty+f=0$, or $A^Ty=-f$. For the five-edge example, $y_0=(1,1,0,0,0)^T$ carries input at node $1$ to output at node $3$, with $f=(1,0,-1,0)^T$. If a particular solution $y_0$ exists, every solution is $y_0+z$ for $z\in\mathcal C$, by the [[特解加零空间|affine structure of a linear system's solution set]]. For $f\ne0$ this set does not contain zero and is not the cycle space. Solvability is a separate question; in particular, each connected component must have zero total external injection, because summing its node balances cancels both ends of every internal edge.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后，令 $f_i$ 表示节点 $i$ 的外部注入，输入为正。无积累此时要求 $A^Ty+f=0$，即 $A^Ty=-f$。对五边例，$y_0=(1,1,0,0,0)^T$ 把节点 $1$ 的输入送到节点 $3$ 输出，对应 $f=(1,0,-1,0)^T$。若已有特解 $y_0$，由[[特解加零空间|线性方程组解集的仿射结构]]，全部解为 $y_0+z$，其中 $z\in\mathcal C$。当 $f\ne0$ 时，该集合不含零，不是回路空间。是否可解是另一个问题；特别地，每个连通分量的外部注入总和必须为零，因为把其节点平衡式相加后，每条内部边的两端贡献相消。

The forest subtraction argument above proves spanning for every component.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Stanley 第11章，印刷203–212页／PDF162–171页：核对实环流、基本环、关联矩阵的核、维数和生成森林对应的基（定理11.3、11.8及推论11.7）。原书节点为行、边为列，对应这里的 $A^T$；其自环零列对应本条的零行。
- MIT 图论讲义第3–4页：核对节点守恒和五边例。本条把 $f$ 的正值定义为外部注入，因此使用 $A^Ty=-f$。
