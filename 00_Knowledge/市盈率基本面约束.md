# 稳定股利贴现模型中的市盈率由盈利口径、支付率、增长率和股权成本共同决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a stable dividend-discount model, P/E depends jointly on the earnings basis, payout, growth and cost of equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[戈登增长模型]]适用的稳定阶段，设基期每股收益 $EPS_0>0$，下一期 $EPS_1=EPS_0(1+g)$，固定[[股利支付率]]为 $0<d\le1$，股权成本为 $k_e$，且 $-1<g<k_e$。若股数及分配口径一致，$D_1=dEPS_1$，股利与EPS按同一长期可持续增长率 $g$ 增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a stable phase satisfying the [[戈登增长模型|Gordon model]], take positive base-period EPS, next-period EPS growing at $g$, a fixed [[股利支付率|payout ratio]] $0<d\le1$, and cost of equity $k_e$, with $-1<g<k_e$. Matched shares and distributions give $D_1=dEPS_1$; dividends and EPS share the same sustainable long-run growth rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $P_0=D_1/(k_e-g)$ 分别除以基期、下一期盈利：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Divide the dividend-discount value by base-period and next-period EPS respectively:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{P_0}{EPS_0}=\frac{d(1+g)}{k_e-g},\qquad
\frac{P_0}{EPS_1}=\frac{d}{k_e-g}.
$$

左式是本例的基期盈利倍数，右式是下一期预测倍数。实际报出的历史年度、TTM或forward [[市盈率]]必须注明期间；不能把左式的 $1+g$ 带进已经使用下一期盈利的右式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The left expression uses base-period earnings; the right uses next-period forecast earnings. Actual fiscal-year, TTM and forward [[市盈率|P/E ratios]] require explicit periods. The extra $1+g$ does not belong in the forward expression.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $EPS_0=2$、$g=4\%$、$d=60\%$、$k_e=10\%$，则 $EPS_1=2.08$、$D_1=1.248$、$P_0=20.8$；基期PE为10.4倍，预测PE为10倍。若同一现金流的股权成本变为12%，价值变为15.6，两个倍数分别为7.8和7.5；较低倍数可以只是较高风险要求的结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With base EPS 2, growth 4%, payout 60% and cost of equity 10%, next EPS is 2.08, dividend 1.248 and value 20.8. Base and forward P/E are 10.4 and 10. At a 12% cost of equity for the same cash flows, value is 15.6 and the multiples are 7.8 and 7.5. A lower multiple can reflect higher required compensation for risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这不是任意公司市盈率的恒等式。增长期长短、支付政策变化或不可持续盈利，需要相应多阶段现金流；固定其他条件时的比较，也不能把增长与支持它的再投资当作永远可以独立选择的输入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are conditional model results, not identities for every company's P/E. Changing growth duration, payout or earnings persistence requires the corresponding cash-flow model. A ceteris-paribus comparison does not make growth independent of the reinvestment needed to support it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
