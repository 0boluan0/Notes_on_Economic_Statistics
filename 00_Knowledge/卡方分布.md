# Chi-square distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*卡方分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **chi-square distribution** describes the total obtained by squaring and adding independent standard normal measurements. Squaring removes their signs, so positive and negative deviations both contribute to the total. The distribution tells us how that total varies across repeated possible measurements; in this construction, its parameter records how many independent standard normal terms contribute.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**卡方分布**描述的是：把相互独立的标准正态测量值平方，再加总后所得数值的分布。平方去掉正负号，因此正、负偏差都会贡献到总量中。分布描述这个总量在不同可能测量间怎样变化，在这个构造中，其参数记录有多少个独立标准正态项参与。

> [!example] Two independent standardized errors · 两个独立的标准化误差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose two measurement errors $Z_1,Z_2$ are independent and each has the [[正态分布|standard normal distribution]] $N(0,1)$: mean zero and variance one. Use $Q=Z_1^2+Z_2^2$ to measure their combined squared size. If their recorded values are $1$ and $-2$, the observed total is $1^2+(-2)^2=5$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设两个测量误差 $Z_1,Z_2$ 相互独立，且各自服从[[正态分布|标准正态分布]] $N(0,1)$，即均值为零、方差为一。用 $Q=Z_1^2+Z_2^2$ 衡量合计的平方大小；若本次记录为 $1$ 和 $-2$，观察到的总量为 $1^2+(-2)^2=5$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The random total has a chi-square distribution with **two degrees of freedom**. The value $5$ is one result, not its degrees of freedom. Across repetitions, the two errors and their total change, while the model parameter stays at two.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这个随机总量服从**自由度为二**的卡方分布。数值 $5$ 是某一次结果，并不是自由度；重复测量时，两个误差及总量会变化，模型参数仍为二。

## The integer-degree construction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*整数自由度的构造*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $k\ge1$ be an integer and let $Z_1,\ldots,Z_k$ be [[相互独立|mutually independent]] random variables, each with distribution $N(0,1)$. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $k\ge1$ 为整数，$Z_1,\ldots,Z_k$ 是[[相互独立]]、各自服从 $N(0,1)$ 的随机变量。定义

$$\boxed{Q=\sum_{i=1}^k Z_i^2\sim\chi_k^2.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $\sim$ means “has the distribution”; $\chi_k^2$ names the chi-square law with $k$ degrees of freedom. Here the degrees of freedom count independent standardized normal contributions. Merely writing $k$ squared terms does not establish that count if the terms repeat or depend on one another. In particular, $k=1$ gives $Q=Z^2$ for one standard normal variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

符号 $\sim$ 表示“服从这个分布”；$\chi_k^2$ 是自由度为 $k$ 的卡方分布名称。这里自由度数的是独立、标准化的正态贡献；如果各项重复或相互依赖，不能因为式子写了 $k$ 个平方，就认定自由度为 $k$。特别地，$k=1$ 时就是一个标准正态变量的平方 $Q=Z^2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The support is nonnegative: $Q$ cannot be below zero. For $k\ge1$, it is actually positive with probability one, while zero remains a boundary of its possible range. No single value, including zero, carries positive probability. A probability such as $P(Q>c)$ therefore concerns a range of totals, not the probability of obtaining one exact number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支撑位于非负范围，$Q$ 不可能小于零。$k\ge1$ 时，它以概率一严格为正，但零仍是可能范围的边界。包括零在内的任何单点都没有正概率。因此，$P(Q>c)$ 讨论的是一段总量范围，而不是恰好得到某个数的概率。

## Density, center and spread
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*密度、中心与分散程度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[概率密度函数|probability density]] converts an interval into probability by integration. For a possible value $q>0$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[概率密度函数]]通过对区间积分给出概率。对可能取值 $q>0$，

$$f_k(q)=\frac{q^{k/2-1}e^{-q/2}}{2^{k/2}\Gamma(k/2)};\qquad f_k(q)=0\quad(q<0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The function $\Gamma$ supplies the normalizing constant. For a positive real argument $a$, it is defined by $\Gamma(a)=\int_0^\infty t^{a-1}e^{-t}\,dt$; for example, $\Gamma(1)=1$. The density formula is stated on $q>0$ because its limiting value at zero depends on $k$ and can be infinite. A value assigned to the density at that single point does not change any probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

函数 $\Gamma$ 提供归一化常数；对正实数 $a$，定义为 $\Gamma(a)=\int_0^\infty t^{a-1}e^{-t}\,dt$，例如 $\Gamma(1)=1$。密度公式写在 $q>0$ 上，是因为零点附近的极限随 $k$ 而变，也可能无穷大；在单独一点上怎样指定密度，不影响任何概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $k=2$, the density simplifies to $f_2(q)=\tfrac12e^{-q/2}$ on $q>0$. Thus the probability that the two-error total exceeds $6$ is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$k=2$ 时，密度简化为 $q>0$ 上的 $f_2(q)=\tfrac12e^{-q/2}$。因此，两误差总量超过 $6$ 的概率为

$$P(Q>6)=\int_6^\infty\frac12e^{-q/2}\,dq=e^{-3}\approx0.0498.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[期望|mean]] and [[方差|variance]] are finite for every positive $k$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个正整数 $k$，[[期望|均值]]与[[方差]]都有限：

$$E[Q]=k,\qquad \operatorname{Var}(Q)=2k,\qquad
\operatorname{SD}(Q)=\sqrt{2k}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last expression is the [[标准差|standard deviation]]. The mean has a simple explanation: each standard normal square has expectation $E[Z_i^2]=1$, and expectations add. Integrating the density also gives $E[Q^2]=k(k+2)$, hence the variance is $k(k+2)-k^2=2k$. For $k=2$, the mean is $2$ and the standard deviation is $2$; the distribution is not symmetric around its mean, since it has a lower boundary at zero and extends arbitrarily far to the right.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一项是[[标准差]]。均值的解释很直接：每个标准正态平方的期望为 $E[Z_i^2]=1$，而期望可以相加。对密度积分还可得到 $E[Q^2]=k(k+2)$，所以方差为 $k(k+2)-k^2=2k$。$k=2$ 时，均值和标准差都为 $2$；分布并不以均值为中心对称，因为左侧有零这个边界，右侧却可以无限延伸。

## Adding independent chi-square variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*独立卡方变量相加*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $U\sim\chi_r^2$ and $V\sim\chi_s^2$ are independent, with positive integer degrees $r,s$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $U\sim\chi_r^2$ 与 $V\sim\chi_s^2$ 相互独立，且自由度 $r,s$ 为正整数，则

$$U+V\sim\chi_{r+s}^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two independent groups of normal squares can be combined into one group. Equivalently, the [[矩母函数|moment-generating function]], which records exponential averages, has the following form for a real argument $t$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两组独立的正态平方可以合并成一组。等价地，用记录指数平均值的[[矩母函数]]表示，对实数参数 $t$ 有：

$$M_Q(t)=E[e^{tQ}]=(1-2t)^{-k/2},\qquad t<\tfrac12.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Independence makes the MGF of $U+V$ the product $(1-2t)^{-r/2}(1-2t)^{-s/2}$, whose exponent is $-(r+s)/2$. The finite domain includes a neighborhood of zero, so this identifies the distribution. The condition matters: if $U=V=Z^2$ for one standard normal $Z$, then $U+V=2Z^2$ has variance $8$, whereas a $\chi_2^2$ variable has variance $4$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

独立性让 $U+V$ 的矩母函数成为乘积 $(1-2t)^{-r/2}(1-2t)^{-s/2}$，指数合为 $-(r+s)/2$。有限范围包含零的一个邻域，因此可以据此识别分布。独立条件很重要：若 $U=V=Z^2$ 共用同一个标准正态 $Z$，则 $U+V=2Z^2$ 的方差为 $8$，而 $\chi_2^2$ 的方差为 $4$。

> [!note]- Positive noninteger degrees of freedom · 正的非整数自由度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The density also defines a distribution when $k$ is replaced by any real $\nu>0$. This extension is the Gamma family with **shape $\nu/2$ and scale $2$**; in a shape–rate convention its rate is $1/2$. The scale is the denominator of $q$ in $e^{-q/2}$, while the rate is its coefficient. This is not a construction with a fractional number of normal variables.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把密度中的 $k$ 换成任意实数 $\nu>0$，也会定义一个分布。这个延拓属于 Gamma 分布族，**形状参数为 $\nu/2$、尺度参数为 $2$**；若使用形状—速率约定，速率是 $1/2$。尺度是 $e^{-q/2}$ 中 $q$ 的分母，速率则是它的系数。这不是把“分数个”正态变量平方相加。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The same formulas give mean $\nu$, variance $2\nu$ and MGF $(1-2t)^{-\nu/2}$ for $t<1/2$. Independent chi-square variables with positive real degrees also add their degrees, by the same MGF argument. Some applications separately define $\chi_0^2$ as a point mass at zero; it is not covered by the positive-degree density formula.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 同样的公式给出均值 $\nu$、方差 $2\nu$，以及 $t<1/2$ 时的矩母函数 $(1-2t)^{-\nu/2}$。正实数自由度的独立卡方变量也能相加自由度，证明仍是上述矩母函数乘法。有些应用另行约定 $\chi_0^2$ 为集中在零点的分布；它不包含在正自由度的密度公式中。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Squaring alone does not establish a chi-square law. A random sign taking $-1$ and $1$ equally often has mean zero and variance one, but its square is always $1$, not $\chi_1^2$. Even standardized variables need not be normal. For an actual normal variable $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ with $\sigma>0$, [[正态标准化|standardizing by the true mean and standard deviation]] gives $(X-\mu)/\sigma\sim N(0,1)$; independence between observations must still be checked before adding their squares. The [[Gaussian马氏平方律|Gaussian Mahalanobis chi-square law]] is a separate multivariate application with its own covariance and parameter conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仅仅平方不能确立卡方分布。等概率取 $-1$ 与 $1$ 的随机符号，均值为零、方差为一，但平方恒为 $1$，并非 $\chi_1^2$；标准化后的变量也未必正态。若确实有 $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$、$\sigma>0$，则[[正态标准化|用真实均值与标准差进行标准化]]给出 $(X-\mu)/\sigma\sim N(0,1)$；要把多个平方相加，仍须检查观测之间的独立性。[[Gaussian马氏平方律]]是另一个多元应用，另有协方差与参数使用条件。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [NIST/SEMATECH, Chi-Square Distribution](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda3666.htm): independent standard normal squares, density, nonnegative range, mean and standard deviation. The two-error value and its upper-tail probability are calculated directly here.
- [MIT 18.443, Lecture 1, PDF pp. 4–5](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-443-statistics-for-applications-spring-2015/d49600c6145f78b8e44f71319aeb2292_MIT18_443S15_LEC1.pdf#page=4): one-square and integer-sum construction, density and MGF. Its displayed Gamma density uses the rate convention; this card states both rate and scale explicitly.
- [NIST/SEMATECH, Gamma Distribution](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda366b.htm): positive real shape and positive scale parameterization. Setting location zero, shape $\nu/2$ and scale $2$ gives the positive-real-degree extension. The density integral verifies the MGF domain and moments; independent additivity follows from MGF multiplication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- NIST卡方条目支持独立标准正态平方和、密度、非负范围、均值和标准差；两误差数值及右尾概率直接计算。
- MIT第4–5页核对单个平方、整数个平方和、密度与矩母函数；其所列Gamma密度采用速率参数化，本文明确区分速率与尺度。
- NIST Gamma条目给出正实数形状及正尺度参数化；取位置零、形状 $\nu/2$、尺度 $2$ 得到正实自由度延拓。密度积分核对矩母函数范围及矩，独立可加性由矩母函数乘法得到。
