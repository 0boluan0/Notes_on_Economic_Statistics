# 债券久期、凸性与收益率曲线风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bond Duration, Convexity, and Yield-Curve Risk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把债券价格对收益率曲线小幅变化的一阶和二阶敏感度量化，并区分平行移动与不同期限节点的扭曲。
> **具体锚点：** 修正久期为 5 的债券在收益率上升 10bp 时，价格一阶近似下降 0.5%；较大变化还需凸性修正。
> **核心难点：** 久期是局部近似，普通修正久期隐含平行曲线和固定现金流；含期权债券的现金流会随利率改变。
> **为什么重要：** 到期日相同不代表风险相同，现金流时点、息票、曲线节点和可赎回性才决定真实价格敏感度。
> **继续：** 定价基础见 [[债券定价、收益率与信用利差]]；若管理的是银行整体资产负债而非单只债券，进入 [[银行账簿利率风险：重定价缺口与 Duration Gap]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It quantifies first- and second-order bond-price sensitivity to small yield-curve changes and distinguishes parallel shifts from twists at different maturity nodes.
> **Concrete anchor:** A bond with modified duration 5 has an approximately 0.5% price decline when yield rises 10 basis points; larger changes require a convexity correction.
> **Central difficulty:** Duration is a local approximation. Ordinary modified duration assumes a parallel curve and fixed cash flows, while an option-embedded bond's cash flows change with rates.
> **Why it matters:** Equal maturity dates do not imply equal risk; cash-flow timing, coupons, curve nodes, and callability determine actual price sensitivity.
> **Continue:** For pricing foundations, see [[债券定价、收益率与信用利差|Bond Pricing, Yields, and Credit Spreads]]. If the object is a bank's whole balance sheet rather than one bond, open [[银行账簿利率风险：重定价缺口与 Duration Gap|Interest-Rate Risk in the Banking Book: Repricing Gap and Duration Gap]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 收益曲线与风险类型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Yield-curve risk types*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利率风险包括平行移位、陡峭/扭曲、基差、期权和重定价风险。一个“市场利率”无法覆盖不同期限和参考利率。银行还区分短期净利息收入视角与长期经济价值视角。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interest-rate risk includes parallel shifts, steepening or twists, basis risk, optionality, and repricing risk. One “market rate” cannot cover different maturities and reference rates. Banks also distinguish short-run net-interest-income and long-run economic-value perspectives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对单只固定现金流债券，先用久期近似平行变化，再用 key-rate duration 分解非平行变化；基差来自头寸和对冲工具参考曲线不完全相同；期权风险则要求现金流随曲线重新生成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one fixed-cash-flow bond, begin with duration for a parallel shift and then decompose nonparallel movements with key-rate durations. Basis risk arises when the position and hedge reference different curves. Optionality requires regenerating cash flows as the curve changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Macaulay 与修正久期
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Macaulay and modified duration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Macaulay duration 是现金流现值时点的加权平均；modified duration $D_{mod}=D_{Mac}/(1+y/m)$ 近似 $-\Delta P/P\approx D_{mod}\Delta y$。公式假设小且近似平行的收益率变化，YTM 口径需一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Macaulay duration is the present-value-weighted average timing of cash flows; modified duration $D_{mod}=D_{Mac}/(1+y/m)$ gives the approximation $-\Delta P/P\approx D_{mod}\Delta y$. The formula assumes a small, approximately parallel yield change, and the YTM convention must be consistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

面值 100、两年到期的零息债在年复利收益率 5% 时，Macaulay duration 为 2 年，修正久期为 $2/1.05\approx1.9048$。收益率升 10bp 时，一阶近似价格下降约 $1.9048\times0.001=0.1905\%$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero-coupon bond with face value 100, two years to maturity, and annual-compounded yield of 5% has Macaulay duration 2 years and modified duration $2/1.05\approx1.9048$. A 10-basis-point yield increase gives a first-order price decline of about $1.9048\times0.001=0.1905\%$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## DV01 与凸性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*DV01 and convexity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

DV01 是收益率上升 1bp 的价值变化绝对量近似。凸性加入二阶修正 $\Delta P/P\approx-D_{mod}\Delta y+\frac12C(\Delta y)^2$。普通无期权债券多为正凸，callable 债可出现负凸。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DV01 approximates the absolute value change for a one-basis-point yield increase. Convexity adds the second-order correction $\Delta P/P\approx-D_{mod}\Delta y+\frac12C(\Delta y)^2$. Ordinary option-free bonds usually have positive convexity, while callable bonds can exhibit negative convexity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若组合市值 10,000 万、修正久期 5，则 DV01 近似为 $10{,}000\times5\times0.0001=5$ 万。这个数字的符号通常按内部约定报告；必须说明是“利率升 1bp 的损益”还是“绝对敏感度”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If portfolio market value is 100 million and modified duration is 5, approximate DV01 is $100\text{ million}\times5\times0.0001=50{,}000$. Sign conventions vary internally; state whether the number means P&L for a one-basis-point rate rise or an absolute sensitivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 有效久期与 key-rate duration
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Effective duration and key-rate duration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

含期权产品现金流随利率变化，需用模型重估上下利率情景得到 effective duration。key-rate duration 分解曲线上特定期限节点敏感度，能捕捉非平行变动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For products whose cash flows change with rates because of embedded options, obtain effective duration by model revaluation under up and down rate shocks. Key-rate duration decomposes sensitivity at selected curve maturities and captures nonparallel moves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有效久期近似 $D_{eff}=(P_- - P_+)/(2P_0\Delta y)$，其中 $P_-$ 和 $P_+$ 都必须在新利率下重新估计现金流与期权行使。若只折现原现金流，算出的仍是静态价格敏感度而非真正有效久期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Effective duration is approximately $D_{eff}=(P_- - P_+)/(2P_0\Delta y)$, where both $P_-$ and $P_+$ must regenerate cash flows and option exercise under the new rates. Discounting the original cash flows only still gives a static price sensitivity rather than true effective duration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 用到期期限代替久期，忽略息票和中间现金流。
- 用一个 YTM 平移所有期限，处理明显扭曲的收益率曲线。
- 对可赎回债使用固定现金流修正久期，漏掉负凸性与提前赎回。
- DV01 数值不写币种、组合市值、利率方向和 1bp 单位，无法聚合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Using maturity instead of duration ignores coupons and intermediate cash flows.
- Shifting one YTM across every maturity to analyze an obviously twisted yield curve.
- Using fixed-cash-flow modified duration for a callable bond omits negative convexity and early exercise.
- Reporting DV01 without currency, portfolio value, rate direction, and the one-basis-point unit makes aggregation impossible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Macaulay duration 与 modified duration 分别是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What are Macaulay duration and modified duration respectively?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 前者是现值加权平均现金流时点，后者把它转为价格对收益率的小幅百分比敏感度。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The former is the present-value-weighted average timing of cash flows; the latter converts it into small percentage price sensitivity to yield.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么相同到期日债券久期可不同？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can bonds with the same maturity have different duration?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 息票大小和现金流时点不同；更早收到现金的债券有效暴露期限更短。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Coupon size and cash-flow timing differ; a bond that returns more cash earlier has a shorter effective exposure horizon.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么凸性在大幅利率变化时更重要？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why does convexity matter more for large rate moves?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 久期是一条切线，误差随离开当前点而扩大；凸性加入价格—收益率曲线的弯曲，二阶项与利率变化平方成比例。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Duration is a tangent, so its error grows away from the current point. Convexity adds curvature in the price–yield relation, and the second-order term grows with the square of the rate change.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
