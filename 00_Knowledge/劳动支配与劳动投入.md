# 商品能够支配的劳动量与生产它所需的劳动量是两个不同的计量对象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The labour a commodity can command and the labour required to produce it measure different things*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**劳动支配量（labour commanded）**问“把商品卖掉，能购买多少别人的劳动”；**劳动投入量（labour embodied）**问“生产这件商品需要多少劳动”。前者从商品的[[Smith的价值区分|交换能力]]出发，后者从生产条件出发。即使都用工时表示，也不能因此当作同一个数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Labour commanded** asks how much of other people's labour the proceeds from a commodity can purchase. **Labour embodied** asks how much labour its production requires. The former starts from [[Smith的价值区分|purchasing power in exchange]], the latter from production conditions. Expressing both in hours does not make them the same quantity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个现代记号帮助区分：若商品价格为 $p$，一种同质劳动的每工时工资为 $w>0$，卖出一件商品能够购买的劳动量就是 $p/w$；把生产所需劳动另记为 $a$。例如，售价为 12 元、工资为每工时 3 元，就能购买 4 工时劳动，但这些信息并没有告诉我们 $a$ 是多少。这只是计量口径的示例，不是 Smith 写出的定价方程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Modern notation makes the distinction transparent. If a commodity sells for $p$ and one homogeneous kind of labour costs $w>0$ per hour, its proceeds purchase $p/w$ hours. Denote the labour required for production separately by $a$. A price of 12 and an hourly wage of 3 imply four hours of labour commanded, but do not identify $a$. This is a measurement illustration, not a pricing equation written by Smith.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Smith 在 I.V 讨论的是劳动支配量作为交换价值的尺度；在 I.VI 开头，用海狸和鹿的生产劳动解释交换比例时，却明确限定为**资本积累和土地私有以前**的早期社会，且劳动的艰苦程度与技能差别仍需折算。进入资本和土地有独立所有者的社会后，他又把利润、地租引入价格构成。因此，不能把 $p/w=a$ 当成他对所有商业社会提出的恒等式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In I.V, Smith discusses labour commanded as a measure of exchangeable value. At the start of I.VI, however, his beaver–deer example relates exchange proportions to labour required in an early society **before the accumulation of stock and appropriation of land**, with allowances for hardship and skill. He then introduces profit and rent once capital and land have separate owners. The equality $p/w=a$ is therefore not a universal identity he establishes for commercial society.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一辨析也让[[Smith自然价格|自然价格]]更容易理解：讨论商品应能维持哪些通常报酬，与把商品的交换能力换算成劳动单位，是两个相连但不同的问题。Smith 提出劳动可作为不变价值尺度，是他的理论主张；实际计算时仍须交代工资、技能和劳动质量，不能把不同人的一小时直接视为同质单位。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This distinction also clarifies [[Smith自然价格|natural price]]. Asking which ordinary returns a commodity must sustain differs from expressing its purchasing power in units of labour. Smith's claim that labour provides an invariant measure is his theoretical position. Actual measurement still requires specifying wages, skill, and labour quality rather than treating everyone's hour as homogeneous.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Smith，《国富论》I.V.1–4、7–9](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=8)：核对劳动支配量、不同劳动难以准确折算，以及劳动尺度和货币工资的原文口径。
- [Smith，《国富论》I.VI.1–9](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=9)：核对早期社会的限定、技能与艰苦补偿，以及资本积累和土地私有后利润、地租进入价格构成。$12/3=4$ 为本卡的计量示例，不据此推定生产劳动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Smith, *The Wealth of Nations*, I.V.1–4 and 7–9](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=8): labour commanded, difficulties in comparing different kinds of labour, and the distinction between labour as a standard and money wages.
- [Smith, *The Wealth of Nations*, I.VI.1–9](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=9): the early-society qualification, allowances for skill and hardship, and the introduction of profit and rent. The calculation $12/3=4$ illustrates measurement only; it does not infer production labour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
