# 时间序列干预系数只有在干预时点相对反事实冲击外生时才可作因果解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A time-series intervention coefficient is causal only when intervention timing is exogenous to counterfactual shocks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 在含干预函数 $D_t$ 的动态回归中，系数首先描述“给定模型和其他变量时，$D_t$ 所编码路径与 $y_t$ 的条件差异”。要把它解释为干预的反事实效应，还要相信：若干预没有发生，模型确实能给出同一时期的可信反事实，并且干预时点不会因模型未观测到的未来冲击而被选择。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An intervention coefficient first describes a conditional difference associated with the encoded intervention path. A causal interpretation additionally requires a credible no-intervention counterfactual and intervention timing that is not selected in response to unmodelled future shocks.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个常见破坏机制是：政策因预期 $y$ 即将恶化而启动；同一日期还有其他制度或测量变化；行为主体在正式日期前已经预期并调整。它们分别造成时间选择内生、共同冲击和 anticipation。加入更高阶 ARIMA 误差只能描述残余相关，不能让这些未观测反事实条件自动成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common failures include policy adoption in anticipation of a worsening outcome, coincident reforms or measurement changes, and behavioural adjustment before the official date. Higher-order ARIMA errors can represent residual dependence but cannot manufacture exogenous timing or remove omitted concurrent shocks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程 transfer-function 规格还隐含 no-feedback 条件：$y$ 的过去或预期不能反过来决定 $D$ 或随机输入 $x$。若存在反馈，模型仍可报告给定路径下的条件预测；但把输入称为外生冲击，必须另给制度、设计或工具变量依据。统计显著只说明在所设模型中该系数相对估计不确定性不小，不等于识别条件已经验证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Transfer-function models also rely on a no-feedback condition when the input is treated as exogenous. With feedback, conditional forecasting may remain useful, but structural shock language requires additional institutional, design, or instrumental evidence. Statistical significance does not verify those assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 预警触发政策
> 若政府只在内部预测显示失业将上升时推出补贴，政策阶跃与未公开的预期冲击相关。即使补贴系数显著、创新近似白噪声，也不能仅凭该时间序列回归认定补贴造成了观察到的变化。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a subsidy is introduced only when private forecasts predict rising unemployment, its timing is related to an unobserved expected shock. A significant coefficient and white-looking innovations do not by themselves identify the subsidy's causal effect.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> ARIMA 残差诊断全部通过，是否足以把 step 系数称为政策因果效应？
>
> **答案：** 不足。诊断针对剩余序列结构；因果解释还取决于反事实、干预时点外生性、同期事件和预期效应。
