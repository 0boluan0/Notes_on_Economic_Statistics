# Subspace topology
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*子空间拓扑*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **subspace topology** describes closeness and openness when the allowed points are restricted to a subset. If the world under consideration is $Y=[0,1]$, a small neighbourhood of $0$ only includes points of $Y$. There is no requirement to include negative numbers that are outside this world. This is why $[0,1/2)$ can be open within $Y$, even though it is not open in the full real line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**子空间拓扑**描述把可用的点限制在某个子集后，怎样理解接近与开性。如果当前研究的世界是 $Y=[0,1]$，那么 $0$ 的小邻域只需考虑 $Y$ 内的点，无须包含这个世界之外的负数。因此，$[0,1/2)$ 在 $Y$ 内可以是开集，虽然它在整个实线中不是开集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with a [[拓扑|topological space]] $(X,\mathcal T)$, where $\mathcal T$ is its collection of [[开集|open sets]], and choose any subset $Y\subseteq X$. The symbol $\cap$ means intersection: keep only points belonging to both sets. Intersecting each ambient open set with $Y$ gives its relative open sets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从[[拓扑|拓扑空间]] $(X,\mathcal T)$ 出发，$\mathcal T$ 是它的[[开集]]族，再选任意子集 $Y\subseteq X$。符号 $\cap$ 表示交集，即只留下同时属于两边的点。把每个环境开集与 $Y$ 取交，就得到相对开集。

> [!abstract] Restrict the open sets · 限制开集
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The induced, or relative, topology on $Y$ is
> $$\mathcal T_Y=\{Y\cap U:U\in\mathcal T\}.$$
> Thus $A\subseteq Y$ is open in $Y$ exactly when $A=Y\cap U$ for some open $U$ of $X$. The ambient $U$ need not be unique. Here “subspace” means a subset with this topology; it need not be a linear subspace or be closed in $X$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上式给出 $Y$ 上的诱导拓扑，也称相对拓扑。$A\subseteq Y$ 在 $Y$ 中开，当且仅当存在 $X$ 的开集 $U$ 使 $A=Y\cap U$。这样的环境 $U$ 未必唯一。“子空间”在这里指带此拓扑的子集，不要求它是线性子空间，也不要求它在 $X$ 中闭。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $Y=[0,1]\subseteq\mathbb R$, take $U=(-1,1/2)$. Then $Y\cap U=[0,1/2)$, proving the relative openness claimed above. The entire set $Y$ is open in itself because $Y=Y\cap\mathbb R$. It is also closed in itself because its relative complement is empty. In the ambient real line $Y$ is closed but not open.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于 $Y=[0,1]\subseteq\mathbb R$，取 $U=(-1,1/2)$，便有 $Y\cap U=[0,1/2)$，证明了前面所说的相对开性。整个 $Y$ 在自身中开，因为 $Y=Y\cap\mathbb R$；它在自身中也闭，因为相对补集为空。但在环境实线中，$Y$ 闭而不开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the restricted sets form a topology
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么限制后仍是拓扑*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The empty set and $Y$ arise as $Y\cap\varnothing$ and $Y\cap X$. Arbitrary unions and finite intersections retain the same form:
$$\bigcup_i(Y\cap U_i)=Y\cap\bigcup_iU_i,\qquad
\bigcap_{i=1}^{m}(Y\cap U_i)=Y\cap\bigcap_{i=1}^{m}U_i.$$
The ambient sets on the right are open by the topology axioms, so the relative sets obey those axioms too. The same construction works for the empty subspace, whose only open set is the empty set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

空集和 $Y$ 分别来自 $Y\cap\varnothing$ 与 $Y\cap X$。任意并与有限交保持上式的形式；右边的环境集合根据拓扑公理仍开，所以相对集合也满足拓扑公理。空子空间也适用，它唯一的开集就是空集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $X$ is a [[度量空间|metric space]] with distance $d$, restrict the same distance to pairs of points in $Y$. Its open balls are
$$B_r^Y(y)=Y\cap B_r^X(y),\qquad y\in Y,\ r>0.$$
This restricted metric produces exactly the subspace topology. An ambient open set cuts out relative small balls; conversely, a relatively open set is the intersection with $Y$ of the union of its ambient small balls.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $X$ 是距离为 $d$ 的[[度量空间]]，把同一个距离只用于 $Y$ 内的点对即可。其开球正是上式：环境开球只留下 $Y$ 内的部分。这个限制后的度量产生的拓扑恰好是子空间拓扑：环境开集能截出相对小球；反过来，相对开集也等于其各点环境小球的并与 $Y$ 的交。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Complements and closed sets are also relative
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*补集与闭集也要相对计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A set $A\subseteq Y$ is [[闭集|closed in $Y$]] when $Y\setminus A$ is open in $Y$. Equivalently, $A=Y\cap F$ for some closed $F$ in $X$. To verify this, if $F$ is closed then
$$Y\setminus(Y\cap F)=Y\cap(X\setminus F)$$
is relatively open. Conversely, writing $Y\setminus A=Y\cap U$ with $U$ open gives $A=Y\cap(X\setminus U)$, the required closed-set representation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$A\subseteq Y$ 在 $Y$ 中是[[闭集]]，指的是 $Y\setminus A$ 在 $Y$ 中开。等价地，存在 $X$ 中的闭集 $F$ 使 $A=Y\cap F$。若 $F$ 闭，上式给出的相对补集就是相对开集；反过来，若写出 $Y\setminus A=Y\cap U$ 且 $U$ 开，则 $A=Y\cap(X\setminus U)$，得到所需表示。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[集合闭包|closure]] has a useful compatibility formula for $A\subseteq Y$:
$$\operatorname{cl}_Y A=Y\cap\operatorname{cl}_X A.$$
Indeed, relative open neighbourhoods are ambient open neighbourhoods intersected with $Y$, and because $A\subseteq Y$, the extra intersection does not change whether they meet $A$. Thus only ambient closure points still belonging to $Y$ survive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $A\subseteq Y$，[[集合闭包]]满足上面的相容公式。相对开邻域就是环境开邻域与 $Y$ 的交；由于 $A\subseteq Y$，额外取交不改变是否碰到 $A$。因此只保留那些仍属于 $Y$ 的环境闭包点。

> [!warning] Do not transfer every formula by intersection · 并非每个概念都能直接取交
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Interior and boundary can change differently. With $A=Y=[0,1]$, the relative [[集合内部|interior]] is all of $Y$, but the ambient interior is $(0,1)$. The relative [[集合边界|boundary]] is empty, but $Y\cap\partial_{\mathbb R}A=\{0,1\}$. Always name the space in which a statement of openness, closedness, interior, closure or boundary is being made.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 内部与边界的变化方式不同。取 $A=Y=[0,1]$，相对[[集合内部|内部]]是整个 $Y$，环境内部却为 $(0,1)$；相对[[集合边界|边界]]为空，$Y\cap\partial_{\mathbb R}A$ 却为 $\{0,1\}$。谈开、闭、内部、闭包或边界时，都要说明所在空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This also supplies the appropriate domain for [[连续性|continuity]]. For a function defined only on $Y=[0,1]$, approaching $0$ means using input points that remain in $Y$. There is no need to assign the function values at negative inputs. The topology on the codomain is specified separately; the domain restriction does not decide it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也为[[连续性]]提供合适的定义域。若函数只定义在 $Y=[0,1]$ 上，输入接近 $0$ 时只考虑仍在 $Y$ 内的点，不需要给负输入规定函数值。陪域的拓扑另行指定，限制定义域并不会替它作决定。
