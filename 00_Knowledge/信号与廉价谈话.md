# 信号博弈中的行动直接进入收益，而廉价谈话的消息本身不进入收益，但二者都研究私人信息怎样通过可观察沟通影响后续行动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a signaling game the sender's action directly affects payoffs, whereas in cheap talk the message itself does not; both study how observable communication can transmit private information and affect later action.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区分信号与廉价谈话的关键不是“有没有发送消息”，而是发送者选择的可观察对象是否直接进入收益函数。一般信号模型可写成 $u_i(\theta,s,a)$；典型廉价谈话模型写成 $u_i(\theta,a)$，消息 $m$ 本身不作为收益参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The key distinction is not whether something is communicated, but whether the sender's observable choice directly enters payoffs. A general signaling model may use $u_i(\theta,s,a)$; a canonical cheap-talk model uses $u_i(\theta,a)$, so the message $m$ is not itself a payoff argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 分界在直接收益效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The dividing line is a direct payoff effect.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

教育、担保或等待等信号可能直接带来成本或收益，因此改变信号会在接收者行动不变时仍改变发送者收益。廉价谈话中的消息则不具有这种直接效应：若接收者采取同一行动，仅替换消息不会改变任何玩家的收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Signals such as education, warranties, or delay may directly impose costs or benefits, so changing the signal can change the sender's payoff even when the receiver's action is held fixed. A cheap-talk message has no such direct effect: if the receiver takes the same action, changing only the message does not change either player's payoff.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 收益无关不等于沟通无关
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Payoff-irrelevant does not mean communication-irrelevant.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

廉价谈话的消息仍可改变接收者对发送者类型的信念，继而改变接收者行动和最终收益。在偏好足够一致时，均衡消息可以传递信息；在偏好冲突过强时，均衡可能退化为不传递信息的闲谈。因此，廉价谈话是信号式沟通结构的一个特殊收益边界，而不是与信号博弈完全无关的模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A cheap-talk message can still change the receiver's belief about the sender's type and thereby change the receiver's action and final payoffs. When preferences are sufficiently aligned, equilibrium messages may transmit information; when conflict is too severe, equilibrium communication may become uninformative. Cheap talk is therefore a special payoff boundary within signaling-style communication, not an unrelated model class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
