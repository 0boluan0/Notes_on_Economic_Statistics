# Variance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Variance** measures how far a random variable tends to lie from its mean, using squared distances. The [[期望|mean]] tells us where the distribution is centred; variance adds information about how spread out it is around that centre. Two distributions can have the same mean and different variances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**方差**用平方距离衡量随机变量通常偏离均值多远。[[期望|均值]]告诉我们分布的中心在哪里，方差补充了取值在这个中心周围散开到什么程度。两个分布可以有相同均值，却有不同方差。

> [!example] Same centre, different spread · 中心相同，散布程度不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X$ take $2$ or $6$, each with probability $1/2$. Its mean is $4$. The deviations from the mean are $-2$ and $2$, so the squared deviations are both $4$. Their probability-weighted average is $4$: this is the variance.
>
> Compare a variable taking $3$ or $5$ with equal probability. Its mean is also $4$, but its deviations are $-1$ and $1$, giving variance $1$. A variable that is always $4$ has variance zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $X$ 以各 $1/2$ 的概率取 $2$ 或 $6$，均值为 $4$。偏离均值的量是 $-2$ 和 $2$，平方后都为 $4$。把平方偏离按概率平均，得到的 $4$ 就是方差。
>
> 再看一个以相同概率取 $3$ 或 $5$ 的变量：均值同样是 $4$，但偏离只有 $-1$ 和 $1$，方差为 $1$。如果一个变量始终等于 $4$，方差就是零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Squaring prevents positive and negative deviations from cancelling. Simply averaging $X-E[X]$ would always give zero when the mean is finite, even for a widely spread distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平方是为了避免正负偏离相互抵消。只要均值有限，直接平均 $X-E[X]$ 总会得到零，即使分布散得很开也一样。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Average the squared deviation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把平方偏离取期望*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $\mu=E[X]$ for the mean, where $\mu$ is the Greek letter “mu”. Assume $E[X^2]<\infty$: the squared values have a finite probability-weighted average. This condition also guarantees a finite mean. The notation $\operatorname{Var}(X)$ denotes variance, and $\sigma_X$ denotes [[标准差|standard deviation]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $\mu=E[X]$ 表示均值，其中 $\mu$ 是希腊字母 mu。假设 $E[X^2]<\infty$，也就是平方后的取值按概率平均仍然有限；这个条件同时保证均值有限。记 $\operatorname{Var}(X)$ 为方差，$\sigma_X$ 为[[标准差]]：

> [!abstract] Variance and standard deviation · 方差与标准差
> $$\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[(X-\mu)^2],\qquad
> \sigma_X=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)}.$$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> First subtract the mean, then square, then average under the distribution of $X$. Standard deviation takes the square root of that result.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 先减去均值，再平方，最后按 $X$ 的分布平均。标准差则对所得方差开平方。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a [[概率质量函数|PMF]], average by summing each squared deviation times its probability. With a [[概率密度函数|density]], use an integral with the density as the weight. This is the [[LOTUS|rule for the expectation of a function of a variable]] applied to the function $(x-\mu)^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有[[概率质量函数|PMF]]时，把每个平方偏离乘以对应概率后相加；有[[概率密度函数|密度]]时，以密度作权重积分。这就是把[[LOTUS|随机变量函数的期望计算规则]]应用到函数 $(x-\mu)^2$ 上。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $X$ is measured in seconds, each squared deviation is measured in seconds squared, so variance has units $\mathrm{s}^2$. Standard deviation returns to seconds. In the $2$-or-$6$ example interpreted as seconds, the variance is $4\,\mathrm{s}^2$ and the standard deviation is $2\,\mathrm{s}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $X$ 以秒计，平方偏离的单位就是秒的平方，所以方差单位为 $\mathrm{s}^2$；标准差再回到秒。把上面取 $2$ 或 $6$ 的例子解释为秒，方差就是 $4\,\mathrm{s}^2$，标准差则是 $2\,\mathrm{s}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A useful calculation formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把中心化的平方展开来算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expanding the square gives $X^2-2\mu X+\mu^2$. Because $\mu$ is a constant, [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]] lets us average the three terms separately. Using $E[X]=\mu$ yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

展开平方得到 $X^2-2\mu X+\mu^2$。因为 $\mu$ 是常数，[[期望线性性]]允许分别平均三项，再利用 $E[X]=\mu$，得到

$$\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[X^2]-2\mu E[X]+\mu^2
=E[X^2]-\mu^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $E[X^2]$ is the mean of the squared values, while $\mu^2=(E[X])^2$ is the square of the mean. In the $2$-or-$6$ example, they are $20$ and $16$, so the variance is $4$. Their distinction is developed in [[原点矩与中心矩|raw and central moments]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $E[X^2]$ 是先平方再平均，$\mu^2=(E[X])^2$ 是先平均再平方。对于取 $2$ 或 $6$ 的例子，二者分别为 $20$ 和 $16$，相减得到方差 $4$；这种区别见[[原点矩与中心矩]]。

## What zero and finite variance mean
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*方差为零和方差有限分别意味着什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A squared deviation is nonnegative, so variance cannot be negative. It is zero exactly when $P(X=\mu)=1$: the variable equals its mean **almost surely**, meaning with probability one. This permits exceptional outcomes of probability zero; it need not say that the variable is identical at every imaginable outcome.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平方偏离非负，因此方差不可能为负。方差为零，当且仅当 $P(X=\mu)=1$，也就是变量**几乎必然**等于均值：“几乎必然”指概率为一。这仍允许存在概率为零的例外结果，不必意味着每一个可能列出的结果都相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite variance requires a finite second moment $E[X^2]$. A finite mean alone is not enough. If the mean is finite but the average squared deviation diverges, the variance is $+\infty$; if the mean is undefined, the usual centred definition cannot be used. See [[矩存在性的使用边界|moment existence conditions]] before using a formula or theorem that assumes finite variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限方差需要二阶矩 $E[X^2]$ 有限，只有均值有限还不够。如果均值有限，但平方偏离的平均值发散，方差为 $+\infty$；如果均值本身没有定义，就不能使用通常的中心化定义。应用要求有限方差的公式或定理前，先检查[[矩存在性的使用边界]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing a variable's scale follows the [[方差的仿射变换|variance rule for affine transformations]]. Adding two different random variables is another operation: their sum's variance can also contain a [[和的方差协方差项|covariance term]], so variances do not generally add as means do.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

改变变量的尺度时，要使用[[方差的仿射变换]]。把两个随机变量相加则是另一种运算：结果的方差还可能包含[[和的方差协方差项|协方差项]]，因此不能像均值那样一般地直接相加。
