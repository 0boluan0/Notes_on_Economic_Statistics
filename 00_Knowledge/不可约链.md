# Irreducible Markov chains
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*不可约 Markov 链*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **irreducible chain** lets the system travel from every allowed state to every other allowed state with positive probability in some finite number of steps. The route need not be direct. A three-stop circuit $1\to2\to3\to1$ is irreducible: following the circuit eventually reaches any chosen stop from any starting stop.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**不可约链**要求：从任何允许的状态出发，都能以正概率在有限步内到达其他任何状态。路线不必一步直达。三站循环 $1\to2\to3\to1$ 就不可约：沿循环走，从任意出发站都能到达任意目标站。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a [[离散时间Markov链|discrete-time Markov chain]] on a nonempty finite or countably infinite state set $S$, with a fixed [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|time-homogeneous rule]]. In the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]], $p_{ij}$ is the one-step probability from $i$ to $j$. Write $p_{ij}^{(n)}$ for the probability of being at $j$ after $n$ steps when started at $i$. For a finite matrix $P$, these are the entries of $P^n$ by [[DTMC转移复合|transition composition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑非空有限或可数无限状态集 $S$ 上的[[离散时间Markov链]]，并使用固定的[[DTMC时间齐次转移核|时间齐次规则]]。按[[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]]，$p_{ij}$ 表示从 $i$ 一步到 $j$ 的概率；$p_{ij}^{(n)}$ 表示从 $i$ 出发，$n$ 步后位于 $j$ 的概率。有限矩阵 $P$ 下，这些数是 $P^n$ 的元素，见[[DTMC转移复合]]。

> [!important] Every ordered pair has a route · 每对有向起终点都有路线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The chain is irreducible exactly when
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 链不可约，当且仅当
>
> $$\text{for every }i,j\in S,\quad
> \text{there exists an integer }n\ge0\text{ such that }p_{ij}^{(n)}>0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The step count may depend on the pair $(i,j)$. The condition applies to every ordered pair, so it supplies travel in both directions. Equivalently, the entire state set is one [[马氏链沟通类|communicating class]]. Zero steps supply self-accessibility; they do not help travel between distinct states.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 步数可以随起终点 $(i,j)$ 改变。条件针对每一对有序状态，因此两个方向都要可达；等价地，整个状态集只有一个[[马氏链沟通类|沟通类]]。零步只提供状态对自身的可达，不提供不同状态之间的路线。

## A cycle is enough, but a one-way connection is not
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*循环足以互达，单向连通却不够*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the three-stop circuit, the matrix and its square are

$$P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&1\\1&0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
P^2=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1\\1&0&0\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}.$$

For example, $p_{13}=0$ but $p_{13}^{(2)}=1$: state 1 reaches 3 through 2. Every pair is connected within two steps, although many one-step entries are zero. Positive one-step probabilities everywhere are sufficient for irreducibility, but are not necessary. If instead the only moves are $1\to2$ and $2\to2$, then 2 cannot reach 1 and the two-state chain is reducible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三站循环的矩阵及平方如上。例如 $p_{13}=0$，但 $p_{13}^{(2)}=1$：1 经由 2 到达 3。任意两状态在至多两步内互达，尽管很多一步概率为零。因此，全部一步概率都正是不可约的充分条件，不是必要条件。若改成只有 $1\to2$、$2\to2$，则 2 无法返回 1，两状态链便可约。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Irreducibility concerns the transition rule on the declared state set, not just a particular starting distribution. In that reducible two-state example, starting at 2 keeps every observed state equal to 2, but does not make the original chain on $\{1,2\}$ irreducible. Restricting the model to the [[闭沟通类|closed class]] $\{2\}$ produces a different, one-state chain that is irreducible. More generally, restricting a chain to any closed communicating class gives an irreducible chain without changing its internal transition probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不可约性针对指定状态集上的转移规则，不是只针对某个初始分布。在上述可约两状态例中，从 2 出发虽然只能观察到 2，也没有让原来 $\{1,2\}$ 上的链变得不可约。若把模型限制在[[闭沟通类]] $\{2\}$ 上，则得到另一条不可约的单状态链。一般地，限制到任意闭沟通类，都会得到内部转移概率保持不变的不可约链。

> [!warning] Reachability does not remove periodic motion · 互达不排除周期运动
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The three-cycle has $P^3=I$, where $I$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. Starting at 1, the distribution cycles among the three point masses rather than converging. Positive returns to 1 occur only after $3,6,9,\ldots$ steps, so its [[状态周期|period]] is three. There is no single power $P^n$ with all entries positive. Thus irreducibility does not require a common step count that works for every pair, and does not by itself imply convergence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 三周期链满足 $P^3=I$，其中 $I$ 是[[单位矩阵]]。从 1 出发，分布在三个单点质量之间循环，不收敛。只有 $3,6,9,\ldots$ 步能正时间返回 1，所以[[状态周期|周期]]为三；没有任何一个 $P^n$ 的全部元素都正。因此，不可约不要求存在对所有状态对同时有效的共同步数，也不单独保证收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite chain, irreducibility does guarantee a [[有限不可约链稳态|unique stationary probability distribution, positive on every state]], without requiring aperiodicity. The three-cycle has stationary row $(1/3,1/3,1/3)$ even though other starting laws oscillate. [[有限链逐步收敛|Convergence from every initial law]] requires aperiodicity as well in the finite irreducible setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限链，不可约确实保证[[有限不可约链稳态|平稳概率分布唯一且处处为正]]，无需非周期条件。三周期链的平稳行就是 $(1/3,1/3,1/3)$，尽管其他初始分布会振荡。在有限不可约范围内，若要[[有限链逐步收敛|从每个初始分布逐步收敛]]，还需要非周期性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definition also applies to countably infinite chains, where those finite-state conclusions need not hold. For example, moving right or left on the integers with positive probabilities connects every pair, but with probabilities $3/4$ right and $1/4$ left there is a positive chance never to return to the starting state. This is a [[闭沟通类|closed irreducible class]] that is not [[常返与暂态|recurrent]]. Mutual accessibility says that a return is possible, not that it occurs with probability one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义同样适用于可数无限链，但有限状态结论不能直接照搬。例如，在整数线上以正概率向左或向右一格，任意两整数都互达；若向右概率为 $3/4$、向左为 $1/4$，则存在正概率永远不返回出发点。这是一个[[闭沟通类|闭的不可约类]]，却不是[[常返与暂态|常返类]]。互达只说明返回有可能，不说明返回以概率一发生。
