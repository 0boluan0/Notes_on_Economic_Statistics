# Stationary points under quasiconcavity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拟凹驻点不保证最优*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[驻点|stationary point]] has zero first-order change, but that alone does not compare its value with nearby values. Even adding [[拟凹函数|quasiconcavity]] does not guarantee a maximum. A smooth cubic provides a counterexample on the entire real line, with no boundary or missing-derivative issue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[驻点]]的一阶变化为零，但这本身没有比较该点与附近点的函数值。即使再加上[[拟凹函数|拟凹性]]，仍不能保证最大。光滑的三次函数在整条实线上就能给出反例，无需借助边界或导数不存在的情况。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Verify each part of the counterexample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把反例的每个条件逐一核对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$, $f(x)=x^3$. The domain is open and convex. For $a<b$, the identity $b^3-a^3=(b-a)(a^2+ab+b^2)>0$ shows that $f$ is increasing. By [[一维单调函数的拟凹拟凸性|the interval monotonicity theorem]], it is both quasiconcave and [[拟凸函数|quasiconvex]]. Thus this is a function satisfying the proposed shape hypothesis, not merely an arbitrary stationary-point example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$、$f(x)=x^3$。定义域开且凸。对 $a<b$，恒等式 $b^3-a^3=(b-a)(a^2+ab+b^2)>0$ 说明函数递增，因此由[[一维单调函数的拟凹拟凸性]]可知，它既拟凹又[[拟凸函数|拟凸]]。所以这个函数确实满足所讨论的形状假设，而不只是随便找来的驻点例子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[导数|derivative]] at zero is the limit of the difference quotient:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零点的[[导数]]就是差商的极限：

$$f'(0)=\lim_{h\to0,\,h\ne0}\frac{f(h)-f(0)}h=\lim_{h\to0}h^2=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In one variable, this finite derivative being zero is the stationary-point condition. But a [[局部极值与绝对极值|local maximum]] would require some radius $\delta>0$ for which all inputs within that distance have value at most $f(0)$. For every proposed $\delta>0$, choose $h=\delta/2$. Then $0<h<\delta$ and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一元时，这个有限导数为零就是驻定条件。但[[局部极值与绝对极值|局部最大]]要求存在半径 $\delta>0$，使该距离内的所有输入都不超过 $f(0)$。对任意候选 $\delta>0$，取 $h=\delta/2$，则 $0<h<\delta$，并且

$$f(-h)=-h^3<0=f(0)<h^3=f(h).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The positive displacement defeats a local maximum; the negative displacement defeats a local minimum. Such positive and negative displacements can be found in every neighborhood. Therefore zero is neither local extremum and consequently neither global extremum. A global extremum would satisfy the same comparison in every neighborhood.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正位移否定局部最大，负位移否定局部最小；任给邻域都能找到这样的两点。因此零点既不是局部极大也不是局部极小，更不是全局极值；全局极值必然在每个邻域里满足同样的比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The monotone cubic is both quasi-properties by [[一维单调函数的拟凹拟凸性|the one-dimensional theorem]], yet its zero derivative at the origin identifies no extremum. Thus [[凹函数驻点全局最优|the stationary global-optimum result for concave functions]] cannot simply substitute quasiconcavity for concavity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$f(x)=x^3$ 在实线上单调，故由[[一维单调函数的拟凹拟凸性]]既拟凹又拟凸。但 $f'(0)=0$，而零点附近同时有正值和负值，所以它既不最大也不最小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Which implication fails
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*究竟哪一步不能沿用*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a differentiable [[凹凸性|concave function]] on an open convex domain, the [[凹函数驻点全局最优|stationary-point global maximum theorem]] uses a tangent upper bound. At a zero derivative, that bound puts every other function value below or equal to the value at the point. Quasiconcavity only gives the minimum-endpoint segment comparison; it supplies no such tangent upper bound. In the cubic example, the tangent at zero is horizontal, yet every positive input lies above its height.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于开凸域上的可微[[凹凸性|凹函数]]，[[凹函数驻点全局最优]]使用切线或切平面给出的上界。导数为零时，该上界将其他点的值全部压在驻点值之下或与其相等。拟凹性只给出最小端点的线段比较，并不提供这样的切线上界。三次函数在零点的切线水平，却在每个正输入处都高于这条切线。

因此不能把[[凹函数驻点全局最优]]中的凹性直接换成拟凹性。使用更一般的优化充分条件时，必须重新检查其额外假设。

> [!warning] A failed sufficient condition · 充分性失效的边界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This example disproves the general inference “quasiconcave + stationary implies optimal.” It does not say that quasiconcave functions can never have optimal stationary points. For instance, a constant function on $\mathbb R$ has every point stationary and globally maximal. Additional optimization theorems must be read with their own assumptions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 本例否定的是“拟凹且驻定就一定最优”这一普遍推断，并不是说拟凹函数的驻点永远不能最优。例如 $\mathbb R$ 上常函数的每一点既驻定又是全局最大点。更进一步的优化定理必须连同自己的假设使用。
