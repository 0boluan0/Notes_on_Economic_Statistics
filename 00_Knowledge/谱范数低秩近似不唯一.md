# Nonuniqueness of spectral-norm low-rank approximations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*谱范数低秩近似不唯一*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The smallest possible error need not identify a single best approximation. The [[谱范数|spectral norm]] watches only the largest output error over unit input directions. If one direction already sets that maximum, another direction can sometimes be changed without raising it. This leaves freedom even when the leading singular directions are clearly separated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小误差未必确定唯一的最佳近似。[[谱范数]]只关注全部单位输入方向中的最大输出误差。如果一个方向已经决定最大值，改变另一个方向也可能不让最大值上升。因此，即使领先奇异方向明显分开，也可能留下选择自由。

> [!example] A strict gap still leaves room / 严格间隔仍留下余地
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take the [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] $A=\operatorname{diag}(3,2,1)$ and allow rank at most 1. The [[截断SVD|truncation]] $B_0=\operatorname{diag}(3,0,0)$ has residual $\operatorname{diag}(0,2,1)$, while $B_1=\operatorname{diag}(2,0,0)$ has residual $\operatorname{diag}(1,2,1)$. Both spectral errors are 2, the largest diagonal magnitude. The [[截断SVD最佳低秩近似|best-approximation theorem]] says 2 is the minimum. Thus these are two different optimal matrices, despite the strict gap $3>2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取[[对角矩阵]] $A=\operatorname{diag}(3,2,1)$，允许秩至多为1。[[截断SVD|截断矩阵]] $B_0=\operatorname{diag}(3,0,0)$ 的残差为 $\operatorname{diag}(0,2,1)$；另一个候选 $B_1=\operatorname{diag}(2,0,0)$ 的残差为 $\operatorname{diag}(1,2,1)$。谱误差都是最大对角模2，且[[截断SVD最佳低秩近似|最佳近似定理]]说明2就是最小值。因此它们是两个不同的最优矩阵，尽管截断间隔严格满足 $3>2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a difference in the matrices themselves, not merely two ways to write the same SVD. For input $(1,0,0)^T$, the two approximations predict $(3,0,0)^T$ and $(2,0,0)^T$. Here $T$ is [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Their [[Frobenius范数|Frobenius errors]] also differ: $\sqrt5$ versus $\sqrt6$. The additional first-coordinate error counts in an accumulated measure, even though it stays below the worst-direction error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里是矩阵本身不同，不只是同一 SVD 的不同写法。输入 $(1,0,0)^T$ 时，两近似分别预测 $(3,0,0)^T$ 和 $(2,0,0)^T$。$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]；它们的 [[Frobenius范数|Frobenius 误差]]也不同，分别为 $\sqrt5$ 和 $\sqrt6$：额外的第一坐标误差会被累计度量计入，即使它没有超过最坏方向的误差。

## A construction for every nontrivial rank budget
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*任意非平凡秩预算都有这种构造*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a real or complex $m\times n$ matrix, $m,n\ge1$, with [[矩阵秩|rank]] $r$. Choose an integer $1\le k<r$. Write its positive [[奇异值|singular values]] in descending order and take one [[SVD秩一展开|paired expansion]] $A=\sum_{j=1}^r\sigma_j u_jv_j^*$. The vectors on each side are perpendicular unit vectors, and $^*$ is conjugate transpose. The standard truncation $A_k=\sum_{j=1}^k\sigma_j u_jv_j^*$ has optimal error $\sigma_{k+1}>0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为实或复 $m\times n$ 矩阵，$m,n\ge1$，[[矩阵秩|秩]]为 $r$。取整数 $1\le k<r$，把正[[奇异值]]降序排列，选定一组[[SVD秩一展开|配对展开]] $A=\sum_{j=1}^r\sigma_j u_jv_j^*$。每一侧的向量都是相互垂直的单位向量，$^*$ 表示共轭转置。标准截断 $A_k=\sum_{j=1}^k\sigma_j u_jv_j^*$ 的最优误差为 $\sigma_{k+1}>0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose any retained index $i\le k$ and a positive amount $0<\delta\le\sigma_{k+1}$. Reduce only its retained coefficient:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任选一个已保留下标 $i\le k$，以及正数 $0<\delta\le\sigma_{k+1}$，只减少这一项的保留系数：

$$
\widetilde A=A_k-\delta u_iv_i^*,
\qquad
A-\widetilde A=\delta u_iv_i^*+\sum_{j=k+1}^r\sigma_j u_jv_j^*.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All outputs of $\widetilde A$ still lie in the span of $u_1,\ldots,u_k$, so its rank is at most $k$. Its coefficient in direction $i$ is $\sigma_i-\delta\ge0$. In the residual, the new error $\delta$ and the discarded errors occupy perpendicular input and output directions. The [[谱范数与最大奇异值|largest stretch]] is therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\widetilde A$ 的全部输出仍在 $u_1,\ldots,u_k$ 张成的空间内，所以秩至多为 $k$；第 $i$ 方向系数为 $\sigma_i-\delta\ge0$。在残差中，新增误差 $\delta$ 与原来舍弃的误差分别占据相互垂直的输入、输出方向，因此[[谱范数与最大奇异值|最大伸缩]]为

$$
\|A-\widetilde A\|_2
=\max\{\delta,\sigma_{k+1}\}
=\sigma_{k+1}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because $\delta>0$ and $u_iv_i^*$ is nonzero, $\widetilde A\ne A_k$. Both attain the minimum, proving nonuniqueness. In fact, varying $\delta$ through $0<\delta<\sigma_{k+1}$ produces infinitely many distinct candidates, all still of exact rank $k$. At the allowed endpoint $\delta=\sigma_i=\sigma_{k+1}$, the altered coefficient can vanish; the at-most-$k$ constraint still permits it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $\delta>0$ 且 $u_iv_i^*$ 非零，$\widetilde A\ne A_k$。二者都达到最小值，证明了非唯一性。事实上，令 $\delta$ 在 $0<\delta<\sigma_{k+1}$ 内连续变化，可得到无穷多个不同候选，且都仍恰有秩 $k$。在允许的端点 $\delta=\sigma_i=\sigma_{k+1}$，被改系数可能消失，但“秩至多 $k$”仍允许该候选。

> [!important] The exact scope / 精确适用范围
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every finite real or complex matrix and integer $1\le k<\operatorname{rank}(A)$, the spectral-norm best approximation of rank at most $k$ is nonunique. At $k=0$, the zero matrix is the only candidate. At $k\ge\operatorname{rank}(A)$, the minimum error is zero and its only minimizer is $A$. In particular, a rank-one matrix has no nontrivial budget in the stated interval.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对任意有限维实或复矩阵以及整数 $1\le k<\operatorname{rank}(A)$，谱范数下秩至多 $k$ 的最佳近似不唯一。$k=0$ 时唯一候选是零矩阵；$k\ge\operatorname{rank}(A)$ 时最小误差为零，唯一极小点为 $A$。特别地，秩一矩阵不存在所述区间内的非平凡预算。

## Do not transfer uniqueness between norms
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*不同范数的唯一性不能互相套用*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strict boundary gap makes the standard truncated matrix independent of the chosen paired bases, but it does not say that every optimal spectral approximation must be that matrix. The construction above changes a retained amplitude instead of merely rotating tied directions. Under the [[Frobenius低秩近似唯一性|Frobenius criterion]], the same modification adds $\delta^2$ to the squared residual, so it ceases to be optimal. This is why the norm must be named whenever uniqueness is discussed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

截断处的严格间隔会让标准截断矩阵不依赖所选配对基，但这并不表示所有谱范数最优近似都必须等于该矩阵。上述构造改变的是保留项的幅度，而非仅旋转重复方向。换成 [[Frobenius低秩近似唯一性|Frobenius 判据]]，同一修改会让残差平方增加 $\delta^2$，因而不再最优。所以讨论唯一性时，必须说明使用哪种范数。
