# 营运资本等于股东权益加非流动负债再减非流动资产
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Working capital equals equity plus noncurrent liabilities minus noncurrent assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在同一主体、同一时点且项目分类完整时，由[[会计恒等式]] $CA+NCA=CL+NCL+E$ 移项：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the same entity and date with an exhaustive classification, rearrange the [[会计恒等式|accounting identity]] $CA+NCA=CL+NCL+E$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$WC=CA-CL=(E+NCL)-NCA.$$

其中 $NCA$ 为非流动资产，$NCL$ 为非流动负债，$E+NCL$ 为这一口径的长期资本。例：$700-300=(960+740)-1300=400$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$NCA$ denotes noncurrent assets, $NCL$ noncurrent liabilities, and $E+NCL$ long-term capital under this definition. For example, $700-300=(960+740)-1300=400$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正[[营运资本]]表示长期资本超过非流动资产，差额净支持流动资产；负值表示反向缺口。这是资产负债表融资结构恒等式，不要求追踪某笔钱“先买长期资产、再买存货”，也不把非流动负债全部定义为有息借款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive [[营运资本|working capital]] means long-term capital exceeds noncurrent assets and provides net support to current assets; a negative value indicates the reverse gap. This balance-sheet identity does not trace a chronological spending order or classify every noncurrent liability as interest-bearing borrowing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
