# Integral bounds for a series remainder
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*积分判别的余项界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once a series is known to converge, a finite sum still leaves an infinite tail uncounted. **Integral remainder bounds** put that missing amount between two computable areas. They answer “how much have I left out?” and help decide how many terms are enough for a desired accuracy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知道级数收敛以后，有限次求和仍然遗漏了无限长的尾部。**积分判别余项界**把这个遗漏量夹在两个可计算的面积之间，回答“还少算了多少”，并帮助决定达到所需精度要算多少项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let a real [[无穷级数|series]] have finite sum $S=\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n$. Its first $N$ terms give the partial sum $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N a_n$. The remainder is $R_N=S-S_N=\sum_{n=N+1}^\infty a_n$: the first omitted term is $a_{N+1}$, not $a_N$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实数项[[无穷级数|级数]]有有限和 $S=\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n$，前 $N$ 项构成部分和 $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^N a_n$。余项是 $R_N=S-S_N=\sum_{n=N+1}^\infty a_n$：被漏掉的第一项是 $a_{N+1}$，不是 $a_N$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two nearby integrals surround the omitted sum
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两个相邻起点的积分夹住遗漏量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose that from some positive integer $N_0$ onward, $a_n=f(n)$, where $f$ is continuous, positive and nonincreasing on $[N_0,\infty)$. These are the usual [[级数积分判别|integral-test conditions]]. For an integer $N\ge N_0$, with convergence already established,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设从某个正整数 $N_0$ 开始，有 $a_n=f(n)$，其中 $f$ 在 $[N_0,\infty)$ 上连续、为正且单调不增。这是常用的[[级数积分判别|积分判别条件]]。对整数 $N\ge N_0$，在已经确认收敛的前提下，

> [!important] Lower start gives the upper bound · 起点较早，得到上界
>
> $$L_N=\int_{N+1}^\infty f(x)\,dx
> \le R_N
> \le U_N=\int_N^\infty f(x)\,dx.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $L_N$ and $U_N$ name the lower and upper error bounds. Adding the same $S_N$ throughout gives $S_N+L_N\le S\le S_N+U_N$. Because the omitted terms are positive, $S_N$ underestimates $S$, even if some of the earlier, already included terms are negative.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $L_N$、$U_N$ 分别表示误差的下界与上界。各边加上同一个 $S_N$，就得到 $S_N+L_N\le S\le S_N+U_N$。遗漏项为正，所以 $S_N$ 低估 $S$，即使前面已经包含的某些项为负，这一点仍成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the starting indices, fix an omitted index $n\ge N+1$. On $[n,n+1]$, the curve is below the rectangle of height $f(n)$; on $[n-1,n]$, it is above that same height. Hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要看清起点为何相差一，固定某个遗漏项 $n\ge N+1$。在 $[n,n+1]$ 上，曲线不高于高度为 $f(n)$ 的矩形；在 $[n-1,n]$ 上，曲线不低于同一高度。因此

$$\int_n^{n+1}f(x)\,dx\le f(n)\le\int_{n-1}^{n}f(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sum from $n=N+1$ to a finite $M>N$. The left integrals join into $\int_{N+1}^{M+1}f$, while the right integrals join into $\int_N^Mf$. Only after this finite comparison do we let $M\to\infty$. The series and corresponding tail integral converge, so their finite limits give the stated bounds. Nonstrict inequalities allow flat stretches; the same proof also allows nonnegative terms and proper local integrability as in the integral test's extended version.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $n=N+1$ 加到有限的 $M>N$：左侧积分拼成 $\int_{N+1}^{M+1}f$，右侧拼成 $\int_N^Mf$。完成这个有限比较后，才令 $M\to\infty$。级数及相应尾积分都收敛，所以取有限极限即得所述界。使用非严格不等式可以容纳水平的一段；与积分判别的推广版本一样，同一证明也允许非负项与正常局部可积。

> [!example] The reciprocal-square series · 平方倒数级数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $a_n=1/n^2$, convergence follows from the [[p级数判别|p-series criterion]]. The [[无穷远 p 积分判别|power-tail integral]] is $\int_c^\infty x^{-2}\,dx=1/c$ for $c>0$, giving
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $a_n=1/n^2$，[[p级数判别]]保证收敛。由[[无穷远 p 积分判别|幂函数尾积分]]，$c>0$ 时有 $\int_c^\infty x^{-2}\,dx=1/c$，所以
>
> $$\frac1{N+1}\le R_N\le\frac1N.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With $N=100$, the error of the first 100 terms is at most $0.01$, and the true sum belongs to $[S_{100}+1/101,\ S_{100}+1/100]$. The width of that interval is $1/10100$, much smaller than the error bound of $S_{100}$ itself. These are different quantities: uncertainty in the corrected interval versus omitted area in the uncorrected partial sum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $N=100$，前100项的误差不超过 $0.01$；真值位于 $[S_{100}+1/101,\ S_{100}+1/100]$。这个区间的宽度是 $1/10100$，远小于 $S_{100}$ 本身的误差上界。两者含义不同：一个是补上尾部估计后的区间宽度，一个是原部分和遗漏的面积。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choose a cutoff using a sufficient guarantee
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*按足够保证精度的条件选择截断点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a requested absolute error at most $\varepsilon>0$, it is enough to choose an integer $N\ge N_0$ with $U_N\le\varepsilon$. In the reciprocal-square example, $1/N\le\varepsilon$ is sufficient. If the request is strictly less than $\varepsilon$ and you use only the displayed nonstrict bound, choose $U_N<\varepsilon$. A bound exceeding the target does not prove that the true error exceeds it; the bound may be conservative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若要求绝对误差至多为 $\varepsilon>0$，选择满足 $N\ge N_0$ 且 $U_N\le\varepsilon$ 的整数 $N$ 即可。平方倒数例子中，$1/N\le\varepsilon$ 是充分条件。若要求严格小于 $\varepsilon$，并且只使用上面的非严格界，就选 $U_N<\varepsilon$。上界超过目标，并不证明实际误差也超标；这个界可能较保守。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, for $a_n=n^{-p}$ with $p>1$, the upper bound is $U_N=N^{1-p}/(p-1)$. Thus $N\ge[1/((p-1)\varepsilon)]^{1/(p-1)}$, rounded upward to a positive integer, guarantees $R_N\le\varepsilon$. The inequality is a sufficient cutoff rule, not necessarily the smallest possible number of terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般地，对 $a_n=n^{-p}$、$p>1$，上界为 $U_N=N^{1-p}/(p-1)$。因此，取不小于 $[1/((p-1)\varepsilon)]^{1/(p-1)}$ 的正整数 $N$，就能保证 $R_N\le\varepsilon$。这是保证精度的充分截断规则，不一定给出最少项数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the series diverges, there is no finite $S$ from which to subtract $S_N$; an infinite upper integral supplies no finite error guarantee. For alternating signs, the positive-rectangle argument does not directly control the signed remainder. Use the separate [[交错级数误差界|alternating-series error bound]] when its hypotheses hold. These estimates control mathematical truncation error; numerical rounding in the computed $S_N$ is a separate contribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若级数发散，就没有有限的 $S$ 可减去 $S_N$；无穷的上界积分也不能保证有限误差。若符号交错，正矩形论证不能直接控制带符号余项；相应条件成立时，应使用独立的[[交错级数误差界]]。这些估计控制数学上的截断误差，实际计算 $S_N$ 时的舍入误差是另一部分。
