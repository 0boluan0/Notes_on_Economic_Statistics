# 正项递减级数截断后的余项夹在从下一整数与当前整数开始的两个尾积分之间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The remainder of a convergent positive decreasing series lies between tail integrals starting at the next and current integers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n=S$ 收敛，并有函数 $f$ 在 $[N_0,\infty)$ 上连续、为正、单调不增，满足 $f(n)=a_n$。对整数 $N\ge N_0$，令 $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^Na_n$、$R_N=S-S_N$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n=S$ converges and $f$ is continuous, positive, and nonincreasing on $[N_0,\infty)$, with $f(n)=a_n$ there. For an integer $N\ge N_0$, define $S_N$ and $R_N=S-S_N$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\int_{N+1}^{\infty}f(x)\,dx
\le R_N=\sum_{n=N+1}^{\infty}a_n
\le\int_N^{\infty}f(x)\,dx.
$$

被略去的第一项是 $a_{N+1}$：左侧从 $N+1$ 积分给下界，右侧从 $N$ 积分给上界。将同一个 $S_N$ 加到两边，就得到级数和 $S$ 的区间；正项尾部使 $S_N$ 本身低估真值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first omitted term is $a_{N+1}$. Starting the integral at $N+1$ gives the lower bound; starting at $N$ gives the upper bound. Adding $S_N$ gives an interval for $S$, while the positive tail makes $S_N$ an underestimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是 [[级数积分判别]] 的矩形比较应用于尾部后的定量结果。先确认积分判别条件和收敛性，再选 $N$ 使上界积分不超过要求的误差；如果积分发散，这个公式不会提供有限误差保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the quantitative form of the [[级数积分判别|integral test]] applied to the tail. First verify the hypotheses and convergence; then choose $N$ so the upper integral is below the required error. A divergent integral provides no finite error guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $a_n=1/n^2$，由 [[无穷远 p 积分判别]] 计算尾积分得 $1/(N+1)\le R_N\le1/N$。取 $N=100$，前 100 项的误差不超过 $0.01$；加上下界与上界，还能把真值夹进 $[S_{100}+1/101,\ S_{100}+1/100]$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a_n=1/n^2$, the [[无穷远 p 积分判别|p-integral formula]] gives $1/(N+1)\le R_N\le1/N$. With 100 terms, the error is at most $0.01$, and the sum lies in $[S_{100}+1/101,\ S_{100}+1/100]$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
