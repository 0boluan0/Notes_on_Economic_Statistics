# The midpoint rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*中点法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A function may be easy to evaluate at individual inputs even when its total integral is hard to calculate. The **midpoint rule** samples the centre of each small interval, treats that height as representative of the whole piece, and adds height times width. It is one particular [[黎曼和|Riemann sum]] used as a finite numerical approximation to a [[定积分|definite integral]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个函数可能很容易在某个输入处求值，却不容易直接算出整个积分。**中点法**在每一小段的中心采样，用这个高度代表整段，再累加高度乘宽度。它是把一种特定的[[黎曼和]]用作[[定积分]]的有限数值近似。

> [!example] Two samples for a logarithm · 用两个采样值近似对数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Approximate $\int_1^2 dx/x$. Split $[1,2]$ at $3/2$, giving two pieces of width $1/2$. Their centres are $5/4$ and $7/4$, not the three endpoints $1,3/2,2$. Reading $f(x)=1/x$ there gives heights $4/5$ and $4/7$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 近似 $\int_1^2 dx/x$。在 $3/2$ 处分割 $[1,2]$，得到两个宽为 $1/2$ 的小段。中心分别是 $5/4$ 和 $7/4$，不是三个端点 $1,3/2,2$。在中心读取 $f(x)=1/x$，得到高度 $4/5$ 和 $4/7$。
>
> $$M_2=\frac12\left(\frac45+\frac47\right)=\frac{24}{35}\approx0.685714.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[自然对数积分表示|exact integral is $\ln2$]], approximately $0.693147$, so this two-sample estimate is close but not equal to it. The multiplier $1/2$ is the width of each piece. Omitting it would add heights rather than accumulated contributions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[自然对数积分表示]]，精确积分为 $\ln2\approx0.693147$，所以两个采样值给出的估计接近真值，却不等于真值。前面的 $1/2$ 是每段宽度；漏掉它，累加的就只是高度，不是各段的累积贡献。

## Put the pieces and their centres into a formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把分段和中心写成公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a<b$ be finite real numbers, let $f$ be real-valued on $[a,b]$, and let $N\ge1$ be an integer counting equal subintervals. Their common width is $h=(b-a)/N$, and their endpoints are $x_i=a+ih$ for $i=0,\ldots,N$. Piece $i$ runs from $x_{i-1}$ to $x_i$; its centre is $m_i$. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<b$ 为有限实数，$f$ 为 $[a,b]$ 上的实值函数，用正整数 $N\ge1$ 表示等宽小段的数量。共同段宽为 $h=(b-a)/N$，端点为 $x_i=a+ih$，其中 $i=0,\ldots,N$。第 $i$ 段从 $x_{i-1}$ 延伸到 $x_i$，中心记作 $m_i$。于是

$$m_i=\frac{x_{i-1}+x_i}{2}=a+\left(i-\frac12\right)h,
\qquad M_N=h\sum_{i=1}^{N}f(m_i).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The symbol $\sum$ adds the $N$ terms. There are $N+1$ endpoints but only $N$ midpoint samples; $N$ need not be even. For $N=1$, the formula is $(b-a)f((a+b)/2)$. To form a finite value $M_N$, the sampled values only need to be finite; a claim about approximating an integral also requires that integral to exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求和号 $\sum$ 把 $N$ 项相加。共有 $N+1$ 个端点，却只有 $N$ 个中点采样值；$N$ 不必是偶数。$N=1$ 时，公式为 $(b-a)f((a+b)/2)$。要算出有限的 $M_N$，只需所取样的函数值有限；若要声称它在近似某个积分，还须确认那个积分存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalently, $M_N=(b-a)\bigl(N^{-1}\sum_i f(m_i)\bigr)$: average the sampled heights, then multiply by the full interval length. If $f$ is a velocity and $x$ is time, the result has units of distance and keeps the sign of motion. Negative values contribute negatively; this is not automatically geometric area or total distance. Unequal pieces require their own widths, rather than one common multiplier $h$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也可以写成 $M_N=(b-a)\bigl(N^{-1}\sum_i f(m_i)\bigr)$：先平均采样高度，再乘整个区间长度。如果 $f$ 是速度、$x$ 是时间，结果具有长度单位，并保留运动方向的符号。负函数值产生负贡献，不能自动当作几何面积或总路程。不等宽的小段则须各用自己的宽度，不能统一乘同一个 $h$。

## Why a straight line is integrated exactly
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么直线恰好算准*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a piece centred at $m$, an affine function $f(x)=px+q$ can be written $f(m)+p(x-m)$, where $p,q$ are fixed real constants. The constant part integrates to $hf(m)$. The positive and negative contributions of $p(x-m)$ cancel over the symmetric interval $[m-h/2,m+h/2]$, so its integral is zero. This proves exactness on every piece, and hence on the whole interval by [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在以 $m$ 为中心的小段上，一次或常函数 $f(x)=px+q$ 可写成 $f(m)+p(x-m)$，其中 $p,q$ 是固定实常数。常数部分的积分为 $hf(m)$；在对称区间 $[m-h/2,m+h/2]$ 上，$p(x-m)$ 的正负贡献抵消，积分为零。因此每一段都精确，再由[[定积分区间可加性]]得到全区间精确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=3x+1$ on $[0,2]$ with $N=2$, the centres are $1/2$ and $3/2$, and $h=1$. The two heights are $5/2$ and $11/2$, so $M_2=8$, exactly the integral. Curvature can leave an error: for $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$ with $N=2$, $M_2=\tfrac12[(1/4)^2+(3/4)^2]=5/16$, while [[Newton-Leibniz公式|endpoint evaluation]] gives the true value $1/3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $[0,2]$ 上的 $f(x)=3x+1$，取 $N=2$，中心为 $1/2,3/2$，段宽为 $1$。两个高度是 $5/2,11/2$，所以 $M_2=8$，恰等于积分。弯曲则可能留下误差：对 $[0,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x^2$，取 $N=2$，得到 $M_2=\tfrac12[(1/4)^2+(3/4)^2]=5/16$；[[Newton-Leibniz公式]]给出的真值却是 $1/3$。

## More pieces and a certified accuracy are different claims
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*增加段数与保证精度是两件事*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f$ is Riemann integrable on $[a,b]$, these midpoint sums tend to its integral as $N\to\infty$: they are Riemann sums whose largest piece width $h$ tends to zero. [[连续函数可积|Continuity throughout a finite closed interval]] is a familiar sufficient condition. No derivative is needed for this convergence statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上黎曼可积，中点和在 $N\to\infty$ 时趋于其积分：它们是最大段宽 $h$ 趋于零的黎曼和。[[连续函数可积|有限闭区间上的连续性]]是一个常见充分条件。这条收敛结论不需要导数。

> [!note] A finite result does not carry its own error certificate · 一个有限结果不会自带误差保证
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A large $N$ alone does not give a universal numerical tolerance. The [[中点积分误差界|midpoint error theorem]] uses a bound on the second derivative over the whole interval to certify an absolute error. It also explains under- or overestimation under the corresponding curvature conditions. These are extra guarantees beyond the sampling rule.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 仅说 $N$ 很大，不能给出通用的数值容差。[[中点积分误差界]]利用整个区间上的二阶导数界保证绝对误差，并在相应曲率条件下解释低估或高估。这些都是采样规则之外的额外保证。
