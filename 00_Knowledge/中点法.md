# 中点法用每个等宽小区间中点的函数值乘宽度近似定积分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The composite midpoint rule approximates an integral using the value at the centre of each equal-width subinterval*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $a<b$，取 $N$ 个等宽小区间，$h=(b-a)/N$、$x_i=a+ih$。中点法在第 $i$ 段取 $m_i=(x_{i-1}+x_i)/2=a+(i-\tfrac12)h$，把 $f(m_i)h$ 相加；$f$ 连续时，该 [[黎曼和]] 随 $N\to\infty$ 趋于 [[定积分]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a<b$, use $N$ equal subintervals, $h=(b-a)/N$, and $x_i=a+ih$. The midpoint rule samples at $m_i=(x_{i-1}+x_i)/2=a+(i-\tfrac12)h$ and sums $f(m_i)h$. For continuous $f$, this [[黎曼和|Riemann sum]] converges to the [[定积分|definite integral]] as $N\to\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
M_N=h\sum_{i=1}^{N}f(m_i).
$$

方法以每段中点的高度代表整段，所以只需 $N$ 个中点函数值。中点两侧的一次变化互相抵消，因此一次函数可被精确积分；一般曲线仍有误差，何时能保证精度由 [[中点积分误差界]] 给出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One midpoint height represents each subinterval, requiring $N$ function values. Linear variation cancels between the two sides of a midpoint, so affine functions are integrated exactly. Curved functions generally have an error; [[中点积分误差界|the midpoint error bound]] gives the conditions for a guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\int_1^2dx/x$ 取 $N=2$，$h=1/2$，中点为 $5/4$、$7/4$，于是 $M_2=\tfrac12(\tfrac45+\tfrac47)=24/35\approx0.685714$。这里函数值的平均数还要乘整个区间长度；直接把函数值相加会遗漏宽度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\int_1^2dx/x$ with $N=2$, $h=1/2$ and the midpoints are $5/4$ and $7/4$. Thus $M_2=\tfrac12(\tfrac45+\tfrac47)=24/35\approx0.685714$. The mean sample height must be multiplied by the interval length; adding heights alone omits the width.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 在 $[0,2]$ 上用 $N=2$ 近似 $\int_0^2(3x+1)\,dx$，中点为何不是 $0,1,2$？结果是否精确？
>
> **答案：** $0,1,2$ 是端点；中点为 $1/2,3/2$，$h=1$，所以 $M_2=5/2+11/2=8$，恰等于真值。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $\int_0^2(3x+1)\,dx$ with $N=2$, why are $0,1,2$ not the sample points, and is the result exact? **Answer:** Those are endpoints. The midpoints are $1/2,3/2$, and $h=1$, giving $M_2=5/2+11/2=8$, the exact integral.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
