# The natural exponential function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*自然指数函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **natural exponential**, written $e^x$ or $\exp(x)$, is an [[指数函数|exponential function]] with a particularly convenient scale: its instantaneous rate of increase equals its current value. The constant $e\approx2.71828$ is its base. This choice makes a proportional growth rate appear directly in a formula, instead of carrying a separate conversion factor for the base.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**自然指数函数**记作 $e^x$ 或 $\exp(x)$，是采用一种特别方便尺度的[[指数函数]]：它的瞬时增长速度，恰好等于当前函数值。常数 $e\approx2.71828$ 是它的底数。这种选择让相对增长率直接出现在公式里，不必再带一个由底数造成的换算因子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The input can be any real number, while the output is always positive: $e^0=1$, $e^1=e$, and $e^{-1}=1/e$. A negative input gives a positive value below one, not a negative output. The function increases and takes every positive value exactly once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入可以是任意实数，输出始终为正：$e^0=1$、$e^1=e$、$e^{-1}=1/e$。负输入给出小于一的正值，并不产生负输出。函数递增，恰好一次地取遍每个正值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[导数|derivative]] measures change per unit of input near the point in question. Writing $E(x)=e^x$, the defining rate normalisation is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[导数]]衡量某一点附近，输入每变化一单位所对应的输出变化速度。写成 $E(x)=e^x$，这个速率标准化的刻画是

$$E(0)=1,\qquad E'(x)=E(x).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a small increment $h$, this means $E(x+h)-E(x)\approx E(x)h$. At $x=0$, the value and slope are both one; at $x=1$, both are $e$. The approximation describes a small change, not an exact finite-step equality. In particular $e^{0.01}\approx1.01005$, close to but not exactly $1.01$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对很小的增量 $h$，它意味着 $E(x+h)-E(x)\approx E(x)h$。在 $x=0$，函数值和斜率都为一；在 $x=1$，两者都为 $e$。这是小变化的近似，并非有限步长的精确等式。例如 $e^{0.01}\approx1.01005$，接近但并不等于 $1.01$。

> [!example] A rate expressed directly · 速率直接写进公式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $Q(t)=100e^{0.05t}$. The [[链式法则|chain rule]], which accounts for the changing inner input $0.05t$, gives $Q'(t)=0.05Q(t)$. At time zero, the amount is $100$ and its instantaneous increase is $5$ units per unit of time. After one time unit it is about $105.127$, not $105$: the rate is applied continuously to the changing amount. A negative rate, as in $100e^{-0.05t}$, gives decay.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $Q(t)=100e^{0.05t}$。[[链式法则]]把内层输入 $0.05t$ 的变化计入，得到 $Q'(t)=0.05Q(t)$。初始数量为 $100$，当时的瞬时增加速度是每单位时间 $5$ 单位。过一单位时间后约为 $105.127$，而不是 $105$，因为速率持续作用于已经变化的数量。若速率为负，例如 $100e^{-0.05t}$，就表示衰减。

## How this function can be constructed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*这个函数可以怎样构造出来*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rate condition should identify an actual function, not just a hoped-for graph. One construction starts with an ordinary [[定积分|definite integral]], without using $e$ or logarithms beforehand. For $u>0$, let
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

速率条件需要对应一个确实存在的函数。一个构造方法先从普通[[定积分]]出发，不预先使用 $e$ 或对数。对 $u>0$，定义

$$L(u)=\int_1^u\frac{dt}{t}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This integral accumulates the positive function $1/t$; reversing the endpoints makes it negative when $0<u<1$. The [[积分求导定理|fundamental theorem for accumulation integrals]] gives $L'(u)=1/u>0$, so $L$ is continuous and strictly increasing. Splitting the integral and [[定积分换元|changing variables to $t=av$]] shows $L(ab)=L(a)+L(b)$ for $a,b>0$. In particular, $L(2^n)=nL(2)$ and $L(2^{-n})=-nL(2)$, with $L(2)>0$. These values grow without bound in both directions, and the [[介值定理|intermediate value theorem]] fills every value between them. Thus the range of $L$ is all real numbers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个积分累积正函数 $1/t$；$0<u<1$ 时上下限反向，所以结果为负。[[积分求导定理]]给出 $L'(u)=1/u>0$，因此 $L$ 连续且严格递增。把积分分段并[[定积分换元|代入 $t=av$]]，可得对 $a,b>0$ 有 $L(ab)=L(a)+L(b)$。特别地，$L(2^n)=nL(2)$、$L(2^{-n})=-nL(2)$，且 $L(2)>0$。这些值向正负两边都无界延伸，[[介值定理]]保证连续函数还会填满它们之间的每个值。因此 $L$ 的值域就是全部实数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We can therefore define $E$ as the inverse of $L$: $E(x)$ is the unique positive number with $L(E(x))=x$. Since $L(1)=0$, we get $E(0)=1$. The [[反函数导数|inverse-derivative rule]] takes the reciprocal of the original slope at the corresponding point and gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

于是可以把 $E$ 定义为 $L$ 的反函数：$E(x)$ 就是满足 $L(E(x))=x$ 的唯一正数。由于 $L(1)=0$，有 $E(0)=1$。[[反函数导数|反函数求导规则]]在对应点取原斜率的倒数，从而给出

$$E'(x)=\frac{1}{L'(E(x))}=E(x).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product law for $L$ also yields $E(x+y)=E(x)E(y)$. Set $e=E(1)$; this law agrees with integer and rational powers of $e$, and continuity extends that agreement to all real inputs. Thus $E(x)=e^x$. This construction establishes the rate condition without assuming a formula for the derivative of a logarithm or a sequence limit defining $e$. Once $E$ is established, its inverse is called the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$L$ 的乘积法则还给出 $E(x+y)=E(x)E(y)$。令 $e=E(1)$，这个法则与 $e$ 的整数、有理数次幂一致，连续性再把一致性扩展到所有实数输入，因此 $E(x)=e^x$。这个构造建立了速率条件，而没有先假设对数求导公式，或用某个序列极限定义 $e$。建立 $E$ 后，它的反函数就称为[[自然对数]]。

## The slope characterisation used in calculus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*微积分中使用的斜率刻画*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the exponential family, let $M(a)=\lim_{h\to0}(a^h-1)/h$, the slope at zero. The construction above gives $M(e)=1$. More generally, [[指数函数求导|differentiation of exponentials]] identifies $M(a)=\ln a$, so $e$ is the unique positive base with this slope. The often-used [[自然常数极限|limit $(1+1/n)^n\to e$]] is another characterisation proved later; it is not an extra assumption in the construction above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对指数函数族，令 $M(a)=\lim_{h\to0}(a^h-1)/h$，即零点处的斜率。上面的构造给出 $M(e)=1$。进一步由[[指数函数求导]]可知 $M(a)=\ln a$，所以 $e$ 是斜率为一的唯一正底数。常用的[[自然常数极限|极限 $(1+1/n)^n\to e$]]是后续证明的另一个刻画，不是上面构造额外借用的前提。
