# 使用后续走势才能确定的缺口类别不能提前作为缺口当期信号
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A gap category requiring subsequent prices cannot be used as a signal on the gap date.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当期高低价可以确认 [[价格缺口]] 是否存在，却不自动告诉我们它处于一段未来趋势的中途还是末尾。如果把“随后继续走强”或“几天后回补并反转”写进分类条件，分类标签只能在这些后续条件已观察到后使用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Current highs and lows can establish the existence of a [[价格缺口|price gap]], but not automatically its location within the middle or end of a future trend. A category defined using subsequent continuation, filling, or reversal becomes available only after those outcomes are observed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原课把趋势途中出现的缺口称为持续性缺口，把快速行情末段的缺口称为消耗性缺口。为了在当时提出预测，可以事先固定一套只用已知数据的候选识别法；但那是关于类别的预测，不是已经知道真实后续路径。事后分类可以用来研究结果，不能倒填为当时已掌握的交易输入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The course calls gaps within a continuing move continuation gaps and those near its end exhaustion gaps. A pre-specified, past-data-only detector can predict such a category, but cannot already know the future path. Retrospective classification can study outcomes; it cannot be backdated into a trading input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如两条价格路径在缺口日以前完全相同，此后甲持续上涨，乙回落补缺。当日可得资料相同，却有两种后续结局；用结局给缺口命名，并不能证明当日已有能力区分甲乙。应按 [[预测时点与信息集]] 记录每个标签的可用日期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two paths can be identical through the gap date, then diverge: one continues upward and the other falls back through the gap. Identical information at the gap date is compatible with both outcomes. Naming gaps by their outcomes does not establish an ability to distinguish those outcomes in advance. Record label availability using [[预测时点与信息集|the information-set convention]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
