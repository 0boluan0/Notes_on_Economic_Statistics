# 卡尔多检验问新状态能否补偿原状态而希克斯检验排除原状态的反向补偿
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Kaldor asks whether the new state can compensate the old; Hicks rules out compensation in the reverse direction.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同一次 $A\to B$ 改变，Kaldor 检验问“在新状态 $B$ 再分配后，能否 Pareto 优于 $A$”；Hicks 检验问“在原状态 $A$ 再分配后，是否无法 Pareto 优于 $B$”。两者评估的再分配起点相反，不能把“赢家能补偿输家”同时当作两个检验的完整定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the same move $A\to B$, Kaldor asks whether redistribution from $B$ can Pareto-improve on $A$. Hicks asks whether redistribution from $A$ cannot Pareto-improve on $B$. Their starting points differ; “winners can compensate losers” does not fully define both.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定同一批人及其偏好比较，令 $C(S)$ 为状态 $S$ 下允许转移可达的配置。采用 [[Pareto改进与有效|人人不差、至少一人严格更好]] 的 $\succ_P$ 约定：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the people and their preference comparisons. Let $C(S)$ contain allocations attainable by permitted transfers in state $S$, and use the [[Pareto改进与有效|no-one-worse, someone-strictly-better]] convention for $\succ_P$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{aligned}
K(A\to B)&:\quad\exists\widetilde B\in C(B),\ \widetilde B\succ_P A,\\
H(A\to B)&:\quad\nexists\widetilde A\in C(A),\ \widetilde A\succ_P B.
\end{aligned}
$$

因此 Hicks 通过意味着反向 Kaldor 不通过。若某教材把无差异边界也列为“通过”，须重新核对弱、严格不等号；不能只按中文名判断。[[潜在补偿准则]] 所用的正向检验本身不包含这个反向检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus a Hicks pass means a reverse Kaldor failure. Texts may handle indifference boundaries differently, so inspect their weak and strict comparisons. The forward [[潜在补偿准则|potential compensation criterion]] alone does not perform the reverse check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个自造的可达集合例说明差别：用始终不变的两人效用指标，设原状态效用为 $A=(4,4)$，新状态为 $B=(2,5.5)$；两种状态下再分配可达的效用集合分别满足下式。这里的不等式是例子给定的可行边界，不是社会福利函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A constructed attainable-set example separates the tests. Using fixed utility indices for both people, take $A=(4,4)$ and $B=(2,5.5)$, with redistribution possibilities below. These inequalities specify feasibility, not a social welfare function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C(A)=\{(u_1,u_2)\ge0:u_1+u_2\le8\},
\qquad
C(B)=\{(u_1,u_2)\ge0:u_1/2+u_2\le6.5\}.
$$

在 $B$ 下，$(4.5,4.25)$ 用满 $4.5/2+4.25=6.5$，且两人都优于 $A$，所以正向 Kaldor 通过。但在 $A$ 下，$(2.25,5.75)$ 用满 $8$，且两人都优于 $B$，所以反向 Kaldor 也通过，正向 Hicks 不通过。这就是为什么一次正向潜在补偿结论不能当作无歧义的社会排序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
From $B$, $(4.5,4.25)$ is feasible and improves both people relative to $A$, so forward Kaldor passes. From $A$, $(2.25,5.75)$ is feasible and improves both relative to $B$, so reverse Kaldor also passes and forward Hicks fails. A forward potential-compensation result therefore need not give an unambiguous social ranking.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同时检查正向 Kaldor 与 Hicks 可以排除这类直接逆转；这仍不是自动获得完整、传递的所有状态排序，也没有要求实际补偿已经支付。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Checking both forward Kaldor and Hicks rules out this direct reversal. It does not automatically yield a complete, transitive ranking of all states or establish that compensation was paid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
