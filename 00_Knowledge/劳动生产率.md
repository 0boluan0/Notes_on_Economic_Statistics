# 劳动生产率是一定时期内产出量与对应劳动投入之比

劳动生产率衡量一单位劳动投入对应多少产出。部门分析通常以增加值衡量产出，本组采用可比价格的增加值除以同期就业人数；按工时衡量时，则改为增加值除以实际工作总时数。人数与工时回答的是不同单位的问题，不能直接混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Labour productivity measures output per unit of labour input. Sectoral analysis commonly uses value added; these notes divide value added at comparable prices by employment for the same period. An hours-based measure instead divides by total hours actually worked. Persons and hours define different units and are not interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设部门增加值为 $Y_i$、对应就业人数为 $L_i>0$，则每就业者劳动生产率为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For sectoral value added $Y_i$ and corresponding employment $L_i>0$, productivity per employed person is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p_i=\frac{Y_i}{L_i}.
$$

例如，一年中某部门可比价增加值为 120 个货币单位，同期平均就业人数为 60，则 $p_i=2$ 个货币单位／就业者。若这 60 人共工作 120,000 小时，每小时增加值是 $120/120000=0.001$；不能把“2”和“0.001”当成同单位的高低比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If annual value added is 120 constant-price currency units and average employment is 60, productivity is 2 currency units per employed person. If those people work 120,000 hours, value added per hour is $120/120000=0.001$. Comparing 2 with 0.001 without their units is meaningless.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $L_i$ 表示就业，不是全部人口，也不是“就业＋失业”的劳动力；分母区别见[[人均GDP]]和[[失业率]]。人数、岗位和工时也须核清：一人多岗时，不能把分部门重复计数的岗位总数当成不重复的就业人数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $L_i$ denotes employment, not total population or the labour force of employed plus unemployed people; compare [[人均GDP|GDP per capita]] and the [[失业率|unemployment rate]]. Distinguish persons, jobs and hours as well: multiple jobs held by one person cannot be summed as distinct employed people.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较时间变化时，应剔除产出价格变化，沿用[[实际GDP增长]]中的实际量原则。劳动生产率还受资本、技术和组织方式等影响，不是单个劳动者努力程度的评分，也不是[[增长核算的 TFP 残差|全要素生产率残差]]。把各部门合成总体时，接着使用[[劳动生产率加总]]的匹配权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For changes over time, remove output-price changes using the volume principle in [[实际GDP增长|real GDP growth]]. Labour productivity also depends on capital, technology and organization. It is neither a score of individual effort nor a [[增长核算的 TFP 残差|total-factor-productivity residual]]. To combine sectors, use the matching weights in [[劳动生产率加总|labour-productivity aggregation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
