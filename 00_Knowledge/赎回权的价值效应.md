# 其余合同与风险相同时发行人赎回权使债券价值不高于无赎回权债券

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With other contractual and risk conditions unchanged, an issuer call makes a bond worth no more than its noncallable counterpart.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把同一组无赎回债务权利作为比较基准。加入[[可赎回债券|发行人赎回权]]后，持有人把决定何时按约定价格结束债务的选择交给对方。在一致的无套利估值模型中，若赎回权价值为 $C\ge0$，则：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the same debt rights without a call as the baseline. Adding an [[可赎回债券|issuer call]] gives the counterparty the option to terminate those rights on the agreed terms. Under a consistent arbitrage-free valuation model, with call value $C\ge0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_{\mathrm{callable}}=P_{\mathrm{straight}}-C\le P_{\mathrm{straight}}.$$

最小行权对照：只有这一次赎回决策、当期息票刚付完、无违约与交易摩擦，并可立即交割。若继续持有的债务价值为 110、赎回款为 102，发行人选择赎回，持有人得 102；若继续价值为 95，则不赎回。因此这个决策点的价值为 $\min(V_{\mathrm{continue}},102)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a single exercise decision immediately after a coupon, assume no default or trading frictions and immediate settlement. With continuation value 110 and redemption payment 102, the issuer calls and the holder receives 102; at continuation value 95, the issuer leaves the debt outstanding. Value at that decision point is therefore $\min(V_{\mathrm{continue}},102)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利率下降可能同时抬高普通债价值和发行人赎回权价值，因而压缩可赎回债的上涨空间。但赎回尚未开放、需要通知、期间仍付息或赎回价随规则变化时，当前价格并非一律以某个固定赎回价封顶。上述比较也不意味着不同息票或不同信用的两只真实债券必有同样价格排序。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Falling rates can raise both straight-bond value and the issuer's call value, limiting appreciation. But a closed call window, notice period, intervening coupons, or variable redemption rule prevents a universal fixed cap on today's price. The comparison likewise does not order two real bonds with different coupons or credit risks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
