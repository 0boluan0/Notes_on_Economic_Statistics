# 平方贫困缺口是全体人口标准化贫困短缺平方的平均值

平方贫困缺口 $P_2$ 对每个人的标准化贫困短缺先平方，再对全体人口取平均。与[[贫困缺口指数]]仅加总短缺相比，平方使较大缺口在汇总中得到更高的相对权重，因而能区分相同总缺口在贫困者之间的不同分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The squared poverty gap $P_2$ squares each person's normalized poverty shortfall and averages over the whole population. Unlike the [[贫困缺口指数|poverty gap index]], which simply sums shortfalls, squaring assigns larger gaps greater relative weight. It can therefore distinguish different distributions of the same total shortfall among poor people.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设[[贫困线]] $z>0$，非贫困者缺口取零，等权个人的定义是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a [[贫困线|poverty line]] $z>0$, assign zero gaps to nonpoor people. For equally weighted individuals,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
g_i=\frac{\max\{z-y_i,0\}}{z},\qquad
P_2=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n g_i^2.
$$

当 $z=100$，收入为 40、90、120、150 时，$P_2=(0.6^2+0.1^2)/4=0.0925$。若收入 90 的贫困者将 25 转给收入 40 的贫困者，变成 65、65、120、150，则[[贫困人口比例]]仍为 0.5，$PG$ 仍为 0.175，但 $P_2=2(0.35^2)/4=0.06125$。同样两个人贫困、总短缺不变，最深的短缺却已缓和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $z=100$ and incomes of 40, 90, 120, and 150, $P_2=(0.6^2+0.1^2)/4=0.0925$. If the poor person earning 90 transfers 25 to the one earning 40, incomes become 65, 65, 120, and 150. The [[贫困人口比例|headcount ratio]] remains 0.5 and $PG$ remains 0.175, but $P_2=2(0.35^2)/4=0.06125$. The same two people remain poor with the same total shortfall, but the deepest deprivation has eased.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个比较固定贫困线、人口与价格口径，并让转移双方仍低于线、收入次序不反转。平方函数的凸性解释了这种均等化为何降低 $P_2$。它不是货币补足成本，也不是 $PG^2$：后者先平均再平方，会丢掉个体缺口的分散程度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This comparison fixes the line, population, and price basis; both parties remain below the line and their income ranking does not reverse. Convexity of the square explains why this equalization lowers $P_2$. It is neither a monetary top-up cost nor $PG^2$: squaring the average first loses information about variation in individual gaps.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它是 Foster–Greer–Thorbecke（FGT）家族中指数为 2 的情形；$P_0$ 数贫困人口，$P_1$ 平均缺口，$P_2$ 平均平方缺口。实际调查须按人口权重平均 $g_i^2$；在福利非负时 $0\le P_2\le1$，负值或异常值处理属于[[贫困测量的福利口径]]，不能靠公式自动解决。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It is the exponent-2 member of the Foster–Greer–Thorbecke (FGT) family: $P_0$ counts poverty, $P_1$ averages gaps, and $P_2$ averages squared gaps. Survey estimates average $g_i^2$ with population weights. Nonnegative welfare gives $0\le P_2\le1$; negative or anomalous observations require an explicit [[贫困测量的福利口径|welfare-data convention]], not an automatic solution from the formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
