# 若 S_t 表示每单位外币的本币价格则 S_t 上升表示本币贬值且倒数报价会反转方向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If $S_t$ is domestic currency per unit of foreign currency, a rise in $S_t$ is a domestic depreciation, and the reciprocal quotation reverses the direction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子口径
> 汇率必须同时写出“分子货币/分母货币”。本卡固定
> $$S_t=\frac{\text{本币}}{\text{一单位外币}}.$$
> 因而 $S_t$ 上升表示买一单位外币需要更多本币：外币升值、本币贬值。若改报 $1/S_t$，升贬值语言和变化符号随之反转。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An exchange rate must identify both its numerator and denominator currencies. This card fixes $S_t$ as domestic currency per unit of foreign currency. A rise in $S_t$ means that one unit of foreign currency costs more domestic currency: the foreign currency appreciates and the domestic currency depreciates. Using $1/S_t$ reverses the appreciation language and the sign of the change.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若原报价的有限变化率为 $g=(S_{t+1}-S_t)/S_t$，则倒数报价的变化率精确为
$$
\frac{1/S_{t+1}-1/S_t}{1/S_t}=-\frac{g}{1+g},\qquad g>-1.
$$
例如从 $0.80$ GBP/USD 变成 $0.84$ GBP/USD，美元的英镑价格上升 $5\%$，所以美元对英镑升值、英镑对美元贬值。倒数报价从 $1.25$ USD/GBP 变成约 $1.1905$ USD/GBP，变化率约为 $-4.76\%$。方向相反，但有限百分比的绝对值通常不完全相同；只有对数变化满足精确的等幅反号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the original quotation has the finite rate of change $g=(S_{t+1}-S_t)/S_t$, the reciprocal quotation changes by exactly $-g/(1+g)$, for $g>-1$. For example, a move from 0.80 to 0.84 GBP per USD raises the sterling price of a dollar by 5%, so the dollar appreciates and sterling depreciates. The reciprocal quotation moves from 1.25 to about 1.1905 USD per GBP, a change of about -4.76%. The signs reverse, but finite percentage changes generally do not have equal absolute magnitudes; only log changes reverse with exactly equal magnitude.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即期汇率与同期限远期汇率必须使用同一报价方向，才能计算 $F_t/S_t$、远期点或跨币种回报。本卡只固定名义汇率的测量语言；汇率决定机制需要另行给出资产定价或宏观模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Spot and maturity-matched forward rates must use the same quotation before calculating $F_t/S_t$, forward points, or cross-currency returns. This card fixes the nominal-exchange-rate measurement convention only; exchange-rate determination requires a separate asset-pricing or macroeconomic model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若 $S_t$ 从 7.0 上升到 7.2 人民币/美元，哪种货币贬值？改报美元/人民币后，变化方向怎样？
>
> **答案：** 人民币对美元贬值、美元升值；倒数的美元/人民币报价下降。不能在不声明报价时只说“汇率上升”。
