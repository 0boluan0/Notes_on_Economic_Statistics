# 判断公债的代际负担须同时核算税款债权收益与承接的真实资产
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assess intergenerational debt incidence using taxes, creditor receipts and inherited real assets together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公债的代际负担，是相对于明确基准，各代因融资安排而发生的消费机会或福利变化；未来税款只是其中一项。先说明政府购买路径是否相同，再对每代列出税款、债券购买与偿付、私人资本或对外净资产，以及公共资产和服务。最后才讨论筹税扭曲、风险与福利权重，不能用“还债在未来”代替完整比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Intergenerational debt incidence is the change in each generation's consumption opportunities or welfare relative to a specified counterfactual. Future taxes are only one item. Identify whether purchases are fixed, then record each cohort's taxes, bond purchases and receipts, private capital or net foreign assets, and public assets and services. Tax distortions, risks and welfare weights require further assessment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看一个条件化的两期资源账户。今天老年人与年轻人的禀赋各为 $20$，政府购买固定为 $10$。设年轻人今天消费 $10$、总储蓄 $10$，这项行为在两种方案中固定；真实资本和政府债券的明年毛回报均设为每单位 $1.1$。这是比较给定储蓄路径的例子，不是断言家庭必然如此选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a conditional two-date resource account. Today, old and young cohorts each have endowments of $20$, and government purchases are fixed at $10$. Hold the young cohort's current consumption at $10$ and total saving at $10$ in both cases. Both real capital and government bonds return $1.1$ per unit next year. These saving paths are assumptions, not predictions of household behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 今天的项目 | 向老年人征税 $10$ | 向年轻人举债 $10$ |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 老年人消费 | 10 | 20 |
| 年轻人消费 | 10 | 10 |
| 政府购买 | 10 | 10 |
| 真实资本投资 | 10 | 0 |
| 资源使用合计 | 40 | 40 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Today's allocation | Tax the old $10$ | Borrow $10$ from the young |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Old cohort consumption | 10 | 20 |
| Young cohort consumption | 10 | 10 |
| Government purchases | 10 | 10 |
| Real capital investment | 10 | 0 |
| Total resource use | 40 | 40 |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

明年老年人已不在，年轻人另有禀赋 $100$。税收方案留下资本回收 $11$，可消费 $111$；债务方案则向这一代征税 $11$ 偿还其债券 $11$，两项抵消后可消费 $100$。本例未来少掉的 $11$ 来自资本路径，而不是把税款与同代债券收入都算成损失。今天老年人多消费 $10$，今天的年轻一代明年少消费 $11$，两期资源变化与所设资本回报一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next year the old cohort is gone and the young have another endowment of $100$. The tax-financed case also returns $11$ from capital, allowing consumption of $111$. In the debt-financed case, a tax of $11$ finances a bond receipt of $11$ for that same cohort, leaving consumption of $100$. The missing $11$ comes from the capital path, not from counting both taxes and bond payments as losses. The intertemporal resource change matches the assumed capital return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个结果依赖给定的储蓄与资本替代，不能推广为“公债总是损害后代”。私人储蓄抵消可能改变[[公债挤出条件|资本渠道]]，有效的[[代际联系与债务中性|代际转移]]可能改变家庭计划，新增公共资产也可能使后代受益。债权人与纳税人若属于不同人、不同代或境外主体，还要逐项保留其分配；[[转移支付核算|内部货币支付的抵消]]不等于所有人的福利变化都为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This result depends on the specified saving and capital substitution; it does not show that debt always harms future generations. Saving offsets may alter the [[公债挤出条件|capital channel]], effective [[代际联系与债务中性|intergenerational transfers]] may alter family plans, and new public assets may benefit later cohorts. Different taxpayers and creditors require separate distributional accounts. [[转移支付核算|Cancellation of internal payments]] does not make every person's welfare change zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

核算各代变化之后，是否值得用今天的收益换未来的损失，还涉及[[社会贴现与代际评价]]。债券市场利率可以描述本例的资产回报，却不能单独决定代际福利权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After measuring cohort changes, judging a trade-off between present gains and future losses involves [[社会贴现与代际评价|social discounting and intergenerational evaluation]]. The market rate can describe the example's asset return without determining social welfare weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
