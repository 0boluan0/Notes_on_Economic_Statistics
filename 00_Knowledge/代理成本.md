# 代理成本包括委托人的监督支出、代理人的保证支出和剩余损失

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Agency costs comprise the principal's monitoring expenditures, the agent's bonding expenditures, and residual loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Jensen–Meckling 的定义中，代理成本（agency costs）不仅是为了治理冲突而花的钱，还包括治理后仍未消除的决策偏离给委托人造成的福利损失。三部分须按同一评价口径计算，避免把同一损失重复计入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Jensen–Meckling's definition, agency costs include not only resources spent on governance but also the principal's welfare loss from decision divergence that remains afterward. The three components need a consistent valuation basis and must not double-count the same loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 监督支出（monitoring）：委托人用于观察和约束代理人的资源，也可包括预算、报酬政策和经营规则的设计。
- 保证支出（bonding）：代理人用资源作出可信约束，保证不实施某些损害行为或在发生时补偿委托人；它不是委托人发给代理人的所有奖金。
- 剩余损失（residual loss）：给定上述安排后，实际决策仍偏离委托人利益最大化决策而造成的福利减损。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Monitoring: resources used by the principal to observe and constrain the agent, including the design of budgets, compensation policies, and operating rules.
- Bonding: resources committed by the agent to credibly constrain harmful behavior or assure compensation if it occurs; this is not a synonym for every bonus paid by the principal.
- Residual loss: the principal's welfare reduction from remaining divergence between actual decisions and those maximizing the principal's welfare, given the governance arrangements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如监督耗费 5、代理人保证耗费 3，而治理后仍存在折价为 7 的剩余损失，则总代理成本为 15。把监督从 5 提到 9 只减少 2 的剩余损失，会使总成本上升；“更严的监督”不是不计成本的目标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If monitoring costs 5, bonding costs 3, and remaining welfare loss is valued at 7, total agency costs are 15. Increasing monitoring from 5 to 9 while reducing residual loss by only 2 raises total cost; stricter monitoring is not a costless objective.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
