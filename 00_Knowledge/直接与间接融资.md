# 直接融资让资金提供者持有最终融资者证券而间接融资让其持有中介负债
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Direct finance gives fund suppliers the ultimate borrower's security; indirect finance gives them an intermediary's liability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 判断融资是直接还是间接，先固定要分类的那一段资金链及其资金提供者，再沿索取权看资产负债表：
> - **直接融资：** 该段的资金提供者持有资金使用者发行的证券；
> - **间接融资：** 该段的资金提供者持有金融中介的负债，而中介持有最终融资者的贷款或证券。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> First fix the financing leg being classified and the supplier of funds on that leg; then trace claims across balance sheets rather than counting the institutions present. In direct finance, that supplier holds a security issued by the user of funds. In indirect finance, that supplier holds an intermediary's liability, while the intermediary holds the ultimate borrower's loan or security.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若企业发行债券，家庭通过券商买入，家庭的资产仍是企业债券，企业直接面对债券持有者的合同索取权，因此属于直接融资。若家庭持有银行存款，银行再向企业放贷，家庭的资产是银行负债，银行的资产才是企业贷款，因此属于间接融资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a firm issues a bond that a household buys through a broker, the household still owns the firm's bond and the firm directly owes the security holder: this is direct finance. If the household holds a bank deposit and the bank lends to the firm, the household owns a bank liability and the bank owns the business loan: this is indirect finance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一条较长的资金链可以同时包含两种关系。例如“家庭购买基金份额，基金购买企业债”：家庭持有基金的负债，从家庭到企业看存在间接融资；基金又直接持有企业债，从基金到企业这一段看是直接融资。若不先固定分析起点，“最终投资者”容易把家庭与基金两个资产负债表混在一起。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One longer funding chain can contain both relationships. Consider a household buying a fund share and the fund buying a corporate bond. The household holds the fund's liability, so the household-to-firm chain contains indirect finance; the fund itself directly holds the corporate bond, so the fund-to-firm leg is direct finance. Without fixing the analytical starting point, the phrase “ultimate investor” can collapse the household's and fund's two balance sheets into one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一区分描述合同链，不是说某一张储户钞票被银行原封不动转交给借款人。银行放贷、结算和资金管理的会计机制另有约束；在这里需要追踪的是“谁对谁负有何种金融承诺”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This distinction describes a chain of contractual claims. It does not imply that a particular depositor's banknotes are mechanically handed to a borrower. Bank lending, settlement, and funding operate under their own accounting constraints; the present question is who owes which financial promise to whom.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 承销商、经纪商、交易所或托管机构参与证券发行，并不会自动把直接融资改成间接融资；关键仍是它们是否把最终信用资产留在自己的资产负债表上，并向储蓄者发行另一层负债。
- 共同基金份额通常是基金的索取权，而基金持有一组最终证券，因此从资产负债表链看也存在一层中介；它与吸收可按面值支取存款的银行并不承担完全相同的承诺。
- “直接”不等于成本低或没有信息问题；“间接”也不等于中介替最终借款人无条件承担全部损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The participation of an underwriter, broker, exchange, or custodian does not automatically turn direct finance into indirect finance. The question is whether the institution retains the ultimate credit asset on its balance sheet and issues another liability to savers.
- A mutual-fund share is a claim on the fund, which owns a portfolio of ultimate securities, so the balance-sheet chain includes an intermediary. Its promise is nevertheless different from a bank deposit redeemable at par.
- “Direct” does not mean cheap or free of information problems; “indirect” does not mean that the intermediary unconditionally absorbs every loss of the ultimate borrower.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一家投资银行承销企业债后立即把债券出售给投资者。这为什么仍可能是直接融资？
>
> **答案：** 在被分类的“债券持有者—企业”这一段，持有者的资产是企业债权；投资银行主要促成发行，并未长期用自己的负债替换企业证券。机构参与与资产负债表中介不是一回事。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can an underwritten corporate-bond issue still count as direct finance?
>
> **Answer:** On the security-holder-to-firm leg being classified, the holder's asset is the firm's debt claim. The investment bank facilitates issuance rather than permanently replacing the corporate security with its own liability. Institutional participation is not the same as balance-sheet intermediation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
