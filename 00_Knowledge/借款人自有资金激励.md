# 自有资金减少外部偿付压力时可为借款人保留足够收益以维持努力激励

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Own funds can preserve effort incentives by reducing external repayment needs and leaving the borrower a sufficient stake in success.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键不只是借款人“有钱”，而是他在不同选择下实际得到什么。考虑风险中性、有限责任的单期项目：努力时成功概率为 $p_H$，偷懒时为 $p_L<p_H$ 并得到私人收益 $B>0$；成功时借款人保留 $R_b$，失败时为零。努力激励条件是：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
What matters is not merely being wealthy, but the payoffs attached to different choices. In a one-period, risk-neutral, limited-liability project, diligence gives success probability $p_H$; shirking gives $p_L<p_H$ and private benefit $B>0$. The borrower keeps $R_b$ on success and zero on failure. Diligence requires:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$p_HR_b\ge p_LR_b+B\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad(p_H-p_L)R_b\ge B.$$

固定投资规模和项目技术时，更多实际投入的自有资金可以减少所需外部融资，使合同有空间把更多成功收益留给借款人。例如概率差为 0.3、偷懒收益为 20，借款人至少需要保留 $20/0.3\approx66.67$ 的成功收益；保留 50 不足，保留 75 则满足条件。自有资金不是凭其名称产生激励，而是通过这类收益约束起作用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For fixed investment size and technology, more own funds actually invested can reduce external financing needs and make room for a larger success payoff to the borrower. With a probability gap of 0.3 and private benefit of 20, the required success stake is at least $20/0.3\approx66.67$: 50 is insufficient, while 75 satisfies the condition. Own funds operate through payoff constraints, not through their label alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若财富并未投入或承担风险、合同没有改变，或者财富同时改变项目选择和风险偏好，就不能直接推出行为更稳健。这个机制不等于“净值高的人一定更诚实”，也不把内部资金看作没有机会成本。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If wealth is not invested or exposed, the contract is unchanged, or wealth also changes project choices and risk preferences, safer behavior does not follow automatically. This mechanism neither equates high net worth with honesty nor treats internal funds as free of opportunity cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
