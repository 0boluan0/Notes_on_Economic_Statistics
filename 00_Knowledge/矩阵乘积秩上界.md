# Rank bound for a matrix product
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*矩阵乘积的秩上界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Putting two matrix actions in succession cannot create more independent output directions than either action can supply. The [[矩阵秩|rank]] counts these independent directions: it is the dimension of the [[列空间|column space]], the set of all attainable outputs. The product may lose directions when the first action feeds the second an input that the second sends to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把两个矩阵作用接起来，不会产生比其中任一步所能提供的更多独立输出方向。[[矩阵秩|秩]]数的正是这些独立方向：它是[[列空间]]，即全部可达输出组成的空间的维数。如果前一步把某个方向交给后一步，而后一步把它送到零，乘积就可能丢失方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two limits apply at once
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两个限制同时成立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$ and $B\in\mathbb F^{n\times p}$, with positive integers $m,n,p$ and $\mathbb F=\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. The order is $x\mapsto Bx\mapsto A(Bx)$: $B$ acts first. The [[矩阵乘法|matrix product]] $AB$ has $m$ rows and $p$ columns, and
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$、$B\in\mathbb F^{n\times p}$，$m,n,p$ 为正整数，$\mathbb F=\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。作用顺序是 $x\mapsto Bx\mapsto A(Bx)$，即先做 $B$。[[矩阵乘法|矩阵乘积]] $AB$ 有 $m$ 行、$p$ 列，并且

> [!important] The smaller rank is an upper bound · 较小的秩给出上界
> $$\boxed{\operatorname{rank}(AB)\le
> \min\{\operatorname{rank}(A),\operatorname{rank}(B)\}.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The notation $\min$ chooses the smaller of the two numbers. Neither factor needs to be square or invertible, and the zero matrix is allowed. This is an upper bound, not an assertion of equality.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\min$ 表示两个数中较小的那个。两个因子都不必是方阵或可逆矩阵，也允许零矩阵。这是一条上界，并不宣称等号成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[矩阵乘积逐列作用|columnwise identity]] says every column of $AB$ is a combination of columns of $A$. Therefore $C(AB)\subseteq C(A)$. Both spaces lie in $\mathbb F^m$, so the dimension of the smaller space cannot exceed that of the larger:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[矩阵乘积逐列作用|逐列恒等式]]说明 $AB$ 的每一列都是 $A$ 各列的组合，所以 $C(AB)\subseteq C(A)$。两个空间都处于 $\mathbb F^m$，子空间的维数不会超过包含它的空间，因而

$$\operatorname{rank}(AB)\le\operatorname{rank}(A).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the other bound, use the [[矩阵乘积逐行作用|rowwise identity]]: every row of $AB$ is a combination of rows of $B$. Hence $\operatorname{Row}(AB)\subseteq\operatorname{Row}(B)$, with both consisting of $p$-entry rows. The [[行空间|row spaces]] here are algebraic spans, using ordinary scalar combinations even over $\mathbb C$. The theorem that [[行秩等于列秩|row rank equals column rank]] converts their dimension comparison into
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一侧用[[矩阵乘积逐行作用|逐行恒等式]]：$AB$ 的每一行都是 $B$ 各行的组合，所以 $\operatorname{Row}(AB)\subseteq\operatorname{Row}(B)$，两者都由含 $p$ 个元素的行组成。这里的[[行空间]]是代数张成，复数域上也使用普通标量组合。再由[[行秩等于列秩]]，把行空间维数的比较转换为

$$\operatorname{rank}(AB)\le\operatorname{rank}(B).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both inequalities hold for the same product, proving the boxed result. This compares dimensions, not the spaces themselves: $C(A)$ lies in $\mathbb F^m$ while $C(B)$ lies in $\mathbb F^n$, so those two spaces need not even share an ambient space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个不等式对同一个乘积同时成立，合起来就是框中结论。这里比较的是维数，不是把空间认作相同：$C(A)$ 位于 $\mathbb F^m$，$C(B)$ 位于 $\mathbb F^n$，二者甚至不一定处于同一个环境空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check a rectangular example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*核验一个矩形例子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the shared rowwise and columnwise example:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用逐行、逐列卡片的共同例子：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&-1\\1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&0\\3&1&4\end{pmatrix},\qquad
AB=\begin{pmatrix}-1&3&-4\\1&2&0\\3&1&4\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The superscript $T$ denotes ordinary [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. The two columns of $A$ are independent because their last two coordinates form $(1,0)^T$ and $(0,1)^T$. The first two columns of $B$ are independent: a zero combination with coefficients $\alpha,\beta$ requires $\alpha+2\beta=0$ and $3\alpha+\beta=0$, which force both coefficients to zero. Thus both factors have rank two. The last two coordinates of the first two columns of $AB$ are $(1,3)^T$ and $(2,1)^T$, exactly the first two columns of $B$, so they too are independent. The upper bound now forces $\operatorname{rank}(AB)=2$. A $3\times3$ product can therefore have rank two even when both rectangular factors have the greatest rank their sizes allow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上标 $T$ 表示普通[[矩阵转置|转置]]。$A$ 的两列独立，因为它们的末两个坐标分别为 $(1,0)^T$、$(0,1)^T$。$B$ 的前两列也独立：若用系数 $\alpha,\beta$ 组合为零，则须满足 $\alpha+2\beta=0$、$3\alpha+\beta=0$，从而两个系数都为零。所以两个因子的秩均为二。$AB$ 前两列的末两个坐标分别为 $(1,3)^T$、$(2,1)^T$，正好是 $B$ 的前两列，因此也独立；上界随即迫使 $\operatorname{rank}(AB)=2$。可见即使两个矩形因子都达到各自尺寸允许的最大秩，得到的 $3\times3$ 乘积仍可只有秩二。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## When the inequality is strict
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 严格下降何时发生

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a minimal example of a lost direction:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看一个最简单的方向丢失例子：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}0\\1\end{pmatrix},\qquad AB=(0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each factor has rank one, but the product has rank zero. For a scalar input $t$, $B$ produces $(0,t)^T$, and $A$ reads only the first coordinate, so the final output is always zero. The image $C(B)$ lies entirely in the [[零空间|null space]] $N(A)=\{y:Ay=0\}$. More generally, [[矩阵乘积为零|$AB=0$ exactly when $C(B)\subseteq N(A)$]]. Nonzero factors alone cannot prevent this loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个因子的秩都是一，乘积的秩却为零。对标量输入 $t$，$B$ 产生 $(0,t)^T$，而 $A$ 只读取第一坐标，所以最终输出总为零。像空间 $C(B)$ 完全落在[[零空间]] $N(A)=\{y:Ay=0\}$ 内。一般地，[[矩阵乘积为零|$AB=0$ 当且仅当 $C(B)\subseteq N(A)$]]；仅仅要求因子非零，无法阻止这种丢失。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Invertible factors preserve the other rank
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A$ is square and [[逆矩阵|invertible]], the upper bound gives $\operatorname{rank}(AB)\le\operatorname{rank}(B)$. But $B=A^{-1}(AB)$, so applying the same established bound to this product gives the reverse inequality. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A$ 是方阵且[[逆矩阵|可逆]]，上界给出 $\operatorname{rank}(AB)\le\operatorname{rank}(B)$；又因为 $B=A^{-1}(AB)$，对这个乘积再次应用已证明的上界，便得到反向不等式。因此

$$\operatorname{rank}(AB)=\operatorname{rank}(B)\qquad(A\text{ invertible}).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Similarly, if $B$ is square and invertible, then $A=(AB)B^{-1}$ proves
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，若 $B$ 是方阵且可逆，由 $A=(AB)B^{-1}$ 得到

$$\operatorname{rank}(AB)=\operatorname{rank}(A)\qquad(B\text{ invertible}).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All sizes must still match: the first case has $m=n$, the second has $n=p$. The other matrix may remain rectangular. Invertibility is sufficient, not necessary, for equality: the singular matrix $D=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ satisfies $D^2=D$, so its product with itself keeps rank one. Finally, a [[矩阵乘积外积展开|sum of outer-product terms]] may contain repeated or cancelling directions; counting its written nonzero terms does not determine the product rank.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

尺寸仍须匹配：第一种情况有 $m=n$，第二种有 $n=p$，另一个矩阵仍可为矩形。可逆是等号成立的充分条件，不是必要条件：奇异矩阵 $D=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ 满足 $D^2=D$，与自身相乘仍保持秩一。最后，[[矩阵乘积外积展开|外积项之和]]可能含重复或抵消的方向，数出写了几个非零项，不能确定乘积的秩。
