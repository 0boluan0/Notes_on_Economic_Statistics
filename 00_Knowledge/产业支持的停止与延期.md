# 产业支持的停止与延期应依据原定目标、学习证据和可行替代的净收益复核

对于以幼稚产业学习为理由的限期支持，应预先规定目标、观察指标、复核期和默认到期安排；届时依据实际学习进展、最终竞争力与政策净收益，决定停止、调整或有条件延期。期限不是“到某年必定成熟”的预测，延期也不能只靠受益企业希望继续获得支持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For time-limited support justified by infant-industry learning, set objectives, indicators, review dates and a default termination arrangement in advance. Review actual learning, eventual competitiveness and policy net benefits to decide whether to stop, adjust or extend conditionally. A deadline does not predict certain maturity, and beneficiaries' desire for continued support is not enough to justify extension.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**先查学习，不把销量当作答案。** 销售增加可能只是补贴暂时降低成本，也可能包含持续的生产能力改善。要看可比质量下的单位投入、缺陷率、生产率等指标，结合累计生产经验、建厂准备期和未受支持活动的变化。对照组比较需要可信的[[公共项目反事实|反事实依据]]；仅有受支持企业前后成本下降，不能识别政策造成的学习，更不能识别[[学习外部性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First assess learning rather than treating sales as the answer. Higher sales can reflect temporary cost relief or lasting capability gains. Examine inputs per quality-adjusted unit, defects and productivity, accounting for cumulative experience, construction lead time and changes in unsupported activities. Comparisons require a credible [[公共项目反事实|counterfactual]]: a supported firm's cost decline alone identifies neither policy-induced learning nor a [[学习外部性|learning externality]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**再查支持理由是否仍成立。** 世界银行报告以Mill–Bastable检验概括两个共同要求：有学习并具有最终达到国际竞争力的前景，且政策收益超过成本。观察到目标行业投入效率提高，可以支持进一步调查，却不能替代完整判断；财政、消费者、下游使用者和其他机会的成本也可能改变结论。已有[[公共项目净现值边界|净现值评价边界]]仍然适用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next reassess the justification. The World Bank report summarizes the Mill–Bastable test as requiring both learning with eventual international competitiveness and policy benefits exceeding costs. Improved input efficiency warrants investigation but is not a complete test; fiscal costs, consumers, downstream users and forgone opportunities can change the conclusion. The existing [[公共项目净现值边界|limits of net-present-value assessment]] still apply.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**把停止与延期都约束起来。** 没有可信进展或净收益依据时，不应因已投入很多钱就自动续期；有实际生产率进展和最终竞争力的可信依据时，也不应无视建厂与学习所需时间。世界银行2026年报告建议十年自动到期并设置满足条件的延期途径，这是报告的实践建议，不是所有行业的通用最优期限。续期应说明新期限、待检验的改善和终止条件，使后续决定仍可追责。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Discipline both termination and extension. Past expenditure alone does not justify renewal without credible progress or net-benefit evidence; conversely, credible productivity gains and eventual competitiveness should be assessed against construction and learning time. The 2026 World Bank report proposes ten-year automatic termination with conditional extension as practical guidance, not a universally optimal horizon. Renewal should specify a new horizon, testable improvements and termination conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，支持三年后产量翻倍，但每件质量相同产品的实物投入未改善，不能仅凭扩产就以“完成学习”续期。若试产推迟两年而实际生产一年已有可信效率改善，也不能只凭日历年数断言应停；仍须比较继续支持、修改工具和停止等可行路径的未来增量收益与成本。这里是复核方法的教学对照，不是给任一项目的延期结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, doubled output after three years, with no improvement in physical inputs per quality-equivalent unit, does not alone justify renewal on learning grounds. If production began two years late and one operating year shows credible efficiency gains, elapsed calendar time alone likewise does not settle termination. Compare the future incremental benefits and costs of continuing, changing instruments and stopping. These are teaching contrasts, not renewal decisions for actual projects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本规则针对“学习后可减少依赖”的限期支持。若理由是持续存在的外部性，合适的矫正也可能需要持续；若某项有益制度豁免适合普及，退出特殊待遇还可能通过把改革推广给其他企业来实现。应复核原来的问题和工具，而不是把所有产业政策都机械套成同一种倒计时。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rule concerns time-limited support premised on reduced dependence after learning. Persistent externalities may call for persistent correction, while a beneficial regulatory exemption might be generalized to other firms rather than withdrawn from current recipients. Review the original problem and instrument instead of imposing one countdown on every industrial policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
