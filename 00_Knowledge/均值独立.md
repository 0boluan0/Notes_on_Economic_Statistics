# Mean independence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*均值独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Mean independence** says that learning $X$ does not change the average predicted for $Y$. A [[条件期望|conditional mean]] $E[Y\mid X]$ averages $Y$ using the information supplied by $X$. If this average is the same in every relevant information state, $Y$ is mean independent of $X$. The direction matters: $Y$ is the outcome being averaged; $X$ supplies the information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**均值独立**表示：知道 $X$ 后，对 $Y$ 的平均预测没有改变。[[条件期望|条件均值]] $E[Y\mid X]$ 使用 $X$ 提供的信息来平均 $Y$；如果在所有有意义的信息状态下，这个平均值都相同，就说 $Y$ 对 $X$ 均值独立。方向很重要：被平均的结果是 $Y$，提供信息的是 $X$。

> [!example] The same average wait · 相同的平均等待
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose two routes, $X=0$ and $X=1$, are equally likely. On route $0$, the wait $Y$ is $8$ or $12$ minutes, equally likely. On route $1$, it is $6$ or $14$, equally likely. Both route means are $10$, and the overall mean is also $10$. Knowing the route does not improve the mean forecast: always predict $10$. Yet the second route is more variable; the information can still matter for predicting a long wait.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设线路 $X=0$、$X=1$ 等概率出现。线路 $0$ 的等待时间 $Y$ 等概率为 $8$、$12$ 分钟，线路 $1$ 等概率为 $6$、$14$ 分钟。两条线路的均值均为 $10$，总体均值也为 $10$。知道线路后，均值预测仍然是 $10$；但第二条线路波动更大，线路信息对预测很长的等待仍可能有用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The definition and its direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定义与方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume $Y$ is **integrable**, meaning $E[|Y|]<\infty$, so its average is a finite number. The definition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $Y$ **可积**，即 $E[|Y|]<\infty$，从而平均值是有限数。定义为

$$
E[Y\mid X]=E[Y]\qquad\text{almost surely}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Almost surely” means with probability one. In a discrete model, check each $x$ with $P(X=x)>0$: the group mean $E[Y\mid X=x]$ must equal the overall mean. For continuous $X$, use the conditional-expectation definition; dividing by $P(X=x)=0$ is not a valid calculation. Conditional means on probability-zero information states can have different versions without changing the condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“几乎处处”表示以概率一成立。离散模型中，对每个满足 $P(X=x)>0$ 的 $x$，检查组内均值 $E[Y\mid X=x]$ 是否等于总体均值。连续 $X$ 要使用条件期望的定义，不能除以 $P(X=x)=0$。在概率为零的信息状态上，条件均值可有不同版本，不改变这条性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This definition only needs a finite absolute first moment of **$Y$**; it does not require a mean or variance for $X$. Reversing the question asks whether $E[X\mid Y]=E[X]$, which first requires $X$ to be integrable. Even when both means exist, one direction need not imply the other. The [[均值独立不推独立|complete counterexample]] shows this using a small joint table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一定义只要求 **$Y$** 的绝对一阶矩有限，不要求 $X$ 有均值或方差。反过来问的是 $E[X\mid Y]=E[X]$，首先需要 $X$ 可积；即使两个均值都存在，一个方向也不自动推出另一个方向。[[均值独立不推独立]]用完整的小联合表展示这一点。

## What the restriction leaves open
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*它没有限制什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[随机变量独立|Independence]] keeps the whole conditional distribution unchanged, and therefore implies mean independence when $Y$ is integrable. Mean independence only fixes one average. Conditional spread, tail probabilities and other features can still change, as the route example illustrates. For a zero-one outcome, however, the [[二元结果条件均值|mean already determines its success probability and hence its whole distribution]]; that special case is stronger.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[随机变量独立|独立性]]保持整个条件分布不变，所以在 $Y$ 可积时推出均值独立。均值独立只固定一个平均值，条件波动、尾部概率等仍可改变。零一结果是特殊情况：它的[[二元结果条件均值|均值已经确定成功概率，从而确定整个分布]]，因此这个特殊条件更强。

> [!note] When covariance is available · 协方差存在时
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $X$ and $Y$ have finite second moments, mean independence of $Y$ from $X$ implies zero [[协方差|covariance]]. More generally, integrability of $X$, $Y$ and $XY$ suffices. [[条件期望取出已知量|Taking out the known $X$]] and then using [[全期望公式|total expectation]] gives $E[XY]=E[X E[Y\mid X]]=E[X]E[Y]$. The additional [[矩存在性的使用边界|moment conditions]] make the covariance statement meaningful; they are not part of the definition above.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $X,Y$ 二阶矩有限，$Y$ 对 $X$ 均值独立就推出[[协方差]]为零。更一般地，只要 $X$、$Y$、$XY$ 都可积即可：先[[条件期望取出已知量|提出已知的 $X$]]，再用[[全期望公式]]，得到 $E[XY]=E[X E[Y\mid X]]=E[X]E[Y]$。这些额外的[[矩存在性的使用边界|矩条件]]让协方差结论有意义，并不是上面定义的一部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[独立性强弱关系|implication hierarchy]] records these relationships. The reverse step from zero covariance fails: [[零协方差不推均值独立|cancelling positive and negative product contributions]] can hide a changing conditional mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[独立性强弱关系]]整理这些蕴含关系。零协方差不能反推出均值独立，因为[[零协方差不推均值独立|正负乘积贡献的抵消]]可以掩盖随信息变化的条件均值。
