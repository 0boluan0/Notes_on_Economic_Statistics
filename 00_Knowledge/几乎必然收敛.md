# Almost sure convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*几乎必然收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine watching one complete run of a random experiment: its first approximation, second approximation, and so on without end. **Almost sure convergence** says that, with probability one, the values along that run settle towards the target. The starting point after which they stay close may differ from one run to another.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

想象观察随机试验的一次完整运行：从第一个近似、第二个近似，一直看到无限往后。**几乎必然收敛**说的是：以概率 1，这次运行中的数值会趋近目标。不同运行开始持续接近目标的时刻可以不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To describe a run, let $\omega$ be one outcome in a [[概率空间|probability space]]. Each [[随机变量|random variable]] $X_n$ assigns it a number $X_n(\omega)$. Fixing $\omega$ gives an ordinary sequence of numbers $X_1(\omega),X_2(\omega),\ldots$, called a sample path here. The target is $X(\omega)$ on that same outcome; it need not be a constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为了描述一次运行，用 $\omega$ 表示[[概率空间]]中的一个结果。每个[[随机变量]] $X_n$ 都给它指定数值 $X_n(\omega)$。固定 $\omega$ 后，$X_1(\omega),X_2(\omega),\ldots$ 就是一个普通数列，这里称为一条样本路径。目标是同一结果下的 $X(\omega)$，不一定是常数。

> [!important] Convergence on one probability-one event · 在同一个概率为一的事件上收敛
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For real-valued $X_n$ and $X$ on the same probability space, write $X_n\xrightarrow{a.s.}X$ if
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$P\!\left(\{\omega:\lim_{n\to\infty}X_n(\omega)=X(\omega)\}\right)=1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On this one event, every path satisfies: for each $\varepsilon>0$, there is an index $N(\omega,\varepsilon)$ after which **all** errors are smaller than $\varepsilon$. “Almost everywhere” is the measure-theoretic name for the same idea, here using the probability measure.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在同一概率为 1 的事件上，每条路径都满足：对每个 $\varepsilon>0$，存在依赖该路径和容忍值的 $N(\omega,\varepsilon)$，使得此后**所有**误差都小于 $\varepsilon$。测度论中称为“几乎处处”，这里使用的是概率测度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The probability-one condition permits exceptional outcomes with total probability zero. It does not mean that convergence holds for every outcome, and it does not mean merely a very high probability such as $0.999$. It also imposes no independence requirement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

概率为 1 允许总概率为零的异常结果存在；它既不等于对每个结果都成立，也不等于 $0.999$ 这样非常高但小于 1 的概率。定义同样不要求独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One draw, many approximations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $\Omega=[0,1]$ with the [[连续均匀分布|uniform probability]] and let $U(\omega)=\omega$: draw once and reuse that number throughout the sequence. Define the [[事件指示变量|indicator]]
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $\Omega=[0,1]$，赋予[[连续均匀分布|均匀概率]]，并令 $U(\omega)=\omega$：只抽一次，此后始终使用这个数。定义[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]

$$X_n=\mathbf1\{U\le1/n\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the draw is $U=0.3$, the sequence begins $1,1,1,0,0,\ldots$ and stays zero from $n=4$ onwards. In general, for every $U>0$, choose an integer $N>1/U$. Then $n\ge N$ gives $1/n<U$ and hence $X_n=0$. At the exceptional outcome $U=0$, however, the sequence is constantly one. Since $P(U=0)=0$, we have $X_n\xrightarrow{a.s.}0$ despite that exception. There is no single finite $N$ making all outcomes $U>0$ zero: arbitrarily small positive draws need longer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若抽到 $U=0.3$，序列从 $1,1,1,0,0,\ldots$ 开始，从 $n=4$ 起一直为零。一般地，对每个 $U>0$，选整数 $N>1/U$，则 $n\ge N$ 时 $1/n<U$，所以 $X_n=0$。但在异常结果 $U=0$ 上，序列始终为 1。由于 $P(U=0)=0$，尽管存在这一例外，仍有 $X_n\xrightarrow{a.s.}0$。不存在一个有限的共同 $N$，让所有 $U>0$ 从此都变成零：任意接近零的正数需要等得更久。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example eventually becomes exactly equal to its target, but the definition requires only approaching it: $Y_n=U+1/n$ converges to $U$ on every path while never equalling it. This is stronger than controlling the error probability at one index at a time; [[几乎必然收敛推出概率收敛|the implication to convergence in probability]] explains why, and gives the moving-interval counterexample to the converse. A [[强大数定律|strong law of large numbers]] applies this pathwise meaning to sample averages.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上例最终会精确等于目标，但定义只要求趋近：$Y_n=U+1/n$ 在每条路径上都收敛到 $U$，却从不等于它。这比逐个索引控制误差概率更强；[[几乎必然收敛推出概率收敛|相应的蕴含关系]]解释了原因，并给出移动区间构成的反向反例。[[强大数定律]]把这种逐路径的收敛用于样本均值。

> [!warning] A path limit does not control large rare errors · 路径极限不控制罕见大误差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Reusing the same $U$, let $Z_n=n\mathbf1\{U\le1/n\}$. Every $U>0$ again eventually gives zero, so $Z_n\to0$ almost surely. Yet $E[Z_n]=n(1/n)=1$ and $E[Z_n^2]=n^2(1/n)=n$. The [[期望|expectations]] do not approach zero, nor is there [[均方收敛|mean-square convergence]]. Pathwise convergence alone cannot justify exchanging a limit with an expectation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 仍用同一个 $U$，令 $Z_n=n\mathbf1\{U\le1/n\}$。每个 $U>0$ 最终也会给出零，所以 $Z_n$ 几乎必然收敛到零。但 $E[Z_n]=n(1/n)=1$，$E[Z_n^2]=n^2(1/n)=n$。[[期望]]没有趋于零，也没有[[均方收敛]]；仅凭路径极限，不能交换极限与期望。
