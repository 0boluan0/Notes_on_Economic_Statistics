# 公开市场操作是中央银行通过市场证券交易实施货币政策的工具
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Open-market operations are central-bank securities transactions used to implement monetary policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

央行在市场买卖证券，或进行带有约定反向交易的回购操作，可以改变准备金供给和相关市场条件。交易可能是永久性的买断或卖断，也可能是到期反转的临时操作。先看交易的期限与结算，再判断它在操作框架中的作用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A central bank can buy or sell securities outright or transact with an agreed reversal, affecting reserve supply and market conditions. Outright operations are permanent transactions; repos reverse at maturity. Identify settlement and maturity before interpreting their policy role.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准、没有同步抵销的购券例子中，央行买入100的证券并用准备金结算，证券资产和准备金负债各增加100。向银行卖出100则可作相反方向。非银行卖方的存款经过哪家银行增加，直接复用[[央行购债结算链]]，不要把交易金额直接当成新增银行贷款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a standard unoffset purchase settled in reserves, buying 100 of securities increases central-bank securities and reserve liabilities by 100. An outright sale to a bank can reverse these entries. For a non-bank seller's deposit and settlement chain, reuse [[央行购债结算链|central-bank purchase settlement]], not a presumed equal increase in bank loans.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在稀缺准备金模型中，增加供给可压低隔夜市场利率；在[[充足准备金框架]]的平坦需求区间，同样规模的数量调整可能几乎不改变利率。操作也可抵销现金需求或其他央行负债变化，以维持原操作目标。因此“做了公开市场购买”还不足以判断[[准备金数量与政策立场|政策立场]]。旨在压低较长期融资成本的大规模购买另见[[量化宽松]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a scarce-reserves model, more supply can lower the overnight rate; on the flat segment in an [[充足准备金框架|ample-reserves framework]], a similar adjustment may barely move it. Operations can also offset currency demand or other liability changes to maintain the existing target. Purchases alone therefore do not identify the [[准备金数量与政策立场|policy stance]]. See [[量化宽松|quantitative easing]] for large-scale purchases aimed at longer-term conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
