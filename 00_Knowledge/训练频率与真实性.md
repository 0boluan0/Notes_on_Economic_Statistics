# 内容在预训练分布中高频出现，只表示模型获得更多相关预测暴露，不能证明相应命题为真
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*High frequency in the pretraining distribution means more relevant prediction exposure, not that the corresponding claim is true*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 解释边界
> [[自回归目标|Next-token 训练]]把文本当作待预测的观测序列，而不是经过事实裁决的命题集合。一个说法出现得更频繁，通常只意味着模型有更多机会因预测相关 token 而得到梯度更新；错误、广告模板和协调转载同样可以高频。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [[自回归目标|Next-token training]] treats text as observed sequences to predict, not as propositions that have passed a truth tribunal. A more frequent statement usually gives the model more opportunities to receive gradient updates from its tokens; errors, advertising templates, and coordinated republication can be frequent too.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从频率能推出什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What frequency can establish*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若上下文模式 $c$ 在 [[预训练分布]] 中具有较大概率质量，训练过程就会更常在 $c$ 附近计算损失。模型是否最终复现该模式，还受容量、优化、相邻证据和提示影响，因此“高频”甚至不保证逐字输出；它更不提供外部世界的真实性证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a context pattern $c$ has greater mass in the [[预训练分布]], training evaluates loss near $c$ more often. Whether the model later reproduces it also depends on capacity, optimization, neighboring evidence, and prompting, so frequency does not even guarantee verbatim output. It certainly does not supply evidence about the external world's truth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

真实性必须另查来源、测量和可复核证据。模型概率可以帮助发现“某种表述在训练文本中常见”，却不能把流行度变成事实性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Truth requires separate sources, measurements, and auditable evidence. Model probability may reveal that a formulation is common in training text, but it cannot turn popularity into factuality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 最小反例
> 某错误健康说法被大量网站转载。模型能稳定续写它，可能正是因为训练成功拟合了重复模式；这既不表示模型没有学到分布，也不表示该说法为真。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A false health claim is copied across many websites. A model's reliable continuation may reflect successful fitting of the repeated pattern. It shows neither failure to learn the distribution nor truth of the claim.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个说法在训练集中出现十万次，模型也给它很高概率。现有证据能否判定它为真？
>
> **答案：** 不能。现有证据只描述训练暴露和模型概率；还需要独立事实来源或测量。

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Lin, Hilton, and Evans (2022), *TruthfulQA*](https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.229/)：用常见误解构造问题，并观察到模型会模仿人类文本中的流行错误，直接支持“分布频率与真实性必须分开”。
- [Brown et al. (2020), *Language Models are Few-Shot Learners*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165)：第 2.2 节展示训练样本频率由过滤与混合决定，而 next-token 目标并不包含外部事实验证。
