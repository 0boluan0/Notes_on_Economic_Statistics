# The segment criterion for quasiconcavity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拟凹性线段判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[拟凹函数|quasiconcavity]] test can be expressed without drawing every upper contour set: along the straight segment between any two inputs, the function must never fall below the **smaller endpoint value**. For example, if the endpoints give values 2 and 5, every mixture must give at least 2; it need not give at least 5.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[拟凹函数|拟凹性]]可以不用逐个画出上轮廓集来检验：任意两个输入之间的整条线段上，函数值都不能低于**较小的端点值**。例如两端值为 2 和 5，则每个混合点的值至少为 2，不必至少为 5。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The domain and the comparison
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先确定定义域与比较对象*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f:U\to\mathbb R$, where $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is a [[凸集|convex set]] and $n\ge1$ is an integer. Convexity of the domain means that $z=tx+(1-t)y$ remains in $U$ for every $x,y\in U$ and $t\in[0,1]$. Here $t$ is the weight on $x$, $1-t$ the weight on $y$, and $\min\{f(x),f(y)\}$ selects the smaller of their two real values. Under these assumptions, the following is an equivalence. The quantifier over $x,y$ ranges over $U$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f:U\to\mathbb R$，其中 $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 为[[凸集]]，$n\ge1$ 为整数。凸域保证任意 $x,y\in U$、$t\in[0,1]$ 的混合点 $z=tx+(1-t)y$ 仍在 $U$ 中。$t$ 是 $x$ 的权重，$1-t$ 是 $y$ 的权重；$\min\{f(x),f(y)\}$ 选出两个实数输出中较小的一个。以下等价式中的 $x,y$ 都取自 $U$。

对凸域上的实函数，
$$
f\text{ 拟凹}\iff f(tx+(1-t)y)\ge\min\{f(x),f(y)\}\quad\forall x,y,\ t\in[0,1].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The function need not be continuous or differentiable; $U$ need not be open or bounded. At $t=0$ or $t=1$, or when $x=y$, the inequality is automatic. Empty and one-point domains also satisfy the same convention: an empty domain has no pair to test, and a one-point domain has only constant mixtures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不要求函数连续或可微，也不要求 $U$ 开或有界。$t=0$、$t=1$ 或 $x=y$ 时，不等式自动成立。空域与单点域也符合相同约定：空域没有点对可检验，单点域的混合始终返回同一点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the two statements imply each other
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两种表述为什么互相推出*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First suppose $f$ is quasiconcave. Its [[上轮廓集|upper contour set]] at a real threshold $k$ is $C_k^+=\{u\in U:f(u)\ge k\}$, the inputs meeting that threshold. Fix any $x,y\in U$ and choose $k=\min\{f(x),f(y)\}$. Both endpoints meet this threshold, so they belong to $C_k^+$. Quasiconcavity makes this set convex; consequently every $z=tx+(1-t)y$ belongs to it and $f(z)\ge k$. This is the segment inequality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先假设 $f$ 拟凹。实数门槛 $k$ 对应的[[上轮廓集]]为 $C_k^+=\{u\in U:f(u)\ge k\}$，即所有达到该门槛的输入。固定任意 $x,y\in U$，取 $k=\min\{f(x),f(y)\}$，则两端都达到门槛，所以都在 $C_k^+$ 中。拟凹性保证该集合凸，故每个 $z=tx+(1-t)y$ 都在其中，即 $f(z)\ge k$，这正是线段不等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, assume the inequality holds for every pair and weight. Now fix an arbitrary real $k$ and any $x,y\in C_k^+$. Their values are both at least $k$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，假设任意点对和权重都满足线段不等式。固定任意实数 $k$，再取任意 $x,y\in C_k^+$。两端值都至少为 $k$，因此

$$f(tx+(1-t)y)\ge\min\{f(x),f(y)\}\ge k.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus every mixture remains in $C_k^+$, proving that this upper contour set is convex. If it is empty, convexity is vacuous. Since $k$ was arbitrary, every upper contour set is convex, which is exactly quasiconcavity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以每个混合点仍在 $C_k^+$ 中，证明该上轮廓集凸。若集合为空，凸性自动成立。由于 $k$ 任意，所有上轮廓集都凸，这恰好就是拟凹性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose the lower endpoint value as a threshold to derive the segment inequality from convex [[上轮廓集|upper sets]]. Conversely, the inequality preserves every common threshold between its qualifying endpoints. It compares against the worse endpoint, unlike the weighted-average comparison of [[凹凸性|concavity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

必要性取门槛 $k=\min\{f(x),f(y)\}$，由[[上轮廓集]]凸得到。充分性固定任意门槛，若两端都至少为 $k$，不等式使全部混合也至少为 $k$。

## Using the test and recognizing its strength
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*怎样使用，以及它有多强*

> [!example] One failed pair is decisive · 一对失败点就足够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^2$ on $\mathbb R$, take $x=-1$, $y=1$ and $t=1/2$. Both endpoint values are 1, but the midpoint value is 0. Since $0<\min\{1,1\}=1$, this function is not quasiconcave on the real line. Its upper set at threshold 1 contains both endpoints but omits the midpoint.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对实线上的 $f(x)=x^2$，取 $x=-1$、$y=1$、$t=1/2$。两端值都为 1，中点值却为 0。因为 $0<\min\{1,1\}=1$，它在实线上不拟凹。门槛 1 的上轮廓集包含两端，却遗漏中点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[凹凸性|Concavity]] would require the larger lower bound $tf(x)+(1-t)f(y)$. That weighted average is always at least the smaller endpoint value, so concavity implies this criterion. The reverse implication fails: [[一维单调函数的拟凹拟凸性|the monotone cubic]] passes the minimum-endpoint test, but at inputs 0 and 2 its midpoint value 1 is below the chord height 4.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[凹凸性|凹性]]要求更强的下界 $tf(x)+(1-t)f(y)$。这个加权平均总是不小于较小端点值，因此凹性推出本判据。反向不成立：[[一维单调函数的拟凹拟凸性|单调三次函数]]符合最小端点判据，但输入 0 与 2 的中点值为 1，低于弦的高度 4。

它只与较差的端点比较，不要求超过较好的端点或两端的加权平均。后者是更强的[[凹凸性|凹性]]要求。
