# 凸域上的函数拟凹当且仅当线段上的值不低于两个端点值的较小者

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A function on a convex domain is quasiconcave exactly when its value on each segment is at least the smaller endpoint value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对凸域上的实函数，
$$
f\text{ 拟凹}\iff f(tx+(1-t)y)\ge\min\{f(x),f(y)\}\quad\forall x,y,\ t\in[0,1].
$$
必要性取门槛 $k=\min\{f(x),f(y)\}$，由[[上轮廓集]]凸得到。充分性固定任意门槛，若两端都至少为 $k$，不等式使全部混合也至少为 $k$。

它只与较差的端点比较，不要求超过较好的端点或两端的加权平均。后者是更强的[[凹凸性|凹性]]要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose the lower endpoint value as a threshold to derive the segment inequality from convex [[上轮廓集|upper sets]]. Conversely, the inequality preserves every common threshold between its qualifying endpoints. It compares against the worse endpoint, unlike the weighted-average comparison of [[凹凸性|concavity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
