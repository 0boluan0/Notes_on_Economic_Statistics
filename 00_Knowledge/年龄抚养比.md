# 年龄抚养比是指定非劳动年龄人口相对于劳动年龄人口的数量比

年龄抚养比按年龄分组比较人口数量。采用世界银行 WDI 的口径，总年龄抚养比是 0–14 岁与 65 岁及以上人口之和，除以 15–64 岁人口，再乘以 100；结果表示每 100 名劳动年龄人口对应多少名其他年龄人口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The age dependency ratio compares population counts by age. Under the World Bank WDI convention, total dependency equals people aged 0–14 plus those aged 65 and over, divided by people aged 15–64 and multiplied by 100. It reports other-age persons per 100 working-age persons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
D_{\rm age}=100\frac{N_{0\text{–}14}+N_{65+}}{N_{15\text{–}64}}.
$$

用相同分母只保留年轻或老年人口，就是年轻或老年分项，两者相加等于总比。它不同于老年人口占总人口的比例。比较不同来源时先核年龄界限；15–64 岁是本指标的统计约定，不是所有地区的法定就业或退休界限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keeping only the younger or older population in the numerator gives the corresponding component, with the same denominator; the components sum to total dependency. The old-age component differs from older people’s share of total population. Age boundaries must be aligned across sources: 15–64 is this indicator’s convention, not a universal legal employment or retirement range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若非劳动年龄人口与劳动年龄人口从 $40/60$ 变为 $30/70$，总比从约 66.7 降至 42.9。这一变化仅由人口构成决定，不必等到教育或就业改善才成立；但它不能证明已经实现[[人口红利]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If other-age and working-age populations change from $40/60$ to $30/70$, the total ratio falls from about 66.7 to 42.9. This arithmetic change does not wait for improvements in education or employment, but it does not establish a realized [[人口红利|demographic dividend]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“抚养”只是年龄代理：有些老年人仍工作，部分劳动年龄人口没有就业。真实生产与消费的年龄模式见[[经济供养比]]；税收与公共给付的年龄模式见[[财政供养比]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Dependency” is an age proxy: some older people work and some working-age people do not. For age patterns of production and consumption, see the [[经济供养比|economic support ratio]]; for taxes and public benefits, see the [[财政供养比|fiscal support ratio]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
