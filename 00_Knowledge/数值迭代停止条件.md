# 数值迭代的停止条件应绑定误差指标并保留失败出口

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Numerical stopping criteria should bind an error indicator and retain a failure exit*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子决策规则
> 数值迭代应在开始前规定“什么证据表示已经足够好”：例如残差、区间宽度、目标函数变化或有理论依据的步长界，并把阈值与问题单位相连。还必须设置迭代上限、越界或非有限值等失败出口；程序输出一个数不等于它找到了可信答案。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Before a numerical iteration begins, define what evidence counts as good enough: for example, a residual, interval width, objective change, or theoretically justified step bound, with a threshold tied to problem units. Also retain failure exits for iteration limits, domain escape, or non-finite values. Printing a number does not mean that a trustworthy answer was found.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只等 `new_guess == old_guess` 不足以定义收敛：迭代可能在相邻浮点值之间循环，也可能因精度耗尽而停滞在错误位置。反过来，只用 `math.isclose(new_guess, old_guess)` 也未必足够；若问题病态或更新公式失效，步长很小而残差仍可很大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Waiting only for `new_guess == old_guess` does not define convergence: an iteration may cycle between adjacent floats or stagnate at the wrong point after exhausting precision. Conversely, `math.isclose(new_guess, old_guess)` alone may also be insufficient; on an ill-conditioned problem or failed update formula, the step can be tiny while the residual remains large.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对求根，停止证据必须与算法能够保证的量对齐：[[求根残差|残差]] $|f(x_k)|$ 衡量方程缺口，只有满足 [[残差控制根误差|额外转换条件]] 才能给出位置界；[[二分误差界|有效括区间的宽度]] 可以直接给出位置证书；Newton 的小步长或连续数位不变还可能只是停滞，必须结合 [[Newton失效边界|导数、有限值与失败机制]] 判断。[[固定步长求根|固定步长扫描]] 耗尽网格后的结论受 [[固定步长漏根|网格漏根边界]] 限制：仅缩小残差容差不会让网格自动命中。所有方法还要设置最大迭代数和明确失败状态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For root finding, stopping evidence must match what the algorithm can certify. A [[求根残差|residual]] $|f(x_k)|$ measures equation defect and yields a positional bound only under the [[残差控制根误差|additional conversion conditions]]; the width of a [[二分误差界|valid bracket]] can directly certify position; and a small Newton step or unchanged displayed digits may merely signal stagnation, so they must be interpreted with [[Newton失效边界|derivative, finite-value, and failure checks]]. Exhausting a [[固定步长求根|fixed-step scan]] is limited by the [[固定步长漏根|grid-miss boundary]]: tightening the residual tolerance does not make the grid hit the acceptance region. Every method also needs an iteration limit and an explicit failure state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> Newton 迭代中相邻两次 `x` 完全相等，为什么仍要检查残差和失败状态？
>
> **答案：** 相等可能只是舍入导致无法继续移动；若 $|f(x)|$ 仍大，它是停滞而不是成功收敛。
