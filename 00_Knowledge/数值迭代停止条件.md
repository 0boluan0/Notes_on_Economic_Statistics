# Stopping numerical iterations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*数值迭代的停止条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An iterative algorithm repeatedly updates a candidate in an attempt to solve a problem. A **stopping rule** says when to stop calculating and what conclusion the available evidence permits. These are different questions: reaching a work limit stops the computation, while satisfying a justified accuracy target can support a successful result. A stopped loop need not have solved its mathematical problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

迭代算法反复更新候选，尝试解决一个问题。**停止条件**规定何时结束计算，以及已有证据允许得出什么结论。这是两个不同的问题：工作上限可以让计算结束，而满足有依据的精度目标才能支持成功结果；循环停下并不等于数学问题已经解决。

> [!important] Choose the evidence before the loop · 先确定证据，再开始迭代
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Before a numerical iteration begins, define what evidence counts as good enough: for example, a residual, interval width, objective change, or theoretically justified step bound, with a threshold tied to problem units. Also retain failure exits for iteration limits, domain escape, or non-finite values. Printing a number does not mean that a trustworthy answer was found.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 数值迭代应在开始前规定“什么证据表示已经足够好”：例如残差、区间宽度、目标函数变化或有理论依据的步长界，并把阈值与问题单位相连。还必须设置迭代上限、越界或非有限值等失败出口；程序输出一个数不等于它找到了可信答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A tolerance is an allowed discrepancy, expressed in the units of a stated quantity. [[浮点容差比较|Absolute and relative comparisons]] tell you how two numbers are compared; they do not choose the quantity that matters or prove it bounds the unknown error. In particular, a small objective change can be a practical termination signal, but by itself is not proof of proximity to an optimizer or of a small optimality gap.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

容差是某个指定量允许的差异，必须带着该量的单位来理解。[[浮点容差比较|绝对与相对容差比较]]说明怎样比较两个数，却不会替你选择应关心哪个量，也不会自动证明它能控制未知误差。尤其是，目标函数变化很小可以作为实际停止信号，但它本身不证明接近最优解，也不证明距离最优值的差距很小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A small step may be stagnation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*步长很小也可能只是停滞*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Waiting only for `new_guess == old_guess` does not define convergence: an iteration may cycle between adjacent floats or stagnate at the wrong point after exhausting precision. Conversely, `math.isclose(new_guess, old_guess)` alone may also be insufficient; on an ill-conditioned problem or failed update formula, the step can be tiny while the residual remains large.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只等 `new_guess == old_guess` 不足以定义收敛：迭代可能在相邻浮点值之间循环，也可能因精度耗尽而停滞在错误位置。反过来，只用 `math.isclose(new_guess, old_guess)` 也未必足够；若问题病态或更新公式失效，步长很小而残差仍可很大。

> [!example] No movement while the equation is still wrong · 没有移动，但方程仍未满足
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose the problem is $f(x)=x^2-2=0$ and an update proposes $x_{k+1}=1+10^{-20}$ from $x_k=1$. Under usual binary64 rounding, both stored values equal 1. The computed step is zero and an equality or closeness check passes, but the residual is still $|1^2-2|=1$. The loop can report stagnation; it has no evidence of an accurate root.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设要求解 $f(x)=x^2-2=0$，某次更新从 $x_k=1$ 提出 $x_{k+1}=1+10^{-20}$。在通常的 binary64 舍入下，两个存储值都等于 1。计算步长为零，相等或接近检查会通过，但残差仍是 $|1^2-2|=1$。循环可以报告停滞，却没有得到根准确的证据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Likewise, for minimising $F(x)=(x-10)^2$, moving from 0 to a tiny positive $h$ changes the objective by $F(h)-F(0)=-20h+h^2$, which can be arbitrarily small. The new point remains close to 10 units from the minimiser. A step size, a change in objective and a mathematical error bound must therefore be interpreted separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，对最小化 $F(x)=(x-10)^2$，从 0 移到很小的正数 $h$ 时，目标变化为 $F(h)-F(0)=-20h+h^2$，可以任意小，但新点离最小点仍接近 10 个单位。因此，步长、目标变化以及数学误差界必须分别解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the evidence actually certifies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*证据究竟保证什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For root finding, stopping evidence must match what the algorithm can certify. A [[求根残差|residual]] $|f(x_k)|$ measures equation defect and yields a positional bound only under the [[残差控制根误差|additional conversion conditions]]; the width of a [[二分误差界|valid bracket]] can directly certify position; and a small Newton step or unchanged displayed digits may merely signal stagnation, so they must be interpreted with [[Newton失效边界|derivative, finite-value, and failure checks]]. Exhausting a [[固定步长求根|fixed-step scan]] is limited by the [[固定步长漏根|grid-miss boundary]]: tightening the residual tolerance does not make the grid hit the acceptance region. Every method also needs an iteration limit and an explicit failure state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对求根，停止证据必须与算法能够保证的量对齐：[[求根残差|残差]] $|f(x_k)|$ 衡量方程缺口，只有满足 [[残差控制根误差|额外转换条件]] 才能给出位置界；[[二分误差界|有效括区间的宽度]] 可以直接给出位置证书；Newton 的小步长或连续数位不变还可能只是停滞，必须结合 [[Newton失效边界|导数、有限值与失败机制]] 判断。[[固定步长求根|固定步长扫描]] 耗尽网格后的结论受 [[固定步长漏根|网格漏根边界]] 限制：仅缩小残差容差不会让网格自动命中。所有方法还要设置最大迭代数和明确失败状态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A concrete comparison helps. For $f(x)=x^2-2$ and candidate 1.4, a residual target of 0.05 is met because $|f(1.4)|=0.04$. If the actual requirement is a position error at most 0.01, this residual test is insufficient: the verified interval $[1,2]$ and lower derivative bound 2 only give a bound of 0.02. The same candidate can pass one specification and fail to establish another. The [[残差控制根误差|worked conversion example]] shows how candidate 1.414 instead obtains a position bound below 0.001.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看一个具体比较：对 $f(x)=x^2-2$ 和候选 1.4，因为 $|f(1.4)|=0.04$，残差目标 0.05 已经满足。但若实际要求位置误差至多 0.01，这个残差检查还不够：已验证的区间 $[1,2]$ 与导数下界 2 只能给出 0.02 的界。同一个候选可以满足一种规格，却不能据此确认另一种规格。[[残差控制根误差|换算例子]]进一步说明，候选 1.414 怎样得到低于 0.001 的位置界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a sign-changing bracket $[a,b]$ of a continuous function, returning its midpoint gives a root-distance bound $(b-a)/2$ in exact arithmetic. Merely returning any point inside it gives the weaker bound $b-a$. The [[二分误差界|bisection error bound]] states the counting and arithmetic assumptions. A computed residual also needs an evaluation-error allowance if the requested output is a rigorous mathematical certificate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对连续函数的异号括区间 $[a,b]$，在精确算术下，返回中点给出的根距离界为 $(b-a)/2$；若只知道返回点在区间内，则一般只能给出较弱的界 $b-a$。[[二分误差界]]列出了计数与算术前提。若要求输出严格的数学误差保证，计算残差还需要计入函数求值误差。

## Preserve why the iteration stopped
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*保留停止的原因*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful result retains the candidate, the tested quantity and tolerance, and the reason for termination. First check that evaluations and assumptions needed by the acceptance test are valid. Then report success only for the criterion actually met. If that criterion is not met, a work limit, a domain violation, a non-finite evaluation or stagnation is an exit without the requested certificate. Such an exit does not prove that the mathematical problem has no solution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有用的结果应保留候选值、实际检查的量和容差，以及停止原因。先确认验收所需的求值与条件有效，再只针对真正满足的判据报告成功。如果判据未满足，达到工作上限、越出定义域、求值得到非有限值或发生停滞，都只是未获得所需保证的退出；这些退出不证明数学问题无解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, “candidate 1.4, absolute residual 0.04, residual tolerance 0.05 met” is a specific result. “Candidate 1.4 after the iteration cap; position target not certified” is a different result even though the printed candidate is identical. A library’s `converged` flag has that library method’s stopping meaning; inspect its documented criterion and termination reason rather than treating the field name as an independent mathematical theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，“候选 1.4，绝对残差 0.04，满足残差容差 0.05”是明确的结果；“迭代达到上限后留下候选 1.4，尚未确认位置目标”则是另一种结果，尽管打印出的候选相同。库函数的 `converged` 字段依赖该方法的停止语义；应查看其文档规定的判据与终止原因，不能把字段名称当成独立的数学定理。

> [!question]- Interpret an unchanged Newton iterate · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In Newton iteration, why should the residual and failure status still be checked when two successive values of `x` are exactly equal?
>
> **Answer:** Rounding may prevent further movement. If $|f(x)|$ is still large, the equality signals stagnation rather than a successful root certificate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> Newton 迭代中相邻两次 `x` 完全相等，为什么仍要检查残差和失败状态？
>
> **答案：** 相等可能只是舍入导致无法继续移动；若 $|f(x)|$ 仍大，它是停滞而不是成功收敛。
