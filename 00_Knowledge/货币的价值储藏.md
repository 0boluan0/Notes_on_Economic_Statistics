# 价值储藏跨期转移购买力但不保证实际价值稳定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A store of value transfers purchasing power through time but does not guarantee stable real value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 货币的价值储藏功能是把今天获得的购买能力保留到以后使用。它通常能维持名义面值，却不保证扣除通胀后的实际购买力，也不保证没有发行人、账户访问或制度风险。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Money's store-of-value function carries purchasing capacity from receipt today to use later. It normally preserves nominal face value, but it does not guarantee purchasing power after inflation or eliminate issuer, access, and institutional risk.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“能储值”是程度命题。低而稳定的通胀下，货币适合短期支付缓冲；高通胀会迅速侵蚀实际购买力。股票、房屋或黄金可能长期保值更好，却因价格波动和变现摩擦不一定更适合作为日常货币。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Serving as a store of value is a matter of degree. Under low and stable inflation, money is useful as a short-term payment buffer; high inflation can rapidly erode its real purchasing power. Shares, property, or gold may preserve long-run value better, yet price volatility and conversion frictions can make them less suitable as everyday money.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 面值稳定不等于实际购买力稳定。
- “价值储藏”不等于“提供正投资回报”。
- 一项资产可能是较好的长期储值工具，却不是按面值即时可支付的货币。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Stable face value is not stable real purchasing power.
- A store of value need not provide a positive investment return.
- An asset may preserve long-run value well without being money that settles immediately at par.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 现金一年后仍是 100 元，为什么不能仅凭这一点说其价值完全没有变化？
>
> **答案：** 100 元的名义面值未变，但若物价上涨，它能购买的商品和服务减少，实际购买力已经下降。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Cash still has a face value of 100 one year later. Why does that not prove its value was unchanged?
>
> **Answer:** Its nominal face value is unchanged, but if prices rose it buys fewer goods and services, so real purchasing power fell.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
