# 规范标准不能只由数据决定但规范论证中的事实前提仍须接受检验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Data alone do not determine a normative criterion, but a normative argument's factual premises remain open to testing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“我们愿意为帮助低收入者付出多少资源”包含价值选择；“某项方案实际上帮助了谁、花了多少资源”包含经验判断。后一部分即使出现在[[规范分析|规范论证]]里，也必须有与所作判断相适应的模型或证据；若声称是政策造成的变化，还须说明[[因果研究设计检查|反事实与识别依据]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“How many resources are we willing to devote to helping low-income people?” involves a value choice. “Whom did this programme actually help, and at what resource cost?” involves empirical claims. Within a [[规范分析|normative argument]], those claims still require appropriate models or evidence. Claims that the policy caused a change additionally need a [[因果研究设计检查|counterfactual and identification strategy]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定评价规则也可以作定量比较。例如，把某政策的两人效用变化写成 $(-1,+2)$，并事先采用同一口径的 $W=u_1+u_2$，便得到 $\Delta W=1$。这是一项条件计算：效用变化是否估对、效用尺度能否这样比较、为何采用等权相加，分别是需要回答的问题。数字不会消除规范选择，规范选择也不会使计算失去意义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A specified criterion permits quantitative comparison. If a policy changes two people's utilities by $(-1,+2)$ and the adopted, consistently scaled criterion is $W=u_1+u_2$, then $\Delta W=1$. This is a conditional calculation: whether the changes are estimated correctly, whether the scales permit this comparison, and why equal weighting is chosen are separate questions. Numbers do not eliminate the normative choice, and the normative choice does not make calculation meaningless.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，遇到结论分歧，先拆成“对后果的判断不同”与“对同一后果的评价不同”。前者可进一步研究；后者需要把[[社会福利函数|福利标准与权重]]公开讨论，而不把它伪装成纯粹的数据结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When conclusions differ, separate disagreement about consequences from disagreement about how to value the same consequences. The former invites further empirical inquiry; the latter calls for an explicit discussion of [[社会福利函数|welfare criteria and weights]], rather than presenting the choice as a purely data-driven result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
