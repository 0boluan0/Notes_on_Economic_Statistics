# 限制进入和持续限量供给可以让 Smith 的市场价格长期高于自然价格
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Restricted entry and sustained undersupply can keep Smith's market price above natural price for long periods*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价格高于[[Smith自然价格|自然价格]]以后，能否吸引更多供给，是 Smith 的[[自然价格的趋向机制|价格回归机制]]能否运行的关键。如果少数经营者拥有排他权，潜在竞争者不能进入，它们又把供给维持在不足以满足有效需求的水平，高价格和超常报酬就可能长期保留。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether a price above [[Smith自然价格|natural price]] attracts additional supply is central to Smith's [[自然价格的趋向机制|gravitation mechanism]]. If a few sellers possess exclusive rights, potential rivals cannot enter, and supply remains below effectual demand, high prices and extraordinary returns may persist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Smith 不只举独占贸易公司的例子，也讨论行会特权、学徒法规等限制职业进入的制度。它们程度不同，却可能阻断同一个环节：看到某种工作报酬高，并不意味着任何人都能进入、扩大供应，把报酬差异压平。只画“高利润 → 新进入”一根箭头，会漏掉这种制度约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Smith discusses not only exclusive trading companies but also corporate privileges and apprenticeship rules that restrict entry into occupations. These arrangements differ in strength but can obstruct the same step: observing high returns does not mean anyone can enter, increase supply, and narrow the gap. An arrow from high profits straight to entry would omit the institutional constraint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

类似的持久偏离还可能来自商业或制造秘密，以及特别稀少的土壤、产地条件。它们不全是法律赋予的垄断权，但都说明新增供给不一定能跟上需求。例如，增加普通葡萄园的面积，未必能复制某个稀有产地的酒。这些情况也解释了为什么“长期偏高”不能一概当成很快会消失的偶然波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Persistent deviations may also arise from commercial or manufacturing secrets, or exceptionally scarce soil and location. These are not all legally granted monopolies, but each shows why additional supply may fail to catch up with demand. Expanding ordinary vineyards, for example, need not reproduce wine from a distinctive scarce location. A lasting premium is therefore not necessarily a temporary accident that will soon disappear.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的比较基准仍是 Smith 的**通常报酬价格**，不能直接替换成现代边际成本。现代模型还要明确需求、成本、价格与销量的联动，再研究[[垄断利润最大化|垄断者选择哪个价格—数量组合]]。Smith 关于垄断者尽量索取高价的说法，也不意味着现代最优垄断价必然等于最高一位买者的愿付价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The benchmark here remains Smith's **price sustaining ordinary returns**, not modern marginal cost. A modern model must specify demand, costs, and the relation between price and sales before analysing [[垄断利润最大化|the monopolist's chosen price–quantity pair]]. Smith's description of monopolists obtaining a high price likewise does not imply that the optimal monopoly price in a modern model equals the highest individual willingness to pay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
