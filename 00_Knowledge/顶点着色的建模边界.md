# 普通顶点着色只处理成对冲突而容量时长与偏好需要另建模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Ordinary vertex coloring represents pairwise conflicts; capacity, duration, and preference require additional modeling*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 普通 vertex coloring 只保证：凡是已经用 edge 表达的成对冲突，其两个端点不会得到同一种颜色。它不会自行知道一个时段能容纳多少对象、任务持续多久、资源是否有不同类型，也不会比较不同可行方案的成本或偏好。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Ordinary vertex coloring guarantees only that the endpoints of every represented pairwise conflict receive different colors. It does not know slot capacities, task durations, resource types, costs, or preferences unless those features are modeled separately.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，把考试颜色解释为时段时，proper coloring 可以防止同一学生同时考试，却不能保证某个时段有足够教室，也不能表达三小时考试跨越两个一小时区间。若“同一天连续两场”也不允许，必须把这一规则转成额外冲突或改用更丰富的 scheduling model；不能指望色数公式替现实补上缺失约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For exams, a proper coloring can prevent a student from sitting two exams simultaneously, but it cannot ensure adequate room capacity or represent a three-hour exam spanning two one-hour intervals. If consecutive exams on one day are also forbidden, that rule must become additional conflicts or enter a richer scheduling model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三个相近问题尤其容易混淆：

- **vertex coloring** 给顶点分组，使相邻顶点异色；
- **edge coloring** 给边分组，使共享端点的边异色；
- **matching** 从候选边中选出端点互不重复的一部分。

它们都可能被口语称为“处理冲突”，但决策对象与输出完全不同。先问“我要给什么东西分组，还是要选择哪些关系”，往往比先套算法更重要。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Vertex coloring groups vertices so adjacent vertices differ; edge coloring groups edges so incident edges differ; matching selects a subset of candidate edges with no shared endpoints. They may all be described informally as handling conflict, but their decision objects and outputs are different. First decide what is being grouped or selected.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

地图着色也先依赖建模：区域是顶点，哪些接触算 adjacency 必须由应用定义。若只在一个点接触是否算相邻没有预先规定，图论定理不能替你决定。颜色结论永远只对已经画出的 adjacency graph 负责。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Map coloring likewise begins with modeling: regions are vertices, and the application must define which contacts count as adjacency. Graph theory cannot retroactively decide whether touching at a single point counts. Its coloring conclusion is only as meaningful as the adjacency graph supplied.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与辨别
> 一张考试冲突图已经三着色，是否足以断定三个教室能完成全部考试？
>
> **答案：**不足。三种颜色只说明三个时段可以消除已编码的成对时间冲突；每个时段需要多少教室、教室容量和考试时长都没有由普通着色回答。
