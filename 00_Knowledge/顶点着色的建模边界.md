# What a vertex-coloring model guarantees
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*顶点着色的建模边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A coloring can be mathematically correct while the proposed schedule is unusable. The distinction is between satisfying the graph's constraints and having encoded all the real constraints. Ordinary [[Proper 着色与独立集划分|proper vertex coloring]] separates every pair already joined by a conflict edge. It does not supply missing room capacities, durations, resource restrictions or preferences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

着色在数学上完全合法，得到的排期却仍可能无法执行。区别在于：满足图中的约束，不等于现实约束已经全部写进图。普通[[Proper 着色与独立集划分|合法顶点着色]]会分开每一对已用冲突边相连的对象，却不会补上没有建模的教室容量、持续时间、资源限制或偏好。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## State the promise of each edge
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*说清每条边承诺检查什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a finite simple undirected conflict graph $G=(V,E)$, vertices are the objects to group. An edge $\{u,v\}\in E$ means that those two objects may not share one color. For a positive integer $k$, a coloring $c:V\to\{1,\ldots,k\}$ assigns a group label, and the complete mathematical requirement is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在有限简单无向冲突图 $G=(V,E)$ 中，顶点是要分组的对象。边 $\{u,v\}\in E$ 表示这两个对象不能共用一种颜色。取正整数 $k$，着色 $c:V\to\{1,\ldots,k\}$ 指定组标签，数学要求完整地写成

$$\{u,v\}\in E\quad\Longrightarrow\quad c(u)\ne c(v).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A missing edge means that this model imposes no different-color requirement on that pair; it is not independent evidence that the real objects are compatible. Conversely, inserting an unjustified edge removes schedules that may actually be feasible. The [[图模型语义|meaning of the graph]] must therefore be checked before using a coloring theorem.

For exams, take one vertex per exam and connect two exams when a student must take both. If each color denotes one common, nonoverlapping time block long enough for the assigned exams, a proper coloring prevents that student from being assigned two exams in the same block. Exams with no shared students can share a color in this conflict model. Room availability is a separate question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

缺少一条边，表示此模型没有要求这一对异色，并不独立证明它们在现实中兼容；反过来，没有依据地加边，会排除本来可行的安排。因此，使用着色定理之前要先检查[[图模型语义|图的含义]]。

对考试，每场考试取一个顶点；两场有共同考生时连边。若每种颜色代表一个共同的、不重叠且足以容纳所分配考试的时间块，那么合法着色能防止同一考生在同一块内被安排两场考试。没有共同考生的考试可以在这个冲突模型中同色，至于教室是否够用，则是另一个问题。

> [!example] Every pair fits, but all three do not · 两两放得下，三个一起放不下
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Three exams $a,b,c$ have disjoint student groups. Each needs one room for the whole time block, and only two suitable rooms are available in that block. There are no student-conflict edges, so assigning all three exams color $1$ is a proper coloring. It still needs three rooms at once and violates the room limit.
>
> This capacity rule cannot be represented exactly by merely adding ordinary conflict edges on the same three exam vertices while keeping every pair allowed to share a block. Every pair fits in two rooms, so exact pairwise compatibility requires all three pairs to be nonedges. That graph then also permits all three to share a color. One needs an additional group-size constraint or another richer model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 三场考试 $a,b,c$ 的考生互不重叠，每场都在整个时间块内占用一间教室，但该块只有两间适用教室。学生冲突图没有边，因此把三场都染成颜色 $1$ 是合法着色；它却同时需要三间教室，违反容量限制。
>
> 若仍只用原来的三个考试顶点，又要保留每一对都能同一时段的事实，就不能仅靠添加普通冲突边精确表达这个容量规则。任意两场都放得下，所以每一对都必须无边；这样的图却也允许三场全同色。必须额外限制同组大小，或使用更丰富的模型。

## Time, eligibility and costs add more information
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*时间、资格与成本需要更多信息*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Colors in ordinary coloring are interchangeable labels. Swapping color names preserves every inequality $c(u)\ne c(v)$. In a timetable, however, slots have lengths and an order. A three-hour exam cannot be represented as occupying just one one-hour block; its occupied intervals must be modeled. Likewise, “these exams must use different, nonconsecutive slots” is stronger than “they must have different colors”: slots $1$ and $2$ are different but consecutive, while $1$ and $3$ are not consecutive. Adding a conflict edge alone enforces inequality, not temporal separation. The schedule needs constraints that refer to actual times or slot positions.

If an exam can use only certain rooms or a task can use only certain machines, each vertex needs an allowed set of resource labels. A preferred morning slot, a room cost or a penalty for late completion also needs explicit data and an objective. Minimizing the number of colors does not automatically minimize those costs. If the only rule really is pairwise inability to share interchangeable resources, ordinary coloring may be an exact model; additional restrictions are not required just because the application is a schedule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通着色中的颜色是可互换的标签。交换颜色名字，所有 $c(u)\ne c(v)$ 仍成立。但时间表中的时段有长短和次序：三小时考试不能只按占用一个一小时块来表示，需要明确它占据的区间。同样，“两场考试必须排在相异且不相邻的时段”比“颜色不同”更强：时段 $1,2$ 不同却相邻，$1,3$ 则不相邻。仅添加一条冲突边只强制不相等，并不强制时间间隔，排期还需要涉及实际时间或时段位置的约束。

若考试只能使用某些教室，或任务只能在某些机器上运行，每个顶点还需要允许的资源标签集合。偏好上午、房间成本或迟交惩罚，也需要显式数据和目标。最小化颜色数不会自动最小化这些成本。若真实规则确实只有“哪些对象不能共用可互换资源”的成对限制，普通着色就可能已经精确；并不是凡属排期都一定需要更复杂的模型。

## Decide what is being grouped or selected
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先分清要给什么分组，还是要选择哪些关系*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the simple path $a-b-c$, three nearby problems have different outputs. **Vertex coloring** colors $a,b,c$; one can give $a,c$ color $1$ and $b$ color $2$. **Edge coloring** instead colors the two edges $ab,bc$ and requires different colors because they share endpoint $b$. A **[[匹配定义|matching]]** selects edges with no repeated endpoint: here it may choose $ab$ or $bc$, but cannot choose both. Grouping every object and selecting a compatible subset of relations are different decisions.

In a [[二分图配对关系|bipartite eligibility graph]], the two sides might be students and projects; an edge means that an assignment is allowed. A matching chooses assignments without reusing an endpoint. In a coloring conflict graph, an edge means that sharing a color is forbidden. The same drawn line therefore carries opposite practical meanings in these two models. An arrow meaning “must happen before” is another relation again: ordinary inequality of colors does not express its direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在简单路径 $a-b-c$ 上，三个相近问题的输出不同。**顶点着色**给 $a,b,c$ 染色，可以让 $a,c$ 用颜色 $1$，$b$ 用颜色 $2$。**边着色**改为给边 $ab,bc$ 染色；它们共享端点 $b$，所以要求异色。**[[匹配定义|匹配]]**则选择端点不重复的边：这里可以选 $ab$ 或 $bc$，不能同时选两条。给每个对象分组，与选出一部分兼容关系，是不同决策。

在[[二分图配对关系|二分图允许关系模型]]中，两侧可以是学生和项目，边表示允许这项分配；匹配从中选出不重复使用端点的分配。在着色冲突图中，边却表示禁止同色。因此，同样画成一条线，在两个模型中承载的实际含义相反。表示“必须先于”的箭头又是另一种关系，普通颜色不等式并不能表达它的方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Map coloring also starts with a convention: regions are vertices, and the application must specify which contacts count as adjacency. Whether touching at a single point counts is not decided by the coloring algorithm. If a theorem requires a planar adjacency graph, its hypotheses must be checked for the graph actually constructed. Changing the contact rule can change that graph.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

地图着色同样先要约定：区域是顶点，哪些接触算相邻由应用规定。仅在一点接触是否算相邻，不由着色算法决定。如果某个定理要求邻接图是平面的，就必须对实际构造的图检查这一前提；改变接触规则可能改变图本身。

> [!question]- Three colors or three rooms? · 三种颜色还是三间教室？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A proper three-coloring of an exam-conflict graph gives a schedule using at most three conflict-free groups. If colors denote time blocks, it gives at most three such blocks under the stated block assumptions. It does not say that three rooms suffice: one color class might contain many exams, and room capacities and durations still require checking. The meaning of a color must be fixed before interpreting its count.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考试冲突图的一份合法三着色给出至多三个无已编码冲突的组。若颜色表示时间块，在相应时间块假设下，就得到至多三个这样的块；它不表示三间教室足够，因为某个颜色类可能包含很多考试，房间容量和时长仍需检查。解释颜色数量之前，必须先固定颜色代表什么。
