# 在给定静态订单簿和撮合假设下分档配量并按数量加权可得成交均价

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under a specified static book and matching assumptions, allocate fills across price levels and quantity-weight them to obtain the average execution price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先明确交易方向、数量 $Q>0$、限价及其他约束，并假设订单执行期间相关簿况不变、允许按对手方存量价格逐档成交。买入从最低可用卖价向上取量，卖出从最高可用买价向下取量；遇到限价或其他执行边界即停止。不能把不合格档位的数量拿来补足成交。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify side, quantity $Q>0$, the limit and other constraints. Assume the relevant book stays fixed during execution and fills may take resting opposing prices across levels. Buy from the lowest available ask upward; sell from the highest available bid downward. Stop at a limit or other execution boundary rather than using ineligible levels to complete the order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将符合条件的价位按上述顺序记为 $p_1,\ldots,p_k$，其数量为 $q_j$。第 $j$ 档成交量是该档可用量与剩余委托量的较小值；总成交量 $Q_f$ 若为正，再计算均价：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write eligible levels in that order as $p_1,\ldots,p_k$ with sizes $q_j$. A level fills the smaller of its available size and the unfilled order quantity. If total filled quantity $Q_f$ is positive, calculate the average over actual fills:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
f_j=\min\!\left(q_j,\max\!\left(0,Q-\sum_{i<j}f_i\right)\right),
\qquad Q_f=\sum_{j=1}^{k}f_j,
\qquad \bar P=\frac{\sum_{j=1}^{k}p_jf_j}{Q_f}.
$$

卖盘为 100.00 元 50 股、100.20 元 100 股，买入 120 股且两档均合格，则 $f_1=50,f_2=70,Q_f=120$：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With asks of 50 shares at 100.00 and 100 at 100.20, a 120-share buy eligible for both levels gives $f_1=50,f_2=70,Q_f=120$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\bar P=\frac{50(100.00)+70(100.20)}{120}
=\frac{12014}{120}\approx100.1167.
$$

若只允许执行第一档，报告成交 50 股、均价 100.00、未成交 70 股；不能仍用 120 作均价分母。若一股未成交，均价未定义。这是该订单的实际配量加权，不是整个市场某段时间的 VWAP 基准；使用旧快照也不能保证未来真实执行。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If only the first level is eligible, report 50 filled at an average of 100.00 and 70 unfilled; do not divide by the intended 120. With no fills the average is undefined. This is the order's own quantity-weighted fill price, not a market-wide interval VWAP benchmark. An old snapshot does not guarantee future execution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
