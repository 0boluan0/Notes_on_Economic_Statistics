# 线性产品在指定输入与固定条件下的价值是该输入的仿射函数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A linear product has value affine in a specified input while other conditions are fixed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在风险管理中，说产品对某输入 $S$ 是线性的，通常指：**固定时间和其他输入后，$V(S)=aS+b$**。因此这里包含截距，数学上更准确地说是“仿射”。在该区间内，每增加同样的 $S$，价值变化相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A product is linear in an input $S$ when, holding time and other inputs fixed, $V(S)=aS+b$. The financial convention includes an intercept and is therefore affine in mathematical terminology. Equal changes in $S$ produce equal value changes within that range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直接求导就得到 $\Delta=a$、$\Gamma=0$。例如不付股息股票的远期多头价值为 $V=S-Ke^{-r\tau}$，对现货 $S$ 的 Delta 恒为 1、Gamma 为 0；它对利率 $r$ 却不是线性的。外汇版本的斜率须按 [[外汇远期估值]] 计算，不等于未贴现的到期外币数量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiation gives $\Delta=a$ and $\Gamma=0$. A long forward on a non-dividend-paying stock has value $S-Ke^{-r\tau}$ and unit spot Delta, but is nonlinear in $r$. For FX, use [[外汇远期估值|the discounted foreign-currency quantity]] as the spot slope.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**线性必须带着输入说。** 若 $a$ 或 $b$ 还依赖波动率，对 $S$ 线性并不能推出 [[Vega]] 为零；若输入换成收益率或对数价格，也须重新判断。这一条件决定什么时候 [[Delta-Gamma价格近似|一阶价格展开]] 在固定条件下已经精确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Linearity must name its coordinate. If $a$ or $b$ depends on volatility, zero spot Gamma does not imply zero [[Vega|Vega]]. Changing coordinates also requires reassessment. This condition determines when a [[Delta-Gamma价格近似|first-order price expansion]] is already exact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Sources and verification*

- The definition and derivative results follow directly from an affine function. The volatility-dependent coefficient is a logical counterexample, not a claim that all standard forwards depend on volatility.
