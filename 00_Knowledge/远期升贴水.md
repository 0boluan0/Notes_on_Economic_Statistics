# 在本币每单位外币报价下 F_t 除以 S_t 减一衡量外币远期升贴水且倒数报价反转方向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With domestic currency per unit of foreign currency, $F_t/S_t-1$ measures the foreign currency's forward premium or discount, and the reciprocal quotation reverses its direction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子度量
> 对同一到期日且同一报价方向的即期率 $S_t$ 与远期率 $F_t$，外币的简单远期升贴水为
> $$p_t^F=\frac{F_t}{S_t}-1.$$
> $F_t>S_t$ 表示外币远期升水、本币远期贴水；$F_t<S_t$ 表示外币远期贴水。改用倒数报价时方向反转。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For maturity-matched spot and forward rates quoted in the same direction, the foreign currency's simple forward premium is $p_t^F=F_t/S_t-1$. If $F_t>S_t$, the foreign currency is at a forward premium and the domestic currency at a forward discount; if $F_t<S_t$, the foreign currency is at a forward discount. The reciprocal quotation reverses the direction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场也常报 forward points $F_t-S_t$；其数值依赖报价单位，不能跨币种直接比较。使用对数报价时，$f_t-s_t=\log(F_t/S_t)$。简单百分比、对数差和年化远期点是不同口径，必须同时写到期日、年化方法和报价方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Markets also quote forward points, $F_t-S_t$, whose scale depends on the quotation unit and is not directly comparable across currency pairs. With log quotations, the premium is $f_t-s_t=\log(F_t/S_t)$. Simple percentages, log differences, and annualized forward points are different conventions and require an explicit maturity, annualization method, and quotation direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 [[抵补利率平价]] 的条件成立，
$$
\frac{F_t}{S_t}=\frac{R_d}{R_f};
$$
在低利率的一期简单近似下，外币远期升水约为 $i_d-i_f$。这是现货、远期和可比利率之间的无套利价格关系，不表示 $F_t$ 是 $S_{t+1}$ 的无条件期望。只有再加入零货币风险溢价、理性预期等额外假设，远期率才可能成为未来即期率的无偏预测；这些假设的经验边界见 [[UIP经验检验]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If covered interest parity holds, $F_t/S_t=R_d/R_f$; for small one-period simple rates, the foreign-currency forward premium is approximately $i_d-i_f$. This is a no-arbitrage pricing relation among spot, forward, and comparable interest rates. It does not make $F_t$ the unconditional expectation of $S_{t+1}$. Interpreting the forward as an unbiased predictor additionally requires assumptions such as a zero currency risk premium and rational expectations; see the empirical boundaries of UIP.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若 $S_t=0.80$ GBP/USD、$F_t=0.792$ GBP/USD，哪种货币有远期贴水？
>
> **答案：** $F_t/S_t-1=-1\%$，所以美元相对英镑有约 1% 远期贴水。若改报 USD/GBP，方向反转。
