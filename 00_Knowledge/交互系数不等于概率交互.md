# 非线性二元模型中线性指数的交互系数通常不等于概率尺度交互效应

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a nonlinear binary model, an interaction coefficient in the linear index generally differs from the interaction effect on the probability scale*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 参数尺度与概率尺度是两个对象
> 若 $p=G[\eta(x_1,x_2)]$，两个连续变量的概率交互效应为
> $$
> \frac{\partial^2p}{\partial x_1\partial x_2}
> =g'(\eta)\frac{\partial\eta}{\partial x_1}\frac{\partial\eta}{\partial x_2}
> +g(\eta)\frac{\partial^2\eta}{\partial x_1\partial x_2}.
> $$
> 在线性指数含标准乘积项 $\beta_{12}x_1x_2$ 时，$\beta_{12}$ 直接给出 $\partial^2\eta/(\partial x_1\partial x_2)$，但概率交互还含链接曲率项，而且 $\beta_{12}$ 也会随取值进入 $\eta$ 与两个一阶导数。因此交互系数不是概率交互本身；系数为 0 不推出概率交互为 0，二者符号也不必相同。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $p=G[\eta(x_1,x_2)]$, the continuous-variable probability interaction contains both a link-curvature term and a direct index-interaction term, as shown above. With a standard product term $\beta_{12}x_1x_2$, $\beta_{12}$ directly gives $\partial^2\eta/(\partial x_1\partial x_2)$, but it can also enter $\eta$ and both first derivatives at the stated covariate values. The coefficient is therefore not the probability interaction itself: a zero coefficient does not imply zero probability interaction, and their signs need not agree.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例：logit 指数为 $\eta=-1+0.5D_1+0.5D_2$，没有 $D_1D_2$ 项。四格预测概率约为 $0.269,0.378,0.378,0.500$，其差异之差约为 $0.014$，仍不为 0。这不表示模型漏估了一个参数，而是说明“指数可加”不等于“概率可加”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, consider the logit index $\eta=-1+0.5D_1+0.5D_2$, which contains no $D_1D_2$ term. The four predicted probabilities are approximately $0.269,0.378,0.378,0.500$, whose difference in differences is about $0.014$ rather than zero. This does not imply an omitted parameter; it shows that additivity of the index is not additivity of probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

报告时应从同一个完整拟合模型计算目标位置上的交叉偏导或四格概率对比，并给出相应不确定性。不能把回归表中的交互系数直接改写成百分点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reporting should calculate the cross-partial or four-cell probability contrast at the target covariate configuration from the same complete fitted model and quantify its uncertainty. An interaction coefficient from a regression table cannot simply be relabeled as percentage points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
