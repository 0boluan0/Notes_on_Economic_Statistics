# Closed communicating classes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*闭沟通类*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **closed communicating class** is a group of mutually reachable states with no possible exit. The system can keep moving within the group; “closed” means that it cannot move outside, not that it stops moving. For example, a chain that alternates between two states forever has a two-state closed class, although neither individual state traps the chain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**闭沟通类**是一组内部互相可达、又没有任何可能出口的状态。系统可以一直在组内移动；“闭”表示不能出去，不表示停止移动。例如，一条永远在两个状态之间交替的链有一个二状态闭类，但任意单个状态都留不住这条链。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work with a fixed [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|time-homogeneous transition rule]] on a nonempty finite or countably infinite set $S$. In the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]], $p_{ij}$ is the probability of moving from $i$ to $j$ in one step. First identify a [[马氏链沟通类|communicating class]] $C$: a maximal group in which every state can reach every other along finite positive-probability routes. Closedness is an additional requirement on that class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑非空有限或可数无限状态集 $S$ 上固定的[[DTMC时间齐次转移核|时间齐次转移规则]]。采用[[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]]，$p_{ij}$ 是从 $i$ 一步到 $j$ 的概率。先找到一个[[马氏链沟通类|沟通类]] $C$，即内部任意两状态都能沿有限正概率路线互达的最大状态组。“闭”是对这个类增加的要求。

> [!important] No positive-probability exit · 没有正概率出口
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A communicating class $C$ is closed exactly when
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 沟通类 $C$ 闭，当且仅当
>
> $$p_{ij}=0\quad(i\in C,\ j\notin C),
> \qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad
> \sum_{j\in C}p_{ij}=1\quad(i\in C).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The equivalence uses nonnegative probabilities and a total row sum of one. It is enough to check one-step exits: a finite route leaving $C$ would have to take a first step from inside to outside. Thus the condition also says that no outside state is accessible from any state in $C$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两种写法等价，是因为概率非负且每行总和为一。只需检查一步出口：任何离开 $C$ 的有限路线，都必须有第一次从类内迈到类外的那一步。因此，这个条件也等价于从任意类内状态都无法到达任何类外状态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the chain starts in $C$, the probability of being outside at any fixed step is zero; the event of ever leaving is a countable union of such zero-probability events. It therefore stays in $C$ for all future steps with probability one. The same holds after an entry into $C$. An incoming arrow from outside does not violate closedness: only outgoing arrows matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若从 $C$ 内出发，每个固定步数后在类外的概率都为零；“曾经离开”是这些零概率事件的可数并，因此以后所有步骤都以概率一留在 $C$。从外面进入以后也是如此。从类外指向类内的箭头不会破坏闭性：需要排除的是向外的箭头。

## A closed pair and an absorbing singleton
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*闭类可以有内部运动*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In state order $1,2,3,4$, consider

$$P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0&0\\1/2&0&1/2&0\\0&0&0&1\\0&0&1&0\end{pmatrix}.$$

The class $\{1,2\}$ is not closed: state 2 has probability $1/2$ of jumping to 3. The class $\{3,4\}$ is closed: both of its rows put total probability one inside the pair. Its internal transitions are described by

$$P_{\{3,4\}}=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.$$

Neither 3 nor 4 is absorbing. By contrast, a singleton $\{a\}$ is a closed class exactly when $p_{aa}=1$; such an $a$ is an [[吸收状态|absorbing state]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按 $1,2,3,4$ 排列状态，取上面的 $P$。类 $\{1,2\}$ 不闭，因为状态 2 有 $1/2$ 的概率跳到 3。类 $\{3,4\}$ 闭，因为这两行的全部概率都分配在组内；它的内部转移就是上面的二阶交换矩阵。3、4 都不是吸收状态。相反，单点集 $\{a\}$ 是闭类当且仅当 $p_{aa}=1$，这样的 $a$ 称为[[吸收状态]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Restricting the original transition matrix to a closed class keeps every row sum equal to one and therefore defines a chain on that class without changing any probabilities. The restricted chain is [[不可约链|irreducible]]: class members already have routes to one another, and such routes stay within the class. For a nonclosed class, deleting outside columns loses probability from at least one row. Renormalising those rows would specify a different transition rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把原转移矩阵限制在一个闭类内，每行和仍为一，因此无需改变概率就得到该类上的一条链。限制后的链[[不可约链|不可约]]：同类状态本来就互相可达，而且这些路线不会绕出该类。对非闭类，直接删掉类外列会使至少一行丢失概率；再把行重新归一化，就指定了不同的转移规则。

> [!warning] A closed set is not automatically a class · 闭集合不一定是沟通类
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Any subset with no outgoing transition is a **closed set**. It need not be a maximal mutually communicating group. For example, two distinct absorbing states form a closed set together, but cannot reach each other, so they form two classes. The entire state space $S$ is always closed. Use [[闭类不等于不可约|the distinction between closed sets, closed classes and irreducibility]] before drawing a conclusion about the whole chain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 任意没有出口的子集都可称为**闭集合**，但它不一定是互相可达的最大状态组。例如，两个不同的吸收状态合在一起是闭集合，但两者无法互达，所以属于两个类。整个状态空间 $S$ 永远是闭集合。判断全链前，要分清[[闭类不等于不可约|闭集合、闭沟通类与不可约]]。

## Closed does not always mean recurrent
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*没有出口与必然返回的区别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A state is [[常返与暂态|recurrent]] if, starting there, the chain returns at some positive time with probability one. Every recurrent communicating class is closed. The converse holds for a **finite** closed class, even if other parts of the chain are infinite: apply the [[有限链类分解|finite-state result]] to its restricted chain. For an infinite closed class, the chain can keep visiting new states inside the class instead of returning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个状态[[常返与暂态|常返]]，是指从它出发后，以概率一在某个正时间返回。每个常返沟通类都闭；反过来，**有限**闭类中的状态都常返，即使链的其他部分无限也成立，因为可以把[[有限链类分解|有限状态结论]]用于限制后的链。对无限闭类，链可能一直访问类内的新状态，而不返回原状态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a concrete boundary, take all integers and move one unit right with probability $3/4$, left with probability $1/4$. Every pair of integers is connected in both directions by a positive route, so the entire integer line is one closed class. Yet a walk starting at 1 hits 0 with probability only $1/3$. Starting at 0, it can first move right and then never return, with probability $(3/4)(2/3)=1/2$. Closedness rules out departure from the class; it does not rule out drifting away within an infinite class.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体地，取全部整数，每步以 $3/4$ 的概率向右一格、以 $1/4$ 的概率向左一格。任意两整数之间都有双向正概率路线，所以整条整数线是一个闭类。但从 1 出发，最终到达 0 的概率只有 $1/3$；从 0 出发，先向右再永远不返回的概率为 $(3/4)(2/3)=1/2$。闭性排除的是离开这个类，不是排除在无限类内部越走越远。
