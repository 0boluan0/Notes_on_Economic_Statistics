# 市场深度是在给定时点和价格范围内可交易的数量

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market depth is the quantity available to trade at a specified time and within a specified price range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市场深度描述某个时点、某一侧和价格范围内的可交易数量。使用[[限价订单簿]]衡量时，须说明是最佳一档数量、若干档累计量，还是某个价格容忍范围内的数量；显示深度是特定数据覆盖下的可见部分，不自动等于全部真实可执行流动性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Market depth describes quantity available on a specified side and within a price range at a particular time. In a [[限价订单簿|limit order book]], specify whether it means best-level size, cumulative levels or quantity within a price tolerance. Displayed depth is the visible portion covered by the data, not automatically all truly executable liquidity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设可见卖盘价位为 $a_j$，对应数量为 $q_j$。在不考虑其他执行限制的静态快照中，不高于买入价格上限 $L$ 的累计卖侧显示深度为：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let visible asks have prices $a_j$ and quantities $q_j$. In a static snapshot abstracting from other execution restrictions, cumulative displayed ask depth no higher than purchase-price bound $L$ is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
D_{\mathrm{ask}}(L)=\sum_{j:a_j\le L}q_j.
$$

若卖盘为 100.00 元 50 股、100.20 元 100 股，则 $D_{\mathrm{ask}}(100.00)=50$，$D_{\mathrm{ask}}(100.20)=150$。两个范围对应不同深度，所以只报“深度 150”缺少必要口径。历史上一天成交一万股，也不表示此刻 100.00 元处有一万股可以购买。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With 50 shares offered at 100.00 and 100 at 100.20, $D_{\mathrm{ask}}(100.00)=50$ and $D_{\mathrm{ask}}(100.20)=150$. Reporting “depth of 150” alone omits the price range. Historical daily volume of ten thousand shares does not mean ten thousand shares are available at 100.00 now.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

撤单、成交、新订单和隐藏数量都会使观察与实际执行存在差别。较深的静态订单簿可减少同一数量订单需要跨越的价位，但不能单独保证之后的[[市场冲击]]小，或保证压力期深度仍然存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cancellations, executions, new orders and hidden size can separate an observation from actual execution. A deeper static book can reduce the levels traversed by a given order, but alone guarantees neither low subsequent [[市场冲击|market impact]] nor surviving depth under stress.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sources and verification

- [CME, How CME Group Ag Markets Operate，“The Central Limit Order Book”及其执行实例](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/articles-and-reports/overview-what-makes-ags-markets-work)：核对逐价数量、数据层级和订单间取消导致可用量减少。累计深度式与 50／150 股例为该口径的定义性计算。
- [ECB, Financial Stability Review，2023 年 5 月专题，第 2 节](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/special/html/ecb.fsrart202305_01~830184261b.es.html)：核对深度、交易规模及最佳报价数量，不把窄价差当作充足深度。

- [CME, How CME Group Ag Markets Operate, CLOB and execution examples](https://www.cmegroup.com/education/articles-and-reports/overview-what-makes-ags-markets-work) verifies price-level quantities, data granularity and cancellations between orders. The cumulative formula and 50/150-share example implement the stated measurement.
- [ECB, Financial Stability Review, May 2023 special feature, Section 2](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/special/html/ecb.fsrart202305_01~830184261b.es.html) supports depth, trade size and the distinction from a narrow spread.
