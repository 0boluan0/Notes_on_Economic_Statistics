# 签名数学有效不能单独证明签名发生时密钥仍可信
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mathematical signature validity alone does not prove that the key was trusted when signing occurred*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 数字签名验证只检查签名、消息与公钥是否匹配。若私钥后来泄露，攻击者也能生成数学上有效的新签名；仅凭签名中的自报时间，验证者无法判断它是在泄露或撤销之前还是之后生成。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Digital-signature verification checks whether the signature, message, and public key match. If the private key is later compromised, an attacker can also create mathematically valid new signatures. A self-reported time inside the signed content does not by itself let a verifier determine whether signing occurred before or after compromise or revocation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可信时间戳可以把一段数据的摘要交给独立时间戳机构，由该机构签发带可信时间的 token。若时间戳覆盖已有签名值，它可为“这份签名最迟在该时点已经存在”提供独立证据；它不自动证明签名者当时被授权，也不能替代证书路径、撤销信息和具体验证策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A trusted timestamp submits a digest of data to an independent time-stamping authority, which issues a token containing a trusted time. When the timestamp covers an existing signature value, it can independently show that the signature existed no later than that time. It does not automatically prove that the signer was authorised then, nor does it replace certificate paths, revocation information, and the applicable validation policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，长期验证历史签名要明确时间证据来自哪里、当时适用哪条信任规则，以及证书或密钥状态怎样解释。单独看到今天的 `signature valid` 不能反推出过去某一时刻的可信状态。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Long-term validation of a historical signature must identify the source of timing evidence, the trust rule applicable at that time, and how certificate or key status is interpreted. Seeing `signature valid` today cannot by itself reconstruct trust at an earlier time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 私钥周一泄露，证书周二撤销。周三收到一个内容自称“周日签署”且数学验证有效的文件，为什么还不能认定它确实在泄露前签署？
>
> **答案：** 攻击者在周一后也能生成签名并把消息时间写成周日。需要独立可信的时间证据及相应证书／撤销策略，才能判断签名是否在密钥仍可信时已经存在。

## 来源与核验

- [RFC 3161, Sections 1–2, *Internet X.509 Public Key Infrastructure Time-Stamp Protocol*](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3161)：核对时间戳机构对数据摘要签发带可信时间的 token，以及时间戳可用于证明数据在给定时点前已存在。
- [RFC 5280, Sections 5–6, *Internet X.509 PKI Certificate and CRL Profile*](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc5280)：核对证书撤销状态与路径验证属于独立于签名数学运算的信任判断。
- NIST SP 800-102 已于 2025-07-01 撤回，因此本卡没有把它当作现行规范引用；时间戳定义与历史状态边界改由仍有效的 RFC 3161 与 RFC 5280 支撑。
