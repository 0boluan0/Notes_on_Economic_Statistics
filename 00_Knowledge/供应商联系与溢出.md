# 本地供应商与外资客户建立联系并不足以证明存在生产率溢出

外资客户向本地企业采购，建立了供应联系，却不自动证明[[外资正向生产率溢出]]。须进一步区分：供应商只是多接订单，还是在可比投入下改善了产出；变化是否由外资联系造成；由此带来的收益是否已通过费用、采购价格等安排被外资客户完全取得。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Purchases from a domestic firm establish a supplier linkage but do not automatically establish a [[外资正向生产率溢出|positive FDI productivity spillover]]. Distinguish additional orders from improved output at comparable inputs, examine whether the foreign linkage caused the change, and determine whether fees, procurement prices or other arrangements let the foreign customer capture the entire resulting benefit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造一个只说明收益归属的例子：已知客户传授的方法在扣除供应商实施成本后创造10的收益，且除此之外没有其他影响。若客户以费用取得4、没有其他回收安排，仍有6未被客户内部化；若通过费用及采购价格合计取得全部10，则该例没有剩余的未内部化收益。因此，“技术帮助收费”不必排除部分溢出，“存在技术帮助”也不必意味着溢出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider an example solely about benefit accrual. Assume a customer's method creates a benefit of 10 after the supplier's implementation costs, with no other effects. If the customer captures 4 through a fee and nothing through other arrangements, 6 remains uninternalized by the customer. If fees and procurement-price adjustments capture all 10, no residual uninternalized benefit remains in this example. Charging for assistance therefore need not rule out a partial spillover, while assistance itself need not imply one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Javorcik（2004）研究立陶宛1996–2000年制造业企业，发现与后向联系相一致的正向生产率关联，但没有得到稳健的水平或前向溢出证据。其后向联系指标利用行业投入产出权重和下游外资存在构造潜在联系，并非逐家观察到的真实供货名单。这样的研究支持一种有条件的机制证据，不能推出所有行业、所有所有权形式均必然获得溢出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Javorcik (2004) studies Lithuanian manufacturing firms in 1996–2000 and finds positive productivity associations consistent with backward linkages, but no robust evidence of horizontal or forward spillovers. Its backward-linkage measure combines industry input–output weights with foreign presence downstream; it is not an observed list of each firm's actual customers. This provides conditional evidence on a mechanism, not a guarantee across industries or ownership forms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际评价应按[[因果研究设计检查]]明确比较对象：外资客户可能选择原本更有效率的供应商，市场份额重配和价格变化也可能改变观察指标。应把联系、能力变化、生产率结果和收益归属分别核验，而不是把“本地采购比例提高”直接写成“技术溢出成立”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the [[因果研究设计检查|causal-design checklist]] to define the comparison: foreign customers may select suppliers that were already more productive, while reallocation and price changes can alter observed measures. Verify linkages, capability changes, productivity outcomes and benefit accrual separately rather than equating a higher local-procurement share with demonstrated technological spillovers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
