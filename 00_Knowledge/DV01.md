# DV01是指定收益率每变动一个基点对应的带符号一阶金额敏感度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*DV01 is signed first-order currency sensitivity per basis-point change in a specified yield*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

DV01 将价值对指定利率因素的导数缩放到 $1$ bp。本页明确采用 $\mathrm{DV01}_x=-10^{-4}\partial V/\partial x$，因此普通固定非负现金流多头对自身 YTM 的 DV01 为正；该因素上升 $1$ bp 的一阶损益是 $-\mathrm{DV01}_x$，不是 $+\mathrm{DV01}_x$。$x$ 必须是小数年利率或明确以同样尺度计量的曲线冲击参数；任意曲线形变下的符号仍须由导数判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DV01 scales a value derivative to one basis point. This page explicitly uses $\mathrm{DV01}_x=-10^{-4}\partial V/\partial x$, positive against own YTM for an ordinary long position in fixed nonnegative payments. First-order P&L for a one-basis-point increase in that factor is $-\mathrm{DV01}_x$, not $+\mathrm{DV01}_x$. The factor $x$ must be a decimal annual rate or a specified curve-shock parameter in the same units; the derivative determines the sign for an arbitrary curve deformation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同一 YTM 坐标下的债券头寸，由[[金额久期]]可得
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a bond position using the same YTM coordinate, [[金额久期|money duration]] gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\mathrm{DV01}_y=10^{-4}qP D_{\mathrm{mod}}.$$

若头寸全价为 $10{,}000{,}000$ 元、$D_{\mathrm{mod}}=5$，则 DV01 为 $5{,}000$ 元／bp。同量空头为 $-5{,}000$ 元／bp；曲率忽略时，加息 $3$ bp 的多头损益约为 $-15{,}000$ 元。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For full position value 10,000,000 and modified duration 5, DV01 is 5,000 currency units per bp. An equal short position has DV01 of −5,000; ignoring curvature, a three-basis-point increase gives the long position an approximate P&L of −15,000.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

数值估计可以对称重估。若 $h$ 用小数利率表示，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A symmetric repricing estimate, with $h$ in decimal rate units, is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\mathrm{DV01}_x\approx10^{-4}\frac{V(x-h)-V(x+h)}{2h}.$$

当 $h=10^{-4}$，右端等于两侧价格之差的一半。它估计局部导数，不保证等于单向加息 $1$ bp 的精确价格差。若系统直接报告加息损益，其符号与本页相反；若只报绝对幅度，则缺少方向，不能直接用于[[组合DV01聚合|多空风险相抵]]。还须辨清冲击的是自身 YTM、哪条曲线还是哪个期限节点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $h=10^{-4}$, the expression is half the difference between the two values. It estimates a local derivative, not necessarily the exact one-sided price change. A system reporting upward-shock P&L uses the opposite sign; an absolute-only number lacks direction and cannot directly support [[组合DV01聚合|long-short netting]]. Also identify whether the shock changes own YTM, a specific curve or a tenor node.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [CME Group，Calculating the Dollar Value of a Basis Point，PDF 第 1–2 页](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/interest-rates/files/Calculating_the_Dollar_Value_of_a_Basis_Point.pdf#page=1)：支持以小幅双向重估或修正久期估计 DV01，以及 DV01 随价格—收益率位置变化。CME 的多头幅度写法在本页显式扩展为带头寸方向的导数约定；不把绝对值用于相抵。
- [MIT 15.401，Slide／PDF 第 36 页](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-401-finance-theory-i-fall-2008/df418b972d36cd53ae5c375b8af61e53_MIT15_401F08_lec04.pdf#page=36)：支持导数关系；$10^{-4}$ 缩放、对称差分和三基点例子独立复算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- CME pp. 1–2 supports small symmetric repricing and modified-duration estimates, and shows that DV01 changes along the price-yield curve. Its long-position magnitude is explicitly extended here to a signed position derivative, without absolute-value netting.
- MIT Slide 36 supports the derivative relation. The basis-point scaling, symmetric difference and three-basis-point example were checked independently.
