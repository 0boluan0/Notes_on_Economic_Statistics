# GCD–LCM product identity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*公因公倍乘积*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $12$ and $18$, the greatest common divisor is $6$ and the least common multiple is $36$. Their product is $6\cdot36=216$, exactly $12\cdot18$. This identity connects two different tasks: the [[最大公因数|gcd]] finds the largest positive divisor shared by the inputs, whereas the [[最小公倍数|lcm]] finds their smallest positive shared multiple.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $12,18$，最大公因数是 $6$，最小公倍数是 $36$；它们的乘积 $6\cdot36=216$ 恰好等于 $12\cdot18$。这条恒等式把两个不同任务联系起来：[[最大公因数]]找两数共有的最大正因子，[[最小公倍数]]找两数共有的最小正倍数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For positive integers $a,b$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正整数 $a,b$，

$$\gcd(a,b)\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=ab.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After the [[欧几里得算法|Euclidean algorithm]] gives the gcd, compute $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=(a/\gcd(a,b))b$ without factoring the inputs into primes. For example, $\gcd(12,18)=6$, so $\operatorname{lcm}(12,18)=2\cdot18=36$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，用[[欧几里得算法]]求出 gcd 后，就可以通过 $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=(a/\gcd(a,b))b$ 求公倍数，不必先做素因数分解。例如 $\gcd(12,18)=6$，所以 $\operatorname{lcm}(12,18)=2\cdot18=36$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $a,b$ are the two inputs, and $g=\gcd(a,b)>0$ divides $a$, so $a/g$ is an integer. The quotient formula is a consequence of the identity, not the definition of lcm. For the displayed example, the [[欧几里得算法|Euclidean algorithm]] gives $18=1\cdot12+6$ and $12=2\cdot6+0$, hence $g=6$. Divide $12$ by $6$ first to get $2$, then multiply by $18$ to get $36$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $a,b$ 是两个输入，$g=\gcd(a,b)>0$ 且整除 $a$，所以 $a/g$ 是整数。商的公式是恒等式的推论，不是 lcm 的定义。对上例，[[欧几里得算法]]给出 $18=1\cdot12+6$、$12=2\cdot6+0$，因此 $g=6$；先计算 $12/6=2$，再乘 $18$，得到 $36$。

## Why the product reconstructs the inputs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么恰好相乘还原

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By [[唯一素因数分解|unique prime factorization]], let $\alpha_p,\beta_p$ be the exponents of each prime appearing in either input, using zero for an absent prime. The [[最大公因数|gcd]] takes $\min(\alpha_p,\beta_p)$, while the [[最小公倍数|lcm]] needs $\max(\alpha_p,\beta_p)$ to accommodate both inputs. Their product adds these exponents, and the minimum plus the maximum equals $\alpha_p+\beta_p$, the exponent in $ab$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[唯一素因数分解]]，对两数中出现的每个素数 $p$ 写指数 $\alpha_p,\beta_p$，未出现时指数取 $0$。[[最大公因数]]能在两个数里都取到的指数是 $\min(\alpha_p,\beta_p)$；[[最小公倍数]]至少要容纳两数各自的指数，因此取 $\max(\alpha_p,\beta_p)$。两者相乘时指数相加，而 $\min(\alpha_p,\beta_p)+\max(\alpha_p,\beta_p)=\alpha_p+\beta_p$，正好是 $ab$ 的素因子指数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An exponent counts repeated copies of a prime: $2^3$ contains three factors $2$. For positive integers, [[唯一素因数分解|unique prime factorization]] makes these counts unambiguous. Let $P$ be the finite set of primes occurring in either $a$ or $b$, and write
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

指数记录一个素数重复出现的次数，例如 $2^3$ 含有三个因子 $2$。对正整数，[[唯一素因数分解]]保证这些次数确定。令 $P$ 为在 $a$ 或 $b$ 中出现的素数所成的有限集合，写成

$$a=\prod_{p\in P}p^{\alpha_p},\qquad b=\prod_{p\in P}p^{\beta_p}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product symbol $\prod$ means multiply the indicated factors once for each $p\in P$. The counts $\alpha_p,\beta_p$ are nonnegative integers; an absent prime has count zero, with $p^0=1$. If $a=b=1$, then $P$ is empty and both products are $1$ by the empty-product convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连乘符号 $\prod$ 表示对每个 $p\in P$ 都乘上指定因子。次数 $\alpha_p,\beta_p$ 是非负整数；没有出现的素数取次数零，且 $p^0=1$。若 $a=b=1$，则 $P$ 为空，按照空乘积约定，两边的乘积都是 $1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why do exponent comparisons test divisibility? If positive $u$ divides positive $v$, write $v=uk$ with positive integer $k$. Factoring both $u$ and $k$ shows that every prime count in $v$ is at least the corresponding count in $u$. Conversely, if all those count inequalities hold, the nonnegative differences form the prime factorization of an integer $k$ with $v=uk$. Thus divisibility is exactly the comparison of every prime count, using zero for missing primes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么比较指数能够判断整除？若正整数 $u$ 整除正整数 $v$，可写成 $v=uk$，其中 $k$ 是正整数。分解 $u,k$ 后可见，$v$ 的每个素数次数都不小于 $u$ 中对应的次数。反过来，若这些次数不等式全部成立，非负的次数差就能构成一个整数 $k$ 的素因数分解，使 $v=uk$。因此，整除恰好对应逐个素数次数比较，未出现的素数按零计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To divide **both** $a$ and $b$, a common divisor can use at most the smaller count of each prime. The notation $\min(\alpha_p,\beta_p)$ means that smaller count. Put $g=\prod_{p\in P}p^{\min(\alpha_p,\beta_p)}$. This $g$ divides both inputs, and every positive common divisor divides $g$. A positive divisor of $g$ cannot exceed $g$, so $g$ really is the greatest common divisor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要**同时整除** $a,b$，公因数能取的每种素数次数至多是两者中较小的那个；$\min(\alpha_p,\beta_p)$ 就表示这个较小次数。令 $g=\prod_{p\in P}p^{\min(\alpha_p,\beta_p)}$。这个 $g$ 整除两个输入，而每个正公因数又都整除 $g$。$g$ 的正因子不会大于 $g$，因此它确实是最大公因数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To be divisible **by both** inputs, a positive common multiple must contain at least the larger count of each prime. The notation $\max(\alpha_p,\beta_p)$ means that larger count. Put $L=\prod_{p\in P}p^{\max(\alpha_p,\beta_p)}$. Both inputs divide $L$, and $L$ divides every positive common multiple, even one containing extra primes. Hence every such multiple is at least $L$, proving $L$ is the least common multiple.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要**同时被两个输入整除**，正公倍数至少要包含两者中较大的每种素数次数；$\max(\alpha_p,\beta_p)$ 表示这个较大次数。令 $L=\prod_{p\in P}p^{\max(\alpha_p,\beta_p)}$。两个输入都整除 $L$，而 $L$ 又整除每个正公倍数，即使后者含有额外素数也如此。因此任何正公倍数都不小于 $L$，证明它确实是最小公倍数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any two counts, the smaller and larger are just the original pair in one order; equality causes no exception. Therefore their sum is $\alpha_p+\beta_p$. Multiplication adds prime counts, so $gL$ and $ab$ have the same count for every prime and are equal. The empty case gives $1\cdot1=1\cdot1$ as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意两个次数，较小值与较大值只是把原来的两个数按大小排列；两者相等时也没有例外。因此它们之和为 $\alpha_p+\beta_p$。相乘时素数次数相加，所以 $gL$ 与 $ab$ 对每个素数都有相同次数，两个整数相等。空集合情形也给出 $1\cdot1=1\cdot1$。

> [!example] Read the two prime counts · 读出两种素数的次数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Write $12=2^2\cdot3^1$ and $18=2^1\cdot3^2$. For prime $2$, the smaller/larger counts are $1,2$; for prime $3$, they are also $1,2$. Thus $g=2^1\cdot3^1=6$ and $L=2^2\cdot3^2=36$. In $gL$, both prime counts are $1+2=3$, giving $2^3\cdot3^3=216=12\cdot18$. This explains the result, rather than merely checking a numerical equality.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 写出 $12=2^2\cdot3^1$、$18=2^1\cdot3^2$。素数 $2$ 对应的较小／较大次数是 $1,2$，素数 $3$ 对应的也为 $1,2$。因此 $g=2^1\cdot3^1=6$，$L=2^2\cdot3^2=36$。在 $gL$ 中，两种素数的次数都为 $1+2=3$，得到 $2^3\cdot3^3=216=12\cdot18$。这说明了结果的原因，而不只是检查数值相等。

## Keep the two-input scope and conventions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 保留两输入范围与取值约定

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This two-input identity does not extend unchanged to three inputs: $2,4,8$ have gcd $2$ and lcm $8$, whose product $16$ differs from $2\cdot4\cdot8=64$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是两个数的恒等式，不能原样推广为“三个数的 gcd 乘 lcm 等于三数乘积”。例如 $2,4,8$ 的 gcd 为 $2$、lcm 为 $8$，乘积 $16$ 并不是 $2\cdot4\cdot8=64$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two positive inputs, gcd $1$ gives $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=ab$. For example, $8$ and $15$ are coprime, so the lcm is $120$. If $a\mid b$, the gcd is $a$ and the lcm is $b$, again giving the identity; $4,12$ illustrate this case. Equality of inputs is allowed too: gcd and lcm of $a,a$ both equal $a$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对两个正输入，若 gcd 为 $1$，则 $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=ab$。例如 $8,15$ 互素，lcm 为 $120$。若 $a\mid b$，gcd 为 $a$、lcm 为 $b$，仍得到恒等式，$4,12$ 就是这种情形。输入相等也允许：$a,a$ 的 gcd 与 lcm 都等于 $a$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nonzero signed integers, use positive gcd and the absolute-value extension of lcm. Then the right side becomes $|ab|$, not necessarily $ab$: for $-4,6$, the product is $2\cdot12=24=|(-4)6|$. If one input is zero and the other is nonzero, the convention $\operatorname{lcm}(a,0)=0$ preserves $\gcd(a,0)\operatorname{lcm}(a,0)=|a\cdot0|=0$. If both are zero, adopting the separate convention $\gcd(0,0)=0$ makes the product identity read $0=0$; the quotient $|ab|/\gcd(a,b)$ must not be used there because it would divide by zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对非零的带符号整数，采用正的 gcd 与 lcm 的绝对值扩展后，右边应写 $|ab|$，不一定等于 $ab$。例如 $-4,6$ 给出 $2\cdot12=24=|(-4)6|$。若一个输入为零而另一个非零，约定 $\operatorname{lcm}(a,0)=0$ 后仍有 $\gcd(a,0)\operatorname{lcm}(a,0)=|a\cdot0|=0$。若两者都为零，同时采用另行约定 $\gcd(0,0)=0$ 后，乘积恒等式就变成 $0=0$；此时不能使用商 $|ab|/\gcd(a,b)$，因为会除以零。
