# 非银行金融、市场型融资与影子银行描述不同维度因而重叠但不等价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Nonbank finance, market-based finance, and shadow banking describe different dimensions, so they overlap without being equivalent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 三个标签分别问什么
> - **非银行金融**先按机构或监管边界分类：主体不是传统存款银行。
> - **市场型融资**按融资渠道和运作系统分类：资金经由证券市场、参与机构与基础设施提供。
> - **影子银行**是更窄的功能与脆弱性标签：关注传统银行体系外的类银行信用中介和挤兑、杠杆或风险转移问题。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Nonbank finance classifies an entity or regulatory perimeter. Market-based finance classifies a channel and operating system built around markets. Shadow banking is a narrower functional and vulnerability label for bank-like credit intermediation outside traditional banking. The questions differ, so the sets cannot be collapsed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一家养老基金用长期稳定负债持有公开债券，可以同时属于非银行金融和市场型融资，却未必有短期融资或易挤兑的影子银行结构。反过来，一条依赖短期回购为贷款或证券融资的非银行链条，则可同时落入三个范围。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pension fund with long, stable liabilities holding public bonds can be both nonbank and market-based finance without necessarily forming a short-funded, run-prone shadow-banking structure. A nonbank chain that relies on short-term repo to finance loans or securities can fall within all three descriptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证券化也只是一种交易结构。证券化资产由长期投资者持有、不加短期杠杆时，不能仅凭“证券化”三个字判定影子银行风险；若该链条又发行可迅速撤回的索取权并依赖续作融资，风险诊断才会改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Securitisation is a transaction structure, not a risk verdict. Long-term investors can hold securitised assets without short-term leverage, whereas a chain that issues runnable claims and depends on funding rollover requires a different diagnosis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> “该机构是非银行，所以它是影子银行”缺少了哪一层判断？
>
> **答案：** 缺少对活动和资产负债表的功能诊断：它是否做信用中介，是否有期限、流动性、杠杆、短期融资或不完全风险转移等脆弱性。

## 来源与核验

- [Financial Stability Board, Non-Bank Financial Intermediation](https://www.fsb.org/work-of-the-fsb/financial-innovation-and-structural-change/non-bank-financial-intermediation/)：核对非银行生态的广泛性，以及从中识别类银行系统性风险活动的狭口径。
- [Bank of England, *Assessing the Resilience of Market-Based Finance*](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/report/2021/assessing-the-resilience-of-market-based-finance)：核对市场型融资包含市场、非银行机构和基础设施，因而与单纯机构分类不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- The FSB page was checked for the broad nonbank ecosystem and the narrower bank-like systemic-risk activities identified within it.
- The Bank of England report was checked for market-based finance as a system of markets, nonbank institutions, and infrastructure rather than a simple entity label.
