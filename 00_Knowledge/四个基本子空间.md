# Four fundamental subspaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*四个基本子空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real matrix, the **four fundamental subspaces** organize two questions about inputs and two about outputs. Which outputs can the matrix produce, and which linear constraints must those outputs satisfy? Which input changes produce no change at all, and which input directions are perpendicular to these invisible changes? Keeping the input and output sides separate makes the four spaces easier to understand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一个实矩阵，**四个基本子空间**把关于输入与输出的四个问题放在一起：矩阵能产生哪些输出，这些输出必须满足哪些线性约束？哪些输入改变量完全不改变输出，哪些输入方向又与这些“看不见的改变量”垂直？先分清输入侧与输出侧，四个空间就容易理解了。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One matrix, two sides
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同一个矩阵的输入侧与输出侧*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the real $3\times4$ matrix below. It takes four input coordinates to three output coordinates:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑下面的 $3\times4$ 实矩阵。它把四个输入坐标变成三个输出坐标：

$$A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2&0&0\\0&0&1&0\\1&2&1&0\end{bmatrix},\qquad
Ax=\begin{bmatrix}x_1+2x_2\\x_3\\x_1+2x_2+x_3\end{bmatrix},\qquad
x=\begin{bmatrix}x_1\\x_2\\x_3\\x_4\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write an output as $b=Ax$. Every output has the form $(s,t,s+t)^T$ with real $s,t$, and every such vector occurs by choosing $x=(s,0,t,0)^T$. The superscript $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Thus the [[列空间|column space]] is a plane of reachable outputs. The relation $b_3=b_1+b_2$ can also be written $(-1,-1,1)b=0$: the column vector $(-1,-1,1)^T$ gives a cancelling combination of the output coordinates and belongs to the [[左零空间|left nullspace]]. In fact, $A^Ty=0$ requires $y_1+y_3=0$ and $y_2+y_3=0$, so every left-null vector is a multiple of $(-1,-1,1)^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把输出记作 $b=Ax$。每个输出都形如 $(s,t,s+t)^T$，其中 $s,t$ 为实数，而取 $x=(s,0,t,0)^T$ 就能得到每个这样的向量。上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。所以[[列空间]]是可达输出所成的一个平面。关系 $b_3=b_1+b_2$ 也可以写成 $(-1,-1,1)b=0$：列向量 $(-1,-1,1)^T$ 提供一组使输出坐标相消的权重，属于[[左零空间]]。进一步解 $A^Ty=0$，得到 $y_1+y_3=0$、$y_2+y_3=0$，所以每个左零向量都是 $(-1,-1,1)^T$ 的倍数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the input side, solving $Ax=0$ requires $x_1=-2x_2$ and $x_3=0$, while $x_4$ is free. Hence the [[零空间|nullspace]] consists of the invisible input changes $(-2u,u,0,v)^T$, where $u,v$ are arbitrary real numbers. The independent coefficient rows $(1,2,0,0)$ and $(0,0,1,0)$ span the [[行空间|row space]], because the third row is their sum; they describe the two combinations of inputs that the matrix reads. These four spaces have the following bases, where $\operatorname{span}$ denotes all [[张成|linear combinations]]:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在输入侧，解 $Ax=0$ 要求 $x_1=-2x_2$、$x_3=0$，而 $x_4$ 自由。因此[[零空间]]由不可见的输入改变量 $(-2u,u,0,v)^T$ 组成，其中 $u,v$ 为任意实数。两个无关系数行 $(1,2,0,0)$、$(0,0,1,0)$ 张成[[行空间]]，因为第三行就是它们的和；它们描述矩阵读取的两种输入组合。四个空间分别有下面这些基；$\operatorname{span}$ 表示全部线性组合，即[[张成]]：

$$\begin{aligned}
C(A)&=\operatorname{span}\{(1,0,1)^T,(0,1,1)^T\},\\
N(A^T)&=\operatorname{span}\{(-1,-1,1)^T\},\\
C(A^T)&=\operatorname{span}\{(1,2,0,0)^T,(0,0,1,0)^T\},\\
N(A)&=\operatorname{span}\{(-2,1,0,0)^T,(0,0,0,1)^T\}.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each listed pair is independent, and the displayed coordinate descriptions show that it generates the entire space. The first two spaces live in $\mathbb R^3$ and have dimensions two and one; the last two live in $\mathbb R^4$ and each have dimension two. A [[基|basis]] supplies independent generators, and its length is the [[维数|dimension]] of that space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

列出的每一对向量都无关，而上面的坐标描述说明它们生成了整个相应空间。前两个空间位于 $\mathbb R^3$，维数分别为二、一；后两个位于 $\mathbb R^4$，维数各为二。[[基]]提供无关的生成向量，其长度就是该空间的[[维数]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Definitions and dimensions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一般定义与维数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$, where $m,n$ are positive integers. Write $r=\operatorname{rank}(A)$ for its [[矩阵秩|rank]], defined as the dimension of its column space. The four spaces are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$，其中 $m,n$ 是正整数。记 $r=\operatorname{rank}(A)$ 为[[矩阵秩|秩]]，即列空间维数。四个空间分别为

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Space | Members | Ambient space | Dimension |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Column space $C(A)$ | All outputs $Ax$ | $\mathbb R^m$ | $r$ |
| Left nullspace $N(A^T)$ | All $y$ satisfying $A^Ty=0$ | $\mathbb R^m$ | $m-r$ |
| Row space $C(A^T)$ | All combinations of rows, written as columns | $\mathbb R^n$ | $r$ |
| Nullspace $N(A)$ | All $x$ satisfying $Ax=0$ | $\mathbb R^n$ | $n-r$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 子空间 | 成员 | 环境空间 | 维数 |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 列空间 $C(A)$ | 全部输出 $Ax$ | $\mathbb R^m$ | $r$ |
| 左零空间 $N(A^T)$ | 满足 $A^Ty=0$ 的全部 $y$ | $\mathbb R^m$ | $m-r$ |
| 行空间 $C(A^T)$ | 全部行组合，按列向量排坐标 | $\mathbb R^n$ | $r$ |
| 零空间 $N(A)$ | 满足 $Ax=0$ 的全部 $x$ | $\mathbb R^n$ | $n-r$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The dimension counts follow from two separate theorems. [[行秩等于列秩|Row rank equals column rank]] gives $\dim C(A^T)=r$. [[秩零度定理|Rank–nullity]] applied to $A:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ gives $\dim N(A)=n-r$; applied to $A^T:\mathbb R^m\to\mathbb R^n$ it gives $\dim N(A^T)=m-r$. The dimensions add to the ambient dimension separately on each side.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

维数计数来自两个独立定理。[[行秩等于列秩]]给出 $\dim C(A^T)=r$。对 $A:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R^m$ 应用[[秩零度定理]]，得到 $\dim N(A)=n-r$；再对 $A^T:\mathbb R^m\to\mathbb R^n$ 应用它，得到 $\dim N(A^T)=m-r$。每一侧的两个维数，分别加成那一侧的环境维数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the standard [[内积|inner product]] $u^Tv$, the input pair and the output pair are [[基本子空间正交补|orthogonal-complement pairs]]: $N(A)=C(A^T)^\perp$ and $N(A^T)=C(A)^\perp$. The superscript $\perp$ denotes the [[正交补|orthogonal complement]], all vectors perpendicular to the given space. For example, $Ax=0$ says exactly that every row has zero inner product with $x$, hence so does every row combination. This explains why null directions are invisible to all row measurements. The full complement theorem and its decomposition consequences have their own entry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用标准[[内积]] $u^Tv$ 时，输入侧与输出侧各构成一对[[基本子空间正交补|正交补]]：$N(A)=C(A^T)^\perp$、$N(A^T)=C(A)^\perp$。上标 $\perp$ 表示[[正交补]]，即与给定空间全部向量正交的向量集合。例如 $Ax=0$ 恰好表示每一行与 $x$ 的内积为零，所以每个行组合与 $x$ 的内积也为零。这解释了为什么所有行测量都看不到零空间方向。完整的正交补定理及其分解后果有独立条目。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every $y\in N(A^T)$ imposes $y^Tb=0$ on a reachable output $b=Ax$, since $y^TAx=0$. In the example this is $-b_1-b_2+b_3=0$. The [[左零空间相容性|left-nullspace compatibility theorem]] explains why satisfying **all** these constraints is also sufficient for reachability; a single selected constraint need not be sufficient in general.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个 $y\in N(A^T)$ 都对可达输出 $b=Ax$ 施加约束 $y^Tb=0$，因为 $y^TAx=0$。本例就是 $-b_1-b_2+b_3=0$。[[左零空间相容性]]说明，满足**全部**此类约束也足以保证可达；一般情况下，仅检查挑出的一条约束不一定足够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the ambient spaces and scalar convention explicit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 不能混用环境空间

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rows have $n$ coordinates and columns have $m$. For a $4\times7$ real matrix, the left nullspace is a subspace of $\mathbb R^4$, while the nullspace is a subspace of $\mathbb R^7$. Even if $m=n$, input and output roles remain different; equality of their dimensions does not identify their members. For a zero matrix, the column and row spaces are $\{0\}$, while the two nullspaces fill their respective ambient spaces. Thus a “space” here may have dimension zero, and the four named spaces need not be four distinct sets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行有 $n$ 个坐标，列有 $m$ 个坐标。对一个 $4\times7$ 实矩阵，左零空间是 $\mathbb R^4$ 的子空间，零空间则是 $\mathbb R^7$ 的子空间。即使 $m=n$，输入与输出的角色仍然不同，维数相等不意味着成员相同。零矩阵的列空间和行空间都是 $\{0\}$，两个零空间却各自充满所在的环境空间。因此这里的“空间”可以是零维的，四个名称也不保证对应四个互不相同的集合。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a complex matrix $A\in\mathbb C^{m\times n}$, specify the standard Hermitian inner product $\langle u,v\rangle=u^*v$. The star means [[矩阵转置|conjugate transpose]]. The orthogonal geometry uses
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对复矩阵 $A\in\mathbb C^{m\times n}$，要明确采用标准 Hermitian 内积 $\langle u,v\rangle=u^*v$，星号表示[[矩阵转置|共轭转置]]。正交几何使用的是

$$\underbrace{C(A),\ N(A^*)}_{\mathbb C^m},\qquad
\underbrace{C(A^*),\ N(A)}_{\mathbb C^n}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Their complex dimensions are still $r,m-r,r,n-r$. However, the **algebraic row space**, with rows merely turned into columns, is $C(A^T)$; it need not equal $C(A^*)$. For example, if $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&i\end{bmatrix}$ with $i^2=-1$, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它们的复维数仍依次为 $r,m-r,r,n-r$。但是，仅把行竖写成列的**代数行空间**是 $C(A^T)$，它不一定等于 $C(A^*)$。例如 $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&i\end{bmatrix}$，其中 $i^2=-1$，则

$$C(A^T)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{(1,i)^T\},\qquad
C(A^*)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{(1,-i)^T\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These lines differ: matching their first coordinates would force a scalar multiplier of one, which cannot change $i$ to $-i$. Likewise, $N(A^T)$ and $N(A^*)$ are different conventions for left-null column vectors in general; the [[左零空间|left-nullspace definition]] distinguishes them. For Hermitian geometry it is $N(A^*)=C(A)^\perp$ and $N(A)=C(A^*)^\perp$. The identity $y^*Ax=(A^*y)^*x$ explains the conjugation. Over real numbers, $A^*=A^T$ and the two conventions coincide.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这两条复直线不同：第一坐标相等会迫使比例系数为一，但它无法把 $i$ 变成 $-i$。同样，一般情况下，$N(A^T)$ 与 $N(A^*)$ 是两种不同的左零列向量约定；[[左零空间]]中明确区分了它们。Hermitian 几何中成立的是 $N(A^*)=C(A)^\perp$、$N(A)=C(A^*)^\perp$。恒等式 $y^*Ax=(A^*y)^*x$ 解释了共轭的来源。实数情况下 $A^*=A^T$，两种约定才合在一起。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To compute bases, first identify the space and its ambient coordinates, then follow [[RREF读取子空间基|the appropriate RREF rule]]. [[行操作与四个子空间|Row operations]] preserve the row space and nullspace but can move the column space and left nullspace. The four-space picture does not license copying all four bases from a row-reduced matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际求基时，先确定要找的空间及其环境坐标，再使用[[RREF读取子空间基|对应的 RREF 规则]]。[[行操作与四个子空间|行操作]]保持行空间与零空间，却可能改变列空间与左零空间的具体位置。四空间框架并不允许从行化简后的矩阵中直接照抄四组基。
