# 未获补偿的后续知识收益和中间品加价使Romer均衡研发低于模型内社会最优
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Uncompensated benefits to future research and intermediate-input markups make equilibrium research lower than the social optimum within the Romer model.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 [[Romer品种扩张模型]] 的设定内，社会规划者评价新设计的全部资源收益，而私人研究回报由 [[Romer专利价值|可取得的专利收益]] 决定。二者之间有两项明确缺口，不能只用“技术很重要”来说明研发不足。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within the [[Romer品种扩张模型|Romer expanding-variety model]], the planner values all resource benefits of a design, while private research is rewarded through [[Romer专利价值|capturable patent returns]]. Two specific wedges explain the difference; the importance of technology alone does not establish underinvestment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一，[[Romer研发知识外溢|新设计提高未来研究者的研发效率]]，但原设计者不能向这些研究者收取相应费用。这项未获补偿的收益不会进入专利价格，因而也不会完整进入 [[Romer研发人力配置|研究岗位可支付的工资]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, [[Romer研发知识外溢|a new design raises future researchers' productivity]], but its creator cannot charge those researchers for the benefit. The return is absent from patent value and therefore from the full remuneration that could support [[Romer研发人力配置|research employment]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二，[[Romer中间品加价|中间品加价]]使机器的使用量低于按资源边际成本配置时的用量，并使专利持有者可取得的经营收益不能代表该设计的全部社会价值。即使设计能够排他出售，也不会因此自动解决这项市场势力造成的配置缺口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Second, [[Romer中间品加价|intermediate-input markups]] restrict machine use relative to allocation at marginal resource cost, and the operating returns captured by a patent holder do not represent the design's full social value. Excludability permits sale of the design but does not itself remove this market-power wedge.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原文可用两种平衡增长率把方向表达出来。令 $\gamma=1-\alpha-\beta\in(0,1)$、$\Theta=\alpha/(\alpha+\beta)\in(0,1)$、$\Lambda=\Theta/\gamma$；$H$ 是固定总人力资本，$\delta>0$ 是研发效率，$\rho>0$ 与 $\sigma>0$ 是时间贴现率和效用曲率。私人均衡与规划者的增长式分别为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original paper expresses the comparison through balanced growth rates. Define $\gamma=1-\alpha-\beta\in(0,1)$, $\Theta=\alpha/(\alpha+\beta)\in(0,1)$ and $\Lambda=\Theta/\gamma$. Total human capital $H$ is fixed, $\delta$ is research productivity, and $\rho,\sigma$ describe time preference and utility curvature. The decentralized and planner growth rates are:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
g=\frac{\delta H-\Lambda\rho}{\Lambda\sigma+1},\qquad
g^*=\frac{\delta H-\Theta\rho}{\Theta\sigma+(1-\Theta)}.
$$

当两个正增长内点都存在并满足跨期可行条件时，$g^*>g$，而 $H_A=g/\delta$，所以规划者投入更多人力研发。从 $\Lambda$ 到更小的 $\Theta$ 体现纠正加价，从分母的 $1$ 到 $1-\Theta$ 体现纳入后续知识收益。这个比较承接 [[Romer平衡增长率]] 的均衡推导，不把更快增长本身当作任何情境下福利更高的证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When both positive interior paths exist and satisfy the intertemporal conditions, $g^*>g$. Since $H_A=g/\delta$, the planner devotes more human capital to research. Replacing $\Lambda$ with the smaller $\Theta$ reflects correction of the markup, while replacing $1$ with $1-\Theta$ incorporates subsequent research benefits. This comparison builds on [[Romer平衡增长率|equilibrium growth]]; faster growth by itself is not a general welfare criterion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，模型内的研发不足是有来源的福利方向，但现实补贴判断还要知道财政成本、实施方式和外部收益大小。原文在比较两条平衡增长路径时也未解出政策转换的完整转轨。不能只看上面 $g^*>g$ 就给出某项现实补贴的最优金额或断言补贴越大越好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Underinvestment is therefore a model-based welfare result. Actual subsidy design additionally requires fiscal costs, implementation details and the size of external benefits. The paper's balanced-path comparison also does not solve the complete policy transition. The growth inequality alone identifies neither an optimal real-world subsidy amount nor a case for unlimited subsidies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
