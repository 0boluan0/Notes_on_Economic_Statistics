# 现金流量利息保障倍数是经营活动现金流量净额与利息支出之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cash-flow interest coverage is net operating cash flow divided by interest charges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CPA定义为 $CFO/I$，分母与[[利息保障倍数]]相同，包含本期费用化和资本化利息。ABC的经营活动现金流量净额246、利息110，得 $246/110\approx2.2364$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CPA defines this as $CFO/I$, using the same denominator as [[利息保障倍数|earnings-based interest coverage]], including expensed and capitalised interest. ABC's operating cash flow of 246 and interest of 110 give about 2.2364.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它用现金基础观察支付保障，但必须知道CFO是否已扣利息，以及分母是应计利息还是实际已付利息。CPA示例的利息付款列筹资现金流；若另一报表分类不同，直接搬同一公式可能不具同样的“付息前现金”含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The measure examines cash-based coverage, but one must know whether CFO already deducts interest and whether the denominator is accrued interest or cash interest paid. CPA's example classifies interest payments within financing cash flow. A different reporting classification may remove the same pre-interest-cash interpretation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保持[[财务比率口径匹配]]，并分别看本金、投资和营运现金需要。过去现金流不是未来支付保证；利息为零时比率未定义，不能自动赋予普通“无限安全”的评级。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply [[财务比率口径匹配|basis matching]] and separately examine principal, investment, and operating needs. Historical cash flow does not guarantee future payment. Zero interest makes the ratio undefined rather than assigning an ordinary infinitely-safe rating.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
