# Positive determinant and positive definiteness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正行列式与正定性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A positive [[行列式|determinant]] combines information about all directions into one number. For a real [[实对称矩阵|symmetric matrix]], it multiplies the real eigenvalues, so two negative factors can produce a positive result. [[正定矩阵|Positive definiteness]] needs every direction to be positive; a positive product does not establish that.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正的[[行列式]]把各方向的信息合并成一个数。对[[实对称矩阵]]，它是全部实特征值的乘积，所以两个负因子也能得到正结果。[[正定矩阵|正定性]]要求每个方向都正；乘积为正，并不能证明每个因子为正。

> [!example] Two negative directions, positive product · 两个负方向与一个正乘积
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]] below has a positive determinant but gives a negative quadratic value at $e_1=(1,0)^T$. The notation $\operatorname{diag}$ places the listed numbers on the main diagonal and sets all other entries to zero. Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 下面的[[对角矩阵]]行列式为正，却在 $e_1=(1,0)^T$ 上产生负二次型值。记号 $\operatorname{diag}$ 把列出的数依次放在主对角线上，其余元素全部设为零；$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。
>
> $$D=\operatorname{diag}(-1,-2),\qquad \det D=2,\qquad e_1^TDe_1=-1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Indeed, $x^TDx=-x_1^2-2x_2^2<0$ for every nonzero real $x$, so $D$ is [[负定矩阵|negative definite]]. This failure already occurs in dimension two. In dimension one, $A=[a]$ has determinant $a$, and a positive determinant does imply positive definiteness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际上，对每个非零实向量 $x$，都有 $x^TDx=-x_1^2-2x_2^2<0$，所以 $D$ [[负定矩阵|负定]]。反例在二维就已出现；一维时 $A=[a]$ 的行列式就是 $a$，行列式为正，确实足以推出正定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The determinant records parity, not every sign
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*行列式记录负号的奇偶，不记录每个符号*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ be symmetric with finite $n\ge1$. Its [[特征对|eigenvalues]] $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$ are real, and the [[行列式与特征值乘积|determinant-product identity]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ 实对称，$n\ge1$ 为有限整数。它的[[特征对|特征值]] $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$ 都为实数，由[[行列式与特征值乘积]]恒等式得到

$$\det A=\prod_{j=1}^n\lambda_j.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\det A>0$, no eigenvalue is zero and the number of negative eigenvalues is even, counting repeated eigenvalues with their multiplicities. That number may be $0$, $2$, $4$, and so on. If $\det A<0$, the number is odd, which rules out positive definiteness. A zero determinant means at least one zero eigenvalue and also rules out positive definiteness, but by itself does not decide whether the matrix is semidefinite or indefinite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\det A>0$，特征值没有零，负特征值的个数为偶数，重复特征值按重数计数。这个数可能是 $0$、$2$、$4$，等等。若 $\det A<0$，负特征值个数为奇数，所以不可能正定。若行列式为零，则至少有一个零特征值，也不可能正定，但单凭这一点不能区分半定与不定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive definiteness implies $\lambda_j>0$ for every $j$, hence $\det A>0$. This proves necessity. The missing reverse implication is exposed by the following three-dimensional example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正定要求每个 $\lambda_j>0$，因此必有 $\det A>0$，这证明了必要性。下面的三维例子揭示反向推理的缺口：

$$A=\operatorname{diag}(-1,-2,3),\qquad \det A=6.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $e_1=(1,0,0)^T$ the quadratic value is $-1$, while at $e_3=(0,0,1)^T$ it is $3$. Thus $A$ is [[不定二次型|indefinite]], with two negative eigendirections and one positive one. A positive determinant can therefore occur for a positive-definite, negative-definite or indefinite matrix, depending on dimension and the actual signs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $e_1=(1,0,0)^T$ 上二次型值为 $-1$，在 $e_3=(0,0,1)^T$ 上却为 $3$。所以 $A$ 对应[[不定二次型]]，有两个负特征方向和一个正特征方向。可见，正行列式可能出现在正定、负定或不定矩阵中，具体要看维数与实际符号。

## How much extra information is enough?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*还需要多少额外信息*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real symmetric $2\times2$ matrix $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix}$, the complete test is $a>0$ and $\det A=ac-b^2>0$. The first condition chooses the positive sign rather than the negative-definite possibility. It is the first [[顺序主子式|leading principal minor]]. In dimension $n$, [[Sylvester 正定判据|Sylvester’s criterion]] requires all $n$ leading principal minors to be positive; the determinant is only the last one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实对称二阶矩阵 $\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b&c\end{pmatrix}$，完整判据是 $a>0$ 且 $\det A=ac-b^2>0$。第一个条件排除了负定的可能，它就是第一个[[顺序主子式]]。在 $n$ 维，[[Sylvester 正定判据]]要求全部 $n$ 个顺序主子式都正；整个行列式只是最后一个。

> [!warning] Positive diagonal plus positive determinant still fails in higher dimensions · 高维再加正对角仍不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $M=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&2\\2&1&2\\2&2&1\end{pmatrix}$, every diagonal entry is positive and $\det M=5>0$. Yet $x=(1,-1,0)^T$ gives $x^TMx=-2$. The leading order-two minor is $1-4=-3$, so the full test rejects it. The [[正对角元的正定边界|diagonal-entry boundary]] explains why coordinate-axis checks miss such a direction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $M=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&2\\2&1&2\\2&2&1\end{pmatrix}$，每个对角元都正，且 $\det M=5>0$，但 $x=(1,-1,0)^T$ 给出 $x^TMx=-2$。它的二阶顺序主子式为 $1-4=-3$，所以完整判据能否定正定性。[[正对角元的正定边界]]解释了坐标轴检查为何漏掉这样的方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is one useful extra premise: if $A$ is already known to be [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefinite]], a nonzero determinant excludes zero eigenvalues and forces all of them to be positive. This is the [[正定与半正定可逆性|semidefinite invertibility criterion]]. The premise supplies the missing sign information; the determinant alone never supplied it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个有用的额外前提是：若已经知道 $A$ [[半正定矩阵|半正定]]，那么行列式非零就能排除零特征值，使所有特征值都严格为正。这就是[[正定与半正定可逆性|半正定的可逆性判据]]。缺少的符号信息来自半正定前提，而不是行列式本身。
