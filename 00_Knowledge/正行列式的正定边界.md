# 实对称矩阵的行列式为正仍不足以推出正定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A positive determinant cannot rule out an even number of negative eigendirections*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 最小反例
> 对实对称矩阵，$\det A=\prod_i\lambda_i$。因此 $\det A>0$ 只说明负特征值个数为偶数，并不说明这个数为零。反例
> $$A=\operatorname{diag}(-1,-2,3)$$
> 的行列式为 $6>0$，却有两个负方向。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a real symmetric matrix, $\det A=\prod_i\lambda_i$. A positive determinant shows only that the number of negative eigenvalues is even, not that it is zero. The displayed diagonal matrix has determinant $6$ and two negative eigendirections.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $x=e_1$ 就有 $x^TAx=-1<0$，所以它不正定。正定确实推出 $\det A>0$，但反向不成立：determinant 把所有方向的符号乘成一个数，偶数个负号会互相抵消。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking $x=e_1$ gives a negative quadratic value. Positive definiteness does imply a positive determinant, but the converse fails because multiplication loses the locations of the signs and an even number of negatives cancel.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二维也不能只看 determinant：$\operatorname{diag}(-1,-2)$ 的 determinant 为正，却是负定。二维正定还需一个顺序主子式为正，例如 $a_{11}>0$；高维需所有 Sylvester 顺序主子式或其他完整判据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The failure already appears in two dimensions: $\operatorname{diag}(-1,-2)$ has positive determinant but is negative definite. A full positive-definiteness criterion needs the remaining leading minors or an equivalent complete test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个非奇异实对称矩阵 determinant 为负，关于负特征值个数能推出什么？
>
> **答案：** 负特征值个数为奇数；因此矩阵不可能正定。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If a nonsingular real symmetric matrix has negative determinant, what can you conclude about the number of its negative eigenvalues?
>
> **Answer:** It has an odd number of negative eigenvalues, so it cannot be positive definite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
