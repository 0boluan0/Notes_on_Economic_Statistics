# 给定生产条件与可分配收入时工资成本提高会减少留给资本的剩余

工资与资本收益要从同一份可分配收入中支付。如果产出、销售收入和其他应扣成本不变，工资账单增加，留给资本的剩余就会减少。**保持什么不变，是这句话成立的关键。** 它不表示现实中任何加薪都会使利润按同一金额下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Wages and the return to capital are paid from the same distributable income. If output, sales revenue and other relevant costs remain unchanged, a larger wage bill leaves less for capital. **The maintained conditions are essential.** This does not say that every observed wage increase reduces profit by the same amount.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $V$ 为扣除本次分析中其他应扣项目后、待分给劳动与资本的收入，$W$ 为每单位劳动工资，$L$ 为劳动量，$\Pi$ 为留给资本的剩余：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $V$ be income remaining for labour and capital after the other deductions relevant to the analysis, $W$ the wage per unit of labour, $L$ labour input and $\Pi$ the residual for capital:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Pi=V-WL.
$$

Ricardo 用销售价值 1,000 的工业生产例说明：同一生产过程工资总额若从 600 变成 800，余下部分就从 400 变成 200。这一步是分配算术。若还要解释工资为什么提高，原书给出的通道是食物和必需品生产变难，使 [[李嘉图自然工资|维持劳动家庭所需的工资成本]] 上升，而该工业生产的收入并未因此同步增加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ricardo illustrates the arithmetic with manufactured output worth 1,000: increasing the wage bill for the same process from 600 to 800 reduces the remainder from 400 to 200. His explanation for the wage increase is a separate step. More difficult production of food and necessities raises the [[李嘉图自然工资|cost of maintaining labouring households]] without correspondingly increasing revenue from the unchanged industrial process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 $\Pi$ 必须按已列出的成本范围理解。若尚未扣资本机会成本，它是资本剩余，不是已经扣尽所有要素成本的纯经济利润；若现实企业还有材料、折旧、利息或税费，不能把销售收入直接减工资当作其净利润。相关口径可对照 [[生产者剩余与利润]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Interpret $\Pi$ on the stated cost basis. If the opportunity cost of capital has not been deducted, it is a capital residual, not pure economic profit after all factor costs. A real firm's materials, depreciation, interest or taxes also preclude identifying sales minus wages with net profit. Compare the cost distinction in [[生产者剩余与利润|producer surplus and profit]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若工资、生产率、雇佣量或售价一起变化，就应重新求 $V-WL$，而不能只看工资的方向。尤其是 [[粮价与货币贬值|普遍名义涨价]]，与食品相对变贵不同；所有账面数字同比变化时，利润率可以不变。长期积累的问题还要进一步研究剩余与资本的比率，以及经营者是否愿意再投，见 [[利润率与积累压力]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When productivity, employment or selling prices change with wages, recalculate $V-WL$ rather than relying on the direction of wages alone. [[粮价与货币贬值|A general nominal price increase]] differs from relatively dearer food: proportional changes in all monetary amounts can leave the profit rate unchanged. Long-run accumulation further requires considering returns relative to capital and the incentive to reinvest; see [[利润率与积累压力|profit rates and accumulation pressure]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ricardo（1821），[《原理》VI.1–6](https://www.econlib.org/library/Ricardo/ricP.html?chapter_num=7#6.1)：支持给定工业生产条件下工资与资本收益的分配、1,000／600／800 数例，以及食品生产困难的工资成本通道。
- $V$ 按本卡列明的成本口径定义；400、200 已分别相减核验。现代企业的净利润还需按适用的收入与成本项目核算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chapter VI supplies the fixed-production comparison, numerical example and food-cost mechanism. The definition of $V$ makes the cost basis explicit without attributing a modern income statement to Ricardo. Both residuals have been recomputed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
