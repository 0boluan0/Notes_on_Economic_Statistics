# 多模态大模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Multimodal Large Language Models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 让一个生成模型联合处理文字、图像及其他模态，把感知结果接入语言推理和输出。
> **具体锚点：** 回答图表问题需要先辨认坐标轴、图例和数值关系，再用语言表达；仅生成流畅描述不代表视觉定位正确。
> **核心难点：** 模态对齐会压缩信息，模型可能靠语言先验猜答案；OCR、空间定位、细粒度计数和生成质量必须分开测。
> **为什么重要：** 不同架构都叫“多模态”，但输入粒度、融合位置、训练目标和可生成模态差异很大。
> **继续：** 先看视觉表示如何变成语言模型可读 token，再区分桥接式与早融合；用 [[LLM 评测]] 的证据原则检查。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Problem addressed:** Let a generative model jointly process text, images, and other modalities by connecting perception to language reasoning and output.
> **Concrete anchor:** Answering a chart question requires identifying axes, legends, and numerical relationships before expressing them in language; a fluent description does not prove correct visual grounding.
> **Central difficulty:** Cross-modal alignment compresses information, and a model may guess from language priors. OCR, spatial grounding, fine-grained counting, reasoning, and generation quality need separate measurement.
> **Why it matters:** Architectures called multimodal can differ substantially in input granularity, fusion location, training objective, and which modalities they generate.
> **Continue with:** Trace how visual representations become language-model-readable tokens, distinguish bridge-based from early-fusion designs, and apply the evidence principles in [[LLM 评测|LLM Evaluation]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 视觉与文本表示对齐
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Aligning Visual and Text Representations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图像编码器把像素变成 patch/features；投影层、Q-Former 或 cross-attention 把视觉信息接到语言空间。对比学习先对齐全局图文表示，生成训练则要求模型按条件预测文本或其他 token。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An image encoder converts pixels into patches or features. A projection layer, Q-Former, or cross-attention connects visual information to the language space. Contrastive learning can align global image and text representations, while generative training asks the model to predict text or other tokens conditionally.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若输入图像为 $H\times W$、patch 为 $P\times P$，最简单的视觉序列约有 $(H/P)(W/P)$ 个 token。提高分辨率会二次增加 patch 数；切片、动态分辨率或 token pooling 在细节、序列长度和计算之间取舍。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an $H\times W$ image and $P\times P$ patches, the simplest visual sequence has about $(H/P)(W/P)$ tokens. Increasing resolution grows patch count quadratically; tiling, dynamic resolution, and token pooling trade detail against sequence length and computation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对比学习常把匹配图文 pair 的表示拉近、非匹配 pair 推远，善于全局语义检索；它不必保留每个字符或坐标。生成式问答需要从视觉 token 提取任务相关细节，因此“CLIP 表示很好”不能自动推出 OCR 或空间 reasoning 很强。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Contrastive learning typically brings matched image–text representations closer and separates mismatched pairs, supporting global semantic retrieval. It need not preserve every character or coordinate. Generative question answering must extract task-specific details from visual tokens, so strong CLIP-style representations do not automatically imply strong OCR or spatial reasoning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 冻结编码器加桥接器
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Frozen Encoders with a Bridge*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CLIP+语言模型一类方案可冻结强视觉和语言组件，只训练中间桥接，成本低且保留原能力。但桥接瓶颈可能丢失细节，组件各自的表征也未必适合精确空间任务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CLIP-plus-language-model designs can freeze strong visual and language components and train only a bridge, reducing cost and preserving existing capabilities. The bridge can become an information bottleneck, and each component's representation may be unsuitable for precise spatial tasks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

BLIP-2 的 Q-Former 用一组可学习 query 从冻结图像编码器抽取有限视觉表示，再映射给冻结语言模型。该设计说明 bridge 不是简单格式转换，而是在有限 token 预算下选择哪些视觉信息能进入语言模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BLIP-2's Q-Former uses learned queries to extract a limited visual representation from a frozen image encoder and map it to a frozen language model. The bridge is therefore not a simple format converter; it selects which visual information enters the language model under a limited token budget.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 视觉指令微调
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Visual Instruction Tuning*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把图像—问题—答案组织成指令数据，使模型学会对话、描述与问答。合成数据能扩覆盖，也会传播教师幻觉和模板偏差。训练答案提到图中不存在的细节时，模型会学会视觉幻觉。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Visual instruction tuning organizes image–question–answer examples so a model learns dialogue, description, and question answering. Synthetic data expands coverage but also propagates teacher hallucinations and template bias. If training answers mention details absent from the image, the model learns visual hallucination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

instruction tuning 改善模型怎样响应视觉请求，不等于提升底层分辨率或新增视觉证据。若错误集中在小字和密集图表，继续增加普通图像描述数据可能只让回答更流畅，不能修复输入中已丢失的像素细节。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Instruction tuning improves how the model responds to visual requests; it does not automatically raise underlying resolution or create missing evidence. If errors cluster on small text and dense charts, adding ordinary image-caption data may only make answers more fluent without recovering pixel detail already lost at input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 早融合与混合模态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Early Fusion and Mixed Modalities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

早融合把不同模态离散成统一 token 序列，在同一模型中建模输入和输出，能支持跨模态生成；代价是序列更长、tokenizer/损失更复杂。桥接式模型和统一生成模型不能只按参数量比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Early fusion discretizes modalities into a unified token sequence and models inputs and outputs in one system, supporting cross-modal generation. The cost is longer sequences and more complex tokenizers and losses. Bridge-based and unified generative models cannot be compared by parameter count alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

融合位置决定互动带宽：后融合各模态先独立压缩，效率高但细粒度交互受限；cross-attention 让语言 token 读取视觉表示；早融合让统一 self-attention 建模，但增加训练与 serving 成本。应从任务所需的跨模态关系决定，而不是追求最“统一”的架构。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fusion location controls interaction bandwidth. Late fusion compresses modalities independently and is efficient but limits fine-grained interaction; cross-attention lets language tokens read visual representations; early fusion uses unified self-attention but raises training and serving cost. The required cross-modal relationships should determine the design rather than a desire for the most unified architecture.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 定位、OCR 与分辨率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Grounding, OCR, and Resolution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

文档/图表任务依赖高分辨率、文字识别、版面顺序和坐标 grounding。缩放或固定 patch 可能让小字消失；先检查模型实际收到的像素/切片，再归因于“推理”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Document and chart tasks depend on high resolution, text recognition, reading order, and coordinate grounding. Resizing or fixed patches can erase small text; inspect the pixels and tiles actually received by the model before blaming reasoning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

grounding 可以输出 box、point、mask 或带坐标 token 的文本。评价时要把“说对对象名称”和“定位对实例”分开；多个相同物体、遮挡和坐标归一化会暴露只靠语言模板的答案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Grounding can output boxes, points, masks, or text containing coordinate tokens. Evaluation should separate naming the correct object from locating the correct instance; repeated objects, occlusion, and coordinate normalization expose answers driven only by language templates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：图表数值错误的排查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Diagnose a Wrong Chart Value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

问题问“蓝线在 2024 年的值是多少”，模型答 80，真实为 60。先确认缩放后年份与刻度仍可辨认；再单独问图例颜色、2024 横坐标和蓝线交点，区分 OCR、legend binding 与坐标读取；最后才检查是否把 60 与 80 的比较做错。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The question asks for the blue line's 2024 value; the model answers 80, while the chart shows 60. First verify that the year and tick labels remain legible after resizing. Then query legend color, the 2024 x-coordinate, and the blue-line intersection separately to distinguish OCR, legend binding, and coordinate reading. Only then inspect whether a comparison between 60 and 80 failed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

制作反事实版本，把蓝线与红线颜色互换而保持数据不变。如果答案仍跟原先的语义类别而不是新颜色走，模型依赖语言先验或旧图例；若随颜色换但数值仍错，问题更可能在坐标定位或分辨率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Create a counterfactual version that swaps blue and red while keeping data fixed. If the answer follows the original semantic category rather than the new color, the model relies on language prior or the old legend. If it follows the color but the value remains wrong, coordinate grounding or resolution is more likely responsible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 评测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Evaluation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分别测识别、定位、关系、OCR、推理和生成；用反事实图像、遮挡和语言先验对照确认模型真的看图。开放式打分需人工核验或明确 rubric，避免只奖励描述长度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Measure recognition, grounding, relationships, OCR, reasoning, and generation separately. Use counterfactual images, occlusion, and language-prior controls to confirm the model actually uses the image. Open-ended scoring needs human verification or an explicit rubric so it does not merely reward description length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 图像移除后答案几乎不变：说明题目可由语言先验作答，重写为图像特有、反事实或细节定位任务。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Answers barely change when the image is removed: the task is solvable from language priors; rewrite it around image-specific, counterfactual, or detail-grounding evidence.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- OCR 错误随字号急剧增加：检查预处理、分辨率和 tiling，再评估识别器，而不是调解码温度。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  OCR errors rise sharply as text shrinks: inspect preprocessing, resolution, and tiling before evaluating recognition; decoding temperature is not the first remedy.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 描述正确但 box 错：分开语义识别与空间 grounding，检查坐标系、resize 映射和实例歧义。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The description is right but the box wrong: separate semantic recognition from spatial grounding and inspect coordinate systems, resize mapping, and instance ambiguity.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 输出幻觉与训练 caption 模板一致：审计合成教师、负样本和无答案样本，要求模型能表达视觉证据不足。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Hallucinations match training-caption templates: audit synthetic teachers, negative examples, and unanswerable cases, and require the model to express insufficient visual evidence.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum Self-Check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 模型答对常识性图片问题，为什么还不能证明它看懂图？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does a correct answer to a commonsense image question not prove image understanding?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 答案可能来自语言先验；需用反事实、遮挡或细节定位测试确认视觉证据被使用。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The answer may come from a language prior. Counterfactuals, occlusion, or detail-grounding tests are needed to confirm use of visual evidence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 冻结视觉编码器加桥接器的主要权衡是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is the main trade-off of a frozen visual encoder with a bridge?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 训练便宜并复用能力，但桥接容量和预训练表征可能丢失精细空间/文字信息。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Training is cheaper and reuses existing capabilities, but bridge capacity and pretrained representations can lose fine spatial or textual information.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 图表数值错误时，先检查什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What should be checked first when a chart value is wrong?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 输入分辨率、裁剪/切片、OCR 与坐标/图例定位，再检查语言推理。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Check input resolution, cropping or tiling, OCR, and coordinate or legend grounding before language reasoning.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么提高输入分辨率不一定只带来好处？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does higher input resolution not bring only benefits?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> patch/token 数通常快速增加，带来更长序列、显存和延迟；还需 bridge 有能力保留并让模型使用新增细节。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Patch or token count grows quickly, raising sequence length, memory, and latency; the bridge and model must also preserve and use the added detail.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
