# 函数形式、误差协方差与识别失败属于不同层且不能用同一种修复互换
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Functional-form, error-covariance, and identification failures are different layers and their remedies are not interchangeable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> “回归有问题”至少要分成三个对象：条件均值怎样表示、误差怎样共同波动、解释变量能否与结构误差分离。三者会在残差或输出中同时留下迹象，却不由同一种工具修复。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A problematic regression must distinguish at least three objects: the representation of the conditional mean, the covariance structure of the errors, and whether regressors are separated from the structural error. The layers can leave overlapping symptoms but require different remedies.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 层 | 典型失败 | 修复针对什么 | 不能自动得到什么 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 均值表示 | 漏掉平方项、交互、趋势、结构变点或错误变换 | 改写 $E(Y\mid X)$ 的候选形式 | 因果识别 |
| 协方差 | 异方差、序列或组内相关 | 稳健方差、WLS/GLS 或依赖模型 | 正确的均值或外生性 |
| 识别 | 遗漏混杂、同时性、解释变量测量误差，或由潜在结果/结构误差驱动的样本选择 | 研究设计、工具变量、选择模型或其他识别结构 | 靠更漂亮残差自动解决 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Layer | Typical failure | What a remedy targets | What it does not automatically deliver |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean representation | Missing squares, interactions, trends, structural breaks, or a wrong transformation | A revised candidate for $E(Y\mid X)$ | Causal identification |
| Covariance | Heteroskedasticity, serial or within-cluster dependence | Robust variance, WLS/GLS, or a dependence model | A correct mean or exogeneity |
| Identification | Omitted confounding, simultaneity, regressor measurement error, or selection driven by potential outcomes or structural errors | Research design, instruments, a selection model, or another identifying structure | A solution from prettier residuals |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，给工资方程加入 $experience^2$ 可以修复经验收益的曲率，却不会使教育与未观测能力突然无关；换稳健标准误可以修复特定推断公式，却不会补回遗漏变量。修复之前先命名失败层，是避免“工具有效但答错问题”的最短路径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding $experience^2$ can repair curvature in a wage equation without making education independent of unobserved ability. Robust standard errors can repair a variance formula without restoring an omitted variable. Naming the failed layer before choosing the remedy prevents a valid tool from answering the wrong question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 换成异方差稳健标准误，能否修复遗漏混杂造成的系数偏差？
>
> **答案：** 不能。它修正推断的协方差估计，不重新识别被混杂污染的目标系数。
