# Layers of regression specification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*回归设定问题的层次*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A regression can describe the average outcome badly, attach the wrong uncertainty to a useful estimate, or answer a different question from the one the researcher intended. **Specification layers distinguish these problems before choosing a remedy.** Adding a curved term, changing a standard error and finding a credible source of causal variation solve different problems, even when the same residual plot first draws attention to them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个回归可能没有描述好平均结果，可能给一个有用估计配上了错误的不确定性，也可能回答了研究者根本不想问的问题。**设定问题分层，就是在选补救方法前先分清这些问题。** 添加曲线项、更换标准误、找到可信的因果变化来源，各自解决不同问题；最初提示它们的，却可能是同一张残差图。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First name the [[估计对象三分|estimand]], the population quantity the analysis is trying to learn. The [[条件期望|conditional mean]] describes the average outcome at given predictor values; a [[条件均值与线性投影|linear projection]] is the best linear summary under a stated squared-error criterion. A causal effect instead asks what would change under an intervention. Calling all three “the regression coefficient” hides the distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先命名[[估计对象三分|估计对象]]，也就是分析要了解的总体量。[[条件期望|条件均值]]描述给定解释变量值时的平均结果；[[条件均值与线性投影|线性投影]]是在指定平方误差准则下最好的线性概括。因果效应则问干预后会发生什么变化。把三者都叫“回归系数”，会掩盖区别。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A curved mean is not automatically a failed linear summary
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*均值是曲线，不一定意味着线性概括没有价值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X$ take $-1,0,1$ with equal probabilities, and let $Y=X^2+\varepsilon$, where $\varepsilon$ is independent of $X$, has mean zero and variance one. The conditional mean is $E[Y\mid X]=X^2$. Among lines $a+bX$, the best population fit is $a=2/3$, $b=0$: $E[X]=0$, $E[X^2]=2/3$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=E[X^3]=0$. Its mean errors at the three $X$ values are $1/3,-2/3,1/3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $X$ 等概率取 $-1,0,1$，$Y=X^2+\varepsilon$，其中 $\varepsilon$ 独立于 $X$、均值零、方差一。条件均值是 $E[Y\mid X]=X^2$。在线 $a+bX$ 中，总体最佳拟合是 $a=2/3$、$b=0$，因为 $E[X]=0$、$E[X^2]=2/3$、$\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=E[X^3]=0$。三个 $X$ 位置上的平均误差分别为 $1/3,-2/3,1/3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the intended claim is that a straight line equals the conditional mean, the curve contradicts it; including a square can represent this mean. If the stated goal is the best straight-line summary for this population, the zero projection slope is a valid target. Inference for that target still needs suitable sampling, moments and covariance estimation. A different target is not repaired merely by changing the standard error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若原本主张直线就是条件均值，这条曲线便与主张不符；加入平方项可以表达这个均值。若目标明说是该总体的最佳直线概括，那么零投影斜率就是有效目标；针对它的推断仍需合适的抽样、矩与协方差条件。目标不同，不能只靠换标准误来修复。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A correct mean can have unequal or dependent noise
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*均值正确，噪声仍可大小不一或相互依赖*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a separate example, let $X=0$ or $1$, and set $Y=20+10X+(1+2X)\varepsilon$, where the independent sign $\varepsilon$ is equally likely to be $-1$ or $1$. The straight-line mean $20+10X$ is correct, but the conditional error variances are $1$ and $9$. This is [[异方差|heteroskedasticity]], not a missing mean curve. [[误差序列相关|Serial correlation]] and within-group dependence concern how different observations share shocks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另看一个例子：$X$ 取 $0$ 或 $1$，令 $Y=20+10X+(1+2X)\varepsilon$，独立符号 $\varepsilon$ 等可能取 $-1$、$1$。直线均值 $20+10X$ 正确，但两组条件误差方差分别为 $1$、$9$。这是[[异方差]]，并不是漏掉均值曲线。[[误差序列相关]]与组内依赖则关心不同观测怎样共享冲击。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A justified [[标准误口径匹配|covariance estimator]] can change the uncertainty while leaving OLS coefficients fixed. WLS or GLS instead changes the fit; when the target is a descriptive projection, reweighting may change that target too. Neither operation supplies missing causal assumptions. These choices belong in [[误差协方差决策|the covariance decision]], after the estimand and information units are clear.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有依据的[[标准误口径匹配|协方差估计方法]]可以在保留 OLS 系数时改变不确定性。WLS 或 GLS 则改变拟合；目标是描述性投影时，重新加权还可能改变目标本身。两种操作都不会补上因果假设。先弄清估计对象与信息单位，再进入[[误差协方差决策]]。

> [!example] A clean regression can still have the wrong causal interpretation · 干净的回归仍可能被错误地解释成因果
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider a hypothetical system with independent standard [[正态分布|normal]] variables $A,V,E$. Let $A$ represent an unobserved common cause, $D=A+V$ an exposure, and $Y=2D+A+E$ the outcome. In this specified system, changing $D$ by one while holding $A,E$ fixed changes $Y$ by $2$.
>
> The observed population regression slope is instead
>
> $$\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(D,Y)}{\operatorname{Var}(D)}=2+\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(D,A)}{\operatorname{Var}(D)}=2+\frac12=2.5.$$
>
> In fact, $r=Y-2.5D=0.5A-0.5V+E$ has covariance zero with $D$. Both are linear combinations of independent Gaussian variables, so [[Gaussian仿射闭包|$(D,r)$ is jointly Gaussian]]; [[联合高斯独立判据|zero covariance gives independence here]]. Thus $E[Y\mid D]=2.5D$ and the conditional error variance is the constant $1.5$. The observational mean and variance look entirely regular, yet $2.5$ is not the system’s causal coefficient $2$. Robust standard errors around the same slope cannot change this.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑一个假设系统，$A,V,E$ 相互独立且都服从标准[[正态分布]]。$A$ 表示未观察到的共同原因，$D=A+V$ 是暴露量，$Y=2D+A+E$ 是结果。这个指定系统中，保持 $A,E$ 不变，把 $D$ 增加一，会使 $Y$ 增加 $2$。
>
> 观察数据的总体回归斜率按上式计算却是 $2.5$：$D$ 与共同原因 $A$ 的协方差为 $1$，$D$ 的方差为 $2$，于是多出 $1/2$。
>
> 事实上，$r=Y-2.5D=0.5A-0.5V+E$ 与 $D$ 的协方差为零。两者都是独立 Gaussian 变量的线性组合，因此[[Gaussian仿射闭包|$(D,r)$ 联合 Gaussian]]，所以[[联合高斯独立判据|这里零协方差推出独立]]。于是 $E[Y\mid D]=2.5D$，条件误差方差恒为 $1.5$。观察均值与方差完全规整，$2.5$ 却不是系统中的因果系数 $2$。围绕同一斜率换稳健标准误，改变不了这件事。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an [[遗漏变量偏差|omitted-confounding example]] relative to the causal target, not an argument that every omitted variable creates bias. Identification must be justified through the research design and explicit assumptions. Simultaneity, some measurement errors, and selection driven by unobserved determinants can create related identification problems, but each needs its own argument. Adding an experience-squared term to a wage model may describe curvature better without separating education from unobserved ability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是相对于因果目标的[[遗漏变量偏差|遗漏混杂例子]]，并不是说任何遗漏变量都会造成偏差。识别要由研究设计与明确假设论证。同时性、某些测量误差、受未观测决定因素影响的样本选择，也可能造成识别问题，但各需具体分析。工资模型加入经验平方项，可以更好地描述曲率，却不会因此让教育与未观测能力分离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use the symptom to choose a question
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用症状选择下一步问题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An arc, a fan or a run of positive [[回归残差|residuals]] is evidence to investigate, not a unique diagnosis. [[残差协方差诊断|Residual diagnostics]] compares possible mean, scale and dependence explanations. Large standard errors can also reflect limited information or nearly redundant predictors; an unstable numerical computation needs a rank and algorithm check, not an automatic causal explanation. Several problems can coexist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[回归残差]]中的弧形、漏斗或连续正值，提供调查线索，不是唯一诊断。[[残差协方差诊断]]比较均值、尺度与依赖的不同解释。标准误很大，也可能反映信息不足或解释变量近乎重复；数值计算不稳定要检查秩与算法，不能自动归因为因果问题。多种问题可以同时存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the next question explicitly: Is the mean representation adequate for the stated goal? Does the uncertainty calculation match the sampling and dependence? What identifies the proposed causal effect? A high $R^2$, a clean plot or a nonsignificant diagnostic does not answer all three. [[样本正交与总体外生性|OLS residual orthogonality]] is a fitting identity and cannot certify structural exogeneity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把下一步问题写清：均值表示是否适合所声明的目标？不确定性计算是否匹配抽样与依赖？所声称的因果效应靠什么识别？高 $R^2$、干净图形或未显著的诊断，都不能同时回答三问。[[样本正交与总体外生性|OLS 残差正交]]只是拟合恒等式，不能认证结构外生性。
