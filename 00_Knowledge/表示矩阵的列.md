# Columns of a matrix representation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*表示矩阵的列*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To turn a linear rule into a matrix, find what it does to each input basis vector. Write each resulting output in the chosen output basis, and put that coefficient list into the corresponding **column**. The column is a numerical description of the output; the output itself might be an arrow, a polynomial or another kind of vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要把一个线性规则写成矩阵，先看它分别把每个输入基向量送到哪里，再把每个输出写成指定输出基的组合，将系数列放入对应的**列**。矩阵列是输出的数字描述；输出本身可能是箭头、多项式或者其他向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For instance, differentiation sends the polynomials $1,x,x^2$ to $0,1,2x$. If we describe both input and output using the ordered basis $(1,x,x^2)$, the three columns must describe precisely these three outputs. This gives the matrix below without guessing where the coefficients belong.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，求导把多项式 $1,x,x^2$ 分别送到 $0,1,2x$。若输入、输出均用有序基 $(1,x,x^2)$ 描述，三列就必须分别描述这三个输出。这样便能得到下面的矩阵，不必猜系数该摆在哪里。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reading a column as output coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把一列读成输出的坐标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $T:V\to W$ be a [[线性映射|linear map]] between finite-dimensional [[向量空间|vector spaces]] over the same field $\mathbb F$. Choose ordered [[基|bases]] $\mathcal B=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$ for $V$ and $\mathcal C=(w_1,\ldots,w_m)$ for $W$. A coordinate column $[y]_{\mathcal C}$ lists the unique coefficients that express $y$ using the $w_i$ in that order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $T:V\to W$ 是同一标量域 $\mathbb F$ 上有限维[[向量空间]]之间的[[线性映射]]，输入有序[[基]]为 $\mathcal B=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)$，输出有序基为 $\mathcal C=(w_1,\ldots,w_m)$。坐标列 $[y]_{\mathcal C}$ 按 $w_i$ 的顺序列出表示 $y$ 的唯一一组系数。

> [!important] Construct one column at a time · 逐列构造
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[线性映射与矩阵表示|representing matrix]] is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[线性映射与矩阵表示|表示矩阵]]为
>
> $$A=[T]_{\mathcal C\leftarrow\mathcal B}
> =\bigl(\,[T(v_1)]_{\mathcal C}\ \cdots\ [T(v_n)]_{\mathcal C}\,\bigr)
> \in\mathbb F^{m\times n}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Equivalently, $T(v_j)=\sum_{i=1}^m a_{ij}w_i$. The entry $a_{ij}$ in row $i$, column $j$ is the amount of output basis vector $w_i$ in the image of input basis vector $v_j$. Each column has $m$ entries; there are $n$ columns.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 等价地，$T(v_j)=\sum_{i=1}^m a_{ij}w_i$。第 $i$ 行、第 $j$ 列的 $a_{ij}$，表示输入基向量 $v_j$ 的像中含多少份输出基向量 $w_i$。每列含 $m$ 个数，一共有 $n$ 列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why do these columns work for every input? Write $x=\sum_{j=1}^n c_jv_j$. Linearity gives $T(x)=\sum_j c_jT(v_j)$, so output coordinates are the same weighted combination of the columns:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么这些列对所有输入都有效？写出 $x=\sum_{j=1}^n c_jv_j$。线性性给出 $T(x)=\sum_j c_jT(v_j)$，所以输出坐标就是各列按同一组系数形成的组合：

$$[T(x)]_{\mathcal C}
=\sum_{j=1}^n c_j[T(v_j)]_{\mathcal C}
=A\begin{pmatrix}c_1\\\vdots\\c_n\end{pmatrix}
=A[x]_{\mathcal B}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The middle equality is the [[Ax的列组合解释|column-combination meaning of matrix multiplication]]. It also proves uniqueness: the coordinate column of $v_j$ is $e_j$, with one in position $j$ and zeros elsewhere. Multiplication $Ae_j$ selects column $j$, so that column is forced to be $[T(v_j)]_{\mathcal C}$. The underlying [[线性映射的基向量像|basis-image theorem]] explains why choosing basis images determines the entire linear map.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中间的等式就是[[Ax的列组合解释|矩阵乘法的列组合解释]]。它也证明唯一性：$v_j$ 的坐标列为 $e_j$，即第 $j$ 个位置为一，其余为零。$Ae_j$ 选出第 $j$ 列，所以该列必须等于 $[T(v_j)]_{\mathcal C}$。背后的[[线性映射的基向量像|基像定理]]解释了为何指定基向量的像就能确定整个线性映射。

## Differentiating polynomials
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把多项式求导写成矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $P_2$ be the real vector space of polynomials of degree at most two, including zero. For $D:P_2\to P_2$, let $D(p)=p'$ be the [[导数|derivative]]. The input and output basis is $\mathcal B=(1,x,x^2)$; the symbol $x$ here is the polynomial variable. We have
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $P_2$ 是次数至多为二的实多项式组成的向量空间，包括零多项式。取 $D:P_2\to P_2$，令 $D(p)=p'$ 表示[[导数|求导]]。输入、输出基均为 $\mathcal B=(1,x,x^2)$；这里的 $x$ 是多项式的自变量。于是

$$
D(1)=0,\qquad D(x)=1,\qquad D(x^2)=2x,
$$
$$
[0]_{\mathcal B}=\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
[1]_{\mathcal B}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
[2x]_{\mathcal B}=\begin{pmatrix}0\\2\\0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Put these coordinate columns next to one another:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把这三列坐标依次并排放置：

$$
[D]_{\mathcal B\leftarrow\mathcal B}
=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&2\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}.
$$

> [!example] Check an actual polynomial · 用一个多项式检查
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The polynomial $p(x)=3+4x+5x^2$ has coordinate column $(3,4,5)^T$, where superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], turning the listed row into a column. Multiplying gives $(4,10,0)^T$, which represents $4+10x+0x^2=p'(x)$. The number $2$ in row two, column three records the derivative of $x^2$; we are not differentiating matrix entries.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 多项式 $p(x)=3+4x+5x^2$ 的坐标列为 $(3,4,5)^T$，其中上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把所列的一行变成一列。相乘得到 $(4,10,0)^T$，表示 $4+10x+0x^2=p'(x)$。第2行第3列的数字 $2$ 来自 $x^2$ 的导数，不是在对矩阵元素求导。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same differentiation formula can instead be declared as a map $D:P_2\to P_1$, where $P_1$ contains polynomials of degree at most one. With output basis $(1,x)$, its matrix is $\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&2\end{pmatrix}$, of size $2\times3$. The missing final row reflects the smaller declared output space. It does not change the derivative polynomial.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也可以把相同的求导公式定义为 $D:P_2\to P_1$，其中 $P_1$ 由次数至多为一的多项式组成。输出基改为 $(1,x)$ 后，矩阵为 $\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&2\end{pmatrix}$，尺寸为 $2\times3$。少掉的最后一行反映了所声明输出空间更小，求出的导数多项式没有改变。

## Dimensions and basis order
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*尺寸与基的顺序*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^3$ given by $T(a,b)=(a+b,2b,a-b)$, use standard bases. The two basis inputs are $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$, and their images are $(1,0,1)$ and $(1,2,-1)$. Hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^3$，令 $T(a,b)=(a+b,2b,a-b)$，两端采用标准基。两个输入基向量为 $(1,0)$、$(0,1)$，其像依次为 $(1,0,1)$、$(1,2,-1)$，因此

$$[T]=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second column has three entries because it describes one output vector in $\mathbb R^3$. It is not a row. Reversing the two input basis vectors reverses the columns; changing the output basis requires re-expressing every image in that new basis. With a nonstandard output basis, writing down the output's usual coordinates would give the wrong columns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二列有三个数，因为它描述的是 $\mathbb R^3$ 中的一个输出向量，不应把它横着放成一行。将两个输入基向量倒序，会使两列倒序；改变输出基，则需要把每个像重新写成新输出基的组合。输出基不是标准基时，直接写输出的通常坐标会得到错误的列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A size check catches incompatible input and output lengths, but cannot verify basis choices. Label both bases before constructing the matrix. In the special case of the identity map with different bases on its two sides, this same column rule gives a [[换基矩阵的方向|change-of-coordinate matrix]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

尺寸检查能发现输入输出长度不匹配，却不能验证基是否用对。构造矩阵前要标明两端的基。恒等映射两端使用不同基时，同一个列规则便给出[[换基矩阵的方向|坐标转换矩阵]]。
