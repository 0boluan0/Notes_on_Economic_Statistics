# 标准推动浪计数须检查回撤起点第三浪长度和第一第四浪不重叠
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Validate a standard impulse count by checking retracement limits, wave-3 length, and non-overlap between waves 1 and 4.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检验一套 [[标准推动浪]] 标签时，先固定同一级别、观察尺度及价格幅度口径，再检查规则。本卡以线性价格差及不重叠的标准现货推动结构为教学对象；这些约束用来判断标签，不是市场价格的禁行线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To check a [[标准推动浪|standard impulse]] label, fix the degree, observational scale, and price-magnitude convention first. This example uses linear price differences and the standard non-overlapping cash-market structure. Constraints validate labels; they do not forbid real prices from crossing levels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以向上推动为例：第二浪不能回撤到第一浪起点；第三浪必须越过第一浪终点，且在 $1,3,5$ 三个同级推进段中不能同时短于另外两段；第四浪的价格范围不能进入第一浪范围。第四浪也不得完全回撤第三浪。下降版本将高低方向反转，仍比较推进幅度。还须满足定义卡中的内部细分，不能只验几个端点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an upward impulse, wave 2 must not fully retrace wave 1. Wave 3 must exceed wave 1's end and cannot be shorter than both waves 1 and 5. Wave 4 must not enter wave 1's price range or fully retrace wave 3. Reverse directions for a downward version while comparing advancing magnitudes. Internal subdivisions remain required; endpoint checks alone are insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如假设各段端点也是相应极值，序列 $100\to110\to104\to125\to116\to130$ 通过上述价格检查：第二浪终点104仍高于100；第1、3、5浪价差为10、21、14；第四浪低点116高于第一浪顶部110。这个例子只验价格约束，内部更细浪形还未给出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume segment endpoints are the relevant extrema. The sequence $100\to110\to104\to125\to116\to130$ passes the stated price checks: 104 exceeds the start of 100, advancing differences are 10, 21, and 14, and wave 4's low of 116 exceeds wave 1's high of 110. Finer internal waves have not been supplied, so this is not a full count validation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若第四浪低点改为108，就进入第一浪的 $[100,110]$ 区间：这套标准推动标签失效，需要重判模式或级别；不是价格“不能”跌到108。不得临时移起点或改变幅度尺度来保住原标签。第五浪也不能仅凭这组规则被保证一定创新高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing wave 4's low to 108 enters wave 1's range $[100,110]$, invalidating this standard impulse count. Reconsider the pattern or degree; the price itself is not prohibited. Do not shift the origin or magnitude convention merely to preserve a label. These rules also do not guarantee a new high in wave 5.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
