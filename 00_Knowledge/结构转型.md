# 结构转型是经济活动在不同产业之间持续重新配置的过程

结构转型关注一个经济体“由哪些产业生产、劳动者在哪里工作”怎样随发展而改变。它可以表现为农业、工业和服务业的就业份额、增加值份额发生持续变化；不是只看总产出增加了多少。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Structural transformation concerns how an economy's composition of production and employment changes during development. It appears in sustained changes in the employment and value-added shares of agriculture, industry and services, rather than simply in the amount of total output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一个经济体的农业就业份额从 60% 降到 50%，另一个部门从 40% 升到 50%，这是就业结构变化。若各部门就业人数同比例增长，就业份额可以完全不变，尽管总就业和总产出都可能增加。比较结构，必须先说清自己观察的是[[产出与就业份额|就业还是增加值份额]]，以及产业如何分类。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If agriculture's employment share falls from 60% to 50% while another sector's share rises from 40% to 50%, employment structure has changed. Proportional employment growth in every sector can leave the shares unchanged even as total employment and output increase. Specify whether you are comparing [[产出与就业份额|employment or value-added shares]] and how industries are classified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

农业就业份额下降、服务业份额上升，是研究长期发展时常见的模式；这不要求每个国家的每个阶段都依次由农业、工业、服务业成为最大部门。这里的“工业”也不能不加说明就与“制造业”互换：采矿、建筑和公用事业是否在内，会改变比较对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Declining agricultural employment shares and rising service shares are common long-run patterns. They do not require agriculture, industry and services each to become the largest sector in that order in every country. Nor should industry and manufacturing be interchanged without specifying whether mining, construction and utilities are included.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

产业、地域和正规性是不同的分类轴。工厂可以设在农村，城市也有农业以外的低生产率工作；[[非正规部门]]不是与农业、工业、服务业并列的第四种产业。要判断重配是否提高生产效率，还得测量各部门的[[劳动生产率]]，再看[[离农与生产率重配|就业转向哪里]]。要判断生活是否改善，则回到[[经济发展]]的评价范围。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Industry, location and formality are different classification axes. A factory may be rural, and urban non-agricultural work may have low productivity. The [[非正规部门|informal sector]] is not a fourth industry alongside agriculture, industry and services. Assessing productive gains requires sectoral [[劳动生产率|labour productivity]] and [[离农与生产率重配|the destination of employment shifts]]. Assessing better lives requires the broader criteria of [[经济发展|economic development]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Herrendorf、Rogerson、Valentinyi（2013），[Growth and Structural Transformation，NBER Working Paper 18996](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w18996/w18996.pdf)，引言及第2.1–2.2.1节，印刷第2、4–7、9页：支持跨产业经济活动重配的定义、就业与增加值口径，以及历史样本中的长期模式；第4页脚注3提醒其 manufacturing 使用了较宽的统计范围，本卡明确使用“工业”而不将口径混写。
- IMF（2018），[WEO 第3章 Manufacturing Jobs: Implications for Productivity and Inequality，独立章节 PDF 第1、12页](https://www.imf.org/~/media/Files/Publications/WEO/2018/April/c3.ashx)：支持农业就业转向服务业并不一定经过制造业扩张，以及服务业内部差异使固定产业排序失效。
- [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/01_不同国家的经济发展模式#5. 增长与发展|原课堂的增长与发展记录]]：提供投入、产业和生活结果的课程范围；份额例子为构造，不是国别数据。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Herrendorf, Rogerson and Valentinyi provide the definition and measurement distinctions. Their broad use of “manufacturing” requires attention to the sector boundary. The IMF chapter supports the qualification of a fixed industry sequence. The classroom record supplies the course context; the share example is constructed, not a country observation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
