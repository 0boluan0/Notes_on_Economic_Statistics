# 连环替代法沿指定顺序逐项更新因素并用相邻结果差分摊总变动
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chain substitution updates factors in a specified order and allocates the total change through differences between successive results.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先把目标指标写成因素的函数，列明基准值和比较值，再固定替代顺序。每一步只把下一个因素从基准更新到比较值，其余保留该步所处状态；相邻两步结果之差就是该顺序下分给此因素的影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Express the target as a function of its factors, list their baseline and comparison values, and fix an order. At each step update only the next factor, keeping the other factors at their current states. The difference between consecutive results is that factor's allocated effect under this order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

材料费用 $C=qap$ 中，产量 $q$ 从120到140、单位耗用 $a$ 从9到8、价格 $p$ 从5到6。按产量、耗用、价格的顺序：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For material cost $C=qap$, output rises from 120 to 140, usage per unit falls from 9 to 8, and price rises from 5 to 6. Updating output, usage, then price gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$5400=120\times9\times5\to6300\to5600\to6720.$$
$$\Delta C=900-700+1120=1320.$$

三因素不是方法限制。相邻差会望远镜相消，故贡献之和等于总差；但单项贡献通常随顺序改变，见[[连环替代顺序边界]]。这是一种恒等关系下的差额分摊，不是独立估计的因果效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The method is not limited to three factors. Successive differences telescope, so allocations sum to the total change, but individual allocations generally depend on the order; see [[连环替代顺序边界|the order-dependence boundary]]. This is an accounting allocation, not an independently identified causal effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
