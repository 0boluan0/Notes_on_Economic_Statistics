# 贪心着色至多使用最大度加一色但结果依赖顶点顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Greedy coloring uses at most maximum degree plus one colors, but its result depends on vertex order*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子算法
> 对非空 finite simple graph，先固定一个顶点顺序，再依次把当前顶点染成“已着色邻居尚未使用的最小颜色”。若最大度为 $\Delta(G)$，当前顶点至多被邻居禁止 $\Delta(G)$ 种颜色，因此总有一种颜色可从 $1,\ldots,\Delta(G)+1$ 中选出：
> $$
> \chi(G)\le \Delta(G)+1.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a nonempty finite simple graph, fix a vertex order and give each current vertex the smallest color not used by its already colored neighbors. At most $\Delta(G)$ colors can be forbidden, so one of $1,\ldots,\Delta(G)+1$ is always available and $\chi(G)\le\Delta(G)+1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是一个构造性上界：算法结束时可以逐边检查着色是否合法。但它没有证明使用的颜色数最少，因为选择顶点的先后顺序会改变早期占用的颜色，也会改变后续顶点面临的禁色集合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a constructive upper bound: the resulting coloring can be checked edge by edge. It does not prove minimality, because the vertex order changes which colors are occupied early and therefore which colors later vertices are forbidden to use.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如路径 $v_1-v_2-v_3-v_4$ 本来是二分图，$\chi=2$。若按 $v_1,v_4,v_2,v_3$ 着色，前两个点都取颜色 1，$v_2$ 取颜色 2，而最后的 $v_3$ 同时邻接颜色 2 的 $v_2$ 与颜色 1 的 $v_4$，于是贪心会使用第三色。合法并不等于最优。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The path $v_1-v_2-v_3-v_4$ is bipartite and has chromatic number two. In the order $v_1,v_4,v_2,v_3$, the first two vertices both receive color 1, $v_2$ receives color 2, and $v_3$ sees colors 2 and 1 at its two neighbors, so greedy coloring introduces color 3. The coloring is valid but not optimal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\Delta+1$ 有时恰好：complete graph $K_{\Delta+1}$ 的每个顶点都必须异色。对较大的 $m$，它在 star $K_{1,m}$ 上却很松：最大度是 $m$，但中心一种颜色、所有 leaves 另一种颜色就够了，故色数只有 2。maximum degree 因而提供一般上界，不能被倒过来当作一般下界。对 null graph，$\Delta(G)$ 未必有统一约定，不应在没有声明时直接套这个式子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bound is tight for $K_{\Delta+1}$, whose vertices all need distinct colors. For large $m$, it is very loose for the star $K_{1,m}$, which has maximum degree $m$ but chromatic number two. Maximum degree therefore supplies a universal upper bound, not a general lower bound. For the null graph, $\Delta(G)$ itself is convention-dependent, so the formula should not be invoked without stating a convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与辨别
> 贪心算法用了 6 种颜色，能否立刻写 $\chi(G)=6$？
>
> **答案：**不能。这个运行只给 $\chi(G)\le6$。要得到等号，还需证明五种颜色不可能，或者取得能与某个下界相遇的更好着色。
