# Greedy coloring and the degree bound
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*贪心着色的最大度加一上界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Greedy coloring assigns colors one vertex at a time. At the current vertex, look only at neighbors that already have colors, then use the smallest positive integer not among their colors. Earlier decisions are kept. This simple rule always builds a [[Proper 着色与独立集划分|proper coloring]] of a finite simple undirected graph: adjacent vertices end up with different colors. It need not use the fewest possible colors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

贪心着色每次给一个顶点分配颜色。处理当前顶点时，只看已经着色的邻居，再选没有被这些邻居使用的最小正整数作为颜色；已经作出的决定保持不变。这个简单规则总能为有限简单无向图构造[[Proper 着色与独立集划分|合法着色]]，使相邻顶点最终异色，但不一定使用最少的颜色。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same path, two different orders
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*同一条路径，两种处理次序*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take four vertices with exactly the edges of the path $v_1-v_2-v_3-v_4$. Colors $1,2,3$ are just labels for groups, not numerical costs. Process the vertices in the order $v_1,v_4,v_2,v_3$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取四个顶点，恰好有路径 $v_1-v_2-v_3-v_4$ 上的三条边。颜色 $1,2,3$ 只是分组标签，不是数值成本。按 $v_1,v_4,v_2,v_3$ 的次序处理：

| Current vertex · 当前顶点 | Colors on already colored neighbors · 已着色邻居的颜色 | Chosen color · 所选颜色 |
|---|---|---|
| $v_1$ | None · 无 | $1$ |
| $v_4$ | None · 无 | $1$ |
| $v_2$ | $1$ at $v_1$ · $v_1$ 的 $1$ | $2$ |
| $v_3$ | $2$ at $v_2$, $1$ at $v_4$ · $v_2$ 的 $2$、$v_4$ 的 $1$ | $3$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The resulting colors in vertex order $v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4$ are $(1,2,3,1)$, so all three edges join different colors. Instead process $v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4$: the output is $(1,2,1,2)$. Two colors suffice, and one cannot suffice because an edge is present. Thus this path has [[色数|chromatic number]] $2$, although the first greedy run uses $3$. This is an order effect, not a failure of properness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按顶点次序 $v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4$ 记录，第一次的结果是 $(1,2,3,1)$，三条边的两端确实都异色。若改按 $v_1,v_2,v_3,v_4$ 处理，结果则为 $(1,2,1,2)$。两色足够；图中存在边，所以一色不够。因此这条路径的[[色数]]为 $2$，虽然第一次贪心运行用了 $3$ 色。这体现的是顺序影响，不是合法性失效。

## Why a color is always available
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么总能找到可用颜色*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite simple undirected graph with $n\ge1$ vertices, listed once each as $v_1,\ldots,v_n$. In this model, the [[度数、入度与出度|degree]] $\deg(v)$ is the number of neighbors of $v$. The **maximum degree** is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $G=(V,E)$ 为含 $n\ge1$ 个顶点的有限简单无向图，把所有顶点各列一次，记为 $v_1,\ldots,v_n$。在这个模型中，[[度数、入度与出度|度数]] $\deg(v)$ 就是顶点 $v$ 的邻居数。**最大度**定义为

$$\Delta(G)=\max_{v\in V}\deg(v).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $c(v)$ for the assigned color. Just before coloring $v_i$, its forbidden-color set is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $c(v)$ 表示分配给 $v$ 的颜色。在给 $v_i$ 着色之前，被禁止的颜色集合为

$$F_i=\{c(v_j):j<i,\ \{v_j,v_i\}\in E\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This set collects distinct colors, not neighbors: several neighbors with color $1$ forbid only one color. There are at most $\deg(v_i)\le\Delta(G)$ already colored neighbors, so $F_i$ contains at most $\Delta(G)$ colors. The palette $\{1,\ldots,\Delta(G)+1\}$ has one more color than that. At least one remains available, and the smallest available positive integer is therefore at most $\Delta(G)+1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里收集的是不同颜色，不是邻居本身：多个邻居都用颜色 $1$，只会禁止一种颜色。已经着色的邻居至多有 $\deg(v_i)\le\Delta(G)$ 个，所以 $F_i$ 至多包含 $\Delta(G)$ 种颜色。备选集合 $\{1,\ldots,\Delta(G)+1\}$ 多出一种，必然至少有一种可用，因此最小可用正整数也不超过 $\Delta(G)+1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rule also preserves properness at every step. Before any vertex is colored, there is no conflicting colored pair. When $v_i$ is added, its color differs from every already colored neighbor; pairs among earlier vertices are unchanged. Induction therefore keeps every edge whose endpoints are colored proper. After the finite list is exhausted, every vertex is colored, and every edge has been checked when its later endpoint was processed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个规则还在每一步保持合法性。开始没有已着色的冲突点对；加入 $v_i$ 时，它与所有已着色邻居异色，而早先顶点之间的关系没有改变。因此归纳可知，每一步的已着色部分都合法。有限列表处理完后，所有顶点都有颜色；每条边都在它较晚被处理的端点处得到保证。

> [!theorem] An upper bound for every vertex order · 任意顶点次序的上界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On a nonempty finite simple undirected graph, the greedy rule produces a proper coloring with some number $q$ of used colors satisfying
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对非空有限简单无向图，贪心规则产生合法着色。若实际用了 $q$ 种颜色，则
>
> $$\chi(G)\le q\le\Delta(G)+1.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $\chi(G)$ is the chromatic number, the smallest possible number of colors in any proper coloring. The left inequality follows because this particular output is one available coloring; the right inequality is the guarantee just proved. A claim of [[精确色数的上下界|exact optimality]] still needs a matching lower bound.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\chi(G)$ 是色数，即全部合法着色中所需颜色数的最小值。左侧不等式来自这份具体可行方案，右侧来自刚才的保证；要声称[[精确色数的上下界|恰好最优]]，还需要相遇的下界。

## A sharp bound can also be a loose estimate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*上界有时精确，有时很松*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a complete graph $K_{r}$, every pair of different vertices is adjacent. Each vertex has degree $r-1$, and all $r$ vertices need different colors. Thus $\chi(K_r)=r=\Delta(K_r)+1$ for $r\ge1$: no universally smaller bound can replace this one for all simple graphs.

In the star $K_{1,m}$ with $m\ge1$, one center is joined to $m$ leaves and there are no other edges. Its maximum degree is $m$, yet one color for the center and another for all leaves gives two colors. An edge forces at least two, so its chromatic number is exactly $2$. Having many neighbors does not mean those neighbors conflict with each other. Maximum degree gives an upper bound, not a general lower bound on chromatic number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完全图 $K_r$ 中，任意两个不同顶点都相邻。每个顶点的度数为 $r-1$，全部 $r$ 个顶点必须异色，所以对 $r\ge1$ 有 $\chi(K_r)=r=\Delta(K_r)+1$。因此，对所有简单图都成立的普遍上界不能统一再减小。

星形图 $K_{1,m}$（$m\ge1$）只有一个中心与 $m$ 个叶子相连，没有其他边。它的最大度为 $m$，但中心一种颜色、所有叶子另一种颜色就足够；存在边又迫使至少两色，因此色数恰为 $2$。一个点有很多邻居，不表示那些邻居之间也冲突。最大度给出的是色数上界，而不是一般下界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nonempty edgeless graph has $\Delta=0$; the rule gives color $1$ to every vertex and uses exactly one color. On the empty vertex set it performs no steps and uses zero colors, but the maximum over an empty set requires a separate convention, so the displayed theorem was stated for nonempty graphs. A self-loop would require a vertex to differ from itself and prevents any proper coloring. Parallel copies of a non-loop conflict do not forbid additional colors; one may first replace them by a single conflict edge and apply the simple-graph theorem. Edge weights do not change this unweighted inequality constraint by themselves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非空无边图的 $\Delta=0$，算法给每个顶点颜色 $1$，恰用一色。空顶点集上算法不执行任何步骤，使用零色；但空集合上的最大值需要另作约定，因此上面的定理限定非空图。自环要求一个顶点与自身异色，会使合法着色不存在。非自环冲突的平行副本不会新增禁色，可以先合并为一条冲突边，再用简单图定理。边权本身也不会改变这里“不等颜色”的无权约束。

> [!question]- Six colors found · 找到了六色方案
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If one greedy run uses six colors, can we conclude $\chi(G)=6$? No. It establishes $\chi(G)\le6$. Equality needs a reason that five colors cannot suffice; a better coloring may instead lower the upper bound. Changing the order is a way to seek an improvement, not a proof that the current output was optimal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一次贪心运行用了六色，能立即断定 $\chi(G)=6$ 吗？不能，它只给出 $\chi(G)\le6$。等号需要证明五色一定不够；也可能找到更好的着色，把上界降低。改变顺序可以寻找改进，并不能证明当前结果已最优。
