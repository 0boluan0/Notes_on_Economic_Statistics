# Antithetic variates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*对偶变量与对偶抽样*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Antithetic sampling** runs a simulation at a random input and at a deliberately related counterpart, then averages the two outputs. The aim is to make an unusually high output accompany a low one, so their average fluctuates less. The counterpart must preserve the intended input distribution; merely reversing a number is not enough.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**对偶抽样**在一个随机输入及其特意构造的对应输入上各运行一次模拟，再平均两个输出。希望一个偏高时，另一个偏低，从而让平均值少些波动。对应输入必须保留原本的抽样分布；仅仅把一个数反过来并不够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A pair whose average is always one half
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每对的平均恰好都是二分之一*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $U$ be [[连续均匀分布|uniform on $[0,1]$]]. Its reflected value $1-U$ is also uniform on that interval. An input $0.1$ is paired with $0.9$, and $0.4$ with $0.6$. If the simulation records $f(u)=u$, the two recorded outputs always average to $1/2$. This equals the target [[期望|expectation]] $E[U]$ exactly, so this particular paired output has zero variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $U$ 在 $[0,1]$ 上服从[[连续均匀分布]]，反射后的 $1-U$ 仍在同一区间上均匀分布。输入 $0.1$ 配上 $0.9$，$0.4$ 配上 $0.6$。若模拟记录的是 $f(u)=u$，两次输出的平均始终为 $1/2$，恰好等于目标[[期望]] $E[U]$。这个特例的配对输出方差为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a general measurable function $f$ with $E|f(U)|<\infty$, record one complete pair as
$$Z=\frac{f(U)+f(1-U)}2.$$
The two inputs have the same [[边际分布|marginal distribution]], so both outputs have the same expectation $\mu=E[f(U)]$. [[期望线性性|Linearity of expectation]] then gives $E[Z]=\mu$. This preserves the target without requiring independence within the pair; in fact, $U$ determines its counterpart completely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一般可测函数 $f$，若 $E|f(U)|<\infty$，把完整一对记录为上式 $Z$。两个输入具有相同[[边际分布]]，所以两输出的期望都是 $\mu=E[f(U)]$；[[期望线性性]]随即给出 $E[Z]=\mu$。保留目标不需要对内独立，实际上一个 $U$ 已经完全决定对应输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Compare two evaluations with two evaluations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两次函数计算应当与两次函数计算比较*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $Y=f(U)$ has finite variance $\sigma^2$, and write $Y'=f(1-U)$. Each has variance $\sigma^2$, but their [[协方差|covariance]] $C=\operatorname{Cov}(Y,Y')$ measures how their fluctuations move together. The [[和的方差协方差项|variance of a sum]] gives
$$\operatorname{Var}(Z)=\frac{\sigma^2+C}{2}.$$
Two independent evaluations have average variance $\sigma^2/2$. Therefore the paired average improves that benchmark exactly when $C<0$; $C=0$ leaves variance unchanged, and $C>0$ makes it worse. For $\sigma^2>0$, writing $\rho=C/\sigma^2$ gives a variance ratio $1+\rho$. The ratio lies between $0$ and $2$; if the output is constant, correlation is undefined but the variance comparison is trivial.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $Y=f(U)$ 的方差 $\sigma^2$ 有限，并记 $Y'=f(1-U)$。两者方差相同，而[[协方差]] $C$ 描述波动怎样共同移动。由[[和的方差协方差项]]可得上式。两次独立函数计算的平均方差是 $\sigma^2/2$，因此只有 $C<0$ 时配对才降低这个基准的方差；$C=0$ 不变，$C>0$ 反而更差。若 $\sigma^2>0$，记相关系数 $\rho=C/\sigma^2$，配对与独立平均的方差比为 $1+\rho$，介于 $0$ 与 $2$。输出恒定时相关系数无定义，但方差比较直接成立。

> [!example] Opposite inputs can give identical outputs · 相反输入也可能给相同输出
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $f(u)=(u-1/2)^2$. Since $f(1-u)=f(u)$, the pair duplicates the output instead of cancelling it. Direct integration gives $E[f(U)]=1/12$ and $\operatorname{Var}(f(U))=1/180$. One paired average still has variance $1/180$, while two independent evaluations averaged together have variance $1/360$. The antithetic construction doubles the variance at this equal evaluation count.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $f(u)=(u-1/2)^2$，有 $f(1-u)=f(u)$，配对是在复制输出，而非抵消。积分得到期望 $1/12$、方差 $1/180$。一次配对平均的方差仍为 $1/180$，两次独立计算的平均却只有 $1/360$；按相同函数计算次数，这个对偶构造把方差加倍了。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the scalar-uniform construction, a monotone $f$ makes $f(U)$ and $f(1-U)$ move in opposite directions and gives nonpositive covariance, provided the second moments are finite. This is a sufficient pattern, not a requirement or a guarantee of a large gain. In a more complicated simulation, inspect the complete output function; the labels “opposite inputs” and “threshold” alone do not determine the sign. [[配对方差缩减的协方差方向|The pairing distinction]] explains why averaging and differencing need opposite signs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在一维均匀反射构造中，若 $f$ 单调且二阶矩有限，$f(U)$ 与 $f(1-U)$ 朝相反方向变化，协方差非正。这是一个充分模式，不是必要条件，也不保证收益很大。复杂模拟要检查完整输出函数，不能仅凭“输入相反”或“含阈值”判断符号。[[配对方差缩减的协方差方向]]解释了平均与求差为什么需要相反的符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The independent unit is a complete pair
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*独立重复的单位是一整对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Generate $R$ [[独立同分布|iid]] base inputs $U_1,\ldots,U_R$ for a fixed positive integer $R$, and form $Z_r=[f(U_r)+f(1-U_r)]/2$. Then $\widehat\mu=R^{-1}\sum_r Z_r$ is unbiased, and with finite second moments its variance is $\operatorname{Var}(Z)/R$. It uses $2R$ function evaluations, so the independent-evaluation benchmark uses $2R$ evaluations too.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对预先固定的正整数 $R$，生成 $R$ 个[[独立同分布|iid]]基础输入 $U_1,\ldots,U_R$，每个构造一个配对平均 $Z_r$。估计量 $\widehat\mu=R^{-1}\sum_r Z_r$ 无偏，二阶矩有限时方差为 $\operatorname{Var}(Z)/R$。它计算了 $2R$ 次函数，独立抽样基准也应计算 $2R$ 次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $R\ge2$, estimate uncertainty from the $R$ pair averages:
$$s_Z^2=\frac1{R-1}\sum_{r=1}^R(Z_r-\overline Z)^2,\qquad \widehat{\operatorname{SE}}(\overline Z)=\frac{s_Z}{\sqrt R}.$$
This is the [[Monte Carlo均值标准误|sample-mean standard-error calculation]] applied to complete independent outputs. Treating all $2R$ component outputs as independent ignores their covariance. A normal confidence interval still needs its own [[Monte Carlo近似区间|approximation conditions]]; the SE formula alone does not supply them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $R\ge2$，从 $R$ 个配对平均按上式估计不确定性。这是对完整独立输出使用[[Monte Carlo均值标准误|样本均值标准误公式]]；若假装所有 $2R$ 个组成输出独立，就漏掉了协方差。正态置信区间仍需另外检查[[Monte Carlo近似区间|近似条件]]，标准误公式本身不会提供这些条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reflection must preserve the whole input law
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*反射必须保留整个输入分布*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a centred [[联合Gaussian|Gaussian vector]] $X\sim N(0,\Sigma)$, the vector $-X$ has the same joint distribution. For a vector uniform on the unit cube, with independent uniform coordinates, use componentwise $1-U$. A distribution symmetric about $c$ can use $2c-X$. For a nonsymmetric distribution, $-X$ generally has a different law; simply negating it can change the expectation being estimated. Check the joint law of a simulated path or vector, not just each coordinate in isolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对中心化[[联合Gaussian|高斯向量]] $X\sim N(0,\Sigma)$，$-X$ 保留联合分布。单位立方体上的均匀向量，其各坐标独立均匀，可逐坐标用 $1-U$；关于 $c$ 对称的分布可用 $2c-X$。非对称分布的 $-X$ 通常已换了分布，直接取负可能改变所估期望。模拟路径或向量需要核对联合分布，不能只看每个坐标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, equal function-evaluation counts need not mean equal runtime: generating the counterpart may be cheap, while evaluating it or synchronising paths may be costly. [[方差缩减比较口径|Efficiency comparisons]] combine the variance of the complete pair with its actual cost. A mathematical variance gain and a practical speed gain are separate conclusions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后，相同函数计算次数未必代表相同运行时间：生成对应输入可能很便宜，计算输出或同步路径却可能昂贵。[[方差缩减比较口径]]把完整一对的方差与实际成本一起考虑；数学方差收益与实际速度收益是两个结论。

- Owen 第 8 章第 8.2 节（PDF pp. 5–8）核对保持分布的反射、式 (8.2)–(8.3)、同函数计算次数的方差比较、成本限定及基于配对平均的推断。这里对一维单调函数写为协方差非正，容纳恒定输出。
