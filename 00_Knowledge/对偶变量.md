# 对偶变量把每个随机输入与保持同一边际分布的反向输入配对，并平均两次输出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Antithetic variates pair each random input with a distribution-preserving opposite and average the two outputs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 若 $U\sim\operatorname{Uniform}(0,1)$，则 $1-U$ 仍服从同一分布。对偶变量用同一对 $(U,1-U)$ 计算两个输出并平均，希望一高一低的变化互相抵消。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $U$ is uniform, then $1-U$ has the same distribution. Antithetic sampling averages outputs from the pair $(U,1-U)$ so that opposing movements may cancel.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $Y=f(U)$，一对函数求值形成

$$
Y_{\mathrm{anti}}
=\frac{f(U)+f(1-U)}2.
$$

因为两项边际分布相同，$E[Y_{\mathrm{anti}}]=E[f(U)]$。例如 $f(u)=u$ 时，每对输出平均恒为 $1/2$，这个特例的方差直接降为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both terms have the same marginal law, so the pair average preserves the target expectation. For $f(u)=u$, every pair averages exactly to one half and the variance is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“反向输入”必须保持原抽样分布；在 $N(0,\Sigma)$ 中常用 $Z$ 与 $-Z$，在高维均匀输入中常用逐分量 $U$ 与 $1-U$。它是否优于两次独立函数求值，取决于输出协方差，见 [[配对方差缩减的协方差方向]]。非单调或复杂阈值函数不保证收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The opposite input must preserve the sampling law. Common examples are $Z$ and $-Z$ for a centred Gaussian, or componentwise $U$ and $1-U$ for uniform inputs. Improvement over two independent evaluations depends on output covariance and is not guaranteed for non-monotone or complex threshold functions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
