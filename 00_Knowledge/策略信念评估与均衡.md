# 策略—信念评估是候选策略与信念的完整对象，均衡是在该对象上再施加最优性与一致性条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A strategy–belief assessment is the complete candidate object consisting of strategies and beliefs; an equilibrium further imposes optimality and consistency conditions on that object.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

assessment 写作 $(\sigma,\mu)$：$\sigma$ 是完整策略剖面，$\mu$ 是各信息集上的信念系统。任意一对形式完整的 $(\sigma,\mu)$ 都是候选 assessment，但只有满足相应解概念条件的 assessment 才是均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An assessment is written $(\sigma,\mu)$: $\sigma$ is a complete strategy profile and $\mu$ is a belief system over information sets. Any formally complete pair $(\sigma,\mu)$ is a candidate assessment, but it is an equilibrium only if it satisfies the conditions of the relevant solution concept.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 候选对象与解概念
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Candidate object versus solution concept*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对完美贝叶斯均衡而言，策略必须在每个信息集给定信念后序贯最优，路径上信念必须由策略和 Bayes 法则生成。更强的序贯均衡还要求整套 assessment 可由完全混合策略的极限一致地逼近。因而“assessment”不是“均衡”的同义词，而是均衡条件所检验的对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For perfect Bayesian equilibrium, strategies must be sequentially optimal at every information set given beliefs, and on-path beliefs must be generated from strategies by Bayes' rule. The stronger sequential-equilibrium concept additionally requires the entire assessment to arise consistently as a limit of completely mixed strategies. “Assessment” is therefore not a synonym for “equilibrium”; it is the object tested by equilibrium conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 只给策略不完整
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strategies alone are incomplete.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在动态不完全信息博弈中，同一接收者策略可能在不同信念下是最优或非最优，尤其是路径外信号后的行动。若不同时给出这些信息集上的信念，就无法检验接收者的序贯理性，也无法完整陈述 PBE 候选。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a dynamic game of incomplete information, the same receiver strategy may be optimal under one belief and suboptimal under another, especially after an off-path signal. Without beliefs at those information sets, one cannot test the receiver's sequential rationality or fully state a candidate PBE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
