# Rank-one matrices and outer products
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*秩一矩阵的外积刻画*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **rank-one matrix** has only one independent output direction. Its columns may look different, but every column is a scalar multiple of one nonzero column vector. An **outer product** records this pattern with two vectors: one supplies the common column direction, and the other lists the multiplier for each column. Here [[矩阵秩|rank]] is the dimension of the [[列空间|column space]], the set of all linear combinations of the columns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**秩一矩阵**只有一个独立的输出方向。各列看起来可以不同，但每一列都是同一条非零列向量的标量倍数。**外积**用两个向量记录这个结构：一个提供共同的列方向，另一个列出各列的倍数。这里的[[矩阵秩|秩]]是[[列空间]]的维数；列空间由各列的全部线性组合组成。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One column direction, three multipliers
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一个列方向，三个倍数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the real matrix below. Its columns are the first column, four times that column, and five times that column:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑下面的实矩阵。三列依次为第一列、第一列的四倍、第一列的五倍：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&4&5\\2&8&10\end{pmatrix}
=\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix}}_{u}
\underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}1&4&5\end{pmatrix}}_{v^T}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both $u=(1,2)^T$ and $v=(1,4,5)^T$ are column vectors. The superscript $T$ denotes ordinary [[矩阵转置|transpose]], which turns $v$ into a row. Multiplying a column by a row gives a matrix: $(2\times1)(1\times3)=2\times3$. In column $j$, the whole vector $u$ is multiplied by the entry $v_j$. This construction is the outer product $uv^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$u=(1,2)^T$ 与 $v=(1,4,5)^T$ 都是列向量。上标 $T$ 表示普通[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把 $v$ 变成一行。列乘行产生矩阵：$(2\times1)(1\times3)=2\times3$。第 $j$ 列把整条向量 $u$ 乘以分量 $v_j$；这种构造就是外积 $uv^T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
All outputs lie in $\operatorname{span}\{u\}$, meaning all scalar multiples of $u$; [[张成|span]] collects every allowed linear combination. Because the first column is nonzero, this space is a line and its dimension is exactly one. The matrix has six nonzero entries, but rank counts independent directions rather than entries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所有输出都落在 $\operatorname{span}\{u\}$ 中，也就是 $u$ 的全部标量倍数；[[张成]]收集所有允许的线性组合。因为第一列非零，这个空间是一条直线，维数恰为一。矩阵虽然有六个非零元素，秩计算的却是独立方向数，而不是元素数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The characterization and both directions of proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*刻画及两个方向的证明*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $m,n$ be positive integers, and let $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$, where all entries and coefficients use the same scalar field $\mathbb F=\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. For column vectors $u\in\mathbb F^m$ and $v\in\mathbb F^n$, the outer product is defined entry by entry by
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $m,n$ 为正整数，$A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$，所有元素与系数使用同一个标量域 $\mathbb F=\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。对列向量 $u\in\mathbb F^m$、$v\in\mathbb F^n$，外积的逐元素定义为

$$(uv^T)_{ij}=u_i v_j,\qquad 1\le i\le m,\quad 1\le j\le n.$$

> [!important] Exactly one nonzero column direction · 恰有一个非零列方向
> $$\boxed{\operatorname{rank}(A)=1
> \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
> A=uv^T\text{ for some }u\ne0,\ v\ne0.}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both factors must be nonzero vectors. Individual coordinates, and even entire columns or rows of $A$, may still be zero. The matrix need not be square.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个因子都必须是非零向量；但它们的个别坐标，以及 $A$ 的某些整列或整行，仍可为零。矩阵不必为方阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**From the product to rank one.** If $A=uv^T$, its $j$th column is $v_j u$, so every column lies in the line spanned by $u$. Since $v\ne0$, some $v_j\ne0$; since $u\ne0$, that column is nonzero. It also recovers $u$ by division by $v_j$. Thus the column space equals $\operatorname{span}\{u\}$ and has dimension exactly one, not merely at most one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**从外积推出秩一。** 若 $A=uv^T$，第 $j$ 列就是 $v_j u$，所以所有列都落在 $u$ 张成的直线上。因为 $v\ne0$，至少有一个 $v_j\ne0$；又因为 $u\ne0$，这一列非零。把它除以 $v_j$，还能恢复 $u$。因此列空间恰好等于 $\operatorname{span}\{u\}$，维数恰为一，而不只是至多为一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**From rank one to the product.** If $\operatorname{rank}(A)=1$, at least one column is nonzero. Choose such a column as $u$. A nonzero vector in a one-dimensional space spans that space, so every column $a_j$ has the form $a_j=v_j u$. The coefficient is unique: if $v_j u=\widetilde v_j u$, a nonzero coordinate of $u$ gives $v_j=\widetilde v_j$. Collecting these coefficients into $v=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)^T$ gives $A=uv^T$. At the position of the chosen column, the coefficient is $1$, so $v$ is nonzero as required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**从秩一构造外积。** 若 $\operatorname{rank}(A)=1$，至少有一列非零，选它作为 $u$。一维空间中的非零向量能张成整个空间，所以每一列 $a_j$ 都可写成 $a_j=v_j u$。系数唯一：若 $v_j u=\widetilde v_j u$，比较 $u$ 的任意非零坐标，就得到 $v_j=\widetilde v_j$。把这些系数收集成 $v=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)^T$，便得到 $A=uv^T$。被选作 $u$ 的那一列，其系数为 $1$，所以 $v$ 也确实非零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a direct calculation, choose any coordinate $i$ with $u_i\ne0$. Then all coefficients can be read from one row: $v_j=a_{ij}/u_i$. In the opening example, choosing the first column as $u$ and using its first coordinate yields $(1,4,5)$. The rank-one premise is what ensures that these coefficients reproduce every row, not just the row used to compute them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际计算时，可选一个满足 $u_i\ne0$ 的坐标，随后从同一行读出全部系数：$v_j=a_{ij}/u_i$。开头例子选第一列作为 $u$，再使用它的第一坐标，就得到 $(1,4,5)$。这里靠“秩为一”的前提保证这些系数能复原每一行，而不只是计算系数时使用的那一行。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The action on an input $x\in\mathbb F^n$ makes the same structure visible:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把它作用于输入 $x\in\mathbb F^n$，同一结构就表现为

$$Ax=u(v^Tx)=u\left(\sum_{j=1}^n v_jx_j\right).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First the row $v^T$ produces one scalar; then that scalar multiplies $u$. Over the real field, all outputs therefore lie on one line through the origin. Over the complex field, they form one complex-dimensional span. The ordinary scalar sum here uses no conjugation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先由行 $v^T$ 产生一个标量，再用这个标量乘 $u$。在实数域上，所有输出因此落在同一条过原点的直线上；在复数域上，它们组成一个复一维张成空间。这里的普通标量求和不取共轭。

## Scaling, zero factors and complex conventions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The factorization is not unique. For a nonzero scalar $c$ in the same field,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

外积分解并不唯一。对同一数域中的任意非零标量 $c$，

$$uv^T=(cu)(v/c)^T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing the scale of the column direction is cancelled by the reciprocal change in its coefficient list. For example, the opening matrix also uses $2u$ together with $v/2$. With $u$ fixed, however, the coefficients are unique by the proof above. If either factor is the zero vector, the product is the zero matrix and has rank zero. A nonzero vector may have zero coordinates: $u=(1,0)^T$, $v=(0,2)^T$ gives $\begin{pmatrix}0&2\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$, still of rank one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把列方向放大，可以用系数列表的反向缩放抵消。例如，开头的矩阵也能用 $2u$ 与 $v/2$ 表示；但固定 $u$ 后，由上面的证明，系数就是唯一的。若任意一个因子为零向量，乘积就是零矩阵，秩为零。非零向量仍可含零坐标：$u=(1,0)^T$、$v=(0,2)^T$ 给出 $\begin{pmatrix}0&2\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$，秩仍为一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over $\mathbb C$, the [[Hermitian 矩阵#Transpose, then conjugate|conjugate transpose]] is $v^*=\overline v^{\,T}$: conjugation replaces $a+bi$ by $a-bi$, with $i^2=-1$. The product $uv^*$ also has rank one when both factors are nonzero, but it is generally a different matrix from $uv^T$ for the **same** $u,v$. For example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\mathbb C$ 上，[[Hermitian 矩阵#Transpose, then conjugate|共轭转置]]为 $v^*=\overline v^{\,T}$：共轭把 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$，其中 $i^2=-1$。两个因子都非零时，$uv^*$ 同样秩为一；但对**同一对** $u,v$，它通常与 $uv^T$ 是不同的矩阵。例如，

$$u=v=\begin{pmatrix}1\\i\end{pmatrix},\qquad
uv^T=\begin{pmatrix}1&i\\i&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
uv^*=\begin{pmatrix}1&-i\\i&1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two conventions describe the same class of rank-one matrices after changing the second factor: set $w=\overline v$, then $w^*=v^T$ and $uv^T=uw^*$. This explains the conjugate-transpose notation used in the [[SVD秩一展开|rank-one terms of an SVD]]; it does not license replacing $T$ by $*$ while leaving the vectors fixed. No orthogonality or unit-length assumption is needed for the characterization here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更换第二个因子后，两种约定描述的是同一类秩一矩阵：令 $w=\overline v$，则 $w^*=v^T$，于是 $uv^T=uw^*$。这说明了 [[SVD秩一展开|SVD 秩一项]]使用共轭转置的记法，却不允许在保持向量不变时直接把 $T$ 换成 $*$。本卡的刻画不要求正交性，也不要求向量长度为一。

> [!warning] A column times a row has the required shape · 列乘行才给出所需尺寸
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The outer product has size $m\times n$. Reversing the order to $v^Tu$ is only defined when $m=n$, and then gives a $1\times1$ product, identified with a scalar, rather than a general $m\times n$ array. Multiple outer products may also point in different output directions, so their sum need not have rank one; [[矩阵乘积外积展开|the outer-product expansion of a matrix product]] treats that separate construction.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 外积尺寸为 $m\times n$。反过来写 $v^Tu$，只有 $m=n$ 时才有定义，此时得到可视为标量的 $1\times1$ 乘积，而不是一般的 $m\times n$ 数表。多个外积还可能提供不同输出方向，所以它们的和未必秩一；[[矩阵乘积外积展开]]另行讨论这种求和构造。
