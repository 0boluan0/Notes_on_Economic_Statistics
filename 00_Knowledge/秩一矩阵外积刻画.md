# 非零矩阵的秩为一当且仅当它可以写成两个非零向量的外积
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A nonzero matrix has rank one if and only if it is an outer product of two nonzero vectors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rank-one matrix has just one independent column direction. Choose a nonzero column vector $u$ for that direction; a second column vector $v$ stores the multiplier for each column. The transpose turns $v$ into the row needed to form a matrix: $(m\times1)(1\times n)=m\times n$. This is the outer product $uv^T$. Here [[矩阵秩|rank]] means the dimension of the column space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

秩一矩阵只有一个独立列方向。用非零列向量 $u$ 表示这个方向，再用列向量 $v$ 存放每一列的倍数。把 $v$ 转置成行，尺寸才是 $(m\times1)(1\times n)=m\times n$，得到外积 $uv^T$。这里的[[矩阵秩|秩]]指列空间的维数。

> [!summary] 外积刻画
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A nonzero matrix has rank one exactly when it can be written as $uv^T$ with both $u$ and $v$ nonzero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对非零矩阵 $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$，以下两件事等价：
> 1. $\operatorname{rank}(A)=1$；
> 2. 存在非零向量 $u\in\mathbb F^m$、$v\in\mathbb F^n$，使
>    $$
>    A=uv^T.
>    $$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A=uv^T$ with both factors nonzero, column $j$ is $v_j u$. All columns lie in the span of $u$, and at least one is nonzero, so the column space has dimension one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A=uv^T$ 且 $u,v$ 都非零，那么第 $j$ 列是 $v_j u$。全部列都落在 $\operatorname{span}\{u\}$ 中，并且至少一列非零，所以列空间恰好是一维。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conversely, if $A$ has rank one, choose any nonzero column as $u$. Every column belongs to its one-dimensional column space and has a unique coefficient $v_j$ relative to $u$. Collecting these coefficients into $v=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)^T$ gives $A=uv^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反过来，若 $\operatorname{rank}(A)=1$，从 $A$ 中选一条非零列作为 $u$。每一列都属于同一个一维列空间，因而可唯一写成 $v_j u$；把这些系数排成 $v=(v_1,\ldots,v_n)^T$，就得到 $A=uv^T$。

$$
\begin{pmatrix}1&4&5\\2&8&10\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}1&4&5\end{pmatrix}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $u=(1,2)^T$ and $v=(1,4,5)^T$. The three columns are $u,4u,5u$. Reversing the orientation to row times column produces a scalar when the inner sizes match, not this matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

此处 $u=(1,2)^T$、$v=(1,4,5)^T$，三列依次为 $u,4u,5u$。若改为行乘列，内侧尺寸匹配时得到的是标量，而非这个矩阵。

## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The factorization is scale-nonunique. A zero factor produces the zero matrix, so both factors must be nonzero. Over $\mathbb C$, the equivalent $uv^*$ notation is also common.
Reciprocal nonzero rescaling leaves the product unchanged. In the complex notation, renaming the conjugate of the second factor converts one representation into the other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

外积分解不唯一。对任意 $c\ne0$，
$$
uv^T=(cu)(v/c)^T.
$$
若 $u$ 或 $v$ 为零，外积就是零矩阵，秩为 $0$；因此“外积的秩为一”必须明确两个因子都非零。复数情形也可按内积习惯写成 $uv^*$，这只是把第二个因子的坐标取共轭后重新命名。

> [!question]- 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can $uv^T$ not have two independent columns?
>
> **Answer:** Column $j$ is always $v_j u$, so every column lies in the span of $u$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $uv^T$ 为什么不可能有两个线性无关的列？
>
> **答案：** 第 $j$ 列始终是 $v_j u$，全部列都落在 $\operatorname{span}\{u\}$ 中。
