# 利润再投在提高劳动需求且工资与招工条件不变时扩大雇佣

[[Lewis 二元经济模型]]中的扩张链条是：资本主义部门获得剩余，将其中一部分再投为生产资本，使劳动需求增加，再利用[[无限劳动供给|给定工资附近的充足劳动]]扩大雇佣。利润增加不是再投资的同义词；劳动吸收也不是新增资本金额自动对应的固定人数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expansion in the [[Lewis 二元经济模型|Lewis model]] proceeds from capitalist surplus to reinvestment in productive capital, higher labour demand and recruitment using [[无限劳动供给|abundant labour near the prevailing wage]]. More profit is not itself reinvestment, and an investment amount does not automatically translate into a fixed number of jobs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个单一商品、价格为 1、无折旧和其他成本的教学版本明确这条链。资本 $K$ 在当期固定，企业按给定工资 $\bar w>0$ 选择劳动 $L$，最大化 $F(K,L)-\bar wL$。若劳动边际产出递减且内点最优，就有
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A teaching version with one good priced at one, no depreciation and no other costs makes the chain explicit. Given current capital $K$ and wage $\bar w>0$, the firm chooses labour $L$ to maximize $F(K,L)-\bar wL$. With diminishing marginal product of labour and an interior optimum,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
F_L(K_t,L_t)=\bar w,\qquad
\Pi_t=F(K_t,L_t)-\bar wL_t,\qquad
K_{t+1}=K_t+s_\pi\Pi_t,\quad 0\le s_\pi\le1.
$$

$\Pi_t$ 是此简化模型扣工资后的资本剩余，尚未另扣资本机会成本，不应当作现实会计净利润或扣尽所有要素成本的纯经济利润。$s_\pi$ 是实际投回生产资本的比例，不是看到利润后就可以假定为 1。若 $s_\pi\Pi_t>0$，且新增资本使相同 $L$ 下的 $F_L$ 上升，工资和劳动可得性又未改变，新的交点才要求更多劳动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\Pi_t$ is the simplified model's surplus after wages, before a separate capital opportunity-cost deduction. It is not accounting net profit or pure economic profit after all factor costs. The share $s_\pi$ measures actual reinvestment in productive capital, not an assumed value of one. Employment rises when $s_\pi\Pi_t>0$, additional capital raises $F_L$ at the previous employment level, and the wage and labour availability remain unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造例取 $F(K,L)=4\sqrt{KL}$、$\bar w=2$，则 $F_L=2\sqrt{K/L}=2$，所以 $L=K$。初期 $K_0=10$ 时，$L_0=10$、产出为 40、工资总额为 20、剩余为 20。若实际再投四分之一，下一期 $K_1=15$；在仍能按工资 2 招到 15 单位劳动时，$L_1=15$、产出为 60、剩余为 30。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the constructed technology $F(K,L)=4\sqrt{KL}$ and wage $\bar w=2$. Then $F_L=2\sqrt{K/L}=2$ implies $L=K$. Starting from $K_0=10$, employment is 10, output 40, wages total 20 and surplus is 20. Reinvesting one quarter gives $K_1=15$. If 15 units of labour remain available at wage 2, employment becomes 15, output 60 and surplus 30.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子中剩余额上升，但资本主义部门内部的剩余份额仍为一半；不能把“利润额”“利润率”和“占全经济收入的份额”混说。如果剩余全部消费，便没有上述新增资本；如果技术改变了用工方式、工资成本上升或没有足够可招募劳动，也须重新求劳动需求。课堂 Figure 3 画的是在给定工资线下，连续再投资抬高边际产出曲线的特定过程，不是所有投资项目的就业保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Surplus rises in this example, but its share within capitalist output stays at one half. Profit amounts, rates of return and shares of economy-wide income are different quantities. Consuming all surplus provides none of the additional capital above. Changed production techniques, higher wage costs or insufficient recruitable labour require labour demand to be recalculated. The classroom's Figure 3 shows a particular sequence of reinvestment and rising marginal-product schedules at a given wage, not a job guarantee for every investment project.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
