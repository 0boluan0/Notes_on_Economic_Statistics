# 白色三兵是低位或整理后连续三根阳线收盘逐次抬高的组合

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three white soldiers is a formation after a low-price or stable period in which three consecutive up candles have progressively higher closes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

白色三兵是价格在低位或稳定区域后出现的三根连续阳[[K线]]组合：收盘逐根抬高，每根接近本期最高价，后两根开盘位于前一实体内部或附近。本课也称红三兵；“白”“红”来自绘图约定，判断仍以收盘高于开盘为准。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three white soldiers consists of three consecutive up [[K线|candlesticks]] after a low-price or stable period. Closes rise progressively and lie near each period's high; the later openings are inside or near the preceding body. The course also calls them three red soldiers. These colors are display conventions; an up candle closes above its open.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仅有三根阳线不够：若后两根实体明显缩小或上影线拉长，已经偏离稳定推进的经典轮廓，原课另称前方受阻或停顿变形。第三根收盘后才有完整组合。学派将其解释为转强候选，不由三根阳线推出后续必涨；相反方向的三根长阴线结构见[[三只乌鸦]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three up candles alone are insufficient. Substantially shrinking later bodies or long upper shadows depart from the classical steady-advance structure; the course distinguishes advance-block and stalled variants. The formation is complete at the third close. It is interpreted as possible strengthening, not a guaranteed future rise. See [[三只乌鸦|three black crows]] for the opposite long-down-candle structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
