# 线性层把多个输入合并计算时可以用一次权重读取支撑更多乘加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Batching linear-layer inputs can reuse a weight read across more multiply-adds*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一个线性层处理多个已知输入时，权重相同。把输入按行合成矩阵后，读入的一个权重可以参与多行的乘加，而不必为每行分别从显存读取一遍。这是批量计算可能提高 [[算术强度]] 的原因；“可以”取决于实际分块和缓存实现。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A linear layer uses the same weights for multiple known inputs. Stacking those inputs into rows lets one loaded weight participate in several rows' multiply-adds. Such reuse can increase [[算术强度|arithmetic intensity]], depending on the implementation's tiling and caching.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $X\in\mathbb R^{m\times k}$、$W\in\mathbb R^{k\times n}$，计算 $Y=XW$。按一次乘法加一次加法计两个 FLOP；若输入和权重各从所选存储层级读一次、输出写一次，三者每元素均为 $b$ 字节，则理想估计为

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $Y=XW$ with the displayed shapes, count a multiply-add as two FLOPs. If inputs and weights are each read once and output written once at the selected memory level, with $b$ bytes per element, an ideal estimate is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
F\approx2mkn,\qquad D\approx b(mk+kn+mn),\qquad
I\approx\frac{2mkn}{b(mk+kn+mn)}.
$$

当 $kn$ 远大于 $mk+mn$，权重流量主导分母，便有 $I\approx2m/b$。这解释了增加输入行数为何能摊薄权重搬运：算术工作随 $m$ 增长，但权重矩阵只占一份。实际权重可能多次读入，输出也可能另经融合处理，因此这不是每个 kernel 都必然实现的流量公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $kn$ dominates $mk+mn$, weight traffic dominates and $I\approx2m/b$. Arithmetic grows with the input-row count while the weight matrix remains one copy. Real kernels may reread weights or fuse output handling, so this traffic estimate is not an implementation guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $k=n=4096$、$b=2$。$m=1$ 时 $I\approx1.00$ FLOP/byte，$m=32$ 时约为 $31.51$；不是 32 倍的实测加速，而是上述流量假设下的强度变化。它能否带来速度收益，要继续与 [[Roofline模型|设备的两条上界]] 比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $k=n=4096$ and $b=2$, intensity is about 1.00 FLOP/byte at $m=1$ and 31.51 at $m=32$. This is a modeled intensity change, not a measured 32-fold speedup. Its performance implications depend on the [[Roofline模型|device's two roofs]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[预填充]]的许多提示位置已知，往往能提供较多输入行；普通单请求 [[增量解码]] 每轮只有一个新位置。将多个请求合批也能增加行数，但它们的 [[KV cache]] 仍是各自的状态，注意力阶段还要读取各自历史。长历史、小矩阵和调度干扰都可能改变总瓶颈，不能把“prefill 算力受限、decode 带宽受限”写成无条件定律。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[预填充|Prefill]] often supplies many known prompt rows, whereas ordinary single-request [[增量解码|incremental decoding]] supplies one new position per step. Batching requests adds rows, but their [[KV cache|KV caches]] remain distinct and attention reads their respective histories. Context length, matrix shape, and scheduling can change the total bottleneck; prefill and decode do not have unconditional resource labels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
