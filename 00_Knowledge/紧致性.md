# Compactness
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*紧致性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine covering a set with open patches. The patches may be tiny, overlap, and form an infinite collection. A set is **compact** if every such covering can be reduced to finitely many of the patches already offered, while still covering every point. The set itself can contain infinitely many points: the finite requirement concerns the selected patches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

想象用一些开的小片覆盖一个集合。这些小片可以很小、可以重叠，也可以有无穷多个。若无论怎样覆盖，总能从已经给出的这些小片中挑出有限多个，仍盖住每一个点，就称这个集合**紧致**。集合本身完全可以有无穷多个点；“有限”要求限制的是选出的覆盖片数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What an open cover supplies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*开覆盖提供了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work in a [[拓扑|topological space]] $X$: its topology specifies which subsets are [[开集|open]]. Let $K\subseteq X$ be the set we want to cover. An indexed family $(U_i)_{i\in I}$ is an **open cover of $K$** when every $U_i$ is open in $X$ and every point of $K$ belongs to at least one of them:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[拓扑|拓扑空间]] $X$ 中讨论，拓扑规定哪些子集是[[开集]]。设 $K\subseteq X$ 是要覆盖的集合。一族带索引的集合 $(U_i)_{i\in I}$ 是 $K$ 的**开覆盖**，指每个 $U_i$ 都在 $X$ 中开，并且 $K$ 的每个点都至少属于其中一个：

$$K\subseteq\bigcup_{i\in I}U_i.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The set $I$ labels the patches; it need not be finite or countable. The union symbol means “belonging to at least one patch”. Patches may extend outside $K$, so containment, rather than equality with $K$, is the appropriate condition. A **subcover** selects some of these same $U_i$ and still covers $K$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

集合 $I$ 给各片编号，不要求有限，也不要求可数。并集符号表示“至少属于其中一片”。小片可以延伸到 $K$ 外，因此这里要求包含 $K$，而不要求并集恰好等于 $K$。**子覆盖**从原来的这些 $U_i$ 中选取一部分，并且仍然覆盖 $K$。

> [!important] Every cover, a finite selection · 每个覆盖都有有限选择
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The set $K$ is compact if every open cover $(U_i)_{i\in I}$ has a finite subcover: there is a finite subset $J\subseteq I$ such that
> $$K\subseteq\bigcup_{i\in J}U_i.$$
> Different covers may require different finite selections and different numbers of patches. A single convenient cover does not establish compactness.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若每个开覆盖 $(U_i)_{i\in I}$ 都存在有限索引集 $J\subseteq I$，使所选的 $U_i$ 仍覆盖 $K$，则 $K$ 紧致。不同覆盖可以需要不同的选择与不同的有限片数。找到一个方便的覆盖，并不能证明紧致性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $\{X\}$ is a one-set open cover of every subset of $X$. Its existence tells us nothing about other covers. To prove noncompactness, it is enough to find **one** open cover for which no finite selection works. To prove compactness, we must handle an arbitrary open cover.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，$\{X\}$ 是 $X$ 每个子集的单片开覆盖，但这完全不能保证其他覆盖也能有限选取。要否定紧致性，只需找到**一个**无法抽出有限子覆盖的开覆盖；要证明紧致性，则必须处理任意给定的开覆盖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Infinitely many points can still be compact
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*无穷多个点也可以紧致*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every finite set is compact: for each of its finitely many points, choose one covering patch containing that point. The empty set is compact too; choosing no patches covers it. More interestingly, consider the following infinite subset of the usual real line:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个有限集合都紧致：给定开覆盖后，为有限多个点各选一片包含它的覆盖片即可。空集也紧致，不选任何覆盖片就已盖住它。更有意思的是通常实数线中的这个无限集合：

$$K=\{0\}\cup\{1/n:n=1,2,3,\ldots\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take any open cover. Some member $U$ contains $0$. Because $U$ is open, it contains an interval $(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)$ for some $\varepsilon>0$. Choose an integer $N\ge2$ with $N>1/\varepsilon$. Then all $1/n$ with $n\ge N$ already lie in $U$. Only the finitely many points $1,1/2,\ldots,1/(N-1)$ remain to be covered; choose one cover member for each. Together with $U$, these give a finite subcover. This proves compactness directly: one neighbourhood handles the entire tail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任取一个开覆盖。其中必有一片 $U$ 包含 $0$。因为 $U$ 开，存在 $\varepsilon>0$ 使 $(-\varepsilon,\varepsilon)\subseteq U$。选整数 $N\ge2$ 且 $N>1/\varepsilon$，则所有 $n\ge N$ 对应的 $1/n$ 都已落在 $U$ 中。剩下的只有有限多个点 $1,1/2,\ldots,1/(N-1)$，为它们各选一片即可。连同 $U$ 就得到有限子覆盖。这是直接证明：一个邻域便处理了整个尾部。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The closed interval $[0,1]$ is another infinite compact set; its compactness follows from [[Heine–Borel 定理的边界|the Heine–Borel theorem]]. Neither example says that an infinite cover must already have finitely many members. It says that finitely many of its members suffice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

闭区间 $[0,1]$ 也是无限紧致集，其紧致性可由 [[Heine–Borel 定理的边界|Heine–Borel 定理]]得到。两个例子都不是说无限覆盖本来只有有限多片，而是说其中有限多片已经足够。

> [!example] A cover that cannot be shortened enough · 无法有限缩减的覆盖
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $K=(0,1)$ in $\mathbb R$, let $U_n=(1/n,1)$ for integers $n\ge2$. Every $x\in(0,1)$ belongs to a patch once $n>1/x$, so this is an open cover. In any nonempty finite selection, let $N$ be the largest index. The patches are nested, so their union is just $(1/N,1)$, which misses, for example, $1/(2N)\in K$. The empty selection also fails. Thus $(0,1)$ is not compact.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\mathbb R$ 中取 $K=(0,1)$，令 $U_n=(1/n,1)$，其中整数 $n\ge2$。每个 $x\in(0,1)$ 在 $n>1/x$ 时就落入相应覆盖片，因此这确实是开覆盖。任意非空有限选择都有最大索引 $N$；由于这些区间嵌套，其并集只是 $(1/N,1)$，会漏掉 $K$ 中的 $1/(2N)$。空选择也不覆盖 $K$。所以 $(0,1)$ 不紧致。

## Which space supplies the open sets?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*开集属于哪个空间？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
We can instead view $K$ as a space in its own right, with the [[子空间拓扑|subspace topology]]. Its open sets have the form $K\cap U$ with $U$ open in $X$. The two cover tests agree: intersecting an ambient cover with $K$ gives a relative cover, and every relative cover arises this way. A finite selection covers $K$ in one description exactly when it does in the other. Thus saying “$K$ is compact in $X$” means that its inherited topology is compact; it does not require $K$ itself to be open in $X$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也可以把 $K$ 看成独立空间，采用[[子空间拓扑]]；它的开集形如 $K\cap U$，其中 $U$ 在 $X$ 中开。两种覆盖检验等价：将环境开覆盖与 $K$ 取交得到相对开覆盖，而每个相对开覆盖都可这样表示。同一有限选择在一种描述中盖住 $K$，当且仅当在另一种描述中也盖住。因此，“$K$ 在 $X$ 中紧致”说的是其继承拓扑紧致，并不要求 $K$ 本身在 $X$ 中开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The open-cover definition applies to arbitrary topological spaces. In a [[度量空间|metric space]], [[紧致性的序列判别|sequences give an equivalent test]]: every sequence must have a subsequence converging to a point still in the set. In finite-dimensional Euclidean space, closedness and boundedness provide another equivalent test. Those are theorems with specific hypotheses, not replacements for the general definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开覆盖定义适用于任意拓扑空间。在[[度量空间]]中，[[紧致性的序列判别|序列提供等价检验]]：每条序列必须有一个收敛子列，且极限仍在集合内。有限维欧氏空间中，闭且有界又提供一种等价检验。这些是带有特定条件的定理，不是一般定义本身。
