# 同一凸域上的凹函数的非负加权和仍然凹

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A nonnegative weighted sum of concave functions on a common convex domain is concave*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f_i$ 在同一凸域凹、$a_i\ge0$，则 $F=\sum_i a_if_i$ 凹。将每个弦不等式乘 $a_i$ 后求和即得
$$
F(tx+(1-t)y)\ge tF(x)+(1-t)F(y).
$$
允许零权重；负权重则可能反转曲率，例如 $-x^2$ 乘 $-1$ 后为凸函数。仿射函数同时凹凸，因此在非负价格 $p$ 下，凹生产函数 $g$ 的利润 $pg(x)-w^Tx$ 仍凹。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying each concavity inequality by its nonnegative weight and adding proves the result. Zero weights are allowed; negative ones may reverse curvature. Since affine functions are also concave, concave production combined with nonnegative output prices and linear costs yields concave profits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
