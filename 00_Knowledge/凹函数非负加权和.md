# Nonnegative sums of concave functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*凹函数的非负加权和*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding concave functions with fixed nonnegative weights preserves concavity. Each function already rewards mixing inputs at least as much as mixing their output values; scaling these comparisons without reversing them and then adding keeps the same property. This gives a way to build a new concave objective from simpler pieces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把凹函数乘以固定非负权重再相加，仍然得到凹函数。每个函数在混合输入处的值，已经不低于混合输出的值；非负缩放不反转比较方向，再相加就保留了这项性质。因此，可以从简单函数拼出较复杂的凹目标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recall that a [[凸集|convex domain]] contains the segment between any two inputs. For a [[凹凸性|concave function]], the value at $tx+(1-t)y$ is at least $tf(x)+(1-t)f(y)$ whenever $0\le t\le1$. The same input mixture must be available to every function in the sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[凸集|凸定义域]]包含任意两输入之间的线段。[[凹凸性|凹函数]]要求：$0\le t\le1$ 时，$tx+(1-t)y$ 处的值不低于 $tf(x)+(1-t)f(y)$。求和中的每个函数都必须能接受同一个混合输入。

> [!example] Two shifted parabolas · 两条平移后的抛物线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $\mathbb R$, let $f_1(u)=-u^2$ and $f_2(u)=-(u-2)^2$. Both are concave. Give them weights two and three:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$F(u)=2f_1(u)+3f_2(u)=-5u^2+12u-12.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At inputs zero and two, $F(0)=-12$ and $F(2)=-8$, whose average is $-10$. At their midpoint, $F(1)=-5$, which is higher. This one comparison illustrates the rule; the proof below covers every pair and every mixture. The weights add to five, not one, which is allowed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\mathbb R$ 上，$f_1(u)=-u^2$ 与 $f_2(u)=-(u-2)^2$ 都凹。分别给权重二和三，得到上式。两个端点的值为 $F(0)=-12$、$F(2)=-8$，平均为 $-10$；中点值 $F(1)=-5$ 更高。这只是展示一次比较；下面的证明覆盖全部端点与权重。这里的函数权重和为五，无须等于一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The finite-sum statement
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有限加权和的结论*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f_1,\ldots,f_k:U\to\mathbb R$ be a finite family of concave, real-valued functions on the same convex set $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, with $n,k\ge1$. Choose constants $a_1,\ldots,a_k\ge0$ and define $F(x)=\sum_{i=1}^k a_i f_i(x)$. Then $F$ is concave on $U$. The index $i$ selects a function; its constant $a_i$ is its weight. No differentiability is required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f_1,\ldots,f_k:U\to\mathbb R$ 是同一凸集 $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 上有限个实值凹函数，$n,k\ge1$。取固定常数 $a_1,\ldots,a_k\ge0$，定义 $F(x)=\sum_{i=1}^k a_i f_i(x)$，则 $F$ 在 $U$ 上凹。指标 $i$ 用来选择函数，常数 $a_i$ 就是它的权重；这里不要求函数可微。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplying each concavity inequality by its nonnegative weight and adding proves the result. Zero weights are allowed; negative ones may reverse curvature. Since affine functions are also concave, concave production combined with nonnegative output prices and linear costs yields concave profits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f_i$ 在同一凸域凹、$a_i\ge0$，则 $F=\sum_i a_if_i$ 凹。将每个弦不等式乘 $a_i$ 后求和即得
$$
F(tx+(1-t)y)\ge tF(x)+(1-t)F(y).
$$
允许零权重；负权重则可能反转曲率，例如 $-x^2$ 乘 $-1$ 后为凸函数。仿射函数同时凹凸，因此在非负价格 $p$ 下，凹生产函数 $g$ 的利润 $pg(x)-w^Tx$ 仍凹。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Add the defining inequalities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把定义中的不等式相加*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take any $x,y\in U$ and $0\le t\le1$, and write $z=tx+(1-t)y$. Convexity places $z$ in the domain. For each index $i$, concavity gives $f_i(z)\ge t f_i(x)+(1-t)f_i(y)$. Multiplying by $a_i\ge0$ keeps the direction, including equality when $a_i=0$. Summing the finite list gives

$$\begin{aligned}
F(z)&=\sum_{i=1}^k a_i f_i(z)\\
&\ge\sum_{i=1}^k a_i\bigl[t f_i(x)+(1-t)f_i(y)\bigr]\\
&=tF(x)+(1-t)F(y).
\end{aligned}$$

Since the endpoints and $t$ were arbitrary, this is exactly concavity of $F$. All functions are finite-valued, so a zero coefficient causes no undefined product with infinity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任取 $x,y\in U$、$0\le t\le1$，令 $z=tx+(1-t)y$。凸性保证 $z$ 在定义域内。对每个 $i$，凹性给出 $f_i(z)\ge t f_i(x)+(1-t)f_i(y)$；乘以 $a_i\ge0$ 不改变不等号方向，$a_i=0$ 时变为等式。把有限个式子相加，便得到所列推导。由于端点与 $t$ 任取，这正是 $F$ 的凹性。所有函数都取有限实数值，因此零权重不会产生零乘无穷的未定义表达式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The input weights $t$ and $1-t$ add to one because they locate a point on a segment. The function weights $a_i$ have a different role and need not add to one. Some or all may be zero; if all are zero, $F$ is the constant zero function and is concave. If the functions originally have different convex domains, the same proof applies on their common intersection. An empty intersection supplies no input to evaluate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入的权重 $t$ 与 $1-t$ 和为一，是为了在线段上定位；函数的权重 $a_i$ 扮演不同角色，无须和为一。可以有部分零权重，甚至全部为零；全零时 $F$ 为常数零，仍凹。若原先的函数定义在不同凸域上，可在共同交集上使用同一证明；交集为空时则没有可以计算的输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why linear costs do not spoil concavity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么线性成本不破坏凹性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An affine function has the form $x\mapsto c^Tx+b$, a constant plus a linear expression, and gives equality in every chord comparison. It is therefore both concave and convex. For production $g(x)$, a fixed output price $p\ge0$ and a fixed cost vector $w$, the profit $p g(x)-w^Tx$ is concave whenever $g$ is concave on a convex input domain. Here $x$ records input quantities, $g(x)$ is output, and $w^Tx=\sum_j w_jx_j$ is the [[向量点积|dot product]] giving linear cost. The negative sign on this affine term does not hurt: an affine function of either sign is still concave.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仿射函数形如 $x\mapsto c^Tx+b$，即常数加上线性式，在每个弦比较中都取等，所以既凹又凸。对于生产函数 $g(x)$、固定产出价格 $p\ge0$ 和固定成本向量 $w$，若 $g$ 在凸投入域上凹，利润 $p g(x)-w^Tx$ 也凹。这里 $x$ 记录投入数量，$g(x)$ 是产量，$w^Tx=\sum_j w_jx_j$ 是给出线性成本的[[向量点积]]。仿射项前的负号不会破坏结论，因为任意符号的仿射函数仍然凹。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For instance, take $g(u)=4u-u^2$ on $[0,4]$, price $p=2$ and unit cost $w=3$. Then profit is $\Pi(u)=2(4u-u^2)-3u=5u-2u^2$, a concave function on the same interval. This proves its shape. Whether an optimum exists, and which point attains it, are separate questions; the [[凸优化全局性|global-optimality theorem]] describes one consequence once a local optimum is present.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如在 $[0,4]$ 上取 $g(u)=4u-u^2$，产出价格 $p=2$、单位投入成本 $w=3$，则利润 $\Pi(u)=2(4u-u^2)-3u=5u-2u^2$ 在同一区间上凹。这证明的是函数形状；最优值是否存在、由哪一点取得，是另外的问题。[[凸优化全局性]]说明了已经存在局部最优点时的一项后果。

## The weights must stay fixed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*权重必须保持固定*

> [!warning] Nonnegative at each input is not enough · 逐点非负还不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $[0,1]$, both $f_1(u)=u$ and $f_2(u)=-u$ are affine and hence concave. If the weights depend on the input, taking $a_1(u)=u$ and $a_2(u)=1-u$ gives $F(u)=2u^2-u$. These weights are nonnegative and even sum to one, yet $F(0)=0$, $F(1)=1$ and $F(1/2)=0<1/2$. The resulting function is not concave. The theorem uses the same constants when comparing all three inputs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[0,1]$ 上，$f_1(u)=u$ 与 $f_2(u)=-u$ 都是仿射函数，因此都凹。若让权重依赖输入，取 $a_1(u)=u$、$a_2(u)=1-u$，就得到 $F(u)=2u^2-u$。权重处处非负，甚至和为一，但 $F(0)=0$、$F(1)=1$、$F(1/2)=0<1/2$，结果并不凹。定理在比较三个输入时，使用的是同一组常数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A negative constant weight can also invalidate the guarantee: multiplying $-u^2$ by $-1$ produces $u^2$, whose midpoint at inputs $-1,1$ lies below their average. This does not say that every negative-weight combination fails; affine terms show otherwise. Nonnegative weights are a sufficient rule for arbitrary concave summands. The finite-sum theorem alone does not settle convergence or finite-valuedness for an infinite series of functions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

负常数权重也可能使保证失效：把 $-u^2$ 乘以 $-1$ 得到 $u^2$，在输入 $-1,1$ 之间，中点值低于端点平均。但这并不是说每个含负权重的组合都失败，仿射项就是例子；非负权重是适用于任意凹函数项的充分规则。有限求和定理本身也不负责判断无穷函数级数的收敛或有限值性质。
