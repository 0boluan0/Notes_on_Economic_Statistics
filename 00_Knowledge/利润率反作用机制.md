# 剩余价值率提高与生产资料贬值能够抵消资本构成上升对利润率的压力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A higher surplus-value rate and cheaper means of production can offset pressure from a higher capital composition.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[资本构成与利润率|可比口径的利润率关系]] $p'=x/(1+j)$ 中，提高剩余价值率 $x$ 能增大分子；降低同量生产资料的价值，可以抑制 $j=c_a/v_a$ 的上升。同一次生产技术进步可能既改变用工和设备组合，又通过[[相对剩余价值生产]]或生产资料降价改变价值比例，所以应共同考察这些效果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[资本构成与利润率|profit-rate relation on a comparable basis]], $p'=x/(1+j)$, a higher surplus-value rate $x$ enlarges the numerator, while cheaper means of production can restrain the rise of $j=c_a/v_a$. One technical change may alter labour and equipment requirements while also changing value relations through [[相对剩余价值生产|relative surplus-value production]] or cheaper inputs. These effects must be examined together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $x=1,j=4,p'=20\%$ 出发，若 $j$ 升到9，要保持原利润率，$x$ 就须升到2。一般地，在相同周转口径下，恰好抵消需要
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Starting from $x=1,j=4,p'=20\%$, a rise in $j$ to nine requires $x$ to rise to two to preserve the original rate. More generally, with the same turnover basis, exact offset requires
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{x_2}{x_1}=\frac{1+j_2}{1+j_1},\qquad x_1>0.
$$

这是判断所需幅度的代数条件。它并未证明劳动力再生产时间实际能下降多少、工作日能延长多少，或设备能降价多少；这些须由相应生产和劳动条件支持。技术构成的物量增长与价值比率的区别，见[[资本有机构成]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an algebraic condition for the required magnitude. It does not establish how far labour-power reproduction time can actually fall, hours can rise or equipment can become cheaper; those changes require support from the relevant production and labour conditions. See [[资本有机构成|organic composition]] for the distinction between physical growth and value ratios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

卷III第14章还讨论工资压低到劳动力价值以下、相对过剩人口支持新生产部门、以及贸易降低生产资料和生活资料成本等反作用。原典把这些因素放在下降倾向内部讨论，并没有给出一条可直接套用到每个时期的净变化幅度。沿这条课程线，至少要分别追踪构成、剩余率和周转，才能判断实际合成结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volume III, Chapter 14 also discusses wages below the value of labour power, relative surplus population supporting new industries, and trade cheapening means of production and subsistence. The text treats these as counteracting influences within the tendency and supplies no net magnitude directly applicable to every period. This course interface therefore requires tracking composition, surplus-value rates and turnover separately before judging their combined result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
