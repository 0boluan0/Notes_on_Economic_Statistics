# Universal claims and counterexamples
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*全称命题与反例*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A claim about **every** allowed input promises more than a list of successful examples. One failure is enough to break that promise, whereas many successes may still leave an unchecked input. This difference explains why testing can discover a false claim without, by itself, proving an unrestricted one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
关于**每个**允许输入的断言，比一串成功例子作出了更强的承诺。一个失败就足以打破承诺；很多次成功，却仍可能留下未检查的输入。这种不对称解释了为什么测试可以发现错误命题，却不能仅凭这些测试证明没有限制的普遍断言。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $D$ be the stated **domain**, the set of objects allowed in the claim, and let $P(x)$ be a [[命题与谓词|predicate]], a condition that is true or false once $x$ and its meaning are fixed. The universal statement $\forall x\in D\,P(x)$ says that $P$ holds for every member of $D$. The symbol $\forall$ means “for every”; $x\in D$ means “$x$ belongs to $D$.” A **counterexample** is an object $a\in D$ for which $P(a)$ is false.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $D$ 是所声明的**论域**，也就是命题允许的对象集合；$P(x)$ 是[[命题与谓词|谓词]]，在固定输入 $x$ 与含义后具有真假的条件。全称命题 $\forall x\in D\,P(x)$ 表示 $D$ 中每个成员都满足 $P$；$\forall$ 读作“对每个”，$x\in D$ 表示“$x$ 属于 $D$”。一个**反例**就是属于 $D$、却使 $P(a)$ 为假的对象 $a$。

> [!important] Success and failure have different force · 成功与失败的作用不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Over a domain that has not been exhausted, checking any finite number of examples establishes only those cases and cannot exclude failure elsewhere. In contrast, one valid counterexample is enough to make a universal statement false.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对一个未穷尽的论域，检查再多有限例子也只说明这些例子成立，不能排除未检查对象中的失败。相反，全称命题只要有一个合法反例就为假。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the [[量词否定规则|negation rule]] for a universal claim:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这正是全称命题的[[量词否定规则]]：

$$\neg\bigl[\forall x\in D\,P(x)\bigr]\quad\equiv\quad\exists x\in D\,\neg P(x).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\neg$ means “not,” $\exists$ means “there exists at least one,” and $\equiv$ means that the two statements have the same truth condition. The right side does **not** say that every object fails. It asks for one failure in the same original domain. The reason is direct: if an allowed object fails, “every object succeeds” cannot hold; conversely, a universal claim is false precisely when some allowed object fails it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里 $\neg$ 表示“非”，$\exists$ 表示“至少存在一个”，$\equiv$ 表示两条陈述的真值条件相同。右边**没有**说每个对象都失败，只要求原论域内的一个失败。理由很直接：只要一个允许的对象失败，“每个对象都成功”就不成立；反过来，全称命题为假，恰好意味着有允许的对象不满足它。

## Forty successes and the next input
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*四十次成功之后的输入*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $p(n)=n^2+n+41$. The values $p(0)$ through $p(39)$ are prime, but $p(40)=41^2$. The first forty successes may motivate a conjecture, but they do not prove that $p(n)$ is prime for every $n\in\mathbb N$. The single case $n=40$ refutes that universal claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
令 $p(n)=n^2+n+41$。从 $n=0$ 到 $39$，$p(n)$ 都是素数；但 $p(40)=41^2$。前四十个成功值可以启发猜想，却没有证明 $\forall n\in\mathbb N,\ p(n)$ 为素数。$n=40$ 则一次性否定了该全称命题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mathbb N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$, following the cited course. A [[素数|prime]] is an integer greater than one with no positive divisors except one and itself. At the allowed input $40$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里按所引课程约定，$\mathbb N=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$。[[素数]]是大于一、正因子只有一和自身的整数。对于允许的输入 $40$，

$$p(40)=40^2+40+41=1681=41\cdot41.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $1<41<1681$, this is an exact certificate that $p(40)$ is not prime. The input $40$ is the counterexample; the factorization verifies its failure. No estimate of how frequently the formula gives primes is needed. The refutation also does not say that the first forty values were wrong or that every later value is composite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
因为 $1<41<1681$，这个精确分解证明了 $p(40)$ 不是素数。反例对象是输入 $40$，因式分解核实了它为什么失败，不必估计这个公式多大比例会产生素数。反驳也没有宣称前四十个结果算错了，更没有宣称此后的每个值都是合数。

## What successful tests leave undecided
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*成功测试还留下什么未定部分？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose only inputs in a finite set $S\subseteq D$ have been checked and all pass. This establishes $\forall x\in S\,P(x)$. If an input $a\in D$ remains outside $S$, those test results alone contain no value for $P(a)$. Without additional mathematical information, they are compatible with either success or failure there. This does not prove the original claim false; it explains why the tests alone do not prove it true.

An additional argument can bridge that gap. For example, the statement “every integer has an even product $n(n+1)$” can be proved by the exhaustive even/odd cases in [[分类证明的完备性|proof by cases]]. A proof that reduces all inputs to finitely many representatives must justify that reduction; its coverage argument, together with the checks, does the work that isolated tests cannot do.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
假设只检查了有限子集 $S\subseteq D$ 中的输入，而且全部通过，那么得到的是 $\forall x\in S\,P(x)$。若 $D$ 中还有一个不属于 $S$ 的输入 $a$，这些测试本身没有给出 $P(a)$ 的真值。在没有额外数学信息时，它们既容许该处成功，也容许该处失败。这不是证明原命题为假，而是解释测试本身为什么还不能证明它为真。

额外论证可以补上缺口。例如，“每个整数的 $n(n+1)$ 都是偶数”可用[[分类证明的完备性|分类证明]]中的偶数、奇数两类完整证明。如果某份证明把全部输入化归到有限个代表情形，就必须说明这种化归为什么覆盖所有输入；覆盖性论证与逐项核验共同完成了孤立测试做不到的工作。

## Finite domains and existence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*有限论域与存在命题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the finite domain $D=\{-1,0,1\}$, checking $(-1)^2=1$, $0^2=0$, and $1^2=1$ proves that every member has a nonnegative square: these are all the allowed objects. The same three calculations alone do not cover all real numbers. Changing the domain changes the claim, even when the displayed formula stays the same.

An existential claim $\exists x\in D\,P(x)$ asks for at least one success. A [[全称证明与存在见证|witness]] with verified membership and property can therefore establish it. In the polynomial example, $n=0$ gives $p(0)=41$, a prime, so it proves that some allowed input produces a prime. The success at $0$ and the failure at $40$ coexist: “some succeeds” and “not every input succeeds” are compatible statements. Failing to find a witness is not itself proof that none exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对于有限论域 $D=\{-1,0,1\}$，检查 $(-1)^2=1$、$0^2=0$、$1^2=1$，就证明了每个成员的平方非负，因为所有允许对象已被列尽。但这三次计算本身没有覆盖所有实数；即使公式相同，换论域也已经换了命题。

存在命题 $\exists x\in D\,P(x)$ 只要求至少一个成功。核实对象属于论域并满足性质后，一个[[全称证明与存在见证|见证]]就足以建立它。多项式例子中，$n=0$ 给出素数 $p(0)=41$，因而证明存在产生素数的允许输入。$0$ 处成功与 $40$ 处失败完全可以并存：“有些成功”与“并非全部成功”相容。没有找到见证，本身也不是不存在见证的证明。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- If the domain is finite and every element has genuinely been checked, exhaustive verification can be a proof. The issue is coverage, not the mere use of examples.
- One example may prove an existential statement when it is a valid witness. It does not by itself establish a universal pattern.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 若论域本身是已被完整枚举的有限集合，逐项检查所有元素可以构成穷尽证明；问题不在“用了例子”，而在是否覆盖了论域。
- 一个例子可以证明存在命题，只要它确实是所需的 witness；它不能凭自身证明普遍规律。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the original claim has hypotheses, the counterexample must satisfy them too; [[反例有效性|counterexample validity]] gives that candidate-level check. In the empty domain there is no counterexample and no existential witness: by the usual convention the universal statement is true and the existential statement false. An expression undefined at a proposed input first requires a domain or definition check; it must not be silently assigned the truth value “false.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若原命题带有前提，反例也必须满足这些前提；[[反例有效性]]负责检查具体候选。空论域中既没有反例，也没有存在见证；按通常约定，全称命题为真、存在命题为假。若表达式在候选输入处没有定义，应先核对论域或定义，不能偷偷把“未定义”当作“假”。

> [!question]- Testing an unrestricted claim · 检验无限范围的断言
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A program passes one million tests. Why do those tests alone not prove correctness for every integer input?
>
> **Answer:** The integer domain has not been exhausted, so an untested input may still be a counterexample. Tests provide evidence and can expose faults, but they do not discharge a universal quantifier.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 程序通过一百万个测试，为什么仍不能单凭这些测试证明“对所有整数输入都正确”？
>
> **答案：** 整数输入没有被穷尽，未测试输入仍可能包含反例；测试提供证据和故障发现能力，但不是覆盖全称量词的证明。
