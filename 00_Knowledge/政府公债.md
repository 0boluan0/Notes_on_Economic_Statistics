# 政府公债是政府通过举借形成并按约定承担偿付义务的债务
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Public borrowing creates debt that the government is obliged to service on agreed terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政府向债权人取得资金，同时承担未来偿还本金、支付利息或两者兼有的义务，就是公债融资。发行政府[[债券]]是常见形式，贷款也是举借方式；不能把“公债”理解成政府收到的一笔不用偿付的税款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The government obtains funds in exchange for an obligation to repay principal, pay interest, or both. Issuing government [[债券|bonds]] is one form; taking loans is another. Borrowing is not a tax receipt without a repayment obligation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，政府按面值发行一张本金100、年息5、两年到期的债券：今天取得100，按约定第一年付5，第二年付105。这里列的是承诺，不是债权人一定能收到的金额；也不能从债券由政府发行，就推出它没有违约、币值或流动性风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A two-year bond issued at par with principal 100 and annual interest 5 provides 100 now and promises payments of 5 and 105. These are contractual amounts, not guaranteed receipts. Government issuance does not eliminate default, currency-value or liquidity risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读一笔公债，先明确债务人是中央还是地方、币种、到期日和利率条款。统计[[政府债务总额与净额|全部政府债务]]时，范围还可能包括应付账款等并非主动发行债券形成的负债。具体融资记录见[[财政收入与融资]]，到期能否续借则见[[公债再融资风险]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the debtor, currency, maturity and interest terms. [[政府债务总额与净额|Total government debt]] can also include payables not created through bond issuance. See [[财政收入与融资|revenue versus financing]] for recording and [[公债再融资风险|rollover risk]] for refinancing at maturity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
