# Generator matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*生成矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **generator matrix** records how quickly a continuous-time system leaves each state and where it can go next. A machine can fail at rate $2$ per hour and be repaired at rate $3$ per hour. These numbers describe local speeds; they are not probabilities of failure or repair during a whole hour. The generator packages all such rates into one array.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**生成矩阵**记录连续时间系统从各状态离开的快慢，以及接下来可以去哪里。例如，机器的故障率为每小时 $2$，维修完成率为每小时 $3$。这些数描述局部变化速度，不是整整一小时内故障或修好的概率。生成矩阵把各方向的速率放在同一张表里。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[CTMC定义|continuous-time Markov chain]] stays in a discrete state and then jumps to another. Here we use the usual pure-jump model on a finite or countable state set $S$, with a finite total rate at each state, and assume [[CTMC时间齐次转移函数|time homogeneity]]: the rule depends on elapsed time, not the calendar starting time. With the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]], $q_{ij}$ refers to a jump **from $i$ to $j$**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[CTMC定义|连续时间 Markov 链]]在离散状态中停留，再跳到另一个状态。这里采用有限或可数状态集合 $S$ 上的标准纯跳模型，每个状态的总速率有限，并假设[[CTMC时间齐次转移函数|时间齐次]]：规则只依赖经过的时间，不依赖从哪个日历时点开始。采用[[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]]，$q_{ij}$ 表示**从 $i$ 到 $j$** 的跳转速率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read one row as a rate budget
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把一行读成速率收支*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the working and failed states, in that order, the machine has
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按“正常、故障”的状态顺序，前面的机器对应

$$Q=\begin{pmatrix}-2&2\\3&-3\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first row says that the only departure from working is failure, at rate $2$. Its diagonal entry is $-2$, the negative total departure rate. The second row says the same thing about repair at rate $3$. A negative diagonal is not a negative probability: it accounts for the first-order decrease in the probability of remaining in the starting state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一行表示：从正常状态离开的唯一方向是故障，速率为 $2$；对角元 $-2$ 则是总离开率的相反数。第二行相应记录速率为 $3$ 的维修。负对角元不是负概率，而是在记录“仍处于起点状态”的概率在一阶近似中怎样减少。

> [!abstract] A stable conservative Q-matrix · 稳定、保守的 Q 矩阵
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Write $Q=(q_{ij})$. Its off-diagonal entries are nonnegative, the total exit rate $q_i$ is finite for each $i$, and the diagonal balances that total:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 写成 $Q=(q_{ij})$。非对角元非负，每个状态 $i$ 的总出口率 $q_i$ 有限，对角元抵消这份总速率：
>
> $$q_{ij}\ge0\quad(j\ne i),\qquad
> q_i:=\sum_{j\ne i}q_{ij}<\infty,\qquad q_{ii}=-q_i.$$
> $$\sum_{j\in S}q_{ij}=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Stable” here means a finite rate at every individual state. “Conservative” for the **Q-matrix** means zero row sums: there is no extra instantaneous killing rate outside $S$. These are technical meanings, not claims about long-run stability. For a finite matrix, zero row sums can be written $Q\mathbf1=0$, where $\mathbf1$ is the column containing only ones. The [[出口率|exit rate]] is a sum over $j\ne i$; summing the whole row would instead give zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里“稳定”指每个状态的速率分别有限；**Q 矩阵**的“保守”指行和为零，没有另外设置流向 $S$ 之外的瞬时杀死率。这些是术语，不是在声称系统长期稳定。有限矩阵的零行和可写成 $Q\mathbf1=0$，其中 $\mathbf1$ 是所有分量都为一的列向量。[[出口率]]只对 $j\ne i$ 求和；若把整行相加，得到的反而是零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $q_i=0$, every off-diagonal entry in that row is zero. The state never leaves, so it is absorbing. If the machine cannot be repaired, its failed-state row is $(0,0)$; the generator is then $\begin{pmatrix}-\lambda&\lambda\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$ for failure rate $\lambda\ge0$. Diagonal entries are therefore **nonpositive**, not necessarily strictly negative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $q_i=0$，这一行的非对角元全部为零，过程不会离开该状态，因此它是吸收态。如果机器不能维修，故障状态对应的行就是 $(0,0)$；故障率为 $\lambda\ge0$ 时，生成矩阵为 $\begin{pmatrix}-\lambda&\lambda\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$。所以对角元是**非正**，不一定严格小于零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From local rates to transition probabilities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*局部速率怎样联系转移概率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $p_{ij}(h)=\Pr_i(X_h=j)$ be the probability of being in $j$ after duration $h$, when starting from $i$; $\Pr_i$ denotes that starting condition. In zero time the chain stays at its starting state. The generator is the right-hand [[导数|derivative]] at duration zero:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $p_{ij}(h)=\Pr_i(X_h=j)$ 表示从 $i$ 出发，经过时长 $h$ 后处于 $j$ 的概率；$\Pr_i$ 表示这个起始条件。在零时长内，过程仍在起点。生成矩阵给出时长为零处的右[[导数]]：

$$q_{ij}=\lim_{h\downarrow0}\frac{p_{ij}(h)}h\ (j\ne i),\qquad
q_{ii}=\lim_{h\downarrow0}\frac{p_{ii}(h)-1}{h}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $q_{ij}h$ is a short-time approximation, not an exact transition probability for arbitrary $h$; see the [[CTMC短时概率展开|short-time expansion]]. Rates have units of inverse time, while $P(h)=(p_{ij}(h))$ contains dimensionless probabilities. On a finite state space every matrix satisfying the displayed Q-matrix conditions gives a non-explosive CTMC, with [[CTMC矩阵指数|transition matrix $P(h)=e^{hQ}$]]. An initial distribution must also be specified to determine the process law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，$q_{ij}h$ 是短时近似，不是任意 $h$ 下的精确转移概率，见[[CTMC短时概率展开]]。速率的单位是时间的倒数，$P(h)=(p_{ij}(h))$ 中则是没有单位的概率。有限状态空间中，满足上述 Q 矩阵条件就能构造非爆炸 CTMC，其[[CTMC矩阵指数|转移矩阵为 $P(h)=e^{hQ}$]]。要确定整个过程的概率律，还需要指定初始分布。

> [!warning] Zero row sums do not rule out explosion · 零行和不能排除爆炸
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On countably many states, rates can increase along a path even though each $q_i$ is finite. [[CTMC爆炸|Explosion]] means infinitely many jumps occur in a finite amount of time. For example, jumps $n\to n+1$ at rates $2^n$ have mean holding times $2^{-n}$, whose sum from $n=1$ is finite. The chain can therefore explode despite every Q row summing to zero. For the minimal process, which is killed at explosion, probabilities on the original state set can then sum to less than one. Conservativity of the Q-matrix must not be confused with conservation of probability by the process for all times. [[有界率非爆炸|Uniformly bounded exit rates]] are a sufficient non-explosion condition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 状态可数无穷时，沿路径遇到的速率可能越来越快，即使每个 $q_i$ 都有限。[[CTMC爆炸|爆炸]]指有限时间内发生无限次跳跃。例如，以速率 $2^n$ 从 $n$ 跳到 $n+1$，各次平均停留时间为 $2^{-n}$，从 $n=1$ 开始的总和有限。因此，即使 Q 的每行和都为零，链仍可能爆炸。对于在爆炸时终止的最小过程，原状态集合上的转移概率和就可能小于一。不能把 Q 矩阵的代数保守性，混同于过程在所有时刻都保持总概率一。[[有界率非爆炸|出口率一致有界]]是排除爆炸的一条充分条件。

## What if a row sums to a negative number?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*若某行的和为负数？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **subgenerator** may deliberately omit a terminal state. For example, the row $(-2,0.5)$ has deficit $1.5$: departure at rate $0.5$ goes to the listed second state, while killing at rate $1.5$ goes outside the listed space. Introduce an absorbing cemetery state $\Delta$, meaning “the process has terminated”. The extended row becomes $(-2,0.5,1.5)$ and the cemetery row is zero. This restores zero row sums for the specified killing mechanism. It does not by itself remove a separate possibility of explosion in an infinite state model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**子生成矩阵**可能有意不列出某个终止状态。例如，行 $(-2,0.5)$ 的缺额为 $1.5$：速率 $0.5$ 流向已列出的第二个状态，速率 $1.5$ 则用于离开所列空间。加入一个吸收的墓地状态 $\Delta$，表示“过程已经终止”，这一行就变成 $(-2,0.5,1.5)$，墓地状态对应的行全为零。这样恢复了指定杀死机制的零行和，但不能据此排除无限状态模型中另一种爆炸可能。
