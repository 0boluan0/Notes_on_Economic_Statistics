# 权益乘数是同一口径总资产与股东权益之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equity multiplier is total assets divided by equity on a consistent basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

权益乘数 $EM=A/E$，在 $E>0$ 时表示一元账面权益对应多少资产。它反映融资结构中的杠杆，不是每一元现金投资“创造”多少资产的生产效率。ABC期末 $2000/960\approx2.0833$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equity multiplier $EM=A/E$ measures assets per unit of positive book equity. It captures financing leverage, not productive efficiency in creating assets from cash investment. ABC's end-period value is $2000/960\approx2.0833$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同口径下，$EM=1+L/E=1/(1-L/A)$；分别联系[[产权比率]]和[[资产负债率]]。用于[[杜邦分解]]时，若资产周转率使用平均资产，权益乘数也要配对平均资产和相应权益，才能精确抵消。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a consistent basis, $EM=1+L/E=1/(1-L/A)$, linking it to [[产权比率|liabilities-to-equity]] and [[资产负债率|liabilities-to-assets]]. In [[杜邦分解|DuPont decomposition]], average assets in turnover must match those in the multiplier, together with the corresponding equity basis, for exact cancellation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

权益很小时乘数可能很大；权益为零时未定义、为负时失去通常正杠杆解释。乘数上升也不能单独证明股东价值增加，见[[ROE与杠杆的解释边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A small equity denominator can produce a large multiplier. Zero equity makes it undefined, while negative equity defeats the usual positive-leverage interpretation. An increase alone does not prove value creation; see [[ROE与杠杆的解释边界|the ROE and leverage boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
