# 粗死亡率是一定时期的死亡数除以同期总人口人年并按每千人年表示的死亡频率

粗死亡率衡量全体人口在一段时期内的死亡频率，分母是同期人口暴露的人年数。它没有消除年龄结构差异，因此不能单凭粗死亡率较高就断言各年龄的生存条件更差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The crude death rate measures mortality events over an interval relative to population exposure in person-years. It does not standardize age composition, so a higher crude death rate alone does not establish worse survival conditions at each age.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设期间死亡数为 $D$、人口暴露为 $PY$ 人年，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $D$ deaths and population exposure of $PY$ person-years,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
CDR=1000\frac{D}{PY}.
$$

只为说明构成效应，设年轻组与年长组死亡率分别为每千人年 10 和 100，两地这些组别死亡率完全相同。如果两组占人口人年的比例分别是 $90\%/10\%$ 与 $70\%/30\%$，两地粗死亡率却分别为 19 和 37。差异来自暴露的年龄权重，不是组别死亡率变坏。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a composition example, suppose younger and older groups have mortality rates of 10 and 100 per 1,000 person-years, identical in two populations. Exposure shares of $90\%/10\%$ and $70\%/30\%$ nevertheless produce crude death rates of 19 and 37. The difference comes from age weights, not worse group-specific mortality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[人口转型]]中“死亡水平下降”需要结合年龄别死亡率或生存指标理解。伴随[[人口老龄化]]，即使各年龄生存条件改善，粗死亡率仍可能因年龄权重变化而上升。把死亡事件纳入人口总量时，使用[[人口变动恒等式]]并保持时间口径一致。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Declining mortality during the [[人口转型|demographic transition]] should be interpreted with age-specific mortality or survival measures. During [[人口老龄化|population ageing]], a crude death rate can rise even as age-specific survival improves. Use the [[人口变动恒等式|population balancing equation]] for population totals with consistent time coverage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
