# 累积函数的增减由被积函数符号决定而凹凸由被积函数增减决定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An accumulation function increases according to the integrand's sign and curves according to the integrand's monotonicity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对连续 $f$，$G(x)=\int_a^xf(t)dt$ 满足 $G(a)=0$、$G'=f$。所以 $f>0$ 的区间上 $G$ 严格增加，$f<0$ 时严格减少；$f$ 从正变负给 $G$ 的局部极大，从负变正给局部极小。$f(x_0)=0$ 本身只表示一条水平切线，仍要看两侧符号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity gives a zero base value and derivative equal to the integrand. Positive and negative intervals determine strict increase and decrease; sign changes classify local extrema. A zero integrand alone gives a horizontal tangent, not an extremum classification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

进一步，$f$ 递增的区间上 $G$ 是凸函数（图像在弦下），$f$ 递减时是凹函数；若 $f$ 可导，可直接用 $G''=f'$，见[[导数判凹凸]]。凹凸转换也需要检查两侧，而不是只解 $f'=0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Where the integrand increases, the accumulation is convex and lies below its chords; where the integrand decreases, it is concave. If the integrand is differentiable, its derivative is the accumulation's second derivative. Curvature changes require a two-sided check, not merely a zero second derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $G(x)=\int_0^xe^{-t^2}dt$：$G$ 是奇函数，处处递增；$G''=-2xe^{-x^2}$，所以左半轴凸、右半轴凹，在原点有拐点，切线为 $y=x$。[[高斯积分]]另行证明 $G(x)\to\pm\sqrt\pi/2$，因此图像逐渐贴近两条水平渐近线。函数一直上升而高度仍有上界，并不矛盾：后面增加的面积越来越小且总尾面积有限。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gaussian accumulation is odd and strictly increasing, convex on the negative half-line and concave on the positive half-line, with tangent $y=x$ at its inflection point. The separate [[高斯积分|Gaussian integral]] supplies its finite limiting heights. Perpetual increase is compatible with bounded height because the remaining tail area is finite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
