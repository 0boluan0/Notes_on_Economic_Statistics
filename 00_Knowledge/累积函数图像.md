# Graphing an accumulation function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*累积函数图像*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An accumulation graph shows the **total built up so far**, not the current height of the function being integrated. If a flow rate is positive but falling, the accumulated amount is still rising—just more slowly. Reading the graph therefore requires separating three questions: where is the accumulated total, which way is it moving, and how is its slope changing?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

累积函数的图像显示的是**截至当前位置已经累积的总量**，不是被积函数此刻的高度。如果流入速率为正但正在下降，累积总量仍然增加，只是增加得越来越慢。因此读图时要分清三个问题：总量现在有多高、它往哪个方向变化、它的斜率又怎样变化？

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let a real function $f$ be [[一点连续|continuous]] on an interval $I$ containing more than one point and a fixed reference point $a$. Its [[累积函数|accumulation function]] is $G(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$ for $x\in I$; $t$ is the dummy variable and $x$ is the graph's horizontal input. Each finite segment used lies in $I$. The starting value is $G(a)=0$, and the [[积分求导定理|fundamental theorem for accumulation]] gives $G'(x)=f(x)$ at every interior point. At an included endpoint, use the corresponding one-sided derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实值函数 $f$ 在包含不止一个点且包含固定参考点 $a$ 的区间 $I$ 上[[一点连续|连续]]。它的[[累积函数]]是 $G(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$，其中 $x\in I$，$t$ 是积分哑变量，$x$ 才是图像横轴上的输入；每段实际使用的有限区间都在 $I$ 内。起始值为 $G(a)=0$，[[积分求导定理]]给出每个内点处 $G'(x)=f(x)$。若包含定义域端点，则使用相应的单侧导数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Height records an integral; slope records a value
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*高度记录积分，斜率记录点值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To find the height $G(b)$, add the **signed** area from $a$ to $b$. By [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]], the change between two graph points is $G(y)-G(x)=\int_x^y f(t)\,dt$. Knowing only $f(b)$ gives the slope at $b$, not the accumulated height there. Changing the reference point from $a$ to $c$ gives $G(x)-G(c)$, a vertical translation with the same slopes and curvature.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要找高度 $G(b)$，应把从 $a$ 到 $b$ 的**有向**面积相加。由[[定积分区间可加性]]，两个图像点之间的高度差为 $G(y)-G(x)=\int_x^y f(t)\,dt$。只知道 $f(b)$，得到的是 $b$ 处的斜率，不是此前累积的高度。把参考点从 $a$ 改为 $c$，新函数就是 $G(x)-G(c)$，图像只作竖直平移，斜率和凹凸不变。

> [!example] A falling rate can still increase the total · 速率下降，总量仍可上升
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $f(t)=1-t$ on $[0,3]$, starting at $a=0$. Then $G(x)=x-x^2/2$. On $(0,1)$ the rate is positive, so $G$ rises, although the rate is falling. At $x=1$, $f(1)=0$ while $G(1)=1/2$: a horizontal tangent does not mean zero accumulated total. After $1$, the rate is negative and $G$ falls; it reaches height zero at $x=2$, where its slope is already $-1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[0,3]$ 上取 $f(t)=1-t$，从 $a=0$ 开始，则 $G(x)=x-x^2/2$。在 $(0,1)$ 上速率为正，所以即使速率正在下降，$G$ 仍然上升。到 $x=1$ 时，$f(1)=0$，却有 $G(1)=1/2$：水平切线不等于累计总量为零。超过 $1$ 后速率为负，$G$ 开始下降；到 $x=2$ 才回到零高度，此时斜率已经是 $-1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read increase and turning points from the sign of the integrand
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*由被积函数的符号判断增减和转向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On an interval where $f>0$ throughout, $G$ is strictly increasing; where $f<0$ throughout, it is strictly decreasing. The weak signs $f\ge0$ and $f\le0$ give nondecrease and nonincrease. These statements follow from $G'=f$ and the [[导数单调性判据|derivative test for monotonicity]], or directly by comparing the added integrals. If $f=0$ on a whole interval, $G$ is constant there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $f$ 处处为正的区间上，$G$ 严格递增；在 $f$ 处处为负的区间上，$G$ 严格递减。非严格条件 $f\ge0$ 和 $f\le0$ 分别给出非减与非增。这些结论由 $G'=f$ 和[[导数单调性判据]]得到，也可直接比较新增积分。若 $f$ 在整段为零，$G$ 就在那里保持常值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At an interior point $c$, positivity just to the left and negativity just to the right make $G(c)$ a strict [[局部极值与绝对极值|local maximum]]; reversing those signs gives a strict local minimum. This is the [[一阶导数检验|first derivative test]] applied to $G$. In contrast, $f(c)=0$ alone says only $G'(c)=0$. For $f(t)=t^2$, accumulation from zero gives $G(x)=x^3/3$, which keeps increasing through zero despite its horizontal tangent. Domain endpoints require one-sided comparisons, not an imagined second side outside the domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在内点 $c$ 处，如果紧邻左侧 $f>0$、紧邻右侧 $f<0$，则 $G(c)$ 是严格的[[局部极值与绝对极值|局部极大值]]；反过来变号，则是严格局部极小值。这就是对 $G$ 使用[[一阶导数检验]]。而单独的 $f(c)=0$ 只说明 $G'(c)=0$。例如 $f(t)=t^2$ 时，从零开始的累积是 $G(x)=x^3/3$，它穿过零点时仍持续增加，尽管切线水平。定义域端点要作单侧比较，不能虚构定义域外的另一侧。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read curvature from changes in the slope
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*由斜率的变化判断凹凸*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Because the slope of $G$ is $f$, a nondecreasing $f$ makes $G$ [[凹凸性|convex (concave up)]], meaning its graph lies on or below each chord. A nonincreasing $f$ makes $G$ concave (concave down). The [[导数判凹凸|derivative criterion for convexity]] uses monotonicity of $G'$, so this step does not require $f$ to be differentiable. If $f'$ does exist throughout the interior, then $G''=f'$ gives the familiar second-derivative version. Included endpoints follow by continuity of $G$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $G$ 的斜率就是 $f$，$f$ 非减时，$G$ 为[[凹凸性|凸函数（向上弯）]]，即图像不高于连接两点的弦；$f$ 非增时，$G$ 为凹函数（向下弯）。[[导数判凹凸]]使用的是 $G'$ 的单调性，所以这一步不要求 $f$ 可导。若内部处处存在 $f'$，则 $G''=f'$ 给出熟悉的二阶导数版本；包含的端点可由 $G$ 的连续性延伸。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[拐点|inflection point]] requires an actual change of concavity across the point. Solving $f'(c)=0$ when that derivative exists is not enough. For instance, $f(t)=t^3$ accumulated from zero gives $G(x)=x^4/4$ and $G''(x)=3x^2\ge0$: the second derivative vanishes at zero, but the graph remains convex on both sides.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[拐点]]要求两侧确实发生凹凸转换。即使 $f'(c)$ 存在，仅解出 $f'(c)=0$ 也不够。例如 $f(t)=t^3$ 时，从零开始累积得到 $G(x)=x^4/4$，且 $G''(x)=3x^2\ge0$；二阶导数在零点为零，但两侧仍然都是凸的。

> [!example] The Gaussian accumulation curve · 高斯累积曲线
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $G(x)=\int_0^x e^{-t^2}\,dt$ for all real $x$. We have $G(0)=0$, $G'(x)=e^{-x^2}>0$ and $G''(x)=-2xe^{-x^2}$. Thus $G$ is strictly increasing, convex on the negative half-line and concave on the positive half-line. At zero it has an inflection point with slope $1$, so its tangent is $y=x$; an inflection point need not have a horizontal tangent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对所有实数 $x$，令 $G(x)=\int_0^x e^{-t^2}\,dt$。则 $G(0)=0$，$G'(x)=e^{-x^2}>0$，$G''(x)=-2xe^{-x^2}$。所以 $G$ 严格递增，负半轴上凸，正半轴上凹。零点是斜率为 $1$ 的拐点，因此切线为 $y=x$；拐点并不要求切线水平。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The integrand is even, meaning $f(-t)=f(t)$. Reflection and reversed bounds give $G(-x)=-G(x)$, so $G$ is odd. The separate [[高斯积分|Gaussian integral]] supplies the finite total on each half-line, hence
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 被积函数是偶函数，即 $f(-t)=f(t)$；把积分区间反射并处理反向上下限，得到 $G(-x)=-G(x)$，所以 $G$ 是奇函数。独立的[[高斯积分]]给出每条半轴上的有限总量，因此
>
> $$\lim_{x\to\infty}G(x)=\frac{\sqrt\pi}{2},
> \qquad\lim_{x\to-\infty}G(x)=-\frac{\sqrt\pi}{2}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These are horizontal asymptotes. Continued increase is compatible with a finite limiting height because the remaining positive tail area tends to zero. Merely observing $G'(x)\to0$ would not prove a finite limit: on $x\ge1$, $\int_1^x dt/t=\ln x$ grows without bound even though its derivative tends to zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这两条水平线是渐近线。函数持续上升与极限高度有限并不冲突，因为剩余的正尾面积趋于零。仅观察到 $G'(x)\to0$ 还不能证明极限有限：在 $x\ge1$ 上，$\int_1^x dt/t=\ln x$ 无界增长，但它的导数也趋于零。
