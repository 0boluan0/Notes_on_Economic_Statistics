# Todaro 迁移、城市失业与非正规部门
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Todaro Migration, Urban Unemployment, and Informality*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释为什么城市已有失业，农村劳动者仍可理性迁移：他们比较的是获得高工资正式岗位的预期收入，而不是必然收入。
> **具体锚点：** 农村收入为 60，城市正式工资为 120，获得正式工作概率为 60%，则简化预期城市收入为 72；即使 40% 面临失业，迁移仍可有吸引力。
> **核心难点：** 城市高工资同时提高成功后回报和迁移人数；若岗位增长不足，提高工资可反而提高均衡城市失业。
> **为什么重要：** 它防止政策只看已就业城市工人的工资，而忽略求职队列、非正规就业和迁移响应。
> **继续：** 若关注从剩余劳动力到工资转折的宏观积累过程，回到 [[Lewis—Fei–Ranis 二元经济模型]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It explains why rural workers can rationally migrate despite urban unemployment: they compare expected income from a chance of obtaining a high-wage formal job, not guaranteed income.
> **Concrete anchor:** If rural income is 60, the formal urban wage is 120, and the probability of formal employment is 60%, simplified expected urban income is 72. Migration can remain attractive despite a 40% risk of unemployment.
> **Central difficulty:** A high urban wage raises both the payoff from success and the number of migrants. If job creation lags, raising the wage can raise equilibrium urban unemployment.
> **Why it matters:** It prevents policy from observing only wages among employed urban workers while ignoring the job queue, informal work, and migration response.
> **Continue:** For the macro accumulation process from surplus labor to a wage turning point, return to [[Lewis—Fei–Ranis 二元经济模型|The Lewis–Fei–Ranis Dual-Economy Model]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 预期收入而非观察工资
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Expected income rather than the observed wage*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

迁移由城市工资乘获得正式工作的概率与农村收入比较，而非只比较观察工资。提高城市正式工资若不增加岗位，可能吸引更多迁移并提高城市失业，这是 Todaro paradox 的来源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Migration responds to the urban wage multiplied by the probability of obtaining formal employment relative to rural income, rather than to the observed urban wage alone. If a higher formal wage does not create enough jobs, it can attract more migrants and increase urban unemployment, the source of the Todaro paradox.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小静态表达是

$$p_u w_u +(1-p_u)w_I-c \geq w_r,$$

其中 $p_u$ 是获得正式城市工作的概率，$w_u$ 是正式工资，$w_I$ 是失业或非正规状态的收入，$c$ 是迁移成本，$w_r$ 是农村收入。原始简化模型可把失业收入设为零，现实应显式容纳非正规就业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A minimal static representation is

$$p_u w_u +(1-p_u)w_I-c \geq w_r,$$

where $p_u$ is the probability of formal urban employment, $w_u$ the formal wage, $w_I$ income while unemployed or informal, $c$ migration cost, and $w_r$ rural income. A stripped-down original model can set unemployment income to zero, but a realistic application should explicitly allow informal work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 算例与比较静态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example and comparative statics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $w_r=60$、$w_u=120$、$w_I=20$、$c=5$、$p_u=0.5$，则预期净城市收入为 $0.5\times120+0.5\times20-5=65$，迁移有吸引力。若正式工资提高到 140 但岗位不变，更多人迁移会使 $p_u$ 下降。均衡调整不是每个人都得到 20 的收入上升，而可能是队列变长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $w_r=60$, $w_u=120$, $w_I=20$, $c=5$, and $p_u=0.5$. Expected net urban income is $0.5\times120+0.5\times20-5=65$, so migration is attractive. If the formal wage rises to 140 while jobs stay fixed, additional migration lowers $p_u$. Equilibrium adjustment need not give each migrant 20 more income; it may lengthen the job queue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所谓“悖论”不是说提高工资在任何条件下都增加失业。它需要工资高于市场清算水平、正式岗位供给响应不足，且迁移对预期收入响应。若更高工资显著提高生产率或岗位数，结论可不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The “paradox” does not say that a wage increase raises unemployment under every condition. It requires a wage above market-clearing levels, an insufficient formal-job response, and migration that responds to expected income. If higher wages substantially raise productivity or the number of jobs, the conclusion can differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 非正规就业与循环迁移
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Informal work and circular migration*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

迁移者可能进入非正规就业而非公开失业；家庭分散风险、汇款和土地权利产生循环/季节迁移。二元标签应容纳多个城市和农村子部门。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Migrants may enter informal work rather than open unemployment. Household risk sharing, remittances, and land rights generate circular or seasonal migration. A dual-sector label should allow multiple urban and rural subsectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 政策含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Policy implications*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

教育、住房、户籍、基础设施、农业生产率和城市就业政策互相作用。只限制迁移可能保护城市指标却降低家庭机会；只补贴城市工资可能加剧迁移失衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Education, housing, registration rules, infrastructure, agricultural productivity, and urban employment policy interact. Restricting migration may improve urban indicators while reducing household opportunity; subsidizing urban wages alone can intensify migration imbalance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 直接比较城市已就业者工资和农村收入，忽略就业概率、非正规收入和迁移成本。
- 把提高正式工资对已就业者的好处，当成对所有潜在迁移者的同样效果。
- 把非正规就业等同于零收入失业，导致预期收入与政策评估失真。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Comparing wages of employed urban workers directly with rural income while ignoring job probability, informal income, and migration cost.
- Treating benefits to already employed formal workers as the same effect for every potential migrant.
- Equating informal employment with zero-income unemployment and distorting expected-income and policy calculations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Todaro 模型中迁移者比较的是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What do migrants compare in the Todaro model?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 城市收入乘就业概率等形成的预期收入，与农村收入及迁移成本比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> They compare expected urban income, formed from wages and employment probabilities, with rural income and migration cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 提高城市工资为何可能增加城市失业？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a higher urban wage increase urban unemployment?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 若岗位数量响应不足，更高工资提高预期城市收益，吸引的迁移可能超过新增就业。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> If jobs respond weakly, the higher wage raises expected urban returns and can attract more migrants than the number of new jobs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明非正规收入 $w_I$ 为什么改变迁移诊断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why informal income $w_I$ changes the migration diagnosis.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 求职期间若能有非正规收入，获得不到正式岗位不再等于零收入，因而预期城市收益、停留时间和政策效果都会变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> If job seekers can earn informal income, failure to obtain a formal job no longer means zero income. Expected urban returns, duration of stay, and policy effects all change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
