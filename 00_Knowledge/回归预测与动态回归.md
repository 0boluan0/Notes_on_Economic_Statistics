# 回归预测与动态回归
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Regression forecasting and dynamic regression*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当时间序列同时受价格、活动、政策或其他外部变量驱动，用回归表达这些效果，再用滞后与 ARIMA 误差处理动态。
> **具体锚点：** 电力需求受温度、工作日和自身惯性影响；温度效应可非线性，残差还可有季节自相关。
> **核心难点：** 未来 predictor 未知时，预测是对其假设值的条件结果；加入因变量滞后后，当期系数只是短期效应。
> **为什么重要：** 它将“什么在驱动结果”与“未解释部分怎样随时间依赖”分开，同时支持条件预测和干预分析。
> **继续：** 先明确 predictor 的未来信息与因果地位，再选分布滞后、因变量滞后或 ARIMA 误差；评估遵循 [[预测问题、基准方法与评估]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** When a time series is driven by prices, events, policy, or other external variables, regression represents those effects while lags and ARIMA errors represent dynamics.
> **Concrete anchor:** Electricity demand depends on temperature, working days, and its own persistence; temperature effects may be nonlinear and residuals may still have seasonal autocorrelation.
> **Central difficulty:** If future predictors are unknown, forecasts are conditional on assumed predictor paths. Once lagged outcomes enter, a contemporaneous coefficient is only a short-run effect.
> **Why it matters:** It separates external drivers from temporal dependence in the unexplained component and supports conditional forecasting and intervention analysis.
> **Continue with:** Clarify the future information and causal status of predictors before choosing distributed lags, lagged outcomes, or ARIMA errors. Evaluate using [[预测问题、基准方法与评估|forecasting workflow and evaluation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 静态回归与时间信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Static regression and time-indexed information*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用趋势、季节虚拟、活动、价格和其他 predictor 解释结果；误差若自相关，可用 regression with ARIMA errors。未来 predictor 未知时必须另预测或设置情景，结果区间应反映其不确定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use trend, seasonal indicators, events, prices, and other predictors to explain the outcome. If errors are autocorrelated, use regression with ARIMA errors. Unknown future predictors must be forecast separately or assigned scenario paths, and resulting uncertainty should be reflected in forecast intervals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最简单模型是 $y_t=\beta_0+\beta^Tx_t+n_t$，其中 $n_t$ 而不是 $y_t$ 本身被设为 ARIMA。这与“把 OLS 做完再给标准误打补丁”不同：自相关误差会改变系数的有效估计、似然、预测递推和区间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The basic model is $y_t=\beta_0+\beta^Tx_t+n_t$, where $n_t$, rather than $y_t$ itself, follows an ARIMA process. This is not the same as fitting OLS and patching standard errors afterward: autocorrelated errors affect efficient coefficient estimation, likelihood, forecast recursion, and intervals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 动态回归与干预
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Dynamic regression and intervention*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 分布滞后与动态乘数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distributed lags and dynamic multipliers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

动态回归可包含解释变量的当前/滞后项、因变量滞后和 ARMA 误差。加入因变量滞后会改变系数为短期效应，长期效应需汇总动态乘数。干预变量要区分脉冲、阶跃和渐进影响，并处理同期反向因果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dynamic regression can contain current and lagged predictors, lagged outcomes, and ARMA errors. Adding lagged outcomes makes coefficients short-run effects, while long-run effects require sums of dynamic multipliers. Intervention variables must distinguish pulse, step, and gradual effects and address contemporaneous reverse causality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 ADL(1,1)：
$$
y_t=\alpha+\rho y_{t-1}+\beta_0x_t+\beta_1x_{t-1}+u_t,
$$
$x$ 当期永久增加 1 的 impact effect 是 $\beta_0$，下一期额外效应为 $\rho\beta_0+\beta_1$，若 $|\rho|<1$，长期效应是 $(\beta_0+\beta_1)/(1-\rho)$。只报 $\beta_0$ 会把完整动态反应误读为当期回归系数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the ADL(1,1) model
$$
y_t=\alpha+\rho y_{t-1}+\beta_0x_t+\beta_1x_{t-1}+u_t,
$$
a permanent unit increase in $x$ has impact effect $\beta_0$, next-period additional effect $\rho\beta_0+\beta_1$, and, if $|\rho|<1$, long-run effect $(\beta_0+\beta_1)/(1-\rho)$. Reporting only $\beta_0$ mistakes the contemporaneous coefficient for the full dynamic response.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 因变量滞后与 ARIMA 误差的区分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Lagged outcomes versus ARIMA errors*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因变量滞后 $y_{t-1}$ 进入条件均值，表示过去结果在控制 $x$ 后仍直接传递；ARIMA 误差说未被 $x$ 解释的冲击有持续性。两种规范有时预测接近，但系数含义、初始条件与因果解释不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A lagged outcome $y_{t-1}$ enters the conditional mean and says that the previous outcome transmits directly after controlling for $x$. ARIMA errors say that unexplained innovations persist. The specifications can forecast similarly in some cases, but coefficient meaning, initial conditions, and causal interpretation differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同时放入大量 $y$ 滞后、$x$ 滞后和高阶 ARMA 误差很容出现近似重复动态。应从机制提出小型候选，检查根、残差和 rolling-origin 结果，而不是让所有动态项竞相“吸收”同一相关。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Including many lags of $y$, many lags of $x$, and high-order ARMA errors easily duplicates dynamics. Propose a small candidate set from the mechanism, then inspect roots, residuals, and rolling-origin performance rather than letting all terms compete to absorb the same dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 干预变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Intervention variables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

脉冲变量只在事件期为 1，表示一次性异常；阶跃变量在事件后永久为 1，表示水平换档；斜坡/渐进干预表示效果逐步建立。与 transfer function 或因变量滞后结合时，一次脉冲也可以引出多期衰减路径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pulse variable equals one only at the event date and represents a one-off anomaly. A step variable remains one after the event and represents a permanent level shift. A ramp or gradual intervention represents an effect that builds over time. Combined with a transfer function or lagged outcome, even a one-time pulse can generate a multi-period decay path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

干预系数不自动是因果效应。如果政策是因为预期未来 $y_t$ 会恶化才实施，干预时点与未建模的预期冲击相关；这是内生性问题，不是增加 ARIMA 阶数可修复的残差问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An intervention coefficient is not automatically causal. If policy is introduced because future deterioration in $y_t$ is anticipated, intervention timing correlates with an unmodelled expected shock. That is endogeneity, not a residual problem repairable by increasing ARIMA order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 未来 predictor 与条件预测
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Future predictors and conditional forecasts*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预测 $y_{T+h}$ 需要 $x_{T+1:T+h}$。日历变量与已排定活动可能已知；温度、收入、价格等需要外部预测或情景。常用软件给出的区间通常条件于输入的 $x$ 路径，并未自动加入 $x$ 本身的预测不确定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Forecasting $y_{T+h}$ requires $x_{T+1:T+h}$. Calendar variables and scheduled events may be known, whereas temperature, income, and prices need external forecasts or scenarios. Standard software intervals are often conditional on the supplied $x$ path and do not automatically include uncertainty in forecasts of $x$ itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此应区分三种输出：已知 predictor 下的条件预测；给定“低/中/高”路径的情景预测；对 predictor 与 $y$ 的联合不确定性做模拟的无条件预测。三者的区间不能混为同一含义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish three outputs: a conditional forecast under known predictors; scenario forecasts under low, central, and high paths; and an unconditional forecast that simulates joint uncertainty in predictors and the outcome. Their intervals do not have the same meaning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：温度与电力需求
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: temperature and electricity demand*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可设 $y_t$ 为日用电量，用温度 $T_t$、$T_t^2$、工作日虚拟与日历项建回归，再对误差拟合季节 ARIMA。$T_t$ 和 $T_t^2$ 共同表示冷/热两端需求较高的 U 形。未来 14 天的预测必须输入已知日历与天气预报；如果固定温度为 26°C，输出应明示是“在26°C 情景下”的条件预测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $y_t$ be daily electricity demand and regress it on temperature $T_t$, $T_t^2$, a working-day indicator, and calendar terms, with seasonal ARIMA errors. Together, $T_t$ and $T_t^2$ represent a U-shaped relationship in which very cold and very hot days have higher demand. A 14-day forecast requires known calendar values and weather forecasts. Fixing temperature at 26°C produces a conditional forecast explicitly labelled "under the 26°C scenario."
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 诊断与边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnostics and boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检查回归函数形式、变量的可用时点、动态根、innovations 的自相关/条件异方差、结构突变与高杠杆时点。预测评估中，predictor 路径也必须按历史可用信息生成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check regression functional form, predictor availability timing, dynamic roots, autocorrelation and conditional heteroskedasticity of innovations, structural breaks, and high-leverage dates. During forecast evaluation, predictor paths must also be generated from historically available information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若目标是因果，还需要额外识别条件：严格外生性、自然实验、工具变量或可辩护的干预设计。高外样本预测能力不会单独证明因果，加入因变量滞后也不会自动控制所有混杂。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Causal goals require additional identification conditions such as strict exogeneity, a natural experiment, an instrument, or a defensible intervention design. Strong out-of-sample prediction alone does not establish causality, and lagged outcomes do not automatically control all confounding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 回归预测中未来解释变量未知怎么办？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What should be done when future predictors are unknown in regression forecasting?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 为它们建立预测或给条件情景，并把其不确定性纳入结果；不能偷用未来实际值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Forecast them or provide conditional scenarios and include their uncertainty where relevant; never use future realised values covertly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### ADL(1,1) 中为什么 $\beta_0$ 不是长期效应？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is $\beta_0$ not the long-run effect in an ADL(1,1)?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> $\beta_0$ 只是当期 impact；$x$ 的滞后项与 $y$ 的自回归会在后续期间继续传播，稳定时长期效应为 $(\beta_0+\beta_1)/(1-\rho)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> $\beta_0$ is only the impact effect. Lagged $x$ and autoregressive propagation through $y$ continue later; under stability the long-run effect is $(\beta_0+\beta_1)/(1-\rho)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 阶跃干预与脉冲干预回答的问题有何不同？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How do step and pulse interventions answer different questions?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 阶跃检查事件后水平是否永久换档；脉冲检查事件期是否有一次性异常，虽然动态传播可使脉冲影响持续多期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A step tests for a permanent level shift after an event; a pulse tests for a one-time anomaly at the event, though dynamics can propagate that pulse over several periods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话区分因变量滞后与 ARIMA 误差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distinguish lagged outcomes from ARIMA errors in your own words.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 因变量滞后说结果本身的过去值进入条件均值；ARIMA 误差说回归未解释的冲击持续。前者直接改变系数的动态效应解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A lagged outcome puts the past outcome itself in the conditional mean; ARIMA errors make unexplained innovations persistent. The former directly changes the dynamic interpretation of regression coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
