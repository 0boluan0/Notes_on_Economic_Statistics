# 行列式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Determinants*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 用一个标量记录方阵对有向体积的缩放，并判断线性变换是否压扁某个维度。
> **具体锚点：** $\det A=0$ 意味着单位方形被压成了零面积的线段或点，所以不可能从输出唯一恢复输入。
> **核心难点：** 行列式不是“矩阵里所有数的某种和”，而是一个交替多线性体积因子；行操作对它的影响必须逐类跟踪。
> **为什么重要：** 可逆性、Jacobian 变量变换、特征方程和线性方程的唯一性都使用它。
> **继续：** 用消元理解计算，用体积缩放理解结构；特征方程见 [[特征值、对角化与线性动力系统]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** A determinant records the signed volume-scaling factor of a square linear map and detects whether a dimension is collapsed.
> **Concrete anchor:** If $\det A=0$, the unit square or cube is flattened into a lower-dimensional object, so the input cannot be recovered uniquely from the output.
> **Central difficulty:** A determinant is an alternating multilinear volume factor, not an arbitrary aggregate of entries. Each type of row operation changes it differently.
> **Why it matters:** Invertibility, Jacobian changes of variables, characteristic equations, and uniqueness of square linear systems use determinants.
> **Continue with:** Interpret computation through elimination and structure through volume; see [[特征值、对角化与线性动力系统|eigenvalues and linear dynamics]] for characteristic equations.

## 行列式的结构含义
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The structural meaning of a determinant*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\det A$ 是有向体积缩放因子；为零意味着某个维度被压扁，矩阵不可逆。行交换变号、某行缩放使行列式同倍缩放、给一行加另一行倍数不变。计算应优先利用消元和结构，而非大规模余子式展开。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\det A$ is the signed volume-scaling factor. A zero determinant means that some dimension is flattened and the matrix is not invertible. Swapping rows changes the sign, scaling one row scales the determinant by the same factor, and adding a multiple of one row to another leaves it unchanged. Computation should exploit elimination and structure rather than large cofactor expansions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 乘法性与可逆性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Multiplicativity and invertibility*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\det(AB)=\det A\det B$ 说明复合变换的体积因子相乘。因此可逆 A 满足 $\det(A^{-1})=1/\det A$，而 $\det A=0$ 与列向量线性相关、存在非零零空间向量、$Ax=b$ 不对每个 b 有唯一解等价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\det(AB)=\det A\det B$ says that volume factors multiply under composition. Hence an invertible $A$ has $\det(A^{-1})=1/\det A$. The condition $\det A=0$ is equivalent to dependent columns, a nontrivial null space, and failure of $Ax=b$ to have a unique solution for every $b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：通过消元计算并解释
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: computation and interpretation through elimination*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对
$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&0\\2&5&1\\0&1&3\end{pmatrix},
$$
用“加一行的倍数”消元：$R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$ 得第二行 $(0,1,1)$，再 $R_3\leftarrow R_3-R_2$ 得 $(0,0,2)$。没有换行或行缩放，所以 $\det A=1\cdot1\cdot2=2$。非零结果同时说明三列张成 $\mathbb R^3$，平行六面体的有向体积是 2，且方程对任意 b 有唯一解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For
$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&0\\2&5&1\\0&1&3\end{pmatrix},
$$
use determinant-preserving row additions: $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$ gives $(0,1,1)$, then $R_3\leftarrow R_3-R_2$ gives $(0,0,2)$. No rows were swapped or scaled, so $\det A=1\cdot1\cdot2=2$. The nonzero value also says that the columns span $\mathbb R^3$, their parallelepiped has signed volume two, and $Ax=b$ has a unique solution for every $b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 诊断与错误边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnostics and failure modes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行列式为负只表示取向翻转，不是“负体积”；绝对值才是体积缩放。行列式很小只是一个可能的病态信号，并不能单独量化矩阵的 2-范数条件数；数值敏感性应看奇异值比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A negative determinant indicates reversed orientation, not “negative volume”; its absolute value is the volume factor. A small determinant may signal trouble but does not by itself quantify the 2-norm condition number. Numerical sensitivity is governed by singular-value ratios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 行列式为 0 在几何和方程上各意味着什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does a zero determinant mean geometrically and for linear equations?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 几何上至少一个维度被压扁；代数上列向量相关、矩阵不可逆，$Ax=b$ 不可能对每个 b 都有唯一解。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Geometrically, at least one dimension is flattened. Algebraically, columns are dependent, the matrix is singular, and $Ax=b$ cannot have a unique solution for every $b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 哪种行操作不改变行列式？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Which row operation leaves a determinant unchanged?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 给某一行加上另一行的倍数；换行会变号，缩放一行会按同一倍数缩放行列式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Adding a multiple of one row to another. Swapping rows changes the sign, and scaling one row scales the determinant by the same factor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 $\det(AB)=\det A\det B$ 符合几何直觉？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is $\det(AB)=\det A\det B$ geometrically natural?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> B 先按 $\det B$ 缩放并可能翻转取向，A 再对结果按 $\det A$ 缩放；复合的总因子应相乘。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> $B$ first scales signed volume by $\det B$, then $A$ scales the result by $\det A$; the factors multiply under composition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明为什么行列式很小不等于已经知道条件数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain why a small determinant does not determine the condition number.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 行列式是全部奇异值的乘积，它会随整体尺度改变；条件数看最大与最小奇异值的比率，是对相对压扁方向的度量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The determinant is the product of all singular values and changes with overall scale. The condition number is the ratio of largest to smallest singular value and measures relative directional flattening.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
