# Determinant
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*行列式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **determinant** assigns one scalar to a square matrix: a matrix with the same number of rows and columns. It condenses information about the matrix's action into a number, written $\det A$ or $|A|$. For real matrices, it describes signed volume scaling; a zero value signals that some direction has been collapsed. These are useful interpretations of the number, not a way to recover every matrix entry from it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**行列式**把一个方阵，也就是行数与列数相同的矩阵，对应到一个标量，记作 $\det A$ 或 $|A|$。它用一个数概括矩阵作用的一些信息：对实矩阵，它描述有向体积的缩放；值为零表示某个方向被压扁。这些是这个数的用途，并不意味着能靠一个行列式值恢复所有矩阵元素。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Start with two dimensions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 二阶情形

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a $2\times2$ matrix, multiply the two diagonal entries and subtract the product of the other two:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $2\times2$ 矩阵，先乘主对角线上的两个数，再减去另外两个数的乘积：

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det A=ad-bc.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$ has determinant $2\cdot3-1\cdot0=6$. Its action sends $(x,y)$ to $(2x+y,3y)$, so the unit square becomes a parallelogram with base $2$ and height $3$. Its area is $6$. By contrast, $B=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}$ has determinant $4-4=0$: every output is of the form $(t,2t)$, a line rather than a two-dimensional region. The precise general statements are [[行列式体积与取向|volume and orientation]] and [[可逆性与非零行列式|the determinant criterion for invertibility]]. Here the bars in $|A|$ mean a determinant, not entrywise absolute values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&3\end{pmatrix}$ 的行列式为 $2\cdot3-1\cdot0=6$。它把 $(x,y)$ 送到 $(2x+y,3y)$，所以单位正方形变成底为 $2$、高为 $3$ 的平行四边形，面积是 $6$。相比之下，$B=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&4\end{pmatrix}$ 的行列式为 $4-4=0$：每个输出都是 $(t,2t)$，只落在一条直线上，不再填出二维区域。一般结论分别见[[行列式体积与取向]]和[[可逆性与非零行列式]]。这里 $|A|$ 的竖线表示行列式，不是对每个元素取绝对值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Three conditions specify the number
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*三个条件确定这个数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n\ge1$ be an integer and $A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$. The entries and coefficients belong to the same field $\mathbb F$, usually $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$; the definition below also works over any field. A candidate scalar function $D$ must satisfy three conditions. Write $D(r_1,\ldots,r_n)$ when its matrix has rows $r_1,\ldots,r_n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $n\ge1$ 为整数，$A\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}$。矩阵元素与系数取自同一个数域 $\mathbb F$，通常是 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$；下面的定义也适用于任意域。候选标量函数 $D$ 必须满足三个条件。若矩阵的各行为 $r_1,\ldots,r_n$，就把函数值写成 $D(r_1,\ldots,r_n)$。

> [!important] Normalized, multilinear and alternating · 归一化、多线性与交替
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Normalization:** $D(I_n)=1$, where the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I_n$ has diagonal entries $1$ and all other entries $0$.
>
> **Separate row linearity:** hold every row except row $k$ fixed. Replacing that row by $\alpha u+\beta v$, for row vectors $u,v$ and scalars $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F$, gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **归一化：**$D(I_n)=1$；[[单位矩阵]] $I_n$ 的对角元为 $1$，其余元素为 $0$。
>
> **逐行线性：**固定第 $k$ 行以外的所有行。对行向量 $u,v$ 和标量 $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F$，把这一行换成 $\alpha u+\beta v$，得到
>
> $$D(\ldots,\alpha u+\beta v,\ldots)
> =\alpha D(\ldots,u,\ldots)+\beta D(\ldots,v,\ldots).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Alternation:** if any two rows are equal, then $D=0$. The unique function satisfying these conditions is the **determinant**, $D(A)=\det A$. “Multilinear” means separately linear in every row; it does not mean linear in the entire matrix at once.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **交替性：**只要任意两行相同，就有 $D=0$。满足这些条件的唯一函数就是**行列式**，即 $D(A)=\det A$。“多线性”表示对每一行分别线性，不是对整张矩阵同时线性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The normalization fixes the scale. Alternation makes repeated rows contribute zero. Separate linearity then determines what happens to every combination of rows. For $n=1$, there are no two distinct row positions to compare; linearity and $D([1])=1$ simply give $\det[a]=a$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

归一化固定尺度，交替性让重复行的贡献归零，逐行线性再决定任意行组合的结果。$n=1$ 时，没有两个不同的行位置可比较；线性性和 $D([1])=1$ 直接给出 $\det[a]=a$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why swapping rows negates the value
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么交换两整行会取负*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix all other rows, and put $u+v$ in each of two selected row positions. The rows are equal, so the result is zero. Expanding in those two rows gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定其他行，在两个选定的行位置都放入 $u+v$。两行相同，因此结果为零；再对这两行分别展开，得到

$$0=D(\ldots,u,\ldots,u,\ldots)+D(\ldots,u,\ldots,v,\ldots)
+D(\ldots,v,\ldots,u,\ldots)+D(\ldots,v,\ldots,v,\ldots).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first and last terms vanish by alternation. Thus exchanging the two whole rows negates the value. This derivation uses no division by $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一项与最后一项由交替性归零，因此交换两整行会把函数值取负。这个推导没有除以 $2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the $2\times2$ formula, let $e_1=(1,0)$ and $e_2=(0,1)$ be the two standard coordinate rows. The rows of $A$ are $ae_1+be_2$ and $ce_1+de_2$. Expanding both gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对二阶公式，令 $e_1=(1,0)$、$e_2=(0,1)$ 为两条标准坐标行。$A$ 的两行为 $ae_1+be_2$、$ce_1+de_2$；分别展开得

$$\begin{aligned}
D(A)&=acD(e_1,e_1)+adD(e_1,e_2)+bcD(e_2,e_1)+bdD(e_2,e_2)\\
&=0+ad-bc+0.
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $D(e_1,e_2)=D(I_2)=1$, and the swapped order has value $-1$. This explains the formula used in the opening examples.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其中 $D(e_1,e_2)=D(I_2)=1$，交换后的次序取值为 $-1$。这就解释了开头例子所使用的公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, expand every row in standard coordinate rows. Terms with repeated coordinate rows vanish. The remaining terms arrange all $n$ coordinate rows in some order; successive row swaps and normalization determine each value. Thus there is **at most one** candidate $D$. The [[Leibniz 行列式公式|Leibniz formula]] supplies the explicit sum and verifies that it really has all three properties, proving **existence** as well. The definition specifies the properties; the formula constructs and verifies the object rather than assuming it already exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，把每一行都按标准坐标行展开。含重复坐标行的项归零，剩下的项把全部 $n$ 条坐标行按某种顺序排列；逐次换行和归一化决定每项取值。因此候选函数 $D$ **至多只有一个**。[[Leibniz 行列式公式]]给出显式求和，并验证它确实满足全部三条性质，从而同时证明**存在性**。定义说明对象应具备的性质，公式负责构造与核验，并没有先假定对象存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the scope of each statement
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separate row linearity does not imply $\det(A+B)=\det A+\det B$. Over $\mathbb R$, for example, $\det(I_2+I_2)=\det(2I_2)=4$, while $\det I_2+\det I_2=2$. Adding whole matrices changes several rows simultaneously; expanding them produces cross terms. Similarly, exchanging two entries **within one row** is not exchanging two whole rows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逐行线性不能推出 $\det(A+B)=\det A+\det B$。例如，在实数域上，$\det(I_2+I_2)=\det(2I_2)=4$，而 $\det I_2+\det I_2=2$。把整张矩阵相加会同时改变多行，逐行展开后还会出现交叉项。同样，只交换**某一行内部**的两个元素，也不是交换两整行。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ordinary determinant here is for square matrices. Rectangular maps use other constructions, such as the [[矩形Jacobian体积因子|Gram-based volume factor]] or [[奇异值|singular values]]. A complex determinant is a complex scalar, not necessarily a positive or negative real number; for example, $\det\begin{pmatrix}i&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}=i$, where $i^2=-1$. Ordinary algebraic multiplication is used, with no added conjugation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的普通行列式用于方阵；矩形映射的体积信息使用其他构造，例如[[矩形Jacobian体积因子|基于 Gram 矩阵的体积因子]]或[[奇异值]]。复矩阵的行列式是复标量，不一定是可比较正负的实数；例如 $\det\begin{pmatrix}i&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}=i$，其中 $i^2=-1$。计算使用普通代数乘法，不额外取共轭。

> [!warning] Repeated rows must vanish, even in characteristic two · 特征二仍须要求重复行为零
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A field has characteristic two when $1+1=0$, so $-z=z$ for every scalar $z$. In that setting, the swap equation $D(\text{swapped rows})=-D(\text{original rows})$ alone does not force repeated rows to have value zero. For instance, over arithmetic modulo $2$, the separately linear function $F\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}=ad+bc+ac$ is normalized at $I_2$ and unchanged by swapping rows, but $F\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}=1$. Alternation must therefore be stated as repeated-row vanishing. Outside characteristic two, the swap equation does imply vanishing because $D=-D$ gives $2D=0$ and division by $2$ is allowed. The determinant formula $ad-bc$ works in either case; in characteristic two, subtraction equals addition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 数域的特征为二，意思是 $1+1=0$，从而每个标量都满足 $-z=z$。此时，只要求 $D(\text{换行后})=-D(\text{换行前})$，无法迫使重复行取值为零。例如，在模 $2$ 算术中，分别对两行线性的函数 $F\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}=ad+bc+ac$ 在 $I_2$ 处归一化且换行后不变，但 $F\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}=1$。因此交替性必须明确要求重复行归零。特征不为二时，换行取负可推出这一点，因为 $D=-D$ 给出 $2D=0$，而此时允许除以 $2$。行列式公式 $ad-bc$ 在两种情形都成立，只是在特征二时减法等同加法。
