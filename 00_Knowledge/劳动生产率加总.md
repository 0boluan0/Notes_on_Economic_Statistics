# 同口径总劳动生产率等于部门劳动生产率按就业份额加权的和

当部门增加值和就业都能按同一范围加总时，总体每就业者的[[劳动生产率]]，恰好等于各部门生产率按就业份额加权的平均值。这是比值的加总恒等式，不是假设所有部门生产率相同，也不需要把每个部门当成同样大小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When sectoral value added and employment aggregate over the same population of activities, overall [[劳动生产率|labour productivity]] per employed person equals the employment-share-weighted average of sectoral productivity. This is an aggregation identity, not an assumption of equal productivity or equally sized sectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设部门互斥并覆盖研究总体，$Y=\sum_iY_i$，$L=\sum_iL_i$，且各 $L_i>0$。定义 $p_i=Y_i/L_i$、$s_i=L_i/L$，有
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let mutually exclusive sectors cover the study aggregate, with $Y=\sum_iY_i$, $L=\sum_iL_i$ and each $L_i>0$. Define $p_i=Y_i/L_i$ and $s_i=L_i/L$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P=\frac{Y}{L}
=\sum_i\frac{L_i}{L}\frac{Y_i}{L_i}
=\sum_i s_i p_i,\qquad \sum_i s_i=1.
$$

两部门共有 100 名就业者，部门 A 有 60 人且 $p_A=2$，部门 B 有 40 人且 $p_B=8$。于是 $Y_A=120$、$Y_B=320$，$P=440/100=0.6\times2+0.4\times8=4.4$；直接取 $(2+8)/2=5$，则错误地给两部门相同权重。[[产出与就业份额]]进一步解释为什么不能把产出份额当作这里的权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With 100 employed people, sector A employs 60 at $p_A=2$, and B employs 40 at $p_B=8$. Their value added is 120 and 320, so $P=440/100=0.6\times2+0.4\times8=4.4$. The unweighted average $(2+8)/2=5$ incorrectly treats the sectors as equally large. [[产出与就业份额|Output and employment shares]] explains why output shares are not the weights here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

加总前要检查价格、时期、部门分类和覆盖范围。此处 $Y$ 是这些部门增加值之和，不能未经核对就替换成另一发布总量。跨期实际分析应使用一致且可加的价格口径；已链式连接的部门量值通常不具有可加性，不能因为都标着“实际”就强行相加。需要回到适用的现价／上年价分项或官方贡献分解方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check prices, periods, sector definitions and coverage before aggregating. Here $Y$ is the sum of the included sectors' value added, not automatically another published aggregate. Real comparisons over time require a consistent, additive price basis. Chain-linked sectoral volumes are generally nonadditive; their “real” label does not justify summing them. Use the applicable current-price/previous-year-price components or official contribution methodology.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若改用每小时增加值，令总工时为 $H=\sum_iH_i$，权重也必须改为 $H_i/H$，而不是继续使用就业人数份额。零劳动投入部门或部门重复覆盖须先另作一致处理，不能靠 $0/0$ 或重复计数维持恒等式。两期变化在这些口径处理后再接[[劳动生产率分解]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For value added per hour, let $H=\sum_iH_i$ and use hours shares $H_i/H$, not employment shares. Zero-labour sectors and overlapping coverage need consistent treatment rather than $0/0$ or double-counting. Once these conventions are aligned, proceed to the [[劳动生产率分解|two-period decomposition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
