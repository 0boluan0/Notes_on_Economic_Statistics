# 估值终值是显性预测期末对其后全部现金流所作的当时价值估计
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Terminal value estimates, at the end of the explicit forecast period, the value of all subsequent cash flows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估值终值把逐年预测结束之后的现金流收束成一个数。例如逐年列出第 1 至第 5 年的 FCFF 后，$TV_5$ 表示站在第 5 年末看第 6 年及以后现金流的价值。它不是第 5 年的额外营业收入，也不是已经折回今天的金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Terminal value summarises cash flows beyond the annual forecast. After explicitly projecting years 1–5, $TV_5$ values cash flows from year 6 onward as of the end of year 5. It is neither extra operating revenue in year 5 nor a present value at time zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若从第 $n+1$ 年开始现金流以 $g$ 永续增长，匹配的稳定期资本成本为 $k_s>g$，则由[[增长永续年金]]：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If cash flow from year $n+1$ onward grows perpetually at $g$, with a matching stable-period required return $k_s>g$, the [[增长永续年金|growing-perpetuity relation]] gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$TV_n=\frac{CF_{n+1}}{k_s-g}.$$

若显性期每年匹配折现率均为 $k_h$，这部分今天的价值是 $TV_n/(1+k_h)^n$。例如 $CF_6=60$、$k_s=8\%$、$g=3\%$，$TV_5=1200$；若前 5 年适用 10%，现值为 $1200/(1.10)^5\approx745.11$。稳定期的 8% 不能代替前 5 年的 10%。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a constant explicit-period rate $k_h$, the time-zero contribution is $TV_n/(1+k_h)^n$. If $CF_6=60$, $k_s=8\%$, and $g=3\%$, then $TV_5=1200$. Discounting the first five years at 10% gives approximately 745.11. The stable-period 8% does not replace the earlier 10%.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

清算或出售也能给出预测期末价值，但现金归属、税费与估值依据要另说明。如果用可比公司的市场倍数估计退出价，终值就含有相对定价假设；它不能被当作完全由自身现金流推导出的结果。无论采用哪种方法，都要避免把显性期最后一笔现金同时放进逐年预测和终值分子。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Liquidation or sale can also close a forecast, with explicit claim, tax, and valuation assumptions. An exit price based on market comparables introduces relative-pricing assumptions. Under any method, avoid counting the final explicit-period cash flow both separately and in the terminal numerator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
