# Infinite series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*无穷级数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **infinite series** asks what happens when we keep adding the entries of an ordered list. We never finish infinitely many additions in one step. Instead, we add the first term, then the first two, then the first three, and examine the resulting running totals. A finite limit of those totals is called the sum of the series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**无穷级数**研究的是：沿着一个有顺序的列表不断相加，累计结果会怎样。我们并不是在某一步做完无穷次加法，而是先加第一项，再加前两项、前三项，考察这些累计总量。若它们趋于一个有限数，这个数就叫作级数的和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, add $1/2$, then $1/4$, then $1/8$, each new contribution being half the previous one. Keep the new contribution separate from the amount accumulated so far:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，先加 $1/2$，再加 $1/4$、$1/8$，每次新增量都是上一次的一半。注意区分这一次加入的数与目前已经累积的数：

| Number of terms / 已加项数 $N$ | New term / 新增项 $a_N$ | Running total / 累计值 $S_N$ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $1$ | $1/2$ | $1/2$ |
| $2$ | $1/4$ | $3/4$ |
| $3$ | $1/8$ | $7/8$ |
| $4$ | $1/16$ | $15/16$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The new terms approach zero, while the running totals approach one. These are two different sequences and two different limits. The second one is what defines the infinite sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

新增项趋于零，累计值却趋于一。这是两个不同的数列、两个不同的极限；定义无穷和的是后者。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From finite totals to the definition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从有限部分和到定义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work with real numbers $a_1,a_2,\ldots$, each of which is defined and finite. The [[序列收敛|sequence]] $(a_n)_{n\ge1}$ is this ordered list; $n$ labels an individual position. Its **$N$th partial sum** adds only the first $N$ terms, where $N$ is a positive integer:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里讨论实数 $a_1,a_2,\ldots$，每一项都须有定义且有限。[[序列收敛|数列]] $(a_n)_{n\ge1}$ 就是这个有序列表，$n$ 标记单项的位置。**第 $N$ 个部分和**只加前 $N$ 项，其中 $N$ 是正整数：

$$S_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}a_n=a_1+a_2+\cdots+a_N.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The summation symbol $\sum$ instructs us to add the indicated terms. With a finite upper index $N$, this is ordinary finite arithmetic. The notation $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ names the infinite series; the symbol $\infty$ says there is no final index, not that an extra term $a_\infty$ is added.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

求和符号 $\sum$ 表示把指定的各项相加。上标为有限的 $N$ 时，这就是普通有限次加法。$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ 表示无穷级数；$\infty$ 表示索引没有最后一项，并不是额外加一个 $a_\infty$。

> [!important] Convergence concerns the partial sums · 收敛检查部分和
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The series **converges** if there is a finite real number $S$ such that $S_N\to S$ as $N\to\infty$. We then write
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若存在有限实数 $S$，使 $N\to\infty$ 时 $S_N\to S$，就称级数**收敛**，并记为
>
> $$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n=S.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the partial sums have no finite real limit, the series **diverges**. The infinite-series notation may still name that divergent object, but does not supply a finite sum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若部分和没有有限实数极限，就称级数**发散**。仍然可以用无穷级数符号指称这个发散对象，但它没有因此获得有限的和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The meaning of [[序列收敛|$S_N\to S$]] is precise: for every requested error $\varepsilon>0$, there is an integer cutoff $N_0$ such that $|S_N-S|<\varepsilon$ for **every** $N\ge N_0$. Here $\varepsilon$ is the allowed error and $N_0$ is the point after which all totals must meet it. One close total, or even many close totals, is not enough.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[序列收敛|$S_N\to S$]] 的含义是：任给误差要求 $\varepsilon>0$，都能找到整数门槛 $N_0$，使**每个** $N\ge N_0$ 都有 $|S_N-S|<\varepsilon$。其中 $\varepsilon$ 是允许误差，$N_0$ 是之后所有累计值都必须达到要求的起点。只出现一次、甚至多次接近，并不够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check the example at every finite stage
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先核对每个有限阶段*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening example $a_n=2^{-n}$, finite subtraction gives the formula. Multiplying $S_N=1/2+1/4+\cdots+2^{-N}$ by $2$ shifts the same finite list; subtracting $S_N$ cancels the middle terms:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头的例子是 $a_n=2^{-n}$。它的公式可以先由有限次相减得到：把 $S_N=1/2+1/4+\cdots+2^{-N}$ 乘以 $2$，相同的有限列表错开一项；再减去 $S_N$，中间项消掉：

$$2S_N-S_N=1-2^{-N},\qquad S_N=1-2^{-N}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $|1-S_N|=2^{-N}\to0$, proving $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}2^{-n}=1$. For an error below $0.01$, every $N\ge7$ works because $2^{-7}=1/128<0.01$. No finite partial sum equals $1$; reaching the limit at a finite stage is not required. The broader family is treated in [[几何级数|geometric series]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 $|1-S_N|=2^{-N}\to0$，证明 $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}2^{-n}=1$。若要求误差小于 $0.01$，每个 $N\ge7$ 都可行，因为 $2^{-7}=1/128<0.01$。没有任何有限部分和等于 $1$，但收敛并不要求有限步抵达极限；更一般的同类结构见[[几何级数]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Always read the starting index. The series $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}2^{-n}$ includes the additional first term $2^0=1$, so its sum is $2$, not $1$. Its partial sum through index $N$ contains $N+1$ terms. Relabelling a list consistently leaves it unchanged; silently adding or removing its first entry does not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

起始索引也要看清。$\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}2^{-n}$ 多了首项 $2^0=1$，所以和是 $2$，不是 $1$；截至索引 $N$ 的部分和含有 $N+1$ 项。一致地重标索引不改变列表，悄悄增删首项却会改变它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Divergence has more than one form
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*发散不只有越来越大一种形式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $1+1+1+\cdots$, the partial sums are $S_N=N$, which tend to $+\infty$. One may write $\sum1=+\infty$ to record this extended-limit behaviour; it is still divergence under the finite-sum definition. For $-1-1-1-\cdots$, the totals tend to $-\infty$ instead.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于 $1+1+1+\cdots$，部分和为 $S_N=N$，趋于 $+\infty$。可以用 $\sum1=+\infty$ 记录这种广义极限行为，但按有限和的定义，它仍然发散。对于 $-1-1-1-\cdots$，累计值则趋于 $-\infty$。

> [!warning] Bounded totals can still diverge · 部分和有界也可能发散
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $1-1+1-1+\cdots$, odd-indexed partial sums equal $1$ and even-indexed ones equal $0$. They remain bounded but have no common limit. Looking only at the even partial sums would miss this failure. Pairing the terms as $(1-1)+(1-1)+\cdots$ selects those even cutoffs; it does not prove convergence of the original full sequence of partial sums.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $1-1+1-1+\cdots$，奇数索引的部分和为 $1$，偶数索引的部分和为 $0$。它们保持有界，却没有共同极限。只看偶数部分和，会漏掉这个失败；把项配成 $(1-1)+(1-1)+\cdots$，只是选择了那些偶数截断点，并没有证明原来的全部部分和收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Individual terms becoming small is also insufficient: the [[级数通项判别|nth-term test]] explains why $a_n\to0$ is necessary for convergence but cannot establish it. A [[级数判别法选择|convergence test]] addresses whether a finite total exists; obtaining its numerical value may require a separate formula or an error estimate. An [[反常积分|improper integral]] likewise starts with finite truncations and then takes a limit, but truncates an interval rather than a count of terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单项变小同样不够：[[级数通项判别]]解释了为什么 $a_n\to0$ 是收敛的必要条件，却不能单凭它证明收敛。[[级数判别法选择|收敛判别]]回答有限总和是否存在；求出具体数值，还可能需要另一个公式或误差估计。[[反常积分]]也先作有限截断再取极限，但截断的是区间，而不是项数。
