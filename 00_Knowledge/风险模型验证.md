# 风险模型验证评估模型在既定用途下的可靠性与限制，并把概念、数据与实施、结果分析和持续监控接入治理

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Risk model validation assesses reliability and limitations for an intended use, connecting conceptual, data and implementation review, outcomes analysis, and ongoing monitoring to governance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 验证回答“这个模型在这个用途下是否可靠到可以受控使用”
> 风险模型验证不是寻找一张永久正确证书，而是依据模型的目的、方法、数据、实现和表现，识别其可靠范围、限制与错误，并判断是否需要修复、重校准、限制用途或重新开发。验证活动的强度应随模型用途、重要性、复杂度、变化频率和数据限制调整。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Risk model validation does not seek a permanent certificate of correctness. It uses the model's purpose, methods, data, implementation, and performance to identify its reliable scope, limitations, and errors, and to determine whether repair, recalibration, use restrictions, or redevelopment are warranted. The rigour of validation should reflect use, materiality, complexity, change frequency, and data constraints.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 六层检查框架

1. **既定用途与概念健全性。** 先固定模型要支持的决策，再审查理论结构、变量选择、关键假设、判断性调整、替代方法与已知限制。统计拟合良好不等于可用于设计之外的问题。
2. **数据。** 检查来源、定义、覆盖、代表性、质量、缺失和异常处理，以及开发、校准与验证样本是否匹配用途。
3. **实施。** 核对代码、公式、参数、配置、数据变换、头寸映射和接口是否忠实实现获批方法，并检查访问、版本和变更控制。
4. **结果分析。** 用样本外或跨期测试、benchmark、敏感度、极端输入和 backtesting 比较模型输出与适当的现实结果。
5. **持续监控。** 产品、暴露、客户、数据相关性、市场机制或模型改变后，持续检查性能与限制，并按重要性触发行动。
6. **治理与行动。** 明确开发、使用、验证、审批和审计职责，形成有专业能力与客观性的有效质疑，并记录限制、例外、整改和使用条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. **Intended use and conceptual soundness.** Define the decision the model supports, then review theory, variable choices, assumptions, judgmental adjustments, alternatives, and known limitations. Good statistical fit does not justify use outside the design objective.
2. **Data.** Review sources, definitions, coverage, representativeness, quality, missingness, outlier treatment, and whether development, calibration, and validation samples fit the intended use.
3. **Implementation.** Verify that code, formulas, parameters, configuration, transformations, position mapping, and interfaces faithfully implement the approved method, with suitable access, version, and change controls.
4. **Outcomes analysis.** Compare outputs with appropriate real-world outcomes using out-of-sample or out-of-time tests, benchmarks, sensitivities, extreme inputs, and backtesting.
5. **Ongoing monitoring.** Reassess performance and limitations as products, exposures, clients, data relevance, market mechanisms, or the model change, and trigger proportionate action.
6. **Governance and action.** Assign development, use, validation, approval, and audit responsibilities, establish expert and objective effective challenge, and record limitations, exceptions, remediation, and use conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这六层是跨来源的学习框架，不声称每个法域都把它们逐字列为同一种法律义务。美国 2026 年联合指引把概念健全性、结果分析与持续监控列为模型验证组成，并把职责、控制和文档放在更广的模型风险治理中；Basel MAR30 则对获准使用市场风险内部模型法的银行规定更具体的独立验证、数据、头寸、估值、风险变换、回测和持续复核要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The six layers are a cross-source learning framework, not a claim that every jurisdiction states the same legal checklist verbatim. The 2026 US interagency guidance identifies conceptual soundness, outcomes analysis, and ongoing monitoring as validation components, while placing responsibilities, controls, and documentation in the wider governance of model risk. Basel MAR30 imposes more specific independent-validation, data, position, valuation, risk-transformation, backtesting, and ongoing-review requirements on banks approved to use the market-risk internal models approach.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 最小自检
> 一个 VaR 模型的例外率与名义概率一致，是否已经完成风险模型验证？
>
> **答案：** 没有。该结果只覆盖结果分析的一部分；仍需核对模型用途和假设、数据与头寸映射、生产实现、其他性能证据、持续监控和治理行动。具体边界见 [[风险模型验证边界]]。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** If a VaR model's exception rate matches its nominal probability, is risk model validation complete?
>
> **Answer:** No. That result covers only part of outcomes analysis. Intended use and assumptions, data and position mapping, production implementation, other performance evidence, ongoing monitoring, and governance actions still require review. See [[风险模型验证边界|the model-validation boundary]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- “按风险调整验证强度”不等于只保留最方便的一项测试；高重要性或复杂用途需要更完整证据。
- 独立验证、开发者测试、模型使用者反馈和内部审计承担不同职责，不能互相替代。
- 验证结论必须绑定具体模型版本、数据、用途和日期；重大变化后应重新评估。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Risk-proportionate validation does not mean retaining only the most convenient test; material or complex uses require broader evidence.
- Independent validation, developer testing, model-user feedback, and internal audit have different responsibilities and are not substitutes.
- A validation conclusion belongs to a specific model version, data set, use, and date; material change calls for reassessment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Federal Reserve、OCC 与 FDIC, [*Revised Guidance on Model Risk Management*（2026）](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/guidance/supervisory-guidance-on-model-risk-management.htm)，§§III、V–VI：核验按用途和重要性调整验证强度、验证对可靠性与限制的评估、概念健全性、结果分析、持续监控，以及更广治理中的有效质疑、职责和控制。
- [Federal Reserve SR 26-2](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/SR2602.htm)：核验 2026 修订指引取代 SR 11-7 的关系及其主要适用范围，避免把美国监管指引写成全球统一法律清单。
- [Basel Framework, MAR30.2、30.5–30.16](https://www.bis.org/basel_framework/chapter/MAR/30.htm)：核验市场风险内部模型法下的概念健全性、独立验证、治理参与、压力测试、文档、数据、头寸、估值、风险变换与回测要求。
