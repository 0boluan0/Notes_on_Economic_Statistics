# Probability mass function (PMF)
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*概率质量函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **probability mass function**, or **PMF**, tells us how likely each numerical value is. It describes a **discrete random variable**, whose probability is concentrated on finitely or countably many values. Think of it as a table: one column lists the values, and another gives the probability of each value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**概率质量函数**，简称 **PMF**，告诉我们每个数值出现的概率。它描述**离散随机变量**：概率集中在有限个或可数多个数值上的变量。可以先把 PMF 想成一张表：一列列出数值，另一列写出对应概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[随机变量|random variable]] is a rule that turns an experimental outcome into a number. Its PMF describes the probabilities of those recorded numbers. Several original outcomes can produce the same number, so we may need to combine their probabilities before writing the table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[随机变量]]是一条把试验结果记成数字的规则，PMF 描述的是这些**记录下来的数字**各有多大概率。几个不同的原始结果可能被记成同一个数，因此填概率表时，可能需要先合并它们的概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Building the probability table
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从一次骰子试验建立概率表*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Roll a fair six-sided die, and let $X$ record $1$ if the result is $5$ or $6$, and $0$ otherwise. The six original outcomes each have probability $1/6$, as in the [[离散概率空间|discrete probability space]] example. But $X$ has only two possible values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

掷一枚公平六面骰子，掷出 $5$ 或 $6$ 时让 $X$ 记作 $1$，其余结果记作 $0$。和[[离散概率空间]]的例子一样，六个基本结果各有 $1/6$ 的概率；但 $X$ 只有两个可能取值。

> [!example] Two values from six outcomes · 六个结果，两个数值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Four die outcomes give $X=0$, while two give $X=1$. Adding the probabilities within each group gives the PMF:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 四个骰子结果被记成 $0$，两个被记成 $1$。分别把每组的概率相加，就得到 PMF：
>
> | $x$ | $0$ | $1$ |
> |---|---|---|
> | $p_X(x)$ | $4/6=2/3$ | $2/6=1/3$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The two masses sum to $1$. For any other value, such as $x=2$, the PMF is $0$, because the recording rule never produces that value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这两个概率加起来为 $1$。对于其他数值，例如 $x=2$，PMF 等于 $0$，因为这条记录规则不会产生那个值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If we instead let $Y$ record the face value itself, its PMF is $p_Y(y)=1/6$ for $y=1,\ldots,6$, and $0$ elsewhere. Both variables come from the same die roll, but their different recording rules produce different probability tables.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果另用 $Y$ 直接记录骰子点数，它的 PMF 就在 $y=1,\ldots,6$ 处各等于 $1/6$，其余位置为 $0$。两个变量来自同一次掷骰子，但记录规则不同，概率表也就不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reading the formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把概率表写成公式*

> [!abstract] The PMF definition · PMF 的定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a discrete random variable $X$, its probability mass function is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 离散随机变量 $X$ 的概率质量函数定义为
>
> $$p_X(x)=P(X=x).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The subscript $X$ identifies the variable. The input $x$ is a number we ask about, and $p_X(x)$ is the probability that the observed value equals that number. For another variable $Z$, a statement $p_Z(2)=0.3$ would mean exactly $P(Z=2)=0.3$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 下标 $X$ 说明这是哪个变量的 PMF；输入 $x$ 是我们关心的某个数，$p_X(x)$ 就是观测值恰好等于该数的概率。对于另一个变量 $Z$，$p_Z(2)=0.3$ 这句话直接表示 $P(Z=2)=0.3$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every entry is a probability, so it is nonnegative. If $S$ is the finite or countable set carrying all the probability, the entries must also sum to $1$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每一项都是概率，所以必须非负。若 $S$ 是承载全部概率的有限或可数取值集合，那么这些概率还必须加起来等于 $1$：

$$
p_X(x)\geq0,\qquad \sum_{x\in S}p_X(x)=1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To find the probability that $X$ lies in a set $A$, add the masses of the listed values that belong to $A$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要求 $X$ 落在某个集合 $A$ 里的概率，就把表中属于 $A$ 的那些数值的概率加起来：

$$
P(X\in A)=\sum_{x\in A\cap S}p_X(x).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $A\cap S$ means the values shared by the two sets, and $\sum$ means to add their probabilities. For the face-value variable $Y$, the event $Y\geq5$ contains the values $5$ and $6$, giving $P(Y\geq5)=p_Y(5)+p_Y(6)=1/3$. A PMF therefore determines probabilities for whole groups of values, as well as for individual values.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $A\cap S$ 表示两个集合共有的数值，$\sum$ 表示把它们的概率加起来。对于直接记录点数的 $Y$，事件 $Y\geq5$ 包含 $5$ 和 $6$，所以 $P(Y\geq5)=p_Y(5)+p_Y(6)=1/3$。因此，PMF 既给出单个数值的概率，也能确定一组数值的总概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why this is a mass function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*“质量”表示概率集中在一个个点上*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The word “mass” means that a probability amount is attached to a point. In the first example, the point $1$ carries probability mass $1/3$. A [[概率密度函数|probability density function]] works differently: its height is not the probability of a single point, and interval probabilities are obtained by integration. The distinction is explained in [[PMF不等于密度|PMF versus density]]. A PMF represents the whole distribution only when all its probability is concentrated on finitely or countably many points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“质量”的意思是：把一份概率放在某个点上。在第一个例子里，数值 $1$ 这个点承载了 $1/3$ 的概率。[[概率密度函数]]的含义不同：它的高度不是某个点的概率，需要在区间上积分才能得到区间概率；具体区别见 [[PMF不等于密度]]。只有当全部概率集中在有限或可数多个点上时，PMF 才能完整描述这个分布。
