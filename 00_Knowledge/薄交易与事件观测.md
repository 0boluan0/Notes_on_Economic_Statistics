# 停牌薄交易与事件日不确定会改变可观测价格反应窗口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Trading halts, thin trading, and event-date uncertainty change the observable price-reaction window*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 事件研究观察的是成交价格，不是连续可见的潜在价值。停牌时没有可成交价格，薄交易使“日收盘价”可能来自更早成交，事件日不确定则可能把真正反应错放到相邻日期；这些问题会改变 [[异常收益|AR]]/[[累计异常收益|CAR]] 的时间归属和基准估计。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Event studies observe transaction prices, not continuously visible latent value. A halt removes tradable prices, thin trading can make a “daily close” come from an earlier trade, and event-date uncertainty can assign the reaction to the wrong adjacent day. These problems alter the timing and benchmark estimation of AR and CAR.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非同步交易会偏移收益的方差、协方差和市场模型 beta，影响以市场模型计算的正常收益。影响对活跃证券可能很小，对小盘、低流动性或事件期成交骤减的证券却可能重要；不能只凭平均文献结果宣布无需处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nonsynchronous trading can bias return variances, covariances, and market-model beta, thereby affecting normal returns. The effect may be small for actively traded securities but material for small, illiquid, or event-period inactive securities. An average finding in prior studies is not a licence to ignore the issue in the actual sample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若公告出现在收盘前后不确定，可预先使用两日窗口或更精确时间戳；若停牌跨越公告，复牌价格会集中吸收停牌期间的多条信息，不能把复牌跳空无条件归给单一事件。应报告停牌、零收益、缺失成交、价格来源与复牌处理规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If it is unclear whether an announcement arrived before or after the close, use a prespecified two-day window or a more precise timestamp. If a halt spans the announcement, the reopening price can bundle all information received during the halt; the reopening jump cannot be attributed unconditionally to one event. Report halts, zero returns, missing trades, price sources, and reopening rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 公司停牌三天后复牌上涨 12%，能否把 12% 全记在第一天目标公告上？
>
> **答案：** 不能。停牌期没有连续成交，复牌价可能同时吸收多条消息；需明确可观测窗口、并发信息和复牌规则，再限定解释。
