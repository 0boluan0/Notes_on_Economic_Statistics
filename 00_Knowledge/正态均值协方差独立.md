# Independence of the normal sample mean and covariance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正态样本均值与样本协方差的独立性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sample mean describes the sample's center, while the sample covariance describes its spread around that center. Both use the same observations, so their independence is a substantive result. Under iid Gaussian sampling, learning the sample covariance does not change the distribution of the sample mean, and conversely. This concerns **random statistics across repeated samples**, not an ability to alter one summary while holding a realized data table fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

样本均值描述这批数据的中心，样本协方差描述围绕这个中心的变化。它们使用同一批观测，因此独立性不是理所当然的。在 iid Gaussian 抽样下，知道样本协方差不会改变样本均值的分布，反过来也一样。这说的是**反复抽样时两个随机统计量的关系**，并非允许在固定数据表里任意改变一个汇总量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n\ge2$ and let $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ be [[独立同分布|iid]] $p$-coordinate [[联合Gaussian|Gaussian vectors]] with common mean $\mu$ and covariance $\Sigma$, where $p\ge1$. Coordinates within an observation need not be independent. Define the [[样本均值向量|mean]] and [[样本协方差矩阵|covariance statistic]]
$$\bar X=\frac1n\sum_iX_i,\qquad
S=\frac1{n-1}\sum_i(X_i-\bar X)(X_i-\bar X)^T.$$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $n\ge2$ 个[[独立同分布|iid]] 的 $p\ge1$ 维 [[联合Gaussian|Gaussian 向量]] $X_1,\ldots,X_n$，共同均值为 $\mu$、协方差为 $\Sigma$。同一个观测内的坐标不必独立。上式分别定义[[样本均值向量]]与[[样本协方差矩阵|样本协方差统计量]]。

> [!important] Center and spread separate under this model · 此模型下中心与散布独立
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $$\bar X\sim N_p\!\left(\mu,\frac{\Sigma}{n}\right),\qquad \bar X\perp\!\!\!\perp S.$$
> The symbol $\perp\!\!\!\perp$ denotes [[随机变量独立|statistical independence]]. The result allows singular $\Sigma$ and singular $S$: no inverse or density is needed for independence. Positive definiteness and sample-size restrictions enter later if an application needs $S^{-1}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\perp\!\!\!\perp$ 表示[[随机变量独立|统计独立]]。即使 $\Sigma$ 或 $S$ 奇异，结论仍成立；独立性本身无需逆矩阵或密度。应用中若需要 $S^{-1}$，才要另加正定性与样本量条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## With two observations, use a sum and a difference
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两个观测时，改看和与差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two scalar observations from $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$, set
$$M=\frac{X_1+X_2}{\sqrt2},\qquad D=\frac{X_1-X_2}{\sqrt2}.$$
They are jointly Gaussian because they are fixed linear transformations of the Gaussian sample. Independence and equal variances of the observations give
$$\operatorname{Cov}(M,D)=\frac12(\sigma^2-\sigma^2)=0.$$
The [[联合高斯独立判据|Gaussian independence criterion]] therefore gives $M\perp\!\!\!\perp D$. Since $\bar X=M/\sqrt2$ and $S=D^2$, the mean uses only $M$ and the variance uses only $D$. Functions of these independent parts remain independent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个标量观测来自 $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ 时，把它们换成标准化的和 $M$ 与差 $D$。它们是 Gaussian 样本的固定线性变换，因而仍联合 Gaussian。观测独立且方差相同，使 $\operatorname{Cov}(M,D)=(\sigma^2-\sigma^2)/2=0$，由[[联合高斯独立判据]]得到 $M,D$ 独立。由于 $\bar X=M/\sqrt2$、$S=D^2$，均值只使用 $M$，方差只使用 $D$；对独立部分分别取函数，独立性仍保留。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Separate the mean from the complete residual block
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把均值与整个残差块分开*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For general $n,p$, write $R_i=X_i-\bar X$. The concatenated vector consisting of $\bar X,R_1,\ldots,R_n$ is jointly Gaussian by [[Gaussian仿射闭包|affine closure]]. Compute the cross-covariance between the mean and each residual:
$$\operatorname{Cov}(\bar X,R_i)
=\operatorname{Cov}(\bar X,X_i)-\operatorname{Cov}(\bar X)
=\frac{\Sigma}{n}-\frac{\Sigma}{n}=0.$$
Here independence across observations gives $\operatorname{Cov}(\bar X,X_i)=\Sigma/n$ and the [[样本均值协方差|sample-mean covariance]] is also $\Sigma/n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般 $n,p$ 下，令 $R_i=X_i-\bar X$。由[[Gaussian仿射闭包]]，把 $\bar X,R_1,\ldots,R_n$ 拼成的整个向量仍联合 Gaussian。均值与每个残差的交叉协方差如上式，等于 $\Sigma/n-\Sigma/n=0$；其中跨观测独立给出 $\operatorname{Cov}(\bar X,X_i)=\Sigma/n$，[[样本均值协方差]]也为 $\Sigma/n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus the mean is independent of the **whole stacked residual block**, while the residuals sum to zero and generally depend on one another. Since $S=(n-1)^{-1}\sum_iR_iR_i^T$ is a function of that block, it is independent of the mean. This argument also covers degenerate Gaussian vectors; zero-variance directions cause no problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此均值与**整个拼接的残差块**独立；残差相加为零，通常相互依赖，这不影响这个结论。$S=(n-1)^{-1}\sum_iR_iR_i^T$ 是残差块的函数，所以也与均值独立。退化 Gaussian 向量仍可使用这段证明，零方差方向不会造成困难。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An equivalent proof uses an orthogonal change of coordinates across observations. One direction contains $\sqrt n\,\bar X$, while $n-1$ perpendicular directions contain zero-mean Gaussian contrasts. The [[样本协方差Wishart律|Wishart proof]] uses those contrasts to derive the distribution of $S$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另一种等价证明在观测索引上作正交坐标变换：一个方向承载 $\sqrt n\,\bar X$，其余 $n-1$ 个垂直方向承载零均值 Gaussian 对比。[[样本协方差Wishart律|Wishart 抽样律的证明]]利用这些对比推导 $S$ 的分布。

> [!warning] Zero covariance with the variance is not the proof · 不能只算均值与方差不相关
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The Gaussian criterion is applied to the mean and the **linear residual vector**, not directly to the mean and the quadratic statistic $S$. For two iid fair signs $X_i\in\{-1,1\}$, $\bar X=0$ exactly when $S=2$, while $|\bar X|=1$ exactly when $S=0$. They are dependent, yet $\operatorname{Cov}(\bar X,S)=0$ by symmetry. The [[Wishart抽样假设|sampling-assumption counterexample]] gives their probabilities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> Gaussian 判据作用于均值和**线性的残差向量**，不能直接作用于均值与二次统计量 $S$。若两次 iid 观测等概率取 $\{-1,1\}$，$\bar X=0$ 当且仅当 $S=2$，$|\bar X|=1$ 当且仅当 $S=0$，显然相互依赖；但对称性又使 $\operatorname{Cov}(\bar X,S)=0$。[[Wishart抽样假设]]中的反例给出各自概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This separation is one ingredient in exact normal-sampling procedures such as the one-sample $t$ statistic and [[单样本Hotelling T²|Hotelling's $T^2$]]. Their reference distributions also need the appropriate variance or Wishart law and, where an inverse appears, enough rank. Independence by itself is not the whole calibration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种分离是正态抽样下单样本 $t$ 统计量和[[单样本Hotelling T²|Hotelling $T^2$]]精确推断的一个组成部分。完整参考分布还需要相应方差或 Wishart 抽样律；出现逆矩阵时，还要有足够的秩。独立性本身并不是全部校准条件。
