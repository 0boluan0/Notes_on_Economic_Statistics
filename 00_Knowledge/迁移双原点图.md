# Harris–Todaro双原点图以相反方向读取两部门劳动量并用预期收入交点确定迁移均衡

读[[Harris–Todaro模型]]的双原点图，先固定整条横轴代表同一个总劳动力，再分别从左侧农业原点和右侧城市原点量劳动人数。**现代部门曲线画面上向右上倾斜，不表示它的劳动需求随工资上升而增加**：它的劳动量是从右向左增加的。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read the [[Harris–Todaro模型|Harris–Todaro]] dual-origin diagram by fixing the total labour force across the horizontal axis, then measuring agricultural labour from the left and urban labour from the right. The visually upward-sloping modern-sector curve does not imply that labour demand rises with wages: its employment increases from right to left.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 按课堂图7-9读 E、Z 和横轴区间

打开[[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=37|课堂PDF第37页原图]]，配合[[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=38|第38页图解]]。对应Todaro与Smith第11版的Figure7.12；不同版本编号不同，以下沿用课堂标记。线段长度与横轴点名必须分清：图上标作 $L_M$ 的点位于右侧，但现代岗位量是线段 $O_ML_M$，不是从左原点量出的坐标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Open the [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=37|classroom figure]] with its [[02_Economy/10_发展经济学/发展经济学拍屏ppt.pdf#page=38|explanation]]. It corresponds to Figure 7.12 in the 11th edition; the course uses a different figure number. Distinguish axis labels from lengths: modern employment is the segment $O_ML_M$, not the left-origin coordinate of the point labelled $L_M$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 图中对象 | 读法与经济含义 |
|---|---|
| $O_AO_M$ | 全部劳动力；左端为农业原点，右端为现代部门原点 |
| $AA'$ | 农业劳动边际产出价值/工资需求曲线；从左向右增加农业劳动 |
| $MM'$ | 现代部门劳动需求曲线；从右向左增加现代部门就业 |
| $E$ | 灵活工资、充分就业基准；两部门工资相等，横轴的两部门劳动量刚好相加为总量 |
| $\bar W_M$ 与 $MM'$ 的交点 | 给定制度工资下确定现代岗位量 $O_ML_M$；不是迁移后的全部城市劳动力 |
| $qq'$ | 给定工资与岗位量时，随城市竞争池大小变化的城市预期收入轨迹 |
| $Z$ | $qq'$ 与农业收入曲线的交点；农业收入等于城市预期收入，而不等于制度工资 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read total labour across the whole axis. Agricultural labour is measured from the left and modern employment from the right. $E$ is the flexible-wage, full-employment benchmark. The institutional wage fixes modern jobs; $qq'$ traces expected urban earnings as the competing pool changes. $Z$ equates those expected earnings with agricultural earnings.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令从左量出的农业劳动坐标为 $x$，总劳动力为 $L$，给定现代岗位量为 $M>0$、城市工资为 $\bar W_M>0$。无其他城市收入时，迁移竞争池为 $S=L-x$，所以在 $S\ge M$ 的可行区间内：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $x$ measure agricultural labour from the left, $L$ total labour, $M>0$ the given number of modern jobs, and $\bar W_M>0$ the urban wage. With zero other urban earnings, the urban pool is $S=L-x$, so on the feasible domain $S\ge M$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$e_U(x)=\bar W_M\frac{M}{L-x},\qquad e_U'(x)=\frac{\bar W_MM}{(L-x)^2}>0.$$

$qq'$ 在画面上向右上倾斜，是因为 $x$ 越大、城市竞争池越小、岗位概率越高；它不是新增的工业劳动需求曲线。农业收入曲线在图中向右下倾斜；其与 $e_U(x)$ 相交的位置才给出相应迁移均衡。两部门产品价格和生产条件须保持与这张图的曲线一致；若同时改变它们，不能只移动一个未改变定义的点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The expected-income locus rises visually because a larger rural labour force leaves a smaller urban pool and a higher job probability. It is not another industrial labour-demand curve. Its crossing with the downward-sloping rural earnings curve determines the relevant migration equilibrium; prices and production conditions must remain consistent with the plotted curves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $Z$ 点，农业就业为 $O_AL_A$，城市劳动力为 $O_ML_A$，现代就业仍为 $O_ML_M$。两线段之差，即中间的 $L_AL_M$ 区间，是**未获现代岗位的人数**。只有在这些人都未就业且属于劳动力、没有其他城市岗位的基准中，它才等于城市失业人数；有[[非正规就业]]时必须重新区分状态，并相应修改 $qq'$ 的收入公式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $Z$, agricultural employment is $O_AL_A$, the urban pool is $O_ML_A$, and modern employment remains $O_ML_M$. The intervening segment $L_AL_M$ counts people without modern jobs. It equals unemployment only in the benchmark where they are jobless members of the labour force with no other urban employment. [[非正规就业|Informal work]] requires different state counts and a revised expected-income locus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课堂的 $E\to Z$ 是从灵活工资基准到制度工资下迁移均衡的比较，不是说真实经济先经过 $E$ 再沿某条线走到 $Z$；两部门当期工资也没有在 $Z$ 相等。具体政策改变如何移动岗位量和城市劳动池，分别看[[城市工资与迁移反应]]与[[岗位创造与诱致迁移]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Comparing $E$ and $Z$ contrasts two regimes; it is not a historical trajectory, and realised sectoral wages are not equal at $Z$. See [[城市工资与迁移反应|wage changes]] and [[岗位创造与诱致迁移|job creation]] for their different effects on jobs and the urban pool.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
