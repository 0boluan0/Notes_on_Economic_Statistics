# 终值现值占比是终值折现额占同一权利口径总现值的比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The discounted terminal-value share is terminal value discounted to today divided by total present value on the same claim basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

终值现值占比回答：这一组现金流的总现值中，有多少由显性预测期之后的现金支持。分子是已经折回估值日的[[估值终值]]，分母是同日、同一资产与权利范围的总现值，不能用未来时点的终值直接除以今天的价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The discounted terminal-value share measures how much of a cash-flow stream's total present value comes from cash beyond the explicit forecast. Its numerator is [[估值终值|terminal value]] discounted to the valuation date; its denominator uses the same date, asset scope, and claims. Do not divide future-date terminal value directly by today's value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如在[[企业现金流估值|FCFF经营估值]]中，若经营价值 $V_{\mathrm{op},0}>0$，使用该模型一致的折现规则：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For [[企业现金流估值|FCFF operating valuation]] with positive operating value $V_{\mathrm{op},0}$, use the model's consistent discounting rules:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$s_T=\frac{PV_0(TV_n)}{V_{\mathrm{op},0}},\qquad V_{\mathrm{op},0}=PV_0(FCFF_{1:n})+PV_0(TV_n)>0.$$

沿用企业现金流估值中的两年算例：前两年现金40、50，第二年末终值1200，前两年折现率10%。因此终值现值为 $1200/1.1^2\approx991.74$，总经营价值为 $40/1.1+1250/1.1^2\approx1069.42$，占比约92.74%。没有把另行估值的现金余额、非经营资产或普通股权利调整混入这个经营口径分母。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reuse the two-year FCFF example: cash of 40 and 50, terminal value of 1200 at year-end 2, and a 10% rate for the first two years. Terminal PV is approximately 991.74, total operating value is approximately 1069.42, and the share is about 92.74%. Separately valued cash balances, non-operating assets, and common-equity adjustments are not mixed into this operating-value denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

占比高本身不能判定估值错误，但说明结论集中依赖远期假设，应检查稳定期何时开始、[[终值增长的再投资约束|增长与再投资回报是否相容]]、隐含销售和市场份额是否符合经济尺度，再做[[估值敏感性]]及[[反向估值]]。不存在仅凭某个统一百分比就宣告失效的规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A high share alone does not invalidate a valuation, but highlights reliance on distant assumptions. Check the start of stability, [[终值增长的再投资约束|growth–reinvestment–return consistency]], and the economic plausibility of implied sales and market share, then use [[估值敏感性|sensitivity analysis]] and [[反向估值|reverse valuation]]. No universal percentage alone establishes invalidity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若显性期现金流现值为负，占比还可能超过100%；这反映早期投入抵减远期价值，而不是一种概率。若总现值为零，比例无定义；为负时，也不宜继续按“正总价值的组成百分比”解释，应直接分别报告两部分现值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Negative explicit-period cash-flow PV can make the share exceed 100%: early investment offsets later value, and the share is not a probability. It is undefined at zero total value. With negative total value, do not interpret it as a composition percentage of a positive total; report the two PV components separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Damodaran，Estimating Terminal Value](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/valquestions/termvalapproaches.htm)：终值时点与显性期之后现金的范围。占比公式由同一口径现值分解直接整理；92.74%独立复算共享算例，零／负分母的解释来自比例运算条件。
- [Damodaran，2011年课程答疑，10月10日与10月17日](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/eqemailfall11.html)：高终值占比不能单独否定DCF；稳定期参数应共同一致，并可从市价反求隐含增长。
- [Damodaran，The Stable Growth Rate](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/valquestions/stablegrowthrate.htm) 与[[终值增长的再投资约束]]：长期经济尺度及增长所需再投资的约束。正文复核顺序是据这些条件整理的应用诊断，不把历史数例或经验阈值当作现行统一要求。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Damodaran's terminal-value note supports timing and cash-flow scope. The share follows directly from PV decomposition; the shared example and denominator conditions are independently checked. The October 10 and 17 course responses reject judging DCF solely by terminal share and support joint stable-phase assumptions and implied-growth checks. The stable-growth note and shared reinvestment atom constrain the long-run economics; the review sequence is a diagnostic synthesis, not a universal historical threshold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
