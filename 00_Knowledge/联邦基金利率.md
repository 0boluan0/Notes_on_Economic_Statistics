# 联邦基金利率是美国存款机构在联邦基金市场借入无担保短期美元资金的利率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The federal funds rate is the rate on unsecured short-term dollar borrowing by depository institutions in the U.S. federal funds market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

联邦基金市场的借款人是存款机构，出借人包括其他存款机构和某些其他实体，尤其是政府支持企业。货币政策主要关注其中的隔夜交易：资金今天借出、下一营业日归还。它不是央行直接给每个家庭和企业设定的贷款利率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Depository institutions borrow from other depository institutions and certain other entities, particularly government-sponsored enterprises. Policy focuses on overnight trades, repaid on the next business day. This is not a rate the central bank directly assigns to household and business loans.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

FOMC可以设定希望市场交易靠近的目标区间，却不直接指定每笔交易的成交价。假设目标区间为4.00%–4.25%，一笔交易可能成交在4.10%；这个4.10%是借贷双方达成的价格，不是FOMC另设的一项工具利率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The FOMC can set the range towards which it wants trading to move without fixing every trade price. With a hypothetical 4.00%–4.25% range, a trade might occur at 4.10%. That is the counterparties' agreed price, not a separate FOMC instrument rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每天不同交易的汇总指标是[[有效联邦基金利率]]；其统计口径与单笔成交价、目标区间都要分开。本卡的数字仅说明交易和政策目标的不同角色，不是当前报价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Daily trades are summarised by the [[有效联邦基金利率|effective federal funds rate]], whose methodology is separate from a trade price or target range. The numbers here only illustrate roles, not current quotes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

央行通过[[央行管理利率]]和准备金供给影响这个市场价格；市场为何可能偏离管理利率，见[[管理利率的市场边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[央行管理利率|Administered rates]] and reserve supply influence the traded rate; [[管理利率的市场边界|access and intermediation limits]] explain possible deviations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
