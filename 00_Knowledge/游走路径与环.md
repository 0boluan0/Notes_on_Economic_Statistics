# Walks, paths and cycles
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*游走、路径与环*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **walk** records a sequence of steps along edges. A **path** reaches its destination without revisiting a vertex. A **closed walk** returns to its starting vertex, while a **cycle** closes up without repeating an edge or revisiting a vertex along the way. These words describe the actual sequence of steps, not just the shape of the graph containing it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**游走**记录沿边前进的一串步骤；**路径**到达终点时不重复访问顶点；**闭游走**回到起点；**环**则闭合一圈，途中不重复边，也不重复访问顶点。这些词描述的是实际走过的序列，不只是所在图的形状。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, take three distinct vertices $u,v,w$ and the two undirected edges $u-v$ and $v-w$. The trip $u,v,w$ is a path of length two. The trip $u,v,u,v,w$ is a walk of length four: it traverses the edge $u-v$ three times before reaching $w$. Repeated traversals count separately when measuring length.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，取三个不同顶点 $u,v,w$，以及两条无向边 $u-v$ 与 $v-w$。行程 $u,v,w$ 是长度二的路径。行程 $u,v,u,v,w$ 是长度四的游走：它先走过 $u-v$ 这条边三次，再到达 $w$。计算长度时，同一条边被走过几次，就计几次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Record the vertices and the chosen edges
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同时记录顶点与选中的边*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First specify the [[图类型的建模边界|graph type]]: directed or undirected, and whether loops or parallel edges are allowed. Write a finite walk as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
先明确[[图类型的建模边界|图类型]]：有向还是无向，是否允许自环和平行边。一个有限游走写成

$$W=(v_0,e_1,v_1,e_2,v_2,\ldots,e_k,v_k).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $k$ is a nonnegative integer, the vertices are $v_0,\ldots,v_k$, and each $e_i$ is the edge used for step $i$, from $v_{i-1}$ to $v_i$. The **length** is $k$, the number of edge traversals. In a directed graph, $e_i$ must point from $v_{i-1}$ to $v_i$; it cannot be used backward. An undirected edge may be used either way. The same edge may occur at several positions in a walk.

When parallel edges exist, the vertex sequence alone need not determine the walk: two different edges can connect the same pair of vertices. Keeping the $e_i$ labels records which edge was used. In a simple graph, vertex notation such as $u,v,w$ is enough when the connecting edges are understood.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
其中 $k$ 是非负整数，$v_0,\ldots,v_k$ 是沿途顶点，$e_i$ 是第 $i$ 步从 $v_{i-1}$ 到 $v_i$ 使用的边。**长度**为 $k$，即走边的次数。有向图中，$e_i$ 必须从 $v_{i-1}$ 指向 $v_i$，不能倒用；无向边则可以双向走过。游走允许同一条边出现在多个位置。

存在平行边时，仅给顶点序列不一定能确定游走，因为同一对顶点可能由不同边相连。保留 $e_i$ 的标识，才能说明走的是哪条边。在简单图中，连接边已经明确时，可以简写为 $u,v,w$ 这样的顶点序列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Which repetitions are allowed?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*哪些重复被允许？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- A **walk** may repeat vertices and edges.
- A **path** is a walk whose vertices $v_0,\ldots,v_k$ are all distinct.
- A **closed walk** satisfies $v_k=v_0$. Apart from this requirement, it may repeat vertices and edges.
- A **cycle** is represented by a closed walk with $k\ge1$, with distinct edges $e_1,\ldots,e_k$ and distinct vertices $v_0,\ldots,v_{k-1}$. Only the final return $v_k=v_0$ repeats a vertex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- **游走**可以重复顶点和边。
- **路径**是顶点 $v_0,\ldots,v_k$ 全都不同的游走。
- **闭游走**满足 $v_k=v_0$；除此之外仍允许重复顶点和边。
- **环**用这样的闭游走表示：$k\ge1$，边 $e_1,\ldots,e_k$ 互不相同，顶点 $v_0,\ldots,v_{k-1}$ 互不相同，只有最后返回的 $v_k=v_0$ 重复了顶点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The walk consisting only of a vertex $u$ has length zero. It is both a path and a closed walk, but it is not a cycle. A positive-length cycle is not a path under this convention because its starting vertex occurs again at the end. A path also need not be shortest: in a triangle, $a,b,c$ and $a,c$ are both paths from $a$ to $c$, with different lengths. The connection with [[游走去环得到路径|shortest walks]] requires a proof, not a new definition of “path.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
只含一个顶点 $u$ 的游走长度为零，既是路径，也是闭游走，但不是环。按此约定，正长度的环也不是路径，因为它在最后再次出现起点。路径不必最短：三角形中的 $a,b,c$ 与 $a,c$ 都是从 $a$ 到 $c$ 的路径，却有不同长度。它与[[游走去环得到路径|最短游走]]的关系需要证明，不能把“最短”直接塞进路径定义。

## Cycle length depends on the graph type
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## cycle 的长度边界来自图类型

> [!warning] Returning on the same edge is not a cycle / 沿同一条边返回不构成环
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If an undirected graph has just one edge $e$ between $u$ and $v$, then $(u,e,v,e,u)$ is a length-two closed walk. Its vertices repeat only at the endpoints, but its edge repeats, so it is **not** a cycle. Checking vertices alone would give the wrong answer.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 若无向图在 $u,v$ 之间只有一条边 $e$，则 $(u,e,v,e,u)$ 是长度二的闭游走。虽然顶点只在首尾重复，但边重复了，所以它**不是**环。只检查顶点会得出错误结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two **distinct parallel undirected edges** $e,f$ give a different case: $(u,e,v,f,u)$ is a two-cycle. In a directed graph, the two different edges $u\to v$ and $v\to u$ also form a directed two-cycle; neither is being traversed backward. These opposite directed edges are not parallel directed edges. A permitted self-loop gives a one-cycle $(u,e,u)$.

A simple undirected graph has neither loops nor parallel edges, so its cycles have length at least three. Choosing another starting vertex around the same undirected cycle, or going around it in reverse, describes the same cycle subgraph. A directed cycle must retain its permitted direction; reversing the sequence need not be a valid directed walk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
两条**不同的无向平行边** $e,f$ 则不同：$(u,e,v,f,u)$ 是二环。有向图中，两条不同边 $u\to v$ 与 $v\to u$ 也构成有向二环，其中没有倒用任何一条边；这两条反向有向边不属于同向平行边。若允许自环，$(u,e,u)$ 就是一环。

简单无向图没有自环和平行边，所以环至少长三。沿同一个无向环改选起点，或者反向绕行，描述的仍是同一个环子图。有向环则必须保留允许的方向，倒序未必仍是合法有向游走。

> [!question]- Classify the whole trip / 判断整段行程
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the undirected triangle $a-b-c-a$, consider $a,b,c,a,b,a$. It has length five and is a closed walk. It is not a path, since vertices repeat, and it is not a cycle, since it revisits $a,b$ and reuses the edge $a-b$. The shorter segment $a,b,c,a$ is a cycle. A walk can contain a cycle without itself being a cycle.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 在无向三角形 $a-b-c-a$ 中，考虑 $a,b,c,a,b,a$。它长五，是闭游走；由于重复顶点，它不是路径；由于再次访问 $a,b$ 并重复边 $a-b$，它也不是环。较短的片段 $a,b,c,a$ 才是环。包含环与整段行程本身就是环，是两件不同的事。
