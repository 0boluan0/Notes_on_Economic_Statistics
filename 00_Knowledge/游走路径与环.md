# 游走允许重复而路径与环排除相应的顶点重复
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Walks allow repetition; paths and cycles exclude the relevant repeated vertices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 对一张图：
> - **walk（游走）**沿相邻边依次前进，可以重复顶点和边；长度是走过的边数。
> - **path（路径）**是不重复顶点的 walk；单个顶点是长度 $0$ path。
> - **closed walk（闭游走）**首尾是同一顶点，内部仍可重复。
> - **cycle（环）**是正长度 closed walk，除相同的首尾外不重复顶点。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a graph:
> - A **walk** follows adjacent edges and may repeat vertices or edges; its length is the number of traversed edges.
> - A **path** is a walk with no repeated vertices; a single vertex is a length-zero path.
> - A **closed walk** begins and ends at the same vertex but may repeat internal vertices.
> - A **cycle** is a positive-length closed walk with no repeated vertex except the identical first and last vertex.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 digraph 中，每一步都必须沿箭头方向。序列 $u,v,w$ 只有在 $u\to v$ 与 $v\to w$ 都存在时才是 directed walk；看到反向边 $v\to u$ 不能倒着使用。无向边则可以从任一端走向另一端。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every step in a digraph must follow the arrow direction. The sequence $u,v,w$ is a directed walk only when both $u\to v$ and $v\to w$ exist; an edge $v\to u$ cannot be traversed backward. An undirected edge may be traversed from either endpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## cycle 的长度边界来自图类型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cycle length depends on graph type*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

simple undirected graph 没有 self-loop 或 parallel edges，所以 cycle 至少长 $3$；沿一条边去到邻点再原路返回只是长度 $2$ closed walk，不是 cycle。digraph 若有 $u\to v$ 与 $v\to u$，可有 directed 2-cycle；若允许 self-loop，还可有 1-cycle。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A simple undirected graph has neither loops nor parallel edges, so every cycle has length at least three. Traversing one edge and immediately returning is a length-two closed walk, not a cycle. A digraph may have a directed two-cycle from $u\to v$ and $v\to u$, and a permitted self-loop gives a one-cycle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 在无向 triangle $a-b-c-a$ 上走 $a-b-c-a-b-a$。它是 walk、closed walk、path、cycle 中的哪些？
>
> **答案：**它沿边且首尾同为 $a$，所以是 closed walk，也当然是 walk；它重复 $a,b$，所以不是 path；内部重复顶点，故整条 closed walk 也不是 cycle，尽管其中包含 cycle $a-b-c-a$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the undirected triangle $a-b-c-a$, traverse $a-b-c-a-b-a$. Which of walk, closed walk, path, and cycle describe the full sequence?
>
> **Answer:** It follows edges and begins and ends at $a$, so it is a closed walk and therefore a walk. It repeats $a$ and $b$, so it is not a path. It also repeats an internal vertex, so the full closed walk is not a cycle, although it contains the cycle $a-b-c-a$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
