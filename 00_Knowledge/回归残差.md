# 回归残差是观测结果与拟合结果之差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A regression residual is the observed outcome minus its fitted value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 对回归拟合值 $\hat y=X\hat\beta$，样本残差向量定义为
> $$
> \hat u=y-\hat y=y-X\hat\beta,
> $$
> 因而第 $i$ 个残差是 $\hat u_i=y_i-\hat y_i$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For fitted regression values $\hat y=X\hat\beta$, the sample residual vector is defined as $\hat u=y-\hat y=y-X\hat\beta$, so the residual for observation $i$ is $\hat u_i=y_i-\hat y_i$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 残差不是不可观测误差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A residual is not the unobserved error*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

残差由样本和估计结果计算得到，是可观测的拟合缺口。模型误差 $u_i$ 则描述数据生成过程中没有被回归函数解释的部分，通常不可直接观测。即使模型写成 $y=X\beta+u$，也不能把 $\hat u$ 当成 $u$ 的逐项真值；估计系数会让残差满足样本内约束，例如含截距 OLS 的残差和为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A residual is a computable gap determined by the sample and the fitted model. The model error $u_i$ represents the component left unexplained by the data-generating regression function and is usually unobserved. Even when the model is written as $y=X\beta+u$, the fitted residuals $\hat u$ are not observation-by-observation truth about $u$; coefficient estimation imposes sample constraints, such as a zero residual sum for OLS with an intercept.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若某人的观测结果是 $y_i=12$，回归模型给出的拟合值是 $\hat y_i=10.5$，则残差为 $\hat u_i=1.5$。正号表示模型对这一观测低估了结果；它本身不说明低估来自遗漏变量、随机波动还是数据问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an observed outcome is $y_i=12$ and the fitted value is $\hat y_i=10.5$, then $\hat u_i=1.5$. The positive sign means that the model underpredicts this observation; by itself it does not identify omitted variables, random variation, or a data problem as the cause.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> 为什么样本残差 $\hat u_i$ 不能直接当成模型误差 $u_i$ 的观测值？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why can a sample residual $\hat u_i$ not be treated directly as an observed value of the model error $u_i$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 残差使用估计出来的 $\hat\beta$，会受全体样本和拟合约束影响；模型误差是相对于真实回归关系定义的不可观测量。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** A residual uses the estimated coefficient vector $\hat\beta$ and is shaped by the full sample and fitting constraints, whereas the model error is defined relative to the true regression relationship and is unobserved.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
