# Regression residuals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*回归残差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **regression residual** records the gap left after fitting a model to an observation: take the observed outcome and subtract the fitted outcome. If a person’s recorded outcome is $12$ and the fitted model gives $10.5$, the residual is $1.5$. Its positive sign says that this fit underpredicts this observation; it does not tell us why.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**回归残差**记录模型拟合后还剩多少缺口：用实际观察结果减去拟合结果。某人的记录值为 $12$、拟合值为 $10.5$，残差就是 $1.5$。正号表示这次拟合低估了该观测，但没有说明低估的原因。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $y_i$ for observation $i$’s outcome and $\hat y_i$ for its fitted value; a hat marks a quantity obtained from the fitted model. The residual is $\hat u_i=y_i-\hat y_i$, in the same units as the outcome. A negative residual means overprediction, and zero means an exact fit at that observation. In an outcome-versus-predictor plot, it is a **signed vertical gap**, rather than the shortest geometric distance to the fitted line.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $y_i$ 表示第 $i$ 个观测结果，$\hat y_i$ 表示其拟合值；“帽子”表示由拟合模型得到的量。残差为 $\hat u_i=y_i-\hat y_i$，单位与结果相同。负残差表示高估，零表示该观测被恰好拟合。在结果对解释变量的图上，它是**带正负号的竖直缺口**，不是点到拟合线的最短几何距离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Collect the gaps into a vector
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把各个缺口放在一个向量里*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n$ real observations, collect the outcomes into the column vector $y$ and the fitted values into $\hat y$. In a model [[参数线性|linear in its coefficients]], let $X$ be the $n\times p$ design matrix: each row describes one observation, and its $p$ columns contain the chosen features, including a column of ones if an intercept is fitted. Let $\hat\beta$ be the $p$ fitted coefficients. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $n$ 个实数观测，把结果排成列向量 $y$，拟合值排成 $\hat y$。在[[参数线性|对系数线性]]的模型中，$X$ 是 $n\times p$ 设计矩阵：每行对应一个观测，$p$ 列放入选定的特征；拟合截距时包含一列全为 $1$ 的数。$\hat\beta$ 是 $p$ 个拟合系数。因此

$$
\hat y=X\hat\beta,\qquad \hat u=y-\hat y=y-X\hat\beta,\qquad y=\hat y+\hat u.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The subtraction defines residuals for any fitted predictions. [[普通最小二乘|Ordinary least squares (OLS)]] is a particular fitting rule: it chooses coefficients to minimise the residual sum of squares $\mathrm{RSS}=\sum_{i=1}^n\hat u_i^2$. The definition of a residual does not by itself require OLS, normally distributed errors, or a correct model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只要有拟合预测值，就能用减法定义残差。[[普通最小二乘|普通最小二乘（OLS）]]是其中一种拟合规则：选择使残差平方和 $\mathrm{RSS}=\sum_{i=1}^n\hat u_i^2$ 最小的系数。残差这个定义本身不要求使用 OLS、误差正态，或模型正确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A residual and a model error use different reference values
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 残差不是不可观测误差

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a specified model writes the observed sample as $y=X\beta+u$. Here $\beta$ is the model’s coefficient vector and $u$ is the corresponding error vector; neither is generally observed. Substituting this equation into the residual definition gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设一个指定模型把观察样本写成 $y=X\beta+u$。这里 $\beta$ 是模型的系数向量，$u$ 是相应的误差向量，两者通常都不可直接观察。代入残差定义可得

$$\hat u=u-X(\hat\beta-\beta).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus every residual uses an estimated reference value. Even under a correctly specified model, a residual need not equal that observation’s true error. Under a [[条件均值与线性投影|conditional-mean model]], the reference function is the population conditional average; under a best-linear-projection interpretation, it is the best population linear approximation. Those two definitions of the model error can themselves differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，每个残差使用的都是估计出来的参照值。即使模型设定正确，残差也不必等于该观测真正的误差。在[[条件均值与线性投影|条件均值模型]]中，参照函数是总体条件平均；在最佳线性投影解释中，参照函数是总体上最好的线性近似。这两种模型误差本身也可能不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A three-point fit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

> [!example] One error can affect several residuals · 一个误差会影响多个残差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Fit an intercept and slope to $(x_i,y_i)=(0,1),(1,2),(2,4)$ by OLS. The fitted line is $\hat y=5/6+(3/2)x$. The fitted values are $5/6,7/3,23/6$, so the residuals are $1/6,-1/3,1/6$. They sum to zero; their squared sum is $1/6$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对三点 $(0,1),(1,2),(2,4)$ 做含截距和斜率的 OLS，得到 $\hat y=5/6+(3/2)x$。拟合值为 $5/6,7/3,23/6$，残差为 $1/6,-1/3,1/6$，残差之和为零，平方和为 $1/6$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If, for illustration, the underlying coefficient values were actually intercept $1$ and slope $1$, this realised sample would have model errors $(0,0,1)$. Fitting changes the intercept and slope, spreading the resulting residual gaps across all three rows. This hypothetical reference model illustrates the identity above; the observed three points alone do not identify those true coefficients.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若为说明区别，假定底层系数确实是截距 $1$、斜率 $1$，这份已实现样本的模型误差就是 $(0,0,1)$。拟合改变了截距和斜率，于是三个观测都出现残差缺口。这是假定一个参照模型来展示上面的恒等式；仅靠三点数据不能认定这就是真实系数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For unconstrained OLS, the [[OLS正规方程|normal equations]] force residuals to have zero inner product with each included design column. If an intercept is included, their sum is zero. These sample constraints hold even when the proposed population model is wrong. [[样本正交与总体外生性|Sample orthogonality and population exogeneity]] explains why they cannot certify that model’s error assumptions. Without an intercept, a zero residual sum is not generally guaranteed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无约束 OLS 的[[OLS正规方程|正规方程]]强制残差与每个已纳入的设计列内积为零；包含截距时，残差之和为零。即使提出的总体模型有误，这些样本约束仍会成立。[[样本正交与总体外生性]]解释为什么它们不能验证模型的误差假设。没有截距时，一般不保证残差之和为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Residuals are useful for checking patterns left after fitting, but a large raw residual alone is not a complete diagnostic. [[学生化残差|Studentization]] compares a residual with a variance-based scale; [[删点影响定义|case-deletion influence]] asks how the fitted answer changes when an observation is removed. These answer different questions from the residual’s basic observed-minus-fitted definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

残差可以帮助检查拟合后留下的模式，但单个原始残差较大，并不是完整诊断。[[学生化残差]]将残差与基于方差的尺度比较；[[删点影响定义|删点影响]]问的是删掉观测后拟合答案改变多少。这些问题与“观察减拟合”的基本定义各有职责。
