# 正定协方差且收益约束独立时目标收益最小方差组合有唯一仿射闭式解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Positive-definite covariance and an independent return constraint give a unique target-return minimum-variance solution affine in the target.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $\Sigma\succ0$，且 $\mu$ 不是全一向量 $\mathbf1$ 的倍数。允许卖空，仅施加 $\mathbf1^Tw=1$ 与 $\mu^Tw=r$ 两个约束。则每个实数目标 $r$ 都可达，且最小化 $w^T\Sigma w$ 的权重唯一。令
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume $\Sigma\succ0$ and $\mu$ is not a multiple of $\mathbf1$. Allow unrestricted short selling and impose only $\mathbf1^Tw=1$ and $\mu^Tw=r$. Every real target $r$ is then feasible, and the variance-minimizing weights are unique. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
A=\mathbf1^T\Sigma^{-1}\mathbf1,\quad
B=\mathbf1^T\Sigma^{-1}\mu,\quad
C=\mu^T\Sigma^{-1}\mu,\quad
D=AC-B^2>0.
$$

闭式为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The closed form is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\boxed{w(r)=\Sigma^{-1}\left(\frac{C-Br}{D}\mathbf1+
\frac{Ar-B}{D}\mu\right)},\qquad
\boxed{v(r)=\frac{Ar^2-2Br+C}{D}}.
$$

## 推导与条件检查
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Derivation and condition checks*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵 $[\mathbf1\ \mu]^T\Sigma^{-1}[\mathbf1\ \mu]$ 是加权 Gram 矩阵。因为两列独立且 $\Sigma^{-1}\succ0$，它正定，所以 $D>0$；这不是只由“有多个资产”就能推出的条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matrix $[\mathbf1\ \mu]^T\Sigma^{-1}[\mathbf1\ \mu]$ is a weighted Gram matrix. Independent columns and $\Sigma^{-1}\succ0$ make it positive definite, hence $D>0$. Merely having multiple assets does not establish this condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $\mathcal L=\tfrac12w^T\Sigma w-\lambda(\mathbf1^Tw-1)-\gamma(\mu^Tw-r)$。驻点要求 $\Sigma w=\lambda\mathbf1+\gamma\mu$，代回两个约束得
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use $\mathcal L=\tfrac12w^T\Sigma w-\lambda(\mathbf1^Tw-1)-\gamma(\mu^Tw-r)$. Stationarity gives $\Sigma w=\lambda\mathbf1+\gamma\mu$; substitution into the constraints yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\begin{bmatrix}A&B\\B&C\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}\lambda\\\gamma\end{bmatrix}
=\begin{bmatrix}1\\r\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\lambda=\frac{C-Br}{D},\quad\gamma=\frac{Ar-B}{D}.
$$

这给出 $w(r)$，而 $w^T\Sigma w=\lambda+\gamma r$ 给出 $v(r)$。对任意满足两条齐次约束的扰动 $h$，交叉项 $h^T\Sigma w=0$；因此其他可行权重的方差为 $v(r)+h^T\Sigma h$，严格正定性证明唯一全局最优。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This gives $w(r)$, and $w^T\Sigma w=\lambda+\gamma r$ gives $v(r)$. For any perturbation $h$ satisfying both homogeneous constraints, $h^T\Sigma w=0$. Every other feasible allocation has variance $v(r)+h^T\Sigma h$, proving unique global optimality by positive definiteness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\mu=c\mathbf1$，则 $D=0$：只有 $r=c$ 可达，该目标下回到[[全局最小方差解]]；其他目标不可行。若 $\Sigma$ 奇异，则普通逆不存在，应回到原约束问题检查[[协方差奇异性|零方差方向]]与最优解，而不能直接把公式中的逆改成广义逆并忽略条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\mu=c\mathbf1$, then $D=0$: only $r=c$ is attainable, reducing to the [[全局最小方差解|GMV problem]]; all other targets are infeasible. If $\Sigma$ is singular, return to the constrained problem and inspect [[协方差奇异性|zero-variance directions]] and its solutions. Blindly replacing the inverse by a generalized inverse does not remove these checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小算例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $\mu=(0.08,0.12)^T$、$\Sigma=\begin{bmatrix}0.04&0.015\\0.015&0.09\end{bmatrix}$。得到 $A=800/27$、$B=8/3$、$C=32/125$、$D=64/135$。目标 $r=0.10$ 给出 $w=(1/2,1/2)^T$、$v=0.04$。这些是指定输入下的最优结果，不保证估计输入准确，见[[组合优化的输入误差]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With the stated two-asset inputs, $A=800/27$, $B=8/3$, $C=32/125$, and $D=64/135$. Target $r=0.10$ gives equal weights and $v=0.04$. These are optimal for the specified inputs, not evidence that estimated inputs are accurate; see [[组合优化的输入误差|input estimation error]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
