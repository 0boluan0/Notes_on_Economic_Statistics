# 不变与可变资本按增殖作用划分而固定与流动资本按周转方式划分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Constant and variable capital distinguish roles in producing value; fixed and circulating capital distinguish turnover forms.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[不变资本]]与[[可变资本]]问资本的不同部分在价值增殖中怎样起作用；[[固定与流动资本]]问生产资本的预付价值怎样逐步或整体收回并更新。对同一项生产投入，要分别沿这两个判据判断，不能从其中一个名称直接推出另一个。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[不变资本|Constant capital]] and [[可变资本|variable capital]] distinguish how different parts function in producing value. [[固定与流动资本|Fixed and circulating capital]] distinguish how advanced productive-capital values are recovered and renewed, piecemeal or as a whole. Apply the two criteria separately to the same productive input; one label does not directly determine the other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 投入在生产中的职能 | 增殖作用 | 周转方式 |
|---|---|---|
| 多轮使用的机器 | 不变资本：转移已有价值 | 固定资本：逐步转值与收回 |
| 一轮耗用的原料、燃料 | 不变资本：转移已有价值 | 流动资本：该预付值整体周转 |
| 购买相应期间劳动力的预付 | 可变资本：使用劳动力再生产其等价并产生剩余 | 流动资本：需按相应周转重新预付 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

| Productive function | Role in value production | Turnover form |
|---|---|---|
| Machinery used over repeated rounds | Constant: transfers existing value | Fixed: value transferred and recovered piecemeal |
| Materials and fuel consumed in one round | Constant: transfers existing value | Circulating: the advance turns over as a whole |
| Advance for labour power over the relevant period | Variable: labour power reproduces its equivalent and produces surplus | Circulating: the advance requires renewal |

<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原料这一行是最小判别例：它属于不变资本，又属于流动资本。所以“不变”没有“不随产量变化的固定成本”的意思，“流动”也不能直接改称可变资本。劳动力预付在流动资本中占一部分，仍保有与原料不同的价值增殖作用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Raw material provides the smallest diagnostic example: it is constant capital and circulating capital. “Constant” therefore does not mean a fixed cost unaffected by output, and “circulating” cannot simply be renamed variable capital. The labour-power advance belongs to circulating capital while retaining its distinct role in producing value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应用时先说明物品当前的职能，例如机器是在卖方仓库待售，还是作为买方的生产设备使用。上表的固定／流动分类针对生产资本中的投入；没有先确定这个范围，单凭“是一台机器”就分类会漏掉商品资本的阶段。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before applying the table, identify the item's current function: for example, whether a machine awaits sale in its manufacturer's warehouse or serves as the buyer's equipment. The fixed/circulating classification here concerns inputs within productive capital. Classifying from “it is a machine” alone misses its commodity-capital stage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
