# Brownian motion with drift
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*带漂移布朗运动*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A Brownian model with drift describes a quantity with a steadily moving average and continuous random fluctuations around it. Imagine a position that starts at $3$, whose average decreases by $2$ per time unit, while random motion has scale $4$. The model is

$$X_t=3-2t+4W_t,$$

where $t\ge0$ is time and $W$ is [[标准布朗运动|standard Brownian motion]]: it starts at zero and has continuous paths and independent Gaussian displacements. A typical path of $X$ does not follow the straight line $3-2t$; that line describes its average over repeated realizations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

带漂移布朗模型描述一个平均水平匀速移动、同时围绕平均水平连续随机波动的量。想象位置从 $3$ 出发，平均每个时间单位下降 $2$，随机运动的尺度为 $4$，就得到上式。

其中 $t\ge0$ 是时间，$W$ 是[[标准布朗运动]]：从零开始、路径连续、具有独立的 Gaussian 位移。$X$ 的一条典型路径并不沿直线 $3-2t$ 运动；这条直线表示重复实现时的平均位置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The three parameters
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*三个参数各管什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, choose a deterministic starting value $x\in\mathbb R$, a deterministic drift rate $\mu\in\mathbb R$, and a constant diffusion scale $\sigma>0$. Define the real-valued process

$$X_t=x+\mu t+\sigma W_t,\qquad t\ge0.$$

The initial position is $X_0=x$ almost surely. The term $\mu t$ moves the average at rate $\mu$, which may be positive, negative or zero. The random term $\sigma W_t$ sets the spread around that average. Since adding a straight line and multiplying by a constant preserve continuity, almost every whole [[样本路径|sample path]] of $X$ is continuous.

The words “diffusion scale” or “volatility” refer here to $\sigma$. Its square $\sigma^2$ is the **variance rate**: over one time unit the random increment has variance $\sigma^2$. For an interval of length $h$, the [[带漂移布朗增量|increment calculation]] gives variance $\sigma^2h$ and [[标准差|standard deviation]] $\sigma\sqrt h$. If position is measured in metres and time in seconds, $\mu$ has units metres/second and $\sigma$ has units metres/$\sqrt{\text{second}}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，取确定的起点 $x\in\mathbb R$、确定的漂移率 $\mu\in\mathbb R$，以及常数扩散尺度 $\sigma>0$，就定义了上式的实值过程。

初始位置几乎必然为 $X_0=x$。$\mu t$ 让平均水平以速率 $\mu$ 移动，$\mu$ 可正、可负，也可为零；$\sigma W_t$ 决定围绕平均水平的随机分散。加上直线和乘以常数都保持连续性，所以 $X$ 几乎每条完整[[样本路径]]都连续。

这里“扩散尺度”或“波动率”指 $\sigma$，其平方 $\sigma^2$ 才是**方差增长率**：一个时间单位内的随机增量方差为 $\sigma^2$。长度为 $h$ 的区间，[[带漂移布朗增量|增量计算]]给出方差 $\sigma^2h$、[[标准差]] $\sigma\sqrt h$。若位置单位为米、时间单位为秒，则 $\mu$ 的单位为米／秒，$\sigma$ 的单位为米／$\sqrt{\text{秒}}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reading the example at two times
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

> [!example] The centre moves while the spread grows · 中心移动，分散增大
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $X_t=3-2t+4W_t$, at $t=1$ the [[期望|mean]] is $1$ and the standard deviation is $4$. At $t=4$ the mean is $-5$ and the standard deviation is $8$. The mean decrease does not force every realized path to decrease: a positive Brownian displacement can outweigh the negative drift over a particular interval. The variance at $t=4$ is $64$, not $8$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $X_t=3-2t+4W_t$，$t=1$ 时[[期望|均值]]为 $1$、标准差为 $4$；$t=4$ 时均值为 $-5$、标准差为 $8$。平均下降不要求每条实际路径都下降：某段时间的正向布朗位移可以超过负漂移。$t=4$ 的方差是 $64$，不是 $8$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same parameter reading applies to $X_t=5+t+3W_t$: the starting value is $5$, the average increases at rate $1$, and the diffusion scale is $3$. The starting value shifts all levels equally; it cancels from every increment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样地，$X_t=5+t+3W_t$ 表示起点 $5$、平均以速率 $1$ 上升、扩散尺度 $3$。起点让所有水平值一起平移，在任意增量中都会消去。

## Scope and degenerate cases
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 必要边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Within the convention $\sigma>0$, this is standard Brownian motion only when $x=0$, $\mu=0$ and $\sigma=1$. Allowing $\sigma=0$ gives the deterministic line $X_t=x+\mu t$, a useful degenerate extension of the formula, rather than Brownian motion with positive diffusion. Its increments are constants and hence independent, but have zero variance. The line is a stationary level process only if $\mu=0$; with positive diffusion, the increasing variance prevents [[布朗运动不平稳|stationarity of the levels]].

This is an **additive** model: for $t>0$ and $\sigma>0$, $X_t$ has a [[正态分布|normal distribution]] with support on the whole real line, so a positive starting value does not guarantee positivity. A geometric Brownian model instead uses an exponential transformation to model proportional changes and preserve positivity from a positive starting value. The two models describe different quantities.

Here $x,\mu,\sigma$ are fixed constants. Replacing them with random or time-dependent quantities changes the model and does not automatically retain the same stationary independent increment laws. The next card establishes those laws for the stated constant-parameter model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $\sigma>0$ 的约定下，只有 $x=0$、$\mu=0$、$\sigma=1$ 时它才是标准布朗运动。允许 $\sigma=0$ 时，公式退化为确定性直线 $X_t=x+\mu t$；这是有用的退化扩展，不是扩散尺度为正的布朗运动。其增量都是常数，因此仍独立，但方差为零。这条直线只有在 $\mu=0$ 时才是平稳的水平过程；正扩散尺度时，增长的方差会破坏[[布朗运动不平稳|水平过程的平稳性]]。

这是**加法型**模型：当 $t>0$、$\sigma>0$ 时，$X_t$ 的[[正态分布]]支撑整个实数轴，正起点不保证以后始终为正。几何布朗模型则通过指数变换描述比例变化，并从正起点保持正值。两种模型描述的对象不同。

这里的 $x,\mu,\sigma$ 都是固定常数。将它们换成随机量或随时间变化的量，会改变模型，不能自动保留相同的平稳独立增量律。下一张增量卡针对这里的常参数模型给出推导。
