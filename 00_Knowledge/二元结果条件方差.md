# Conditional variance of a binary outcome
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*二元结果的条件方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A zero-one outcome can only fail or occur, but the uncertainty about which happens depends on its probability. If an event is almost certain or almost impossible, the outcome is easier to predict. Around probability one half, either result is plausible. The [[条件方差|conditional variance]] measures this remaining spread around the group’s own mean.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零一结果只能不发生或发生，但不确定程度取决于事件概率。事件几乎必然发生或几乎不可能发生时，结果较容易预测；概率接近一半时，两种结果都很可能。[[条件方差]]衡量的是结果围绕本组均值剩下多少波动。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Y\in\{0,1\}$ and write $p(X)=P(Y=1\mid X)$. The [[二元结果条件均值|conditional mean is $p(X)$]]. Squaring zero or one leaves it unchanged, so $Y^2=Y$. Substituting into the conditional second-moment formula gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $Y\in\{0,1\}$，记 $p(X)=P(Y=1\mid X)$。[[二元结果条件均值|条件均值就是 $p(X)$]]。零或一平方后不变，所以 $Y^2=Y$。代入条件二阶矩公式：

$$
\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)=E[Y^2\mid X]-E[Y\mid X]^2=p(X)-p(X)^2=p(X)[1-p(X)].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an almost-sure identity for conditional random variables. All needed moments exist because $Y$ is bounded. At a fixed information state with event probability $p$, the same result can be read directly from squared deviations: the deviation of one from the mean $p$ is $1-p$, while the deviation of zero is $-p$. Their probability-weighted squared average is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是条件随机变量之间几乎处处成立的恒等式。由于 $Y$ 有界，所需矩全部存在。在事件概率固定为 $p$ 的信息状态下，也可以直接看偏离的平方：结果一与均值 $p$ 的偏离是 $1-p$，结果零的偏离是 $-p$。按概率加权后，偏离的平方平均为

$$
p(1-p)^2+(1-p)p^2=p(1-p).
$$

> [!example] A probability of 0.2 · 概率为0.2时
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a default indicator with probability $0.2$, the mean is $0.2$ and the variance is $0.2\times0.8=0.16$. Directly, the squared error is $0.8^2$ when default occurs and $0.2^2$ otherwise, giving $0.2\times0.64+0.8\times0.04=0.16$. A group with probability $0.8$ has the same variance: the likely and unlikely outcomes have exchanged roles.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 违约指标的概率为 $0.2$ 时，均值为 $0.2$，方差为 $0.2\times0.8=0.16$。直接看误差：违约时平方距离为 $0.8^2$，未违约时为 $0.2^2$，加权得到 $0.2\times0.64+0.8\times0.04=0.16$。概率为 $0.8$ 的组方差也相同，只是常见结果与少见结果交换了角色。

## 这个公式说明什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What the formula tells us*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p(1-p)=\frac14-\left(p-\frac12\right)^2,\qquad 0\le p\le1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The largest possible variance is $1/4$, attained at $p=1/2$. At $p=0$ or $1$, it is zero: the information determines the outcome almost surely. Moving toward either endpoint reduces this variance, but the variance alone does not identify the probability; $p$ and $1-p$ always give the same answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最大方差为 $1/4$，在 $p=1/2$ 时达到；$p=0$ 或 $1$ 时方差为零，意味着信息几乎处处确定结果。向任一端移动都会减小方差，但方差本身不能反推出唯一概率，因为 $p$ 与 $1-p$ 总给出相同答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Prediction errors and varying group spreads
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*预测误差与组间波动*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the true-probability residual $u=Y-p(X)$, the conditional mean is zero. Therefore $E[u^2\mid X]=\operatorname{Var}(u\mid X)=p(X)[1-p(X)]$. In a correctly specified [[线性概率模型|linear probability model]], $p(X)$ is represented by the chosen linear predictor. If this variance changes with $X$, errors are heteroskedastic, meaning different information states have different conditional spreads.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用真实概率构造残差 $u=Y-p(X)$，其条件均值为零。因此 $E[u^2\mid X]=\operatorname{Var}(u\mid X)=p(X)[1-p(X)]$。正确设定的[[线性概率模型]]用选定的线性预测表示 $p(X)$；若这个方差随 $X$ 变化，误差就具有异方差，即不同信息状态有不同的条件波动。

> [!warning] Changing probabilities need not change variance · 概率变化未必改变方差
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Groups with probabilities $0.2$ and $0.8$ both have variance $0.16$. Thus binary outcomes do not logically force heteroskedasticity; the precise requirement is that $p(X)[1-p(X)]$ varies. [[LPM异方差|The LPM heteroskedasticity boundary]] develops the regression implication.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 概率为 $0.2$ 和 $0.8$ 的组，方差都为 $0.16$。所以二元结果不会在逻辑上强制异方差；准确条件是 $p(X)[1-p(X)]$ 随信息变化。[[LPM异方差]]进一步说明回归中的含义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtracting any square-integrable prediction $g(X)$ known from $X$ still leaves conditional variance $p(X)[1-p(X)]$: within a group it only shifts the outcomes. But if $g(X)\ne p(X)$, the conditional **mean squared error** includes an extra term, $E[(Y-g(X))^2\mid X]=p(X)[1-p(X)]+[p(X)-g(X)]^2$. Variance describes spread around the conditional mean; it is not automatically the full error of an incorrect probability prediction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

减去任何平方可积、可由 $X$ 确定的预测 $g(X)$ 后，条件方差仍是 $p(X)[1-p(X)]$，因为在组内只是平移结果。但若 $g(X)\ne p(X)$，条件**均方误差**还多一项：$E[(Y-g(X))^2\mid X]=p(X)[1-p(X)]+[p(X)-g(X)]^2$。方差描述围绕条件均值的波动，并不自动等于错误概率预测的全部误差。
