# 农业短缺点与商业化点分别标记食品供养约束的出现和农业工资由制度决定转为边际产出决定

在[[费景汉–拉尼斯模型]]中，短缺点关注可供每位工业劳动者使用的食品剩余，商业化点关注农业雇主何时开始按劳动边际产出竞争用工。它们不是同一个“农村人口用完”的时刻；固定农业技术、初始人口和制度收入的基准模型可以有两个不同阈值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[费景汉–拉尼斯模型|Fei–Ranis model]], the shortage point concerns food surplus per industrial worker, whereas the commercialization point concerns agricultural employers beginning to bid for labour according to its marginal product. Neither means that the rural population has disappeared. With fixed agricultural technology, initial population and institutional income, the baseline can have two distinct thresholds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设总劳动为 $N$，转入工业的劳动为 $x$，留农劳动为 $L_A=N-x$，农业产出为 $F_A(L_A)$。基准起点把全部产出分给农业劳动者，确定制度食品收入 $b=F_A(N)/N$；随后第一、二阶段维持这个初始 $b$，并不每次按新的 $F_A(L_A)/L_A$ 重算。这与[[边际产出与退出收入|随留守人数重新均分收入]]是不同的分配规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let total labour be $N$, industrial labour $x$, remaining agricultural labour $L_A=N-x$, and agricultural output $F_A(L_A)$. At the baseline's starting point, all output is allocated to agricultural workers, fixing institutional food income at $b=F_A(N)/N$. Phases I and II retain this initial $b$ rather than continually resetting it to $F_A(L_A)/L_A$. This differs from [[边际产出与退出收入|equal sharing recalculated among remaining household members]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在初始生产函数有零边际产出区间的情况下，三个阶段按下面的条件区分：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the initial production function contains a zero-marginal-product segment, distinguish the phases as follows:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 阶段 | 农业边际产出 | 农业食品收入规则 | 再移出少量劳动 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 第一阶段 | $MP_A=0$ | 固定为 $b$ | 不减少农业产出 |
| 第二阶段 | $0<MP_A<b$ | 仍固定为 $b$ | 已减少农业产出，不能再称无损释放 |
| 第三阶段 | $MP_A>b$ | 竞争用工下随 $MP_A$ 上升 | 农业实际收入和工业招工条件发生变化 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Phase I has $MP_A=0$ and food income $b$: labour withdrawal leaves agricultural output unchanged. Phase II has $0<MP_A<b$ while income remains $b$: withdrawal already sacrifices agricultural output. In Phase III, $MP_A>b$ and competitive bidding raises agricultural income with marginal product, changing the terms on which industry can recruit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一、二阶段的边界是基准短缺点：零边际产出区间结束，随后[[平均农业剩余]]低于起初每位工业劳动者可带走的食品基准。第二、三阶段的边界是商业化点：$MP_A=b$，制度收入与边际贡献的差距消失。若农业技术变化，食品短缺边界必须重新按食品剩余判断，不能永远把它定义成 $MP_A$ 刚离开零的位置。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the baseline, the Phase I–II boundary is the shortage point: the zero-marginal-product segment ends, after which [[平均农业剩余|average agricultural surplus]] falls below the original food benchmark per industrial worker. The Phase II–III boundary is commercialization, where $MP_A=b$ and the gap between institutional income and marginal contribution closes. If agricultural technology changes, the food-shortage boundary must be reassessed using surplus; it is not universally the point at which $MP_A$ first becomes positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课堂第35页上下图共用劳动配置轴：工业劳动从左向右增加，农业劳动从右向左增加；下图农业产出从右端原点向下计量。因此，下方农业生产曲线的斜率要按农业轴的方向读。图中 $L_1$ 对应基准短缺点，$L_2$ 对应商业化点，区间 $L_1$ 到 $L_2$ 已存在正的农业产出损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two panels on course slide 35 share a labour-allocation axis: industrial labour increases from left to right, agricultural labour from right to left. Agricultural output in the lower panel is measured downward from the right-hand origin, so its slope must be read using the agricultural axes. The slide's $L_1$ corresponds to the baseline shortage point and $L_2$ to commercialization. Agricultural output is already sacrificed between them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上图工资水平线延伸到 $L_2$，须额外固定食品相对工业品价格，并让工业工资与农业食品收入保持固定比例。这样第一、二阶段的工业品计价工资才可不变；$MP_A$ 超过 $b$ 后才因工资竞争上升。只说“总人口没有增加”不能推出价格不变。Ranis–Fei原文图1则纳入[[食品价格与工业工资|食品相对价格反馈]]，工业品计价工资可以在短缺点便开始上升，到商业化点再加强；不能把两幅图的“转折点”标签无条件互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the upper-panel wage line to remain horizontal until $L_2$, additionally hold the food price relative to industrial goods fixed and tie industrial wages to agricultural food income at a fixed ratio. Industrial-goods wages can then remain constant in Phases I and II, rising through wage competition after $MP_A$ exceeds $b$. Constant population alone does not fix prices. Ranis and Fei's original Diagram 1 includes [[食品价格与工业工资|food-price feedback]]: the industrial-goods wage can start rising at the shortage point and rise further at commercialization. The turning-point labels in these diagrams are not interchangeable without their assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，令 $N=100$，$F_A(L_A)=20L_A-L_A^2/8$（$0\le L_A\le80$），其后产出固定为800。初始 $b=8$。当 $x=20$、$L_A=80$ 时零边际区间结束；当 $x=52$、$L_A=48$ 时 $MP_A=20-L_A/4=8$，到达商业化点。$x=40$ 时 $MP_A=5$，已有农业损失却仍低于制度收入；它正好说明为何不能把两个点合并。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, set $N=100$ and $F_A(L_A)=20L_A-L_A^2/8$ for $0\le L_A\le80$, with output fixed at 800 thereafter. Initial $b=8$. At $x=20$ and $L_A=80$, the zero-marginal-product segment ends. At $x=52$ and $L_A=48$, $MP_A=20-L_A/4=8$, reaching commercialization. At $x=40$, marginal product is already positive at 5 but remains below institutional income. This separates the two thresholds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
