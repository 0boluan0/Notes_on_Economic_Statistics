# Stopping time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*停时*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **stopping time** is a rule for choosing when to stop that can be checked as observations arrive. “Stop when the first head appears” is such a rule: when a head appears, we know it is time to stop. We need not know the stopping date beforehand. What matters is that at each date we can tell whether the rule has already stopped, using the information available by then.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**停时**是一种能够随着观察逐步判断的停止规则。“第一次出现正面就停”是这样的规则：正面出现时，就知道应当停止。不必事先知道最终会在哪一刻停；关键是每个时刻都能用截至当时的信息判断规则是否已经停止。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[滤过定义|filtration]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ records the information available at each time. Each $\mathcal F_t$ is a [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]] of events whose truth the available information can determine, and $\mathcal F_s\subseteq\mathcal F_t$ when $s\le t$. The information includes the current observation, not just observations strictly before $t$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[滤过定义|滤过]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ 记录各时刻拥有的信息。每个 $\mathcal F_t$ 都是一个[[σ-代数]]，其中事件的真假可以由当前信息判断；当 $s\le t$ 时，有 $\mathcal F_s\subseteq\mathcal F_t$。这里包含当期刚得到的观察，不只包含严格早于 $t$ 的观察。

> [!example] Stop on a head, with a two-toss deadline · 看到正面即停，最多抛两次
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $\xi_1,\xi_2$ record two tosses as $1$ for heads and $0$ for tails. At time $0$ neither is revealed; at time $1$ we know $\xi_1$; at time $2$ we know both. Define $T=1$ if $\xi_1=1$, and $T=2$ otherwise. Then
> $$\{T\le0\}=\varnothing,\qquad
> \{T\le1\}=\{\xi_1=1\},\qquad
> \{T\le2\}=\Omega.$$
> The empty event means “never true”, and $\Omega$ means all possible outcomes. Each question is answerable at the stated time. If the first toss is tails, the rule still stops at the deadline, whatever the second result.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用 $\xi_1,\xi_2$ 记录两次抛掷，正面记 $1$、反面记 $0$。时刻零尚未揭晓任何结果，时刻一知道 $\xi_1$，时刻二知道两次结果。若第一次为正面就令 $T=1$，否则令 $T=2$。上式中的空事件表示不可能发生，$\Omega$ 表示全部可能结果；每个问题都能在所写的时刻判断。第一次若为反面，无论第二次结果如何，规则都在截止时刻停止。

## The event that defines a stopping time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定义检查的是一个事件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work on a [[概率空间|probability space]] $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ with a filtration contained in $\mathcal F$. Use either discrete times $I=\mathbb N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$ or continuous times $I=[0,\infty)$. A random time is a [[随机变量|measurable recording rule]] $T$ taking values in $I\cup\{\infty\}$; the extra value means that the rule never stops.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[概率空间]] $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ 上给定包含于 $\mathcal F$ 的滤过。时间可以离散，$I=\mathbb N_0=\{0,1,2,\ldots\}$，也可以连续，$I=[0,\infty)$。随机时间 $T$ 是取值于 $I\cup\{\infty\}$ 的[[随机变量|可测记录规则]]；额外的 $\infty$ 表示规则永不停止。

> [!abstract] The stopping-time test · 停时判据
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $T$ is a stopping time relative to $(\mathcal F_t)$ exactly when
> $$\{T\le t\}=\{\omega\in\Omega:T(\omega)\le t\}\in\mathcal F_t
> \qquad\text{for every finite }t\in I.$$
> The set contains all outcomes on which stopping has occurred by $t$. Membership in $\mathcal F_t$ says that the time-$t$ information can distinguish this event. Merely knowing the numerical probability $P(T\le t)$ does not establish that membership.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $T$ 相对于 $(\mathcal F_t)$ 是停时，当且仅当每个有限 $t\in I$ 都满足上式。这个集合收集所有“在 $t$ 以前或恰好在 $t$ 已停止”的结果；属于 $\mathcal F_t$ 表示时刻 $t$ 的信息能够辨认这个事件。只知道一个数值 $P(T\le t)$，还不能证明事件属于该信息集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The filtration is part of the definition. Revealing extra information can turn a previously invalid rule into a stopping time; if $\mathcal F_t\subseteq\mathcal G_t$ at every time, every $\mathcal F$-stopping time remains a $\mathcal G$-stopping time. In the extreme case where all ambient information $\mathcal F$ is revealed at time zero, every nonnegative measurable random time is a stopping time for the constant filtration $\mathcal G_t=\mathcal F$. Thus “without looking into the future” refers to the information model, not just the written name of a rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

滤过是定义的一部分。提前揭示额外信息，可能让原本不合格的规则成为停时；若每个时刻都有 $\mathcal F_t\subseteq\mathcal G_t$，原来的 $\mathcal F$-停时仍然是 $\mathcal G$-停时。极端地，如果环境信息 $\mathcal F$ 在零时刻全部揭晓，那么对常值滤过 $\mathcal G_t=\mathcal F$，任何非负可测随机时间都是停时。所以“不偷看未来”针对所选信息模型，不能只看规则的名字。

## Equivalent tests in discrete time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 离散时间的等价写法

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For integer-valued $T\in\mathbb N_0\cup\{\infty\}$, the following three conditions are equivalent, with each required for every $n\ge0$:
$$\{T=n\}\in\mathcal F_n,\qquad
\{T\le n\}\in\mathcal F_n,\qquad
\{T>n\}\in\mathcal F_n.$$
The first asks whether stopping occurs exactly now; the second whether it has happened already; the third whether it has not yet happened.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $T\in\mathbb N_0\cup\{\infty\}$ 时，上面三组条件等价，每组都对所有 $n\ge0$ 提出要求。第一组问“恰好现在停吗”，第二组问“已经停了吗”，第三组问“仍然没有停吗”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To pass from exact times to “already stopped”, combine the finitely many branches:
$$\{T\le n\}=\bigcup_{k=0}^n\{T=k\}.$$
Each branch belongs to $\mathcal F_k\subseteq\mathcal F_n$, and a sigma-algebra is closed under finite unions. Conversely,
$$\{T=n\}=\{T\le n\}\setminus\{T\le n-1\},$$
where $\{T\le-1\}=\varnothing$ handles $n=0$. Taking complements gives the third test. At time zero the condition really is $\{T=0\}\in\mathcal F_0$; time zero must not be silently omitted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从恰好停止的时刻得到“已经停止”，只需把有限多个分支取并。每个分支属于 $\mathcal F_k\subseteq\mathcal F_n$，而 σ-代数对有限并封闭。反过来，用已在 $n$ 前停止的事件减去已在 $n-1$ 前停止的事件；约定 $\{T\le-1\}=\varnothing$，便包含 $n=0$。再取补集得到第三种判据。零时刻的要求确实是 $\{T=0\}\in\mathcal F_0$，不能悄悄略去。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In continuous time, keep the original $\{T\le t\}$ test. Combining all exact-time events $\{T=s\}$ for real $s\le t$ can require an uncountable union; a sigma-algebra guarantees only countable-union closure. Consequently checking each exact-time event alone is not a replacement. The definition itself needs no right-continuity assumption; particular continuous-time hitting or stopping theorems may need extra regularity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续时间应保留原来的 $\{T\le t\}$ 判据。把所有实数 $s\le t$ 的事件 $\{T=s\}$ 合起来，可能需要不可数并，而 σ-代数只保证对可数并封闭。因此，逐个检查恰好停止的事件不能替代原定义。定义本身不要求滤过右连续；具体连续时间命中或停止定理可能另需正则条件。

## A stopping rule need not eventually stop
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 定义不保证最终停止

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rule $T\equiv\infty$ is a stopping time: all its finite-time stopping events are empty. More generally, being a stopping time, being finite with probability one, having finite expectation, and having a fixed finite bound are different assertions. Since times are nonnegative,
$$T\le M\text{ a.s. for some finite }M
\ \Longrightarrow\ E[T]<\infty
\ \Longrightarrow\ P(T<\infty)=1.$$
Neither reverse implication holds in general. An unbounded [[几何分布|geometric waiting time]] can have finite mean, while an [[必达不推有限期望|almost-surely finite hitting time can have infinite mean]]. These distributional claims do not by themselves verify the information test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$T\equiv\infty$ 也是停时，因为在任何有限时刻已经停止的事件都是空集。一般地，停时、概率一有限、期望有限、有固定有限上界，是四件不同的事。时间非负，所以固定上界推出期望有限，期望有限又推出概率一有限；两个反向推论通常都不成立。没有固定上界的[[几何分布|几何等待时间]]可以有有限均值；[[必达不推有限期望|几乎必然有限的命中时间也可以有无限均值]]。这些分布性质本身都没有完成信息判据的检查。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[离散首次到达停时|first-entrance theorem]] proves when an observed discrete process supplies a stopping time. The [[末次到达通常非停时|last-visit counterexample]] shows why retrospective choices can fail. Once stopping is legitimate, [[停时信息集|information at a stopping time]] defines what is known at that random time; the stopping rule alone does not make future increments independent or justify optional stopping formulas.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[离散首次到达停时]]解释如何从离散观察构造停时；[[末次到达通常非停时]]展示事后挑选时刻为何可能失败。确定规则确实是停时后，[[停时信息集]]才定义在该随机时刻掌握什么。仅凭停时性质，不能推断未来增量独立，也不能直接使用可选停止公式。
