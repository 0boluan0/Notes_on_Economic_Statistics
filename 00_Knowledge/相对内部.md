# Relative interior
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*相对内部*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **relative interior** asks whether a point has a small surrounding region that stays inside a set, while allowing movement only within the set's supporting flat space. A line segment drawn in a plane has no two-dimensional thickness, yet a point halfway along it has room to move a little in either direction along its line. Relative interior captures this second sense of being inside.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**相对内部**问的是：只允许在集合所在的平直空间内移动时，一点周围能否找到一整小块区域，仍然留在集合中？画在平面中的线段没有二维厚度，但其中点沿着所在直线向两侧移动一点，仍在线段上。相对内部描述的就是后一种“在里面”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The ordinary [[集合内部|interior]] uses the whole specified ambient space, such as $\mathbb R^2$. For relative interior, the ambient space is fixed to be the [[仿射包|affine hull]] $\operatorname{aff}(C)$, the smallest flat space containing the set $C$. This choice of ambient space is part of the definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通的[[集合内部]]使用已经指定的整个环境空间，例如 $\mathbb R^2$。相对内部则把环境固定为集合 $C$ 的[[仿射包]] $\operatorname{aff}(C)$，即包含它的最小平直空间。选用哪个环境，是定义的一部分。

> [!example] A segment in the plane · 平面中的线段
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $C=\{(t,1):0\le t\le2\}$. Its affine hull is the line $H=\{(t,1):t\in\mathbb R\}$. Around $(1,1)$, a disk of radius $1/2$ contains points above and below the segment, so the disk does not fit in $C$. But its intersection with $H$ is the short open segment $\{(t,1):1/2<t<3/2\}$, which does fit. Thus $(1,1)$ is a relative interior point even though it is not an interior point in the plane.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $C=\{(t,1):0\le t\le2\}$，仿射包为直线 $H=\{(t,1):t\in\mathbb R\}$。以 $(1,1)$ 为中心、半径为 $1/2$ 的开圆盘含有线段上下方的点，因此整个圆盘放不进 $C$；但它与 $H$ 的交集是短开线段 $\{(t,1):1/2<t<3/2\}$，可以完全放进去。所以 $(1,1)$ 是相对内点，却不是平面环境中的内点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep only the part of the ball inside the hull
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*只保留球落在仿射包中的部分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, with $n\ge1$, use the [[欧氏范数|Euclidean norm]] to measure distance: $\|y-x\|_2$ is the straight-line distance from $x$ to $y$. The open ball $B_r(x)=\{y\in\mathbb R^n:\|y-x\|_2<r\}$ contains points less than $r$ away, where $r>0$. The relative interior, written $\operatorname{ri}(C)$ or $\operatorname{relint}(C)$, is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n$（$n\ge1$），使用[[欧氏范数]]测量距离：$\|y-x\|_2$ 是从 $x$ 到 $y$ 的直线距离。开球 $B_r(x)=\{y\in\mathbb R^n:\|y-x\|_2<r\}$ 收集距离小于正数 $r$ 的点。相对内部记为 $\operatorname{ri}(C)$ 或 $\operatorname{relint}(C)$：

$$
\operatorname{ri}(C)=
\{x\in C:\text{there exists }r>0\text{ with }
B_r(x)\cap\operatorname{aff}(C)\subseteq C\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The intersection means “keep only points of the ball that also lie in the affine hull.” This restricted ball must stay entirely in $C$. One suitable positive radius is enough, and different points may use different radii. This is exactly the ordinary interior taken in the [[子空间拓扑|subspace topology]] of the affine hull:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交集表示“只保留既在球里、又在仿射包里的点”。这个受限制的球必须全部留在 $C$ 中。只要有一个合适的正半径即可，不同点可以选不同的半径。这正是在仿射包的[[子空间拓扑]]中取普通内部：

$$\operatorname{ri}(C)=\operatorname{int}_{\operatorname{aff}(C)}(C).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the endpoints still fail
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么端点仍不属于相对内部*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Return to the segment $C=\{(t,1):0\le t\le2\}$. For any $0<t<2$, choose $r=\tfrac12\min(t,2-t)>0$. Every point in $B_r((t,1))\cap H$ still has its first coordinate between $0$ and $2$, so it lies in $C$. At the left endpoint $(0,1)$, however, every positive radius includes the point $(-r/2,1)$ in the restricted ball; that point is outside $C$. At the right endpoint, $(2+r/2,1)$ gives the same obstruction. Consequently,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回到线段 $C=\{(t,1):0\le t\le2\}$。对任意 $0<t<2$，取 $r=\tfrac12\min(t,2-t)>0$，则 $B_r((t,1))\cap H$ 中每一点的第一坐标仍在 $0$ 与 $2$ 之间，因此仍属于 $C$。但左端点 $(0,1)$ 处，任意正半径都让 $(-r/2,1)$ 落入受限制的球，而它不属于 $C$；右端点处则有 $(2+r/2,1)$ 造成同样的问题。因此，

$$
\operatorname{ri}(C)=\{(t,1):0<t<2\},
\qquad \operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R^2}(C)=\varnothing.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second equality holds because a disk of any radius $r>0$ around $(t,1)$ contains $(t,1+r/2)$, which is outside the horizontal line. Restricting to the line removes this irrelevant vertical obstruction, but it does not remove the obstruction at the segment's endpoints.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第二个等式成立，是因为以 $(t,1)$ 为中心的任意正半径 $r$ 的圆盘，都包含离开水平线的 $(t,1+r/2)$。把环境限制到直线，去掉的是无关的竖直方向障碍，并没有去掉线段端点处的障碍。

> [!warning] Relative to the hull, not to the set itself · 环境是仿射包
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Taking the interior of $C$ inside $C$ itself would always give $\operatorname{int}_C(C)=C$, including both endpoints. That is not $\operatorname{ri}(C)$. Nor can we replace the affine hull by an arbitrary larger plane without checking whether the result changes. Always identify $\operatorname{aff}(C)$ first.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若在 $C$ 自己内部取内部，总会得到 $\operatorname{int}_C(C)=C$，包括两个端点；这不是 $\operatorname{ri}(C)$。也不能随意用一个更大的平面替换仿射包，却不检查结果是否改变。应先确定 $\operatorname{aff}(C)$。

## Single points, empty sets and full-dimensional sets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*单点、空集与满仿射维数集合*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a singleton $C=\{p\}$, the affine hull is also $\{p\}$. Every ball around $p$, after intersection with that hull, contains only $p$. Therefore $\operatorname{ri}(\{p\})=\{p\}$, although its ordinary interior in $\mathbb R^n$ is empty for $n\ge1$. For the empty set, there is no member that could pass the test, so $\operatorname{ri}(\varnothing)=\varnothing$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $C=\{p\}$ 是单点集，仿射包也只有 $p$。以 $p$ 为中心的任何球，与这个仿射包相交后都只剩 $p$，所以 $\operatorname{ri}(\{p\})=\{p\}$；但当 $n\ge1$ 时，它在 $\mathbb R^n$ 中的普通内部为空。空集没有任何成员可以接受检验，因此 $\operatorname{ri}(\varnothing)=\varnothing$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\operatorname{aff}(C)=\mathbb R^n$, the intersection with the hull changes nothing, and relative interior equals ordinary interior. For example, a filled disk has its open disk as both interiors. The definition does not require $C$ to be convex, and a nonempty set need not have a relative interior point: the two endpoints $\{(0,1),(2,1)\}$ have the whole line as their affine hull, but every restricted ball around either endpoint contains missing nearby points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\operatorname{aff}(C)=\mathbb R^n$，与仿射包取交不会改变球，相对内部就等于普通内部。例如，填满的闭圆盘的这两种内部都等于开圆盘。定义不要求 $C$ 为凸集；非空集合也未必有相对内点：只含两个端点的 $\{(0,1),(2,1)\}$，仿射包虽是整条直线，但每个端点周围受限制的球都含有原集合缺少的邻近点。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Boyd and Vandenberghe, *Convex Optimization*, §2.1.3, printed p. 23 / PDF p. 37](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf#page=37): relative interior is interior in the affine hull, with the ball-intersection test and a lower-dimensional example. The book uses closed balls; the open-ball version here is equivalent because a closed ball of half the radius lies inside the open ball. The segment, endpoints, singleton, empty set and two-point examples are verified directly above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Boyd 与 Vandenberghe 第 2.1.3 节，印刷第 23 页／PDF 第 37 页：核对相对内部是在仿射包中取内部、球与仿射包相交的检验，以及低维例子。原书使用闭球；本文的开球版本等价，因为半径缩小一半的闭球包含在原开球中。正文直接验证线段、端点、单点、空集和两点集的结果。
