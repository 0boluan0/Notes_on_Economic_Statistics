# 双重差分法用对照组的同期变化构造处理组未处理时的反事实变化

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Difference-in-differences uses the comparison group's contemporaneous change to construct the treated group's untreated counterfactual change*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> **它解决什么：** 当一个组经历政策、另一个组没有时，用两组“前后变化的差”构造处理组未受政策时的反事实变化。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** When one group experiences a policy and another does not, DID uses the difference between their before–after changes to construct the treated group's counterfactual change without the policy.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有处理组的前后差还不够。处理后结果的变化里，既可能有政策作用，也可能有通货膨胀、季节、宏观周期等所有人共同经历的时间变化。DID 再减去对照组在同一时期的变化，意图扣掉这部分共同变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A before–after change for the treated group is not enough. It may contain the policy effect together with inflation, seasonality, the business cycle, and other changes shared by everyone. DID subtracts the comparison group's change over the same period in an attempt to remove that common movement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

处理组 $G=1$ 在 post 期受处理，对照组 $G=0$ 始终未处理。DID 为
$[E(Y\mid G=1,post)-E(Y\mid G=1,pre)]-[E(Y\mid G=0,post)-E(Y\mid G=0,pre)]$。
它用对照组变化估计处理组在无处理时本会发生的共同变化，目标通常是 treated 的 ATT。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Group $G=1$ receives treatment in the post period, while group $G=0$ remains untreated. DID is
$[E(Y\mid G=1,post)-E(Y\mid G=1,pre)]-[E(Y\mid G=0,post)-E(Y\mid G=0,pre)]$.
It uses the comparison group's change to estimate the common change the treated group would have experienced without treatment. The usual target is the ATT for the treated group.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个四格例子

假设政策实施前后，处理组的平均结果从 $20$ 变成 $26$，对照组从 $30$ 变成 $34$。第一个差分是 $6$，但其中有 $4$ 也出现在对照组。第二个差分给出 $6-4=2$。DID 的主张不是“处理组最后比对照组好”，而是“处理组比其由对照组趋势推出来的反事实多变化了 2”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the treated group's mean outcome rises from $20$ to $26$, while the comparison group's mean rises from $30$ to $34$. The first difference is $6$, but $4$ of that movement also appears in the comparison group. The second difference is therefore $6-4=2$. DID does not claim that the treated group ends at a better level. It claims that the treated group changes by 2 more than the counterfactual implied by the comparison trend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**具体锚点：** 新泽西上调最低工资、宾夕法尼亚未上调；DID 比较两地就业前后变化，而不是只比政策后水平。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Concrete anchor:** New Jersey raised its minimum wage while Pennsylvania did not. DID compares the change in employment across the two states rather than comparing only their post-policy levels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Card 与 Krueger 在政策前后调查新泽西和宾夕法尼亚东部的快餐店。这个例子最值得记住的不是某个估计值，而是对照组在设计中的角色：宾夕法尼亚的变化被用来代表新泽西快餐店若没有加薪政策时本会经历的变化。这个代表是否可信，取决于后续的识别假设，而不是由“四格相减”本身保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Card and Krueger surveyed fast-food restaurants in New Jersey and eastern Pennsylvania before and after the policy. The durable lesson is not a particular estimate but the role of the comparison group: Pennsylvania's change stands in for what New Jersey restaurants would have experienced without the wage increase. Whether that substitution is credible depends on the identifying assumptions; the four-cell subtraction does not guarantee it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 为什么 DID 要“再差一次”？在上面的四格例子中，若对照组也上升了 4，直接把处理组上升 6 全算成政策作用会犯什么错？
>
> **答案：** 处理组的前后差同时含政策作用和共同时间变化。第二个差分用对照组的 4 扣掉后者；否则会把共同变化误算成政策作用。

## 来源与核验

- Card & Krueger (1994), [*Minimum Wages and Employment: A Case Study of the Fast-Food Industry in New Jersey and Pennsylvania*](https://eml.berkeley.edu/~card/papers/min-wage-ff-nj.pdf)：核验新泽西—宾夕法尼亚的政策、样本与前后比较设计。
- Callaway & Sant’Anna (2021), [*Difference-in-Differences with Multiple Time Periods*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeconom.2020.12.001)：核验 DID 用对照组结果路径构造处理组未处理结果路径的解释，以及 ATT 作为目标参数。
