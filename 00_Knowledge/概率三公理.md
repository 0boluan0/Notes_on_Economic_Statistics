# The axioms of probability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*概率三公理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **probability axioms** are consistency rules for assigning probabilities to events. They say that probabilities cannot be negative, that the whole set of possible outcomes has probability one, and that probabilities add when events do not overlap. They do not tell us that a particular coin is fair: the probability assigned to heads is a modelling choice that must respect these rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**概率三公理**规定，给事件分配概率时必须保持怎样的一致性：概率不能为负，全部可能结果的总概率为一，而不重叠事件的概率可以相加。它们不会直接告诉我们某枚硬币是否公平；给正面分配多少概率，是必须遵守这些规则的建模选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a [[概率空间|probability space]] $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$, $\Omega$ is the set of possible outcomes, $\mathcal F$ collects the events, and $P(A)$ is the probability assigned to an event $A$. The [[σ-代数|sigma-algebra]] $\mathcal F$ ensures that complements and countable unions remain events. The axioms constrain the numbers assigned to them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[概率空间]] $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ 中，$\Omega$ 是可能结果的集合，$\mathcal F$ 收集模型中的事件，$P(A)$ 是分配给事件 $A$ 的概率。[[σ-代数]] $\mathcal F$ 保证取补集、可数并后仍然得到事件；公理则约束给它们分配的数字。

> [!abstract] Three requirements for a probability measure · 概率测度的三个要求
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Nonnegativity:** $P(A)\ge0$ for every event $A$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **非负性：** 对每个事件 $A$，都有 $P(A)\ge0$。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Normalisation:** $P(\Omega)=1$. The outcome must belong to the sample space.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **归一化：** $P(\Omega)=1$，试验结果必定落在样本空间内。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Countable additivity:** for any finite or countably infinite family of pairwise disjoint events, the probability of their union is the sum of their probabilities. “Countably infinite” means that the events can be listed as $A_1,A_2,A_3,\ldots$. In this notation,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **可数可加性：** 对有限或可数无穷多个两两互斥的事件，它们的并集概率等于各自概率之和。“可数无穷”表示可以把事件排成 $A_1,A_2,A_3,\ldots$ 这样的列表，写成公式就是
>
> $$P\!\left(\bigcup_{j=1}^{\infty}A_j\right)=\sum_{j=1}^{\infty}P(A_j),\qquad A_i\cap A_j=\varnothing\ \text{for }i\ne j.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Pairwise disjoint means that no outcome belongs to two different events, so at most one can occur. The union $\bigcup_j A_j$ means “at least one occurs”; the sum $\sum_j P(A_j)$ adds their probabilities. When infinitely many terms are present, their [[无穷级数|infinite sum]] is the limit of the finite partial sums.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两两互斥表示任何两个不同事件都不共享结果，所以至多发生其中一个。并集 $\bigcup_j A_j$ 表示“至少发生一个”，求和 $\sum_j P(A_j)$ 则把各自概率相加。若有无穷多项，这个[[无穷级数|无穷和]]指的是有限部分和的极限。

^core

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fair die, let $A=\{1,2\}$ and $B=\{3\}$. They are disjoint, so $P(A\cup B)=2/6+1/6=3/6$. Every result in the union is counted once. This is the simplest use of the third axiom.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于一枚公平骰子，令 $A=\{1,2\}$、$B=\{3\}$。它们不重叠，所以 $P(A\cup B)=2/6+1/6=3/6$。并集中的每个结果都恰好被计算一次，这是第三条公理最简单的用法。

## Familiar rules follow from these three
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*常用公式怎样从三条规则得到*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The empty event $\varnothing$ has probability zero. Since it is disjoint from $\Omega$ and adding it changes nothing, additivity gives $1=1+P(\varnothing)$, hence $P(\varnothing)=0$. For an event $A$, its complement $A^c=\Omega\setminus A$ means that $A$ does not occur. These two events are disjoint and together fill $\Omega$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

空事件 $\varnothing$ 的概率为零。它与 $\Omega$ 不相交，而把它并入 $\Omega$ 后集合不变；可加性因此给出 $1=1+P(\varnothing)$，即 $P(\varnothing)=0$。事件 $A$ 的补事件 $A^c=\Omega\setminus A$ 表示 $A$ 没有发生。这两个事件互斥，合起来又正好是 $\Omega$，于是

$$P(A)+P(A^c)=1,\qquad P(A^c)=1-P(A).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the [[补事件法|complement rule]]. Because both probabilities are nonnegative, each is at most one. Thus the familiar range $0\le P(A)\le1$ follows from the axioms rather than requiring an extra rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就是[[补事件法]]。因为这两个概率都非负，所以每个都不超过一。因此，熟悉的范围 $0\le P(A)\le1$ 可以从公理推出，不需要再加一条规则。

> [!warning] Overlap is counted twice by ordinary addition · 重叠部分会被重复计算
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On a fair die, let $A$ be “even” and $B$ be “at least $4$”. Both have probability $3/6$, but $A\cup B=\{2,4,5,6\}$ has probability $4/6$, not $1$. The outcomes $4$ and $6$ lie in both events. The [[互斥事件加法|general addition rule]] subtracts their overlap once. The third axiom requires disjointness, which is different from [[事件独立|independence]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 公平骰子中，令 $A$ 为“点数是偶数”，$B$ 为“点数至少是 $4$”。二者的概率都是 $3/6$，但 $A\cup B=\{2,4,5,6\}$ 的概率是 $4/6$，不是 $1$。原因是 $4$ 和 $6$ 同时属于两个事件。[[互斥事件加法|一般加法公式]]会把重叠部分减掉一次。第三条公理要求的是互斥，它与[[事件独立|独立]]是不同的概念。

## Where the countable condition matters
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*可数这个条件在哪里起作用*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the possible outcomes are the positive integers $1,2,3,\ldots$, and assign $P(\{n\})=2^{-n}$, meaning $1/2^n$. Each outcome is an event on its own, and these events are disjoint. The first $m$ outcomes have total probability $1-2^{-m}$; summing the [[几何级数|geometric series]] over all positive integers gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设可能结果为正整数 $1,2,3,\ldots$，并指定 $P(\{n\})=2^{-n}$，也就是 $1/2^n$。每个结果单独组成一个事件，这些事件两两互斥。前 $m$ 个结果的总概率为 $1-2^{-m}$；把所有正整数对应的[[几何级数]]加起来，得到

$$P(\Omega)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}2^{-n}=\frac12+\frac14+\frac18+\cdots=1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite additivity handles any fixed number of terms. Countable additivity additionally identifies the probability of the entire union with the infinite sum. On infinite spaces, finite additivity alone does not imply this stronger requirement; it cannot simply be substituted for the third axiom.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限可加性处理的是任意给定的有限项。可数可加性还进一步要求：整个无穷并集的概率，必须等于这个无穷和。在无限空间中，仅有有限可加性并不能推出这个更强的要求，不能直接用它替代第三条公理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, there is no probability measure making all positive integers equally likely. If their common probability were positive, a sufficiently large finite collection would have probability greater than one. If it were zero, countable additivity would give probability zero to their union, contradicting $P(\Omega)=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，不存在让所有正整数等可能的概率测度。若每个整数共有的概率为正，只要取足够多个，有限总和就会超过一；若每个概率都为零，可数可加性又会让它们的并集概率为零，与 $P(\Omega)=1$ 冲突。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This does not rule out a [[连续均匀分布|uniform distribution on a real interval]]. On $[0,1]$, each exact point has probability zero, but the whole interval has probability one. Its points cannot be put into a countable list. The axiom allows countable sums; it does not require adding probabilities over an uncountable family of singleton events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这并不排除[[连续均匀分布|实数区间上的均匀分布]]。在 $[0,1]$ 上，每个精确点的概率都为零，整个区间的概率却为一。区间内的所有点无法排成可数列表；公理允许的是可数求和，没有要求对不可数多个单点事件也这样相加。
