# 概率测度满足非负性、全集归一化和可数可加性

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A probability measure is nonnegative, normalised, and countably additive*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 在事件 σ-代数上，概率满足 $P(A)\ge0$、$P(\Omega)=1$，以及对两两互斥事件 $A_j$，$P(\bigcup_jA_j)=\sum_jP(A_j)$。这里的并允许有限或可数无穷多个事件。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Probability is nonnegative, assigns one to the whole space, and adds across finite or countably infinite collections of pairwise disjoint events.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

先由可加性得 $P(\Omega)=P(\Omega)+P(\varnothing)$，所以 $P(\varnothing)=0$。再用 $A\cap A^c=\varnothing$、$A\cup A^c=\Omega$ 得 $P(A^c)=1-P(A)$，见[[补事件法]]。非互斥事件直接相加会重复计数，见[[互斥事件加法]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Additivity implies the empty event has probability zero. Applying it to an event and its complement yields the [[补事件法|complement rule]]. Overlapping events cannot simply be added because their intersection is counted twice; see [[互斥事件加法|the addition rule]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
