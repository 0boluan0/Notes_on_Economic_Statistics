# 经营价值转为母公司股权价值须补入未计资产并扣除债务与非归母权益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bridge operating value to parent equity by adding excluded assets and subtracting debt and non-controlling equity claims.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确认[[企业现金流估值]]覆盖了哪些业务，再逐项判断“这个资产或权利是否已经算过、归谁所有”。[[企业自由现金流|FCFF]] 的现值属于所覆盖经营业务的全部资本提供者，并不直接属于母公司普通股。本卡的简式及 800 算例不包含优先股、养老金缺口及其他未列权利或义务；在该范围下，算到**母公司普通股与既有期权共同对应的股权价值**。分到每股由[[估值日每股价值]]接着处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First establish which businesses are covered by [[企业现金流估值|FCFF valuation]], then ask whether each asset or claim is already included and who owns it. The present value of [[企业自由现金流|FCFF]] belongs to all capital providers of those operations. The simplified formula and the example yielding 800 assume no preferred stock, pension shortfall, or other unlisted claim or obligation. Within that scope, the bridge ends at parent equity shared by common stock and existing options. [[估值日每股价值|The per-share allocation]] is the next step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以包含子公司全部经营的合并估值为起点，各项均用同一估值日、同一货币和一致价值口径：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Starting from a consolidated valuation that includes all subsidiary operations, use the same valuation date, currency, and consistent valuation basis:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$E_{parent}=V_{op}+C_{sep}+H_{sep}-D-NCI.$$

$C_{sep}$ 是未在经营价值中计入的现金资产价值；$H_{sep}$ 是另行估值、尚未计入的持股价值；$D$ 是对应合并范围的债务价值；$NCI$ 是子公司股权中不归母公司的部分。债务和持股的原始账面数并不自动代表其当前价值；[[净金融负债]]的管理报表分类也不自动等于这里的估值调整。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$C_{sep}$ is cash value excluded from operating value; $H_{sep}$ is separately valued holdings not already included; $D$ is debt value for the same consolidated scope; and $NCI$ is subsidiary equity belonging to outside owners. Original book amounts need not represent current values. The management-accounting classification of [[净金融负债|net financial debt]] does not automatically supply these valuation adjustments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若实际存在尚未处理的[[优先股]]，应按估值日市值扣除这部分权利；未交易时估计其市值，而不是直接使用账面金额。扣除范围还须与[[加权平均资本成本]]中的融资口径对齐，才能把剩余权益归到普通股与既有期权。已经通过其他一致调整排除的同一权利，不再扣一次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For outstanding [[优先股|preferred claims]] not yet handled, deduct their market value at the valuation date, estimating that value if they are untraded rather than mechanically using a book amount. Align the claim scope with the financing basis in [[加权平均资本成本|WACC]] so that the remaining equity belongs to common shares and existing options. Do not deduct a claim again if another consistent adjustment has already excluded it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

养老金缺口则先核对估值日尚未覆盖的经济义务及其补足现金，不能看到一个账面余额就机械扣除，也不预设统一税率调整。若填补同一历史缺口的未来支出已完整进入现金流估值，就不能再扣同一缺口；反之，在价值桥中另扣缺口时，也不能把同一补足现金再次列入预测支出。正常当期服务成本不等于填补这笔历史缺口，不能仅因预测含有养老金费用就认定缺口已经处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For pension shortfalls, examine the uncovered economic obligation and funding cash flows at the valuation date rather than mechanically subtracting a book balance or assuming a universal tax adjustment. If the valuation already fully includes future payments repairing the same historical deficit, do not deduct that deficit again. Conversely, a separate bridge deduction must not be combined with forecast deductions for the same catch-up payments. Current service costs are distinct from repairing that historical deficit, so the presence of pension expenses alone does not establish that the shortfall has been handled.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个独立算例：每年末合并 FCFF 为 100，永续不增长，匹配的[[加权平均资本成本|WACC]]为 10%，故经营价值为 1000；现金及未并表持股的收益均未混入这组 FCFF。另有现金价值 50、未并表持股价值 30、合并债务价值 200。子公司无债务、现金及其他权利，其经营与股权价值均为 400；母公司持有 80%，所以 $NCI=20\%\times400=80$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an independent example, consolidated year-end FCFF is 100 forever with no growth and a matching [[加权平均资本成本|WACC]] of 10%, giving operating value of 1000. This FCFF excludes returns on separately valued cash and unconsolidated holdings. Those assets are worth 50 and 30, and consolidated debt is worth 200. A subsidiary has no debt, cash, or other claims; both its operating and equity values are 400. The parent owns 80%, so outside equity is 80.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$E_{parent}=1000+50+30-200-80=800.$$

这 800 还不能直接除以普通股数，因为其中仍有既有期权的权利。若改用分拆路线，母公司自身经营价值为 $1000-400=600$，应算 $600+50+30-200+80\%\times400=800$；不能保留完整子公司经营价值后，又加一遍所持子公司股权。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The 800 still includes existing option claims and is not yet divisible solely among common shares. A sum-of-parts route gives parent-only operating value of 600, then $600+50+30-200+80\%\times400=800$. Keeping the entire subsidiary operating value and also adding the parent's equity stake would count the holding twice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，若把本例净债务明确定义为 $200-50=150$，可改算 $1000+30-150-80=800$，此后不能再加现金 50。现金分类须与 FCFF 的营运资金、现金收益处理一致；若起点已经是[[股权自由现金流|FCFE]]的现值，也不能再照搬 FCFF 路线扣一次债务。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If net debt is explicitly defined here as $200-50=150$, the equivalent bridge is $1000+30-150-80=800$; do not add the cash again. Cash classification must match working-capital and cash-income treatment in FCFF. If the starting value already discounts [[股权自由现金流|FCFE]], do not copy the FCFF bridge and subtract debt again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Damodaran，Cash, Cross Holdings and Other Non-Operating Assets，PDF15–18、40–47](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/papers/cashvaluation.pdf#page=15)：重开现金分开估值、净额桥、合并／分拆持股及价值口径；PDF15、40、41、47 已目视。1000→800 为独立构造并交叉复算的例子。采用其估值逻辑，不采用 2005 年会计分类描述作为现行准则。
- [Damodaran，Employee Stock Options and Restricted Stock，PDF24](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/papers/esops.pdf#page=24)：重开并目视既有期权与普通股共同分享股权价值的分配边界；具体每股计算由后续原子承担。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cash paper supports separate asset valuation, netting, consolidation boundaries, and value measurement; the specified pages were reopened and visually checked. Its 2005 accounting descriptions are not treated as current standards. The option paper supports the remaining allocation between existing options and common shares. The example is independent and reconciles across both routes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Damodaran，What should you subtract out to get to equity value?](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/valquestions/debttosubtract.htm)：重开首段的优先股交易市值／未交易时估计市值，以及养老金未拨足义务段；支持额外权利识别，不将账面数直接当估值扣项。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The preferred-stock paragraph and the unfunded-pension paragraph were reopened. They support traded or estimated market value for preferred claims and recognition of uncovered pension obligations, not a mechanical book-value deduction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Damodaran，Valuation，PDF86](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/country/val2dayGreece2025.pdf#page=86)：重开并目视原页，核对另扣养老金缺口与预测同一补足现金不能双计。正文对当期服务成本与历史缺口补足的区分，是限定“同一义务”的应用说明；不据此展开养老金精算或假定统一税收处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
PDF86 was reopened and visually checked for double counting between a separate pension-shortfall deduction and the corresponding forecast funding cash. Distinguishing current service costs from historical catch-up payments applies the same-obligation boundary; this does not extend to actuarial valuation or a universal tax treatment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
