# 证券市场线把 Beta 映射到模型期望收益而不是已实现收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The security market line maps beta to model-implied expected return, not realized return*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子关系
> 标准 CAPM 的证券市场线（SML）为
> $$E(R_i)=R_f+\beta_i\bigl[E(R_m)-R_f\bigr].$$
> 截距是无风险利率，斜率是市场风险溢价，横轴是 beta。它适用于单项资产和任意组合，因为定价对象是系统风险暴露；这与用总波动 $\sigma$ 描述有效组合的资本市场线（CML）不同。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The standard CAPM security market line is $E(R_i)=R_f+\beta_i[E(R_m)-R_f]$. Its intercept is the risk-free rate, its slope the market risk premium, and its horizontal axis beta. It applies to individual assets and portfolios because it prices systematic exposure. This differs from the capital market line, which relates total volatility to expected return only for efficient portfolios.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $R_f=3\%$、$E(R_m)-R_f=5\%$、$\beta_i=1.2$ 时，模型要求回报为 $9\%$。这个数字是给定模型与输入的期望/必要回报，不是对下一期实际收益的承诺。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $R_f=3\%$, a market risk premium of $5\%$, and $\beta_i=1.2$, the model-implied required return is $9\%$. This is an expected or required return conditional on the model and its inputs, not a promise about the next realized return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“实际收益在 SML 上方”只说明这一期出现正的模型残差，不能单凭一次实现值证明资产先前被低估或经理有能力。只有当分析师在当前价格下形成的**条件期望收益**高于模型要求回报，并且现金流预测与风险估计可信时，才形成模型意义上的正 alpha/低估候选；仍需面对模型和市场代理边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A realized return above the SML is merely a positive model residual for that period; one realization cannot prove prior underpricing or managerial skill. A model-relative positive-alpha or underpricing candidate arises only when a credible conditional expected return at the current price exceeds the required return, and even then the model and market-proxy boundaries remain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 某股票去年实际回报 20%，CAPM 事前要求回报 9%。能否直接断言它去年年初被低估？
>
> **答案：** 不能。11 个百分点可能是事后好消息或随机残差；低估是年初价格相对当时可得信息下期望现金流与要求回报的判断，不由一次已实现收益倒推。
