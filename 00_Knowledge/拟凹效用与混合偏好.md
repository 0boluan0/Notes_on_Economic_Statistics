# Quasiconcave utility and mixtures
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*拟凹效用与混合偏好*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A mixture of two consumption bundles contains some of each bundle. Quasiconcavity says that this mixture does at least as well as the **worse endpoint** in the consumer's ranking. If the endpoints are equally desirable, that guarantee applies to both. It allows indifference and does not promise strict improvement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个消费组合的混合，各取原组合的一部分。拟凹性保证：按消费者的偏好排序，混合至少不差于**较差端点**。若两端同样好，这个保证便同时适用于两端；它允许无差异，不承诺严格改善。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Bundles and the ranking represented by utility
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*消费组合与效用表示的排序*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $D\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be a nonempty [[凸集|convex consumption domain]], where the positive integer $n$ counts goods. A bundle $x=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ records their quantities. Convexity means that $z=tx+(1-t)y$ is feasible whenever $x,y\in D$ and $0\le t\le1$. Each quantity is mixed with the same weight $t$; this is an actual bundle, not a lottery that randomly delivers one endpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $D\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 是非空[[凸集|凸消费域]]，正整数 $n$ 表示商品种类数。组合 $x=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ 记录各商品数量。凸性表示：只要 $x,y\in D$ 且 $0\le t\le1$，$z=tx+(1-t)y$ 也可行。各商品都用同一个权重 $t$ 混合；得到的是实际组合，不是随机交付某个端点的彩票。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A real-valued [[效用函数|utility function]] $u:D\to\mathbb R$ represents a preference ranking: $x\succeq y$ means that $x$ is at least as desirable as $y$, and this holds exactly when $u(x)\ge u(y)$. Equality means indifference. These numbers are ordinal: their order matters, while a difference of ten does not by itself measure ten units of happiness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实值[[效用函数]] $u:D\to\mathbb R$ 表示偏好排序：$x\succeq y$ 意为 $x$ 至少与 $y$ 同样好，且恰好对应 $u(x)\ge u(y)$；等号表示无差异。这些数值是序数表示：重要的是大小次序，差十并不自动表示相差十单位幸福。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Which endpoint does the guarantee compare with?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*保证究竟在与哪个端点比较？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[拟凹函数|quasiconcavity definition]] requires every [[上轮廓集|upper contour set]] to be convex. Its equivalent [[拟凹性线段判据|segment criterion]] compares the mixture with the smaller endpoint value:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[拟凹函数|拟凹性定义]]要求每个[[上轮廓集]]为凸集。等价的[[拟凹性线段判据]]把混合值与较小端点值比较：

$$
u(tx+(1-t)y)\ge\min\{u(x),u(y)\},\qquad x,y\in D,\quad 0\le t\le1.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[拟凹性线段判据|segment inequality]] guarantees the lower endpoint utility. Equal endpoint utilities make that a bound relative to both; unequal ones do not. Linear utility between zero and ten gives five at the midpoint, disproving the stronger claim. Weak preference includes indifference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在凸消费域上，[[拟凹函数|拟凹效用]]满足 $u(tx+(1-t)y)\ge\min\{u(x),u(y)\}$。若 $u(x)=u(y)$，混合弱优于两端；若效用不同，只能保证不差于较差的那个。弱优允许无差异，不等于严格喜欢混合。

$u(z)=z$，端点 $0,10$ 的中点效用为 $5$，就否定了“任意混合都比两个端点好”。这是[[拟凹性线段判据]]的偏好解释，不是额外的平均效用要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see the equal-value case directly, set $u(x)=u(y)=k$. Both inputs belong to the upper contour set $\{w\in D:u(w)\ge k\}$, and convexity keeps their mixture there. If instead $u(x)<u(y)$, both inputs meet the threshold $u(x)$, so that is the shared guarantee. No comparison with the larger value $u(y)$ follows. At $t=0$ or $t=1$ the mixture is an endpoint, so even the equal-value case cannot promise strict improvement for every weight.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直接看等值情形：设 $u(x)=u(y)=k$，两点都在上轮廓集 $\{w\in D:u(w)\ge k\}$ 中，凸性使混合仍在其中。若 $u(x)<u(y)$，两点共同满足的门槛是 $u(x)$，所以保证也是这个较低值；不能由此推出与较高值 $u(y)$ 的比较。$t=0$ 或 $t=1$ 时混合就是端点，因此即使两端等值，也不可能对所有权重都承诺严格改善。

> [!example] Equal endpoints can improve or tie · 同效用端点可以改善，也可以持平
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $u(x_1,x_2)=\min\{x_1,x_2\}$ on the nonnegative quadrant, $(2,6)$ and $(6,2)$ both have utility $2$, while their midpoint $(4,4)$ has utility $4$. All upper contour sets are intersections of the quadrant with the conditions $x_1\ge k$ and $x_2\ge k$, so the function is quasiconcave. In contrast, for $v=x_1+x_2$, the same three bundles all have value $8$. Linear utility satisfies the criterion with equality along this segment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在非负象限上取 $u(x_1,x_2)=\min\{x_1,x_2\}$，$(2,6)$ 和 $(6,2)$ 的效用都是 $2$，中点 $(4,4)$ 的效用却为 $4$。各上轮廓集是象限与条件 $x_1\ge k$、$x_2\ge k$ 的交集，因此函数拟凹。反过来，对 $v=x_1+x_2$，同样三个组合的值都是 $8$；线性效用在这条线段上满足判据的等号。

## Why this is an ordinal shape restriction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么这是关于排序的形状限制*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replace $u$ by $v=g\circ u$, where $g$ is strictly increasing on an interval containing $u(D)$. Every pair keeps the same order and ties. Applying $g$ to the minimum inequality gives the same inequality for $v$, since $g(\min\{a,b\})=\min\{g(a),g(b)\}$. Thus this mixture conclusion does not depend on the chosen numerical scale. [[单调变换保持拟凹性|Monotone transformations of quasiconcave functions]] explain the shape result; a merely nondecreasing transformation may create new ties and need not represent exactly the same preferences.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把 $u$ 换成 $v=g\circ u$，其中 $g$ 在包含 $u(D)$ 的区间上严格递增，每对组合的大小次序和相等关系都不变。对最小值不等式应用 $g$，利用 $g(\min\{a,b\})=\min\{g(a),g(b)\}$，便得到 $v$ 的同一判据。因此混合结论不依赖所选数值尺度。[[单调变换保持拟凹性]]解释形状的保持；仅仅非减的变换可能制造新无差异，未必表示完全相同的偏好。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Quasiconcavity is weaker than the numerical average-value inequality for a [[凹函数|concave function]]. It needs no continuity or derivative. When two-good [[无差异曲线|indifference curves]] are sufficiently regular, their slopes can also express the preference shape; the additional conditions belong to [[拟凹效用的递减MRS|diminishing MRS]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拟凹性弱于[[凹函数]]对数值加权平均所作的不等式要求，也不要求连续或可导。二商品[[无差异曲线]]足够正规时，还可以用斜率表达偏好形状；额外条件见[[拟凹效用的递减MRS]]。
