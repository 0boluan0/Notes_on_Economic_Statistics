# 拟凹效用使混合不差于较差端点而同效用端点的混合不差于两端

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Quasiconcave utility makes a mixture at least as good as the worse endpoint, or both endpoints when they have equal utility*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在凸消费域上，[[拟凹函数|拟凹效用]]满足 $u(tx+(1-t)y)\ge\min\{u(x),u(y)\}$。若 $u(x)=u(y)$，混合弱优于两端；若效用不同，只能保证不差于较差的那个。弱优允许无差异，不等于严格喜欢混合。

$u(z)=z$，端点 $0,10$ 的中点效用为 $5$，就否定了“任意混合都比两个端点好”。这是[[拟凹性线段判据]]的偏好解释，不是额外的平均效用要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[拟凹性线段判据|segment inequality]] guarantees the lower endpoint utility. Equal endpoint utilities make that a bound relative to both; unequal ones do not. Linear utility between zero and ten gives five at the midpoint, disproving the stronger claim. Weak preference includes indifference.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
