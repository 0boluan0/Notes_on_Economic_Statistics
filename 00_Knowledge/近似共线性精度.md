# 在外生性和满列秩仍成立时，近似共线性不使 OLS 系统有偏，却会放大相关系数的方差并使其对样本和设定敏感
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When exogeneity and full column rank still hold, near multicollinearity does not systematically bias OLS, but it inflates coefficient variances and makes the affected estimates sensitive to samples and specifications*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心后果
> 近似共线性没有破坏系数解的代数唯一性；它让焦点回归量在剔除其他回归量后只剩很少变异。数据仍能算出一个答案，却缺少把相近解释分开的信息，因此单个系数区间变宽、符号和大小更容易随样本或控制集合改变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Near multicollinearity does not destroy algebraic uniqueness. It leaves little variation in the focal regressor after the other regressors are removed. A coefficient can still be computed, but the data contain little information with which to distinguish nearby explanations, so its interval widens and its sign or magnitude becomes more sensitive to samples and controls.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## FWL 把机制写出来
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*FWL exposes the mechanism*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $\tilde x_j=M_{-j}x_j$ 是把 $x_j$ 对其他设计列残差化后的向量。[[FWL残差化定理]]给出
$$
\hat\beta_j
=\beta_j+\frac{\tilde x_j'u}{\tilde x_j'\tilde x_j}.
$$
在经典同方差条件下，给定 $X$ 有
$$
\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_j\mid X)
=\frac{\sigma^2}{\tilde x_j'\tilde x_j}.
$$
其他回归量越能解释 $x_j$，$\tilde x_j'\tilde x_j$ 越小，方差就越大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $\tilde x_j=M_{-j}x_j$ be the residual from projecting $x_j$ on the remaining design columns. FWL yields $\hat\beta_j=\beta_j+(\tilde x_j'u)/(\tilde x_j'\tilde x_j)$. Under classical homoskedastic errors, its conditional variance is $\sigma^2/(\tilde x_j'\tilde x_j)$. The more completely the other regressors explain $x_j$, the less residual variation remains and the larger the variance becomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[零条件均值无偏性]]控制的是分子的条件中心；共线性控制的是分母所含的独立设计信息。把这两者分开，才能理解“无偏但几乎没有用”为什么是真实风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero conditional mean controls the conditional centre of the numerator; collinearity controls the independent design information in the denominator. Separating them explains how an estimator can remain unbiased yet be too imprecise to answer the question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 整体拟合稳定不表示每个系数稳定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stable overall fit does not imply stable individual coefficients*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若数据能稳定识别 $\beta_1+\beta_2$，却很难识别两者之差，那么改变样本时两个系数可以一升一降，而拟合值几乎不变。于是整体 $R^2$ 或联合检验可能很强，逐项检验却很弱；这只是可能出现的诊断模式，不是单凭结果表就能确诊的定理。其检验含义见[[回归联合显著边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the data identify $\beta_1+\beta_2$ well but identify their difference poorly, the two estimates may move in opposite directions across samples while fitted values barely change. Overall fit or a joint test can therefore be strong while marginal tests are weak. This is a possible diagnostic pattern, not a theorem that diagnoses collinearity from an output table alone; see [[回归联合显著边界|the boundary between joint and marginal significance]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 加入一个与 $x_j$ 高度相关但真正无关的回归量后，为什么 $\hat\beta_j$ 仍可无偏而标准误上升？
>
> **答案：** 在外生性仍成立时，估计误差的条件中心仍为零；但残差化后的 $\tilde x_j$ 变异减少，方差分母变小。
