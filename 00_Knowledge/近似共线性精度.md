# Precision under near multicollinearity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*近似共线性精度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two predictors can move almost together while their individual regression coefficients move in opposite directions. The fitted predictions may barely change. [[多重共线性|Near multicollinearity]] makes it difficult to separate those coefficients because the data contain little variation in one predictor after the other predictors have been accounted for. This card explains the precision mechanism, including the assumptions needed to turn that geometric observation into a variance statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个预测变量几乎同步变化时，它们的回归系数可以一升一降，而拟合预测几乎不变。[[多重共线性|近似共线性]]使这些系数难以区分，因为在排除其他预测列能解释的部分后，焦点变量只剩少量变动。本卡解释这种精度机制，并说明把几何事实转化为方差结论还需要哪些假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Isolate the variation that estimates one coefficient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## FWL 把机制写出来

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X$ be a fixed real $n\times p$ [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full-column-rank]] design, with $p$ counting the intercept if present. In $y=X\beta+u$, $\beta$ is the specified target coefficient vector and $u$ the model-error vector. Focus on column $x_j$ and regress it on all the remaining design columns. Call the resulting [[回归残差|residual vector]] $r_j$: it is the part of $x_j$ those columns cannot linearly fit. Let $\hat\beta_j$ be the OLS coefficient estimated for $x_j$. The [[FWL残差化定理|FWL identity]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $X$ 为固定的实数 $n\times p$ [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]设计，$p$ 在有截距时包含截距。在 $y=X\beta+u$ 中，$\beta$ 是指定的目标系数向量，$u$ 是模型误差向量。关注第 $j$ 列 $x_j$，把它对全部其余设计列回归，得到[[回归残差|残差向量]] $r_j$，即其他列无法线性拟合的部分。令 $\hat\beta_j$ 为 $x_j$ 的 OLS 系数估计。[[FWL残差化定理]]给出

$$\hat\beta_j-\beta_j=\frac{r_j^Tu}{r_j^Tr_j}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]; $r_j^Tr_j$ is the sum of the squared remaining predictor values. It is positive by full rank. If errors have [[条件期望|zero conditional mean]] $E[u\mid X]=0$ and finite second moments, the right side has mean zero: [[零条件均值无偏性|OLS remains conditionally unbiased]]. A small denominator does not shift that mean. It can, however, amplify error in the numerator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上标 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，$r_j^Tr_j$ 是剩余预测变量数值的平方和，满列秩保证它为正。如果误差满足[[条件期望|零条件均值]] $E[u\mid X]=0$ 且二阶矩有限，右侧平均为零，即 [[零条件均值无偏性|OLS 仍条件无偏]]。分母小不会改变这个平均中心，但可能放大分子中的误差。

> [!important] The variance needs an error model · 方差需要误差模型
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the conditional error covariance is $\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2I_n$, where $I_n$ is the identity matrix and $0<\sigma^2<\infty$, the errors are uncorrelated with a common conditional variance. Then
>
> $$\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_j\mid X)=\frac{\sigma^2}{r_j^Tr_j}.$$
>
> At a **fixed noise variance and comparable predictor units**, less remaining squared variation means a larger coefficient variance. Normality is not needed for this identity, and uncorrelated errors need not be independent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若条件误差协方差为 $\operatorname{Cov}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2I_n$，其中 $I_n$ 为单位矩阵、$0<\sigma^2<\infty$，则各误差不相关且条件方差相同，此时系数方差为 $\sigma^2/(r_j^Tr_j)$。在**噪声方差固定、预测变量单位可比**时，剩余变动的平方和越小，系数方差越大。这个恒等式不需要正态性，不相关也不等于相互独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a general finite conditional error [[协方差矩阵|covariance matrix]] $\Omega$, the formula is instead $\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_j\mid X)=r_j^T\Omega r_j/(r_j^Tr_j)^2$. The error variation in this direction also matters. A statement about near dependence alone cannot supply the true variance, and changing the noise model, units, target, or sample size prevents a simple unconditional comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若条件误差[[协方差矩阵]]为一般有限矩阵 $\Omega$，则公式变为 $\operatorname{Var}(\hat\beta_j\mid X)=r_j^T\Omega r_j/(r_j^Tr_j)^2$，误差在这个方向上的变动也有影响。仅凭近似依赖不能给出真实方差；改变噪声模型、单位、目标或样本量之后，也不能再作无条件的简单比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Four observations reveal the weak direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*四个观测展示薄弱方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take two centered columns $z=(-1,-1,1,1)^T$ and $w=(-1,1,-1,1)^T$. Their inner product is zero and each squared length is $4$. Construct $x=z+\delta w$, with a nonzero number $\delta$, and fit an intercept, $z$, and $x$. Regressing $x$ on the intercept and $z$ leaves exactly $r_x=\delta w$, so $r_x^Tr_x=4\delta^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取两个均值为零的列 $z=(-1,-1,1,1)^T$ 和 $w=(-1,1,-1,1)^T$。它们的内积为零，平方长度均为 $4$。用非零数 $\delta$ 构造 $x=z+\delta w$，拟合截距、$z$ 和 $x$。把 $x$ 对截距及 $z$ 回归，恰好留下 $r_x=\delta w$，所以 $r_x^Tr_x=4\delta^2$。

> [!example] The same noise, less separating variation · 同样噪声，更少的区分信息
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With $\sigma^2=1$, choosing $\delta=1$ gives coefficient variance $1/4$. Choosing $\delta=0.1$ gives variance $25$: one hundred times as large, with a tenfold sampling standard deviation. In the second design, $x=(-1.1,-0.9,0.9,1.1)^T$, nearly the same as $z$.
>
> If the observed outcome vector changes by $0.1w$, the fitted coefficient of $x$ changes by $0.1/\delta$. At $\delta=0.1$ that is $+1$, while the coefficient of $z$ changes by $-1$. The fitted vector changes only by $0.1w$. These are exact algebraic changes, not a numerical rounding error.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定 $\sigma^2=1$，取 $\delta=1$ 时，系数方差为 $1/4$；取 $\delta=0.1$ 时，方差为 $25$，扩大一百倍，抽样标准差扩大十倍。第二个设计中，$x=(-1.1,-0.9,0.9,1.1)^T$，与 $z$ 几乎相同。
>
> 若观察到的结果向量改变 $0.1w$，$x$ 的拟合系数改变 $0.1/\delta$。当 $\delta=0.1$ 时，这等于增加 $1$，而 $z$ 的系数减少 $1$；拟合向量只改变 $0.1w$。这是精确代数下的变化，不是数值舍入错误。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $\delta=0$, the two columns coincide and the separate slopes cease to be unique. For any nonzero $\delta$, uniqueness returns, but it need not return with useful precision. Accurate arithmetic and a stable solver help compute the unique answer; they do not reduce the statistical noise assumed above. The related numerical mechanism is explained by [[小奇异值放大噪声|directional amplification under pseudoinversion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $\delta=0$ 时，两列重合，单独斜率不再唯一；任何非零 $\delta$ 都会恢复唯一性，却不一定恢复有用的精度。精确计算和稳定算法有助于算对那个唯一答案，却不能减少上述统计噪声。相关的数值机制见[[小奇异值放大噪声]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Individual coefficients and fitted combinations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 整体拟合稳定不表示每个系数稳定

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the example, $\beta_z z+\beta_x x=(\beta_z+\beta_x)z+\delta\beta_xw$. Under the same spherical error model, the estimated sum $\hat\beta_z+\hat\beta_x$ has variance $\sigma^2/4$, whereas the separate $\hat\beta_x$ has variance $\sigma^2/(4\delta^2)$. When $|\delta|$ is small, the sum remains estimable with much less variance. Predictions at combinations resembling the observed design can remain stable while the individual slopes fluctuate. This does not guarantee precise predictions at new combinations that separate $x$ and $z$ substantially.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本例中，$\beta_z z+\beta_xx=(\beta_z+\beta_x)z+\delta\beta_xw$。在同一球形误差模型下，估计和 $\hat\beta_z+\hat\beta_x$ 的方差为 $\sigma^2/4$，单独 $\hat\beta_x$ 的方差却为 $\sigma^2/(4\delta^2)$。当 $|\delta|$ 很小时，斜率之和仍可用小得多的方差估计。单独斜率波动时，对接近观察设计的变量组合所作预测仍可能稳定；但这不保证在 $x$ 与 $z$ 明显分离的新组合上仍能精确预测。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A strong joint test alongside weak individual tests is one possible pattern, not a diagnosis by itself; [[回归联合显著边界|joint and individual tests]] ask different questions. Nor must a reported standard error rise in every comparison of fitted models. The classical estimate is $s/\sqrt{r_j^Tr_j}$, where $s^2=\mathrm{RSS}/(n-p)$ for $n>p$. Here RSS is the full-model residual sum of squares. Both $s^2$ and the coefficient can change between fits. Distinguish the true sampling [[标准差|standard deviation]] from its estimated [[标准误含义|standard error]], and from the realised test statistic or interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

联合检验很强、单项检验很弱，只是一种可能模式，不能单凭它确诊；[[回归联合显著边界|联合与单项检验]]回答不同问题。比较拟合模型时，报告的标准误也不必每次上升。经典估计是 $s/\sqrt{r_j^Tr_j}$，其中 $n>p$ 时 $s^2=\mathrm{RSS}/(n-p)$，RSS 为完整模型的残差平方和。不同拟合的 $s^2$ 和系数本身都可能变化。需要区分真实抽样[[标准差]]、其估计值[[标准误含义|标准误]]，以及实际得到的检验统计量或区间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an added predictor is truly irrelevant to a fixed target and the same spherical error model remains valid, the variance comparison is developed precisely in [[无关变量代价|the cost of an irrelevant predictor]]. It is a comparison of true variances under stated assumptions, not a guarantee that every sample's reported standard error increases. [[方差膨胀因子|VIF]] expresses a related design comparison while holding the focal predictor's total spread fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果新增预测变量对一个固定目标真正无关，而且同一球形误差模型继续成立，[[无关变量代价]]会精确展开方差比较。那是在给定假设下比较真实方差，并不保证每个样本报告的标准误都上升。[[方差膨胀因子]]则在固定焦点变量总变动时，表达一种相关的设计比较。
