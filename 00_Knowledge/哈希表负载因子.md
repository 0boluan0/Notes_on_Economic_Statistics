# 负载因子控制哈希表的平均桶长或探测压力

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The load factor controls average bucket length or probe pressure in a hash table*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子性能量
> 活条目负载因子 $\alpha=n/m$ 比较条目数 $n$ 与槽位数 $m$。拉链法中它恰好等于平均桶长；没有积累墓碑的开放寻址中，它控制空槽有多难找到。若删除留下墓碑，还必须另看“有效条目或墓碑所占槽位”的填充率。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The live load factor $\alpha=n/m$ compares the number of live entries $n$ with the number of slots $m$. Under chaining it is exactly the mean bucket length; in open addressing without accumulated tombstones, it controls how hard empty slots are to find. When deletion leaves tombstones, the fraction of slots holding either a live entry or a tombstone must also be tracked.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拉链表所有桶的平均长度恰好是 $\alpha$；在合适的随机哈希假设下，目标桶的期望长度才由 $O(1+\alpha)$ 控制。$\alpha$ 可以超过 1，但桶内比较随之增加。在没有墓碑的开放寻址中，每个槽直接容纳至多一个条目，所以继续插入时要求 $\alpha<1$；在均匀探测假设下，查找一个不存在的键或插入一个新键的期望探测次数至多为 $1/(1-\alpha)$，越接近满表，代价越陡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Across a chained table, the mean bucket length is exactly $\alpha$; only under a suitable randomized-hashing assumption is the expected target-bucket length controlled by $O(1+\alpha)$. The load factor may exceed one, but bucket comparisons increase with it. In tombstone-free open addressing, each slot directly holds at most one entry, so continuing insertion requires $\alpha<1$. Under uniform probing, an unsuccessful lookup or insertion of a new key takes at most $1/(1-\alpha)$ expected probes, rising sharply as the table fills.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若表中还有 $d$ 个墓碑，仅看活负载 $n/m$ 会低估探测压力。可另记填充率 $\beta=(n+d)/m$：查找不能在墓碑处停止，所以在重建清理之前，失败查找更接近由 $\beta$ 而不是 $\alpha$ 决定。不同实现也可能用后移删除等机制消除墓碑，此时应按实际槽位状态定义指标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the table contains $d$ tombstones, the live load $n/m$ understates probe pressure. Track a fill ratio such as $\beta=(n+d)/m$: a search cannot stop at a tombstone, so unsuccessful searches are governed more closely by $\beta$ until a rebuild clears deleted markers. An implementation may instead use a path-preserving deletion method such as backward shifting; metrics should match the slot states that implementation actually maintains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 阈值不是普适常数

具体实现在什么负载下扩容，取决于探测策略、桶结构、内存布局和性能目标。“达到 0.7 必须扩容”可以是某个实现的规则，却不是哈希表定义本身。通用判断是：要把负载维持在所选碰撞方案能够承受的范围，并通过测量验证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exact resize threshold depends on the probing strategy, bucket representation, memory layout, and performance target. “Resize at 0.7” may be a valid implementation rule, but it is not part of the definition of a hash table. The general requirement is to keep load within the range the chosen collision strategy can sustain and verify it by measurement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与辨别
> 为什么开放寻址中活负载不高，失败查找仍可能很慢？
>
> **答案：** 删除留下的墓碑不计入活条目数，却不能终止查找；若墓碑很多，填充率仍高，探测序列仍会很长。
