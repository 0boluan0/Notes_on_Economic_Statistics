# 抵押品是为债务履行提供担保并使债权人在约定条件下可依法处置受偿的资产

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Collateral is an asset securing a debt that a creditor may realize for repayment under the relevant contractual and legal conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里把 collateral 广义译为抵押品：借款安排将特定资产用于担保，借款人不按约履行时，债权人可依担保权利和执行程序获得偿付。不同法域的抵押、质押或其他担保形式不在此混作同一种法律结构。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Collateral is used here in its broad economic sense: identified assets secure borrowing, and failure to perform gives the creditor recourse through the relevant security rights and enforcement process. Mortgages, pledges, and other security arrangements are not treated as legally identical across jurisdictions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

房屋担保贷款是常见例子。房屋的估计价值、可强制出售后的净回收额和借款人失去房屋的代价可以不同；有抵押也不保证全额收回贷款。它同样不意味着债权人一见违约就能不经程序无条件取得整项资产，或借款人的全部[[净值]]都已成为担保物。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A loan secured by a home is a familiar example. Appraised value, net recovery after enforcement, and the borrower's cost of losing the home can differ; collateral does not guarantee full recovery. Nor does default imply unconditional ownership of the entire asset without process, or that all of the borrower's [[净值|net worth]] has been pledged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Sources and verification

- [ECB, What is collateral?](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/ecb-and-you/explainers/tell-me/html/collateral.en.html)，定义与住房贷款示例：支持抵押品作为履约担保的基本概念，不将其简述读作所有执行程序或全额回收保证。
