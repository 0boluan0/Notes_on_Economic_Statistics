# 共同先验约束玩家主观事前信念的一致性，不等于真实或研究者估计的数据生成分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A common prior constrains the consistency of players' subjective ex-ante beliefs; it is not the same object as the true or researcher-estimated data-generating distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心区别
> 共同先验 $\pi$ 是博弈模型中的 belief 对象：所有玩家的[[事中信念]]都应由它按自身类型条件化得到。数据生成分布 $P^*$ 则描述状态、类型或观测数据在现实或研究者统计模型中怎样产生。二者可以相等，但共同先验假设本身不推出 $\pi=P^*$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A common prior $\pi$ is a belief object in the game: every player's [[事中信念|interim belief]] is obtained by conditioning it on that player's type. A data-generating distribution $P^*$ instead describes how states, types, or observations arise in the world or in the researcher's statistical model. The two may coincide, but the common-prior assumption alone does not imply $\pi=P^*$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三个概率对象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three Probability Objects*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- $\pi$：玩家在博弈模型中共享的主观事前分布；
- $P^*$：研究者用来描述真实生成机制的概率律；
- $\widehat P$：研究者依据有限数据得到的估计分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- $\pi$: the subjective ex-ante distribution shared by the players in the game model;
- $P^*$: the probability law used by the researcher to describe the true generating mechanism;
- $\widehat P$: an estimated distribution obtained from finite data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

历史频率或估计结果不会自动成为玩家行动时的 belief；反过来，在模型中采用共同先验也不证明该分布准确描述现实生成机制。只有额外加入并论证正确认知、理性预期、校准或识别条件时，才能把这些概率对象相互对应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Historical frequencies or estimates do not automatically become the beliefs players use when acting. Conversely, imposing a common prior in a model does not establish that the same distribution accurately describes the real generating mechanism. Connecting these objects requires an additional and justified assumption such as correct beliefs, rational expectations, calibration, or identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
