# 逐时点边际分布即使完全相同也不能确定随机过程的跨时点依赖
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Identical one-time marginal distributions do not determine a process's dependence across times*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心边界
> 边际分布 $\mathcal L(X_t)$ 只描述单个时点。预测、持续性、共同极端和增量行为依赖 $\mathcal L(X_{t_1},\ldots,X_{t_n})$；即使两个过程的每个一维边际都相同，它们的有限维联合分布仍可不同。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A one-time marginal law describes one coordinate only. Prediction, persistence, joint extremes, and increment behaviour depend on joint laws across times, which may differ even when every one-dimensional marginal agrees.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $T=\mathbb Z$，取 $Z\sim N(0,1)$，并令 $Z_t\overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}N(0,1)$。定义
$$
X_t=Z,
\qquad
Y_t=Z_t.
$$
对每个固定的 $t$，都有 $X_t\overset d=Y_t\sim N(0,1)$。但当 $s\ne t$ 时，
$$
\operatorname{Cov}(X_s,X_t)=1,
\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(Y_s,Y_t)=0.
$$
更强地，$(X_s,X_t)$ 总落在对角线 $x_s=x_t$ 上，而 $(Y_s,Y_t)$ 的两个坐标独立。因此相同边际既不能确定依赖，也不能确定过程律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The constant-in-time process and the IID process have the same standard-normal marginal at every time. Their two-time laws differ completely: the first is supported on the diagonal, while the second has independent coordinates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> “每天的收益都服从同一个分布”是否足以说明每天相互独立？
>
> **答案：** 不足。相同边际只说明每一天单独看时分布相同；独立性是联合分布的性质。
