# Marginal distributions and temporal dependence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*边际分布与时序依赖*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Knowing the distribution at every individual time does not tell us how values at different times move together. A sensor could draw one random value and repeat it forever, or draw a fresh independent value at each reading. If both use the same distribution, a histogram for any one time cannot tell these mechanisms apart. Their predictions and changes over time are nevertheless different.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

知道每个单独时点的分布，并不能告诉我们不同时点的数值怎样一起变化。一个传感器可以随机抽一个值后永远重复，也可以每次读数都重新独立抽值。如果抽值时使用同一个分布，只看任何一个时点的直方图，就无法分辨这两种机制；它们的预测和随时间变化的方式却完全不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The single-time law $\mathcal L(X_t)$ is a [[边际分布|marginal distribution]], where $\mathcal L$ means “law”. The law of $(X_s,X_t)$ retains how two times are paired; more generally, [[有限维分布|finite-dimensional distributions]] describe any finite selection jointly. This is the time-indexed instance of the general fact that [[边际分布不定联合分布|marginals do not determine a joint law]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单时点的律 $\mathcal L(X_t)$ 是一个[[边际分布]]，其中 $\mathcal L$ 表示“分布”。$(X_s,X_t)$ 的律保留了两个时点怎样配对的信息；更一般的[[有限维分布]]则联合描述任意有限时点组。这是[[边际分布不定联合分布|边际无法确定联合律]]这一事实在时间指标上的体现。

> [!example] Repeat one draw or redraw independently · 重复一次抽值，还是独立重抽？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let time range over the integers $T=\mathbb Z$. Draw $Z\sim N(0,1)$ once. Separately, take an [[独立同分布|IID family]] $(Z_t)_{t\in\mathbb Z}$ with that same [[正态分布|standard normal distribution]]: mean $0$, variance $1$. Define
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 让时间取整数 $T=\mathbb Z$。只抽一次 $Z\sim N(0,1)$；另外取一族[[独立同分布]]的 $(Z_t)_{t\in\mathbb Z}$，也都服从均值 $0$、方差 $1$ 的[[正态分布|标准正态分布]]。定义：
>
> $$X_t=Z,\qquad Y_t=Z_t.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At every fixed $t$, both observations have law $N(0,1)$. Yet observing $X_s$ reveals $X_t$ exactly for all other times: it repeats the same number. Observing $Y_s$ leaves the law of $Y_t$ unchanged when $s\ne t$, because those draws are independent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在每个固定 $t$，两个观测都服从 $N(0,1)$。但观测到 $X_s$，就准确知道了所有其他时点的 $X_t$，因为它们重复同一个数；观测到 $Y_s$，则不会改变 $s\ne t$ 时 $Y_t$ 的分布，因为它们独立抽取。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## See the missing information in two coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*用两个坐标看见丢失的信息*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For distinct times $s,t$, the pair $(X_s,X_t)$ always lies on the diagonal $x_s=x_t$. The independent pair $(Y_s,Y_t)$ has a continuous joint density, so that diagonal has probability zero. Thus a two-time event distinguishes the processes even though every one-time distribution agrees:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $s,t$ 不同时，$(X_s,X_t)$ 总在对角线 $x_s=x_t$ 上。独立配对 $(Y_s,Y_t)$ 有连续的联合密度，这条对角线的概率为零。因此，虽然每个单时点分布都相同，一个双时点事件就能把两个过程区分开：

$$P(X_s=X_t)=1,\qquad P(Y_s=Y_t)=0\quad(s\ne t).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Their [[协方差|covariance]], which measures linear co-movement, also differs. Since all means are zero, covariance here is the expectation of the product. The copied pair gives $E[Z^2]=1$; independence gives $E[Z_s]E[Z_t]=0$ for the fresh pair:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

衡量线性共同变化的[[协方差]]也不同。由于均值均为零，这里的协方差就是乘积的期望。复制配对给出 $E[Z^2]=1$，而独立性让重新抽取的配对给出 $E[Z_s]E[Z_t]=0$：

$$\operatorname{Cov}(X_s,X_t)=1,\qquad\operatorname{Cov}(Y_s,Y_t)=0\quad(s\ne t).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The increments expose a practical consequence. $X_t-X_s$ is always zero, while $Y_t-Y_s$ has mean zero and variance $1+1=2$. A claim about changes cannot be recovered from the two separate marginal variances without their dependence. Likewise, persistence and the probability that two times are simultaneously extreme require joint information. In general, zero covariance alone does not establish independence; independence was part of the construction of $Y$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

增量展示了实际后果。$X_t-X_s$ 恒为零，而 $Y_t-Y_s$ 的均值为零、方差为 $1+1=2$。如果缺少依赖信息，就不能只凭两个单独的边际方差判断变化幅度。持续性、两个时点同时出现极端值的概率，也需要联合信息。一般情况下，零协方差本身并不证明独立；这里 $Y$ 的独立性来自模型的构造。

> [!question]- Do identical daily distributions imply independence? · 每天分布相同就独立吗？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. Repeating one draw already gives identical distributions every day and complete dependence. “Identically distributed” and “independent” are separate requirements in IID. To determine a real-valued process law, the [[有限维分布决定律|extension and uniqueness theorem]] uses the entire consistent family of finite-dimensional laws, not just its one-time members.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不。重复同一次抽值，已经让每天分布相同，却完全依赖。IID 中“同分布”和“独立”是两个独立要求。要确定实值过程的律，[[有限维分布决定律|扩张与唯一性定理]]需要整族一致的有限维分布，而非仅有其中的单时点成员。
