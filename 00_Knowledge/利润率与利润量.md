# 利润率下降可以与利润总量增加同时发生
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A falling profit rate can coexist with an increasing mass of profit.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Marx利润率|利润率]]比较利润与预付资本，利润量则是一定期间取得的利润总额。两者通过 $p=p'C$ 联系：即使每单位资本的利润变少，只要资本规模扩大得足够多，利润总量仍可增加。比较必须采用相同期间与资本估值口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Marx利润率|profit rate]] relates profit to advanced capital, whereas the mass of profit is the total over a specified period. They are related by $p=p'C$. Even if profit per unit of capital falls, a sufficiently large expansion of capital can raise total profit. Comparisons require consistent periods and capital valuation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用利润等于所生产剩余价值的分析条件，并假定每个比较期间都是一次可比周转。原典的一个例子以相同货币单位写成：初始 $c_a=4,v_a=2,s=2$；后来 $c_a=15,v_a=3,s=3$。两期剩余价值率都是100%，但
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Retain the analytical assumption that profit equals the surplus value produced and take each comparison period as one comparable turnover. One example in the text uses the same monetary unit throughout: initially $c_a=4,v_a=2,s=2$, and later $c_a=15,v_a=3,s=3$. The surplus-value rate is 100% in both cases, but
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p'_1=\frac{2}{4+2}=33\tfrac13\%,\qquad
p'_2=\frac{3}{15+3}=16\tfrac23\%.
$$

利润率减半，利润总量却由2增至3、增加50%。资本规模从6变18、扩大为三倍，超过了维持原利润量所需的两倍规模。原典以百万为货币数量单位；缩放单位不改变比率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rate halves while total profit rises from two to three, an increase of 50%. Capital triples from six to 18, exceeding the doubling required merely to preserve the initial profit mass. The original uses millions as the monetary unit; rescaling does not alter the ratios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，利润率下降还不足以说明可供[[Marx资本积累|积累]]的剩余额减少。反过来，利润量增加也没有证明全部剩余会被再投；消费与资本化的分配仍须另行观察。率、额和实际追加资本分别回答不同的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A lower profit rate therefore does not by itself show that the surplus available for [[Marx资本积累|accumulation]] has shrunk. Conversely, a larger profit mass does not establish that all surplus is reinvested; its division between consumption and capitalisation remains a separate question. Rates, amounts and actual additional capital answer different questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
