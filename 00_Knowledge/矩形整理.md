# 矩形整理是价格在近水平支撑与阻力之间反复往返的形态

矩形整理是在明确观察尺度下，价格反复接近一个近水平低点区和一个近水平高点区，两侧大致平行、没有持续收敛或扩张的价格形态。两区分别作为候选[[支撑位]]与[[阻力位]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rectangle consolidation is a price formation that repeatedly tests approximately level low and high zones at a specified observation scale. Its broadly parallel boundaries neither persistently converge nor expand, and serve as candidate [[支撑位|support]] and [[阻力位|resistance]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如价格多次在100附近回升、在110附近回落，可形成候选矩形；“附近”须用事先规定的价差或比例容差解释。它可看作近水平的[[趋势线通道]]，但只有一段从100涨到110的单向行情，没有往返检验，不能仅凭两个端点认定矩形整理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated rebounds around 100 and retreats around 110 can form a candidate rectangle, with “around” defined by a predetermined price or percentage tolerance. It resembles a near-horizontal [[趋势线通道|trend-line channel]]. A single rise from 100 to 110, without repeated tests, is not enough to establish the pattern.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩形可处在较大趋势的暂歇阶段，但形状本身没有确定未来离开方向，也没有统一最长持续期。若要把离开区间作为信号，还须固定边界、突破口径和确认时点；区间在突破前的存在，不等于此前已知道突破后会延续原趋势。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A rectangle may interrupt a larger trend, but its geometry does not determine the eventual exit direction or impose a universal maximum duration. Using an exit as a signal requires fixed boundaries, a breakout criterion and a confirmation time. Recognizing the prior range does not mean its subsequent continuation was already known.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
