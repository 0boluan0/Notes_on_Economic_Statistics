# 李嘉图的工资人口反馈以生活资料和资本扩张速度为条件

在 Ricardo 的历史模型里，市场工资高于 [[李嘉图自然工资|维持家庭生活的自然工资]]，会改善劳动者的生活与养育条件；劳动力人口随后增加，又对工资形成向下调整的压力。这条机制连接的是收入、家庭延续和劳动供需，并不是“雇主今天加薪，明天工资就跌回原位”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Ricardo's historical model, a market wage above the [[李嘉图自然工资|natural wage needed to maintain family life]] improves living and child-rearing conditions. A subsequent increase in the labouring population puts downward pressure on wages. The mechanism connects income, household reproduction and labour supply over time; it is not an immediate reversal of a wage increase.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定一条自然工资线。如果资本增加扩大劳动需求，而劳动供给尚未跟上，市场工资会上升。之后若家庭和人口反应增加了可用劳动，且劳动需求不再继续外移，工资才有向自然水平回落的压力。反过来，工资低于自然水平造成的贫困与人口变化，或新的劳动需求增加，也会影响恢复过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with a fixed natural-wage line. Capital expansion raises market wages if it increases labour demand before supply catches up. If later household and population responses expand available labour while demand stops shifting, wages face pressure to return toward their natural level. Below that level, privation and population change, or renewed demand for labour, affect the recovery process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**持续的资本扩张会改变这个过程。** Ricardo 特别指出：一轮新增劳动刚响应了资本带来的需求，下一轮资本扩张就可能又创造新的劳动需求，因此市场工资可以在不定长时期内一直高于自然工资。读课堂上的 $A\to B\to A'$ 一类路径时，应检查每一段究竟假定资本固定，还是资本仍在增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Continuing capital expansion changes the adjustment.** Ricardo explicitly allows market wages to remain above their natural level for an indefinite period: once labour responds to one increase in demand, another capital expansion may create new demand. When reading a classroom path such as $A\to B\to A'$, identify which steps hold capital fixed and which allow it to keep growing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自然工资线本身也可能移动。若人口与食品需求扩张遇到更难的农业生产条件，维持同一生活篮子需要更高的工业品工资；若农业技术改善或食品进口降低这项成本，压力又会改变。横轴用人口还是劳动量、纵轴用货币还是工业品工资，都要事先说明，不能拿同一条线同时表示几个口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The natural-wage line may itself move. When population and food demand meet less favourable agricultural conditions, maintaining a given basket requires a higher manufactured-goods wage. Agricultural improvements or cheaper food imports can change that pressure. Specify whether the horizontal axis measures population or labour and whether wages are in money or manufactured goods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是一套有条件的历史解释，不是现代所有社会的生育规律。判断它在某处是否适用，需要另外检查生育、死亡、迁移、劳动参与与资本需求的实际反应。它与 [[利润再投与劳动吸收|Lewis 模型的利润再投和招工机制]] 可以对照，但两者对人口反应和可得劳动的设定不能混用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a conditional historical account, not a universal modern fertility law. Applying it requires evidence on fertility, mortality, migration, participation and capital-driven labour demand. It can be compared with [[利润再投与劳动吸收|reinvestment and recruitment in the Lewis model]], but their assumptions about population responses and available labour are not interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ricardo（1821），[《原理》V.5–8、12–16](https://www.econlib.org/library/Ricardo/ricP.html?chapter_num=6#5.5)：支持劳动供需、工资与家庭人口反馈；V.8 明确允许持续扩张中市场工资长期高于自然工资。V.3、16 支持生活资料成本与习惯性标准可能变化。
- 课堂点位只说明阅读时应区分哪些条件在变化，不给出原典未提供的速度、年限或收敛方程；没有把当代人口反应当作已验证事实。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chapter V supports the supply-and-demand feedback and explicitly qualifies convergence under continuing capital expansion. The classroom notation is a guide to tracking assumptions, not a source for adjustment speeds or an estimated population equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
