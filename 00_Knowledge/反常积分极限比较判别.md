# 正函数比值趋于正常数时反常积分具有相同敛散性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Positive functions whose ratio tends to a positive finite constant have improper integrals with the same convergence behaviour*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 设 $f,g$ 在避开同一问题端点的每个有限截断区间上正常可积，并且在该端点附近 $f,g>0$。若
> $$\lim\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=c,\qquad 0<c<\infty,$$
> 则 $\int f$ 与 $\int g$ 同时收敛或同时发散。极限可以是任意有限正常数，不必恰好等于 1。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> Suppose $f$ and $g$ are properly integrable on every finite truncation away from the same problematic endpoint and are positive near that endpoint. If $\lim f(x)/g(x)=c$ with $0<c<\infty$, then $\int f$ and $\int g$ either both converge or both diverge. The limit may be any positive finite constant; it need not equal one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

理由来自直接比较：取足够小的 $\eta\in(0,c)$，当 $x$ 足够接近问题端点时，
$$(c-\eta)g(x)\le f(x)\le(c+\eta)g(x).$$
因此 $f$ 被 $g$ 的两个正常数倍夹住；乘正常数不会改变一个非负反常积分的敛散性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The result follows from direct comparison. For a sufficiently small $\eta\in(0,c)$, near the endpoint $(c-\eta)g(x)\le f(x)\le(c+\eta)g(x)$. Thus $f$ lies between two positive constant multiples of $g$, and multiplying a nonnegative improper integral by a positive finite constant does not change convergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如
$$f(x)=\frac1{x^2+3x},\qquad g(x)=\frac1{x^2},$$
则 $f/g=x^2/(x^2+3x)\to1$，所以两者尾部同收敛。极限比较刻画的是主导量级；它不会告诉你两个积分的精确值相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=1/(x^2+3x)$ and $g(x)=1/x^2$, the ratio $f/g=x^2/(x^2+3x)\to1$, so their tails have the same convergence behaviour. Limit comparison captures dominant scale; it does not claim that the exact integral values are equal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 极限为零或无穷时不能套“双向同敛散”

若 $f/g\to0$，则 $\int g$ 收敛可推出 $\int f$ 收敛；等价地，$\int f$ 发散可推出 $\int g$ 发散，但由 $\int g$ 发散不能判断 $\int f$。若 $f/g\to\infty$，则 $\int g$ 发散可推出 $\int f$ 发散；等价地，$\int f$ 收敛可推出 $\int g$ 收敛，但由 $\int g$ 收敛不能判断 $\int f$。除此之外的反向均无结论；必须回到不等式方向，而不是把所有比值极限都当成同敛散。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f/g\to0$, convergence of $\int g$ implies convergence of $\int f$; equivalently, divergence of $\int f$ implies divergence of $\int g$, but divergence of $\int g$ gives no conclusion about $\int f$. If $f/g\to\infty$, divergence of $\int g$ implies divergence of $\int f$; equivalently, convergence of $\int f$ implies convergence of $\int g$, but convergence of $\int g$ gives no conclusion about $\int f$. None of the remaining reverse implications follows. Return to the inequality direction rather than treating every ratio limit as an equivalence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> $f/g\to2$ 与 $f/g\to0$ 的结论为什么不同？
>
> **答案：** 极限 2 把两者互相夹在正常数倍之间，能双向比较；极限 0 只说明 $f$ 更小，只保留一个方向的信息。
