# Limit comparison for improper integrals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*反常积分极限比较判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Two positive tails can differ in shape but accumulate at comparable rates. **Limit comparison** tests whether their ratio approaches a positive finite constant. If it does, each function is eventually trapped between two fixed positive multiples of the other, so they have the same convergence behaviour. This uses [[反常积分直接比较判别|direct comparison]] without requiring a convenient inequality from the original starting point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两条正曲线的形状可以不同，却按相近的量级累积。**极限比较判别**检查它们的比值是否趋于有限正常数；若是，每个函数最终都被另一个的两个固定正常数倍夹住，因此同敛散。这利用的是[[反常积分直接比较判别]]，不要求从原起点就容易写出合适的不等式。

> [!example] Simplify the ratio before integrating · 先化简比值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $x\ge1$, compare $f(x)=1/(x^2+3x)$ with $g(x)=1/x^2$. Both are positive and continuous on each finite tail interval. Their ratio is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $x\ge1$ 上，把 $f(x)=1/(x^2+3x)$ 与 $g(x)=1/x^2$ 比较。两者都为正，并在每个有限尾部区间上连续。比值为
>
> $$\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=\frac{x^2}{x^2+3x}=\frac{1}{1+3/x}\longrightarrow1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[无穷远 p 积分判别|p-integral test at infinity]] gives $\int_1^\infty g=1$, so $\int_1^\infty f$ converges too. The ratio limit does not say the integral values are equal. For instance, $\int_1^\infty2x^{-2}\,dx=2$ while $\int_1^\infty x^{-2}\,dx=1$; their ratio is the perfectly valid constant $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[无穷远 p 积分判别]]，$\int_1^\infty g=1$，因此 $\int_1^\infty f$ 也收敛。但比值极限不表示两个积分值相等。例如 $\int_1^\infty2x^{-2}\,dx=2$，而 $\int_1^\infty x^{-2}\,dx=1$；比值恒为 $2$，同样符合判别条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The conditions and the reason they work
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*条件与理由*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work near one specified endpoint. At $+\infty$, assume real-valued $f,g$ are Riemann integrable on every $[A,R]$, for a finite $A$ and every finite $R>A$, and that both are positive for all sufficiently large $x$. The denominator must therefore be nonzero wherever the ratio is used. As in the [[反常积分|improper-integral definition]], convergence means a finite limit of proper finite truncations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只考察一个指定的问题端点。对于 $+\infty$，设实值函数 $f,g$ 在每个 $[A,R]$ 上黎曼可积，其中 $A$ 有限，$R>A$ 任意且有限；并要求充分大的 $x$ 处两者都为正。因此，使用比值的地方分母不能为零。与[[反常积分]]的定义一样，收敛指正常有限截断的值有有限极限。

> [!important] A positive finite ratio gives equivalence · 有限正常数给出双向结论
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $\lim_{x\to+\infty}f(x)/g(x)=c$ with $0<c<\infty$, then $\int_A^\infty f$ and $\int_A^\infty g$ either both converge or both diverge. The constant $c$ need not be one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $\lim_{x\to+\infty}f(x)/g(x)=c$，且 $0<c<\infty$，则 $\int_A^\infty f$ 与 $\int_A^\infty g$ 同时收敛或同时发散。常数 $c$ 不必等于一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[无穷极限与无穷远极限|meaning of the ratio limit]] lets us choose an error tolerance $c/2$. Beyond some finite cutoff $B\ge A$, the ratio lies between $c/2$ and $3c/2$. Multiplication by the positive $g(x)$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按[[无穷极限与无穷远极限|比值极限的含义]]，可选误差阈值 $c/2$。超过某个有限截断 $B\ge A$ 后，比值介于 $c/2$ 与 $3c/2$ 之间；乘上正的 $g(x)$，得到

$$\frac c2g(x)\le f(x)\le\frac{3c}{2}g(x),\qquad x\ge B.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\int_B^\infty g$ is finite, the upper inequality proves convergence of $\int_B^\infty f$. Conversely, if the latter is finite, the lower inequality gives $g\le(2/c)f$ and proves convergence of $\int_B^\infty g$. Multiplying a finite truncated integral by a fixed positive constant multiplies its limit by that constant; it cannot change convergence. The proper segment $[A,B]$ only adds a finite number, as explained by [[反常积分敛散的局部性|locality of convergence]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\int_B^\infty g$ 有限，右侧不等式证明 $\int_B^\infty f$ 收敛。反之，若后者有限，左侧不等式可写成 $g\le(2/c)f$，从而证明 $\int_B^\infty g$ 收敛。固定正常数乘有限截断积分，只会把极限也乘同一个常数，不改变收敛性。正常区间 $[A,B]$ 只增加有限数，这就是[[反常积分敛散的局部性]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use the ratio at the endpoint you are testing
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*比值必须趋向同一个问题端点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a finite right endpoint $b$, require proper integrability on every $[A,t]$ with $A<t<b$, positivity sufficiently close to $b$, and a ratio limit as $x\uparrow b$. At a finite left endpoint $a$, choose a finite $B>a$, require proper integrability on every $[t,B]$ with $a<t<B$, positivity sufficiently close to $a$, and a ratio limit as $x\downarrow a$. In each case the same bounds by positive multiples compare the one-sided truncated integrals. The argument also works on a left infinite tail as $x\to-\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于有限右端点 $b$，要求每个 $[A,t]$（$A<t<b$）上正常可积，在足够接近 $b$ 时为正，并取 $x\uparrow b$ 的比值极限。对有限左端点 $a$，选有限的 $B>a$，要求每个 $[t,B]$（$a<t<B$）上正常可积，在足够接近 $a$ 时为正，并取 $x\downarrow a$ 的比值极限。每种情形中，两个正常数倍的界都能比较相应单侧截断积分；对左无穷尾部 $x\to-\infty$ 也同理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, as $x\to0^+$, $[(1+x)/\sqrt{x}]/[1/\sqrt{x}]=1+x\to1$. The [[零附近 p 积分判别|integral of $1/\sqrt{x}$ near zero]] is finite, so $\int_0^1(1+x)/\sqrt{x}\,dx$ converges. This says nothing about the same function at infinity. If several endpoints or singularities are present, [[反常积分逐坏点拆分|check each separately]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $x\to0^+$ 时，$[(1+x)/\sqrt{x}]/[1/\sqrt{x}]=1+x\to1$。由[[零附近 p 积分判别]]，$1/\sqrt{x}$ 在零附近积分有限，所以 $\int_0^1(1+x)/\sqrt{x}\,dx$ 收敛。但这并不说明同一函数在无穷远的情况。若有多个端点或奇点，须[[反常积分逐坏点拆分|逐个检查]]。

## Zero and infinity leave only one comparison direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 极限为零或无穷时不能套“双向同敛散”

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the same positivity and local-integrability assumptions. If $f/g\to0$, then eventually $f\le g$; if $f/g\to+\infty$, then eventually $f\ge g$. Direct comparison gives the following conclusions. The integrals in the table refer to the same specified one-sided endpoint.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

继续保留正性与局部可积条件。若 $f/g\to0$，最终有 $f\le g$；若 $f/g\to+\infty$，最终有 $f\ge g$。因此直接比较只给出下列结论；表中的积分都针对同一个指定的单侧问题端。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Ratio limit | A valid conclusion | Equivalent contrapositive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $0$ | $\int g$ converges $\Rightarrow\int f$ converges | $\int f$ diverges $\Rightarrow\int g$ diverges |
| $+\infty$ | $\int g$ diverges $\Rightarrow\int f$ diverges | $\int f$ converges $\Rightarrow\int g$ converges |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 比值极限 | 有效结论 | 等价的逆否命题 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $0$ | $\int g$ 收敛 $\Rightarrow\int f$ 收敛 | $\int f$ 发散 $\Rightarrow\int g$ 发散 |
| $+\infty$ | $\int g$ 发散 $\Rightarrow\int f$ 发散 | $\int f$ 收敛 $\Rightarrow\int g$ 收敛 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The missing directions really are undecided. At infinity, $f=x^{-2},g=x^{-1}$ has ratio tending to zero but only $f$ converges. A zero ratio can also occur with two divergent integrals: take $f=x^{-1},g=x^{-1/2}$. For a ratio tending to infinity with convergent $g=x^{-2}$, choosing $f=x^{-3/2}$ gives convergence, whereas $f=x^{-1}$ gives divergence. All examples start at $1$, so there is no unrelated singularity at zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有列出的方向确实无法判断。在无穷远，$f=x^{-2},g=x^{-1}$ 的比值趋于零，却只有 $f$ 积分收敛；也可能比值为零而两者都发散，例如 $f=x^{-1},g=x^{-1/2}$。若比值趋于无穷，且 $g=x^{-2}$ 积分收敛，选 $f=x^{-3/2}$ 会得到收敛，选 $f=x^{-1}$ 则发散。这些例子都从 $1$ 开始，不混入零点处无关的奇点。

> [!question]- Why is a limit of two different from zero? · 极限为二与零有何不同？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A limit of $2$ eventually gives both a positive lower multiple and a finite upper multiple, so either integral controls the other. A limit of $0$ supplies only a useful upper comparison for $f$; there is no fixed positive lower multiple of $g$ that must stay below $f$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 极限为 $2$ 时，最终同时存在正的下倍数与有限的上倍数，所以两边能互相控制。极限为 $0$ 时，只留下对 $f$ 有用的上界比较；不能保证存在 $g$ 的某个固定正倍数始终低于 $f$。
