# 贫困者平均缺口是只对贫困人口计算的平均标准化短缺

贫困者平均缺口 $IGR$ 只问“已经被识别为贫困的这些人，平均离[[贫困线]]多远”。这里将距离除以贫困线以便比较，因此它是标准化比例，不是货币金额；其分母是贫困人口，而非总人口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The income gap ratio $IGR$ asks how far the people already classified as poor fall below the [[贫困线|poverty line]] on average. The shortfall is normalized by that line, so the result is a proportion rather than a currency amount. Its denominator is the poor population, not the whole population.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对等权个人，设 $q>0$ 人低于 $z>0$。与[[贫困人口比例]] $H$ 和[[贫困缺口指数]] $PG$ 的关系为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For equally weighted people, suppose $q>0$ fall below $z>0$. Its relationship to the [[贫困人口比例|headcount ratio]] $H$ and the [[贫困缺口指数|poverty gap index]] $PG$ is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
IGR=\frac1q\sum_{y_i<z}\frac{z-y_i}{z},\qquad
PG=H\,IGR.
$$

四人收入 40、90、120、150，$z=100$ 时，$IGR=(0.6+0.1)/2=0.35$，而 $PG=0.5\times0.35=0.175$。若使用人口权重，IGR 的分母改为贫困者权重之和；保持同一权重口径时，乘积关系仍成立。没有贫困者时 $H=PG=0$，但条件平均本身没有对象，不能用 $PG/H$ 做除零运算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For incomes of 40, 90, 120, and 150 with $z=100$, $IGR=(0.6+0.1)/2=0.35$, whereas $PG=0.5\times0.35=0.175$. With population weights, divide by the total weight of poor people; the product identity still holds with consistent weights. If nobody is poor, $H=PG=0$, but the conditional average has no population and $PG/H$ is undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读变化时要留意谁还在分母中。例如仅把收入 90 的人提高至 100：没人收入下降，$H$ 从 0.5 降到 0.25，$PG$ 从 0.175 降到 0.15；但剩下唯一贫困者收入 40，$IGR$ 反而从 0.35 升到 0.6。这是贫困者构成变化，不代表某个人变穷。IGR 应连同 H 阅读。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Track who remains in the denominator. If only the person earning 90 rises to 100, nobody loses income: $H$ falls from 0.5 to 0.25 and $PG$ from 0.175 to 0.15. Yet the remaining poor person earns 40, so $IGR$ rises from 0.35 to 0.6. That is a compositional change among the poor, not evidence that someone became poorer. Read IGR together with H.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
