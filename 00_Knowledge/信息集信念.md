# 信息集信念是在已知某信息集到达的条件下，对该信息集内可能节点或历史的概率分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An information-set belief is a conditional probability distribution over the possible nodes or histories within an information set, given that the information set has been reached.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心区别
> [[博弈信息集]]说明行动者无法分辨集合中的哪一个节点已经发生；信息集信念 $\mu(\cdot\mid I)$ 则量化他在已知到达 $I$ 时怎样分配这些可能性。它不是对“下一步行动”的概率，也不总是对类型的概率；只有在[[信号博弈]]中节点恰好对应发送者类型时，才可写成类型 posterior。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Core distinction*
>
> An [[博弈信息集|information set]] records that the decision-maker cannot distinguish which node in the set has occurred. The belief $\mu(\cdot\mid I)$ quantifies how the player distributes probability across those possibilities, conditional on reaching $I$. It is not a probability over the next action and need not be a probability over types; it becomes a type posterior in a [[信号博弈|signaling game]] only when the nodes correspond to sender types.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 信念系统的正式对象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The formal object of a belief system*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\mathcal H$ 是所有信息集的集合，一份信念系统是对每个 $I\in\mathcal H$ 指定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\mathcal H$ is the collection of all information sets, a belief system assigns to every $I\in\mathcal H$
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mu(\cdot\mid I)\in\Delta(I),
\qquad
\sum_{x\in I}\mu(x\mid I)=1.
$$

$x$ 是信息集中的节点或到达该节点的历史。概率的条件是“$I$ 已经到达”，因此不能把 $\mu(x\mid I)$ 解读为游戏开局时到达 $x$ 的无条件概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $x$ is a node in the information set, or the history leading to that node. The conditioning event is that $I$ has been reached, so $\mu(x\mid I)$ is not the unconditional probability, measured from the start of the game, of reaching $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当信息集只有一个节点时，belief system 仍然覆盖它，只是概率已经被唯一钉住；这个容易被忽略的边界见[[单点信息集信念]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A belief system still covers a singleton information set; its probability is simply pinned down uniquely. See [[单点信息集信念|belief at a singleton information set]] for this easily overlooked boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 在信号博弈中为何表现为对类型的 posterior
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why it appears as a posterior over types in a signaling game*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接收者观察信号 $s$ 后的信息集通常包含多个节点 $(\theta,s)$，每个节点对应一个可能的发送者类型。此时可把
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After observing signal $s$, the receiver's information set typically contains several nodes $(\theta,s)$, each corresponding to a possible sender type. In that case we can write
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p(\theta\mid s)
=\mu\bigl((\theta,s)\mid I(s)\bigr)
$$

写成“观察到 $s$ 后对类型 $\theta$ 的 posterior”。只要 $s$ 以正概率出现，posterior 将由[[路径上一致性|Bayes 条件化]]确定；若 $s$ 在候选策略下概率为零，则进入[[路径外信念]]的边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
as the posterior probability of type $\theta$ after observing $s$. If $s$ occurs with positive probability, the posterior is determined by [[路径上一致性|Bayesian conditioning]]. If $s$ has zero probability under the candidate strategy, the problem falls under [[路径外信念|off-path beliefs]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与事中信念的区别
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Difference from an interim belief*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[事中信念]]与信息集信念都属于条件概率，但前者条件于自身类型，后者条件于动态历史已经把玩家带到某个信息集。两者的精确区分见[[事中与信息集信念]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both an [[事中信念|interim belief]] and an information-set belief are conditional probabilities. The former conditions on the player's own type; the latter conditions on a dynamic history having led the player to an information set. See [[事中与信息集信念|interim versus information-set beliefs]] for the precise distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 同一个 $p$ 的含义必须由信息集决定
> 在 Beer–Quiche 中，$p$ 可表示“看到 Beer 后，接收者认为发送者是 weak 的概率”。它是 Beer 后信息集中 weak 节点的条件概率，而不是 Nature 在开局抽到 weak 的先验，也不是接收者选择 Fight 的概率。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *The meaning of the same $p$ depends on the information set.*
>
> In Beer–Quiche, $p$ may denote the receiver's probability that the sender is weak after observing Beer. It is the conditional probability of the weak node in the information set after Beer, not Nature's prior probability of drawing the weak type and not the probability that the receiver chooses Fight.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 在接收者的信息集 $I$ 上，$\mu(x\mid I)$ 表示“选择行动 $x$ 的概率”还是“已知到达 $I$ 时位于节点 $x$ 的条件概率”？
>
> **答案：** 是后者。信息集 belief 分布在信息集内的节点或历史上；玩家对行动的随机化属于行为策略，不属于这份 belief。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Self-check*
>
> At the receiver's information set $I$, does $\mu(x\mid I)$ mean the probability of choosing action $x$, or the conditional probability of being at node $x$ given that $I$ has been reached?
>
> **Answer:** The latter. An information-set belief is a distribution over nodes or histories within the information set; randomization over actions belongs to the behavioral strategy, not to this belief.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
