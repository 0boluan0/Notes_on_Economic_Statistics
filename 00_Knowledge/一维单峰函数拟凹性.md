# Single-peaked functions on an interval
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一维单峰函数与拟凹性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A single-peaked shape on a real interval rises toward a highest region and then falls away. “Rises” allows equal values, so the top may be a plateau. The useful consequence is about every height: two inputs at or above a threshold cannot have a below-threshold gap between them. This is [[拟凹函数|quasiconcavity]], which requires convex [[上轮廓集|upper contour sets]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实数区间上的单峰形状，先向最高区域上升，再向另一侧下降。“上升”允许函数值不变，因此峰顶可以是一段平台。它对每个高度都有一个结果：两个达到门槛的输入之间，不会夹着低于门槛的缺口。这正是[[拟凹函数|拟凹性]]所要求的[[上轮廓集]]凸性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Include the turning point on both sides
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两侧条件都包含转折点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $I\subseteq\mathbb R$ be a nonempty interval and $f:I\to\mathbb R$. An interval is a [[凸集|convex domain]]: it contains every point between any two of its inputs. “Nondecreasing” means $u\le v$ implies $f(u)\le f(v)$; “nonincreasing” reverses the output inequality. The sufficient shape condition is the following.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $I\subseteq\mathbb R$ 为非空区间，$f:I\to\mathbb R$。区间是[[凸集|凸定义域]]：任意两个输入之间的点都仍在域内。“非减”指 $u\le v$ 推出 $f(u)\le f(v)$；“非增”则把输出不等号反向。下面给出充分的形状条件。

> [!important] A complete shape condition · 完整形状条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> There is a point $c\in I$ such that $f$ is nondecreasing on $I\cap(-\infty,c]$ and nonincreasing on $I\cap[c,\infty)$. Both restrictions include the value at $c$. Then $f$ is quasiconcave on $I$. No continuity or differentiability is required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 存在 $c\in I$，使 $f$ 在 $I\cap(-\infty,c]$ 上非减，在 $I\cap[c,\infty)$ 上非增。两个限制都包含 $c$ 处的值。则 $f$ 在 $I$ 上拟凹，不要求连续或可导。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every input on the left has value at most $f(c)$, as does every input on the right. Thus $c$ is a maximizing input, but need not be the only one. Any point in a flat highest interval can serve as $c$. An included endpoint can also serve: one of the two restrictions is then only a singleton.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左侧每个输入的值都不超过 $f(c)$，右侧也是如此。因此 $c$ 是一个最大点，但未必唯一；最高平台里的任一点都可作 $c$。如果区间包含端点，也可用该端点作 $c$，此时一侧限制只含一个点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A nondecreasing-then-nonincreasing shape gives interval upper contour sets and hence [[拟凹函数|quasiconcavity]]. Flat peaks and nondifferentiability are allowed. The assumption concerns the entire shape, not merely uniqueness of the highest point. See also [[唯一峰值不推拟凹|the unique-peak boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若区间上存在分界点 $c$，$f$ 在 $c$ 左侧非减、右侧非增，则任意门槛以上的输入集合是一段区间、空集或整个域，所以 $f$ [[拟凹函数|拟凹]]。峰顶可以有平台，不要求可导。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why every threshold survives mixing
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么每个门槛都能保住*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose any $a,b\in I$ with $a\le b$, and any $z\in[a,b]$. If $z\le c$, then $a\le z\le c$, so nondecrease on the left gives $f(z)\ge f(a)$. If $z\ge c$, then $c\le z\le b$, so nonincrease on the right gives $f(z)\ge f(b)$. At $z=c$ both arguments are valid. In every case,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任取 $a,b\in I$ 且 $a\le b$，再任取 $z\in[a,b]$。若 $z\le c$，则 $a\le z\le c$，左侧非减给出 $f(z)\ge f(a)$；若 $z\ge c$，则 $c\le z\le b$，右侧非增给出 $f(z)\ge f(b)$。$z=c$ 时两种论证都可用。因此总有

$$f(z)\ge\min\{f(a),f(b)\}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the [[拟凹性线段判据|segment criterion]]. To see the all-threshold conclusion directly, fix any real $k$ and take $a,b$ with $f(a),f(b)\ge k$. Every intervening $z$ then also has $f(z)\ge k$. Thus $\{x\in I:f(x)\ge k\}$ contains every point between any two of its members, so it is an interval when nonempty and is convex even when empty. Since $k$ was arbitrary, all upper contour sets are convex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这正是[[拟凹性线段判据]]。也可直接看全部门槛：固定任意实数 $k$，若 $f(a),f(b)\ge k$，则中间每个 $z$ 也满足 $f(z)\ge k$。因此 $\{x\in I:f(x)\ge k\}$ 包含其任意两点之间的全部点；非空时它是区间，空集也凸。由于 $k$ 任意，所有上轮廓集都凸。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A flat peak with corners
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有拐角的平顶例子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the whole real line, define a ramp, a flat top, and a descending ramp:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在整条实线上，把一段上坡、平顶与下坡接起来：

$$f(x)=\begin{cases}x+1,&x<0,\\1,&0\le x\le2,\\3-x,&x>2.\end{cases}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Any $c\in[0,2]$ satisfies the shape condition. The maximum is 1, attained throughout $[0,2]$. For $k>1$ the upper set is empty. For $k\le1$, solving the two ramp inequalities gives exactly $[k-1,3-k]$: for example, threshold 1 gives $[0,2]$, and threshold 0 gives $[-1,3]$. Corners at 0 and 2 do not affect this proof. The plateau also shows why the conclusion is weak: mixtures of distinct top inputs need not strictly improve their value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任意 $c\in[0,2]$ 都满足形状条件。最大值为 1，在整个 $[0,2]$ 上取得。$k>1$ 时上轮廓集为空；$k\le1$ 时，解两侧斜坡上的不等式，恰得 $[k-1,3-k]$。例如门槛 1 给出 $[0,2]$，门槛 0 给出 $[-1,3]$。0 与 2 处的拐角不影响论证。平台也说明结论是弱的：两个不同峰顶输入的混合，不必严格改善函数值。

## Endpoints, jumps, and an unattained peak
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*端点、跳跃与未取得的峰*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A function that is nondecreasing or nonincreasing on its entire interval is covered separately by [[一维单调函数的拟凹拟凸性|the monotonicity argument]]. This does not require an attained peak. For example, $f(x)=x$ on $(0,1)$ is quasiconcave although there is no maximizing input in the domain. It would be incorrect to choose $c=1\notin I$ under the displayed turning-point condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若函数在整个区间上非减或非增，可直接使用[[一维单调函数的拟凹拟凸性|单调性论证]]，不要求峰值实际取得。例如 $(0,1)$ 上的 $f(x)=x$ 拟凹，却没有域内最大点；不能在上面的转折点条件中偷偷选取 $c=1\notin I$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Discontinuity is allowed too: $f(x)=1$ on $[0,1]$ and $f(x)=0$ elsewhere on $\mathbb R$ satisfies the condition with $c=1/2$. Its upper sets are the whole line for $k\le0$, $[0,1]$ for $0<k\le1$, and empty for $k>1$. A one-point domain is immediate; if the empty domain is admitted, quasiconcavity is vacuous there, without needing a point $c$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也允许不连续：令实线上的 $f(x)$ 在 $[0,1]$ 内等于 1，其余地方等于 0，取 $c=1/2$ 就满足条件。上轮廓集在 $k\le0$ 时是全实线，在 $0<k\le1$ 时是 $[0,1]$，在 $k>1$ 时为空。单点域显然成立；若允许空域，其拟凹性真空成立，无需选点 $c$。

> [!warning] Do not omit the value at the turning point · 不要漏掉转折点的值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $[-1,1]$, let $h(0)=0$ and $h(x)=1$ for $x\ne0$. Each open side of 0 is constant, but the upper set at threshold 1 omits 0 between $-1$ and 1. This is not quasiconcave. Including $c$ in both monotonicity restrictions rules out that dip.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[-1,1]$ 上，令 $h(0)=0$，其余 $h(x)=1$。零点两侧的开区间上各自都是常数，但门槛 1 的上轮廓集遗漏了 $-1$ 与 1 之间的零点，因此不拟凹。两侧单调条件都包含 $c$，正是为了排除这种下凹缺口。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The one-dimensional order is essential to the proof. [[唯一峰值不推拟凹|A unique highest point]] alone does not control lower thresholds; in several dimensions it supplies no rise-then-fall ordering along every segment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“单峰”在这里指完整的先升后降结构；仅有唯一全局最大点不足以保证低水平的上轮廓集连通。多维的[[唯一峰值不推拟凹]]同样提醒：定位最高点不等于控制全部轮廓集。
