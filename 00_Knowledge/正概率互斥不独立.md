# Mutual exclusion and independence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*互斥与独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Mutually exclusive** events cannot happen together. **Independent** events satisfy a different requirement: learning that one happened leaves the probability of the other unchanged, when that conditional probability is defined. If two events each have positive probability but cannot happen together, learning that one happened rules out the other. That is why such events are dependent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**互斥**表示两个事件不能同时发生。**独立**要求的是另一件事：在相应条件概率有定义时，知道其中一个发生，不会改变另一个的概率。如果两个事件各自都有正概率，却不能同时发生，那么知道一个发生，就排除了另一个。因此，这样的事件不独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An event is a set of outcomes, and $P(A)$ denotes the probability of event $A$. The intersection $A\cap B$ contains outcomes satisfying both events. Mutual exclusion means $A\cap B=\varnothing$, where $\varnothing$ is the empty set. In contrast, [[事件独立|independence]] means $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

事件是一组结果，$P(A)$ 表示事件 $A$ 的概率。交集 $A\cap B$ 包含同时符合两个事件的结果。互斥意味着 $A\cap B=\varnothing$，其中 $\varnothing$ 是空集。[[事件独立|独立]]要求的则是 $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)$。

> [!example] Two different faces on one roll · 同一次投掷的两个不同点数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On one fair die roll, let $A=\{1\}$ and $B=\{2\}$. Both have probability $1/6$, but no outcome belongs to both:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 同一次公平骰子投掷中，令 $A=\{1\}$、$B=\{2\}$。二者的概率都是 $1/6$，但没有任何结果同时属于它们：
>
> $$P(A\cap B)=0,\qquad P(A)P(B)=\frac16\times\frac16=\frac1{36}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The two sides differ, so the events are dependent. Once the result is known to be $1$, the chance of $2$ becomes $0$, down from $1/6$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两边不同，所以两个事件不独立。知道点数为 $1$ 后，点数为 $2$ 的概率从 $1/6$ 变成了 $0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same reasoning works whenever $A\cap B=\varnothing$ and both $P(A)>0$ and $P(B)>0$. The intersection has probability zero, but the product is positive:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只要 $A\cap B=\varnothing$，且 $P(A)>0$、$P(B)>0$ 同时成立，同样的推理就适用。交集概率为零，概率乘积却为正：

$$P(A\cap B)=0\ne P(A)P(B).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using [[条件概率|conditional probability]] makes the change explicit: $P(B\mid A)=P(A\cap B)/P(A)=0$, which differs from $P(B)>0$. For calculations, keep the roles separate: [[互斥事件加法|disjointness permits adding probabilities without subtracting overlap]], while independence permits multiplying probabilities for an intersection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用[[条件概率]]可以直接写出这种改变：$P(B\mid A)=P(A\cap B)/P(A)=0$，与原先的 $P(B)>0$ 不同。在计算中也应分清各自作用：[[互斥事件加法|互斥允许直接相加概率而无须扣除重叠]]；独立则允许用概率乘积计算交集。

## 零概率边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The zero-probability boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The positive-probability qualification matters. If $P(A)=0$, then $A\cap B$ is contained in $A$, so its probability is also zero. Therefore, for every event $B$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正概率这个条件不能省略。若 $P(A)=0$，由于 $A\cap B$ 包含在 $A$ 中，它的概率也为零。因此，对任何事件 $B$ 都有

$$P(A\cap B)=0=P(A)P(B).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By the product definition, a zero-probability event is independent of every event, including itself. It can therefore be both disjoint from another event and independent of it. The empty event provides a simple example; [[零概率不等于不可能|a zero-probability event need not be empty]] in a continuous model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按照乘积定义，零概率事件与任何事件都独立，甚至与自己独立。因此，它可以既与另一个事件互斥，又与之独立。空事件就是一个简单例子；在连续模型中，[[零概率不等于不可能|零概率事件也未必为空]]。

> [!warning] Do not divide by a zero probability · 不要除以零概率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $P(A)=0$, the elementary ratio defining $P(B\mid A)$ is undefined. Use the product definition of independence at this boundary. The conditional-probability explanation above applies when the conditioning event has positive probability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $P(A)=0$，定义 $P(B\mid A)$ 的初等比值没有意义。这个边界应使用独立性的乘积定义；前面的条件概率解释适用于条件事件概率为正的情形。
