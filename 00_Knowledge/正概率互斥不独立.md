# 正概率互斥事件不可能独立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mutually exclusive events with positive probabilities cannot be independent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 若 $A\cap B=\varnothing$ 且 $P(A)>0,P(B)>0$，则
> $$P(A\cap B)=0\neq P(A)P(B).$$
> 所以 $A$ 与 $B$ 不独立。观察到其中一个事件发生，会把另一个事件的条件概率降为零。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> If $A\cap B=\varnothing$ and $P(A)>0,P(B)>0$, then
> $$P(A\cap B)=0\neq P(A)P(B).$$
> Therefore $A$ and $B$ are not independent. Observing either event makes the conditional probability of the other zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 零概率边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Zero-probability boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“互斥必不独立”缺少正概率条件。若 $P(A)=0$，则 $P(A\cap B)=0=P(A)P(B)$，按照乘积分解定义，$A$ 与任意事件 $B$ 独立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Mutually exclusive events are never independent” is missing a positive-probability condition. If $P(A)=0$, then $P(A\cap B)=0=P(A)P(B)$, so by the factorisation definition $A$ is independent of every event $B$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两个正概率事件互斥时，独立性等式的哪一边失败？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When two positive-probability events are mutually exclusive, which side of the independence equation fails?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 交集概率为 $0$，但边际概率乘积为正，所以两边不相等。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The intersection probability is zero while the product of the marginal probabilities is positive, so the two sides differ.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
