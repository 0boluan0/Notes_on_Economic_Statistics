# 更新过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Renewal processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 描述设备更换、重复服务或故障等事件之间具有一般 IID 间隔时，累计事件数和长期奖励怎样增长。
> **具体锚点：** 灯泡坏后立即换新，每只寿命独立同分布但不必为指数；更换次数形成更新过程。
> **核心难点：** 离开指数间隔后无记忆性消失，年龄、剩余寿命和总寿命是不同随机量。
> **为什么重要：** 可靠性、库存、排队、保险和长期平均成本都需要超越 Poisson 的到达模型。
> **继续：** 先从 [[泊松过程]] 理解指数间隔特例，再学习更新函数、更新奖励和 inspection paradox。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It describes cumulative event counts and long-run rewards when successive replacements, services, or failures are separated by general iid intervals.
> **Concrete anchor:** A failed light bulb is replaced immediately. Bulb lifetimes are iid but need not be exponential, and the replacement count forms a renewal process.
> **Central difficulty:** Once exponential interarrivals are abandoned, memorylessness disappears; age, residual life, and total lifetime become different random quantities.
> **Why it matters:** Reliability, inventory, queueing, insurance, and long-run average cost require arrival models more general than Poisson.
> **Continue with:** Begin with the exponential special case in [[泊松过程|the Poisson process]], then study the renewal function, renewal rewards, and the inspection paradox.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 更新过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The renewal-process definition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更新过程只要求正的 IID 间隔，Poisson 是指数间隔特例。更新函数 $m(t)=E[N(t)]$ 通常不再线性；长期在有限均值条件下 $N(t)/t\to1/E[X]$。无记忆性消失后，年龄、剩余寿命和总寿命必须区分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A renewal process requires only positive iid interarrival times, with the Poisson process as the exponential special case. The renewal function $m(t)=E[N(t)]$ is generally no longer linear. Under a finite-mean condition, $N(t)/t\to1/E[X]$ in the long run. Without memorylessness, age, residual life, and total lifetime must be distinguished.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令间隔 $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ 为正 IID，更新时刻 $S_n=\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$，计数 $N(t)=\max\{n:S_n\le t\}$。$N(t)\ge n$ 与 $S_n\le t$ 等价，但 $N(t)$ 的增量一般不独立，因为当前年龄携带了关于下一次更新的信息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the positive iid intervals be $X_1,X_2,\ldots$, define renewal epochs $S_n=\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$, and let $N(t)=\max\{n:S_n\le t\}$. The events $N(t)\ge n$ and $S_n\le t$ are equivalent, but increments of $N(t)$ are generally dependent because the current age contains information about the next renewal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 更新函数与更新方程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The renewal function and renewal equation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对间隔分布 F，更新函数满足 $m(t)=F(t)+\int_0^t m(t-x)dF(x)$：要么第一次更新在 t 前发生一次，要么在首次更新时间 x 后重新开始。这个递归结构而非简单的 $\lambda t$ 是一般更新计算的核心。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For interarrival distribution $F$, the renewal function satisfies $m(t)=F(t)+\int_0^t m(t-x)dF(x)$. Either the first renewal occurs before $t$, or the process restarts after the first renewal at time $x$. This recursive structure, rather than a simple $\lambda t$, is central to general renewal calculations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长期基本结论是更新率趋于 $1/\mu$，其中 $\mu=E[X]$。它给增长斜率而非精确有限时点均值；把 $m(t)$ 在所有 t 上直接替换为 $t/\mu$ 会忽略初始和边界效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The fundamental long-run result is that the renewal rate approaches $1/\mu$, where $\mu=E[X]$. This gives an asymptotic slope, not the exact finite-time mean. Replacing $m(t)$ by $t/\mu$ for every $t$ ignores initial and boundary effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 更新奖励
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Renewal rewards*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若每个周期产生 IID 或适当可积奖励 $R_i$，长期单位时间奖励趋于 $E[R]/E[X]$。关键是以完整周期为单位定义分子和分母；维修成本、正常运行时间或库存缺货损失都可放入同一框架。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If each cycle produces an iid or suitably integrable reward $R_i$, the long-run reward per unit time approaches $E[R]/E[X]$. The numerator and denominator must be defined over the same complete cycle. Maintenance cost, uptime, and stockout loss can all be placed in this framework.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：预防性更换
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: preventive replacement*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设备在故障时更换成本 $C_f$，若活到年龄 T 则预防更换成本 $C_p$。一个周期长度为 $L=\min(X,T)$，周期成本为 $C=C_f\mathbf1\{X<T\}+C_p\mathbf1\{X\ge T\}$。长期平均成本率为 $E[C]/E[L]$，应对 T 优化这个比率，而不是只最小化单次更换成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose replacement after failure costs $C_f$, while preventive replacement at age $T$ costs $C_p$. A cycle has length $L=\min(X,T)$ and cost $C=C_f\mathbf1\{X<T\}+C_p\mathbf1\{X\ge T\}$. The long-run average cost rate is $E[C]/E[L]$. The decision variable $T$ should minimise this ratio, not merely the cost of one replacement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 年龄、剩余寿命与 inspection paradox
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Age, residual life, and the inspection paradox*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机时点更可能落在较长周期内，所以“正在经历的总寿命”并不服从原始间隔分布，而是长度偏置。乘客随机到站看到的平均剩余等待可能超过 $E[X]/2$；只有指数分布因无记忆性保持相同剩余寿命分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A randomly chosen observation time is more likely to fall in a long cycle, so the total lifetime currently being experienced is not distributed like an original interarrival; it is length-biased. A passenger arriving at a random time may face an expected residual wait greater than $E[X]/2$. Only the exponential distribution retains the same residual-life distribution through memorylessness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 误区与失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Misconceptions and failure diagnostics*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检查间隔是否可视为独立同分布、是否存在零或负间隔、分布均值是否有限、以及每次更新后系统是否真正重置。老化、季节性、维修质量依赖历史或共同环境冲击都会破坏普通更新假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check whether interarrival times are plausibly iid, whether zero or negative intervals occur, whether the mean is finite, and whether the system genuinely resets after each renewal. Ageing, seasonality, repair quality that depends on history, or common environmental shocks all violate the ordinary renewal assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 更新过程比 Poisson 过程放宽了什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does a renewal process relax relative to a Poisson process?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 间隔仍 IID，但可来自任意正分布，不必指数，因此不再有无记忆性和线性更新函数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Interarrival times remain iid but may follow any positive distribution rather than being exponential, so memorylessness and a linear renewal function are lost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么长期更新率是 $1/E[X]$ 而不是 $E[1/X]$？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is the long-run renewal rate $1/E[X]$ rather than $E[1/X]$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 长期总时间是许多周期长度的和，平均每周期耗时趋于 $E[X]$；完成周期数除以总时间因此趋于其倒数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Long-run elapsed time is a sum of many cycle lengths, so time per cycle approaches $E[X]$. The number of completed cycles divided by total time therefore approaches its reciprocal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 随机到站时为什么更容易遇到较长间隔？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is a randomly arriving observer more likely to encounter a long interval?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 长间隔占据时间轴的份额更大，被随机时点命中的概率与长度成比例，因此观察到的是长度偏置分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Long intervals occupy more of the time axis, so a random time hits them with probability proportional to their length, producing a length-biased distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
