# 限价订单簿记录未成交限价委托及其价格数量和优先关系

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A limit order book records unfilled limit orders, their prices and quantities, and their matching priority.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

限价订单簿是记录尚未成交的[[限价单]]的账簿或系统状态，至少需要分清买卖方向、限价和剩余数量；逐订单记录还可以保留撮合所需的优先信息。买方出价越高越有竞争力，卖方要价越低越有竞争力。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A limit order book is the record or system state of unfilled [[限价单|limit orders]], distinguishing side, limit price and remaining quantity. Order-level records can also retain information needed for matching priority. Higher bids and lower asks are more competitive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一份假设的连续交易快照如下；数量单位均为股，价格单位均为元／股。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The following hypothetical continuous-trading snapshot uses shares for quantities and currency units per share for prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 买价 Bid | 买量 Bid size | 卖价 Ask | 卖量 Ask size |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 99.80 | 80 | 100.00 | 50 |
| 99.60 | 120 | 100.20 | 100 |

最佳卖价 100.00 只对应当前该档的 50 股，不是任意数量的报价。新订单、撤单和成交都会改变簿内状态。行情若只显示每档合计数量，就不能由此还原同档各订单的时间顺序；若只展示有限档位或显示数量，也不能把它当作全部潜在买卖意愿。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The best ask of 100.00 is associated with 50 displayed shares at that level, not unlimited size. Submissions, cancellations and executions change the book. Aggregated price-level data do not reveal the time order of individual orders. Limited levels or displayed size do not reveal all potential trading interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

订单簿是“现在有哪些订单”的对象，[[价格时间优先]]是“合格订单怎样排队”的规则，[[逐档成交均价]]则是“给定规则和快照怎样计算执行结果”的方法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The book describes outstanding orders; [[价格时间优先|price–time priority]] specifies how eligible orders queue; [[逐档成交均价|price-level execution averaging]] calculates fills under stated rules and a snapshot.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
