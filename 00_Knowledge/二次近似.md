# Quadratic approximation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*二次近似*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A tangent line records a function's value and slope at one input. A **quadratic approximation** also records how that slope changes, allowing the local model to bend. It adds a term proportional to the square of the input increment. The aim is a more informative model near the same base point, not permission to use a local formula arbitrarily far away.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

切线记录函数在一个输入处的值与斜率。**二次近似**进一步记录斜率怎样变化，让局部模型也能弯曲。它增加一个与输入增量平方成比例的项，目的是在同一基点附近提供更多信息，不是允许把局部公式随意用到远处。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $a$ for the base point, $x=a+h$ for the input to estimate, and $h$ for the signed increment. The [[线性近似|linear approximation]] is $f(a)+f'(a)h$. The [[高阶导数|second derivative]] $f''(a)$ is the derivative of $f'$ at $a$: it measures the local change in slope, not the square of $f'(a)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把基点记为 $a$，待估计的输入写成 $x=a+h$，其中 $h$ 是带符号的增量。[[线性近似]]为 $f(a)+f'(a)h$。[[高阶导数|二阶导数]] $f''(a)$ 是 $f'$ 在 $a$ 处的导数，衡量斜率的局部变化，不是一阶导数的平方。

> [!important] Match three pieces of local information · 匹配三项局部信息
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Assume $f$ is differentiable on an open interval around $a$, and its derivative $f'$ is differentiable at $a$. This makes the finite number $f''(a)$ well defined. The quadratic approximation is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设 $f$ 在 $a$ 周围的一个开区间上可导，并且导数函数 $f'$ 在 $a$ 可导，于是有限数 $f''(a)$ 有定义。二次近似为
>
> $$Q_a(x)=f(a)+f'(a)(x-a)+\frac{f''(a)}2(x-a)^2.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It is the unique polynomial of degree at most two matching $f(a)$, $f'(a)$ and $f''(a)$. Under the stated assumptions its remainder satisfies
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 它是在 $a$ 同时匹配 $f(a)$、$f'(a)$、$f''(a)$ 的唯一次数不超过二的多项式。在上述假设下，余项满足
>
> $$f(a+h)=f(a)+f'(a)h+\frac{f''(a)}2h^2+o(h^2).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $o(h^2)$ means that the remainder divided by $h^2$ tends to zero as nonzero $h\to0$. Twice continuously differentiable functions near $a$ satisfy the assumptions, but continuity of $f''$ is not needed for this conclusion.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $o(h^2)$ 的意思是：当非零 $h\to0$ 时，余项除以 $h^2$ 趋于零。在 $a$ 附近二阶连续可微的函数当然满足假设，但这一结论不需要 $f''$ 连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the coefficient is one half
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*二次项为什么要除以二*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with an unknown polynomial $Q(a+h)=A+Bh+Ch^2$, where $A,B,C$ are constants to determine. Its value at $h=0$ is $A$, its first derivative there is $B$, and its second derivative is $2C$. Matching the three pieces of information forces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先设未知多项式为 $Q(a+h)=A+Bh+Ch^2$，其中 $A,B,C$ 是待确定的常数。它在 $h=0$ 处的值是 $A$，一阶导数是 $B$，二阶导数为 $2C$。匹配三项信息便要求

$$A=f(a),\qquad B=f'(a),\qquad 2C=f''(a).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $C=f''(a)/2$. The factor $1/2$ compensates for the factor $2$ produced by differentiating $h^2$ twice; it is not an arbitrary convention. These equations determine all three coefficients, proving uniqueness. “Quadratic” means degree **at most** two: if $f''(a)=0$, the polynomial can be linear or constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 $C=f''(a)/2$。系数 $1/2$ 补偿的是对 $h^2$ 连续求两次导所产生的因子 $2$，不是任意规定。这三条方程确定了全部系数，也就证明了唯一性。“二次”表示次数**不超过**二；若 $f''(a)=0$，结果也可以是一次或常数多项式。

> [!example] The logarithm correction · 对数的二次修正
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $f(x)=\ln(1+x)$, with domain $x>-1$, and expand at $a=0$. For the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]], the [[对数函数求导|derivative rule]] gives $f'(x)=1/(1+x)$ and then $f''(x)=-1/(1+x)^2$. Thus $f(0)=0$, $f'(0)=1$, $f''(0)=-1$, and
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $f(x)=\ln(1+x)$，定义域为 $x>-1$，在 $a=0$ 展开。对[[自然对数]]，[[对数函数求导]]给出 $f'(x)=1/(1+x)$，进而 $f''(x)=-1/(1+x)^2$。因此 $f(0)=0$、$f'(0)=1$、$f''(0)=-1$，得到
>
> $$Q_0(x)=x-\frac{x^2}{2},\qquad Q_0(0.1)=0.095.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The actual value is $\ln1.1\approx0.095310180$. The quadratic error has magnitude about $0.000310180$, compared with about $0.004689820$ for the linear estimate $0.1$. These decimal values compare this particular example; they are not a general error guarantee for every quadratic approximation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 真值为 $\ln1.1\approx0.095310180$。二次近似的误差绝对值约为 $0.000310180$，而线性估计 $0.1$ 的误差约为 $0.004689820$。这些小数比较的是本例，不是对所有二次近似的通用误差保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For sine at zero, [[正弦余弦求导|the derivatives]] give $f(0)=0$, $f'(0)=1$ and $f''(0)=0$, so the quadratic approximation is still $x$. No quadratic term should be invented. Similarly, a positive $f''(a)$ makes the fitted parabola open upwards; a sign at one point does not establish [[凹凸性|convexity]] on an entire interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对零点处的正弦函数，[[正弦余弦求导]]给出 $f(0)=0$、$f'(0)=1$、$f''(0)=0$，所以二次近似仍然是 $x$，不能人为添加二次项。同样，$f''(a)>0$ 使拟合抛物线开口向上，但一点的符号不能证明整个区间上的[[凹凸性|凸性]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the remainder is smaller than the squared increment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么余项比增量平方更小*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define the error as a function of the increment:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把误差写成增量的函数：

$$E(t)=f(a+t)-f(a)-f'(a)t-\frac{f''(a)}2t^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It has $E(0)=0$ and $E'(t)=f'(a+t)-f'(a)-f''(a)t$. Differentiability of $f'$ at $a$ means $E'(t)/t\to0$. Therefore, for any $\varepsilon>0$, sufficiently small $|t|$ gives $|E'(t)|\le\varepsilon|t|$, including $t=0$ where $E'(0)=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它满足 $E(0)=0$，且 $E'(t)=f'(a+t)-f'(a)-f''(a)t$。$f'$ 在 $a$ 可导，意味着 $E'(t)/t\to0$。因此，给定任意 $\varepsilon>0$，只要 $|t|$ 充分小，就有 $|E'(t)|\le\varepsilon|t|$；在 $t=0$ 处，因 $E'(0)=0$ 也成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a sufficiently small nonzero $h$, apply the [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]] to $E$ on the segment between $0$ and $h$. It applies because $f$ is differentiable throughout the surrounding interval, so $E$ is continuous on this segment and differentiable inside. Some point $\xi$ strictly between $0$ and $h$ satisfies $E(h)=hE'(\xi)$. Since $|\xi|<|h|$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对充分小的非零 $h$，在连接 $0$ 与 $h$ 的线段上，对 $E$ 应用[[拉格朗日中值定理]]。由于 $f$ 在周围区间处处可导，$E$ 在线段上连续、内部可导，满足所需条件。某个严格位于 $0$ 与 $h$ 之间的 $\xi$ 满足 $E(h)=hE'(\xi)$。由 $|\xi|<|h|$，

$$|E(h)|\le |h|\varepsilon|\xi|\le\varepsilon h^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
As the tolerance $\varepsilon$ was arbitrary, $E(h)/h^2\to0$. This proves the claimed local error order without assuming a third derivative or continuity of the second derivative. It does not supply a numerical radius for a specified tolerance unless the derivative estimates are made quantitative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $\varepsilon$ 任意，便有 $E(h)/h^2\to0$。这在不假设三阶导数、也不假设二阶导数连续的情况下，证明了所述局部误差阶。但如果导数估计还没有具体化，它仍不会为指定容差给出一个数值半径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## More local information is not a fixed-step error certificate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*更多局部信息不等于固定步长的误差保证*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any fixed real constant $C$, let $f_C(x)=x+x^2+Cx^3$. Every member has the same value, first derivative and second derivative at zero, so each has the same quadratic approximation $x+x^2$. Yet at $h=0.1$ the error is $0.001C$, which can be arbitrarily large as $C$ varies. For each fixed $C$, the remainder is still $o(h^2)$ because $Ch^3/h^2=Ch\to0$. The neighbourhood needed for a tolerance depends on the function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意固定实常数 $C$，令 $f_C(x)=x+x^2+Cx^3$。这些函数在零点的值、一阶导数和二阶导数完全相同，所以二次近似都是 $x+x^2$。但在 $h=0.1$ 处，误差为 $0.001C$，随 $C$ 改变可以任意大。对每个固定的 $C$，余项仍然是 $o(h^2)$，因为 $Ch^3/h^2=Ch\to0$。满足某个容差所需的邻域，会依赖具体函数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A quadratic approximation also need not beat a linear approximation at every fixed point. For $f(x)=x+x^2-x^3$ about zero, the linear model is $x$ and the quadratic model is $x+x^2$. At $x=1$, the true value and linear estimate are both $1$, while the quadratic estimate is $2$. For certified error at a chosen input, use direct algebra or an applicable [[Taylor余项|Taylor remainder theorem]] with its extra whole-interval derivative conditions. The local second-order conclusion alone is not a Lagrange third-derivative bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二次近似也不一定在每个指定点都优于线性近似。对 $f(x)=x+x^2-x^3$ 在零点展开，线性模型为 $x$，二次模型为 $x+x^2$。在 $x=1$ 处，真值和线性估计都为 $1$，二次估计却是 $2$。若要保证指定输入处的误差，应直接作代数估计，或使用满足额外整段导数条件的[[Taylor余项]]定理。仅有局部二阶结论，不能当作含三阶导数的 Lagrange 误差界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When multiplying approximations, all contributions of the requested order matter. For example, the product of $1+h+ch^2$ and $1+h+dh^2$ has quadratic coefficient $1+c+d$, where $c,d$ are constants. Multiplying only the linear parts gives $(1+h)^2$ and incorrectly keeps just the coefficient $1$. A complete quadratic model must include the factors' own second-order contributions. The same derivative-matching idea extends to [[Taylor多项式|higher-order Taylor polynomials]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相乘时，所求阶数的全部贡献都必须保留。例如，$1+h+ch^2$ 与 $1+h+dh^2$ 的乘积，二次项系数为 $1+c+d$，其中 $c,d$ 是常数。如果只把线性部分相乘，得到 $(1+h)^2$，就会误以为二次项系数只有 $1$。完整二次模型还要计入两个因子各自的二阶贡献。同一种导数匹配思路可以继续推广到[[Taylor多项式]]。
