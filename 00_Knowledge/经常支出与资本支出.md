# 经常与资本支出按交易的运转或资产性质区分而不按效益持续多久区分
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Current and capital expenditure are distinguished by the operating or asset nature of the transaction, not by how long its benefits last.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经常支出通常用于当期运转、服务提供和经常性转移；资本支出涉及取得或重大改善非金融资产，以及按所采用制度列入资本项的转移。读一个具体预算时，必须查它采用的资本边界：不同预算制度不一定与 [[GFS费用与支出|GFS 的费用、非金融资产交易]] 一一对应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Current expenditure normally supports ongoing operations, services and current transfers. Capital expenditure covers nonfinancial asset acquisition or major improvement, together with transfers classified as capital under the applicable system. Budget classifications need not map one-to-one into [[GFS费用与支出|GFS expense and asset transactions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区分新建校舍、日常维修与教师工资，比记“投资有长期效益、消费没有”更可靠：校舍建设形成资产；维持原有功能的普通维修是当期运转；显著延长使用寿命或增加容量的重大改造，则可能计入资产。教师工资即使帮助学生形成长期能力，也不会仅凭这个效果改列为政府固定资产投资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Distinguish a new school building, routine maintenance and teachers' wages. Construction creates an asset; ordinary maintenance preserves its normal function; a major improvement can extend useful life or capacity and be capitalised. Long-lasting educational benefits do not by themselves turn teachers' wages into government fixed-asset investment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“资本”也不等于“政府自己购买”：专用于受助者取得资产的资本补助，可以同时是 [[购买支出与转移支出|转移支出]]。所以至少要分别回答资产归谁、支出属于哪种交易，而不能看到“建设资金”四个字就认定政府新增同额资产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A capital grant can also be a [[购买支出与转移支出|transfer]]. Identify who owns the asset and what transaction occurred; a construction grant does not necessarily create an equal government-owned asset.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个分类适合安排预算和追踪资产，不是项目优劣排名。修好现有水管可能比新建一个闲置设施更有价值；项目投用后的人员和维护费用也须进入后续预算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The classification supports budgeting and asset tracking, not a ranking of project quality. Maintaining useful infrastructure may outperform building an unused facility. Staffing and maintenance after completion must also appear in future budgets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
