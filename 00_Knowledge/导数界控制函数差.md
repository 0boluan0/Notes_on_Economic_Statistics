# Bounding function changes with derivatives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*导数界控制函数差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A speed limit over an entire trip limits how much the position can change. A **derivative bound** does the same for a function: if its slope stays between known numbers while the input moves from one point to another, its total output change is trapped between corresponding bounds. This produces an exact inequality rather than a tangent approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

整段行程中的速度限制，会限制位置最多改变多少。**导数界**对函数起同样的作用：输入从一点移到另一点时，只要整段斜率都在已知范围内，总输出变化就被夹在相应范围内。这给出的是精确不等式，而不是切线近似。

> [!example] A range for a change in a square · 给平方值的变化划定范围
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^2$ and $2\le x\le3$, the [[导数|derivative]] is $2x$, between $4$ and $6$. The input travels a distance of $1$, so the output change must lie between $4$ and $6$. Indeed, $f(3)-f(2)=9-4=5$. We did not need to know the exact change to guarantee its range.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $f(x)=x^2$，当 $2\le x\le3$ 时，[[导数]]为 $2x$，处于 $4$ 与 $6$ 之间。输入走了 $1$，因此输出变化必在 $4$ 与 $6$ 之间。实际计算为 $f(3)-f(2)=9-4=5$。即使不知道精确变化，也能保证它所在的范围。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The bound must cover the interval
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*上界和下界必须覆盖整段*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume $a<b$, $f$ is [[一点连续|continuous]] on $[a,b]$—nearby inputs give outputs tending to the function value—and differentiable at every point of $(a,b)$. These are the hypotheses of the [[拉格朗日中值定理|mean value theorem]]. Suppose fixed finite numbers $m,M$ satisfy $m\le f'(t)\le M$ for every $t\in(a,b)$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<b$，$f$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上[[一点连续|连续]]，即输入趋近某点时输出趋于该点函数值，并在 $(a,b)$ 每一点可导。这些正是[[拉格朗日中值定理]]的条件。若存在固定有限数 $m,M$，使每个 $t\in(a,b)$ 都满足 $m\le f'(t)\le M$，则

> [!important] From slopes to a finite change · 从斜率到有限变化
> $$
> m(b-a)\le f(b)-f(a)\le M(b-a).
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The bounds need not be attained minima or maxima. The derivative need not be continuous, and derivatives at the two endpoints are not required. What is required is the bound at every interior point, together with continuity of $f$ at the endpoints.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这些界不必是实际取得的最小值或最大值。导数不必连续，也不要求两端点的导数存在；要求的是每个内部点都满足导数界，并且 $f$ 在端点连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The mean value theorem supplies some unknown $c\in(a,b)$ such that $f(b)-f(a)=f'(c)(b-a)$. Since $b-a>0$, multiplying $m\le f'(c)\le M$ by this positive length proves the result. The point $c$ may be unknown; the whole-interval bound covers it wherever it lies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

中值定理给出某个未知的 $c\in(a,b)$，满足 $f(b)-f(a)=f'(c)(b-a)$。因为 $b-a>0$，将 $m\le f'(c)\le M$ 乘以这个正长度，就得到结论。即使不知道 $c$ 在哪里，整段成立的界也一定覆盖它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Control the size, not just the sign
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*控制变化的大小*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $|f'(t)|\le L$ throughout the interval, where $L\ge0$, use $m=-L$ and $M=L$. Ordering either pair of endpoints gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若整段都有 $|f'(t)|\le L$，其中 $L\ge0$，取 $m=-L$、$M=L$ 即可。将任意两个端点按大小排列，得到

$$
|f(b)-f(a)|\le L|b-a|.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the same constant $L$ works for every pair of points in an interval, this property is called a Lipschitz bound: the output distance is at most $L$ times the input distance. For sine with angles in radians, [[正弦余弦求导|its derivative is cosine]] and $|\cos t|\le1$, so $|\sin b-\sin a|\le|b-a|$ for any real $a,b$. For example, an input error of at most $0.02$ radians changes the sine value by at most $0.02$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若同一个常数 $L$ 对区间内任意两点都有效，这称为 Lipschitz 界：输出距离最多是输入距离的 $L$ 倍。弧度制下，[[正弦余弦求导|正弦的导数是余弦]]，且 $|\cos t|\le1$，所以任意实数 $a,b$ 都有 $|\sin b-\sin a|\le|b-a|$。例如输入误差不超过 $0.02$ 弧度，正弦值的变化就不超过 $0.02$。

> [!warning] Endpoint slopes are insufficient · 只看端点斜率不够
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On $[0,1]$, take $p(t)=3t^2-2t^3$. Both endpoint derivatives are zero, but $p(1)-p(0)=1$. Treating those two values as a global bound $L=0$ would wrongly predict no change. The interior derivative $p'(t)=6t(1-t)$ reaches $1.5$ at $t=1/2$, so the proposed bound never covered the interval.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $[0,1]$ 上取 $p(t)=3t^2-2t^3$。两个端点的导数都是零，但 $p(1)-p(0)=1$。若把两次端点观测当作全局界 $L=0$，就会错误地预测函数不变。内部导数 $p'(t)=6t(1-t)$ 在 $t=1/2$ 达到 $1.5$，因此那个界根本没有覆盖整段。

## Apply the same idea to an approximation error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把同一思路用于近似误差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[线性近似|tangent approximation]] at $a$ is $f(a)+f'(a)(x-a)$. Define its error by $R(t)=f(t)-f(a)-f'(a)(t-a)$, so $R(a)=0$ and $R'(t)=f'(t)-f'(a)$. If $f$ is differentiable at $a$ and has the required continuity and differentiability along the segment from $a$ to $x$, a uniform bound $|f'(t)-f'(a)|\le\varepsilon$ there gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$a$ 处的[[线性近似|切线近似]]为 $f(a)+f'(a)(x-a)$。把误差写成 $R(t)=f(t)-f(a)-f'(a)(t-a)$，则 $R(a)=0$、$R'(t)=f'(t)-f'(a)$。若 $f$ 在 $a$ 可导，并在从 $a$ 到 $x$ 的线段上满足所需的连续、可导条件，只要该段内部统一有 $|f'(t)-f'(a)|\le\varepsilon$，就得到

$$
|f(x)-f(a)-f'(a)(x-a)|\le\varepsilon|x-a|.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is a numerical error certificate once the interval-wide $\varepsilon$ has been justified. In the square example at $a=3$, $x=3.01$, the derivative changes by at most $0.02$, so the error is at most $0.02(0.01)=0.0002$. The actual error is $0.0001$: a valid bound need not be the tightest one. [[Taylor余项|Taylor remainder bounds]] can give sharper estimates when their additional derivative assumptions hold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只要区间上的 $\varepsilon$ 已经证实，这就是数值误差保证。在平方函数 $a=3$、$x=3.01$ 的例子里，导数最多改变 $0.02$，所以误差不超过 $0.02(0.01)=0.0002$；真实误差为 $0.0001$。有效的界不一定最紧，若额外导数条件成立，[[Taylor余项]]可以给出更精细的估计。
