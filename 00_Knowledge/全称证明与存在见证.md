# Universal proofs and existential witnesses
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

全称证明与存在见证

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“All integers have this property” asks for an argument that covers every integer. “Some integer has this property” asks whether at least one suitable integer exists. The first goal cannot be settled by a few successful examples; for the second, one verified example can be enough.

Write $D$ for the domain of objects and $P(x)$ for a [[命题与谓词|predicate]], a statement whose truth can be evaluated once $x$ is specified. The goal $\forall x\in D\,P(x)$ is universal: every member must pass. The goal $\exists x\in D\,P(x)$ is existential: at least one member must pass. These different goals suggest different openings to a proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“所有整数都有这个性质”要求一个覆盖每个整数的论证；“某个整数有这个性质”只问是否至少存在一个合适的整数。前者不能靠几个成功例子解决；后者可能只需一个经过核验的例子。

用 $D$ 表示对象的论域，用 $P(x)$ 表示[[命题与谓词|谓词]]，即指定 $x$ 后可以判断真假的陈述。目标 $\forall x\in D\,P(x)$ 要求每个成员都满足条件；目标 $\exists x\in D\,P(x)$ 要求至少一个成员满足条件。证明的开头应回应这两种不同要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Start with an arbitrary object, or construct a witness

A direct universal proof keeps its object arbitrary. A constructive existence proof supplies a witness. The following recipe describes those two routes; an existence proof can also be nonconstructive, as discussed under the boundary below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从任意对象开始，或构造一个见证

全称命题的直接证明保留对象的任意性；构造性的存在证明给出一个见证。下面的做法针对这两条路线；存在性也可以通过后文边界段所说的非构造性方法建立。

> [!important] Two proof openings / 两种证明入口
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To prove $\forall x\in D\,P(x)$, take an **arbitrary** $x\in D$ and prove $P(x)$ without adding special conditions on that object. To prove $\exists x\in D\,P(x)$, supply a concrete object or construction and verify both membership in $D$ and property $P$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 要证明 $\forall x\in D\,P(x)$，先取**任意** $x\in D$，在不加入该对象特有条件的前提下证明 $P(x)$。要证明 $\exists x\in D\,P(x)$，给出一个具体对象或构造规则，并核验它属于 $D$ 且满足 $P$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, proving “every integer has a nonnegative square” cannot begin by choosing only $x=3$; take arbitrary $x\in\mathbb Z$ and use the order properties of the reals to obtain $x^2\ge0$. To prove “there exists an even prime,” choose the witness $2$ and verify separately that it is even and prime.

Here is the full first argument. Let $x$ be any integer. If $x\ge0$, then $x^2$ is the product of two nonnegative numbers. If $x<0$, then $-x>0$ and $x^2=(-x)^2>0$. Thus $x^2\ge0$ in either case. Nothing used the particular value of $x$, so the conclusion applies to every integer, including zero and negative integers.

For the second argument, $2$ is an integer, it is even because $2=2\cdot1$, and its positive divisors are exactly $1$ and $2$, so it is [[素数|prime]]. These checks establish that the proposed witness lies in the domain and satisfies both required properties.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，要证明“每个整数的平方非负”，不能只选 $x=3$；应取任意 $x\in\mathbb Z$，再用实数有序性得到 $x^2\ge0$。要证明“存在偶素数”，取 witness $2$，并分别核验它是偶数且是素数。

第一个论证可以完整写成：任取整数 $x$。若 $x\ge0$，则 $x^2$ 是两个非负数的乘积；若 $x<0$，则 $-x>0$，从而 $x^2=(-x)^2>0$。两种情况都得到 $x^2\ge0$。推导没有使用 $x$ 的特殊取值，因此覆盖每个整数，包括零和负整数。

第二个论证中，$2$ 属于整数论域；由 $2=2\cdot1$ 可知它是偶数；它的正因数恰好为 $1$ 和 $2$，所以它是[[素数]]。这些核验同时说明见证属于论域，并满足要求的两个性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Use the hypothesis without choosing a special value

For a statement such as “every even integer has an even square,” the object is arbitrary **among the integers satisfying the premise**. Let $n$ be an arbitrary even integer. By the definition of evenness there is an integer $k$ with $n=2k$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

使用题设，而不是挑选特殊数值

证明“每个偶整数的平方仍为偶数”时，对象在**满足前提的整数中**保持任意。取任意偶整数 $n$。由偶数定义，存在整数 $k$ 使 $n=2k$，于是

$$
n^2=(2k)^2=4k^2=2(2k^2).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $2k^2$ is an integer, this is the definition of an even integer. The integer $k$ may depend on $n$; the proof does not require one fixed $k$ to work for every $n$. This is a [[直接证明|direct proof]]: assume the stated premise and derive the conclusion. Cases where the premise is false impose no further obligation on this implication, by the [[蕴含真值条件|truth condition for implication]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$2k^2$ 仍是整数，因此这个式子正好符合偶数定义。这里的 $k$ 可以随 $n$ 改变，并不要求一个固定的 $k$ 同时适用于所有 $n$。这是[[直接证明]]：从题设前提出发推出结论。根据[[蕴含真值条件]]，前提为假的对象不再对这一蕴含提出额外证明义务。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A witness can depend on earlier universal variables

Consider $\forall x\in\mathbb Z\,\exists y\in\mathbb Z\;(y>x)$. First fix an arbitrary integer $x$, then choose $y=x+1$. The chosen $y$ is an integer and $y-x=1>0$, so it works for that $x$. Because the construction works for every $x$, it proves the full statement.

The reversed [[量词次序|quantifier order]], $\exists y\in\mathbb Z\,\forall x\in\mathbb Z\;(y>x)$, asks for one integer $y$ larger than every integer. It is false: for any proposed $y$, taking $x=y$ makes $y>x$ false. Choosing $y=x+1$ after seeing $x$ cannot meet a demand to choose $y$ before all choices of $x$.

Membership is a separate obligation. For example, the real number $1/2$ satisfies $2x=1$, but it is not an integer, so it is not a witness for an existential statement whose domain is $\mathbb Z$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

见证可以依赖先前的全称变量

考虑 $\forall x\in\mathbb Z\,\exists y\in\mathbb Z\;(y>x)$。先固定任意整数 $x$，再取 $y=x+1$。所取的 $y$ 仍是整数，且 $y-x=1>0$，所以它适合这个 $x$。这一构造对每个 $x$ 都有效，因而证明了整个命题。

颠倒[[量词次序]]得到 $\exists y\in\mathbb Z\,\forall x\in\mathbb Z\;(y>x)$，要求一个固定整数 $y$ 大于所有整数。这是假命题：对任何候选 $y$，取 $x=y$ 就使 $y>x$ 不成立。在知道 $x$ 后取 $y=x+1$，不能满足“先选一个 $y$，再面对所有 $x$”的要求。

属于论域是独立的核验义务。例如，实数 $1/2$ 满足 $2x=1$，但它不是整数，所以不能充当整数论域上存在命题的见证。

## Boundary
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Arbitrary” does not mean “randomly sampled.” It means the proof uses no property of the object beyond membership in the stated domain.

An existential proof may be nonconstructive, for example by contradiction or exhaustive cases. It must still establish existence rather than merely assert that an object should be findable.

The first statement allows all stated hypotheses and established facts about the domain; it rules out extra assumptions such as setting an arbitrary integer equal to $3$. Finitely many tests can settle a universal claim only if they exhaust the stated finite domain, or if a separate argument shows that the tested cases cover it. A few selected integers do not exhaust $\mathbb Z$.

If the domain is empty, $\forall x\in D\,P(x)$ is true because no member violates $P$, whereas $\exists x\in D\,P(x)$ is false because no witness belongs to $D$. This is the empty-domain extension of the usual semantics; a proof that starts “take arbitrary $x\in D$” does not establish that $D$ contains an object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“任意”不等于“随机抽一个”。它表示证明没有利用对象在论域条件之外的特殊性质。

非构造性存在证明也可能有效，例如反证或穷尽分类；但最终必须确实建立存在性，不能只说“应该能找到”。

这里允许使用所有题设假设以及已经建立的论域性质，排除的是把任意整数另行设为 $3$ 等额外限制。有限次检验只有在穷尽给定有限论域，或另有论证说明所检验情形覆盖论域时，才足以建立全称结论。随意选几个整数并没有穷尽 $\mathbb Z$。

若论域为空，则 $\forall x\in D\,P(x)$ 为真，因为没有成员违反 $P$；$\exists x\in D\,P(x)$ 为假，因为不存在属于 $D$ 的见证。这是通常量词语义在空论域上的扩展；“任取 $x\in D$”的证明开头本身不建立 $D$ 非空。

> [!question]- Recall and apply / 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To prove that every even integer has an even square, why begin with an arbitrary even integer $n$ rather than set $n=4$?
>
> **Answer:** $n=4$ covers only one instance. An arbitrary $n$ satisfying the premise, used only through the definition of evenness, makes the argument apply to every object in the domain.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 为证明 $\forall n\in\mathbb Z$，若 $n$ 偶则 $n^2$ 偶，开头为什么应写“取任意偶整数 $n$”，而不是“令 $n=4$”？
>
> **答案：** $n=4$ 只覆盖一个实例；取任意满足前提的 $n$，并只使用偶数定义，才能让推导适用于论域中每个对象。
