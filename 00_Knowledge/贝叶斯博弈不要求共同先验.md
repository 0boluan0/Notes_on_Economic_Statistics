# 在事中信念作为原语的贝叶斯博弈定义中，共同先验是附加假设而非必备条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a formulation that takes interim beliefs as primitives, a common prior is an additional assumption rather than a requirement for a Bayesian game*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用 interim-belief formulation 时，模型直接为每名玩家的每个类型给定

$$
p_i(\cdot\mid t_i)\in\Delta(\Theta\times T_{-i}).
$$

这些条件信念足以定义玩家在观察自身类型后的决策问题；即使不存在一份能同时生成所有这些信念的联合分布，模型仍是一场可分析的贝叶斯博弈。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under an interim-belief formulation, the model directly assigns a conditional belief to every type of every player. These beliefs are sufficient to define each player's decision problem after observing their own type, even when no single joint distribution generates all of them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 共同先验是额外相容性条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The common prior is an additional consistency condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若存在 $\pi\in\Delta(\Theta\times T)$，使各玩家的条件信念都等于 $\pi(\cdot\mid t_i)$，博弈才额外满足[[共同先验|共同先验假设]]。这项条件把玩家的信念联系到同一份 ex-ante 概率，但不是上述 interim 定义的一部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The game additionally satisfies the common-prior assumption only if there is a distribution $\pi\in\Delta(\Theta\times T)$ whose conditioning produces every player's type-contingent beliefs. This condition links those beliefs to one ex-ante probability distribution; it is not part of the interim definition itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“贝叶斯博弈是否要求共同先验”取决于教材采用的定义口径。有些教材把共同先验直接列入模型；使用本命题时，应明确采用的是以玩家事中信念为原语的口径。零概率类型能否由 $\pi$ 条件化得到，还要另查[[零概率类型的条件信念]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether the term “Bayesian game” includes a common prior depends on the author's convention. Some texts build a common prior into the model, so this proposition must be stated specifically for the interim-belief formulation. Conditioning at zero-probability types requires a separate treatment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[MIT OCW 14.126, Muhamet Yildiz, *Game Theory Lecture Notes*, Chapter 3 §3.1, Definition 3.1，正文 p.68](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14.126-game-theory-spring-2024/mit14_126_s24_yildiz-lecture-notes.pdf)：直接以 $p_i(\cdot\mid t_i)$ 定义贝叶斯博弈，并把 common prior 另列为条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
MIT defines the Bayesian game directly through $p_i(\cdot\mid t_i)$ and states the common-prior property as an additional condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[同一讲义 Chapter 9 opening，正文 p.266 注 1](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14.126-game-theory-spring-2024/mit14_126_s24_yildiz-lecture-notes.pdf)：明确说明可在类型空间上允许 differing priors，common prior 并非定义贝叶斯博弈所必需。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Chapter 9 note explicitly states that Bayesian games can allow differing priors on the type space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[UC Berkeley, Federico Echenique, *Game Theory Lecture Notes*, Chapter 9，正文 pp.80–81](https://eml.berkeley.edu/~fechenique/lecture_notes/game_theory.pdf)：展示把共同先验纳入有限 Bayesian game 定义的另一种教材口径，并指出该假设并非普遍接受。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Berkeley notes illustrate the alternative convention that includes a common prior in the definition and note that the assumption is not universally accepted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
