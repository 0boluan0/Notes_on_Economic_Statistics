# 图的基本结构、路径与遍历
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Graph fundamentals, paths, and traversal*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 图把“对象”抽象为顶点、把“对象之间的关系”抽象为边；路径与遍历进一步回答从一个对象能到哪里、最少经过多少条边，以及怎样不漏不重地探索整个关系网络。
> **具体锚点：** 把地铁站作为顶点、相邻站作为无向边。BFS 从起点按 0、1、2、… 条边分层，因此第一次到达某站时就得到无权最短路；DFS 则沿一条分支走到底，适合揭示连通结构和环。
> **核心难点：** 先分清 directed/undirected、walk/path/cycle 和 reachability/connectivity，再谈算法；BFS 的最短路结论只适用于无权或等权边，DFS parent tree 通常不是最短路树。
> **为什么重要：** 依赖、网络、网页链接、状态空间和冲突关系都能建图；后续的 tree、DAG、matching 与 coloring 都以这里的对象和遍历不变量为前提。
> **继续：** 先看 [[#先声明你建的是什么图]] 与 [[#Walk、path、cycle 与可达性]]，再比较 [[#BFS：按距离分层]] 和 [[#DFS：沿分支深入再回溯]]；无环结构进入 [[无环图：树、生成树、DAG 与拓扑排序]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** A graph abstracts objects as vertices and relationships as edges. Paths and traversals then answer which objects are reachable, how few edges are needed, and how to explore the entire relationship network without omissions or uncontrolled repetition.
> **Concrete anchor:** Treat subway stations as vertices and direct connections as undirected edges. BFS explores in layers of 0, 1, 2, … edges, so the first discovery of a station gives an unweighted shortest path. DFS follows one branch as far as possible and is useful for revealing connectivity and cycles.
> **Central difficulty:** Distinguish directed from undirected graphs, walks from paths and cycles, and reachability from connectivity before choosing an algorithm. BFS gives shortest paths only for unweighted or equal-weight edges; a DFS parent tree is generally not a shortest-path tree.
> **Why it matters:** Dependencies, networks, hyperlinks, state spaces, and conflicts can all be modeled as graphs. Trees, DAGs, matchings, and colorings all depend on the objects and traversal invariants established here.
> **Continue with:** Begin with [[#先声明你建的是什么图|specifying the graph model]] and [[#Walk、path、cycle 与可达性|walks, paths, cycles, and reachability]], then compare [[#BFS：按距离分层|BFS]] with [[#DFS：沿分支深入再回溯|DFS]]. For acyclic structure, continue to [[无环图：树、生成树、DAG 与拓扑排序|acyclic graphs, trees, spanning trees, DAGs, and topological order]].

## 先声明你建的是什么图
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First specify the graph you are modeling*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图写成 $G=(V,E)$。$V$ 是顶点集合，$E$ 是边集合；真正的建模决定是“顶点代表什么”和“连边代表什么”。同一批对象可以因研究问题不同得到完全不同的图：网页以 hyperlink 为有向边，合作关系常用无向边，课程先修用有向边，考试冲突用无向边。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A graph is written $G=(V,E)$, where $V$ is the vertex set and $E$ the edge set. The substantive modeling choice is what vertices represent and what an edge means. The same objects can yield entirely different graphs for different questions: hyperlinks are directed, collaboration is often undirected, prerequisites are directed, and exam conflicts are undirected.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 directed graph 中，边 $(u,v)$ 有方向，$u\to v$ 不自动给出 $v\to u$；要区分 indegree 与 outdegree。在 undirected simple graph 中，边是不同顶点组成的无序对，不允许 self-loop 或 parallel edges。若应用允许重复线路、容量不同的平行通道或自环，应明确改用 multigraph/weighted graph，而不是默默套 simple-graph 定理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a directed graph, the edge $(u,v)$ has orientation: $u\to v$ does not automatically imply $v\to u$, and in-degree must be distinguished from out-degree. In an undirected simple graph, an edge is an unordered pair of distinct vertices, with neither self-loops nor parallel edges. If an application permits repeated routes, parallel channels with different capacities, or self-loops, explicitly use a multigraph or weighted graph rather than silently applying simple-graph theorems.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限无向图，handshaking lemma 给出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite undirected graph, the handshaking lemma states
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sum_{v\in V}\deg(v)=2|E|.
$$

它是对 vertex–edge incidence pairs 的双重计数：按顶点计数得到左侧，按边计数时每条边贡献两个端点。直接推论是奇度顶点个数必为偶数。对有限 digraph，则 $\sum_v indeg(v)=\sum_v outdeg(v)=|E|$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This double-counts vertex–edge incidence pairs: counting by vertex gives the left-hand side, while counting by edge makes every edge contribute two endpoints. An immediate consequence is that the number of odd-degree vertices is even. For a finite digraph, $\sum_v indeg(v)=\sum_v outdeg(v)=|E|$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 邻接表还是邻接矩阵
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Adjacency lists or an adjacency matrix?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

adjacency list 为每个顶点存邻居，空间通常是 $O(|V|+|E|)$，适合 sparse graph 和逐邻居遍历。adjacency matrix 用 $|V|\times|V|$ 的表记录是否有边，空间为 $O(|V|^2)$，能常数时间查询指定两点是否相邻，也适合用矩阵幂计数固定长度 walks。复杂度结论必须连同表示法一起说；“遍历所有邻居”在两种表示下成本不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An adjacency list stores the neighbors of each vertex, normally using $O(|V|+|E|)$ space and supporting sparse graphs and neighbor-by-neighbor traversal. An adjacency matrix uses a $|V|\times|V|$ table, takes $O(|V|^2)$ space, answers a specified adjacency query in constant time, and permits matrix powers to count fixed-length walks. Complexity claims must name the representation: scanning all neighbors has different costs in the two representations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Walk、path、cycle 与可达性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Walks, paths, cycles, and reachability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- walk 是沿相邻边前进的序列，可以重复顶点和边；长度是走过的边数。
- path 是不重复顶点的 walk；因此有限图中的 path 长度至多 $|V|-1$。
- closed walk 首尾相同；cycle 是除首尾外不重复顶点的 closed walk。在 simple undirected graph 中，cycle 至少含 3 条边，沿同一条边走到邻点再折返不算 cycle；directed graph 则可由相反方向的两条边形成长度 2 的 directed cycle。
- 从 $u$ 到 $v$ 的 distance 是最短 path 的边数；不可达时记为 $\infty$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- A walk is a sequence following adjacent edges and may repeat vertices or edges; its length is the number of traversed edges.
- A path is a walk with no repeated vertex, so a path in a finite graph has length at most $|V|-1$.
- A closed walk begins and ends at the same vertex; a cycle repeats no vertex except its endpoints. In a simple undirected graph, a cycle has at least three edges, so traversing one edge and immediately returning along it is not a cycle. In a directed graph, two oppositely oriented edges may form a directed two-cycle.
- The distance from $u$ to $v$ is the number of edges in a shortest path; it is $\infty$ when $v$ is unreachable from $u$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任何从 $u$ 到 $v$ 的 walk 都包含一条不更长的 path：若某顶点重复，删除两次出现之间的 closed subwalk；持续删除直到没有重复。于是 shortest walk 一定是 path。相同思路说明正 closed walk 至少含一个 cycle，但它本身未必是 cycle。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every walk from $u$ to $v$ contains a path no longer than the walk: if a vertex repeats, delete the closed subwalk between two occurrences and continue until no repetition remains. Therefore, every shortest walk is a path. The same idea shows that a positive closed walk contains a cycle, although the closed walk itself need not be a cycle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在无向图中，“存在 path”把顶点分成 connected components；图只有一个 component 时称 connected。在有向图中，$u$ 可达 $v$ 不保证反向可达：两点互相可达才属于同一 strongly connected component；忽略方向后连通只给 weak connectivity。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an undirected graph, the existence of a path partitions vertices into connected components; the graph is connected when it has one component. In a directed graph, reachability from $u$ to $v$ need not imply the reverse. Vertices belong to the same strongly connected component only when they are mutually reachable; connectivity after ignoring orientation gives only weak connectivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 搜索共同不变量：第一次发现与 parent forest
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The shared search invariant: first discovery and a parent forest*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

BFS 与 DFS 都维护三类状态：尚未发现、已发现但未完成、已完成。一个顶点第一次被发现时记录 parent edge；除每棵搜索树的根外，每个已发现顶点恰有一个 parent，这些边不会形成 cycle，因而构成 search tree 或 forest。邻接表下，每个顶点只发现一次、每条边只检查常数次，总时间是 $O(|V|+|E|)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BFS and DFS both maintain three conceptual states: undiscovered, discovered but unfinished, and finished. At a vertex's first discovery, record a parent edge. Except for the root of each search tree, every discovered vertex has exactly one parent; these edges cannot form a cycle and therefore form a search tree or forest. With adjacency lists, each vertex is discovered once and each edge examined only a constant number of times, giving $O(|V|+|E|)$ time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“发现时标记”而不是“取出后才标记”很重要。若两个 frontier 顶点都看到同一未标记邻居，延迟标记会把它重复加入队列或栈，破坏一个顶点一个 parent 的不变量并造成无谓工作。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mark a vertex when it is discovered, not only when it is later removed from the queue or stack. If two frontier vertices both see the same unmarked neighbor, delayed marking can insert it twice, breaking the one-parent-per-vertex invariant and creating unnecessary work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## BFS：按距离分层
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*BFS: exploring by distance layers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

BFS 使用 FIFO queue。从源点 $s$ 开始，先处理所有距离 1 的邻居，再处理距离 2 的顶点，依次外扩。核心不变量是：队列中的顶点按已知距离非递减排列；处理距离 $k$ 的顶点时，任何新发现邻居都有距离至多 $k+1$，而更短路径若存在，其前驱本应更早被处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
BFS uses a FIFO queue. Starting from source $s$, it processes all vertices at distance one, then distance two, and so on. Its central invariant is that queued vertices appear in nondecreasing discovered distance. When processing a vertex at distance $k$, every newly discovered neighbor has distance at most $k+1$, while any shorter path would have had a predecessor processed earlier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此顶点第一次被 BFS 发现时，记录的 $dist[v]=dist[parent[v]]+1$ 就是真实的最少边数，沿 parent pointers 反向即可重建一条 shortest path。这个结论只比较边数：若边权不同，三条低权边可能优于一条高权边，应改用 DAG shortest path、Dijkstra 或 Bellman–Ford 等与权重条件匹配的方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Therefore, when BFS first discovers a vertex, $dist[v]=dist[parent[v]]+1$ is the true minimum number of edges. Following parent pointers backward reconstructs a shortest path. This result minimizes edge count only. With unequal weights, three low-weight edges may beat one high-weight edge, so use DAG shortest paths, Dijkstra, or Bellman–Ford according to the weight assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

BFS tree 还可给出 connected component、二分图的奇偶层着色，以及一个“某顶点为何可达”的 path certificate。它不给所有最短路，只给由邻居遍历顺序选出的一棵 shortest-path tree；不同邻接顺序可得到不同 parent，但距离相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A BFS tree also supplies a connected component, parity layers for bipartite testing, and a path certificate explaining why a vertex is reachable. It does not return every shortest path, only one shortest-path tree selected by neighbor order. Different adjacency orders may change parents while leaving distances unchanged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## DFS：沿分支深入再回溯
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*DFS: descending a branch and then backtracking*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

DFS 使用 recursion 或显式 LIFO stack：从当前顶点选择一个未发现邻居继续深入，直到无路可走才回溯。它正确找到所有从源点可达的顶点，但 parent path 只保证是一条 path，不保证最短；DFS 的价值来自嵌套的发现/完成区间和祖先关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
DFS uses recursion or an explicit LIFO stack. It chooses an undiscovered neighbor and continues as deeply as possible, backtracking only when no unexplored edge remains. It correctly finds every vertex reachable from the source, but a parent path is merely a path, not necessarily a shortest one. DFS derives its power from nested discovery/finish intervals and ancestor relationships.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 directed graph 中，搜索到指向当前 recursion stack 上灰色祖先的 back edge，就得到一个 directed cycle：祖先到当前点的 tree path 加该边闭合。指向已经完成的黑色顶点不代表 cycle，因为该点已不在当前祖先链上。在 undirected graph 中，回到直接 parent 的反向边是同一条无向 tree edge，必须忽略；遇到其他已发现祖先才给 cycle certificate。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a directed graph, an edge to a gray ancestor still on the current recursion stack is a back edge and certifies a directed cycle: combine the ancestor-to-current tree path with that edge. An edge to a finished black vertex does not itself indicate a cycle because that vertex is no longer on the active ancestor chain. In an undirected graph, the edge back to the immediate parent is the same undirected tree edge and must be ignored; an edge to another discovered ancestor supplies the cycle certificate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对整张图做 full DFS，要在每棵搜索树完成后从任意未发现顶点重新开始。无向图中每棵 DFS tree 正好覆盖一个 component；有向图中一次 DFS 只覆盖该根可达的区域，不能直接当 strongly connected components。DAG 的 reverse finishing order 给 topological order，详见 [[无环图：树、生成树、DAG 与拓扑排序#DAG 与拓扑排序]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A full DFS restarts from an arbitrary undiscovered vertex after each search tree finishes. In an undirected graph, each DFS tree covers exactly one connected component. In a directed graph, one DFS covers only the region reachable from its root and is not by itself a strongly connected-component decomposition. For a DAG, reverse finishing order yields a topological order; see [[无环图：树、生成树、DAG 与拓扑排序#DAG 与拓扑排序|DAGs and topological ordering]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->

## 完整例子：同一张图，BFS 与 DFS 回答不同问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: BFS and DFS answer different questions on the same graph*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑无向图，边为 $S-A,S-B,A-C,B-C,C-D$。从 $S$ 做 BFS，layers 是 $\{S\}$、$\{A,B\}$、$\{C\}$、$\{D\}$，所以 $dist(S,D)=3$。parent 可能给 $S-A-C-D$，也可能因邻接顺序给 $S-B-C-D$；两者都含 3 条边。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the undirected graph with edges $S-A,S-B,A-C,B-C,C-D$. BFS from $S$ produces the layers $\{S\}$, $\{A,B\}$, $\{C\}$, and $\{D\}$, so $dist(S,D)=3$. Parent pointers may yield $S-A-C-D$ or, under a different adjacency order, $S-B-C-D$; both use three edges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 DFS 按 $A,C,B,D$ 的邻居顺序探索，可能得到 tree path $S-A-C-B$，回溯后再到 $D$。它仍发现全部顶点，并且边 $B-S$（从当前递归关系看）揭示 cycle $S-A-C-B-S$，但 DFS tree 中到某点的路径顺序不承担 shortest-distance 语义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If DFS explores neighbors in the order $A,C,B,D$, it may create the tree path $S-A-C-B$, backtrack, and then visit $D$. It still discovers every vertex, and the edge $B-S$ reveals the cycle $S-A-C-B-S$ through the active ancestor relationship, but paths in the DFS tree do not carry shortest-distance semantics.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若把所有边定向为 $S\to A,S\to B,A\to C,B\to C,C\to D$，$B$ 遇到已完成的 $C$ 并不产生 directed cycle；只有再加入 $D\to A$，DFS 才会在相应 recursion stack 上发现 back edge，得到 $A\to C\to D\to A$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If every edge is oriented as $S\to A,S\to B,A\to C,B\to C,C\to D$, reaching an already finished $C$ from $B$ does not create a directed cycle. Only after adding $D\to A$ can DFS encounter a back edge on the relevant recursion stack and return the cycle $A\to C\to D\to A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 诊断与选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnostics and method choice*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 任务 | 首选入口 | 输出/证书 | 不能推出 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 无权最少边数 | BFS | distance layers、parent shortest path | 加权最短路 |
| 单源可达 | BFS 或 DFS | visited set、parent path | 强连通分量（仅一次 directed search） |
| 无向 components | full BFS/DFS | 一棵树对应一个 component | 有向强连通分量 |
| directed cycle | DFS | 指向灰色祖先的 back edge 与 cycle | 指向黑色点就成环 |
| DAG topological order | Kahn 或 full DFS | 全部顶点顺序；失败时 cycle | 对含环图给合法顺序 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Task | Preferred entry | Output or certificate | What it does not imply |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum edge count in an unweighted graph | BFS | Distance layers and a parent shortest path | Weighted shortest paths |
| Single-source reachability | BFS or DFS | Visited set and a parent path | Strong components from one directed search |
| Undirected components | Full BFS/DFS | One search tree per component | Directed strong components |
| Directed-cycle detection | DFS | A back edge to a gray ancestor and a cycle | That any edge to a black vertex closes a cycle |
| DAG topological order | Kahn's algorithm or full DFS | A full vertex order; a cycle on failure | A legal order for a cyclic graph |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果算法结果反常，按这个顺序检查：边方向是否建反；顶点是否在发现时标记；图是否加权；无向 DFS 是否错误地把 parent edge 当 back edge；是否只从一个根搜索却声称覆盖全图；邻接表示的复杂度是否被遗漏。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a result looks wrong, check in this order: whether edge directions were reversed; whether vertices were marked at discovery; whether the graph is weighted; whether undirected DFS mistakenly treated the parent edge as a back edge; whether a single-root search was claimed to cover the whole graph; and whether the graph representation was omitted from the complexity analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：walk 中有重复顶点时，为什么总能得到一条不更长的 path？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why does a walk with repeated vertices always contain a path no longer than the walk?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 两次出现同一顶点之间是一段 closed subwalk；删掉它仍把原来的前后两段接在同一顶点上，并严格缩短或保持目标可达。反复删除直到没有重复顶点，就得到 path。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The segment between two occurrences of the same vertex is a closed subwalk. Deleting it reconnects the prefix and suffix at that vertex while preserving reachability and reducing length. Repeating this removal leaves a path.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 BFS 第一次发现顶点就能确定无权最短距离？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can BFS fix an unweighted shortest distance at first discovery?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> FIFO queue 保证先处理所有较小距离层。若新顶点存在更短路径，它在那条路径上的前驱应更早被处理并更早发现该顶点，与“现在才第一次发现”矛盾。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The FIFO queue processes every smaller-distance layer first. If the newly discovered vertex had a shorter path, its predecessor on that path would have been processed earlier and would already have discovered it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一条边权为 100 的直达边与三条各权为 1 的边同时存在，BFS 会选择哪条？这说明什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If a direct edge of weight 100 competes with three edges of weight one each, which does BFS choose, and what does that show?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> BFS 选择一条边的直达路，因为它最小化边数；总权重却是 100 而非 3。这说明带权最短路必须使用与权重条件匹配的算法。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> BFS chooses the one-edge direct route because it minimizes edge count, even though its total weight is 100 rather than three. Weighted shortest paths require an algorithm matched to the weight assumptions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### directed DFS 遇到黑色顶点为什么不能直接宣布有环？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can directed DFS not declare a cycle merely upon reaching a black vertex?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 黑色顶点已经完成，不在当前 recursion stack 的祖先链上；这条边可能只是指向一个共享的已完成依赖。指向灰色祖先的 back edge 才与 tree path 闭合成 directed cycle。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A black vertex is finished and no longer lies on the current recursion-stack ancestor chain; the edge may simply point to a shared completed dependency. A back edge to a gray ancestor closes a directed cycle with the tree path.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### adjacency list 下 BFS/DFS 为什么是 $O(|V|+|E|)$？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why do BFS and DFS run in $O(|V|+|E|)$ time with adjacency lists?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 每个顶点只被发现和处理一次；每条 directed edge 检查一次，无向边在两端列表中至多检查两次。总工作因此与顶点数和边数之和同阶。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Each vertex is discovered and processed once. Every directed edge is examined once, while an undirected edge appears in two lists and is examined at most twice. Total work is therefore linear in vertices plus edges.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
