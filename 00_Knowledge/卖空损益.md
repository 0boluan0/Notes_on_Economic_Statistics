# 固定股数卖空的损益由卖出款减买回成本并计入借券及分配补偿等现金流确定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fixed-quantity short-sale profit equals sale proceeds less repurchase cost, adjusted for borrowing, distribution compensation, and other cash flows*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

持有固定 $N>0$ 股普通股票空头，从每股 $S_0$ 卖出，到每股 $S_1$ 买回，假设无拆股等数量调整。忽略现金流时间价值时，令 $I$ 为期间实际获付的利息或返利，$F$ 为借券、融资及交易费用，$D$ 为须向出借方支付的分配补偿，则该头寸的净损益为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a fixed short position of $N>0$ ordinary shares sold at $S_0$ and repurchased at $S_1$, without share-count adjustments such as splits. Ignoring the time value of cash flows, let $I$ be actual interest or rebates received, $F$ borrowing, financing, and trading costs, and $D$ distribution compensation owed to the lender. Net profit is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Pi=N(S_0-S_1)+I-F-D.
$$

这是逐项现金流相加，不是收益率。若需要跨期价值，先把每笔收支按[[现金流评价时点|共同评价时点]]计量；若要计算投资者收益率，还须明确实际投入权益以及追保、提款等外部现金流，不能自动用卖出所得作本金分母。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This sums cash flows; it is not a return rate. For dated valuation, move receipts and payments to a [[现金流评价时点|common valuation date]]. An investor return additionally requires the actual equity contribution and treatment of margin deposits or withdrawals; short-sale proceeds are not automatically the invested-capital denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $N=100,S_0=50,S_1=40,I=0,F=60,D=40$，净损益为 $1,000-60-40=900$ 元。股价只跌 0.5 元时，价差收入 50 元不足以覆盖相同的 100 元成本，仍亏 50 元。“看对下跌方向”不足以保证盈利。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $N=100,S_0=50,S_1=40,I=0,F=60,D=40$, net profit is $1,000-60-40=900$. A price decline of only 0.5 produces 50 before the same 100 of costs, leaving a loss of 50. Correctly predicting direction need not produce a profit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对未对冲的普通股票空头，若股价非负却没有有限上界，纯价差收益最多为 $NS_0$，而亏损无有限上界。这个结论依赖该价格范围和未对冲头寸；不能外推到所有证券、有限支付衍生品或已对冲组合。保证金与强制平仓也不是在跳价或缺乏可成交卖盘时保证买回价格的保险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an unhedged ordinary-stock short with a nonnegative price and no finite price ceiling, price-only gain is bounded by $NS_0$, whereas loss has no finite bound. The conclusion depends on that price range and unhedged position, not every security, bounded-payoff derivative, or hedged portfolio. Margin and forced close-out do not insure the execution price through jumps or unavailable liquidity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
