# 可信因果研究必须把目标量、分配机制、对照反事实、识别假设、威胁与适用总体连成一条可审计证据链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A credible causal study must connect its estimand, assignment mechanism, counterfactual comparison, identifying assumptions, threats, and target population into an auditable chain*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 一个显著系数不是研究设计
> 因果结论的最短完整链条是：
> $$
> \text{问题与 estimand}
> \rightarrow\text{处理怎样产生}
> \rightarrow\text{谁代表反事实}
> \rightarrow\text{识别假设}
> \rightarrow\text{匹配的估计量与统计推断}
> \rightarrow\text{威胁、边界与适用总体}.
> $$
> 任一环缺失，后面的精确计算都不能自动补回。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A causal claim needs a complete chain: question and estimand; assignment mechanism; the comparison representing the counterfactual; identifying assumptions; a matching estimator and inference procedure; and finally threats, boundaries, and the target population. Precise calculations downstream cannot replace a missing upstream link.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 逐环检查，而不是先看 p 值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Audit each link before reading the p-value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. **目标：** 说明单位、处理版本、结果、时间范围、目标总体与效应口径。
2. **数据生成：** 说明抽样、处理分配、纳入与缺失怎样发生；区分随机抽样和随机分配。
3. **反事实：** 指出观察数据中的哪组单位或哪个时期被用来代表所需的反事实，并说明为什么这种比较可信。
4. **识别：** 写出真正承担因果解释的条件，如交换性、排除限制、平行趋势、连续性或无操纵；不要只写方法名。
5. **估计与推断：** 说明实际使用哪个估计量、权重怎样产生、标准误是否需要聚类以及若需要应在哪一层，并确认这些选择对应前面定义的目标量和分配层级。
6. **威胁：** 检查提前反应（anticipation）、外溢、同期冲击、样本构成变化、测量误差、结果缺失和模型外推。
7. **适用范围：** 区分样本内识别对象与希望推广到的人群、时期和政策版本。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. **Target:** State the units, treatment versions, outcome, horizon, target population, and causal contrast.
2. **Data generation:** Explain sampling, assignment, inclusion, and missingness; distinguish random sampling from random assignment.
3. **Counterfactual:** Identify which observed units or periods are used to represent the required counterfactual, and explain why that comparison is credible.
4. **Identification:** State the assumption doing the causal work—exchangeability, exclusion, parallel trends, continuity, or no manipulation—not merely the method's name.
5. **Estimation and inference:** State the estimator actually used, how the weights are produced, whether standard errors require clustering and, if so, at what level; verify that these choices match the previously defined target and assignment level.
6. **Threats:** Examine anticipation, spillovers, concurrent shocks, changes in sample composition, measurement error, outcome missingness, and model extrapolation.
7. **Scope:** Separate the identified object in the study sample from the people, periods, and policy versions to which the claim is extended.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 检验可以找矛盾，通常不能证明核心假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnostics can reveal contradictions but usually cannot prove the core assumption*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机化记录可以直接核对分配机制，但观察研究的关键反事实假设往往不可完全检验。DID 的处理前趋势、RD 的密度与协变量连续性、IV 的平衡与安慰剂结果，能发现某些不相容迹象；它们通过也不等于平行趋势、排除限制或无未测混杂已经被证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Randomisation records can directly audit an assignment mechanism, but the central counterfactual assumption in an observational study is often not fully testable. Pretreatment trends in DID, density and covariate continuity in RD, and balance or placebo outcomes for IV can expose particular contradictions. Passing them does not prove parallel trends, exclusion, or absence of unmeasured confounding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

稳健性分析也要对应威胁。换十种无关的控制集，只能说明结果对那十种规格不敏感；它不能替代一个说明处理为何近似外生的设计论证。最诚实的结论要同时报告支持链条的证据、仍不可检验的部分，以及结论因此被限制到什么范围。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Robustness checks must target a threat. Ten arbitrary control sets show sensitivity to those ten specifications, not why treatment is as-if exogenous. A defensible conclusion reports evidence supporting the chain, what remains untestable, and how those limits narrow the claim.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 五个处理前时期的联合前趋势检验得到 $p=0.34$，但置信区间仍容许具有经济意义的趋势差异。研究者最多能据此写到哪一步？还必须补充哪些设计论证？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A joint pretrend test over five pretreatment periods gives $p=0.34$, but its confidence intervals still allow economically meaningful trend differences. What is the strongest defensible statement, and which design arguments are still needed?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 最多可以写“在这些处理前时期、这套规格和检验力下，没有发现与平行趋势明显不相容的证据”。仍要论证对照组为何可信，并检查同期冲击、提前反应、样本构成和政策时点；置信区间允许的差异与未观察到的处理后反事实趋势也必须如实保留。详见 [[前趋势检验边界]]。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** At most, the study found no clear evidence incompatible with parallel trends for those pretreatment periods, that specification, and that power. It still must justify the comparison group and examine concurrent shocks, anticipation, composition, and policy timing; differences allowed by the confidence intervals and the unobserved post-treatment counterfactual trend must remain explicit. See [[前趋势检验边界|the pretrend-test boundary]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Athey and Imbens (2017), “The State of Applied Econometrics: Causality and Policy Evaluation”](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257%2Fjep.31.2.3)：核对研究设计、安慰剂与敏感性分析、外部效度及因果回归解释的分工。
- [Hernán and Robins, *Causal Inference: What If*](https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/miguel-hernan/wp-content/uploads/sites/1268/2024/04/hernanrobins_WhatIf_26apr24.pdf)：核对目标试验式问题定义、处理版本、交换性、正值性、失访与选择等证据链环节。
- [[前趋势检验边界]]：复用已独立核验的 DID 专门结论，即处理前趋势检验只能排查部分不相容证据，不能证明处理后的反事实平行趋势。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Athey and Imbens (2017) was checked for research-design reasoning, placebo and sensitivity analyses, external validity, and the causal interpretation of regression methods.
- Hernán and Robins, *Causal Inference: What If*, was checked for target-trial-style question definition, treatment versions, exchangeability, positivity, loss to follow-up, and selection.
- [[前趋势检验边界|The pretrend-test boundary]] supplies the separately verified DID-specific result that pretreatment tests can detect some incompatible evidence but cannot prove the post-treatment counterfactual trend.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
