# Pivots, rank and free variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*主元、秩与自由变量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After elimination, some unknowns can be chosen and the equations determine the others. **Pivot counting** tells us how many choices remain: for a system with $n$ unknowns and coefficient-matrix rank $r$, a consistent solution has $n-r$ freely chosen coordinates. Checking that the system is consistent comes first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

消元后，一些未知量可以自己选择，其余未知量由方程确定。**数主元**告诉我们还剩多少选择：含 $n$ 个未知量、系数矩阵秩为 $r$ 的相容方程组，有 $n-r$ 个坐标可自由指定。先确认方程组有解，才能把这个数解释为真实解的自由度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two pivots among four unknowns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*四个未知量中的两个主元*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose reversible row operations have turned the augmented matrix into
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设经过可逆行操作，增广矩阵变为

$$
[R\mid d]=
\left[\begin{array}{rrrr|r}
1&2&0&-1&4\\
0&0&1&3&2\\
0&0&0&0&0
\end{array}\right].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $R$ is the transformed coefficient matrix, and $d$ is the right-hand side transformed by the same operations. The last row says $0=0$, so it imposes no contradiction. A [[主元|pivot]] is a nonzero row's first nonzero entry in echelon form; the pivots here are in columns $1$ and $3$. Thus $x_1,x_3$ are pivot variables, while $x_2,x_4$ are [[自由变量|free variables]]. Set $x_2=s$ and $x_4=t$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $R$ 是变换后的系数矩阵，$d$ 是同步变换后的右端。最后一行表示 $0=0$，没有矛盾。[[主元]]是阶梯形中一个非零行的首个非零项；本例主元位于第 $1$、$3$ 列。因此 $x_1,x_3$ 是主元变量，$x_2,x_4$ 是[[自由变量]]。令 $x_2=s$、$x_4=t$，得到

$$x_1=4-2s+t,\qquad x_3=2-3t,\qquad
x=(4-2s+t,\ s,\ 2-3t,\ t)^T.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The superscript $T$ writes the list as a column via [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. Every pair $(s,t)$ gives a solution, and every solution appears exactly once, because its second and fourth coordinates recover $s,t$. There are two independent choices, despite three written equations and four unknowns. A free variable can occur in an equation and affect the pivot variables; “free” does not mean absent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上标 $T$ 用[[矩阵转置|转置]]把列表写成列向量。每一对 $(s,t)$ 都给出一个解，而且每个解恰好出现一次，因为它的第二、第四坐标能取回 $s,t$。虽然写了三个方程、有四个未知量，独立选择只有两个。自由变量可以出现在方程中并影响主元变量；“自由”并不表示“没有出现”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the count is n minus r
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么恰好剩下 n−r 个选择*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$ and $b\in\mathbb F^m$, for positive integers $m,n$ and one scalar field $\mathbb F$. Row reduction gives $R=EA$ and $d=Eb$, where $E$ is an invertible $m\times m$ matrix recording the row operations. Thus [[行操作的解集不变性|the same solution set]] is described by $Rx=d$. Take $R$ in [[行最简形|RREF]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A\in\mathbb F^{m\times n}$、$b\in\mathbb F^m$，其中 $m,n$ 为正整数，全部运算使用同一标量域 $\mathbb F$。行化简给出 $R=EA$、$d=Eb$，其中可逆的 $m\times m$ 矩阵 $E$ 记录所有行操作。因此 $Rx=d$ 描述的是[[行操作的解集不变性|同一个解集]]。取 $R$ 为[[行最简形]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If there are $r$ nonzero rows, their pivots occupy $r$ distinct columns. The [[RREF读取行空间基|nonzero rows form a row-space basis]]: they span the unchanged row space, and their distinct pivot positions make them independent. By [[行秩等于列秩|row-rank equality]], their number equals the [[矩阵秩|rank]] of $A$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若有 $r$ 个非零行，它们的主元占据 $r$ 个不同列。[[RREF读取行空间基|非零行构成行空间基]]：它们张成保持不变的行空间，而不同的主元位置保证线性无关。根据[[行秩等于列秩]]，其个数就是 $A$ 的[[矩阵秩|秩]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The other $n-r$ coefficient columns have no pivots. If every zero row of $R$ has a zero entry in $d$, choose the variables in these columns arbitrarily. Each nonzero row then determines exactly one pivot variable, since its pivot is one and the other pivot columns have zero entries in that row. This proves both that every parameter choice works and that it determines a unique solution. Ordinary echelon form gives the same conclusion by back-substitution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其余 $n-r$ 个系数列没有主元。如果 $R$ 的每个零行在 $d$ 中都对应零，就可以任意指定这些列的变量。每个非零行随后唯一确定一个主元变量，因为主元等于一，而且该行在其他主元列上的元素均为零。这同时证明了“每组参数都可用”和“每组参数确定唯一解”。普通阶梯形通过回代也给出同样结论。

> [!important] Count coefficient columns, after checking consistency · 先检查相容，再数系数列
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A consistent system with $n$ unknowns and rank $r$ has $n-r$ free coordinates. If $r=n$, it has one solution. If $r<n$ over $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$, it has infinitely many. Over a finite field with $q$ elements, it instead has $q^{\,n-r}$ solutions. A contradictory row means zero solutions, regardless of the number of nonpivot coefficient columns.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 含 $n$ 个未知量、秩为 $r$ 的相容系统有 $n-r$ 个自由坐标。若 $r=n$，解唯一；若 $r<n$ 且标量是实数或复数，解有无穷多个。若标量域只有 $q$ 个元素，则解数是 $q^{\,n-r}$。一旦出现矛盾行，解数就是零，无论系数部分还有几个非主元列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $Ax=0$, consistency is automatic. Set one free coordinate to one and the others to zero in turn to obtain the [[RREF读取零空间基|nullspace basis]]. Consequently $\dim N(A)=n-r$, in agreement with [[秩零度定理|rank–nullity]]. For nonzero $b$, the solution set is a translate of that nullspace, as explained in [[特解加零空间|particular solution plus nullspace]]; its parameter count need not describe a subspace through zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $Ax=0$，相容性自动成立。依次让一个自由坐标为一、其余为零，得到[[RREF读取零空间基|零空间基]]，因此 $\dim N(A)=n-r$，与[[秩零度定理]]一致。对非零 $b$，解集是零空间的平移，见[[特解加零空间]]；参数个数描述的集合未必是经过零点的子空间。

## Boundaries of the counting rule
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the final zero in the example's right-hand side is replaced by $1$, the last equation becomes $0=1$. The coefficient matrix still has two pivots, but there is no actual solution to parameterize. Fully reducing the augmented matrix creates a pivot in its last column. That is an inconsistency signal, not a fifth variable. In particular, count $n$ from the coefficient matrix $A$, excluding the augmented column.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把例子右端的最后一个零换成 $1$，最后一式就成为 $0=1$。系数矩阵仍有两个主元，但没有实际解可供参数化。把增广矩阵完全化简后，最后一列会出现主元；这是无解信号，不是第五个变量。尤其要从系数矩阵 $A$ 数未知量个数 $n$，不能把增广列算进去。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A pivot is identified from the echelon structure, not from a nonzero entry that happened to be on the original diagonal. A nonpivot column may have nonzero entries, as columns $2$ and $4$ do above. The count also does not equal the number of zero rows: zero rows concern redundant equations, while free columns concern choices of unknowns. If columns are reordered, record the new variable order before translating the answer back.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

主元要从阶梯结构识别，不能把原矩阵对角线上碰巧非零的项都算进去。非主元列也可以含有非零元素，就像上面的第 $2$、$4$ 列。自由变量数也不等于零行数：零行反映冗余方程，自由列反映未知量的选择。若重排列，必须记录新的变量次序，最后再还原答案。

> [!question]- What can four pivots tell you? · 四个主元能说明什么？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A matrix has seven coefficient columns and four pivots. Its homogeneous system has three free variables. For a specified nonzero right-hand side, we must first test consistency; if consistent, it also has three free variables. The pivot count alone cannot certify that this right-hand side is attainable.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个矩阵有七个系数列、四个主元。它的齐次系统有三个自由变量。对指定的非零右端，须先检查相容性；若相容，也有三个自由变量。仅凭主元数，不能保证这个右端可达。
