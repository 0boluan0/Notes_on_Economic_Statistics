# 世界银行收入组别、HDI等级与IMF经济体分类使用不同标准

看到“高收入”“极高人类发展”或“发达经济体”时，先确认是谁分类、用什么指标、对应哪一年。这些标签不是一张表的三个别名，也没有一个可通用于所有机构的“发达国家收入线”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When reading “high income,” “very high human development,” or “advanced economy,” identify the classifier, criteria, and reference year. These labels are not interchangeable, and there is no single income cutoff for “developed” status across all institutions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 分类 / Classification | 依据 / Basis | 标签告诉你什么 / Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 世界银行收入组别 / World Bank income groups | Atlas 法换算的[[国民总收入|人均 GNI]] / Atlas-method GNI per capita | 处在哪个收入区间 / Position within income bands |
| UNDP 人类发展等级 / UNDP human development categories | [[人类发展指数|HDI]]，综合健康、教育与收入 / HDI across health, education, and income | 综合指数处在哪个区间 / Band of a composite index |
| IMF WEO 经济体分类 / IMF WEO economy groups | 参考人均收入、出口多样化、国际金融体系融入等 / Income, export diversification, financial integration, and other considerations | 分析所用的经济体组别 / Analytical economy grouping |

Atlas 法对本年及前两年的汇率作通胀差异调整后平均，以减弱汇率波动对换算收入的影响；它不是[[购买力平价|PPP]]。世界银行收入阈值会按年调整，使用时应注明分类财政年度及所依据的收入年份。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The Atlas method averages the current and two preceding exchange rates after adjustment for inflation differences, reducing the effect of exchange-rate fluctuations. It is not [[购买力平价|PPP]]. World Bank thresholds are updated annually, so specify the classification fiscal year and underlying income year.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

UNDP 2025 技术说明把 HDI 不低于 0.800 列为“极高人类发展”；这个区间名称不等于“联合国认定为发达国家”。IMF 的分组也不机械套用一条人均 GDP 门槛；WEO 明确说明分类不依照严格统一的经济标准。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
UNDP's 2025 technical notes classify HDI values of at least 0.800 as “very high human development,” not as a UN designation of developed-country status. IMF grouping likewise does not mechanically apply one GDP-per-capita cutoff; WEO explicitly does not use strict uniform economic criteria.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，一份国别表可以同时列这三种分类，但比较时应保留三列及各自的定义。某国进入高收入组，不能据此直接改写它的 HDI 等级或 IMF 组别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A country table may include all three classifications, but should retain separate columns and definitions. Entering a high-income group does not by itself change a country's HDI category or IMF group.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
