# Noise amplification by small singular values
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*小奇异值放大噪声*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A measurement can reveal one coefficient strongly and another only weakly. Recovering the weak coefficient then requires dividing by a small response scale, which also enlarges any measurement error in that direction. This amplification is already present in the exact inverse problem; it can occur even with a perfect numerical solver.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一次测量可能有力地显示某个系数，却只能微弱地显示另一个。恢复后者时，就必须除以很小的响应尺度，同方向的测量误差也会一起变大。这种放大已经存在于精确的反问题中，即使数值求解完全无误，也会发生。

> [!example] One measurement direction is weak · 一个测量方向很弱
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let
>
> $$A=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&10^{-4}\\0&0\end{bmatrix},\qquad b_0=(2,10^{-4},0)^T.$$
>
> The noiseless coefficients are $(2,1)^T$. Adding $10^{-3}$ only to the second observation gives $(2,11)^T$: the coefficient changes by $10^{-3}/10^{-4}=10$. An error of the same size in the first observation changes the first coefficient by only $10^{-3}$. An error in the third observation changes no coefficient: every attainable output has third coordinate zero, so that component remains in the fitting residual.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取上述 $A,b_0$，无噪声系数为 $(2,1)^T$。仅在第二个观测上增加 $10^{-3}$，恢复的系数变为 $(2,11)^T$：第二项改变 $10^{-3}/10^{-4}=10$。第一观测上同样大的误差只让第一系数改变 $10^{-3}$。第三观测上的误差则不改变任何系数：所有可产生的输出第三坐标都为零，那部分误差只能留在拟合残差中。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Singular directions separate the effects
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用奇异方向分开观察*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep a finite $m\times n$ real or complex matrix $A$ fixed. The [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse]] $A^+$ maps data $b$ to the [[最小范数最小二乘解|least-squares coefficient vector of smallest Euclidean norm]], $x^+=A^+b$. For rank $r\ge1$, a [[紧SVD|compact SVD]] writes

$$A=U_r\Sigma_rV_r^*,\qquad A^+=V_r\Sigma_r^{-1}U_r^*.$$

The columns $u_i$ of $U_r$ are unit directions in the $m$-dimensional observation space; the columns $v_i$ of $V_r$ are unit directions in the $n$-dimensional coefficient space. Each family is [[标准正交组|orthonormal]]. The positive [[奇异值|singular values]] $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_r>0$ satisfy $Av_i=\sigma_i u_i$: input direction $v_i$ produces output direction $u_i$, stretched by $\sigma_i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定有限维 $m\times n$ 实或复矩阵 $A$。[[Moore–Penrose 伪逆]] $A^+$ 把数据 $b$ 映到[[最小范数最小二乘解|欧氏范数最小的最小二乘系数]] $x^+=A^+b$。当秩 $r\ge1$ 时，[[紧SVD]]给出上述两个公式。$U_r$ 的列 $u_i$ 是 $m$ 维观测空间中的单位方向；$V_r$ 的列 $v_i$ 是 $n$ 维系数空间中的单位方向，各组都是[[标准正交组]]。正[[奇异值]] $\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_r>0$ 满足 $Av_i=\sigma_i u_i$：输入方向 $v_i$ 产生输出方向 $u_i$，伸缩倍数为 $\sigma_i$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $^*$ means conjugate transpose, reducing to ordinary transpose for real matrices. For complex entries it also changes $a+bi$ to $a-bi$, with $i^2=-1$. Thus $u_i^*\varepsilon$ is the coordinate of an observation error $\varepsilon$ along $u_i$. The [[伪逆SVD公式|pseudoinverse formula]] reverses each positive stretch; it never divides by a zero singular value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $^*$ 表示共轭转置，实矩阵时就是普通转置；复数时还把 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$，其中 $i^2=-1$。所以 $u_i^*\varepsilon$ 是观测误差 $\varepsilon$ 在 $u_i$ 上的坐标。[[伪逆SVD公式]]撤销每个正的伸缩量，不会对零奇异值作除法。

> [!important] Exact fixed-matrix perturbation formula · 固定矩阵的精确扰动公式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If data change from $b_0$ to $b_0+\varepsilon$, write
>
> $$\varepsilon=\sum_{i=1}^r\varepsilon_i u_i+\varepsilon_\perp,
> \quad \varepsilon_i=u_i^*\varepsilon,\quad \varepsilon_\perp\perp C(A).$$
>
> Here $C(A)$ is the [[列空间|column space]], the set of attainable outputs, and $\perp$ denotes [[正交|orthogonality]]. Then
>
> $$\delta x^+=A^+\varepsilon=\sum_{i=1}^r\frac{\varepsilon_i}{\sigma_i}v_i,
> \qquad \|\delta x^+\|_2^2=\sum_{i=1}^r\frac{|\varepsilon_i|^2}{\sigma_i^2}.$$
>
> The component outside the column space is removed by $A^+$; each remaining component is scaled by its own reciprocal singular value.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 数据由 $b_0$ 改为 $b_0+\varepsilon$ 时，可按上式分解误差。$C(A)$ 是由全部可能输出组成的[[列空间]]，$\perp$ 表示[[正交]]，$\varepsilon_\perp$ 是列空间外的垂直分量。伪逆把它消去；其余每个误差坐标分别除以相应的正奇异值，得到上面的解变化与长度平方公式。

## Absolute amplification and relative conditioning
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*绝对放大与相对条件性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since all $\sigma_i\ge\sigma_r$, the formula gives the sharp [[谱范数|operator-norm]] bound

$$\|\delta x^+\|_2\le\frac{1}{\sigma_r}\|\varepsilon\|_2.$$

Equality is possible when the error points along a left singular direction for $\sigma_r$. This is an **absolute** amplification factor. A small positive singular value does not mean every realized error is large: an error may miss that direction. If $A=0$, its pseudoinverse is zero, every data vector is mapped to zero, and this reciprocal bound is replaced by zero rather than division by $\sigma_r$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于所有 $\sigma_i\ge\sigma_r$，上式给出精确的[[谱范数|算子范数]]上界 $\|\delta x^+\|_2\le\|\varepsilon\|_2/\sigma_r$。误差沿最小正奇异值对应的左奇异方向时可以取等号。这是**绝对**放大倍数。存在小正奇异值，并不表示每次实际误差都大，因为误差可能避开那个方向。若 $A=0$，伪逆为零，全部数据都映到零；此时放大系数为零，不再除以不存在的 $\sigma_r$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For full column rank, the relative matrix condition number is instead $\kappa_2(A)=\sigma_1/\sigma_n$. For $A=0.01I_2$, where $I_2$ is the identity matrix, the absolute factor is $100$ but $\kappa_2(A)=1$: both coordinates and the unperturbed solution are scaled equally. Conversely $A=\operatorname{diag}(100,1)$ has condition number $100$ but absolute factor only $1$. Always state [[条件数缩放边界|units and scaling]]. For a general least-squares target, relative solution error also depends on how much of $b$ lies in the column space; if $A^+b=0$, a relative solution error has no nonzero reference denominator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满列秩时，相对矩阵条件数是另一个量 $\kappa_2(A)=\sigma_1/\sigma_n$。对 $A=0.01I_2$，$I_2$ 为单位矩阵，绝对放大倍数为 $100$，但条件数是 $1$，因为两个坐标与未扰动解都按相同比例放大。反过来，$A=\operatorname{diag}(100,1)$ 的条件数为 $100$，绝对放大倍数却只有 $1$。必须声明[[条件数缩放边界|单位与缩放]]。对一般最小二乘目标，相对解误差还与 $b$ 有多少分量落在列空间有关；如果 $A^+b=0$，相对解误差就没有非零的参考分母。

## What can and cannot remove the amplification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*改善恢复时改变了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With singular values $1$ and $10^{-5}$, equal-sized errors in their left singular directions create coefficient changes differing by a factor of $10^5$. A more stable numerical solver can reduce additional rounding damage, but cannot remove this exact-data sensitivity. Also, the formulas fix $A$: if the matrix changes, the singular directions and even its rank can change, so this calculation is not a general perturbation bound for both inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若奇异值为 $1$ 和 $10^{-5}$，在两个左奇异方向上加入同样大的误差，系数变化相差 $10^5$ 倍。更稳定的求解器可以减少额外舍入损伤，却不能消除这种精确数据敏感性。此外，上述公式固定 $A$；矩阵改变时，奇异方向乃至秩都可能改变，所以它不是同时扰动两类输入的一般误差界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[截断SVD反演|Truncated SVD inversion]] removes selected small positive directions, while [[岭的SVD滤波公式|ridge filtering]] shrinks their contribution. These change the recovery rule and may discard part of the signal as well as noise. They require a modelling or accuracy decision; they are not simply more accurate evaluations of the original exact pseudoinverse. An adopted [[数值秩|numerical rank]] likewise needs a tolerance and scaling convention. Algebraic uniqueness of $A^+$ alone guarantees neither reliable recovery nor information about coefficients in the null space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[截断SVD反演]]去掉选定的小正奇异方向，[[岭的SVD滤波公式|岭滤波]]则收缩这些方向的贡献。它们改变恢复规则，除了噪声也可能舍弃部分信号，需要建模或精度方面的决定，并不只是把原精确伪逆算得更准。采用[[数值秩]]同样需要容差与缩放口径。$A^+$ 在代数上唯一，既不保证恢复可靠，也不会创造零空间中系数的信息。
