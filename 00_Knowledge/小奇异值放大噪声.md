# 固定 A 时，伪逆把 b 在第 i 个左奇异方向的扰动按 1/σ_i 缩放到第 i 个右奇异方向
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*With A fixed, the pseudoinverse maps a perturbation in the ith left-singular direction of b to the ith right-singular direction, scaled by 1/σ_i*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 方向性机制
> 若 $A=U_r\Sigma_rV_r^*$，则 $b$ 在左奇异向量 $u_i$ 方向上的扰动 $\varepsilon_i u_i$ 会产生
> $$
> A^+(\varepsilon_i u_i)
> =\frac{\varepsilon_i}{\sigma_i}v_i.
> $$
> 因而这个方向的绝对扰动被缩放为 $1/\sigma_i$ 倍；当 $\sigma_i$ 相对于当前尺度很小时，微小观测误差可以造成很大的解变化。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $A=U_r\Sigma_rV_r^*$, a perturbation $\varepsilon_i u_i$ in the left-singular direction $u_i$ produces $A^+(\varepsilon_i u_i)=(\varepsilon_i/\sigma_i)v_i$. The absolute perturbation is therefore scaled by $1/\sigma_i$ in that direction. When $\sigma_i$ is small relative to the working scale, a small observation error can cause a large change in the solution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把观测写成 $b=b_0+\varepsilon$，并将误差分解为
$$
\varepsilon=\sum_{i=1}^{r}\varepsilon_i u_i+\varepsilon_\perp,
\qquad \varepsilon_\perp\perp C(A).
$$
则
$$
A^+\varepsilon
=\sum_{i=1}^{r}\frac{\varepsilon_i}{\sigma_i}v_i.
$$
与列空间正交的 $\varepsilon_\perp$ 被 $A^+$ 消去，而各个可识别方向的误差分别由对应的 $1/\sigma_i$ 控制。这是固定矩阵 $A$、只扰动观测 $b$ 时的方向性结论；它不替代对矩阵扰动或相对条件数的单独分析。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $b=b_0+\varepsilon$ and decompose the error as $\varepsilon=\sum_{i=1}^{r}\varepsilon_i u_i+\varepsilon_\perp$, where $\varepsilon_\perp\perp C(A)$. Then $A^+\varepsilon=\sum_{i=1}^{r}(\varepsilon_i/\sigma_i)v_i$. The component orthogonal to the column space is removed, while each identifiable direction is controlled by its own reciprocal singular value. This is a directional statement for perturbations in $b$ with $A$ fixed; perturbations in $A$ and relative condition numbers require separate analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，若 $\sigma_i=10^{-4}$，而观测在 $u_i$ 方向上含有 $10^{-3}$ 的误差，则该误差对解中 $v_i$ 系数的贡献为 $10$。伪逆在代数上仍然唯一，但“唯一存在”不表示能够从有限精度数据中稳定恢复该方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if $\sigma_i=10^{-4}$ and the observation contains an error of $10^{-3}$ in the $u_i$ direction, that error contributes $10$ to the coefficient along $v_i$. The pseudoinverse remains algebraically unique, but uniqueness does not imply stable recovery from finite-precision data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

抑制这种放大需要额外的建模决定。[[截断SVD反演]]硬删除低于阈值的方向，[[岭的SVD滤波公式|岭正则化]]连续收缩各个正奇异方向；两者都不是重新计算同一个精确伪逆。把哪些方向视为可分辨，则必须声明 [[数值秩]] 的容差与尺度口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppressing this amplification requires an additional modelling decision. [[截断SVD反演|Truncated SVD inversion]] removes directions below a threshold, whereas [[岭的SVD滤波公式|ridge regularization]] continuously shrinks positive singular directions; neither is merely a recomputation of the same exact pseudoinverse. Any declaration of which directions are resolvable must also state the tolerance and scaling convention used for [[数值秩|numerical rank]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两个奇异方向的奇异值分别为 $1$ 和 $10^{-5}$，两个方向上都有同样大小的观测误差。在固定 $A$ 的前提下，哪个方向会在伪逆解中更敏感？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Two singular directions have singular values $1$ and $10^{-5}$ and contain observation errors of the same magnitude. With $A$ fixed, which direction is more sensitive in the pseudoinverse solution?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** $10^{-5}$ 对应的方向；该方向的误差被缩放为 $10^5$ 倍，而前一方向只被缩放为 $1$ 倍。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The direction associated with $10^{-5}$. Its error is scaled by $10^5$, whereas the other direction is scaled by only $1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
