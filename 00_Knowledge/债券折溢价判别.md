# 规则息票债券在付息后按票息与同周期收益率的大小判定折溢价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Immediately after a coupon, compare the period coupon rate with the matching yield to determine discount, par, or premium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对面值 $F>0$、剩余 $N\ge1$ 个完整付息期、每期息票 $C\ge0$、最后按面值偿还的债券，在付息后以每期收益率 $i>-1$ 定价。其价格低于、等于或高于面值，分别对应每期票息率 $C/F$ 小于、等于或大于 $i$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider face value $F>0$, $N\ge1$ complete remaining periods, coupons $C\ge0$, and redemption at par. Immediately after a coupon, price the bond at a period yield $i>-1$. Price is below, equal to, or above par exactly when the period coupon rate $C/F$ is below, equal to, or above $i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $a_N(i)=\sum_{k=1}^{N}(1+i)^{-k}>0$。由 $i\,a_N(i)=1-(1+i)^{-N}$，得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a_N(i)=\sum_{k=1}^{N}(1+i)^{-k}>0$. Using $i\,a_N(i)=1-(1+i)^{-N}$ gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P=C\,a_N(i)+F(1+i)^{-N},\qquad P-F=(C-iF)a_N(i).$$

正因年金因子为正，$P-F$ 与 $C-iF$ 同号。$i=0$ 时使用求和定义 $a_N(0)=N$，故 $P=F+NC$，无需除以零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since the annuity factor is positive, $P-F$ has the sign of $C-iF$. At $i=0$, use $a_N(0)=N$, giving $P=F+NC$ without division by zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，面值 $100$、年票面利率 $8\%$、半年付息，若 EAR 为 $8.16\%$，每期贴现率是 $\sqrt{1.0816}-1=4\%$，恰与每期票息率相同，故平价。直接比较年票面 $8\%$ 与 EAR $8.16\%$ 得出折价，会混淆口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For face value 100, an 8% annual coupon paid semiannually, and an 8.16% EAR, the period discount rate is $\sqrt{1.0816}-1=4\%$, equal to the period coupon rate. The bond is at par. Comparing the 8% annual coupon directly with the 8.16% EAR would incorrectly suggest a discount.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的价格没有应计利息；在两次付息日之间，[[债券全价]]可能因包含将付息票而高于面值。非面值赎回、浮息或不规则现金流也不能直接套这条判别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is no accrued interest in this coupon-date price. Between payment dates, the [[债券全价|dirty price]] may exceed par because it includes the approaching coupon. Non-par redemption, floating coupons, and irregular cash flows also require a different calculation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
