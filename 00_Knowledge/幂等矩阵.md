# Idempotent matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*幂等矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix is **idempotent** when applying its transformation twice gives exactly the same result as applying it once. After the first application, the output is already in a state that the transformation leaves unchanged. This describes a kind of projection, but it does not require the discarded direction to be perpendicular to the retained space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个矩阵**幂等**，是指它对应的变换作用两次，结果与一次完全相同。第一次作用后，输出已经落入变换不会再改变的状态。这描述了一类投影，但不要求被丢弃的方向垂直于保留的空间。

> [!example] Applying the rule again changes nothing / 再做一次不再改变
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let
>
> $$P=\begin{bmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> It sends $(x,y)^T$ to $(x+y,0)^T$. Applying the rule again gives $(x+y+0,0)^T$, the same vector. In particular, $(1,2)^T$ becomes $(3,0)^T$ and stays there. The superscript $T$ means [[矩阵转置|transpose]], used here to write a column vector.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上述 $P$ 把 $(x,y)^T$ 送到 $(x+y,0)^T$；再作用一次，得到的仍是 $(x+y,0)^T$。例如 $(1,2)^T$ 先变成 $(3,0)^T$，随后不再变化。$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，这里用来写列向量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real or complex square matrix $P$ of size $n$, the definition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实或复 $n$ 阶方阵 $P$，定义为

$$P^2=P.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $P^2=PP$ is [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]], representing two successive transformations. It does not mean squaring each entry. The equality is equivalent to $P(Px)=Px$ for every vector $x$. Both the zero matrix and the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]] $I_n$ are idempotent: one discards everything, while the other changes nothing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $P^2=PP$ 是[[矩阵乘法]]，表示连续执行两次变换，不是逐元素平方。矩阵等式等价于对每个向量 $x$ 都有 $P(Px)=Px$。零矩阵和[[单位矩阵]] $I_n$ 都幂等：前者丢弃所有分量，后者不作任何改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The retained and discarded parts
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*保留的部分与丢弃的部分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[列空间|column space]] $C(P)$ contains all possible outputs $Pu$. Each is fixed: $P(Pu)=Pu$. Conversely, any fixed vector $y=Py$ is an output and therefore belongs to $C(P)$. Thus the retained space is exactly the space of fixed vectors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[列空间]] $C(P)$ 包含全部可能输出 $Pu$；每个输出都固定不动，因为 $P(Pu)=Pu$。反过来，满足 $y=Py$ 的固定向量本身也是一个输出，所以属于 $C(P)$。因此，保留空间恰好是全部固定向量组成的空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every input splits as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任意输入都可拆成

$$
x=Px+(I_n-P)x.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first part belongs to $C(P)$. The second is in the [[零空间|nullspace]] $N(P)$, because $P(I_n-P)x=(P-P^2)x=0$. The two spaces intersect only at zero: a vector that is both fixed and sent to zero must be zero. This is a [[直和|direct-sum decomposition]], with a unique retained part and discarded part. It need not be an orthogonal decomposition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一部分属于 $C(P)$；第二部分属于[[零空间]] $N(P)$，因为 $P(I_n-P)x=(P-P^2)x=0$。两个空间只有零向量相交：既保持不动、又被送到零的向量只能为零。这构成一个[[直和]]分解，保留与丢弃的部分都唯一，但不一定正交。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening matrix, $C(P)$ is the horizontal axis and $N(P)$ is the line spanned by $(1,-1)^T$. Indeed,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头矩阵的 $C(P)$ 是水平轴，$N(P)$ 是由 $(1,-1)^T$ 张成的直线。具体地，

$$
(x,y)^T=(x+y,0)^T+(-y,y)^T.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The input is projected onto the horizontal axis along a slanted direction. This is why $P(1,2)^T=(3,0)^T$ is not the nearest horizontal-axis point: that point is $(1,0)^T$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入沿倾斜方向被投到水平轴上。因此 $P(1,2)^T=(3,0)^T$ 不是水平轴上离原输入 $(1,2)^T$ 最近的点；最近的是 $(1,0)^T$。

> [!important] Perpendicular projection adds a condition / 垂直投影还需一个条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under the usual Euclidean inner product, an idempotent matrix is an [[正交投影矩阵判别|orthogonal projector]] exactly when $P^*=P$, where $^*$ is conjugate transpose. For real matrices, this means symmetry. The example matrix is idempotent but not symmetric, so it is an oblique projection. In contrast, $\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{bmatrix}$ gives the perpendicular projection onto the same horizontal axis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在通常欧氏内积下，幂等矩阵成为[[正交投影矩阵判别|正交投影矩阵]]，当且仅当 $P^*=P$；$^*$ 为共轭转置，实数域中就是对称条件。开头矩阵幂等但不对称，因而是斜投影；显示的对角矩阵才是投到同一水平轴的垂直投影。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an idempotent matrix is invertible, multiplying $P^2=P$ by its inverse gives $P=I_n$. Nonidentity idempotent matrices therefore lose some input information. Their usefulness is precisely in keeping a chosen component, as in the products $AA^+$ and $A^+A$ associated with the [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆|Moore–Penrose pseudoinverse]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若幂等矩阵可逆，在 $P^2=P$ 两侧乘逆矩阵就得到 $P=I_n$，所以非单位幂等矩阵一定丢失部分输入信息。它们的用途正是保留指定分量，例如 [[Moore–Penrose 伪逆]]中的乘积 $AA^+$ 与 $A^+A$。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Penrose, “A generalized inverse for matrices” (1955), p.409, Hermitian idempotents](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100030401): distinguishes idempotence from the added Hermitian condition and identifies the pseudoinverse products as Hermitian idempotents.
- [Axler, *Linear Algebra Done Right*, 6.57 and 6.69, PDF pp.229 and 235](https://linear.axler.net/LADR4e.pdf#page=229): orthogonal-projection identities and the two pseudoinverse projectors. The general direct-sum argument, oblique example and invertibility conclusion above follow directly from $P^2=P$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Penrose 原论文第409页区分幂等与额外 Hermitian 条件，并说明伪逆乘积的 Hermitian 幂等性质。
- Axler 第6.57、6.69条核对正交投影恒等式及两类伪逆投影；一般直和论证、斜投影例子和可逆性结论由 $P^2=P$ 直接推出。
