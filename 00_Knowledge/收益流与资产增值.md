# 同一收益流及其资本化形成的资产增值不能作为两份独立收益相加
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A benefit flow and the asset appreciation that capitalises it are not two independent benefits.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果房价上涨只是未来通勤便利等收益被买方提前计入价格，那么“每年享受便利”和“房屋因便利而升值”是在衡量同一项改善。两者都列入社会总收益，会把相同价值计算两次。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If buyers capitalise future accessibility benefits into house prices, the flow of convenience and the corresponding appreciation measure the same improvement. Adding both double counts that value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在一个完全资本化的自造例中，某住宅每年多带来价值 100 的便利，永久持续，真实年贴现率恒为 5%。若唯一变化就是这项便利，则房价增加：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed full-capitalisation example, a home gains amenities worth 100 per year forever at a constant real rate of 5%. If nothing else changes, appreciation is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\Delta P=\sum_{t=1}^{\infty}\frac{100}{1.05^t}
=\frac{100}{0.05}=2000.
$$

用收益流得到现值 2000，或在上述条件下用房价增值估计 2000，是两条估值路径；相加为 4000 没有创造新的效益。将来的购房者支付更高房价，也不能与卖房者的收入割裂核算，见 [[转移支付核算]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Discounting the flow or using its price capitalisation gives 2,000 through two measurement routes, not 4,000 of value. Future buyers' higher payments must also be reconciled with sellers' receipts through [[转移支付核算|transfer accounting]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这条边界并不意味着所有房价变化都等于项目效益。供给约束、预期、其他设施或人群变化也会改变价格；只有识别出由同一改善造成且已经计入的部分，才谈得上去重。如何从价格中识别属性价值，见 [[享乐价格估值]]。分配分析仍可单独追踪原业主、租客与新买方分别得失多少，但不能因此再增加一份总收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Not every price change equals a project benefit. Supply constraints, expectations, other amenities and sorting can move prices. Identify the already-counted component before removing duplication; see [[享乐价格估值|hedonic valuation]]. Distributional analysis may still distinguish owners, renters and new buyers without creating another aggregate benefit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
