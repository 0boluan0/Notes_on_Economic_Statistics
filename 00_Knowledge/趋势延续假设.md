# 趋势延续假设是已形成的价格方向在相关尺度内倾向继续的技术分析前提
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Trend persistence assumes that an established price direction tends to continue over the relevant horizon.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

趋势延续假设认为，在所研究的时间尺度内，已经形成的价格方向倾向于继续，而不是每一步都立即反转。它为趋势跟随提供研究动机；实际使用前仍须定义如何识别趋势、从何时观察、预测多远，以及用什么数据检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Trend persistence assumes that an established price direction tends to continue over the horizon being studied rather than reverse immediately at every step. It motivates trend-following research, but application still requires a trend definition, an observation time, a forecast horizon, and a test design.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如前十日的平均价格上升，接下来一天可能上涨，也可能下跌。“前十日均值上升”是已经发生的描述；“未来五日的收益分布因此改变”是需要单独检验的预测命题。既不能从回看一段明显上涨的图就认定当时已经可识别，也不能把长期方向套到任意短周期。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After the average price rises over ten days, the next day can still rise or fall. The higher past average is a description; a resulting change in the next five days' return distribution is a separate predictive hypothesis. A trend that looks obvious afterward need not have been identifiable then, and a long-horizon direction need not apply at every shorter horizon.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[均线交叉规则]] 提供一种可明确计算的趋势规则，但规则定义不是有效性证明。即使样本中观察到收益延续，也不能单独判定它源于投资者反应不足，见 [[动量识别边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[均线交叉规则|moving-average crossover rule]] gives one computable trend rule, but its definition does not establish its effectiveness. Even observed return continuation does not by itself identify investor underreaction as the cause; see the [[动量识别边界|limits of mechanism identification from momentum]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
