# 公共品、搭便车与 Samuelson 条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Public Goods, Free Riding, and the Samuelson Condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原主题名称：公共品与外部性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Original topic name: Public Goods and Externalities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释非竞争且难排他的共同消费为何导致支付意愿隐瞒和私人供给不足，并给出有效供给数量的边际条件。
> **具体锚点：** 工厂排污只承担私人成本，污染损害落在居民；路灯一旦提供，新增居民使用几乎不减少他人照明且难排除。
> **核心难点：** 公共品由非竞争性和非排他性定义，不等于“政府提供的东西”；外部性要区分边际私人和社会成本/收益。
> **为什么重要：** 碳税、排放交易、疫苗、知识和公共基础设施都由这些结构决定政策。
> **继续：** 先画边际曲线和缺失市场，再比较税、补贴、数量、产权和公共供给。

> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Explain why non-rival and difficult-to-exclude consumption leads people to hide willingness to pay and causes underprovision, and state the marginal condition for efficient provision.
> **Concrete anchor:** A factory bears only its private production cost while residents bear pollution damage; once a streetlight is provided, an additional resident can benefit with almost no reduction in others' lighting and is difficult to exclude.
> **Central difficulty:** Public goods are defined by non-rivalry and non-excludability, not by government provision. Externalities require distinguishing private from social marginal costs or benefits.
> **Why it matters:** These structures determine the appropriate analysis of carbon taxes, emissions trading, vaccines, knowledge, and public infrastructure.
> **Continue with:** Draw the relevant marginal curves and identify the missing market before comparing taxes, subsidies, quantity regulation, property rights, and public provision.

公共品问题问的是：当一个人的消费很难减少他人可用量，而且未付费者很难被排除时，私人市场为何难以揭示真实支付意愿并提供有效数量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The public-goods problem asks why private markets struggle to reveal willingness to pay and provide an efficient quantity when one person's consumption scarcely reduces availability to others and non-payers are difficult to exclude.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 公共品分类
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Classifying Goods*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

竞争性问一人使用是否减少他人可用量，排他性问能否以合理成本阻止未付费者。纯公共品两者皆否；共有资源竞争但难排他，俱乐部品不太竞争但可排他。拥挤和技术可让分类随规模改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Rivalry asks whether one person's use reduces what remains for others; excludability asks whether non-payers can be kept out at reasonable cost. A pure public good is neither rival nor excludable. A common resource is rival but difficult to exclude from, while a club good is relatively non-rival but excludable. Congestion and technology can change the classification as scale changes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分类应针对具体消费场景而不是物品名称。公园在低使用量时近似非竞争，拥挤后变得竞争；数字内容可低成本复制，却可能通过登录和版权技术实现排他。因此“公共品”不是“由政府生产”的同义词。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Classification applies to a specific consumption setting, not merely to the name of an object. A park may be nearly non-rival at low use but rival when congested. Digital content can be copied at negligible cost yet made excludable through authentication and copyright technology. “Public good” is therefore not synonymous with “produced by government.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 搭便车与 Samuelson 条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Free Riding and the Samuelson Condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

个人可享受公共品却不透露支付意愿，私人供给往往不足。社会最优的纯公共品数量满足所有人的边际支付意愿纵向相加等于边际成本；私人物品则在共同价格下横向加数量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
People may enjoy a public good without revealing their willingness to pay, so private provision is often insufficient. At the efficient quantity of a pure public good, individuals' marginal willingness to pay is summed vertically and set equal to marginal cost. For a private good, quantities are instead summed horizontally at a common price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

写成条件即 $\sum_i MRS_i=MRT$：由于同一单位公共品同时被所有人消费，社会边际收益是个人边际收益之和。这个条件描述效率，不直接回答怎样诚实获得支付意愿、怎样分摊税费或哪一级政府应供给。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The condition is $\sum_i MRS_i=MRT$: because everyone consumes the same unit of the public good, the social marginal benefit is the sum of individual marginal benefits. This is an efficiency condition; it does not by itself reveal truthful willingness to pay, allocate tax burdens, or determine which level of government should provide the good.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：社区防洪
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Community Flood Protection*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若两户对额外一单位防洪的边际支付意愿分别为 60 和 40，而边际成本为 80，则社会边际收益为 100，仍应增加防洪；若各户单独购买，任何一户都可能因只能计算自己的收益而拒绝。强制筹资、受益收费或集体契约是在解决融资与偏好揭示，不是改变公共品本身的技术性质。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose two households' marginal willingness to pay for another unit of flood protection is 60 and 40, while marginal cost is 80. The social marginal benefit is 100, so protection should be expanded. If each household must purchase alone, either may refuse because it counts only its own benefit. Tax finance, benefit charges, or collective contracts address financing and preference revelation; they do not change the technological nature of the public good itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 供给方式与诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Provision Arrangements and Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政府供给、政府融资私人生产、俱乐部收费、慈善与社区自治都是可能制度。选择时比较排他技术、拥挤、辖区范围、偏好异质性、征收成本和问责。把“市场供给不足”直接跳成“中央政府亲自生产”遗漏了大量可行中间制度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Possible institutions include government production, public finance with private production, club fees, charity, and community self-governance. Compare exclusion technology, congestion, geographic reach, preference heterogeneity, collection costs, and accountability. Moving directly from “private provision is insufficient” to “the central government must produce it” skips many feasible institutional arrangements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么公园不因政府建设就自动是公共品？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is a park not automatically a public good merely because the government built it?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 公共品按消费的竞争性与排他性定义；收费、拥挤和容量可使公园更像俱乐部品或共有资源。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A public good is classified by rivalry and excludability. Fees, congestion, and capacity can make a park resemble a club good or a common resource.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Samuelson 条件为什么纵向加总支付意愿？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does the Samuelson condition sum willingness to pay vertically?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 同一单位公共品同时服务所有人，因此应把每个人对这一共同单位的边际收益相加，再与提供该单位的边际成本比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The same unit serves everyone simultaneously, so each person's marginal benefit from that shared unit is added before being compared with its marginal cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 发现搭便车后，为什么还不能立刻决定由谁生产？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does identifying free riding not immediately determine who should produce the good?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 搭便车说明私人融资可能不足；生产、融资和治理仍可由不同主体承担，需要比较成本、信息和问责。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Free riding shows that private finance may be insufficient. Production, finance, and governance can still be assigned to different actors and must be compared in terms of cost, information, and accountability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
