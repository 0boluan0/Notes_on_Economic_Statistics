# Indefinite quadratic forms
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*不定二次型*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An indefinite quadratic form takes positive values in some directions and negative values in others. To establish this, it is enough to exhibit one input of each sign. For example, $q(x_1,x_2)=x_1^2-x_2^2$ equals $1$ at $(1,0)$ and $-1$ at $(0,1)$. Those two evaluations already prove indefiniteness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不定二次型在某些方向取正值，在另一些方向取负值。证明它不定，只需找出一个正值输入与一个负值输入。例如，$q(x_1,x_2)=x_1^2-x_2^2$ 在 $(1,0)$ 处等于 $1$，在 $(0,1)$ 处等于 $-1$。这两次计算已经完成证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[二次型|real quadratic form]] is a scalar function $q(x)=x^TAx$ made from squares and coordinate products, with a fixed real $n\times n$ matrix $A$, an integer $n\ge1$, and a variable column $x\in\mathbb R^n$. The superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. If $A$ is not symmetric, [[二次型对称化|replace it by its symmetric part]] $S=(A+A^T)/2$ without changing any value of $q$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[二次型|实二次型]]是由平方项与坐标乘积组成的标量函数 $q(x)=x^TAx$。其中 $A$ 是固定的实 $n\times n$ 矩阵，$n\ge1$ 为整数，$x\in\mathbb R^n$ 为可变列向量，$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]。如果 $A$ 不对称，可以[[二次型对称化|换成对称部分]] $S=(A+A^T)/2$，而不改变 $q$ 的任何取值。

> [!important] Two signs are required · 必须有正负两种取值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The real quadratic form $q$ is **indefinite** if there exist vectors $u,v\in\mathbb R^n$ such that
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 实二次型 $q$ **不定**，是指存在向量 $u,v\in\mathbb R^n$，使
>
> $$q(u)>0,\qquad q(v)<0.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both vectors are necessarily nonzero, since $q(0)=0$. For a real symmetric matrix, saying that the matrix is indefinite means its quadratic form has this property. We compare the scalar values, not the signs of matrix entries.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因为 $q(0)=0$，两个向量必定都非零。说一个实对称矩阵不定，就是说它对应的二次型具有这个性质。比较的是标量取值，不是矩阵元素的符号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Zero directions do not settle the question
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*零值方向不能单独决定分类*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A form may vanish on many vectors and still be indefinite. Take
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个二次型即使在许多向量上等于零，也仍可能不定。考虑

$$S=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad q(x_1,x_2)=2x_1x_2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both coordinate axes give zero, so checking only those axes misses both signs. But $q(1,1)=2$ and $q(1,-1)=-2$. In contrast, $q(x_1,x_2)=x_1^2$ also has a nonzero zero direction, yet never takes a negative value. It is [[半正定矩阵|positive semidefinite]], not indefinite.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿两条坐标轴计算都得到零，所以只查坐标轴会漏掉正负两种取值。但 $q(1,1)=2$，$q(1,-1)=-2$。相比之下，$q(x_1,x_2)=x_1^2$ 也有非零零值方向，却从不取负值，因此属于[[半正定矩阵|半正定]]，不属于不定情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Similarly, a [[半负定矩阵|negative-semidefinite]] form cannot be indefinite because it never takes a positive value. Failing to prove positive definiteness is not enough: a negative-definite form or the zero form also fails that test. “The test is inconclusive” describes missing information; “the form is indefinite” is a definite conclusion that both signs occur.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，[[半负定矩阵|半负定]]二次型从不取正值，也不可能不定。没能证明正定还不够：负定二次型或零二次型同样不正定。“判据没有给出结论”表示信息不足；“二次型不定”则是已经确定正负两种取值都存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Indefinite does not mean singular
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*不定与奇异是两件事*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $S=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)$, a [[对角矩阵|diagonal matrix]], the form is $q(x_1,x_2,x_3)=x_1^2-x_2^2$. It has values $1$ and $-1$ at $(1,0,0)$ and $(0,1,0)$, so it is indefinite. Its third column is zero, so it is [[逆矩阵#Require recovery in both orders|singular]] and has determinant zero. The two-dimensional matrix $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ is also indefinite but has determinant $-1$ and is invertible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对[[对角矩阵]] $S=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,0)$，二次型为 $q(x_1,x_2,x_3)=x_1^2-x_2^2$。它在 $(1,0,0)$、$(0,1,0)$ 处分别为 $1$、$-1$，所以不定。它的第三列为零，因而是[[逆矩阵#Require recovery in both orders|奇异矩阵]]，行列式为零。二维矩阵 $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ 也不定，但行列式为 $-1$，且可逆。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[正定与半正定的谱判据|spectral sign criterion]] provides another way to identify the two signs: for a real symmetric matrix, its quadratic form is indefinite exactly when it has at least one positive and one negative eigenvalue. Zero eigenvalues may coexist with them. In one real dimension, $q(x)=ax^2$ cannot have both signs, so an indefinite real quadratic form needs at least two dimensions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[正定与半正定的谱判据|特征值符号判据]]提供另一种识别方法：实对称矩阵的二次型不定，当且仅当它至少有一个正特征值和一个负特征值；零特征值可以同时存在。一维实数情形 $q(x)=ax^2$ 不可能具有两种符号，因此不定实二次型至少需要二维。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What happens near the origin
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*原点附近会发生什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two witnesses also describe arbitrarily small displacements. Since $q(tu)=t^2q(u)$ and $q(tv)=t^2q(v)$, any nonzero sufficiently small real $t$ gives both a value above $q(0)=0$ and a value below it as close to the origin as desired. Thus the origin is neither a local minimum nor a local maximum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个见证方向还描述了任意小的位移。因为 $q(tu)=t^2q(u)$、$q(tv)=t^2q(v)$，取任意非零且足够小的实数 $t$，就能在离原点任意近的位置，同时找到高于与低于 $q(0)=0$ 的函数值。因此原点既不是局部极小点，也不是局部极大点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a general nonlinear function, an indefinite Hessian leads to the [[Hessian 鞍点判据|Hessian saddle-point test]] under its interior-stationary and $C^2$ assumptions. That theorem uses a Taylor remainder; the pure quadratic form here needs only its exact scaling identity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对一般非线性函数，不定 Hessian 在内点驻点及 $C^2$ 假设下导向 [[Hessian 鞍点判据]]。那个定理需要控制 Taylor 余项；这里的纯二次型只需精确的缩放恒等式。
