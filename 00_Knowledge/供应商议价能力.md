# 供应商议价能力是投入品提供方争取更有利交易条件的能力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Supplier bargaining power is input providers' ability to obtain more favorable transaction terms.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

供应商可通过提高投入价格、减少服务或收紧交付与付款条件争取价值。若下游难以转嫁成本，其利润可能受到挤压。这里的供应商不只指原材料卖方，还可能提供专门劳动、设备、场地或其他关键投入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppliers may capture value through higher input prices, reduced service, or tighter delivery and payment terms. Downstream profit can fall if those costs cannot be passed on. Inputs may include specialized labor, equipment, premises, and other essentials, not just raw materials.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，已获认证的关键零件只有少数来源，改换供应商须重新测试，可能增强供应商能力；“供应商少”本身还不足够，须问买方是否能替代投入、推迟采购或自行生产。不同投入应分别判断，不能把粮食原料与独家设备混成一个供应商评分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A critical certified component with few suppliers and costly retesting may give suppliers leverage. Supplier count alone is insufficient: can the buyer substitute inputs, postpone purchases, or produce internally? Analyze different inputs separately rather than assigning one score to commodity grain and exclusive equipment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
