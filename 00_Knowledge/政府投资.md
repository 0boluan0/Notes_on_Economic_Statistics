# 政府投资是政府为形成或改善长期公共生产与服务能力而安排的资本项目投入
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Public investment is government-arranged investment in capital projects that create or improve lasting public productive and service capacity.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政府投资在本卡指政府为形成或改善长期公共生产与服务能力而安排的资本项目投入，例如新建供水设施、扩建学校或对桥梁进行重大改造。它着眼于形成以后能够持续使用的能力，而不只是支付本期已经提供的服务。经济讨论中也有把教育、技能和知识积累称为广义公共投资的用法；不能因此把这类支出全部记为固定资产投资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here, public investment means government-arranged capital-project investment in lasting productive or service capacity, such as a water system, a school extension or a major bridge upgrade. It creates capacity for future use rather than merely paying for current services. Broader economic usage can include education, skills and knowledge accumulation; this does not make every such expenditure a fixed-asset investment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“政府安排”不要求所有施工、运营工作都由政府自己完成。政府可以直接实施，也可以通过合同组织其他主体参与；应分别判断 [[公共品的筹资、生产与治理|谁筹资、谁生产、谁治理]]。采用 [[政府和社会资本合作]] 时，还要阅读合同中的公共服务责任、私人管理责任和报酬条件，不能只凭资产名称判断安排的性质。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Government arrangement does not require government employees to build and operate everything. Separate [[公共品的筹资、生产与治理|funding, production and governance]] roles. For a [[政府和社会资本合作|public–private partnership]], inspect public-service obligations, private management responsibilities and payment conditions rather than relying on the asset's name.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在中国《政府投资条例》2019 年文本中，第 2 条采用更具体的法律口径：在中国境内使用预算安排资金进行固定资产投资建设，包括新建、扩建、改建和技术改造等。第 6 条又区分直接投资、资本金注入、投资补助和贷款贴息等资金安排。这是该条例的适用范围，不是所有国家、所有统计表共同采用的定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Article 2 of China's 2019 Government Investment Regulation uses a jurisdiction-specific scope: budget-funded fixed-asset construction in China, including new construction, extensions, reconstruction and technical upgrading. Article 6 distinguishes direct investment, capital injections, investment subsidies and interest subsidies. This legal perimeter is not a universal definition for every country or statistical table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读取统计数据还要再核对账户：GFS 的非金融资产净投资记录相关资产交易并扣除固定资本消耗，不等于任何被称为“政府投资”的预算资金总额；具体计算边界见 [[GFS费用与支出]]。而项目投入能否改善生活，需要相对于 [[公共项目反事实|没有该项目时的基准情景]]，比较资源的 [[社会机会成本]] 与服务收益，不能由投资金额本身推出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check the accounting measure separately. GFS net investment in nonfinancial assets records relevant asset transactions and deducts consumption of fixed capital; it is not interchangeable with every budget aggregate labelled government investment. See [[GFS费用与支出|GFS expense and expenditure]] for its boundary. Whether investment improves lives depends on service benefits and [[社会机会成本|social opportunity cost]] relative to the [[公共项目反事实|without-project baseline]], not on the amount invested alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
