# Linear map
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*线性映射*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **linear map** is a function that respects the way vectors are added and scaled. We can first combine inputs and then apply the function, or first apply it to each input and then combine the outputs with the same coefficients. Both routes must give the same answer. Here an **image** simply means an output: the image of $v$ under $T$ is $T(v)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**线性映射**是一种保持向量加法与数乘的函数。可以先组合输入、再应用函数，也可以先分别求输出、再按原来的系数组合输出，两条路线必须得到同一结果。这里的**像**就是输出：向量 $v$ 在映射 $T$ 下的像是 $T(v)$。

> [!example] Combining before or after mapping · 先组合还是先映射
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$ given by $T(s,t)=(s+t,t)$: add the second coordinate to the first, and keep the second. For $u=(1,0)$ and $v=(0,1)$, we obtain
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑 $T:\mathbb R^2\to\mathbb R^2$，规则为 $T(s,t)=(s+t,t)$：把第二坐标加到第一坐标，第二坐标保持不变。取 $u=(1,0)$、$v=(0,1)$，得到
>
> $$T(u)=(1,0),\qquad T(v)=(1,1),\qquad
> T(u+v)=T(1,1)=(2,1)=T(u)+T(v).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The same rule gives $T(3u-2v)=T(3,-2)=(1,-2)=3T(u)-2T(v)$. These computations illustrate the requirement; proving linearity means checking it for every allowed input and coefficient, not just these examples.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 同样，$T(3u-2v)=T(3,-2)=(1,-2)=3T(u)-2T(v)$。这些计算展示了要求是什么；要证明线性，仍须对所有允许的输入和系数检查，不能只靠这几个例子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The rule for all inputs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*对所有输入都成立的规则*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $V$ and $W$ be [[向量空间|vector spaces]] over the **same scalar field** $\mathbb F$, usually $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. A vector space supplies addition, scalar multiplication and a zero vector. The domain $V$ contains the inputs, and the codomain $W$ contains the outputs; they need not be the same space or have the same dimension. A function $T:V\to W$ is **linear over $\mathbb F$** when
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $V,W$ 是**同一标量域** $\mathbb F$ 上的[[向量空间]]，通常取 $\mathbb R$ 或 $\mathbb C$。向量空间提供加法、数乘和零向量。定义域 $V$ 容纳输入，陪域 $W$ 容纳输出；两者不必是同一个空间，也不必具有相同维数。若函数 $T:V\to W$ 满足

$$T(\alpha u+\beta v)=\alpha T(u)+\beta T(v)
\quad\text{for every }u,v\in V,\ \alpha,\beta\in\mathbb F,$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
then combining inputs commutes with applying $T$. The symbols $\alpha,\beta$ are scalar coefficients, and $Tu$ is an alternative notation for $T(u)$. Equivalently, the two requirements are **additivity**, $T(u+v)=T(u)+T(v)$, and **homogeneity**, $T(cu)=cT(u)$ for every scalar $c\in\mathbb F$. The combined formula gives these by choosing coefficients $1,1$ or $c,0$; conversely, additivity followed by homogeneity gives the combined formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

就称它是**在 $\mathbb F$ 上的线性映射**。其中 $\alpha,\beta$ 是标量系数，$Tu$ 也表示 $T(u)$。等价地，要求同时满足**加法保持** $T(u+v)=T(u)+T(v)$ 和**数乘保持** $T(cu)=cT(u)$，其中 $c\in\mathbb F$ 任意。在合并公式中取系数 $1,1$ 或 $c,0$，便得到这两条；反过来，先用加法保持、再用数乘保持，就得到合并公式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening example, take arbitrary real pairs $u=(s,t)$, $v=(p,q)$ and real scalars $\alpha,\beta$. Direct calculation proves the required identity:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对开头的例子，取任意实数坐标对 $u=(s,t)$、$v=(p,q)$ 及实数标量 $\alpha,\beta$。直接计算就能证明所需恒等式：

$$\begin{aligned}
T(\alpha u+\beta v)
&=T(\alpha s+\beta p,\alpha t+\beta q)\\
&=(\alpha(s+t)+\beta(p+q),\alpha t+\beta q)\\
&=\alpha T(u)+\beta T(v).
\end{aligned}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What linearity forces—and what it does not
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*线性性迫使什么成立，又不保证什么？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking the scalar to be zero gives $T(0_V)=0_W$, where the subscripts distinguish the zero vectors in the two spaces. Repeated addition and scaling also give, for every finite list of inputs $v_1,\ldots,v_k$ and scalar coefficients $c_1,\ldots,c_k$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把标量取为零，便得到 $T(0_V)=0_W$，下标区分两个空间各自的零向量。重复使用加法保持和数乘保持，还得到：对任意有限输入组 $v_1,\ldots,v_k$ 和标量系数 $c_1,\ldots,c_k$，

$$T\!\left(\sum_{j=1}^k c_jv_j\right)=\sum_{j=1}^k c_jT(v_j).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[基|basis]] gives every input one unique expression as such a combination. Consequently its images determine the whole linear map. The separate [[线性映射的基向量像|basis-image extension theorem]] also proves existence: any prescribed outputs on a finite basis extend to exactly one linear map. The images may coincide or be zero; for example, the zero map sends every input to $0_W$ and is linear.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[基]]使每个输入都有唯一的这种组合表示，所以知道基向量的像，就能确定整个线性映射。[[线性映射的基向量像]]还证明存在性：对有限基任意指定输出，都能延拓成唯一线性映射。输出可以相同，也可以是零；例如零映射把所有输入都送到 $0_W$，它仍然是线性的。

> [!warning] A zero test can disprove, but cannot prove, linearity · 零向量检验只能排除，不能单独证成
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $m,n$ be positive integers, $A$ a fixed $m\times n$ matrix over $\mathbb F$, and $b\in\mathbb F^m$ a fixed vector. For $x\in\mathbb F^n$, the rule $F(x)=Ax+b$ is an **affine map**, a [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] followed by a fixed translation. If $b\ne0$, then $F(0)=b\ne0$, so it is not linear. When $b=0$, distributivity of matrix multiplication proves linearity. In the other direction, $q(x)=x^2$ on $\mathbb R$ does send zero to zero, but $q(1+1)=4\ne2=q(1)+q(1)$: passing the zero test is insufficient.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $m,n$ 为正整数，$A$ 是 $\mathbb F$ 上固定的 $m\times n$ 矩阵，$b\in\mathbb F^m$ 是固定向量。对 $x\in\mathbb F^n$，规则 $F(x)=Ax+b$ 是**仿射映射**，即先做[[矩阵乘法]]、再做固定平移。若 $b\ne0$，则 $F(0)=b\ne0$，所以不线性；当 $b=0$ 时，矩阵乘法的分配律保证线性。反过来，实数上的 $q(x)=x^2$ 虽把零送到零，却满足 $q(1+1)=4\ne2=q(1)+q(1)$，因此通过零向量检验还不够。

## Whole polynomials can be inputs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*输入也可以是一整个多项式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $P_2$ be the real vector space of polynomials of degree at most two, including the zero polynomial, and let $P_1$ contain those of degree at most one. The operation $D:P_2\to P_1$ taking a [[导数|derivative]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $P_2$ 是次数不超过二的实系数多项式连同零多项式组成的向量空间，$P_1$ 则由次数不超过一的多项式组成。求[[导数]]的操作 $D:P_2\to P_1$ 为

$$D(a+bx+cx^2)=b+2cx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here one entire polynomial is one input vector; $x$ is its variable, and $a,b,c$ are its coefficients. Combining polynomial coefficients before applying this rule gives $D(\alpha p+\beta q)=\alpha Dp+\beta Dq$ for real scalars $\alpha,\beta$. Thus differentiation is linear as a map between these function spaces, even though its inputs need not have straight-line graphs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里一整个多项式是一个输入向量，$x$ 是多项式的变量，$a,b,c$ 是系数。先组合多项式系数、再应用这条规则，便得到 $D(\alpha p+\beta q)=\alpha Dp+\beta Dq$，其中 $\alpha,\beta$ 为实数。因此，求导作为这些函数空间之间的映射是线性的，尽管输入函数的图像未必是直线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The scalar field matters too. For a complex number $z=a+bi$, with real $a,b$ and $i^2=-1$, its conjugate is $\overline z=a-bi$. The map $C(z)=\overline z$ on $\mathbb C$ preserves addition and real scalar multiplication, so it is real-linear. It is not complex-linear: $C(i\cdot1)=-i$, whereas $iC(1)=i$. Linearity always refers to the specified scalar field.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标量域也很重要。对复数 $z=a+bi$，其中 $a,b$ 为实数、$i^2=-1$，其共轭为 $\overline z=a-bi$。映射 $C(z)=\overline z$ 保持加法和实数倍乘，所以把 $\mathbb C$ 看作实向量空间时，它是线性的；但它不保持复数倍乘，因为 $C(i\cdot1)=-i$，而 $iC(1)=i$。线性性始终相对于已指定的标量域而言。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A map specifies what happens to the objects themselves. For finite-dimensional domain and codomain, choosing an ordered basis in each space gives a matrix representation; changing the bases can change the representing matrix without changing the map. This distinction is developed in [[线性映射与矩阵表示|linear maps and their matrix representations]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

映射规定对象本身受到怎样的作用。对有限维定义域与陪域，在两边各选一组有序基，才得到矩阵表示；换基可能改变表示矩阵，但不改变映射本身。详见[[线性映射与矩阵表示]]。
