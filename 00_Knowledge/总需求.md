# 总需求是在其他需求条件给定时不同价格水平对应的国内产出计划总支出
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Aggregate demand relates the price level to planned spending on domestic output, holding other demand conditions fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 AD-AS 模型中，总需求曲线 AD 给出每个一般价格水平 $P$ 下计划购买的实际国内产出 $Y$。需求来自消费、投资、政府购买与净出口；这里是**计划支出关系**，不只是把事后[[国内生产总值|GDP 核算恒等式]]重新命名。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an AD-AS model, AD shows planned purchases of real domestic output $Y$ at each general price level $P$. Spending comes from consumption, investment, government purchases, and net exports. This is a **planned expenditure relationship**, not merely a new label for the ex-post [[国内生产总值|GDP accounting identity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准模型中，价格变化可经实际财富、利率和净出口渠道影响支出，形成向下倾斜的 AD。纵轴是价格水平而不是通胀率；同一条曲线上移动表示 $P$ 变化，信心、财政或外部需求等其他条件改变则可能构成[[总需求冲击|曲线移动]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the standard model, prices affect spending through real wealth, interest rates, and net exports, producing a downward-sloping AD curve. The vertical axis is the price level, not inflation. A price change moves along a curve; changes in confidence, policy, or external demand can [[总需求冲击|shift the curve]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如示意关系 $P=200-Y$ 表示在给定需求条件下，$P=100$ 对应计划产出 100；若需求条件改变为 $P=220-Y$，同一价格下计划支出变为 120。这是教学关系，不是估计所得宏观方程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The illustrative relation $P=200-Y$ gives planned output 100 at $P=100$. After a shift to $P=220-Y$, planned spending at the same price is 120. These are constructed teaching equations, not estimated macroeconomic equations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
