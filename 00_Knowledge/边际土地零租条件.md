# 最高成本的在产土地只有在价格恰等于其无租成本时才是零租边际

“目前耕种的土地中成本最高”描述的是排序；“再增加产量恰能覆盖全部无租成本”描述的是边际条件。**前一句本身推不出零地租。** 当已耕土地全部用满容量，粮价可以高于所有在产土地的无租成本，连其中最差的一类也有剩余。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Having the highest cost among active land grades is a ranking; just covering all non-rent costs on an additional unit is a marginal condition. **The ranking alone does not imply zero rent.** If all active grades are at capacity, price can exceed every active grade's non-rent cost, leaving a surplus even on the worst of them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用 [[有限容量供给画法]] 的构造：三类土地单位成本为 2、4、6 货币／吨，各有 10 吨／期容量。成本包含正常资本报酬，没有其他待扣费用或土地替代用途收入；在竞争租赁能实现剩余归属时，第 $i$ 类土地每期的总租金为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the construction in [[有限容量供给画法|drawing finite-capacity supply]]: unit costs of 2, 4 and 6 currency units per tonne and capacities of 10 tonnes per period each. Costs include a normal capital return, with no further deductions or alternative-use income. Where competitive leasing transfers the surplus to the landowner, total rent per period is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
R_i=(P-c_i)q_i.
$$

$P-c_i$ 是每吨差额，乘本期产量后才得到总租金。令反需求 $P=3.5-0.1Q$，均衡为 $Q=10,P=2.5$：只有第一类地满产，第二类的成本 4 尚未达到。第一类既是最好也是最差的在产地，却有 $R_1=(2.5-2)10=5$。这已构成“最差在产地必零租”的反例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$P-c_i$ is a surplus per tonne, not total rent. With inverse demand $P=3.5-0.1Q$, equilibrium is $Q=10,P=2.5$. Only the first grade operates, at capacity; price remains below the second grade's cost of 4. The sole active grade is both the best and the worst active land, yet its rent is $(2.5-2)10=5$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若需求变成 $P=4.5-0.1Q$，仍有 $Q=10$，价格却升为 3.5，第一类总租金成为 15。再变成 $P=5.5-0.1Q$，才得到 $P=4,Q=15$：第二类只用一半容量，其增量产出恰好覆盖成本，成为真正的零租边际。这三步区分了涨价、增产与开垦新等级，不能把它们当成每次需求变化都同时发生。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing demand to $P=4.5-0.1Q$ keeps output at 10 while raising price to 3.5 and rent to 15. With $P=5.5-0.1Q$, equilibrium instead becomes $P=4,Q=15$: the second grade uses only half its capacity and provides a genuinely zero-rent incremental margin. Price increases, output expansion and entry of a new grade need not occur together after every demand change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在本模型里，要以某等级的成本决定价格，须有该等级可竞争性启用的闲余容量、允许部分启用，而且均衡需求恰落在这段供给上。若价格高于成本，经营者仍能增加产量；若低于成本，这部分便退出，因此 $P=c_m$。没有闲余容量，这个调整理由就失效。土地的其他用途、合同约束或进入限制也必须另计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a grade's cost to determine price here, capacity must remain competitively available, partial use must be feasible, and equilibrium demand must intersect that supply segment. A price above cost permits profitable expansion; a price below cost induces withdrawal, giving $P=c_m$. Without spare capacity, this adjustment argument fails. Alternative land uses, contractual restrictions and entry barriers require separate treatment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Ricardo 的 [[级差地租]] 例是相对无租生产选择的比较。在上述只有第一类地满产的状态，正租来自容量稀缺，不能说成第一类相对另一块已耕无租地的肥力差额。理解原理论时，应先找它使用的无租边际，再判断现实或构造例是否具备相同条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ricardo's [[级差地租|differential-rent]] example compares returns with a rent-free production alternative. In the state where only the first grade is active at capacity, positive rent reflects capacity scarcity, not a fertility advantage over another cultivated rent-free plot. Identify the theory's rent-free margin before applying it to a different setting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ricardo（1821），[《原理》II.3–15](https://www.econlib.org/library/Ricardo/ricP.html?chapter_num=3#2.3)：支持可得土地、竞争性资本收益和无租末次投入的论证背景。有限容量反例是对推广条件的检查，不称为原书定理。
- 本卡三个均衡均由分段供给与相应反需求共同求出；租金依单位成本、产量和价格逐项计算。结论限定在没有额外费用、替代用途收入和阻碍调整的限制时。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chapter II supplies the competitive and rent-free-margin background. The constructed counterexample tests an extension of that argument rather than attributing a new theorem to Ricardo. All three equilibria and rents have been checked within the stated cost and adjustment assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
