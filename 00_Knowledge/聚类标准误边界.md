# Cluster-robust inference
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*聚类标准误的适用边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Six students from three schools may contain less independent information than six students sampled separately. Pupils in the same school can share an unobserved teaching shock. A [[标准误含义|standard error]] describes how an estimate would vary across repeated samples; treating shared shocks as six independent surprises can misstate that variability. Cluster-robust inference keeps a fitted coefficient and estimates its uncertainty while allowing specified groups of observations to move together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

来自三所学校的六名学生，可能比六名独立抽取的学生提供更少的独立信息。同校学生可能共享未观测的教学冲击。[[标准误含义|标准误]]描述估计量在重复抽样中的波动；把共同冲击当成六次独立意外，可能算错波动。聚类稳健推断保留已拟合系数，在允许指定组内观测共同波动的前提下估计其不确定性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **cluster** here is a group defined for the sampling or dependence model, such as a school, state or repeatedly observed person. It is not a group discovered by an unsupervised clustering algorithm. Choosing the group is part of the statistical argument, not merely supplying an ID column to software.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的**簇**是按抽样或依赖模型规定的组，例如学校、州、被反复观测的个人；不是无监督聚类算法发现的类别。如何分组属于统计论证，不只是给软件输入一列 ID。

> [!example] A school shock appears twice · 学校冲击出现两次
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $Y_{gi}=\mu+A_g+e_{gi}$, where $\mu$ is the common mean, $g=1,2,3$ labels schools and $i=1,2$ labels pupils. All three school shocks $A_g$ and six pupil shocks $e_{gi}$ are independent, mean zero and variance one. Each score has variance $2$; two pupils in the same school have covariance $1$; scores from different schools are independent.
>
> The overall mean is $\bar Y=\mu+(A_1+A_2+A_3)/3+\sum_{g,i}e_{gi}/6$. Its variance is $1/3+1/6=1/2$. Pretending the six scores were independent would give $2/6=1/3$. This is an exact calculation from the stated model, not a claim that a three-cluster variance estimate is reliable.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\mu$ 是共同均值，$g$ 标学校，$i$ 标学生。三个学校冲击与六个个人冲击都独立、零均值、单位方差。每个成绩方差为 $2$，同校两个成绩协方差为 $1$，不同学校独立。总体平均的方差为 $1/3+1/6=1/2$；假装六个成绩独立只得到 $1/3$。这是指定模型的精确计算，不是说三个簇已经足以可靠估计聚类方差。

## What the one-way covariance estimate includes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*单向聚类方差保留哪些项*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the basic model-based argument, use real $y=X\beta+u$ where $y$ holds $n\ge1$ responses, $u$ their model errors, $\beta$ the $k\ge1$ coefficients, and $X$ the $n\times k$ design with [[线性无关|independent columns]]. Assume $E(u\mid X)=0$ and finite conditional second moments of the errors. Treat $X$ as fixed or condition on the full design. Partition rows into $G$ nonempty clusters. Assume error vectors from different clusters are independent conditional on $X$; within a cluster the covariance pattern is unspecified. Weaker cross-cluster assumptions require a theorem that justifies them, not the word “robust”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在基础的模型式论证中，使用实数模型 $y=X\beta+u$，其中 $y$ 保存 $n\ge1$ 个响应，$u$ 是模型误差，$\beta$ 有 $k\ge1$ 个系数，$X$ 是各列[[线性无关]]的 $n\times k$ 设计矩阵；要求 $E(u\mid X)=0$，且误差有有限条件二阶矩。固定 $X$，或给定整个设计。将行分成 $G$ 个非空簇，假定给定 $X$ 后不同簇的误差向量相互独立；簇内协方差模式不指定。更弱的跨簇假设需要相应定理，不能靠“robust”一词自动获得。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X_g$ and $u_g$ be the design rows and errors in cluster $g$, and let $\Omega_g=\operatorname{Cov}(u_g\mid X)$. The [[协方差失效下的OLS|general OLS covariance]] contains a middle matrix $X^T\Omega X$. Cross-cluster covariances vanish under the stated assumptions, so this middle becomes $\sum_g X_g^T\Omega_gX_g$. It retains every within-cluster cross-product instead of keeping only individual error variances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $X_g,u_g$ 为第 $g$ 簇的设计行与误差，$\Omega_g=\operatorname{Cov}(u_g\mid X)$。[[协方差失效下的OLS|一般 OLS 协方差]]的中间矩阵是 $X^T\Omega X$；在上述假设下跨簇协方差为零，所以变成 $\sum_g X_g^T\Omega_gX_g$，保留了簇内所有交叉项，而非仅保留各个误差自己的方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After OLS, the [[回归残差|residual vector]] is $\hat u=y-X\hat\beta$. Let $\hat u_g$ collect its entries in cluster $g$, and define the $k$-component cluster contribution $s_g=X_g^T\hat u_g$. The uncorrected, one-way cluster covariance estimate (often called CR0) is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OLS 后的[[回归残差]]为 $\hat u=y-X\hat\beta$，$\hat u_g$ 收集第 $g$ 簇的残差。定义有 $k$ 个分量的簇贡献 $s_g=X_g^T\hat u_g$，则未作小样本修正的单向聚类协方差估计，常称 CR0，为

$$\widehat V_{\mathrm{CR0}}=(X^TX)^{-1}\left(\sum_{g=1}^G s_gs_g^T\right)(X^TX)^{-1}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The coefficient, fitted values and residuals are unchanged; estimated standard errors are square roots of this matrix’s diagonal entries. The within-cluster outer product is not a reliable estimate of an entire covariance matrix from just one observed cluster. Reliability comes from averaging many suitable cluster contributions. With one observation per cluster this uncorrected expression reduces to [[异方差稳健协方差|HC0]]. Different finite-sample corrections change the expression, so the precise estimator and reference distribution should be reported.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

系数、拟合值和残差都不变；估计标准误取该矩阵对角元的平方根。只看到一个簇，不能靠它的外积准确估计整个簇内协方差；可靠性来自对许多合适的簇贡献求平均。每簇只有一个观测时，这个未修正式退化为[[异方差稳健协方差|HC0]]。不同小样本修正会改变表达式，因此应说明具体估计版本与检验参考分布。

## Choosing clusters starts with the design
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先根据设计决定簇*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify the target population, how units entered the sample, and how treatment or other common exposures were assigned. The school example is a model-based dependence argument. In a finite-population or randomized-design analysis, uncertainty also depends on which clusters were sampled and which assignments could have occurred. A visible residual correlation or a changed p-value alone does not determine the correct clustering level.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先确定目标总体、样本单位如何进入数据，以及处理或共同暴露如何分配。学校例子是按误差依赖模型推理；有限总体或随机分配设计还要考虑哪些簇被抽中、哪些分配本来可能发生。仅凭看见残差相关，或者换分组后 p 值变了，不能决定正确聚类层级。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, individuals may be observed for several years while policy is assigned by state. If the inferential design treats states as independent and allows people and years within a state to share shocks, a person ID splits relevant dependence across clusters; state clustering keeps it together. Clustering by state-year would still split a state’s serial dependence. Conversely, broader grouping is not automatically safer: it changes the design claim and leaves fewer contributions to average.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如个人被多年重复观测，而政策按州分配。若推断设计把州视为独立单位，并允许州内个人和年份共享冲击，那么按个人 ID 分簇会把相关部分拆到不同簇；按州分簇才把它们放在一起。按“州×年份”分簇仍会拆开同一州的跨期依赖。反过来，更粗的分组并不自动更安全：它改变设计主张，也减少了可平均的簇贡献。

> [!warning] Across-cluster dependence is still a boundary · 跨簇依赖仍是边界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If people in different states share an unmodeled common year shock, ordinary state clustering does not allow that cross-state covariance. Removing a common mean effect can help only to the extent that it actually removes the relevant dependence. Non-nested state and year dependence may require a justified multi-way method; calling state-year cells the clusters is not the same operation. Multi-way methods also have cluster-count and dependence conditions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若不同州的人仍共享未建模的年份冲击，普通按州聚类不包含这项跨州协方差。控制共同均值效应，只在确实消除了相关依赖的范围内有帮助。州和年份这种非嵌套依赖可能需要有理论依据的多向方法；把“州×年份”单元当作簇并不是同一操作。多向方法也有簇数与依赖条件。

## Many rows do not guarantee many independent contributions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*行数多不保证独立贡献多*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The conventional approximation lets the number of clusters $G$ grow, with suitable moment and design conditions and no cluster dominating the uncertainty of the coefficient being tested. Arbitrary within-cluster covariance does not mean arbitrary dependence across the entire dataset. More pupils within twelve schools do not create more than twelve school-level replications of a common school shock. A dataset with $20{,}000$ people but only $12$ policy-assignment states can therefore still have a serious few-cluster problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常规近似让簇数 $G$ 增长，并要求合适的矩与设计条件，且不能让某个簇支配被检验系数的不确定性。允许簇内协方差模式未知，不等于允许整份数据任意依赖。在十二所学校中继续增加学生，不会增加学校共同冲击的独立重复次数。所以即使有 $20{,}000$ 人，只有 $12$ 个政策分配州时，少簇问题仍可能很严重。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is no universal safe cutoff. Unequal cluster sizes, unusually influential clusters and treatment concentrated in very few clusters can make inference poor even when the headline cluster count looks substantial. Small clusters are not themselves the same problem as few clusters: many clusters with few observations each are a standard setting, whereas a few enormous clusters do not automatically validate the conventional approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有普遍适用的安全簇数门槛。簇大小悬殊、个别簇影响极强、处理只集中在很少几个簇，都可能使看似不少的簇数仍不足以支持良好近似。每簇观测少不等于簇数少：许多小簇是常见理论情形，几个巨大簇却不能自动保证常规近似有效。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With few clusters, residual-based covariance estimates and the normal or $t$ reference approximation can both be inaccurate. A bias-reduced covariance with adjusted degrees of freedom, a wild cluster bootstrap that perturbs whole cluster residual vectors, or randomization inference under an actual assignment mechanism may help when their assumptions fit. None is a universal rescue: very few treated clusters or extreme imbalance can remain difficult, and simply using $t_{G-1}$ is not a general exact-law theorem. Choose the method from the design before selecting a favorable p-value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

簇数少时，基于残差的协方差估计与正态或 $t$ 参考近似都可能不准。可在条件适合时采用减少偏差的协方差及自由度修正、整簇扰动残差的 wild cluster bootstrap，或基于真实分配机制的随机化推断。但这些都不是万能补救：处理簇极少或极度不平衡仍可能困难，简单改用 $t_{G-1}$ 也不是一般的精确分布定理。应根据设计选方法，而不是挑有利的 p 值。

> [!note] One giant cluster cannot estimate arbitrary dependence · 一个巨大簇不能估计任意依赖
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If all observations are put in one cluster, then $s_1=X^T\hat u=0$ by the OLS normal equations, and the uncorrected CR0 matrix is zero. This is not evidence of perfect precision. It exposes the absence of independent cluster replication; the usual correction involving $G-1$ is also unavailable at $G=1$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若把所有观测放进一个簇，OLS 正规方程使 $s_1=X^T\hat u=0$，未修正 CR0 矩阵反而等于零。这不证明估计完美精确，而是暴露出没有独立簇重复；含 $G-1$ 的通常修正也无法在 $G=1$ 使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Cluster-robust standard errors can be larger or smaller than an unclustered estimate; “robust” is not an ordering by size. They neither establish [[估计量一致性|consistency]] of the coefficient nor repair confounding or a wrong conditional mean. [[相关行下的多响应GLS|GLS]] models covariance to change estimation weights; cluster-robust covariance instead targets uncertainty under a specified grouping and approximation. Their roles and assumptions should be assessed separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

聚类标准误可能大于或小于未聚类估计，“稳健”不是按数值大小排序。它不能建立系数的[[估计量一致性|一致性]]，也不能修复混杂或错误条件均值。[[相关行下的多响应GLS|GLS]] 通过协方差模型改变估计权重；聚类稳健协方差则在规定分组与近似条件下估计不确定性，两者职责与假设需要分别判断。

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- [Cameron and Miller, *A Practitioner’s Guide to Cluster-Robust Inference* (2015)](https://cameron.econ.ucdavis.edu/research/Cameron_Miller_JHR_2015_February.pdf), PDF pp.3–8,17–20,24–31: cluster sandwich, within/across-cluster distinction, multi-way setting, few-cluster limitations and correction choices. The school arithmetic and one-cluster degeneracy are directly derived here. No numeric cluster cutoff is promoted to a universal guarantee.
- [Abadie, Athey, Imbens and Wooldridge, *When Should You Adjust Standard Errors for Clustering?*, NBER Working Paper24003 (2017), PDF pp.2–4](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24003/w24003.pdf): this working-paper version supports the target-population, sampling and assignment perspective, and the warning against choosing clusters solely from residual correlations or automatic coarsening.
- [[02_Economy/01_Econometrics/13_面板数据模型.md#1.2. 面板数据的混合估计|The permanent panel-data course section]] supplies the pooled-model and repeated-unit context; the present card separates covariance inference from coefficient identification.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Cameron–Miller 指定页核对聚类三明治、簇内与跨簇限制、多向与少簇问题及修正方法；学校算例与单簇退化直接推导。Abadie 等 2017 年工作论文用于目标总体、抽样和分配机制的视角。永久面板课程用于本地范围；本卡单独区分推断与系数识别。
