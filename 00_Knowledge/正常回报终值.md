# 在稳定留存且新增投资仅赚股权成本的模型中每股终值等于下一期每股盈利除以股权成本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With stable retention and new investments earning only the cost of equity, terminal share value equals next-period earnings per share divided by that cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设第 $n$ 年末开始按稳定期估值：第一笔未来每股盈利为 $E_{n+1}$，每年留存比例固定为 $0\le b<1$，余下全部分配，股数不变。既有盈利持续，新留存资金从下一年起仅赚恒定股权成本 $k_e>0$；没有其他融资、税或经营变化。于是稳定期盈利和股利增长率为 $g=bk_e$，第一笔股利为 $(1-b)E_{n+1}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At year-end $n$, let next-period EPS be $E_{n+1}$, with constant retention $0\le b<1$, full distribution of the rest, and unchanged shares. Existing earnings persist; new retention earns only constant equity cost $k_e>0$ from the following year. With no other changes, stable earnings and dividend growth is $g=bk_e$, and the first dividend is $(1-b)E_{n+1}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

代入[[戈登增长模型]]，得到：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substitution into the [[戈登增长模型|Gordon model]] gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P_n=\frac{(1-b)E_{n+1}}{k_e-bk_e}=\frac{E_{n+1}}{k_e}.$$

例如 $E_{n+1}=120$、$k_e=10\%$，若留存 40%，第一笔股利为 72，增长 4%，则 $72/(0.10-0.04)=1200$；若全部分配，$120/0.10$ 也为 1200。在这个特例中，留存会带来增长，但新增投资净现值为零。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With next-period EPS of 120 and an equity cost of 10%, retaining 40% gives a first dividend of 72 and growth of 4%, hence value 1200. Full payout also gives 1200. Retention generates growth but zero incremental NPV in this special case.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分子必须是 $E_{n+1}$，因为第 $n$ 年股利已经在显性期计算。公式也不意味着稳定期盈利不再增长，或原来高回报的投资立即丧失全部盈利；限制的是未来新增投资回报。$b=1$ 时股利恒为零，上述约分前是未满足 $k_e>g$ 的边界，不能靠约分直接认证同一股利模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The numerator is $E_{n+1}$ because year-$n$ dividends are already explicit. Earnings may still grow; only returns on new investment are restricted. At $b=1$, dividends are zero and $k_e>g$ fails, so cancellation does not validate the same dividend model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
