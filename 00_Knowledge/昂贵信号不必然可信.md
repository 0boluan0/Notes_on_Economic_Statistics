# 信号的绝对成本不能保证可信性；可信分离必须由不同类型的激励约束共同支撑
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The absolute cost of a signal does not guarantee credibility; credible separation must be supported by incentive constraints across types.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“这个信号很贵”本身既不能证明高类型愿意发送它，也不能证明低类型不愿模仿。可信性来自信号引致的后续行动与类型相关成本共同形成的相对收益，使计划中的类型选择对该类型而言优于所有可行偏离。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“This signal is expensive” by itself proves neither that the high type wants to send it nor that the low type will refrain from imitating it. Credibility comes from relative payoffs: the induced continuation action and type-dependent signaling cost must jointly make the prescribed choice better than every feasible deviation for each type.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 分离需要双向激励相容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Separation requires incentive compatibility in both directions.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若候选分离策略把类型 $\theta$ 配给信号 $s(\theta)$，接收者在信号 $s$ 后采取 $a(s)$，则必须对每个可行信号 $s'\in S$ 满足：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a candidate separating strategy assigns signal $s(\theta)$ to type $\theta$ and the receiver takes action $a(s)$ after signal $s$, it must satisfy the following condition for every feasible signal $s'\in S$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
U_1\!\left(\theta,s(\theta);a(s(\theta))\right)
\ge
U_1\!\left(\theta,s';a(s')\right)
\quad\text{for every }s'\in S.
$$

在两类型模型中，这既包括低类型不模仿高类型，也包括高类型不改用低类型的信号。只检查其中一个方向，不能建立分离均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a two-type model, this includes both the low type not imitating the high type and the high type not switching to the low type's signal. Checking only one direction is insufficient to establish a separating equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 成本必须放进净收益比较
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cost must enter a comparison of net payoffs.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

绝对成本很高时，信号可能把所有类型都挡在外面；绝对成本不高时，只要它对低类型相对更昂贵，也可能支撑分离。就业市场信号中的教育之所以可能可信，不是因为教育“昂贵”这一事实，而是因为教育成本随生产率类型变化，且工资反应能同时满足高类型发送、低类型不模仿的约束。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An absolutely high cost may deter every type. Conversely, a modest cost may support separation if it is relatively more burdensome for the low type. In job-market signaling, education can be credible not merely because it is “costly,” but because its cost varies with productivity type and the wage response can make education attractive to the high type while deterring imitation by the low type.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
