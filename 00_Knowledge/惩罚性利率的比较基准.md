# 惩罚性利率必须指定比较基准便利类型和操作框架
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A penalty rate must identify its benchmark, facility, and operating framework*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子失败模式
> “惩罚性”是相对于某个正常融资基准和政策目的的定价判断，不是“央行贷款利率永远高于任意同业利率”的跨制度恒等式。比较时必须锁定 facility、期限、抵押、借款人信用质量、市场基准和当时 operating framework。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Penalty” is a pricing judgment relative to a normal funding benchmark and policy purpose, not a cross-regime identity that central-bank credit always costs more than every interbank loan. Fix the facility, maturity, collateral, borrower quality, market benchmark, and operating framework before comparing rates.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

美国 primary credit 提供了具体反例：截至 2026-08-29，primary credit rate 为 3.75%，FOMC 自 2026-07-30 起维持的 federal funds target range 为 3.50%–3.75%。Fed 说明该便利自 2020 年 3 月以来定在目标区间上沿，并在市场利率高于 primary credit rate 时鼓励合格银行借入并按需使用。Secondary credit 同期为 4.25%，又按另一套资格与定价运行。因此一句笼统的“贴现率高于同业拆借率”会混淆产品和基准。

## 易错边界

- target range 上沿不等于每笔实际 federal funds transaction 或其他 secured/unsecured interbank loan 的固定上界。
- secondary、seasonal、primary credit 的定价逻辑不同。
- Bagehot 的保护性定价思想不能代替读取当前规则。

> [!question]- 自检
> 要检验“贴现率更贵”，至少要补哪三类信息？
>
> **答案：** 具体便利与期限、拿来比较的市场利率/交易、以及当时的操作框架和日期；抵押与借款人条件也要匹配。

## 来源与核验

- [Federal Reserve Board, Discount Window](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/discountrate.htm)：核对 primary credit 自 2020 年 3 月以来与目标区间上沿的关系及市场利率可高于它的边界。
- [Federal Reserve Board, Regulation A §201.51](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/regulations/section-20151-interest-rates-applicable-to-credit-extended-by-a-federal-reserve-bank.htm)：核对 primary、secondary 与 seasonal credit 的不同定价；制度信息核验于 2026-08-29。
- [Federal Reserve, Decisions Regarding Monetary Policy Implementation, 29 July 2026](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/files/monetary20260729a1.pdf)：核对自 2026-07-30 起 3.50%–3.75% 的目标区间与 3.75% primary credit rate。
