# 支出规则是对指定覆盖范围的政府支出水平或增长率施加持续性数值约束的财政规则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An expenditure rule imposes a lasting numerical constraint on the level or growth of government expenditure within a specified scope.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

支出规则直接约束支出总量，而不是同时约束收入和支出的差额。规则可以采用名义水平、实际增速或 GDP 占比，覆盖总支出或排除某些项目；这些选择会改变政府能控制什么、规则如何应对经济波动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An expenditure rule constrains spending rather than the difference between revenue and expenditure. It may use nominal levels, real growth or a GDP share, with specified exclusions. These choices affect controllability and cyclical behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若支出上限在衰退中不随当期收入下降而下调，税收端的[[财政自动稳定器]]可以在上限内运行。若还希望失业给付自动增加，就必须检查其是否被计入一个已经用满的上限。以当期 GDP 占比设上限时，GDP 下跌本身可能压低允许的支出金额，因此不能把所有支出规则都称为逆周期规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a ceiling does not fall with current revenue in a downturn, revenue-side [[财政自动稳定器|automatic stabilisers]] can operate. Automatic benefit increases additionally depend on whether they count against an already binding ceiling. A current-GDP-ratio ceiling can reduce permitted nominal spending when GDP falls; expenditure rules are not uniformly countercyclical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收入端不受这条规则直接约束：即使支出守住上限，减税仍可能扩大赤字。因此它通常需要与[[债务锚与操作规则]]中的长期参照相协调，并明确税收优惠等可能改变约束效果的项目。具体年度上限如何与预测比较，见[[支出基线与支出上限]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revenue is not directly constrained: tax cuts can widen the deficit despite spending compliance. Expenditure rules therefore need consistency with [[债务锚与操作规则|long-term fiscal anchors]] and explicit treatment of items such as tax expenditures. See [[支出基线与支出上限|baselines and ceilings]] for annual planning.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
