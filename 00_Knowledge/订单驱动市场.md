# 订单驱动市场按参与者提交的订单和撮合规则组织成交

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An order-driven market organises trades through submitted orders and matching rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

订单驱动市场是把参与者提交的买卖订单，按价格、数量及其他适用条件集中或系统化撮合的市场机制。典型实现是[[限价订单簿]]；即时成交所需的对手数量来自可匹配的订单，而非必须先找到一个承担全部交易的指定交易商。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An order-driven market matches submitted buy and sell orders under applicable price, quantity and other conditions. A [[限价订单簿|limit order book]] is a typical implementation. Immediate execution draws on compatible opposing orders, rather than necessarily requiring a designated dealer to take the entire trade.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，甲的卖单是 100.00 卖 50 单位，乙愿意以不高于 100.00 买 30 单位。若适用规则允许撮合，这两笔订单可形成 30 单位交易；余下 20 单位仍可能等待其他买家。提交了订单不表示订单必定成交。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose one order offers 50 units at 100.00 and another seeks 30 units at no more than 100.00. If the applicable rules permit matching, 30 units can trade, leaving 20 offered units potentially waiting for other buyers. Submission is not a guarantee of execution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

订单驱动可以采用[[集合竞价]]或[[连续竞价]]。订单由普通投资者、机构或[[做市商]]提交，不改变撮合机制的身份；公开显示的订单也未必覆盖全部可交易意愿。它与[[报价驱动市场]]的区别不等于“有报价／无报价”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Order-driven trading can use a [[集合竞价|call auction]] or [[连续竞价|continuous auction]]. Orders may come from individual investors, institutions or [[做市商|market makers]] without changing the matching mechanism. Displayed orders need not reveal all trading interest. The contrast with a [[报价驱动市场|quote-driven market]] is not the presence versus absence of quoted prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
