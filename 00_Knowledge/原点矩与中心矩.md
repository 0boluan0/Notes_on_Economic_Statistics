# Raw and central moments
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*原点矩与中心矩*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **moment** summarises a distribution by averaging a power of a quantity. The power might be one, two, three, and so on. **Raw moments** use the values of the variable as measured from zero. **Central moments** first subtract the mean, so they use deviations from the distribution's own centre. The distinction is where we put the reference point before raising values to a power.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**矩**通过“对某个量取幂，再平均”来概括一个分布；幂可以是一次、二次、三次等。**原点矩**直接使用变量相对于零的取值；**中心矩**先减去均值，使用相对于分布自身中心的偏离。区别在于：取幂之前，把哪个位置当作参照点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here “average” means [[期望|expectation]], so each contribution receives its probability weight. A moment does not generally give the whole distribution; it retains one particular numerical summary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的“平均”指[[期望]]，每项贡献都要按概率加权。一个矩通常不能给出整个分布，它保留的是某一种数值概括。

> [!example] Squaring values and squaring deviations answer different questions · 平方取值与平方偏离不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose $X$ takes $2$ and $6$, each with probability $1/2$, so its mean is $4$. The second raw moment squares $2$ and $6$ before averaging. The second central moment instead squares the deviations $2-4=-2$ and $6-4=2$:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设 $X$ 以各 $1/2$ 的概率取 $2$ 和 $6$，均值为 $4$。二阶原点矩把 $2$ 和 $6$ 平方后平均；二阶中心矩则先计算偏离 $2-4=-2$、$6-4=2$，再平方后平均：
>
> $$E[X^2]=\tfrac12(2^2)+\tfrac12(6^2)=20,$$
> $$E[(X-4)^2]=\tfrac12(-2)^2+\tfrac12(2)^2=4.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Add ten to every value: the outcomes become $12$ and $16$, with mean $14$. The second raw moment becomes $200$, but the second central moment remains $4$, because the deviations are still $-2$ and $2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 如果每个取值都加十，结果变成 $12$ 和 $16$，均值为 $14$。二阶原点矩变成 $200$，二阶中心矩却仍为 $4$，因为偏离依旧是 $-2$ 和 $2$。

## The order tells us which power to average
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*阶数决定平均的是几次幂*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $k$ be a positive integer called the **order**, and let $\mu=E[X]$ be the mean. For finite moments of order $k$, assume $E[|X|^k]<\infty$: the $k$th power of the variable's magnitude must have a finite expectation. This also guarantees a finite mean and finite central moment of that order. The [[矩存在性的使用边界|moment existence conditions]] explain why powers require their own integrability checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $k$ 是一个正整数，称为**阶数**，并用 $\mu=E[X]$ 表示均值。讨论有限的 $k$ 阶矩时，假设 $E[|X|^k]<\infty$：变量大小的 $k$ 次幂需要具有有限期望。这个条件也保证均值和同阶中心矩有限。[[矩存在性的使用边界]]解释了为什么不同幂次需要分别检查可积性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definitions are then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

于是，定义分别为

$$\text{Raw moment of order }k:\quad E[X^k],\qquad
\text{Central moment of order }k:\quad E[(X-\mu)^k].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $k=1$, raising to the first power changes nothing. For $k=2$, squaring removes the sign. Odd powers retain the sign, so positive and negative contributions can offset each other. As the order increases, the weighting of larger magnitudes relative to smaller ones becomes more pronounced; rare extreme values can therefore matter greatly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $k=1$ 时，一次幂不会改变原数；$k=2$ 时，平方去掉正负号。奇数次幂则保留符号，因此正负贡献可能抵消。阶数越高，较大数值相对于较小数值的权重差距越明显，罕见的极端值也就可能产生很大影响。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The first two orders connect familiar quantities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*前两阶怎样连接均值与方差*

| Order · 阶数 | Raw moment · 原点矩 | Central moment · 中心矩 |
|---|---|---|
| First · 一阶 | $E[X]=\mu$ | $E[X-\mu]=0$ |
| Second · 二阶 | $E[X^2]$ | $E[(X-\mu)^2]=\operatorname{Var}(X)$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first central moment is zero because [[期望线性性|linearity of expectation]] gives $E[X-\mu]=E[X]-\mu=0$. It therefore tells us nothing about spread. The second central moment is [[方差|variance]], which averages squared deviations instead.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一阶中心矩为零，是因为[[期望线性性]]给出 $E[X-\mu]=E[X]-\mu=0$，因此它不能反映散布程度。二阶中心矩就是[[方差]]，平均的是平方偏离。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the second moment is finite, expanding the square gives the relation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当二阶矩有限时，展开平方可以得到

$$E[X^2]=\operatorname{Var}(X)+\mu^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second raw moment contains both spread about the mean and the squared location of the mean relative to zero. In the example, $20=4+4^2$. It equals variance only when the mean is zero. It also differs from $(E[X])^2$, which squares the mean after averaging.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二阶原点矩同时包含均值周围的散布，以及均值相对于零的位置的平方。上例中，$20=4+4^2$。只有均值为零时，它才等于方差。它也不同于 $(E[X])^2$；后者是先平均，再把均值平方。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Higher orders and other function expectations
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*更高阶矩与一般函数期望*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[偏度|Skewness]] uses the third central moment, and [[峰度|kurtosis]] uses the fourth, each divided by an appropriate power of the standard deviation so that the result has no measurement units. Their definitions require the relevant finite absolute moments and a positive standard deviation. The linked cards explain those summaries; the moment formulas alone do not establish that their values exist.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[偏度]]使用三阶中心矩，[[峰度]]使用四阶中心矩，并分别除以标准差的适当次幂，使结果不带计量单位。它们的定义需要相应绝对矩有限，而且标准差为正。具体解释在对应卡片中；仅写出矩的公式，不代表数值一定存在。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A function $g$ can also transform a variable in ways other than a power. The [[LOTUS|expectation-of-a-function rule]] computes $E[g(X)]$ using the distribution of $X$. For example, $g(x)=|x|$ gives an average magnitude. Such expressions are function expectations in general; they are raw or central power moments when the function has the corresponding power form. Some course material uses “moment” more broadly, so check the function being averaged.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

函数 $g$ 也可以对变量做幂函数以外的变换。[[LOTUS|随机变量函数的期望规则]]用 $X$ 的分布计算 $E[g(X)]$；例如，$g(x)=|x|$ 给出平均大小。一般来说，这些式子是函数的期望；当函数具有相应的幂形式时，才是这里讨论的原点幂矩或中心幂矩。有些课程材料对“矩”采用更宽的称呼，所以要看清究竟在平均哪个函数。
