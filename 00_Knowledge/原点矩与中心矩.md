# 原点矩平均取值的幂，中心矩平均偏离均值的幂

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Raw moments average powers of values; central moments average powers of centred values*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正整数 $k$，有限 $k$ 阶原点矩为 $E[X^k]$，要求 $E|X|^k<\infty$；若 $\mu=E[X]$，$k$ 阶中心矩为 $E[(X-\mu)^k]$。一阶原点矩是均值，一阶中心矩为 0，二阶中心矩是方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a positive integer order with finite absolute moment, a raw moment averages powers of X and a central moment averages powers of X minus its mean. The first raw moment is the mean, the first central moment is zero, and the second central moment is variance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
两者不能混用：$E[X^2]=\operatorname{Var}(X)+\mu^2$。偏度与峰度分别标准化三阶、四阶中心矩。一般 $E[g(X)]$ 是函数的期望；当 $g$ 是相应幂函数时，才是这里的幂矩。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The second raw moment contains both variance and squared mean. Skewness and kurtosis standardise central moments of orders three and four. A general function expectation need not be a power moment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
