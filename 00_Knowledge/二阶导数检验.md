# Second derivative test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*二阶导数检验*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A horizontal tangent can sit at the bottom of a valley, the top of a hill, or a flat point through which the function keeps rising. The **second derivative test** distinguishes the first two possibilities when the slope changes at a nonzero rate. It classifies a nearby shape; it does not compare the point with the entire domain.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

水平切线可能位于谷底、峰顶，也可能只是函数继续上升时经过的平坦点。**二阶导数检验**在斜率以非零速率变化时，区分前两种情况。它判断附近的形状，不把该点与整个定义域比较。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[导数|first derivative]] $f'(x)$ is the slope. Its derivative, the [[高阶导数|second derivative]] $f''(x)$, measures how the slope changes. A [[驻点|stationary point]] $c$ satisfies $f'(c)=0$. A strict [[局部极值与绝对极值|local minimum]] means that every sufficiently nearby allowed input different from $c$ gives a value strictly larger than $f(c)$; a strict local maximum reverses that comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[导数|一阶导数]] $f'(x)$ 是斜率；对它求导得到的[[高阶导数|二阶导数]] $f''(x)$ 衡量斜率怎样变化。[[驻点]] $c$ 满足 $f'(c)=0$。严格[[局部极值与绝对极值|局部最小点]]表示：每个足够近且不同于 $c$ 的合法输入，其函数值都严格大于 $f(c)$；严格局部最大把比较反向。

> [!example] Two stationary points of a cubic · 三次函数的两个驻点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=3x-x^3$, the [[整数幂求导|power rule]] gives $f'(x)=3-3x^2$ and $f''(x)=-6x$. The stationary inputs are $-1$ and $1$. At $-1$, the second derivative is $6>0$, giving a strict local minimum with value $-2$. At $1$, it is $-6<0$, giving a strict local maximum with value $2$. These are local extrema: this cubic has neither an absolute maximum nor an absolute minimum on the whole real line.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $f(x)=3x-x^3$，[[整数幂求导]]给出 $f'(x)=3-3x^2$、$f''(x)=-6x$。驻点输入是 $-1$ 与 $1$。在 $-1$，二阶导数为 $6>0$，得到严格局部最小值 $-2$；在 $1$，二阶导数为 $-6<0$，得到严格局部最大值 $2$。这只是局部极值：该三次函数在整个实数线上既没有绝对最大值，也没有绝对最小值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the test assumes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*检验究竟需要什么条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $c$ be an [[集合内部|interior point]] of the real domain: an open interval $(c-r,c+r)$ lies in the domain for some radius $r>0$. Assume $f$ is differentiable throughout that interval and the function $f'$ is differentiable at $c$. Thus the finite number $f''(c)$ exists, even though $f''$ need not be continuous or exist at every other nearby point. Also require $f'(c)=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $c$ 是实数定义域的[[集合内部|内点]]，即存在半径 $r>0$，使开区间 $(c-r,c+r)$ 全部包含在定义域内。假设 $f$ 在该区间处处可导，并且函数 $f'$ 在 $c$ 可导，于是有限数 $f''(c)$ 存在；不要求 $f''$ 连续，也不要求它在其他每个附近点都存在。此外，还要求 $f'(c)=0$。

> [!important] A nonzero second derivative decides the local type · 非零二阶导数决定局部类型
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under these assumptions, $f''(c)>0$ gives a **strict local minimum**, and $f''(c)<0$ gives a **strict local maximum**. If $f''(c)=0$, this test gives no conclusion. If the required derivatives do not exist, its hypotheses are not met.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在这些假设下，$f''(c)>0$ 给出**严格局部最小**，$f''(c)<0$ 给出**严格局部最大**。若 $f''(c)=0$，本检验没有结论；若所需导数不存在，则不满足检验假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why a quadratic term determines the sign
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么二次项能控制函数值的差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $h=x-c$ for the signed displacement and $d=f''(c)$. The [[二次近似|quadratic approximation]] under exactly the assumptions above says
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把带符号的位移记为 $h=x-c$，并记 $d=f''(c)$。在恰好上述假设下，[[二次近似]]给出

$$f(c+h)-f(c)=f'(c)h+\frac d2h^2+R(h),\qquad \frac{R(h)}{h^2}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $R(h)$ is the remaining error; the limit is over nonzero $h\to0$. At a stationary point the linear term vanishes. Dividing by the positive number $h^2$ leaves $d/2+R(h)/h^2$. When $d\ne0$, choose a small enough neighbourhood that $|R(h)/h^2|<|d|/4$. The error then cannot reverse the sign of $d/2$. Consequently $f(c+h)-f(c)$ is positive on both sides if $d>0$, and negative on both sides if $d<0$. This proves strictness, rather than merely suggesting a parabola-shaped sketch.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $R(h)$ 是剩余误差，极限取非零的 $h\to0$。在驻点，一次项消失。除以正数 $h^2$ 后，剩下 $d/2+R(h)/h^2$。当 $d\ne0$，取足够小的邻域，使 $|R(h)/h^2|<|d|/4$，误差就无法改变 $d/2$ 的符号。因此，$d>0$ 时两侧都有 $f(c+h)-f(c)>0$；$d<0$ 时两侧都小于零。这证明了严格极值，而不只是凭抛物线图形作猜测。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is also a direct connection to the [[一阶导数检验|first derivative test]]. The definition of $f''(c)$ gives $f'(c+h)/h\to d$ because $f'(c)=0$. If $d>0$, this quotient is positive for all sufficiently small nonzero $h$, so $f'$ is negative just to the left and positive just to the right. For $d<0$, the signs reverse. A commonly taught stronger assumption is that $f''$ exists nearby and is continuous at $c$; then its sign persists nearby and [[导数判凹凸|the interval derivative criteria]] give the same conclusion. Continuity of $f''$ is a sufficient route, not an additional requirement of the version stated here.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也直接连接[[一阶导数检验]]。由 $f'(c)=0$，二阶导数的定义给出 $f'(c+h)/h\to d$。若 $d>0$，对所有足够小的非零 $h$，这个商为正，于是左侧的 $f'$ 为负、右侧为正；$d<0$ 时反向。教材常采用更强的假设：$f''$ 在附近存在且在 $c$ 连续。此时二阶导数在附近保持同号，[[导数判凹凸|区间上的导数判据]]也能得到相同结论。二阶导数连续是一条充分路线，不是本卡版本额外必需的条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What zero curvature and nonzero slope do not tell you
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*二阶为零与一阶非零要分别处理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At zero, $x^4$, $-x^4$ and $x^3$ all have first and second derivatives equal to zero. The first has a strict minimum, the second a strict maximum, and the third no local extremum because its nearby values have both signs. Thus $f''(c)=0$ neither rules out extrema nor establishes an inflection point. If differentiability fails, another argument may still work: $|x|$ has a strict minimum at zero directly from $|x|>0$ for $x\ne0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在零点，$x^4$、$-x^4$、$x^3$ 的一阶与二阶导数都等于零，但第一种有严格最小，第二种有严格最大，第三种附近既有正值又有负值，没有局部极值。因此，$f''(c)=0$ 既不排除极值，也不证明存在拐点。若不可导，其他论证仍可能有效：$|x|$ 在零点有严格最小，直接由 $x\ne0$ 时 $|x|>0$ 得到。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The stationary-point requirement cannot be replaced by curvature. For $f(x)=x+x^2$, $f''(0)=2>0$ but $f'(0)=1$. For small positive and negative $h$, $f(h)-f(0)=h(1+h)$ has the sign of $h$, so zero is not an extremum. More generally, the [[无约束一阶条件|interior first-order necessary condition]] explains why a nonzero finite derivative excludes a local extremum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

曲率不能代替驻点要求。对 $f(x)=x+x^2$，虽然 $f''(0)=2>0$，但 $f'(0)=1$。对足够小的正、负 $h$，$f(h)-f(0)=h(1+h)$ 与 $h$ 同号，所以零点不是极值。一般情形由[[无约束一阶条件|内部一阶必要条件]]说明：非零有限导数排除内部局部极值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finally, the test is local. For $f(x)=x^2+x^3$, it gives a strict minimum at zero, but $f(-2)=-4<f(0)$. Absolute extrema require comparison over the stated domain, for example through the [[闭区间极值法|closed-interval method]] when applicable. In several variables, the corresponding [[Hessian 局部极小判据|minimum]] and [[Hessian 局部极大判据|maximum]] criteria inspect a quadratic form in every direction; one second partial derivative alone is not enough.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后，本检验只作局部判断。对 $f(x)=x^2+x^3$，它给出零点的严格最小，但 $f(-2)=-4<f(0)$。绝对极值必须与指定定义域上的值比较，例如在满足条件时用[[闭区间极值法]]。多元情形的 [[Hessian 局部极小判据]]与[[Hessian 局部极大判据]]检查所有方向上的二次型，不能只看一个二阶偏导。
