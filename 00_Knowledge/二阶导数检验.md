# 驻点处非零二阶导数的符号可充分判定严格局部极值而二阶导数为零时无结论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*At a stationary point a nonzero second derivative is sufficient to classify a strict local extremum while a zero second derivative is inconclusive*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 若 $f'(c)=0$ 且 $f''$ 在 $c$ 附近存在并在 $c$ 连续，则 $f''(c)>0$ 使 $c$ 成为严格局部最小点，$f''(c)<0$ 使其成为严格局部最大点。若 $f''(c)=0$ 或不存在，本检验不作结论。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $f'(c)=0$ and the second derivative exists near $c$ and is continuous at $c$, then $f''(c)>0$ gives a strict local minimum and $f''(c)<0$ a strict local maximum. If $f''(c)=0$ or is unavailable, this test is inconclusive.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续性使 $f''$ 在 $c$ 附近保持同号，于是 $f'$ 在该邻域严格递增或递减。结合 $f'(c)=0$，正二阶导数令 $f'$ 从负跨到正，负二阶导数令它从正跨到负，本质上把 [[一阶导数检验]] 压缩成一个点上的曲率条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity keeps the second derivative's sign stable near $c$, making $f'$ locally increasing or decreasing. Together with $f'(c)=0$, this forces the first-derivative sign change needed for the first derivative test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$x^4$ 与 $-x^4$ 在零点都有 $f''(0)=0$，却分别是最小与最大；$x^3$ 同样有零二阶导数却不是极值。因此“$f''=0$ 就是拐点”或“就没有极值”都错。多元版本需要 Hessian 在所有方向上的定号，不能只看某个二阶偏导。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At zero, $x^4$, $-x^4$, and $x^3$ all have zero second derivative, yet give a minimum, a maximum, and no extremum. Thus a zero second derivative neither classifies an extremum nor establishes an inflection point. In several variables, the analogous test requires the Hessian's sign over every direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么检验先要求 $f'(c)=0$？
>
> **答案：** 非零斜率意味着函数在足够小的一侧继续上升、另一侧继续下降，不能在内部形成局部极值；曲率不能替代一阶必要条件。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why does the test first require $f'(c)=0$?
>
> **Answer:** A nonzero derivative gives values above $f(c)$ in one sufficiently small direction and below it in the other, excluding an interior local extremum. Curvature cannot replace the first-order necessary condition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
