# Characterizing Brownian motion by its covariance kernel
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用协方差核识别布朗运动*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sometimes a random process is given by a formula, and it is unclear whether it is [[标准布朗运动|standard Brownian motion]]. Checking every joint increment distribution separately would be cumbersome. For a **Gaussian process**, there is a shorter route: calculate its mean and covariance, then check the path condition.

“Gaussian process” means that every finite list of its values forms a [[联合Gaussian|jointly Gaussian vector]], allowing degenerate coordinates. Being normal at each time separately is weaker. The [[协方差|covariance]] kernel $K(s,t)=\operatorname{Cov}(X_s,X_t)$ records dependence between times; “centered” means $E[X_t]=0$ at every time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有时随机过程由一个公式给出，我们想判断它是不是[[标准布朗运动]]。逐一检查所有增量的联合分布会很繁琐。对 **Gaussian 过程**，可以走较短的路线：计算均值和协方差，再检查路径条件。

“Gaussian 过程”要求任意有限组过程值都组成[[联合Gaussian|联合 Gaussian 向量]]，允许退化坐标；每个时刻各自正态是更弱的条件。[[协方差]]核 $K(s,t)=\operatorname{Cov}(X_s,X_t)$ 描述时刻之间的依赖；“中心化”表示每个时刻都满足 $E[X_t]=0$。

> [!important] The characterization · 识别条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real process $X=(X_t)_{t\ge0}$ is standard Brownian motion if and only if it is a centered Gaussian process with
>
> $$K(s,t)=\min(s,t)=s\wedge t\qquad(s,t\ge0)$$
>
> and, with probability one, its entire path on $[0,\infty)$ is continuous. The minimum is the earlier of the two times. If only the mean, Gaussianity and kernel have been established, every almost-surely continuous modification is Brownian; the current version must still satisfy the path requirement.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 实值过程 $X=(X_t)_{t\ge0}$ 是标准布朗运动，当且仅当它是中心 Gaussian 过程，协方差核满足上式，并且以概率一，其整条路径在 $[0,\infty)$ 上连续。$\min(s,t)$ 或 $s\wedge t$ 表示两个时刻中较早的一个。如果目前只核对了均值、Gaussian 性和协方差核，则任何几乎必然连续的修正版本都是布朗运动；当前版本仍须满足路径条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The forward direction is the [[布朗有限维分布|finite-dimensional law of Brownian motion]]. For the reverse direction, the starting point is already forced: $\operatorname{Var}(X_0)=K(0,0)=0$, and $E[X_0]=0$, so $X_0=0$ almost surely. We next recover the whole increment structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正向结论就是[[布朗有限维分布]]。反向识别时，起点已经被条件确定：$\operatorname{Var}(X_0)=K(0,0)=0$，再结合 $E[X_0]=0$，得到 $X_0=0$ 几乎必然。接下来重建完整的增量结构。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## How the kernel gives stationary independent increments
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 协方差核怎样推出平稳独立增量

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $0\le s<t$, the difference $X_t-X_s$ is a linear combination of jointly Gaussian variables. Its mean is zero, and its variance is

$$\operatorname{Var}(X_t-X_s)=K(t,t)+K(s,s)-2K(s,t)=t+s-2s=t-s.$$

Therefore $X_t-X_s\sim N(0,t-s)$; the distribution depends only on interval length, which is stationary increments.

For independence, take any finite collection $0\le s_1<t_1\le s_2<t_2\le\cdots\le s_m<t_m$. The increment vector is jointly Gaussian, since it is a fixed linear transformation of finitely many process values. For $i<j$,

$$\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Cov}(X_{t_i}-X_{s_i},X_{t_j}-X_{s_j})
&=K(t_i,t_j)-K(t_i,s_j)-K(s_i,t_j)+K(s_i,s_j)\\
&=t_i-t_i-s_i+s_i=0.
\end{aligned}$$

The covariance matrix is diagonal. The [[联合高斯独立判据|joint-Gaussian independence criterion]] then gives **mutual independence** of all increments. Zero pairwise covariances would not suffice without the joint Gaussian assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $0\le s<t$，差 $X_t-X_s$ 是联合 Gaussian 变量的线性组合，均值为零，方差由上式得到 $t-s$。所以 $X_t-X_s\sim N(0,t-s)$，分布只依赖区间长度，即增量平稳。

再取任意有限组 $0\le s_1<t_1\le s_2<t_2\le\cdots\le s_m<t_m$。增量向量是有限个过程值的固定线性变换，因此联合 Gaussian。对 $i<j$，按上式展开协方差，四项正好抵消，得到零。于是整个协方差矩阵为对角阵，[[联合高斯独立判据]]给出所有增量**相互独立**；没有联合 Gaussian 假设时，两两协方差为零并不足够。

> [!example] Recognizing a rescaled process · 识别缩放后的过程
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $W$ be standard Brownian motion and set $X_t=W_{4t}/2$. Its finite vectors are fixed linear transformations of Gaussian vectors, its mean is zero, and
>
> $$\operatorname{Cov}(X_s,X_t)=\frac14\min(4s,4t)=\min(s,t).$$
>
> Rescaling time and height preserves continuity of each continuous path, so this criterion identifies $X$ as standard Brownian motion. Dividing by $4$ instead would give covariance $\min(s,t)/4$, so that process would have the wrong standard scale.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $W$ 是标准布朗运动，设 $X_t=W_{4t}/2$。它的有限向量是 Gaussian 向量的固定线性变换，均值为零，协方差如上式等于 $\min(s,t)$。对连续路径缩放时间和高度仍保持连续，因此判据说明 $X$ 仍是标准布朗运动。如果误除以 $4$，协方差会变成 $\min(s,t)/4$，就不是标准尺度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the continuous version is stated separately
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么还要单列连续版本

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A modification $\widetilde X$ agrees with $X$ almost surely at **each fixed time**: $P(\widetilde X_t=X_t)=1$ for every $t$. This allows the exceptional event to depend on $t$. Almost-sure path continuity instead concerns one event on which **all** times fit together continuously.

For this particular Gaussian kernel a continuous modification does exist. One route is the Kolmogorov continuity criterion: Gaussian increments give the [[原点矩与中心矩|fourth-moment]] identity (the expected fourth power)

$$E\bigl[|X_t-X_s|^4\bigr]=3|t-s|^2,$$

which supplies its required moment bound on each bounded time interval. This is an additional path-regularity theorem, not an assertion that the current version is automatically continuous. The distinction and the role of a continuous modification are explained in [[有限维分布不定路径正则性|finite-dimensional laws versus path regularity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

修正版本 $\widetilde X$ 与 $X$ 在**每个固定时刻**几乎必然相等：对每个 $t$，都有 $P(\widetilde X_t=X_t)=1$，但例外事件可以随 $t$ 改变。路径几乎必然连续则要求存在同一个事件，使其中**所有**时刻拼成连续函数。

对于这里的 Gaussian 核，连续修正版本确实存在。一种证明路线是 Kolmogorov 连续性判据：Gaussian 增量给出上面的[[原点矩与中心矩|四阶矩]]公式，即四次幂的期望，满足判据在每个有界时间区间要求的矩上界。这是额外的路径正则性定理，并不是说当前版本自动连续。修正版本和量词的区别见[[有限维分布不定路径正则性]]。

> [!warning] Two assumptions that the kernel cannot replace · 协方差核不能代替的两项假设
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the **version** issue, start with continuous Brownian $W$ and an independent time $U$ with the [[连续均匀分布|uniform distribution]] on $(1,2)$. Set $Y_t=W_t+\mathbf1_{\{t=U\}}$, where the [[事件指示变量|indicator]] is one exactly at $t=U$. Any fixed finite list of times misses $U$ with probability one, so $Y$ has exactly the Brownian finite-dimensional laws. Nevertheless each continuous path of $W$ gains a spike at $U$, making that path of $Y$ discontinuous.
>
> For the **Gaussian** issue, take an independent random multiplier $A$, equal to $0$ or $\sqrt2$ with probabilities $1/2$ each, and put $Z_t=AW_t$. Then $E[Z_t]=0$ and $\operatorname{Cov}(Z_s,Z_t)=E[A^2]\min(s,t)=\min(s,t)$, and paths remain continuous. But for $t>0$, $Z_t$ has probability $1/2$ at zero and positive variance $t$. It is neither a nondegenerate normal nor a constant, so this process is not Gaussian and not Brownian.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对于**版本**问题，从连续布朗运动 $W$ 出发，再取独立且服从[[连续均匀分布]]的 $U\sim\mathrm{Unif}(1,2)$，设 $Y_t=W_t+\mathbf1_{\{t=U\}}$；这里的[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]仅在 $t=U$ 时取一。任意固定的有限时刻列表都以概率一避开 $U$，所以 $Y$ 与布朗运动具有完全相同的有限维分布。但 $W$ 的每条连续路径都在 $U$ 处多出一个尖峰，所得 $Y$ 路径不连续。
>
> 对于 **Gaussian 假设**，取独立随机乘数 $A$，以各半概率取 $0$ 和 $\sqrt2$，令 $Z_t=AW_t$。其均值为零，协方差为 $E[A^2]\min(s,t)=\min(s,t)$，路径也仍连续。然而对 $t>0$，$Z_t$ 在零点有 $1/2$ 的概率质量，同时方差 $t>0$；它既不是非退化正态，也不是常数，所以该过程不是 Gaussian，也不是布朗运动。
