# 锚定效应是数量判断受到先前显著数值的牵引
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Anchoring is the pull of a previously salient number on a numerical judgment*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

锚定效应是数量估计受到初始数值影响，后续判断仍向该数值靠拢的现象。初始值可由题目、他人或自己的不完整计算提供。经典解释是从起点调整不足；定义这一现象不要求所有锚定都由完全相同的心理过程产生。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Anchoring is the influence of an initial number on a numerical estimate, with the later judgment remaining pulled toward it. The starting value may come from a question, another person, or one's own incomplete calculation. Insufficient adjustment is the classic explanation, but the phenomenon need not always arise through an identical psychological process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原始实验让受试者先看到转盘给出的 $10$ 或 $65$，再估计联合国成员中非洲国家的比例；两组估计中位数分别为 $25\%$ 和 $45\%$。转盘数值不是关于该比例的有效资料，却影响了回答。这是该实验的结果，不是说任意锚点都会产生固定幅度的变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the original experiment, subjects first saw a wheel-generated number of $10$ or $65$, then estimated the percentage of United Nations members that were African countries. Median estimates were $25\%$ and $45\%$, respectively. The wheel supplied no relevant information about the percentage but influenced answers. This is an experimental result, not a fixed effect size for every anchor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

并非所有利用参考信息的估计都“有偏”：有效历史数据或有信息含量的初始值可以合理影响判断。识别锚定偏差须考察数值的相关性及在共同信息下是否产生不当牵引。[[前景参考点]]负责划分得失，而锚定影响数值估计；买入价可能在不同任务中承担其中一种或两种角色，但不能省略任务区分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Not every estimate informed by a reference is biased: valid historical data or an informative starting value may rationally matter. Identifying anchoring bias requires examining relevance and inappropriate influence under common information. A [[前景参考点|prospect reference point]] separates gains from losses, whereas anchoring affects an estimate. A purchase price can serve either or both roles in different tasks, which must be distinguished.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
