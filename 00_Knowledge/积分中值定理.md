# Mean value theorem for integrals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*积分中值定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An average is a height calculated from an entire interval. Must that height occur at an actual point of the graph? The **mean value theorem for integrals** says yes when the function is continuous on a finite closed interval of positive length. A continuously varying temperature, for instance, reaches its time-average temperature at least once during that interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平均值是由整个区间算出的一个高度。这个高度一定在图像的某一点出现过吗？**积分中值定理**说明：如果函数在一个长度为正的有限闭区间上连续，答案就是肯定的。例如，连续变化的温度在这段时间中至少会有一次达到自己的时间平均值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[积分平均值|integral average]] $f_{\mathrm{avg}}$ is the signed total divided by the interval length. The theorem guarantees attainment of this value, beyond merely defining it. If $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ is continuous and $a<b$ are finite, there is a point $c\in[a,b]$ such that
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[积分平均值]] $f_{\mathrm{avg}}$ 是带符号的总累积除以区间长度。本定理在这个定义之外，额外保证该值确实被取到。若实值函数 $f$ 在有限闭区间 $[a,b]$ 上连续且 $a<b$，则存在 $c\in[a,b]$，使

$$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx=f(c)(b-a),\qquad f(c)=f_{\mathrm{avg}}=\frac1{b-a}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $c$ is an input position, whereas $f(c)$ is the average height. The theorem does not say that $c$ is the midpoint or that there is only one such point. Negative values are allowed; the integral remains signed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $c$ 是输入位置，$f(c)$ 才是平均高度。定理并没有说 $c$ 是区间中点，也没有说这样的点只有一个。函数允许取负值，积分仍按带符号的累积理解。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The average lies between heights the graph connects
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*平均值夹在图像连起来的两个高度之间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Continuity on a finite closed interval first guarantees [[连续函数可积|integrability]]. The [[极值定理|extreme value theorem]] also gives an attained minimum $m$ and maximum $M$. Since $m\le f(x)\le M$ everywhere, [[积分上下界估计|integral bounds]] give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限闭区间上的连续性首先保证[[连续函数可积|可积]]；[[极值定理]]还保证函数取到最小值 $m$ 与最大值 $M$。由于处处 $m\le f(x)\le M$，由[[积分上下界估计]]可得

$$m(b-a)\le\int_a^b f(x)\,dx\le M(b-a),\qquad m\le f_{\mathrm{avg}}\le M.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dividing preserves the inequalities because $b-a>0$. If $m=M$, the function is constant and every point works. Otherwise choose points where the minimum and maximum occur. On the segment connecting those two inputs, the [[介值定理|intermediate value theorem]] ensures that the continuous function takes every height between $m$ and $M$, including $f_{\mathrm{avg}}$. This proves the claim without first finding an antiderivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因为 $b-a>0$，相除时不等号不变。如果 $m=M$，函数是常数，任意点都符合要求。否则，选出取到最小值、最大值的两个输入；在连接它们的线段上，[[介值定理]]保证连续函数取遍 $m,M$ 之间的所有高度，自然包括 $f_{\mathrm{avg}}$。这个证明不需要先找原函数。

> [!example] The location need not be the midpoint · 所在位置未必是中点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $f(x)=x^2$ on $[0,1]$, the integral and average are both $1/3$. Solving $c^2=1/3$ within the interval gives $c=1/\sqrt3$. At the midpoint $1/2$, the height would instead be $1/4$. On $[-1,1]$, the same function still has average $1/3$, but two points work: $c=\pm1/\sqrt3$. A constant function has infinitely many working points.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $[0,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x^2$，积分与平均值都是 $1/3$。在区间内解 $c^2=1/3$，得到 $c=1/\sqrt3$；中点 $1/2$ 的函数值却是 $1/4$。如果改用 $[-1,1]$，同一函数的平均值仍为 $1/3$，但有两个符合要求的点 $c=\pm1/\sqrt3$；常数函数则有无穷多个。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why integrability alone is insufficient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么只有可积还不够*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f(x)=0$ on $[0,1/2)$ and $f(x)=1$ on $[1/2,1]$. This function is Riemann integrable: only partition pieces meeting its single jump can make the upper and lower sums differ, and their total length can be made arbitrarily small. Its integral is $1/2$, so its average on $[0,1]$ is $1/2$. But its only values are $0$ and $1$. No point gives the average height.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $[0,1/2)$ 上 $f(x)=0$，$[1/2,1]$ 上 $f(x)=1$。该函数黎曼可积：只有接触唯一跳跃点的分割小段可能造成上下和之差，而这些小段的总长度可以任意小。它的积分为 $1/2$，所以在 $[0,1]$ 上的平均值为 $1/2$；但函数只取 $0,1$ 两种值，没有一点取到平均高度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus a function may have an average without attaining it. Continuity is a sufficient condition for the attainment guarantee, not a necessary condition for any particular function to attain its average. In the step example, changing only the value at $1/2$ to $1/2$ leaves the integral unchanged but creates a point attaining the average; the function is still discontinuous.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，函数可以有平均值却不取到平均值。连续性是保证取到平均值的充分条件，并不是某个具体函数取到平均值的必要条件。例如，只把上述阶梯函数在 $1/2$ 处的点值改成 $1/2$，积分不变，却出现了一个取到平均值的点；函数仍然不连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## How this relates to the derivative mean value theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*它与导数中值定理有什么联系*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For continuous $f$, its [[累积函数|accumulation]] $G(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$ is continuous on $[a,b]$, and the [[积分求导定理|fundamental theorem for accumulation]] gives $G'=f$ in the interior. Applying the [[拉格朗日中值定理|derivative mean value theorem]] to $G$ produces an interior point $c$ satisfying
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 连续，则[[累积函数]] $G(x)=\int_a^x f(t)\,dt$ 在 $[a,b]$ 连续，且[[积分求导定理]]给出内部 $G'=f$。对 $G$ 使用[[拉格朗日中值定理]]，可以得到一个内部点 $c$，满足

$$f(c)=G'(c)=\frac{G(b)-G(a)}{b-a}=\frac1{b-a}\int_a^b f(t)\,dt.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is another proof, and shows that under these hypotheses a suitable point can even be chosen in $(a,b)$. The two theorems concern different objects: one finds an average **height of $f$**, while the derivative theorem compares a **secant slope of $G$** with one of its tangent slopes. The accumulation theorem connects them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是另一种证明，还说明在这些假设下，符合要求的点甚至可以选在 $(a,b)$ 内。两个中值定理关注的对象不同：本定理寻找 $f$ 的平均**高度**，导数中值定理则把 $G$ 的**割线斜率**与某处切线斜率相比较；积分求导定理把两者连接起来。
