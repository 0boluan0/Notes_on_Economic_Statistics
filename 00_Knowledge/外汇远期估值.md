# 外汇远期多头价值等于贴现外币收入的本币价值减去贴现本币支付
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A long FX forward is worth the domestic value of discounted foreign receipts minus discounted domestic payments*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先读清收付方向。设到期**收 $N$ 单位外币、付 $NK$ 本币**，现货 $S$ 报“本币/外币”，两币种到期零息贴现因子为 $D_f,D_d$。在无摩擦、可按相应曲线借贷且忽略信用和基差调整的复制口径下，当前本币价值为

$$V=N D_f S-NK D_d.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a contract receiving $N$ foreign units and paying $NK$ domestic units, quote spot as domestic per foreign currency. With discount factors $D_f,D_d$, frictionless curve-consistent borrowing and lending, and no credit or basis adjustment, its domestic value is $ND_fS-NKD_d$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

推导只需两笔零息头寸：今天买入并存入 $ND_f$ 外币，到期变成 $N$；今天借入 $NKD_d$ 本币，到期归还 $NK$。合起来恰好复制收付。反方向合约把整式取负；到期交割价 $K$ 固定，不随今天的公平远期报价改写。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Buy and invest $ND_f$ foreign units today to receive $N$ at maturity; borrow $NKD_d$ domestic units to repay $NK$. These two legs replicate the contract. Reverse the sign for the opposite contract, and keep the agreed delivery price $K$ fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程例子收 100 万欧元、付 130 万美元，一年有效利率分别为 4% 和 3%。因此

$$V=\frac{1{,}000{,}000}{1.04}S-\frac{1{,}300{,}000}{1.03},\qquad \Delta_S=\frac{1{,}000{,}000}{1.04}\approx961{,}538.46\ \text{EUR}.$$

欧元升值使这笔合约增值，Delta 为正。固定两条曲线时，抵销它需要相反方向的外币现货暴露；是卖空约 961,538.46 欧元，不是卖空未贴现的 100 万欧元。若利率给的是连续复利率，则分别用 $e^{-r_f\tau}$ 和 $e^{-r_d\tau}$，不能混用两套口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the course's one-year receipt of EUR 1 million against USD 1.3 million, effective annual rates of 4% and 3% give positive spot Delta of EUR 961,538.46. Offsetting spot exposure has the opposite sign. Continuously compounded rates would instead use exponential discount factors; do not mix conventions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是对汇率的局部或固定曲线对冲，不抵销两条利率曲线的变动；融资、结算和实际借贷利差也仍需计入。数量如何由敏感度变成订单，见 [[Delta对冲]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This spot hedge does not eliminate changes in the two rate curves, financing or settlement costs. See [[Delta对冲|Delta hedging]] for converting sensitivities into signed orders.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
