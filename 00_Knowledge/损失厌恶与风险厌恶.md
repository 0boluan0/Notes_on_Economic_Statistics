# 损失厌恶比较得失不对称而风险厌恶比较确定结果与风险结果
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Loss aversion compares gain–loss asymmetry, whereas risk aversion compares certainty with risk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[损失厌恶]]比较同幅收益与损失的价值。风险厌恶则是在固定背景和评价口径下，对所讨论的一类风险结果 $X$，弱偏好确定得到 $E[X]$ 而非承担 $X$。在期望效用表示下，若对某结果区间内所有有限支撑风险都作此比较，则等价于效用函数在该区间凹；这一等价不能直接套在使用非线性[[前景决策权重]]的模型上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[损失厌恶|Loss aversion]] compares equal-sized gains and losses. Risk aversion means weakly preferring a sure $E[X]$ to the risky outcome $X$ over the relevant class of risks, holding the background and evaluation convention fixed. Under expected utility, making this comparison for every finite-support risk in an outcome interval is equivalent to concavity of utility on that interval. This equivalence cannot simply be transferred to models with nonlinear [[前景决策权重|decision weights]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小对比：取 $v(x)=\sqrt{x}$（收益）和 $v(x)=-2\sqrt{-x}$（损失），并令权重等于实际概率。该函数满足同幅损失厌恶，但比较“确定损失 $100$”与“各半概率损失 $200$ 或不损失”时，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a minimal contrast, use $v(x)=\sqrt{x}$ for gains and $v(x)=-2\sqrt{-x}$ for losses, with weights equal to actual probabilities. This function exhibits equal-sized loss aversion. Yet comparing a sure loss of $100$ with an equal-probability loss of $200$ or zero gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$V_{\rm sure}=-20,\qquad V_{\rm risky}=\tfrac12(-2\sqrt{200})+\tfrac12(0)=-10\sqrt2\approx-14.142.$$

两方案的货币期望损失都是 $100$，而较大的前景价值属于风险方案。因此损失厌恶可以与这一损失区间的风险寻求同时存在。反过来，仅在收益区间观察到风险厌恶，也没有识别出跨越参考点的损失厌恶。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both alternatives have an expected monetary loss of $100$, but the risky alternative has greater prospect value. Loss aversion can therefore coexist with risk seeking over this loss range. Conversely, risk aversion observed only over gains does not identify loss aversion across the reference point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同样，“风险厌恶”不意味着偏好只由均值与方差决定；那是另一项[[均值方差偏好边界|需要额外条件的简化]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Risk aversion also does not imply that preferences depend only on mean and variance; that is a [[均值方差偏好边界|separate simplification requiring additional conditions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
