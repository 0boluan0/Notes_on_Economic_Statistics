# The fundamental matrix of an absorbing chain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*吸收链基本矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before a process finishes, it may revisit the same active state many times. The **fundamental matrix** collects the expected number of those visits: choose a starting transient state to select a row, and choose the transient state to be counted to select a column. Its entries are counts averaged over possible paths, so they can exceed one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

过程结束之前，可能反复访问同一个活动状态。**基本矩阵**把这些访问次数的期望收集起来：选择起始暂态，就确定一行；选择要统计的暂态，就确定一列。元素是在所有可能路径上平均的次数，因此可以超过一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work with a fixed finite [[吸收链|absorbing Markov chain]] in the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row convention]]. In its [[吸收链标准形|canonical form]], $Q$ is the $t\times t$ transient-to-transient block, with $t\ge1$. Every nonabsorbing state eventually reaches absorption, and the [[基本矩阵存在条件|finite convergence criterion]] guarantees the following inverse and infinite sum are well defined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑固定的有限[[吸收链|吸收 Markov 链]]，采用[[Markov矩阵左右约定|行约定]]。在其[[吸收链标准形]]中，$Q$ 为 $t\times t$ 的暂态到暂态块，$t\ge1$。每个非吸收状态最终都会吸收；[[基本矩阵存在条件]]保证下面的矩阵逆与无穷和有意义。

> [!important] A sum over all observation times · 汇总全部观测时点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The fundamental matrix is
>
> $$N=(I_t-Q)^{-1}=I_t+Q+Q^2+\cdots=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}Q^n.$$
>
> Here $I_t$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity]], so the sum includes time zero. For transient states $i,j$, $N_{ij}$ is the [[期望|expected]] number of visits to $j$ before absorption, starting from $i$. The course notes use $W$ for this same matrix; changing its name does not change the object.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 基本矩阵由上式定义，其中 $I_t$ 是[[单位矩阵]]，因此求和包含时刻零。对暂态 $i,j$，$N_{ij}$ 是从 $i$ 出发、吸收之前访问 $j$ 的次数之[[期望]]。本课程原笔记把同一个矩阵记为 $W$，符号不同不改变对象。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Start with one transient state
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先看只有一个暂态的情形*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the process stays in its only transient state with probability $q$, where $0\le q<1$, and is absorbed otherwise. Then $Q=(q)$ and

$$N=1+q+q^2+\cdots=\frac1{1-q}.$$

The initial visit is certain. Another visit at time one requires surviving one step, with probability $q$; a visit at time two requires surviving twice, with probability $q^2$. If $q=1/2$, the expected count is $2$, although a particular path may have one, two, three or more visits. If $q=0$, the expected count is $1$, not zero: absorption occurs on the first transition after the initial visit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设只有一个暂态，每步以 $q$ 的概率留在原处，以其余概率吸收，其中 $0\le q<1$。于是 $Q=(q)$，基本矩阵就是上述几何级数。初始访问确定发生；时刻一再次访问需要存活一步，概率为 $q$；时刻二访问需要存活两步，概率为 $q^2$。若 $q=1/2$，期望次数为2，但具体路径可以访问一次、两次、三次或更多。若 $q=0$，期望次数为1而非零：过程在初始访问之后的第一次转移才吸收。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why summing probabilities counts visits
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么概率相加能够统计访问*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $(X_n)$ denote the state process and let $\tau=\inf\{n\ge0:X_n\text{ is absorbing}\}$ be the first absorption time. Starting at transient $i$ gives $\tau\ge1$. Define

$$V_j=\sum_{n=0}^{\tau-1}\mathbf1_{\{X_n=j\}}
=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\mathbf1_{\{X_n=j\}}.$$

The [[事件指示变量|indicator]] is one when the state equals $j$ and zero otherwise. The equality holds because an absorbing path can never return to a transient $j$. Each finite partial count is nonnegative and the counts increase to $V_j$. [[单调收敛定理|Monotone convergence]] and [[期望线性性|linearity for finite sums]] therefore give

$$E_i[V_j]=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\Pr_i(X_n=j)
=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(Q^n)_{ij}=N_{ij}.$$

Here $E_i$ and $\Pr_i$ mean expectation and probability with $X_0=i$. The visits need not be independent. The convergence criterion ensures the resulting expectation is finite, rather than merely an extended nonnegative expectation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $(X_n)$ 表示状态过程，令 $\tau=\inf\{n\ge0:X_n\text{ 是吸收态}\}$ 为首次吸收时刻。从暂态 $i$ 开始时有 $\tau\ge1$。上式 $V_j$ 统计吸收前对 $j$ 的访问次数；[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]在状态等于 $j$ 时为一，否则为零。吸收以后不可能再到暂态 $j$，所以把求和延伸到全部非负时点也不改变计数。有限部分计数非负，且递增到 $V_j$，因此[[单调收敛定理]]与有限求和的[[期望线性性]]给出期望恒等式。$E_i$、$\Pr_i$ 分别指 $X_0=i$ 时的期望与概率；并不要求各次访问独立。收敛判据进一步保证这个期望有限，而不只是一个可能为无穷的非负期望。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two active states reveal the row and column roles
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两个活动状态展示行与列的职责*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Order four states as $(s_1,s_2,a,b)$, with $a,b$ absorbing, and take

$$P=\begin{pmatrix}
1/2&1/4&1/4&0\\
0&1/2&1/4&1/4\\
0&0&1&0\\
0&0&0&1
\end{pmatrix},\quad
Q=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&1/4\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix}.$$

Subtract $Q$ from the identity and verify the inverse by multiplication:

$$I_2-Q=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&-1/4\\0&1/2\end{pmatrix},\qquad
N=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix},\qquad (I_2-Q)N=I_2.$$

Starting at $s_1$, the expected visits to $s_1$ and $s_2$ are $2$ and $1$. Starting at $s_2$, one never reaches $s_1$, and the expected visits to $s_2$ are $2$. The asymmetry reflects the direction of the transitions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

按 $(s_1,s_2,a,b)$ 排列四个状态，其中 $a,b$ 吸收，取上述 $P$ 与暂态块 $Q$。从单位矩阵减去 $Q$，再相乘核对逆，得到 $N$。从 $s_1$ 出发，对 $s_1,s_2$ 的期望访问次数分别为2、1；从 $s_2$ 出发，永远到不了 $s_1$，对 $s_2$ 的期望访问次数为2。矩阵的不对称反映了转移的方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In particular, $N_{12}=1$ does **not** mean that $s_2$ is reached with probability one. Before leaving $s_1$, the competing exit probabilities to $s_2$ and $a$ are equal; the probability of reaching $s_2$ is $1/2$. If reached, it contributes an expected two visits. Thus the expected count is $(1/2)\cdot2=1$. A visit count includes repeated visits; a hitting probability counts only whether there was any visit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

特别地，$N_{12}=1$ **不表示**以概率一到达 $s_2$。在离开 $s_1$ 时，通向 $s_2$ 与 $a$ 的两个出口概率相等，所以到达 $s_2$ 的概率是 $1/2$；到达之后，期望访问两次，因此总体期望次数为 $(1/2)\cdot2=1$。访问次数计入重复访问，命中概率只问有没有访问过。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The identity $N=I_t+QN$ gives the same interpretation by the first step: count the initial visit, then, if the next state remains transient, add the expected visits from that new starting state. Multiplying by the exit block gives [[吸收概率矩阵为NR|probabilities of the eventual absorbing destination]]; adding the expected visit counts across each row gives [[期望吸收时间为N1|mean absorption times]]. Those are different questions built from this visit matrix.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

恒等式 $N=I_t+QN$ 也有第一步解释：先计初始访问；下一步若仍在暂态区，再加从新状态出发的期望访问。用出口块相乘，可求[[吸收概率矩阵为NR|最终吸收到各个终态的概率]]；逐行加总期望访问次数，可求[[期望吸收时间为N1|平均吸收时间]]。这些是利用同一个访问矩阵回答的不同问题。

> [!note] What is and is not inside N · N 包含什么、不包含什么
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Only transient starting and destination states index $N$. Every diagonal entry is at least one because time zero is counted; off-diagonal entries may be zero. Its rows are not probability distributions and need not sum to one. Starting already absorbed gives zero transient visits and absorption time zero, but it is not another row of this $t\times t$ matrix. If all states are absorbing, the transient visit problem is empty.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $N$ 的起点与目标索引都只包含暂态。对角元至少为一，因为计入时刻零；非对角元可以为零。其各行不是概率分布，也不要求行和为一。若起点已经吸收，则暂态访问次数和吸收时间都为零，但这不是该 $t\times t$ 矩阵中的另一行。若全部状态都吸收，暂态访问问题为空。
