# 长上下文语言模型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Long-Context Language Models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 让模型在一次调用中接收并综合超出普通窗口的长文档、长对话或多份材料。
> **具体锚点：** 把一本报告放进 128k 窗口后，模型理论上能读取全书，但关键证据埋在中间时仍可能漏掉。
> **核心难点：** 名义可输入长度、训练覆盖长度、远距离信息的有效利用和推理资源成本是四个不同概念。
> **为什么重要：** “窗口更大”常被误当成“任何位置都能可靠记住”，从而掩盖位置偏差、干扰和成本。
> **继续：** 先用位置×长度×干扰实验测有效上下文；需要更新、筛选和引用时转到 [[RAG（检索增强生成）]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Problem addressed:** Let a model receive and synthesize long documents, conversations, or multiple sources that exceed an ordinary context window in one call.
> **Concrete anchor:** A full report may fit in a 128k-token window, yet the model can still miss decisive evidence buried in the middle.
> **Central difficulty:** Nominal input capacity, training-length coverage, effective use of distant information, and inference-resource cost are four different concepts.
> **Why it matters:** A larger window is often mistaken for reliable memory at every position, hiding positional bias, interference, and cost.
> **Continue with:** Measure effective context across position, length, and distractors; use [[RAG（检索增强生成）|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] when material must be updated, selected, and cited.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!source] 本节依据
> - Lost in the Middle 与 LongBench 原论文：核验证据位置、长度、干扰和不同长上下文任务的评测边界。
> - PagedAttention 原论文：核验长序列 serving 中 KV cache 的系统成本。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> - The Lost in the Middle and LongBench papers verify evaluation boundaries across evidence position, length, interference, and different long-context tasks.
> - The PagedAttention paper verifies the systems cost of the KV cache in long-sequence serving.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 窗口容量不等于有效利用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Window Capacity Is Not Effective Use*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

扩大位置范围和高效 attention 让模型接收更多 token，但有效利用通常低于名义窗口：中间信息可能被忽略，重复/冲突材料会干扰，延迟和 KV cache 增长。必须用目标任务上的不同位置、长度和干扰实验验证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Extending positional range and using efficient attention let a model accept more tokens, but effective use is usually below nominal capacity. Information in the middle may be ignored, repeated or conflicting material interferes, and latency and KV cache grow. Validation must vary position, length, and distractors on the target task.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“支持 128k”至少可能只表示 API 接受这么长的 token 序列；它不自动说明模型在 128k 处的 retrieval、multi-hop integration、精确引用和指令遵循都可靠。每种能力要用对应任务单独测量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Supports 128k” may mean only that the API accepts a sequence of that length. It does not establish reliable retrieval, multi-hop integration, exact citation, or instruction following at 128k; each capability requires its own task-level measurement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么长度和位置会改变表现
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why Length and Position Change Performance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

位置表示必须覆盖新距离，模型还要在训练中看到足够长的依赖并学会使用。即使 attention 计算允许全连接，有限精度、softmax 竞争、局部模式偏好和大量无关 token 也会稀释关键证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positional representations must cover new distances, and training must expose the model to sufficiently long dependencies and teach their use. Even with fully connected attention, finite precision, softmax competition, local-pattern preference, and many irrelevant tokens can dilute decisive evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Lost-in-the-middle 现象指模型对开头或结尾证据表现较好，而证据位于长输入中部时下降。它不是所有模型和任务的固定曲线，却提示评测不能总把答案放在最后，也不能只测“needle”是否出现。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lost-in-the-middle phenomenon describes stronger performance when evidence appears near the beginning or end and weaker performance when it is buried in the middle of a long input. It is not a fixed curve for every model and task, but it shows that evaluation must vary evidence position and cannot test only whether a needle appears.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 长上下文的任务类型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Long-Context Task Types*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- retrieval：从长输入找出一个明确事实；这是必要能力，却不证明能整合多段证据。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Retrieval: locate one explicit fact in a long input. This is necessary but does not establish multi-passage integration.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- aggregation：统计、归纳或比较分散项目，需要覆盖而非只找到一个 needle。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Aggregation: count, summarize, or compare dispersed items, requiring coverage rather than one needle.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- multi-hop reasoning：连接相距很远且单独不足的证据，最易受干扰与中间错误影响。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Multi-hop reasoning: connect distant pieces of evidence that are individually insufficient, making the task vulnerable to interference and intermediate errors.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- generation with constraints：在长规格、对话或代码库约束下输出，需同时保持指令与局部事实。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Constrained generation: produce output under a long specification, conversation, or codebase while preserving both instructions and local facts.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：位置×干扰矩阵
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: A Position-by-Interference Matrix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把同一关键句分别放在 10%、50%、90% 位置，并设置 0、10、100 个语义相近 distractors。若准确率从 98%/96%/98% 在无干扰时变为 92%/61%/90% 在 100 个干扰时，问题不是“窗口装不下”，而是中间位置与检索竞争的交互。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Place the same decisive sentence at 10%, 50%, and 90% of the context, with 0, 10, and 100 semantically similar distractors. If accuracy changes from 98%/96%/98% without distractors to 92%/61%/90% with 100 distractors, the issue is not that the window cannot fit the text; it is an interaction between middle position and retrieval competition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再把任务从抄取一句改为结合三处证据，即可区分单点 retrieval 与跨段 synthesis。每次同时记录输入 token、输出 token、TTFT、TPOT 与 KV 显存，避免只提高准确率却忽略不可接受的服务成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Then change the task from copying one sentence to combining three pieces of evidence, separating point retrieval from cross-passage synthesis. Record input and output tokens, TTFT, TPOT, and KV memory so accuracy gains are not reported without unacceptable serving cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与 RAG 的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary with RAG*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长上下文适合少量材料的整体综合、需要保留全局结构或检索会丢失关系的任务。[[RAG（检索增强生成）]] 适合大型、频繁更新、权限分层并要求证据追踪的语料。常见组合是先检索候选文档，再在较长上下文中整合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Long context suits holistic synthesis over a small set of materials, tasks that need global structure, or cases where retrieval would lose relationships. [[RAG（检索增强生成）|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] suits large, frequently updated, permissioned corpora that require evidence tracing. A common hybrid first retrieves candidate documents and then integrates them in a longer context.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 短输入正确、长输入错误：控制证据位置和 distractor 数，区分位置偏差、干扰与长度外推。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Short inputs work and long inputs fail: control evidence position and distractor count to separate positional bias, interference, and length extrapolation.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 能找到事实却不能综合：换成显式 multi-hop 与 aggregation 测试，不要用 needle retrieval 代表全部长上下文能力。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Facts can be found but not synthesized: use explicit multi-hop and aggregation tests instead of treating needle retrieval as complete long-context ability.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- OOM 或延迟非线性上升：分解 attention 计算、KV cache、batch 与调度，转到 [[LLM 推理效率]]。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Memory failures or nonlinear latency growth appear: decompose attention computation, KV cache, batch, and scheduling, then use [[LLM 推理效率|LLM Inference Efficiency]].
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 冲突材料下随意选一版：保留来源与时间元数据，要求显式处理冲突；必要时先用检索过滤版本。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  The model arbitrarily selects one conflicting version: preserve provenance and timestamps, require explicit conflict handling, and filter versions through retrieval when needed.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum Self-Check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么名义 1M token 窗口不等于能可靠使用任意位置的 1M token？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does a nominal one-million-token window not imply reliable use of one million tokens at every position?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 位置外推、注意力稀释和训练分布会使不同位置/长度的有效利用不均，需任务实测。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Positional extrapolation, diluted attention, and the training distribution create uneven effective use across positions and lengths, requiring task-level measurement.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### needle retrieval 达到 100% 为什么仍不能证明长文综合可靠？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does perfect needle retrieval not prove reliable long-document synthesis?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 单点抄取不要求覆盖、聚合或连接多段冲突证据；这些任务有不同失败面，必须单独测。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Copying one point does not require coverage, aggregation, or connecting conflicting passages. Those tasks have different failure modes and need separate tests.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 哪三个维度最少要一起变化来测有效上下文？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Which three dimensions should at minimum vary together when measuring effective context?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 总长度、关键证据位置和 distractor/冲突强度；再按任务类型记录准确率与服务成本。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Vary total length, decisive-evidence position, and distractor or conflict strength, then record accuracy and serving cost by task type.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
