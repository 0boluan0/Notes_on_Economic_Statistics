# 排放强度可调整时产量税通常不能复制直接排放税的全部减排激励
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When emissions intensity is adjustable, an output tax generally cannot reproduce every abatement incentive of a direct emissions tax.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

产量税按产品数量收费，排放税按实际排出的污染数量收费。二者可以同时鼓励减产，但只有后者直接奖励“保持产量、通过清洁投入或技术少排污染”。比较它们时，应问**税额是否随着真正的损害来源改变**，而不是只看两者都让企业交钱。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An output tax charges products; an emissions tax charges pollution released. Both may encourage lower output, but only the latter directly rewards cleaner production at unchanged output. Compare whether liability changes with the actual source of harm, not merely whether firms make payments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[产量、排放与减排|固定排放强度]] $e=\alpha q$、$\alpha>0$，没有其他减排渠道且实施条件相同的模型中，单位排放税 $t$ 对应单位产量税 $\tau=t\alpha$，因为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With [[产量、排放与减排|fixed emissions intensity]] $e=\alpha q$, $\alpha>0$, no alternative abatement margin and identical implementation conditions, emissions rate $t$ corresponds to output rate $\tau=t\alpha$, since
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$te=t\alpha q=\tau q.$$

如果企业能改变技术 $z$，使 $e=g(q,z)$，则给定 $q$ 的纯产量税 $\tau q$ 不随 $z$ 变化；实际排放税 $t g(q,z)$ 则会随减排而减少。纯产量税可能仍间接影响技术选择，但没有逐单位少排就少缴税的直接激励。这正是用产量代理污染时可能漏掉的行为边际。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If technology $z$ changes emissions to $e=g(q,z)$, a pure output tax $\tau q$ does not vary with $z$ at fixed output, whereas liability $t g(q,z)$ falls with abatement. Output taxation can affect technology indirectly, but does not directly reward each emission unit avoided. This is the margin a pollution proxy may omit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例：生产 10 单位时原排放强度为 2，排放 20。每单位排放税 3 等价于旧技术下每单位产品税 6，二者都收 60。若花费 20 改进设备，保持产量而把排放降到 10，排放税降为 30，税款节省 30 超过技术成本；产量税仍为 60，单靠这项税没有投资的直接奖励。此例固定产量、无其他设备收益，只隔离这个边际。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At output ten and intensity two, emissions are twenty. An emissions rate of three and output rate of six both initially collect sixty. Equipment costing twenty can hold output fixed and cut emissions to ten. Emissions tax then falls to thirty, saving more than the equipment cost; output tax remains sixty. The example fixes output and excludes other equipment benefits to isolate this margin.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当实际排放难以观测，而投入或产品与损害联系足够稳定时，代理税基仍可能是可实施的近似。应说明映射、遗漏的调整渠道和核实成本，再与[[排放标准与技术标准]]等可行方案比较；不能只因为存在理论上的直接[[庇古税]]，就假设现实中一定能够准确征收。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When emissions are difficult to observe and inputs or products are reliably related to damage, a proxy can remain a feasible approximation. State the mapping, omitted margins and verification costs, then compare [[排放标准与技术标准|performance or technology standards]] and other feasible alternatives. A theoretical [[庇古税|Pigouvian tax]] does not establish that accurate direct charging is practicable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
