# 观察条件均值差只有在一致性、可比性与正值性等识别条件下才等于因果效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An observed conditional-mean contrast equals a causal effect only under conditions such as consistency, exchangeability, and positivity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 回归先描述“谁和谁不同”，因果还要说明“若改变处理会怎样”
> 令 $A\in\{0,1\}$ 为处理，$L$ 为处理前协变量。观察条件均值
> $$
> m_a(l)=E[Y\mid A=a,L=l]
> $$
> 是数据中实际处于处理状态 $a$、协变量为 $l$ 的人的平均结果。若同时有一致性、条件交换性
> $$
> Y(a)\perp A\mid L,
> $$
> 以及正值性 $P(A=a\mid L=l)>0$，才可写
> $$
> E[Y(a)\mid L=l]=m_a(l).
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $A\in\{0,1\}$ be treatment and $L$ pretreatment covariates. The observed conditional mean $m_a(l)=E[Y\mid A=a,L=l]$ is the average outcome among units actually observed with treatment $a$ and covariates $l$. Only with consistency, conditional exchangeability $Y(a)\perp A\mid L$, and positivity $P(A=a\mid L=l)>0$ can it identify $E[Y(a)\mid L=l]$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三个条件各自补上一块缺口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Each condition fills a different gap*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一致性把实际接受 $a$ 的人的观察结果连接到 $Y(a)$；交换性说在给定 $L$ 后，处理组与对照组可代表彼此的反事实；正值性保证目标协变量层内两种处理都有可观察支持。一个条件不能替代另一个。若某类人必然接受处理，就无法仅从该层数据学习他们未处理时的平均结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consistency links the observed outcome of a unit receiving $a$ to $Y(a)$. Exchangeability says that, within $L$, treated and untreated units can represent one another's counterfactual outcomes. Positivity supplies observed support for both treatment levels in each target covariate stratum. None substitutes for another: if a group always receives treatment, its untreated mean cannot be learned from that stratum alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

满足这些条件后，条件效应
$$
E[Y(1)-Y(0)\mid L=l]
=m_1(l)-m_0(l)
$$
才有因果含义。若目标就是这些数据所代表的同一总体，再按该总体的 $L$ 分布标准化，才能得到相应 ATE。换到另一个目标总体就是换 estimand。目标总体中每个相关的 $L$ 取值都要在研究总体中有数据支持，还要补充可迁移性条件：例如给定 $L$ 的条件处理效应可迁移；更强但也足够的条件是两种处理下的条件反事实均值都可迁移。满足相应条件后，才可使用目标总体的 $L$ 分布重新加权。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under these conditions, $m_1(l)-m_0(l)$ identifies $E[Y(1)-Y(0)\mid L=l]$. If the target is the same population represented by the data, averaging over that population's distribution of $L$ gives the corresponding ATE. Moving to another target population changes the estimand. Every relevant value of $L$ in the target population must have support in the study population, and an additional transportability condition is needed: one may assume that the conditional treatment effect transports given $L$; a stronger sufficient condition is that both conditional counterfactual means transport. Only then may the target population's distribution of $L$ be used for reweighting.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归系数还多一层函数形式边界。即使给定 $L$ 后处理分配满足交换性，线性系数也可能只是异质条件效应的某种加权投影；反过来，即使条件均值模型拟合得很准，没有交换性，它描述的仍只是观察关联。[[条件均值与线性投影]] 负责“均值还是投影”的区分，本卡只负责“观察量何时能解释成干预量”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A regression coefficient adds a functional-form boundary. Even when treatment assignment is exchangeable conditional on $L$, a linear coefficient may be only a weighted projection of heterogeneous conditional effects. Conversely, even a well-fitted conditional-mean model remains associational without exchangeability. [[条件均值与线性投影|The conditional-mean versus projection atom]] handles the statistical object; this card handles when that object can represent an intervention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 给定年龄、教育和行业后，参加培训者与未参加者的平均工资相同。能否据此宣布培训的条件因果效应为零？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Conditional on age, education, and industry, trainees and non-trainees have the same average wage. Does this establish a zero conditional causal effect?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。这个等式先是观察条件均值差为零；还要说明处理定义一致、给定这些变量后不存在未测混杂，并且各层都有处理与对照支持。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. It first establishes a zero observed conditional-mean contrast. A causal conclusion also needs consistency, no residual confounding after conditioning, and support for both treatment levels in the relevant strata.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
