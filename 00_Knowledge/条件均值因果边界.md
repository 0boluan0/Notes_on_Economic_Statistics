# Conditional means and causal effects
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*条件均值与因果效应*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A wage difference between trainees and non-trainees first compares **two groups that actually exist**. A causal effect asks what would happen to **the same target population** under training versus no training. These are different comparisons. This card explains a common sufficient route from an observed [[条件期望|conditional mean]] to a causal mean, and why fitting the observed mean accurately does not itself establish that route.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参加培训者与未参加者的工资差，首先比较的是**实际存在的两个群体**；因果效应问的是**同一个目标总体**若接受培训、若不接受培训，各会怎样。这是不同的比较。本卡说明：通过一条常用的充分识别路径，怎样把观察[[条件期望|条件均值]]连接到因果均值，以及为什么准确拟合观察均值本身还不够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Name the observed and counterfactual quantities
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先说清观察量与反事实量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A=1$ mean receiving a specified training programme and $A=0$ receiving the specified comparison. Let $L$ collect characteristics measured before treatment, and $Y$ be the observed wage at a fixed later time. The [[潜在结果反事实|potential outcome]] $Y(a)$ is the wage that this same unit would have under treatment $a$. Its treatment effect is $Y(1)-Y(0)$, even though the two outcomes are generally [[反事实不可同时观察|not both observed for the same unit]]. Assume finite absolute means $E|Y|<\infty$ and $E|Y(a)|<\infty$ for $a=0,1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $A=1$ 表示接受明确规定的培训项目，$A=0$ 表示规定的对照安排；$L$ 收集处理前特征，$Y$ 是指定后续时点的观察工资。[[潜在结果反事实|潜在结果]] $Y(a)$ 表示同一个单位接受处理 $a$ 时会有的工资，单位效应为 $Y(1)-Y(0)$，尽管通常[[反事实不可同时观察|无法同时看到同一单位的两个结果]]。以下假设观察结果与两种潜在结果的绝对均值都有限，即 $E|Y|<\infty$、$E|Y(a)|<\infty$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a covariate value $l$, distinguish
$$m_a(l)=E[Y\mid A=a,L=l],\qquad
\mu_a(l)=E[Y(a)\mid L=l].$$
The first averages observed wages among people who actually received $a$ within that stratum. The second averages what everyone in that stratum would earn if assigned $a$. The conditional causal effect is $\tau(l)=\mu_1(l)-\mu_0(l)$. Initially, $m_1(l)-m_0(l)$ is only an observed contrast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定协变量取值 $l$，上式区分两个量：$m_a(l)$ 对这一层中**实际接受 $a$** 的人平均观察工资；$\mu_a(l)$ 对这一层所有人**若都接受 $a$** 的潜在工资取平均。条件因果效应为 $\tau(l)=\mu_1(l)-\mu_0(l)$；最初的 $m_1(l)-m_0(l)$ 只是观察对比。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With discrete $L$, statements concern strata with positive population probability. With continuous $L$, these are conditional-expectation functions defined almost everywhere; one does not divide by $P(L=l)=0$. The conditions below hold almost everywhere in the target covariate distribution, not at arbitrarily selected null-probability points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

离散 $L$ 时，只讨论总体概率为正的层；连续 $L$ 时，它们是几乎处处定义的条件期望函数，不能除以 $P(L=l)=0$。以下条件在目标总体的协变量分布下几乎处处成立，不对任意选定的零概率点提出逐点断言。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A sufficient identification route
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 三个条件各自补上一块缺口

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Consistency** connects treatment to the observed outcome: $Y=Y(A)$, so actual recipients of $a$ reveal $Y(a)$. The interventions and outcome time must be defined clearly. Writing only $Y(a)$ also presumes that other people's assignments do not alter this person's outcome; if there is interference, the intervention notation must include it. This causal use of “consistency” is different from [[估计量一致性|an estimator becoming accurate as sample size grows]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**一致性**把处理与观察结果连接起来：$Y=Y(A)$，所以实际接受 $a$ 的人展示 $Y(a)$。干预和观察时点要定义清楚。只写 $Y(a)$ 也预设别人的处理不会改变本人的结果；若存在干扰，就需在干预记号中纳入它。这里的因果“一致性”与[[估计量一致性|估计量随样本量增长接近目标]]是不同概念。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Conditional exchangeability**, written $Y(a)\perp A\mid L$ for each $a$, says that within $L$, receiving treatment carries no additional information about the distribution of the potential outcome under that fixed $a$. The independence symbol concerns $Y(a)$, not the observed $Y$, which can change with treatment. For identifying means, the weaker **conditional mean exchangeability**
$$E[Y(a)\mid A,L]=E[Y(a)\mid L]$$
already suffices. It keeps the average unchanged even if other distributional features differ, as in [[均值独立|mean independence]]. Full conditional independence is a convenient stronger sufficient assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**条件交换性**写作每个 $a$ 下的 $Y(a)\perp A\mid L$：给定 $L$ 后，实际是否接受处理不再提供关于固定处理 $a$ 下潜在结果分布的额外信息。独立符号针对 $Y(a)$，不是可能随处理而变的观察结果 $Y$。只识别均值时，更弱的上式**条件均值交换性**就已足够：它保持平均不变，允许分布其他特征不同，类似[[均值独立]]。完整的条件独立是一个方便、但更强的充分假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Positivity** supplies both observed treatment groups at the covariate values being used: $0<P(A=1\mid L)<1$ almost surely in this population. If everyone in a relevant stratum is treated, that stratum supplies no observed untreated mean. This is a population support condition, not a guarantee that every small finite sample contains both groups in every stratum. Very small probabilities can make estimation unstable even when they are positive.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**正值性**保证所用协变量取值下有两种处理的观察支持，即在本总体中几乎处处有 $0<P(A=1\mid L)<1$。某个相关层里若人人都接受处理，该层就没有观察到的未处理均值。这是总体支持条件，不保证每份小样本的每一层都恰好抽到两组；概率很小，即使为正，也会使估计不稳定。

> [!important] Why the observed mean can identify the causal mean · 观察均值怎样识别因果均值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under consistency, conditional mean exchangeability and positivity,
> $$\underbrace{E[Y\mid A=a,L=l]}_{m_a(l)}
> =E[Y(a)\mid A=a,L=l]
> =\underbrace{E[Y(a)\mid L=l]}_{\mu_a(l)}.$$
> The first equality uses consistency; the second uses mean exchangeability. Positivity makes the observed conditional mean available on the target strata. Hence $m_1(l)-m_0(l)=\tau(l)$ under this sufficient set of assumptions. These are population equalities; estimating $m_a$ reliably from finite data is a further statistical task.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一致性、条件均值交换性和正值性共同给出上式：第一步用一致性，把实际接受 $a$ 的人的 $Y$ 换成 $Y(a)$；第二步用均值交换性，去掉实际分组 $A=a$。正值性让目标层中对应的观察条件均值有定义。因此在这组充分假设下，$m_1(l)-m_0(l)=\tau(l)$。这些是总体等式；用有限数据可靠估计 $m_a$，还需要另做统计工作。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The same groups, different weights
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*同样的层，不同的权重*

> [!example] Training helps by four, while the observed groups differ by sixteen · 效应为四，观察组差却为十六
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Construct a population with $P(L=0)=P(L=1)=1/2$, potential wages $Y(0)=50+20L$ and $Y(1)=54+20L$. Assign training with probability $0.2$ when $L=0$ and $0.8$ when $L=1$. Within a stratum the potential outcomes are fixed, so exchangeability holds; both probabilities are positive, and observe $Y=Y(A)$. This specified construction justifies the assumptions; a fitted table alone would not.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 构造一个总体：$L=0,1$ 各占一半，潜在工资为 $Y(0)=50+20L$、$Y(1)=54+20L$。在 $L=0$ 层以 $0.2$ 的概率分配培训，在 $L=1$ 层以 $0.8$ 的概率分配。层内潜在结果固定，所以交换性成立；两组概率为正，且观察 $Y=Y(A)$。是这个明确构造支持了假设，单凭拟合出来的表不能证明它们。
>
> | $L$ | $A$ | Joint probability · 联合概率 | Observed $Y$ · 观察工资 |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | $0$ | $0$ | $0.4$ | $50$ |
> | $0$ | $1$ | $0.1$ | $54$ |
> | $1$ | $0$ | $0.1$ | $70$ |
> | $1$ | $1$ | $0.4$ | $74$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both stratum effects are $4$. However, $80\%$ of trainees have $L=1$, while only $20\%$ of non-trainees do. Thus $E[Y\mid A=1]=0.2(54)+0.8(74)=70$, and $E[Y\mid A=0]=0.8(50)+0.2(70)=54$. The raw difference is $16$. Giving both treatment scenarios the **same population weights** gives $E[Y(1)]=(54+74)/2=64$ and $E[Y(0)]=(50+70)/2=60$, recovering the effect $4$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两层效应都为 $4$，但培训者中 $80\%$ 来自 $L=1$，未培训者只有 $20\%$。所以处理组观察均值为 $70$，对照组为 $54$，原始组差是 $16$。把两个处理情景都按**同一总体权重**平均，得到潜在均值 $64$、$60$，差为真正的 $4$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the same population, [[全期望公式|total expectation]] gives the average treatment effect (ATE)
$$E[Y(1)-Y(0)]=E_L[m_1(L)-m_0(L)].$$
The subscript $L$ means average over the target population's covariate distribution. For discrete $L$, multiply each stratum contrast by $P(L=l)$ and sum. This is **standardization**, not separate weighting by the covariate distributions of the observed treatment groups.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同一总体，[[全期望公式]]给出上式平均处理效应（ATE）。下标 $L$ 表示按目标总体的协变量分布平均；离散时，用每个条件对比乘 $P(L=l)$ 再相加。这叫**标准化**，不能分别套用观察处理组、对照组各自的协变量权重。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What this route does not claim
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*这条路径没有宣称什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sufficient assumptions are not all logically necessary for a numerical contrast to equal an effect. For example, let a baseline type $G$ take $0$ or $1$ with equal probabilities, and independently let a sign $S$ take $-1$ or $1$ with equal probabilities. Set $A=G$, $U=(1+G)S$, and $Y(a)=U+4a$, with no covariates $L$. Then $E[U\mid A]=0$, so the observed mean contrast and ATE both equal $4$. Yet $Y(0)$ takes $\pm1$ in group $A=0$ and $\pm2$ in group $A=1$: full exchangeability fails. Mean exchangeability is enough here. The construction is a counterexample to necessity, not a way to verify exchangeability from observed group means.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这组充分假设，并非让某个数值对比恰好等于效应的全部逻辑必要条件。例如，处理前类型 $G$ 等概率取 $0,1$，独立的符号 $S$ 等概率取 $-1,1$；令 $A=G$、$U=(1+G)S$，潜在结果为 $Y(a)=U+4a$，不使用协变量 $L$。此时 $E[U\mid A]=0$，观察均值差与 ATE 都等于 $4$；但 $Y(0)$ 在 $A=0$ 组取 $\pm1$，在 $A=1$ 组取 $\pm2$，完整交换性不成立。均值交换性在这里已经够用。这个构造反驳的是必要性，不能拿它把观察组均值当成交换性的验证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Other research designs can identify causal targets under different assumptions, and a contrast can also agree by accidental cancellation. Without a justified identification argument, an observed equality alone does not establish a causal interpretation. Conversely, lack of support prevents this unrestricted within-stratum comparison; extrapolating into an empty treatment stratum requires additional substantive or structural assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其他研究设计可以在不同假设下识别因果目标，数值对比也可能偶然抵消后相等。没有得到论证的识别依据，仅有观察等式不能确立因果解释。反过来，缺少支持会阻断这里不加函数形式约束的层内比较；向空缺处理层外推，需要额外实质或结构假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A regression coefficient adds another question. Even with a valid identification route, a coefficient may be only a weighted [[条件均值与线性投影|linear approximation]] to heterogeneous conditional effects. A well-fitted conditional-mean model does not prove the causal assumptions. Adding controls indiscriminately can also create [[后处理控制偏误|post-treatment bias]] or [[碰撞点控制偏误|collider bias]]; selecting $L$ requires knowledge of the data-generating process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回归系数还多一个问题：即使识别路径有效，一个系数也可能只是异质条件效应的某种加权[[条件均值与线性投影|线性近似]]。条件均值拟合得好，不证明因果假设。任意增加控制还可能造成[[后处理控制偏误]]或[[碰撞点控制偏误]]，选择 $L$ 需要数据生成过程的知识。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Changing the target population changes the averaging distribution and potentially the effects themselves. Reweighting study contrasts to another population requires support for its covariate values and a transportability argument, such as equality of the conditional causal effects across populations given $L$. Equality of both conditional potential-outcome means is stronger and also sufficient. Differences in unmeasured effect modifiers, treatment versions or interference can invalidate transport; changing the weights alone cannot settle that question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

换目标总体，会改变平均权重，也可能改变效应本身。把研究总体的条件对比重新加权到另一总体，要求其协变量取值有研究支持，并有可迁移性依据，例如给定 $L$ 后两个总体的条件因果效应相同。两种处理下的条件潜在均值都相同，是更强而同样充分的条件。未测效应修饰因素、处理版本或干扰模式不同，都可能破坏迁移；仅改变权重不能解决这些问题。

> [!question]- Equal wages after adjustment · 调整后工资相同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Trainees and non-trainees have equal observed mean wages at the same age, education and industry. Does that establish zero conditional causal effect? No: it establishes a zero observed contrast. A causal conclusion requires a justified identification route, and these controls must be suitable for it. Under the sufficient conditions above, the equality would identify zero conditional effect on the relevant support.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 年龄、教育、行业相同时，培训者与未培训者观察平均工资相同，是否证明条件因果效应为零？不能；它先说明观察对比为零。因果结论需要得到论证的识别路径，且所选控制适合该路径。若上述充分条件成立，这个等式才识别相关支持上的零条件效应。
