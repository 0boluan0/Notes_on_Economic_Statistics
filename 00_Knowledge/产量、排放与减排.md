# 产量是产品数量而排放量是污染释放量、减排量是相对基准减少的排放
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Output measures products, emissions measure pollution released, and abatement measures emissions reduced relative to a baseline.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

产量 $q$ 回答“生产多少产品”，排放量 $e$ 回答“释放多少污染”，减排量 $a$ 回答“相对什么基准少排了多少”。先写清各自单位、时间和污染物；**减排增加与排放减少是同一变化的两种表达，产量却未必同步减少**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Output $q$ asks how much is produced; emissions $e$ ask how much pollution is released; abatement $a$ asks how much less is emitted relative to a stated baseline. Specify units, period and pollutant. More abatement means fewer emissions against that baseline, but not necessarily less output.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若技术和投入选择用 $z$ 表示，排放可以写成 $e=g(q,z)$。在固定基准排放 $e_0$ 下，$a=e_0-e$。因此减排可以来自减产，也可以来自同样产量下更清洁的投入、生产工艺或末端处理。$e_0$ 是本次比较的基准，不是一个随企业当前选择任意上调的奖励额度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With technology and input choices denoted by $z$, emissions can be written as $e=g(q,z)$. For fixed baseline emissions $e_0$, abatement is $a=e_0-e$. Lower output, cleaner inputs, production changes or end-of-pipe treatment can all reduce emissions. The baseline is a stated comparator, not an allowance that the firm may arbitrarily increase.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最简单的课堂模型假定排放强度固定：$e=\alpha q$。若每单位产品排放 2 单位污染，产量从 10 降到 8，则排放从 20 降到 16，减排是 4 而不是 2。若安装设备后仍生产 10、但强度降为 1，则排放降到 10，减排 10，产量没有变。两种减排方式不能被一个“产量下降”箭头同时代表。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The simplest classroom model fixes emissions intensity: $e=\alpha q$. At two emission units per product, reducing output from ten to eight lowers emissions from twenty to sixteen: abatement is four, not two. Keeping output at ten while equipment lowers intensity to one instead cuts emissions to ten. An output-reduction arrow cannot represent both mechanisms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

看图时先辨认横轴：若是排放，向右是多污染；若是减排，向右是少污染。每单位排放的[[边际外部损害]]也不能不经换算就当成每单位产出的损害。这一区分决定了[[产量税与排放税|税基能否覆盖全部调整渠道]]，也决定[[按量减排补贴|补贴的支付基准]]是否明确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Read the horizontal axis first: moving right means more pollution on an emissions axis but less pollution on an abatement axis. [[边际外部损害|Marginal damage]] per emission unit is not automatically damage per output unit. The distinction determines [[产量税与排放税|which adjustment margins a tax base reaches]] and whether an [[按量减排补贴|abatement subsidy]] has a clear payment baseline.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
