# Cuts in undirected graphs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*无向图中的割*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **cut** divides all vertices of a graph into two nonempty sides. An edge crosses the cut when its endpoints lie on opposite sides. Think of putting every location into either an inside group or an outside group, then identifying the connections between the groups. Drawing a line on a picture is only an aid: the partition of vertices determines the cut, regardless of where edges happen to be drawn.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**割**把图中的全部顶点分成两个非空部分。一条边的两个端点位于不同部分时，它就跨越这个割。可以想象把每个地点归入“内部”或“外部”，再找出两组之间的连接。图上画一条分界线只是辅助；真正确定割的是顶点划分，而不是边在图中画在哪里。

> [!example] Which edges cross? · 哪些边跨割？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the triangle on vertices $a,b,c$, take one side to be $S=\{a\}$ and the other to be $\{b,c\}$. The edges $ab$ and $ac$ cross the cut; $bc$ does not, because both its endpoints are outside $S$. Here $ab$ denotes the undirected connection between $a$ and $b$. The vertex set $\{a\}$ and the crossing-edge set $\{ab,ac\}$ are different kinds of objects.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在顶点为 $a,b,c$ 的三角形中，取一边为 $S=\{a\}$，另一边为 $\{b,c\}$。边 $ab,ac$ 跨割，$bc$ 不跨割，因为它的两个端点都在 $S$ 外。这里 $ab$ 表示 $a,b$ 之间的无向连接。顶点集合 $\{a\}$ 与跨割边集合 $\{ab,ac\}$ 是两种不同的对象。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The two sides and the crossing-edge set
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两侧顶点与跨割边集*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite [[图类型的建模边界|undirected graph]]. Choose a nonempty proper subset $S$ of $V$, written $\varnothing\ne S\subsetneq V$. Its complement $V\setminus S$ consists of every vertex not in $S$. The two sides are disjoint, together contain all vertices, and are both nonempty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $G=(V,E)$ 是有限[[图类型的建模边界|无向图]]。取 $V$ 的一个非空真子集 $S$，记作 $\varnothing\ne S\subsetneq V$。补集 $V\setminus S$ 包含所有不在 $S$ 中的顶点。两侧互不重叠，合起来是全部顶点，而且都非空。

> [!definition] Cut and crossing edges · 割与跨割边
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The partition into $S$ and $V\setminus S$ is a **cut**. Its **crossing-edge set** is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 把顶点分为 $S$ 与 $V\setminus S$ 的划分称为一个**割**。其**跨割边集**为
>
> $$\delta(S)=\{e\in E:e\text{ has exactly one endpoint in }S\}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Thus $S$ is a vertex set, while $\delta(S)$ is an edge set. Exchanging the two sides gives the same undirected cut and $\delta(S)=\delta(V\setminus S)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因此，$S$ 是顶点集，$\delta(S)$ 是边集。交换两侧，仍是同一个无向割，且 $\delta(S)=\delta(V\setminus S)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Some texts use “cut” for the crossing-edge set itself; this card distinguishes the partition from its set $\delta(S)$. If parallel edges are allowed, each edge retains its identity, so two different connections between the same opposite-side endpoints both belong to $\delta(S)$. A self-loop never crosses: both endpoints are the same vertex. Arbitrary reference arrows assigned to undirected edges for matrix calculations do not change this membership rule. Directed cuts, which can distinguish entering from leaving edges, require their own directional convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
有些教材也把跨割边集本身称作“割”；本卡区分顶点划分与它对应的集合 $\delta(S)$。若允许平行边，每条边仍有独立身份：连接相同两侧端点的两条不同边，都属于 $\delta(S)$。自环不会跨割，因为两个端点是同一顶点。为矩阵计算而给无向边任意添加的参考箭头，不改变上述归属规则；区分流入边与流出边的有向割，则需要另行约定方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A cut need not divide the graph into two connected pieces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两侧不必各自连通*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take the four-cycle $a-b-c-d-a$ and choose $S=\{a,c\}$. All four edges cross. Within either side there are no edges, so neither side is connected, but the partition is still a valid cut. In general, selecting the sides does not impose any connectivity condition inside them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
取四环 $a-b-c-d-a$，令 $S=\{a,c\}$，则四条边全部跨割。两侧内部都没有边，因此两侧各自都不连通，但这个划分仍然是合法的割。一般而言，选定两侧并没有要求它们内部连通。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the original graph is [[无向图连通分量|connected]], every such cut has at least one crossing edge. Choose a vertex on each side and a path between them. At the first step when that path leaves $S$, its edge belongs to $\delta(S)$. Without connectedness, the crossing set can be empty: a graph consisting of the separate edges $ab$ and $cd$ has $\delta(\{a,b\})=\varnothing$, although both sides are nonempty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
如果原图[[无向图连通分量|连通]]，每个这样的割都至少有一条跨割边。两侧各选一个顶点，取连接它们的路径；路径第一次离开 $S$ 的那一步，使用的边就属于 $\delta(S)$。若不要求原图连通，跨割边集可以为空：图只有互不相连的两条边 $ab,cd$ 时，$\delta(\{a,b\})=\varnothing$，但两侧仍都非空。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Deleting every edge in $\delta(S)$ leaves no path from one side to the other, because any such path would need a first crossing edge. It need not leave exactly two connected components: deleting all four crossing edges in the four-cycle example leaves four isolated vertices. Also, a graph with zero or one vertex admits no nontrivial cut. If a text permits $S=\varnothing$ or $S=V$, these are called trivial cuts; their crossing sets are empty. Here “cut” means a nontrivial cut unless explicitly stated otherwise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
删去 $\delta(S)$ 中全部边后，两侧之间不再有路径，因为任何跨侧路径都必须经过第一条跨割边。但删除后不一定恰有两个连通分量：在四环例子中删去全部四条跨割边，就剩四个孤立顶点。零顶点或单顶点图没有非平凡割。有些教材允许 $S=\varnothing$ 或 $S=V$，称作平凡割，它们的跨割边集为空；本卡未另行说明时，“割”指非平凡割。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Compare weights only after identifying the crossing edges
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先找跨割边，再比较权重*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Weights are not needed to define a cut. If each edge has a finite real weight $w(e)$ and $\delta(S)\ne\varnothing$, a **light edge of this cut** is an edge $e\in\delta(S)$ satisfying $w(e)\le w(f)$ for every $f\in\delta(S)$. A minimum exists because this edge set is finite and nonempty; ties are allowed. An empty crossing set has no light edge.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
定义割不需要权重。若每条边有有限实权 $w(e)$，且 $\delta(S)\ne\varnothing$，则这个割的**最轻边**是满足 $e\in\delta(S)$，并且对每条 $f\in\delta(S)$ 都有 $w(e)\le w(f)$ 的边。跨割边有限且非空，所以存在最小权重，允许并列。空的跨割边集没有最轻边。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the triangle above, assign $w(ab)=2$, $w(ac)=5$, and $w(bc)=-1$. For $S=\{a\}$ the light edge is $ab$, even though $bc$ is cheaper: $bc$ is not a crossing edge and is not a candidate. The [[MST 割性质|MST cut property]] is a further theorem explaining when such a light edge can be included without losing global minimum weight. It is not part of the definition of a cut.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
在前面的三角形中，令 $w(ab)=2$、$w(ac)=5$、$w(bc)=-1$。对 $S=\{a\}$，最轻跨割边是 $ab$，虽然 $bc$ 更便宜，但它不跨割，不是候选。[[MST 割性质]]是进一步的定理，解释何时选择这样的最轻边仍能保留全局最小权重；它不是割的定义的一部分。
