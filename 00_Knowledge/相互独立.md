# Mutual independence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*相互独立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Mutual independence** extends independence to a whole collection. It requires the probability product rule to work whenever we select finitely many members of that collection, whether we select two, three, or more. This is what lets us multiply several probabilities to calculate the chance that all the selected events happen together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**相互独立**把独立性的要求扩展到一整组成员：无论从中选出两个、三个，还是更多有限个成员，概率的乘法规则都必须成立。正是这个条件，让我们能把多个概率相乘，求这些选定事件同时发生的概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For two events, [[事件独立|independence]] means $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)$, where $\cap$ means “both occur”. With three events, we need to check every pair and the triple. Checking only the triple leaves the pair conditions untested; checking only the pairs leaves the triple condition untested.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个事件的[[事件独立|独立性]]表示 $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)$，其中 $\cap$ 表示“同时发生”。有三个事件时，既要检查每一对，也要检查三个一起。只查三重交集会漏掉逐对条件，只查各对则会漏掉三重条件。

## 最小例子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three coin tosses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] Three events require four checks · 三个事件的四项检查
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider a model in which all eight strings of three coin results, from $HHH$ to $TTT$, have probability $1/8$. Here $H$ means heads and $T$ tails. Let $A_i$ mean “toss $i$ is heads”, so each $A_i$ has probability $1/2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑一个模型：从 $HHH$ 到 $TTT$，三次硬币结果的八种组合各有 $1/8$ 的概率。$H$ 表示正面，$T$ 表示反面。令 $A_i$ 表示“第 $i$ 次为正面”，那么每个 $A_i$ 的概率都是 $1/2$。
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> P(A_1\cap A_2)&=\tfrac14=P(A_1)P(A_2),\\
> P(A_1\cap A_3)&=\tfrac14=P(A_1)P(A_3),\\
> P(A_2\cap A_3)&=\tfrac14=P(A_2)P(A_3),\\
> P(A_1\cap A_2\cap A_3)&=\tfrac18=P(A_1)P(A_2)P(A_3).
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For any chosen pair, two of the eight strings have heads in both selected positions, giving $2/8=1/4$. For all three positions, only $HHH$ qualifies, giving $1/8$. All four conditions hold, so these three events are mutually independent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 任取两个位置，八种组合里都有两种在这两个位置上同时为正面，所以概率为 $2/8=1/4$。要求三个位置全为正面时，只有 $HHH$ 符合，所以概率为 $1/8$。四项条件全部成立，因此三个事件相互独立。

## Any finite selection
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*把“每一种选法”写成定义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use $I$ as the collection of labels for the events. For example, $I=\{1,2,3\}$ labels the three events above. A set $J\subseteq I$ tells us which labels we select; $J=\{1,3\}$ selects just $A_1$ and $A_3$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $I$ 收集事件的编号。例如，上面的三个事件对应 $I=\{1,2,3\}$。其中的子集 $J\subseteq I$ 指定我们选哪些编号；$J=\{1,3\}$ 就是只选 $A_1$ 和 $A_3$。

> [!abstract] Definition for events · 事件族的定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The events $\{A_i\}_{i\in I}$ are mutually independent if, for every finite $J\subseteq I$ containing at least two labels,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 事件族 $\{A_i\}_{i\in I}$ 相互独立，是指对每个至少包含两个编号的有限子集 $J\subseteq I$，都有
>
> $$P\!\left(\bigcap_{j\in J}A_j\right)=\prod_{j\in J}P(A_j).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The large intersection $\bigcap$ means that all selected events occur; $\prod$ means to multiply their probabilities. The same rule must hold for every selection, including non-adjacent labels.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 大写的交集符号 $\bigcap$ 表示所有选中的事件同时发生；$\prod$ 表示把它们的概率相乘。每一种选法都必须成立，也包括不相邻的编号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original family may be infinite. Its independence is still defined by every **finite** selection; the definition does not ask us to begin with an undefined infinite product. For a finite family of four events, for instance, we check all six pairs, all four triples, and the group of four.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原来的事件族可以有无限多个成员，它的独立性仍由每一种**有限**选法来定义，不需要先写一个尚未定义的无限乘积。对于只有四个事件的族，则需要检查六对、四组三个，以及四个一起。

## A collection of random variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*随机变量族的版本*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For [[随机变量|random variables]], each selected member can answer a numerical question: for example, “Is $X_1\leq2$?” or “Is $X_3$ in this interval?” [[随机变量独立|Independence of two variables]] requires all such pairs of events to factorise. Mutual independence extends that requirement to every finite selection of variables and every choice of their numerical events.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于[[随机变量]]，每个选中的成员都可以对应一个数值问题，例如“$X_1\leq2$ 吗”或“$X_3$ 落在这个区间里吗”。[[随机变量独立|两个变量独立]]要求所有这样的事件对都能作乘积分解；相互独立把这个要求扩展到每一种有限变量选择及其所有数值事件选择。

$$
P\!\left(\bigcap_{j\in J}\{X_j\in B_j\}\right)
=\prod_{j\in J}P(X_j\in B_j).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here all variables are on the same probability space, $J$ is any finite set of distinct labels, and each $B_j$ is any Borel set of real numbers, such as a point or interval. Choosing only two labels gives [[两两独立|pairwise independence]]. The converse fails: pairwise checks can miss dependence involving three variables together, as the [[两两独立不推出相互独立|pairwise-but-not-mutual counterexample]] shows. Mutual independence also does not require the variables to have the same distribution; that additional requirement appears in [[独立同分布|i.i.d.]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里所有变量定义在同一概率空间上，$J$ 是任意有限个不同编号组成的集合，每个 $B_j$ 是任意 Borel 实数集合，例如单点或区间。只选两个编号，就得到[[两两独立]]的要求；反过来不成立，因为逐对检查可能漏掉三个变量共同参与的依赖，具体见[[两两独立不推出相互独立|两两独立但不相互独立的反例]]。相互独立也不要求变量有相同分布；[[独立同分布|i.i.d.]] 另外加入了这个条件。
