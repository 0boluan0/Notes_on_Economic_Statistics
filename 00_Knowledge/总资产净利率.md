# 总资产净利率是期间净利润与匹配总资产存量的比率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net-income return on assets is period net income divided by a matched total-asset stock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总资产净利率衡量每一元总资产占用对应多少净利润。以 $A^*>0$ 表示明确选定的资产存量口径，公式为 $ROA_{NI}=NI/A^*$；资产为零时不能计算。评估全年表现时常用平均资产；CPA 本章例题使用期末资产，ABC 的 $136/2000=6.8\%$ 就属于期末口径，不能悄悄换成平均资产后仍沿用该结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This ratio measures net income per unit of total assets. With $A^*>0$ denoting the explicitly chosen stock basis, $ROA_{NI}=NI/A^*$; zero assets make it undefined. Annual analysis often uses average assets. CPA's chapter example instead uses ending assets, giving ABC $136/2000=6.8\%$; changing to average assets changes the result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在收入 $S\ne0$ 且口径一致时，$NI/A^*=(NI/S)(S/A^*)$：[[营业净利率]]说明销售留下多少利润，[[总资产周转率]]说明资产支持多少销售。两者一起看，才不会把薄利快销与高利润低周转机械排名。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For nonzero revenue and consistent measurement, $NI/A^*=(NI/S)(S/A^*)$. [[营业净利率|Net margin]] captures profit per sale, while [[总资产周转率|asset turnover]] captures sales per unit of assets. Read them together rather than mechanically ranking low-margin/high-turnover and high-margin/low-turnover businesses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这只是 ROA 的一种定义。净利润已经扣除融资费用，而总资产由债权人和股东等共同支持，所以它不是融资前全部资本的经营回报率。若另一个来源以 EBIT 或税后经营利润作分子，应保留其明确名称与公式，不能因为同写 ROA 就直接比较；分离经营和融资可转入[[净经营资产净利率]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is one ROA convention. Net income is after financing costs, whereas assets are supported by creditors and shareholders; the ratio is not a pre-financing operating return on all capital. A source using EBIT or after-tax operating profit has a different measure even if it also calls it ROA. To separate operating performance from financing, use [[净经营资产净利率|return on net operating assets]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
