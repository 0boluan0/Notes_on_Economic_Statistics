# 久期价格近似用当前修正久期估计小幅收益率变化的一阶价格影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Duration pricing uses current modified duration to estimate the first-order effect of a small yield change*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在现金流及估值日固定时，把全价 $P(y)$ 在当前收益率附近作[[线性近似]]。$\Delta y$ 必须与修正久期求导使用同一个收益率坐标；年名义收益率以小数输入，例如 $50$ bp 是 $0.005$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With cash flows and valuation date fixed, take the [[线性近似|linear approximation]] to full price $P(y)$ around the current yield. The change $\Delta y$ must use the same coordinate as modified duration; 50 bp in decimal nominal annual yield is $0.005$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$P(y+\Delta y)\approx P(y)+P'(y)\Delta y=P(y)\bigl(1-D_{\mathrm{mod}}\Delta y\bigr),$$
$$\frac{\Delta P}{P}\approx-D_{\mathrm{mod}}\Delta y.$$

三年期、面值 $100$、年息票 $8$ 的债券，在年复利 $y=5\%$ 时 $P=108.169744$、$D_{\mathrm{mod}}=2.659091$。升至 $5.5\%$ 时，线性估计为 $106.731578$，直接重估为 $106.744833$；两者接近但不相等。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A three-year bond with face value 100 and annual coupons of 8 has $P=108.169744$ and $D_{\mathrm{mod}}=2.659091$ at an annually compounded 5% yield. At 5.5%, the linear estimate is 106.731578 versus an exact repricing of 106.744833: close, but not equal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对固定非负未来现金流，价格函数为凸函数，切线低于真实价格曲线，因此这一阶估计会低估同一收益率降幅带来的价格上涨、或高估同一收益率升幅带来的价格下跌。冲击较大、现金流或曲线因素改变时，应重新估值；“久期是精确导数”不能消除有限变动的误差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For fixed nonnegative future payments, the price function is convex and lies above its tangent. The linear estimate therefore understates a gain from a yield decrease or overstates a loss from a yield increase. Reprice when the shock is large or cash flows or curve factors change; an exact duration derivative does not eliminate finite-change error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
