# 残差图能提示设定失配的方向却不能证明模型正确或给出唯一修复
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Residual plots can indicate the direction of misspecification but cannot prove correctness or identify a unique repair*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子诊断
> 应把残差分别对拟合值、焦点解释变量、观测顺序和可信的候选遗漏变量作图。不同图暴露不同失配方向；一张“看起来随机”的图只说明该投影没有显示明显模式，不是模型正确性的证明。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Plot residuals against fitted values, focal regressors, observation order, and credible omitted candidates. Different plots expose different failure directions. A plot that appears random means only that this projection shows no obvious pattern; it does not prove the model correct.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 可见模式 | 首先提示 | 单独不能证明 |
|---|---|---|
| U 形或系统曲线 | 均值函数漏掉非线性项或变换 | 唯一正确函数形式 |
| 扇形或扩散程度改变 | 异方差或响应尺度问题 | 系数已经有偏 |
| 随时间成段同号 | 趋势、动态遗漏或序列相关 | 唯一滞后结构 |
| 单个大纵向残差 | 可能的 outcome outlier | 影响力；还需结合 leverage 与删点敏感性 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Visible pattern | First suggestion | What it cannot prove alone |
|---|---|---|
| U-shape or systematic curve | Missing nonlinearity or transformation in the mean | The unique correct functional form |
| Funnel or changing spread | Heteroskedasticity or a response-scale problem | That coefficients are already biased |
| Long same-sign runs over order | Omitted trend or dynamics, or serial dependence | A unique lag structure |
| One large vertical residual | A possible outcome outlier | Influence, which also depends on leverage and deletion sensitivity |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

OLS 已经令残差与实际放入设计矩阵的列样本正交，所以简单检查其与原始已含列的线性相关不能验证总体外生性。诊断应寻找未被当前列空间吸收的结构，并结合研究机制、数据质量和独立验证提出少量可解释的备择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
OLS already makes residuals sample-orthogonal to the columns actually included in the design matrix, so their simple linear correlation with included columns cannot validate population exogeneity. Diagnostics should seek structure outside the current column space and combine it with mechanism, data quality, and independent validation to form a small set of interpretable alternatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆提示
> residual-versus-fitted 图没有明显模式，是否证明没有遗漏混杂？
>
> **答案：** 不证明。该图主要寻找当前拟合尺度上的可见均值或方差结构；遗漏混杂、测量误差、选择机制、局部设定错误或样本外不稳定仍可能存在。
