# Limits of residual plots
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*残差图的解释边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A residual plot shows **where a fitted model misses the observed outcomes**. If the outcome is $y_i$ and the model's fitted value is $\hat y_i$, the residual is $e_i=y_i-\hat y_i$: positive means the model predicted too little, negative means it predicted too much. A plot places these discrepancies against another quantity, such as a predictor, fitted value, or time. Its horizontal axis determines which kind of remaining structure you can see.

The plot helps generate and check explanations for poor fit. It cannot, on its own, establish that a model is correct or identify the unique cause of a pattern. First distinguish the goal: [[设定问题分层|approximating an average response, describing error variation, and identifying a causal effect]] require different evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

残差图展示**拟合的模型在哪些地方没能贴合实际观测**。实际结果为 $y_i$，拟合值为 $\hat y_i$，则残差 $e_i=y_i-\hat y_i$：正值表示预测偏低，负值表示预测偏高。把这些差值对解释变量、拟合值或时间作图，才能看到相应方向上还剩什么结构。横轴的选择决定了这张图能暴露哪类问题。

它能帮助提出和检查失配原因，却不能单独证明模型正确，也不能唯一确定图形背后的机制。先区分目标：[[设定问题分层|描述平均关系、刻画误差变化和识别因果效应]]需要不同证据。

## One view can hide another pattern
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个观察方向可能遮住另一个方向的结构*

> [!example] Four observations / 四条观测
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose two measured predictors $x$ and $w$ each take values $-1$ and $1$. The four outcomes below follow $y=2x+3w$. Fit [[普通最小二乘|ordinary least squares]], which minimizes the sum of squared residuals, using only an intercept and $x$; the fitted line is $\hat y=2x$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 两个已测量的解释变量 $x,w$ 都取 $-1$ 和 $1$，以下四条数据满足 $y=2x+3w$。只用截距和 $x$ 做[[普通最小二乘]]，即让残差平方和最小，得到 $\hat y=2x$。
>
> | $x$ | $w$ | $y$ | $\hat y$ | $e=y-\hat y$ |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | $-1$ | $-1$ | $-5$ | $-2$ | $-3$ |
> | $-1$ | $1$ | $1$ | $-2$ | $3$ |
> | $1$ | $-1$ | $-1$ | $2$ | $-3$ |
> | $1$ | $1$ | $5$ | $2$ | $3$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Against fitted values, both groups have residuals $-3,3$: neither the group average nor the spread changes. Against $w$, however, the residual is exactly $3w$. If $w$ will be known when predicting, the second plot reveals information that the first view hides.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对拟合值作图时，两组的残差都是 $-3,3$，组均值和离散程度都没变化。对 $w$ 作图时，却清楚出现 $e=3w$。如果预测时能够获得 $w$，第二张图就暴露了第一张图没展示的信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is an arithmetic illustration, not a four-point test of model adequacy. It also does **not** show that omitting $w$ biases the coefficient on $x$. If all four combinations are equally likely in a population, the [[条件期望|average of $Y$ at a fixed $x$]] is $E(Y\mid x)=2x$, whereas $E(Y\mid x,w)=2x+3w$. A model can describe the first target exactly while leaving useful information for the second target unused.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这是算术示例，不是用四个点就能完成的模型检验。它也**没有**说明遗漏 $w$ 一定让 $x$ 的系数有偏：若总体中四种组合等可能，[[条件期望|固定 $x$ 后的平均结果]]是 $E(Y\mid x)=2x$，而 $E(Y\mid x,w)=2x+3w$。模型可以准确描述第一个目标，同时没有利用第二个目标所需的信息。

## Read a pattern as a clue
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把图形读成线索*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose plots that answer plausible questions about the data. A curve in residuals against a predictor suggests a missing shape in the mean; a fan suggests changing spread, but mean misspecification and fitting effects also need checking. Runs of positive and negative residuals over time can reflect drift, omitted dynamics, or dependent errors. If a predictor itself changes systematically over time, a time plot alone may not separate those explanations. A large vertical residual identifies a poorly fitted outcome; [[异常杠杆影响区分|leverage and influence]] ask different questions about predictor location and how much the fitted result changes.

For scale and time-dependence checks, [[残差协方差诊断|residual covariance diagnostics]] explains why fitting already changes residual variances and correlations. Look at the size and scientific importance of a pattern, not just its visual presence. Sparse data can hide departures, and searching many plots can produce chance patterns. A diagnostic clue calls for an interpretable candidate and further evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选图时要先有一个针对数据的合理问题。对解释变量的残差曲线提示均值关系可能漏了某种形状；扇形提示离散程度变化，但也要排查均值失配和拟合本身的作用。随时间连续同号可能来自漂移、遗漏的动态关系或误差依赖；若解释变量也随时间系统变化，单靠时间图未必能区分这些解释。一个很大的纵向残差表示该结果没拟合好；[[异常杠杆影响区分|杠杆与影响]]分别关心解释变量位置，以及该观测能多大程度改变拟合结果。

要进一步检查尺度与时间依赖，可读[[残差协方差诊断]]，理解拟合本身为什么已经改变了残差的方差与相关。除了有没有形状，还要看大小和实际意义。数据稀疏会掩盖偏离，反复搜索许多图也会遇到偶然形状；线索需要转化为可解释的候选模型，再找证据比较。

> [!important] Zero correlation is partly built in / 零相关可能是拟合强制的
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With an intercept, ordinary least squares forces $\sum_i e_i=0$ and $\sum_i x_{ij}e_i=0$ for every included predictor column $j$. These [[OLS正规方程|normal equations]] hold on the fitting sample regardless of whether the model describes the population correctly. When the relevant sample variances are positive, the corresponding sample correlations are therefore zero. A zero linear trend does not rule out curvature, and [[样本正交与总体外生性|sample orthogonality does not establish population exogeneity]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 含截距的普通最小二乘会强制 $\sum_i e_i=0$，并对每个已放入模型的解释变量列 $j$ 强制 $\sum_i x_{ij}e_i=0$。这些[[OLS正规方程]]在拟合样本上成立，与模型是否正确描述总体无关。相关样本方差为正时，对应的样本相关系数因此为零。没有线性趋势不代表没有弯曲；[[样本正交与总体外生性|样本正交也不能证明总体外生性]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A useful conclusion names both the view and the limit: “No clear curvature appeared against fitted values in this sample.” It is stronger than the evidence to conclude “There are no omitted confounders.” Follow the clue through [[设定修复循环|a traceable model revision]], then examine the revised model again.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有用的结论应说清观察方向和边界，例如“这个样本的残差对拟合值图没有明显曲率”。据此断言“没有遗漏混杂”就超出了证据。沿着线索进入[[设定修复循环]]，修改以后再检查新模型。
