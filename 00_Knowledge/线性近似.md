# Linear approximation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*线性近似*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **linear approximation** replaces a function near one chosen input by its tangent line. It starts with a value we know, then adds “local rate of change times the input change”. The purpose is to estimate nearby values with simple arithmetic while keeping track of the fact that the tangent is only a local model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**线性近似**在选定输入附近，用函数的切线代替原函数。从一个已知函数值出发，加上“局部变化率乘以输入变化”，就得到附近的估计值。它让我们能用简单计算估计邻近值，同时记住切线只是局部模型。

> [!example] Estimate a nearby square root · 估计附近的平方根
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To estimate $\sqrt{4.1}$, start at $a=4$, where $f(a)=2$ is easy to calculate. For $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$, the [[导数|derivative]] is $f'(x)=1/(2\sqrt{x})$, so the local rate at $4$ is $1/4$. The input increment is $h=4.1-4=0.1$. Thus
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 为了估计 $\sqrt{4.1}$，选基点 $a=4$，这里的 $f(a)=2$ 很容易计算。对 $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$，[[导数]]为 $f'(x)=1/(2\sqrt{x})$，所以在 $4$ 处的局部变化率是 $1/4$。输入增量为 $h=4.1-4=0.1$，于是
>
> $$\sqrt{4.1}\approx 2+\frac14(0.1)=2.025.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The estimated **change** is $0.025$; the estimated **function value** is $2.025$. The actual value is about $2.024845673$, so this estimate is high by about $0.000154327$. Below we also obtain an exact bound without relying on that decimal comparison.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 估计的**变化量**是 $0.025$，估计的**函数值**是 $2.025$。真值约为 $2.024845673$，因此这次估计偏高约 $0.000154327$。下面还会给出不依靠小数比较的精确误差界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Keep the base point in the formula
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*公式中必须保留基点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f$ be a real-valued function defined on an open interval around $a$, and assume it is differentiable at $a$. The number $a$ is the **base point**, $x$ is a nearby input, and $h=x-a$ is its signed increment. A negative $h$ moves to the left. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设实值函数 $f$ 定义在 $a$ 周围的一个开区间上，并在 $a$ 可导。$a$ 是**基点**，$x$ 是附近的输入，$h=x-a$ 是带符号的增量；$h<0$ 表示向左移动。定义

$$L_a(x)=f(a)+f'(a)(x-a),\qquad L_a(a+h)=f(a)+f'(a)h.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the unique affine function matching both the value and slope at $a$: $L_a(a)=f(a)$ and $L_a'(a)=f'(a)$. “Affine” means a constant plus a constant multiple of the input; the line need not pass through the origin. The subscript $a$ reminds us which tangent line we selected. A different base point generally gives a different approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它是在 $a$ 同时匹配函数值与斜率的唯一仿射函数：$L_a(a)=f(a)$、$L_a'(a)=f'(a)$。“仿射”表示一个常数加上输入的常数倍，这条直线不必经过原点。下标 $a$ 提醒我们选的是哪一条切线；换一个基点，通常就会得到另一个近似式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The adjective “linear” describes the degree of this local model, not the original function. A constant model is allowed when $f'(a)=0$. For $f(x)=x^2$ at $a=0$, the linear approximation is simply $0$; it records zero first-order change, although the function itself does change quadratically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“线性”描述局部模型的次数，不是说原函数必须线性。若 $f'(a)=0$，模型可以是常数。例如 $f(x)=x^2$ 在 $a=0$ 的线性近似就是 $0$；它记录一阶变化为零，但原函数仍有二次变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the approximation sign actually guarantees
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*近似号究竟保证了什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the signed remainder, or approximation error, as $R_1(h)=f(a+h)-L_a(a+h)$. The [[导数|definition of the derivative]] says that the difference quotient approaches $f'(a)$. Subtracting that limit gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把带符号的余项，也就是近似误差，记为 $R_1(h)=f(a+h)-L_a(a+h)$。[[导数]]的定义说明：差商趋于 $f'(a)$。从差商中减去这个极限，得到

$$\frac{R_1(h)}h
=\frac{f(a+h)-f(a)}h-f'(a)\longrightarrow0
\qquad(h\to0,\ h\ne0).$$

> [!important] Error vanishes at the input-increment scale · 在输入增量尺度下误差消失
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This is written $R_1(h)=o(h)$, read “little-o of $h$”. Equivalently, $|R_1(h)|/|h|\to0$, so
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这记作 $R_1(h)=o(h)$，读作“$h$ 的小 o”。等价地，$|R_1(h)|/|h|\to0$，因此
>
> $$f(a+h)=f(a)+f'(a)h+o(h).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every $\varepsilon>0$, all sufficiently small nonzero $h$ satisfy $|R_1(h)|<\varepsilon|h|$. The threshold depends on the function, base point and requested tolerance. This compares error with the input increment, not with the output value $f(a+h)$; it is not automatically a relative-error bound for that output.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对每个 $\varepsilon>0$，所有足够小的非零 $h$ 都满足 $|R_1(h)|<\varepsilon|h|$。多小才够，取决于函数、基点与要求的容差。这里是把误差与输入增量比较，不是与输出值 $f(a+h)$ 比较，所以并不自动给出输出值的相对误差界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivative itself is enough for this local conclusion; a second derivative is not required. In the square example at $a=0$, the error is $h^2$, and $h^2/|h|=|h|\to0$. By contrast, $|x|$ at zero has no derivative: its right slope is $1$ and its left slope is $-1$, so no single line matches both sides to first order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个局部结论只需要可导，不需要二阶导数。刚才平方函数在 $a=0$ 的例子中，误差是 $h^2$，而 $h^2/|h|=|h|\to0$。相比之下，$|x|$ 在零点不可导：右侧斜率是 $1$，左侧是 $-1$，没有一条直线能在一阶尺度上同时匹配两侧。

## A numerical error needs its own justification
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*指定步长的误差需要另行核对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening square-root example, algebra gives more than an asymptotic statement. For $h>-4$, rationalizing the difference yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头的平方根例子，可以用代数得到比渐近关系更具体的保证。对 $h>-4$，将差值有理化，得到

$$\sqrt{4+h}-2=\frac{h}{\sqrt{4+h}+2},$$

$$R_1(h)=\sqrt{4+h}-2-\frac h4
=-\frac{h^2}{4(\sqrt{4+h}+2)^2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $h=0.1$, the denominator is at least $64$, so $|R_1(0.1)|\le0.01/64=0.00015625$. This is a verified finite-step error bound for this example. For other functions, [[导数界控制函数差|interval bounds on derivatives]] or a [[Taylor余项|Taylor remainder estimate under its additional assumptions]] can supply a bound. The symbol $o(h)$ alone does not tell us whether a particular step such as $0.1$ meets a numerical tolerance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $h=0.1$ 时，分母至少为 $64$，所以 $|R_1(0.1)|\le0.01/64=0.00015625$。这是针对该例、已经验证的有限步长误差界。其他函数可以使用[[导数界控制函数差|区间上的导数界]]，或者在满足额外条件时使用[[Taylor余项]]估计。单凭 $o(h)$，还不能知道 $0.1$ 这样的指定步长是否达到了某个数值精度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a base point that is both convenient and close to the target. For the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]], $\ln(1+h)\approx h$ is an expansion at $h=0$, with allowed inputs $h>-1$. At $h=100$, the estimate $100$ is far from $\ln101\approx4.61512$; the formula has not promised accuracy there. Domain validity and proximity are separate checks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选基点时，要同时考虑容易计算与靠近目标。对于[[自然对数]]，$\ln(1+h)\approx h$ 是以 $h=0$ 为中心的展开，合法输入要求 $h>-1$。在 $h=100$ 处，估计值 $100$ 远离 $\ln101\approx4.61512$；公式并没有承诺那里准确。定义域合法与足够靠近，是两件分别要检查的事。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When combining first-order approximations, retain the constant and linear contributions consistently. A quadratic term produced by multiplying two lines is not automatically the full [[二次近似|quadratic approximation]]: the omitted second-order terms of the original factors may contribute at that same order. The next card explains how the second derivative fixes the complete correction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

组合一阶近似时，要一致地保留常数项与一次项。两条线性式相乘后出现的二次项，不会自动成为完整的[[二次近似]]：原来各因子被省略的二阶项，也可能在相同阶数作出贡献。下一张卡会解释二阶导数如何确定完整修正。
