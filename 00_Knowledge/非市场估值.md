# 非市场估值为没有直接交易价格的效益或损害变化建立货币价值度量

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Non-market valuation assigns monetary measures to benefit or harm changes without direct transaction prices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非市场估值是在明确基准、变化、人群与时期后，为没有直接交易价格的福利影响建立货币度量。例如免费公园没有门票收入，不意味着改善环境的价值为零；估值所得也不表示政府真的收到这笔钱。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Non-market valuation measures welfare impacts without direct transaction prices after specifying the baseline, change, population and period. A free park can provide valuable improvements despite earning no admission revenue; its estimated value is not government cash receipts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以支付意愿衡量时，问题是“在这个收入与替代选择背景下，为这项变化最多愿意放弃多少货币”。可以从[[显示偏好估值|实际选择]]或[[条件估值|明确的假想调查]]取得证据；不同证据需要各自的识别与问卷条件。即使观察到相关市场价格，也要检查它是否可靠反映所评价的变化，不能用附近房价本身代替公园总价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A willingness-to-pay measure asks how much money someone would surrender for the specified change, given income and alternatives. Evidence may come from [[显示偏好估值|observed choices]] or [[条件估值|a defined hypothetical survey]], each with its own identification requirements. Related market prices must also be assessed; nearby house prices are not the park's total value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小核算：假设已可靠测得100户对同一年度改善各愿付20元，且对象不重叠，该年度货币收益为2000元。这里加总的是估计福利价值，不是门票销售；若把20元的一次性全项目支付意愿再逐年重复，就改变了原估值对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If 100 distinct households each reliably value the same one-year improvement at 20, its annual monetary benefit is 2,000. This aggregates estimated welfare value, not ticket sales. Repeating a one-off whole-project valuation every year would change what was originally valued.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
