# 策略—信念评估（assessment）是行为策略组合与每个信息集上的信念系统组成的有序对
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An assessment is an ordered pair consisting of a behavioral-strategy profile and a belief system at every information set.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> assessment 不是“对答案作评价”，而是动态博弈候选解的完整对象 $(\sigma,\mu)$：$\sigma$ 说明每个信息集发生时怎样行动，$\mu$ 说明行动者在该信息集内怎样相信此前历史。只给策略或只给 belief，都还没有完整描述行动与推断。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *What it is*
>
> In game theory, an assessment is not an evaluation of an answer. It is the complete candidate object $(\sigma,\mu)$ for a dynamic game: $\sigma$ specifies behavior whenever an information set is reached, while $\mu$ describes the decision-maker's beliefs about the preceding history within that information set. Strategies alone, or beliefs alone, do not fully describe both behavior and inference.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两个组成部分承担不同角色
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The two components play different roles.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在具有行为策略的[[扩展式博弈]]中，策略组合写成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an [[扩展式博弈|extensive-form game]] with behavioral strategies, the strategy profile is written
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\sigma=\bigl(\sigma_i(\cdot\mid I)\bigr)_{i,I\in\mathcal H_i},
$$

其中 $\sigma_i(\cdot\mid I)$ 是玩家 $i$ 在信息集 $I$ 上对可选行动的概率分布。[[信息集信念|信念系统]]写成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $\sigma_i(\cdot\mid I)$ is player $i$'s probability distribution over available actions at information set $I$. The [[信息集信念|belief system]] is written
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\mu=\bigl(\mu(\cdot\mid I)\bigr)_{I\in\mathcal H},
\qquad
\mu(\cdot\mid I)\in\Delta(I).
$$

二者组成 assessment
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Together they form the assessment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
(\sigma,\mu).
$$

$\sigma$ 回答“无论走到哪里，接下来怎么做”；$\mu$ 回答“如果到达这个无法区分历史的信息集，我认为自己位于哪个节点”。belief 不是玩家的行动随机化，策略也不是对过去历史的概率判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\sigma$ answers, “What will I do next wherever the game goes?” $\mu$ answers, “If this information set is reached and I cannot distinguish the preceding histories, which node do I believe I am at?” A belief is not the player's randomization over actions, and a strategy is not a probability assessment over past histories.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它为什么只是候选对象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why it is only a candidate object*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

assessment 只说明“策略是什么、belief 是什么”，不自动保证两者理性或一致。均衡是在这个对象上进一步施加条件；只提交策略组合也不足以形成 assessment。两层对象的区别见[[策略信念评估与均衡]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An assessment states what the strategies and beliefs are; it does not automatically make them rational or consistent. An equilibrium imposes additional conditions on this object, and a strategy profile alone is not an assessment. See [[策略信念评估与均衡|assessment versus equilibrium]] for the distinction between these two levels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] Beer–Quiche 中 assessment 的最小清单
> $\sigma$ 必须给出 weak 与 strong 各自选 Beer/Quiche 的分布，以及接收者在看到 Beer、看到 Quiche 后的行动分布；$\mu$ 必须给出两个观察后信息集中对 weak/strong 的概率。只有这整套对象才能继续检查 PBE。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Minimal assessment checklist for Beer–Quiche*
>
> $\sigma$ must specify the weak and strong types' distributions over Beer and Quiche, as well as the receiver's action distribution after observing Beer and after observing Quiche. $\mu$ must assign probabilities to weak and strong at each of the two resulting information sets. Only this complete object can then be tested for PBE.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个候选答案给出了每个玩家在每个信息集的行动，却没有写任何 posterior。它是否已经是 assessment？
>
> **答案：** 不是。它只有行为策略组合 $\sigma$，缺少每个信息集上的 belief system $\mu$；因此也还不能被判定为 PBE 或 sequential equilibrium。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> *Self-check*
>
> A candidate answer specifies every player's action at every information set but gives no posterior. Is it already an assessment?
>
> **Answer:** No. It contains only the behavioral-strategy profile $\sigma$ and lacks the belief system $\mu$ at every information set. It therefore cannot yet be evaluated as a PBE or sequential equilibrium.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
