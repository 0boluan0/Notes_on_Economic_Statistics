# 项目融资回答前期资金如何筹集而付费来源回答服务成本最终由谁承担
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Project financing explains how capital is raised, whereas payment sources explain who ultimately funds the service.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

项目融资讨论投资阶段的资金从哪里来，例如银行贷款、债券或股东投入；付费来源讨论项目日后靠什么收入支付运营支出、还本付息并向权益投资者提供回报，例如使用者收费、政府付款或二者组合。前者解决资金到位的时间和工具，后者回答账单最后由谁支付。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financing concerns capital raised through instruments such as loans, bonds and equity. Payment sources are revenues that cover operations, debt service and equity returns, such as user charges, government payments or a combination. Financing bridges the timing of investment; payment sources identify who pays the bills.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设建设投入为 100，项目公司借款 70、股东出资 30。这个安排使初始 100 到位，却没有创造用于偿债和运营的收入。若道路不向使用者收费，而政府按道路可用性付款，私人债权和股权就是融资来源，公共预算则是合同付款来源。是否能按约支付，还取决于绩效条件、现金流和相关风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose construction costs 100, financed by debt of 70 and equity of 30. This raises the initial 100 but does not create operating or repayment revenue. If a road has no user toll and government pays for availability, debt and equity provide financing while the public budget provides contractual payments. Actual payments depend on performance conditions, cash flows and risks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这也是 [[政府和社会资本合作|PPP]] 能引入私人资金，却不能让公共设施变成“免费资金”的原因。把付款推到以后，或把承诺放在预算外，并不会消除政府合同义务和风险；采用何种安排仍要比较全寿命期的 [[社会机会成本|资源成本]]，而不是只看建设当年少支出了多少现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This explains why [[政府和社会资本合作|PPPs]] can mobilize private capital without making infrastructure free. Deferred or off-budget commitments do not eliminate contractual public obligations or risk. Compare whole-life [[社会机会成本|resource costs]], not merely the reduction in cash paid during construction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，读到“由社会资本融资”时，下一问是：它靠谁付款收回投入，付款与什么绩效相联系，收入不足的风险由谁承担？最后一个问题转到 [[PPP风险分配]]，不能从“钱最初由谁拿出”直接推出答案。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When a project is privately financed, ask who supplies repayment revenues, which performance conditions govern payment, and who bears revenue shortfalls. The final question requires [[PPP风险分配|risk-allocation analysis]]; it is not answered by identifying the initial capital provider.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [World Bank PPP Reference Guide, Finance Structures for PPP](https://ppp.worldbank.org/finance-structures-ppp)，项目现金流、债务与股权、追索权部分：支持初始融资与运营收入、偿债和权益回报的区分；70 + 30 = 100 为自建资金构成例，不假定任何利率或回报保证。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Supports financing instruments and the role of project revenues in operations and capital payments. The funding split is an illustrative arithmetic example, not a return guarantee.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [World Bank PPP Reference Guide, PPP Contract Types and Terminology](https://ppp.worldbank.org/ppp-contract-types-and-terminology)，Payment Mechanism：支持使用者付费、政府付费、混合付费及可用性等绩效条件。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Supports user, government and mixed payment mechanisms and performance conditions such as availability.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [World Bank PPP Reference Guide, Infrastructure Challenges and How PPPs Can Help](https://ppp.worldbank.org/infrastructure-challenges-and-how-ppps-can-help)，PPP Pitfalls：支持延期付款及预算外承诺不能消除公共财政负担的边界。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Supports the warning that deferred and off-budget commitments do not remove public fiscal burdens.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
