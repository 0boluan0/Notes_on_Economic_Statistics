# 制度、地理与长期发展差距
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Institutions, Geography, and Long-Run Development Gaps*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释为什么有些地区长期能累积投资、技术与国家能力，而另一些地区持续面临低生产率和不稳定规则。
> **具体锚点：** 高收入国家往往拥有更可预期的产权与更高的国家能力，但这个相关性同时也可能由收入反向改善制度，或由历史共同塑造二者。
> **核心难点：** 制度、地理与历史相互作用；找到一个与收入相关的变量，并不等于找到单一终极原因。
> **为什么重要：** 它决定政策是只需补贴当期投资，还是需要改变激励、执行能力与权力约束。
> **继续：** 先用因果图区分制度机制、反向因果和共同原因；再到 [[发展政策的国家能力、公共投入与评价]] 看政策如何落地。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It explains why some places repeatedly accumulate investment, technology, and state capacity while others remain trapped in low productivity and unstable rules.
> **Concrete anchor:** High-income countries often have more predictable property rights and stronger state capacity, but the correlation may also arise because income improves institutions or because history jointly shaped both.
> **Central difficulty:** Institutions, geography, and history interact. Finding a variable correlated with income is not the same as discovering one ultimate cause.
> **Why it matters:** The answer determines whether policy should merely subsidize current investment or change incentives, implementation capacity, and constraints on power.
> **Continue:** Use a causal graph to separate institutional mechanisms, reverse causality, and common causes. Then see [[发展政策的国家能力、公共投入与评价|State Capacity, Public Inputs, and Evaluation in Development Policy]] for implementation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三类解释不能简化成排他竞赛
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Three explanations should not be reduced to a winner-takes-all contest*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

产权、国家能力、政治约束、疾病环境、市场可达性和殖民历史相互作用。跨国回归易受反向因果和遗漏，可信研究需历史设计、微观机制和多类证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Property rights, state capacity, political constraints, disease environments, market access, and colonial history interact. Cross-country regressions are vulnerable to reverse causality and omitted variables, so credible research needs historical designs, micro-level mechanisms, and multiple kinds of evidence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

地理可以直接影响疾病负担、运输成本和农业生产率；也可以通过历史定居和殖民策略影响制度。制度则会改变人们投资、创新、组织企业和纳税的回报。两条路径可以同时存在。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Geography can directly affect disease burden, transport cost, and agricultural productivity; it can also affect institutions through historical settlement and colonial strategies. Institutions in turn change returns to investment, innovation, firm organization, and tax compliance. Both paths can operate at once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 用因果图检查“制度导致收入”
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Use a causal graph to inspect the claim that institutions cause income*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将制度记为 $I$，收入记为 $Y$，历史或地理共同原因记为 $H$。想估计的路径是 $I\rightarrow Y$，但数据中可能还存在 $Y\rightarrow I$ 和 $H\rightarrow I, H\rightarrow Y$。仅观察 $I$ 与 $Y$ 同向变化，无法区分三条路径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let institutions be $I$, income be $Y$, and a common historical or geographic cause be $H$. The target path is $I\rightarrow Y$, but the data may also contain $Y\rightarrow I$ and $H\rightarrow I, H\rightarrow Y$. Observing $I$ and $Y$ move together does not distinguish these three paths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有说服力的证据链至少要回答：历史变异为何与今日收入没有其他直接通道？制度如何持续？具体哪个投资、人力资本或市场进入机制响应？结果是否对样本、指标和历史误差稳健？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A persuasive evidence chain should answer at least four questions. Why does the historical variation have no other direct route to present income? How did institutions persist? Which concrete investment, human-capital, or market-entry mechanism responded? Is the result robust to sample choice, measures, and historical measurement error?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个具体诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A concrete diagnostic example*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若土地登记改革后投资上升，先不要直接宣称“产权已造成发展”。检查改革是否同时到达原本就更富的地区，投资是长期固定投资还是短期土地投机，信贷获得或纠纷解决是否真正改变，以及改革成本由谁承担。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If investment rises after a land-registration reform, do not immediately conclude that “property rights caused development.” Check whether reform first reached places already richer, whether investment was long-lived productive capital or short-run land speculation, whether credit access or dispute resolution actually changed, and who bore the reform's costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把一个国家治理指数当成制度全部，忽略它可能本身受收入影响。
- 找到历史工具变量后，只检查与制度的相关性，不辩护排除性限制。
- 把一个历史样本的局部平均效应扩展成所有国家、所有制度的普遍系数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Treating one governance index as the whole of institutions while ignoring that income may itself affect the index.
- Finding a historical instrument, checking only its correlation with institutions, and never defending the exclusion restriction.
- Expanding a local average effect in one historical sample into a universal coefficient for every country and institution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 制度与收入相关为何不直接证明制度因果？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does correlation between institutions and income not directly prove an institutional causal effect?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 制度可能受收入反向影响，且地理、历史等共同因素同时塑造二者。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Income may improve institutions in reverse, while geography, history, and other common factors may shape both.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释，为什么地理和制度可能同时是机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why geography and institutions can both be mechanisms.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 地理可直接影响疾病、农业和运输，也可通过历史定居影响制度；制度又改变在给定地理下的投资和组织激励。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Geography can directly affect disease, agriculture, and transport and can shape institutions through settlement history. Institutions then change investment and organizational incentives under that geography.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 评估一项产权改革时，除投资额外还应跟踪哪类机制结果？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When evaluating a property-rights reform, what mechanism outcomes should be tracked beyond investment spending?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 可跟踪信贷获得、纠纷时长、被没收风险、投资期限、生产率与进入退出，并检查分配效果。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Track credit access, dispute duration, expropriation risk, investment horizon, productivity, entry and exit, and distributional effects.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
