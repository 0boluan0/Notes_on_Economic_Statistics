# Bounded trajectories in continuous time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*连续系统的全轨迹有界判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bounded continuous trajectory stays within some finite radius as time moves forward. It may settle, keep rotating, or first grow and then shrink. For a constant linear system, eigenvalue real parts control exponential growth, while the Jordan structure decides what happens exactly on the boundary between growth and decay.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续轨迹有界，是指随着时间向前推进，它始终留在某个有限半径内。它可以停在某处、不断旋转，也可以先增长再缩小。对常系数线性系统，特征值实部控制指数增长；恰好处在增长与衰减边界时，还须检查 Jordan 结构。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Rotation and drift have different outcomes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*旋转与漂移有不同结果*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the two real matrices below in $x'=Ax$, where the prime means a time [[导数|derivative]]. The first rotates the state; the second continually adds the constant second coordinate into the first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑下面两个实矩阵对应的系统 $x'=Ax$，撇号表示对时间求[[导数]]。第一个使状态旋转，第二个则不断把保持常量的第二坐标加进第一坐标。

$$R=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad e^{tR}=\begin{pmatrix}\cos t&-\sin t\\\sin t&\cos t\end{pmatrix},$$
$$B=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad e^{tB}=\begin{pmatrix}1&t\\0&1\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $R$, every initial vector keeps its [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]]. A nonzero trajectory keeps rotating and has no limit as $t\to\infty$. For $B$, starting at $x_0=(0,1)^T$ gives $x(t)=(t,1)^T$, which is unbounded; $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]] to write a column. Both matrices have only eigenvalues with real part zero. That fact alone cannot distinguish the two behaviors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $R$，每个初值都保持[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]，非零轨迹不断旋转，在 $t\to\infty$ 时没有极限。对 $B$，初值 $x_0=(0,1)^T$ 给出 $x(t)=(t,1)^T$，无界增长；$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把坐标写成列。两个矩阵的特征值实部都为零，仅凭这一点无法区分两种行为。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The criterion includes the imaginary-axis condition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*判据还包括虚轴上的条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a fixed real or complex $n\times n$ matrix, $n\ge1$, in the homogeneous equation $x'=Ax$. Time is restricted to $t\ge0$, and there is no external input. The [[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解|matrix-exponential solution]] is $x(t)=e^{tA}x_0$. For real or complex coordinates, use the standard [[内积|inner-product length]] $\|x\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_j|x_j|^2}$, where the bars mean modulus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为固定实或复 $n\times n$ 矩阵，$n\ge1$，齐次方程为 $x'=Ax$。只看 $t\ge0$，并且没有外部输入。[[常系数线性系统的矩阵指数解]]给出 $x(t)=e^{tA}x_0$。对实或复坐标，使用标准[[内积]]给出的长度 $\|x\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_j|x_j|^2}$，其中竖线表示模。

> [!important] Nonpositive real parts are only the first requirement · 非正实部只是第一项要求
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every initial state has a bounded trajectory for $t\ge0$ exactly when every complex eigenvalue satisfies $\operatorname{Re}\lambda\le0$ and each eigenvalue on the imaginary axis, $\operatorname{Re}\lambda=0$, is [[半单特征值|semisimple]]. This means all Jordan blocks for those boundary eigenvalues have size one. Repeated boundary eigenvalues are allowed if their eigenspace dimensions equal their algebraic multiplicities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个初值的轨迹在 $t\ge0$ 上都有界，当且仅当每个复特征值满足 $\operatorname{Re}\lambda\le0$，且虚轴上的特征值，即 $\operatorname{Re}\lambda=0$ 的特征值，都[[半单特征值|半单]]。这表示这些边界特征值的 Jordan 块全部为一阶。允许边界上出现重根，只要特征空间维数达到相应代数重数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $\lambda=a+ib$, $a=\operatorname{Re}\lambda$ is the real part, with real $a,b$ and $i^2=-1$. A scalar mode evolves as $e^{\lambda t}$ and has magnitude $e^{at}$. Negative $a$ means decay, positive $a$ means growth, and $a=0$ leaves constant magnitude. The eigenvalue zero is included on the imaginary axis: a size-one zero block gives a constant state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $\lambda=a+ib$，$a=\operatorname{Re}\lambda$ 是实部，$a,b$ 为实数且 $i^2=-1$。标量模式按 $e^{\lambda t}$ 演化，模为 $e^{at}$。$a<0$ 表示衰减，$a>0$ 表示增长，$a=0$ 则保持模不变。虚轴包括零特征值：一阶零块给出常量状态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Where the extra growth comes from
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*额外增长来自哪里*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over $\mathbb C$, [[Jordan标准形的域条件|Jordan form]] gives $A=SJS^{-1}$, hence $e^{tA}=Se^{tJ}S^{-1}$. A fixed invertible coordinate change preserves boundedness in both directions. The [[Jordan块的指数|exponential of a size-r Jordan block]] is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在复数域中，[[Jordan标准形的域条件]]保证 $A=SJS^{-1}$，从而 $e^{tA}=Se^{tJ}S^{-1}$。固定的可逆换坐标在两个方向都保持有界性。[[Jordan块的指数|一个大小为 r 的 Jordan 块的指数]]为

$$e^{tJ_r(\lambda)}=e^{\lambda t}\left(I_r+tN+\frac{t^2}{2!}N^2+\cdots+\frac{t^{r-1}}{(r-1)!}N^{r-1}\right).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $r$ is a positive integer, $I_r$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity]], $N$ has ones immediately above the diagonal and $N^r=0$, and $m!$ is the product of the positive integers up to $m$, with $0!=1$. If $r=1$, the parentheses contain only $I_1$. When the real part is negative, every fixed power of $t$ is eventually overcome by exponential decay; [[连续系统谱稳定性|the asymptotic-stability proof]] supplies an explicit bound. Thus even larger blocks strictly to the left of the axis are bounded.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $r$ 为正整数，$I_r$ 是[[单位矩阵]]，$N$ 仅在紧邻主对角线之上的位置放1，满足 $N^r=0$；$m!$ 是从1到 $m$ 的正整数乘积，约定 $0!=1$。当 $r=1$ 时，括号中只有 $I_1$。实部为负时，每个固定次数的 $t$ 的幂最终都被指数衰减压过，[[连续系统谱稳定性|渐近稳定的证明]]给出了具体上界。因此严格位于虚轴左侧的较大块仍然有界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the imaginary axis, $|e^{\lambda t}|=1$. A size-one block is bounded, but a block of size at least two has the entry $te^{\lambda t}$, whose magnitude is $t$. Its second coordinate basis vector therefore yields an unbounded trajectory. A positive real part already causes growth along an ordinary eigenvector. These facts prove necessity as well as sufficiency of the criterion. For real $A$, an unbounded complex trajectory also certifies an unbounded real one: its real and imaginary component trajectories cannot both be bounded.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在虚轴上，$|e^{\lambda t}|=1$。一阶块有界，但大小至少为二的块含有元素 $te^{\lambda t}$，其模为 $t$；该块的第二个坐标基向量因此产生无界轨迹。若实部为正，沿普通特征向量就已经增长。这些事实同时证明了判据的必要性和充分性。对实矩阵，一条无界复轨迹也保证存在无界实轨迹，因为它的实部与虚部轨迹不可能同时有界。

## Boundedness, stability and the direction of time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有界性、稳定性与时间方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bound for a trajectory may depend on $x_0$. In finite dimension, boundedness for every initial state is equivalent to a single multiplier $C$ satisfying $\|e^{tA}x_0\|_2\le C\|x_0\|_2$ for all $t\ge0$ and all $x_0$. To obtain $C$, bound the finitely many trajectories from coordinate basis vectors by $B_j$; the triangle inequality and [[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式|Cauchy–Schwarz]] give $C=\sqrt{\sum_jB_j^2}$. This also bounds the [[谱范数|induced matrix norm]] of $e^{tA}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条轨迹的上界可以依赖 $x_0$。有限维中，全部初值的轨迹有界，等价于存在统一倍数 $C$，使全部 $t\ge0$、全部初值都满足 $\|e^{tA}x_0\|_2\le C\|x_0\|_2$。为得到 $C$，先用 $B_j$ 界住有限个坐标基向量出发的轨迹；由三角不等式与 [[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式]]，可取 $C=\sqrt{\sum_jB_j^2}$。这也界住了 $e^{tA}$ 的[[谱范数|诱导矩阵范数]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Starting within $\varepsilon/C$ then keeps the state within $\varepsilon$, which is Lyapunov stability of zero for this system. [[连续系统谱稳定性|Asymptotic stability]] also requires every initial trajectory to tend to zero, so every real part must be strictly negative. Forward-time boundedness says nothing about backward time: $x(t)=e^{-t}x_0$ is bounded for $t\ge0$ but grows as $t\to-\infty$ when $x_0\ne0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从半径 $\varepsilon/C$ 内出发，就会始终留在半径 $\varepsilon$ 内，这就是该系统零点的 Lyapunov 稳定性。[[连续系统谱稳定性|渐近稳定]]还要求所有初值的轨迹趋零，所以全部实部必须严格为负。向前有界不代表向后也有界：$x(t)=e^{-t}x_0$ 在 $t\ge0$ 上有界，但当 $x_0\ne0$ 时，沿 $t\to-\infty$ 会增长。

> [!question] Purely imaginary eigenvalues of a normal matrix · 正规矩阵的纯虚特征值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A [[Normal 矩阵|normal matrix]] commutes with its conjugate transpose and has an orthonormal eigenvector basis over $\mathbb C$. If all its eigenvalues are purely imaginary, every mode has constant magnitude. All trajectories are bounded and, in fact, preserve their standard length; for positive dimension the system is not asymptotically stable. Normality supplies the missing size-one-block condition. Without it, the opening $B$ is a counterexample.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[Normal 矩阵|正规矩阵]]与自身的共轭转置可交换，在复数域中具有标准正交特征向量基。如果全部特征值都是纯虚数，每个模式的模就保持不变。所有轨迹有界，事实上标准长度保持不变；在正维数下，该系统不渐近稳定。正规性补上了缺少的一阶块条件；去掉这一条件，开头的 $B$ 就是反例。
