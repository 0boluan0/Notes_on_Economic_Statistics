# 独立同分布且具有正有限方差时，标准化样本均值依分布收敛到标准正态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For iid random variables with positive finite variance, the standardised sample mean converges in distribution to the standard normal law*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 中心化与缩放都不能省
> 若 $X_i$ 独立同分布，$E[X_i]=\mu$，且 $0<\sigma^2=\operatorname{Var}(X_i)<\infty$，则 $\sqrt n(\bar X_n-\mu)/\sigma\Rightarrow N(0,1)$。中心化移除随 $n$ 累积的均值，$\sqrt n/\sigma$ 把不断缩小的均值误差放回非退化尺度。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the $X_i$ are iid with mean $\mu$ and variance $0<\sigma^2<\infty$, then $\sqrt n(\bar X_n-\mu)/\sigma$ converges in distribution to the standard normal law. Centering removes the accumulating mean, while scaling by $\sqrt n/\sigma$ puts the shrinking mean error on a non-degenerate scale.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 定理的等价写法

令 $S_n=\sum_{i=1}^nX_i$、$\bar X_n=S_n/n$。经典 i.i.d. CLT 为

$$
\frac{S_n-n\mu}{\sigma\sqrt n}
=\frac{\sqrt n(\bar X_n-\mu)}{\sigma}
\Rightarrow Z,
\qquad Z\sim N(0,1).
$$

等价地，对每个实数 $z$，

$$
P\!\left(
\frac{\sqrt n(\bar X_n-\mu)}{\sigma}\le z
\right)\longrightarrow\Phi(z).
$$

不中心化时，$S_n/(\sigma\sqrt n)$ 中的均值项 $\sqrt n\mu/\sigma$ 通常发散；不乘 $\sqrt n$ 时，[[弱大数定律]] 使 $\bar X_n-\mu$ 退化到 0。两种写法都要求 $\sigma^2>0$；若方差为 0，样本均值恒等于 $\mu$，不存在这个非退化标准化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sum and sample-mean forms are identical. Without centering, the mean term generally diverges after standardisation; without the $\sqrt n$ rescaling, the sample-mean error collapses to zero. Positive variance is essential for this non-degenerate form. If the variance is zero, every observation equals $\mu$ almost surely and division by $\sigma$ is undefined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 渐近正态不等于有限样本处处准确

CLT 本身不给指定样本量 $n$ 的近似误差率，也不告诉我们“多大才算大样本”。由于标准正态 CDF 连续，分布函数的绝对误差事实上会随 $n\to\infty$ 一致趋于 0；但经典定理只给极限，不给这个收敛的速度，而且深尾相对误差仍可能很差。强偏态、厚尾但有限方差、稀有事件或慢收敛分布都可能需要很大的 $n$。若方差不存在，经典形式不能直接使用；标准 Cauchy 的样本均值仍为 Cauchy，就是明确反例。

对 $S_n\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,p)$，正态近似常用均值 $np$、方差 $np(1-p)$，离散概率还可考虑连续性修正。诸如“$np$ 与 $n(1-p)$ 都至少为 5 或 10”的阈值只能作为有限样本诊断启发，不是 CLT 的条件、必要充分判据或精度保证；接近边界或要求尾部精度时应比较精确二项概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The CLT gives no error rate for a specified sample size and no universal threshold for a “large” sample. Continuity of the normal CDF does make the absolute CDF error converge uniformly, but the classical theorem supplies no rate, and relative error in deep tails may still be poor. Skewness, heavy tails with finite variance, and rare events can make convergence slow; infinite variance invalidates the classical form. For binomial counts, rules such as requiring both $np$ and $n(1-p)$ to exceed five or ten are diagnostics only, not theorem assumptions or accuracy guarantees. Exact binomial probabilities remain preferable when feasible, especially near boundaries or in the tails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：Bernoulli 样本比例

若 $X_i\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(p)$ i.i.d.，则 $\mu=p$、$\sigma^2=p(1-p)$。只要 $0<p<1$，

$$
\frac{\sqrt n(\bar X_n-p)}{\sqrt{p(1-p)}}\Rightarrow N(0,1).
$$

取 $p=0.5$、$n=400$。观察到 $\bar X_n=0.54$ 时，相应标准化值为

$$
z=\frac{\sqrt{400}(0.54-0.5)}{\sqrt{0.5(1-0.5)}}=1.6.
$$

这里 $np=n(1-p)=200$ 只说明常见诊断没有报警；它并不把正态近似变成精确二项概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For iid Bernoulli observations, the standardised sample proportion has the displayed normal limit when $0<p<1$. With $p=0.5$, $n=400$, and an observed proportion of $0.54$, the standardised value is $1.6$. Large values of $np$ and $n(1-p)$ merely make a common diagnostic reassuring; they do not make the approximation exact.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检
> 某序列 i.i.d.，$E[X_i]=7$、$\operatorname{Var}(X_i)=9$。写出 $\bar X_n$ 的经典 CLT 标准化。若把 7 省略，是否仍趋于标准正态？
>
> **答案：** 正确形式是 $\sqrt n(\bar X_n-7)/3\Rightarrow N(0,1)$。不能省略 7；否则 $\sqrt n\bar X_n/3$ 多出 $7\sqrt n/3$ 的发散中心项，不会趋于标准正态。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The correct standardisation is $\sqrt n(\bar X_n-7)/3$. Omitting the centering term adds the diverging shift $7\sqrt n/3$, so the result cannot converge to the standard normal law.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
