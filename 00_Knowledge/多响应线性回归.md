# Multivariate linear regression
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*多响应线性回归*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose each student has a mathematics score and a reading score, and we want to relate both to study time. **Multivariate linear regression** uses the same predictors for several outcomes measured on the same observational units. Each outcome has its own coefficients; the model can also describe how their unexplained fluctuations move together.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设每名学生都有数学和阅读两项成绩，我们想用学习时间解释这两项结果。**多响应线性回归**对同一批观测单位的多个结果使用同一套预测变量。每个结果有自己的系数，模型还可以描述各结果未解释的波动如何共同变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here “multivariate” refers to several **responses**. Several predictors with only one response is usually called multiple regression. The [[多响应与多解释变量回归|distinction between responses and predictors]] matters: adding another predictor adds a model term; adding another response adds an outcome equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 multivariate 指多个**响应**。多个预测变量、只有一个响应，通常称 multiple regression。[[多响应与多解释变量回归|响应与解释变量的区别]]很重要：增加一个预测变量是在方程中加一项，增加一个响应则是增加一个结果方程。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read one row before the whole matrix
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先读懂一行，再看整张矩阵*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $t_i$ be study time for student $i$. A possible mean model is $E(Y_{i1}\mid X)=50+5t_i$ for mathematics and $E(Y_{i2}\mid X)=40+2t_i$ for reading. A [[条件期望|conditional mean]] averages the response at the specified predictor information; it does not say every student has that score. We treat the observed design $X$ as fixed, or condition on it throughout.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $t_i$ 为第 $i$ 名学生的学习时间。一个可能的均值模型是：数学 $E(Y_{i1}\mid X)=50+5t_i$，阅读 $E(Y_{i2}\mid X)=40+2t_i$。[[条件期望|条件均值]]是在指定预测信息下的平均结果，不是说每名学生恰好得到这个分数。下文把观测设计 $X$ 视为固定，或始终条件于它。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The row $(1,t_i)$ lists an intercept and study time. Multiplying it by the coefficient matrix produces both means. For three students with times $0,1,2$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行向量 $(1,t_i)$ 依次记录截距和学习时间；与系数矩阵相乘就同时产生两个均值。三个学生的时间为 $0,1,2$ 时，

$$X=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\1&1\\1&2\end{bmatrix},\qquad
B=\begin{bmatrix}50&40\\5&2\end{bmatrix},\qquad
XB=\begin{bmatrix}50&40\\55&42\\60&44\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first column of $B$ belongs to mathematics; the second belongs to reading. The first row contains their intercepts and the second their study-time slopes. [[矩阵乘法|Matrix multiplication]] combines each student’s design row with each response’s coefficient column. The displayed values are illustrative population coefficients, not estimates from the three students.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$B$ 的第一列属于数学，第二列属于阅读；第一行放截距，第二行放学习时间的斜率。[[矩阵乘法]]把每名学生的设计行与每个响应的系数列组合起来。这里给的是示意总体系数，不是从这三个学生估计出来的数值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What each matrix contains
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每张矩阵装什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In general, use $n$ observations, $q$ responses and $k$ encoded regression terms, all positive integers. With real entries, write
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一般地，设有 $n$ 个观测、$q$ 个响应和 $k$ 个编码后的回归项，三者均为正整数。对实数数据，写成

$$Y_{n\times q}=X_{n\times k}B_{k\times q}+U_{n\times q},\qquad E(U\mid X)=0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$Y$ contains the observed responses; the same row index must identify the same unit in $X$ and $Y$. The **design matrix** $X$ contains the regressors, including a column of ones if there is an intercept. $B$ contains the unknown coefficients. $U=Y-XB$ is the model error, the difference from the true conditional mean. The dimension check $(n\times k)(k\times q)=n\times q$ explains why the product fits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$Y$ 装观测响应，$X$ 与 $Y$ 的同一个行号必须指向同一观测单位。**设计矩阵** $X$ 装回归项；有截距时，一列全为一，并已计入 $k$。$B$ 装未知系数。$U=Y-XB$ 是模型误差，即观测与真实条件均值之差。维度检查 $(n\times k)(k\times q)=n\times q$ 说明了为什么能相乘。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalently, $Y_{ij}=\sum_{\ell=1}^k X_{i\ell}B_{\ell j}+U_{ij}$: $i$ selects a unit, $j$ a response, and $\ell$ a regressor. Assuming finite first moments, the zero conditional-error mean makes $E(Y\mid X)=XB$. Merely defining $U=Y-XB$ does not guarantee its conditional mean is zero; that is a substantive model assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逐项写就是 $Y_{ij}=\sum_{\ell=1}^k X_{i\ell}B_{\ell j}+U_{ij}$：$i$ 选观测，$j$ 选响应，$\ell$ 选回归项。在一阶矩存在时，误差条件均值为零使得 $E(Y\mid X)=XB$。仅仅定义 $U=Y-XB$ 并不保证其条件均值为零，这是实质性的模型假设。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Linear” means linear in the unknown coefficients. A fixed column such as $t_i^2$ or a group indicator may be included in $X$. The resulting curve can be nonlinear in study time while remaining linear in $B$. Shared predictors do not require equal slopes across the response columns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“线性”是指对未知系数线性。$X$ 可以包含事先确定的 $t_i^2$ 列或组别指示列；结果曲线可以对学习时间弯曲，同时仍对 $B$ 线性。共享预测变量也不要求不同响应的斜率相等。

> [!example] Observations need not equal their means / 观测不必等于均值
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the three-student model, suppose the observed rows are $(51,39),(54,44),(63,43)$. Subtracting $XB$ gives error rows $(1,-1),(-1,2),(3,-1)$. Their sample sum need not be zero: $E(U\mid X)=0$ describes an average over the model’s possible samples.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 三个学生若实际得到 $(51,39),(54,44),(63,43)$，减去 $XB$ 后的误差行为 $(1,-1),(-1,2),(3,-1)$。这一次样本中的误差之和不必为零；$E(U\mid X)=0$ 说的是模型中可能样本的平均。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The mean model leaves other questions open
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*均值模型还没有回答的事*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The equation does not specify a probability distribution for $U$. Responses within a row may be correlated, and dependence between rows is a separate question. A [[矩阵正态误差结构|matrix-normal error model]] adds a particular Gaussian structure; it is not required merely to write the mean model or compute least squares. If errors are identically distributed but $XB$ varies across rows, the response rows still need not be identically distributed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个方程没有指定 $U$ 的概率分布。同一行内响应可以相关，行与行之间的依赖则是另一个问题。[[矩阵正态误差结构]]额外加入某种 Gaussian 结构；写出均值模型或计算最小二乘本身不要求它。即使误差同分布，只要 $XB$ 随行变化，响应行仍可能不同分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fitting uses data to replace $B$ with $\hat B$. The resulting residuals $R=Y-X\hat B$ differ from the unobserved errors $U$; see [[多响应残差与误差SSP|residuals and error SSCP]]. For [[共享设计的逐响应OLS|ordinary least squares with this shared design]], each response can be fitted separately. [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|Full column rank]] makes the coefficient solution unique; redundant columns can give the same fitted mean with different coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

拟合时用数据把 $B$ 替换为 $\hat B$，得到的残差 $R=Y-X\hat B$ 与不可直接观测的误差 $U$ 不同，见[[多响应残差与误差SSP]]。对[[共享设计的逐响应OLS|共享设计下的普通最小二乘]]，各响应可以分别拟合。[[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]保证系数解唯一；冗余列可能让不同系数产生相同拟合均值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $r=\operatorname{rank}(X)$, fitting leaves $n-r$ residual directions per response, reducing to $n-k$ only when the columns are linearly independent. These dimensions matter later for [[误差SSP秩边界|error-SSCP rank]]. With $X$ of size $120\times5$ and $Y$ of size $120\times3$, $B$ is $5\times3$ and $U$ is $120\times3$; if $r=5$, residual degrees of freedom are $115$, not $120-15$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $r=\operatorname{rank}(X)$，每个响应拟合后留下 $n-r$ 个残差方向；只有各列线性独立时才等于 $n-k$。这些维度会影响[[误差SSP秩边界]]。$X$ 为 $120\times5$、$Y$ 为 $120\times3$ 时，$B$ 为 $5\times3$、$U$ 为 $120\times3$；若 $r=5$，残差自由度是 $115$，不是 $120-15$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The shared-design form presumes aligned, observed response rows and the same available regressor columns. Different missing rows or response-specific designs require an explicitly different setup or justified handling. Joint questions about coefficients belong to [[多响应一般线性假设|multivariate linear hypotheses]]; [[多响应因果边界|causal interpretation]] requires additional study-design assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个共享设计形式要求响应行对齐、相应响应已观测，并使用同一套可用回归列。不同的缺失行或响应专属设计需要明确另设模型或说明处理依据。跨响应的系数问题见[[多响应一般线性假设]]；[[多响应因果边界|因果解释]]还需要额外研究设计条件。

The Gaussian model on slide3 is a special distributional model, not a prerequisite for the mean equation.
- [[01_Math/04_多元统计分析/07_多元线性回归multivariate linear regression.md#1.1. 模型形式|Permanent course Chapter7 §1.1–1.3]]: course notation and the warning that its main regression example has a single response.
