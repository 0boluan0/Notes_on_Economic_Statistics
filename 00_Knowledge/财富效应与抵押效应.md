# 财富效应改变可消费资源评价而抵押效应改变有担保融资条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A wealth effect changes the assessment of consumable resources; a collateral effect changes secured financing conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一次资产涨价可以经过两条不同链：[[财富渠道]]使持有者觉得终身资源改变，从而调整愿意消费多少；抵押效应使贷方接受的担保价值、额度或条款改变，从而影响能够融资多少。[[抵押品]]是否可执行、哪项约束真正绑定，都需要另查；市值增加不自动成为到账现金。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One asset-price change can follow two paths. The [[财富渠道|wealth channel]] changes desired spending through lifetime resources; collateral effects change available finance through pledgeable value, limits or terms. Check enforceability of [[抵押品|collateral]] and the binding constraint. A capital gain is not automatically cash received.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，一项抵押资产价值从 100 升至 120，既有债务为 60。假定贷款人允许总债务最高为抵押价值的 80%，且允许追加借款，其他条件不变：额度从 80 升至 96，未用空间从 20 升至 36，增加 16。20 的资产增值与 16 的新增借款空间并非同一金额。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose collateral rises from 100 to 120 against debt of 60. Assume total debt may reach 80% of collateral value, additional borrowing is permitted and other conditions are unchanged. The limit rises from 80 to 96, and unused capacity from 20 to 36: an increase of 16, distinct from the capital gain of 20.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这 16 还不是实际贷款：若借款人无需求、收入审查不通过或另一个额度更紧，就未必借得出来。若真的新增贷款 16，存款和债务同时增加，不能再把 16 当作第二次财富增加，见[[贷款与借款人净财富]]。也不能把资产增值和贷款额相加，直接报告为消费增长。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The extra 16 is not an actual loan. Demand, income underwriting or another tighter limit may prevent borrowing. If 16 is borrowed, deposits and debt rise together; it is not another wealth gain, as explained by [[贷款与借款人净财富|loans and borrower net wealth]]. Neither capital gains nor loan capacity can simply be added into consumption growth.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有借款需求的人仍可能有财富效应；一项不能直接出售消费但可用于有效担保的资产，则可能通过融资约束影响支出。抵押条件与后续资产价格、净值反复作用形成放大反馈时，完整机制复用[[金融加速器]]，不在这里另写一套。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Someone without borrowing demand may still exhibit a wealth effect, while an asset usable as effective security can affect spending through financing constraints. Feedback among collateral, asset values and net worth belongs to the existing [[金融加速器|financial accelerator]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
