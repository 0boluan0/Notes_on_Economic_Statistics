# 头肩底是中谷低于两肩并在右肩后突破颈线的三谷形态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An inverse head-and-shoulders pattern has a central trough below two shoulders and completes with an upward neckline break after the right shoulder.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

头肩底是下跌后依次形成左肩、较低头部和较高右肩，并在右肩之后向上突破颈线的反转形态。两谷间反弹形成的两个高点连成颈线，作为 [[阻力位]]参考；中间谷最低、两肩处于相近低位。它是 [[头肩顶]]的方向镜像，而不是三个近同低谷。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inverse head-and-shoulders pattern follows a decline: a left shoulder, a lower head and a higher right shoulder precede an upward neckline break. The two intervening rebound highs define the neckline as a [[阻力位|resistance]] reference. The head is lowest and the shoulders lie at similar levels. This reverses the direction of a [[头肩顶|head-and-shoulders top]] rather than requiring three similar lows.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

颈线可倾斜，须按 [[趋势线]]的两点时间价格坐标计算突破当时的线值。三个谷的候选轮廓不足以确认完成；肩位容差、极值可得时点与突破口径要先冻结，只有此后满足条件才发出信号，并依 [[交易信号时序]]等待可能的成交。不能把事后识别的最低谷当成当时已经知道的买点。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sloped neckline must be evaluated at the time of the potential break using its two time-price anchors, as with a [[趋势线|trendline]]. Three troughs alone do not complete the pattern. Freeze shoulder tolerance, turning-point availability and breakout conventions, then let a qualifying signal precede any possible fill under [[交易信号时序|execution timing]]. A retrospectively identified lowest trough was not automatically a known entry point at the time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如三个选定谷为 90、80、90，中间两峰在 $(2,100),(4,96)$，则 $N(t)=100-2(t-2)$。右肩之后 $t=6$ 的颈线为 92；无额外缓冲的收盘严格上破规则下，93 满足条件，92 不满足。条件成立不保证之后继续上涨。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For selected troughs of 90, 80 and 90 with rebound highs at $(2,100)$ and $(4,96)$, $N(t)=100-2(t-2)$. At $t=6$ after the right shoulder the neckline is 92. A strict closing-break rule without an extra buffer accepts 93, but not 92. Completion does not ensure further gains.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
