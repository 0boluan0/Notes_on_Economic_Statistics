# 哈希表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Hash Tables*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把大键空间映射到有限槽位，以平均常数时间完成查找、插入和删除。
> **具体锚点：** 两个不同键可能得到同一槽位，因此 hash 值相同不表示键相等，碰撞必须被显式处理。
> **核心难点：** 平均 O(1) 依赖哈希分布、负载因子与扩容；最坏情形仍可退化到 O(n)。
> **为什么重要：** dictionary、set、缓存和索引结构都依赖相同的不变量。
> **继续：** 先区分 hash、槽位与键相等，再比较 chaining 与 open addressing。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Problem addressed:** Map a large key space into a finite array of slots to support average constant-time lookup, insertion, and deletion.
> **Concrete anchor:** Two different keys can map to the same slot, so an equal hash does not mean equal keys and collision handling is mandatory.
> **Central difficulty:** Average O(1) depends on hash distribution, load factor, and resizing; worst-case cost can still degrade to O(n).
> **Why it matters:** Dictionaries, sets, caches, and indexing structures rely on the same invariants.
> **Continue with:** Distinguish a hash value, a table slot, and key equality before comparing chaining with open addressing.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 哈希
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Hashing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

hash table 平均插入/查找 O(1)，碰撞用开放寻址或链表处理；最坏可 O(n)。性能依赖哈希分布、负载因子和扩容。Python dict 保持插入顺序，但复杂度保证仍是平均语境。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A hash table supports average O(1) insertion and lookup, resolving collisions with open addressing or chaining; worst-case cost can be O(n). Performance depends on hash distribution, load factor, and resizing. Python dictionaries preserve insertion order, but their complexity statement remains an average-case one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相等键必须产生相等 hash；不同键允许碰撞。若键在表中期间改变了参与 hash/equality 的内容，查找路径不再与插入路径一致，因此可变 list 不能作 Python dict key。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal keys must produce equal hashes, while different keys may collide. If the content used by hashing or equality changes while a key is stored, lookup no longer follows the insertion path; this is why a mutable list cannot be a Python dictionary key.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 负载因子、碰撞与扩容
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Load Factor, Collisions, and Resizing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

负载因子是元素数与槽位数的比。chaining 让一个槽保存多个条目；open addressing 在数组内探测其他槽。负载过高会增加链长或探测长度，扩容重哈希用空间换回平均性能。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Load factor is the ratio of entries to slots. Chaining stores multiple entries at one slot, while open addressing probes other slots in the array. A high load factor lengthens chains or probe sequences; resizing and rehashing spend space to restore average performance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：为何必须再比较键
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Why Keys Must Still Be Compared*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设表有 8 个槽，`h(k)=k mod 8`，键 5 与 13 都进入槽 5。查找 13 时先定位槽，再逐项比较完整键；只比较 hash 会把 5 误认为 13。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a table has eight slots and `h(k)=k mod 8`. Keys 5 and 13 both enter slot 5. Looking up 13 first locates that slot and then compares complete keys; comparing only hashes would mistake 5 for 13.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 查找偶发变慢：记录负载因子、碰撞/探测分布和扩容事件，而不只看平均吞吐。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Lookup occasionally becomes slow: measure load factor, collision or probe distribution, and resize events rather than only average throughput.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 自定义键找不到：检查对象是否在插入后改变，以及 `__eq__` 与 `__hash__` 是否保持一致。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  A custom key cannot be found: inspect whether it changed after insertion and whether `__eq__` remains consistent with `__hash__`.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 声称“hash 表没有碰撞”：这是不可能的有限映射假设；正确目标是处理碰撞并控制分布。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  A design claims that a hash table has no collisions: that is impossible for a finite mapping of a larger key space; the goal is to handle collisions and control distribution.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Minimum Self-Check*

### 为什么平均 O(1) 不等于最坏 O(1)？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does average O(1) not imply worst-case O(1)?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 大量键可碰撞到同一链或探测簇，单次查找需检查 O(n) 个条目；平均界依赖分布与容量管理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Many keys can collide into one chain or probe cluster, requiring O(n) checks; the average bound relies on distribution and capacity management.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 相等与 hash 必须满足什么关系？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What relationship must equality and hashing satisfy?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 相等对象必须有相等 hash；相等 hash 不要求对象相等，因此仍要做键比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Equal objects must have equal hashes; equal hashes do not imply equal objects, so keys must still be compared.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 扩容为什么通常是摊销成本？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is resizing normally an amortized cost?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 偶尔需要 O(n) 重建，但容量按比例增长会让昂贵扩容被许多便宜操作分摊。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> An occasional O(n) rebuild is spread over many cheap operations when capacity grows geometrically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
