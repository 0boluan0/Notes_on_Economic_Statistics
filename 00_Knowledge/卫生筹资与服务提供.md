# 卫生筹资说明资金如何筹集统筹和支付而服务提供说明谁实际开展诊疗或预防
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Health financing concerns raising, pooling and paying out funds; provision concerns who delivers treatment or prevention.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

卫生筹资问钱从哪里来、预付资金怎样分担风险、用什么规则向服务方付款；服务提供问医院、诊所等机构实际做了什么。**公立还是私立的机构身份，不能替代对付费安排的判断。**
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Health financing asks where money comes from, how prepaid funds share risks, and how providers are paid. Provision concerns the care delivered by hospitals, clinics and other organisations. Public or private ownership does not identify the payment arrangement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设一项诊疗收费 100，公共保险支付 80，患者自付 20，而诊所由私人经营。这是私人机构提供服务、公共保险与个人共同付费；不能把整笔 100 因为诊所私有而归作个人自付。反过来，公立医院也可能收取患者自付费用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a privately owned clinic charges 100, with public insurance paying 80 and the patient paying 20. Private provision coexists with mixed payment; the full 100 is not out-of-pocket spending merely because the clinic is private. A public hospital can likewise receive patient copayments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，比较两种医疗制度时，至少分别列清资金来源、风险统筹、购买与支付规则、服务提供者；“社会医保”“税收筹资”这样的名称本身不足以回答全部问题。这个区分是[[公共品的筹资、生产与治理|筹资与生产分工]]在卫生领域的具体应用，不要求被提供的每项诊疗都是公共品。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare revenue sources, pooling, purchasing and payment rules, and providers separately. Labels such as social insurance or tax funding do not answer all these questions. This applies the distinction between [[公共品的筹资、生产与治理|financing and production]] without assuming every treatment is a public good.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
