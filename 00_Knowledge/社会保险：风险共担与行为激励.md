# 社会保险：风险共担与行为激励
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Social Insurance: Risk Sharing and Behavioral Incentives*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 在私人保险因选择、行为或相关风险而不完整时，设计能平滑坏状态消费又不过度削弱激励的公共保障。
> **具体锚点：** 失业保险平滑失业期消费，但更慷慨给付也可能改变求职激励；完整评价要同时看保险价值和行为反应。
> **核心难点：** 逆向选择、道德风险和相关冲击是不同失灵；强制参保可改善风险池，却不能消除给付后的行为变化。
> **为什么重要：** 失业、疾病、残疾与长寿风险会直接改变家庭消费和劳动选择，单看财政支出会漏掉保险价值。
> **继续：** 依次识别缺失市场、消费平滑收益、行为弹性、筹资成本和给付规则，再判断覆盖与慷慨程度。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** When private insurance is incomplete because of selection, behavior, or correlated risk, design public protection that smooths consumption in bad states without excessively weakening incentives.
> **Concrete anchor:** Unemployment insurance smooths consumption during unemployment, but more generous benefits may also change search incentives. A complete evaluation must consider both insurance value and behavioral responses.
> **Central difficulty:** Adverse selection, moral hazard, and correlated shocks are distinct failures. Mandatory participation can improve the risk pool but cannot eliminate post-benefit behavioral responses.
> **Why it matters:** Unemployment, illness, disability, and longevity risk directly alter household consumption and labor choices; fiscal spending alone omits the value of insurance.
> **Continue with:** Identify the missing market, consumption-smoothing gain, behavioral elasticity, financing cost, and benefit rules in that order before choosing coverage and generosity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

社会保险回答的是：私人市场难以完整覆盖失业、疾病、残疾和长寿风险时，怎样强制扩大风险池并提供消费平滑，同时控制由保障本身带来的行为改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Social insurance asks how to expand risk pools and smooth consumption when private markets cannot fully cover unemployment, illness, disability, or longevity risk, while controlling behavioral changes created by the protection itself.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 社会保险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why Social Insurance Exists*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

失业、疾病、残疾和长寿风险可能因逆向选择、道德风险和相关冲击而难以私人保险。政府强制参与可扩大风险池，但给付规则改变劳动、储蓄和报告行为。最优设计权衡消费平滑、再分配与激励。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unemployment, illness, disability, and longevity risks may be difficult to insure privately because of adverse selection, moral hazard, and correlated shocks. Mandatory participation can enlarge the risk pool, but benefit rules change work, saving, and reporting behavior. Optimal design balances consumption smoothing, redistribution, and incentives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

逆向选择解释为什么自愿参保者可能集中高风险类型；道德风险解释保障后预防、求职或申报行为如何改变；相关冲击解释经济衰退或疫情时大量理赔为何同时发生。强制参保主要缓解风险池选择，不能自动消除后两者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adverse selection explains why voluntary enrollment may attract disproportionately high-risk types. Moral hazard explains how prevention, job search, or reporting changes after coverage. Correlated shocks explain why many claims arrive simultaneously during recessions or pandemics. Mandates mainly address selection into the risk pool; they do not automatically eliminate the other two problems.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 保险价值与激励成本
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Insurance Value and Incentive Cost*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

保险价值来自把资源从低边际效用的好状态转移到高边际效用的坏状态。激励成本来自给付降低坏状态的私人边际损失，使预防或离开坏状态的努力下降。评价要比较边际给付带来的消费平滑收益与行为、筹资和行政成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Insurance creates value by transferring resources from good states with low marginal utility to bad states with high marginal utility. Incentive costs arise because benefits reduce the private marginal loss in the bad state, potentially weakening prevention or effort to exit that state. Evaluation compares the consumption-smoothing gain from an additional benefit with behavioral, financing, and administrative costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：失业保险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Unemployment Insurance*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

提高 replacement rate 会直接提高失业期消费并降低家庭被迫借贷或出售资产的需要，也可能延长失业持续期。持续期增加不是全部福利成本：若求职者获得更合适岗位，匹配质量可能提高。需要同时观察消费、搜索、再就业工资、岗位持续和财政成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Raising the replacement rate directly supports consumption during unemployment and reduces forced borrowing or asset sales, but it may lengthen unemployment spells. A longer spell is not automatically a pure welfare cost: better search may improve match quality. Evaluation should jointly examine consumption, search, re-employment wages, job duration, and fiscal cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 设计与失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Design and Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设计维度包括覆盖资格、等待期、给付水平、持续期、经验费率、共同支付和审核。若降低给付只迫使流动性受限者接受低质量选择，可能损害保险目标；若审核过弱导致申报激增，可能是验证和信息设计问题，不等于保险本身没有价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Design dimensions include eligibility, waiting periods, benefit levels, duration, experience rating, cost sharing, and verification. Cutting benefits may undermine insurance if liquidity-constrained recipients are merely forced into poor choices. A surge in claims under weak verification may reveal a monitoring and information-design problem rather than proving that insurance has no value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 社会保险为何可能同时提高福利又扭曲行为？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can social insurance improve welfare while also distorting behavior?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它在坏状态下平滑消费，但降低个人承担的边际损失，可能改变预防、求职或申报激励。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It smooths consumption in bad states but reduces the marginal loss borne by the individual, potentially changing prevention, job-search, or reporting incentives.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 强制参保主要缓解什么问题？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What problem does mandatory participation mainly mitigate?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它限制低风险者退出，从而扩大并稳定风险池，主要缓解逆向选择；仍需另行处理道德风险和相关冲击。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It limits exit by low-risk people and thereby enlarges and stabilizes the risk pool, mainly mitigating adverse selection; moral hazard and correlated shocks still require separate treatment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 失业持续期增加为何不一定等于同等大小的社会损失？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is a longer unemployment spell not necessarily an equally large social loss?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 部分延长可能换来更好的岗位匹配和更高再就业工资；应把匹配收益、消费保险和财政/产出成本共同评价。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Some additional search may produce better matches and higher re-employment wages; match quality, consumption insurance, and fiscal or output costs must be evaluated jointly.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
