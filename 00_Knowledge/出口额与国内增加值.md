# 出口总额记录跨境销售而国内增加值只计本地生产环节新增的价值

出口总额包含出口品中的进口投入价值；出口中的国内增加值则追踪本经济体生产单位直接或间接创造、并包含在出口中的增加值。因此，出口企业自身的增加值、本地供应链合计的增加值和出口销售额是不同对象，复用[[国内生产总值|GDP的增加值核算原则]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Gross exports include the value of imported inputs. Domestic value added embodied in exports traces value created directly or indirectly by producers in the exporting economy. The exporter's own value added, that of its domestic supply chain, and export sales are different objects; use the [[国内生产总值|GDP value-added accounting principle]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

构造一个只有两家生产企业的经济体，同期没有存货变化、折旧、税费或其他生产：组装厂出口100，直接进口零件70，并向本地供应商购买10；供应商为此进口投入5。组装厂增加值为 $100-70-10=20$，供应商增加值为 $10-5=5$；出口中的国内增加值合计25，也等于最终出口100减直接及间接进口75。本地采购10不是全部本地增加值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider an economy with only two producers and no inventory changes, depreciation, taxes or other production. An assembler exports 100, directly imports components worth 70, and buys 10 from a local supplier, which imports inputs worth 5. Their value added is respectively $100-70-10=20$ and $10-5=5$. Domestic value added embodied in exports is 25, also equal to final exports of 100 minus direct and indirect imports of 75. Local purchases of 10 are not entirely domestic value added.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再设组装厂由境外常住投资者全资持有，20中有12为本地常住工人的工资、8为归境外投资者的利润；供应商的5全部归本地常住单位，且没有其他跨境初次收入。则本例GDP为25，但[[国民总收入|GNI]]为 $25-8=17$。那8仍是境内生产创造的增加值；按直接投资收入核算，即使利润留存再投资而没有实际汇出，收入归属也不能改成全部属于本地居民。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now suppose the assembler is wholly owned by a nonresident investor. Of its 20, resident workers receive wages of 12 and the investor earns profits of 8; the supplier's 5 accrues entirely to residents, with no other cross-border primary income. GDP is 25 but [[国民总收入|GNI]] is $25-8=17$. The 8 remains domestic value added. Under direct-investment income accounting, retaining and reinvesting those earnings does not make them all income accruing to domestic residents.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个简化式不能直接当作复杂经济体的“全部出口减全部进口”：进口还可能用于本地最终消费，出口也可能含回流的本国增加值。评价收益还须区分[[国内增加值率与总量|增加值比例与金额]]，并保留[[GDP与福利|生产、收入与福利的边界]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example does not justify subtracting all imports from all exports in a complex economy: imports can serve domestic final demand and exports may contain returning domestic value added. Distinguish [[国内增加值率与总量|shares from amounts]] and preserve the [[GDP与福利|boundary between production, income and welfare]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
