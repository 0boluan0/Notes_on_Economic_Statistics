# 总资产周转率是营业收入与相应总资产存量之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Total asset turnover is revenue divided by the corresponding total-asset stock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总资产周转率 $T=S/A$ 描述一元资产占用支持多少营业收入，通常采用期间平均资产。CPA本章为简便使用期末资产，ABC的 $3000/2000=1.5$；这两个存量口径应分清。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Total asset turnover $T=S/A$ measures revenue supported per unit of assets, generally using period-average assets. CPA simplifies with ending assets: $3000/2000=1.5$ for ABC. Keep these stock conventions distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其倒数 $A/S$ 是每元收入对应的资产占用；乘期间天数 $D$ 得折算天数 $DA/S$。收入、资产均为正时这些表达可相互换算。它们不代表资产在市场上实际清算需要的时间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The reciprocal $A/S$ measures assets per unit of revenue; multiplying by period length gives implied days $DA/S$. With positive revenue and assets these forms are interchangeable, but they do not measure actual market-liquidation time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它在[[杜邦分解]]中连接销售与资产。比率变化需用[[资产周转分解]]定位应收、存货和长期资产，而不是把高周转独立当作高盈利：周转快但利润率低，未必有较高资产回报。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Turnover links revenue and assets in [[杜邦分解|DuPont decomposition]]. Use [[资产周转分解|asset decomposition]] to locate changes in receivables, inventory, and long-term assets. High turnover alone does not mean high profitability: a low margin may offset rapid turnover.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
