# Limit comparison test for series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*级数极限比较判别*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The terms $1/(n^2-1)$, starting at $n=2$, look much like $1/n^2$. But they are larger than $1/n^2$, so comparison with this convergent lower bound does not prove convergence. **Limit comparison** resolves the difficulty by asking whether the ratio of the terms approaches a finite positive scale factor. If it does, each tail can be bounded by a constant multiple of the other, so the two series have the same convergence behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从 $n=2$ 开始的 $1/(n^2-1)$ 看起来很像 $1/n^2$，但它比 $1/n^2$ 更大，因此拿这个收敛下界作比较，不能证明目标收敛。**极限比较**改问：两者通项的比值是否趋于某个有限正比例？如果是，两边的尾部就都能被另一边的常数倍控制，从而同敛散。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Recall that an [[无穷级数|infinite series]] converges when its finite partial sums approach a finite total. Here $a_n$ is a target term and $b_n$ is a simpler comparison term, such as the known [[p级数判别|p-series]] term $n^{-p}$. The index $n$ tends through integers; a [[序列收敛|limit of the ratios]] means that every sufficiently late ratio stays within each prescribed positive tolerance of the limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[无穷级数]]收敛，是指有限部分和趋于有限总量。这里用 $a_n$ 表示目标通项，$b_n$ 表示较简单的比较通项，例如已知的 [[p级数判别|p级数]]通项 $n^{-p}$。索引 $n$ 沿整数增加；[[序列收敛|比值存在有限极限]]，是指对每个给定的正容差，足够靠后的全部比值都一直位于极限的该容差以内。

> [!important] A finite positive ratio limit · 有限正比值极限
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let all terms be finite real numbers. Suppose $a_n\ge0$ and $b_n>0$ from some index onward, and
> $$\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{a_n}{b_n}=L,\qquad 0<L<\infty.$$
> Then $\sum a_n$ converges if and only if $\sum b_n$ converges. If they diverge, both partial-sum sequences tend to $+\infty$. This compares convergence, not the numerical values of their sums.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设各项都是已定义的有限实数，从某个索引起 $a_n\ge0$、$b_n>0$，且 $a_n/b_n\to L$，其中 $0<L<\infty$。那么 $\sum a_n$ 与 $\sum b_n$ 同收敛或同发散；发散时，两者的部分和都趋于 $+\infty$。这比较的是敛散性，不是断言二者的和相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The comparison denominator must be positive on the tail, so the ratio is defined there. The positive limit also forces $a_n>0$ eventually. There is no requirement that either sequence of terms be decreasing, and $L$ need not equal $1$. Finite prefixes can differ, but every included term must be defined: changing where a valid tail starts does not make an original $1/0$ meaningful.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较分母在尾部必须为正，比值才在那里有定义；正极限也会迫使 $a_n$ 最终严格为正。不要求两列通项递减，$L$ 也不必等于 $1$。有限前缀可以不同，但原级数纳入的每一项都必须有定义；改用合法尾部，不能让原先的 $1/0$ 变得有意义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## How a limit produces two comparisons
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*极限如何产生两边的比较*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose the positive tolerance $L/2$. The definition of the ratio limit gives one index after which $|a_n/b_n-L|<L/2$. Multiplying by the positive $b_n$ yields
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取正容差 $L/2$。比值极限的定义保证，从某个索引起，所有项都满足 $|a_n/b_n-L|<L/2$。乘上正数 $b_n$，得到

$$\frac L2 b_n\le a_n\le\frac{3L}{2}b_n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $\sum b_n$ converges, the upper bound proves convergence of $\sum a_n$ by [[级数直接比较判别|direct comparison]]. Conversely, the lower bound can be rearranged as $b_n\le(2/L)a_n$; convergence of $\sum a_n$ therefore proves convergence of $\sum b_n$. A fixed positive multiplier does not change convergence, because it multiplies every finite partial sum by that same number and can be divided out. These two implications give the equivalence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $\sum b_n$ 收敛，由上界及[[级数直接比较判别]]可知 $\sum a_n$ 收敛。反过来，下界可以写成 $b_n\le(2/L)a_n$；因此 $\sum a_n$ 收敛又推出 $\sum b_n$ 收敛。固定正倍数不改变敛散性，因为每个有限部分和都只乘同一个数，也能再除回来。两个方向合起来，就得到同敛散。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对开头的例子，

$$\frac{1/(n^2-1)}{1/n^2}=\frac1{1-n^{-2}}\longrightarrow1\qquad(n\ge2).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $\sum 1/n^2$ converges, so does $\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}1/(n^2-1)$. The limit has supplied an eventual upper bound that the original inequality failed to provide. For a scale factor other than one, $(3n+2)/(n^3+1)$ divided by $1/n^2$ equals $(3+2/n)/(1+1/n^3)\to3$, so this target series also converges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于 $\sum1/n^2$ 收敛，$\sum_{n=2}^{\infty}1/(n^2-1)$ 也收敛；极限提供了原先那个不等式没有给出的最终上界。比例也可以不是 $1$：$(3n+2)/(n^3+1)$ 除以 $1/n^2$ 得到 $(3+2/n)/(1+1/n^3)\to3$，所以这个目标级数同样收敛。

> [!example] Keeping the starting index valid · 保证起点合法
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $n\ge2$, let $a_n=1/\sqrt{n^5-n^2}$ and $b_n=n^{-5/2}$. Factoring $n^5$ out of the square root gives
> $$\frac{a_n}{b_n}=\frac1{\sqrt{1-n^{-3}}}\longrightarrow1.$$
> The p-series exponent is $5/2>1$, so both series converge. At $n=1$ the original denominator is zero; starting at $2$ is part of specifying a valid series.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $n\ge2$，令 $a_n=1/\sqrt{n^5-n^2}$、$b_n=n^{-5/2}$。从根号中提出 $n^5$，得到 $a_n/b_n=1/\sqrt{1-n^{-3}}\to1$。因为 p 级数的指数 $5/2>1$，两者都收敛。$n=1$ 时原分母为零，因此从 $2$ 开始，是合法定义原级数的一部分。

## Zero, infinity and a ratio that does not settle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零、无穷，以及没有稳定极限的比值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Retain eventual $a_n\ge0$ and $b_n>0$. If $a_n/b_n\to0$, eventually $a_n\le b_n$. Thus convergence of $\sum b_n$ implies convergence of $\sum a_n$, but the two need not behave alike. With $b_n=1/\sqrt n$, either choice $a_n=1/n$ or $a_n=1/n^2$ has ratio tending to zero; the first target diverges and the second converges, although the benchmark diverges in both cases.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

仍假设最终 $a_n\ge0$、$b_n>0$。若 $a_n/b_n\to0$，最终就有 $a_n\le b_n$，所以 $\sum b_n$ 收敛可以推出 $\sum a_n$ 收敛，但不能断言同敛散。取 $b_n=1/\sqrt n$，无论选 $a_n=1/n$ 还是 $a_n=1/n^2$，比值都趋零；前一个目标发散，后一个目标收敛，而比较级数在两种情形都发散。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $a_n/b_n\to+\infty$, the ratio eventually exceeds every fixed positive level; in particular, eventually $a_n\ge b_n$. Thus divergence of $\sum b_n$ implies divergence of $\sum a_n$. This is a limit to infinity, not merely a ratio with arbitrarily large isolated values. Again there is no equivalence: with $b_n=1/n^3$, either $a_n=1/n^2$ or $a_n=1/n$ has ratio tending to infinity, but the first target converges and the second diverges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $a_n/b_n\to+\infty$，比值最终会超过每个固定正高度；特别地，最终 $a_n\ge b_n$。因此 $\sum b_n$ 发散可以推出 $\sum a_n$ 发散。这里要求的是趋于正无穷，而不仅是偶尔出现任意大的比值。仍然不能断言同敛散：取 $b_n=1/n^3$，$a_n=1/n^2$ 与 $a_n=1/n$ 的比值都趋于无穷，但前者目标收敛，后者发散。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the ratio has no limit, this limit test gives no verdict. Another comparison may still work. For $a_n=(2+(-1)^n)/n^2$ and $b_n=1/n^2$, the ratio alternates between $1$ and $3$, but $0<a_n\le3b_n$ proves convergence directly. Failure of a test's hypothesis is not a proof of divergence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若比值没有极限，本判别法就不给结论，但仍可能有别的比较可用。例如 $a_n=(2+(-1)^n)/n^2$、$b_n=1/n^2$ 的比值在 $1$ 与 $3$ 之间交替，没有极限；可是 $0<a_n\le3b_n$ 已能直接证明收敛。判别法的条件不满足，并不等于证明了发散。
