# 最终正项的比值趋于有限正数时两个级数同敛散
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Two eventually positive series have the same convergence behaviour when their term ratio tends to a finite positive number*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a_n,b_n$ 最终都为正，且比值极限存在并满足 $0<L<\infty$。则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $a_n$ and $b_n$ are eventually positive and their ratio has a limit $L$ with $0<L<\infty$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{a_n}{b_n}=L
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum a_n\text{ 收敛}\iff\sum b_n\text{ 收敛}.
$$

分母 $b_n$ 在所比较的尾部必须非零。$L$ 不必等于 1；有限正比例只缩放尾部大小。具体地，充分大的 $n$ 满足 $(L/2)b_n\le a_n\le(3L/2)b_n$，再对两边用 [[级数直接比较判别]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The denominator must be nonzero on the compared tail. The limit need not equal one: a finite positive factor only rescales the tail. Eventually $(L/2)b_n\le a_n\le(3L/2)b_n$, so the [[级数直接比较判别|direct comparison test]] applies in both directions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若只假设 $a_n\ge0$、$b_n>0$ 最终成立，边界 $L=0$ 只允许从 $\sum b_n$ 收敛推出 $\sum a_n$ 收敛；$L=+\infty$ 只允许从 $\sum b_n$ 发散推出 $\sum a_n$ 发散。不能把这两个边界当成“同敛散”。比值不趋于一个极限时，本卡的极限版本也不给结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With eventual $a_n\ge0$ and $b_n>0$, the boundary $L=0$ only transfers convergence from $\sum b_n$ to $\sum a_n$; $L=+\infty$ only transfers divergence from $\sum b_n$ to $\sum a_n$. Neither boundary gives equivalence. This limit version is also inconclusive when the ratio has no limit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如对 $n\ge2$，令 $a_n=1/\sqrt{n^5-n^2}$、$b_n=1/n^{5/2}$，则 $a_n/b_n=(1-n^{-3})^{-1/2}\to1$。因为 $5/2>1$，[[p级数判别]] 给出两者都收敛。起点取 2 是为了保证原项有定义。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n\ge2$, let $a_n=1/\sqrt{n^5-n^2}$ and $b_n=1/n^{5/2}$. Their ratio tends to one, so both series converge by the [[p级数判别|p-series test]]. Starting at two ensures that the original terms are defined.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
