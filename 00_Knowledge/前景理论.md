# 前景理论用相对于参考点的得失及决策权重描述不确定选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Prospect theory describes uncertain choice through reference-dependent gains and losses and decision weights*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前景理论是一种描述不确定选择的模型：先把结果相对于[[前景参考点]]编码为收益或损失，再结合结果的价值 $v(x)$ 与[[前景决策权重]]评价可选前景。这里的“前景”是可能结果及其发生条件构成的备选方案；价值尺度不是货币期望值，也不是规定投资者应当如何行动的规范。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Prospect theory is a descriptive model of uncertain choice. Outcomes are coded as gains or losses relative to a [[前景参考点|reference point]], then evaluated using an outcome value function and [[前景决策权重|decision weights]]. A prospect is an alternative comprising possible outcomes and the conditions under which they occur. Its value is neither an expected monetary payoff nor a prescription for how investors should act.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1979 年版本区分编辑与评价。对至多两个非零结果的普通简单前景，结果 $x,y$ 的概率为 $p,q$，其余概率对应 $0$；若 $p+q<1$，或两结果分处零点两侧（可含零），则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The 1979 version distinguishes editing from evaluation. For a regular simple prospect with at most two nonzero outcomes, $x,y$ occur with probabilities $p,q$, and any remaining probability gives zero. If $p+q<1$, or the outcomes lie on opposite sides of zero, allowing equality, then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$V(x,p;y,q)=\pi(p)v(x)+\pi(q)v(y),\qquad v(0)=0.$$

例如，自拟取 $v(100)=10$、$\pi(1/2)=0.4$，则“各半概率得到 $100$ 或 $0$”的前景价值为 $4$，货币期望值却为 $50$。这些参数只用于演示两种尺度，不是经验通用常数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For illustration, let $v(100)=10$ and $\pi(1/2)=0.4$. A fifty-fifty prospect paying $100$ or zero has prospect value $4$, but monetary expectation $50$. These illustrative values distinguish the scales; they are not universal empirical constants.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上式不是任意多个结果的通用公式：1979 年版本对全部严格同号的两结果前景先分离确定部分；1992 年累积版本则使用[[累积前景权重计算|排序后累积概率的差分权重]]。通常提出的收益侧凹、损失侧凸与[[损失厌恶]]也是不同特征，不能合并成“所有情形都风险厌恶”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is not a universal formula for arbitrarily many outcomes. The 1979 model segregates a sure component for strictly same-sign two-outcome prospects; the 1992 cumulative version uses [[累积前景权重计算|differences of transformed cumulative probabilities]]. The usual gain-side concavity, loss-side convexity, and [[损失厌恶|loss aversion]] are distinct features, not a claim of universal risk aversion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
