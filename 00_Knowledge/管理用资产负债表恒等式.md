# 同一完整分类下净经营资产等于净金融负债加股东权益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a complete consistent classification, net operating assets equal net financial debt plus equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将同一时点的全部资产、负债分别完整划入经营或金融侧，不重复也不遗漏，原来的 $A=L+E$ 就能重排为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Classify all assets and liabilities at one date as operating or financial, with neither omissions nor double counting. The original $A=L+E$ can then be rearranged:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$OA+FA=OL+FL+E\quad\Longrightarrow\quad OA-OL=(FL-FA)+E,$$
$$NOA=NFD+E.$$

左侧是[[净经营资产]]，右侧是支持这一净占用的[[净金融负债]]和股东权益。ABC 为 $1722=762+960$。这说明经营资产扣除经营负债后由什么资本支持，而不是说经营负债已不再需要清偿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The left side is [[净经营资产|NOA]]; the right side is its support from [[净金融负债|NFD]] and equity. ABC gives $1722=762+960$. This identifies financing for net operating investment without implying that operating liabilities no longer require settlement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

检查不平时，应回到[[管理用报表分类]]核对范围、日期和重分类，而不是补一个无来源的“其他”项强行凑平。若使用期间平均存量，同一套线性平均也保留恒等式；但不能拿期末 NOA 与平均 NFD、平均 E 混配。该式也是[[管理用ROE分解]]中 $NOA/E=1+NFD/E$ 的起点，除法另要求 $E\ne0$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the equation fails, check scope, timing, and [[管理用报表分类|classification]], rather than adding an unsupported balancing item. A consistent linear average preserves the identity, but ending NOA cannot be mixed with average NFD and equity. It also yields $NOA/E=1+NFD/E$ in [[管理用ROE分解|the ROE decomposition]], provided equity is nonzero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
