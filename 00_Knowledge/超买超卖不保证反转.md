# 指标进入超买或超卖区域并不保证价格马上反转
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Entering an overbought or oversold indicator region does not guarantee an immediate price reversal.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[超买超卖]] 描述的是一个指标相对既定阈值的当前状态，不能仅由这个标签推出下一期价格必然反向变化。在持续走势中，指标可以连续多期停留在同一区域。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[超买超卖|Overbought and oversold]] describe an indicator's current state relative to chosen thresholds. The label alone does not imply an opposite price move next period. An indicator can remain in the same extreme region for successive periods during a persistent move.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 [[RSI]] 可以直接构造反例：从 $100$ 起，收盘价每天增加 $1$。首 $N$ 个变化的平均上涨量为 $1$，平均下跌量为 $0$；以后按 Wilder 递推仍为 $1$ 和 $0$，因此 RSI 持续等于 $100$，而价格仍持续上涨。每天减少 $1$ 的有限正价格序列，同理可在 RSI 为 $0$ 时继续下跌。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A direct [[RSI]] counterexample starts at $100$ and adds $1$ to the close each day. The initial average gain is $1$ and average loss $0$; Wilder updates keep those values unchanged. RSI remains $100$ while prices keep rising. Similarly, a finite sequence of positive prices falling by $1$ per day can continue downward while RSI stays at $0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这只反驳“极端状态必然马上反转”，没有证明所有阈值规则都无预测价值。如果要使用反转策略，需要另行规定触发方式、持有期和退出，再检验对应条件收益及 [[证券交易成本]]。把阈值从 $70$ 调到 $80$ 也不能消除上述逻辑反例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example refutes guaranteed immediate reversal, not every possible predictive use of thresholds. A reversal strategy needs its own trigger, holding period, and exit rule, followed by tests of conditional returns and [[证券交易成本|trading costs]]. Raising a threshold from $70$ to $80$ does not remove the counterexample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
