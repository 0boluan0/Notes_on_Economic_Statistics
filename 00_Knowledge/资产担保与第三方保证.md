# 资产担保依托特定担保物而第三方保证增加约定的偿付义务人

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Collateral security relies on specified assets, whereas a third-party guarantee adds a party with agreed payment obligations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资产担保通过[[抵押品|特定资产上的担保权益]]支持债权；第三方保证则由保证人承诺，在约定情形下承担指定债务的偿付责任。前者首先要找“对哪项资产有什么权利”，后者首先要找“哪位保证人承担多大范围的什么义务”。二者可以同时存在，不能把英文 secured 与 guaranteed 都理解为同一种无风险承诺。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Collateral security supports a claim through [[抵押品|rights in specified assets]]. A third-party guarantor undertakes defined payment obligations in the agreed circumstances. For the former, identify the asset and security rights; for the latter, identify the guarantor and the scope of its obligations. Both can coexist. Neither “secured” nor “guaranteed” means an identical risk-free promise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，债权额为 100，而唯一可回收的担保物净变现款只有 70，且本例排除其他追索来源，有资产担保仍只能回收 70。换成另一份只保证最多 20 的第三方保证，即使保证人完全履约，也只能从该保证获得最多 20；不能仅凭“有保证”就推断债权 100 必定全部收回。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a claim is 100 but the only recoverable collateral produces net proceeds of 70, with no other recourse in this example. Security still yields only 70. Under a separate guarantee limited to 20, even full guarantor performance supplies at most 20 from that guarantee. The existence of a guarantee alone cannot establish full recovery of the claim of 100.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际判断还要检查担保权的成立与可执行性、处置成本和[[受偿顺序]]，或保证的覆盖范围、触发条件与保证人的履约能力。保证人有[[净值]]不等于债权人已取得其某项资产的担保权；只有资产担保也不自动增加一个对债务全面负责的第三方。具体法律名称和执行程序须按辖区与合同辨认。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Actual recovery depends on the validity and enforceability of security, realisation costs and [[受偿顺序|priority]], or on guarantee coverage, triggers and the guarantor's ability to perform. A guarantor's [[净值|net worth]] does not give the creditor a security interest in a particular asset. Collateral security alone does not automatically add a third party liable for the entire debt. Legal terminology and enforcement procedures depend on the jurisdiction and contract.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
