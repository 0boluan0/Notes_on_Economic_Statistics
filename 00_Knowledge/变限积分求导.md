# Differentiating an integral with moving bounds
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*变限积分求导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An integral can change because the interval being accumulated moves. Imagine a strip whose height at position $t$ is $f(t)$. Moving its right end to the right adds a contribution; moving its left end to the right removes one. Each contribution depends both on the height there and on how fast that endpoint moves. This is why differentiating an integral with two moving bounds produces **two terms**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

积分会随着被累积区间的移动而改变。想象一条在位置 $t$ 高度为 $f(t)$ 的带状区域：右端向右移动会增加一部分，左端向右移动会去掉一部分。每一端的贡献既取决于那里的高度，也取决于端点移动得多快。因此，对两个端点都在移动的积分求导，会出现**两项**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, let $H(x)=\int_x^{2x}t\,dt$. The integration variable $t$ runs across the strip, while $x$ controls its two ends. The upper endpoint $2x$ has speed $2$ and height $2x$; the lower endpoint $x$ has speed $1$ and height $x$. The predicted rate is $(2x)2-x=3x$. Direct evaluation gives $H(x)=3x^2/2$, confirming that derivative. The same algebra works when the written bounds are reversed in numerical order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，令 $H(x)=\int_x^{2x}t\,dt$。积分变量 $t$ 在带状区域内取值，$x$ 则控制两端。上限 $2x$ 的变化率为 $2$，那里的高度是 $2x$；下限 $x$ 的变化率为 $1$，高度是 $x$，因此预期总变化率为 $(2x)2-x=3x$。直接计算确实得到 $H(x)=3x^2/2$，求导即为 $3x$。即使书写的上下限在数值上反向排列，同一套有向积分规则仍然有效。

> [!important] Endpoint value times endpoint speed · 端点函数值乘以端点变化率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A clean two-sided version assumes a real function $f$ [[一点连续|continuous]] on an open interval $I$. Let $u,v$ be differentiable on an open interval $J$, with $u(x),v(x)\in I$ for every $x\in J$. For
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 一个直接适用双侧导数的版本是：实值函数 $f$ 在开区间 $I$ 上[[一点连续|连续]]，函数 $u,v$ 在开区间 $J$ 上可导，并且每个 $x\in J$ 都满足 $u(x),v(x)\in I$。令
>
> $$H(x)=\int_{u(x)}^{v(x)}f(t)\,dt.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Then the [[导数|derivative]] with respect to the external input $x$ is
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 则相对于外部输入 $x$ 的[[导数]]为
>
> $$H'(x)=f(v(x))v'(x)-f(u(x))u'(x).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $u$ is the lower **written bound**, $v$ the upper written bound, and primes mean derivatives with respect to $x$. Neither $u(x)\le v(x)$ nor monotonicity of either endpoint is required. Since $I$ is an interval, the entire segment between the two endpoints lies in the continuous domain of $f$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $u$ 是**书写在下方的积分限**，$v$ 是书写在上方的积分限，撇号表示对 $x$ 求导。不要求 $u(x)\le v(x)$，也不要求任一端点函数单调。由于 $I$ 是区间，连接两个端点的整段都位于 $f$ 的连续定义域内。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One accumulation function explains both terms
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*一个累积函数解释两端贡献*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose a fixed reference point $a\in I$ and form the [[累积函数|accumulation function]] $G(s)=\int_a^s f(t)\,dt$, where $s$ is its input. By [[定积分区间可加性|oriented interval additivity]],
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $I$ 内固定一个参考点 $a$，构造[[累积函数]] $G(s)=\int_a^s f(t)\,dt$，其中 $s$ 是它的输入。由[[定积分区间可加性|有向区间可加性]]，

$$H(x)=G(v(x))-G(u(x)).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[积分求导定理|fundamental theorem for accumulation]] gives $G'(s)=f(s)$. Apply the [[链式法则|chain rule]] separately to the two compositions: changing $x$ first changes an endpoint, which then changes $G$. Thus
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[积分求导定理]]给出 $G'(s)=f(s)$。对这两个复合函数分别使用[[链式法则]]：$x$ 先改变端点，再通过端点改变 $G$，于是

$$H'(x)=G'(v(x))v'(x)-G'(u(x))u'(x)
=f(v(x))v'(x)-f(u(x))u'(x).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This proof also explains the minus sign: the accumulated part up to the lower bound is subtracted. If the lower bound is constant, its derivative is zero. If $u=v$ as functions throughout $J$, the integral is identically zero and the two derivative terms cancel. Equality of the endpoints at just one input does not make the derivative vanish there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个证明也解释了负号：必须减去下限以前的累积部分。若下限为常数，它的导数就是零；若两个端点函数在整个 $J$ 上都相同，积分恒为零，两项也恒相消。但两个端点只在某一个输入处相等，并不意味着那一点的导数为零。

> [!example] No antiderivative formula is needed · 不必先找到原函数表达式
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the continuous function $f(t)=e^{-t^2}$ and bounds $u(x)=x$, $v(x)=x^2$, the rule gives
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对连续函数 $f(t)=e^{-t^2}$，取 $u(x)=x$、$v(x)=x^2$，得到
>
> $$\frac d{dx}\int_x^{x^2}e^{-t^2}\,dt
> =2xe^{-x^4}-e^{-x^2}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It holds for every real $x$, including $0<x<1$, where $x^2<x$. At $x=0$ the integral is zero, but its derivative is $-1$: at that instant the lower bound moves with speed $1$ and the upper bound has speed $0$. Neither a closed formula for the integral nor an extra sign correction for reversed bounds is needed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 它对每个实数 $x$ 都成立，包括 $x^2<x$ 的 $0<x<1$。在 $x=0$ 处，积分值为零，导数却为 $-1$：此时下限的变化率为 $1$，上限的变化率为 $0$。既不必先算出积分的闭式表达式，也不应因上下限反向再额外添一个负号。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Check the whole interval and the source of the change
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*检查整个积分区间，以及变化来自哪里*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $f$ is initially given only on a finite closed interval $[L,R]$, continuity there and differentiable bounds that stay in $[L,R]$ still suffice. Extend $f$ constantly beyond each endpoint; this produces a continuous function on the real line without changing any of the required integrals, so the proof above applies. At an endpoint of the **external input domain**, a derivative is instead interpreted from the allowed side unless a two-sided extension is given.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果 $f$ 原本只定义在有限闭区间 $[L,R]$ 上，只要它在那里连续，且可导的上下限始终留在 $[L,R]$ 内，同样足够。把 $f$ 在区间两端之外分别延伸为端点常值，就得到实线上的连续函数，又不会改变任何实际使用的积分，因此仍可套用上述证明。若到达的是**外部输入定义域**的端点，则在没有双侧延伸时，应把导数理解为允许方向的单侧导数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A singularity inside the integration interval is a different issue. For instance, $\int_{-1}^{x}dt/t$ with $x>0$ is not an ordinary Riemann integral across zero. Evaluating an endpoint formula cannot repair that missing integral. Continuity is a convenient sufficient hypothesis here; when it fails, the displayed theorem alone gives no conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

积分区间内部出现奇点，是另一回事。例如 $x>0$ 时，$\int_{-1}^{x}dt/t$ 不能作为跨过零点的普通黎曼积分使用；代入端点公式不能弥补积分本身不存在的问题。这里的连续性是一组方便的充分条件；条件不成立时，不能只靠本定理下结论。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The integrand must also be fixed as $x$ changes. For $K(x)=\int_0^x xt\,dt=x^3/2$, the derivative is $3x^2/2$. Counting only the moving upper end would give $x^2$, missing the change of the integrand throughout the interval. Similarly, $\int_0^x e^{xt}\,dt$ has internal parameter dependence and is outside this fixed-integrand rule. Differentiation under an integral with a parameter requires additional hypotheses and a contribution from that internal change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $x$ 改变时，被积函数本身还必须保持固定。例如 $K(x)=\int_0^x xt\,dt=x^3/2$ 的导数为 $3x^2/2$；若只计算移动上端点的贡献，会得到 $x^2$，漏掉整个积分区间内被积函数随 $x$ 改变的部分。同样，$\int_0^x e^{xt}\,dt$ 含有内部参数依赖，不属于本卡的固定被积函数规则。含参数的积分求导需要另查条件，并计入内部变化的贡献。
