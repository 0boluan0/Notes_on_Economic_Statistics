# 连续被积函数的变限积分求导等于上端贡献减去下端贡献
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Differentiating an integral with variable bounds gives the upper-end contribution minus the lower-end contribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f$ 在区间 $I$ 连续，$u(x),v(x)$ 可导且取值在 $I$ 内。则
$$\frac d{dx}\int_{u(x)}^{v(x)}f(t)\,dt
=f(v(x))v'(x)-f(u(x))u'(x).$$
先把上、下限分别代入被积函数，再乘各自的变化率；下限项带负号。积分经过的整段都必须位于 $f$ 的连续区间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Evaluate the continuous integrand at each moving endpoint and multiply by that endpoint's derivative, subtracting the lower contribution. Both bounds and the entire intervening interval must remain in the integrand's continuity interval.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

推导只用两项已有知识。取 $G(s)=\int_a^sf(t)dt$，区间可加性把原式写成 $G(v(x))-G(u(x))$；[[积分求导定理]]给 $G'=f$，[[链式法则]]再产生 $v',u'$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the integral as the difference of two evaluations of one accumulation function. The accumulation theorem supplies its derivative, and the chain rule supplies both endpoint derivatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如
$$\frac d{dx}\int_x^{x^2}e^{-t^2}dt
=2xe^{-x^4}-e^{-x^2}.$$
它对所有实数成立，即使 $x^2<x$，有向积分已经处理了端点顺序。如果被积函数本身也含外部参数，如 $\int_0^x e^{xt}dt$，这里的固定 $f(t)$ 公式就不够：还要考虑内部函数随 $x$ 改变的贡献。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The example holds for all real inputs, including reversed endpoint order. If the integrand itself depends on the external parameter, the fixed-integrand rule is insufficient: its internal parameter dependence also contributes to the derivative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
