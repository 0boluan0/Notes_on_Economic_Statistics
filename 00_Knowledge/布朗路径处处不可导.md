# Nowhere differentiability of Brownian paths
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*布朗路径处处不可导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[标准布朗运动|standard Brownian motion]] moves continuously, yet its position has no finite instantaneous velocity at any time, with probability one. Even after choosing one complete [[样本路径|sample path]], zooming in does not reveal a tangent with a finite slope. This is a stronger form of [[连续不必可导|continuity without differentiability]] than a graph with just one corner.

A [[导数|derivative]] at time $t$ would require the average velocities
$$\frac{W_{t+h}-W_t}{h}$$
to approach one finite number as the nonzero time step $h$ shrinks. Continuity makes the numerator small; it does not control how fast it shrinks relative to $h$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[标准布朗运动]]的运动连续，但以概率一，它在任何时刻都没有有限的瞬时速度。即使选定了一整条[[样本路径]]，不断放大也不会出现具有有限斜率的切线。这比只有一个尖角的图像更强，是[[连续不必可导]]的路径例子。

时刻 $t$ 存在[[导数]]，要求上面的平均速度在非零时间步长 $h$ 缩小时趋近同一个有限数。连续性只使分子变小，没有规定它相对于分母 $h$ 缩小得多快。

> [!important] One event, every time · 同一事件中的所有时刻
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For standard Brownian motion $W$,
> $$
> P\!\left(\forall t\ge0:
> \limsup_{\substack{h\to0\\h\ne0,\ t+h\ge0}}
> \left|\frac{W_{t+h}-W_t}{h}\right|=\infty\right)=1.
> $$
> The infinite upper limit means that arbitrarily close to each time there are slopes of arbitrarily large magnitude. It does **not** say that every sequence of slopes tends to infinity. At $t=0$, only $h>0$ is allowed. Since a finite derivative would make all sufficiently local slopes bounded, this rules out a finite derivative at every time.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上式表示：在同一个概率一事件中，对每个 $t\ge0$，任意近的尺度上都能找到绝对值任意大的差商。上极限无穷并不表示每一列差商都趋于无穷。在 $t=0$ 只能取 $h>0$。若有限导数存在，充分近处的差商就必须有界，所以此结论排除了每个时刻的有限导数。

## Why the order of quantifiers matters
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 量词不能交换

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed deterministic time $t$, a probability-one assertion may discard an exceptional set depending on $t$. The theorem instead discards **one** probability-zero set of outcomes and controls **all** times on each remaining path. An uncountable union of null events need not be null.

For example, let $U$ be [[连续均匀分布|uniform]] on $(0,1)$. For each fixed $t$, $P(U=t)=0$, but every outcome has some time $t=U$. Thus statements made separately at each deterministic time cannot settle what happens at a time selected by the path. The same issue appears in [[有限维分布不定路径正则性|finite-dimensional distributions and path regularity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定一个确定时刻 $t$ 时，概率一结论可以排除一个随 $t$ 改变的例外集。这里的定理却只排除**同一个**概率零的结果集合，然后控制余下每条路径上的**全部**时刻。不可数个零概率事件的并集未必仍是零概率。

例如令 $U$ 在 $(0,1)$ 上服从[[连续均匀分布]]。对每个固定 $t$，都有 $P(U=t)=0$，但每个结果都存在一个时刻 $t=U$。所以逐个确定时刻的陈述，不能直接决定路径选出来的时刻会发生什么。[[有限维分布不定路径正则性]]中也会遇到相同的量词问题。

## What square-root scaling explains
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 平方根尺度只提供直觉

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At a fixed time and for $h>0$, $W_{t+h}-W_t$ has law $N(0,h)$, so its standard deviation is $\sqrt h$. The difference quotient therefore has law $N(0,1/h)$ and standard deviation $1/\sqrt h$. For $h=0.01$ these two standard deviations are $0.1$ and $10$; for $h=0.0001$ they are $0.01$ and $100$.

The displacements shrink while slopes spread out. This explains how continuity can coexist with roughness. These are distributional scales, not a deterministic size for every increment or a proof about all times simultaneously. A drawn simulation joins finitely many values by straight segments; the slopes of those segments are properties of that interpolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在固定时刻取 $h>0$，增量 $W_{t+h}-W_t$ 服从 $N(0,h)$，标准差是 $\sqrt h$。差商因而服从 $N(0,1/h)$，标准差为 $1/\sqrt h$。当 $h=0.01$ 时，这两个标准差分别为 $0.1$ 与 $10$；当 $h=0.0001$ 时，分别为 $0.01$ 与 $100$。

位移缩小，斜率的分布却更分散，这解释了连续与粗糙如何并存。但这些是分布尺度，不是每个增量的确定大小，也不是同时控制所有时刻的证明。模拟图通常用直线连接有限个采样值；那些线段的斜率属于插值图像。

> [!note]- How the proof covers every time · 证明怎样覆盖所有时刻
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here is the counting argument behind the simultaneous statement. Fix positive integers $M,T$. Suppose some $t\in[0,T]$ has a neighborhood in which
> $$|W_s-W_t|\le M|s-t|.$$
> For a sufficiently large integer $n$, let $k=\lfloor nt\rfloor$ and take the three consecutive grid increments between $(k+1)/n,(k+2)/n,(k+3)/n,(k+4)/n$. All four times are on the right of $t$ and within $4/n$ of it. The triangle inequality bounds the magnitude of each increment by $7M/n$, including when $t=0$.
>
> For any fixed $k$, these three increments are independent $N(0,1/n)$ variables. A standard normal density is at most $1/\sqrt{2\pi}$, so
> $$P(|N(0,1/n)|\le7M/n)\le\frac{14M}{\sqrt{2\pi n}}.$$
> There are at most $\lfloor nT\rfloor+1$ possible values of $k$. By the union bound, the probability $P(B_n)$ that any such block has all three increments this small satisfies
> $$P(B_n)\le(\lfloor nT\rfloor+1)
> \left(\frac{14M}{\sqrt{2\pi n}}\right)^3\longrightarrow0.$$
> A path with the proposed local slope bound would belong to $B_n$ for every sufficiently large $n$. That event has probability zero: for each $N$, $\bigcap_{n\ge N}B_n$ has probability at most $\inf_{n\ge N}P(B_n)=0$, and there are only countably many $N$. Finally take the countable union over integers $M,T$. Any finite local upper limit would supply some such $M,T$, so almost surely none exists anywhere.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 下面是同时控制所有时刻的计数思路。固定正整数 $M,T$。假设某个 $t\in[0,T]$ 在一个邻域内满足 $|W_s-W_t|\le M|s-t|$。对充分大的整数 $n$，取 $k=\lfloor nt\rfloor$，考察从 $(k+1)/n$ 到 $(k+4)/n$ 的三段相邻网格增量。四个端点都在 $t$ 的右侧且相距不超过 $4/n$，三角不等式使每段增量的绝对值不超过 $7M/n$；这个安排也覆盖 $t=0$。
>
> 固定 $k$ 时，这三个增量独立且服从 $N(0,1/n)$。标准正态密度不超过 $1/\sqrt{2\pi}$，因此每个增量落在上述小区间内的概率满足第一个界。可能的 $k$ 至多有 $\lfloor nT\rfloor+1$ 个，用并集概率上界得到 $P(B_n)$ 的第二个界，它趋于零。
>
> 若一条路径真的存在局部斜率界，它对所有充分大的 $n$ 都必须属于 $B_n$。对每个 $N$，事件 $\bigcap_{n\ge N}B_n$ 的概率至多为 $\inf_{n\ge N}P(B_n)=0$；再对可数个 $N$ 取并仍是零。最后对正整数 $M,T$ 取可数并。任何有限的局部上极限都会被某组 $M,T$ 覆盖，所以几乎必然任何时刻都不存在这样的有限上极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This theorem also connects to [[布朗路径无限总变差|infinite total variation of Brownian paths]]. The bridge is a separate real-analysis fact: [[有界变差函数几乎处处可导|bounded-variation functions are differentiable at almost every time]]. A nowhere differentiable path on a positive-length interval cannot satisfy that property. The linked Brownian variation card also gives a direct proof from quadratic variation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本定理也通向[[布朗路径无限总变差]]，其中需要一条独立的实分析桥梁：[[有界变差函数几乎处处可导]]。一条在正长度区间上处处不可导的路径不可能满足那项性质。布朗总变差卡还给出从二次变差直接出发的证明。
