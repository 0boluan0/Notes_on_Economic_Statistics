# 曲线描绘把定义域、极限、导数符号和凹凸性合成为定性正确的函数图像
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Curve sketching combines domain, limits, derivative signs, and concavity into a qualitatively correct graph*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子方法
> 一张可信的定性图按以下顺序建立：先定定义域、对称性、截距、端点与渐近线；再列临界点和不可导分界；随后在各连通区间判断 $f'$、$f''$ 的符号；最后标出极值、拐点和端点行为并检查这些信息能否同时成立。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A reliable qualitative graph begins with domain, symmetry, intercepts, endpoints, and asymptotes. It then lists critical and nondifferentiable separators, determines the signs of $f'$ and $f''$ on each connected interval, and finally marks extrema, inflection points, and endpoint behavior while checking that all features are mutually consistent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

顺序很重要。对 $f(x)=x/\ln x$，$x>0$ 且 $x\ne1$ 先把定义域拆成两段；$x=1$ 是竖直渐近线而不是临界点。随后
$$
f'(x)=\frac{\ln x-1}{(\ln x)^2}
$$
只在 $x=e$ 为零，于是符号表给出右支先降后升。若先机械解 $f'=0$ 而忽略定义域，就无法画出两条分离分支。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Order matters. For $f(x)=x/\ln x$, the domain $x>0$, $x\ne1$ first splits the graph into two branches; $x=1$ is a vertical asymptote, not a critical point. Only then does the derivative identify $x=e$ and its sign chart. Solving $f'=0$ before examining the domain misses the global structure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

图像不要求按比例精确，但每个标记要受证据支持。$f''(c)=0$ 只给 [[拐点]]候选，$f'(c)=0$ 只给极值候选；水平或竖直渐近线也不决定函数从哪一侧靠近。最后用几个易算点、单位或原函数符号做一致性检查，而不是用绘图软件替代论证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sketch need not be to scale, but every marked feature needs a reason. Zeros of the second derivative merely nominate inflection candidates, and zeros of the first derivative merely nominate extremum candidates. An asymptote does not specify the side of approach. Sample values and sign checks validate the synthesis; plotting software does not replace it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么间断点要在导数符号表之前处理？
>
> **答案：** 它们先把定义域拆成不能跨越的连通区间；跨间断比较导数符号不能建立一条连续分支的单调性或凹凸性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why should discontinuities be examined before constructing a derivative sign chart?
>
> **Answer:** They divide the analysis into connected intervals. Comparing derivative signs across a discontinuity does not establish the monotonicity or concavity of one continuous branch.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
