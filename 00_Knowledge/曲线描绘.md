# Curve sketching
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*曲线描绘*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Curve sketching** reconstructs the main shape of a function from facts that the graph must satisfy: where it exists, where it rises or falls, how it bends, and what happens near its boundaries. The purpose is a justified qualitative picture, not a drawing to exact scale. A few plotted points can help, but cannot by themselves rule out a turn or a break between them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**曲线描绘**根据图像必须满足的事实还原其主要形状：它在哪里有定义、哪里上升或下降、怎样弯曲，以及靠近边界时如何变化。目标是一张有理由支持的定性图，不是严格按比例的绘图。几个取样点能辅助判断，却不能单独排除点与点之间的转折或断开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[导数|first derivative]] $f'(x)$ measures the slope. The [[导数单调性判据|derivative sign criterion]] turns its sign on an entire interval into increase or decrease. The [[导数判凹凸|second-derivative criterion]] concerns how the slopes change: $f''>0$ means convex, or concave up; $f''<0$ means concave, or concave down. Rising and bending upwards are different properties.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[导数|一阶导数]] $f'(x)$ 衡量斜率。[[导数单调性判据]]把它在整个区间上的符号转成递增或递减。[[导数判凹凸|二阶导数判据]]关心斜率如何变化：$f''>0$ 表示凸，即 concave up；$f''<0$ 表示凹，即 concave down。上升与向上弯是两种不同性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Start with the allowed inputs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先确定哪些输入合法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The domain is the set of inputs for which the original formula is defined. Write it before differentiating; cancellation must not silently fill a missing point. Record any included endpoints, intercepts and useful symmetry. Then examine [[函数极限|limits]] at excluded boundaries and at each unbounded end. A finite boundary limit gives a possible open endpoint, while the appropriate infinite limit can identify an [[渐近线|asymptote]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义域是原式有定义的输入集合。求导前先写清定义域；约分不能悄悄补上缺失的点。再记录实际包含的端点、截距和有用的对称性，然后检查排除的边界及各个无界方向的[[函数极限|极限]]。有限边界极限可以给出一个空心端点，相应的无穷极限则可能确定[[渐近线]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[临界点|critical input]] is an interior input in the domain where the derivative is zero or fails to exist. An excluded boundary can split the sign analysis without being a critical point. Included endpoints are checked separately. These distinctions prevent a sketch from connecting branches through a forbidden input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[临界点]]要求输入是定义域内部的点，且导数为零或不存在。被排除的边界也会分割符号分析，却不是临界点。实际包含的端点另行检查。这些区别防止把两条分支跨过禁止的输入连在一起。

## Follow one complete example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*沿着一个完整例子作图*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $f(x)=x/\ln x$, where the [[自然对数|natural logarithm]] is defined for $x>0$ and vanishes at $x=1$. The domain is $(0,1)\cup(1,\infty)$. Thus there are two branches to analyse, and neither zero nor one belongs to the graph. The function is negative on the first branch and positive on the second; it has no intercept on either coordinate axis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $f(x)=x/\ln x$。[[自然对数]]要求 $x>0$，且在 $x=1$ 为零，所以定义域是 $(0,1)\cup(1,\infty)$。要分析两条分支，零和一都不属于图像。第一条分支的函数值为负，第二条为正；图像与两条坐标轴都没有交点。

$$\lim_{x\to0^+}f(x)=0,\qquad
\lim_{x\to1^-}f(x)=-\infty,\qquad
\lim_{x\to1^+}f(x)=+\infty,\qquad
\lim_{x\to+\infty}f(x)=+\infty.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Near zero, $\ln x<-1$ eventually, so $|x/\ln x|\le x\to0$, with the values below zero. Near one, the numerator tends to one while the denominator tends to zero with the indicated side's sign. At infinity, [[洛必达法则|L’Hôpital’s rule]] applies to $\ln x/x$: both numerator and denominator tend to infinity, the denominator derivative is one, and the derivative ratio $1/x$ tends to zero. Since $\ln x/x>0$ eventually, its reciprocal tends to $+\infty$. This proves the last limit; one very large numerical value would not prove it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

靠近零时，最终有 $\ln x<-1$，所以 $|x/\ln x|\le x\to0$，且函数值从下方接近零。靠近一时，分子趋于一，分母带着对应侧的符号趋零。在正无穷处，对 $\ln x/x$ 使用[[洛必达法则]]：分子分母都趋于无穷，分母导数为一，导数比 $1/x$ 趋零。由于 $\ln x/x$ 最终为正，其倒数趋于 $+\infty$。这就证明了最后一个极限，不能只用某个很大的数值代替证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently, draw an open limiting point at $(0,0)$ and the vertical asymptote $x=1$. There is no horizontal asymptote at infinity. There is no sloping affine asymptote either: $f(x)/x=1/\ln x\to0$ would force its slope to be zero, whereas $f(x)\to+\infty$ rules out a finite intercept.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，原点画为空心的极限位置，并画出竖直渐近线 $x=1$。无穷远处没有水平渐近线，也没有斜的仿射渐近线：$f(x)/x=1/\ln x\to0$ 会迫使候选直线的斜率为零，但 $f(x)\to+\infty$ 又排除了有限截距。

## Use signs to determine motion and bending
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用符号确定增减与弯曲*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Apply the [[商法则|quotient rule]] and [[对数函数求导|derivative of the logarithm]] on the domain. Differentiating again, or first rewriting $f'=(\ln x)^{-1}-(\ln x)^{-2}$, gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在定义域内使用[[商法则]]与[[对数函数求导]]。再求导一次，也可以先改写 $f'=(\ln x)^{-1}-(\ln x)^{-2}$，得到

$$f'(x)=\frac{\ln x-1}{(\ln x)^2},\qquad
f''(x)=\frac{2-\ln x}{x(\ln x)^3}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first denominator is positive, so the first derivative changes sign only at $x=e$. For the second derivative, the numerator changes sign at $e^2$ and the denominator changes sign across the excluded input one. Here $e$ is the base of the natural exponential, with $\ln e=1$. These formulas establish signs on whole intervals, not just at sample points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一阶导数的分母为正，所以它只在 $x=e$ 改变符号。二阶导数的分子在 $e^2$ 变号，分母跨过被排除的一时变号。这里 $e$ 是自然指数的底数，满足 $\ln e=1$。这些公式保证了整个区间上的符号，并不只检查某个取样点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Interval | $f'$ | $f''$ | Shape |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $(0,1)$ | $-$ | $-$ | Decreasing, concave down |
| $(1,e)$ | $-$ | $+$ | Decreasing, concave up |
| $(e,e^2)$ | $+$ | $+$ | Increasing, concave up |
| $(e^2,\infty)$ | $+$ | $-$ | Increasing, concave down |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

左支始终递减且向下弯；右支先递减、后递增，弯曲方向则到 $e^2$ 才从向上弯变为向下弯。

> [!example] Read the sign chart as one picture · 把符号表读成一张图
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The left branch starts just below the open origin and falls to $-\infty$ as $x\to1^-$. The right branch falls from $+\infty$ to $(e,e)$, then rises without bound. By the [[一阶导数检验|first derivative test]], $(e,e)$ is a strict local minimum and the minimum of the entire right branch. It is not a minimum of the whole domain, whose left branch is unbounded below. At $(e^2,e^2/2)$ the right branch is still rising, but changes from concave up to concave down: this is its [[拐点|inflection point]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 左支从空心原点的下方开始，随着 $x\to1^-$ 下降到 $-\infty$。右支从 $+\infty$ 下降到 $(e,e)$，然后无界上升。由[[一阶导数检验]]，$(e,e)$ 是严格局部最小点，也是整条右支的最小点；它不是全定义域的最小点，因为左支向下无界。在 $(e^2,e^2/2)$，右支仍在上升，但由向上弯改成向下弯，因此这里是[[拐点]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Across $x=1$, the second derivative also changes sign, but no continuous graph point exists there, so this is not another inflection point. Likewise, $f'(x)\to0$ as $x\to0^+$ describes a boundary slope limit; it does not make zero a critical point or assign a value $f(0)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

跨过 $x=1$ 时二阶导数也变号，但那里没有连续的图像点，所以不是另一个拐点。同样，$x\to0^+$ 时 $f'(x)\to0$ 描述的是边界处的斜率极限，不会让零成为临界点，也不会自动赋予 $f(0)$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Finish by checking compatibility
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*最后检查这些信息能否同时成立*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check the proposed drawing against the original function's sign, every included or excluded point, and both derivative sign charts. A graph can rise while its slope decreases, as this example does after $e^2$. A stationary point need not be an extremum, and a zero second derivative need not give an inflection. Any such label requires its own test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把画出的图与原函数的正负、每个实际包含或排除的点、两张导数符号表逐项对照。曲线可以一边上升一边斜率减小，本例在 $e^2$ 之后正是如此。驻点未必是极值点，二阶导数为零也未必有拐点；每个标记都要经过相应检验。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On a closed bounded interval, the [[闭区间极值法|closed-interval method]] adds endpoint comparisons when an absolute extremum is requested. For a qualitative sketch, compute the information needed to settle the requested shape; if inflections are part of the question, the concavity analysis must be supplied. A plot or a few easy values are useful consistency checks, while the interval arguments justify the shape.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在闭有界区间上，如果题目要求绝对极值，[[闭区间极值法]]还要比较端点值。定性作图应计算足以确定所问形状的信息；若题目要求拐点，就必须给出凹凸分析。绘图或几个易算点可以检查一致性，区间论证才为图像提供理由。
