# 标准误估计统计量在重复抽样中的标准差，而残差标准差估计观测噪声尺度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A standard error estimates the repeated-sampling standard deviation of a statistic, whereas a residual standard deviation estimates the scale of observation noise*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> 若从同一数据生成过程反复抽样，每次得到的统计量 $T$ 会不同。standard error 估计这种抽样波动：
> $$
> SE(T)=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(T)},
> \qquad
> \widehat{SE}(T)=\sqrt{\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(T)}.
> $$
> residual standard deviation 则描述单个观测围绕模型拟合值的典型偏离。前者回答“估计量有多精确”，后者回答“观测本身有多分散”。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Across repeated samples from the same data-generating process, a statistic $T$ changes. Its standard error estimates that sampling variation. A residual standard deviation instead measures the typical deviation of an observation from its fitted value. The former concerns estimator precision; the latter concerns observation-level noise.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 两个对象不能互换
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The two objects are not interchangeable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在满秩的经典同方差线性回归中，残差方差通常由

$$
\hat\sigma^2=\frac{RSS}{n-p}
$$

估计，其中 $p=\operatorname{rank}(X)$，在满列秩时等于估计参数个数；$\hat\sigma$ 与结果变量 $Y$ 同单位。系数 $\hat\beta_j$ 的标准误则来自系数协方差矩阵的对角元：

$$
se(\hat\beta_j)
=\sqrt{\left[\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(\hat\beta)\right]_{jj}}.
$$

它与 $\hat\beta_j$ 同单位，例如“工资单位／教育年”。残差标准差只是计算经典系数标准误的一个输入；设计矩阵提供的信息量、有效样本量和采用的协方差估计同样会改变最终标准误。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a full-rank classical homoskedastic linear regression, $\hat\sigma=\sqrt{RSS/(n-p)}$, with $p=\operatorname{rank}(X)$, has the same units as the response. The standard error of $\hat\beta_j$ is the square root of the corresponding diagonal element of the estimated coefficient covariance matrix and has the same units as that coefficient. Residual variation is therefore one input to a classical coefficient standard error, not the standard error itself; design information, effective sample size, and the covariance estimator also matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这一区分解释了为什么两个量可以朝不同方向变化。在噪声尺度近似不变、设计同样有信息的情形下，增加独立观测通常会降低系数标准误，却不会要求残差标准差同步下降。反过来，残差很小也不能保证某个系数估得精确：若解释变量几乎没有独立变化，系数标准误仍可能很大。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two quantities can move differently. With roughly unchanged noise and an equally informative design, more independent observations usually reduce coefficient standard errors without requiring a smaller residual standard deviation. Conversely, small residuals do not guarantee a precise coefficient when the relevant regressor supplies little independent variation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

标准误描述抽样精度，不判断系数是否识别了目标参数，也不证明模型正确。一个很小但口径错误的标准误仍会给出失真的推断；协方差估计应怎样匹配数据结构由 [[标准误口径匹配]] 单独处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A standard error describes sampling precision rather than identification or model correctness. A small standard error computed under the wrong covariance structure can still support invalid inference; matching the covariance estimator to the data structure is handled separately in [[标准误口径匹配|covariance-estimator matching]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两个研究使用相似的变量分布和数据生成过程。研究 B 的独立样本量约为研究 A 的四倍，但两者 residual standard deviation 几乎相同。B 的回归系数 standard error 能否约为 A 的一半？这是否与 residual standard deviation 相同相矛盾？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Two studies have similar designs and data-generating processes. Study B has about four times as many independent observations as study A, while their residual standard deviations are nearly equal. Can B's coefficient standard error be about half of A's, and would that contradict the equal residual standard deviations?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 可以，也不矛盾。在规则条件下，系数标准误常随独立信息量按约 $1/\sqrt n$ 缩小；四倍样本量可使它约减半。residual standard deviation 仍在描述单个观测的噪声尺度，两个研究可以相近。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** Yes. Under regular conditions, coefficient standard errors often shrink approximately as $1/\sqrt n$, so four times as much independent information can halve the standard error. The residual standard deviation still describes observation-level noise and may remain nearly unchanged.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
