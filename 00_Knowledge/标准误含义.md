# Standard error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*标准误的含义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An average calculated from a sample would usually change if we drew another sample. Its **standard error** describes the size of that sampling fluctuation. More generally, a standard error is the [[标准差|standard deviation]] of a [[统计量|statistic]] under its [[抽样分布|sampling distribution]]. It measures the spread of repeated estimates, rather than the spread of individual observations inside one dataset.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从样本算出的平均数，换一份样本通常会变。**标准误**描述这种抽样波动的大小。一般地，它是[[统计量]]在[[抽样分布]]下的[[标准差]]：衡量反复抽样得到的估计值有多分散，不是衡量同一份数据里的各个观察有多分散。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real-valued statistic $T$ with finite variance, write
$$SE(T)=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(T)}.$$
Here the variance refers to newly generated samples under the stated model. If we condition on a fixed regression design $X$, the corresponding definition is $SE(T\mid X)=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(T\mid X)}$. The conditioning and sampling mechanism are part of the question; changing them can change the answer. If the variance is not finite, there is no finite standard error of this kind.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实值统计量 $T$ 方差有限时，标准误是上述方差的平方根。这里的方差针对明示模型下重新生成的样本；若固定回归设计 $X$ 作条件分析，就使用 $\operatorname{Var}(T\mid X)$。抽样机制与条件口径是问题的一部分，改变它们可能改变答案。方差不有限时，这种意义下没有有限标准误。

> [!example] Observations fluctuate more than their average · 观察值与均值的尺度不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Draw twice independently with replacement from $0,2,4,6$, each with probability $1/4$. The population mean is $3$ and an individual draw has variance
> $$\sigma^2=\frac{(0-3)^2+(2-3)^2+(4-3)^2+(6-3)^2}{4}=5.$$
> The [[抽样分布|distribution of the two-draw average]] has variance $5/2$, so
> $$SD(X_i)=\sqrt5\approx2.24,\qquad SE(\bar X_2)=\sqrt{5/2}\approx1.58.$$
> The first describes individual draws around $3$; the second describes sample averages around $3$. Both are in the original measurement units. Their difference is about which random object is being measured.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $0,2,4,6$ 等概率、放回独立抽两次，总体均值为 $3$，单次观察方差为 $5$，[[抽样分布|两次均值的分布]]方差为 $5/2$。因此单次观察标准差约为 $2.24$，均值标准误约为 $1.58$。前者描述每个观察围绕 $3$ 的波动，后者描述不同样本平均数围绕 $3$ 的波动；二者都用原测量单位，但衡量的随机对象不同。

## The true standard error and its estimate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*真实标准误与从本次数据估出来的标准误*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In that teaching example the whole population law is given, so the standard error can be calculated exactly. Usually it depends on unknown quantities. Software then reports an **estimated** standard error,
$$\widehat{SE}(T)=\sqrt{\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(T)},$$
where the hat marks estimation. The estimate itself varies from sample to sample. Many books and software outputs call both quantities “standard error”; check whether the formula uses true parameters or quantities estimated from the data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

教学例子给出了整个总体分布，所以可以精确计算。现实中标准误通常依赖未知量，软件于是报告上式的**估计标准误**；帽子表示估计，这个估计值本身也随样本变化。许多书和软件把真实量与估计量都称为 standard error，要看公式使用的是真实参数还是样本估计值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n\ge2$ [[独立同分布|iid]] observations with finite variance $\sigma^2$, the sample mean has $SE(\bar X_n)=\sigma/\sqrt n$. A common estimate replaces $\sigma$ by the sample standard deviation $s$:
$$s=\sqrt{\frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\bar X_n)^2},\qquad \widehat{SE}(\bar X_n)=\frac{s}{\sqrt n}.$$
The $n-1$ denominator gives the usual [[样本协方差无偏分母|unbiased variance estimate]] under these iid conditions. For the observed pair $(0,2)$, $\bar x_2=1$, $s^2=2$, and the estimated standard error is $1$, while the true value in the known population example is $\sqrt{5/2}$. One sample need not recover the exact sampling spread. Neither $s$ nor the estimated standard error is automatically unbiased just because the usual $s^2$ is unbiased for $\sigma^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $n\ge2$ 个方差有限的 [[独立同分布|iid]] 观察，均值标准误为 $\sigma/\sqrt n$，常用上式样本标准差 $s$ 替换未知 $\sigma$；在这些 iid 条件下，分母 $n-1$ 给出通常的[[样本协方差无偏分母|无偏方差估计]]。若这次观察为 $(0,2)$，则均值为 $1$、$s^2=2$，估计标准误为 $1$；已知总体下真实值却是 $\sqrt{5/2}$。一份样本不必恰好恢复真实抽样尺度。即使通常的 $s^2$ 对 $\sigma^2$ 无偏，也不能自动推出开平方后的 $s$ 或估计标准误无偏。

## A residual standard deviation measures a different object
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 两个对象不能互换

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the linear model $y=X\beta+u$, $y$ contains $n$ outcomes, the columns of $X$ specify the predictors, and $u$ is the unobserved model error. [[普通最小二乘|OLS]] fits coefficients $\hat\beta$ by minimizing the sum of squared [[回归残差|residuals]] $\hat u=y-X\hat\beta$. Take fixed full-column-rank $X$, correct zero error mean, and $\operatorname{Var}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2I_n$: all model errors have the same finite variance and different errors are uncorrelated. Put $p=\operatorname{rank}(X)$, including the intercept if one is fitted, and require $n>p$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在线性模型 $y=X\beta+u$ 中，$y$ 含 $n$ 个结果，$X$ 的列规定解释变量，$u$ 是未观察到的模型误差。[[普通最小二乘|OLS]]通过最小化[[回归残差]] $\hat u=y-X\hat\beta$ 的平方和拟合系数。这里固定满列秩的 $X$，假设正确的零误差均值及 $\operatorname{Var}(u\mid X)=\sigma^2I_n$：模型误差方差相同且有限，不同误差互不相关。令 $p=\operatorname{rank}(X)$，若有截距也计入，并要求 $n>p$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The residual sum of squares is $RSS=\sum_i\hat u_i^2$. The residual standard deviation, also often called the residual standard error, is
$$\hat\sigma=\sqrt{\frac{RSS}{n-p}}.$$
It estimates the model-error scale $\sigma$ in outcome units. The [[OLS经典协方差估计|classical coefficient covariance estimate]] is instead
$$\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(\hat\beta\mid X)=\hat\sigma^2(X^TX)^{-1},\qquad
\widehat{SE}(\hat\beta_j\mid X)=\sqrt{\left[\widehat{\operatorname{Var}}(\hat\beta\mid X)\right]_{jj}}.$$
The brackets select the $j$th diagonal element: the estimated variance of coefficient $j$. Its square root has the coefficient's units, such as wage units per year of education. Thus $\hat\sigma$ is only one input; the design also controls how well a coefficient can be distinguished.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

残差平方和为 $RSS=\sum_i\hat u_i^2$，上式 $\hat\sigma$ 叫残差标准差，也常叫 residual standard error。它估计模型误差尺度 $\sigma$，单位与结果变量相同。系数标准误则来自[[OLS经典协方差估计|系数协方差估计]]的对角元平方根；$[\cdot]_{jj}$ 选取第 $j$ 个系数的估计方差。系数标准误与该系数同单位，例如“工资单位／教育年”。所以残差标准差只是一个输入，设计提供的信息还决定了系数能估得多精确。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Small residuals do not guarantee a small coefficient standard error when predictors have little independent variation. Conversely, with roughly unchanged noise and similarly informative independent observations, increasing the sample size can reduce coefficient standard errors without requiring $\hat\sigma$ to fall. The fitted residuals themselves need not be independent or all have variance $\sigma^2$; $\hat\sigma$ estimates the original model-error scale, not a common marginal variance of every fitted residual.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

解释变量缺少独立变化时，残差很小也不保证系数标准误小。反过来，噪声尺度近似不变、独立观察同样有信息时，增加样本可以减小系数标准误，却不要求残差标准差下降。拟合残差本身也不必独立、每项方差也不必等于 $\sigma^2$；$\hat\sigma$ 估计的是原模型误差尺度，不是每项拟合残差共同的边际方差。

> [!warning] Precision is not accuracy or a model check · 精度不是准确性或模型检验
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A standard error describes spread around the estimator's own mean. It does not measure the bias relative to the target, prove identification, or validate the model. Even a rule that always returns the same wrong number has standard error zero. The iid formula $s/\sqrt n$ and the classical regression formula require their sampling conditions; see [[标准误口径匹配|matching the covariance estimator to the data structure]] for dependence and heteroskedasticity.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 标准误描述估计量围绕自身均值的分散程度，不衡量它偏离目标的偏差，也不证明识别或模型正确。一个总是给出同一错误数值的规则，标准误甚至等于零。$s/\sqrt n$ 与经典回归公式各有抽样条件，相关性与异方差要另按[[标准误口径匹配|数据结构选择协方差估计]]。

> [!question]- Four times as much independent information · 四倍独立信息量
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If independent observations have the same distribution and finite variance, multiplying $n$ by four halves the sample mean's true standard error while leaving the individual-observation standard deviation unchanged. For regression, the same approximate comparison additionally needs a similarly informative design and stable noise scale. Equal residual standard deviations in two studies therefore do not conflict with different coefficient standard errors.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若独立观察同分布且方差有限，样本量变四倍，均值真实标准误减半，单次观察标准差却不变。回归中使用类似近似，还需设计的信息结构和噪声尺度保持可比。因此两项研究的残差标准差相近，完全可以同时有不同的系数标准误。
