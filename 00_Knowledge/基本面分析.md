# 基本面分析通过经济与经营机制评估资产的现金流、风险和价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fundamental analysis evaluates an asset's cash flows, risk, and value through economic and operating mechanisms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本面分析（fundamental analysis）研究资产背后的经济活动：谁会在何时支付多少现金，这些支付受什么影响，承担相应风险需要什么回报。对公司而言，它把宏观环境、[[行业]]结构与企业经营接到可检验的财务预测，再与价格比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fundamental analysis studies the economic activity behind an asset: who will pay cash, how much and when, what affects those payments, and what return compensates their risk. For a company, it connects macro conditions, [[行业|industry]] structure, and operations to testable financial forecasts and compares the resulting valuation with price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如“电力成本上升”还不是盈利预测。需要接着查：公司每单位产品用多少电，合同何时重定价，能否提价，销量是否受损。只有这些环节明确，才有理由改变[[收入驱动预测|收入假设]]和成本假设。研究也可以从一家公司自下而上开始；宏观—行业—公司是检查层次，不是所有问题唯一的发现顺序。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Electricity costs rise” is not yet an earnings forecast. Check energy use per unit, contract repricing, the ability to raise prices, and the effect on volume before changing [[收入驱动预测|revenue assumptions]] and costs. Research can also begin bottom-up with a firm; the three levels are checks, not a compulsory discovery sequence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

合理的价值估计不保证赚取超额收益：其他人可能已采用同样信息，预测也可能错误。[[反向估值]]帮助明确价格在给定模型下要求什么，[[投资论点核验]]负责以后用事实更新判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A reasonable valuation does not guarantee excess returns: the information may already be reflected in price, and forecasts may be wrong. [[反向估值|Reverse valuation]] identifies what a price requires under a model; [[投资论点核验|thesis verification]] governs later updates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
