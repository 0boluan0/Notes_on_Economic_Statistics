# 广告份额竞赛是参与者以连续投入按相对份额争夺固定价值、且各自承担投入成本的 Tullock 彩票竞赛
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An advertising-share contest is a Tullock lottery contest in which players make continuous costly investments to compete for a fixed value according to their relative shares*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**广告份额竞赛**把“广告越多，赢得顾客的机会越大”简化成一个相对投入模型。两个参与者同时选择 $x_i\ge 0$；奖品或市场的共同价值为 $v>0$。若至少一方投入为正，参与者 $i$ 获胜或取得市场的概率为

$$
p_i(x_i,x_j)=\frac{x_i}{x_i+x_j}.
$$

若双方都不投入，通常规定各以 $1/2$ 的概率获胜。因此期望收益是

$$
u_i(x_i,x_j)=v\,p_i(x_i,x_j)-x_i.
$$
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The model lets two players choose nonnegative efforts simultaneously for a prize worth $v$. Positive efforts determine success probabilities in proportion to relative effort, and both efforts are paid. A fair tie rule assigns probability one half to each player when both choose zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 它描述的是概率份额，不是确定性最高价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*It Models Probabilistic Shares, Not a Deterministic Highest Bid*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只要 $x_i>0$，即使投入低于对手，参与者仍有正概率获胜。这一点把它与[[全支付竞赛]]区分开来：全支付竞赛也是人人付出成本，但通常由最高投入者确定性获胜。两种模型的收益不连续性、最佳反应和均衡形式因此完全不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A lower positive effort still wins with positive probability. This distinguishes the lottery contest from an all-pay contest, where the highest bid wins deterministically even though everyone pays. Their best-response and equilibrium structures are consequently different.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 零投入规则不是无关紧要的补丁
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Zero-Effort Rule Is Substantive*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比例式在 $(0,0)$ 没有定义，所以模型必须明说零点怎样处理。采用公平平局规则后，对手投入为零时，自己选择任意很小的正投入都几乎取得全部价值；但连续策略中不存在“最小正数”。这会导致[[广告竞赛反应函数|对零投入没有最佳反应]]，却不会阻止模型拥有一个双方都正投入的均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The proportional rule is undefined at zero–zero, so the model must specify that case. Under a fair tie rule, an arbitrarily small positive effort almost secures the whole prize against zero, but a continuous action space has no smallest positive effort. This creates a missing best response at zero without ruling out a positive-effort equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么不能把 $p_i=x_i/(x_i+x_j)$ 直接代入 $x_i=x_j=0$？
>
> **答案：** 分母为零，模型在该点尚未定义。必须另设平局规则；这个规则还会影响零点附近的最佳反应。
