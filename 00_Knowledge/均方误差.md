# Mean squared error
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*均方误差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An estimate can miss its target by being too high or too low. If we simply average signed errors, these misses can cancel. **Mean squared error (MSE)** first squares each error and then averages over possible samples. It measures how far an estimation rule tends to land from its target, with larger misses receiving a larger penalty.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

估计值可能偏高，也可能偏低。直接平均带符号的误差，会让两种偏离抵消。**均方误差 MSE**先把每次误差平方，再按可能样本取平均，用来衡量估计规则的结果离目标多远；更大的偏离会受到更重的惩罚。

^core

> [!example] Square first, then average · 先平方，再平均
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At one fixed model value, suppose the target is $10$ and an estimator $T$ takes $10,12,6$ with equal probabilities. Its errors are $0,2,-4$, so its squared errors are $0,4,16$. Therefore
> $$\operatorname{MSE}(T)=\frac{0+4+16}{3}=\frac{20}{3}\approx6.67.$$
> The average signed error is $(0+2-4)/3=-2/3$, whose square is only $4/9$. The order of operations matters: the mean of squared errors is not the square of the mean error.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 固定一个模型值，目标为 $10$，估计量 $T$ 等概率取 $10,12,6$。误差是 $0,2,-4$，平方误差为 $0,4,16$，所以 MSE 为 $20/3\approx6.67$。带符号误差的平均为 $-2/3$，再平方却只有 $4/9$。先平方再平均，与先平均再平方，是不同运算。

## The expectation is over the estimator's sampling distribution
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*按估计量的抽样分布取期望*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Separate the [[估计对象三分|target, estimator and observed estimate]]. Let $\theta$ index the data model and let $g(\theta)$ be the finite real target. For an estimator $T$,
$$\operatorname{MSE}_\theta(T;g)=E_\theta[(T-g(\theta))^2].$$
The [[期望|expectation]] uses the [[抽样分布|sampling distribution]] at a fixed $\theta$. It averages over possible datasets, rather than averaging unknown parameter values. If the target is $\theta$ itself, write $E_\theta[(T-\theta)^2]$. State the sample size as well: changing the amount of data may change the rule's MSE.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先区分[[估计对象三分|目标、估计量与估计值]]。$\theta$ 标记数据模型，$g(\theta)$ 是有限实数目标，上式给出估计量 $T$ 的 MSE。[[期望]]按固定 $\theta$ 时的[[抽样分布]]计算，平均的是各种可能数据集，不是各种未知参数值。若目标就是 $\theta$，写成 $E_\theta[(T-\theta)^2]$。还应说明样本量，因为数据量变化可能改变 MSE。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Squared error is nonnegative, so its expectation can be defined as a nonnegative extended value, including $+\infty$. A finite MSE requires $E_\theta[T^2]<\infty$ for a fixed finite target. One finite observed estimate does not imply a finite MSE. A particular sample supplies one squared error only if the true target is also known; it does not reveal the repeated-sampling expectation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平方误差非负，其期望可取非负扩展值，包括 $+\infty$。目标为固定有限数时，MSE 有限要求 $E_\theta[T^2]<\infty$。一次估计值有限，不说明 MSE 有限。某份样本只有在真目标也已知时，才提供一次平方误差；它并没有给出重复抽样的期望。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The units are squared. If the target is measured in kilograms, MSE is measured in kilograms squared. The **root mean squared error**, $\operatorname{RMSE}=\sqrt{\operatorname{MSE}}$, restores kilograms. In the example, RMSE is $\sqrt{20/3}\approx2.58$ in the target's units. Taking a square root does not turn it into an average absolute error; that is a different loss measure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

MSE 的单位是目标单位的平方：若目标用千克计，MSE 用平方千克计。**均方根误差 RMSE**是 $\sqrt{\operatorname{MSE}}$，恢复为千克。本例 RMSE 为 $\sqrt{20/3}\approx2.58$，单位与目标相同。开平方后也不是平均绝对误差，后者使用另一种损失。

## What a comparison of MSEs means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*比较 MSE 时，比较条件也要相同*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
MSE includes both systematic displacement from the target and variation around the estimator's own mean. When second moments are finite, the [[MSE偏差方差分解|bias–variance decomposition]] writes it as squared [[估计量偏差|bias]] plus [[方差|variance]]. The [[标准误含义|standard error]] measures only the square root of the variance; RMSE measures the square root of total squared error relative to the target.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

MSE 同时包含抽样中心与目标之间的系统偏移，以及估计量围绕自身中心的波动。二阶矩有限时，[[MSE偏差方差分解]]把它写成[[估计量偏差|偏差]]平方加[[方差]]。[[标准误含义|标准误]]只对方差开平方；RMSE 则对相对目标的总平方误差开平方。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare rules for the same target, model value, sampling design and sample size. Smaller MSE means smaller expected squared error under those conditions; it need not mean smaller bias, and it need not hold at other parameter values. A rule can perform better near one parameter value and worse elsewhere. Whether squared-error loss fits the application is another question: it penalizes positive and negative errors symmetrically and increases quadratically with their size.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较规则时，应固定相同目标、模型值、抽样设计与样本量。MSE 较小表示在这些条件下期望平方误差较小，不必表示偏差较小，也不保证其他参数值处仍然更优。某规则可以在一个参数区域好、在另一个区域差。平方损失是否适合应用还要另问：它对同等大小的正负误差处罚相同，并按误差大小的平方增加。

> [!note] Estimation MSE and prediction MSE average different errors · 估计误差与预测误差的对象不同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This card concerns error in estimating a fixed target. A prediction MSE instead compares a predicted outcome with an actual outcome and must say whether the outcome is from training data or a new case, and what randomness is averaged. A regression's average squared training residual is not automatically the MSE of a coefficient estimator or its future prediction risk.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 本卡讨论估计固定目标的误差。预测 MSE 比较预测结果与实际结果，还要说明是训练数据还是新样本，以及平均哪些随机性。回归训练残差的平方平均，不自动等于系数估计量的 MSE，也不自动等于未来预测风险。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If MSE tends to zero as $n$ grows, a probability bound gives [[估计量一致性|consistency]]: large errors cannot retain a substantial probability while their squared average vanishes. This is a sufficient condition, not a general equivalence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 MSE 随 $n$ 增长趋于零，概率界可推出[[估计量一致性|一致性]]：平均平方误差消失时，大误差不可能仍以明显概率出现。这是充分条件，不是一般的等价关系。
