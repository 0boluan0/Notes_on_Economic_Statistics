# 均方误差是估计值与目标之间平方误差的重复抽样期望

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mean squared error is the sampling expectation of squared estimation error*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心
> 对固定目标 $\theta$，$\operatorname{MSE}_\theta(T)=E_\theta[(T-\theta)^2]$。它同时惩罚偏离目标与随机波动，单位是目标单位的平方；其平方根为 RMSE，与目标同单位。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> MSE averages squared estimation error under repeated sampling. It accounts for systematic displacement and random variation. Its units are squared; its square root, RMSE, has the target’s units.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
^core

若平方误差等概率为 $0,4,16$，MSE 是 $(0+4+16)/3=20/3$，不是平均误差的平方。存在有限二阶矩时，[[MSE偏差方差分解]]将其写为偏差平方加方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equally likely squared errors 0, 4, and 16 give MSE $20/3$. This is not the square of mean error. With a finite second moment, the [[MSE偏差方差分解|bias–variance decomposition]] separates the two components.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
