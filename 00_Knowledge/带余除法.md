# Division with remainder
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*带余除法*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dividing seventeen objects into groups of five leaves three complete groups and two objects. The division theorem makes that description precise and extends it to every integer input, including negative inputs. It picks one quotient and one remainder by requiring the remainder to be nonnegative and smaller than the positive divisor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把十七个物品按每组五个分组，会得到三组完整的五个，再剩两个。除法定理把这个描述变得精确，并扩展到包括负数在内的每个整数输入。要求余数非负而且严格小于正除数，便选定了一组唯一的商和余数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[整除|Divisibility]] means an exact integer multiple. Division with remainder also handles inputs that are not exact multiples: it writes the gap explicitly instead of changing the divisor or permitting a fractional quotient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[整除]]要求输入恰好是除数的整数倍。带余除法也能处理不是整数倍的输入：它把差额明确写成余数，而不是更改除数，或允许整数商变成分数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an integer $a$ and a positive integer $n$, there is a unique pair of integers $q,r$ satisfying
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定整数 $a$ 和正整数 $n$，存在唯一一对整数 $q,r$ 满足

$$a=qn+r,\qquad 0\le r<n.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $q$ is the quotient and $r$ the remainder. Its range matters: $17=3\cdot5+2$ gives quotient $3$ and remainder $2$. The alternative $17=2\cdot5+7$ does not qualify because $7$ is outside the required range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$q$ 是商，$r$ 是余数。余数的范围是约定的关键：$17=3\cdot5+2$ 给出商 $3$、余数 $2$；虽然也有 $17=2\cdot5+7$，但 $7$ 不在规定范围内。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The input $a$ is the **dividend**, and $n$ is the **divisor**. “Unique pair” fixes both $q$ and $r$ for the given $a,n$; it does not say the same pair works for different inputs. The permitted remainders are the $n$ integers $0,1,\ldots,n-1$. Zero remainder is allowed: $n\mid a$ holds exactly when the theorem returns $r=0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

输入 $a$ 叫**被除数**，$n$ 叫**除数**。“唯一一对”是在给定 $a,n$ 后同时确定 $q,r$，并不是说不同输入使用同一对数。允许的余数恰好是 $0,1,\ldots,n-1$ 这 $n$ 个整数；其中包含零。当且仅当定理给出的余数为零时，才有 $n\mid a$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why existence and uniqueness hold
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么存在且唯一

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $\lfloor t\rfloor$, called the **floor** of $t$, means the greatest integer not exceeding $t$. Thus $q=\lfloor a/n\rfloor$ means $q\le a/n<q+1$. For the rational number $a/n$ here, such an integer exists: put $K=|a|+1$, where $|a|$ is the nonnegative absolute value of $a$. Because $n\ge1$, we have $-K<a/n<K$. Among the finitely many integers from $-K$ to $K$ that do not exceed $a/n$, choose the largest. The set contains $-K$, and its largest member $q$ satisfies $q+1>a/n$; otherwise $q+1$ would be another allowed, larger member.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

符号 $\lfloor t\rfloor$ 叫作 $t$ 的**下取整**，表示不超过 $t$ 的最大整数。因此 $q=\lfloor a/n\rfloor$ 的含义就是 $q\le a/n<q+1$。这里的有理数 $a/n$ 确实有这样的整数：令 $K=|a|+1$，其中 $|a|$ 是 $a$ 的非负绝对值。因为 $n\ge1$，所以 $-K<a/n<K$。在 $-K$ 到 $K$ 之间、不超过 $a/n$ 的有限个整数里选最大的一个。集合包含 $-K$，因此非空；最大成员 $q$ 必满足 $q+1>a/n$，否则 $q+1$ 也是一个允许且更大的成员。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose $q=\lfloor a/n\rfloor$. Multiplying $q\le a/n<q+1$ by positive $n$ yields $0\le a-qn<n$, so $r=a-qn$ works. If also $a=q'n+r'$, subtraction gives $(q-q')n=r'-r$. The right side lies strictly between $-n$ and $n$, where the only multiple of $n$ is zero. Hence $r=r'$ and then $q=q'$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $q=\lfloor a/n\rfloor$，则 $q\le a/n<q+1$，乘以正数 $n$ 后得到 $0\le a-qn<n$，所以 $r=a-qn$ 合要求。若还有 $a=q'n+r'$，相减得 $(q-q')n=r'-r$；右边严格介于 $-n$ 与 $n$ 之间，唯一可能的 $n$ 的倍数是 $0$。因此 $r=r'$，再得 $q=q'$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first part proves existence: $q$ and $r=a-qn$ are integers and meet both requirements. In the second part, the alternative $q^{\prime},r^{\prime}$ must also satisfy $0\le r^{\prime}<n$. A nonzero integer multiple of positive $n$ has absolute value at least $n$, whereas $|r^{\prime}-r|<n$. Hence their difference must be zero. Finally $n\ne0$ allows cancellation to obtain equality of the quotients. Both the sign and the strict upper bound on remainders are doing work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一部分证明存在性：$q$ 与 $r=a-qn$ 都是整数，且满足等式与范围两项要求。第二部分中，另一组 $q^{\prime},r^{\prime}$ 也必须满足 $0\le r^{\prime}<n$。正数 $n$ 的非零整数倍，其绝对值至少为 $n$；但 $|r^{\prime}-r|<n$，所以这个差只能为零。最后由 $n\ne0$ 才能约去 $n$，得到两个商相等。余数的非负性和严格上界都在证明中起作用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Negative inputs still have nonnegative remainders
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*负输入仍采用非负余数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem also applies to negative inputs: $-11=(-2)\cdot7+3$, so the remainder is $3$, not $-4$. A programming language's `%` operator should not be assumed to implement this convention without checking. The [[欧几里得算法|Euclidean algorithm]] uses the remainder's bounds to prove termination, while [[模运算|modular arithmetic]] uses it to choose a standard representative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

负数同样适用：$-11=(-2)\cdot7+3$，所以余数是 $3$，不是 $-4$。不要未经检查就把某种编程语言的 `%` 当作这里的余数定义。[[欧几里得算法]]依赖这个非负、严格小于除数的范围来证明终止；[[模运算]]用它选取同余类的标准代表。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $a=-11,n=7$, the adjacent multiples are $-14$ and $-7$. The input lies between them: $-14\le-11<-7$. Starting at $-14$ leaves a forward gap of $3$. Rounding $-11/7$ toward zero would instead choose $q=-1$ and produce $r=-4$; the equation would hold, but the required remainder range would fail. The floor rounds down on the number line, so the correct quotient is $-2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $a=-11,n=7$，相邻的两个倍数是 $-14$ 与 $-7$，输入满足 $-14\le-11<-7$。从 $-14$ 向前到 $-11$ 的差额是 $3$。如果把 $-11/7$ 向零截断，会选到 $q=-1$、$r=-4$；虽然等式成立，却不满足余数范围。下取整是沿数轴向下取，因此正确的商是 $-2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Other boundaries follow the same rule: $0=0\cdot n+0$ for every positive $n$; dividing by $1$ gives $q=a,r=0$; and exact multiples such as $-14=(-2)\cdot7+0$ have zero remainder. With divisor $2$, the only remainders are $0$ and $1$, which gives the even/odd alternatives for every integer, including negative integers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

其他边界也遵守同一规则：对每个正整数 $n$，都有 $0=0\cdot n+0$；除以 $1$ 时得到 $q=a,r=0$；精确倍数如 $-14=(-2)\cdot7+0$ 的余数为零。除数取 $2$ 时，余数只能是 $0$ 或 $1$，于是包括负整数在内的每个整数都恰好属于偶数或奇数之一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the divisor must be positive here
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*本定理为什么限定正除数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $n=0$, the range $0\le r<0$ has no solution, so this theorem supplies no pair even when $a=0$. Omitting the range would not repair uniqueness: $a=q\cdot0+a$ would allow every integer $q$. This does not contradict the valid relation $0\mid0$ in [[整除|divisibility]], which only asks whether a multiplication equation has some integer witness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $n=0$ 时，范围 $0\le r<0$ 没有解，因此即使 $a=0$，本定理也不给出商余数对。删去范围也不能修复唯一性，因为 $a=q\cdot0+a$ 会容许任意整数 $q$。这与[[整除]]中 $0\mid0$ 成立不矛盾，后者只问乘法等式是否有某个整数见证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A negative divisor needs a stated convention. One consistent extension for $d<0$ uses $a=qd+r$ with $0\le r<|d|$: apply this theorem to $|d|$, then negate the quotient. For example, $-11=2(-7)+3$. This is an explicitly changed remainder bound, not an application of the impossible range $0\le r<d$ when $d<0$. The main theorem above keeps $n>0$ throughout.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

负除数需要另行说明约定。一种一致的扩展是在 $d<0$ 时写成 $a=qd+r$，要求 $0\le r<|d|$：先对 $|d|$ 应用定理，再把商取反。例如 $-11=2(-7)+3$。这里明确改变了余数上界，并不是在 $d<0$ 时使用不可能满足的 $0\le r<d$。上面的主定理始终保持 $n>0$。
