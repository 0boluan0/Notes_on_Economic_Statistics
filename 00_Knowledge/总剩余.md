# 总剩余是在同一核算边界内将买卖双方货币剩余相加的交易净收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Total surplus adds buyers' and sellers' monetary surpluses within one accounting boundary.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总剩余（TS）是在同一市场、时期和货币口径下，将 [[消费者剩余]] 与 [[生产者剩余]] 相加得到的交易净收益。买卖双方使用同一成交价时，付款在加总中抵消，剩下买方估值减去供给的机会成本；它不是不附带价值判断的社会福利总分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Total surplus combines [[消费者剩余|consumer surplus]] and [[生产者剩余|producer surplus]] on the same market, time and monetary basis. A common transaction payment cancels, leaving buyer valuation less supply opportunity cost. This is not a value-free social welfare score.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对按支付意愿与边际成本有效排序的交易，以 $MB$ 表示买方边际支付意愿、$MC$ 表示边际机会成本。采用可作精确货币收益解释的需求（例如相关范围内准线性、收入效应为零），且无外部性、未计入的真实成本或其他市场扭曲时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For efficiently ordered transactions, let MB denote marginal willingness to pay and MC marginal opportunity cost. Suppose demand admits an exact monetary-benefit interpretation, for example under quasilinearity over the relevant range. Also exclude externalities, omitted real costs and distortions elsewhere:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
TS(Q)=CS+PS
=\int_0^Q[MB(q)-MC(q)]\,dq.
$$

例如某配置下 $CS=12.5$、$PS=12.5$，总剩余就是 $25$。这一步只做加总；如何在可行数量之间选择使总剩余最大的配置，见 [[总剩余最大化]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If an allocation has CS of $12.5$ and PS of $12.5$, its total surplus is $25$. This is an addition, not yet a choice among feasible quantities; that decision is covered by [[总剩余最大化|Total surplus maximisation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若税收使买方支付价与卖方实收价不同，完整的市场福利账户应加入政府净收入；补贴则扣除政府净支出，见 [[转移支付核算]]。若已直接用上面的收益减资源成本积分，就不能再加一次税收收入。固定成本随方案改变时也须另行计入，不能把未扣固定成本的面积当作全部净收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a tax wedge, include net government receipts; with subsidies, deduct net expenditure, as explained in [[转移支付核算|Transfer accounting]]. Do not add tax revenue again to a direct benefit-minus-resource-cost integral. Changes in fixed cost must also be accounted for.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

货币一元在不同人之间以相同权重相加，并不代表它带来相同效用。有显著收入效应时，先查 [[剩余与补偿度量]]；分配评价仍须另外说明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal weights on monetary units do not imply equal utility effects across people. Consult [[剩余与补偿度量|Surplus versus compensation measures]] when income effects matter, and state distributional judgements separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
