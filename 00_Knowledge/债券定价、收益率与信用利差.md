# 债券定价、收益率与信用利差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bond Pricing, Yields, and Credit Spreads*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把债券价格还原成逐期合同现金流、无风险利率、违约与回收、流动性及嵌入期权的共同结果。
> **具体锚点：** 面值 100、年息票 5、两年到期的无违约债券，在要求收益率 6% 时价格约为 98.17；要求收益率高于票面率，所以折价交易。
> **核心难点：** 到期收益率只是把一整组现金流压成一个内部收益率；信用利差也不是违约概率本身。
> **为什么重要：** 固定收益的估值、相对价值、损益归因和风险管理都依赖把利率、信用和可选性分开。
> **继续：** 即期率与远期率见 [[利率、即期远期与期限结构]]；价格敏感度见 [[债券久期、凸性与收益率曲线风险]]；单笔信用损失见 [[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It explains a bond price as the joint result of period-by-period contractual cash flows, risk-free rates, default and recovery, liquidity, and embedded options.
> **Concrete anchor:** A default-free bond with face value 100, annual coupon 5, and two years to maturity is worth about 98.17 at a required yield of 6%. The required yield exceeds the coupon rate, so the bond trades at a discount.
> **Central difficulty:** Yield to maturity compresses an entire cash-flow schedule into one internal rate of return, and a credit spread is not itself a default probability.
> **Why it matters:** Fixed-income valuation, relative value, profit-and-loss attribution, and risk management all require rate, credit, and optionality components to be separated.

## 先写现金流，再选择贴现曲线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Write the cash flows first, then choose the discount curve*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券合同先决定每个日期可能支付什么，再由对应日期、状态和风险的贴现因子决定今天价值。无违约固定息票债的基本式为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bond contract first determines what may be paid on each date; discount factors matching those dates, states, and risks then determine present value. For a default-free fixed-coupon bond,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
P=\sum_{t=1}^{n}\frac{C}{(1+s_t)^t}+\frac{F}{(1+s_n)^n},
$$

其中 $s_t$ 是第 $t$ 期即期利率。若用一个 YTM $y$ 表示，则求解的是 $P=\sum C/(1+y)^t+F/(1+y)^n$。后者便于报价，却不表示所有期限真实共享同一个即期率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
where $s_t$ is the spot rate for maturity $t$. If price is quoted through a single YTM $y$, solve $P=\sum C/(1+y)^t+F/(1+y)^n$. This is convenient for quotation, but it does not mean all maturities truly share one spot rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 债券定价
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Bond pricing*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定息票债券价值是息票年金加本金现值。市场收益率高于票面率时折价，低于时溢价，临近到期价格在无违约条件下向面值收敛。嵌入赎回、回售、转换和浮息条款会改变现金流与凸性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A fixed-coupon bond is worth the present value of its coupon annuity plus principal. It trades at a discount when market yield exceeds the coupon rate and at a premium when market yield is lower; absent default, price converges toward face value as maturity approaches. Embedded call, put, conversion, and floating-rate terms alter cash flows and convexity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两年、面值 100、年息票 5、要求收益率 6% 的债券价格为 $5/1.06+105/1.06^2\approx98.17$。一年后若收益率仍为 6%，债券除息后价格约为 $105/1.06\approx99.06$；折价逐步摊向面值是持有回报的一部分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A two-year bond with face value 100, annual coupon 5, and required yield 6% is priced at $5/1.06+105/1.06^2\approx98.17$. If the yield remains 6% one year later, its ex-coupon price is about $105/1.06\approx99.06$. The pull of the discount toward par is part of holding return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 收益率与信用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Yield and credit*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

YTM 是合同现金流的内部收益率，信用债还要考虑违约概率和回收。信用利差包含预期损失、风险溢价、流动性和税收，不能直接读成违约概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
YTM is the internal rate of return on contractual cash flows; a credit bond also requires default probability and recovery to be considered. A credit spread contains expected loss, risk premium, liquidity, and taxes and cannot be read directly as a default probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最简一期间近似中，若风险中性违约概率为 $q$、违约损失率为 $LGD$，信用利差可粗略满足 $s\approx q\times LGD$。现实中期限、多期违约、回收时点、系统风险补偿、流动性和嵌入期权都会破坏这个简化，因此它只能作数量级检查。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the simplest one-period approximation, if risk-neutral default probability is $q$ and loss given default is $LGD$, the credit spread may roughly satisfy $s\approx q\times LGD$. Maturity, multi-period default, recovery timing, systematic-risk compensation, liquidity, and embedded options break this simplification in practice, so it is only an order-of-magnitude check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 嵌入期权怎样改变价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How embedded options change value*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可赎回债券等于普通债券减去发行人持有的赎回权：利率下降、普通债价值上涨时，发行人更可能按约定价格赎回，因此投资者上行被截断。可回售债券则包含投资者的卖回权，在利率上升或信用恶化时提供保护。可转债同时包含债权与转股选择，不能只用普通 YTM 比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A callable bond equals an otherwise identical straight bond minus the issuer's call option. When rates fall and the straight bond appreciates, the issuer is more likely to call at the contractual price, capping investor upside. A puttable bond contains an investor put that protects against rising rates or worsening credit. A convertible combines debt with a conversion option and cannot be compared using a plain YTM alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 用票面利率当贴现率，会把合同支付比例错当成市场要求回报。
- 用无风险 YTM 折信用债全部合同现金流而不调整违约与流动性，会高估价格。
- 把两只期限相同但赎回权不同的债券直接按利差比较，会把期权价值写成信用差异。
- 把 YTM 当作必然实现的持有回报，忽略违约、提前出售与息票再投资。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Using the coupon rate as the discount rate confuses a contractual payment ratio with the market-required return.
- Discounting all contractual cash flows of a credit bond at a risk-free YTM without adjusting for default and liquidity overstates price.
- Comparing same-maturity bonds with different call provisions solely by yield spread mislabels option value as credit difference.
- Treating YTM as a guaranteed holding return ignores default, early sale, and coupon reinvestment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么要求收益率上升会压低固定现金流债券价格？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does a higher required yield reduce the price of a fixed-cash-flow bond?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 合同现金流不变时，更高机会成本使每个未来支付的贴现因子下降；为提供更高预期回报，今天必须用更低价格买入。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With contractual cash flows unchanged, a higher opportunity cost lowers every future payment's discount factor. The bond must be bought at a lower price today to offer the higher expected return.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 信用利差为什么不等于违约概率？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is a credit spread not equal to default probability?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 还包含回收率、系统风险溢价、流动性、税收和技术供需。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It also contains recovery, systematic-risk premium, liquidity, taxes, and technical supply and demand.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么可赎回债券在利率大跌时上涨可能受限？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why can a callable bond's appreciation be limited when rates fall sharply?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 利率下降使旧高息债更值钱，也使发行人更有动力按赎回价收回并低息再融资；投资者把这项选择权给了发行人，所以超过赎回价的上行被压缩。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Lower rates make the old high-coupon bond more valuable but also encourage the issuer to call it at the specified price and refinance cheaply. Because investors granted that option to the issuer, upside above the call price is compressed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
