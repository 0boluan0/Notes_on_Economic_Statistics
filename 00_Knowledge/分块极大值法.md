# 分块极大值法将数据分块并以每块最大值建模极端水平
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The block-maxima method partitions observations and models extremes using one maximum per block*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分块极大值法把观测按固定规则分成若干块，从每块提取最大值，再对这些块最大值建模。例如六个损失观测按每三个一块分为 $(2,7,4)$ 与 $(9,1,6)$，得到的极值样本是 $(7,9)$，不是原来的六个损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The block-maxima method partitions observations by a specified rule, extracts each block's maximum, and models the resulting sample. Dividing six losses into blocks $(2,7,4)$ and $(9,1,6)$ produces the maxima sample $(7,9)$, not the original six losses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一块含 $n$ 个[[独立同分布]]观测、共同 CDF 为 $F$，则块最大值 $M_n$ 满足下式。独立性使“所有观测都不超过 $x$”的概率可以相乘；仅有相同边际分布不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a block of $n$ [[独立同分布|i.i.d.]] observations with common CDF $F$, the maximum $M_n$ has the following law. Independence permits multiplication of the probabilities that every observation is at most $x$; identical marginals alone do not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
M_n=\max(X_1,\ldots,X_n),\qquad P(M_n\le x)=F(x)^n.
$$

有限块的精确分布是 $F^n$；[[广义极值分布|GEV]] 的理由来自[[极大值极限定理]]，不是“取过最大值就精确服从 GEV”。实际拟合可以把位置与尺度吸收到 GEV 参数中；理论极限仍要求适当归一化与吸引域条件。固定总样本下，增大块长会减少可用于拟合的块数，因此需要权衡极限近似误差与估计不确定性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The exact finite-block CDF is $F^n$. A [[广义极值分布|GEV]] approximation is motivated by the [[极大值极限定理|extremal types theorem]], not by an exact GEV law for every maximum. A fitted GEV can absorb location and scale, while the limiting theory still requires appropriate normalisation and a domain-of-attraction condition. With a fixed total sample, longer blocks leave fewer maxima for estimation, creating an approximation–uncertainty trade-off.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

*Sources and verification*

- Martin Haugh，[Extreme Value Theory，PDF 第 6–8、14 页](https://www.columbia.edu/~mh2078/QRM/EVT_MasterSlides.pdf#page=14)：核对块最大值对象、$F^n$、归一化极限和块长／块数的权衡。六个观测的分块例为本文自拟并逐块取最大值复核。

- Haugh's notes, PDF pp. 6–8 and 14, support the block maximum, $F^n$, normalised limits, and the block-size/sample-size trade-off. The six-observation example is independently constructed and checked block by block.
