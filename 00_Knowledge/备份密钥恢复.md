# 加密备份只有在独立可取得解密密钥时才可恢复
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An encrypted backup is recoverable only when its decryption key remains independently obtainable*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 备份加密能保护副本泄露，却让恢复依赖解密密钥。若唯一密钥只存在于被备份系统中，整机丢失可能同时毁掉数据与钥匙；若密钥与备份在同一访问边界内无保护地存放，又削弱机密性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Backup encryption protects exposed copies but makes restoration depend on the decryption key. If the only key exists inside the system being backed up, a total loss can destroy both data and key. If the key is stored unprotected within the same access boundary as the backup, confidentiality is weakened.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可恢复设计要让至少一条密钥恢复路径不被同一故障或攻击同时破坏，并明确谁能取回、需要几方授权、恢复材料如何轮换和审计。分离不是把密钥随便复制到更多地方，而是在可用性与未授权访问之间建立可测试的控制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A recoverable design keeps at least one key-recovery path from being destroyed by the same failure or attack and specifies who may retrieve it, how many approvals are required, and how recovery material is rotated and audited. Separation is not arbitrary duplication of keys; it is a testable control balancing availability against unauthorised access.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

密钥路径本身也要进入恢复演练：证明授权人员能在目标环境、目标时限内取得正确密钥并解密真实备份，同时验证普通操作人员或备份存储账号不能绕过分离控制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The key path must be included in restoration exercises. The exercise should show that authorised staff can obtain the correct key and decrypt a real backup in the target environment and time limit, while ordinary operators or a compromised backup-storage account cannot bypass the separation control.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 云端每天保存加密备份，但唯一解密密钥只在原服务器磁盘。原服务器完全损毁后，缺少了哪项恢复条件？
>
> **答案：** 缺少独立可取得的解密密钥。密文副本存在不等于能够恢复数据。

## 来源与核验

- [NIST SP 800-57 Part 1 Rev. 5, Sections 8.3 and 8.4](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/57/pt1/r5/final)：核对密钥备份、归档与恢复属于密钥生命周期控制，且恢复材料本身需要机密性、完整性和访问保护。
- [NIST SP 800-34 Rev. 1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/34/r1/upd1/final)：交叉核对恢复能力必须覆盖恢复所需资源、人员与程序，而不能只确认数据副本存在。
