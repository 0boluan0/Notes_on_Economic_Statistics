# 开放寻址删除不能把槽位直接恢复为空

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Deletion in open addressing cannot simply turn a slot back into never-used empty space*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子不变量
> 在开放寻址中，“从未使用的空槽”会终止失败查找。若删除操作把一个已使用槽直接改成这种空状态，它可能截断后来键的探测路径；通常需要墓碑标记或能保持路径的等价机制。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In open addressing, a never-used empty slot terminates an unsuccessful search. If deletion turns a used slot directly into that state, it can cut off the probe path of keys placed later. A tombstone or an equivalent path-preserving mechanism is normally required.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设键 A 与 B 的探测序列都先经过槽 3。A 占据槽 3 后，B 因碰撞被放到槽 4。若删除 A 时把槽 3 设为“从未使用”，查找 B 到达槽 3 就会错误地断定 B 不存在，永远看不到槽 4。墓碑表示“这里目前没有有效条目，但探测链曾经过这里”：查找继续，插入则可以在合适时复用它。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose keys A and B both probe slot 3 first. A occupies slot 3, so B is placed in slot 4 after a collision. If deleting A marks slot 3 as never used, a lookup for B stops there and incorrectly concludes that B is absent. A tombstone means “no live entry is here, but a probe path passed through”: lookup continues, while insertion may reuse the position under the implementation's rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

墓碑本身也会积累并拉长探测，因此实现常在扩容、缩容或专门整理时重建表。这里要记住的核心不是某个标记名称，而是三种状态的语义不能混为一谈：从未使用、当前占用、曾经占用但已删除。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Tombstones can accumulate and lengthen probes, so implementations often clear them during resizing, shrinking, or a dedicated rebuild. The essential point is not the marker's name but the distinction among three states: never used, currently occupied, and formerly occupied but deleted.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与辨别
> 为什么开放寻址删除不能总是简单写入普通空值？
>
> **答案：** 普通空值会让查找提前停止，可能遮住同一探测序列中更靠后的键。
