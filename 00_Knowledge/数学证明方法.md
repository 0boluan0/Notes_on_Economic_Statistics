# 数学证明方法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Methods of mathematical proof*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把“看起来是真的”变成从明示定义和已知命题推出结论的可检查论证。
> **具体锚点：** 要证明偶数之和仍为偶数，关键不是列很多例子，而是把偶数写成 $2k$ 并对任意两个对象推导。
> **核心难点：** 不同量词结构决定证明策略；反例能否定全称命题，却不能证明存在之外的普遍结论。
> **为什么重要：** 算法正确性、离散结构和数学建模都依赖清晰处理“任意”“存在”“如果”。
> **继续：** 先翻译逻辑结构，再选直接、逆否、反证、归纳或不变量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Proof turns a claim that merely appears true into a checkable argument derived from explicit definitions and established results.
> **Concrete anchor:** To prove that the sum of two even integers is even, write arbitrary even integers as $2a$ and $2b$; a long list of examples cannot cover every case.
> **Central difficulty:** The quantifier structure determines the strategy. One counterexample refutes a universal claim, but examples do not establish a universal conclusion.
> **Why it matters:** Algorithm correctness, discrete structures, and mathematical modelling all depend on disciplined handling of “for every,” “there exists,” and “if.”
> **Continue with:** Translate the statement into logical form, then choose direct proof, contrapositive, contradiction, induction, or an invariant.

## 命题、量词与否定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Propositions, quantifiers, and negation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明前把语句写清：$\forall x\exists y$ 与 $\exists y\forall x$ 不同。否定全称是存在反例，否定存在是所有对象都不满足。蕴含 $P\Rightarrow Q$ 的逆命题未必真，逆否命题 $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$ 与原命题等价。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the statement precisely before proving it: $\forall x\exists y$ differs from $\exists y\forall x$. Negating a universal claim produces an existential counterexample, whereas negating an existential claim says that every object fails the property. The converse of $P\Rightarrow Q$ need not be true, but the contrapositive $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$ is logically equivalent to the original implication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个实用步骤是先标出前提、结论和量词范围，再把定义展开。如果结论是“存在”，通常需要构造见证；如果是“对所有”，就必须从任意对象出发。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A practical first pass is to mark the premises, conclusion, and scope of every quantifier, and then expand the definitions. An existential conclusion usually calls for a witness; a universal conclusion requires starting with an arbitrary object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 直接证明、逆否与反证
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Direct proof, contrapositive, and contradiction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直接证明从假设展开定义到结论；当结论的否定更易利用时用逆否；反证法假设命题为假并推出矛盾。策略应服务于结构，不是为了“看起来高级”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A direct proof expands the assumptions and definitions until the conclusion follows. Use a contrapositive when the negated conclusion is easier to exploit, and use contradiction by assuming the claim false and deriving an impossibility. The strategy should fit the logical structure, not serve as decoration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 归纳法
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Induction*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通归纳证明基例，并假设 n 情形推出 n+1；强归纳可使用所有较小情形。归纳假设只能用于已覆盖的规模，递推步骤必须真正缩小问题且基例覆盖所有终止路径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ordinary induction proves a base case and shows that the case $n$ implies the case $n+1$; strong induction may use every smaller case. The induction hypothesis applies only to sizes already covered. A recursive step must genuinely reduce the problem, and the base cases must cover every termination path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 良序与最小反例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Well-ordering and minimal counterexamples*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

非空自然数集合有最小元。若假设存在反例并取最小反例，再构造更小反例，就矛盾。它与强归纳等价但在整除、算法终止和结构分解中常更自然。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every nonempty set of natural numbers has a least element. If a counterexample exists, choose a smallest one and derive an even smaller counterexample; the contradiction rules out all counterexamples. This method is equivalent in strength to strong induction but is often more natural for divisibility, termination, and structural decomposition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 不变量与算法正确性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Invariants and algorithm correctness*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

循环不变量需证明初始化、保持和终止时推出目标；termination 还需一个严格下降且有下界的量。只证明“每步看起来合理”不等于算法对所有输入正确。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A loop invariant requires proofs of initialisation, preservation, and implication of the goal at termination. Termination also needs a quantity that decreases strictly and is bounded below. Showing that each step “looks reasonable” does not establish correctness for every input.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 反例与边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Counterexamples and boundaries*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反例应满足全部前提却违反结论。失败的证明也可能暴露缺失条件，应修正命题而不是偷偷加强假设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A counterexample must satisfy every premise while violating the conclusion. A failed proof may reveal a missing condition; repair the statement instead of silently strengthening its assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：奇数平方仍为奇数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: the square of an odd integer is odd*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

命题是“对任意整数 $n$，若 $n$ 为奇数，则 $n^2$ 为奇数”。取任意奇数 $n=2k+1$，则 $n^2=4k^2+4k+1=2(2k^2+2k)+1$，因而按奇数的定义得证。这个证明的主干是“任意对象 → 展开定义 → 回到目标定义”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The claim is: for every integer $n$, if $n$ is odd, then $n^2$ is odd. Take an arbitrary odd integer $n=2k+1$. Then $n^2=4k^2+4k+1=2(2k^2+2k)+1$, which is odd by definition. The proof follows the pattern “arbitrary object → expand the premise's definition → reach the conclusion's definition.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么举一百个例子不能证明全称命题？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can one hundred examples not prove a universal claim?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 全称命题覆盖无限或未枚举对象；有限例子只提供证据，不能排除剩余对象中的反例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A universal statement covers infinitely many or unenumerated objects. Finitely many examples provide evidence but cannot exclude a counterexample among the remaining objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 归纳证明最容易漏掉哪两部分？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Which two parts are most easily omitted from an induction proof?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 基例是否覆盖所有起点，以及归纳步是否只使用已被归纳假设保证的较小情形。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Check that the base cases cover every starting path and that the induction step uses only smaller cases already guaranteed by the hypothesis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 证明循环正确为什么还要证明终止？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must termination be proved in addition to a loop invariant?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不变量只说明若到某步性质保持；若循环永不结束，就不会产生满足后置条件的输出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> An invariant says what remains true at each reached step. If the loop never terminates, it never produces an output satisfying the postcondition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 何时应优先尝试逆否证明？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When should you try a contrapositive proof first?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 当结论是“不属于”“不可整除”或其他否定性质，而且从结论的否定能直接展开定义时，逆否往往比直接推导更短。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A contrapositive is often shorter when the conclusion is a negative property and negating it yields a concrete definition that can be used directly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
