# 财务比率是为解释财务关系而用一个财务数量除以另一个财务数量的指标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A financial ratio divides one financial quantity by another to describe a financially meaningful relationship.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

财务比率将报表或市场数据写成 $X/Y$，用来描述偿债资源、资产利用、盈利或市场定价等关系。定义必须说明分子、分母和观察期间；并不是任意两项相除就自动有可用的经济解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A financial ratio expresses statement or market data as $X/Y$ to describe relationships such as debt coverage, asset use, profitability, or market pricing. Its definition must identify the numerator, denominator, and observation period. Dividing arbitrary figures does not automatically produce an economically useful measure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如[[流动比率]]是流动资产/流动负债，回答一元流动负债对应多少流动资产；[[营业净利率]]是净利润/营业收入，回答一元销售对应多少净利润。两者即使数值相同，也不能比较谁的“收益”更高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[流动比率|current ratio]] measures current assets per unit of current liabilities, whereas [[营业净利率|net profit margin]] measures net profit per unit of revenue. Equal numerical values do not make them comparable returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同倍数缩放分子和分母不改变比率，所以它有助于跨规模比较；但业务、风险、会计和时点差异并未因此消失。零分母、负分母与极小分母应在[[财务比率口径匹配]]中单独处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Scaling numerator and denominator by the same factor leaves a ratio unchanged, facilitating comparisons across sizes. Business, risk, accounting, and timing differences remain. Handle zero, negative, and very small denominators explicitly through [[财务比率口径匹配|measurement-basis checks]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
