# 顺序服务的可赎回存款在提供流动性保险时可在特定条件下产生自我实现的协调性挤兑
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Redeemable deposits with sequential service can provide liquidity insurance yet generate a self-fulfilling coordination run under specific conditions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子机制
> Diamond–Dybvig 模型把银行的价值和脆弱性放在同一份活期存款契约中：存款人事前不知道自己何时需要消费，银行把资金投入长期且提前清算回报较低的资产，并承诺按请求顺序支付固定的早期提款。该承诺能在正常均衡中为真正需要提前消费的人提供流动性保险，却也可能让“别人会先取款”的预期成为每位耐心存款人立即提款的理由。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The Diamond–Dybvig model places a bank's value and fragility in the same demand-deposit contract. Depositors do not know ex ante when they will need to consume; the bank invests in a long asset whose early liquidation return is lower and promises a fixed early withdrawal payment in request order. In the normal equilibrium, this promise insures those who genuinely need early consumption, but it can also make an expectation that others will withdraw first a reason for every patient depositor to withdraw immediately.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 流动性保险与顺序服务
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Liquidity insurance and sequential service*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模型有三个时期。存款人在时期 0 投资，时期 1 才私下知道自己是需要早期消费的类型，还是能够等到时期 2 的耐心类型。长期技术若持有到时期 2 产生较高回报，若在时期 1 提前清算则只能得到较低回报。直接各自持有资产无法按照事后私人类型有效地互相保险；银行通过活期存款把早期消费承诺与长期投资组合起来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The model has three dates. Depositors invest at date 0 and privately learn at date 1 whether they need early consumption or can wait until date 2. The long technology yields a higher return if held to date 2 and a lower return if liquidated at date 1. Direct individual ownership cannot efficiently insure privately observed timing needs; a bank combines an early-consumption promise with long investment through a demand-deposit contract.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“顺序服务”（sequential service）指银行只能根据存款人在提款队列中的位置支付，不能用队列后方尚未观察到的提款总量来重写前面已经支付的金额。它不是无关紧要的操作细节：先到者拿到固定早期支付，剩余资产由后到者共同承担，因此提前提款的收益取决于别人是否先取。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sequential service means that the bank can condition a payment on a depositor's place in the withdrawal queue but not on withdrawals by people later in the queue that have not yet been observed. It is not an incidental operating detail. Early arrivals receive the promised early payment while later arrivals share what remains, so the payoff from withdrawing early depends on whether others do so first.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 多重均衡需要什么条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Conditions for multiple equilibria*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在**无挤兑均衡**中，只有早期消费类型提款，耐心类型等待长期资产到期，存款契约实现比各自持有资产更好的风险分担。在**挤兑均衡**中，若许多人现在提款会耗尽可变现资源并使等待者的时期 2 回报低于立即提款，耐心类型也会选择排队；预期于是自我实现，提前清算长期资产并造成真实资源损失。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the **no-run equilibrium**, only early consumers withdraw and patient depositors wait for the long asset to mature, so the deposit contract improves risk sharing over individual asset holding. In the **run equilibrium**, if widespread current withdrawal exhausts liquid resources and makes a waiter's date-2 payoff lower than the early withdrawal payment, patient depositors also join the queue. The expectation becomes self-fulfilling, forces premature liquidation, and destroys real resources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

多重均衡不是由“银行”这个名称自动产生。这里至少依赖：早期消费需要是私人信息；长期资产提前清算有成本；契约承诺可随时赎回并按顺序服务；固定早期支付在大规模提款时会侵蚀留给等待者的资源；存款人对他人行动形成互相强化的预期；且没有可信的暂停兑付、保险或其他安排消除抢跑收益。改变这些契约、信息或制度条件，均衡集合也会改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiple equilibria do not follow automatically from the label “bank.” The mechanism relies on privately observed early consumption needs, costly early liquidation of a long asset, redeemability with sequential service, an early fixed payment that depletes resources available to waiters under mass withdrawal, mutually reinforcing beliefs about others' actions, and the absence of a credible suspension, insurance, or other arrangement that removes the first-mover payoff. Changing these contractual, informational, or institutional conditions changes the equilibrium set.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 与现实挤兑和系统反馈的边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary with real-world runs and systemic feedback*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Diamond–Dybvig 隔离的是：即使银行在无挤兑状态下可以完成长期支付，协调失败也可能触发挤兑。它不主张所有历史挤兑都脱离基本面。坏资产信息、真实偿付能力担忧、未受保存款、批发融资拒绝续作和操作性流动性不足都可能引发或强化现实挤兑；诊断时仍要使用 [[流动性与偿付能力]]，不能看到排队就假定银行资产良好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Diamond–Dybvig isolates the possibility that coordination failure can trigger a run even when the bank could honour long-run claims in the no-run state. It does not claim that every historical run is detached from fundamentals. Information about bad assets, genuine solvency concerns, uninsured deposits, refusal to roll wholesale funding, and operational liquidity shortages can all initiate or intensify real runs. Diagnosis still requires [[流动性与偿付能力|separating liquidity from solvency]]; a queue is not evidence that the assets are sound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本原子解释个人为何抢跑；[[挤兑火售反馈]] 解释集中撤资怎样经现金缺口、火售、资本与抵押品价格传播到其他机构。存款保险、央行流动性、资本重组和处置各自处理不同缺口，直接复用 [[银行危机工具分工]]；安全网可能削弱事前约束，复用 [[金融安全网的道德风险]]。原模型中的存款保险结论不能脱离覆盖范围、可信度和制度条件外推为对所有债权人的保证。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This atom explains why an individual depositor may run; [[挤兑火售反馈|run and fire-sale feedback]] explains how concentrated withdrawals propagate through cash shortfalls, fire sales, capital, and collateral prices. Deposit insurance, central-bank liquidity, recapitalisation, and resolution address different gaps and are reused from [[银行危机工具分工|the crisis-tool taxonomy]]. Their ex-ante incentive cost is reused from [[金融安全网的道德风险|the safety-net moral-hazard atom]]. The model's deposit-insurance result cannot be extrapolated beyond its coverage, credibility, and institutional conditions into a guarantee of every creditor.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么“存款人担心别人先取，所以自己也先取”还不是完整的 Diamond–Dybvig 解释？
>
> **答案：** 还必须说明存款契约怎样提供早期流动性保险、为什么支付按顺序进行、长期资产提前清算为何有损失，以及大规模早取怎样使等待者的回报低于立即提款。缺少这些条件，只是在复述恐慌，而没有给出多重均衡机制。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why is “a depositor withdraws because others may withdraw first” not yet a complete Diamond–Dybvig explanation?
>
> **Answer:** The account must also show how the deposit contract provides early-consumption insurance, why service is sequential, why premature liquidation is costly, and how mass early withdrawal makes waiting less attractive than withdrawing. Without those conditions, it merely restates panic rather than explaining the multiple-equilibrium mechanism.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
