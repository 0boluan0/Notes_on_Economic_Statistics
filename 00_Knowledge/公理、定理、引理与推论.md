# Axioms, theorems, lemmas, and corollaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*公理、定理、引理与推论*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A proof needs starting points and results built from those starting points. **Axiom** identifies a statement adopted at the foundation of the chosen framework. **Theorem**, **lemma**, and **corollary** identify proved results and the jobs they do in an exposition. These words describe the status or role of a [[命题与谓词|proposition]]; they are not four different kinds of number, function, or geometric object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
证明需要起点，也需要从起点建立起来的结果。**公理**标明所选体系在基础处接受的陈述；**定理、引理、推论**标明已经证明的结果，以及它们在叙述中承担的工作。这些词描述一个[[命题与谓词|命题]]的地位或角色，并不是四种不同的数、函数或几何对象。

> [!summary] Starting assumptions and proved results · 起点与已证结果
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An **axiom** is accepted as a starting proposition within a specified formal system. A **theorem**, **lemma**, and **corollary** have all been proved: a theorem is usually a result of central interest, a lemma supports a later argument, and a corollary follows from an established result in only a few steps.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> **公理**是在某个形式系统中接受为起点的命题。**定理、引理和推论**都已经得到证明：定理通常指重要结论，引理为后续论证提供支点，推论则从已有结果经过很短的推导得到。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Relative to which framework?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*相对于哪个体系？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A framework specifies its meanings, accepted starting assumptions and allowed inference rules. Within that framework, an axiom is available without first deriving it from the other assumptions. Calling it an axiom does not establish it as a fact in every possible setting. A proof establishes a consequence of the adopted assumptions; when those assumptions are true in an interpretation and the reasoning preserves truth, the consequence is true there too. This is the role of [[数学证明的有效推理|valid mathematical inference]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
一个体系要说明术语含义、接受的起始假设和允许的推理规则。在该体系内，公理可以作为起点使用，不必先由其他假设推出。称它为公理，并没有证明它在所有可能的环境里都成立。证明建立的是已采用假设的后果；当这些假设在某种解释下为真，且推理保真时，后果在那里也为真。这正是[[数学证明的有效推理]]所说明的作用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Axiom” is relative to a formal system or an explicitly adopted framework. In another system, the same sentence may be a theorem, an additional assumption, or false.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
“公理”总是相对于一个形式系统或当前约定而言；换系统后，同一命题可能成为可证明定理、额外假设或不成立的陈述。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a small illustration, suppose a description of a collection begins by accepting “every object in this collection is red.” That is a starting assumption for the described setting. If an identified object $a$ belongs to the collection, “$a$ is red” follows. The same starting sentence need not describe a different collection containing a blue object. The inference does not turn the original assumption into a universally established fact about all collections.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
看一个小例子：某个集合的描述先接受“这个集合的每个对象都是红色的”。这就是该描述采用的起始假设。如果指定对象 $a$ 属于这个集合，就能推出“$a$ 是红色的”。换成含有蓝色对象的另一集合，原来的起始陈述就未必成立；上述推理不会把原假设变成关于所有集合的普遍事实。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **local hypothesis** is different again. While proving an [[蕴含真值条件|implication]] “if $H$, then $Q$,” we may temporarily assume $H$. That yields a conclusion conditional on $H$; it does not adopt $H$ as a global axiom or prove that $H$ holds. Also, a familiar result accepted without reproving it in the current paragraph may be an earlier theorem, rather than a new foundational axiom. State which role it plays.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**局部假设**又是另一种用法。证明[[蕴含真值条件|蕴含]]“若 $H$ 则 $Q$”时，可以暂时假设 $H$；得到的结论以 $H$ 为条件，并没有把 $H$ 提升为全局公理，也没有证明 $H$ 本身成立。同样，当前段落直接使用而不重证的熟悉结果，可能是先前定理，并不是新加的基础公理；应说明所用的是哪一种。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One chain of reasoning, three result labels
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*同一条论证链里的三种结果名称*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a short piece of integer arithmetic. “Even” means expressible as twice an integer. We use the usual integer rules for addition and multiplication as already available background. First prove a supporting result: the sum of two even integers is even. If $a=2r$ and $b=2s$ for integers $r,s$, then $a+b=2(r+s)$, and $r+s$ is an integer. We may call this result a **lemma** because it will support the next argument.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
考虑一段简短的整数论证。“偶数”指可以写成某个整数的两倍；整数的通常加法、乘法规则作为已有背景。先证明一个辅助结果：两个偶数的和仍为偶数。若整数 $r,s$ 满足 $a=2r$、$b=2s$，则 $a+b=2(r+s)$，而 $r+s$ 仍是整数。因为这个结果将支撑下一段论证，可以把它叫作**引理**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the result of interest is that the sum of three even integers is even. For even $a,b,c$, apply the lemma to $a,b$ to obtain even $a+b$, and apply it again to $a+b,c$. This proves the stated result, which this exposition may label its **theorem**. As a **corollary**, the sum of any three integers each divisible by $4$ is even: each such integer is $4k=2(2k)$ and hence even, so the theorem applies immediately. The corollary still has a proof; its proof is short because the theorem has already done the main work.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
如果关注的主要结果是“三个偶数的和仍为偶数”，对偶数 $a,b,c$ 先把引理用于 $a,b$，得到偶数 $a+b$，再把引理用于 $a+b,c$，就证明了该结果；这篇叙述可以把它称为**定理**。随后得到一个**推论**：三个都能被 $4$ 整除的整数之和为偶数。每个数都是 $4k=2(2k)$，所以为偶数，立即套用定理即可。推论仍有证明，只是此前的定理已承担主要工作，因此这一步很短。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These names describe a result's role in a mathematical exposition rather than a strict ranking of value. A lemma introduced to support one theorem may later become the most widely used result in a field. When reading a proof, ask whether the result has been established, which assumptions it depends on, and how it is used here; the label “theorem” does not eliminate those dependencies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这些名字说明结论在一段数学叙述中的角色，不形成严格的价值等级。今天为主定理服务的 lemma，后来可能成为整个领域最常用的结果。阅读证明时，真正要问的是：它是否已被证明、允许依赖哪些前提、此处怎样使用；不能因为标题写着 theorem 就跳过依赖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same sum-of-two-even-integers statement could be the main theorem of a different lesson. Renaming it changes neither its formula nor the proof obligation. A valid finished argument must include or cite a proof of each lemma it relies on. The proof can appear immediately or later in the exposition, provided the dependencies are clear and do not form a circular argument. A label alone is never evidence that a printed argument is valid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
“两个偶数之和仍为偶数”也可以是另一节课的主定理。改名不会改变公式，也不会降低证明义务。一份有效的完整论证，须包含或引用它所依赖的每个引理的证明。证明可以立即出现，也可以稍后给出，但依赖关系应明确，且不能形成循环论证；标签本身不能保证印出的论证有效。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A definition stipulates meaning and is not automatically a truth-valued proposition merely because it is called a definition. Proofs unfold definitions; they do not normally prove a definition true.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
定义规定术语含义，不因被称作“定义”就是真假命题；证明通常从定义展开，但不是在证明定义本身为真。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, defining “even” by the existence of an integer $k$ with $n=2k$ fixes a word's meaning. Showing that $6$ is even is then a proposition proved by taking $k=3$. The definition and that assertion have different jobs. A description that purports to name a special object can also require separate existence or uniqueness checks; calling it a definition does not supply those checks automatically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
例如，用“存在整数 $k$ 使 $n=2k$”规定“偶数”的含义，是在固定术语；“$6$ 是偶数”则是一个命题，可取 $k=3$ 来证明。定义与这个断言承担不同任务。若某段描述试图命名一个特殊对象，还可能需要另行检查存在性或唯一性，不能仅靠“定义”二字完成这些检查。

> [!question]- Apply the distinction · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A paper proves one result and then derives another in two lines. Why is the second result naturally a corollary rather than an axiom?
>
> **Answer:** It is derived from an established result rather than accepted without proof as a starting point; “corollary” names precisely this short derivation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 某篇论文先证明一个结果，再只用两行从它推出另一个结论。第二个结论为什么适合叫 corollary，而不是 axiom？
>
> **答案：** 它依赖已经证明的结果并由推理得到，不是未经证明接受的起点；“推论”描述的正是这种很短的派生关系。
