# One-variable constrained optimization
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*单变量约束优化*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Constrained optimization asks for the best choice among the choices that are actually allowed. If a box must hold a fixed volume, making every side smaller saves material but eventually violates that requirement. The constraint tells us which dimensions can go together; the objective tells us what to make as small or as large as possible. A problem becomes one-variable optimization when its allowed choices can be described by one independent number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

约束优化是在真正允许的选择中找最好的一个。盒子必须装下固定体积时，把每条边都缩小虽然省材料，却会违反容量要求。约束说明哪些尺寸可以同时采用，目标则说明希望哪个量尽量小或尽量大。若允许的选择能够由一个独立的数描述，问题就能化为单变量优化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Turn the design into a function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把设计变成一个函数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider an open-top box whose base is required to be square. Let $x>0$ be its base side, $y>0$ its height, and $V>0$ its fixed volume. Here $x,y$ are choices and $V$ is a given parameter. The volume equation is the constraint. The area of the base plus four walls is the objective:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑一个底面要求为正方形的无盖盒。令 $x>0$ 为底边长，$y>0$ 为高，$V>0$ 为给定的固定体积。这里 $x,y$ 是待选择的尺寸，$V$ 是已给定的参数。体积方程是约束，底面加四面侧壁的面积是目标：

$$x^2y=V,\qquad A=x^2+4xy.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solving the constraint gives $y=V/x^2$. Every $x>0$ now determines exactly one admissible height, so substituting it into the objective loses no feasible box in this square-base problem:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由约束解出 $y=V/x^2$。现在每个 $x>0$ 都唯一确定一个合法高度，所以把它代入目标，不会遗漏这个正方形底面问题中的任何可行盒子：

$$A(x)=x^2+\frac{4V}{x},\qquad x\in(0,\infty).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This interval is the feasible domain: the inputs representing permitted designs. Neither $x=0$ nor infinity is a box dimension to substitute. If the problem also imposes a maximum height or a minimum width, those conditions must be converted into extra restrictions on $x$ before optimization. A substitution that retains only one branch of a constraint must account for the other feasible branches separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个区间是可行域，即表示允许设计的输入范围。$x=0$ 与无穷都不是可以代入的盒子尺寸。如果题目还限制最大高度或最小宽度，必须先把它们改写成对 $x$ 的附加限制。若某次消元只保留了约束的一条解支，其他可行解支就必须另行处理。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A derivative finds a candidate; its sign proves the optimum
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*导数先找候选，再用符号证明最优*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[导数|derivative]] measures the local change of area as the base side changes. An interior optimum where the derivative exists must be among the [[临界点|critical points]]. Holding $V$ constant gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[导数]]衡量底边长变化时面积的局部变化率。内部可导的最优点一定在[[临界点]]候选中。保持 $V$ 不变，得到

$$A'(x)=2x-\frac{4V}{x^2}=\frac{2(x^3-2V)}{x^2},\qquad x_*=(2V)^{1/3}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The denominator is positive. Thus $A'<0$ for $0<x<x_*$ and $A'>0$ for $x>x_*$. The [[导数单调性判据|derivative test for monotonicity]] shows that area decreases all the way to $x_*$ and increases everywhere after it. This proves a unique global minimum over the entire feasible domain, rather than merely identifying a stationary equation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分母为正，因此 $0<x<x_*$ 时 $A'<0$，$x>x_*$ 时 $A'>0$。由[[导数单调性判据]]，面积一直减小到 $x_*$，之后一直增大。这证明了它是整个可行域上唯一的全局最小点，而不仅是驻点方程的一个根。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The limiting designs also check the conclusion: as $x\to0^+$ the term $4V/x$ tends to infinity, and as $x\to\infty$ the term $x^2$ does so. Extremely narrow or extremely wide bases cannot improve the result. Alternatively $A''(x)=2+8V/x^3>0$ throughout the domain, so the first derivative is strictly increasing there; this is a whole-domain argument, not just a second derivative evaluated at one point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

极端形状也能核对结论：$x\to0^+$ 时 $4V/x$ 趋于无穷，$x\to\infty$ 时 $x^2$ 趋于无穷。极窄或极宽的底面都不能得到更小面积。也可检查全域 $A''(x)=2+8V/x^3>0$，从而一阶导数在全域严格递增；这使用整个定义域的信息，不只是计算某一点的二阶导数。

> [!example] Translate the answer back to dimensions / 把答案译回尺寸
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At the optimum, $V=x_*^3/2$, so $y_*=V/x_*^2=x_*/2$ and $A_{\min}=3x_*^2=3(2V)^{2/3}$. The base is twice the height. For $V=4\,\mathrm{m}^3$, the answer is $x_*=2\,\mathrm m$, $y_*=1\,\mathrm m$, and $A_{\min}=12\,\mathrm{m}^2$. The original volume and all positivity requirements are satisfied.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 最优处 $V=x_*^3/2$，所以 $y_*=V/x_*^2=x_*/2$，$A_{\min}=3x_*^2=3(2V)^{2/3}$：底边长是高度的两倍。若 $V=4\,\mathrm{m}^3$，得到 $x_*=2\,\mathrm m$、$y_*=1\,\mathrm m$、$A_{\min}=12\,\mathrm{m}^2$，满足原来的体积与全部正值要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Other feasible domains need their own final comparison
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*可行域改变，最终比较也要改变*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a continuous objective on a finite closed interval, the [[闭区间极值法|closed-interval method]] compares every interior critical point and both endpoints, including corners where the derivative does not exist. On open or unbounded domains, an optimum need not be attained: $f(x)=x$ on $0<x<1$ has [[上确界#The lower boundary by reflection|infimum]] $0$, its greatest lower bound, but no minimum, because every permitted positive $x$ can be replaced by $x/2$. An excluded boundary limit is not a feasible winning design. If the feasible set has several components, compare the outcomes across them as well.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

连续目标定义在有限闭区间上时，[[闭区间极值法]]比较所有内部临界点与两个端点，也包括导数不存在的尖角。可行域开或无界时，最优值未必取得：在 $0<x<1$ 上，$f(x)=x$ 的[[上确界#The lower boundary by reflection|下确界]]是 $0$，即最大的下界，却没有最小值，因为每个合法的正数 $x$ 都可以换成更小的 $x/2$。被排除边界上的极限不是一个可行的获胜设计。可行域分为几段时，还须比较各段的结果。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The box can also be handled with [[隐式求导|implicit differentiation]], which differentiates along a differentiable solution of the constraint without first substituting it. From $x^2y(x)=V$, obtain $y'=-2y/x$. The [[乘积法则|product rule]] then gives $dA/dx=2x+4y+4xy'=2x-4y$, so stationarity yields $x=2y$ directly. This shortcut supplies the same candidate ratio; the global comparison still needs the reasoning above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

盒子问题也可以用[[隐式求导]]：沿约束的可导解支求导，不先代入消元式。由 $x^2y(x)=V$ 得到 $y'=-2y/x$，再用[[乘积法则]]得到 $dA/dx=2x+4y+4xy'=2x-4y$，驻点条件直接给出 $x=2y$。这个捷径给出相同的候选比例，全局比较仍需要上面的论证。

> [!note] State what has actually been optimized / 说明实际优化的范围
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The square base was part of this example's specification. If arbitrary rectangular bases are allowed, a separate symmetry argument or [[多元优化.canvas|multivariable analysis]] is needed to justify restricting them to squares. Likewise, any physical constraint omitted from the model is absent from the guarantee.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 正方形底面是本例的既定要求。如果允许任意矩形底面，就需额外的对称化论证或[[多元优化.canvas|多元分析]]，才能把搜索限制在正方形上。同样，没有写入模型的实际限制，也不在这个最优性保证之内。
