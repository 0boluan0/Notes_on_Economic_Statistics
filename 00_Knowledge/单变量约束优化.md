# 单变量约束优化先把目标与可行域写清并用约束消元再比较所有可行候选
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*One-variable constrained optimization states the objective and feasible set, eliminates the constraint, and compares every feasible candidate*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子方法
> 文字题先明确目标量、选择变量、固定参数、约束和可行域；再用约束消去多余变量，把目标写成一个变量的函数；求临界点并检查端点、不可导点和极端行为；最后把数学候选翻译回带单位的原问题并验证全局性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Start by identifying the objective, choice variables, fixed parameters, constraint, and feasible set. Use the constraint to reduce the objective to one variable, find its critical points, inspect endpoints and limiting shapes, and then translate the winning feasible candidate back into the original quantities and units.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定体积 $V$ 的无盖方盒若底面为边长 $x$ 的正方形、高为 $y$，则约束 $x^2y=V$ 与目标 $A=x^2+4xy$ 角色不同。消去 $y$ 得
$$
A(x)=x^2+\frac{4V}{x},\qquad x>0.
$$
驻点满足 $x^3=2V$。还要检查 $x\to0^+$ 与 $x\to\infty$ 时 $A\to\infty$，才能确认它是全局最小，而不只是一个方程的根。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an open box with fixed volume, the volume constraint eliminates the height and gives $A(x)=x^2+4V/x$ for $x>0$. The stationary equation $x^3=2V$ supplies a candidate. The limits at both ends of the feasible domain establish that it is the global minimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有时可沿约束直接 [[隐式求导]]，快速得到最优比例；但这种捷径仍不拥有存在性与边界论证。对称形状也不能只凭直觉假设，除非题目已给出或另有对称化证明。多自由度或多约束问题再进入 [[多元优化.canvas]]，而不是硬塞成一元式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Implicit differentiation along a constraint can reveal an optimal ratio quickly, but it does not supply attainment or boundary analysis. Symmetry should not be assumed without being given or proved. Problems with genuinely multiple degrees of freedom belong to multivariable optimization rather than being forced into a one-variable formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么“令导数为零”不能作为这类题的第一步？
>
> **答案：** 在定义目标函数、约束和可行域之前，还不知道对哪个变量求导、哪些点可行，也不知道边界在哪里。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why is “set the derivative to zero” not the first step in this kind of problem?
>
> **Answer:** Until the objective, constraints, and feasible domain are defined, we do not know which variable to differentiate with respect to, which points are feasible, or where the boundaries lie.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
