# 约束性价格上限是低于无管制竞争均衡价格的法定最高价

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A binding price ceiling is a legal maximum price below the unregulated competitive equilibrium price*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**价格上限是交易可收取的法定最高价格。** 在其他条件不变、无管制竞争均衡存在且上限得到执行的模型中，上限低于该均衡价格，才会阻止市场按原均衡价格成交；这里把这种上限称为**约束性价格上限**。设上限为 $\bar p$、原均衡价格为 $p^*$，其条件是：

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**A price ceiling is the maximum price that may legally be charged.** Holding other conditions fixed, with an unregulated competitive equilibrium and an enforced ceiling, a ceiling below the equilibrium price prevents transactions at that original price. This is the sense in which the ceiling is **binding** here. If the ceiling is $\bar p$ and the original equilibrium price is $p^*$, the condition is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\bar p<p^*.
$$

上限高于或等于 $p^*$ 时，原均衡仍然合法，价格上限本身不迫使价格偏离原均衡。在需求曲线向下、供给曲线向上的标准模型中，约束性上限使该价格下的需求量大于供给量，形成超额需求；成交多少还需使用[[短边成交条件]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the ceiling is at or above $p^*$, the original equilibrium remains legal, so the ceiling itself does not force a departure from it. With downward-sloping demand and upward-sloping supply, a binding ceiling makes quantity demanded exceed quantity supplied at the capped price. Actual transactions additionally depend on [[短边成交条件|the conditions for short-side trading]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

降价引起的是沿既定曲线的**需求量增加和供给量减少**；人口、偏好、技术等其他因素变化才需要重新判断曲线是否移动。短期存量完全固定时，供给量可以不变，超额需求仍会出现。现实中的租金涨幅限制、新房豁免或重新出租规则，需要先转成相应制度模型，才能应用这一价格上限定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The lower price causes an **increase in quantity demanded and a decrease in quantity supplied along the existing curves**. Changes in population, preferences, technology, or other determinants require a separate assessment of curve shifts. With a completely fixed short-run stock, quantity supplied can remain unchanged while excess demand emerges. Rent-increase limits, exemptions for new construction, and rules for new tenancies must first be represented in their own institutional model before applying this definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
