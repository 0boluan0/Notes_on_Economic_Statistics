# Asymptotic stability in discrete time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*离散时间的渐近稳定性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A discrete-time system updates its state in separate steps. The **state** is the list of quantities being tracked, written as a vector. The zero state is **asymptotically stable** if a sufficiently small departure stays small at every later step and eventually disappears. For a fixed finite-dimensional homogeneous linear system, we can decide this by checking its eigenvalues.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

离散时间系统一步一步地更新状态。**状态**就是正在跟踪的各个量，把它们写成一个向量。若足够小的偏离在以后每一步都保持小，并最终消失，就称零状态**渐近稳定**。对固定、有限维的齐次线性系统，可以通过检查特征值作出判断。

> [!example] Shrinking with and without sign changes · 一路缩小与交替缩小
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $x_k$ be the state after $k$ steps, where $k=0,1,2,\ldots$. Starting from $x_0=(2,1)^T$, apply the same matrix at every step; $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]] so the coordinates form a column:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $x_k$ 表示第 $k$ 步后的状态，$k=0,1,2,\ldots$。从 $x_0=(2,1)^T$ 出发，每一步都作用同一个矩阵；$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把坐标写成列：
>
> $$x_{k+1}=Ax_k,\qquad A=\begin{pmatrix}1/2&0\\0&-3/4\end{pmatrix},\qquad x_k=\begin{pmatrix}2(1/2)^k\\(-3/4)^k\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The first coordinate halves each time. The second changes sign but its magnitude shrinks by a factor $3/4$. Both approach zero. Changing the initial coordinates only changes their starting sizes, so this happens for every initial state, not just the displayed one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 第一坐标每步减半；第二坐标交替变号，但大小每步乘 $3/4$。二者都趋于零。改变初始坐标只改变起始大小，因此每个初值都会这样，而不只是展示的这一条轨迹。

## From repeated updates to the spectral test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从反复更新走到谱判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a fixed real or complex $n\times n$ matrix, with integer $n\ge1$, and consider $x_{k+1}=Ax_k$ with no added input. Repeated [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] gives $x_k=A^kx_0$, with $A^0=I_n$, the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. Zero is an **equilibrium** because starting at zero leaves the state at zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 是固定的实或复 $n\times n$ 矩阵，整数 $n\ge1$，系统为 $x_{k+1}=Ax_k$，没有额外输入。反复做[[矩阵乘法]]，得到 $x_k=A^kx_0$，其中 $A^0=I_n$ 是[[单位矩阵]]。零是一个**平衡点**，因为从零出发后始终留在零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[特征对|eigenvalue]] $\lambda$ has a nonzero vector $v$ satisfying $Av=\lambda v$. Along this direction, $A^kv=\lambda^kv$. The magnitude $|\lambda|$ therefore measures the per-step factor for that mode. For a complex number $\lambda=a+ib$, where $a,b$ are real and $i^2=-1$, its magnitude is $\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$. We check all complex eigenvalues, even when $A$ itself is real.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[特征对|特征值]] $\lambda$ 对应某个非零向量 $v$，满足 $Av=\lambda v$。沿这个方向有 $A^kv=\lambda^kv$，所以模 $|\lambda|$ 表示该模式每一步的倍乘因子。对复数 $\lambda=a+ib$，其中 $a,b$ 为实数、$i^2=-1$，模为 $\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$。即使 $A$ 本身是实矩阵，也要检查它的全部复特征值。

> [!important] Strictly inside the unit circle · 严格位于单位圆内
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For this system, the following statements are equivalent:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对这个系统，以下说法等价：
>
> $$\begin{aligned}
> &\text{zero is asymptotically stable}\\
> &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A^kx_0\to0\ \text{for every initial vector }x_0\\
> &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A^k\to0\\
> &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \rho(A)<1.
> \end{aligned}$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The [[谱半径|spectral radius]] $\rho(A)=\max_\lambda|\lambda|$ is the largest eigenvalue magnitude. Thus the last inequality requires **every** eigenvalue to satisfy $|\lambda|<1$. This is also called **Schur stability** of the matrix.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> [[谱半径]] $\rho(A)=\max_\lambda|\lambda|$ 是最大的特征值模。因此最后一个不等式要求**每个**特征值都满足 $|\lambda|<1$。矩阵的这种性质也称为 **Schur 稳定性**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the statement covers every initial state
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么结论能覆盖所有初值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $A^k\to0$, multiplying by any fixed $x_0$ gives $x_k\to0$. Conversely, apply the all-initial-state claim to each coordinate [[基|basis vector]] $e_j$, the vector with a single $1$ in position $j$. The vector $A^ke_j$ is column $j$ of $A^k$. All these finitely many columns approach zero, so the entire matrix approaches zero. The [[矩阵幂趋零判据|matrix-power criterion]] then gives precisely $\rho(A)<1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $A^k\to0$，乘任意固定 $x_0$ 就得到 $x_k\to0$。反过来，把“所有初值都趋零”用于每个坐标[[基|基向量]] $e_j$，即第 $j$ 个位置为1、其余为0的向量。$A^ke_j$ 正是 $A^k$ 的第 $j$ 列。有限多个列都趋零，整个矩阵便趋零，再由[[矩阵幂趋零判据]]得到准确条件 $\rho(A)<1$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Convergence also supplies the “stays small” part of stability. In the [[谱范数|induced Euclidean matrix norm]], a convergent sequence $A^k$ is bounded: there is a finite constant $C\ge1$ with $\|A^k\|_2\le C$ for every $k\ge0$. For real or complex coordinates, use $\|x\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_{j=1}^n|x_j|^2}$, the length from the standard coordinate [[内积|inner product]]; $|x_j|$ is the modulus of the $j$th coordinate. Hence $\|x_k\|_2\le C\|x_0\|_2$. To keep the state within a chosen radius $\varepsilon>0$, start within $\varepsilon/C$. Conversely, local attraction of a neighborhood implies attraction of every initial vector here: scale any vector into that neighborhood and use linearity to scale its trajectory back.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收敛也保证稳定性中“始终保持小”的部分。在[[谱范数|诱导欧氏矩阵范数]]下，收敛序列 $A^k$ 必有界：存在有限常数 $C\ge1$，使全部 $k\ge0$ 都满足 $\|A^k\|_2\le C$。对实或复坐标，使用 $\|x\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_{j=1}^n|x_j|^2}$，即标准坐标[[内积]]给出的长度；$|x_j|$ 是第 $j$ 个坐标的模。于是 $\|x_k\|_2\le C\|x_0\|_2$。若希望状态始终在半径 $\varepsilon>0$ 内，只需从半径 $\varepsilon/C$ 内出发。反过来，本系统若能吸引某个邻域的全部初值，就能吸引所有初值：把任意向量缩小到那个邻域，再利用线性把整条轨迹放大回来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem does not require [[对角化|diagonalization]]. A [[Jordan块的幂|Jordan-block power]] can include a fixed-degree polynomial in $k$ multiplied by a decaying geometric factor. When $0<|\lambda|<1$, the geometric decay eventually dominates that polynomial; zero-eigenvalue blocks become exactly zero after finitely many powers. Such blocks can therefore decay even though an eigenvector basis is unavailable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定理不要求[[对角化|可对角化]]。[[Jordan块的幂]]中可能含有关于 $k$ 的固定次数多项式，再乘上衰减的几何因子。只要 $0<|\lambda|<1$，几何衰减最终会压过多项式；零特征值的块则在有限次幂后精确归零。因此，即使没有特征向量基，这些块仍会衰减。

> [!warning] The boundary is not decay · 边界不等于衰减
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $A=[-1]$, a nonzero state alternates signs forever. A quarter-turn rotation keeps its distance from zero. Both have eigenvalue magnitudes equal to $1$ and fail asymptotic stability. Whether boundary modes remain bounded is the separate [[离散系统有界判据|boundedness criterion]]. Even with $\rho(A)<1$, [[非正规矩阵瞬态|transient growth]] may occur before eventual decay; the norm need not decrease at every step.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $A=[-1]$，非零状态永远交替变号；四分之一圈旋转则一直保持到零的距离。二者的特征值模都等于1，都不渐近稳定。边界模式是否仍然有界，需要另用[[离散系统有界判据]]。即使 $\rho(A)<1$，最终衰减之前也可能出现[[非正规矩阵瞬态|暂时放大]]，范数不必每步都下降。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One observed decaying path cannot establish an all-initial-state statement; see [[单轨迹与系统稳定性|hidden unstable modes]]. Also keep time conventions separate: $x_{k+1}=-2x_k$ grows in magnitude, whereas the [[连续系统谱稳定性|continuous-time]] equation $x'=-2x$ decays. This test concerns a fixed matrix with no forcing; checking the eigenvalues of each matrix in a changing sequence is a different problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只看到一条衰减轨迹，不能证明关于全部初值的结论，见[[单轨迹与系统稳定性|被隐藏的不稳定模式]]。还要分清时间约定：$x_{k+1}=-2x_k$ 的大小会增长，而[[连续系统谱稳定性|连续时间]]方程 $x'=-2x$ 会衰减。本判据针对固定矩阵、没有外加输入的系统；逐个检查随时间变化的矩阵，是另一种问题。
