# 净财务杠杆是同口径净金融负债与股东权益之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Net financial leverage is net financial debt divided by equity on a consistent basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

净财务杠杆（NFL）表示相对于股东权益的净金融融资规模：$NFL=NFD/E^*$，分子[[净金融负债]]与分母权益须采用同一时点或同一种期间平均。ABC 的期末 $762/960=0.79375$，即每元权益对应约 0.794 元净金融负债。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NFL measures net financial financing relative to equity: $NFL=NFD/E^*$. [[净金融负债|NFD]] and equity must share a date or averaging convention. ABC's ending value is 0.79375, about 0.794 units of net financial debt per unit of equity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它不等于[[产权比率]]的全部负债除权益，也不等于[[权益乘数]]的全部资产除权益。由[[管理用资产负债表恒等式]]，$NOA/E=1+NFL$；这个关系衡量融资结构，并不是固定经营成本引起利润敏感性的[[经营杠杆]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
NFL differs from [[产权比率|total liabilities-to-equity]] and the [[权益乘数|total assets-to-equity multiplier]]. The [[管理用资产负债表恒等式|management balance-sheet identity]] gives $NOA/E=1+NFL$. This concerns financing structure, not [[经营杠杆|operating leverage]] arising from fixed operating costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

权益为零时无定义；正权益下，负 NFL 表示净金融资产，而不是“负的违约风险”。杠杆对 ROE 的贡献还取决于经营回报与净融资成本之差，见[[管理用ROE分解]]；仅看 NFL 的高低不能判定偿债能力或股东价值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero equity makes NFL undefined. With positive equity, negative NFL means a net financial asset position, not negative default risk. Its contribution to ROE also depends on the operating-financing spread in [[管理用ROE分解|the ROE decomposition]]. NFL alone does not determine solvency or shareholder value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
