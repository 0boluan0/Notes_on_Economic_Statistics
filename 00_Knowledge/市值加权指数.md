# 市值加权指数按成分证券纳入计算的市场价值分配权重
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A capitalization-weighted index assigns weights in proportion to the market value included for each constituent.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

市值加权看的是“价格乘以纳入指数的股数”，而不只看单股价格。设纳入股数为 $q_{it}$，没有另加权重上限时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Capitalization weighting uses price times the share count included in the index, not share price alone. With included shares $q_{it}$ and no additional capping rule:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
I_t=\frac{\sum_i p_{it}q_{it}}{D_t},\qquad
w_{it}=\frac{p_{it}q_{it}}{\sum_jp_{jt}q_{jt}}.
$$

若甲价 10 元、有 1,000 股纳入，乙价 100 元、仅 20 股纳入，甲权重为 $10{,}000/12{,}000=5/6$。低单价不意味着低权重。总市值口径用总股数；自由流通市值口径按规则排除不供公众流通的部分。股数、样本和流通因子变化时，要按[[指数除数调整]]维持可比性，不能直接把市值新增当成价格上涨。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If A trades at 10 with 1,000 included shares and B at 100 with 20, A's weight is $5/6$. A lower share price does not imply a lower weight. Total-capitalization and free-float versions use different eligible share counts. Changes to shares, membership or float require the applicable [[指数除数调整|divisor adjustments]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在成分和持股数量不变、无须特殊公司行动处理的区间内，一期收益聚合使用期初权重，见[[单期组合收益]]；不是用期末权重倒乘同一期收益。若区间内再平衡或调整样本，就应先分段计算再链接各段收益。这个持续维护的指数也不等同于把不同期间的总市值直接作比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Over an interval with unchanged constituents and holdings and no special corporate-action treatment, return aggregation uses beginning weights, as in [[单期组合收益|portfolio returns]], not end weights multiplied by the same period's returns. Rebalancing or constituent changes within the interval require subperiod calculation and return linking. A maintained index is not simply the ratio of unadjusted market capitalizations at two dates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
