# 回归模型比较与选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Regression model comparison and selection*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 当多个回归模型都能拟合同一批数据时，根据实际目标判断哪个模型更合适，而不是把“样本内拟合最好”误当作唯一答案。
> **具体锚点：** 在工资预测中加入大量 polynomials 与 interactions 一定不会提高 training RSS，却可能让 validation error 变差；在因果研究中，删掉一个 p 值大的 confounder 还可能改变 estimand 并制造偏误。
> **核心难点：** nested F test、adjusted $R^2$、AIC/BIC 与 cross-validation 回答不同问题；任何 score 都只有在 response、sample、candidate set 和使用目标可比较时才有意义。
> **为什么重要：** model selection 同时影响 fit、generalization、interpretability 与后续 inference；若选择过程被隐藏，漂亮的 coefficient table 会低估真实 uncertainty。
> **继续：** 先读 [[#先决定模型用于什么]]，再按目标进入 [[#同一样本内怎样比较 fit 与复杂度]] 或 [[#预测目标：validation、cross-validation 与 test set]]；若问题是均值函数怎样写，转到 [[回归模型设定与函数形式]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** When several regressions fit the same data, model comparison chooses a model for the actual purpose instead of treating the best in-sample fit as the only answer.
> **Concrete anchor:** Adding many polynomials and interactions to a wage-prediction model cannot increase training RSS, yet it may worsen validation error. In a causal study, dropping a high-p-value confounder may change the estimand and create bias.
> **Central difficulty:** Nested F tests, adjusted $R^2$, AIC or BIC, and cross-validation answer different questions. A score is meaningful only when the response, sample, candidate set, and intended use are genuinely comparable.
> **Why it matters:** Model selection affects fit, generalisation, interpretability, and subsequent inference. If the selection process is hidden, a polished coefficient table understates uncertainty.
> **Continue with:** Begin by [[#先决定模型用于什么|deciding what the model is for]], then use [[#同一样本内怎样比较 fit 与复杂度|in-sample complexity criteria]] or [[#预测目标：validation、cross-validation 与 test set|out-of-sample validation]] as appropriate. For the construction of the conditional-mean function itself, continue to [[回归模型设定与函数形式|regression specification and functional form]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先决定模型用于什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First decide what the model is for*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“哪个模型最好”没有脱离目的的答案。至少要区分四种用途：描述 sample association、预测新 observations、估计某个结构或因果 parameter、以及用简洁模型传达机制。它们可能选择不同 variables、loss function 与 complexity penalty。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is no purpose-free answer to “which model is best.” At minimum, distinguish description of sample associations, prediction of new observations, estimation of a structural or causal parameter, and communication of a mechanism through a parsimonious model. These purposes may require different variables, loss functions, and complexity penalties.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 主要用途 | 首要问题 | 典型证据 | 不能据此推出 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 描述 | 哪个 equation 清楚概括当前 sample？ | residual pattern、effect scale、parsimony | 因果有效或样本外稳定 |
| 预测 | 对 future/held-out data 的 loss 多大？ | validation/test error、cross-validation | coefficient 是 causal effect |
| 因果解释 | specification 是否识别目标 estimand？ | research design、identification assumptions、sensitivity | 最高 $R^2$ 就是正确 causal model |
| 机制表达 | 变量和函数形式是否对应 theory？ | interpretable restrictions、external validity | 简单模型一定预测最好 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Primary use | First question | Typical evidence | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Description | Which equation clearly summarises the observed sample? | Residual patterns, effect scales, parsimony | Causal validity or out-of-sample stability |
| Prediction | What is the loss on future or held-out data? | Validation or test error, cross-validation | That a coefficient is a causal effect |
| Causal explanation | Does the specification identify the target estimand? | Research design, identification assumptions, sensitivity | That the highest $R^2$ is the correct causal model |
| Mechanism communication | Do the variables and functional form represent the theory? | Interpretable restrictions and external validity | That the simplest model predicts best |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较之前还要固定 comparability：同一个 response scale、同一 set of observations、相容的 likelihood 或 loss、以及明确的 candidate set。若一个 log-wage model 与 level-wage model 的 outcome scale 不同，或 missingness 让两个模型使用不同 rows，裸 $R^2$、RSS、AIC/BIC 往往不能直接横比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Comparability must also be fixed before scoring: the same response scale, the same observations, a compatible likelihood or loss, and an explicit candidate set. If one model uses log wages and another uses wage levels, or missingness gives the models different rows, raw $R^2$, RSS, AIC, or BIC values are generally not directly comparable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 同一样本内怎样比较 fit 与复杂度
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Comparing fit and complexity within one sample*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### R² 与 adjusted R²
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*$R^2$ and adjusted $R^2$*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有 intercept 且使用 ordinary least squares 时，
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With an intercept and ordinary least squares,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
R^2=1-\frac{RSS}{TSS}.
$$

$R^2$ 是样本中被线性拟合解释的变异比例，增加变量不下降；调整 $R^2$ 加惩罚但仍不是因果质量或样本外预测的保证。模型比较应按目的使用外样本误差、信息准则、理论限制或预先规定检验。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$R^2$ is the proportion of sample variation explained by the linear fit and cannot fall when regressors are added. Adjusted $R^2$ introduces a penalty, but it still does not guarantee causal validity or out-of-sample predictive accuracy. Depending on the purpose, model comparison should use out-of-sample error, information criteria, theoretical restrictions, or prespecified tests.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $k$ 包括 intercept，常用 adjusted form 是
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $k$ includes the intercept, a common adjusted form is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\bar R^2
=1-\frac{RSS/(n-k)}{TSS/(n-1)}.
$$

它只有在新增变量改善 fit 足够多时才会上升，但 penalty 来自 degrees of freedom，并没有真正把模型拿到 unseen data 上测试。高 $\bar R^2$ 也不能排除 residual curvature、endogeneity、data leakage 或 extrapolation。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It rises only when added variables improve fit enough to offset their degrees-of-freedom cost, but this penalty does not test the model on unseen data. A high $\bar R^2$ does not rule out residual curvature, endogeneity, data leakage, or extrapolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Nested F test 只检验明确的 restrictions
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A nested F test examines explicit restrictions only*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 smaller model 能由 larger model 设置 $q$ 个 coefficients 为特定值而得到，可以用 [[回归系数推断与线性假设检验#多个限制：F 与 Wald 联合检验|joint F/Wald test]] 判断这些 restrictions 与数据是否相容。它适合“这些 polynomials 是否 jointly zero”一类预先定义问题；它不是在无穷模型空间中自动找到 truth 的机器。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the smaller model is obtained from the larger one by fixing $q$ coefficients, a [[回归系数推断与线性假设检验#多个限制：F 与 Wald 联合检验|joint F or Wald test]] can assess whether those restrictions are compatible with the data. This is appropriate for a prespecified question such as whether several polynomial terms are jointly zero; it is not an automatic search for truth over an unlimited model space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当两个 models nonnested、response 不同、sample 不同，普通 nested F formula 没有合法的 restricted/unrestricted interpretation。即使模型 nested，反复 forward/backward testing 之后的最终 p-value 也不能当作一次预先指定检验来解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When models are nonnested, use different responses, or use different samples, the ordinary nested F formula has no valid restricted-versus-unrestricted interpretation. Even for nested models, the final p-value after repeated forward or backward testing is not equivalent to a single prespecified test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### AIC 与 BIC：likelihood fit 加 complexity penalty
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*AIC and BIC: likelihood fit plus a complexity penalty*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对在同一数据上、用可比较 likelihood 估计的候选模型，常见定义为
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For candidate models fitted to the same data with comparable likelihoods, common definitions are
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
AIC=-2\log L(\hat\theta)+2k,
\qquad
BIC=-2\log L(\hat\theta)+k\log n.
$$

第一项奖励 fit，第二项惩罚 parameters；在候选集合中数值更低者优先。AIC 与 BIC 的绝对数值没有独立实质含义，只有 differences 对同一次比较有用。$n$ 较大时 BIC penalty 通常更强，因此常偏向更简单模型；这不表示它在所有 prediction 或 causal tasks 中都更好。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term rewards fit and the second penalises the number of parameters; lower values are preferred within the candidate set. Absolute AIC or BIC values have no independent substantive meaning; only differences within the same comparison are useful. For larger $n$, BIC usually penalises complexity more strongly and therefore often favours simpler models. This does not make it universally superior for prediction or causal analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不同软件对 Gaussian regression 可能省略共同 constants，课程中的 $\log(\hat\sigma^2)+2k/n$ 等 scaled forms 也可能看起来不同。只要是在同一 convention、同一 sample、同一 likelihood 内求 differences，排序可以一致；不要跨软件和 outcome scales 比裸数字。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Software may omit common constants for Gaussian regression, and course formulas such as $\log(\hat\sigma^2)+2k/n$ may use a scaled form. Rankings can agree when differences are taken under one convention, sample, and likelihood. Raw values should not be compared across software conventions or response scales.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 预测目标：validation、cross-validation 与 test set
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Prediction: validation, cross-validation, and the test set*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参数相对样本过多会拟合噪声、样本内残差小但外样本差。预先设定、交叉验证、正则化和留出评估针对预测过拟合；它们不自动解决因果选择偏差。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Too many parameters relative to the sample fit noise, yielding small in-sample residuals but poor out-of-sample performance. Prespecification, cross-validation, regularization, and holdout evaluation address predictive overfitting; they do not automatically solve selection bias in causal analysis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个最基本划分是：training set 用于 estimate；validation set 或 cross-validation folds 用于选择 model/hyperparameters；test set 只在选择结束后做一次 final evaluation。若看过 test performance 后继续调模型，test set 已经变成 validation data，不再给诚实的 out-of-sample estimate。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The basic separation is that the training set estimates models, a validation set or cross-validation folds select the model and hyperparameters, and the test set is used once for final evaluation. If the model is tuned after inspecting test performance, the test set has become validation data and no longer provides an honest out-of-sample estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $K$-fold cross-validation 中，data 被分成 $K$ 份；每次在 $K-1$ 份训练、在剩下一份评价，最后平均 validation loss。所有会从数据学习的 preprocessing——imputation、standardisation、feature selection、PCA——都必须在每个 training fold 内重新 fit；先对 full dataset 处理会把 validation information 泄漏进 training。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In $K$-fold cross-validation, the data are divided into $K$ parts. Each model is trained on $K-1$ parts and evaluated on the remaining part, and the validation losses are averaged. Every data-dependent preprocessing step, including imputation, standardisation, feature selection, and principal-component analysis, must be fitted within each training fold. Preprocessing the full data first leaks validation information into training.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

random folds 还必须匹配 deployment structure：time series 应保留时间顺序，clusters 或同一人的 repeated observations 不应随意跨 folds，否则 validation task 比真正预测更容易。loss 也应匹配决策，例如 RMSE 强调大误差，MAE 的尺度不同，classification probability 需要 proper scoring rule。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Random folds must also match the deployment structure. Time-series validation should preserve temporal order, and clusters or repeated observations from one individual should not be split casually across folds; otherwise the validation task is easier than real prediction. The loss must match the decision as well: RMSE emphasises large errors, MAE has a different target, and probability forecasts require a proper scoring rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个对比例子：training fit 与 generalisation 分离
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A comparison example: separating training fit from generalisation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑两个用于预测 $\log(wage)$ 的 hypothetical models。Model A 使用 education、experience 和 $experience^2$；Model B 另加 20 个 interactions 与高阶 terms。假设同一 split 得到：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider two hypothetical models for predicting $\log(wage)$. Model A uses education, experience, and $experience^2$. Model B adds twenty interactions and high-order terms. Suppose the same split gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 模型 | Training RMSE | Validation RMSE | Parameters |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 0.312 | 0.329 | 4 |
| B | 0.244 | 0.381 | 24 |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Model | Training RMSE | Validation RMSE | Parameters |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 0.312 | 0.329 | 4 |
| B | 0.244 | 0.381 | 24 |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Model B 的 training fit 明显更好，因为增加 regressors 不会让 OLS RSS 上升；但 validation error 更差，说明新增 flexibility 在这个 prediction task 中主要拟合了 sample-specific noise。若目标改为检验一个 theory-specified interaction，不能仅因 validation RMSE 略差就删掉它；这再次说明 selection criterion 要服从问题，而不是反过来定义问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Model B fits the training data much better because adding regressors cannot increase OLS RSS, yet its validation error is worse. For this prediction task, the added flexibility has mainly fitted sample-specific noise. If the goal were instead to test a theory-specified interaction, a small deterioration in validation RMSE would not by itself justify deleting that term. The criterion must serve the question rather than define it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 因果模型为什么不能按 prediction score 自动删变量
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why a causal model cannot delete variables automatically by prediction score*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因果 analysis 的目标通常是 treatment coefficient，而不是最小化 outcome prediction error。一个 confounder 即使对 $Y$ 的 incremental prediction 很小，也可能因与 treatment 强相关而显著改变 omitted-variable bias；一个 post-treatment variable 可能提高 $R^2$，却改变或破坏原目标 estimand。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The target of causal analysis is often a treatment coefficient rather than minimum outcome-prediction error. A confounder may add little incremental prediction of $Y$ yet materially change omitted-variable bias because it is strongly related to treatment. A post-treatment variable may improve $R^2$ while changing or destroying the original estimand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此 causal variable selection 的顺序应是：先定义 treatment、outcome、population 和 estimand；依据 temporal ordering、theory 与 causal graph 区分 confounders、mediators、colliders；再使用 fit/precision 工具优化仍然合法的 specification。cross-validation、regularization 与 AIC/BIC 都不能独立证明 exogeneity，相关识别问题见 [[计量研究：从相关到因果]] 与 [[内生性、识别、IV、2SLS 与 GMM]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Causal variable selection should therefore define the treatment, outcome, population, and estimand first; use temporal order, theory, and a causal graph to distinguish confounders, mediators, and colliders; and only then optimise fit or precision among admissible specifications. Cross-validation, regularisation, AIC, and BIC cannot independently establish exogeneity. See [[计量研究：从相关到因果|econometric research from association to causality]] and [[内生性、识别、IV、2SLS 与 GMM|endogeneity and identification]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个可复用的选择流程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A reusable selection workflow*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 写出 use case、target estimand 或 prediction target，以及真正会遇到的新数据。
2. 定义 admissible candidate set；先排除 temporal ordering 或 theory 上不合法的 variables。
3. 保持 response、observations、weights 和 likelihood/loss 可比较。
4. 若检验 prespecified nested restrictions，用 F/Wald；若比较 likelihood models，用 AIC/BIC；若预测，用结构匹配的 validation。
5. 检查 residuals、stability、subgroups 与 influential observations；一个 scalar score 不能完成 model validation。
6. 记录 selection path，并在 final inference 中承认搜索；保留真正 untouched 的 test data。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. State the use case, target estimand or prediction target, and the new-data setting that will actually occur.
2. Define the admissible candidate set and first exclude variables that violate temporal ordering or the theory of the problem.
3. Keep the response, observations, weights, and likelihood or loss comparable.
4. Use an F or Wald test for prespecified nested restrictions, AIC or BIC for comparable likelihood models, and structurally appropriate validation for prediction.
5. Inspect residuals, stability, subgroups, and influential observations; one scalar score cannot validate a model.
6. Record the selection path, acknowledge search in final inference, and retain genuinely untouched test data.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 常见错误 | 后果 | 修复 |
|---|---|---|
| 选 training $R^2$ 最大的模型 | complexity 只会被奖励 | 用 penalty 或 out-of-sample loss |
| 在不同 samples 上比 AIC/BIC | likelihood difference 混入数据差异 | 固定 observations 与 likelihood convention |
| 用普通 F test 比 nonnested models | 没有合法的 restricted model | 使用适合目标的 information criterion、validation 或明确 nonnested method |
| 在 test set 上反复调参 | test performance 乐观偏误 | test set 只在流程结束后使用一次 |
| 按 p 值删 causal controls | 可能改变 estimand 或制造 omitted-variable bias | 先按 identification 决定 admissible controls |
| 隐藏 stepwise/search 过程 | nominal inference 过度自信 | 报告搜索并做 validation/sensitivity 或 post-selection inference |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Common error | Consequence | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| Select the model with the largest training $R^2$ | Complexity is rewarded without limit | Use a penalty or out-of-sample loss |
| Compare AIC or BIC across different samples | Likelihood differences are mixed with data differences | Fix the observations and likelihood convention |
| Use an ordinary F test for nonnested models | There is no valid restricted model | Use a purpose-appropriate information criterion, validation, or explicit nonnested method |
| Tune repeatedly on the test set | Test performance becomes optimistically biased | Use the test set once after the process is complete |
| Delete causal controls by p-value | The estimand may change or omitted-variable bias may appear | Determine admissible controls from identification first |
| Hide stepwise or search procedures | Nominal inference becomes overconfident | Report the search and use validation, sensitivity analysis, or post-selection inference |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么加入变量后 training $R^2$ 不会下降，却仍可能过拟合？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why can training $R^2$ never fall when variables are added, yet overfitting still occur?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 新模型包含旧模型作为一个特殊情况，OLS 至少可以把新 coefficients 设为 0，因此 training RSS 不会上升。但额外自由度可能追随当前 sample 的 noise，使 unseen-data error 上升。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The larger model contains the smaller model as a special case, so OLS can always set the new coefficients to zero and avoid increasing training RSS. The added flexibility may nevertheless track sample-specific noise and increase error on unseen data.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### adjusted R²、AIC/BIC 与 cross-validation 分别使用了什么信息？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What information do adjusted $R^2$, AIC or BIC, and cross-validation use?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> adjusted $R^2$ 在 sample fit 上做 degrees-of-freedom adjustment；AIC/BIC 对 maximised likelihood 加 parameter penalty；cross-validation 直接用 held-out folds 估计指定 prediction loss。三者的目标和 assumptions 不相同。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Adjusted $R^2$ applies a degrees-of-freedom adjustment to in-sample fit. AIC and BIC add a parameter penalty to the maximised likelihood. Cross-validation estimates a chosen predictive loss on held-out folds. Their targets and assumptions differ.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 两个模型使用不同 response transformations，能否直接比较 AIC？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Can AIC be compared directly when two models use different response transformations?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 通常不能直接比较裸值，因为 likelihood 位于不同 outcome scales，Jacobian 与 constants 也可能不同。应先建立同一 probability model 和可比较 likelihood，或在原决策尺度上比较 validated predictive loss。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Usually not by raw values, because the likelihoods are defined on different response scales and may differ in Jacobian terms and constants. Establish a comparable probability model or compare validated predictive loss on the decision scale.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一个 confounder 的 p 值很大，为什么仍不能自动删除？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a confounder not be deleted automatically when its p-value is large?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> confounding 取决于它同时与 treatment 和 outcome 的关系，不取决于它自己的 coefficient 是否显著。删除它可能让 treatment coefficient 吸收遗漏路径，改变目标估计。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Confounding depends on the variable's relations with both treatment and outcome, not on whether its own coefficient is significant. Deleting it may make the treatment coefficient absorb the omitted path and change the target estimate.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么 cross-validation 的 preprocessing 必须在每个 training fold 内完成？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must preprocessing be fitted inside each training fold during cross-validation?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 若先用全体 data 决定 scaling、imputation 或 features，validation fold 已经影响了 fitted pipeline，loss 会低估真正面对 unseen data 的误差。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If the full data determine scaling, imputation, or features, the validation fold has already influenced the fitted pipeline and the loss understates error on genuinely unseen data.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
