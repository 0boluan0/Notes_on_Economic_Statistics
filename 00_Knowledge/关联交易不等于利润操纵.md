# 关联关系或关联交易本身不足以证明利润操纵
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A related-party relationship or transaction alone does not establish earnings manipulation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[关联方交易]]是需要特别理解与披露的经济关系，不是“虚假交易”的同义词。关联关系使定价、信用和业务选择可能不同于独立交易，所以它提升核查必要性，却不自动给出有罪结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[关联方交易|related-party transaction]] requires special understanding and disclosure, but is not synonymous with a fictitious transaction. Relatedness may affect prices, credit, and business choices, warranting scrutiny without itself proving wrongdoing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如母公司以有合同、实际交付和合理成本分摊证据的方式向子公司提供服务，可以是真实交易。反例方向上，即使双方没有关联关系，虚构交付或提前确认收入也可能导致报表失真；“是否关联”不是充分的真假分类器。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A parent may genuinely supply a subsidiary under documented delivery and supportable cost allocation. Conversely, parties need not be related for fictitious delivery or premature revenue recognition to distort reporting. Relatedness alone cannot classify transactions as true or false.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要进一步判断，应检查经济实质、定价依据、回款、会计确认和披露，并按报告主体处理内部抵销。也不能因管理层声称“按市场价格”就停止核查：IAS 24 要求“与独立交易条件相当”的披露能够被证实。完整程序见[[盈利质量核查]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Examine substance, pricing evidence, collection, recognition, disclosure, and entity-specific consolidation treatment. Management's arm's-length assertion is not enough: IAS 24 requires such an assertion to be substantiated. See [[盈利质量核查|earnings-quality checks]] for the procedure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
