# 贸易、产业政策与全球价值链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Trade, Industrial Policy, and Global Value Chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 判断一个发展中经济体如何通过贸易、外资与有针对性的产业政策进入更高生产率活动，同时避免把保护变成永久租金。
> **具体锚点：** 临时支持一家刚开始生产的零部件企业，可让它在实干中学习并进入跨国供应链；若没有出口、生产率或退出纪律，同一支持可只保护高成本企业并伤害下游。
> **核心难点：** 政策必须区分真实学习外部性与企业对补贴的私人需求，并解决政府也不知道哪些行业会成功的信息问题。
> **为什么重要：** 静态比较优势不能单独回答一个国家如何获得新能力；但动态学习也不是任意保护的通行证。
> **继续：** 政策是否能执行、如何把公共投入与工业支持组合，见 [[发展政策的国家能力、公共投入与评价]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It asks how a developing economy can use trade, foreign investment, and targeted industrial policy to enter higher-productivity activities without turning support into permanent rents.
> **Concrete anchor:** Temporary support for a new component producer can enable learning by doing and entry into a multinational supply chain. Without export, productivity, or exit discipline, the same support may protect a high-cost firm and harm downstream users.
> **Central difficulty:** Policy must distinguish a genuine learning externality from a firm's private demand for subsidy while addressing the government's own uncertainty about which sectors can succeed.
> **Why it matters:** Static comparative advantage cannot by itself explain how a country acquires new capabilities, but dynamic learning is not a license for arbitrary protection.
> **Continue:** For implementation capacity and the combination of public inputs with industrial support, see [[发展政策的国家能力、公共投入与评价|State Capacity, Public Inputs, and Evaluation in Development Policy]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 贸易的静态收益与动态能力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Static gains from trade and dynamic capabilities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开放可扩大市场、引入投入和技术并按比较优势配置，但调整成本、学习和分配并不自动解决。静态贸易收益与动态产业能力是不同问题；汇率、物流和规则决定企业能否进入出口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Openness can enlarge markets, introduce inputs and technology, and reallocate activity according to comparative advantage, but it does not automatically resolve adjustment costs, learning, or distribution. Static gains from trade and dynamic industrial capability are different questions; exchange rates, logistics, and rules determine whether firms can export.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一项贸易改革的完整评估要至少追踪四条路径：消费者和下游获得更便宜的商品/投入；进口竞争提高或淘汰本地企业；出口市场扩大规模；劳动者和资本在地区与行业间调整。只报总出口额无法判断这四条路径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A complete trade-reform evaluation should track at least four paths: cheaper goods and inputs for consumers and downstream firms; productivity gains or exit under import competition; scale expansion through export markets; and reallocation of workers and capital across regions and industries. Total exports alone cannot distinguish these paths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 产业政策的经济理由
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Economic rationales for industrial policy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

学习外部性、协调失败、融资约束和信息发现可支持干预。工具包括基础设施、研发、信贷、采购和暂时保护。有效设计需明确可测目标、竞争/出口纪律、退出机制，防止寻租和永久保护。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Learning externalities, coordination failures, financing constraints, and information discovery can justify intervention. Tools include infrastructure, R&D, credit, procurement, and temporary protection. Effective design needs measurable objectives, competition or export discipline, and an exit mechanism to limit rent seeking and permanent protection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

诊断顺序应是“市场失败—工具—可验证中间结果—退出规则”。例如若理由是学习外溢，便应测量单位成本随累积产量下降、人员流动或供应商扩散；若只观察被支持企业的产出增加，无法证明外溢。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The diagnostic sequence should be “market failure → instrument → verifiable intermediate outcome → exit rule.” If the rationale is learning spillover, measure whether unit cost falls with cumulative production and whether knowledge diffuses through workers or suppliers. Output growth in the subsidized firm alone does not establish a spillover.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 外资、技术与全球价值链
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Foreign investment, technology, and global value chains*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

FDI 可带来资本、管理和市场接入，spillover 取决于本地技能、供应商联系和吸收能力。只进入低附加值环节未必自动升级；政策要关注学习和国内连接，而非仅流入规模。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Foreign direct investment can bring capital, management, and market access, but spillovers depend on local skills, supplier links, and absorptive capacity. Entry into a low-value-added stage does not guarantee upgrading; policy should focus on learning and domestic linkages rather than inflow volume alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如外资组装厂的出口很高，但若零部件全部进口、管理与设计职能不在本地、工人离职后技能不可转移，国内增值与能力扩散可很小。更好的指标包括国内增值、本地供应商生产率、人员流动和功能升级。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An export-oriented foreign assembly plant can export heavily while importing every component, keeping design and management abroad, and transferring few skills when workers leave. Domestic value added and capability diffusion may remain small. Better indicators include domestic value added, local-supplier productivity, personnel mobility, and functional upgrading.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 全球风险与韧性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Global risk and resilience*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

商品周期、债务、气候、疫情和地缘冲突改变外部约束。韧性政策要权衡多样化与效率，并避免把“自给”当无成本保险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Commodity cycles, debt, climate, pandemics, and geopolitical conflict change external constraints. Resilience policy must trade off diversification against efficiency and avoid treating self-sufficiency as costless insurance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

供应链韧性不等于每种中间品都国产。它可来自多个可切换供应商、合理库存、通用设计、贸易协定与危机时的物流能力。本地生产若依赖同一进口关键投入，地理上国产也不一定减少风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Supply-chain resilience does not mean producing every intermediate domestically. It can come from switchable suppliers, sensible inventories, modular design, trade agreements, and crisis logistics. Domestic production that depends on one imported critical input need not reduce risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 用出口额或 FDI 流入规模直接代表国内学习和增值。
- 只证明被保护企业活下来，却不证明它已降低成本、外溢知识或接近无支持竞争力。
- 以“市场会选”否认所有协调与学习失败，或以“动态比较优势”为任何产业补贴辩护。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Using export value or FDI inflow as a direct proxy for domestic learning and value added.
- Showing that a protected firm survived without showing lower cost, knowledge spillover, or movement toward competitiveness without support.
- Denying every coordination or learning failure because “the market selects,” or defending any subsidy through “dynamic comparative advantage.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 幼稚产业保护要可信需要哪些条件？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What conditions make infant-industry protection credible?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 可识别学习外部性、暂时性、绩效/出口纪律、竞争与退出机制，以及能防止俘获的执行能力。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An identifiable learning externality, temporariness, performance or export discipline, competition and exit mechanisms, and implementation capacity that limits capture.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### FDI 流入为什么不自动产生技术扩散？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does FDI not automatically create technology spillovers?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 需要本地技能、供应链连接、人员流动和吸收能力；外资也可能形成封闭飞地。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Spillovers require local skills, supply-chain links, personnel mobility, and absorptive capacity; foreign investment can instead remain an enclave.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话区分静态贸易收益与动态能力建设。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words the difference between static gains from trade and dynamic capability building.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 前者来自给定生产能力下的专业化、竞争和更便宜投入；后者是经济体通过学习、技能、供应商和创新获得原本没有的生产能力。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Static gains come from specialization, competition, and cheaper inputs given existing capabilities. Dynamic capability building creates new productive abilities through learning, skills, suppliers, and innovation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
