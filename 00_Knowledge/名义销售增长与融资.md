# 价格与销量共同决定名义销售增长而融资影响取决于经营资金随名义收入变化的假设
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price and volume jointly determine nominal sales growth, while its financing effect depends on how operating investment responds to nominal revenue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对业务组合不变的单一产品，收入等于价格乘销量。若价格增长 $\pi$、销量增长 $q$，名义销售增长率为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a single product with unchanged mix, revenue equals price times volume. Price growth $\pi$ and volume growth $q$ imply nominal sales growth:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$g_S=(1+\pi)(1+q)-1=\pi+q+\pi q.$$

价格涨 10%、销量涨 5%，收入增长是 15.5%，不是 15%。这里的价格必须是企业实际实现的同口径售价；一般消费物价指数不是这家企业的售价预测。多产品情况应按[[收入驱动预测]]分别考虑组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A 10% price increase and 5% volume increase give 15.5% revenue growth, not 15%. The price must be the firm's realised comparable selling price; a general consumer-price index is not its own price forecast. Handle product mix through [[收入驱动预测|revenue-driver forecasting]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若进一步假定净经营资产与**名义销售额**保持固定比例，单纯涨价也会使模型预测更大的资金占用。例如 $S_0=3000$、净经营资产收入比 0.605、净利率 4.5%、留存率 70%、无可动用金融资产；上述 15.5% 增长给出：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If net operating assets are further assumed to remain a fixed fraction of nominal sales, even price-only growth increases modelled investment needs. With initial sales of 3,000, a 0.605 net-operating-asset ratio, 4.5% margin, 70% retention, and no available financial assets, 15.5% sales growth gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$EFN=0.605\times465-0.045\times0.70\times3465=172.1775.$$

但这个融资结果依赖比例假设，不是通胀的机械定律。账面固定资产不会因产品涨价就立即同比重估，应收取决于回款条件，存货采购价格也未必与售价同涨；成本和净利率可能同时变化。应先分别改这些驱动，再重新求[[外部融资需求]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This funding result depends on proportionality, not an automatic law of inflation. Book fixed assets do not instantly revalue with selling prices; receivables depend on collection terms and inventory purchase prices may move differently. Costs and margins can also change. Revise those drivers before recomputing [[外部融资需求|external financing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
