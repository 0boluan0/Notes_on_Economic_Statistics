# 内含增长率是在既定经营比例下仅靠留存利润补足净经营资产增长的销售增长率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under the net-operating-asset convention, internal growth is the sales growth funded entirely by retained profit at fixed operating ratios.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 CPA 的融资模型中，内含增长率是不动用金融资产、不新增融资性借款或股权，仅靠当期留存利润补足新增[[净经营资产]]时的销售增长率。经营负债仍可随销售自然增加，所以这里的“无外部融资”不是说所有负债都保持不变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the CPA financing model, internal growth uses neither available financial assets nor new financing debt/equity: retained current income alone funds the increase in [[净经营资产|net operating assets]]. Operating liabilities may still grow spontaneously with sales, so “no external financing” does not mean that every liability is fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设经营资产和经营负债占销售比分别为 $a,l$，净利率为 $m$，留存率为 $b$，以上比率均保持不变。基期销售 $S_0>0$，增长率为 $g$。让[[外部融资需求]]为零：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let operating assets and liabilities be fixed fractions $a,l$ of sales, with constant net margin $m$ and retention $b$. For initial sales $S_0>0$ and growth $g$, set [[外部融资需求|EFN]] to zero:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$0=(a-l)S_0g-mbS_0(1+g).$$

当 $a-l>mb\ge0$ 时，解得有限的非负内含增长率：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $a-l>mb\ge0$, the finite nonnegative solution is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$g_{\mathrm{int}}=\frac{mb}{a-l-mb}.$$

例如基期销售 3,000、经营资产 2,000、经营负债 185，故 $a-l=1815/3000=0.605$。保持净利率 4.5%、留存率 70%，得到 $g=0.0315/(0.605-0.0315)\approx5.49\%$。这个点上新增留存刚好抵消经营净占用的增加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With initial sales of 3,000, operating assets of 2,000, and operating liabilities of 185, $a-l=0.605$. A 4.5% net margin and 70% retention give internal growth of approximately 5.49%. Retained income exactly covers the additional net operating investment at that point.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在上述正分母条件下，计划增长超过该值会产生正融资缺口，低于它则可能有剩余。若净经营资产为负、经营比例改变或 $mb\ge a-l$，该式不再给出同样的正有限增长上界，应直接检查资金方程。其他教材以总资产且不允许自然负债增加定义内含增长，分母口径不同，不能只看 IGR 缩写就互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Under these positive-denominator conditions, faster planned growth creates a funding gap and slower growth may leave a surplus. Negative net operating assets, changing ratios, or $mb\ge a-l$ require examining the funding equation directly. Texts defining internal growth with total assets and no spontaneous liability increase use a different convention; the abbreviation IGR does not make the formulas interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
