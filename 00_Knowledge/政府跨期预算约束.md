# 政府跨期预算约束把期初债务连接到未来初级盈余与终端债务的现值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The government intertemporal budget constraint links initial debt to the present values of future primary surpluses and terminal debt.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

跨期预算约束把每年的收支接成同一条时间线。先考虑确定性、单一计价单位、利率路径已给定的简化政府账户，没有货币发行、资产交易、估值变化或违约减记。$B_0$为时点0债务，$PB_t$为第$t$期末的[[初级预算余额|初级盈余]]，$i_t>-1$为这期利率，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Combine annual accounts along one timeline. Consider a deterministic single-currency account with given interest rates and no money issuance, asset transactions, revaluation or default write-downs. Let $B_0$ be date-0 debt, $PB_t$ the end-period [[初级预算余额|primary surplus]] and $i_t>-1$ the period rate:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
B_t=(1+i_t)B_{t-1}-PB_t.
$$

令$D_t=\prod_{s=1}^{t}(1+i_s)$、$D_0=1$。把每期方程除以$D_t$，利用$D_t=(1+i_t)D_{t-1}$，得到$B_{t-1}/D_{t-1}=PB_t/D_t+B_t/D_t$。连续相加，中间债务项抵销：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $D_t=\prod_{s=1}^{t}(1+i_s)$ and $D_0=1$. Divide each budget by $D_t$ and rearrange to obtain $B_{t-1}/D_{t-1}=PB_t/D_t+B_t/D_t$. Summation cancels intermediate debt terms:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
B_0=\sum_{t=1}^{T}\frac{PB_t}{D_t}+\frac{B_T}{D_T}.
$$

这正是[[现金流同点折现加总|逐期折现到同一时点]]。例如$B_0=100$、每期利率5%、第一期盈余20、第二期偿清，必须有$PB_2=89.25$：第一期末债务85，第二期本息89.25，而$20/1.05+89.25/1.05^2=100$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This applies [[现金流同点折现加总|common-date discounting]]. With initial debt 100, interest 5%, first-period surplus 20 and full repayment in period 2, the second surplus must be 89.25. Debt after period 1 is 85, and the two surpluses discount to 100.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有另加$\lim_{T\to\infty}B_T/D_T=0$及现值收敛条件，才能把无限期式写为$B_0=\sum_{t\ge1}PB_t/D_t$。有限期恒等式本身没有让终端项消失；债务占GDP趋零，也不等于按利率折现后的债务趋零。无庞氏融资限制与终端资产的处理必须说清，不能只用“以后总会还”替代。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The infinite-horizon equality without terminal debt requires $\lim_{T\to\infty}B_T/D_T=0$ and convergence. The finite identity does not imply this condition, and debt vanishing relative to GDP need not vanish when discounted at interest rates. State the no-Ponzi restriction and treatment of terminal assets explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

加入货币发行、资产收益与处置、风险或违约时，要相应扩展账户和估值，不能原样搬用这个简化式；政府是否存在可行的实际履约路径，另见[[债务可持续性]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Money issuance, asset income and sales, risk or default require extended accounts and valuation. Whether an implementable repayment path exists is the separate question of [[债务可持续性|debt sustainability]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
