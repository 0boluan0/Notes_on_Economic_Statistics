# Convergence in L¹
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

L¹收敛（平均收敛）

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sequence converges in **$L^1$** when its average absolute error relative to a specified random variable tends to zero. First compare the two values obtained in the same outcome, then take the size of the difference, and finally average using their probabilities. This measures typical error while still counting the contribution of rare large errors.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

序列在**$L^1$中收敛**，是指它与某个指定随机变量之间的平均绝对误差趋于零：先比较同一次结果中的两个值，再取差的绝对大小，最后按概率求平均。这样既考虑常见误差，也计入罕见大误差的贡献。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[随机变量|random variables]] $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ and $X$ are defined on one [[概率空间|probability space]], so $X_n-X$ has an outcome-by-outcome meaning. Assume they are integrable, meaning $E|X_n|<\infty$ and $E|X|<\infty$. The [[期望|expectation]] here is a weighted average over possible outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[随机变量]]$X_1,X_2,\ldots$和$X$定义在同一个[[概率空间]]上，才能逐个结果比较$X_n-X$。假设它们可积，即$E|X_n|<\infty$且$E|X|<\infty$；这里的[[期望]]是按可能结果的概率加权的平均。

> [!important] Mean absolute error tends to zero · 平均绝对误差趋零
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> We write $X_n\xrightarrow{L^1}X$ when $E|X_n-X|\to0$. Equivalently, for every $\varepsilon>0$, all sufficiently late terms have $E|X_n-X|<\varepsilon$. The notation $\|Y\|_1=E|Y|$ turns the same condition into $\|X_n-X\|_1\to0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $X_n\xrightarrow{L^1}X$表示$E|X_n-X|\to0$。等价地，对每个$\varepsilon>0$，从某项起全部平均绝对误差都小于$\varepsilon$。若记$\|Y\|_1=E|Y|$，同一条件可写成$\|X_n-X\|_1\to0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Compute an error before naming the convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先算误差，再判断收敛

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $B$ equal zero or one with equal probability, and put $X_n=B+1/n$, $X=B$. For every outcome the error is exactly $1/n$, so $E|X_n-X|=1/n\to0$. The limit is random, but the error calculation is simple.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令$B$以相同概率取0或1，$X_n=B+1/n$，$X=B$。每个结果的误差都恰好为$1/n$，故$E|X_n-X|=1/n\to0$。极限可以是随机的，误差仍能直接计算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now let $U$ be uniform on $(0,1)$ and $Y_n=n\mathbf1_{\{U\le1/n\}}$. The [[指示变量|indicator]] makes $Y_n$ equal $n$ with probability $1/n$, and zero otherwise. Although the nonzero probability shrinks, $E|Y_n-0|=n(1/n)=1$. Therefore $Y_n$ does not converge to zero in $L^1$. The full tail analysis is in [[L1有界不推一致可积|the rare-spike counterexample]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再令$U$在$(0,1)$均匀分布，$Y_n=n\mathbf1_{\{U\le1/n\}}$。[[指示变量]]让$Y_n$以$1/n$概率取$n$，其余为零。非零概率虽然变小，$E|Y_n-0|=n(1/n)=1$却不变，因此它不在$L^1$中收敛到零。完整尾部分析见[[L1有界不推一致可积|稀有尖峰反例]]。

> [!warning] Do not cancel positive and negative errors · 不让正负误差抵消
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> $E|X_n-X|$ is not $|E[X_n]-E[X]|$. If every $X_n$ equals a fair $\pm1$ variable and $X=0$, the means agree but $E|X_n-X|=1$. Equal means alone do not establish $L^1$ convergence.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $E|X_n-X|$不是$|E[X_n]-E[X]|$。若每个$X_n$都等于一个公平取$\pm1$的变量，而$X=0$，期望虽然相同，平均绝对误差仍为1。只看期望相等，不能证明$L^1$收敛。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two useful consequences
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个直接后果

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking expectations preserves the limit because cancellation can only reduce an average absolute error:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由于正负抵消只会缩小误差，有

$$|E[X_n]-E[X]|\le E|X_n-X|\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It also implies [[依概率收敛|convergence in probability]]. For any fixed tolerance $\varepsilon>0$, apply [[Markov不等式|Markov’s inequality]] to the nonnegative error:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

它也推出[[依概率收敛]]。对任意固定容差$\varepsilon>0$，给非负误差应用[[Markov不等式]]：

$$P(|X_n-X|>\varepsilon)\le\frac{E|X_n-X|}{\varepsilon}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The reverse direction needs tail control; the [[一致可积与L1收敛|uniform-integrability criterion]] supplies the exact condition. Replacing the absolute error by its square gives the different notion of [[均方收敛|mean-square convergence]]. Neither definition means that every individual error must decrease monotonically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反向需要额外控制尾部，[[一致可积与L1收敛|一致可积判据]]给出准确条件。把绝对误差换成误差平方，则得到另一个概念——[[均方收敛]]。这两种定义都不要求每一个误差逐步单调变小。

- [Durrett, Lemma2.2.2, printed p57](https://math.duke.edu/~rtd/PTE/PTE5_011119.pdf#page=65)：取$p=1$得到平均绝对误差趋零推出依概率收敛。正文的三个简单误差例子直接计算核验。
