# 融资利息循环需要同时求解借款增加与利息压低留存利润的反馈
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Financing-interest circularity requires jointly solving additional borrowing and its effect on retained profit through interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

预计利润越少，内部留存越少，所需借款越多；借款越多，利息又会进一步减少利润。这一闭环不能靠“先算完利润，再借刚好够的钱”解决，除非预计利润本来已经包括相应利息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lower forecast profit means less retention and more borrowing; more borrowing adds interest and further reduces profit. Computing profit first and borrowing the apparent gap does not close this loop unless that profit already includes the corresponding interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个可直接解出的模型说明。$K$ 是扣除可动用金融资产后、扣留存利润之前的资金需要；缺口全部由新增借款 $D$ 填补。设 $NI^*$ 已扣现有债务利息、尚未扣新借款利息，新借款全年计息率为 $r$，留存率为 $b$；新增利息可按税率 $T$ 当期抵税，且这些假设不随 $D$ 变化：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $K$ be the need after available financial assets but before retained profit, funded entirely by new debt $D$. Let $NI^*$ include existing interest but exclude new interest. New debt incurs a full year's rate $r$, retention is $b$, and new interest is currently deductible at tax rate $T$. Hold these assumptions fixed:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$NI=NI^*-rD(1-T),\qquad D=K-bNI.$$

代入并整理：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Substitution gives:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$D=\frac{K-bNI^*}{1-br(1-T)}.$$

例如 $K=100$、$NI^*=50$、$b=80\%$、$r=10\%$、$T=25\%$，先忽略反馈会得到借款 60。联立后 $D=60/0.94\approx63.83$，利息压低净利润约 4.79，留存约 36.17，两者相加才回到资金需要 100。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With $K=100$, $NI^*=50$, $b=80\%$, $r=10\%$, and $T=25\%$, ignoring feedback suggests debt of 60. Solving jointly gives debt of about 63.83, net income lower by about 4.79, and retained income of about 36.17. Debt plus retention then equals 100.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

线性模型也能迭代：每次以新借款更新利息，再更新留存和借款。但这一迭代只有在反馈系数满足 $|br(1-T)|<1$ 时才从任意起点收敛到唯一固定点；“多算几次”不是一般保证。分母为零时要检查是否无解或不唯一；算出负借款时则应切换到资金剩余、还债或投资的处理规则。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The linear model can also be iterated, updating interest, retention, and debt. Convergence from any starting value requires $|br(1-T)|<1$; repetition alone is not a general guarantee. A zero denominator requires checking nonexistence or nonuniqueness. Negative borrowing calls for a surplus, repayment, or investment regime instead.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实际借款若只占用半年、利率随杠杆变化、存在额度上限，或者亏损使税盾暂不可用，就要重写相应关系，按[[现金预算]]确定时点。固定净利率只是另一种明确的预测假设；项目值不值得做仍由[[净现值决策]]判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Half-year borrowing, leverage-dependent rates, credit limits, or unusable tax deductions require revised equations and timing from the [[现金预算|cash budget]]. A fixed net margin is an alternative forecast assumption; [[净现值决策|NPV]] remains the test of whether the project creates value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
