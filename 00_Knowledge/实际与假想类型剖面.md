# 类型空间中只有一个类型剖面对应所建模的实际情形，其余剖面可以只为表达玩家的信念层级而存在
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Only one type profile in a type space corresponds to the modeled actual situation; the remaining profiles may exist solely to represent the players' belief hierarchies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心判断
> 在给定的实际不完全信息情形中，玩家真实拥有一个类型剖面 $t^*=(t_i^*)_{i\in N}$。类型空间仍会列出其他 $t\in T$，因为实际类型可能认为别的类型、别的信念或别的高阶信念是可能的。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the actual incomplete-information situation being modeled, the players have one realized type profile $t^*=(t_i^*)_{i\in N}$. The type space nevertheless contains other $t\in T$ because the actual types may consider different types, beliefs, or higher-order beliefs possible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 假想剖面的表示作用
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Representational Role of Hypothetical Profiles*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

类型 $t_i^*$ 的[[事中信念]]把概率赋给 $\Theta\times T_{-i}$。被赋予概率的其他玩家类型又各自携带 beliefs，于是模型能够递归表示“我怎样看对方，以及我怎样看对方怎样看我”。为完成这套表示，类型空间可以包含并未在实际情形中实现的类型剖面。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[事中信念|interim belief]] of type $t_i^*$ assigns probabilities over $\Theta\times T_{-i}$. Those possible types of the other players carry beliefs of their own, allowing the model to represent recursively what one player believes about another player's beliefs. This representation may require type profiles that are not realized in the actual situation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，“某个类型剖面出现在类型空间中”只说明它属于表示所需的可能情形，不能单独推出对应的人、信号或事件在现实中实际发生。实际性由被建模的实现状态决定，不由类型列表本身决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Accordingly, the presence of a type profile in the type space establishes only that it is a possible situation needed by the representation. It does not by itself show that the corresponding person, signal, or event actually occurs. Actuality is determined by the modeled realized state, not by membership in the type list.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
