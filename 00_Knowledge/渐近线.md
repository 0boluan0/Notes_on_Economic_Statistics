<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Asymptotes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*渐近线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **asymptote** is a straight line that describes how a graph approaches an infinite end. For $f(x)=1+1/x$, very large positive inputs give values close to $1$, so the line $y=1$ describes the far-right end. Near $x=0$ from the right, the output grows without bound while the graph approaches the vertical line $x=0$. The input direction and the output behaviour are different parts of each statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**渐近线**是描述图像在某个无限延伸方向上如何靠近的直线。对于 $f(x)=1+1/x$，很大的正输入使输出接近 $1$，所以直线 $y=1$ 描述图像的右端行为。从右侧靠近 $x=0$ 时，输出无界增大，图像则靠近竖直线 $x=0$。每个陈述都要分清输入趋近方式与输出行为。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[函数极限|finite limit]] means that the output error eventually stays below every positive tolerance. An [[无穷极限与无穷远极限|infinite output limit]] means that every sufficiently close allowed input passes each signed height threshold. Here we use the usual calculus definitions below for real-valued $f$; they refer to the whole indicated side or infinite direction, not just a selected sequence of graph points.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[函数极限|有限极限]]表示输出误差最终保持在任意正容差内；[[无穷极限与无穷远极限|输出的无穷极限]]则要求所有充分近的合法输入都越过任意指定的带方向高度门槛。本卡对实值函数 $f$ 采用下面通常的微积分定义，检查的是整个指定侧或无穷方向，而不只是挑出的某一列图像点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A vertical asymptote needs one infinite one-sided limit
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一侧出现无穷极限就可确立竖直渐近线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The line $x=a$ is a **vertical asymptote** if at least one of the following [[双侧极限判定|one-sided limits]] is $+\infty$ or $-\infty$. The chosen side must contain allowed inputs arbitrarily close to $a$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若以下[[双侧极限判定|单侧极限]]中至少一个等于 $+\infty$ 或 $-\infty$，就称直线 $x=a$ 为**竖直渐近线**。讨论的那一侧必须确实存在任意靠近 $a$ 的合法输入。

$$\lim_{x\to a^-}f(x),\qquad\lim_{x\to a^+}f(x).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=1/x$, the right limit at zero is $+\infty$ and the left limit is $-\infty$; both identify the same vertical line. Agreement of the two signs is unnecessary. A function defined only for $x>0$, such as $\ln x$, can have the vertical asymptote $x=0$ using just its right limit $-\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x)=1/x$，零点右极限为 $+\infty$，左极限为 $-\infty$；它们识别的是同一条竖直线，不要求两个符号相同。只在 $x>0$ 定义的 $\ln x$ 也能仅凭右极限 $-\infty$，得到竖直渐近线 $x=0$。

> [!warning] A hole and unbounded oscillation require different checks · 空洞与无界振荡要分别检查
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $(x^2-1)/(x-1)$ with $x\ne1$, cancellation gives nearby values $x+1$ and the finite limit $2$. The missing point is a removable hole, not a vertical asymptote. Also, $\sin(1/x)/x$ is unbounded on each side near zero but has zeros at $x=1/(n\pi)$ and their negatives, for positive integers $n$. It has no signed infinite limit on either side, so zero is not a vertical asymptote under the stated convention. Unboundedness alone is insufficient.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $x\ne1$ 上的 $(x^2-1)/(x-1)$，约分给出附近取值 $x+1$，极限为有限数 $2$，因此缺点是可去空洞，不是竖直渐近线。另一方面，$\sin(1/x)/x$ 在零点两侧都无界，却在 $x=1/(n\pi)$ 及其相反数处为零，其中 $n$ 为正整数。它在任一侧都没有带符号的无穷极限，所以按上述约定，零点不是竖直渐近线。仅有无界性并不够。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These limits omit the input $a$. Assigning a finite value at $a$ cannot remove an existing infinite side limit. For instance, defining $f(0)=0$ while keeping $f(x)=1/x$ for $x\ne0$ leaves $x=0$ a vertical asymptote; it adds one graph point on that line without making the function continuous there.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些极限排除输入 $a$ 本身，因此在 $a$ 补一个有限值不能消除已存在的单侧无穷极限。例如保留 $x\ne0$ 时的 $f(x)=1/x$，再定义 $f(0)=0$，仍有竖直渐近线 $x=0$；这只是在该直线上加了一个图像点，并没有使函数在那里连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Horizontal and sloping lines are checked by a vanishing difference
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用差值趋零检验水平线与斜线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix one direction, $x\to+\infty$ or $x\to-\infty$, in which the domain is unbounded. For real constants $m,b$, the line $y=mx+b$ is an asymptote in that direction if
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定一个定义域无界的方向：$x\to+\infty$ 或 $x\to-\infty$。对实常数 $m,b$，若下式成立，直线 $y=mx+b$ 就是该方向上的渐近线：

$$f(x)-(mx+b)\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The difference measures the vertical gap at the same input. Its absolute value divided by the fixed positive number $\sqrt{1+m^2}$ is the perpendicular distance to the line, so this condition also gives distance tending to zero. If $m=0$, the line is **horizontal**, $y=b$, and the condition is simply $f(x)\to b$. If $m\ne0$, it is an **oblique** or slant asymptote.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

差值测量同一输入处的竖直间距。把其绝对值除以固定正数 $\sqrt{1+m^2}$，便是到直线的垂直距离，因此这个条件也使距离趋于零。$m=0$ 时是**水平渐近线** $y=b$，条件就是 $f(x)\to b$；$m\ne0$ 时称为**斜渐近线**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=(x^2+1)/x=x+1/x$, the difference from $y=x$ is $1/x$, tending to zero in either infinite direction. Both ends therefore have the same slant asymptote. They need not do so: for $g(x)=\sqrt{x^2+1}$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x)=(x^2+1)/x=x+1/x$，与 $y=x$ 的差为 $1/x$，在两个无穷方向都趋于零，所以两端有同一条斜渐近线。但两端也可以不同：对于 $g(x)=\sqrt{x^2+1}$，

$$g(x)-|x|=\frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+1}+|x|}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus its right asymptote is $y=x$ and its left asymptote is $y=-x$. The absolute value cannot be replaced by $x$ on the negative side.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此右端渐近线是 $y=x$，左端是 $y=-x$；负半轴上不能把绝对值直接换成 $x$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Finding a candidate line and checking it
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先找候选直线，再检验*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In one fixed infinite direction, an affine asymptote must satisfy $m=\lim f(x)/x$ and $b=\lim(f(x)-mx)$, with both limits finite. Indeed, dividing $f(x)=mx+b+o(1)$ by $x$ gives the first limit; subtracting $mx$ gives the second. Here $o(1)$ denotes an error tending to zero. Conversely, a finite second limit $b$ proves that the difference from $mx+b$ tends to zero. Finding the slope limit alone is not enough: for $f(x)=x+\sqrt{x}$ on $(0,\infty)$, $f(x)/x\to1$ but $f(x)-x\to+\infty$, so there is no affine asymptote at $+\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在固定的一个无穷方向，仿射渐近线必须满足 $m=\lim f(x)/x$、$b=\lim(f(x)-mx)$，且两个极限都有限。理由是：由 $f(x)=mx+b+o(1)$ 除以 $x$ 可得第一式，减去 $mx$ 可得第二式；这里 $o(1)$ 只是趋于零的误差。反过来，第二个极限若为有限数 $b$，就已经证明与 $mx+b$ 的差趋于零。只找到斜率极限并不够：在 $(0,\infty)$ 上，$f(x)=x+\sqrt{x}$ 满足 $f(x)/x\to1$，却有 $f(x)-x\to+\infty$，所以正无穷方向没有仿射渐近线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the course example, $(x+1)/(x+2)=1-1/(x+2)$ for $x\ne-2$. The rewriting immediately gives the horizontal line $y=1$, the left limit $+\infty$ and right limit $-\infty$ at $-2$, and hence the vertical line $x=-2$. It also shows that this particular function never equals $1$. This last fact comes from its formula, not from the word “asymptote”.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程例子在 $x\ne-2$ 时可写成 $(x+1)/(x+2)=1-1/(x+2)$。这个改写立即给出水平线 $y=1$；在 $-2$ 处左极限为 $+\infty$、右极限为 $-\infty$，因此还有竖直线 $x=-2$。它还说明这个特定函数从不等于 $1$，但这条结论来自其公式，不是来自“渐近线”这个名称。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## An asymptote need not be a barrier
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*渐近线不必是一堵墙*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x>0$, $(1+\sin x)/x$ approaches zero and meets the horizontal asymptote at $x=3\pi/2+2k\pi$, $k=0,1,\ldots$. It stays nonnegative, so those meetings are not sign crossings. By contrast, $\sin x/x$ crosses zero at each positive integer multiple of $\pi$. Its bound $|\sin x/x|\le1/|x|$ and the [[夹逼定理|squeeze theorem]] show that it still approaches the horizontal asymptote $y=0$ in both infinite directions. Intersections at finite inputs do not contradict the limiting statement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $x>0$ 时，$(1+\sin x)/x$ 趋于零，并在 $x=3\pi/2+2k\pi$、$k=0,1,\ldots$ 处碰到水平渐近线。它始终非负，所以这些相交不是变号穿越。相比之下，$\sin x/x$ 在每个正整数倍 $\pi$ 处穿过零点；由 $|\sin x/x|\le1/|x|$ 和[[夹逼定理]]，它仍在两个无穷方向都靠近水平渐近线 $y=0$。有限输入处的相交不违背极限陈述。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In [[曲线描绘|curve sketching]], record the domain, the approach direction and the relevant limit with each asymptote. Derivative signs can then describe the branches between these boundaries; the asymptote alone does not prescribe which side the graph must stay on.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用于[[曲线描绘]]时，要随每条渐近线记录定义域、趋近方向和对应极限。导数符号再描述这些边界之间的分支；渐近线本身不规定图像必须一直停在哪一侧。
