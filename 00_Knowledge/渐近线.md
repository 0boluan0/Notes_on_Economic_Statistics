# 渐近线是图像沿某一支无限延伸时距离趋于零的直线
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An asymptote is a line approached in distance as a branch of the graph extends to infinity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 若 $f(x)\to\pm\infty$ 当 $x\to a^\pm$，则 $x=a$ 是相应侧的竖直渐近线。若 $f(x)\to L$ 当 $x\to\pm\infty$，则 $y=L$ 是水平渐近线。更一般地，若
> $$
> f(x)-(mx+b)\to0\qquad(x\to\pm\infty),
> $$
> 则 $y=mx+b$ 是该方向的渐近线；$m\ne0$ 时称斜渐近线，$m=0$ 时就是水平渐近线。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A vertical asymptote occurs when $f(x)$ diverges as $x$ approaches a finite boundary from a specified side. A horizontal asymptote occurs when $f(x)$ tends to a finite constant at infinity. More generally, $f(x)-(mx+b)\to0$ defines an asymptote in the chosen infinite direction; it is oblique when $m\ne0$ and horizontal when $m=0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

渐近线是极限陈述，不是“图像永远不能穿过的墙”。例如 $f(x)=1/x+\sin x/x$ 可在有限位置与水平渐近线 $y=0$ 相交。左右方向也要分开：一个竖直边界两侧可以趋向不同无穷，一个函数在 $+\infty$ 与 $-\infty$ 可以有不同渐近线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An asymptote is a limiting statement, not a wall the graph can never cross. The two directions must also be separated: a vertical boundary can have different one-sided behavior, and a function can have different asymptotes at positive and negative infinity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

找渐近线之前先确认定义域。代数约分可能隐藏原函数的孔洞，而孔洞不等于竖直渐近线；只有相应极限发散才是。[[曲线描绘]] 把渐近线与截距、临界点和凹凸区间放在同一张结构表中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check the domain before searching for asymptotes. Algebraic cancellation can hide a removable hole, which is not a vertical asymptote; divergence of the corresponding limit is required. Curve analysis combines asymptotes with intercepts, critical points, and concavity intervals.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么函数图像穿过 $y=L$ 不足以否定它是水平渐近线？
>
> **答案：** 水平渐近线只约束 $x\to\pm\infty$ 的差值极限，不限制有限位置的相交。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why does crossing $y=L$ not rule out that line as a horizontal asymptote?
>
> **Answer:** A horizontal asymptote constrains the limiting difference as $x\to\pm\infty$, not intersections at finite inputs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
