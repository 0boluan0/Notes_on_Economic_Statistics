# 同步指标是与指定总体经济活动基准大致同时变动的时间序列
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A coincident indicator moves broadly alongside a specified aggregate-activity benchmark.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同步指标用来描述经济当前所处的活动状态，例如工业生产、就业或实际收入的某些口径。同步说的是经济所对应的**观测时期**，不是发布日期，更不保证每个序列每月都朝同一方向变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Coincident indicators describe current economic activity, using measures such as industrial production, employment, or real income. Coincidence concerns the **observation period**, not the release date, and does not require every component to move together each month.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如第一季度实际 GDP 对应第一季度产出，但可能到第二季度才首次公布并在以后修订。它可以衡量同期活动，却不能在第一季度最后一天无误、完整地获知。判断实时状态必须处理这一信息缺口。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First-quarter real GDP measures first-quarter production but may first be released in the second quarter and later revised. Measuring contemporaneous activity does not make the complete value available on the quarter's last day.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

读取名单时要固定国别和基准，不把 GNP（国民生产总值）与[[国内生产总值|GDP]]混称，也不因某指标发布快就称它[[先行指标|先行]]。实时可得性由[[经济数据的实时口径]]另行核对。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the jurisdiction and benchmark, distinguish GNP from [[国内生产总值|GDP]], and do not call a measure [[先行指标|leading]] merely because it is released quickly. Availability is a separate [[经济数据的实时口径|real-time data]] question.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
