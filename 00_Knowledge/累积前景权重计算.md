# 累积前景权重由排序后的累积概率变换之差计算
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Cumulative prospect weights are differences of transformed cumulative probabilities after ranking outcomes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

计算已知概率的累积前景评价时，先固定[[前景参考点]]，合并相同结果，再将得失排序为 $x_1<\cdots<x_n$；概率满足 $p_i\ge0$、$\sum_i p_i=1$。给定严格递增的 $w^+,w^-:[0,1]\to[0,1]$，且两者均满足 $w(0)=0,w(1)=1$：收益从最好结果向下累计，损失从最坏结果向上累计。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a prospect with known probabilities, fix the [[前景参考点|reference point]], combine identical outcomes, and order gains and losses as $x_1<\cdots<x_n$, with nonnegative probabilities summing to one. Given strictly increasing $w^+,w^-:[0,1]\to[0,1]$ with endpoints zero and one, accumulate gains from the best outcome downward and losses from the worst upward.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\pi_i=\begin{cases}
w^+\!\left(\sum_{j=i}^n p_j\right)-w^+\!\left(\sum_{j=i+1}^n p_j\right),&x_i\ge0,\\[3pt]
w^-\!\left(\sum_{j=1}^i p_j\right)-w^-\!\left(\sum_{j=1}^{i-1}p_j\right),&x_i<0.
\end{cases}\qquad V=\sum_{i=1}^n\pi_i v(x_i),\quad v(0)=0.$$

空和取零；本式把中性结果放入收益分支，其价值贡献仍为零。所有结果非负时权重和为 $1$；所有结果非正且含零时，可把零结果的权重重设为其余权重之和距 $1$ 的剩余量，因为 $v(0)=0$，这不改变 $V$，但不是上式对零结果自动给出的权重。得失混合时两侧使用各自的变换，权重总和一般不必为 $1$。这些权重处理均没有改变原来的概率分布。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Empty sums are zero. The formula assigns a neutral outcome to the gain branch, but its value contribution remains zero. Entirely nonnegative prospects have a unit weight sum. For an entirely nonpositive prospect containing zero, its neutral weight can be reset to one minus the sum of the other weights: this leaves $V$ unchanged because $v(0)=0$, but is not the neutral weight automatically supplied above. For mixed gains and losses, the two transformations need not yield a unit total. None of these weight conventions changes the underlying probabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个三结果自拟算例：$x=(0,100,200)$，$p=(1/4,1/2,1/4)$，$v(x)=x$，并仅为演算取 $w^+(p)=\sqrt p$。它是满足端点与单调条件的示意函数，不是论文估计的典型权重曲线。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one three-outcome illustration, take $x=(0,100,200)$, $p=(1/4,1/2,1/4)$, $v(x)=x$, and $w^+(p)=\sqrt p$. This illustrative function satisfies the required endpoints and monotonicity; it is not the paper's estimated characteristic weighting curve.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$\pi_1=1-\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\qquad
\pi_2=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}-\frac12,\qquad
\pi_3=\frac12,$$
$$V=0\pi_1+100\pi_2+200\pi_3=50+50\sqrt3\approx136.603.$$

三个权重非负且和为 $1$。若误把 $\sqrt{p_i}$ 分别当成权重，就会丢掉排序与差分步骤，算成另一种评价，不是本式的累积前景模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three weights are nonnegative and sum to one. Using $\sqrt{p_i}$ separately would omit ranking and differencing and produce a different evaluation, not this cumulative prospect model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
