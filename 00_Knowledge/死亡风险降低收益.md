# 死亡风险降低的货币收益按同一人群各人的风险变化支付意愿加总

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Monetary benefits of mortality-risk reduction aggregate each affected person's willingness to pay for their risk change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

核算一项政策的死亡风险降低收益，先定义受影响人群、基准风险、政策造成的风险降幅及所属时期，再加总每人对该变化的支付意愿。若每人的变化足够小，可用匹配该背景的[[统计生命价值]] $v_i$ 作局部近似；未加分配权重时：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Define the affected population, baseline risks, policy-induced reductions and time horizon, then aggregate individual willingness to pay. For sufficiently small changes, use [[统计生命价值|VSL]] $v_i$ matched to each background as a local approximation. Without distributional weights:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$B=\sum_i W_i(\Delta p_i)\approx\sum_i v_i\Delta p_i;\qquad B\approx Nv\Delta p\quad\text{（同质情形）}.$$

这里 $N\Delta p$ 是预期死亡人数的减少，不是识别出了确定获救的个人。自造单年例：2万人各降风险 $10^{-5}$，匹配VSL为100万元；另1万人各降 $2\times10^{-5}$，匹配VSL为200万元。预期死亡减少 $0.2+0.2=0.4$ 人，货币收益为20万加40万，共60万元，不能只把0.4乘一个未经说明的共同VSL。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$N\Delta p$ is a reduction in expected fatalities, not an identification of certain survivors. In a constructed one-year example, 20,000 people each receive a $10^{-5}$ reduction with a VSL of one million; another 10,000 receive $2\times10^{-5}$ with a VSL of two million. Expected fatalities fall by 0.4 and benefits total 600,000. Multiplying 0.4 by an unexplained common VSL would discard the stated heterogeneity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

加总前要匹配货币价格年、收入与人群背景、风险种类及期间，避免同一人的同一风险降幅重复计入。一次性支付意愿不能再作为每年收益重复；年风险不能直接配终身风险的估值。较大风险变化要重新估计该完整变化的支付意愿，不把局部VSL无限外推。收入会影响支付能力和支付意愿，是否使用共同政策值或分配权重必须另作明确判断，不能让它隐含在加总中。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Match currency price year, income and population context, risk type and horizon, and avoid repeating the same person's risk reduction. Do not repeat one-off willingness to pay annually or combine annual risks with lifetime valuations. Revalue large changes instead of indefinitely extrapolating a local VSL. Since income affects willingness and ability to pay, common policy values or distributional weights require an explicit separate judgement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
