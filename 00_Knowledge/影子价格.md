# 影子价格是约束右端边际放宽时最优价值的一阶变化率；在正则可微条件下它由对应乘子表示
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A shadow price is the first-order change in optimal value from a marginal relaxation of a constraint and is represented by the corresponding multiplier under regular differentiability*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 考虑
> $$
> v(b)=\max_x f(x)\quad\text{s.t.}\quad h(x)=b,
> $$
> 并取
> $$
> \mathcal L(x,\lambda;b)=f(x)-\lambda\,[h(x)-b].
> $$
> 在 [[包络定理]] 的正则与可微条件下，
> $$
> \frac{dv}{db}=\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial b}=\lambda^*.
> $$
> 因而乘子衡量约束右端增加一小单位时，最优目标值的一阶变化。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $v(b)$ be the maximum of $f(x)$ subject to $h(x)=b$, and use $\mathcal L(x,\lambda;b)=f(x)-\lambda[h(x)-b]$. Under the regularity and differentiability conditions of the [[包络定理|envelope theorem]], $dv/db=\partial\mathcal L/\partial b=\lambda^*$. The multiplier therefore measures the first-order change in optimal value from a small increase in the constraint right-hand side.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 符号来自约定，不来自名称
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The sign comes from the convention, not the name*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若改用 $\widetilde{\mathcal L}=f+\widetilde\lambda[h(x)-b]$，同一个问题会有 $dv/db=-\widetilde\lambda$。所以报告影子价格时必须同时写约束方向和 Lagrangian 定义，不能只抄一个乘子数值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If instead $\widetilde{\mathcal L}=f+\widetilde\lambda[h(x)-b]$ is used, the same problem has $dv/db=-\widetilde\lambda$. A shadow-price statement must therefore include the constraint direction and Lagrangian convention rather than reporting a multiplier number in isolation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

资源上限 $q(x)\le b$ 可写成 $g(x,b)=q(x)-b\le0$，并取
$$
\mathcal L=f(x)-\lambda[q(x)-b].
$$
于是 $dv/db=\lambda^*\ge0$：放宽资源上限不会在局部降低最大价值。约束绑定是否能推出严格正影子价格是另一条逻辑边界，见 [[绑定不推正乘子]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A resource limit $q(x)\le b$ can be written as $g(x,b)=q(x)-b\le0$ with $\mathcal L=f(x)-\lambda[q(x)-b]$. Then $dv/db=\lambda^*\ge0$: a marginal relaxation cannot locally lower the maximum value. The failed converse from binding to a positive shadow price is explained in [[绑定不推正乘子|the binding-versus-positive-multiplier boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## “影子价格”不等于市场价格或有限变动收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A shadow price is neither automatically a market price nor a finite-change payoff*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\lambda^*$ 是参考参数附近的一阶边际量。它近似
$$
v(b+\Delta b)-v(b)\approx\lambda^*\Delta b
$$
只适用于足够小且没有制度切换的变化。大幅放宽可能改变活动约束、最优技术或最优分支，使乘子随 $b$ 变化。只有在额外制度含义成立时，才可把它解释为企业愿为一单位资源支付的最高价格。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\lambda^*$ is a first-order marginal quantity near the reference parameter. The approximation $v(b+\Delta b)-v(b)\approx\lambda^*\Delta b$ is valid only for a sufficiently small change that does not switch regimes. A large relaxation may change the active set, technology, or optimizer branch, causing the multiplier itself to vary. It becomes a firm's maximum willingness to pay for one unit of the resource only under additional institutional assumptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不要再用“最小罚金”替代这个定义：普通乘子与精确罚函数的区别由 [[乘子不是精确罚金]] 单独说明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Do not replace this sensitivity definition with a “minimum fine” story; [[乘子不是精确罚金|the distinction from exact-penalty methods]] explains why the two constructions differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若价值函数在 $b$ 处有折点，单一影子价格可能不存在；应报告左、右边际价值或适当的对偶乘子集合。若约束重复导致乘子不唯一，单个乘子的经济归属也可能无法识别，尽管总价值效应仍可确定。选择路径与价值光滑性的区分见 [[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the value function has a kink at $b$, a single shadow price may not exist; report one-sided marginal values or an appropriate set of dual multipliers. If duplicate constraints make multipliers nonunique, the economic attribution to an individual constraint may be unidentified even when the total value effect is well defined. See [[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微|the optimizer-jump boundary]] for the separate smoothness distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 在 $\mathcal L=f(x)-\lambda[h(x)-b]$ 的约定下，若 $\lambda^*=3$，$b$ 增加一个很小的 $\Delta b$ 时，价值的一阶变化是什么？
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under $\mathcal L=f(x)-\lambda[h(x)-b]$, if $\lambda^*=3$, what is the first-order value change from a small increase $\Delta b$?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** $v(b+\Delta b)-v(b)\approx3\Delta b$，前提是变化足够小且正则制度没有切换。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** $v(b+\Delta b)-v(b)\approx3\Delta b$, provided the change is small and the regular regime does not switch.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
