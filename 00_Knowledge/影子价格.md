# Shadow prices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*影子价格*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **shadow price** measures how the optimal objective value responds to a specified small change in a constraint. Think of a factory whose output is limited by capacity: if one extra fraction of capacity would raise optimized profit, the derivative tells you the marginal value of that capacity. Its units are objective units per unit of the stated constraint parameter. It is a sensitivity of the [[值函数|value function]], not a market quotation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**影子价格**衡量某条约束发生指定的小变化时，最优目标值怎样响应。例如，工厂的产出受到容量限制；如果容量增加一点会提高重新优化后的利润，相关导数就表示容量的边际价值。它的单位是“目标值单位／所指定的约束参数单位”。它是[[值函数]]的敏感度，不是直接观察到的市场报价。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For an upper bound $q(x)\le b$, increasing $b$ really relaxes the constraint: every previously feasible choice remains feasible. For an equality $h(x)=b$, changing $b$ moves the feasible set and need not enlarge it; the marginal value can be negative. First specify which perturbation is being valued. The equality formula below uses an unrestricted multiplier $\lambda$; its sign is not the nonnegativity restriction used for an inequality multiplier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对上限 $q(x)\le b$，增加 $b$ 确实是在放松限制：原来所有可行选择仍然可行。对等式 $h(x)=b$，改变 $b$ 是移动可行集，未必扩大它，因此边际价值可以为负。必须先说清评估的是哪一种扰动。下面等式公式中的乘子 $\lambda$ 不限制符号，不能套上不等式乘子非负的要求。

> [!important] A multiplier becomes a value derivative under hypotheses · 乘子的导数解释需要前提
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $v(b)$ be the maximum of $f(x)$ subject to $h(x)=b$, and use $\mathcal L(x,\lambda;b)=f(x)-\lambda[h(x)-b]$. Under the regularity and differentiability conditions of the [[包络定理|envelope theorem]], $dv/db=\partial\mathcal L/\partial b=\lambda^*$. The multiplier therefore measures the first-order change in optimal value from a small increase in the constraint right-hand side.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑
> $$
> v(b)=\max_x f(x)\quad\text{s.t.}\quad h(x)=b,
> $$
> 并取
> $$
> \mathcal L(x,\lambda;b)=f(x)-\lambda\,[h(x)-b].
> $$
> 在 [[包络定理]] 的正则与可微条件下，
> $$
> \frac{dv}{db}=\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial b}=\lambda^*.
> $$
> 因而乘子衡量约束右端增加一小单位时，最优目标值的一阶变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a concrete equality example, maximize $-x^2$ subject to $x=b$. The only feasible choice is $x^*=b$, so $v(b)=-b^2$. Stationarity of $\mathcal L=-x^2-\lambda(x-b)$ gives $\lambda^*=-2b$, exactly $v'(b)$. At $b=1$, increasing the prescribed equality target lowers the maximum value to first order. This is not a contradiction: an equality shift is not an upper-bound relaxation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个具体等式例子是最大化 $-x^2$，满足 $x=b$。唯一可行选择为 $x^*=b$，所以 $v(b)=-b^2$。对 $\mathcal L=-x^2-\lambda(x-b)$ 求驻点，得到 $\lambda^*=-2b$，恰等于 $v'(b)$。在 $b=1$，提高规定的等式目标会使最大价值一阶下降；这并不矛盾，因为移动等式不是放宽资源上限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The sign comes from the convention, not the name
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 符号来自约定，不来自名称

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If instead $\widetilde{\mathcal L}=f+\widetilde\lambda[h(x)-b]$ is used, the same problem has $dv/db=-\widetilde\lambda$. A shadow-price statement must therefore include the constraint direction and Lagrangian convention rather than reporting a multiplier number in isolation.

A resource limit $q(x)\le b$ can be written as $g(x,b)=q(x)-b\le0$ with $\mathcal L=f(x)-\lambda[q(x)-b]$. Then $dv/db=\lambda^*\ge0$: a marginal relaxation cannot locally lower the maximum value. The failed converse from binding to a positive shadow price is explained in [[绑定不推正乘子|the binding-versus-positive-multiplier boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若改用 $\widetilde{\mathcal L}=f+\widetilde\lambda[h(x)-b]$，同一个问题会有 $dv/db=-\widetilde\lambda$。所以报告影子价格时必须同时写约束方向和 Lagrangian 定义，不能只抄一个乘子数值。

资源上限 $q(x)\le b$ 可写成 $g(x,b)=q(x)-b\le0$，并取
$$
\mathcal L=f(x)-\lambda[q(x)-b].
$$
于是 $dv/db=\lambda^*\ge0$：放宽资源上限不会在局部降低最大价值。约束绑定是否能推出严格正影子价格是另一条逻辑边界，见 [[绑定不推正乘子]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The nonnegative derivative requires that only the resource bound changes: keep the objective, the domain and all other constraints fixed. Feasible-set inclusion already makes $v$ nondecreasing, even before differentiability is considered. By contrast, a lower requirement $q(x)\ge b$ becomes tighter as $b$ rises. Writing $g=b-q(x)\le0$ and $\mathcal L=f-\lambda[b-q(x)]$ gives $v'(b)=-\lambda^*\le0$ whenever the derivative interpretation applies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上述非负导数要求只有资源上限改变，目标、定义域和其他约束保持不变。即使尚未讨论可微性，可行集包含关系也已经保证 $v$ 非减。相反，下限要求 $q(x)\ge b$ 会随 $b$ 增加而收紧。将其写为 $g=b-q(x)\le0$，采用 $\mathcal L=f-\lambda[b-q(x)]$，在导数解释成立时得到 $v'(b)=-\lambda^*\le0$。

> [!example] A capacity price you can calculate · 可直接算出的容量价格
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Maximize profit $4x-x^2$ subject to $0\le x\le b$, with $b>0$. Completing the square gives $4x-x^2=4-(x-2)^2$, so $x^*(b)=\min\{b,2\}$. The capacity multiplier and value are
>
> $$v(b)=\begin{cases}4b-b^2,&0<b<2,\\4,&b\ge2,\end{cases}\qquad
> \lambda^*(b)=\begin{cases}4-2b,&0<b<2,\\0,&b\ge2.\end{cases}$$
>
> The lower constraint is slack, so its multiplier is zero. The upper-bound stationarity equation is $4-2x^* -\lambda^*=0$. At $b=1$, the optimized profit is $3$ and the capacity shadow price is $2$. An increase of $0.1$ is predicted to add $0.2$; the actual increase is $v(1.1)-v(1)=0.19$. The discrepancy is the quadratic remainder, not a different sign convention.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $0\le x\le b$、$b>0$ 下最大化利润 $4x-x^2$。配方 $4x-x^2=4-(x-2)^2$ 给出 $x^*(b)=\min\{b,2\}$，值函数与容量乘子如上。下限松弛，乘子为零；上限的驻点式为 $4-2x^*-\lambda^*=0$。当 $b=1$ 时，最优利润是 $3$，容量影子价格为 $2$。上限增加 $0.1$，一阶预测利润增加 $0.2$；实际增加 $v(1.1)-v(1)=0.19$。差额来自二次余项，而非符号约定不同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $b=2$, the constraint still binds but its multiplier is zero. For a small negative $\Delta b$, $v(2+\Delta b)-v(2)=-(\Delta b)^2$; for a positive increment it is zero. Dividing by $\Delta b$ gives a derivative of zero from both sides. The active set changes and $x^*(b)$ has a kink, yet the value derivative exists. This is the concrete distinction behind [[绑定不推正乘子|binding with a zero multiplier]] and the separate [[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微|choice-versus-value smoothness boundary]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $b=2$ 时，上限仍绑定，乘子却为零。对小的负增量 $\Delta b$，有 $v(2+\Delta b)-v(2)=-(\Delta b)^2$；正增量时价值变化为零。分别除以 $\Delta b$，两侧导数都趋于零。活动集切换，$x^*(b)$ 出现折点，但值函数导数仍存在。这正说明[[绑定不推正乘子|绑定可以对应零乘子]]，以及[[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微|选择与价值的光滑性需要分开判断]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A supporting bound gives another sensitivity route
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 由对偶上界得到敏感度的另一条路线

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A smooth optimizer branch is not the only way to justify the multiplier interpretation. For the upper-bound perturbation $q(x)\le b$, suppose the reference problem has finite value, [[Slater强对偶|strong duality]] holds, and dual-optimal multipliers are attained. Keep the objective, common domain and other constraints fixed. Using those reference multipliers for a perturbed bound $b+\Delta b$ gives the following upper bound: the Lagrangian changes only by $\lambda^*\Delta b$, and it still bounds every feasible payoff from above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

光滑最优分支并不是证明乘子解释的唯一办法。对上限扰动 $q(x)\le b$，假设参考问题的值有限，[[Slater强对偶|强对偶]]成立，且存在取得对偶最优的乘子。保持目标、共同定义域及其他约束不变，把参考点的这组乘子用于新上限 $b+\Delta b$；Lagrangian 只增加 $\lambda^*\Delta b$，仍是每个新可行收益的上界，因此得到

$$
v(b+\Delta b)\le v(b)+\lambda^*\Delta b.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $v$ is finite on an open interval around $b$ and differentiable at $b$, divide this inequality by a positive increment to obtain $v'(b)\le\lambda^*$, and by a negative increment to obtain $v'(b)\ge\lambda^*$. Thus $v'(b)=\lambda^*$. This argument uses value differentiability and attained dual optimality, not a fixed active set. Without differentiability the supporting bound still has meaning, but its slope must not be reported as an ordinary derivative merely because a multiplier exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $v$ 在 $b$ 的某开邻域内有限，并在 $b$ 可微，先把这个不等式除以正增量，极限给出 $v'(b)\le\lambda^*$；除以负增量则反向，给出 $v'(b)\ge\lambda^*$，所以 $v'(b)=\lambda^*$。这条论证用的是值函数可微及对偶最优取得，不要求活动集固定。若不可微，上界仍有意义，但不能仅凭乘子存在，就把它的斜率报成普通导数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A shadow price is neither automatically a market price nor a finite-change payoff
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## “影子价格”不等于市场价格或有限变动收益

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\lambda^*$ is a first-order marginal quantity near the reference parameter. The approximation $v(b+\Delta b)-v(b)\approx\lambda^*\Delta b$ is a local first-order approximation when the value is differentiable and the multiplier has been identified with that derivative. An unchanged regular regime is one sufficient route, not a necessary condition. A large relaxation may change the active set, technology, or optimizer branch, causing the multiplier itself to vary. It becomes a firm's maximum willingness to pay for one unit of the resource only under additional institutional assumptions.

Do not replace this sensitivity definition with a “minimum fine” story; [[乘子不是精确罚金|the distinction from exact-penalty methods]] explains why the two constructions differ.

If the value function has a kink at $b$, a single shadow price may not exist; report one-sided marginal values or an appropriate set of dual multipliers. If duplicate constraints make multipliers nonunique, the economic attribution to an individual constraint may be unidentified even when the total value effect is well defined. See [[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微|the optimizer-jump boundary]] for the separate smoothness distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\lambda^*$ 是参考参数附近的一阶边际量。它近似
$$
v(b+\Delta b)-v(b)\approx\lambda^*\Delta b
$$
这一近似要求该处的值函数可微，且已证明乘子就是这个导数；活动制度不切换是一种常用充分路线，并非必要条件。大幅放宽可能改变活动约束、最优技术或最优分支，使乘子随 $b$ 变化。只有在额外制度含义成立时，才可把它解释为企业愿为一单位资源支付的最高价格。

不要再用“最小罚金”替代这个定义：普通乘子与精确罚函数的区别由 [[乘子不是精确罚金]] 单独说明。

若价值函数在 $b$ 处有折点，单一影子价格可能不存在；应报告左、右边际价值或适当的对偶乘子集合。若约束重复导致乘子不唯一，单个乘子的经济归属也可能无法识别，尽管总价值效应仍可确定。选择路径与价值光滑性的区分见 [[优化器跳跃不推价值不可微]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The precise approximation is $v(b+\Delta b)-v(b)=v'(b)\Delta b+o(|\Delta b|)$, where the remainder divided by $|\Delta b|$ tends to zero. In the capacity example, increasing $b$ from $1$ to $3$ gains only $1$ unit of profit, not the $2\times2=4$ predicted by extending the original slope over the whole interval. A willingness-to-pay interpretation also requires a monetary objective and care about costs already included in it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

准确的一阶表述是 $v(b+\Delta b)-v(b)=v'(b)\Delta b+o(|\Delta b|)$，其中余项除以 $|\Delta b|$ 后趋于零。容量例子中，从 $b=1$ 放宽到 $b=3$，利润只增加 $1$，不是把原斜率延伸到整段所得的 $2\times2=4$。解释为支付意愿，还须目标具有货币含义，并注意哪些资源成本已经包含在目标中。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Parameter units matter. If the same bound is written $a[q(x)-b]\le0$ for a positive constant $a$, its multiplier becomes $\widetilde\lambda=\lambda/a$. But $\mathcal L_b=a\widetilde\lambda=\lambda$: the value derivative per original unit of $b$ is unchanged. A bare multiplier number, without its constraint scaling, does not specify a shadow price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参数单位也很重要。若把同一上限写成 $a[q(x)-b]\le0$，其中 $a>0$ 为常数，相应乘子变为 $\widetilde\lambda=\lambda/a$；但 $\mathcal L_b=a\widetilde\lambda=\lambda$，按原来每单位 $b$ 衡量的价值导数不变。只报一个没有约束缩放约定的乘子数值，无法确定影子价格。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the attribution issue, maximize $x$ subject to $x\le b_1$ and $x\le b_2$. The value is $v(b_1,b_2)=\min\{b_1,b_2\}$. At $(0,0)$, every pair $\lambda_1,\lambda_2\ge0$ with $\lambda_1+\lambda_2=1$ is dual-optimal. Relaxing only $b_1$ has right marginal value zero, while tightening it gives left marginal value one: there is no two-sided derivative in that coordinate. If instead a single parameter moves both bounds together, $b_1=b_2=b$, then $v(b,b)=b$ and its derivative is the uniquely determined sum $\lambda_1+\lambda_2=1$. The perturbation determines what can be identified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于归属问题，考虑在 $x\le b_1$、$x\le b_2$ 下最大化 $x$，值函数为 $v(b_1,b_2)=\min\{b_1,b_2\}$。在 $(0,0)$，每一组满足 $\lambda_1,\lambda_2\ge0$ 且 $\lambda_1+\lambda_2=1$ 的乘子都是对偶最优。单独放宽 $b_1$ 的右侧边际值为零，收紧时的左侧边际值为一，该坐标没有双侧导数。若改为同一个参数同时移动两个上限，即 $b_1=b_2=b$，则 $v(b,b)=b$，导数是唯一确定的和 $\lambda_1+\lambda_2=1$。究竟能识别什么，取决于规定的扰动。

> [!question] Read the sign and units · 自检
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under $\mathcal L=f(x)-\lambda[h(x)-b]$, if $\lambda^*=3$, what is the first-order value change from a small increase $\Delta b$?
>
> **Answer:** $v(b+\Delta b)-v(b)\approx3\Delta b$, provided the change is small and the regular regime does not switch.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $\mathcal L=f(x)-\lambda[h(x)-b]$ 的约定下，若 $\lambda^*=3$，$b$ 增加一个很小的 $\Delta b$ 时，价值的一阶变化是什么？
>
> **答案：** $v(b+\Delta b)-v(b)\approx3\Delta b$，前提是变化足够小且正则制度没有切换。
