# Mean vectors
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*均值向量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **mean vector** averages each coordinate of a random vector, keeping the coordinates in their original order. Averaging height and averaging weight produce two numbers with two meanings; the mean vector stores them together. It is a fixed summary of the probability model, not one randomly observed person.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**均值向量**对随机向量的各坐标分别求平均，并保留原来的坐标顺序。平均身高与平均体重是含义不同的两个数，均值向量把它们放在一起。它是概率模型的固定汇总量，不是某个随机观察到的人。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reading the coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 怎样读这个向量

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let the [[随机向量|random vector]] $X=(H,W)^T$ record height in centimetres and weight in kilograms. In a toy population, $(160,50)^T$ and $(180,80)^T$ each occur with probability $1/2$. An [[期望|expectation]] is a probability-weighted average, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令[[随机向量]] $X=(H,W)^T$ 记录身高（厘米）与体重（千克）。简化总体中，$(160,50)^T$、$(180,80)^T$ 各以概率 $1/2$ 出现。[[期望]]是概率加权的平均，因此

$$E[X]=\frac12\begin{bmatrix}160\\50\end{bmatrix}+\frac12\begin{bmatrix}180\\80\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}170\\65\end{bmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first entry is $170$ cm and the second is $65$ kg. They should not be averaged together into one scalar: they describe different quantities and units. No profile in this model equals $(170,65)^T$, yet the vector is its mean. A mean need not be a possible observation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一项为 $170$ 厘米，第二项为 $65$ 千克。不能把它们再混成一个脱离标签的标量平均：两个坐标描述不同的量，也使用不同单位。模型中没有档案恰好等于 $(170,65)^T$，它却仍是均值；均值不必是某次可能的观察值。

> [!important] Componentwise expectation · 逐坐标期望
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a real random vector $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$, assume $E|X_j|<\infty$ for every $j=1,\ldots,p$. Its finite mean vector is defined by
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对实随机向量 $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_p)^T$，假设每个 $j=1,\ldots,p$ 都满足 $E|X_j|<\infty$。有限均值向量定义为
>
> $$\mu=E[X]=\big(E[X_1],\ldots,E[X_p]\big)^T\in\mathbb R^p.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\mu$ is the Greek letter mu, conventionally used for a mean. **Integrable** means that the expected absolute value is finite. The condition applies to each coordinate separately and does not require independence or a normal distribution. It ensures that every entry of $\mu$ is an ordinary finite real number; see [[矩存在性的使用边界|the boundary for existence of moments]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $\mu$ 是常用来表示均值的希腊字母 mu。**可积**表示绝对值的期望有限。条件要对每个坐标分别检查，不要求坐标独立，也不要求正态分布。它保证 $\mu$ 的每项都是普通有限实数，详见[[矩存在性的使用边界]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a vector taking finitely many values $x^{(1)},\ldots,x^{(r)}$ with probabilities $q_1,\ldots,q_r$, the same definition gives $E[X]=\sum_{i=1}^r q_i x^{(i)}$. The weights are probabilities of possible vectors; they are not automatically the equal weights $1/n$ of an observed data table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若随机向量只取有限个值 $x^{(1)},\ldots,x^{(r)}$，对应概率为 $q_1,\ldots,q_r$，同一定义给出 $E[X]=\sum_{i=1}^r q_i x^{(i)}$。这里的权重是各可能向量的概率，并不会自动等于某张观察数据表中的等权权重 $1/n$。

> [!warning] One missing coordinate mean breaks the finite-vector formula · 一个坐标缺失均值，有限向量公式就不能照用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A random vector can be well-defined even if one coordinate has no finite first moment. In that case, do not treat $E[X]$ as a finite vector and proceed with ordinary matrix arithmetic. Finite second moments are not required for a mean vector; they enter the separate finite [[协方差矩阵|covariance-matrix]] definition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 随机向量本身可以有良好定义，但某个坐标没有有限一阶矩。此时不能把 $E[X]$ 当作有限向量继续进行普通矩阵运算。均值向量不要求二阶矩有限；那是有限[[协方差矩阵]]定义中的另一项条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What the centre does not determine
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*中心不能确定哪些信息*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The two-profile model above and the constant vector $Y=(170,65)^T$ have the same mean. Their distributions differ: $Y$ never varies, while $X$ takes two different values. Their covariance matrices also differ. A coordinate's [[方差|variance]] is its expected squared distance from its mean; for example, $\operatorname{Var}(Y_1)=0$ but $\operatorname{Var}(H)=\tfrac12(160-170)^2+\tfrac12(180-170)^2=100$. Thus a shared mean does not determine spread or the joint law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

上面的两档案模型与恒定向量 $Y=(170,65)^T$ 具有同一均值，但分布不同：$Y$ 从不变化，$X$ 却有两个不同取值。协方差矩阵也不同。一个坐标的[[方差]]是它偏离自身均值的平方的期望；例如 $\operatorname{Var}(Y_1)=0$，而 $\operatorname{Var}(H)=\tfrac12(160-170)^2+\tfrac12(180-170)^2=100$。因此，相同中心不能确定波动或联合分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The population mean $\mu$ is fixed, although it may be unknown. A [[样本均值向量|sample mean vector]] is computed from randomly sampled observations and can vary across samples. How a fixed change of units or a fixed linear combination moves the population mean is the separate [[均值向量仿射变换|affine mean-transformation rule]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总体均值 $\mu$ 是固定量，尽管它可能未知。[[样本均值向量]]由随机抽到的观测计算，换一批样本可能得到不同结果。固定的单位变换或线性组合怎样移动总体中心，由[[均值向量仿射变换]]单独说明。
