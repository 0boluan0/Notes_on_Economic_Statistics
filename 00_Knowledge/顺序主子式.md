# 第 k 阶顺序主子式是矩阵左上角 k 阶主子矩阵的行列式

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The kth leading principal minor is the determinant of the upper-left k-by-k principal submatrix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵 $A$ 的第 $k$ 阶顺序主子式为 $L_k=\det A_{1:k,1:k}$，即保留前 $k$ 行与前 $k$ 列。每阶只有一个，$n$ 阶方阵合计 $n$ 个。它是[[主子式]]的特殊子集。

三阶矩阵只需依次取左上角一个元素、左上角二阶块、整个矩阵。不能把其他二阶主子式或返回的子矩阵本身当成 $L_2$。[[Sylvester 正定判据]]使用全部 $L_k>0$；放宽为非负时不能推出半正定，见[[顺序主子式不推半正定]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The kth leading principal minor retains the first k row and column indices. There is exactly one at each order, forming a special subset of [[主子式|principal minors]]. The entire sequence supports [[Sylvester 正定判据|Sylvester's strict criterion]], but merely nonnegative leading minors do not imply positive semidefiniteness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
