# 图上的距离是最短路径长度而可达性只问路径是否存在
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Graph distance is shortest-path length; reachability asks only whether a path exists*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 顶点 $v$ 从 $u$ **可达（reachable）**，是指存在一条 $u$ 到 $v$ 的 path。图距离
> $$
> \operatorname{dist}(u,v)=\min\{\text{$u$ 到 $v$ 的 path 长度}\}
> $$
> 在不可达时约定为 $\infty$。reachability 是 yes/no；distance 在可达之后进一步比较最少边数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Vertex $v$ is **reachable** from $u$ when a path from $u$ to $v$ exists. The graph distance
> $$
> \operatorname{dist}(u,v)=\min\{\text{lengths of paths from $u$ to $v$}\}
> $$
> is defined as $\infty$ when no such path exists. Reachability is a yes/no question; distance additionally minimizes the number of edges.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长度 $0$ path 使每个顶点从自身可达且 $\operatorname{dist}(u,u)=0$。在 digraph 中距离通常不对称：$u$ 能到 $v$ 不代表 $v$ 能回到 $u$。在 unweighted undirected graph 的同一 component 内，距离才是通常的对称 metric。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The length-zero path makes every vertex reachable from itself and gives $\operatorname{dist}(u,u)=0$. Directed distance is generally asymmetric: $u$ reaching $v$ does not make $v$ reach $u$. Within one component of an unweighted undirected graph, distance has the usual symmetry of a metric.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三角不等式来自路径拼接
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The triangle inequality comes from concatenating paths*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $u\leadsto x$ 与 $x\leadsto v$，把两段 shortest paths 拼成 walk，再删去重复段，得到
$$
\operatorname{dist}(u,v)\le
\operatorname{dist}(u,x)+\operatorname{dist}(x,v).
$$
等号成立当且仅当 $x$ 位于**某一条** $u$ 到 $v$ 的 shortest path 上；不要求每条 shortest path 都经过 $x$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $u\leadsto x$ and $x\leadsto v$, concatenate shortest paths and remove any repeated detour. This gives
$$
\operatorname{dist}(u,v)\le
\operatorname{dist}(u,x)+\operatorname{dist}(x,v).
$$
Equality holds exactly when $x$ lies on **some** shortest path from $u$ to $v$, not necessarily every shortest path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 权重边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Weight boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的 length 是边数。若一条边携带非相等成本，weighted distance 应改为 path weights 的最小总和；它可能与最少边数给出不同路线，甚至需要非负权等额外条件才能选用特定算法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here, length means edge count. With unequal edge costs, weighted distance instead minimizes total path weight. It may choose a route with more edges, and particular algorithms may require additional assumptions such as nonnegative weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 已知 $u$ 可达 $v$，能否推出 $\operatorname{dist}(u,v)=\operatorname{dist}(v,u)$？
>
> **答案：**不能。在 digraph 中可能只有 $u\to v$，此时前者为 $1$，后者为 $\infty$。对无向图，任一路径可反向读取，才有对称性。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $v$ is reachable from $u$, must $\operatorname{dist}(u,v)=\operatorname{dist}(v,u)$?
>
> **Answer:** No. A digraph may contain only $u\to v$, giving distances one and infinity respectively. Symmetry follows in an undirected graph because every path can be reversed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
