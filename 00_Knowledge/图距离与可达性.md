# Graph distance and reachability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*图距离与可达性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Reachability** asks whether a route exists. **Graph distance** asks how many edges the shortest route uses. In an unweighted transport graph, an edge is one permitted step; its length on the drawing does not determine this distance. Two stations drawn close together can still require several steps to reach.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**可达性**问是否存在路线，**图距离**进一步问最短路线要经过几条边。在无权交通图中，一条边就是允许走的一步；图上画出的线段长短并不决定这种距离。画得很近的两个站，也可能要经过多步才能到达。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Start with two routes
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先看两条路线*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take four vertices $u,a,b,v$ and the undirected edges $\{u,a\},\{a,v\},\{u,b\},\{b,v\}$. Both $u,a,v$ and $u,b,v$ are [[游走路径与环|paths]]: they follow edges without repeating vertices. Each uses two edges, and there is no direct edge from $u$ to $v$. Thus $v$ is reachable from $u$ and their distance is $2$. The shortest route need not be unique. Add an isolated vertex $z$, with no incident edges: $z$ is unreachable from $u$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取四个顶点 $u,a,b,v$ 和四条无向边 $\{u,a\},\{a,v\},\{u,b\},\{b,v\}$。$u,a,v$ 与 $u,b,v$ 都是[[游走路径与环|路径]]：沿边前进且不重复顶点。两条路线都经过两条边，而 $u,v$ 之间没有直接边，所以 $v$ 从 $u$ 可达，距离为 $2$。最短路线不必唯一。再加入一个没有相接边的孤立顶点 $z$，则从 $u$ 无法到达 $z$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Define the permitted steps first
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先明确允许怎样走*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work with a finite [[图类型的建模边界|graph]], either directed or undirected. Write $V$ for its vertex set and $u,v\in V$ for two vertices. In a directed graph every step must follow its arrow; an undirected edge can be traversed either way. A path has no repeated vertex, and its **length** is its number of edges. A single vertex is a length-zero path. Parallel edges or self-loops, when the model allows them, do not change these conventions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑有限[[图类型的建模边界|图]]，可以有向，也可以无向。$V$ 表示顶点集合，$u,v\in V$ 是其中的两个顶点。有向图中每一步必须顺着箭头，无向边则可以双向经过。路径不重复顶点，其**长度**是经过的边数；单个顶点算长度为零的路径。若模型允许平行边或自环，上述长度与路径约定仍不变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $u\leadsto v$ when some path starts at $u$ and ends at $v$; equivalently, $v$ is reachable from $u$. Then define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若存在从 $u$ 出发、到 $v$ 结束的路径，记为 $u\leadsto v$，也就是 $v$ 从 $u$ 可达。定义

$$d(u,v)=\operatorname{dist}(u,v)=
\begin{cases}
\min\{\text{length of a path from }u\text{ to }v\},&u\leadsto v,\\
\infty,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The minimum is attained: when a path exists, its possible lengths form a nonempty set of nonnegative integers, and a path in an $n$-vertex graph uses at most $n-1$ edges. Here $\infty$ is a symbol for no route, not an extremely long route. In particular, $d(u,u)=0$, and $d(u,v)=0$ only when $u=v$. The [[游走去环得到路径|detour-deletion theorem]] says that any walk can be reduced to a path without increasing its length or changing its endpoints. Consequently one obtains the same reachability and minimum length by initially allowing repeated vertices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个最小值确实能取到：只要存在路径，可能长度就组成一个非空的非负整数集合；有 $n$ 个顶点的图中，路径最多经过 $n-1$ 条边。这里的 $\infty$ 表示没有路线，并不是某条特别长的路线。尤其 $d(u,u)=0$，而 $d(u,v)=0$ 只能发生在 $u=v$ 时。[[游走去环得到路径|删除绕行段的定理]]说明，任何游走都可以在不增加长度、不改变端点的情况下化为路径。因此，起初即使允许重复顶点，得到的可达性与最短长度也相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Reachability is reflexive because of the zero-length path and transitive because routes can be joined and their repeated detours removed. It need not be symmetric. In the directed chain $1\to2\to3$, $d(1,3)=2$ but $d(3,1)=\infty$. Even when both directions are reachable, their distances can differ; a directed three-cycle gives distance $1$ along an edge and $2$ in the reverse direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

零长度路径使可达关系自反；拼接路线再去掉重复段，使其具有传递性。但它不一定对称。有向链 $1\to2\to3$ 中，$d(1,3)=2$，而 $d(3,1)=\infty$。即使两个方向都可达，距离也可能不同：有向三环上，顺着一条边的距离为 $1$，反向则为 $2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Joining routes gives the triangle inequality
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 三角不等式来自路径拼接

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $u\leadsto x$ and $x\leadsto v$. Join a shortest $u$-to-$x$ path to a shortest $x$-to-$v$ path. They may share other vertices, so the result is initially a walk. Deleting repeated detours cannot increase its edge count. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $u\leadsto x$ 且 $x\leadsto v$。把一条 $u$ 到 $x$ 的最短路径和一条 $x$ 到 $v$ 的最短路径接起来。两段可能还共享其他顶点，所以起初得到的是游走；删除重复绕行段不会增加边数。因此

$$d(u,v)\le d(u,x)+d(x,v).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This inequality also holds with unreachable pairs if we use $a+\infty=\infty$ for $a\ge0$ and $\infty+\infty=\infty$: a right-hand side of $\infty$ gives no finite bound. If both terms on the right are finite, equality holds exactly when $x$ lies on **some** shortest path from $u$ to $v$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对不可达点对，约定 $a+\infty=\infty$（$a\ge0$）以及 $\infty+\infty=\infty$，不等式仍成立；右边为 $\infty$ 时，它没有提供有限上界。若右边两项都有限，那么等号成立恰好当 $x$ 位于 $u$ 到 $v$ 的**某一条**最短路径上。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove the equality claim, first assume equality. The joined shortest paths have total length $d(u,v)$. If that walk repeated a vertex, deleting the resulting positive-length detour would give a shorter route, a contradiction. It is therefore itself a shortest path through $x$. Conversely, every segment of a shortest path is shortest between its own endpoints: replacing a segment by a shorter path and deleting any repetitions would shorten the whole route. Splitting a shortest path at $x$ therefore gives equality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明等号判据：先假设等号成立。拼接后的长度恰好为 $d(u,v)$；如果其中重复了顶点，就能删掉一个正长度绕行段，得到更短路线，矛盾。所以拼接本身就是经过 $x$ 的最短路径。反过来，最短路径的每一段也必须是其两端之间的最短路径：否则用更短路径替换，再删除重复段，就能缩短整条路线。因此，把经过 $x$ 的最短路径在 $x$ 处分开，便得到等号。

> [!warning] Some shortest path, with finite distances · 有限距离下的某一条最短路
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In the four-vertex example, $a$ lies on one shortest $u$-to-$v$ path but not on the route through $b$. The equality does not require every shortest path to pass through $a$. Do not infer a path from an equality involving infinities: for three distinct isolated vertices, $\infty=\infty+\infty$ holds under the convention, but no path joins them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 四点例中，$a$ 位于一条 $u$ 到 $v$ 的最短路径上，却不在经过 $b$ 的那条路上；等号不要求每条最短路都经过 $a$。也不能从含无穷的等式反推路径：三个不同孤立顶点之间虽按约定有 $\infty=\infty+\infty$，却根本没有连接它们的路径。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In an undirected graph a path can be reversed, giving $d(u,v)=d(v,u)$. On one [[无向图连通分量|connected component]] all distances are finite, and nonnegativity, the zero-distance rule, symmetry and the triangle inequality make this a [[度量空间|metric]]. On a disconnected graph the cross-component value $\infty$ falls outside the finite-valued metric definition. Directed distance generally fails symmetry.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无向图中的路径可以反向读取，因此 $d(u,v)=d(v,u)$。在同一个[[无向图连通分量]]内，全部距离有限；非负性、零距离条件、对称性和三角不等式共同使它成为[[度量空间|度量]]。不连通图跨分量的距离为 $\infty$，不属于有限值度量的定义；有向距离则通常不满足对称性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Edge counts and edge costs are different
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 权重边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here every edge costs one step. If edges carry costs, minimizing their sum is a different question: a direct edge of cost $10$ loses to a two-edge route with costs $1$ and $1$, even though it uses fewer edges. A weighted-distance definition must specify the permitted routes and weights. With zero costs, distinct vertices can have distance zero; negative closed routes can make repeated travel keep reducing the cost. The unweighted proof above does not settle those variants or the assumptions of a particular shortest-path algorithm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里每条边都只计一步。若边带有成本，最小化成本之和就是另一问题：一条成本 $10$ 的直接边，虽然边数更少，却不如成本分别为 $1$、$1$ 的两边路线便宜。加权距离必须另行明确允许的路线与权重。零成本可使不同顶点间距离为零；负成本闭合路线可能让反复行进不断降低成本。上面的无权证明没有替这些变体或特定最短路算法补足假设。
