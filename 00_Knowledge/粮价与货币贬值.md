# 食品相对价格上涨与普遍名义涨价对利润率不是同一种冲击

看到粮价和货币工资都上涨，还不能判断 Ricardo 所说的利润压力是否出现。要先问：**粮食相对其他商品更贵了，还是同样的商品普遍需要更多货币购买？** 前者改变真实的购买力关系，后者可能只是计价单位的价值改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Higher grain prices and money wages do not by themselves establish Ricardo's pressure on profits. First ask whether **food has become dearer relative to other goods, or whether goods generally require more money**. The former changes real purchasing-power relations; the latter may reflect a change in the value of the monetary unit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若食品价格 $P_F$ 相对工业品价格 $P_M$ 上涨，维持固定食品消费 $b$ 所需的工业品工资部分 $bP_F/P_M$ 会提高。如果工业技术、产量和其他成本未补偿这项上涨，[[工资利润的分配约束|工资成本就会挤压资本剩余]]。Ricardo 的土地生产困难论关注的是这类相对价格和资源成本变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the food price $P_F$ rises relative to the manufactured-goods price $P_M$, maintaining fixed food consumption $b$ requires a larger manufactured-goods wage component $bP_F/P_M$. Unless industrial technology, output or other costs compensate, [[工资利润的分配约束|higher wage costs reduce the capital residual]]. Ricardo's argument about agricultural difficulty concerns this relative-price and resource-cost change.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

另作一个纯计价反例：某项生产收入 1,000、工资账单 600、资本剩余 400，投入资本以当期价格计值为 2,000，故此口径收益率为 20%。若货币价值下降使这些价格和货币计值全部翻倍，便得到收入 2,000、工资 1,200、剩余 800、资本 4,000；收益率仍是 20%，实物工资也没因翻倍而提高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a pure change of monetary scale, take revenue of 1,000, wages of 600, a capital residual of 400 and capital valued at current prices of 2,000, giving a 20% return on this basis. If all prices and monetary valuations double, the figures become 2,000, 1,200, 800 and 4,000. The return remains 20%, and the real wage has not doubled.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个反例只说明“货币工资上升”不足以推出利润率下降。它不是说实际通货膨胀总是中性：如果售价、工资、债务或资本价格没有同比调整，不同人的真实收入仍会变化。这里的“货币贬值”指单位货币购买力降低，也不能直接等同于某个汇率下跌。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The counterexample establishes only that a higher money wage is insufficient to infer a lower profit rate. Actual inflation need not be neutral: prices, wages, debts and capital valuations may adjust differently. A fall in the value of money here means lower purchasing power of the monetary unit, not automatically a decline in a particular exchange rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- Ricardo（1821），[《原理》VII.38、45](https://www.econlib.org/library/Ricardo/ricP.html?chapter_num=8#7.38)：VII.38 比较同比货币计价与利润率；VII.45 直接区分粮食生产困难与货币价值下降造成的两种涨价，并比较工业售价、工资与利润率。
- 收入、工资、剩余、资本全部翻倍的数字为独立教学构造；两次收益率分别核算为 $400/2000=800/4000=20\%$。不把纯计价例当作实际通胀或汇率政策的经验结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Chapter VII distinguishes the two price changes and their implications for wages, industrial prices and profit rates. The proportional-scaling example is constructed and both return calculations have been checked. It is not an empirical claim about inflation or exchange-rate policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
