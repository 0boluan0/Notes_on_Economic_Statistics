# 止损限价单是在达到约定触发条件后转为限价单的委托

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stop-limit order becomes a limit order when its specified trigger condition is met.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

止损限价单是由触发条件与执行限价共同规定的条件委托。止损价负责决定何时启动，启动后的[[限价单]]负责限定买入最高价或卖出最低价；两种价格可以相同，也可以不同。触发所用的成交价、报价及时段等条件仍由具体规则规定。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stop-limit order combines a trigger condition with an execution limit. The stop determines activation; the resulting [[限价单|limit order]] constrains the maximum purchase or minimum sale price. The two prices may differ. Trade-price versus quote triggers and eligible sessions remain rule-specific.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，卖出止损价为 95 元、卖出限价为 94 元。若按适用规则触发后，当前最佳可执行买价为 93 元，则不能按 93 元卖出。若委托仍有效，它只能等待出现不低于 94 元的合格成交机会；即使曾经触发，也可能一直无法成交或只成交一部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a sell stop is 95 and its sell limit is 94. If, after activation under the applicable rule, the best executable bid is 93, the order cannot sell at 93. While still valid, it must wait for an eligible opportunity at 94 or better. Activation can therefore leave it wholly or partly unfilled.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

与[[止损单]]相比，止损限价单以价格边界限制不利执行，却不能同时保证退出。若市场继续下跌，持仓风险可能仍然保留；“已触发”“已发出限价单”和“已经成交”是三个不同状态。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compared with a [[止损单|stop order]], a stop-limit order constrains adverse execution prices but cannot also guarantee exit. Exposure may remain if the market keeps falling. Triggered, active as a limit order, and executed are three different states.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
