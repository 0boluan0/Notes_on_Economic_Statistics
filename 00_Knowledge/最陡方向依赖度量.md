# Steepest ascent under a quadratic metric
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*最陡方向依赖度量*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Move one unit” needs a rule for measuring a displacement. If one coordinate is expensive to change, a fair one-unit step may move less in that coordinate. The function can stay the same while its best direction changes, because the set of allowed unit steps has changed. The [[梯度最陡方向|usual gradient direction]] is the answer for Euclidean length; a weighted quadratic length gives a different answer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“移动一个单位”需要先规定如何衡量位移。如果某个坐标的改变代价更高，公平的一单位步子就可能在这个坐标上走得更少。函数本身可以不变，而最佳方向改变，因为允许的一单位步子集合变了。[[梯度最陡方向|通常的梯度方向]]对应欧氏长度；带权的二次长度会给出另一种答案。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f$ be [[全微分|differentiable]] at an interior point $a\in\mathbb R^n$, with $n\ge1$, and write $g=\nabla f(a)$. Its first-order change under displacement $v$ is $g^Tv$. Choose a real symmetric [[正定矩阵|positive-definite matrix]] $M$ of size $n\times n$: symmetry means $M^T=M$, where $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], and positive definiteness means $v^TMv>0$ for every $v\ne0$. It defines the [[内积|inner product]] $\langle u,v\rangle_M=u^TMv$ and the length
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f$ 在内点 $a\in\mathbb R^n$ 处[[全微分|可微]]，$n\ge1$，记 $g=\nabla f(a)$。位移 $v$ 的一阶变化为 $g^Tv$。选取 $n\times n$ 的实对称[[正定矩阵]] $M$：对称指 $M^T=M$，其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]；正定指每个非零 $v$ 都有 $v^TMv>0$。它定义[[内积]] $\langle u,v\rangle_M=u^TMv$，以及长度

$$\|v\|_M=\sqrt{v^TMv}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The metric is fixed at the point for this comparison. We maximize the same linear change $g^Tv$ over $\|v\|_M=1$. For $M=\operatorname{diag}(1,4)$, the unit directions lie on $v_1^2+4v_2^2=1$: a pure horizontal unit step has size $1$, while a pure vertical unit step has size $1/2$. Here $\operatorname{diag}$ specifies the diagonal entries and sets the others to zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本次比较把该点的度量固定下来，在 $\|v\|_M=1$ 上最大化同一个线性变化 $g^Tv$。例如 $M=\operatorname{diag}(1,4)$ 时，单位方向满足 $v_1^2+4v_2^2=1$：纯横向单位步长为 $1$，纯纵向单位步长只有 $1/2$。这里 $\operatorname{diag}$ 指定对角线元素，其他位置为零。

> [!important] The normalized optimizer · 归一化最优解
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $g\ne0$, there is one maximizing vector:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $g\ne0$，最大化向量唯一：
>
> $$v_*=\frac{M^{-1}g}{\sqrt{g^TM^{-1}g}},\qquad D_{v_*}f(a)=\sqrt{g^TM^{-1}g}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The minimizing vector is $-v_*$, with the negative of this rate. “Unit” here means unit $M$-length, not necessarily unit Euclidean length.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 最小化向量为 $-v_*$，变化率取上述值的负数。“单位”在这里指 $M$-长度为一，欧氏长度不一定为一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the inverse matrix appears
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么逆矩阵会出现

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive definiteness makes $M$ invertible: $Mv=0$ would imply $v^TMv=0$, forcing $v=0$. Set $q=M^{-1}g$, equivalently solve $Mq=g$. Then $g^Tv=q^TMv=\langle q,v\rangle_M$. Thus $q$ represents the same derivative using the new inner product: it is the gradient for this chosen metric.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正定性保证 $M$ 可逆：如果 $Mv=0$，就有 $v^TMv=0$，从而只能 $v=0$。令 $q=M^{-1}g$，等价于解方程 $Mq=g$。那么 $g^Tv=q^TMv=\langle q,v\rangle_M$。所以，$q$ 用新的内积表示同一个导数，它就是这套度量下的梯度。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bound can be reduced to the ordinary [[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式|Cauchy–Schwarz inequality]]. The symmetric positive square root $M^{1/2}$ and its inverse $M^{-1/2}$ exist by the [[实对称矩阵谱定理|spectral theorem]]: diagonalize $M$ and take positive square roots of its positive eigenvalues. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个上界可归结为通常的[[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式]]。由[[实对称矩阵谱定理]]，把 $M$ 对角化并对各个正特征值取正平方根，就得到对称正定的平方根 $M^{1/2}$ 及其逆 $M^{-1/2}$。于是

$$g^Tv=(M^{-1/2}g)^T(M^{1/2}v)\le\sqrt{g^TM^{-1}g}\sqrt{v^TMv}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a unit $M$-length, the second factor is $1$. With $g\ne0$, equality requires $M^{1/2}v$ to be a positive multiple of $M^{-1/2}g$, hence $v$ a positive multiple of $M^{-1}g$. Since $q^TMq=g^TM^{-1}g>0$, dividing $q$ by its $M$-length gives precisely $v_*$ and proves uniqueness. When $M=I$, the identity matrix, this reduces to the Euclidean result. When $g=0$, all unit directions give rate zero; the displayed normalization is then undefined and no direction is singled out.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位 $M$-长度使第二个因子等于 $1$。当 $g\ne0$ 时，等号要求 $M^{1/2}v$ 是 $M^{-1/2}g$ 的正倍数，也就是 $v$ 是 $M^{-1}g$ 的正倍数。由于 $q^TMq=g^TM^{-1}g>0$，用 $q$ 的 $M$-长度归一化，就恰好得到 $v_*$，并证明唯一性。若 $M=I$（单位矩阵），公式回到欧氏结论。若 $g=0$，所有单位方向的变化率均为零；此时归一化公式无定义，也没有被单独选出的最佳方向。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Changing coordinate units changes the comparison
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 变量单位会隐含改变度量

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x,y)=x+y$, the original Euclidean gradient is $(1,1)^T$. Change the second coordinate to $z=100y$. The same function becomes $\widetilde f(x,z)=x+z/100$, with coordinate gradient $(1,0.01)^T$. If one now imposes Euclidean unit length in $(x,z)$, the allowable original displacements satisfy $dx^2+10000\,dy^2=1$. That is a different metric, $M=\operatorname{diag}(1,10000)$, in the original coordinates. The new optimum, when mapped back, points along $(1,0.0001)^T$, not $(1,0.01)^T$ in the original coordinates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x,y)=x+y$，原坐标的欧氏梯度是 $(1,1)^T$。把第二坐标换成 $z=100y$，同一个函数变为 $\widetilde f(x,z)=x+z/100$，坐标梯度为 $(1,0.01)^T$。如果现在对 $(x,z)$ 使用欧氏单位长度，原坐标的允许位移就满足 $dx^2+10000\,dy^2=1$，对应原坐标中另一套度量 $M=\operatorname{diag}(1,10000)$。把新最优方向换回原坐标，方向是 $(1,0.0001)^T$，而不是原坐标中的 $(1,0.01)^T$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To preserve the original notion of distance, instead measure the new displacement by $dx^2+dz^2/10000$. In general, for an invertible linear change $z=Ax$, use $M_z=A^{-T}M_xA^{-1}$ and $g_z=A^{-T}g_x$. Here $A^{-T}$ means the transpose of $A^{-1}$. Substitution verifies that both $v^TMv$ and $g^Tv$ are unchanged when $v_z=Av_x$, so the corresponding maximizing physical displacement is unchanged. Units alone do not alter the geometry if the metric is transformed with them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要保持原来的距离含义，应对新位移使用 $dx^2+dz^2/10000$。一般地，对可逆线性换坐标 $z=Ax$，取 $M_z=A^{-T}M_xA^{-1}$、$g_z=A^{-T}g_x$，其中 $A^{-T}$ 表示 $A^{-1}$ 的转置。代入 $v_z=Av_x$ 可验证 $v^TMv$ 与 $g^Tv$ 都保持不变，因此对应的最佳实际位移也保持不变。只要度量随坐标一起转换，单位变化本身不会改变几何。

## A weighted two-coordinate example
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小例子

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Take $g=(1,1)^T$ and $M=\operatorname{diag}(1,4)$. Solving $Mq=g$ gives $q=(1,1/4)^T$. Its squared $M$-length is $1+4(1/4)^2=5/4$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $g=(1,1)^T$、$M=\operatorname{diag}(1,4)$。解 $Mq=g$ 得 $q=(1,1/4)^T$，其 $M$-长度平方为 $1+4(1/4)^2=5/4$，因此

$$v_*=\begin{pmatrix}2/\sqrt5\\1/(2\sqrt5)\end{pmatrix},\qquad g^Tv_*=\sqrt5/2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Check the unit constraint directly: $4/5+4(1/20)=1$. The Euclidean maximizing vector $(1/\sqrt2,1/\sqrt2)^T$ has squared $M$-length $5/2$, so it is not an admissible unit step in this comparison. For $M=\operatorname{diag}(1,9)$, the direction instead follows $(1,1/9)^T$, since the second coordinate has an even higher length cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

直接核对单位约束：$4/5+4(1/20)=1$。欧氏最优向量 $(1/\sqrt2,1/\sqrt2)^T$ 的 $M$-长度平方为 $5/2$，在本次比较中并不是允许的一单位步子。若改为 $M=\operatorname{diag}(1,9)$，方向则沿 $(1,1/9)^T$，因为第二坐标的长度代价更高。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Symmetry and strict positivity matter. A merely semidefinite $M$ can assign zero length to a nonzero displacement; the formula may lack an inverse or a finite maximum. For $M=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$ and $g=(0,1)^T$, the “unit” constraint only requires $v_1^2=1$ and leaves $v_2$ unrestricted, so $g^Tv=v_2$ is unbounded. The theorem also concerns a local first-order rate, not an automatically safe finite optimization step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对称与严格正定都重要。仅半正定的 $M$ 可能给非零位移零长度，公式可能没有逆矩阵，也可能没有有限最大值。若 $M=\operatorname{diag}(1,0)$、$g=(0,1)^T$，“单位”约束只要求 $v_1^2=1$，不限制 $v_2$，于是 $g^Tv=v_2$ 无上界。这个定理考察的也是局部一阶变化率，并不自动给出可以放心使用的有限优化步长。

### Sources and verification

The book treats descent; ascent reverses the sign. The proof, diagonal examples, general coordinate transformation and singular boundary here are independently expanded.

- Boyd–Vandenberghe §9.4–9.4.1 核对归一化最陡方向、实对称正定二次范数及换坐标解释；原书讨论下降，上升反转符号。正文另行展开证明、对角例子、一般坐标变换及奇异边界。
