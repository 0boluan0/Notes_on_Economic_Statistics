# 相关类型指观察自身类型会改变对他人类型或收益参数的条件分布
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Types are correlated when observing one's own type changes the conditional distribution of other players' types or payoff parameters*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

类型**相关**，意味着自己的私人信息也携带了关于他人类型或收益状态的信息；形式上，$p_i(\theta,t_{-i}\mid t_i)$ 会随 $t_i$ 改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Types are **correlated** when a player's private information also carries information about other players' types or the payoff state; formally, $p_i(\theta,t_{-i}\mid t_i)$ varies with $t_i$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个最小联合分布

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Minimal Joint Distribution*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设两名玩家的类型都为 $H$ 或 $L$，共同联合分布为

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose both players' types are either $H$ or $L$, with the following common joint distribution:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| $t_1\backslash t_2$ | $H$ | $L$ | 行边际 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $H$ | 0.40 | 0.10 | 0.50 |
| $L$ | 0.10 | 0.40 | 0.50 |
| 列边际 | 0.50 | 0.50 | 1.00 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| $t_1\backslash t_2$ | $H$ | $L$ | Row marginal |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| $H$ | 0.40 | 0.10 | 0.50 |
| $L$ | 0.10 | 0.40 | 0.50 |
| Column marginal | 0.50 | 0.50 | 1.00 |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

无条件看，玩家 2 为 $H$ 的概率是 $0.50$。但玩家 1 观察到自己为 $H$ 后，应用[[条件概率]]得到

$$
P(t_2=H\mid t_1=H)
=\frac{0.40}{0.40+0.10}
=0.80.
$$

若玩家 1 观察到自己为 $L$，则

$$
P(t_2=H\mid t_1=L)
=\frac{0.10}{0.10+0.40}
=0.20.
$$

两种自身类型面对的最佳行动可能因此不同。把 0.50 填进两种类型的期望收益，会得到错误的[[贝叶斯纳什均衡|BNE]]条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The unconditional probability that player 2 is type $H$ is $0.50$, but observing $t_1=H$ raises the conditional probability to $0.80$, while observing $t_1=L$ lowers it to $0.20$. Own type therefore changes what player 1 knows about player 2, so the two own types may have different best actions. Using $0.50$ in both types' expected-payoff calculations would produce the wrong [[贝叶斯纳什均衡|BNE]] conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从相关性进入决策

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*From Correlation to Decision-Making*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断出相关性后，期望收益必须保留玩家已经观察到的 $t_i$。有限求和、一般积分以及何时可以改用无条件边际，见[[相关类型下的事中期望收益]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once correlation has been identified, expected payoff must preserve the own type $t_i$ that the player has already observed. See [[相关类型下的事中期望收益|interim expected payoff under correlated types]] for the finite sum, general integral, and the condition under which an unconditional marginal is valid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 独立是让条件退化的特殊情形

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Independence Is the Special Case in Which Conditioning Collapses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有在相应独立条件成立时，才有

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only when the relevant independence condition holds do we have
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p_i(t_{-i}\mid t_i)=p_i(t_{-i}).
$$

边际分布相同不代表独立；上表中两人的边际都为 $(0.5,0.5)$，类型却明显正相关。也不能因为题目没有直接写“correlated”就自动假设独立；必须从给定联合分布、信号生成过程或明确的 independence assumption 判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equal marginals do not imply independence: both types in the table have marginal distribution $(0.5,0.5)$, yet the types are positively correlated. Nor may independence be assumed merely because a problem does not explicitly say “correlated”; it must follow from the joint distribution, the signal-generating process, or an explicit independence assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[全局博弈]]给出一个重要来源：即使各人的测量噪声彼此独立，只要信号都含有同一个随机基本面，观察自己的信号仍会更新对他人信号的判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[全局博弈|Global games]] provide an important source of correlation. Even when measurement errors are independent across players, signals that share the same random fundamental make one's own signal informative about the signals observed by others.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 使用上表，玩家 1 观察到 $t_1=L$。他应给 $t_2=L$ 多大概率？若仍使用无条件边际 0.50，会遗漏什么信息？
>
> **答案：** 条件概率为 $0.40/(0.10+0.40)=0.80$。使用 0.50 会遗漏“自己是低类型”对他人也更可能是低类型所提供的信息，从而可能改变期望收益与最佳反应。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check.** Using the table above, player 1 observes $t_1=L$. What probability should they assign to $t_2=L$? If they still use the unconditional marginal $0.50$, what information do they omit?
>
> **Answer:** The conditional probability is $0.40/(0.10+0.40)=0.80$. Using $0.50$ omits the information that one's own low type makes the other player more likely to be a low type as well, which can change expected payoffs and the best response.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
