# 菱形形态是价格波动区间先扩张后收敛形成的四边轮廓

菱形形态是在同一观察尺度下，价格高低点围成的区间先向上下两侧扩张、再向中间收敛，从而形成近似菱形四边轮廓的多期结构。它不是要求四条边严格等长的欧氏几何菱形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A diamond pattern is a multi-bar structure in which the range bounded by price highs and lows first expands on both sides and then contracts, producing an approximately diamond-shaped outline. Its four sides need not have equal Euclidean lengths.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

前半段类似[[喇叭形态]]：高点抬高、低点降低；后半段类似[[对称三角形]]：高点降低、低点抬高。只有其中一半，不能认定已经出现完整的菱形轮廓。例如高点先110→120再115→110，低点先100→90再95→100，体现先宽后窄的方向对照；实际识别还须保留这些高低点的时间顺序。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first part resembles a [[喇叭形态|broadening formation]], with higher highs and lower lows; the second resembles a [[对称三角形|symmetrical triangle]], with lower highs and higher lows. Either half alone does not establish the full outline. Highs first moving 110→120 then 115→110, and lows first moving 100→90 then 95→100, illustrate the change from expansion to contraction; actual identification must retain the turning points' temporal order.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

边界、分段位置、近似容差和高低点确认规则须一致。收敛半段尚未出现时，不能用后来补齐的右半边冒充当时已经知道的菱形；完整轮廓也不保证最终向下或向上离开。原课图中的下破属于示例路径，而不是定义的一部分。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use consistent boundaries, segmentation, tolerances and pivot-confirmation rules. Before contraction develops, the future right half cannot be treated as an already known diamond. Even a completed outline does not guarantee either exit direction. The course's downside break is an example path, not part of the definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
