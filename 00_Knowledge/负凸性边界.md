# 赎回或提前还款响应足够强时债券可在部分利率区间呈现负凸性

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sufficiently strong call or prepayment responses can make a bond negatively convex over part of the rate range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在价格为正且对指定曲线冲击二阶可微的区间，负凸性指 $P''(x)<0$，即 [[有效凸性]] 为负。对典型可赎回或可提前还款固定利率资产，降息提高提前结束高息支付的动机，限制上涨空间；加息时这项限制减弱，较长支付仍留在投资者手中。这种不对称可以压过固定现金流本身的正凸性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Where price is positive and twice differentiable with respect to a specified curve shock, negative convexity means $P''(x)<0$, hence negative [[有效凸性|effective convexity]]. For a typical callable or prepayable fixed-rate asset, falling rates encourage early termination of high-rate payments and limit appreciation. As rates rise, that constraint recedes and longer payments remain outstanding. This asymmetry can outweigh fixed-cash-flow positive convexity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[赎回权的价值效应]] 给出同条件分解 $P_{\mathrm{callable}}=P_{\mathrm{straight}}-V_{\mathrm{call}}$。沿同一利率因素求二阶导数，只有当 $V_{\mathrm{call}}''>P_{\mathrm{straight}}''$ 时才由此得到负凸性；“期权价值非负”本身不是这条二阶不等式。远离赎回有吸引力的区间、赎回保护期或不同条款下，仍可能出现正凸性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The matched-contract decomposition in [[赎回权的价值效应|the issuer-call value effect]] is $P_{\mathrm{callable}}=P_{\mathrm{straight}}-V_{\mathrm{call}}$. Differentiating twice along one common rate factor gives negative convexity from this decomposition only when $V_{\mathrm{call}}''>P_{\mathrm{straight}}''$. A nonnegative option value does not itself imply that second-derivative inequality. Positive convexity can remain away from the attractive-call region, during call protection, or under different terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个仅用于检查符号的局部价格模型是 $P(x)=100-100x-2000x^2$，其中 $x$ 是小数年率位移。在 $x=0$，久期为 1、凸性为 $-40$；上下各移动 1 个百分点得 $P(-0.01)=100.8$、$P(0.01)=98.8$。降息赚 0.8，而等幅加息亏 1.2，三个价格的中央差分同样给出 $-40$。这不是某只债券的校准估值，也不外推该二次函数到任意利率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A local model used only to check signs is $P(x)=100-100x-2000x^2$, with $x$ a decimal annual-rate shift. At zero, duration is 1 and convexity is $-40$. One-percentage-point shifts give $P(-0.01)=100.8$ and $P(0.01)=98.8$: a gain of 0.8 versus a loss of 1.2. The central-difference estimate is also $-40$. This is not a calibrated bond valuation, and the quadratic is not extrapolated to arbitrary rates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[提前还款选择权]] 还取决于费用、搬家、借款人行为和再融资约束，不能仅凭一个利率阈值断定所有贷款的支付变化。跨越不光滑行权边界时，应明确报告情景差分而非不存在的导数；收益率期限曲线自身的弯曲也不是这里的负凸性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[提前还款选择权|Prepayment]] also depends on costs, mobility, borrower behaviour, and refinancing constraints; one rate threshold does not determine every loan's cash flows. Across a nonsmooth exercise boundary, report scenario differences rather than a nonexistent derivative. Curvature across yield maturities is a different object from negative price convexity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
