# 美国现行 M1 包含公众通货需求存款和其他流动存款
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Current U.S. M1 comprises currency held outside official and bank vaults, demand deposits, and other liquid deposits*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定义
> 按 Federal Reserve H.6 于 2026-08-25 发布的美国口径，M1 由三部分相加：货币存量中的通货、经调整的需求存款，以及其他流动存款。这里的其他流动存款包括其他支票类账户和储蓄存款（含货币市场存款账户）。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Under the U.S. definition in the Federal Reserve H.6 release dated 25 August 2026, M1 is the sum of money-stock currency, adjusted demand deposits, and other liquid deposits. Other liquid deposits include other checkable accounts and savings deposits, including money-market deposit accounts.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

H.6 的 M1 通货排除美国财政部、联邦储备银行和存款机构金库持有的纸币硬币，因此它面向非银行公众的货币持有。需求存款还排除若干机构和官方部门余额，并扣除托收中款项与 Federal Reserve float；分析精确数据时必须保留这些脚注。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
H.6 money-stock currency excludes notes and coins held by the U.S. Treasury, Federal Reserve Banks, and depository-institution vaults, so it measures currency outside those holders. Demand deposits also exclude specified institutional and official balances and deduct cash items in collection and Federal Reserve float; precise data work must retain these footnotes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 易错边界

- 现行美国 M1 已包括储蓄存款，不能继续套用 2014 年“仅通货、旅行者支票和支票存款”的教材列表。
- M1 的 currency 与所有已发行实物通货不是同一集合，因为银行库存现金被排除。
- 这是美国、H.6、2026-08-25 发布状态下的定义；未来修订必须回查官方页面。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Current U.S. M1 includes savings deposits, so the 2014 textbook list of currency, travellers' checks, and checkable deposits is obsolete.
- M1 currency is not every physical note and coin issued, because vault cash is excluded.
- This is the U.S. H.6 definition as released on 25 August 2026; future use must recheck the official page.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么看到美国储蓄存款大幅进入 M1 时，不能马上解释成同额的新交易货币被创造？
>
> **答案：** 先要检查定义变化；自 2020 年 5 月起储蓄存款被重新纳入 M1，这可以是分类变化而不是同额新增资产。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why should a large entry of U.S. savings deposits into M1 not immediately be interpreted as an equal creation of new transaction money?
>
> **Answer:** Check the definition first. Savings deposits were reclassified into M1 beginning in May 2020, so the change can be statistical rather than an equal creation of assets.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
