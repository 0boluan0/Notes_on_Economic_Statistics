# Marx 的利润率是一定期间的利润相对于预付总资本的比例
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Marx's rate of profit relates profit over a specified period to total capital advanced.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利润率把一定期间的利润 $p$ 与为该生产预付的总资本 $C>0$ 相比，记为 $p'=p/C$。分母包含[[不变资本]]和[[可变资本]]的预付额，写作 $C=c_a+v_a$；它与[[预付与耗用资本|本期耗用成本]]不同，也与以销售收入为分母的[[营业利润率]]不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The profit rate compares profit $p$ over a specified period with total capital $C>0$ advanced for that production: $p'=p/C$. Its denominator includes advances of [[不变资本|constant capital]] and [[可变资本|variable capital]], written $C=c_a+v_a$. It differs from [[预付与耗用资本|current consumed cost]] and from the revenue denominator of an [[营业利润率|operating profit margin]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

卷III第3章暂时假定，这一资本取得的利润等于它在相应期间生产的剩余价值，即 $p=s$；并暂不展开利息、地租等分割，以及一般利润率形成后各资本的利润分配。在这个分析层次，才进一步写成
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volume III, Chapter 3 provisionally assumes that the profit accruing to a capital equals the surplus value it produces during the relevant period, $p=s$. It sets aside distribution into interest, rent and other forms, and allocation of profit through a general profit rate. At this analytical stage one can therefore write
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p'=\frac{s}{c_a+v_a}.
$$

若该期间恰为可变资本的一次周转，令 $x=s/v_a$ 为该轮[[剩余价值率]]，$j=c_a/v_a$ 为预付资本的价值构成比，且 $v_a>0$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the period is one turnover of variable capital, let $x=s/v_a$ be its [[剩余价值率|rate of surplus value]] and $j=c_a/v_a$ the value-composition ratio of advanced capital, with $v_a>0$. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p'=\frac{s/v_a}{1+c_a/v_a}=\frac{x}{1+j}.
$$

例如，同一轮中 $c_a=80,v_a=20,s=20$，便有 $x=1,j=4,p'=20\%$。这先是该周转期的比率。若一年完成 $n$ 次可比周转，预付规模和每轮剩余率保持稳定，并统一资本估值口径，全年剩余为 $s_{\rm year}=nxv_a$，年利润率才为 $nx/(1+j)$。例如 $n=2$ 时全年剩余40、年率40%；把单轮20%称为年率需要另有“一年一轮”的条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For one turnover with $c_a=80,v_a=20,s=20$, we obtain $x=1,j=4,p'=20\%$. This is initially the rate for that turnover period. If a year contains $n$ comparable turnovers, advances and the per-turnover surplus-value rate remain stable, and capital valuation is consistent, annual surplus is $s_{\rm year}=nxv_a$ and the annual profit rate is $nx/(1+j)$. With $n=2$, annual surplus is 40 and the annual rate is 40%. Calling the single-turnover 20% an annual rate additionally requires one turnover per year.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课堂使用 $q$ 表示可变资本，沿用该符号时可以令 $q=v_a$；但必须先核对 $q$ 是一次预付，还是多次周转累计的工资支出。相同字母替换不能消除这个计量区别。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The classroom uses $q$ for variable capital. It can represent $v_a$ only after checking whether $q$ is an advance or cumulative wage expenditure across several turnovers. Renaming a symbol does not remove that measurement distinction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
