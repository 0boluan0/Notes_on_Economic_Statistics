# 对称矩阵与正定二次型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Symmetric matrices and positive-definite quadratic forms*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 利用实对称矩阵可被正交对角化的结构，把二次型、曲率、能量和方差分解到互不干扰的方向。
> **具体锚点：** 协方差矩阵是对称半正定的，因为 $x^T\Sigma x=\operatorname{Var}(x^TX)\ge0$；其特征值是各正交方向上的方差。
> **核心难点：** “所有对角元为正”不足以保证正定；必须检查所有方向的 $x^TAx$，或使用等价的特征值/Cholesky/Sylvester 条件。
> **为什么重要：** 严格凸性、高斯模型、协方差、最小二乘和数值稳定性都依赖它。
> **继续：** 先用谱定理把方向分开，再把二次型符号转成特征值符号。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Real symmetric matrices can be orthogonally diagonalised, separating quadratic forms, curvature, energy, and variance into noninteracting directions.
> **Concrete anchor:** A covariance matrix is positive semidefinite because $x^T\Sigma x=\operatorname{Var}(x^TX)\ge0$; its eigenvalues are variances along orthogonal directions.
> **Central difficulty:** Positive diagonal entries do not guarantee positive definiteness. One must control $x^TAx$ in every direction or use an equivalent eigenvalue, Cholesky, or Sylvester condition.
> **Why it matters:** Strict convexity, Gaussian models, covariance, least squares, and numerical stability rely on this structure.
> **Continue with:** Use the spectral theorem to separate directions, then translate the sign of a quadratic form into the signs of eigenvalues.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 对称矩阵与谱定理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Symmetric matrices and the spectral theorem*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实对称矩阵拥有实特征值和一组标准正交特征向量，故 $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$。这让二次型、矩阵幂和优化能在互不干扰的正交方向上分析。一般矩阵不享有这些保证，不能机械套用 $Q\Lambda Q^T$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A real symmetric matrix has real eigenvalues and an orthonormal eigenbasis, so $A=Q\Lambda Q^T$. Quadratic forms, matrix powers, and optimisation can therefore be analysed along mutually orthogonal directions. General matrices do not share these guarantees, so $Q\Lambda Q^T$ cannot be applied mechanically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 二次型与正定性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Quadratic forms and positive definiteness*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$x^TAx$ 只由 A 的对称部分决定。若对所有非零 x 都为正，A 正定；对称矩阵正定等价于全部特征值为正，也等价于 Sylvester 主子式条件和可写成 $A=R^TR$。在二阶优化中，Hessian 正定意味着严格局部凸。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$x^TAx$ depends only on the symmetric part of $A$. A matrix is positive definite when this quantity is positive for every nonzero $x$. For symmetric matrices, that is equivalent to all eigenvalues being positive, to Sylvester's leading-principal-minor criterion, and to a Cholesky form $A=R^TR$. In second-order optimisation, a positive-definite Hessian gives strict local convexity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：从特征值读出二次型
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: reading a quadratic form through eigenvalues*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$。特征向量 $(1,1)^T$ 与 $(1,-1)^T$ 对应特征值 3 和 1，所以 A 正定。将任意 $x$ 写成单位特征向量坐标 $x=z_1q_1+z_2q_2$，则 $x^TAx=3z_1^2+z_2^2>0$。相比直接展开 $2x_1^2+2x_1x_2+2x_2^2$，谱坐标直接显示了每个方向的曲率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$. Eigenvectors $(1,1)^T$ and $(1,-1)^T$ have eigenvalues three and one, so $A$ is positive definite. Writing any $x$ in the orthonormal eigenbasis as $x=z_1q_1+z_2q_2$ gives $x^TAx=3z_1^2+z_2^2>0$. Unlike the raw expansion $2x_1^2+2x_1x_2+2x_2^2$, spectral coordinates expose curvature direction by direction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 诊断与失败模式
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnostics and failure modes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

半正定允许某些非零方向上二次型为零，所以不能单独保证严格极值或可逆性。Sylvester 判据中的“主子式”对正定是按左上角顺序的 leading principal minors，不能只检查所有 $1\times1$ 对角元。Cholesky 失败可能来自非正定，也可能来自浮点误差下的近奇异性，需配合特征值与容差解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Positive semidefiniteness allows nonzero directions with zero quadratic value, so it does not by itself imply a strict extremum or invertibility. Sylvester's criterion for positive definiteness uses the ordered leading principal minors, not merely all $1\times1$ diagonal entries. Cholesky failure may reflect genuine non-positive-definiteness or near-singularity under floating-point error and should be interpreted with eigenvalues and tolerances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么协方差矩阵不会有负特征值？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a covariance matrix not have a negative eigenvalue?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 任意 x 都有 $x^T\Sigma x=\operatorname{Var}(x^TX)\ge0$，所以它半正定；对称半正定矩阵的特征值非负。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every $x$, $x^T\Sigma x=\operatorname{Var}(x^TX)\ge0$, so the matrix is positive semidefinite; a symmetric positive-semidefinite matrix has nonnegative eigenvalues.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么对角元全为正仍不能证明正定？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why do positive diagonal entries not prove positive definiteness?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 对角元只检查坐标轴方向，交叉项可以让某个斜方向上的 $x^TAx$ 为负；例如 $\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix}$ 有特征值 3 和 $-1$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Diagonal entries test only coordinate-axis directions; cross-terms can make $x^TAx$ negative along a diagonal direction. For example, $\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix}$ has eigenvalues three and minus one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Hessian 正定在驻点处能推出什么，不能推出什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does a positive-definite Hessian at a stationary point establish, and what does it not establish?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 它证明严格局部最小；若没有整个可行域上的凸性或其他全局结构，不能单独证明全局最小。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It establishes a strict local minimum. Without convexity over the feasible region or another global argument, it does not prove a global minimum.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话说明谱定理为什么能简化二次型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why the spectral theorem simplifies a quadratic form.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 正交特征向量给出不相互混合的坐标，在该基下 $x^TAx$ 变成各坐标平方按特征值加权之和，符号与曲率一目了然。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Orthonormal eigenvectors give nonmixing coordinates in which $x^TAx$ is a weighted sum of squared coordinates, with eigenvalues as weights; signs and curvature become explicit.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
