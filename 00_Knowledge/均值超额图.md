# 均值超额图将候选阈值与超过该阈值的样本平均超额配对作图
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A mean excess plot pairs each candidate threshold with the sample mean excess above it.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定损失样本 $L_1,\ldots,L_n$，均值超额图是点 $(v,\hat e(v))$ 的图，其中横轴是阈值，纵轴只平均严格超过阈值后的超额；它不是超过阈值的原损失均值。只在 $N_v>0$ 时定义：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For losses $L_1,\ldots,L_n$, the mean excess plot consists of points $(v,\hat e(v))$. Its vertical coordinate averages the excesses over a threshold, not the original losses that exceed it. It is defined only when $N_v>0$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
N_v=\sum_{i=1}^n\mathbf1_{\{L_i>v\}},\qquad
\hat e(v)=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n(L_i-v)\mathbf1_{\{L_i>v\}}}{N_v}.
$$

对应的总体对象是 $e(v)=E[L-v\mid L>v]$，要求 $P(L>v)>0$ 且尾部期望有限。若 $u$ 以上为精确 GPD、$\xi<1$，由[[GPD阈值稳定性]]与 GPD 均值得到
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The population counterpart is $e(v)=E[L-v\mid L>v]$, requiring a positive exceedance probability and a finite tail expectation. If the tail above $u$ is exactly GPD with $\xi<1$, [[GPD阈值稳定性|threshold stability]] and the GPD mean give
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
e(v)=\frac{\beta_u+\xi(v-u)}{1-\xi},
\qquad v\ge u,\quad \beta_u+\xi(v-u)>0.
$$

这条直线的斜率是 $\xi/(1-\xi)$：有限均值前提下，上升、水平、下降分别与正、零、负形状相容。若精确 GPD 的 $\xi\ge1$，总体均值超额无限；有限样本仍能画图，但不能套用有限直线的解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The slope is $\xi/(1-\xi)$. Under the finite-mean assumption, rising, horizontal, and falling lines are compatible with positive, zero, and negative shape. For an exact GPD with $\xi\ge1$, the population mean excess is infinite. A finite sample still produces a plot, but the finite-line interpretation is invalid.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如样本为 $[2,3,5,9]$，$\hat e(3)=(2+6)/2=4$，$\hat e(5)=4$，而 $\hat e(9)$ 无定义。最后一点只靠一个观测；这两个水平点不够证明指数尾。实务上应同时报告 $N_v$，避免由末端少数点决定阈值；相邻点共用观测，也不能当成独立回归数据来证明尾型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $[2,3,5,9]$, $\hat e(3)=(2+6)/2=4$ and $\hat e(5)=4$, while $\hat e(9)$ is undefined. The last defined point uses only one observation; two horizontal points do not establish an exponential tail. Report $N_v$, avoid choosing a threshold from a few terminal points, and do not treat overlapping plotted averages as independent regression data that prove a tail family.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
