# Confidence interval coverage
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*置信区间的覆盖率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **confidence interval** is produced by a rule that converts a sample into a range of candidate parameter values. **Coverage** asks how often that rule captures the fixed true parameter when we repeatedly collect data in the stated way. The interval changes between samples; the population quantity being estimated does not.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**置信区间**由一条把样本转换成参数候选范围的规则产生。**覆盖率**问的是：按声明的方式反复收集数据时，这条规则多大比例能罩住固定的真实参数。不同样本得到的区间会变化，被估计的总体量不随这些抽样而改变。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One fixed mean, many possible intervals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一个固定均值，许多可能的区间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose we take $n=25$ [[独立同分布|iid]] measurements from a [[正态分布|normal distribution]] with unknown mean $\mu$ and **known** standard deviation $\sigma=10$. The sample mean $\overline X$ is an [[估计对象三分|estimator]] of $\mu$: it varies with the sample. Its [[标准误含义|standard error]], the standard deviation of its sampling distribution, is $\sigma/\sqrt n=2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设从[[正态分布]]中取 $n=25$ 个[[独立同分布|iid]]测量，均值 $\mu$ 未知，标准差**已知**为 $\sigma=10$。样本均值 $\overline X$ 是 $\mu$ 的[[估计对象三分|估计量]]，会随样本改变；它的[[标准误含义|标准误]]是抽样分布的标准差，等于 $\sigma/\sqrt n=2$。

> [!example] The rule and the interval observed today · 构造规则与今天得到的区间
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use the rule $I(X)=[\overline X-z_{0.975}\,2,\ \overline X+z_{0.975}\,2]$, where $z_{0.975}\approx1.96$ leaves probability $0.975$ to its left under a standard normal. If the observed mean is $52$, the reported interval is approximately $[48.08,55.92]$.
>
> To visualize repeated sampling, suppose the true mean is $50$. A sample mean of $52$ produces an interval covering $50$; a sample mean of $55$ produces approximately $[51.08,58.92]$, which misses it. The value $50$ stayed put. Different samples moved the interval.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 采用规则 $I(X)=[\overline X-z_{0.975}\,2,\ \overline X+z_{0.975}\,2]$，其中 $z_{0.975}\approx1.96$ 是标准正态分布左侧累积概率为 $0.975$ 的数。如果观察到均值 $52$，就报告约为 $[48.08,55.92]$ 的区间。
>
> 为了理解重复抽样，暂设真实均值为 $50$。样本均值为 $52$ 时，区间罩住 $50$；另一次样本均值若为 $55$，区间约为 $[51.08,58.92]$，就会漏掉它。$50$ 没有移动，是不同样本让区间移动。

## How the interval obtains its coverage
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 区间怎样获得覆盖率

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$ for the full random sample, and $I(X)=[L(X),U(X)]$ for the interval rule. The two functions $L$ and $U$ calculate the lower and upper endpoints from the data. For a fixed true parameter $\theta$, its coverage is
$$c(\theta)=P_\theta\{\theta\in I(X)\}.$$
Here $0<\alpha<1$ is the allowed miss rate: $\alpha=0.05$ corresponds to $95\%$ confidence. The symbol $\in$ means “belongs to”; $P_\theta$ means probability calculated with that true parameter. A finite-sample confidence guarantee of at least $1-\alpha$ requires $c(\theta)\ge1-\alpha$ for **every** parameter value permitted by the stated model, including the allowed values of any nuisance parameters. The nominal level $1-\alpha$ is the intended guarantee; the actual coverage is a property of the rule and model, not its label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $X=(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$ 表示完整随机样本，$I(X)=[L(X),U(X)]$ 表示区间规则，函数 $L,U$ 从数据算出下、上端点。固定真实参数 $\theta$ 后，上式定义覆盖率；$0<\alpha<1$ 是允许漏掉真值的概率上限，例如 $\alpha=0.05$ 对应 $95\%$ 置信水平。$\in$ 表示“属于”，$P_\theta$ 表示按这个真实参数计算概率。有限样本中至少 $1-\alpha$ 的置信保证，要求对模型允许的**每个**参数值，包括允许的干扰参数取值，都有 $c(\theta)\ge1-\alpha$。名义水平 $1-\alpha$ 是预期的保证，实际覆盖率由规则和模型决定，不是贴上标签就成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the normal example, the standardized mean $Z=(\overline X-\mu)/(\sigma/\sqrt n)$ has a standard normal distribution. The quantile $z=z_{0.975}$ therefore satisfies $P(-z\le Z\le z)=0.95$. Rearranging the same event gives
$$|\overline X-\mu|\le z\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mu\in\left[\overline X-z\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n},\ \overline X+z\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt n}\right].$$
The arrow means the two events are equivalent. This is why the rule has coverage $0.95$ for every $\mu$. The exact statement uses the exact [[总体分位数|normal quantile]]; replacing it by $1.96$ gives approximately the same coverage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在正态例子中，标准化均值 $Z=(\overline X-\mu)/(\sigma/\sqrt n)$ 服从标准正态分布。因此分位数 $z=z_{0.975}$ 满足 $P(-z\le Z\le z)=0.95$。把同一个事件整理，就得到上面的等价关系，双向箭头表示两边是同一事件。这说明该规则对每个 $\mu$ 都有 $0.95$ 的覆盖率。精确陈述使用精确的[[总体分位数|正态分位数]]；用 $1.96$ 代替时，覆盖率近似相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The familiar form $\widehat\theta\pm c\,\widehat{\operatorname{SE}}(\widehat\theta)$ is only a shape. The estimator, standard error, critical value $c$, and [[抽样分布|reference distribution]] must fit the same assumptions. With $n\ge2$ iid normal observations and an unknown positive variance, the usual mean interval uses a [[Student t分布|Student t]] quantile and the sample standard deviation; this is a different calibration from the known-scale example. Robust standard errors with large-sample normal critical values require their own asymptotic justification. Switching the standard error alone does not automatically preserve the old finite-sample guarantee.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见外形 $\widehat\theta\pm c\,\widehat{\operatorname{SE}}(\widehat\theta)$ 只是一个形式。估计量、标准误、临界值 $c$ 以及[[抽样分布|参考分布]]必须来自相匹配的假设。对 $n\ge2$ 个 iid 正态观测，正方差未知时，通常的均值区间使用 [[Student t分布|Student t]] 分位数与样本标准差，和开头已知尺度的例子采用不同校准。稳健标准误配大样本正态临界值，需要自己的渐近依据；只换标准误，不能自动保留原来的有限样本保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A fixed reported interval is not a posterior probability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*已经报告的固定区间不是后验概率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After observing the sample, $I(x)=[48.08,55.92]$ is fixed, and the frequentist model also treats $\mu$ as fixed. This interval either covers it or does not; we usually do not know which. The coverage guarantee does not assign a $95\%$ posterior probability to “$\mu$ is in this particular interval.” That posterior question needs an additional probability model for the unknown parameter. Nor does a mean interval claim that $95\%$ of individual observations fall inside it: uncertainty about a mean differs from the spread of measurements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

观察样本后，$I(x)=[48.08,55.92]$ 的端点已经固定；频率学派模型也把 $\mu$ 视为固定。这个区间要么罩住它，要么没有，通常我们不知道是哪种。覆盖保证并没有为“$\mu$ 落在当前这个具体区间内”赋予 $95\%$ 的后验概率；这个后验问题还需要为未知参数建立额外概率模型。均值区间也不声称包含 $95\%$ 的单个观测，因为均值的不确定性与测量值本身的分散程度不同。

> [!note] 94 successful intervals out of 100 · 一百个区间中九十四个覆盖
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A procedure with actual coverage exactly $0.95$ can produce $94$ covering intervals in $100$ independent repetitions. The coverage count is [[二项分布|binomial]] with mean $95$, not a count forced to equal $95$. The observed fraction $94/100$ estimates the procedure's coverage in that experiment; it gives neither $94\%$ nor $95\%$ posterior probability to the already calculated 101st interval.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 实际覆盖率恰为 $0.95$ 的程序，独立重复一百次后，可能只有九十四个区间罩住真值。覆盖个数服从均值为 $95$ 的[[二项分布]]，不是被强制固定为 $95$。观察到的 $94/100$ 是这次实验对程序覆盖率的经验估计，既不会给已经算出的第 101 个区间产生 $94\%$，也不会产生 $95\%$ 的参数后验概率。

## Exact, approximate, and selected intervals
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*精确区间、近似区间与选择后的区间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite-sample validity can mean coverage **at least** the nominal level rather than equality; discrete data often prevent exact equality without extra randomization. An asymptotic claim concerns a limit as sample size grows, commonly coverage tending to the nominal level under specified conditions. It does not assert exact coverage for the current sample size, and a universal sample-size cutoff cannot replace those conditions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限样本有效性可以指覆盖率**至少**达到名义水平，并不一定恰好相等；离散数据常使不额外随机化时无法处处取得等号。渐近声明讨论样本量增大时的极限，常见的是在指定条件下覆盖率趋近名义水平，不是说当前样本量已经精确达标。一个通用的样本量门槛不能代替这些条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Incorrect dependence assumptions or treating a model selected from the same data as prespecified can spoil coverage. A shorter interval is therefore not automatically more informative: it may simply understate uncertainty. [[选择后推断|Selection-aware inference]] handles the changed procedure. Likewise, coverage for one prespecified parameter does not automatically give simultaneous coverage for every interval printed in a table.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

依赖结构假设错误，或把同一数据筛选出的模型当作预先指定，都可能破坏覆盖率。因此较短的区间未必更有信息，也可能只是低估了不确定性；[[选择后推断]]处理的是改变后的程序。同样，对一个预先指定参数的覆盖保证，并不自动变成表中全部区间的同时覆盖保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why matching tests and intervals agree
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么相匹配的检验与区间会一致*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each candidate value $\theta_0$, perform the same specified level-$\alpha$ test of $H_0:\theta=\theta_0$, and retain exactly the values it does not reject. This constructs a confidence **set**. If the set is an interval, it is a confidence interval. The true parameter is excluded exactly when its true null is rejected, so a false-rejection bound of $\alpha$ yields coverage at least $1-\alpha$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个候选值 $\theta_0$，使用同一套已规定的水平 $\alpha$ 检验来检查 $H_0:\theta=\theta_0$，恰好保留没有被拒绝的值，便构成一个置信**集**；如果这个集合是区间，就是置信区间。排除真参数，恰好等于拒绝了对应的真实零假设，所以错误拒绝概率不超过 $\alpha$，就给出覆盖率至少 $1-\alpha$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the normal mean rule above, use the test that rejects when $|\overline X-\theta_0|>z\sigma/\sqrt n$. Its retained values are exactly the displayed closed interval; the strict rejection inequality also fixes what happens at an endpoint. This correspondence is an inversion of the **same** tests, including boundary conventions. It cannot be inferred merely because two procedures display a similar standard error or critical value. [[p值条件解释|P-values]] express the corresponding tail calculation, not the probability that each retained candidate is true.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对前面的正态均值规则，采用 $|\overline X-\theta_0|>z\sigma/\sqrt n$ 时拒绝的检验，留下来的值恰好组成已写出的闭区间；严格大于的拒绝不等式也规定了端点如何处理。这种对应来自对**同一族**检验的反演，包含边界约定，不能仅凭两边出现类似的标准误或临界值就断言成立。[[p值条件解释|p 值]]表达相应尾概率，并不为每个保留下来的候选值赋予真实性概率。
