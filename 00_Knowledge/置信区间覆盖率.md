# 频率学派置信水平描述区间构造程序的长期覆盖率，而不是当前参数落入区间的后验概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A frequentist confidence level describes the long-run coverage of an interval procedure, not a posterior probability that the current parameter lies inside the realised interval*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子边界
> 设数据为随机变量 $X$，区间规则为 $I(X)$。一个覆盖率不低于 $1-\alpha$ 的 confidence procedure 在所声明的模型与校准条件下要求
> $$
> P_\theta\!\left\{\theta\in I(X)\right\}\ge 1-\alpha.
> $$
> 精确程序在可达时可取等号；渐近程序通常要求覆盖率为 $1-\alpha+o(1)$。概率属于“反复抽样并反复构造区间”的程序；观测当前数据后，区间端点已经固定，频率学派不会因此把固定参数改写成有 $1-\alpha$ 概率落在其中。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For random data $X$ and an interval rule $I(X)$, a confidence procedure with nominal coverage $1-\alpha$ requires $P_\theta\{\theta\in I(X)\}\ge 1-\alpha$ under its stated conditions. An exact procedure may attain equality when possible; an asymptotic procedure typically has coverage $1-\alpha+o(1)$. The probability belongs to the repeated procedure. Once the present data are observed, the interval endpoints are fixed; frequentist coverage does not turn the fixed parameter into a random variable with posterior probability $1-\alpha$ of lying inside them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 区间怎样获得覆盖率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How an interval obtains its coverage*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

许多双侧区间具有

$$
\hat\theta
\pm
c_{1-\alpha/2}\,se(\hat\theta)
$$

的形式，但这个外形本身不保证覆盖率。standard error、critical value 和 reference distribution 必须来自同一套抽样假设与校准。例如，经典正态线性模型中的有限样本 $t$ 临界值，与依赖大样本正态近似的稳健区间并不是可以任意互换的装饰。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Many two-sided intervals take the form $\hat\theta\pm c_{1-\alpha/2}se(\hat\theta)$, but the shape alone does not guarantee coverage. The standard error, critical value, and reference distribution must belong to the same sampling assumptions and calibration. A finite-sample $t$ critical value from a classical normal linear model is not interchangeable with a robust interval justified by a large-sample normal approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，“95% interval”是一个带条件的承诺。误差依赖结构设错、有效样本量太小、模型由同一数据筛选后仍按预先指定模型处理（见 [[选择后推断]]），都会使实际覆盖率偏离 95%。区间越窄也不自动越好；只有在覆盖承诺可信时，宽度才可解释为精度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A “95% interval” is therefore a conditional guarantee. A misspecified dependence structure, too little effective information, or treating a data-selected model as prespecified (see [[选择后推断|post-selection inference]]) can move actual coverage away from 95%. A narrower interval is not automatically better; its width measures precision only when the coverage claim is credible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若一个 $1-\alpha$ confidence interval 正是通过反演同一族双侧 level-$\alpha$ 点原假设检验构造的，那么 null value $\theta_0$ 落在区间外，当且仅当相应检验拒绝 $H_0:\theta=\theta_0$。这个对应来自“区间由该检验反演而成”，不能只凭两边看起来使用了相同 standard error 或 critical value 就自动断言。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If a $1-\alpha$ confidence interval is constructed by inverting the same family of two-sided level-$\alpha$ point-null tests, then $\theta_0$ lies outside the interval exactly when the corresponding test rejects $H_0:\theta=\theta_0$. This equivalence comes from the inversion construction; it does not follow merely because two procedures appear to use the same standard error or critical value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个正确校准的 95% 区间程序被独立重复 100 次，其中 94 个区间覆盖真实参数。对第 101 次已经算出的具体区间，能否据此写“参数有 94% 概率落在这个区间里”？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A correctly calibrated 95% interval procedure is repeated independently 100 times, and 94 intervals cover the true parameter. For the already calculated 101st interval, can one therefore say that the parameter has a 94% probability of lying inside it?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不能。94/100 是有限重复中的经验覆盖率，会围绕名义 95% 波动；它没有把第 101 个固定区间变成参数的后验分布。频率学派的陈述仍是这种构造规则在重复抽样中的覆盖性质。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** No. The 94/100 result is a finite-run empirical coverage rate that can fluctuate around 95%; it does not create a posterior distribution for the parameter in the 101st realised interval. The frequentist statement remains a property of the repeated construction rule.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
