# 边际革命与 Marshall 综合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Marginal Revolution and Marshallian Synthesis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释稀缺条件下最后一单位的评价怎样影响选择与交换，并说明 Marshall 如何把边际需求、生产成本和调整时间合进局部均衡。
> **具体锚点：** 水的总用途极大，但在供给充足时再多一杯水的边际效用很低；钻石总用途较小，却因稀缺而有较高边际评价。价格比较的是边际条件，不是总用途。
> **核心难点：** Jevons、Menger 与 Walras 并非提出同一套模型；边际效用、一般均衡和 Marshall 的局部均衡必须区分。供给与需求谁更“决定价格”也取决于所考察的时间期。
> **为什么重要：** 现代消费者选择、需求曲线、机会成本、一般均衡与弹性分析都从这些转向中发展出来。
> **继续：** 先区分总效用与边际效用，再比较 Walras 的多市场相互依赖和 Marshall 的时间化局部均衡；古典起点见 [[Smith、Ricardo 与古典政治经济学]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It explains how the valuation of the last unit under scarcity shapes choice and exchange, and how Marshall combined marginal demand, production cost, and adjustment time in partial equilibrium.
> **Concrete anchor:** Water has immense total usefulness, yet when it is abundant one additional glass has low marginal utility. Diamonds have less total usefulness but can command a high marginal valuation because they are scarce. Price comparisons concern marginal conditions, not total usefulness.
> **Central difficulty:** Jevons, Menger, and Walras did not propose one identical model. Marginal utility, general equilibrium, and Marshallian partial equilibrium must be distinguished. Whether demand or supply appears to “determine price” also depends on the time period examined.
> **Why it matters:** Modern consumer choice, demand curves, opportunity cost, general equilibrium, and elasticity analysis developed from these shifts.
> **Continue:** First distinguish total from marginal utility, then compare Walrasian interdependence across markets with Marshall's time-structured partial equilibrium. For the classical starting point, see [[Smith、Ricardo 与古典政治经济学|Smith, Ricardo, and Classical Political Economy]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> - Menger, *Principles of Economics* 与 Walras, *Elements of Pure Economics*：核验主观价值因果链与多市场一般均衡的不同研究入口。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> - Jevons, *[The Theory of Political Economy](https://www.econlib.org/library/YPDBooks/Jevons/jvnPE.html?chapter_num=7)*, verifies final degree of utility, diminishing marginal utility, and exchange conditions.

## 边际转向改变了什么
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What the Marginal Turn Changed*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分析对象从“一个物品总共多有用”转向“在已有数量下，再增加或减少一点会改变多少满足”。若效用为 $U(q)$，边际效用是 $MU(q)=dU/dq$。边际效用递减表示随着持有量增加，下一单位通常不如前一单位急迫；它不表示总效用必然下降。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The object of analysis shifted from “how useful is the whole stock?” to “how much does satisfaction change when the existing quantity changes slightly?” If utility is $U(q)$, marginal utility is $MU(q)=dU/dq$. Diminishing marginal utility means that as holdings grow, the next unit is usually less urgent than the previous one; it does not mean that total utility must fall.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这解决了价值悖论的一部分：丰富的必需品可有很高总效用而低边际效用，稀缺的非必需品可有较高边际效用。它仍不是完整价格理论，因为供给条件、收入、替代品、市场结构和其他人的需求也会进入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This addresses part of the value paradox: an abundant necessity can have high total utility and low marginal utility, while a scarce non-necessity can have high marginal utility. It is not yet a complete price theory, because supply conditions, income, substitutes, market structure, and other people's demand also matter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Jevons、Menger 与 Walras 的不同入口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Different Entry Points in Jevons, Menger, and Walras*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Jevons 用可微的效用与交换边际条件组织分析；Menger 更强调需求重要性、可用数量和高阶财货之间的因果联系；Walras 把多个市场的供求方程同时联立。三者都推动边际分析，却不能被压成一句“价值由主观效用决定”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Jevons organized analysis through differentiable utility and marginal conditions of exchange. Menger emphasized causal connections among the importance of wants, available quantities, and higher-order goods. Walras solved supply-and-demand equations across several markets simultaneously. All advanced marginal analysis, but they cannot be reduced to the single sentence “value is determined by subjective utility.”
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Walras 的关键是相互依赖：一个市场的价格改变家庭预算、企业成本和其他市场需求，局部变化需与全体系一致。这个一般均衡对象后来被进一步形式化；不要把后来的完整 Arrow–Debreu 条件全部反投射给早期 Walras。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Walras's key object was interdependence: a price change in one market alters household budgets, firm costs, and demand elsewhere, so a local change must be consistent with the entire system. General equilibrium was formalized further later; the complete later Arrow–Debreu conditions should not be projected backward onto early Walras.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 消费者边际条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Consumer's Marginal Condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若消费者购买两种商品，内部最优的基本条件是

$$\frac{MU_x}{p_x}=\frac{MU_y}{p_y},$$

即最后一元花在不同商品上带来的边际效用相等。若一边更高，把支出移向该商品可提高满足。边界解、不可分商品、收入效应和不确定性会改变简单条件的适用方式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a consumer buying two goods, the basic interior optimum condition is

$$\frac{MU_x}{p_x}=\frac{MU_y}{p_y},$$

so the marginal utility from the last unit of currency spent is equalized across goods. If one side is larger, shifting expenditure toward that good can raise satisfaction. Corner solutions, indivisible goods, income effects, and uncertainty change how the simple condition applies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### Worked example：怎样移动最后 10 元
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Reallocating the Last Ten Units of Spending*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设最后 10 元用于食品带来 6 个效用单位，用于娱乐带来 3 个效用单位。把 10 元从娱乐移到食品会提高效用。随着食品增加，其边际效用下降；随着娱乐减少，其边际效用上升，直到每元边际效用相等或遇到边界。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose the last ten units of currency spent on food yield six utility units, while the same spending on entertainment yields three. Moving ten from entertainment to food raises utility. As food consumption rises its marginal utility falls, while reducing entertainment raises its marginal utility, until marginal utility per unit of currency is equalized or a boundary is reached.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Marshall：供求剪刀与时间
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Marshall: Supply-and-demand Scissors and Time*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Marshall 的局部均衡暂时固定其他市场，分析某一市场的需求和供给共同决定价格与数量。把价格只归于效用或只归于成本，就像争论剪刀哪一片刀刃完成切割；两边的相对作用随调整时间改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Marshallian partial equilibrium temporarily holds other markets fixed and analyzes how demand and supply jointly determine price and quantity in one market. Attributing price only to utility or only to cost resembles arguing over which blade of a pair of scissors performs the cutting; their relative influence changes with adjustment time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 市场期：现有存量近乎固定，需求冲击主要反映在价格。
- 短期：企业可改变劳动和原料，却不能完全调整厂房与产能。
- 长期：资本、进入退出和生产方法能够调整，供给对价格的反应更充分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Market period: the available stock is nearly fixed, so a demand shock appears mainly in price.
- Short run: firms can vary labor and materials but cannot fully adjust plant and capacity.
- Long run: capital, entry and exit, and production methods can adjust, making supply more responsive to price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如突然增加鲜鱼需求时，当日捕获量几乎固定，价格可能大涨；数月内船只可增加出海与劳动；更长时间内船队、冷链和进入退出都能变化。同一个需求冲击在不同时间窗下不能用同一条供给曲线解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If demand for fresh fish suddenly rises, the day's catch is nearly fixed and price may jump. Over months, boats can increase trips and labor; over longer horizons, fleets, cold chains, and entry or exit can change. The same demand shock cannot be explained with one unchanged supply curve across all horizons.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 弹性与局部均衡的使用边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Elasticity and the Limits of Partial Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

价格弹性把响应标准化为百分比：$\varepsilon=(dq/q)/(dp/p)$。它使不同单位和市场可比较，但弹性取决于价格区间、时间、替代品和市场边界。一个短期估计不能直接当长期政策参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Price elasticity standardizes response in percentage terms: $\varepsilon=(dq/q)/(dp/p)$. It permits comparison across units and markets, but depends on the price range, time horizon, substitutes, and market boundary. A short-run estimate cannot automatically serve as a long-run policy parameter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当收入效应大、投入市场联动强或政策同时改变多个价格时，局部均衡的“其他条件不变”可能太强，需要一般均衡或跨市场模型。选择方法取决于外溢是否对问题实质重要，而不是一般均衡永远比局部均衡高级。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When income effects are large, input markets are tightly linked, or policy changes several prices at once, the partial-equilibrium ceteris paribus assumption may be too strong and a general-equilibrium or cross-market model may be needed. The method depends on whether spillovers materially affect the question; general equilibrium is not automatically superior in every application.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 把边际效用递减写成总效用下降。
- 用“主观价值”一句话抹平 Jevons、Menger 与 Walras 的方法差异。
- 说需求决定短期价格、成本决定长期价格，却不说明供给能在各期限调整什么。
- 把一条局部需求曲线当成不受收入、替代品和其他市场影响的自然常数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Treating diminishing marginal utility as falling total utility.
- Using “subjective value” to erase methodological differences among Jevons, Menger, and Walras.
- Saying demand determines short-run price and cost determines long-run price without specifying which supply adjustments become possible over each horizon.
- Treating a local demand curve as a natural constant unaffected by income, substitutes, or other markets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话区分总效用与边际效用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distinguish total utility from marginal utility in your own words.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 总效用是已有全部数量带来的满足，边际效用是数量再改变一点带来的增量；前者很高时，后者仍可很低。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Total utility is satisfaction from the entire existing quantity; marginal utility is the additional satisfaction from a small quantity change. The former can be high while the latter is low.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么钻石—水例子不能只靠边际效用得到完整市场价格？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can marginal utility alone not deliver a complete market price in the diamond–water example?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 价格还取决于供给稀缺性、生产成本、收入、替代品、市场结构和其他人的需求；边际效用只说明需求一侧的重要机制。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Price also depends on supply scarcity, production cost, income, substitutes, market structure, and other buyers' demand. Marginal utility supplies an important demand-side mechanism, not the whole market outcome.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么同一需求冲击在市场期与长期可能产生不同价格变化？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can the same demand shock produce different price changes in the market period and the long run?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 市场期存量近乎固定，数量难增；长期中资本、技术与进入退出可调整，供给更有弹性，因此价格承担的调整比例不同。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Stock is nearly fixed in the market period, so quantity cannot rise much. In the long run, capital, technology, and entry or exit can adjust, making supply more elastic and changing how much adjustment falls on price.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 什么时候应从 Marshall 式局部均衡转向一般均衡？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When should analysis move from Marshallian partial equilibrium to general equilibrium?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 当收入反馈、投入价格、多个市场联动或政策外溢会实质改变结论时；若这些反馈很小，局部均衡反而更透明。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> When income feedback, input prices, multi-market links, or policy spillovers materially change the conclusion. If those feedbacks are small, partial equilibrium is often more transparent.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
