# Discovery marks and parent forests
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*搜索标记与父森林*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When searching a graph, finding a vertex and finishing its exploration are different events. A **discovery mark** says that we have already found the vertex, even if its neighbors still await inspection. A **parent** records which earlier vertex first led us there. Keeping these two records at first discovery prevents repeated scheduling and preserves a route back to the search root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

搜索一张图时，找到一个顶点和完成对它的探索是两件事。**发现标记**表示已经找到这个点，即使它的邻居还没查看；**父顶点**记录第一次从哪个更早发现的点走到了这里。在首次发现时保存这两项信息，既能防止重复安排工作，也能保留通向搜索根的路线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider a finite [[图类型的建模边界|graph]] with vertex set $V$ and edge set $E$. In a directed graph we follow outgoing edges; in an undirected graph either direction is allowed. An [[邻接表与邻接矩阵|adjacency list]] lists the permitted next steps from a vertex. A source $s\in V$ is the starting vertex. The **frontier** consists of search work already discovered but still waiting for, or participating in, further exploration.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑顶点集为 $V$、边集为 $E$ 的有限[[图类型的建模边界|图]]。有向图沿出边前进，无向边允许双向行走。[[邻接表与邻接矩阵|邻接表]]列出从一个顶点允许迈出的下一步；源点 $s\in V$ 是搜索起点。**搜索前沿**（frontier）保存已经发现、但仍在等待或参与后续探索的工作。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One discovery, one parent
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一次发现，一个父顶点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Mark $s$ discovered before scheduling it, and give it no parent. When inspecting an edge from an already discovered $u$ to $v$, first check whether $v$ has been discovered. If not, mark it immediately, set $\operatorname{parent}[v]=u$, record the discovering edge, and schedule its exploration. If $v$ was already discovered, keep its original parent. A later search root likewise has no parent. A null parent therefore does not mean “undiscovered”: roots are discovered too. Use a separate mark, or membership in a parent dictionary that also contains roots.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在安排源点 $s$ 的探索前，先标记它已发现，并把它设为无父顶点。检查从已发现顶点 $u$ 到 $v$ 的边时，先判断 $v$ 是否已经发现；若没有，就立即标记，令 $\operatorname{parent}[v]=u$，记录本次发现所用的边，再安排它的探索。若 $v$ 已发现，则保留原来的父顶点。之后重启搜索所选的新根也没有父顶点。因此，“父顶点为空”不等于“未发现”：根也已发现。可以单独存标记，或检查包含根在内的父记录字典中是否已有该顶点。

> [!example] Two routes to the same vertex · 两条路线遇到同一点
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take edges $s\to a$, $s\to b$, $a\to x$, $b\to x$. Breadth-first search uses a first-in, first-out queue: the vertex that has waited longest is processed next. After processing $s$, the queue is $[a,b]$. Processing $a$ discovers $x$, sets its parent to $a$, and leaves $[b,x]$. When $b$ examines its edge to $x$, the mark is already present, so $x$ is not inserted again and its parent stays $a$. The parent route to $x$ is $s,a,x$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取边 $s\to a$、$s\to b$、$a\to x$、$b\to x$。广度优先搜索使用先进先出队列：等待最久的点先被处理。处理 $s$ 后，队列为 $[a,b]$；处理 $a$ 时发现 $x$，把它的父顶点设为 $a$，队列变成 $[b,x]$。随后 $b$ 查看通向 $x$ 的边时，标记已经存在，因此不会重复入队，也不会改写父顶点。到 $x$ 的父路径就是 $s,a,x$。

## Why the parent edges form a forest
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*父边为什么构成森林*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Initially a root is a single vertex. Every later discovery attaches a new vertex by exactly one edge to a vertex already present. Adding such a leaf cannot create a cycle. Inductively, one search produces a [[无向树|tree]] on its discovered vertices when edge directions are ignored. Each non-root has exactly one parent, and following parents goes strictly backward in discovery order. In a finite graph this chain must end at the root. Reverse the chain to obtain a [[游走路径与环|path]] from the root to the vertex; in a directed graph the recorded edges have precisely this forward direction, even though following parent pointers backward is not generally a legal directed route.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

起初，一个根只有自身一个顶点。之后每次发现，都用恰好一条边把一个新顶点接到已有顶点上；加入这样的叶顶点不会形成环。逐步归纳可知，忽略方向后，一次搜索的父边在已发现顶点上构成一棵[[无向树|树]]。每个非根顶点恰有一个父顶点，沿父指针走会严格退到更早发现的点；有限图中最终必到根。把这条链反向排列，就得到从根到该点的一条[[游走路径与环|路径]]。有向图中记录边的方向恰是这个正向方向，而沿父指针倒着走本身通常不是合法的有向路线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If all scheduled work is eventually completed and every outgoing adjacency entry is inspected, a fresh source search, started with every vertex undiscovered, finds exactly the vertices [[图距离与可达性|reachable]] from its source. A discovered vertex has its parent-path certificate. Conversely, if a path led to an undiscovered vertex at termination, its first undiscovered vertex would follow a discovered one; scanning that edge would have discovered it, a contradiction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果所有安排的工作最终都会完成，且每个顶点的全部出邻接项都被检查，那么从全部顶点均未发现的初始状态开始，一次单源搜索恰好找到从源点[[图距离与可达性|可达]]的顶点。已发现点有父路径作为证书。反过来，若结束时还有一条路径通向未发现点，取路径上第一个未发现点，其前驱已经发现；检查这条边时便应发现它，矛盾。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After one search finishes, [[全图搜索与强连通边界|restart at an undiscovered vertex]] if any remain, retaining all old marks. Each new root starts another tree, so the combined parent edges form a [[森林|forest]]. On an undirected graph, these trees span its connected components. On a directed graph they still form a forest after forgetting arrows, but their vertex sets need not be strongly connected components. Parallel edges may offer several possible discovering edges; store the one actually used. A self-loop cannot discover its already marked endpoint. An empty graph gives an empty forest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一次搜索完成后，若还有未发现点，就[[全图搜索与强连通边界|以其中一个点为新根重启]]，同时保留此前的全部标记。每个新根开启一棵树，合起来便是[[森林]]。在无向图中，这些树分别覆盖各个连通分量；在有向图中，忽略箭头后仍是森林，但各棵树的顶点集不一定是强连通分量。平行边可能提供多条发现边，要记录真正选用的那一条；自环的终点早已标记，不能发现新点。空图得到空森林。

## Mark before scheduling more work
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么必须在发现时标记

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the example, postponing the mark until removal lets both $a$ and $b$ insert $x$ before either copy is processed. The parent may also be overwritten. Thus, for this one-record-per-vertex search rule, marking, fixing the parent and scheduling must be one logical discovery step. This is a sequential algorithmic invariant, not a claim that every correct graph-search implementation must use identical instructions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在前面的例子中，若等取出顶点时才标记，$a,b$ 就可能在任何一份 $x$ 被处理前各插入一份，父记录也可能被覆盖。因此，对本卡“每个顶点只有一份搜索记录”的规则，标记、确定父顶点和安排探索必须属于同一个逻辑发现步骤。这是顺序算法的不变量，并不是说每一种正确图搜索实现都必须使用完全相同的指令。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The difference can affect memory. For a positive integer $p$, take distinct vertices $s,a_1,\ldots,a_p,b_1,\ldots,b_p$. Let $s$ point to every $a_i$, and every $a_i$ to every $b_j$. With delayed marking, processing all $a_i$ before the queued $b_j$ can leave $p^2$ pending copies, although there are only $2p+1$ vertices. Marking on discovery schedules each $b_j$ once. Other algorithms may safely discard duplicates later, but they need their own parent and cost analysis; the guarantee here is at most one search record per vertex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这种差别会影响内存。取正整数 $p$，以及彼此不同的顶点 $s,a_1,\ldots,a_p,b_1,\ldots,b_p$。令 $s$ 指向每个 $a_i$，每个 $a_i$ 又都指向每个 $b_j$。延迟标记时，先处理完全部 $a_i$、尚未处理排队的 $b_j$，就可能积累 $p^2$ 份待处理副本，尽管总共只有 $2p+1$ 个顶点。发现时标记则每个 $b_j$ 只安排一次。其他算法可以在之后安全丢弃副本，但需要另外论证父记录和成本；这里保证的是每个顶点至多一份搜索记录。

## Discovery is not completion
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*发现并不等于完成*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three colors can record stages: white means undiscovered, gray means discovered but unfinished, and black means finished. [[BFS 分层与无权最短路|BFS]] can obtain its shortest-path result with a discovered bit and FIFO order. In standard recursive [[DFS 可达性与非最短路径|depth-first search]], a new child is explored immediately and its call finishes before the parent resumes. The gray vertices then form the current active ancestor chain. An iterative implementation that preserves this structure stores stack frames, including where each suspended neighbor scan should resume.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以用三色记录阶段：白色为未发现，灰色为已发现但未完成，黑色为已完成。[[BFS 分层与无权最短路|BFS]] 用发现标记和 FIFO 次序就能得到最短路径结论。标准递归[[DFS 可达性与非最短路径|深度优先搜索]]会立即探索新子节点，子调用结束后父调用才继续，因此灰色顶点构成当前活动祖先链。若用迭代方式保留这种结构，栈帧还要记住每个暂停的邻接扫描应从哪里恢复。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For [[有向 DFS 的环证书|directed-cycle detection]], an edge to an active ancestor is different from an edge to a finished vertex; one visited bit cannot distinguish them. Nor may every vertex waiting in an arbitrary last-in, first-out work stack be called an ancestor: pushing several siblings in advance puts unrelated pending vertices there. The discovery invariant alone guarantees a parent forest and reachability, not DFS finishing-order properties or shortest paths under an arbitrary processing order. The separate [[邻接表图搜索复杂度|running-time bound]] also specifies the data-structure costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于[[有向 DFS 的环证书|有向环检测]]，指向活动祖先的边和指向已完成顶点的边不同；一个 visited 位不能区分二者。也不能把任意后进先出工作栈里等待的每个顶点都称为祖先：提前压入多个兄弟节点时，栈里会有尚待处理但并非祖先的点。发现不变量本身保证父森林与可达性，并不让任意处理顺序都具有 DFS 完成次序性质或最短路保证。另见[[邻接表图搜索复杂度|运行时间界]]，其中还要明确数据结构操作的成本。
