# 顶点应在第一次发现时标记并用父边形成搜索森林
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mark each vertex at first discovery and let parent edges form a search forest*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子不变量
> BFS 与 DFS 都在第一次看见未发现顶点 $v$ 时立即：标记 $v$ 已发现、记录发现它的边 $(parent[v],v)$，再把 $v$ 交给之后的 queue、stack 或递归处理。这样每个非根顶点恰有一个 parent；全部 parent edges 构成一棵 search tree，多次从新根重启则构成 search forest。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Both BFS and DFS, upon first seeing an undiscovered vertex $v$, immediately mark it discovered, record the discovering edge $(parent[v],v)$, and only then place $v$ into the queue, stack, or recursive call. Every non-root vertex therefore has exactly one parent. Parent edges form a search tree, or a search forest when the search restarts from new roots.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

parent edges 不会形成 cycle。若沿 parent pointers 从任何顶点反向走，每一步都到达一个更早发现的顶点；有限图中最终到达根，不可能回到出发点。于是反向 parent chain 还给出一条“为什么该点可达”的 path certificate。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Parent edges cannot form a cycle. Following parent pointers always moves to a vertex discovered earlier, so in a finite graph the chain ends at a root rather than returning to its starting point. Reversing this chain gives a path certificate explaining why the vertex is reachable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么必须在发现时标记
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why marking must happen at discovery*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若等到顶点从 queue 或 stack 取出后才标记，两个 frontier vertices 可能在它尚未被处理时都看到它，并各自把它加入 frontier。这样会产生重复工作与竞争的 parent；在稠密图中，frontier 记录数还可从每点至多一份的 $O(|V|)$ 膨胀到 $O(|E|)$。标记与入 frontier 必须是同一个逻辑动作。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If marking is delayed until removal from the queue or stack, two frontier vertices may both see the still-unmarked vertex and insert it separately. This creates duplicate work and competing parents. In a dense graph, the frontier may grow from at most one record per vertex, $O(|V|)$, to $O(|E|)$ records. Marking and frontier insertion must be one logical action.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三色语言可以区分搜索阶段：白色未发现，灰色已发现但未完成，黑色已完成。BFS 的最短路只需“未发现/已发现”即可实现；DFS 的 directed-cycle detection 则必须知道某点是否仍在当前活动递归栈，不能只保存一个 `visited` bit。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A three-color vocabulary distinguishes white undiscovered vertices, gray discovered-but-unfinished vertices, and black finished vertices. BFS shortest paths can be implemented with only undiscovered versus discovered. Directed-cycle detection in DFS additionally needs to know whether a vertex is still on the active recursion stack; one `visited` bit is insufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> $s$ 的两个邻居 $a,b$ 都连接到 $x$。若 BFS 在 dequeue 时才标记，可能发生什么？
>
> **答案：**处理 $a$ 与 $b$ 时，$x$ 都还未 dequeue，二者会各把 $x$ enqueue 一次并可能分别写 parent。发现时立即标记则第一个发现者确定 parent，第二次检查看到已发现并跳过。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Two neighbors $a,b$ of $s$ are both adjacent to $x$. What may happen if BFS marks only at dequeue time?
>
> **Answer:** While processing $a$ and $b$, $x$ has not yet been dequeued, so both may enqueue it and write competing parents. Marking on discovery lets the first discovery fix the parent and makes the second check skip $x$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
