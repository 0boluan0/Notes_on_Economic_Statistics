# Law of total probability
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*全概率公式*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **law of total probability** finds an overall probability by working through all the ways an outcome can arise. If two factories supply a shipment, the shipment's defect rate depends on both factories' defect rates **and how much each factory supplies**. The law combines these contributions without counting any part twice or leaving any part out.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**全概率公式**通过分别考虑各种可能情况，求出整体概率。两家工厂共同供应一批货物时，整批货物的缺陷率不仅取决于各厂的缺陷率，还取决于**各厂供应了多少**。全概率公式把各部分的贡献合起来，要求既不重复计算，也不遗漏。

> [!example] Combining two factories' defect rates · 合并两家工厂的缺陷率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Factory A supplies 60% of the parts and has a 2% defect rate. Factory B supplies the remaining 40% and has a 5% defect rate. For a uniformly chosen part, the [[概率乘法法则|multiplication rule]] gives each factory's contribution to the probability of a defect:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> A 厂供应 60% 的零件，缺陷率为 2%；B 厂供应其余 40%，缺陷率为 5%。等可能随机抽一个零件，用[[概率乘法法则]]可分别计算两家工厂对“抽到缺陷零件”的贡献：
>
> $$P(D\cap A)=0.60\times0.02=0.012,\qquad P(D\cap B)=0.40\times0.05=0.020.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $D$ means “defective”, and $A,B$ identify the factory. Every defective part comes from exactly one of the two factories, so
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 其中 $D$ 表示“有缺陷”，$A,B$ 表示零件的来源。每个缺陷零件恰好来自其中一家，所以
>
> $$P(D)=0.012+0.020=0.032=3.2\%.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a hypothetical shipment of 10,000 parts matching these proportions exactly, A contributes 120 defective parts and B contributes 200. The combined 320 defects make the weighting visible. Adding the two defect rates, $2\%+5\%$, mixes proportions measured within different groups; taking their ordinary average, $3.5\%$, would give the factories equal weight despite their different supply shares.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

想象一批恰好符合这些比例的 10,000 个零件：A 厂贡献 120 个缺陷零件，B 厂贡献 200 个，总共 320 个。这样就能看清权重的作用。直接算 $2\%+5\%$，混加了两个不同组内部的比例；直接取普通平均得到 $3.5\%$，则相当于给两家工厂相同的权重，忽略了它们的供货比例不同。

## Choosing cases that cover the whole experiment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*怎样选择完整、不重叠的情况*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The **sample space** is the set of possible outcomes; an **event** is a set of outcomes satisfying a description, as introduced in [[概率空间|probability spaces]]. A **partition** divides the sample space into groups $H_1,\ldots,H_k$: every outcome belongs to exactly one group. Thus the groups are **mutually exclusive** (no overlap) and **exhaustive** (nothing omitted). In the example, the two groups identify the factory of origin.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**样本空间**是全部可能结果的集合，**事件**是满足某个描述的一组结果，见[[概率空间]]。**分区**把样本空间分成 $H_1,\ldots,H_k$ 几组，使每个结果恰好属于一组。因此这些组既**互斥**，也就是没有重叠，又**完备**，也就是没有遗漏。前面的例子就是按来源工厂分组。

> [!abstract] Weight the probability within each case · 给各情况内的概率加权
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $H_1,\ldots,H_k$ partition the sample space and every $P(H_j)>0$, then for any event $B$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $H_1,\ldots,H_k$ 构成样本空间的分区，且每个 $P(H_j)>0$，则对任意事件 $B$，
>
> $$P(B)=\sum_{j=1}^{k}P(B\mid H_j)P(H_j).$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The index $j$ labels the cases, and $\sum$ adds their contributions. $P(B\mid H_j)$ is the [[条件概率|probability of $B$ within case $H_j$]]; multiplying by the case's probability $P(H_j)$ converts it to a share of the whole experiment.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 下标 $j$ 给各种情况编号，$\sum$ 把各项贡献加起来。$P(B\mid H_j)$ 是[[条件概率|情况 $H_j$ 内部的 $B$ 概率]]；乘上该情况的概率 $P(H_j)$，就换成了它对整个试验的贡献。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Why does this work? Split $B$ into the pieces $B\cap H_j$, meaning “$B$ occurs in case $j$”. The pieces cannot overlap and together contain all of $B$, so the [[互斥事件加法|addition rule for disjoint events]] gives $P(B)=\sum_jP(B\cap H_j)$. Replacing each intersection probability with its conditional product yields the formula above. The same argument works for a countable partition, using countable additivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为什么这样算成立？把 $B$ 分成 $B\cap H_j$ 这些小块，即“$B$ 在第 $j$ 种情况下发生”。这些小块互不重叠，而且合起来覆盖整个 $B$，所以根据[[互斥事件加法]]，$P(B)=\sum_jP(B\cap H_j)$。再把每一项交集概率改写成条件概率的乘积，就得到上面的公式。利用可数可加性，同一论证也适用于由可数个分组组成的分区。

> [!warning] Check the grouping before the arithmetic · 先检查分组，再计算
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “From factory A” and “defective” are not a partition: a part can satisfy both, or neither. Likewise, listing only A is incomplete when B also supplies parts. If a partition case has probability zero, use the intersection sum $P(B)=\sum_jP(B\cap H_j)$; its contribution is zero without forming an undefined conditional probability.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> “来自 A 厂”和“有缺陷”不能组成分区：同一个零件可能同时满足两者，也可能都不满足。B 厂也供货时，只列 A 厂同样不完整。若某个分区的概率为零，可以使用交集求和式 $P(B)=\sum_jP(B\cap H_j)$；该项贡献为零，无须写出未定义的条件概率。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This calculation is especially useful when probabilities within groups are easy to specify, but the overall probability is not given. [[Bayes法则|Bayes' rule]] then uses that overall probability to ask the reverse question: after observing a defect, which factory is the part more likely to come from?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当各组内部的概率容易确定，而整体概率没有直接给出时，这个方法尤其有用。[[Bayes法则]]再利用这里算出的整体概率，回答反方向的问题：已经发现零件有缺陷，它更可能来自哪家工厂？
