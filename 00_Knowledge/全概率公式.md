# 全概率公式按完备互斥分区加权条件概率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The law of total probability weights conditional probabilities across a disjoint exhaustive partition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 若 $H_1,\ldots,H_k$ 两两互斥、并集为整个样本空间，且每个 $P(H_j)>0$，则对任意事件 $B$，
> $$P(B)=\sum_{j=1}^{k}P(B\mid H_j)P(H_j).$$
> 它把事件 $B$ 拆成互斥部分 $B\cap H_j$，再用乘法法则求和。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
>
> &nbsp;
> If $H_1,\ldots,H_k$ are pairwise disjoint, their union is the entire sample space, and each $P(H_j)>0$, then for any event $B$,
> $$P(B)=\sum_{j=1}^{k}P(B\mid H_j)P(H_j).$$
> The formula splits $B$ into the disjoint pieces $B\cap H_j$, then sums them using the multiplication rule.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分层必须既互斥又完备：重叠会重复计算，遗漏会漏掉概率质量。若某个分区事件概率为零，应直接使用联合概率形式 $P(B)=\sum_jP(B\cap H_j)$，而不是写未定义的条件概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The cases must be both disjoint and exhaustive: overlap double-counts mass, while omission loses mass. If a partition event has probability zero, use the joint-probability form $P(B)=\sum_jP(B\cap H_j)$ rather than an undefined conditional probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么把 $P(B\mid H_j)$ 直接相加通常是错的？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Why is it generally wrong to add the $P(B\mid H_j)$ values directly?
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> **答案：** 不同 $H_j$ 出现的概率不同；必须用 $P(H_j)$ 加权后再相加。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Answer:** The cases $H_j$ need not be equally likely; each conditional probability must be weighted by $P(H_j)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
