# 威廉指标是收盘价距窗口最高价的标准化距离指标
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Williams %R measures the standardized distance between the closing price and the window's highest price.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

威廉指标（Williams %R）用收盘价距近期最高价的距离，占该期最高与最低价差的比例，描述收盘价在区间中的位置。本卡采用带负号的 $-100$ 至 $0$ 点口径：越接近 $0$，收盘越接近窗口顶部；越接近 $-100$，越接近底部。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Williams %R locates the close within a recent range by dividing its distance from the highest price by the high-low range. This card uses the negative scale from $-100$ to $0$: values near $0$ place the close near the top, while values near $-100$ place it near the bottom.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定观测频率和正整数窗口 $N$。以 $H_t,L_t,C_t$ 表示当期最高价、最低价和收盘价，窗口包括当前期，所有价格须使用同一调整口径：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Specify the observation frequency and positive integer window $N$. Let $H_t,L_t,C_t$ denote the period's high, low, and close. Include the current period in the window and use one consistent price-adjustment basis:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
H^{(N)}_t=\max_{0\le j<N}H_{t-j},\qquad
L^{(N)}_t=\min_{0\le j<N}L_{t-j},
$$
$$
\%R_t=-100\frac{H^{(N)}_t-C_t}{H^{(N)}_t-L^{(N)}_t},\qquad
H^{(N)}_t>L^{(N)}_t.
$$

同一窗口还可以写成正距离 $WR^+=-\%R$，或从区间底部向上计量的位置 $RSV=100+\%R$。后者是 [[随机指标KDJ]] 的原始输入；三者的方向或原点不同，不能直接共用同一组数值阈值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the same window, a positive distance is $WR^+=-\%R$, while the position measured upward from the bottom is $RSV=100+\%R$. The latter supplies the raw input to [[随机指标KDJ|KDJ]]. These representations differ in direction or origin, so their numerical thresholds cannot be used interchangeably.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如窗口最高 $110$、最低 $90$、当期收盘 $106$，则 $\%R=-20$、$WR^+=20$、$RSV=80$。未乘 $100$ 的区间位置比例是 $0.8$；它等于 $80\%$，但不能拿数值 $0.8$ 直接与 $80$ 点比较。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a window high of $110$, low of $90$, and close of $106$, $\%R=-20$, $WR^+=20$, and $RSV=80$. The unscaled position is $0.8$, equivalent to $80\%$; a numerical value of $0.8$ cannot be compared directly with an $80$-point threshold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完整窗口不足时不输出。若最高等于最低，则区间宽度为零，本卡记为未定义（NA），不产生阈值信号。TA-Lib 在此返回 $0$ 是其软件约定，不代表存在一个有宽度的区间且收盘恰好位于顶部。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
No value is produced before a complete window exists. If its high equals its low, the range is zero; this card records NA and generates no threshold signal. TA-Lib returns $0$ in this case as a software convention, not evidence that the close sits at the top of a nonzero range.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原课 PDF 第841页的 WR 是正距离，第842页的 WMS 则是从底部计量的位置，方向互补；后者用于 KDJ 时应按 $0$–$100$ 点的 RSV 输入。阅读原课 $20/80$ 法则前，须先确认采用的是哪种方向与尺度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Course PDF page 841 defines WR as a positive distance, while page 842 defines WMS as position measured from the bottom; the two directions are complementary. For KDJ, the latter must enter as RSV on a $0$–$100$ scale. Establish the representation before interpreting the course's $20/80$ rules.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
