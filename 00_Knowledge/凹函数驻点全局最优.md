# 可微凹函数的驻点是全局最大点

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A stationary point of a differentiable concave function is a global maximizer*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f$ 在开凸域 $U$ 上可微且凹，$Df(x^*)=0$，则由[[凹性一阶判据]]，对任意 $y\in U$，
$$
f(y)\le f(x^*)+Df(x^*)(y-x^*)=f(x^*).
$$
因此驻点为全局最大点。更一般地，在能够使用同一切平面上界的凸可行集上，$Df(x^*)(y-x^*)\le0$ 对所有可行 $y$ 成立即足够，包括边界情形。

该结论以凹性为前提，不能仅用[[拟凹函数|拟凹性]]替换，也不保证驻点或最大值一定存在。最小化对凸函数使用反向不等式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[凹性一阶判据|tangent upper bound]] becomes the stationary value when the derivative vanishes, proving global maximality. A nonpositive derivative toward every feasible point also suffices whenever the same bound applies. Concavity cannot simply be replaced by [[拟凹函数|quasiconcavity]], and the result does not guarantee existence of a stationary point or an attained optimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
