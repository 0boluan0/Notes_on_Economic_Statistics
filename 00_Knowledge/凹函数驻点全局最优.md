# Stationary points of concave functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*凹函数的驻点与全局最优*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[驻点|stationary point]] is a point where a differentiable function has zero first-order change. That fact alone does not identify a maximum. For a [[凹凸性|concave function]], however, every tangent affine approximation lies above the whole graph. If that upper bound is horizontal, no other input can have a higher value. This is why a stationary point of a differentiable concave function is a **global maximizer**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[驻点]]是可微函数一阶变化为零的位置，仅凭这一点还不能判断最大值。对于[[凹凸性|凹函数]]，每一个切平面给出的仿射近似都压在整张图像上方。如果这个上界是水平的，就没有别的输入能给出更高的值。这说明了可微凹函数的驻点为什么是**全局最大点**。

> [!example] One derivative, a global conclusion · 从零导数到全局结论
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider $f(q)=8q-q^2-3$ on $\mathbb R$. The derivative $f^\prime(q)=8-2q$ vanishes at $q=4$. Since $f^{\prime\prime}(q)=-2$ everywhere, the [[导数判凹凸|derivative criterion]] shows the function is concave. Completing the square makes the global comparison visible: $f(q)=13-(q-4)^2\le13=f(4)$. For instance, $f(5)=12$, below the value $13$ at the stationary point.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 考虑 $\mathbb R$ 上的 $f(q)=8q-q^2-3$。导数 $f^\prime(q)=8-2q$ 在 $q=4$ 为零；处处 $f^{\prime\prime}(q)=-2$ 由[[导数判凹凸]]说明函数凹。配方把全局比较直接写出来：$f(q)=13-(q-4)^2\le13=f(4)$。例如 $f(5)=12$，低于驻点处的 $13$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the tangent bound is enough
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么一个切平面上界就够了*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ be an open [[凸集|convex set]] and let $f:U\to\mathbb R$ be differentiable and concave. Convexity keeps every segment between two inputs in the domain; openness permits small displacements in all directions. The [[全微分#实值函数、梯度与全微分|derivative]] $Df(x^*)$ is a row acting on a displacement, with $Df(x^*)h=\nabla f(x^*)^Th$. The star marks the candidate input, not an exponent. Suppose $x^*\in U$ and $Df(x^*)=0$. Applying the [[凹性一阶判据|first-order concavity criterion]] at that one candidate gives the following bound for every $y\in U$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ 是开[[凸集]]，$f:U\to\mathbb R$ 可微且凹。凸性保证两输入之间的线段不离开定义域，开性允许向所有方向作微小位移。[[全微分#实值函数、梯度与全微分|导数]] $Df(x^*)$ 是作用于位移的行，满足 $Df(x^*)h=\nabla f(x^*)^Th$；星号标记候选输入，不是幂。下面把切平面上界用在这个候选点。

设 $f$ 在开凸域 $U$ 上可微且凹，$Df(x^*)=0$，则由[[凹性一阶判据]]，对任意 $y\in U$，
$$
f(y)\le f(x^*)+Df(x^*)(y-x^*)=f(x^*).
$$
因此驻点为全局最大点。更一般地，在能够使用同一切平面上界的凸可行集上，$Df(x^*)(y-x^*)\le0$ 对所有可行 $y$ 成立即足够，包括边界情形。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[凹性一阶判据|tangent upper bound]] becomes the stationary value when the derivative vanishes, proving global maximality. A nonpositive derivative toward every feasible point also suffices whenever the same bound applies. Concavity cannot simply be replaced by [[拟凹函数|quasiconcavity]], and the result does not guarantee existence of a stationary point or an attained optimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的关键是“任意 $y$”：导数为零消掉的是上界中的整个线性项，所以比较范围是全域，不仅是驻点附近。$Df(x^*)=0$ 也不表示 $f(x^*)=0$，开头例子中的函数值是 $13$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A feasible boundary can have nonzero gradient
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*边界最优可以有非零梯度*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For constraints, take a nonempty convex feasible set $C\subseteq U$; “feasible” means allowed by the problem. If $x^*\in C$ and $Df(x^*)(y-x^*)\le0$ for every $y\in C$, the same tangent inequality yields $f(y)\le f(x^*)$ throughout $C$. The derivative need not vanish: only the directions toward allowed points matter. For $f(x)=x$ and $C=[0,1]$, the candidate $x^*=1$ has derivative $1$, but $f^\prime(1)(y-1)=y-1\le0$ for every feasible $y$, so it is a global maximizer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有约束时，取非空凸可行集 $C\subseteq U$；“可行”就是问题允许的选择。若 $x^*\in C$ 且对所有 $y\in C$ 都有 $Df(x^*)(y-x^*)\le0$，同一个切平面不等式就在整个 $C$ 上给出 $f(y)\le f(x^*)$。此时不要求梯度为零，只需检查朝向允许点的方向。对 $f(x)=x$、$C=[0,1]$，$x^*=1$ 的导数是 $1$，但对每个可行 $y$，都有 $f^\prime(1)(y-1)=y-1\le0$，所以它是全局最大点。

> [!warning] Check the shape assumption · 先核对函数形状
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The increasing function $f(x)=x^3$ has $f^\prime(0)=0$, but $f(1)>f(0)$, so its stationary point is not a maximum. It is [[拟凹函数|quasiconcave]]: each upper level set is an interval of the form $[\sqrt[3]{a},\infty)$. That weaker property does not provide the tangent upper bound used here. The theorem is also conditional on finding a stationary point; it does not produce one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 递增函数 $f(x)=x^3$ 满足 $f^\prime(0)=0$，但 $f(1)>f(0)$，所以这个驻点不是最大点。它是[[拟凹函数|拟凹的]]：每个上水平集都是形如 $[\sqrt[3]{a},\infty)$ 的区间。这个较弱性质不能提供这里使用的切平面上界。定理也以已经找到驻点为条件，并不自动产生一个驻点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For minimization, reverse the curvature and tangent inequality: a stationary point of a differentiable convex function is a global minimizer. [[严格凹不保证解存在|Existence and uniqueness]] require their own arguments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

该结论以凹性为前提，不能仅用[[拟凹函数|拟凹性]]替换，也不保证驻点或最大值一定存在。最小化对凸函数使用反向不等式。
