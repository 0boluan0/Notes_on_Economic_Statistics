# 严格套利排除终值亏损而风险价差交易只押注相对价格纠偏
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strict arbitrage rules out terminal losses, whereas a risky relative-value trade bets on a correction in relative prices*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[套利]]要求完整且可实施的现金流组合满足无亏损条件；风险价差交易则买入相对便宜的一方、卖出或对冲相对昂贵的一方，期待价格关系恢复。后者可能具有正的预期收益，却仍可能亏损。实务和套利限制文献常把后者也称为 arbitrage，不能把这个用词自动当成无风险证明。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[套利|Arbitrage]] requires a complete, implementable cash-flow portfolio satisfying the no-loss condition. A risky relative-value trade buys the apparently cheap side and sells or hedges the expensive side in anticipation of a restored price relationship. It can have positive expected profit and still lose money. Practice and the limits-of-arbitrage literature often also call such trades “arbitrage”; that terminology is not a proof of risklessness.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别区别时问：期末支付是否逐状态相同，还是仅在某个估值模型、历史相关性或收敛预测下接近？单期零利率、无费用下，以 98 买 A、以 100 卖空 B，保留现金 2。若某个可能状态中 A 支付 80、B 支付 100，组合终值为 $80-100+2=-18$；“现在差 2”并没有排除亏损。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ask whether terminal payments match state by state or are merely expected to be close under a valuation model, historical correlation, or convergence forecast. In a one-period, zero-interest, costless example, buy A for 98, short B for 100, and retain cash 2. If a possible terminal state pays 80 on A and 100 on B, wealth is $80-100+2=-18$. The current spread of 2 has not ruled out a loss.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使期末合同支付确实相抵，中途追加保证金、借券续借与融资期限仍属于策略的可实施条件，见[[收敛前退出风险]]。若合同本身不同，则还要检查未被对冲的现金流与交割差异；[[负基差交易]]展示了信用相关价差交易的具体核对口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even when terminal contractual payments offset, interim margin calls, stock-loan continuation, and funding maturity remain implementability conditions; see [[收敛前退出风险|exit before convergence]]. Different contracts additionally require checking unhedged cash flows and delivery differences. [[负基差交易|Negative-basis trading]] provides a concrete credit-related relative-value example.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，“没有可实施套利”不等于已经证明[[有效市场假说|价格充分反映某信息集]]。无套利约束的是可行现金流之间的价格关系；信息效率还涉及信息、正常收益模型与交易成本。一个估值偏离若只能通过承担风险来交易，两种判断便不能直接互换。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consequently, the absence of implementable arbitrage is not itself proof that [[有效市场假说|prices fully reflect an information set]]. No-arbitrage restricts prices of feasible cash flows; informational efficiency also concerns information, a normal-return model, and trading costs. When exploiting an estimated valuation discrepancy requires bearing risk, the two judgments are not interchangeable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

The −18 outcome is an independently constructed counterexample.

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Kohn, Section 1, PDF / printed pp. 4–6, reopened, supports the distinction from statistical trades that can lose and the dependence of arbitrage pricing on trading assumptions and related cash flows.
