# Interaction between two categorical variables
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*两个类别变量的交互*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An interaction asks whether the difference between two groups changes when we look within another category. Suppose $D_1$ records attendance at a training course and $D_2$ records whether someone works at site B rather than site A. Both are [[事件指示变量|indicators]]: a value of $1$ means membership, and $0$ means the reference category. Comparing trained and untrained workers separately at each site gives two gaps. Their difference is the interaction on the outcome's mean scale.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交互回答的是：换到另一个类别内比较时，原来的组间差异是否改变。设 $D_1$ 记录是否参加培训，$D_2$ 记录是否在 B 场地、而不是 A 场地工作。它们都是[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]：$1$ 表示属于该类别，$0$ 表示参照类别。分别在两个场地比较参加者与未参加者，会得到两个组差；这两个差的差，就是结果均值尺度上的交互。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read four means before four coefficients
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先读四个均值，再读四个系数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $Y$ be the outcome, such as an assessment score. Assume $E|Y|<\infty$ and that all four category combinations have positive probability. The [[条件期望|conditional mean]] $\mu_{ab}=E[Y\mid D_1=a,D_2=b]$ is the average outcome in cell $(a,b)$; the first subscript always refers to $D_1$. Consider these illustrative population means:

| Training category | Site A: $D_2=0$ | Site B: $D_2=1$ |
|---|---:|---:|
| Not trained: $D_1=0$ | $\mu_{00}=2$ | $\mu_{01}=4$ |
| Trained: $D_1=1$ | $\mu_{10}=5$ | $\mu_{11}=10$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $Y$ 为结果，例如测评分数。假设 $E|Y|<\infty$，且四种类别组合的概率都严格为正。[[条件期望|条件均值]] $\mu_{ab}=E[Y\mid D_1=a,D_2=b]$ 是 $(a,b)$ 格内的平均结果，第一个下标始终对应 $D_1$。上表是一个总体均值示例：A 场地的未参加者、参加者分别为 $2,5$；B 场地分别为 $4,10$。

> [!example] Compare the gaps, not just the levels · 比较组差的变化
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At site A, the training-group gap is $5-2=3$. At site B, it is $10-4=6$. The gap therefore changes by $6-3=3$. The interaction is $3$, not the level $10$ in the joint category, nor the single gap $6$ at site B. These are observed-group comparisons; their causal interpretation has not yet been established.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> A 场地的培训组差为 $5-2=3$，B 场地为 $10-4=6$，因此组差增加了 $6-3=3$。交互为 $3$，不是联合类别的水平 $10$，也不是 B 场地内单独的组差 $6$。这里比较的是实际群体，尚未论证因果解释。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why a product term encodes this comparison
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*乘积项怎样编码这个比较*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the four means in one expression:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用一个式子表达四个均值：

$$
E[Y\mid D_1,D_2]=\beta_0+\beta_1D_1+\beta_2D_2+\beta_{12}D_1D_2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The product $D_1D_2$ equals $1$ only in cell $(1,1)$; elsewhere it is zero. Each $\beta$ is a constant coefficient. Substituting the four codes gives the following table. It is a **saturated** representation of these four cell means: four freely chosen finite means can be represented exactly, rather than being forced to satisfy an additive pattern.

| Cell $(D_1,D_2)$ | Mean represented by the coefficients |
|---|---|
| $(0,0)$ | $\beta_0$ |
| $(1,0)$ | $\beta_0+\beta_1$ |
| $(0,1)$ | $\beta_0+\beta_2$ |
| $(1,1)$ | $\beta_0+\beta_1+\beta_2+\beta_{12}$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

乘积 $D_1D_2$ 只在 $(1,1)$ 格取 $1$，其他格都为零；各个 $\beta$ 是常数系数。上表逐一代入四种编码，得到相应均值。这是四格均值的**饱和**表示：任意四个有限均值都能被准确表达，不必被迫遵循“两个组差简单相加”的形式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Solving from the first cell onward gives $\beta_0=\mu_{00}$, $\beta_1=\mu_{10}-\mu_{00}$ and $\beta_2=\mu_{01}-\mu_{00}$. The last coefficient supplies whatever the first three terms still lack in the joint cell:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从第一格往后解，得到 $\beta_0=\mu_{00}$、$\beta_1=\mu_{10}-\mu_{00}$、$\beta_2=\mu_{01}-\mu_{00}$。最后一个系数补足前三项在联合格内缺少的部分：

$$
\beta_{12}=\mu_{11}-\mu_{10}-\mu_{01}+\mu_{00}
=(\mu_{11}-\mu_{01})-(\mu_{10}-\mu_{00}).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the example, $(\beta_0,\beta_1,\beta_2,\beta_{12})=(2,3,2,3)$, so the joint cell is $2+3+2+3=10$. Setting $\beta_{12}=0$ would instead require $\mu_{11}=5+4-2=7$: the training gap would then be the same at both sites. The formula is symmetric in the two indicators: it also measures how the site gap changes with training category. Reversing the coding of one indicator reverses the sign of the contrast, so the coding and subtraction order must accompany its reported value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本例的系数为 $(2,3,2,3)$，联合格得到 $2+3+2+3=10$。若限制 $\beta_{12}=0$，就会要求 $\mu_{11}=5+4-2=7$，即两个场地内的培训组差相同。公式对两个指标是对称的：它也衡量场地差异随培训类别改变了多少。只把一个指标的 $0/1$ 编码反过来，会使该对比变号，因此报告数值时也要说明编码与相减顺序。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Translate the question into the right contrast
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把问题翻译成正确的对比*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[指示变量系数|indicator coefficient]] $\beta_1$ is the training gap only at $D_2=0$. At $D_2=1$, that gap is $\beta_1+\beta_{12}$; it is not $\beta_{12}$ alone. Similarly, the site gap is $\beta_2$ among untrained workers and $\beta_2+\beta_{12}$ among trained workers. This is the [[交互主效应基准|reference-value interpretation of lower-order coefficients]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[指示变量系数]] $\beta_1$ 只是在 $D_2=0$ 时的培训组差。到 $D_2=1$ 时，组差是 $\beta_1+\beta_{12}$，不是单独的 $\beta_{12}$。同理，未参加者内的场地差为 $\beta_2$，参加者内为 $\beta_2+\beta_{12}$；这就是[[交互主效应基准|低阶系数依赖参照取值]]的含义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“No interaction” asks whether the two training gaps are equal, $\beta_{12}=0$. “No training-group difference at either site” asks two conditions, $\beta_1=0$ and $\beta_1+\beta_{12}=0$, equivalently $\beta_1=\beta_{12}=0$. These are different questions. An average gap also needs specified site weights; equal weights in our table give $(3+6)/2=4.5$. Use [[线性组合推断|inference for the stated coefficient combination]], including covariances, rather than treating every question as a test of one printed coefficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“没有交互”问两个培训组差是否相等，即 $\beta_{12}=0$；“两个场地都没有培训组差”则同时要求 $\beta_1=0$、$\beta_1+\beta_{12}=0$，等价于 $\beta_1=\beta_{12}=0$。二者不是同一个问题。平均组差还要明确场地权重，本表等权平均为 $(3+6)/2=4.5$。应对[[线性组合推断|明确指定的系数组合进行推断]]并计入协方差，不能把所有问题都读成某一行系数的检验。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## From population means to a fitted sample
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从总体均值到样本拟合*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a sample, let $n_{ab}$ be the number of usable observations in cell $(a,b)$ and $\bar Y_{ab}$ its sample mean. With all $n_{ab}>0$, unweighted [[普通最小二乘|OLS]] using exactly the four columns $1,D_1,D_2,D_1D_2$, and no further constraints, fits each cell at its sample mean. To see why, rewrite the sum of squared residuals as four separate within-cell sums; each is minimised at that cell's average. The same coefficient formulas therefore hold with $\mu_{ab}$ replaced by $\bar Y_{ab}$. Equal cell counts are not required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在样本中，令 $n_{ab}$ 为 $(a,b)$ 格的可用观测数，$\bar Y_{ab}$ 为格内样本均值。若每格都非空，使用且仅使用 $1,D_1,D_2,D_1D_2$ 四列、不加其他约束的非加权[[普通最小二乘|OLS]]，会把每格拟合为自己的样本均值。原因是残差平方和可分成四个格内平方和，每一项都在对应样本平均数处最小。因此把公式中的 $\mu_{ab}$ 换成 $\bar Y_{ab}$，就得到样本系数；四格人数不必相等。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are algebraic facts about means and fitted values, not a normality assumption or a standard-error formula. Extra controls, observation weights or restrictions require their own interpretation. Empty cells and sparse observations raise different questions about [[交互稀疏支持|estimability and data support]]. With more than two levels per factor, the same idea uses multiple indicator products and multiple cell contrasts; there is no single coefficient that captures every possible interaction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些是均值与拟合值的代数事实，不是正态性假设，也不是标准误公式。若增加控制变量、观察权重或模型限制，需要重新说明解释。空格与稀疏观测分别涉及[[交互稀疏支持|可估性和数据支持]]。因子的水平超过两个时，同一思路需要多个指示乘积与多个格间对比，不再由一个系数概括所有交互。

> [!warning] A difference in differences is not yet a causal effect · 差异之差还不是因果效应
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $D_1$ marks a treatment group and $D_2$ a post-treatment period, the algebra becomes the [[2×2 DID回归等价|two-group, two-period DID interaction]]. A causal interpretation still needs a justified identification argument; the usual DID route invokes conditions such as parallel trends and no anticipation. For ordinary group comparisons, see [[条件均值因果边界|conditional means and causality]]. If the model instead applies a nonlinear link to the mean, first reconstruct the means on the outcome scale: an index interaction is generally not the [[概率交互效应|interaction between predicted probabilities]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $D_1$ 是处理组、$D_2$ 是处理后时期，代数就成为[[2×2 DID回归等价|两组两期 DID 交互项]]。因果解释仍需合理的识别论证；通常的 DID 路径使用平行趋势、无预期等条件。普通群体比较的边界见[[条件均值因果边界]]。若模型对均值使用非线性链接，应先还原结果尺度上的均值：指数内的交互项一般不是[[概率交互效应|预测概率之间的交互]]。
