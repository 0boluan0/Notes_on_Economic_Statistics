# 在线性饱和 2×2 模型中，两个指示变量的交互系数等于四格条件均值的差异之差
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In a saturated linear 2×2 model, the interaction coefficient between two indicators equals the difference in differences across the four conditional means*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 四个格子决定四个系数
> 对 $D_1,D_2\in\{0,1\}$，写
> $$
> E[Y\mid D_1,D_2]
> =\beta_0+\beta_1D_1+\beta_2D_2+\beta_{12}D_1D_2.
> $$
> 这是四格均值的饱和参数化，交互系数为
> $$
> \beta_{12}
> =(\mu_{11}-\mu_{01})-(\mu_{10}-\mu_{00}),
> $$
> 即 $D_2$ 从 0 变 1 后，$D_1$ 的组差改变了多少。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For binary $D_1,D_2$, the model above is a saturated parameterisation of the four cell means. Its interaction coefficient is $(\mu_{11}-\mu_{01})-(\mu_{10}-\mu_{00})$: how much the $D_1$ group difference changes when $D_2$ moves from zero to one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| $(D_1,D_2)$ | 条件均值 |
|---|---|
| $(0,0)$ | $\beta_0$ |
| $(1,0)$ | $\beta_0+\beta_1$ |
| $(0,1)$ | $\beta_0+\beta_2$ |
| $(1,1)$ | $\beta_0+\beta_1+\beta_2+\beta_{12}$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| $(D_1,D_2)$ | Conditional mean |
|---|---|
| $(0,0)$ | $\beta_0$ |
| $(1,0)$ | $\beta_0+\beta_1$ |
| $(0,1)$ | $\beta_0+\beta_2$ |
| $(1,1)$ | $\beta_0+\beta_1+\beta_2+\beta_{12}$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\beta_1$ 只是在 $D_2=0$ 层内的 $D_1$ 差异，$\beta_2$ 只是在 $D_1=0$ 层内的 $D_2$ 差异。若要问另一个层内的简单效应，必须相加相应交互系数；若要问因子整体，则应构造相应联合对比。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\beta_1$ is the $D_1$ difference only within $D_2=0$, and $\beta_2$ is the $D_2$ difference only within $D_1=0$. A simple effect in the other layer requires adding the interaction coefficient, while a factor-wide question requires the corresponding joint contrast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $D_1$ 是处理组、$D_2$ 是处理后时期时，这个代数对象成为[[2×2 DID回归等价|2×2 DID 回归交互项]]。但“交互等于四格差异之差”不自动说明它等于因果 ATT；那还需要平行趋势等识别条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When $D_1$ denotes treatment group and $D_2$ the post period, this algebraic object becomes the [[2×2 DID回归等价|2×2 DID regression interaction]]. Equality with the four-cell difference in differences does not by itself identify a causal ATT; that requires conditions such as parallel trends.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若四格均值为 $\mu_{00}=2,\mu_{10}=5,\mu_{01}=4,\mu_{11}=10$，交互系数是多少？
>
> **答案：** $(10-4)-(5-2)=3$。$D_1$ 的组差从 3 增加到 6，增加量为 3。
