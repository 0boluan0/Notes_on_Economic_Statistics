# Marx 所说的商品是为他人需要而生产并通过交换转让的有用劳动产品
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For Marx, a commodity is a useful product of labour produced for others and transferred through exchange*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 Marx 的商品分析中，**商品（commodity）**是满足他人需要、作为交换对象生产的劳动产品。它一方面具有[[Marx使用价值|使用价值]]，另一方面具有[[Marx商品价值|价值]]；这两方面共同规定商品，不能只凭“这个东西有用”就判定它是商品。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In Marx's analysis, a **commodity** is a product of labour produced to meet another person's wants through exchange. It has both [[Marx使用价值|use-value]] and [[Marx商品价值|value]]. Usefulness alone does not establish its commodity character.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一批自种蔬菜中，留下自家食用的部分直接满足生产者的需要，送到市场用于交换的部分才按商品分析。两部分可以有相同的物理性质；改变的是产品进入他人消费的社会关系。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Of a crop of home-grown vegetables, the portion retained for the grower's own consumption directly meets that person's needs; the portion produced for market exchange is analysed as a commodity. Their physical properties may be identical. What differs is the social relation through which the product reaches its consumer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“为别人生产”还须落实为交换。原文以封建地租粮和什一税粮说明：产品虽供地主或教士使用，却不会仅因此成为商品。商品形式也有历史条件；有劳动和社会分工的地方，不必然已由商品交换组织全部生产。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Production for someone else must also involve exchange. The text uses rent-corn and tithe-corn to show that goods supplied to a landlord or priest do not become commodities simply because others consume them. The commodity form has historical conditions: labour and a social division of labour do not by themselves imply that commodity exchange organises all production.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
