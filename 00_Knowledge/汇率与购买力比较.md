# 跨国比较应按金融金额或实际购买量的目的选择汇率与购买力换算

先问要比较什么，再换货币单位。如果问题是“这笔本币能够结算多少外币债务”，市场汇率相关；如果是“不同经济体生产或消费的实际量有多大”，通常要使用对应的[[购买力平价|PPP]]。两者回答不同问题，不能只凭哪一个数更大来选择。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose the question before converting currencies. Market exchange rates matter for settling foreign-currency obligations. Corresponding [[购买力平价|PPPs]] are generally appropriate for comparing production or consumption volumes across economies. The conversions answer different questions; a larger number is not a reason to prefer one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 需要回答的问题 / Question | 对应口径 / Appropriate basis |
|---|---|
| 不同经济体平均生产规模多大？ / Average production across economies? | 同一比较体系的 GDP PPP 与[[人均GDP]] / GDP PPP and GDP per capita within the same comparison system |
| 家庭最终消费支出对应的消费量多大？ / Volumes corresponding to household final consumption expenditure? | 家庭消费 PPP / Household consumption PPP |
| 包括政府和非营利机构提供的个人服务后，消费量多大？ / Consumption including publicly or charitably provided individual services? | 实际个人消费（AIC）及其 PPP / Actual individual consumption and its PPP |
| 外币账单需要多少本币？ / Local money required for a foreign invoice? | 交易适用的市场汇率 / Applicable transaction exchange rate |
| 一国内部实际生产比去年增加多少？ / Domestic real production growth? | 可比的国内实际 GDP 时间序列 / Comparable domestic real GDP series |

例如，本币金额为 800，市场汇率为 8 本币／美元，对应美元金额是 100；若适用 PPP 为 4 本币／国际元，则对应可比购买力为 200 国际元。这两个结果并不冲突：前者是外币金额，后者是经相对价格调整的比较量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An amount of 800 local units converts to 100 US dollars at an exchange rate of 8 local units per dollar. An applicable PPP of 4 local units per international dollar yields 200 international dollars. These results do not conflict: one is a foreign-currency amount; the other is a relative-price-adjusted comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

家庭最终消费支出还包括按核算规则计入的自给品、实物收入和自住住房服务，不只包括付现购买。实际个人消费进一步包括政府或非营利机构提供给个人的教育、医疗等服务，但不等于把所有政府支出都算成家庭消费。PPP 也不能自动解决年份不同、调查福利口径不同或数据修订的问题。记录比较年份、价格基准和发布版本，具体检查沿用[[经济数据的实时口径]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Household final consumption expenditure also includes own-produced goods, income in kind and owner-occupied housing services under the accounting rules; it is not limited to cash purchases. Actual individual consumption additionally includes individual services such as education and health care supplied by government or nonprofit institutions, not all government spending. PPP conversion does not by itself resolve mismatched years, survey welfare definitions, or revisions. Record the comparison year, price basis, and release vintage using the checks in [[经济数据的实时口径|real-time data conventions]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [World Bank ICP，PPP 的用途与限制](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/icp/brief/VC_Uses)：按生产、消费、金融金额及国内增长选择口径，分项 PPP 不互换。
- [World Bank ICP，概念与定义中的 AIC](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/icp/brief/ICP2021_Concepts_definitions)：个人服务与集体政府消费的区别；换算数例为独立构造。
- [SNA 2008，§9.56–57](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/nationalaccount/docs/SNA2008.pdf#page=244)：家庭最终消费支出的现金、实物、自给品与自住住房范围。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
ICP usage guidance supports matching conversion methods to purposes; its AIC definition distinguishes individual services from collective government consumption. SNA specifies household consumption in cash and in kind, including own-produced goods and housing services. The numerical conversion is constructed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
