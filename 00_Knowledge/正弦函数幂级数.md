# 正弦函数的Maclaurin级数由交错奇次幂组成并在整个实轴成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Maclaurin series for sine has alternating odd powers and is valid on the whole real line*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以弧度为自变量，对所有实数 $x$，
$$
\sin x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^nx^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}
=x-\frac{x^3}{3!}+\frac{x^5}{5!}-\cdots,
\qquad R=\infty.
$$
中心处各阶导数按 $0,1,0,-1$ 循环，所以偶次项为零，奇次项交错。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With angles in radians, the displayed expansion holds for every real $x$. Derivatives at zero cycle through $0,1,0,-1$, eliminating even powers and alternating the odd coefficients.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

当 $x\ne0$ 时，相邻非零项的绝对值比值为 $|x|^2/[(2n+2)(2n+3)]\to0$；$x=0$ 时为恒零级数。因此 $R=\infty$。此外，正弦所有阶导数的绝对值都不超过 $1$，由 [[Taylor余项]]，$|R_{N,0}(x)|\le |x|^{N+1}/(N+1)!\to0$，才把级数的和确认为 $\sin x$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x\ne0$, the ratio of consecutive nonzero term magnitudes tends to zero; at $x=0$, every term is zero. Thus the radius is infinite. Every derivative of sine is bounded by one, so the [[Taylor余项|Taylor remainder]] also tends to zero and identifies the sum as sine.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如用 $x-x^3/6$ 近似 $\sin x$。它同时是四次 Taylor 多项式，因为四次项为零，故误差可直接界为 $|x|^5/5!$；在 $|x|\le0.2$ 上不超过 $2.67\times10^{-6}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $x-x^3/6$ is also the fourth-degree Taylor polynomial because the fourth coefficient is zero. Its error is bounded by $|x|^5/5!$, at most $2.67\times10^{-6}$ on $|x|\le0.2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
