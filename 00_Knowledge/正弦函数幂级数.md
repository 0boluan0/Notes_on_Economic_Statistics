# The sine power series
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*正弦函数幂级数*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The sine power series expresses an angle's sine using only powers, factorials and addition. It begins with the small-angle approximation $\sin x\approx x$, then corrects that estimate by subtracting $x^3/6$, adding $x^5/120$, and continuing. The infinite expression is exact; a finite truncation comes with a controllable error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正弦函数幂级数用幂、阶乘与加法表示一个角的正弦。它从小角度近似 $\sin x\approx x$ 开始，再减去 $x^3/6$、加上 $x^5/120$，逐步修正。无穷表达式给出精确值，截取有限项则可以控制误差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here the angle $x$ is measured in radians: an arc of length $s$ on a circle of radius $r$ subtends $x=s/r$ radians. The sine is the vertical coordinate on the unit circle. Radians are also the convention in [[正弦余弦求导|the sine and cosine derivative formulas]] used below.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的角 $x$ 以弧度计量：半径为 $r$ 的圆上，弧长为 $s$ 的角为 $x=s/r$ 弧度；正弦是单位圆上对应点的纵坐标。下面使用的[[正弦余弦求导]]公式也以弧度为前提。

> [!important] Odd powers with alternating coefficients / 奇次幂与交错系数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For every real $x$ in radians, the series below converges absolutely to $\sin x$. Its [[幂级数收敛半径|radius]] is infinite.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对以弧度计量的每个实数 $x$，下面的级数都绝对收敛到 $\sin x$，[[幂级数收敛半径|半径]]为无穷。

$$\sin x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^n x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}=x-\frac{x^3}{3!}+\frac{x^5}{5!}-\cdots.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The index $n=0,1,2,\ldots$ selects powers $1,3,5,\ldots$; it is not itself the power. The factorial $k!=1\cdot2\cdots k$ divides the term of power $k$, with $0!=1$. Thus the first three nonzero terms are $x$, $-x^3/6$, and $x^5/120$. Every even-power coefficient, including the constant coefficient, is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

索引 $n=0,1,2,\ldots$ 依次选择幂次 $1,3,5,\ldots$，它本身不是幂次。幂次为 $k$ 的项除以阶乘 $k!=1\cdot2\cdots k$，并约定 $0!=1$。因此前三个非零项为 $x$、$-x^3/6$、$x^5/120$；所有偶次项，包括常数项，系数都为零。

## Recovering the formula and proving it
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*恢复公式并证明它*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Differentiation cycles through $\sin x,\cos x,-\sin x,-\cos x$ and back to $\sin x$. At zero, these values cycle through $0,1,0,-1$. In the [[Taylor级数|Maclaurin coefficient formula]], divide the derivative of order $k$ at zero by $k!$. Even orders vanish; odd orders give the alternating coefficients above.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

依次求导得到 $\sin x,\cos x,-\sin x,-\cos x$，然后回到 $\sin x$；在零处的值按 $0,1,0,-1$ 循环。根据 [[Taylor级数|Maclaurin 系数公式]]，把零处第 $k$ 阶导数值除以 $k!$：偶数阶为零，奇数阶就给出上述交错系数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For fixed $x\ne0$, apply the [[级数比值判别|ratio test]] to the displayed nonzero-power terms. Their successive magnitude ratio is $|x|^2/[(2n+2)(2n+3)]\to0$, proving absolute convergence. At $x=0$, every term is zero. Inserting the omitted zero coefficients changes no partial-sum limit; it simply repeats some partial sums.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $x\ne0$ 时，对上式按非零幂次排列的项用[[级数比值判别]]：相邻项绝对值之比为 $|x|^2/[(2n+2)(2n+3)]\to0$，因而绝对收敛。$x=0$ 时每项为零。把省略的零系数项插回去，不改变部分和极限，只会重复某些部分和。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To establish [[Taylor级数等于函数|equality with sine]], let $P_N$ be its Taylor polynomial through order $N$. Every derivative of sine has magnitude at most $1$ on the segment from $0$ to $x$. Hence [[Taylor余项|Taylor's remainder estimate]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

为证明 [[Taylor级数等于函数|这个和就是正弦]]，令 $P_N$ 为保留到 $N$ 阶的 Taylor 多项式。连接 $0$ 与 $x$ 的线段上，正弦各阶导数绝对值都不超过 $1$，所以由 [[Taylor余项]]估计，

$$|\sin x-P_N(x)|\le\frac{|x|^{N+1}}{(N+1)!}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For fixed $x\ne0$, successive values of the last bound have ratio $|x|/(N+2)$, eventually at most $1/2$, and therefore tend to zero. At $x=0$, the remainder is already zero. This proves the identity, beyond merely showing that the candidate series converges.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $x\ne0$ 后，最后这个上界的相邻值之比为 $|x|/(N+2)$，最终至多为 $1/2$，因此趋零；$x=0$ 时余项已经为零。这一步才证明恒等式，而不只是候选级数有和。

> [!example] Two terms near zero / 零点附近只取两项
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $P(x)=x-x^3/6$. Its degree is three, but it also equals the Taylor polynomial through order four because the fourth coefficient is zero. We may therefore use the fifth-order remainder bound $|\sin x-P(x)|\le |x|^5/5!$. On $|x|\le0.2$, this is at most $1/375000\approx2.6667\times10^{-6}$. At $x=0.2$, $P(x)=149/750\approx0.1986667$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $P(x)=x-x^3/6$。它的次数为三，但第四项系数为零，所以它也等于保留到四阶的 Taylor 多项式。于是可用五阶余项上界 $|\sin x-P(x)|\le |x|^5/5!$。在 $|x|\le0.2$ 上，误差至多为 $1/375000\approx2.6667\times10^{-6}$；取 $x=0.2$，近似值为 $149/750\approx0.1986667$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a degree measurement $d$, substitute $x=\pi d/180$ rather than inserting $d$ directly. Infinite radius guarantees convergence as more terms are kept for each fixed input; it does not give a small error for a fixed short polynomial at arbitrarily large angles. The [[余弦函数幂级数|cosine expansion]] follows by justified termwise differentiation of this sine expansion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若角用度数 $d$ 表示，应先代入 $x=\pi d/180$，不能直接把 $d$ 填入。无限半径保证固定输入后增加项数可以收敛，并不保证固定的短多项式在任意大角度处误差都小。对此正弦展开作合法的逐项求导，就能得到[[余弦函数幂级数]]。
