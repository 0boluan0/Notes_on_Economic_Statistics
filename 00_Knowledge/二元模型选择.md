# 二元模型选择应由估计目标、支持、函数形式与样本外概率表现决定，而不是比较原始系数大小

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The choice of a binary-outcome model should be driven by the estimand, support, functional form, and out-of-sample probability performance, not by the magnitudes of raw coefficients*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 先选问题，再选模型
> 按 [[模型选择目标]]，先确定需要的是条件概率描述、样本外风险预测还是因果效应。LPM、logit 和 probit 都可以表示 $P(Y=1\mid X)$，但使用不同尺度和函数形式；原始系数不在同一尺度，不能因 logit 系数比 probit 或 LPM 大就宣布模型更好。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> LPM, logit, and probit share the target $P(Y=1\mid X)$ but use different scales and functional forms. Their raw coefficients are not on a common scale, so a larger logit coefficient than its probit or LPM counterpart does not make logit the better model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若目标是透明的平均概率差、局部线性近似或含大量固定效应的基准，LPM 往往有用，但要检查越界、非线性并匹配稳健或聚类推断。若个体概率必须合法、关系明显弯曲或要使用似然框架，logit/probit 更自然；两者主要在链接与尾部形状上不同，实质比较应回到同一协变量配置下的预测和效应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An LPM is often useful when the goal is a transparent average probability difference, a local linear approximation, or a baseline with many fixed effects, but out-of-range predictions and nonlinearity should be checked and the inference should use an appropriate robust or clustered structure. Logit or probit is more natural when individual probabilities must remain valid, the relationship is visibly curved, or a likelihood framework is needed. The two nonlinear models differ mainly in their links and tail shapes; a substantive comparison should return to predictions and effects at the same covariate configurations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选择至少要核对四件事：

1. **估计目标：** 条件关联、平均概率效应、个体风险预测还是某个因果效应？
2. **支持与规格：** 目标比较是否有数据支持，变换和交互是否足以表达关系，是否发生分离？
3. **推断结构：** 独立、聚类或面板依赖是否得到正确处理？
4. **预测证据：** 在未参与拟合的数据上，用与任务一致的 proper loss、排序指标和概率校准比较完整 pipeline。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

At a minimum, compare four things:

**1.** **Estimand:** Is the target a conditional association, an average probability effect, an individual risk prediction, or a particular causal effect?<br>
**2.** **Support and specification:** Does the data support the target comparison, do the transformations and interactions represent the relationship adequately, and is separation present?<br>
**3.** **Inference structure:** Are independence, clustering, or panel dependence handled correctly?<br>
**4.** **Predictive evidence:** On data not used for fitting, compare the complete pipelines using a task-appropriate proper loss, ranking measures, and probability calibration.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

分类 accuracy 只评价给定阈值下的硬分类，不足以选择概率模型；训练内拟合优度也不能代替样本外表现。模型选择更不会修复内生性：可信因果解释来自研究设计，不来自把 LPM 换成 logit。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Classification accuracy evaluates hard classifications at one threshold and is insufficient for choosing a probability model. In-sample goodness of fit cannot replace out-of-sample performance either. Model choice also cannot repair endogeneity: credible causal interpretation comes from the research design, not from replacing an LPM with logit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
