# Convergence of finite Markov chains
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*有限链逐步收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Imagine restarting the same machine many times from a chosen condition and recording its condition after exactly $n$ steps. **Stepwise distributional convergence** means that the probabilities in this late-time snapshot approach a fixed distribution as $n$ grows. It does not mean that a machine eventually stops changing condition, or that we have averaged its earlier observations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

想象把同一台设备从指定状态反复启动，并记录恰好 $n$ 步后处于什么状态。**逐步分布收敛**表示：随着 $n$ 增大，这张较晚时点的概率快照趋近某个固定分布。它没有说设备最后停止变化，也没有对此前的观测取平均。

> [!example] A probability that settles while the machine still moves · 概率稳定，设备仍可移动
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use states $1,2$ and the [[Markov矩阵左右约定|row transition convention]]: row $i$ gives the next-state probabilities when currently at $i$. Take
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 使用状态 $1,2$ 和[[Markov矩阵左右约定|行转移约定]]：第 $i$ 行列出当前位于 $i$ 时，下一步各状态的概率。令
>
> $$P=\begin{pmatrix}0.9&0.1\\0.2&0.8\end{pmatrix}.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $q_n=\Pr(X_n=2)$, where $X_n$ is the state at step $n$. Splitting according to the current state gives $q_{n+1}=0.1(1-q_n)+0.8q_n=0.1+0.7q_n$. Its fixed value is $1/3$, so
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $q_n=\Pr(X_n=2)$，其中 $X_n$ 是第 $n$ 步的状态。按当前状态分解，得到 $q_{n+1}=0.1(1-q_n)+0.8q_n=0.1+0.7q_n$。固定值为 $1/3$，因此
>
> $$q_n=\frac13+0.7^n\left(q_0-\frac13\right)\longrightarrow\frac13.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Starting at state $1$ gives $q_0=0$ and then $0.1,0.17,0.219,\ldots$. Every initial probability $q_0\in[0,1]$ has the same limit. Thus the distribution approaches $(2/3,1/3)$, although both directions of movement remain possible.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从状态 $1$ 开始时，$q_0=0$，随后为 $0.1,0.17,0.219,\ldots$。任意初始概率 $q_0\in[0,1]$ 都有同一极限。因此分布趋于 $(2/3,1/3)$，但两个方向的移动始终仍有可能。

## What guarantees the same limit from every start?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*什么条件保证所有初态都趋于同一分布？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $S$ be a nonempty finite state set. A [[DTMC时间齐次转移核|time-homogeneous transition matrix]] $P$ uses the same probabilities at every step; its entries are nonnegative and each row sums to one. Write $p_{ij}^{(n)}=(P^n)_{ij}=\Pr_i(X_n=j)$, where $\Pr_i$ means starting at $i$ and $P^0$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. An initial probability row $\mu$ has nonnegative entries summing to one, and its step-$n$ law is $\mu P^n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $S$ 为非空有限状态集。[[DTMC时间齐次转移核|时间齐次转移矩阵]] $P$ 每一步使用相同概率，各条目非负，每行之和为一。记 $p_{ij}^{(n)}=(P^n)_{ij}=\Pr_i(X_n=j)$，其中 $\Pr_i$ 表示从 $i$ 出发，$P^0$ 是[[单位矩阵]]。初始概率行 $\mu$ 非负且总和为一，第 $n$ 步的分布为 $\mu P^n$。

> [!important] Finite, irreducible and aperiodic · 有限、不可约、非周期
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose the chain is [[不可约链|irreducible]]: every state can reach every other along a finite positive-probability route. Suppose also that it is [[状态周期|aperiodic]]: the greatest common divisor of possible positive return lengths is one. Then its unique [[有限不可约链稳态|stationary distribution]] $\pi$, satisfying $\pi P=\pi$, obeys
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设链[[不可约链|不可约]]，即任意状态能沿有限正概率路线到达任意其他状态；再假设它[[状态周期|非周期]]，即可能的正回返步数的最大公约数为一。那么唯一的[[有限不可约链稳态|平稳分布]] $\pi$ 满足 $\pi P=\pi$，并有
>
> $$p_{ij}^{(n)}\longrightarrow\pi_j\quad(i,j\in S),\qquad
> \mu P^n\longrightarrow\pi\quad\text{for every initial law }\mu.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> These are limits of probabilities. Since there are finitely many coordinates, their absolute errors also sum to zero in the limit. Irreducibility supplies a unique positive stationary law; aperiodicity removes the timing obstruction to approaching it at every late step.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这些是概率的极限。由于坐标只有有限多个，绝对误差之和也趋于零。不可约性提供唯一且处处为正的平稳律；非周期性排除了逐个较晚时点趋近它时的时间障碍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the machine example both off-diagonal entries are positive, so the states communicate; each state can also remain where it is for one step, giving period one. A self-loop is an easy sufficient check for aperiodicity in an irreducible chain, not a necessary condition: other return lengths can have gcd one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设备例子里两个非对角条目都为正，因此两状态互通；每个状态又能一步留在原处，所以周期为一。对不可约链，自环是检查非周期性的一种方便充分条件，并非必要条件：其他回返步数也可能具有最大公约数一。

## Why repeated transitions forget the initial law
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*重复转移为什么能淡化初始分布？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Finite irreducibility and aperiodicity imply that some common power $P^m$ with positive integer $m$ has every entry strictly positive. The route-length reason is that return lengths with gcd one can be concatenated to produce every sufficiently large length; append a route to the destination, then take a common threshold over finitely many pairs. Thus, after blocks of $m$ steps, every starting state has a shared positive opportunity to reach every destination.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限、不可约且非周期意味着存在某个正整数 $m$，使共同的幂 $P^m$，全部条目严格为正。路线长度上的原因是：最大公约数为一的回返长度可以拼接出所有充分大的长度，再接上到目标的路线；最后对有限多个状态对取共同门槛。因此每经过 $m$ 步，无论从哪里开始，都有一份共同的正机会到达各个目标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here is a quantitative form of that common opportunity. Let $\Pi$ be the matrix whose every row equals $\pi$. Because $P^m$ and $\pi$ have positive entries, choose a number $\delta$ strictly between zero and one small enough that $(P^m)_{ij}\ge\delta\pi_j$ for every $i,j$. Then the residual matrix $Q$ defined below is also row-stochastic:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可以把这份共同机会写得更精确。令 $\Pi$ 的每一行都是 $\pi$。由于 $P^m$ 和 $\pi$ 的条目均为正，可以选足够小的 $0<\delta<1$，使所有 $i,j$ 都满足 $(P^m)_{ij}\ge\delta\pi_j$。于是下面定义的剩余矩阵 $Q$ 也非负且每行之和为一：

$$P^m=\delta\Pi+(1-\delta)Q,\qquad
Q=\frac{P^m-\delta\Pi}{1-\delta}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a probability error row $v=\mu-\pi$, the entries sum to zero, so $v\Pi=0$. Multiplication by a stochastic matrix cannot increase the sum of absolute errors: if $\|v\|_1=\sum_j|v_j|$, then $\|vQ\|_1\le\|v\|_1$, by the triangle inequality and the row sums of $Q$. Each block therefore contracts that error by at least the factor $1-\delta$. Repeating blocks and then any remaining steps gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对概率误差行 $v=\mu-\pi$，各条目之和为零，所以 $v\Pi=0$。乘以随机矩阵不能增加绝对误差之和：若 $\|v\|_1=\sum_j|v_j|$，则利用三角不等式与 $Q$ 的行和得到 $\|vQ\|_1\le\|v\|_1$。因此每一段至少把误差压缩一个 $1-\delta$ 因子。重复这些区段，再走完剩余步数，有

$$\|\mu P^n-\pi\|_1\le2(1-\delta)^{\lfloor n/m\rfloor}\longrightarrow0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The floor $\lfloor n/m\rfloor$ counts completed blocks, and two is an upper bound on the absolute difference sum between probability rows. This proves convergence without assuming a diagonalization. The constants depend on the chain; the theorem alone does not identify a useful numerical stopping time for a particular application.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下取整 $\lfloor n/m\rfloor$ 数出已完成的区段；二是任意两个概率行之差的绝对值总和上界。这证明了收敛，不需要假设可对角化。常数依赖具体链；定理本身并未替具体应用算出实用的停止步数。

> [!warning] The quantifier “every start” matters · “每个初态”很重要
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For the deterministic swap $P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, the unique stationary law is $(1/2,1/2)$, but a point start alternates forever. Starting at that stationary law is nevertheless constant from the outset. Thus aperiodicity is necessary for convergence from **every** start in the finite irreducible setting, not for every specially chosen initial law.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对确定性交换矩阵 $P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$，唯一平稳律为 $(1/2,1/2)$，但从单点出发会永远交替；若一开始就取平稳律，则始终不变。所以在有限不可约范围内，非周期性是**全部**初态逐步收敛所必需的，并不是说任何特殊初始律都必须振荡。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Irreducibility is not necessary for every possible convergence problem: $\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ sends all initial laws to $(1,0)$ in one step, while being reducible. General finite-chain convergence is handled by the [[Markov矩阵幂收敛判据|matrix-power criterion]]. On a countably infinite state space, irreducibility and aperiodicity alone do not guarantee a stationary probability limit; the corresponding theorem also needs [[正常返与零常返|positive recurrence]], meaning finite mean positive return times. Finally, [[周期链Cesaro平均|averaging distributions]] and [[Markov时间平均收敛|averaging a single path]] are separate conclusions that can survive periodicity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不可约并不是所有收敛问题的必要条件：$\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$ 虽可约，却在一步内把所有初始律送到 $(1,0)$。一般有限链的情形由[[Markov矩阵幂收敛判据]]处理。对可数无限状态空间，不可约与非周期本身不足以保证平稳概率极限；对应定理还需要[[正常返与零常返|正常返]]，即有限的平均正回返时间。最后，[[周期链Cesaro平均|分布取平均]]和[[Markov时间平均收敛|单条路径取平均]]是另外两个结论，即使有周期也可能成立。
