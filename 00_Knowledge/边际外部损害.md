# 边际外部损害是活动增加一单位给他人造成的额外损失
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Marginal external damage is the additional loss imposed on others by one more unit of an activity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

边际外部损害衡量：**在当前活动水平上，再增加一点活动，会使他人的损失增加多少**。先固定活动变量、时期、受影响人群和货币估值口径。若其总损害为可微函数 $D(q)$，则
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Marginal external damage asks how much additional harm a small increase in activity causes at the current level. Specify the activity, period, affected population and monetary valuation. For differentiable total damage $D(q)$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$MD(q)=D'(q).$$

设教学例中 $D(q)=q^2/2$。在 $q=50$ 处，总损害为1250，平均损害为25，边际损害为50。它们分别回答“总共损失多少”“每单位平均多少”“下一小单位增加多少损失”，不能互换。有限地从50升到51，损害实际增加50.5；用50近似这一变化，是当前点的局部近似。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With constructed damage $D(q)=q^2/2$, at $q=50$ total damage is 1,250, average damage is 25 and marginal damage is 50. These answer different questions. Increasing activity from 50 to 51 raises damage by 50.5; using 50 for that finite change is a local approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $q$ 是产量而实际排放为 $e=g(q)$，则每单位产出的边际损害是 $D_e'(e)g'(q)$，不一定等于每单位排放的边际损害。只有明说一单位产出增加一单位同质排放，才可以不经换算地使用相同数值。多个受害者的货币损害还需在一致边界内加总，注意[[收益流与资产增值|同一损失的资本化不能重复计入]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If output $q$ generates emissions $e=g(q)$, marginal damage per unit of output is $D_e'(e)g'(q)$, which need not equal damage per unit emitted. Numerical equivalence requires an explicit one-for-one relationship. Aggregate affected parties consistently and avoid [[收益流与资产增值|double-counting capitalised versions of the same loss]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在只有负生产外部性、没有遗漏其他得失的局部模型中，$MSC=MPC+MD$。这条式子先做社会资源与福利核算，并不会自动让企业面对这个成本；改变其激励是[[外部性内部化]]的问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a partial model with only a negative production externality and complete accounting of effects, $MSC=MPC+MD$. This is social accounting; making the firm face the effect requires [[外部性内部化|internalisation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
