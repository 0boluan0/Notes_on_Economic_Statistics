# Competing exponential clocks
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*竞争指数时钟*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Several different events may end the same wait: a machine may fail because its power supply fails or because a mechanical part breaks. **Competing exponential clocks** model a separate waiting time for each possible event. The first clock to ring determines both when the wait ends and which event occurs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一段等待可能由不同事件结束：机器既可能因电源故障停机，也可能因机械部件损坏停机。**竞争指数时钟**为每种候选事件分别设一个等待时间。最先响的时钟，同时决定什么时候结束等待，以及发生哪种事件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An [[指数分布|exponential waiting time]] with rate $a>0$ has survival probability $P(T>t)=e^{-at}$ and mean $1/a$. Rate is measured per unit time. For the competition below, the clocks are [[相互独立|mutually independent]]: joint probabilities for any finite selection factor into products. Independence is a modelling assumption, not a consequence of calling the events different.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

率为 $a>0$ 的[[指数分布|指数等待时间]]满足 $P(T>t)=e^{-at}$，均值为 $1/a$。速率的单位是“每单位时间”。下面假设各时钟[[相互独立]]，即任意有限组时钟的联合概率都按乘积分解。独立是模型假设，不会仅仅因为事件名称不同就自动成立。

> [!example] Two ways for a machine to fail / 机器的两种故障
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose independent power and mechanical clocks have rates $2$ and $1$ per hour. Their individual mean waits are $1/2$ and $1$ hour. The machine stops at the earlier time, whose rate is $3$ and mean is $1/3$ hour. The probability that power fails first is $2/3$; mechanical failure comes first with probability $1/3$. We add the rates, not the mean waits.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设电源与机械时钟相互独立，速率分别为每小时 $2$ 和 $1$。各自的平均等待时间为 $1/2$ 小时和 $1$ 小时。机器在较早的时刻停机，整体等待的速率为 $3$，均值为 $1/3$ 小时。电源先出问题的概率为 $2/3$，机械先出问题的概率为 $1/3$。相加的是速率，不是平均等待时间。

## The first time and the winning label
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*最早时刻与获胜编号*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $j$ label a finite or countable collection of clocks, with finite rates $a_j\ge0$. For $a_j>0$, take $T_j\sim\operatorname{Exp}(a_j)$; a zero-rate clock never rings, so define $T_j=\infty$. Write $a=\sum_j a_j$. With infinitely many clocks, first define $T=\inf_jT_j$, the greatest lower bound of all waiting times: an infimum need not in general be achieved by a clock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $j$ 标记有限个或可数多个时钟，每个速率 $a_j\ge0$ 且有限。若 $a_j>0$，令 $T_j\sim\operatorname{Exp}(a_j)$；零速率时钟永远不响，记 $T_j=\infty$。总速率记作 $a=\sum_j a_j$。时钟无限多时，先定义 $T=\inf_jT_j$，即所有等待时间的下确界；一般来说，下确界未必由某个时钟实际取得。

> [!important] Finite positive total rate / 正的有限总速率
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $0<a<\infty$, then with probability one there is a unique winning clock $J$, and $T=T_J$. Its time and label satisfy the formulas below. In particular, $T$ and $J$ are independent: learning how long the race lasted does not change the winner probabilities.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $0<a<\infty$，则以概率一存在唯一获胜时钟 $J$，且 $T=T_J$。最早时刻与获胜编号满足下式，特别地，$T$ 与 $J$ 独立：知道这一轮等待了多久，不会改变各时钟获胜的概率。
>
> $$T\sim\operatorname{Exp}(a),\qquad P(J=j)=\frac{a_j}{a},\qquad P(T>t,J=j)=e^{-at}\frac{a_j}{a}\quad(t\ge0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $J=\operatorname*{argmin}_jT_j$ names the **label** attaining the minimum, whereas $T$ is a **time**. A zero-rate label has winning probability zero. This result also covers a countably infinite collection such as $a_j=2^{-j}$ for $j=1,2,\ldots$: the total is $1$, so $T\sim\operatorname{Exp}(1)$ and $P(J=j)=2^{-j}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $J=\operatorname*{argmin}_jT_j$ 表示取得最小值的**编号**，$T$ 则是一个**时间**。零速率编号的获胜概率为零。定理也覆盖可数无穷族，例如 $a_j=2^{-j}$，$j=1,2,\ldots$：总速率为 $1$，所以 $T\sim\operatorname{Exp}(1)$，且 $P(J=j)=2^{-j}$。

## Why the rates add
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么速率相加*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For finitely many clocks, no event before $t$ means every clock survives to $t$. Independence gives $\prod_j e^{-a_jt}=e^{-at}$. For a countable family, apply this to its first $m$ clocks and let $m$ increase: the events that all selected clocks survive decrease to the event that every clock survives. Probability respects this decreasing limit. Formally, $P(T\ge t)=e^{-at}$ for $t>0$; this continuous survival function gives the exponential law.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

时钟有限时，“在 $t$ 之前没有事件”就是所有时钟都等到 $t$ 以后。独立性给出 $\prod_j e^{-a_jt}=e^{-at}$。时钟可数无穷时，先对前 $m$ 个应用这个公式，再让 $m$ 增加：所有已选时钟都未响的事件逐步缩小，极限正是全部时钟都未响的事件，概率也相应取极限。严格地，对 $t>0$ 有 $P(T\ge t)=e^{-at}$；这个连续生存函数给出指数分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a positive-rate label $j$, clock $j$ must ring at time $u$ while all other clocks survive past $u$. Its [[概率密度函数|density]] is $a_je^{-a_ju}$ and the other survival factor is $e^{-(a-a_j)u}$. Consequently,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对正速率编号 $j$，时钟 $j$ 必须在 $u$ 时刻响，而其他时钟都要等过 $u$。前者的[[概率密度函数|密度]]为 $a_je^{-a_ju}$，其他时钟共同未响的因子为 $e^{-(a-a_j)u}$，因此

$$P(T_j>t,\ T_j<T_k\text{ for every }k\ne j)=\int_t^\infty a_je^{-au}\,du=\frac{a_j}{a}e^{-at}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $t=0$, these disjoint winning events have total probability $\sum_j a_j/a=1$. This also proves that the infimum is attained almost surely in the countable case, rather than merely assuming a first clock exists. The displayed factorisation establishes the independence of time and label.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $t=0$，这些互不相交的获胜事件总概率为 $\sum_j a_j/a=1$。这也证明了可数情形中下确界几乎必然能够取得，而不是先假设一定有第一个时钟。上式的概率分解进一步给出时间与编号的独立性。

## From clocks to a Markov chain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从时钟构造 Markov 链*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At state $i$ of a [[CTMC定义|continuous-time Markov chain]], assign rate $a_j=q_{ij}$ to each possible destination $j\ne i$. The total [[出口率|exit rate]] is $q_i=\sum_{j\ne i}q_{ij}$. When $q_i>0$, the [[CTMC指数停留时间|holding time]] has rate $q_i$ and the [[嵌入跳链|genuine-jump chain]] selects $j$ with probability $q_{ij}/q_i$. Conditional on the current state, use fresh mutually independent clocks with that state's rates; their joint law does not depend on the earlier path. The [[指数分布无记忆性|memoryless property]] also permits compatible still-running exponential clocks to be reset without changing their conditional remaining-time law. Separate physical clocks are a useful construction, not an additional requirement on every CTMC model.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[CTMC定义|连续时间 Markov 链]]的状态 $i$，给每个候选去向 $j\ne i$ 设置速率 $a_j=q_{ij}$。总[[出口率]]为 $q_i=\sum_{j\ne i}q_{ij}$。当 $q_i>0$ 时，[[CTMC指数停留时间|停留时间]]的速率是 $q_i$，[[嵌入跳链|真实跳链]]以概率 $q_{ij}/q_i$ 选中 $j$。给定当前状态，按该状态的速率使用新的一组相互独立时钟；它们的联合分布不再依赖此前路径。[[指数分布无记忆性|无记忆性]]也允许对适用的、尚在运行的指数时钟重新计时，而不改变其条件剩余时间分布。分别设置物理时钟是一种有用的构造，并非每个 CTMC 模型额外必须满足的要求。

> [!warning] Zero total, infinite total, or dependent clocks / 三种边界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $a=0$, all clocks are silent: $T=\infty$ and there is no winner. If a countable independent family has $\sum_j a_j=\infty$, its infimum is $0$ almost surely, but every positive-rate clock has a strictly positive waiting time almost surely. Hence no clock attains that infimum; this is not an ordinary exponential race with an “infinite parameter”. Independence matters too: setting $T_1=T_2$ with an Exp$(1)$ variable produces a tie every time and an Exp$(1)$ minimum, not Exp$(2)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $a=0$，所有时钟都不响：$T=\infty$，不存在获胜者。若可数独立族满足 $\sum_j a_j=\infty$，则下确界几乎必然为 $0$，但每个正速率时钟的等待时间都几乎必然严格为正，因此没有时钟取得这个下确界，不能把它当成参数为“无穷”的普通指数竞赛。独立性也不能省略：令 $T_1=T_2$ 等于同一个 Exp$(1)$ 变量，会每次同时响，最小值仍是 Exp$(1)$，而不是 Exp$(2)$。

> [!question] Check the units / 检查单位
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For rates $4$ and $6$ per hour, what are the mean first-event time and the second clock's winning probability? The answers are $1/(4+6)=1/10$ hour and $6/(4+6)=0.6$. The first is a duration; the second is dimensionless.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若两个速率为每小时 $4$ 和 $6$，首次事件的平均等待时间、第二个时钟获胜的概率分别是多少？答案是 $1/(4+6)=1/10$ 小时和 $6/(4+6)=0.6$。前者是时长，后者没有单位。
