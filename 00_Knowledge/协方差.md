# Covariance
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*协方差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Covariance** averages how two variables deviate from their own means **together**. If both are above their means, or both below, their deviations have a positive product. If one is above and the other below, the product is negative. Covariance is the probability-weighted balance of these products. You need the [[期望|expectation]], or weighted average, of each variable before you can measure these deviations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**协方差**把两个变量相对各自均值的**共同偏离**取平均：都高于均值或都低于均值，偏差乘积为正；一个高、一个低，乘积为负。协方差就是按概率加权后，这些正负乘积的净结果。计算偏差之前，需要先有每个变量的[[期望]]，也就是加权平均值。

> [!abstract] Average the centred product · 平均中心化后的乘积
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Write $\mu_X=E[X]$ and $\mu_Y=E[Y]$ for the two means. When $E[X^2]$ and $E[Y^2]$ are finite, their covariance is the finite number
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 用 $\mu_X=E[X]$、$\mu_Y=E[Y]$ 表示两个均值。若 $E[X^2]$、$E[Y^2]$ 都有限，则协方差是有限的数值
>
> $$\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=E[(X-\mu_X)(Y-\mu_Y)].$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Centred” means subtracting the mean. Finite second moments are a convenient sufficient condition. More generally, the same definition is finite when both means are finite and the centred product is absolutely integrable: $E[|(X-\mu_X)(Y-\mu_Y)|]<\infty$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> “中心化”就是减去均值。有限二阶矩是一组方便的充分条件；更一般地，只要两个均值有限，且中心化乘积绝对可积，即 $E[|(X-\mu_X)(Y-\mu_Y)|]<\infty$，同一定义也给出有限协方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Calculating it from a small table
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*用一张小表计算*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $X$ label a bus route as $0$ or $1$, and let $Y$ be the waiting time in minutes. Suppose their [[联合分布|joint distribution]], which assigns probabilities to pairs, is the following table. The means are $E[X]=3/4$ and $E[Y]=5$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $X$ 把公交线路标为 $0$ 或 $1$，$Y$ 表示等待分钟数。下表是给取值组合分配概率的[[联合分布]]。两个均值分别为 $E[X]=3/4$、$E[Y]=5$。

| $X$ | $Y$ | Probability / 概率 | $(X-3/4)(Y-5)$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $0$ | $0$ | $1/8$ | $15/4$ |
| $0$ | $4$ | $1/8$ | $3/4$ |
| $1$ | $4$ | $3/8$ | $-1/4$ |
| $1$ | $8$ | $3/8$ | $3/4$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the first row both deviations are negative, so their product is positive. In the third row $X$ is above its mean but $Y$ is below its mean, so their product is negative. Weighting the last column gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一行中两个偏差都负，乘积为正；第三行中 $X$ 高于自己的均值，$Y$ 低于自己的均值，乘积为负。对最后一列加权，得到

$$\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=\frac18\frac{15}4+\frac18\frac34+\frac38\left(-\frac14\right)+\frac38\frac34=\frac34.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The positive answer describes an average tendency, not a promise that every row moves in the same direction. Expanding the definition gives a quicker computational identity:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正结果表达的是平均倾向，并不保证每一行都同向偏离。把定义里的乘积展开，还能得到更便于计算的恒等式：

$$\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=E[XY]-E[X]E[Y].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $E[XY]=0+0+(3/8)4+(3/8)8=9/2$, so $9/2-(3/4)5=3/4$ again. The subtraction removes the contribution from the two means; $E[XY]$ alone is not covariance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $E[XY]=0+0+(3/8)4+(3/8)8=9/2$，再减去 $(3/4)5$，仍得 $3/4$。减去均值乘积，是为了剔除均值本身的贡献；$E[XY]$ 单独并不是协方差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Units and what the number tells us
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*单位与数值的含义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Covariance has the product of the two variables' units: centimetres times kilograms for height and weight, for example. In the bus table, $X$ is a numerical label with no physical unit and $Y$ is in minutes, so the covariance is in minutes. Converting the wait to seconds multiplies it by $60$, changing the covariance from $3/4$ to $45$. More generally, for constants $a,b,c,d$,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

协方差的单位是两变量单位的乘积，例如身高用厘米、体重用公斤时，协方差单位是“厘米·公斤”。公交表中 $X$ 是没有物理单位的数值标签，$Y$ 用分钟，因而协方差单位是分钟。把等待时间换成秒，数值乘以 $60$，协方差也从 $3/4$ 变成 $45$。一般地，对常数 $a,b,c,d$，

$$\operatorname{Cov}(aX+c,bY+d)=ab\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Adding a constant changes a mean by the same amount, so it leaves centred deviations unchanged. Multiplication rescales them. Also $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=\operatorname{Cov}(Y,X)$, and $\operatorname{Cov}(X,X)=\operatorname{Var}(X)$: [[方差|variance]] is the same centred-product idea applied twice to one variable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

加上常数时，均值也增加同样的量，所以中心化后的偏差不变；乘以常数则会缩放偏差。此外，$\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=\operatorname{Cov}(Y,X)$，而 $\operatorname{Cov}(X,X)=\operatorname{Var}(X)$：[[方差]]就是对同一个变量使用两次中心化乘积。

> [!warning] A single summary of dependence · 依赖关系的一个摘要
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A zero covariance means the weighted centred products cancel. It need not mean that one variable tells us nothing about the other; the full square-function example is in [[零协方差不推独立|zero covariance does not imply independence]]. To compare linear co-movement without units, divide by the two standard deviations to obtain [[相关系数|Pearson correlation]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 协方差为零表示加权后的中心化乘积抵消了，不能据此说一个变量完全不提供另一个变量的信息；完整平方函数反例见[[零协方差不推独立]]。要消除单位后比较线性共同变化，可以除以两个标准差，得到[[相关系数|Pearson 相关系数]]。
