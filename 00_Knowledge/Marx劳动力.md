# Marx 所说的劳动力是人生产使用价值时能够运用的体力和脑力能力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*For Marx, labour-power is the aggregate of physical and mental capacities a person can exercise in producing use-values*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**劳动力（labour-power）**是活的人所具有、在生产使用价值时能够运用的体力和脑力能力；**劳动（labour）**是这种能力的实际发挥。劳动力指能力，劳动指活动，在分析雇佣关系时必须区分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Labour-power** is the aggregate of physical and mental capacities a living person can exercise in producing use-values; **labour** is their actual exercise. One is a capacity, the other an activity. The distinction is essential when analysing wage labour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，订立八小时用工契约时，劳动者在约定期间把劳动力交给购买者使用；随后实际进行的裁衣等[[具体劳动|生产活动]]，才是劳动。出售的时间范围和历史条件见[[劳动力商品条件]]。这里的劳动力按个人能力理解，与[[失业率|人口统计中就业者和失业者合计的 labour force]] 是不同对象。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, an eight-hour employment contract places the worker's labour-power at the buyer's disposal for the agreed period; the subsequent [[具体劳动|productive activity]], such as tailoring, is labour. See [[劳动力商品条件|the conditions for labour-power as a commodity]] for the temporal and historical conditions of its sale. Labour-power here concerns a person's capacity, distinct from [[失业率|the statistical labour force comprising employed and unemployed people]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

劳动力的价值与这种能力的实际使用结果也要分别考察。维持和再生产该能力需要生活资料，决定[[劳动力价值]]的分析从这里出发；能力被使用后，劳动才实际形成产品并创造新价值。这使劳动力的价值和工作日内形成的新价值成为两个可以分别计量的量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The value of labour-power and the result of using it must also be examined separately. Maintaining and reproducing the capacity requires means of subsistence, which is the starting point for analysing the [[劳动力价值|value of labour-power]]. When the capacity is exercised, labour produces goods and creates new value. The value of the capacity and new value created during the working day are therefore separately measurable quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
