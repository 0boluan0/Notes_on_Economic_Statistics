# Concavity and quasiconcavity
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*凹性与拟凹性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[凹凸性|Concavity]] and [[拟凹函数|quasiconcavity]] both limit how far a function's value can fall between two inputs, but they use different benchmarks. Concavity requires the value at a mixture to be at least the **weighted average** of the endpoint values. Quasiconcavity only requires it to be at least the **smaller** endpoint value. For endpoint values $2$ and $10$, the midpoint must reach $6$ under concavity, but only $2$ under quasiconcavity. Meeting the stronger requirement automatically meets the weaker one.

The input domain must be a [[凸集|convex set]]: mixing two allowed inputs must still give an allowed input. The mix $tx+(1-t)y$ uses a weight $t\in[0,1]$; at $t=1/2$ it is the midpoint. These are mixtures of **inputs**, before evaluating the function.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[凹凸性|凹性]]和[[拟凹函数|拟凹性]]都限制两个输入之间的函数值能低到哪里，但参照线不同：凹性要求不低于端点值的**加权平均**，拟凹性只要求不低于**较小的端点值**。端点值若为 $2$ 和 $10$，中点在凹性下至少要达到 $6$，在拟凹性下只需达到 $2$。较强条件成立，较弱条件就自动成立。

输入的定义域须是[[凸集]]，即两个允许的输入混合后仍被允许。$tx+(1-t)y$ 中 $t\in[0,1]$ 是混合权重，$t=1/2$ 给出中点；这里先混合的是输入，再计算函数值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why every concave function passes the threshold test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么凹函数一定通过门槛检验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Every concave function is quasiconcave, but the converse is false*

Let $f:U\to\mathbb R$ be concave on a convex domain $U$. Fix any real threshold $k$. Its [[上轮廓集|upper contour set]] contains the allowed inputs whose values are at least $k$. If $x$ and $y$ both belong to this set, concavity gives the first inequality below; their shared lower bound $k$ gives the second. Thus every mixture still passes the threshold. As the argument works for **every** $k$, all upper contour sets are convex, which is exactly quasiconcavity. Empty sets and sets with one point also satisfy convexity; they do not need separate exceptions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $f$ 在凸域凹，两端 $f(x),f(y)\ge k$ 时，
$$
f(tx+(1-t)y)\ge tf(x)+(1-t)f(y)\ge k,
$$
所以每个[[上轮廓集]]凸，$f$ [[拟凹函数|拟凹]]。取负号得到凸函数必拟凸。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Equivalently, the weighted average $tf(x)+(1-t)f(y)$ always lies between the two endpoint values, so the concavity lower bound implies the [[拟凹性线段判据|minimum-endpoint segment bound]]. Reversing the inequalities shows that every convex function is [[拟凸函数|quasiconvex]]: its value at a mixture is at most the endpoint average and hence at most the larger endpoint value.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这段推理中，$k$ 是任意实数：只要两个输入都达到门槛，整条线段就达到门槛，因而所有上轮廓集都是凸集。空集和只有一点的集合也符合凸集定义。换一种看法，加权平均一定不低于两个端点值的较小者，所以凹性直接给出[[拟凹性线段判据]]。反转不等号，同理得到凸性推出[[拟凸函数|拟凸性]]。

## A product that is quasiconcave but not concave
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个拟凹却不凹的乘积函数

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!example] The midpoint reaches the lower endpoint, but misses the average
> Consider $f(a,b)=ab$ with $a>0$ and $b>0$. At $p=(1,1)$ and $q=(3,3)$ the values are $1$ and $9$. The midpoint $(2,2)$ has value $4$. It passes the quasiconcave comparison $4\ge\min\{1,9\}=1$, but fails the concave comparison $4\ge(1+9)/2=5$. This single failure disproves concavity. To establish quasiconcavity, we still need a proof for all inputs, not just this pair.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 中点超过较低端点，却低于平均值
> 在 $a>0,b>0$ 上考虑 $f(a,b)=ab$。$p=(1,1)$ 与 $q=(3,3)$ 的函数值是 $1$、$9$，中点 $(2,2)$ 的值是 $4$。它通过拟凹性的比较 $4\ge\min\{1,9\}=1$，却通不过凹性的比较 $4\ge(1+9)/2=5$。这一对输入足以否定凹性；要肯定拟凹性，还须覆盖全部输入。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $k\le0$, every input in the positive quadrant passes the threshold, so the upper contour set is the whole convex domain. For $k>0$, the threshold condition $ab\ge k$ becomes $b\ge k/a$. Take two passing inputs $(a_1,b_1)$ and $(a_2,b_2)$. The reciprocal inequality below says that the weighted average of the two reciprocal values is at least the reciprocal at the mixed input:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{t}{a_1}+\frac{1-t}{a_2}-\frac{1}{ta_1+(1-t)a_2}
=\frac{t(1-t)(a_1-a_2)^2}{a_1a_2(ta_1+(1-t)a_2)}\ge0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since $b_i\ge k/a_i$, the mixture satisfies
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
tb_1+(1-t)b_2
\ge k\left(\frac{t}{a_1}+\frac{1-t}{a_2}\right)
\ge\frac{k}{ta_1+(1-t)a_2}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
It therefore passes the same threshold. This proves convexity for every upper contour set and establishes quasiconcavity on $(0,\infty)^2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$k\le0$ 时，正象限内全部输入都达到门槛，上轮廓集就是整个凸域。$k>0$ 时，$ab\ge k$ 等价于 $b\ge k/a$。上面第一个恒等式说明“倒数的加权平均”不小于“加权平均的倒数”；再代入 $b_i\ge k/a_i$，便得到混合后的输入仍满足乘积至少为 $k$。这覆盖了全部门槛与全部线段，才完成拟凹性的证明。

反向反例：$f(x,y)=xy$ 在 $(0,\infty)^2$ 的正门槛上轮廓集为 $y\ge k/x$，因 $k/x$ 凸而凸；非正门槛给整个域。但其 Hessian 为 $\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$，不定，所以不凹。此例不能脱离正象限条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The concavity inequality preserves every shared threshold, proving convexity of each [[上轮廓集|upper contour set]]. The product function on the positive quadrant disproves the converse: its upper sets are convex but its Hessian is indefinite. The domain restriction is essential.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[Hessian凹凸性判据|Hessian test]] is another way to reject concavity: for the displayed matrix, directions $(1,1)$ and $(1,-1)$ give quadratic-form values $2$ and $-2$. This is an [[不定二次型|indefinite quadratic form]], so it cannot be negative semidefinite. The numerical midpoint example already proves the failure without requiring matrix calculus.

> [!warning] Keep the positive-quadrant domain
> On all of $\mathbb R^2$, the same product is not even quasiconcave: $(-1,-1)$ and $(1,1)$ both give value $1$, but their midpoint $(0,0)$ gives $0$. The level-$1$ upper contour set is therefore not convex.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也可用[[Hessian凹凸性判据]]检验上面的矩阵：沿 $(1,1)$ 与 $(1,-1)$ 两个方向得到的二次型值分别为 $2$ 和 $-2$，所以是[[不定二次型]]，不能是半负定。前面的中点数值已经足以否定凹性，不必先掌握矩阵微积分。

> [!warning] 正象限不能省略
> 若把定义域改成整个 $\mathbb R^2$，$(-1,-1)$ 与 $(1,1)$ 的乘积均为 $1$，中点 $(0,0)$ 的乘积却为 $0$。门槛为 $1$ 的上轮廓集不凸，函数连拟凹都不满足。
