# 可回售债券赋予持有人按约定条件在到期前要求发行人买回债券的权利

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A puttable bond gives its holder the right to require the issuer to repurchase it before maturity under specified terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可回售债券（puttable / putable bond）把提前退出的合同选择交给持有人。回售日期、价格、通知方式和触发条件由合同规定；持有人可以选择不回售，而继续保留债券。它与[[可赎回债券]]的关键区别是由谁决定行权。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A puttable bond gives the holder a contractual early-exit choice. The contract determines dates, prices, notice requirements, and triggers; the holder may decline to exercise and keep the bond. The key distinction from a [[可赎回债券|callable bond]] is who controls exercise.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如持有人可在第三年付息后按每 100 面值回售给发行人，但其他日期只能按市场条件卖给别人。合同回售和二级市场出售是两种不同退出方式：前者依条款要求买回，后者还需要市场买方和可成交价格。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, a holder may put the bond to the issuer at 100 per 100 face value after the third-year coupon, while sales on other dates depend on market terms. Contractual repurchase and secondary-market sale are different exit routes: one invokes a contract, the other needs a market buyer and executable price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

回售增加一项偿付请求，但不自动消除发行人违约风险。发行人无力支付时，权利的存在不等于现金已到手；第三方担保或流动性支持若存在，也须另外识别其义务与信用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A put adds a payment claim without automatically eliminating issuer default risk. If the issuer cannot pay, the right is not equivalent to cash received. Any third-party guarantee or liquidity support requires separate identification of obligations and credit exposure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
