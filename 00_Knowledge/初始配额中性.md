# 固定初始配额在不改变边际激励与可行选择的竞争条件下不改变最终减排配置
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Fixed initial allowances leave the final abatement allocation unchanged under competitive conditions that preserve marginal incentives and feasible choices.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

初始配额中性说的是：在总配额不变的基准竞争模型中，重新分配固定、无条件的初始配额只改变企业的财富和净付款，不改变其减排激励。它不是“配额怎么发都没有后果”，更不是关于所有市场条件的无条件定理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Initial-allocation neutrality means that reallocating fixed, unconditional allowances while preserving the cap changes wealth and net payments without changing abatement incentives in a benchmark competitive model. It does not mean allocation has no consequences or that neutrality holds under every market condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个足够的基准是：企业在许可市场上接受价格、最小化成本；总预算和企业集合固定；配额可自由转让且没有阻碍调整的交易摩擦；完整履约；成本与可行选择不因财富、融资约束或其他监管待遇而变。初始配额不随当前或未来产量、排放、停产等行为更新。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A sufficient benchmark has price-taking cost minimisers, a fixed cap and firm set, freely transferable allowances without adjustment-blocking frictions, full compliance, and costs and feasible choices unaffected by wealth, finance or differential regulation. Initial allocations do not update with output, emissions or closure decisions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令减排为 $a_i$、固定基准排放为 $e_{0i}$、初始配额为 $l_i$。企业在给定价格 $p$ 下最小化
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a_i$ be abatement, $e_{0i}$ fixed baseline emissions and $l_i$ initial allowances. At a given price $p$, the firm minimises
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
C_i(a_i)+p(e_{0i}-a_i-l_i)
=[C_i(a_i)+p(e_{0i}-a_i)]-p l_i.
$$

最后一项与企业选择无关。重分配 $l_i$ 不改每家在给定价格下的最优排放需求；总配额也不变，所以市场出清的配置集合不变。严格凸等条件使配置唯一时，才可进一步说同一个最终配置不变。免费配额仍有出售价值，使用它排放并非没有机会成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last term is independent of the firm's choice. Reallocating $l_i$ leaves emissions demand at each price unchanged; unchanged total supply then leaves the equilibrium allocation set unchanged. Only when conditions such as strict convexity ensure uniqueness can one assert the same unique allocation. Free allowances retain a sale value, so using them has an opportunity cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[配额净交易与边际减排|两企业例]]中，同一个最终排放(25,75)可以对应初始配额(50,50)，也可以对应(10,90)；净交易从(−25,+25)变为(+15,−15)。配置不变而谁收款改变，[[转移支付核算|分配与资源成本]]应分别解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[配额净交易与边际减排|two-firm example]], final emissions $(25,75)$ can arise from endowments $(50,50)$ or $(10,90)$, changing net purchases from $(-25,+25)$ to $(+15,-15)$. The allocation remains unchanged while recipients of payments change; distinguish [[转移支付核算|distribution from resource costs]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若大企业能操纵许可价格、缺现金者因多获配额才能融资治理，或继续生产会换来下一期更多配额，上述常数项逻辑就不再足够。不同交易成本结构的影响也不同，不能反向宣称“只要有任何交易成本就一定不具中性”。免费配置与拍卖收入如何使用的总体福利比较，亦超出这一固定禀赋命题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The constant-term argument is insufficient if firms manipulate prices, extra allowances relax financing constraints, or continued production earns future allocations. Transaction-cost structures also differ; any positive transaction cost does not automatically prove non-neutrality. Comparing free allocation with auction revenue use lies beyond this fixed-endowment result.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [Montgomery (1972)，原刊扫描](https://www.mobt3ath.com/uplode/book/book-93471.pdf#page=14)，印刷 pp.408–409，Theorem 3.3及解释：支持固定初始许可价值与企业选择分离；本卡给出单一同质配额的简化推导。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Theorem 3.3 and its explanation separate fixed initial allowance value from firms' choices. The card derives the single-homogeneous-allowance version.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [Hahn and Stavins (2011)，作者原刊 PDF](https://stavins.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum8111/files/stavins/files/hahn_stavins_in_journal_of_law_economics.pdf#page=6)，印刷 pp.S271–S276，§3.1–3.4：核对交易成本结构、市场力量、流动性与行为依赖配置等边界；其研究不同免费配置的范围不被扩大为拍卖与免费配置完全等价。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Sections 3.1–3.4 examine transaction-cost structures, market power, liquidity and conditional allocations. The paper's scope across free allocations is not expanded into complete equivalence with auctions.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
