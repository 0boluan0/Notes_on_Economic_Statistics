# 可持续增长率是在不增减外部股权且经营效率和融资分配政策不变时可支持的销售增长率
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sustainable growth is the sales growth supported without external equity changes while operating efficiency and financing/distribution policies remain fixed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

可持续增长率（SGR）回答一个有条件的融资问题：如果不增发或回购股权，维持净利率、资产周转率、资本结构和利润留存率，靠利润留存带来的权益增长以及同比例增加的负债，销售可以按多大速度增长？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
SGR answers a conditional financing question: without issuing or repurchasing equity, and with unchanged net margin, asset turnover, capital structure, and retention, how fast can sales grow using retained income and proportionate additional liabilities?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

关键是**负债可以增加，但负债与权益的比例不变**。这与[[内含增长率]]仅靠留存补足净经营资产的条件不同。还需假设权益变化只有本期留存利润，没有另行注资、回购、其他综合收益或其他直接权益调整；净利率已包含对应融资成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Liabilities may increase, but their ratio to equity stays fixed. This differs from [[内含增长率|internal growth]] funded only by retention after spontaneous operating financing. Equity must change solely through current retained income, with no new contributions, repurchases, other comprehensive income, or other direct adjustments. The margin must include the associated financing cost.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如期初资产 100、负债 40、权益 60，本期留存增加权益 6。维持同样资本结构，需要负债增加 4、资产增加 10；资产周转率不变时，销售也增长 10%。为什么留存 6 能支持资产增加 10？因为保持比例的债务融资同时提供了另外 4。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose initial assets are 100, liabilities 40, and equity 60. Retention adds 6 to equity. Maintaining the capital structure requires 4 more liabilities and 10 more assets; unchanged asset turnover supports 10% sales growth. Retention supports more asset growth because proportionate debt provides the other 4.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体计算必须先辨明 ROE 用期初还是期末权益，见[[增长率与权益时点]]。用管理用报表时，也可用净经营资产周转率和[[净财务杠杆]]表达相同逻辑，前提是经营/金融分类一致，分子仍是归属于相同权益的净利润。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before calculating, distinguish beginning- from ending-equity ROE; see [[增长率与权益时点|equity timing in growth formulas]]. Management-use statements can express the same logic through net-operating-asset turnover and [[净财务杠杆|net financial leverage]], with consistent classifications and the same-scope net-income numerator.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“可持续”不表示保证有市场、银行一定放款、风险不变或增长必然增值。它是固定政策下的资金支持速度；经营可行性需另查，价值判断见[[增长不等于增值]]，政策变化造成的过渡年差异见[[实际增长与可持续增长]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Sustainable” does not guarantee demand, lending availability, constant risk, or value creation. It describes financing capacity under fixed policies. Operational feasibility is separate; see [[增长不等于增值|growth versus value creation]] and [[实际增长与可持续增长|transition-year growth differences]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
