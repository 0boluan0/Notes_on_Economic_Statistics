# 纳什均衡与混合策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Nash Equilibrium and Mixed Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原主题名称：纳什均衡、混合策略与寡头竞争
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Original topic name: Nash Equilibrium, Mixed Strategies, and Oligopoly Competition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 在每个人都正确回应别人选择时，找出没有玩家愿意单独改变的策略组合。

> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** Find strategy profiles in which everyone is responding optimally to everyone else, so no player wants to deviate alone.
> **Concrete anchor:** Matching Pennies has no pure-strategy intersection, but when both players randomize with equal probability, neither can raise expected payoff by changing alone.
> **Central difficulty:** A Nash equilibrium is a profile of mutual best responses, not a globally optimal outcome; in a mixed equilibrium, probabilities are pinned down by making the opponent indifferent.
> **Why it matters:** It is the basic predictive benchmark for static games and many models of industrial organization.

## 最佳反应与 Nash 均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Best Responses and Nash Equilibrium*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定对手策略，最佳反应最大化自己的收益。策略组合 $s^*$ 是 Nash 均衡，当每个 $s_i^*$ 都是对 $s_{-i}^*$ 的最佳反应。求有限博弈时可在收益矩阵标出最佳反应交点；连续行动则写各玩家一阶条件并检查二阶条件、边界和可行性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given the opponents' strategies, a best response maximizes a player's own payoff. A strategy profile $s^*$ is a Nash equilibrium when every $s_i^*$ is a best response to $s_{-i}^*$. In a finite game, mark each player's best responses in the payoff matrix and find their intersections. With continuous actions, derive each player's first-order condition and then check second-order conditions, boundaries, and feasibility.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Nash 均衡是一个“没有单边有利偏离”的局部稳定条件。它既不要求结果 Pareto 有效，也不要求玩家在联合改变策略后仍然不想改变；判断时必须逐个玩家固定其他人的策略，而不是比较所有结果的收益总和。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A Nash equilibrium is a local stability condition: no unilateral deviation is profitable. It requires neither Pareto efficiency nor robustness to coordinated deviations. Test it one player at a time while holding everyone else's strategy fixed, rather than comparing total payoffs across outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 纯策略求解步骤
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Procedure for Solving Pure-strategy Equilibria*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 明确每个格子的收益顺序，并逐列标出行玩家的最佳反应、逐行标出列玩家的最佳反应。
2. 同时被两方标出的格子是纯策略 Nash 候选。
3. 对每个候选直接比较所有单边偏离，处理并列最佳反应和边界行动。
4. 若没有交点，不表示不存在均衡；有限博弈仍可能存在混合策略均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
1. Fix the payoff ordering in every cell. Mark the row player's best responses column by column and the column player's best responses row by row.
2. Cells marked for both players are pure-strategy Nash candidates.
3. For each candidate, compare every unilateral deviation explicitly, including tied best responses and boundary actions.
4. No intersection does not mean that no equilibrium exists; a finite game may still have a mixed-strategy equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 混合策略
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mixed Strategies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

混合策略是对纯策略的概率分布，不是玩家“犹豫”。在内点混合均衡中，一个玩家只会给带正概率的纯策略相同的期望收益；实际用自己的混合概率让对手无差异。还要检查未进入支持集的策略收益不更高。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A mixed strategy is a probability distribution over pure strategies, not hesitation by the player. In an interior mixed equilibrium, every pure strategy used with positive probability must yield that player the same expected payoff. Operationally, choose your mixing probability so that the opponent is indifferent, and then verify that no strategy outside the support yields a higher payoff.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对于一个 $2\times2$ 博弈，设列玩家以概率 $q$ 选择左。先写行玩家两种纯策略的期望收益 $U_R(上\mid q)$ 与 $U_R(下\mid q)$，令二者相等求出 $q$；再用同样方法求行玩家概率 $p$。最后检查 $p,q\in[0,1]$，并确认支持集外不存在更优策略。注意：求出的 $q$ 是列玩家为了让行玩家无差异而采用的概率，不是行玩家自己的混合概率。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a $2\times2$ game, suppose the column player chooses Left with probability $q$. Write the row player's expected payoffs $U_R(Up\mid q)$ and $U_R(Down\mid q)$ and set them equal to solve for $q$; then solve analogously for the row player's probability $p$. Finally check that $p,q\in[0,1]$ and that no strategy outside the support is better. The probability $q$ is chosen by the column player to make the row player indifferent; it is not the row player's own mixing probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：Matching Pennies
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: Matching Pennies*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行玩家在双方相同时得 1、不同得 $-1$，列玩家收益相反。设列玩家以 $q$ 选正面，则行玩家选正面的期望收益为 $2q-1$，选反面为 $1-2q$；令二者相等得 $q=1/2$。对称地 $p=1/2$。没有纯策略均衡，但双方各以一半概率随机化构成唯一混合均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The row player receives 1 when the choices match and $-1$ otherwise; the column player's payoff is the opposite. If the column player chooses Heads with probability $q$, the row player's expected payoff from Heads is $2q-1$, while the payoff from Tails is $1-2q$. Indifference gives $q=1/2$, and symmetry gives $p=1/2$. There is no pure-strategy equilibrium, but both players randomizing equally is the unique mixed equilibrium.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 比较静态与失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Comparative Statics and Failure Diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参数变化应先改变收益，再看最佳反应集合怎样移动。若计算得到概率不在 $[0,1]$，通常说明猜错了支持集；若只令一方无差异，尚未构成均衡；若把“期望收益相等”误写成“所有结果收益相等”，则混淆了纯策略收益和给定对手随机化后的期望收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For comparative statics, change the payoffs first and then trace how the best-response sets move. A probability outside $[0,1]$ usually means the proposed support is wrong. Making only one player indifferent is not yet an equilibrium. Equating payoffs in every realized outcome confuses pure-outcome payoffs with expected payoffs against the opponent's randomization.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Minimal Self-check*

### 用自己的话定义 Nash 均衡。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Define Nash equilibrium in your own words.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 在别人策略固定时，每个人当前策略都是自己的最佳选择，因此没有人能靠单方面偏离提高收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Holding everyone else's strategy fixed, each player's current strategy is a best response, so nobody can gain through a unilateral deviation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 两策略完全混合均衡为何让对手无差异？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why must an opponent be indifferent between two strategies in a fully mixed equilibrium?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 若对手严格偏好其中一个纯策略，就不会给另一个正概率；只有期望收益相等时两者才能同时在支持集。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> If the opponent strictly preferred one pure strategy, the other would receive zero probability. Both can be in the support only when their expected payoffs are equal.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 求混合概率时，为什么用自己的概率让对手无差异？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When solving for mixing probabilities, why does your probability make the opponent indifferent?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 对手是否愿意混合取决于其各纯策略面对我的随机化时的期望收益；因此我的概率进入对手的无差异条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The opponent is willing to mix only if their pure strategies have equal expected payoffs against my randomization, so my probability enters the opponent's indifference condition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
