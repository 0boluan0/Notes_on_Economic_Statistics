# 公债到期本金的替代资金需求与利率变化的成本传导不是同一风险
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Replacement funding for maturing public debt and the transmission of rate changes into its cost are distinct risks.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从政府发行人的角度，[[公债再融资风险|再融资]]关注到期本金需要替代资金的时点；利率重定价关注利率变化何时传入债务融资成本。短期固定利率债在续借时通常同时面对两者；长期浮息债则可以在本金尚未到期时重置息票。二者有重叠，但不相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the government issuer, [[公债再融资风险|refinancing]] concerns replacement funding for maturing principal, while repricing concerns the transmission of rate changes into debt costs. Short-term fixed-rate debt commonly faces both at rollover. Long-term floating-rate debt can reset its coupon before principal matures. The exposures overlap but are not identical.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设三种债券本金均为 $100$、按年付息、无提前偿还或回售条款，第一年息票均为 $4$。第一年末，新发行固定债的利率变为 $7\%$；浮息债下一年的基准由 $3\%$ 变为 $6\%$，固定加点仍为 $1\%$。比较合同动作与下一年利息：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider three bonds with principal $100$, annual coupons, no early-redemption or put options, and a first-year coupon of $4$. At the end of year one, new fixed-rate issuance costs $7\%$. The floating benchmark for the next year changes from $3\%$ to $6\%$, with a fixed spread of $1\%$. Compare contractual actions and next-year interest:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 工具 | 第一年末本金或条款变化 | 第二年息票 |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 一年期固定债 | 本金 $100$ 到期；若全额续借，按新利率发行 | 续借成功且本金仍为 $100$ 时为 $7$ |
| 五年期浮息债 | 本金未到期；下一年息票率重置为 $6\%+1\%$ | $7$ |
| 五年期固定债 | 本金未到期；合同息票率仍为 $4\%$ | $4$ |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Instrument | End-of-year-one event | Year-two coupon |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| One-year fixed-rate bond | Principal $100$ matures; full renewal uses the new rate | $7$ if renewed successfully with principal $100$ |
| Five-year floating-rate bond | No principal maturity; next coupon resets to $6\%+1\%$ | $7$ |
| Five-year fixed-rate bond | No principal maturity; coupon rate stays $4\%$ | $4$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

浮息合同细节复用[[浮动利率债券]]，不变的息票约定复用[[固定息票债券]]。本例第一年息票仍为 $4$，重置影响第二年，不把新利率倒填到已经结束的计息期。一年期债是否能成功续借是额外条件，不能从新发行利率数字自动推出市场准入。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
See [[浮动利率债券|floating-rate bonds]] and [[固定息票债券|fixed-coupon bonds]] for the contractual definitions. The first-year coupon remains $4$; resetting affects year two rather than rewriting the completed accrual period. Successful rollover of the one-year bond is a separate condition, not implied by a quoted new-issue rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，延长平均到期期限不等于锁定利息支出，还要检查浮息或指数化条款和重置日程。长期固定债在本例近期不用续借且息票不变，也不代表其市场价格不变或不存在违约、汇率等其他风险；这里区分的是发行人的融资成本暴露，不是持有人债券价格对利率的敏感度。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Longer average maturity does not lock in interest costs; rate or indexation terms and reset schedules also matter. The long fixed-rate bond in this example avoids near-term rollover and coupon changes, but its market price and other risks can still change. The distinction concerns issuer funding-cost exposure, not investor bond-price sensitivity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
