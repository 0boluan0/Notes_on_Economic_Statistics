# Singular value decomposition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*奇异值分解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A matrix can mix several input coordinates into each output coordinate. A **singular value decomposition (SVD)** finds perpendicular input directions whose images are also perpendicular. Along each surviving direction, the matrix then only multiplies by a nonnegative stretch factor. Two changes of orthonormal coordinates make this simple action visible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

矩阵可能把多个输入坐标混合到每个输出中。**奇异值分解（SVD）**寻找一组相互垂直的输入方向，使它们的像也彼此垂直。沿每个保留下来的方向，矩阵只乘上一个非负伸缩倍数。两次标准正交坐标变换让这种简单作用显现出来。

> [!example] Two inputs, three outputs / 两个输入、三个输出
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Consider
> $$A=\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\0&1\\0&0\end{bmatrix}.$$
> It sends $(x_1,x_2)^T$ to $(3x_1,x_2,0)^T$: one input direction is stretched by 3, another by 1, and the third output coordinate stays zero. For input $(1,2)^T$, the output is $(3,2,0)^T$. The independent stretches are already visible here; a general matrix needs a change of directions. The superscript $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]], used to write columns.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 上述矩阵把 $(x_1,x_2)^T$ 送到 $(3x_1,x_2,0)^T$：第一输入方向伸长3倍，第二方向保持原长，第三输出坐标恒为零。例如 $(1,2)^T$ 变成 $(3,2,0)^T$。它已经显出独立伸缩，一般矩阵需要先换方向。$T$ 为[[矩阵转置|转置]]，这里用于写列向量。

## The factors and their dimensions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*三个因子与各自尺寸*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a real or complex $m\times n$ matrix $A$, with $m,n\ge1$, a **full SVD** is
$$A=U\Sigma V^*,\qquad
U\in\mathbb F^{m\times m},\quad
\Sigma\in\mathbb R^{m\times n},\quad
V\in\mathbb F^{n\times n}.$$
Here $\mathbb F$ is $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb C$. The star means conjugate transpose: transpose and replace $a+bi$ by $a-bi$, where $i^2=-1$. Over the reals it is ordinary transpose.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对实或复 $m\times n$ 矩阵 $A$，$m,n\ge1$，**完整 SVD** 如上。$\mathbb F$ 表示实数域 $\mathbb R$ 或复数域 $\mathbb C$。星号是共轭转置：转置后把 $a+bi$ 换成 $a-bi$，其中 $i^2=-1$；实数时就是普通转置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The columns of $U$ and $V$ are complete [[标准正交组|orthonormal bases]] of the output and input spaces: unit lengths and zero inner products between distinct columns. Thus these square factors are [[Unitary 矩阵|unitary]], or [[正交矩阵|orthogonal]] in the real case. They satisfy $U^*U=I_m$, $V^*V=I_n$, where $I_k$ is the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. They preserve length; the stretching is carried by $\Sigma$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$U,V$ 的列分别是输出、输入空间的完整[[标准正交组|标准正交基]]：长度为1，不同列内积为零。因此这两个方阵为[[Unitary 矩阵|酉矩阵]]，实数时为[[正交矩阵]]，满足 $U^*U=I_m$、$V^*V=I_n$，$I_k$ 是[[单位矩阵]]。它们保持长度，真正的伸缩由 $\Sigma$ 承担。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rectangular diagonal matrix $\Sigma$ is zero away from its main diagonal. Its $p=\min(m,n)$ diagonal positions hold the [[奇异值|singular values]]
$$\sigma_1\ge\cdots\ge\sigma_p\ge0.$$
A value greater than 1 stretches, a value between 0 and 1 shrinks, and zero erases a direction. In the example, choose $U=I_3$, $V=I_2$, $\Sigma=A$. For a $5\times3$ matrix, full $U,\Sigma,V$ have sizes $5\times5$, $5\times3$, $3\times3$; the right-side vectors live in the three-dimensional input space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

长方对角矩阵 $\Sigma$ 在主对角线外为零；$p=\min(m,n)$ 个对角位置放入上述非负降序[[奇异值]]。大于1表示拉伸，0与1之间表示缩短，零表示消去方向。例子中可取 $U=I_3$、$V=I_2$、$\Sigma=A$。对 $5\times3$ 矩阵，完整 $U,\Sigma,V$ 为 $5\times5$、$5\times3$、$3\times3$，右侧向量位于三维输入空间。

## Read coordinates, stretch, rebuild
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*读取坐标、伸缩、合成*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[矩阵乘法|Matrix multiplication]] acts from right to left:
$$Ax=U\bigl(\Sigma(V^*x)\bigr).$$
First $V^*x$ reads coordinates along the input basis. Next $\Sigma$ scales corresponding coordinates, erases zero directions and handles the different input/output dimensions. Finally $U$ combines the output basis vectors. The $i$th columns $v_i,u_i$ are the [[奇异向量|right and left singular vectors]]. For $1\le i\le p$,
$$Av_i=\sigma_i u_i.$$
Each positive value pairs a unit input direction with a unit output direction. If $n>m$, the remaining $n-p$ input basis vectors are also sent to zero, although there are no extra diagonal positions for them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[矩阵乘法]]从右向左作用：$V^*x$ 先读取输入基坐标，$\Sigma$ 再伸缩对应坐标、消去零方向并处理输入输出尺寸差异，最后 $U$ 组合输出基。第 $i$ 列 $v_i,u_i$ 是[[奇异向量|右、左奇异向量]]，对 $1\le i\le p$ 有 $Av_i=\sigma_i u_i$。每个正值配对一条单位输入与单位输出方向。若 $n>m$，其余 $n-p$ 条输入基也被送到零，但没有额外对角位置。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Every finite real or complex matrix admits this representation; the [[SVD存在性|existence theorem]] supplies that guarantee. Keeping all positive directions gives the exact [[紧SVD|compact SVD]]. Discarding some positive directions instead gives a different matrix, a [[截断SVD|truncated approximation]]. The two bases are generally different, so SVD is not the same operation as [[SVD与特征分解|eigendecomposition]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

每个有限维实或复矩阵都能这样表示，保证来自 [[SVD存在性|存在定理]]。保留全部正方向得到仍精确的[[紧SVD]]；若再舍去一些正方向，就得到另一个矩阵，即[[截断SVD|截断近似]]。两侧基通常不同，所以 SVD 不等于[[SVD与特征分解|特征分解]]。
