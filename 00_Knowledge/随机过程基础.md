# 随机过程基础
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Foundations of stochastic processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把“随时间变化的不确定对象”表示为一族相互依赖的随机变量，并先分清时间、状态和观测机制。
> **具体锚点：** 每天温度、电话累计到达数、机器运行状态和资产价格都按时间记录，但分别需要连续状态、计数、跳转链或连续路径模型。
> **核心难点：** 一条观测路径不是过程的全部分布；相同边际分布也可能对应完全不同的时间依赖。
> **为什么重要：** Markov 链、Poisson 过程、平稳时间序列和 Brownian motion 都只是这套框架中的不同假设组合。
> **继续：** 若关注时间平移后的统计规律，进入 [[平稳性、遍历性与谱]]；具体过程见 [[泊松过程]]、[[离散时间马尔可夫链]]、[[连续时间马尔可夫链]] 与 [[布朗运动]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It represents an uncertain object evolving over time as a family of dependent random variables and begins by separating time, state, and observation mechanisms.
> **Concrete anchor:** Daily temperature, cumulative telephone arrivals, machine operating states, and asset prices are all time-indexed, yet they call for continuous-state, counting, jump-chain, or continuous-path models respectively.
> **Central difficulty:** One observed path is not the full distribution of a process. Processes with identical marginal distributions may have completely different temporal dependence.
> **Why it matters:** Markov chains, Poisson processes, stationary time series, and Brownian motion are different combinations of assumptions within this common framework.
> **Continue with:** For invariance under time shifts, go to [[平稳性、遍历性与谱|stationarity, ergodicity, and spectral analysis]]. For particular process families, see [[泊松过程|Poisson processes]], [[离散时间马尔可夫链|discrete-time Markov chains]], [[连续时间马尔可夫链|continuous-time Markov chains]], and [[布朗运动|Brownian motion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 随机过程的对象
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The object described by a stochastic process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

随机过程 $\{X_t:t\in T\}$ 是一族随机变量，也可看成每个样本点对应一条函数路径。时间指标可离散或连续，状态空间也可离散或连续。有限维分布共同决定过程的概率规律；均值和协方差只给二阶摘要。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A stochastic process $\{X_t:t\in T\}$ is a family of random variables. Equivalently, every outcome determines a function-valued sample path. The time index may be discrete or continuous, and so may the state space. The collection of finite-dimensional distributions determines the probabilistic law of the process; the mean and covariance provide only second-order summaries.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“固定 t 看随机性”和“固定样本点看路径”是两种互补视角。前者产生随机变量 $X_t$ 的分布；后者产生 $t\mapsto X_t(\omega)$ 的函数。连续时间过程是否有连续路径、跳跃路径或几乎处处不可导路径，是过程分布之外还需明确的正则性问题。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There are two complementary views: fix $t$ and examine randomness across outcomes, or fix an outcome and examine its path over time. The first produces the distribution of $X_t$; the second produces the function $t\mapsto X_t(\omega)$. For continuous-time processes, continuity, jumps, and almost-sure non-differentiability are additional path-regularity properties that must be stated explicitly.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 有限维分布与依赖
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Finite-dimensional distributions and dependence*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意 $t_1,\ldots,t_k$，向量 $(X_{t_1},\ldots,X_{t_k})$ 的联合分布描述这些时点如何共同变化。只给每个 $X_t$ 的边际分布不够：令所有时点共享同一个随机变量，与令各时点独立抽样，可以有相同边际却有截然不同的路径和预测性质。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any $t_1,\ldots,t_k$, the joint distribution of $(X_{t_1},\ldots,X_{t_k})$ describes how those times vary together. Marginal distributions alone are insufficient. A process that repeats the same random variable at every time and a process that redraws independently at every time may have identical marginals but radically different paths and predictive behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在一致性条件下，全部有限维分布确定过程的概率律。实际建模通常用更紧凑的结构指定它们：独立增量、Markov 性、Gaussian 性、平稳性，或一个带随机创新的递推方程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subject to consistency conditions, all finite-dimensional distributions determine the law of a process. In practice, they are specified more compactly through structure such as independent increments, the Markov property, Gaussianity, stationarity, or a recursive equation driven by random innovations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 过程分类的四个问题
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Four questions for classifying a process*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一，时间集合 T 是离散还是连续；第二，状态空间是有限、可数还是连续；第三，路径是累计计数、分段常数、连续还是更一般；第四，历史以何种方式影响未来。四个问题比按课程章节背模型名称更能排除不适用模型。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First ask whether the time set $T$ is discrete or continuous. Second, ask whether the state space is finite, countable, or continuous. Third, determine whether paths are cumulative counts, piecewise constant, continuous, or more general. Fourth, specify how the past affects the future. These questions eliminate inappropriate models more effectively than memorising model names by chapter.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：同一数据形状，不同过程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: similar-looking data, different processes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

某服务器每分钟记录一次请求数。若记录的是“这一分钟新增请求”，可以用计数变量序列；若记录的是“截至当前累计请求”，路径必须单调不减；若只记录“空闲/繁忙”，状态空间变成二元。三份数据都可能画成时间序列，但状态定义决定转移、增量和似然完全不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose a server records a value every minute. If the value is the number of new requests during that minute, it is a sequence of count variables. If it is the cumulative number of requests so far, the path must be non-decreasing. If it records only idle or busy, the state space is binary. All three datasets can be plotted as time series, but the state definition changes the transitions, increments, and likelihood completely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 建模顺序
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Modelling order*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先明确时间/状态空间和观测机制，再判断要描述到达、状态转移、连续噪声还是平稳信号。不要因为数据按时间排列就自动套用某一过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First specify the time and state spaces and the observation mechanism. Then decide whether the target is arrivals, state transitions, continuous noise, or a stationary signal. Data arranged by time do not automatically justify any particular process.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

进一步写出条件问题：给定过去，未来还依赖什么？若只依赖当前状态，Markov 模型可能合适；若不相交增量独立且发生率稳定，Poisson 过程可能合适；若只关心二阶平移不变结构，平稳过程模型可能合适。每个“可能”都需要诊断，而不是由名称确认。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Next write the conditional question explicitly: given the past, what does the future still depend on? If only the current state matters, a Markov model may be appropriate. If disjoint increments are independent and the event rate is stable, a Poisson process may be appropriate. If only second-order shift-invariant structure matters, a stationary-process model may be appropriate. Each “may” requires diagnosis rather than confirmation by terminology.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 一组相同的边际分布能否唯一确定随机过程？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does a collection of identical marginal distributions uniquely determine a stochastic process?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不能。还需要不同时点之间的联合分布；相同边际可以配上完全不同的时间依赖。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> No. Joint distributions across times are also needed; the same marginals can be combined with entirely different temporal dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 样本路径和 $X_t$ 的分布分别固定了什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is fixed when examining a sample path, and what is fixed when examining the distribution of $X_t$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 样本路径固定随机结果而让时间变化；$X_t$ 的分布固定时点而比较不同随机结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A sample path fixes the random outcome and lets time vary. The distribution of $X_t$ fixes the time and compares different random outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么“数据按时间排列”不足以选择 ARMA？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why is ordering data by time insufficient to justify an ARMA model?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 还需检查状态定义、平稳性、相关结构、观测机制和模型用途；到达计数、状态跳转和结构突变都可能需要其他过程。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The state definition, stationarity, dependence, observation mechanism, and purpose must also be checked. Arrival counts, state transitions, and structural breaks may require different processes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
