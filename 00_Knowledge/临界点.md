# Critical points
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*临界点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **critical point** is an interior input that deserves closer inspection when searching for peaks and valleys: the graph has a zero derivative there, or has no derivative there. It is a candidate, not a verdict. A flat tangent need not mark a peak: $f(x)=x^3$ keeps increasing through $0$. A sharp corner can mark a minimum: $f(x)=|x|$ reaches its smallest value at $0$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**临界点**是在寻找峰谷时需要进一步检查的内部输入位置：函数在这里的导数等于零，或者导数不存在。它是候选，不是判决。水平切线未必对应峰顶：$f(x)=x^3$ 穿过 $0$ 后仍继续增大；尖角却可以对应谷底：$f(x)=|x|$ 在 $0$ 取得最小值。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Which inputs count?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先确认输入合法，而且是内点*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f:D\to\mathbb R$ be a real-valued function of one real variable, where $D\subseteq\mathbb R$ is its domain, the set of allowed inputs. An [[集合内部|interior point]] $c$ here means interior **in the real line**: some $r>0$ satisfies $(c-r,c+r)\subseteq D$. Both small positive and small negative moves are then allowed. The [[导数|derivative]] $f'(c)$ is the finite limit of the difference quotient $[f(c+h)-f(c)]/h$ as $h\to0$, where $h$ is a nonzero input increment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f:D\to\mathbb R$ 是一元实值函数，$D\subseteq\mathbb R$ 是定义域，也就是允许输入的集合。这里的[[集合内部|内点]] $c$ 指相对于**整条实数线**的内点：存在 $r>0$，使 $(c-r,c+r)\subseteq D$，因此向正、负两个方向的小移动都合法。[[导数]] $f'(c)$ 是差商 $[f(c+h)-f(c)]/h$ 在 $h\to0$ 时的有限极限；$h$ 表示一个非零输入增量。

> [!important] The convention used here · 本卡的约定
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An interior point $c\in\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R}D$ is **critical** if $f'(c)=0$ or the finite derivative $f'(c)$ does not exist. The input $c$ is the critical point; $f(c)$ is its corresponding output, called a critical value under this convention. The graph point is the pair $(c,f(c))$. Endpoints are classified separately.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若内点 $c\in\operatorname{int}_{\mathbb R}D$ 满足 $f'(c)=0$，或者有限导数 $f'(c)$ 不存在，就称 $c$ 为**临界点**。输入 $c$ 是临界点；对应输出 $f(c)$ 在这一约定下称为临界值；图像上的点则是有序对 $(c,f(c))$。端点另列。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When the derivative exists and equals zero, the point is [[驻点|stationary]]. Thus stationary interior points are one kind of critical point, while corners and other failures of differentiability supply another kind. A divergent difference quotient is not a finite derivative equal to zero. This definition does not assume that $f$ is continuous at every candidate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

导数存在且为零时，该点是[[驻点]]。因此，内部驻点是临界点的一类，尖角等不可导点则是另一类。差商发散不能算作“导数等于零”。这个定义也没有假设函数在每个候选处连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why candidates are not classifications
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*导数为零与不可导都只给候选*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x^3$, $f'(0)=0$, but every small $h>0$ gives $f(-h)<f(0)<f(h)$. There are both lower and higher nearby values, so zero is neither a [[局部极值与绝对极值|local maximum nor a local minimum]]. For $g(x)=|x|$, the difference quotient at zero is $1$ for $h>0$ and $-1$ for $h<0$, so its derivative does not exist; nevertheless $g(x)\ge g(0)=0$ for every real $x$. Zero is both a local and an absolute minimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $f(x)=x^3$，虽然 $f'(0)=0$，但任意小的 $h>0$ 都满足 $f(-h)<f(0)<f(h)$，附近既有更低值又有更高值，所以零点既不是[[局部极值与绝对极值|局部最大点，也不是局部最小点]]。对 $g(x)=|x|$，零点的差商在 $h>0$ 时为 $1$，在 $h<0$ 时为 $-1$，因此不可导；但每个实数 $x$ 都满足 $g(x)\ge g(0)=0$，所以零点同时是局部和绝对最小点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Nondifferentiability alone is not a verdict either. The function equal to $x$ for $x\le0$ and $2x$ for $x>0$ has left and right slopes $1$ and $2$ at zero. It is critical there but takes negative values just to the left and positive values just to the right, so there is no extremum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不可导本身也不能决定类型。令函数在 $x\le0$ 时等于 $x$，在 $x>0$ 时等于 $2x$；零点的左、右斜率分别为 $1$、$2$。零点是临界点，却因左侧附近有负值、右侧附近有正值而不是极值点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why an interior extremum must be a candidate
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*内部极值为什么不会漏出候选范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $c$ is an interior local maximum. For sufficiently small $h>0$, the difference quotient is at most zero; for sufficiently small $h<0$, its numerator is still nonpositive but its denominator changes sign, so the quotient is at least zero. If a finite two-sided derivative exists, its common limit must therefore be both $\le0$ and $\ge0$, hence zero. At a local minimum the inequalities reverse and give the same conclusion.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $c$ 是内部局部最大点。对足够小的 $h>0$，差商不大于零；对足够小的 $h<0$，分子仍不大于零，但分母改变符号，因此差商不小于零。若有限双侧导数存在，共同极限就必须同时满足 $\le0$ 和 $\ge0$，从而等于零。局部最小点的两个不等式反向，结论相同。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This is the one-variable [[无约束一阶条件|interior first-order necessary condition]]. If the derivative does not exist, the point is already covered by the other part of the critical-point definition. Hence every interior local extremum is critical under this convention. The examples above show why the converse fails.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这就是一元情形的[[无约束一阶条件|内部一阶必要条件]]。若导数不存在，该点已被临界点定义的另一部分涵盖。因此，在本约定下，每个内部局部极值点都是临界点；上面的例子说明反向推论不成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Endpoints and missing inputs need separate treatment
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*端点与定义域外的点不能混入*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $f(x)=x$ on $[0,1]$, the minimum is attained at $0$ and the maximum at $1$. Neither endpoint is interior in $\mathbb R$, so neither is a critical point under this definition. The allowed one-sided slopes equal $1$, not zero. They are still essential candidates in the [[闭区间极值法|closed-interval method]]. In contrast, $0$ is not even an allowed input for $1/x$ and is not a critical point or an attained extremum; it is a boundary where one may investigate limits.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对定义在 $[0,1]$ 上的 $f(x)=x$，最小值在 $0$ 取得，最大值在 $1$ 取得。两个端点都不是相对于 $\mathbb R$ 的内点，因此按本定义都不是临界点；合法的单侧斜率为 $1$，也不为零。但[[闭区间极值法]]仍必须检查它们。相比之下，$0$ 根本不是 $1/x$ 的合法输入，既不是临界点，也不是取得极值的位置，只是可以考察极限的定义域边界。

> [!note] Read the terminology before the test · 先对齐术语约定
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Some courses use “critical” only for zero derivatives; others include nondifferentiable inputs or label endpoints differently. This card keeps interior zero-or-undefined derivatives together and endpoints separate. The mathematical search must still include every relevant kind of candidate, whatever the labels. A [[一阶导数检验|first-derivative test]] or another justified comparison is needed to decide what a candidate actually is.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 有些课程只把零导数点称为 critical；另一些还包含不可导输入，或对端点采用不同叫法。本卡把内部零导数点与不可导点合称临界点，端点另列。无论名称怎样，数学上都不能漏查相关类型的候选。要判断某个候选究竟是什么，还需[[一阶导数检验]]或其他有依据的比较。
