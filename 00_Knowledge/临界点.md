# 临界点是函数定义域内部导数为零或不存在的点
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A critical point is an interior domain point where the derivative is zero or does not exist*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 对定义域为 $D$ 的一元函数 $f$，本卡将临界点定义为满足下列条件的内点 $c\in\operatorname{int}D$：$f'(c)=0$，或者 $f'(c)$ 不存在。输入 $c$ 是临界点，对应输出 $f(c)$ 是临界值。端点另列；临界点只负责列候选，不负责判定候选的类型。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a one-variable function on $D$, this card calls an interior point $c\in\operatorname{int}D$ critical when $f'(c)=0$ or the derivative does not exist. The input $c$ is the critical point and the output $f(c)$ its critical value. Endpoints are listed separately. Critical points generate candidates; they do not classify them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$f(x)=x^3$ 在 $0$ 有临界点，因为 $f'(0)=0$，但它没有局部极值；$f(x)=|x|$ 在 $0$ 有临界点，因为导数不存在，并在那里取得最小值。两例一起说明：只解 $f'=0$ 会漏候选，而找到临界点也不能宣布找到极值。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x^3$, zero is critical because the derivative vanishes, yet it is not an extremum. For $|x|$, zero is critical because the derivative fails to exist, and it is a minimum. Thus solving only $f'=0$ can miss candidates, while finding a critical point does not establish an extremum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定义域之外的断点不是临界点。例如 $1/x$ 的 $x=0$ 不属于定义域；它会把分析区间分开，却不是可以代入比较的函数点。端点是否称为临界点因教材约定而异，本库把它们在 [[闭区间极值法]] 中单列，避免名称差异掩盖必须检查的候选。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A break outside the domain is not a critical point. Zero separates the domain of $1/x$ but is not a function point that can be evaluated. Textbooks differ on whether endpoints are called critical; this vault lists endpoints separately in the closed-interval method so the terminology cannot hide a required candidate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么 $f'(c)=0$ 既不是局部极值的充分条件，也不是所有极值的必要条件？
>
> **答案：** $x^3$ 给出零导数但无极值；端点极值和像 $|x|$ 的不可导极值不必满足零导数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Check:** Why is $f'(c)=0$ neither sufficient for a local extremum nor necessary for every extremum?
>
> **Answer:** The function $x^3$ has a zero derivative at zero but no extremum there. Endpoint extrema and nondifferentiable extrema such as that of $|x|$ need not satisfy a zero-derivative condition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
