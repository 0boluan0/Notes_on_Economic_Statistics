# 证券工具与现金流权利
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Securities and Cash-Flow Rights*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 识别股票、债券、基金和衍生品究竟承诺什么现金流、赋予什么控制权，以及投资者承担什么状态依赖的损益。
> **具体锚点：** 公司资产价值从 120 降到 80、债务面值为 100 时，债权人优先取得 80、股东剩余为零；上涨到 160 时，债权人通常仍只得 100、股东取得剩余 60。
> **核心难点：** 不能把工具只按“风险高低”排列；优先级、期限、担保、嵌入期权、杠杆和交易对手会共同改变损益形状。
> **为什么重要：** 只有先画出权利和现金流，估值、收益计算、对冲与风险归因才不会用错模型。
> **继续：** 交易怎样发生见 [[证券市场、订单与结算]]；固定收益定价见 [[债券定价、收益率与信用利差]]，权益定价见 [[股票与企业价值评估]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It identifies what cash flows and control rights stocks, bonds, funds, and derivatives actually promise and what state-dependent gains and losses investors bear.
> **Concrete anchor:** If firm asset value falls from 120 to 80 while debt face value is 100, creditors have priority over the 80 and shareholders receive zero. If asset value rises to 160, creditors usually still receive only 100 and shareholders receive the residual 60.
> **Central difficulty:** Instruments cannot be placed on one simple scale from low to high risk; seniority, maturity, collateral, embedded options, leverage, and counterparties jointly determine the payoff shape.
> **Why it matters:** Valuation, return measurement, hedging, and risk attribution use the correct model only after rights and cash flows have been mapped.
> **Continue:** For how trading occurs, see [[证券市场、订单与结算|Securities Markets, Orders, and Settlement]]. For fixed-income pricing, see [[债券定价、收益率与信用利差|Bond Pricing, Yields, and Credit Spreads]]; for equity pricing, see [[股票与企业价值评估|Equity and Enterprise Valuation]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 先画状态—现金流图
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Draw the state–cash-flow map first*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别工具时依次问：谁必须向谁支付、支付金额由什么状态决定、谁先得到支付、持有人有没有选择权、亏损是否有限、是否需要追加保证金。工具名称相同而条款不同，经济暴露也可能完全不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When identifying an instrument, ask in order: who must pay whom, which state determines the amount, who is paid first, whether the holder has an option, whether losses are limited, and whether margin can be called. Instruments with the same name but different terms can create entirely different economic exposures.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 股票、债券、基金与衍生品
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stocks, bonds, funds, and derivatives*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

普通股有剩余现金流和治理权，优先股介于股债，债券按优先级、担保、票息和期限规定合同现金流。基金把资产池份额化。远期、期货、期权和互换的价值来自标的与合同条款，常用于转移风险也能加杠杆。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common stock carries residual cash-flow and governance rights; preferred stock lies between debt and common equity; bonds specify contractual cash flows through seniority, collateral, coupons, and maturity. Funds divide a pooled portfolio into units. Forwards, futures, options, and swaps derive value from an underlying and contractual terms; they can transfer risk and can also create leverage.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 股权与债权：优先级和剩余索取
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Equity and debt: priority and residual claims*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把企业到期资产价值记为 $V_T$、零息债面值记为 $F$。忽略破产成本时，债权人得到 $\min(V_T,F)$，股东得到 $\max(V_T-F,0)$。因此股权的到期损益形状类似对企业资产的看涨期权；杠杆提高会把同一资产波动集中到更薄的股权上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let firm asset value at maturity be $V_T$ and zero-coupon debt face value be $F$. Ignoring bankruptcy costs, creditors receive $\min(V_T,F)$ and shareholders receive $\max(V_T-F,0)$. Equity therefore has a payoff shape similar to a call option on firm assets; leverage concentrates the same asset volatility in a thinner equity layer.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债券也不是一种统一现金流：固定或浮动票息、次级或优先、担保或无担保、可赎回、可回售、可转换都会改变利率敏感度、信用损失与可选性。看到“债券”二字时，至少读到现金流表和优先级。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A bond is not one uniform cash flow either. Fixed versus floating coupons, senior versus subordinated status, secured versus unsecured claims, and callable, puttable, or convertible terms all change rate sensitivity, credit loss, and optionality. When you see the word bond, read at least as far as the cash-flow schedule and seniority.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 基金：包装不等于底层风险消失
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Funds: packaging does not eliminate underlying risk*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开放式基金通常按净值申购赎回，交易所交易基金在二级市场交易并依靠申赎机制连接市价与净值，封闭式基金份额数量较固定且可能长期折溢价。基金提供分散、治理和运营便利，但底层资产风险、费用、流动性错配与跟踪误差仍由持有人承担。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Open-end funds normally issue and redeem at net asset value; exchange-traded funds trade in secondary markets and use creation–redemption mechanisms to link market price to NAV; closed-end funds have relatively fixed shares and may trade at persistent discounts or premiums. Funds provide diversification, governance, and operational convenience, but holders still bear underlying-asset risk, fees, liquidity mismatch, and tracking error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 衍生品：权利、义务与杠杆
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Derivatives: rights, obligations, and leverage*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

远期和期货让双方在未来按约定价格交易，通常双方都有履约义务；看涨期权买方有权但无义务按执行价买入，到期价值为 $\max(S_T-K,0)$，买方最大初始损失通常是期权费；互换交换一组现金流。名义本金很大不等于当前价值同样大，但小额保证金也不等于最大风险小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Forwards and futures commit both sides to transact later at an agreed price. A call-option buyer has the right but not the obligation to buy at the strike, with maturity value $\max(S_T-K,0)$, and the buyer's maximum initial loss is normally the premium. A swap exchanges sets of cash flows. A large notional amount does not mean equally large current value, while a small margin deposit does not mean small maximum risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 收益与风险口径
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Return and risk conventions*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

持有期收益包括价格变化、息票/分红和汇率。算术平均适合单期预期，几何平均描述复合增长。波动率只衡量分布离散，不涵盖流动性、信用和尾部全部风险。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Holding-period return includes price change, coupons or dividends, and currency movement. The arithmetic mean is appropriate for a one-period expectation, while the geometric mean describes compound growth. Volatility measures dispersion only; it does not capture all liquidity, credit, and tail risk.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个从 100 涨到 150、再跌回 100 的资产，两期收益分别为 50% 和 −33.33%。算术平均约 8.33%，但两期几何平均为 0%，与财富从 100 回到 100 一致。跨期财富问题不能用算术平均替代复合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An asset that rises from 100 to 150 and then falls back to 100 earns 50% and −33.33% in the two periods. The arithmetic mean is about 8.33%, but the two-period geometric mean is 0%, consistent with wealth returning from 100 to 100. Arithmetic averaging cannot replace compounding in multi-period wealth questions.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 用“债券较安全”替代对优先级、担保、期限和违约状态的检查。
- 把买入期权的有限损失误推到期权卖方；卖方损失形状可能高度不对称。
- 只用名义本金比较衍生品规模，而不看当前重置价值、潜在未来敞口和保证金。
- 把基金历史波动当作基金结构本身的风险，忽略底层资产与赎回条款。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Replacing analysis of seniority, collateral, maturity, and default states with the slogan that bonds are safer.
- Extending the option buyer's limited loss to the option writer, whose losses can be highly asymmetric.
- Comparing derivatives only by notional amount without examining replacement value, potential future exposure, and margin.
- Treating a fund's historical volatility as structural fund risk while ignoring underlying assets and redemption terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么股权可以看成企业资产上的剩余索取权？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why can equity be viewed as a residual claim on firm assets?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 合同债权和其他优先权先被满足，资产价值超过这些固定或优先承诺的部分才归股东；若资产不足，有限责任使普通股最低降到零。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Contractual debt and other senior claims are paid first; only asset value above those fixed or prior commitments belongs to shareholders. If assets are insufficient, limited liability floors common equity at zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 波动率低是否说明证券风险低？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Does low volatility imply that a security has low risk?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 不一定。信用跳跃、流动性枯竭、尾部损失和杠杆可能未被日常波动充分反映。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Not necessarily. Credit jumps, disappearing liquidity, tail losses, and leverage may not be adequately reflected in ordinary daily volatility.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么期权费很小不能推出期权头寸风险很小？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why does a small option premium not imply that the option position has little risk?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 期权费是当前价格；损益由标的、执行价、期限和买卖方向决定。买方损失通常限于期权费，裸卖方在不利状态的损失可远大于收到的期权费。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The premium is the current price; payoff depends on the underlying, strike, maturity, and whether the option is bought or written. A buyer's loss is normally limited to the premium, while an uncovered writer can lose far more than the premium received.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
