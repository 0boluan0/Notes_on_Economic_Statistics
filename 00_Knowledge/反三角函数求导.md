# Derivatives of inverse trigonometric functions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*反三角函数求导*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An inverse trigonometric function recovers an angle from a sine, cosine or tangent value. For example, $\arcsin(1/2)=\pi/6$: an angle of $\pi/6$ radians has sine $1/2$. Its derivative answers a different question: **how sensitive is the recovered angle to a small change in the input value?**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

反三角函数根据正弦、余弦或正切的数值反求角度。例如 $\arcsin(1/2)=\pi/6$：$\pi/6$ 弧度的角，其正弦值为 $1/2$。它的导数回答另一个问题：**输入数值稍微改变时，反求出的角度有多敏感？**

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choose one angle before differentiating
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*求导前先确定返回哪一个角*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Many angles have the same sine, so “find an angle” alone does not define a function. A principal branch chooses one interval on which the original trigonometric function is one-to-one. We use the standard choices below, with all returned angles in radians. Here $\arcsin x$ is also written $\sin^{-1}x$; that notation means an inverse function, not the reciprocal $1/\sin x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

许多角有相同的正弦值，所以仅说“找一个角”还不能定义函数。主值分支会选定一个区间，使原来的三角函数在该区间上一一对应。下表使用标准选择，返回的角都以弧度计。$\arcsin x$ 也记作 $\sin^{-1}x$；这里表示反函数，不是倒数 $1/\sin x$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
| Inverse function | Allowed input | Returned angle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $\arcsin x$ | $[-1,1]$ | $[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$ |
| $\arccos x$ | $[-1,1]$ | $[0,\pi]$ |
| $\arctan x$ | $\mathbb R$ | $(-\pi/2,\pi/2)$ |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[反函数导数|inverse-function derivative rule]] applies where the original function has a nonzero derivative on the chosen branch: the inverse slope is the reciprocal of that original slope at the corresponding angle. [[正弦余弦求导|The sine and cosine derivative formulas]] supply those slopes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[反函数导数|反函数导数公式]]在原函数沿所选分支的导数非零处适用：反函数的斜率，等于对应角处原函数斜率的倒数。[[正弦余弦求导]]给出这里需要的原函数斜率。

> [!abstract] Formulas and their domains · 公式及其适用域
> $$
> \frac{d}{dx}\arcsin x=\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}},\qquad -1<x<1,
> $$
> $$
> \frac{d}{dx}\arccos x=-\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}},\qquad -1<x<1,
> $$
> $$
> \frac{d}{dx}\arctan x=\frac1{1+x^2},\qquad x\in\mathbb R.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The input domains of $\arcsin$ and $\arccos$ include $\pm1$, but their finite derivative formulas apply only in the interior. The negative sign for $\arccos$ reflects its decreasing principal branch.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $\arcsin$、$\arccos$ 的输入定义域包含 $\pm1$，但有限导数公式只适用于内部。$\arccos$ 的负号对应它的主值分支随输入递减。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Recover the signs from the branches
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*从分支确定符号*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $y=\arcsin x$ with $-1<x<1$, then $\sin y=x$ and $-\pi/2<y<\pi/2$. In this interval $\cos y>0$, so $\cos y=\sqrt{1-x^2}$. The inverse rule gives $y'=1/\cos y=1/\sqrt{1-x^2}$. Choosing the positive square root is justified by the branch, not by an arbitrary algebraic choice.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $y=\arcsin x$ 且 $-1<x<1$，则 $\sin y=x$、$-\pi/2<y<\pi/2$。在这个区间内 $\cos y>0$，因此 $\cos y=\sqrt{1-x^2}$。反函数公式给出 $y'=1/\cos y=1/\sqrt{1-x^2}$。选择正平方根是由分支保证的，不是随意的代数选择。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $y=\arccos x$, the interior branch is $0<y<\pi$. Now $\sin y>0$ and $\sin y=\sqrt{1-x^2}$. Since the derivative of $\cos y$ with respect to $y$ is $-\sin y$, the inverse rule gives $y'=-1/\sin y=-1/\sqrt{1-x^2}$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $y=\arccos x$，内部对应 $0<y<\pi$。此时 $\sin y>0$，且 $\sin y=\sqrt{1-x^2}$。由于 $\cos y$ 对 $y$ 的导数是 $-\sin y$，反函数公式给出 $y'=-1/\sin y=-1/\sqrt{1-x^2}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y=\arctan x$, we have $\tan y=x$ and $-\pi/2<y<\pi/2$. Using [[商法则|the quotient rule]] on $\tan y=\sin y/\cos y$ gives $(\tan y)'=1/\cos^2y=1+\tan^2y$. This is positive throughout the branch. Its reciprocal is $y'=1/(1+x^2)$, for every real $x$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $y=\arctan x$，有 $\tan y=x$、$-\pi/2<y<\pi/2$。对 $\tan y=\sin y/\cos y$ 使用[[商法则]]，得到 $(\tan y)'=1/\cos^2y=1+\tan^2y$。它在整个分支上为正，取倒数得到 $y'=1/(1+x^2)$，适用于所有实数 $x$。

> [!example] A value and a sensitivity · 函数值与敏感度
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> At $x=1/2$, the recovered sine angle is $\pi/6$, while its derivative is $2/\sqrt3\approx1.1547$. An input increase of $0.001$ therefore produces an angle increase of approximately $0.0011547$ radians near that point. For a differentiable inner input $u(x)$ with $|u(x)|<1$, the [[链式法则|chain rule]] gives $(\arcsin u(x))'=u'(x)/\sqrt{1-u(x)^2}$. For example,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 在 $x=1/2$，反求出的正弦角是 $\pi/6$，而导数为 $2/\sqrt3\approx1.1547$。因此在该点附近，输入增加 $0.001$，角度约增加 $0.0011547$ 弧度。若内层输入 $u(x)$ 可导且 $|u(x)|<1$，[[链式法则]]给出 $(\arcsin u(x))'=u'(x)/\sqrt{1-u(x)^2}$。例如，
>
> $$
> \frac d{dx}\arcsin(2x)=\frac2{\sqrt{1-4x^2}},
> \qquad |x|<\frac12.
> $$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What happens at the endpoints
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*端点处会发生什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At $x=\pm1$, neither $\arcsin$ nor $\arccos$ has a finite derivative, even if we allow a one-sided derivative from within its domain. To see why at $x=1$ for arcsine, put $x=\cos t$ with $t\downarrow0$. Then $\arcsin x=\pi/2-t$, so the difference quotient from the endpoint is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $x=\pm1$，即使允许从定义域内部取单侧导数，$\arcsin$ 和 $\arccos$ 也没有有限导数。以 $x=1$ 的反正弦为例，令 $x=\cos t$、$t\downarrow0$，则 $\arcsin x=\pi/2-t$，从端点出发的差商为

$$
\frac{\arcsin x-\pi/2}{x-1}
=\frac{t}{1-\cos t}\longrightarrow+\infty.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[正弦基本极限|companion cosine limit]] gives $(1-\cos t)/t\to0$ through positive values. Odd symmetry of arcsine gives the same positive infinite slope at $-1$; the identity $\arccos x=\pi/2-\arcsin x$ gives negative infinite slopes for arccosine. These are unbounded slopes, not finite real derivatives. In contrast, $1+x^2$ is always positive, so arctangent has no finite-input endpoint problem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[正弦基本极限|伴随的余弦极限]]给出 $(1-\cos t)/t$ 从正值趋于零。反正弦的奇对称性给出 $-1$ 端点同样的正无穷斜率；恒等式 $\arccos x=\pi/2-\arcsin x$ 给出反余弦两端的负无穷斜率。这些是无界斜率，不是有限实数导数。相比之下，$1+x^2$ 始终为正，反正切没有有限输入处的端点问题。
