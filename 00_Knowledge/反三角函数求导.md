# 反三角函数的导数由反函数导数公式和原三角恒等式确定
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Inverse-trigonometric derivatives follow from the inverse-function derivative formula and the original trigonometric identities*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子公式
> 在主值分支上，
> $$
> \frac{d}{dx}\arcsin x=\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}},\quad |x|<1,
> $$
> $$
> \frac{d}{dx}\arccos x=-\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}},\quad |x|<1,
> \qquad
> \frac{d}{dx}\arctan x=\frac1{1+x^2},\quad x\in\mathbb R.
> $$
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> On their principal branches, $(\arcsin x)'=1/\sqrt{1-x^2}$ and $(\arccos x)'=-1/\sqrt{1-x^2}$ for $|x|<1$, while $(\arctan x)'=1/(1+x^2)$ for every real $x$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $y=\arcsin x$，则 $x=\sin y$ 且 $y\in[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$。由 [[反函数导数]]，$y'=1/\cos y$；在这个分支 $\cos y\ge0$，所以 $\cos y=\sqrt{1-x^2}$。$\arctan$ 同理由 $1/\sec^2y=1/(1+x^2)$ 得到。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y=\arcsin x$, we have $x=\sin y$ on $[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$. The inverse rule gives $y'=1/\cos y$, and the chosen branch makes $\cos y=\sqrt{1-x^2}$. For arctangent, the reciprocal of $\sec^2y$ becomes $1/(1+x^2)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公式中的分支和定义域不是附注。$\arcsin$ 与 $\arccos$ 在 $x=\pm1$ 仍有函数值，但对应原三角函数的导数为零：对 $\arcsin$，端点对应 $y=\pm\pi/2$，故 $\cos y=0$；对 $\arccos$，端点对应 $y=0$ 或 $\pi$，故原函数 $\cos y$ 的导数 $-\sin y=0$。反函数导数公式的分母因此为零，两者在端点都没有有限的普通导数；平方根的正号则由各自主值分支决定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The branch and domain are part of the formula. Arcsine and arccosine have values at $x=\pm1$, but the derivative of the corresponding original trigonometric function vanishes there. For arcsine, the endpoints correspond to $y=\pm\pi/2$, where $\cos y=0$; for arccosine, they correspond to $y=0$ or $\pi$, where the derivative of $\cos y$ is $-\sin y=0$. The denominator in the inverse-derivative formula therefore vanishes, so neither inverse has a finite ordinary derivative at its endpoints. Each principal branch fixes the positive square root used in the interior formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
