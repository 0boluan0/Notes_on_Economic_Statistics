# 小ω记号表示一个函数相对于另一个函数的比值趋于正无穷
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Little omega notation means that one function's ratio to another tends to positive infinity*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对同一输入域上最终严格为正的函数 $f,g$，**小 $\omega$ 记号** $f=\omega(g)$ 表示 $f(n)/g(n)\to+\infty$。等价地，对任意指定的常数 $M>0$，从某个阈值起总有 $f(n)\ge Mg(n)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For eventually strictly positive functions $f,g$ on the same input domain, **little omega notation** $f=\omega(g)$ means $f(n)/g(n)\to+\infty$. Equivalently, for every specified constant $M>0$, the inequality $f(n)\ge Mg(n)$ holds beyond some threshold.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
f=\omega(g)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
g=o(f).
$$

因为比值最终正，$g/f=1/(f/g)$ 趋零当且仅当 $f/g$ 趋正无穷。这是 [[小o记号]] 的反向比较。例：$n^2=\omega(n)$，因为 $n^2/n=n\to\infty$；而 $100n$ 只是 $\Omega(n)$，不是 $\omega(n)$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Since the ratio is eventually positive, $g/f=1/(f/g)$ tends to zero exactly when $f/g$ tends to positive infinity. Thus little omega reverses [[小o记号|little o]]. For example, $n^2=\omega(n)$ because its ratio is $n\to\infty$, whereas $100n$ is only $\Omega(n)$, not $\omega(n)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\omega$ 要求最终超过**每个**固定常数倍；[[大Ω记号]] 只要求超过**某个**正常数倍。因此 $\omega(g)$ 蕴含 $\Omega(g)$，反向不成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Little omega requires eventually exceeding **every** fixed multiple; [[大Ω记号|big Omega]] requires only **some** positive multiple. Consequently, little omega implies big Omega, but not conversely.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
