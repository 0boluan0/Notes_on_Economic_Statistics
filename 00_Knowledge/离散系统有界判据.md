# Bounded trajectories in discrete time
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*离散系统的全轨迹有界判据*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A trajectory is **bounded** when its length stays below some finite limit for all future steps. It can keep moving forever without approaching zero. Here we ask whether this holds for every starting state of a fixed linear system, rather than just for one carefully chosen initial vector.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条轨迹**有界**，是指它的长度在所有未来步数上都不超过某个有限上界。它可以一直运动，而不趋近零。本卡检查固定线性系统的每个初始状态是否都如此，而不只是某个特别选中的初始向量。

> [!example] Alternation stays bounded; a shear can grow · 交替可以有界，剪切却会增长
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The scalar recurrence $x_{k+1}=-x_k$ starting at $x_0=2$ alternates between $2$ and $-2$: it stays bounded but has no limit. In contrast, for the matrix below, $x_0=(0,1)^T$ gives $x_k=(k,1)^T$; $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]] to write a column.
> $$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad A^k=\begin{pmatrix}1&k\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\quad(k\ge0).$$
> Its only eigenvalue is also on the unit circle, but the first coordinate grows without bound.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 标量递推 $x_{k+1}=-x_k$ 从2出发，在2与−2之间交替：有界，却没有极限。上面的矩阵从 $x_0=(0,1)^T$ 出发，则得到 $x_k=(k,1)^T$；$T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]，把坐标写成列。其唯一特征值也在单位圆上，第一坐标却无界增长。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The condition at the unit circle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*单位圆上的额外条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $A$ be a fixed real or complex $n\times n$ matrix, $n\ge1$, and let $x_{k+1}=Ax_k$ with no external input. Repeated [[矩阵乘法|multiplication]] gives $x_k=A^kx_0$, with $A^0=I_n$, the [[单位矩阵|identity matrix]]. Measure length by $\|x\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_{j=1}^n|x_j|^2}$, from the standard [[内积|inner product]]. For a complex coordinate, the bars mean its modulus.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $A$ 为固定实或复 $n\times n$ 矩阵，$n\ge1$，系统为没有外部输入的 $x_{k+1}=Ax_k$。反复[[矩阵乘法|相乘]]得到 $x_k=A^kx_0$，其中 $A^0=I_n$ 是[[单位矩阵]]。用标准[[内积]]给出的 $\|x\|_2=\sqrt{\sum_{j=1}^n|x_j|^2}$ 衡量长度；复坐标上的竖线表示模。

> [!important] Two requirements, both needed · 两项条件缺一不可
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Every initial state has a bounded trajectory exactly when every complex eigenvalue satisfies $|\lambda|\le1$, and each eigenvalue with $|\lambda|=1$ is [[半单特征值|semisimple]]. Semisimple means its algebraic and geometric multiplicities agree, equivalently all its Jordan blocks have size one. Only the eigenvalues on the boundary need this extra condition.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 每个初值的轨迹都有界，当且仅当所有复特征值满足 $|\lambda|\le1$，并且每个 $|\lambda|=1$ 的特征值都[[半单特征值|半单]]。半单表示代数重数等于几何重数，等价于对应 Jordan 块全部为一阶。只有边界上的特征值需要这项额外条件。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first condition is $\rho(A)\le1$, using the [[谱半径|spectral radius]], but that alone does not exclude the opening shear. Real matrices must also be checked using their nonreal eigenvalues. A repeated boundary root is allowed: $I_2$ has the root $1$ twice and leaves every trajectory constant.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一项也就是[[谱半径]]满足 $\rho(A)\le1$，但单靠它排除不了开头的剪切。实矩阵也必须检查非实特征值。边界上的重根是允许的：$I_2$ 的根1重复两次，却使每条轨迹都保持常量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why only boundary Jordan blocks cause trouble
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么只在边界禁止长 Jordan 块*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Work over $\mathbb C$, where [[Jordan标准形的域条件|Jordan form exists]], and write $A=SJS^{-1}$ with invertible $S$. Fixed changes of coordinates preserve boundedness in both directions. Therefore inspect one [[Jordan块的幂|Jordan-block power]] at a time. Inside the unit circle, its entries combine a fixed-degree polynomial in $k$ with geometric decay, and tend to zero; a zero-eigenvalue block becomes exactly zero after finitely many steps. This is the mechanism in the [[矩阵幂趋零判据|vanishing-power theorem]]. Outside the circle, even an ordinary eigenvector grows by $|\lambda|^k$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

放到复数域中，满足[[Jordan标准形的域条件]]，可写 $A=SJS^{-1}$，其中 $S$ 可逆。固定的可逆换坐标在两个方向都保持有界性，因此可以逐个检查[[Jordan块的幂]]。单位圆内部，元素中的固定次数多项式乘上几何衰减后趋零；零特征值块则在有限步后精确归零。这正是[[矩阵幂趋零判据]]中的机制。单位圆外，即使沿普通特征向量，也会按 $|\lambda|^k$ 增长。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
On the unit circle, a size-one block contributes just $\lambda^k$, whose modulus is one. A block of size at least two has first superdiagonal entry $k\lambda^{k-1}$, of modulus $k$. Choosing the second coordinate vector inside that block produces this growing first coordinate. The corresponding initial vector in the original coordinates is obtained by multiplying by $S$, so a genuine unbounded trajectory exists. For real $A$, if that vector is complex, split it into real and imaginary parts: they cannot both yield bounded real trajectories when their complex combination is unbounded.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单位圆上，一阶块只给出模恒为1的 $\lambda^k$。大小至少为二的块，第一条超对角线含有 $k\lambda^{k-1}$，其模为 $k$。在这个块中选择第二个坐标基向量，就会产生上述增长的第一坐标；乘以 $S$ 换回原坐标，便得到实际的无界轨迹。对实矩阵，如果这一向量是复的，就拆成实部与虚部；若两条实轨迹都有界，它们的复组合也有界，矛盾。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The bound may depend on the initial state
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*每条轨迹的上界可以依赖初值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The claim means: for each $x_0$, there is a finite $M(x_0)$ bounding all its later lengths. It does not require one absolute bound for arbitrarily large initial vectors. In finite dimension it is equivalent to a single **amplification bound** $C$ independent of $x_0$ and $k$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

结论是：对每个 $x_0$，存在某个有限 $M(x_0)$ 界住它以后的全部长度；不是要求任意大的初始向量也共用一个绝对上界。在有限维中，它等价于存在一个不依赖初值和步数的**放大倍数上界** $C$：

$$\|A^kx_0\|_2\le C\|x_0\|_2\qquad\text{for every }x_0\text{ and }k\ge0.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To see this, apply the trajectory assumption to the finitely many coordinate [[基|basis vectors]] $e_j$ and call their bounds $B_j$. Writing $x_0=\sum_jx_j e_j$ and using the triangle inequality followed by [[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式|Cauchy–Schwarz]] gives $\|A^kx_0\|_2\le\sum_j|x_j|B_j\le\sqrt{\sum_j B_j^2}\,\|x_0\|_2$. Conversely, an amplification bound immediately bounds each fixed initial trajectory. This is also called **power boundedness** of $A$, or boundedness of $\|A^k\|_2$ in the [[谱范数|induced matrix norm]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把轨迹有界假设用于有限个坐标[[基|基向量]] $e_j$，设相应上界为 $B_j$。写 $x_0=\sum_jx_j e_j$，先用三角不等式，再用 [[Cauchy–Schwarz不等式]]，得到 $\|A^kx_0\|_2\le\sum_j|x_j|B_j\le\sqrt{\sum_j B_j^2}\,\|x_0\|_2$。反过来，放大倍数上界当然界住每条固定初值轨迹。这也称矩阵 $A$ **幂有界**，即[[谱范数|诱导矩阵范数]]中的 $\|A^k\|_2$ 有界。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choosing $\|x_0\|_2<\varepsilon/C$ keeps every future state within radius $\varepsilon$. Thus for this homogeneous linear system, all-trajectory boundedness also expresses Lyapunov stability of zero. [[离散系统谱稳定性|Asymptotic stability]] additionally requires convergence to zero and replaces the closed-disk test with the strict condition $\rho(A)<1$. A quarter-turn rotation and the opening alternation satisfy boundedness but not that stronger property.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

选取 $\|x_0\|_2<\varepsilon/C$，就能使全部未来状态都留在半径 $\varepsilon$ 内。因此对这个齐次线性系统，全轨迹有界也表达零点的 Lyapunov 稳定性。[[离散系统谱稳定性|渐近稳定]]还要求趋零，其条件改为严格的 $\rho(A)<1$。四分之一圈旋转和开头的交替都符合有界性，却不符合更强的渐近稳定。

> [!question] A quick application · 一个直接应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $A$ is diagonalizable and every eigenvalue has modulus at most one, are all trajectories bounded? Yes: all Jordan blocks already have size one. The converse need not hold; blocks strictly inside the circle may be larger. These statements concern a fixed matrix and no external forcing.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $A$ 可对角化，且全部特征值的模不超过1，是否全轨迹有界？是，因为所有 Jordan 块已经为一阶。逆向却不一定成立：严格位于单位圆内的块允许更大。这里始终讨论固定矩阵、无外部强迫。
