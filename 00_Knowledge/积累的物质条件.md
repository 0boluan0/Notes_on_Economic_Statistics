# 剩余价值资本化需要可用的追加生产资料与相应劳动条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Capitalising surplus value requires additional means of production and corresponding labour conditions.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Marx资本积累|资本积累]]要扩大实际生产，社会剩余产品中就须有可追加使用的生产资料，以及支持相应劳动的生活资料。个别资本家能够出钱购买，还取决于这些产品已经存在或能在需要时供应；市场交换可以转移物品的所有权，却不能把既有物品自动变成另一种实物。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To expand actual production through [[Marx资本积累|accumulation]], the social surplus product must include usable additional means of production and the means of subsistence supporting the corresponding labour. An individual capitalist's purchasing power requires those goods to exist or become available when needed. Exchange transfers ownership but does not automatically turn existing goods into different physical objects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里把世界生产作为一个整体考察。若只研究一国，它可以把奢侈品出口换取生产资料和生活资料；判断该国的实物条件时，就要把贸易提供的投入也计入，而不能只看国内剩余产品的组成。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The argument treats world production as one whole. A particular country may export luxuries in exchange for means of production and subsistence. Its material conditions must therefore include inputs supplied by trade, not only the composition of its domestic surplus product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设要把40个货币单位投入新增生产，其中30用于材料和设备，10用于购买[[Marx劳动力|劳动力]]的使用。这个安排只有在相应材料、设备、劳动能力和生活资料能够结合时才实现。若社会新增产品全是供资本家消费的奢侈品，账上把40改列为投资，并没有同时提供这一组生产要素。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose 40 monetary units are assigned to additional production: 30 for materials and equipment and 10 for the use of [[Marx劳动力|labour power]]. The arrangement works only when the corresponding inputs, labour capacity and subsistence can be combined. If the entire additional social product consists of luxuries for capitalist consumption, recording 40 as investment does not supply those productive elements.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

追加劳动不总是追加人数。若既有劳动者的工时和劳动强度都不增加，扩大所用劳动就需要额外劳动力；若这些条件改变，应分别检查实际增加了多少劳动及其再生产需要。工资是资本购买劳动力的支出，工人再用工资购买生活资料，不能把两次交易当成资本直接购买了食物来充当可变资本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Additional labour need not always mean additional people. If existing workers' hours and intensity do not rise, expanding labour requires additional labour power. When those conditions change, examine the additional labour and its reproduction needs separately. Capital buys labour power with wages, and workers use those wages to buy subsistence; these are two transactions, not capital directly buying food as variable capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*Sources and verification*

- 卷II，[第8章§I](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1885-c2/ch08.htm)，劳动力与生活资料的两次交易段：资本购买的是劳动力。MIA 所据 I. Lasker 英译、Progress Publishers 1956 版。40、30、10为教学构造。

Volume I supplies the composition of the social product and the qualified requirement for additional labour power; note 2 states the world-production scope and the qualification for trade. Volume II distinguishes purchasing labour power from workers purchasing subsistence; MIA identifies its source as I. Lasker's translation, Progress Publishers, 1956. The allocation example is constructed.
