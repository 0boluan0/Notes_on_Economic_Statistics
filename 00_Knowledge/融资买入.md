# 融资买入是以借款支付部分证券购买价并以账户资产支持借款的交易
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Buying on margin finances part of a securities purchase with borrowing supported by account assets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

融资买入（buying on margin）是投资者在证券融资账户中借入资金，支付部分证券购买价，以账户内约定资产支持借款的交易。它同时建立证券多头和融资债务；股价下跌不自动减免欠款，支付利息、补足担保及处置权利由规则与合同约定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Buying on margin uses borrowing in a securities margin account to finance part of a purchase, with agreed account assets supporting the loan. It creates both a long securities position and financing debt. A price decline does not automatically reduce the debt; interest, collateral, and liquidation terms follow the rules and agreement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，自有资金 4,000 元加借款 6,000 元买入 10,000 元股票。忽略现金和其他资产负债，账户权益是 $10,000-6,000=4,000$ 元，而不是 10,000 元。若股票跌至 5,000 元且借款未变，权益已为负 1,000 元；实际过程中可能更早触发追加担保或平仓。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, 4,000 of own funds plus a 6,000 loan buys 10,000 of stock. Ignoring cash and other assets or liabilities, account equity is $10,000-6,000=4,000$, not 10,000. A fall in stock value to 5,000 with unchanged debt leaves equity of minus 1,000, although calls or liquidation may occur earlier.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，持股公司的[[有限责任|股东有限责任]]不免除投资者对证券融资贷款的偿还义务。借款也不是单独“创造收益”；它改变自有资金承担的损益比例并产生融资成本，见[[融资买入收益率]]。何种证券可融资、最高比例及追保规则须按实际市场和账户确认，例中的 60% 借款不是通用许可比例。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The issuer's [[有限责任|shareholder limited liability]] does not discharge the investor's margin-loan obligation. Borrowing changes the profit or loss borne per unit of own funds and incurs financing costs; see [[融资买入收益率|leveraged purchase returns]]. Eligibility, maximum borrowing, and calls depend on the market and account. The example's 60% debt share is not a universal permitted rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
