# 剩余价值是雇佣劳动创造并由资本占有的超过劳动力价值等价物的价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Surplus value is value created by wage labour beyond labour-power's equivalent and appropriated by capital*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

剩余价值（surplus value）是雇佣工人在生产中新创造的价值中，超过劳动力价值等价物并由资本占有的部分，通常记作 $s$。在劳动力按其价值出售的分析条件下，若活劳动新创造的价值为 $N$、可变资本为 $v$，则 $s=N-v$；这部分价值是 [[剩余劳动]] 的物化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Surplus value, conventionally denoted by $s$, is the part of the new value created by wage labour that exceeds the equivalent of labour-power's value and is appropriated by capital. Under the assumption that labour-power sells at its value, if living labour creates new value $N$ and variable capital is $v$, then $s=N-v$. This value is materialised [[剩余劳动|surplus labour]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若从产品总价值开始计算，要先区分转移来的旧价值与新价值。按 [[产品价值分解]] 的符号，产品价值 $W=c_u+v+s$，其中 $c_u$ 是当期耗用的生产资料价值。因此：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Starting from the total value of the product requires separating transferred old value from new value. Using the notation of [[产品价值分解|product-value decomposition]], $W=c_u+v+s$, where $c_u$ is the value of means of production used up during the period. Thus:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
s=N-v=W-c_u-v.
$$

教学例中，工人按正常且不变的强度劳动8小时，每小时平均社会劳动表现为100价值单位；一天劳动力按其400单位价值支付。新价值800中剩余价值为400。若当天耗用并转移的生产资料价值为1200，则也可算成 $2000-1200-400=400$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed example, the worker works eight hours at normal, unchanged intensity, with each hour of average social labour expressed in 100 value units. A day's labour-power is paid at its value of 400. New value of 800 contains surplus value of 400. If 1,200 units of means-of-production value are consumed and transferred that day, the same result is $2000-1200-400=400$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

剩余价值的定义回答“剩余是什么”；[[等价交换与剩余]] 解释它怎样生成，[[剩余价值率]] 衡量其相对量，[[Marx资本积累]] 讨论它怎样再次作为资本使用。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The definition identifies the surplus. [[等价交换与剩余|Exchange of equivalents and surplus production]] explains how it arises, the [[剩余价值率|rate of surplus value]] measures its relative magnitude, and [[Marx资本积累|capital accumulation in Marx]] examines its renewed use as capital.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- Marx，《资本论》卷I，[第7章第2节](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch07.htm#S2)，劳动力自身价值与其使用创造的价值的区分，以及劳动过程越过等价物再生产时点的说明；[第9章第1节](https://www.marxists.org/archive/marx/works/1867-c1/ch09.htm#S1)，新价值 $v+s$、剩余价值量 $s$ 与剩余劳动的对应：支持定义、价值分解和生产关系。MIA 所载 Moore / Aveling 英译、Engels 编，1887英文初版底本，标示处纳入德文第四版改动。8小时与2000单位例为教学构造。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Volume I, Chapter 7, Section 2 distinguishes the value of labour-power from the value its use creates and explains production beyond the point at which its equivalent is reproduced. Chapter 9, Section 1 supports new value $v+s$, the amount $s$, and its relation to surplus labour. The MIA text uses the Moore and Aveling translation edited by Engels, based on the 1887 first English edition with indicated fourth German edition changes. The eight-hour and 2,000-unit illustration is constructed.
