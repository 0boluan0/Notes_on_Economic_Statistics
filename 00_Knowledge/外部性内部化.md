# 外部性内部化是使原本遗漏的他人得失进入行动者的决策激励
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Internalising an externality brings previously omitted effects on others into the actor's decision incentives*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

外部性内部化，是让行动者在选择活动时考虑原先没有承担的外部损害，或没有取得的外部收益。它可以通过与行为挂钩的价格和合同，也可以通过共同决策组织实现。关键是**多做或少做一单位时，行动者的取舍是否计入相关外部影响**。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Internalisation makes the actor account for external harm previously unborne or benefits previously uncaptured. Behaviour-linked prices, contracts or joint decision-making can provide it. The test is whether the marginal choice now reflects the relevant external effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如每增加一单位排放给旁人带来20的损失。若排放者每单位须支付20，其决策中就多了这项边际代价。若只要求一次性支付200，且此款完全不随以后排放而变，在没有收入效应等其他渠道的例子中，就没有改变下一单位排放的激励。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If another unit emitted causes damage of twenty, a payment of twenty per unit adds that marginal cost to the choice. A fixed payment of two hundred that never changes with later emissions does not alter the next-unit incentive in an example without income effects or other channels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，发生赔款不一定证明已经内部化；没有现金付款也不一定证明完全没有内部化。[[合并内部化]]通过联合净收益作决策，协商则须检查[[外部性谈判区间|具体协议改变什么行为]]。即使激励得到纠正，也要另外记录钱归谁、实际损害是否获赔，以及仍有多少残余影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compensation alone does not establish internalisation, and the absence of payment does not rule it out. [[合并内部化|Integration]] can align joint returns, while bargaining requires an [[外部性谈判区间|agreement that changes behaviour]]. Distribution of money, compensation for remaining damage and residual effects remain separate accounts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
