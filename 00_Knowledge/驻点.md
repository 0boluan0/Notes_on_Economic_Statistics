# Stationary points
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*驻点：一阶变化为零的位置*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **stationary point** is an input where a differentiable function has zero first-order change. In one variable, the tangent is horizontal. With several inputs, the linear part of the change is zero for every small displacement. This describes the derivative at a point; it does not say that the function is constant nearby or that the point is an optimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**驻点**是可微函数一阶变化为零的输入位置。一元时，切线是水平的；多元时，不论怎样作微小位移，变化的线性部分都为零。这描述的是一点的导数，并不是说函数在附近恒定，也不是说这个点一定最优。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider the real-valued function
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看实值函数

$$f(x,y)=5+(x-1)^2+2(y+2)^2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[偏导数|partial derivative]] changes one input while fixing the other. Here $\partial_x f=2(x-1)$ and $\partial_y f=4(y+2)$. They both vanish precisely at $a=(1,-2)$. The [[全微分#实值函数、梯度与全微分|gradient]] is the column containing these partials, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[偏导数]]只改变一个输入、固定其余输入。这里 $\partial_x f=2(x-1)$、$\partial_y f=4(y+2)$，两者同时为零恰好给出 $a=(1,-2)$。[[全微分#实值函数、梯度与全微分|梯度]]就是这些偏导组成的列向量，因此

$$\nabla f(x,y)=\begin{pmatrix}2(x-1)\\4(y+2)\end{pmatrix},\qquad \nabla f(1,-2)=\begin{pmatrix}0\\0\end{pmatrix}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The stationary **input** is $(1,-2)$; the output there is $f(1,-2)=5$, not zero. If the displacement is $h=(h_1,h_2)$, direct substitution gives $f(a+h)-f(a)=h_1^2+2h_2^2$. There are no terms linear in $h_1,h_2$, but a nonzero displacement still changes the function. This example is also a strict minimum because that difference is positive for $h\ne0$; the extra sign comparison establishes the minimum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

驻点**输入**是 $(1,-2)$，对应输出 $f(1,-2)=5$，并不是零。令位移为 $h=(h_1,h_2)$，直接代入可得 $f(a+h)-f(a)=h_1^2+2h_2^2$：没有关于 $h_1,h_2$ 的一次项，但非零位移仍会改变函数值。这个例子还恰好是严格最小点，因为 $h\ne0$ 时差值为正；真正证明最小的是这一步额外的符号比较。

## What the zero derivative means
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*零导数的准确含义*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $f:U\to\mathbb R$, where $U$ is an [[开集|open subset]] of $\mathbb R^n$, $n\ge1$ is a finite integer, and $a\in U$. A whole small ball around $a$ then consists of allowed inputs. [[全微分|Differentiability at $a$]] means that one [[线性映射|linear map]] $Df(a)$, respecting addition and real scalar multiplication, approximates changes in all directions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $f:U\to\mathbb R$，其中 $U$ 是 $\mathbb R^n$ 中的[[开集]]，$n\ge1$ 为有限整数，且 $a\in U$。这保证 $a$ 周围整个小球内的输入都合法。$f$ 在 $a$ 处[[全微分|可微]]，表示存在同一个保持加法和实数倍乘的[[线性映射]] $Df(a)$，同时近似所有方向上的变化：

$$f(a+h)-f(a)=Df(a)[h]+r(h),\qquad \frac{r(h)}{\|h\|_2}\longrightarrow0\quad(h\to0,\ h\ne0).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $h$ is the displacement, $r(h)$ is the remaining error, and $\|h\|_2=(\sum_i h_i^2)^{1/2}$ is its [[欧氏范数|Euclidean length]]. The error divided by the length tends to zero along every way of approaching the origin.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $h$ 是位移，$r(h)$ 是剩余误差，$\|h\|_2=(\sum_i h_i^2)^{1/2}$ 是位移的[[欧氏范数|欧氏长度]]。误差除以位移长度后，沿任何趋向原点的方式都趋于零。

> [!important] Definition · 定义
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The point $a$ is **stationary** when $f$ is differentiable there and $Df(a)$ is the zero linear map: $Df(a)[h]=0$ for every $h\in\mathbb R^n$. With the gradient written as a column, $Df(a)[h]=\nabla f(a)^T h$, so stationarity is equivalent to $\nabla f(a)=0$. Here $T$ denotes [[矩阵转置|transpose]]. In one variable this is the finite [[导数|derivative]] condition $f'(a)=0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $f$ 在 $a$ 可微，且 $Df(a)$ 是零线性映射，即每个 $h\in\mathbb R^n$ 都满足 $Df(a)[h]=0$，就称 $a$ 为**驻点**。采用列梯度时，$Df(a)[h]=\nabla f(a)^T h$，所以驻定等价于 $\nabla f(a)=0$。其中 $T$ 表示[[矩阵转置|转置]]；一元情形就是有限[[导数]]满足 $f'(a)=0$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The point must belong to the function's domain. At a stationary point the increment itself is the error $r(h)$, so it is smaller than first order in the stated limiting sense. For the quadratic example, $0\le h_1^2+2h_2^2\le2\|h\|_2^2$; dividing by $\|h\|_2$ proves this directly. Merely calculating zero partial derivatives does not establish differentiability: [[偏导不可推可微|partial derivatives alone do not guarantee a common linear approximation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个点必须属于函数的定义域。在驻点，增量本身就是误差 $r(h)$，所以按上述极限含义，它比一阶变化更小。前面的二次函数满足 $0\le h_1^2+2h_2^2\le2\|h\|_2^2$，除以 $\|h\|_2$ 就直接证明了这一点。仅仅算出全部偏导为零，还不能证明可微：[[偏导不可推可微|偏导存在本身不保证共同的线性近似]]。

## Stationary does not mean optimal
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*驻定与最优是两个判断*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $g(x)=x^3$, the [[整数幂求导|power rule]] gives $g'(x)=3x^2$, so zero is stationary. Nevertheless, $g(h)>g(0)$ for $h>0$ and $g(h)<g(0)$ for $h<0$. Thus zero is neither a [[局部极值与绝对极值|local minimum nor a local maximum]]. The function continues increasing through its horizontal tangent. Conversely, $|x|$ has a minimum at zero, but its left and right slopes are $-1$ and $1$: there is no derivative there, so it is not stationary under this definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 $g(x)=x^3$，[[整数幂求导]]给出 $g'(x)=3x^2$，所以零点是驻点。然而 $h>0$ 时 $g(h)>g(0)$，$h<0$ 时 $g(h)<g(0)$，故零点既不是[[局部极值与绝对极值|局部最小点，也不是局部最大点]]。函数穿过水平切线位置后仍继续上升。反过来，$|x|$ 在零点取得最小，但左右斜率分别是 $-1$ 与 $1$，该点不可导，所以按此定义并非驻点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[无约束一阶条件|interior first-order necessary condition]] explains the valid connection: a differentiable interior local extremum must be stationary. It requires both differentiability and interiority. If a smooth function is optimized only over a constrained subset of its domain, its ambient derivative and the allowed movements are separate objects; a boundary optimum need not be stationary. A derivative defined only from one side or relative to a restricted domain needs its own convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[无约束一阶条件|内部一阶必要条件]]给出正确联系：可微函数的内部局部极值必须是驻点。这里既需要可微，也需要内点。若只在光滑函数定义域的某个约束子集上优化，原函数的导数与允许的移动是两回事；边界最优点不必驻定。只从单侧或相对于受限定义域定义的导数，需要另行说明约定。

## Reading the term critical point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*辨认 critical point 的课程口径*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
SOFP uses “critical point” for vanishing first derivatives in its smooth setting. Many calculus texts, and the vault's one-variable [[临界点|critical-point definition]], also include interior points where the finite derivative does not exist. Under that broader convention, a stationary point is one kind of critical point; a corner can be critical without being stationary. The term must be read together with the source's definition, rather than treated as an unconditional synonym.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

SOFP 在光滑语境中用 critical point 表示一阶导数为零。许多微积分教材以及本库的一元[[临界点]]定义，还把内部有限导数不存在的点包括进来。按这个较宽的约定，驻点是临界点的一类，尖角可以是临界点却不是驻点。因此要结合来源的定义辨认术语，不能无条件当成同义词。
