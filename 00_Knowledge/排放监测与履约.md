# 排放监测与履约把可核实的排放总量与同一期间的配额缴回义务逐项对齐
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Emissions monitoring and compliance reconcile verifiable total emissions with allowance-surrender obligations for the same period.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[总量排放交易]]要兑现总量，不能只查“有没有买配额”，而要先查排放是否完整可核实，再查同一期间有没有足够有效配额并实际缴回。测量、报告、核验和履约是相连但不同的步骤；测量更频繁不等于履约周期也更短。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Delivering a [[总量排放交易|cap-and-trade]] target requires more than checking allowance purchases. First establish complete verifiable emissions, then confirm sufficient eligible allowances for the same period and actual surrender. Measurement, reporting, verification and compliance are connected but distinct; frequent measurement does not imply a shorter compliance period.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一次核对按以下顺序进行：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use the following reconciliation sequence:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

1. 明确义务主体、污染物、质量单位、空间范围和履约期间；浓度或单位产出排放率不能未经流量、活动量等换算就当总排放。
2. 检查测量覆盖与方法：记录方法是否能捕捉企业实际采用的减排方式，并保留质量保证、校准和审计证据。
3. 对缺测期执行事前规定的替代方法，不把“没有读数”视为零；汇总整期、去除重复后形成核定排放量。
4. 对齐配额账本：期初有效余额、流入流出、适用期、规则允许的其他来源，以及已经缴回或注销的数量。
5. 按截止规则核定、缴回并处理不足；缴回后不能再把同一配额给另一笔排放使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

&nbsp;
**1.** Specify the liable entity, pollutant, mass unit, spatial scope and compliance period. Concentrations or emission rates need conversion using flows or activity.<br>
**2.** Check coverage and whether the method captures the abatement actually used; retain quality-assurance, calibration and audit evidence.<br>
**3.** Apply pre-specified missing-data procedures rather than treating absent readings as zero; compile complete, unduplicated emissions.<br>
**4.** Reconcile eligible opening allowances, transfers, vintages, other authorised sources and prior surrender or cancellation.<br>
**5.** Verify and surrender by the deadline, address shortages, and prevent reuse of surrendered allowances.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

自造最小账本：同一期间三段排放12、13、15吨，其中第三段15由题目给定的合规缺值替代规则得到。核定总量40；期初有效配额35、截止前净买5，缴回40后余额0。若把缺测段当0，就会把排放错记为25并虚留15份配额。此例只示核对逻辑，不规定现实缺值算法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A constructed ledger records twelve, thirteen and fifteen tonnes over three intervals, with the last amount supplied by the problem's compliant missing-data rule. Total emissions are forty. Opening allowances of thirty-five plus net purchases of five cover surrender of forty. Treating the missing interval as zero would falsely report twenty-five tonnes and leave fifteen allowances. This illustrates reconciliation, not a real-world substitution formula.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方法还须与激励相容：只按燃料投入推估排放，可能捕捉燃料替换，却漏掉不改燃料用量的末端治理收益；应检查计量是否记录制度允许的调整渠道。[[产量、排放与减排|产出、排放和减排]]的单位不能混用。[[排污费|按排放收费]]同样需要可靠税基，并非只有交易才需要监测。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Measurement must capture relevant incentives. Fuel-input estimates may capture fuel switching but miss end-of-pipe abatement that leaves fuel use unchanged. Check whether allowed adjustment channels are measured, and distinguish [[产量、排放与减排|output, emissions and abatement]]. An [[排污费|emissions charge]] also needs a reliable base; monitoring is not unique to trading.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

处罚是履约激励的一部分，但普通处罚、补足配额和[[排放价格走廊|合法价格上限通道]]不是同一机制。是否付罚款后仍须补缴，应按制度规则判断；不能从“有罚款”直接推出总量已得到保证。现实监测与执法会占用资源，须另计而不与配额购买转移重复。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Sanctions support compliance, but ordinary penalties, making good allowance shortfalls and an [[排放价格走廊|authorised ceiling option]] are different mechanisms. Programme rules determine whether a fine leaves a surrender obligation. Penalties alone do not prove that the cap is achieved. Monitoring and enforcement use resources that must be counted separately from allowance-payment transfers.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
