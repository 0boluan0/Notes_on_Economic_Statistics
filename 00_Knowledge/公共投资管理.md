# 公共投资管理是将项目规划、评价、预算、实施与运营复盘衔接起来的制度安排
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Public investment management connects project planning, appraisal, budgeting, implementation, operation and review through institutional arrangements.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

公共投资管理（PIM）是管理[[政府投资|公共资本项目]]整个生命周期的制度安排：用明确的职责、信息和决策程序，把提出项目、判断价值、安排预算、组织实施、运营维护与事后复盘衔接起来。它管理的是项目怎样被选择并持续发挥作用，不只是怎样批准一笔建设支出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Public investment management governs the lifecycle of [[政府投资|public capital projects]]. Responsibilities, information and decision procedures connect project proposals, appraisal, budgeting, implementation, operation, maintenance and review. Its concern is how projects are selected and made to function over time, not simply how construction spending is approved.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条可检查的管理链应回答：项目是否符合明确需求、是否经过评价和必要的独立复核；哪些项目进入可承担的预算；实施中谁记录成本、进度和质量，并决定是否调整；投入使用后谁负责运营维护；完成后如何比较计划与实际，将经验反馈给后续选择。世界银行的 PIM 框架把这些列为相互衔接的环节；具体审批权限、金额门槛和程序仍取决于当地制度，不能把一个诊断框架当作各国统一的法律。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A checkable management chain asks whether a project addresses an established need and receives appraisal and appropriate independent review; which projects enter an affordable budget; who monitors cost, time and quality and decides on adjustments; who operates and maintains the asset; and how actual performance is compared with plans to inform later choices. The World Bank framework connects these stages, but approval powers, thresholds and procedures remain jurisdiction-specific rather than a universal legal code.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，学校扩建不能只批准校舍建设款：投入使用需要教师、设备与持续维护，必须确定责任主体，并把后续负担接入[[中期预算框架]]。这些成本也要进入[[公共项目评价范围|适当期限的评价账户]]；若建设方案忽略它们，预算安排与预期服务就没有衔接好。具体的社会价值比较由[[成本—收益分析]]等方法承担，PIM 不替代这些评价方法。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, a school extension requires more than an approved construction budget. Teachers, equipment and continuing maintenance need assigned responsibility and provision in the [[中期预算框架|medium-term budget framework]]. Relevant costs also belong within the [[公共项目评价范围|appraisal horizon]]. Omitting them disconnects financing from expected service delivery. Methods such as [[成本—收益分析|cost-benefit analysis]] assess social value; PIM does not replace those methods.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

建立办公室、发布规则或按期完工，都只是链条中的部分证据。能否改善学习或健康，仍须区分[[项目投入产出与结果|资源、交付和结果]]，检查使用情况与机制，并处理反事实问题。事后复盘可以核对成本超支、延误及运营表现；仅有完成率或采用管理制度后的经济增长，不能单独识别项目或制度的因果效果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Creating an office, issuing rules or finishing on time provides evidence about only part of the chain. Improvements in learning or health require distinguishing [[项目投入产出与结果|inputs, outputs and outcomes]], examining use and mechanisms, and addressing the counterfactual. Ex post review can assess overruns, delays and operation, but completion rates or growth following institutional adoption do not by themselves identify causal effects.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [World Bank IEG（2021），*World Bank Support for Public Financial and Debt Management in IDA-Eligible Countries*](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/986281615997480668/pdf/World-Bank-Support-for-Public-Financial-and-Debt-Management-in-IDA-Eligible-Countries-An-Independent-Evaluation.pdf)，第4章，印刷第39、42–44、47页（PDF第63、66–68、71页）：第39页区分制度措施与实现结果所需的能力；Table 4.1列项目评价、复核、预算、实施、调整、运营与完成评价环节；Box 4.2说明职责、信息与管理能力；第47页明确该报告不能据国家间相关性认定PIM支持的因果效果。这是对FY08–17支持的评价及诊断框架，不是现行统一审批法规。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Chapter 4 distinguishes institutional steps from operational capacity, sets out the connected stages in Table 4.1 and responsibilities in Box 4.2, and states the limits of causal attribution. Its evaluation period and diagnostic framework do not constitute universal current approval law.
  <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- [HM Treasury，*The Green Book*（2026）](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book-appraisal-and-evaluation-in-central-government/the-green-book-2026#shortlist-appraisal)，第6章“Time horizon”“Classifying social costs and social benefits”，第10章“Planning for evaluation in the appraisal process”：支持覆盖运营维护、维持服务收益所需成本，以及在批准前安排监测评价并核对实际结果。学校例是据这些条件构造的应用，不是已估计的项目效果。
  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  These sections support lifecycle coverage, maintenance costs needed to sustain benefits, and evaluation planning before approval. The school is an illustrative application, not an estimated project effect.
