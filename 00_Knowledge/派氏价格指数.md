# 派氏价格指数比较报告期数量篮子在报告期和基期价格下的价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The Paasche price index compares the current quantity basket at current and base-period prices.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

派氏指数让分子、分母使用同一组报告期数量 $q_{it}$，问“今天这篮子按今天和过去价格分别值多少”。在口径一致、分母为正时，基值 $B$ 对应：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Paasche uses current quantities $q_{it}$ on both sides: what would today's basket cost at current versus past prices? With consistent definitions, a positive denominator and base value $B$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$I_P=B\frac{\sum_i p_{it}q_{it}}{\sum_i p_{i0}q_{it}}.$$

基期价格为 10、20，报告期为 12、18；报告期数量为 1、3。当前篮子现价值 66，按旧价值 70，指数为 $100\times66/70\approx94.286$。注意分母是 $\sum p_0q_t$，不能换成 $\sum p_0q_0$：换后就混入了数量变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For prices changing from 10 and 20 to 12 and 18 and current quantities 1 and 3, the basket values are 66 and 70, giving approximately 94.286. Replacing the denominator with $\sum p_0q_0$ would mix quantity change into the comparison.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

课程以报告期发行股数代替报告期成交量的式子，仍是这个“当前数量固定两边”的结构，只是所测对象不同。它不能因此直接等同于持续维护的[[市值加权指数]]；后者还涉及样本变更、流通调整和[[指数除数调整|除数连续性]]。对称结合基期与报告期篮子见[[Fisher价格指数]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Using current outstanding shares rather than current traded quantities retains the same current-basket structure but changes what is measured. It is not automatically a maintained [[市值加权指数|capitalization-weighted index]], which also requires constituent, float and [[指数除数调整|divisor]] rules. The [[Fisher价格指数|Fisher index]] combines the two basket perspectives symmetrically.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
