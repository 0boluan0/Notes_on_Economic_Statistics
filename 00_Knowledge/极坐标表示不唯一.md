# Non-unique polar coordinates
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*极坐标表示不唯一*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Different coordinate instructions can end at the same point. Turning an extra full circle changes the angle written on the page but not its direction. Reversing both the chosen direction and the radial sign also leaves the point unchanged. This is a property of the [[极坐标|polar coordinate map]] $(r,\theta)\mapsto(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)$, not an error in converting coordinates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

不同的坐标指令可以到达同一个点。多转一整圈会改变纸面上的角度数值，却不改变方向；把所选方向和径向符号同时反转，也不改变所得点。这是[[极坐标]]映射 $(r,\theta)\mapsto(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta)$ 本身的性质，不是换算出错。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## All representations of a non-origin point
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*非原点的全部等价表示*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose $r\ne0$. For every integer $k$, the following polar pairs represent the same Cartesian point:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $r\ne0$。对每个整数 $k$，下面的极坐标对表示同一个直角坐标点：

$$(r,\theta+2k\pi),\qquad (-r,\theta+(2k+1)\pi).$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first family adds full turns. In the second, adding an odd multiple of $\pi$ reverses the unit direction, and changing $r$ to $-r$ reverses it again. For example, $(-2,\pi/3)$ and $(2,4\pi/3)$ both give $(-1,-\sqrt3)$. The coordinate pairs are different; their images in the plane are equal.

These are **all** the possibilities. If $(s,\phi)$ represents the same non-origin point, its distance from the origin gives $|s|=|r|$. Thus $s=r$ or $s=-r$. In the first case the unit directions are equal, so $\phi-\theta$ is an integer multiple of $2\pi$. In the second they are opposite, so the angle difference is an odd multiple of $\pi$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一族增加整圈角度。第二族增加奇数倍的 $\pi$，先把单位方向反转，再将 $r$ 改为 $-r$，又反转一次。例如，$(-2,\pi/3)$ 与 $(2,4\pi/3)$ 都给出 $(-1,-\sqrt3)$。坐标对不同，映到平面上的点相同。

而且这就是**全部**可能。若 $(s,\phi)$ 表示同一个非原点，比较到原点的距离就有 $|s|=|r|$，因此 $s=r$ 或 $s=-r$。前一种情形中单位方向相同，故 $\phi-\theta$ 是 $2\pi$ 的整数倍；后一种情形中方向相反，角度差就是 $\pi$ 的奇数倍。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the point $(1,-1)$, three valid pairs are $(\sqrt2,7\pi/4)$, $(\sqrt2,-\pi/4)$, and $(-\sqrt2,3\pi/4)$. The last pair chooses a second-quadrant ray but moves backwards, ending in the fourth quadrant. Checking $r\cos\theta$ and $r\sin\theta$ is a reliable way to verify a proposed representation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

点 $(1,-1)$ 的三组合法表示是 $(\sqrt2,7\pi/4)$、$(\sqrt2,-\pi/4)$、$(-\sqrt2,3\pi/4)$。最后一组先选第二象限的射线，再反向移动，因此落在第四象限。检查 $r\cos\theta$ 与 $r\sin\theta$，可以直接验证一组候选表示。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choosing one representative
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*人为选定唯一代表*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For each **non-origin** point, requiring $r>0$ and $0\le\theta<2\pi$ gives exactly one representative. More generally, any half-open angular interval of length $2\pi$ works. A half-open interval includes one endpoint and excludes the other: the two endpoint directions coincide, so keeping both would duplicate a ray, while excluding both would omit it.

The origin is different. Every pair $(0,\theta)$ represents it, including every angle in the chosen half-open interval. One may additionally assign angle zero to the origin as a bookkeeping convention, but this angle is not determined by the geometry. Merely imposing $r\ge0$ and a one-turn angle range does not make the origin's angle unique.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对每个**非原点**，规定 $r>0$ 且 $0\le\theta<2\pi$，就恰好得到一组代表。更一般地，任意长度为 $2\pi$ 的半开角区间都可以。半开意味着只包含一侧端点：两个端点的方向相同，若两端都保留，会重复一条射线；若两端都排除，又会漏掉它。

原点不同。每一组 $(0,\theta)$ 都表示原点，包括所选半开区间中的每个角度。可以额外规定原点的角度为零，作为记账规则，但这个角度不是几何位置决定的。仅要求 $r\ge0$ 并限制一圈角度，并不能使原点的极角唯一。

> [!warning] Equal points do not make two parameter descriptions identical · 点相同不等于参数描述相同
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A canonical coordinate convention chooses one label per point. It does not determine the order or number of visits in a curve. When $r=f(\theta)$, restrict or transform the parameter only after checking the resulting Cartesian map and interval; [[极坐标曲线的覆盖与重描|curve coverage and retracing]] require that additional information.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 坐标规范只为每个点选择一个标签，不决定曲线经过各点的顺序和次数。对 $r=f(\theta)$，限制或变换参数之前，要核对所得直角坐标映射与区间；[[极坐标曲线的覆盖与重描]]还需要这些信息。
