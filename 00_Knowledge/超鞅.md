<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# Supermartingale
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*超鞅*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **supermartingale** is a process whose conditional average future value is no greater than its current value. Using the information available now, its predicted average change is nonpositive. The word “super” refers to the current value being an upper bound for the future conditional average; it does not mean that the process grows or that each future outcome is smaller.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**超鞅**是一种过程：给定当前信息后，未来值的条件平均不大于当前值，因此预测的平均变化非正。“超”对应的是当前值位于未来条件平均的上方，并不表示过程增长，也不表示每个未来结果都更小。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A game with an unfavourable average change
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

> [!example] A downward conditional average can include a gain · 条件均值向下，单步仍可能盈利
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Start at $X_0=0$. At each independent step, gain $1$ with probability $1/4$ and lose $1$ with probability $3/4$. After seeing the past, the next increment still has mean $1/4-3/4=-1/2$. If the current total is $x$, then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 从 $X_0=0$ 开始，每一步独立地以概率 $1/4$ 收益 $1$，以概率 $3/4$ 损失 $1$。看过历史之后，下一步增量的均值仍为 $1/4-3/4=-1/2$。若当前累计值为 $x$，则
>
> $$\frac14(x+1)+\frac34(x-1)=x-\frac12\le x.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> There is still probability $1/4$ of going up by one. Such a rise does not contradict the supermartingale condition. At each fixed time, $|X_n|\le n$, so integrability is automatic.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 仍有 $1/4$ 的概率上升一单位，这并不违背超鞅条件。每个固定时刻都有 $|X_n|\le n$，所以可积性自动满足。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same calculation covers independent integrable increments $\xi_k$ having a common mean $\mu\le0$, even when their distributions differ. Set $X_0=0$, $X_n=\sum_{k=1}^n\xi_k$, and use the [[自然滤过|natural filtration]] $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$. The next increment is independent of the known history, giving $E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=X_n+\mu\le X_n$. The finite sum is integrable and known at time $n$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一计算适用于相互独立的可积增量 $\xi_k$，只要共有均值 $\mu\le0$，不必同分布。令 $X_0=0$、$X_n=\sum_{k=1}^n\xi_k$，使用[[自然滤过]] $\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)$。下一步增量独立于已知历史，于是 $E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]=X_n+\mu\le X_n$；有限和可积，而且在时刻 $n$ 已知。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The condition compares predictions made from the same information
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*用同一信息比较当前值与未来均值*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[随机过程|stochastic process]] $X=(X_t)$ records values at different times on one probability space. Its [[滤过定义|filtration]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ specifies the information available over time, with $\mathcal F_s\subseteq\mathcal F_t$ for $s\le t$. [[适应过程|Adaptedness]] means $X_t$ is $\mathcal F_t$-measurable: the current information can determine the current value. The [[条件期望|conditional expectation]] $E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]$ averages the later value while retaining the earlier information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[随机过程]] $X=(X_t)$ 在同一概率空间上记录各时刻的值。[[滤过定义|滤过]] $(\mathcal F_t)$ 指定随时间可用的信息，满足 $s\le t$ 时 $\mathcal F_s\subseteq\mathcal F_t$。[[适应过程|适应性]]表示 $X_t$ 对 $\mathcal F_t$ 可测，即当前信息可以确定当前值。[[条件期望]] $E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]$ 则保留较早信息，对较晚的值求平均。

> [!important] Supermartingale relative to the given filtration · 相对于指定滤过的超鞅
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A real adapted process $X$ is a supermartingale with respect to $(\mathcal F_t)$ when $E|X_t|<\infty$ at every time and, for each fixed $s\le t$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $$\boxed{E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]\le X_s\quad\text{almost surely}.}$$
>
> 实值适应过程 $X$ 相对于 $(\mathcal F_t)$ 是超鞅，是指每个时刻都有 $E|X_t|<\infty$，且每对固定的 $s\le t$ 都满足上式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The requirement $E|X_t|<\infty$ is [[期望|integrability]], imposed at each time; it does not demand a bound uniform over time or a finite variance. “Almost surely” allows a probability-zero exceptional event for each fixed time pair. The values themselves may be positive or negative. Equality is allowed, so every [[鞅定义|martingale]] is a supermartingale. Changing the filtration can change this property even when the random values remain the same.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$E|X_t|<\infty$ 是逐时点的[[期望|可积性]]要求，不要求对所有时间统一有界，也不要求方差有限。“几乎必然”允许每对固定时刻对应的零概率例外。过程值本身可以正，也可以负；不等式允许等号，所以每个[[鞅定义|鞅]]都是超鞅。即使随机值不变，改变滤过也可能改变超鞅性质。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## One-step losses in conditional mean
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 离散时间的一步判据

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At integer times, after establishing adaptedness and integrability, it suffices to check either equivalent condition below for every $n$, almost surely:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在整数时间，确认适应性与可积性之后，逐期检查以下任一等价条件即可；它们对每个 $n$ 都在几乎必然意义下陈述：

$$
E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]\le X_n
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
E[X_{n+1}-X_n\mid\mathcal F_n]\le0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[塔式法则|tower property]] and preservation of inequalities by conditional expectation extend this to longer horizons. The first extension is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[塔式法则]]与条件期望的保序性把一步条件延长到更远未来。先看两步：

$$
\begin{aligned}
E[X_{n+2}\mid\mathcal F_n]
&=E\!\left[E[X_{n+2}\mid\mathcal F_{n+1}]\mid\mathcal F_n\right]\\
&\le E[X_{n+1}\mid\mathcal F_n]\le X_n
\quad\text{a.s.}
\end{aligned}
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeating the argument gives every finite number of steps. This is a discrete-time criterion; continuous time has no “next” real time, so checking one chosen grid does not by itself verify the full continuous-time definition. Independence is useful in the opening example but is not required by the definition.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重复即可覆盖任意有限步数。这是离散时间判据；连续时间不存在“下一个”实数时刻，因此只检查某个选定网格，本身并不能验证完整的连续时间定义。独立性在开头例子中方便使用，但不是定义的要求。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Reversing the sign reverses the comparison
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*取负号会反转比较方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Conditional expectation is linear, so the defining inequality gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

条件期望是线性的，因此定义中的不等式给出

$$E[-X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]=-E[X_t\mid\mathcal F_s]\ge -X_s\quad\text{a.s.}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus $X$ is a supermartingale exactly when $-X$ is a [[次鞅|submartingale]] relative to the same filtration; adaptedness and integrability survive negation. A process that is both a submartingale and a supermartingale for the same filtration has equality, and is a martingale. Taking ordinary expectations also shows $E[X_t]\le E[X_s]$, but a decreasing unconditional mean alone does not test every available-information state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以，$X$ 是超鞅，当且仅当 $-X$ 是相对于同一滤过的[[次鞅]]，因为取负号仍保留适应性和可积性。对同一滤过既是次鞅又是超鞅，就同时有两个方向的不等式，因而是鞅。再取普通期望可得 $E[X_t]\le E[X_s]$，但普通均值下降本身并没有检查每一种已知信息状态。

> [!question]- Does one upward step disprove the property? · 一次上升能否否定超鞅？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> No. The opening model rises with probability $1/4$ at each step while its conditional expected increment is $-1/2$. To test the definition, compare the conditional future average with the current value using the specified filtration, rather than inspecting the direction of a single realised path.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 不能。开头模型每步以概率 $1/4$ 上升，而条件平均增量为 $-1/2$。检查定义时，应使用指定滤过比较未来条件平均与当前值，不能只看某条实际路径的一步方向。
