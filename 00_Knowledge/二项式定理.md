# Binomial theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*二项式定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The binomial theorem tells us what appears when a sum of two terms is raised to a nonnegative integer power. It gives both the powers and their coefficients—the numbers multiplying them—without requiring us to write every intermediate product.

Start with two factors. The distributive law selects one term from each factor:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
二项式定理说明：把两个项的和取非负整数次幂，展开后会出现哪些幂，以及各自的系数——乘在它们前面的数。它让我们不必写出所有中间乘积，也能得到这些系数。

先看两个因子。分配律让我们从每个因子中各选一项：

$$
(a+b)^2=(a+b)(a+b)=aa+ab+ba+bb=a^2+2ab+b^2.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For ordinary real or complex numbers, $ab=ba$, so the two middle choices produce the same product. The coefficient $2$ records the two ways to choose which factor contributes $b$. A coefficient is therefore a count of choices that merge into one term.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
对普通实数或复数，有 $ab=ba$，所以中间两种选择得到相同的乘积。系数 $2$ 记录的是：可以有两种方式指定哪个因子贡献 $b$。因此，一个系数就是合并成同一项的选择数量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choosing the positions that contribute the second term
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*选择哪些位置贡献第二项*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $n\ge0$ be an integer. For $n\ge1$, regard $(a+b)^n$ as a product of $n$ labelled factor positions. To get a term with exactly $k$ copies of $b$ and $n-k$ copies of $a$, choose $k$ of those positions for $b$; all remaining positions contribute $a$. The [[组合数|binomial coefficient]] $\binom nk$ counts that position subset. We may repeatedly choose the symbol $b$, but each factor position is used only once.

Every expanded choice has exactly one value of $k$, from $0$ to $n$. Collecting all choices with the same value of $k$ gives the theorem.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
设 $n\ge0$ 是整数。$n\ge1$ 时，把 $(a+b)^n$ 看成 $n$ 个有不同位置的因子的乘积。要得到恰好 $k$ 个 $b$ 和 $n-k$ 个 $a$，就选 $k$ 个位置贡献 $b$，其余位置都贡献 $a$。[[组合数]] $\binom nk$ 数的正是这个位置子集。符号 $b$ 可以多次被选中，但每个因子位置只使用一次。

展开中的每一种选择都恰好对应一个 $k$，范围从 $0$ 到 $n$。把 $k$ 相同的选择合并，就得到定理。

> [!important] Binomial expansion / 二项式展开
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a nonnegative integer $n$ and real or complex $a,b$,
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 对非负整数 $n$ 以及实数或复数 $a,b$，
>
> $$
> \boxed{(a+b)^n=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom nk a^{n-k}b^k}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The summation sign adds one term for each integer $k=0,1,\ldots,n$. At $k=0$ all factors contribute $a$; at $k=n$ all contribute $b$, so both endpoint coefficients are $1$. For $n=0$, this is the constant polynomial $1$: there is one empty product and no factor choices. Zeroth powers in this polynomial identity denote empty products, including when a variable is later set to zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 求和符号表示把整数 $k=0,1,\ldots,n$ 对应的各项相加。$k=0$ 时全部选 $a$；$k=n$ 时全部选 $b$，因此两个端点的系数都是 $1$。$n=0$ 时得到常数多项式 $1$：只有一个空乘积，没有因子需要选择。这个多项式恒等式中的零次幂按空乘积解释，即使之后把变量代成零也是如此。

> [!example] Finding one coefficient / 只求一个系数
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $(x+2)^4$, a term containing $x^2$ must choose $x$ from two factors and $2$ from the other two. There are $\binom42=6$ choices of the two constant positions, each contributing $2^2x^2$. Hence the coefficient of $x^2$ is $6\cdot4=24$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 在 $(x+2)^4$ 中，要得到含 $x^2$ 的项，必须从两个因子选 $x$，另外两个因子选 $2$。选那两个常数位置有 $\binom42=6$ 种方式，每种贡献 $2^2x^2$，因此 $x^2$ 的系数是 $6\cdot4=24$。
>
> $$
> (x+2)^4=x^4+8x^3+24x^2+32x+16.
> $$

## Useful substitutions and boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*有用的代入与适用边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Setting $a=b=1$ gives $\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom nk=2^n$. This also counts all subsets of $n$ objects by their sizes. Setting $a=1,b=-1$ gives $\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-1)^k\binom nk=0$ for $n\ge1$: the even-size and odd-size subset counts cancel. At $n=0$, that alternating sum has just the term $1$, so the same zero conclusion does not apply.

The rows of coefficients can be constructed with [[Pascal恒等式|Pascal's identity]]. For sums with more than two terms, the analogous expansion is the [[多项式定理|multinomial theorem]].

The finite formula above assumes a nonnegative integer exponent. Negative or noninteger exponents require other expansion results and their convergence conditions. It also uses commutative multiplication: for matrices $A,B$ that do not commute, the square contains $AB+BA$, which cannot in general be replaced by $2AB$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
令 $a=b=1$，得到 $\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom nk=2^n$；这也可以理解为按大小分类，数完 $n$ 个对象的全部子集。令 $a=1,b=-1$，当 $n\ge1$ 时得到 $\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-1)^k\binom nk=0$，即偶数大小与奇数大小的子集数量相互抵消。$n=0$ 时这个交替和只有一项 $1$，不能沿用等于零的结论。

各行系数可以用 [[Pascal恒等式]]逐行构造。若相加的项不止两个，对应的展开见[[多项式定理]]。

这里的有限公式要求指数是非负整数。负指数或非整数指数需要另外的展开定理以及相应的收敛条件。公式也使用了乘法交换性：若矩阵 $A,B$ 不可交换，平方展开中会出现 $AB+BA$，一般不能把它改成 $2AB$。
