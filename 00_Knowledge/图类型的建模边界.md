# 图的有向性、重边、自环与权重必须由模型显式声明
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Direction, parallel edges, self-loops, and weights must be declared explicitly*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子区分
> - **有向图（directed graph）：**边 $(u,v)$ 是有序对，$u\to v$ 不自动给出 $v\to u$。
> - **无向图（undirected graph）：**边 $\{u,v\}$ 没有方向，交换端点不产生新边。
> - **简单图（simple graph）：**通常指无自环、同一对端点间无平行边的图；本地 6.042J 对 simple graph 还要求无向。
> - **多重图（multigraph）：**允许同一对端点之间有多条可区分的边。
> - **加权图（weighted graph）：**给边附数值；权重可能是距离、时间、成本、容量或相似度，运算法则必须随语义声明。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> - A **directed graph** uses ordered pairs $(u,v)$; $u\to v$ does not imply $v\to u$.
> - An **undirected graph** uses unordered endpoint pairs $\{u,v\}$.
> - A **simple graph** has no self-loops or parallel edges; in the local 6.042J text, “simple graph” is also specifically undirected.
> - A **multigraph** permits distinguishable parallel edges between the same endpoints.
> - A **weighted graph** attaches a number to an edge. Whether it means distance, time, cost, capacity, or similarity determines how it may be used.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些形容词回答不同问题，不能互相替代。directed/undirected 规定端点次序；weighted/unweighted 规定边是否携带额外数值。simple 同时排除 self-loops 与 parallel edges；一旦放宽到 multigraph 或更一般的图类，必须分别声明重边和自环是否允许。一个图可以同时是 directed、weighted、并允许 parallel edges。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These adjectives answer different questions. Directed versus undirected concerns endpoint order, while weighted versus unweighted concerns attached values. A simple graph excludes both self-loops and parallel edges. Once the model is generalized to a multigraph or another broader graph class, permission for parallel edges and self-loops must be stated separately. A graph may be directed, weighted, and permit parallel edges at the same time.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 定理适用边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Boundary of theorem use*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 简单无向图中的 cycle 至少有三条边；若允许两条平行边，它们可能构成长度为 $2$ 的 cycle；若允许 self-loop，还可能有长度为 $1$ 的 cycle。
- 无向 simple graph 的 degree 等于邻居数；multigraph 中 degree 计的是 incident edge occurrences，二者不再必然相等。
- “最少经过几条边”与“总权重最小”是两个目标。等权或无权图的 BFS 结论不能默默推广到任意权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->

**1.** A cycle in a simple undirected graph has at least three edges. Parallel edges may form a two-cycle, and a self-loop may form a one-cycle under conventions that admit them.<br>
**2.** In a simple undirected graph, degree equals the number of neighbors. In a multigraph, degree counts incident edge occurrences, so the two quantities can differ.<br>
**3.** Minimizing the number of edges and minimizing total weight are different objectives. The BFS guarantee for unweighted or equal-weight graphs does not extend silently to arbitrary weights.<br>
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 两座城市之间有三班不同航班。若航班是边，为什么 simple graph 会丢信息？只给这条城市对连一条权重为 3 的边能否总是修复？
>
> **答案：**三班航班可能有不同时间、票价或容量，simple graph 会把它们合并。权重 3 只保留“数量”，不能恢复每班航班的属性，也不能支持逐航班选择；需要 parallel edges，或把“航班”本身纳入状态模型。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Three different flights connect the same pair of cities. Why does a simple graph lose information? Does replacing them by one edge of weight three always repair the loss?
>
> **Answer:** The flights may differ in departure time, fare, or capacity, which a simple graph collapses. A weight of three records only the count and cannot support choosing a particular flight. Use parallel edges or put the flight identity into the state model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
