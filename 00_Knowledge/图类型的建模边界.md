# Graph-type modeling boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*图类型的建模边界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A picture of two cities joined by a line leaves several questions unanswered. Is travel allowed in both directions? Does the line stand for one flight or several distinct flights? Is its label a fare, a duration or a capacity? **Graph type** makes these structural choices explicit after [[图模型语义|the objects and edge meanings]] have been chosen. Direction, parallel edges, self-loops and weights are different choices; one adjective does not settle all four.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
两个城市之间画一条线，还没有回答几个问题：能否双向通行？这条线代表一班航班，还是多班可区分的航班？旁边的数字是票价、时间，还是容量？在确定[[图模型语义|对象与边的含义]]之后，**图类型**把这些结构选择说明白。方向、平行边、自环和权重是不同选择，不能用一个形容词代替全部说明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use $G=(V,E)$, with vertex set $V$ and edge set $E$, together with the endpoint information for each edge. The examples below are finite. A theorem may additionally require $V$ to be nonempty: the local MIT MCS definitions do so, whereas allowing $V=\varnothing$ is an explicitly stated extension, as in [[森林|the empty forest]]. A nonempty vertex set with no edges is a different case: it contains isolated vertices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
用 $G=(V,E)$ 表示图，$V$ 是顶点集合，$E$ 是边集合，并为每条边记录端点。本卡的例子都是有限图。具体定理还可能要求 $V$ 非空：本地 MIT MCS 的图定义有这个要求，而允许 $V=\varnothing$ 是需要明确说明的扩展，例如[[森林|空森林]]。顶点非空而没有边则是另一种情形，它含有孤立顶点。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Direction describes endpoint roles
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*方向规定端点角色*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a **directed graph**, each edge has a tail and a head. Without parallel edges, the ordered pair $(u,v)$ identifies the edge $u\to v$: $u$ is the tail and $v$ the head. Reversing the order gives a different possible edge, and $u\to v$ alone does not assert that $v\to u$ exists. In a transition model, a directed [[游走路径与环|walk]] must follow these arrows.

An **undirected edge** has endpoints with no chosen order. When they are distinct, the two-element set $\{u,v\}$ identifies their pair; exchanging $u$ and $v$ does not create another edge. Such an edge can be traversed either way. Whether traversal represents travel at all still depends on the model: a conflict edge expresses a constraint, not a transport connection.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**有向图**的每条边有起点和终点。不允许平行边时，有序对 $(u,v)$ 可以标识边 $u\to v$：$u$ 是起点，$v$ 是终点。颠倒次序得到另一条可能的边；单有 $u\to v$ 不表示 $v\to u$ 也存在。在状态转换模型中，有向[[游走路径与环|游走]]必须沿这些箭头前进。

**无向边**的端点没有先后次序。端点不同时，二元集合 $\{u,v\}$ 标识这对端点；交换 $u,v$ 不产生另一条边。这样的边可以双向走过。至于“走过”是否有实际交通含义，仍取决于模型：冲突边表示约束，并非运输通道。

> [!note] Reference arrows on an undirected graph / 无向图上的参考箭头
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A signed [[关联矩阵势差映射|incidence matrix]] gives each undirected edge an arbitrary reference arrow to choose signs. For a non-loop edge $u\to v$ with $u\ne v$, its row has $-1$ at $u$ and $+1$ at $v$. Reversing that reference reverses the row and the sign of the same physical flow's coordinate. It does not turn a two-way connection into a one-way road.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 带符号[[关联矩阵势差映射|关联矩阵]]为每条无向边任取参考箭头来约定正负号。对非自环边 $u\to v$（$u\ne v$），该行在 $u$ 列写 $-1$，在 $v$ 列写 $+1$。反转参考方向会把这一行及同一物理流的坐标符号反转，并不会把双向连接变成单行道。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Distinguish an edge from its endpoints
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*边本身与端点组合并不总是同一回事*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **self-loop** has the same vertex at both ends. **Parallel edges** are distinct edges with the same endpoints; in a directed model this means the same ordered tail–head pair. Thus $u\to v$ and $v\to u$, with $u\ne v$, are not parallel directed edges.

A **multigraph** allows parallel edges, but authors differ on whether that word also allows self-loops. State the loop convention separately. If two flights $e_1,e_2$ both go from $u$ to $v$, keep their edge identities as well as their endpoints. A set of ordered pairs alone would store $(u,v)$ just once and lose the distinction. An endpoint assignment to separately identified edges resolves this without treating a set as if it stored duplicate elements.

A **simple graph** has no loops and no parallel edges. MIT MCS uses the term specifically for undirected graphs; MIT 6.006 also speaks of simple directed graphs. In the latter convention, opposite directed edges are allowed, but neither a self-loop nor a duplicate edge in the same direction is allowed. Check which convention a theorem uses.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**自环**的两个端点是同一个顶点。**平行边**是端点相同、但本身可以区分的多条边；在有向模型中，这意味着起点与终点组成的有序对相同。因此 $u\ne v$ 时，$u\to v$ 和 $v\to u$ 不是有向平行边。

**多重图**允许平行边，但不同作者对这个词是否同时允许自环有不同约定，必须单独声明。若两班航班 $e_1,e_2$ 都从 $u$ 飞到 $v$，就要同时保留边的身份及其端点。仅用有序对集合只会把 $(u,v)$ 存一次，丢掉两班航班的区别。给分别标识的边指定端点，就能避免把集合误当成可以重复存放同一元素的结构。

**简单图**没有自环和平行边。MIT MCS 专指无向的简单图；MIT 6.006 也使用有向简单图这一说法。后一约定允许反向的两条有向边，但不允许自环或同方向重复边。使用定理前应确认它采用哪一种约定。

## Weights need a meaning and a range
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*权重需要含义与取值范围*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **weighted graph** assigns a value $w(e)$ to each edge $e$; $w(e)$ means “the weight of this edge.” For real weights, this is a function $w:E\to\mathbb R$. State the units and permitted values. Distance and cost may be added along a route, whereas route capacity may instead be limited by its smallest edge capacity. Weighted does not itself mean nonnegative, and an undirected graph can be weighted just as a directed one can.

A zero-weight edge is still an edge if it belongs to $E$. Zero travel time permits a free transition in a travel-time model. Zero conductance contributes no connection to the positive-conductance support used by a [[加权图 Laplacian|weighted graph Laplacian]]. Thus an edge's presence and its effective role in a particular weighted operator must be distinguished. A storage scheme must also distinguish an absent edge from a permitted zero weight; see [[邻接表与邻接矩阵|graph representations]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**加权图**为每条边 $e$ 指定一个数值 $w(e)$，它表示“这条边的权重”。若权重为实数，就是一个函数 $w:E\to\mathbb R$。需要说明单位与允许的取值。距离和成本可能沿路线相加，路线容量则可能取决于各边容量中的最小值。“加权”本身不表示权重非负，无向图也可以像有向图一样带权。

若零权边属于 $E$，它仍是一条边。在旅行时间模型中，零时间意味着一次不耗时的转换；而零电导不会成为[[加权图 Laplacian|加权图 Laplacian]]所用正电导支撑图中的连接。因此，边是否存在与它是否对某个加权算子产生作用，需要区分。存储时也必须分清没有边与合法的零权重，见[[邻接表与邻接矩阵]]。

## Check the convention behind a theorem
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 定理适用边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Cycle length.** Under the undirected [[游走路径与环|cycle]] convention used here, edges are distinct and vertices do not repeat except at the closing endpoint. A simple undirected cycle therefore needs at least three edges. Two distinct parallel edges can form a two-cycle; a self-loop can form a one-cycle when loops are permitted. Going out and back on the very same undirected edge repeats that edge and is not a cycle.

**Degree.** In an undirected simple graph, [[度数、入度与出度|degree]] equals the number of neighbors. With two parallel edges to one other vertex, there is one neighbor but two edge incidences. Under the usual undirected endpoint-incidence convention, a loop contributes two to degree. A directed loop contributes one to in-degree and one to out-degree. These are edge counts, not sums of edge weights.

**Shortest route.** [[BFS 分层与无权最短路|Breadth-first search]] minimizes edge count. If every edge has the same positive cost $c$, a route of $k$ edges costs $kc$, so minimizing either gives the same result. Equal zero costs make every route cost zero; equal negative costs do not give this minimization equivalence. With unequal costs, a one-edge route of cost $10$ can lose to a two-edge route costing $1+1=2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
**环长。** 本卡的无向[[游走路径与环|环]]约定要求边不重复，顶点除首尾闭合外不重复。因此简单无向环至少需要三条边。两条不同平行边可以形成二环，允许自环时自环可以形成一环。沿同一条无向边走出去又原路返回重复了边，不算环。

**度数。** 在无向简单图中，[[度数、入度与出度|度数]]等于邻居数。两条平行边连接同一个邻点时，邻居只有一个，但边关联有两个。采用通常的无向端点关联计数时，自环对度数贡献二；有向自环对入度、出度各贡献一。这些是边的计数，不是权重和。

**最短路线。** [[BFS 分层与无权最短路|广度优先搜索]]最小化边数。若每条边的成本都是同一正数 $c$，经过 $k$ 条边的路线成本为 $kc$，最小化二者等价。若成本同为零，所有路线的成本都为零；若同为负数，则没有这种最小化等价。成本不同时，一条成本 $10$ 的直达边可能不如成本 $1+1=2$ 的两边路线。

> [!question]- Can three flights become one edge of weight three? / 三班航班能压成一条权重为三的边吗？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Only for a question that needs their count and no lost details. Three flights may have different departure times, fares and capacities. A single weight of three cannot recover those attributes or identify which flight was selected. Keep parallel edges with individual attributes, or include flight/time information in the vertex states when the scheduling question requires it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
> 只有问题仅需数量、不需被丢掉的细节时才可以。三班航班可能有不同的出发时间、票价和容量。一个权重三无法恢复这些属性，也无法标识最终选择了哪一班。可以保留带各自属性的平行边；若时刻安排问题需要，也可以把航班或时间信息放进顶点状态。
