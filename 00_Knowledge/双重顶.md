# 双重顶是上涨后两次近同高试探并跌破中间谷位的形态
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A double top forms after an advance when two tests of a similar high are followed by a break below the intervening trough.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

双重顶是在先前上涨之后，价格两次到达近似同一高位、两峰之间有一次回落，并在第二峰之后向下突破中间谷位的反转形态。两峰构成近似 M 形轮廓；只有轮廓时仍是候选，突破中间 [[支撑位]]才满足这里采用的完成条件。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A double top follows an advance: two peaks reach approximately the same high, with a trough between them, and price subsequently breaks below that trough. The M-shaped outline is only a candidate; breaking the intervening [[支撑位|support]] completes the pattern under this convention.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“近同高”、峰谷的识别窗口及突破采用盘中价还是收盘价，都须事前固定。第二峰出现不等于之后必跌；跌破后的反弹回试也不是必经步骤。教材图中的 E 是已实现路径上的回试，不能事前称为保证存在的“最后退出机会”。订单与收益仍按 [[交易信号时序]]处理。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix the peak-height tolerance, turning-point window and intraday-versus-close breakout convention in advance. A second peak does not ensure a decline, and a retest after breakdown need not occur. Point E in the course figure depicts a realised retest, not a guaranteed final exit opportunity. Orders and returns still follow [[交易信号时序|signal and execution timing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最小对照：已识别两峰均为 100，中间谷为 94。若事前选择“收盘严格低于谷位、无额外缓冲”，第二峰后收盘 94 尚未确认，93 才确认。这里确认的是形态条件，不是此后收益的符号。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For identified peaks at 100 and an intervening trough at 94, suppose the rule requires a close strictly below the trough without an extra buffer. A subsequent close at 94 does not confirm; 93 does. This confirms a pattern condition, not the sign of later returns.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
