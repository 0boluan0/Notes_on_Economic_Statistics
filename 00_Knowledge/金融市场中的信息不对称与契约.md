# 金融市场中的信息不对称与契约
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Asymmetric Information and Contracting in Financial Markets*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 解释资金提供者看不清借款人的真实类型和后续行动时，为什么市场会出现信贷配给，以及抵押、净值、限制性条款和金融中介为什么有用。
> **具体锚点：** 一家银行把贷款利率从 8% 提到 15%，安全项目退出而高风险项目留下；银行即使仍有人愿意借，也可能宁愿拒贷。
> **核心难点：** 要先区分签约前的隐藏类型（逆向选择）与签约后的隐藏行动（道德风险），再判断一种契约究竟改变了信息、筛选还是激励。
> **为什么重要：** 它说明“价格提高就能清空市场”在信贷市场可能失效，也解释了金融结构为何不是一组任意的制度安排。
> **继续：** 若要看这些摩擦怎样经资产价格、净值和挤兑被放大，进入 [[金融危机、挤兑与金融加速器]]；若要量化单笔贷款风险，进入 [[信用风险、CDS 与信用 VaR|信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It explains why credit rationing can arise when fund providers cannot observe borrowers' true types or subsequent actions, and why collateral, net worth, covenants, and financial intermediaries can help.
> **Concrete anchor:** A bank raises its lending rate from 8% to 15%; safe projects leave while risky projects remain. The bank may then refuse additional loans even though some borrowers are still willing to borrow.
> **Central difficulty:** First distinguish hidden types before contracting (adverse selection) from hidden actions after contracting (moral hazard), then ask whether a contract changes information, screening, or incentives.
> **Why it matters:** It shows why a higher price need not clear a credit market and why financial structure is not an arbitrary collection of institutions.
> **Continue:** To see how these frictions are amplified through asset prices, net worth, and runs, open [[金融危机、挤兑与金融加速器|Financial Crises, Runs, and the Financial Accelerator]]. To quantify risk on an individual loan, open [[信用风险、CDS 与信用 VaR|Credit Risk: PD, LGD, EAD, and Rating Migration]].

## 先定位问题：资金双方知道的并不一样
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Locate the problem first: the two sides do not know the same things*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

借款人通常比出资人更清楚两件事：项目在签约前究竟有多好，以及拿到钱后究竟会怎样行动。前者是隐藏类型，后者是隐藏行动。信息不对称本身不等于市场必然失败；真正要追踪的是，它怎样改变参与者构成、可选行动和最终收益。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Borrowers usually know two things better than fund providers do: how good the project really is before contracting, and what they will do after receiving the funds. The former is a hidden type; the latter is a hidden action. Asymmetric information does not by itself imply inevitable market failure. The key is to trace how it changes who participates, which actions are available, and the resulting payoffs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

判断题目时可以按时间切开：若问题是“谁愿意签约、谁被吸引进来”，先查逆向选择；若问题是“签约后为什么改变行为”，先查道德风险。同一制度可能同时回应两者，但机制必须分别说清楚。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When diagnosing a problem, split it by timing. If the question is who is willing to contract or which types are attracted, begin with adverse selection. If the question is why behavior changes after contracting, begin with moral hazard. One institution may address both, but the mechanisms must be stated separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 逆向选择
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Adverse selection*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交易前，价格/利率会改变参与者类型构成。投资者无法区分项目质量时，好项目可能退出，市场萎缩。筛选、信用评分、抵押、自有资金和关系金融用可观察信号缓解，但也可能排除缺乏资产的优质借款人。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before a transaction, the price or interest rate can change the composition of participating types. When investors cannot distinguish project quality, good projects may leave and the market may shrink. Screening, credit scores, collateral, borrower equity, and relationship finance use observable signals to mitigate the problem, but they may also exclude good borrowers who lack assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么加息可能降低银行的期望收益
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why a higher lending rate can reduce a bank's expected return*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把银行每单位贷款的期望收益写成一个简化式：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the bank's expected return per unit of lending in a simplified form:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
R(i)=\Pr(\text{repay}\mid i)\,(1+i)+\Pr(\text{default}\mid i)\,\text{Recovery}-1.
$$

提高合同利率 $i$ 会提高“正常还款时”的收入，却也可能降低还款概率：安全借款人先退出，留下的借款人平均风险更高；已有借款人还可能改选上行更大、下行由银行承担更多的项目。因此 $R(i)$ 不必随 $i$ 单调上升，超过某一点后银行可能不再加息，而是限制贷款数量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Raising the contractual rate $i$ increases revenue when repayment occurs, but it may also reduce the repayment probability. Safer borrowers may exit first, leaving a riskier average pool, while remaining borrowers may switch toward projects with greater upside and more downside borne by the bank. Therefore $R(i)$ need not rise monotonically with $i$. Beyond some point, the bank may stop raising the rate and instead limit the quantity of credit.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

信贷配给不是“没有人愿意按现行利率借钱”，而是仍有外观看来相同、愿意接受该利率的申请者得不到贷款。这里未出清的原因不是价格被行政固定，而是价格本身会恶化银行面对的风险组合。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Credit rationing does not mean that nobody wants to borrow at the prevailing rate. It means that some observably similar applicants who are willing to accept that rate still do not receive loans. The market fails to clear not because the price is administratively fixed, but because the price itself worsens the risk pool facing the bank.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 道德风险与委托代理
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Moral hazard and principal–agent problems*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

融资后，借款人可能选择更高风险、转移资产或减少努力，因为损失部分由资金提供者承担。限制性契约、监控、分期融资、股权和声誉重新分配激励；没有一种契约在所有状态都完美。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
After financing, borrowers may choose greater risk, divert assets, or reduce effort because part of the loss is borne by fund providers. Covenants, monitoring, staged financing, equity, and reputation reallocate incentives; no contract is perfect in every state.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

有限责任制造了一个关键不对称：项目成功时，股东保留较多上行；项目极差时，债权人的损失更大。杠杆越高，股东越可能偏好高波动项目，即使该项目降低企业总价值。契约设计的任务不是消灭风险，而是让决策者承担更多自己选择造成的边际后果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Limited liability creates a crucial asymmetry: shareholders retain much of the upside when a project succeeds, whereas creditors bear more of the loss in very bad states. The higher the leverage, the more shareholders may prefer a high-volatility project even when it lowers total firm value. Contract design does not eliminate risk; it makes decision-makers bear more of the marginal consequences of their choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见工具分别作用于不同环节：抵押品提高违约时借款人的损失；自有资金让借款人与出资人共同承担结果；限制性条款排除资产转移或额外举债；监控提高隐藏行动被发现的概率；分期融资保留停止后续投入的选择权。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common tools act at different points. Collateral raises the borrower's loss upon default; borrower equity makes the borrower share the outcome with the fund provider; covenants restrict asset diversion or additional borrowing; monitoring raises the probability that hidden actions are detected; staged financing preserves the option to stop later funding.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 债务、股权与中介
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Debt, equity, and intermediation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

债务在正常状态只需验证违约与否，降低信息成本，但高杠杆放大风险转移；股权分享上行却需要持续治理。银行通过信息生产和委托监督克服小投资者重复成本，同时产生期限和流动性错配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In normal states, debt requires verification mainly when default occurs, reducing information costs, but high leverage magnifies risk shifting. Equity shares upside but requires continuing governance. Banks overcome the duplicated costs that small investors would face by producing information and performing delegated monitoring, while simultaneously creating maturity and liquidity mismatches.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里没有“债务一定优于股权”的一般结论。收益是否容易核验、资产能否抵押、控制权是否重要、项目何时需要再融资以及司法执行是否可靠，都会改变最合适的契约。中介也不是免费解决方案：委托银行监督后，还要监督银行自身，并处理其短债长投带来的脆弱性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is no general conclusion that debt is always superior to equity. The appropriate contract depends on whether returns are easy to verify, whether assets can be pledged, how important control rights are, when the project needs refinancing, and whether legal enforcement is reliable. Intermediation is not a free solution either: once monitoring is delegated to a bank, the bank itself must be supervised, and its vulnerability from funding long assets with short liabilities must be managed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 算例：同一利率怎样筛走安全项目
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked example: how one interest rate screens out the safe project*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设两个借款人都需要借 100。安全项目以 90% 概率支付 125、否则支付 0；风险项目以 50% 概率支付 200、否则支付 0。借款人有限责任且没有抵押。若到期需偿还 110，安全借款人的期望剩余为 $0.9\times(125-110)=13.5$，风险借款人的期望剩余为 $0.5\times(200-110)=45$，两者都申请。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose two borrowers each need 100. A safe project pays 125 with probability 90% and zero otherwise; a risky project pays 200 with probability 50% and zero otherwise. Borrowers have limited liability and no collateral. If repayment is 110, the safe borrower's expected residual is $0.9\times(125-110)=13.5$, while the risky borrower's is $0.5\times(200-110)=45$, so both apply.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若偿还额升到 130，安全项目即使成功也不足以支付合同额，借款人得不到任何剩余，因而退出；风险借款人成功时仍保留 70，期望剩余为 35，仍会申请。银行面对的申请者因此从混合池变成全是风险项目。这个例子展示的是筛选效应，不是在声称现实银行只能观察这两个结果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If repayment rises to 130, even a successful safe project cannot cover the contractual amount, leaving its borrower no residual, so that borrower exits. A successful risky project still leaves 70, for an expected residual of 35, so the risky borrower continues to apply. The bank's applicant pool therefore changes from a mixture to entirely risky projects. The example illustrates a selection effect; it does not claim that real banks observe only these two outcomes.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 失败诊断：不要把所有融资困难都叫信息不对称
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Failure diagnosis: not every financing difficulty is asymmetric information*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 若所有人都同样知道项目很差，拒贷是基本面判断，不是逆向选择。
- 若风险来自利率或宏观状态变化，而不是借款人隐藏类型或行动，应先分析市场风险或现金流风险。
- 若抵押要求使优质但贫穷的借款人退出，制度缓解了银行的信息问题，却可能造成配置与分配代价。
- 若看到“提高利率后违约更多”，还不能自动断言逆向选择；必须说明参与者构成或行为怎样随利率改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- If everyone knows equally well that a project is poor, loan rejection reflects fundamentals, not adverse selection.
- If risk comes from interest-rate or macroeconomic changes rather than hidden borrower types or actions, analyze market risk or cash-flow risk first.
- If collateral requirements exclude good but asset-poor borrowers, the institution may ease the bank's information problem while creating allocative and distributional costs.
- Observing more defaults after a rate increase is not by itself proof of adverse selection; the analysis must show how participant composition or behavior changed with the rate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从原主题看向金融危机
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The bridge from the original topic to financial crises*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> **它解决什么：** 解释资金提供者不知道借款人质量或行动时，为何需要抵押、契约、中介与监管，以及这些摩擦如何放大危机。
> **具体锚点：** 提高贷款利率可能吸引更高风险借款人并促使已有借款人冒险，银行因此可能选择信贷配给而非继续涨价。
> **核心难点：** 逆向选择发生在交易前，道德风险发生在交易后；危机放大还涉及资产负债表和流动性反馈。
> **为什么重要：** 金融结构不是纯技术安排，而是对信息、执行与系统外部性的制度回应。
> **继续：** 单家银行机制见 [[商业银行资产负债管理|商业银行经营、监管与货币供给]]；信用风险量化见 [[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|信用风险、CDS 与信用 VaR]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** It explains why collateral, contracts, intermediaries, and regulation are needed when fund providers do not know borrowers' quality or actions, and how these frictions amplify crises.
> **Concrete anchor:** Raising a lending rate may attract riskier borrowers and induce existing borrowers to take more risk, so a bank may choose credit rationing rather than another rate increase.
> **Central difficulty:** Adverse selection occurs before a transaction and moral hazard after it; crisis amplification additionally involves balance-sheet and liquidity feedback.
> **Why it matters:** Financial structure is not merely a technical arrangement but an institutional response to information, enforcement, and systemic externalities.
> **Continue:** For the mechanics of an individual bank, see [[商业银行资产负债管理|Commercial Bank Asset–Liability Management]]. For quantitative credit risk, see [[信用风险：PD、LGD、EAD 与评级迁移|Credit Risk, CDS, and Credit VaR]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 逆向选择和道德风险按时间怎样区分？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How are adverse selection and moral hazard distinguished by timing?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 逆向选择是签约前隐藏类型影响谁参与；道德风险是签约后隐藏行动影响行为。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Adverse selection concerns hidden types before contracting and affects who participates; moral hazard concerns hidden actions after contracting and affects behavior.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么提高贷款利率可能反而降低银行期望收益？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why can a higher lending rate reduce a bank's expected return?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 高利率可筛走安全借款人并诱导更冒险项目，违约风险上升可能超过利息增加。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> A higher rate can screen out safe borrowers and induce riskier projects, so the increase in default risk can outweigh the additional interest.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么抵押品既可能改善激励，也可能排除好项目？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words: why can collateral improve incentives yet exclude good projects?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 抵押品让借款人在违约时承担更多损失，能减少冒险；但项目质量和现有财富并非一回事，缺乏可抵押资产的优质借款人也可能被挡在市场外。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Collateral makes borrowers bear more loss upon default and can reduce risk-taking. But project quality is not the same as existing wealth, so good borrowers without pledgeable assets may also be excluded.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 看到银行拒绝一部分愿意支付现行利率的申请者，你还需要什么证据才能判断是信贷配给机制？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If a bank rejects some applicants willing to pay the prevailing rate, what more evidence is needed before diagnosing the credit-rationing mechanism?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 需要确认申请者在可观察特征上相近，并说明更高利率怎样改变借款人类型或行动、从而降低银行期望收益；单纯的额度约束或基本面恶化不够。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Establish that applicants are similar on observable characteristics and explain how a higher rate changes borrower types or actions enough to lower the bank's expected return. A bare quantity limit or deterioration in fundamentals is not sufficient.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
