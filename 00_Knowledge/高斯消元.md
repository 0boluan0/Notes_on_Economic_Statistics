# Gaussian elimination
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*高斯消元*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Gaussian elimination** makes a linear system easier to solve by removing an unknown from successive equations. Each replacement is reversible, so the easier equations describe exactly the same solutions. First create a staircase of leading coefficients; then work upward from its last equation. This second stage is **back-substitution**.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**高斯消元**通过逐步从方程中消去未知量，使线性方程组更容易求解。每次替换都可以撤销，因此较简单的方程描述的仍是完全相同的解。先把首个非零系数排成阶梯，再从最后一条方程向上求解；后一步称为**回代**。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Eliminate once, then substitute back
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 最小例子

$$\begin{cases}x+2y=5,\\3x+7y=16.\end{cases}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Three copies of the first equation contain the same $3x$ as the second. Subtract them from the second equation, keeping the first equation:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

第一条方程的三倍与第二条有相同的 $3x$。从第二条减去这三倍，同时保留第一条：

$$\begin{cases}x+2y=5,\\(3x+7y)-3(x+2y)=16-3\cdot5,\end{cases}
\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad
\begin{cases}x+2y=5,\\y=1.\end{cases}$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The last equation gives $y=1$. Substituting that into the first gives $x=3$. Conversely, adding three copies of the retained first equation restores the original second equation, so no solution was discarded or introduced. Direct substitution checks $3+2=5$ and $9+7=16$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

末条给出 $y=1$，代回首条得到 $x=3$。反过来，把保留下来的首条的三倍加回第二条，就能恢复原第二条，因此没有丢掉或引入解。直接代入也得到 $3+2=5$、$9+7=16$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An **augmented matrix** records the coefficients and the right-hand side in one array. The bar separates these two parts; its last column is not another unknown. With columns ordered as $x,y$, the same calculation is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**增广矩阵**把系数与右端放在同一张数表里。竖线分隔两部分，末列不是额外未知量。按 $x,y$ 排列系数列，同一次计算写成

$$\left[\begin{array}{cc|c}1&2&5\\3&7&16\end{array}\right]
\xrightarrow{\ R_2\leftarrow R_2-3R_1\ }
\left[\begin{array}{cc|c}1&2&5\\0&1&1\end{array}\right].$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$R_i$ means row $i$. The arrow means “replace row 2 using its current entries and row 1”; it is an instruction, not an equation claiming that a row equals itself minus another row. The right-hand side must undergo the same subtraction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$R_i$ 表示第 $i$ 行。箭头的意思是“用当前第二行与第一行计算新第二行”，这是替换指令，不是说某行等于自己减去另一行。右端也必须作相同的减法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Build the staircase without dividing by zero
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*建立阶梯，避免除以零*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the system as $Ax=b$: $A$ has $m$ equations as rows and $n$ unknowns as columns, $x$ collects the unknowns, and $b$ collects the right-hand sides. Here $m,n$ are positive integers and all numbers are real or complex. Three permitted row operations are swapping two rows, multiplying a row by a **nonzero** scalar, and adding a multiple of a different row to it. Each can be undone; [[行操作的解集不变性|applying it to both blocks preserves the solution set]]. An [[初等矩阵|elementary matrix]] records one such operation as multiplication.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把系统写成 $Ax=b$：$A$ 的 $m$ 行对应方程、$n$ 列对应未知量，$x$ 收集未知量，$b$ 收集右端。这里 $m,n$ 是正整数，数值取实数或复数。允许的三类行操作是交换两行、一行乘**非零**标量，以及给一行加上另一条不同行的倍数。每一步都能撤销；[[行操作的解集不变性|对左右两块同步操作就保持解集]]。一个[[初等矩阵]]可以用乘法记录一次这样的操作。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start at the first unused row and scan the coefficient columns from left to right. If the current column has a nonzero entry among the unused rows, move one such row into the current position if needed. That nonzero entry is the next [[主元|pivot]]. If the pivot is $p$ and an entry below it is $a$, subtract $a/p$ times the pivot row from that lower row. Its current-column entry becomes $a-(a/p)p=0$. Apply the operation to the entire row, including $b$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从第一条尚未处理的行开始，按从左到右的顺序检查系数列。若当前列在未处理的行中有非零元，必要时交换行，把它移到当前行；这个非零元就是下一个[[主元]]。主元为 $p$、其下某元素为 $a$ 时，从下面那行减去主元行的 $a/p$ 倍，当前列便变成 $a-(a/p)p=0$。操作作用于整行，包括右端 $b$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If every unused entry in the current coefficient column is zero, skip that column rather than divide by zero. After clearing below a pivot, move to the next row and continue to its right. Earlier pivot columns remain cleared because the rows now being combined already have zeros there. Only finitely many rows and columns can be visited, so this process terminates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若当前系数列在未处理的行中全为零，就跳过该列，而不是除以零。清除主元下方后，移到下一行，并继续检查右侧列。此前的主元列保持清零，因为现在参加组合的行在那些位置都已经为零。行列数量有限，所以过程会结束。

> [!example] A zero candidate can require a row swap · 候选位置为零时可以换行
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In $y=2$, $x+y=3$, the first coefficient of the first row is zero. Swapping the two equations gives $x+y=3$, $y=2$, hence $x=1$. The initial zero did not mean that elimination failed. A column with no available nonzero entry is skipped, as in the system $y+2z=3$ with unknowns $x,y,z$: the column for $x$ has no pivot.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $y=2$、$x+y=3$，第一行首系数为零。交换两条方程后成为 $x+y=3$、$y=2$，从而 $x=1$。最初的零不表示消元失败。若一列确实没有可用非零元，则跳过；例如以 $x,y,z$ 为未知量的系统 $y+2z=3$ 中，$x$ 对应的列没有主元。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Read what the final equations allow
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*从最终方程读出允许的解*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A row echelon coefficient matrix has zero rows at the bottom and each successive nonzero row's leading entry strictly to the right of the previous one. Entries below each leading entry are zero. The leading entries need not equal $1$, and the matrix need not be square.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

行阶梯形系数矩阵的零行在底部，下一条非零行的首个非零元严格位于上一条的右侧，每个首非零元下方都为零。首非零元不必等于 $1$，矩阵也不必是方阵。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First inspect rows with all coefficient entries zero. A remaining right-hand side $d\ne0$ means $0=d$, so there is no solution. A row $0=0$ imposes no restriction. If every such row is consistent, choose the [[自由变量|nonpivot unknowns]] freely and back-substitute from the last nonzero equation upward, dividing only by its nonzero pivot. This describes all solutions, not just one trial solution. For example, $y+2z=3$ has $(x,y,z)=(s,3-2t,t)$ for arbitrary $s,t$; adding $0=1$ would make the same coefficient pattern inconsistent.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先检查系数全为零的行。若剩余右端 $d\ne0$，就是 $0=d$，系统无解；$0=0$ 则不增加约束。若这些行都相容，就先任取[[自由变量|非主元未知量]]，再从最后一条非零方程向上回代，只除以非零主元。这样得到全部解，不只是某个试出的解。例如 $y+2z=3$ 的全部解为 $(x,y,z)=(s,3-2t,t)$，其中 $s,t$ 任意；若再加一条 $0=1$，同一个系数图案就变成无解系统。

## What this method does and does not require
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here Gaussian elimination means forward elimination followed, when the system is consistent, by back-substitution. Continuing to normalize pivots to $1$ and clear entries **above** them produces [[行最简形|reduced row echelon form]], often called Gauss–Jordan reduction. Some texts use “Gaussian elimination” for the broader row-reduction procedure; the stopping point should therefore be stated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡用高斯消元指先向下消元，再在系统相容时回代。若继续把主元化为 $1$ 并清除其**上方**的元素，就得到[[行最简形]]，常称 Gauss–Jordan 化简。有些教材把更广义的行化简也叫 Gaussian elimination，因此应说清在哪一步停止。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Elimination does not require an invertible square $A$: rectangular and singular systems still yield either a contradiction or a parameterized solution set. Exact reversibility also does not by itself prove numerical reliability. With floating-point arithmetic, a nonzero but very small pivot can create large multipliers; pivot selection and rounding require a separate [[条件性与算法稳定性|stability analysis]]. No universal numerical guarantee follows just from this algebraic algorithm.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

消元不要求 $A$ 是可逆方阵：长方阵或奇异系统仍能给出矛盾或参数化的解集。精确代数上的可逆，也不能单独证明数值计算可靠。浮点计算中，非零但很小的主元可能产生很大的乘数，选主元与舍入需要另外作[[条件性与算法稳定性|稳定性分析]]；仅靠这套代数算法不能推出普遍数值保证。

> [!question]- Why exclude scaling by zero? · 为什么不能把一行乘零？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> It erases an equation and has no inverse. Replacing $x=1$ by $0=0$ allows every $x$, so it can enlarge the solution set. Nonzero scaling can be reversed by its reciprocal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 它会抹掉一条方程，而且不能撤销。把 $x=1$ 换成 $0=0$ 后，所有 $x$ 都满足，因此解集可能扩大。非零缩放则可用倒数撤销。
