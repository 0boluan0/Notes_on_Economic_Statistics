# 优势策略均衡由每个玩家都选择一项不依赖对手选择的优势策略构成，因此它一定是纳什均衡而反向不成立
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A dominant-strategy equilibrium consists of every player choosing a strategy that is optimal regardless of the opponents' choices; it is therefore a Nash equilibrium, but the converse need not hold*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**优势策略均衡**要求每个人都能在不知道别人会怎样选时，仍确定自己的均衡策略至少不劣于任何替代策略。它比“给定别人恰好这样选，我不愿改变”的 [[纳什均衡]]要求更强。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A **dominant-strategy equilibrium** requires every player to have a strategy that remains at least as good as every alternative without knowing what the opponents will choose. This is stronger than a [[纳什均衡|Nash equilibrium]], which asks only whether a player wants to deviate given the opponents' particular equilibrium choices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 正式条件
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Formal Condition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

策略组合 $s^*$ 是优势策略均衡，当且仅当对每名玩家 $i$、每项偏离 $s_i'$ 和每种对手策略组合 $s_{-i}$，都有

$$
u_i(s_i^*,s_{-i})\ge u_i(s_i',s_{-i}).
$$

若对所有 $s_i'\ne s_i^*$ 与所有 $s_{-i}$ 都严格大于，则称为**严格优势策略均衡**。这里的量词顺序很关键：先固定玩家自己的同一项 $s_i^*$，再让对手的所有可能选择逐一变化。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A profile $s^*$ is a dominant-strategy equilibrium exactly when the displayed inequality holds for every player, every unilateral alternative, and every possible opponents' profile. If every comparison against a distinct alternative is strict, the profile is a **strict dominant-strategy equilibrium**. The quantifiers matter: the same $s_i^*$ must survive every possible choice by the opponents.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么它一定是 Nash
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why It Must Be Nash*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

优势策略条件对所有 $s_{-i}$ 成立，当然也对均衡候选中的特定 $s_{-i}^*$ 成立。因此每名玩家的 $s_i^*$ 都是对 $s_{-i}^*$ 的[[最佳反应]]，所以 $s^*$ 是 Nash 均衡。

反向不成立。协调博弈里的 $(A,A)$ 可以是 Nash：给定对方选 $A$，自己也选 $A$ 最好；但当对方改选 $B$ 时，$A$ 未必仍然最好，所以 $A$ 不是优势策略。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Dominance holds against every $s_{-i}$ and therefore also against the particular $s_{-i}^*$ in the candidate profile. Every $s_i^*$ is consequently a [[最佳反应|best response]] to $s_{-i}^*$, which proves that the profile is Nash. The converse fails: a coordination action may be optimal against the matching action and hence support Nash, while ceasing to be optimal when the opponent chooses something else.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 囚徒困境中的严格优势策略均衡
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Strict Dominance in the Prisoner's Dilemma*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

|  | 合作 $C$ | 背叛 $D$ |
|---|---|---|
| 合作 $C$ | $(3,3)$ | $(0,5)$ |
| 背叛 $D$ | $(5,0)$ | $(1,1)$ |

无论另一人合作还是背叛，自己选 $D$ 都比 $C$ 收益更高，所以 $(D,D)$ 是严格优势策略均衡。与此同时，$(C,C)$ 让双方都更好；这说明“预测稳定”与“结果有效率”必须交给[[均衡与效率|两套不同检验]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Whether the other prisoner cooperates or defects, choosing $D$ gives a higher payoff than choosing $C$, so $(D,D)$ is a strict dominant-strategy equilibrium. Yet both players prefer $(C,C)$. The example separates strategic stability from the welfare test in [[均衡与效率|equilibrium versus efficiency]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 先识别对象，再使用结论
> “$s_i$ 占优 $s_i'$”只是两项策略之间的比较；“$s_i$ 是优势策略”要求它优于该玩家的全部替代策略；“$s^*$ 是优势策略均衡”又要求每名玩家都拥有并采用这样的策略。三句话不能互换。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “$s_i$ dominates $s_i'$” compares two strategies. Calling $s_i$ dominant requires comparison with all of that player's alternatives. A dominant-strategy equilibrium additionally requires every player to choose such a strategy. These are three distinct claims.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
