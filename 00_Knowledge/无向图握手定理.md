# The handshaking lemma
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*无向图握手定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An undirected edge has two ends. If we count the edge ends at every vertex and then add these counts, each edge has contributed twice. This is the **handshaking lemma**: the sum of the vertices' [[度数、入度与出度|degrees]] is twice the number of edges. The familiar handshake picture is the same count: one handshake contributes one to each participant's tally.

For example, the path $a-b-c$ and an isolated vertex $d$ have degrees $1,2,1,0$. Their sum is $4$, twice the two edges. The isolated vertex contributes zero; the graph need not be connected.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条无向边有两个端点。先在每个顶点统计边的端点，再把这些计数相加，每条边就贡献了两次。这就是**握手定理**：全图顶点的[[度数、入度与出度|度数]]和等于边数的两倍。握手的比喻也是同一种计数：一次握手给两位参与者的个人记录各加一次。

例如路径 $a-b-c$ 再加一个孤立顶点 $d$，各点度数为 $1,2,1,0$，总和 $4$ 恰好是两条边的两倍。孤立点贡献零，图不必连通。

> [!theorem] Count both ends · 数出每条边的两端
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $G=(V,E)$ be a finite undirected graph. Parallel edges are counted as distinct edges; if loops are allowed, each loop contributes two to the degree of its vertex. Then
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $G=(V,E)$ 是有限无向图。平行边作为不同边分别计数；若允许自环，每个自环给所在顶点的度数贡献二。则
>
> $$
> \sum_{v\in V}\deg(v)=2|E|.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $|E|$ is the number of edges, and the sum runs over every vertex once. In particular, the formula holds for all finite simple undirected graphs.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 其中 $|E|$ 是边数，求和对每个顶点恰取一次。尤其，所有有限简单无向图都满足此式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the two counts agree
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 证明

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First consider a simple graph. Form the set of vertex–edge incidence pairs
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先看简单图。构造顶点与边的关联对集合

$$
I=\{(v,e):v\text{ is an endpoint of }e\}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An incidence pair records a vertex and an edge having that vertex as an endpoint. Fixing $v$ gives $\deg(v)$ pairs, so grouping the pairs by vertex gives $|I|=\sum_v\deg(v)$. Fixing an edge gives two pairs, one for each of its two different endpoints, so grouping by edge gives $|I|=2|E|$. These are two complete counts of the same set: a [[双重计数|double-counting proof]].

To include loops, distinguish the **two endpoint occurrences** of each edge, even if both occur at the same vertex. Label those occurrences $(e,1)$ and $(e,2)$, and attach each to its vertex. There are exactly $2|E|$ occurrences. The number attached to $v$ is exactly $\deg(v)$ under the stated convention. Grouping the occurrences by vertex proves the same identity. Parallel edges have different edge identities $e$, so their occurrences remain distinct.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一份关联对记录一个顶点，以及以它为端点的一条边。固定 $v$，这样的配对有 $\deg(v)$ 份，因此按顶点分组得到 $|I|=\sum_v\deg(v)$。固定一条边，它有两个不同端点，贡献两份配对，因此按边分组得到 $|I|=2|E|$。这是对同一集合的两种完整计数，即[[双重计数]]证明。

要包含自环，应区分每条边的**两个端点出现位置**，即使它们对应同一个顶点。把这两个位置标成 $(e,1),(e,2)$，分别归到其所在顶点。共有 $2|E|$ 个端点位置；归到 $v$ 的数量，按约定恰好是 $\deg(v)$。再按顶点分组，就证明了同一恒等式。平行边拥有不同的边身份 $e$，不会被合并。

> [!warning] A loop still has two ends · 自环仍有两个端点位置
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a loop at $v$, the plain pair $(v,e)$ appears only once in a set. Therefore the simple-graph incidence set above must be replaced by endpoint occurrences when loops are admitted. For two $a$–$b$ edges, one loop at $a$, and one $b$–$c$ edge, the degrees are $4,3,1$, with any additional isolated vertices contributing $0$. Their sum is $8=2\times4$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 自环位于 $v$ 时，普通配对 $(v,e)$ 在集合中只出现一次。因此允许自环后，应把上面的简单图关联集合换成端点位置。两条 $a$–$b$ 边、一个 $a$ 上的自环和一条 $b$–$c$ 边给出度数 $4,3,1$，额外孤立点贡献 $0$；总和为 $8=2\times4$。

## Odd degrees and the limits of the test
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 推论与边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The degree sum is an even integer. Every even-degree vertex contributes an even number. If there are $k$ odd-degree vertices, their degrees can each be written as $2r+1$ for an integer $r\ge0$; adding them gives an even number plus $k$. Since the whole sum is even, **$k$ must be even**. This includes $k=0$ and does not require the graph to be connected.

These are necessary checks on a proposed degree list, not a general existence test. The list $(3,3,3,2,1)$ has even sum $12$ and four odd entries. To establish that a simple graph with these degrees actually exists, label its vertices $a,b,c,d,e$ and use the edges
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

度数和是偶整数。每个偶度顶点贡献偶数；若有 $k$ 个奇度顶点，把每个奇度写为某个整数 $r\ge0$ 对应的 $2r+1$，相加就得到“一个偶数加上 $k$”。全体度数和既然为偶数，**$k$ 也必须是偶数**。这包括 $k=0$，不要求图连通。

这些只是拟定度数列表必须通过的检查，不是通用的存在性判据。例如 $(3,3,3,2,1)$ 的总和为偶数 $12$，奇数项有四个；要证明它确实能由简单图实现，还可以把顶点标为 $a,b,c,d,e$，直接取边

$$
ab,\ ac,\ ad,\ bc,\ be,\ cd.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $ab$ denotes the undirected edge joining $a$ and $b$. Counting at $a,b,c,d,e$ gives exactly $(3,3,3,2,1)$, so the construction certifies existence. By contrast, $(3,3,1,1)$ has even sum and an even number of odd entries but cannot be the degrees of a simple graph on four vertices: the two degree-$3$ vertices must each connect to both remaining vertices, forcing each remaining degree to be at least $2$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $ab$ 表示连接 $a,b$ 的无向边。在 $a,b,c,d,e$ 处逐个计数，恰得 $(3,3,3,2,1)$，所以构造证明了存在。反之，$(3,3,1,1)$ 虽然总和及奇度项数量都是偶数，却不可能是四顶点简单图的度数：两个度数为 $3$ 的顶点都必须连接另外两个顶点，迫使后两者的度数至少为 $2$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a finite directed graph, count heads and tails separately. Every edge has one of each, including a loop, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对有限有向图，应把边头、边尾分开数。每条边各有一个边头和一个边尾，自环也一样，因此

$$
\sum_{v\in V}\deg^-(v)=|E|=\sum_{v\in V}\deg^+(v).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Neither separate sum needs to be even: the single arrow $a\to b$ already gives one vertex with odd indegree and one with odd outdegree. The sum of **both** counts over all vertices is $2|E|$. Thus the undirected odd-degree conclusion must not be applied to indegrees alone or outdegrees alone.

If there are no edges, every term and both sides of the handshaking identity are zero. If the vertex set is also empty, the empty sum is zero, so the same formula holds under that explicitly allowed convention. Finiteness matters for the integer and parity argument here; infinite degree totals do not justify reasoning with an “even infinity” or subtracting infinite quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这两种独立的总和都不必为偶数：仅有箭头 $a\to b$ 时，奇入度顶点恰有一个，奇出度顶点也恰有一个。把全体顶点的**两种**度数都加起来，才得到 $2|E|$。因此，无向图的奇度结论不能直接用于单独的入度或出度。

没有边时，每个度数以及握手恒等式两边都为零。若也允许顶点集为空，空和仍为零，同一公式继续成立。这里的整数与奇偶性论证以有限性为前提；无限的度数总和不能用“偶数个无穷”或无穷量相减来处理。
