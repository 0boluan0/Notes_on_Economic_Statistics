# 有限无向图的度数和等于边数的两倍
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The degree sum of a finite undirected graph is twice its number of edges*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子定理
> 对有限无向 simple graph $G=(V,E)$，
> $$
> \sum_{v\in V}\deg(v)=2|E|.
> $$
> 这就是 handshaking lemma。它不是对某种图形画法的观察，而是对所有 vertex–edge incidence pairs 的双重计数。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a finite undirected simple graph $G=(V,E)$,
> $$
> \sum_{v\in V}\deg(v)=2|E|.
> $$
> This is the handshaking lemma. It follows from double-counting vertex–edge incidence pairs, not from the appearance of a drawing.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 证明
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Proof*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令
$$
I=\{(v,e):v\text{ 是边 }e\text{ 的端点}\}.
$$
先固定顶点 $v$，与它配对的边恰有 $\deg(v)$ 条，所以 $|I|=\sum_v\deg(v)$。再固定一条 simple undirected edge $e=\{u,v\}$，它有两个不同端点，因而对 $I$ 贡献两对。于是 $|I|=2|E|$，两种计数相等即得结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let
$$
I=\{(v,e):v\text{ is an endpoint of }e\}.
$$
Fixing a vertex $v$ gives exactly $\deg(v)$ pairs, so $|I|=\sum_v\deg(v)$. Fixing a simple undirected edge $e=\{u,v\}$ gives two pairs, one for each distinct endpoint, so $|I|=2|E|$. Equating the two counts proves the lemma.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 推论与边界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Corollaries and boundary*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

总度数必为偶数。偶度顶点贡献偶数，因此奇度顶点的数量也必为偶数；否则奇数个奇数之和为奇数，与总和为偶数矛盾。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The total degree is even. Even-degree vertices contribute an even amount, so the number of odd-degree vertices must itself be even; an odd number of odd summands would have an odd sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对 digraph，正确的对应式是
$$
\sum_v\deg^-(v)=\sum_v\deg^+(v)=|E|,
$$
因为每条 directed edge 各贡献一个 head incidence 与一个 tail incidence。若允许无向 self-loop，只有在 degree 把 loop 的两个端点 incidence 都计入时，$2|E|$ 形式才保持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a digraph, the corresponding identities are
$$
\sum_v\deg^-(v)=\sum_v\deg^+(v)=|E|,
$$
because every directed edge contributes one head incidence and one tail incidence. If undirected self-loops are admitted, the formula $2|E|$ remains valid only under the convention that a loop contributes two incidences to degree.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 是否存在 degree sequence 为 $(3,3,3,2,1)$ 的有限无向 simple graph？
>
> **答案：**handshaking 只能先确认“没有被必要条件排除”：总和为 $12$，奇度项有四个。它不能单独证明存在。继续构造可取边 $ab,ac,ad,bc,be,cd$，各点 degree 依次为 $(3,3,3,2,1)$，所以这个 sequence 确实可实现。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Does a finite undirected simple graph with degree sequence $(3,3,3,2,1)$ exist?
>
> **Answer:** Handshaking only shows that the sequence is not ruled out: its sum is $12$ and it has four odd entries. It does not prove existence. A construction does: use edges $ab,ac,ad,bc,be,cd$, which give degrees $(3,3,3,2,1)$. Thus the sequence is graphical, but the construction—not handshaking alone—certifies it.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
