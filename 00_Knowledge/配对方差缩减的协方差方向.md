# Covariance in paired simulation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*配对模拟中的协方差方向*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Pairing two simulation outputs is useful only in relation to what we do with them. When we **subtract**, fluctuations that move together cancel. When we **average**, fluctuations that move in opposite directions cancel. That is why a positive covariance can help one calculation while hurting the other.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把两个模拟输出配成一对是否有用，要看接下来怎样组合它们。做**减法**时，同向波动可以相消；求**平均**时，反向波动可以相消。因此，同一个正协方差可能帮助一种计算，却妨碍另一种计算。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here [[协方差|covariance]] measures the expected product of two centred fluctuations. Assume all outputs below have finite second moments. Each comparison keeps the individual [[边际分布|output distributions]] fixed and changes only their dependence; otherwise a changed variance may come from changing the experiment itself. The benchmark is independent outputs with those same marginal distributions and the same evaluation count.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[协方差]]是两个输出偏离各自均值后的乘积期望。以下假定各输出二阶矩有限。每次比较都保持两个输出各自的[[边际分布]]不变，仅改变它们之间的依赖；否则，方差变化可能来自更换实验本身。基准是边际分布相同、函数计算次数相同的独立输出。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Shared fluctuations disappear from a difference
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*共同波动会从差值中抵消*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Suppose two plans produce outputs $A$ and $B$, and the target is $\Delta=E[A]-E[B]$. One paired run records $D=A-B$. [[期望线性性|Linearity of expectation]] gives $E[D]=\Delta$ whatever their dependence. Let their variances be $v_A,v_B$, and covariance be $C$. Then
$$\operatorname{Var}(D)=v_A+v_B-2C.$$
Independent runs have $C=0$, giving $v_A+v_B$. Consequently $C>0$ lowers the difference variance, $C=0$ leaves it unchanged, and $C<0$ raises it. [[共同随机数|Common random numbers]] try to obtain useful positive covariance by comparing plans under shared random inputs; shared inputs alone do not guarantee the sign.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设两个方案输出为 $A,B$，目标为 $\Delta=E[A]-E[B]$，一次配对记录差值 $D=A-B$。无论怎样依赖，[[期望线性性]]都给出 $E[D]=\Delta$。设两个方差为 $v_A,v_B$，协方差为 $C$，则差值方差如上。独立运行的基准为 $v_A+v_B$，所以正协方差降低差值方差，零协方差不变，负协方差增大方差。[[共同随机数]]通过共享随机输入尝试产生有用的正协方差，但共享输入本身不保证符号。

> [!example] A common shock cancels exactly · 共同冲击恰好抵消
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $U$ be uniform on $[0,1]$, $A=10+2U$ and $B=8+2U$. Both variances and their covariance equal $1/3$. Every paired difference is $2$, so its variance is $1/3+1/3-2/3=0$. Using independent uniform inputs for the two plans keeps their expected difference $2$ but gives variance $2/3$ for one difference. The gain comes from cancelling the shared fluctuation, not from changing either plan’s distribution.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 令 $U$ 在 $[0,1]$ 均匀分布，$A=10+2U$、$B=8+2U$。两个方差与协方差都是 $1/3$，配对差值恒为 $2$，方差为零。改用两个独立均匀输入，期望差仍为 $2$，一次差值的方差却为 $2/3$。收益来自抵消共同波动，而非改变任一方案的分布。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The reverse sign really can hurt: if both output variances are $1$ and their covariance is $-0.3$, one difference has variance $1+1-2(-0.3)=2.6$, exceeding the independent benchmark $2$. Pairing is not automatically a variance reduction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

相反符号确实可能有害：若两输出方差均为 $1$、协方差为 $-0.3$，一次差值方差为 $1+1-2(-0.3)=2.6$，高于独立基准 $2$。配对不是自动的方差缩减。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Opposite fluctuations disappear from an average
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*反向波动会从平均中抵消*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now suppose $Y$ and $Y'$ each have the same expectation $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$. The paired output $Z=(Y+Y')/2$ estimates $\mu$, and
$$\operatorname{Var}(Z)=\frac{\sigma^2+\operatorname{Cov}(Y,Y')}{2}.$$
The independent average has variance $\sigma^2/2$. Thus a negative covariance helps this average, while a positive covariance hurts it. This is the goal of [[对偶变量|antithetic variates]]. For $Y=U$ and $Y'=1-U$, both variances are $1/12$ and their covariance is $-1/12$: the average is always $1/2$, with variance zero. The independent two-output average has variance $1/24$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现在假设 $Y,Y'$ 各自的期望均为 $\mu$、方差均为 $\sigma^2$，配对输出 $Z=(Y+Y')/2$ 估计 $\mu$，其方差如上。独立平均的方差为 $\sigma^2/2$，因此负协方差有利，正协方差有害。这是[[对偶变量]]的目标。取 $Y=U,Y'=1-U$，两个方差均为 $1/12$，协方差为 $-1/12$，平均恒为 $1/2$、方差为零；两次独立输出的平均方差则是 $1/24$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both conclusions are instances of one [[和的方差协方差项|variance identity]]. For fixed real weights $a,b$,
$$\operatorname{Var}(aA+bB)=a^2v_A+b^2v_B+2abC.$$
The sign of $ab$ determines which covariance sign lowers variance relative to independence. If either weight is zero, covariance makes no difference. With unequal variances, the average formula is $(v_A+v_B+2C)/4$; the sign rule is unchanged. With infinite second moments, these finite-variance subtractions are not justified.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

两个结论都来自同一个[[和的方差协方差项|方差恒等式]]。对固定实数权重 $a,b$，上式中 $ab$ 的符号决定了哪种协方差方向有利；任一权重为零，协方差就不起作用。方差不等时，平均的方差为 $(v_A+v_B+2C)/4$，符号判断仍相同。二阶矩无穷时，不能继续使用这些有限方差相减的式子。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Carry the pair through the uncertainty calculation
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*计算不确定性时也要保留配对*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed number $R$ of iid complete pairs, the $R$ differences $D_r$ are iid, as are the $R$ averages $Z_r$. Their sample means have variance $\operatorname{Var}(D)/R$ or $\operatorname{Var}(Z)/R$. For $R\ge2$, use the sample standard deviation of those complete outputs divided by $\sqrt R$ to obtain the [[Monte Carlo均值标准误|estimated standard error]]. Calculating two separate standard errors and adding them discards the covariance; resampling the two components separately also destroys the intended pairing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定重复 $R$ 个 iid 完整配对时，$R$ 个差值 $D_r$ 相互独立同分布，$R$ 个平均 $Z_r$ 也是如此。它们的均值方差分别为 $\operatorname{Var}(D)/R$ 或 $\operatorname{Var}(Z)/R$。当 $R\ge2$，用这些完整输出的样本标准差除以 $\sqrt R$ 得到[[Monte Carlo均值标准误|估计标准误]]。分别算两个标准误再相加会丢掉协方差；把两部分分别重新抽样，也会破坏所需配对。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are variance comparisons at equal output counts. Synchronisation, random-input generation and function evaluation can have different costs. A positive covariance is necessary for a strict variance gain of the stated difference at fixed marginals, but it is not by itself a universal requirement for lower runtime at a chosen precision. Use [[方差缩减比较口径|cost at common precision]] for that practical question, and check [[共同随机数输入同步|input alignment]] before interpreting a shared-seed experiment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

以上比较固定了输出次数，而同步、随机输入生成、函数计算的成本可能不同。固定边际时，正协方差是上述差值获得严格方差收益的必要条件，却不能单独成为“达到某精度所需运行时间更短”的普遍必要条件。实际成本问题应使用[[方差缩减比较口径|同等精度成本]]，解释共享种子的实验前，还要检查[[共同随机数输入同步|输入的同义对齐]]。
