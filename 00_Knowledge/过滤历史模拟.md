# 过滤历史模拟先标准化历史冲击，再按当前条件尺度重映射
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Filtered historical simulation standardises historical shocks and then remaps them to the current conditional scale*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它改变情景生成假设
> 过滤历史模拟用历史条件波动率去除旧尺度，再用当前预测尺度恢复冲击。它减少波动制度混杂，但结果开始依赖所选条件波动率或协方差模型。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Filtered historical simulation removes each historical volatility scale and restores shocks at the current forecast scale. It reduces mixing across volatility regimes but makes results depend on the selected conditional-volatility or covariance model.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

单因子示意为

$$
r_i^*
=\frac{r_i}{\widehat\sigma_i}
\widehat\sigma_{t_0+1\mid t_0}.
$$

多因子情形应保留同一日期的标准化残差向量，并用一致的当前条件协方差结构重新映射。逐列独立缩放、抽取或拼接会改变联合依赖。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In multiple dimensions, preserve each date's standardised residual vector and remap it with a coherent current conditional covariance structure. Independent column-wise scaling, drawing, or mixing changes joint dependence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 过滤后的多因子冲击能否逐列从不同日期抽取？
>
> **答案：** 不能。应保留同一日期的联合标准化残差，除非另有明确联合生成模型。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check:** After filtering, can a multi-factor shock draw each column from a different date?
>
> **Answer:** No. Preserve the joint standardized residual from one date unless a separate joint-generation model is explicitly specified.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 边界

- 过滤不使方法“无模型”；它新增波动率、协方差与残差假设。
- 历史未见的尾部不会因缩放自动出现。
- 当前尺度预测错误会直接传入风险估计。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Filtering is not model-free; it adds volatility, covariance, and residual assumptions.
- Scaling does not create tail shapes absent from history.
- An incorrect current-scale forecast directly contaminates risk estimates.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
