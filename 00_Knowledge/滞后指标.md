# 滞后指标是相对于指定经济活动基准通常较晚转折的时间序列
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A lagging indicator is a time series that typically turns after a specified economic-activity benchmark.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

滞后指标反映活动变化后逐步显现的结果，可用于确认持续性或调整压力。它的“滞后”是经济时序，不是因为统计部门单纯迟发数据，也不必每次恰好晚六个月。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Lagging indicators reflect consequences that emerge after activity changes and can help confirm persistence or adjustment pressures. Their lag is economic timing, not merely slow publication, and need not equal six months.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如失业持续时间可能在活动开始恢复后仍偏高，因为长期失业者重新匹配岗位需要时间。TCB 对应项目是平均**失业**持续周数，不能误写为平均就业持续周数；该项目通常还需反向处理才与活动改善同向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Unemployment duration may remain high after activity starts recovering because job matching takes time. The corresponding TCB component is average **unemployment** duration, not employment duration, and is usually inverted to align with improving activity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

CPI、PPI、利率等不能只按名称永久归为滞后。应说明用什么序列、相对哪个转折及哪个样本，再与[[先行指标]]和[[同步指标]]组合；确认周期不等于预测证券回报。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
CPI, PPI, and interest rates are not permanently lagging by name alone. Specify the series, reference turning point, and sample before combining [[先行指标|leading]] and [[同步指标|coincident]] measures. Confirming a cycle does not predict a security's return.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
