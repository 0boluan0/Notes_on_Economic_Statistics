# 学习效应是累积实践经验带来的成本、质量或作业能力改善
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Learning effects are improvements in cost, quality, or operating ability arising from accumulated practical experience.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

学习效应来自反复实践中获得的知识和改进，例如更熟练的操作、更少的返工或更好的流程。它关注累积经验，而[[规模经济]]关注给定技术下的当期生产规模；两者可以共存，但不是同一个解释。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Learning effects arise from knowledge and improvements gained through practice, such as greater proficiency, less rework, or better processes. They concern accumulated experience, whereas [[规模经济|economies of scale]] concern current output scale at given technology. Both can coexist but are distinct explanations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，在当期产量、质量和投入价格相同的条件下，较有经验的团队每件需 8 小时，而早期需 10 小时；若差异确由实践改进引起，就是学习效应。它不要求当期生产规模扩大，也不保证所有企业永远按固定比例降成本。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
At the same current output, quality, and input prices, an experienced team may need eight hours per item instead of ten. If practice caused the improvement, this is a learning effect. Current production need not expand, and no fixed rate of perpetual cost reduction is guaranteed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经验曲线可用累积产量描述成本变化，但观察到“累积产量高、成本低”仍须排除技术更新、投入价格或产品组合等其他解释；经验积累只是识别学习的一项证据。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An experience curve can relate cost to cumulative output. Observing higher cumulative output and lower cost still requires checking technological change, input prices, and product mix. Accumulated production is evidence relevant to learning, not a sufficient causal test.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
