# 后训练信号是在预训练基座上继续塑造输出行为时，目标函数从示范、比较或评价中读取的监督信息
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A post-training signal is supervisory information read from demonstrations, comparisons, or evaluations by an objective that continues shaping model behaviour after pretraining*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> 预训练先得到一个基座模型。之后若还要更新模型，就必须告诉目标函数“哪些输出应更可能、哪些应更不可能”。示范回答、候选之间的比较和对输出的评价，都可以提供这种信息。它们是**后训练信号**；SFT、奖励建模、强化学习或直接偏好优化则是利用信号的不同方法。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Pretraining first produces a base model. Further updates need information about which outputs should become more or less likely. Demonstration responses, comparisons among candidates, and evaluations of outputs can all provide such information. These are post-training signals; SFT, reward modelling, reinforcement learning, and direct preference optimisation are different ways to use them.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 信号与算法是两层东西
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The signal and the algorithm are separate layers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条示范记录通常给出提示 $x$ 与目标回答 $y^*$；一条成对比较记录给出 $(x,y_w,y_l)$，只声明在这两个候选中 $y_w$ 被选中；评价还可能先被写成一个标量，再进入策略更新。记录本身说明“观察到了什么监督信息”，损失函数与优化算法才规定“怎样把这条信息变成参数更新”。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A demonstration record usually pairs a prompt $x$ with a target response $y^*$. A pairwise comparison gives $(x,y_w,y_l)$ and states only that $y_w$ was selected from the pair. An evaluation may first be represented as a scalar before entering a policy update. The record states what supervisory information was observed; the loss and optimisation algorithm determine how that information changes parameters.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以，看到“使用了人类反馈”还不足以复现训练。还要问反馈是完整示范、二选一、排序、评分还是规则判定；候选怎样产生；哪些位置计入损失；以及最后采用哪一种目标。相同的比较数据可以先训练显式奖励模型，也可以直接进入偏好目标，二者不是同一管线。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Uses human feedback” is not enough to reproduce a training run. One must still know whether the feedback is a demonstration, pairwise choice, ranking, rating, or rule-based judgment; how candidates were generated; which positions enter the loss; and which objective consumes the record. The same comparison data can train an explicit reward model or enter a direct preference objective, yielding different pipelines.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 数据来源不决定监督角色
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Data provenance does not determine its supervisory role*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

同一段文字可以在预训练语料中充当普通续写，也可以在 SFT 数据里充当希望模型模仿的回答。偏好也不必由人直接给出，示范也不必由人亲自撰写。判断信号类型，要看这条记录在目标函数中扮演什么角色，而不是只看它来自人、模型还是规则。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The same text can be an ordinary continuation in pretraining and a response to imitate in SFT. Preferences need not be supplied directly by people, and demonstrations need not be handwritten. Signal type is determined by the record's role in the objective, not merely by whether its source is a person, model, or rule.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 最小例子
> 对同一问题，记录 A 只有一条编辑过的理想回答；记录 B 给出两个回答并标明更偏好的一个。A 提供示范信号，B 提供相对偏好信号。它们都可用于后训练，却不能互换目标函数。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For one prompt, record A contains a single edited ideal response. Record B contains two responses and marks the preferred one. A supplies a demonstration signal; B supplies a relative preference signal. Both can support post-training, but they do not define interchangeable objectives.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 一个系统收集 chosen/rejected 回答对。只凭数据格式，能否断定它训练了独立奖励模型并使用 PPO？
>
> **答案：** 不能。同一偏好对可以供显式奖励模型管线使用，也可以直接进入 DPO 一类目标。要继续看损失和更新算法。

## 来源与核验
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Sources and verification*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- [Ouyang et al. (2022), *Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback*](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.02155#S3.SS1)：第 3.1 节把示范数据、比较数据和奖励模型驱动的策略更新分成三个步骤，直接展示“监督记录”与“使用它的算法”需要分别说明。
- [Rafailov et al. (2023), *Direct Preference Optimization*](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.18290#S3)：第 3–4 节说明同一类成对偏好既可先拟合奖励模型再做 RL，也可通过策略参数化直接构造偏好损失。
