# 平均实际收入增长本身不能保证贫困人口比例下降

同一组人的平均实际收入增加，只说明收入总量扩大；新增收入落到谁手里，决定哪些人能越过[[贫困线]]。这里的均值来自测量贫困的同一份家庭收入或消费分布，不能直接拿[[人均GDP]]替代。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An increase in a fixed population's mean real income establishes that total income rose. Who receives the increase determines which people cross the [[贫困线|poverty line]]. The mean here belongs to the same household income or consumption distribution used to measure poverty; [[人均GDP|GDP per capita]] is not a direct substitute.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定人口、价格口径和实际绝对贫困线 $z=100$，考虑以下教学分配：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hold the population, price basis, and real absolute poverty line $z=100$ fixed. Consider these constructed distributions:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 状态 / State | 四人实际收入 / Four real incomes | 均值 / Mean | 贫困人口比例 / Headcount |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 原状 / Initial | 40、90、120、150 | 100 | $2/4$ |
| 总量增加 / Higher total | 30、80、95、235 | 110 | $3/4$ |
| 总量不变、分配改变 / Same total, redistribution | 100、100、100、100 | 100 | $0/4$ |

第二行均值增长了 10%，但原本高于贫困线的第三个人跌到线下，[[贫困人口比例]]反而上升。第三行说明，在这一给定时点上，重新分配也可以减贫，而不需要先增加总收入；它没有证明这种分配在现实中可以无成本实行，或能长期维持。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the second row, mean income rises by 10%, yet the third person falls below the line, increasing the [[贫困人口比例|headcount ratio]]. The third row shows that redistribution can reduce poverty at a given time without first increasing total income. It does not establish that this allocation is costless to implement or sustainable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若额外假设每个人的实际收入非负，并都乘以同一个大于 1 的系数，那么在固定绝对线下，贫困率不会上升，但未必下降：如果无人越线，人数指标就不变。此时已经发生的线下改善可以由[[贫困缺口指数]]记录。若使用会随社会收入变动的相对线，则要重新判断，见[[绝对贫困与相对贫困]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If every person's real income is nonnegative and is multiplied by the same factor greater than 1, the headcount ratio cannot increase at a fixed absolute line, but it need not fall: nobody may cross the threshold. The [[贫困缺口指数|poverty gap index]] can register improvement below the line. A relative line that changes with social income requires a different assessment; see [[绝对贫困与相对贫困|absolute and relative poverty]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，评估减贫时要把均值变化和分配变化一起看。上表只证明“均值增长不足以保证人数减贫”这一边界，不估计一项增长或再分配政策的因果效果。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assess poverty reduction using both changes in the mean and changes in distribution. The table establishes a logical boundary—mean growth is insufficient to guarantee a lower headcount—not the causal effect of a growth or redistribution policy.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Bourguignon's discussion supports analyzing poverty through the survey welfare mean and relative distribution at a fixed absolute line. The numerical distributions are independently constructed and checked.
