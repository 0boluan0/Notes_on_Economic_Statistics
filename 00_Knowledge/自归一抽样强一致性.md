# 若提议分布覆盖目标支持且目标函数在 p 下绝对可积，自归一化重要性抽样强一致
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*If the proposal covers the target support and the target function is absolutely integrable under p, self-normalised importance sampling is strongly consistent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 有限样本有偏不妨碍比率在大样本下收敛到正确目标
> 设 $X_i\overset{iid}{\sim}q$，$q(x)>0$ whenever $p(x)>0$，$\widetilde w(x)=c\,p(x)/q(x)$ 且 $c>0$。若 $E_p|h(X)|<\infty$，则 SNIS 估计量几乎必然收敛到 $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $X_i$ be iid from $q$, let $q$ be positive wherever $p$ is positive, and let $\widetilde w=c\,p/q$ with $c>0$. If $E_p|h(X)|<\infty$, then the SNIS estimator converges almost surely to $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些条件使两次强大数定律都可用：

$$
E_q[\widetilde w(X)]=c<\infty,
\qquad
E_q[|h(X)|\widetilde w(X)]
=cE_p|h(X)|<\infty.
$$

因此

$$
\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N h(X_i)\widetilde w(X_i)
\xrightarrow{a.s.}c\mu,
\qquad
\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N\widetilde w(X_i)
\xrightarrow{a.s.}c.
$$

分母极限 $c$ 严格为正，所以两者之比几乎必然收敛到 $\mu$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both weighted terms are integrable under $q$. The strong law sends the numerator average to $c\mu$ and the denominator average to the strictly positive constant $c$, so their ratio converges almost surely to $\mu$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里要求 $q$ 覆盖所有 $p>0$ 的区域，比普通重要性抽样针对一个固定 $h$ 的充分条件更强，因为 SNIS 的分母也在估计归一化常数。强一致性既不消除 [[自归一抽样有限样本偏差|有限样本偏差]]，也不保证有限方差或正态区间；后两者还要检查加权二阶矩。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
SNIS needs proposal support wherever the target is positive because its denominator also estimates the normalising constant. Strong consistency neither removes finite-sample bias nor guarantees finite variance or a normal approximation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 可核验自检
> 为什么证明中必须确认分母极限严格大于零，而不能只说分子和分母各自收敛？
>
> **答案：** 只有分母收敛到非零常数时，取比率才在极限处连续；这里该常数是 $c>0$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A ratio is stable under convergence only when its denominator tends to a nonzero limit; here that limit is $c>0$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
