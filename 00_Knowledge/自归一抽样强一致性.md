# Strong consistency of self-normalised importance sampling
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*自归一抽样的强一致性*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A short self-normalised run can be biased, yet one continuing run can settle at the correct target. **Strong consistency** means that, with probability one, the estimates approach the target along the same infinite sequence of simulated observations. For [[自归一化重要性抽样|SNIS]], the basic proof follows two averages: the weighted output and the weight itself. Each obeys a [[强大数定律|strong law]], and their limits form the desired ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一次短的自归一运行可能有偏，同一轮持续增加样本却仍可趋向正确目标。**强一致性**是说，以概率一，沿同一串无限模拟观测得到的估计会趋近目标。对[[自归一化重要性抽样|SNIS]]，基本证明跟踪两个平均值：加权输出平均与权重平均。两者分别服从[[强大数定律]]，其极限之比就是所求目标。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Watch the two-point estimate settle
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*先看二点例子如何趋近目标*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use $p(1)=3/4$, $p(0)=1/4$, proposal $q(1)=q(0)=1/2$, and $h(x)=x$. The weights $p/q$ are $3/2$ and $1/2$. Let $K_N$ be the number of ones among the first $N$ iid proposal draws. Then
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

沿用 $p(1)=3/4$、$p(0)=1/4$，提议 $q(1)=q(0)=1/2$，并取 $h(x)=x$。权重 $p/q$ 分别为 $3/2$、$1/2$。令 $K_N$ 表示前 $N$ 个 iid 提议观测中 $1$ 的个数，那么

$$
\widehat\mu_N=\frac{(3/2)K_N}{(3/2)K_N+(1/2)(N-K_N)}
=\frac{3(K_N/N)}{1+2(K_N/N)}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
By the strong law for the [[事件指示变量|indicator]] of outcome $1$, $K_N/N\to1/2$ almost surely. Substitution gives $\widehat\mu_N\to(3/2)/2=3/4$. The [[自归一抽样有限样本偏差|finite-sample calculation]] still gives expectation $1/2$ at $N=1$ and $5/8$ at $N=2$. These facts concern different questions: the mean of a fixed-size procedure versus its limit along one continuing sequence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对结果为 $1$ 的[[事件指示变量]]使用强大数定律，得到 $K_N/N\to1/2$ 几乎必然成立，代入后有 $\widehat\mu_N\to(3/2)/2=3/4$。[[自归一抽样有限样本偏差|有限样本计算]]仍给出 $N=1$ 时期望 $1/2$、$N=2$ 时期望 $5/8$。它们回答不同问题：固定样本量程序的平均，与同一条持续序列上的极限。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A sufficient theorem and its proof
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*一条充分定理及其证明*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $p,q$ be normalised [[概率密度函数|densities]] on the same space, or [[概率质量函数|mass functions]] in a discrete problem. Fix them throughout the run. Assume $q>0$ wherever $p>0$ almost everywhere, and let the real output $h$ satisfy $E_p|h(X)|<\infty$. Generate one [[独立同分布|iid]] sequence $X_1,X_2,\ldots$ from $q$. The computable weights have the form $\widetilde w=c\,p/q$, with one constant $0<c<\infty$. The target is $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $p,q$ 是同一空间上已归一化的[[概率密度函数|密度]]，离散问题则是[[概率质量函数]]，并在整轮运行中保持固定。假定几乎处处在 $p>0$ 处都有 $q>0$，实值输出 $h$ 满足 $E_p|h(X)|<\infty$；从 $q$ 生成同一串[[独立同分布|iid]]观测 $X_1,X_2,\ldots$。可计算权重形如 $\widetilde w=c\,p/q$，共同常数满足 $0<c<\infty$，目标为 $\mu=E_p[h(X)]$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Coverage permits the density-ratio cancellation over the whole target, including the denominator function $1$. Since weights are nonnegative,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

覆盖条件允许在完整目标上约去密度比，也包括分母对应的常数函数 $1$。权重非负，因此

$$
E_q[\widetilde w(X)]=c,\qquad
E_q[|h(X)|\widetilde w(X)]=cE_p|h(X)|<\infty.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus both quantities are integrable under the sampling law. The strong law applies to each average, and the two probability-one convergence events hold together:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

所以这两个量在实际抽样分布下都可积。分别应用强大数定律，两个概率一的收敛事件也同时以概率一发生：

$$
A_N=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N h(X_i)\widetilde w(X_i)\xrightarrow{a.s.}c\mu,
\qquad
B_N=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^N\widetilde w(X_i)\xrightarrow{a.s.}c>0.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The notation $a.s.$ means [[几乎必然收敛|almost-sure convergence]]. On those paths, eventually $B_N>c/2$, so division is valid and $A_N/B_N\to\mu$. More explicitly, $(A_N-\mu B_N)/B_N\to0$: the numerator tends to zero while the denominator stays away from zero. A nonzero denominator limit is the step that makes two converging averages yield a converging ratio.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

符号 $a.s.$ 表示[[几乎必然收敛]]。在这些路径上，最终必有 $B_N>c/2$，于是可以相除且 $A_N/B_N\to\mu$。更明确地，$(A_N-\mu B_N)/B_N\to0$：分子趋于零，分母则远离零。分母极限非零，正是把两个均值的收敛变成比率收敛的关键。

## Early zero denominators do not vanish by definition
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*前期零分母仍须明确处理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The assumptions allow zero weights on part of the proposal space. Set $a=P_q(\widetilde w(X)>0)$. Because $E_q\widetilde w=c>0$, we have $a>0$. For fixed $N$, independence gives $P(S_N=0)=(1-a)^N$, where $S_N=\sum_{i=1}^N\widetilde w(X_i)$. This can be positive. Along one sequence, the first positive weight arrives in finite time almost surely; all later weight sums are positive because the weights are nonnegative.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设允许提议空间的某些地方权重为零。记 $a=P_q(\widetilde w(X)>0)$，因为 $E_q\widetilde w=c>0$，所以 $a>0$。对固定 $N$，独立性给出 $P(S_N=0)=(1-a)^N$，其中 $S_N=\sum_{i=1}^N\widetilde w(X_i)$，这个概率可能为正。沿同一条序列，第一次正权重几乎必然在有限时间出现；之后权重和始终为正，因为各权重非负。

> [!important] Define the sequence, then take its limit · 先定义序列，再讨论极限
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> To make a real-valued estimator for every $N$, assign any fixed finite value on $S_N=0$ and use the ratio otherwise. Under this theorem that fallback affects only an almost surely finite initial segment, so it does not change strong consistency. It can change finite-sample expectations. Resampling until a positive total appears instead changes the sampling procedure and needs its own finite-sample analysis.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若要让每个 $N$ 都对应一个实值估计量，可在 $S_N=0$ 时填入任意固定有限数，其余时候使用比率。在本定理条件下，这只影响几乎必然有限的一段开头，不改变强一致性，却可能改变有限样本期望。若改成一直重抽直到权重和为正，则改变了抽样程序，需要另外分析有限样本性质。

## What fails when coverage is missing
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*漏掉目标区域时会趋向什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Full target coverage is a sufficient general condition, not a logical necessity for every particular $h$. Let $Q=\{q>0\}$. If coverage fails but $P_p(Q)>0$, the same proof instead gives the ratio of partial integrals:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

完整目标覆盖是一般充分条件，并非对每个特殊 $h$ 都逻辑必要。记 $Q=\{q>0\}$。若漏掉部分目标，但 $P_p(Q)>0$，同样证明得到的却是部分积分之比：

$$
\widehat\mu_N\xrightarrow{a.s.}
\frac{\int_Q h(x)p(x)\,dx}{\int_Q p(x)\,dx}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $P_p(Q)=\int_Q p(x)\,dx$ is the target probability of the visible region. This limit renormalises the target inside $Q$; it need not equal the original mean. In the two-point model, sampling only $1$ makes SNIS always $1$, not $3/4$. Ordinary IS for $h(x)=x$ could omit $0$, whose contribution is zero, and still return $3/4$. SNIS also estimates the denominator, so that narrower [[重要性抽样支持条件|fixed-integrand coverage rule]] cannot simply be copied. Constants and other special functions may coincide despite missing coverage; if $P_p(Q)=0$, all weights are zero almost surely and the ratio is unavailable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里 $P_p(Q)=\int_Qp(x)\,dx$ 是目标分布给可见区域的概率。该极限在 $Q$ 内重新归一化目标，不一定等于原均值。在二点模型中，若只抽 $1$，SNIS 就恒为 $1$，而非 $3/4$。普通 IS 对 $h(x)=x$ 可以漏掉零贡献的 $0$，仍返回 $3/4$；SNIS 还估计分母，不能直接照搬较窄的[[重要性抽样支持条件|固定被积函数覆盖规则]]。常数及某些特殊函数仍可能恰好相等；若 $P_p(Q)=0$，权重几乎必然全部为零，比率无法使用。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The theorem uses first absolute moments, not finite second moments. It therefore supplies no general finite-sample bias bound, convergence of expectations, finite variance or usual normal interval. Those require additional analysis of the ratio and weighted terms. An adaptive proposal or dependent simulation chain is also outside this iid proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本定理使用一阶绝对矩，并不要求二阶矩有限。因此它不提供普遍的有限样本偏差界、期望收敛、有限方差或通常的正态区间；这些都需要进一步分析比率及加权项。自适应提议或依赖模拟链，也不属于这份 iid 证明的范围。
