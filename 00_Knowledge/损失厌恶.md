# 损失厌恶是同幅损失的负面价值大于同幅收益的正面价值
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Loss aversion assigns a larger negative value to a loss than the positive value of an equal-sized gain*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

损失厌恶是相对于同一[[前景参考点]]、在所讨论幅度区间内，等量损失比等量收益具有更大的价值影响。令 $v(0)=0$，对可比较的 $a>0$，这里用下式表达这种得失不对称：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Loss aversion is an asymmetry around the same [[前景参考点|reference point]]: over the magnitudes under discussion, an equal-sized loss has a greater value impact than a gain. With $v(0)=0$, this is expressed for comparable $a>0$ as
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$-v(-a)>v(a).$$

一个示意函数是 $v(x)=\sqrt{x}$（$x\ge0$），$v(x)=-2\sqrt{-x}$（$x<0$）。于是收益 $100$ 的价值为 $10$，损失 $100$ 的价值为 $-20$。系数 $2$ 是自拟示例，不是损失厌恶的定义常数或所有人的经验参数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An illustrative function is $v(x)=\sqrt{x}$ for gains and $v(x)=-2\sqrt{-x}$ for losses. A gain of $100$ has value $10$ and a loss of $100$ has value $-20$. The factor of two is illustrative, not a defining constant or a universal empirical estimate.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个比较跨越收益与损失两侧，不能仅由收益侧凹或损失侧凸来判定；也不直接表示任何风险选择都会被拒绝。选择还取决于备选结果和[[前景决策权重]]，具体区别见[[损失厌恶与风险厌恶]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This comparison spans gains and losses; gain-side concavity or loss-side convexity alone does not establish it. Nor does it imply rejection of every risky choice. Choices also depend on the alternatives and [[前景决策权重|decision weights]]; see [[损失厌恶与风险厌恶|loss aversion versus risk aversion]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
