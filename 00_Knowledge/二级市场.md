# 二级市场是投资者买卖已经发行证券的市场
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A secondary market is a market for trading securities that have already been issued.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

二级市场让既有证券换持有人。买方把钱付给卖方、取得证券，通常没有新增证券，也没有把这笔买价直接交给发行人。它既可以通过[[证券交易所]]组织，也可以发生在[[场外市场]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A secondary-market trade changes the holder of an existing security. The buyer pays the seller and acquires the security; ordinarily, no new security is created and the purchase price does not go directly to the issuer. Trading can occur through an [[证券交易所|exchange]] or in an [[场外市场|OTC market]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

甲把此前认购的债券以 980 元卖给乙，发行人的合同债务通常没有因为这次转让新增 980 元。乙此后能主张哪些付息、还本权利，仍取决于债券合同及转让安排。二级市场的作用是提供退出和价格信号；它对后续融资的影响见[[二级流动性与发行价格]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If A sells a previously acquired bond to B for 980, the issuer does not ordinarily incur an additional debt of 980 merely because ownership changes. B's payment rights depend on the bond contract and transfer arrangements. Secondary trading provides an exit and price signals; its connection to future issuance is explained in [[二级流动性与发行价格|secondary liquidity and issue pricing]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
