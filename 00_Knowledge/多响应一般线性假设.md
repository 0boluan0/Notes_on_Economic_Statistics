# General linear hypotheses for multiple responses
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*多响应一般线性假设*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A model may describe two scores for each student, yet the question of interest can be much narrower: does a teaching programme have zero association with **both** scores, or does it have the **same** association with the two scores? These questions can disagree. If the two programme coefficients are both $5$, equality holds while the claim that both are zero fails. A general linear hypothesis writes the intended question precisely before a test statistic is chosen.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

模型可以同时描述每名学生的两项成绩，但实际问题往往更具体：某教学项目在**两项**成绩上的系数是否都为零，还是两项系数是否**相等**？这两个问题可以得到不同答案。如果两项系数都是 $5$，相等成立，“都为零”却不成立。一般线性假设先把所问的问题写准确，再选择检验统计量。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Choose coefficient rows and response columns
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*选择系数行与响应列*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In [[多响应线性回归|multivariate linear regression]] $Y=XB+U$, the $k\times q$ matrix $B$ has one row for each encoded predictor term and one column for each response. An intercept counts as a predictor term here. Left [[矩阵乘法|matrix multiplication]] combines rows; right multiplication combines columns. Use fixed matrices
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[多响应线性回归]] $Y=XB+U$ 中，$k\times q$ 系数矩阵 $B$ 的每行对应一个已编码回归项，每列对应一个响应，这里的回归项包含使用的截距。[[矩阵乘法]]中，左乘组合行，右乘组合列。取固定矩阵

$$
C\in\mathbb R^{g\times k},\qquad A\in\mathbb R^{q\times s},\qquad
D\in\mathbb R^{g\times s},\qquad H_0:CBA=D.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$C$ selects or contrasts coefficient rows; its $g$ rows describe predictor-side restrictions. The $s$ columns of $A$ select or combine responses. $D$ states the proposed values, which need not be zero. $H_0$ denotes the [[零假设与备择假设|null hypothesis]] being tested. The matrix sizes make $CBA$ and $D$ comparable, but do not by themselves guarantee estimability, independent restrictions, or a valid reference distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$C$ 选择或对比系数行，它的 $g$ 行描述预测变量方向的限制；$A$ 的 $s$ 列选择或组合响应；$D$ 给出假设中的数值，可以非零。$H_0$ 表示要检验的[[零假设与备择假设|零假设]]。尺寸相容使 $CBA$ 能与 $D$ 比较，但尺寸本身不保证可估性、限制互不冗余或参考分布有效。

> [!example] Two distinct questions · 两个不同的问题
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let the two rows of $B$ be the intercept and a programme indicator's coefficient; let its columns be mathematics and reading scores on a comparable scale. Taking $C=[0\ \ 1]$ selects the programme row.
>
> With $A=I_2$ (the identity leaves the two responses unchanged) and $D=[0\ \ 0]$, the null says both programme coefficients are zero. With $A=(1,-1)^T$ and $D=[0]$, it says their difference is zero. If that row of $B$ is $(2,5)$, these left sides are $(2,5)$ and $-3$, respectively. Setting the second $D$ to $[-3]$ would instead ask whether the difference equals $-3$.
>
> For three responses, $A=(1,-1,0)^T$ compares the first two and ignores the third. A raw difference needs meaningful compatible units; otherwise specify scientifically meaningful weights, as in [[响应尺度与线性变换|response transformations]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $B$ 两行分别为截距和项目指示变量的系数，两列为尺度可比较的数学与阅读成绩。$C=[0\ \ 1]$ 取出项目那一行。若 $A=I_2$、$D=[0\ \ 0]$，问题是两项系数是否都为零；若 $A=(1,-1)^T$、$D=[0]$，问题是两项之差是否为零。项目系数为 $(2,5)$ 时，两个左端分别为 $(2,5)$ 和 $-3$。把后一问题的 $D$ 改成 $[-3]$，才是在问差值是否为 $-3$。
>
> 三个响应时，$A=(1,-1,0)^T$ 比较前两个，忽略第三个。直接相减需要单位相容且差值有实际含义；否则应先按科学问题规定权重，见[[响应尺度与线性变换]]。

## Compare the hypothesis and the error in the same space
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*假设与误差必须处于同一空间*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the following ordinary formula, assume the shared design $X$ has [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full column rank]], $C$ has $g\ge1$ linearly independent rows, and $A$ has $s\ge1$ linearly independent columns. Fit [[共享设计的逐响应OLS|shared-design OLS]] to obtain $\hat B$. Its [[多响应残差与误差SSP|residual SSP]] is $E_{SSP}=R^TR$, where $R=Y-X\hat B$. Define
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面的常规公式假设共享设计 $X$ [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]，$C$ 的 $g\ge1$ 行线性无关，$A$ 的 $s\ge1$ 列线性无关。由[[共享设计的逐响应OLS|共享设计 OLS]]得到 $\hat B$；令 $R=Y-X\hat B$，[[多响应残差与误差SSP|残差 SSP]] 为 $E_{SSP}=R^TR$。定义

$$
\Delta=C\hat BA-D\in\mathbb R^{g\times s},\qquad
G=C(X^TX)^{-1}C^T\in\mathbb R^{g\times g},
$$
$$
H_A=\Delta^TG^{-1}\Delta,\qquad E_A=A^TE_{SSP}A.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$\Delta$ is the estimated departure from the null, not the unknown population departure. $G$ gives the design's scale for coefficient contrasts; the rank conditions make it [[正定矩阵|positive definite]] and invertible. $H_A$ is the hypothesis SSP, measuring that departure in the design scale. $E_A=(RA)^T(RA)$ summarizes residual variation in exactly the same $s$ transformed responses. Both are $s\times s$. In particular, $A=I_q$ gives the full response SSP; for a general $A$, compute the projected SSP rather than mixing two different response spaces.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$\Delta$ 是估计得到的偏离，不是未知的总体偏离；$G$ 给出系数对比在设计中的尺度，秩条件使它[[正定矩阵|正定]]且可逆。$H_A$ 是按这个尺度度量偏离的假设 SSP；$E_A=(RA)^T(RA)$ 汇总完全相同的 $s$ 个变换响应的残差变动。两者都是 $s\times s$。特别地，$A=I_q$ 给出完整响应 SSP；一般 $A$ 则要实际计算投影后的 SSP，不能混用不同响应空间。

> [!example] Compute a one-response contrast · 算出一个响应对比
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Use four observations, a group indicator $t=(0,0,1,1)^T$, and
> $$X=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\1&0\\1&1\\1&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
> Y=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\3&2\\4&6\\4&8\end{bmatrix}.$$
> The group means are $(2,2)$ and $(4,7)$, so
> $$\hat B=\begin{bmatrix}2&2\\2&5\end{bmatrix},\qquad
> R=\begin{bmatrix}-1&0\\1&0\\0&-1\\0&1\end{bmatrix},\qquad E_{SSP}=2I_2.$$
> For $C=[0\ \ 1]$, $A=(1,-1)^T$ and $D=0$, the inverse Gram matrix is $\begin{bmatrix}1/2&-1/2\\-1/2&1\end{bmatrix}$, hence $G=1$, $\Delta=2-5=-3$, $H_A=9$ and $E_A=4$. The transformed response column is $(-1,1,-2,-4)^T$; its residuals are $(-1,1,1,-1)^T$, whose squares indeed sum to $4$. This is a one-response difference question. Testing both original coefficients against zero instead gives $H=\begin{bmatrix}4&10\\10&25\end{bmatrix}$ and $E=2I_2$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取四条观测，分组指示变量为 $t=(0,0,1,1)^T$，设计与响应矩阵如上。两组均值分别为 $(2,2)$、$(4,7)$，所以截距为 $(2,2)$、组间系数差为 $(2,5)$，得到所列 $\hat B,R,E_{SSP}$。选择 $C=[0\ \ 1]$、$A=(1,-1)^T$、$D=0$ 后，逆 Gram 矩阵的右下角为 $1$，因此 $G=1$、$\Delta=-3$、$H_A=9$、$E_A=4$。变换响应列为 $(-1,1,-2,-4)^T$，其残差为 $(-1,1,1,-1)^T$，平方和确实为 $4$。这是单个响应差的问题；若检验两个原始系数都为零，才得到所列 $2\times2$ 的 $H$ 与 $E$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The displayed arithmetic does not produce a p-value by itself. Classical [[MANOVA四种统计量|multivariate criteria]] and their [[MANOVA检验校准|reference laws]] additionally require the relevant error model, estimable restrictions and adequate residual rank. A usual exact model has fixed $X$, correct mean $XB$ and iid Gaussian error rows with positive-definite covariance $\Sigma$; the transformed covariance is $A^T\Sigma A$. The restriction dimension is $g$, the effective response dimension is $s$, and residual degrees of freedom are $n-\operatorname{rank}(X)$. Realized $\operatorname{rank}(H_A)$ need not equal $g$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些矩阵运算本身还不给出 p 值。经典[[MANOVA四种统计量|多元统计量]]及其[[MANOVA检验校准|参考分布]]还要求相应误差模型、限制可估和足够残差秩。常用精确模型固定 $X$、真实均值为 $XB$，误差行为 iid Gaussian、协方差 $\Sigma$ 正定；变换后的协方差为 $A^T\Sigma A$。限制维数为 $g$，有效响应维数为 $s$，残差自由度为 $n-\operatorname{rank}(X)$；实际 $\operatorname{rank}(H_A)$ 不一定等于 $g$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A valid question must survive the parameterization
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*问题必须不依赖任意的参数表示*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With a rank-deficient design, different coefficient matrices can describe the same mean table. A coefficient contrast is **estimable** when its value is fixed by that mean table. For example, two identical intercept columns identify only the sum of their coefficients: adding $1$ to the first and subtracting $1$ from the second changes no fitted mean. A test of the first coefficient alone therefore depends on an arbitrary choice; a test of their sum can be meaningful. For a predictor-side contrast $c^T$, the condition is that $c^T$ belongs to the [[行空间|row space]] of $X$, equivalently it vanishes on the [[零空间|nullspace]] of $X$. This checks that all coefficient changes invisible to the mean are also invisible to the contrast.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

秩亏设计中，不同系数矩阵可以描述同一均值表。**可估**表示对比的数值能由这张均值表确定。例如，两列完全相同的截距只能确定系数之和；第一个系数加 $1$、第二个减 $1$，拟合均值不变。因此只检验第一个系数会依赖任意选择，检验二者之和则可以有明确含义。对预测变量侧的对比 $c^T$，条件是它属于 $X$ 的[[行空间]]，等价于它在 $X$ 的[[零空间]]上取零：凡是均值看不见的系数改变，对比也必须看不见。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Redundant rows of $C$ or columns of $A$ should be reduced to independent directions **only after checking that $D$ respects the same dependencies**. Repeating one restriction with proposed values $0$ and $1$ gives an impossible null; dropping either row would change it. Generalized-inverse formulas for estimable functions can handle a deficient design, but do not identify arbitrary coefficients. A direction chosen after seeing which result looks strongest also needs [[选择后推断|selection-aware inference]]. Finally, a [[多响应联合显著边界|joint rejection]] does not identify every response as significant, and a coefficient restriction alone supplies no [[多响应因果边界|causal identification]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $C$ 行或 $A$ 列冗余，应在**检查 $D$ 满足同样依赖关系后**缩到独立方向。同一限制一行要求等于 $0$、另一行要求等于 $1$，零假设根本不可能成立，任意删掉一行都会改变问题。可估函数可用广义逆处理秩亏设计，但广义逆不会识别任意系数。若看过结果后才挑最强方向，还需要[[选择后推断]]。最后，[[多响应联合显著边界|总体拒绝]]不意味着每个响应显著，系数限制本身也不提供[[多响应因果边界|因果识别]]。
