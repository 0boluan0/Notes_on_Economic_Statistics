# Added-variable plots
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*附加变量图（偏回归图）*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An added-variable plot shows the part of a multiple regression that determines **one predictor's fitted coefficient**. If both income and education predict an outcome, an income-versus-outcome scatterplot mixes their associations. The added-variable plot first removes the other included predictors' linear explanation from **both** the outcome and the chosen predictor, then plots what remains against what remains.

The horizontal coordinate is therefore no longer the original income. It is how much that observation's income exceeds or falls short of the value fitted from the other predictors. The vertical coordinate has the analogous meaning for the outcome. A straight line through this new cloud has exactly the chosen coefficient from the complete [[普通最小二乘|ordinary least-squares fit]]. This is a visual form of the [[FWL残差化定理|Frisch–Waugh–Lovell identity]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

附加变量图展示多元回归中**决定某一个解释变量拟合系数的那部分关系**。若收入与教育都用来预测结果，原始“收入—结果”散点会混合二者的关联。附加变量图先从**结果和所关注变量两边**分别扣除其他已含变量的线性解释部分，再把剩余部分对剩余部分作图。

横坐标因此不再是原收入，而是这条观测的收入比“用其他变量拟合出的收入”高或低多少；纵坐标对结果作同样处理。这组新散点上的直线斜率，恰好等于完整[[普通最小二乘]]中所关注变量的系数。这是 [[FWL残差化定理]]的图形表达。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Residualise both axes on the same controls
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两边都对同一组控制变量取残差*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use $n$ complete observations and an unweighted fit. Let $y$ be the $n$-vector of outcomes, $x$ the chosen predictor column, and $Z$ the $n\times r$ matrix of other included columns, **including the intercept**. Here a control means an included regressor; its inclusion alone does not justify a causal interpretation. Suppose $[Z\ x]$ has [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|full column rank]], so the full coefficient vector is unique. The full fit can be written $y=Z\hat\gamma+x\hat\beta+e$, with fitted control coefficients $\hat\gamma$, chosen slope $\hat\beta$, and [[回归残差|residual vector]] $e$.

Regress $y$ on $Z$ and retain residuals $v$. Separately regress $x$ on the **same** $Z$ using the same rows and retain residuals $w$. Plot $(w_i,v_i)$. Matrix notation packages these two operations: $P_Z$ is the [[满列秩投影公式|projection that returns fitted values from $Z$]], $I_n$ leaves an $n$-vector unchanged, and $M_Z=I_n-P_Z$ subtracts that fitted part.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

采用 $n$ 条完整观测及不加权拟合。$y$ 是 $n$ 维结果向量，$x$ 是所关注的一列解释变量，$Z$ 是由其他已含列组成的 $n\times r$ 矩阵，**包括截距列**。这里“控制”只表示放入了回归，不能凭这个词获得因果解释。假设 $[Z\ x]$ [[满列秩与OLS唯一性|满列秩]]，完整系数向量因而唯一。把完整拟合写成 $y=Z\hat\gamma+x\hat\beta+e$，其中 $\hat\gamma$ 是控制列的拟合系数，$\hat\beta$ 是所关注的斜率，$e$ 是[[回归残差|残差向量]]。

把 $y$ 对 $Z$ 回归，保留残差 $v$；另把 $x$ 对**同一个** $Z$ 回归，使用同样的观测行，保留残差 $w$；画 $(w_i,v_i)$。矩阵写法把这两步合在一起：$P_Z$ 是[[满列秩投影公式|用 $Z$ 返回拟合值的投影]]，$I_n$ 保持 $n$ 维向量不变，$M_Z=I_n-P_Z$ 则扣除拟合部分。

$$
P_Z=Z(Z'Z)^{-1}Z',\qquad
M_Z=I_n-P_Z,\qquad
v=M_Zy,\quad w=M_Zx.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A prime denotes transpose. The [[FWL残差化定理|FWL identity]] now gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

撇号表示转置。此时 [[FWL残差化定理|FWL 恒等式]]给出

$$
v=\hat\beta w+e,\qquad
w'e=0,\qquad
\hat\beta=\frac{w'v}{w'w}.
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thus the fitted line passes through the origin, and its vertical residuals are the **same** $e_i$ as in the full regression. The denominator $w'w=\sum_i w_i^2$ is positive by full column rank: $x$ contains some variation that $Z$ cannot reproduce. The identities need no normality or independence assumption. Since the intercept is in $Z$, both plotted coordinates have sample mean zero; adding a free intercept to the final line also estimates zero. If $x$ is completely reproduced by $Z$, then $w=0$, the slope formula divides by zero, and there is no separately identified coefficient to display.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此拟合线经过原点，竖直残差仍是完整回归中的**同一组** $e_i$。满列秩保证分母 $w'w=\sum_iw_i^2>0$：$x$ 中存在 $Z$ 无法完全重现的变动。这些恒等式不需要正态性或独立性假设。由于 $Z$ 含截距，两个坐标的样本均值都为零；最后拟合线即使再放入自由截距，也会得到零。若 $x$ 已被 $Z$ 完全重现，则 $w=0$，斜率公式会除以零，也就没有能单独识别的系数可展示。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The raw slope can be different
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*原始斜率可以与它不同*

> [!example] Four observations / 四条观测
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take a control $z$ and the data below. The full regression on $1,z,x$ fits $\hat y=10+3z+2x$. Regressing $x$ on $1,z$ fits $\hat x= z$; regressing $y$ on $1,z$ fits $10+5z$. Subtract these two fitted values to obtain the added-variable coordinates.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对下表的控制变量 $z$ 和数据，完整回归使用 $1,z,x$，得到 $\hat y=10+3z+2x$。把 $x$ 对 $1,z$ 回归，拟合值为 $\hat x=z$；把 $y$ 对 $1,z$ 回归，拟合值为 $10+5z$。分别扣除这两个拟合值，就得到附加变量图坐标。
>
> | $z$ | $x$ | $y$ | $w=x-z$ | $v=y-(10+5z)$ | $e=v-2w$ |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | $-1$ | $-2$ | $4$ | $-1$ | $-1$ | $1$ |
> | $-1$ | $0$ | $6$ | $1$ | $1$ | $-1$ |
> | $1$ | $0$ | $12$ | $-1$ | $-3$ | $-1$ |
> | $1$ | $2$ | $18$ | $1$ | $3$ | $1$ |
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Here $w'v=8$ and $w'w=4$, so the plotted slope is $2$, the full-model coefficient. An ordinary line through the original $(x,y)$ points with an intercept has slope $28/8=3.5$. That larger raw slope includes the linear association carried by $z$. The calculation illustrates adjustment for an included column, not a claim that controlling for $z$ makes the coefficient causal.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这里 $w'v=8$、$w'w=4$，所以图中斜率为 $2$，与完整模型系数相同。对原始 $(x,y)$ 含截距拟合直线，斜率却是 $28/8=3.5$。这个较大的原始斜率混入了 $z$ 所携带的线性关联；算例说明如何扣除已含列，并没有声称控制 $z$ 后就有因果解释。

## Read coefficient information and unusual points
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*读出系数信息与特殊观测*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A large $|w_i|$ means observation $i$ has unusual predictor variation **after** the chosen controls. It can carry substantial leverage for this coefficient even if its original $x_i$ looks ordinary. Conversely, an extreme original $x_i$ may be well predicted by $Z$ and contribute little remaining variation. A large vertical departure from the fitted line is the full-model residual, so it flags a poorly fitted outcome.

The precise connection to [[Hat杠杆值|leverage]] is $h_{ii}^{\rm full}=h_{ii}^{Z}+w_i^2/(w'w)$: $h_{ii}^{Z}$ is leverage from the control-only fit, and the extra nonnegative term is the leverage added by this column. This follows because $w$ is perpendicular to every column of $Z$. Horizontal position therefore describes one contribution to leverage, not total leverage. [[异常杠杆影响区分|Actual influence]] asks how a specified fitted result changes when a case is removed; it depends on more than horizontal position. Investigate unusual cases and refit when assessing deletion effects—do not simply erase a point from coordinates that were calculated using it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$|w_i|$ 很大，说明观测 $i$ 在**扣除这些控制列以后**仍有特殊的解释变量变动。即使原始 $x_i$ 不显眼，它也可能对这个系数有较大的杠杆作用；反过来，原始 $x_i$ 很极端，却可能被 $Z$ 预测得很好，剩余变动并不多。偏离图中拟合线很远的点，其竖直差就是完整模型残差，提示该结果拟合得不好。

与 [[Hat杠杆值|杠杆值]]的准确联系是 $h_{ii}^{\rm full}=h_{ii}^{Z}+w_i^2/(w'w)$：$h_{ii}^{Z}$ 是仅用控制列拟合时的杠杆值，后面的非负项是加入这一列所增加的杠杆。这来自 $w$ 与 $Z$ 每一列都垂直。因此横向位置表示一部分杠杆，而不是总杠杆。[[异常杠杆影响区分|实际影响]]关心删去观测后某个指定拟合结果改变多少，不只取决于横向位置。评估删点影响时应调查观测并重新拟合，不能只从已经使用该观测计算出来的坐标中擦去一个点。

> [!warning] The horizontal axis has changed / 横轴已经改变
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A curve in $v$ against $w$ is not directly a response curve in the original $x$. This plot can expose unusual structure, but it does not generally reveal which nonlinear transformation of $x$ is appropriate. Use a [[偏残差图|partial-residual plot]] to retain the original $x$ axis when examining shape, and check its own limitations too. Neither plot establishes exogeneity or causality.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> $v$ 对 $w$ 的曲线并不是原始 $x$ 对应的响应曲线。它可以暴露特殊结构，却不能一般地告诉你该对原始 $x$ 采用什么非线性变换。检查形状时，可用保留原始 $x$ 横轴的[[偏残差图]]，同时注意那张图自身的限制。两种图都不能证明外生性或因果关系。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Keep the sample, controls, and fitting rule identical throughout. The formulas here concern unweighted OLS; weighted fits require a corresponding weighted construction. Also, equality of slopes and residuals does not license reading the final one-predictor regression's default standard error: retain the full model's degrees of freedom and the appropriate [[标准误口径匹配|covariance-estimation rule]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

各步骤必须使用同样的样本、控制列及拟合规则。本卡公式针对不加权 OLS，加权拟合需要相应的加权构造。斜率与残差相同，也不表示可以直接读取最后这个单变量回归默认给出的标准误；仍要沿用完整模型的自由度，以及恰当的[[标准误口径匹配|协方差估计口径]]。
