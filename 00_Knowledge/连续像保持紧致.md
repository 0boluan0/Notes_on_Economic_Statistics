# Continuous images of compact spaces
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*连续像保持紧致*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Start with a compact set of inputs and apply a continuous map. The set of outputs is compact too. Here [[紧致性|compactness]] means that every cover by open sets has a finite subcover; [[连续性|continuity]] means that preimages of open sets are open. The theorem works because continuity turns an output-covering problem into an input-covering problem, where compactness can be used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从一个紧致的输入集合出发，经过连续映射后，得到的输出集合仍然紧致。这里的[[紧致性]]指每个开覆盖都有有限子覆盖；[[连续性]]指开集的原像仍是开集。定理成立的原因是：连续性把“覆盖所有输出”的问题拉回为“覆盖所有输入”的问题，便能调用输入集合的紧致性。

> [!example] Squaring a closed interval · 把闭区间内的数平方
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $K=[0,2]$ and $f(x)=x^2$. The input set is compact by the [[Heine–Borel 定理的边界|Heine–Borel theorem]], and $f$ is continuous. Its image, meaning the set of all outputs, is $f(K)=[0,4]$. The theorem guarantees compactness of this output set even before we describe its endpoints. Other continuous maps may fold or collapse the input set; they need not be one-to-one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $K=[0,2]$、$f(x)=x^2$。输入集合由 [[Heine–Borel 定理的边界|Heine–Borel 定理]]知为紧集，$f$ 连续。所有输出组成的像集是 $f(K)=[0,4]$。即使尚未写出这两个端点，定理也已经保证像集紧致。其他连续映射可以把输入集合折叠或压缩，不必一一对应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The statement and the two directions of a map
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*定理，以及映射的正向像与原像*

> [!important] Compact image theorem · 紧致像定理
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> If $K$ is a compact topological space, $Y$ is a topological space and $f:K\to Y$ is continuous, then the image $f(K)=\{f(x):x\in K\}$ is compact with its [[子空间拓扑|subspace topology]] from $Y$. Neither space needs a metric. The theorem also includes $K=\varnothing$, whose image is empty and compact.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 若 $K$ 是紧致拓扑空间，$Y$ 是拓扑空间，且 $f:K\to Y$ 连续，则像集 $f(K)=\{f(x):x\in K\}$ 在来自 $Y$ 的[[子空间拓扑]]下紧致。不要求两个空间有度量。允许 $K=\varnothing$：此时像集为空集，仍然紧致。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A forward image takes inputs to their outputs. A preimage goes the other way at the level of sets: for $V\subseteq Y$, $f^{-1}(V)=\{x\in K:f(x)\in V\}$ collects all inputs whose outputs lie in $V$. This notation does not require an inverse function. If the original map is defined on a larger space $X$ and $K\subseteq X$ is compact, apply the theorem to its restriction to $K$; continuity is retained under restriction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

正向像把输入变成输出。原像则在集合层面反向追问：给定 $V\subseteq Y$，$f^{-1}(V)=\{x\in K:f(x)\in V\}$ 收集所有输出落在 $V$ 内的输入。这个记号不要求存在反函数。如果原映射定义在更大的空间 $X$ 上，而 $K\subseteq X$ 紧致，就把映射限制在 $K$ 上使用定理；限制映射仍然连续。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Pull back a cover, then keep finitely many sets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*把覆盖拉回去，再留下有限多个集合*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Choose any family $\{V_i\}_{i\in I}$ of sets open in $Y$ that covers $f(K)$. Testing such ambient open covers is equivalent to testing relatively open covers of $f(K)$: every relatively open set has the form $V\cap f(K)$ for an open set $V$ in $Y$.

For each input $x\in K$, its output belongs to at least one $V_i$, so $x\in f^{-1}(V_i)$. These preimages therefore cover $K$. Continuity makes each preimage open in $K$. Compactness now provides finitely many indices $i_1,\ldots,i_m$ with
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

任取一族在 $Y$ 中开的集合 $\{V_i\}_{i\in I}$，使它们覆盖 $f(K)$。检验这类环境开覆盖，等价于检验 $f(K)$ 自己的相对开覆盖：每个相对开集都可以写成 $V\cap f(K)$，其中 $V$ 在 $Y$ 中开。

对每个输入 $x\in K$，输出 $f(x)$ 至少落在一个 $V_i$ 里，所以 $x\in f^{-1}(V_i)$。因此这些原像覆盖 $K$；连续性又保证每个原像在 $K$ 中开。紧致性于是给出有限多个索引 $i_1,\ldots,i_m$，满足

$$
K=\bigcup_{j=1}^{m}f^{-1}(V_{i_j}).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Now take any output $y\in f(K)$. By the meaning of image, $y=f(x)$ for some $x\in K$. That input lies in one selected preimage, so its output lies in the corresponding $V_{i_j}$. Thus the selected $V_{i_1},\ldots,V_{i_m}$ cover $f(K)$. The original open cover was arbitrary, so the image is compact. For the empty domain the empty subfamily already suffices.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

再任取一个输出 $y\in f(K)$。根据像集的含义，存在 $x\in K$ 使 $y=f(x)$。这个输入属于某个选中的原像，因此它的输出属于对应的 $V_{i_j}$。于是选中的 $V_{i_1},\ldots,V_{i_m}$ 覆盖 $f(K)$。原开覆盖是任意选的，所以像集紧致。定义域为空时，直接选空的子族便已足够。

> [!tip] What the proof transfers · 证明到底传递了什么
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Continuity supplies **open preimages**; compactness supplies **finitely many indices**. We then use the open sets with those same indices to cover the image. We do not need forward images of open sets to be open, or the identity $f(f^{-1}(V))=V$: the correct identity is $f(f^{-1}(V))=V\cap f(K)$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 连续性提供**开的原像**，紧致性提供**有限多个索引**，最后用这些索引对应的开集覆盖像集。证明不需要开集的正向像仍开，也不需要 $f(f^{-1}(V))=V$；正确恒等式是 $f(f^{-1}(V))=V\cap f(K)$。

## What cannot be reversed
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*哪些方向不能反推*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A compact image does not force a compact domain. The continuous constant map $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$, $f(x)=0$, has the compact image $\{0\}$. Its domain is not compact: the open intervals $(-n,n)$, for positive integers $n$, cover $\mathbb R$, but no finite subfamily does. This example also shows that a continuous map need not have compact preimages of compact sets, since $f^{-1}(\{0\})=\mathbb R$.

Continuity cannot simply be dropped from the forward theorem. On the compact interval $[0,1]$, set $g(0)=0$ and $g(x)=1/x$ for $x>0$. This map is discontinuous at zero, and its image $\{0\}\cup[1,\infty)$ is unbounded, hence not compact in $\mathbb R$. When the map is continuous, the domain is **nonempty**, and outputs are real numbers, compactness of the image yields the [[极值定理|extreme value theorem]]: a largest and a smallest output are actually attained.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

像集紧致不能反推出定义域紧致。连续常值映射 $f:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R$、$f(x)=0$ 的像集是紧集 $\{0\}$，但定义域不紧致：正整数 $n$ 对应的开区间 $(-n,n)$ 覆盖整条实线，却没有有限子覆盖。这同时说明，连续映射未必把紧集的原像变成紧集，因为 $f^{-1}(\{0\})=\mathbb R$。

正向定理中的连续性也不能直接删掉。在紧区间 $[0,1]$ 上，令 $g(0)=0$，并对 $x>0$ 令 $g(x)=1/x$。此映射在零点不连续，像集 $\{0\}\cup[1,\infty)$ 无界，因此在实线中不紧致。当映射连续、定义域**非空**且输出为实数时，紧致像进一步给出[[极值定理]]：最大和最小的输出都确实能取到。
