# 冒泡排序每轮把未定部分的最大元素推进已定后缀

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Each bubble-sort pass moves the largest unsettled item into the settled suffix*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 原子不变量
> 从左到右比较相邻项目，只在左键大于右键时交换；完成一轮后，当前未定部分的最大项目已经到达其最终位置。重复直到一整轮没有交换，就得到有序排列。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Scan left to right through adjacent items and swap only when the left key is greater than the right key. After one pass, the largest item in the unsettled region has reached its final position. Repeating until a full pass makes no swap yields a sorted permutation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 自然解释

最大项目遇到右边更小的邻居时不断向右交换，像气泡上浮。若实现带“本轮是否交换”的标记，已经有序的输入只需一次线性扫描；完全逆序时仍需要平方级比较和移动。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The largest item repeatedly swaps rightward whenever it meets a smaller neighbour, like a rising bubble. With a “did this pass swap?” flag, sorted input needs one linear scan; reverse input still requires quadratic comparisons and moves.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!warning] 边界
> $\Theta(n)$ 最好情形依赖提前停止；固定执行全部轮次的变体即使输入有序仍做平方级比较。稳定性也依赖只交换严格逆序对：若相等键也交换，就会破坏原相对顺序。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The $\Theta(n)$ best case requires early stopping; a variant that always executes all passes still performs quadratically many comparisons on sorted input. Stability also requires swapping only strict inversions: swapping equal keys can reverse their relative order.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 回忆与应用
> 为什么“一轮没有交换”足以结束？
>
> **答案：** 因为每一对相邻项目都已满足非下降关系；相邻关系全部有序就推出整个序列有序。
