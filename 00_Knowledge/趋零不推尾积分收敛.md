# A vanishing integrand can have a divergent tail
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*趋零不推尾积分收敛*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A decreasing flow rate can become arbitrarily small while the amount delivered over unlimited time keeps growing. The height $f(x)$ at a late position and the accumulated total $\int_a^T f(x)\,dx$ answer different questions. **Knowing only that $f(x)\to0$ does not tell us that the total approaches a finite number.**
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一个不断下降的流量，可以小到任意程度，但在无限长时间里送出的总量仍然不断增长。远处某一位置的高度 $f(x)$ 与累计总量 $\int_a^T f(x)\,dx$ 回答的是不同问题。**只知道 $f(x)\to0$，不能推出总量趋于某个有限数。**

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the [[反常积分|definition of an improper integral]], each cutoff $T$ is finite and the function must be integrable on each interval $[a,T]$. Convergence then means a finite limit of these integral values as $T\to\infty$. In contrast, a [[无穷极限与无穷远极限|function limit at infinity]] tests the individual values $f(x)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[反常积分]]的定义中，每个截断点 $T$ 都有限，函数必须在各个 $[a,T]$ 上可积；收敛要求这些积分值在 $T\to\infty$ 时具有有限极限。相比之下，[[无穷极限与无穷远极限|函数在无穷远的极限]]检查的是单个函数值 $f(x)$。

> [!example] $1/x$: small heights, unlimited accumulation · 高度趋零，累积无界
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Take $f(x)=1/x$ on $[1,\infty)$. For any requested height $\varepsilon>0$, every $x>\max\{1,1/\varepsilon\}$ satisfies $0<f(x)<\varepsilon$. This proves $f(x)\to0$. The function is continuous on every finite $[1,T]$, and its [[自然对数|natural-logarithm]] antiderivative gives, by the [[Newton-Leibniz公式|endpoint formula]],
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $[1,\infty)$ 上的 $f(x)=1/x$。给定任意高度要求 $\varepsilon>0$，每个 $x>\max\{1,1/\varepsilon\}$ 都满足 $0<f(x)<\varepsilon$，所以 $f(x)\to0$。它在每个有限区间 $[1,T]$ 上连续，[[自然对数]]是它的原函数，由 [[Newton-Leibniz公式|端点公式]]得到
>
> $$A(T)=\int_1^T\frac{dx}{x}=\ln T-\ln1=\ln T.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> As $T\to\infty$, $\ln T\to+\infty$. Thus $\int_1^\infty dx/x$ diverges. The integrand tends to zero, but the accumulation function $A$ has no finite limit. This single example disproves the proposed implication, even for positive, continuous, strictly decreasing functions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 当 $T\to\infty$ 时，$\ln T\to+\infty$，因此 $\int_1^\infty dx/x$ 发散。被积函数趋于零，但累积函数 $A$ 没有有限极限。这个例子已经否定了所讨论的推出关系，即使函数为正、连续且严格递减也不够。

## Why the ever-smaller heights still add up
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*越来越小的高度为什么仍然累积无界*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Split the tail into $[1,2]$, $[2,4]$, $[4,8]$, and so on. On the $k$th block $[2^k,2^{k+1}]$, where $k=0,1,2,\ldots$, the exact contribution is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把尾部拆成 $[1,2]$、$[2,4]$、$[4,8]$ 等倍增区间。在第 $k$ 段 $[2^k,2^{k+1}]$（$k=0,1,2,\ldots$）上，精确贡献为

$$\int_{2^k}^{2^{k+1}}\frac{dx}{x}
=\ln(2^{k+1})-\ln(2^k)=\ln2.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The interval doubles in width as the endpoint heights halve, leaving the same positive contribution each time. After $n$ blocks, [[定积分区间可加性|interval additivity]] gives $\int_1^{2^n}dx/x=n\ln2$. These totals grow without bound.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

区间宽度加倍时，两端高度减半，每段仍留下相同的正贡献。累积 $n$ 段以后，由[[定积分区间可加性]]得到 $\int_1^{2^n}dx/x=n\ln2$；这些总量无界增长。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
There is also a simple lower rectangle: throughout that block, $1/x\ge1/2^{k+1}$, while the width is $2^k$. Its area is $1/2$, so each block contributes at least $1/2$. Because $f\ge0$, accumulated area never decreases as $T$ increases. Once $T\ge2^n$, the total is at least $n/2$; this proves divergence for all sufficiently large cutoffs, not just for the doubling sequence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

也可以只看一个内接矩形：在整段上都有 $1/x\ge1/2^{k+1}$，宽度是 $2^k$，所以矩形面积为 $1/2$，每段至少贡献 $1/2$。由于 $f\ge0$，$T$ 增大时累计面积不会减少。一旦 $T\ge2^n$，总量至少为 $n/2$；这证明了对所有充分大的截断点都发生发散，而不只是倍增数列上的现象。

## What extra information can decide the integral
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*还需要什么信息才能判断积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Compare $1/x$ with $1/x^2$. Both tend to zero, but
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

将 $1/x$ 与 $1/x^2$ 比较：它们都趋于零，但

$$\int_1^T\frac{dx}{x^2}=1-\frac1T\longrightarrow1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The [[无穷远 p 积分判别|power-integral test]] explains the distinction in this family: $\int_1^\infty x^{-p}\,dx$ converges precisely for real $p>1$. For a different nonnegative integrand, a valid [[反常积分直接比较判别|comparison with a known tail]] may provide the needed control. Being merely smaller than $1/x$ is not enough: $1/(2x)$ is smaller everywhere on this interval, yet its truncated total is $\tfrac12\ln T\to\infty$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[无穷远 p 积分判别]]说明了这一函数族的区别：实数 $p$ 对应的 $\int_1^\infty x^{-p}\,dx$ 恰在 $p>1$ 时收敛。对其他非负被积函数，可以尝试[[反常积分直接比较判别|与已知尾部作有效比较]]，获得所需控制。仅仅小于 $1/x$ 仍不够：$1/(2x)$ 在此区间上处处更小，但它的截断总量仍为 $\tfrac12\ln T\to\infty$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This card disproves the implication from a zero function limit to a finite integral. The reverse implication is a separate question: without extra assumptions, a finite integral need not force the integrand to tend to zero either. That direction is explained by the narrowing spikes in [[尾积分收敛不推函数趋零|a convergent tail with nonvanishing heights]]. Neither direction should be borrowed from the behaviour of an infinite series.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

本卡否定的是“函数极限为零，就有有限积分”这个方向。反向是另一问题：不加额外假设，积分有限也未必迫使函数趋零；该方向由[[尾积分收敛不推函数趋零|高度不降、宽度缩小的尖峰反例]]解释。两个方向都不能直接照搬无穷级数的性质。

> [!question]- After seeing $f(x)\to0$ · 看到趋零以后
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For a positive function tending to zero, what should you investigate next? Ask how much it contributes over long intervals, or find a justified comparison with a known convergent or divergent tail. The single limit value has not answered that question.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对趋于零的正函数，下一步应研究什么？问它在越来越长的区间上贡献多少累积量，或寻找有依据的比较，把它与已知收敛或发散的尾部联系起来。只知道极限值，还没有回答这个问题。
