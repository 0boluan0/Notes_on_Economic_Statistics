# Causal interpretation of multiple-response regression
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*多响应回归的因果解释*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
People who attend a training programme may score higher in both maths and reading. A multiple-response regression can describe these differences and assess their sampling uncertainty. A causal question asks something further: **how would these same people have performed under a different intervention?** Recording more outcomes does not by itself make programme attendance comparable to random assignment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

参加培训的人，数学和阅读成绩可能都更高。多响应回归可以描述这些差异，并评估它们的抽样不确定性。因果问题还要多问一步：**如果对同一批人采用另一种干预，他们的表现会怎样？** 多记录几个结果，并不会自动使参加培训这件事变得像随机分配一样可比。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Define the intervention and the effect
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*先定义干预与效应*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $T=1$ denote assignment to a clearly specified training programme and $T=0$ assignment to its specified comparison. Here $T$ is an assignment indicator, not a test statistic. For outcome $j$, the [[潜在结果反事实|potential outcome]] $Y_j(t)$ is what that person would have under assignment $t$. Collect the outcomes into the vector $Y(t)$. If each coordinate is [[期望|integrable]], meaning its expected absolute value is finite, the vector of average causal effects is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $T=1$ 表示被分配到一个定义清楚的培训方案，$T=0$ 表示被分配到指定的对照方案。这里 $T$ 是分配指示变量，不是检验统计量。对第 $j$ 个结果，[[潜在结果反事实|潜在结果]] $Y_j(t)$ 表示这个人在分配为 $t$ 时会得到的结果；把各结果收成向量 $Y(t)$。若各坐标[[期望|可积]]，即绝对值的期望有限，平均因果效应向量为

$$
\tau=E[Y(1)-Y(0)].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
This compares two interventions in the **same target population**. In contrast, $E[Y\mid T=1]-E[Y\mid T=0]$ compares the people who actually received different assignments. These groups may have differed before training. Each person's [[反事实不可同时观察|unobserved counterfactual]] is why a bridge from the observed comparison to $\tau$ is needed. Even $\tau=0$ says only that average effects are zero; it does not say that every individual's effect is zero.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个量比较的是**同一目标总体**在两种干预下的结果。相比之下，$E[Y\mid T=1]-E[Y\mid T=0]$ 比较实际分到不同组的人；他们在培训之前就可能不同。每个人都有[[反事实不可同时观察|无法同时观察的反事实结果]]，因此需要额外依据把观察比较连接到 $\tau$。即便 $\tau=0$，它也只表示平均效应为零，不表示每个人的效应都为零。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Two clear associations with no causal effect
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*两个明确的关联，却没有因果效应*

> [!example]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Let $L$ be readiness measured before training: half the population has $L=1$ and half has $L=0$. More-ready people are more likely to enter training:
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 设 $L$ 为培训前的准备程度：总体一半为 $L=1$，一半为 $L=0$。准备较充分的人更容易进入培训：
>
> $$
> P(T=1\mid L=1)=0.8,\qquad P(T=1\mid L=0)=0.2.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Suppose that for either assignment $t=0,1$, potential scores are
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 假设无论分配为 $t=0$ 还是 $t=1$，潜在成绩均为
>
> $$
> Y_1(t)=50+10L+\varepsilon_1,\qquad
> Y_2(t)=40+5L+\varepsilon_2.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The noise vector has mean zero, finite variances, and is independent of $L$ and $T$. The same person's noise is held fixed across the two assignments. Because $t$ does not enter either expression, every person's treatment effect is zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 噪声向量均值为零、方差有限，并与 $L,T$ 独立；比较同一个人的两种分配时，使用同一份噪声。由于两个表达式都不含 $t$，每个人的处理效应都是零。
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> But $P(T=1)=0.5$. [[Bayes法则|Bayes' rule]] gives $P(L=1\mid T=1)=0.8$ and $P(L=1\mid T=0)=0.2$. Thus the observed group means are
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 然而 $P(T=1)=0.5$。由 [[Bayes法则|Bayes 法则]]得到 $P(L=1\mid T=1)=0.8$、$P(L=1\mid T=0)=0.2$，所以观察到的组均值是
>
> $$
> E[Y\mid T=1]=\begin{pmatrix}58\\44\end{pmatrix},
> \qquad
> E[Y\mid T=0]=\begin{pmatrix}52\\41\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The observed difference is $(6,3)^{\mathsf T}$, although the causal difference is $(0,0)^{\mathsf T}$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 观察差异为 $(6,3)^{\mathsf T}$，因果差异却为 $(0,0)^{\mathsf T}$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The observational [[条件期望|conditional mean]] is exactly $E[Y\mid T]=(52,41)^{\mathsf T}+T(6,3)^{\mathsf T}$. A correctly specified linear conditional-mean model can therefore have nonzero assignment coefficients even when there is no causal effect. With independent sampled people, a suitably calibrated association test can detect these population differences. The example does not assert a Gaussian error model; exact MANOVA calibration is a separate assumption to check.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这里的观察[[条件期望|条件均值]]恰好是 $E[Y\mid T]=(52,41)^{\mathsf T}+T(6,3)^{\mathsf T}$。因此，条件均值线性模型即使设定正确，分配系数也可能不为零，而实际因果效应仍为零。若独立抽样得到这些人，适当校准的关联检验可以识别这些总体差异。这个例子并未假设高斯误差；精确 MANOVA 校准还需要另行检查。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## What connects an observed mean to a causal mean?
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*什么条件能把观察均值接到因果均值？*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
One common sufficient route uses pretreatment covariates $L$ and the [[条件均值因果边界|identification conditions for conditional means]]. **Consistency** says that the observed outcome equals the potential outcome under the assignment actually received: $Y=Y(T)$. The interventions must be well defined. Writing only $Y(t)$ also assumes that other people's assignments do not change this person's outcome; spillovers require a richer intervention description.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

一条常用的充分路径，是借助处理前协变量 $L$ 和[[条件均值因果边界|条件均值的识别条件]]。**一致性**要求观察结果等于实际分配对应的潜在结果，即 $Y=Y(T)$；干预本身必须定义清楚。只写 $Y(t)$ 也假设别人的分配不会改变这个人的结果；若存在溢出效应，就需要更丰富的干预描述。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Conditional exchangeability** requires $Y(t)\perp T\mid L$ for each $t=0,1$: among people with the same $L$, assignment carries no further information about the potential-outcome vector. **Positivity** requires $0<P(T=1\mid L)<1$ for almost every covariate value in the target population. It ensures that both assignments are represented where the causal comparison is needed. Measuring more covariates or obtaining a small p-value does not prove exchangeability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**条件交换性**要求对 $t=0,1$ 都有 $Y(t)\perp T\mid L$：在 $L$ 相同的人中，分配不再提供有关潜在结果向量的额外信息。**正值性**要求目标总体中几乎处处满足 $0<P(T=1\mid L)<1$，保证需要进行因果比较的协变量区域中，两种分配都有支持。多记录几个协变量或得到很小的 p 值，都不能证明交换性。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With integrable outcomes and these conditions, define $m_t(l)=E[Y\mid T=t,L=l]$. Coordinate by coordinate,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在结果可积且上述条件成立时，定义 $m_t(l)=E[Y\mid T=t,L=l]$。逐坐标有

$$
E[Y(t)\mid L]
=E[Y(t)\mid T=t,L]
=E[Y\mid T=t,L],
$$
$$
\tau=E_L[m_1(L)-m_0(L)].
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Exchangeability justifies the first equality and consistency the second; positivity makes the observed comparison available. The outer expectation averages over the target population's covariate distribution. In the readiness example, comparing people within the same $L$ removes the observed difference and recovers the zero effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

交换性支持第一个等号，一致性支持第二个等号，正值性保证对应的观察比较可获得。外层期望按目标总体的协变量分布取平均。在准备程度的例子中，在同一 $L$ 内比较就会消除观察差异，恢复零因果效应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are sufficient assumptions for this route, not necessary conditions for every causal design. Even when identification is justified, a coefficient from a [[条件均值与线性投影|linear projection]] need not equal the target average effect if effects vary with $L$ or the mean model is misspecified. If the identified conditional means truly satisfy $m_t(l)=h(l)+t\tau$ with a constant vector $\tau$, then that vector does represent the causal contrast. The model and the estimand must match.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些是这条识别路径的充分假设，不是每一种因果设计都必须满足的条件。即便已有识别依据，若效应随 $L$ 改变，或均值模型设定错误，[[条件均值与线性投影|线性投影]]中的某个系数也未必等于目标平均效应。如果已识别的条件均值确实满足 $m_t(l)=h(l)+t\tau$，且 $\tau$ 是固定向量，它才表示这里的因果对比。模型必须与目标量相匹配。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## A causal joint test needs both design and calibration
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*因果联合检验同时需要设计依据和统计校准*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[随机分配识别|Random assignment]] can provide exchangeability by design. Consistency, adequate treatment support, outcome follow-up, and the target population still matter. For example, randomizing an invitation identifies an effect of assignment under the appropriate conditions; actual attendance may be selected, so its effect is a different question. Other designs, such as valid instrumental-variable designs, use other assumptions and may target a different causal effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[随机分配识别|随机分配]]可以由设计提供交换性，但一致性、足够的处理支持、结果随访和目标总体仍然重要。例如，随机发出邀请，在适当条件下识别的是分配效应；实际参加与否可能经过选择，其效应就是另一个问题。有效工具变量等其他设计使用不同的假设，也可能识别不同的因果效应。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Once a causal mean vector has been identified, a prespecified, correctly calibrated multivariate test can test a causal null about that vector. The design or identification argument supplies the causal interpretation; Pillai, Wilks, or the matrix form $CBA=D$ does not supply it. Rejection still carries sampling uncertainty, and [[多响应联合显著边界|joint rejection does not locate every affected response]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在因果均值向量得到识别后，预先规定且校准正确的多元检验，可以检验关于该向量的因果零假设。因果解释来自设计或识别论证，不是由 Pillai、Wilks 或矩阵形式 $CBA=D$ 提供。拒绝结果仍带有抽样不确定性，而且[[多响应联合显著边界|联合拒绝不会直接定位每个受影响的响应]]。

> [!tip]
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A causal report should say what intervention and population are being compared, which outcomes and time points are targeted, why assignment groups are comparable, and which model supplies uncertainty. “The multivariate p-value is small” answers none of these questions by itself.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因果报告应说明比较什么干预和总体、关注哪些结果及时间点、为什么分配组可比，以及用什么模型量化不确定性。“多元检验的 p 值很小”本身回答不了这些问题。
