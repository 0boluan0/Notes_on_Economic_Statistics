# 财政收入改变交易净值而融资改变金融资产和负债的组合
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Revenue changes net worth through transactions; financing changes financial assets and liabilities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

政府收到钱，先问它因为什么收到。按GFS，征税与发债不是同一类：前者形成[[财政收入]]，后者形成融资负债。归还借款本金是消灭负债，支付利息才是费用；把本金也算成当期费用，会把同一借款的取得和偿还误当成两次收入支出。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Ask why money was received. In GFS, taxation generates [[财政收入|revenue]]; debt issuance creates a financing liability. Principal repayment extinguishes debt, whereas interest is an expense. Treating principal as an expense confuses financing with current revenue and expenditure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

下面四项都可带来现金，但只有第一项涉及收入确认；已经确认过的应收税款，在收现时不再增加收入。假定资产按当前价值出售，没有另含赠与或价格重估。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Each transaction below can bring in cash, but only the first involves revenue recognition; collection of an already recognised tax receivable does not generate revenue again. Asset sales are at current recorded value, without a separate transfer or revaluation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 交易 | GFS中的主要记录 | 对净借出／净借入余额的直接影响 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 收取本期已确认的税款 | 收入；若此前已记应收，则收款只结清应收 | 收入确认时提高余额，不在收款时重复算 |
| 新借款 | 现金与负债同时增加 | 融资项，本身不改善余额 |
| 出售股票资产 | 一项金融资产换成现金 | 融资项，本身不改善余额 |
| 出售原有建筑物 | 非金融资产处置换成现金 | 减少非金融资产净投资，改善该余额，但不是收入 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taxes improve the balance when recognised, not again upon collection. Borrowing and equity-asset sales are financing transactions. Selling an existing building reduces net investment in nonfinancial assets and improves net lending/borrowing, without becoming revenue.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

最后一行尤其重要：不能把所有资产出售都叫“表下融资”，也不能把卖楼后赤字缩小解释成经常性税收能力提高。下一步用[[财政赤字]]把收入、费用、非金融资产和融资四部分接起来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Not every asset sale is below-the-line financing, and a smaller deficit after selling a building does not establish stronger recurring revenue capacity. [[财政赤字|The fiscal deficit]] connects the four account components.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
