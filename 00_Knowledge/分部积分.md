# Integration by parts
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*分部积分*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
**Integration by parts** trades one integral for another by moving a derivative from one factor to the other. The trade is useful when the new integral is easier to calculate, or when repeated trades produce an equation we can solve. It comes from the [[乘积法则|product rule for derivatives]], which accounts for the changes of both factors of a product.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**分部积分**把导数从一个因子转移到另一个因子，从而用一个新积分替换原积分。新积分更容易算，或者重复几次后能形成可解的等式，这种交换才有用。它来自[[乘积法则]]：乘积的变化要同时计入两个因子的变化。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider $\int xe^x\,dx$, where an [[原函数|indefinite integral]] asks for functions whose derivative is $xe^x$. Differentiating $xe^x$ almost gives what we want, but the [[指数函数求导|exponential derivative]] and product rule produce an extra $e^x$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

考虑 $\int xe^x\,dx$。这里的[[原函数|不定积分]]要求找出导数为 $xe^x$ 的函数。对 $xe^x$ 求导几乎就得到目标，但[[指数函数求导]]与乘积法则会多产生一项 $e^x$：

$$(xe^x)'=xe^x+e^x,\qquad (xe^x-e^x)'=xe^x.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Subtracting an antiderivative of that extra term solves the problem: $\int xe^x\,dx=e^x(x-1)+C$. Integration by parts organizes exactly this subtraction.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

减去多出那一项的一个原函数，就解决了问题：$\int xe^x\,dx=e^x(x-1)+C$。分部积分正是把这种减法系统地写下来。

## What the symbols mean
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*公式里的符号分别做什么*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write the integrand as $u(x)v'(x)$. Choose $u$ to differentiate, and choose $v'$ whose antiderivative $v$ you can obtain. The [[微分|differential notation]] $du=u'(x)\,dx$ and $dv=v'(x)\,dx$ is shorthand for these derivatives; $dv$ includes $dx$, and $v$ is one chosen antiderivative of $v'$, not the whole family of them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

把被积函数写成 $u(x)v'(x)$。选 $u$ 来求导，再选一个能求出原函数 $v$ 的 $v'$。[[微分]]记号 $du=u'(x)\,dx$、$dv=v'(x)\,dx$ 是这些导数的简写；$dv$ 包含 $dx$，而 $v$ 是 $v'$ 的一个选定原函数，不是它的全部原函数组成的族。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Assume $u,v$ have continuous first derivatives on an open interval. The product rule gives $(uv)'=u'v+uv'$, hence $uv'=(uv)'-vu'$. If $H$ is any antiderivative of $vu'$, differentiating $uv-H$ gives $uv'$. Thus the usual shorthand is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

假设 $u,v$ 在一个开区间上具有连续的一阶导数。乘积法则给出 $(uv)'=u'v+uv'$，所以 $uv'=(uv)'-vu'$。若 $H$ 是 $vu'$ 的任一原函数，对 $uv-H$ 求导便得到 $uv'$。通常简写为

> [!important] A product minus the new integral · 乘积减去新积分
> $$\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du.$$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> This identity describes antiderivatives up to an additive constant on the interval. Calculate one representative, then include $+C$ in the final indefinite answer. The minus sign comes from rearranging the product rule.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 这个恒等式按“区间上相差一个加法常数”的意义理解。算出一个代表后，在最终不定积分答案中加入 $+C$。负号来自乘积法则的移项。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For the opening example, $u=x$, $du=dx$, $dv=e^x dx$, $v=e^x$. The formula gives $xe^x-\int e^x dx$, which is the subtraction already found. Choosing $v+K$ instead of $v$, with $K$ constant, makes no difference to the family of answers: the extra product $Ku$ is cancelled by an antiderivative of $Ku'$. The [[原函数相差常数|constant-difference theorem]] explains the final freedom in $C$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

开头的例子取 $u=x$、$du=dx$、$dv=e^x dx$、$v=e^x$。公式得到 $xe^x-\int e^x dx$，正是前面发现的减法。若把 $v$ 换成 $v+K$（$K$ 为常数），答案族不变：多出的乘积 $Ku$ 会被 $Ku'$ 的原函数抵消。最终的 $C$ 自由度由[[原函数相差常数]]解释。

## Definite integrals keep both limits
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*定积分必须保留两端与余下积分的限*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $a<b$ be finite and suppose $u,v$ are continuous on $[a,b]$, differentiable inside, and their first derivatives extend continuously to the endpoints. These are the usual $C^1([a,b])$ sufficient conditions. Integrating the rearranged product rule and using the [[Newton-Leibniz公式|endpoint evaluation theorem]] gives
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $a<b$ 为有限数；$u,v$ 在 $[a,b]$ 连续、内部可导，而且一阶导数能连续延伸到端点。这就是通常的 $C^1([a,b])$ 充分条件。对重排后的乘积法则积分，再使用 [[Newton-Leibniz公式|端点评价定理]]，得到

$$\int_a^b u(x)v'(x)\,dx
=\underbrace{u(b)v(b)-u(a)v(a)}_{[uv]_a^b}
-\int_a^b v(x)u'(x)\,dx.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The bracket means “upper endpoint value minus lower endpoint value.” The remaining integral still has the same limits. For example,
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

方括号表示“上端点值减去下端点值”。余下的积分仍保留相同上下限。例如

$$\int_0^1xe^x\,dx=[xe^x]_0^1-\int_0^1e^x\,dx=e-(e-1)=1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A definite integral is a number, so this result has no $+C$. If an endpoint is infinite or the integrand has a singularity, first work on an ordinary finite interval and then check the required [[反常积分|improper-integral limits]]. The finite-interval formula alone does not justify subtracting divergent quantities.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

定积分给出一个数，因此答案没有 $+C$。若端点在无穷远，或被积函数有奇点，应先在普通有限区间应用公式，再核对[[反常积分]]要求的极限。有限区间公式本身不能授权把发散的量直接相减。

## Choose a trade you can actually carry out
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*选择真正能执行的交换*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $x>0$, write $\ln x=(\ln x)\cdot1$. Choosing $u=\ln x$, $dv=dx$ gives $du=dx/x$ by the [[对数函数求导|logarithm derivative]], and $v=x$. Therefore
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在 $x>0$ 上，把 $\ln x$ 写成 $(\ln x)\cdot1$。取 $u=\ln x$、$dv=dx$，由[[对数函数求导]]得到 $du=dx/x$，并且 $v=x$。于是

$$\int\ln x\,dx=x\ln x-\int x\frac1x\,dx=x\ln x-x+C.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Taking $dv=\ln x\,dx$ while this is the very integral you do not yet know would assume the answer as a prerequisite. The algebraic identity remains true for an existing $v$, but that choice has supplied no way to find it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果当前还不会算的恰好是这个积分，却取 $dv=\ln x\,dx$，就把待求答案当成了前提。对已经存在的 $v$，代数恒等式仍然成立；只是这种选择没有提供找到 $v$ 的办法。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
LIATE is a memory aid for trying logarithmic, inverse trigonometric, algebraic, trigonometric and exponential factors as $u$, in that order. It is not a theorem or a requirement to use whole visible factors. Check whether $v$ is obtainable and what happens to the remaining integral. For $xe^x$, reversing the useful choice to $u=e^x$, $dv=x\,dx$ gives $x^2e^x/2-\frac12\int x^2e^x dx$: the identity is valid, but the polynomial degree has increased.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

LIATE 是一个尝试顺序的记忆提示：对数、反三角、代数、三角、指数因子依次优先选作 $u$。它不是定理，也没有要求必须整块选取眼前的某个因子。应检查 $v$ 是否能求，以及新积分发生了什么变化。对 $xe^x$，若反过来取 $u=e^x$、$dv=x\,dx$，得到 $x^2e^x/2-\frac12\int x^2e^x dx$：恒等式合法，但多项式次数反而提高了。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Repeated integration can also be useful without becoming simpler at every step. If the original integral returns with a coefficient that lets us solve a new equation, use [[循环分部积分|cyclic integration by parts]]. Each individual step must still use an antiderivative already available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

重复使用时，不必要求每一步都变得更简单。如果原积分重新出现，而且系数能形成一个可解的新等式，就可以使用[[循环分部积分]]。但每一步仍必须采用已经能得到的原函数。
