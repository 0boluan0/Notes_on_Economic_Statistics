# 公债改变筹款和税负时点而不推迟已经发生的政府购买所占用的资源
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Debt financing changes funding and tax timing, not the date when an already-made government purchase uses resources.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

比较同一条政府购买路径的税收融资与[[政府公债|举债融资]]时，必须区分筹款和资源使用。政府今天购买劳动、设备或服务，今天就占用了这些资源；发行债券取得资金，并不把这次购买变成未来才发生的资源成本。未来税款和偿债支付改变谁在何时付款与收款，其真实经济后果还取决于储蓄、投资、税收扭曲和分配。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a fixed path of government purchases, distinguish funding from resource use. Buying labour, equipment or services today uses resources today, whether financed by taxes or [[政府公债|public borrowing]]. Future taxes and debt service change who pays and receives funds; their real consequences also depend on saving, investment, tax distortions and distribution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设只有今天与明年两期，政府今天购买价值 $10$ 的服务、明年没有购买，借款年利率为 $10\%$。今天征税 $10$，或今天借款 $10$、明年征税 $11$ 偿还，均安排了今天价值 $10$ 的购买。按同一利率作[[现金流同点折现加总]]，后者税款现值为 $11/1.1=10$；这只说明此例两种税款日程的现值相同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
With two dates, suppose the government buys services worth $10$ today and nothing next year, at a borrowing rate of $10\%$. It can tax $10$ now, or borrow $10$ and tax $11$ next year. Both finance today's purchase. Using [[现金流同点折现加总|a common valuation date]], the later tax has present value $11/1.1=10$. This establishes equal tax present values in the example, not neutrality of every economic effect.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若偿债收款人与纳税人都在同一社会核算边界内，同期、同单位、同权重的税款与债权收入须按[[转移支付核算]]配对；不能把偿债金额再当作同一笔政府服务的第二次资源消耗。对国外债权人的支付不在国内账户内抵消，筹税的行为扭曲也不是纯转移。即使内部支付抵消，私人资本或公共资产路径仍可能改变。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When taxpayers and creditors are both inside the social accounting boundary, match taxes and creditor receipts using [[转移支付核算|transfer accounting]] with common dates, units and weights. Debt service is not a second use of the resources already purchased. Payments to foreign creditors do not cancel within a domestic account, and tax distortions are not pure transfers. Internal cancellation also does not imply unchanged private capital or public assets.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，“今天借钱”既不是“今天没有成本”，也不能单凭偿还日期推出“全部成本由后代承担”。固定购买路径下的储蓄反应见[[公债挤出条件]]，代际净效应见[[公债代际负担]]，税收时点何时中性见[[李嘉图等价]]。若比较的是新增公共项目，还必须另评项目的服务和资产收益，而不是只比较融资方式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Borrowing today neither removes today's resource cost nor proves that all costs fall on future generations. See [[公债挤出条件|crowding-out conditions]], [[公债代际负担|intergenerational incidence]] and [[李嘉图等价|Ricardian equivalence]] for those separate questions. A new public project additionally requires assessment of its services and assets, not merely its financing method.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
