# 概率为零的事件不必是空事件

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An event of probability zero need not be empty*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$P(A)=0$ 只表示 $A$ 没有概率质量，不保证 $A=\varnothing$。例如在 $[0,1]$ 的均匀概率空间中，每个单点集合 $\{x\}$ 都非空却概率为 0。空事件的概率必为 0，反向一般不成立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Zero probability does not imply an empty event. Every singleton in a uniform probability space on the unit interval is nonempty but has zero probability. The implication from emptiness to zero probability has no general converse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
不可把不可数多个单点的零概率按可数可加性相加：公理只允许有限或可数和。整个 $[0,1]$ 的概率仍是 1，最终实现某个精确值与单点零概率并不矛盾。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Countable additivity cannot be applied to an uncountable union of singletons. The whole interval still has probability one, and a realised exact value is compatible with zero probability at every fixed singleton.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
