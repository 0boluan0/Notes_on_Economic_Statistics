# 利润增长可能被新增营运资本和资本开支抵消而不增加当期自由现金流
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Profit growth may be offset by additional working-capital investment and capital expenditure, leaving less current free cash flow.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

利润按会计确认时间计量，现金还要看回款、存货付款与资本投入。比较增长方案时，应按照[[项目增量现金流]]把“采用方案”与“不采用方案”在同一期间比较；不要把利润的同比增量与另一口径的当期总投资拼起来。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Profit follows accounting recognition; cash also depends on collection, inventory payments, and capital investment. Under [[项目增量现金流|incremental cash-flow analysis]], compare the with-plan and without-plan states over the same period. Do not combine year-over-year incremental profit with total investment measured on a different basis.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设同一来年有以下两种方案。为隔离经营机制暂设税率为零，所有金额使用同一单位，其他现金流不变：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Consider two plans for the same coming year. To isolate the operating mechanism, set tax to zero, use one common monetary unit, and hold other cash flows fixed:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

| 项目 | 不扩张 | 扩张 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 收入 | 1,000 | 1,200 |
| EBIT（利润率 10%） | 100 | 120 |
| 当年新增经营营运资本 | 0 | 45 |
| 当年资本开支减折旧 | 0 | 15 |
| FCFF | 100 | 60 |

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Without expansion: revenue 1,000, EBIT 100, no net reinvestment, FCFF 100. With expansion: revenue 1,200, EBIT 120, working-capital investment 45 and net capex 15, FCFF 60.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

扩张方案的营运资本 45 由新增应收 30、存货 25、应付 10 构成：$30+25-10=45$。因此扩张相对不扩张的当年现金流差为：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Expansion requires 30 more receivables and 25 more inventory, partly financed by 10 more payables: $30+25-10=45$. Its same-year cash-flow difference relative to no expansion is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$120-45-15-100=20-45-15=-40.$$

负 40 是**两种方案的差额**，不是扩张企业的总现金流为负，也不是已证明项目毁值。后来回款、资产使用与处置的现金还没计算；完整价值判断继续用[[净现值决策]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Negative 40 is the difference between plans, not negative total company cash flow or proof of value destruction. Later collections, asset use, and disposal remain to be counted; use [[净现值决策|NPV]] for the full value judgment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [SEC, Beginners' Guide to Financial Statements, Cash Flow Statements](https://www.sec.gov/about/reports-publications/beginners-guide-financial-statements)：核对利润、非现金调整、经营资产负债与投资付款的区别。
- [[项目增量现金流]]与[[企业自由现金流]]：复用方案比较和 FCFF 口径；表格及差额为独立构造与验算。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The SEC supports the distinction between profit, noncash adjustments, operating assets/liabilities, and investment payments. The existing incremental-cash-flow atom and FCFF definition supply the consistent comparison basis. The table and differences are independently constructed and checked.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
