# 博弈类型是玩家的私人信息状态，并通过对收益参数与他人类型的信念编码其信念层级
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A game-theoretic type is a player's private information state and encodes a hierarchy of beliefs about payoff parameters and other players' types*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

玩家 $i$ 的**类型** $t_i$ 是其私人信息状态。它既可包含成本、估值或能力等私人信息，也确定玩家对未知收益参数和其他玩家类型的条件信念，因而间接编码更高阶信念。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Player $i$'s **type** $t_i$ is their private information state. It may contain private information about costs, valuations, or abilities, and it determines conditional beliefs about unknown payoff parameters and other players' types, thereby encoding higher-order beliefs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 类型怎样确定信念

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How a Type Determines Beliefs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

给定 $t_i$，玩家持有

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given $t_i$, the player holds
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
p_i(\theta,t_{-i}\mid t_i)
$$

来判断物理参数和他人类型。[[类型与收益参数]]解释信息状态 $t_i$ 与物理状态 $\theta$ 的区别；不能从简化记号推出“类型只是一项成本数字”。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Given $t_i$, the player uses $p_i(\theta,t_{-i}\mid t_i)$ to assess the payoff parameter and other players' types. [[类型与收益参数|Types and payoff parameters]] distinguishes the information state $t_i$ from the physical state $\theta$; simplified notation does not make a type merely a cost number.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 类型怎样承载信念层级

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How Types Carry Hierarchies of Beliefs*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

- 一阶 belief：玩家对 $\theta$ 等物理状态的概率判断；
- 二阶 belief：玩家对他人一阶 beliefs 的判断；
- 更高阶 belief：继续判断他人怎样判断自己的判断。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- First-order belief: the player's probabilistic assessment of physical states such as $\theta$;
- second-order belief: the player's assessment of other players' first-order beliefs;
- higher-order belief: further assessments of how others assess the player's own assessments.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[Harsanyi类型空间]]通过 $t_i$ 对 $(\theta,t_{-i})$ 的 belief 递归生成这些层级。[[实际与假想类型剖面]]进一步说明未实际发生的类型剖面为何仍能参与这套表示。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A [[Harsanyi类型空间|Harsanyi type space]] recursively generates these levels from the type's belief over $(\theta,t_{-i})$. [[实际与假想类型剖面|Actual and hypothetical type profiles]] explains why profiles that are not realized can still be needed in the representation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 类型不是行动，也不是现实中的新增玩家

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Type Is Neither an Action nor an Additional Real Player*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

低成本企业和高成本企业可以是同一家企业的两个可能类型。企业实际选择“进入”才是行动；完整的[[贝叶斯策略]]还要分别说明低成本与高成本时怎样行动。有限模型中把各类型列作辅助决策者的做法见[[类型代理表示]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A low-cost firm and a high-cost firm can be two possible types of the same firm. “Enter” is an action, while a complete [[贝叶斯策略|Bayesian strategy]] prescribes what the firm does at both types. The finite-model device that lists types as auxiliary decision makers is explained in [[类型代理表示|the interim-agent representation]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 某企业有高、低两种成本类型，并可选择进入或退出。以下三者中，哪一个是类型、哪一个是行动、哪一个才可能是完整策略：低成本；进入；“低成本时进入、高成本时退出”？
>
> **答案：** “低成本”是类型，“进入”是行动，“低成本时进入、高成本时退出”才是覆盖两个可能类型的完整纯策略。把两个成本类型列成两个代理玩家只是一种求解表示。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **Self-check.** A firm can have a high-cost or low-cost type and can choose to enter or stay out. Among the following, which is a type, which is an action, and which can be a complete strategy: low cost; enter; “enter when low cost and stay out when high cost”?
>
> **Answer:** “Low cost” is a type, “enter” is an action, and “enter when low cost and stay out when high cost” is the complete pure strategy covering both possible types. Listing the two cost types as two agent players is only a solution representation.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
