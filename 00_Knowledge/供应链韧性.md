# 供应链韧性是供应链在冲击后通过适应和调整恢复稳定运作的能力
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Supply chain resilience is the capacity to adapt and adjust after a shock in order to restore stable operations.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

供应链韧性关心的是：原料、生产或运输遭到干扰后，整条链能否调整采购、生产和交付安排，以及多快、多有效地恢复所需的服务。按照这里采用的 OECD 口径，韧性的证据是恢复能力，不是从未发生中断；评价时必须说清要恢复的产品、数量、质量与服务水平。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Supply chain resilience concerns whether a chain can adjust sourcing, production and delivery after disruptions to inputs, production or transport, and how quickly and effectively it restores the required service. Under the OECD definition used here, resilience is demonstrated by recovery capacity rather than the absence of disruption. An assessment must specify the product, quantity, quality and service level to be restored.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，两条链都要每日交付 100 件合格零件，遭遇同一种港口中断后，一条用 10 天、另一条用 3 天恢复并稳定达到这一交付水平。后一条在这个情景中恢复更快；这不能证明它面对停电、需求骤变或另一种运输中断也一定更有韧性。保持危机期间不停产的能力通常另称稳健性（robustness），与冲击后的恢复能力相关，但不是同一个观察指标。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, two chains each need to deliver 100 compliant components per day. After the same type of port disruption, one takes ten days and the other three days to regain and sustain that delivery level. The second recovers faster in this scenario; this does not establish its superiority under power failures, sudden demand changes or other transport disruptions. The capacity to continue producing during a crisis is commonly termed robustness. It is related to recovery capacity but is not the same observable measure.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实现韧性可以同时依靠冗余和调整能力：库存、备用产能或供应商提供缓冲；兼容的替代投入、可转换的物流和生产安排支持切换；信息、预警、人员训练与决策权限使这些选择能及时启用。备用资源要付出成本，名单上有替代供应商也不等于能立即供货。因此，要检查实际恢复路径，不能只数库存或供应商，更不能只看[[国产化与供应链韧性|国产比例]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Resilience can draw on both redundancy and adaptation. Inventories, spare capacity and backup suppliers provide buffers; compatible substitutes and switchable logistics and production arrangements enable adjustment; information, early warnings, training and decision authority allow timely execution. Backup resources are costly, and a supplier appearing on a list need not be ready to deliver. Assessment therefore requires a feasible recovery path, not just counts of inventories or suppliers, and certainly not the [[国产化与供应链韧性|domestic production share]] alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 来源与核验

- [OECD（2025），*OECD Supply Chain Resilience Review*](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/06/oecd-supply-chain-resilience-review_9930d256/94e3a8ea-en.pdf)，Box 5.1、§5.1–5.3，印刷第99–102页（PDF第101–104页）：支持恢复导向的定义、冗余与灵活性及响应能力的组合、替代投入兼容性和成本约束，以及 robustness 的区分。100 件与 10/3 天是用于说明同一恢复标准的构造例，不是实测排名。

  <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
  Box 5.1 and Sections 5.1–5.3 support the recovery-based definition, the combination of redundancy, flexibility and responsiveness, compatibility and cost constraints, and the distinction from robustness. The 100-unit and ten/three-day example is constructed to illustrate a common recovery standard, not an empirical ranking.
