# 收入效应与替代效应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Income and Substitution Effects*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 价格、工资或利率变化后，为什么人的总反应可能方向不确定。
> **具体锚点：** 工资税降低净工资后，替代效应让闲暇相对便宜，推动少工作；收入效应让人变穷，若闲暇是正常品，又推动多工作。因此只知道税率变化，不能断言工时怎样变。
> **核心难点：** 替代效应只保留相对价格变化并补偿购买力；收入效应再加入实际购买力变化。普通需求观察到的是二者之和，不是其中任何一个。
> **为什么重要：** 劳动供给、商品需求、储蓄和税收超额负担都依赖这项分解；不分解就容易把机制方向说反。
> **继续：** 先看“图形分解”，再用“判断步骤”处理具体政策；税收应用见 [[税收归宿与超额负担#劳动、储蓄与收入效应]]。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> **What it solves:** Explain why a person's total response to a change in price, wage, or interest rate may have an uncertain direction.
> **Concrete anchor:** When a payroll tax lowers the net wage, the substitution effect makes leisure relatively cheaper and encourages less work. The income effect makes the person poorer and, if leisure is normal, encourages more work. A tax change alone therefore does not determine the change in hours.
> **Central difficulty:** The substitution effect retains the relative-price change while compensating purchasing power; the income effect then introduces the change in real purchasing power. Ordinary demand observes their sum, not either component alone.
> **Why it matters:** Labour supply, commodity demand, saving, and the excess burden of taxation all depend on this decomposition. Without it, the direction of the mechanism is easily reversed.
> **Continue with:** Start with the graphical decomposition, then use the diagnostic steps for a particular policy. For taxation, continue to [[税收归宿与超额负担#劳动、储蓄与收入效应|labour, saving, and income effects]].
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 一个价格变化其实同时改变两件事
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*A Price Change Alters Two Things at Once*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

商品 $x$ 涨价时，它相对其他商品变贵，同时给定收入能买到的商品总量也减少。前者产生替代效应，后者产生收入效应。把“涨价后少买”全部叫作替代效应，会把两个不同机制混在一起。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
When good $x$ becomes more expensive, it rises in price relative to other goods and a fixed income buys less overall. The first change generates the substitution effect; the second generates the income effect. Calling the entire reduction in purchases a substitution effect conflates two distinct mechanisms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

替代效应问：如果按新相对价格选择，但给消费者足够补偿，使其仍能达到原来的效用水平，他会怎样调整组合？对于自身价格上升的商品，补偿性需求不会增加：消费者会转向相对便宜的替代品。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The substitution effect asks how the consumer would adjust at the new relative prices if compensated enough to remain at the original utility level. Compensated demand for a good cannot rise when its own price increases: the consumer shifts toward relatively cheaper alternatives.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

收入效应问：撤掉这项补偿、让实际购买力从补偿状态变到最终状态后，消费怎样再变化。正常品会随实际购买力下降而减少；劣等品可能增加。因此收入效应的方向取决于商品性质和消费者所在区间。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The income effect asks what happens when that compensation is removed and real purchasing power moves from the compensated state to the final state. Demand for a normal good falls as purchasing power falls, whereas demand for an inferior good may rise. Its direction therefore depends on the good and the consumer's position.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Marshallian 与 Hicksian 需求
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Marshallian and Hicksian Demand*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Marshallian demand $x(p,m)$ 固定名义收入 $m$，所以同时包含替代效应和收入效应；它对应通常观察到的需求曲线。Hicksian demand $h(p,u)$ 固定效用 $u$，通过调整收入补偿价格变化，只保留替代效应，因此也叫补偿需求。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Marshallian demand $x(p,m)$ holds nominal income $m$ fixed and therefore combines substitution and income effects; it corresponds to the ordinary observed demand curve. Hicksian demand $h(p,u)$ holds utility $u$ fixed by adjusting income to compensate for the price change, isolating the substitution effect. It is therefore also called compensated demand.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对商品 $i$ 和价格 $p_j$，Slutsky equation 写成：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For good $i$ and price $p_j$, the Slutsky equation is:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{\partial x_i}{\partial p_j}
=
\frac{\partial h_i}{\partial p_j}
-x_j\frac{\partial x_i}{\partial m}.
$$

右边第一项是补偿替代效应；第二项是购买力变化带来的收入效应。对自身价格，第一项不为正。若商品是正常品，价格上涨造成的两项通常都减少需求；若是劣等品，两项相反。只有当劣等品的收入效应强到超过替代效应时，才出现价格上涨反而多买的 Giffen 情形。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The first term on the right is the compensated substitution effect; the second is the income effect created by the loss of purchasing power. For an own-price change, the first term is non-positive. For a normal good, both effects of a price increase usually reduce demand. For an inferior good they oppose each other. A Giffen outcome occurs only when the inferior-good income effect is strong enough to dominate the substitution effect, so demand rises with the good's own price.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 图形分解
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Graphical Decomposition*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

从初始最优点 $A$ 出发，某商品涨价使预算线旋转，最终最优点为 $C$。先画一条与新预算线平行、但与原无差异曲线相切的补偿预算线，切点记为 $B$。$A\rightarrow B$ 是替代效应：效用不变，只改变相对价格；$B\rightarrow C$ 是收入效应：相对价格不再变，只撤掉补偿。$A\rightarrow C$ 才是实际观察到的总效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Begin at the initial optimum $A$. A price increase rotates the budget line and produces the final optimum $C$. Draw a compensated budget line parallel to the new budget line but tangent to the original indifference curve, at point $B$. The movement $A\rightarrow B$ is the substitution effect: utility is fixed while relative prices change. The movement $B\rightarrow C$ is the income effect: relative prices remain fixed while compensation is removed. Only $A\rightarrow C$ is the total effect observed in behaviour.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Hicks 补偿保持效用不变；Slutsky 补偿则给出刚好能在新价格下买得起原组合的收入。二者对有限价格变化给出的中间点可能不同，但都在区分相对价格变化与购买力变化；做题时必须先说明采用哪一种补偿。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Hicks compensation holds utility constant. Slutsky compensation instead supplies just enough income to keep the original bundle affordable at new prices. Their intermediate points may differ for a finite price change, but both separate the relative-price change from the purchasing-power change. State explicitly which compensation is being used.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## Worked example：Cobb–Douglas 消费者
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Worked Example: A Cobb–Douglas Consumer*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

设 $u(x,y)=\sqrt{xy}$，收入为 100，$p_y=1$，且 $p_x$ 从 10 上升到 20。Marshallian demand 为 $x=m/(2p_x)$，所以实际消费从 $5$ 降到 $2.5$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $u(x,y)=\sqrt{xy}$, income be 100, $p_y=1$, and $p_x$ rise from 10 to 20. Marshallian demand is $x=m/(2p_x)$, so actual consumption falls from $5$ to $2.5$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

初始效用为 $u_0=\sqrt{5\times50}=\sqrt{250}$。在新价格下保持 $u_0$ 所需的 Hicksian demand 为 $h_x=u_0\sqrt{p_y/p_x}\approx3.536$。因此替代效应为 $3.536-5=-1.464$，收入效应为 $2.5-3.536=-1.036$，两者相加得到总效应 $-2.5$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Initial utility is $u_0=\sqrt{5\times50}=\sqrt{250}$. At the new prices, Hicksian demand holding $u_0$ constant is $h_x=u_0\sqrt{p_y/p_x}\approx3.536$. The substitution effect is therefore $3.536-5=-1.464$, and the income effect is $2.5-3.536=-1.036$. Their sum is the total effect of $-2.5$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这个例子中 $x$ 是正常品，所以涨价造成的购买力下降与替代效应同向。若 $x$ 是劣等品，第二段移动会反向，但不能因此自动断言总需求上升；还要比较两个效应的大小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In this example, $x$ is normal, so the loss of purchasing power reinforces the substitution effect. If $x$ were inferior, the second movement would reverse direction, but that would not by itself imply that total demand rises; the magnitudes of the two effects must still be compared.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 劳动供给、储蓄与政策
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Labour Supply, Saving, and Policy*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在劳动—闲暇选择中，工资是闲暇的机会成本。工资上升的替代效应使闲暇变贵，推动多工作；若闲暇是正常品，收入效应使人因更富而多休闲、少工作。两者相反，所以个体劳动供给可能增加也可能减少。工资税降低净工资时，两种方向同时反转，总效应仍不能只凭理论确定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In the labour-leisure choice, the wage is the opportunity cost of leisure. The substitution effect of a wage increase makes leisure more expensive and encourages more work. If leisure is normal, the income effect makes the person wealthier and encourages more leisure and less work. The effects oppose one another, so individual labour supply may rise or fall. A wage tax that lowers the net wage reverses both directions, leaving the total response theoretically ambiguous.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在跨期消费中，利率改变今天消费与未来消费的相对价格。替代效应决定把消费移向相对便宜的时期；收入效应还取决于此人原本是净储蓄者还是净借款者。因此“利率上升必然增加储蓄”也不是一般结论。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In intertemporal choice, the interest rate changes the relative price of present and future consumption. The substitution effect shifts consumption toward the relatively cheaper date, while the income effect also depends on whether the person begins as a net saver or a net borrower. It is therefore not generally true that a higher interest rate must increase saving.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 判断步骤与失败诊断
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Diagnostic Procedure and Failure Modes*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

处理具体问题时依次问：哪个相对价格变了；若补偿到原效用，替代效应把选择推向哪里；购买力怎样变；相关商品或闲暇是正常品还是劣等品；最后比较两项大小。只有前四步不能确定总效应时，答案应明确写成“理论方向不确定，需要偏好参数或数据”，而不是强行猜方向。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a concrete problem, ask in order: Which relative price changed? If utility were compensated to its initial level, where would substitution move the choice? How did purchasing power change? Is the relevant good or leisure normal or inferior? Finally, compare the magnitudes. If the first four steps do not determine the total effect, state that its direction is theoretically ambiguous and requires preference parameters or data rather than guessing.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常见错误包括：把“收入效应”误解成名义收入必须变化；把普通需求曲线当作纯替代反应；看到劣等品就直接判断为 Giffen good；以及把工资、利率或税率变化后的总反应写成必然方向。另一个错误是混用 Hicks 与 Slutsky 补偿而不说明中间点为何不同。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Common errors include assuming that an income effect requires nominal income to change; treating ordinary demand as a pure substitution response; calling every inferior good a Giffen good; and assigning a necessary direction to the total response after a wage, interest-rate, or tax change. Another is mixing Hicks and Slutsky compensation without explaining why their intermediate points differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 最小自检
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimal Self-check*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释：为什么商品价格上涨可以在名义收入不变时产生收入效应？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*In your own words, why can a price increase create an income effect even when nominal income is unchanged?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 因为给定收入能买到的商品组合缩小了，实际购买力下降。收入效应描述的是这项购买力变化，不要求工资或现金收入本身改变。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The set of bundles affordable with the given income shrinks, so real purchasing power falls. The income effect describes this change in purchasing power; wages or cash income need not change.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 某正常品价格上涨时，替代效应和收入效应分别把其需求推向哪里？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*When the price of a normal good rises, in which direction does each effect move its demand?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 替代效应使消费者减少相对变贵的商品；购买力下降也使正常品需求减少，所以两项同向。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The substitution effect reduces demand for the relatively more expensive good. The loss of purchasing power also reduces demand for a normal good, so the two effects reinforce each other.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 工资上升后工时为什么可能上升也可能下降？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why may hours worked rise or fall after a wage increase?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 替代效应使闲暇更贵，推动多工作；若闲暇是正常品，收入效应使人更富，推动多休闲。总工时取决于哪一项更大。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> The substitution effect makes leisure more expensive and encourages more work. If leisure is normal, the income effect makes the person wealthier and encourages more leisure. Total hours depend on which effect is larger.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 劣等品与 Giffen good 的区别是什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What is the distinction between an inferior good and a Giffen good?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 劣等品只表示收入上升时需求下降；Giffen good 还要求价格上涨造成的正向收入效应强于负向替代效应，使自身价格与总需求同向变化。前者不自动推出后者。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> An inferior good merely has lower demand when income rises. A Giffen good additionally requires the positive income effect of an own-price increase to dominate the negative substitution effect, so price and total demand move together. Inferiority alone is not enough.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
