# 货币政策公告的信息效应是央行透露的经济判断改变公众预期而产生的影响
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The information effect of a monetary policy announcement arises when the central bank's economic assessment changes the public's expectations.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

货币政策公告的信息效应指公众从利率决定或随附沟通中，学到关于当前或未来经济的判断，因而调整现金流、风险和行为预期。它不同于“在经济前景给定时，政策本身变得更紧或更松”的冲击；同一公告可以同时包含两者。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An information effect occurs when the decision or accompanying communication changes beliefs about the economy and hence cash flows, risk or behaviour. It differs from a policy tightening or easing holding the economic outlook fixed. One announcement can contain both.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，降息意外可以降低贴现率，同时让公众认为央行掌握了更差的需求信息。若预期现金流下调足够多，股价仍可下跌；这不能直接证明降息本身压低股价。具体估值两项怎样相抵，复用[[政策传导的估值渠道]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An unexpected cut can lower discount rates while signalling worse demand information. A sufficiently large downward cash-flow revision can still lower equities; this does not establish that easing itself depressed them. Reuse [[政策传导的估值渠道|the separate cash-flow and discount-rate analysis]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

Jarociński–Karadi 的研究利用利率与股价在公告窗口的共同变动来区分两类冲击：其符号约束把纯紧缩对应为利率上、股价下，把正向经济信息对应为二者同上。该研究还假定窗口中主要由这两类结构冲击系统性驱动价格；这些是识别限制，不是看到任意一次同涨就证明央行掌握了好消息。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Jarociński–Karadi distinguish shocks using announcement-window co-movement: their sign restrictions associate pure tightening with higher rates and lower equities, and favourable economic information with increases in both. They also assume two structural shocks systematically drive the window movements. These are identifying restrictions, not proof that every joint increase reveals favourable private information.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用自造的两冲击混合展示边界：将利率意外 $s$ 以基点计，股票回报 $v$ 以百分点计，设 $s=m+n$、$v=-0.04m+0.04n$。其中 $m$ 是纯政策分量，$n$ 是正向信息分量，系数完全由例子指定。若 $m=20,n=30$，观察到 $s=50,v=0.4$：利率和股票同涨，却仍包含 20 基点的紧缩分量。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a constructed mixture, measure the rate surprise $s$ in basis points and equity return $v$ in percentage points. Let $s=m+n$ and $v=-0.04m+0.04n$, with policy component $m$, favourable information component $n$, and assumed loadings. If $m=20,n=30$, observations are $s=50,v=0.4$: both rise despite a twenty-basis-point tightening component.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只有在本例载荷已知时，才可代数解出两分量；真实数据还需估计、限制和稳健性检验。窄窗口减少无关新闻，并不自动排除央行信息、市场对反应函数的再学习或噪声。由公告相关性走到因果推断，继续见[[货币政策冲击识别]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Only the example's known loadings allow direct algebraic recovery. Actual data require estimation, restrictions and robustness checks. Narrow windows reduce unrelated news without automatically eliminating information, learning about the reaction function or noise. See [[货币政策冲击识别|monetary shock identification]] for the causal step.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
