# 差分方程与滞后算子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Difference equations and lag operators*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 快速恢复
> **它解决什么：** 把“今天由过去怎样生成”写成递推关系，并判断冲击会衰减、持续还是爆炸。
> **具体锚点：** 蛛网模型中本期供给由上期价格决定，价格偏离均衡后的路径由一个一阶差分方程控制。
> **核心难点：** 通解由齐次解和特解组成；稳定性由特征根决定，而不是看到序列暂时下降就断言稳定。
> **为什么重要：** ARMA、VAR、动态经济模型和数值算法都用同一递推骨架。
> **继续：** 有随机扰动时进入 [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测|平稳时间序列、相关结构与 ARMA]]；多变量动态进入 [[VAR、脉冲响应与 Granger 因果|动态回归、VAR、脉冲响应与 Granger 因果]]。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!summary] Quick recovery
> **What it solves:** It writes how the present is generated from the past as a recurrence and determines whether a disturbance decays, persists, or explodes.
> **Concrete anchor:** In a cobweb model, current supply depends on last period's price, and a first-order difference equation governs the path back toward or away from equilibrium.
> **Central difficulty:** A general solution combines homogeneous and particular solutions. Stability follows from characteristic roots, not from a temporarily declining sample path.
> **Why it matters:** ARMA, VAR, dynamic economic models, and numerical algorithms share this recursive backbone.
> **Continue with:** Add random innovations in [[ARMA 模型：识别、估计、诊断与预测|ARMA modelling]], or move to [[VAR、脉冲响应与 Granger 因果|VAR and impulse-response analysis]] for multivariate dynamics.

## 一阶差分方程
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*First-order difference equations*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$y_t=ay_{t-1}+b$ 的稳态为 $y^*=b/(1-a)$（$a\ne1$），偏离满足 $y_t-y^*=a^t(y_0-y^*)$。$|a|<1$ 时收敛，$a<0$ 产生交替振荡；$|a|>1$ 发散。先求稳态再看偏离，通常比反复代入更清楚。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $y_t=ay_{t-1}+b$, the steady state is $y^*=b/(1-a)$ when $a\ne1$, and deviations satisfy $y_t-y^*=a^t(y_0-y^*)$. The path converges when $|a|<1$, alternates when $a<0$, and diverges when $|a|>1$. Solving for the steady state and then studying deviations is usually clearer than repeated substitution.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

推导只需要两步：由 $y^*=ay^*+b$ 求出不动点，再用 $x_t=y_t-y^*$ 得 $x_t=ax_{t-1}$。若 $a=1$，不存在有限稳态（除非 $b=0$），而是 $y_t=y_0+bt$；这就是单位根对常数输入的累积。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The derivation has two steps: solve the fixed-point equation $y^*=ay^*+b$, then define $x_t=y_t-y^*$ to obtain $x_t=ax_{t-1}$. If $a=1$, no finite steady state exists unless $b=0$; instead $y_t=y_0+bt$. This is accumulation of a constant input under a unit root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 高阶方程与特征根
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Higher-order equations and characteristic roots*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$y_t-a_1y_{t-1}-\cdots-a_py_{t-p}=g_t$ 的齐次部分对应特征多项式。不同根给指数模式，重根附带多项式因子，复根给阻尼或爆炸振荡。离散稳定要求按所用多项式约定，动态根位于单位圆内；使用 lag-polynomial 约定时常等价表述为滞后多项式零点在单位圆外，必须说明约定。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The homogeneous part of $y_t-a_1y_{t-1}-\cdots-a_py_{t-p}=g_t$ has a characteristic polynomial. Distinct roots generate exponential modes, repeated roots add polynomial factors, and complex roots generate damped or explosive oscillations. Under a growth-factor convention, discrete stability requires dynamic roots inside the unit circle; under a lag-polynomial convention, the equivalent statement is often that lag-polynomial zeros lie outside it. The convention must be stated.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $y_t=1.2y_{t-1}-0.32y_{t-2}$ 的增长根满足 $r^2-1.2r+0.32=0$，得 $r=0.8,0.4$，所以任意初始偏离是 $c_1(0.8)^t+c_2(0.4)^t$ 并最终衰减。同一方程的滞后多项式 $1-1.2L+0.32L^2$ 的零点是 $1/0.8$ 与 $1/0.4$，都在单位圆外。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, the growth roots of $y_t=1.2y_{t-1}-0.32y_{t-2}$ solve $r^2-1.2r+0.32=0$, giving $r=0.8,0.4$. Every initial deviation is therefore $c_1(0.8)^t+c_2(0.4)^t$ and eventually decays. The zeros of the corresponding lag polynomial $1-1.2L+0.32L^2$ are the reciprocals $1/0.8$ and $1/0.4$, both outside the unit circle.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 滞后与差分算子
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Lag and difference operators*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$Ly_t=y_{t-1}$，$\Delta=1-L$。用多项式 $\phi(L)y_t$ 可以因式分解和求逆；若 $|\phi|$ 满足稳定条件，逆可展开为无限 MA。算子是作用规则，不是普通数值，交换和求逆前要检查边界与收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
$Ly_t=y_{t-1}$ and $\Delta=1-L$. A polynomial $\phi(L)y_t$ can be factorised and, under stability conditions, inverted as a convergent infinite moving average. Operators are rules acting on a sequence rather than ordinary numbers, so boundaries and convergence must be checked before commuting or inverting them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

基本恒等式包括 $\Delta y_t=(1-L)y_t$、$\Delta^2=(1-L)^2$ 以及季节差分 $\Delta_s=1-L^s$。注意 $\Delta_s$ 不等于 $\Delta^s$：前者比较相隔 $s$ 期，后者连续做 $s$ 次一阶差分。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Useful identities include $\Delta y_t=(1-L)y_t$, $\Delta^2=(1-L)^2$, and seasonal differencing $\Delta_s=1-L^s$. Importantly, $\Delta_s$ is not $\Delta^s$: the former compares observations $s$ periods apart, whereas the latter applies first differencing $s$ times.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 特解、冲击与长期乘数
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Particular solutions, shocks, and long-run multipliers*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

常数、趋势、周期或一次冲击对应不同特解猜法。单位冲击后的路径就是 impulse response。稳定线性模型的长期乘数等于动态系数总效应，但当存在单位根时该和可能不收敛。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Constant, trend, cyclical, and one-off inputs call for different particular-solution guesses. The path after a unit shock is an impulse response. In a stable linear model, the long-run multiplier is the sum of dynamic effects, but that sum may fail to converge in the presence of a unit root.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $y_t=ay_{t-1}+cx_t$ 且 $|a|<1$，$x_t$ 永久增加 1 的当期效应为 $c$，后续效应为 $ac,a^2c,\ldots$，长期水平效应是 $c/(1-a)$。若 $a=1$，分母为零不是计算小故障，而是说明没有有限长期均衡效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $y_t=ay_{t-1}+cx_t$ with $|a|<1$, a permanent unit increase in $x_t$ has impact $c$, followed by $ac,a^2c,\ldots$, so the long-run level effect is $c/(1-a)$. When $a=1$, the zero denominator is not a computational nuisance; it says that no finite long-run equilibrium effect exists.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 经济模型中的稳定性
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Stability in economic models*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

差分方程只描述给定机制下的动态。理性预期、前瞻变量、非线性或制度变化会改变边界条件；数学稳定不等同于经济福利最优。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A difference equation describes dynamics only under a specified mechanism. Rational expectations, forward-looking variables, nonlinearity, or regime changes alter boundary conditions; mathematical stability is not the same as economic welfare optimality.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Minimum self-check*

### 一阶方程 $y_t=ay_{t-1}+b$ 的稳定性怎样判断？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*How is stability determined for $y_t=ay_{t-1}+b$?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 把它写成偏离稳态的形式 $y_t-y^*=a(y_{t-1}-y^*)$；$|a|<1$ 时偏离衰减。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> Write it in deviation form $y_t-y^*=a(y_{t-1}-y^*)$. Deviations decay when $|a|<1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 为什么特征根和 lag-polynomial 的‘单位圆内外’说法有时相反？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Why do characteristic-root and lag-polynomial conventions sometimes reverse the inside-versus-outside unit-circle statement?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 两种定义使用互为倒数的根；必须先看多项式是写在时间增长因子 r 上还是滞后算子 L 上。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The conventions use reciprocal roots. First determine whether the polynomial is written in a time-growth factor $r$ or in the lag operator $L$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 单位根对长期冲击意味着什么？
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*What does a unit root imply for the long-run effect of a shock?*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> 冲击不按几何速度消失，水平会永久移动，长期乘数和均值回归的常规结论失效。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> The shock does not disappear geometrically; the level shifts permanently, and ordinary conclusions about long-run multipliers and mean reversion fail.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

### 用自己的话解释为什么 $\Delta_s\ne\Delta^s$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Explain in your own words why $\Delta_s\ne\Delta^s$.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!answer]- 答案
> $\Delta_s=1-L^s$ 直接比较本期与 $s$ 期前；$\Delta^s=(1-L)^s$ 是一阶差分的 $s$ 次叠加，会产生不同的二项式权重。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> [!answer]- Answer
> $\Delta_s=1-L^s$ compares the present directly with $s$ periods earlier, whereas $\Delta^s=(1-L)^s$ repeats first differencing $s$ times and produces different binomial weights.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
