# Closed sets
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*闭集*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A set is **closed** when every point outside it has a small open region that stays outside it. On the real line, the interval $[0,1]$ has this property: if you stand to its left or right, you can move a little without entering the interval. This way of looking from the outside gives the definition in spaces where a picture of “endpoints” is not available.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

**闭集**可以从外部理解：每个不属于它的点，都有一小块完全留在外面的开区域。在实数线上，区间 $[0,1]$ 就有这个性质；站在区间左侧或右侧，都可以稍微移动而不进入区间。这种从外面判断的方式，也适用于无法画出“端点”的空间。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix a space $X$ and a [[拓扑|topology]] $\tau$, which specifies which subsets of $X$ count as [[开集|open sets]]. If $F\subseteq X$, its complement $X\setminus F$ consists of the points of $X$ not in $F$. The definition is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

先固定空间 $X$ 及其[[拓扑]] $\tau$，即指定哪些 $X$ 的子集算作[[开集]]。对 $F\subseteq X$，补集 $X\setminus F$ 是 $X$ 中不属于 $F$ 的点组成的集合。定义为

$$F\text{ is closed in }X\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad X\setminus F\text{ is open in }X.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In a [[度量空间|metric space]] $(X,d)$, the distance $d(x,y)$ lets us express the outside region as a ball. Write $B_r(x)=\{y\in X:d(x,y)<r\}$. Then closedness means that for every $x\in X\setminus F$ there is some radius $r>0$ with $B_r(x)\subseteq X\setminus F$. The radius may depend on $x$. This is just the open-set test applied to the complement.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

在[[度量空间]] $(X,d)$ 中，距离 $d(x,y)$ 使我们可以用球描述外部区域。记 $B_r(x)=\{y\in X:d(x,y)<r\}$。闭性就是：每个 $x\in X\setminus F$ 都存在某个半径 $r>0$，使 $B_r(x)\subseteq X\setminus F$。半径可以随 $x$ 改变。这只是把开集判据应用于补集。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Checking an interval from outside
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 从区间外部检查

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Use $X=\mathbb R$, the real numbers with the usual distance $d(x,y)=|x-y|$. For $F=[0,1]$, the complement is $(-\infty,0)\cup(1,\infty)$. If $x<0$, radius $r=-x/2$ keeps the whole ball to the left of $0$. If $x>1$, radius $r=(x-1)/2$ keeps it to the right of $1$. Every outside point passes the test, so $[0,1]$ is closed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

取 $X=\mathbb R$，即带通常距离 $d(x,y)=|x-y|$ 的实数空间。对 $F=[0,1]$，补集为 $(-\infty,0)\cup(1,\infty)$。若 $x<0$，取 $r=-x/2$，整个球都留在 $0$ 左侧；若 $x>1$，取 $r=(x-1)/2$，整个球都留在 $1$ 右侧。每个外部点都通过检查，所以 $[0,1]$ 闭。

> [!warning] Closed does not mean “not open” · 闭不等于“不开”
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> A set and its complement are different sets. Closedness asks whether the **complement** is open; it does not ask whether the original set fails to be open. In $\mathbb R$, $[0,1)$ is neither: its member $0$ has no ball contained in $[0,1)$, while the complement's member $1$ has no ball contained in the complement. By contrast, $\varnothing$ and the entire space $X$ are always both open and closed, since they are each other's complements and both are open by the topology axioms.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 集合与它的补集是两个不同的集合。闭性问的是**补集**是否开，不是原集合是否不开。在 $\mathbb R$ 中，$[0,1)$ 两者都不是：它的成员 $0$ 没有完全包含在 $[0,1)$ 内的球，而补集的成员 $1$ 也没有完全留在补集内的球。相反，$\varnothing$ 与整个空间 $X$ 永远既开又闭，因为它们互为补集，并且拓扑公理保证两者都开。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The real interval $(0,1)$ is open but not closed: the complement contains $0$, yet every ball around $0$ meets $(0,1)$. The interval $[0,1]$ is closed but not open: every ball around its member $0$ contains negative numbers. These examples distinguish the two tests without treating them as opposite labels.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

实区间 $(0,1)$ 开但不闭：补集包含 $0$，可是 $0$ 周围每个球都会碰到 $(0,1)$。区间 $[0,1]$ 闭但不开：其成员 $0$ 周围每个球都包含负数。两种检验由此区分开来，而不是一对互相否定的标签。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The ambient space is part of the statement
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 环境空间也是陈述的一部分

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the ambient space is changed to $Y=[0,1]$ with the inherited distance, the set $[0,1]$ is now the whole space. It is therefore open as well as closed in $Y$. Balls are restricted to points of $Y$; a ball around $0$ no longer contains negative numbers. This is the [[子空间拓扑|subspace topology]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若把环境空间改成带继承距离的 $Y=[0,1]$，集合 $[0,1]$ 现在就是整个空间，因此在 $Y$ 中既开又闭。球只包含属于 $Y$ 的点，$0$ 周围的球不再包含负数。这就是[[子空间拓扑]]。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Closedness itself can change too. Let $A=(0,1]$ and $Y=(0,2)$. In $Y$, its complement is $(1,2)$, open relative to $Y$, so $A$ is closed in $Y$. In $\mathbb R$, the complement is $(-\infty,0]\cup(1,\infty)$, which is not open at $0$; thus $A$ is not closed in $\mathbb R$. The missing point $0$ matters only in an ambient space that contains it.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

闭性本身也会改变。令 $A=(0,1]$、$Y=(0,2)$。在 $Y$ 中，补集是相对于 $Y$ 开的 $(1,2)$，所以 $A$ 在 $Y$ 中闭。在 $\mathbb R$ 中，补集变成 $(-\infty,0]\cup(1,\infty)$，它在 $0$ 处不开，因此 $A$ 在 $\mathbb R$ 中不闭。缺少的点 $0$，只有在包含它的环境空间中才构成问题。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The rules for combining closed sets belong to [[闭集运算|closed-set operations]]. A separate [[闭集的序列判别|sequence criterion]] characterizes closedness in metric spaces; it is not an unrestricted replacement for the open-complement definition in every topological space.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

组合闭集时的规则见[[闭集运算]]。另有[[闭集的序列判别]]在度量空间中刻画闭性，但它不能不加范围限制地替代任意拓扑空间中的开补集定义。
