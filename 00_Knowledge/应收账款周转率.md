# 应收账款周转率是期间销售流量与相应应收款项存量之比
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Receivables turnover is sales during a period divided by the corresponding receivables stock.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

应收账款周转次数 $T=S_c/\overline{AR}$，理论上分子取赊销净额、分母取相应期间平均应收。若赊销资料不可得，可明确用营业收入代理；现销比例不同会影响比较。CPA本例把应收票据与应收账款合并，并为简便用期末数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Receivables turnover is $T=S_c/\overline{AR}$, conceptually using net credit sales and corresponding average receivables. Revenue may be used as an explicitly stated proxy when credit-sales data are unavailable; different cash-sales shares affect comparability. CPA combines notes and trade receivables and uses ending balances for simplicity.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若期间长 $D$ 天，折算周转天数为 $D/T$，应收/销售比为 $1/T$。ABC按期末口径，$AR=398+20=418$，$S=3000$，故次数7.1770、折算天数 $365\times418/3000\approx50.8567$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a period of $D$ days, implied collection days are $D/T$ and receivables-to-sales is $1/T$. Under CPA's ending-balance convention, $AR=418$ and revenue of 3,000 give turnover of 7.1770 and about 50.8567 implied days.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平均数、减值前后及销售含税范围需在[[财务比率口径匹配]]中统一；折算天数不保证每张发票都在该天数回收。天数少也不自动更好：信用期20天而18天收回，与信用期10天却15天收回，需比较逾期而非只比15与18。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Align averaging, impairment, and sales-tax bases through [[财务比率口径匹配|measurement-basis matching]]. Implied days do not guarantee collection of every invoice within that time. Fewer days are not automatically better: collecting in 18 days against 20-day terms differs from collecting in 15 days against 10-day terms.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
