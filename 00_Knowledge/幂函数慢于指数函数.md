# 每个固定实数次幂都比固定底数大于一的指数函数增长得慢
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Every fixed real power grows more slowly than an exponential with fixed base greater than one*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定 $a>1$ 和 $r\in\mathbb R$，对正实数 $x\to\infty$，有 $x^r=o(a^x)$。特别地，无论多项式的固定次数有多大，最终都被任一固定底数大于一的指数函数压过。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For fixed $a>1$ and $r\in\mathbb R$, as positive real $x\to\infty$, $x^r=o(a^x)$. In particular, every fixed polynomial degree is eventually outgrown by an exponential with any fixed base greater than one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{x^r}{a^x}=\exp\bigl(r\ln x-x\ln a\bigr)\longrightarrow0.
$$

证明先比较指数。由 [[对数慢于正幂]]，$\ln x/x\to0$，所以
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
To prove this, compare the exponent first. By [[对数慢于正幂|logarithms growing more slowly than positive powers]], $\ln x/x\to0$, hence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{r\ln x-x\ln a}{x}
=r\frac{\ln x}{x}-\ln a
\longrightarrow-\ln a<0.
$$

从某点起指数不超过 $-\frac12(\ln a)x$，趋于负无穷；取自然指数即得比值趋零。证明也覆盖 $r=0$ 与 $r<0$，且限制到正整数输入时仍成立。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Eventually the exponent is at most $-\frac12(\ln a)x$, so it tends to negative infinity. Exponentiating gives a ratio tending to zero. This also covers $r=0$ and $r<0$, and remains valid on positive integer inputs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $n^{100}=o(1.01^n)$。这里不提供一个“小输入也更快”的结论；交叉点与实际成本常数可能很大。条件 $a>1$ 不能删去：$n$ 并不是 $o(1^n)$。指数与阶乘的下一层比较可借 [[斯特林公式]]，不必重新把阶乘当成一个多项式。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $n^{100}=o(1.01^n)$. This does not claim superiority on small inputs; the crossover and practical cost constants may be large. The condition $a>1$ is essential: $n$ is not $o(1^n)$. For the next comparison between exponentials and factorials, use [[斯特林公式|Stirling's formula]] rather than treating factorial growth as polynomial.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
