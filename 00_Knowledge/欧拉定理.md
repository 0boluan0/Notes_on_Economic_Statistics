# Euler's theorem in modular arithmetic
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*欧拉定理*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Powers of five modulo twelve repeat: $5,5^2,5^3,5^4$ leave remainders $5,1,5,1$. Euler’s theorem gives an exponent that returns a suitable base to remainder one, even when the modulus is composite. The exponent comes from counting all remainders that are coprime to the modulus; it need not be the first return for that particular base.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

五的幂模十二会重复：$5,5^2,5^3,5^4$ 的余数依次是 $5,1,5,1$。欧拉定理给出一个使合适底数回到余数一的指数，即使模数是合数也适用。这个指数来自与模数互素的全部余数的个数，不一定是该底数首次回到一的时刻。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix an integer modulus $n>1$. By [[同余|congruence]], $x\equiv y\pmod n$ means that $n$ divides $x-y$. The [[最大公因数|gcd]] condition $\gcd(a,n)=1$ says that $a$ and $n$ are coprime. The [[欧拉函数|totient]] $\varphi(n)$ counts representatives $0\le r<n$ with that same coprimality property. A [[模逆元|modular inverse]] of $a$ is a representative $b$ with $ab\equiv1\pmod n$; the [[模逆元存在条件|inverse criterion]] gives one exactly when $\gcd(a,n)=1$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

固定整数模数 $n>1$。按[[同余]]定义，$x\equiv y\pmod n$ 表示 $n$ 整除 $x-y$。[[最大公因数]]条件 $\gcd(a,n)=1$ 表示底数与模数互素。[[欧拉函数]] $\varphi(n)$ 统计满足同样互素条件的代表 $0\le r<n$。$a$ 的[[模逆元]]由满足 $ab\equiv1\pmod n$ 的 $b$ 代表；[[模逆元存在条件]]保证，恰好在 $\gcd(a,n)=1$ 时存在这样的逆元。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## The exponent and its hypotheses
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*指数及其条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n\ge2$ and an integer $a$ coprime to $n$, Euler's theorem gives the displayed congruence. Consequently, a nonnegative exponent may be reduced modulo $\varphi(n)$ under that same coprimality assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $n\ge2$、$a\in\mathbb Z$ 且 $\gcd(a,n)=1$，则
$$
a^{\varphi(n)}\equiv1\pmod n.
$$
因此在此前提下，对非负整数指数 $k$，可把指数按 $\varphi(n)$ 取余：$a^k\equiv a^{\,k\bmod\varphi(n)}\pmod n$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The input $a$ can be positive, zero or negative as an integer, but the gcd hypothesis excludes every multiple of $n$, including zero. Negative coprime integers are allowed. The theorem uses $n>1$; the separate definition $\varphi(1)=1$ does not silently extend this statement’s modular-inverse convention to modulus one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

底数 $a$ 的整数论域允许正数、零和负数，但互素条件排除了 $n$ 的所有倍数，包括零；负的互素整数仍然允许。定理采用 $n>1$，单独的定义值 $\varphi(1)=1$ 并没有悄悄把这里的模逆元约定扩展到模一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why multiplication only rearranges the list
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*为什么乘法只是重排列表*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Let $m=\varphi(n)$ and list the distinct coprime representatives as $r_1,\ldots,r_m$. This list is nonempty because one is coprime to $n$. For each $r_i$, multiply by $a$ and take the standard remainder modulo $n$. Two facts make the resulting list a rearrangement of the original one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

令 $m=\varphi(n)$，把互不相同的互素代表列成 $r_1,\ldots,r_m$。一与 $n$ 互素，所以列表非空。把每个 $r_i$ 乘以 $a$，再取模 $n$ 的标准余数。下面两件事保证所得列表只是原列表的重排。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
First, each product remains invertible. If $ab\equiv1$ and $r_i s_i\equiv1\pmod n$, then $(ar_i)(bs_i)\equiv1\pmod n$; the product has an inverse and hence is coprime to $n$. Second, no two classes merge: if $ar_i\equiv ar_j\pmod n$, multiply by $b$ to obtain $r_i\equiv r_j\pmod n$. Since the $r_i$ lie in $0,\ldots,n-1$, this means $r_i=r_j$. There are thus $m$ distinct outputs in a set with exactly $m$ elements, so every element occurs once.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

首先，乘积仍可逆。若 $ab\equiv1$、$r_i s_i\equiv1\pmod n$，则 $(ar_i)(bs_i)\equiv1\pmod n$，所以乘积有逆元，也就与 $n$ 互素。其次，不会把两个类合并：若 $ar_i\equiv ar_j\pmod n$，两边乘以 $b$ 得 $r_i\equiv r_j\pmod n$。由于这些代表都在 $0,\ldots,n-1$ 内，只能有 $r_i=r_j$。因此在恰有 $m$ 个元素的集合中得到了 $m$ 个不同输出，每个元素必恰出现一次。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Compare products, then cancel an invertible factor
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*比较乘积，再消去可逆因子*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplication by the invertible element $a$ permutes the finite list of invertible residues. Comparing the products before and after multiplication gives the displayed equation. Their common product is invertible, so the [[同余消去律|cancellation rule]] finishes the proof. The assumption $\gcd(a,n)=1$ makes multiplication by $a$ a permutation; the product is cancellable because each $r_i$ was chosen to be invertible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明把所有可逆余数列为 $r_1,\ldots,r_{\varphi(n)}$。乘以可逆的 $a$ 后，每个 $ar_i$ 仍可逆，且不同余数不会变成相同余数，否则可约去 $a$。所以乘法只是把整个有限列表重新排列。比较乘积，得到
$$
a^{\varphi(n)}\prod_i r_i\equiv\prod_i r_i\pmod n.
$$
由于每个 $r_i$ 都可逆，它们的乘积也可逆，应用 [[同余消去律]] 即得结论。这里 $\gcd(a,n)=1$ 保证乘以 $a$ 能重排列表；最后能消去乘积，则来自每个 $r_i$ 都选自可逆余数。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More explicitly, put $R=r_1\cdots r_m$. The product of the unreduced values $ar_i$ is $a^mR$; replacing each by its congruent standard remainder preserves the product modulo $n$. The rearranged list has product $R$, so $a^mR\equiv R\pmod n$. If $s_i$ is an inverse of $r_i$, their product $S=s_1\cdots s_m$ satisfies $RS\equiv1$. Multiplying by $S$ gives $a^m\equiv1$. This proves the final cancellation, rather than assuming that an arbitrary nonzero residue can be cancelled.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

具体地，令 $R=r_1\cdots r_m$。尚未取余的 $ar_i$ 的乘积为 $a^mR$；把每项换成与它同余的标准余数，不改变乘积的同余类。重排列表的乘积又是 $R$，所以 $a^mR\equiv R\pmod n$。若 $s_i$ 是 $r_i$ 的逆元，则 $S=s_1\cdots s_m$ 满足 $RS\equiv1$。两边乘以 $S$ 就得到 $a^m\equiv1$。这说明了最后为什么能够消去，并没有假定任意非零余数都能消去。

> [!example] The permutation modulo twelve · 模十二的置换
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $a=5,n=12$, the coprime list is $(1,5,7,11)$. Multiplication by five gives standard remainders $(5,1,11,7)$. Their product is still $385\equiv1\pmod{12}$. Comparing products gives $5^4\cdot385\equiv385$; the factor $385$ is invertible, so $5^4\equiv1$. The four in the exponent is the length of the list.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 取 $a=5,n=12$，互素列表为 $(1,5,7,11)$；各项乘五后，标准余数为 $(5,1,11,7)$。它们的乘积仍是 $385\equiv1\pmod{12}$。比较乘积得到 $5^4\cdot385\equiv385$，因子 $385$ 可逆，所以 $5^4\equiv1$。指数四来自列表的长度。

## Reduce exponents without changing the base conditions
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*约简指数时保持底数条件*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write a nonnegative exponent as $k=qm+r$, with integers $q\ge0$ and $0\le r<m$, using [[带余除法|division with remainder]]. Then $a^k=(a^m)^q a^r\equiv a^r\pmod n$. If $r=0$, the right side is $a^0=1$: exponent zero is legitimate because the coprimality hypothesis makes $a\ne0$. For example, $101=25\cdot4+1$ gives $5^{101}\equiv5\pmod{12}$, whereas $100=25\cdot4$ gives remainder one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

由[[带余除法]]，把非负指数写成 $k=qm+r$，其中整数 $q\ge0$、$0\le r<m$。于是 $a^k=(a^m)^q a^r\equiv a^r\pmod n$。若 $r=0$，右侧就是 $a^0=1$；互素条件使 $a\ne0$，所以零指数没有问题。例如 $101=25\cdot4+1$ 给出 $5^{101}\equiv5\pmod{12}$，而 $100=25\cdot4$ 给出余数一。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
More generally, two nonnegative exponents whose difference is an integer multiple of $m$ give congruent powers of this same coprime base. A negative difference does not require defining a negative power: compare the larger exponent with the smaller one, then reverse the congruence if needed. Actually using a negative exponent is an additional convention based on a modular inverse, outside the nonnegative-exponent statement above. For instance, the inverse of two modulo five is three; a modular interpretation of $2^{-1}$ would refer to that class, not to a rational number being inserted into an integer congruence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

更一般地，同一个互素底数的两个非负指数，只要差是 $m$ 的整数倍，对应幂就同余。差为负并不需要定义负幂：先比较较大指数与较小指数，必要时再把同余式反向即可。真正使用负指数则需要另外通过模逆元作出定义，超出上面的非负指数陈述。例如二模五的逆元是三；若采用模意义下的 $2^{-1}$，指的是这个类，并不是把有理数直接放进整数同余式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $5^{100}\equiv1\pmod{12}$ because $\varphi(12)=4$ and five is coprime to twelve. Without coprimality, exponent reduction fails: $2^4\equiv0\pmod8$, whereas reducing the exponent modulo $\varphi(8)=4$ would give one. The totient exponent is not necessarily minimal; $5^2\equiv1\pmod{12}$ already holds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\varphi(12)=4$ 且 $\gcd(5,12)=1$，故 $5^{100}\equiv1\pmod{12}$。若底数不互素，就不能这样约简指数：$\varphi(8)=4$，但 $2^4\equiv0\pmod8$，而把指数 $4$ 错误地减成 $0$ 会得到 $2^0=1$。欧拉函数给出的周期也不一定最小，如 $5^2\equiv1\pmod{12}$。

> [!warning] Coprimality does the work · 互素是关键条件
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Being nonzero is insufficient: two is nonzero modulo eight, but shares a factor two with the modulus. Euler’s exponent reduction does not apply to it. If the hypothesis fails, compute the particular power directly or use another justified method; failure of a hypothesis alone does not determine every particular remainder. [[模重复平方法|Repeated squaring]] computes nonnegative modular powers without requiring coprimality.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 非零并不足够：二模八不是零余数，却与模数共有因子二，不能应用欧拉定理约简指数。条件失败时，应直接计算具体幂或使用另外有依据的方法；仅凭条件失败，不能决定每个具体幂的余数。[[模重复平方法]]计算非负模幂时不要求底数与模数互素。
