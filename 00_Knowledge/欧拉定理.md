# 与模数互素的整数提升到欧拉函数给出的次方后同余于一
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An integer coprime to the modulus becomes congruent to one when raised to the totient exponent*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

若 $n\ge2$、$a\in\mathbb Z$ 且 $\gcd(a,n)=1$，则
$$
a^{\varphi(n)}\equiv1\pmod n.
$$
因此在此前提下，对非负整数指数 $k$，可把指数按 $\varphi(n)$ 取余：$a^k\equiv a^{\,k\bmod\varphi(n)}\pmod n$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For $n\ge2$ and an integer $a$ coprime to $n$, Euler's theorem gives the displayed congruence. Consequently, a nonnegative exponent may be reduced modulo $\varphi(n)$ under that same coprimality assumption.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

证明把所有可逆余数列为 $r_1,\ldots,r_{\varphi(n)}$。乘以可逆的 $a$ 后，每个 $ar_i$ 仍可逆，且不同余数不会变成相同余数，否则可约去 $a$。所以乘法只是把整个有限列表重新排列。比较乘积，得到
$$
a^{\varphi(n)}\prod_i r_i\equiv\prod_i r_i\pmod n.
$$
由于每个 $r_i$ 都可逆，它们的乘积也可逆，应用 [[同余消去律]] 即得结论。这里 $\gcd(a,n)=1$ 保证乘以 $a$ 能重排列表；最后能消去乘积，则来自每个 $r_i$ 都选自可逆余数。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Multiplication by the invertible element $a$ permutes the finite list of invertible residues. Comparing the products before and after multiplication gives the displayed equation. Their common product is invertible, so the [[同余消去律|cancellation rule]] finishes the proof. The assumption $\gcd(a,n)=1$ makes multiplication by $a$ a permutation; the product is cancellable because each $r_i$ was chosen to be invertible.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如 $\varphi(12)=4$ 且 $\gcd(5,12)=1$，故 $5^{100}\equiv1\pmod{12}$。若底数不互素，就不能这样约简指数：$\varphi(8)=4$，但 $2^4\equiv0\pmod8$，而把指数 $4$ 错误地减成 $0$ 会得到 $2^0=1$。欧拉函数给出的周期也不一定最小，如 $5^2\equiv1\pmod{12}$。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, $5^{100}\equiv1\pmod{12}$ because $\varphi(12)=4$ and five is coprime to twelve. Without coprimality, exponent reduction fails: $2^4\equiv0\pmod8$, whereas reducing the exponent modulo $\varphi(8)=4$ would give one. The totient exponent is not necessarily minimal; $5^2\equiv1\pmod{12}$ already holds.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
