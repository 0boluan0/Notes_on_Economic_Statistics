# 现金流量表是按活动类别列示期间现金及现金等价物流量并解释余额变化的报表
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A cash-flow statement reports period cash and cash-equivalent flows by activity and explains the change in their balance.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

现金流量表（statement of cash flows）以期间现金及现金等价物的流入流出为对象。现金等价物是可迅速转换为已知现金金额、价值变动风险很小的短期高流动性投资，不是把所有证券都算成现金。本卡按普通非金融企业的经营、投资、筹资三类活动理解这张表。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A cash-flow statement concerns receipts and payments of cash and cash equivalents during a period. Cash equivalents are short-term liquid investments convertible into known cash amounts with insignificant value risk, not all marketable securities. This atom uses the operating, investing, and financing classification for an ordinary non-financial business.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

经营活动主要涉及销售商品、提供服务及相应经营收付；投资活动包括购建和出售长期生产性资产等；筹资活动反映借款和所有者资本等融资安排的现金变化。比如现金购置自用设备是投资流出，收到借款本金是筹资流入；后者不因收到现金就成为收入。具体利息、股利等分类需核对适用准则及政策，不能只凭中文名称判断。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Operating activities concern trading or service operations and their related receipts and payments. Investing includes acquiring or disposing of long-lived productive assets. Financing concerns cash changes in borrowing and owner funding. Buying equipment for use is an investing outflow; receiving loan principal is a financing inflow, not revenue merely because cash arrived. Interest, dividends, and similar items require the applicable classification rules and policies.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

三类现金流合计仍不一定等于报告货币下现金余额的全部变动。外币现金的汇率变动影响须单独调节；现金及现金等价物范围也须与[[资产负债表]]及附注一致。例如期初20，经营净流入12、投资净流出8、筹资净流入5，另有汇率影响−1，期末为28，而不是29。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The three flow categories need not account for the entire change in the reporting-currency balance. Exchange-rate effects on foreign-currency cash are reconciled separately, and the cash-equivalent scope must match the [[资产负债表|balance sheet]] and notes. Opening cash of 20, operating inflow of 12, investing outflow of 8, financing inflow of 5, and an exchange effect of −1 produce closing cash of 28 rather than 29.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

没有现金收付的投资或融资交易不应伪装成当期现金流。例如债转股可以改变负债与权益，却不产生该次转换的现金流；相关交易仍需按要求披露。报表经营现金流也不等于[[企业自由现金流]]，后者另有经营税负、再投资和索取权口径。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Non-cash investing or financing transactions are not current cash flows. A debt-for-equity conversion, for example, changes liabilities and equity without a cash flow from the conversion, while remaining subject to disclosure. Reported operating cash flow also differs from [[企业自由现金流|FCFF]], whose operating-tax, reinvestment, and capital-provider scope must be specified separately.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
