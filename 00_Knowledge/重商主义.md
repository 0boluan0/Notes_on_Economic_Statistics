# 重商主义是多样政策论述的后称而不是一套统一理论
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mercantilism is a retrospective label for diverse policy arguments, not one unified theory*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 它是什么
> “重商主义”是后来的总称：它把十六至十八世纪有关货币、对外贸易、制造业、航运、殖民管制与国家力量的多种论述放到一个家族中。这个标签便于比较，但不能反推所有作者共享一套定义、模型或政策清单。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> “Mercantilism” is a later umbrella label for diverse sixteenth- to eighteenth-century arguments about money, foreign trade, manufactures, shipping, colonial regulation, and state power. The label is useful for comparison, but it does not imply that every writer shared one definition, model, or policy programme.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，Thomas Mun 论证一国对外贸易总额的顺差可带回金银；其他作者关心禁止金属输出、就业、低利率、海运、关税、特许公司或特定产业。即使两人都支持某项贸易限制，他们也可能对限制的目标、适用商品、计算贸易差额的方法和金银的作用持不同理由。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Thomas Mun, for example, argued that an overall surplus in foreign trade could return gold and silver. Other writers focused on prohibiting bullion exports, employment, low interest, shipping, customs, chartered companies, or particular industries. Even two advocates of trade restrictions could disagree about the objective, the goods covered, how the trade balance should be calculated, and what role specie played.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此，“重商主义者相信财富就是金子”最多是对部分文本的争论性概括，不能充当整个时期的共同公理。分析时应先说明作者、国家、年代和政策对象，再判断某一主张属于怎样的货币、贸易或国家建设问题；不能用后来的标签替代原文差异。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“Mercantilists believed that wealth was gold” is therefore at most a contestable summary of some texts, not a common axiom for an entire period. Analysis should identify the writer, polity, date, and policy object before locating an argument within monetary, commercial, or state-building problems. A later label cannot substitute for differences in the texts.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 两篇文章都主张限制进口，为什么还不能据此说它们属于同一套完整理论？
>
> **答案：** 同一政策工具可能服务于不同目标，并依赖不同的货币、就业、财政或安全论证；还必须比较作者的机制、范围和例外。

## 来源与核验

- [Jacob Viner, *Studies in the Theory of International Trade*, Chapter I](https://www.econlib.org/library/NPDBooks/Viner/vnSTT.html?chapter_num=4)：专门警告不要从少数文本推导一个包罗万象的简单“重商主义教义”，并逐项展示 bullion、贸易差额及政策理由的分歧。
- [Thomas Mun, *England's Treasure by Forraign Trade*（University of Michigan, EEBO 影印）](https://quod.lib.umich.edu/e/eebo/A51598.0001.001?view=toc)：作为“贸易差额带来 treasure”的具体原始文本，而不是整个重商主义的定义。
- [Adam Smith, *The Wealth of Nations*, Book IV, Chapter I](https://www.econlib.org/library/Smith/smWN.html?chapter_num=25)：核对“commercial or mercantile system”本身是 Smith 用来组织批判的体系化对象；本卡没有把他的批判性重构直接当成此前所有作者的自我认同。
