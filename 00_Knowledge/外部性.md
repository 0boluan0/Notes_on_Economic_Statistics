# 外部性是未通过价格或契约进入行动者决策的他人收益或损害
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*An externality is a benefit or harm to others that prices or contracts leave outside the decision-maker's incentives*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

外部性是一个人的生产或消费直接改变他人的生产条件或福利，而相关收益或损害没有充分进入前者的选择激励。例如，上游工厂排污降低下游渔民的捕获量，却既不为损害付款，也不因少排污而得到相应回报；工厂便可能只按自己的收益和成本决定排放。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
An externality occurs when someone's production or consumption directly changes others' productive opportunities or welfare without the corresponding benefit or harm being fully reflected in the actor's incentives. An upstream factory may reduce downstream catches without paying for the damage or being rewarded for avoiding it, so it chooses emissions using only its own returns and costs.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“外部”指**相对于这项决策的核算边界**，不一定在企业外、国外或很远的地方；也不要求行动者完全不知道后果。知道会伤害渔民，却不用承担这部分后果，仍可能留下激励缺口。相反，某人自己承担的噪声不适，不能仅因它是不利后果就算作对他人的外部性。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
“External” refers to the boundary of the decision, not necessarily a distant place or another country. Awareness of harm does not ensure an incentive to account for it. Conversely, discomfort borne entirely by the person making the choice is not an external effect on someone else merely because it is undesirable.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

识别时说清三件事：**谁决定什么、谁受到怎样的影响、哪条价格或契约没有覆盖它**。生产和消费都可能产生正负影响。单凭竞争使别人少赚钱，还须先区别[[金钱外部性|价格传导的得失]]；发现真实外溢后，再用[[边际外部损害]]和[[外部性数量偏离]]检查它是否以及怎样改变配置。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Identify who chooses what, who is affected and how, and which price or contract fails to cover the effect. Both production and consumption can create positive or negative effects. Distinguish [[金钱外部性|price-mediated gains and losses]] before diagnosing harm from competition; use [[边际外部损害|marginal external damage]] and [[外部性数量偏离|the quantity wedge]] to analyse the allocation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
