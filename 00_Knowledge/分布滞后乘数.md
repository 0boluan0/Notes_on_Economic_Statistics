# 分布滞后系数分别给出脉冲响应而其部分和给出阶跃响应
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Distributed-lag coefficients give pulse responses while their partial sums give step responses*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 核心命题
> 对 $y_t=\alpha+\sum_{j=0}^{q}\gamma_jx_{t-j}+\eta_t$，若 $x_t$ 只在时点 $t$ 增加一个单位，$y_{t+h}$ 的响应为 $\gamma_h$；若 $x$ 从 $t$ 起永久增加一个单位，期限 $h$ 的水平响应为
> $$
> \sum_{j=0}^{\min(h,q)}\gamma_j,
> $$
> 因而有限分布滞后的长期阶跃乘数是 $\sum_{j=0}^{q}\gamma_j$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> In a finite distributed-lag model, a one-period unit pulse in $x_t$ produces response $\gamma_h$ at horizon $h$. A permanent unit step produces the partial sum of transfer weights through that horizon, converging to their full sum after all specified lags have arrived.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

“当期乘数” $\gamma_0$ 只回答变化发生当期的反应；它不是完整长期效应。反过来，$\sum_j\gamma_j$ 只对应**永久保持的一单位路径变化**。若问题问的是一次性广告投放、临时税收冲击或只有一期的异常，就应读单个脉冲响应序列，而不是把系数求和后称为长期影响。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The impact multiplier $\gamma_0$ is only the contemporaneous response. The sum of coefficients answers a different experiment: a unit increase that persists permanently. A one-off advertisement, temporary tax shock, or isolated anomaly requires the pulse-response sequence rather than an automatic long-run sum.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

这些公式首先是模型内的条件比较。把它们解释为因果响应还需要 $x$ 的路径变化与结构误差正交、其他同期干预可排除、系数在比较期间稳定等条件。若这些条件不成立，乘数仍可描述预测模型怎样映射输入路径，却不能直接命名为政策效应。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
These are conditional model comparisons. Causal language additionally requires the predictor path to be exogenous to the structural error, competing interventions to be ruled out, and coefficients to remain stable. Without those conditions the multipliers still describe how the forecasting model maps an input path into an outcome path.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!example] 两期滞后
> 若 $(\gamma_0,\gamma_1,\gamma_2)=(2,1,-0.5)$，一次单位脉冲依次产生 $(2,1,-0.5)$；永久阶跃在前三期产生水平响应 $(2,3,2.5)$，长期保持在 $2.5$。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> With weights $(2,1,-0.5)$, a unit pulse generates responses $(2,1,-0.5)$, whereas a permanent step generates level responses $(2,3,2.5)$ and then remains at $2.5$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 为什么“长期乘数等于系数和”不能用于一次性脉冲？
>
> **答案：** 系数和是每一期都持续增加一个单位时各批输入的叠加；一次性脉冲只沿 $(\gamma_0,\gamma_1,\ldots)$ 传播。
