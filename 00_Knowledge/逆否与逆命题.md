# Contrapositive and converse
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*逆否命题与逆命题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Turning an “if–then” statement around can produce a different claim. Negating both parts **and** reversing their order has a different effect: it preserves the original truth condition. Keeping these operations separate tells us which restatement can replace a claim and which one needs its own proof.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
把“如果……那么……”的两端交换，可能得到另一条命题；把两端**同时否定并交换顺序**，效果则不同：它保留原来的真值条件。分清这两种操作，才能知道哪种改写可以替代原命题，哪种需要另行证明。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Fix propositions $P,Q$ in the same context, using ordinary classical logic. An [[蕴含真值条件|implication]] $P\Rightarrow Q$ is false exactly when its antecedent $P$ is true and its consequent $Q$ false. The symbol $\neg$ means negation. Starting from this conditional gives four familiar forms.

| Form | Formula | Operation on the original |
|---|---|---|
| Original | $P\Rightarrow Q$ | Keep both parts |
| Contrapositive | $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$ | Reverse and negate both parts |
| Converse | $Q\Rightarrow P$ | Reverse only |
| Inverse | $\neg P\Rightarrow\neg Q$ | Negate both parts without reversing |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
在同一语境中固定命题 $P,Q$，使用通常的经典逻辑。[[蕴含真值条件|蕴含]] $P\Rightarrow Q$ 恰在前件 $P$ 真、后件 $Q$ 假时为假；$\neg$ 表示否定。上表四行依次是原命题、逆否命题、逆命题和否命题（inverse）：逆否同时交换与否定，逆命题只交换，否命题只否定两端而不交换。

> [!important] Which change preserves the claim? · 哪种改写保留原命题？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> For $P\Rightarrow Q$, the contrapositive is $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$ and is logically equivalent to the original implication. The converse is $Q\Rightarrow P$ and is generally a different claim. Establishing $P\Leftrightarrow Q$ requires proving both directions.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 对 $P\Rightarrow Q$：逆否命题是 $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$，与原命题逻辑等价；逆命题是 $Q\Rightarrow P$，通常是另一个命题。要得到双条件 $P\Leftrightarrow Q$，必须把两个方向都证明出来。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why the contrapositive is equivalent
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*为什么逆否命题等价*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The contrapositive fails only if $\neg Q$ is true and $\neg P$ false. That says precisely that $Q$ is false and $P$ true—the original conditional's only failing combination. Hence both conditionals agree on all inputs. A complete [[真值表等价检验|truth-table comparison]] also displays the converse and inverse; T and F mean true and false.

| $P$ | $Q$ | Original | Contrapositive | Converse | Inverse |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T | T | T | T | T | T |
| T | F | F | F | T | T |
| F | T | T | T | F | F |
| F | F | T | T | T | T |
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
逆否命题只在 $\neg Q$ 真、$\neg P$ 假时失败，这正是 $Q$ 假、$P$ 真，也就是原蕴含唯一的失败情形。因此，两者在所有输入下都相同。完整的[[真值表等价检验|真值表比较]]同时列出逆命题与否命题，T、F 分别表示真、假。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The original and contrapositive columns match in every row, as do the converse and inverse columns. The two pairs differ in the middle rows. “Equivalent” means agreement for **every** assignment, not merely that two particular statements happen to be true together. The inverse is the contrapositive of the converse, which also explains their equivalence.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
原命题与逆否命题两列逐行相同，逆命题与否命题两列也逐行相同，但这两组在中间两行不同。“等价”要求**每种**赋值都一致，不是两个具体陈述碰巧同时为真。否命题本身就是逆命题的逆否命题，这也解释了后二者为什么等价。

## Keep the integer example in one domain
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*整数例子始终保持同一个论域*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The contrapositive of “if $n$ is divisible by $4$, then $n$ is even” is “if $n$ is not even, then $n$ is not divisible by $4$,” and the two are equivalent. Its converse, “if $n$ is even, then $n$ is divisible by $4$,” is false, with $n=2$ as a counterexample.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
“若 $n$ 被 $4$ 整除，则 $n$ 为偶数”的逆否是“若 $n$ 不是偶数，则 $n$ 不被 $4$ 整除”，二者等价。它的逆命题“若 $n$ 为偶数，则 $n$ 被 $4$ 整除”是假的，$n=2$ 就是反例。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $n$ ranges over the integers throughout. Write $P(n)$ for divisibility by $4$ and $Q(n)$ for evenness. The original universal claim follows because $n=4k$ gives $n=2(2k)$ for an integer $k$. At $n=2$, $P$ is false and $Q$ true: the original and contrapositive are true at that input, but both the converse and inverse are false. In particular, the inverse says “not divisible by $4$ implies not even,” and this same input refutes it.

Proving the original by instead proving its contrapositive is the procedure of [[逆否证明|proof by contraposition]]. This card explains why that replacement is legitimate; it does not supply a proof of every conditional that can be written in this form.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里 $n$ 始终取整数。令 $P(n)$ 表示被 $4$ 整除，$Q(n)$ 表示为偶数。原全称命题成立，是因为对整数 $k$，$n=4k$ 就给出 $n=2(2k)$。在 $n=2$ 处，$P$ 假而 $Q$ 真：原命题与逆否命题在该输入处都真，逆命题与否命题却都假。特别地，否命题说“不被 $4$ 整除就不是偶数”，也被同一个输入推翻。

通过证明逆否命题来证明原命题，就是[[逆否证明]]这一程序。本卡解释这种替换为什么合法，但并没有替所有写成该形式的具体条件句给出证明。

## Do not confuse contraposition with negating the whole claim
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
*逆否改写不等于否定整条命题*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For predicates $P(x),Q(x)$ on a fixed domain $D$, the equivalence applies at each $x$, so
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
若 $P(x),Q(x)$ 是固定论域 $D$ 上的谓词，等价式对每个 $x$ 都适用，所以

$$
\forall x\in D\,(P(x)\Rightarrow Q(x))
\quad\equiv\quad
\forall x\in D\,(\neg Q(x)\Rightarrow\neg P(x)).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Both sides retain the universal quantifier and the same domain. In contrast, to say the entire universal claim is **false**, use [[量词否定规则|quantifier negation]] and the implication's false row:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
两边都保留全称量词与同一论域。相反，若要说整条全称命题是**假的**，须使用[[量词否定规则]]及蕴含的假值行：

$$
\neg\bigl[\forall x\in D\,(P(x)\Rightarrow Q(x))\bigr]
\quad\equiv\quad
\exists x\in D\,(P(x)\land\neg Q(x)).
$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Here $\forall$ means “for every,” $\exists$ means “there exists,” and $\land$ means “and.” The right side asks for one counterexample in the original domain. It is neither the contrapositive nor the inverse. Any additional fixed hypotheses and parameters must also stay fixed. If $D$ is empty, both universal statements are true and the existential counterexample statement is false; contraposition does not create an object.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
这里 $\forall$ 表示“对每个”，$\exists$ 表示“存在”，$\land$ 表示“并且”。右边要求原论域中的一个反例，它既不是逆否命题，也不是否命题。其它固定的假设与参数也必须保持不变。若 $D$ 为空，两条全称命题都真，而存在反例的命题为假；逆否改写不会凭空创造一个对象。

## Boundaries
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 边界

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
- Do not mistake $\neg P\Rightarrow\neg Q$ for the contrapositive. It is the inverse, which is equivalent to the converse but not generally to the original implication.
- For a particular mathematical relation, the converse may happen to be true as well. That requires additional mathematical facts and does not follow from implication alone.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
- 不要把 $\neg P\Rightarrow\neg Q$ 误写成逆否；这叫 inverse，且与 converse 彼此等价，但不保证与原命题等价。
- 当某个具体命题碰巧满足逆命题时，原命题与逆命题可以同时为真；这来自额外数学事实，不来自蕴含的逻辑形式。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
A biconditional $P\Leftrightarrow Q$ means that both implications hold. It is true when $P,Q$ are both true **or both false**; it does not assert that they are both true. For example, “an integer is even iff it is divisible by $2$” follows from the definition of evenness. Its two directions are justified by that definition, rather than by a rule permitting every implication to be reversed.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
双条件 $P\Leftrightarrow Q$ 表示两个方向的蕴含都成立。$P,Q$ 同真**或同假**时它都为真，并不宣称两者都真。例如“整数为偶数，当且仅当它被 $2$ 整除”，两个方向由偶数的定义得到，而不是因为所有蕴含都允许倒过来说。

> [!question]- Name the transformation · 回忆与应用
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Given “differentiable $\Rightarrow$ continuous,” what are “not continuous $\Rightarrow$ not differentiable” and “continuous $\Rightarrow$ differentiable”? Which follows from the original claim?
>
> **Answer:** The first is the contrapositive and is equivalent to the original. The second is the converse and is not guaranteed.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 已知“可微 $\Rightarrow$ 连续”。“不连续 $\Rightarrow$ 不可微”与“连续 $\Rightarrow$ 可微”分别是什么？哪一个由原命题保证？
>
> **答案：** 前者是逆否，必与原命题等价；后者是逆命题，不由原命题保证。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
In that prompt, keep the same real function and the same interior point, with an ordinary finite derivative. The underlying result is [[可导必连续|differentiability implies continuity]]; the logical transformation preserves those hypotheses. A converse would need its own mathematical justification, and [[连续不必可导|a continuous function need not be differentiable]] supplies the boundary.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
自检中要保持同一个实函数、同一个定义域内点，并使用通常的有限导数。原数学结论是[[可导必连续]]；逻辑改写必须保留这些前提。逆命题需要自己的数学依据，而[[连续不必可导]]给出了这条边界。
