# 产业后备军是相对于资本增殖需要而过剩的劳动人口
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*The industrial reserve army is labouring population surplus relative to capital's requirements for expansion.*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

产业后备军指资本积累和生产变动中反复形成、相对于资本增殖所需就业而多余的劳动人口。这里的“过剩”以资本需要购买多少劳动力为参照；它不表示这些人的生活需要已经满足，也不表示社会不再有可以完成的有用劳动。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The industrial reserve army is the labouring population repeatedly made surplus by accumulation and changes in production, relative to the employment required for capital's expansion. “Surplus” concerns how much labour power capital requires; it does not mean those people's needs are satisfied or that society has no useful work left to do.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

[[积累与劳动需求]]会同时出现吸收和排斥：设备更新排出一些工人，扩张又可吸收其中一部分，停滞或危机再增加未充分就业者。后备劳动又能为突然扩张提供可招募的人，并通过就业竞争约束在岗工人的工资要求。因此，这是生产变动与劳动市场相互作用的机制。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
[[积累与劳动需求|Accumulation and labour demand]] involve attraction and displacement: equipment renewal releases workers, expansion can recruit some, and stagnation or crisis adds people without full employment. Reserve labour can supply recruits for sudden expansion and constrain employed workers' wage claims through competition. This is an interaction between production changes and the labour market.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

原典包括完全失业与部分就业，并区分流动、潜在、停滞等形式。比如，因某部门更新设备而失去职位的人，后来可能被扩张中的部门吸收；在转换期间仍会承受收入损失与重新就业困难。这不是以每名工人永远不再就业来定义后备军。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The text includes wholly unemployed and partially employed workers and distinguishes floating, latent and stagnant forms. Someone displaced by equipment renewal may later be recruited into an expanding sector, while still experiencing income loss and difficulty finding work during the transition. Membership does not require permanent exclusion from employment.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

接到其他课程时，要保留三个不同判据：[[无限劳动供给]]问特定工资附近能否持续招到额外劳动；[[剩余劳动力]]的课堂口径问劳动移出会否减少来源部门产出；统计[[失业率]]则按未就业、求职和可工作等条件分类。它们与产业后备军可能涉及部分相同的人，却没有同一个定义或分母。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Across courses, retain three separate criteria: [[无限劳动供给|unlimited labour supply]] asks whether additional labour remains recruitable near a given wage; the classroom criterion for [[剩余劳动力|surplus labour]] asks whether withdrawal reduces source-sector output; the statistical [[失业率|unemployment rate]] classifies people using joblessness, search and availability criteria. These may cover some of the same people as the reserve army, but their definitions and denominators differ.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
