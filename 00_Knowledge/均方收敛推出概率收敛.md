<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
# From mean-square to probability convergence
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

*均方收敛为何能控制超差概率*

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If the average squared error of an approximation becomes tiny, a substantial error cannot still occur with a substantial probability. Every such occurrence contributes a fixed positive amount to that average. This is why [[均方收敛|mean-square convergence]] guarantees [[依概率收敛|convergence in probability]].
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

如果一个近似的平均平方误差已经很小，明显的误差就不可能仍以明显的概率出现：每次这样的误差，都会给平方误差的平均贡献一个固定的正值。这就是[[均方收敛]]能够保证[[依概率收敛]]的原因。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Write $X_n$ for the approximation at step $n$ and $X$ for its target. They are real-valued, square-integrable random variables on one probability space, so $X_n-X$ is the error for the same outcome. The mean-square assumption is $E[(X_n-X)^2]\to0$; the probability conclusion asks whether the chance of exceeding **every fixed** tolerance $\varepsilon>0$ tends to zero. Independence is not required.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

用 $X_n$ 表示第 $n$ 个近似，$X$ 表示目标。它们是同一概率空间上平方可积的实值随机变量，因此 $X_n-X$ 比较同一个结果下的误差。均方收敛假设为 $E[(X_n-X)^2]\to0$；概率结论则询问：超过**每个固定**正容忍值 $\varepsilon$ 的概率是否趋于零。这里不要求独立。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Turning squared error into a probability bound
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 正向蕴含

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The squared error $W_n=(X_n-X)^2$ is nonnegative. The [[Markov不等式|Markov inequality]] says that a nonnegative random variable exceeds a positive threshold $t$ with probability at most its mean divided by $t$. Use $t=\varepsilon^2$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

平方误差 $W_n=(X_n-X)^2$ 非负。[[Markov不等式]]说明：一个非负随机变量超过正阈值 $t$ 的概率，至多为其均值除以 $t$。代入 $t=\varepsilon^2$：

> [!important] A quantitative implication · 可计算的概率保证
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> P(|X_n-X|>\varepsilon)
> &=P((X_n-X)^2>\varepsilon^2)\\
> &\le\frac{E[(X_n-X)^2]}{\varepsilon^2}
> \longrightarrow0.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Squaring preserves the comparison because both magnitudes are nonnegative and $\varepsilon>0$. The denominator stays fixed while the numerator tends to zero. Hence $X_n\xrightarrow{L^2}X$ implies $X_n\xrightarrow pX$.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 因为比较的是非负大小且 $\varepsilon>0$，平方前后的事件相同。分母固定不变，分子趋于零，因此 $X_n\xrightarrow{L^2}X$ 推出 $X_n\xrightarrow pX$。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, if $E[(X_n-X)^2]\le4/n$, the probability of an error larger than $0.2$ is at most $100/n$ (and at most one). At $n=2000$ this gives a bound of $0.05$. It is an upper bound, not the actual error probability, and it becomes uninformative when it exceeds one.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

例如，若 $E[(X_n-X)^2]\le4/n$，误差超过 $0.2$ 的概率至多为 $100/n$，当然也至多为 1。在 $n=2000$ 时，上界为 $0.05$。它只是上界，不是实际误差概率；若算出的上界超过 1，就没有提供额外信息。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
## Why rare errors can defeat the reverse direction
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
## 为什么不能一般反推

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Draw one $U$ [[连续均匀分布|uniformly on $[0,1]$]], and define $X_n=n\mathbf1\{U\le1/n^2\}$, where the [[事件指示变量|indicator]] equals one on the stated event and zero elsewhere. This constructs the whole sequence on one space. Its distribution is
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

只抽一次[[连续均匀分布|均匀分布在 $[0,1]$]] 上的 $U$，令 $X_n=n\mathbf1\{U\le1/n^2\}$，其中[[事件指示变量|指示变量]]在所写事件发生时为 1，否则为 0。这明确地把整个序列放在同一空间上。它的分布为

$$P(X_n=n)=\frac1{n^2},\qquad P(X_n=0)=1-\frac1{n^2}.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For any fixed $\varepsilon>0$, eventually $n>\varepsilon$, so $P(|X_n|>\varepsilon)=1/n^2\to0$. Thus $X_n\xrightarrow p0$. Every variable has a finite second moment, but
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

对任意固定 $\varepsilon>0$，最终都有 $n>\varepsilon$，所以 $P(|X_n|>\varepsilon)=1/n^2\to0$，即 $X_n\xrightarrow p0$。每个变量的二阶矩都有限，但

$$E[X_n^2]=n^2\frac1{n^2}=1.$$

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
The shrinking probability is exactly offset by the growing squared size. The sequence therefore does **not** converge in mean square to zero. In this particular construction it even converges to zero [[几乎必然收敛|almost surely]]: for each $U>0$, eventually $1/n^2<U$ and the spike disappears forever. Neither probability nor almost-sure convergence alone controls average squared error.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

越来越小的概率被越来越大的误差平方恰好抵消，因此这个序列**不**均方收敛到零。在这个具体构造中，它甚至[[几乎必然收敛]]到零：对每个 $U>0$，最终 $1/n^2<U$，尖峰从此消失。依概率收敛或几乎必然收敛本身，都不能控制平均平方误差。

> [!tip] What extra information can help? · 加什么条件可能加强结论？
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Extra control must stop squared error from hiding in increasingly rare, increasingly large values. One sufficient condition is [[一致可积|uniform integrability]] of the **squared errors** $\{(X_n-X)^2\}$, together with $X_n\xrightarrow pX$. Indeed, the squared errors then converge in probability to zero, because $P((X_n-X)^2>t)=P(|X_n-X|>\sqrt t)\to0$ for each $t>0$. The probability version of the uniform-integrability criterion makes their expectations tend to zero; the linked [[一致可积与L1收敛|almost-sure version]] is a stronger-assumption special case. Mere boundedness of these expectations is insufficient: the example above keeps all of them equal to one.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->
>
> 额外条件需要阻止平方误差藏进越来越罕见、越来越巨大的值中。一种充分条件是**平方误差族** $\{(X_n-X)^2\}$ [[一致可积]]，同时 $X_n\xrightarrow pX$；平方误差于是依概率收敛到零，因为对每个 $t>0$，有 $P((X_n-X)^2>t)=P(|X_n-X|>\sqrt t)\to0$。一致可积判据的依概率版本使它们的期望趋于零；链接中的[[一致可积与L1收敛|几乎必然版本]]是使用更强收敛假设的特例。只要求这些期望有界还不够，上面的反例就始终等于一。
