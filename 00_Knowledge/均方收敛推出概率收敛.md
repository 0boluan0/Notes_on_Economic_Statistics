# 均方收敛必然推出依概率收敛，而依概率收敛一般不能反推均方收敛
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Mean-square convergence implies convergence in probability, but convergence in probability does not imply mean-square convergence in general*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!summary] 二阶矩控制强于偏离概率
> 若 $X_n\xrightarrow{L^2}X$，则必有 $X_n\xrightarrow pX$。反向一般不成立：小概率上的巨大误差可以让超过任意固定阈值的概率趋于零，却让均方误差不趋于零。
> <!-- bilingual-en:start -->
> Mean-square convergence always implies convergence in probability. The converse can fail because rare but very large errors may vanish in probability while keeping expected squared error away from zero.
> <!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 正向蕴含

若 $E[(X_n-X)^2]\to0$，则对每个 $\varepsilon>0$，把 [[Markov不等式]] 用在非负随机变量 $(X_n-X)^2$ 上可得

$$
\begin{aligned}
P(|X_n-X|>\varepsilon)
&=P((X_n-X)^2>\varepsilon^2)\\
&\le\frac{E[(X_n-X)^2]}{\varepsilon^2}
\longrightarrow0.
\end{aligned}
$$

这正是 $X_n\xrightarrow pX$ 的定义。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Applying Markov's inequality to the non-negative variable $(X_n-X)^2$ bounds every fixed-tolerance error probability by the mean squared error divided by $\varepsilon^2$. Hence $L^2$ convergence implies convergence in probability.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

## 为什么不能一般反推

令

$$
P(X_n=n)=\frac1{n^2},
\qquad
P(X_n=0)=1-\frac1{n^2}.
$$

对任意固定 $\varepsilon>0$，最终都有

$$
P(|X_n|>\varepsilon)=\frac1{n^2}\longrightarrow0,
$$

所以 $X_n\xrightarrow p0$。但

$$
E[X_n^2]=n^2\cdot\frac1{n^2}=1
$$

始终不趋于 0，因此 $X_n$ 不均方收敛到 0。依概率收敛只看明显偏离发生得是否越来越少；均方收敛还会放大小概率的大误差。

<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
If $X_n=n$ with probability $1/n^2$ and zero otherwise, then $X_n\to0$ in probability, but $E[X_n^2]=1$ for every $n$. Rare errors are enough to break the converse.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

> [!question]- 自检
> 若已知 $X_n\xrightarrow pX$，再加上什么类型的信息才可能把结论加强为均方收敛？
>
> **答案：** 还要控制平方误差的尾部。例如，若已知 $X_n\xrightarrow{p}X$，并能证明平方误差族 $\{(X_n-X)^2\}$ 统一可积，就可推出 $E[(X_n-X)^2]\to0$；仅有依概率收敛不够。
