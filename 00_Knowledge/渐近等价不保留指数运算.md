# 指数中的相对误差趋零仍可能使指数函数的比值无界
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
*Vanishing relative error in an exponent can still produce an unbounded ratio after exponentiation*
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

即使 $f\sim g$，对固定 $a>1$，也不能直接推出 $a^f\sim a^g$，甚至不能保证 $a^f=\Theta(a^g)$。原因是 [[渐近等价]] 控制 $(f-g)/g$，而取指数后的比值取决于绝对差 $f-g$：
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Even if $f\sim g$, a fixed base $a>1$ does not guarantee $a^f\sim a^g$, or even $a^f=\Theta(a^g)$. [[渐近等价|Asymptotic equivalence]] controls $(f-g)/g$, whereas the ratio after exponentiation depends on the absolute difference $f-g$:
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

$$
\frac{a^{f(n)}}{a^{g(n)}}=a^{f(n)-g(n)}.
$$

例如 $f(n)=n+\sqrt n$、$g(n)=n$。虽然 $f/g=1+1/\sqrt n\to1$，但 $3^f/3^g=3^{\sqrt n}\to\infty$，所以 $3^f$ 连 $O(3^g)$ 都不是。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For example, let $f(n)=n+\sqrt n$ and $g(n)=n$. Although $f/g=1+1/\sqrt n\to1$, the ratio $3^f/3^g=3^{\sqrt n}$ tends to infinity, so $3^f$ is not even $O(3^g)$.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

要合法比较，应先问绝对差是否受控。固定 $a>1$ 时，$a^f\sim a^g$ 当且仅当 $f-g\to0$；$a^f=\Theta(a^g)$ 当且仅当 $f-g$ 最终在两个有限常数之间。这两点由对正比值取 $\log_a$ 得到，不能把相对误差条件替换进去。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
For a valid comparison, first ask whether the absolute difference is controlled. With fixed $a>1$, $a^f\sim a^g$ holds exactly when $f-g\to0$, while $a^f=\Theta(a^g)$ holds exactly when $f-g$ eventually lies between two finite constants. Both follow by taking $\log_a$ of the positive ratio; a relative-error condition cannot replace them.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->

因此将渐近近似代入非线性函数之前，先写出变换后真正需要控制的误差。不是所有“小误差”在变换后仍小。
<!-- bilingual-en:start -->
Before substituting an asymptotic approximation into a nonlinear function, identify the error actually required by the transformed comparison. An error that is small in one sense need not remain small after transformation.
<!-- bilingual-en:end -->
